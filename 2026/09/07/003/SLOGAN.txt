Minimum real rank of diagonal-ones J2-free binary matrices is r(2..5)=2,3,3,4 with 6 and 390 minimizers in 1 and 5 simultaneous-permutation orbits.
Directed 4-cycle block-diagonal tiling achieves r(n)<=ceil(3n/4) for all n and is optimal among row-weight<=2 matrices; ceil(n/2)<=r(n) proved to n<=8, full ceil(3n/4) conjectured.
