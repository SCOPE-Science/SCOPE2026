# Independent audit — 2026-09-30

## Final claim

For the undirected Cayley graph of PSL(2,13) generated by the elementary transvection pair and their inverses, the graph has 1092 vertices, exact diameter 10 with sphere distribution `[1,4,12,25,48,96,180,279,317,120,10]`, and exact girth 6 with 28 reduced length-6 identities and none of length at most 5.

## Correctness — PASS

A separate implementation using exact matrix multiplication modulo 13, projective sign canonicalization, BFS, and reduced-word enumeration reproduced all headline values. It visited 1092 PSL classes, obtained diameter 10 and the stated sphere distribution, verified the diameter witness, found no reduced identity at lengths 1–5, and found exactly 28 at length 6. The witness `aaBaaB` evaluates to `-I`, hence to the identity in PSL(2,13).

Residual risk: this is an exhaustive computational proof, so its trust base is exact integer arithmetic plus the independently implemented BFS and word-enumeration logic.
