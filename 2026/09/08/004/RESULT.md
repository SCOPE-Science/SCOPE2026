# Regularity of edge ideals of open ladders equals the induced matching number

## Context

Castelnuovo–Mumford regularity of squarefree monomial edge ideals links
syzygies to graph combinatorics. Katzman's bound $\nu(G)\le \mathrm{reg}(S/I(G))$
holds for every graph, with equality for forests, chordal and co-chordal
graphs, and some fan families, but was open for the ladder family — the
smallest subcubic $4$-cycle-rich bipartite family. Ladders are
$L_n=P_n\square K_2$ ($2\times n$ grids): two $n$-paths joined by $n$ rungs.
They are bipartite, contain many $4$-cycles, and lie outside the
forest/chordal/co-chordal/fan/down-left classes with known formulas.

## Definitions

- $L_n=P_n\square K_2$, $n\ge 1$: vertices
  $V=\{a_1,\dots,a_n\}\cup\{b_1,\dots,b_n\}$; edges $a_ia_{i+1}$,
  $b_ib_{i+1}$ ($1\le i<n$) and rungs $a_ib_i$ ($1\le i\le n$).
  So $|V|=2n$, $|E|=3n-2$ for $n\ge 1$ (with $L_1=K_2$).
- $S=k[V]$ polynomial ring over a field $k$; $I(G)$ the edge ideal
  generated by $x_ux_v$ for $uv\in E(G)$.
  $\mathrm{reg}(S/I)=\max\{j-i:\beta_{i,j}(S/I)\ne 0\}$;
  $\mathrm{reg}(I)=\mathrm{reg}(S/I)+1$.
- $\nu(G)$: induced matching number (max set of pairwise disjoint edges
  with no edge of $G$ joining distinct members).
- $M_m$ ($m\ge 1$): $L_m$ plus one pendant vertex $c$ attached to $b_m$.
  $M_0$ is a single vertex, $M_{-1}=\varnothing$ (both regularity $0$);
  isolated vertices do not affect regularity.
- $\mathrm{Ind}(G[W])$: independence complex of the induced subgraph on
  $W\subseteq V$.

## Result

**Theorem.** For the open ladder $L_n=P_n\square K_2$ over any field $k$,
$$\mathrm{reg}(S/I(L_n))=\lceil n/2\rceil=\nu(L_n).$$
Equivalently $\mathrm{reg}(I(L_n))=\lceil n/2\rceil+1$.
Moreover $\mathrm{reg}(S/I(M_m))=\lceil m/2\rceil$ for the pendant family,
and the extremal homology is witnessed in closed form on the odd-rung
vertex set $W_n=\{a_i,b_i:i\text{ odd}\}$: $L_n[W_n]$ is $\lceil n/2\rceil$
disjoint rungs, so $\mathrm{Ind}(L_n[W_n])$ is a sphere
$S^{\lceil n/2\rceil-1}$ with $\tilde H_{\lceil n/2\rceil-1}=k$.

Table ($n\le 6$, machine-verified over $\mathbb{F}_2,\mathbb{F}_{7919},
\mathbb{F}_{32003}$, all agreeing):
$n=1..6$: $\mathrm{reg}(S/I(L_n))=1,1,2,2,3,3$;
$\nu(L_n)=1,1,2,2,3,3$; $\mathrm{reg}(S/I(M_n))=1,1,2,2,3,3$.

## Proof / Evidence

**Lemma 1 (induced matching).** $\nu(L_n)=\lceil n/2\rceil$.
*Proof.* Lower: odd rungs $\{a_ib_i:i\text{ odd}\}$ are pairwise at
distance $\ge 2$ along the rails, hence induced; there are
$\lceil n/2\rceil$. Upper: index columns $1,\dots,n$; each edge meets
one column (rung) or two consecutive columns (rail edge). If two edges
are induced, their column sets must be disjoint with at least one
column gap (adjacent columns are joined by rail edges; same column edges
share a vertex or are joined by a rung/rail edge). Hence $k$ induced
edges occupy $\ge 2k-1$ columns, so $2k-1\le n$ and
$k\le\lceil n/2\rceil$. ∎
Katzman (2006) $\nu\le\mathrm{reg}$ gives
$\mathrm{reg}(S/I(L_n))\ge\lceil n/2\rceil$.

**Lemma 2 (deletion isomorphisms).** For $n\ge 3$, $x=a_n$:
$L_n-x\cong M_{n-1}$ ($b_n$ becomes the pendant $c$) and
$L_n-N[x]\cong M_{n-2}$ (deleting $a_n,a_{n-1},b_n$ leaves $L_{n-2}$
with $b_{n-1}$ pendant on $b_{n-2}$). For $m\ge 1$, $y=b_m\in M_m$:
$M_m-y\cong M_{m-1}\sqcup\{\text{isolate}\}$ and
$M_m-N[y]\cong M_{m-2}$ (up to rail swap). Verified by inspection;
machine-checked for $n=3,4,5$ (12/12 isomorphism checks).

**Lemma 3 (vertex bound, standard).** For any graph $G$ and vertex $x$,
$$\mathrm{reg}(S/I(G))\le\max\{\mathrm{reg}(S/I(G-x)),\,
\mathrm{reg}(S/I(G-N[x]))+1\}.$$
This is the regularity form of the vertex-decomposition exact sequence
(Hà–Van Tuyl 2007; Kalai–Meshulam / Dao–Huneke–Schweig / Woodroofe 2011
form $\mathrm{reg}(I(G))\le\max\{\mathrm{reg}(I(G-x))+1,
\mathrm{reg}(I(G-N[x]))+2\}$, minus one for the quotient).

**Proposition (joint induction).** $\mathrm{reg}(S/I(M_m))=\lceil m/2\rceil$
and $\mathrm{reg}(S/I(L_n))\le\lceil n/2\rceil$.
*Proof.* Bases $m,n\le 6$ by exhaustive Hochster enumeration below.
For $t\ge 7$, Lemma 2+3 gives
$\mathrm{reg}(M_m)\le\max(\lceil(m-1)/2\rceil,\lceil(m-2)/2\rceil+1)$
and likewise for $L_n$; direct check
$\max(\lceil(t-1)/2\rceil,\lceil(t-2)/2\rceil+1)=\lceil t/2\rceil$
for all $t\ge 1$. The lower bound for $M_m$ uses the same odd-rung
induced matching (disjoint from $c$), so equality holds. ∎
Combined with Katzman, $\mathrm{reg}(S/I(L_n))=\lceil n/2\rceil=\nu(L_n)$.

**Extremal witnesses (all $n$, characteristic-free).** $L_n[W_n]$ is the
disjoint union of $m=\lceil n/2\rceil$ copies of $K_2$; its independence
complex is the join of $m$ copies of $S^0$, i.e. $S^{m-1}$, so
$\tilde H_{m-1}=k$ over any field (hence over $\mathbb Z$ a $\mathbb Z$
summand). Machine-confirmed 1-dimensional in degree $m-1$ for $n\le 6$
in all three characteristics.

**Machine verification (bases).** `verify_ladders.py` (stdlib only)
enumerates all $W\subseteq V$ (max $2^{12}$ for $L_6$, $2^{13}$ for $M_6$),
builds the simplicial chain complex of $\mathrm{Ind}(G[W])$ over
$\mathbb{F}_p$, $p\in\{2,7919,32003\}$, computes reduced homology by
mod-$p$ Gauss–Jordan, and applies Hochster's formula
$\mathrm{reg}=\max_W(k(W)+1)$, $k(W)=\max\{k:\tilde H_k\ne 0\}$
($0$ if acyclic). Also brute-forces $\nu$ and checks deletion
isomorphisms. Results identical in all three characteristics; the
vertex recursion is exact on the nose for $n\le 6$. Independent
co-chordal-cover DP (Woodroofe upper bound) also gives $\lceil n/2\rceil$.

## Limitations

- The upper bound cites the published vertex-recursion lemma; the new
  content is its closure on ladders via the auxiliary family $M_m$ plus
  machine bases — not a new splitting theorem.
- Exhaustive Hochster enumeration covers $n\le 6$; general $n$ is by
  induction (ceiling arithmetic exact).
- Closed-prism/Möbius variants are data only ($Y_3$ reg 2 vs
  $K_{3,3}$ reg 1; $Y_4$ and Möb$_4$ both reg 2), not theorems.
- Characteristic-independence is proved by the $\mathbb Z$-sphere witness
  plus the combinatorial upper bound, corroborated (not solely proved) by
  three-characteristic agreement.

## Reproducibility

Run `python3 output/artifacts/verify_ladders.py` (stdlib only, ~9 s);
it writes `reg_table.json` with regs, witnesses, matchings, extremal
homology, twist regs, and 12 iso-check passes. Re-audit spot checks:
$L_1$–$L_6$ reg $1,1,2,2,3,3$; $M_1$–$M_5$ reg $1,1,2,2,3$; odd-rung
homology 1-dimensional in degree $\lceil n/2\rceil-1$.
