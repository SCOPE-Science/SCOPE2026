For five explicit canonical representatives of the Fedorov parallelohedron combinatorial types, the exact real illumination numbers are 8 (cube), 6 (hexagonal-prism representative), 6 (rhombic-dodecahedron representative), 5 (elongated-rhombic-dodecahedron representative), and 4 (truncated-octahedron representative).
Optimality is certified twice: by pairwise-conflict cliques of sizes 8, 6, 6, 5, 4 and by complete realizable sign-cell enumeration with exact set-cover optima over all real directions. These values are proved for the displayed representatives and their affine images, not for every realization of each Fedorov combinatorial type.
