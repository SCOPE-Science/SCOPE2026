Intersecting 3-graphs on 9-13 vertices have maximum diversity D(n)=n-3 attained by the triangle (two-out-of-three) family with 3n-8 edges and max degree 2n-5, and every non-star has minimum degree at most 3 so delta>=4 forces a star.
Machine-checked branch-and-bound over triples meeting a fixed edge certifies UNSAT of diversity >=n-2 and of non-star min-degree >=4 for each n=9..13, with an independent verifier replay and brute-force cross-checks; extremal-type uniqueness is not claimed.
