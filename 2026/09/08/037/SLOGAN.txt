Mahler spectrum over the 16 GL(2,Z)-classes of reflexive lattice polygons is exactly {27/4 (x2, dual triangle pair), 8 (x6), 35/4 (x4), 9 (x4, ids with 3/4/5/6 vertices)}, min 27/4 max 9 in Euclidean areas.
Exactly 3 reflexive classes are centrally symmetric: two parallelograms with Mahler product 8 and one hexagon with product 9, so symmetric M is 8 iff parallelogram, giving sharp reflexive-family stability gap G=1 above 8.
