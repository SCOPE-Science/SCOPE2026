# Certified Kronecker atlas to n=10 with a Murnaghan-ray certificate and S10 argmax witness

## Context

Kronecker coefficients \(g(\lambda,\mu,\nu)\) govern tensor products of irreducible complex characters of \(S_n\). They are central to Murnaghan/Stembridge stability theory, the Saxl staircase-positivity program, Geometric Complexity Theory positivity benchmarks, and symmetric-function test data. General stability theorems give structural results but not the complete small-\(n\) table below. Blasiak (arXiv:1209.2018, 2012; published 2017) explicitly noted that the maximum size of a Kronecker coefficient for \(n=10\) is 117, so the value \(M(10)=117\) is prior art. The contribution here is the independently certified complete atlas through \(n=10\), the exact ray computation through \(n=12\), and the unique maximizing triple plus a term-by-term certificate.

## Definitions

- A partition \(\lambda\vdash n\) indexes the irreducible character \(\chi^\lambda\) of \(S_n\).
- \(g(\lambda,\mu,\nu)=\frac1{n!}\sum_C |C|\chi^\lambda(C)\chi^\mu(C)\chi^\nu(C)\), over conjugacy classes \(C\), with \(|C|=n!/z_\rho\) for cycle type \(\rho\).
- Partitions are in decreasing lexicographic order.
- Ray: \(\lambda(n)=\mu(n)=(n-3,2,1)\), \(\nu(n)=(n-2,1,1)\), for \(6\le n\le12\).

### (b) Murnaghan-ray values

For \(\lambda(n)=\mu(n)=(n-3,2,1)\), \(\nu(n)=(n-2,1,1)\),
\[
g_6=g_7=\cdots=g_{12}=4.
\]
The computed window is constant from its first defined point \(n=6\). This is an in-window observation; the computation does not independently establish a minimal abstract stability threshold below the first defined shape.

### (c) Certified \(n=10\) argmax witness

The prior-known maximum value is \(M(10)=117\). This computation additionally identifies the unique ordered maximizing triple as
\(\lambda=\mu=\nu=(4,3,2,1)\) and verifies
\[
\sum_C |C|\chi(C)^3 = 117\cdot 10! = 424569600
\]
term-by-term over the 42 conjugacy classes.

## Proof / evidence

Two structurally different from-partitions routes produce identical \(S_n\) character tables for \(1\le n\le12\): Murnaghan–Nakayama rim-hook recursion, and Young-subgroup permutation characters followed by Kostka-number unitriangular inversion. Every Kronecker coefficient through \(n=10\) is evaluated from each table by the exact class-sum inner product with divisibility checks. The ray points \(n=11,12\) are evaluated by both tables. Row/column orthogonality, identity-column hook-length dimensions, transpose symmetry, and \(S_3\) symmetry of the Kronecker table are checked.

A separate audit implementation rebuilt the character tables and exhaustively recomputed all ordered triples through \(n=10\), reproducing the nonzero/zero counts, maxima, argmax multiplicities, ray values, and the 42-term \(n=10\) certificate.

## Limitations

- The complete exhaustive atlas stops at \(n=10\).
- Character tables were also certified at \(n=11,12\), but no full Kronecker census is claimed there.
- The ray constancy is computed only for \(6\le n\le12\); general stabilization is a theorem from the literature, not a consequence of these seven evaluations.
- The value \(M(10)=117\) is not claimed as novel; Blasiak explicitly recorded that maximum in earlier work.
- No Saxl or GCT conjecture is proved.
