Exact polar-dual volumes and Mahler products for five reflexive 3-polytopes spanning normalized volumes 4,6,8,10,12: (Vol,Vol*,M)=(4,64,256),(6,54,324),(8,48,384),(10,42,420),(12,36,432), each certified by exact facet enumeration and two agreeing determinant triangulations.
The simplex witness attains normalized product 256 (Euclidean 64/9, vertex sum zero) hence is the absolute general 3D Mahler minimizer, and the octahedron attains 384 (Euclidean 32/3) hence is the absolute symmetric minimizer; all other reflexive P satisfy Vol(P*)>=256/Vol(P) (384/Vol(P) if symmetric).
