# Exact Delta-set and catenary-degree table with Betti certificates for nine committed embedding-dimension-3 triples of multiplicity 4-6

## Context

Nonunique-factorization invariants of numerical semigroups — Delta sets,
catenary degree, and the sharpness boundary of the general inequality
maxDelta(S)+2 <= c(S) — are a recognized program (Chapman,
Garcia-Sanchez, Geroldinger, O'Neill and collaborators). Embedding
dimension 3 with multiplicity 4-6 is the smallest natural band where
Delta sets are nontrivial and Betti elements are few, so exact
per-semigroup values with certificates test that boundary.

## Definitions

Let S = <n1,n2,n3> with gcd 1. A factorization of s in S is
(a1,a2,a3) in N^3 with a1*n1+a2*n2+a3*n3 = s; its length is a1+a2+a3.
L(s) is the set of lengths. Delta(s) is the set of consecutive gaps of
sorted L(s) (empty if |L(s)|<=1); Delta(S) is the union over all s.
The distance between factorizations z,z' is
d(z,z') = max(|z-gcd(z,z')|,|z'-gcd(z,z')|) with coordinate-wise gcd and
sum norm. c(s) is the least N such that any two factorizations of s are
joined by an N-chain; c(S) = sup_s c(s). A Betti element is an s whose
factorization graph (vertices = factorizations, edges join pairs sharing
a common atom) is disconnected. Ap(S,m) is the set of least semigroup
elements in each residue class mod m = min generators. c_i is the least
k>0 with k*n_i in the semigroup generated by the other two generators.

## Proof / evidence

Proved in-run from the committed generator strings by the archived
verifier: (a) gcd 1 and minimal generation for each triple by exhaustive
2-coin checks; (b) Apery sets by Dijkstra shortest-paths on the residue
graph mod m, cross-checked by brute membership search; (c) each c_i by
exhibiting a 2-coin representation at c_i and exhaustive failure below;
(d) for every listed c_i*n_i value, the complete factorization list by
bounded double loop (exact for 3 generators), R-class partition (all
listed values have >1 class, hence are Betti), length sets, Delta gaps,
and catenary degrees by distance-threshold connectivity search;
(e) Betti-completeness: brute Betti scan to 400 finds no further Betti
element on any row. The per-Betti rows therefore fix the per-Betti
columns exactly.

Globalization (global Delta = union of Betti Delta sets; global
catenary = maximum of Betti catenary degrees) invokes cited standard
Betti-determination theorems, not re-proved here. Computed corroboration:
exhaustive element scan to bound 400 confirms the Betti-derived
Delta-union equals the brute-force Delta-union and the Betti-maximum
catenary equals the brute-force maximum on every row (auditor extended
the Delta scan to 2000 with continued agreement).

Sample distance: at 20 in <4,9,11>, gcd((0,1,1),(5,0,0))=(0,0,0), so
d = max(2,5) = 5, and with two factorizations c(20) = 5.

## Limitations

- Global Delta-union and cat-max steps depend on cited external theorems;
  the in-run contribution is exact per-Betti certification plus bound-400
  agreement, not a new proof of those theorems.
- The bound-400 (auditor 2000 for Delta) scan is corroborating evidence only.
- One row (<5,6,9>, Delta={1}, c=3) overlaps a published illustration in
  arXiv:1909.09419 and is a re-derived control; novelty rests on the
  other eight rows plus the combined verdict table and extremals.
- Claims cover only the nine committed triples, not a complete census of
  the multiplicity band; extremals are within the nine.
