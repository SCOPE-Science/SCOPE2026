Odd overpartitions with parts prime to 3 satisfy S(m) = 2*t(m) mod 4, i.e. 2 mod 4 iff m with factors of 3 removed is a square else 0 mod 4; hence S(p) = 0 mod 4 for all primes p != 3 and S(2m) = 0 mod 4 for odd m.
Proof is a one-line mod-4 two-dissection G(q) = G(q^2)(1+2T(q)) plus a commuting fixed-point-free involution pair whose V4-orbits have size 4 off 2*t(m) rectangles, giving S(m) = 4k+2t(m) bijectively.
S(4n+2) = 0 mod 4 subcase overlaps Sang-Shi (4.33); the exact odd square criterion, prime family, and involution witness are new.
