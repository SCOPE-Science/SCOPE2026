Fang et al. (arXiv:2606.14367) Conjecture 1 — 4-way involution equidistribution of Class-69 length-2 mesh patterns — is false: patterns split {A,B} vs {C,D} on involutions from n=3 (avoidance 2 vs 1; histograms {0:2,1:1,2:1} vs {0:1,1:2,2:1}), widening to 1407 vs 735 at n=9. On the full symmetric group all four agree through n=9 (e.g. n=7: 1956/1690/887/365/120/21/1), so the 2019 full-group conjecture is untouched.
