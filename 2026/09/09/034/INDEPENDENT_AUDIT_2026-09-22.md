Classical Kronecker theory supplies the pencil canonical forms, so those forms themselves are prior. The reduction of the five-dimensional centre-at-least-two two-step slice, its faithful-locus condition, ordered-pair families and explicit structure constants were not located in the checked compatible-algebra literature.
