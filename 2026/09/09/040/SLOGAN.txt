Minimal graph-type Rédei blocking sets in PG(2,13) are certified here at sizes 21, 23 and 24 with exact direction sets, tangents and line spectra.
No nine-direction function occurs among all monomials, binomials, trinomials, or degree<=6 polynomials over F_13, so any size-22 graph-function representative has at least four nonzero terms and degree at least 7; this is only a graph-family obstruction, and size-22 Rédei-type minimal blocking sets were already reported by Kadoo (2010).
