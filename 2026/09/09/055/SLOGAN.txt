C0: y^2=x^6+3x^4-5x^2+7 has no integral points: f(x) is 3 mod 4 for even x and 2 mod 4 for odd x, never a square; 2-adically f(Z_2) mod 8 = {3,6,7} avoids squares {0,1,4}, so C(Z_2) is empty.
Any affine rational point x=a/b in lowest terms on C0 has 4 dividing b: otherwise homogenized numerator N(a,b) is 3 mod 4, 2 mod 4, or 5 mod 8, each a quadratic non-residue; projective closure has two Q-points at infinity so emptiness is affine-integral only.
