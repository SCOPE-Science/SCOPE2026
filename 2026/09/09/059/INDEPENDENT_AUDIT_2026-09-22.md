## Scientific value — PASS, qualified

An explicit compact SKT central threefold whose BC(1,1) dimension drops under a named deformation is useful test data for non-Kähler Hodge theory. The result is only a local direction and does not compute all nearby BC groups or an SKT deformation stratum.
