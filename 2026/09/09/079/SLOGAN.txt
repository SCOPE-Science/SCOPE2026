Central-unipotent 7-lift class C_q in SL(2,q) is mixed, not never-Ramanujan: Borel set S_B={-I,u(+-1),u(+-2),u(+-3)} generates an order-2q subgroup so X(S_B) is disconnected at every prime q>=7; explicit S_R in C_7 (r1=(4,2,6,5), r2=(2,4,5,0), r3=(1,4,0,1) mod 7) generates SL(2,7) with lambda2(X(S_R))<=4.89<2*sqrt(6) by exact integer Bareiss and Rayleigh certificates, hence Ramanujan on 672 vertices.
