Every solvable subgroup of the free Burnside group B(2,665) is cyclic of order dividing 665, so B(2,665) has trivial solvable radical and no nontrivial finite normal subgroup.
Proved at the Adian bound from finite-subgroup cyclicity, trivial centre, and 665-squarefree arithmetic; full amenable radical and C*-simplicity at 665 remain open.
