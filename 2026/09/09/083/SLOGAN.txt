Chatelet surface y^2-17z^2=(x^2-3)(x^2-5) over Q is everywhere locally soluble: the integral smooth point (2,4,1) is a Q_v-point for each of inf,2,3,5,17 and reduces to a smooth F_p-point for every other prime p. Discriminant 2^8*3*5 forces bad set {inf,2,3,5,17}; diagonal Brauer sum for (17,x^2-3) is 0, so no Hasse failure at this cell.
