## Correctness — PASS

E₂ is balanced and convex. Its Minkowski gauge equals K_E(0,·): the linear lower bound follows from a supporting complex linear functional and Schwarz, while radial discs give the upper bound. For any normalized injective f with B_r⊂f(E₂)⊂B₁, metric monotonicity gives r m(v)≤|df₀(v)|≤m(v), so its derivative is an admissible linear embedding with at least the same inner radius. This correctly reduces the nonlinear squeezing supremum to linear maps. For A* A with off-diagonal c, phase choices make the outer maximum increase and the inner minimum decrease as |c| increases; diagonal maps suffice. Along |z₂|²=u, the boundary has |z₁|²=1−u² and an isotropic scaling t has outer squared norm t²(1+u−u²), whose maximum is 5t²/4 at u=1/2. It maps into B₁ iff t≤2/√5, and B₁⊂E₂ gives an inner ball of radius t. For any diagonal a,b, evaluating the outer norm at u=1/2 gives at least (5/4)min(a²,b²), while the inner radius cannot exceed min(a,b); hence no larger r is possible. Thus s_E₂(0)=2/√5.
