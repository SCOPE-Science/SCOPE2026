Equal-mass Brill–Lindquist data (bare masses 1,1; separation 2): archived finite-difference calculations give sampled stability-potential values near Q_min=0.157901 and mean(Q)=0.159996 in code units, with lower-resolution replay in the same range.
These data are reproducible numerical evidence for positive stability of the individual minimal-surface candidate, not a validated continuum enclosure for the exact MOTS principal eigenvalue; prior literature already studies MOTS stability in Brill–Lindquist binary data.
