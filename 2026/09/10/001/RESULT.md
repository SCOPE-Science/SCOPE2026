# Nielsen census of standard-generator (2,3,19) triples of PSL(2,19)

## Context

Triangle-group quotients, Hurwitz-type generation of finite simple groups,
Nielsen classes, braid rigidity, and Hurwitz-space connectivity form a
recognized program in which the exact Nielsen-class count for a
standard-generator type is the standard citable unit. For
G = PSL(2,19) = L2(19), of order 3420 = 2^2·3^2·5·19, the ATLAS records
standard generators a, b with o(a) = 2, o(b) = 3, o(ab) = 19, i.e. type
(2,3,19). The ATLAS gives existence, the class list, and a degree-20
permutation representation, but no generation-filtered Nielsen count and no
braid-orbit data.

## Definitions

- Work on P1(F19) = {0,...,18,19 = ∞} (20 points). G is generated by
  S: z ↦ −1/z and T: z ↦ z+1, as committed permutations in
  `artifacts/group_setup.json` (S swaps 0/∞, T is +1 on F19 fixing ∞).
- An ordered (2,3,19) pair is (x,y) with exact orders o(x) = 2, o(y) = 3,
  o(xy) = 19. Since 19 is prime, o(xy) = 19 is checked by
  (xy)^19 = 1 and xy ≠ 1.
- z = (xy)^(−1); Nielsen triple (x,y,z) satisfies xyz = 1.
- Simultaneous conjugacy: (x,y) ~ (c^(−1)xc, c^(−1)yc), c ∈ G.
- Aut(G)-action adds the outer diagonal automorphism δ: z ↦ 2z
  (2 a non-square mod 19).
- Hurwitz moves on triples (a,b,c), abc = 1:
  σ1(a,b,c) = (b,b^(−1)ab,c), σ2(a,b,c) = (a,c,c^(−1)bc), plus inverses.
  Pure-braid moves are σ1^2, σ2^2 (preserve the ordered (2,3,19) position
  pattern); full-braid moves are σ1^±1, σ2^±1 (move across the six
  position patterns).

## Result

In the committed degree-20 representation of G = PSL(2,19) (|G| = 3420):

1. **Count.** Exactly **6840** ordered pairs (x,y) satisfy
   o(x) = 2, o(y) = 3, o(xy) = 19. **All 6840 generate G**
   (0 non-generating), so the generation-filtered Nielsen number is
   **N = 6840**.
2. **Uniform fibers.** The 6840 pairs project uniformly: each of the 171
   involutions occurs in 40 pairs, each of the 380 elements of order 3 in
   18 pairs, each of the 360 elements of order 19 as xy in 19 pairs
   (40·171 = 18·380 = 19·360 = 6840).
3. **G-classes.** Simultaneous conjugacy partitions the 6840 pairs into
   **2 classes of 3420**, distinguished by z ∈ 19A vs 19B: z is not
   conjugate to z^(−1) in G (checked over all 3420 conjugators), and the
   two 19-classes have 180 + 180 = 360 elements.
4. **Aut-orbit.** The diagonal map δ has permutation order 18, normalizes G,
   satisfies δ² ∈ G, δ ∉ G, and ⟨G,δ⟩ has order 6840 = |PGL(2,19)|.
   Conjugation by δ preserves exact orders and sends the witness pair out
   of its G-class (sends z-class to its inverse). Hence the two G-classes
   fuse into a **single Aut(G)-orbit of all 6840 pairs**.
5. **Witness + BSGS.** First enumerated pair
   x = [5,8,19,15,18,0,10,16,1,12,6,14,9,17,11,3,7,13,4,2],
   y = [11,18,6,13,1,8,15,3,10,17,5,12,0,7,14,2,9,16,4,19]
   satisfies o(x) = 2, o(y) = 3, o(xy) = 19. Schreier–Sims chain with base
   [0,1,2] gives orbit lengths 20, 19, 9, so |⟨x,y⟩| = 20·19·9 = 3420.
6. **Pure braid.** ⟨σ1²,σ2²⟩ preserves the 6840-set (closure verified) and
   has **2 orbits of 3420**, coinciding with the 19A/19B split
   (diameter ≤ 17 from the witness in the logged BFS).
7. **Full braid.** On the extended Nielsen set E of all six position
   patterns (6840 triples per pattern, |E| = 41040, closure verified),
   ⟨σ1,σ2⟩ has **2 orbits of 20520**, each meeting every pattern in
   exactly 3420 triples; an explicit 2-move word joins a (2,3,19) triple
   to a (3,2,19) triple (diameter ≤ 31 in the logged BFS).
8. **Degree datum.** The reduced (unramified, simultaneous-conjugacy)
   Hurwitz covering degree is N/|G| = 6840/3420 = **2 reduced classes**.
9. **Generation theorem.** Every exact-order (2,3,19) pair generates:
   if H = ⟨x,y⟩ then 19 | |H|, so H lies in a maximal subgroup containing
   a 19-element. Maximal subgroups of PSL(2,19) (Dickson) are, up to
   conjugacy, two classes of A5 (60), D20 (20), D18 (18), and the Borel
   N(⟨z⟩) = 19⋊9 of order 171. A5 has order prime to 19; D20, D18 have
   rotation subgroups of order coprime to 19 so contain no 19-element;
   only the Borel contains 19-elements, and it has odd order 171, hence
   no involution. No proper subgroup contains both x and xy, so H = G.

## Proof / evidence

- G rebuilt from committed S, T by BFS closure: |G| = 3420; order
  distribution {1:1, 2:171, 3:380, 5:684, 9:1140, 10:684, 19:360},
  matching ATLAS centraliser orders (20, 9, 19 for 2A, 3A, 19A/B).
  Independently re-executed by the auditor.
- Exhaustive 171 × 380 = 64980 scan with exact-order checks gives 6840;
  auditor re-executed and confirmed 6840 plus uniform fibers.
- Generation by per-pair BFS closure (logged `gen_pairs.json`, 0
  non-generating) plus the maximal-subgroup proof above; auditor confirmed
  the group-theoretic conclusion and spot-checked closures.
- BSGS orbit–stabilizer chain (Schreier's lemma) giving 20·19·9 = 3420;
  auditor re-executed.
- G-orbit of witness by direct conjugation = 3420; z-class split verified
  (z0 ≁ z0^(−1); 180+180 disjoint 19-classes; pairs 3420/3420 = G-orbits);
  auditor re-executed.
- Delta checks (order 18, normalizes, δ² ∈ G, ⟨G,δ⟩ = 6840, swaps
  z-classes); auditor re-executed.
- Pure-braid closure on all 6840 pairs (0 violations) and BFS 2 × 3420;
  auditor re-executed. Full-braid pattern counts 6 × 6840 = 41040;
  auditor re-executed; the 2 × 20520 orbit decomposition and diameter
  bounds are from the logged scripts (`step6b.json`, `step6_full.py`).
- `artifacts/verify.py` rebuilds G from the two committed permutations
  and re-proves order, class sizes, 6840, generation, BSGS, 3420+3420
  split, |PGL| = 6840, and pure-braid 2 × 3420, printing VERIFY_OK
  (stdlib only, ~2–4 min).

## Limitations

- Counts are certified for the committed degree-20 representation
  (faithful by the order check |G| = 3420).
- Aut(G) = PGL(2,19) identification uses the exhibited ⟨G,δ⟩ of order
  6840 plus the known fact Out(PSL(2,19)) = C2 (ATLAS).
- The Hurwitz degree datum is the reduced (unramified) count N/|G| = 2.
- Full-braid 2 × 20520 and diameter bounds ≤ 17/≤ 31 rest on the logged
  BFS scripts; the independent verifier certifies the pure-braid core.
- Note: DRAFT §9 states the Borel order once as 57; the correct order is
  171 = 19 ⋊ 9 (used correctly elsewhere, e.g. |N| = 171). The generation
  conclusion (Borel is odd, hence no involution) is unaffected.
