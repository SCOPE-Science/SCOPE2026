# Second symbolic power of triangle-dumbbell cover ideals: universal gap witness, exact defect 5 at D_1, and no uniform defect 1

## Context

Let $D_l$ ($l \ge 1$) be the triangle dumbbell: left triangle $T_L=\{a_1,a_2,a_3\}$,
right triangle $T_R=\{b_1,b_2,b_3\}$, joined by the bridge path
$a_1=z_0,z_1,\dots,z_{l-1},z_l=b_1$ ($l=1$ is the single bridge edge $a_1b_1$;
$l \ge 2$ adds interior vertices $z_1,\dots,z_{l-1}$).
The admitted target claimed the uniform defect-1 decomposition
$J(D_l)^{(2)}=J(D_l)^2+(w_l)$ for all $l$, where $w_l$ is the full vertex
product. Complete minimal-2-cover enumeration undertaken to prove this instead
refuted it: four extra minimal gap generators already at $D_1$, and growing
families at every $l$. This record states the corrected picture.

## Definitions

Work over $S=k[V(D_l)]$ (any field; all ideals below are monomial, hence
characteristic-free). The **cover ideal** $J(D_l)$ is generated by all vertex
covers (the Alexander dual of the edge ideal). Its minimal primes are the edge
primes $P_e=(x_i,x_j)$, $e=ij \in E(D_l)$, so
$J(D_l)=\bigcap_{e} P_e$ and the second symbolic power is
$J(D_l)^{(2)}=\bigcap_{e} P_e^2$ (Herzog–Hibi–Trung).
A monomial $x^a$ lies in $J(D_l)^{(2)}$ iff $a$ is a **2-cover**:
$a_i+a_j \ge 2$ for every edge $ij$.
$J(D_l)^2$ is generated by pair-sums $c_1+c_2$ of vertex covers; restricting to
minimal covers suffices, and $x^a \in J(D_l)^2$ iff some minimal-cover
pair-sum $p \le a$ coordinatewise.
The **second symbolic defect** $\mathrm{sdefect}_2$ is the minimal number of
generators of $J^{(2)}/J^2$, which for these monomial ideals equals the number
of minimal 2-covers outside $J^2$ (Lemma E below).
Write $w_l$ for the product of all vertices of $D_l$
($\deg w_1=6$, $\deg w_l=l+5$), and with $(T_L \mid T_R)$ exponent notation
$m_1=(1,1,1 \mid 2,0,2)$, $m_2=(1,1,1 \mid 2,2,0)$,
$m_3=(2,0,2 \mid 1,1,1)$, $m_4=(2,2,0 \mid 1,1,1)$ (at $l \ge 2$ with $1$s on
all bridge-interior vertices, denoted $M_1,\dots,M_4$).

## Result

(A) **Universal gap witness.** For every $l \ge 1$,
$w_l \in J(D_l)^{(2)} \setminus J(D_l)^2$. This contains the preset fallback
claim as its $l=1$ case ($7$ edge-sums $=2$; degree gap $6 < 8=2\tau(D_1)$).

(B) **Exact defect at $D_1$; the defect-1 claim is false.**
$J(D_1)^{(2)}=J(D_1)^2+(w,m_1,m_2,m_3,m_4)$ minimally, i.e.
$\mathrm{sdefect}_2(J(D_1))=5$. In particular
$J(D_1)^{(2)} \ne J(D_1)^2+(w)$.

(C) **No uniform defect 1 at any bridge length.**
For every $l \ge 1$ the five patterns $w_l,M_1,\dots,M_4$ are minimal 2-covers
outside $J(D_l)^2$ (the $M_i$ have zero entries, so never multiples of $w_l$);
hence $\mathrm{sdefect}_2(J(D_l)) \ge 5$ for all $l$ and
$J(D_l)^{(2)} \ne J(D_l)^2+(w_l)$ at every bridge length.
Exact computed defects for $l=1,\dots,12$:
$5, 7, 13, 20, 29, 47, 70, 110, 171, 263, 411, 634$.

Interpretation. A single triangle has second defect $1$ (gap $111$). The
dumbbell mixes this one-sided gap with ordinary doubled patterns ($202/220$)
on the other side; the bridge kills the $(022 \mid 111)$-type products but
lets the four mixed witnesses through. Longer bridges add further interior
gap patterns.

## Proof / evidence

**Lemma 1 (covering number).** $\tau(D_l)=4+\lfloor(l-1)/2\rfloor$.
Any cover meets $T_L$ in $\ge 2$ and $T_R$ in $\ge 2$ vertices, and for
$l \ge 2$ its intersection with $Z=\{z_1,\dots,z_{l-1}\}$ covers the path on
$l-1$ vertices, contributing $\ge \lfloor(l-1)/2\rfloor$. The bound is sharp
via $C^*=\{a_1,a_2,b_1,b_2\}\cup\{z_{2j}\}$, verified to be a cover of that
size. Brute-force $\tau$ matches the formula for $l \le 12$.

**Lemma 2 (completeness).** If $a$ is a 2-cover with $a_i \ge 3$, then
$a-e_i$ is still a 2-cover; hence minimal 2-covers live in $\{0,1,2\}^n$ and
enumeration over $\{0,1,2\}^n$ is complete. Minimal 2-covers are exactly the
minimal monomial generators of $J^{(2)}$.

**Lemma 3 (defect identity).** $\mathrm{sdefect}$ equals the number of minimal
2-covers outside $J^2$: each such monomial must occur literally in any
monomial generating set over $J^2$ (a divisor in $J^{(2)}$ of a minimal
2-cover equals it), and together they generate $J^{(2)}$ over $J^2$ (graded
Nakayama / antichain argument; see `proof_notes.md` Lemma E).

*Theorem A.* Every edge-sum of $w_l$ is $1+1=2$, so $w_l \in J^{(2)}$.
$\deg w_l=l+5 < 2\tau(D_l)$ ($6+2k$ vs $2k+5$ for $l=2k$; $8+2k$ vs $2k+6$
for $l=2k+1$), and every nonzero monomial of $J^2$ has degree $\ge 2\tau$,
so $w_l \notin J^2$.

*Theorem B.* $D_1$ has $8$ minimal covers (all size $4$) and $15$ minimal
2-covers (complete $\{0,1,2\}^6$ enumeration). The full 15-row table
(`D1_min2_table.json`) gives an explicit minimal-cover pair-sum below each
of the $10$ monomials in $J^2$ and GAP status for the $5$ others
($w,m_1,\dots,m_4$). E.g. $m_1=(1,1,1,2,0,2)$: $b_2$-exponent $0$ forces
$b_1,b_3$ in both covers $c_1,c_2$, while $c_1+c_2 \le (1,1,1)$ on $T_L$
allows $\le 3$ slots but two triangle-covers need $\ge 4$; $m_1$ is not a
multiple of $w$ ($b_2=0$). By Lemma 3, sdefect $=5$ exactly.

*Theorem C.* The five patterns are 2-covers at every $l$ (triangle sums by
inspection; bridge-path values all in $\{1,2\}$), minimal (every positive
entry locked: triangle $1$s by neighboring $1$s, $2$s by adjacent $0$s,
interior $1$s by a $1$-valued path neighbor, endpoints by bridge/triangle
edges — full case analysis in `proof_notes.md`), and outside $J(D_l)^2$
(same $T_L/T_R$ slot-counting as above at every $l$; $w_l$ by Theorem A).
All three facts machine-verified for $l \le 12$. Exact defects $l \le 12$
by complete enumeration, cross-checked for $l \le 5$ by an independent
all-covers code path (CROSS_CHECK PASS).

## Limitations

Hand proofs of the lemmas and Theorems A–C are complete as stated;
computation corroborates to $l=12$ and is essential only for the finite
$D_1$ table and the census values. No closed formula for
$\mathrm{sdefect}_2(D_l)$ is claimed — only exact values to $l=12$, the
universal lower bound $5$, and the noted interior-pattern growth (verified
subfamily to $l \le 9$). Characteristic-independent throughout.
