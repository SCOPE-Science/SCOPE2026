Odd-OSASM reciprocal t-symmetry fails at order 5: X^O_5(1,t)=3t+7t^2+9t^3+9t^4+4t^5 has support 1..5, so any reciprocal monomial shift is forced to be 6, but c1=3 != c5=4 (and c2=7 != c4=9). Order 3 is reciprocal with shift 4. Exhaustive stdlib enumeration through order 5 reproduces the published ASM/DSASM/OSASM counts and the odd total 32.
