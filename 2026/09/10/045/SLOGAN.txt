Differential Galois group of the deformed-Airy operator y''-(z+1/z)y=0 over C(z) is SL2(C) by complete exact Kovacic elimination (no rational Riccati or symmetric-square solution; infinity irregular so not finite).
z=0 is proved regular singular (Katz slope 0, resonant exponents {0,1} with logarithmic second solution), so no Stokes matrices exist at 0 and the Stokes-at-zero witness is unsatisfiable; the only irregular singularity is infinity (Airy-type slope 3/2, exp parts +-2z^{3/2}/3).
