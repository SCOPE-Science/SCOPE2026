Exhaustive census of all 371,293 AGL-normalized monic degree-7 polynomials over F_13 finds no 9-direction function (histogram 8:13, 11:117, 12:1196, 13:369967). This rules out that degree-7 normalized family as a source of 22-point graph-type Rédei sets; it does not question the prior existence of size-22 Rédei-type minimal blocking sets reported by Kadoo (2010).
