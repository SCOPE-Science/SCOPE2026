Density-3/2 Y-rigidity: every tangent cone of a 2D integral varifold with bounded mean curvature in R^4 at 2-density 3/2 is the static triple half-plane up to O(4) rotation.
No branched stationary integral 2-cone of density 3/2 exists; nonzero points have density 1 (plane) or 3/2 (planar 120-degree triple), and the all-plane case is excluded by the 3π link-length obstruction.
