# Transcendental-free certificate and the −119 ruling obstruction on the diagonal quartic K3 Z_694

## Context

Diagonal quartic K3 surfaces over Q are the stated open frontier of the
Skorobogatov–Poonen–Ieronymou program on whether Brauer–Manin explains
weak-approximation and Hasse failures. General tools exist (Bright's
algorithm for the algebraic Brauer group; the Ieronymou–Skorobogatov–Zarhin
sufficient condition for the full Brauer group to be algebraic; odd-torsion
constancy results; asymptotic counts), but per-surface certificates deciding
the transcendental part and scoping the standard fibration vehicles are new
when executed for a named surface.

## Definitions

Let

Z = Z_694 : 2x_0^4 + 7x_1^4 + 8x_2^4 − 17x_3^4 = 0 ⊂ P^3_Q,

P_0 = [1:1:1:1] (note 2+7+8−17 = 0), and let D be the 3-adic residue disc
{x ∈ Z(Q_3) : x_i ∈ Z_3, x_i ≡ 1 mod 3 for all i} around P_0.

Write the surface in normalized form x_0^4 + a_1 x_1^4 + a_2 x_2^4 + a_3 x_3^4 = 0
with a_1 = 7/2, a_2 = 4, a_3 = −17/2. Let H_D ⊂ Q*/Q*^4 be the subgroup
generated by −1, 4, a_1, a_2, a_3. Let Br(Z) be the Brauer group and
Br_1(Z) = Ker(Br(Z) → Br(Z̄)) the algebraic part.

## Result

For Z_694:

1. Z is a smooth K3 with good reduction at 3, and P_0 ∈ Z(Q) has smooth
   F_3-residue [1:1:1:1].
2. Br(Z_694) = Br_1(Z_694): there are no transcendental Brauer classes.
   Precisely, {2,3,5} ∩ H_D = ∅, so ISZ Corollary 3.3 applies.
3. In normalized form, all six Bright pair-ratios a_i a_j/(a_k a_l) have
   squarefree part −119.
4. No ruling line of the tangent section at P_0 is defined over Q: the
   tangent-section conic has quadratic part (63/2)u² + 56uv + 40v² of
   discriminant −1904 = −16·119, a nonsquare in Q; the two rulings are
   conjugate over Q(√−119).
5. No line on Z defined over Q has coordinates in [−12,12]^4, and P_0 is
   the only Q-point with coordinates in that box (up to the projective
   sign identifications forced by fourth powers: 8 projective points).
6. D contains a logged 1-dimensional Hensel subfamily: x_1 = x_2 = 1,
   x_3 = 1 + 3t_0 lifts uniquely to x_0 ≡ 1 mod 3 (dF/dx_0(P_0) = 8 ≡ 2
   mod 3); 9 solutions logged mod 27.

## Proof / evidence

(1) Partials 4a_ix_i³ vanish jointly only at 0. Mod 3 the coefficients are
(2,1,2,1), all nonzero, so the Jacobian mod 3 vanishes only at 0: smooth
mod 3, good reduction. 2+7+8−17 = 0 gives P_0.
Replay: `python3 output/artifacts/step1.py` → STEP1_OK.

(2) The generators −1, 4, 7/2, 4, −17/2 are supported on primes {2,7,17}
plus sign, so 3 and 5 cannot lie in H_D by prime support. Writing an
element as (−1)^s 2^a 7^b 17^c, the 17-exponent forces the −17/2-exponent
to 0 mod 4 and the 7-exponent forces the 7/2-exponent to 0 mod 4, leaving
2(e_4+e_2) = 1 mod 4, impossible; hence 2 ∉ H_D. By
Ieronymou–Skorobogatov–Zarhin, *On the Brauer group of diagonal quartic
surfaces*, Cor. 3.3 (Ieronymou Thesis Thm. 5.2 for the 2-primary part and
Thm. 3.2 for odd ℓ ≥ 7, ℓ = 3, 5), {2,3,5} ∩ H_D = ∅ implies
Br(Z_694) = Br_1(Z_694). Replay: `hd_check.py` → HD_CHECK_OK.

(3) Direct squarefree-part computation of the six ratios; every ratio
equals −119 times a square in Q*. Replay: `br1_algebra.py` (six −119 rows).

(4) Tangent plane at P_0: 2a + 7b + 8c − 17d = 0. Parametrize
b = s, c = t, d = 1, a = (17 − 7s − 8t)/2; substituting in the cone and
translating u = s−1, v = t−1 leaves quadratic part
(63/2)u² + 56uv + 40v² of discriminant
56² − 4·(63/2)·40 = −1904 = −16·119, nonsquare in Q. Hence the two
tangent-section lines are defined over Q(√−119), not Q. Any Q-line through
P_0 would give a Q-point of this tangent cone. Replay:
`fibration_attempt.py`.

(5) A line spanned by p, q ∈ Q⁴ lies on Z iff Σa_ip_i^{4−e}q_i^e = 0 for
e = 0,…,4. Exhaustion over [−12,12]^4 (192 raw / 8 projective points, all
the P_0 sign orbit) finds no pair satisfying all five conditions.
Replay: `qline_search.py`, `qline_search12.py`.

(6) dF/dx_0(P_0) = 8 is a 3-adic unit; Newton lift digit-by-digit base 3
gives the unique x_0 ≡ 1 mod 3 for each x_3 = 1 + 3t_0; verified mod 27.
Replay: `hensel_family.py` → HENSEL_FAMILY_OK with 9 rows.

## Limitations

- No computation of Br_1(Z)/Br_0, no Hilbert-symbol invariant table, no
  density/weak-approximation theorem in D. Any Brauer obstruction on this
  surface, if present, is algebraic (2-primary), by (2).
- The ruling refutation covers lines through P_0 and boxed lines
  generally; it does not rule out Q-conics or non-ruling genus-1
  fibrations through D.
- Odd-torsion constancy results (Ieronymou 2022) are context, not reproved.

## Reproducibility

All claims replay with stdlib Python (+ sympy for the discriminant and
Galois-degree context scripts) via the seven scripts in
`output/artifacts/`. The boxed census enumerates all 25^4 integer vectors
exactly.
