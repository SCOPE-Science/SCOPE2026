Every Johnson-stable rank-5 paving matroid on n>=12 points contains a U_{2,5} minor: contracting any 3-set leaves a matching pair-graph with an independent 5-set. Hence the claimed infinite cyclic U_{2,5}-free sparse-paving family (all large primes n==1 mod 20) cannot exist. Sharp at n>=12; illustrated by replayable checks at n=17 and n=41.
