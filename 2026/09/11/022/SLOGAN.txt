On a general genus-6 curve with general M in Pic^10, no stable rank-2 degree-10 bundle with det M and h0>=4 has a line quotient of degree <=6; stability kills e<=5 and W-incidence images of dimension <=4<6 kill e=6. Any counterexample to empty B(2,M,4) at (2,10,4) must have minimal quotient degree at least 7; the degree-7 stratum can dominate and full emptiness remains open.
