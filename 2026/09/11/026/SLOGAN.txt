No ideal-simple skew brace of order p^2q exists for distinct odd primes p,q: a unique characteristic Sylow subgroup (order q if q>p, order p^2 if q<p) is always a proper ideal, so the rigidity clause holds vacuously and no odd boundary simple with q dividing p+1 can exist; oddness, not q∤p+1, is the essential hypothesis (order 12 is the p=2 exception with Sylow non-uniqueness).
