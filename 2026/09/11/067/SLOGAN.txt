No 4-atom degree-5 cubature exists for 2D fractional Brownian motion at H=3/8; stronger: no 5-atom degree-4 cubature for any H>1/4. Shuffle symmetrization reduces path-space cubature to Gaussian quadrature on the endpoint N(0,I_2), where dim P_2=6 forces a vanishing quadric and a p-squared contradiction, so at least 6 atoms are necessary.
