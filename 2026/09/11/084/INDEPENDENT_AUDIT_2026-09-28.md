## Correctness

Each E_p has mass 4/(p+1). For distinct primes, common Fourier frequencies occur only at multiples of pq; the one-zero coordinate classes contribute at most (8/3)psi(p)psi(q)/(pq) in total and the both-nonzero class at most 1/(9p^2q^2). These errors are summable on every tail. Since sum_p 4/(p+1) diverges, Chung-Erdos gives tail-union measure tending to 1, and intersecting all tail unions gives full limsup measure.
