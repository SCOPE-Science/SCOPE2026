The explicit 4-polytope stability bound d_H <= 20*W_2^{1/4} is false: P=[-1,1]^4 and Q=[-1.2,1.2]x[-0.9,0.9]^3 are centroidal 8-facet boxes in the admitted class with identical normalized cone-volume measures (W_2=0) but optimal-translation Hausdorff distance 0.2. The normalized cone-volume map is not injective on centered boxes, so no finite-constant Wasserstein modulus holds without extra hypotheses.
