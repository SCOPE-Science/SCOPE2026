# An ℵ1-presentable but not finitely presentable one-object category from independent path swaps

## Setup

Let `G` be the graph with one vertex and three loops `a,b,c`, and let `M={a,b,c}*` be its free monoid, viewed as the endomorphism monoid of the free one-object category. For every integer `n>=2` put

`p_n = a^n b c^n`,  `q_n = c^n b a^n`.

Let `S` be the equivalence relation on the set of words generated by the pairs `(p_n,q_n)` without closure under contexts, and let `≡` be the monoid congruence generated by the same pairs. Define

`Q = M/≡ = <a,b,c | a^n b c^n = c^n b a^n, n>=2>`.

For `N>=2`, let `Q_N` be the quotient obtained by imposing every displayed relation except the `N`-th one.

## Result

1. `Q` is nontrivial and infinite: every relation preserves the Parikh vector, so the three exponent counts descend to `Q`.
2. The comparison from the plain Graph-level quotient `M/S` to the underlying graph of `Q` is not injective. For `p_2=aabcc` and `q_2=ccbaa`, the words `aaabcc=a p_2` and `accbaa=a q_2` lie in distinct singleton `S`-classes but become equal in `Q` by left whiskering.
3. Every relation is independent of all the others. For each `N>=2` there is a homomorphism `phi_N:Q_N -> End(X_N)`, with `|X_N|=N+3`, that separates `p_N` from `q_N`.
4. Consequently `Q` is not finitely presentable as a monoid and is not finitely presentable as an object of `Cat`. Since `Q` is a countable category, it is ℵ1-presentable in `Cat`. Thus its least regular presentability cardinal is ℵ1.

## Explicit separator

Take

`X_N={s_0,x_1,...,x_N,y_N,z}`

and use right actions. Let `B` be the constant map to `s_0`. Define `A` by

- `s_0 A=x_1`, `x_i A=x_{i+1}` for `1<=i<N`;
- `x_N A=y_N A=z A=z`.

Define `C` by

- `s_0 C=x_1`, `x_i C=x_{i+1}` for `1<=i<=N-2`;
- `x_{N-1} C=y_N`;
- `x_N C=y_N C=z C=z`.

Set `phi_N(a)=A`, `phi_N(b)=B`, `phi_N(c)=C`. Because `B` is constant,

`phi_N(p_n)=const_{s_0 C^n}`,  `phi_N(q_n)=const_{s_0 A^n}`.

The two orbits agree at `x_n` for `n<N`, disagree as `y_N` versus `x_N` at `n=N`, and are both absorbed at `z` for `n>N`. Hence `phi_N(p_n)=phi_N(q_n)` for every `n≠N`, but `phi_N(p_N)≠phi_N(q_N)`. Therefore `phi_N` factors through `Q_N` and separates the omitted relation.

## Non-finite presentability

If a finite subset of the displayed relations generated the full congruence, choosing `N` outside that subset would contradict the separator above. More generally, if `Q` had any finite monoid presentation on a finite generating set, standard Tietze/equational compactness reduces that finite presentation to consequences of finitely many of the displayed relations, again contradicting relation independence.

The inclusion of one-object categories (`Mon`) into `Cat` preserves filtered colimits, and for one-object targets the corresponding hom-sets are exactly monoid homomorphisms. Thus finite presentability of `Q` in `Cat` would imply finite presentability of its endomorphism monoid, which has just been ruled out.

Finally, `M` is countable, so `Q` has one object and at most countably many morphisms. In the locally finitely presentable category `Cat`, any countable small category is ℵ1-presentable: a functor out of it involves only countably many object/morphism choices and equations, all of which factor through one stage of an ℵ1-filtered colimit. Since `Q` is not finitely (ℵ0-)presentable, the least regular presentability cardinal is ℵ1.

## Reproducibility

Run `python3 artifacts/verify_separation.py`. The script checks the finite-state separators for a range of `N`; the orbit argument above is the all-`N` proof. The independent audit also replayed the construction for every `N=2,...,100`.

## Limitations

No claim is made about decidability of the word problem of `Q`, coherence for unrelated families, or a general criterion for finite presentability. The novelty claim is only for this explicit relation family and separator construction; the locally-presentable-category background is standard.
