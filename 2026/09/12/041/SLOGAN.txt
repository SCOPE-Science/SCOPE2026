For f_n = a^n b a^n - b^{n+1} in Q<a,b>, the integral representation a -> 4-cycle shift, b -> diag(1,0,1,0) kills exactly the even f_n, so f_3 is not in the two-sided ideal (f_2) and (f_2) is strictly smaller than the full ideal. L-cycle plus diagonal-projector representations vanish exactly when 2n = 0 mod L and the support is n-invariant, so none separates f_2,f_3 from any later f_N.
