The J_(3,2,1,1,1,1) multiwedge of the octahedral 2-sphere is the 9-vertex 5-sphere with minimal nonfaces {0,1},{2,3},{4,5,6,7,8}, i.e. the join of a square 4-cycle with a 4-simplex boundary. Its moment-angle manifold is S^3 x S^3 x S^9 with cohomology the exterior algebra on generators of degrees 3,3,9; its Sullivan minimal model has zero differential, so it is formal with all 205 defined triple Massey products vanishing in their indeterminacy quotients.
