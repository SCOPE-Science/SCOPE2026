No RS-pullback of the Boalch-Klein 7-branch Painlevé VI solution y_K (theta (2,2,2,4)/7) can come from a finite-monodromy Schwarz seed in any degree: its explicit monodromy has a noncommuting order-7 pair that no cyclic, dihedral, tetrahedral, octahedral, or icosahedral seed admits. Any future RS hunt for y_K may therefore ignore all finite seeds and search infinite-monodromy hypergeometric seeds only.
