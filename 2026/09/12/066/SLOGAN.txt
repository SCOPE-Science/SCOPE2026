In the product of two cyclic groups of order 9, an explicit 16-point set is extension-maximal 9-sum-free, while an explicit 20-point set has exactly 1832 pairwise-intersecting 9-sums and is extension-maximal without two disjoint 9-sums; these give lower bounds 17 for the ordinary Harborth constant and 21 for the disjoint-pair threshold.
