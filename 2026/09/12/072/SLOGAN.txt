Kummer operator xY''+(1/2-x)Y'-tY over C(t,x) at transcendental t has classical Galois group SL2 and full PPV group SL2 defined by det=1 alone: no rational Riccati, symmetric-square, or Schlesinger Lax-matrix solutions, hence non-isomonodromic across x=0 regular and x=infinity rank-one irregular singularities. Integer fibers are Borel Liouvillian and half-integer fibers carry Liouvillian solutions, with explicit witnesses at t=0,-1/2,1/2,1,-1.
