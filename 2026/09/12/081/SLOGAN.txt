Kaad-Kyed t-deformed Dirac on quantum SU(2) has no simple 1-2-3 dimension spectrum for t!=1: at (t,q)=(1/2,1/2), b=1, one-dimensional blocks V^n_{i0} are D-eigenvectors with eigenvalues converging to -2/3 (multiplicity n+1), so Tr(|D|^{-z}) is infinite for every z and no meromorphic extension exists.
