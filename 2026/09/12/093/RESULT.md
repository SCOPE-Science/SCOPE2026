# Derived classification of quantum polynomial planes: disproof of the permutation/inversion biconditional

## Context

Let $k$ be algebraically closed of characteristic $0$. For
$q=(q_{12},q_{13},q_{23})\in(k^{\*})^{3}$ let

$$A_q=k\langle x,y,z\rangle/(yx-q_{12}xy,\ zx-q_{13}xz,\ zy-q_{23}yz).$$

For generic $q$ (every $q_{ij}\ne 1$, no $q_{ij}$ a root of unity) $A_q$ is a
quadratic $3$-dimensional Artin-Schelter regular algebra: Koszul, global
dimension $3$, Hilbert series $(1-t)^{-3}$. Its noncommutative projective
plane is $\mathrm{qgr}\,A_q=\mathrm{grmod}\,A_q/\mathrm{tors}$ and the
investigated invariant is $D^b(\mathrm{qgr}\,A_q)$ up to triangulated
equivalence. The admitted target asked whether

$$D^b(\mathrm{qgr}\,A_q)\simeq D^b(\mathrm{qgr}\,A_{q'})\iff q'\text{ from }q
\text{ by variable permutation and }q_{ij}\mapsto q_{ij}^{\pm1},$$

with a proof/disproof by explicit witness pairs.

## Definitions

- Generic: all $q_{ij}\ne 1$ and no $q_{ij}$ a root of unity.
- Diagonal Zhang twist: for diagonal automorphism $\phi(x)=ax$,
  $\phi(y)=by$, $\phi(z)=cz$, twisting system $\tau_n=\phi^n$, product
  $u\star v=u\,\tau_m(v)$ for $u$ of degree $m$.
- Claimed finite relation: group generated by permuting generators and
  independent inversions $q_{ij}\mapsto q_{ij}^{-1}$; orbit of at most
  $6\cdot 2^3=48$ triples whose every coordinate is some $q_{ij}^{\pm1}$.

## Result

The stated biconditional is FALSE. For

$$q=(2,3,5),\qquad q'=(4,6,5)$$

over any algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, both triples are
generic, $D^b(\mathrm{qgr}\,A_q)$ is triangulated-equivalent to
$D^b(\mathrm{qgr}\,A_{q'})$ via an explicit diagonal Zhang-twist
graded-Morita witness with scales $(a,b,c)=(2,1,1)$, yet $q'$ is not obtained
from $q$ by any permutation of variables combined with independent inversions
$q_{ij}\mapsto q_{ij}^{\pm1}$. Hence the "only if" direction, and the
biconditional, is disproved.

## Proof / Evidence

1. Twist formula. For degree-one generators $x_i\star x_j=c_jx_ix_j$ with
$(c_1,c_2,c_3)=(a,b,c)$. Since $x_jx_i=q_{ij}x_ix_j$ for $j>i$,
$x_j\star x_i=c_iq_{ij}x_ix_j=(c_i/c_j)q_{ij}(x_i\star x_j)$. Thus
$A_q^\tau\cong A_{q'}$ with $q_{12}'=(a/b)q_{12}$,
$q_{13}'=(a/c)q_{13}$, $q_{23}'=(b/c)q_{23}$. The twist invariant
$J=q_{12}q_{23}/q_{13}$ is preserved.
2. Instantiation. $(a,b,c)=(2,1,1)$ sends $(2,3,5)$ to $(4,6,5)$:
$2\cdot2=4$, $2\cdot3=6$, $1\cdot5=5$, with $J=10/3$ on both sides.
Twisted-relation residuals vanish.
3. Equivalence. By Zhang (1996), $A$ and $A^\tau$ have equivalent graded
module categories; the functor preserves torsion, so Artin-Zhang descent
gives $\mathrm{QGr}\simeq\mathrm{QGr}$,
$\mathrm{qgr}\,A_q\simeq\mathrm{qgr}\,A_{q'}$, hence triangulated
$D^b(\mathrm{qgr}\,A_q)\simeq D^b(\mathrm{qgr}\,A_{q'})$.
4. Genericity. Entries are integers $>1$ in $\mathbb{Q}\subset k$; in
characteristic $0$ no such integer is $1$ or a root of unity. Both algebras
satisfy the triangle-of-lines point-scheme hypothesis.
5. Orbit exclusion. Every coordinate of any permutation/inversion image of
$(2,3,5)$ lies in $\{2^{\pm1},3^{\pm1},5^{\pm1}\}$, but $q_{12}'=4$ is outside
this set in characteristic $0$. Exhaustive enumeration of all $48$
permuted/inverted triples confirms $(4,6,5)$ is absent.
Machine check `output/artifacts/verify_pair.py` passes all steps.

The true diagonal-twist orbit is $1$-dimensional with invariant $J$; any
classification must account for this continuous moduli, not only finite data.

## Limitations

This disproves the "only if" direction and hence the biconditional; it does
not give a replacement full classification of $D^b(\mathrm{qgr}\,A_q)$ by
$q$-parameters. Zhang's theorem and Artin-Zhang descent are cited as black
boxes. Genericity is checked over $\mathbb{Q}$, valid in every
characteristic-zero field. The "if" direction for permutation/inversion pairs
is standard and unaffected.

## Reproducibility

Run `python3 output/artifacts/verify_pair.py` (uses only `fractions` and

and exhaustive $48$-orbit exclusion, and twisted-relation residuals.
