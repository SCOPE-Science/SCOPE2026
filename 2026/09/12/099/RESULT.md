# S-class 3-vanishing across three cyclotomic layers of Q(sqrt(-83)) at p=3: a certified obstruction to lambda_fine = 3

## Context

Let K = Q(sqrt(-83)), p = 3, and K_infty/K the cyclotomic Z_3-extension with layers K = K_0 ⊂ K_1 ⊂ K_2 ⊂ ... . The admitted target asked whether the fine Iwasawa module X_fine(K) — the Galois group over K_infty of the maximal unramified abelian pro-3 extension of K_infty in which every prime above 3 splits completely (equivalently the strict fine 3-class tower module) — satisfies (mu_fine, lambda_fine) = (0, 3). The lambda half of such a claim requires persistent 3-primary mass in the finite-level fine quotients. This record reports what was established en route: the exact structure of those quotients at layers 0, 1, and 2.

## Definitions

Let S_n be the set of primes of K_n above 3. The S-ideal class group (fine level-quotient) is Cl^{S_n}(K_n) = Cl(K_n) / <[P] : P in S_n>, the quotient of the ordinary ideal class group by the subgroup generated by the decomposition primes above 3. Write e^S_n for the exponent of 3 in |Cl^{S_n}(K_n)|, i.e. the 3-primary S-class group is trivial iff e^S_n = 0. The logarithmic 3-class group is Jaulent's bnflog(-, 3) invariant.

## Result (headline)

For K = Q(sqrt(-83)) at p = 3 with S the primes above 3:

- Cl(K) ≅ C_3. Both primes above 3 are nonprincipal of exact order 3 and each generates Cl(K). Hence the 3-part of Cl^S_0 is trivial: e^S_0 = 0. Certified unconditionally (PARI bnfcertify = 1).
- Cl(K_1) ≅ C_63 ≅ C_9 × C_7. Both primes above 3 (totally ramified, e = 3, f = 1 over K) have exact order 9, and each generates the full 3-Sylow C_9. The order-3 class group of K capitulates fully in K_1. Hence the 3-part of Cl^S_1 is trivial: e^S_1 = 0. Certified unconditionally (bnfcertify = 1, independent bnr confirmation of order 63).
- Conditionally on GRH (uncertified bnfinit only): Cl(K_2) is cyclic of order 10774323 = 3^3 · 7 · 109 · 523, and both primes above 3 have exact order 27, each generating the full 3-Sylow C_27. Hence e^S_2 = 0 under GRH.
- The logarithmic 3-class groups bnflog(-, 3) are trivial at K, K_1 (certified) and K_2 (GRH-conditional).

Corollary (obstruction, marked as inference): a fine invariant lambda_fine = 3 requires persistent 3-primary growth in the fine level-quotients. The data show the opposite — the decomposition primes already generate the entire 3-primary part at every computed layer (C_3/C_9 ladder certified, C_27 under GRH) — structurally obstructing (mu_fine, lambda_fine) = (0, 3) and redirecting future work toward lambda_fine = 0. The step from this obstruction to a certified exact Iwasawa pair via a fine control theorem with local error terms is NOT closed here and is explicitly excluded.

## Proof / evidence

Base field: K = bnfinit(x^2+83), K.clgp = [3,[3]], K.no = 3. idealprimedec(K,3) gives two primes each with e = f = 1 (3 splits; Kronecker(-83,3) = +1). bnfisprincipal coordinates of the two primes are [1] and [2] mod 3: both exact order 3, generators. Hence Cl^S_0 = C_3/<generator> = 1.

First layer: K_1 is the degree-6 compositum of K with the unique cubic subfield of Q(zeta_9) (minimal polynomial x^3-3x+1, discriminant 81); reduced absolute polynomial y^6+243y^4+2y^3+20676y^2-504y+613869. bnfinit with flag 1 plus bnfcertify = 1 gives K1.clgp = [63,[63]], K1.no = 63 = 3^2 · 7; bnrinit(K1,1) independently confirms 63. idealprimedec(K1,3) gives two primes with e = 3, f = 1. Their bnfisprincipal coordinates are [7] and [56] mod 63: 3·7 = 21, 9·7 = 0; 3·56 = 42, 9·56 = 0, so each has exact order 63/gcd(63,7) = 9. Since 56 = -7 mod 63, <7> = <56> is the unique order-9 subgroup, i.e. the full 3-Sylow. The S-quotient C_63/<order-9> has order 7, so its 3-part is trivial. Capitulation: the lift 7·O_K1 + (em(a)+2) of a nonprincipal degree-1 prime above 7 is the unit ideal. Order-63 witnesses at 17/37/109-layer primes (e.g. (17,1): x9 = 45, x7 = 49, x21 = 21, x63 = 0) confirm cyclicity. bnflog(K1,3) = trivial.

Second layer (GRH-conditional): K_2 is the degree-18 compositum with the degree-9 subfield of Q(zeta_27); bnfinit succeeds with K2.no = 10774323 = 3^3 · 7 · 109 · 523, K2.cyc = [10774323] (cyclic). Prime classes above 3 have coords [9178127] and [1596196]; gcd(10774323, 9178127) = gcd(10774323, 1596196) = 399049 = 7 · 109 · 523, so each has exact order 10774323/399049 = 27 = full 3-Sylow. 3-part projections are units mod 27 (20 and 7); discrete-log self-checks pass (3m, 9m match PARI powering coords; 27m = 0). bnflog(K2,3) = trivial. bnfcertify(K2) did not finish in a 570 s wall window, so all K_2 statements are GRH-conditional.

## Limitations

K_2 statements are GRH-conditional only. The finding is a finite-level obstruction, not a proof of any exact Iwasawa pair: the control-theoretic step (norm maps, local unit images, decomposition subgroups, T-action/characteristic ideal) is open. The mu_fine = 0 half is expected from general abelian-base theorems but not re-proved. PARI certification is computational rather than hand-checked.
