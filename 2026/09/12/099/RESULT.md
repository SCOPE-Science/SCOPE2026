# Finite-level S-class 3-vanishing through the first three cyclotomic layers of Q(sqrt(-83)) at p=3

## Context

Let K = Q(sqrt(-83)), p = 3, and K_infinity/K be the cyclotomic Z_3-extension with layers K_n. For S_n the primes of K_n above 3, define the S-ideal class group

Cl^{S_n}(K_n) = Cl(K_n) / < [P] : P in S_n >.

This record reports exact finite-level class-group and decomposition-prime data obtained while investigating a fine-Iwasawa question. It makes **no deduction of the infinite-level fine Iwasawa lambda invariant from the first three layers**.

## Result

- **Layer K_0 = K (unconditional):** Cl(K) is cyclic of order 3. The two primes above 3 are nonprincipal classes of exact order 3 and each generates Cl(K). Hence the 3-primary part of Cl^{S_0}(K) is trivial. PARI `bnfcertify` returned 1. The logarithmic 3-class group reported by `bnflog(K,3)` is trivial.
- **Layer K_1 (unconditional):** Cl(K_1) is cyclic of order 63. The two primes above 3 have class coordinates 7 and 56 modulo 63, hence each has exact order 9 and generates the full 3-Sylow subgroup. Consequently the 3-primary part of Cl^{S_1}(K_1) is trivial. PARI `bnfcertify` returned 1, and `bnflog(K_1,3)` is trivial.
- **Layer K_2 (GRH-conditional / uncertified BNF):** the archived PARI `bnfinit` output gives a cyclic class group of order 10,774,323 = 3^3 * 7 * 109 * 523. The two primes above 3 have coordinates 9,178,127 and 1,596,196; each has gcd 399,049 with the class-group order and therefore exact order 27, the full 3-Sylow. Thus the 3-primary part of Cl^{S_2}(K_2) is trivial conditional on that BNF computation. `bnflog(K_2,3)` is likewise reported trivial under the same conditional status. `bnfcertify(K_2)` did not finish in the recorded wall-time window.

## Verification

The archived transcript `artifacts/verification_log.txt` records the PARI class groups, prime-class coordinates, certification flags, capitulation check, and logarithmic-class outputs. The group-order consequences are elementary: in C_63, the classes 7 and 56 have order 9; in C_10774323, the two reported coordinates have gcd 399049 with the group order, so they have order 27. LMFDB independently confirms that Q(sqrt(-83)) has class number 3.

## Interpretation and scope

These calculations establish a **finite-level vanishing pattern only**. Three initial vanishing S-class 3-parts do not by themselves determine, bound, or obstruct an eventual Iwasawa lambda invariant. Any infinite-level conclusion requires an appropriate control/asymptotic theorem and verification of its hypotheses and local error terms; that step is not supplied here.

## Auxiliary correction

The historical transcript incorrectly inferred capitulation from the ideal generated by 7 and an embedded square root plus 2. That ideal is the unit ideal, not a lift of a prime above 7: the base norm of the latter generator is 87, coprime to 7. In contrast, for the genuine base prime generated by 7 and the square root minus 1, the lifted ideal has norm 343 and independently recomputed class coordinate 42 modulo 63. Thus the claimed capitulation is false and has been removed. The six prime-above-3 coordinates and all three finite S-class conclusions remain exactly reproduced. The original transcript is retained as historical evidence, not endorsed as correct on this auxiliary point.
