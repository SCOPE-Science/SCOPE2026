The (⇒) Zoll–Crofton claim on projective RP^2 is false: the Busemann metric from even density f=1+0.3(ξ_3^2−1/3) is smooth, reversible, projective and Zoll with common prime line length Λ=4π, yet its Crofton density is nonconstant over [0.9,1.2]. The (⇐) direction holds by the Crofton count; exact fiber m=A+Bcos2α, convexity and 8π circle lengths are machine-checked.
