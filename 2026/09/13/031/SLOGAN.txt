For the Tate duplication Lattes map of degree 4 from [2] on E=C5*/5^Z, the Berkovich Julia set is exactly the skeleton segment Sigma and the Fatou set is its complement of preperiodic indifferent disks, with infinity in a fixed indifferent disk of multiplier 4. Every Fatou disk is preperiodic and no Berkovich disk wanders, deciding the q=5 rank-one case outside the known rank>=2 wandering construction.
