## Correctness — PASS

The order-3 character identity gives normalized Fourier coefficients O(p^{-1/2}), and Erdős-Turán yields the O(log p/sqrt(p)) upper bound. For p congruent to 7 modulo 12 the cubic-residue subgroup is symmetric under negation, so the three Gaussian periods are real. If 2 is a cubic non-residue then the k=1 and k=2 coefficients correspond to two distinct periods; the zero-sum and square-sum identities force one to have size at least a fixed multiple of sqrt(p). Chebotarev in Q(zeta_12,cuberoot(2)) supplies infinitely many such primes.

The literature inspected develops order-3 Gauss sums and counting/distribution properties of cubic residues, but no source located states the two-sided star-discrepancy rate for the normalized cubic-residue point set, and Resultary returned only the same record as an exact match.

## Value — PASS

The theorem pins the natural square-root discrepancy scale from both sides for a canonical multiplicative subgroup, with a log-free infinitely-often lower bound. That is a motivated exact asymptotic-order statement in uniform distribution.
