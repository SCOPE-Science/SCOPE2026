The fixed-s 120-degree law is false: the 120-degree trihedral cone has strictly positive fractional mean curvature >=0.17 uniformly for s in [3/4,1), while cone stationarity forces zero curvature, so it is never a stationary fractional double-bubble blow-up cone.
Exact cylinder reduction and half-plane subtraction give H(120deg,s)>0 with an explicit inscribed-disc bound; numerics (~2.85 down to ~1.97 in 3D) confirm a large margin vanishing only at 180 degrees.
