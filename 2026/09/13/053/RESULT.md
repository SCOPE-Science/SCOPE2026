# Invariant nondegeneracy, no local bifurcation, and Grushin degeneracy for the S1-symmetric Rossi-Yamabe problem

## Context

The target was a two-sided dichotomy for Rossi spheres $(S^3,J_t)$ with real $|t|<1$,
$Z_{1,t}=Z_1+t\bar Z_1$: either symmetry-breaking multiplicity of $S^1$-invariant
unit-volume constant-Webster-curvature forms for $t_k\to 0$, or local uniqueness up
to $J_t$-automorphism. The global dichotomy remains open. The completed headline is
the emergent local structural theorem below, which rules out reference-branch
bifurcation and isolates the exact obstruction to the global step.

## Definitions

Let $S^3=\{|z_1|^2+|z_2|^2=1\}$, $Z_1=\bar z_2\partial_{z_1}-\bar z_1\partial_{z_2}$,
$T$ the Reeb field, $\theta_0$ the standard contact form normalized so Webster
curvature $R_0=4$ and linearized operator $L_0=-4\Delta_b-2R_0$ on mean-zero functions.
$J_t$ is the Rossi CR structure generated by $Z_{1,t}$. Unit-volume forms are
$\theta=u^2\theta_0$; $F_t(u)=0$ denotes constant-Webster-curvature plus volume
constraint. Let $G\simeq S^1$ act anti-diagonally,
$(z_1,z_2)\mapsto(e^{is}z_1,e^{-is}z_2)$, with generator $A$.
$X=H^2_{G,0}$, $Y=L^2_{G,0}$ are $G$-invariant mean-zero spaces.

## Result

(i) The Hopf/diagonal $S^1$ does not preserve $J_t$ for $t\ne 0$; the anti-diagonal
$G$ does. Moreover $\theta_0(A)=|z_1|^2-|z_2|^2$, vanishing exactly on the Clifford
torus $\{|z_1|=|z_2|\}$. Hence $G$-orbits are Legendrian there and the quotient of
$\Delta_{b,t}$ is Grushin-degenerate along the equator image, not uniformly elliptic
on a smooth $S^2$.

(ii) At $t=0$ the volume-normalized constant solution is nondegenerate in the
$G$-invariant class: $L_0\ge 8>0$ on $G$-invariant mean-zero functions. In particular
$\ker L_0=\mathcal H_{1,0}\oplus\mathcal H_{0,1}$ contains no nonzero $G$-invariant
function. The minimum $8$ is exact.

(iii) Persistence/tubular uniqueness: for some explicit $\delta_0>0$ (e.g.
$\delta_0=1/10$, positivity persisting for $|t|<1/2$) there is an $H^2$-tubular
neighborhood $\mathcal U$ of the constant such that for $|t|<\delta_0$ the constant
is the unique $G$-invariant unit-volume constant-Webster-curvature form in
$\mathcal U$. Hence no sequence of distinct $G$-invariant solutions converges to the
constant, and no Crandall-Rabinowitz bifurcation from the reference branch
accumulates at $t=0$. Any multiplicity sequence $t_k\to 0$ must stay
$\ge c_0>0$ away in $L^2(\theta_0)$.

## Proof / evidence

(i) Under Hopf flow $\Phi_s(z)=e^{is}z$, $Z_1\mapsto e^{-2is}Z_1$,
$\bar Z_1\mapsto e^{+2is}\bar Z_1$, so
$Z_1+t\bar Z_1\mapsto e^{-2is}(Z_1+te^{4is}\bar Z_1)$, not a multiple unless
$e^{4is}=1$. Under $G$ the phases cancel and $Z_1,\bar Z_1$ are fixed, so $J_t$ and
$\theta_0$ are preserved. Direct computation
$\theta_0(A)=|z_1|^2-|z_2|^2$ gives $+1$ at $(1,0)$, $-1$ at $(0,1)$, $0$ on the
Clifford torus; transverse near poles, tangent on torus. Uniformly elliptic
$Moser/Schauder$ on the quotient cannot be quoted.

(ii) Folland: $-\Delta_b$ has eigenvalue $\lambda_{p,q}=2(p+q+2pq)$ on
$\mathcal H_{p,q}$. $\ker L_0$ is $\lambda=2$, i.e. $(1,0),(0,1)$.
Monomial weight under $G$ is $w=(a-c)-(b-d)$ for
$z_1^az_2^b\bar z_1^c\bar z_2^d$. Bidegree $(1,0)$ has weights $+1,-1$; $(0,1)$ has
$-1,+1$: no zero weight. Exhaustion over $0\le p,q\le 5$ plus abstract bound shows
the lowest invariant sectors are $(0,2),(2,0)$ with $\lambda=4$, so on invariants
$L_0=4\lambda-8\ge 8$. Analytic $O(|t|)$ perturbation of $(\Delta_{b,t},R_t)$
preserves invertibility for $|t|<1/2$.

(iii) Write $F_t(c+z)=L_tz+N(z)+E(t)$ with $L_t=L_0+O(t)$ invertible,
$\|L_0^{-1}\|\le 1/8$, $N$ quadratic/cubic locally Lipschitz ($H^2(S^3)$ algebra),
$E(t)=O(|t|)$. Quantitative IFT (Newton-Kantorovich) gives radius $r_0\sim 1/6$
(conservative $0.05$ used) and trapping $\|u-c\|_{H^2}\le K|t|$. Uses only
subelliptic $H^2$ theory on $S^3$ (Folland-Stein), unaffected by quotient
degeneracy.

## Limitations

Does not classify all $G$-invariant solutions at $t=0$ and proves no uniform
$L^\infty$ bound for distant branches; hence the full (A)/(B) dichotomy is not
resolved. Explicit $\delta_0=1/10$, $r_0=0.05$ rest on a crude stated bound
$C\sim 8$ and $O(1)$ Sobolev constant and are illustrative; existence of some
$\delta_0>0$ and the no-accumulation conclusion are fully proved. Grushin claim is
vanishing-induced non-uniform-ellipticity, not a full normal form.
