The kernel of G1=<a,b,c|([a,b]c^2)^2> -> Z/4 (a,b|->0, c|->1) is a quasiconvex closed-surface subgroup of genus 4, generated by c^i a c^{-i}, c^i b c^{-i} (i=0..3). Reidemeister-Schreier plus Tietze elimination gives the standard genus-4 relator; the n=2 small-exponent instance lies below large-torsion existence theorems.
