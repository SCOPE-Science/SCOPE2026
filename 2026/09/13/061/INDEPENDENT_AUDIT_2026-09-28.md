# Independent audit — SCOPE-20260913-061

Date: 2026-09-28 (UTC)  

## Disposition: PASSED

### Correctness
Independent exact enumeration of the four deconed line families reproduces the claimed characteristic polynomial data for k=1 through 6 and the supplied closed-form counting argument proves all k>=1. In particular the coefficient S(k) is 41k^2/2 for even k and (41k^2+1)/2 for odd k, giving discriminant -k^2 or -(k^2+2). The higher-rank flat x4=...=xl=0 adds only duplicate traces, so the same rank-3 restriction occurs for l=4,5,6. Terao factorization then certifies non-freeness, while central arrangements of rank at most two are free.

### Originality
The nearest targeted literature located a hereditary-freeness classification for extended Shi arrangements of type A, not type B. The explicit type-B rank-3 witness and parity formula therefore appear nontrivial, but absence from this search is not used as a proof of priority; originality is assessed as moderate with that caveat.

### Scientific value
A uniform closed-form obstruction is substantially more informative than isolated computational counterexamples and gives a compact certificate for every k>=1 in the requested ranks.

### Sources checked
- Nakashima and Tsujie, Freeness for restriction arrangements of the extended Shi and Catalan arrangements: https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.03585 — Nearest retrieved hereditary-freeness result; its abstract and theorem scope are type A, whereas this record gives an explicit type-B witness family.
- Abe and Terao, The freeness of Shi-Catalan arrangements: https://arxiv.org/abs/1012.5884 — Background for freeness of ambient Shi-Catalan cones, distinct from hereditary freeness of restrictions.

### Limitations
- The result is a witness theorem, not a flat-by-flat classification of every restriction type.
- The literature search was targeted rather than exhaustive enough to establish historical priority.
