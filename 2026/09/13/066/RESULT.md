# Bounded prime gaps in x^0.53 intervals at 1e12: verified certificate

## Context

The admitted TARGET asks: fix theta=0.53, X0=10^12, L=10^7, C=80. Is it true that
for every integer x in [X0, X0+L] the short interval [x-x^theta, x] contains two
distinct primes p<q with q-p<=80? A complete answer is either a rigorous verified
prime census certifying the property for every such x, or an explicit certified
counterexample. This record resolves the TARGET positively with an auditable
finite certificate.

## Definitions

Let X0=10^12, L=10^7, theta=0.53, C=80, and W0=2290867.
Exact big-integer arithmetic gives W0^100 <= X0^53 < (W0+1)^100, so
W0=floor(X0^0.53). Since t -> t^0.53 is strictly increasing, every x>=X0
satisfies x^0.53>=W0, hence [x-W0,x] is contained in [x-x^0.53,x].

## Result

For every integer x in [10^12, 10^12+10^7], the interval [x-x^0.53, x]
contains two distinct primes p<q with q-p<=80. The TARGET answer is TRUE.
It is certified by the following five prime pairs (gaps 28, 8, 10, 6, 6):

- (999999999961, 999999999989)
- (1000002290813, 1000002290821)
- (1000004581663, 1000004581673)
- (1000006872517, 1000006872523)
- (1000009163341, 1000009163347)

## Proof / evidence

Put F_k = a_k + W0 where (a_k, b_k) is the k-th pair above.
Coverage holds by exact integer checks: b_0=999999999989<=X0;
b_1<=F_0+1=1000002290829; b_2<=F_1+1=1000004581681;
b_3<=F_2+1=1000006872531; b_4<=F_3+1=1000009163385;
F_4=1000011454208>=X0+L=1000010000000.
Let k be the first index with F_k>=x. If k=0, x>=X0>=b_0 and a_0=F_0-W0>=x-W0.
If k>=1, x>F_{k-1} gives x>=b_k and x<=F_k gives a_k>=x-W0. In either case
a_k,b_k lie in [x-W0,x], hence in [x-x^0.53,x], with gap at most 80.

Primality is self-contained: each chain integer n satisfies
n<=1000009163347<(1000005)^2=1000010000025, so trial division by all primes up
to 1000005 (generated by a plain sieve; the prime 1000003 is included) is a
complete deterministic proof. The verifier also cross-checked with deterministic
Miller-Rabin (bases 2,3,5,7,11, valid below 2152302898747). The independent
audit re-ran the verifier (CERTIFICATE VALID) and re-checked all primalities,
gaps, W0 inequalities, and covering inequalities. A segmented sieve over
[999997709133,1000010000000] (12290868 integers) found 444785 primes and zero
bad x among all 10000001 sliding windows of constant width W0; this census is
supporting evidence, while the five-pair chain above is the load-bearing proof.

## Limitations

The certificate covers exactly the stated finite range [X0,X0+L] with
theta=0.53 and C=80. It implies nothing about other heights, narrower
exponents, or smaller gap bounds. Census-wide primality outside the ten chain
primes rests on the reproducible segmented sieve; the chain certificate itself
is proved by the self-contained verifier script.
