The framed parallel-{0,2} crystalline lifting ring over Z_3 at the niveau-2 mod-3 point over Q_{3^2} with inertial type omega_2+omega_2^3 and determinant omega*ur_2 vanishes: R=0. Any such lift would force its determinant onto chi_cyc^2 on inertia, reducing to the trivial character, while the residual determinant is quadratic on inertia; hence no crystalline lift exists.
