## Final claim assessed

For the order-seven exotic elliptic algebra in the stated invariant construction, the subalgebra generated by Reynolds averages of the two quadratic and four degree-seven Smith-Tate central generators has growth dimension at most three, while the exotic algebra has growth dimension four; therefore the exotic algebra is not module-finite over that Reynolds-averaged subalgebra.

## Correctness: PASS

The central-growth obstruction is sound after one nonessential asymptotic coefficient is corrected. For order seven, published PI-Sklyanin results give four degree-seven central generators and two quadrics. Under the stated compatible Klein-four action, the four-dimensional degree-seven space has zero trace on each nonidentity translation and hence is the regular representation, so Reynolds projection has one-dimensional image. Thus the proposed invariant subalgebra is a quotient of a weighted polynomial ring with weights \(2,2,7\), of growth dimension at most three, whereas the exotic algebra has Hilbert series \((1-t)^{-4}\) and growth dimension four. Module-finiteness would force equal growth dimension, a contradiction. Fresh coefficient counts gave ratios \(118.07,377.84,834.20,1761.16\) at degrees \(20,50,100,200\). The filed text's asymptotic ratio \(14m/3\) is off by a factor of two; the correct coefficientwise ratio is asymptotic to \(28m/3\). This repair strengthens, rather than weakens, divergence and does not change the theorem.
