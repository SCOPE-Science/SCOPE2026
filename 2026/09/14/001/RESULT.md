# PI-finiteness of the exotic elliptic algebra at order 7 via explicit invariants: disproof of the Reynolds-averaged route

## Context

Let `k` be algebraically closed of characteristic 0. Let `S = S(E, tau)` be the
four-dimensional Sklyanin algebra attached to an elliptic curve `E` and a
translation automorphism `tau` of exact order 7. At finite order `S` is PI and
finite over its full centre. Let `Gamma = (Z/2)^2` act compatibly with the
2-torsion translations (commuting with `tau`) and by conjugation on the matrix
factor, and put `R = S tensor M_2(k)` and `A = R^Gamma`, the Chirvasitu--Smith
exotic elliptic algebra. By hypothesis `A` shares the polynomial Hilbert series
in four variables `HS_A(t) = 1/(1-t)^4`, so
`dim A_n = (n+1)(n+2)(n+3)/6`, and the degree-20 line scheme.

The target asks whether `A` is module-finite over the explicit invariant
subalgebra `C` generated by `Gamma`-averaged lifts of the Smith--Tate central
generators of `Z(S)`, with PI-degree at most 14 and finite rank computable by
the Reynolds operator.

## Definitions

Smith--Tate at odd order `n = 7`: `Z(S)` is generated by two quadrics
`Omega_1, Omega_2` of degree 2 and four elements `z_0,...,z_3` of degree 7,
with relations in degree `2n = 14`. Lifts to `R` are `z |-> z tensor 1` since
scalars `1 in M_2` are fixed by conjugation. With the Reynolds projector
`Rey = (1/4) sum_{g in Gamma} g`, define `C = k<Rey(Omega_i tensor 1),
Rey(z_j tensor 1)>`, a commutative graded subalgebra of `Z(A)` with at most 6
homogeneous generators (2 of degree 2, 4 of degree 7). The target claims `A`
is module-finite over this explicit `C`.

## Result

The stated PI-finiteness route is false. With the above notation at `tau` of
exact order 7, `A` is NOT module-finite over `C` at any rank or degree bound.
Reynolds collapses the four degree-7 central generators to at most one, so
`C = k[y1,y2,w]` of weights `(2,2,7)` or smaller, while `HS_A = 1/(1-t)^4`
has cubic coefficients versus at most quadratic for `C`. Hence
`GKdim(A) = 4` versus `GKdim(C) <= 3`. Only `C`-finiteness is refuted, not
PI-ness of `A` over its own full centre.

## Proof / evidence

Lemma 1 (character theory, proved and enumerated): a 4-dimensional
representation `V` of `V_4 = (Z/2)^2` with all non-identity traces 0 is the
regular representation; each linear character occurs once, so
`dim V^Gamma = 1` and `Rey` projects onto exactly one dimension.

Lemma 2 (admitted structural input plus elementary geometry): degree count
`2a + 7b = 7` forces `(a,b) = (0,1)`, so `Z(S)_7 = span{z_0,...,z_3}` is
4-dimensional; relations in degree 14 do not affect degree 7. `Gamma` acts by
graded automorphisms, hence preserves centre and degree, so `Z(S)_7` is
`Gamma`-stable. The admitted `Gamma`-equivariant section model identifies it
with `H^0(E'',M)` on the quotient elliptic curve `E'' = E/<tau>` for a
degree-4 bundle; Riemann--Roch gives `h^0 = 4`, and fixed-point-free
holomorphic Lefschetz gives trace 0 for every non-identity `g in Gamma`.

Lemma 3 (collapse): by Lemmas 1--2, `Z(S)_7` is the regular representation, so
all `Rey(z_j)` lie in its 1-dimensional invariant subspace and are collinear:
`Rey(z_j) = c_j w`. Thus `C = k[y1,y2,w]` with `deg = (2,2,7)`, possibly with
dependencies or `w = 0`.

Theorem (growth obstruction, analytic): let `P = k[Y1,Y2,W]` with weights
`(2,2,7)` surjecting onto `C`, so `dim C_m <= dim P_m` coefficientwise with
`dim P_m <= (m+7)(m+2)/14` (quadratic). If `A = sum_{j=1}^r C f_j` with
homogeneous `f_j` of degree `d_j`, then
`dim A_m <= sum_j dim P_{m-d_j} <= r(m+7)(m+2)/14`. But
`dim A_m = (m+1)(m+2)(m+3)/6` is cubic, ratio `~ (14/3)m -> infinity`
(1761 at `m = 200`), so no fixed `r` works with any shifts. Degenerate cases
with fewer generators are strictly smaller. Numerical certificate:
weights-`(2,2,7)` coefficients `n=0..13` are `[1,0,2,0,3,0,4,1,5,2,6,3,7,4]`;
ratios 47.7 at `n=10`, 202 at 30, 834 at 100, 1761 at 200; partial sums to 60:
`A = 635376` vs `P = 1710`; growth exponents `~3.78` vs `~2.63`.

## Limitations

The Smith--Tate generation pattern at order 7 and the `Gamma`-equivariant
section model of `Z(S)_7` are taken as the admitted structural input named in
the topic fingerprint; the collapse, generator count, and growth contradiction
are proved from them. The compatible `Gamma` action is per the topic
hypothesis. PI-ness of `A` over its own full centre and its true PI-degree are
not decided here.
