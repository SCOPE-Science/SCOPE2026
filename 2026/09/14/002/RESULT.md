# Truncation surjectivity with nonempty connected fibers for an exceptional-divisor-type algebra

## Context

Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic not 2 or 3. Consider 3-generator quadratic algebras and their truncated point schemes, which parametrize truncated point modules via multilinearized relations in products of projective planes. The admitted target asks for the geometry of the truncation morphism for a Hwang exceptional-divisor algebra at the degenerate Sklyanin point: is phi: W_4 -> W_3 surjective with nonempty connected scheme-theoretic fibers, with an explicit singularity certificate.

## Definitions

Let E = k<x,y,z>/(x^2, y^2, zx+zy). For d = 2,3,4 let W_d subset (P^2_k)^d be the truncated point scheme cut out by the three bidegree-(1,1) relations on each consecutive pair of factors. With coordinates a=(a_0:a_1:a_2), b=(b_0:b_1:b_2),

W_2 = V(f_1,f_2,f_3), f_1 = a_0 b_0, f_2 = a_1 b_1, f_3 = a_2 (b_0+b_1),

and W_3 (resp. W_4) is defined by f_i = 0 on each of the two (resp. three) consecutive pairs. Let phi: W_4 -> W_3 forget the last factor. Let P = ([0:0:1],[0:0:1],[0:0:1]).

## Result

Then:

1. phi is surjective; every closed point of W_3 extends to W_4.
2. Every scheme-theoretic fiber of phi is isomorphic to P^r_k with r in {0,1}; in particular every fiber is nonempty and connected. Generically r = 0 (reduced point [0:0:1]); in degenerate strata (e.g. c = [0:0:1] or [0:1:0]) r = 1.
3. W_3 is singular at P, which lies on the intersection of two irreducible components; the Jacobian at P has rank 2 while the codimension is 3.

Moreover E contains exactly two monomial lines (k x^2, k y^2) hence is not a degenerate-Sklyanin monomial presentation, and its skew-symmetric projection has rank 1, hence it is not a De Laet Heisenberg quotient carrying two independent commutator relations.

## Proof / evidence

Fix c = (c_0:c_1:c_2). The fiber condition (c,d) in W_2 is the linear system M(c) d = 0 with M(c) = [[c_0,0,0],[0,c_1,0],[c_2,c_2,0]]. The third column is identically zero, so e_2 = [0:0:1] satisfies M(c) e_2 = 0 for every c. Hence s(c) = e_2 is a global regular section of W_2 -> P^2 (first factor). For any closed q = (a,b,c) in W_3, (a,b,c,e_2) satisfies relations on consecutive pairs and lies in W_4 above q. Since phi is projective hence closed, closed-point surjectivity gives scheme surjectivity.

The scheme fiber over q = (a,b,c) depends only on c: Proj k[d_0,d_1,d_2]/J_c with J_c = (c_0 d_0, c_1 d_1, c_2(d_0+d_1)). Every generator lies in the homogeneous prime (d_0,d_1), so the fiber is nonempty and contains e_2. J_c is generated by linear forms, so the fiber is a linear subspace P^r with r = 2 - rank M(c) >= 0 since rank <= 2. Projective spaces are connected.

At P, in the chart a_2 b_2 c_2 != 0 with x_0 = a_0/a_2 etc., the ideal is (x_0 x_1, y_0 y_1, x_1+y_1, x_1 x_2, y_1 y_2, x_2+y_2) with Jacobian at origin of rank exactly 2 (2x2 minor 1, characteristic-free; all other rows zero). Eliminating y_1 = -x_1, y_2 = -x_2 gives k[x_0,y_0,x_1,x_2]/(x_0 x_1, y_0 x_1, x_1 x_2) = k[...]/(x_1) cap (x_0,y_0,x_2), i.e. V(x_1) ~= A^3 and V(x_0,y_0,x_2) ~= A^1 meeting at P. Local dimension 3, codimension 3 in A^6, tangent dimension 4 > 3: singular on a component intersection.

## Limitations

The computation is for the explicit relation space above over algebraically closed k of characteristic not 2 or 3. The identification of this presentation with Hwang thesis Chapters 5-6 exceptional-divisor algebra is taken from the admitted topic fingerprint. No claim is made about other exceptional algebras or general truncation maps.

## Reproducibility

Exact SymPy script output/artifacts/verify.py checks section identities, fiber ranks/strata, P in W_3, Jacobian rank 2 with minor 1, component factorization, symmetric rank 3 and skew rank 1, and the two-monomial-line count. All checks pass; re-executed during audit. All minors are 0 or +-1, hence valid in any characteristic not 2 or 3.
