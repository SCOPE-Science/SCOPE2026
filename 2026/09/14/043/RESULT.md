# Weak tracial cone approximation follows from regularity in simple nuclear C*-algebras

## Context

The Toms–Winter regularity program predicts that regularity properties of simple
nuclear C*-algebras — strict comparison of positive elements, stable rank one,
Jiang–Su stability, and finite nuclear dimension — coincide. A natural
projectionless test of regularity is whether hereditary subalgebras admit
uniform tracial approximation by cones over finite-dimensional algebras, for
which there is no K-theoretic obstruction since cones are projectionless and
2-norms erase K-theory. The target asks this for every hereditary subalgebra
generated by a Pedersen element.

## Definitions

Let A be separable, simple, amenable (nuclear), finite, non-elementary, with
nonzero densely defined traces and regular Cuntz-semigroup structure (in this
finite separable simple setting: strict comparison and stable rank one).
For a in Ped(A)+ write Her(a) for the hereditary subalgebra generated by a.
Property (WTAC): for every such a, every finite F in the unit ball of Her(a),
and every epsilon > 0, there exist a finite-dimensional C*-algebra D and a
*-homomorphism Phi : C_0((0,1]) tensor D -> Her(a) such that each x in F is
within uniform normalized-trace 2-norm epsilon of Phi(d_x) for some contraction
d_x in the cone. By the Winter–Zacharias correspondence, cone homomorphisms are
in bijection with completely positive contractive order-zero maps D -> Her(a).

## Result

Theorem: every A as above has property (WTAC). In particular, each Her(a)
satisfies the same hypotheses (separable, simple, nuclear, finite,
non-elementary, nonzero densely defined traces, strict comparison, stable
rank one), hence is Z-stable, and finite subsets of its unit ball are uniformly
approximable in all normalized 2-norms by contractions in the image of a single
order-zero map from a finite-dimensional algebra.

## Proof / evidence

Reduce to B = Her(a) with a nonzero (zero case trivial); permanence of
simplicity, nuclearity, finiteness, non-elementariness, traces, strict
comparison, and stable rank one to hereditary subalgebras is standard. The
resolved Toms–Winter theorem (unital case plus established non-unital
extension) gives B Z-stable, hence complemented partitions of unity (CPoU).
Nuclearity makes each GNS fiber von Neumann algebra injective, hence
hyperfinite by Connes, yielding local finite-dimensional 2-norm approximants;
Winter–Zacharias and Loring stability lift them to cpc order-zero maps valid
uniformly over weak* neighborhoods covering the compact a-normalized trace
simplex. CPoU glues the finitely many local maps into one global cpc
order-zero map D -> B in the tracial ultrapower, promoted by Kirchberg's
epsilon-test and converted to the required cone homomorphism. Cited black
boxes (Toms–Winter, Connes, Winter–Zacharias, CPoU gluing, epsilon-test) are
used as stated, not re-proved.

## Limitations

Proves only the stated finite regular non-elementary direction; no converse is
claimed. Relies as black boxes on the resolved Toms–Winter conjecture including
its non-unital stable form, Connes injectivity-implies-hyperfiniteness, the
cone/order-zero correspondence with L2 stability, CPoU gluing, and Kirchberg's
epsilon-test. Covers exactly the finite case with nonzero densely defined
traces. No numerical computation or external search underlies the argument.

## Reproducibility

Verify Step 1 permanence facts from standard C*-algebra references; apply the
cited Toms–Winter, Connes, Winter–Zacharias/Loring, CPoU, and epsilon-test
results as black boxes; follow the fiber-to-neighborhood-to-finite-cover-to-
gluing construction in output DRAFT.md for arbitrary a, F, epsilon.
