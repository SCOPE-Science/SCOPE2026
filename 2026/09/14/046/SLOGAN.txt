Weibull (stretched-exponential, 0<r<1) branching random walk in supercritical i.i.d. environment: with R(d_n(Y))=L_n(Y), a_n(Y)=1/R'(d_n(Y)), b_n(Y)=d_n(Y)+tau_n(Y), the scaled extremal process converges vaguely to Cox cluster sum T_k delta_{iota_k-log(upsilon(Y^<- )W)}. Maximum satisfies (M_n-b_n(Y))/a_n(Y)->mixture of Gumbels; deterministic centring impossible since fluctuations grow as sigma sqrt(n).
