# Homotopy Invariance Beyond Row-Finite Higher-Rank Graphs: KK-Rigidity for Twisted Relative Algebras

## Context

Homotopy invariance of K-theory under 2-cocycle deformation is a recognized program in operator algebras: Echterhoff-Luck-Phillips-Walters for groups, Gillaspy for transformation groups and row-finite source-free higher-rank graphs (Pacific J. Math. 278 (2015), Theorem 4.1), Kumjian-Pask-Sims for real-lift homotopies, Boenicke for homotopies of twists on ample groupoids satisfying Baum-Connes with coefficients (J. Noncommut. Geom. 15 (2021), Theorem A), and Mundey-Sims (2025) for self-similar amenable actions on row-finite graphs with no sources. Gillaspy's higher-rank graph paper explicitly left the finitely aligned case as future work because the Kumjian-Pask path groupoid does not apply there. Finitely aligned graphs are the largest class to which one profitably associates a C*-algebra, via Yeend's boundary-path groupoid and the Raeburn-Sims-Yeend relative theory with satiated exhaustive sets.

## Definitions

Let Lambda be a countable finitely aligned higher-rank graph (k-graph): a countable small category with degree functor d: Lambda -> N^k satisfying unique factorization; finite alignment means minimal common extensions MCE(lambda,mu) are finite. Let FE(Lambda) be the finite exhaustive sets and E subset FE(Lambda) satiated (closed under the Raeburn-Sims-Yeend saturation operations). Let Z^2(Lambda,T) be normalized categorical 2-cocycles c(lambda,mu) with values in the circle. A homotopy is a family {c_t}_{t in [0,1]} such that t |-> c_t(lambda,mu) is continuous for every composable pair (pointwise continuity; no uniformity assumed). Write C*(Lambda,c;E) for the Sims-Whitehead-Whittaker twisted relative Cuntz-Krieger algebra, generated by the universal (Lambda,c;E)-family s_lambda^{c,E}. Let G_E be Yeend's boundary groupoid restricted to the E-boundary partial(Lambda;E): second countable locally compact Hausdorff etale ample amenable, with twisted algebra C*(G_E,sigma_c) isomorphic to C*(Lambda,c;E).

## Result (headline theorem)

Let Lambda be a countable finitely aligned higher-rank graph, E subset FE(Lambda) satiated, and {c_t} a pointwise-continuous homotopy of normalized categorical 2-cocycles. Then there exists a canonical separable nuclear C([0,1])-algebra C(Lambda,E,c_bullet), generated by a universal C([0,1])-linear twisted relative Cuntz-Krieger family for the pooled cocycle tilde{c}(lambda,mu)(t) = c_t(lambda,mu), with fiber C(Lambda,E,c_bullet)_t isomorphic to C*(Lambda,c_t;E) via the evaluation map ev_t. Every ev_t is a KK-equivalence, and the composite KK-equivalence [ev_0]^{-1} otimes [ev_t] : C*(Lambda,c_0;E) -> C*(Lambda,c_t;E) carries each vertex class [s_v^{c_0,E}] to [s_v^{c_t,E}] for every vertex v.

## Proof / evidence

Pooled cocycle and universal algebra: tilde{c} satisfies the cocycle identity in C([0,1],T) fiberwise, giving a normalized Z^2(Lambda,C([0,1],T)) class. The universal C*-algebra for C([0,1])-linear (Lambda,tilde{c};E)-families exists by the standard Blackadar-Raeburn-Sims-Yeend envelope argument: all generators are partial isometries hence contractive, and fiber representations {s_lambda^{c_t,E}} separate points. Countability gives separability; the central map Phi: C([0,1]) -> Z(M(C)) is injective because vertex generators are nonzero in every fiber; the gauge action descends.

Fiber identification: the quotient C_t = C/I_t with I_t = C_0([0,1]\\{t}) C carries the descended gauge action and a (Lambda,c_t;E)-family with nonzero vertices; the Sims-Whitehead-Whittaker twisted relative satiated gauge-invariant uniqueness theorem (Theorem 3.16 with core criterion Theorem 3.13) gives injectivity of the descended evaluation, hence an isomorphism onto C*(Lambda,c_t;E). The non-vanishing of Delta(t)_F for F outside E holds because its image is the universal fiber gap projection.

Groupoid continuity: on the product etale groupoid H = G_E x [0,1], Sigma((g,t),(h,t)) = sigma_{c_t}(g,h) satisfies the cocycle identity fiberwise. Joint continuity uses the Kumjian-Pask-Sims finite-word chart formula: on each basic overlap chart, sigma_{c_t} is one fixed finite word in finitely many c_t(lambda_i,mu_i) independent of t, hence jointly continuous from pointwise continuity. Amenability gives full = reduced. The twisted cylinder sections give a C([0,1])-linear surjection Psi: C -> C*(H,Sigma), an isomorphism by the Blanchard fiber criterion since each fiber map is the Sims-Yeend isomorphism.

K-theory plus UCT upgrade: G_E amenable satisfies Baum-Connes with coefficients (Tu), H is ample second countable, so the published Gillaspy/Boenicke theorem gives that each evaluation q_t: C*(H,Sigma) -> C*(G_E,sigma_{c_t}) induces a K-theory isomorphism. Fibers lie in the bootstrap class by Sims-Whitehead-Whittaker Theorem 5.2 (Morita equivalence to AF-by-Z^k crossed products) and the total algebra is nuclear UCT; the mapping cone of q_t then has vanishing K-theory and hence vanishing KK, so [q_t] is KK-invertible (Rosenberg-Schochet). Transfer along Psi gives KK-equivalence of ev_t. Vertex preservation is formal: ev_t sends the canonical lift class [S_v] to [s_v^{c_t,E}] and ev_0 induces a K_0 isomorphism.

Computational illustration (no K-theoretic content): pooled_cocycle_check.py on a single-vertex 2-graph model c_t(lambda,mu) = exp(i t n(lambda) m(mu)) verifies the cocycle identity at 4000 random triples over a 21-point grid (max defect 7.15e-15), pooled identity uniformly, normalization, and Lipschitz continuity. Rerun in audit: PASS.

## Limitations

Invokes as black boxes: Sims-Whitehead-Whittaker twisted relative GIUT and nuclearity/UCT, Kumjian-Pask-Sims finite-word groupoid cocycle construction, Yeend amenability and Hausdorff etale ample structure, Tu Baum-Connes for amenable groupoids, Gillaspy/Boenicke K-theory homotopy theorem, Rosenberg-Schochet bootstrap closure and UCT, Blanchard C(X)-algebra fiber criterion. The computation illustrates only the pooled-cocycle mechanism. The theorem covers satiated E and finitely aligned Lambda; non-satiated or non-finitely-aligned settings are out of scope.
