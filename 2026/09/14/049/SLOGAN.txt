Parity equals perversity for the wild 3-Kronecker KLR algebra at dimension vector (2,2) over F2: all 14 Lusztig fibres are even, so every parity complex E(lambda,F2) is the modular reduction of its IC complex. Hence every graded decomposition number d(t) of R_{(2,2),F2} equals its characteristic-zero canonical-basis value.
