For the 3-Kronecker quiver at dimension vector (2,2), every one of the 14 Lusztig-map fibers is a finite set, P^1-type configuration, or P^1xP^1-type configuration with torsion-free even integral cohomology. This does not by itself imply parity=IC or characteristic-independent KLR decomposition numbers, because the cited Maksimau purity mechanism is Dynkin-scoped.
