For every 3<=k<=n, the Pascaleff-Tonkonog k>2 mutation of the Clifford torus in CP^n has an explicit critical local system and a Newton simplex of volume k^(k-1)(n+1) with a K_k of affine-length-k edges, proving non-displaceability and separating it from Clifford and all lifted Vianna tori. The one-step k>2 wall-crossing is non-binomial; no finite-composition obstruction is claimed.
