PT k>2 toric-mutated monotone torus in CP^n has potential W'=sum_{i>=k}y_i^{-1}+P*Q^k with critical point rho=(1/k,...,1/k,1,...,1), value n+1, hence nonzero Floer cohomology and Hamiltonian non-displaceability. Its Newton simplex has volume k^{k-1}(n+1) with K_k long edges of length k, excluding Clifford and all lifted Vianna tori, so the k>2 wall-crossing is not binomially realizable.
