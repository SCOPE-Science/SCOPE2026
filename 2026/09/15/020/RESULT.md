# Finite-iterate self-compatibility implies hyperfiniteness for hyperfinite-over-hyperfinite equivalence relations

## Context

A countable Borel equivalence relation is hyperfinite if it is an increasing union of finite Borel equivalence relations, equivalently Borel reducible to eventual equality E0. Two major open problems ask whether larger classes are hyperfinite: the Union Problem (increasing unions of hyperfinite relations) and the Hyperfinite-over-Hyperfinite Problem (relations admitting a Borel class-wise Z^2 lexicographic ordering). Su Gao and Ming Xiao, in "An Order Analysis of Hyperfinite Borel Equivalence Relations" (arXiv:2404.17516), proved that a hyperfinite-over-hyperfinite relation generated by a self-compatible Borel Z^2-ordering is hyperfinite (Theorem 1.1/5.3). Self-compatibility compares two Z-orderings of the finite-interval subrelation F: the restriction < of the Z^2-order and the pullback ordering <'_gamma induced by the immediate-predecessor map gamma. The n=1 case was settled there; the finite-iterate question was left open except for a single example in Remark 5.5.

## Definitions

Let E be a countable Borel equivalence relation on a standard Borel space X with a Borel class-wise Z^2-ordering prec. Define xFy iff the prec-interval between x and y is finite; F is Borel hyperfinite. A point is full if the F-classes in its E-class have prec-order type exactly Z; full(X) is Borel E-invariant and E outside it is hyperfinite (Proposition 2.9). On full(X), let A=dom(gamma) be a Borel F-complete section, gamma the immediate F-predecessor injection, eta_A the closest-point selector, and T=gamma circ eta_A the total map shifting each point down exactly one F-class. Let x<y iff xFy and x prec y, and let <'_gamma be the derived ordering comparing eta-images or their gamma-images. Compatibility (Definition 3.1) means some Borel complete section on which the two orders agree or are everywhere opposite in each E-class. For n>=1, <'_{gamma^n} is the analogous derived order from the n-step predecessor map, eta-corrected to be total.

## Result

Theorem (TARGET, proved YES): Let E, prec, F, full(X), gamma, < be as above and let <'_{gamma^n} be the n-step derived ordering. If for some finite n>=1 the pair <, <'_{gamma^n} is compatible, then E is hyperfinite, equivalently E <=_B E0. In particular the Gao-Xiao n=1 theorem extends to every finite n.

## Proof / Evidence

Work on full(X). Fix n and define the mod-n decimation x E_n y iff T^m(x) F T^k(y) for some m,k>=0 with m=k mod n. This is Borel as a countable union of Borel conditions. Reflexivity and symmetry are clear; transitivity follows because each T^r shifts F-class index down by exactly r, so congruent pairs compose with preserved congruence. On full(X) the F-classes in each E-class are Z-indexed and T shifts by -1, so E_n-classes are exactly mod-n residue classes: each E-class splits into exactly n E_n-classes represented by x,T(x),...,T^{n-1}(x), using unboundedness below in Z.

The lex-block order prec restricts to a generating class-wise Z^2-ordering of E_n with the same finite-interval relation: cross-block points of one E_n-class are directly prec-comparable, reindexing retained blocks by j->(j-r)/n preserves order, and points of one E_n-class in distinct F-blocks bracket whole intermediate blocks of other residues, giving infinite intervals, while same-block intervals stay finite. Hence F_{E_n}=F, < is unchanged, and the full part is unchanged.

A valid Section-5 predecessor choice for E_n is any partial Borel injection sending each F-class to its immediate E_n-predecessor (the nth F-predecessor), existing by the same countable-uniformization argument. The target <'_{gamma^n}, built from total T^n circ eta_A restricted to a Borel F-complete section where the n-step map is injective (finite-to-one partition plus locally-finite maximal-anticlique refinement, as in Section 5 and Jackson-Kechris-Louveau Lemma 1.17), is of this valid form. Proposition 5.2's proof uses only equal shift amount and injectivity on a section (finiteness of mismatch sets, local finiteness of R, Borel maximal anticlique splitting), so the n-step version holds verbatim: compatibility with <'_{gamma^n} is equivalent to self-compatibility of E_n in the sense of Definition 5.1.

Apply Theorem 5.3 to E_n: E_n is hyperfinite on full(X). Each E-class there is a union of exactly n E_n-classes, so by Dougherty-Jackson-Kechris Proposition 1.3(vii) (finite extension of hyperfinite is hyperfinite; the same citation as Gao-Xiao p.21), E on full(X) is hyperfinite. Combined over the invariant Borel partition with the hyperfinite non-full part, E is hyperfinite on X.

## Limitations

The statement assumes <'_{gamma^n} denotes the Section-5-type derived order from the n-step predecessor with the standard eta-correction; this is the only reading under which the question is well posed. Borel selection details (chained injections, injective section for the n-step map) are cited to the paper's uniformization and maximal-anticlique toolkit rather than re-proved. The theorem is a sufficiency result (finite-iterate compatibility implies hyperfinite) and does not decide the general Hyperfinite-over-Hyperfinite or Union Problems.

## Reproducibility

Verify E_n Borel/transitive/index-n arithmetic; check lex-block generation and F_{E_n}=F dichotomy; check n-step Proposition 5.2 adaptation; apply Gao-Xiao Theorem 5.3 and DJK Proposition 1.3(vii). Key sources: Gao-Xiao arXiv:2404.17516 Sections 2-5; Dougherty-Jackson-Kechris Trans. AMS 341 (1994).
