The universal claim e(PSp_6(q))=3 for all q=3 mod 4 is false: for PSp_6(3), classes 12 (order 4, centralizer 2304) and 17/18 (inverse pair, order 6, centralizer 15552) satisfy sum_chi chi(g)^3/chi(1)=0 exactly, so 1 is not in C^3 and e(G)>=4. Nearest prior work explicitly left e(PSp_6(q))=3 for 4|(q+1) open.
