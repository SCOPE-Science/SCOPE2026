# Finite-stage spectra of the Obermeyer–Winter Jiang–Su diagonals are pairwise non-homeomorphic graphs

## Context

The Jiang–Su algebra $\mathcal{Z}$ plays a central role in the Elliott classification programme. Obermeyer–Winter (arXiv:2607.03129) construct $\mathcal{Z}$ as an inductive limit of entangled-matrix-cone dimension-drop algebras $\tilde Z_{L,L+1}$ with explicit connecting maps, yielding a $C^*$-diagonal $\tilde D \subset \mathcal{Z}$ with one-dimensional non-locally-connected spectrum (Theorem 4.7). The paper describes each finite-stage diagonal spectrum only as a quotient $X_L$ (Proposition 5.1, Figure 4 of $X_4$) and computes no topological invariants of $X_L$. Whether the limit diagonals for different admissible growth sequences are automorphism-conjugate is left open.

## Definitions

For $L \ge 2$ let $\tilde D_{L,L+1} \subset \tilde Z_{L,L+1}$ be the $C^*$-diagonal of Proposition 4.1 and $X_L$ its character spectrum. By Proposition 5.1, $X_L$ is homeomorphic to the quotient of $\{1,\dots,L\}\times\{0,\dots,L\}\times[0,1]$ by the equivalence relation generated by $0_{i,j}\sim 0_{k,l}$ if $j=l\ge 1$; $0_{i,j}\sim 0_{k,l}$ if $l=0$ and $k=j$; $1_{i,j}\sim 1_{k,l}$ if $j=l$, where $0_{i,j}=(i,j,0)$, $1_{i,j}=(i,j,1)$. Write $R_j$ for the $1$-endpoint class with second coordinate $j$ and $C_i$ for the $0$-endpoint class containing $0_{i,0}$.

## Result

**Theorem.** For every $L \ge 2$, $X_L$ is a connected finite multigraph with exactly $2L+1$ vertices, $L(L+1)$ edges, first Betti number $b_1=L(L-1)$, and $\max_{x\in X_L}\#\pi_0(X_L\setminus\{x\})=L$, attained at $R_0$. Hence $L$ is recovered topologically from $X_L$, and $X_L \not\cong X_{L'}$ for $L \ne L'$.

## Proof / Evidence

The $1$-endpoints fall into $L+1$ classes $R_0,\dots,R_L$ and the $0$-endpoints into $L$ disjoint classes $C_i=\{0_{i,0}\}\cup\{0_{k,i}:k\}$, with no $0$/$1$ identifications. Thus $X_L$ is a multigraph with vertices $R_j,C_i$ and edges $e_{i,j}$ joining $C_i$ to $R_0$ for $j=0$ and $C_j$ to $R_j$ for $j\ge 1$ (an $L$-fold parallel bundle). Connectedness follows from the spanning tree $\{e_{i,0}\}\cup\{e_{1,j}:j\ge 1\}$. Euler gives $b_1=E-V+1=L(L-1)$. Removing $R_0$ leaves exactly $L$ components; removing any $C_i$ leaves $2$; removing $R_j$ ($j\ge 1$) leaves the space connected; bridge interiors disconnect into $2$ while parallel-bundle interiors do not. Hence the punctured-component maximum is exactly $L$. The pairs $(L(L-1),L)$ are distinct for distinct $L\ge 2$. Counts were machine-checked for $2\le L\le 10$ in `output/artifacts/verify_counts.py`.

## Limitations

This covers only the finite-stage spectra $X_L$, not the inverse-limit spectra $X=\varprojlim(X_{L_n},f_{n+1,n})$ or the automorphism-conjugacy of the limit diagonals in $\mathcal{Z}$, which remain open because the bonding maps are determined only at three vertices (Lemma 5.4). Conjugacy implies homeomorphic spectra, so the result is consistent with but does not imply limit non-conjugacy.
