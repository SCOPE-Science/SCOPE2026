Finite-stage Obermeyer-Winter Jiang-Su diagonal spectra XL are connected finite multigraphs with 2L+1 vertices, L(L+1) edges, Betti number L(L-1) and cut-point maximum L, so L is topologically recovered and distinct L give non-homeomorphic spectra. The source paper states only the quotient; this is the first invariant computation and the finite-stage step toward limit conjugacy.
