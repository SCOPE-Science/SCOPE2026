The braided-admitting fusion submonoid bfcFus is not saturated: Vec_{S3} has nonabelian simples so admits no braiding, yet is Morita equivalent to symmetric Rep(S3), placing it in the saturated closure. The closure is the saturated idempotent submonoid {X | X x P Morita Q for braided-admitting P,Q} containing all Vec_G and Rep(G).
