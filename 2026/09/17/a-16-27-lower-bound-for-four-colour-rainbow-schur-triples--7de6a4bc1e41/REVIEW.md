## Correctness

**PASS (same-model assessment).**

The theorem is witnessed by an explicit deterministic colouring. The source proof covers all nine ordered residue pairs and reduces the limit to elementary polygonal-area calculations. The archived verification script recomputes those areas with exact rational arithmetic and direct finite counts agree with the predicted asymptotic behaviour.

## Originality

**PASS, qualified to the best of our knowledge (same-model assessment).**

The closest source, arXiv:2609.18474v1, was posted on 16 Sep 2026, gives the lower bound `10/21` at `k=4`, and explicitly highlights the `k=4` case. Searches for the exact value `16/27`, four-colour rainbow Schur triples, and modular/residue formulations did not locate the present lower bound. Because the closest preprint was only about one day old, unpublished or unindexed follow-up work is the main originality threat.

## Value

**PASS (same-model assessment).**

The result is an asymptotic theorem for an infinite family, not a numerical instance. It improves the first accessible nontrivial four-colour lower bound from `10/21` to `16/27` and supplies a compact mod-3/two-cut construction that may be reusable in further extremal analysis.
