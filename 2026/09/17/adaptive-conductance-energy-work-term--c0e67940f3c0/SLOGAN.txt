For an adaptive bioelectric network, the fixed-coupling energy acquires the exact conductance-work term (1/2) sum Gdot_ij(V_i-V_j)^2. In the 2026 Cortés-Poza morphogenesis model this term is absent from the displayed full-model energy balance, and a two-cell state with Vdot=0 but Gdot>0 gives Edot>0, so adaptive coupling can raise the instantaneous energy even without GRN, wound, or external voltage work.
