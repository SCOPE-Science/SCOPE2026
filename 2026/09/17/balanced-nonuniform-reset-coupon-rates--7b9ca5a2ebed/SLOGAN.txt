For fixed reset probability and balanced non-uniform coupon weights \(w_{i,n}=x_{i,n}/n\), the one-attempt success probability has a uniform saddle-point asymptotic controlled by the empirical profile of the \(x_{i,n}\). The same rate functional governs the exponential growth of the mean and, via Wang-Lu's sharp catastrophe theorem, the decay \(d_K=\Theta(np_n)\); persistent heterogeneity away from uniformity incurs an explicit exponential delay.
