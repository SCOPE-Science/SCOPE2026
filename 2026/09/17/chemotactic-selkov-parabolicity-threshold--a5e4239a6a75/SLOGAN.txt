At the positive equilibrium of the 2026 chemotactic Selkov model, the coefficient of the k^4 term in the dispersion determinant is exactly the determinant of the principal diffusion matrix. The reciprocal-chemotaxis threshold where the preferred wave number diverges is therefore the loss-of-normal-ellipticity boundary; beyond it the linearized PDE has growth Lambda(k)~c|k|^2 and is Hadamard-ill-posed in Sobolev spaces.
