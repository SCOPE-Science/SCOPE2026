For complex $\ell^p$, the optimal Plücker-coordinate multilinear constant is exactly $1$ for $0<p\le2$ and $n^{n(1/2-1/p)}$ for $p>2$: the Fourier matrix saturates the $p=\infty$ endpoint and makes interpolation from $p=2$ sharp. This removes the real Hadamard maximal-determinant obstruction from the complex version of arXiv:2608.00983 Problem P1 and also forces $2^+$ continuity for the real version.
