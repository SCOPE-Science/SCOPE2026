For every noncomplete n-vertex graph, μ_c(G)=n−1 exactly when some vertex x has G−x connected and every nonneighbor of x is universal in G−x (equivalently, every neighbor of x in the complement is a leaf). For n≥5 this completely classifies equality in both connected-mutual-visibility Nordhaus–Gaddum bounds: up to complementation the only extremal graphs are K_{2,n−2} and K_2∨overline(K)_{n−2}.
