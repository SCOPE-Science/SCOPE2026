For rank-two generalized Virasoro conformal modules, the Riccati substitution in Wu--Hong Remark 4.21 requires the missing logarithmic-derivative term -c21'/c21 in the associated second-order linear equation. A valuation-at-infinity argument also gives a broad irreducibility test: when the diagonal difference has degree below the average off-diagonal degree and the two off-diagonal degrees have opposite parity, the Riccati equation has no rational solution.
