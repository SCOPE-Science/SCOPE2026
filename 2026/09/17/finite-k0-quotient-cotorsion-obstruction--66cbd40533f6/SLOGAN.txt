A finite quotient of the Grothendieck group turns the Ren--Wang parity counterexample into a general congruence mechanism: the induced object ideals form a complete ideal cotorsion pair, while their object cotorsion pair is neither special precovering nor special preenveloping. Modulo two the Euler class becomes total-cohomology parity; for higher moduli the signed Euler class is the extension-stable invariant.
