A standardised margin whose 2q-th moment is strictly below the Gaussian 2q-th moment cannot realise every correlation matrix in all dimensions: finite positive cubature converts the moment deficit into a finite elliptope obstruction. Every platykurtic margin therefore fails eventually; for symmetric Beta(a,a), 1/2 <= a < 1, the exact universal-compatibility threshold is dimension 5, while the uniform boundary a=1 has threshold 9.
