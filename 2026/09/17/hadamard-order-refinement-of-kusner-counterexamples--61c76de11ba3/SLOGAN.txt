Xiong's \(p>4\) Kusner counterexample construction works for every Hadamard order \(m>1/\Delta(p)\), not only powers of two. Combined with asymptotic density of Hadamard orders and Swanepoel's stability bound at \(p=4\), this gives
\[
\frac{8+o(1)}{\delta\log(1/\delta)}
\le d_*(4+\delta)\le
\frac{239.213602262732\ldots+o(1)}{\delta}.
\]
