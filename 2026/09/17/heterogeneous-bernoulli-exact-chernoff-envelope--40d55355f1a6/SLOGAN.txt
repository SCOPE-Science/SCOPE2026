Under independent Bernoulli treatment assignment with arbitrary unit propensities and bounded potential outcomes, the centered Horvitz--Thompson estimator has an exact separable worst-case cumulant-generating-function envelope, and unitwise midpoint centering minimizes its two-sided envelope pointwise in the Chernoff parameter. The resulting finite-sample interval reveals the heterogeneous effective-sample-size scale n_eff = n^2 / sum_i[pi_i(1-pi_i)]^{-1}, with a matching endpoint CLT under a no-dominant-unit condition.
