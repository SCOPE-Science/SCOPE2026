A recent Kasparov-cycle preserver theorem uses a displayed notion of "surjective up to compact operators" that is vacuous; a two-fold amplification of B(H) gives an explicit counterexample to the main automorphism conclusions as written. Reversing the condition to the standard quotient-surjectivity form repairs the Kasparov-cycle theorem, while a separate Hamel-complement example shows that the paper's claimed equality on compact operators is still too strong.
