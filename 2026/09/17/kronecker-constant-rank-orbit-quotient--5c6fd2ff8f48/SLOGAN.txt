For elementary \(K_{x+y-1}\)-modules in the constant-full-rank regime, ordinary fixed-arrow isomorphism classes correspond to right \(GL_y\)-orbits of the associated matrix spaces, while standard \(GL_{x+y-1}\times GL_y\) matrix-space equivalence additionally quotients by arrow-basis changes. A concrete \(K_3\), dimension-\((2,2)\) example shows that the larger equivalence identifies two non-isomorphic elementary modules, correcting the literal fixed-arrow classification in Liu (2023) and disambiguating Liu (2026).
