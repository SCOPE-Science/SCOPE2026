A recent “no assumptions” shared-denominator theorem on the negative real axis is false: even the bounded entire function e^{iz} has best uniform rational error exactly 1, while e^z sin(e^{-z}) tends to 0 at -infinity but has type-(n,n) error at least 1/((2n+3/2)pi). The uniform closure is exactly the continuous functions with a finite limit at -infinity, and the published exterior-domain conformal step is incompatible with the slit plane.
