Balanced Poisson-binomial sums admit an explicit prescribed-center stop-loss asymptotic with the full lattice defect retained: the coefficient is a weighted geometric factor evaluated at the heterogeneous saddle. At fixed mean, heterogeneity raises the large-deviation rate above the binomial rate by at least an explicit multiple of the empirical variance of the Bernoulli probabilities, turning Hoeffding's classical convex-order domination into a quantitative exponential penalty.
