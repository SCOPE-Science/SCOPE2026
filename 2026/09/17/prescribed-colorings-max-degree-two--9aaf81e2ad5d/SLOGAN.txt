For every graph G with maximum degree at most two, prescribed color-class sizes are feasible exactly when the largest class is at most alpha(G) and the two largest classes together have size at most |V(G)| minus the number of odd-cycle components. A max-flow proof shows these are the only obstructions, yielding the full stable-partition support of X_G and proving Birken's remainder-sensitive skewed-coloring conjecture for r=2.
