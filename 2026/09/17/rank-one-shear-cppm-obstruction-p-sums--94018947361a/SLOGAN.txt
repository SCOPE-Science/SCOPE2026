For every \(1\le p\le\infty\), every nonzero Banach space \(Z\), and every non-reflexive \(Y\), the diagonal sum \(X=Z\oplus_pY\) fails CPPm via an explicit rank-one nilpotent shear. The example has exact compact-perturbation envelope \(1\), minimum modulus \(\kappa_p(c)^{-1}\), and an arbitrarily large defect ratio \(\kappa_p(c)\), extending the known scalar \(\ell_\infty\)-sum obstruction to all \(p\).
