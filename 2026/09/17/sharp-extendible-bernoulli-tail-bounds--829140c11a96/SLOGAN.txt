Infinite extendibility gives an exact, strictly smaller finite-sample tail interval for exchangeable Bernoulli sums: the upper envelope is the least concave majorant of the binomial tail and has a single tangent-chord breakpoint. At proportional thresholds, however, the worst-case interval converges back to the sharp Markov-type finite-exchangeable limits, so infinite exchangeability plus only the marginal mean does not force concentration.
