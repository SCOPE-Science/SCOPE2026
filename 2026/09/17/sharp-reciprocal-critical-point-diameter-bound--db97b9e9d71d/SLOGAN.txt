For a degree-N polynomial with collinear zeros of diameter D, every zero a satisfies sum |a-zeta|^{-lambda} >= (N-1)(2/D)^lambda for all lambda >= 1, where zeta ranges over critical points. The exponent-1 bound is sharp exactly for an endpoint zero opposite an (N-1)-fold endpoint cluster, settling the Tang--Zhang reciprocal-distance conjecture for collinear zeros.
