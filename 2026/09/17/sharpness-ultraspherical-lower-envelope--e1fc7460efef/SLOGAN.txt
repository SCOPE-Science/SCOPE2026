The Castillo--Sadigova interval-by-interval lower-envelope theorem for normalized ultraspherical polynomials is sharp in its parameter: within the full Jacobi range alpha>-1, it holds exactly for alpha>=0. For -1/2<alpha<0 a degree-5 polynomial already beats the proposed degree-1 envelope at the first transition point; at alpha=-1/2 a Chebyshev counterexample appears, while for -1<alpha<-1/2 the normalized family is unbounded below at x=0.
