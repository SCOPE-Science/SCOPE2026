For the Laustsen--Wirzenius direct sums of Baernstein, Schreier and ell_p spaces, the sharp nilpotency index of strictly singular operators modulo compacts is attained by powers of one operator: there is a strictly singular T with T^(k+1)=0 but T^k non-compact. In particular, every B_p and every p-convexified Schreier space S_p has a non-compact strictly singular square-zero operator.
