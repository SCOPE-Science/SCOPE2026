Stable exceptions to exact-j positive k-th-power representations are the gaps of the numerical semigroup <a^k-1 : a>=2>. This exposes a p=3 counterexample to Benfield--Lippard Theorem 10.7, repairs the theorem for every odd prime p>=5 (preserving its p=11 corollary), and extends the modular lower-bound mechanism to prime-power moduli via the Carmichael exponent.
