## correctness

PASS

Aoki's full primary PDF was inspected and confirms the exact definitions: W(S,C) is the interval family satisfying both extremal boundary conditions; a pair is saturated exactly when W(S,C) is a singleton; bar-omega is |Max(C\S)|+|Min(S\C)|; and Theorem 6.5 gives gldim Lambda_P as the maximum bar-omega over saturated pairs. For a star, every interval is a singleton leaf or a center-containing subset. The submitted case split gives bar-omega<=r for r>=2. Taking S=lower leaves plus center and C=center plus upper leaves has S union C=P; Aoki's Proposition 6.1 says W has minimum S union C when nonempty, so the exhibited P forces W={P}, and bar-omega=r. The r=1 case gives 2. The maximum-degree corollary uses the full subposet on a vertex and its Hasse neighbors, which is itself a star, and Aoki–Escolar–Tada Theorem 4.1 supplies monotonicity.
