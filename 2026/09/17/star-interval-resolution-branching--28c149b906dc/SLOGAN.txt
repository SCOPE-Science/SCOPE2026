For every orientation of the star Hasse graph K_{1,r}, the interval endomorphism algebra has global dimension max{2,r}, so interval-resolution global dimension is max{0,r-2}. Consequently every finite poset P satisfies int-res-gldim(P) >= max{0, Delta(P)-2}; the bound is sharp on stars and is unbounded even though star incidence algebras are hereditary.
