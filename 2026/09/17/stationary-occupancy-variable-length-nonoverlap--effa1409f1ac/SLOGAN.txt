A variable-length non-overlapping code satisfies a stationary occupancy inequality: for every stationary source, the sum of codeword cylinder probabilities weighted by codeword length is at most one.  Under a uniform q-ary source this gives a weighted Kraft inequality and forces average length L to satisfy q^(L+1) >= |S|L for all q>=2, with q^L >= |S|L for q>=3.
