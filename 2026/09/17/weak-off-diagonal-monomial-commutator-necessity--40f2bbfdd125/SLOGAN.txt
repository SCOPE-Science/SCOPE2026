Weak \(L^p\to L^{q,\infty}\) boundedness of a monomial-curve Hilbert-transform commutator in any dimension forces the sharp anisotropic Campanato condition \(b\in\operatorname{BMO}^{\gamma,\alpha}\), with \(\alpha/|\beta|=1/p-1/q\). Combined with the known strong sufficiency range, this gives strong/weak boundedness equivalence there and extends the boundedness-necessity side of the previously plane-only theory to all dimensions.
