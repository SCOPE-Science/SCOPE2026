The conjectured weighted-cosine bound ||C_Σ(U,V)||_F^2 <= n^2/γ fails already in dimension three: an exact symmetric example exceeds it by 41/520, and a fixed orthogonal frame violates it for every spectrum diag(t,1,t^-1) with t >= 3/2. The bound is nevertheless valid for all n <= 2, making dimension three minimal, and an embedded order-four example shows the failure persists even when a real Hadamard frame exists.
