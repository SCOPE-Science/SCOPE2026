# Accelerated filtrations break arbitrary-filtration entropy–growth implications

## Statement

Let \(A\) be an infinite-dimensional affine unital algebra over a field \(k\), and let \(U\subset A\) be a finite-dimensional generating subspace containing \(1\). For an integer \(b\ge 2\), put
\[
V_0=0,\qquad V_n=U^{\,b^n}\quad(n\ge 1),
\]
where \(U^r\) denotes the span of products of \(r\) elements of \(U\). Since \(1\in U\), the spaces \(U^r\) are increasing.

**Theorem A (universal entropy acceleration).**  
The sequence \(\mathcal F_b=\{V_n\}_{n\ge0}\) is an exhaustive finite-dimensional algebra filtration, and
\[
h_{\rm alg}(A,\mathcal F_b)\ge \log b.
\]
Consequently, every infinite-dimensional affine algebra admits finite-dimensional filtrations with arbitrarily large positive lower bounds for algebraic entropy, independently of its intrinsic growth type.

For the polynomial algebra the value is exact.

**Corollary A.1.** For \(A=k[x]\), with
\[
V_0=0,\qquad
V_n=\operatorname{span}_k\{1,x,\ldots,x^{b^n}\},
\]
one has
\[
h_{\rm alg}(k[x],\mathcal F_b)=\log b,
\qquad
\operatorname{GKdim}k[x]=1,
\]
while \(k[x]\) has polynomial, not exponential, intrinsic growth.

Thus, for arbitrary finite-dimensional filtrations, positive algebraic entropy does **not** imply infinite Gelfand–Kirillov dimension or exponential intrinsic growth.

## Proof of Theorem A

For \(n,m\ge1\),
\[
V_nV_m\subseteq U^{b^n+b^m}\subseteq U^{b^{n+m}}=V_{n+m},
\]
because \(b^n+b^m\le b^{n+m}\) for \(b\ge2\). Products involving \(V_0=0\) are trivial. Each \(V_n\) is finite-dimensional, and
\[
\bigcup_{n\ge1}U^{b^n}=A,
\]
so \(\mathcal F_b\) is an exhaustive finite-dimensional filtration.

Because \(A\) is infinite-dimensional, the chain
\[
U\subset U^2\subset U^3\subset\cdots
\]
is strictly increasing at every step. Indeed, if \(U^r=U^{r+1}\) for some \(r\), then
\(U^rU=U^r\), hence \(U^s=U^r\) for all \(s\ge r\), and the algebra generated by \(U\) would be the finite-dimensional space \(U^r\).

It follows that, for \(n\ge2\),
\[
\dim(V_n/V_{n-1})
=\dim U^{b^n}-\dim U^{b^{n-1}}
\ge b^n-b^{n-1}.
\]
Therefore
\[
h_{\rm alg}(A,\mathcal F_b)
=\limsup_{n\to\infty}
\frac{\log\dim(V_n/V_{n-1})}{n}
\ge \log b.
\]
This proves the theorem.

For \(k[x]\),
\[
\dim(V_n/V_{n-1})=b^n-b^{n-1}\qquad(n\ge2),
\]
so the same limit is exactly \(\log b\).

## Consequences for arXiv:2609.18144v1

Schwarz and Sebandal, *Growth functions of algebras and an application to Leavitt path algebras* (arXiv:2609.18144v1, submitted 16 September 2026), allow arbitrary finite-dimensional filtrations in Definition 2.7 and state, among other claims:

- Theorem 3.7: for a filtered algebra with a finite-dimensional filtration, the intrinsic growth of \(A\), the growth of the associated graded algebra, and the growth of \(n\mapsto\dim V_n\) agree;
- Theorem 3.9: positive algebraic entropy forces \(\operatorname{GKdim}A=\infty\);
- Theorem 3.14: positive algebraic entropy forces exponential intrinsic growth.

The filtration above on \(k[x]\) gives
\[
\dim V_n=b^n+1,
\]
which is exponential in the filtration index, whereas the intrinsic growth of \(k[x]\) is linear. Hence Theorem 3.7 is false as stated, and the same example directly contradicts Theorems 3.9 and 3.14.

The same mechanism also invalidates any arbitrary-filtration conclusion excluding intermediate growth. In particular, let \(A\) be any affine algebra of intermediate growth, such as the classical two-generated Smith example coming from a universal enveloping algebra with subexponential but non-polynomial growth. The accelerated filtration \(\mathcal F_2\) has
\[
h_{\rm alg}(A,\mathcal F_2)\ge\log2>0,
\]
while the underlying algebra remains of intermediate growth.

There is an analogous module counterexample. Regard \(M=A=k[x]\) as its regular left module and equip it with the same accelerated filtration. Then \(M\) is finitely generated and has positive filtered-module entropy, while
\[
\operatorname{GKdim}_A M=1.
\]
Thus an arbitrary-filtration module statement asserting that positive entropy forces infinite module GK dimension also requires an additional comparability hypothesis.

These counterexamples do not assert that results proved specifically for standard, natural, or otherwise linearly controlled filtrations are false.

## What remains true for an arbitrary exhaustive filtration

Let \(A\) be affine, let \(U\) be a finite-dimensional generating subspace containing \(1\), and write
\[
D_n=U^n.
\]
For any exhaustive algebra filtration \(\mathcal F=\{V_n\}\), finite-dimensional or not, the finiteness of \(U\) and exhaustivity give a constant \(s\) such that \(U\subseteq V_s\). Hence
\[
D_n=U^n\subseteq V_{sn}.
\]

**Proposition B (one-sided growth comparison).**  
For every finite-dimensional exhaustive filtration,
\[
\mathcal G(A)\le \mathcal G\bigl(n\mapsto\dim V_n\bigr),
\]
and therefore
\[
\operatorname{GKdim}A
\le
\limsup_{n\to\infty}\log_n\dim V_n.
\]
The reverse inequality need not hold.

The accelerated filtration on \(k[x]\) shows that this one-sided statement is sharp: its right-hand growth is exponential while the left-hand growth is polynomial.

## A sufficient repair: linear control by a standard filtration

The missing reverse comparison follows from a simple coarse linear bound.

**Proposition C (linear-control repair).**  
Assume that for some integers \(a\ge1\), \(c\ge0\),
\[
V_n\subseteq U^{an+c}\qquad(n\gg0).
\]
Then
\[
\mathcal G\bigl(n\mapsto\dim V_n\bigr)=\mathcal G(A)
\]
and
\[
\operatorname{GKdim}A
=
\limsup_{n\to\infty}\log_n\dim V_n.
\]
Moreover, if \(h_{\rm alg}(A,\mathcal F)>0\), then \(A\) has exponential intrinsic growth.

### Proof

The inclusion \(U^n\subseteq V_{sn}\) from exhaustivity gives one growth inequality, and
\[
V_n\subseteq U^{an+c}
\]
gives the reverse one.

For the entropy implication, set \(d(r)=\dim U^r\). If \(h_{\rm alg}>0\), choose
\(0<\eta<h_{\rm alg}\). For infinitely many \(n\),
\[
\dim(V_n/V_{n-1})\ge e^{\eta n}.
\]
Therefore
\[
d(an+c)\ge e^{\eta n}
\]
along an infinite sequence. Since multiplication induces a surjection
\[
U^r\otimes U^s\longrightarrow U^{r+s},
\]
the sequence \(\log d(r)\) is subadditive, so
\[
\lim_{r\to\infty}\frac{\log d(r)}r
\]
exists. The displayed lower bounds force this limit to be positive. Thus \(d(r)\) is bounded below exponentially for all sufficiently large \(r\), while finite generation gives an exponential upper bound; hence \(\mathcal G(A)=\mathcal E_1\).

A module version needs only the upper control. If \(M\) is generated by a finite-dimensional subspace \(F\) and a filtered module has pieces \(\Omega_n\) satisfying
\[
\Omega_n\subseteq U^{an+c}F\qquad(n\gg0),
\]
then positive filtered-module entropy forces
\[
\operatorname{GKdim}_A M=\infty.
\]
Indeed, exponentially large filtration quotients along a subsequence give exponentially large values of \(\dim U^rF\) along a linearly related subsequence, which makes the GK-dimension limsup infinite.

## Relation to prior literature

Bock, Gil Canto, Martín Barquero, Martín González, Ruiz Campos and Sebandal (2024) already emphasize that algebraic entropy depends on the filtration. Their Proposition 3.2 shows that the linear reindexing \(W_n=V_{kn}\) multiplies entropy by \(k\), and Remark 3.3 notes that a nonzero finite entropy can thereby be made arbitrarily large. They also prove preservation of zero entropy for several restricted changes of filtration, including linear reindexings and standard filtrations.

The present observation is different: exponential reindexing
\[
V_n=U^{b^n}
\]
can create positive entropy even when the standard filtration has entropy zero. In particular, \(k[x]\) has zero entropy for its standard filtration but entropy \(\log b\) for the accelerated filtration above.

The filtered–graded GK-dimension identity traditionally used in this area is normally stated with additional finite-generation or good-filtration hypotheses on the associated graded objects. A published secondary use of Krause–Lenagan, Proposition 6.6, for example explicitly assumes that \(\operatorname{gr}(A)\) is finitely generated. The self-contained counterexamples above do not depend on resolving the exact bibliographic formulation of that proposition.

## Limitations and scope

The correction concerns claims formulated for arbitrary finite-dimensional or quotient-finite filtrations. It does not by itself invalidate statements whose hypotheses fix a standard or natural filtration, nor statements proved independently in the graded setting.

Originality is asserted only to the best of our knowledge. Filtration dependence of algebraic entropy is prior work and is not claimed as new. The claimed contribution is the universal accelerated-filtration obstruction, the explicit \(k[x]\) contradiction to the arbitrary-filtration growth/GK implications in arXiv:2609.18144v1, and the one-sided/linearly-controlled repair above.

Because arXiv:2609.18144 is a very recent v1, a subsequent author revision or concurrent correction may supersede this observation.
