For the anisotropic metric d_{p,kappa}((x,t),(y,s))=(|x-y|^p+|t-s|^{p/kappa})^{1/p} with kappa>=1, strong Besicovitch covering holds exactly when p>=kappa; below the threshold even weak BCP fails. The sharp boundary is the convexity transition p/kappa=1, extending the known kappa=2 parabolic classification to every anisotropy exponent.
