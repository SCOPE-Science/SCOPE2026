For connected simple graphs \(G,H\), the full-set Nash--Williams ceiling gives the exact arboricity of \(G*H\) whenever \(K\le2\), or \(K=3\) with total cycle rank at most \(7\), or \(K\ge4\) with total cycle rank at most \((K-1)(K-2)+1\). In particular, total cycle rank \(7\) is a sharp universal threshold: at cycle rank \(8\) a cone example has full-set ceiling \(3\) but arboricity \(4\).
