Minimum prefix-free graph construction remains NP-complete over the binary alphabet: the synchronized code 001 h(e(a)) 10 with h(0)=10, h(1)=11 and guard 1^k satisfies the exact Baláž--Popa reduction hypotheses. The hard binary instances need only k=O(log N), while every fixed k is polynomial over a binary alphabet by the existing O(2^q N) algorithm.
