For the backward-shift orbit formula \(L_g f=\sum_k\beta_k(S^*)^kg\), the exact everywhere-defined threshold is \(g\in\mathrm{BMOA}\), with compactness exactly at \(\mathrm{VMOA}\). The explicit \(H^2\) symbol \(g(z)=\sum_{m\ge0}(m+1)^{-3/4}z^m\) contradicts Lemma 2.1 of arXiv:2609.19311v1 and yields finite-section growth \(\|L_g f_N\|\gtrsim N^{1/4}\).
