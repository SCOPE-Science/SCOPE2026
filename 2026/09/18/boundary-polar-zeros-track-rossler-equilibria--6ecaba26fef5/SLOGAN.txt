Two of the three simple averaged zeros used in arXiv:2609.17336v1 lie at zero polar radius and are exactly the blown-up limits of the two equilibrium branches created by the same Rössler unfolding. The angular reparametrization is singular there, so the cited first-order averaging theorem certifies only the positive-radius zero; the source's numerical seeds for the two boundary labels have scaled radius 1 rather than 0.
