Chair44's 2-adic hierarchy phase sharply constrains Euclidean symmetry: a nontrivial cyclic symmetry is possible only on a dense Haar-null phase set of 2-adic Hausdorff dimension 1, while every noncyclic symmetry forces an ordinary integral phase. Consequently all symmetry groups of order greater than 4, including any hypothetical order-24 example, reduce up to translation to the zero-phase fiber; invariant random Chair44 tilings are almost surely asymmetric.
