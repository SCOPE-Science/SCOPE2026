Concavity plus a square-root envelope is enough to force strict comparison for one-sided and symmetric cusp diffusions via Larsen's two-variable criterion. The logarithmically damped family sqrt(r)/log(e/r)^gamma has strict comparison for every gamma>=0, while its derivative enters local L2 exactly at gamma>1/2 and the classical Yamada-Ogura strict-comparison modulus test fails throughout.
