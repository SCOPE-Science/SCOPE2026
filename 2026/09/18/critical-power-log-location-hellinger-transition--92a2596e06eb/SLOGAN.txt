At the critical power boundary α=1, logarithmic endpoint damping creates a second location-information phase transition: the Hellinger modulus has a log correction for κ<1, a log-log correction at κ=1, and ordinary quadratic Fisher behavior only for κ>1. This yields location-rate orders n^{-1/2}(log n)^{-(1-κ)/2}, (n log log n)^{-1/2}, and n^{-1/2}, respectively.
