# Primary-source comparison and threshold notation

The comparison is to Caro, Skrekovski and Zarb, *Spreads of degrees in graphs*, arXiv:2609.19762v1, Section 2 and Problem 22.

Section 2.3 supplies an explicit sufficient threshold
\[
\max\{(2d+5h+1)^2/(4\delta),\;(2(d-\delta)/h+3)^2\}.
\]
At \(\delta=1\), \(k=1\), hence \(h=2\), it is quadratic in \(d\), not \(2d+1\). Problem 22 separately asks for the **smallest** onset threshold and suggests a linear scale. In this record, the onset parameter means that smallest threshold in the admissible-order sense of Problem 22, not the particular sufficient number in the proof of Theorem 13. Writing this minimum as \(n_*\) removes any ambiguity between the two usages of \(n_0\).

The weighted-window bound, packed sequences and attainability criterion in Section 2.2 do not alone supply graphicality at every admissible order \(n\ge 2d+1\). This record supplies the degree-sequence constructions and their graphicality proof, together with the obstruction at \(n=2d\). No claim is made that the general sufficient threshold is false or that the parent paper's notation is intrinsically erroneous.
