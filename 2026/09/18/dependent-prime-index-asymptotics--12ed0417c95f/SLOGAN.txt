Finite changes to the one-generator prime-index set have an exact transfer law: each deleted prime contributes a factor (log x)^(-1/2) and each added prime contributes (log x)^(1/2), with an explicit constant. Consequently every multiplicatively dependent pair r=m^u, s=m^v omitted from the recent two-generator theorem has a strong asymptotic governed by the numerical-semigroup genus (u-1)(v-1)/2.
