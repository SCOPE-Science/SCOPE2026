For an i.i.d. random string with largest symbol mass p_*<1, total distinct-substring complexity satisfies Var(D_n)=O_{p_*}(n(log n)^4) and D_n-E D_n=O_P(sqrt(n)(log n)^2). The mechanism is a high-probability O(log^2 n) Hamming Lipschitz constant obtained by truncating at the longest repeated substring.
