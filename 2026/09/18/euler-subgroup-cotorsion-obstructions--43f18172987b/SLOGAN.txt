Euler characteristic, not parity itself, is the mechanism behind the new ideal/object cotorsion gap: every proper subgroup \(H<\mathbb Z\) yields a complete ideal cotorsion pair whose associated object pair is incomplete, with two exact obstructions in \(\mathbb Z/H\). Total-cohomology divisibility is genuinely parity-specific: its full subcategory is extension closed only for moduli \(1\) and \(2\).
