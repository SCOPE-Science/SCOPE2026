The Hopf-Langford system of arXiv:2609.18010 has an exact planar autonomous amplitude reduction with weighted divergence D s^(gamma-1), where D=(1+2 gamma)mu-2 gamma alpha. Off D=0 no local invariant torus can surround the circular orbit; on D=0 a prior-integrable center is surrounded by a continuum of neutral tori, so the claimed unique Neimark-Sacker torus and its perturbative bifurcation curve are incompatible with the exact dynamics.
