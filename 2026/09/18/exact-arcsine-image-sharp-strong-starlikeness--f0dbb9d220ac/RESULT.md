# Exact normalized-arcsine image and sharp strong-starlikeness

## Theorem

Let
\[
\phi(z)=1+\frac{2}{\pi}\arcsin z,\qquad z\in\mathbb D,
\]
with the principal branch, and write \(w=x+iy\). Then
\[
\boxed{\phi(\mathbb D)=
\left\{x+iy:0<x<2,\ 
\sinh^2\!\left(\frac{\pi y}{2}\right)
<
\sin^2\!\left(\frac{\pi x}{2}\right)\right\}.}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
|y|<
\frac2\pi\operatorname{arsinh}\!\left(\sin\frac{\pi x}{2}\right),
\qquad 0<x<2.
\]

Thus the image is a bounded convex leaf, not the full strip
\(\{0<\Re w<2\}\). It has sharp half-height
\[
\sup_{\phi(\mathbb D)}|\Im w|=\frac2\pi\operatorname{arsinh}1,
\]
and exact area
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Area}(\phi(\mathbb D))=\frac{16G}{\pi^2}},
\]
where \(G=\int_0^1\arctan t\,dt/t\) is Catalan's constant.

Moreover,
\[
\boxed{\phi(\mathbb D)\subset\{w:|\arg w|<\pi/4\}},
\]
and \(\pi/4\) is the smallest possible sector half-angle. Consequently the
Ma--Minda class
\[
\mathcal S^*_{\arcsin}
=\left\{f:\frac{zf'(z)}{f(z)}\prec\phi(z)\right\}
\]
satisfies the sharp inclusion
\[
\boxed{\mathcal S^*_{\arcsin}\subset\mathcal{SS}^*(1/2)}.
\]

## Proof

Put
\[
\zeta=\arcsin z=u+iv.
\]
For the principal branch on \(\mathbb D\), \(|u|<\pi/2\) and \(z=\sin\zeta\). Since
\[
|\sin(u+iv)|^2=\sin^2u+\sinh^2v,
\]
the condition \(|z|<1\) is exactly
\[
\sin^2u+\sinh^2v<1
\]
inside the principal strip. If
\[
w=1+\frac2\pi\zeta=x+iy,
\]
then
\[
u=\frac\pi2(x-1),\qquad v=\frac\pi2y.
\]
Substitution gives
\[
0<x<2,\qquad
\sinh^2\frac{\pi y}{2}<\sin^2\frac{\pi x}{2}.
\]
Conversely, these inequalities put
\(\zeta=\frac\pi2(w-1)\) in the principal strip with
\(|\sin\zeta|<1\), so \(z=\sin\zeta\in\mathbb D\) and
\(\arcsin z=\zeta\). This proves the exact image formula.

Convexity follows independently from
\[
1+\frac{z\phi''(z)}{\phi'(z)}
=\frac1{1-z^2},
\qquad
\Re\frac1{1-z^2}>\frac12.
\]

The vertical width at \(x\) is
\[
\frac4\pi\operatorname{arsinh}\!\left(\sin\frac{\pi x}{2}\right).
\]
Hence, with
\[
I(a)=\int_0^{\pi/2}\operatorname{arsinh}(a\sin t)\,dt,
\]
\[
\operatorname{Area}(\phi(\mathbb D))
=\frac{16}{\pi^2}I(1).
\]
Differentiating under the integral,
\[
I'(a)=
\int_0^{\pi/2}\frac{\sin t}{\sqrt{1+a^2\sin^2t}}\,dt
=\frac{\arctan a}{a}.
\]
Since \(I(0)=0\), \(I(1)=G\), proving the area formula.

For \(0<x\le1\),
\[
|y|
<
\frac2\pi\operatorname{arsinh}\!\left(\sin\frac{\pi x}{2}\right)
<
\frac2\pi\sin\frac{\pi x}{2}
<x.
\]
For \(1\le x<2\),
\[
|y|<\frac2\pi\operatorname{arsinh}1<1\le x.
\]
Therefore \(|\arg w|<\pi/4\). If
\[
Y(x)=\frac2\pi\operatorname{arsinh}\!\left(\sin\frac{\pi x}{2}\right),
\]
then \(Y(x)/x\to1\) as \(x\downarrow0\). Interior points can approach the
upper and lower boundary cusps, so their arguments approach
\(\pm\pi/4\); the sector is sharp.

Subordination now gives
\[
\left|\arg\frac{zf'(z)}{f(z)}\right|<\frac\pi4
\]
for every \(f\in\mathcal S^*_{\arcsin}\), which is strong starlikeness of
order \(1/2\). Sharpness for the whole class follows from
\[
f_0(z)=z\exp\!\left(\frac2\pi\int_0^z\frac{\arcsin t}{t}\,dt\right),
\qquad
\frac{zf_0'(z)}{f_0(z)}=\phi(z),
\]
because the right-hand side has arguments arbitrarily close to
\(\pm\pi/4\).

## Relation to recent literature

arXiv:2609.20288v1 introduces \(\mathcal S^*_{\arcsin}\) and, immediately
before Definition 1.1, identifies \(\phi(\mathbb D)\) with the full vertical
strip \(0<\Re w<2\). The exact calculation above shows that the strip is only
a containing strip. The paper's displayed image is consistent with the bounded
leaf obtained here.

This correction does not by itself invalidate the coefficient and Hankel
determinant estimates later in that preprint; no dependence of those proofs on
the false strip equality was found.

The related arXiv:2607.27293v2 studies a companion convex class generated by
the same \(\phi\). Its abstract and revision metadata were inspected, but its
full text was not inspected. Accordingly, no originality claim concerning the
companion convex class is made here.

## Limitations and originality scope

The sine/inverse-sine conformal identities used in the proof are classical.
The originality claim is limited, to the best of our knowledge, to the
correction and exact image package as applied to this recently studied
normalized-arcsine Ma--Minda class, together with the sharp strong-starlikeness
consequence. The result does not re-audit all coefficient or determinant
theorems of arXiv:2609.20288v1.
