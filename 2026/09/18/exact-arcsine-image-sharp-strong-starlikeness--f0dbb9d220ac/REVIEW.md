## Correctness

**PASS.**

The exact image follows by writing \(\zeta=\arcsin z=u+iv\) and using
\[
|\sin(u+iv)|^2=\sin^2u+\sinh^2v.
\]
Inside the principal strip \(|u|<\pi/2\), the condition \(|z|<1\) is therefore exactly
\(\sin^2u+\sinh^2v<1\). The affine normalization
\(w=1+2\zeta/\pi=x+iy\) converts this to
\[
0<x<2,\qquad
\sinh^2(\pi y/2)<\sin^2(\pi x/2).
\]
The converse is valid because a point satisfying these inequalities lies in the principal inverse-sine strip and maps under sine into \(\mathbb D\).

The strip assertion in arXiv:2609.20288v1 is contradicted directly by the finite vertical bound
\[
|\Im w|<(2/\pi)\operatorname{arsinh}1.
\]
Convexity is independently checked by
\[
1+z\phi''/\phi'=1/(1-z^2),
\]
whose real part exceeds \(1/2\) in the unit disk.

The area calculation was checked by differentiating
\[
I(a)=\int_0^{\pi/2}\operatorname{arsinh}(a\sin t)\,dt,
\]
which gives \(I'(a)=\arctan(a)/a\), hence \(I(1)=G\). The sector estimate reduces to the elementary strict inequalities
\(\operatorname{arsinh}s<s\) and \(\sin t<t\), while sharpness follows from the boundary ratio \(Y(x)/x\to1\) as \(x\downarrow0\).

For the class consequence, subordination places \(zf'/f\) inside the exact image, so \(|\arg(zf'/f)|<\pi/4\). The canonical function satisfying \(zf_0'/f_0=\phi\) belongs to the class and realizes arguments arbitrarily close to the sector boundary, proving that order \(1/2\) cannot be lowered.

No numerical experiment is needed for the proof.

## Originality

**PASS, to the best of our knowledge.**

The most relevant source, arXiv:2609.20288v1, was inspected in full-text form. Immediately before Definition 1.1 it identifies \(\phi(\mathbb D)\) with the full vertical strip \(0<\Re w<2\). Searches within that paper found no sectorial description, no strong-starlikeness consequence for \(\mathcal S^*_{\arcsin}\), and no Catalan-constant area formula.

Literature searches used exact and synonymous formulations including normalized arcsine mapping, arcsine Ma--Minda class, leaf-shaped starlike domain, strongly starlike, sectorial inclusion, image of the unit disk under arcsine, and the source identifier arXiv:2609.20288. No prior source giving the same exact image formula together with the sharp order-\(1/2\) class inclusion was located.

Classical references on sine and inverse-sine conformal mapping, including DLMF §4.15, make the elementary inverse-map identity prior knowledge. This record does not claim novelty for the identity \(|\sin(u+iv)|^2=\sin^2u+\sinh^2v\), for Catalan's constant, or for the definition of strong starlikeness. The claimed contribution is the correction and exact geometric characterization as applied to this newly studied normalized-arcsine Ma--Minda class, including the sharp sector consequence.

A related preprint, arXiv:2607.27293v2, studies the companion convex class generated by the same \(\phi\). Its abstract and revision metadata were inspected, but its full text was not accessible for theorem-level checking. It is the source most plausibly capable of containing overlapping geometric observations. No search result exposed the exact image formula or the strong-starlikeness conclusion. This leaves a concrete residual originality risk, and no claim concerning the companion convex class is made.

The current SCOPE archive was checked by source identifier, normalized-arcsine terminology, strong-starlikeness terminology, and Catalan/image keywords. No overlapping record was found.

## Value

**PASS.**

The source paper's geometric identification is not a cosmetic description: replacing an unbounded strip by the exact bounded leaf changes the strongest immediate geometric information carried by the defining subordination. The exact domain yields a sharp sector half-angle \(\pi/4\), which in turn gives an exact strong-starlikeness order for the entire newly introduced class. The area \(16G/\pi^2\), finite height, and convexity provide independent geometric invariants and checks of the corrected image.
