For fixed k>1, the number of partitions with eventual weighted-representation difference c has the exact asymptotic C_k 2^T/T^{k/2}, where C_k=2^k(2k/pi)^{k/2}/sqrt(k+3), independently of fixed c. More generally, if c_T/sqrt(T) tends to gamma, the same constant acquires the Gaussian factor exp(-2k^2 gamma^2/(k+3)).
