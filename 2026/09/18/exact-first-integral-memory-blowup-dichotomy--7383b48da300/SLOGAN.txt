For the exponential-memory F2 model in arXiv:2609.15470v1, the initial memory value v(0) produces an exact trichotomy: v(0)<1 gives positive logarithmic blow-up, v(0)>1 gives negative logarithmic blow-down, and v(0)=1 is singular at the initial time. An exact first integral also fixes the leading constants to A3=sqrt(2/alpha) and A4=1/sqrt(2 alpha) and yields the first subleading terms.
