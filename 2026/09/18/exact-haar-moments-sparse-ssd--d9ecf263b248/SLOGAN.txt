Exact Haar second moments sharpen the new sparse-SSD analysis: for f(x)=g(Rx), classical Haar SSD has an unrestricted stationarity rate 2Lnθ/(dN), with θ=[d+2+(s-1)(n-d)/(n-1)]/(n+2). This removes the source theorem's sampling-dimension restrictions, improves its coefficient by at least 16x wherever that theorem applies, and is sharp on rank-one quadratics.
