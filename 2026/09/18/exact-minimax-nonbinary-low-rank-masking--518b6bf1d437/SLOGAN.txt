Uniform exact-rank masks attain the exact minimax maximal-correlation leakage under a rank constraint for every nonbinary finite field, and more generally for all rectangular bilinear-form spaces except the binary square case. The optimum is (q^(d+e-r)-q^d-q^e+1)/((q^d-1)(q^e-1)), even when secret invertible left/right transformations are allowed.
