On the a=0 trigonometric Nosé–Hoover axis, each regular energy torus is exactly conjugate to a linear Kronecker flow with rotation number rho_b(h)=b(1-h)/Omega(h). On 0<h<2 this rotation is strictly monotone, so periodic energy tori form a countable dense resonance set while irrational tori have full measure; any continuous first integral on a full regular annulus factors through H, making the source's elliptic second integrals necessarily branchwise rather than global.
