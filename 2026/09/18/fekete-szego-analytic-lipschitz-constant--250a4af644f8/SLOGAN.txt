The universal analytic-Lipschitz/inner-metric comparison on simply connected plane domains admits the explicit lower constant \(c_0>6.49\times10^{-4}\), improving the recently stated \(1.29\times10^{-42}\) scale by more than \(5\times10^{38}\). The gain comes from a sharp Fekete–Szegő bound for the second derivative of the pre-Schwarzian logarithm and a sharper Gaussian strip estimate.
