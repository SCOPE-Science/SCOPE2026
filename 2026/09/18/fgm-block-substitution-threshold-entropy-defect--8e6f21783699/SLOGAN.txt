Directly substituting an m-dimensional independence copula into one argument of a bivariate FGM copula is valid exactly when $|\theta|\le(2^m-1)^{-1}$; the correct mixed density is generated by the Euler operator $(1+u\partial_u)^{m-1}$, not the ordinary scalar chain rule. Even strictly inside this valid range, copula entropy is nonadditive by an explicit positive amount, separating closure failure from entropy failure.
