# Exact FGM obstruction to unrestricted copula block substitution

## Main theorem

Let
\[
\Phi_\theta(u,z)=uz\{1+\theta(1-u)(1-z)\},\qquad |\theta|\le 1,
\]
be the bivariate Farlie--Gumbel--Morgenstern (FGM) copula. For an integer \(m\ge2\), let
\[
\Pi_m(x_1,\ldots,x_m)=\prod_{i=1}^m x_i
\]
be the independence copula, and let the second inner copula be the one-dimensional identity. Define
\[
C_{\theta,m}(x_1,\ldots,x_m,z)
=\Phi_\theta(\Pi_m(x_1,\ldots,x_m),z).
\]
Then \(C_{\theta,m}\) has the correct copula boundary conditions and uniform one-dimensional margins, but
\[
\boxed{
C_{\theta,m}\text{ is an }(m+1)\text{-copula}
\iff
|\theta|\le \frac{1}{2^m-1}.
}
\]
Thus unrestricted substitution of multivariate copulas into the arguments of another copula is not closed on the class of all copulas.

Moreover, throughout the nontrivial valid region
\[
0<|\theta|<\frac1{2^m-1},
\]
the composite is a bounded absolutely continuous copula but its copula entropy satisfies
\[
\boxed{H(C_{\theta,m})<H(\Phi_\theta).}
\]
Since \(H(\Pi_m)=H(\mathrm{id})=0\), entropy is not additive for this substitution even where the substituted function remains a valid finite-entropy copula.

These conclusions give explicit obstructions to Proposition 2.2, Theorem 2.3, Proposition 3.1, Theorem 4.1, and the bounded/\(L^p\) closure assertions of Proposition 5.1 in Lu (2026), *Copula Operad and Copula Entropy*, arXiv:2609.20512.

## Sharp validity threshold

Write
\[
u=\prod_{i=1}^m x_i.
\]
Then
\[
C_{\theta,m}=uz+\theta uz(1-u)(1-z).
\]
Differentiating in all \(m+1\) variables gives the exact density candidate
\[
\boxed{
g_{\theta,m}(x,z)
=1+\theta(1-2z)(1-2^m u).
}
\]
Indeed,
\[
\partial_{x_1}\cdots\partial_{x_m}u=1,
\qquad
\partial_{x_1}\cdots\partial_{x_m}u^2=2^m u.
\]
The factor \((1-2z)(1-2^m u)\) ranges exactly over
\[
[-(2^m-1),\,2^m-1].
\]
Hence the mixed derivative is nonnegative everywhere exactly when
\[
|\theta|(2^m-1)\le1.
\]
Together with the already-correct boundary and marginal identities, this proves sufficiency. If the inequality fails, continuity gives an open set on which the mixed derivative is negative, hence a small rectangle with negative \(C_{\theta,m}\)-volume; this proves necessity.

For \(m=2\), the threshold is \(|\theta|\le1/3\). At \(\theta=1\),
\[
C(x,y,z)=xyz\{1+(1-xy)(1-z)\}
\]
is not a trivariate copula even though all three ingredients are copulas. Its mixed derivative is
\[
1+(1-2z)(1-4xy),
\]
which equals \(-0.792\) at \(x=y=0.9,z=0.1\).

## Why the closure argument fails

If \(V_1,\ldots,V_m\) are independent uniform random variables, then
\[
\Pi_m(V_1,\ldots,V_m)=\prod_{i=1}^m V_i
\]
is not uniform for \(m\ge2\). In fact, for \(0<u<1\),
\[
\Pr\!\left(\prod_{i=1}^mV_i\le u\right)
=u\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}\frac{(-\log u)^j}{j!}.
\]
Thus evaluating a multivariate copula CDF at a cube-uniform vector does not generally yield a uniform scalar. This invalidates the probabilistic step used for the closure claim in Proposition 2.2 of the source paper.

## Correct higher-order chain rule for a product block

The claimed density product also omits higher derivatives generated by a multivariate inner CDF. Let \(\Phi\) be a smooth bivariate outer copula, set
\[
f_z(u)=\partial_z\Phi(u,z),
\qquad
c(u,z)=\partial_u\partial_z\Phi(u,z),
\]
and substitute \(u=\prod_{i=1}^m x_i\). Then
\[
\partial_{x_1}\cdots\partial_{x_m} f_z(u)
=\frac1u(u\partial_u)^m f_z(u).
\]
Using Stirling numbers of the second kind \(S(m,j)\), this becomes
\[
\boxed{
g(x,z)=
\sum_{j=1}^m S(m,j)u^{j-1}\partial_u^{j-1}c(u,z).
}
\]
The proposed Jacobian product retains only the \(j=1\) term. For \(m=2\), the correct formula is
\[
g=c+u\,\partial_uc.
\]
For the FGM density this immediately gives \(1+\theta(1-2z)(1-2^m u)\).

## Strict entropy nonadditivity inside the valid region

Let \(X_1,\ldots,X_m,Z\) be independent uniforms on \([0,1]\), and define
\[
Y_k=2^k\prod_{i=1}^kX_i,
\qquad
B_k=1-Y_k,
\qquad
A=1-2Z.
\]
The composite density is \(1+\theta A B_m\), whereas the outer FGM density has the same representation with \(B_1\).

Because
\[
Y_{k+1}=Y_k(2X_{k+1}),
\qquad
\mathbb E[2X_{k+1}]=1,
\]
we have \(\mathbb E[Y_{k+1}\mid Y_k]=Y_k\). Therefore
\[
B_1\le_{\rm cx}B_m
\]
in convex order. For every \(z\ne1/2\) and nonzero \(\theta\) in the strict validity region, the function
\[
b\mapsto(1+\theta(1-2z)b)\log(1+\theta(1-2z)b)
\]
is strictly convex on the relevant support. Integrating the strict convex-order inequality over \(z\) gives
\[
\int g_{\theta,m}\log g_{\theta,m}
>
\int \phi_\theta\log\phi_\theta,
\]
and hence
\[
H(C_{\theta,m})<H(\Phi_\theta).
\]

For additional quantitative detail, in the strict validity region
\[
H(C_{\theta,m})
=-\sum_{k\ge1}
\frac{\theta^{2k}\mu_{2k,m}}
{(2k-1)(2k)(2k+1)},
\]
where
\[
\mu_{2k,m}
=\sum_{j=0}^{2k}\binom{2k}{j}\frac{(-2^m)^j}{(j+1)^m}.
\]
In particular,
\[
H(C_{\theta,m})
=-\frac{\theta^2}{6}\left[\left(\frac43\right)^m-1\right]+O(\theta^4),
\]
while
\[
H(\Phi_\theta)=-\frac{\theta^2}{18}+O(\theta^4).
\]
For \(m=2\),
\[
H(C_{\theta,2})-H(\Phi_\theta)
=-\frac{2}{27}\theta^2+O(\theta^4).
\]

## The proposed \(L\log L\) counterexample is not a copula example

Theorem 5.2 of the source paper introduces normalized densities
\[
\psi(x,y)=A(xy)^{-a},\qquad 0<a<1.
\]
Normalization over the unit square forces \(A=(1-a)^2\), but then
\[
\int_0^1\psi(x,y)\,dy=(1-a)x^{-a},
\]
which is not identically one. Thus \(\psi\) is not a copula density unless \(a=0\). The displayed construction therefore does not establish non-closure of the \(L\log L\) class. This does not prove that \(L\log L\) is closed; it only shows that the stated counterexample is inadmissible.

## Relation to prior literature and originality boundary

Nested or hierarchical Archimedean copulas are established prior art, and that literature already requires compatibility conditions for nested expressions to define proper copulas. In particular, McNeil's sufficient nesting condition and later work by Hofert and collaborators use complete-monotonicity conditions on generator compositions. The broad message that copula nesting may require restrictions is therefore not new.

The contribution here is narrower: an exact obstruction to the unrestricted block-substitution operation asserted in arXiv:2609.20512, including the sharp FGM threshold \(1/(2^m-1)\), the corrected Euler/Stirling mixed-derivative operator, and strict entropy failure throughout the valid nonzero FGM region. Targeted searches for these exact and equivalent formulations did not locate prior statements. The author's 2024 research report presents the same operadic and entropy-additivity claims, not this obstruction.

## Limitations

This result does not classify all outer/inner copula tuples for which block substitution is valid. The exact threshold is for an FGM outer copula with an independence block and an identity block. The Stirling-operator formula is for a product inner block and a smooth bivariate outer copula, not arbitrary multivariate inner copulas. The defect in the source paper's \(L\log L\) example does not settle whether a different valid \(L\log L\) counterexample exists.

The full text of McNeil's older nested-Archimedean work was not inspected; accessible theorem statements and later expositions confirm that additional nesting conditions are standard. This remains a residual originality risk for a more abstract equivalent formulation, although no evidence was found for the exact FGM threshold or entropy calculation above.
