Finite positive step profiles in the scale-invariant m-fold Euler reduction have an exact similarity law: the rightmost endpoint satisfies t b_n -> 1/c_n, the sector mass satisfies t||g||_1 -> 1, and g(t,y/t) -> c_n 1_(0,1/c_n] in L1. The rescaled Green field converges explicitly, producing the limiting transport velocity y(c_n y-1).
