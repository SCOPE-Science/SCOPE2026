For the shifted-Erlang ENSO model of arXiv:2609.08127v1, the printed scaling \(\widetilde a=a/\alpha=a|G|\) does not compensate Fourier attenuation: it makes the small-signal feedback magnitude \(a\kappa|G|^2\). Exact frequency matching instead requires multiplying by \(|G|^{-1}\), and the phase-equivalent delay is strictly smaller than the mean delay for every nonzero kernel width.
