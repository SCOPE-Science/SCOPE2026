For a finite-dimensional strict Morita context, the prescribed componentwise support-\(\tau\)-tilting torsion class transports to the intersection of the two corner torsion classes, while a support-\(\tau\)-tilting direct induction transports to their join. The bilateral cross conditions are exactly equality of the transported corner classes; for \(M_2(k^n)\), all \(4^n\) ordered corner pairs induce support-\(\tau\)-tilting modules but only \(2^n\) realize the prescribed componentwise class.
