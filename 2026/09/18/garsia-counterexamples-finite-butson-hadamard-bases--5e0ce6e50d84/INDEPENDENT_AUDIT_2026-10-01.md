## Final claim

Garsia counterexamples among finite Butson-Hadamard bases

## Correctness — PASS

Lewko's finite two-ordering ingredients provide a bad \(m\)-term trigonometric block and a permutation \(\sigma\) forcing the prescribed arithmetic-progression order inside either \(	au\) or \(\sigma\circ	au\). Sampling the open bad set on sufficiently fine prime cyclic groups preserves a fixed positive density. The block matrix with columns \(u_n\) and \(v_n\) is exactly orthonormal on \(2M\) atoms by cyclic-character orthogonality and the layer sign; all entries are \(2M\)-th roots. Restricting any full column ordering to the distinguished \(u\)-columns and assigning zeros elsewhere preserves the relevant partial sums, while primeness makes the progression step a permutation of the grid. This establishes the finite complete Butson basis claim.

Checked sources: Lewko, On Kolmogorov's rearrangement problem and Garsia's conjecture, arXiv:2609.18491; Bourgain, On Kolmogorov's rearrangement problem for orthogonal systems and Garsia's conjecture, LNM 1376 (1989); Dutkay–Han–Sun, Divergence of the mock and scrambled Fourier series on fractal measures, arXiv:1103.4380; Resultary 2026-09-17: An explicit iterated-log lower bound for the finite Garsia rearrangement constant
