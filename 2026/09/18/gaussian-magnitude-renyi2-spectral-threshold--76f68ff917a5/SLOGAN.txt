For a centered Gaussian correlation matrix R, the Rényi-2 total correlation of the coordinatewise magnitudes is finite exactly when λ_max(R)<2 and, on that domain, equals an explicit average of 2^n Gaussian determinant cross-integrals over sign conjugates. In equicorrelation this creates a genuinely multivariate L2 phase transition: every pair can have finite magnitude dependence while the joint Rényi-2 total correlation is infinite.
