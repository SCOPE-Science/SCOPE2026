Xiong's all-p>4 Kusner counterexample construction works at every sufficiently large Hadamard order, not only powers of two. Combining dense Hadamard orders with the p=4 stability theorem gives the first explicit onset window for the minimal counterexample dimension: between order 1/((p-4) log(1/(p-4))) and order 1/(p-4), with an explicit 239.2136... upper constant.
