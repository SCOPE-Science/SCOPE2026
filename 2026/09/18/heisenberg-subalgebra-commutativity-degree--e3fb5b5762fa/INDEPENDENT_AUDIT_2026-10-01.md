## Correctness — PASS

The subalgebra classification was reconstructed from the central quotient: center-containing subalgebras are arbitrary inverse images of subspaces, while center-avoiding subalgebras are graphs of functionals over totally isotropic subspaces. For graph pairs the nonpermutability condition is exactly agreement of the functionals on the intersection together with nonorthogonality of the two isotropic spaces. Re-deriving the Möbius inversion count gives the displayed finite-sum formula. Independent exact evaluation reproduced the small-rank values, and a complete enumeration of the five-dimensional algebra over the two-element field reproduced 158 subalgebras, 18964 permutable ordered pairs, and 6000 nonpermutable ordered pairs.

Sources checked: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19086; published archive record SCOPE-20260917-8a9eab50ab21.

Residual risks: The exhaustive computation is finite evidence only; the all-rank theorem rests on the symplectic counting proof.
