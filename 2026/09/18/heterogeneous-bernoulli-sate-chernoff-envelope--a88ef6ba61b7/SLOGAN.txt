For midpoint-differenced SATE estimation under independent heterogeneous Bernoulli assignment, the worst-case two-sided Laplace transform over bounded potential outcomes is exactly a product of centered-Bernoulli MGFs evaluated at the rare-arm probabilities. This yields a computable Chernoff interval with heterogeneous variance-scale dependence and recovers the optimal 1/sqrt(n q) rare-arm order in the equal-propensity limit.
