For the hexahedral face reduction f(s,t)=a(t)s^2+b(t)s+c(t), the reduced vertex value h(t)=c-b^2/(4a) satisfies h''''<=0 wherever a>0. Therefore Zhang's quartic stationarity equation has at most three positive-a roots and at most one interior minimum-capable root; at a simple stationary root, det Hessian = P'/(2a), giving an exact curvature screen that reduces the post-root-solve interior candidate set from at most four per face to at most one.
