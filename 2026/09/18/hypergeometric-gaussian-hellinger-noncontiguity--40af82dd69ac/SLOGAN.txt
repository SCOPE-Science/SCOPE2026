At the Gaussian boundary of the beta-precision hypergeometric family, the squared Hellinger distance is \(H^2(P_a,N(0,1))\sim a/[2\log(1/a)]\), so the iid detection boundary is \(a_n\asymp(\log n)/n\), not \(n^{-1/2}\). The source paper's root-\(n\) hypergeometric alternatives are asymptotically noncontiguous and perfectly separable by extremes, while its CDF-based minimum-distance statistic remains first-order null at the sharper Hellinger-critical scale.
