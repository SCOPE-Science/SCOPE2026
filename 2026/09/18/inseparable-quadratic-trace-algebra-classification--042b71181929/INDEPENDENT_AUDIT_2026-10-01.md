# Independent audit — Inseparable quadratic extensions add a missing family of trace-prime algebras

**Disposition: PASSED.**

## Correctness

**PASS** — For a nonzero \(F\)-linear map \(t:E\to E\) on a quadratic field extension, rank two makes \(t\) surjective and the trace-algebra axiom forces multiplication by \(t(1)\). Rank one gives image \(Fz\); applying the axiom at a nonzero image element forces \(z\in F^\times\), so the map is an arbitrary nonzero functional \(E\to F\). Conversely every such functional satisfies the axiom. In a purely inseparable quadratic extension the field trace is zero, so these functionals are not \(x\mapsto\operatorname{Tr}(cx)\). The explicit \(F_2(s)\subset F_2(s)(u)\), \(u^2=s\), example checks directly.

## Originality

**PASS** — The complete primary preprint arXiv:2609.19797v1 was inspected. Its Lemma 7 explicitly asserts that every rank-one trace has form \(\operatorname{Tr}_{E/F}(cx)\) over an arbitrary field, and its proof divides by a trace quantity that vanishes in the inseparable case; Proposition 11 repeats the resulting incomplete family. Targeted searches found no correction predating this 2026-09-18 record. A near-identical SCOPE correction exists on 2026-09-19, which postdates the audited record.

## Scientific value

**PASS** — The claim repairs a false advertised arbitrary-field classification, gives an exhaustive replacement family, and isolates separability as the exact missing hypothesis while leaving the finite-field results intact. Correcting a classification theorem with a concrete family of counterexamples is a substantive mathematical contribution.
