For the Lu–Zhang modified Van der Pol–Rayleigh system, every positive root of \(\beta m^2-m+\alpha=0\) produces an invariant line \(y=mx\) when \(a=-(m+m^{-1})\), and that line excludes every periodic orbit. In particular \((\alpha,\beta,a)=(1/10,1/10,-10)\) lies inside Conjecture 5.1 but has no limit cycle.
