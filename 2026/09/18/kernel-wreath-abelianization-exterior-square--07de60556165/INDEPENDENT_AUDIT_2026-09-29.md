## Correctness

The group-homological calculation is correct. Coordinatewise abelianization has kernel (R')^T, and explicit compensated commutators place every coordinate copy of R' inside G'. For the abelianized base A, the exact sequence 0 -> K -> A^T -> T -> 0 and Shapiro's lemma give an injection T tensor T -> K_T with cokernel ker(A -> T). Commutators of lifts of T occupy exactly the alternating-tensor subgroup; quotienting by it leaves the exterior-square correction. The invariant-factor identity |T tensor T|=|T| |wedge^2 T|^2 then simplifies the order to |R^ab||T||wedge^2 T|.

### Independent checks

- For |T|>=3, checked the two compensating coordinates used to isolate [r,s] at one chosen coordinate; for |T|=2, checked the common compensator-power construction, whose compensating entries commute.
- Re-derived the low-degree homology sequence for 0->K->A^T->T->0: A^T is the regular induced T-module, so H_1(T,A^T)=0 and H_1(T,T_triv)=T tensor_Z T.

## Originality

PASS, narrowly and to the best of current searchable knowledge. The full 13-page v1 of Damele's September 2026 kernel--wreath preprint was independently retrieved and inspected; it proves the kernel construction, its order, preservation of abelian quotients, diagonal embedding, and iteration, but contains no abelianization, derived-subgroup, tensor-square, exterior-square, or Schur-multiplier formula. Leite--Ocampo's 2026 work determines abelianization for the full permutational wreath pullback H^n semidirect G, a nearby but different construction; it does not itself cover the coupled index-|T| augmentation/top kernel audited here. Standard full-wreath augmentation/abelianization and the homological tools are prior art.

## Scientific value

The formula upgrades qualitative quotient preservation to an exact first-derived-quotient invariant, identifies the noncyclic target's exterior-square/Schur-multiplier contribution, and gives exact abelianization and derived-subgroup growth under iteration. The distinction between cyclic and noncyclic targets is structurally informative for the new kernel--wreath towers.

## Limitations

- The theorem determines only the order of G^ab, not its invariant-factor decomposition or a splitting over R^ab.
- Older group-extension literature may encode the same coupled augmentation-kernel calculation under different terminology; the novelty verdict is explicitly qualified.
- The construction assumes a finite nontrivial abelian target T and a surjection R -> T.
