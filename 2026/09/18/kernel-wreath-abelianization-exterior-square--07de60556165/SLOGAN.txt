For Damele's kernel-wreath group $G=\ker\varepsilon\le R^T\rtimes T$, the abelianization order is $|G^{\mathrm{ab}}|=|R^{\mathrm{ab}}||T||\bigwedge^2T|$. Thus a cyclic $C_p$ step multiplies the multiplicative abelianization order exactly by $p$, while a noncyclic target contributes an additional Schur-multiplier factor.
