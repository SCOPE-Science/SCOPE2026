Huang's new logarithmically monotone counterexamples are completely invisible to Köthe duality: the renormed Marcinkiewicz space and the closed ideal ker Phi have the same associate space as the ambient standard Marcinkiewicz space, and both Köthe bidualize back to that ambient space. In the closed-ideal example the explicit missing unit vector has distance exactly 1 from the ideal, so the defect is norm-large but can only be detected by singular, non-Köthe functionals.
