For the new even Ma–Wang maximal curves, the explicit subgroup of order $(q^n+1)q(q^2-1)$ is exactly both the normalizer and the centralizer of the degree-$(q^n+1)$ Kummer deck group. Unequal multiplicities of the two local Kummer exponent types force every normalizing automorphism to act trivially on the deck group, so any additional full automorphism must move the Kummer structure.
