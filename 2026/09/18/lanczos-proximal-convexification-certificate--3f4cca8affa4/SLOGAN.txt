For PDNQP's stated proximal rule, the shifted curvature is exactly delta*beta minus the Lanczos Ritz overestimate of lambda_min, so a finite Ritz estimate does not certify convexification. For every finite Lanczos budget the paper's delta=0.01 admits an open set of failing starts, while delta>2 is the sharp unconditional safety factor if no information beyond a Ritz value and the infinity-norm bound is used.
