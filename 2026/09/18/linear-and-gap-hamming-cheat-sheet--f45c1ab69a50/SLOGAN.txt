Replacing the quadratic exact-prefix-weight circuit in Wang--Wu's Gap-Hamming cheat sheet by a balanced binary counter reduces the AND count to O(km). The unchanged fully linear PCP and rectangle proof then give total functions with randomized communication O(log N log log N) and monochromatic-rectangle density at most 2^{-Omega(sqrt(N)/log^2 N)}, hence P^{NP^cc}=Omega(sqrt(N)/log^2 N).
