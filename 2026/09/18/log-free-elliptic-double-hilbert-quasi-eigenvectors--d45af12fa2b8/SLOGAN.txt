Every nondegenerate ellipse has an exact double-Hilbert indicator defect determined only by the angle between the two row functionals of its inverse affine map. Diagonal ellipses satisfy \(\|(\mathbf H-I)\chi_{E_\varepsilon}\|_2/\|\chi_{E_\varepsilon}\|_2=\sqrt{(8/\pi)\arctan\varepsilon}\), giving bounded smooth strictly convex log-free quasi-eigenvectors.
