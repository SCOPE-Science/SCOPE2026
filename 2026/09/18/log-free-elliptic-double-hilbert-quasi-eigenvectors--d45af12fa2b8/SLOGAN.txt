Elongated ellipses aligned with an invariant diagonal are exact quantitative indicator quasi-eigenvectors of the double Hilbert transform:
\(\|(\mathbf H-I)\chi_{E_\varepsilon}\|_2/\|\chi_{E_\varepsilon}\|_2=\sqrt{(8/\pi)\arctan\varepsilon}\sim\sqrt{8/\pi}\,\varepsilon^{1/2}\).
For the recent truncated-strip construction, the published \(\sqrt{\varepsilon|\log\varepsilon|}\) upper rate is sharp with leading constant \(2/\pi\), so the logarithm belongs to that truncation rather than to boundedness itself.
