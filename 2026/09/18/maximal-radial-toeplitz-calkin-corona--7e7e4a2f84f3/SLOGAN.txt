For the logarithmic weight \(h(r)=\frac12(\log r)^{3/2}\) outside the unit disk, every bounded degree-diagonal sequence is realized by a bounded radial Toeplitz operator modulo compacts, in every complex dimension. Hence the radial Toeplitz Calkin image is the full \(\ell^\infty/c_0\cong C(\beta\mathbb N_0\setminus\mathbb N_0)\), in sharp contrast with the projectionless square-root-uniform corona of the classical Gaussian Fock space.
