Klech's 2026 maximum-spectral-radius conjecture for strongly connected digraphs with \(m\) vertices and \(m+2\) arcs is true: the unique extremizer is the three-petal directed rose with cycle lengths \(1,2,m-1\), and in the loopless class it is the rose with lengths \(2,2,m-2\). The proof reduces every graph to first-return excursions from one or two branching vertices and compares the resulting Perron equations.
