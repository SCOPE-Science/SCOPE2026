For finitely many nondegenerate signal maxima, the zero-diffusion slow cell-polarization limit localizes on the explicit scale t^{-1/4}: support width is t^{-1/4}, peak height is t^{1/2}, and the Lagrange-multiplier gap is t^{-1/2}. After Hessian rescaling, every peak converges to the universal parabolic cap (1-|y|^2/2)_+, whose integral recovers the determinant weights of the source paper.
