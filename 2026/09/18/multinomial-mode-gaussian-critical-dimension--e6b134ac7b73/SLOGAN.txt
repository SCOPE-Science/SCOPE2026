For an equiprobable multinomial with a growing number \(m\) of categories, the Gaussian density at a mode has the exact critical distortion \(P_{N,m}/G_{N,m}\to e^{-c/12}\) when \(m^2/N\to c\), so modal relative accuracy holds exactly below the \(m=o(\sqrt N)\) scale. Explicit Bernoulli-polynomial corrections push the accuracy threshold through \(N^{2/3},N^{3/4},\ldots\), while the standard Gaussian unit-cell continuity correction has critical profile \(P_{N,m}/C_{N,m}\to\sqrt{1+c/12}\,e^{-c/24}\).
