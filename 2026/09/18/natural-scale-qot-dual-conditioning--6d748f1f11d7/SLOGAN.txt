Sharp support geometry turns the QOT dual from a seemingly vanishing-curvature problem into an epsilon-uniform strongly concave problem on its natural O(epsilon^{2/(d+2)}) potential neighborhood. The softest dual Hessian curvature stays Theta(1), the stiffest grows Theta(epsilon^{-2/(d+2)}), and the local condition number is therefore Theta(epsilon^{-2/(d+2)}).
