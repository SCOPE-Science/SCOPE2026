Connected non-complete graphs satisfy \(\mu_c(G)=n-1\) exactly when deleting one vertex leaves a connected graph and that vertex lies at the center of a star component in the complement. Consequently, if both a graph and its complement are connected, then the Nordhaus--Gaddum bounds sharpen sharply to \(2n-4\) and \((n-2)^2\), with equality for every order \(n\ge4\).
