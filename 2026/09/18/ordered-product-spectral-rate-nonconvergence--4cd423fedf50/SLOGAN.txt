A simple smooth period-four cocycle with Lyapunov exponents a>b>0 has ordered-product spectral rate h_{2k}=a but h_{2k+1}=(a+b)/2, so a non-degenerate top exponent does not force h_L to converge. The missing ingredient is endpoint spectral transversality: Oseledets controls singular/covariant growth, not the eigenvalues of a finite propagator between different phases.
