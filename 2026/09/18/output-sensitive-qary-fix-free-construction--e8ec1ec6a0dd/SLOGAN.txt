The deterministic $q$-ary three-length $3/4$ fix-free construction can be implemented in $O(N_2\log N_2+\mu_3\lambda_3)$ time rather than $O(N_2^2+N_3\lambda_3)$. One-sided uniqueness makes each greedy update one lazy group decrement plus at most one point decrement, and the longest layer is generated directly from prefix/suffix Cartesian products.
