Among 3-colorings in which all even integers have one color and every odd integer uses one of the other two, the maximum asymptotic rainbow-Schur fraction is exactly 9/22. An exact threshold-graph cut recursion shows that the Hegde--Kumar--Pratibha lower construction is optimal throughout this entire parity-restricted class, and near-extremizers must split the odd integers in asymptotic proportions 5/11 and 6/11.
