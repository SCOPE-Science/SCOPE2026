For every three-dimensional Lie algebra over F_q, the subalgebra commutativity degree is determined solely by the number t of Lie-subalgebra planes: sd(L)=1-q(q+1)(q^2+q+1-t)/(q^2+q+t+3)^2. Exactly four values occur; solvable algebras are indexed by the 0, 1, 2, or q+1 projective eigenlines of a 2-by-2 adjoint action, while every perfect three-dimensional algebra has t=q+1.
