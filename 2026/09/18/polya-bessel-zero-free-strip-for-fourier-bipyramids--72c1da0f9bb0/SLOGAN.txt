For elongated Fourier bipyramids in every dimension d>=3, a classical convex-kernel positivity mechanism turns the qualitative zero-free strip into an explicit Bessel-root strip: kappa(Omega_{alpha,d}) >= alpha r_d, where (d-2)J_{(d-3)/2}(r_d)=r_d J_{(d-1)/2}(r_d). This gives an explicit sufficient counterexample threshold in every dimension and analytically certifies the previously non-rigorous fixed example (alpha,d)=(100,3).
