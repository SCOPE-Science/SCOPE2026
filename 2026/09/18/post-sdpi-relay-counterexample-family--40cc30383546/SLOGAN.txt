Shiu's BSC(1/4) relay counterexample extends to an entire orthogonal-channel family: if the source-to-relay post-processing contraction coefficient is eta, the proposed three-scheme expression is at most 1+eta C2 while the true capacity is 1+min(C1,C2). For BSC links, every 0<delta1<=delta2<1/2 is a strict counterexample, with an explicit gap 4 delta1(1-delta1)(1-h2(delta2)).
