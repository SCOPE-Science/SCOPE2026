Pro-star reversibility forces every unit to be self-adjoint. Consequently it collapses completely on semiperfect rings: such a ring is pro-star-reversible exactly when it is commutative with the identity involution; by contrast, a domain is pro-star-reversible exactly when its involution fixes its units, and F4 with Frobenius is the smallest finite star-reversible but non-pro-star-reversible example.
