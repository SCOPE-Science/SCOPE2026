For every fixed integer r >= 2, universal ARC is equivalent to TC2 on r-fold trivial extensions alone, and even to TC2 on Frobenius algebras admitting a Nakayama automorphism of exact order r. The mechanism is a self-injective corner criterion: if A=eΓe and _A eΓ lies in add(A⊕DA), then TC2 for Γ forces ARC for A; consequently one ARC counterexample would generate TC2 counterexamples of every finite Nakayama order r>=2.
