# Review

## Correctness

PASS. The main obstruction is a direct consequence of the simultaneous rank criteria in Bao's Theorem 1.3: case (c)(i) forces exactly one of sqrt(A), sqrt(C) to lie in Q(omega), while positive E1 and E3 rank respectively require those two square roots. Hence case (c)(i) contributes at most total rank two. The specialization to Bao's Example 6.3 is checked algebraically: m=3/(32n^3-1) is never a rational square by a modulo-8 argument, the two E2 cube arguments are explicit cubes over Q(omega,sqrt(m)), the E1 cube condition holds, and the E3 square-root condition fails. Exact symbolic substitution verifies both displayed rational sections of E0.

The conclusion uses Bao's Theorem 1.3 as stated rather than independently reproving its full Mordell-Weil classification. Bao's Proposition 3.6 separately rederives from Bremner the quadratic rank criterion that supplies the missing E3 condition, so the contradiction within Example 6.3 does not depend only on the example's own prose.
