For a free associative algebra, abelianization detects zero locally nilpotent derivations in rank two but fails sharply from rank three onward.  Every elementary commutative action \(f\partial_{x_i}\) then has an affine family of locally nilpotent lifts modeled on the commutator ideal of the frozen free subalgebra, whose degree-\(d\) dimension is \((n-1)^d-\binom{n+d-2}{d}\).
