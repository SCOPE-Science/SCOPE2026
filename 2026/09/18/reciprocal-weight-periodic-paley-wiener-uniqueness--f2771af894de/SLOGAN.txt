For the periodic spectrum S=A+Z, dyadically regular critical weights W_L(t)=1+|t|L(1+|t|) admit uniformly discrete uniqueness exactly when sum_j(2^{-j}+L(2^j))^{-1} converges. In particular, the critical Sobolev weight 1+|t| log^beta(e+|t|) has a sharp second-order threshold: uniqueness holds exactly for beta>1.
