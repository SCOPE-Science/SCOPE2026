For uniform rectangular matrices over F_q, low-rank factor masks have exact maximal correlation q^{-r}, while uniform rank-ball masks have exact value q^r C_{m-1,n-1}(r)/V_{m,n}(r) <= q^{-r}. A rectangular converse remains within a factor two for r <= min(m,n)-2, and the same extension yields a sharp aspect-ratio-aware differential-privacy obstruction.
