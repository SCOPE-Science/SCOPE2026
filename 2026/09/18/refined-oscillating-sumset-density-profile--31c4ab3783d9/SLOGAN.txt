Exact counting of the CRT union in Leonetti's oscillating-sumset construction sharpens the fixed-parameter bound to d_*(A+A) <= r(2 delta)^(r-1) - (r-1)(2 delta)^r. Optimizing the construction shows that the extremal lower-density profile with d(A)=alpha and d^*(A+A)=1 has logarithmic exponent one as alpha tends to zero.
