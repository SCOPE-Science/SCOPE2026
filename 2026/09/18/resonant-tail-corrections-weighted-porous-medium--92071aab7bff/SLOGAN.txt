The universal tail c_* xi^{-sigma/(p-1)} has a hidden second-order transition at m=2p-1: diffusion forces a universal power correction below the threshold, produces a logarithmic resonance at the threshold, and loses to a profile-dependent homogeneous mode above it. A second cancellation occurs when m sigma/(p-1)=N-2, where the radial diffusion curvature of the leading power vanishes.
