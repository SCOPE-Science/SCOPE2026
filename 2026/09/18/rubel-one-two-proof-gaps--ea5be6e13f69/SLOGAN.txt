The proof of the claimed all-domain equality Rubel(1)=Rubel(2) in arXiv:2609.20607v1 uses finite inner diameter in an unproved reverse direction and then converts a second arc-length derivative into an analytic second derivative while omitting the curvature term. The curve γ(t)=t+i sin t with f(z)=z² gives an explicit obstruction to that second inference; finite inner diameter nevertheless implies Rubel(1) on every plane domain.
