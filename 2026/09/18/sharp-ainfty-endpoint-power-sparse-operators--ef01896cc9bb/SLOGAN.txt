For every 0<r<1, a finite nested dyadic sparse chain admits two weights with [omega,v]_{A1}=1 and [omega]_{Ainfty} asymptotic to N, while the weak L1 sparse operator norm grows like N^(1/r-1). Hence the new A_infinity exponent 1/r-1 in the Goncalves-Lorist endpoint estimate is sharp independently of the mixed A1 factor.
