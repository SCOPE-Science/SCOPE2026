For the truncated diagonal strip \(V_\varepsilon=\{|x_1-x_2|<\varepsilon,\ |x_2|<1\}\), the double-Hilbert quasi-eigenvector defect has an exact convergent expansion and satisfies \(R(\varepsilon)\sim(2/\pi)\sqrt{\varepsilon\log(1/\varepsilon)}\). Thus the logarithmic loss in the new continuous construction is sharp and yields a genuine \(\sqrt{\log(1/\varepsilon)}\) separation from its dyadic analogue at matched width.
