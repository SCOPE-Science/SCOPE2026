For a fixed positive FitzHugh--Nagumo activator barrier, constant-proportional recovery profiles W=cV admit an exact recovery-diffusion criterion governed by the positive normalized curvature [V'']_+/V. The exponential tail in arXiv:2609.14944v1 saturates the activator budget, giving D_sharp=(gamma-tilde_gamma)/sup([V'']_+/V), while the paper's Lipschitz-based sufficient bound can be arbitrarily conservative.
