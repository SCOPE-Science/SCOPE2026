Equal-magnitude positive and perfectly balanced signed second-Gaussian-chaos coordinates have the same fourth-order effective rank r4=m, yet their maxima Gaussianize at m >> (log p)^3 and m >> (log p)^2, respectively. At the critical scales the Kolmogorov error converges to an explicit shifted-Gumbel profile, so effective rank alone cannot encode the sharp maximum-Gaussianization boundary.
