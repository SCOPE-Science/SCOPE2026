## Correctness

If a reduced-product class has zero reduced center-valued trace, subtracting the coordinate traces changes the representative by a norm-null sequence and produces exact coordinate trace zero. Wang’s uniform finite-von-Neumann theorem then gives one commutator per coordinate with a universal norm-product bound; reciprocal rescaling makes both factor sequences bounded, so they define quotient elements. The converse is tracial. Contractivity and the identity of the trace map on the center give the exact quotient-distance formula, and every tracial state vanishes on the commutator kernel and therefore factors uniquely through the center.

## Originality

Hardy’s pseudomatricial result covers unique trace and self-adjoint trace-zero self-commutators in a narrower matrix-ultraproduct setting. Resultary’s broader C*-quotient theorem is dated 2026-09-20 and therefore postdates this record.
