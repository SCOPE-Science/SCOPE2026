In the subcritical regime ν<1/2, the unique orthant Skorokhod regulator differs from half the boundary local time only through Stieltjes mass carried by the singular variation of the moving boundary. Hence every locally absolutely continuous boundary is strongly well posed without the Brownian-scale modulus condition, while increasing boundaries admit an exact contact-measure criterion; in particular min(t^α,1) is solvable even for α≤1/2.
