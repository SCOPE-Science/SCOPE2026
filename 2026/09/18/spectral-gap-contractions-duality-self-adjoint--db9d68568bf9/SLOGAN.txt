For compact operators satisfying \(T^*J=JT\) on a smooth complex Banach space, every nonzero Riesz projection is contractive, and every residual projection obtained after completing a spectral modulus level is contractive. The resulting spectral remainder has exact norm equal to the next eigenvalue modulus, which supplies a uniformly bounded projection argument closing the reported operator-norm convergence gap in arXiv:2608.06873v1.
