In the engineered three-oscillator Stuart-Landau phase model of arXiv:2609.20632v1, synchrony and splay have explicit second-order stability exponents. Harmonic cancellation uses eta=epsilon^2/(4a), but stability matching instead selects eta=3epsilon^2/(8a) at leading order, with an exact finite-epsilon eta_* that makes the two local marginal-stability boundaries coincide in the truncated phase model.
