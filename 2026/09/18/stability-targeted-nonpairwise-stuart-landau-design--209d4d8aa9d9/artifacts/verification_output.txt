lambda_sync = -3*eps*cos(rho) + (6*eta - 9*eps**2/(2*a))*sin(rho)**2
Re(lambda_splay) = 3*eps*cos(rho)/2 + 6*eta*sin(rho)**2 + 9*eps**2*cos(2*rho)/(8*a)
Im(lambda_splay) = -3*eps*(2*a + 3*eps*cos(rho))*sin(rho)/(4*a)
x_* = (a - sqrt(a**2 + 3*eps**2/2))/eps
eta_* = eps**2*(2*a**2 - a*sqrt(4*a**2 + 6*eps**2) + 3*eps**2/4)/(a*(4*a**2 - 2*sqrt(2)*a*sqrt(2*a**2 + 3*eps**2) + eps**2))

rho_* series (a=1, eps=r): pi/2 + 3*r/4 - 27*r**3/128 + O(r**5)
