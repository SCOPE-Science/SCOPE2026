## Correctness

For each nonzero translation, slicing the strictly convex body parallel to that translation gives strictly convex lower endpoints, strictly concave upper endpoints, and therefore a strictly concave chord-width function. Every positive width level lies in the boundary of a convex superlevel set and is null in the base; local Lipschitz regularity of the endpoint graph transfers this to zero surface measure on the boundary intersection. Ortega-Cerdà's full primary paper was inspected at Remark 6.4, and surface measure then satisfies each hypothesis of that boundary criterion.
