For every dimension N>=1 and every gamma in [-1,0), the exceptional BSVY Sobolev space W^{1,1}(gamma) is a proper subspace of BV(gamma). For -1<gamma<0 a jump function already witnesses strictness, whereas at gamma=-1 jumps have infinite endpoint quasi-seminorm but a compactly supported Cantor staircase has finite quasi-seminorm, revealing a jump-to-Cantor transition.
