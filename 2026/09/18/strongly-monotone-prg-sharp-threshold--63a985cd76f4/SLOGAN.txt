For affine reflected gradient with symmetric part at least sigma I and operator norm at most L, the exact universal stability ceiling is lambda L < tau_*(sigma/L), where 3 tau_*^3 + 4(sigma/L) tau_*^2 - tau_* - 2(sigma/L)=0. The threshold rises sharply from 1/sqrt(3) in the merely monotone limit to 2/3 at condition ratio one, and a planar rotation-dilation attains the boundary.
