Below the orthant threshold \(\nu<1/2\), the gap between the Skorokhod regulator and half the local time is exactly a weighted Stieltjes charge of the moving boundary on the contact set. Consequently every locally absolutely continuous finite-variation boundary is strongly well posed, including \(b(t)=ct^\alpha\) with \(0<\alpha\le 1/2\), despite failure of the previous \(o(\sqrt h)\) boundary condition.
