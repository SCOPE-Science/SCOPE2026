For surjective maps between unital C*-algebras, Matsuzaki's two norm identities plus involution preservation already force an exact quotient factorization: T = u Psi q, where q is a C*-quotient, Psi is a real *-isomorphism, and u is a central symmetry. Thus injectivity is not needed; its only role is to make the forced C*-ideal ker T vanish.
