Faraco--Rungi's proposed equivalence between non-elementarity and ambient Zariski density already fails in projective dimension three. The standard PSp(2m,R) subgroup preserves an alternating form but no projective quadratic form, so it is non-elementary under the quadric definition while remaining proper Zariski closed; known Zariski-dense surface subgroups of Sp(4,R) give discrete surface-group counterexamples as well.
