For every fixed r >= 2, Enomoto's TC2-to-ARC reduction works with the cyclic r-fold trivial extension T_r(A), not only T_2(A). Consequently universal ARC is equivalent to TC2 restricted to basic self-injective algebras whose Nakayama permutation consists entirely of r-cycles; the standard T_r(A) has Nakayama outer order exactly r.
