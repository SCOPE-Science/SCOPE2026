Under the published definition \(U=\Sigma^*\times T\), the three-nonterminal grammar \(A_1\to A_2A_3,\ A_2\to x_1,\ A_3\to x_1'\) is contained in the free-group word problem, yet Algorithm 4.4 rejects it. The failure is the replacement of group equality \(x_1x_1'=e\) by literal free-monoid equality to the empty word.
