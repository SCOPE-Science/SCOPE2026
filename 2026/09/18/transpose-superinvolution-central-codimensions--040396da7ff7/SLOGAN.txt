For $M_2(F)$ with its canonical grading and transpose superinvolution, the full and central codimensions collapse to central-binomial formulas: $c_n^*=\binom{2n+2}{n+1}-2^n$ and $c_n^{z,*}=\frac{3n+1}{n+1}\binom{2n}{n}-2^{n-1}$. The nonidentity central layer has dimension $\binom{2n}{n}-2^{n-1}$, asymptotically one quarter of the full multilinear quotient.
