For a bijective approximate Fischer–Muszély map between positive cones of JB-algebras, the Hatori–Oi linear asymptotic core is an order isomorphism exactly when the inverse map also has finite FM defect; the two asymptotic cores are then exact inverses. A weighted c0 family has forward defect ε but inverse defect exactly ε/inf w_n, giving a sharp phase transition that explains the dense-nonsurjective example.
