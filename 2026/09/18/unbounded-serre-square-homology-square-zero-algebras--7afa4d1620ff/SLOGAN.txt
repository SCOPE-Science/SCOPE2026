For the square-zero local algebra \(A_r=k\oplus V\) with \(\dim V=r\ge2\), the Serre square \(D A_r\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{A_r}D A_r\) has nonzero homology in every positive degree, with \(\dim\operatorname{Tor}_n=(r^2-1)^2r^{n-2}\) for \(n\ge2\). This gives an explicit missing-Tor-amplitude counterexample to the finite-row premise of Theorem 4.1 in arXiv:2609.20220v1.
