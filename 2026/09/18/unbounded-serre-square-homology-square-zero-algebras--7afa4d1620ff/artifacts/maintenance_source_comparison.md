The projective cover has trace-zero radical kernel. Subsequent kernels are semisimple. Exact tensor ranks give
\[
\dim\operatorname{Tor}_1=r(r^2-2),\qquad
\dim\operatorname{Tor}_n=(r^2-1)^2r^{n-2}\quad(n\ge2).
\]
All are positive for \(r\ge2\), in any characteristic. This defeats finite amplitude, not the existence of a bounded-above projective model. It does not invalidate the separate Gorenstein case or prove divergence for every possible unbounded construction.
