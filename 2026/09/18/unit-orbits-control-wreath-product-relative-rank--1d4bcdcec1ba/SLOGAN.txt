For finite transformation monoids, the exact relative rank of a wreath product is controlled by the orbits of the top monoid's unit group: rank(S wr_X R : H wr_X G) = rank(R:G) + (# G-orbits on X) rank(S:H). This yields an orbit-weighted formula for arbitrary iterated wreath products and supplies explicit counterexamples to Lemmas 3.2 and 3.8 of arXiv:2609.20521v1 while leaving its main full-transformation/symmetric-group theorem intact.
