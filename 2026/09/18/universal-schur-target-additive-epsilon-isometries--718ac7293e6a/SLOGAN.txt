A single separable Schur Banach space admits one-sided exact additive epsilon-isometries from every nonzero separable real Banach space at every positive scale, while no separable non-Schur Banach space embeds into it isometrically. The construction combines Banach--Mazur universality of C[0,1] with Kalton's gauged Lipschitz-free Schur theorem.
