# Accelerated filtrations decouple algebraic entropy from affine growth

## Result

Let \(\mathsf{k}\) be a field. The algebraic entropy of an arbitrary finite-dimensional filtration can be made positive—even arbitrarily large—without changing the intrinsic growth of the underlying affine algebra.

### 1. A polynomial-growth counterexample family

Let \(A=\mathsf{k}[x]\). For an integer \(q\ge 2\), set
\[
V_0=0,\qquad
V_n=\operatorname{span}_{\mathsf{k}}\{1,x,\ldots,x^{q^n}\}\quad(n\ge1).
\]
Then \(\mathcal F^{(q)}=\{V_n\}_{n\ge0}\) is a finite-dimensional exhaustive algebra filtration and
\[
\boxed{\operatorname h_{\rm alg}(A,\mathcal F^{(q)})=\log q.}
\]
Nevertheless
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{GKdim}A=1}
\]
and \(A\) has polynomial, not exponential, growth.

Consequently, for arbitrary finite-dimensional filtrations, positive algebraic entropy does **not** imply infinite GK dimension or exponential intrinsic growth. In particular this family contradicts Theorems 3.9 and 3.14 and Corollary 3.15 of arXiv:2609.18144v1 as stated. It also contradicts the growth equality in that paper's Theorem 3.7. For \(q\ge3\), it additionally contradicts Remark 3.8, since \(\log q>1=\operatorname{GKdim}\mathsf{k}[x]\).

### 2. Universal acceleration

More generally:

> **Theorem.** Every infinite-dimensional affine \(\mathsf{k}\)-algebra admits a finite-dimensional exhaustive filtration for which the defining limsup of algebraic entropy is \(+\infty\).

Hence an affine algebra has algebraic entropy zero for **every** finite-dimensional filtration if and only if it is finite dimensional.

### 3. A sufficient repair condition

Let \(A\) be affine, let \(\mathcal S=\{S_n\}\) be a standard finite-dimensional filtration generated by a finite-dimensional subspace containing \(1\), and let \(\mathcal F=\{V_n\}\) be another finite-dimensional filtration. Assume that for some integer \(C>0\),
\[
V_n\subseteq S_{Cn}\qquad(n\gg0).
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{\operatorname h_{\rm alg}(A,\mathcal F)>0\Longrightarrow A\text{ has exponential growth}.}
\]
Moreover, since exhaustivity of \(\mathcal F\) automatically gives \(S_n\subseteq V_{pn}\) for some \(p>0\), the two filtrations have equivalent growth under linear rescaling whenever the displayed upper bound holds.

A standard classical sufficient hypothesis is that \(\operatorname{gr}_{\mathcal F}A\) is finitely generated. Under that hypothesis the usual filtered--graded GK-dimension theorem applies. Thus the missing finite-generation/linear-control condition restores the intended comparison; this repair is prior art, not an originality claim here.

## Proof

For the polynomial example, if \(m,n\ge1\), then
\[
q^m+q^n\le q^{m+n},
\]
so \(V_mV_n\subseteq V_{m+n}\). The filtration is finite dimensional, nested, and exhaustive. For \(n\ge2\),
\[
\dim(V_n/V_{n-1})
=(q^n+1)-(q^{n-1}+1)
=(q-1)q^{n-1}.
\]
Therefore
\[
\operatorname h_{\rm alg}(A,\mathcal F^{(q)})
=\lim_{n\to\infty}
\frac{\log((q-1)q^{n-1})}{n}
=\log q.
\]
The standard fact \(\operatorname{GKdim}\mathsf{k}[x]=1\) gives the claimed separation.

For universal acceleration, choose a finite-dimensional generating subspace \(W\subset A\) containing \(1\), and put \(S_m=W^m\). Since \(A\) is infinite dimensional, \(\dim S_m\to\infty\). Recursively choose integers
\[
r_1<r_2<\cdots
\]
so that, for every \(n\ge2\),
\[
r_n\ge \max_{1\le i<n}(r_i+r_{n-i})
\]
and
\[
\dim S_{r_n}-\dim S_{r_{n-1}}\ge \lceil e^{n^2}\rceil.
\]
This is possible because \(\dim S_m\) is unbounded. Put \(V_0=0\) and \(V_n=S_{r_n}\). Then
\[
V_iV_j\subseteq S_{r_i+r_j}\subseteq S_{r_{i+j}}=V_{i+j},
\]
so \(\{V_n\}\) is a finite-dimensional exhaustive filtration. Its successive quotients satisfy
\[
\frac{\log\dim(V_n/V_{n-1})}{n}\ge n,
\]
so the defining limsup is \(+\infty\). The finite-dimensional converse is immediate from the definition of algebraic entropy.

For the repair statement, let
\[
h=\operatorname h_{\rm alg}(A,\mathcal F)>0
\]
and choose \(0<\varepsilon<h\) (any positive \(\varepsilon\) if the limsup is infinite). Along infinitely many \(n\),
\[
\dim(V_n/V_{n-1})\ge e^{\varepsilon n}.
\]
Hence
\[
\dim S_{Cn}\ge \dim V_n\ge e^{\varepsilon n}
\]
along an infinite subsequence. If \(d_m=\dim S_m\), then \(S_{m+n}=S_mS_n\), hence
\[
d_{m+n}\le d_md_n.
\]
Fekete's lemma applied to \(\log d_m\) shows that \(d_m^{1/m}\) has a limit. The displayed subsequence bound forces that limit to be at least \(e^{\varepsilon/C}>1\). Since \(A\) is finitely generated, its growth is also bounded above exponentially, so its intrinsic growth is exponential.

Finally, if \(\operatorname{gr}_{\mathcal F}A\) is finitely generated by homogeneous classes, choose finitely many lifts and let \(U\) be their span together with \(1\). Induction on filtration degree shows \(V_n\subseteq U^n\): modulo \(V_{n-1}\), every element of \(V_n\) is a linear combination of products of the chosen homogeneous generators of total degree \(n\), and hence of at most \(n\) lifted factors. Thus finite generation of the associated graded algebra supplies precisely the required linear control.

## Context and literature boundary

Schwarz--Sebandal, arXiv:2609.18144v1, state for arbitrary finite-dimensional filtrations that positive algebraic entropy forces infinite GK dimension (Theorem 3.9), and then exponential growth (Theorem 3.14). Their Theorem 3.7 attributes to Krause--Lenagan, Proposition 6.6, an equality between intrinsic growth and the growth of an arbitrary finite-dimensional filtration. Classical filtered--graded GK theory includes an additional finite-generation hypothesis on the associated graded algebra; accessible later uses of Krause--Lenagan Proposition 6.6 state this hypothesis explicitly.

The same 2026 paper's Leavitt path algebra criterion, Theorem 3.26, uses the standard Leavitt path filtration \(W_n=W_1^n\). The counterexamples above therefore do not invalidate that specific PI criterion.

The 2024 paper that introduced this filtered algebraic entropy already emphasizes its dependence on filtration and proves that linear reindexing can multiply a nonzero entropy. It also remarks that its results might suggest zero entropy could persist across filtrations. The filtration above shows that nonlinear acceleration can take the standard zero entropy of \(\mathsf{k}[x]\) to \(\log q>0\), and the universal construction shows that the phenomenon is not special to polynomial rings.

## Originality and limitations

The classical requirement that \(\operatorname{gr}_{\mathcal F}A\) be finitely generated in the filtered--graded GK comparison is prior art and is not claimed as new. The contribution claimed here is the explicit accelerated-filtration counterexample family to the 2026 entropy-growth statements, together with the universal acceleration theorem showing that every infinite-dimensional affine algebra admits a finite-dimensional filtration of divergent algebraic entropy, and the resulting sharp failure of filtration-independent positivity.

To the best of our knowledge, targeted searches did not locate these statements in the algebraic-entropy literature. Re-filtering literature predating the 2024 entropy definition may contain equivalent filtration-growth observations in different language; this is the principal residual originality risk.
