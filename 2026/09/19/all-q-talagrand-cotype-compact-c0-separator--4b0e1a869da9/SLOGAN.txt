Wu's Walsh--Hadamard counterexample at q=2 actually violates Talagrand's operator-cotype comparison at every fixed finite q>=2, with gap at least a constant times (log n)^(1/q). After normalization and c0-block gluing, each q admits a compact operator from a closed subspace of c0 into c0 with finite Gaussian cotype and finite (q,1)-summing norm but no Rademacher cotype q.
