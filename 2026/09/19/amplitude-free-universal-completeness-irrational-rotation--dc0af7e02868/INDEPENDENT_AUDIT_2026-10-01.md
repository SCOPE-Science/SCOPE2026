## Final claim

For every irrational alpha and every nonzero beta satisfying the stated nonresonance condition, the centered irrational-rotation frequency set is uniformly discrete of density one and universally complete on every measurable spectrum of measure below one, with no amplitude restriction in one dimension.

## Correctness

PASS. The finite-amplitude extension was checked at the critical places: nonresonance prevents all possible zero gaps; bounded displacement gives density one; poles remain within a beta-dependent bounded distance of integer labels; the exceptional residue condition is finite per index pair; nonresonance gives distinct poles; weighted pole summability survives; the ergodic pole-density bound is unchanged; and the Jensen zero-versus-pole density contradiction therefore persists. None of these steps requires beta to be small once constants may depend on beta.

## Value

PASS. Removing the amplitude restriction is a natural completion of the explicit irrational-rotation construction, extending it from a perturbative regime to every nonresonant amplitude while preserving the same universal measurable-spectrum conclusion.
