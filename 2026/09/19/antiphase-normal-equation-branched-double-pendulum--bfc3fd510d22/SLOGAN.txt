The symmetric branched double pendulum has an exact scalar normal equation for infinitesimal antiphase motion, with a time-dependent stiffness determined by the finite-amplitude in-phase trajectory. For the reported apparatus the release stiffness stays positive until 84.43°, so the observed 47.8° onset is necessarily dynamical rather than a static release-point instability; near equilibrium, the closest quadratic parametric channel is the nonzero sum-frequency modulation f_low+f_high≈2f_d.
