The fixed-size AR(1) KLT→DCT-II endpoint has a sharp growing-dimension boundary layer: the leading KLT mode converges to DCT DC exactly when N(1-rho)→0. At N(1-rho)→kappa>0 it approaches a Robin-limit profile with an explicit nonzero angle, proving that the orthogonal correction factors in the exact DCT-core KLT factorization need not vanish uniformly with transform size.
