For the two classes highlighted by Abedi--Moslehian, arbitrary-state Hilbert-module quotients are complete for every module exactly at a finite-support boundary: finite-rank support projections for c0-sums of compact-operator algebras, and finite atomic support for C0(X). Infinite support already makes the standard-module GNS quotient incomplete, while mixed finite-rank states strictly extend the pure-state positive case.
