On homogeneous Allen–Cahn states, the source IEQ and SAV schemes are exactly the same recurrence after the natural shift normalization C_IEQ = epsilon^2 C0/|Omega|. The arbitrary auxiliary energy shift then acts as a genuine discrete dynamical parameter: it strictly lowers the overshoot threshold and, in an explicit time-step window, has a closed-form critical value that flips the sign of the second phase increment.
