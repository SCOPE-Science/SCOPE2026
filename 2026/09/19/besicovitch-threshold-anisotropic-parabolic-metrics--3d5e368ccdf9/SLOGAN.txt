For each fixed dimension n and anisotropy a>=2, the mixed-power parabolic metrics d_{p,a}= (|x-y|^p+|t-s|^{p/a})^{1/p} admit a single strong-Besicovitch covering multiplicity valid uniformly for every p>=a. The threshold p=a itself is prior; the surviving result is uniformity across the entire positive regime.
