In the sharp degree-four univariate moment-SOS example of arXiv:2609.20544, the explicit optimal atomic certificate has a universal r^{-2} boundary layer. Its unique infeasible atom approaches x=1 but retains asymptotic mass 2/a^4 = 0.9655400431..., where a tanh(a)=1; the full rescaled certificate converges to an explicit discrete law whose trace identities recover both unit mass and the sharp 1/(2r(r-1)) gap.
