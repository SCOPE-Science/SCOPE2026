A squared Cayley transform turns the two Dilcher--Kim--Stolarsky reciprocal polynomial families into positive-coefficient degree-n polynomials, forcing every zero into the open left half-plane and yielding a Cauchy limiting zero law on the imaginary axis. The same reduction gives the exact previously absent companion discriminant
Disc(q_n)=(-1)^n 2^{4n(n-1)}(n+2)(n+1)^{2n-2}(((n+2)^n+n^n)/2)^2.
