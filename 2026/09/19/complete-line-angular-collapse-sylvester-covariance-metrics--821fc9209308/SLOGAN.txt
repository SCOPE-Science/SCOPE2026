The two-parameter Sylvester-power covariance metric is complete exactly on p+q=2, the same line on which dilation and inversion are isometries.  On that complete line, the non-mean region |p-q|>2 exhibits angular collapse: after constant normalization, a quarter-turn swapping eigendirections of a condition-number-kappa matrix costs asymptotically pi*kappa^((2-|p-q|)/4), hence tends to zero exactly beyond |p-q|=2.
