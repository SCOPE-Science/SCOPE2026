Every open cell of the normalized ultraspherical lower envelope has a unique minimizing degree, and every finite breakpoint has exactly the two adjacent minimizers, except the single Legendre anomaly at x=-1/3 where degrees 1, 2, and 5 tie. Consequently (alpha,x,k)=(0,-1/3,5) is the unique obstruction to de Oliveira Filho's arbitrary-minimizer monotonicity question for alpha>=0.
