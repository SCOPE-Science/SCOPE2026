After Castillo–Sadigova v2 settled all positive ultraspherical parameters, the remaining Legendre case can still be classified completely: open cells have a unique minimizing degree, breakpoints have only the adjacent pair, except x=−1/3 where degrees 1, 2, and 5 tie. That triple is the unique Legendre obstruction to de Oliveira Filho’s arbitrary-minimizer monotonicity question.
