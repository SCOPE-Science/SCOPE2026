For maximum-perturbed reflected Brownian motion with nu<1/2, local-time solvability is governed by whether the boundary's Stieltjes variation charges the canonical contact set. A variation-a.e. Brownian-LIL criterion closes the missing 1/2-Hölder endpoint, while every locally absolutely continuous boundary is admissible with no modulus restriction—even b(t)=t^alpha for alpha<1/2.
