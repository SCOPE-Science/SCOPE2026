At the critical linear boundary of a compactly supported power-log location family, a slowly varying exponent creates a second phase transition: logarithmic Hellinger information for kappa<1, a log-log critical point at kappa=1, and finite Fisher information for kappa>1. Wang--Gao's dyadic quantile functional has a universal sharp ratio to the inverse Hellinger modulus throughout the infinite-information side, but an exact Laplace calculation shows that in the regular regime a fixed dyadic grid can retain a nonvanishing lattice phase.
