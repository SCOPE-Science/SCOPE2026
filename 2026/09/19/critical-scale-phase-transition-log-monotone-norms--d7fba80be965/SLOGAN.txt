Huang's logarithmically monotone Marcinkiewicz construction has a sharp critical scale governed by L_n(1-p_n): if it tends to 0, the moment seminorm collapses exactly to a fully symmetric tail seminorm; if it tends to c>0, an explicit strong-symmetry defect remains; if it diverges, the kernel loses Hardy--Littlewood solidity. For the power law 1-p_n=r_n^{-alpha}, alpha=1 is the exact boundary and alpha>1 is a previously untreated fully symmetric phase.
