The shifted two-point cyclic obstruction on Chebyshev--Lobatto grids has an exact method-level asymptotic: at phase a its best certificate is (C(a)+o(1)) H^{-1} exp[-pi H/(2 sin(pi a))]. The half shift a=1/2 is the unique fixed phase attaining the optimal pi/2 exponential coefficient, with leading constant 8 exp(gamma-1)/pi and optimal period asymptotic (pi exp(1-gamma)/8) exp(pi H/2).
