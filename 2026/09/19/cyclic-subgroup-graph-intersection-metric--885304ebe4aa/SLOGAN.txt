For cyclic subgroups H,K of a finite group, the cyclic-subgroup-graph distance is exactly Ω(|H:H∩K|)+Ω(|K:H∩K|), and saturated chains through H∩K give an induced geodesic. This yields sharp corrections to both induced-subgraph claims in Proposition 2.5 of arXiv:2409.13796v2, including explicit C4×C2 and cyclic-group counterexamples.
