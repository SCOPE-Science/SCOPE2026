Squarefree cyclotomic indices have unique even 2-adic roots \(\beta_n\) of \(\Phi_n(\beta_n)=-1\), and \(\nu_2(\beta_m-\beta_n)\) is exactly the least prime on which the two prime supports differ. The resulting 2-adic Cantor set meets exactly \(2^{\pi(B-1)}\) residue classes modulo \(2^B\), so its residue tree branches precisely at prime depths.
