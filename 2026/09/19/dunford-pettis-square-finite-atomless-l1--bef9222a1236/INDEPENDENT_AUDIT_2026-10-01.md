## Correctness

The proof was reconstructed from the assigned RESULT.md. The atomic band contributes a representable term. On the atomless band, positive Dunford-Pettis cross-space factors admit forward/reverse measure kernels; any positive-measure atomic kernel part yields a positive suboperator that sends a weakly null Rademacher sequence to a norm-bounded-away-from-zero sequence, so both kernels are atomless almost everywhere. The averaged product symbol is therefore an integral of convolutions of atomless measures and vanishes on the strongly independent polar set. Regular decomposition extends this to arbitrary factors. The compressed symbol then annihilates the closed square, gives the exact distance and complemented quotient copy, while Lewis-Stegall factorization gives the lower ideal inclusion and the purely atomic Schur case gives the converse boundary. No finite experiment is used as an infinite proof.
