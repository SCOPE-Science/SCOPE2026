For every finite measure L1 space with a nonzero atomless part, the closed square of the Dunford--Pettis ideal lies strictly between the representable and Dunford--Pettis ideals. A cross-space atomless-disintegration lemma yields an exact quotient distance formula and a contractively complemented copy of M0(P), while purely atomic finite L1 spaces are exactly the case where the square fills the whole Dunford--Pettis ideal.
