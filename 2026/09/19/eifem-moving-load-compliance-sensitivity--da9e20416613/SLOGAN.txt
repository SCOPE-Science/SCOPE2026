A fixed physical load F does not stay fixed in reduced coordinates when a design-dependent EIFEM map T(mu) is used: the coarse load is T(mu)^T F, so compliance sensitivity needs a 2 F_c'(mu)^T u_c term. Omitting it can reverse the gradient sign and makes the stiffness-only sensitivity arbitrarily coordinate-gauge dependent, even when the reduced model is exact.
