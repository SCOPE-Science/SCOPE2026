For bounded Poisson–Laguerre marks with endpoint tail P(A-M≤y)~Cy^β, the marks of large-inradius cells localize at scale (log ρ)^(-(d-2)/d): after the natural rescaling they converge to Gamma(β, rate 2A), independently of the Gumbel inradius score. In d=3 this also yields the universal -(β/3) log log ρ correction to the shift and identifies a missing +log γ term in both displayed formulas of Example 3.11 of arXiv:2609.20750v1.
