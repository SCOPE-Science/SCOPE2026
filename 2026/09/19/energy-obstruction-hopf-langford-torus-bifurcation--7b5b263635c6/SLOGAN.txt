For the Hopf–Langford subfamily of arXiv:2609.18010v1, an exact change w=r^gamma gives an amplitude energy satisfying E'=T(w')^2 with T=(1+2gamma)mu-2gamma alpha. Hence no surrounding Neimark–Sacker torus can exist for T!=0, while T=0 is a first-integrable center carrying a continuum of tori rather than a generic one-sided torus bifurcation.
