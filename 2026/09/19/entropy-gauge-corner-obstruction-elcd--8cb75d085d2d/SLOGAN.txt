The four-corner ELCD rule of arXiv:2609.19838v1 does not generally minimize its stated convex Euler entropy over the density-pressure rectangle: the exact minimizer can lie in the interior of the top edge. More fundamentally, absolute entropy-density ranking changes under the equivalent gauge eta -> eta + c rho, so an arbitrary entropy reference can switch the selected characteristic linearization; ranking by eta/rho removes this gauge dependence.
