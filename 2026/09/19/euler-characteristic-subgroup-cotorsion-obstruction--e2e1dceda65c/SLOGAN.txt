Replacing parity by an arbitrary Euler-characteristic subgroup \(B\le\mathbb Z\) yields a complete ideal cotorsion pair in a Hom-finite weakly idempotent complete Frobenius category, while the associated object pair is complete exactly when \(B=\mathbb Z\). For every proper \(B\), special object approximations are obstructed precisely by the truncated Euler characteristics leaving \(B\).
