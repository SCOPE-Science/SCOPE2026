For a uniform finite-field factor mask UV with k<=r<n, the distance of UV from a uniform k-by-n matrix is exactly p_{k,n}(1-p_{k,r}), not merely at most 1-p_{k,r}. Consequently, Low-Rank Factors have row/column individual-security leakage Theta_q(q^{k-r}), so vanishing leakage occurs exactly when the rank surplus r-k diverges.
