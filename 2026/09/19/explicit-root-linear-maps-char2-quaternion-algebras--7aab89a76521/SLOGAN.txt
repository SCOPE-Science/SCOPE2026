For a characteristic-two quaternion division algebra Q=[a,b)_F with finite 2-rank [F:F^2]=2^r, the quotient H/A has an explicit left-Q basis of size 2^(r-1), and its coordinate dual gives every root-linear map. Over F_2((t)), the formula z=u^2+t v^2 yields the concrete root-linear map alpha(f+zj)=u+vj, making the nonlocal part of the recent Hermitian range-compatible classification explicit.
