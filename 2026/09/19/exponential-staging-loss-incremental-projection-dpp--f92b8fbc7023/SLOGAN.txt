One-column-at-a-time multi-stage ARP can attain its full 2^d worst-case factor even for the actual orthogonal column-subset error, with an exact and uniquely ordered dominant right singular basis. On the same matrices, one-shot ARP tends to the classical d+1 factor, so preserving earlier pivots can cost an exponential factor 2^d/(d+1) in expected squared Frobenius error.
