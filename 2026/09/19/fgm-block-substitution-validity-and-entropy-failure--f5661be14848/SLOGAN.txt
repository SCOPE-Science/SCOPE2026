Naive block substitution is not closed on copulas: for an FGM outer copula and product inner blocks of arities r,s, validity holds exactly for -(2^r-1)^{-1}(2^s-1)^{-1} <= theta <= 1/max(2^r-1,2^s-1). Even inside that valid window, block enlargement strictly decreases copula entropy, so the unrestricted closure and entropy-additivity claims of arXiv:2609.20512v1 fail as stated.
