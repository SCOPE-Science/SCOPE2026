For every odd r>=3 and every admissible order n, the least possible number of vertices covered by a 2-regular subgraph of a simple r-regular n-vertex graph is n-max{0,floor((n-2(r+2))/(r^2-3))}. A Walecki-block construction attains the finite-order bound at every admissible n, extending the previously sharp arithmetic-progression examples; the cubic specialization was already known.
