In rectangular Gaussian binary MIMO, the number of Hamming-one neighbors beating the planted word converges to Poisson(e^{-c/2}/(2 sqrt(pi))) when alpha*rho = 2 log N - log log N + c. Equivalently, the one-flip local-stability SNR has a standard Gumbel limit after explicit centering and scaling, sharpening the known divergent-slack one-bit converse while not claiming the full ML critical window.
