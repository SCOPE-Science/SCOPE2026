Every nondegenerate quadratic shift-invariant permutation classified by Feng--Wang--Yu--Zhang is affine-equivalent to independent copies of one ordinary odd chi map: chi_ell^(x d), with d=gcd(n,v-w) and ell=n/d. Consequently the family has exactly tau(n0)-1 nondegenerate affine types for n=2^k n0, inverse degree (ell+1)/2, and differential/linear tables that factor exactly through chi_ell.
