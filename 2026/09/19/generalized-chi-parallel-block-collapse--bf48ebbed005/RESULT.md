# Generalized chi permutations collapse to parallel odd chi blocks

## Result

Let
\[
F_{n,v}(x)_i=x_i+x_{i+v}x_{i+2v}+x_{i+2v}
\]
and
\[
G_{n,v}(x)_i=x_i+x_{i-2v}x_{i-v}+x_{i-v},
\]
with all indices modulo \(n\). Put
\[
d=\gcd(n,v),\qquad \ell=n/d.
\]
If \(\ell\) is odd, then after a permutation of coordinates
\[
F_{n,v}\cong \chi_\ell^{\times d},
\]
where \(\chi_\ell(z)_t=z_t+z_{t+1}z_{t+2}+z_{t+2}\). Moreover, if
\[
A(z)_t=1+z_{-t},
\]
then the corresponding block of \(G_{n,v}\) is \(A\chi_\ell A\). Hence
\[
G_{n,v}\cong (A\chi_\ell A)^{\times d}
\]
and is affinely conjugate to \(\chi_\ell^{\times d}\).

Consequently, every nonlinear permutation in Corollary 3 of Feng--Wang--Yu--Zhang, arXiv:2609.19548v1, is affine equivalent to \(d\) parallel copies of one ordinary odd-dimensional chi map. If \(n=2^k n_0\) with \(n_0\) odd, the possible block lengths are exactly the divisors \(\ell>1\) of \(n_0\). Thus the nonlinear permutations in that classified family form exactly
\[
\boxed{\tau(n_0)-1}
\]
affine-equivalence classes.

For every such nonlinear permutation \(P\),
\[
\boxed{\deg(P^{-1})=\frac{\ell+1}{2}}
\]
where \(\ell=n/\gcd(n,v-w)\) in the notation of Corollary 3. In particular,
\[
\deg(P^{-1})\le \frac{n_0+1}{2}.
\]
For the explicit families \(F_{n,v}\) and \(G_{n,v}\), their order is
\[
\boxed{2^{\lfloor\log_2\ell\rfloor}},
\]
and, for every positive integer \(j\),
\[
\deg(F_{n,v}^j)=\deg(G_{n,v}^j)=1+r_j,
\]
where \(r_j\) is the largest integer \(r\le(\ell-1)/2\) whose binary 1-bits are all 1-bits of \(j\). Their fixed-point counts satisfy
\[
|\operatorname{Fix}(F_{n,v}^j)|=|\operatorname{Fix}(G_{n,v}^j)|
=|\operatorname{Fix}(\chi_\ell^j)|^d.
\]

## Proof

The step \(v\) partitions the coordinates into the \(d\) disjoint cycles
\[
C_r=\{r+tv:0\le t<\ell\},\qquad 0\le r<d.
\]
Write \(z^{(r)}_t=x_{r+tv}\). Then directly
\[
F_{n,v}(x)_{r+tv}
=z^{(r)}_t+z^{(r)}_{t+1}z^{(r)}_{t+2}+z^{(r)}_{t+2},
\]
so regrouping coordinates by the \(C_r\) gives \(F_{n,v}=\chi_\ell^{\times d}\).

For the second family the same regrouping gives the block map
\[
g(z)_t=z_t+z_{t-2}z_{t-1}+z_{t-1}.
\]
The affine involution \(A(z)_t=1+z_{-t}\) satisfies, by direct expansion in characteristic two,
\[
A\chi_\ell A=g.
\]
This proves the two block decompositions.

Now consider the general map from Corollary 3,
\[
P_i=x_{i+u}+x_{i+v}(x_{i+w}+1).
\]
Put \(a=v-w\), \(b=u-w\), and \(z_i=x_{i+w}\). If \(b=-a\), then
\[
P_i=F_{n,a}(z)_{i-a};
\]
if \(b=2a\), then
\[
P_i=G_{n,a}(z)_{i+2a}.
\]
Thus the input and output differences from the explicit families are coordinate permutations. Feng--Wang--Yu--Zhang prove that these are precisely the permutation cases, with \(2^k\mid a\). Equivalently \(\ell=n/\gcd(n,a)\) is odd, so every classified nonlinear permutation is affine equivalent to \(\chi_\ell^{\times d}\).

Conversely every divisor \(\ell>1\) of \(n_0\) occurs: take \(a=n/\ell\) and the branch \(b=2a\). Hence there are at most \(\tau(n_0)-1\) affine classes.

Kriepke--Kyureghyan prove for odd \(\ell\) that
\[
\deg(\chi_\ell^{-1})=(\ell+1)/2.
\]
The inverse of a direct product is the direct product of the inverses, and algebraic degree is invariant under invertible affine changes of input and output. Therefore every map in the class indexed by \(\ell\) has inverse degree \((\ell+1)/2\). Distinct odd \(\ell\) give distinct inverse degrees, so two different block lengths cannot be affine equivalent. The class count is therefore exactly \(\tau(n_0)-1\).

The same Kriepke--Kyureghyan results give the order and exact degree of every iterate of \(\chi_\ell\). Direct products preserve the order and maximum coordinate degree, and affine conjugacy preserves both. Fixed points of a direct product multiply, proving the remaining formulas.

## Cryptographic consequence

The even-dimensional permutations supplied by this family are not new irreducible \(n\)-bit nonlinear layers. If \(k>0\), then every valid step has
\[
d=\gcd(n,v)\ge 2^k,
\]
so the state splits into at least \(2^k\) independent nonlinear blocks, each of odd size at most \(n_0\). Increasing the 2-adic part of the nominal state dimension therefore does not increase the inverse algebraic degree. For example, at \(n=320=64\cdot5\), every nonlinear member is affine equivalent to 64 parallel copies of \(\chi_5\), and every inverse has algebraic degree 3.

If \(n_0\) is prime, all nonlinear permutations in the classified family lie in one affine-equivalence class despite the multiple shift parameterizations counted in the source paper.

## Relation to prior work

Feng, Wang, Yu and Zhang completely characterize the relevant shift-invariant quadratic permutations and state that all permutation cases are shift equivalent to their two explicit families. Their proof also introduces the cycles generated by the shift step, but it does not state the direct-product affine classification, the exact number of affine classes, or the transferred inverse-degree formula. The paper only remarks that the behavior of iterates of its two explicit families is similar.

Kriepke and Kyureghyan determine the order, inverse degree, iterate degrees and fixed-point structure of the ordinary odd-dimensional chi map. Those results are used here as input after the block decomposition and are not claimed as new.

Targeted searches for the new paper together with `inverse degree`, `direct product`, `block decomposition`, and `affine equivalence`, as well as searches of the current SCOPE archive by source identifier and claim family, found no earlier statement of this collapse. Because the motivating preprint is very recent and the decomposition is elementary once the step cycles are isolated, near-simultaneous or folklore coverage remains a material originality risk.

## Limitations

The affine-equivalence statement concerns the quadratic shift-invariant family classified in arXiv:2609.19548v1, not all low-latency Boolean permutations. The exact order and iterate/fixed-point formulas are asserted for the two explicit families, for which the reduction is by conjugacy; arbitrary members of Corollary 3 are only two-sided affine equivalent, which need not preserve iteration dynamics. No claim is made that the ordinary chi invariants used here are new.

`artifacts/verify_block_collapse.py` exhaustively checks the two block identities for all admissible \((n,v)\) with \(3\le n\le13\), verifies the inverse-degree formula on representative dimensions through 15, and verifies the order formula on representative cases.
