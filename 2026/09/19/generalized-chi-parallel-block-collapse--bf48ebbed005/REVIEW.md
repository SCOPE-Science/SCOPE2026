# Review

**Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.**

## Correctness

PASS. Regrouping coordinates along the cycles generated by the shift step gives an exact direct product, not an asymptotic or heuristic reduction. The second explicit family is conjugate blockwise through the explicit affine involution A(z)_t=1+z_{-t}. Substitution into the two branches of Corollary 3 shows that the general classified permutation differs only by input/output coordinate shifts. The inverse-degree, order and iterate statements then use published invariants of the ordinary odd-dimensional chi map. Distinct block lengths have distinct inverse degrees, proving that the divisor-indexed affine classes are genuinely different. Exhaustive small-instance checks agree with the formulas.

Potential failure modes were checked explicitly: gcd cycles cover every coordinate exactly once; the second-family conjugacy includes the required complement as well as reversal; the class-count lower bound uses inverse degree rather than assuming different gcd values are inequivalent; and dynamics are not transferred to a merely two-sided affine-equivalent general map.

## Originality

PASS, to the best of our knowledge. The 2026 preprint proves the permutation classification and notes that its explicit families have similar iterate behavior. Its proof uses shift cycles while studying collision equations, so the direct-product observation is close to the source and carries nontrivial folklore/near-simultaneous risk. However, the inspected v1 does not state the affine product decomposition, the tau(n0)-1 affine-class count, or the exact inverse-degree formula for the newly classified family. Searches combining the paper identifier/title and generalized-chi notation with inverse degree, direct product, block decomposition and affine equivalence found no prior coverage. Searches of the current SCOPE archive by arXiv identifier, authors and claim family found no overlap.

The most relevant prior source, Kriepke--Kyureghyan (CRYPTO 2024 / ePrint 2024/801), was inspected for the exact ordinary-chi inverse degree, order and iterate-degree theorems. Those theorems are prior art and are used rather than re-claimed. Related ChiChi and generalized-chi constructions concern different families and do not, in the material inspected, state this classification for the Feng--Wang--Yu--Zhang maps.

## Value

PASS. The result changes the interpretation of the new even-dimensional family: nominal dimension can grow through parallel repetition without increasing the size of the nonlinear block or the inverse algebraic degree. It also compresses a linear-size parameterized family to tau(n0)-1 affine types and transfers exact known chi invariants immediately. This is directly relevant when evaluating the family as a cryptographic nonlinear layer.

## Limitations

The decomposition is elementary after the correct coordinate grouping, so independent discovery is plausible. The result applies only to the shift-invariant quadratic family classified in arXiv:2609.19548v1. Iteration dynamics are transferred only for the two explicit conjugate families; a general Corollary 3 representative is merely two-sided affine equivalent. No independent validation or independent audit has been performed.
