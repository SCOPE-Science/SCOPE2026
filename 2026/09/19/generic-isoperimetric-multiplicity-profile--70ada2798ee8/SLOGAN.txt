For a generic smooth metric on a closed connected manifold, the volume fractions with a unique isoperimetric region form a dense G_delta set containing every rational fraction except 1/2. The normalized L1 diameter of the minimizer fiber is upper semicontinuous, its positive superlevel sets are closed nowhere dense, and the unique minimizer varies continuously in strict BV; at half volume nearby minimizers collapse to the complementary minimizing pair modulo complementation.
