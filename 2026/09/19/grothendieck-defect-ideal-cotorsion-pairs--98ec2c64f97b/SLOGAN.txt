Finite-index subgroups \(H\le K_0(\mathcal S)\) turn the Ren--Wang parity obstruction into a finite abelian defect group \(G=K_0(\mathcal S)/H\): the associated ideal cotorsion pair remains complete, while special object approximations exist exactly when two truncation classes vanish in \(G\). Every pair in \(G\times G\) occurs, and every finite abelian group is realizable as a defect group.
