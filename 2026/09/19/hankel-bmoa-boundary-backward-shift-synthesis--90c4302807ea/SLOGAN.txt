The operator L_g in arXiv:2609.19311v1 is the classical analytic Hankel operator with matrix (alpha_{m+j}), so it is bounded on H^2 exactly when g is in BMOA, not for every g in H^2. The explicit choice g=f=sum_{n>=0}(n+1)^(-2/3) z^n gives L_g f outside H^2, invalidating Lemma 2.1 as stated and leaving the downstream projection-rigidity proof chain unestablished.
