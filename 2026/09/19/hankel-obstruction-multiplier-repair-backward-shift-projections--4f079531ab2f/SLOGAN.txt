The all-H2 synthesis operator used in arXiv:2609.19311v1 is a Hankel matrix and fails already for coefficients (n+1)^(-3/4); boundedness has the classical BMOA threshold. The intended projection rigidity nevertheless survives: for a backward shift of arbitrary Hilbert multiplicity, every nonzero coefficient shadow of a minimal invariant subspace has one common model space, by a direct inner-multiplier argument.
