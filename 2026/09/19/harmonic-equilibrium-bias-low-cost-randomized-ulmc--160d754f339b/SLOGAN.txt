On a harmonic target, the new low-cost LC-REI heats position by +alpha h^2/6 and velocity by +alpha^2 kappa h^2/3 at equilibrium, while ALUM cools position by the same leading amount and RMM cancels both h^2 defects. More generally, no one-gradient predictor obtained by changing only the random intermediate-time law and predictor-noise amplitude can match the full Gibbs covariance through order h^2.
