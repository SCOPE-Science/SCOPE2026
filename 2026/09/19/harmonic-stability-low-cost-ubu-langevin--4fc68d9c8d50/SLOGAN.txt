The new low-cost UBU Langevin integrators have exact, sharply different harmonic stability regions: LC-UBU permits s<2z coth(z/2), LCT-UBU additionally requires z<2, while LCP-UBU permits s<4(z+2)(z+4)/(z^2+8z+8). Consequently, at fixed quadratic curvature and large friction, their maximum stable steps scale respectively like 2gamma/lambda, 2/gamma, and 2/sqrt(lambda).
