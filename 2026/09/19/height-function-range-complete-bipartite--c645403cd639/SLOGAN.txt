Complete bipartite graphs have explicit two-point range laws for standard and lazy integer height functions: \(\widehat h(K_{a,b})=2-2/(2^a+2^b-2)\) and \(h(K_{a,b})=2-2^{a+b}/N_L(a,b)\), with \(N_L=3^a+3^b+2^{a+b}-2^{a+1}-2^{b+1}+1\). At fixed order, stars uniquely maximize both expectations and balanced bicliques uniquely minimize them; in the lazy model these extremes tend to 2 and 1 respectively.
