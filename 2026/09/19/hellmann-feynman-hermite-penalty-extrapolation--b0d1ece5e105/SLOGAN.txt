Hellmann–Feynman derivatives turn q penalized constrained eigenpairs into a 2q-order inverse-penalty Hermite extrapolation. With penalty levels rho and 2rho, 5 f(rho)-4 f(2rho)+rho f'(rho)+8 rho f'(2rho) improves the generic two-level value-only O(rho^-2) estimate to O(rho^-4) without another eigenpair solve.
