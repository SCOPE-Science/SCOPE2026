Higher Neil-type constraints admit an exact pre-quadratic reduction: the section radius is governed by a classical Schur coefficient functional.  Szász extremals and zero-free binomial sections give explicit sharp radii for the first four nonconstant sections and, for every fixed offset, an eventual algebraic root law with asymptotic constant λ_k=log(2∑_{j≤k}(binom(2j,j)/4^j)^2).
