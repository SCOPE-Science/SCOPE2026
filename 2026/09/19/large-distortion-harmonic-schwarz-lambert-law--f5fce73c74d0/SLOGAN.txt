For the sharp centered harmonic Schwarz constant under pointwise distortion, the extremal ellipse flattens at the non-algebraic rate
tau_K ~ 1/(K log K), more precisely tau_K = 1/(K[-W_{-1}(-e/(4K))])(1+o(1)).
This yields 4/pi-M_K ~ 2/(pi K^2 log K) and an exact L2 boundary-degeneration law with rate 2/sqrt(pi K log K).
