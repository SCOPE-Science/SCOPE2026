For the largest positive ultraspherical zero, the square-root scale has an exact complete-Bernstein threshold: sqrt(lambda+d) z_{n,1}(lambda) works exactly for d <= (2 h_n^2+2n-1)/4, where h_n is the largest Hermite zero. This closes the only nonsharp case in the source paper's all-d square-root classification and gives d_n^*/n -> 3/2.
