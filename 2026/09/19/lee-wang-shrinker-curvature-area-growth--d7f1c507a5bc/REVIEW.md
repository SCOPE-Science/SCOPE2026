# Same-model review

**Same-model review: passed. Cross-model review: not yet performed.**

## Correctness

PASS. The formulas were checked independently from the explicit Lee--Wang parametrization. The canonical scaling converts the Lee--Wang relation to the standard self-shrinker convention. The induced metric is conformal after the angular rescaling y=√(pq)θ, so the Gaussian-curvature formula follows from the two-dimensional conformal-curvature identity. The total-curvature integral reduces to an exact derivative after t=tanh μ. The Euclidean ball-area formula follows from the exact radial coordinate |Y|² and direct integration of the area form. Differentiating the resulting area ratio reduces its critical-point equation to monotonicity of a sinh(2a), which proves uniqueness of the overshoot. The asymptotic expansion follows from the exact radical/arcosh expression. For the Möbius quotient, the twofold covering halves area and total curvature, and the metric lower bound gives the systolic estimate with a rigid equality case.

The numerical constants for the Möbius peak and density crossing were reproduced by the accompanying standalone script using bounded bisection and the exact scalar equations.

## Originality

PASS, to the best of our knowledge. Lee--Wang (2009) gives the family, metric, area form, self-similar relation, and Schoen--Wolfson cone asymptotics. Braxton--Lee--Zhu (2026) gives the embedded Möbius quotient, its metric/second fundamental form/asymptotic link, proves F-stability, and computes its Gaussian entropy. Those ingredients are treated as prior art.

The inspected recent stability preprint does not state the centered unweighted Euclidean ball-area formula, the unique area-ratio overshoot/crossing law, the logarithmic excess, the exact total Gaussian curvature, or the systole computation. Searches using Lee--Wang shrinker, Möbius shrinker, Hamiltonian-stationary shrinker, area growth/area ratio, Gaussian or total curvature, and geodesic/systole formulations did not locate these claims. The Castro--Lerma classification of Hamiltonian-stationary self-similar Lagrangian surfaces was also checked for equivalent formulations without locating them.

The most relevant source not available for inspection is K. Braxton's forthcoming 2027 thesis *Morse index and nullity of a self-shrinking Möbius band in R^4*, cited by the 2026 stability preprint. Because it concerns the same explicit surface, it could contain overlapping geometric calculations. The motivating preprint is also very recent, so later revisions or unindexed parallel work remain a residual originality risk.

## Value

PASS. The result supplies exact global intrinsic and extrinsic geometry for a newly established stable higher-codimension shrinker and, simultaneously, for the full Lee--Wang family. The nonmonotone centered area-growth law is particularly informative: the shrinker has one sharp finite-radius overshoot, crosses its limiting conical density exactly once, and approaches that density from above with a universal positive logarithmic correction. The exact finite total curvature and, for the embedded Möbius quotient, the sharp systole provide complementary intrinsic invariants.

## Limitations

The general family is treated as a parameterized immersion with multiplicity. The result does not address nonlinear dynamical stability, realization as a singularity of a compact flow, or F-stability of the other Lee--Wang members. Euclidean ball-area growth is different from Gaussian entropy. The forthcoming same-surface thesis was not inspected, and cross-model review has not been performed.
