A monotone degenerate mKdV–Burgers shock has a universal 1/xi tail but a dispersion-dependent logarithmic correction. For fixed end states and viscosity, two dispersion strengths kappa_1 and kappa_2 satisfy xi^2(U_{kappa_1}-U_{kappa_2})/log xi -> 2(kappa_2-kappa_1)/(3s), a translation-invariant fingerprint invisible to coarse two-sided decay bounds.
