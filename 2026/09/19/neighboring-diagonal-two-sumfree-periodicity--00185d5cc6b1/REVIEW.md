# Review

**Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.**

## Correctness

**PASS.** The proposed closed form was checked against the greedy definition
both structurally and computationally.

The decisive structural identities are
\[
(R_f+R_f)\cap R_f=\varnothing
\]
and
\[
[2f+1,3f]+R_f=(\mathbb Z/(5f+1)\mathbb Z)\setminus R_f.
\]
Their interval decompositions are explicit in `RESULT.md`. The first prevents
a periodic-tail member from being generated by earlier tail members; the
second guarantees that every sufficiently large nonmember is a sum of an
earlier prefix element and an earlier tail element. The finite transition
before the periodic regime is separately covered by exact interval sumsets.
This supplies both halves required by the greedy definition: every listed
integer is admissible, and every omitted integer is forbidden.

Potential boundary failures were checked at the smallest allowed values.
The same formula works for \(f=3,4\), but \(f=4\) has the exceptional minimal
difference period \(2\) because the length-six displayed word is three copies
of \((1,6)\). This agrees with the exceptional period value stated in the
motivating preprint. The \(f=3\) period is the paper's displayed
\((1,4,1,5,5)\) example.

The verification artifact checks the modular identities for
\(3\le f\le500\) and 1200 greedily generated terms for every
\(3\le f\le100\). It is supporting evidence, not a substitute for the general
proof.

## Originality

**PASS, to the best of our knowledge.** The full text of van Berkel and
Bosma's arXiv:2609.18522v1 was inspected. It states the general periodicity
conjecture, gives a precise conjectural period-length function, reports large
computations, and identifies proved families through \(g\le2f\). Its
Conjecture 5 predicts the period on \(d=g-f=f+1\), including the exceptional
\(f=4\) value, but the inspected text does not state a theorem or closed
membership formula for the whole \(g=2f+1\) family.

The preceding arXiv:2609.16843v1 was also checked for its stated proved
region; the later paper summarizes that theorem as
\(f+1\le g<2f\). A 2025 Journal of Integer Sequences paper discussing greedy
sumfree sequences treats the broader historical periodicity question rather
than this parameterized family.

Searches were made for the exact notation and synonymous formulations,
including `S_{f,2f+1}`, `g=2f+1`, strict greedy 2-sumfree periodicity, the
modulus `5f+1`, and the predicted period `f+2`. They returned the motivating
preprint and general background, but no prior theorem covering the formula
proved here. No inaccessible source was identified whose title or indexed
statement specifically suggests this same family. Because the motivating
preprint was posted only days ago, contemporaneous unindexed work remains the
main residual originality risk.

## Value

**PASS.** The theorem proves an infinite strip immediately beyond the current
proved boundary \(g\le2f\), confirms Conjecture 5 there, and upgrades a
period-length prediction to an exact closed description of every term. The
identity
\[
Q+R=R^c
\]
also isolates a compact mechanism for proving greediness of an eventual
residue pattern, potentially useful in neighboring families.

The result is deliberately not presented as progress on the full
two-parameter periodicity conjecture beyond this strip.

## Limitations

No claim is made for \(f=1,2\), for \(g\ge2f+2\), or for optimal general
criteria forcing periodicity. No independent validation or formal
proof-assistant verification has been performed.
