For a finite nilpotent group G=P×H, where P is supported on the primes dividing k and gcd(k,exp H)=1, the maximum k-power-avoiding set has size |H|A+ε s_k(H), with A and ε determined by the single rooted primary tree. The coprime term is an explicit order-spectrum sum, yielding a closed formula for s_p(C_n) for every cyclic group and every prime p.
