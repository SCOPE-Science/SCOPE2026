For the norm-one torus T in F_{q^2} with q odd, the exact additive length is controlled by chi(1-4/N(S)): every nonzero element has length at most three, |T+T|=(q^2+2q+3)/2, and T+T+T misses only 0 exactly when q is 1 modulo 3. This extends the recent characteristic-three torus decomposition to every odd characteristic and exposes the precise obstruction hidden by the identity 4=1.
