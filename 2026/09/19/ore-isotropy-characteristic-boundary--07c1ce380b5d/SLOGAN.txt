The unbounded-isotropy criterion for local nilpotence on A_h holds over every characteristic-zero field: algebraic closedness is unnecessary. This is sharp in the field characteristic, since in every characteristic p>0 the algebra A_x has the locally finite non-LND E(x)=x, E(t)=0 whose isotropy contains t↦t+q(x^p) and therefore automorphisms of unbounded degree.
