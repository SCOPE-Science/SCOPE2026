The distinct-index kth Walsh square function of a singleton has an exact binomial Lp profile, with growth n^{k/p} below p=2 and n^{k/2} above p=2, plus an explicit 1/log n critical crossover. This exact identity corrects a normalization error in Lemma 6.3 of arXiv:2609.09040v1 while preserving that paper's claimed optimality of the fractional exponent k/p.
