Constant modular Laplacian dynamics modulo p^r has an exact prime-power renormalization: the kernel at time p^n is a p^(n-r+1)-dilation of the fixed kernel at time p^(r-1). More generally, every time admits an exact base-p digit factorization, rigorously explaining the binary-like mod-4/mod-8 and ternary-like mod-9 clocks reported computationally in arXiv:2609.20416v1.
