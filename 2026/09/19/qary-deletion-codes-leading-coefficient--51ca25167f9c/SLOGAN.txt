The `2t-1` existential deletion-code redundancy coefficient is not binary-specific: every q-ary alphabet admits t-deletion-correcting codes with bit redundancy `(2t-1) log_2 n + O_t(log q + log log n)`. The binary substring-spectrum proof transfers once complement boundary bits are replaced by arbitrary symbols unequal to the edited symbol; all remaining alphabet dependence contributes only `q^{O_t(R)}` in witness descriptions.
