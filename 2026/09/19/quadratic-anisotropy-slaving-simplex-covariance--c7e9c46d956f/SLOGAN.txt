For every anisotropic covariance trajectory in the diffusive simplex-volume model with 2 <= n < d, the traceless mode decays at alpha_perp while forcing the mean covariance to approach equilibrium from above at exactly 2 alpha_perp. The ratio of the mean excess to squared Frobenius anisotropy converges to the universal constant n(n-1)/(2 d lambda_* [d(n-2)+n]), and the simplex interaction energy has an analogous universal quadratic tail.
