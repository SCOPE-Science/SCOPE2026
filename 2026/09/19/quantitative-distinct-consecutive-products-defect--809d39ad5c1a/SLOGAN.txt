Chojecki's canonical density-one set with distinct consecutive-block products is quantitatively denser than the published theorem states: its complement is O_B(X(log X)^(-B)) for every fixed B. Using Li's 1/24 almost-all prime-interval theorem and re-optimizing the witness forest lowers the short rejected-gap loss from X^(9/10+o(1)) to X^(5/6+epsilon).
