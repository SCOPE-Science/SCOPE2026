For an n-vertex connected complete multipartite graph, the random greedy MIS is the entire part containing the first inspected vertex, so its size has the size-biased part-size law. Its variance is uniquely maximized by the complete split graph K_{a_n,1,...,1}, where a_n=ceil((3n+sqrt(9n^2-16n))/8), giving asymptotic maximum (27/256)n^2.
