For a uniform random length-n word over any fixed d-letter alphabet, the expected deficit in distinct substrings is n log_d n + O_d(n), so E[D_n]=n(n+1)/2-n log_d n+O_d(n). A finite collision sandwich also shows that the random expectation is within O_d(n), explicitly at most 3dn/[2(d-1)], of the maximum possible distinct-substring count.
