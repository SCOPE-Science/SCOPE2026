For a uniform random length-n word over a fixed d-letter alphabet, the distinct-substring deficit has the second-order expansion R_{n,d}=n log_d n+n P_d({log_d n})+O_d((log n)^2), where P_d is an explicit one-periodic function with Fourier coefficients Gamma(1-chi)/(log(d) chi(1+chi)). For d=2 its mean is -1.109948863612... and its peak-to-peak digital oscillation is about 3.45e-7; an independent finite collision sandwich also gives the coefficient-one logarithmic deficit and an O_d(n) additive gap from the extremal count.
