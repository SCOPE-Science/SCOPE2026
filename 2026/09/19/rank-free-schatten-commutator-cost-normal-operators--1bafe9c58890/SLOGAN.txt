For 1/p+1/q<1/2 and 1/r=1/p+1/q, the optimal product of S_p and S_q factor norms in a commutator representation of a finite-rank normal T is comparable, with rank-free constants, to ||T||_r. For every nonzero positive finite-rank target this gives an exact existence phase transition at 1/p+1/q=1/2, quantitatively sharpening the classical Brown--Anderson threshold.
