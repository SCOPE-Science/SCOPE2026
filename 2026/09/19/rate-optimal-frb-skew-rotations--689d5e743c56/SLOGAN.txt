For the FRB/RFB skew family A=γLJ, B=LJ, the exact asymptotic spectral radius is uniquely minimized for γ>-1 at λL=1/(2√(1+γ)), where the characteristic roots coalesce, giving rate 1/√(γ+2). Thus the largest stable step can be far from the fastest one: at matched skew γ=1 the rate-optimal step is 1/(2√2 L), not the sharp stability ceiling 1/(2L).
