The generalized packing–covering conjecture holds for every linear code of redundancy at most 15 over every finite field. The sole new residual case is binary \((\rho,t,R_t)=(15,3,6)\); shortening its dual reduces the problem to a \([N,6]\) code with \(d_4\ge N-4\), and a PG(5,2) multiplicity argument forces \(N\le64\), after which the exact covering-volume inequality is impossible.
