Finite positive step packets in the scale-invariant m-fold 2D Euler reduction have a universal renormalized attractor: after scaling y=c_* t theta by the outermost plateau height, every packet converges in L1 to c_* 1_(0,1]. Consequently t||g(t)||_1 -> 1; for a single step the inner edge satisfies a~Lambda b^2 and 1/b=ct-2 cot(2pi/m) log t+O(1).
