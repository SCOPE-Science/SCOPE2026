For centered Gaussian magnitudes, Rényi total correlation at order gamma>1 is finite exactly when lambda_max(R)<gamma/(gamma-1), so folding away all signs does not enlarge the likelihood-integrability range. Integer orders admit an exact finite sign-determinant formula; at order two an even-Hermite Parseval identity gives the bivariate law D2=-log(1-rho^4) and quartic pairwise / sixth-order triangle dependence near independence.
