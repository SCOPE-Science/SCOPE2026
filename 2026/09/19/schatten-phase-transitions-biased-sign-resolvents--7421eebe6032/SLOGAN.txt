Acuaviva's biased-sign resolvents exhibit an entropy-energy Schatten transition: for weights exp(c j^gamma), subexponential gamma<1 gives no finite Schatten membership, superexponential gamma>1 gives every S_p, and geometric weights have critical exponent log_a 2 with an exact weak-Schatten boundary. Finite Schatten membership also forces the resolvent measure to be absolutely continuous, so the singular compact resolvents in the Schur example lie outside every finite Schatten class.
