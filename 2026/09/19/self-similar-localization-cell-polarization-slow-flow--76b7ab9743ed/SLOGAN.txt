For the zero-diffusion slow cell-polarization flow, an exact threshold coordinate obeys the localization clock t G(sigma(t)) -> 1. Homogeneous signal maxima therefore force explicit self-similar rates; in the generic Morse case the peak width is t^(-1/4), support area t^(-1/2), peak height t^(1/2), and the rescaled profile is an explicit elliptical positive part.
