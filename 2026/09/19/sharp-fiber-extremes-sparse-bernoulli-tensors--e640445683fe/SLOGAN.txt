At logarithmic sparsity d=c log n, the maximum fiber of an order-k Bernoulli tensor has a sharp large-deviation constant x determined by c(x log x-x+1)=k-1, with a -log log n/(2 log x) second-order correction. This forces any n^{-r} spectral-norm bound to use C at least sqrt(h^{-1}((k-1+r)/c)) and yields an explicit Lambert-W divergence below logarithmic sparsity.
