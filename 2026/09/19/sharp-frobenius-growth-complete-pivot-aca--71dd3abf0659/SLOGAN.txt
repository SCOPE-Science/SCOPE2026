A complete-pivot ACA step on an n by m residual can enlarge its Frobenius norm by at most sqrt(nm/(n+m-1)), and this scale is sharp: in N by N matrices a unique largest pivot can cause amplification arbitrarily close to N/sqrt(2N-1) ~ sqrt(N/2). Largest-entry pivoting is therefore not a Frobenius-descent rule, although the positive-semidefinite diagonal-pivot case remains monotone.
