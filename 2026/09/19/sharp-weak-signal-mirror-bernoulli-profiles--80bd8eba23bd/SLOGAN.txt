For Smirnov's mirror Bernoulli products, weak-signal computational-basis efficiency is the first absolute moment of the normalized Rademacher profile up to a uniform O(||a||_2^2) error. All subsequential efficiencies are Rademacher--Gaussian profile moments and fill exactly [1/sqrt(2),1], with the diffuse regime converging to sqrt(2/pi); the same expansion yields d_TV(P,Q) ~ sqrt(V/pi) in a diffuse local Bernoulli-product regime.
