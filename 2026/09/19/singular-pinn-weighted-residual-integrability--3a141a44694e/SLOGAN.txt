For smooth hard Dirichlet trials \(\widehat u\sim a d\) in \(-\Delta u=u^{-\alpha}\), the strong residual scales as \(d^{-\alpha}\). The standard squared residual is locally integrable only for \(\alpha<1/2\), while the proposed factor \(1+\beta\widehat u^{-\alpha}\) lowers the threshold to \(\alpha<1/3\); at \(\alpha=1/2\) the fixed-trial weighted quasi-uniform loss grows as \(N^{1/2}\).
