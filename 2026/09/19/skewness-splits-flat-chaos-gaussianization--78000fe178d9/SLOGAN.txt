For flat signed second Gaussian chaos, a nonzero spectral sign imbalance makes maxima Gaussianize only beyond fourth effective rank of order (log p)^3, while exact sign balance lowers the sharp scale to (log p)^2. Two indefinite spectra can have the same fourth effective rank and fourth cumulant at every p yet have Kolmogorov distance to the Gaussian maximum tending to 1 and 0 respectively.
