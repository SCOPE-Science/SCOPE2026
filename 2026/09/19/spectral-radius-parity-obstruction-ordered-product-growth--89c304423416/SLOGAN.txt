The spectral-radius block statistic h_L of arXiv:2609.18017v1 need not converge to the maximal Lyapunov exponent: a smooth planar period-two map has every one-step spectral radius below one, positive Lyapunov exponent, and h_L alternating forever between negative odd-horizon and positive even-horizon values. A largest-singular-value block rate has the correct subadditive Lyapunov limit and is exact at every horizon in the counterexample.
