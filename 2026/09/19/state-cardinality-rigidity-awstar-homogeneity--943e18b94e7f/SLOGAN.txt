Under Arulseelan's simultaneous kappa-homogeneity and kappa-state-separation hypotheses, the cardinal is intrinsic: kappa=max(aleph_0,s(M)), and the algebra is lambda-homogeneous exactly for infinite lambda<=kappa. In the local faithful-corner theorem, the maximal p-copy cardinal is therefore choice-independent; it is countable exactly in the global faithful-state regime, and otherwise equals the minimum size of a separating family of ordinary states.
