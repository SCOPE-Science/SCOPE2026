Identical leakage across a linear computation is controlled by the minimum B-rank of a computation-code word: a one-subsymbol product attack compresses to at most that many B-subsymbols per base share. For one weighted linear output, rank one occurs exactly when the coefficient classes in F/B are dependent, forcing no genuine one-subsymbol gain whenever K >= [F:B], and in particular for every two-input relation over a quadratic extension.
