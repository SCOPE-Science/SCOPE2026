Among 2-connected n-vertex graphs containing an (n-2)-clique, the sharp Szeged–Wiener equality η(G)=2n has exactly one isomorphism type for every n≥11: the Zhang–Li clique-plus-two-vertex construction. At n=10 there is exactly one additional type; a complete Venn-cell formula for η classifies all two-vertex extensions of a clique.
