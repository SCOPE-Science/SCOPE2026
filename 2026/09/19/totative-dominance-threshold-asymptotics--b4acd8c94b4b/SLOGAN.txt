The exact dominance thresholds for \(\varphi(n)>k\pi(n)\) and for nonprime versus prime totatives have logarithms governed by the same Lambert-W inverse. In particular, \(\log N_k=Y(k)(1+O(e^{-c\sqrt{\log k}}))\), \(\log M_k=Y(k+1)(1+O(e^{-c\sqrt{\log k}}))\), so the conjectured pair \(M_k,N_{k+1}\) is exponentially close on the logarithmic scale.
