Two summands have a sharp positive ordinary-modulus median barrier that disappears for three summands in the first critical dimension. If |A+B|>=I in M_{2n-1} or M_{2n}, then lambda_n(|A|+|B|)>=1/sqrt(2); the constant is sharp in M_3, M_6, all odd median dimensions >=3, and all even median dimensions >=6.
