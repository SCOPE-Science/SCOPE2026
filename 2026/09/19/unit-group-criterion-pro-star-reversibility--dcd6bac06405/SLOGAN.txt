For a unital *-ring, pro-*-reversibility is exactly *-reversibility plus pointwise fixation of the entire unit group. Consequently every clean (hence semiperfect, Artinian, or local) pro-*-reversible ring has trivial involution and is commutative, while F_2[x] with f(x)^*=f(x+1) shows that nontrivial involutions remain possible outside the clean setting.
