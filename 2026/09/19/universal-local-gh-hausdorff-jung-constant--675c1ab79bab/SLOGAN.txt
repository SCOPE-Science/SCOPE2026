For every closed Riemannian n-manifold, the best small-scale ratio d_GH(A,M)/d_H(A,M) tends to the Euclidean Jung constant sqrt((n+1)/(2n)), regardless of the sign of curvature. A scale-optimized lower bound has curvature parameter kappa d_H^2, while deleted geodesic balls satisfy d_GH=c_n r+O(r^3), giving the sharp universal asymptotic.
