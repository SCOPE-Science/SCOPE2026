For the newly solved affine dual Minkowski problem with general measures, the exact \(W_p\) distance from normalized data to nonsolvability is the minimum \(L^p\) spherical distance needed to push the measure into a closed hemisphere. This transport depth is the sharp perturbation radius preserving existence, is concave and 1-Lipschitz, is comparable within factor \(\pi/2\) to the centered inradius of a one-sided \(L^p\) moment body, and at \(W_\infty\) equals \(\arcsin\) of the centered inradius of the convex hull of the support.
