The balanced-viscosity micropolar Couette Fourier Green system of arXiv:2609.20109v1 is exactly reducible to Weber's equation, giving a closed 2x2 parabolic-cylinder fundamental matrix. For every fixed nonzero sheared Fourier mode, both singular values share the same cubic enhanced-dissipation exponent while their condition number acquires an explicit quadratic-in-time exponential splitting.
