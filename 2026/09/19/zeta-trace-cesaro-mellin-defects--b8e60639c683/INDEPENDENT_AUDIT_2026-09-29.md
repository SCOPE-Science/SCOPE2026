## Three-axis assessment

- **Correctness — PASS:** PASS. The lattice-sum argument is sound. Writing the kernel product error as a sum-minus-integral error gives |Delta_{p,q}(n,k)| <= C/(nk)(1+log(n/k))^{p+q-2}; summing its square over n>=k yields a column norm O(k^{-3/2}), and the absolutely summable rank-one column decomposition is therefore trace class. The multi-factor statement follows from the two-sided ideal property. Because all W_r are lower triangular and W_r(n,n)=n^{-1} only for r=1, the trace of the trace-class defect is zeta(s) exactly for a pure Cesaro product and zero otherwise. The explicit m=2 harmonic-number formula is consistent with the same calculation.

The result is therefore a plausible new sharpening, while older Cesaro/Hausdorff and Euler--Maclaurin literature remains a residual terminology risk rather than demonstrated coverage.

The scope is appropriately limited to the integer Cesaro convolution semigroup.
