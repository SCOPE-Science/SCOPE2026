# Independent scientific audit

## Final claim

For the integer Mellin-convolution semigroup generated by the Cesaro kernel, the sampled-ratio quantization is multiplicative modulo trace class; more generally every finite integer-power defect in this semigroup is trace class, and the pure Cesaro power defect has trace \(\zeta(m)\) while the mixed defects have the stated zero trace.

## Correctness — PASS

For the explicit kernels \(\omega_r(u)=(-\log u)^{r-1}/(r-1)!\) on \((0,1]\), the product defect is a lower-triangular lattice-sum minus integral error. The polynomial-exponential variation estimate gives a summable column-norm bound of order \(k^{-3/2}\), so the column decomposition converges in trace norm. The diagonal is multiplicative across factors; \(\omega_r(1)=0\) for \(r\ge2\), while \(\omega_1(1)=1\), so trace-class diagonal summation gives zero for mixed defects and \(\sum_{k\ge1}k^{-m}=\zeta(m)\) for the pure Cesaro power defect.

**Residual risks.** The trace-norm bound is semigroup-specific and does not identify the optimal Schatten class for arbitrary source kernels.

The Bellavita--Stylogiannis primary paper proves only a Hilbert--Schmidt multiplicative defect for its general kernel algebra and explicitly lists Schatten refinements as an open direction. The 2025 Rhaly paper classifies Schatten membership of Rhaly matrices themselves, not these Mellin product defects.
