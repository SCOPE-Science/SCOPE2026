The diagonal-corrected additive forward/backward Gauss-Seidel inverse B=(D+L)^{-1}+(D+L^T)^{-1}-D^{-1} has the exact factorization B=(D+L^T)^{-1}(D-L^TD^{-1}L)(D+L)^{-1}, so it is SPD exactly when ||D^{-1/2}LD^{-1/2}||_2<1. Under weak coupling it cancels all first-order inverse error and yields quadratic spectral clustering, while an explicit 3x3 example shows that SPD of A alone does not make B SPD.
