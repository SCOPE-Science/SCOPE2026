For every graph Γ and every prime power q, irreducible characters of the graphical group G_Γ(F_q) are enumerated by ranks of Rossmann's alternating support matrix in every characteristic: ch(Γ,i;q)=q^(n-2i)·#{y: rank B_Γ(y)=2i}. This removes the odd-characteristic restriction and gives closed formulas for complete bipartite graphs.
