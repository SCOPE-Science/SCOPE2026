Asymmetrizing the two halves of the recent balanced-tripartite triangle construction gives f(n,t) <= F_t(p)+F_t(q) whenever n >= 3t+p+q. Choosing (p,q)=(floor(sqrt(2)t)+1,t) disproves the 4t^3 bound already for n >= 4t+floor(sqrt(2)t)+1, improving the known threshold coefficient from about 6.236 to 5.414.
