At the rational order ν = -15/16, the function x^(ν+1)e^(-x)I_ν(x) has F''(2) < 0, giving an exact counterexample to Baricz's 2010 question asking for strict complete monotonicity throughout ν ∈ (-1,-1/2]. The sign is certified by finite rational bounds on I_{1/16}(2)/I_{-15/16}(2) and persists on an open neighborhood of -15/16.
