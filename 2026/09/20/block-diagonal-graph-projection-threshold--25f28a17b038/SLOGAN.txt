For maximal diagonal operators from an ℓ_p-sum to an ℓ_q-sum with p<q, the relative projection constant of the graph is, within universal factors, the threshold balance between the retained weight size and the ℓ_r norm of the reciprocal large-weight tail. For weights n^α this gives a re-entrant complementability transition and sharp finite-section growth N^{min(α,δ-α)}, with the critical law (log N)^δ.
