Keeping the full signed neural-spectrahedron block before taking a nuclear norm gives a convex QNN regularization lower bound that pointwise dominates both recent separate nuclear/Euclidean lower bounds. The induced output-weight regularizer is exactly max(|s|,|t|) on aligned rank-one aggregates (hence an l-infinity penalty for scalar input), while an orthogonal rank-one family has the exact closed form (S^2+2ST+2T^2)/(S+2T).
