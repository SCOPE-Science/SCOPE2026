For n=3q with q>=3, the path blow-up B(1,2,3,...,3,2,1) has gpn=(121/36)3^q+(33/2)q-141/4 and exceeds the equal-layer G_{3,q} construction by exactly 10*3^(q-2)-15. This improves the leading construction constant from 9/4 to 121/36 and supplies the construction side of Knor et al.'s 2026 Problem 14.
