For the classical Burke–Shaw flow, two exact quadratic balance laws split the forcing parameter into three rigorous regimes: all trajectories escape for d<0, every trajectory converges to the equilibrium line for d=0, and every bounded d>0 regime satisfies <x^2>=d/g and <z>=1/k+(g/(kd))<(x+y)^2>. The positive-forcing identities apply equally to periodic orbits and compactly supported invariant measures.
