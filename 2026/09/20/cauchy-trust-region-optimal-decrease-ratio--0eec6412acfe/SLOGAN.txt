For every SPD quadratic trust-region subproblem, the Cauchy point achieves at least 1/[(u^TBu)(u^TB^{-1}u)] of the exact predicted decrease, uniformly over the trust radius. Hence the sharp condition-number-only fraction is 4kappa/(kappa+1)^2, and fraction-of-Cauchy decrease converts sharply to fraction-of-optimal decrease on uniformly SPD models.
