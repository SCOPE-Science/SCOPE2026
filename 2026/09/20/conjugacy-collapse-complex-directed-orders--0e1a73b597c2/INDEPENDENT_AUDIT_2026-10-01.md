# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The algebraic-conjugacy proof reconstructs. K=k·Qbar is algebraic over k, so every T in X_k is transcendental over K. The source's trdeg_k R=1 observation makes C algebraic over k(T), hence over K(T); since C is algebraically closed it is an algebraic closure of K(T). A K-isomorphism K(T)->K(U) therefore extends to an automorphism of C, and because it fixes k and all algebraic numbers it carries the localized base ring, its integral closure and the cone P_T to their U analogues. Continuum distinctness follows from T_c=pi+c pi^2 and the source equality criterion. Finally, every continuous field automorphism of C fixes R pointwise and is identity or complex conjugation; both stabilize each P_T, proving that conjugacy between distinct cones is discontinuous.
