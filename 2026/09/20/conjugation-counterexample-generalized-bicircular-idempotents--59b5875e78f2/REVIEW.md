# Same-model review

**Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.**

## Correctness

The construction was checked directly from conjugate-linearity. For unit phases \(\lambda_1\ne\lambda_2\), the identity
\[
\overline{\lambda_1-\lambda_2}
=-\frac{\lambda_1-\lambda_2}{\lambda_1\lambda_2}
\]
implies \(JP_i=\lambda_iP_i\). This yields complementary orthogonal idempotents and \(\lambda_1P_1+\lambda_2P_2=J\). The exact phase classification follows by restricting \(aJ+bI\) to a complex line generated by a fixed vector of \(J\); constancy of \(|ae^{-it}+be^{it}|\) forces \(a\overline b=0\).

The concrete \(C^1[0,1]\) example was also checked on the constant function \(i\): for \((\lambda_1,\lambda_2)=(1,i)\), one gets \(P_1(i)=1\), \(P_2(i)=i-1\), and hence \(\|(P_1-P_2)i\|_\sigma=\sqrt5\ne1=\|i\|_\sigma\). Pointwise conjugation is exactly a Form IV isometry with \(c=1\), \(\beta=1\), and \(\phi=\mathrm{id}\), so the example directly targets Theorem 3.4 rather than merely the abstract summary.

## Originality

Originality is asserted only **to the best of our knowledge** and only for the identification/formalization of this counterexample to the current arXiv statement together with the exact phase-locus observation. The general phenomenon that generalized bi-circular projections can fail to be averages of the identity with an isometric reflection is older: Abubaker--Botelho--Jamison (arXiv:1208.2012; 2014 publication) explicitly give such examples. Botelho--Miura's 2019 corrigendum also states that a previous differentiable-function-space classification omitted an additional case. Accordingly, no claim is made that conjugation-generated examples are historically first here.

The current arXiv:2609.18967v1 was inspected at its abstract, definitions, Form IV isometry description, and Theorem 3.4. Searches by the exact identifier/title and by combinations of generalized bi-circular idempotent, conjugation, real-linearity, correction, counterexample, and error did not locate a public correction or comment already stating this specific contradiction. The accessible abstract of the 2019 corrigendum was inspected, but its full journal text was not available for inspection in this review. A closely related construction there is therefore the main residual bibliographic risk; it would reduce novelty of the mechanism but would strengthen, rather than remove, the contradiction to the 2026 v1 claim.

## Value

The counterexample falsifies a dichotomy stated in the abstract of a current functional-analysis preprint and the corresponding Form IV theorem. The universal conjugation formulation explains the mechanism and shows that the failure is not a one-off numerical example: every conjugated complex normed space supplies the same obstruction for non-antipodal phase pairs. The exact phase classification identifies precisely why one associated isometry does not imply bi-circularity.

## Limitations

The result does not provide a replacement classification, does not audit all other results in the source paper, and does not establish that the elementary conjugation mechanism itself is new. Only the current v1 statement is assessed; a later revision could correct it. The full 2019 corrigendum was not inspected, and very recent or poorly indexed comments may not have been found.
