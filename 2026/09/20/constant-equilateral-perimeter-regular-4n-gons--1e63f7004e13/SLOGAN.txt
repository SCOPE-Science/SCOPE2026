For every regular \(4n\)-gonal Minkowski norm, every origin-containing inscribed equilateral triangle has one common perimeter, so \(L_3=M_3\). The proof gives explicit constant-length moving triangles for the previously uncovered \(N\equiv4,8\pmod{12}\) families and settles the 2025 \(H_{16}\) and \(H_{32}\) conjectures.
