Final claim assessed: There are explicit one-dimensional Hermite extremal rays whose self-product is not extremal, and Fourier-dually explicit extremals whose self-convolution is not extremal.

The chosen degree-four Hermite parameters lie on the source paper's extremal boundary. Therefore the self-product and its Fourier transform are strictly positive, and the source paper's zero obstruction excludes extremality. Fourier duality gives the convolution statement. Independent symbolic checks reproduce the boundary identity and negative discriminant.
