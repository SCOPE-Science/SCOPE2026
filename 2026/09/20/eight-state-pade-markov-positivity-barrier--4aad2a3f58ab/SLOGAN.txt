For the fourth-order [2/2] Padé / two-stage Gauss–Legendre end-step map, every CTMC generator on at most seven states is positivity-safe for all sufficiently small steps, but an eight-state pure-birth chain is not. For that sharp witness the approximate transition matrix is stochastic exactly at h=0 and on the intermediate window 0.596640561867... <= h <= 2*sqrt(3), so shrinking the step can itself destroy positivity.
