For the Euler entropy used in arXiv:2609.19838v1, minimizing over the stated density-pressure rectangle is not reducible to its four corners: the exact density is the clipped value e^{-1}p_max^{1/gamma}. Moreover, raw-entropy ELCD selection changes under addition of an affine conserved quantity, so it depends on entropy normalization even though the Euler equations and entropy Hessian do not.
