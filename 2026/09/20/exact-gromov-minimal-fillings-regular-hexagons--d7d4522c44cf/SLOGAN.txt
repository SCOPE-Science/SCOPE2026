For any cyclic six-point metric whose three distance levels come from a nondecreasing concave chord profile, the exact one-dimensional Gromov minimal-filling weight is min{a+2b, 3(a+c)/2}. This gives explicit exact formulas for regular hexagons in Euclidean, spherical, and hyperbolic geometry; for Euclidean side length s the value is (1+2sqrt(3))s.
