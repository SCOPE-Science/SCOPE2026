For uniformly randomized two-coordinate Gauss-Seidel with an independent bounded stale-read delay D, the diagonal-scaled mean-square error obeys the exact scalar recurrence S_{k+1}=S_k/2+(r^2/2)E[S_{k-D}], so the asymptotic factor q is the unique root of 2q-1=r^2 E[q^{-D}]. This makes delay ordering exact: larger delays, and at fixed mean more variable delays in convex order, worsen q; near singularity the leading slowdown depends only on the mean delay and variance first enters at second order.
