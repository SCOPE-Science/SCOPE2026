For every m >= 10, the minimum total storage of a t=1 combinatorial batch code with n=m+5 items and retrieval requirement k=6 is exactly N(m+5,6,m)=m+18. A 10-server base code with parameters (15,28,6,10), verified by the restricted Hall condition, combines with the known N(m+4,6,m)=m+16 diagonal and singleton extension to give the infinite family.
