Every fixed finite-index sublattice of the exponent lattice for prime indices p_{r^A s^B} has a complete strong counting asymptotic. Its leading logarithmic constant depends only on the lattice index h, through the exact factor h^{-1/3}; as a collision application, the dependent generators r^q, s^q and rs reduce to A ≡ B (mod q) and retain the (log x)^{2/3} scale rather than a spurious rank-three scale.
