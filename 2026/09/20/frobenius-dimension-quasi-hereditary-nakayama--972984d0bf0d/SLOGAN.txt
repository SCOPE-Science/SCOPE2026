For a connected basic quasi-hereditary Nakayama algebra with n>=2 simples, the Frobenius dimension dim Hom_A(D(A),A) is at most n^2+1, and this bound is attained. In the cyclic case, quasi-heredity forces an endpoint plateau that permits at most one two-dimensional Hom(I_i,P_j); all other injective-projective Hom spaces have dimension at most one.
