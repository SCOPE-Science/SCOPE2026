All subalgebras of a finite Heisenberg Lie algebra split into center-containing subspaces and graphs over totally isotropic subspaces, yielding an exact symplectic formula for subalgebra commutativity degree in every rank. The formula exhibits a field-size phase transition (limits 0, 0, 5/9, then 1 by rank) and shows that, over every fixed finite field, the degree tends to 1 as Heisenberg rank grows.
