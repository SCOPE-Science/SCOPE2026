For any SPD quadratic with entrywise nonnegative inverse, the unique fixed iid coordinate sampling that maximizes the sharp expected A-energy contraction is p proportional to C^{-1}1, where C is the unit-diagonal normalized Hessian. For the 1D Dirichlet Poisson matrix this gives the explicit parabolic profile p_i=6i(n+1-i)/[n(n+1)(n+2)] and improves the sharp contraction constant over uniform sampling by an asymptotic factor 12/pi^2.
