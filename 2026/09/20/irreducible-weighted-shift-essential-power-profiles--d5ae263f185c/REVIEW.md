## Correctness

The proof reconstructs. For a strictly positive submultiplicative sequence \(a_0=1,a_1,a_2,\ldots\), the ratio blocks \(\beta_k=a_{k+1}/a_k\) obey every required contiguous-product upper bound. When a new bridge is appended, only finitely many new crossing products are created, so a positive bridge can be chosen small enough to satisfy all of them. Repetition of every finite prefix arbitrarily far out gives \(\|W^n\|=a_n\) and, along a weakly null orthonormal sequence of block starts, forces \(\|W^n\|_e=a_n\). Positive weights make the shift irreducible. The exact root-rate consequence follows from the submultiplicativity of \(a_n=\rho_n^n\) for nonincreasing \(\rho_n\).
