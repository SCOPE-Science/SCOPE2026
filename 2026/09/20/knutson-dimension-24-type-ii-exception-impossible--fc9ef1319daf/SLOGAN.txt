The sole dimension-24 Type-II exception in Martín Duro's Knutson classification is non-realizable: its three-dimensional simples would have stabilizer order 2, contradicting the Nichols--Zoeller consequence |G[χ]| | (deg χ)^2. Hence every semisimple Hopf algebra over an algebraically closed field of characteristic 0 and of dimension at most 31 is of Knutson type.
