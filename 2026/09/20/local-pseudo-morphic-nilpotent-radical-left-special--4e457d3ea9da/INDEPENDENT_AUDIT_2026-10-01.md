# Independent audit — 2026-10-01

## Final claim

A local ring with nilpotent Jacobson radical is left special as soon as one nonzero element in its last nonzero radical layer is left pseudo-morphic; hence every local left pseudo-morphic ring with nilpotent radical is left special.

## Correctness — PASS

For a nonzero element in the last radical layer, locality makes its left annihilator equal to the Jacobson radical, hence its generated left ideal is simple. Representing that ideal as the annihilator of c forces the last radical layer to equal that annihilator. Right multiplication by c then embeds every radical quotient into the next radical layer, giving finite left length by downward induction. The generated ideal Rc and the radical both have codimension one, so Rc equals the radical; c is nilpotent and the standard left-special criterion applies.
