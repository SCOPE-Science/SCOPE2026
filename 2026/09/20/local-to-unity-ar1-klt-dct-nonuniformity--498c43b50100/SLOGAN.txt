For the principal AR(1) KLT mode, the DCT-II limit is uniform in growing block length only when N(1-rho) tends to zero. Under local-to-unity scaling, the exact KLT-DCT overlap converges to an explicit nontrivial function of the block-length/correlation-length ratio, exposing a sharp rowwise nonuniformity hidden by the fixed-N rho-to-1 limit.
