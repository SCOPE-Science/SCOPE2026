For the classical Lorenz–Stenflo system, weak rotation 0<s<=sigma^2/3 makes the static pitchfork threshold rho=1+s/sigma^2 the exact global stability boundary of the origin, including the nonhyperbolic equality case. An explicit positive-definite storage factorization turns the nonlinear problem into a LaSalle argument and shows why the threshold ceases to be passivity-sharp once the dominant linear gain moves to nonzero frequency.
