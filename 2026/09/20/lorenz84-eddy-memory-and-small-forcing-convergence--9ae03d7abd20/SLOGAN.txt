On every bounded complete Lorenz-84 trajectory, the zonal-flow deficit is the exponentially weighted past eddy energy: F-X(t)=∫_0^∞ e^{-as}(Y²+Z²)(t-s) ds. Every compactly supported invariant measure satisfies E[Y²+Z² | X]=a(F-X); moreover, if F<1 and 2√(1+b²)G²<a(1-F)³, the global attractor collapses to one equilibrium and every trajectory converges to it.
