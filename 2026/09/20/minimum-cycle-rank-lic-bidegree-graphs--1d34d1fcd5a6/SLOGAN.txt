For every two-element positive degree set {a,b}, the first cycle rank permitting a locally irregular-connected realization is exactly b(a-1)/2 when a is odd or b is even, and a(b-1)/2 when a is even and b is odd. All minimum-cycle-rank realizations are classified, including the flexible {2,b} odd family; the known cycle-rank-2 cases {2,3} and {2,4} are recovered exactly.
