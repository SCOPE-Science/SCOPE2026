For every k>=1 and h>=1, the smallest k-stepwise irregular tree with maximum degree 1+hk is unique and has 1+(1+hk) sum_{j=0}^{h-1} ((h-1)!/j!) k^{h-1-j} vertices. The extremizer is the recursively layered tree in which each nonroot class-i vertex has exactly ik children in class i-1; it has radius h and diameter 2h.
