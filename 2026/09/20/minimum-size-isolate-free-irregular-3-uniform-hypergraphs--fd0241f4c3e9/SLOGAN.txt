For every n >= 6, an isolate-free irregular simple 3-uniform hypergraph on n vertices needs at least ceil(n(n+1)/6) edges, and this lower bound is always attainable. Explicit recursive constructions realize degree set {1,...,n}, except for n = 1 or 4 mod 6 where {1,...,n-2,n,n+1} gives the minimum divisibility correction.
