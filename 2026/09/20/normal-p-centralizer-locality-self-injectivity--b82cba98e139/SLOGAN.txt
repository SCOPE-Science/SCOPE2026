For a normal p-subgroup P, (kG)^P is local exactly when C_G(P) is a p-group, and in that local regime it is self-injective/Frobenius/symmetric exactly when P is central. If P acts fixed-point-freely on G\P by conjugation, then (kG)^P is explicitly the square-zero extension Z(kP) ⋉ k^{[G:P]-1} with augmentation action.
