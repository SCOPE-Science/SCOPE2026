# Independent scientific audit

## Final claim

For the integral closure of the corner ideal generated by x squared, y to b, and z to c, nonnormality occurs exactly when b and c are odd and coprime and the midpoint integer one half of bc plus one is absent from the numerical semigroup generated by b and c; the equivalent residue test and explicit second-power witness follow.

## Correctness — PASS

The Reid--Roberts--Vitulli theorem reduces three-variable monomial normality to integral closedness through the second power. Every possible second-power hole reduces to an x-exponent-one factorization, equivalent to whether the two-generator numerical semigroup meets one explicit interval. Even product bc is immediately normal. For odd noncoprime b,c, a Frobenius-bound semigroup point lies in every interval. For odd coprime b,c, exactly one of the adjacent midpoint integers lies in the semigroup, giving the iff classification and explicit hole. An independent finite replay of the exact interval criterion over thousands of pairs found no mismatch.
