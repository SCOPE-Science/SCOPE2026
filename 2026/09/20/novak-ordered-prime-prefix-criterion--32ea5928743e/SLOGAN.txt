Novák numbers admit a canonical increasing-prime construction: after writing n as ordered prime powers, each new prime must divide 2^M+1 for the preceding prime-power prefix M, and this condition is also sufficient. Consequently, primitive Novák numbers with exactly two distinct prime factors are exactly 3^a q with q | Phi_{2*3^a}(2), yielding infinitely many such primitive solutions and P_2(x) >= floor(log_3 log x)-1 for x >= e^9.
