Cheung--Qi's generalized one-parameter mean is always locally convex immediately to the right of its logarithmic-curvature center, so their 2007 global concavity conjecture is false for every parameter and every unequal input pair. For the symmetric product, the near-diagonal expansion gives $(\log[J_\alpha(r)J_\alpha(-r)])''=-(2\alpha/15)u^4+O(u^6)$, disproving the conjectured exterior logarithmic convexity at every prescribed finite exterior parameter.
