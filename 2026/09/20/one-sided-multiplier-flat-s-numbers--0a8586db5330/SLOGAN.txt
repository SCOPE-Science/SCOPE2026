Every nonzero one-sided multiplier on a normalized symmetrically normed Banach ideal of compact operators has a completely flat classical s-number profile: all approximation, Bernstein, Gelfand and Kolmogorov numbers equal its operator norm. A rank-one Hilbertian witness also gives exact distances to compact, finitely strictly singular and strictly singular operators, and yields a sharp essential-norm floor for two-sided multipliers.
