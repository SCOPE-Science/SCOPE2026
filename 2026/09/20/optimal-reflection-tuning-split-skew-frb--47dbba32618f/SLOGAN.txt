For the split-skew test family of constant-reflection forward-reflected-backward splitting, the exact robust reflection weight is (1+sqrt(3))/4. It raises the sharp split-skew step-size ceiling from the standard FRB value 1/2 to sqrt((8/3)(7sqrt(3)-12)) = 0.575860290886..., with two equioscillating worst cases at resolvent-skew ratios 0 and 1/2.
