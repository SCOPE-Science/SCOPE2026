After symmetrization, every occupancy-invariant extremal problem for uniform pairwise-independent allocations has only one remaining constraint: the mean pair-collision count is binom(n,2)/q. Consequently, for 2 <= n <= q the exact all-distinct probability range is [max(0, 1-binom(n,2)/q), 1-1/q], and every value in the interval is attainable.
