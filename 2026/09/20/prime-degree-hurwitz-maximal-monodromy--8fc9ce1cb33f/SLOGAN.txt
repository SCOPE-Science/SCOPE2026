A compatible prime-degree Hurwitz passport whose local profile contains a unique prime part q<=p-3 and no other q-divisible part has forced maximal parity-compatible monodromy in every connected realization: A_p if all defects are even, S_p otherwise. The criterion is exact for detecting a pure q-cycle inside local cyclic inertia, and it determines trivial deck groups and the genus of the Galois closure directly from the passport.
