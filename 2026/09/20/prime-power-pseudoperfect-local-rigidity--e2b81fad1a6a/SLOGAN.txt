Prime power pseudoperfect integrality has an exact local criterion: for p^a || n, n/p^a is congruent to p-1 modulo p^a; for at most four prime supports this is equivalent to the defining Egyptian-fraction identity. Among three-support numbers p^a q r^c with p<q<r and the middle prime occurring once, the only prime power pseudoperfect numbers are 6*7^c.
