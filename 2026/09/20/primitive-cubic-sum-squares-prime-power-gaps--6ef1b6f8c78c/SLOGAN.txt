In a primitive positive solution of x^2+y^2=z^3, a prime-power coordinate gap |x-y| forces the Gaussian-cube parameters into only two one-parameter families: a star-number branch a=b+1 or a negative-Pell branch (a+b)^2-3(a-b)^2=-2, apart from exactly two 3-power exceptions. The only powers of 3 that occur as such gaps are 9 and 27.
