The binary mirror-vanishing theorem fails sharply over every alphabet q>=3: dimension-two [2d+1,2,d]_q codes can have A_{d+1}=...=A_{d+t}=0 yet A_{2d+1}>0 exactly when t <= d-ceil(d/(q-1)). The obstruction already occurs at equality in the binary theorem's dimension hypothesis and allows gaps of linear length in d.
