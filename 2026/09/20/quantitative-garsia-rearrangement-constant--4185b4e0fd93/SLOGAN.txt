The optimal finite Garsia rearrangement constant for unimodular orthonormal systems has the explicit lower growth \(\mathfrak G_N\gtrsim \log^{(6)}N\). The proof quantifies Lewko's permutation-pattern construction using Karagulyan's logarithmic Fourier maximal obstruction and Gowers's explicit Szemerédi bound; Bourgain's \(O(\log\log N)\) upper bound remains far away.
