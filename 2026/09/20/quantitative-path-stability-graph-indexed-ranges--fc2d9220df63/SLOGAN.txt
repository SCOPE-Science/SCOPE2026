Path maximality has a sharp stability scale in the standard graph-indexed random-walk model: every nonpath bipartite graph loses at least 1/(60 sqrt(n)), while a one-fork tree loses asymptotically 1/sqrt(2 pi n). The lazy model is also quantitatively unique, with a universal 1/(2000 n^(3/2)) gap and a stronger 2/(405 sqrt(n)) gap for nonpath trees.
