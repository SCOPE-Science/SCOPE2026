For the Rabinovich--Fabrikant flow, compact ergodic recurrence off z=0 obeys the exact law <z>=gamma/2+(gamma/(4 alpha))<(x+y)^2>, forcing recurrent states into the half-space with the sign of gamma. At gamma=0 all nonconstant periodic orbits are exactly the circles z=0, x^2+y^2=r^2<1 with period 2*pi/sqrt(1-r^2), while gamma<0 makes the physical half-space z>=0 collapse exponentially to the origin.
