Uniform random linear syndrome maps obey a source-independent constant-factor guesswork moment law: conditioning on m q-ary parity checks replaces the unconstrained rank by its q^{-m}-thinned rank up to constants depending only on q and the moment order. Consequently they attain the optimal all-rate exponent rho[H_{1/(1+rho)}/n-(m/n)log_2 q]_+, including a low-rate positive-part transition and moment cascade absent from the existing subcritical coset formula.
