For the positive-parameter classical Rössler system, the equilibrium discriminant is exactly the threshold for any compact recurrent dynamics: a nonempty compact invariant set exists iff c^2>=4ab. Every compact invariant probability measure obeys an exact variance-gap law Var(y)=(<z>-z_-)(z_+-<z>) and an arithmetic-harmonic height identity; at c^2=4ab the saddle-node equilibrium supports the unique compact invariant probability measure.
