The Rucklidge flow has an exact polynomial balance, dF/dt = -a x^2 + (1/2)(dz/dt)^2. It forces every non-equilibrium compact invariant probability measure to have a>0 and every bounded forward orbit with a<=0 to converge to an equilibrium, while giving an exact L2 rate ratio on every periodic orbit; in particular it rules out a bounded recurrent attractor at the published parameter example (a,b)=(-0.1,-1).
