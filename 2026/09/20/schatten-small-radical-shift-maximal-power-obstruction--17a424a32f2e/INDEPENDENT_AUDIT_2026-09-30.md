## Correctness — PASS

The separator/plateau construction is internally consistent. For n_m=P_{m-1}+1, every earlier-starting n_m block meets the separator while every later block uses weights at most h_m; the m-th plateau attains h_m^{n_m}. The same plateau and submultiplicativity give ||T^{m n_m}||=h_m^{m n_m}, so normalized powers witness the unit-ball obstruction for every m. Compactness, injectivity, quasinilpotence, radicality, and the all-Schatten consequence under an all-l_p envelope follow as stated.

## Originality — PASS (narrow)

Peters–Wogen (1999) already contains the qualitative weighted-shift counterexample and normalized-power mechanism, so those are not new. The surviving claim is the arbitrary prescribed null-envelope strengthening, in particular choosing an envelope in every l_p so that the same obstruction is generated by an operator in every Schatten class. The checked Peters–Wogen and Peters (2024) sources do not state that strengthening.

## Scientific value — PASS

The arbitrary-envelope control cleanly separates extremely rapid singular-value decay from uniform topological nilpotence and yields an explicit all-Schatten generator with maximal finite-power obstruction. This is a useful strengthening of the classical construction despite its reliance on the same mechanism.
