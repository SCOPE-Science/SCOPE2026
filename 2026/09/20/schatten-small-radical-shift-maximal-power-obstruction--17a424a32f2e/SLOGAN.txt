For every prescribed positive null envelope there is an injective compact quasinilpotent unilateral weighted shift below that envelope whose radical algebra satisfies sup_{||X||<=1} ||X^m||^(1/m)=1 for every m>=2. Choosing an envelope in every ell_p makes the generator belong to every Schatten class, so arbitrarily strong Schatten regularity does not force uniform topological nilpotence.
