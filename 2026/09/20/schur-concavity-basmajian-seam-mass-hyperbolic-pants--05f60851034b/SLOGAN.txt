For a hyperbolic pair of pants, the Basmajian mass contributed by its three inter-cuff seams has an exact closed formula and is strictly Schur-concave in the three cuff lengths at fixed total boundary length. Equal cuffs uniquely maximize that mass, its exact range is determined, and the three seams always contribute less than one half of the total boundary length, with the constant 1/2 asymptotically sharp.
