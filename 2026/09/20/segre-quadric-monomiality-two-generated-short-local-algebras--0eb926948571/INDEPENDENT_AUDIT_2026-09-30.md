## Correctness — PASS

With J^3=0 the algebra is determined by the surjective multiplication tensor V⊗V→W, and isomorphisms act by the same g∈GL(V) on both tensor factors; hence monomializability is exactly diagonal-GL equivalence of K=ker(mu) to a coordinate subspace. The d=1 and d=3 rank-one criteria are correct. For d=2, coordinate planes are precisely the two ruling types or the diagonal/crossed secants, and the matched unordered factor-line condition is exactly what allows one basis change to normalize a secant. The argument is characteristic-free. The 1+2+4+2+1 coordinate-orbit count is consistent.

## Originality — PASS

Authorized full text of Bardzell–Green (1999) and Mojiri (2006) was obtained after OA/preprint attempts and inspected completely. Bardzell–Green gives a general grading/Hochschild-cohomology characterization; Mojiri gives V_p/uniserial-module recognition criteria and loosely constricted classes. Neither states the two-loop radical-cube-zero tensor-kernel classification, the Segre ruling/matched-secant test, or the ten monomial isomorphism types. Targeted searches likewise found no equivalent low-dimensional theorem.

## Scientific value — PASS

This gives a concrete, basis-free recognition theorem in the smallest genuinely nontrivial short-local class, valid over arbitrary fields including characteristic 2, and turns a broad isomorphism-to-monomial problem into ranks and a line-versus-quadric incidence test.

## Limitations

- The theorem is restricted to split local algebras with J^3=0 and dim(J/J^2)=2.
- A historically equivalent formulation under different tensor-orbit terminology remains possible, but the two closest general recognition papers were checked in full and do not supply it.
