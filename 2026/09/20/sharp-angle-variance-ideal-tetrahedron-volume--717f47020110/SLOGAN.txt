For ideal hyperbolic tetrahedra, fixing the squared dihedral-angle deviation Q from the regular triple gives the exact maximal-volume profile V <= 2 Lambda(pi/6 + sqrt(Q/6)), attained only by the isosceles branch (pi/3+2r, pi/3-r, pi/3-r). Consequently v3-V >= (3 v3/(2 pi^2)) Q with a globally optimal constant forced by degeneration.
