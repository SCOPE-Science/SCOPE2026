Ivanova's \(s\ge4r\) inequality for a tangential quadrilateral has the globally sharp angular stability refinement
\(s-4r\ge[3(3\sqrt3-4)/\pi^2]\,r\sum_i(A_i-\pi/2)^2\), equivalently for the area deficit \(K-4r^2\).
The constant is approached by tangential quadrilaterals with angle vector tending to \((\pi,\pi/3,\pi/3,\pi/3)\), while equality for a proper quadrilateral occurs only at the square.
