The exact sharp L4/L2 constant for complex degree-two spherical harmonics on S^{n-1} is [3(n+2)(5n^2-13n+12)/((n-1)(n+4)(n+6))]^{1/4}. For n>=4 the only extremizers are constant-phase zonal quadratics gamma((v·omega)^2-1/n); for n=2,3 every real degree-two harmonic is extremal up to constant phase. On S^2 this gives the complete degree-two global answer to the classical L4 extremal problem.
