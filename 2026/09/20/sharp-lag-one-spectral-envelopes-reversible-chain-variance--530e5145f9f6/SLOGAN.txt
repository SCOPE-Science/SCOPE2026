For a positive reversible Markov-chain observable with lag-one correlation rho and spectral ceiling beta<1, the full autocorrelation sequence satisfies rho^k <= rho_k <= rho beta^(k-1), yielding exact sharp finite-sample and asymptotic variance intervals. Without a spectral ceiling, fixed positive lag-one correlation gives no finite upper bound on integrated autocorrelation time, even among finite-state geometrically ergodic chains.
