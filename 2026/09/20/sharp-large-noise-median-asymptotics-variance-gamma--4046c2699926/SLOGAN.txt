Variance-gamma medians approach their large-noise limits through five sharp regimes: a power law for r<1, a reciprocal logarithm at r=1, a sigma^{-(r-1)} correction for 1<r<3, a sigma^{-2} log sigma critical law at r=3, and a universal sigma^{-2} scale for r>3. The two phase transitions are inherited from the small-argument branches of the modified Bessel density.
