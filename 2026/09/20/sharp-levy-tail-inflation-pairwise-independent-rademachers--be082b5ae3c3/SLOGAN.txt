For pairwise-independent symmetric Rademacher increments, the threshold-2 analogue of Lévy's maximal-to-terminal tail ratio is at most n+1 for odd n, and this factor is attained for every n=2^m-1 with m>=4. A six-character prefix forces the maximal event almost surely while the full Walsh-character family leaves the terminal tail at its smallest possible probability 1/(n+1).
