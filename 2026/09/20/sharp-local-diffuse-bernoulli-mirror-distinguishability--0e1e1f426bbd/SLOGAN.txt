For Smirnov's mirror Bernoulli products, computational-basis total variation has the uniform weak-signal expansion tau/Delta = E|sum w_i epsilon_i| + O(sum a_i^2), giving the sharp local efficiency range [1/sqrt(2),1]. Under diffuse signal, the efficiency converges to the explicit strictly increasing curve (2 Phi(sqrt(v))-1)/sqrt(1-e^{-v}), from sqrt(2/pi) to 1.
