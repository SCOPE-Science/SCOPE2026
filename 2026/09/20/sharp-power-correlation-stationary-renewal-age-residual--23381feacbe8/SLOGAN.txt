For every r>0, the stationary renewal age/residual power correlation Corr(A^r,R^r) has an exact distribution-free attainable interval [ell_r,u_r), with deterministic interarrivals attaining the lower endpoint and two-point laws approaching the upper endpoint. The classical [-1,1/2) range is the r=1 case; for large r the whole interval collapses to 0, while exponential interarrivals are exactly the sign-change benchmark.
