For the shifted Vietoris coefficients q_k=((k+alpha)^lambda(k+beta)^mu)^(-1), all sine partial sums are positive on (0,pi) exactly when lambda+mu>=1. Below the threshold, even partial sums have endpoint slope coefficient -(1/2)n^(1-lambda-mu)+o(n^(1-lambda-mu)) and the universal boundary-layer profile n^(lambda+mu) S_n(pi-y/n)->-(1/2)sin y.
