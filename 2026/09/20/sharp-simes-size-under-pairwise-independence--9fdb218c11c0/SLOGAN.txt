For three exactly uniform null p-values, pairwise independence is not enough for Simes or global-null BH validity. The exact worst-case Simes size is piecewise: alpha+4 alpha^2/9 up to alpha=3/5, 4 alpha/3-alpha^2/9 up to alpha=3/4, and 2 alpha-alpha^2 thereafter, with explicit continuous extremizers.
