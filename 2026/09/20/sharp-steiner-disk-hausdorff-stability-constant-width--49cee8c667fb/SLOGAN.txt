For a planar convex body of constant width \(w\), the area deficit from the disk controls the Hausdorff distance to the canonical Steiner disk sharply:
\(\pi w^2/4-A(K)\ge 2\pi\,d_H(K,B_S)^2\).
The constant \(2\pi\) is asymptotically attained by smooth strictly convex constant-width bodies, while equality itself occurs only for the disk.
