For every finite simple graph G of order n>=2, 2 rho^o(G) <= n + rho_L^o(G), with the sharper 2 rho^o(G) <= n when rho_L^o(G)=1. Hence the exact maximum of rho^o-rho_L^o over n-vertex graphs (even connected graphs) is floor((n-2)/2); for trees the same value holds for every n>=5, with orders 2--4 as the only small exceptions.
