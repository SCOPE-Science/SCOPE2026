For bounded potential outcomes under complete randomization, the difference-in-means SATE estimator has sharp worst-case moderate-deviation exponent 2*pi*(1-pi)/R^2, attained already by a binary sharp-null population. Under fixed-stratum randomization the sharp exponent becomes 2/(R^2 Gamma), where Gamma=sum_j w_j/[p_j(1-p_j)], and matching Serfling confidence bounds improve recent Hoeffding constants.
