## correctness

PASS

For a nonpath tree, dim(T)=ell-e and Z(T)=P(T). A maximum matching in the forest induced by major vertices, together with d(v)-2 incident-edge deletions adjusted at matched endpoints, produces a path cover of size at most ell-1-nu(H), proving Z-dim<=e-1-nu(H). A minimum vertex cover of H yields an independent set S of at least e-nu exterior majors; their degree at least three and edge-disjoint incidence gives n-1>=3|S|, hence the fixed-order floor((n-4)/3) bound. The once-subdivided cubic caterpillar has dim=2 and path-cover number m+1; pendant subdivision handles the other congruence classes. Equality at n=3m+1 forces every edge incident to the independent exterior-major set, eliminating the cover and interior majors and forcing the unique subdivided-path core. Finite enumeration is corroboration only.
