The k=7 case of Chu's 2021 divisor-sum conjecture is proved: for n=2^(alpha-1)p^(beta-1) with p<3*2^(alpha-1)-1, n divides sigma_7(n) exactly for the even perfect numbers other than 8128. The proof combines Chu's general valuation lemmas, a new beta=4 elimination, and an exact finite reduction for the remaining cases.
