In every annihilator C*-algebra $A=(\bigoplus_\alpha K(H_\alpha))_{c_0}$, an element is a single commutator exactly when its normalized trace vanishes on every finite-dimensional summand. The single-commutator set is therefore a closed linear subspace, $A/\mathcal C_1(A)\cong c_0(F)$ isometrically, with exact distance given by the supremum of the finite-block normalized traces.
