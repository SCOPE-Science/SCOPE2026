On scalar random-curvature quadratics, the 2026 out-of-sample OSGM ratio feedback targets 1/E[A] and hypergradient feedback targets E[1/A], while one-step mean-square SGD targets E[A]/E[A^2]. These distinct targets yield sharp raw-proposal support thresholds kappa < 3+2sqrt(2) for ratio and kappa < 3.546455... for hypergradient, and quantify when clipping or the hypergradient null step becomes essential.
