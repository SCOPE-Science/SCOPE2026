For SPD spectra in [mu,L], the optimal two Richardson steps whose individual stages are Euclidean nonexpansions undergo an exact transition at kappa=1+sqrt(2). Beyond it the unique safe pair is {2/L, 2/(L+2mu)} with factor (kappa-2)/(kappa+2), and requiring every first-order factor to be nonexpansive forces Theta(kappa log(1/epsilon)) rather than Chebyshev's Theta(sqrt(kappa) log(1/epsilon)) spectral complexity.
