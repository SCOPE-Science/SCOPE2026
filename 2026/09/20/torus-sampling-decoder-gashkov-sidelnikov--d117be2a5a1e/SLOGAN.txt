For every weight-three syndrome of an original ternary Gashkov-Sidel'nikov code, a uniformly sampled norm-one torus summand completes to a minimum three-term decomposition with probability 1/2 + O(q^{-1/2}). The exact number of successful torus probes is half the F_q-point count of an explicit elliptic curve, giving 2 + O(q^{-1/2}) expected probes and q/6 + O(sqrt(q)) minimum-weight coset leaders.
