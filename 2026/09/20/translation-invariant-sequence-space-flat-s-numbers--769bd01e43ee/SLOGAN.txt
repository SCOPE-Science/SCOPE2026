On l_p(G) (1<=p<infinity) and c_0(G) for every infinite discrete group G, every bounded operator commuting with all left translations satisfies a_n=b_n=c_n=d_n=||T|| for every n. Its distances to the compact, finitely strictly singular and strictly singular ideals also equal ||T||, witnessed almost isometrically on a 1-complemented copy of l_p or c_0.
