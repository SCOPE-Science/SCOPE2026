Three-row perfect hash families that are invariant under simultaneous translation by a finite abelian group are exactly tri-colored sum-free sets developed by the group: their maximum size is |G| times the tri-colored sum-free number. Over F_p^n this gives the exact structured growth exponent 1+log_p(theta_p), exposing a polynomial loss from translation symmetry; a 20-cap in F_81 yields PHF(3;1620,81,3).
