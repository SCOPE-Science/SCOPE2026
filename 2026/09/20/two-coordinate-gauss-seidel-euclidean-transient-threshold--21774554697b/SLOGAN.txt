For every 2x2 SPD system, updating the coordinate with the larger diagonal first minimizes one-sweep Euclidean Gauss-Seidel error amplification. At condition number kappa the exact best-order minimax factor is delta*sqrt(1+delta^2), delta=(kappa-1)/(kappa+1), giving the sharp universal nonexpansion threshold kappa≈8.352410032; above it even the better of the two orders can be transiently expansive.
