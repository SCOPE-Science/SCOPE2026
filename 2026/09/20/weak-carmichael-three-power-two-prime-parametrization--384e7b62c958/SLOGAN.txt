Weak Carmichael numbers of the form \(3^a p q\) admit an exact finite divisor parametrization, with explicit \(O(3^{2a})\) and \(O(3^{3a})\) bounds for \(p\) and \(q\). In particular, the complete \(9pq\) slice consists of exactly \(13833=3^2\cdot29\cdot53\) and \(321201=3^2\cdot89\cdot401\), resolving an internally inconsistent 2013 computation statement.
