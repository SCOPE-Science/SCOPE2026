Weighted Jacobi has an exact Euclidean one-step safety ceiling: omega_E(A)=lambda_min(A^{-1}D+DA^{-1}). For 2x2 SPD systems of condition number kappa, the sharp robust ceiling is 1+1/kappa for kappa<=3 and (14kappa-kappa^2-1)/(8kappa) thereafter; standard Jacobi is universally nonexpansive only through 3+2sqrt(2), and beyond 7+4sqrt(3) some systems admit no positive Euclidean-safe damping at all.
