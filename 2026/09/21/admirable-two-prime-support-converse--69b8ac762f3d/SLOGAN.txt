Admirable numbers of the form 2^a p^b admit a complete classification: the odd-prime exponent is only 1 or 3, and b=3 occurs exactly for the Mersenne-cube family 2^a(2^(a+1)-1)^3. The b=1 case consists of a binary-deviation prime family, the isolated number 40, and an even-perfect-divisor Mersenne family.
