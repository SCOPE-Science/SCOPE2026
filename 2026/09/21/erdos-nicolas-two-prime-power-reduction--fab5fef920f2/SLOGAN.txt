For n=2^a p^b with p odd prime, the initial-divisor-sum property is completely classified for b=1 and excluded for b=2; 24 is the unique Erdős–Nicolas number with prime odd part. For b>=3, every possible solution satisfies p | 2^(a+1)-1, p<=2^a, b odd, and b<=a+v_p(2^(a+1)-1)-1, making the remaining search finite for each a.
