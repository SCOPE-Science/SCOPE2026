For square-integrable one-sample U-statistics, distinct delete-one jackknife pseudo-values have an exact negative-semidefinite covariance contributed only by Hoeffding orders two and above; exact uncorrelatedness occurs if and only if the kernel is additive. In the scalar case their correlation lies sharply in [-(m-1)/(m(n-2)+1), 0], and the negative cross-pseudo-value covariance is exactly the finite-sample upward bias of the jackknife covariance estimator.
