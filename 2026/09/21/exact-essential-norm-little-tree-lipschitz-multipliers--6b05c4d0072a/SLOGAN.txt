For every bounded multiplier on a little weighted Lipschitz space of a locally finite rooted tree, the essential norm is exactly the radial limsup of an explicit coordinate-row norm.  For the Allen--Colonna--Easley weighted space this yields the sharp formula
||M_psi||_e = limsup_{|v|->infinity} (|psi(v)| + |v| H_{|v|-1} |Delta psi(v)|),
closing the earlier max{A,B} versus A+B gap on the little space.
