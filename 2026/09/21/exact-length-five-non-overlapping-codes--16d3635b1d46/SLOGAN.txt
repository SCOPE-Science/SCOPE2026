For every q >= 4, the largest q-ary non-overlapping code of length five has exactly a(q-a)^4 words, where a=floor((q+2)/5), and every maximum code is AB^4 or B^4A for an alphabet partition with |A|=a. Thus Blackburn's k=n-1 construction is exactly optimal at length five from q=4 onward; S(2,5)=2 and S(3,5)=17 are the two small exceptions.
