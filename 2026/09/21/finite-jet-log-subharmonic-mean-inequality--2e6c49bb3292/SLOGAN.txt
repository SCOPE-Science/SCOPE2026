A finite nonconstant Taylor jet of log u forces Mochizuki's ball-versus-sphere inequality locally: if the first nonzero jet has degree m, then A(u^p)<M(u)^p for every p<1+2m/n. Hence the full intermediate range holds for positive real-analytic log-subharmonic functions, and any smooth positive counterexample must be flat to infinite order in log u at the bad point.
