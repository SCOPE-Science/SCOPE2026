For every finite abelian group, the exact number of fixed-point-free automorphisms is the automorphism-group order times a product of finite-field linear-derangement proportions, one factor for each cyclic-exponent multiplicity. In particular, for pairwise distinct exponents a_1<...<a_n, theta(A,1)=p^{sum_i(2n-2i+1)a_i-n}(p-2)^n, settling the d=1 slice of a 2018 theta-value problem in all ranks.
