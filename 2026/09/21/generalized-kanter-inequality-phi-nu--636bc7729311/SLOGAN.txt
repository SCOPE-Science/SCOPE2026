For \(\Phi_\nu(x)=e^{-x}x^{-\nu}(I_\nu(x)+I_{\nu+1}(x))\), the sharp Kanter-type extension
\[
\Phi_\nu(2r)\ge \frac{\Gamma(r+1/2)}{\sqrt{\pi}\,2^\nu\Gamma(r+\nu+1)}
\]
holds for every \(\nu\ge-1/2\) and \(r\ge0\), with equality exactly when \(r=0\) or \(\nu=-1/2\). The proof reduces the order parameter to a decreasing weight acting on a one-sign-change kernel and proves the limiting weighted integral positive through a digamma–exponential-integral identity.
