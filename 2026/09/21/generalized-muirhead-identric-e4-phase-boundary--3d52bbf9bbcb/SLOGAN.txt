The unresolved Wang–Chu–Qiu \(E_4\) region has a sharp continuous phase boundary \(s=C(d)\), where \(C(d)=\inf_{z>0}\log\cosh(dz)/(z\coth z-1)\). For \(d\ge\log2\) every \(E_4\) point satisfies \(M>I\) globally, while for \(\sqrt{2/5}<d<\log2\) the region splits sharply into global domination, boundary equality, and genuinely mixed-sign behavior.
