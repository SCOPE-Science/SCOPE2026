For every graph with no even cycle, the degree-q^i character count of its graphical group over F_q (q odd) is q^(n-2i) times the edge-support polynomial summing (q-1)^|S| over supports of matching number i. For even cycles a single explicitly counted Pfaffian-cancellation locus shifts (q-1)^(2r-1) points from top rank to the next rank, giving polynomial formulas for every cycle.
