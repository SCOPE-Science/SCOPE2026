For a split basic quasi-hereditary Nakayama algebra with n >= 2 simple modules, F(A)=dim Hom_A(D(A),A) is at most n^2+1, and the cyclic Kupisch series (2,n+1,n,n-1,...,3) attains equality. In the cyclic case, quasi-heredity forces all Hom(I_s,P_j) to have dimension at most 1 except for at most one pair, which can have dimension 2.
