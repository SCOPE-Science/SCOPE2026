Over every field of characteristic 2, the local Frobenius algebra k[t]/(t^{2d}) gives a counterexample to the converse of Murray's 2005 norm criterion: with the standard Frobenius form B and u=1+t, the norm N_sigma(u)=u is central, but B is alternating while B_u is not. The case d=1 is a dimension-two counterexample, the smallest possible.
