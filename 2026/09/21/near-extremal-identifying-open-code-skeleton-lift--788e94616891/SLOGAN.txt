Twice subdividing every edge of an arbitrary bounded-degree skeleton and filling each center to degree Delta with pendant P2 arms gives an exact identifying-open-code formula: gamma_IOC = n - |V(H)|. Cycle skeletons have density (2Delta-2)/(2Delta-1), and path skeletons give trees approaching the same value, strictly exceeding the construction density proposed as a 2026 open direction for every Delta >= 4.
