On the normal strongly-monotone/Lipschitz half-disk, the exact real degree-two minimax polynomial is p(z)=1-(1+mu/L)z+z^2 with factor 1-mu/L. It is realized by an unequal-step extragradient cycle, while the best common-step extragradient has the strictly larger exact factor (L^2+L mu+mu^2)/(L+mu)^2.
