# A 12-dimensional three-step nilpotent Lie algebra without a Novikov structure

## Statement

Let `k` be a field of characteristic zero. There exists a 12-dimensional, four-generated, three-step nilpotent Lie algebra over `k` that admits no Novikov structure.

More explicitly, let `g` have basis `x1,...,x12` and the following nonzero brackets (up to skew-symmetry):

\[
[x_1,x_2]=x_5,\qquad [x_3,x_4]=-x_5,\qquad [x_1,x_4]=x_6,
\]
\[
[x_3,x_5]=-x_{11},\qquad [x_1,x_6]=x_{10},\qquad [x_3,x_8]=x_9,
\]
\[
[x_1,x_7]=x_{11},\qquad [x_4,x_5]=-x_{12},\qquad [x_1,x_8]=x_{12},
\]
\[
[x_4,x_6]=x_9,\qquad [x_2,x_3]=x_7,\qquad
[x_4,x_7]=-x_9-2x_{10}+2x_{11},
\]
\[
[x_2,x_4]=x_8,\qquad
[x_2,x_5]=-2x_9-2x_{10}+2x_{11},\qquad
[x_2,x_7]=-2x_9-2x_{10}+2x_{11}.
\]

Then `g` has no bilinear product `x·y` satisfying

\[
x\cdot y-y\cdot x=[x,y],
\]

the left-symmetric identity, and the Novikov identity

\[
(x\cdot y)\cdot z=(x\cdot z)\cdot y.
\]

## Relation to the 2008 example

Burde, Dekimpe and Vercammen gave a 13-dimensional four-generated three-step nilpotent Lie algebra with no Novikov structure. Denote their basis by the same symbols `x1,...,x13`. In that algebra `x9,...,x13` are central. The algebra above is the central quotient

\[
\mathfrak g_{13}/\langle x_{13}+2x_9+2x_{10}-2x_{11}\rangle.
\]

Indeed, in the quotient

\[
x_{13}=-2x_9-2x_{10}+2x_{11},
\]

which gives exactly the displayed 12-dimensional bracket table. Thus the Jacobi identity is inherited from the 13-dimensional algebra. Moreover

\[
\gamma_2(\mathfrak g)=\langle x_5,\ldots,x_{12}\rangle,
\qquad
\gamma_3(\mathfrak g)=\langle x_9,x_{10},x_{11},x_{12}\rangle,
\qquad
\gamma_4(\mathfrak g)=0,
\]

so the quotient is still three-step nilpotent and has four-dimensional abelianization.

## Proof of nonexistence

Write `Li=L(x_i)` and `Ri=R(x_i)` for left and right multiplication. Columns encode images of basis vectors. Every Novikov structure on a three-step nilpotent Lie algebra must satisfy the following linear consequences used in the 2008 obstruction:

1. all terms of the lower central series are two-sided ideals, together with
   \(\gamma_{i+1}\cdot\gamma_{j+1}\subseteq\gamma_{i+j+1}\);
2. \(L_i x_j-L_j x_i=[x_i,x_j]\);
3. the cyclic identity
   \[
   x_i\cdot[x_j,x_k]+x_j\cdot[x_k,x_i]+x_k\cdot[x_i,x_j]=0;
   \]
4. the operator identity
   \[
   L([x_i,x_j])+\operatorname{ad}([x_i,x_j])
   -[\operatorname{ad}(x_i),L_j]-[L_i,\operatorname{ad}(x_j)]=0.
   \]

Exact row reduction over the rationals gives ranks

\[
1088,\quad 1376,\quad 1504,\quad 1678
\]

after these four successive groups of constraints, leaving 50 free entries among the \(12^3=1728\) entries of the left multiplications. The complete elimination is reproduced by `artifacts/verify.py` using exact rational arithmetic.

A compact certificate can be read from the reduced system. Put

\[
a=L_8(12,1),\quad b=L_8(12,2),\quad c=L_8(12,3),\quad d=L_8(12,4),
\]

where `Li(r,s)` denotes the `(r,s)` matrix entry. For the `(11,2)` entry of `[R_2,R_3]`, all summands with intermediate index `t<=4` vanish because the two right-hand factors vanish, and all summands with `t>=9` vanish because the two left-hand factors vanish. The remaining reduced entries imply

\[
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
t & R_2(11,t) & R_3(t,2) & R_3(11,t) & R_2(t,2)\\ \hline
5 & -1-2a+d & 2c & -a & 2b\\
6 & * & 0 & \tfrac14+a & 1+4a\\
7 & -1-2a+b+2c & -2a & c & -1-4a+2d\\
8 & * & 0 & \tfrac12+2a & -2c
\end{array}
\]

(the starred entries multiply zero and are irrelevant). Hence

\[
\begin{aligned}
[R_2,R_3]_{11,2}
={}&2c(-1-2a+d)+2ab-(\tfrac14+a)(1+4a)\\
&+(-1-2a+b+2c)(-2a)-c(-1-4a+2d)\\
&+(\tfrac12+2a)(2c)\\
={}&-\tfrac14.
\end{aligned}
\]

But right multiplications commute in every Novikov algebra. Thus `[R_2,R_3]=0`, contradicting the displayed entry. Therefore `g` admits no Novikov structure.

## Significance and limitations

The 2008 paper supplied a 13-dimensional three-step nilpotent counterexample and proved that every three-step nilpotent Lie algebra generated by at most three elements admits a Novikov structure. The construction above lowers the dimension of the explicit four-generated counterexample from 13 to 12 by a one-dimensional central quotient.

No claim of minimality is made: this result does not rule out counterexamples of dimension at most 11. It also concerns characteristic zero; no positive-characteristic extension is asserted.

Targeted searches for 12-dimensional examples, smaller three-step nilpotent counterexamples, central quotients of the 2008 example, and synonymous formulations located the 2008 construction and later sources continuing to cite that 13-dimensional example, but no prior 12-dimensional construction. Originality is therefore asserted only to the best of our knowledge.

## Reproducibility

Run

```text
python artifacts/verify.py
```

with Python 3. The script uses only the standard library and exact `fractions.Fraction` arithmetic. It reconstructs the linear system and verifies

```text
linear ranks: 1088, 1376, 1504, 1678; free variables: 50
[R_2,R_3]_(11,2) = -1/4
```
