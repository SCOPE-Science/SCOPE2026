For every odd n and even r, the divisor power mean sigma_r(n)/tau(n) has a rigid 2-adic residue modulo 8: it is always 1 when r is divisible by 4, while for r congruent to 2 mod 4 it is 1 for n congruent to ±1 mod 8 and 5 for n congruent to ±3 mod 8. Consequently every odd RMS number, and more generally every odd sigma_r-number with r congruent to 2 mod 4, is congruent to ±1 mod 8.
