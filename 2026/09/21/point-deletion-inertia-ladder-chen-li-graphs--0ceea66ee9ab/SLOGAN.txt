Deleting t point vertices from the Chen--Li graph W_k lowers its positive inertia by exactly t while preserving k-1 negative eigenvalues: In(W_k-R)=(binom(k,2)+1-t,0,k-1). The one-point deletions form an infinite family of connected reduced equality graphs and answer Akbari--Elphick--Kumar--Pragada--Tang Problem 3.3.
