At prime-support size two, Machacek's Fermat/Mersenne constructions are exhaustive: prime-power pseudoperfect numbers are exactly 2^a(2^a+1)^b with 2^a+1 prime, while prime-power Giuga numbers are exactly 2^a(2^a-1) with 2^a-1 prime. The odd prime may repeat arbitrarily only on the pseudoperfect/Fermat side.
