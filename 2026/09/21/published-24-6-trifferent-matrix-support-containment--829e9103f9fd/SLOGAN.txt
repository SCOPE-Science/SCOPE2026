The 6x24 matrix printed as a ternary trifferent inner code in Bishnoi–D'haeseleer–Gijswijt–Potukuchi (2024) is not trifferent: the row combinations r2+r3 and r1+r4 have strictly nested supports. The matrix therefore cannot certify the paper's concatenation argument as written, although this does not rule out a corrected [24,6]_3 witness.
