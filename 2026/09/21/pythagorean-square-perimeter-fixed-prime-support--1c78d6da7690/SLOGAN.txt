For any prescribed finite set S of odd primes, primitive Pythagorean triangles with square perimeter and exact prime support {2}∪S are classified by a signed logarithmic fractional-part condition. Their number up to perimeter X is asymptotic to binom(2r-1,r)(log X)^r / (2·4^r·r!·∏_{p∈S} log p), so every such support occurs infinitely often.
