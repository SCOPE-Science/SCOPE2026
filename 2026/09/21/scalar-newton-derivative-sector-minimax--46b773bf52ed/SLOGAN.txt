For scalar C1 root finding with m <= f' <= L, the exact worst one-step root-error factor of constant-relaxation Newton is max(|1-gamma/kappa|, |1-gamma*kappa|), so the optimal constant damping is 2*kappa/(kappa^2+1) with factor (kappa^2-1)/(kappa^2+1). Undamped Newton is globally convergent through the sharp boundary kappa=2 but admits an explicit two-cycle for every kappa>2; moreover the minimax derivative-dependent multiplicative relaxation cancels the Newton denominator and reduces to the optimally tuned fixed-slope iteration.
