For Steiner-centered planar bodies of constant width, the area deficit controls every two-direction support difference by the sharp kernel M(d)=(1-cos d)/2+(pi/2-d)sin d. Its antipodal endpoint gives the sharp canonical Hausdorff inequality D>=2*pi*d_H^2, while odd q-fold symmetry improves the coefficient to 2q*cot(pi/(2q)).
