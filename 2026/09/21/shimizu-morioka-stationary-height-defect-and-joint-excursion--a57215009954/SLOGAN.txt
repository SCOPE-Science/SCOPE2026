For every compactly supported invariant probability measure of the positive-parameter Shimizu–Morioka flow, the heightwise law E[x^2|z]=bz and the exact defect E[(z-1/2)^2]=1/4+E[y^2]/b hold. Equality consists exactly of mixtures of the three equilibria; every other stationary state assigns positive mass to the simultaneous excursion z>1 and |x|>sqrt(b).
