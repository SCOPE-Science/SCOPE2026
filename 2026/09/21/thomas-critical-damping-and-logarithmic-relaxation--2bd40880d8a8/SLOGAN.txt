At the Thomas cyclic sine ring's pitchfork threshold b=1, the origin remains globally attracting: only a codimension-one strong-stable set decays exponentially, while every other orbit synchronizes and obeys 1/x_i(t)^2=t/3-(1/20)log t+C+o(1). Thus the exact global stability threshold is the closed condition b>=1, not merely b>1.
