For positive-definite 2×2 A and invertible Hermitian 2×2 B, the sign of det(A^k+|AB|^p)−det(A^k+|BA|^p) is exactly the sign of kp, with strictness precisely when A and B do not commute. This gives a complete two-dimensional phase law and resolves the corresponding positive- and negative-exponent determinant conjectures in order two.
