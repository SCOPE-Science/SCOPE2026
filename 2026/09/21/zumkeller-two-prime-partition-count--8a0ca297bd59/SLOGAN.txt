For every odd prime p, the number of unordered equal-sum divisor bipartitions of 2^a p is exactly floor((2^(a+1)-1+p)/(2p)); equivalently, the partitions are indexed by positive odd r with pr <= 2^(a+1)-1. The fibers have explicit prime intervals and limiting mass 2/(4k^2-1), while the aggregate count equals the sum of omega(m) over odd m up to 2^(a+1)-1.
