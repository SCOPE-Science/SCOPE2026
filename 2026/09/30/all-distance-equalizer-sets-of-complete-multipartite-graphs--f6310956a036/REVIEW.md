# Same-model review

## Correctness
The proof was reconstructed from the exact distance table of a complete multipartite graph. For an outside pair in one part, every selected vertex is equidistant from the pair. For an outside pair in distinct parts, a selected vertex is equidistant from the pair exactly when it lies in a third part. This reduces the definition to a complete combinatorial dichotomy: either one part is fully selected, so it cannot contribute an outside endpoint and itself supplies a third-part witness, or, when no part is full, every part has an outside vertex and at least three supported parts are necessary and sufficient. The invalid-set enumerator then partitions disjointly by support size zero, one, or two, with each supported part restricted to a nonempty proper subset. The minimum-set cases follow directly. Edge cases checked explicitly include complete graphs, stars, complete bipartite graphs, \(K_{2,2,2}\), parts of size three, and unequal multipartite graphs. The standalone exhaustive certificate agrees with the classification, coefficient vectors, and minimum-set counts for every complete multipartite isomorphism type through order eight.

## Originality
The 2021 source introducing the parameter was checked directly. It proves the complete-bipartite and complete-multipartite minimum equidistant-dimension formulas, but the accepted claim does not treat those numerical formulas as new. The 2024 complexity/lexicographic-product follow-up and current indexed papers on other graph families and products were also checked for stronger or equivalent coverage. Exact-phrase, synonym, parameter, and structural searches did not locate an all-set characterization, selection-profile formula, or generating polynomial for arbitrary complete multipartite graphs. Semantic searches in the published result collection likewise returned no equivalent claim; the closest items concern other complete-multipartite parameters such as outer multiset dimension or phylogenetic rank. The accumulated local findings were searched for equidistant, distance-equalizer, complete-multipartite enumerators, and equivalent support/full-part formulations, with no overlap. Originality is therefore assessed PASS only for the structural and enumerative refinement, on a best-of-knowledge basis.

## Value
The result upgrades a known minimum-cardinality formula to a complete description of every feasible set. That classification immediately yields counts by part-selection profile, a multivariate polynomial retaining part information, the ordinary cardinality polynomial, the total number of distance-equalizer sets, and exact counts of minimum sets. It is reusable for random-subset questions, weighted variants, and comparisons among multipartite shapes, rather than being only another isolated exact value. Because the proof reduces the metric condition to a support/full-part condition, the formulas are transparent and efficiently computable for arbitrarily many parts.

## Closest literature
The closest primary source is González--Hernando--Mora, *The Equidistant Dimension of Graphs*, arXiv:2107.10805v1 / DOI 10.1007/s40840-022-01295-z, which introduces the parameter and gives the complete-multipartite minimum dimension. Gispert-Fernández--Rodríguez-Velázquez, DOI 10.3934/math.2024744, develops complexity and lexicographic-product theory. Neither checked source gives the accepted all-set classification or enumerators. Semantic database searches also returned nearby complete-multipartite results for different parameters, not stronger or equivalent coverage.

## Scientific limitations
The novelty check cannot rule out an unindexed or differently named equivalent result. The exhaustive computation is finite and corroborative only. The theorem is confined to complete multipartite graphs; no claim is made for general diameter-two graphs or perturbed multipartite classes.
