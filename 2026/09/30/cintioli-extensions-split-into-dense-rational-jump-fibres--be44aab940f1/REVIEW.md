# Review

## Correctness
PASS. For every nonempty relative cylinder, Cintioli's extension theorem gives the Join Property. Proposition A.2 therefore applies inside that cylinder for every target degree above \(\mathbf0'\). Because the entire extension is contained in \(\mathrm{GL}_1\), the generalized-low fibre used in Proposition A.2 equals the ordinary jump fibre. This yields infinitely many distinct fibre degrees in every relative cylinder. Density and absence of isolated points follow directly. Countability of the fibre as a set of reals follows from \(B\leq_{\mathrm T}B'\leq_{\mathrm T}A\). Sierpiński's theorem then gives the homeomorphism with \(\mathbb Q\). The disjoint-cover and continuum-index claims were checked separately.

## Originality
PASS. The ownership source states the local Join Property and, in an independent appendix, countable infinitude of jump-inversion degree fibres. It does not state that each fibre is dense, has no isolated points, is homeomorphic to \(\mathbb Q\), or that the class is partitioned into continuum many such dense fibres. Exact literature searches for these formulations and semantic searches for local jump-fibre density found no equivalent or stronger result. The closest indexed records concerned unrelated degree spectra or generalized equivalence relations.

## Value
PASS. The conclusion gives an exact topological type for every jump level inside a single special \(\Pi^0_1\) extension, converting two degree-theoretic source statements into a strong global decomposition. It shows that every jump target above \(\mathbf0'\) is not merely represented but is represented densely and without isolated witnesses throughout the class. The continuum-sized partition by canonical jump fibres is a reusable structural constraint directly relevant to the source's stated question about further fibre structure.

## Closest literature
Cintioli's 2026 paper is the ownership source and contains both ingredients. Jananthan--Simpson provide the earlier class-restricted jump and pseudojump background. Sierpiński's 1920 theorem supplies the topological characterization. No source located in the targeted searches states the dense-rational-fibre synthesis.

## Scientific limitations
The result is a derived structural consequence, not a new priority construction. It does not determine the order type of Turing degrees within a fibre and does not provide effective homeomorphisms.
