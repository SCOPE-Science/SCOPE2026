# Same-model review

## Correctness
The proof was reconstructed from the distance-two geometry of complete multipartite graphs. The ordinary general-position lemma, mutual-maximal-distance relation, simplicial-vertex characterization, and convex-subset criterion were each derived independently and then recombined. The case split for the dual polynomial was checked for overlap and edge cases, including complete graphs, stars, \(K_{2,1}\), \(K_{2,2}\), \(K_{3,2}\), a single large part with singleton parts, and several 2-parts with singleton parts. The standalone exhaustive certificate agrees with every classification and every coefficient formula through order eight.

## Originality
The closest source is Tian--Klavžar's paper introducing the dual, total, and outer variants and proving the general characterizations used here. Exact and semantic searches of that source, later survey material, and current indexed literature did not locate a complete-multipartite dual-set classification or the displayed complete-multipartite dual enumerator. The accumulated local result collection was also checked for the claim, parameter names, synonyms, and equivalent convex-complement formulations, with no overlap. Possible implicit prior coverage of the outer-number consequence through mutually-maximally-distant or strong-resolving-graph results is explicitly excluded from the novelty claim. Ordinary general-position facts and the source characterizations are likewise credited as prior work.

## Value
The dual classification is structurally informative rather than a single exact number: it determines every feasible set, its counting polynomial, and the exact zero/nonzero phase boundary. The family includes complete graphs, stars, complete bipartite graphs, and arbitrary multipartite mixtures, making the result useful as a test family for general theory and for enumerative refinements of graph-position parameters.

## Closest literature
The closest paper is Jing Tian and Sandi Klavžar, *Variety of general position problems in graphs*, arXiv:2402.17338v1 (27 February 2024), later published with DOI 10.1007/s40840-024-01788-z. It supplies the variant definitions and general structural characterizations, but the checked text does not treat complete multipartite graphs. Later survey coverage checked for these variants discusses other graph products and families but does not provide the formulas proved here.

## Scientific limitations
Priority is best-of-knowledge, not an absolute guarantee. The exhaustive computation is finite corroboration, not a proof. No independent audit, formal proof-assistant verification, or expert attestation has been performed. The outer and total formulas are companion consequences and are not relied upon as the core originality claim.
