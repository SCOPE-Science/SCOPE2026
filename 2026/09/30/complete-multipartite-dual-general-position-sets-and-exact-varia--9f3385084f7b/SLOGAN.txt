For \(G=K_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}\), dual general-position sets have a sharp structural dichotomy: they either live inside the unique possible non-singleton part, or, when every part has size at most two, choose exactly one vertex from every 2-part. This yields closed counting polynomials for the dual, total, and outer variants and shows \(\operatorname{gp}_d(G)=0\) whenever at least two parts are non-singleton and one has size at least three.
