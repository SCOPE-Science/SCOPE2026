For \(G=K_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}\), the mixed metric dimension is \(N\) with at least two singleton parts, \(N-2\) for bipartite graphs with both parts at least three, and \(N-1\) otherwise. This supplies a complete correction of a published 2022 formula; the same paper's edge-dimension formula also conflicts with an earlier exact theorem for \(r\ge3\).
