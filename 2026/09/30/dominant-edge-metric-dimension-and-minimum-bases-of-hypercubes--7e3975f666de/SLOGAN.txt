For every binary hypercube \(Q_n\) with \(n\ge3\), a parity class is simultaneously a minimum vertex cover and an edge metric generator, so \(\operatorname{Ddim}_e(Q_n)=2^{n-1}\). Moreover, those two parity classes are the only minimum bases; the sole nontrivial small exception is \(Q_2\), where the value is \(3\).
