For hypercubes, the first two nontrivial distance-limited mutual-visibility numbers are exact: \(\mu_2(Q_n)=n+1\) for \(n\ge2\), while \(\mu_3(Q_n)=2n-1\) for \(n\ge5\) with the sharp small exceptions \(3,5,8\) at \(n=2,3,4\). The proof turns the visibility problem into a bounded-Hamming-diameter problem and then excludes the unique diameter-three extremal family because it blocks a two-step geodesic.
