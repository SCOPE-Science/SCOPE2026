# Same-model scientific review
## Correctness assessment
**PASS.** Homogeneity gives the \(\operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb P)\) count. Duality has only the antichain as a fixed labeled poset. The published unique-sole-maximal representative theorem gives the \(p_{k-1}\) rotation-class bijection. Triple-class duality leaves exactly the antichain rotation class fixed, giving the \(\operatorname{Max}\) formula. Equality partitions give the Stirling transform. Edge cases were checked explicitly and finite enumeration agrees through four labels.

## Originality assessment
**PASS, best of knowledge.** The closest papers classify the five groups and finite rotations but do not state these exact profiles, the \(p_k/p_{k-1}\) reduction, or arity-three separation. Targeted semantic searches before and after derivation found no equivalent or stronger result.

## Value assessment
**PASS.** The result gives an exact quantitative profile for the whole five-element reduct lattice, reduces two nontrivial groups to classical labeled-poset enumeration, and shows ordered triples suffice to distinguish all five groups.
