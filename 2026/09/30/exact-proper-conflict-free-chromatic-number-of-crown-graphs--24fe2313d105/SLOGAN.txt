For the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M\), proper conflict-free coloring has the exact transition \(2,3,4\): \(\chi_{\mathrm{pcf}}(\operatorname{Cr}_2)=2\), \(\chi_{\mathrm{pcf}}(\operatorname{Cr}_3)=3\), and \(\chi_{\mathrm{pcf}}(\operatorname{Cr}_n)=4\) for every \(n\ge4\). The key obstruction is that any color used on both bipartition classes is forced onto a single deleted matched pair.
