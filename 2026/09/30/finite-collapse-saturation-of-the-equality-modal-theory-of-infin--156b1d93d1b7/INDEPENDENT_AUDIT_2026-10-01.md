# Independent scientific review

## Final claim

In every finite-collapse-rich wide subcategory of infinite sets, pure-equality first-order modal truth agrees pointwise with the all-function semantics; in particular finite-to-one maps have the same sentential and formulaic modal validities as all functions.

## Correctness

**PASS.** Wołoszyn’s partition normal form for infinite-set categories makes modal truth depend only on the equality partition of the finitely many named tuple entries. For a possible all-function step, the image tuple induces a coarsening of the current partition; finite-collapse richness supplies an endomap of the same infinite world realizing exactly that coarsening. Induction on formula complexity, including the extra coordinate introduced by a quantifier, therefore transfers every all-function modal step to the restricted category. The converse direction is immediate because the restricted arrows are functions. The finite-to-one category contains every finite collapse used in the proof.

## Originality

**PASS.** The complete arXiv source states the all-function and surjection classifications and proves the partition normal form, but a full-text search contains no finite-to-one class and the paper’s listed infinite-set categories do not include the finite-to-one subcategory. Resultary returned the submitted record as the only close hit; no equivalent finite-collapse-rich transfer principle or finite-to-one equality-modal classification was located.

## Value

**PASS.** The result identifies a natural robustness boundary of a newly classified modal semantics: arbitrary maps can be replaced by the much smaller finite-to-one class because only finitely many named equalities are observable. This is a structural transfer lemma with immediate reuse for other morphism restrictions, not a parameter recomputation.
