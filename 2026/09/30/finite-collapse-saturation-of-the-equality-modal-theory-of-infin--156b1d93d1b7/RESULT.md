# Finite-collapse saturation of the equality-modal theory of infinite sets
## Finding
Let \(\mathrm{InfSet}_\to\) denote the category whose objects are infinite sets and whose arrows are all functions. Call a wide subcategory \(\mathcal C\subseteq \mathrm{InfSet}_\to\) **finite-collapse rich** when, for every infinite set \(W\), every finite subset \(S\subseteq W\), and every equivalence relation \(E\) on \(S\), the category \(\mathcal C\) contains an endomap \(h:W\to W\) such that \(h\) is the identity on \(W\setminus S\) and
\[
h(s)=h(t)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad sEt
\]
for all \(s,t\in S\).

For every finite-collapse-rich \(\mathcal C\), every formula \(\varphi(\bar x)\) of first-order modal logic in the pure equality language, every infinite \(W\), and every finite tuple \(\bar a\in W^{|\bar x|}\),
\[
W,\bar a\models_{\mathcal C}\varphi
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
W,\bar a\models_{\mathrm{InfSet}_\to}\varphi.
\]
Thus the equality-modal semantics is already saturated by finite collapses inside individual worlds.

Every finite-collapse endomap is finite-to-one. Consequently the category of infinite sets and finite-to-one functions has exactly the same equality-modal semantics as \(\mathrm{InfSet}_\to\). Combining this with Wołoszyn's classification gives sentential propositional validities \(\mathrm{Triv}\) and formulaic propositional validities \(\mathrm{Grz.2}\).

## Assumptions and scope
All worlds are infinite sets. The nonmodal language contains equality only; finitely many parameters may be treated as additional coordinates of the tuple. The modal operator is interpreted covariantly along category arrows in the standard Kripke-category semantics. The comparison category \(\mathcal C\) is assumed to have all infinite sets as objects and to be a subcategory of the all-functions category.

The finite-collapse hypothesis is genuinely weaker than allowing arbitrary functions between worlds. For example, it is satisfied by the wide subcategory generated by finite-support collapse endomorphisms, even if there are no nonidentity arrows between distinct underlying sets. It is also satisfied by the category of all finite-to-one functions.

## Proof
For a finite tuple \(\bar a\), write \(\operatorname{part}(\bar a)\) for the partition of its coordinate positions induced by equality of entries. Wołoszyn's modality-and-quantifier elimination theorem for infinite sets with all functions implies the following partition invariance: for every equality-modal formula \(\psi(\bar y)\), its truth at an infinite world under the all-functions semantics depends only on \(\operatorname{part}(\bar y)\), not on the cardinality of the infinite world or on the particular entries realizing that partition.

We prove the displayed semantic equivalence by induction on \(\varphi\). Atomic equality, Boolean connectives, and quantifiers are immediate from the induction hypothesis, since both semantics use the same underlying set at a fixed world and quantifiers range over that same set.

It remains to treat possibility. The forward implication
\[
W,\bar a\models_{\mathcal C}\Diamond\psi
\Longrightarrow
W,\bar a\models_{\mathrm{InfSet}_\to}\Diamond\psi
\]
is immediate because every \(\mathcal C\)-arrow is a function.

Conversely, suppose
\[
W,\bar a\models_{\mathrm{InfSet}_\to}\Diamond\psi.
\]
Choose a function \(f:W\to U\) to an infinite set \(U\) such that
\[
U,f(\bar a)\models_{\mathrm{InfSet}_\to}\psi.
\]
Let \(p=\operatorname{part}(\bar a)\) and \(q=\operatorname{part}(f(\bar a))\). A function can only identify coordinates, so \(q\) is a coarsening of \(p\). Let \(S\) be the finite set of entries occurring in \(\bar a\), and put two elements of \(S\) in the same \(E\)-class exactly when their corresponding coordinates are identified by \(f\). By finite-collapse richness there is an endomap \(h:W\to W\) in \(\mathcal C\), equal to the identity off \(S\), with
\[
\operatorname{part}(h(\bar a))=q.
\]
Partition invariance for the all-functions semantics therefore gives
\[
U,f(\bar a)\models_{\mathrm{InfSet}_\to}\psi
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
W,h(\bar a)\models_{\mathrm{InfSet}_\to}\psi.
\]
By the induction hypothesis, the right-hand statement is equivalent to
\[
W,h(\bar a)\models_{\mathcal C}\psi.
\]
Since \(h\) is a \(\mathcal C\)-arrow, this witnesses
\[
W,\bar a\models_{\mathcal C}\Diamond\psi.
\]
This completes the induction; necessity follows by duality.

For the finite-to-one corollary, the required collapse map may send every member of each \(E\)-class to a chosen representative and fix all points outside \(S\). Every fiber is finite, so this map is finite-to-one. The exact \(\mathrm{Triv}\) and \(\mathrm{Grz.2}\) classifications then transfer from the all-functions case.

## Verification
The proof was reconstructed at the level of formulas and assignments, including the modal induction step. The critical edge cases are harmless: the empty tuple has the unique empty partition; repeated entries are already encoded by \(p\); and the simulating arrow can always be chosen as an endomap of the original world, so no cardinal comparison between distinct infinite worlds is needed.

A finite combinatorial check enumerated every partition of \(n\le 7\) coordinates and every coarsening pair. For each pair it explicitly constructed the induced finite collapse on tuple-values and verified that the target equality partition is obtained. This is a sanity check only; the proof above is not computational.

## Relationship to prior work
Wołoszyn, *The modal theory of the category of sets* (arXiv:2603.25550), proves simultaneous modality and quantifier elimination for the equality language for the listed morphism classes of functions, surjections, injections, inclusions, bijections, and identities, and classifies infinite-world all-function formulaic validities as \(\mathrm{Grz.2}\) with sentential validities \(\mathrm{Triv}\). The paper's proof repeatedly uses finite quotient maps that merge only finitely many tuple-values, but it does not state the finite-collapse-rich invariance criterion or classify finite-to-one arrows as a separate morphism class.

The present result isolates the exact local mechanism needed from the arrow class: every finite equality-pattern coarsening must be realizable inside the current world. It therefore strengthens the finite-to-one corollary to a robustness theorem for any finite-collapse-rich wide subcategory.

## Limitations
The theorem is specific to the pure equality language on infinite sets. Extra predicates, functions, orders, topology, or algebraic structure can make the actual images of elements matter beyond their equality partition, so finite collapses need not simulate arbitrary arrows. The argument also does not cover a uniformly bounded \(k\)-to-one arrow class for fixed finite \(k\), because a tuple with more than \(k\) distinct entries may require a collapse with a larger fiber. Finite worlds require separate cardinality bookkeeping and are not covered here.
