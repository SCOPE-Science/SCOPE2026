For every finite simple \(3\)-uniform, \(2\)-regular hypergraph \(G\), the number of independent \(4\)-sets is at most \(\binom{n}{4}-n(2n-7)/3\). Equality is completely characterized by dual cubic multigraphs formed from even cycles with alternating doubled edges, yielding the unrestricted \((r,d,k)=(3,2,4)\) coefficient case of the recent regular-hypergraph conjecture.
