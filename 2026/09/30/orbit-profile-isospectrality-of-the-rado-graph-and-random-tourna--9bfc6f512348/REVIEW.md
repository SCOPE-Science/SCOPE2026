# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reduces both finite induced structures to the same bit space \(\mathbb F_2^{\binom{k}{2}}\). Global complement/reversal is translation by the all-one vector; vertex switching generates the cut space of dimension \(k-1\); for \(k\ge3\) the all-one vector is not a cut. The resulting quotient dimensions give the displayed formulas, with \(k=0,1,2\) handled separately. The equality-pattern argument gives the Stirling transform for tuples with repetitions. Exhaustive coset enumeration through \(k=6\) and independent Stirling recomputation through arity \(7\) agree with every formula. The non-conjugacy witness is invariant under permutation-group conjugacy: coordinate transposition fixes both undirected binary orbitals but exchanges the two tournament orientation orbitals.

## Originality
PASS, best-of-knowledge. The closest literature is the reduct classification in arXiv:1309.2165 / doi:10.1112/plms/pdv037, which explicitly lists the two families and their generating operations but does not state these orbit-profile formulas or the cross-family profile-isospectrality conclusion. Targeted checks for random-tournament reduct profiles, switching orbit counts, and graph-versus-tournament profile equivalence located no equivalent or stronger statement. The nearest indexed findings concerned unrelated Rado realizations or other switching enumerations rather than this complete five-profile correspondence.

## Value
PASS. The result supplies a concrete structural limitation of numerical oligomorphic profiles: even the complete tuple-orbit sequence cannot recover whether the two binary orbitals are self-paired or mutually paired. The phenomenon is systematic across the corresponding five reduct profiles rather than being an isolated equality at a few arities, and the affine cut-space proof is reusable for related homogeneous switching structures.

Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.

## Scientific limitations
The conclusion is about numerical orbit counts. It does not establish abstract non-isomorphism of all corresponding higher reduct groups, nor does it compare their full orbit algebras. The literature assessment is best-of-knowledge rather than a formal exhaustive bibliographic proof.
