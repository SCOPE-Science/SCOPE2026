For \(G=K_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}\), solid location-domination is controlled exactly by the number of non-singleton parts: \(\gamma^{DLD}(G)=N-\max\{1,p\}\), while self location-domination is almost rigid, with \(\gamma^{SLD}(G)=N-1\) only when there is exactly one singleton part and \(N\) otherwise. The theorem also classifies every code and gives exact code-size generating functions.
