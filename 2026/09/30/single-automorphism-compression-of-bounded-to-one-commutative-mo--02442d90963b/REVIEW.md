# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The orbit relation is a countable union of Borel equalizer sets and each equivalence class is a countable union of finite fibers, so it is a countable Borel equivalence relation. The 2026 theorem gives hyperfiniteness for exactly the stated bounded-to-one countable commutative-monoid actions. Dougherty--Jackson--Kechris then supplies both a Borel \(\mathbb Z\)-action on the same standard Borel space with the identical orbit relation and the reduction \(E_M^X\leq_B E_0\). The generator of that \(\mathbb Z\)-action is a Borel automorphism. In the nonsmooth case, Harrington--Kechris--Louveau gives \(E_0\leq_B E_M^X\), yielding bireducibility.

An independent 2026 asymptotic-dimension proof also recovers the hyperfiniteness theorem for this action class, providing a current cross-check of the only recent input used in the argument.

## Originality
PASS. The closest recent papers prove hyperfiniteness and sharp asymptotic-dimension bounds, respectively, but do not state the resulting single-automorphism compression together with the exact smooth-versus-\(E_0\) Borel-degree dichotomy for bounded-to-one commutative monoid actions. Targeted searches for this action class together with “single Borel automorphism,” “\(\mathbb Z\)-action,” “\(E_0\),” “bireducible,” and “smooth” did not locate an equivalent or stronger explicit statement. The conclusion is a synthesis of the recent theorem with classical general results, and is presented as such rather than as a new hyperfiniteness argument.

## Value
PASS. The result converts a hyperfiniteness theorem into two concrete classification statements. First, countably many possibly noninvertible commuting bounded-to-one maps can be replaced, without changing orbit equivalence, by one invertible Borel transformation. Second, nonsmooth orbit relations in this newly established class all have exactly the benchmark degree \(E_0\), ruling out intermediate nonsmooth degrees below it.

## Closest literature
Shinko--Weilacher--Yu prove the ownership hyperfiniteness theorem. Wang--Yu--Zhu independently recover it using Borel asymptotic dimension. Dougherty--Jackson--Kechris provide the classical \(\mathbb Z\)-action realization and upper comparison with \(E_0\); Harrington--Kechris--Louveau provide the classical lower comparison in the nonsmooth case.

## Scientific limitations
The finding is an exact consequence of known theorems, not a new construction. The replacement automorphism preserves only orbit equivalence, not the original monoid dynamics. The result does not distinguish smooth actions internally and does not extend beyond the bounded-to-one commutative setting.
