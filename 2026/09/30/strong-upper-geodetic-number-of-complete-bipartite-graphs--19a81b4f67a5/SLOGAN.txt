For \(K_{a,b}\) with \(1\le a\le b\), the strong upper geodetic number is \(2\) for \(K_{1,1}\), is \(b\) for nontrivial stars, and is \(b+1\) whenever \(a\ge2\). Maximum minimal sets are completely classified, yielding exact counts, including \(\binom{a}{2}b\) in the unbalanced case \(2\le a<b\).\n
