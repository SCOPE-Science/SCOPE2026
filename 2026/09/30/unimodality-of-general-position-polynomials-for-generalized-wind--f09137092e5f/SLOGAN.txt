For every generalized windmill \(W_{n,m}=K_1\vee nK_m\) with \(n\ge2\) and \(m\ge1\), the general position polynomial is \(\psi(W_{n,m};x)=(1+x)^{nm}+x+n x((1+x)^m-1)\), and its coefficient sequence is always unimodal. Thus all friendship graphs and stars lie in one natural infinite unimodal family.
