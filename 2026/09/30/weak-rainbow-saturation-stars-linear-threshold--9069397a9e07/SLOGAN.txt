For every \(\ell\ge3\) and \(n\ge2\ell-3\), the weak rainbow saturation number of the star is exactly \(\operatorname{rwsat}(n,S_\ell)=\binom{\ell}{2}-1\). A local endpoint-state rule turns the universal coloring condition into a sharp degree-counting argument, while \(K_\ell-e\) plus isolates supplies the matching construction.
