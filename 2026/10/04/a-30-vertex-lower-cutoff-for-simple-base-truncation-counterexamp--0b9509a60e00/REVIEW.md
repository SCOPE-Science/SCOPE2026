# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The lower bound \(\chi'_s(T(G))\ge6\) is structural: the three triangle edges and three incident external edges at every truncated base vertex form a six-clique in \(L(T(G))^2\). The upper bound is exhaustive for all connected simple cubic bases of orders \(4\), \(6\), and \(8\): generation gives \(1,2,5\) isomorphism types, matching the independent classical census, and an exact 6-coloring is found and rechecked for each conflict graph.

## Originality
PASS. The 2025 open-problem source records truncated prisms as the positive family. The 2026 Tanwar counterexample uses a cubic multigraph base and explicitly leaves the simple-base truncation version open. Focused searches under the truncation, strong-edge-coloring, and line-graph-square formulations found no source covering all simple cubic bases through order eight. The main residual risk is that the inaccessible body of the 2026 preprint could contain an unindexed remark about these finite cases; its abstract and indexed proposition-level excerpts do not imply the 24-vertex part of the claim.

## Value
PASS. This is a natural finite cutoff for an explicitly open simple-base question: all eight smallest connected simple cubic base types are eliminated as counterexamples, forcing any counterexample to start at base order at least ten and truncation order at least thirty. The cutoff is not merely a recomputation of a standard invariant table because the cubic census supplies only the graph types, not their truncation strong chromatic indices.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest sources are arXiv:2511.02892, which poses the truncation question and cites the truncated-prism case, and arXiv:2607.23462, which constructs a seven-color example from a cubic multigraph base while leaving simple bases open. The result remains finite: the 19 simple cubic bases on ten vertices and all larger bases are unresolved here.
