For the exceptional \(3A_1\) cubics with \(9\rho^2-14\rho+9=0\), the Hessian quartic has exactly ten singular points with basket \(7A_1+3A_3\). Its Hessian rank-drop scheme still has length ten but only seven support points: four reduced points and three curvilinear double points, and those three collisions are exactly the \(A_3\) singularities.
