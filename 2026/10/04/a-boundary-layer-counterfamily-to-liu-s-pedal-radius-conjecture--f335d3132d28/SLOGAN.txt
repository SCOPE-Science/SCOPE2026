Liu's conjecture \(R_p+\sqrt2\,r_p<R\) for pedal triangles is false. In the fixed isosceles triangle \(A=(-1,0), B=(1,0), C=(0,2)\), points \(P=(0,y)\) sufficiently close to the midpoint of \(AB\) from the interior reverse the inequality, and \(P=(0,1/100)\) gives an exact certified counterexample.
