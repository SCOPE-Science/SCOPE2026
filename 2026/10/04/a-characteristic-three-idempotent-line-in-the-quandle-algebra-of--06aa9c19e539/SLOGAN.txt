For the smallest latin dihedral quandle, the coefficient characteristic creates a sharp idempotent jump: \(K[R_3]\) has exactly eight idempotents when \(\operatorname{char}K\ne3\), but in characteristic \(3\) its nonzero idempotents form an affine line. Over \(\mathbb F_q\) with \(3\mid q\), the exact count is therefore \(q+1\).
