For the lattice norm \(\|f\|_\nu=\|f\|_\infty+\int|f|\,d\nu\), every signed measure has an exact dual norm given by a one-parameter clipping threshold, and norm attainment is characterized by simultaneous equality in the total-variation and \(L^1\)-\(L^\infty\) components. The entire band of measures singular to a full-support \(\nu\) keeps its total-variation norm but contains no nonzero norm-attainer; for nonatomic \(\nu\), distinct point evaluations are non-attaining and lie at dual distance \(2\).
