For the unweighted rank-two long-memory LIL, the normalized boundary constant admits an explicit closed-form Schur remainder: \(\lambda_{\max}(K_\alpha)\) stays within \(2\sqrt2(1-2^{-\varepsilon/2}/(1+\varepsilon))\) of its critical value when \(\varepsilon=1-2\alpha\). This turns the boundary \(O(\varepsilon)\) statement into a computable two-sided enclosure at every interior parameter.
