For the monomial nonlinear multiview map of Example 6.7, the graph chart \(X=D=1\) is \(C^2=ABZ^2\), so the graph is nonnormal. Its chart normalization is \(T^2=AB\) with \(C=ZT\), and the conductor \((Z,C)\) separates a codimension-one nonnormal plane from a normal transverse \(A_1\) singular stratum.
