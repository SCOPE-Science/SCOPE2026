# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The contradiction is exact: Wen's logarithmic roof has a forward stable pair whose accumulated roof difference is a divergent harmonic-type series, whereas a Hölder representative contributes an absolutely summable geometric tail and a continuous coboundary contributes only a convergent endpoint term. The explicit family \(r_c\) is positive for every \(0<c<1\), is non-Hölder by a one-symbol asymptotic test, and is continuously cohomologous to the constant roof.

## Originality
PASS. The closest inspected source proves the particular non-Hölder failure and the Hölder sufficient theorem, while the standard special-flow reference proves conjugacy for cohomologous positive roofs. The standard conjugacy result covers the mechanism used for the positive examples, so that component is not treated as standalone novelty. The accepted originality is the source-specific obstruction showing that Wen's logarithmic roof cannot be moved into any Hölder class by a continuous coboundary. Targeted searches found no inspected statement implying this conclusion.

## Value
PASS. The result refines the interpretation of the 2026 counterexample: non-Hölder regularity by itself is not the decisive obstruction to local product structure. The divergence of the stable roof cocycle is invariant, up to a convergent endpoint correction, under continuous cohomology and therefore identifies a structural obstruction directly aligned with the source proof.
