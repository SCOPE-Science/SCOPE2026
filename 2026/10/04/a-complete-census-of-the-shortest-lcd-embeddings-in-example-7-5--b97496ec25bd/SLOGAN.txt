The shortest-LCD family behind An–Kim Example 7.5 contains exactly \(1536\) labeled embeddings: \(768\) have Gray distance \(6\), \(720\) have Gray distance \(7\), and exactly \(48\) attain the optimal Gray distance \(8\). Those \(48\) optimal binary LCD \([20,6,8]\) images realize two distinct weight enumerators, so at least two permutation-inequivalent optimal Gray-image types occur.
