Both Sprott-C extensions advertised as hyperchaotic reduce to a three-dimensional autonomous base plus nonexpanding added directions. The memristive model has the exact first integral \(y+\phi\), and its full Lyapunov spectrum is the base spectrum together with \(0\) and \(-b\), so neither printed system can have two positive asymptotic Lyapunov exponents in the parameter regimes used by the source.
