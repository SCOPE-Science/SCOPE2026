In the delayed fear-stage predator-prey model, the published adult-predator limit is false because delayed juvenile maturation cannot be discarded. A Lyapunov-Krasovskii argument instead gives the valid delay-independent sufficient threshold \(\mathcal R_P=\eta b_2c_2K/((1+b_1K)(d_2+c_2)d_3)<1\), under which both predator stages vanish and the prey tends \(K=(r-d_1)/c_1\).
