# Review

## Correctness
PASS. Example 11 of arXiv:2609.28354v1 gives the \((23)\)-ordered value as \(\zeta(-a)\zeta(-c)\) when \(\ell_{12}=0\). Example 19 gives the regular value as that product plus \((-1)^{c+1}\zeta(-a-c-1)/(c+1)\). Their simultaneous vanishing is therefore equivalent to one of \(a,c\) being positive even and \(a+c\) being odd, which forces both diagonal exponents positive. Conversely, the product vanishes by parity and Theorem 1 gives vanishing of the regular value. The final implication to the all-six common-zero condition is logically one-way: if all six vanish, these two vanish. No finite experiment is used to justify the universal quantifiers.

## Originality
PASS. The source states the all-orders characterization as Conjecture 2 but does not prove its only-if direction on this slice. It also does not state that the regular and \((23)\)-ordered limits alone detect the necessary condition when \(\ell_{12}=0\). Searches by source identifier, ordered-limit terminology, common-zero terminology, the decoupled condition, and implication-equivalent formulations found the source itself but no paper or published finding that states or implies this result. The residual risk is that the short consequence may appear in later work not yet indexed.

## Value
PASS. The slice \(\ell_{12}=0\) is the natural decoupling boundary of the rank-two root-system zeta function, not an arbitrary parameter cut. The result proves an infinite slice of the converse half of a newly posed conjecture and reduces its necessary condition there to two explicit ordered limits. This cleanly separates boundary behavior from the genuinely coupled case \(\ell_{12}>0\).

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is Rutard--Shinohara, arXiv:2609.28354v1, which supplies the definitions, the two rank-two formulas, the regular-value forward vanishing theorem, and the general conjecture. The present claim does not prove the if direction for all six ordered limits and does not address positive off-diagonal exponent or higher rank.
