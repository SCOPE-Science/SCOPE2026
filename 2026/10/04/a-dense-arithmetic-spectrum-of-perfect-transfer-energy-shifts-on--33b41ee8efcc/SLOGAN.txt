With all complete-graph couplings fixed at the source value \(2\), exact transfer is not confined to the tuning \(\Delta=2n\): it occurs for a countable dense set of positive equal shifts. The shifts are classified exactly by the opposite-parity rational values of \(\beta=(\Delta-2n)/\sqrt{4n^2-4(n-4)\Delta+\Delta^2}\), and every transfer time is an odd multiple of the corresponding minimum.
