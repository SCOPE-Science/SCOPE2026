## Value
PASS. Quasismoothness checks for weighted hypersurfaces ordinarily require a subset/monomial test. In this infinite two-heavy-weight family the theorem replaces that test by two elementary divisibility checks valid in every dimension and converts it into a quantitative statement: quasismooth anticanonical members are asymptotically sparse among canonical ambients. This is a structural simplification and a global distribution result rather than a finite census.
