For \(X=\mathbb P(1^r,a,b)\), quasismoothness of a general anticanonical hypersurface is equivalent to two explicit three-way divisibility tests at the two heavy weights. Inside the canonical strip, the number of successful weight pairs is \(O(r^{3/2})\) among \(3r(r+1)/2\) possibilities, so their proportion tends to zero.
