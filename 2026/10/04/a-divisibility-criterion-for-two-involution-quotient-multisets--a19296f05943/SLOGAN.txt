If \(s\) and \(t\) are distinct involutions in a finite group and \(n=\operatorname{ord}(st)\ge3\), then a full-size quotient multiset supported on \(\{s,t\}\) is realizable exactly when the number of copies of \(s\) is divisible by \(n\). In the dihedral subgroup \(\langle s,t\rangle\cong D_{2n}\), this leaves exactly the three multiplicities \(0,n,2n\).
