On the Gorenstein canonical non-terminal boundary of \(\mathbb P(1^r,a,b)\), the associated reflexive simplex has Kronecker \(h^*\)-polynomial exactly for the equal-weight point or the divisor branch \((a,b)=(c,c+r)\) with \(c\mid r\). Thus \(\tau(r)+1\) of the \(\tau(2r)+1\) Gorenstein boundary members have all \(h^*\)-roots on the unit circle.
