# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Each metric piece is bi-Lipschitz equivalent to a subset of a Euclidean space, hence its free space is linearly isomorphic to a free space covered by Mason's 2026 Dunford--Pettis theorem. Countable \(\ell_1\)-sums of Dunford--Pettis spaces retain the property. For separable \(X\), the Godefroy--Kalton lifting theorem places a complemented copy of \(X\) in \(\mathcal F(X)\), so failure of the Dunford--Pettis property for \(X\) forces failure for \(\mathcal F(X)\). A complemented embedding of \(\mathcal F(X)\) into the target sum would therefore contradict permanence to complemented subspaces.

## Originality
PASS. The closest source, arXiv:2609.28842v1, proves the Euclidean-subset theorem and only states the finite-dimensional \(\ell_1\)-sum nonisomorphism for \(\ell_p\), \(1<p<\infty\). The original Candido--Cúth--Doucha Question 8 is likewise restricted to \(\ell_p\). Full-text comparison with arXiv:2005.06555v2 found broad self-sum and doubling-space decompositions but no theorem implying the arbitrary-source complemented-embedding obstruction. Multiple statement-level semantic searches found no covering result. The main residual risk is that this short consequence may exist as an uncatalogued remark or in a later revision of the very recent focal preprint.

## Value
PASS. The claim turns a single classical-family nonisomorphism into a uniform decomposition obstruction for every separable Banach source failing the Dunford--Pettis property, and it allows arbitrary finite-dimensional metric pieces rather than only canonical linear sections. The complemented-embedding form is stronger than mere nonisomorphism and directly constrains finite-dimensional \(\ell_1\)-model strategies in Lipschitz-free space classification.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest sources are Mason's 2026 Dunford--Pettis theorem and its \(\ell_p\) corollary, Candido--Cúth--Doucha's Question 8, and Albiac--Ansorena--Cúth--Doucha's general theory of infinite sums. The result is limited to complemented embeddings and to metric pieces with finite-dimensional bi-Lipschitz models; it does not settle the \(\ell_1\) source case or arbitrary doubling pieces.
