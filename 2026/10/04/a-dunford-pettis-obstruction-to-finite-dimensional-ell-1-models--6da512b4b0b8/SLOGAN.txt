If a separable Banach space \(X\) fails the Dunford--Pettis property, then \(\mathcal F(X)\) cannot be a complemented subspace of any countable \(\ell_1\)-sum of free spaces over metric spaces with finite-dimensional bi-Lipschitz models. This uniformly extends the recent \(\ell_p\) finite-section nonisomorphism from canonical sections to arbitrary finite-dimensional metric pieces.
