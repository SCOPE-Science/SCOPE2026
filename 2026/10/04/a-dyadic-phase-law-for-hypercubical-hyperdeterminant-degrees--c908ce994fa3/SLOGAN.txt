For the format \(2^{\times d}\) hyperdeterminant, the two-adic valuation equals the factorial baseline \(d-s_2(d)\) for even \(d\), exceeds it by exactly one for \(d\equiv3\pmod4\), and exceeds it by at least two for \(d\equiv1\pmod4\).
