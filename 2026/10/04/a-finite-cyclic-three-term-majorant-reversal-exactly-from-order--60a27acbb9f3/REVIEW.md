# Review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The claim reduces exactly to a root-of-unity average depending only on the character order. The derivative estimate \(\|D''\|_1\le2232\pi\) is reconstructed from \(|P|\le3\), \(|P'|\le4\), and \(|P''|\le10\), and twice-integrated Fourier coefficients give the explicit aliasing error \(372\pi^2/L^2\). Outward-rounded interval evaluation certifies the finite range and the lower bound for the continuous mean inferred from the \(10000\)-point average. The four exceptional orders are evaluated exactly. The computation supports, but does not replace, the analytic all-order tail proof.

## Originality
**PASS.** The accessible primary literature proves the corresponding continuous-circle inequality and explicitly reports an older discrete-uniform numerical lower bound, but the inspected material does not supply a rigorous all-order finite-cyclic theorem, the exact threshold \(L=5\), or the uniform quantitative gap proved here. Searches using finite-cyclic, roots-of-unity, discrete-uniform, three-term-idempotent, and Hardy--Littlewood-majorant formulations produced no covering published statement. The strongest residual risk is the inaccessible full text of Mockenhaupt's 1996 thesis; Krenedits' published description specifically characterizes its relevant lower bound as numerical and says that the attempt did not yield a proof.

## Value
**PASS.** The pair \(1+e_1\pm e_3\) is the classical minimal three-term Hardy--Littlewood example. Determining exactly when its continuous majorant reversal survives passage to a complete finite character grid is a natural finite analogue. The result gives a sharp transition at character order \(5\), not an arbitrary modulus table, and supplies a uniform positive margin useful for finite-group transference and discretization arguments.

## Closest literature and limitations
Krenedits, arXiv:1006.0409v1, proves the continuous \(2<p<4\) case and records Mockenhaupt's earlier discrete-uniform numerical attempt. Pan--You--Wang--Zhou--Chen, arXiv:2609.09740v1, proves the full continuous three-term conjecture but does not state the all-grid finite-cyclic theorem. The full Mockenhaupt thesis was not directly inspectable, so the originality conclusion retains that explicit residual risk.
