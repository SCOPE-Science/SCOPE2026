## Correctness
PASS. The exact almost-perfect identity gives \((A-1)\sigma(N)=AN-1\). The proper-divisor sum first forces both odd primes above \(A\). Strict finite-prime-power abundancy bounds then yield a positive cross-multiplied difference whose exact decomposition forces \(A(p+q-1)-pq\ge1\). This is algebraically equivalent to the claimed hyperbolic bound, and the explicit prime bounds follow. No finite experiment is used as an infinite proof.
