For Gaussian independence with scalar linear kernels, the published cross-HSIC studentizer is exactly arcsine and bounded at half-sample size \(n=3\), but becomes unbounded for every \(n\ge4\). In fact \(\Pr(|T_n|>t)\ge c_n t^{-(n-1)}\) locally at an explicit denominator singularity, forcing \(\mathbb E|T_n|^p=\infty\) for every \(p\ge n-1\).
