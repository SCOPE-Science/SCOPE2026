# Review of A five-dimensional nilpotent-free subspace of \(M_3(\mathbb F_3)\)

## Correctness
PASS. The five displayed generators are independent. The complete \(3^5\)-element enumeration checks two exact and equivalent nilpotency criteria, and both isolate only the zero matrix. The claim is finite and exhaustive; no inference from sampling is used.

## Originality
PASS. The closest inspected primary source is Wigderson, arXiv:2609.29177v1. It formulates the dimension-\(6\) target for \(M_3(\mathbb F_3)\) as part of Conjecture 8 and explicitly says the finite-field construction is unclear. Its extension-field construction gives dimension \(3\), not \(5\). Exact and alias searches did not locate this five-dimensional statement. Residual risk remains that an equivalent construction appears in literature under a different matrix-space equivalence or terminology.

## Value
PASS. This is a natural finite lower bound at the first odd-field \(3\times3\) case of a newly stated extremal problem. It improves the direct dimension-\(3\) extension-field construction to dimension \(5\), leaving only one dimension to the conjectural target. No maximality claim is made.

## Closest literature and limitations
