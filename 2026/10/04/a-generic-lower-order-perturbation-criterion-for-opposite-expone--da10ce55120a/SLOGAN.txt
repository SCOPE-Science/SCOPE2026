For \(\widetilde Q=-Q+R\) with \(1\le\deg R<\deg Q\), the opposite-leading-coefficient Baker-omitted-value problem has a generic positive answer: if \(Q\) and \(R\) share no fundamental ray, then \(\exp(Q)+\exp(\widetilde Q)+P\) has a Baker omitted value at \(\infty\) for every nonconstant polynomial \(P\). The condition excludes only finitely many leading-coefficient directions for a fixed lower degree of \(R\).
