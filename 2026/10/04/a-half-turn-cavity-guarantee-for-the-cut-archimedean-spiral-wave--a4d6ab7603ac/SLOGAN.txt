A shifted comparison disk gives a rigorous half-turn criterion for the cut Archimedean spiral waveguide: for every \(a>0\) and \(\beta\ge\pi\), the Dirichlet Laplacian has a bound state below \((2a)^{-2}\). This improves the published analytic disk condition \(\beta>2j_{0,1}\approx4.805\) without claiming that \(\pi\) is the true critical angle.
