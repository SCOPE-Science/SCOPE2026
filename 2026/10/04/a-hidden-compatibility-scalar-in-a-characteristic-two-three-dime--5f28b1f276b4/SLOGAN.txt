In characteristic \(2\), the three-dimensional family \(P_{3,22}(q)\) carries a basis-independent compatibility scalar \(\kappa=q\): all nonzero parameters have the same associative and Lie component types but remain pairwise nonisomorphic as Poisson algebras. This failure of component types to determine the Poisson structure is dimension-minimal, and over \(\mathbb F_{2^m}\) it yields \(2^m-1\) distinct nonzero-parameter classes over one fixed component pair.
