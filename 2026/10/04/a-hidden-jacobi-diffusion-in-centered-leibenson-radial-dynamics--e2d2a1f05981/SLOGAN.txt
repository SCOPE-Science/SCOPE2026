For the centered Leibenson process, the normalized radial power \(Q_t=(|X_t-o|/R(t))^{p/(p-1)}\) is exactly a stationary Jacobi diffusion after logarithmic time. Its two-time correlation is explicit: \(\operatorname{Corr}(Q_s,Q_t)=(s/t)^\Lambda\) for \(0<s\le t\), with \(\Lambda\) given in the accompanying result.
