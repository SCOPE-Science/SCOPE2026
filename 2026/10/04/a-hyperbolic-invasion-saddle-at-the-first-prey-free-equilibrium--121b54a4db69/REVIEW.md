# Same-model review

## Correctness — PASS
The source explicitly gives \(\lambda_1=r_1>0\) and the remaining quadratic \(\lambda^2-B_1\lambda+B_2=0\) with \(B_1<0\), \(B_2>0\). Vieta's relations imply that the remaining two eigenvalues either are both negative real numbers or form a conjugate pair with real part \(B_1/2<0\). Hence the equilibrium has exactly one unstable direction and no center direction. Standard saddle-node theory requires a zero eigenvalue, so the source's “saddle-node” label is incompatible with its own spectrum.

## Value — PASS
This is not merely a terminology preference: hyperbolicity versus saddle-node degeneracy changes the local phase portrait and whether a codimension-one equilibrium collision can occur there. The exact transverse eigenvalue \(r_1>0\) also gives a robust ecological conclusion—first-prey invasion of the first-prey-free boundary state—which remains independent of the refuge and switching coefficients appearing only in higher-order or within-boundary dynamics at that point.
