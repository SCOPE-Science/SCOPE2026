At the first-prey-free equilibrium \(P_4\), the model's own factorization gives one eigenvalue \(r_1>0\) and two eigenvalues with negative real parts under \(B_1<0\) and \(B_2>0\). Thus \(P_4\) is a hyperbolic invasion saddle, not a saddle-node, and the missing first prey can always invade to first order under those conditions.
