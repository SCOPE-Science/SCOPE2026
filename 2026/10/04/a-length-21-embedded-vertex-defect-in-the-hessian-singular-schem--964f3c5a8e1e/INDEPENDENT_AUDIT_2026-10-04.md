For f=s^3+g the Hessian determinant is a scalar multiple of sh, so on s=1 the Jacobian singular scheme is cut out by the four cubic partials K of the Hessian quartic h. The ten pentahedral nodes form the saturation I. Maximal-rank openness then establishes the stated nonempty open family; the local node decomposition is radical away from the vertex.
