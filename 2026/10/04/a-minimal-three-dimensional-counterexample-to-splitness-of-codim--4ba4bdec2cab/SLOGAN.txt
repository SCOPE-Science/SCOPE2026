The codimension-only splitness question for semiconnected standard gradings already fails in dimension \(3\): in \(k\times k[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)\), the degree-zero factor ideal has quotient dimension \(\dim_k A_0\) but is not split. Dimension \(3\) is minimal, and the example has tangent dimension \(0\) against \(\dim_k A_1=1\), pinpointing the missing hypothesis.
