In the published delayed-SIRS persistence proof, the AM–GM product carries an omitted factor \(\rho(t)=\frac{S(t-\tau)}{S(t)}\frac{1+\kappa I(t)}{1+\kappa I(t-\tau)}\). An exact admissible history has \(\widetilde{\mathcal R}_0^S=80/21>1\) but violates the claimed generator upper bound, so that proof does not establish mean persistence as written.
