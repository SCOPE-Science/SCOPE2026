For the reduced dryland plant model, the quadratic equation for biomass roots needs a second, sharp feasibility test: \(0<u<(m+\sqrt{m^2+4a})/2\). Without it, the published equilibrium classification can label a state with negative water density as positive; \(a=b=d=m=1\), \(c=-3\) gives the exact witness \((u,v)=(2,-1)\).
