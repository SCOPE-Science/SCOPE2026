# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The final claim is For the complex \(D_{6,3}\) of simple graphs on six labeled vertices whose domination number is at least \(3\), \(D_{6,3}\simeq \bigvee^{115} S^4 \vee \bigvee^{24} S^5\). The proof reduces the statement to a finite discrete-Morse certificate. The embedded verifier reconstructs all \(4502\) simplices, checks the \(2181\) matching pairs, checks acyclicity across all \(21300\) Hasse cover relations, and computes the exact integral Morse differential from critical dimension \(5\) to critical dimension \(4\) as zero. Forman's theorem then leaves one \(0\)-cell, \(115\) \(4\)-cells, and \(24\) \(5\)-cells. Since the resulting \(4\)-skeleton is \(3\)-connected, Hurewicz identifies \(\pi_4\) with \(H_4\), so the zero differential forces every \(5\)-cell attaching map to be null-homotopic. Boundary-rank computations over both \(\mathbb F_2\) and \(\mathbb F_3\) independently reproduce the expected Betti numbers. The proof does not infer an infinite statement from finite sampling.

## Originality
PASS. The closest primary paper, arXiv:1901.07130, explicitly reports only \(\chi(D_{6,3})=92\) and poses the mixed-dimensional wedge question rather than supplying the homotopy type. Focused searches for the object name, domination-complex aliases, the exact sphere multiplicities, and mixed-dimensional wedge language found no statement implying this decomposition. A previous record for the same object gives only mod-\(2\) homology; the new wedge decomposition strictly implies that record, while the converse fails because homology does not determine attaching maps. Residual risk is limited to a recent, non-indexed, or differently phrased duplicate.

## Value
PASS. This is not an arbitrary small-complex census: \(D_{6,3}\) was specifically highlighted in the primary source, and the source asked whether the family contains a wedge of spheres in different dimensions. The theorem gives an exact homotopy type for that named test case and supplies the requested mixed-dimensional example.
