PASS. The claim is proved through an exact coordinate-face decomposition. The independent pair term is exact, the two negative-pair terms are controlled analytically by Gaussian conditioning, and the triple face is controlled by the Stieltjes-matrix lower-corner density bound plus fixed-width shell estimates. Quantile inversion is explicit. The finite checker is only supplementary.

## Originality
PASS. The motivating 2026 paper explicitly leaves one-sided nonpositive Gaussian second terms with at least one negative correlation open. Kortschak's closest Gaussian second-order theorem covers fixed negative edges at Pareto index one but excludes the zero-correlation boundary where the logarithm appears. Yang and Zhang treat a bivariate regime and explicitly do not focus on negative tail orders. The finite-level harmonic-mean and first-order universal-calibration papers do not imply the mixed trivariate coefficient. Targeted indexed-literature and statement-level searches found no equivalent statement.

Residual risk remains that an equivalent special case may be buried in older multivariate risk-aggregation literature under different terminology; this is recorded rather than treated as disproved by search failure.

## Value
PASS. The result settles a natural minimal mixed-face instance of the stated open problem and gives an operational calibration consequence: the independent logarithmic penalty survives exactly on the zero-correlated edge and disappears on the two strictly negative edges.
