For a general smooth complete-intersection curve of type \((a,b)\) in \(\mathbb P^3\) with \(a,b\ge4\), the Cayley quadrisecant degree is odd by an exact mod-\(8\) criterion: either one degree is odd and the even degree is \(4\) or \(6\pmod8\), or both degrees are odd with \(a+b\equiv0\) or \(2\pmod8\). On the corresponding real zero-dimensional locus, odd degree forces a real quadrisecant line.
