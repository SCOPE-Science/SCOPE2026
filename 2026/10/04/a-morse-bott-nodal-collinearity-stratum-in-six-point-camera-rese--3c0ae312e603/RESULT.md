# A Morse-Bott nodal collinearity stratum in six-point camera resectioning
## Finding
For the six world points \(q_1=[1:0:0:0],q_2=[0:1:0:0],q_3=[0:0:1:0],q_4=[0:0:0:1],q_5=[1:1:1:1],q_6=[1:2:3:5]\), let \(X\subset(\mathbb P^2)^6\) be the one-camera six-point resectioning variety. Its defining six-focal is the determinant \(F=\det M\) of the square \(18\times18\) focal matrix. The locus \(C\) of collinear image sextuples is contained in \(\operatorname{Sing}(X)\). At \(p_i=[1:i:0]\) for \(1\le i\le6\), the affine Hessian of \(F\) has rank \(4\), and its kernel is exactly the \(8\)-dimensional tangent space of \(C\). Hence \(C\) is locally the full singular locus there and \(X\) has a transverse ordinary double point; equivalently, locally analytically \(X\cong\mathbb A^8\times\{u_1^2+u_2^2+u_3^2+u_4^2=0\}\). Consequently a dense open subset of \(C\) has this transverse nodal type.

## Assumptions and scope
Work over \(\mathbb C\). Consider the six world points
\[
q_1=[1:0:0:0],\quad q_2=[0:1:0:0],\quad q_3=[0:0:1:0],\quad q_4=[0:0:0:1],\quad q_5=[1:1:1:1],\quad q_6=[1:2:3:5].
\]
All fifteen \(4\times4\) determinants obtained from four of these vectors are nonzero, so no four points are coplanar. For one camera and six world points, Theorem 6 and Definition 5 of Connelly--Duff--Loucks-Tavitas imply that the resectioning ideal is generated by the single square six-focal determinant \(F=\det M\), where \(M\) has size \(18\times18\). The exact verifier reconstructs this matrix directly.

Let \(C\subset(\mathbb P^2)^6\) denote the locus of sextuples lying on one image line. It is irreducible of dimension \(8\): choose the line in \(\mathbb P^{2\vee}\) and then choose six points on that line.

## Proof
Write \(Q=[q_1\ \cdots\ q_6]\), a \(4\times6\) matrix. Since \(Q\) has rank \(4\), its relation space \(R=\ker Q\) has dimension \(2\). If \(p_1,\ldots,p_6\) lie on a line with covector \(\ell\in(\mathbb C^3)^*\), then \(\ell^T p_i=0\) for all \(i\). For every relation \(\alpha=(\alpha_i)\in R\), form a left-kernel vector for the six-focal matrix by taking its \(i\)-th three-entry block to be \(\alpha_i\ell\). The first twelve columns vanish because \(\sum_i\alpha_i q_i=0\), and the \(i\)-th focal column vanishes because \(\alpha_i\ell^T p_i=0\). Two independent relations therefore give two independent left-kernel vectors, so
\[
\operatorname{rank}M\le16
\]
throughout \(C\). Thus \(F=\det M=0\), and every first derivative of \(F\) with respect to an image coordinate is a \(17\times17\) cofactor and also vanishes. Hence \(C\subset\operatorname{Sing}(X)\).

Now pass to the affine chart \(p_i=[1:y_i:z_i]\) and take the collinear point \(c\) with \(y_i=i\) and \(z_i=0\). Exact arithmetic gives \(\operatorname{rank}M(c)=16\). The \(12\times12\) Hessian of the affine six-focal at \(c\) has rank \(4\); an explicit normal \(4\times4\) Hessian minor has determinant \(484\). The tangent space of \(C\) is generated by the six independent \(y_i\)-motions and the two infinitesimal line motions
\[
\delta z_i=\delta a+i\,\delta b,
\]
so \(\dim T_cC=8\). The exact verifier checks that this tangent space is contained in the Hessian kernel. Since the Hessian kernel also has dimension \(12-4=8\), equality holds.

The holomorphic Morse-Bott lemma now gives analytic coordinates in which \(C\) is the critical manifold and
\[
F=u_1^2+u_2^2+u_3^2+u_4^2.
\]
Therefore \(X=V(F)\) is locally \(\mathbb A^8\) times a three-dimensional ordinary double point. Hessian rank \(4\) is an open condition on \(C\), so the same transverse nodal type holds on a dense open subset of the collinearity locus.

## Verification
Run `verify_resection_collinear_node.py` with Python 3 and SymPy. It checks the no-four-coplanar condition, reconstructs the square six-focal determinant, verifies two symbolic left-kernel vectors on a generic affine collinear sextuple, and then checks at \(p_i=[1:i:0]\) that the focal matrix has rank \(16\), the gradient vanishes, the Hessian has rank \(4\), its kernel equals the \(8\)-dimensional collinearity tangent space, and a normal Hessian minor equals \(484\).

## Relationship to prior work
Connelly, Duff, and Loucks-Tavitas introduced the resectioning variety and proved that when no four world points are coplanar, its ideal is generated by the focal polynomials; for six points and one camera this specializes to the single square six-focal determinant used here. Their paper develops the ideal, universal Gröbner basis, duality, and Euclidean-distance-degree questions, but the inspected text does not state the singular collinearity stratum or its local analytic type.

Classical camera-resectioning literature discusses degenerate configurations in which camera recovery is non-unique. That broader degeneracy theory motivates checking collinear image data, but the searched sources did not supply the scheme-theoretic statement \(C\subset\operatorname{Sing}(X)\) together with the Morse-Bott Hessian calculation and transverse ordinary-double-point classification.

## Limitations
The claim does not determine the complete global singular locus of the six-point resectioning hypersurface. It proves that the full collinearity locus is singular and identifies the generic local type on that locus. An equivalent result may exist in classical projective-camera literature under different terminology; targeted exact and semantic searches did not locate one.
