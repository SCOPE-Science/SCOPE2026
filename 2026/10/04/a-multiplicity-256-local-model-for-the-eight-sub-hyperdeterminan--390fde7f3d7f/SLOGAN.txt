At the diagonal rank-two tensor \(T=e_0^{\otimes4}+e_1^{\otimes4}\), the eight coordinate \(2\times2\times2\) hyperdeterminants form a local complete intersection whose tangent cone is defined by the squares of the eight Hamming-weight \(1\) and \(3\) coordinates. Its Hilbert--Samuel multiplicity is \(256\), giving a scheme-theoretic explanation for the canonical interior point where all eight boundary-surrogate quartics vanish.
