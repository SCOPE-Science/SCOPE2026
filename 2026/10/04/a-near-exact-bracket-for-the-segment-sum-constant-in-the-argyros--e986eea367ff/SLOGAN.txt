For the new Argyros–Motakis tree norm, the optimal uniform norm of finite segment sums lies in an interval of width below \(8.368\times10^{-24}\) around \(1.05452803886698882025294\). The published constant \(2\) can therefore be sharpened by almost a factor of two using the first forced size jumps of the very-fast-growing norming family.
