In the normalized reflection-symmetric family \(T_{p,h}=\operatorname{conv}\{(-1,0,0),(1,0,0),(0,p,0),(0,0,h)\}\), the incenter lies on the Euler affine hull exactly on the symmetric locus \(p=h\) and one explicit algebraic branch \((p+h)^2=4ph(ph+2)^2/(5ph+6)\) with \(ph\ge2\). The point \((p,h)=(\sqrt7,3/\sqrt7)\) gives an exact non-orthocentric, non-biregular example, showing that the general tetrahedral problem extends genuinely beyond the previously characterized orthocentric class.\n
