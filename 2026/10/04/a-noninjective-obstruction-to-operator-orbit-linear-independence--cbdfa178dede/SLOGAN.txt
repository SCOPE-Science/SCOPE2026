A tight \(g\)-fusion frame can have an infinite-dimensional operator span while a bounded noninjective representing operator forces an explicit dependence \(\Theta_2=\Theta_1/2\), refuting Proposition 4.1 and Theorem 4.3(ii) of arXiv:2305.08182v1 as stated. Injectivity of the representing operator on \(\operatorname{span}\{M_k\}\) is enough to restore the linear-independence proposition.
