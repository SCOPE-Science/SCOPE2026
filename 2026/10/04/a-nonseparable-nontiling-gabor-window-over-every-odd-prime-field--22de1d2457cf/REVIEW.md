# Review
## Correctness
PASS. The proof fixes the Fourier normalization, derives flat spectrum for \(h\), obtains exact orthogonality of its translations and then of the \(p^2\) Gabor atoms, and proves the unique zero of \(h\) using the minimal polynomial of a primitive \(p\)-th root of unity. The nontiling, nonproduct, nonpositive, nonconstant-modulus and nonconstant-Fourier-modulus conclusions each follow from separate explicit arguments. The bundled checker reproduces all finite identities for seven primes but is not used to justify the universal quantifier.

## Originality
PASS. The closest source is Iosevich–Kolountzakis–Lyubarskii–Mayeli–Pakianathan, which ends by asking for simultaneous realization of exactly the six properties proved here. Their explicit examples either use product windows or full support. Zhou's 2021 and 2024 finite-dimensional Gabor papers classify time-frequency support sets in a cyclic \(\mathbb Z_n\) setting; the 2024 paper explicitly distinguishes that setting from the separable \(\mathbb F_p^d\) problem. Frederick–Mayeli treats continuous \(\mathbb R^d\) Riesz systems generated by characteristic functions. Targeted literature and database searches found no source stating this construction or a stronger result implying it.

## Value
PASS. The result gives a uniform explicit affirmative answer, for every prime \(p\ge5\), to a concrete open existence question in the primary finite-field Gabor literature. The mechanism is structural rather than a small census: a one-zero perfect sequence is bent along the nonlinear row shift \(x-y^2\), while a quadratic Gauss sum controls the full Fourier modulus.

## Closest literature and limitations
The central comparison is arXiv:1712.09120, especially its Theorem 1.7, Theorem 1.8, Example 7.1 and final open question. Later cyclic finite-dimensional classifications do not dominate the present finite-field window-support statement. The result is not a classification, does not cover \(p=2,3\), and novelty cannot be made logically absolute from database search alone.
