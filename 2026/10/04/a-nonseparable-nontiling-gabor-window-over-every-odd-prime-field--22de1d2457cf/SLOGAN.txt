For every prime \(p\ge5\), an explicit chirped row-shift of a one-zero perfect sequence gives a Gabor orthonormal basis on \(L^2(\mathbb F_p^2)\) whose window is nonproduct, nonpositive, nontiling, and has genuinely varying time- and frequency-side magnitudes. This supplies all six properties asked for simultaneously in the finite-prime-field Gabor-window question.
