The incommensurate preferred-orientation Kagome spectral probability from Baradaran--Exner's high-energy criterion admits an exact one-dimensional Cauchy integral, with its only root-order transition at \(q=\sqrt{3/5}\). The integral equals \(0.639080422792129545\ldots\), sharpening the previously reported two-dimensional numerical value near \(0.639081\).
