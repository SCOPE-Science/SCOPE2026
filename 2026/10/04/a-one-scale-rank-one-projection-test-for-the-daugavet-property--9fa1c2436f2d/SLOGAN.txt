For real Banach spaces of dimension greater than one, the Daugavet property can be tested using only rank-one projections at one scalar scale: either \(\|I+P\|=1+\|P\|\) for every rank-one projection \(P\), or \(\|I-P\|=1+\|P\|\) for every such \(P\), is already equivalent to the full Daugavet property. The key is that equality at scale \(1\) propagates to all positive multiples, while every nonzero square of a rank-one operator is a positive scalar multiple of a rank-one projection.
