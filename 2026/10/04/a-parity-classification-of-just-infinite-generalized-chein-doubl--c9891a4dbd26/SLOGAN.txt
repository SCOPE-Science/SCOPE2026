For \(G=\langle a\rangle\cong\mathbb Z\), an admissible generalized Chein double is just-infinite exactly in the inversion case \((a^*=a^{-1},g_0=1)\) or in the identity-involution case \(g_0=a^k\) with \(k\) odd. Even \(k\) gives \(\mathbb Z\oplus C_2\), an explicit obstruction to loop-level inheritance.
