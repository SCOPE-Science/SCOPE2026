# Verification

The claim is verified by an exact case split, not by finite sampling.

1. For \(G=\langle a\rangle\cong\mathbb Z\), every involutory anti-automorphism is either \(a\mapsto a\) or \(a\mapsto a^{-1}\).
2. In the inversion case, writing \(g_0=a^k\), the condition \(g_0^*=g_0\) gives \(a^{-k}=a^k\), so \(k=0\). Paiva's Theorem 4.1 then applies to the standard Chein double.
3. In the identity case, the published multiplication gives \(ua=au\) and \(u^2=a^k\), hence the exact presentation \(\langle a,u\mid[a,u]=1,\ u^2=a^k\rangle\).
4. The relation lattice is generated by \((-k,2)\), whose Smith invariant is \(\gcd(2,k)\). Therefore the group is \(\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/\gcd(2,k)\mathbb Z\).
5. Odd \(k\) gives \(\mathbb Z\), while even \(k\) gives \(\mathbb Z\oplus C_2\); the latter has a nontrivial finite normal subgroup of infinite index.

No claim is made for generalized doubles over base groups other than the infinite cyclic group. Independent audit has not been performed.
