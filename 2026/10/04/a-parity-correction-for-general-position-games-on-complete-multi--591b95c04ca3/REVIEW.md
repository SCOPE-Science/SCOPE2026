# Review of A parity correction for general-position games on complete multipartite graphs

## Correctness
PASS. After the first move, B has exactly two strategic branch types: choose the same part, fixing the terminal length to that part size, or choose a different part, fixing the terminal length to the number of parts. The parity analysis gives the two published winner criteria and the corrected joint outcome table. The smallest-counterexample statement follows by enumerating integer partitions of orders four through six subject to all parts having size at least two. Independent recursive minimax verification agrees for all tested types through order ten.

## Originality
PASS. The 2021/2022 achievement source states the achievement parity criterion. The 2022/2024 avoidance source states the avoidance parity criterion and then asserts the incompatible equivalence that this finding corrects. Searches using the claimed equivalence, the counterexamples \(K_{3,3}\) and \(K_{2,2,2}\), and complete-multipartite achievement/avoidance terminology found no prior correction. A later survey restates the two propositions separately but did not expose the correction in the inspected material. Residual risk remains for unindexed or differently phrased corrections.

## Value
PASS. This is a correction to a published conclusion about two graph games, not a new parameter or arbitrary slice. It identifies infinite counterfamilies, gives the exact corrected joint outcome classification, and determines the first counterexamples. The correction is useful because the false equivalence reverses the interpretation of whole parity-homogeneous families.
