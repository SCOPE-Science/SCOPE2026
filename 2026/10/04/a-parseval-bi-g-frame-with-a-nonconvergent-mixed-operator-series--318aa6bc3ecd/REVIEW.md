For the unilateral shift S with Gamma_j=I, Lambda_1=I+S and Lambda_j=S^j-S^{j-1}, the finite mixed sums telescope exactly to I+S^N. The scalar quadratic sums tend to ||f||^2 because <S^Nf,f> tends to zero by an l2 tail estimate, satisfying the printed Parseval condition. The vector sums f+S^Nf cannot converge in norm for nonzero f because S is an isometry and S^Nf converges weakly to zero. Under two g-Bessel assumptions, bounded analysis/synthesis operators give unconditional convergence and the quadratic lower bound makes the resulting bounded self-adjoint operator positive and invertible.
