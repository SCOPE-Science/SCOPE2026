For the Erdős circle-intersection invariant, every convex \(n\)-gon with \(n\ge4\) satisfies \(N(K)\le2n-4\); in particular quadrilaterals have \(N(K)\le4\) and pentagons have \(N(K)\le6\). Triangles admit the exact phase split \(N(T)=4\) for acute triangles and \(N(T)=2\) for right or obtuse triangles.
