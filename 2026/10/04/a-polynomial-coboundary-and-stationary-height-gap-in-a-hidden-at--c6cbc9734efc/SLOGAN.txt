The published stable-equilibrium chaotic flow has an exact polynomial coboundary \(\dot G=a+5y-x^2\), forcing every compact stationary state to obey \(\langle y\rangle=-a/5+\langle x^2\rangle/5\). Every non-equilibrium stationary state lies strictly above the equilibrium in mean \(y\) and satisfies \(\operatorname{Var}(y)>\langle x^2\rangle\).
