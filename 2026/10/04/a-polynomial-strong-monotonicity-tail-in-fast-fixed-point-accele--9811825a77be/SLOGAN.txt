For the source FFP2 recurrence on the condition-number-one quadratic \(f(x)=\lambda x^2/2\), an admissible parameter regime has the exact logarithmic tail \(|z_k|=k^{-\nu/\eta+o(1)}\) and \(|x_k|=k^{-(1+\nu/\eta)+o(1)}\). Thus strong monotonicity alone does not make the unmodified anchored recurrence R-linearly convergent.
