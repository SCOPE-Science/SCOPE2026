If \(3^k p\) with \(k\ge2\) is a perfect totient, the part \(u\) of \(p-1\) coprime to \(6\) must satisfy \(\phi(u)/u>3(p+2)/(4(p-1))>3/4\). Thus its odd prime support cannot contain any set whose Euler product is at most \(3/4\), excluding in particular \(p\equiv1\pmod{35}\), \(p\equiv1\pmod{55}\), and \(p\equiv1\pmod{65}\).
