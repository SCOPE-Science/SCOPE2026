For independently privatized e-values from disjoint blocks, heterogeneous canonical Gaussian budgets collapse to one scalar \(V=\sum_k\Delta_k^2/\mu_k^2\): the released product has sharp privacy \(\mu_{\mathrm{prod}}=\Delta_{\max}/\sqrt V\) and expected log-evidence loss \(V/2\). Therefore \(2L\mu_{\mathrm{prod}}^2=\Delta_{\max}^2\), and local privacy caps plus a product-level cap have the exact optimum \(V^*=\max\{\sum_k\Delta_k^2/b_k^2,\Delta_{\max}^2/\bar\mu^2\}\).\n
