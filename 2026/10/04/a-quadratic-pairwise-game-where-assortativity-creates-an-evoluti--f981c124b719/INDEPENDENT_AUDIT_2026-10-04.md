# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Direct differentiation of the stated invasion fitness gives the selection gradient \(D_r(x)=(-2+3r)(x-1/2)\), convergence slope \(-2+3r\), and mutant-direction curvature \(-1+2r\). At \(x=1/2\) the exact invasion fitness is \((2r-1)(y-1/2)^2/2\). Thus convergence stability holds for \(r<2/3\), ESS curvature for \(r<1/2\), and disruptive curvature for \(1/2<r<2/3\). The degenerate endpoints are correctly excluded from the strict branching interval.

## originality

PASS

Iyer and Killingback use the same assortative pairwise formalism and explicitly conjecture that assortment's inhibition of evolutionary branching may hold more generally, but their proofs concern continuous snowdrift and tragedy-of-the-commons payoff classes. Fresh literature and Resultary searches did not locate an earlier smooth pairwise-game counterexample with a convergence-stable singular strategy changing from ESS to branching as assortativity increases.

## value

PASS

The exact quadratic example identifies mixed payoff curvature as a concrete mechanism by which positive assortativity can create, rather than suppress, local evolutionary branching. It gives a mathematically sharp boundary to a published broad conjectural phenomenon and separates the generic pairwise formalism from additional biological payoff restrictions.

The dated certificate retains the supplied scientific assessment, sources and limitations.
