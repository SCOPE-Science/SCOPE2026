For Lipschitz generative distributional regression on a full-dimensional translation family, every continuous bottleneck \(\mathbb R^p\to\mathbb R^q\) with \(q<p\) has a strictly positive population energy-score regret at bounded encoder--generator Lipschitz product. Quantitatively, the certified floor decays as \((1+2L_eL_d)^{-(p-1)}\), so approximating the abstract measurable one-coordinate reduction forces the Lipschitz product to diverge.
