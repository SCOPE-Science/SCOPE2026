For finite non-singleton characteristic sets, the sharp \(p=2\) discrete maximal-gradient ratio has an explicit deficit below \(\kappa_2=\pi^2/3-3\). If \(m\) is the component count and \(L\) is the gap from the largest to the second-largest support point, the squared ratio loses at least \(1/[mL(L+1)^2(L+2)(2L+1)]\).
