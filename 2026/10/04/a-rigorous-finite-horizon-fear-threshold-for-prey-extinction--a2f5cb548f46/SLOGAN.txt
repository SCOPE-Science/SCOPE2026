For the fear-driven herd-behavior predator-prey model, a computable comparison gives an explicit sufficient threshold \(k>K(T)\) that guarantees prey extinction before a prescribed horizon \(T\). On the motivating paper's Figure 6 data, \(T=1.5\) gives \(K(T)\approx15.37918897486502\), so \(k=16\) is rigorously certified even though the much smaller plotted value \(k=0.03\) is not covered by this conservative bound.
