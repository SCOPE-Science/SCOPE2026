In the published model's single shared exposed class, progression from \(E\) to \(I_j\) or \(C\) is transfer of an already infected individual, not a new infection. The resulting rank-one next-generation matrix gives an additive threshold \(\mathcal R_*=A+\sum_jB_j\); with two strains one can have \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{print}}<1<\mathcal R_*\), so the printed local-stability criterion can reverse the disease-free verdict.
