For the symmetric hyperbolic-sine jerk flow with \(b,c,d>0\), nontrivial compact recurrence exists exactly when \(a>d\). The exact coboundary \(\dot P=c y^2+(d-a)x^2+d\,x(\sinh x-x)\) forces every bounded trajectory to converge to the origin on the entire closed side \(a\le d\), while the opposite side contains a symmetry-related pair of nonzero equilibria.
