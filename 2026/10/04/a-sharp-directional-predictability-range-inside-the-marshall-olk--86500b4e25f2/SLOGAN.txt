Inside the Marshall–Olkin family, fixing Kendall\'s tau also fixes Spearman\'s rho, but it does not fix directional predictability: Chatterjee\'s \(\xi\) ranges sharply from \(16t^2/(t+3)^2\) up to the unattained supremum \(2t/(3-t)\). The two directional \(\xi\) values reveal which Marshall–Olkin parameter is larger, so they recover asymmetry that concordance measures erase.
