On a scalar quadratic, the \(\kappa=4\) Polar-Express surrogate has the exact fixed-step frontier \(\gamma a=2\varepsilon\): below it the minimizer is globally attracting, at equality the gradient decays as \(k^{-1/4}\), and above it a stable nonzero two-cycle is born. The matched value \(\gamma a=\varepsilon\) cancels the linear term and gives local fifth-order convergence.
