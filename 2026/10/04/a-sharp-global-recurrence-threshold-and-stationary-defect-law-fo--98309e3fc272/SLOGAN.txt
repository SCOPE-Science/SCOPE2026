For \(0<a<1\), the Qi flow has an exact recurrence boundary at \(c_*=2\sqrt a-a\): below it every trajectory converges to the origin, while at it every compact stationary state is a mixture of three equilibria. Across all compact stationary states, \(\mathbb E[(\sqrt a\,x-y)^2]=b(c-c_*)\mathbb E[z]\), turning the equilibrium discriminant into a sharp global stationary defect law.
