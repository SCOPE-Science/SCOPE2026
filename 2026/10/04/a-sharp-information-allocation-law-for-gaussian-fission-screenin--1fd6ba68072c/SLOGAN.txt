For Gaussian fission followed by a positive sign screen and an independent one-sided z confirmation, equal information splitting is never power-optimal when \(0<\alpha<1/2\): the unique optimum always reserves more Fisher information for inference. In the weak-signal limit, \(\tau_*\to e^{-z_{1-\alpha}^2/2}/(2\alpha)\); at \(\alpha=0.05\), only about \(13.0\%\) of information should go to screening under this objective.
