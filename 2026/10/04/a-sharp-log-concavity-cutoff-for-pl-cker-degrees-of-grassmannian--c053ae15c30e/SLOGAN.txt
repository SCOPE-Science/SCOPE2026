For fixed \(n\), the Plücker degrees \(D_{n,k}\) of \(\operatorname{Gr}(k,\mathbb C^n)\) always rise strictly toward the center, but log-concavity has a sharp cutoff: it holds exactly for \(n\le11\). From \(n=12\) onward it already fails at \(k=2\), and an explicit increasing edge ratio proves the failure persists forever.
