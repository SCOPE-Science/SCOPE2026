On a scalar quadratic, OS-FBS has an exact online-learning threshold: for \(0<\eta a(1-\gamma a)<2\) the primal error decays like \(\exp(-\Theta(k^2))\), with two-step termination at the midpoint, while at and above \(2\) the projected preconditioner falls into a boundary two-cycle and the primal rate reverts exactly to the base linear contraction. The null-step safeguard accepts every probe throughout, so monotonicity alone does not detect the loss of acceleration.
