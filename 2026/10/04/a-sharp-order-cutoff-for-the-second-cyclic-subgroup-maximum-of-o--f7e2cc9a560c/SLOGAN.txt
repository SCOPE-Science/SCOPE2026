For odd \(p\), the regular \(p\)-groups have the exact second cyclic-subgroup maximum \(M_{p,n}=(2p^{n-1}-p^{n-2}+p-2)/(p-1)\); hence this is the true second maximum for every order \(p^n\) with \(3\le n\le p\). The cutoff is sharp because \(C_p\wr C_p\) at order \(p^{p+1}\) lies in the exceptional \(\Omega_1(G)=G\) branch and exceeds that bound by \(p^{p-2}(p-1)\) cyclic subgroups.
