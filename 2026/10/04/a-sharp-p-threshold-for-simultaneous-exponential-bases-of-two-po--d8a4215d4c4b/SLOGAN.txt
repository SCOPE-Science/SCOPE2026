For two-point subsets of a finite abelian group, simultaneous exponential bases are governed exactly by whether the associated annihilator subgroups cover the dual group. If \(p\) is the least prime divisor of \(|G|\), any \(p\) such subsets always share a basis, while \(p+1\) can already fail in \(\mathbb Z_p^2\); in particular every pair of two-point subsets shares a basis.
