In proportionate-mixing household epidemics, \(R_I\) lies sharply between \(R_*\) and \(\sqrt{R_*}\). For growing epidemics, an \(R_*\)-ranking can reverse under \(R_I\) exactly below the sharp quadratic frontier \(R_{*,A}<R_{*,B}^2\), and a finite four-person-household example realizes the reversal \(3>2\) versus \(R_{I,A}<2=R_{I,B}\).
