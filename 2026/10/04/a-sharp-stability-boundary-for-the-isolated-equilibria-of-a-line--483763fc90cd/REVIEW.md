# Review

## Correctness
PASS. Solving the equilibrium equations gives the two isolated points for every \(a,b,c>0\). Direct substitution into the Jacobian yields the cubic stated in the finding. The cubic Routh determinant is exactly \(bc^2(b-a)/a^2\), so the sign pattern proves the complete strict classification for \(b\ne a\); the boundary factorization is exact. At \((5,2,34)\), exact arithmetic reconstructs a nonzero constant coefficient and therefore excludes the zero eigenvalue reported for the isolated equilibria.

## Originality
PASS. The closest primary source states the modified vector field and the isolated equilibria but supplies only a numerical spectrum at one parameter point; its symbolic characteristic polynomial is for the equilibrium line, not the isolated equilibria. The cited Wang precursor has a different first equation and a different isolated-equilibrium cubic. The later rebuttal addresses a Shilnikov argument for that precursor rather than this modified flow. Exact-equation, exact-polynomial, DOI/title, alias, and implication searches found no source giving the sharp \(b=a\) boundary or the unstable-dimension-two classification for the modified flow.

## Value
PASS. This is a parameter-global local classification of the nontrivial equilibria of the motivating flow, not a single numerical correction. It identifies the exact stability boundary, the unstable dimension on the published side of that boundary, and the precise imaginary-pair spectrum at equality. Those facts are directly relevant to interpreting parameter-dependent dynamics in a paper whose stated isolated-equilibrium spectrum contains a zero eigenvalue.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest literature is the 2020 introducing article itself and the 2009 precursor it cites. The former does not contain the claimed isolated-equilibrium polynomial or boundary; the latter concerns a different vector field. The 2012 rebuttal of the precursor is relevant context but not covering work. The result is local to the integer-order flow; it makes no claim about fractional-order stability, basin size, global chaos, or nonlinear bifurcation at \(b=a\). Unindexed equivalent work remains a residual literature risk.
