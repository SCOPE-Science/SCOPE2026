For the integer-order Chen--Lei--Lu--Dai--Qiu--Zhong flow, the two isolated equilibria are asymptotically stable exactly when \(b>a\), have unstable dimension two when \(0<b<a\), and reach a simple imaginary pair at \(b=a\). At the published parameters \((a,b,c)=(5,2,34)\), their exact spectrum has no zero eigenvalue.
