For Condat's simplex-projection algorithm, the cleanup loop has the sharp support-conditioned maximum \(T_{N,K}=[N(N+1)-(K-1)K]/2\) when \(K\ge2\), while \(K=1\) forces the loop to start from a singleton and costs exactly one cleanup examination. Thus the algorithm's quadratic worst case is already attainable at support \(K=2\), but disappears completely at support \(K=1\).
