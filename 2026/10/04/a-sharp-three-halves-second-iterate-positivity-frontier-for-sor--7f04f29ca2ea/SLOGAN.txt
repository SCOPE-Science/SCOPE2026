For forward SOR started from zero on every \(2\times2\) positive definite Stieltjes system, the second iterate stays nonnegative for all nonnegative right-hand sides exactly up to the robust overrelaxation frontier \(\omega=3/2\). Above \(3/2\), arbitrarily weak coupling already permits a convergent SOR trajectory with positive first iterate and positive exact solution to acquire a negative coordinate on the second iterate.
