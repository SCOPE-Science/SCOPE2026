For three-knot natural cubic interpolation with adjacent mesh ratio \(r\), data in \([0,M]\) can undershoot by exactly
\[
M\,\frac{\max\{r^{-1},r^2\}}{3\sqrt3(1+r)}.
\]
Equal spacing uniquely minimizes this defect at \(M/(6\sqrt3)\), while sufficiently skew meshes make the negative excursion arbitrarily large even though all three data values stay bounded and nonnegative.
