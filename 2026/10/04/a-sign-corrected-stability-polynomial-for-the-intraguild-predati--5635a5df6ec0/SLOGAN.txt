The intraguild-predation supplement reverses all three non-leading signs of the Jacobian characteristic polynomial. An exact positive coexistence equilibrium is Hurwitz stable under the true cubic but is rejected by the paper's stated test; in the paper's notation the corrected conditions are \(\sigma_2<0\), \(\sigma_1<0\), \(\sigma_0<0\), and \(\sigma_1\sigma_2+\sigma_0>0\).
