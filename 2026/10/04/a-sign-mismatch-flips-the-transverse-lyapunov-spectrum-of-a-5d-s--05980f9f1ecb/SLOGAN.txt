For the 5D Sprott-C extension as printed with \(\dot s=w-ds\) and \(d>0\), the added two-dimensional fiber has two strictly negative Lyapunov exponents, so compact recurrent dynamics can have at most one positive exponent and cannot be hyperchaotic. The source's equilibrium signs, divergence, characteristic polynomial, and reported leading exponents instead match the different plus-sign equation \(\dot s=w+ds\), which the article later writes explicitly.
