The claim has a symbolic all-order proof. ## Value — PASS
The result gives a sharp local-perturbation boundary for the canonical odd cyclic tournament: a single adjacent outcome reversal changes the exceptional circle-type directed flag complex into a contractible complex, and the mechanism is exactly one filled triangle. This is a structural statement for every odd order and is naturally motivated by the finite regular-tournament classifications and by robustness questions for tournament-derived topology.

## Closest literature and limitations

The theorem does not cover arbitrary edge reversals or multiple reversals. The finite verification through 17 is supplemental, not the infinite proof. Full access to one plausible sectionable-tournament source was unavailable, so a bounded originality risk remains.
