In Calussi--Carlini--Fatabbi--Lorenzini Example 4.1, the degree-five zero-dimensional singular scheme is supported at one point, not five: the quartic Hadamard surface has two smooth branches meeting transversely there. Its completed germ is \(\mathbb C[[u,v,s,t]]/(us,ut,vs,vt)\), whose Jacobian singular scheme is \((u,v,s,t)^2\) of length \(5\).
