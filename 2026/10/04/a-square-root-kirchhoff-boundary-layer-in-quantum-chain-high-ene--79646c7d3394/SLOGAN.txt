In the Exner--Pekař chain graph at \(\gamma=0\), nonresonant high-energy bands have a critical Kirchhoff boundary layer \(t_m\asymp m^{-1/2}\). Along fixed-phase subsequences with \(\sqrt m\,t_m\to\tau\), the translated band has an explicit finite interval limit and width \(\frac{96}{\pi^3\ell^2\tau^2}\left|\frac{\sin a+\sin b}{\sin a\sin b}\right|\).
