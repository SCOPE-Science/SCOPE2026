On the event that a new bivariate observation kills every current Pareto record, the previous record count is exactly a uniform-permutation cycle count: \(C_{n,k}=\left[{n-1\atop k}\right]/(n^2(n-1)!)\). This identity yields \(\Pr\{K_n=k\}-A_{n,k}\sim n^{-3}(\log n)^{k-1}/(4(k-1)!)\) for every fixed \(k\ge1\), proving the fixed-\(k\) error-rate conjecture in Fill\'s Remark 3.11(b).\n
