# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reduces the claim to two standard sharp triangle facts and one elementary lattice fact. Gerretsen gives \(s^2\le4R^2+4Rr+3r^2\), Euler gives \(r/R\le1/2\), and direct differentiation shows the resulting coefficient of \(r\) is maximized at \(r/R=1/2\). The unit square covering radius gives \(r\le\sqrt2/2\) for every lattice-free triangle. All quantifiers and the nondegeneracy assumption are explicit.

## Originality
PASS with residual historical-literature risk. The inspected González Merino–Henze full text states the conjecture \(p(K)-4R(K)\le2\), proves the \(p-2D\) conjecture for triangles, and does not state the perimeter–circumradius triangle conclusion. Targeted exact-functional, alias, constant, and lattice-free-triangle searches found no stronger coverage. Older Awyong–Scott work supplies component lattice inequalities rather than this perimeter–circumradius conclusion. A synonymous older formulation remains possible.

## Value
PASS. Triangles are a natural and explicitly treated subclass in the source paper. Establishing the missing perimeter–circumradius conjecture there, with the stronger numerical bound \(3\sqrt6-4\sqrt2<2\), closes a motivated gap rather than adding an arbitrary parameter slice. The non-sharp constant is disclosed and leaves a concrete refinement problem.
