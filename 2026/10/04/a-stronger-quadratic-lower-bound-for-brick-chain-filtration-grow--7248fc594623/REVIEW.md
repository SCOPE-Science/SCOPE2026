# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The claim is a direct specialization of the general \(q\)-Kronecker filtration-count inequality followed by an explicit rectangle hook-product asymptotic. The parameter identities, hook-product formula, Stirling expansion, cancellation of \(n^2\log n\), and normalization by the module length \(11n\) were reconstructed independently. The finite verifier checks arithmetic but is not used in place of the infinite proof.

## Originality
PASS. The primary source states the general inequality, then develops a different \(q=3\) square-staircase asymptotic and leaves the exact limsup open. Targeted full-text inspection found no \(q=7\), \(a=2n\), or full-rectangle asymptotic specialization. Searches for the exact constant and equivalent formulations did not find a covering statement. The main residual risk is incomplete indexing of very recent work.

## Value
PASS. The explicit bound \((4+8\log2-7\log3)/121\) improves the published lower endpoint by about \(24.9\%\) for an invariant whose exact asymptotic value is open. The improvement comes from a natural unequal rectangular parameter family and is large enough to be mathematically informative.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest literature is Enomoto's arXiv:2609.10217, which supplies the general counting inequality and the weaker published asymptotic endpoint. Ringel's arXiv:2606.27879 studies a different chain-length invariant. The present result is only a lower bound from one family and does not establish the optimal constant.
