For \(K=\mathbb F_{p^2}\) and any index \(m\ge4\) with \(m\mid p+1\) and \(m<p+1\), every nontrivial character of \(H=(K^\times)^m\) that is trivial on \(\mathbb F_p^\times\) makes the compressed Fourier matrix monomial. It has exactly \(m\) nonzero entries, all of modulus \(p\), so \(m^2-m\) entries vanish even though the matrix is invertible.
