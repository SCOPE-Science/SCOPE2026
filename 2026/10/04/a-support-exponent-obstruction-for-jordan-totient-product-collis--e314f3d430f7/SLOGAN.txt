Any collision of \(n^aJ_s(n)^b\) has a strict weighted imbalance on the primes occurring on only one side: \(\prod r^{(a+sb)e_r-2sb}<1\). Hence in the unresolved range \(a<sb\) a collision must use an exponent-one prime, and \(n^aJ_s(n)^b\) is injective on all powerful integers for every \(a,b,s\ge1\).
