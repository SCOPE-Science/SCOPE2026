\[
c(TX_n)=(1+x)^3(1+y)^{n+1}
\]
and \(H=x+y\). The rank-at-most-two determinantal dual has codimension \(n-1\), so no polar degree beyond index \(4\) survives. The gcd proof is exact from
\[
\delta_0-\delta_4=n+1,\qquad \delta_2=3n^2.
\]
The finite checker is regression evidence only.
