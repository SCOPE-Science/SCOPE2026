For exact steepest descent on a quadratic, the familiar balanced two-eigenvalue two-step Euclidean factor \(((\kappa-1)/(\kappa+1))^2\) stops being locally robust once \(\kappa\) exceeds the sharp threshold \(\kappa_0\approx13.1626503757\). Above that threshold, an arbitrarily small third spectral mode can make the two-step Euclidean contraction strictly worse.
