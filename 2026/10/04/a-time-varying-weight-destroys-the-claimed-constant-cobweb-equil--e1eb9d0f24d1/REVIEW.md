# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The source defines the generalized weighted Caputo derivative using \((\omega p)'\), and its Proposition 1 states that applying the associated integral recovers \(((\omega p)(t)-(\omega p)(0))/\omega(t)\). For the source's candidate \(p^*=-\delta/\lambda\), the model equation requires the fractional derivative to vanish. Proposition 1 then forces \(p^*(\omega(t)-\omega(0))/\omega(t)=0\). For nonzero \(p^*\), a constant equilibrium therefore requires a constant weight. The explicit witness with \(p^*=5\) and \(\omega(t)=1+t\) gives the exact contradiction \(0=5/2\) at \(t=1\).

## Originality
PASS. Statement-level searches of the motivating article, its DOI, the constant-equilibrium claim, weighted Caputo constants, and time-varying weight formulations found no erratum or published source-specific correction. The closest generalized weighted fractional-calculus literature supplies operator background rather than this cobweb-model implication. The current ledger's earlier findings concern different models and mechanisms and do not imply this result.

## Value
PASS. The time-varying weight is one of the motivating model's advertised innovations, so the equilibrium and local-stability theorem failing precisely outside the constant-weight subclass changes the interpretation of that advertised extension. The correction is structural rather than a cosmetic proof repair: the perturbation equation acquires a forcing term and the claimed common constant equilibrium is absent for nonconstant weights.

## Closest literature and limitations
The primary source itself contains all ingredients needed for the correction: Definition 4, Proposition 1, Theorem 5, and numerical examples with varying weights. Earlier work on generalized weighted fractional operators explains the calculus but does not make the disputed cobweb equilibrium claim. The present result does not solve the corrected nonautonomous stability problem and does not affect the paper's constant-weight ABC reduction.
