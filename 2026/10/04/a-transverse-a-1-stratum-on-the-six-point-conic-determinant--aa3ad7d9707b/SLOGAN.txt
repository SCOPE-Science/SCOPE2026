For the six-point conic determinant, a generic configuration with exactly five distinct collinear points and the sixth point off the line is not merely singular: its singular locus is smooth of dimension \(9\) there and the transverse analytic germ is \(A_1\). An exact witness has multi-Veronese rank \(4\) and Hessian rank \(3\), with Hessian kernel equal to the nine-dimensional tangent space of the five-collinear stratum.
