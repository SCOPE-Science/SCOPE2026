The isosceles trapezoid \(K=\operatorname{conv}\{(-1,-1),(1,-1),(1/2,1),(-1/2,1)\}\) has different finite-\(p\) Minkowski critical points: \(t_2\in(-0.103,-0.102)\) while \(t_3\in(-0.119,-0.118)\) on its symmetry axis. Thus the finite-\(p\) critical points need not coincide, giving a negative answer to Lai--Jin Problem 5.4.
