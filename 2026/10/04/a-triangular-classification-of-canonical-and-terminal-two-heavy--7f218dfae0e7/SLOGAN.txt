For \(X=\mathbb P(1^r,a,b)\) with \(r\ge2\) and \(d=b-a\), canonicity is exactly \(d\le r\) and \(a\le r+d\), while terminality is obtained by making both inequalities strict. The canonical and terminal parameter sets therefore contain exactly \(3r(r+1)/2\) and \(3r(r-1)/2\) weight pairs, respectively.
