For \(f_a(x,y)=x^4+a(y-x^2)^2\), choosing the short GD step \(1/(2a)\) makes two short steps plus one Polyak step locally Q-linear for every \(a>0\), with exact epoch distance factor \(3/4\) and objective factor \(81/256\). With only one short step, the ravine-scaled normal multiplier is exactly \(8a/9\), giving the sharp normal-stability boundary \(a=9/8\).
