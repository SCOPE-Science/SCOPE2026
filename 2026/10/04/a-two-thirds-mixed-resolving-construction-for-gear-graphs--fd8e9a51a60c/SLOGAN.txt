The published gear-graph formula \(\operatorname{mdim}(G_n)=n\) fails for every \(n\ge4\): an explicit inserted-vertex construction gives \(\operatorname{mdim}(G_n)\le\lceil2n/3\rceil\). Among inserted vertices, mixed resolving sets are exactly those whose omitted indices have cyclic pairwise distance at least \(3\).
