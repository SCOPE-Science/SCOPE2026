For the two-line-plus-one-point family \(E_p\subset\mathbb F_p^2\) from the quantitative-spectrality open problem, exponential basis partners are exactly spanning connected unicyclic bipartite graphs whose two alternating cycle matchings have different frequency-label multisets. For every such partner, the Fourier determinant is exactly \(p^p\) times the magnitude of one alternating cyclotomic cycle sum.
