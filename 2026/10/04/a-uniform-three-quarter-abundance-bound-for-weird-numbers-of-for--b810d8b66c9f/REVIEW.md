# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reconstructs the published exact criterion, converts it to a forbidden interval for \(up+vq\), and uses a distinct-residue count to force \((p-M)(q-M)>M^2/4\). The complementary case \(q>a\) is handled independently and more strongly. Boundary conditions and quantifiers were checked explicitly. The exact finite replay through \(k=8\) reproduces all published counts and is treated only as corroboration.

## Originality
PASS. The closest full text proves \(M<a<M(M+1)\) and labels \(a<(M+1)(M+2)/3\) as a conjecture. Focused searches for the three-quarter bound, its equivalent product inequality, and the residue formulation found no match. Melfi's 2015 theorem is a sufficient construction, while the 2016 extension develops constructions with more factors; neither statement implies this universal necessary inequality.

## Value
PASS. This is a uniform, structural improvement to the proved side of an explicit open abundance bound for a standard primitive-weird-number family. It improves the leading quadratic coefficient from \(1\) to \(3/4\), applies to every member of the family, and introduces a residue-packing obstruction that can plausibly be sharpened further.

## Closest literature and limitations
The nearest source is D. E. Iannucci, arXiv:1504.02761v1. The theorem does not prove the conjectured coefficient asymptotic to \(1/3\), does not claim optimality of \(3/4\), and does not address other factorization types. A differently phrased or poorly indexed related bound remains a residual originality risk.
