For every weird \(n=2^k p q\), with \(M=2^{k+1}-1\) and abundance \(a=\sigma(n)-2n\), one has \(a<3M^2/4+M\). A residue-packing consequence of the exact semiperfectness obstruction improves the previously proved universal \(M(M+1)\) bound, while remaining short of the conjectured one-third-scale bound.
