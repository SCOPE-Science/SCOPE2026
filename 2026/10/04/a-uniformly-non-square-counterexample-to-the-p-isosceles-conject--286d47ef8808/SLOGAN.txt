There is a uniformly non-square Banach plane whose \(p\)-isosceles constant at \(p=4\) still attains Baronti--Bertella’s universal upper bound. For the explicit hexagonal norm \(\|(s,t)\|_*=\max\{|s-ct|,|cs+t|,d|s-t|\}\), with \(c=\sqrt{\sqrt{17}-4}\) and \(d=(2+c+c^2)/4\), one has \(H_4=\bigl((71+17\sqrt{17})/64\bigr)^{1/4}\), disproving their all-\(p\) uniform-non-squareness conjecture.
