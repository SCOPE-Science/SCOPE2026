At fixed codimension and fixed sum of defining degrees, the topological Euler characteristic of a smooth complex complete-intersection surface strictly increases under every genuine balancing move, except once: \((2,4)\to(3,3)\) changes \(64\) to \(63\). Consequently balanced multidegrees uniquely maximize and one-heavy multidegrees uniquely minimize \(\chi_{\mathrm{top}}\) and \(b_2\), with that single codimension-two reversal.
