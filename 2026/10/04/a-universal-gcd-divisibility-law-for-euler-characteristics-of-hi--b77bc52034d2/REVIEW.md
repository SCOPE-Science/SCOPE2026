# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. De Cataldo's published product formula is sufficient. Formal logarithmic differentiation gives the exact recurrence \(n a_n=e\sum_{j=1}^n\sigma_1(j)a_{n-j}\), so \(e\mid n a_n\); Euclid's lemma after dividing by \(\gcd(|e|,n)\) proves the theorem. The zero-Euler-characteristic case is handled separately by the product \(F(q)=1\). Independent exact product expansion agrees with the recurrence and divisor in all replayed cases.

## Originality
PASS. The primary Hilbert-scheme source gives the product but not the gcd-divisibility. The closest inspected coloured-partition sources connect the same coefficients to Hilbert schemes or study inequalities/log-concavity, but not this all-length divisor. Exact-phrase, p-adic, recurrence, coloured-partition, and Hilbert-scheme searches found no equivalent statement. A residual possibility of an older arithmetic formulation remains, but no concrete plausible source was left unresolved.

## Value
PASS. The result is a uniform arithmetic constraint on a central family of moduli spaces. It gives immediate prime-adic information from \(\chi_c(S)\) alone and is sharp in the universal sense at \(n=1\); the K3 divisor \(24/\gcd(24,n)\) is a concrete high-value specialization.

## Closest literature and limitations
De Cataldo proves the generating product, while Bringmann--Kane--Pahari--Rolen and Chern--Fu--Tang study other properties of the associated coloured-partition coefficients. The finding does not determine exact valuations, individual cohomology, refined genera, singular surfaces, or higher-dimensional Hilbert schemes.
