Let \(m_{\mathrm{FPP}}(S^1)\) denote the least size of a finite \(T_0\)-space with the fixed point property and weak homotopy type \(S^1\). Combining the small-poset collapse theorem with Barmak's \(14\)-point circle model gives \(10 \le m_{\mathrm{FPP}}(S^1) \le 14\), leaving five candidate cardinalities.
