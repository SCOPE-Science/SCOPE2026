# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The rational iterate is exact, polynomial entropy is invariant under positive iterates, and the scalar-twist upper bound follows from an explicit spanning cover whose size is linear in the time horizon. The nonconstant lower bound is supplied by the published rotation-set theorem after verifying that the rotation set is \(\{0\}\times\psi(\mathbb T)\). The constant and cancellation boundary cases were checked separately.

## Originality
PASS. The closest 2026 source treats the identical rational skew products but retains a Lipschitz assumption for its upper-bound proposition and does not state the continuity-only exact dichotomy. Focused semantic searches found no equivalent result. Correa--Pujals concerns irrational cylindrical cascades. Grycan-Gérard--Marco contains a scalar angle-action proposition that conflicts with the explicit universal scalar spanning bound rather than implying this theorem.

## Value
PASS. The theorem closes a natural classification problem for the rational subfamily discussed in the motivating paper, removes unnecessary regularity, and gives an exact rotation-set/entropy criterion. It also identifies a mathematically consequential regularity artifact in related polynomial-entropy literature.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is H. Corrêa, “Polynomial entropy and rotation sets,” DOI 10.1007/s00013-026-02286-3. Its Section 5 gives the rational iterate and lower bound, while Proposition 5.1 assumes Lipschitz regularity. The result here does not address irrational-base skew products or classify higher-dimensional fiber velocities. The literature comparison is non-exhaustive, and a 2023 fibered-systems proposition states a conflicting scalar value whose broader consequences are not analyzed here.
