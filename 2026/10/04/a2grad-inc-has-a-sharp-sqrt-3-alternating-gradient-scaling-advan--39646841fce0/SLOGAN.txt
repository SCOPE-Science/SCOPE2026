On a persistent alternating gradient, the practical A2Grad-inc quadratic-weight scale is asymptotically smaller by exactly \(1/\sqrt{3}\) than the A2Grad-uni and A2Grad-exp scales. Consequently its adaptive inner-step magnitude is asymptotically larger by exactly \(\sqrt{3}\), with \(\sqrt{k}|\Delta x_k|\to\sqrt{3}/\beta\).
