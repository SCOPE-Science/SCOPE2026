# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof was reconstructed from definitions. The only nontrivial domain input is the Bessaga--Pełczyński selection principle for normalized weakly null sequences in \(\ell_p\), followed by the fact that normalized block basic sequences are isometric to the canonical basis. Since \((\ell_p)^*=\ell_{p^*}\subseteq\ell_r\) exactly for \(r\ge p^*\), the selected subsequence is weakly \(r\)-summable, contradicting weak \(r\)-singularity. This forces a non-weakly-null maximizing sequence and WMP then gives norm attainment. The converse and perturbation/inheritance steps were checked against arXiv:2609.03988v1.

## Originality
PASS. The focal paper proves the one-way implication \(\mathrm{SMP}_r\Rightarrow\mathrm{WMP}\), exact results for \((\ell_p,\ell_q)\), \((\ell_p,c_0)\), and \((\ell_p,L_p[0,1])\), plus the perturbation implications. It does not state that for arbitrary \(Y\), WMP is already sufficient for \(\mathrm{SMP}_r\) whenever \(r\ge p^*\), nor the resulting exact perturbation threshold under isometric \(\ell_p\)-containment. Semantic searches using statement, threshold, alias, and perturbation formulations found no covering published finding. The closest results concerned different compact-perturbation notions or unrelated \(\ell_p\) thresholds.

Residual risk: the positive implication is concise and abstracts a mechanism visible in two proofs of the focal preprint, so an equivalent observation could be unindexed or appear in a later revision.

## Value
PASS. The theorem identifies a universal transfer principle depending only on the domain \(\ell_p\), not on the codomain: above \(p^*\), the new weakly singular maximizing hierarchy carries no extra information beyond WMP. With an isometric \(\ell_p\) inside the codomain, it also produces a sharp, simultaneous phase boundary for maximizing and perturbation properties. This is structurally stronger than another target-by-target computation.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is Blanco Ardila--Miranda, arXiv:2609.03988v1. Its Corollary 2.3 and Theorem 2.4 contain the same block-basis mechanism for two named codomains, while Proposition 2.1, Theorem 2.2, Proposition 4.1, and Examples 4.2 supply the reverse implication, inheritance, perturbation transfer, and sharp negative model. The new theorem is limited to finite \(r\), and the exact perturbation threshold requires WMP plus isometric \(\ell_p\)-containment.
