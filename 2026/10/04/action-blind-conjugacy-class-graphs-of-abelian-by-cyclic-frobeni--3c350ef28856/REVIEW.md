# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. Fixed-point-freeness gives a trivial center, free complement orbits on nonidentity kernel elements, and one conjugacy class for each nontrivial complement coset. The abelian kernel and cyclic complement therefore give two clique blocks. A mixed pair cannot commute; if it generated a nilpotent subgroup, its coprime Hall factors would commute, contradicting fixed-point-freeness. Metabelianity makes the solvable graph complete. The scalar and split \(C_3\)-actions are distinguished by the number of invariant projective lines in the characteristic derived subgroup. The included verifier reconstructs the groups and graphs for \(p=7\) and \(p=13\).

## Originality
PASS. The 2024 primary survey poses determination questions but does not give this formula. The 2009 solvable-group theorem places Frobenius groups in the disconnected commuting-graph regime, but it does not state the exact two-clique decomposition, equality with the nilpotent graph, or action blindness. Later forbidden-subgraph work covers several overlapping families but does not state this arbitrary abelian-kernel cyclic-complement theorem. Targeted database searches found no equivalent result. The inaccessible full text of a 2017 nilpotent-conjugacy-class article remains a stated bibliographic risk.

## Value
PASS. The result completely determines three conjugacy-class graphs on a natural Frobenius family and turns the formula into uniform nonisomorphic collisions. It therefore gives a concrete structural limitation on graph-based group recognition, directly aligned with the determination problem highlighted in the primary survey.
