For every finite Frobenius group \(K\rtimes C_m\) with abelian kernel, the commuting and nilpotent conjugacy class graphs are exactly \(K_{(|K|-1)/m}\sqcup K_{m-1}\), while the solvable graph is complete. Consequently the three graphs are blind to the fixed-point-free action, and explicit nonisomorphic \(\mathbf F_p^2\rtimes C_3\) actions have identical triples of conjugacy-class graphs.
