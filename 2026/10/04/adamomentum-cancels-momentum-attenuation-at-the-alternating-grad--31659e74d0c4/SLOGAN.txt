On an alternating gradient, AdaMomentum's momentum-squared denominator can exactly cancel the attenuation created by its first-moment EMA. In the signal-dominated limit the restoration factor is \((1+\beta_1)/(1-\beta_1)\), equal to \(19\) at \(\beta_1=0.9\); with zero damping the normalized alternating update has magnitude exactly \(\alpha\) for every \(\beta_1<1\).
