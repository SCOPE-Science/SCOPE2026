Under persistent alternating gradients, Adai's bias-corrected squared-gradient state becomes exact immediately, erasing \(\beta_2\) and freezing a parameterwise inertia determined only by relative coordinate energy. In the unclipped regime, the asymptotic update amplitude is \(\eta\sqrt{\overline V}\,\beta_0\sqrt r/(2r-\beta_0)\), so larger alternating-gradient coordinates receive smaller updates; at the source defaults, \(G=(2,1)\) yields amplitudes \(2\eta/31\) and \(\eta/7\).
