For distinct prime cyclic groups in Gomaa's relational language, the least distinguishing rank is at most the shortest relevant addition-chain length \(\Lambda(p)\), hence at most \(n+O(n/\log n)\) when \(2^n<p<2^{n+1}\). This reduces the archived factor-two gap to a sublinear additive gap over the known \(n+1\) lower bound.
