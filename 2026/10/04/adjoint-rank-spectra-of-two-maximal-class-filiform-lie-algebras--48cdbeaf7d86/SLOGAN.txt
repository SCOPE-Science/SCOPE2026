For the two explicit maximal-class filiform families \(L_n(q)\) and \(M_n(q)\), \(n\ge6\), every adjoint rank occurs in a completely determined finite list with closed multiplicities. Their element commutativity degrees are respectively \(q^{-2}+(q^2-1)q^{-n}\) and \(q^{-4}+2(q^2-1)q^{-n}\), so the invariant separates the families in every dimension in the stated range.
