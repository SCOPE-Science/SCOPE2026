The Adelakun–Odusote four-dimensional memristive Sprott-A flow has the exact first integral \(I=w-x/a\), so its motion is confined to three-dimensional affine leaves. Every compact ergodic invariant measure has at least two neutral Lyapunov exponents; with negative Lyapunov sum it can have at most one positive exponent, excluding dissipative hyperchaos.
