A connected threshold graph is identifiable exactly when every \(1\)-block is a singleton and the first \(0\)-block has size at least two. In that case every identifying code is controlled by one omitted vertex from selected \(0\)-blocks plus a disjoint interval-hitting condition on the \(1\)-vertices, yielding the full code polynomial and \(\gamma_{\mathrm{ID}}(G)=\sum_i a_i\).
