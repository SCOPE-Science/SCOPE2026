For a complete multipartite graph, an initial set is \(k\)-forcing exactly when one initially black part can clear all white vertices outside it in one force and at most \(k\) whites then remain inside that part. This yields \(F_k(G)=N-\max_i(\min\{k,n_i-1\}+\min\{k,N-n_i\})\) and an exact inclusion-exclusion count of all minimum sets.
