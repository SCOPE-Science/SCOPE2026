For a complete multipartite graph, every vertex subset of size at most \(k\) is a \(k\)-limited packing, while a larger set of size \(s\) is feasible exactly when every part contributes at least \(s-k+1\) vertices. This yields an exact all-set enumerator and the closed formula \(L_k(G)=\min\{N,\max\{k,\min\{k+m-1,\lfloor r(k-1)/(r-1)\rfloor\}\}\}\).
