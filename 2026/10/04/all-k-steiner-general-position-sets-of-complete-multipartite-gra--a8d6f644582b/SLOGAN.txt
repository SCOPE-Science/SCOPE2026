A vertex set in a complete multipartite graph is \(k\)-Steiner general position exactly when it lies in one part or uses at most \(k-1\) vertices from every part. This yields an exact size enumerator and \(\operatorname{sgp}_k(K_{n_1,\ldots,n_r})=\max\{\max_i n_i,\sum_i\min(n_i,k-1)\}\), together with the exact number of maximum sets.
