For a connected threshold graph, every \(k\)-tuple total dominating set is determined by just two inequalities: it contains at least \(k\) vertices from the final \(1\)-block and at least \(k+1\) vertices in total. Hence all \(k\)-tuple total-domination polynomials collapse to functions of only the order \(N\) and final-block size \(b\), and every inclusion-minimal set has size \(k+1\).
