For every coprime split companion Lucas sequence \(V_n(P,Q)=a^n+b^n\) with positive square discriminant and nonzero terms, every local p-part is Dold. Odd-prime realizability occurs exactly when no term is divisible by \(p\), while the prime \(2\) has the explicit criterion \(Q\) even or \(\nu_2(P)=1\).
