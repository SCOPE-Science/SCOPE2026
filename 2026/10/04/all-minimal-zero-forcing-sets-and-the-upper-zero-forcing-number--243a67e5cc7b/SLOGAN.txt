Every minimal zero forcing set of a spider is assembled arm-by-arm from root singletons, leaf triggers, or adjacent non-leaf pairs, with exactly one omitted arm and one sharp shallow-pair compatibility rule. The classification yields \(\overline Z(T)=k-1+\max(\mathbf 1_{\{q\le1\}},\min(k-1,L))\), where \(q\) counts unit legs and \(L\) counts legs of length at least four.
