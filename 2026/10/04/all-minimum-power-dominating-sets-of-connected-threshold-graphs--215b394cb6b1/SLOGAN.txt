For a connected threshold graph \(0^{a_1}1^{b_1}\cdots0^{a_p}1^{b_p}\), every minimum power dominating set is a singleton, and the eligible vertices are exactly the one-block vertices lying at or after the last zero-block of size at least two, together with \(A_1\) precisely when \(a_1\le2\) and all later zero-blocks are singletons. This gives an exact count of all optimal one-PMU locations.\n
