For \(N=p^2\) with \(p\) an odd prime, a three-by-three Fourier minor is singular exactly when one index set is concentrated in one residue class modulo \(p\) and the other is not spread across three distinct classes. The residue patterns determine the rank exactly and yield \(p^4(p-1)^2(p-2)(6p^2-5p-2)/36\) singular minors in closed form.
