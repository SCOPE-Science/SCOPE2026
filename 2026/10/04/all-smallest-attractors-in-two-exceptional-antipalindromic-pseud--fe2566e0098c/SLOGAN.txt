For \(n\ge3\), every smallest attractor can be listed explicitly in the two antipalindromic pseudostandard boundary families: \((01)^n\) has \(n^2-1\) of them, while \(0(1100)^{n-1}1\) has \(2n(n-2)\). The classification is by simple residue conditions on the two attractor positions, with one excluded endpoint pair in the first family and two excluded boundary pairs in the second.
