For a complete multipartite graph with every part of size at least two, all strong edge geodetic sets are obtained by omitting vertices from at most one part, with the exact omission bound governed by \(\rho(s)=s-1\) for even part size and \(\rho(s)=s-2\) for odd part size. This yields a closed size enumerator and an exact count of all minimum strong edge geodetic sets.
