For every connected complete multipartite graph, a super dominating set can omit at most two vertices; omitting two is possible exactly when the omitted vertices lie in distinct parts that each still contain a selected vertex. Hence the entire cardinality enumerator collapses to \(\mathcal S_G(x)=x^N+N x^{N-1}+E_2x^{N-2}\), where \(E_2\) counts cross-part pairs drawn from non-singleton parts.
