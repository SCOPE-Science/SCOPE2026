# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof starts from the exact semidirect-product quotient lemma in the recent source and the explicit meridional action in the classical Kanenobu--Kazama presentation. Iterating that action gives period six. The six coinvariant quotients are computed directly, and the only nontrivial non-Heisenberg kernel is \(C_3\), occurring in residues \(2\) and \(4\) precisely when \(3\mid k\). The descended action is inversion. A separate finite group checker confirms the residue table for representative odd moduli.

## Originality
PASS. The recent source treats the clean odd-\(k\) family at exponents divisible by six and explicitly warns that other exponents can shrink the kernel; it does not calculate the needed precise automorphism there. The older paper provides the action but predates the new quotient construction. Targeted searches found no all-exponent modulo-six classification or intermediate \(C_3\rtimes C_n\) family.

## Value
PASS. This closes the exponent restriction in a newly introduced infinite family of knotted-torus exterior replacements. It gives an exact arithmetic criterion for survival of the nonabelian kernel and exhibits a sharp transition from a full order-\(k^3\) Heisenberg kernel to a \(C_3\) remnant or no kernel at all.
