# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. Writing an almost-abelian Lie algebra as \(L=\mathbf F_q t\ltimes_A V\) gives
\[
[\alpha t+u,\beta t+v]=\alpha A(v)-\beta A(u).
\]
If \(\alpha\ne0\), the adjoint image is exactly \(A(V)\), of dimension \(r\). If \(\alpha=0\), the adjoint image is zero when \(u\in\ker A\) and otherwise is the one-dimensional span of \(A(u)\). These cases are exhaustive and give the stated multiplicities. The centralizer and commutativity-degree formulas follow by rank-nullity and the standard adjoint-image average. The packaged checker reconstructs the brackets and adjoint ranks independently over four finite fields.

## Originality

PASS. The 2024 primary paper introduces the invariant and its adjoint-image method but does not state an almost-abelian theorem in the inspected full text. Avetisyan's structural paper classifies almost-abelian Lie algebras but does not study finite-field commuting probability. A 2026 follow-up gives several low-dimensional possible-value results, including special cases compatible with the formula, but contains no arbitrary-rank almost-abelian classification. Targeted structural and exact-formula searches found no covering theorem. A residual risk remains from older bilinear-map or solvable-Lie-algebra literature using different terminology.

## Value

PASS. The result gives a complete all-dimensional formula on a standard solvable Lie-algebra family and, more strongly, the full adjoint-rank distribution. It shows that the commutativity degree forgets canonical-form data but recovers \(\dim L^2\) exactly inside the family, providing a natural structural interpretation rather than an isolated calculation.
