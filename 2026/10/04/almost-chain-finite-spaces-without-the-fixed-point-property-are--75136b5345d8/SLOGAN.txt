Among nonempty finite \(T_0\)-spaces in which each point is incomparable with at most one other, the spaces admitting a fixed-point-free self-map are exactly the ordinal sums of two-point antichains, hence exactly the canonical minimal finite sphere models. The fixed-point-free map is unique and becomes the antipodal map of degree \((-1)^k\) on the cross-polytope boundary.
