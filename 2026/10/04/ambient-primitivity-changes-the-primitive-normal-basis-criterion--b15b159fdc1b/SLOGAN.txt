Under Lee's Definition 25 as printed, an ambient-primitive normal basis of \(D(1,b)\) exists exactly when \(D(1,b)=\mathbb F_{q^n}\), not merely when \(D(1,b)=\mathbb F_{p^{l\mu}}\). The Dickson pair \((4,3)\) gives an explicit counterexample with \(D(1,b)=\mathbb F_4\subsetneq\mathbb F_{64}\); changing primitivity to the smaller field restores the intended equivalence.
