On the coordinate boundary \(e=0\) of the octanomial cubic, the fixed point \([1:0:0:0]\) has a complete three-step ADE ladder: \(A_1\) for \(ab-cf\ne0\), \(A_2\) for \(ab-cf=0\) but \(ah+bg-cd\ne0\), and \(A_3\) when both vanish, provided \(abcgh\ne0\). The \(A_3\) endpoint is certified by the nonzero reduced quartic coefficient \(-b^2gh/(a^2c)\).\n
