# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In the affine chart at \(P=[1:0:0:0]\), the gradient is \((e,0,0)\), so the marked point is singular exactly on \(e=0\). There the Hessian determinant is \(2c(ab-cf)\). On \(D=ab-cf=0\), its kernel is generated by \((-c,b,a)\), and direct restriction of the cubic gives \(ab(ah+bg-cd)t^3\). In the adapted coordinates, the transverse Hessian has determinant \(-a^2\); solving its critical graph to second order and substituting gives reduced cubic coefficient proportional to \(T=ah+bg-cd\), and on \(T=0\) the quartic coefficient is \(-b^2gh/(a^2c)\neq0\). The analytic splitting lemma therefore gives exactly the stated \(A_1,A_2,A_3\) trichotomy under \(abcgh\neq0\). An independent symbolic reconstruction reproduced all five critical identities; the finite sample points in the package are corroborative only.
