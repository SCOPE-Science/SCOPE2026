The published trace-determinant lemma for the Ricker predator-prey coexistence equilibrium can label the same admissible fixed point both a source and a saddle. The correct source region is \(|D|>1\) with \(|T|<|D+1|\), and an exact parameter witness inside the paper's own coexistence regime has multipliers \(-2\pm\sqrt{11/10}\), so it is unambiguously a saddle.
