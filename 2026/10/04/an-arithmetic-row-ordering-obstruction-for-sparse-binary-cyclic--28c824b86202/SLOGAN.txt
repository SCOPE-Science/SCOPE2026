For the scalar saturated cyclic permutation test, a binary predictor with exactly two active rows has a power proxy governed entirely by the arithmetic of their cyclic spacing. If \(N=n/\gcd(n,d)\) is even the proxy collapses to zero, while for odd \(N\) it is exactly \(\sqrt{4n/(nN-1)}\); at the minimal \(5\%\) case \(n=20\), only four of nineteen oriented spacings avoid collapse.
