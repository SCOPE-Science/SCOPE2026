# Review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The two normalized angle-bisector equations parameterize every nonzero circle tangent to the two nonparallel limiting lines. Exact symbolic elimination gives a squarefree degree-\(8\) tangency-discriminant polynomial on each branch. Exact Sturm counting gives \(8\) real roots on the plus branch and \(6\) on the minus branch, with \(q=0\) excluded. Since the source independently establishes exactly \(14\) real \(QL^2\) circles for the same configuration, the necessary-condition root counts are saturated and the exact split is \(8+6\).

## Originality
**PASS.** The initiating paper gives only the total \(14\) real \(QL^2\) solutions and identifies the maximal-real-count problem as open. Searches by source identifier, angle-bisector terminology, branch-count formulation, and the explicit \(8+6\) split found no covering statement. The 2009 thesis remains a residual risk because its full text was not independently used to exclude a branch-level observation.

## Value
**PASS.** The unresolved \(QL^2\) constituent is explicitly identified as the bottleneck for the next real-circle target. The refinement shows that one complete degree-\(8\) branch is already totally real and that the entire deficit is one conjugate pair on the other branch, sharply localizing where any \(16\)-real improvement must occur.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is Brysiewicz, arXiv:2609.01521v1, especially Section 2.2 and Table 1. It records the total \(14\) but not the branch split. The result does not settle whether \(16\) real \(QL^2\) solutions are possible and does not independently recertify the source's 14 tangency points.
