The classical Fehlberg RK4(5) pair has an exact scalar-linear error-estimator resonance at \(h\lambda=8/3\): its fourth- and fifth-order outputs coincide at \(1613/117\) even though the exact multiplier is \(e^{8/3}\). Near that point the true-error-to-estimator ratio diverges like \(1/|h\lambda-8/3|\), and a fixed resonant step produces zero estimated error forever while the relative global error tends to \(1\).
