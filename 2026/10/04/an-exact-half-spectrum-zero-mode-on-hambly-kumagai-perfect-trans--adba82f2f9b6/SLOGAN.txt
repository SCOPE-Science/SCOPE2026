For the Krawtchouk perfect-transfer Hamiltonian on every nontrivial Hambly-Kumagai diamond level, exactly one half of all one-excitation modes are pinned at zero energy: \(\dim\ker H_\ell=|V(HK_\ell)|/2=(4^\ell+2)/3\). Consequently the finite-level integrated density of states has the exact mass split \(1/4,1/2,1/4\) across negative, zero, and positive energies.
