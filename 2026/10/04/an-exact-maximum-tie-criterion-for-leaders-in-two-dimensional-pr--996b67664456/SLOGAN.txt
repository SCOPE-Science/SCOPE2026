For a two-dimensional product sample, leader existence is exactly the event that the two marginal sample-maximum index sets intersect. If \(K_{X,n}\) and \(K_{Y,n}\) are their sizes, then \(a_n\to0\) exactly when \(K_{X,n}K_{Y,n}/n\to0\) in probability, and the leader property is equivalent to this normalized product retaining a uniformly positive tail.
