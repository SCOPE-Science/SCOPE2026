For an explicit two-state rare-entrance chain, the normalized sliding-count Rayleigh quotient approaches the \(1/4\) obstruction as \(1/4+2^{-1/2}n^{-1/4}+O(n^{-1/2})\). Within the natural balanced scaling \(\rho_n=1-A n^{-1/4}\), \(\varepsilon_n=B n^{-3/4}\), the coefficient \(2^{-1/2}\) is the unique minimum.
