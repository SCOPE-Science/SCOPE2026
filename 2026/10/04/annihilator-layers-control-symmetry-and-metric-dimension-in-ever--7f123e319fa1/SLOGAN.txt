For every finite commutative chain ring with residue-field size \(q\) and length \(\ell\), the zero-divisor graph is a valuation-layer blow-up whose automorphism group is the product of the layer symmetric groups, and both determining number and metric dimension equal \(q^{\ell-1}-\ell\). Thus \((q,\ell)\) determines the graph even though it need not determine the ring.
