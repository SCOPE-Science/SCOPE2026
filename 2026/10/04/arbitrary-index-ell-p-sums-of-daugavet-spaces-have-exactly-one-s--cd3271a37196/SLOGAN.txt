For every infinite index set \(\Gamma\) and \(1<p<\infty\), the origin is an SCD point of the unit ball of any nontrivial \(\ell_p\)-sum \(\left(\bigoplus_{\gamma\in\Gamma}X_\gamma\right)_p\). If every coordinate space has the Daugavet property, that origin is the unique SCD point, even at arbitrary nonseparable density.
