Equal independent-set blow-ups preserve the strongest possible arc-cycle spectrum of regular tournaments: if \(T\) is regular of order \(c\ge5\), then every arc of \(T[\overline{K}_{\alpha}]\) lies on a directed cycle of every length \(3\le\ell\le c\alpha\). The proof splices lifted Alspach cycles across at most \(\alpha\) clone layers.
