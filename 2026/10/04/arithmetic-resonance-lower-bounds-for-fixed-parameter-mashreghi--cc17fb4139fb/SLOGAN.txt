The canonical two-pole Mashreghi–Ransford sharpness example has an exact arithmetic profile at fixed \(\beta\): its lower-bound ratio is \(1\) off odd rational resonances and \(\sec(\pi/(2q))\) when \(\arccos(1/\beta)/\pi\) has odd reduced denominator \(q\). The universal extremal value \(2/\sqrt3\) is the shortest resonance \(q=3\), corresponding to \(\beta=2\).
