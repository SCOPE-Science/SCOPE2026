# Review of Arithmetic safety for Gaussian-derivative Gabor frames

## Correctness

PASS. After a unitary dilation, the rectangular system with window \(g_m=\phi^{(m)}\) reduces to a semi-regular system generated by a scaled Gaussian derivative. The derivative window has stable integer shifts because its Fourier periodization is continuous and strictly positive.

The semi-regular criterion gives, from any non-frame system, a nonzero Gaussian shift-invariant entire function \(F\) whose \(m\)-th derivative vanishes on a lattice. For \(N>m\) translates of \(F\), differentiating the Wronskian \(s\) times produces determinants with row orders \(k+\alpha_k\). If \(s<N-m\), \(N\) distinct row orders cannot avoid \(m\): the minimum sum of \(N\) distinct nonnegative integers avoiding \(m\) exceeds the baseline by \(N-m\). Hence the Wronskian has multiplicity at least \(N-m\) at every common \(m\)-th derivative zero.

The source's translate-independence and automorphy steps do not use the derivative order. The rescaled Wronskian therefore lies in the same Gaussian shift-invariant class, and the sharp zero-density theorem yields
\[
(N-m)/(N\delta)\le1.
\]
For irrational \(\delta\), arbitrary \(N\) force \(\delta\ge1\). For \(\delta=p/q\), choosing \(N=q\) when \(q>m\) yields \(q-p\le m\); when \(q\le m\), that inequality is automatic. This proves the stated one-sided classification.

## Originality

PASS. The full recent primary source was inspected through its semi-regular reduction, Wronskian-amplification lemma, translation-character independence, automorphy calculation, zero-density theorem, and completion of the first-Hermite proof. It treats common first-derivative zeros and obtains multiplicity \(N-1\); it does not state the higher-order multiplicity \(N-m\) or the resulting denominator-deficit condition for a single Gaussian-derivative window.

The open-access Gröchenig--Romero--Stöckler paper was inspected. Its sharp derivative-sampling theorem samples the full jet up to a prescribed multiplicity, and its Gabor consequence is multi-window, using a basis of polynomial differential operators. It does not imply the single-window conclusion for \(\phi^{(m)}\).

The standard higher-Hermite literature is not covering because, beyond first order, the standard Hermite window and the fixed-scale Gaussian derivative are different polynomial-times-Gaussian functions. Candidate-specific published-finding searches for Gaussian derivatives, rational-density deficits, Wronskian amplification, and irrational lattice safety returned no covering result.

The main residual risk is terminological: an older theorem on derivative-of-Gaussian or Hermitian-wavelet Gabor systems could be indexed under language not surfaced by the searches.

## Value

PASS. The finding extracts a dimension-free arithmetic law from a very recent exact first-Hermite method. It simultaneously recovers the complete subcritical Gaussian safety statement at \(m=0\), the first-Hermite rational obstruction at \(m=1\), and for every higher derivative excludes all irrational failures and all rational failures outside a denominator strip of width \(m\).

This is structurally meaningful rather than a routine parameter substitution: the proof requires a new higher-order Wronskian ramification count. The result sharply narrows where a complete frame-set analysis for each Gaussian derivative must look, especially for the second derivative, where only rational products with \(q-p\le2\) can remain problematic.
