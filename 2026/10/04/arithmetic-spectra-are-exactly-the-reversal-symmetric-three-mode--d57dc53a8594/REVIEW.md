# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reconstructs the exact cycle-boundary weight map from the CSD spectral component recurrence. Two reversal equations imply \(\mu'=1-\mu\); full support then forces the normalized middle spectral location to be \(s=1/2\). The remaining equations yield exactly \(\mu=1/2\pm\sqrt2/4\) and the two stated weight vectors. A direct polynomial calculation gives a common two-cycle multiplier \(Q=h^4/(2m^2-h^2)^2\) on all three eigenspaces. The bundled exact-arithmetic verifier replays the constructive identities and \(Q=1/49\) at \(\kappa=3\).

## Originality
PASS with a recorded access risk. The primary recent CSD paper proves generic doubly exponential convergence outside a null exceptional set but does not state a classification of that set. The closest located prior record, Li--Wang DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22209278, advertises the specific \((1,2,3)\) period-two witness with four-update factor \(1/49\). Targeted searches for arithmetic spectra, reversal symmetry, three-mode projective two-cycles, and condition-number formulas did not locate the iff classification given here. The Li--Wang full manuscript was not accessible for line-by-line inspection, so unadvertised equivalent material remains a residual risk.

## Value
PASS. This is a natural structural boundary question created by the coexistence of an almost-everywhere theorem and exact exceptional witnesses. The result identifies a necessary-and-sufficient spectral geometry condition inside a canonical reversal symmetry class, shows uniqueness of the spectral weights, and turns the isolated \(1/49\) example into an exact condition-number family. The claim is explicitly not promoted to a classification of all exceptional CSD behavior.

## Closest literature and limitations
Ran Gu, arXiv:2609.25800v1, supplies the exact CSD recurrence and generic theorem for \(2j>q\). Yu Li and Qihang Wang, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22209278, supply the closest advertised exceptional three-mode witness. The present statement is narrower than a complete exceptional-set classification and stronger than the accessible special-case witness only in the stated reversal-symmetric class. Full-text access to the close Li--Wang manuscript remains the main originality risk.
