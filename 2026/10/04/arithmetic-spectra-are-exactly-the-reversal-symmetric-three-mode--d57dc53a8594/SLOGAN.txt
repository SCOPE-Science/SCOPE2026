For three active eigenvalues and CSD cycle length \(j=2\), a full-support projective two-cycle that exactly reverses the extreme spectral weights exists if and only if the eigenvalues form an arithmetic progression. Its unique weights are \(p_\pm=(3/8\pm\sqrt2/4,\,1/4,\,3/8\mp\sqrt2/4)\), and its exact four-update contraction is \(Q=(\kappa-1)^4/(\kappa^2+6\kappa+1)^2\).
