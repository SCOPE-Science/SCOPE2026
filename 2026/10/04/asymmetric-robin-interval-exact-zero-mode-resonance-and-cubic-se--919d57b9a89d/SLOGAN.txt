For a one-edge quantum graph with unequal nonzero real Robin parameters, the zero mode occurs exactly on \(\ell=\lambda_0^{-1}+\lambda_\ell^{-1}\). Precisely on that resonance surface the secular zero jumps from order \(1\) to order \(3\), while the scattering multiplicity remains \(1\) and \(g_0-N/2=-1/2\) stays fixed.
