# Review of Automorphism groups of finite Boolean trusses

## Correctness

**PASS.** The proof reconstructs the full automorphism group from the published intrinsic decomposition. The critical point is that the commuting, left, and right leaves are definable from multiplication, so an automorphism cannot mix coordinates. The commutative coordinate fixes the unique multiplicative absorber and therefore becomes a Boolean-ring automorphism; each heap coordinate remains an arbitrary affine automorphism. The converse maps preserve both operations. Small exhaustive enumerations agree with the formula.

## Originality

**PASS.** The closest paper, arXiv:2609.31051v1, gives the Boolean-truss decomposition and isomorphism classification but no automorphism-group theorem; its inspected full text has no occurrence of “automorph.” The earlier arXiv:2101.09484 discusses truss isomorphisms and translations but does not state this finite symmetry formula. Targeted searches for equivalent formulations using Boolean trusses, rectangular bands, heaps, and holomorphs did not locate a covering result.

The main residual risk is terminology: an older semigroup/torsor result could encode the same conclusion without Boolean-truss language.

## Value

**PASS.** The automorphism group is a natural invariant following an isomorphism classification. The theorem gives exact symmetry groups and orders uniformly for all finite Boolean trusses and makes orbit-stabilizer and labeled-counting questions explicit.
