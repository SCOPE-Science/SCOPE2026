# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For \(d\ge3\), reducing modulo \(\mathfrak m^2\) and using \(x_ix_j=0\) shows the invertible linear part has one nonzero entry in each row and column, hence is monomial. On a nonprincipal target branch, multiplying by the principal valuation-one branch forces the component into the annihilator of a unit times \(x_j\), namely \(kx_j^{d-1}\). Conversely the displayed substitutions preserve all relations and have invertible linear part. Off-branch top-degree shears add under composition, branch substitutions form \(J_d(k)^n\), and permutations give the split semidirect product. At \(d=2\), \(\mathfrak m^2=0\), so the full group is \(\operatorname{GL}_n(k)\). Counting coefficients over \(\mathbb F_q\) gives the stated order. The exhaustive small-field script is a consistency check only.

PASS

The closest general papers on automorphism groups of zero-dimensional monomial algebras explicitly work over algebraically closed characteristic-zero fields.
