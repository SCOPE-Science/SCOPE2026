For an absolute sum whose two coordinate axes are strict away from \(\ell_\infty\), the symmetric strong diameter hierarchy collapses completely: \(\mathrm{SSD}(d)\mathrm P\) fails for every \(d>0\), regardless of the two nonzero factors. In particular, every \(\ell_p\)-sum with \(1\le p<\infty\) and at least two nonzero summands has no positive symmetric strong diameter scale.
