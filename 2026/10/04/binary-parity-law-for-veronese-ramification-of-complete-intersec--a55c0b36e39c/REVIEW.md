# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. In the range \(k<\min_i d_i\), the complete-intersection ideal contributes no degree-\(k\) relation, so the ambient degree-\(k\) forms define a base-point-free series with exactly \(\binom{N+k}{k}\) sections. The Wronskian line-bundle degree and complete-intersection adjunction give the claimed total ramification. Parity then reduces exactly to Kummer--Lucas for \(\binom{N+k}{k}\) and oddness of \(k\prod_i d_i\). The real consequence is the standard conjugation-pairing argument for an effective real divisor.

The bounded checker independently replays the degree and parity identities but is not used as an infinite proof.

## Originality
PASS. Full-text inspection of the closest classical Wronskian, hyperosculation, and real-ramification sources found the premises and related special cases, but not the low-Veronese complete-intersection binary phase law or its real forced-inflection consequence. Claim-specific searches using both geometric and binary-carry formulations did not locate an equivalent theorem.

Residual risk: because the derivation is elementary once the classical formulas are combined, an unindexed older source could contain the same corollary explicitly.

## Value
PASS. The theorem classifies a natural ramification invariant over an infinite family of dimensions, multidegrees, and Veronese orders. The low-order condition has a geometric meaning--it is exactly the interval before the defining equations create relations--and odd parity gives a direct real-geometric existence consequence.
