# Review of BMOA is the sharp endpoint for bounded harmonic splitting

## Correctness

PASS. A bounded harmonic map on the disk is the Poisson extension of an \(L^\infty\) boundary function. Under the canonical normalization \(g(0)=0\), the positive Fourier projection of that boundary function is exactly the boundary function of \(h\), while the positive Fourier projection of its complex conjugate, after removing the constant term, is exactly the boundary function of \(g\). The classical endpoint Riesz projection
\[
L^\infty(\mathbb T)\to\mathrm{BMO}(\mathbb T)
\]
therefore gives quantitative \(\mathrm{BMOA}\) control of both components. The derivative-Carleson estimate is the standard equivalent \(\mathrm{BMOA}\) norm.

For sharpness, the full 2026 primary theorem was checked: for every \(0<k<1\) it constructs a bounded globally one-to-one harmonic map with
\[
|g'|\le k|h'|
\]
and unbounded \(h\). Choosing \(k=(K-1)/(K+1)\) gives maximal dilatation at most \(K\). If \(g\) were bounded, then \(h=f-\overline g\) would be bounded, so both components are unbounded. The universal estimate places both in \(\mathrm{BMOA}\).

## Originality

PASS. The 2026 primary source proves failure of boundedness of the analytic part under boundedness, global univalence, and quasiconformality, but does not identify the surviving endpoint space or conclude that both canonical components lie in \(\mathrm{BMOA}\setminus H^\infty\).

The older harmonic-function-space literature supplies the \(\mathrm{BMO}\)/\(\mathrm{BMOA}\) component machinery, but predates the 2026 globally univalent arbitrarily-small-distortion counterexamples and therefore cannot imply the sharpness statement on those subclasses.

Candidate-specific searches using the source identifier, bounded harmonic splitting, analytic/coanalytic \(\mathrm{BMOA}\) components, and fixed quasiconformal distortion returned no implication-equivalent published finding. The closest database records concern unrelated harmonic univalence, BMOA operator theory, and half-plane variation problems.

The main residual risk is that this short endpoint synthesis may have been noted informally or in unindexed commentary after the 2026 preprint.

## Value

PASS. The new preprint shows that the natural \(H^\infty\) expectation fails in a surprisingly rigid geometric class. The finding supplies the exact standard endpoint that still survives uniformly and proves that the failure is sharp at the level of canonical component spaces for every prescribed \(K>1\). This separates the projection-theoretic endpoint from geometric regularity: global univalence and arbitrarily small quasiconformal distortion do not improve \(L^\infty\)-boundary projection beyond \(\mathrm{BMOA}\).

The result is therefore a natural endpoint repair of the source phenomenon rather than a parameter substitution or isolated estimate.
