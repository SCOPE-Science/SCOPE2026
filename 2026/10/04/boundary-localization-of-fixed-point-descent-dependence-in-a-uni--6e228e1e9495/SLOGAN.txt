For a uniform permutation, a fixed-point indicator correlates with a descent indicator only when the two positions touch, giving an explicit bidiagonal cross-covariance law. All interior contributions cancel in the total descent count, so \(\operatorname{Cov}(F,D)=-1/n\) comes entirely from the two endpoints; adding the wrap-around descent cancels those endpoints and makes every fixed-point indicator uncorrelated with the cyclic descent count.
