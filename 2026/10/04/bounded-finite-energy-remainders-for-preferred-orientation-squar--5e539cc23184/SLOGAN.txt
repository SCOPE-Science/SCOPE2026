For the preferred-orientation square and hexagonal lattices, the published \(O(m^{-1})\) high-energy width errors actually have no first-order term. This yields bounded, rather than logarithmically growing, finite-energy remainders: \(M_{\square}(K)=K-(8/\pi)\sqrt K+O(1)\) and \(M_{\hexagon}(K)=8(\sqrt3-1)\sqrt K/\pi+O(1)\).
