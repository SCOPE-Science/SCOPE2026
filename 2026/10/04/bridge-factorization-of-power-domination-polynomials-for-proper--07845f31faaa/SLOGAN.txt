For every proper double star, power domination separates exactly across its central bridge: a side is feasible precisely when its center is selected or all but at most one of its leaves are selected. Therefore \(\mathcal P(D_{a,b};x)=\mathcal P(K_{1,a};x)\mathcal P(K_{1,b};x)\), and for \(a,b\ge3\) the two centers form the unique minimum power dominating set.
