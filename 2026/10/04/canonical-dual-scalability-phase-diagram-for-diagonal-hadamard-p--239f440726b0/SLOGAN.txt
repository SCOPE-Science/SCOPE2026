For a normalized real Hadamard basis \(\{x_i\}\), the canonical dual of \(\{x_i\}\cup\{Dx_i\}\) is scalable exactly when the squared diagonal entries of \(D\) have at most two values, and two values \(u,v\) satisfy \(uv\le1\). The two-level scaling is explicit: the two canonical-dual blocks receive factors \(\sqrt{1-uv}\) and \(\sqrt{2+u+v}\).
