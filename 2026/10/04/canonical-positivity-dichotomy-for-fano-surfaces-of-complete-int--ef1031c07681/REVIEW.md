# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The published canonical formula reduces the theorem to an exact integer inequality. With
\[
q=k+1,
\qquad
R_i=\binom{d_i+k}{k},
\]
the expected-dimension equation gives
\[
qB=\sum_i(d_i-1)R_i-(q^2+2).
\]
The cases of at least three equations, exactly two equations, and one equation are exhaustive. The first is strictly positive by the quadratic lower bound; the second has equality only for \(q=2\) and two quadrics, forcing \(r=5\); the one-equation case is strict for degree at least three, while the quadric possibility is incompatible with the stated range and integrality. Since the Pluecker class is ample, positive \(B\) means ample \(K_F\).

The bounded checker independently replays the classification but is not used as an infinite proof.

## Originality
PASS. Full-text inspection of the closest surface paper found the canonical-class formula, individual surface calculations, the two-quadric abelian example, and the irregularity classification, but not the uniform theorem that the canonical coefficient is always nonnegative with exactly one zero. Claim-specific searches using canonical, general-type, trivial-canonical, and two-quadric formulations found no equivalent statement.

Residual risk: the proof is short after the known formula, so an unindexed classical source could contain the same corollary.

## Value
PASS. The theorem supplies a complete birational-type boundary for a canonical family of parameter surfaces: every expected-dimensional Fano surface in the standard range is of general type except the unique abelian surface of lines on two quadrics. This simultaneously excludes del Pezzo and K3 behavior and isolates the exact geometric boundary case.

Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
