For every \(1<p<\infty\), adding any finite or infinite number of additional Daugavet summands beyond the classical two-summand case does not change the slice Daugavet thickness of an \(\ell_p\)-sum: it remains exactly \(2^{1/p}\). The proof is cardinality-free because finite dual truncation gives the lower bound while a two-coordinate slice gives the upper bound.
