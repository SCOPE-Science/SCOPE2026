Centroid-alignment efficiency tensorizes under Cartesian products: \(c_{n+d,m}\le c_{n,m}c_{d,m}\). The exact planar obstruction therefore gives \(c_{n,m}\le(4/9)^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}\) for every fixed \(m\ge2\), providing an exponential higher-dimensional upper bound for the fixed-cardinality problem.
