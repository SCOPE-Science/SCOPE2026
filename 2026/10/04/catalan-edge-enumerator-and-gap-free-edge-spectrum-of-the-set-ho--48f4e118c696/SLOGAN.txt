For the nonhomogeneous set-homogeneous \(3\)-hypergraph \(M_3\), finite types are plane binary trees, and the edge-refined age polynomial satisfies \(P_n(q)=\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}q^{i\binom{n-i}{2}}P_i(q)P_{n-i}(q)\); only five vertices miss an edge count, while every count \(0,\ldots,\binom n3\) occurs for \(n\ge6\).
