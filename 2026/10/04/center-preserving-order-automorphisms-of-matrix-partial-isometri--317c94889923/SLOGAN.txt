For the normalized Jia--Ji order automorphisms of matrix partial isometries, preserving the scalar unitary circle is equivalent to a scalar parameter \(A=\alpha I\), and two distinct nonidentity scalar probes already force this. The induced circle action is exactly an arbitrary Möbius or anti-Möbius disk automorphism fixing \(1\), with the orthogonality-preserving identity and conjugation as the two zero-parameter endpoints.
