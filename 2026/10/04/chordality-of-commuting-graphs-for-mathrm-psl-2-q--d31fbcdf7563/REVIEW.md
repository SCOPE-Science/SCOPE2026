# Review
## Correctness
PASS. The proof splits the parameter range into characteristic two, \(q=5\), and odd \(q>5\). The positive cases use abelian centralizers. For odd \(q>5\), the standard dihedral involution centralizer gives the exact size and degree of the induced involution graph. Its clique number is at most \(3\), while its edge count is at least \(2n\); this contradicts the \(2n-3\) edge bound for chordal graphs of clique number at most \(3\). The supplied replay independently checks representative finite cases and an explicit induced cycle.

## Originality
PASS. The 2023 source explicitly treats cographs rather than chordal graphs for the Lie-type portion and leaves simple-group commuting-graph chordality as an open classification direction. Exact searches for the \(\mathrm{PSL}_2(q)\) chordality statement found no matching result. The 2025/2026 commuting-involution-graph paper studies automorphisms and local structure, not this chordality classification. The closest indexed findings concern other graph constructions or other \(\mathrm{PSL}_2\) invariants and do not imply the claim.

## Value
PASS. The result settles an infinite rank-one family inside a stated finite-simple-group chordality problem. The obstruction is structural rather than computational: exact involution density exceeds a general extremal bound for chordal graphs of clique number \(3\).

The main residual risk is bibliographic: an equivalent theorem could exist under terminology not found by the exact and semantic searches. The mathematical proof itself does not depend on a novelty assumption.
