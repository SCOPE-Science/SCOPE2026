A finite multi-agent relational model needs only \(\chi(G)\) copies of its world set to become proper, where \(G\) records pairs jointly accessible to every agent; this improves the known \(m\)-copy construction to \(m\chi(G)\) worlds. The \(m^2\) bound is nevertheless worst-case sharp for two-agent S5 models, while one-agent properization is possible only when the target is already proper.
