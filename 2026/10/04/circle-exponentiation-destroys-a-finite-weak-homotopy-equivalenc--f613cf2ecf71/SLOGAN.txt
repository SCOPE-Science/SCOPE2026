For the four-point finite circle \(C\) and the nine-point weakly contractible but noncontractible space \(R\), the mapping space \(\operatorname{Map}(C,R)\) has \(461\) points and weak homotopy type \(\bigvee^4 S^1\). Thus the weak equivalence \(R\to *\) becomes a non-equivalence after exponentiation by \(C\).
