For the square \(Q_a=[-a,a]^2\), the circumradius–area Cheeger-type problem has an exact optimizer: intersect the square with a centered disk of radius \(a\sec(\pi/4-\alpha/2)\), where \(\alpha=\cos\alpha\). The minimum value is \(\cos(\pi/4-\alpha/2)/(4a\alpha)\), so the optimal subset is neither the incircle nor the whole square.
