For equal-strength nonselective weak measurements of stabilizers, the residual factor on each syndrome character is \(\zeta\) raised to a binary codeword weight. Consequently the exact worst-sector contraction is \(\zeta^{d}\), so choosing a measurement schedule is a classical minimum-distance design problem; for \(r=3\), adding only the triple-product stabilizer improves the generator-only factor from \(\zeta\) to \(\zeta^2\).
