The one-braid toric-mosaic integer program has an exact all-parameter solution: it is feasible exactly for \(q\ge3p-4\), is constant at \(3p-4\) through \(q=4p-4\), and thereafter equals \(p-2+\lceil q/2\rceil\) except for a one-tile penalty when \(q\equiv2\pmod4\). Thus the construction's published solver step can be replaced by a closed formula and explicit optimal \((h,v)\) witnesses.
