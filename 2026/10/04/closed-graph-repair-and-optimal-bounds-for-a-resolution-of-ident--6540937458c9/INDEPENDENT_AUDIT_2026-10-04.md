# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Condition (I) in the published theorem says that for every \(f\), the unweighted coefficient sequence lies in the Hilbert direct sum; it does not state a uniform Bessel constant. Define the everywhere-defined linear analysis operator \(Uf=(\Lambda_jP_{W_j}f)_j\). Coordinatewise convergence plus boundedness of every coordinate operator shows that a convergent graph sequence has the claimed limit, so the closed graph theorem makes \(U\) bounded and supplies the uniform constant \(B_0=\|U\|^2\). Writing \(a_j=\|\Lambda_jP_{W_j}f\|^2\), the resolution identity gives \(\|f\|^2=\sum_j v_j a_j\). Cauchy-Schwarz yields \(\|f\|^4\le(\sum_j a_j)(\sum_j v_j^2a_j)\le B_0\|f\|^2\sum_jv_j^2a_j\), hence the lower bound \(1/B_0\). With \(M=\sup_jv_j\), \(v_j^2\le Mv_j\) gives the upper bound \(M\). The scalar one-atom and two-atom constructions attain or approach both constants and force \(MB_0\ge1\); an independent scalar calculation reproduced the advertised upper sharpness formula.
