The theorem statement and proof were checked in the open journal PDF, including the pointwise quantifier in condition (I) and the subsequent uniform use of the same constant. The source definition of a resolution of the identity was checked as unconditional convergence.

The analysis map was verified to have a closed graph by coordinate convergence in the Hilbert direct sum. The two frame inequalities then follow symbolically from the resolution identity and Cauchy–Schwarz.

For sharpness, the one-atom model gives equality in the lower bound. When \(MB_0>1\), the two-atom coefficients satisfy \(q_1+q_2=B_0\) and \(Mq_1+\varepsilon q_2=1\), while the weighted coefficient tends to \(M\) as \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\). No computational extrapolation is used.

Limit: no claim is made that these are the optimal bounds of every individual family; they are the optimal universal functions of \(B_0\) and \(M\).
