# Review

PASS. For each ordered distinct pair, the generated subquasigroup is the unique three-point Steiner quasigroup; homogeneity therefore gives 2-transitivity. Hence the 2-closure is the full symmetric group. Ordered distinct block triples form one orbit, so every element of the 3-closure preserves the block relation and therefore the quasigroup product. Monotonicity of \(k\)-closures gives equality with the automorphism group for all larger arities.
