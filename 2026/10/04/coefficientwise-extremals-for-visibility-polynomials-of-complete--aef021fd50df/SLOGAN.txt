Among noncomplete complete multipartite graphs of fixed order \(N\), the visibility-polynomial coefficientwise maximum is attained exactly when every part has size at most \(2\). A coefficientwise minimum is the star only for \(3\le N\le5\); from \(N=6\) onward no minimum exists because the star uniquely minimizes the cubic coefficient but loses the \((N-1)\)-st coefficient to \(K_{3,N-3}\).
