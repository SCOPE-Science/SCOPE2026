For generalized FRB on the coercive scalar mode \(F(x)=ax\), the exact stability ceiling is \(a\tau<2/(1+2\alpha)\), and the unique fastest step is \(a\tau=1/(1+\alpha)\) with factor \(\sqrt{\alpha/(1+\alpha)}\). Thus scalar stability and rate favor \(\alpha\downarrow1/2\), opposite to the direction of the source's generic Lyapunov safety coefficient.
