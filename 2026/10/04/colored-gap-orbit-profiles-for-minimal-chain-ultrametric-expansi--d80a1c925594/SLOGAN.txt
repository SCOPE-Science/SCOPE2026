For Braunfeld’s minimal expansion of a chain-valued generic ultrametric, an injective labeled \(n\)-tuple is exactly a permutation with one of \(d\) hierarchy levels painted on each adjacent gap, so its orbit count is \(n!d^{n-1}\). Allowing repetitions gives \(b_d(n)=\mathcal F_n(d)/d\), linking the full tuple profile to ordered-Bell/Fubini polynomials and recovering the chain height already from two-point orbits.
