A planar anisotropic Cheeger set inherits every orthogonal symmetry shared by its convex domain and its Wulff body. Thus a \(k\)-rotational domain with a \(\widetilde k\)-rotational Wulff body has at least \(\gcd(k,\widetilde k)\)-fold Cheeger symmetry, and \(k\mid\widetilde k\) suffices for full \(k\)-fold symmetry plus contact with every Cañete edge.\n
