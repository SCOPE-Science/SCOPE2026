The finite \(r\)-Kronecker path algebra over \(\mathbf F_q\) has exactly \(q^r+2\) element-centralizers and commuting probability \(q^{-2}+(q^2-1)q^{-r-2}\), with every nonzero radical commutator having the same fiber size. Thus equal centralizer counts need not imply equal commuting probabilities: \(A_{2,2}\) and \(A_{1,4}\) are both \(6\)-centralizer but have probabilities \(7/16\) and \(19/64\).
