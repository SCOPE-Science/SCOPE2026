For \(n\ge2\), the commuting conjugacy class graph of \(H_n(q)\) is exactly \(\overline{\operatorname{Sp}(2n,q)}[K_{q-1}]\), giving its full integral spectrum, diameter \(2\), and clique number \(q^n-1\). It is strongly regular for \(q=2\), while for every \(q>2\) its two types of adjacent pairs have different common-neighbor counts.
