For generalized Sprott M, compact recurrence obeys the exact balance \(\mathbb E[(a+bx+y)^2]=b\mathbb E[z^2]\): \(b\le0\) permits only mixtures of the two equilibria. When \(b>0\), every nonconstant periodic orbit has \(P>2\pi/\sqrt b\) and every non-equilibrium compact recurrent state must cross both the equilibrium-root slab and both signs of \(z\).
