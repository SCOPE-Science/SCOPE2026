For generalized Sprott Q, two exact stationary coboundaries create a sharp compact-recurrence phase boundary: when \(a\le b\), only mixtures of the two equilibrium atoms can be compact invariant probability measures. When \(a>b\), every non-equilibrium compact recurrent state must straddle the slab \(-a<y<0\), and every nonconstant periodic orbit has \(P>2\pi/\sqrt{a-b}\).
