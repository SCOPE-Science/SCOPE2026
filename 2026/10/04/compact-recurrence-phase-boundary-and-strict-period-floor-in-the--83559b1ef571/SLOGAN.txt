The generalized Sprott R flow has an exact compact-recurrence boundary at \(a=0\): for \(a<0\) only the equilibrium measure survives, while for \(a=0\), \(b\ne0\) no compact invariant probability measure exists. For \(a>0\), \(\operatorname{Var}(y)=a\operatorname{Var}(x)\), and every nonconstant periodic orbit satisfies \(P>2\pi/\sqrt a\).
