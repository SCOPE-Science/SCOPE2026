The generalized Sprott I flow has a sharp compact-recurrence boundary at \(a=0\): for \(a<0\) only the origin survives, while at \(a=0\) every compact invariant measure lies on an equilibrium parabola. For \(a>0\), exact energy and cubic-moment laws force \(y<-a/b\) excursions and every nonconstant cycle satisfies \(P>2\pi\sqrt{b/(a(b+1))}\).
