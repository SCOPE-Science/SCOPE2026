The known optimum \(A(5,3)=4\) in the Ulam metric has exactly \(4020\) labeled maximum codes, and every one is equidistant with all six pairwise distances equal to \(3\). Under common symbol relabeling together with position reversal, the maxima form exactly \(24\) orbits with sizes \(60\), \(120\), or \(240\).
