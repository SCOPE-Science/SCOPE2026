The cubic–absolute-value flow claimed to have only the equilibrium line \((0,0,z)\) actually has at least one isolated equilibrium for every \(a,b>0\), and three isolated equilibria when \(0<4ab<1\). Their exact Routh–Hurwitz classification includes a stable negative branch precisely for \(2/9<ab<1/4\), so hiddenness cannot be inferred from the published line-only equilibrium claim.
