For the maximal number \(\Delta_1(n)\) of distances in an \(n\)-point \(1\)-homogeneous metric space, the remaining even cases have the exact value \(\Delta_1(n)=(3n+n_2)/4\), where \(n_2\) is the \(2\)-part of \(n\). Together with the known odd case \(\Delta_1(n)=(n+1)/2\), this completely resolves the finite distance-count problem posed by Bargetz et al.
