# Review

## Correctness
**PASS.** Enomoto's Proposition 4.1 supplies an exact minimal two-step sequence for every \(U_\lambda\), including the map \(P_2\to P_1\) given by \(\alpha_1-\lambda\beta_1\), the explicit embedding of \(U_{q\lambda}\) into \(P_2\), and \(\Omega^2(U_\lambda)\cong U_{q\lambda}\). Splicing these sequences gives the stated alternating projective resolution. Applying Hom into \(U_\mu\) produces one-dimensional cochain spaces, scalar differentials \(\mu-q^r\lambda\), and zero intervening differentials because length-two paths annihilate \(U_\mu\). The resulting cohomology gives the claimed formula for every \(r\ge0\). The finite-order exact-period statement also uses the published distinct-parameter Hom vanishing and the dimension \(10\) of every odd syzygy.

## Originality
**PASS.** The motivating paper was inspected in full around the module construction, minimal sequence, syzygy iteration, and distinct-parameter Hom lemma. It states the syzygy orbit needed to prove nonperiodicity but does not state the pairwise Ext formula or the self-Ext algebra. Targeted semantic searches under the exact module names, syzygy formula, self-Ext terminology, and finite-order variant found no covering result. Erdmann's earlier band-module paper contains the broader multiplicative-syzygy mechanism and root-of-unity periodicity, but not this Ext computation for Enomoto's algebra. The closest Ext-finite classification is radical-cube-zero and therefore does not imply the claim here.

## Value
**PASS.** The finding extracts a natural homological invariant of the very module that witnesses the new counterexample to the periodicity conjecture. In the infinite-order case it shows a sharp separation between nonperiodicity and self-extension abundance: the witness is nonprojective and nonperiodic while its total self-Ext algebra has dimension only \(2\). The same formula identifies the finite-order boundary exactly, including the exact period and all self-Ext degrees, so the statement is structural rather than an arbitrary finite calculation.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest structural precedent is Erdmann's \(\Omega^2(M(\lambda))\cong M(v\lambda)\) formula for band modules, including her root-of-unity periodicity remark. The present result uses the explicit minimal maps of the new \(\Lambda(q)\) family to compute all pairwise Ext groups. A residual bibliographic risk remains because equivalent statements may be unindexed or phrased differently, and the motivating source is recent. No claim is made about all \(\Lambda(q)\)-modules or about the full Hochschild or bimodule periodicity theory.
