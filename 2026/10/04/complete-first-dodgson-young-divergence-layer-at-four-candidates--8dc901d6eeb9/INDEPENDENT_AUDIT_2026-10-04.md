# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The claim is a genuinely exhaustive finite theorem. I independently reconstructed the standard four-candidate Dodgson and Young scores over all \(24^3=13824\) labeled three-voter profiles, without treating the saved verifier log as proof. Dodgson was computed by exhaustive upward moves of the focal candidate in each ballot (moves among other candidates cannot improve its pairwise margins), and Young by exhaustive nonempty voter-subset deletion with weak Condorcet target. The independent census reproduced exactly 1008 divergent profiles, the histogram \(12288,528,144,576,288\), and strict \(D(P)\subsetneq Y(P)\) in every divergent case. The package verifier additionally double-computes each score and canonicalizes all divergent profiles, yielding seven candidate/voter-relabeling classes of orbit size 144. The one-voter boundary is immediate, and the three-candidate lower candidate boundary is supplied by prior literature.

A targeted Resultary search for those exact and equivalent formulations found no implication-equivalent published record. The result is therefore original to the best of the inspected evidence.
