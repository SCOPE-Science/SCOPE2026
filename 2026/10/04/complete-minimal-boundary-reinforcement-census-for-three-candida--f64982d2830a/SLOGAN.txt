At the three-candidate maximin reinforcement boundary, exactly \(18\) unordered anonymous pairs occur, all with electorate split \(5+10\), and they form only \(3\) candidate-relabeling classes. Each of the \(18\) minimal unions has a unique paradox-producing partition: the \(5\)-voter side has the shared winner as Condorcet winner, the \(10\)-voter side is cyclic, and merging creates a different Condorcet winner.
