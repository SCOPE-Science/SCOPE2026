Three-candidate tie-independent IRV first admits a homogeneous no-show paradox at \(11\) voters, where exactly \(60\) anonymous labeled-candidate profiles are bad. Up to candidate relabeling the entire boundary consists of ten classes, precisely \((4,0,0,3,t,4-t)\) and \((4,0,1,2,t,4-t)\) for \(0\le t\le4\), and every bad profile has one harmful move: two \(A\succ B\succ C\) voters abstain and change the winner from \(C\) to \(B\).\n
