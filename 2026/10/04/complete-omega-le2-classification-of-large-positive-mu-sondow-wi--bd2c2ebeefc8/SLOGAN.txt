For positive \(\mu\), every \(\mu\)-Sondow witness larger than \(\mu\) with at most two prime factors is completely forced: it is a prime with \(\mu=p-1\), a prime square with \(\mu=p(p-1)\), or \(pq\) with \(\mu=(p-1)(q-1)-1\). In particular, any large \(673\)-Sondow witness must have at least three prime factors counted with multiplicity.
