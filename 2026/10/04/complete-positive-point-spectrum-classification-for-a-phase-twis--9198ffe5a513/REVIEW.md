# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The published determinant is reconstructed with the same normalization and exact quasimomentum dependence. The positive-measure Floquet argument reduces point spectrum to vanishing of the coefficient \(2k(k^2-1)\sin(k\ell)\). The two resulting cases are checked exactly, and the converse is supplied by the direct-integral flat-band construction. The bundled checker reproduces the algebraic identities from the packaged source code.

## Originality
PASS. The closest source, arXiv:2108.04708, gives the determinant and one sufficient \(k=1\) flat-band relation, but not the necessity/exhaustion theorem. The endpoint model arXiv:1710.02664 has a different flat-band structure, and the magnetic follow-up arXiv:2302.04601 uses different determinants. Targeted published-record and prior-result searches found no equivalent or dominating statement. Residual risk is limited to an unindexed equivalent observation under different flat-band terminology.

## Value
PASS. The classification identifies the full positive point spectrum of a natural periodic family emphasized for its transport transition: it is generically empty and collapses on a codimension-one resonance to the single infinitely degenerate level \(E=1\). This supplies a complete structural boundary that the source's example alone does not provide.

## Closest literature and limitations
The result is restricted to the equilateral nonmagnetic square lattice, interior phase \(0<\mu<\pi/2\), positive energies, and coupling-length scale one. It does not classify negative point spectrum or endpoint phases.
