The 56 six-vertex tournaments have exactly seven out-neighborhood-complex homotopy types: 34 points, 11 copies of \(S^1\), 4 copies of \(\bigvee^2 S^1\), 2 copies of \(\bigvee^3 S^1\), one \(\bigvee^4 S^1\), 3 copies of \(S^0\), and one \(S^1\sqcup\mathrm{pt}\). The maximum \(\beta_1=4\) is attained by a unique tournament isomorphism class.
