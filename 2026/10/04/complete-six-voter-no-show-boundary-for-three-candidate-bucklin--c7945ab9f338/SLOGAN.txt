Three-candidate Bucklin voting first exhibits the tie-independent no-show paradox at \(6\) voters, where exactly \(18\) anonymous labeled profiles occur in \(3\) candidate-relabeling classes. After normalizing the abstainer to \(A\succ B\succ C\), the entire boundary is \((1,t,0,3,2-t,0)\) for \(t\in\{0,1,2\}\), and every bad profile has exactly one profitable one-voter abstention.\n
