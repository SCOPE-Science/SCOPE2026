# Review: Complete spectral and tiling moduli for three-point integer sets

## Correctness
PASS. The unit-circle lemma, Bézout reduction, difference-set test for a three-point spectrum, invertible finite-state periodicity argument, finite-group Fourier support argument, and residue-fiber decomposition collectively prove the full statement. The finite verifier checks bounded cases but is not used as a substitute for the proof.

## Originality
PASS with recorded literature risk. The recent finite-pair source establishes rationality, while older three-digit measure literature contains the modulo-three existence condition. Targeted statement-level searches did not surface the exact normalized-spectrum family, the exact denominator \(3g\), the complete tiling-complement family, or the counts \(g^2\) and \(3^g\). Full-text access to some highly relevant sources was unavailable, and that risk is retained rather than treated as proof of novelty.

## Value
PASS. The result gives a natural complete classification at the first nontrivial prime cardinality in a problem explicitly linked by recent work to finite cyclic Fuglede structure. The exact spectral and tiling moduli expose information substantially stronger than existence or rationality alone.

## Closest literature and limitations
Fu and Song, arXiv:2607.19858, is the closest recent source: it proves rationality for all finite integer spectral pairs. Wang, Yin and Zhang, DOI 10.1515/forum-2023-0114, records the primitive modulo-three condition in a three-digit Cantor-Moran setting. Fu and Wang, DOI 10.1016/j.jat.2020.105494, studies spectra for three-digit Cantor-Moran measures. The present claim is restricted to finite three-point integer sets, and literature coverage remains limited by unavailable full texts noted in the audit.
