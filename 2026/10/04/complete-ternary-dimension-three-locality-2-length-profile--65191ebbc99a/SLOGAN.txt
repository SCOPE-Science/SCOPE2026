For ternary linear dimension-three codes with all-symbol locality \(2\), the exact minimum length is \(5,5,6\) at distances \(1,2,3\), and equals the Griesmer bound \(d+\lceil d/3\rceil+\lceil d/9\rceil\) for every \(d\ge4\). A nine-residue family of multisets in \(\mathrm{PG}(2,3)\) gives the complete all-distance construction.
