# Complete theta-class relations for three-point generalized theta configurations

## Finding
For every integer \(m\ge 4\), let \(\Theta_m\) be the graph with two vertices joined by \(m\) parallel edges, let \(B_3(\Theta_m)\) be the unordered configuration space of three points on \(\Theta_m\), and let
\[
A_m=\ker\!\left(\mathbb Z^{m}\xrightarrow{\;\sum\;}\mathbb Z\right)
\]
be the augmentation lattice of the edge set. Permuting the parallel edges induces compatible \(S_m\)-actions, and there is an \(S_m\)-equivariant isomorphism
\[
H_2(B_3(\Theta_m);\mathbb Z)\cong \bigwedge^3 A_m.
\]
Choose a consistent orientation for the theta class \(\tau_{ijkl}\) supported on four distinct ordered edges \(i,j,k,l\). Under the isomorphism it maps to
\[
(e_j-e_i)\wedge(e_k-e_i)\wedge(e_l-e_i).
\]
Consequently, for any fixed edge \(r\), the classes \(\tau_{rijk}\) with \(i,j,k\ne r\) distinct form an integral basis. All integral relations among oriented four-edge theta classes are generated by the five-term boundary relations
\[
\tau_{ijkl}-\tau_{rjkl}+\tau_{rikl}-\tau_{rijl}+\tau_{rijk}=0
\]
for five distinct edges \(r,i,j,k,l\), with signs determined by the displayed ordering. In particular,
\[
\operatorname{rank} H_2(B_3(\Theta_m);\mathbb Z)=\binom{m-1}{3}.
\]

## Assumptions and scope
The graph \(\Theta_m\) is regarded as a topological graph; subdivisions do not change the configuration-space homotopy type. The configuration space is unordered. The statement is integral and concerns homological degree \(2\) and exactly three particles. The \(S_m\)-action is the action induced by permuting the \(m\) parallel edges. The sign of an individual theta generator depends on the chosen orientation convention, while the equivariant module and relation lattice do not.

## Proof
Explode one of the two essential vertices of \(\Theta_m\). The resulting graph is the \(m\)-armed star \(S_m\). In the reduced Świątkowski complex of \(S_m\), privilege edge \(e_0\), put \(x_i=e_i-e_0\) for \(1\le i\le m-1\), and write \(a_i\) for the corresponding half-edge difference at the star center. After the unimodular change of polynomial variables \(t=e_0\), \(x_i=e_i-e_0\), the complex is the two-term complex
\[
R\otimes A_m\xrightarrow{d}R,\qquad d(a_i)=x_i,
\]
where \(R=\mathbb Z[t,x_1,\ldots,x_{m-1}]\). Hence there is no homology in degree \(2\) for the star.

In weight \(2\), the first homology is the degree-one syzygy module of the regular sequence \(x_1,\ldots,x_{m-1}\). Its integral basis is represented by the Koszul syzygies
\[
x_k a_j-x_j a_k,
\]
so \(H_1(B_2(S_m);\mathbb Z)\cong\bigwedge^2 A_m\).

The vertex-explosion exact sequence gives an injection
\[
H_2(B_3(\Theta_m);\mathbb Z)\hookrightarrow
A_m\otimes\bigwedge^2 A_m.
\]
Under the preceding identification, the connecting map multiplies the summand indexed by \(x_i\) by \(x_i\). Thus its kernel consists exactly of the degree-two Koszul relations
\[
x_i\otimes(x_j\wedge x_k)-x_j\otimes(x_i\wedge x_k)+x_k\otimes(x_i\wedge x_j),
\]
which are the image of the injective Koszul map
\[
\bigwedge^3 A_m\longrightarrow A_m\otimes\bigwedge^2 A_m.
\]
Exactness therefore yields \(H_2(B_3(\Theta_m);\mathbb Z)\cong\bigwedge^3 A_m\).

It remains to identify the embedded theta classes. The four-edge case in the source literature is the generator of \(H_2(B_3(\Theta_4);\mathbb Z)\). Its explicit chain representative is alternating in the four edge labels. Together with naturality of vertex explosion under an embedding of four selected edges, this sends the oriented class on \(i,j,k,l\) to the corresponding Koszul element. This gives
\[
\tau_{ijkl}\longmapsto(e_j-e_i)\wedge(e_k-e_i)\wedge(e_l-e_i).
\]
The formula is invariant under simultaneous permutation of edge labels, proving \(S_m\)-equivariance.

Fix an edge \(r\). The classes containing \(r\) map to the standard exterior basis of \(\bigwedge^3 A_m\), so they form an integral basis. If \(r\notin\{i,j,k,l\}\), exterior expansion gives
\[
(e_j-e_i)\wedge(e_k-e_i)\wedge(e_l-e_i)
=(e_j-e_r)\wedge(e_k-e_r)\wedge(e_l-e_r)
-(e_i-e_r)\wedge(e_k-e_r)\wedge(e_l-e_r)
+(e_i-e_r)\wedge(e_j-e_r)\wedge(e_l-e_r)
-(e_i-e_r)\wedge(e_j-e_r)\wedge(e_k-e_r),
\]
which is exactly the displayed five-term relation. Conversely, every theta class not containing \(r\) is reduced by one such relation to the fixed-edge basis, and the fixed-edge classes are independent. Hence these five-term relations generate the entire integral relation lattice.

## Verification
The included script `verify_theta_relations.py` independently builds the integer matrix sending every oriented four-edge theta symbol to its exterior-coordinate vector relative to a fixed edge. For \(4\le m\le10\), it verifies exactly that every five-edge boundary relation maps to zero, every non-fixed-edge class reduces by the claimed five-term relation, and the fixed-edge classes are the standard basis. It also checks ranks modulo \(2,3,5,7,101\): the theta map has rank \(\binom{m-1}{3}\) and the boundary-relation matrix has rank \(\binom{m-1}{4}\). The proof above, rather than these finite checks, establishes the theorem for every \(m\ge4\).

## Relationship to prior work
An and Knudsen identify the reduced Świątkowski model and vertex-explosion exact sequence, prove that \(H_2(B_3(\Theta_4);\mathbb Z)\) is free of rank one, define theta classes by embeddings of \(\Theta_4\), and prove that planar second homology is generated by the atomic theta class together with the other atomic operations. Their paper does not state the complete relation lattice among all four-edge theta classes in \(\Theta_m\), nor the \(S_m\)-module identification above. In its introduction it explicitly asks for universal relations in degree \(2\) and for combinatorial bases of second homology; the present theorem answers both questions on this generalized-theta, three-particle slice.

Ko and Park give a general first-homology formula for graph braid groups, while Appiah and collaborators compute
\[
\chi(B_3(\Theta_m))=\frac{m(m-2)(m-7)}6.
\]
Those results can recover the numerical second Betti number after combining first-homology and Euler-characteristic information. The numerical rank is therefore not the originality point here. The new content is the integral, edge-permutation-equivariant identification with \(\bigwedge^3 A_m\), the fixed-edge theta basis, and the complete five-term presentation of theta-class relations. A 2026 study of \(B_3(\Theta_m)\) concerns three-manifold models and uses the Euler characteristic; it does not state this module or relation presentation.

## Limitations
No claim is made about higher particle numbers, higher homological degrees, ordered configuration spaces, or multiplicative cohomology. The isomorphism is canonical up to the global orientation convention used for theta generators. The finite script is a consistency check, not a proof for unbounded \(m\); the unbounded statement rests on the reduced Świątkowski complex, the vertex-explosion exact sequence, and exactness of the Koszul complex for the polynomial variables \(x_1,\ldots,x_{m-1}\).
