# Same-model review

## Claim
For every integer \(m\ge 4\), let \(\Theta_m\) be the graph with two vertices joined by \(m\) parallel edges, let \(B_3(\Theta_m)\) be the unordered configuration space of three points, and let \(A_m=\ker(\mathbb Z^m\xrightarrow{\sum}\mathbb Z)\) be the edge augmentation lattice. Permuting edges gives \(S_m\)-actions, and \(H_2(B_3(\Theta_m);\mathbb Z)\cong\bigwedge^3 A_m\) equivariantly. Under a consistent orientation, the theta class on ordered distinct edges \(i,j,k,l\) maps to \((e_j-e_i)\wedge(e_k-e_i)\wedge(e_l-e_i)\). Hence the theta classes containing any fixed edge form an integral basis, and all integral relations among oriented four-edge theta classes are generated by the five-term boundary relations on five distinct edges.

## Correctness
PASS. Exploding one essential vertex reduces the calculation to the two-term reduced Świątkowski complex of a star. In weights two and three, the required syzygies are exactly the first two Koszul syzygy layers of the regular sequence of edge differences. The vertex-explosion exact sequence identifies the full second homology with \(\bigwedge^3 A_m\). Naturality identifies embedded four-edge theta classes with the exterior wedges in the claim. The fixed-edge classes map to a basis, and the five-term expansion both produces and generates all integral relations. The included verifier checks the finite coordinate matrices independently for \(4\le m\le10\) in several characteristics.

## Originality
PASS. The closest source, An--Knudsen, supplies the four-edge theta generator and explosion machinery but does not state the generalized-theta \(S_m\)-module or complete relation lattice. Appiah et al. compute the Euler characteristic, and Ko--Park give first-homology formulas, so the numerical rank alone is not treated as original. Jain--Vo's later same-family paper studies manifold models and likewise does not state the module or relations. Targeted published-result searches for the object, particle number, exterior-power description, symmetric action, and theta relations did not locate an equivalent statement. Residual risk remains that a differently indexed specialized source could contain the same representation-theoretic formulation.

## Value
PASS. The edge-permutation module, fixed-edge basis, and complete five-term relation presentation explain the structure of all embedded theta classes at once, rather than giving only a Betti number. This is a natural reusable description for functorial and representation-theoretic work on graph configuration spaces, and it gives a concrete family-level answer to the source paper's explicit questions about degree-two relations and combinatorial bases.

## Closest literature and limitations
The proof uses the reduced Świątkowski model and vertex-explosion exact sequence from An--Knudsen. It is restricted to unordered three-point configurations of generalized theta graphs in degree two. No statement is made about higher particle numbers, ordered configuration spaces, or cohomology products.
