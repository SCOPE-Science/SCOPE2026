For the generalized theta graph \(\Theta_m\), the second homology of the unordered three-point configuration space is not merely free of rank \(\binom{m-1}{3}\): as an edge-permutation module it is \(\bigwedge^3 A_m\), where \(A_m\) is the augmentation lattice of the \(m\) parallel edges. Four-edge theta classes containing any fixed edge form an integral basis, and every relation among theta classes is generated by the natural five-term boundary relations on five edges.
