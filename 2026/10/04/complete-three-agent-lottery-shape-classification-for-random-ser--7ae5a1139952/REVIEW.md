# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The universe is all \(6^3=216\) strict three-agent profiles. The proof partitions it into six disjoint structural cells determined by first-choice occupancy, ranking repetition, and—only in one cell—the singleton's location relative to the duplicated ranking. Every cell has a direct six-priority-order calculation, and the cell sizes sum to \(216\). The verifier independently evaluates every profile with two serial-dictatorship implementations and checks CRE both by top trading cycles and by exhaustive blocking-coalition core enumeration.

Risk: the classification is finite and deliberately restricted to \(3\times3\); no larger-market pattern is inferred.

## Originality
**PASS.** Abdulkadiroğlu--Sönmez already prove the stronger general equality of serial-dictatorship and assigned-endowment-core frequencies for each efficient matching, so that equivalence is explicitly prior. Bogomolnaia--Moulin analyze three-agent random priority and reduce the three-agent preference domain by symmetry, but the inspected full text does not state the five possible multiplicity spectra, their exact labeled-profile counts, the structural six-cell criterion, or the impossibility of support size \(5\). Targeted exact-phrase, count, support-size, and equivalence searches located no such table or theorem.

Risk: failed search is not a proof of novelty; an unindexed exercise, note, or supplementary enumeration could contain the same small-market classification.

## Value
**PASS.** RSD is a canonical assignment lottery, and the 1998 core-from-random-endowments theorem identifies a deep alternative implementation of the same rule. The new result resolves the entire first nontrivial market's *lottery geometry*: not just which outcomes can occur, but every possible probability shape and exactly which preference structures produce it. The missing support size \(5\) and the six-cell structural criterion are reusable small-market facts for comparison, testing, and mechanism examples rather than an arbitrary numerical slice.

Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
