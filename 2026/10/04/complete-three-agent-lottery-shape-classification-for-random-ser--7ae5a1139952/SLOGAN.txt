With three agents and three objects, random serial dictatorship has exactly five possible deterministic-support probability shapes: \((1)\), \((1/2,1/2)\), \((1/2,1/3,1/6)\), \((1/3,1/3,1/6,1/6)\), and the uniform six-point lottery. Across the \(216\) strict preference profiles their counts are \(48,36,72,54,6\), so support size \(5\) never occurs; the same multiplicities arise from core-from-random-endowments.\n
