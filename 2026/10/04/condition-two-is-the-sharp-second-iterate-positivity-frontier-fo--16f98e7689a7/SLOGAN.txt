For exact-line-search steepest descent on Stieltjes systems, condition number \(2\) is the sharp universal threshold guaranteeing a nonnegative second iterate from \(x^{(0)}=0\) and \(b\ge0\). Every larger condition number admits a three-dimensional diagonal counterexample, while in dimensions one and two all iterates stay nonnegative whenever the exact solution is nonnegative.
