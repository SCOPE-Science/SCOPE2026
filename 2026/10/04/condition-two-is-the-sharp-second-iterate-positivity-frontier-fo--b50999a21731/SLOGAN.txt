For unpreconditioned MINRES on Stieltjes systems, condition number \(2\) is the sharp universal frontier guaranteeing a nonnegative second iterate from \(x^{(0)}=0\) and \(b\ge0\). The second-step solution polynomial has its zero at an exact weighted average of pairwise spectral sums, yielding a three-dimensional counterexample for every \(\kappa>2\).
