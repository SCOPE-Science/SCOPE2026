Every compact stationary state of the positive-parameter Liu system obeys \(\mathbb E[y\mid x]=x\), \(\mathbb E[x^2\mid z]=(c/h)z\), and an exact quadratic defect about the equilibrium height \(b/k\). Unless it is a mixture of the three equilibria, it must spend positive stationary mass above \(z=b/k\); every nonconstant periodic orbit therefore reaches above that height.
