Every compact stationary state of the Chen system obeys the exact conditional laws \(\mathbb E[y\mid x]=x\) and \(\mathbb E[xy\mid z]=bz\), together with \(\mathbb E[(z-(2c-a))x^2]=a\mathbb E[(y-x)^2]\). Unless it is a mixture of the three equilibria, it must place positive stationary mass above the nonzero-equilibrium height \(z=2c-a\).
