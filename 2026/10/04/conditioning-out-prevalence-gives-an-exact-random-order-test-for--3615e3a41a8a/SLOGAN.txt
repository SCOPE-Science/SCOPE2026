Under the iid Bernoulli random-ranking null, conditioning AP@k on the observed number of relevant items removes the unknown prevalence exactly and yields a finite-sample randomization test. The resulting ordering fluctuation has asymptotic variance \(p^3(1-p)/k\), versus \(5p^3(1-p)/k\) before conditioning, so four fifths of the leading null variance comes from prevalence-count noise.
