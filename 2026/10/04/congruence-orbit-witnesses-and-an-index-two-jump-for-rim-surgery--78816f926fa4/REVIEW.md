# Review

## Correctness
PASS. Niu's exact stabilizer theorem reduces the problem to the oriented rim-class stabilizer or its sign-set stabilizer. The sign map has kernel the oriented stabilizer and is split by a hyperelliptic involution acting as \(-I\) on integral homology. For each odd prime, squared Dehn twists lie in the Rokhlin-form stabilizer and realize all symplectic transvection coefficients modulo \(p\); these transvections act transitively on nonzero homology classes. Orbit--stabilizer therefore gives the two finite congruence bounds.
