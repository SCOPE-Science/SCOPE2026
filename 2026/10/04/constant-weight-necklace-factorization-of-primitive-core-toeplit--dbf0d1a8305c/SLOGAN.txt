For a primitive degree-\(d\) finite-field core, the canonical exterior-degree Toeplitz recurrence polynomial factors exactly by cyclic orbits of weight-\(k\) binary necklaces. If \(\gcd(d,k)=1\), every factor has degree \(d\), and the canonical polynomial is irreducible only at the two edge degrees \(k=1\) and \(k=d-1\).
