# Contiguity census of the minimal six-vertex projective plane
## Finding
Let \(K\) be the six-vertex simplicial complex with facets \(012,013,024,035,045,125,134,145,234,235\), the minimal triangulation of \(\mathbb{RP}^2\). Exactly \(6396\) vertex maps \(K\to K\) are simplicial. Their contiguity classes are exactly one class of size \(6336\), containing every constant map and every non-automorphism, together with \(60\) singleton classes consisting precisely of the simplicial automorphisms. The large class is exactly the set of self-maps inducing zero on \(H_1(K;\mathbf F_2)\), while every automorphism induces the identity on this one-dimensional group.

Thus direct simplicial contiguity on this minimal triangulation is extremely rigid on symmetries: every non-automorphism lies in the single null class, while each of the \(60\) automorphisms is isolated from all other simplicial self-maps.

## Assumptions and scope
The complex \(K\) has vertex set \(\{0,1,2,3,4,5\}\) and the ten facets
\[
012,\ 013,\ 024,\ 035,\ 045,\ 125,\ 134,\ 145,\ 234,\ 235.
\]
A vertex map is simplicial when it sends every simplex to a simplex. Two simplicial maps \(f,g:K\to K\) are contiguous when \(f(\sigma)\cup g(\sigma)\) is a simplex for every simplex \(\sigma\subseteq K\); a contiguity class is a connected component of the equivalence relation generated by this relation.

The ten facets use all \(15\) edges, every edge lies in exactly two facets, and every vertex link is a five-cycle. Hence \(K\) is a connected closed triangulated surface with Euler characteristic \(6-15+10=1\), therefore a triangulation of \(\mathbb{RP}^2\). The archive-era reference of Köhler and Lutz lists the six-vertex projective plane as regular with automorphism group \(A_5\), hence of order \(60\).

## Proof
There are only \(6^6=46656\) vertex maps. The verifier tests every one against all ten facets and obtains exactly \(6396\) simplicial self-maps. Their image-cardinality distribution is \(6\) maps with one image vertex, \(930\) with two image vertices, \(5400\) with three image vertices, no maps with four or five image vertices, and \(60\) maps with six image vertices. The last \(60\) are precisely the vertex permutations preserving the ten facets, hence the simplicial automorphisms.

To compute contiguity classes without omitting long-range contiguous pairs, use the following elementary reduction. If \(f\) and \(g\) are contiguous, replace the values of \(f\) by the corresponding values of \(g\) one source vertex at a time. Every intermediate map sends each simplex into a subset of \(f(\sigma)\cup g(\sigma)\), hence is simplicial, and consecutive intermediate maps are contiguous. Therefore the full contiguity classes are exactly the connected components of the graph whose vertices are simplicial maps and whose edges join contiguous maps differing at one source vertex.

The verifier constructs this reduced graph exhaustively. It has \(6396\) vertices and \(40860\) undirected edges. Breadth-first search from a constant map reaches exactly \(6336\) maps. The remaining \(60\) maps are exactly the automorphisms, and each has no reduced-graph neighbor. By the reduction above, each is therefore a singleton full contiguity class. This proves the \(6336+60\) class decomposition.

For the homology statement, orientational signs are irrelevant over \(\mathbf F_2\). The verifier forms the edge and triangle boundary matrices and checks ranks \(\operatorname{rank} d_1=5\) and \(\operatorname{rank} d_2=9\), so \(H_1(K;\mathbf F_2)\) is one-dimensional. It uses the cycle carried by the nonface triangle \(014\) and the cocycle supported on edges \(01,02,13,24,34\); the cocycle vanishes on every triangle boundary and evaluates to \(1\) on that cycle. For every one of the \(6396\) self-maps the induced value on this generator is then computed directly. Exactly the \(60\) automorphisms act nontrivially, and over the one-dimensional field vector space this nonzero action is the identity. Thus the \(6336\)-map class is exactly the zero-action class.

## Verification
The complete finite check is implemented in `verify.py`. It reconstructs \(K\) from the displayed facets, verifies the surface and homology data, enumerates all \(46656\) vertex maps, reconstructs all simplicial self-maps, independently identifies automorphisms as facet-preserving permutations, builds the one-coordinate contiguity graph, computes its components, and evaluates the induced \(H_1(-;\mathbf F_2)\) action. `CENSUS.json` records the expected exact counts used as a compact replay target.

The decisive computation is exhaustive rather than sampled: every vertex map and every possible one-coordinate change is checked. The proof that one-coordinate changes generate every contiguity class is symbolic and does not depend on the computation.

## Relationship to prior work
Köhler and Lutz enumerate vertex-transitive triangulated manifolds and list the six-vertex projective plane with automorphism group \(A_5\). Barmak and Minian recall the standard definition of contiguity classes and emphasize that contiguity is strictly stronger than ordinary homotopy. The closest published database record located in the overlap search is published-finding corpus record `2026/9/9/SCOPE036`, which identifies the same ten-facet complex as the unique six-vertex pure two-complex carrying integral torsion and verifies its projective-plane topology, but it does not enumerate simplicial self-maps or contiguity classes.

Targeted searches using the phrases “six vertex projective plane”, “simplicial self maps”, “endomorphism monoid”, “contiguity classes”, “isolated automorphisms”, and the numerical signatures \(6396\), \(6336\), and \(60\) did not locate a source implying the stated census. The search result is evidence of noncoverage, not a proof of priority.

## Limitations
The finding concerns direct simplicial maps on this fixed minimal triangulation. It does not classify simplicial maps after subdivision, and it does not assert that contiguity classes coincide with ordinary homotopy classes; in fact contiguity is generally stricter. The originality assessment is limited by ordinary literature-search coverage, so an obscure prior computation of the same endomorphism census remains a residual risk.
