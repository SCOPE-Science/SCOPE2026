For the minimal six-vertex triangulation \(K\) of \(\mathbb{RP}^2\), the \(6396\) simplicial self-maps form exactly \(61\) contiguity classes: one \(6336\)-map class containing every non-automorphism, and \(60\) singleton automorphism classes. The large class is exactly the zero-action class on \(H_1(K;\mathbf F_2)\).
