On the minimal seven-vertex torus, all \(27{,}937\) non-automorphic simplicial self-maps form one null contiguity class, while the \(42\) affine automorphisms are singleton contiguity classes. Geometric realization collapses those \(42\) singleton classes to just six ordinary homotopy classes, so the direct simplicial maps exhibit \(43\) contiguity classes but only seven classical homotopy classes.
