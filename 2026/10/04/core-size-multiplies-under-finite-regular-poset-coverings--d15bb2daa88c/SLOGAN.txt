For every finite regular poset covering arising from an admissible finite-group coloring, Stong core cardinality multiplies by the sheet number because beat points lift and reflect fiberwise. Applied to the 13-point minimal model of \(\mathbb{RP}^2\), its universal double cover is a 26-point core of weak type \(S^2\), not finite-space-homotopy equivalent to the six-point minimal sphere model.
