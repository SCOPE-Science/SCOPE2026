# Same-model review

## Correctness

PASS. The source's endemic equation is a quadratic with \(C_0>0\) in the saturated-treatment regime and with \(C_2\) proportional to \(1-\mathcal R_0^2\). Vieta's formulas therefore give a complete positive-root classification. The exact parameter witness yields \(\mathcal R_0^2=29/30\), a negative linear coefficient, positive discriminant, and two positive roots. Direct substitution reconstructs two positive equilibria of the original five equations.

## Originality

PASS. Backward bifurcation and quadratic root tests are prior theory and are not claimed as new. The accepted claim is the correction to the specific 2020 Lemma 3.1, its exact discriminant formulation, and an admissible witness. Exact-source and semantic searches located no published correction. Related saturated vector-borne models use similar coefficient-sign reasoning but do not imply this source-specific result.

## Value

PASS. The source uses equilibrium multiplicity to discuss subthreshold endemicity and backward bifurcation. A generic claim of one positive subthreshold equilibrium is incompatible with the source's own polynomial; the corrected classification identifies exactly when there are two, one double, or no positive endemic roots and therefore repairs a structurally important part of the model analysis.

## Closest literature and limitations

Hu, Yin, and Wang (2019), DOI 10.1155/2019/1352698, is the closest fully inspected related saturated-rate vector-borne model; it has a different system and a different equilibrium polynomial. Lashari et al. (2013), DOI 10.12785/amis/070138, establishes backward bifurcation in another vector-borne model. The present finding does not classify stability of the two subthreshold equilibria or re-audit the later optimal-control calculation.
