Direct averaging of the printed Yang–Wei–Moroz ODE gives the positive-radius branch through \(a_1\omega^2+j_1\), not the combination used in the published theorem. The corrected branch is simple when \(a_1+d_1\ne0\) and is asymptotically stable exactly when \(a_1+d_1>0\), \(c_1>0\), and \(a_1\omega^2+j_1<0\), together with its real-existence condition.
