For commensurate widths \(d_1=ps\) and \(d_2=qs\), the surviving flat bands form the exact lattice \(B(2n+1)+(\pi r/s)^2\). Their branch-label density is asymptotically the fraction \(1/(p+q)\) of the two decoupled layer labels, and the decoupled spectrum is a direct-sum union rather than the two-transverse-energy sum printed in Eq. (3.3a) of the source.
