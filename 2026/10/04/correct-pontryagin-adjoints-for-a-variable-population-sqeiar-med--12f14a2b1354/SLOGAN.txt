In the variable-population SQEIAR media-control model, differentiating the stated six-state Hamiltonian requires common incidence terms caused by the state dependence of \(N=S+Q+E+I+A+R\). The published adjoint system omits these terms and also sends the susceptible quarantine costate through the wrong adjoint coordinate, so the printed optimality system must be corrected before it can certify the reported controls.
