For the Cui–Li four-dimensional flow, the nonzero equilibria have fourth coordinate \(\sigma r(de-ce-2a)/(em)\), not the published \(\sigma r(de-ce-2ad)/(em)\), and their exact characteristic polynomial differs accordingly. At the paper's baseline parameters each corrected nonzero equilibrium has unstable dimension three, so the published instability conclusion survives while its stated equilibrium coordinates and local spectrum do not.
