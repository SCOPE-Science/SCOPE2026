In the anisotropic two-qubit XY--DM model of Qin et al., thermal entanglement is governed exactly by \(q_B(\beta)=\cosh^2(K\beta)-(G^2/\Delta_B^2)\sinh^2(\Delta_B\beta)\): concurrence is positive precisely when \(q_B\notin[0,2]\). This yields a unique field at which an intermediate-temperature entanglement island disappears and shows \(T_c(B)\sim B/\log(2B/G)\), correcting the paper's stated field-independent critical temperature for \(J=1,\gamma=0.6,D=0.5\).
