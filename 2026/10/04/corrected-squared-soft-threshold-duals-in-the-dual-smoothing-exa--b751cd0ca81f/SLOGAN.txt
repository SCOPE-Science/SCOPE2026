For \(h_\lambda(x)=\|x\|_1+\frac\lambda2\|x\|_2^2\), the exact conjugate is the squared soft threshold \(h_\lambda^*(y)=\|\operatorname{soft}(y,1)\|_2^2/(2\lambda)\). The expanded Section 3.3 formula in arXiv:2512.08167v1 has the residual-square sign reversed, making the printed expression nonconvex and propagating an explicit error into two displayed decentralized dual examples.
