The final conclusive-teleportation construction in arXiv:quant-ph/0010113v1 is not a valid quantum instrument as printed: its state decomposition is not normalized and its displayed POVM matrices fail Kraus completeness. For the stated partially entangled basis, the repaired optimal unit-fidelity success probability is \(P_{\mathrm{succ}}^{\mathrm{opt}}=2(1-x^2)\), vanishing continuously in the product-basis limit.
