In the third environment-assisted reversal scheme of Xu et al., the published choice \(q=2d-d^2\) is not optimal: the unique concurrence maximizer is \(q=d\), giving \(C_{\max}=\sqrt{1-d}\) with success probability \(1-d\). The published point is strictly dominated in both concurrence and success probability for every \(0<d<1\), and the full efficient branch is \(0\le q\le d\).
