A countable coordinatewise product with infinitely many non-singleton compact factors is maximally non-deterministic: for every \(\varepsilon>0\), one pair of distinct points can remain \(\varepsilon\)-close under the entire group, so \(\operatorname{ND}_{\varepsilon}(X)=\operatorname{ND}(X)=\partial(G,\rho)\). Equivalently, a countable product action can be expansive only when all but finitely many factors are singletons, with the remaining factor actions expansive.
