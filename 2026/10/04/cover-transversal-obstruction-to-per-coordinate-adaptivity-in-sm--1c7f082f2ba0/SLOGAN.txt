SM3's cover compression has an exact combinatorial cross-coordinate vulnerability: the minimum number of off-target gradient spikes that can suppress coordinate \(i\) in one round is the transversal number of the cover sets containing \(i\). For the standard row-column and tensor-slice covers this number is exactly \(2\), and two spikes can make the coordinate's next update arbitrarily smaller than AdaGrad's despite zero prior gradient at that coordinate.
