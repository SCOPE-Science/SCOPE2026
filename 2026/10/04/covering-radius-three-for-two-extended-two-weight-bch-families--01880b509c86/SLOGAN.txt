Two infinite distance-four BCH families introduced in 2025 have covering radius exactly \(3\): the family with \(n=(2^{2s}+1)(2^s-1)\) and the family with \(n=(2^{2s}-1)/3\). For either parity extension, if \(r\) is the base redundancy and \(N\) the extended length, the complete coset-leader distribution is \(1,N,2^r-1,2^r-N\) in weights \(0,1,2,3\).
