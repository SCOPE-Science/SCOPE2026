# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

A finite reduced ring is a product of finite fields. In that product, the principal ideal of a vertex is determined exactly by its nonempty proper coordinate support, so cozero adjacency is support incomparability and each support class has size \(\prod_{i\in A}(q_i-1)\). Distinct supports have distinct open neighborhoods, making these precisely the false-twin classes. Their quotient is the incomparability graph of the proper nonempty Boolean lattice and has \(2^r-2\) vertices, recovering \(r\). For \(r\ge3\), its minimum-degree vertices are exactly singleton and co-singleton supports, forming two \(K_r\) cliques joined by a perfect matching. One side’s twin-class sizes recover \(q_i-1\); swapping the sides can satisfy both matched multiplicity equations only when all factors are \(\mathbb F_2\), where no ambiguity remains. For \(r=2\), the graph is \(K_{q_1-1,q_2-1}\). The packaged exhaustive verifier was inspected, and the symbolic reconstruction independently checks the side-swap argument.
