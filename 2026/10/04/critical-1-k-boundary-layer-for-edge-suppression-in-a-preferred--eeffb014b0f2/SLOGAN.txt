In the preferred-orientation rectangular strip, edge suppression persists through every shrinking resonance window with \(k|\delta|\to\infty\), but at the critical scale \(\delta\sim\xi/k\) the boundary-to-bulk mass ratio has an explicit finite Bloch-phase-dependent limit that is generically nonzero. This identifies \(1/k\) as the sharp local phase scale at which the fixed-window high-energy suppression theorem changes character.
