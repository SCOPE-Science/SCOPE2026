For a scalar least-squares mode of fixed-step PDAL/PDHG at fixed product \(r=\tau\sigma a^2\), the primal/dual ratio is asymptotically fastest exactly at the repeated-root boundary \(\beta_*=4a^2(1-r)\). At the Malitsky--Pock scalar linesearch ceiling \(r=\delta^2\), this becomes \(\beta_*=4a^2(1-\delta^2)\).
