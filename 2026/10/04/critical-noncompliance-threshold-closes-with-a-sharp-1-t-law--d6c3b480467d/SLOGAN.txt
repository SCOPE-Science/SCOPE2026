At the behavioral reproduction threshold \(Kq/(\nu+\delta)=1\), the recent noncompliance SIR models do not switch to instability: when the disease reproduction ratio is subcritical, the fully compliant disease-free equilibrium remains globally attracting but noncompliance clears only as \(X(t)\sim1/(qt)\). The infected and recovered classes still decay exponentially, so the critical slowdown is confined to the behavioral susceptible exchange.
