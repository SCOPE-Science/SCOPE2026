For odd primes \(p<50\), non-Frobenius Gauss-sum equalities on \(\mathbb F_{p^3}^\times\) occur exactly at \(p=11,23,37\). At \(p=37\) there are four extra equality pairs of orders \(28\) and \(42\) beyond the order-\(14\)/quadratic pure-sum class, including two order-\(28\) pairs with non-midpoint digit sums.
