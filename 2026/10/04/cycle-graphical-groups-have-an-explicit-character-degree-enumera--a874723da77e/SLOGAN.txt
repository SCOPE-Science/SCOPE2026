For every cycle \(C_n\), the rank distribution of its generic antisymmetric weighted adjacency matrix over \(\mathbf F_q\) has a closed Lucas-polynomial formula with one explicit full-support correction. For odd \(q\), this gives an explicit polynomial count of every irreducible character degree of \(\mathbf G_{C_n}(\mathbf F_q)\), answering Rossmann's character-enumeration question for the entire cycle family.
