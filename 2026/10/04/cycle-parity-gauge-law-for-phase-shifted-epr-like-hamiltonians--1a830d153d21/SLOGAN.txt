On phase-shifted EPR-like Hamiltonians, simple cycles split sharply by parity: every phase on an odd cycle is locally gauge-removable, while an even cycle carries one alternating flux whose first spectral signature occurs exactly in moment order \(m\) as \(2m!s^m\cos\Phi\). For \(C_3\), the complete phase-independent spectrum gives the exact gap \(2(1-s^2)\).
