For an extraspecial exponent-\(p\) group of order \(p^{2n+1}\), noncentral cyclic subgroups form \(p\)-element fibers over the projective points of the symplectic quotient, and commutation is exactly projective orthogonality. This gives \(\operatorname{csd}(G)=(p^2NR+4pN+4)/(pN+2)^2\) and the fixed-\(p\) limit \(1/p\).
