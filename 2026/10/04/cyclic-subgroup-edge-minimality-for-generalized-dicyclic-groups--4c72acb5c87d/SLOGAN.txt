For every generalized dicyclic group \(\operatorname{Dic}(A,y)\) with finite abelian kernel \(A\), the cyclic-subgroup graph is obtained from that of \(A\) by attaching \(|A|/2\) leaves at \(\langle y\rangle\). This proves cyclic edge-minimality against the cyclic group of the same order, with equality only for \(A\cong C_2\) or \(A\cong C_6\).
