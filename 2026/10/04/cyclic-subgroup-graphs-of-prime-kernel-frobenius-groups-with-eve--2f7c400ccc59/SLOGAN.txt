For every faithful Frobenius group \(C_r\rtimes C_m\) with \(r\) prime and even \(m\ge4\), the cyclic-subgroup graph is \(r\) copies of the graph for \(C_m\) glued at the trivial subgroup, plus the kernel as one leaf. Hence \(|E|=1+r\,e(m)\), which is strictly larger than the cyclic-group edge count of order \(rm\).
