# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof separates the three parameter regimes and reconstructs every implication. In finite dimension, centrality of \(z\) permits reduction to an eigenspace; the determinant of \(UV=\mu VU\) forces \(\mu\) to be a root of unity, and the nonresonant last relation then yields \(U+V=2I\). The displayed positive-operator identity forces \(U=V=I\), after which \(\mu=1\). At a resonance, explicit clock-shift matrices give a distinct finite-dimensional trace, with \(t=2\) handled separately by a distinct character. For \(|t|>2\), functional calculus makes \(z+z^*-t\mathbf1\) invertible and collapses the algebra to \(\mathbb C\). For interior nonresonant \(t\), the irrational rotation quotient gives the distinct amenable trace required by the cited construction.

PASS. The source presents a single explicit counterexample and emphasizes a general algebra-to-correlation construction. The exact parameter classification exposes the mathematical reason the example works: a dense arithmetic set of roots-of-unity traces is precisely where finite-dimensional uniqueness fails, while a dense complementary set supports the finite/amenable trace separation. This is a structural boundary, not a coefficient substitution exercise, and it shows that the source mechanism is arithmetically discontinuous under arbitrarily small changes of the defining real coefficient.
