In the memoryless-direction slice of AdamW, positive decoupled weight decay lowers the exact scalar-quadratic chattering threshold from \(c=2\) to \(c=2-\delta\). Above that boundary an exact symmetric two-cycle has amplitude \(a=\alpha/(2-\delta)-\varepsilon/h\); with \(\beta_2=0\) it is locally attracting, while below the boundary every trajectory converges to the minimizer.
