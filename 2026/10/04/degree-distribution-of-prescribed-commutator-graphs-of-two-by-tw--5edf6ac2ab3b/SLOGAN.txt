For \(R=M_2(\mathbf F_q)\), the degree multiset of the \(r\)-noncommuting graph depends only on whether \(r=0\), \(r\ne0\) is traceless, or \(\operatorname{tr}(r)\ne0\). For nonzero traceless \(r\), exactly \(q^3-q\) vertices meet the exceptional commutator fiber, because \(r\in[x,R]\) for nonscalar \(x\) exactly when \(\operatorname{tr}(rx)=0\).
