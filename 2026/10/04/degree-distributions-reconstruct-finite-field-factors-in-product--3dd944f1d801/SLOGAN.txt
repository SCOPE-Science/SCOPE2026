For a product \(\prod_i M_{n_i}(\mathbf F_q)\) with every \(n_i\ge2\), the trace-graph degree strata are indexed exactly by support size, and their total multiplicities are the elementary symmetric polynomials in \(q^{n_i^2}-1\). Consequently the graph reconstructs \(q\) and the complete unordered multiset of matrix sizes, giving an if-and-only-if isomorphism classification for this finite-field direct-product family.
