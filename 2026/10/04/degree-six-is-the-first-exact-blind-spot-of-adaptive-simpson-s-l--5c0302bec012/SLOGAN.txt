For the classical one-panel versus two-half-panel adaptive Simpson test, degree six is the first polynomial degree at which the local error signal can vanish while the true error is nonzero. The entire degree-six blind kernel satisfies \(4a_4+5a_6=0\), and the strictly positive witness \(p(t)=-t^6+\frac54t^4+\frac1{84}\) is accepted with value \(4/21\) although its exact integral is \(5/21\).
