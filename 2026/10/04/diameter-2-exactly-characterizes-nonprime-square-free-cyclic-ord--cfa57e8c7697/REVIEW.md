## Correctness
PASS. The two directions are logically separated. The square-free direction uses multiplicativity of \(\psi\). For the converse, the Bertrand-prime selection is valid for every repeated exponent \(\alpha\ge2\); the two selected subgroups are incomparable; and every possible common neighbor is exhausted by the lower/intersection and upper/join cases. The prime-power divisibility criterion excludes both cases.
