For the two-dimensional nearest-neighbor square lattice, the canonical odd threshold resonance has the exact dipole far field \(R_j(x)=i x_j/(\pi|x|_2^2)+O(|x|_2^{-3})\), while the canonical even threshold eigenstate has the quadrupole far field \(Q(x)=(x_1^2-x_2^2)/(\pi|x|_2^4)+O(|x|_2^{-4})\). This turns the source’s momentum-space threshold classification into explicit spatial localization laws.
