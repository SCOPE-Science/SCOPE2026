The three six-point minimal finite models of \(\bigvee^3 S^1\) do not have the same direct \(H_1\)-action repertoire. The complete-bipartite model and its opposite realize \(421\) integral homology endomorphisms, while \(K_{3,3}\setminus e\) realizes \(113\); in all three, an \(H_1\)-automorphism is induced exactly by a homeomorphism.
