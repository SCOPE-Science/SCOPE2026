In the published Hubei SIHR fit, \(H+R\) cannot equal cumulative confirmed cases because the recovered compartment also receives never-confirmed infections through \(\gamma I\). The printed fitted values make every post-start increment of \(H+R\) about \(10.0763\%\) larger than the model-consistent confirmation increment, so a consistent calibration needs a separate counter with inflow \(\delta I\) or different compartment semantics.
