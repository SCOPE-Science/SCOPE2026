A harmonic Bergman trace on any fixed ray is exactly the trace of one analytic shadow, so the recent \(p=2\) Riesz–Fejér estimate needs no condition \(f(0)=0\). The resulting bound keeps directional analytic/coanalytic cancellation and improves uniformly to \(\lambda_\alpha(2\|f\|_{a^2_\alpha}^2-|f(0)|^2)\).
