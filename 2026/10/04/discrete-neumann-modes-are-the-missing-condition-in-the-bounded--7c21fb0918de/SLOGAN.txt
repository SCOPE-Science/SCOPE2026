On a bounded habitat, a negative interval in the continuous Turing dispersion curve is not enough: an actual Neumann eigenvalue must fall inside it. For the 2026 supplemented predator--prey model, an exact rational parameter set satisfies the paper's \(R_5\) condition while every mode is stable on \(l=1/10\), yet modes \(k=2,3\) are unstable on \(l=1\).
