For \(W_{n,m}=K_1\vee nK_m\) with \(n,m\ge2\), dissociation sets split exactly into independent blade choices of size at most two when the center is absent and at most one outer vertex when the center is present. This gives \(D_{W_{n,m}}(z)=(1+mz+\binom{m}{2}z^2)^n+z+nmz^2\) and shows that maximal dissociation sets have only the two sizes \(2\) and \(2n\).
