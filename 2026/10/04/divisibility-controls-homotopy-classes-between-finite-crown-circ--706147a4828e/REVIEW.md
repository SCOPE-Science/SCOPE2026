# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof uses a lift to the infinite fence, an exact increment bound, explicit cancellation and zero-pair transport homotopies, and a separate rigidity argument at equality. The executable check covers representative divisibility and nondivisibility cases but is not used as the infinite proof.

## Originality
PASS. Farley and Currie--Visentin enumerate raw order-preserving maps. Cavallo--Sattler define crown winding and give the shorter-to-longer zero-winding lemma. Čukić--Kozlov give the closest analogue for graph Hom complexes of cycles, where edge collapse is forbidden. The inspected sources do not state the all-parameter finite-space homotopy classification. The previously published diagonal crown theorem is exactly the special case \(m=n\).

Residual risk: the graph-Hom analogy is close, and an unlocated explicit bridge could reduce originality.

## Value
PASS. The theorem gives a complete two-parameter homotopy classification for the standard finite circle models, with all realizable degrees and a sharp divisibility boundary for rigid maximal-winding maps.
