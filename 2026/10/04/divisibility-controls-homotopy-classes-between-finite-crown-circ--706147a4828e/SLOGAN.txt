For finite crown circle models, winding degree determines the homotopy class except at the exact divisibility boundary. When \(n\mid m\), the two maximal winding degrees split into \(n\) isolated maps each, giving \(\left|[\mathfrak C_m,\mathfrak C_n]\right|=2\lfloor(m-1)/n\rfloor+1+2n\,\mathbf 1_{n\mid m}\).
