On the terminal layer \(\mathbb P(1^r,a,a+r-1)\), a general anticanonical hypersurface is quasismooth exactly when \(a\) divides one of \(r\), \(2r-2\), or \(2r-1\). The exact count is a three-divisor-function formula, and cumulatively these quasismooth members have density \(3\log R/R+O(1/R)\), hence density zero.
