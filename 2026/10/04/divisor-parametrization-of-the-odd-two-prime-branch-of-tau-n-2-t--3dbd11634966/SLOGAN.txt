The odd two-prime branch of \(\tau(n^2)=\tau(\varphi(n))\) has an exact divisor parametrization: for each \(t\) with \(2\cdot3^t+1\) prime, its solutions are indexed by the divisors \(d>3\) of \(3(2t-1)\). In particular, the branch contains exactly \(\tau(3(2t-1))-2\) solutions for each such \(t\).
