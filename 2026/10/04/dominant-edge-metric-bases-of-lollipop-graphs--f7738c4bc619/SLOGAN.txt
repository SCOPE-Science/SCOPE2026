For every lollipop \(L_{m,\ell}\) with \(m\ge3\) and \(\ell\ge2\), every vertex cover already distinguishes all edges by edge distance. Thus \(\operatorname{Ddim}_e(L_{m,\ell})=m-1+\lfloor\ell/2\rfloor\), and the dominant edge metric bases are exactly the minimum vertex covers, with an explicit parity-dependent count.
