# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The nonidentity vertices split into the transversal clique \(Q^*\) and clique blocks \(B_L=(L\setminus\{1\})\times Q\), one for each order-\(p\) line \(L\le C_p^d\). Distinct \(B_L\) have no edges between them, while \(Q^*\) sees exactly the mixed-order vertices in each block. Each block contains pure order-\(p\) vertices with no external neighbors, forcing every dominating set to meet all \(t\) blocks. One mixed generator per block attains ordinary domination. A size-\(t\) selector has no selected neighbor across blocks, so total domination needs one additional \(Q^*\) vertex and \(t+1\) is sharp. In any paired dominating set, a matching edge can serve selected vertices from at most one required block, forcing at least \(t\) matching edges; two mixed generators per block attain \(2t\). The argument covers \(d=1\) and all distinct prime orientations because \(\varphi(pq)=(p-1)(q-1)\ge2\).

## originality

PASS

Bera--Dey--Patra--Sahoo determine ordinary domination for broad proper-power-graph families, including nilpotent groups with at most two prime divisors, so the ordinary value is treated as prior coverage rather than novelty. The surviving contribution is the exact total and paired domination classification for this mixed-prime family and the unbounded paired-versus-ordinary gap. The closest published SCOPE result found for total/paired proper-power domination concerns finite prime-power groups only and does not imply the present mixed-prime block/transversal matching obstruction.
