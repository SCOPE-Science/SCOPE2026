For \(G\cong C_p^d\times C_q\) with distinct primes \(p,q\), the proper power graph has \(\gamma=t\), \(\gamma_t=t+1\), and \(\gamma_{\mathrm{pr}}=2t\), where \(t=(p^d-1)/(p-1)\). Thus total domination stays one above ordinary domination while the paired-domination gap grows without bound with the number of order-\(p\) lines.
