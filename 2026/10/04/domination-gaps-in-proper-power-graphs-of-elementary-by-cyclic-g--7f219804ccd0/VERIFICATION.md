The verification reconstructs the group and graph from first principles for five representative parameter triples.

For each \\(C_p^d\\times C_q\\), `verify.py` enumerates all group elements, generates every cyclic subgroup by repeated addition, and joins two nonidentity elements exactly when one belongs to the cyclic subgroup generated by the other. It then searches all subsets through the claimed optimum for ordinary and total domination, and all even subsets through the claimed optimum for paired domination, using an exact recursive perfect-matching test.

The cases include cyclic order \\(6\\), rank-two elementary abelian factors over \\(\\mathbb F_2\\) and \\(\\mathbb F_3\\), and two different cyclic prime factors. The largest tested graph has \\(19\\) vertices.

The finite search is corroborative only. The arbitrary-rank proof is the block decomposition and the argument that each perfect-matching edge can account for selected vertices from at most one required order-\\(p\\) line block.
