If \(L_k\) is the smallest \(k\)-layered number, then along every unbounded set of existing \(k\)-layered levels, \(\log\log L_k\) is at least \((e^{-\gamma}-o(1))k\). Thus the minimal size is at least double-exponential in \(k\), strengthening the lower-growth direction of Jokar's 2022 conjecture.
