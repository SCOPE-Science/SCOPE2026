In the Kirchhoff-to-preferred-orientation square-lattice interpolation, the singular regime \(m t_m\to\tau\) has an explicit cell-band fraction \(F_\ell(\tau)=1-\frac{4}{\pi}\arctan(\min\{r,r^{-1}\})\), with \(r=4\ell/(\pi^2\tau)\). The spectrum pinches to zero Lebesgue fraction exactly at \(\tau=4\ell/\pi^2\) and re-expands on both sides.
