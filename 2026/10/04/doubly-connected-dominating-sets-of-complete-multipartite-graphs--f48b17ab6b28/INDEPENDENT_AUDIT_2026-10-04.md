# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In a complete multipartite graph a nonempty induced subgraph is connected iff it is a singleton or meets at least two parts, while a selected set dominates iff it meets at least two parts or equals an entire part. Their conjunction yields exactly the first support condition; applying connectivity to the complement yields c=1 or q(D)>=2. Inclusion-exclusion over disconnected selected sets, disconnected complements, their two-part intersection, and non-dominating singletons gives the polynomial. A fresh independent brute-force check of all 37 nondecreasing complete-multipartite profiles of orders 2 through 7 verified the literal definition, structural criterion, and every polynomial coefficient.

## originality

PASS

The 2024 full text covers the minimum doubly connected domination number for complete multipartite graphs, not the all-set classification or polynomial. The 2019 polynomial paper is the most plausible coverage source; accessible abstract/metadata name friendship and cactus-chain families, and authorized institutional retrieval remained unavailable (HTTP 403), so its unseen text is recorded as a residual risk rather than treated as evidence. Resultary found no separate complete-multipartite polynomial theorem.

## value

PASS

A complete feasible-set classification and exact domination polynomial for a standard graph family are natural reusable invariants; the result strictly strengthens the previously known scalar minimum and is not merely a recomputation of it.
