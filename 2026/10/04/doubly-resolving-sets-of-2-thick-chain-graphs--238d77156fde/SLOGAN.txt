For every 2-thick chain graph with at least two canonical levels, a vertex set is doubly resolving exactly when it omits at most one vertex per twin class, except for one possible forbidden omission pair at each end of the nesting order. Consequently \(\psi(G)=N-2p+\mathbf 1_{\{\beta_1=2\}}+\mathbf 1_{\{\alpha_p=2\}}\), and the full doubly-resolving-set polynomial has a four-term endpoint inclusion-exclusion formula.
