# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The operator and Fredholm determinant are taken from the primary source. The shift \(\Delta_{6\pm\eta}=\Delta_6\pm\eta\) is exact because \(\gamma\) occurs additively only in \(w_0\). The identity \(\varepsilon(k+\boldsymbol\pi)=6-\varepsilon(k)\) gives an exact lower/upper determinant reflection. The source's Theorems 3.8 and 3.9 provide, respectively, the unique threshold extremum and the square-root cusp needed for the ball and energy-profile limits. Monotonicity of the determinant in the exterior spectral parameter gives uniqueness of the relevant exterior zero.

## Originality
PASS. The closest inspected primary source is the Rasulov--Dilmurodov paper itself: it proves the critical no-exterior-eigenvalue theorem and threshold determinant expansion but does not state the \(\gamma\)-detuned bound-state pockets, their exact two-edge translation, cubic phase volume, or parabolic scaled energy profile. The 2006 Albeverio--Lakaev--Djumanova paper has a related lower-threshold quasi-momentum cusp for a different rank-one Friedrichs model; it does not imply the dual detuning theorem. The 2020/2021 Lakaev--Kurbanov--Alladustov work treats coupling-threshold eigenvalue expansions in a different two-dimensional rank-one model. Searches using pocket, detuning, virtual-level, Fredholm-determinant, and quasi-momentum aliases found no stronger statement that covers the claim.

## Value
PASS. The finding quantifies how a threshold resonance of zero quasi-momentum measure turns into a finite phase-space region of true bound states under a natural model parameter. The exact lower/upper duality, the \(\eta^3\) phase-volume law, and the \(\eta^2\) energy dome are structural threshold data rather than a routine evaluation at a chosen parameter.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest mathematical mechanism is the linear quasi-momentum Fredholm cusp in arXiv:math/0604282v1. The present model has a simultaneous lower and upper virtual level and an exact spectral reflection around energy \(9\) when the parameter is detuned symmetrically about \(\gamma=6\). The energy-profile statement is intentionally restricted to compact subsets of the limiting pocket interior, and no embedded-spectrum statement is made.
