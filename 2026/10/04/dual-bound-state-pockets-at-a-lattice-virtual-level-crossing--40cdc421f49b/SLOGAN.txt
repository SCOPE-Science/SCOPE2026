At the dual virtual-level point of the Rasulov--Dilmurodov lattice model, detuning \(\gamma\) below or above \(6\) creates exactly translated lower- and upper-edge bound-state pockets. Their quasi-momentum radius is \(R\eta+o(\eta)\), their volume is \((4\pi/3)R^3\eta^3+o(\eta^3)\), and their interior eigenvalue depth has an explicit parabolic \(\eta^2\)-profile.
