Every connected complete multipartite graph has outer mutual-visibility chromatic number \(1\) when complete and \(2\) otherwise. For the dual and total versions the only infinite obstructions in this family are stars: dual is infinite exactly on \(K_{1,n}\) with \(n\ge3\), while total is infinite exactly on \(K_{1,n}\) with \(n\ge2\); all other noncomplete cases have value \(2\).
