# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof has three independent exact steps: four unit complex numbers summing to zero admit an antipodal perfect matching; for \(N=2^m\), the congruence \(kx\equiv N/2\pmod N\) is equivalent to the single valuation condition \(v_2(k)=m-1-v_2(x)\); and after normalization by the minimum pairwise valuation, a four-set has parity split \(1+3\) or \(2+2\), which yields exactly the stated balanced-level alternatives. The explicit constructions realize every allowed pattern. Exact cyclotomic enumeration through \(N=32\) agrees with every part of the formula.

## Originality
PASS with residual indexing risk. Lam–Leung gives general vanishing-sum structure, Krahmer–Pfander–Rashkov gives finite-group uncertainty/rank results, and Filaseta–Finch–Nicol gives cyclotomic-divisibility restrictions for sparse \(0,1\)-polynomials. The inspected statements do not provide the fixed-four-point dyadic shell decomposition, classify the possible shell unions, or state the resulting exact zero-count spectrum. Searches also checked aliases involving mask polynomials, torsion zeros, four-term root-of-unity sums, dyadic Fourier zeros, and cyclic uncertainty.

## Value
PASS. Four-point indicators are the first support size at which several antipodal pairings can compete. The theorem replaces a support-size bound by a complete structural description of all zero frequencies and all possible zero counts for every cyclic dyadic order. The valuation-shell form is a reusable local invariant for sparse Fourier masks and sharply separates coefficient-one behavior from arbitrary sparse coefficients.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest inspected literature supplies the general cancellation mechanism or broad uncertainty bounds, not the complete shell geometry. The theorem is limited to four-element indicators on cyclic dyadic groups; a differently phrased equivalent result in specialized mask-polynomial or tiling literature remains the main residual originality risk.
