For four-point indicators on \(\mathbb Z_{{2^m}}\), every Fourier zero is an entire \(2\)-adic frequency shell forced by a perfect matching whose two differences have the same valuation. The attainable zero counts are exactly \(0\), \(2^r\) for \(0\le r\le m-3\), and \(2^r+2^s\) for \(0\le r<s\le m-1\).
