For dynamically equivalent positive resident states in the Seno–Goyal model, a rare juvenile-specific alien gains exactly the resident predator’s mean load \(\bar p\) in transverse Lyapunov exponent over an adult-specific alien at the same \(\alpha\). Thus \(\alpha_{J,c}=e^{-\bar p}\alpha_{A,c}\), analytically extending the source’s equilibrium stage ordering to recurrent oscillatory resident states at the local-invasion level.
