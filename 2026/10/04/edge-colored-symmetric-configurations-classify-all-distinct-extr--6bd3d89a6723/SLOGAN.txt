All-distinct constant-composition codes meeting the size bound \(|C|=n\) are exactly proper edge-colorings of Levi graphs of symmetric configurations \(n_w\). For \(w=6\), the known configuration spectrum closes the previously unresolved coding threshold at \(N_{\mathrm{ccc}}(\llbracket1,1,1,1,1,1\rrbracket)=34\).
