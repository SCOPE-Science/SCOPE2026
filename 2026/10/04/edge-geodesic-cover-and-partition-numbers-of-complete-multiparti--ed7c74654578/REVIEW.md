# Review of Edge-geodesic cover and partition numbers of complete multipartite graphs

## Correctness
PASS. Every two-edge geodesic is confined to one unordered pair of multipartite classes, which yields an additive lower bound. The three parity cases for \(K_{a,b}\) explicitly partition each block into \(\lceil ab/2\rceil\) geodesics, proving equality for both cover and partition numbers. The odd-part identity and dual-tightness criterion follow exactly. Independent exact optimization reproduces both minima on every tested multipartite type.

## Originality
PASS. The motivating 2022 preprint defines the edge-geodesic cover and partition quantities and proves their dual relation with edge general position, but its inspected full text treats torus graphs, hypercubes, and Beneš networks rather than complete bipartite or multipartite graphs. A 2018 survey covers vertex isometric-path covers of complete multipartite graphs, not edge covers. Targeted database and web searches for the exact specialization, sum formula, parity form, and partition alias found no equivalent statement. Residual risk remains for differently phrased or non-indexed work.

## Value
PASS. The edge-geodesic cover/partition problem is one half of the dual min-max framework in the motivating source. Complete multipartite graphs are a canonical family, and the result simultaneously solves both invariants, exposes an exact odd-part parity defect, and determines precisely when the associated edge-general-position dual inequality is tight.
