Complete multipartite edge-geodesic covering splits exactly over its complete bipartite blocks. Thus \(\operatorname{gcover}_e(G)=\operatorname{gpart}_e(G)=(|E(G)|+\binom{o}{2})/2\), where \(o\) is the number of odd-sized parts, and the edge-general-position dual bound is tight exactly for \(o\le1\).
