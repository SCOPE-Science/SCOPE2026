For every connected crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n\) with \(n\ge3\), the edge metric dimension is \(n-1\), and every basis chooses exactly one vertex from each matched pair except one omitted pair. Thus there are exactly \(n2^{n-1}\) edge metric bases, showing that deleting a perfect matching from \(K_{n,n}\) cuts the edge metric dimension from \(2n-2\) to \(n-1\).\n
