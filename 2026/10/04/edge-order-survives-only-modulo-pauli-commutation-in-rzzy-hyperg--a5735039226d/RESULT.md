# Edge order survives only modulo Pauli commutation in RZZY hypergraph states
## Finding

For each oriented 3-uniform hyperedge \(e=(i,j,k)\), write
\[
P_e=Z_iZ_jY_k,\qquad U_e(\theta_e)=\exp(-i\theta_e P_e).
\]
If two hyperedges \(e,f\) share vertices, let \(m(e,f)\) count the shared vertices at which one edge uses the target role \(Y\) and the other uses the control role \(Z\). Then
\[
P_eP_f=(-1)^{m(e,f)}P_fP_e.
\]
Therefore any adjacent transposition of two edges with even \(m(e,f)\) leaves the product of RZZY gates exactly unchanged. The state produced by the construction cannot encode a full total edge order; it is invariant under all swaps generated by these commuting pairs.

This obstruction is complete for the two star families written in arXiv:2609.30399v1. For the single-vertex-overlap star,
\[
P_k=Y_0Z_{2k+1}Z_{2k+2},
\]
so distinct generators share only qubit \(0\), with the same local factor \(Y\), and commute pairwise. For the double-vertex-overlap star,
\[
P_k=Z_0Z_1Y_{k+2},
\]
so distinct generators share qubits \(0,1\), with the same local factors \(Z,Z\), and again commute pairwise. Consequently, for any fixed weighted star with \(m\ge2\) edges, all \(m!\) total orderings of its edges generate exactly the same unitary and the same quantum state.

The phenomenon is not that order is always invisible. For the two-edge double-overlap chain,
\[
A=Z_0Z_1Y_2,\qquad B=Z_1Z_2Y_3,
\]
the shared qubit \(2\) carries \(Y\) in \(A\) and \(Z\) in \(B\), while qubit \(1\) carries \(Z\) in both, so \(AB=-BA\). If
\[
|\psi_{10}\rangle=e^{-ibB}e^{-iaA}|+\rangle^{\otimes4},\qquad
|\psi_{01}\rangle=e^{-iaA}e^{-ibB}|+\rangle^{\otimes4},
\]
then
\[
\langle\psi_{01}|\psi_{10}\rangle
=1-2\sin^2(a)\sin^2(b).
\]
Thus generic chain orderings are distinct; for \(a=b=\pi/4\), the overlap is \(1/2\) and the fidelity is \(1/4\).

## Assumptions and scope

The statement concerns the RZZY encoding explicitly defined in arXiv:2609.30399v1, with each oriented edge carrying two \(Z\) roles and one \(Y\) role. We compare total orders of the same labeled weighted edge set, so the edge weights remain attached to their edges while only the multiplication order changes.

The theorem establishes a necessary quotient of the proposed edge order. It does not claim that commutation equivalence is the only source of non-uniqueness: special weights, global phases, or additional state-dependent coincidences can identify still more ordered descriptions.

## Proof

On a shared qubit, equal Pauli factors commute, while \(YZ=-ZY\). Pauli factors on different qubits commute. Therefore each shared vertex with different roles contributes one minus sign when \(P_e\) and \(P_f\) are exchanged, giving
\[
P_eP_f=(-1)^{m(e,f)}P_fP_e.
\]
If \(m(e,f)\) is even, then \([P_e,P_f]=0\), and functional calculus gives
\[
\exp(-i\theta_eP_e)\exp(-i\theta_fP_f)
=
\exp(-i\theta_fP_f)\exp(-i\theta_eP_e)
\]
for all real weights. Hence an adjacent swap of a commuting pair preserves the full circuit operator, not merely its action on \(|+\rangle^{\otimes n}\). Repeating such swaps proves that the encoding factors through the quotient of edge words by these commuting transpositions.

For the single-vertex star, every pair of generators has role-mismatch count zero because the only shared vertex is the common \(Y\)-target. For the double-vertex star, every pair again has mismatch count zero because both shared vertices are \(Z\)-controls. This proves complete order blindness of the two star families.

For the displayed two-edge chain, \(A\) and \(B\) anticommute. Expanding the two products,
\[
e^{-ibB}e^{-iaA}
=
c_ac_bI-i s_ac_bA-i c_as_bB+s_as_bAB,
\]
\[
e^{-iaA}e^{-ibB}
=
c_ac_bI-i s_ac_bA-i c_as_bB-s_as_bAB,
\]
where \(c_x=\cos x\) and \(s_x=\sin x\). On \(|+\rangle^{\otimes4}\), the four vectors obtained by applying \(I,A,B,AB\) are mutually orthogonal because every nonidentity product among these Pauli strings contains at least one \(Y\) or \(Z\) factor and therefore has zero expectation in \(|+\rangle^{\otimes4}\). Taking the inner product yields
\[
c_a^2c_b^2+s_a^2c_b^2+c_a^2s_b^2-s_a^2s_b^2
=
1-2s_a^2s_b^2.
\]

## Verification

A deterministic standalone checker reconstructs the Pauli strings, verifies pairwise commutation for both star families, verifies anticommutation for the two-edge chain, evaluates the two chain orderings as explicit \(16\times16\) matrices, and checks the analytic overlap formula at several parameter values including \(a=b=\pi/4\). The checker is a finite consistency test; the general commutation theorem above is analytic.

## Relationship to prior work

arXiv:2609.30399v1 introduces the RZZY construction, states that noncommutation for certain adjacent-edge permutations enables an edge-ordered encoding, and then presents the two star families whose generators have the commuting forms used above. The present result shows that noncommutation somewhere in the class is not sufficient for a one-to-one encoding of full edge order: the star subclasses lose the order completely, and in general the encoding is invariant under every commuting adjacent swap.

General Pauli-circuit work such as arXiv:2305.10966 uses commutation of Pauli rotations for circuit optimization. That literature supplies the broad circuit principle but does not, in the inspected material, analyze this 2026 hypergraph construction, identify the exact role-mismatch parity rule for its edges, or state the complete star-order collapse.

## Limitations

This result does not classify every possible degeneracy of the RZZY representation. It proves a universal non-injectivity mechanism and an exact collapse for the two star families. A complete classification would also have to account for exceptional weights, global-phase periodicities, and any coincidences not generated by pairwise commuting swaps.
