Eight elements are the first finite Boolean carrier on which a unary expansion can fail the congruence extension property: all two- and four-element Boolean frames have CEP. On \(\mathcal P(\{1,2,3\})\), exactly \(1{,}216{,}800\) unary operations fail CEP, in \(205{,}724\) isomorphism classes; already \(13\) of the \(45\) normal closure operators fail, in four classes.
