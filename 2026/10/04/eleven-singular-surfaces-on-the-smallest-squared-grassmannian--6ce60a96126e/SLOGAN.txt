For \(\operatorname{sGr}(2,4)\), the reduced singular locus is exactly eight coordinate planes and three smooth quadrics. Generically along every component the transverse singularity is \(A_1\), and the eleven surfaces are the positive-dimensional fixed-locus images for the \((\mathbb Z/2)^3\) sign quotient of \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,4)\).
