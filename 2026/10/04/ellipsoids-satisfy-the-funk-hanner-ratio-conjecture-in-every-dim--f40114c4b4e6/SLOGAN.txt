For every centered ellipsoid \(E\) and every same-dimensional Hanner polytope \(H\), the Holmes--Thompson Funk-volume quotient \(V_E(R)/V_H(R)\) increases strictly with radius in every dimension \(n\ge2\). Hence the Faifman--Vernicos--Walsh reversed Bishop--Gromov Hanner-ratio conjecture holds strictly for ellipsoids.
