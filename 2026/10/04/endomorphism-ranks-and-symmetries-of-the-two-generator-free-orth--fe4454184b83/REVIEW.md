# Review

## Correctness

PASS. Freeness gives a bijection between endomorphisms and ordered pairs of elements, so there are exactly \(96^2\) endomorphisms and the image of the endomorphism determined by \((u,v)\) is exactly the subalgebra generated by that pair. The bundled closure computation enumerates all \(9216\) pairs and reaches the least operation-closed set in every case, producing the exact rank table.

The automorphism order is independently proved from the center. The unique central atom with a six-element interval fixes the \(\mathrm{MO}_2\) factor; the other four central atoms may be permuted. The four middle \(\mathrm{MO}_2\) elements may be permuted exactly by the centralizer of the complement pairing, a group \(C_2\wr S_2\cong D_8\). Hence the full orthomodular automorphism group is \(D_8\times S_4\) of order \(192\), matching the rank-\(96\) endomorphism count.

## Value

PASS. The primary source turns the two-generator free orthomodular lattice into an explicit finite coordinate object and itself points to a larger uncomputed symmetry group. The result completes that symmetry calculation and, more substantially, gives the entire compression spectrum of all \(9216\) self-maps. The rank table quantifies every possible size of a two-generated image, while the \(15\)-orbit reduction and exact generating-pair count provide natural invariants for further symbolic and computational work with the new coordinate calculus.

The closest source is Emori–Iriki–Khrennikov–Kondo (2026), which gives the decomposition \(F_2\cong\mathrm{MO}_2\times2^4\), normal forms, and the explicit remark that the full automorphism group is larger than the orthocomplement symmetry it studies. The automorphism factorization can be derived from standard product and center arguments once the decomposition is known, so the strongest originality component is the exhaustive endomorphism rank profile and its consequences. Older subalgebra-generation literature remains a residual search risk.
