For \(K_r=\operatorname{conv}(\mathbb B^3\cup\{\pm r e_1,\pm r e_2,\pm r e_3\})\), the illumination number is \(4\) for every \(1<r<\sqrt2\), but jumps to \(6\) at \(r=\sqrt2\). Thus the regular-octahedral cap-body extremizer is an endpoint phenomenon: any common inward radial perturbation of its six spikes drops to the three-dimensional minimum.
