The lag-L Fourier coefficient of each squared STFT slice contains exactly one window-overlap term because the window is supported on a short interval and the cyclic dimension exceeds twice the lag. This exactly recovers every endpoint signal product. Equal data then force a two-step ratio recurrence, and odd dimension plus coprimality makes that recurrence a single cycle, leaving only one global unimodular factor. An independent small odd-cycle reconstruction reproduced all endpoint products to numerical roundoff; the theorem itself follows from exact Fourier orthogonality.
