The proposed type-(1) phase reversal at \(b=a^2+2\) cannot hold on the stated closed rational domain: the designated endpoint pair \((0,1)\) always has \(\mathcal L([\phi_{a,b}(G(0))])=\sqrt{a^2+4}<\sqrt{b^2+4}=\mathcal L([\phi_{a,b}(G(1))])\). Thus Conjecture 5.1(II)(1) of arXiv:2609.32450v1 fails for every \(a\ge2\) and \(b\ge a^2+2\).
