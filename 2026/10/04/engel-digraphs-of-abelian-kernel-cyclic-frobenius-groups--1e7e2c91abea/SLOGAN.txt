For every finite Frobenius group \(K\rtimes C_m\) with abelian kernel, the entire Engel digraph is determined by the Frobenius partition: \(K^\#\) and the \(|K|\) conjugate-complement blocks are precisely the strong components, and every intercomponent arc points from a complement block to the kernel block. In fact \(\Gamma(G)=\Gamma_2(G)\), and the underlying graph is \(\mathrm K_{|K|-1}\vee |K|\,\mathrm K_{m-1}\).
