## Correctness

PASS. The random recursive tree is generated by independent uniform parent choices. A labeled vertex \(i\ge2\) survives as a leaf exactly when no later parent choice equals \(i\), giving probability \((i-1)/(n-1)\). Pairing this survival event with a later root attachment gives the exact covariance in (10), and the two accepted formulas follow by telescoping or triangular summation.

The root-degree variance is the variance of an independent Bernoulli sum. The leaf variance follows from the exact one-step leaf recursion. Exact enumeration through \(n=9\) independently reconstructs all local and global covariances.
