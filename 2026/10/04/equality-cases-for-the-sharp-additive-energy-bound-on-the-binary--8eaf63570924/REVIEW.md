# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The argument reconstructs the sharp induction for \(E(A)\le |A|^{\log_2 6}\), tracks every equality condition, and proves the only additional structural step: two proper affine Boolean cubes of the same rank with identical difference-multiplicity functions are translates. That rigidity follows because integer-linear independence gives unique coefficients in \(\{-1,0,1\}^m\), and the nonzero differences of maximal multiplicity recover exactly the signed generators. The converse energy calculation is exact. Exhaustive enumeration through dimension four gives no counterexample.

## Originality
PASS with a stated residual literature risk. The 2017 Kane--Tao source proves the sharp ordinary-energy inequality but does not state an if-and-only-if equality classification in the inspected theorem, lemma, or surrounding discussion. The 2021 discrete-cube energy paper broadens the energy theory without supplying this classification in the inspected material. The 2025 Crmarić--Kovač--Shiraki paper gives the recent generalized sharp bound and explicitly points to the full cube as an equality example; the inspected equality discussion does not classify all equality sets. Targeted searches for affine/Hilbert-cube extremizers, homometric formulations, and stronger covering statements found no statement implying the theorem.

## Value
PASS. Equality structure is a natural missing component of a sharp inequality. The theorem identifies every extremizer, immediately forces equality cardinalities to be powers of two, and turns an example family into a complete structural description. The rigidity mechanism is elementary and reusable, and the result is a natural precursor to possible stability questions.
