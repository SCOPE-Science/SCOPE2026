# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For compact invariant support, antiderivatives of arbitrary continuous one-coordinate test functions give \(\mathbb E[y\mid z]=0\) and \(\mathbb E[x+z\mid y]=y\). Coordinate stationarity gives \(\mathbb E[h(x)]=0\); the observables \(x^2/2\) and \((\beta y^2+z^2)/2\) give \(\mathbb E[xy]=\mathbb E[xh(x)]=\mathbb E[y^2]\). The piecewise-linear geometry has \(xh(x)\le0\) on \(|x|\le r\), with zeros only at \(-r,0,r\), and \(xh(x)>0\) outside. If no mass lies outside, the defect forces \(y=0\) and invariance reduces every support orbit to one of the three equilibria. Applying the same law to a periodic orbit forces one point outside; the zero-mean law for \(h(x)\) rules out remaining entirely in either exterior half-line, so the orbit also enters \(|x|<r\) and has at least two threshold crossings. The exact checker independently verifies the polynomial generator identities and the canonical value \(r=3/2\).
