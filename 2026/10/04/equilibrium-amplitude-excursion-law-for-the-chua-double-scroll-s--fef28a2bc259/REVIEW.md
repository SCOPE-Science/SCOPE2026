\mathbb E[x+z\mid y]=y,\qquad
\mathbb E[h(x)]=0.

\[
\mathbb E[y^2]=\mathbb E[xh(x)].
\]

This proves the equilibrium-mixture equality classification and the outer-excursion conclusion. The periodic crossing argument additionally uses continuity and the zero mean of \(h(x)\).
