For the three-segment Chua double-scroll system, every compact stationary state satisfies the exact defect \(\mathbb E[y^2]=\mathbb E[xh(x)]\ge0\). Unless it is a mixture of the three equilibria, it must place positive mass beyond the nonzero-equilibrium voltage amplitude \(r=(m_1-m_0)/m_1\); for the canonical slopes this is \(r=3/2\).
