Every compact stationary state of the cubic Clapp oscillator has its barycenter on the equilibrium line and obeys the exact law \(\int v_1^3\,d\mu=\rho^2\int v_1\,d\mu\). Any one-sided non-equilibrium ergodic state must place positive mass on both sides of the outer-equilibrium threshold \(|v_1|=\rho\).
