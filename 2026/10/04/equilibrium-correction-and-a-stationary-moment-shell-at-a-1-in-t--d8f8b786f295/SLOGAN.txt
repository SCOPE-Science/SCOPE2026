At \(a=1\), the Ray–Ghosh equilibrium cubic contains a spurious \(x=-1\) factor, while every compact invariant probability measure obeys the exact shell \(\int (x-1/2)^2\,d\mu=b+1/4\). Non-equilibrium ergodic recurrent states must visit both signs of \((x-x_-)(x-x_+)\), and every nonconstant periodic orbit crosses \(x_-\) or \(x_+\); for \(b\neq2\) both are equilibrium levels, whereas at \(b=2\) only \(x_+=2\) is an equilibrium.
