For the published 5D memristive flow, the equilibria form the entire line \(\mathcal E=\{(0,0,0,0,s):s\in\mathbb R\}\), and every compact stationary state obeys the exact shell law \(\langle(z-c/2)^2\rangle=c^2/4+c\langle\dot x^2\rangle/(a^2b)\). Any non-equilibrium compact recurrent state must therefore place positive mass outside the slab \(0\le z\le c\).
