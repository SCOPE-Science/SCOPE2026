For any two equivalent Hilbert norms \(p,q\) on a real Hilbert space, the intrinsic Hilbert distance in the cone of all equivalent norms is exactly \(3d_{\log}([p],[q])\). If \(q(x)^2=\langle Ax,x\rangle_p\) with \(m=\inf\sigma(A)\) and \(M=\sup\sigma(A)\), the exact triangular-defect comparison constants are \(m/\sqrt M\) and \(M/\sqrt m\).
