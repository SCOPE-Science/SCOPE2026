# Same-model review

## Claim
For the real two-dimensional Lorentz sequence space \(X=d^{(2)}(w,2)\) with weights \(w_1\ge w_2>0\), put \(a=w_2/w_1\). Then \(d_{\mathrm{BM}}(X,\ell_2^2)^2=C_{\mathrm{NJ}}(X)=C_{\mathrm{Pt}}(X)=2/(1+a)=2w_1/(w_1+w_2)\).

## Correctness
**PASS.** After normalizing \(w_1=1\), the Lorentz norm is exactly the maximum of two positive quadratic forms. The Euclidean sandwich gives all three upper bounds. For Banach--Mazur distance, the lower bound is not restricted to diagonal ellipses: an arbitrary positive-definite matrix \(A\) is considered, inclusion in both defining ellipses forces both diagonal entries of \(A\) to be at least \(1\), and the two diagonal boundary points of the Lorentz ball force \(\lambda^2\ge2/(1+a)\). Explicit pairs and triples attain the Jordan--von Neumann and Ptolemy bounds. The proof covers the complete parameter interval \(a\in(0,1]\).

**PASS.** This is a complete formula across every weight ratio in the \(q=2\) Lorentz plane, and it ties three standard but conceptually different Banach-space invariants by an exact identity.
