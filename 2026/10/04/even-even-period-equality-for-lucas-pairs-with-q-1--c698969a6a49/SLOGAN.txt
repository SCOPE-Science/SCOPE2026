For \(q=-1\), the period equality \(\pi_U(m)=\pi_V(m)\) holds whenever \(e_V(m)\) exists even when both the Lucas parameter \(p\) and modulus \(m>2\) are even, resolving the missing case of Fiebig--Mbirika--Spilker Question 5.3. The key obstruction is exact: even-indexed \(V_n\) have \(2\)-adic valuation \(1\), while odd-indexed terms have valuation \(\nu_2(|p|)\).
