For even \(k\), every cycle of Das's largest-prime-divisor map contains a prime at most \(\max\{\rho(k),k(\rho(k)-1)/2\}\), where \(\rho(k)\) is the least prime not dividing \(k\). Thus every even \(k\ge4\) with \(3\nmid k\) has at most \(\pi(k)\) cycles, replacing the generic \(k\sqrt{k}/2\) prime-locator scale by \(k\).
