# Review

## Correctness
PASS. For normalized coprime support \(0<A<B\), each equal-frequency pair in the \(r\)-th power has integer multiplicity difference \(t(-(B-A),B,-A)\). The sharp cutoff \(|t|B\le r\) and the unique residual-triple parameterization give every exact coefficient of the even moment. At the first nontrivial resonance \(r=B\), the cosine amplitude is \(2\binom{B}{A}\); the positive-row binomial sequence is strictly increasing up to the midpoint, giving support recovery up to reflection. An independent exact convolution enumeration agreed in 869 finite test cases, which corroborate but do not replace the all-parameter proof.

## Originality
PASS, with explicit limitations. The rank-one collision method and Parseval are classical. The first-collision cutoff for \(\{0,1,N\}\) is stated in arXiv:2609.09740v2 and is not new. Neuwirth's general trinomials concern \(L^\infty\) maximum modulus, while Krenedits treats special signed three-term majorant comparisons. The additional claim is the exact coefficient polynomial for every \(\{0,A,B\}\) with coprime gaps and the inverse determination of \(A\) from the first response width; no independent direct prior statement was identified. Earlier implicit formulae or arXiv preprint variants remain priority risks.

## Scientific value
PASS. Determining when coefficient phase first becomes visible in an even Fourier moment is a natural inverse-spectral question, and the exact threshold and oscillation width recover the normalized three-point support up to reflection. This is a narrow result: non-even exponents, non-unimodular coefficients and minimization of higher-harmonic moment polynomials are not addressed.
