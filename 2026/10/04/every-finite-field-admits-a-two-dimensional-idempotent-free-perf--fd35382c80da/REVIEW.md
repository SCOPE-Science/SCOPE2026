# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The structure matrix has determinant \(-1\), hence \(E_d^2=E_d\) over every field. The idempotent equations reduce exactly to \(d=b^{-1}-b^2\) for \(b\ne0\), while \(b=0\) forces the zero element. Because \(K^\times\) has one fewer element than \(K\), at least one parameter is omitted. The one-dimensional lower bound is exact.

PASS. The result identifies a sharp field boundary for a recent evolution-algebra conjecture: every finite field already has a counterexample in the smallest possible positive dimension, including characteristic \(2\). The parameter-omission mechanism is uniform and structurally explains why finite fields differ from \(\mathbb C\).
