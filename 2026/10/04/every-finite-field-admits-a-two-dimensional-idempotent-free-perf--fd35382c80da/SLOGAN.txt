Over every finite field \(K=\mathbb F_q\), some parameter \(d\notin\{b^{-1}-b^2:b\in K^\times\}\) makes \(e_1^2=e_2\) and \(e_2^2=e_1+d e_2\) a perfect evolution algebra with no non-zero idempotent. Thus the minimum dimension of an idempotent-free non-solvable evolution algebra over any finite field is exactly \(2\).
