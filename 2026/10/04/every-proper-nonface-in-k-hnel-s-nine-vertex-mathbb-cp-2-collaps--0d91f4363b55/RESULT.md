# Every proper nonface in Kühnel's nine-vertex \(\mathbb{CP}^{2}\) collapses to a tetrahedral sphere
## Finding
Let \(K\) be Kühnel's nine-vertex triangulation of \(\mathbb{CP}^{2}\). For every nonempty proper vertex set \(S\subsetneq V(K)\), the induced subcomplex \(K[S]\) is a simplex when \(S\) is a face of \(K\), and when \(S\) is not a face, \(K[S]\) simplicially collapses to the tetrahedral sphere \(\partial\Delta^{3}\). Hence every proper induced subcomplex is either contractible or simple-homotopy equivalent to \(S^{2}\), with the dichotomy decided exactly by face membership.

There are exactly \(255\) proper nonfaces. By cardinality, their counts are \(36,90,84,36,9\) on \(4,5,6,7,8\) vertices, respectively. The supplied collapse certificate reduces every one of them to a four-vertex tetrahedral boundary. The certified sequence lengths are \(0\) for the \(36\) four-vertex cases, \(7\) for the \(90\) five-vertex cases, \(18,19,20,21\) for respectively \(9,27,27,21\) of the six-vertex cases, \(40\) for all \(36\) seven-vertex cases, and \(72\) for all \(9\) eight-vertex cases.

## Assumptions and scope
The complex \(K\) is the standard nine-vertex triangulation described by the two \(H_{54}\)-orbits of the facets \(\langle12456\rangle\) and \(\langle12459\rangle\), where \(H_{54}\) is generated by \(\alpha=(147)(258)(369)\), \(\beta=(123)(465)\), and \(\gamma=(12)(45)(78)\). This reconstruction has face vector \( (9,36,84,90,36)\) and satisfies complementarity. The finding concerns only nonempty proper induced subcomplexes of this fixed combinatorial model; it makes no assertion about arbitrary triangulations of \(\mathbb{CP}^2\).

An elementary collapse here means deletion of a pair \(\tau\subset\sigma\) in which \(\sigma\) is maximal, \(\tau\) has codimension one in \(\sigma\), and no other maximal simplex contains \(\tau\).

## Proof
If \(S\) is a face of \(K\), simplicial closure implies that every nonempty subset of \(S\) is a face, so the induced complex \(K[S]\) is the full simplex on \(S\) and is contractible.

For a proper nonface \(S\), `collapse_cert.json` gives an explicit finite list of elementary collapse pairs. `verify.py` reconstructs \(K\) from the published generators and seed facets, forms the induced complex \(K[S]\), and checks every pair against the current complex: the upper simplex is maximal, the lower simplex is a codimension-one face, and that lower simplex lies in no other maximal simplex. It then deletes exactly that pair. At the end it checks that the remaining maximal faces are the four triangles of the boundary of a three-simplex.

The verification ranges over all \(2^9-2=510\) nonempty proper vertex sets. It independently checks complementarity and obtains \(255\) face sets and \(255\) nonfaces; the certificate contains exactly one entry for each of the latter. Therefore no proper vertex set is omitted. Elementary collapse preserves simple-homotopy type, so each certified nonface-induced complex is simple-homotopy equivalent to \(\partial\Delta^3\cong S^2\).

## Verification
Run `python verify.py` in the directory containing `verify.py` and `collapse_cert.json`. The expected terminal line is:

`VERIFY_OK facets=36 face_vector=9,36,84,90,36 proper_faces=255 proper_nonfaces=255 nonfaces=4:36,5:90,6:84,7:36,8:9 collapse_lengths=4:0x36;5:7x90;6:18x9,19x27,20x27,21x21;7:40x36;8:72x9`

The verifier does not infer collapsibility from homology. It checks every elementary collapse in every sequence directly against the evolving simplicial complex.

## Relationship to prior work
Kühnel and Banchoff introduced the nine-vertex model, and later descriptions give the exact \(H_{54}\)-orbit presentation used here. Bagchi and Datta prove the complementarity property and the face vector; their 2001 paper also explicitly notes that the complement of any facet induces the standard four-vertex two-sphere. Thus the \(36\) smallest target spheres are already visible in the literature.

The new statement is the global collapse dichotomy for all proper induced subcomplexes. Complementarity determines face versus nonface, while tightness controls inclusion maps in homology; neither inspected statement supplies elementary collapse sequences for the \(90+84+36+9\) larger nonface-induced subcomplexes or a simple-homotopy classification of every proper induced subcomplex. Focused searches for equivalent formulations and exact collapse tables found no covering result.

## Limitations
This is a finite theorem about one canonical triangulation. The certificate proves existence of the displayed collapse reductions but does not claim uniqueness of a reduction order, shellability, or an analogous theorem for other triangulations of \(\mathbb{CP}^2\). The detailed Arnoux--Marin crystallographic treatment is a residual literature risk because its full PDF was not successfully retrieved in this run; inspected later exact-object sources do not attribute an induced-collapse theorem to it.
