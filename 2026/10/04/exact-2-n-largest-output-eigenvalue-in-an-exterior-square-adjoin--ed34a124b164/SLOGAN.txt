For the adjoint-twist summand \(V_{(2,1^{n-2},0)}\subset\wedge^{n-2}\mathbb C^n\otimes\wedge^2\mathbb C^n\), \(n\ge5\), the exact largest output eigenvalue of either complementary channel is \(2/n\), while coherent inputs attain only \(1/(n-2)\). The maximizers are precisely the rank-two-projection directions \(P-(2/n)I\), up to symmetry and phase.
