The first asymmetric finite case of Kaneko--Stoll's \(k=3\) binary-weight problem closes completely: if odd factors have weights \(2\) and \(3\), then a weight-\(3\) product occurs only for \((a,b)=(3,7)\) or \((5,7)\). The published global bound reduces the proof to an exhaustive \(119\)-case exponent census.
