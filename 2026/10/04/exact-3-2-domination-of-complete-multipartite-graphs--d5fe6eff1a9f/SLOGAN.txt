For \(G=K_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}\), the Cody--Detore \(3,2\)-domination number is \(N\) only for complete graphs, is \(2\) exactly when \(r=2\) or a part has order two, and is \(3\) otherwise. All minimum sets are classified by the part-occupancy profiles \(2\), \(3\), \(2+1\), and, only for three parts, \(1+1+1\), giving an exact enumerator.
