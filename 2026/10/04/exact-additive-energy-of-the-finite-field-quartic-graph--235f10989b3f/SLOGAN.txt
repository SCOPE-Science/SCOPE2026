For the finite-field quartic graph \(C_4=\{(t,t^4):t\in F_q\}\), the additive energy is exactly \(3q^2-5q+3\) when \(q\equiv1\pmod3\) and \(3q^2-q-1\) when \(q\equiv2\pmod3\). Equivalently, the constant-input fourth extension moment has an exact arithmetic split detected by the presence of cube roots of unity.
