On a quadratic with spectrum \(\{\mu,1\}\), the practical residual-ratio adaptive gradient method kills the top eigenspace in its first normalized step but still identifies the original minimax step \(2/(1+\mu)\). Its rate estimate obeys the exact scalar law \(q_{k+1}=1-\mu-\mu q_k\), so the parameter error alternates and contracts by exactly \(\mu\) per update.
