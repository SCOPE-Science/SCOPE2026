For circumellipses of a fixed parallelogram, the normalized area excess is exactly hyperbolic in an affine-invariant half-log anisotropy: \(\operatorname{area}(E)/\operatorname{area}(Q)=(\pi/2)\cosh\delta\). This yields the sharp global stability bound \(\operatorname{area}(E)/\operatorname{area}(Q)-\pi/2\ge(\pi/4)\delta^2\).
