# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For a fixed antecedent family P, strong material implication transforms a consequent Q into the complement of P union Q; weak material implication additionally adjoins the empty team. Uniform preservation of union closure for every union-closed Q is therefore equivalent to the complement of P being union closed in the relevant nonempty sense, which is exactly nonempty-downward closure of P. Dually, uniform strong intersection preservation is equivalent to proper-upward closure of P, while adjoining the empty team weakens this to nonempty-proper-upward closure. The necessity witnesses follow by choosing the smallest closure-stable consequents exposing a violating pair. When P is itself union or intersection closed, these conditions force the stated principal ideal/upset or punctured forms. An independent full enumeration for three base points reproduced all four equivalences over every antecedent and every closure-stable consequent.

## originality

PASS

Barbero and Yang explicitly prove that strong and weak material implication fail to preserve union and intersection closure in general, and their appendix supplies counterexamples. Their complete primary text does not classify which fixed antecedent team propositions make preservation hold uniformly over all closure-stable consequents. The audited result supplies exactly that antecedent boundary, including the strong/weak intersection distinction and principal ideal/upset corollaries. Current Resultary searches under material implication, closure preservation, fixed antecedent and team-proposition terminology found no equivalent classification.

PASS
