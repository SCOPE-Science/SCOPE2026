A regular hyperbolic Reuleaux odd-\(n\)-gon of width \(d\) has the exact arc angle \(\alpha=\arccos((\sinh^2(d/2)+\cos(\pi/n))/(1+\sinh^2(d/2)))\), hence \(L=n\alpha\sinh d\) and \(A=n\alpha(1+\cosh d)-2\pi\). For fixed width, these converge to the perimeter and area of the hyperbolic disk of radius \(d/2\).\n
