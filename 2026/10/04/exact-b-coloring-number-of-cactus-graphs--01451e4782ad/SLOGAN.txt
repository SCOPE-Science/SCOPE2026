For every finite simple connected cactus \(G\) with at least one edge, \(q_B(G)=\max\{\Delta(G),4\}\) when a \(C_4\) is present, \(q_B(G)=3\) when \(G\) is an odd cycle, and \(q_B(G)=\Delta(G)\) otherwise. In particular, every cactus with \(\Delta(G)\ge4\) is degree-optimal for \(B\)-coloring, and the threshold four is sharp.
