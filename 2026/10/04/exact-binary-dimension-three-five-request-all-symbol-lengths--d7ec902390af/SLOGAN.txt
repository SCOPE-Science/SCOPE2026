For binary dimension \(3\), five-request all-symbol PIR and batch codes have the same exact minimum length: \(\operatorname{ASP}(3,5,2)=\operatorname{ASB}(3,5,2)=10\). Exact exhaustion rules out every length at most \(9\), while a ten-column witness serves all \(252\) five-request multisets of its stored symbol types.
