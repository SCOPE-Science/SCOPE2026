# Review

Correctness: PASS. Metric and graph preservation make distance plus adjacency an invariant of an ordered off-diagonal pair. The primary source supplies strict homogeneity, so two induced two-point ultrametric graph structures with the same invariant are carried to one another by a global automorphism. Its finite-extension property realizes both adjacency choices at every canonical distance \(2^{-k}\). Singleton homogeneity gives one diagonal orbit. These facts prove the classification and the point-stabilizer consequence.

Originality: PASS, with an explicit priority caveat. Exact web searches, broader searches on automorphism orbits of homogeneous ultrametric graphs, and the semantic finding index did not locate this binary orbital decomposition. The 2023 primary paper provides the construction and homogeneity but, in the checked material, does not enumerate its pair orbits. The 2015 pure-ultrametric paper proves an arity-at-most-\(2\) theorem for isometry groups, but it does not include the graph relation or the two graph-status orbits on each sphere. Because the argument is a short consequence of homogeneity, folklore duplication remains possible.

Value: PASS. The result gives the first natural permutation-group invariant of the paper's Baire-space graph in closed form. It shows precisely how the extra random graph relation modifies the pure metric symmetry: it doubles every nonzero metric sphere into adjacent and nonadjacent orbitals, while the infinitely many metric scales force countably infinite permutation rank. This is a compact quantitative bridge between the inverse-limit Fraïssé construction and its automorphism group.
