For binary length-\(n\) words, the exact number of distinct adjacent-pair multispectra is \(3m^2-m+2\) for \(n=2m\) and \(3m^2+2m+2\) for \(n=2m+1\), so this is also the exact optimum size of a read-length-two reconstruction code. The singleton multispectrum classes are precisely the words with at most one transition, plus the two alternating words when \(n\ge4\) is even.
