# Exact birational threshold in the seventh-order weighted-plane family
## Finding
For every even integer \(n\ge2\), put
\[
a=n^2-1,\qquad b=n^2+1,\qquad c=2n^3,
\]
and consider
\[
X_n=\mathbf P(a,b,c),\qquad H_n=\mathcal O_{X_n}(1).
\]
Then the smallest positive integer \(m\) for which the complete linear system
\[
|mH_n|
\]
defines a birational rational map is
\[
m_{\mathrm{bir}}(X_n,H_n)
=(n+1)(n^3-1)
=n^4+n^3-n-1.
\]
The source proves that
\[
\varepsilon_n=\operatorname{mld}(X_n)=\frac1n.
\]
Consequently
\[
m_{\mathrm{bir}}(X_n,H_n)\sim \varepsilon_n^{-4}.
\]
This gives the exact threshold requested for the source's seventh-order family and places its actual exponent strictly between the published necessary exponent \(7/2\) and sufficient exponent \(9/2\).

## Assumptions and scope
Work over \(\mathbf C\). The integer \(n\) is even and at least \(2\). The weights \(a,b,c\) are pairwise coprime, as in the source, so \(X_n\) is the stated well-formed weighted projective plane.

For an integer \(m\ge0\), let
\[
S_m=\{(x,y,z)\in\mathbf Z_{\ge0}^3:ax+by+cz=m\}.
\]
These triples index the monomial sections of \(\mathcal O_{X_n}(m)\).

On the dense torus of \(X_n\), ratios of degree-\(m\) monomials are the characters whose exponent vectors lie in the subgroup generated by
\[
S_m-S_m
\]
inside
\[
L=\{(r,s,t)\in\mathbf Z^3:ar+bs+ct=0\}.
\]
Thus \(|mH_n|\) is birational precisely when those exponent differences generate \(L\). In particular, if \(S_m\) is contained in an affine line, the map cannot be birational.

## Proof
First note that
\[
u=(b,-a,0),\qquad v=(-n,-n,1)
\]
belong to \(L\), and
\[
u\times v=-(a,b,c).
\]
Because \((a,b,c)\) is primitive, \(u,v\) form a \(\mathbf Z\)-basis of \(L\).

We prove that \(S_m\) is affinely collinear whenever
\[
m<M:=(n+1)(n^3-1).
\]

For \((x,y,z)\in S_m\), define
\[
D=x-y,\qquad T=x+y+2nz.
\]
The weighted-degree equation becomes
\[
m=n^2T-D.
\]
For two factorizations of the same \(m\), subtracting this identity gives
\[
D-D'=n^2(T-T').
\]
Since
\[
T-T'=(x-x')+(y-y')+2n(z-z')
\]
and
\[
D-D'=(x-x')-(y-y'),
\]
the integer \(T-T'\) is even. Hence all values of \(D\) occurring in \(S_m\) lie in one arithmetic progression with step \(2n^2\).

Set
\[
f(D)=n^2|D|-D.
\]
Because \(T\ge x+y\ge|D|\),
\[
m=n^2T-D\ge f(D).
\]
If a fixed \(D\)-level contains two distinct points of \(S_m\), then their \(z\)-coordinates differ. One of them has \(z\ge1\), so
\[
T\ge |D|+2n
\]
and therefore
\[
m\ge f(D)+2n^3.
\]

Suppose \(S_m\) is not affinely collinear. If it contains points on at least three distinct \(D\)-levels, the extreme levels differ by at least \(4n^2\). Since \(f\) is V-shaped with slopes \(-(n^2+1)\) and \(n^2-1\),
\[
\min_D\max\{f(D),f(D+4n^2)\}
=2(n^4-1).
\]
For \(n\ge2\),
\[
2(n^4-1)-M=(n-1)(n^3+1)>0,
\]
so this is impossible when \(m<M\).

It remains to consider exactly two \(D\)-levels. They must be adjacent, since separation at least \(4n^2\) already gives the previous contradiction, and one level must contain at least two points. If the lower level \(D\) is repeated, then
\[
m\ge
\max\{f(D)+2n^3,\ f(D+2n^2)\}.
\]
The minimum occurs with the two arguments equal in the interval \([-2n^2,0]\), at
\[
D=-n^2+n+1,
\]
and equals
\[
(n^2-1)(n^2+n+1)=M.
\]
If the upper level is repeated instead, the analogous minimum is
\[
(n^2+1)(n^2+n-1)>M.
\]
Thus no noncollinear \(S_m\) exists below \(M\), and \(|mH_n|\) is not birational for \(m<M\).

At \(m=M\), the following three monomial exponent vectors belong to \(S_M\):
\[
s_0=(n,n^2-1,0),\qquad
s_1=(n^2+n+1,0,0),\qquad
s_2=(0,n^2-n-1,1).
\]
Their differences are
\[
s_1-s_0=u,\qquad s_2-s_0=v.
\]
Since \(u,v\) are a \(\mathbf Z\)-basis of \(L\), the corresponding monomial ratios generate the full function field of the dense torus. Hence \(|MH_n|\) is birational. This proves the exact threshold.

Finally, the source gives \(\varepsilon_n=1/n\), so
\[
M=n^4+n^3-n-1\sim n^4=\varepsilon_n^{-4}.
\]

## Verification
The accompanying `verify.py` checks all displayed polynomial identities, the two minimizer values, the basis cross product, and the comparison
\[
2(n^4-1)>M.
\]
It also exhaustively enumerates the monomial fibers for the even values
\[
2\le n\le12
\]
and confirms that the first noncollinear fiber occurs exactly at \(M\), where the displayed two differences have lattice index one.

The finite enumeration is a consistency check, not the proof for arbitrary \(n\). The infinite statement follows from the symbolic \(D\)-level argument above.

## Relationship to prior work
Bie's paper introduces exactly this weighted-projective family and proves
\[
\varepsilon_n=\frac1n,\qquad
H_n^2=\frac{1}{2n^3(n^4-1)}.
\]
Its Remark 10.4 derives only the necessary estimate
\[
|mH_n|\ \text{birational}\Longrightarrow
m\ge\sqrt{2n^3(n^4-1)}
\asymp\varepsilon_n^{-7/2}.
\]
The same paper gives a sufficient rank-one order \(\varepsilon_n^{-9/2}\), explicitly says the lower estimate is not a calculation of the actual threshold, and asks for that actual threshold in its further-questions section.

The calculation here answers that question on the stated family:
\[
m_{\mathrm{bir}}=n^4+n^3-n-1
\asymp\varepsilon_n^{-4}.
\]
Targeted searches for the weight triple, this closed formula, the exact-threshold wording, and equivalent monomial-generation formulations did not locate a prior statement of the result.

## Limitations
The theorem concerns only the specific seventh-order weighted-projective family from the source. It does not improve the source's uniform birationality theorem for all rank-one \(\varepsilon\)-lc surfaces and does not determine a universal optimal exponent.

The argument determines the first birational multiple, not the base locus, degree, or singularities of the image for smaller multiples. For \(m<M\), it proves non-birationality by showing the monomial exponent fiber is affinely collinear; it does not classify all lower-dimensional images.

The originality search cannot exclude an unindexed calculation of this exact numerical-semigroup fiber in older weighted-projective literature, although the initiating paper itself states that the actual threshold for this family is undetermined.
