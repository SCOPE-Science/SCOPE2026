For the real plane \(\ell_4^2\), the Du–Li Birkhoff i-angle cosine constant is exactly \(C_B=\sqrt{1/6-u_0^2/2}=0.2873902713995240563\ldots\), where \(u_0\) is the unique root in \((0,1)\) of \(9u^4+18u^3+3u^2-2=0\). The computation reduces every unit Birkhoff-orthogonal pair to a one-variable maximization on \([0,1]\).\n
