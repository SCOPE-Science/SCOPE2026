## correctness

PASS

Under exchange symmetry the cleared equilibrium equations have common coefficients A=d/K, B=d/L and C=d-a1/K. Subtracting them factors exactly as (x1-x2)(A(x1+x2)+C)=0. The off-diagonal branch therefore has sum S=a1/d-K and product P=a0/[d(1/L-1/K)]. Two distinct positive off-diagonal equilibria exist exactly when S>0, P>0 and S²>4P, which is the stated triple condition a1>dK, L<K and discriminant positivity. The diagonal equation has exactly one positive root, so the strict region has exactly three positive equilibria. At equality the two off-diagonal roots coalesce on the diagonal; outside the strict region they are absent. Sontag's Theorem 1 then converts exactly-three positive equilibria into the global two-node/one-saddle bistable phase portrait. Independent symbolic subtraction rechecked the key factorization.
