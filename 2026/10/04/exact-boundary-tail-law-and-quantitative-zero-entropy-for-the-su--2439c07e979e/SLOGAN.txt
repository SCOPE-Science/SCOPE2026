For the summable degree-two Blaschke sequence with zero topological entropy, the forward limit satisfies the exact tail law \(\|F-F_n\|_{L^2(m)}^2=2\bigl(1-\prod_{j>n}\lambda_j\bigr)\). This gives an explicit finite-horizon entropy bound and proves that the non-autonomous metric entropy is zero, while isolating a quantitative tail scale not supplied by the general qualitative zero-entropy theory.
