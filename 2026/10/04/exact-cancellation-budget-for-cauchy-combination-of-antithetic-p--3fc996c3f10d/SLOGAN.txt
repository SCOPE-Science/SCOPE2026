For independent antithetic p-value pairs, the weighted Cauchy statistic is exactly Cauchy with scale \(A=\sum_j|w_{j,+}-w_{j,-}|=1-2\sum_j\min(w_{j,+},w_{j,-})\). The usual Cauchy calibration is exact exactly when no pair is weighted on both sides, strictly conservative for \(0<A<1\), and completely nonrejecting when every pair is internally balanced.
