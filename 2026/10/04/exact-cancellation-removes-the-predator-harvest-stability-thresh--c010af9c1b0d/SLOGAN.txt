For the predator-extinction periodic orbit, the geometric factor in the published Floquet ratio cancels exactly against the logistic flight, leaving \(\rho_1=(1-p_2E_l)e^{-\mu T}\). Hence every biologically admissible predator-harvest efficiency is locally orbitally stabilizing in the stated finite-cycle regime, and the proposed positive \(p_2\)-threshold does not separate stability from instability.
