For normalized real nonnegative trigonometric polynomials whose only nonconstant frequencies are \(1\), \(2\), and \(4\), the sharp first-harmonic coefficient is \(3/2\), with a unique extremizer. Equivalently, the sharp value of \(\psi(1)\) for real positive-definite sequences supported on \(\{0,\pm1,\pm2,\pm4\}\) is \(3/4\).
