For finite weak orders \(P=A_1\oplus\cdots\oplus A_h\) and \(Q=B_1\oplus\cdots\oplus B_k\), the exact cardinality of \(\operatorname{Map}(P,Q)\) is determined by only two states per target level: whether the previous source level uses one or at least two points at its top occupied target level. This yields an exact \(O(hk^2)\) transfer recurrence for arbitrary positive level sizes.
