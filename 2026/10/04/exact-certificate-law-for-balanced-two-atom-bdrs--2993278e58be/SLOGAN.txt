For balanced two-atom transport, the new BDRS/O-BDRS certificate collapses to an exact one-parameter law: the repair term is identically zero and the relative certificate slack is \(\eta/(p\Delta)+o(p^{-1})\). Consequently the common-\(\eta\) certified stopping count of O-BDRS is asymptotically exactly a factor \(1/\lambda\) of BDRS on this benchmark.
