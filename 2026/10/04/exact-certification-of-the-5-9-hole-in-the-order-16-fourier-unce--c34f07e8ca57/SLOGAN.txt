The order-\(16\) Fourier support pair \( (5,9) \), previously supported by a numerical scan of \(49{,}969{,}920\) five-by-seven submatrices, is rigorously impossible. An exact cyclotomic-rank enumeration reduces the problem to \(195{,}195\) translation-orbit representatives and certifies that none satisfies the exact-support rank criterion.
