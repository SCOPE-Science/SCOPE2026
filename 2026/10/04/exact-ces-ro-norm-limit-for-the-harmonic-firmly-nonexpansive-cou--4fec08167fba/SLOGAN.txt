For the harmonic firmly nonexpansive counterexample of Bauschke–Tung, the Cesàro norms do not merely stay away from zero: they converge to the explicit Gaussian-logarithmic constant \(e^{-\delta^2\pi^2/6}[\int_0^1 e^{-\delta^2(\log r)^2}\,dr]^{1/2}\). The same constant has a closed form through \(\operatorname{erfc}\).
