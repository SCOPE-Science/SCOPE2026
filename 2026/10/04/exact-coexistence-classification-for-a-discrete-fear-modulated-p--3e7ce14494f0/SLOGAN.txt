The positive-equilibrium quadratic in the Du–Han–Lei fear map does not always have exactly one positive root: depending on \(C_1,C_2\) and \(\Delta\), it can have zero, one, or two. Its constant term satisfies \(C_2=-K\gamma(1+kqc)\log\lambda_u\), exposing a two-coexistence regime that can persist even when prey cannot invade the predator-only equilibrium.
