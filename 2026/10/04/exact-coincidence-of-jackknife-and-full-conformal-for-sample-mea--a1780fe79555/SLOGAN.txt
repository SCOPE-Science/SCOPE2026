For intercept-only least squares with absolute residuals, jackknife+ is not merely close to full conformal: under the standard finite-sample quantile convention the two prediction sets are identical for every sample. Thus exchangeable coverage improves from the generic jackknife+ bound to \(1-\alpha\), and with distinct augmented residuals it is exactly \(\lceil(1-\alpha)(n+1)\rceil/(n+1)\).
