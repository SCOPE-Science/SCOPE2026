For the common-delay interior equilibrium of the delayed Beverton–Holt competition model, the characteristic quasipolynomial collapses to a quadratic in \(\lambda e^{\lambda\tau}\). This gives an exact stability threshold \(\tau_c\) in closed form and places the paper's Figure 2 cases \(\tau=2\) and \(\tau=4\) on opposite sides of the sharp boundary \(\tau_c\approx3.59049\).
