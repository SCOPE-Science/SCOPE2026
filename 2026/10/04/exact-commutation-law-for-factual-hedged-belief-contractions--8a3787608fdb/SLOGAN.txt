For factual hedged belief contractions, order-independence has an exact propositional test: two contractions commute on every unconstrained Kripke model exactly when the inputs are equivalent or their disjunction is a tautology. Each contraction is idempotent, and on \(n\) variables exactly \(2^{2^n}+3^{2^n}-1\) ordered input pairs commute modulo classical equivalence.
