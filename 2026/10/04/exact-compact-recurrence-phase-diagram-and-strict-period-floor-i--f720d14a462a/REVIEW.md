## correctness

PASS

Invariance of z and y gives E[x]=-b and E[z^2]=b. With v=x+b=z' and w=-x-ay=z'', direct symbolic differentiation of F=vw+v^2/2+a(z^3/3-bz) gives LF=w^2-a v^2. This classifies the sign regimes: b<0 is impossible; a=0,b>0 is impossible; a<0,b>0 forces v=w=0 and hence only the two equilibrium atoms; the b=0 cases follow by support tangency. For a>0,b>0, equality z^2=b almost surely also forces equilibrium mixtures. On a periodic orbit, Wirtinger's inequality applied to zero-mean z' gives P>=2pi/sqrt(a); the eliminated jerk equation rules out equality. Fresh symbolic differentiation independently reproduced the coboundary and eliminated jerk identity.
