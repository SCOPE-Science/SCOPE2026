Generalized Sprott S has an exact compact-recurrence phase diagram: negative \(b\) forbids compact invariant statistics, while negative \(a\) with positive \(b\) leaves only the two equilibrium atoms. For \(a>0,b>0\), every non-equilibrium compact recurrent state crosses \(|z|=\sqrt b\), and every nonconstant periodic orbit satisfies \(P>2\pi/\sqrt a\).
