# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The 2025 construction gives, for every discretely represented ideal, a projection \(P:\ell_\infty\to c_{0,\mathcal I}\) with \(\|P\|\le2\). The 2026 lower bound says that no projection onto \(c_{0,\mathcal I}\) for a proper ideal can have norm below \(2\). Hence the complementation constant is exactly \(2\) and the displayed \(P\) itself has norm \(2\). For \(Q=I-P\), the block formula and norm-one ultrafilter limits give \(\|Qx\|_\infty\le\|x\|_\infty\), while \(Q\mathbf1=\mathbf1\), so \(\|Q\|=1\).

## Originality
PASS, with residual short-corollary risk. The 2025 source states the explicit projection and upper bound but predates the 2026 universal lower bound. The 2026 source states the lower bound and explicitly asks whether complemented ideals can have constants above \(2\); in the inspected discussion it does not isolate discretely represented ideals as a solved subclass. Targeted claim-level searches for finite intersections of maximal ideals, strongly \(\omega\)-maximal ideals, and discretely represented ideals did not locate the exact constant statement.

## Value
PASS. The result gives a clean partial answer to an explicit recent open problem on a natural class central to the preceding structural theory. It covers every finite intersection of maximal ideals and every strongly \(\omega\)-maximal ideal, and it also identifies exact norms of the canonical complementary projections. This distinguishes a large class where the universal lower bound is sharp from the unresolved general complemented case.
