Every proper discretely represented ideal \(\mathcal I\) has \(\lambda_{\mathrm{comp}}(c_{0,\mathcal I},\ell_\infty)=2\). For the canonical block decomposition, the projection onto \(c_{0,\mathcal I}\) has norm \(2\), while its positive complementary projection has norm \(1\), giving a broad affirmative subclass of the recent open complementation-constant question.
