At reciprocal level \(q=1/r\), complete-null Knockoff+ is exactly a first-passage problem for a fair \(+1/-r\) random walk, giving a closed finite-sample Fuss-Catalan formula and limit \(f_r^r\) with \(2f_r=1+f_r^{r+1}\). At \(q=0.1\), the limiting complete-null FDR is only \(0.000981367289898861\ldots\), despite the nominal \(0.1\) guarantee.
