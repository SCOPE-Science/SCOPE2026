Every compact stationary state of the generalized Sprott H flow obeys the exact conditional laws \(\mathbb E[x\mid z]=bz\) and \(\mathbb E[x\mid y]=-ay\), together with two sharp equilibrium-centered defects. Unless the measure is supported on the two equilibria, it must both enter and leave the height slab \(-b/a<z<0\), while every nonconstant periodic orbit also crosses the horizontal equilibrium slab \([-b^2/a,0]\).
