Every compact stationary state of the Sprott A/Nosé–Hoover flow obeys the conditional law \(\mathbb E[y^2\mid z]=a\); for \(a\ne0\), each radius shell is also balanced by \(\mathbb E[z\mid Q]=0\), where \(Q=x^2+y^2+z^2\). For \(a>0\), the exact defect \(\operatorname{Cov}(y^2,Q)=(2a)^{-1}\mathbb E[(LQ)^2]>0\) forces radial motion and sign excursions, while \(a<0\) admits no compact invariant probability measure and \(a=0\) leaves only the equilibrium line.
