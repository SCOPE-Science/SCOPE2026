Constant-step scalar Signum does not have an isolated stable oscillation: it has a whole normally attracting continuum of two-cycles. Momentum narrows the possible cycle-center bias to \(|b|<\delta(1-\beta)/[2(1+\beta)]\), but every cycle still has half-amplitude \(\delta/2\), and transverse attraction slows to the exact two-step factor \(\beta^2\).
