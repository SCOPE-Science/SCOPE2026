For the \(n\) vertices of a regular Euclidean polygon with chord distance, the Geller–Misiurewicz convexity gap has an exact modulo-\(4\) formula, attained by the largest odd cyclic separation not exceeding \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\). The values converge to the circle gap \(2(\sqrt2-1)R\), with quadratic convergence when \(4\mid n\) and first-order arithmetic oscillation in the other residue classes.
