For identity-map \(\ell_1\) S-FSPS on a centered box, every positive nonincreasing vanishing nonsummable smoothing schedule has exact accumulated \(\ell_1\) motion \(\|x^0\|_1\), and hence finite Euclidean primal length. Therefore power smoothing converges with finite length for all \(0<\beta\le1\), including the \(\beta\le1/2\) range outside the source paper's general theorem.
