For the weighted four-state Cartesian powers used in a continuous-time Grover-search model, a corner sees only \((d+1)^2\) spectral atoms at most, rather than the ambient \(4^d\) states; for irrational \(p\) the bound is exact, while the homogeneous case has exactly \(4d+1\) active atoms for \(d\ge2\). The rank-one oracle and the constant initial state stay inside this cyclic subspace, giving an exact reduced Hamiltonian and Green function; at \(d=5\), \(p=1/2\), the active dimension is \(21\) instead of \(1024\).
