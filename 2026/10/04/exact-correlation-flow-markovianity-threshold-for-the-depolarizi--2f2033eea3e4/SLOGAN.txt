The depolarizing–Ising model has the exact Luo–Fu–Song correlation-flow threshold \(\gamma^*_{\mathrm{LFS}}(z)=(1-z^2)/\sqrt{3(1+2z^2)}\), replacing the numerical boundary in the source. Its universal value is \(1/\sqrt3\), which explains the reported \(\gamma^*\approx0.6\) at zero memory-spin magnetization.
