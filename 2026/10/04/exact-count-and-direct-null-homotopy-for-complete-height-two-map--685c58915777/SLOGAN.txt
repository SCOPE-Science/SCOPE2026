For \(p,q\ge2\) and \(n\ge3\), there are exactly \(n(3^p+3^q-2)\) direct continuous maps from the complete height-two space \(P_{p,q}\) to the crown \(\mathfrak C_n\), and every one is finite-space null-homotopic. The target threshold is sharp at \(n=2\), where the four-point crown has a non-null identity map.\n
