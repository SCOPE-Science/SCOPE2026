For the constant global surface-average interaction on a three-dimensional Dirac sphere, a gap eigenvalue exists exactly when \(|\lambda|>1/(8\pi mR^3)\); it is unique as an energy, has multiplicity two, and traverses the entire gap monotonically. Its birth from the lower edge is quadratic in \(\lambda-\lambda_c\), while strong coupling approaches the upper edge at order \(1/\lambda\).
