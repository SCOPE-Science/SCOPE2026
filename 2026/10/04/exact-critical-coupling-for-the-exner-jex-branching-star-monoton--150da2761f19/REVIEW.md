# Same-model review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The negative-energy edge equation is solved explicitly and the central \(\delta\)-condition reduces to one scalar secular equation. At \(\kappa=2\), the axial Robin condition with endpoint coupling \(-2\) is satisfied by a pure exponential for every axial length, making the axial logarithmic derivative exactly \(2\). Substitution into the two fixed mirror arms yields the closed form \(2(21-e^4)/(e^4+7)\), numerically matching the source's \(-1.09088\). Ground-state positivity makes this eigenvalue the lowest one. Hellmann--Feynman monotonicity in the central coupling fixes whether \(\kappa\) lies above or below \(2\), and the explicit length derivative of the axial logarithmic derivative gives the strict sign of the energy derivative.

## Originality

**PASS, narrowly scoped.** The direct source reports the transition only numerically and says that the critical branch has a pure exponential axial component; it does not state the exact threshold or prove that the same threshold works for every positive axial length. Its Remark 4.2 specifically identifies the zero-index finite-edge case as requiring subtler analysis. Searches using the reported decimal, source title, critical-coupling terminology, and exact-formula aliases did not locate this closed form.

The closest general source, Berkolaiko--Kuchment, concerns analytic dependence of quantum-graph spectra on lengths and vertex conditions and does not specialize to this star. Kurasov's later ground-state paper addresses positivity for broader vertex conditions, not this monotonicity threshold. Residual risk remains because the computation is short enough to have appeared in unindexed notes.

**PASS.** The finding turns the source's numerical phase boundary into an exact analytic constant, proves that it is independent of the varied edge length, and completely resolves the exceptional \(\sigma=0\) edge in the canonical branching example used to show failure of chain monotonicity. It also certifies that the plotted two-regime picture has no hidden length-dependent transition curve.
