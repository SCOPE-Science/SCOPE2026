The two lower-threshold couplings in the nearest-neighbour lattice delta model obey the exact identity \(\lambda_s=n\lambda_c/(\lambda_c-(n-1))\). In two dimensions this makes the phase portrait explicit: \(\lambda_s=\pi/(\pi-2)\), \(\lambda_c=\pi/(4-\pi)\), with \(A=(\pi/(\pi-2),\pi)\) and \(B=(\pi/(4-\pi),\pi/(\pi-2))\).
