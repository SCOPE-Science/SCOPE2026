On a radial boundary-active quadratic, B-adaPG with the nonseparable Hellinger-ball kernel obeys an exact one-dimensional dual recursion even though that kernel fails the source paper's Bregman-zone assumption. The trajectory nevertheless satisfies \(x^k\to u\), its cumulative step mass \(S_k\) must diverge, and \(1-t_k\sim[2(c-1)^2S_k^2]^{-1}\).
