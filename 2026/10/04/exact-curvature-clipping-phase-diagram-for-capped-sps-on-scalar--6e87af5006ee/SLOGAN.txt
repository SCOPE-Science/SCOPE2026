On heterogeneous scalar quadratics, the cap in \(\mathrm{SPS}_{\max}\) clips curvature weights exactly: the stationary target uses weights \(\min(a_i,q/\gamma_b)\), where \(q=1/(2c)\). Tight caps preserve the true curvature-weighted minimizer, loose caps recover the known curvature-blind target, and mixed caps have an explicit stationary bias and variance.
