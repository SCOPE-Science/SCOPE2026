For the pineapple graph \(P_{p,q}\), the exact Lin--Lu--Yau curvature-distortion number is \(\max\{1,\frac{p-2}{4}(\sqrt{1+8q/((p-1)(p-2))}-1)\}\). The optimum is attained by increasing only the clique edges incident with the pendant-attachment vertex, and unit weights work exactly up to \(q\le p(p-1)/(p-2)\).
