For \(G=K_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}\) with \(n_1\ge\cdots\ge n_r\), the D-conflict graph is the join of the line graphs \(L(K_{n_i,n_j})\), so \(\chi'_D(G)=\sum_{i<j}\max\{n_i,n_j\}\). This proves Wang's conjectured \(\Delta(\Delta+1)/2\) bound for every complete multipartite graph, with equality exactly for complete graphs.
