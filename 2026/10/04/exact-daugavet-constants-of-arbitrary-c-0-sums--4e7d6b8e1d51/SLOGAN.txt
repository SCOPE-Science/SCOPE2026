For every family of nonzero real Banach spaces, the Daugavet constant in their \(c_0\)-sum is exactly the supremum of the component Daugavet constants: \(\operatorname{dc}_{(\oplus X_\gamma)_{c_0}}((x_\gamma))=\sup_\gamma\operatorname{dc}_{X_\gamma}(x_\gamma)\). For finite families this is the corresponding exact maximum law for \(\ell_\infty\)-sums.
