# Same-model review
## Correctness
PASS. The proof starts from the exact distance formula stated for the orthogonal-simplex family and performs an exact normalization. The identity
\[
E(n,s)^2-2(\sqrt2-1)=\frac{(x-(\sqrt2-1))^2+(q-1)(\sqrt2-1)^2}{q+x}
\]
was reconstructed algebraically and replayed symbolically. The nonattainment and near-extremizer characterization follow from explicit nonnegative terms; the continued-fraction construction supplies a genuine infinite approaching sequence. Numerical output is not used as an infinite proof.

## Originality
PASS. The closest primary source, arXiv:2607.27041v2, contains the same family and its exact distance formula, and therefore already covers the lower inequality itself. It does not state that \(\sqrt{2(\sqrt2-1)}\) is the exact infimum of the family, the sum-of-squares defect identity, or the iff asymptotic characterization. The exact direct-sum paper arXiv:2603.18268v1 is an ingredient but does not perform this optimization. Targeted searches by the constant, remainder form, construction, source remark, and normalized aliases found no covering statement.

## Value
PASS. The construction is the source's canonical witness for near-sharpness of its main Banach--Mazur bound when divisibility fails. The exact defect profile replaces an approximate sharpness comparison by an exact natural invariant and identifies the complete asymptotic regime that is worst for this construction. This is not a free renaming or an arbitrary finite computation.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest literature is Brandenberg--González Merino--Grundbacher, arXiv:2607.27041v2, especially Theorem 1.3 and Remark 4.5, together with the direct-sum machinery in Grundbacher--Kobos, arXiv:2603.18268v1. The finding is restricted to that orthogonal-simplex family and does not claim global optimality for all bodies of fixed dimension and asymmetry. A residual bibliographic risk remains because an uncatalogued source could contain the same elementary optimization.
