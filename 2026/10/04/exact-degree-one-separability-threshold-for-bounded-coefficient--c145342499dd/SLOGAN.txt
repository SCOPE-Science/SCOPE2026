For Pauli Hamiltonians whose term-overlap graph has maximum degree one and whose coefficients satisfy \(|J_i|\le J\), the exact universal separability boundary is \(\beta J\le\operatorname{arctanh}(\sqrt2-1)=\tfrac12\log(1+\sqrt2)\). A single commuting edge, \(H=-J(X\otimes X+Z\otimes Z)\), becomes NPT immediately above that boundary, so the degree-one case omitted from the recent \(\Delta\ge2\) theorem has a finite sharp threshold.
