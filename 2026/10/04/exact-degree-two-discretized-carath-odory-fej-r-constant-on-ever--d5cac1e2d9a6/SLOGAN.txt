For every cyclic grid of size \(N\ge5\), the degree-two discretized Carathéodory–Fejér constant is \(-2(u+v)/(1+2uv)\), where \(u\) and \(v\) are the two adjacent cosine samples straddling \(-1/\sqrt2\). The value equals the continuous constant \(\sqrt2\) exactly when \(8\mid N\), and is strictly larger otherwise.
