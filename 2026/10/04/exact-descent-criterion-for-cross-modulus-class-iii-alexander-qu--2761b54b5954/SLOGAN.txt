For cyclic degree-one fibers, the non-identical-order class-(iii) formula descends exactly when \(r_i\mid(1-k_i)r_j\) for every ordered pair, and it is a quandle exactly when all cross-scalars are units. This validates the source\'s \(\mathbb Z_2\bigsqcup\mathbb Z_4\) example but shows that its \(\mathbb Z_1\bigsqcup\mathbb Z_5\) class-(iii) identification needs extra cross-fiber data.
