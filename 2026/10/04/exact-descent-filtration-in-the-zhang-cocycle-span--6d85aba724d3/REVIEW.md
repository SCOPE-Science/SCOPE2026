# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The source gives a necessary-and-sufficient two-stage descent criterion, not merely a sufficient test. Its exact rotation pairings reduce the integral first-stage condition to \(2(a v_2-c v_3)=0\). The figure-eight knot has \((v_2,v_3)=(-1,0)\), forcing \(a=0\); the right trefoil has \((v_2,v_3)=(1,1)\), then forcing \(c=0\). The remaining integral \(\beta_1\)-axis is killed at the unparametrized stage because the figure-eight has \(\beta_1(\operatorname{Roll}_0)=2\). Modulo \(2\), the first condition is automatic and the second obstruction is \(c_0v_2+c_2v_3\), so the same witnesses force \(c_0=c_2=0\). The source proves the four mod-\(2\) classes independent. The packaged script replays exactly these coefficient rows.

## Originality
PASS. The primary preprint proves the criterion and records positive descents of integral \(\beta_1\), mod-\(2\) \(\bar\beta_1\), and \(\beta_3\), but does not state the exact descending submodule or subspace of the full displayed spans. Searches using the class names, descent terminology, the two closed-knot quotients, and kernel/subspace formulations found no equivalent exhaustive classification. Older Mortier work predates the new \(\beta\)-classes. The main residual risk is that this short corollary may appear in a later revision or an unindexed note.

## Value
PASS. This is a natural complete classification attached to the source's new descent criterion, not an arbitrary coefficient slice. It shows exactly how the natural low-order cocycle spans change under the two geometric quotients: integral ranks \(3\to1\to0\), while mod-\(2\) dimensions \(4\to4\to2\). In particular, it proves the negative complements to the source's positive examples and isolates which classes can actually be used on each closed-knot space.
