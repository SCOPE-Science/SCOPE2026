Inside Zhang's natural low-order cocycle spans, descent is completely rigid: over \(\mathbb Z\), only the \(\beta_1\)-axis reaches parametrized closed knots and nothing survives the unparametrized quotient. Over \(\mathbb F_2\), all four named classes reach parametrized closed knots, while exactly \(\bar\beta_1\) and \(\beta_3\) span the unparametrized-descending plane.
