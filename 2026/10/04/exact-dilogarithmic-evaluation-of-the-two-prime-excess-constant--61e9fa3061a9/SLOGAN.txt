The two-prime excess constant \(I=\int_0^2G(w)\,\mathrm{d}w\) from Hughes's density theorem admits an exact closed form in \(\pi^2\), logarithms, \(\operatorname{Li}_2(-1/2)\), and \(\operatorname{Li}_2(-3/4)\). This replaces the source's quadrature-only value of the full integral by a symbolic evaluation.
