# Review

Correctness: PASS. For a transitive automorphism group, every orbit on unordered pairs is a regular orbital graph and the valencies sum to \(n-1\). Valency-one orbits are invariant perfect matchings. Their mate involutions lie in the permutation centralizer, whose action is semiregular, so its order divides \(n=2m\). With \(m\) odd, the centralizer has at most \(m\) involutions by Sylow counting. This gives at most \((3m-1)/2\) positive pair-orbits and hence at most \((3m+1)/2\) distances. The published \(D_m\) construction attains this for every odd \(m\).

Originality: PASS, with a priority caveat. The primary paper proves the same numerical formula only when the odd factor is prime and explicitly asks for the composite-odd-factor cases. Searches for the exact formula, the vertex-transitive edge-coloring formulation, and the pair-orbit/centralizer formulation found no covering statement. The extension is short and uses standard permutation-group facts, so an unpublished or differently indexed observation remains possible.

Value: PASS. The result closes an entire infinite family explicitly left open in the source, including the first unresolved table case \(n=18\), and replaces a prime-dependent chain argument by a compact structural proof.
