For the real Hölder-dual Banach planes \(\ell_3^2\) and \(\ell_{3/2}^2\), the optimal global Dragomir-symmetry parameter for Birkhoff--James orthogonality is exactly \(\sqrt{1-\left(\frac{27}{17+7\sqrt7}\right)^{2/3}}\). The worst asymmetry is attained at an interior coordinate ratio, not on an axis or the diagonal.
