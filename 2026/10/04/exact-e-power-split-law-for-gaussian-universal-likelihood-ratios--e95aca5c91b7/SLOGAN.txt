For a scalar Gaussian split likelihood-ratio e-value, the expected log evidence has the exact form \(\frac{n-m}{2}(\mu^2-1/m)\), making the optimal training size an explicit threshold rule. Under \(\mu_n=h/\sqrt n\), the optimal training fraction tends to \(1/|h|\) for \(|h|>1\), while for fixed nonzero signal it vanishes at rate \(1/(|\mu|\sqrt n)\).
