# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof covers the full parameter square. In the interior it reduces the nonunital channel by invertible diagonal local filters to a trace-preserving unital channel with Bloch singular values \((\lambda,\lambda,\lambda^2)\), applies the established exact unital tensor-square criterion, and derives the quadratic boundary. A separate partial-transpose calculation for the singlet gives the same boundary. Endpoints are checked explicitly. No finite computation is used as a substitute for the universal proof.

## Originality
PASS. Masajada, Fellous-Asiani, and Streltsov explicitly state the singlet criterion as Numerical Conjecture 8 and support it numerically. Filippov, Rybár, and Ziman prove the unital criterion but only report numerical agreement for generalized amplitude damping. A later general source-placement implication does not supply the converse needed here. Targeted semantic searches, alias searches, and current/prior-ledger overlap checks did not locate a covering exact theorem. The principal residual risk is an equivalent result under remote quantum-Sinkhorn or cone-preserver terminology.

## Value
PASS. The result closes a recent explicit conjecture and gives an exact continuum threshold for a standard thermal-noise model. It replaces a fine numerical scan by a structural theorem and provides a simple analytic decision rule for entanglement annihilation.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest inputs are arXiv:2506.06089v1 for the explicit conjecture, arXiv:1110.3757v1 / Phys. Rev. A 85, 012303 for the unital criterion and earlier numerical generalized amplitude-damping evidence, and arXiv:2609.18803v1 for a broader one-way source-placement implication. The theorem here is limited to two identical qubit generalized amplitude-damping channels; asymmetric pairs and higher-dimensional or multipartite settings remain outside its scope.
