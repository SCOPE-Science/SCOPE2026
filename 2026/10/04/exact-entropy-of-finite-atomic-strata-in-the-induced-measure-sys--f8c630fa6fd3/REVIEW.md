# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The simplex map is a continuous surjective semiconjugacy from \(\operatorname{id}_{\Delta_{m-1}}\times T^{\times m}\) onto the full support-cardinality stratum, giving the sharp upper bound. The binary-weight map is a continuous injective equivariant map from \(K^m\); unique subset sums recover colliding coordinate labels, so it gives a conjugate invariant subsystem and the matching lower bound. Compactness and forward invariance are explicit.

Targeted searches for finite-support, support-at-most-\(m\), and exact \(m\)-fold entropy formulations found no covering result. The residual priority risk is recorded rather than treated as proof of novelty.

PASS. This is a natural structural invariant of the canonical finite-atomic filtration, not an arbitrary parameter slice.
