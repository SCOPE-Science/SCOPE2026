For every compact forward-invariant \(K\), allowing at most \(m\) atoms in the induced probability-measure system multiplies ordinary topological entropy by exactly \(m\): \(h_{\mathrm{top}}(T_*|_{\mathcal M_{\le m}(K)})=m\,h_{\mathrm{top}}(T|_K)\). The lower bound is a binary-weight product embedding, while the matching upper bound comes from the simplex parametrization of all \(m\)-atomic measures.
