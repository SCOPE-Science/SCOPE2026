# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof is reconstructed from the defining inequalities. Translation by \((1,1)\) partitions \(\mathbb F_q^2\) into length-\(p\) orbits. The substitution \(\psi(a_0+i,b_0+i)=z_i+i\) converts the conditions exactly into cyclic proper-color constraints with a constant allowed list on each orbit. Counting the diagonal and off-diagonal orbits gives the product formula. Small-alphabet obstructions and positivity for \(q\ge4\) follow directly. The bundled checker reproduces representative constructions but is supplementary to the proof.

## Originality
PASS. The closest primary source, arXiv:2603.27271v1, states the map condition over \(\mathbb F_q\), then gives one explicit \(\mathbb F_4\) map. Its later self-synchronizing DNA results remain specialized to \(\mathbb F_4\). Searches using the original inequalities, synchronizer terminology, exact enumeration language, and the cycle-coloring reformulation did not locate the all-field count or threshold. The earlier 2020 CIS-code paper does not contain the later self-synchronizing map condition.

## Value
PASS. The map is a necessary ingredient in the recent conditional construction, so deciding its availability is mathematically motivated. The result gives both a sharp boundary and a closed enumeration, rather than a one-off small-alphabet example. It also prevents an unsupported extrapolation to \(q=3\) while showing that the map condition itself is not intrinsically quartic.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest inspected source is W.-H. Choi, arXiv:2603.27271v1, especially Theorem 10 and Definition 11. The predecessor is W.-H. Choi, H. J. Kim, and Y. Lee, Designs, Codes and Cryptography 88 (2020), DOI:10.1007/s10623-020-00802-2. Literature search is not a proof of absolute novelty, and the claim concerns only the algebraic map ingredient, not a full nonquartic extension of the DNA algorithms.
