For every Kraus-rank-two qubit channel, finite rank-one environment measurements produce exactly the optimally corrected fidelity interval \([(1+s_1-s_2)/2,(1+s_1+s_2)/2]\), where \(s_1\ge s_2\) are the Takagi singular values of the determinant quadratic form. Measurement choice is irrelevant exactly when \(s_2=0\): amplitude damping has this property, while dephasing spans \([\max(p,1-p),1]\).
