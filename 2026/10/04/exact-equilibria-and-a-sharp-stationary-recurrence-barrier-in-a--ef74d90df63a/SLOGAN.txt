For the Benkouider–Sambas four-dimensional flow, the exact equilibria undergo a line-to-pair transition at \(d=0\), and every compact stationary state with \(d\ne0\) obeys the sharp law \(\int z^2\,d\mu=(b/c)(d^2+\operatorname{Var}_\mu(y))\ge bd^2/c\). Equality occurs only for mixtures of the two equilibria, while \(d\) and \(-d\) are conjugate by a state reflection.
