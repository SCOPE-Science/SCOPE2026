The Xu–Shekofteh–Akgül–Li–Panahi flow has a complete positive-parameter equilibrium classification and no linearly stable equilibrium; every compact stationary state also lies on an exact second-moment/variance curve. At the paper's parameter values, the true fixed points are \((\sqrt3-1,\pm\sqrt{2\sqrt3},0)\): the printed \(\pm3.4641\) ordinates are \(y^2\), not \(y\), while the reported eigenvalues match the corrected points.
