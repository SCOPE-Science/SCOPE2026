# Same-model review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The proof treats arbitrary finite block decompositions and arbitrary entangled references. Complete positivity of \(X\mapsto d\operatorname{Tr}(X)I-X\) yields the sharp domination for the proposed replacement state, while a maximally entangled sector witness yields the matching lower bound. The same witness proves the exact factor \(p\) for flagged partial erasure. Endpoints and classical/full-quantum limits agree.

## Originality

**PASS, narrowly scoped.** The primary source defines the same diamond reconstruction problem for general algebras and proves information-disturbance bounds but does not solve the flagged-erasure optimization. Kuperberg introduces the direct-sum shape and its \(p\)-norm capacity invariants but does not derive a diamond erasure radius. Bény--Oreshkov use worst-case entanglement fidelity rather than the displayed diamond formula. Targeted searches did not locate the exact general formula. A residual risk remains that an equivalent conditional-expectation/replacer identity is stated elsewhere under different terminology.

## Value

**PASS.** Total erasure is a canonical stress test for hybrid memories. The theorem gives an exact single-shot benchmark in the same metric used by approximate algebra correction, proves that gauge multiplicities are irrelevant, unifies classical and fully quantum cases, and links the answer to the squared \(2\)-norm of the established hybrid-memory shape.
