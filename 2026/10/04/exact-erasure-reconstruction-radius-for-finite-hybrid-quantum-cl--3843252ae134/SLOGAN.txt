For a finite hybrid algebra \(\mathcal A=\bigoplus_i(\mathcal B(\mathcal H_{A_i})\otimes I_{B_i})\), standard erasure with probability \(p\) has exact optimal reconstruction error \(\epsilon_{\mathcal A}=2p(1-1/\sum_i d_i^2)\). Gauge multiplicities disappear: the answer depends only on \(\dim_{\mathbb C}\mathcal A=\sum_i d_i^2\), the squared \(2\)-norm of the hybrid-memory shape.
