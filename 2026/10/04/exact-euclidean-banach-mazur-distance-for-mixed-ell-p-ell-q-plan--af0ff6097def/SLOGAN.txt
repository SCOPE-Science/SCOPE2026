For the mixed real plane \(N_{\lambda,p,q}=(\|\cdot\|_p^2+\lambda\|\cdot\|_q^2)^{1/2}\), the exact Euclidean Banach--Mazur distortion is the square root of its von Neumann--Jordan constant through both same-side regimes \(1\le p\le q\le2\) and \(2\le p\le q\le\infty\). The proof is a sharp signed-permutation symmetry reduction to the axial and diagonal Euclidean directions.
