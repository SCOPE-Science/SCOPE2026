For the first non-Hilbert even exponent in the two-dimensional Cesàro family, the exact Euclidean Banach--Mazur distortion is algebraic and is attained by the quadratic norm \(|(x,y)|_*=\sqrt{x^2+y^2/\sqrt{17}}\). If \(s_*\) is the unique positive root of \((1+s)^3(\sqrt{17}-s)=16s\), then \(d_{\mathrm{BM}}(\mathrm{ces}_4^{(2)},\ell_2^2)^2=\sqrt{16+(1+s_*)^4}/(\sqrt{17}+s_*^2)\approx1.26068368922021387435\).
