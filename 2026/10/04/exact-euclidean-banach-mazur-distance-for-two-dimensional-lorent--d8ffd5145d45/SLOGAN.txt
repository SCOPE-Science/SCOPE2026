For the two-dimensional Lorentz norm \(d^{(2)}(\omega,q)\) with \(1<q<2\), the exact Euclidean Banach–Mazur distance has a two-branch formula with phase boundary \(\omega=2^{q/2}-1\). Signed-permutation symmetry forces a Euclidean circle to be optimal, and a one-variable radial maximization supplies the exact distortion.
