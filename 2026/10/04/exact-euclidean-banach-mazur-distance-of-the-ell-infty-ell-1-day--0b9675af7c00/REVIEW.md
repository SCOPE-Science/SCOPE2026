# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof covers arbitrary Euclidean pullbacks through symmetry averaging, then solves the resulting one-parameter problem exactly. The two sector ratios and their extrema give the global distortion \(D(t)\), whose unique minimum is \(4/3\) at \(t=3\). The resulting quadratic form is positive definite, and explicit vectors attain both sharp bounds.

A later Day–James Banach–Mazur source was inspected where it gives a finite-parameter equality regime; its displayed hypothesis excludes \(p=\infty\). Targeted searches for aliases, the endpoint, the exact distance, and the constant comparison found no statement implying this formula. The remaining risk is an unlocated equivalent convex-geometric calculation under different terminology.

## Value
PASS. This is the exact Hilbertian distortion and optimal ellipse of a canonical Day–James endpoint. The formula also proves a nonzero structural gap from the known von Neumann–Jordan constant, so the result is not a routine substitution into a known equality theorem.

## Closest literature and limitations
