Across the complete normalized absolute symmetric two-dimensional family with cubic polynomial numerical index zero, the exact Euclidean Banach--Mazur distance is \(d_{\mathrm{BM}}^4=\max\{2/(1+\beta),(1+\beta)/2\}\). Finite-group averaging shows that no tilted ellipse improves on the Euclidean pullback, and the remaining optimization reduces to the axis-versus-diagonal extrema of the quartic norm.
