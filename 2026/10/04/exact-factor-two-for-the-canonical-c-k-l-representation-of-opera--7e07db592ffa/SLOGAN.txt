For every nontrivial bounded distributive lattice \(L\), the canonical restriction \(\operatorname{FBL}\langle L\rangle\to C(K_L)\) has norm \(1\) and inverse norm exactly \(2\). The canonical strong unit \(u=|\delta_m|\vee|\delta_M|\) is a universal sharpness witness: \(\|u\|=2\) while its \(C(K_L)\)-norm is \(1\).
