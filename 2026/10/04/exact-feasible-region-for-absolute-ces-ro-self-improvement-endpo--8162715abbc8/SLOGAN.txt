For fixed \(p\) and optimal absolute-Cesàro constant \(K>1\), the supremal self-improvement exponent can be any \(Q\in[pK/(K-1),\infty]\), and every such value is realized by a positive operator on \(\ell^p\). The published lower threshold is therefore the only restriction on the pair \(\bigl(K,q_*(T)\bigr)\).
