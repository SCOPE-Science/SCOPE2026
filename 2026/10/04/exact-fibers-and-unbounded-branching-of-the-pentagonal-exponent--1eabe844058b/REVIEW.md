# Same-model review

## Correctness
**PASS.** Decomposing \(m=2^k u\) and \(n=2^\ell w\) with odd \(u,w\) gives the exact equations \(\ell=k+t\) and \(w=ub_t\), where \(t=\nu_2(k+2)\) and \(b_t=(\ell+2-t)/2^t\). This proves both directions of the fiber formula with no unproved infinite step. The recurrence used for unboundedness gives \(\nu_2(a_{R+1}-a_j)=a_j\) because the first power of two in the telescoping difference is followed only by terms divisible by one higher power. Exact replay matches direct enumeration for all targets through \(400000\).

## Originality
**PASS.** The closest source is Saikia--Sarma, arXiv:2609.25739v1. Its inspected Section 2.1 proves disjointness of three selected forward orbits and Remark 4 explicitly notes noninjectivity through \(e(2)=e(3)=6\). It does not state an exact inverse-fiber formula or unbounded fiber multiplicity. published-finding corpus and public statement searches for the exact map, valuation equation, preimages/fibers, orbit merging, and unbounded multiplicity found no equivalent or dominating result. Residual risk remains from unindexed contemporaneous work or a more general dynamical statement under different terminology.

## Value
**PASS.** The map is not incidental: it is the arithmetic dynamical mechanism behind the recent orbit-telescoping proof. Quantifying its global noninjectivity is therefore mathematically motivated. The theorem both classifies every one-step fiber and proves that merger multiplicity is unbounded, showing that extensions of the telescoping method cannot rely on a uniform finite indegree bound. This is an infinite structural lemma rather than a finite table or routine parameter variation.

## Closest literature and limitations
The source paper's qualitative collision is recovered as the first case \(e^{-1}(6)=\{2,3\}\), but its selected three-orbit argument remains untouched. The result does not classify all forward-orbit intersections, nor does it claim minimal targets for each multiplicity. The explicit existence targets grow extremely rapidly.
