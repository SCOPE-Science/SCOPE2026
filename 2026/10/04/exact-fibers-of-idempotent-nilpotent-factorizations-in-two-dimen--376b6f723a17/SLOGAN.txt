For \(2\times2\) matrices over \(\mathbb F_q\), the ordered idempotent–nilpotent factorization count is exactly \(q^3+2q^2+1\) at zero, \(q+1\) at each nonzero nilpotent, \(q-1\) at each nonnilpotent singular matrix, and \(0\) at invertible matrices. The same profile holds with the factor order reversed.
