For the commuting specialization of the correlated zero-Stein-rate construction, every finite block is governed by the single overlap \(g_n=c+(1-c)q^n\): relative entropy and optimized max-relative entropy are \(-\log_2 g_n\), while \(\beta_{\varepsilon,n}=(1-\varepsilon)g_n\). Trace-preserving smoothing is also exact: \(Z_n^\delta=\log_2\max\{1,(1-\delta/2)/g_n\}\), so the smoothing threshold is \(2(1-g_n)\).
