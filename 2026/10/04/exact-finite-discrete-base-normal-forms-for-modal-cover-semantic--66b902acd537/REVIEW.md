# Review

## Correctness

PASS. On the equality preorder with only identity local covers, modal refinement and modal localization are tautological and localized up-sets are all subsets of \(W\). CM behavior at a world is therefore exactly an upset of the Boolean lattice, equivalently a monotone Boolean function, with a unique finite antichain of minimal covers.

For SL, modal inclusion restricts every cover of \(w\) to \(\varnothing\) or \(\{w\}\), yielding exactly the false, identity, and true behaviors. PLL's modal identity removes the false behavior, and the remaining two raw families automatically satisfy modal transitivity.

For finite \(\mathrm{CK}_{\Box}\), seriality makes each cover family nonempty and confluence makes it downward directed under inclusion. Finiteness implies the total intersection is itself a cover, hence a least cover. This gives exactly the usual Kripke-box clause. Exhaustive finite replay agrees with all four classifications.

PASS. Valliappan's 2026 paper defines the four modal-cover semantics and their algebraic operators but does not analyze the finite discrete base. Full-text searches found no discrete specialization, Dedekind count, pointwise SL/PLL normal form, or finite least-cover theorem.

The identification of Dedekind numbers with monotone Boolean functions is classical background, and finite powerset representations of meet-preserving modal operators are standard. The originality claim is the unified exact specialization of the new modal-cover conditions: \(M_n^n\), \(3^n\), \(2^n\), and \(2^{n^2}\), with canonical modal-cover normal forms and a direct CK-box collapse.

Targeted published-finding and public-literature searches did not locate an equivalent result.

## Value

PASS. The source presents modal covers as a genuine enlargement of relational semantics and develops four distinct intuitionistic modal systems within one framework. The discrete base is the canonical finite case where intuitionistic localization contributes no additional order structure. The theorem therefore isolates exactly what the modal-cover layer itself contributes: full monotone-neighborhood freedom for CM, sharp pointwise collapses for the lax systems, and complete relational collapse for CK-box. This provides a compact baseline for finite model construction and semantic comparison across all four systems.
