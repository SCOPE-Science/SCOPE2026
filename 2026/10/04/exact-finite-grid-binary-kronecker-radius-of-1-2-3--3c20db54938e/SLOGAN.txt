For the three characters \(\{1,2,3\}\) on \(\mathbb Z_N\), the exact binary angular Kronecker radius is \(\tfrac14\) for \(4\mid N\), \(\tfrac{N+2}{4N}\) for \(N\equiv2\pmod4\), and \(\tfrac{N+3}{4N}\) for odd \(N\). This exactly quantifies the finite cyclic sampling loss over the continuous radius \(\tfrac14\).
