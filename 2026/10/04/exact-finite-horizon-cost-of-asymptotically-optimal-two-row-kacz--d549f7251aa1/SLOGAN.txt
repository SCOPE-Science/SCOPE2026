For two-row cyclic Kaczmarz, the relaxation that minimizes asymptotic spectral radius is exactly a defective double-root choice, and its finite-sweep Euclidean norm is \(q^k(\sqrt{1+4k^2s^2/c^2}+2ks/c)\). It is strictly worse than the unrelaxed sweep at horizon one for every nonorthogonal row pair, with the crossover for nearly parallel rows occurring on the \(s^{-1/2}\) scale at critical scaled horizon \(\sqrt{3}/2\).
