# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reduces a 1-type over a finite meet-closed tree to the standard one-point generated extension \(A\subseteq\langle Ab\rangle\), which introduces at most \(b\) and the maximal meet \(c=\max\{a\wedge b:a\in A\}\). Quantifier elimination makes the resulting finite meet diagram complete. The three nonalgebraic cases are exhaustive, each has exactly one type for each of the \(m\) vertices/gaps, and density plus infinite ramification realizes them. The closure bound \(2n-1\) follows by induction from the same one-point generation lemma and has explicit sharp examples.

## Originality
PASS. The primary literature inspected contains all necessary structural ingredients but only qualitative or polynomial type-count bounds. Kaplan--Rzepecki--Siniora give the Fraïssé/QE framework and meet-closure description; Estevan--Kaplan give the local \(+2\) generation lemma and use polynomial finite-type counting for NIP; Mennuni classifies global invariant 1-types. Targeted searches for exact finite-parameter formulas, the coefficient four, and the sharp envelope \(8n-4\) did not locate an equivalent theorem. Residual folklore risk remains because the final deduction is short.

## Value
PASS. Exact finite-parameter type growth is a natural local-complexity invariant of an \(\omega\)-categorical dp-minimal Fraïssé theory. The formula sharpens a previously qualitative polynomial bound, separates the contribution of definable closure from the three geometric extension modes, and gives the exact extremal profile over parameter sets of fixed cardinality.

The finite checker is supportive rather than a substitute for the infinite proof. It exhaustively confirms the one-point extension signature count for old meet-trees of size at most four and the closure bound through seven ambient vertices.
