For the one-sided Gaussian model-X CRT with the marginal-covariance statistic, finite Monte Carlo resampling has an exact power law. Under \(\beta_n=\sigma h/\sqrt n\), exact-size resampling budgets incur an explicit negative \(1/B\) power correction; at \(\alpha=0.05\) and \(h=2\), \(B=19\) gives limiting power \(0.5579895753\) versus \(0.6387600313\) with an exact null quantile.
