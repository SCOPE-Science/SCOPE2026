In the two-item BTL accumulation model, the exact finite-round MSE is governed by a product \(\prod_{k\le t}(1-1/(mk^2))\), and the infinite-round risk inflation is \(m[1-\sin(\pi/\sqrt m)/(\pi/\sqrt m)]\). This is strictly below \(\pi^2/6\) at every finite batch size and converges to that universal constant as \(m\to\infty\), while the synthetic rounds leave the realized initial MLE unchanged in conditional expectation.
