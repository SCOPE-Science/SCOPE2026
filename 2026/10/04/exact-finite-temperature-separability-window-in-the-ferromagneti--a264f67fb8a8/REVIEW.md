# Same-model review

## Correctness

**PASS.** Direct exponentiation of the two invariant \(2\times2\) Hamiltonian blocks gives the Gibbs \(X\)-state. The standard \(X\)-state concurrence has exactly two candidate positive terms, reproducing the source's undercritical and overcritical expressions without relying on branch-order assumptions. Since \(\nu(D)=\sqrt{g^2+D^2}\) is monotone, each candidate has at most one threshold. Inverting the two hyperbolic inequalities gives the stated endpoints. Exact identities place the two thresholds on opposite sides of \(\nu_c=z+a\), and elementary low-temperature asymptotics show both converge to \(D_c\). The packaged checker independently tests the Gibbs-state conditions and boundary signs.

## Originality

**PASS, narrowly scoped.** The refined Gürkan--Pashaev source explicitly says that positive temperature creates a zero-concurrence interval containing \(D_c\), but the inspected full text leaves its endpoints graphical/implicit. The earlier 2007 preprint gives only the high-\(D\) condition for this ferromagnetic XYZ+DM setting. Targeted research-database and literature searches did not locate the inverse-hyperbolic endpoint formulas or a complete piecewise all-temperature zero-set classification.

The claim does not take ownership of the Hamiltonian, concurrence formulas, the level crossing, or the qualitative existence of a separable interval. Residual risk is that the elementary inversion has appeared in unindexed or differently parameterized later work.

## Value

**PASS.** The result converts a qualitative and plotted re-entrant-entanglement phenomenon into an exact phase diagram. It gives both experimentally relevant DM thresholds at any temperature, identifies when thermal noise has already pushed the left threshold to zero, and proves how the finite-temperature window is centered around the zero-temperature level crossing in the precise order \(D_-<D_c<D_+\). These are natural control parameters of the published model rather than an arbitrary slice.
