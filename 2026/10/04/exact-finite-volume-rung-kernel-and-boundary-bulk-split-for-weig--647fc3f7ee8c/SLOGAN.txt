The weighted finite ladder has an explicit rung determinantal kernel: the known infinite geometric kernel \(\rho\alpha^{|i-j|}\) is multiplied by an exact two-boundary correction. This makes the endpoint limit \(1-\alpha\) and the bulk limit \((1-\alpha)/(1+\alpha)\) simultaneously visible; at \(c=1\) they are \(\sqrt3-1\) and \(1/\sqrt3\).
