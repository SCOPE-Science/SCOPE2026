For equal-weight pairwise-independent fair signs, the first absolute moment has an exact finite-\(n\) spectrum: it can be as small as \(1\) at even lengths but as large as the parity-lattice chord immediately below \(\sqrt n\). Thus the normalized \(L_1\) size ranges from \(o(1)\) to \(1-o(1)\) relative to its fixed \(L_2\) norm.
