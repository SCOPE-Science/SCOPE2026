For the Yan–Jie–Zhang flow, the symmetry-related equilibria are stable for \(1<a<3/2\) and undergo simultaneous supercritical Hopf bifurcations at \(a=3/2\), with exact crossing speed \(6/13\) and first Lyapunov coefficient \(-2\sqrt3/3\). Every non-equilibrium bounded complete trajectory also obeys \(z(t)>1\) through an exact exponential-memory law; at the paper's \(a=3\), the central equilibrium is a real saddle rather than a saddle-focus.
