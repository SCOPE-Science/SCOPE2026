For two equiprobable scalar quadratic clients, exact SPAM has a closed-form first-moment phase boundary: \((2-p)\rho^2t^2<(1+t)^2\), where \(\rho=\delta/a\) and \(t=\gamma a\). At maximal heterogeneity and \(t=20\), stability requires \(p>0.8975\), sharply separating \(p=0.9\) from \(p=0.1\) at the first-moment level.
