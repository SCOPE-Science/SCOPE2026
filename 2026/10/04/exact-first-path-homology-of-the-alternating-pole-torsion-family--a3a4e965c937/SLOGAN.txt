For the alternating-pole digraph \(D_n\) with a central directed cycle of length \(2n\), non-regular integral path homology satisfies \(\widetilde H_1(D_n;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z\) for every \(n\ge2\). This proves the published all-parameter torsion conjecture and identifies the free summand.
