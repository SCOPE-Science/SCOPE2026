# Verification

The theorem is checked at two levels. First, the proof gives an all-parameter integral presentation: the degree-two boundary subgroup is generated by the cycles \(R_i\) and the same-parity differences of \(T_i\), while the degree-one cycle lattice has the single primitive alternating relation. The quotient is therefore \(\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z\).

Second, `verify.py` reconstructs the digraph directly from its definition and enumerates every allowed elementary two-path for \(2\le n\le12\). It builds the non-regular degree-two cancellation basis from endpoint classes and verifies that no illegal boundary term survives. It checks \(\operatorname{rank}\Omega_2=4n-2\) and cycle rank \(4n-1\), then prints `ALTERNATING_POLE_PATH_H1_VERIFY_OK`.

The finite computation is not an exhaustive proof over all \(n\); it is a regression check for the explicit structural argument. The statement is limited to the non-regular integral convention used in the motivating source.
