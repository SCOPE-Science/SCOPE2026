For the alternating-divisor totient gap \(\Delta(n)=\chi(n)-\varphi(n)\), the zero fiber is exactly \(1\) and the primes, the unit fiber is exactly the prime squares together with \(8\), and the fiber \(2\) is exactly \(27\) together with \(2q\) for odd primes \(q\). Thus every composite integer satisfies \(\chi(n)>\varphi(n)\).
