Among five-player weighted simple voting games, exactly \(17\) of \(117\) isomorphism classes violate Deegan–Packel local monotonicity. The sparsest violations have exactly \(3\) minimal winning coalitions and form one class, represented by \([8;5,3,2,1,1]\), where a strictly more desirable player receives \(1/6\) versus \(2/9\) for the less desirable player.
