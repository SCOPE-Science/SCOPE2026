For Bell-diagonal two-qubit states at fixed concurrence \(C\), Hilbert–Schmidt geometric discord fills an exact interval whose upper extremizer switches at \(C=1/13\): a rank-three Bell face wins below the crossover, while the isotropic Werner ray wins above it. The lower boundary for \(C>0\) is \(C^2/2\), attained on a Bell-rank-two edge.
