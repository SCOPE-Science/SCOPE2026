Adjoining \(r\ge2\) incomparable maxima to a finite \(T_0\)-space turns each fixed-point-free self-map \(g\) into exactly \((u(g)+r-1)^r\) fixed-point-free extensions, where \(u(g)\) counts common upper bounds of \(g(X)\). For the non-Hausdorff suspension this gives the exact count \(\sum_g(u(g)+1)^2\).
