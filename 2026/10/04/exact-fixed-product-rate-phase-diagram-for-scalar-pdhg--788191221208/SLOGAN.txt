For the symmetric scalar strongly convex-concave saddle, fixed-product PDHG tuning has a sharp transition: below \(\tau\sigma=1/2\) the two fastest ratios are asymmetric critical-damping pairs, while above it the unique fastest ratio is balanced. The globally fastest constant pair is \(\tau=\sigma=1/\sqrt2\) with factor \(\sqrt2-1\), whereas the classical residual-balancing updates preserve the initial product exactly.
