At Rényi order \(2\), a fixed-sum transfer between two Bernoulli parameters has an exact quadratic collision law, so the entropy optimizer is obtained by projecting one explicit autocorrelation-dependent product onto the feasible interval. For two coins, balancing is optimal exactly when \(|p+q-1|\le1/\sqrt3\); outside that band an unequal or deterministic split has strictly larger collision entropy.
