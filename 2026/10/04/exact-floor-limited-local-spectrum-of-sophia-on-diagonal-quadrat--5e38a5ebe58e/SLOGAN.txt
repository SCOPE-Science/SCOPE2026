On diagonal quadratics, Sophia's limiting local mode depends on curvature only through \(\chi=\min\{\eta/\gamma,\eta\lambda/\varepsilon\}\). When \(\eta/\gamma\) lies in the momentum plateau, every curvature above \(\lambda_{\rm plateau}=\varepsilon(1-\sqrt{\beta_1})/[\eta(1+\sqrt{\beta_1})]\) has the same exact local spectral radius \(\sqrt{\beta_1}\), while flatter directions slow down because of the denominator floor.
