For a nonzero function on \(\mathbb F_5^d\) supported exactly on an affine parallelogram, the Fourier-support size is precisely one of \(\{16,20,21,22,23,24,25\}5^{d-2}\). The missing values \(17\cdot5^{d-2}\), \(18\cdot5^{d-2}\), and \(19\cdot5^{d-2}\) arise from a Möbius-rigidity obstruction for nonfactorable bilinear polynomials on the fifth-root grid.
