Every regular \(n\)-gon has an exact Fraenkel asymmetry obtained from one circular-cap angle: \(\mathcal A_F(P_n)=\frac{2n}{\pi}(\beta_n-\sin\beta_n\cos\beta_n)\), with \(\beta_n=\arccos(\cos(\pi/n)/\sqrt{\sin(2\pi/n)/(2\pi/n)})\). This yields \(\mathcal A_F(P_n)\sim4\pi^2/(9\sqrt3\,n^2)\) and makes the regular-polygon perimeter/asymmetry constant equal to \(3\sqrt{3\pi}/4\).\n
