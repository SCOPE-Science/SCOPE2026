For the two-dimensional nonabelian Lie algebra \(L\) over \(\mathbb F_q\), the rank-\(n\) relatively free algebra has exact dimension \(n+(n-1)(q^n-1)\). Its derived ideal is the space of pointwise orthogonal sections \(F(a)\in a^\perp\) over every nonzero \(a\in\mathbb F_q^n\), with value zero at the origin.
