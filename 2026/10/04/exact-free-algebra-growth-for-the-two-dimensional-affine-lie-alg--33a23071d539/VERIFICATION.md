# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

A relatively free algebra in the variety generated by a finite algebra is realized by the algebra of term functions on \(L^n\) generated by coordinate projections. Writing \(x_i=a_ie+b_if\), every commutator is \(f\)-valued and linear in \(b\); the basic coefficient vectors are \(s_{ij}(a)=a_i e_j-a_j e_i\). Bracketing an \(f\)-valued term with \(x_r\) multiplies its coefficient by \(-a_r\), so the derived ideal contains every polynomial-function multiple of every \(s_{ij}\). Since over \(\mathbb F_q\) every scalar function on \(k^n\) is represented by a polynomial, coefficients can be localized pointwise. For each nonzero \(a\), the vectors \(s_{ij}(a)\) span \(a^\perp\), while all vanish at the origin; conversely all brackets stay in this module. This proves the intrinsic description. The generator span has dimension \(n\) and intersects the derived ideal trivially, yielding the formula. The actual verifier independently closes term functions under brackets for small prime-field cases, and a separate reimplementation reproduced dimensions 5, 17, and 10 for \((q,n)=(2,2),(2,3),(3,2)\).

## originality

PASS

Bahturin--Olshanskii's recent full primary paper contains a broader semidirect-product family and, when the irreducible abelian module has dimension one, includes this same two-dimensional affine Lie algebra. Their result determines only the exponential growth rate \(q\) through upper and lower estimates; it does not give the exact rank-\(n\) dimension or the pointwise hyperplane description of the derived ideal. Resultary returned no earlier exact formula. A highly relevant 1979 paper by Bahturin on identities of free polynilpotent Lie algebras could not be obtained in full after ordinary open-access attempts and an authorized institutional retrieval timed out; that source is therefore retained as a specific residual risk, not treated as evidence of noncoverage.

## value

PASS

The two-dimensional affine Lie algebra is the smallest nonabelian Lie algebra and appears as the base case of a recent finite-field growth construction. Replacing a known exponential rate by an exact formula at every free rank, together with a concrete intrinsic description of the derived ideal, is a natural structural refinement likely to be reusable in finite-field PI and locally finite variety questions.
