# Review
## Correctness
PASS. The theorem is finite and exhaustive. Exact Gaussian-integer Fourier sums test spectral orthogonality, and the tiling test uses the equivalent difference-set condition together with the exact cardinality product. Translation normalization loses no spectra or complements. All \(65535\) nonempty subsets were covered, and the affine quotient uses all \(96\) invertible linear maps and all \(16\) translations.

## Originality
PASS. Malikiosis (arXiv:2005.05800) surveys positive two-generator results through \(\mathbb Z_p\times\mathbb Z_{p^n}\), which does not contain \(\mathbb Z_4^2\). Zhou (arXiv:2412.16865) directly studies \(\mathbb Z_{p^2}^2\) but proves special-complement structure rather than the all-subset Fuglede equivalence or census. Fan--Kadir (arXiv:2609.00087) gives failures only in much larger finite abelian \(p\)-groups. Exact alias and statement searches did not locate the \(2155\)-set or \(19\)-orbit classification.

## Value
PASS. The group \(\mathbb Z_4^2\) is the smallest square \(\mathbb Z_{p^2}^2\), just beyond the best-known positive two-generator families cited by the anchor literature. A complete exact census and affine classification is a reusable boundary datum for the finite Fuglede problem, especially in view of later counterexamples in larger exponent-four groups.

The main limitation is priority risk from unindexed small-group computations or theses; no statement about larger square prime-power groups is made.
