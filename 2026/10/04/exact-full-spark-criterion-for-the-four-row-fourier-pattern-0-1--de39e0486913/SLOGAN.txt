For every \(N\ge5\), the Fourier row pattern \(\{0,1,3,4\}\) is full spark exactly when \(\gcd(N,6)=1\). It is divisor-uniform exactly when \(3\nmid N\) and \(4\nmid N\), so every \(N\equiv2\) or \(10\pmod{12}\) gives an infinite-family analogue of the classical uniformly distributed but singular order-\(10\) example.
