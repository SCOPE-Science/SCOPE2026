For the Lorentz plane \(N_\omega(s,t)=\max\{|s|,|t|\}+\omega\min\{|s|,|t|\}\), the Gao constant equals \((1+\omega)^2/[2(1+\omega^2)]\) up to \(\omega=\sqrt2-1\) and \(1/(1+\omega^2)\) thereafter. The transition switches the extremizer from a coordinate-swapped pair to a sign-reflected pair and settles the \(r=1\) endpoint of the Lorentz range explicitly left open in the motivating source.
