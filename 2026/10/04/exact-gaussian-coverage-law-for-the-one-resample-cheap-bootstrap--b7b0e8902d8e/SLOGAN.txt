For a Gaussian sample mean, Lam's one-resample Cheap Bootstrap has an exact occupancy-mixture coverage law and is strictly anti-conservative at every finite \(n\). Its coverage deficit is \(a_\alpha(3+5a_\alpha^2)/(2\pi(1+a_\alpha^2)^2n)+O(n^{-3/2})\), making the general \(O(n^{-1})\) error explicit in closed form.
