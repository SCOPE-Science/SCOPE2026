For the commensurate \(2:1:1\) honeycomb, the lower Exner–Turek gap condition has an exact staircase count: the \(m\)-th GC2 interval appears exactly when \(|\eta b|>\sqrt{5/3}((m+1/2)\pi-\operatorname{sgn}(\eta)\arcsin(1/4))\). This sharpens the known rational-ratio statement from “at most finitely many” GC2 gaps to a complete threshold law for the simplest nonequilateral commensurate dilation.
