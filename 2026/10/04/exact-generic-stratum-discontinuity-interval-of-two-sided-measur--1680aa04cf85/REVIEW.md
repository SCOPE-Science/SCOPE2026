# Same-model review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The proof reduces the disturbance of a Werner state under arbitrary product-basis dephasing to the single overlap functional \(S(U)=\sum_{i,j}|U_{ij}|^4\). The bounds \(1\le S(U)\le m\) are sharp, and connectedness of \(U(m)\) makes every intermediate value attainable. Compactness of the marginal-eigenbasis flag manifolds gives the converse: every limit along nondegenerate-marginal states must arise from some limiting \(U\). Explicit product perturbations realize every such value. The accompanying script independently replays the matrix identities in several dimensions.

## Originality

**PASS, narrowly scoped.** The direct source already proves noncontinuity and chooses aligned and mutually unbiased limiting bases in its Werner-state example. Those facts are treated as prior work. The accepted claim is only the exact complete cluster interval over the nondegenerate-marginal stratum, the arbitrary-basis formula, the proof that there are no additional cluster values, and the universal relative oscillation \(1/(m+1)\). Targeted searches for these formulations did not locate an equivalent result. Later trace-norm and fidelity-based MiN papers concern different measures.

## Value

**PASS.** The source's discontinuity is a qualitative pathology of a measurement-defined correlation quantity. The exact cluster set turns that pathology into a dimension-dependent local stability statement: it quantifies the full ambiguity caused by arbitrarily small generic perturbations and identifies the complete interpolation between its two extremal basis geometries. This is directly relevant to interpreting numerical or experimental estimates near maximally mixed marginals.
