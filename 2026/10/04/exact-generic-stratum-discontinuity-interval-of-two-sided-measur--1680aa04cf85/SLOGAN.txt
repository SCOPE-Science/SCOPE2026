For every \(m\otimes m\) Werner state, the two-sided Hilbert--Schmidt MiN has an exact generic-stratum cluster interval: nearby states with nondegenerate marginals can converge to precisely the values from \(\frac{m}{m+1}N_{AB}(\rho_x)\) up to \(N_{AB}(\rho_x)\), and no others. The entire discontinuity is controlled by the fourth-power overlap \(S(U)=\sum_{i,j}|U_{ij}|^4\), with aligned and mutually unbiased bases giving the two endpoints.
