# Review

## Correctness

PASS. Under the common constant subgradient, the published DoG and DoWG formulas reduce exactly to the displayed scalar recurrences. DoG's logarithmic product expansion has a summable third-order remainder. DoWG's normalized state is a one-dimensional monotone map with a unique positive fixed point; its derivative is strictly below one there, which proves summable ratio errors and the geometric product asymptotic.

Risk: only the pre-hit startup phase is classified.

## Originality

PASS. The defining papers motivate rapid distance adaptation and establish convergence and pointwise comparisons, but the inspected material does not state the exact DoG product asymptotic, the DoWG Tribonacci ratio, or the resulting first-hit separation. Later comparative work remains qualitative about DoWG's greater aggressiveness. Focused published-record searches found no implication-equivalent result.

Residual risk: an equivalent short recurrence calculation may exist in unindexed optimizer notes.

## Value

PASS. Escaping a conservative initial distance estimate is the defining parameter-free adaptation problem these methods address. The theorem gives a trajectory-level explanation of DoWG's more aggressive weighting and identifies a qualitative change in startup complexity on a canonical coherent signal, without converting that fact into an unsupported universal performance claim.

Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
