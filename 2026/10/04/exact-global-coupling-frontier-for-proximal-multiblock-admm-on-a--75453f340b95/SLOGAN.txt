On a minimal nonconvex three-state dynamics chain, equal-parameter proximal multiblock ADMM is Schur stable exactly under two explicit inequalities in the curvature ratio \(\chi=a/\rho\) and proximal scale \(p=\rho\eta\). At \(\chi=4\), every block solve is strongly convex for \(p<1/2\) but the coupled method is stable only for \(p<1/10\), showing that blockwise proximal convexification does not guarantee global ADMM stability.
