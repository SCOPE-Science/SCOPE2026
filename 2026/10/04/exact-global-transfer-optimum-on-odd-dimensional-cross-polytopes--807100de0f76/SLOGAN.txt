For every cross-polytope graph with \(N=4k+2\) vertices, the exact best antipodal transfer probability is \(\cos^2(\pi/N)\), attained at \(t=\pi/2\pm\pi/N\). Optimizing the time improves the near-perfect-transfer infidelity from order \(N^{-1}\) at the previously evaluated time to order \(N^{-2}\).
