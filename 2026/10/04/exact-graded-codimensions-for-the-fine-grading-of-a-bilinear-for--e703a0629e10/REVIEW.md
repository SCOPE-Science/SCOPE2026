# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. For a fixed labeled degree tuple, each input homogeneous component is zero- or one-dimensional, so the quotient dimension is at most one. Two or more odd nonzero-degree multiplicities force the total degree outside the support. With at most one odd multiplicity, an explicit bracketing pairs equal basis vectors and is nonzero because \(\beta(v_i,v_i)\ne0\). Fourier counting then yields the closed formula. The packaged checker confirms the finite combinatorics on a broad small-parameter grid.

## Originality
PASS. The closest recent paper, DOI 10.1016/j.ffa.2026.102826, gives the fine-grading identities and a basis of the relatively free graded algebra over finite fields, but its inspected full text contains no codimension, cocharacter, or PI-exponent statement. Semantic research-record searches and prior accepted-claim checks found no equivalent formula. The older arXiv:2009.02530 extension to \(B_n\) uses the scalar \(\mathbb Z_2\)-grading, not the fine grading. Residual risk remains that older graded-codimension literature may encode the same count under different terminology.

## Value
PASS. The formula gives the complete multilinear graded codimension sequence, not a spot computation, and shows that its exponential growth rate is exactly \(n+1=\dim B_n\). The Klein specialization gives an explicit closed sequence for symmetric \(2\times2\) matrices. The result is field-independent across all characteristics other than \(2\), whereas the motivating fine-grading identity theorem is finite-field-specific.

Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
