For the fine \((\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^n\)-grading of the bilinear-form Jordan algebra \(B_n\), every multilinear degree assignment contributes either \(0\) or \(1\), controlled only by a parity vector. This gives the exact field-independent formula \(c_m^G=2^{-n}\sum_{j=0}^n\binom{n}{j}(n+1-2j)^{m+1}\) and graded growth exponent \(n+1\).
