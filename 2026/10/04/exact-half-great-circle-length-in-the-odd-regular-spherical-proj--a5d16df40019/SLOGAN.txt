For every odd \(n\ge3\), the unique spherical member of the regular \(n\)-gon reflective projective-billiard family has a midpoint orbit of exact spherical length \(\pi\). By local constancy, the connected open primitive family around that witness also has common length exactly \(\pi\), sharpening the source's general congruence \(L\equiv\pi\pmod{2\pi}\).
