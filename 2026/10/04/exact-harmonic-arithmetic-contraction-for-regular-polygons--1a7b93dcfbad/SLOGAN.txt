For a centered regular odd \(n\)-gon, the harmonic symmetrization fits optimally into exactly \(4s/(s+1)^2\) times the arithmetic symmetrization, with \(s=\sec(\pi/n)\). Thus regular odd polygons themselves attain the sharp planar harmonic–arithmetic contraction envelope, and the deficit from \(1\) is quartic: \(1-\beta_n\sim\pi^4/(16n^4)\).\n
