For an exponential sample, the minimum is independent of the pair formed by the range and unbiased sample variance. The range and variance themselves have exact positive covariance \(\bigl(H_{n-1}^2+H_{n-1}^{(2)}\bigr)/((n-1)\lambda^3)\), while their correlation decays like \((\sqrt3/(2\pi))(\log n)^2/\sqrt n\).
