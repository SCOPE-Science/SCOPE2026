For equal-weight Aggregated Momentum on any strictly convex quadratic, the exact constant-step stability ceiling is \(\gamma L<2K/\sum_i(1+\beta_i)^{-1}\), twice the harmonic mean of the single-buffer factors \(1+\beta_i\). The only unit-circle crossing is at \(-1\), so heterogeneous passive damping reshapes the stable roots but cannot push the absolute quadratic stepsize ceiling beyond that of the largest-\(\beta\) buffer.
