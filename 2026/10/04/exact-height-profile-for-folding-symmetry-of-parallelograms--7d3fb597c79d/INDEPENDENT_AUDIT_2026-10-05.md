# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For fixed height the first two corrected fold terms are increasing in shear while the third is strictly decreasing, so the unique minimizer is the first crossing. Solving the two candidate crossings and comparing them gives the cubic transition. Independent reconstruction gave s₀=0.8060634335253696 and h₀=0.591829148600584; a 10,001-point shear grid over 100 heights agreed with the closed profile to mesh-scale error.

## originality

PASS

Goenka et al. provide the corrected three-fold formula and prove the global unattained infimum by sending normalized height to zero. They do not optimize at each fixed positive height, identify the unique shear as a function of height, or locate the interior three-mechanism transition. Nowicka's earlier global bound is explicitly corrected by the later paper.

## value

PASS

Normalized height measures distance from the degenerate extremizing regime, so the exact constrained profile is a natural stability theorem. The interior change of active fold mechanism and sharp quadratic penalty provide structural information beyond the published global infimum.
