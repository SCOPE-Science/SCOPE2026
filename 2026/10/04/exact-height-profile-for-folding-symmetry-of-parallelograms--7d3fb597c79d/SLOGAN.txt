At fixed normalized height \(h\), parallelogram folding symmetry has an exact sharp two-branch minimum, with a unique optimal shear and a phase transition at \(h_0\approx0.591829148600584\). Near the degenerate golden-ratio extremizing regime, the excess over \(1/\varphi\) is sharply \(\frac{5-\sqrt5}{20}h^2+O(h^4)\).
