The Q-order-one exact-line-search BFGS construction of Liu, Li, and Wen has a stronger feature than stated: its Hessian approximations satisfy \(\|B_k-\nabla^2F(0)\|_{\mathrm{op}}\to0\). Thus the failure of every adjacent-iterate power \(p>1\) persists even under full matrix recovery of the true Hessian.
