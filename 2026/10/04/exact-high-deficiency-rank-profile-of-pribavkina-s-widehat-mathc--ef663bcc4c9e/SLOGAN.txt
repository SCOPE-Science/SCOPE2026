For Pribavkina's completed semi-flower automata \(\widehat{\mathcal F}(k,u)\), every target rank from \(k\) down to \(1\) has an exact threshold: rank at most \(k-r\) first appears at length \(k+r(k+1)\). The witness \(u(au)^r\) is optimal for every letter \(a\), and the lower bound comes from distinct phase classes forcing nonoverlapping copies of the unbordered word \(u\).
