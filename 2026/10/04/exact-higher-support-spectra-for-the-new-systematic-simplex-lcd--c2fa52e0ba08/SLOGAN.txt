For the new binary and ternary LCD-optimal codes generated by \([I_m\mid S_q(m)]\), where \(S_q(m)\) contains every nonzero vector of \(\mathbb F_q^m\), the full generalized Hamming hierarchy is \(d_r=q^m-q^{m-r}+r\). A closed inclusion-exclusion formula also gives the exact number of subcodes at every possible support size.
