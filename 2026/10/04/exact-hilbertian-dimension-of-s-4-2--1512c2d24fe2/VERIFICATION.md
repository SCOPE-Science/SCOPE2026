# Verification

The proof is symbolic and self-contained. It uses the exact two-eigenvalue trace identity for \(A^*A\), diagonalization of a positive Hermitian form, holomorphic constant-modulus rigidity for the coordinate-torus action, coefficient comparison in a biquadratic polynomial, and a direct determinant-polynomial proof of the dimension bound for singular subspaces of \(M_2(\mathbb C)\).

No numerical experiment is used. The positive examples are rank-one row embeddings, whose sole nonzero singular value is exactly the Euclidean norm of the row. The result is limited to complex-linear exact isometries in the stated dimensions and does not assert an independent audit or external validation.
