For the fixed-temperature regret-to-confidence boundary \(R_\beta(B)=A/[\beta(1-\beta)]+B/(1-\beta)\), the unique horizon-optimal pre-data choice is \(\beta^*=\sqrt A/(\sqrt A+\sqrt{A+B})\). It lowers the source's \(\beta=1/2\) threshold by exactly \((\sqrt{A+B}-\sqrt A)^2\) and asymptotically halves the dominant threshold when \(B/A\) is large.
