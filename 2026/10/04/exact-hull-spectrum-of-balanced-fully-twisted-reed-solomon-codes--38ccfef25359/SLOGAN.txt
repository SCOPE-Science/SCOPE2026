In the balanced fully twisted Reed–Solomon family, the hull splits into independent \(2\times2\) Vandermonde blocks, so its dimension is exactly \(\mathbf 1_{\{\eta_1^2=-1\}}+\#\{i\ge2:\eta_i+\eta_{k+2-i}=0\}\). This turns the recent sufficient LCD test into an if-and-only-if criterion and yields the complete hull-size enumerator.
