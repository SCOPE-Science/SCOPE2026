# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Equilibrium elimination is immediate from \(dx/dt\) in the fourth coordinate when \(d>0\). Direct determinant expansion of the actual Jacobian gives the submitted factorization; an independent symbolic characteristic-polynomial calculation in this audit matched it. For the cubic, the exact sign evaluations are \(q_d(-40)=16000+65d>0\), \(q_d(0)=25d>0\), and \(q_d(25)=-10000<0\), together with the cubic end behavior, producing one root in each stated interval. These three disjoint real roots exhaust the cubic; \(-3\) is a distinct negative root because \(q_d(-3)>0\).

The source article and preprint print the same four-dimensional system and Jacobian but report the parameter-independent eigenvalue list \(-40,25,-3,0\). Focused same-system searches did not locate a correction deriving the \(d\)-dependent cubic or the all-\(d>0\) index-two hyperbolicity theorem. Resultary returned the submitted record and corrections for other Lorenz-type systems, not a prior same-system factorization.
