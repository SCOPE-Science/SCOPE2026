For the flow \(\dot x=40(y-x)+w\), \(\dot y=10x+25y-xz\), \(\dot z=xy-3z\), \(\dot w=dx\) with \(d>0\), the origin is always hyperbolic with a two-dimensional unstable manifold: its spectrum has one root below \(-40\), the root \(-3\), one root in \((0,25)\), and one root above \(25\). The zero eigenvalue printed in the source is therefore spurious.
