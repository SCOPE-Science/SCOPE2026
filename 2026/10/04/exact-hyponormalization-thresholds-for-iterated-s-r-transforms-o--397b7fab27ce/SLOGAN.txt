For positive real quaternionic unilateral weighted shifts, arbitrary iterated \(S_r\)-transforms have an exact dyadic formula, and \(p\)-hyponormality at level \(k\) is equivalent to \(w_m\le w_{m+2^k}\) for all \(m\ge2^k-1\). Thus the hyponormal iterates form an exponent-independent tail, and every finite threshold or permanent failure is realizable.
