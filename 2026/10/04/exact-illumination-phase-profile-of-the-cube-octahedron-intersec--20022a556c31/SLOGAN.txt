For \(K_c=[-1,1]^3\cap\{\lVert x\rVert_1\le c\}\), the illumination number jumps from \(6\) at the octahedron to the dimension-minimal value \(4\) for every \(1<c<3\), then jumps to \(8\) at the cube. The same four tetrahedral directions illuminate the entire open family, including the regular truncated octahedron, cuboctahedron, and regular truncated cube.\n
