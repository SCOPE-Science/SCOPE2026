For every \(n\ge2\), the commuting graph of \(2\times2\) upper triangular matrices over \(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z\) has independence number \(\psi(n)=n\prod_{p\mid n}(1+1/p)=|\mathbb P^1(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)|\). This answers the 2024 independence-number question and shows its proposed formula fails already at \(n=4\), where the exact value is \(6\), not \(48\).
