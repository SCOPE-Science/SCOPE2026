For every finite-dimensional bipartite Gibbs state, the infinite-temperature mutual information has exact leading term \(\beta^2\operatorname{Tr}(H_{\mathrm{int}}^2)/(2d)\), where \(H_{\mathrm{int}}\) is the Hilbert--Schmidt interaction projection of the Hamiltonian. Disjoint Ising bonds realize \(I=m[x\tanh x-\log\cosh x]\), proving that both quadratic temperature decay and linear boundary scaling are asymptotically sharp.
