For the classical \(D'_n\) slowly synchronizing automata, the first rank drops have an exact rigid cost: \(\lambda_n(s)=3s-2\) with unique optimum \(a(b^2a)^{s-1}\) up to \(h_n\), where \(h_n=2n/3\) if \(3\mid n\) and \(h_n=\lfloor n/3\rfloor+1\) otherwise. The next deficiency is strictly more expensive, exposing a mod-three saturation boundary in the hole orbit.
