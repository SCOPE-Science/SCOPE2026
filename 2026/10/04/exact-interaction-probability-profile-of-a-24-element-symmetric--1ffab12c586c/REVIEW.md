# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof was reconstructed directly from the two group laws of the order-\(24\) brace. The fixed-space and centralizer counts in \(H=\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb F_2)\) give \(96\), \(168\), and \(60\) ordered pairs for \(\operatorname{Pb}\), \(P_{\lambda}\), and \(P_t\), respectively. An exhaustive checker over all \(576\) ordered pairs independently reproduces those counts, as well as the underlying-group commuting counts \(288\) and \(120\).

## Originality
PASS. The motivating full text was inspected at the definitions, Lemma 5.1, Lemma 6.1, and Example 6.12. It gives the brace and the Sylow-local value \(5/12\), but not the global values \(1/6\), \(7/24\), or \(5/48\). The earlier foundational commuting-probability paper was also inspected and predates this example. Exact-value, construction-alias, published-index, and own-ledger searches found no equivalent or stronger statement. The remaining risk is that a computational brace table could encode the same values without searchable prose.

## Value
PASS. This is a motivated exact invariant calculation on the paper's main symmetric boundary example. It simultaneously calibrates three interaction probabilities, proves strictness of both underlying-group bounds for \(\operatorname{Pb}\), and gives the exact local/global ratio \(5/2\). The claim is intentionally limited to this example and does not overstate a general theorem.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest sources are arXiv:2609.16269v1, which contains the example and the local \(5/12\) calculation, and arXiv:2603.16771v1, which develops global brace commuting probability and its bounds. The new claim is narrower than a classification and does not determine all possible probability profiles or local-to-global ratios.
