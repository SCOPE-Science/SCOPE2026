For the binary asymmetric Damerau channel with exactly one \(0\)-deletion and at most one adjacent transposition, the number of length-\(n\) parents of a received word depends only on which zero gaps are nonempty. The worst inverse list size is \(\lfloor n^2/2\rfloor-n+2\), attained by a unique alternating word for even \(n\ge4\) and by exactly \((n-1)/2\) one-\(11\) words for odd \(n\ge5\).
