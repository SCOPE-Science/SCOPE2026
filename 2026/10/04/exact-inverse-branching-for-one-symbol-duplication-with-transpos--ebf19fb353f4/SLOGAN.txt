For one-symbol duplication with transposition, a received word has exactly \(r-s\) distinct parents, where \(r\) is its number of constant runs and \(s\) counts symbols whose first run is a singleton. The worst-case inverse degree is \(n-1\) for source length \(n\ge2\), and the exact q-ary edge count is \(q^n((q-1)n-q(q-2))+q(q-2)(q-1)^n\).
