# Review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The proof reconstructs all characters of the \((\mathbf Z/n)^5\)-cover, reduces their \(H^1\) to divisors on \(\operatorname{Bl}_4\mathbf P^2\), and computes those dimensions by Riemann--Roch and Serre duality. Only \((d,k)=(2,1)\) and \((4,4)\) survive. Their character counts are respectively \(4\binom{n-1}{2}\) and \(\binom{n-1}{2}\), giving the exact formula. The finite verifier independently checks exponents \(2\) through \(8\), but the general quantifier rests on the proof, not enumeration.

## Originality
**PASS.** Anghel's initiating paper treats the same second Hirzebruch family but proves only \(q(Y'_n)\ge1\) from one character. The closest older same-object source, Bauer--Catanese on complete-quadrangle Hirzebruch--Kummer covers, was inspected together with exact-formula and Hodge-invariant searches; no exact irregularity formula was located in the inspected material. The residual possibility of differently phrased classical coverage is recorded rather than treated as eliminated by search failure.

## Value
**PASS.** Irregularity and geometric genus are fundamental Hodge invariants of this classical cover family. The exact value also quantifies the degree-zero local-cohomology defect in Anghel's new quasi-Gorenstein section rings without graded maximal Cohen--Macaulay modules. The result therefore completes a natural invariant left only qualitatively positive in the initiating source.

## Closest literature and limitations
Anghel supplies the cover, its Chern/Euler invariants, the quasi-Gorenstein section-ring family, and one contributing irregularity character. Bauer--Catanese supplies the closest older explicit same-object model and rigidity results. The present calculation does not determine the Albanese map or variety, the full Hodge ring, or the full graded local-cohomology module.
