For the circular sharpness family in arXiv:2609.28447, the displayed feasible ellipsoid is in fact the unique John ellipsoid of the retained half-cone in every dimension \(n\ge2\). Hence its finite-dimensional ratio is exactly \(\tfrac12(n/(n-1))^{(n-1)/2}\), while no claim is made that this is the optimal ratio among all \(n\)-dimensional bodies.
