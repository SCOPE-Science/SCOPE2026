The \(K_{2,5}\)-free cograph Turán number is completely explicit: after two small boundary orders, it is \(3(n-1)-\delta_{(n-1)\bmod5}\) with correction vector \((0,1,2,3,2)\). The exceptional six-vertex \(K_{2,4}\)-free block propagates into the optimal \(K_{2,5}\) structures and creates a second extremal family in one residue class.
