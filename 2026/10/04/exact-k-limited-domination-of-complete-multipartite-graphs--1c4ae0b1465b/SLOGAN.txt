For a complete multipartite graph, \(k\)-limited domination reduces exactly to a one-dimensional capacity threshold: \(\gamma_k^{\mathrm L}(G)=N-k-T_k(G)\), where \(T_k(G)\) is the largest feasible outside-load surplus. In balanced \(r\)-partite graphs this becomes the closed form \(rm-k-\lfloor k/(r-1)\rfloor\).
