# Review

## Correctness

PASS. The proof does not assume the optimal symmetry center. For \(S(c)=K\cap(2c-K)\), Minkowski interpolation satisfies
\[
\lambda S(c_0)+(1-\lambda)S(c_1)\subseteq S(\lambda c_0+(1-\lambda)c_1).
\]
Planar Brunn–Minkowski therefore makes \(|S(c)|^{1/2}\) concave. Averaging a center over the rotational orbit of a regular polygon proves that the polygon center is optimal. For odd \(n\), the normals of \(P_n\) and \(-P_n\) interlace into the \(2n\) equally spaced normals of a regular \(2n\)-gon with unchanged inradius, and the area ratio is exactly the stated tangent expression. The classical uniqueness theorem for centrally symmetric kernels then gives the optimizer statement. Direct polygon-intersection computations for \(n=3,5,7,9\) reproduce the formula but are not used as proof.
