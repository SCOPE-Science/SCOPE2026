For a regular hyperbolic \(d\)-simplex of edge length \(\ell\), Lassak thickness is exactly \(\operatorname{arsinh}\sqrt{(q-1)(dq+1)/(k((d-k)q+1))}\), where \(q=\cosh\ell\) and \(k=\lceil d/2\rceil\). The minimizing supports are exactly those containing a \(\lfloor d/2\rfloor\)-face, giving \(\binom{d+1}{k}\) minimizers and, in dimension three, precisely the six edge supports.\n
