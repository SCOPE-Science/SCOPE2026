For every finite abelian kernel \(A\), the subgroup and cyclic-subgroup commutativity degrees of \(\operatorname{Dic}(A,y)\) admit exact formulas controlled by the quotient \(Q=A/\langle y\rangle\). Two outside subgroups \(H(B,x)\) and \(H(C,z)\) permute exactly when \((xz^{-1})^2\in BC\), which turns the arbitrary-kernel problem into explicit subgroup-lattice and \(2\)-torsion sums.
