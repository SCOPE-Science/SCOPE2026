For the canonical trapezoidal mollification of the planar disc multiplier, the endpoint convolution-kernel norm has an exact logarithmic coefficient: \(\|K_\delta\|_{4/3}^{4/3}/\log(1/\delta)\to 2^{1/3}\pi^{-2}\mathrm B(1/2,7/6)\). This sharpens the known two-sided order without asserting an exact constant for the much larger multiplier norm.
