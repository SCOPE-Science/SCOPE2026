For the uniform nodal piecewise-affine interpolant of Manià's singular minimizer \(u_*(x)=x^{1/3}\), the exact original energy is \(I[u_n]=Cn-\frac{1}{984150}n^{-4}+O(n^{-5})\) with \(C=0.07619104142716668\ldots\). The interpolants converge uniformly while their energies diverge linearly, quantifying a canonical finite-element manifestation of the Lavrentiev phenomenon.
