For restricted central sections of the cross-polytope, the two normals proposed in the recent maximizer conjecture are simultaneously strict local maxima in dimensions \(5,6,7\). The sparse normal has exact log-volume Hessian spectrum \(-3\) and \((n-8)/n\), so its local stability persists past the conjectured global switch at \(n=6\) and is lost only after the quadratic degeneracy at \(n=8\).
