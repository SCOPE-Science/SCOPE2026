For every infinite \(\Gamma\), every real Hilbert space \(H\) with \(\dim H\ge2\), and every \(1\le p<\infty\), Hardtke’s local-octahedrality index of \(\ell_p(\Gamma;H)\) is exactly \(2^{1/\min\{p,2\}}\). The value changes from \(2^{1/p}\) to \(\sqrt2\) at \(p=2\), exposing a genuinely vector-valued effect for \(p>2\).
