For every nontrivial factorized smooth product singular kernel and every compact tensor input with nonzero masses, the small-level distribution has the exact form \(C\lambda^{-1}\log(1/\lambda)\), with \(C\) given by the product of the masses and spherical \(L^1\) angular norms. This gives an exact coefficient behind the known product \(L\log L\) endpoint scale, not merely comparability.
