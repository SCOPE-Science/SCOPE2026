At the critical power \(p-1\), the compactly supported logarithmic modification \(w_\gamma(x)=|x|^{p-1}(\log(e/|x|))^\gamma\) crosses into the segment-multiplier class exactly when \(\gamma>p-1\). The Hilbert transform remains unbounded for every \(\gamma\), so the threshold gives a sharp monotone separation between large-scale and classical Muckenhoupt behavior.
