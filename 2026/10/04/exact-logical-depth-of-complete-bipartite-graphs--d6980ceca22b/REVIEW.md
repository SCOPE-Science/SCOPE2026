# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The lower bound uses an explicit \(b\)-round Duplicator strategy on \(K_{a,b}\) versus \(K_{a,b+1}\). The upper bound partitions competitors into non-bicliques, separated by a rank-3 class axiom, and bicliques, separated by explicit rank-bounded formulas detecting the maximum and minimum part sizes. The exceptional \(K_2\) case is handled separately. Finite exhaustive checks support the two critical mechanisms.

## Originality
PASS with residual priority risk. Pikhurko--Veith--Verbitsky (2003/2006) gives the exact value from its general theorem only in the imbalanced range \(b\ge a+2\); the balanced and one-off-balanced cases remain within a one-unit gap under that theorem. Searches of published-finding corpus, the 2003/2006 primary paper, the 2010 survey, and targeted web queries did not locate the all-biclique formula. No stronger result found was seen to imply the boundary cases automatically.

## Value
PASS. Complete bipartite graphs are a canonical symmetry-rich family, and logical depth is a standard finite-model-theoretic graph invariant. Closing the two boundary diagonals produces a clean exact formula across the whole family and identifies that the general similarity-class upper bound is already sharp there except for the separately understood \(K_2\) convention.
