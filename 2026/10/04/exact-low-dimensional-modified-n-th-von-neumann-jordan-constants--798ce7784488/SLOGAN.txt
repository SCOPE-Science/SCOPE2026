For every \(n\ge2\), the modified upper \(n\)-th von Neumann--Jordan constant of the real square plane is exactly \(1+(2/n)\max_k a_ka_{n-k}\), where \(a_r\) is the mean absolute displacement of an \(r\)-step simple random walk. Its fixed-dimension profile converges to \(1+2/\pi\), unlike the value \(n\) available in the previously treated high-dimensional \(\ell_\infty\) regime.
