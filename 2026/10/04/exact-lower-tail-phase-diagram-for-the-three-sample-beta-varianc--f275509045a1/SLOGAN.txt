For three i.i.d. \(\operatorname{Beta}(\alpha,\beta)\) observations, the lower tail of the unbiased sample variance has a sharp transition at the endpoint exponent \(2/3\): linear above it, \(y\log(1/y)\) at it, and \(y^{3m/2}\) below it for \(m=\min(\alpha,\beta)\). The exact constants identify how the two Beta endpoints compete or add.
