The canonical LP relaxation of mixed metric dimension on the double fan \(F_{2,n}\) has a unique optimizer: for \(n\ge4\), the two path endpoints have weight \(1\) and every other vertex has weight \(1/2\), giving optimum \(\frac{n+4}{2}\). Against the known integer value \(n+1\), this produces an additive gap \(\frac{n-2}{2}\) and an integrality ratio tending to \(2\).
