For the Ferretti–Teta impurity model, the published sufficient condition \(\gamma>\gamma_c(N,\varsigma)\) has a sharp internal phase transition at \(\gamma=2/\pi\): below it only finitely many particle numbers can be certified, while above it one finite mass ratio can certify every finite \(N\). At criticality the crossing mass grows as \(\varsigma_N\sim\sqrt{(N-1)/6}\), and the all-\(N\) mass diverges as \([3\pi(\gamma-2/\pi)]^{-1/2}\) from above.
