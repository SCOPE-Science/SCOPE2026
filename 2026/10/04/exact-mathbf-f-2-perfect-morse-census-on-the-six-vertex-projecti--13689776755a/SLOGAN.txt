The standard six-vertex triangulation of \(\mathbb{RP}^2\) has exactly \(428{,}400\) \(\mathbf F_2\)-perfect discrete gradient vector fields. They arise from \(7{,}140\) tree-cotree decompositions, with complementary dual cycle lengths \(5\), \(6\), and \(9\) occurring \(726\), \(540\), and \(30\) times.
