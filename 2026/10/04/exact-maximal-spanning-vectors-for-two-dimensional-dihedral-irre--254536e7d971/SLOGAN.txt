Every irreducible complex representation of a finite dihedral group has a maximal-spanning orbit vector, so it does matrix recovery and phase retrieval. For a two-dimensional irreducible, the exact criterion is \(ab\ne0\) and \(|a|\ne|b|\) except at the order-\(2\) resonance, where the second condition becomes \(\operatorname{Im}((a\overline b)^2)\ne0\).
