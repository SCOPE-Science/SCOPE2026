For a regular even \(n\)-gon of circumradius \(R\), the largest inscribed rectangle has area \(2R^2\) when \(4\mid n\), and \(2R^2\cos(\pi/n)\) when \(n\equiv2\pmod4\). Central symmetry reduces every candidate to a centered rectangle, and the optimum is forced by the angular lattice of vertex diagonals.
