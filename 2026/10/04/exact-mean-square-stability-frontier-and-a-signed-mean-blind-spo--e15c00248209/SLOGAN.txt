On the orthonormal \(2\times2\) system, KGSM has the exact mean-square stability frontier \(\beta+2(1-\beta)M<\sqrt{\beta+2}\). Without smoothing, signed mean error still converges for every \(M<1\), but mean-square convergence already fails at \(M=1/\sqrt2\), exposing a genuine blind spot in signed-error analysis.
