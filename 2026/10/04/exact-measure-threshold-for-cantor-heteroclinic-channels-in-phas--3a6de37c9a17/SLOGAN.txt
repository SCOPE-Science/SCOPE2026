The Cantor heteroclinic set in the Dueñas--Vieiro splitting construction is exactly a product of the prescribed one-dimensional zero sets once those zeros lie in the strict negative-splitting region. Its area has an exact infinite-product threshold, and a fixed relative middle-quarter rule has zero area with Hausdorff dimension \(2\log2/\log(8/3)\), while positive area requires a fat-Cantor deletion schedule.
