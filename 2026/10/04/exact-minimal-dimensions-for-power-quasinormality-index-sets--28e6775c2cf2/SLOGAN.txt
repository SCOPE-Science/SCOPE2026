For the arithmetic tail \(\mathcal{PQ}(T)=\{d(q+k):k\in\mathbb N_0\}\), the exact smallest complex matrix size is \(1\) for \(d=q=1\), \(q\) for \(d=1<q\), \(2\) for \(q=1<d\), and \((q-1)d+3\) for \(d,q\ge2\). The optimum separates a two-dimensional periodic sector from the shortest nilpotent cutoff sector.
