For every finite nilpotent group \(G\), the degree of an element in the minimum-generator independence graph \(\Gamma_{d(G)}(G)\) has an exact product formula controlled by Sylow generator ranks and Frattini membership. In particular, Lucchini's divisibility question has an affirmative answer on the whole nilpotent class: \(|g|\mid\deg_{\Gamma_{d(G)}(G)}(g)\).
