For every real sigma-finite \(L_1(\mu)\) point \(f\), the Daugavet and Delta constants coincide exactly: \(\operatorname{dc}(f)=\delta c(f)=1+\|f\|_1-2m(f)\), where \(m(f)\) is the largest \(L_1\)-mass carried by a single atom. This gives one formula for purely atomic, purely atomless, and mixed measures.
