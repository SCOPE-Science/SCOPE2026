**PASS.** The stated cavity-reservoir isometry induces the standard non-unital amplitude-damping channel. Applying it to the complete extended Werner-like density matrix gives the displayed X-state entries and the exact cavity concurrence. The two-reservoir state is obtained by the complementary channel, which exchanges \(\eta\) and \(1-\eta\).
