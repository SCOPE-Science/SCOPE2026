The generalized entangled coherent-state protocol has exact cubic feasibility boundaries for its formal optimal weights. At fixed large amplitude, the linear optimum remains normalizable for \(d\sim|\alpha|^4\) phases, whereas the nonlinear optimum allows only \(d\sim|\alpha|^4/16\); at \(|\alpha|=4\) the exact capacities are \(255\) and \(17\).
