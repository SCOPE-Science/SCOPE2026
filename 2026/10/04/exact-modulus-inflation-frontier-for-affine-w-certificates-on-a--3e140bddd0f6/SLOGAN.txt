For \(f(x)=a x^2/2\), the best possible affine W-certificate at a target ratio \(r=\Delta/G<1\) requires the exact trial-modulus inflation \(\mu/a\ge(1+\sqrt{1-r})/(1-\sqrt{1-r})\). At the exact gap \(r=1\), the optimal descent-slowness is only a nonattained supremum, so the true modulus \(\mu=a\) itself does not admit an affine W-certificate.
