For the regular \(N\)-gon of pure real-qubit states, the optimal \(k\)-copy success probability is exactly \(N^{-1}(\sum_r\sqrt{p_r})^2\), where \(p_r\) is the fair-binomial mass in residue class \(r\pmod N\). This covers the previously untreated aliasing regime \(N\le k\) and gives \(1-P_{N,k}=\tfrac12\cos(\pi/N)^{2k}+O(\cos(\pi/N)^{3k})\); the same polygon uniquely maximizes the fixed-ensemble multiple-Chernoff exponent among \(N\) distinct rebits.
