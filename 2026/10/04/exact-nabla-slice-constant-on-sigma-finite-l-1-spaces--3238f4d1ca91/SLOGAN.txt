For a real \(\sigma\)-finite \(L_1(\mu)\) unit vector, the exact quantitative defect of being a \(\nabla\)-point is controlled only by the largest atomic mass \(m(f)\): the avoiding-slice constant is \(2(1-m(f))\) for \(0<m(f)<1\), but returns to \(2\) at the two qualitative \(\nabla\) regimes \(m(f)=0\) and \(m(f)=1\).
