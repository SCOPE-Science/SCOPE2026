In a uniform Cayley tree, the degree of a marked endpoint has an exact binomial-plus-Bernoulli law conditional on its distance to another marked vertex. The conditional degree decreases strictly in stochastic order with distance, forcing negative covariance for all increasing transforms; in particular \(\operatorname{Cov}(D,\Delta)\to-1\) while the correlation decays like \(-\sqrt{2/(4-\pi)}\,n^{-1/2}\).
