For the finite dual-number ring \(R_q=\mathbb F_q[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)\), the lifts of one nilpotent Jordan block \(J_n\) realize every nilpotency index from \(n\) through \(2n\) with an exact geometric count. In particular, a proportion \(1-q^{-1}\) of all dual parts produce the maximal doubled index \(2n\).
