# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. For odd \(A\), the generalized-dihedral center is trivial. A rotation and a reflection generate exactly the generalized dihedral subgroup on the cyclic subgroup generated by the rotation, while two distinct reflections generate exactly the generalized dihedral subgroup on the cyclic subgroup generated by their product. These two calculations determine every edge. The degree formulas and edge count follow by partitioning mixed and reflection-reflection edges. The packaged replay independently reconstructs the full graph from group multiplication in six cases.

## Originality
PASS. The closest primary source studies the same graph and proves general component and diameter alternatives, but its full text does not state this all-odd-abelian-kernel generalized-dihedral decomposition or its degree and edge formulas. The nilpotent predecessor does not apply. Targeted searches over generalized-dihedral, dihedral, reflection/rotation, Frobenius, and Cayley terminology located no equivalent exact theorem. The residual bibliographic risk is that an equivalent statement may exist under terminology not indexed by those searches.

## Value
PASS. The theorem gives the complete graph, not merely connectivity, for a broad standard family of nonnilpotent groups. It also reveals two useful structural reductions: every noncyclic odd abelian kernel of a fixed order produces the same graph \(\overline{K}_{m-1}\vee K_m\), while cyclic kernels produce complements of unitary Cayley graphs on the reflection side.
