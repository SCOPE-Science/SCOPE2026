For every odd abelian kernel \(A\), the graph \(\Xi(\operatorname{Dih}(A))\) is determined exactly by which elements generate \(A\): active rotations join all reflections, while the reflection graph is \(\operatorname{Cay}(A,S)\) on the non-generators. For noncyclic \(A\) this collapses to \(\overline{K}_{|A|-1}\vee K_{|A|}\), and in all non-edgeless cases the active graph has diameter \(2\).
