The theorem is proved directly from the generalized-dihedral multiplication law; finite computation is only an independent check.

The critical identities are
\[
r_a r_b=ab^{-1},
\qquad
r_b r_a=(ab^{-1})^{-1},
\]
and
\[
\langle x,r_a\rangle=\operatorname{Dih}(\langle x\rangle),
\qquad
\langle r_a,r_b\rangle=\operatorname{Dih}(\langle ab^{-1}\rangle).
\]
Odd order ensures that distinct reflections do not commute and that the center is trivial.

The packaged checker `artifacts/verify.py` constructs
\[
C_3,C_9,C_{15},C_3\times C_3,C_9\times C_3,C_5\times C_5
\]
as kernels, forms each generalized dihedral group, computes the center, enumerates every unordered vertex pair, tests commutativity, closes the subgroup generated by each pair, and compares the resulting graph with the theorem. It also verifies all degree formulas, the edge count, and the complete-join form in noncyclic cases.

It returns `VERIFY_OK`.
