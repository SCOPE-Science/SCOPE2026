For every \(n\ge4\), an acyclic orientation of \(K_{3,n}\) has a non-separating out-branching exactly when it has a unique source and its canonical source-removal code avoids four forced-star patterns. The four obstructions are \(A^1B^nA^2\), \(A^1B^1A^2B^{n-1}\), \(B^1A^3B^{n-1}\), and \(B^1A^1B^{n-1}A^2\).
