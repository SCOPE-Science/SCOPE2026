For the equal two-point cyclic STFT window \(g_a=\delta_0+\delta_a\) with even step order at least \(4\), the full-support phase-retrieval fiber is completely explicit: only independent cycle phases and, on exactly alternating two-level magnitude cycles, one level-swap ambiguity occur. In the one-cycle case, nonvanishing phase retrieval fails exactly for signals whose magnitudes alternate between two distinct constants.
