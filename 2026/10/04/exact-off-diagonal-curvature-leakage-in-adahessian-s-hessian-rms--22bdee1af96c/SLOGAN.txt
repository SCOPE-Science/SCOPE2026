AdaHessian's RMS turns Hutchinson diagonal-estimation variance into a persistent off-diagonal curvature term: with one probe, \(\mathbb E[\overline D_{t,i}^{\,2}]=\|H_{i:}\|_2^2\), not \(H_{ii}^2\). In two dimensions the sharp inflation at condition number \(\kappa\) is \((\kappa+1)/(2\sqrt\kappa)\), and averaging \(m\) probes suppresses the excess exactly by \(1/m\).
