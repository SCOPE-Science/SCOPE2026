For a filtered Boolean power of a finite simple non-abelian Mal'cev algebra by the countable atomless Boolean algebra, every tuple orbit is determined exactly by the set of pointwise finite-algebra orbit types it realizes. Consequently the all-tuple profile is \(2^{q_k-r}\), where \(q_k=|A^k/\operatorname{Aut}(A)|\) and \(r\) is the number of distinct filtering-idempotent orbits.
