For every nested pair of real intervals, ordinary and convexified Hilbert packings coincide exactly and are unchanged by reversed polarity at the same separation scale. This collapses Arya–Mount’s fully convexified diagonal-product problem in one dimension to \(\widehat M_{\mathrm{diag},H}(\eta)\le \widehat M_H(\eta/4)^2\), with optimal multiplicative constant \(1\).
