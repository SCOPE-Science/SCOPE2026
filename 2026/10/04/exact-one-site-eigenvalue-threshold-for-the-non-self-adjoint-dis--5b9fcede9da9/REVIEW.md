# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reduces the one-site eigenvalue problem to the exact finite-dimensional condition \((I+v_0T_0(k))x=0\). Necessity follows immediately from the operator norm. Sufficiency is constructive: a largest-singular-value vector of \(T_0(k)\) determines a block map of any prescribed norm \(Q\ge1/\|T_0(k)\|\), and the free resolvent then gives an explicit square-summable eigenvector. The boundary corollary is an exact comparison with the source enclosure.

## Originality
PASS. The primary paper proves one-site attainability on the diagonal-dominance part of the boundary but does not state or prove the converse obstruction for all one-site potentials, nor the exact minimum one-site norm for an arbitrary off-spectrum target. Targeted searches for single-site, point-interaction, rank-one, Birman–Schwinger, and exact-threshold formulations found no equivalent statement. The scalar discrete Schrödinger analogue has a different scalar resolvent structure and does not imply the matrix-valued discrete Dirac result.

## Value
PASS. The result identifies the exact expressive power and exact failure region of the point-potential strategy used in the source paper's unresolved optimality discussion. It shows that any proof of boundary optimality outside the diagonal-dominance region must use genuinely nonlocal support or a different mechanism, while also giving the exact norm budget for every one-site target eigenvalue.

Closest literature: Cassano–Ibrogimov–Krejčiřík–Štampach, arXiv:1910.10710v1, especially the free resolvent, Theorem 3, Theorem 5, and Remark 7; and Ibrogimov–Štampach, arXiv:1903.08620v1, Section 4. The claim is restricted to off-spectrum targets and unrestricted complex \(2\times2\) site blocks; multi-site optimality remains open here.

Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
