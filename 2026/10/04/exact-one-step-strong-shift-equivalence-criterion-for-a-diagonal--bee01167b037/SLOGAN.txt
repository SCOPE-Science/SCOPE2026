For the generalized Baker slice \(A_{n;b,c}=\begin{pmatrix}n&b\\c&n\end{pmatrix}\), \(B_{n;b,c}=\begin{pmatrix}n&bc\\1&n\end{pmatrix}\) with \(n^2\ne bc\), one-step strong shift equivalence occurs exactly when \(b\mid n\) or \(c\mid n\). Thus Jeandel's \(G_k,H_k\) family is elementary exactly for multiples of \(4\) or \(5\).
