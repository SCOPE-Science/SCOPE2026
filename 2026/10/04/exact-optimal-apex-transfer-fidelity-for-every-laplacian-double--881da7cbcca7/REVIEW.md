# Same-model review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The starting amplitude is the published exact Laplacian join formula specialized to the double cone. Direct differentiation factors as \(F_n'(t)=2n\sin t(\cos t-\cos((n+1)t))/(n+2)\), so every maximum lies in three explicit critical families. Their values reduce to two bounded families and one roots-of-unity family. Maximizing the latter gives the three congruence cases exactly, and an elementary cosine bound proves it dominates the other critical families.

## Value

**PASS.** The result turns a binary perfect-transfer classification into a complete quantitative performance law for every double cone and every internal graph. It shows that the two non-perfect congruence classes nevertheless approach unit fidelity at explicit \(n^{-2}\) rates, with different sharp constants, and that engineering the internal graph cannot beat those limits. This is directly relevant to finite quantum-network design and distinguishes “not perfect” from “how close can it ever get.”
