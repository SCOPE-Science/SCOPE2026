For every Laplacian double cone \(\overline{K_2}\vee H\), the best possible conical-vertex transfer fidelity depends only on \(n=|V(H)|\), not on the edges of \(H\).  Beyond the known perfect case \(n\equiv2\pmod4\), the exact optima are \(\cos^2(\pi/[2(n+2)])\) for odd \(n\) and \(\cos^2(\pi/(n+2))\) for \(n\equiv0\pmod4\).
