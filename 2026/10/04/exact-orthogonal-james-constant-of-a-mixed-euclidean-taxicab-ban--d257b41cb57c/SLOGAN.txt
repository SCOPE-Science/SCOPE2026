For the mixed Euclidean–taxicab Banach plane from Baronti--Papini's Example 5.4, the previously numerical orthogonal James constant is exactly \(2/\sqrt{1+\tau^2}\), where \(\tau\in(\sqrt2-1,1)\) is the unique root of \(\tau^4+2\tau^3-1=0\). An exhaustive support-functional classification shows that the unique maximizing branch occurs at a coordinate-axis point.
