For a Euclidean unit disk translated by \(a\in[0,1)\) relative to the gauge origin, the Balestro–Martini–Teixeira outer asymmetry constant has an exact two-branch phase diagram, with the maximizing tangent configuration bifurcating at the unique root \(a_0\approx0.6833726274\) of \(4a^6+7a^4-2a^2-1=0\). Across the whole family its sharp maximum is \(64\sqrt2/(27\pi)\), attained uniquely at \(a=1/\sqrt3\).
