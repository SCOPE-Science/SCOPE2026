For the symmetric semigroup generated by \(1,\sqrt{p_2},\ldots,\sqrt{p_m}\), discrete distributional Fourier support occurs exactly when \(m\) is odd. Odd \(m\) yields reciprocal-lattice masses plus even derivatives through \(\delta_0^{(m-1)}\), while even \(m\) leaves a nonzero regular Fourier component on every pole-free interval.
