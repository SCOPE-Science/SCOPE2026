For every \(f\ge6\), the strict greedy sequence \(S_{f,2f+3}\) has an explicit tail modulo \(5f+5\), with exactly two transient deletions and one transient insertion before stabilization. Its eventual first-difference period is minimally \(f+4\), proving the next natural off-diagonal family beyond the known \(2f+1\) and \(2f+2\) lines.
