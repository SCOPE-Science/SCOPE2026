At the reflected nonexpansive endpoint \(\mathbb T=-I\) with persistent additive minibatch noise, fixed-batch stochastic Dual-OHM has a bounded terminal noise floor converging to \((\pi^2/4-1)\sigma^2/B\). Under the same oracle, stochastic OHM has terminal mean-square residual noise \(\frac{2\sigma^2(N-1)(2N-1)}{3BN}\sim\frac{4}{3}(\sigma^2/B)N\), giving an exact one-dimensional separation between dual-anchor stability and anchor noise accumulation.
