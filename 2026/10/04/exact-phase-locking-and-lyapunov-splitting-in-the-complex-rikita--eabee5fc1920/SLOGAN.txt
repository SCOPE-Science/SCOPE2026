For the complex Rikitake flow, the relative-phase defect \(J=\operatorname{Im}(\overline{x}y)\) satisfies \(\dot J=-2\beta J\), so every bounded complete orbit is a fixed common-phase rotation of a classical real Rikitake orbit. Every compact ergodic recurrent state therefore acquires exactly two phase-normal Lyapunov exponents, \(0\) and \(-2\beta\).
