For a sinusoidal PIT miscalibration, fixed equal-width bins obey an exact arithmetic phase law rather than a phase-free sinc law: the standard grid is completely blind exactly when \(N\mid2k\). The familiar sinc attenuation is instead the exact average over a uniformly randomized bin origin; at \(N=2k\), aligned bins see zero discrepancy, a half-bin shift sees the full discrepancy, and the phase average is \(2/\pi\) of the continuous value.
