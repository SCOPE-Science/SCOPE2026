The line of equilibria carries every real phase-volume rate: at \(E_s=(0,0,s)\), \(\det D\phi_t=e^{st}\), so one near-zero Lyapunov-sum trajectory cannot make the whole vector field volume preserving. Every compact stationary state also satisfies \(\int x^2\,d\mu=b\int y^2\,d\mu\) and \(\int y^2z\,d\mu=0\).
