# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For an arbitrary slice functional, the dual-coordinate norms \(b_\gamma\) produce \(c_\gamma=b_\gamma^{q-1}\in S_{\ell_p}\). Coordinate Daugavet witnesses give a point in the same slice with distance approaching \(\|a+c\|_p\). The scalar infimum is exact because \(F(t)=(1+t)^p-t^p\) is increasing and coordinate unit vectors approach the infimal coordinate norm. A coordinate-supported slice supplies the matching upper bound. Zero coordinates, unattained infima, singleton families and arbitrary index cardinality are handled by the argument.

## originality

PASS

Choi-Jung introduce the pointwise Daugavet constant and in Section 4 give general absolute-sum lower bounds plus an \(\ell_p\) axis upper bound; the inspected PDF page containing Proposition 4.8 confirms only the coordinate-axis \(2^{1/p}\) estimate, not an arbitrary-point formula. Haller et al. Theorem 2.6 gives the global slice-thickness value \(2^{1/p}\) for two Daugavet summands, again not the pointwise profile. Fresh Resultary search found nearby atomic-\(\ell_1\), scalar-axis, \(c_0\), and sliced-thickness results, none of which imply the claimed smallest-coordinate formula for arbitrary \(\ell_p\)-sums.

## value

PASS

The result computes a recently introduced localized invariant on a natural direct-sum class, identifies the exact finite-sum control parameter, and shows an infinite-index collapse to a flat unit-sphere profile. It is structural information not recoverable from the known global thickness constant and is not a small-instance computation.

The dated certificate retains the supplied scientific assessment, sources and limitations.
