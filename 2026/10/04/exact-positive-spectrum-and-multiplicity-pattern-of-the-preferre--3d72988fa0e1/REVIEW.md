# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The claim is reconstructed from the exact three-sector secular equations in the primary paper. Root existence and uniqueness are proved on every half-period, scalar roots are shown simple, and the endpoint multiplicity is obtained directly from the reduced boundary systems. The asymptotic energy shifts are derived analytically. The bundled finite checks support but do not replace the proof.

## Originality
PASS. The closest source is Exner--Lipovský, arXiv:1906.09091v1, which gives the exact factors but states only the common high-energy enclosure in Theorem 3.2. Lipovský's later habilitation thesis repeats that enclosure. Searches for exact tangent formulations, tetrahedron multiplicity, interlacing/root counts, and broader preferred-orientation coverage found no equivalent or stronger statement. Residual risk remains for unindexed equivalent work under different notation.

## Value
PASS. The result gives a complete global positive-spectrum pattern for a canonical finite preferred-orientation graph: exact equations, exact multiplicities, one-root-per-half-period structure, Weyl-consistent multiplicity twelve per full period, and sharp limiting energy offsets. This materially sharpens the source theorem rather than merely restating its factors.

## Closest literature and limitations
The primary source and the later thesis are the closest literature. The claim is restricted to unit edge lengths, the specified cyclic vertex coupling, and positive energies. It does not address negative spectrum or incommensurate perturbations.
