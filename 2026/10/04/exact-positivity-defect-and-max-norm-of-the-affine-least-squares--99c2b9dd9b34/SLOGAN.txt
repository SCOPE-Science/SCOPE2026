The local \(L^2\) projector onto affine functions on a \(d\)-simplex has the exact positivity defect
\[
C_d=(d+1)\left(\frac{d+1}{d+2}\right)^d-1
\]
and exact \(L^\infty\) norm \(1+2C_d\). A bounded strictly positive input can therefore project to a negative vertex value, with the sharp defect growing like \(d/e\).
