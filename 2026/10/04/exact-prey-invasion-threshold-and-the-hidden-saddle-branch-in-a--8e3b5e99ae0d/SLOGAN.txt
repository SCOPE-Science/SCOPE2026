The 2024 herd predator-prey model has an exact predator-only collision threshold \(a_*=cr+r/H\), and the physical coexistence branch emerges for \(a>a_*\) as a saddle. For the paper's reference parameters this gives \(a_*=0.208\), so the reported transition near \(1.08834\) cannot be a transcritical collision with the predator-only equilibrium.
