For unital qubit mechanisms, pure quantum local differential privacy fixes the largest Bloch singular value exactly, turning the Choi-state concurrence optimization into a three-singular-value extremum. The sharp curve is \(C_{\max}(\varepsilon)=[3\tanh(\varepsilon/2)-1]_+/2\), attained by depolarizing noise and vanishing exactly through \(\varepsilon=\log 2\).
