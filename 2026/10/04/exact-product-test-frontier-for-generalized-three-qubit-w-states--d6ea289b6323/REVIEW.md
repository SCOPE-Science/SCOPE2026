# Review

## Correctness
PASS. For a generalized W state the product-test identity reduces exactly to \(P_{\mathrm{PT}}=(1+p^2+q^2+r^2)/2\). In the highly entangled branch, the published circumradius formula gives \(\omega=4pqr/[1-2(p^2+q^2+r^2)]\). Fixing \(s=p^2+q^2+r^2\), the trigonometric parameterization of triples with sum one gives an exact lower bound on \(pqr\), with equality precisely at one-small/two-equal probabilities. Its induced lower bound \(\omega\ge(2+t)^2/[9(1+t)]\) is monotone and invertible, yielding the stated frontier and equality family. The \(\omega\ge1/2\) branch follows from the published largest-coefficient formula and \(q^2+r^2\le(q+r)^2\). The bundled checker independently confirms the algebra and representative instances. Risk: correctness depends on the cited generalized-W product-overlap theorem, which was inspected in the primary arXiv text.

## Originality
PASS. The 2026 anchor theorem explicitly treats arbitrary finite local dimensions and identifies the fixed-dimension extremal problem as separate. The 2008/2009 generalized-W paper gives the exact overlap formula but does not optimize product-test acceptance at fixed overlap or compare a W-family frontier to the 2026 dimension-free curve. Targeted web and published-finding corpus searches for product-test/W-state, geometric-overlap/local-purity, Meyer-Wallach, circumradius, fixed-dimension, and Pareto-frontier formulations did not locate an equivalent statement. Risk: an equivalent relation between geometric measure and one-qubit linear-entropy/global-entanglement measures may exist in older literature under different terminology.

## Value
PASS. This is a natural complete classification on the canonical generalized W family, not an arbitrary coefficient slice. The family contains the unique globally most geometrically entangled three-qubit state at \(\omega=4/9\) and reaches the bipartite boundary at \(\omega=1/2\). The theorem therefore quantifies, over the entire low-overlap W regime, the finite-local-dimension obstruction flagged by the 2026 product-test paper, including an exact strict deficit from the dimension-free curve and equality states. It supplies a sharp benchmark for the still-open full fixed-\((2,2,2)\) problem.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest sources are arXiv:2607.21477 for the dimension-free product-test curve and its explicit fixed-dimension caveat, arXiv:0806.1314 for the generalized-W maximal-overlap formula, and arXiv:0911.1493 for the W state's extremality among pure three-qubit states. The theorem is deliberately restricted to the closed generalized W family and makes no claim about the full three-qubit state space.
