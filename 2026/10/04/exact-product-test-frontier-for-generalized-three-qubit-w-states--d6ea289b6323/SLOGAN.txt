For generalized three-qubit W states, the product-test acceptance has an exact fixed-family frontier as a function of squared product overlap \(\omega\): below \(1/2\) the unique maximizers have two equal large excitation probabilities, while from \(1/2\) upward the frontier is the bipartite boundary \(1-\omega+\omega^2\). On \([4/9,1/2)\) this frontier lies strictly below the dimension-free product-test curve, with deficit \(1/27\) at the symmetric W state and zero only at \(\omega=1/2\).
