For the classical real Banaś–Frączek plane \(N_\lambda(x_1,x_2)=\max\{\lambda|x_1|,\sqrt{x_1^2+x_2^2}\}\), the exact Ptolemy constant is \(C_{\mathrm{Pt}}=\lambda\) throughout \(1<\lambda\le\sqrt2\). A max-of-two-Ptolemaic-branches upper bound and one explicit three-point configuration meet exactly on this full parameter interval.
