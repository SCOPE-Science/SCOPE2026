# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof covers arbitrary cyclically ordered boundary quadruples. The side-direction calculation is exact, the two alternating midpoint-interval unions each have total parameter length \(\pi\), and the threshold-set argument gives the sharp integral minimum \(4\arctan(b/a)\). The published quadrilateral angular bound then yields \(P(E)\le(a^2+b^2)/(2ab)\), and the four axis endpoints attain equality. The circle case is included.

## Originality
PASS. The closest primary source gives only an interval for the ellipse Ptolemy constant. Finch later proposed the same lower-bound value as the global answer from symbolic and numerical evidence but explicitly left rigorous global maximality open. Searches for exact-value, affine-circle, axis-endpoint, and opposite-angle formulations did not locate a rigorous covering result. Residual risk remains for unindexed literature and an older unpublished thesis that was not independently inspected.

## Value
PASS. This resolves a natural exact invariant for ellipses and supplies a structural lemma: every inscribed quadrilateral has both opposite-angle sums at least \(4\arctan(b/a)\). That lemma is precisely strong enough to close the published upper/lower-bound gap.

## Closest literature and limitations
Harmaala–Klén is the closest rigorous source and Finch is the closest same-claim source. The theorem is limited to planar ellipses; it does not solve the analogous rectangle, regular-hexagon, Reuleaux-triangle, or arbitrary-convex-quadrilateral problems, and it does not classify all maximizers.
