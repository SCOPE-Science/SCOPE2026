For the real Day--James plane \(X=\ell_2-\ell_\infty\), with \(\|(u,v)\|_{2,\infty}=\sqrt{u^2+v^2}\) when \(uv\ge0\) and \(\|(u,v)\|_{2,\infty}=\max\{|u|,|v|\}\) when \(uv\le0\), the Ptolemy constant is exactly \(C_{\mathrm{Pt}}(X)=3/2\). A sharp Hilbert-norm sandwich gives the upper bound, and the explicit triple \((-1,-1),(2,-2),(1,-3)\) attains it.
