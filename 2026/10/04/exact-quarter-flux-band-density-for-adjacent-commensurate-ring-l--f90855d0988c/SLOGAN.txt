At quarter flux, the tightly connected magnetic ring chain with adjacent rational arc ratio \(m/(m+1)\) has exact momentum-band density \(3/4-[4(2m+1)s_m]^{-1}\), where \(s_m\) is the odd member of \(\{m,m+1\}\). These commensurate densities approach \(3/4\) at rate \(1/(8m^2)\) even though the limiting symmetric ratio has density \(1/2\), exposing an explicit arithmetic discontinuity.
