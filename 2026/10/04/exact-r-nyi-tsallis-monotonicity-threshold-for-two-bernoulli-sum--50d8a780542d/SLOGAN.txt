For the sum of two independent Bernoulli variables whose success probabilities do not exceed \(1/2\), Rényi and Tsallis entropy are coordinatewise nondecreasing for every \(0\le q\le2\), while published two-summand counterexamples show failure for every \(q>2\). Thus \(q=2\) is the exact two-summand monotonicity threshold.
