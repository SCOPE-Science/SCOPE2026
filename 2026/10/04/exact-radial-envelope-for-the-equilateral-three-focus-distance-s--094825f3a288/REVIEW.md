# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reduces the angular dependence to the single factor \(\cos(3\theta)\). The two angularly invariant elementary symmetric sums of the squared distances are derived explicitly, and the distance sum \(p\) is shown to be strictly increasing with the distance product \(q\) because \((ep-q)/p>0\). The equality directions and the boundary cases \(\rho=0\) and \(\rho=R\) are included. Dense deterministic numerical checks agree with the symbolic result but are not used to justify the universal statement.

## Originality
PASS with residual historical-search risk. The closest located literature is Sándor (2005), which gives identities and inequalities for the same three distances and the associated Pompeiu triangle, including a non-sharp upper bound for their sum, and Sekino (1999), which treats general \(n\)-ellipse level sets and centers. Neither inspected source states the exact extrema of \(PA+PB+PC\) at fixed \(OP\) or the alternating vertex-ray/side-midpoint-ray equality pattern. Direct searches for the radical endpoint formulas, fixed-centroid-radius formulations, trifocal/polyellipse terminology, and equivalent Fermat--Torricelli formulations likewise found no equivalent statement. Older literature remains a residual risk because terminology is fragmented.

## Value
PASS. The result supplies an exact radial profile for the most symmetric nonclassical polyellipse: at each centered radius it gives both sharp extremal distance sums and all equality directions. This strictly refines the available fixed-center global and non-sharp inequalities, gives certified radial bracketing for equilateral trifocal level sets, and isolates the cubic angular harmonic \(\cos(3\theta)\) as the complete source of anisotropy.
