Algebra p²=S1+2e and e²=S2+2pq gives dq/dp=(ep−q)/p>0 for nonnegative d_i not all zero; hence extrema of p occur exactly at cos3θ=±1. Direct evaluation gives both radical endpoints and all six equality rays. ρ=0 yields 3R, ρ=R admits zero distance without invalidating strict positivity.
