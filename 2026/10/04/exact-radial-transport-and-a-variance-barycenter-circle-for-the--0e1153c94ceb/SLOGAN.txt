Every compact invariant measure of the dissipative Ikeda map transports its entire radial law exactly: \((|z-A|)_\#\mu=(u|z|)_\#\mu\). Consequently its complex variance plus the squared displacement of its barycenter from \(A/(1-u^2)\) is the fixed quantity \((Au/(1-u^2))^2\), independent of the nonlinear phase law.
