A single infinite SARAH inner loop can make its recursive direction vanish geometrically while leaving a strictly positive random optimization error. On the symmetric two-quadratic family the exact residual is \(\mathbb E[x_\infty^2]=x_0^2\alpha h^2/[2-\alpha(1+h^2)]\); at \(\alpha=1\), \(h=1/2\), the limiting iterate is exactly uniform on \([-|x_0|,|x_0|]\) even though \(|v_t|=\mu|x_0|2^{-t}\).
