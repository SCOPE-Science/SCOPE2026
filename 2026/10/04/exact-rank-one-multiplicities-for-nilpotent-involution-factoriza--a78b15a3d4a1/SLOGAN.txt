For a rank-one \(3\times3\) matrix over \(\mathbb F_q\), the exact number of nilpotent–involution factorizations is determined solely by the characteristic and whether the trace vanishes. The four counts are \(4q^3+2q^2+2\), \(2q(q^2-1)\), \(2q^3-q\), and \(q(q^2-1)\) in the odd/trace-zero, odd/nonzero-trace, even/trace-zero, and even/nonzero-trace cases, respectively.
