For every prime power \(q\), the uniform full-recovery coverage-depth problem is exactly solvable for all \([n,2]_q\) codes: distribute the \(n\) columns as evenly as possible among the \(q+1\) projective directions. This yields the exact optimum \(1+(q+1-r)a/(n-a)+r(a+1)/(n-a-1)\) when \(n=a(q+1)+r\).\n
