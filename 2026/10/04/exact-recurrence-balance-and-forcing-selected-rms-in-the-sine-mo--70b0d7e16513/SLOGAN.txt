The sine-modified Sprott-B flow has the exact balance \(\dot H=b-x_1^2\) for \(H=x_3+x_1^2/(2a)\). Thus bounded recurrence is impossible for \(b<0\), collapses to the equilibrium line at \(b=0\), and for \(b>0\) every compact stationary state has the forcing-selected variance \(\operatorname{Var}(x_1)=b\).
