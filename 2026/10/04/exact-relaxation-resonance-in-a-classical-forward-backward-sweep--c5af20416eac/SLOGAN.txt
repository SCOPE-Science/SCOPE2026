In the classical scalar forward–backward sweep counterexample with \(x'=x+u\) and running state weight \(3\), the formal sweep has an exact \(-3\) mode \(1-t\). Constant relaxation converges exactly for \(0<\omega<1/2\), is uniquely fastest in \(L^2\) at \(\omega=2/5\) with factor \(3/5\), and the boundary \(\omega=1/2\) turns that mode into the exact two-cycle canceled by consecutive averaging.
