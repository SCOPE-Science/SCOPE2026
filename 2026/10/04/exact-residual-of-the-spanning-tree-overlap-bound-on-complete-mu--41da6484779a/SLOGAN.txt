For \(G=K_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}\), Omar's new spanning-tree overlap correction for domination coefficients has a closed-form residual: the remaining error at size \(k\) is \(\binom Nk^{-1}\sum_{n_i\ge k+2}(n_i-k-1)\binom{n_i-1}{k-1}\). Consequently the pairwise correction is exact exactly when every part has size at most \(k+1\).
