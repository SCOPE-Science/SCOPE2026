For the subgroup-plus-one-point family \(E_m\subset\mathbb Z_m^2\), every basis Gram spectrum is governed by one cubic whose parameter is the chord length between the two repeated characters. This yields the exact optimum \(\rho(E_m)=2m\) for even \(m\), an exact cubic-root formula for odd \(m\), and the sharp asymptotic \(\rho(E_m)\sim2m\).
