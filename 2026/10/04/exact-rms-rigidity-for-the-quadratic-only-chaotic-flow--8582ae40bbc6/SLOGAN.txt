For the quadratic-only chaotic flow, every bounded forward trajectory has the same exact coordinate-wise RMS amplitudes: \(\langle x^2\rangle=a_1b_2/[b_1(a_1c_2+a_2c_1)]\), \(\langle y^2\rangle=a_2/(a_1c_2+a_2c_1)\), and \(\langle z^2\rangle=a_1/(a_1c_2+a_2c_1)\). In the published multistable example, the coexisting chaotic and period-three branches therefore share exactly the same three mean squares.
