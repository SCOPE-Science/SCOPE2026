For a rank-one-residual Gaussian regression with \(p=n-1\), the full-model residual permutation coefficient test has an exact finite-\(m\) size law and can reject almost half the time at any fixed nominal level below \(1/4\). The obstruction comes from a collapse of every permuted residual statistic to three values of scale \(n^{-1/2}\) while the observed null coefficient remains \(N(0,1)\).
