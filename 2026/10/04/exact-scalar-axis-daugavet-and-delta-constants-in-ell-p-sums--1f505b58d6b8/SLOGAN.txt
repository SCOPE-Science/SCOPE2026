For every nonzero real Banach space \(Y\), every \(1<p<\infty\), and every \(t\in[-1,1]\), the scalar-axis point \((t,0)\in\mathbb R\oplus_pY\) has \(\operatorname{dc}=1-|t|\) but \(\delta c=(1-|t|^p)^{1/p}\). Thus any nonzero transverse \(p\)-summand produces a universal strict quantitative Delta amplification at \(0<|t|<1\).\n
