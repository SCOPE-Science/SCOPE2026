For the equilateral preferred-orientation octahedron, the two previously interval-localized positive spectral series satisfy the exact equations \(\tan^2(k/2)=2k^2+1\) and \(\tan^2(k/2)=1+2/k^2\). Each nonexceptional root has multiplicity three, with sharp offsets \(\pm\sqrt2/K_n\) and \((-1)^n/L_n^2\) at high energy.
