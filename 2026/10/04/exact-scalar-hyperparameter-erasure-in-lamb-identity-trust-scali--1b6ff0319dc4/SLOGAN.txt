With one scalar layer and \(\phi(z)=z\), LAMB exactly forgets the magnitude of its Adam-like direction whenever the iterate does not cross the minimizer. For every sign-coherent scalar objective and \(0<\eta_t<1\), \(x_{t+1}=(1-\eta_t)x_t\); on quadratics this erases curvature, momentum decay, second-moment decay, and epsilon from the parameter trajectory.
