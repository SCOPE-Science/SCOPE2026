For scalar QHM with \(0<\nu<1\), the exact best stepsize is \(s_\star=(1-\beta)/(1-\sqrt{\beta\nu})^2\) and the exact minimum state factor is \(|\beta-\sqrt{\beta\nu}|/(1-\sqrt{\beta\nu})\). The factor vanishes only on QHM's Nesterov slice \(\nu=\beta\), where the scalar state matrix is nilpotent and every initial state is annihilated in at most two updates.
