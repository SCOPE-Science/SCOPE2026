Cubic Alltop fiducials on two prime-dimensional qudits have the exact Schmidt spectrum \((2/d)^{\times(d-1)/2},(1/d),0^{\times(d-1)/2}\) for every prime \(d\ge5\). They therefore realize the recently conjectured magic value \(M_2=\log(d^4/(2d^2-1))\) on an explicit dimension-uniform entanglement stratum, without asserting global maximality.
