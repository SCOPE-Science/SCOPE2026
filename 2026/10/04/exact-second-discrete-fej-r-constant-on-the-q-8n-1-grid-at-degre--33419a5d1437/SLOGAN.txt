For every integer \(n\ge2\), the second discrete Fejér problem at \(p=3\) and \(q=8n+1\) has an exact two-zero extremizer, with zeros at \(3n\) and \(3n+1\). The resulting constant is \(\frac{2\cos(\pi/4-\pi/(4q))\cos(\pi/q)}{1+\sin(\pi/(2q))+\cos(2\pi/q)}\), settling the degree-two subfamily of an open direction highlighted by Ivanov.
