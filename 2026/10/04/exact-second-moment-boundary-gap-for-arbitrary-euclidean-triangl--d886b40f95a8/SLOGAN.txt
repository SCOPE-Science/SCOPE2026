For every nondegenerate Euclidean triangle, the squared distance between two arclength-uniform boundary points has exact mean \((s^2-3r^2)/6\), while two area-uniform interior points have exact mean \((s^2-4Rr-r^2)/9\). Their gap is \((s^2+8Rr-7r^2)/18>0\), giving a strict \(p=2\) boundary-over-interior comparison for every triangle.
