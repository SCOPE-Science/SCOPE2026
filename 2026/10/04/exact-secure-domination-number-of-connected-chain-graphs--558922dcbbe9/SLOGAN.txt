For a connected chain graph, delete all but one vertex from each leaf-twin class; if the reduced bipartition sizes are \(a'\) and \(b'\), then the secure domination number is exactly \(\min\{4,a',b'\}\), plus one for every deleted leaf. In particular the three-by-three half graph has \(\gamma_s=3\), exposing the small-side case missed by a four-extreme-vertex routine.
