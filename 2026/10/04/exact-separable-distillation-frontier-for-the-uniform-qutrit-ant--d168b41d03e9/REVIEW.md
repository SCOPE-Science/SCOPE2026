# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reduces every nonzero success product Kraus branch to one antisymmetric direction by a common-kernel lemma. The resulting two-dimensional matrix identity gives \(\operatorname{Tr}(XY)\ge2\sin(2\theta)|\alpha|^2\). Pairing the separable failure effect with the block-positive swap gives the global converse. A separate six-branch construction attains the bound and has an explicitly separable completion. Boundary cases and normalization were checked directly.

## Originality
PASS. The closest primary source, arXiv:2501.17394, gives the same achievable curve for a broader family but states only numerical evidence of optimality for the symmetric \(q_i=1/3\), \(\theta_i=\pi\) benchmark. Chitambar–Duan arXiv:0811.3739 proves deterministic SEP distillation for a different rank-two mixture and fixed target, so it neither contains nor implies this rank-three antisymmetric-state probability frontier. Targeted semantic and exact-formula searches did not return a stronger covering result. Residual risk remains that an equivalent statement exists in older Werner-state or separable-distillation literature under different terminology.

## Value
PASS. The result replaces a numerical hierarchy indication in a recent entanglement-manipulation benchmark with a short exact theorem and an elementary optimality certificate. The swap-witness argument also explains structurally why the symmetric instance collapses to a closed form, making the benchmark useful for validating future SEP relaxations.
