For the Fano frequency triple \(\{0,1,3\}\subset\mathbb Z_7\), the smallest possible seven-sample peak of a unimodular trinomial is exactly \(\sqrt{(7+\sqrt{21})/2}\). A cubic-root phase choice attains it, producing squared magnitudes \(0\), \((7+\sqrt{21})/2\), and \((7-\sqrt{21})/2\) with multiplicities \(1,3,3\).
