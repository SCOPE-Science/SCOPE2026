The finite-field cubic graph \(C=\{(t,t^3):t\in F\}\) has exact sharp extension constant \(R_C^*(2\to4)^4=3-6/(2|F|+1)\) in characteristics other than \(2\) and \(3\). Every extremizer has one squared magnitude at \(t=0\), a common smaller squared magnitude off zero, and aligned antipodal phase products.
