For the symmetric one-dimensional quantum walk, the Hadamard coin is not the finite-time position-entropy maximizer: at two steps the unique optimum is \(\theta=\arcsin(1/\sqrt3)\) with entropy \(\log_2 3\), and at three steps it is \(\theta=\arccos(2^{-1/4})\) with entropy \(2\). Thus the published “for any \(n\)” Hadamard-maximality statement fails at the earliest nontrivial time \(n=2\).
