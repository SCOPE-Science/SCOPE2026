For the cube–cross-polytope intersection ball \(P_{d,k}=(kB_1^d)\cap[-1,1]^d\) with \(1\le k<d\), the one-skeleton has an explicit all-pairs metric determined only by signed overlap, and its diameter is exactly \(k+1\). Its graph is the signed Johnson graph \(J_{\pm}(d,k,k-1)\), with a closed distance-shell distribution.
