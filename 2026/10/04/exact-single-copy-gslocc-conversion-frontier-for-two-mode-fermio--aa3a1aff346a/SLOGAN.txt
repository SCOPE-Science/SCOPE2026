For the two-mode pure fermionic Gaussian family \( |\psi_\theta\rangle=\cos\theta|00\rangle+\sin\theta|11\rangle \), the exact fine-grained GSLOCC conversion probability to \( |\psi_\phi\rangle \) is \(\min\{\cos^2\theta/\cos^2\phi,\,\sin^2\theta/\sin^2\phi\}\). Concentration matches unrestricted LOCC exactly, while dilution has a strict Gaussian probability penalty.
