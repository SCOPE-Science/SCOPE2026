LaProp has an exact isolated-gradient displacement gain \(\mathcal K(\mu)\) that is independent of its adaptivity parameter \(\nu\). The gain is finite for every \(\mu<1\) but diverges as \(\mu\uparrow1\); at \(\mu=0.9\) it is \(3.0096094482\ldots\), while zero-damping Adam is impulse-summable only when \(\mu<\sqrt{\nu}\).
