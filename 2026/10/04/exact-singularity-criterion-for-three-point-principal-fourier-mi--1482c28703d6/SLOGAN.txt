A three-point principal Fourier minor is singular exactly when one pair difference \(d\) has square-divisor scale \(N\mid d^2\) and the third point satisfies \(N\mid d(u-w)\). Equivalently, if that difference has additive order \(q\), then \(q^2\mid N\) and the third point lies in the same residue class modulo \(q\).
