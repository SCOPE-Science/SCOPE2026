For every real \(\ell_4(I)\) with at least two coordinates, the full two-parameter skew geometric mean constant is the \(\ell_4^2\)-operator norm of \(\begin{pmatrix}\alpha&\beta\\ \beta&-\alpha\end{pmatrix}\), giving an explicit radical formula. At \(\alpha=\beta=1\) the exact value is \(2^{3/4}\).
