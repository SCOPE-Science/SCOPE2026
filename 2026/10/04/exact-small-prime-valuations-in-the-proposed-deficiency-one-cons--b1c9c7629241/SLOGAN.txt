For the construction \(n=M+k-1\) proposed in arXiv:2609.25042v1, the deficiency is indeed one, but every prime \(p\le k\) divides \(inom{n}{k}\). More precisely, \(
u_p(inom{n}{k})=r_p-
u_p(k)\), so the construction is never a good binomial coefficient and does not establish the claimed infinitude.
