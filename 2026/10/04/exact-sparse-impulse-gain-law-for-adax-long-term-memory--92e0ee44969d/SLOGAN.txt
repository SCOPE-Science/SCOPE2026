One isolated gradient has an exact finite AdaX displacement gain \(\mathcal G(\beta_1,\beta_2)\), increasing from \(1\) to the sharp ceiling \(\sqrt{(1+\beta_2)/\beta_2}\) as first-moment memory grows. At \((\beta_1,\beta_2)=(0.9,10^{-4})\) the gain is \(2.8555435149\ldots\), and unlike Adam's analogous impulse response it remains summable for every \(\beta_1<1\).
