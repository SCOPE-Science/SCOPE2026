On an SPD quadratic, Hessian-corrected heavy ball is globally stable exactly when \(L(\beta+2\theta)<2(1+\alpha)\). On a mode of curvature \(\lambda\), the correction replaces momentum \(\alpha\) by \(\alpha-\theta\lambda\); choosing \(\theta=\alpha/\lambda\) and \(\beta=1/\lambda\) cancels that mode in one update.
