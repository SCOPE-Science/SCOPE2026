Every compact stationary Koper state obeys exact fast-nullcline and slow-filter regressions, and the three coordinate speeds satisfy \(k\mathbb E[(c'(x)-\delta\varepsilon)\dot x^2]=(2+\delta)\mathbb E[\dot y^2]\). In the mixed-mode regime \(k<0\), every genuinely recurrent state must therefore enter \(|x|<\sqrt{1+\delta\varepsilon/3}\), an explicit enlargement of the fold strip \(|x|<1\).
