On the scalar strongly convex quadratic, exact NIPG has a closed-form spike-and-reset law: a post-step curvature violation resets the next stepsize to \(c_1/a\), but the current step may already have amplified the objective. From the source's boundary initialization \(a t_0=c_0\), admissible summable expansion sequences produce unbounded second-step amplification; the sharp scalar expansion-safety cap is \(\gamma'_k\le2/c_0-1\).
