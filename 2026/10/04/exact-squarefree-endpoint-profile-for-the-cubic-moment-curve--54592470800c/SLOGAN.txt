For squarefree \(N\) coprime to \(6\), the cubic moment curve has exact critical constant \(\mathcal C_6=\prod_{p\mid N}(6-9/p+4/p^2)^{-1/6}\). Thus critical \(\ell^6\) synthesis holds exactly when the number of prime factors tends to infinity, separating primes from increasingly composite squarefree moduli.
