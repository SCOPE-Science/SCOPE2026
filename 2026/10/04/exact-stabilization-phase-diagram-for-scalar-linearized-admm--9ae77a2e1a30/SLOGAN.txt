On a symmetric scalar quadratic, constant-stabilization L-ADMM has an exact penalty-dependent rate phase diagram: critical damping for \(0<\rho/m<1\), an equal-magnitude sign-alternating optimum for \(1<\rho/m<(1+\sqrt5)/2\), and zero added stabilization beyond the golden-ratio threshold. The general majorization choice \(\eta=m\) is asymptotically rate-optimal only at \(\rho=m\).
