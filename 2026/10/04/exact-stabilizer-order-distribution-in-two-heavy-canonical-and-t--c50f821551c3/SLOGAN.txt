For canonical \(\mathbb P(1^r,a,b)\), the number of spaces whose heavy-coordinate line has generic stabilizer order exactly \(g\) is \(3\Phi(\lfloor r/g\rfloor)\); in the terminal subfamily it is \(3\Phi(\lfloor(r-1)/g\rfloor)\). This refines the isolated-singularity count to the full stabilizer-order distribution.
