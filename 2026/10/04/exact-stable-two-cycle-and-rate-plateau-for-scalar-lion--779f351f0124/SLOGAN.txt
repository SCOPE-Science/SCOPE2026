On a scalar quadratic, constant-step Lion has a unique locally stable nonzero two-cycle exactly when \(0<\eta\lambda<2\) and \(1+\beta_2-2\beta_1>0\); its amplitude is \(\eta/[2-\eta\lambda]\). The exact local factor is \(\max\{|1-\eta\lambda|,\beta_2\}\), with a full plateau where it equals \(\beta_2\).
