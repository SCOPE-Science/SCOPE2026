With a stale eigenbasis, SOAP's stationary Adam normalization can collapse a full-rank matrix update to rank one as \(\varepsilon\to0\), while a refreshed eigenbasis stays spectrally balanced. For a \(45^\circ\)-misaligned \(2\times2\) gradient with spectrum \(\{1,\kappa\}\), the exact stale-update condition number is \(\kappa+(\kappa^2-1)/(2\varepsilon)\).
