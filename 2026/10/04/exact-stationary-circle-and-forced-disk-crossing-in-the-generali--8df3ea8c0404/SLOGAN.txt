Every compact stationary state of the generalized Sprott O flow lies exactly on the mean-square circle law \(\mathbb E[(x+a/2)^2+y^2]=a^2/4\), equivalently \(\operatorname{Var}(x)+\operatorname{Var}(y)=-m(a+m)\) with \(m=\mathbb E[x]=\mathbb E[z]\). Apart from mixtures of the two equilibria, compact recurrence must place mass on both sides of that circle, so every nonconstant periodic orbit crosses it at least twice.
