For generalized Sprott F recurrence, the shifted coordinate lies exactly on a stationary mean-square circle: \(\mathbb E[(x+b/(2a))^2]=(b/(2a))^2\). Unless the stationary state is only a mixture of the two equilibria, it must visit both \(-b/a<x<0\) and the strict exterior, while every nonconstant periodic orbit stays in \(z>0\).
