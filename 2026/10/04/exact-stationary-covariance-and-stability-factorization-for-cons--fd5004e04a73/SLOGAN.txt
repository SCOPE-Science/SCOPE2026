On an additive-noise scalar quadratic, constant-template STORM splits exactly into an optimization mode \(1-s\) and an estimator-memory mode \(1-a\). It keeps SGD's stable interval \(0<s<2\) while reducing stationary iterate variance by \(R(a,s)=a(2-a-s+as)/[(2-a)(a+s-as)]\); lowering \(a\) reduces the noise floor but slows the memory mode.
