In every compact stationary state of the autonomous Vallis El Niño model, the current-speed energy is exactly the excess \(\operatorname{Var}(y)-(C/B)^2\operatorname{Var}(x)=B^{-2}\mathbb E[\dot x^2]\), and this excess vanishes only on equilibrium mixtures. Combined with the known temperature-circle balance, it yields a three-term quadratic budget that resolves temperature variance into current displacement, current acceleration, and mean-temperature departure.
