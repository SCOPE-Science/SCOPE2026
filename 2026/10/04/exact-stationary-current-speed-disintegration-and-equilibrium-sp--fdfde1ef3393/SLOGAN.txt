Every compact stationary state of the Hide–Skeldon–Acheson dynamo obeys the exact binwise current law \(\mathbb E[x^2\mid y]=1-(\kappa/\alpha)y\). After weighting by current energy, its mean disk speed falls below the loaded-equilibrium speed by exactly \(\beta\mathbb E[(x-\lambda z)^2]/(\lambda\mathbb E[x^2])\), with equality only on equilibrium mixtures.
