The Arneodo–Coullet–Tresser forced-oscillator class has two universal stationary laws: \(\mathbb E[z^2]=\mathbb E[y^2]\) and \(\mathbb E[x f(x)]=-\beta\mathbb E[y^2]\), forcing every nonconstant cycle to have \(P\ge2\pi\). For the 1981 symmetric piecewise model, every non-equilibrium compact recurrent state exceeds the outer-equilibrium amplitude \(1+\alpha/\mu\), and the period floor is attained exactly by two harmonic families when \(\beta=\mu\).
