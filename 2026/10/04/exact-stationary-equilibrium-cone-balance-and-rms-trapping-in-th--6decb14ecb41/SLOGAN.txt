In the Wang multiscroll flow, the two equilibrium rays are not merely local geometry: every compact stationary state satisfies \(\mathbb E[(x-k_+y)(x+k_-y)]=0\). Any stationary state that is not an equilibrium mixture must straddle both sides of that equilibrium cone, while its RMS ratio obeys the sharp trap \(k_-<\sqrt{\mathbb E[x^2]/\mathbb E[y^2]}<k_+\).
