# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Stationarity against arbitrary one-variable antiderivatives gives the cyclic conditional identities
\[
\mathbb E[\sin y\mid x]=bx,
\qquad
\mathbb E[\sin z\mid y]=by,
\qquad
\mathbb E[\sin x\mid z]=bz.
\]
Residual orthogonality then gives the three exact variance-speed defects. If any one defect vanishes, the corresponding coordinate speed is zero on invariant support; continuity of the next coordinate on a discrete sine level set propagates constancy around the cycle, so every support trajectory is an equilibrium.

Risk: equality rigidity uses the standard invariance of the support of an invariant probability measure.

## Originality
PASS. The complete same-object 2007 article was inspected and covers the equations, bifurcation route, chaos, multistability, diffusion, symbolic dynamics, and a special conservative \(b=0\) measure calculation, but not the accepted positive-damping conditional laws. Published critical-damping results concern \(b=1\) or \(b\ge1\), while the final claim is restricted to \(0<b<1\). Direct and equivalent published-index searches found no same-object implication.

Risk: the foundational 1999 article was not available as a complete readable full text, and the short generator argument could have appeared incidentally under different terminology.

## Value
PASS. The conditioning follows the system's defining feedback links and therefore measures how each nonlinear drive is statistically calibrated against its damped receiver. The exact speed defects quantify nonequilibrium activity and vanish precisely on equilibrium mixtures, giving a reusable invariant-data check throughout the periodic and chaotic regime.
