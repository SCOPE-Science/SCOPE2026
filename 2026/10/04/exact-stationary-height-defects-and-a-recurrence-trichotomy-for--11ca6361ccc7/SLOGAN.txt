The Newton–Leipnik flow has an exact stationary-statistics trichotomy across the sign of \(\alpha\). For \(\alpha>0\), every compact invariant measure satisfies a conditional law \(\mathbb E[xy\mid z]=\alpha z/5\) and two sharp height defects centered at \(z=-1/20\) and \(z=1/10\), forcing every nontrivial stationary state beyond the slabs \([-1/10,0]\) and \([0,1/5]\).
