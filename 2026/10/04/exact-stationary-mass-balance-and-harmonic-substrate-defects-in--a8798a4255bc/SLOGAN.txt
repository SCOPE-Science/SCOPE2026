Every compact stationary state of the positive-parameter Selkov oscillator obeys \(\mathbb E[x\mid x+y]=b\) and \(\mathbb E[x^2\mid y]=b/y-a\). Its activator variance is exactly both the mean-square mass-balance speed and the harmonic substrate deficit from equilibrium, while a second exact defect identifies logarithmic substrate speed; both defects vanish only at the unique equilibrium.
