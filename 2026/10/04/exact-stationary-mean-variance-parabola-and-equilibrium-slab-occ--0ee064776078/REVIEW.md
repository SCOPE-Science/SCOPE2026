# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Arbitrary one-variable antiderivative tests give the conditional laws, while stationarity of \(y\) and \(z\) gives
\[
\mathbb E[x]=\mathbb E[y^2]=-b\mathbb E[y].
\]
This yields the exact circle law and, by Jensen, the mean–variance parabola. The sign of \(y(y+b)\) on the equilibrium slab, combined with invariance of the compact support, proves the two-sided occupation theorem and the endpoint rigidity.

Risk: support rigidity uses the standard fact that the support of an invariant probability measure is invariant under the flow restricted to its compact support.

## Originality
PASS. The primary and same-author sources cover the simple-flow construction, critical points, dynamic parameter regions, and multistability motivation. Accessible statistical literature concerns approximate two-point spectra. A 2005 paper explicitly localizes periodic orbits for Case P, so no periodic localization claim is made here. Exact semantic searches found no same-object statement of the all-invariant-measures circle law, conditional regression, mean–variance parabola, or two-sided slab occupation.

Risk: complete text of the broad 2004 analytical-properties article and several related papers was unavailable. Their accessible abstracts were compared, and unresolved possible coverage is retained rather than treated as absent.

## Value
PASS. Case P is a natural multistability testbed. The theorem constrains every compact recurrent component by the same exact moment circle and forces every non-equilibrium stationary component to occupy both the interior and exterior of the slab determined by the two equilibrium \(y\)-levels. The canonical system therefore has the exact attractor-independent diagnostic \(\mathbb E[(y+1/2)^2]=1/4\).
