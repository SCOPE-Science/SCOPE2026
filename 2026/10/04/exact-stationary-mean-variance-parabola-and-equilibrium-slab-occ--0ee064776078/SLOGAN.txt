Every compact stationary state of generalized Sprott P lies on the exact moment circle \(\mathbb E[(y+b/2)^2]=b^2/4\), with \(\operatorname{Var}(y)=b^2\theta(1-\theta)\). Unless it is a mixture of the two equilibria, it must put positive mass both inside the equilibrium slab \(-b<y<0\) and outside it.
