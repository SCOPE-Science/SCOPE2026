# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Stationarity against arbitrary one-variable antiderivatives gives the two conditional laws
\[
(k_1A-k_2X)\mathbb E[Y\mid X]+k_3BX-2k_4X^2=0
\]
and
\[
\mathbb E[X\mid Z]=\frac{k_5}{k_3B}Z.
\]
Substitution identifies the coordinate speeds with conditional residuals, yielding both exact mean-square defect formulas. If either defect vanishes, bounded complete trajectories reduce successively through the affine \(Z\)- and \(Y\)-equations to equilibria.

Risk: the theorem assumes compact support so arbitrary antiderivative tests are integrable without additional tail hypotheses.

The nonlinear activator nullcline controls the conditional bromide mean, while the catalyst coordinate has an exact linear regression whose variance gap is precisely mean-square catalyst speed. These are directly testable on the classical oscillatory regime and vanish only for equilibrium mixtures.
