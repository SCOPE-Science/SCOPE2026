Every compact stationary state of the three-variable Oregonator is calibrated to two deterministic nullclines: \((k_1A-k_2X)\mathbb E[Y\mid X]+k_3BX-2k_4X^2=0\) and \(\mathbb E[X\mid Z]=(k_5/(k_3B))Z\). The corresponding conditional fluctuations are exact coordinate-speed defects, and either defect vanishes only on equilibrium mixtures.
