In every compact stationary state of the FitzHugh–Nagumo oscillator, the cubic and linear nullclines are exactly the conditional regressions \(\mathbb E[w\mid v]=v-v^3/3+I\) and \(\mathbb E[v\mid w]=bw-a\). Their regression residuals are precisely the coordinate speeds, producing exact variance defects that vanish only on equilibrium mixtures and forcing every genuinely time-dependent stationary state to occupy both sides of both nullclines.
