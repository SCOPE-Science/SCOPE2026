# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Arbitrary antiderivative test functions give
\[
\mathbb E[y\mid x]=0,
\qquad
\mathbb E[z\mid y]=0.
\]
Thus the relevant first and cross moments vanish. Stationarity of \(xy\) and \(z\) then gives
\[
\mathbb E[y^2]=a,
\qquad
\mathbb E[xz]=-a.
\]
Nonnegativity excludes \(a<0\). At \(a=0\), invariant-support tangency reduces the support exactly to the equilibrium line. For \(a>0\), the variance, covariance, Cauchy–Schwarz, and two-sided-mass conclusions follow immediately.

Risk: the \(a=0\) classification uses the standard invariance of the support of a compactly supported invariant probability measure.

## Originality
PASS. The foundational NE6 article was inspected in full, including its exact equations and bifurcation figure. Targeted searches found no invariant-measure, average, moment, variance, or mean-square formulation. The direct fractional-order follow-up and a later mathematical jerk-system treatment were also compared. Nine semantic-index searches over exact and equivalent formulations returned no NE6 coverage.

Risk: the identities are algebraically short, so an incidental differently phrased observation in unindexed literature remains possible.

## Value
PASS. The parameter introduced to remove equilibria is shown to equal the exact stationary mean-square velocity for every compact recurrent state. This turns a modeling coefficient into a universal measurable amplitude and produces a sharp stationary recurrence boundary at \(a=0\), together with a fixed nonzero \(x\)-\(z\) covariance for positive forcing.
