In the Jafari–Sprott NE6 family, every compact stationary state has the exact amplitude law \(\mathbb E[y^2]=a\) and covariance \(\operatorname{Cov}(x,z)=-a\). Thus \(a<0\) forbids compact stationary recurrence, \(a=0\) collapses it exactly to the equilibrium line, and every positive-\(a\) stationary state has RMS \(y\)-amplitude \(\sqrt a\).
