For canonical Sprott B, every compact invariant probability measure has exact root-mean-square \(y\)-amplitude one. Unless it is supported only on the two equilibria, it must cross both \(|y|<1\) and \(|y|>1\), and the exact defect \(\int y^2z\,d\mu=-\int(x-y)^2\,d\mu<0\) forces recurrent mass into \(z<0\).
