# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Invariance gives
\[
a\mathbb E[|x|]+(1-b)\mathbb E[x]=1,
\]
which is exactly
\[
\frac{\mathbb E[x_+]}{r_+}
+
\frac{\mathbb E[x_-]}{-r_-}
=
1.
\]
If one sign is absent, support invariance confines every complete orbit to one affine Lozi branch. Under
\[
0<b<1,\qquad a>1-b,
\]
each branch has one eigenvalue inside and one outside the unit circle, so its only bounded bi-infinite orbit is its fixed point. This proves endpoint rigidity and compulsory two-sided sign recurrence for every other invariant measure. The marginal law follows from \(y'=bx\).

Risk: the one-sided equality classification uses the standard invariance of the support of an invariant probability measure for a homeomorphism.
