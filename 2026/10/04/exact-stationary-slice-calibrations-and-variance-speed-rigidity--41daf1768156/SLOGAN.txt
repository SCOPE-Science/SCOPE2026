Every compact stationary state of the positive Brusselator is calibrated slice by slice: \(\mathbb E[x\mid x+y]=A\), while activator size-biasing gives \(\mathbb E_\nu[x\mid y]=B/y\). These laws force \(x+y>A\) on every bounded complete positive orbit and yield the exact fluctuation defect \(\operatorname{Var}(y)-\operatorname{Var}(x+y)=\operatorname{Var}(x)=\mathbb E[(\dot x+\dot y)^2]\), with equality only at equilibrium.
