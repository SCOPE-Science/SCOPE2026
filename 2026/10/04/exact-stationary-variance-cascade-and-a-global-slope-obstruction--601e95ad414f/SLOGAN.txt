Every compact stationary state of the classical repressilator has an exact variance cascade from repressive transcription drive to mRNA to protein: \(\operatorname{Var}(R(p_{j_i}))-\operatorname{Var}(m_i)=\mathbb E[\dot m_i^2]\) and \(\operatorname{Var}(m_i)-\operatorname{Var}(p_i)=\beta^{-2}\mathbb E[\dot p_i^2]\). Any genuinely oscillatory or otherwise non-equilibrium compact stationary state therefore requires the maximum Hill-repression slope \(L\) to exceed one.
