For \(R_q=\mathbb F_q[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)\), the strongly nil-clean locus in \(M_n(R_q)\) has exact size \(\sum_{r=0}^{n}{n\brack r}_q q^{2n^2-n-r(n-r)}\). At \(n=2\) and \(q=2\), this is \(224\) of \(256\) matrices, quantifying the gap between nil-clean and strongly nil-clean dual matrices.
