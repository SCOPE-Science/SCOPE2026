For every real \(\ell_q(I)\) with at least two coordinates and \(1\le q<2\), the four-term von Neumann–Jordan constant has the exact value \(\bigl(\lambda^q+(1-\lambda)^q\bigr)^{2/q}+2^{2/q}\lambda(1-\lambda)\). Sharp Clarkson control gives the upper bound, and two disjoint coordinate unit vectors attain it.
