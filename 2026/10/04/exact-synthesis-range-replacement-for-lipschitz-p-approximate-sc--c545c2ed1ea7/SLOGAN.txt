For every Lipschitz \(p\)-approximate Schauder frame with finite \(p\), the synthesis range lies between \(\operatorname{span}(M)\) and \(\overline{\operatorname{span}}(M)\), so its closure is exactly the closed span of the framed subset. A singleton frame in any Banach space of dimension greater than one has proper one-dimensional synthesis range, disproving automatic surjectivity onto the ambient space.
