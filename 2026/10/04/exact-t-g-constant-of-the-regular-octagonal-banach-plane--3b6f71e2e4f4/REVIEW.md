PASS. The primary definition was reconstructed with its sphere and equilateral constraints. On each cell the squared objective is quadratic, so exact endpoint/interior-vertex checking is exhaustive. `verify_octagon_tg.py` carries this out in \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)\), obtains the global product bound \(9-4\sqrt2\), and checks the explicit equality witness. No finite sample is used to infer an unchecked continuum bound.
