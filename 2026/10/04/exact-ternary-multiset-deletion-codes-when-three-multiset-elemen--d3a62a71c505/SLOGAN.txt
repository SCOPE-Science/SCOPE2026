When only three multiset elements survive, ternary multiset deletion codes collapse sharply: \(S_3(n,n-3)=4\) at \(n=5,6\) and \(S_3(n,n-3)=3\) for every \(n\ge7\). A heavy-coordinate disjointness argument gives the infinite upper bound, with explicit four-word witnesses at the two exceptional lengths.
