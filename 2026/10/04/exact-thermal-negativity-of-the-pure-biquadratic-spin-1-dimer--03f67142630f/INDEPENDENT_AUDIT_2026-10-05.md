# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For two spin-one particles, \(\mathbf S_1\cdot\mathbf S_2\) has total-spin eigenvalues \(-2,-1,1\), so its square is exactly \(I+3P_0\). Hence the pure-biquadratic Hamiltonian has one singlet energy \(4K\) and the other eight levels are \(K+B M\). The Gibbs operator therefore equals, up to a common factor, \(e^{-bM}+(r-1)P_0\). After partial transpose, the only potentially negative sectors are two identical exponent-one blocks and one exponent-two block, all with determinant \(1-a^2\). Thus NPT occurs exactly when \(a>1\), equivalently \(r>4\), giving \(T_c=3|K|/\log4\) independently of finite field. The negative eigenvalues give the stated closed negativity formula, and the zero-temperature crossing follows by equating \(4K\) with the polarized energy. The assigned verifier reconstructs the full nine-dimensional Hamiltonian; an independent spin-matrix check also reproduced one squared-dot eigenvalue four and eight eigenvalues one, confirming the load-bearing correction.
