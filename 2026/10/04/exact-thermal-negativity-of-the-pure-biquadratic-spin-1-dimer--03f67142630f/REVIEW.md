**PASS.** The spin-\(1\) identity \((\mathbf S_1\!\cdot\!\mathbf S_2)^2=I+3P_0\) fixes the spectrum. Partial transpose of the Gibbs operator yields three explicit \(2\times2\) blocks, all with determinant \(1-[(r-1)/3]^2\). This proves the exact threshold and strict finite-field positivity below it. The packaged matrix calculation independently reconstructs the Hamiltonian and agrees with the formula.

## Originality

**PASS, narrowly scoped.** The direct source prints a spectrum inconsistent with its Hamiltonian and reports a finite positive-temperature critical field. Earlier optical-lattice work treats bilinear-biquadratic spin-\(1\) thermal entanglement, while the later Sargolzahi--Mirafzali--Sarbishaei paper treats the broader nonuniform-field Hamiltonian and cites the uniform nonlinear case as prior work. None of the inspected sources states the corrected closed formula, the exact \(3|K|/\log4\) threshold, and the no-finite-field conclusion together.

## Value

**PASS.** The correction changes the phase diagram qualitatively: a reported finite-field destruction becomes asymptotic suppression at positive temperature, while the actual finite-field transition is confined to \(T=0\). It also replaces an approximate threshold observation by an exact formula and restores the maximally entangled qutrit singlet ground state.
