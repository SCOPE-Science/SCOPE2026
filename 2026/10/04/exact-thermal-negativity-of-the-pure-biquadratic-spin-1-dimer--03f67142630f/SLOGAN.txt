For \(H=K(\mathbf S_1\!\cdot\!\mathbf S_2)^2+B(S_{1z}+S_{2z})\) with \(K<0\), the exact positive-temperature negativity threshold is \(T_c=3|K|/\log4\), independent of every finite uniform field. Below that threshold the negativity is strictly positive at every finite \(B\); a finite field transition occurs only at \(T=0\), at \(|B|=3|K|/2\).
