For every cyclic group \(\mathbb Z_N\), exact three-point support has a closed uncertainty law: the smallest possible Fourier support is \(N(q_N-2)/q_N\), where \(q_N\) is the least divisor of \(N\) that is at least three. Equivalently, three nonzero Fourier monomials can vanish on at most \(2N/q_N\) frequency points, and this is sharp for every \(N\).
