For every odd prime \(p\), a three-sparse function on \(\mathbb F_p^d\) can lose at most two local Fourier characters, and affine dimension alone determines the exact support scale. Collinear supports have \((p-j)p^{d-1}\) nonzero Fourier coefficients and noncollinear supports have \((p^2-j)p^{d-2}\), with every \(j\in\{0,1,2\}\) attained on every fixed support.
