# Verification

The theorem is proved analytically in `RESULT.md`; finite computation is corroborative.

`verify.py` uses exact integer-vector arithmetic in the cyclotomic field generated by a primitive \(p\)-th root. An element is represented by its \(p\) coefficients modulo the single rational relation \(1+\zeta+\cdots+\zeta^{p-1}=0\); exact zero is therefore equivalent to all coefficient entries being equal. No floating-point tolerance is used.

For \(p\in\{3,5,7,11\}\), the script checks every normalized collinear three-point support, verifies explicit coefficient triples with exactly zero, one, and two local zeros, and exhaustively checks a finite box of nonzero integer coefficients for the upper bound of two zeros. It performs the analogous checks for the normalized noncollinear support \(\{0,e_1,e_2\}\), including the explicit two-zero witness \((\zeta,-(1+\zeta),1)\). It also checks the stated scaling formulas for sample dimensions.

A successful replay prints `VERIFY_OK`. The finite range is not an exhaustive proof of the infinite theorem; the all-prime and all-dimension result rests on the analytic proof.
