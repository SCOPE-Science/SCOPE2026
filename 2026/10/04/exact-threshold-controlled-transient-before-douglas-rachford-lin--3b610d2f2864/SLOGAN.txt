On the two-variable basis-pursuit family \(x_1+a x_2=b\), zero-initialized Douglas--Rachford can spend exactly \(\max\{0,\lfloor\gamma(1+a^2)/b\rfloor-1\}\) iterations before entering its permanent linear regime when \(\gamma\ge b/(1-a)\). After identification, the Euclidean error contracts exactly by \(a/\sqrt{1+a^2}\) per step, so the threshold can make the transient arbitrarily long without changing the eventual rate.\n
