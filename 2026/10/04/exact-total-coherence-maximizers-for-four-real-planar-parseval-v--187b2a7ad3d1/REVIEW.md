# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reduces the real Parseval-frame problem exactly to rank-two orthogonal projections, dualizes the entrywise absolute-value objective against the finite set of Seidel sign matrices, and applies the Ky Fan variational principle. The complete switching classification of order-four Seidel matrices yields top-two eigenvalue sums \(2\) or \(1+\sqrt5\), and the strict spectral gap in the maximizing class gives a unique spectral projector. Exact arithmetic independently checks the displayed projector and its objective value.

## Originality
PASS. The closest primary source, Cahill--Casazza (arXiv:1910.01733), poses the non-equiangular total-coherence problem and structural questions about equal norm and parallel vectors. Nguyen's 2025 dissertation reaches the same \((4,2)\) real case only far enough to prove that unrestricted maximizers cannot all be equal norm: it computes the equal-norm ceiling \(2\sqrt2\) and gives random non-equal-norm examples above \(3\). The inspected sources and targeted searches did not state the sharp unrestricted value \(1+\sqrt5\), the complete equality classification, or the forced parallel pair.

## Value
PASS. This is the smallest natural non-equiangular case highlighted in later work and it closes the optimization problem completely rather than giving another numerical witness. The equality classification also resolves two structural issues in this case: unequal norms are forced, and parallelism is forced rather than merely possible.

## Closest literature and limitations
The theorem is only for real four-vector Parseval frames in dimension two. The complex analogue and larger parameters are not addressed. The dissertation comparison used a materially relevant indexed text extraction because direct full-PDF retrieval was unavailable; this leaves a limited residual priority risk for an unindexed statement elsewhere in that document.
