On the equilibrium-line surface \(b=1/c\), the Li–Wu–Zhang exponential hyperchaotic flow has exact spectrum \(\{0,-a\}\cup\operatorname{roots}(\lambda^2+(c-1)\lambda+\xi^2-c)\), so its line is transversely attracting exactly for \(c>1\) and \(|\xi|>\sqrt c\). This factorization supplies a direct counterexample to the source's published stability inequality and explains both of its reported convergent line-equilibrium trajectories.
