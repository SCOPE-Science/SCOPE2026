The verifier rebuilds the pair deletion-alignment automaton and its three-pair product from definitions. It checks direct graph triangle counts for small lengths and separately certifies the transfer recurrences by exact linear algebra on invariant Krylov spaces. The recurrence certificate, not the finite enumeration, proves the closed form for every \(n\ge1\).

Limits: binary alphabet, one-deletion radius, triangle count only. No independent audit or external validation is represented here.
