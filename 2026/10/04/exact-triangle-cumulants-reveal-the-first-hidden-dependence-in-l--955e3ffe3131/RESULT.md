# Exact triangle cumulants reveal the first hidden dependence in LKJ correlation matrices
## Finding
Let \(C\sim\operatorname{LKJ}_n(\eta)\) with \(n\ge 3\), \(\eta>0\), and \(\nu=n+2\eta-1\). For any three distinct off-diagonal entries, \(\mathbb E[C_{e_1}C_{e_2}C_{e_3}]=\nu^{-2}\) exactly when the three unordered edges form one triangle, and the expectation is \(0\) otherwise. Consequently the off-diagonal entries, although pairwise independent, are not three-wise independent, and \(\mathbb E\,\operatorname{tr}((C-I_n)^3)=n(n-1)(n-2)/\nu^2\). If \(\eta_n/n\to\theta\in[0,\infty)\), then \(n^{-1}\mathbb E\,\operatorname{tr}((C-I_n)^3)\to(1+2\theta)^{-2}\), the third centered moment of the corresponding Marchenko--Pastur limit.

## Assumptions and scope
The LKJ law is taken on the \(n\times n\) elliptope with density proportional to \(\det(C)^{\eta-1}\). The result is finite-dimensional and exact for every \(n\ge3\) and \(\eta>0\). The three entries in the first statement must be distinct off-diagonal coordinates, identified with distinct unordered edges of the complete graph on \(n\) vertices.

The parameter \(\nu=n+2\eta-1\) is the degrees-of-freedom parameter in the restricted-Wishart representation of the LKJ ensemble. The asymptotic statement keeps only the regime \(\eta_n/n\to\theta\in[0,\infty)\); no fluctuation theorem for the cubic trace is asserted.

## Proof
Fix three vertices, relabeled \(1,2,3\), and write \(x=C_{12}\), \(y=C_{13}\), and \(z=C_{23}\). In the restricted-Wishart representation, a principal \(3\times3\) correlation block has an LKJ law with shape
\[
\eta_3=\eta+\frac{n-3}{2},
\]
so the density of
\[
R=\begin{pmatrix}1&x&y\\x&1&z\\y&z&1\end{pmatrix}
\]
is proportional to \(\det(R)^{\eta_3-1}\). The determinant factors as
\[
\det(R)=(1-x^2)(1-y^2)-(z-xy)^2.
\]
Conditionally on \((x,y)\), the admissible interval for \(z\) is therefore
\[
xy-\sqrt{(1-x^2)(1-y^2)}<z<xy+\sqrt{(1-x^2)(1-y^2)},
\]
and the conditional density is an even function of \(z-xy\). Hence
\[
\mathbb E[z\mid x,y]=xy.
\]
The source proves that distinct off-diagonal LKJ entries are pairwise independent and gives their common second moment
\[
\mathbb E[x^2]=\mathbb E[y^2]=\frac1\nu.
\]
Thus
\[
\mathbb E[xyz]
 =\mathbb E[x^2y^2]
 =\mathbb E[x^2]\,\mathbb E[y^2]
 =\frac1{\nu^2}.
\]
This proves the triangle case.

Now take any three distinct unordered edges that do not form a triangle. Their three-edge graph has a vertex of odd degree. Conjugation by a diagonal sign matrix leaves the LKJ density and the elliptope invariant. Flipping the sign at that odd-degree vertex changes the sign of the product of the three selected entries while preserving its distribution, so its expectation is \(0\). This also shows that the triangle moment is the first graph-local obstruction to three-wise independence.

Set \(A=C-I_n\). Since \(A\) has zero diagonal,
\[
\operatorname{tr}(A^3)=\sum_{i,j,k\ \mathrm{distinct}} C_{ij}C_{jk}C_{ki}.
\]
There are \(n(n-1)(n-2)\) ordered triples of distinct vertices, and every summand is a triangle product. Therefore
\[
\mathbb E\,\operatorname{tr}((C-I_n)^3)
 =\frac{n(n-1)(n-2)}{(n+2\eta-1)^2}.
\]
If \(\eta_n/n\to\theta\), division by \(n\) gives the limit \((1+2\theta)^{-2}\). The Marchenko--Pastur law with aspect ratio \(c=(1+2\theta)^{-1}\) has raw moments \(1\), \(1+c\), and \(1+3c+c^2\) through order three, so its third moment centered at \(1\) equals \(c^2=(1+2\theta)^{-2}\).

## Verification
The proof is symbolic and does not depend on numerical evidence. Its critical steps are the exact determinant identity for a \(3\times3\) correlation matrix, symmetry of the conditional LKJ density about \(z=xy\), the published pairwise-independence theorem, and the published marginal variance \(1/\nu\).

As a stress check only, direct Gaussian-Gram simulations at \((n,\eta)=(3,1),(4,1),(5,1),(4,2.5)\) produced triangle moments and cubic-trace means close to the exact formulas; these simulations are not used in the proof.

## Relationship to prior work
Hansen proves exact pairwise independence of distinct off-diagonal entries under every LKJ law, gives their variance \(1/(n+2\eta-1)\), and notes that higher-order dependence remains. The present result identifies the first nonzero higher-order cumulant explicitly: it is supported exactly on graph triangles, with coefficient \((n+2\eta-1)^{-2}\). This immediately yields an exact finite-dimensional cubic spectral moment and matches the third centered moment of Hansen's Marchenko--Pastur spectral limit.

Joe and Kurowicka develop moment recursions for random correlation matrices generated by partial-correlation C-vines. The inspected article advertises and derives first and second moments; it does not state the triangle third moment or the cubic-trace identity above. Focused searches for LKJ third joint moments, triangle products, and cubic spectral moments did not identify an implication-equivalent published formula.

## Limitations
The result gives one exact third-order statistic, not the full joint law of triples or higher-order cumulants. It does not quantify fluctuations of \(\operatorname{tr}((C-I_n)^3)\), nor does it characterize all higher graph-moment patterns. A residual literature risk remains that an older specialized treatment of joint moments of Gaussian sample-correlation coefficients contains an equivalent triangle identity in different notation; the inspected modern moment paper and focused searches did not reveal such coverage.
