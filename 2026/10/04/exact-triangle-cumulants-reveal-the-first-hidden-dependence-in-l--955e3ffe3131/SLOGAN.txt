Under \(\operatorname{LKJ}_n(\eta)\), distinct correlation entries are pairwise independent but their first nontrivial joint dependence is exactly triangular: \(\mathbb E[C_{ij}C_{ik}C_{jk}]=(n+2\eta-1)^{-2}\), while every nontriangle triple of distinct entries has zero third moment. This gives the exact cubic spectral moment \(\mathbb E\,\operatorname{tr}((C-I_n)^3)=n(n-1)(n-2)/(n+2\eta-1)^2\).\n
