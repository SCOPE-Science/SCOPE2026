For \(\Omega_N=\{0,\pm1,\pm3\}\subset\mathbb Z_N\), the exact normalized Turán mass has three arithmetic regimes: \(2\) for even \(N\), \(1+\sec(3\pi/N)\) for odd multiples of \(3\), and an explicit two-contact formula for \(N\equiv\pm1\pmod6\). The last regime is attained by a cubic whose two positive roots straddle no sampled cosine, giving a sharp finite-grid certificate.
