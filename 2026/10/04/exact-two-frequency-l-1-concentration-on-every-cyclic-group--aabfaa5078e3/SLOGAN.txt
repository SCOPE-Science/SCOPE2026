For every cyclic group and every target, the exact two-frequency \(L^1\) point-concentration constant is a maximum over divisors, with each term determined by a finite cosine sum and \(\gcd(a,L)\). For odd prime \(p\) and nonzero target it collapses to \(2\sin(\pi/(2p))\cos(\pi/p)=\pi/p+O(p^{-3})\).\n
