For the square \(Q=[-1,1]^2\), every Ulam floating body is explicit: below half-area, each octant of the boundary is one parabolic arc followed by one rectangular-hyperbolic arc, and above half-area the body follows from an exact complement scaling. Its area is \(4-44\delta/27+(8\delta/9)\log(\delta/2)\) for \(0<\delta\le2\).\n
