For equal-overlap product states sampled as an ordered sequence without replacement, the global unambiguous-discrimination optimum is exactly \((1-s)^k\) for \(k<N\) and \((1-s)^{N-1}(1+(N-1)s)\) for \(k=N\). Fixed local single-system unambiguous measurements attain the same values, proving the unambiguous half of the recent all-\(N,k\) conjecture.
