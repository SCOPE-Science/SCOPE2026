# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Ultrahomogeneity gives the pointed-subalgebra orbit criterion; the finite one-generator classification gives exactly two pointed types at size \(2\) and one at every size \(n\ge3\); strong amalgamation over the constants gives infinitely many realizations of each nonconstant type. The argument distinguishes fixed constants from nonconstant generators and does not infer an infinite statement from finite testing.

## Originality
PASS. The closest primary source, Yamamoto's automorphism-group paper, gives the general pointed-subalgebra principle but its explicit non-precompactness witness varies two-generated types with a fixed unary type. Citkin gives the finite generator classification but no Fraïssé-limit orbit theorem. Exact and implication-shaped searches, including a semantic literature index, found no source stating this unary decomposition. The main residual risk is unindexed folklore.

## Value
PASS. The conclusion gives the full unary permutation profile of a natural homogeneous Heyting algebra: two fixed points and one countably infinite transitive orbit for every finite generator size \(n\ge3\). This makes the source of non-oligomorphicity explicit at the smallest possible arity and provides a reusable invariant for later study of stabilizers and higher tuple actions.

## Closest literature and limitations
The main comparison is Yamamoto, arXiv:2105.01291v4, especially the preliminaries and Theorem 2.1 proof. The finite pointed classification comes from Citkin's work on one-generated Heyting algebras. Higher arities are not addressed.
