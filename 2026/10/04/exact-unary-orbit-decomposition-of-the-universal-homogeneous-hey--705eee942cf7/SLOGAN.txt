For the universal homogeneous countable Heyting algebra \(L\), the point action of \(\operatorname{Aut}(L)\) is completely explicit: \(0\) and \(1\) are the only fixed points, and for every \(n\ge3\) there is exactly one countably infinite orbit of elements generating an \(n\)-element subalgebra. Thus non-oligomorphicity already appears on \(1\)-tuples.
