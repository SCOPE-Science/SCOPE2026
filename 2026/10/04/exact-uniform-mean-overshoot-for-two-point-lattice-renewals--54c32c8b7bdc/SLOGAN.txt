For i.i.d. renewal increments supported on \(\{1,m\}\), the worst expected overshoot occurs at an explicit small boundary \(k=\lfloor\log(\mathbb E X)/[-\log(1-p)]\rfloor\), and its exact value has a closed form. Under \(p=\lambda/m\), the normalized sharp constant tends to \(1-\log(1+\lambda)/\lambda\), strictly below the corresponding normalized Lorden moment bound.
