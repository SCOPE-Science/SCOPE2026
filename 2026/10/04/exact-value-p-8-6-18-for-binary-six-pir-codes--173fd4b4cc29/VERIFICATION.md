# Verification

Run:

`python3 verify.py`

The verifier constructs the cyclic binary \([17,8,6]\) representative generated by \(g(x)=1+x+x^3+x^6+x^8+x^9\). It checks rank \(8\), enumerates all \(256\) primal codewords to verify minimum distance \(6\), and enumerates all \(512\) dual codewords to verify dual minimum distance \(5\).

It then extracts every dual word of weight \(5\). There are exactly \(34\). For each of the \(136\) coordinate pairs it counts how many weight-\(5\) supports contain the pair; \(68\) pairs have incidence two and \(68\) have incidence three. Hence the maximum pair incidence is three.

Finally, it exhausts the elementary size constraints for six pairwise disjoint equal-syndrome recovery sets in length \(17\). Because pairwise symmetric differences are nonzero dual words, every pair of recovery-set sizes sums to at least \(5\), while the six sizes sum to at most \(17\). The only sorted positive solution is \((2,3,3,3,3,3)\). Such a packing would force five distinct dual weight-\(5\) supports through the same coordinate pair, contradicting the verified incidence maximum.

The computation verifies the new lower-bound obstruction for the explicit representative. The transfer to every length-\(17\) candidate uses the published uniqueness classification of the binary \([17,8,6]\) code. The upper bound \(P(8,6)\le18\) is taken from the inspected published PIR source and is not independently reconstructed in this package.
