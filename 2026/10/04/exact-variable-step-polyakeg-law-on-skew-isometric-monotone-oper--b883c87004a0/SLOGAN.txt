On every skew-isometric monotone linear root problem, PolyakEG has the exact variable-step law \(\|x_K-x_\star\|=\|x_0-x_\star\|\prod_{k<K}(1+(L\gamma_k)^2)^{-1/2}\). Under the paper's critical condition, convergence occurs exactly when \(\sum_k(L\gamma_k)^2=\infty\), and the largest admissible constant step is exactly rate-optimal.
