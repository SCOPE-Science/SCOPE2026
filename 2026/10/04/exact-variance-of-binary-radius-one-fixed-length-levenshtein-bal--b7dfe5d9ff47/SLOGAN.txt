For a uniformly random binary length-\(n\) center, the radius-one fixed-length Levenshtein ball has exact variance \(\frac{n^3-9n^2+48n-120}{4}+\frac{n^2+3n+17}{2^{n-1}}-4^{1-n}\). Thus its root-mean-square fluctuation scale is exactly \(\frac12 n^{3/2}(1+O(n^{-1}))\), sharpening the scale information behind the known concentration bound.
