The package verifies the algebraic reduction of the published threshold quantity, the exact derivative identities, the closed-form solution of the threshold equation, the endpoint cancellation at full contaminated-bee return, and the asymptotic limit of the critical return fraction.

`verify.py` also evaluates the parameter values used in the 2019 source and checks that the exact formulas reproduce the two numerical transition values displayed in Figure 5.

The verification is limited to the mathematical claim stated in RESULT.md. It does not validate the empirical calibration of the biological model, spatial extensions, stochastic effects, or the suitability of the model for pesticide policy.
