# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Fuentes García's inspected primary theorem supplies the exact formula
\[
\varepsilon(A,x)=\frac{2n(n+1)b+a}{2n+1},\qquad n=\left\lfloor\sqrt{\frac{a}{2b}}\right\rfloor,
\]
for the non-torsion decomposable elliptic ruled surface away from the distinguished sections. With \(A^2=2ab\), direct algebra gives the stated factorization. The square-slope maximality criterion and chamber lower bounds then follow from the chamber inequalities and an exact one-variable minimization.

The 2008 primer summarizes Fuentes García's formulas and a separate near-maximality existence theorem, but not these conclusions. published-finding corpus searches using the source formula, square-slope aliases, normalized ratios, and chamber language returned no implication-equivalent algebraic-geometric finding.

## Value
PASS. Comparison with the universal upper bound \(\sqrt{A^2}\) is a central local-positivity question in the source literature. The result gives an exact quantitative answer on a standard elliptic ruled surface: every chamber has a sharp stability constant, maximality has a complete arithmetic classification, and the high-chamber deficit decays optimally as \(1/(2n+1)^2\). This is a structural refinement, not an arbitrary finite slice or recomputation table.

Bauer et al. provide the closest broad survey context and explicitly emphasize both the ruled-surface computations and comparison with \(\sqrt{A^2}\). The result is limited to the non-torsion decomposable elliptic case and points off the distinguished sections.
