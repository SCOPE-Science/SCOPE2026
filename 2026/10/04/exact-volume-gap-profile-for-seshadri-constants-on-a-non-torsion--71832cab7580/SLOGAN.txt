On a fixed non-torsion decomposable elliptic ruled surface, the exact Seshadri formula admits a factored volume gap. Maximal local positivity occurs precisely on square-slope rays, while the \(n\)-th chamber has optimal relative square-deficit \(1/(2n+1)^2\); on \(a\ge2b\) the sharp ratio floor is \(2\sqrt2/3\).
