For the six unit balls centered at the vertices of the regular octahedron \(\{\pm e_j/\sqrt2\}\), the spherical hexahedron has exact volume \(4\pi/3+2/(3\sqrt2)-(49/6)\arccos(23/27)\), surface area \(4\pi-20\arccos(23/27)\), and mean width \(2+(\sqrt3/2-10/\pi)\arccos(23/27)\). These formulas close Finch's 2013 exact-evaluation question and give the consistent adjacent-vertex distance \(\sqrt2/3\).
