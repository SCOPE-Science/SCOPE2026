On a complete multipartite graph, a \(\{0,1,2\}\)-labeling is total Roman exactly when positive labels meet at least two parts and every part containing a zero has a label \(2\) outside it. This yields a closed weight enumerator for all total Roman dominating functions, with \(\gamma_{tR}(G)=\min\{N,4,\min_i n_i+2\}\) as a corollary.
