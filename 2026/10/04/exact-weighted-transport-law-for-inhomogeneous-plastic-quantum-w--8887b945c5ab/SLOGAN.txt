For the massless inhomogeneous plastic quantum walk, the commutator constraint is exactly a weighted edge-Lipschitz bound: edge \(x\leftrightarrow x+2\) has length \(2\epsilon/\cos\theta(x+1)\). The Connes spread from a localized state is therefore the expected weighted shortest-path distance, replacing the source's per-step convex optimization by a closed geometric formula.
