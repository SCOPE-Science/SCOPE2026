For SymPol and the optimized constant-bet KL-inf statistic on \(n\) nonnegative inputs, the exact worst-case multiplicative statistic gap is \(1/b_n\), where \(b_n\) is the central binomial point mass at its own mean. This factor is sharp and satisfies \(1/b_n\sim\sqrt{\pi n/2}\), so SymPol's p-value improvement over optimized constant betting can grow only, and can genuinely grow, on the square-root sample-size scale.
