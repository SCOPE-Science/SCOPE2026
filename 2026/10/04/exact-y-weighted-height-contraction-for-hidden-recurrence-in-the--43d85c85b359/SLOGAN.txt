In the positive-parameter Wang–Chen flow, every non-equilibrium compact stationary state has a strictly contracted \(y\)-weighted mean height: \(\mathbb E_\nu[z]=-16a/(1+\mathbb E[\dot z^2])\), lying strictly between the stable-equilibrium height \(-16a\) and zero. The forcing is visible at every represented \(x\)-level through \(\mathbb E[yz\mid x]=-a\), so hidden recurrence necessarily carries negative-height mass.
