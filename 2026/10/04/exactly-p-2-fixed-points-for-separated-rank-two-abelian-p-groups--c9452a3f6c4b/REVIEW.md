# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The fixed subgroup is \(\ker(\varphi-I)\), and because the group is finite its order equals the order of the cokernel. A two-generator presentation of that cokernel yields six explicit \(2\times2\) minors; their gcd gives the exact \(p\)-adic exponent of the fixed subgroup. For exponent gap at least three, the determinant minor reduces modulo \(p^3\) to the product of the two diagonal displacements. At gap two, the proof separately counts exact cancellation against the off-diagonal product. The resulting valuation partitions are exhaustive and disjoint. The packaged replay enumerates automorphism matrices in every formula branch and directly checks fixed points in small examples.

## Originality
PASS. The closest 2018 source gives only the arbitrary-exponent fixed-point-free formula and poses the broader theta-value problem. Exact low-exponent papers cover \((1,2)\), \((1,3)\), and \((2,3)\); the theorem explicitly excludes the covered separated pair \((1,3)\). A 2022 spectrum theorem determines which fixed-point sizes occur, but not multiplicities of automorphisms. Targeted searches found no statement implying the five separated-exponent formulas.

## Value
PASS. The theorem resolves the full \(p^2\)-fixed-point layer for an infinite, naturally defined rank-two domain arising directly from the published distinct-exponent theta problem. It also identifies a genuine arithmetic transition between exponent gap two and larger gaps, rather than merely extending a numerical table.

The main residual risk is bibliographic: an equivalent multiplicity formula may be present under different fixed-point or Reidemeister terminology.
