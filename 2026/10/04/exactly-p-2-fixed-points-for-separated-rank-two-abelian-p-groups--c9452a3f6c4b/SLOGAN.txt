For \(C_{p^a}\oplus C_{p^b}\) with exponent gap at least two, the number of automorphisms fixing exactly \(p^2\) elements admits a complete five-case formula outside the previously computed pair \((a,b)=(1,3)\). The count is controlled by the \(p\)-adic valuation of the determinant minor of \(\varphi-I\), with new cancellation terms appearing exactly when \(b-a=2\).
