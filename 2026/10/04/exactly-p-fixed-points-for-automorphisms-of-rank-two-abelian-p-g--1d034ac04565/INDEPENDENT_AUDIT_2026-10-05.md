# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The standard matrix model partitions automorphisms by whether each diagonal displacement is a unit modulo \(p\). Exactly one unit reduces the kernel to a valuation-one scalar congruence, producing the two symmetric bulk contributions. If both displacements are divisible by \(p\), then for gap at least two an explicit quotient onto \(C_p^2\) or \(C_{p^2}\) rules out a kernel of order \(p\); in the adjacent case the surviving unit off-diagonal branch is solved directly. The four disjoint counts simplify to the displayed formulas. The actual frozen verifier was read and brute-enumerates small parameters across every branch, reproducing all known low-exponent special cases and the characteristic-two transition.

## originality

PASS

The 2018 full primary article gives exactly the \((1,2)\) table and an arbitrary-exponent formula only for fixed-point-free automorphisms, while explicitly posing the general distinct-exponent theta problem. Later low-exponent papers cover \((1,3)\) and \((2,3)\) individually. Senden's spectrum theorem answers occurrence, not multiplicity. The audited theorem uniformly subsumes the known exactly-p special cases and adds every other exponent pair; no searched source gives that arbitrary-exponent multiplicity.
