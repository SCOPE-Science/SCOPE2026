The stable-equilibrium boundary \(\beta=0\) of the cubic jerk flow is generically a Hopf bifurcation with \(l_1=(8\alpha^2-13\alpha+15\gamma-1)/20\). Its sign fixes both the side and stability of the emerging small cycle, while \(8\alpha^2-13\alpha+15\gamma-1=0\) is the degenerate boundary requiring higher-order analysis.
