Lee's translated-domain union theorem admits explicit Riesz bounds in every dimension: with phase gap \(m\) and cross-domain Bessel bound \(C_{12}\), one may take \(L=A_1A_2m^2/[2(A_2+A_1m^2+2C_{12})]\) and \(U=B_1+C_{12}+2B_2\). This supplies a quantitative estimate for the constants that the source paper explicitly left for future investigation.
