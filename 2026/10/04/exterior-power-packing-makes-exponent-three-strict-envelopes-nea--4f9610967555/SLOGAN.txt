The quadratic \(15n^2\) strict-envelope bound for existentially closed exponent-three groups can be replaced by \(n+q_2(n)+q_3(n+q_2(n))+2=n+O(n^{2/3})\) by packing independent commutators into exterior-power bases. Inserted into the source's bounded-amalgamation argument, this lowers the resulting witness bound from \(O(m^8)\) to \(O(m^4)\).
