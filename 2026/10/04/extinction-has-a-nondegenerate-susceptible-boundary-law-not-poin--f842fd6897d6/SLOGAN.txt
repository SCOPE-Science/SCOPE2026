With \(\varepsilon\sigma_1>0\), the printed stochastic SIRS model cannot converge to \(E_0=(\Lambda/\mu,0,0)\): the \(\log S\) equation would force \(\log S(t)/t\to-(\varepsilon\sigma_1)^2/2\), contradicting convergence to a positive constant. On the disease-free face the exact susceptible stationary law is inverse-gamma; at the paper's extinction parameters it has mean \(5\) and variance \(2025/79\).
