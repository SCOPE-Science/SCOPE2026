# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. For an extraspecial \(p\)-group, the quotient by the center is a nondegenerate symplectic \(\mathbf F_p\)-space and commutation is exactly orthogonality. In rank one, a nonzero vector has orthogonal complement equal to its own one-dimensional span, giving precisely \(p+1\) disjoint noncentral cliques of size \(p(p-1)\). In rank at least two, the five displayed quotient vectors have zero pairings exactly on the five cyclically consecutive pairs and nonzero pairings on all five chords; this remains valid in characteristic \(2\). Representatives therefore induce \(C_5\), so the Strong Perfect Graph Theorem rules out perfectness. The packaged checker independently verifies the rank-one decomposition and the witness over four primes.

## Originality

PASS. The 2021 survey poses the broad perfect-commuting-graph classification problem. The 2022 extraspecial recognition paper gives the symplectic model and the order-\(p^3\) clique structure but not this all-rank perfectness criterion. Britnell--Gill classify quasisimple groups and give general component restrictions, which do not classify component-free extraspecial \(p\)-groups. The later forbidden-subgraph paper handles cograph, chordal, split, and threshold behavior and records CA-groups as perfect, covering the rank-one positive behavior but not the uniform higher-rank obstruction. Targeted searches found no equivalent extraspecial rank boundary or induced-\(C_5\) theorem. A finite-geometry reformulation remains the main residual bibliographic risk.

## Value

PASS. The result completely resolves a standard infinite family inside a stated commuting-graph classification problem. It applies to every prime and both extraspecial types, and the higher-rank failure is explained by a fixed five-vector configuration in two symplectic hyperbolic planes rather than by sporadic computation.

Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
