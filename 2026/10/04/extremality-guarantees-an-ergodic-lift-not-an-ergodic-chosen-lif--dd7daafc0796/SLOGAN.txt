For Suda's trajectory-space invariant measures, extremality guarantees the existence of an ergodic lift, but it does not make an arbitrarily chosen lift ergodic. A two-direction circle flow gives the sharp counterexample, while the corrected lift theorem forces the value \(\int f\,d\mu\) to be realizable by a trajectory from \(\mu\)-almost every initial point for every extreme \(\mu\).
