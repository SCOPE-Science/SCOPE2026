In the equal-weight Gaussian \(\alpha\)-Wiener specialization, the largest deviation from the ensemble average is asymptotic to \(\sigma\sqrt{2v_\alpha(t)\log n}\) and has a centered Gumbel profile. This creates a sharp joint particle-number/terminal-time pinning transition with critical windows \((\log n)^{-1/(2\alpha)}\), \((\log n\log\log n)^{-1}\), and \((\log n)^{-1}\) across the three \(\alpha\)-regimes.
