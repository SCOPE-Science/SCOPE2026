With exactly \(f\le5\) non-empty cube faces, empty-face boundary matching imposes exact positional crossing capacities \((n-2)^2\), \(2(n-1)(n-2)\), \(3(n-1)^2\), \(2(n-1)(2n-1)\), and \(5n^2-4n\). Together with the known six-face bound, these give crossing-number lower bounds on the spherical face invariant \(sf_n(K)\).\n
