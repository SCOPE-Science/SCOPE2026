For a regular simplex, the full vector of distances to the facets is an exact Euclidean coordinate system: squared point-to-point distance equals \(n/(n+1)\) times the squared distance between the corresponding facet-distance vectors. Viviani's constant-sum theorem is therefore the first-moment part of an exact metric reconstruction, with facet-distance variance equal to \((n+1)/n\) times squared displacement from the center.
