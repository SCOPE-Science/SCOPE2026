# Review

## Correctness

PASS. On a chain, semicomplementation forces \(a\to0=0\) for every positive \(a\), while conditional identity gives \(0\to0=1\); hence double-negation inflation is automatic and \(\mathsf T=\mathsf F\).

For each antecedent, P2, consequent monotonicity, and P5 make the consequent row a closure map whose non-top fixed points lie strictly below the antecedent. P5 also forces these fixed-point sets to be nested as the antecedent increases. Every interior chain element therefore has a unique first row in which it becomes fixed, and any admissible assignment of such first rows reconstructs a preconditional satisfying all axioms. The independent choices multiply to \((n-2)!\).

The bundled brute-force replay through six elements agrees exactly with the structural classification.

## Originality

PASS. Chen (2026) defines the \(\mathsf T\)- and \(\mathsf F\)-algebra classes and proves finite-model results, but does not classify their structures on finite chains. Holliday's preconditional paper gives the axioms and general representation theory but no finite-chain census.

Targeted database and web searches for preconditionals on finite chains, factorial counts, nested fixed points, conditional identity, and semicomplementation did not locate an equivalent theorem.

## Value

PASS. Finite chains are the basic algebraic models of intuitionistic propositional logic, and Chen's new logic is explicitly designed to interpolate between Heyting implication and broader conditional operations. The theorem shows exactly how much additional conditional structure survives on the simplest finite lattice reducts: factorially many nonisomorphic expansions with the same negation, but only one Heyting implication. It also identifies the sharp first non-Heyting chain size \(4\).

## Closest literature and limitations

Chen (2026), Definition 3.1, Lemma 3.2, Definition 3.3, Remark 3.4, and Examples 3.6--3.7 are the closest recent source. Holliday (2024/2025) supplies the general preconditional axioms and the Heyting characterization.

The result does not enumerate bare \(\mathsf K\)-preconditionals or expansions of arbitrary finite distributive lattices.
