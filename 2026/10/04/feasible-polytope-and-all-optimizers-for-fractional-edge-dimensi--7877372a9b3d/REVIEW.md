# Review of Feasible polytope and all optimizers for fractional edge dimension of complete multipartite graphs

## Correctness
PASS. With at least three parts, every vertex pair is an exact edge resolving neighborhood and every edge resolving neighborhood has at least two vertices, giving necessity and sufficiency of the all-pairs system. In the bipartite case, same-part pairs generate the exact size-two constraints and every remaining resolving neighborhood contains one. The optimizer faces follow from the resulting fractional clique-cover programs. Direct distance reconstruction and original LP solving agree through order ten.

## Originality
PASS. Yi's primary paper gives the scalar complete-multipartite fractional edge dimension but not an if-and-only-if feasible-system description or all optimizer faces. The later combinatorial paper defines minimal edge resolving functions but does not state an arbitrary complete-multipartite optimizer classification. Targeted semantic and exact-phrase searches found no such theorem. The scalar formula is explicitly excluded from the novelty basis.

## Value
PASS. Fractional edge dimension is an optimization invariant, so the full feasible and optimal solution structure is materially stronger than a scalar optimum. The theorem collapses a large edge-pair LP to canonical pair constraints and exactly resolves uniqueness versus a continuum of optima on a standard graph family.
