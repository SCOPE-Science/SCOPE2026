## Correctness
PASS. The Hamiltonian conjugation \(SXH(B,b)XS=H(-B,b)\) is exact. Gibbs functional calculus preserves that conjugacy, and local-unitary invariance plus subsystem-swap covariance of one-sided Hilbert--Schmidt GQD gives the claimed identities. The packaged numerical replay checks the same identities independently on finite random samples and agrees to machine precision.

## Originality
PASS. The anchor paper explicitly reports that its fixed-side GQD curve is asymmetric under magnetic-field reversal, but it does not state the exact opposite-side reflection identity or the resulting evenness of the unordered pair. Searches over the claim, its swap formulation, directional-discord terminology, and implication-equivalent wording did not reveal a covering statement. The general invariance properties of GQD are prior knowledge and are not claimed as new.

## Value
PASS. The identity explains an otherwise qualitative numerical observation, predicts the unplotted opposite-side discord curve without recomputation, and separates a side-label effect from swap-invariant correlation physics. The statement holds for the full real parameter family at every \(T>0\), not only for the plotted parameters.
