# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Fink's positive convolution expansion restricts exactly to the divisor-closed monoid generated by a finite prime set \(Q\): if a product lies in that monoid, every factor does too. Hence, whenever \(Z_Q(s)<2\), Tonelli positivity gives \(A_Q(s)=Z_Q(s)/(2-Z_Q(s))\). If \(Z_Q(1)>2\), continuity yields a unique \(\alpha_Q>1\) with \(Z_Q(\alpha_Q)=2\). Assuming every supported integer is non-ample gives \(A_Q(s)\le Z_Q(s-1)\) for \(s>1\); as \(s\downarrow\alpha_Q\), the exact left side diverges while the finite-prime right side stays finite, a contradiction. Fink's supermultiplicative inequality makes all powers of one witness ample, and divergence of the Euler product over primes beyond any fixed cutoff supplies an admissible finite support there. A fresh independent check reproduced the example support product \(1001/384>2\) and \(\alpha_Q\approx1.1843600447\).

## originality

PASS

Fink's 2020 primary paper explicitly states as Conjecture 1 that ample numbers should avoid the first \(k\) primes for every \(k\), and proves that one witness gives infinitely many by product closure. His 2023 paper gives the global Dirichlet series and the positive convolution expansion. Neither inspected source restricts that expansion to an arbitrary finite prime-support monoid or uses the resulting finite-support pole to force an ample coefficient. A current problem database still listed the conjecture as open with zero recorded solutions, and current Resultary search returned only the assigned all-\(k\) theorem plus a separate \(k=3\) partial result. The support-local Dirichlet-series argument is therefore a genuine new mechanism rather than a specialization of the global formula.

## value

PASS

The result proves a published all-\(k\) conjecture and strengthens it to infinitely many ample numbers on every finite support whose Euler product exceeds two. The support-local pole argument is reusable analytic structure and materially extends the theory beyond isolated computational witnesses.
