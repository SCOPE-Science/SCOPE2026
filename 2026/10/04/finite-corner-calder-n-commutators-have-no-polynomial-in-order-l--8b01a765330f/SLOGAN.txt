A fixed finite set of corners cannot produce polynomial growth in the order of a higher Calderón commutator. If a Lipschitz profile has \(N\) affine pieces, then \(\|T_{A,n}\|_{2\to2}\le\pi N\|A'\|_\infty^n\), so both the general linear factor and the refined \(\sqrt n\) factor are unnecessary on this polygonal subclass.
