# Review

## Correctness

PASS. The source's operators are right composition by \(\leq\) and by \(\leq^{-1}\). Their two possible composites therefore differ exactly by the relations “has a common lower bound” and “has a common upper bound.” Universal commutation is equivalent to equality of those two relations.

When they are equal, the common-bound relation is an equivalence relation: transitivity follows by combining a common lower bound for the first pair with a common upper bound for the second pair and applying the assumed upper/lower equivalence twice. Its classes are exactly comparability components. In a finite quotient poset, pairwise common upper and lower bounds iterate to a greatest and a least element of each component. The converse is immediate from those endpoints. The least simultaneous fixed-point property and preservation of both p-conditions follow by direct relation composition.
