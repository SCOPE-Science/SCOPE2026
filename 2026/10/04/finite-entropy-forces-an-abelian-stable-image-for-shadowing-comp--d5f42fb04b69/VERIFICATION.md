# Verification

The verified claim is that a shadowing endomorphism \(\alpha\) of a compact connected group has infinite topological entropy whenever its stable image \(K\) is nonabelian.

The proof chain checked against the primary source is: stable-image reduction; nontrivial semisimple commutator part for nonabelian \(K\); absence of periodic simple-factor orbits under shadowing; decomposition of each resulting chain into a one-sided or two-sided full shift on a nontrivial simple compact Lie group; infinite entropy of that shift by an explicit separated-set construction; monotonicity of topological entropy through the quotient and invariant subsystem chain.

No computational certificate is needed. The infinite-entropy step is proved for every integer \(m\ge2\), not inferred from a finite sample. The proof does not establish any converse and does not determine entropy on the abelian stable part.
