For every even horizon \(N\ge2\), the exact lemniscate-acceleration guarantee \(L^2R^2/\Omega_N^2\) is strictly smaller than the standard chained OGM--OGM-G guarantee \(64L^2R^2/(N+2)^4\). The advantage therefore has no finite even-horizon crossover, while its guarantee ratio tends to \(\varpi^4/64\approx0.738565\).
