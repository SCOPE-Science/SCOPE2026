For the irrational-rotation Daugavet renorming of \(L_1(\mathbb T)\), every nonempty finite orbit truncation contains an isometric \(\ell_1^2\), so it is neither wMLUR, strictly convex nor Gâteaux smooth. Yet finite truncations converge uniformly to the full smooth, wMLUR norm on the \(L_1\)-unit ball at the explicit tail rate \(\sum_{k\notin F}\sqrt{a_k}\).
