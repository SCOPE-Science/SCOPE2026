For Goldbring--Hart--Kruckman's almost-sure theory of finite metric spaces, every finite-parameter \(n\)-type space splits into finitely many clopen cubes: a pattern with \(k\) genuinely new equality classes contributes \([1/2,1]^{mk+\binom{k}{2}}\). The component count has exponential generating function \(\exp(mz+e^z-1)\), and \(\dim S_n(A)=mn+\binom n2\).
