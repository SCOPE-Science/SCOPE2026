In the exact no-shift Gaussian mean specialization of SAGE, estimating the step-size from only \(r\) summary coordinates creates a computable finite-sample risk penalty. The adaptive update beats the source-only mean exactly when \(n_t/n_s>\sqrt{K_{r,n_s}/r}\); otherwise it is strictly worse, and the large-source threshold is asymptotic to \(r^{-1/2}\).
