# Review

## Correctness

PASS. A topology on a finite carrier has finitely many opens, so arbitrary intersections reduce to finite intersections; hence it is Alexandrov. Its opens are therefore exactly the up-sets of the specialization preorder. Because all original opens are intuitionistic up-sets, the specialization preorder extends the intuitionistic preorder.

In a finite preorder, every point reaches a maximal equivalence class. The union of maximal classes is open and dense, while every dense open must contain every maximal class, making this union the unique least dense open. Sedlár's dense-open support definition then reduces coherent justification exactly to containment of this core.

On a fixed chain, all intuitionistic up-sets are nested tails. Any selection of the \(n-1\) intermediate tails, together with the mandatory empty and whole sets, is a topology. This proves the exact count and the terminal-block multiplicities.

## Originality

PASS. The recent source explicitly distinguishes arbitrary up-space topologies from Alexandrov topologies and defines coherent justification by dense-open support, but it does not state the finite collapse or the maximal-specialization-core characterization. The earlier iS4 paper likewise stresses that general up-spaces strictly extend bi-relational frames, while separately obtaining finite countermodels; it does not identify every finite up-space with its own specialization frame.

Targeted searches for finite topological evidence models, specialization preorders, maximal classes, least dense opens, and chain counts did not locate the same theorem. Standard topology contains the fact that finite spaces are Alexandrov, but the accepted claim combines that fact with the specific TEL dense-support semantics and an exact chain classification.

## Value

PASS. The paper's central semantic distinction between genuinely topological up-spaces and relational specialization models disappears completely on finite carriers. The theorem identifies exactly what survives: one preorder and a canonical least dense open controlling coherent justification. On chains it gives a complete finite census and quantifies the many-to-one collapse from \(2^{n-1}\) topologies to only \(n\) coherent-justification cores. This is a natural structural boundary for finite model search rather than an isolated textbook observation.

## Closest literature and limitations

Sedlár (2026) supplies the TEL semantics and dense-open characterization. De Groot and Shillito (2025) supply the broader iS4 up-space framework and explicitly note that general up-spaces need not be Alexandrov.

The result does not classify full modal theories modulo equivalence, and the exact chain count does not extend unchanged to arbitrary finite intuitionistic preorders.
