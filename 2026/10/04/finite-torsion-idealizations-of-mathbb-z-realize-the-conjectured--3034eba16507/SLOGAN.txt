For every nonzero finite abelian group \(M\) of exponent \(n>1\), the total graph of \(\mathbb Z\ltimes M\) has domination number equal to the smallest prime divisor of \(n\). For mixed-prime exponent this gives a non-Artinian, non-ideal-zero-divisor family attaining the finite-index maximal-annihilator bound conjectured by Chelvam and Asir.
