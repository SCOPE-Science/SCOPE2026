# Review

## Correctness
PASS. Belletti--Detcherry Corollary 6.2(2) gives, for every \(k\) and \(n\), an infinite family of connected compact hyperbolic \(3\)-manifolds all \((Y_k,T_n)\)-equivalent to a base manifold \(M\) that embeds in a rational homology sphere, with no distinct member embedding in another. Setting \(k=d+1\), the family is \(Y_{d+1}\)-equivalent. Massuyeau Proposition 3.32 then gives equality under every Torelli-twist finite-type invariant of degree at most \(d\). The quantifiers and boundary of the claim match these two source statements.

## Originality
PASS. The recent paper states the embedding antichain but does not state its complete degree-bounded finite-type profile. The older finite-type theorem predates the new antichain construction. Targeted searches for equivalent formulations, broader coverage, and the exact cross-source implication did not locate a covering statement.

## Value
PASS. The synthesis shows that every prescribed finite stage of the finite-type hierarchy is simultaneously blind to an infinite hyperbolic antichain for embeddability. This is stronger than exhibiting one pair missed by one invariant and gives a clean structural limitation on finite-degree data.

The scope is intentionally limited to the Torelli-twist finite-type theory and a fixed degree cutoff. It does not claim agreement of all unbounded-degree finite-type invariants or algorithmic undecidability of embeddability.
