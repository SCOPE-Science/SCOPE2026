**PASS.** The source vector field and the critical value \(r=1\) were checked against the full text of Zhang–Deng and the later AIMS presentation of the same model. ## Originality
**PASS.** Zhang–Deng already prove critical global stability and provide the first-transition cubic normal form; those facts are explicitly excluded from the new contribution. Their full first-transition section and discussion were inspected and stop at the cubic term for this transition. Shen's original model paper and Mao et al.'s later boundedness/dynamics paper do not state the quintic physical-amplitude coefficient or the induced logarithmic correction. Database searches for the five-dimensional Lorenz critical transition, quintic center-manifold coefficient, \(t^{-1/2}\) relaxation, and logarithmic correction returned only analogous results for different dynamical systems. Published-record comparison found no same-model equivalent or stronger statement.

## Value
**PASS.** The threshold \(r=1\) is the model's physically distinguished first Rayleigh transition, and the prior literature specifically interprets added modes through enhanced stability and predictability. The new coefficient is the first term in the critical relaxation that depends on the added-mode damping \(d\); it therefore distinguishes the high-mode feedback at a precision invisible to the known cubic normal form. The exact logarithmic correction is a natural long-time invariant of the critical center dynamics rather than an arbitrary parameter slice or a numerical recomputation.
