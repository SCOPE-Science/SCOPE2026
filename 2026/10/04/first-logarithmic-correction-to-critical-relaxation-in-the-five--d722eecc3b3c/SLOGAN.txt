At \(r=1\), the five-dimensional Lorenz model has the center reduction \(\dot x=-\sigma x^3/[b(\sigma+1)]+q x^5+O(x^7)\), so critical relaxation carries an explicit logarithmic correction in \(x^{-2}\). For \(\sigma=10\), \(b=8/3\), \(d=19/3\), \(x(t)^{-2}=15t/22+(9393/36784)\log t+C+o(1)\); the added-mode damping \(d\) first appears at this subleading order.
