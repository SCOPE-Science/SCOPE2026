The released PrimePowers search is an existence-oriented depth-first search, not a minimum-term solver: its first minimum-rank error in the range \(n\ge24\) occurs at \(50\), where it returns \(32+9+9\) instead of the two-term optimum \(25+25\). The paper's five-term example at \(512550\) also has a four-term prime-power decomposition.
