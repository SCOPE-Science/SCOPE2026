Along the canonical endpoint witnesses for five familiar continuous families with zero standardized anti-concentration, the collapse rates are sharply different: logarithmic for Gamma and \(\operatorname{Beta}(1,q)\), linear at the Pareto variance boundary, essential-exponential for Weibull, and Gaussian-in-log-scale for log-normal. The exact Gamma, Beta and Weibull rates also show a factor-two loss in the simplifying bounds used to prove mere convergence to zero.
