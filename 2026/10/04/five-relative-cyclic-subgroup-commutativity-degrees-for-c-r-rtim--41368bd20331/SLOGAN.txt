For the faithful Frobenius group \(G=C_r\rtimes C_{pq}\) with distinct primes \(p,q,r\) and \(pq\mid r-1\), the relative cyclic subgroup commutativity function has exactly five values. The six nontrivial subgroup types highlighted in the literature collapse pairwise at \(C_p,C_q\) and at \(C_r\rtimes C_p,C_r\rtimes C_q\), giving \(|\operatorname{Im} f_1|=5\).
