For fixed dimension \(n\), the dual degrees of two-factor Segre varieties are \(\binom{n-a+1}{a}\): their total is Fibonacci, but their largest term occurs asymptotically at relative dual defect \(1/\sqrt5\), not at the balanced hypersurface. The exact maximizing split is controlled by the smaller root of \(5a^2-(5n+2)a+n^2-1\).
