For every nontrivial smooth centrally symmetric support perturbation of the unit disk that preserves Euclidean perimeter, the second variation of Gaussian torsional rigidity is strictly negative. The exact Fourier-mode Hessian is governed by positive Kummer Dirichlet-to-Neumann eigenvalues \(\lambda_k\), giving a local second-order affirmative result toward the fixed-perimeter question left open by arXiv:2608.29941v1.
