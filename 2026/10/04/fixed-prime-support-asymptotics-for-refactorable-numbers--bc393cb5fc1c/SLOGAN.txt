For any fixed nonempty prime set \(S\) of size \(m\), refactorable integers with exact support \(S\) have counting function asymptotic to \((\log\log x)^{m^2}/((m!)^m\prod_{p\in S}(\log p)^m)\). The condition \(\tau(n)\mid n\) becomes an exact finite set of column-sum inequalities on the \(S\)-smooth factorizations of the shifted prime exponents.
