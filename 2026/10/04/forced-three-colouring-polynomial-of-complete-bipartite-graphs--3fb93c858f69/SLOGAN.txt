For every complete bipartite graph \(K_{m,n}\), forcing with three colours has a rigid two-side structure: apart from total two-monochromatic colourings, one entire part must already use exactly two colours and the other part can only carry the third. This gives the exact closed form \(FC_3(K_{m,n};p)=3(2^m-2)p^m(1-2p)^n+3(2^n-2)p^n(1-2p)^m+6p^{m+n}\).
