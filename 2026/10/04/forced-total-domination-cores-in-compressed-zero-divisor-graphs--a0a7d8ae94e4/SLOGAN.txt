For a nonlocal finite commutative principal ideal ring with \(r\) chain-ring factors, the compressed zero-divisor graph has a forced \(r\)-vertex total-domination core, one vertex per factor. Paired domination equals \(r\) for even \(r\) and \(r+1\) for odd \(r\), with every odd-rank minimum pair-dominating set obtained by adding one arbitrary noncore vertex.
