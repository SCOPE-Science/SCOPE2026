## Correctness
PASS. The four cases reduce to exact exponent-one prime witnesses in the tabulated factorizations. For each witness, the congruence \(q_i\equiv-1\pmod i\) makes \(\sigma(q_i)=q_i+1\) divisible by \(i\), and multiplicativity then gives \(i\mid\sigma(a_i)\). The minimality defining \(a_i\) then forces \(g_i=a_i\).
