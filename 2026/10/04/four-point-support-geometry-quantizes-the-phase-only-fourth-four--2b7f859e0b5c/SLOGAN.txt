For a four-point support in \(\mathbb Z_p\) with \(p\ge11\), the least normalized fourth Fourier moment over equal-magnitude coefficients is quantized by the support's repeated-difference profile: \(19\), \(20\), \(24\), or \(28\). The optimum \(19\), equivalently phase-only merit factor \(16/3\), occurs exactly for affine four-term arithmetic progressions.
