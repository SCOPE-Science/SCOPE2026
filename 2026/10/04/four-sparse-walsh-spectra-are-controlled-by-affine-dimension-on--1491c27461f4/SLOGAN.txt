For every \(d\ge3\), a four-sparse function on \((\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^d\) has Fourier-support size in exactly \(\{2,4,5,6,7,8\}2^{d-3}\). The two affine support types are completely separated: affine planes give local sizes \(1,2,3,4\), while affine-independent supports give local sizes \(5,6,7,8\) with an exact cube-zero-pattern classification.
