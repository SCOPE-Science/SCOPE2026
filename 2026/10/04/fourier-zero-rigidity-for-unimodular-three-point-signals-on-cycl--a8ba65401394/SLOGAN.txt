For an equal-magnitude signal supported on three points of \(\mathbb Z_N\), every nonempty Fourier zero set has exactly \(g\) zeros, except for one explicit modulo-\(3\) support geometry where it has exactly \(2g\), with \(g\) the common support-difference gcd. In particular, three equal-magnitude coefficients on \(\mathbb Z_p\) with prime \(p>3\) can cancel at only one character, and that bound is sharp.
