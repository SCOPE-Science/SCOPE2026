For the Barmak--Minian finite-space realization attached to any finite nontrivial group and \(r\ge1\) distinct nonidentity generators, the point-free orbit collapses to an \((r+2)\)-point chain while the geometric order-complex orbit remains \(\bigvee^r S^1\). Thus a free action on a finite \(T_0\)-space need not make finite-space quotient and geometric order-complex quotient agree even up to weak homotopy.
