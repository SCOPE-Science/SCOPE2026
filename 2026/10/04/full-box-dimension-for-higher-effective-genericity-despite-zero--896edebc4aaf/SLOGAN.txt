For every \(n\ge2\), the \(\Pi^0_n\)-generic and weakly \(\Pi^0_n\)-generic reals are dense enough to require exactly \(2^k\) sets at dyadic scale \(2^{-k}\). They therefore have full box-counting dimension \(1\) even though their Hausdorff dimension is \(0\), giving a quantitative answer to Veltri's Question 3.28.
