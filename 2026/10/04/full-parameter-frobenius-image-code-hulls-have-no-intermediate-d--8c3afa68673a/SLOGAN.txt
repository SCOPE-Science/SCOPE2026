For the full projective parameter space of the Frobenius image-code family \(C_{\lambda,\mu}=\operatorname{im}(\lambda X+\mu X^{q^k})\), every hull has \(\mathbb F_q\)-dimension either \(0\) or \(d=\gcd(k,m)\). The residual interval \(0\le\delta\le d\) therefore collapses to an LCD-versus-full-\(d\)-hull dichotomy.
