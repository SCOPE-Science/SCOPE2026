On an \(n\)-world intuitionistic chain, FIK forward confluence says exactly that nonempty successor rows form a suffix and their maxima increase weakly. This gives the exact Gaussian-binomial census \(F_n=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\begin{bmatrix}n+k-1\\k\end{bmatrix}_2\) and the asymptotic \(F_n\sim3.462746619455064\ldots\,2^{n(n-1)}\).
