For a spider, every general position set of size at least three chooses at most one noncentral vertex from each arm, giving an exact elementary-symmetric polynomial formula. The unique smallest nonunimodal spider has order \(22\), namely \(S(1,1,1,1,2,15)\), and \(S(1,1,1,1,2,m)\) is nonunimodal exactly for \(m\ge15\).
