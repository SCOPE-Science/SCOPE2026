For every primitive cyclic binary Hamming code, the symbol-pair generation profile is completely determined by one gcd: it is zero through radius \(4\), has dimension \(n-\deg\gcd(1+x+x^\ell,x^n-1)\) at radius \(5\), and is already the full code by radius \(6\). Thus the disconnection threshold is always \(4\) or \(5\), with both behaviors occurring among degree-six primitive generators.
