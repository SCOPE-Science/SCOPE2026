For Finch's canonically bisected oblique circular cone, each of the three surface-area ratios treated in the paper is strictly increasing with cone height at fixed horizontal offset. This proves that the global infima really lie on the degenerate boundary and yields exact unique minimizers: \(4x_L/\pi-1\), \(2x_T/\pi\), and \(2x_L/\pi\), where \(2x_L=\pi\cos x_L\) and \(2x_T+\pi=2\pi\cos x_T\).
