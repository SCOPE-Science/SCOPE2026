# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

PASS. Ultrahomogeneity of the Fraisse limit reduces injective ordered-tuple orbits to label-preserving isomorphism types of their finitely generated meet-substructures, and the generated substructure is exactly meet-closure. A finite meet-tree has a rooted-tree cover graph. Coloring tuple entries black and other generated points white produces a Greg tree because any white generated point must be the meet of black descendants from at least two distinct child subtrees. Conversely, in any rooted Greg tree every child subtree of a white vertex contains a black descendant (white leaves are forbidden), so the white vertex is the meet of black descendants chosen in two children; hence the whole meet-tree is generated by its black vertices. This gives the all-n bijection. The root species decomposition yields G=x exp(G)+(exp(G)-1-G), equivalently (1+x)exp(G)=1+2G and the stated Lambert-W branch. Equality partitions of coordinates give b_n=sum S(n,k)a_k and EGF G(e^x-1). Finite enumeration/coefficient checks are corroborative only.
