Every compact stationary state of Sprott C satisfies the heightwise law \(\mathbb E[x^2\mid z]=1\) and the exact partition \(\mathbb E[y^2]+\mathbb E[\dot y^{\,2}]=1\). Unless the measure is a mixture of the two equilibria, recurrence must visit both \(|x|<1\) and \(|x|>1\), so every nonconstant periodic orbit crosses \(|x|=1\) at least twice.
