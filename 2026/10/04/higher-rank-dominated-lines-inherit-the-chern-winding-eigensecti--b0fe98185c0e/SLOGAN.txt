For analytic quasi-periodic cocycles in arbitrary fiber rank, an extremal one-dimensional dominated block satisfies the same exact Chern–winding test as in rank two: it carries a measurable eigensection exactly when \(c_1(L)=0\) and \(k(L)=0\). The rank increase is absorbed by a higher-dimensional projective graph transform; the scalar cohomology and line-bundle normalization are unchanged.
