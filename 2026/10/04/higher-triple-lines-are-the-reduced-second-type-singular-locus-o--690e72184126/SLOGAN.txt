For the Fermat cubic fourfold \(X\), Zhang's higher triple lines are exactly \(\operatorname{Sing}(F_2(X)_{\mathrm{red}})\) set-theoretically. The locus consists of \(180\) smooth Fermat elliptic curves meeting only at \(405\) deep points, with \(9\) deep points on each curve and \(4\) curves through each deep point.
