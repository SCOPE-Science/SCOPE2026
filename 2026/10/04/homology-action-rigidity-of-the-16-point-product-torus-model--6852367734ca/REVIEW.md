# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The claim reduces to a finite exact classification. The included verifier reconstructs the product poset, validates the two cycles and two dual cocycles, exhaustively enumerates all order-preserving self-maps, recomputes every induced integral first-homology matrix, and checks the full multiplicity table. It finds 8,042,896 self-maps, 25 distinct matrices, and exactly 32 rank-two maps; those 32 maps are exactly the bijections.

## Originality
PASS with recorded residual risk. Cianci and Ottina establish that the product model is a 16-point minimal finite torus model and classify the two models of that cardinality, but the inspected primary text does not state this self-map census or the image on first homology. Exact and alias searches for the model, endomorphism monoid, count, matrix image, signed permutations, and Dehn-twist obstruction found no covering statement. The relevant Barmak monograph pages were not fully accessible, so obscure prior coverage cannot be excluded.

## Value
PASS. A minimal model is often chosen precisely to make a space computationally small; this result shows a sharp cost of that compression for dynamics. The model cannot directly realize even the elementary Dehn-twist shear on first homology, and the complete census identifies exactly what survives at minimal cardinality.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest prior work is Barmak's 2011 monograph on algebraic topology of finite spaces and Cianci-Ottina's 2015 preprint `arXiv:1512.06088v1`, which proves minimality and classifies the two 16-point torus models. The present result is only for the product model and does not obstruct realization after subdivision, refinement, or enlargement.
