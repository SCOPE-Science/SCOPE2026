The five-point minimal finite model of \(S^1\vee S^1\) has \(197\) continuous self-maps but only \(31\) induced endomorphisms of \(H_1\cong\mathbb Z^2\): zero, \(18\) rank-one outer products, and the \(12\) integral isometries of \(q(s,t)=s^2+st+t^2\). An induced \(H_1\)-isomorphism occurs exactly for a homeomorphism.
