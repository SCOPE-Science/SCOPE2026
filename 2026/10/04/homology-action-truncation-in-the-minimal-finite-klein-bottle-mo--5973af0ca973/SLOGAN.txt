The two 16-point minimal finite Klein-bottle models realize exactly ten endomorphisms of \(H_1(K;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/2\): precisely the classical parity-allowed types with free multiplier \(k\in\{-1,0,1\}\). The ordinary Klein bottle admits the same parity pattern for arbitrary integer \(k\), so cardinality minimality truncates an infinite homological self-map spectrum to three free multipliers.
