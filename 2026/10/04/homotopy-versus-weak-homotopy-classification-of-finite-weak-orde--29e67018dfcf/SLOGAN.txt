For finite weak orders, ordinary homotopy remembers the full ordered vector of level sizes, while weak homotopy remembers only the height and \(\prod_i(r_i-1)\), unless a singleton level makes the whole space contractible. Thus \(W(2,4,2)\) and \(W(2,2,4)\) are weakly equivalent eight-point cores with order complexes \(\simeq\bigvee^3S^2\), but they are not homotopy equivalent as finite spaces.
