# Review

Claim reviewed: Let \(M=G(8,3)\) be the Möbius–Kantor graph. Its independence complex satisfies \[\operatorname{Ind}(M)\simeq \bigvee^{4}S^{3}\vee S^{4}.\]

## Correctness
PASS. The finite complex is reconstructed exactly. The complete acyclic matching has one critical \(0\)-cell, four critical \(3\)-cells, and one critical \(4\)-cell. The four gradient-path counts from the critical \(4\)-cell are \(2,0,2,2\). Exact boundary elimination over both \(\mathbb F_2\) and \(\mathbb F_3\) gives \(\widetilde\beta_3=4\) and \(\widetilde\beta_4=1\), forcing the integral Morse differential to vanish. The final Hurewicz step then proves the wedge decomposition.
