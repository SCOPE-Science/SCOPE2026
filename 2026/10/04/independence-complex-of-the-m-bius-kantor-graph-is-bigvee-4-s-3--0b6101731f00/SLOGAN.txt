Let \(M=G(8,3)\) be the Möbius–Kantor graph. Its independence complex satisfies \[\operatorname{Ind}(M)\simeq \bigvee^{4}S^{3}\vee S^{4}.\] A verified acyclic matching reduces the complex to four \(3\)-cells and one \(4\)-cell, and modular boundary checks force the remaining attaching map to be null-homotopic.
