# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The field model \(\mathbb F_5(\alpha)\) with \(\alpha^2=2\), the primitive element \(g=1+2\alpha\), the subgroup \(H=\langle g^4\rangle\), and all six characters are explicitly reconstructed. Every compressed-matrix entry is evaluated in \(\mathbb Z[\zeta_{30}]\), and every nonempty square minor is computed exactly modulo \(\Phi_{30}\). The exhaustive census checks \(251\) minors for the trivial character and \(69\) for each nontrivial character. The positive cases have no zero minors; explicit zero minors witness every negative case.

## Originality
PASS relative to the inspected literature and database searches. The closest paper completes the index-\(3\) nontrivial-character case and explicitly summarizes known non-prime cases at indices \(2\) and \(3\). Neither that full text nor the earlier foundational paper gives the \(\mathbb F_{25}\), index-\(4\) classification. Targeted exact-parameter, equivalent-formulation, broader-coverage, and implication searches found no covering result.

## Value
PASS. Index \(4\) is the next symmetry index after the literature's completed index-\(3\) analysis, and the \(\mathbb F_{25}\) case exhibits a nontrivial order-sensitive split: NVM holds at character orders \(1\) and \(6\) but fails at orders \(2\) and \(3\). This supplies a concrete test case for any future general index-\(4\) criterion and shows that invertibility alone is far weaker than NVM even within one fixed subgroup.

The present result is finite and specific to \(\mathbb F_{25}\); a general index-\(4\) characterization remains outside its scope.
