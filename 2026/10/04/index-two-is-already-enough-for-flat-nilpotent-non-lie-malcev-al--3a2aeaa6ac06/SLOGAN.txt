A six-dimensional flat non-Lie Malcev example from arXiv:2609.13950v1 is in fact nilpotent of class \(3\), has a two-dimensional totally isotropic center, and carries a metric of index \(2\). Thus the theorem that flat Lorentzian nilpotent Malcev algebras are Lie is sharp in metric index: failure occurs already at index \(2\).
