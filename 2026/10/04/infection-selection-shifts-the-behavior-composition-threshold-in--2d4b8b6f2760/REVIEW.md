# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Direct quotient-rule differentiation of the published \(S_p\) and \(S_n\) equations yields the exact composition law and log-odds identity. Substitution of the source's influence functions reduces the sign condition to a strictly increasing quadratic on \(I\geq0\), proving a unique positive composition threshold. Evaluating that quadratic at \(I_{\mathrm{cross}}\) proves the strict ordering \(I_{\mathrm{comp}}(S)<I_{\mathrm{cross}}\). The default-parameter witness is checked with exact rational arithmetic.

## Originality
PASS. The motivating paper defines \(I_{\mathrm{cross}}\) from the sign of \(\omega_p-\omega_n\), while its 2020 predecessor qualitatively notes preferential infection of less-prophylactic classes. Neither inspected source states the exact two-state log-odds decomposition, the state-dependent threshold \(I_{\mathrm{comp}}(S)\), or the interval where opinion transfer and susceptible-composition change point in opposite directions. Targeted published-finding corpus and web searches returned related epidemic or switching results but not this formula or implication. Residual novelty risk remains because search is not exhaustive.

## Value
PASS. The distinction separates two mechanisms that the model couples: behavioral conversion and selective depletion by infection. That separation matters when interpreting a measured change in the behavioral composition of the susceptible pool, because such a change need not have the same sign as the explicit opinion-transfer flux. The exact threshold gives a reusable diagnostic for simulations and analytical reductions of this model rather than a parameter-only verbal interpretation.

## Closest literature and limitations
The 2020 predecessor already observes that infection preferentially removes less-prophylactic susceptible classes, so this finding does not claim discovery of that qualitative mechanism. Its added content is the exact two-attitude decomposition and threshold ordering in the 2022 equations. The result does not assert changes to the paper's reported numerical wave counts or public-health conclusions.
