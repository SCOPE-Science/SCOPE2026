In the two-attitude epidemic model, the fixed threshold \(I_{\mathrm{cross}}\) marks only the sign change of the explicit opinion-transfer flux, not the sign change of the prophylactic share among remaining susceptibles. Differential infection creates an exact selection term, giving a lower state-dependent threshold \(I_{\mathrm{comp}}(S)<I_{\mathrm{cross}}\) and allowing the prophylactic share to rise even while opinion flow favors non-prophylaxis.
