In the instantaneous Adam conditioning diagnostic used for dense Hessian blocks, Gaussian initialization gives a finite condition number almost surely but an infinite population mean in every dimension \(d\ge2\). In two dimensions the best attainable conditioning is exactly controlled by row correlation, and a \(45^\circ\)-rotated spectrum \(\{1,\kappa\}\) is maximally resistant: no initialization improves the condition number below \(\kappa\).
