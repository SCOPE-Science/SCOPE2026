The package checks the exact two-dimensional one-sided scaling formula and the reciprocal tail mechanism of the instantaneous Adam condition-number diagnostic.

`verify.py` compares the closed-form row-correlation floor with direct singular-value condition numbers on random SPD matrices, verifies the diagonal and \(45^\circ\)-rotated equality cases, checks the orientation formula, and confirms reciprocal blow-up when one gradient coordinate tends to zero.

Finite numerical checks do not prove non-integrability. The infinite Gaussian expectation is established analytically in `RESULT.md` by a positive-density box argument.
