# Review

## Correctness
**PASS.** For \(R_0>1\), the source equilibrium equation is exactly
\[
C=I^*\left(\beta+\frac{(a/b)\sigma u_0}{1+(a/b)u_2I^*}\right),
\qquad C=(p_0\sigma+\mu)(R_0-1)>0.
\]
Its left-minus-right form is strictly increasing in \(I\), so the positive root is unique. Direct implicit differentiation gives strict signs \(\partial I^*/\partial u_0<0\), \(\partial I^*/\partial u_2>0\), \(\partial I^*/\partial a<0\), and \(\partial I^*/\partial b>0\). The same identity proves \(I^*\to0\) and \(u_0I^*\to Cb/(a\sigma)\) as \(u_0\to\infty\). No finite experiment is used as an infinite proof.

## Originality
**PASS, narrowly construed.** Exact-title, DOI, parameter-alias, threshold-versus-endemic, and four-direction searches in published-finding corpus found no matching finding, and the current ledger lineage contains no prior row for this source or claim. The Fang–Liu–Du paper states the equilibrium equation and unique endemic root but not these comparative statics; its Remark 3.2 instead makes a broad control inference from the absence of information parameters in \(R_0\). Earlier behavioral-vaccination literature already shows that information can affect endemic states, so that broad principle is not claimed as new. The accepted originality is the exact four-parameter theorem and asymptotic for this source equation.

## Value
**PASS.** The result corrects a policy-relevant mathematical interpretation in the motivating paper: invasion-threshold invariance and endemic-burden invariance are different statements. The strict signs identify which information parameters lower or raise the endemic infected coordinate, and the scaling \(I^*\sim Cb/(a\sigma u_0)\) gives a quantitative large-response law. The statement is valuable even without a new global-stability theorem because it changes what can legitimately be inferred from the source's \(R_0\) calculation.

## Closest literature and limitations
Buonomo, d'Onofrio, and Lacitignola (2008) analyze a distinct SIR model with information-dependent vaccination and a Michaelis--Menten response. Kumar, Srivastava, and Gupta (2019) study a different SVIR model with information-induced vaccination and saturated treatment. Buonomo et al. (2022) provide a qualitative precedent that information coverage can reduce an endemic infected level in a meningitis model. Centrone, Perchiazzo, and Salinelli (2026) study lagged incidence-based vaccination and endemic bifurcations. These sources bound the novelty: the broad phenomenon is known, while the exact four signs and fixed-threshold asymptotic for the Fang–Liu–Du SIRM equilibrium were not found. The 2019 and 2022 full bodies were not accessible through the inspected public paths, so that access risk remains explicit.

The accepted claim is limited to equilibrium sensitivity. It does not assert finite-parameter eradication for \(R_0>1\), transient peak monotonicity, stability preservation, or cost-effectiveness.
