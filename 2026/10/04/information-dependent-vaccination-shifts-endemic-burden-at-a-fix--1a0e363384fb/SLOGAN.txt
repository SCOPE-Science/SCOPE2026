In the Fang–Liu–Du SIRM equilibrium with \(R_0>1\), endemic infection \(I^*\) strictly falls with stronger information-dependent vaccination \(u_0\) and faster information growth \(a\), and strictly rises with stronger saturation \(u_2\) and faster information decay \(b\). Indeed \(I^*\to0\) as \(u_0\to\infty\) while \(R_0\) is fixed, so an unchanged invasion threshold does not imply an unchanged endemic burden.
