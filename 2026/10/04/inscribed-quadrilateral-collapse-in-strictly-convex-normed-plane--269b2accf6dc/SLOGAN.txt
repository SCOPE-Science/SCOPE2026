In every real strictly convex normed plane, the four-point inscribed-quadrilateral constant \(J_{\mathrm{in}}\) collapses exactly to the two-point modified von Neumann--Jordan constant: \(J_{\mathrm{in}}(X)=8+8C'_{\mathrm{NJ}}(X)\). Hence \(J_{\mathrm{in}}(\ell_p^2)=8(1+2^{|2/p-1|})\) for all \(1\le p\le\infty\).
