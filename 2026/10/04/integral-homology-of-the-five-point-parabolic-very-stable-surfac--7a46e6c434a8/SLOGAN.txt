For the degree-\(4\) del Pezzo very-stable locus obtained by deleting all sixteen \((-1)\)-curves, the integral homology is exactly \(\mathbf Z,\mathbf Z^{10},\mathbf Z^{25}\) in degrees \(0,1,2\) and vanishes above degree \(2\). In particular \(\chi=16\), and the geometric Hopf map onto the published \(H_2(\pi_1,\mathbf Z)\cong\mathbf Z^{25}\) is an isomorphism.
