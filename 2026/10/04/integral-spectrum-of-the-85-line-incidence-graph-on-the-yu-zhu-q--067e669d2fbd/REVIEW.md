# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The source supplies a complete explicit list of the \(85\) lines on \(X_{-1/8}\). The verifier represents every coefficient exactly in \(\mathbb Q(a,b,i)\) with \(a^4=2\), \(b^4=3\), and \(i^2=-1\), exhausts every unordered line pair by a determinant incidence test, and obtains a \(20\)-regular graph with \(850\) edges. Exact matrix identities prove the displayed spectrum without numerical eigensolvers. Shifting by the adjunction value \(-3\) yields rank \(39\), matching the independently reported source computation.

## Originality
PASS. Yu--Zhu state the complete line list and the rank \(39\) of the line intersection matrix, but not the graph regularity, characteristic polynomial, spectrum, common-neighbor census, or inertia. Full-text checks and targeted searches for equivalent formulations found no source covering the spectral refinement. The rank alone does not imply the new spectrum.

## Value
PASS. The result gives a compact exact combinatorial invariant of the current record 85-line quintic configuration and explains the previously black-box rank computation through the full spectrum. It also exposes a large \(46\)-dimensional relation space and the complete nonzero signature of the line lattice presentation, data directly relevant to the paper's Néron--Severi calculations.

Closest literature/limitations: Yu--Zhu, arXiv:2609.26862v1, owns the surface, completeness and equations of the 85 lines, and the rank-39 computation. This result is a spectral refinement of that exact configuration, not a new construction of a quintic surface or a new proof that it has 85 lines. Unpublished prior computations cannot be excluded.
