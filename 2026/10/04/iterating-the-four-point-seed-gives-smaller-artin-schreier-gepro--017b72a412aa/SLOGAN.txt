Starting from four general points and iterating the new Artin--Schreier lift gives geproci sets of type \((2,2,p,p^2,\ldots,p^{n-3})\) for \(p\ge3\), and \((2,2,4,8,\ldots,2^{n-2})\) for \(p=2\). These types are strictly smaller term by term than the dimension-uniform families in Propositions 4.5 and 4.6, giving an affirmative answer to the 'in particular' clause of the paper's Question 5.2.
