Up to mirror, the number of two-bridge links of even Schubert numerator \(p\) admitting a non-trivial L-space surgery is an explicit divisor-orbit function of \(p-1\) and \(p+1\). Through numerator \(X\), these exceptional links grow like \(\frac14X\log X\), while all two-bridge link types grow like \(\frac{X^2}{4\pi^2}\), so their density is asymptotic to \(\frac{\pi^2\log X}{X}\) and tends to zero.
