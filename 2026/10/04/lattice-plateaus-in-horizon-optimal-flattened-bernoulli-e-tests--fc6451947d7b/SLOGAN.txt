For Bernoulli likelihood-ratio e-processes, the exact probability of no rejection by a fixed horizon is a finite staircase in the power tilt \(\beta\), because only integer path boundaries matter. In the design \((p_0,p_1,\alpha,t)=(2/5,3/5,1/20,30)\), every \(\beta\in[1.4377067867\ldots,1.5201316459\ldots]\) is exactly optimal, while the smooth horizon stationary point \(1.1136398812\ldots\) has strictly larger first-passage type-II error.
