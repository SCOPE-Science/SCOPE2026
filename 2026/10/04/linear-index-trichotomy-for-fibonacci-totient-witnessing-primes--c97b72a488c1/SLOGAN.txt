For a Fibonacci-totient witnessing prime \(p=kq+1\), the rank \(z(p)\) is forced into one of the linear indices \(k\), \(k+2\), or \(2(k-2)\), according to the two Legendre symbols. This turns Goel's previously non-deterministic \(32\le k\le100\) range into a proof: every witness with \(k\le100\) has \(k=2\).
