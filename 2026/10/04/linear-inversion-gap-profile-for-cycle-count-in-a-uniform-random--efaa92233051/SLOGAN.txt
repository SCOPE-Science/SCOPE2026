In a uniform random permutation, the covariance between cycle count and an inversion indicator at positions \(i<j\) is exactly \(-(2(j-i)-1)/(2n(n-1))\). Summing the linear gap profile gives \(\operatorname{Cov}(C,\operatorname{inv})=-(2n-1)/12\), which is exactly the derivative of expected Mallows cycle count at the uniform point.
