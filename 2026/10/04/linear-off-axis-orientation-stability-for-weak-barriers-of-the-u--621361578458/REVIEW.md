# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof was reconstructed from the definitions and normalization of the orientation measure. The weak-barrier projection inequality is applied to all normalized sign diagonals of the cube, and the averaging step is exact. The critical pointwise estimate follows from the exact identities \(\mathbb E X^2=1\) and \(\mathbb E X^4=3-2\sum_i v_i^4\), together with \(|X|\le\sqrt n\). The angular step is the exact maximum of \(\sum_i a_i^2\) over \(a_i\ge0\), \(\sum_i a_i=1\), and \(a_i\le\cos^2\beta\). These steps give the displayed coefficient without an omitted factor from the orientation-measure normalization.

## Originality
PASS. The closest primary source is Kiderlen, arXiv:2605.13449v2. Its Corollary 1.4 controls the same off-facet-normal mass for polytopes by a power of the Jones deficit whose exponent is below one in dimensions \(n\ge3\); its centered-cube discussion cites a direct linear estimate only in dimension two. Steinerberger's predecessor is planar. Exact-topic database searches and broader Rademacher-stability searches did not locate a theorem implying the higher-dimensional weak-barrier estimate. The main residual risk is an unindexed special-cube application of first-absolute-moment Rademacher stability.

## Value
PASS. The result upgrades the natural cube stability law from a sublinear deficit power to a linear deficit law for every \(n\ge3\), with an explicit coefficient. The source paper identifies improving the stability exponent and controlling cube-normal orientations as mathematically useful for sharpening Jones-type lower-bound arguments. The claim is a structural near-equality theorem rather than a routine numerical variant.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest literature is arXiv:2605.13449v2 and the planar predecessor arXiv:2501.01004v1. The coefficient proved here is not claimed sharp, and orientation control alone does not encode where the barrier patches are located. Consequently the theorem does not itself improve the numerical minimum-area lower bound for cube barriers.
