# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reduces the affine class groups to the standard toric divisor sequence for a full-dimensional cone. For lower facets, the ray lattice is identified exactly as the congruence sublattice with coordinate sum divisible by \(2n-1\), yielding \(\mathbf Z/(2n-1)\). For upper facets with \(n\ge3\), ray differences generate the root lattice while all ray sums equal \(-2\), yielding torsion \(\mathbf Z/2\) and free rank \(n(n-2)\). The \(n=2\) upper chart is checked separately by Smith normal form, giving \(\mathbf Z/8\). The embedded checker replays all finite identities for \(2\le n\le8\).

## Value
PASS. These recently introduced K-polystable toric \(\mathbf Q\)-Fano examples are singular, and the exact fixed-point class groups quantify their singularity structure substantially more finely than a binary \(\mathbf Q\)-factoriality statement. The contrast between cyclic order \(2n-1\) on one orbit and free rank \(n(n-2)\) plus \(2\)-torsion on the other gives a natural dimension-dependent structural invariant.
