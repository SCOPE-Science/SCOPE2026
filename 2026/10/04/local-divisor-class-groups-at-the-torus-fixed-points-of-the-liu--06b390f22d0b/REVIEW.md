# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The face description gives exactly n lower rays v_{n,q}; in Z^n they are 2*1-e_q, whose differences generate A_{n-1} and whose coordinate sums are 2n-1, so the ray lattice is exactly the sum-congruence sublattice of index 2n-1 and the simplicial lower chart has Cl=Z/(2n-1). An upper facet has n(n-1) rays of coordinate sum -2. For n>=3, fixing a third index in ray differences produces every e_i-e_j, so the ray lattice is exactly the even-sum sublattice, giving torsion Z/2 and free rank n(n-2). At n=2 the two ray columns (-3,1),(1,-3) have determinant 8 and gcd of entries one, hence coker Z/8. These are precisely the invariants in the affine toric divisor exact sequence. The finite verifier checks incidence/lattice identities for n<=8 but the proof is all-n.

## originality

PASS

Liu--Zhu define the exact small-alpha toric family and give the lattice-polytope/ray data from which the local charts arise. Available primary metadata and detailed indexed construction descriptions do not advertise local divisor class groups; Wu's later spectrum paper is about realizing alpha values and likewise does not advertise this local singularity invariant. The class-group decomposition is a standard toric computation once the rays are known, but the two-orbit all-n calculation, including the exceptional n=2 upper chart and the free-rank defect n(n-2), was not found in the checked literature. Full-text access to the two recent preprints was incomplete, so that limitation is retained as a concrete residual risk rather than treated as proof of noncoverage.

PASS

Local divisor class groups are standard intrinsic singularity invariants, and this exact two-orbit computation sharply distinguishes the fixed-point singularities of a newly introduced all-dimensional Fano family, including a quadratically growing non-Q-factorial free defect. That is mathematically motivated information not supplied merely by knowing the alpha invariant.
