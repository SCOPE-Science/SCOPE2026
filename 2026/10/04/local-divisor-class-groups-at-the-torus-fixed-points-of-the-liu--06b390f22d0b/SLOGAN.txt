For the Liu--Zhu small-alpha toric \(\mathbf Q\)-Fano \(X_n\), the two cyclic orbits of torus fixed points have affine divisor class groups \(\mathbf Z/(2n-1)\) and, respectively, \(\mathbf Z/8\) for \(n=2\) or \(\mathbf Z^{\,n(n-2)}\oplus\mathbf Z/2\) for \(n\ge3\). The second orbit therefore carries a free non-\(\mathbf Q\)-factorial defect growing quadratically with \(n\).
