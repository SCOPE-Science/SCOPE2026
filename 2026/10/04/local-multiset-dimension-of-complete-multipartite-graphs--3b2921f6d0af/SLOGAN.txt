A set locally multiset-resolves a complete multipartite graph exactly when its positive part-counts are pairwise distinct and at most one part is missed. Thus finiteness is equivalent to \(a_i\ge i-1\) for sorted part sizes, and every finite case has \(\operatorname{lmd}(G)=\binom{r}{2}\), with all bases coming from feasible permutations of \((0,1,\ldots,r-1)\).
