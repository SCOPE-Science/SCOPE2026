In a complete multipartite graph, a set is local outer multiset resolving exactly when its selected occupancies are distinct among the parts that still contain an unselected vertex. This turns the invariant into the explicit sorted-part formula \(\operatorname{lodim}(G)=\min_{k\in\mathcal K}(N-\sum_{i=r-k+1}^r n_i+\binom{k}{2})\).
