# Review of Local resolution and monodromy of the Type-II conic-pencil singularity

## Correctness
PASS. The local factorization, branch smoothness, pairwise contacts, delta and Milnor number are reconstructed directly. The two blowups and multiplicities \\(4,6\\) are checked in coordinates. Exact saturation and Gröbner elimination give local Tjurina length \\(10\\). A'Campo's formula then gives \\(\\Delta_p(t)=(t-1)(t^4-1)(t^6-1)\\), whose degree and \\(t=1\\) multiplicity agree with \\(\\mu=11\\) and four branches.

## Originality
PASS. The closest source is the paper containing the arrangement itself. It gives the polynomial, global \\(\\tau=19\\), global \\(\\mu=20\\), and identifies \\(p\\) as the only non-quasihomogeneous point, but does not state the branch-contact matrix, local \\(\\mu\\)/\\(\\tau\\), the two exceptional multiplicities, or the local monodromy polynomial. Exact-expression and semantic searches found no same-object statement. General resolution formulas are not specific coverage.

## Value
PASS. The result supplies the missing local geometric explanation for the unique non-quasihomogeneous point in a paper centered on Alexander polynomials: one tangent pair of order two produces the resolution multiplicities \\(4\\) and \\(6\\), which in turn expose the local order-4 and order-6 monodromy factors. The result is narrow but structurally motivated and reusable in comparisons of global and local monodromy for conic-line arrangements.

## Closest literature and limitations
The lead comparison is A. Dimca, P. Pokora, G. Sticlaru, arXiv:2305.01450, Proposition 4.7. A'Campo's 1975 theorem supplies the general resolution-to-monodromy formula. The claim is local to one named arrangement and does not assert novelty of the abstract singularity type.
