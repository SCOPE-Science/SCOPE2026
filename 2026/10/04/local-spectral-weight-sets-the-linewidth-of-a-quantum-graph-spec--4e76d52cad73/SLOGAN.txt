In the attached-graph spectral filter, a simple visible eigenvalue has a universal Lorentzian strong-coupling profile whose energy FWHM is \(\operatorname{FWHM}_\alpha=4ck_0|\psi_0(v_0)|^2\alpha^{-2}+O(\alpha^{-6})\). The peak area is asymptotic to \(2\pi ck_0|\psi_0(v_0)|^2\alpha^{-2}\), so linewidth spectroscopy directly recovers the eigenfunction's local spectral weight at the attachment point.
