For every finite abelian kernel \(A\), the locating chromatic number of the enhanced power graph of \(\operatorname{Dih}(A)\) is \(|A|+|T(A)|+1\), where \(T(A)\) consists exactly of the kernel involutions lying in no larger cyclic subgroup. For an abelian \(2\)-group with \(r\) cyclic factors and \(s\) factors of order at least \(4\), this becomes \(|A|+2^r-2^s+1\).
