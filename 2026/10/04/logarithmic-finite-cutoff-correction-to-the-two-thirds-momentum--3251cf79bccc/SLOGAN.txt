For the preferred-orientation triangular quantum graph, the known momentum density \(2/3\) has the sharper finite-cutoff law \(M(K)=2K/3-4(\sqrt3\pi\ell)^{{-1}}\log(K\ell)+O(1)\). The logarithm comes from a nontrivial imbalance between shrinking gaps around even flat-band centers and the paired narrow bands around odd centers.
