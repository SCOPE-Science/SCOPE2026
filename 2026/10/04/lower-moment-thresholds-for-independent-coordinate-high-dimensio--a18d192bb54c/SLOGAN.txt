For iid coordinates, the high-dimensional \(\ell_p\) random-walk path has Jin's deterministic Gromov--Hausdorff limit under only a finite \(\max\{2,2p-2\}\)-moment. This reduces the source's \(2p\)-moment hypothesis to finite variance for \(1<p\le2\) and to a \((2p-2)\)-moment for \(p>2\), with the \(p=2\) case already known.
