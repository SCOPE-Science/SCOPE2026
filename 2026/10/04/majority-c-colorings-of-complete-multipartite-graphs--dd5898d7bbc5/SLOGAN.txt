For every complete multipartite graph \(K_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}\) with \(r\ge2\), the majority C-chromatic number is \(2\) exactly when every part size is even, and is \(1\) otherwise. In the two-color case every part is split exactly in half, giving \(\prod_i\binom{n_i}{n_i/2}\) labeled optimal colorings.
