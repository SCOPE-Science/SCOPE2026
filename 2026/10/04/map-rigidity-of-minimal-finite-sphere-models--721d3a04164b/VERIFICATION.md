The proof was checked at the level of finite posets and order complexes. The rank-growth lemma uses only order preservation and the two-point level structure. The cone argument was checked for all source simplices, including those already containing one of the collapsed-level vertices. The equal-dimensional induction was checked from the top target level downward. The standard low-to-high sphere case uses \(\pi_n(S^m)=0\) for \(n<m\).

A finite exhaustive sanity check for small dimensions agreed with the proof: for \(X_1\to X_1\), the only noncollapsed maps are the four automorphisms; for \(X_2\to X_2\), the only noncollapsed maps are the eight automorphisms; and every enumerated map \(X_2\to X_1\) or \(X_3\to X_1,X_2\) collapses a level. These computations are illustrative only and are not used to establish the general result.
