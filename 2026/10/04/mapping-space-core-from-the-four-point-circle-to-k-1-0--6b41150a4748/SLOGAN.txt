For the four-point finite circle \(C\) and the Cianci--Ottina minimal finite Klein bottle \(K_{1,0}\), the pointwise mapping space has exactly \(1{,}288\) maps in \(11\) homotopy components. Its \(40\)-point Stong core is \(K_{1,0}\sqcup\mathfrak C_4\sqcup\mathfrak C_4\sqcup D_8\), separating one Klein-bottle-type component, two circle-type components, and eight contractible components.
