# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The symbolic case split for maps P_{p,q} to P_{r,s} is correct: a map either preserves the two levels, or one lower image enters the target top level forcing the whole source top level to one point, or dually one upper image enters the target bottom level. This gives the displayed total-map formula. A level-preserving map with both restrictions nonconstant is incomparable with every distinct map and hence isolated, giving exactly (r^p-r)(s^q-s) singleton components. Every remaining map compares to a constant map, so all nonisolated maps form one component. The explicit target-valued retraction is monotone, fixes constants, and the chain-contiguity argument yields a strong deformation retraction on order complexes. The exact-commit verifier independently checks the formulas, components and contiguity condition on five parameter tuples; the universal proof is symbolic rather than inferred from those tests.

## originality

PASS

Speed's Hom(P,Q) paper studies the same pointwise Hom-poset for arbitrary finite posets, but its primary abstract and introduction state a Möbius-function formula rather than component topology. Published SCOPE records cover diagonal self-map cases and other special height-two targets, but the checked search did not find a theorem with four independent parameters p,q,r,s together with the explicit homotopy type of the unique nonisolated component. The diagonal self-map results are genuine prior special cases and are not counted as novelty; the accepted remainder is the full off-diagonal family plus its target-valued retraction.

Complete height-two posets are canonical finite graph models and already have separate self-map classifications. The four-parameter theorem gives a natural complete off-diagonal mapping-space decomposition, including the topology of the sole nontrivial component, and cleanly separates rigid singleton maps from the target-type component. This is a structural classification rather than a small finite slice.
