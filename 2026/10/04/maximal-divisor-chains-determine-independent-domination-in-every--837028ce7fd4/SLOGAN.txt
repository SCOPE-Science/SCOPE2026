For every composite \(n\), independent dominating sets of \(\Gamma'(\mathbb Z_n)\) are exactly the unions of entire gcd-cells indexed by maximal divisor chains. Consequently \(D_i\) is the prime-multiset-word generating function stated in RESULT.md, with \(\Omega!/(a_1!\cdots a_r!)\) independent dominating sets and exact ascending/descending formulas for \(\alpha\) and \(\gamma_i\).
