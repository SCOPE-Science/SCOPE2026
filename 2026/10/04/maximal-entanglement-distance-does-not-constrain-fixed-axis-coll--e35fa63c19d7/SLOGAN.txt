At maximal Entanglement Distance, a prescribed collective phase generator can still have any two-qubit pure-state QFI from \(0\) to \(4\). For every even \(N\), maximally entangled-distance probes also realize both \(F_Q=0\) and the Heisenberg endpoint \(F_Q=N^2\) for the same fixed \(J_z\), so the trace-over-local-directions identity is not a generator-specific sensitivity certificate.
