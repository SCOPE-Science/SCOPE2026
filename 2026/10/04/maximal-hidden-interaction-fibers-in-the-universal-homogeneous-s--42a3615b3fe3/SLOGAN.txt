For the universal ultrahomogeneous \(q\)-state stochastic process, the orbit of an \(n\)-tuple is far from reconstructible from all proper subtuples: the fiber over the independent-uniform proper marginals has exact affine dimension \((q-1)^n\). Thus for every \(n\ge3\) there are \(2^{\aleph_0}\) pairwise nonautomorphic \(n\)-tuples with identical proper-subtuple orbit data, the largest possible ambiguity.
