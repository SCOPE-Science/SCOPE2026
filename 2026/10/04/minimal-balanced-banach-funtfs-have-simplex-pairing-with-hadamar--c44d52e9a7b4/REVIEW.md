# Review

## Correctness
PASS. Tightness makes the synthesis map surjective; balance rules out length \(n\). At length \(n+1\), the analysis-synthesis pairing matrix is a scalar idempotent of rank \(n\) with both kernels generated by the all-ones vector, forcing the simplex projection and hence the exact pairings. The Hadamard construction satisfies every Banach norming, balance, and tightness identity directly.

## Originality
PASS. The closest primary source explicitly poses balanced FUNTF existence in real \(\ell_1^n\) as an open question. The inspected Banach FUNTF paper does not provide this balanced minimum-length theorem, while the inspected balanced-frame source is Hilbert-space based. Targeted semantic and exact-phrase searches did not locate the sharp lower bound together with the forced minimum-length pairing law and Banach Hadamard realization.

## Value
PASS. The result supplies a sharp structural boundary for an explicit open existence problem: it identifies the first possible length in every dimension, classifies the entire pairing matrix at that length, and gives infinitely many dimensions where the minimum is attained. The pairing rigidity is reusable independently of Hadamard existence.
