Every balanced Banach FUNTF in dimension \(n\) has at least \(n+1\) elements, and at this minimum length its pairing matrix is forced to have off-diagonal value \(-1/n\). A real Hadamard matrix of order \(n+1\) realizes this sharp minimum in \(\ell_1^n\), yielding an infinite Sylvester family in dimensions \(2^r-1\).
