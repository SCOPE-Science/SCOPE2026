For \(R\cong\prod_{i=1}^r\mathbb F_{q_i}\) with \(r\ge2\), the minimum total and paired dominating sets of the annihilator graph are exactly the vertex pairs whose coordinate supports are complementary. There are precisely \( (2^{r-1}-1)\prod_{i=1}^r(q_i-1) \) such minimum sets, and both domination parameters equal \(2\).
