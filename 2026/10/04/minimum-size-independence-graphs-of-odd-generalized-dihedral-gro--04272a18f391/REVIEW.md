# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. Generation in \(\operatorname{Dih}(A)\) reduces exactly to one reflection plus rotations generating \(A\). Completion of prescribed rotations is equivalent to a rank inequality in every Sylow Frattini quotient, so the four adjacency rules follow without case omission. Primewise counting gives the active-rotation count and the \(Q_0(a)\)-dependent degree formula. The order-divisibility proof is valuation-exact, including the delicate maximal-rank factor \(p^d-p\). The packaged replay exhaustively checks every minimum generating triple and every edge for one non-elementary kernel and one mixed-Sylow kernel.

## Originality
PASS. Lucchini's 2020 paper poses the degree-divisibility question but does not solve this higher-rank generalized-dihedral family. The 2022 rank-independence classification determines when every noncyclic pair is adjacent; for \(d(G)\ge3\) it covers an elementary-abelian scalar special case but does not determine the exact graph when that property fails. The present theorem classifies those missing pairs for arbitrary odd abelian kernels and derives exact degree strata. Searches using rank graph, generalized dihedral, Frattini quotient, pair completion, and degree terminology found no equivalent formula. The main residual risk is an equivalent formulation under flexible-group or irredundant-basis terminology.

## Value
PASS. The result gives a family-level affirmative answer to a published question for generalized dihedral groups with arbitrary odd abelian kernels of rank at least two, including non-elementary and mixed-Sylow examples. The exact graph description is stronger than the divisibility consequence and exposes a new rank-\((d-1)\) obstruction that is invisible in the elementary-abelian specialization.
