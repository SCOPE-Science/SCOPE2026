For \(G=\operatorname{Dih}(A)\) with \(A\) finite abelian of odd order and \(d(A)\ge2\), adjacency in the minimum-size independence graph is governed exactly by ranks in the Sylow Frattini quotients \(A_p/pA_p\), yielding closed degree formulas for all rotations and reflections. These formulas imply \(|g|\mid\deg_{\Gamma_{d(G)}(G)}(g)\) for every \(g\in G\), settling Lucchini's degree-divisibility question on the entire family.
