For a complete multipartite graph \(K_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}\) with \(n_1\le\cdots\le n_r\), minimum-sum vertex cover is solved exactly by exhausting the parts from smallest to largest. The resulting closed formula extends the known value \(\operatorname{msvc}(K_{a,b})=ba(a+1)/2\) from two parts to arbitrarily many parts.
