For a nonempty \(A\subseteq\mathbb Z/p^k\mathbb Z\), the smallest tight exponential frame has exactly \(p^{|R(A)|}\) frequencies, where \(R(A)\) is the set of distinct \(p\)-adic valuations of nonzero differences in \(A\). A canonical optimizer is obtained by freeing exactly the base-\(p\) digit positions corresponding to those difference scales.
