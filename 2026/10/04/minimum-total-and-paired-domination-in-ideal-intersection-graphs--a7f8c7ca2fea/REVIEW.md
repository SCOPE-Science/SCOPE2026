# Review

## Correctness

PASS. The decomposition of a finite commutative principal ideal ring into local chain rings gives a unique exponent vector for each ideal. Coordinatewise intersection is governed by the maximum exponent, so two ideals intersect nontrivially exactly when their active supports intersect. A two-vertex total dominating set must be an edge and must cover every local coordinate; singleton-support ideals prove necessity of the covering condition, and the same condition proves sufficiency. Existence, paired domination, and the exact count then follow without an unproved computational step.

Sources and risks: the full 2011 paper supplies the exact graph definition and algebra-primary context; the full 2014 article supplies the finite-principal-ideal-ring product and chain structure. The finite checker is corroborative only.

## Originality

PASS. Ordinary domination of ideal-intersection graphs is prior work, including the finite principal ideal ring family. The accepted claim is strictly stronger in a different direction: it gives the exact total-domination existence boundary, equality with paired domination, a necessary-and-sufficient description of every minimum set, and a closed enumeration formula. Searches under total-domination, paired-domination, ideal-intersection, principal-ideal-ring, and \(\mathbb Z_n\) formulations did not locate those conclusions. The most relevant full 2014 source contains no occurrence of “total domination” or “paired.”

Sources and risks: the 2019 \(\mathbb Z_m\) paper was accessible only through its abstract, which discusses ordinary domination. An unindexed strengthened-domination paper could therefore remain a residual risk, but no decisive unresolved comparison was found.

## Value

PASS. This is a complete minimum-set classification for a natural algebraic graph family, not merely a numerical parameter evaluation. The closed count records all local nilpotency lengths and specializes immediately to a prime-exponent formula for \(\mathbb Z_n\). The theorem also isolates the exact three structural obstructions to strengthened domination.

Risk: once the active-support model is recognized, the graph-theoretic core is short; the substantive value is the algebra-wide classification and exact enumerator.

Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
