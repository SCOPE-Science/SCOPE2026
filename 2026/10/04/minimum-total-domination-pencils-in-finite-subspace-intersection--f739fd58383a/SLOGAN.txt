In the intersection graph of proper nonzero subspaces of \(\mathbb F_q^n\) with \(n\ge3\), every minimum total dominating set is exactly the full pencil of \(q+1\) hyperplanes through one codimension-two subspace. Thus \(\gamma_t=q+1\), there are \(\begin{bmatrix}n\\2\end{bmatrix}_q\) minimum total sets, and paired domination is \(q+1\) for odd \(q\) and \(q+2\) for even \(q\).
