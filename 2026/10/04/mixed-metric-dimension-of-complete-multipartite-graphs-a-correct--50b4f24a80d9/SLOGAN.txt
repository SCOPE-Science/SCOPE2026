A mixed metric generator of a complete multipartite graph can omit at most two vertices: two omissions are possible only in a bipartite graph with both parts of size at least three, while one omission is possible exactly when every other part is non-singleton. This gives the corrected formula \(\operatorname{mdim}(G)\in\{N-2,N-1,N\}\) and shows, for instance, that \(\operatorname{mdim}(K_{3,3,5})=10\), not \(8\).
