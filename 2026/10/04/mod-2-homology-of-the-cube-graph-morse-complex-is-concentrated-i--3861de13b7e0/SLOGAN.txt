For the cube graph \(Q_3\), the ordinary Morse complex has reduced mod-\(2\) homology \(\mathbb F_2^4\) in degree \(5\) and \(\mathbb F_2^{139}\) in degree \(6\), and zero elsewhere. This gives the first non-cycle hypercube case exactly at the mod-\(2\) homology level and shows that its Morse complex is not a wedge of spheres all in one dimension.\n
