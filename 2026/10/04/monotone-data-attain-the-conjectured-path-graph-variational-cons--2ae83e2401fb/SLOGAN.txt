For the centered Hardy--Littlewood maximal operator on a finite path \(P_n\), every nonnegative monotone input satisfies the conjectured sharp \(1\)-variation factor \(1-1/n\). More precisely, the variation of the maximal function is exactly an endpoint minus the global mean, so any counterexample to the full path conjecture must be nonnegative and genuinely nonmonotone.
