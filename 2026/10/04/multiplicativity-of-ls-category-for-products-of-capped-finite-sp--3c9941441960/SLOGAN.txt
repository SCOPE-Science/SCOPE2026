For capped finite spaces \(X_i=P_i\oplus D_{m_i}\) with noncontractible bases, ordinary and geometric unreduced LS-category are exactly multiplicative: \(\operatorname{cat}(\prod_i X_i)=\operatorname{gcat}(\prod_i X_i)=\prod_i m_i\). For two factors the reduced product-subadditivity defect is exactly \((m-1)(n-1)\), and is therefore unbounded.
