With \(m\) oracle-known nonnulls and null e-values parked at \(1\), full e-BH discovery has the exact pathwise sample cost \(\sum_i\tau_i\), where each \(\tau_i\) is the first crossing of the sharp pooled boundary \(K/(\alpha m)\). For likelihood-ratio streams this gives the exact expectation \(\sum_i(\log(K/(\alpha m))+\mathbb E O_i)/D_i\), exposing the multiplicity interpolation between \(\log(K/\alpha)\) and \(\log(1/\alpha)\).
