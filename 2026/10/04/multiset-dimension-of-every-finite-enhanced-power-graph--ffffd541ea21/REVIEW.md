# Review
## Correctness
PASS. In the full enhanced power graph, the identity is adjacent to every element because \(\langle e,x\rangle=\langle x\rangle\) is cyclic. Hence the graph has diameter at most \(2\). For order at least \(4\), the identity has degree at least \(3\), so the graph cannot be a path. At order \(3\), the unique group is \(C_3\) and its enhanced power graph is \(K_3\), not a path. The published diameter-two theorem therefore gives infinite multiset dimension for every order at least \(3\). Orders \(1\) and \(2\) give paths and hence dimension \(1\) under the cited convention.

## Originality
PASS with a residual indexing risk. The 2022 survey explicitly poses the arbitrary-finite-group multiset-dimension question as Problem 14 and cites the foundational multiset-dimension paper, but does not state this consequence. Exact-phrase and implication-oriented searches for multiset dimension together with enhanced power graphs found the open problem and later papers on other invariants, but no source stating the classification. The mathematical-results search likewise returned no matching enhanced-power-graph multiset-dimension result.

## Value
PASS. The claim is deliberately elementary, but it is a natural complete classification that closes a named published open problem for every finite group rather than a parameter slice or isolated example. It also identifies the decisive structural reason and sharply separates the full graph from proper/deleted variants where the argument no longer applies.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest ingredients are Theorem 3.1 of Simanjuntak--Siagian--Vetrík on non-path diameter-two graphs and Problem 14 of Ma--Kelarev--Lin--Wang. Later enhanced-power-graph papers located in the searches concern universal vertices, cograph/chordal structure, distance spectra, strong domination, and graph reconstruction rather than multiset dimension. The classification does not extend by the same proof to proper enhanced power graphs. Literature searches cannot exclude every unindexed source.
