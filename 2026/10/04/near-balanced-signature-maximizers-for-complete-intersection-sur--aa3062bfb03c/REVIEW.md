# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The complete-intersection normal-bundle sequence gives the Chern classes, and the Hirzebruch signature theorem yields
\[
\sigma(X_{a,b})=\frac{ab(5-a^2-b^2)}3.
\]
At fixed \(S=a+b\), the substitution \(g=b-a\) reduces the entire optimization to the quartic \(10g^2-g^4\) on a parity-restricted set of nonnegative integers. This proves the maximizers uniformly. An exact-integer replay checks 9,799 bidegrees with \(6\le S\le200\) and both closed extremal-value formulas.

## Originality
PASS. The 1980 signature-congruence paper was inspected in full: it develops congruences, generating functions, ramified-cover arguments, and examples but does not state the fixed-sum bidegree extremal classification. The 2005 complete-intersection topology paper was also inspected through the stable tangent-bundle and signature-classification material; it supplies the classical invariant but not the gap-two / gap-one-and-three theorem. Exact and semantic searches for balanced bidegrees, fixed canonical exponent, and signature maxima found no covering theorem.

## Value
PASS. Signature is a fundamental topological invariant of smooth complete-intersection surfaces. Fixing \(a+b\) fixes the canonical-polarization exponent, and the result gives a complete sharp geography statement on this natural slice. The near-balanced extremum and odd two-type tie are not routine consequences of monotonicity.
