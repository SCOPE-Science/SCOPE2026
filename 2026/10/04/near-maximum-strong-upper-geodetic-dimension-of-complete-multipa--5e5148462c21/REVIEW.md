# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The proof is correct. In a complete multipartite graph, each omitted vertex must be assigned to a distinct selected same-part pair lying in another part. This matching-resource interpretation exactly characterizes strong geodeticity. For an N-1 set, deleting a second selected vertex forces the available resource count to collapse to the stated four profiles: K_{1,m}, K_{2,m}, K_{2,1^t}, and K_{2,2,1^t}. The case splits in the package correctly handle whether the omitted vertex belongs to a singleton or non-singleton part and whether other singleton parts exist. The counts of maximum N-1 minimal sets follow from the permissible omitted vertices. A fresh independent exhaustive simulation over all connected complete-multipartite isomorphism types through order eight reproduced every equality family and the N-2 upper bound outside them; the all-order proof is the analytic resource classification.

## originality

PASS

Originality passes. The 2021 primary paper introduces the strong upper geodetic number and proves general results but does not give a complete-multipartite classification. Irsic--Konvalinka study the minimum strong geodetic problem on complete multipartite graphs, which is a different optimization. A prior Resultary result covers the exact upper number for complete bipartite graphs, and the audited package explicitly treats those bipartite families as prior; the new claim is the exact N/N-1 boundary across all complete multipartite graphs, including the additional multipartite families and exclusion of every other part profile.
