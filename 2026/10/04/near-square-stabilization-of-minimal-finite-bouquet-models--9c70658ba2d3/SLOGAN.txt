For fixed square deficit \(d\), the number of minimal finite models of \(\bigvee^{k^2-d}S^1\) stabilizes once \(k\ge d+1\). The stabilized count is \(b_d+2\sum_{1\le r\le\lfloor\sqrt d\rfloor}b_{d-r^2}\), where \(b_e\) counts color-preserving bipartite graphs with \(e\) edges and no isolated vertices.
