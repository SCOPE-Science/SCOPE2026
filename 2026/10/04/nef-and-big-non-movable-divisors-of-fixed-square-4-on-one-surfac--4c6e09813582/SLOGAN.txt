Liu's fixed-surface counterexample can be chosen so that every \(D_n\) is a reduced simple-normal-crossings nef and big divisor with \(D_n^2=4\), while \(h^0(\widetilde X,\mathcal O_{\widetilde X}(mD_n))=1\) throughout \(1\le m<n^2/4+1\). Thus fixed self-intersection, nefness, and bigness still do not give a uniform moving-multiple bound on this surface.
