For the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M\), neighborhood-parity odd coloring has a parity phase transition: \(\chi_o=2\) for even \(n\), \(\chi_o=3\) for \(n=3\), and \(\chi_o=4\) for odd \(n\ge5\). For odd \(n\ge5\), every optimum is obtained by sharing two colors on two deleted matched pairs and using one exclusive color on the remainder of each part, yielding \(12n(n-1)\) named minimum colorings.
