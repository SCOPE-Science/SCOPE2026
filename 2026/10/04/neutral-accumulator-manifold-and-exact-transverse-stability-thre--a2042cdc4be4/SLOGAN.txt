Yogi has a whole ray of optimizer-state equilibria at a scalar quadratic minimizer: the residual second moment is neutral rather than uniquely pinned. At accumulator level \(V>0\), normal attraction is exact iff \(\eta\lambda/(\sqrt V+\varepsilon)<2(1+\beta_1)/(1-\beta_1)\); \(\beta_2\) affects only nonlinear accumulator drift, while large \(V\) stabilizes at the price of arbitrarily slow local convergence.
