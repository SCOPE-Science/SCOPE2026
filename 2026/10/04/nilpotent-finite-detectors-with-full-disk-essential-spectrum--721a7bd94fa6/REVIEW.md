## Correctness
**PASS.** The focal realization theorem applies to the exact product algebra used in the finding. Invertibility in an \(\ell_\infty\)-product requires coordinatewise inverses with uniformly bounded inverse norms; the geometric-series inverse calculation proves that the spectrum of the shift sequence is exactly \(\overline{\mathbb D}\). The polynomial norm formula is obtained from von Neumann's inequality and finite-support approximation to the unilateral shift, so no finite computation is extrapolated to an infinite claim.

This directly clarifies what the new detector theorem does not provide without additional hypotheses.
