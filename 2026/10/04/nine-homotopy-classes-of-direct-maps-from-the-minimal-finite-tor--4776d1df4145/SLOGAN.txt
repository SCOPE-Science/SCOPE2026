For the standard sixteen-point finite torus \(T=C\times C\), direct maps to the four-point circle \(C\) form exactly nine finite-space homotopy classes: one \(2828\)-map null class and eight isolated projection-after-automorphism maps. The induced integral homology maps are only \((0,0)\), \((\pm1,0)\), and \((0,\pm1)\), so the unsubdivided finite model sharply truncates the classical \(\mathbb Z^2\) family.
