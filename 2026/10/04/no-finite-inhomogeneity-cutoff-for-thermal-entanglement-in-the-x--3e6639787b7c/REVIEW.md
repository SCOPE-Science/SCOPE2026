**PASS.** The source Gibbs state is an \(X\)-state. Wootters concurrence reduces exactly to a single positive-part expression whose sign is controlled by \(J\sinh(\eta/T)/\eta-e^{-J_z/T}\). The function \(\sinh x/x\) is strictly increasing, yielding the complete one-threshold topology. Direct matrix reconstruction agrees with the formula. Dominant Gibbs terms give the reciprocal tail \(C\sim J/|b|\).

A closely related 2006 XXZ paper is explicitly retained as a residual risk because its full text was not available for complete comparison.

Targeted semantic and literature searches did not locate a published correction matching the accepted statement.
