For the two-qubit XXZ Gibbs state in an inhomogeneous \(z\)-field, increasing \(|b|\) can never destroy existing concurrence: either the state is entangled for every finite nonzero \(b\), or entanglement turns on once at a \(J_z\)-dependent lower threshold. At large inhomogeneity \(C(T,b)\sim J/|b|\), so the finite upper critical \(b\) claimed in the source does not exist.
