# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof starts from the exact Alexander-polynomial formulas printed for the two `L9a20` twist families. The source proves that all thirteen displayed exponents are distinct for \(|m|\ge3\) and for \(n\ge4\) or \(n\le-5\), so the coefficient of magnitude \(7\) cannot cancel in those infinite ranges. Exact collection of coincident exponents handles the remaining eleven nonzero parameters. Every case has a nonzero coefficient of absolute value greater than \(1\), contradicting the standard Alexander-polynomial constraint for L-space knots.

## Originality
PASS. The primary source uses these polynomials to distinguish its surgery-twin knots but does not classify their L-space-knot status. Baker--Motegi's highly relevant general work on L-space knots in twist families does not supply a theorem forcing zero L-space members in this linking-number-one family. Searches using the exact family identifier, both family names, Alexander coefficients, and L-space terminology found no equivalent complete classification. The principal residual risk is an unindexed short observation or a later revision of the recent preprint.

## Value
PASS. L-space knots are a central comparison class in the same paper's positive strongly-characterising-slope theorem. Showing that the entire explicit negative surgery-twin construction is disjoint from that class gives a complete structural boundary across all parameters and clarifies where L-space Floer methods can and cannot directly enter the new examples.
