In the multi-season epidemic chain, \(r-1\) consecutive seasons with \(\tau<1\) erase every dependence on the incoming immunity state. If \(\beta=\mathbb P(\tau<1)>0\), this gives a global minorization of size \(\beta^{r-1}\), removes the source's atom-at-complete-drift assumption for every \(r\ge2\), and yields an explicit uniform total-variation mixing rate.
