PNM's prescribed noise-amplitude normalization does not preserve its deterministic quadratic stability margin: with noise factor \(\Gamma=(1+\beta_0)^2+\beta_0^2\), the exact base-learning-rate ceiling is \(\eta L<2\sqrt{\Gamma/(2\Gamma-1)}\). The boundary is independent of \(\beta_1\), drops from \(2\) to \(\sqrt2\) as noise amplification grows, and equals \(2\sqrt5/3\) at the paper's default \(\beta_0=1\).
