For \(\Delta x=x\otimes x+a\,y\otimes y\) and \(\Delta y=x\otimes y+y\otimes x\) over \(\operatorname{char}k\ne2\), a nonsquare \(a\) leaves exactly five compatible self-distributive products, versus twenty-one when \(a\) is square. Exactly three of the five nonsquare products also preserve the counit multiplicatively.
