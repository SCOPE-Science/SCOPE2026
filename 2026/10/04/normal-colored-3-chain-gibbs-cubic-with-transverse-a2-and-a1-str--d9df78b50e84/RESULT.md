# Normal colored 3-chain Gibbs cubic with transverse A2 and A1 strata

## Finding
For the colored 3-chain logarithmically sparse symmetric-matrix model, Pavlov gives a prime cubic Gibbs hypersurface \(V=V(f)\subset\mathbb A^6_{\mathbb C}\), where
\[
f=x_{11}x_{13}x_{23}-x_{12}^2x_{23}+x_{12}x_{22}x_{13}-x_{12}x_{13}^2-x_{12}x_{13}x_{33}+x_{12}x_{23}^2-x_{22}x_{13}x_{23}+x_{13}^2x_{23}.
\]
Its reduced singular locus is exactly \(D\cup L\), where
\[
D=V(x_{12},x_{13},x_{23})
\]
is the diagonal 3-plane and
\[
L=V(x_{12},x_{23},x_{11}-x_{33},x_{22}-x_{33}-x_{13})
\]
is a 2-plane. Their intersection is the scalar line. The hypersurface \(V\) is normal. At \(\operatorname{diag}(a,b,c)\in D\) with \(a,b,c\) pairwise distinct, a transverse surface slice has type \(A_2\). At every point of \(L\setminus D\), a transverse threefold slice has type \(A_1\).

## Assumptions and scope
Everything is over \(\mathbb C\). The \(A_2\) assertion is restricted to \((a-b)(b-c)(a-c)\neq0\). The \(A_1\) assertion is restricted to \(L\setminus D\), equivalently \(x_{13}\neq0\) on \(L\). No classification is claimed on the lower-dimensional collision strata inside \(D\).

## Proof
Write \(y=x_{12}\), \(z=x_{13}\), and \(w=x_{23}\). Three first derivatives are
\[
f_{x_{11}}=zw,\qquad f_{x_{33}}=-yz,\qquad f_{x_{22}}=z(y-w).
\]
If \(z=0\), the remaining nontrivial gradient equations reduce to
\[
w(w-2y)=0,\qquad y(2w-y)=0.
\]
The first gives \(w=0\) or \(w=2y\); either option substituted into the second forces \(y=w=0\). Hence this branch is exactly \(D\).

If \(z\neq0\), the displayed derivatives force \(y=w=0\). The remaining gradient equations then reduce to
\[
x_{22}=x_{33}+z,\qquad x_{11}=x_{33},
\]
which gives exactly \(L\setminus D\). Direct substitution annihilates the gradient on both \(D\) and \(L\), proving the reduced singular-locus statement.

The source states that \((f)\) is prime and \(\dim V=5\). The singular locus has dimension \(3\), hence codimension \(2\) in \(V\). A hypersurface is Cohen--Macaulay, so \(V\) satisfies \(S_2\), and codimension-two singular locus gives \(R_1\). Serre's criterion therefore makes \(V\) normal.

At \(p=\operatorname{diag}(a,b,c)\), fix the diagonal coordinates and use \((y,z,w)\) as a normal slice. The slice equation is
\[
(a-b)zw+(b-c)yz-y^2w-yz^2+yw^2+z^2w.
\]
Put \(A=a-b\) and \(B=b-c\). The quadratic part has rank \(2\) and kernel generated by \((A,0,-B)\). Restricting the cubic part to that kernel gives
\[
AB(A+B)t^3=(a-b)(b-c)(a-c)t^3.
\]
For pairwise distinct \(a,b,c\), the coefficient is nonzero. The holomorphic splitting lemma yields transverse normal form \(uv+t^3=0\), hence type \(A_2\).

A point of \(L\setminus D\) can be written as
\[
(q,0,s,q+s,0,q),\qquad s\neq0.
\]
There the Hessian has rank \(4\): its principal minor on coordinates \(x_{11},x_{12},x_{13},x_{23}\) equals \(s^4\), and every \(5\times5\) minor vanishes. Its kernel is the tangent space of \(L\), so the transverse quadratic form is nondegenerate. The transverse hypersurface germ is therefore an ordinary double point \(A_1\).

## Relationship to prior work
Pavlov's paper gives this colored 3-chain model, states \(\dim V=5\), and gives the displayed cubic as the generator of its prime ideal. The inspected case discussion does not state its singular locus, normality, or transverse \(A_2/A_1\) stratification. Pavlov--Sturmfels--Telen provide the general Gibbs-variety framework and irreducibility/primality machinery, but the inspected general statements do not imply this explicit local classification without the Jacobian and local calculations above.

## Limitations
The result is specific to this colored 3-chain cubic. The local types on the pairwise-collision strata of \(D\), including the scalar line \(D\cap L\), are not classified. No general classification of singularities of colored-graph Gibbs varieties is claimed.
