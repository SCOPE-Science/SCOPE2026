For the tangent-double-line pencil orbit closure \(Y_5\), the incidence quadric bundle is its normalization. Its conductor over \(Y_4\simeq\operatorname{Hilb}^2\mathbb P^2\) is exactly the universal length-two family \(\operatorname{Bl}_\Delta(\mathbb P^2\times\mathbb P^2)\), branched over \(Y_3\), which is also the image of the normalization's singular \(\mathbb P^1\)-bundle.
