# Review

## Correctness
PASS. Nonnegativity plus \(h(0)=1\) forces \(h\) to vanish on every nonzero difference in \(A-A\). This yields an integer linear system necessary for weak tiling. The exact checker reduces all normalized subsets under linear automorphisms and proves rational inconsistency for every non-tiling class, while exhibiting exact difference-set tiling witnesses for every surviving class.

## Originality
PASS relative to the checked literature. The 2022 primary paper supplies the definition and proves pd-flatness in dimensions \(1,2\), with partial results in dimension \(3\); its cited higher-dimensional obstruction is over odd prime fields and does not settle the binary four-dimensional group. Targeted searches for \(\mathbb F_2^4\), pd-flatness, normalized weak tiling, and equivalent formulations found no covering statement.

## Value
PASS. The result closes a concrete binary dimension left outside the primary paper's low-dimensional theorem and strengthens pd-flatness by removing Fourier positivity altogether. The exhaustive classification also isolates exactly where normalized nonnegative weak tiling can occur.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is Kiss--Matolcsi--Matolcsi--Somlai, arXiv:2212.05513. Later weak-tiling counterexamples concern different settings or notions. The result is finite and dimension-specific.
