For Assari–Hosseinzadeh–Macpherson's set-homogeneous hypergraph \(N_3\), finite set-orbits are exactly odd-weight binary bracelets. This gives a closed Burnside formula with leading term \(2^{k-2}/k\) and implies that the number of self-converse local orders on \(k\) vertices is exactly \(2^{\lfloor(k-1)/2\rfloor}\).
