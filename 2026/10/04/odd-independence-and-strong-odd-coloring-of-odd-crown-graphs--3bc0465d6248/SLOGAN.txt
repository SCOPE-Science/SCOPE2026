For every odd \(n\ge3\), the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M_n\) has \(\alpha_{\mathrm{od}}=2\) and \(\chi_{\mathrm{so}}=n\); its only maximum odd independent sets are the deleted matched pairs, which also form the unique optimal strong odd coloring up to color names. Hence \(\alpha_{\mathrm{od}}\chi_{\mathrm{so}}=|V|\) throughout this infinite even-degree regular bipartite family.
