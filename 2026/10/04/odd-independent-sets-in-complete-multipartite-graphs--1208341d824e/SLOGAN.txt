For \(G=K_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}\) with \(r\ge2\), odd independent sets are exactly the empty set and the odd-sized subsets of one part. Thus \(\alpha_{\mathrm{od}}(G)\) is the largest odd integer at most \(\max_i n_i\), so \(\alpha_{\mathrm{od}}(G)=\alpha(G)\) exactly when the largest part has odd size.
