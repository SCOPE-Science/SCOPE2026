For every odd \(k\ge3\), the rough Veronese surface \(\mathcal R_{2,k,2}\) is the smooth Hirzebruch surface \(\mathbb F_2\), not the singular weighted projective plane that parametrizes it rationally. The unique base point of \(\mathbb P(1,1,2)\dashrightarrow\mathcal R_{2,k,2}\) is resolved by the minimal toric resolution, and the lifted embedding has polygon \(P_k=\operatorname{conv}\{(0,0),(k,0),(1,(k-1)/2),(0,(k-1)/2)\}\).
