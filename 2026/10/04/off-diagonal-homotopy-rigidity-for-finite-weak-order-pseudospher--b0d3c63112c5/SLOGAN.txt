For unequal finite weak-order pseudospheres of the same height \(h\ge3\), every non-null map is forced to preserve every level and be nonconstant there, and each such map is homotopically isolated. Consequently \(|[P,Q]|=1+\prod_{i=1}^h(q_i^{p_i}-q_i)\), with an exact product formula for the rank on top homology.
