Under approximate conditional isotropy, radial Marchenko--Pastur convergence for every orientation of one fixed proportional rank already forces the actual radius \(R_p\) to converge to the prescribed arbitrary-tail law. A Haar projection converts the source paper's rank-local energy conclusion into \(R_p-\rho_p\xrightarrow{P}0\), eliminating the need to assume spectral convergence at the identity rank or at every rank fraction.
