For the \(30^\circ\)-\(60^\circ\)-\(90^\circ\) triangle, the Fraenkel-optimal equal-area disk is determined by one strictly monotone scalar equation, yielding the exact implicit center \(P=(\sqrt{R^2-3z_*},\sqrt{R^2-z_*})\). The reduction comes from a general regular-regime law: the disk half-chords cut by the three sides are proportional to the corresponding side lengths.
