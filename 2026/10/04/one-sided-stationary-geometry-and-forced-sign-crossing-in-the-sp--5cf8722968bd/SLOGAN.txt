For compact stationary states of Sprott E, three coordinatewise generator identities fix \(\mathbb E[x\mid z]=1/4\), \(\mathbb E[x^2\mid y]=y\), and \(\mathbb E[yz\mid x]=0\), while bounded complete trajectories satisfy \(y>0\). The resulting sharp law \(\mathbb E[y]=1/16+\operatorname{Var}(x)\) is rigid at the unique equilibrium, and every other compact recurrent state must visit both \(z>0\) and \(z<0\).
