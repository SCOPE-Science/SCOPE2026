Among all zero-dimensional plane problems for general hypersurfaces of degree \(d\ge4\), the plane count is odd only for the quintic in \(\mathbb P^9\) and the septic in \(\mathbb P^{14}\). For odd admissible \(d=2m+1\), its parity collapses to the three-row hook number \(2(3q)!/(q!(q+1)!(q+2)!)\) with \(q=m(m+1)/6\), which is odd only for \(q=1,2\).
