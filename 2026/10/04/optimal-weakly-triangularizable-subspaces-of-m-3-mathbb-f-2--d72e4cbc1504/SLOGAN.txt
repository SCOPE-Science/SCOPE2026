The optimal weakly triangularizable subspaces of \(M_3(\mathbb F_2)\) are completely classified: exactly \(35\) six-spaces occur, in three similarity orbits of sizes \(21,7,7\), represented by \(\mathrm T_3(\mathbb F_2)\), \(\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb F_2)\vee M_1(\mathbb F_2)\), and \(M_1(\mathbb F_2)\vee\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb F_2)\). No seven-dimensional weakly triangularizable subspace exists.
