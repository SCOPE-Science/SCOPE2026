The closing-book variational construction admits a computable optimized coefficient: the number of soft-waveguide eigenvalues below a fixed \(\nu\) satisfies \(\liminf_{\beta\downarrow0}\beta N_\beta(\nu)\ge C_\star(\nu)\), with \(C_\star\) explicit in the double-well transverse data. For a delta-profile channel the transverse factor is itself closed-form through the double-delta decay rate \(\kappa\).
