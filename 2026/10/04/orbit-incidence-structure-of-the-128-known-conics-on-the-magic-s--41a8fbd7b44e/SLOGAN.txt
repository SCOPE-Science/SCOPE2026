The 128 known rational conics on the \(3\times3\) magic-square surface form one automorphism orbit, each with stabilizer order \(16\), four trivial points, and six nodes split \(4+2\) between two singular orbits. Equivalently, every trivial point lies on two known conics and the two incident node orbits lie on four each; this does not claim that the 128 known conics exhaust all conics on the surface.
