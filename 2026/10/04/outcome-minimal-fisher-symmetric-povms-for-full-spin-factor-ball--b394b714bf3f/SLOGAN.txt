For every full \(d\)-parameter spin-factor ball with \(d\ge4\), the locally isotropic Gill--Massar optimum \(F=J_\theta/d\) is attained by a single \((d+1)\)-outcome biased-simplex POVM at every interior point. No finite POVM attaining that full-rank Fisher matrix can use fewer outcomes; in the five-parameter \(\Gamma^5\) model the optimum therefore needs exactly six, rather than the ten labeled outcomes of the randomized binary construction.
