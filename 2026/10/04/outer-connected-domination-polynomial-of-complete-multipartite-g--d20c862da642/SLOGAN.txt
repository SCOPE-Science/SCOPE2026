For \(G=K_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}\), outer-connected domination has an exact all-set classification: apart from \(V(G)\), the complement must be a singleton or meet at least two parts, while the selected set must meet at least two parts or equal one whole part. This yields a closed outer-connected domination polynomial and the exact total count \(2^N+N+3r-1-2\sum_i2^{n_i}\).
