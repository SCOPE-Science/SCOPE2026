For a finite abelian group with a nonempty subgroup intersection graph, paired domination fails exactly for \(C_{p^2}\), \(C_p^2\), and \(C_{pq}\). When it exists, the paired-domination number is \(4\) for \(C_2^d\) with \(d\ge3\), is \(p+1\) for \(C_p^d\) with odd \(p\) and \(d\ge3\), and is \(2\) otherwise.
