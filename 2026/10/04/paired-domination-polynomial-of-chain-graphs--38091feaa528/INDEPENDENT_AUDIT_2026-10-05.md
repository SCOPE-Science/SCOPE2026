# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Necessity follows from the bipartite perfect matching and the nested neighborhoods: selected A-vertices through level k can only match to selected B-vertices through level k. The extreme-class requirements are forced by domination and matching. Conversely, a selected B_1 dominates every A-class and a selected A_p dominates every B-class; Hall's condition for any subset of selected A-vertices reduces exactly to the prefix inequalities. Independent brute-force checks on four small chain profiles matched the characterization exactly.

## originality

PASS

Prior work gives the definition, minimum paired-domination algorithms for broad ordered classes, upper-paired-domination algorithms including chain graphs, and an early paired-domination-polynomial announcement whose official publication does not identify its standard families. None of the accessible statements supplies the all-set prefix-ballot characterization or full polynomial for arbitrary chain twin-class profiles. The theorem therefore passes best-of-knowledge originality with explicit residual risk from the inaccessible bodies of the older paired-domination-polynomial/upper-paired sources.

## value

PASS

Chain graphs are a canonical nested-neighborhood class, and an exact all-set polynomial is materially stronger than prior minimum or upper optimization. The ballot-style criterion and dynamic program are reusable structural information rather than a tiny-instance computation.

The dated certificate retains the supplied scientific assessment, sources and limitations.
