For a connected chain graph, paired-dominating sets are exactly the balanced selections whose cumulative \(A\)-counts never exceed the cumulative \(B\)-counts, with at least one selected vertex in \(B_1\) and \(A_p\). This turns the full paired-domination polynomial into a ballot-style dynamic program and gives \(\gamma_{pr}(G)=2\) with exactly \(\alpha_p\beta_1\) minimum sets.
