On the balanced four-mode indefinite quadratic, every finite two-sided paired-Moreau regularization leaves the merit strictly worse conditioned than \(\kappa^2\), and the infimum \(\kappa^2\) is reached only in the infinite-regularization limit. Thus fixed-step gradient descent on this exact merit inherits the squared-residual contraction barrier, even though the underlying stationary-point problem admits a better class-level information benchmark.
