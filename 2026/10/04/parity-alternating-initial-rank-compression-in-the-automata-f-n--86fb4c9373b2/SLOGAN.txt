For odd \(n>3\), the classical slow automata \(F_n\) compress rank with an exact parity-alternating initial profile: \(\lambda_n(s)=3s-2\) for odd \(s\) and \(\lambda_n(s)=3s-3\) for even \(s\), through \(s=(n+1)/2\). The alternation comes from the forced spacing of effective uses of the unique collision letter.
