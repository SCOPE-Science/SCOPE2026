A tightly connected preferred-orientation ring chain has an arithmetic band-density anomaly: if \(L_2/L_3=p/q\) is reduced, its spectral-energy density is \(1/2+1/(2pq)\) when \(p,q\) are both odd and \(1/2\) otherwise. In particular, the asymmetric ratio \(1/3\) has density \(2/3\), correcting the published universal \(1/2\) claim for unequal arcs.
