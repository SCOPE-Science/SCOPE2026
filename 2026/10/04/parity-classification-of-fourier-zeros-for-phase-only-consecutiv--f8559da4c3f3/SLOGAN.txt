For phase-only signals on the consecutive support \(\{0,1,2,3\}\subset\mathbb Z_N\), the Fourier-zero count is exactly \(\{0,1,2\}\) for odd \(N\) and \(\{0,1,2,3\}\) for even \(N\). The mechanism is rigid: three unit-circle roots can coexist with four equal coefficient moduli exactly when two roots are antipodal.
