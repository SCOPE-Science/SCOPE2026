## Correctness
PASS. Proposition 14 of Nie--Ranestad--Sturmfels gives the symmetric determinantal projective degree product. Consecutive-product cancellation yields the stated exact curvature ratio. A second cancellation gives the two-step parity ratio, and the cross-multiplied difference is exactly \(4n^2-1\), proving strict parity-wise decay. The Gamma-ratio rewrite is exact, and \(\Gamma(x+1/2)/\Gamma(x)\sim\sqrt{x}\) gives the claimed limit and the threshold \(17\theta^2=1\). Domain restrictions ensure all neighboring ranks and all product factors are valid.

The inspected complete-quadrics literature studies SDP/ML and dual/intersection degrees, not this ordinary projective-degree sequence. PASS. The result extracts a structural phase diagram from a classical determinantal degree formula: each parity class of coranks has strictly decreasing local multiplicative curvature, and both classes share an explicit asymptotic transition. This organizes the full rank family rather than evaluating isolated degrees, and it gives a concrete comparison point between symmetric and unrestricted determinantal geometries.
