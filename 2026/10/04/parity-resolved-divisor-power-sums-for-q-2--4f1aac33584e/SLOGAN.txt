For Pongsriiam's \(q=2\) divisor-residue problem, the sign is controlled exactly by \(v_2(n)\): the even-minus-odd power sum is negative for every \(n\) precisely when \(s\le-1\), while every \(s>-1\) gives infinitely many adjacent sign changes. For all \(s<0\), the cumulative difference nevertheless has the negative linear bias \((2^s-1)\zeta(1-s)x+o(x)\).
