# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The even-scale proof was reconstructed from the arbitrary-\(L_1\) sharp metric-cotype estimate, Wang's parity-coset/sign comparison, quotient duality, and a quantitative Bartle--Graves section of a weighted closed relation. The odd-scale obstruction was checked directly by the parity character and Hahn--Banach. The argument covers \(p=1\) (hence \(p'=\infty\)) and arbitrary nonempty \(\Gamma\).

## Originality
PASS. The closest focal source gives finite-\(N\) \(\ell_\infty^N\) liftings, imposes finite-dimensionality in its continuous-selection proposition, and states odd-\(m\) failure for regular linear factorizations. The final statement instead gives arbitrary-coordinate continuous nonlinear liftings on even scales and an actually unsolvable adjoint datum on odd scales. Exact and alias searches found no covering statement. Residual risk is that the graph-selection reduction or parity implication may exist as unindexed folklore.

## Value
PASS. The finding turns evenness from a technical hypothesis on a regular factorization into a sharp nonlinear solvability boundary and simultaneously extends the positive side to potentially nonseparable data spaces with a continuous dimension-free selector. This is a structural clarification of the new lifting theory rather than a parameter substitution or finite recomputation.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is Wang, arXiv:2609.32589v1, especially Corollary 1.5, Proposition 1.6, and the odd-\(m\) argument in Theorem 1.3. Cheng--Wang--Xiang, arXiv:2609.08749v2, supplies the arbitrary-\(L_1\) estimate. The selector uses the quantitative Bartle--Graves theorem. The constant \(22\) is not claimed optimal, the selector is not claimed Lipschitz, and the positive theorem requires \(m\ge4\) even with \(n\le m^q\).
