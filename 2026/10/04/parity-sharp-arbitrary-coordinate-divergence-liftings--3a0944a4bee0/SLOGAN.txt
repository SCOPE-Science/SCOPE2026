For arbitrary nonempty \(\Gamma\), the normalized metric-cotype divergence problem on \(\ell_\infty(\Gamma)\) has dimension-free continuous odd homogeneous liftings with bound \(22(1+\varepsilon)\) at every admissible even scale, while every odd scale with \(n\ge2\) has an unsolvable datum. Thus evenness is a sharp nonlinear solvability boundary, not only a regular-factorization condition.
