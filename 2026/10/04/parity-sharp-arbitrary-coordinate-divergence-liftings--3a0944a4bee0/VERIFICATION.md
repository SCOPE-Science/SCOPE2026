# Verification

The final claim was checked symbolically from the operator identities; no finite experiment is used as a substitute for proof.

For the even-scale statement, the critical premise is that the sharp metric-cotype inequality is quantified over arbitrary \(L_1\)-spaces. Taking \(X=\ell_1(\Gamma)\), Wang's parity-coset proof and Banach-valued increment comparison therefore reproduce the normalized forward bound with constant \(22\). Wang's all-Banach-space quotient duality converts this into the adjoint lifting estimate in \(X^*=\ell_\infty(\Gamma)\).

For continuity, the relation \(R=\{(g,h):Ah=Bg\}\) is closed. With \(\|(g,h)\|_R=\max\{\|g\|,\|h\|/22\}\), its projection to the data space has Banach constant exactly \(1\). Quantitative Bartle--Graves gives a continuous positively homogeneous section with factor \(1+\varepsilon\); odd symmetrization preserves both the equation and the norm bound.

For odd \(m\), the exact witness \(F(x)=(-1)^{x_1+x_2}u\) satisfies \(D_{\rm sgn}F=0\) and \(D_{\rm long}F\ne0\). Hahn--Banach separation then produces a datum for which the adjoint equation is inconsistent. This proves nonexistence, not merely failure of a uniform norm estimate.

Unproved limits: optimality of \(22\), Lipschitz regularity of the selector, and any complex-scalar extension are not claimed.
