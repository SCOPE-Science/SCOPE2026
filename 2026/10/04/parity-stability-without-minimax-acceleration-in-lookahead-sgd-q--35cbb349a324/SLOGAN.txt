For fixed-parameter Lookahead-SGD on positive-definite quadratics, odd synchronization periods can stabilize inner steps beyond \(\eta L=2\), while even periods cannot. Yet the exact interval-minimax contraction is still \(((L-\mu)/(L+\mu))^k\); for every \(k\ge2\), the unique robust optimum is \(\alpha=1\) and \(\eta=2/(L+\mu)\), so the stability gain does not produce minimax spectral acceleration.
