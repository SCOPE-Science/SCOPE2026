# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The source's link argument gives a uniform \((AN)^{3N}\) chamber bound for every fixed labeled cyclic decomposition of the vertex slots. Relabeling the slots conjugates the decomposition permutation. Conjugacy classes of permutations are exactly cycle types, and ordinary unoriented unordered link types are unchanged by such relabeling. Hence one representative per component-length partition suffices. Genuine polygonal components have at least \(3\) edges, so the number of relevant models is \(p_{\ge3}(N)\). The Hardy--Ramanujan partition asymptotic then makes this factor \(N^{o(N)}\), yielding the stated \(N^{3N+o(N)}\) upper bound.

## Originality
PASS. The inspected 2026 primary manuscript states the fixed-combinatorics chamber estimate but then sums over at most \(N!\) labeled cyclic decompositions and records \(N^{4N+o(N)}\) for ordinary links. It does not quotient by conjugacy, cycle type, or component-length partitions. Searches using both the final exponent and the structural mechanism found no equivalent statement. The main residual risk is an unindexed later observation or a revision of the recent preprint.

## Value
PASS. Removing the labeled-decomposition factorial is a substantive asymptotic sharpening: it lowers the ordinary-link upper exponent from \(4\) to \(3\), matching the knot-side chamber exponent. It also clarifies that the component-combinatorics contribution is only subexponential once coordinate labels are quotiented out, focusing future improvements on the chamber count or on genuinely topological lower constructions.
