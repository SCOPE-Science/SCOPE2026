For ordinary unoriented unordered polygonal links, the source's \(N!\) sum over labeled component decompositions can be quotiented by vertex relabeling: only the cycle type of the decomposition permutation matters. Thus \(|\mathcal L_N|\le p_{\ge3}(N)(AN)^{3N}=N^{3N+o(N)}\), improving the stated \(N^{4N+o(N)}\) upper bound.
