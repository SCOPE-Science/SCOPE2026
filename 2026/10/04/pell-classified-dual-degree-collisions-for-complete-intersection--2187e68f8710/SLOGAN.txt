Smooth complete-intersection curves of types \((2,m)\) and \((3,n)\) have equal projective-dual degree exactly along the Pell solutions \(x^2-24u^2=1\), with \(m=3u\) and \(n=(x-1)/2\). Apart from the initial reversal of \((2,3)\), this gives infinitely many distinct bidegrees with the same dual degree, beginning with \((2,30)\) and \((3,24)\).
