For a four-point Cartesian rectangle in \(\mathbb Z_m\times\mathbb Z_n\), equal-magnitude coefficients force the Fourier-zero count into only five structural possibilities: \(\{0,2,m,n,m+n-1\}\) when both factors are even and \(\{0,1,m,n,m+n-1\}\) otherwise. The cross phase \(\eta=ad/(bc)\) separates factorized row/column zeros from an isolated antipodal pair.
