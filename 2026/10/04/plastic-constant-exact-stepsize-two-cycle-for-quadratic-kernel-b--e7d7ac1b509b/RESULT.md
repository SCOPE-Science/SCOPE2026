# Plastic-constant exact stepsize two-cycle for quadratic-kernel B-adaPGα

## Finding
For the quadratic-kernel specialization of \(\mathrm{B\text{-}adaPG}_{\alpha}\), there is an exact scalar strongly convex quadratic trajectory whose adaptive stepsizes never converge. Let
\[
f(x)=\frac{\lambda}{2}x^2,\qquad \lambda>0,\qquad g\equiv0,
\]
and use the Euclidean kernel \(\phi(x)=\tfrac12x^2\). Let \(r>1\) denote the plastic constant, the unique real root of
\[
r^3-r-1=0,
\]
and define
\[
a=\frac{1+\sqrt{1+2r^2}}{2r},\qquad b=ra.
\]
If the two positive initialization steps allowed by the algorithm are chosen so that
\[
\lambda\gamma_0=b,\qquad \lambda\gamma_1=a,
\]
then, for every \(m\ge0\),
\[
\lambda\gamma_{2m}=b,\qquad \lambda\gamma_{2m+1}=a.
\]
The orbit is nonconstant: \(a\approx1.178974903895906\) and \(b\approx1.561809226331805\). Nevertheless the iterates converge Q-linearly to the minimizer.

## Assumptions and scope
The result concerns the exact quadratic-kernel update in Remark 2.9 of Ou--Latafat--Themelis, specialized to one dimension with \(Q=1\). The source explicitly permits two positive initial stepsizes \(\gamma_0,\gamma_1\). The claim assumes exact arithmetic, \(x^0\ne0\), and the particular initialization above. It does not claim that this period-two orbit attracts generic initial stepsizes.

For consecutive distinct iterates, the source's local estimates satisfy exactly
\[
\ell_k=L_k=\lambda.
\]
Writing \(t_k=\lambda\gamma_k\), its quadratic-kernel rule therefore becomes
\[
t_{k+1}=t_k\min\!\left\{
\sqrt{1+\frac{t_k}{t_{k-1}}},
\frac{1}{\sqrt{2[t_k^2-t_k]_+}}
\right\}.
\]
Both values \(a,b\) lie in \((1,2)\), so the positive-part term is ordinary multiplication on the claimed orbit.

## Proof
The polynomial \(p(z)=z^3-z-1\) is strictly increasing for \(z>1\), with \(p(1)<0<p(4/3)\). Hence its root \(r\) satisfies \(1<r<4/3<2\). From the displayed formula for \(a\), one has \(1<a<b<2\), where \(b=ra\).

First consider a state with \((t_{k-1},t_k)=(b,a)\). The growth branch equals
\[
\sqrt{1+\frac{a}{b}}=\sqrt{1+\frac1r}=r,
\]
because \(r^3=r+1\). The curvature branch is \([2a(a-1)]^{-1/2}\), and it is strictly larger than \(r\). Indeed, the defining formula for \(a\) is equivalent to
\[
2ra^2-2a-r=0.
\]
Using this identity, the inequality \(2r^2a(a-1)<1\) is equivalent to \(2ra>r+1\); but
\[
2ra=1+\sqrt{1+2r^2}>1+r.
\]
Therefore the growth branch is selected and
\[
t_{k+1}=ar=b.
\]

Now consider \((t_{k-1},t_k)=(a,b)\). Multiplying \(2a(ra-1)=r\) by \(r\) gives
\[
2b(b-1)=r^2,
\]
so the curvature branch is exactly \(1/r\). The growth branch is \(\sqrt{1+r}>1\), whereas \(1/r<1\). Hence the curvature branch is selected and
\[
t_{k+1}=\frac br=a.
\]
Induction yields the exact two-cycle for all subsequent stepsizes.

It remains to verify that the local estimates used above never become undefined. The scalar proximal-gradient update is simply
\[
x^{k+1}=(1-t_{k+1})x^k.
\]
Because \(1<a<b<2\), neither factor \(1-a\) nor \(1-b\) is zero. Thus \(x^k\ne0\) and \(x^k\ne x^{k-1}\) for every finite \(k\), so \(\ell_k=L_k=\lambda\) holds at every update.

Let
\[
q=(a-1)(b-1).
\]
Since \(a,b\in(1,2)\), one has \(0<q<1\), and the two alternating gradient factors give exactly
\[
x^{2m}=q^m x^0,\qquad
x^{2m+1}=(1-a)q^m x^0.
\]
Therefore \(x^k\to0\) Q-linearly. The one-step absolute contraction factors alternate between \(a-1\approx0.178974903895906\) and \(b-1\approx0.561809226331805\), while each pair of steps contracts distance by \(q\approx0.100549752290568\) and objective value by \(q^2\approx0.010110252685695\).

## Verification
The proof is algebraic and does not depend on finite numerical experiments. The accompanying `verify_cycle.py` independently evaluates the plastic constant at high precision, checks the branch inequalities and defining identities, replays the adaptive recurrence for multiple periods, and compares the iterates with the closed forms above. It prints `VERIFY_OK` when all checks pass.

## Relationship to prior work
Ou--Latafat--Themelis give the exact quadratic-kernel update used here and report that a small stepsize may trigger a larger one at the next iteration, identifying this behavior as a direction for further research. Their broader numerical discussion also reports fluctuating adaptive stepsizes.

The Euclidean predecessor of Latafat--Themelis--Stella--Patrinos explicitly reports “seemingly cyclic” adaptive stepsize behavior and states that a rigorous explanation of the empirical oscillations is an open question. That paper does not provide the exact period-two quadratic orbit above. Predefined cyclic gradient schedules are also discussed in that literature, but those schedules are imposed externally rather than generated by this adaptive recurrence.

Targeted semantic and web searches for the exact scalar quadratic recurrence, period-two adaptive orbit, the numerical values \(a,b\), and the plastic constant did not locate an implication-equivalent statement. The closest database records concern different adaptive gradient rules and generic stepsize frontiers.

## Limitations
The construction is a specially initialized one-dimensional quadratic witness. It proves existence of persistent adaptive stepsize oscillation, not genericity or attraction of the cycle. It does not characterize all periodic orbits, nor does it extend the exact plastic-constant formula to nonquadratic kernels. The literature comparison was targeted rather than exhaustive over every historical adaptive-gradient paper, so an older equivalent observation under different notation remains a residual risk.
