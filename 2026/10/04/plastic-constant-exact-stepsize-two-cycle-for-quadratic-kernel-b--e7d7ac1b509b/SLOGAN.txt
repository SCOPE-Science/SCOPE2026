The quadratic-kernel \(\mathrm{B\text{-}adaPG}_{\alpha}\) rule admits an exact nonconstant period-two stepsize orbit on the scalar quadratic \(f(x)=\tfrac{\lambda}{2}x^2\), with the two normalized steps determined by the plastic constant. The iterates still converge Q-linearly, so convergence of the optimization trajectory does not require convergence of the adaptive stepsizes.
