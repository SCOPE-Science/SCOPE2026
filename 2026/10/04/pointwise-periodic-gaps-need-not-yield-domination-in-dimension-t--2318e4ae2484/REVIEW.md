# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The fiber-bunching inequality is checked explicitly, periodic products are computed exactly, irrationality excludes every periodic collision, and the Bernoulli measure gives an exact zero Lyapunov gap. The contradiction with domination uses the standard uniform-gap consequence recorded in the cited source.

## Originality
PASS. The closest published sharpness example inspected is Kassel–Potrie's three-dimensional locally constant construction. Avila–Bochi–Yoccoz provide a two-dimensional boundary classification, and Sadovskaya discusses nearby two-dimensional nonuniform examples, but the inspected sources do not state the exact strongly bunched irrational-diagonal family with a strict gap on every periodic orbit and a zero-gap Bernoulli measure. The main residual risk is an unindexed folklore occurrence of this elementary construction.

## Value
PASS. The example isolates the necessity of uniformity in the lowest possible fiber dimension while preserving strong bunching and allowing arbitrarily small distance from the identity. The invariant measure with zero gap also explains concretely how pointwise periodic positivity fails to control the full invariant-measure spectrum.
