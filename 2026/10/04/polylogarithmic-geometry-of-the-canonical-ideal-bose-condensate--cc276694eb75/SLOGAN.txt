The canonical ideal-Bose condensate right-tail rate admits an explicit inverse-polylogarithm parametrization: it is strictly convex, has a finite universal interior endpoint cost with infinite slope, and changes local geometry at \(\alpha=2\). For \(1<\alpha<2\) it scales as \(\eta^{\alpha/(\alpha-1)}\), at \(\alpha=2\) as \(\eta^2/|\log\eta|\), and for \(\alpha>2\) quadratically.
