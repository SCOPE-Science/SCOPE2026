For every strongly separated two-map self-similar system with contraction ratios \(\rho\) and \(\rho^2\), the critical \(s_{\mathcal F}\)-periodic content is positive. Golden-Mean de Bruijn cycles produce primitive periodic orbits of size \(F_{n+1}\) with gap at least \(\delta_{\mathcal F}\rho^{n-1}\), giving a strictly positive critical normalized-gap limit.
