Every positive counterexample to the small-witness \(\mu\)-Sondow conjecture would force a new Fermat prime beyond \(F_4\). Odd parameters, even non-powers of two, and powers of two with composite successor all have immediate witnesses, while \(145\in S_{256}\) and \(627\in S_{65536}\) dispose of the two nonexcluded known Fermat-prime parameters.
