# Same-model review

## Correctness
**PASS.** For the stated domain, the free-space distance is exactly
\[
\max\{r,a^{1-\alpha}r^\alpha\}.
\]
The additive error is supported on \(0<r<a\), has one critical point, and its global maximum is \((1-\alpha)\alpha^{\alpha/(1-\alpha)}a\). Choosing \(a\) from this exact maximum gives the claimed \(\varepsilon\) and equality radius. The structural steps use the Schur property of the power-gauge free space and the separable isometric-linearization theorem with their hypotheses intact.

## Originality
**PASS.** The closest 2026 source states the result for \(\ell_2\) with the square-root gauge only. The general Kalton and Godefroy-Kalton theorems are ingredients and do not imply the exact quantitative family without the new parameterized rescaling calculation. Searches for arbitrary non-Schur sources, power gauges, equivalent snowflake formulations and the sharp normalization found no covering statement.

## Value
**PASS.** The theorem identifies failure of the Schur property, rather than Hilbert structure, as the broad source-side mechanism behind this approximate-versus-exact embedding separation. It also gives a continuum of canonical target metrics and their exact additive-error scale, making the obstruction reusable rather than an isolated example.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest prior result is Sun-Zhang, arXiv:2609.13937v1. Kalton's 2004 free-space Schur theorem and Godefroy-Kalton's 2003 isometric-linearization theorem supply essential structural inputs. The result is restricted to separable real non-Schur sources and \(0<\alpha<1\); no endpoint, Schur-source, or nonseparable analogue is claimed. Because the derivation is concise, an unindexed equivalent observation remains the principal originality risk.
