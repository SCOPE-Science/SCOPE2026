For every separable real Banach space \(X\) without the Schur property and every \(0<\alpha<1\), a fixed Schur Lipschitz-free target admits exact \(\varepsilon\)-isometries of \(X\) for all \(\varepsilon>0\) but no exact isometric embedding of \(X\). The sharp additive error occurs at distance \(\alpha\varepsilon/(1-\alpha)\).
