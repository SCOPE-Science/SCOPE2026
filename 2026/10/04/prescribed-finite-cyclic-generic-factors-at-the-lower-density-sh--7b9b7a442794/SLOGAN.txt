For every \(m\ge2\), an \(m\)-sheet cyclic wedge of the proximal mixing base gives an E-system with \(\mathscr M_0\)-shadowing whose maximal equicontinuous generic factor is exactly the \(m\)-cycle, even though that cycle is not an ordinary topological factor. Its power \(f_m^r\) is transitive exactly when \(\gcd(r,m)=1\), so these systems realize arbitrary finite cyclic generic obstructions at the sharp endpoint \(\alpha=0\).
