For a prime \(\ell\), a prime \(p\), and squarefree \(m\) coprime to \(p\), the number \(p^{\ell}m\) is never exponential harmonic of type 1, while it is of type 2 exactly when \(p^{\ell-1}+1\mid2m\). Thus the type-2 stratum is either empty or a squarefree-multiple family generated by \(M=(p^{\ell-1}+1)/\gcd(p^{\ell-1}+1,2)\).
