For vector spaces of dimensions \(m<n\) over \(\overline{\mathbb Q}\) or \(\overline{\mathbb F_p}\), the exact Ehrenfeucht--Fraïssé distinguishing round is \(m+2\), not the conjectured \(2m+1\) once \(m\ge2\). The collapse comes from the fact that any finite set of dependence coefficients lies in one finite simple extension of the prime field, so one extra scalar names them all.
