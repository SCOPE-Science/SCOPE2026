# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. Oller-Marcén's Proposition 6 specializes exactly to
\[
\ell\mid P-1
\quad\text{and}\quad
\left(m\mid Q-1\ \text{or}\ \operatorname{ord}_m(P)=\ell\right).
\]
For \(\ell\mid m-1\), cyclicity of \((\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z)^\times\) gives exactly \(\ell-1\) order-\(\ell\) classes modulo \(m\), and the Chinese remainder theorem gives exactly \(\ell-1\) relevant reduced classes modulo \(\ell m\). Fixed-modulus prime distribution then yields the stated densities. The finite checker independently agrees with the exact criterion on \(68448\) admissible ordered prime pairs.

## Originality
PASS. The closest source is Oller-Marcén's general characterization, especially Proposition 6, which gives the structural congruence/order test but does not state a density law for the fixed family \(P^{\ell-1}Q^{m-1}\). Bateman–Erdős–Pomerance–Straus give global distribution theorems for arithmetic numbers, not the leading constant on this thin family. OEIS A003601/A245656 record the global sequence and characteristic function. Focused semantic searches for the exact family, the explicit constant, and the positive-density cross-compensation phenomenon returned no covering published-finding corpus finding. The residual risk is that a later paper may have extracted the same two-block corollary in different language.

## Value
PASS. The result turns a qualitative cyclotomic characterization into an exact distribution law on a natural sparse family. The second asymptotic isolates a genuine interaction phenomenon: a positive proportion of arithmetic products acquire the missing divisor-count factor from the other prime-power block, and the absolute compensation density is the simple constant \((m-2)/(m-1)^2\). This distinguishes the family from the global density-one theorem and gives a quantitative measure of nonlocal integrality.
