A positive rational belongs to the semigroup generated by the factors \(1+1/p\) exactly when deterministic largest-prime peeling succeeds and the terminal \(2\)-\(3\) valuation pair lies in the cone \(x+2y\ge0,\ x+y\ge0\). The procedure uniquely reconstructs every exponent; in particular, the representable positive integers are exactly the \(3\)-smooth integers.
