For every prime power \(q=p^s\) of characteristic \(p\ge5\), the prime-field counterexample mechanism extends to all degree-\(p\) factors of \(U^q-U-a\): at the magic index \(q^n-1\), one has \(\deg_X g=n-1\). Each \(3\le n\le p-1\) therefore yields counterexamples on exactly \(q(q-1)/p\) primes in this Artin–Schreier construction.
