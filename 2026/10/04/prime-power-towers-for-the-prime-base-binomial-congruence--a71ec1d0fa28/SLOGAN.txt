For distinct primes \(p\ge5\) and \(q\), all prime-power solutions \(p^r\) of \(\binom{qn}{n}\equiv q^n\pmod n\) form one initial segment, whose endpoint is the p-adic distance between a binomial limit and the Teichmuller lift of \(q\). The square and cube layers are exactly the order-two and order-three Wieferich conditions.
