For the explicit unlinked-circle edge surface, the elliptic ruling does more than parameterize the lines: the normalization is the product \(E\times\mathbb P^1\). The ruling curve has \(j(E)=24918016/45\), and the product structure comes from the exact relation \(\pi_1^*\mathcal O(2)\simeq\pi_2^*\mathcal O(2)\).
