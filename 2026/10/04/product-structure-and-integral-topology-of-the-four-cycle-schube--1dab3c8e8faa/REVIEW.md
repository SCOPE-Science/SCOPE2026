# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The complement condition \(p_{12}\ne0\) makes the standard big cell global for this object. The row-reduced chart gives \(p_{14}=d\), \(p_{23}=-a\), and \(p_{34}=ad-bc\). Since \(a,d\ne0\), the substitutions \(x=b/a\) and \(y=c/d\) are regular and have the displayed regular inverse. The topology of \(\mathbb C^2\setminus\{xy=1\}\) follows from Alexander duality with \(\mathbb C^*\), and the product Betti numbers follow from integral Kunneth. The exact checker independently verifies the polynomial identities. Risk: the proof uses the standard orientation conventions for Alexander duality, but only the resulting free ranks in degrees \(1\) and \(2\) are needed and those follow from \(H_c^1(\mathbb C^*)\cong H_c^2(\mathbb C^*)\cong\mathbb Z\).

## Originality
PASS with a stated residual literature risk. The lead source explicitly identifies the four-coordinate arrangement and computes only Euler-characteristic/ML-degree data for the cycle family. Its \(d=4\) row is \(0\); the inspected section does not state the product model, integral Betti polynomial, Grothendieck class, or Hodge--Deligne polynomial. Exact and alias published-finding corpus searches returned no covering statement. Broader literature on the full open Grassmannian or torus quotients concerns the open set with all Pluecker coordinates nonzero, not this four-divisor complement. A general complexity-one torus-action description could make this chart implicit, so the originality claim is limited to the explicit named complement and these stated refinements.

## Value
PASS. The source singles out this special cycle arrangement because of its scattering/amplituhedron motivation and reports the exceptional value \(\chi=0\). The product decomposition explains that vanishing structurally, rather than recomputing the same number, and determines the full integral Betti vector and motivic/Hodge--Deligne polynomial. The fact is therefore a reusable topological description of a named special arrangement, not an arbitrary coordinate slice.
