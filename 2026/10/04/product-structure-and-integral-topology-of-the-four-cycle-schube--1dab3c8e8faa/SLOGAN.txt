The four-coordinate cycle complement \(\operatorname{Gr}_{\mathbb C}(2,4)\setminus V(p_{12}p_{14}p_{23}p_{34})\) is algebraically \((\mathbb C^*)^2\times\{xy\ne1\}\). Hence its integral Betti numbers are \((1,3,4,3,1)\), and the two torus factors give a direct geometric explanation of the previously reported Euler characteristic \(0\).
