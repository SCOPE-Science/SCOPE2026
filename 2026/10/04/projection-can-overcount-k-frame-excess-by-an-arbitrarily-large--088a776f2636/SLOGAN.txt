For every closed-range \(K\), projecting a \(K\)-frame onto \(R(K)\) can only increase its deletion excess, and the increase can be as large as \(N-1\) already for an orthonormal basis of \(\mathbb R^N\) with rank-one \(K\). The projected-excess identity remains valid for near \(K\)-Riesz bases and for frames whose atoms already lie in \(R(K)\), but not for arbitrary \(K\)-frames.
