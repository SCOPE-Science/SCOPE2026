# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. For every isomorphism \(T\) to Euclidean space, the equivalent-norm estimate gives \(C_{\mathrm{Pt}}(X)\le(\|T\|\|T^{-1}\|)^2\); infimizing yields \(C_{\mathrm{Pt}}(X)\le d_{\mathrm{BM}}(X,\ell_2^2)^2\). Zuo's exact Ptolemy values then match the elementary identity-map upper distortions \(\sqrt2\lambda\) and \(\lambda\). The endpoint cases were checked separately and agree. No numerical approximation, finite search, or unproved computation is used.

## Originality
PASS. The 2012 primary source was read through the two relevant examples and contains the Ptolemy formulas but no Banach--Mazur statement. The 2018 primary follow-up was read in full and contains no occurrence of Banach--Mazur terminology. Searches using the norm formulas, invariant aliases, equality statement, and parameter ranges did not locate a prior exact Euclidean Banach--Mazur formula for these families. The 2010 renorming paper was inspected through its publisher preview and section snippets; a lawful full-text copy was not available during the literature check, so that incomplete comparison remains an explicit residual risk rather than being treated as a noncoverage certificate.

## Value
PASS. The result converts two natural exact Ptolemy computations into exact affine Euclidean distortions. This is stronger than observing coordinatewise norm equivalence because Banach--Mazur distance optimizes over every invertible linear map. The paired max-norm families are canonical disk/diamond and disk/square truncations, and the proof isolates a reusable saturation mechanism.

## Closest literature and limitations
Zuo (2012) is the closest source because it defines the exact two families and proves their Ptolemy constants. Llorens-Fuster--Mazcuñán-Navarro--Reich (2010) supplies general renorming bounds for Ptolemy constants. Zuo (2018) extends comparable-norm Ptolemy calculations. The finding does not classify all norms satisfying \(C_{\mathrm{Pt}}=d_{\mathrm{BM}}^2\), and an unindexed equivalent statement remains possible.
