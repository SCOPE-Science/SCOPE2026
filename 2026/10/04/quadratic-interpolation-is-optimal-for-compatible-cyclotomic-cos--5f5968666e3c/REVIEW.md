# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Compatibility for affine-linear families stabilizes all previously introduced coefficients, giving \(F_m=c+\sum_{j\le m}a_jx_j\). Exact evaluation at levels \(2,3,4\) yields the three displayed affine formulas and the identity \(2b_3=b_2+b_4\). The converse is explicit. The degree-two upper construction from arXiv:2609.27084v1 was independently reconstructed through its exceptional low-level step and its real-cyclotomic-field induction.

## Originality
PASS. The primary source proves degree at most two but does not state that degree two is minimal, nor does it give a degree-one image relation. Targeted searches for degree-one, linear, optimal, best-possible, and sharp variants in the source found no equivalent claim. The earlier compatible-family paper treats symmetric stable-range rigidity rather than this interpolation sharpness question.

## Value
PASS. The natural sharpness question for a universal quadratic interpolation theorem is whether linear compatible families already suffice. The exact first-three-level plane answers this completely for universal degree: the source bound cannot be improved to one. This is a mathematically motivated boundary result rather than an arbitrary finite computation.

## Closest literature and limitations
Closest is Vélez--Cadavid, arXiv:2609.27084v1, Theorem 2.6. It supplies the upper bound and the framework but not the lower bound. The result here does not classify all infinite sequences admitting degree-one interpolation; it only proves the exact universal minimum and the complete image on levels \(2,3,4\).
