# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The similarity theorem yields the exact infimum-over-similarities formula for \(\rho_{\mathcal E}\). Diagonal scaling gives the upper bound. For the lower bound, a norming functional for each diagonal character is completely contractive at every matrix level and produces a scalar matrix with that character value as an eigenvalue, so every similar tuple has norm at least the corresponding \(V\)-norm. Lie's theorem then gives the solvable-Lie corollary. All quantifiers and finite-dimensional hypotheses were checked, and no finite computation is used as proof.

## Originality
PASS. The focal 2026 paper was inspected through its definition, similarity theorem, and headline commuting theorem; its searchable full text contains no triangularizable, solvable-Lie, or commutator formulation. The 2025 source supplies the similarity characterization but does not state the diagonal-character classification. Targeted database searches for simultaneously triangularizable tuples, upper-triangular formulas, solvable Lie algebras, and quantization dependence returned no covering statement. Beltiţă's 2001 solvable-Lie spectral-radius theory is the strongest conceptual near-match, but it concerns distinct analytic/algebraic/geometric radii rather than \(\rho_{\mathcal E}\) for arbitrary operator-space quantizations.

## Value
PASS. The focal literature draws a boundary between commuting tuples, where quantization is invisible, and general tuples, where it can matter. The result moves that boundary to the strictly larger and algebraically natural class of simultaneously triangularizable tuples and identifies non-solvability as a necessary mechanism for quantization dependence. The noncommuting two-by-two example shows that this is not merely a restatement of the commuting theorem.

## Closest literature and limitations
Scherer--Shalit--Shamovich (arXiv:2605.09354v1) prove the commuting formula and general quantization-dependence examples. Shalit--Shamovich (arXiv:2501.01325) prove the simultaneous-similarity criterion used in the proof. Beltiţă (DOI:10.4064/sm144-2-4) treats other joint spectral radii for solvable Lie algebras. The present statement is finite-dimensional over \(\mathbb C\), concerns only \(\rho_{\mathcal E}\), and gives no converse to the non-solvability obstruction.
