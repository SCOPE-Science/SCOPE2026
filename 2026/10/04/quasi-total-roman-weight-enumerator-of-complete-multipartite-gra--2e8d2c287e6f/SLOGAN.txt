Quasi-total Roman functions on a connected complete multipartite graph split into three exact regimes according to whether label \(2\) occurs in zero, one, or at least two parts. This yields a closed weight enumerator and total-function count; the resulting minimum formula \(\gamma_{qtR}(G)=\min\{N,\min_i n_i+2,4\}\) is treated as a prior-implied corollary rather than the novelty.
