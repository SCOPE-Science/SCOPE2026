For every fixed \(d\ge2\), the number of spanning-tree isomorphism classes in \(K_{d,m}\) is exactly a degree-\(d-1\) quasipolynomial in \(m\) for all \(m>d\), with period dividing \(\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,d)\). A finite core decomposition plus Burnside orbit counting gives an exact rational generating formula and recovers the known leading constant.
