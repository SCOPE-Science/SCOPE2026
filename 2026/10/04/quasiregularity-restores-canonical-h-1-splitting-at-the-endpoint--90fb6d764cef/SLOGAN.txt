Weak harmonic quasiregularity restores the canonical analytic/co-analytic \(H^1\) splitting at the endpoint: if \(f=h+\overline g\in\mathbf h^1\) satisfies the weak \((K,K_0)\)-quasiregular differential inequality, then \(h,g\in H^1\) with an explicit radial bound. This is genuinely endpoint-specific, since ordinary harmonic \(\mathbf h^1\) admits Poisson-kernel examples whose canonical components are not in \(H^1\).
