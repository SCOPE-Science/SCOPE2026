# Verification

The exact checker `artifacts/verify.py` verifies the divisor classes on the nine-point blow-up, the three \((-3)\)-curves and their pairwise intersections, \(M^2=7\), \(K_Y^2=0\), \(K_Y\cdot M=-3\), the discrepancy \(-\frac13\), canonical index \(3\), \(K_{X_B}^2=1\), and sectional genus \(3\). It also checks that the Hilbert numerator dictated by the published minimal free resolution factors as \((1-t)^3(1+3t+3t^2)\), consistent with a three-dimensional Cohen--Macaulay coordinate ring of degree \(7\).

Normality itself uses two inspected source facts not generated by the checker: the displayed minimal free resolution and the statement that the surface has exactly three singular points. With those inputs, Auslander--Buchsbaum gives \(S_2\), the finite singular locus gives \(R_1\), and Serre's criterion gives normality. The analytic identification with \(\frac13(1,1)\) uses the standard Hirzebruch--Jung classification, independently cross-checked in Iyama--Wemyss.
