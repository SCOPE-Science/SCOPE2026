# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof starts from the exact channel parameters in equations (4.6a)–(4.6b) of arXiv:2207.03713v1. On \(\gamma=0\), their discriminant becomes \(|\alpha\beta-4|\), so the two values are exactly \(\sqrt2/\alpha\) and \(\beta/(2\sqrt2)\), with labels exchanged across \(\alpha\beta=4\). Theorem 6.2 and Theorem 6.3 then give the spectral and sub-threshold multiplicity classification. Theorem 7.2 plus the Jacobi count asymptotic used in Theorem 7.3 yields the two-scale bicritical law with a bounded error that is negligible against the diverging sum.

## Originality
PASS. The closest source is the full four-parameter paper itself. It contains the unsimplified channel formulas and selected critical asymptotics, but the inspected text does not state the unordered diagonal factorization, the rectangular two-parameter phase portrait, or the unrestricted sum law at the bicritical corner. The earlier \(\delta\) and \(\delta'\) papers cover the two coordinate thresholds separately. Targeted searches found no stronger statement implying the combined result.

Residual risk: this short diagonal simplification could exist in unindexed notes or under parity-decomposition terminology.

## Value
PASS. The condition \(\gamma=0\) is the canonical no-mixing slice, not an arbitrary parameter choice. Its exact factorization shows that the apparently coupled transition reduces to two independent physical thresholds and that \(\alpha\beta=4\) is only a relabeling locus. The complete phase portrait and the two-scale corner divergence provide a reusable structural description of the model.
