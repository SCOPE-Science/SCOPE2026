# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The product modality is exactly the upward closure of rectangles U times V, while the iterated modality is exactly the set of subsets whose G-large vertical sections form an F-large set. Rectangle membership always implies the section condition. If rectangularization holds, the section witnesses can be uniformized to a common rectangle; conversely, equality of the two filters applied to the union of the chosen vertical fibers produces the rectangularization witnesses after intersecting with Q. Finite filters contain their total intersection and are therefore principal, so the condition holds in both orders. In the infinite example, every vertical section is cofinite and contains zero, while no single cofinite vertical set works for all horizontal coordinates. The verifier exhausts all principal filters through three points per factor as a corroborative finite check.

## originality

PASS

Aghamov–Kudinov–Nguyen–Piribauer introduce the trimodal full-product semantics and prove only the forward mix implication from the product modality to both iterated coordinate modalities. Their completeness construction deliberately uses infinite neighborhood filters without smallest neighborhoods to separate neighborhood products from relational products, but the inspected source does not state the local reverse-implication criterion, the equality of rectangle and Fubini filters, or the explicit cofinite counterexample. The exact rectangularization characterization and its finite collapse therefore survive.

## value

PASS

The theorem identifies the exact local obstruction to eliminating the third modality, rather than merely adding another valid axiom. It also cleanly separates finite normal products, where the extra modality is redundant, from infinite reflexive products, where it need not be. This is a natural structural boundary for the newly introduced trimodal semantics.
