# Verification

For local filters \(\mathcal F\) and \(\mathcal G\), the checker uses the two exact semantic predicates
\[
P\in\mathcal R
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists U\in\mathcal F\ \exists V\in\mathcal G\ (U\times V\subseteq P)
\]
and
\[
P\in\mathcal L
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\{u:P_u\in\mathcal G\}\in\mathcal F.
\]
The symbolic proof shows \(\mathcal R\subseteq\mathcal L\) always and proves \(\mathcal L\subseteq\mathcal R\) exactly from rectangularization, with a converse construction from any rectangularization instance.

On a finite carrier every filter is principal, generated by its total intersection. The bundled `verify.py` exhaustively enumerates all principal cores and all product subsets for coordinate sizes through three, confirming
\[
\mathcal R=\mathcal L
\]
and the symmetric equality in every case.

The infinite \(\mathsf T\)-counterexample is verified symbolically in `RESULT.md`: every vertical section contains a cofinite neighborhood, but no one cofinite vertical set forms a rectangle across all horizontal points.

## Limits

The finite enumeration is corroborative only. The pointwise iff theorem and the infinite boundary are proved directly and are not extrapolated from computation.
