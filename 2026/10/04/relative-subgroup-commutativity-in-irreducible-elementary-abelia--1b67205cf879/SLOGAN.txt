For an irreducible Frobenius group \(C_p^r\rtimes C_q\) with \(r\ge2\), the entire relative subgroup commutativity function is explicit: proper kernel subspaces are controlled only by dimension, the kernel and complements have separate closed values, and \(sd(G)=(s^2+2s+7p^r+1)/(s+p^r+1)^2\), where \(s\) is the number of subspaces of \(\mathbf F_p^r\).
