At half flux, the narrow preferred-orientation square-lattice bands have an explicit fixed-energy scaling limit around \((n\pi)^2\): after recentering, they converge to \([-2\sqrt2,-2]\cup[2,2\sqrt2]\). The four band edges admit explicit \(N_n^{-2}\) corrections, sharpening the previously known band-width and gap-width asymptotics into a complete local spectral profile.
