# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof starts from the source's normalized real Gaussian wavefunction and reconstructs the replica Gaussian integral. Local orthogonal transformations split off party-relative pure modes, leaving the three collective block modes with kernel \(W_3=\alpha I_3+evv^{\mathsf T}\). The exact cancellation of Gaussian normalization gives \(Z_n^{(3)}=\lambda^{n^2/2}/\sqrt{\det(I+tK_3)}\). The key spectral step is rigorous: \(K_3\) is the sum of two positive semidefinite operators of norm \(N\); equality at \(2N\) requires membership in both top eigenspaces, hence invariance under both cyclic replica shifts, whose common fixed space is one-dimensional. The bipartite argument is identical with one cycle. The expansion of the source's \(e^-(a,N)\) gives \(\lambda=(N-1)/(N^2a^2)+O(a^{-4})\). These facts determine the logarithmic powers and the \(O(a^{-2})\) remainder. The bundled finite checker reproduces source Table 1 cases but is not treated as proof.

## Originality
PASS. The 2026 source explicitly labels the all-\(n\), all-\(N\) large-squeezing law as a conjecture and supplies only small-\(N\) exact cases plus a special \(n=3\) expansion. Current exact-title, formula, replica-determinant and localization searches found no later proof. The 2008 unitary-localization theorem for multisymmetric Gaussian states is structurally relevant but concerns residual contangle and does not analyze genuine multi-entropy replica determinants. The present simple-top-eigenvalue argument supplies the missing implication from collective-mode localization to the universal replica exponent.

## Value
PASS. This resolves an explicit recent conjecture in a natural entanglement diagnostic, establishes the leading behavior for all integer replica orders \(n\ge3\) and arbitrary mode partitions, strengthens the statement to an \(O(a^{-2})\) expansion, and gives a finite determinant formula for the partition-dependent constant. It also removes growth with the total physical mode count from the essential determinant by identifying the three collective modes, clarifying why the leading term is partition independent while the constant is not.

The closest limitation is that the result remains at integer replica order and does not solve the analytic continuation to \(n=1\). An equivalent argument under older multi-invariant terminology remains a residual literature risk, but no covering statement was located.
