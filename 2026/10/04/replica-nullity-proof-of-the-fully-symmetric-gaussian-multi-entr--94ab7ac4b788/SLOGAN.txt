For every integer \(n\ge3\), every finite fully symmetric \(N\)-mode Gaussian state and every nonempty tripartition have the conjectured large-squeezing law \(\mathrm{GM}^{(3)}_n=(2-n)(2n)^{-1}\log a+C+O(a^{-2})\). The proof reduces the replica determinant to collective block modes and shows that its large-squeezing singularity has exactly one soft direction.
