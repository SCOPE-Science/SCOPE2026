For the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M\), restrained domination is governed exactly by four one-index boundary obstructions in the omitted vertices, yielding a closed bivariate enumerator for every restrained dominating set. In particular, \(\gamma_r(\operatorname{Cr}_n)=2\), and the only minimum sets are the \(n\) deleted-matching pairs.
