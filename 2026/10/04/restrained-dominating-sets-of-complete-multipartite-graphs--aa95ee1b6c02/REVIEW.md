# Review of Restrained dominating sets of complete multipartite graphs

## Correctness
PASS. In a complete multipartite graph, domination fails for a one-part-supported proper set exactly when that part is not selected in full, while the restraint condition is exactly that the complement meet at least two parts. These two observations give the stated necessary-and-sufficient criterion. The polynomial formulas are obtained by direct support-state counting and inclusion-exclusion, and the minimum cases follow immediately. A definition-level exhaustive checker agrees on all tested graph types through order ten.

## Originality
PASS. The 2024 primary paper with MSC 05C69 studies restrained domination in cubic and special subcubic graphs and contains no located complete-multipartite, complete-bipartite, or restrained-enumerator result. The inspected 2013 survey section records classical restrained-domination values and bondage results but not the present complete-multipartite classification. Targeted published-finding corpus and web searches did not locate an equivalent statement. Residual risk remains for differently phrased or non-indexed literature.

## Value
PASS. Restrained domination is a well-studied domination variant, and complete multipartite graphs are a canonical dense family. The result does more than give a minimum number: it classifies every restrained dominating set, gives the full size distribution, isolates the star obstruction in the bipartite case, and counts all minimum sets exactly.
