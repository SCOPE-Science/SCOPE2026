Two-level Richardson extrapolation of backward Euler is second order but not unconditionally positive: with refinement factor \(r\), positivity on \(y'=-\lambda y\) ends at the unique \(z_r>0\) satisfying \((1+z_r/r)^r=r(1+z_r)\). The standard choice \(r=2\) fails exactly beyond \(z=2+2\sqrt2\), and even extreme refinement improves the positivity radius only as \(\log r+\log\log r+o(1)\).
