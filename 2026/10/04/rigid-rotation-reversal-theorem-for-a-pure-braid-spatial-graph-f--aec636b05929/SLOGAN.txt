In the fixed cubic pure-braid family with \(A=\sigma_1^2\) and \(B=\sigma_2^2\), every word and its reversal define ambient-isotopic spatial graphs via an explicit half-turn. Hence reversal is a genuine geometric redundancy, and length-\(m\) words represent at most \(2^{m-1}+2^{\lceil m/2\rceil-1}\) ambient-isotopy classes.
