For the smooth self-dual antinorm \(f_p(x)=\prod_i(x_i/\sqrt{p_i})^{p_i}\), the only linear hyperplanes whose intersection is everywhere orthogonal to the antisphere are the pairwise weighted-coordinate seams \(x_i/\sqrt{p_i}=x_j/\sqrt{p_j}\). Thus this family has exactly \(\binom d2\) such seams and no non-admissible linear one.
