For a uniform composition, conditioning on more parts shifts the entire maximum-part distribution downward. An exact coordinate-influence identity yields \(-\operatorname{Cov}(K_N,M_N)=O((\log N)^2)\), hence \(\operatorname{Corr}(K_N,M_N)=O((\log N)^2/\sqrt N)\to0^-\), giving a rigorous decorrelation proof for the problem raised in later numerical work.
