In a uniform random recursive tree, forcing arrival \(j\) to attach to the root strictly lowers the final total path length in stochastic order. The exact local covariance is \(-n(H_{j-1}-1)/(j(j-1))\), and summing over arrivals gives a root-degree/path-length covariance of linear order while their correlation decays as \(1/\sqrt{\log n}\).
