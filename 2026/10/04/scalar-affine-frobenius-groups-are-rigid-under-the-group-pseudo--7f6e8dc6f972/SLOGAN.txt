For the scalar Frobenius group \(G(q,m)=(\mathbf F_q,+)\rtimes C_m\), with \(e=\operatorname{ord}_m(p)\), the group pseudo-algebra is exactly \(\{(d,\varphi(d)):d\mid m\}\cup\{(p^e,(q-1)/m)\}\). Its largest codegree, second-largest codegree, and largest-codegree multiplicity recover \(q\) and \(m\), so the pseudo-algebra determines the group within this family.
