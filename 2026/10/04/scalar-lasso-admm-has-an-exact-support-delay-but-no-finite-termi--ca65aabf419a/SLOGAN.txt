For scalar Lasso with \(b>\lambda>0\), zero-initialized unrelaxed ADMM identifies the positive support at the exact index \(j_*=\lfloor\log(b/(b-\lambda))/\log(1+\rho)\rfloor+1\), but then approaches the solution geometrically with factor \(\rho/(1+\rho)\) and never terminates at finite \(\rho\). This gives a minimal counterexample to the published finite-termination statement for the \(K=I\) anisotropic Lasso case while preserving finite activity identification.
