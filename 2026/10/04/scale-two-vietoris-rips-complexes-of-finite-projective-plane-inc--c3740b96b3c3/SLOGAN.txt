For every finite projective plane \(\Pi\) of order \(q\ge 2\), if \(G_\Pi\) is its incidence graph with the shortest-path metric, then \(\mathrm{VR}(G_\Pi;2)\simeq\bigvee^{q^3}S^2\). Consequently its positive-dimensional Vietoris–Rips persistence has exactly \(q^3\) degree-\(1\) bars with birth \(1\) and death \(2\), exactly \(q^3\) degree-\(2\) bars with birth \(2\) and death \(3\), and no other positive-dimensional bars. The scale-two complex is the suspension of the incidence graph, so the multiplicity is the cycle rank \(q^3\).
