For three uniform random points in the unit \(d\)-ball, the known tail \(\mathbb P(\rho_d>R)\sim C_dR^{-(d-1)}\) has an explicit universal second coefficient \(a_d=3d(d^2-1)(3d-1)/(2(2d+1)(3d+1)(4d+1))\). In \(d=3\) this gives \(\mathbb P(\rho_3>R)=3/(55R^2)+432/(25025R^4)+O(R^{-6})\) and an exact first correction to the Fréchet law for maxima.
