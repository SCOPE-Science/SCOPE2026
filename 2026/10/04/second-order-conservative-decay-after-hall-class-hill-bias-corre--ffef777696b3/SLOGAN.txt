For the exact Hall quantile \(U(t)=t^{1/\alpha}(1+c t^{-\beta})\), first-order Hill bias correction leaves the positive residual \(\alpha A(n/k)^2/[\beta(1+2\beta)]\) in the normalized Hill statistic. That residual produces the sharp zero-rescaling conservative exponent \(k\alpha^2A(n/k)^4/[2\beta^2(1+2\beta)^2]\), revealing the next reliability scale beyond the first-order moderate-deviation theorem.
