For the regular spherical simplex obtained by gnomonically lifting a small centered Euclidean regular simplex, the sharp spherical Grünbaum ratio approaches \((1-1/n)^{n-1}\) from above at the exact quadratic rate \((n-2)/(2n(n+1))\) in the squared spherical circumradius. This resolves the coefficient hidden inside the \(O(\rho^2)\) sharpness argument for the canonical symmetric family.
