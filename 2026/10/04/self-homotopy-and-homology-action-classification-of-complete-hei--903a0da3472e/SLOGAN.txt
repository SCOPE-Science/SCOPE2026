For every complete height-two finite space \(P_{p,q}\) with \(p,q\ge3\), the self-maps with zero first-homology action form one homotopy component and every other self-map is isolated. This gives \(|[P_{p,q},P_{p,q}]|=1+(p^p-p)(q^q-q)\), an exact homology-rank profile, and the criterion that integral \(H_1\)-isomorphisms are exactly the \(p!q!\) homeomorphisms.
