# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof uses the explicit fence lift. A monotone crown self-map has lift increments in \({-1,0,1}\), hence winding only \(-1,0,1\). Winding of absolute value \(1\) forces equality in every step and therefore an automorphism. Winding \(0\) yields a closed walk too short to cover every residue; the image lies in a crown with one point removed, an alternating fence that beat-reduces to a point. Minimal-space rigidity then makes distinct automorphisms nonhomotopic. The bundled verifier independently checks the finite boundary cases through \(n=5\).

## Originality
PASS. The closest inspected source, Cavallo--Sattler, uses exactly the same crown and winding formalism but only proves the shorter-to-longer vanishing lemma. Barmak--Minian provide the core/minimality rigidity premise but no crown self-map theorem. Targeted equivalent-formulation, broader-coverage, endomorphism, and published-findings searches found no theorem implying the exact count \(2n+1\). Residual risk remains for older order-theory literature under different terminology.

## Value
PASS. The result is a complete classification for a standard infinite family of finite weak circle models. It isolates the precise gap between weak degree information and finite-space homotopy: zero winding gives one class, whereas each of the \(2n\) symmetries survives as its own class. This is structurally motivated and reusable in comparisons of finite circle models and their mapping spaces.

## Closest literature and limitations
The archive source arXiv:math/0611156 supplies the finite-space core framework and minimal-space rigidity. DOI:10.4153/S0008414X25101466 supplies the crown/fence winding formalism and the unequal-size zero-winding lemma. The theorem here is only about self-maps; unequal-size crown homotopy classes and the full endomorphism monoid remain outside scope. Finite enumeration is corroboration, not the infinite proof.
