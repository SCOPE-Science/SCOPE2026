# Self-homotopy classification of finite weak orders with at least three levels
## Finding
Let \(h\ge 3\) and let
\[
W=A_1\oplus\cdots\oplus A_h,
\qquad |A_i|=r_i\ge 2,
\]
where each \(A_i\) is an antichain and every point of \(A_i\) is below every point of \(A_j\) when \(i<j\). Then
\[
|[W,W]|=1+\prod_{i=1}^{h}\bigl(r_i^{r_i}-r_i\bigr).
\]
There is exactly one non-isolated class: it contains every self-map that fails to preserve every level with a nonconstant restriction, and every map in this class is null-homotopic. Every map that preserves each \(A_i\) and is nonconstant on each \(A_i\) forms a singleton homotopy class.

## Assumptions and scope
Finite \(T_0\)-spaces are regarded as finite posets, so continuous maps are precisely order-preserving maps. The ordinal sum above is connected because \(h\ge 3\). Homotopy of maps is the ordinary topological homotopy of finite spaces; equivalently, it is generated by pointwise comparable order-preserving maps.

The claim concerns heights at least three and assumes every level has at least two points. Two-level cases are outside the stated new scope. No assertion is made for weak orders containing singleton levels, because those spaces are already contractible.

## Proof
Fix a continuous self-map \(f:W\to W\) and write \(I=f(W)\). For each target level put \(B_j=I\cap A_j\), omitting empty \(B_j\).

First suppose every occupied \(B_j\) has at least two points. Pick two distinct points in one such \(B_j\). Their preimages cannot lie in two different source levels: points in different source levels are comparable, whereas two distinct points in the same target antichain are incomparable. More strongly, if a source level \(A_i\) has even one image point in \(B_j\), then every distinct point of \(B_j\) must also have a preimage in \(A_i\); otherwise the same comparability contradiction occurs. Hence every occupied target level \(j\) is assigned to a unique source level \(i(j)\), and every source level occurs among these assignments because its image is nonempty. Thus the number of occupied target levels is at least \(h\). There are only \(h\) target levels, so all levels are occupied and \(j\mapsto i(j)\) is a bijection. Monotonicity makes this bijection order-preserving, hence \(i(j)=j\). Therefore \(f(A_i)\subseteq A_i\) and \(|f(A_i)|\ge2\) for every \(i\).

Conversely, if \(f\) is not level-preserving and nonconstant on every level, the preceding argument shows that some occupied \(B_j\) is a singleton. If only one target level is occupied, connectedness of the image forces that level to be a singleton as well. The induced subposet \(I\) is an ordinal sum of its nonempty level intersections, so its order complex is the join of the corresponding discrete sets. A singleton factor makes this join a cone. Hence \(I\) is contractible, and the factorization \(W\xrightarrow{f}I\hookrightarrow W\) shows that \(f\) is null-homotopic.

Now let \(f\) preserve every level and be nonconstant on every level. The order complex of \(W\) is
\[
\Delta(W)=A_1*\cdots*A_h.
\]
Over \(\mathbb Q\), its top reduced homology is naturally the tensor product
\[
\widetilde H_{h-1}(\Delta(W);\mathbb Q)
\cong
\bigotimes_{i=1}^{h}\widetilde H_0(A_i;\mathbb Q).
\]
A nonconstant function on a finite discrete set induces a nonzero map on reduced degree-zero homology: if two points have distinct images, their difference has nonzero image. Therefore the map induced by \(f\) on top rational homology is a tensor product of nonzero linear maps and is itself nonzero.

Suppose a self-map \(g\) is pointwise comparable with such an \(f\). Comparable maps of finite spaces are homotopic, so \(g\) induces the same nonzero map on top homology. The previous image argument therefore forces \(g\) also to preserve every level and be nonconstant there. But within one antichain two comparable points are equal. Hence pointwise comparability of \(f\) and \(g\) gives \(f=g\). Thus \(f\) has no distinct comparable neighbor in the finite map poset, so its homotopy class is a singleton.

Finally, on a level of size \(r_i\), exactly \(r_i^{r_i}-r_i\) self-functions are nonconstant. Choices on distinct levels are independent, giving \(\prod_i(r_i^{r_i}-r_i)\) isolated non-null classes, plus the single null class.

## Verification
The symbolic argument proves the statement for all permitted parameters. The accompanying `verify.py` is supplementary finite evidence, not an infinite proof. It exhaustively enumerates all monotone self-maps for level sizes \((2,2,2)\) and \((2,2,3)\). For \((2,2,2)\) it finds \(446\) self-maps and exactly \(9\) homotopy components: one component of size \(438\) and eight singleton components, matching
\[
1+(2^2-2)^3=9.
\]
For \((2,2,3)\) it finds \(2507\) self-maps and exactly \(96\) maps of the isolated type, matching
\[
(2^2-2)(2^2-2)(3^3-3)=96.
\]
The verifier also checks, for every enumerated map, that every map outside the isolated type has a contractible image of the form used in the proof. Its recorded terminal status is `VERIFY_OK`.

## Relationship to prior work
Barmak and Minian give the finite-poset model, recall Stong's beat-point and core theory, and state that continuous maps between finite spaces are exactly order-preserving maps. Their work establishes the homotopy-theoretic setting but does not classify self-map homotopy classes of arbitrary layered ordinal sums.

A later paper on pseudospheres explicitly observes that pseudospheres are ordinal sums of trivial posets, showing that the present family is a standard topological-combinatorial object rather than an ad hoc parameter slice. That paper studies order-complex and matroid structure, not the self-map homotopy set.

The closest previously checked result for the same family characterizes when a self-map acts nontrivially on top homology, but it does not determine the null-homotopy component or the cardinality of \([W,W]\). The present proof rederives the needed homology implication from the image structure and adds the full homotopy-component classification.

## Limitations
The theorem is restricted to \(h\ge3\) and \(r_i\ge2\). It does not classify mapping spaces between two different weak orders, nor does it describe the internal homotopy type of the large null component beyond path-connectedness in the map poset. The finite enumerations only test small cases and are not used to justify the general theorem.
