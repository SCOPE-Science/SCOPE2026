# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

If a part used two colors, no vertex of that part could see the other same-part color, and those colors occur nowhere outside the part by properness; thus none of those color classes would have a color-dominating vertex. Hence every b-coloring is exactly one distinct color per part, and every vertex is color-dominating. Realization therefore reduces to matching requirements to part capacities; for two sorted sequences the matching criterion is coordinatewise domination, and nested eligible sets give the product count. Fresh enumeration of K_(3,2,1) reproduces the rigidity and the predicted labeled count.
