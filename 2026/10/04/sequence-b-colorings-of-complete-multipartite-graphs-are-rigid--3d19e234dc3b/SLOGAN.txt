For a complete multipartite graph \(K_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}\), sequence \(b\)-coloring is rigid: every \(b\)-coloring is the part coloring, so the realized finite sequences are exactly the length-\(r\) sequences bounded coordinatewise by \((n_1,\ldots,n_r)\). Thus the unique maximal realized sequence recovers the entire multipartite part-size partition.
