For a fixed positive negative-multinomial cell, the ordinary shape coordinate is completely monotone only for the zero-count cell. Every nonzero cell is strictly log-concave with a unique shape mode \(r_{m,q}\), depending only on total count \(m\) and stopping probability \(q\), and \(r_{m,q}=q m/(1-q)+1/2-(1-q^2)/(24qm)+O(m^{-2})\).
