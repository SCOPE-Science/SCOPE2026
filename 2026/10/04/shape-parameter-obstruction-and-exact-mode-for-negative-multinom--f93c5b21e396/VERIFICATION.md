# Verification

The cell probability was reduced exactly to an exponential times a rising factorial. Differentiating its logarithm yields the finite harmonic equation displayed in RESULT.md; its strict decrease and endpoint limits verify the unique-maximizer statement for every positive integer total count.

For the asymptotic coefficient, substituting \(r=am+1/2+b/m\) into the standard digamma expansion gives the \(m^{-2}\) coefficient
\[
-\frac{24a^2b+24ab+2a+1}{24a^2(a+1)^2}.
\]
It vanishes exactly at \(b=-(2a+1)/(24a(a+1))\), which equals \(-(1-q^2)/(24q)\) when \(a=q/(1-q)\). The derivative of the mode equation is of order \(-m^{-1}\), so an \(O(m^{-3})\) residual gives an \(O(m^{-2})\) root error.
