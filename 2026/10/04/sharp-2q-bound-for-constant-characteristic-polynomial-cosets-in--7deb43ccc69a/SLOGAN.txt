Over every odd finite field \(\mathbb F_q\), a subgroup \(H\leq\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb F_q)\) whose coset \(xH\) has one characteristic polynomial is forced to be abelian and has \(|H|\leq2q\). The bound is sharp exactly for the central extension of a full unipotent root subgroup, with an explicit simultaneous-conjugacy description of the equality pairs.
