For \(X=\mathbb P(1^r,a,b)\), the canonical anticanonical volume is at most \(6^r r^{r-1}\), with one extremizer for \(r\ge3\) and exactly two when \(r=2\). The terminal subfamily has sharp maximum \(64\) at \(\mathbb P(1,1,1,1)\) for \(r=2\), and \((6r-5)^{r+1}/(6(r-1)^2)\) uniquely at \(\mathbb P(1^r,2r-2,3r-3)\) for \(r\ge3\).
