# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For a unit vector and a fixed partition, write \(x,y\) for the two selected energy pieces and \(u,v\) for their complementary dual pieces. Dual reconstruction gives \(1\le\sqrt{xu}+\sqrt{yv}\), while the Bessel bounds give \(u\le B_\Gamma-y\) and \(v\le B_\Lambda-x\). Cauchy--Schwarz therefore yields \(1\le\sqrt{E(S-E)}\), where \(E=x+y\), forcing \(E\ge(S-\sqrt{S^2-4})/2\). For sharpness, choose two real coefficient vectors \(\lambda,\gamma\) with squared norms \(B_\Lambda,B_\Gamma\) and dot product one. The symmetric matrix \(\lambda\lambda^{\mathsf T}+(J\gamma)(J\gamma)^{\mathsf T}\) has trace \(S\) and determinant one; rotating to an eigenbasis makes a mixed weaving attain its smaller eigenvalue, exactly \(A_*\). This also verifies admissibility requires \(B_\Lambda B_\Gamma\ge1\).
