Integer support sharpens Cantelli exactly: if \(\mathbb E X=0\), \(\operatorname{Var}(X)=v\), \(X\in\mathbb Z\), and \(j=\lfloor v/k\rfloor\), then \(\Pr(X\ge k)\le [v+j(j+1)]/[(k+j)(k+j+1)]\). The extremizer is the unique moment-matching law on \(\{-(j+1),-j,k\}\), and the improvement over Cantelli is explicit.
