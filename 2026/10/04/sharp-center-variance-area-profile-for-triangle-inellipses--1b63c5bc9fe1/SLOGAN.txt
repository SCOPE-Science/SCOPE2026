For a triangle inellipse, fixing the affine barycentric spread \(D\) of its center from the centroid determines an exact sharp interval of possible normalized areas, with explicit two-equal-coordinate extremizers and a phase change at \(D=1/8\). In particular, the Steiner area deficit controls center spread by the globally sharp inequality \(1-\operatorname{area}(E)/A_S\ge 2D\).
