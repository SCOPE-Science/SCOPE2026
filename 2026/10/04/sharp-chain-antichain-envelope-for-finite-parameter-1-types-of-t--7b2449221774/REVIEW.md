Splitting off empty-side pairs gives \(c(P)=2J(P)-1+q(P)\). The union map injects nonempty separated pairs into non-antichain subsets, yielding the upper bound; empty/singleton/incomparable-pair antichains and comparable singleton pairs yield the lower bound. The equality classification follows immediately from positive incomparability/comparability gaps.
