Core LARS has a sharp condition-number-dependent trust frontier on SPD quadratics: universal one-step descent holds exactly up to \(\rho_\star(\kappa)=3\sqrt{3}\,\kappa(\kappa+1)/(\kappa^2+\kappa+1)^{3/2}\). A two-eigenvalue mixture attains the boundary, and the safe trust decays as \(3\sqrt{3}/\kappa\) for ill-conditioned layers.
