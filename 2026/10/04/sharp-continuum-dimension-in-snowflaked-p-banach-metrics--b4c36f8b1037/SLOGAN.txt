In a point-separating \(p\)-Banach space with \(0<p<1\), the snowflaked metric \(d_p(x,y)=\|x-y\|^p\) forces every nondegenerate connected set to have Hausdorff dimension at least \(1/p\), and affine segments attain equality. Without point separation the threshold can collapse to \(1\), as the canonical indicator path in \(L_p[0,1]\) shows.
