For the maximum-amplitude singular cycle in the controlled entry-exit toy model, the normalized entry amplitude is strictly increasing with \(R=\sqrt{u_M/u_m}\), but near equal control bounds the fast-variable peak grows only as \(\frac98(R-1)^2\). At large contrast the entry point approaches \(-b\) exponentially while the normalized peak grows as \(R-\log R+o(1)\).
