For fixed canonical component sizes \(0\le u<v\), the Lin--Spreer torus-knot construction has a sharp crossing-number interval from \((v-u)(u+2)^2\) to \(F_{u+3}((v-u)F_{u+3}+F_{u+2}-1)\), with unique extremal knots.
