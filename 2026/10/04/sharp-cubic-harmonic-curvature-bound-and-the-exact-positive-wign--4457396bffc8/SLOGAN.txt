Degree-three spherical harmonics satisfy the sharp dimension-free curvature estimate \(\|\nabla^2q+q\,\mathrm{Id}\|_{\infty,\mathrm{op}}\le8\|q\|_\infty\). For the positive four-dimensional Wigner cubic this makes the source's symbolic convexity constant explicit: \(M_+=16/(3\sqrt3)\), so the exact constant-width deformation range is \(|\varepsilon|\le3\sqrt3\,r/16\).
