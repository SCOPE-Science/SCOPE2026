For convex bodies whose support function has Fourier degree at most \(4\), constant Ou--Pan 3-order width \(\Lambda\) forces the sharp area bound \(A\ge77\pi\Lambda^2/1053\). The unique extremal shape up to rigid motion and translation has centered support \(p(\theta)=\frac{\Lambda}{3}(1+\frac{4\sqrt{190}}{117}\cos2\theta-\frac4{117}\cos4\theta)\), giving an exact finite-mode benchmark for the generalized Blaschke--Lebesgue problem.
