For Cassinian Reuleaux rectangles, the square’s corner-controlled \(3\)-diameter mechanism already fails on the exact aspect-ratio interval \(1/\sqrt{15}\le a<\alpha\), where \(\alpha=(2^{1/3}-1)/\sqrt{1-(2^{1/3}-1)^2}\). An explicit boundary point and two opposite corners produce a strictly larger triple product than every three-corner triple.
