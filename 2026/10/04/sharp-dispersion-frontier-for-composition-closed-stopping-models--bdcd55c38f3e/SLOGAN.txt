For composition-closed positive-integer stopping models with dual count \(J\), the whole finite-variance curve is \(\operatorname{Var}(N_\theta)=\bigl(\mathbb E[J^2]/\mathbb E[J]\bigr)\mathbb E[N_\theta](\mathbb E[N_\theta]-1)\). At fixed growth exponent \(a=\mathbb E[J]\), the unique least-variable dual law is concentrated on the two adjacent integers around \(a\), giving the exact sharp dispersion floor.
