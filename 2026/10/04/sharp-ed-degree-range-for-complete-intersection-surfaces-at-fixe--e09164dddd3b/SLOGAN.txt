For general complete-intersection surfaces of fixed codimension and fixed total defining degree, the generic Euclidean distance degree is uniquely maximized by the most balanced multidegree and uniquely minimized by \((2,\ldots,2,S-2c+2)\). Because \(K_X\cong\mathcal O_X(S-c-3)\), this gives a sharp nearest-point complexity range at fixed canonical polarization exponent.
