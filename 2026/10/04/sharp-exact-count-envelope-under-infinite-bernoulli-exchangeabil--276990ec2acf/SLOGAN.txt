For an infinitely exchangeable Bernoulli sequence with fixed marginal mean, the probability of exactly \(k\) successes has a complete sharp interval: zero is always attainable, while the maximum is the least concave majorant of the binomial point mass. The iid law is the maximizer only for \((k-1)/(n-1)\le\mu\le k/(n-1)\); outside that window a two-atom latent mixture does better.
