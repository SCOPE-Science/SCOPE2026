For the explicit sofic shift where the exact Mañé lemma fails, allowing slack \(\varepsilon\) restores Lipschitz subactions but forces an exponential metric cost. The optimal seminorm satisfies \(\lim_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}\varepsilon\log\Lambda_\alpha(\varepsilon)=\log(1/\alpha)\), giving a sharp quantitative form of the obstruction.
