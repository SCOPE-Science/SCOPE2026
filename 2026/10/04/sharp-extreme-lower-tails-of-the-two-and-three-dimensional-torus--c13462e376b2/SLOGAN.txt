For the explicit centered-exponential torus matching limits, the extreme lower tail changes regime with dimension: \(\log\log(1/\Pr(L_2\le-x))\sim4\pi x\), whereas \(-\log\Pr(L_3\le-x)\sim(8\pi^2/3)x^3\). The corresponding lower quantiles grow like \(-\log\log(1/\alpha)/(4\pi)\) in dimension two and \(-[3\log(1/\alpha)/(8\pi^2)]^{1/3}\) in dimension three.
