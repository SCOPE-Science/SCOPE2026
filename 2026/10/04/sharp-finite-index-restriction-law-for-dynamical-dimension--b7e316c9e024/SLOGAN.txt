Restriction to a finite-index subgroup can enlarge Meyerovitch's dynamical dimension by at most the subgroup index, and this factor is attained exactly by cyclic-layer examples. In particular, \(\dim(X,F)\leq\dim(X,F^q)\leq q\,\dim(X,F)\) for every homeomorphism \(F\) and integer \(q\geq1\), with sharp upper coefficient.
