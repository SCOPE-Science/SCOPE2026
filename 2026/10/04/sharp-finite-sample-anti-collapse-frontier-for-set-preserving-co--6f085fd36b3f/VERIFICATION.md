The proof is algebraic. For any exact set-preserving grid calibrator, the rejected ranks consume at least \(k/\alpha\) of the total null-mean budget \(N\), leaving at most \(N-k/\alpha\) total e-value mass across the \(N-k\) accepted ranks. Therefore their common lower floor cannot exceed the displayed average residual mass.

`verify_floor.py` checks the formula, extremal exactness, strict threshold separation, random feasible perturbations, and the integer lattice boundary over many rational parameter choices. These checks are supplementary; finite enumeration is not used as proof.

Scientific limits: tie-free discrete conformal ranks, one prespecified threshold, exact null mean, and worst-rank floor only.
