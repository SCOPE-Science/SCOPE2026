# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The frontier is rewritten as an infimum of affine functions of the short side, proving concavity without numerics. The zero-width minimizer is unique and nondegenerate. Evaluating at that orientation gives the global tangent bound, and differentiating the implicit minimizer gives the exact quadratic coefficient. The classification theorem converts the analytic frontier into the exact maximum long side in all three ladder-limited corridors for sufficiently small width. The packaged symbolic checker reproduces the algebraic identities and is not used in place of the analytic proof.

## Originality
PASS. The lead 2026 classification gives the raw frontier, the classical endpoint, and the symmetric exact special case, but not the sharp supporting line, the strict anisotropic equality classification, or the endpoint curvature. The closest older finite-width treatment gives a sextic equation rather than these consequences, and the classical ladder literature is confined to zero width. Searches over direct, inverse, asymptotic, concavity, and cube-root-width formulations found no covering statement. The main residual risk is an unindexed derivation from the older sextic.

## Value
PASS. The result gives a quantitative robustness law at the classical ladder threshold. Its linear coefficient is globally sharp, while the quadratic term detects corridor anisotropy and vanishes exactly in the symmetric case. This is a natural structural property of the exact finite-width feasibility boundary, rather than an arbitrary numerical refinement.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest sources are arXiv:2604.01174v1 for the complete corridor classification and inverse frontier, Boute 2004 for the finite-width sextic formulation, Moretti 2002 for the earlier calculus treatment, and Kalman 2007 for the zero-width ladder. The expansion is local at zero width, though the tangent bound on the frontier is global. Only rectangles in the three ladder-limited planar corridors are covered.
