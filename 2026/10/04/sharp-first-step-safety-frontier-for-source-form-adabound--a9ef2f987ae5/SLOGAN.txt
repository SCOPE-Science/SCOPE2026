AdaBound's dynamic upper bound is not automatically a stable bound: on a scalar quadratic, universal first-step descent holds exactly when \((1-\beta_1)h u_1\le2\). For the source-recommended bounds with \(\beta_1=0.9,\ \beta_2=0.999,\ \alpha^\star=0.1\), curvature \(h=1\) gives an upper-clipped first-step objective amplification of \(81.1801\).
