Three-candidate Baldwin voting is tie-independently strategyproof through four voters, but at five voters exactly \(6\) anonymous profiles—one candidate-relabeling orbit—are manipulable. After normalizing the manipulator to \(A\succ B\succ C\), every minimal event is \((2,0,0,1,2,0)\) with the unique profitable report \(BCA\), changing the winner from \(C\) to \(B\).\n
