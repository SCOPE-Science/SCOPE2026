# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reconstructs the exact section-density ratio in the one-positive chamber and reduces the fixed-angle problem to maximizing \(\prod_i(1-\kappa v_i)\) under zero-sum and unit-second-moment constraints. A quadratic Hermite interpolant to \(\log(1-\kappa x)\), with negative third derivative, gives the global product bound and exact equality set. The angular endpoint is obtained by directly checking when the equality geodesic reaches a coordinate wall.

## Originality
PASS. The 2026 minimum theorem gives the global minimizer but not a fixed-angle profile. König and Dirksen provide formulas and earlier extremal information without this radial envelope. The closest located quantitative result gives only \(V/V_{\min}\ge\sec\theta\), a global near-minimizer threshold, and the Hessian. The new statement strictly sharpens that inequality for every positive angle in its range and classifies the fixed-angle equality directions. Searches for equivalent fixed-angle, radial-envelope, product-extremal, and secant formulations did not locate broader coverage.

## Value
PASS. The result upgrades a qualitative minimum and a nonsharp angular stability bound into the exact finite-angle lower envelope in the natural sign chamber, including equality classification and the first asymmetric term beyond the Hessian. The profile is usable for quantitative near-extremal geometry rather than being merely another Taylor coefficient.
