For unit central Kirchhoff noise on an equilateral \(N\)-edge star, the known subcritical threshold \(\alpha<1/4\) is sharp: the critical \(\mathcal H_{1/4}\) spectral energy diverges as \((N\pi)^{-1}\log M\). The exact coefficient shows a \(1/N\) attenuation of ultraviolet energy by the star degree.
