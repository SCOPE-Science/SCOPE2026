On the Gorenstein canonical/non-terminal boundary of \(\mathbb P(1^r,a,b)\), the associated reflexive simplex has an explicit closed \(h^*\)-polynomial; non-unimodality occurs exactly when \(t\ge3\) is odd with \(r\ge2t\), or \(t\ge6\) is even with \(r\ge3t/2\), while \(\mathbb P(1^r,r,r)\) is always unimodal.
