For iid valid two-point null e-values, base e-BH has an exact complete-null FDR envelope given by a finite binomial-tail maximization. At every \(\alpha<1/(e^2+1/2)\), the unique worst calibration is the one-spike law \(p=\alpha/K\), \(E=K/\alpha\) on success, and its FDR tends to \(1-e^{-\alpha}<\alpha\).
