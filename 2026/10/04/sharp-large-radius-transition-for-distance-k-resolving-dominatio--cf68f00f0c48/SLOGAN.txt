For the balanced spider \(S_{q,L}\), distance-\(k\) resolving domination has a sharp large-radius phase transition: it equals \(q\) for \(\lceil L/2\rceil\le k\le L\) and drops to the metric dimension \(q-1\) exactly at \(k=L+1\). After that drop, every optimum is a metric basis whose nearest selected depth is at most \(k-L\), giving an exact count of all minimum sets.
