For every pair in the full spherical Alexandrov--Fenchel family, the deficit Hessian at a geodesic ball is the same Poincaré quadratic form up to the factor \((k-\ell)d_{k+1}\cot^kR/n\). Its only zero modes change the ball's radius or center, and after removing them the sharp quadratic gap is attained by degree-two spherical harmonics.
