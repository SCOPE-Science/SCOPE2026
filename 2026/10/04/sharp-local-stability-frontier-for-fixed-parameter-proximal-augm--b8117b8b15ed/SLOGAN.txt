On a scalar normal negative-curvature mode satisfying the standard composite P-ALM assumptions, strong convexity of every primal subproblem does not by itself guarantee local convergence. The exact fixed-parameter stability region is \(\rho>a\) and \(4/\gamma+\rho>2a\), and for fixed \(\rho>a\) the unique rate-optimal choice is \(\gamma_\star=4\rho/a^2\) with factor \(a/(2\rho-a)\).
