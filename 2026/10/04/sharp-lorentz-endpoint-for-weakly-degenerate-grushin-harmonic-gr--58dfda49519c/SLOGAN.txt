In the one-dimensional weakly degenerate Grushin regime, the sharp strong harmonic-gradient threshold \(p<1/\alpha\) has the exact endpoint replacement \(L^{1/\alpha,\infty}\). The singular Friedrichs profile \(\operatorname{sgn}(x)|x|^{1-2\alpha}\) proves that this Lorentz exponent is optimal and that strong \(L^{1/\alpha}\) still fails.
