For the standard two-sample Loopless SVRG refresh \(p=1/2\), a heterogeneous scalar quadratic with relative curvature spread \(h\) is mean-square stable exactly for \(0<\eta\mu<[1+\sqrt{9+32h^2}]/[2(1+4h^2)]\). The threshold is independent of noninterpolation noise in the component gradients and is much sharper than the general sufficient stepsize bound.
