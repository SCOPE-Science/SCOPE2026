For Gaussian \(K\)-fold universal likelihood-ratio averaging, the raw e-value has the exact critical moment exponent \(q_K=(1+\sqrt{4K-3})/2\): variance exists exactly from \(K=4\) onward, while balanced two-fold cross-fitting has the golden-ratio boundary \((1+\sqrt5)/2\). The log statistic nevertheless has moments of every finite order.
