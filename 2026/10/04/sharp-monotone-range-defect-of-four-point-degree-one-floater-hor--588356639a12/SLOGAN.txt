For four equally spaced nodes, the degree-one Floater–Hormann interpolant can leave the range of bounded monotone data by exactly \(\delta=0.18441311642853\ldots\), where \(\delta\) is the unique positive root of \(15\delta^4+30\delta^3+671\delta^2+656\delta-144=0\). The sharp step extremizer and a strictly increasing positive witness show that pole-freeness does not imply shape preservation.
