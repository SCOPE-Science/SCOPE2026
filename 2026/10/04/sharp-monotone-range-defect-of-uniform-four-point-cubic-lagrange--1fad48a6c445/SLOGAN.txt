For monotone samples in \([0,M]\), the ordinary cubic Lagrange interpolant through four consecutive uniform nodes has the exact sharp central-cell range
\[
-\frac{\sqrt3}{27}M\le P\le\left(1+\frac{\sqrt3}{27}\right)M.
\]
The defect is about \(6.415\%\) of the full data range, and it persists for strictly positive, strictly increasing samples.
