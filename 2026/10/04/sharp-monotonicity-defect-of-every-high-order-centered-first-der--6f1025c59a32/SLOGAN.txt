For the classical \(2m+1\)-point centered first derivative, monotone bounded samples can force a negative derivative as soon as \(m\ge2\). The exact worst defect is
\[
-\frac{M}{h}\left[\frac{m}{m+1}-(H_{2m}-H_m)\right],
\]
and its magnitude increases with order from \(M/(12h)\) toward \((1-\log2)M/h\).
