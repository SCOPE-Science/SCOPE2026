A dephasing qubit Ising model inside the recent Lindblad propagation-of-chaos theorem has the exact normalized entropy \(\mathfrak H_N(t)=r_t\operatorname{artanh}(r_t)[1-\cos(2Jt/N)^{N-1}]\). For every fixed \(t>0\), \(N\mathfrak H_N(t)\to2r_t\operatorname{artanh}(r_t)J^2t^2>0\), so the theorem's \(N^{-1}\) exponent is sharp even with nonzero local dephasing.
