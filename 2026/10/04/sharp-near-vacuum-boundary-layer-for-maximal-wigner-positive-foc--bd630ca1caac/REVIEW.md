# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The exact source formula for maximal coherence is rescaled by \(z=(n!)^{1/n}u t^{-1/n}\). The first three exact Laguerre coefficients give a two-derivative asymptotic expansion around the unique minimum of \(\frac12(u^{-n/2}+u^{n/2})\). Existence and localization of global minimizers follow from divergence of the exact objective at both radial ends together with the source’s \(s_n(t)/\sqrt t\to1\) limit. The implicit-function calculation then yields the radius and value coefficients. The boundary-zero statement follows directly from equality in the source’s angularly minimized Wigner-positivity condition.

## Originality
PASS. The primary source defines \(s_n(t)\) and proves only \(s_n(t)^2=t+o(t)\); it explicitly replaces the exact minimization by a rough admissible lower bound for its entropy argument. The new claim supplies the first two nontrivial fractional-power coefficients and the minimizing-radius law. Targeted searches for Wigner-positive maximal coherence, vacuum–Fock Laguerre boundaries, the exact source identifier, and zero-set terminology found no covering result. The 2025 inverse-zero paper studies rigidity of Wigner nodal sets, not this optimization. The main residual risk is an older equivalent calculation under different quantum-optics normalization or terminology.

## Value
PASS. The source paper makes maximal Fock coherence the natural boundary object for its near-vacuum counterexamples. Replacing \(s_n(t)^2=t+o(t)\) by a sharp boundary-layer expansion quantifies how far the Wigner-positive boundary lies below the pure-state positivity ceiling and locates the phase-space zero that enforces it. The result is a structural asymptotic for a motivated exact optimizer, not a table entry or arbitrary parameter slice.
