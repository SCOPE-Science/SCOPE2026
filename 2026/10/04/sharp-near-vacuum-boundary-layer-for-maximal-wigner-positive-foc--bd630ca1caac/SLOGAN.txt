For the vacuum–Fock \(n\) family, the maximal Wigner-positive coherence has a sharp fractional-power boundary layer: \(s_n(t)/\sqrt t=1-\frac{n^2}{2(n!)^{1/n}}t^{1/n}+O(t^{2/n})\). The Wigner zero that enforces this boundary escapes to squared radius \(R_n(t)=\frac12(n!)^{1/n}t^{-1/n}+\frac{n-2}{2}+O(t^{1/n})\).
