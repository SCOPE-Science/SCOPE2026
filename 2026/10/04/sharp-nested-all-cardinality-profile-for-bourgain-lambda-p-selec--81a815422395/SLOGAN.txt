A single uniformly random ordering of any bounded orthogonal system has, with high probability, all prefix \(\Lambda(p)\) constants on the sharp scale \(\max\{1,\sqrt m\,N^{-1/p}\}\) simultaneously. The trigonometric system forces the same scale for every \(m\)-element subset, so the critical size \(m\asymp N^{2/p}\) is exactly the transition.
