Three-candidate Coombs voting has a tie-independent no-show paradox already at \(10\) voters: exactly \(12\) anonymous labeled profiles, forming only \(2\) candidate-relabeling classes, admit it, and every minimal event is caused by a single abstainer. If exactly two identical abstainers are imposed instead, the sharp minimum is \(13\) voters with \(18\) profiles in \(3\) symmetry classes.
