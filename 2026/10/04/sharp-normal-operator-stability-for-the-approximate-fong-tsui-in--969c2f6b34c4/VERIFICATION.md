# Verification

The argument was checked symbolically from the stated operator inequality. For normal \(T\), continuous functional calculus reduces the hypothesis to \(|z|\le|\operatorname{Re}z|+\varepsilon\) on the spectrum. The scalar identity \(|\operatorname{Im}z|^2=|z|^2-|\operatorname{Re}z|^2\) and the two cases \(|z|\ge\varepsilon\) and \(|z|\le\varepsilon\) give the bound. One-dimensional examples attain equality for every \(M>0\) and \(\varepsilon\ge0\).

The verification establishes only the normal-operator claim. No finite experiment is used as proof, and no assertion is made about the optimal stability exponent for general nonnormal operators.
