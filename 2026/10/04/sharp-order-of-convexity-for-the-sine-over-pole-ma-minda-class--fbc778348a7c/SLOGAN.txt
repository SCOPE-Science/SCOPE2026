For the Ma--Minda target \(\xi(z)=1+\sin z/(1-z)\), the associated classical convex class has exact convexity order \(\mu_*=0.39072298833669261703444293158667137888774859706022\ldots\). The value comes from the unique upper-semicircle minimum of \(\operatorname{Re}\xi\) and is sharp for the canonical extremal.
