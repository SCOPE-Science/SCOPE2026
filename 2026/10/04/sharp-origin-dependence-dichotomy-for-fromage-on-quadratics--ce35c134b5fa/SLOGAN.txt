On centered SPD quadratics, Fromage has the sharp one-step factor \(q_F(\eta,\kappa)=\sqrt{1-4\eta\sqrt{\kappa}/[(1+\eta^2)(\kappa+1)]}\), so every \(\eta>0\) contracts and \(\eta=1\) yields a \(1-\Theta(\kappa^{-1/2})\) guarantee. The gain is origin dependent: on a shifted scalar quadratic the same constant-step rule is conjugate after transients to a rigid log-coordinate rotation and does not converge to the nonzero minimizer.
