Infinite Bernoulli exchangeability leaves parity flexible but not arbitrary: at fixed marginal, the exact range is the convex/concave envelope of \(x\mapsto x^n\) after the de Finetti transform \(x=1-2P\). At fair marginals this gives \([1/2,1]\) for even \(n\), while the odd-\(n\) range converges sharply to \([1/4,3/4]\).
