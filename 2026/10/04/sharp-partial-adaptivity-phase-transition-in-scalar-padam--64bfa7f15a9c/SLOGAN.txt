Padam's partial-adaptivity exponent has a sharp scalar max-memory transition at \(p=1/4\): for \(0<p\le1/4\), sufficiently small nonzero initializations approach a nonzero symmetric two-cycle, while for \(p>1/4\) the same overshoot mechanism self-stabilizes after finitely many steps except at exact resonance. The effect arises from the historical second-moment maximum, not from stochastic noise.
