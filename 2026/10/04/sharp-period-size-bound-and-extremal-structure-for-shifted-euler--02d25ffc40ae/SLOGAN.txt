Every period-\(T\) cycle of \(n\mapsto\varphi(n)+k\) with \(k\ge2\) has maximum at most \([T(k-1)+1]^2\). Equality is possible exactly for the source paper’s prime-progression pattern: the last term must be \(q^2\) with prime \(q=T(k-1)+1\), and every preceding progression term must also be prime.\n
