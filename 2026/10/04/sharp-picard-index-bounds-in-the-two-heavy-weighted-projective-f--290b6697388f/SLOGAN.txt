For \(X=\mathbb P(1^r,a,b)\), the global Cartier defect of the weighted hyperplane class is exactly \(\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)\). Canonical members have the unique sharp maximum \((2r-1)(3r-1)\), while terminal members have maximum \(6\) for \(r=2\) and \((2r-3)(3r-4)\) for \(r\ge3\), with unique equality cases.
