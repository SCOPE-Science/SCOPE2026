Finite parameter sets therefore lift to labeled chord diagrams, and a new nonalgebraic quotient point is a one-chord extension. With 2m existing endpoints there are \(\binom{2m+1}{2}\) raw placements. Complete types over the parameter set are orbits under its pointwise dihedral stabilizer. The adjacent-pair base has trivial such stabilizer for every \(m\ge3\), proving the upper bound is sharp; the cases \(m=1,2\) are finite and are exhaustively checked.

Because the deduction is elementary after identifying the finite cover with chord diagrams, unindexed folklore remains possible.
