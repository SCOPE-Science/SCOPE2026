In a two-site thermal pure-dephasing Holstein witness, the stochastic-phase bath reduction has the exact coherence \(L_R=L_\infty I_0(2a\,\operatorname{csch}(\beta\omega/2)/R)^R\). Consequently its trace-norm error is asymptotic to a strictly positive constant times \(R^{-1}\), proving that the new general \(O(R^{-1})\) convergence rate is sharp in its exponent.
