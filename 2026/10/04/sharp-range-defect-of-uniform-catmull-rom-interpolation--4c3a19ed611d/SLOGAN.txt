For monotone samples in \([0,M]\), the standard uniform Catmull–Rom segment has the exact sharp range
\[
-\frac{2M}{27}\le P(t)\le\frac{29M}{27}.
\]
More generally, cardinal slope factor \(\sigma\in[0,1/2]\) gives an exact defect \(4\sigma M/27\), and even strictly increasing positive samples can produce a negative interpolated value.
