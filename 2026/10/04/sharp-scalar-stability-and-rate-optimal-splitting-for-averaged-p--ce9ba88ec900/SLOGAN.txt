On the scalar split \(F(x)=ax\), \(g(x)=\mu x^2/2\), aPRG is stable for every finite step once \(\mu\ge a\); for \(0<\mu<a\) its exact ceiling is \(\tau<4/[3(a-\mu)]\). When \(0<\mu<2a\), the unique rate-optimal step is \(\tau_\star=2/(2a-\mu)\) with factor \(\sqrt{a/(2a+\mu)}\), strictly beyond the paper's generic \(1/a\) bound.
