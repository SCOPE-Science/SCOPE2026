For the Chen–Lee rigid-body chaos model, non-origin compact stationary recurrence exists exactly when \(\operatorname{sgn}(a)=-\operatorname{sgn}(b)=-\operatorname{sgn}(c)\). In that regime every compact stationary state obeys three exact coordinatewise flux laws and a universal quadratic-energy ratio \(a\mathbb E[x^2]=-b\mathbb E[y^2]=-3c\mathbb E[z^2]\).
