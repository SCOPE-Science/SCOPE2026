For the homogeneous s-wave Mellin multiplier of the regularized three-boson zero-range model, \(\gamma=52/27\) is the exact point at which zero Mellin frequency becomes the global maximizer. For every \(\gamma\ge52/27\), the sharp upper constant is \(\frac{\pi}{2}(\gamma-\gamma_c)\), while below \(52/27\) zero is not even a local maximizer.
