For an isosceles triangle, the best coefficient in \(PA+PB+PC\ge\lambda(r_a+r_b)+2r_c\) has an explicit two-branch formula with a phase transition at height \(h=1\). This sharp profile implies Liu's 2015 median-to-bisector Erdős–Mordell conjecture for every isosceles triangle, with equality only in the equilateral-center case.
