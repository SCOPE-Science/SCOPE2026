Scalar AdaGrad-Norm always settles into the exact gradient-descent stability window \(0<\theta_\infty=\eta\lambda/b_\infty<2\), but that window is sharp: terminal steps can approach either edge, so the exact tail factor \(|1-\theta_\infty|\) can be arbitrarily close to one despite parameter-robust convergence. The startup can be noncontractive only for finitely many steps, with an explicit count bound.
