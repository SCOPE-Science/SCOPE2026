For common projective postselection, a global trace-distance budget \(\tau\) can amplify by at most exactly \(1/\max\{p,q\}\), not by the larger unequal-acceptance factor previously available. The envelope \(D(\rho_P,\sigma_P)\le\min\{1,\tau/\max\{p,q\}\}\) is attained by commuting three-level states for every feasible pair of acceptance probabilities.
