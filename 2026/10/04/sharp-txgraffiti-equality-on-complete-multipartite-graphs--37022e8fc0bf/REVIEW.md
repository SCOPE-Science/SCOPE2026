# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof derives the complete-multipartite estimate \(a\le\Delta\) directly from the degree sequence and edge count, applies the published equality criterion with its hypotheses intact, and exhausts the resulting degree cases. All four surviving graphs are checked directly. The standalone verifier independently confirms the lemma and classification for all \(2693\) complete-multipartite isomorphism types through order \(20\); this finite computation is supplementary rather than the proof.

## Originality
PASS. The closest primary source is Gupta's arXiv:2606.29553v1. It proves the inequality for all connected graphs and gives the equality criterion \(a=(\Delta-1)\alpha\) with \(R=\alpha\), but its full text does not discuss complete multipartite graphs and provides \(K_4\) only as an example. Larson--Pepper 2011 concerns the different relation \(a=\alpha\). Targeted semantic-index and web searches found no covering complete-multipartite sharpness classification.

## Value
PASS. Equality cases of a fresh graph inequality are mathematically motivated, and complete multipartite graphs are a standard broad family. The classification shows that sharpness in this family is exceptionally rigid: only \(P_3\), \(K_3\), \(C_4\), and \(K_4\) survive. The lemma \(a\le\Delta\) supplies the structural reason for that rigidity.

## Closest literature and limitations
The theorem is family-specific and does not classify equality graphs outside the complete multipartite class. Literature indexing is not exhaustive, so an unindexed duplicate remains a residual risk. The finite verifier is not evidence for the infinite quantifier beyond serving as a stress test of the analytic proof.
