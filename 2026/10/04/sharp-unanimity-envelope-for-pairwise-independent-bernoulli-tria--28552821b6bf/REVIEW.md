# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The claim reduces to a three-constraint discrete moment linear program for the success count. Both displayed quadratic majorants are pointwise valid on the entire integer support, and the proposed extremal count laws are nonnegative on exactly the stated branch ranges and satisfy the required factorial moments. Uniform conditioning on strings of fixed count reconstructs pairwise independence. The included exact-rational checker agrees with the formula on \(693\) enumerated vertex problems.

## Originality
PASS. Direct inspection of the closest classical and recent sources found the single-endpoint problem, a general Boolean-function LP framework, and individual exact-count bounds, but not the sharp two-endpoint unanimity envelope. In particular, prior single-endpoint formulas do not imply the even central branch because the optimizer there places mass on both unanimous endpoints. Residual risk remains that an older result under different terminology was not indexed by the targeted searches.

## Value
PASS. The unanimity event is a natural symmetric two-tail Boolean functional. The exact odd/even distinction, the fair-marginal values, the even central transition, and explicit extremizers give structural information not captured by a generic moment bound or by separate endpoint estimates.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest sources are Peled–Yadin–Yehudayoff on the all-one endpoint, Benjamini–Gurel-Gurevich–Peled on bounded-independence Boolean-function LP duality, Kwerel on low-order aggregated probability bounds, and Berend–Ernst–Kontorovich–Kumar on exact single-endpoint expressions. The theorem here assumes common Bernoulli marginals and pairwise independence and does not address heterogeneous marginals or higher-order independence.
