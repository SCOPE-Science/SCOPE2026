For \(X_b\sim\mathrm{Beta}(1,b)\), the exact right tail is bounded by the Gaussian exponent built from its true variance for every deviation exactly when \(b\le b_\star\), where \(b_\star=3.7534772184455018\ldots\). Above this sharp threshold an explicit interior deviation violates the bound, even though the family remains elementary enough for a complete analytic classification.
