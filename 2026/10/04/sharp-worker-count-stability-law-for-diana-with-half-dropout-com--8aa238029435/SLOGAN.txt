For DIANA with independent unbiased half-dropout compression and the canonical shift rate \(\lambda=1/2\), the exact scalar mean-square stability ceiling is \(s_n=[n-4+\sqrt{9n^2+8n+16}]/[2(n+2)]\). It rises strictly from \((\sqrt{33}-3)/6\) for one worker to the uncompressed gradient-descent ceiling \(2\) as the worker count grows.
