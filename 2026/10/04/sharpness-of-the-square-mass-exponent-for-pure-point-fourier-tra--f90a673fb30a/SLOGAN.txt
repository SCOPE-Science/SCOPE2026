The exponent \(2\) in the local Fourier-mass theorem of Boyvalenkov--Favorov is optimal. A positive continuous bounded density has a pure-point distributional Fourier transform whose powered-mass measure \(\sum_\gamma |b_\gamma|^q\delta_\gamma\) is translation bounded exactly for \(q\ge2\), so every \(0<q<2\) fails in one construction.
