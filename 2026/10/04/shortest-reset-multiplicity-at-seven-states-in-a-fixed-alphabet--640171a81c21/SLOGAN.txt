For the seven-state de Bondt--Don--Zantema construction, adding \(b\) to the two-letter automaton preserves the shortest reset length \(32\) but raises the number of shortest reset words from \(331{,}776\) to \(1{,}327{,}104\), exactly a factor of \(4\). The three-letter shortest words split evenly between target states \(3\) and \(4\), whereas every two-letter shortest word resets to state \(4\).
