For countable equivalence relations, sibling counts obey the full \(1/\aleph_0/2^{\aleph_0}\) trichotomy with a class-size test: finitely many nonsingleton classes give \(1\), bounded finite classes with infinitely many finite nonsingletons give \(\aleph_0\), and unbounded finite sizes or infinitely many infinite classes give \(2^{\aleph_0}\).
