For prime cyclic groups in Gomaa's relational language, the published \(2n\) EF upper bound improves to \(n+h(p)\le n+\lceil n/2ceil\), where \(h(p)\) is the smaller binary weight of the two gaps from \(p\) to its adjacent powers of two. When \(p\) is one away from a power of two, this matches the known lower bound exactly at \(n+1\).
