For an all-boundary equilateral quantum-graph resonator, the sharp odd-resonance Dirichlet-to-Neumann pole coefficient is exactly \(C_*=(2\lambda_0/\ell)q_{\min}(D+A)\). At fixed boundary size the complete graph is the unique optimal simple decoration, and for four boundaries it beats the triangle-plus-pendant example by \((5+\sqrt{17})/2\).
