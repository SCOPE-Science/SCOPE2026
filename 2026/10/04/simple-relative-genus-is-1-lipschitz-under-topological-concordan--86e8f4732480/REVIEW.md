# Review

## Correctness
PASS. Pencovitch's exact formula expresses the positive simple relative genus as an integer ceiling of one half of a maximum of affine Levine--Tristram signatures, with a separate genus-zero Arf condition. Powell's topological locally-flat signature inequality bounds the change of every signature coordinate between \(K\) and \(J\) by twice \(g_4^{\mathrm{top}}(K\#-J)\). The maximum therefore changes by at most the same amount, and the ceiling changes by at most the concordance genus. The genus-zero branch is preserved when the distance is zero and is bounded by the same integer when the distance is positive.

## Originality
PASS. The recent source contains exact genus formulas, connected-sum subadditivity, and satellite bounds, but no pairwise concordance-distance, Lipschitz, or crossing-change statement. The supporting signature paper has no ambient simple-genus invariant. Targeted semantic and web searches found no equivalent statement. Residual risk is limited to an unindexed very recent observation.

## Value
PASS. The result supplies a stability theorem for a genus invariant with a complement-fundamental-group constraint, where ordinary geometric cobordism gluing does not automatically preserve admissibility. It turns the recent exact formula into a robust comparison tool and immediately yields a one-crossing-change bound.
