The reduced singular locus of the quartic two-3-disc example is a union of ten complex affine lines, but its Jacobian singular scheme has one extra embedded square-zero infinitesimal at the origin. The four real multiple-exposure boundary points are exactly reduced triple-branch points with local equation \(U^2+Vpq=0\), while every other non-origin singular point is generically transverse \(A_1\).
