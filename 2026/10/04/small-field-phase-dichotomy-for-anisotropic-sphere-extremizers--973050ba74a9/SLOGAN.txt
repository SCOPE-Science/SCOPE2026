On the two smallest anisotropic finite-field spheres, the sharp \(L^2\to L^4\) extremizers have completely different phase geometry: \(q=3\) admits a four-torus of phases determined only by a common opposite-pair product, while \(q=5\) is rigid and allows only scalar additive characters. The \(q=5\) rigidity is certified by an exact index-\(125\) additive-relation lattice computation.
