For a two-digit row \(N=p^s+p^t\), divisibility by \(m\ge3\) blocks every admissible no-borrow subdigit while \(k=mp^{t-1}\) supplies an exact one-borrow witness, so \(v_p(G(N;m))=1\) whenever \(p>m\) and \(m\mid N\). At \(m=5\), this gives infinite families for primes \(p\equiv2,3\pmod5\) whenever \(t-s\equiv2\pmod4\).
