# Review

## Correctness
PASS. For \(N=qM+r\) with \(0\le r<q\), the sparse sum contains the first \(M\) sampled terms and at most one additional bounded term. The denominator difference between \(N\) and \(qM\) contributes \(o(1)\), yielding the exact limsup factor \(1/q\). The identical argument applied to absolute weights proves preservation of non-triviality. The entropy conclusion then follows from the exact correlated-set identity and the published subset power law \(h_{\mathrm{top}}(f^q,Y)=q\,h_{\mathrm{top}}(f,Y)\).

## Originality
PASS. Targeted searches for Bohr chaoticity under powers, time decimation, sampled correlations, and full-entropy abundance did not locate the sparse-weight identity or the general permanence statement. The full text of Hou--Lin--Tian was checked at the definition of correlated sets, Theorems B and C, and Lemma 2.11; it supplies the motivating full-entropy notion and the subset entropy power law but not the claimed transfer. Kawaguchi's structural remarks and shadowing criteria, as well as the inspected foundational Bohr-chaos sources, also do not state this identity. Residual risk remains because the argument is elementary and may have appeared as an unstated or differently phrased observation.

## Value
PASS. The result is a structural stability principle for a new full-entropy strengthening of Bohr chaoticity: once full-entropy correlation abundance is established by any method, it automatically survives every integer sampling rate. The exact point-set identity is stronger than a mere existence argument and also yields iterate permanence of ordinary Bohr chaoticity. It avoids re-verifying shadowing, specification, or another sufficient criterion for each iterate.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is Hou--Lin--Tian, arXiv:2604.05713v1, which defines \(\mathcal N_\vartheta(f,X)\), proves full-entropy abundance under broad orbit-tracing assumptions, and records the subset entropy power law. Kawaguchi, arXiv:2601.06869, records other closure mechanisms for Bohr chaoticity. No converse under powers is asserted here, and the literature search cannot rule out all unpublished or differently indexed prior observations.
