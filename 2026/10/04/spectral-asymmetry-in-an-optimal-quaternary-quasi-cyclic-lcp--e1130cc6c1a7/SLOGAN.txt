In the published quaternary \(n=14\), \(k=9\) quasi-cyclic LCP, both security sides are optimal \([14,9,4]_4\) codes, yet \(C\) and \(D^\perp\) have different complete weight enumerators and different generalized Hamming-weight hierarchies. Their fourth generalized weights are \(9\) and \(8\), respectively, so equal minimum-distance security masks a genuine asymmetry in higher support geometry.
