For good Broyden on symmetric positive definite Stieltjes systems with \(B_0=\gamma I\), the spectral width \(L-\mu\) is the sharp universal threshold guaranteeing a nonnegative second iterate. Below it, a two-dimensional diagonal system with a positive right-hand side and positive exact solution already produces a negative coordinate; with \(\gamma=\mu\), the sharp frontier is condition number \(2\).
