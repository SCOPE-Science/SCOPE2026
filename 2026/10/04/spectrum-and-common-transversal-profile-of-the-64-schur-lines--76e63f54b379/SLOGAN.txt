The intersection graph of the 64 lines on the Segre--Schur quartic has spectrum \(18^1,2^{44},(-4)^8,(-6)^9,(-10)^2\), and every skew pair has exactly 4, 6, 7, 8, or 10 Schur-line transversals. This refines the known rank-20 intersection matrix to inertia \((1,19,44)\) and gives its full eigenvalue decomposition.
