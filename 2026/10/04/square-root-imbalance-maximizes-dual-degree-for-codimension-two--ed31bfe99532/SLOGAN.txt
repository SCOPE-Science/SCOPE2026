For smooth complete-intersection threefolds \(X_{d,e}\subset\mathbb P^5\) with fixed \(d+e\), the dual degree is not generally maximized by the most balanced bidegree: the optimal gap satisfies \(e-d\sim\sqrt{2(d+e-2)}\). The maximizer is unique except for the exact tie sequence \(d+e-2=2k(k+1)\), where two explicitly described adjacent bidegrees tie.
