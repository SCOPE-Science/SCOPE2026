# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The upper bound is an explicit \(2r+1\)-square decomposition. The lower bound uses the projection from the symmetric square onto \(\operatorname{Sym}^2(V^*)\): the coordinate subspace contributes \(r+1\) dimensions in the kernel, while the mixed terms force at least \(r\) independent square directions in the image. This proves the matching lower bound without a finite-search assumption.

## Originality
**PASS.** The recent evolution-envelope paper supplies the reduction to Waring rank and contains the dual-number case, but it does not state the arbitrary-radical square-zero formula. The closest exact simultaneous-Waring results for monomial collections impose support/exponent hypotheses that do not cover the exponent-one star collection here. A full-text check of the closest published record on the same square-zero local family found only a Tor-homology result.

Residual risk remains that older tensor-rank or algebraic-complexity literature states an equivalent formula in different language.

## Value
**PASS.** Square-zero local algebras are a canonical family, and the formula determines the new evolution-dimension invariant uniformly in every radical dimension. The proof is structural, field-uniform outside characteristic \(2\), and extends the known dual-number example rather than merely recomputing an isolated instance.

## Closest literature and limitations
The two primary comparisons are arXiv:2609.32784v1 and arXiv:1711.00089v2. The result is restricted to characteristic different from \(2\); no characteristic-\(2\) analogue is claimed.
