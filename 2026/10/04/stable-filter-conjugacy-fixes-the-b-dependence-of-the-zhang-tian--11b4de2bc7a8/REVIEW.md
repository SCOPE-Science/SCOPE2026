# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The first three equations are exactly closed and independent of \(w\) and \(b\). Variation of constants gives the unique bounded complete lift. Compactness plus backward growth of a nonzero lift difference proves injectivity of projection, and uniform convergence of the memory integral gives continuity of the graph. The tangent cocycle has an invariant vertical line with exact exponent \(-b\) and quotient equal to the base tangent cocycle. The only non-smooth surface is \(x=0\); every such crossing on a compact complete trajectory is transverse because \(x=y=0\) would force the unbounded solution \(z(t)=z(0)-at\). The packaged symbolic check independently confirms the block factorization on both smooth branches.

## Originality
PASS. The introducing article states the exact triangular equations and reports \(b\)-dependent hyperchaotic ranges, but does not derive the bounded-lift graph, conjugacy, or forced \(-b\) exponent. Its public peer-review record asks about hyperchaos and cautions about numerical Lyapunov errors without supplying this structural reduction. Searches using the exact title/DOI with `skew product`, `linear filter`, `forced exponent`, `Lyapunov spectrum`, `hyperchaos correction`, and `erratum`, together with searches for stronger triangular-cocycle coverage, did not find a source implying the system-specific result. General linear skew-product theory supplies background only.

## Value
PASS. The result addresses the claimed added complexity of the four-dimensional construction at its structural source. It distinguishes a stable observation/filter coordinate from a genuinely new dynamical degree of freedom, provides a canonical conjugacy for all compact recurrent dynamics, and gives an exact spectral diagnostic that directly tests the reported \(b\)-induced hyperchaos. This is stronger and more reusable than a parameter-specific numerical discrepancy.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest primary source is Zhang–Tian–Li–Wu–Cui (2021), DOI `10.3390/e23101341`; the closest predecessor is Pham–Volos–Jafari–Kapitaniak (2017), DOI `10.1007/s11071-016-3170-x`. Johnson–Palmer–Sell (1987), DOI `10.1137/0518001`, gives general multiplicative-ergodic theory for linear skew-product flows but not this reduction. Residual risk is that an unindexed later note may have observed the same triangular obstruction. The theorem is limited to \(b>0\) and compact invariant dynamics; it does not interpret finite-time Lyapunov fluctuations as asymptotic exponents.
