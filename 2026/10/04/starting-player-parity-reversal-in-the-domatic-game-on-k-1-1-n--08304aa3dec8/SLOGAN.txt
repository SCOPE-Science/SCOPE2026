For \(G_n=K_{1,1,n}\) with \(n\ge2\), the two domatic-game starting conventions have values \(2\) and \(1\) in opposite parity: Alice-start is \(2\) exactly for odd \(n\), while Bob-start is \(2\) exactly for even \(n\). The ordinary domatic number is always \(3\), so this is an infinite parity-reversal family rather than a change in the underlying domatic partition number.
