For generalized Sprott G, every compact stationary barycenter lies exactly on the segment joining the two equilibria, with \(\operatorname{Var}(x)=(b/a)^2p(1-p)\) when \(p=\mathbb E[z]\). A second exact defect, \(\mathbb E[(z-1/2)^2]=1/4+b^{-2}\mathbb E[(\dot x)^2]\), is rigid only for equilibrium mixtures, while every other recurrent state must cross the equilibrium \(x\)-slab.
