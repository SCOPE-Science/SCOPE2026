# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The coordinate generator identities give \(\mathbb E[x]=\gamma\), \(\mathbb E[y]=\beta\mathbb E[x^2]\), and \(\mathbb E[z]=-(\beta/\alpha)\mathbb E[x^2]\). With \(v=\gamma-x\), \(w=-(y+\alpha z)\), the inspected package certificate differentiates exactly to \(\dot C=\alpha v^2-w^2\), so stationarity gives the derivative-energy law and hence the exact height defect. Equality forces \(x=\gamma\) on invariant support and then the unique equilibrium. Variation of constants in \(\dot y+y=\beta x^2\) on a bounded complete orbit gives a positive past integral; vanishing would force an impossible bounded complete trajectory with \(x\) identically zero on a backward half-line while \(\gamma>0\). On a periodic orbit, \(v\) has mean zero and \(\int \dot v^2=\alpha\int v^2\); Wirtinger's inequality yields \(P\ge2\pi/\sqrt\alpha\), while equality would make \(v\) a first harmonic and the exact jerk equation would force \((\gamma-v)^2\) constant, impossible for a nonconstant harmonic. Thus the bound is strict.
