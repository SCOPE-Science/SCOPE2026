For the normalized T-system with \(m,n>0\), every compact stationary state lies on the exact height-moment circle \(\mathbb E[(z-m/2)^2]=m^2/4\) and obeys \(\mathbb E[xy\mid z]=nz\). Unless it is supported on the three equilibria, it must put positive mass both inside \(0<z<m\) and outside \(0\le z\le m\), forcing every nonconstant periodic orbit to cross both regions.
