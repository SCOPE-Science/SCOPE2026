# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Arbitrary one-variable antiderivative tests give
\[
\mathbb E[x^2\mid y]=1-by,
\qquad
\mathbb E[x\mid z]=-cz.
\]
The first identity forces the support ceiling
\[
y\le\frac1b.
\]
Stationarity of \(z^2/2\) and \((x^2+z^2)/2\) gives
\[
\mathbb E[\dot z^2]
=
bc\,\mathbb E\!\left[
\left(\frac1b-y\right)
\left(a+\frac1c-y\right)
\right].
\]
Zero defect forces \(\dot z=0\) on the invariant support; bounded completeness then reduces every support trajectory to one of the known equilibria. The weighted-mean interval and the strict undershoot statement follow from the same identity.

Risk: the equality classification uses the standard invariance of the support of an invariant probability measure.
