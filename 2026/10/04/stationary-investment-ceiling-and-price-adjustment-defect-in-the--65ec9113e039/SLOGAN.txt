Every compact stationary state of the Huang–Li finance model is confined to \(y\le1/b\), and its mean-square price-adjustment speed is exactly \(\mathbb E[\dot z^2]=bc\,\mathbb E[(1/b-y)(a+1/c-y)]\). The defect vanishes only on equilibrium mixtures; when the two nonzero equilibria exist, every genuinely time-dependent stationary state must spend positive mass below their investment-demand level \(a+1/c\).
