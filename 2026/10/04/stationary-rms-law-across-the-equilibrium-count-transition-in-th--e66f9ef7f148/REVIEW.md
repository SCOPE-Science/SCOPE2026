# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. For the plus-sign Chen–Wang normalization used by the rigorous literature,
\[
F=z+xy+x
\]
satisfies
\[
LF=a-x^2+4y^2.
\]
Stationarity therefore gives the exact RMS law. The conditional laws follow from arbitrary one-variable antiderivative tests. Equality for positive \(a\) forces the compact invariant support onto \(y=0\), then \(z=0\), then \(x^2=a\), giving exactly the two equilibrium atoms. For negative \(a\), equality in the lower \(y\)-RMS bound would force the origin, where \(\dot z=a\ne0\), so the bound is strict.

Risk: support-rigidity steps use the standard invariance of the support of a compactly supported invariant probability measure.

## Originality
PASS. The inspected same-object papers cover the equilibrium-count construction, numerical chaos, integrability, Hopf and zero–Hopf bifurcations, periodic solutions, and dynamics at infinity. They do not state the invariant-measure RMS law or its sharp equality and no-equilibrium consequences. Exact-object semantic searches over moment, RMS, equilibrium-amplitude, and recurrence formulations returned only different-flow analogues.

Risk: the earliest preprint contains a sign inconsistency in its displayed equation, and a differently phrased older stationary observation could remain unindexed. The theorem uses the normalization explicitly fixed in later rigorous full texts.

## Value
PASS. The result turns the model's defining equilibrium-count parameter into a universal stationary constraint. On the positive side it makes the equilibrium amplitude a sharp RMS floor and forces every non-equilibrium compact recurrent component beyond that amplitude. On the negative side it supplies a strict velocity-RMS floor despite the absence of equilibria. These are global recurrence restrictions, not a numerical diagnostic or a restatement of local bifurcation theory.
