The Chen–Wang equilibrium-count parameter obeys an exact stationary RMS law, \(\mathbb E[x^2]=a+4\mathbb E[y^2]\). Above the equilibrium-creation threshold, every non-equilibrium compact recurrent state must exceed the equilibrium amplitude \(\sqrt a\); below it, every compact recurrent state has the strict velocity floor \(\mathbb E[y^2]>-a/4\).
