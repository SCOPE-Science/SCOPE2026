For the positive-parameter Bouali Type III flow, every compact stationary state lives in \(y\ge0\) and obeys \(\mathbb E[x^2\mid y]=1\) on \(y>0\). After separating a possible origin atom of mass \(w\), the exact locking law is \(\mathbb E[x^2]=\mathbb E[y]=1-w\), forcing every non-origin recurrent component across the cylinder \(|x|=1\).
