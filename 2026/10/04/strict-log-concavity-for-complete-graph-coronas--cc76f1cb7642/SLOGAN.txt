For \(H_n=K_n\circ K_1\), every general-position set of size at least three chooses at most one vertex from each core–pendant pair, so \(\psi(H_n;x)=(1+2x)^n+n x^2\). Its coefficients are strictly log-concave for every \(n\ge2\), giving a new positive complete-graph family for the corona-unimodality problem.
