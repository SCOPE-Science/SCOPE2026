The simplex volume of the higher-order unbalanced difference body is strictly unimodal in the imbalance parameter in every dimension and order. In the plane its unique maximizing parameter is \(t_{2,p}=2/(2p+1+\sqrt{(2p-1)(6p-5)})\), and in dimensions at most three the source's pointwise theorem upgrades this to a sharp uniform-in-\(t\) extremal bound for all convex bodies.
