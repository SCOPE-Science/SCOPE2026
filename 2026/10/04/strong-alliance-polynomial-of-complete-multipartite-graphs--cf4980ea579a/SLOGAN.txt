For \(G=K_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}\), strong defensive alliances are exactly the vertex sets whose occupancy in every used part stays below a simple size-dependent cap; they form an upper ideal, and the inclusion-minimal ones are precisely those with at least two tight parts. This yields an exact strong alliance polynomial and the parity law \(a(G)=\lceil N/2\rceil\), except that even \(N\) with an odd part size forces \(a(G)=N/2+1\).
