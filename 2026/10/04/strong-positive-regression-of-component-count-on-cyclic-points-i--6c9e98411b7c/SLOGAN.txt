For a uniform random mapping, component count is an exact Bernoulli-sum regression on the number of cyclic vertices: conditional on \(L_n=m\), it is the cycle count of a uniform \(m\)-permutation. This makes every increasing transform of the two statistics nonnegatively correlated and gives \(\operatorname{Cov}(L_n,C_n)\sim\sqrt{\pi/2}(1-\log2)\sqrt n\) while correlation decays only as \(1/\sqrt{\log n}\).
