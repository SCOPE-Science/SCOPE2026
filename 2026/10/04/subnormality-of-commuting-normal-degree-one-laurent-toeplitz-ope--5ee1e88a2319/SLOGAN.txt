For the commuting-normal Laurent symbol \(\Phi(\zeta)=\zeta A+\overline{\zeta}B\), subnormality is exactly \(A^*A-B^*B\ge0\) together with \((A^*A-B^*B)B=0\). In that case the Toeplitz operator splits canonically into a normal sector and an analytic subnormal sector, allowing mixed subnormal examples beyond global normal-or-analytic dichotomies.
