Wang's finite-model and value-compression proofs yield an explicit small certificate for successor knowing-value satisfiability: a length-\(n\) satisfiable formula has a standard witness of size \(2^{O(n\log n)}\). Consequently the announcement-free satisfiability problem is in \(\mathrm{NTIME}(2^{O(n\log n)})\), hence in NEXPTIME.
