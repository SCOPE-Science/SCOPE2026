For \(q=2^m\), the special \(2\)-group \(UT_3(\mathbf F_q)\) has exactly \(2q^2-q-1\) involutions and \(q(q-1)^2\) elements of order \(4\), so \(\psi(UT_3(\mathbf F_q))=4q^3-4q^2+2q-1\). For \(m\ge2\) this gives a uniform explicit sum-of-element-orders formula beyond the extraspecial boundary of the motivating open problem.
