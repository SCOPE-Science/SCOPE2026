On the symmetric two-objective quadratic Pareto interval, SAA-RMGDA has an exact product law: every nonzero interior start with \(\sigma_0\ge1\) converges to a nonzero point of the same sign under any legal positive-summable Tikhonov schedule. The total Tikhonov mass bounds the total displacement, so unique regularized multipliers do not by themselves impose selection of the central Pareto point.
