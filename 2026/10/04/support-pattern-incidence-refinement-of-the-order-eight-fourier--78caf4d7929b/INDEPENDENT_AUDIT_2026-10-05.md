# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For fixed supports \(S,T\), vanishing outside \(T\) gives a kernel of the Fourier submatrix with rows in the complement of \(T\). Exact nonzero time coordinates are possible precisely when deleting each selected column leaves rank unchanged, and exact nonzero desired Fourier coordinates are possible precisely when adjoining each desired row raises rank by one. Over \(\mathbf C\), finitely many proper kernels cannot cover the nonzero kernel, so these conditions are jointly sufficient. The inspected verifier implements all ranks exactly over \(\mathbf Q(\zeta_8)\) and exhausts all \(65{,}025\) support pairs and both finite symmetry actions. Independently, the displayed matrix sums to \(20{,}929\), is symmetric, and both reported orbit-size distributions sum back to \(20{,}929\) with respectively \(205\) and \(108\) classes.

## originality

PASS

Yang et al. give an equivalent rank criterion for whether a support-cardinality point belongs to the DFT uncertainty diagram and display the order-eight size-level diagram. Their result records zero-versus-positive existence by cardinality, not the number of actual support-set pairs or their symmetry-orbit decomposition. The audited computation refines that diagram to a complete incidence invariant and two quotient classifications. Exact-count searches found no prior table with \(20{,}929\), \(205\), or \(108\).

## value

PASS

The order-eight DFT is the first small dyadic composite example where support locations matter substantially. Refining the published size diagram to a complete location-sensitive incidence table and symmetry quotient is a natural exact invariant and a reusable benchmark for exact-support Fourier geometry, not an arbitrary subset sample.
