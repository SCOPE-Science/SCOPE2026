For a smooth rational normal scroll \(S(a_1,\ldots,a_k)\), the excess projective symmetry above \(k^2+2\) is exactly \(h^1(\mathbb P^1,\operatorname{End}E)\), and this equals the codimension of its projective orbit inside the fixed-degree scroll locus. Balanced scrolls form the unique open orbit, while \(S(1,\ldots,1,d-k+1)\) is the unique most symmetric and highest-codimension smooth type when \(d\ge k+1\).
