# Review

## Correctness

PASS. The source's own definitions make the counterexample direct. The component code contains \(c=(0,g_3,g_3)\) because \((1+x^2)(x+1)=g_3\). Each ring coordinate is \((0,1,1)\), and the printed Gray matrix sends this to \((0,1,0)\), producing a word of weight \(4\). Exact enumeration of all \(4^5=1024\) codewords proves that \(4\) is the minimum distance and gives the displayed weight enumerator. Exact column-subspace enumeration gives generalized weights \((4,8,10,11,12)\).

## Originality

Searches under the paper identifier, published and corrected parameters, generator tuple, Gray-image terminology, and corrigendum terminology found no prior correction. The main residual risk is an unindexed corrigendum or private author communication.

The exact enumerator and higher-weight profile provide a compact, reproducible replacement without overclaiming about the paper's other examples or general theorems.
