The \(10\)-cut complex of the \(4\times4\) grid has nonzero reduced mod-\(2\) homology in both degrees \(4\) and \(5\), with ranks \(4\) and \(2747\). Because the complex is pure \(5\)-dimensional, the degree-\(4\) class obstructs shellability and gives a concrete counterexample to the published all-\(k\) grid-shellability conjecture.
