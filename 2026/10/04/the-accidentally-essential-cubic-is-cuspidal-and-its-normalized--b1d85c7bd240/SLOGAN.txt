Kulkarni--Vemulapalli's "accidentally essential" \(3\times3\) symmetroid is an irreducible rational cubic with one \(A_2\) cusp, while its Cayley scheme is one reduced point.  After normalization, the otherwise undefined kernel map extends to an isomorphism \(\mathbb P^1\to\{Y_0Y_2=2(Y_1+Y_2)^2\}\) sending the cusp preimage to that Cayley point.
