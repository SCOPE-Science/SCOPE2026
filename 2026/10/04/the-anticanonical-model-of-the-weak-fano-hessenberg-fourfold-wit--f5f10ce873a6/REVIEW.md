# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The incidence description follows directly from the Hessenberg conditions: for \(h=(3,3,4,4)\), only \(SP\subset H\) is essential, and a suitable \(H\) exists exactly when \(P\cap SP\ne0\). In Plücker coordinates the condition is the explicitly verified quadratic \(Q=0\) together with the Grassmannian quadric \(G=0\). The pencil computation gives exactly six singular points on \(Z\), and the six local Hessians are symbolically nondegenerate under the sole hypothesis that the eigenvalues are pairwise distinct. The exceptional-fiber calculation gives six codimension-two \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\) surfaces. The anticanonical weight calculation agrees with Abe--Fujita--Zeng's formula, and smallness plus the Gorenstein target gives crepancy.

## Originality
PASS. The closest primary source, arXiv:2003.12286v1, establishes weak-Fano status and the anticanonical class but does not describe the anticanonical target or its singularities. Targeted searches for the same Hessenberg function combined with “anticanonical model”, “small resolution”, “crepant resolution”, “intersection of two quadrics”, and “ordinary double point” found no statement implying the result. Cherepanov's paper treats the same Hessenberg function in torus-orbit/GKM topology but not this birational model. The 2024 automorphism/deformation work has a different principal scope. Residual risk remains that an equivalent birational description occurs under substantially different terminology.

## Value
PASS. The source paper explicitly highlights \(h=(3,3,4,4)\) as a weak-Fano example, while weak-Fano status alone does not explain the failure of ampleness. The result identifies exactly what the anticanonical system contracts, the complete-intersection geometry of the target, every singular stratum, and the crepant resolution structure. This is a natural birational invariant of the highlighted weak-Fano fourfold rather than an arbitrary computation.

## Closest literature and limitations
The proof uses the anticanonical class and weak-Fano classification from arXiv:2003.12286v1. DOI:10.1070/SM9278 supplies a same-object comparison from torus-action topology, and arXiv:2405.18313v1 supplies a later same-family comparison from automorphisms/deformations. No claim is made for other Hessenberg functions, and the literature search cannot exclude uncatalogued equivalent formulations.
