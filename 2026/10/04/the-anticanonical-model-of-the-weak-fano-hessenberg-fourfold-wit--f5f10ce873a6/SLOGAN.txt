For \(X=\operatorname{Hess}(S,(3,3,4,4))\), the anticanonical map forgets the three-plane and gives a small crepant resolution of a fourfold whose singular locus is six disjoint \(\mathbb P^1\)'s. Its base is a \((2,2)\) complete intersection in \(\mathbb P^5\) with exactly six ordinary double points, and the six exceptional surfaces are \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\).
