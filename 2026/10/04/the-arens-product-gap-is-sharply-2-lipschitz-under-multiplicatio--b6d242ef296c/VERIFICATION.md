# Verification

The proof was checked directly from the definitions of the two Arens extensions. For a bounded bilinear map \(h\), each extension has norm \(\lVert h\rVert\), so the difference operator has norm at most \(2\lVert h\rVert\). Linearity in \(h\) gives the global Lipschitz estimate without any compactness or approximation assumption.

For the sharpness algebra, boundedness follows from \(|\sigma_{ij}|=1\) and the \(\ell_1\) norms. Associativity is exact because every product lands in the annihilating scalar coordinate, hence every triple product is zero. The two Arens values are computed by opposite iterated free-ultrafilter limits of \(\sigma_{ij}\), which equal \(1\) and \(-1\). This is an infinite exact argument, not a finite truncation.
