For the Waldron--Witaszek irregular characteristic-two Fano fourfold, \(|-2K_X|\) has exactly seven sections and gives a finite purely inseparable degree-\(8\) map onto the Frobenius twist of its \((2,3)\) complete-intersection cover. The same computation upgrades the known differential-form injection to \(\omega_X^{-2}\hookrightarrow\Omega_X^1\), hence \(h^0(X,\Omega_X^1)\ge7\).
