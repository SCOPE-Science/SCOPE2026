# Review

## Correctness
**PASS.** Exact arithmetic verifies the critical-value point and two degree-one good reductions. The reductions have exact orders \(9\) and \(4\), forcing the original point to be non-torsion. The Eisenstein-module statement then follows from the finite-kernel property of nonzero elliptic-curve endomorphisms, and the fixed subgroup has order three from \(\deg(\rho-1)=3\).

## Originality
**PASS.** The initiating article gives exact critical coordinates and the order-three orbit but, in the inspected full text, does not determine torsion, Mordell--Weil order, pairwise-difference order, or the complete integral relation lattice. The later same-fibration monodromy paper uses the real critical value but does not supply those arithmetic conclusions. Exact-coordinate, equivalent-formulation, broader-coverage, and published-result-index searches located no covering statement. Search failure is not treated as proof of novelty; ultra-recent or unindexed overlap remains a stated residual risk.

## Value
**PASS.** The subgroup generated by the critical values is a natural invariant of the explicit Albanese fibration. Showing that the singular-fiber values generate a non-torsion CM lattice, in contrast to the three smooth symmetry-fixed values forming a torsion subgroup, adds structural information that is directly relevant to arithmetic and monodromy questions.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest sources are Szabó's exact Albanese-map paper and the subsequent Akhmedov--Yeung monodromy paper. The present result does not compute the full Mordell--Weil group over the cubic or CM extension, and it does not determine whether the generated Eisenstein submodule is saturated.
