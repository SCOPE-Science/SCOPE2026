Szabó's three critical values on \(E:y^2=x^3-3888\) are not torsion: the real one reduces to points of exact orders \(9\) and \(4\) at degree-one good primes above \(17\) and \(23\). Their order-three orbit spans a free rank-one \(\mathbf Z[\zeta_3]\)-module, while the three smooth invariant-fiber values form the contrasting order-three fixed subgroup.
