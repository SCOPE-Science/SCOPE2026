For the finite Heisenberg group \(H_n(q)\), the centralizer lattice and Chermak–Delgado lattice are exactly the inverse images of the \(\mathbf F_q\)-subspaces of \(H_n(q)/Z\), hence both are \(L_{2n}(q)\). The maximal measure is \(q^{2n+2}\); it is increasing on all centralizers for every \(q\), but on the full subgroup lattice exactly when \(q\) is prime.
