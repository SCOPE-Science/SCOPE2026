For each of the two minimal nine-point homotopically trivial noncontractible finite spaces \(X\), the full circle-valued mapping space \(\operatorname{Map}(X,C)\) has \(172\) points and strongly deformation retracts onto its four constant maps. Thus \(\operatorname{Map}(X,C)\simeq C\) even though every map \(X\to C\) lies in the same homotopy class.
