# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof was reconstructed from the published multiplication in \(\mathfrak A(V^*)\). The compressions by \(e_0\) and \(e_1\) isolate nonzero diagonal coordinates; either coordinate forces the radical, both force the unit, and with both zero the ideal is exactly a closed linear subspace of the radical. The quotient correspondence then transfers the exhaustive list to ideals of \(\mathcal B(X_V)\) containing \(\mathcal K(X_V)\). Liu--Shen explicitly identify the radical preimage with \(\mathcal E(X_V)\).

## Originality
PASS. Full-text inspection of arXiv:2609.32334v1 found the arbitrary triangular realization, the radical/inessential identification, and the subspace lattice in the radical interval for the universal choice, but not the complete ideal lattice above the compacts for arbitrary \(V\). The older Kania--Laustsen triangular construction supplies only the one-dimensional prototype. Targeted searches for equivalent formulations, stronger coverage, maximal-ideal versions, exact indexed matches, and implication-equivalent statements found no covering result.

## Value
PASS. The result gives a natural complete classification of a central operator-algebra invariant for an entire newly constructed family: all closed ideals above the compacts, the exact two maximal ideals, their intersection, and the precise locus of the arbitrary closed-subspace complexity. It is not an arbitrary parameter slice or a routine numerical refinement.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest sources are Liu--Shen, arXiv:2609.32334v1, and Kania--Laustsen, arXiv:1507.01213. The result is limited to ideals containing the compact operators and to the triangular realization \(X_V\). Because the final ideal calculation is short, an abstract equivalent may exist under different triangular-algebra terminology.
