For \(L=u+v+w/a_3\), the interaction terms cancel and \(L'=-a_1u(u-b)^2/(u+b_2)-a_2v-(a_4/a_3)w\le0\). The zero-derivative set is exactly \(E_0=(0,0,0)\) and \(E_b=(b,0,0)\), both equilibria. Boundedness follows from the Lyapunov sublevel, and LaSalle puts every omega-limit set inside those two points; monotonicity of \(u\) excludes accumulation on both, so every nonnegative solution converges to exactly one. The source itself lists the two axial equilibria, which coincide at the nonzero point under the equality, so its claim that the origin is globally asymptotically stable at equality cannot be correct.
