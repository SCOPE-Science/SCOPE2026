# Same-model review

## Correctness
**PASS.** Under \(b_1=1/a_1=:b\), direct substitution gives
\[
u'=-\frac{a_1u(u-b)^2}{u+b_2}-uv.
\]
For \(L=u+v+w/a_3\), all trophic-transfer terms cancel and
\[
L'=-\frac{a_1u(u-b)^2}{u+b_2}-a_2v-\frac{a_4}{a_3}w.
\]
The zero set is exactly the two equilibria \(E_0\) and \(E_b\). Compact Lyapunov sublevels give global boundedness; LaSalle gives omega-limits in those two points; monotonicity of \(u\) yields convergence to one point. On \(v=0\), the scalar equation gives the exact basin split, and the reciprocal variable \(1/(u-b)\) has derivative tending to \(1/(b+b_2)\), proving the stated sharp rate. The exact-rational checker independently replays the algebraic identities on rational data.

## Originality
**PASS.** The source itself contains the tension that motivates the result: Theorem 1 asserts global attraction to \(E_0\) at \(b_1=1/a_1\), while the later equality-case analysis explicitly retains the second nonhyperbolic equilibrium \(E_b\). Searches by title, DOI, equality condition, equilibrium aliases, global-stability wording, “semistable,” and algebraic-rate terminology did not locate a source-specific correction. Broader strong-Allee literature concerns different equations or the nondegenerate threshold/carrying-state separation. The later tritrophic chapter uses a different model and does not cover the identity above.

## Value
**PASS.** The equality is not an arbitrary slice: it is exactly the condition used by the source to claim global extinction and exactly where its two axial equilibria merge. Correcting the global phase portrait changes the ecological interpretation at that threshold. The exact invariant-plane basin split and coefficient \(b+b_2\) additionally quantify the critical slowing that replaces exponential relaxation at the nonhyperbolic state.
