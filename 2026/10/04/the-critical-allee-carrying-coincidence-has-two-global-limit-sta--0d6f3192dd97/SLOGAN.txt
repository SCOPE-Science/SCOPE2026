At the source model's critical equality \(b_1=1/a_1=:b\), extinction is not global: every nonnegative trajectory converges to either \(E_0\) or the coincident axial equilibrium \(E_b=(b,0,0)\). On the invariant plane \(v=0\), the basin split is exact and trajectories starting above \(b\) satisfy \(t(u(t)-b)\to b+b_2\).
