# Same-model review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The proof reconstructs all quantities from supporting-line distances. Affinity reduces the universal point statement to four vertices. The vertex defect has the exact two-square decomposition
\[
2E=(x-z)^2+(x+z-\sqrt2\,y)^2.
\]
The equality argument uses strict positivity of barycentric weights for an interior point and the positivity of the four vertex defects. No numerical sampling is promoted to an infinite proof.

## Originality
**PASS.** The 2016 source poses the all-\(n\) conjecture and explicitly identifies the triangle as the proved case. Current source inspection found no posted answer. The later full text arXiv:2105.07885 generalizes the Dao–Nguyen–Pham inequality only for a triangle and three circumcircle tangents. Classical and weighted polygon Erdős–Mordell results use different left-hand quantities and do not imply the four-tangent statement. The closest indexed records concern different tangential- or cyclic-quadrilateral invariants. Residual risk remains for inaccessible or unindexed literature, including the unretrieved full 2016 Forum Geometricorum PDF and the unread full 2005 weighted-polygon paper.

## Value
**PASS.** This is the entire quadrilateral case of a natural all-polygon conjecture with the conjectured sharp coefficient, plus an equality classification. It is the first dimension/order beyond the known triangle case rather than a parameter tweak or finite computation.

## Closest literature and limitations
The primary conjecture is MathOverflow 244984 (2016-07-24), which cites Dao–Nguyen–Pham's triangle theorem. Tran's 2021 full text remains triangle-only. Gueron–Shafrir's polygon theorem concerns the classical weighted vertex/side-distance structure, not circumcircle tangent distances. The result does not address \(n\ge5\).
