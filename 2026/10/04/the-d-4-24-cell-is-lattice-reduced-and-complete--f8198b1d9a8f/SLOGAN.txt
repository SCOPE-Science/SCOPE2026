The \(D_4\) root 24-cell \(C=\operatorname{conv}\{\pm e_i\pm e_j\}\) is both lattice reduced and lattice complete with respect to \(D_4^*\). The exact identity \(C=(V_{D_4})^*=2V_{D_4^*}\) turns the two Voronoi conclusions of Codenotti--Freyer into one origin-symmetric four-dimensional example, answering their Question 5.3 affirmatively.
