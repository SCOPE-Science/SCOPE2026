The smooth degree-drop surface in Calussi--Carlini--Fatabbi--Lorenzini Example 4.2 is the cubic scroll \(S(1,2)\), with ideal given by three explicit \(2\times2\) minors. Its degree \(3\) comes from resolving one simple base point of the \((1,2)\) system and contracting the strict transform of the fiber \(z_0=0\).
