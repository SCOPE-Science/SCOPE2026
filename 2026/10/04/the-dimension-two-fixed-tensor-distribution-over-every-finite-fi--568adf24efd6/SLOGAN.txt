For every finite field \(\mathbf F_q\), the fixed-space dimension of \(M^T\otimes M^T\otimes M^{-1}\) for \(M\in\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbf F_q)\) takes only the values \(0,1,2,3,4,8\), and each fiber has an explicit closed formula in \(q\). This completely solves the dimension-two slice of Verhulst's tensor-eigenspace counting question and its Burnside sum recovers the classical two-dimensional algebra count.
