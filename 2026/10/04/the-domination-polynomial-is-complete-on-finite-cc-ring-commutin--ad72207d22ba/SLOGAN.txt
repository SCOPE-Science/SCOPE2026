For finite CC-rings, the ordinary domination polynomial of the commuting graph uniquely recovers the sizes of all clique components, so it determines the commuting graph up to isomorphism. The recovery is explicit through cyclotomic factorization after the substitution \(y=1+x\); when \(R/Z(R)\cong C_p\times C_p\), it also recovers \(p\), \(|Z(R)|\), and \(|R|\).
