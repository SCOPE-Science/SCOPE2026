# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The published sufficient criterion and already-settled equal-degree cases reduce the higher-dimensional gap to the cubic containing a quadric cone. For that case the package separates a second cone by the gcd degree s of the two restricted quadrics on the hyperplane intersection. In dimension n>=4 the first cone's restriction has rank at least four and is irreducible, so s is 0 or 2. The s=0 locus has incidence codimension exceeding the full second-cone parameter dimension by 2n-2. In the s=2 locus, if the second polar vertex is outside the intersection the restricted quadric is unique up to scalar; if it lies in the intersection, the restriction kernel has vector dimension 2n-2, giving total parameter dimension at most 4n-4, still below \(D_n(2)=(n-1)(2n+1)\). I independently rechecked the package's binomial identities \(A-H_0=D+2n-2\), \(A-H_2=D\), the d=4 criterion margin, and \(4n-4<D\) for n>=4.
