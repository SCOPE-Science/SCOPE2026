For every \(n\ge2\) and \(d\ge m\ge2\), the incidence of degree-\(d\) hypersurfaces containing degree-\(m\) involutive cones is birational onto its image except at \((m,d)=(2,2)\), where the generic degree is \(2n\). The new cubic-quadric argument closes the exact higher-dimensional range left open for \(n\ge4\).
