For a positive diagonal system with at least three distinct active eigenvalues, the two-step Lanczos approximation to \(e^{-tA}b\) has a finite and exactly computable positivity horizon. Beyond \(t_*=(\theta_2-\theta_1)^{-1}\log((L-\theta_1)/(L-\theta_2))\), every coordinate belonging to the largest eigenvalue becomes negative even though the exact semigroup remains strictly positive.
