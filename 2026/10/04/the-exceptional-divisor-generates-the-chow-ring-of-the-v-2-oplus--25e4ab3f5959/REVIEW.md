# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof is formal but substantive: it uses the actual affine-torsor presentation of the Fan--Li model, homotopy invariance of Chow groups for affine bundles, and the Cartier-divisor line bundle determined by the tautological section. The sign \([F]=-h\) was checked from \(L=\mathcal O(-1)\). No numerical experiment is being promoted to an infinite statement.

## Originality
PASS. The closest source is Fan--Li itself. It proves the torsor description and computes Grothendieck classes and Euler characteristics, but the checked full text does not state a Chow ring, Picard generator, exceptional divisor class, or normal bundle. Targeted published-finding corpus and web searches did not locate an equivalent or stronger statement. Yasuda's wild McKay result predicts an Euler characteristic, which does not imply the accepted intersection-theoretic conclusion. Residual risk: an unindexed source could contain the same refinement.

## Value
PASS. The result determines the distinguished exceptional class inside the full integral Chow ring and gives its normal bundle. This packages the birational geometry of the unique exceptional divisor into a reusable intersection-theoretic invariant, finer than the known Grothendieck-class/Euler computation and directly relevant to line bundles and further contractions on the resolution.

## Closest literature and limitations
The claim is restricted to the \(V_2^{\oplus n}\) family. It does not extend the classification or address the singular natural models for larger Jordan blocks. Its main geometric input is Fan--Li Proposition 3.6, so the contribution is an intersection-theoretic consequence of that presentation rather than a new resolution construction.
