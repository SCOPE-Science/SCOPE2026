For the Fan--Li model resolving \(V_2^{\oplus n}/C_p\), the full integral Chow ring is \(\mathbf Z[h]/(h^n)\), and the exceptional divisor is the negative hyperplane class: \([F]=-h\). Its normal bundle is the pulled-back tautological line \(\mathcal O(-1)\), so \(F\) itself generates \(\operatorname{Pic}(W)\).
