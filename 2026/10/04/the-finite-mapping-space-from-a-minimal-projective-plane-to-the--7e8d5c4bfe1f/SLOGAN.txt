For the 13-point Cianci--Ottina model \(P\) of \(\mathbb{RP}^2\), the finite compact-open mapping space \(\operatorname{Map}(P,S^0\circledast S^0)\) has \(868\) maps and collapses by \(864\) beat-point deletions to its four constant maps. Thus its core is the four-point finite circle and its homotopy type is \(S^1\).
