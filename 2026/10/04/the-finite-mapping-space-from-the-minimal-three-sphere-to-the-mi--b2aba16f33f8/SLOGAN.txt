The compact-open mapping space from the canonical minimal finite \(3\)-sphere \(X_3\) to the canonical minimal finite \(2\)-sphere \(X_2\) has \(738\) points, and an explicit sequence of \(732\) beat-point deletions reduces it to the six constant maps. Therefore its core is \(X_2\) and \(\operatorname{Map}(X_3,X_2)\simeq X_2\), even though the classical mapping space \(\operatorname{Map}(S^3,S^2)\) has infinitely many components.
