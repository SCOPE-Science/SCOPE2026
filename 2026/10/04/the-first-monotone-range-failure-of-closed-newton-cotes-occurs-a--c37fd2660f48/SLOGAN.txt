For closed Newton–Cotes quadrature, negative individual weights do not destroy the range of bounded monotone data: the exact criterion is nonnegativity of every cumulative weight. The first failure is the \(13\)-point rule \(n=12\), with sharp undershoot \(147227/750750\), while the \(14\)-point rule \(n=13\) unexpectedly restores range preservation despite six negative weights.
