For \(n=2^a p\), strongly pseudoperfect representations are governed exactly by a bit-mask quotient \(p=(2^{a+1}-1-A_I)/(1+B_I)\). The first nonperfect case that genuinely needs more than one omitted complementary divisor pair is \(11776=2^9\cdot23\), uniquely omitting \((16,736)\) and \((64,184)\).
