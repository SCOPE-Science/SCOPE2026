For the Heawood graph \(H\), presented as the incidence graph of the Fano plane with its shortest-path metric, \(\dim_{\mathbb F_2}\operatorname{MH}_{3,4}(H;\mathbb F_2)=336\). This is the first off-diagonal bidegree not forced to vanish by the checked girth theorem for a girth-six graph, and the graph lies outside the checked geodetic exact theory.
