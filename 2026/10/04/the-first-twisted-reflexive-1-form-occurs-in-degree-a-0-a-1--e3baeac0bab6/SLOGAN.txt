For a well-formed quasi-smooth general-type hypersurface \(S_d\subset\mathbf P(a_0,a_1,a_2,a_3)\) with ordered weights and \(d>\sum_i a_i\), the least twist carrying a nonzero reflexive \(1\)-form is exactly \(a_0+a_1\). The threshold is attained by the weighted Koszul form \(a_1x_1\,dx_0-a_0x_0\,dx_1\).
