The four-point minimal finite circle already realizes both classical free-homotopy classes into the 13-point model \(P_2^2\) of \(\mathbb{RP}^2\), without subdividing the domain. Its mapping poset has \(853\) maps in components of sizes \(721\) and \(132\); the smaller component is exactly the maps nonzero on \(H_1(-;\mathbf F_2)\), while the larger component strongly deformation retracts onto the constant-map copy of \(P_2^2\).
