# Review

## Correctness
PASS. Membership in \(\overline D\) is exactly vanishing of the pulled-back extension class in \(H^1(L^{-1}(D))\). The resulting split pullback gives a lift \(L(-D)\to E\). Since \(\deg L(-D)=g\), Riemann--Roch gives a nonzero section; its nonzero image in \(L\) makes it independent of the canonical section from \(\mathcal O_X\). The span dimension follows from exact cohomology dimensions.

## Originality
PASS. The initiating 2025 paper explicitly says the genus-five \(\mathbf P^3\)-existence question is unknown. It also recalls the secant indeterminacy locus and separately defines \(\mathcal H\), but does not state or prove the bridge \(\operatorname{Sec}^{g-1}(X)\subset\mathcal H\). Targeted published-finding corpus and public-literature searches found no equivalent or stronger statement. Older Bertram material is a residual source risk because it supplies the extension/secant framework, but no inspected source was found to contain the Brill--Noether containment or the genus-five correction.

## Value
PASS. The result directly resolves a concrete open uncertainty in a recent algebraic-geometry paper and identifies a canonical family, rather than a single accidental linear space. It is structurally useful because it links the entire unstable secant locus to the determinantal section-jump hypersurface.

Closest literature and limitations: Biswas--Iyer arXiv:2505.15749v2, especially Remark 3.8; Bertram's extension/secant framework. The theorem does not claim genus-five unirationality, because the precise scope of the older quintic-unirationality theorem was not re-established from a primary full text here.
