Every degree-\((g-1)\) secant span \(\overline D\cong\mathbf P^{g-2}\) in the Bertram extension space lies inside the determinantal Brill--Noether hypersurface \(\mathcal H\). In genus \(5\), this gives an explicit family of \(\mathbf P^3\)'s in \(\mathcal H\subset\mathbf P^{12}\), resolving the existence uncertainty in Biswas--Iyer, Remark 3.8.
