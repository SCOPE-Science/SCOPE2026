For the truncated tetrahedral graph with shortest-path metric, the inclusive Rips filtration passes from 12 points to \(\bigvee^3 S^1\), then to \(\bigvee^5 S^2\), and finally to a simplex at the integer thresholds \(1,2,3\). An explicit collapse and acyclic discrete-Morse certificate prove the two nontrivial homotopy types.
