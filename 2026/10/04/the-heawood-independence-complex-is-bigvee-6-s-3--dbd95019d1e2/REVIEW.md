# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

A fresh independent replay reconstructed the Fano incidence graph, enumerated all 458 independent sets with cardinality counts 1,14,70,154,147,56,14,2, and rebuilt the stated sequential matching. It leaves exactly six critical simplices, all of dimension three, and a complete orientation of all 1,582 Hasse covers is acyclic. In reduced discrete-Morse form this gives a basepoint and six three-cells with no lower positive-dimensional cells, hence a wedge of six three-spheres. The package's separate mod-two homology computation is consistent but is not used by itself to infer homotopy type.

## originality

PASS

Best-of-knowledge originality passes. The exact Heawood/Fano-incidence aliases were checked. Perarnau--Perkins give the complete Heawood independence polynomial, which fixes the face vector but not the homotopy type. Nilakantan--Shukla prove an exact homotopy theorem for a cyclic-neighborhood regular-bipartite family, but their 14-vertex cubic graph is not the Heawood graph. Tsukuda gives a general incidence-graph Alexander-duality reduction rather than an evaluated Fano-plane wedge. Resultary returned the assigned record as the exact hit and no stronger published SCOPE implication.

## value

PASS

The Heawood graph is a canonical highly symmetric cubic girth-six incidence graph, and its independence complex is a natural topological invariant already motivated by an exact independent-set extremal literature. The result determines homotopy, not just counts or homology, and contrasts with nearby regular-bipartite families whose homotopy types differ.
