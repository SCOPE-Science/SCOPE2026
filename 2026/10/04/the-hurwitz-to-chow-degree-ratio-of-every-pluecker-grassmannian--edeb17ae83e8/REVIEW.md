# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The tangent bundle of the ordinary Grassmannian is
\[
T_{\operatorname{Gr}(k,n)}\simeq S^\vee\otimes Q,
\]
so its first Chern class is \(nH\) and
\[
K_{\operatorname{Gr}(k,n)}=-nH.
\]
For a general curve cut by \(d-1\) Pluecker hyperplanes, with \(d=k(n-k)\), adjunction gives
\[
2g-2=(d-1-n)D.
\]
The verified general Hurwitz theorem then yields
\[
\operatorname{Hdeg}=2D+2g-2=(d+1-n)D=(k-1)(n-k-1)D.
\]
The verified coisotropic-polar theorem identifies this with \(\delta_1\), while \(\delta_0=D\). The bundled checker is regression evidence only.

## Originality
PASS. The two closest primary sources were inspected at full-text level. One gives the general Hurwitz-degree theorem and the other gives the general coisotropic-polar correspondence. Neither inspected source states the ordinary-Grassmannian specialization. Direct searches for the Hurwitz degree, first polar degree, sectional-genus form, and exact multiplicative factor did not locate an equivalent result.

Residual risk: the specialization is short after the two general inputs are combined, so an older unindexed source could contain it.

## Value
PASS. The result explicitly computes the discriminant degree governing non-transverse complementary linear sections of every ordinary Pluecker Grassmannian. It also gives the exact first-polar to Chow-degree ratio and singles out the Klein quadric as the unique equal-degree case. This is directly relevant to coisotropic geometry and to the degree parameter used in numerical linear-section conditioning.
