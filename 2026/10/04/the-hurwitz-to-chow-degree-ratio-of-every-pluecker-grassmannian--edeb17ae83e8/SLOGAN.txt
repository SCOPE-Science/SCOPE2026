For every non-linear Pluecker Grassmannian \(\operatorname{Gr}(k,n)\), the Hurwitz degree is exactly \((k-1)(n-k-1)\) times the Chow degree. Equivalently, its first two polar degrees satisfy \(\delta_1/\delta_0=(k-1)(n-k-1)\), with equality of Hurwitz and Chow degrees only for \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,4)\).
