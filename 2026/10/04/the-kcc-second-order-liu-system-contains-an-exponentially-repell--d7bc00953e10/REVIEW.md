# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The published SODE was reconstructed as a four-dimensional first-order flow. Direct differentiation gives \(\dot Q=cQ\) for \(Q=\dot z+cz-hx^2\). The inverse reconstruction on \(Q=0\) recovers all three Liu equations exactly, while \(x=0\), \(z=Ae^{ct}+Be^{-ct}\) supplies an explicit extra SODE family when \(A\ne0\). Linearization confirms that the full SODE adds the eigenvalue \(+c\).

Closest indexed findings concern other reduction or stability problems. Residual risk remains that an unindexed correction exists.

## Value
PASS. The issue is structural because the article treats the four-dimensional SODE as equivalent to a three-dimensional physical flow and interprets unconstrained deviation growth dynamically. The exact hypersurface identifies which SODE trajectories and variations are physical, and the source's \(c\)-rate is shown to be the normal growth of the added degree of freedom.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest literature is the source itself, DOI 10.3390/math12131981, together with the Lorenz KCC reformulation arXiv:1504.02880. This result does not develop a constrained KCC replacement and does not reverse the ordinary linear instability of the Liu origin; it isolates the non-equivalence and its exact extra mode.
