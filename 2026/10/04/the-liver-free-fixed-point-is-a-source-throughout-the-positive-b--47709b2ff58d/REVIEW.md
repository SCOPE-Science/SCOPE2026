# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The source's exact diagonal Jacobian at the liver-free fixed point has multipliers \(1+h(1-g)\) and \(1+h(1-\zeta)\). Under \(0<g<1\), \(0<\zeta<1\), and \(h>0\), each is strictly larger than one. The printed sink thresholds are both negative, so the stated sink inequality is simultaneously satisfied for every positive \(h\). The exact rational witness \(g=1/2\), \(\zeta=1/3\), \(h=1\) gives multipliers \(3/2\) and \(5/3\) and thresholds \(-4\) and \(-3\).

## Value
PASS. The local type of the liver-free state is a structural ingredient in the paper's fixed-point classification. Reversing source versus sink throughout the positive boundary-equilibrium parameter regime changes the qualitative interpretation of nearby biological trajectories and is therefore mathematically consequential rather than a notational cleanup.
