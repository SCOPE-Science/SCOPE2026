For bounded-growth age-of-information processes under a common stochastic dominator, \(\mathbb E\log(1+T)<\infty\) is exactly the universal moment threshold forcing \(\tau_n/n\to0\) almost surely. If the logarithmic moment diverges, a unit-growth dominated process can have asymptotically linear spikes, while the finite-log condition is still enough for the vanishing harmonic delay window used in distributed stochastic approximation.
