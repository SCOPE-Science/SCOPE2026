Wuebben's compact Calabi--Yau threefold with one anticanonical \(\mathbb F_1\)-cone point has singularity link \(S^2\times S^3\). Its ordinary Euler characteristic is \(-211\), so the singular threefold is not a rational Poincaré-duality space and cannot have the homotopy type of a closed oriented \(6\)-manifold.
