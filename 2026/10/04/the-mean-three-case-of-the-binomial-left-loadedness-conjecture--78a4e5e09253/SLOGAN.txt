The binomial left-loadedness conjecture holds for the entire mean-three slice: \(\operatorname{Bin}(n,3/n)\) is left-loaded for every integer \(n\ge6\). For \(n>6\), Simmons' inequality gives \(\alpha_1>0\) while an explicit point-mass factorization gives \(\alpha_3<0\), which is enough when there are only three coefficients.
