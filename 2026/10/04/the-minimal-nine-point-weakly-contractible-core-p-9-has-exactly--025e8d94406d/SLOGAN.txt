For the minimal nine-point weakly contractible noncontractible finite space \(P(9)\), exactly eleven nonempty opens are contractible, and only \(U_g\) and \(P(9)\setminus\{g\}\) are inclusion-maximal among them. Those two opens form the unique two-member contractible-open cover, so \(\operatorname{gcat}(P(9))=1\) in the reduced convention (equivalently \(2\) unreduced).
