The unique Liu--Shao equality surface \(X_{18}\subset\mathbf P(2,6,7,9)\) has exactly four singularities: \(A_3\), \(A_1\), \(A_2\), and \(\frac1{21}(1,8)\). The last point has canonical index \(7\) and resolution chain \([-3,-3,-3]\), so the minimal resolution has nine exceptional curves in total.
