PASS. The claim is finite and exhaustively checkable. The verifier enumerates all \(6^6\) set maps, obtains exactly \(446\) order-preserving maps, constructs the pointwise-order mapping space, and checks every one of \(432\) beat-point deletions against the current induced poset. The remaining fourteen maps are exactly six constants and eight homeomorphisms; their induced order is verified to be \(X_2\sqcup D_8\), with no remaining beat points. Barmak's beat-point theorem then turns the checked deletion sequence into a strong deformation retraction.

## Value
PASS. Function spaces are where finite-space homotopies live, so determining the core of a natural self-map space is structural rather than a raw census. The result shows that the entire null component retains a six-point \(S^2\)-core, a strictly stronger statement than knowing it is connected. It also gives a compact exact example of how a minimal finite model can preserve the weak type of a space while its self-map space has its own finite strong-homotopy structure.
