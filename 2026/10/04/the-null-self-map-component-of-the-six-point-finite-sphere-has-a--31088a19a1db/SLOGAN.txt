For the canonical six-point finite model \(X_2\) of \(S^2\), the \(446\)-point compact-open self-map space collapses by beat-point deletions to exactly the six constant maps plus eight isolated homeomorphisms. Thus its core is \(X_2\sqcup D_8\), and the null self-map component strongly deformation retracts onto a copy of \(X_2\).
