For every smooth projective surface, the numerical Euler pairing is integrally Smith-equivalent to a hyperbolic plane plus the negative Néron–Severi intersection lattice. Hence its finite cokernel is the Néron–Severi discriminant group and \(\det M_\chi(X)=|\operatorname{disc}N^1(X)|\), so Euler unimodularity is exactly Néron–Severi unimodularity.
