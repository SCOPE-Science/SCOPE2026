# The pointwise-provability quotient is a cyclic left act
## Finding

Let \(T\supseteq\mathrm{PA}\) be any theory, and fix the acceptable numbering and primitive-recursive program-composition operation
\[
f\bullet g
\]
used by Lengyel.

Write
\[
e\equiv_T d
\]
for **uniform provable equality**:
\[
T\vdash \forall x\,E_{e,d}(x),
\]
and write
\[
e\approx_T d
\]
for **pointwise provable equality**:
\[
\text{for every }n\in\omega,\qquad T\vdash E_{e,d}(\bar n).
\]

Define
\[
M_T=\omega/{\equiv_T},
\qquad
Q_T=\omega/{\approx_T}.
\]

Then:

1. \(M_T\) is a monoid under program composition.
2. The relation induced by \(\approx_T\) on \(M_T\) is always a left congruence.
3. Hence \(Q_T\) is canonically a left \(M_T\)-act:
\[
[f]_{\equiv_T}\cdot[g]_{\approx_T}
=
[f\bullet g]_{\approx_T}.
\]
4. This act is cyclic, generated by
\[
[\mathrm{id}]_{\approx_T}.
\]
5. If \(T\) is consistent, the act is nontrivial.
6. If \(T\) is consistent and recursively enumerable, the induced left congruence is strictly larger than equality on \(M_T\) but is not a right congruence.

Thus the pointwise quotient does not lose all composition structure when its proposed category structure fails. Its exact surviving algebraic structure is a **one-sided quotient**: a cyclic left act over the uniform-provability monoid.

For consistent \(T\), Lengyel's Theorem 3 gives the sharp boundary:
\[
\boxed{
\text{the induced left congruence is two-sided}
\iff
T\text{ proves every true }\Pi^0_1\text{ sentence}.
}
\]

Equivalently, this is exactly the condition under which \(Q_T\) itself acquires representative-independent multiplication.

## Assumptions and scope

The program model, graph formulas \(C_e(x,y)\), Kleene-equality formulas \(E_{e,d}(x)\), and primitive-recursive composition index \(f\bullet g\) are those fixed in the source.

The source supplies the formalized graph law
\[
C_{f\bullet g}(x,z)
\leftrightarrow
\exists y\,
\bigl(C_g(x,y)\wedge C_f(y,z)\bigr),
\]
provably in \(\mathrm{PA}\).

No consistency or recursive-enumerability hypothesis is needed for the existence of the monoid \(M_T\) or for the left action on \(Q_T\).

Consistency is used only to state that \(Q_T\) is nontrivial.

Recursive enumerability, together with consistency, is used for the strict one-sidedness conclusion via Lengyel's obstruction theorem and Gödel's second incompleteness theorem.

The result is stated for unary program indices, exactly where the source already shows the composition obstruction. It does not repair the rejected category structure on the full object system.

## Proof

First observe that
\[
\equiv_T\ \subseteq\ \approx_T,
\]
because a single proof of the universal closure yields each standard numeral instance.

### The uniform quotient is a monoid

We show that \(\equiv_T\) is a two-sided composition congruence.

Suppose
\[
f\equiv_T h.
\]
Then \(T\) proves
\[
\forall y\,E_{f,h}(y).
\]
Using the formalized composition graph law, this universal theorem can be substituted beneath the existential quantifier over the intermediate output. For every fixed \(g\),
\[
f\bullet g\equiv_T h\bullet g.
\]

Likewise, if
\[
g\equiv_T k,
\]
then the universally proved equality of the inner graph formulas can be substituted directly into the same composition law, giving
\[
f\bullet g\equiv_T f\bullet k.
\]

Thus composition descends to \(M_T\).

Associativity and the identity law also hold modulo \(\equiv_T\). Indeed, expansion of the formalized graph equation gives, in \(\mathrm{PA}\),
\[
((f\bullet g)\bullet h)(x)
\simeq
(f\bullet(g\bullet h))(x)
\]
uniformly in \(x\), while the fixed identity index satisfies the analogous uniform left- and right-identity graph equations.

Hence
\[
M_T=\omega/{\equiv_T}
\]
is a monoid.

### Pointwise equality becomes a left congruence on the uniform quotient

Because
\[
\equiv_T\subseteq\approx_T,
\]
pointwise equality induces an equivalence relation on \(M_T\):
\[
[g]_{\equiv_T}\mathrel{\mathcal L_T}[h]_{\equiv_T}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
g\approx_T h.
\]

This definition does not depend on the representatives. Changing either representative by \(\equiv_T\) changes it by \(\approx_T\), and transitivity of \(\approx_T\) preserves the truth value.

Lengyel proves the crucial one-sided compatibility for every \(T\supseteq\mathrm{PA}\):
\[
g\approx_T h
\Longrightarrow
f\bullet g\approx_T f\bullet h.
\]

Therefore
\[
a\mathrel{\mathcal L_T}b
\Longrightarrow
ca\mathrel{\mathcal L_T}cb
\]
for all \(a,b,c\in M_T\).

So \(\mathcal L_T\) is a left congruence on the monoid \(M_T\).

### The canonical cyclic action

A quotient of a monoid by a left congruence is canonically a left act. Explicitly,
\[
[f]_{\equiv_T}\cdot[g]_{\approx_T}
=
[f\bullet g]_{\approx_T}.
\]

Well-definedness in the first variable follows from uniform compatibility under right composition; well-definedness in the second variable is exactly Lengyel's always-valid compatibility under left composition.

Associativity follows from uniform associativity:
\[
([f]_{\equiv_T}[g]_{\equiv_T})\cdot[h]_{\approx_T}
=
[f]_{\equiv_T}\cdot
([g]_{\equiv_T}\cdot[h]_{\approx_T}),
\]
and the identity class acts trivially.

The act is cyclic. For every \(g\),
\[
[g]_{\approx_T}
=
[g\bullet\mathrm{id}]_{\approx_T}
=
[g]_{\equiv_T}\cdot[\mathrm{id}]_{\approx_T}.
\]

Hence
\[
Q_T
\cong
M_T/{\mathcal L_T}
\]
as left \(M_T\)-acts.

### Nontriviality and strict one-sidedness

If \(T\) is consistent, the identity program and a program whose graph is provably empty cannot be pointwise equal. At input \(0\), \(\mathrm{PA}\) proves convergence of the identity program and divergence of the empty program, so consistency forbids a proof of their Kleene equality. Thus \(Q_T\) has at least two elements.

Now assume \(T\) is consistent and recursively enumerable.

Lengyel's Theorem 1 supplies programs \(f,g\) such that
\[
f\approx_T\mathrm{id}
\]
but
\[
f\bullet g\not\approx_T\mathrm{id}\bullet g.
\]

If \(f\equiv_T\mathrm{id}\), uniform compatibility under right composition would imply
\[
f\bullet g\equiv_T\mathrm{id}\bullet g,
\]
hence pointwise equality, a contradiction.

Therefore
\[
f\not\equiv_T\mathrm{id},
\]
so \(\mathcal L_T\) strictly enlarges equality on \(M_T\).

The same displayed pair proves that \(\mathcal L_T\) is not stable under right multiplication. It is therefore genuinely a left congruence and not a two-sided congruence.

Finally, for consistent \(T\), Lengyel's Theorem 3 proves
\[
\approx_T\text{ is a composition congruence}
\iff
T\text{ proves every true }\Pi^0_1\text{ sentence}.
\]
Because \(\equiv_T\subseteq\approx_T\), this is exactly the condition that the descended left congruence \(\mathcal L_T\) on \(M_T\) is two-sided.

This proves all assertions.

## Verification

The proof was replayed directly against the source's formalized composition graph equation and its two equality notions.

The critical checks were:

\[
\equiv_T\subseteq\approx_T;
\]

uniform equality is preserved in both arguments of composition by substitution in the universally quantified graph equation;

the source explicitly proves
\[
g\approx_T h
\Longrightarrow
f\bullet g\approx_T f\bullet h
\]
for every \(T\supseteq\mathrm{PA}\);

the source's obstruction theorem gives a pair witnessing failure in the opposite argument for every consistent recursively enumerable extension of \(\mathrm{PA}\);

and Theorem 3 identifies the exact condition under which the missing compatibility returns.

No finite experiment is used to prove an infinite or proof-theoretic assertion. No external proof certificate is required.

## Relationship to prior work

Lengyel's paper identifies the defect in the proposed pointwise-provability category: pointwise equality is always compatible when the **inner** program is replaced, but can fail when the **outer** program is replaced. It then characterizes exactly when full two-sided congruence is recovered.

The paper does not formulate the surviving structure as a quotient act. In particular, the checked full text contains no occurrence of “left congruence” or “action.” Combining its explicit one-sided compatibility with the separately defined uniform provable equality yields the structural statement above:
\[
Q_T
\]
is always a cyclic left act over the uniform quotient monoid
\[
M_T.
\]

The older papers of Montagna and of Di Paola--Montagna study syntactic recursion categories built from provability equivalences. Their published construction is precisely the setting re-examined by Lengyel. The accessible historical material confirms the two syntactic systems but does not provide the quotient-act formulation checked here.

The result therefore does not contradict the source's negative theorem. It sharpens its interpretation: the failed pointwise quotient retains exactly the algebraic structure naturally associated with an always-valid one-sided congruence.

## Limitations

This result is a structural corollary of the source's formalized composition law and one-sided compatibility theorem; it does not strengthen the source's incompleteness obstruction.

It does not claim that the left \(M_T\)-action is faithful. Distinct uniform-provability classes may induce the same transformation of \(Q_T\).

The result does not restore a category on the pointwise quotient. For consistent recursively enumerable \(T\), the failure of right compatibility remains decisive.

Historical syntactic-category terminology varies, and an equivalent one-sided-action observation may be implicit in older categorical treatments even though it was not located in the checked sources.
