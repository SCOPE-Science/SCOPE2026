For the global relative entropy of entanglement benchmark written in arXiv:2605.16463v1, identity LOCC already attains the post-channel optimum. In the paper's depolarizing Bell-pair example this optimum is exactly \(n[1-H_2(p)]\), so it cannot simultaneously be upper-bounded by an exponentially smaller \(nE_R(\rho_p)e^{-\kappa n}\) with \(\kappa>0\).
