The published six-state fractional prostate-cancer recurrence advances both androgen and PSA with the androgen field \(H_5\), forcing the spurious invariant \(P_n-A_n=P_0-A_0\). The model itself has a distinct PSA field \(H_6\); replacing \(H_5\) by \(H_6\) in the PSA update removes the inconsistency.
