The printed recovery recurrence in a fractional SIAQR model switches from the recovered history \(R\) to the quarantine history \(Q\) inside its higher history corrections. Because the continuous model exactly conserves total population, this substitution has a sharp diagnostic: at \(\gamma=1/2\) an exact admissible history is mapped from total population \(1\) to approximately \(0.5079210632\); replacing those \(Q\)-arguments by \(R\)-arguments restores the missing cancellation.
