The Lassa-fever paper declares a Caputo-Fabrizio model and even writes the corresponding Losada-Nieto integral, but its boundedness transients and Adams-Bashforth-Moulton simulations use the classical Caputo power-law calculus instead. For \(0<\alpha<1\), the two operators have different scalar dynamics, so the printed fractional-order “memory effect” simulations do not validate the stated Caputo-Fabrizio model.
