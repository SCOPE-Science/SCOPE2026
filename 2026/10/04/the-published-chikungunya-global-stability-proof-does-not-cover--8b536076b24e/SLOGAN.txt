The published \(\mathcal R_0<1\) global-stability proof for a 2026 Chikungunya model uses \(\pi_h\ge\pi_m\), but the paper's calibrated values satisfy \(\pi_h=0.0684<0.2223=\pi_m\). Thus the displayed proof does not cover the parameter regime used in the paper's own numerical study, without implying that the theorem itself is false.
