For the Lyapunov function printed in Theorem 5.2, the exact Caputo derivative is \((\phi+\mu)(\mu+\varsigma+\rho)A(R_0S/S^0-1)\). On the article's stated feasible region it is nonpositive everywhere exactly for \(R_0\le\mu/(\omega+\mu)\), so the published step \(S\le S^0\) does not justify the full \(R_0<1\) global-stability claim.
