The supplement calibrates reactive vaccination with an affine target-relaxation ODE, while the stated and released counterfactual model uses multiplicative removal \( -\nu\,\mathrm{VE}\,S\). For a 150-day campaign with \(\mathrm{VE}=0.93\), halving susceptibility under the simulated law requires \(0.49688\%\) per day for either age group, so the supplement's age-specific \(0.658\%\) and \(0.846\%\) rates do not realize its stated half-targets in that model.
