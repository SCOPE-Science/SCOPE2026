In the 2026 ready-to-eat-food listeriosis subsystem, the published zero-food disease-free point is not an equilibrium for positive total food. The corrected clean-food equilibrium has invasion threshold \(\mathcal R_F=\mathcal R_0^L\left(1+\alpha_3F/(p_f-\alpha_4F)\right)\) when \(p_f>\alpha_4F\), and an exact positive-parameter witness has \(\mathcal R_0^L=1/3\) but \(\mathcal R_F=4/3\).
