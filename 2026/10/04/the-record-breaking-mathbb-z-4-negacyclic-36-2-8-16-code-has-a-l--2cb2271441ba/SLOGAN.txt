The record-breaking \(\mathbb Z_4\) negacyclic \([36,2,8,16]\) code in arXiv:2606.08750 has a linear Gray image: after the natural negacyclic Gray-coordinate reorder it is a binary cyclic \([72,12,16]\) code. Its complete weight enumerator and degree-\(60\) cyclic generator are explicit, its dual distance is \(4\), and its Gray rank and kernel dimensions are both \(12\).
