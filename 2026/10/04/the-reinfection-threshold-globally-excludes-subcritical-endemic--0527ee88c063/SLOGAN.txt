In the Khan–Wang–Liu–Xiong SEIRV model, the paper’s local reinfection threshold \(\beta_2^\ast\) is also a global equilibrium-exclusion threshold below \(R_0=1\): if \(R_0\le1\) and \(\beta_2\le\beta_2^\ast\), no positive endemic equilibrium exists. This proves the equilibrium part of the paper’s Numerical Finding 7.1 without asserting global disease-free convergence.
