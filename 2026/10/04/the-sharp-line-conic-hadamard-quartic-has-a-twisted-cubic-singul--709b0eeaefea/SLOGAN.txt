For the explicit sharp line–conic Hadamard quartic of Calussi–Carlini–Fatabbi–Lorenzini, the previously unspecified one-dimensional singular locus is exactly a twisted cubic. Exact Jacobian-radical identities give \(\sqrt{J}=(q_1,q_2,q_3)\), and the three quadrics cut out a projective re-embedding of the degree-three Veronese curve.
