# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The proof is exhaustive rather than heuristic. In a squarefree word every proper border is shorter than half the word; if u is the longest border, every shorter border of the word is also a border of u. Hence every candidate with at least r borders has the form u v u with u already having at least r-1 borders. Independent reimplementation of this recursion over the normalized ternary alphabet reproduced the successive minima 1, 3, 7, 23, 59, 142, found four normalized minimizers at 142, and verified the displayed witness has exactly the five stated borders. Thus the lower bound and exact-five upper witness meet.
