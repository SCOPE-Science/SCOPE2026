The printed stochastic pollution-control game is not a probability-valued replicator process: \([0,1]^3\) is not invariant and, for positive noise, \((1,1,1)\) is not even a point equilibrium. The failure comes from deleting the \(1-x\), \(1-y\), and \(1-z\) boundary factors before adding multiplicative noise, so the stochastic stability claims require a rederived boundary-preserving model.
