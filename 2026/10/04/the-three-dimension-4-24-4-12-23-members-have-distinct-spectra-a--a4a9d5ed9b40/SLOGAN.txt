In the \(q=23,n=24\) specialization of the fixed-distance cyclic family, the three dimension-\(4\) members share \([24,4,12]_{23}\) but have different complete weight spectra and generalized Hamming hierarchies. Their hierarchies are \((12,18,21,24)\), \((12,20,22,24)\), and \((12,22,23,24)\), so the three codes are pairwise monomially inequivalent.
