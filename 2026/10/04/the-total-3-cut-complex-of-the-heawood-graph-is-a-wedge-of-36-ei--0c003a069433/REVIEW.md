PASS. The complete finite face poset is reconstructed from the stated Fano-plane incidence model. The staged matching contains \(7{,}932\) valid cover pairs, and a direct orientation check on all \(109{,}410\) Hasse cover relations is acyclic. Exactly \(36\) unmatched faces remain and every one has dimension \(8\); the empty face is paired. The discrete-Morse conclusion therefore gives \(\Delta_3^t(H)\simeq\bigvee^{36}S^8\). Independent mod-\(2\) boundary-rank calculations reproduce reduced Betti number \(36\) only in degree \(8\).

## Value
PASS. The Heawood graph is a standard, highly symmetric incidence graph and gives a natural test beyond the graph families whose total-cut homotopy types are already explicit. An exact wedge decomposition is stronger than a homology census and provides a concrete benchmark for the behavior of total \(3\)-cut complexes on nonchordal bipartite graphs.
