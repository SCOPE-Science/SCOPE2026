# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reconstructs all hypotheses and numerical bounds. The Severi component has dimension \(17\); the rational-image inequality eliminates a double-cover cubic image and forces a triple-cover conic image; the trigonal Hurwitz locus is irreducible of dimension \(17\). The Coppens--Kato criterion gives gonality \(4\) for the general two-nodal sextic normalization, proving the two loci are distinct. The embedded checker replays the arithmetic.

Targeted semantic and public searches found no exact or stronger coverage, and the prior local findings concern different objects.

## Value
PASS. The result fills a geometrically natural boundary case and classifies all components with generic basepoint-free \(g^2_6\), separating birational Severi geometry from the unique non-birational trigonal-conic geometry.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is Haburcak--Teixidor i Bigas, Section 6, especially Proposition 6.2 and Example 6.6. The proof also uses the classical gonality theorem for nodal plane models. The total component set of \(\mathcal M^2_{8,6}\) is not claimed: possible components whose generic relevant series has basepoints remain outside scope. A classical low-genus statement hidden under different notation is the main originality risk.
