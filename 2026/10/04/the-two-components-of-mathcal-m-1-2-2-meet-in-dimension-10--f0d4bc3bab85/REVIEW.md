The incident-line stratum has dimension 3+4=7, while the double-line stratum has dimension at most 6, so \(\dim D=7\). On a general incident-line boundary point the associated scheme is nonplanar, and AJO Proposition 3.9 gives \(h^0(E(1))=1\), so no dimension is lost.
