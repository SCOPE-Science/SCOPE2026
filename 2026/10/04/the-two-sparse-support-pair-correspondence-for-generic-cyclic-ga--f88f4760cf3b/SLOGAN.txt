For a generic window on \(\mathbb Z_N\), every signal supported on two points a distance \(s\) apart has short-time Fourier support either \(N^2\) or \(N^2-\gcd(N,s)\), and both occur. This proves the complete two-sparse stratum of the cyclic Fourier/STFT support-pair correspondence and gives \(\phi(\mathbb Z_N,2)=N^2-N/p\) for the least prime divisor \(p\) of \(N\).
