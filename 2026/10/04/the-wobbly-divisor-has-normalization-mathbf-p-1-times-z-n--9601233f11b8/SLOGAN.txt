The explicit Bhattacharya--Matsubara parametrization \(\nu_n:\mathbf P^1\times Z_n\to W\) is the normalization of the wobbly divisor for every \(n\ge3\), so \(W\) is non-normal. In genus two, the degree-\(32\) wobbly surface has normalization \(\mathbf P^1\times C\) with \(C\) a genus-\(17\) \((2,2,2,2)\) complete-intersection curve.
