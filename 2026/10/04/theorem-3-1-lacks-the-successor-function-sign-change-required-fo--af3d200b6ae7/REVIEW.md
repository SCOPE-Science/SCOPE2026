# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. System (2.2) prints the lower-threshold impulse \(\Delta x=p_1x\), \(\Delta y=-\tau_1\). Therefore an impact point \(B=(h_1,y_B)\) returns to \(B^+=((1+p_1)h_1,y_B-\tau_1)\), so the successor value from \(A\) is exactly \(y_B-\tau_1-y_A\). Theorem 3.1's proof instead inserts \(p_1\) into the predator coordinate and, in the strict case, explicitly computes two positive successor values before invoking the intermediate value theorem. Same-sign values do not establish a zero. The claim is limited to non-establishment, not falsity of the theorem.

## Originality
PASS. Searches using the exact article title, theorem number, reset parameters, successor-function formulas, and sign-change language located the source and general predecessor methods but no correction, erratum, or prior publication identifying this exact pair of inconsistencies. The closest predecessor explicitly treats a successor function that stays positive as having no fixed point, which supports the logical comparison rather than covering this source-specific diagnosis.

## Value
PASS. The affected theorem is the article's first existence theorem for the unilateral intervention and is used as the analytic counterpart of the reported order-1 controlled orbit. Distinguishing a numerical example from a general existence proof matters for the model's management conclusion: the stated hypotheses currently do not provide the opposite sign needed by the advertised successor-function method. The required repair is mathematically concrete—derive an actual second sign or replace the fixed-point argument—rather than cosmetic notation.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest methodological comparison is Tian et al. (2022), DOI 10.1155/2022/9496599, where the Poincaré successor function is returned height minus starting height and an everywhere-positive successor function is explicitly associated with absence of a fixed point. Tian, Liu, and Sun (2024), DOI 10.3934/era.2024297, studies a related bilateral impulsive fishery model but does not imply the missing sign estimate for the 2026 theorem. The residual risk is that an unlocated author correction or an unstated geometric lemma could repair the theorem; neither was found in the inspected material.
