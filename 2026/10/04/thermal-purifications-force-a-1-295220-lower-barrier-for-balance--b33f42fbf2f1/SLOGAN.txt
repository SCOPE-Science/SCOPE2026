For balanced pure loss, finite-support truncations of a thermal purification force every universal information-variance coefficient to be at least \(2x_*(2-x_*)=1.295220475783829\ldots\), where \((2-x_*)e^{x_*}=2\). Thus the known \(4n\) variance estimate can be improved only down to a coefficient no smaller than this explicit barrier; global optimality of the barrier is not claimed.\n
