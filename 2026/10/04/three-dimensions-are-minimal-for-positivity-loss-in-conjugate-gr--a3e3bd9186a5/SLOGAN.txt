Ordinary conjugate gradients can leave the positive cone even when \(A\) is a Stieltjes matrix, \(A^{-1}>0\), \(b\ge0\), and the exact solution is strictly positive. Dimension three is minimal, and for the tridiagonal family \(A_a\) above the second-iterate failure occurs for some \(b_B\) exactly when \(a>4\).
