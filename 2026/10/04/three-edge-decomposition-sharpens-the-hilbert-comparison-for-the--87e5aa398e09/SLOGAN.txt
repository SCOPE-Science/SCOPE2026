For the first dyadic-tree variation norm of Argyros--Motakis, three-edge coloring gives \((\sqrt2-1)/\sqrt3\le N_1(x)/\|x\|_2\le\sqrt2\), with the upper constant sharp. Its segment indicators satisfy the exact identity \(N_1(\sum_{u\in s}e_u)=\sqrt{|s|}\), quantifying both its Hilbertian geometry and its branching failure.
