# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The theorem is correct. For a compactly supported invariant measure, applying the generator to antiderivatives of arbitrary continuous functions of one coordinate gives zero conditional drift and hence the three conditional expectations. Their integrated and weighted versions yield the exact defect \(\mathbb E[z(z-c)]=(c/b)\mathbb E[(y-x)^2]\). Zero defect forces the invariant support into \(y=x\); tangency then gives either the origin or \(z=c\), and invariance of \(z=c\) forces the two nonzero equilibria. Therefore every other compact stationary state has positive mass where \(z(z-c)>0\), outside the equilibrium-height slab. The polynomial checker correctly verifies the algebraic coboundary but is not used as a substitute for the invariant-measure argument.
