For the minimal nine-point weakly contractible but non-contractible finite space \(R\), the self-map space has \(12{,}575\) points but only three homotopy components: one containing every non-homeomorphism and two isolated automorphisms. Thus the self-homotopy monoid is \(C_2\) with an absorbing zero adjoined, and the same holds for \(R^{\mathrm{op}}\).
