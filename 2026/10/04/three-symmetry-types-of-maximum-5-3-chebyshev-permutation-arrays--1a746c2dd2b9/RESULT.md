# Three symmetry types of maximum \( (5,3) \) Chebyshev permutation arrays
## Finding
Among maximum \( (5,3) \) Chebyshev permutation arrays \(C\subseteq S_5\), there are exactly 192 labeled maxima. Here
\[
d_\infty(\pi,\sigma)=\max_{1\le i\le5}|\pi_i-\sigma_i|,
\]
and the already-known optimum is \(P(5,3)=10\). Under the natural isometry subgroup generated by arbitrary coordinate permutations and the global value reversal \(x\mapsto6-x\), of order \(5!\cdot2=240\), the maxima split into exactly three orbits of sizes \(24,48,120\). Their stabilizer orders are \(10,5,2\), respectively. If \(N_j\) denotes the number of unordered pairs of codewords at distance \(j\), the three orbit types have spectra \((N_3,N_4)=(30,15),(25,20),(28,17)\), respectively.

## Assumptions and scope
A codeword is a permutation of \(\{1,2,3,4,5\}\). A code is a set, so codeword order is irrelevant. "Labeled" means the five coordinate positions and the five numerical symbols retain their given labels. The equivalence statement is only for the explicit natural subgroup consisting of coordinate permutations and the single nontrivial isometry of the value path, reversal \(x\mapsto6-x\); no assertion is made here that this subgroup is the full automorphism group of the compatibility graph.

The exact size \(P(5,3)=10\) is prior work. The finding here is the complete extremizer census and its symmetry decomposition.

## Proof
Build a graph \(G\) on all \(5!=120\) permutations, joining two vertices exactly when their Chebyshev distance is at least \(3\). Then \((5,3)\) permutation arrays are exactly cliques of \(G\). Direct construction gives \(5340\) edges.

The bundled verifier runs a complete Bron--Kerbosch enumeration with pivoting, using exact integer bitsets and no randomized or heuristic step. It visits every maximal clique. The largest size found is \(10\), and exactly \(192\) maximal cliques have that size. This independently recovers the known optimum and proves the labeled extremizer count.

For each maximum clique, the verifier applies every one of the \(120\) coordinate permutations and both choices of value reversal. Every image is checked to be one of the 192 maxima. Orbit closure gives three disjoint orbits of sizes \(24,48,120\), exhausting all 192 maxima. Orbit--stabilizer in the displayed 240-element action gives stabilizer orders \(10,5,2\). Finally, all \(\binom{10}{2}=45\) pairwise distances in an orbit representative are recomputed. Since distances among compatible permutations in \(S_5\) can only be \(3\) or \(4\), the three complete spectra are \((30,15),(25,20),(28,17)\); these also distinguish the three orbit types.

## Relationship to prior work
Kløve, Lin, Tsai and Tzeng introduced this Chebyshev permutation-array problem and the notation \(P(n,d)\) in the archive-era source `arXiv:0907.2682v1` (first public 2009-07-15); their small-parameter table gave a lower value for the \((5,3)\) cell and did not classify its extremizers. Bereg, Haghpanah, Malouf and Sudborough later proved the general exact statement \(P(n,n-2)=10\) for \(n\ge5\), so the numerical optimum \(P(5,3)=10\) is not claimed as new. The 2025 upper-bound paper restates that theorem and develops separable-array bounds; the inspected text does not state a census of the 192 maximum \((5,3)\) arrays or the three-orbit decomposition above.

Targeted searches were also made for the exact parameter, aliases using the infinity metric and limited-displacement/permutation-code terminology, and for extremizer/equivalence/orbit classifications. No source located in those searches implied the 192-count or the three displayed orbit types. This negative search evidence is not a proof of novelty; an unindexed finite census remains a residual risk.

## Limitations
This is a finite classification at the natural base case \(n=5\) of the exact family \(P(n,n-2)=10\). It does not classify maximum arrays for larger \(n\), and the symmetry statement is deliberately restricted to the explicit 240-element subgroup above. The exhaustive computation proves the finite census only under the stated metric and labeling conventions. Independent audit has not been performed.
