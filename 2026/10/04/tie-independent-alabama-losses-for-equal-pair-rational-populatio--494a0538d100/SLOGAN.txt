For primitive populations \((a,a,c)\), every tie-breaking-independent Alabama loss is controlled by one modular residue interval: with \(P=2a+c\), the small state loses exactly when \((P+3c)/6<(ha\bmod P)<P/3\). Thus the exact loss count per period is \(\max\{0,\lceil P/3\rceil-\lfloor(P+3c)/6\rfloor-1\}\).
