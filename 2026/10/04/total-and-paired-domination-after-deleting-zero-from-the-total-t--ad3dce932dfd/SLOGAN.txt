For the zero-deleted total torsion element graph of a finite module with torsion submodule, total domination and paired domination exist in exactly the same cases and have the same exact value. The only obstructions are the isolated-vertex regimes \(|T(M)|=2\) and \(|T(M)|=1\) with \(2\in Z(R)\); otherwise the answer is \(\beta-1\), \(2\beta\), or \(\beta+1\) according to the coset structure.
