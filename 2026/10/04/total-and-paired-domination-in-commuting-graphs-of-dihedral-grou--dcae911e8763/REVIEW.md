# Review

## Correctness

PASS. The group calculation is explicit. Distinct reflections \(sr^i,sr^j\) commute exactly when \(2(j-i)\equiv0\pmod n\), and a reflection commutes with a rotation only when that rotation is central. Hence odd \(n\) gives isolated reflections, while even \(n\) gives the exact component decomposition \(K_{n-2}\sqcup (n/2)K_2\). Total domination is then forced componentwise, and the same selected vertices admit a perfect matching.

Risk: the result depends on the standard noncentral-vertex commuting-graph convention; including central elements changes the conclusion.

## Originality

PASS. The closest primary literature studies clique/independence parameters, distances, detour distances, metric dimension, and resolving polynomials of the same dihedral commuting graph. Searches combining the exact object with total domination, paired domination, and domination number did not locate the stated parity dichotomy or the exact value \(n+2\).

Risk: because the component decomposition is elementary once the commutation relations are written down, an unindexed note could contain the same strengthened domination calculation.

## Value

PASS. The result gives a complete classification of existence and exact values for two standard strengthened domination invariants over the entire dihedral family. It also isolates parity as the precise algebraic obstruction: odd rotation order produces isolated reflections, whereas even rotation order converts them into forced \(K_2\) components.

Risk: the even-case formula follows quickly from the component decomposition, so the value is chiefly in the complete structural classification rather than a technically deep bound.
