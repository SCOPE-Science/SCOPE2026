For composite \(n\), the subgroup intersection graph of \(D_{2n}\) has total domination number \(p\) when \(p^2\mid n\) and \(p+1\) otherwise, where \(p\) is the smallest prime divisor of \(n\). Its paired-domination number is exactly the least even integer at least that value; for prime \(n\), the graph is edgeless and neither parameter exists.
