For a finite commutative ring and a prime ideal \(P\) of size \(s\ge2\), the generalized total graph has equal total and paired domination numbers: \(2|R/P|\) in quotient characteristic two and \(|R/P|+1\) in odd quotient characteristic. All minimum total dominating sets are paired, with exact counts \(\binom{s}{2}^{|R/P|}\) and \(\binom{s}{2}s^{|R/P|-1}\), while \(s=1\) is precisely the isolated-vertex obstruction.
