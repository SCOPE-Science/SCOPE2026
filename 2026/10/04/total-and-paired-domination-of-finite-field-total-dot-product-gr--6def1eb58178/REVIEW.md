# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

At most q perpendicular hyperplanes cannot cover the ambient vector space, giving the lower bound q+1. In odd characteristic an anisotropic two-plane yields q+1 projective directions paired by orthogonal complement. In even characteristic a radical plane gives total domination and a nondegenerate plane plus one vector in its orthogonal complement gives the q+2 paired witness. Independent enumeration reproduced 3/4, 3/4 and 4/4 for (q,n)=(2,3),(2,4),(3,3).

## originality

PASS

Mollahajiaghaei's full arXiv text defines and computes ordinary domination on the same finite-field total dot product graph, but does not formulate total or paired domination. Badawi and Saleh–Megahed likewise treat graph structure or ordinary domination. Resultary and equivalent hyperplane-cover searches found no covering theorem.

PASS

The theorem resolves two standard stronger parameters on a natural algebraic graph family and reveals an even/odd characteristic distinction absent from ordinary domination, including the strict 2<3<4 binary example.
