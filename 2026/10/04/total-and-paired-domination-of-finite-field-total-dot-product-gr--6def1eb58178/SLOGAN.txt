For every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\) and every \(n\ge3\), the total dot product graph satisfies \(\gamma_t=q+1\), while paired domination is \(q+1\) for odd \(q\) and \(q+2\) for even \(q\). The proof converts total domination into a hyperplane cover and realizes the sharp witnesses inside carefully chosen two-dimensional subspaces.
