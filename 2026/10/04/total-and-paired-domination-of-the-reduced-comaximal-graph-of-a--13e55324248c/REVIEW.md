# Review

## Correctness

PASS. The proof reduces comaximality to maximal-ideal membership, uses the Chinese remainder theorem to realize the two forcing support patterns, and proves necessity and sufficiency of meeting each exclusive cell \(T_i\). The paired-domination formula follows from the total lower bound plus parity and an explicit perfect-matching construction. The polynomial is then the direct generating function of the complete classification. The verifier independently confirms the support model and an actual \(\mathbb Z_{30}\) graph.

Risk: none affecting the theorem; the finite polynomial statement is explicitly restricted to finite vertex sets.

## Originality

PASS. The 2015 primary source constructs one clique of size \(n\) and notes that it is a total dominating set, but gives no lower bound for total domination, no classification of all total dominating sets, no total domination polynomial, and no paired-domination value. The 2024 Boolean blow-up paper gives a compatible structural model but studies different invariants. The 2026 independent-domination paper was inspected in full and does not contain total or paired domination of \(CG_J(R)\). published-finding corpus searches for the exact parameter, aliases, and semilocal formulation returned unrelated domination findings.

Risk: because the lower bound is short once the maximal-ideal support model is recognized, an unindexed note could contain the same deduction.

## Value

PASS. The result closes an explicit gap left visible in the 2015 domination paper: the cited construction was already known to be total dominating, but its minimum size was not determined. The theorem upgrades that observation to a complete classification, an exact polynomial for finite rings, and a paired-domination formula, uniformly for all semilocal rings rather than only Artinian rings.

Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
