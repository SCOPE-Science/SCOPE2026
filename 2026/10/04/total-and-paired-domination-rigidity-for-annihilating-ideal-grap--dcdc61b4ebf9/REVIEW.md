# Review

## Correctness

PASS. Ideals in a direct product of fields are indexed by support subsets, and two nonzero proper ideals multiply to zero exactly when their supports are disjoint. Each co-singleton support has exactly one graph neighbor, the complementary singleton, forcing every coordinate minimal ideal into every total dominating set. Those singleton vertices form a clique and dominate all vertices, giving the unique minimum total set. Paired domination then follows from even-cardinality necessity and explicit matchings.

Risk: nontrivial blow-ups are outside the stated hypothesis.

## Originality

PASS. The closest structural literature identifies these annihilating-ideal graphs with strong Boolean graphs and records standard invariants such as degree, clique number, diameter, automorphism group, independence, coloring, and Boolean-graph characterizations. Targeted database and web searches did not locate total domination, paired domination, uniqueness of the minimum total set, or the exact count of minimum paired sets for this family.

Risk: the forcing argument is short once the strong Boolean model is written down, so an unindexed note could contain the same observation.

## Value

PASS. The result gives a complete strengthened-domination classification rather than one isolated value: it identifies the forced algebraic core, proves uniqueness of the minimum total set, determines the paired parity correction, and enumerates every minimum paired set in odd rank. The total domination number directly recovers the number of field factors from the graph.

Risk: the result does not distinguish field cardinalities.
