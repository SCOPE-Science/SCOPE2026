For a direct product of \(r\ge2\) fields, the annihilating-ideal graph has a unique minimum total dominating set: the \(r\) coordinate minimal ideals. Its paired-domination number is \(r\) for even \(r\) and \(r+1\) for odd \(r\); in odd rank there are exactly \(2^r-r-2\) minimum paired dominating sets.
