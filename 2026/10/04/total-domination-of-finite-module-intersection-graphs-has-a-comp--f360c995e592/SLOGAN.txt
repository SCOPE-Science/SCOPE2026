For a finite module \(M\) of composition length at least three, the total domination number of its nonzero-proper-submodule intersection graph is \(2\) except in the homogeneous semisimple case. If \(M\cong (R/\mathfrak m)^\ell\), the exact value is \(|R/\mathfrak m|+1\); length two is precisely the nonempty isolated-vertex obstruction.
