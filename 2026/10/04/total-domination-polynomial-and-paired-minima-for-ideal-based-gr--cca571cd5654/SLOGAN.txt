When \(R/I\) is a finite chain ring, every total dominating set of the ideal-based zero-divisor graph is exactly a subset of size at least two that meets the lifted nonzero socle. This gives \(D_t(\Gamma_I(R),x)=(1+x)^N-(1+x)^{N-m}-mx\) and shows that the minimum paired dominating sets are precisely the two-vertex total dominating sets.
