For \(n\ge2\), the trace graph of \(M_n(\mathbf F_q\times\mathbf F_r)\) has degree fingerprint \(\{(qr)^{n^2-1}s-2,(qr)^{n^2-1}s-1:s\in\{1,q,r\}\}\), and this fingerprint recovers the two field orders individually. Hence two such trace graphs are isomorphic exactly when the matrix sizes agree and the unordered pairs of field orders agree.
