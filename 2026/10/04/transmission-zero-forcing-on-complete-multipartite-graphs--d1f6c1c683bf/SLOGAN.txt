For every noncomplete complete multipartite graph \(K_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}\) with \(r\ge3\), the transmission zero forcing number has exactly the three possible values \(N-2\), \(N-1\), and \(N\), with explicit thresholds determined by the part sizes. The new threshold records the second-step contribution from vertices outside the two critical parts, a mechanism absent from the bipartite case.
