For every tree \(T\) and odd \(q\), the degree-\(q^i\) characters of its graphical group are counted by \(q^{|V(T)|-2i}\) times the coefficient of \(z^i\) in \(\sum_{S\subseteq E(T)}(q-1)^{|S|}z^{\nu(S)}\). A two-state rooted recursion computes this polynomial for every tree because every weighted forest skew matrix has rank exactly twice its support matching number.
