For a nondefective smooth toric surface, the excess of dual degree over primal degree is exactly \(4g+v-4\), where \(g\) is sectional genus and \(v\) is the number of polygon vertices. At fixed \(g\), the sharp minimum is \(4g-1\) exactly for triangular genera \(g=\binom{d-1}{2}\), uniquely realized by the \(d\)-uple Veronese surface; every other genus has minimum \(4g\).
