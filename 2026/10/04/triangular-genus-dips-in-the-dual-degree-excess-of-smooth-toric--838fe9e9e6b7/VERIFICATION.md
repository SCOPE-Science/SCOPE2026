The proof is uniform. For a smooth lattice polygon with normalized area \(A\), boundary lattice count \(B\), and vertex count \(V\), the source-backed discriminant Chern number is \(3A-2B+V\). Pick's theorem and toric adjunction identify the sectional genus with the interior lattice count, giving the exact identity \(\deg X_P^\vee-\deg X_P=4g+V-4\) in the nondefective case.

The bundled exact checker is only regression evidence. It verifies the Veronese triangle formulas through \(d=200\), checks all right triangles with side parameters at most \(200\) against the primitive-edge smoothness test, and verifies the rectangle realization and predicted envelope through genus \(1000\). The infinite theorem is supplied by the determinant proof that every smooth lattice triangle is integrally equivalent to \(d\Delta\) and by the explicit four-vertex construction.

The main scientific limit is the nondefective smooth complete toric embedding. Singular polygons, incomplete linear systems, and higher-dimensional toric varieties are outside the claim.
