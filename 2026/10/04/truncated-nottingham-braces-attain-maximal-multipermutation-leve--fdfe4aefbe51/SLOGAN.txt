For the natural degree-\(N\) Nottingham brace over any field of characteristic different from \(2\), the socle series removes exactly one highest-degree coefficient at each step, so the multipermutation level is \(N-1\). Over \(\mathbb F_p\) for odd \(p\), this reaches the largest level possible for any brace of order \(p^{N-1}\) with finite multipermutation level.
