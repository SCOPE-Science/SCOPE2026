# Same-model review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The orbit-counting step is exact: an injective ordered tuple labels its induced finite \(3\)-hypergraph by tuple position. Universality realizes every finite labeled tetrahedron-free \(3\)-graph, and ultrahomogeneity extends every isomorphism between induced finite substructures, so labeled induced structures and tuple orbits coincide. Nagle–Rödl then gives the entropy limit after division by \(\binom n3\).

The normalization is correct because \(\operatorname{ex}(n,K_4^{(3)})/\binom n3\to\pi(K_4^{(3)})\), while the additive \(o(n^3)\) term becomes \(o(1)\) after the same normalization.

## Value

**PASS.** The result gives an exact bridge between a major open extremal-combinatorics constant and a canonical oligomorphic automorphism group. It lets every future improvement to the tetrahedron Turán density be read immediately as a sharp orbit-entropy bound, and conversely makes the conjectured value \(5/9\) a model-theoretic asymptotic invariant.
