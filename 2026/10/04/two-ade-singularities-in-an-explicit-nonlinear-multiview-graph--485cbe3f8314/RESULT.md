# Two ADE singularities in an explicit nonlinear multiview graph

## Finding

Work over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero. Let
\[
G:\mathbb P^2\dashrightarrow \mathbb P^2\times\mathbb P^2,
\qquad [x:y:z]\longmapsto ([x:y:z],[x^3:yz^2:xy^2]),
\]
and let \(Y=\overline{\operatorname{im}G}\). If \([X:Y:Z]\) and \([A:B:C]\) are coordinates on the two factors, then \(Y\) is the normal toric surface with prime bihomogeneous ideal
\[
I(Y)=\bigl(BX^3-AYZ^2,\;CX^2-AY^2,\;BXY-CZ^2\bigr).
\]
Its singular locus consists of exactly two points,
\[
P=([0:0:1],[0:1:0]),\qquad Q=([0:1:0],[0:0:1]).
\]
The completed local equation at \(P\) is \(uv-w^3=0\), so \(P\) is an \(A_2\) rational double point, while the completed local equation at \(Q\) is \(uv-w^2=0\), so \(Q\) is an \(A_1\) rational double point.

## Assumptions and scope

The source example is Example 6.6 of Cid-Ruiz--Clarke--Mohammadi, where the same monomial map is introduced and its image multidegrees are computed as \(\deg_{(2,0)}(Y)=1\), \(\deg_{(1,1)}(Y)=3\), and \(\deg_{(0,2)}(Y)=4\). The paper's primary MSC is 14E05 and its first public version is arXiv:2112.06216 from 2021-12-12.

The claim here concerns the intrinsic graph closure of that one explicit map. It does not classify singularities of arbitrary nonlinear multiview varieties or arbitrary monomial rational maps. Characteristic zero is assumed so that the standard \(A_n\) surface-singularity terminology has its usual form.

## Proof

Introduce an auxiliary variable \(t\). The graph closure is the multiprojective toric image of the monomial homomorphism
\[
X\mapsto x,\quad Y\mapsto y,\quad Z\mapsto z,\quad
A\mapsto x^3t,\quad B\mapsto yz^2t,\quad C\mapsto xy^2t.
\]
Hence its defining ideal is the elimination kernel of
\[
(X-x,\;Y-y,\;Z-z,\;A-x^3t,\;B-yz^2t,\;C-xy^2t).
\]
Exact lexicographic elimination gives a Gröbner basis whose first three generators are
\[
q_1=BX^3-AYZ^2,\qquad q_2=CX^2-AY^2,\qquad q_3=BXY-CZ^2,
\]
and whose two further completion binomials reduce to zero modulo \((q_1,q_2,q_3)\). Conversely \(q_1,q_2,q_3\) lie in the elimination kernel, so \(I(Y)=(q_1,q_2,q_3)\). Since this is a monomial-image kernel, it is prime, and \(Y\) is toric.

The second component \([x^3:yz^2:xy^2]\) has exactly two base points,
\[
p=[0:0:1],\qquad q=[0:1:0].
\]
Therefore the first projection \(\pi_1:Y\to\mathbb P^2\) is an isomorphism away from \(p\) and \(q\), so any singular point of \(Y\) must lie above one of these two points.

At \(p\), substituting \(X=Y=0\) and \(Z=1\) into the defining ideal leaves only \(C=0\). Thus
\[
\pi_1^{-1}(p)=\{([0:0:1],[A:B:0])\}\cong\mathbb P^1.
\]
On the chart \(Z=B=1\), the graph ideal is exactly
\[
(C-XY,\;X^3-AY).
\]
After eliminating \(C\), this is the hypersurface \(X^3-AY=0\). Its gradient vanishes only at \(X=A=Y=0\), corresponding to \(P\), and the equation is the standard \(A_2\) form after renaming \((A,Y,X)\) as \((u,v,w)\). The complementary chart \(Z=A=1\) is explicitly parametrized by
\[
Y=BX^3,\qquad C=B^2X^4,
\]
so it is smooth. Hence \(P\) is the only singular point over \(p\).

At \(q\), substituting \(X=Z=0\) and \(Y=1\) leaves only \(A=0\). Thus
\[
\pi_1^{-1}(q)=\{([0:1:0],[0:B:C])\}\cong\mathbb P^1.
\]
On the chart \(Y=C=1\), the graph ideal is exactly
\[
(A-X^2,\;XB-Z^2).
\]
After eliminating \(A\), this is the hypersurface \(XB-Z^2=0\), whose gradient vanishes only at \(X=B=Z=0\), corresponding to \(Q\). This is the standard \(A_1\) equation. The complementary chart \(Y=B=1\) is explicitly parametrized by
\[
X=CZ^2,\qquad A=C^3Z^4,
\]
so it is smooth. Hence \(Q\) is the only singular point over \(q\).

It follows that \(\operatorname{Sing}(Y)=\{P,Q\}\), with types \(A_2\) and \(A_1\). Both local models are normal hypersurface singularities, and the surface is smooth elsewhere; therefore \(Y\) is normal.

## Verification

The accompanying `verify_graph_singularities.py` reconstructs the graph elimination ideal over \(\mathbb Q\) using exact SymPy Gröbner-basis arithmetic. It verifies that the elimination kernel is generated by \(q_1,q_2,q_3\), checks the two exceptional fibers, proves the four displayed affine-chart descriptions by equality of Gröbner ideals, and verifies that the critical ideals of \(X^3-AY\) and \(XB-Z^2\) are supported only at the origins. Running the packaged script prints `VERIFY_OK`.

## Relationship to prior work

Cid-Ruiz--Clarke--Mohammadi introduce this exact map as Example 6.6 and use mixed volumes to compute the three multidegrees of its image. Their stated purpose in that example is to illustrate the mixed-volume formula; the inspected example and surrounding section do not give the graph's defining ideal, exceptional fibers, singular locus, normality, or ADE types.

Targeted searches for the exact monomial triple \((x^3,yz^2,xy^2)\), its Rees/graph ideal, and \(A_1/A_2\) singularities returned the source paper itself but no publication asserting the present singularity classification. published-finding corpus searches for the same object and equivalent graph/Rees formulations returned unrelated records rather than a covering result. This search evidence is not a proof of novelty; the originality assessment rests on the statement-level gap between the inspected primary example and the exact local classification proved here.

## Limitations

No claim is made about the minimal resolution, divisor class group, Cox ring, or deformation theory of \(Y\). The argument does not classify which other monomial nonlinear multiview examples have rational double points. Search coverage is necessarily incomplete, so an equivalent computation could exist under different Rees-algebra or toric terminology.
