## Correctness
PASS. The proof reconstructs the complete-intersection dual-degree formula from the twisted conormal bundle and Kleiman's Segre-class formula. The parity calculation is exact: modulo \(2\), the complete homogeneous symmetric polynomial counts weak compositions into the variables whose shifted defining degrees are odd. Kummer--Lucas converts that count to the stated no-carry criterion. In the all-odd multidegree case, homogeneity contributes the exact factor \(2^n\) before the same argument is repeated modulo \(2\).

The bounded checker independently replays the valuation theorem and mod-\(4\) consequences but is not used as an infinite proof.
