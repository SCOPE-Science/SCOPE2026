# Review of Two-coordinate operator-norm formula for a generalized James constant

## Correctness
PASS. The upper bound is exact coordinatewise: the \(2\times2\) matrix norm controls each coordinate pair and summing the \(p\)-th powers controls the smaller of the two global output norms. The lower bound uses a genuine scalar maximizer and a two-coordinate rotation that equalizes the two outputs. The \(p=\infty\) endpoint is treated separately, and the duality and endpoint formulas follow from standard finite-dimensional operator-norm identities.

## Originality
PASS. The defining 2024 paper was inspected in full around the definition and its main theorems. It gives inequalities and a uniform-nonsquareness characterization but no \(\ell_p\) matrix-norm reduction. Searches for equivalent formulations, broader results, exact tables, aliases, and implication coverage in published literature did not locate this statement. The nearest previously catalogued exact \(\ell_p\) result concerns a different one-parameter generalized James constant and does not imply this claim.

## Value
PASS. The reduction is a reusable structural formula for the whole real \(\ell_p\) scale, not an isolated numerical evaluation. It transfers a nonlinear geometric constant to classical two-dimensional operator-norm theory and yields dimension independence, Hölder-duality symmetry, and the exact \(p=1,2,\infty\) values at once.

## Closest literature and limitations
Yang and Yang, DOI 10.7153/mia-2024-27-39, is the closest source because it defines \(J_{\lambda,\mu}(X)\) and proves its principal qualitative relation to uniform non-squareness. Liu, Sarfraz and Li, DOI 10.1515/dema-2021-0033, study a different one-parameter generalized James coefficient. The present result does not claim a closed elementary formula for the intermediate-\(p\) matrix norm, does not treat complex scalars, and requires at least two coordinates.
