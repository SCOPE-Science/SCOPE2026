For real \(\ell_p\) with at least two coordinates, Yang–Yang's two-parameter generalized James constant is exactly the \(\ell_p^2\)-operator norm of the symmetric matrix \(\begin{pmatrix}\lambda&\mu\\ \mu&-\lambda\end{pmatrix}\). This immediately yields dimension-independence, Hölder-duality symmetry, and the exact \(p=1,2,\infty\) values.
