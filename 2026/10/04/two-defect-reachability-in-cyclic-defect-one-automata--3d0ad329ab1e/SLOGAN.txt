For a cycle letter and an arbitrary rank-\((n-1)\) letter, every codimension-two target is reached by exactly two defect-one steps within \(2n\) letters, except only for antipodal holes when \(n\) is even and the excluded-to-duplicated displacement is \(n/2\). This removes the usual coprimality requirement at codimension two and gives an exact parity obstruction to the two-step construction.
