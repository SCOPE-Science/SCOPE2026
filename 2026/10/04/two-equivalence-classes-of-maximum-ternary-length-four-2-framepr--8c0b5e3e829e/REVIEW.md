# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reduces the property exactly to avoidance of 18,792 forbidden triples, exhausts all four symmetry-normalized possibilities for a hypothetical 13-word code, enumerates every 12-word maximum containing 0000, and then traverses the complete equivalence group. The packaged replay reproduces every count and the two representatives. The scope is finite; no experiment is extrapolated to larger parameters.

## Originality
PASS. Chee–Zhang gives constructions, Cheng–Jiang–Wang gives large-alphabet upper bounds, and Rochanakul gives an explicit 12-word ternary example. The prior exact ternary separable result already implies the cardinality ceiling 12, so the accepted claim is not novelty of that number. The assessed new statement is the exact census of 5,616 maximum 2-frameproof codes and their two equivalence classes. Exact-parameter, alias, implication and exact-number searches did not find this statement. The principal residual risk is an unindexed finite computation; the 2019 full text was not openly inspectable beyond preview material.

## Value
PASS. The smallest nonbinary odd-alphabet length-four instance is a natural finite boundary of a classical frameproof-code program, and the later literature itself singles out a ternary 12-word example. Classifying every extremizer and finding two symmetry types with stabilizers 6 and 72 gives structural information not contained in a mere cardinality bound.
