For every finite nonabelian two-generated class-two group, the full commuting graph is the determinant-zero graph on \((\mathbb Z/|G'|\mathbb Z)^2\) with every vertex blown up to a clique of size \(|Z(G)|\). Thus the graph determines exactly \((|Z(G)|,|G'|)\) and, in particular, determines the group up to isoclinism within this family.
