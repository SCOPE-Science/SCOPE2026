A published two-branch formula for the Carmichael function fails first at \(n=12\) in the \(8\nmid n\) branch and at \(n=24\) in the \(8\mid n\) branch. The classical prime-power least-common-multiple formula repairs the statement, while the paper's displayed proof of Theorem 3 survives because it only needs \(p-1\mid\lambda(m)\) for primes \(p\mid m\).
