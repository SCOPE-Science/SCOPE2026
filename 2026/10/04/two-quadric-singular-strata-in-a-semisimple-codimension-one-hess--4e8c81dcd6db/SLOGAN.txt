For \(S=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-1,-1)\) and \(h=(3,4,4,4)\), the singular locus of the semisimple Hessenberg fivefold is exactly two disjoint copies of \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\). Every point on either surface has transverse three-dimensional ordinary-double-point type, so the fivefold is normal.
