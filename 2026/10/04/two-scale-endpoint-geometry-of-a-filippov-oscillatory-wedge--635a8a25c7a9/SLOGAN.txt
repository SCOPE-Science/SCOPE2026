The oscillatory wedge in the symmetric zero-divergence Filippov model has two sharply different endpoint scales. Near \(\mu=0\) its width is \(4\pi\mu-4\sqrt{\pi}\,\mu^{3/2}+O(\mu^2)\), while for large \(\mu\) the upper boundary is of order \(e^{-\pi\mu}\) but the grazing boundary is only of order \(e^{-2\pi\mu-2}\).
