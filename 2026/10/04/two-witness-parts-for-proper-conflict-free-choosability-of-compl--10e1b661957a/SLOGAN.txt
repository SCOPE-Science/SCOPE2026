Every connected complete multipartite graph \(G=K_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}\) with \(r\ge2\) is proper conflict-free \((\mathrm{degree}+2)\)-choosable. If \(r\ge3\), then \(G\) is proper conflict-free \((\mathrm{degree}+1)\)-choosable. The proof reserves one singleton witness color in each of two parts and then colors all remaining parts with palettes disjoint from earlier parts.
