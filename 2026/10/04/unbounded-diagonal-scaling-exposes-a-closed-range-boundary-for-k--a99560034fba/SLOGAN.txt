A closed-range rank-one \(K\) already permits a \(K\)-frame whose unbounded coordinate scaling becomes an orthonormal basis, so boundedness of the diagonal multiplier on \(R(T_F)\) is not forced by preservation of the \(K\)-frame property. The correct invariant is boundedness of the composition \(D_aT_F\); boundedness on the analysis range follows once \(R(T_F)\) itself is closed.
