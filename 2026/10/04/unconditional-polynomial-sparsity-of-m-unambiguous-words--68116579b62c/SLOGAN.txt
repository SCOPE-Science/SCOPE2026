For every fixed ordered alphabet of size \(s\ge2\), the number of \(M\)-unambiguous words of length at most \(n\) is \(O_s(n^{\binom{s+2}{3}})\), because singleton Parikh-matrix fibers inject into a polynomial-size matrix range. Thus their density is exponentially small up to a polynomial factor, giving an unconditional affirmative answer to Teh's 2020 density-zero problem.
