# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reconstructs the congruence-subgroup modification of the unit lattice and then re-runs the shaped-box argument on that sublattice. The covolume change is quantified by \(h_m\le I_m\), the side-length product is matched exactly, the nonlinear biquadratic errors retain their coordinatewise factorization, and the denominator coordinate remains divisible by \(m\). The threshold \(\log T\ge cI_m\) is sufficient to control the longest box side and to exclude \(q=0\).

## Originality
PASS. The closest source proves prescribed divisibility and arbitrary unequal biquadratic Peck savings separately, but not jointly. Targeted statement, alias, parameter, and implication searches did not locate the conjunction or a stronger theorem covering it. Bugeaud--de Mathan's contemporaneous refinement has broader field scope but a comparability condition and no stated denominator divisibility; de Mathan's earlier divisibility paper concerns a different two-dimensional setup.

## Value
PASS. The theorem gives a natural quantitative compatibility law: imposing a denominator multiple consumes the logarithmic saving budget through the unit-congruence index \(I_m\). The explicit consequence using \(I_m\le m^4\) makes the result directly applicable without computing the unit image modulo \(m\).

## Closest literature and limitations
The primary comparison is Dhanda--Flynn--Haynes, arXiv:2609.29469v1, especially Theorem 2, Section 4, Lemma 10, Theorem 3, and equations (28)-(29). The theorem is limited to the natural basis of real biquadratic fields and does not prove the index loss optimal or improve the best possible non-Archimedean exponent.
