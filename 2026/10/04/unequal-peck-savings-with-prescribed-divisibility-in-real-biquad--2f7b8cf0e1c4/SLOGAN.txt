For the natural basis of every real biquadratic field, prescribed divisibility of the denominator and completely unequal Peck logarithmic savings can be imposed simultaneously. The available product saving is \(\log T/I_m\), where \(I_m\) is the unit-congruence index; in particular \(I_m\le m^4\) gives an explicit \(\log T/m^4\) budget.
