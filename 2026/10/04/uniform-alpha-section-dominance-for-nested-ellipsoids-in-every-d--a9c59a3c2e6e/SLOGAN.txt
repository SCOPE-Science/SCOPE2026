For every pair of nested ellipsoids in \(\mathbb R^d\), one parallel family of hyperplanes works simultaneously for all \(\alpha\in(0,1/2]\): an \(\alpha\)-cap of the inner ellipsoid is at most as small, fractionally, as the corresponding cap of the outer ellipsoid. After normalizing the inner ellipsoid to the unit ball, every normal \(u\) pointing weakly toward the outer center, \(c\cdot u\ge0\), has this property.
