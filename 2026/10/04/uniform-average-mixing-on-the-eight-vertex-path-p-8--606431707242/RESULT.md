# Uniform average mixing on the eight-vertex path \(P_8\)
## Finding
For the uniformly coupled eight-spin \(XY\) chain, equivalently the continuous-time quantum walk generated by the adjacency matrix of the path \(P_8\), exact uniform average mixing is possible. There is a probability distribution \(\mu\) of readout times such that
\[
\int M(t)\,d\mu(t)=\frac18J,
\]
where \(M(t)_{uv}=|e^{itA}_{uv}|^2\). A finitely supported choice exists with at most \(17\) readout times.

This resolves the smallest modulus not decided by the current arithmetic theorem. Writing \(q=N+1\), the cases \(q=3,4,5,6,7,8\) belong to its positive families, whereas \(q=9\) belongs to neither its positive nor its negative families.

## Assumptions and scope
The Hamiltonian is the uniformly coupled nearest-neighbor \(XY\) Hamiltonian restricted to the single-excitation sector, so its matrix is the path adjacency matrix \(A=A(P_8)\). Put \(q=9\). Its eigenvalues are
\[
\theta_r=2\cos\!\left(\frac{r\pi}{9}\right),\qquad 1\le r\le8.
\]
The source theorem shows that uniform average mixing is equivalent to realizing the forced cosine coherences
\[
y_{rs}=-\frac18\quad\text{{if }}s-r\text{{ is even}},\qquad y_{rs}=0\quad\text{{if }}s-r\text{{ is odd}}.
\]
Only this published equivalence and its standard convex-hull passage from phase distributions to time distributions are used as non-elementary input.

## Proof
For the positive modes \(1\le r\le4\), exact trigonometric identities give
\[
\theta_3=1,\qquad \theta_4=\theta_2^2-2,\qquad \theta_1=\theta_2+\theta_4.
\]
Moreover \(\theta_2=2\cos(2\pi/9)\) satisfies
\[
\theta_2^3-3\theta_2+1=0.
\]
The polynomial \(x^3-3x+1\) has no rational root and is irreducible over \(\mathbb Q\). Hence \(1,\theta_2,\theta_2^2\) are linearly independent over \(\mathbb Q\), so \(\theta_3,\theta_2,\theta_4\) are a basis of the span of the four positive eigenvalues. Therefore the complete integer relation lattice is generated by
\[
(1,-1,0,-1),
\]
and Kronecker's theorem identifies the positive-mode phase-flow closure with
\[
\mathcal T_9=\left\{(\phi_1,\phi_2,\phi_3,\phi_4)\in(\mathbb R/2\pi\mathbb Z)^4:\ \phi_1-\phi_2-\phi_4\equiv0\pmod{{2\pi}}\right\}.
\]
Extend phases to modes \(5,6,7,8\) by \(\phi_{9-r}=-\phi_r\).

Haar measure on \(\mathcal T_9\) has zero off-diagonal coherences. Indeed, every character coming from a difference \(\phi_r-\phi_s\) has, in the four positive coordinates, a coefficient vector of the form \(\pm e_i\pm e_j\) or \(\pm2e_i\); none is a nonzero multiple of \((1,-1,0,-1)\), the annihilator generator. Thus every such nontrivial character has Haar integral zero. Because Haar measure has full support, its barycenter \(0\) lies in the relative interior of the convex hull of phase-coherence vectors on \(\mathcal T_9\).

Now take the relation-compatible one-parameter curve
\[
(\phi_1,\phi_2,\phi_3,\phi_4)=(5\xi,4\xi,2\xi,\xi).
\]
It lies in \(\mathcal T_9\) because \(5-4-1=0\). After reflection to all eight modes, the integer phase coefficients are
\[
(c_1,\ldots,c_8)=(5,4,2,1,-1,-2,-4,-5).
\]
Within either parity class the absolute differences \(|c_r-c_s|\) are exactly among \(3,6,9\). Between opposite parity classes they are among
\[
1,2,4,5,7,8,10,
\]
so no opposite-parity difference is divisible by \(3\).

Fix any \(1<\lambda<4/3\) and put, with respect to normalized Haar measure on \(\mathbb R/2\pi\mathbb Z\),
\[
f_\lambda(\xi)=1-\frac{\lambda}{4}\bigl(\cos(3\xi)+\cos(6\xi)+\cos(9\xi)\bigr).
\]
Since the cosine sum is at most \(3\),
\[
f_\lambda(\xi)\ge1-\frac{3\lambda}{4}>0.
\]
Fourier orthogonality gives
\[
\int\cos(k\xi)f_\lambda(\xi)\,d\xi=-\frac{\lambda}{8}
\]
for \(k\in\{{3,6,9}}\), and zero for every other positive integer \(k\). Therefore this phase distribution realizes \(\lambda Y^\star\), where \(Y^\star\) is exactly the forced coherence vector.

Since \(0\) is in the relative interior and
\[
Y^\star=(1-\lambda^{-1})0+\lambda^{-1}(\lambda Y^\star),
\]
the target \(Y^\star\) lies in the relative interior of the phase-coherence hull on \(\mathcal T_9\). The time phase flow is dense in \(\mathcal T_9\), so the convex hull of the actual time-coherence curve has the same relative interior as the closed phase hull. Hence \(Y^\star\) is realized by an actual probability distribution of readout times. The source's moment-pair dimension bound gives a support of at most
\[
\lfloor9/2\rfloor^2+1=17
\]
times. Substitution of the forced coherences into the published spectral expansion yields \(J/8\).

## Verification
The accompanying checker verifies the exact integer phase-difference pattern, positivity margin for the concrete choice \(\lambda=6/5\), the cubic and trigonometric eigenvalue identities numerically to high precision, and reconstructs the averaged mixing matrix from the forced coherences to confirm \(J/8\). These finite computations corroborate the algebra; they are not substitutes for the proof of density or the all-time convex-hull argument.

Claim-to-evidence map: the domain is the single-excitation path Hamiltonian on exactly \(8\) sites. The critical proof steps are the complete rational-relation calculation, identification of the phase subtorus, vanishing of all off-diagonal Haar characters, the explicit positive Fourier density on the relation-compatible phase curve, and the relative-interior transfer to the dense time flow. No explicit numerical readout times are claimed; only existence and the \(17\)-point support bound are proved.

## Relationship to prior work
Kang, arXiv:2609.14463v1, proves uniform average mixing when \(q\) is a power of two, a prime, or twice a prime, and proves nonexistence for three obstruction families. The paper explicitly states that moduli outside those two lists remain unresolved and develops the forced-coherence and phase-convexity machinery used here. The case \(q=9\), corresponding to \(P_8\), is outside both lists.

Baptista, Coutinho and Marques, arXiv:2308.16378, introduced the random-time average-mixing framework used by the later work and exhibited small path examples. Their results do not settle \(P_8\).

The present argument differs from the independent-spectrum construction in arXiv:2609.14463v1: the \(q=9\) positive eigenvalues are rationally dependent, but their one relation admits a specially chosen phase curve whose same-parity frequency differences are precisely \(3,6,9\), allowing the required Fourier moments to be imposed without contaminating opposite-parity pairs.

## Limitations
This result settles only \(q=9\). It does not classify all rationally dependent moduli, give explicit numerical readout times, or assert that the \(17\)-point support bound is minimal. The argument relies on the published forced-coherence characterization and convex-hull framework. A literature search did not reveal an earlier \(P_8\) solution, but absence from searches is not a proof of historical novelty.
