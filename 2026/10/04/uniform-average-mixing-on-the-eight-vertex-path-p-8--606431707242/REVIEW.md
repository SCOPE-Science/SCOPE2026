# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof is exact. The cubic \(x^3-3x+1\) is irreducible, so the positive \(q=9\) eigenvalues have exactly one rational relation. Kronecker's theorem therefore gives the stated three-dimensional phase subtorus. Every off-diagonal mode-difference character is nontrivial on that subtorus, so Haar measure has zero coherences. The explicit curve \((5\xi,4\xi,2\xi,\xi)\) lies on the subtorus, and its same-parity phase differences are precisely frequencies \(3,6,9\), while opposite-parity differences avoid those frequencies. The positive density therefore realizes \(\lambda\) times the required coherence target. Relative-interior convexity and density of the time flow yield an actual readout-time law, and the published forced-coherence identity yields \(J/8\).

## Originality
PASS. The closest primary source, arXiv:2609.14463v1, explicitly says that moduli outside its positive and negative families remain unresolved. Its positive construction assumes rational independence and therefore does not cover \(q=9\). Earlier work arXiv:2308.16378 supplies the random-time framework and small path examples but not \(P_8\). Exact-claim, parameter, alias, phase-relation, and implication searches found no covering result. A residual risk remains that an equivalent construction appears in older literature under different terminology.

## Value
PASS. This is the first finite modulus not decided by the new arithmetic classification: all \(q=3\) through \(8\) are positive there, while \(q=9\) is outside both lists. Resolving it positively is a natural boundary case, and the proof isolates a useful mechanism for rationally dependent spectra rather than merely recomputing the source's independent-spectrum cases.

## Closest literature and limitations
The main comparison is arXiv:2609.14463v1. The new theorem uses that paper's forced-coherence characterization and convex-hull framework but replaces its independent-spectrum phase ramp by a relation-compatible curve on the smaller \(q=9\) Kronecker subtorus. The result does not classify other unresolved moduli, give explicit numerical readout times, or prove the \(17\)-time support bound optimal.
