For every concentric hole radius \(a\in(0,1)\), the rotationally invariant lossless mixture of outward-tangent planks is unique: its width tail is \(2(a+h)/\sqrt{h(h+2a)}\) and its total width is \(2\sqrt{1-a^2}\). At \(a=1/2\) this explicitly solves the Abel equation left implicit in the recent plank-stability paper and gives cost \(\sqrt3\).
