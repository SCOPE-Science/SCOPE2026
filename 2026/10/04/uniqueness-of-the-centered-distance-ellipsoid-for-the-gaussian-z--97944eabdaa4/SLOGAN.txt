For every \(n\ge3\), the Ryabogin--Zvavitch Gaussian zonoid has a unique origin-centered Banach--Mazur distance ellipsoid: the sharp inner ball \(b_\infty B_2^n\). Equality in the symmetry-averaged optimization forces equality on the horizontal, axial, and minimizing-slope contact orbits, eliminating every nonsymmetric quadratic perturbation.
