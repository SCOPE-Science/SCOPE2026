At unit rare-event intensity, three-wise independence rigidifies the limiting variance but not the third factorial moment. The weak limits are exactly the nonnegative-integer laws with \(\mathbb EY=1\), \(\mathbb E(Y)_2=1\), and \(\mathbb E(Y)_3\le1\), and every such law is realizable by exchangeable Bernoulli rows with marginal \(1/n\).
