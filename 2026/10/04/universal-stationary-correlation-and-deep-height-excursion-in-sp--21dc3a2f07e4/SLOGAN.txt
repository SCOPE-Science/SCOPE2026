Every non-equilibrium compact stationary state of Sprott N has the same Pearson correlation \(\operatorname{Corr}(y,z)=\sqrt{2/3}\). Its height law is necessarily negatively skewed by \(\mathbb E[(z-1/2)^3]=-\operatorname{Var}(z)\), forcing positive stationary mass below \(z=-1/2\) even though the unique equilibrium sits at \(z=1/2\).
