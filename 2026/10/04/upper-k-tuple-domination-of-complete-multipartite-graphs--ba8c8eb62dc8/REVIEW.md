# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The proof is correct. For a selected vertex in part i the closed-neighborhood count is total selected minus selected in its part plus one; for an unselected vertex it is total selected minus selected in its part. A largest part together with any k minus one outside vertices is feasible throughout the full range and every deletion has an explicit tight witness. Conversely, deletion minimality gives a tight closed-neighborhood witness. If that witness is selected, its part must be fully selected and yields size at most L+k-1; if it is unselected, its selected part count is at most L-1 and gives the same bound. Fresh independent exhaustive replay over all unordered multipartite profiles through total order eight and every feasible k agreed with the formula. The finite replay is corroborative only.

Originality passes. PASS

Upper k-tuple domination is an established fault-tolerant domination invariant with hard computation even on broad restricted classes. Complete multipartite graphs are a canonical structured family, and the theorem gives the exact value over the entire feasible k range together with a uniform extremal construction. This is a natural infinite-class benchmark rather than an arbitrary finite slice.
