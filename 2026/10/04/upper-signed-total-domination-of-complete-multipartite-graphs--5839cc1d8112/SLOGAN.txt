For a complete multipartite graph, minimal signed total dominating functions are controlled exactly by their tight open-neighborhood parts: every part containing a \(+1\)-vertex needs a different tight part. This gives the exact formula \(\Gamma_t^s(G)=\max_{i<j}\min\{n_i+\varepsilon_i,n_j+\varepsilon_j,N-n_i-n_j+\varepsilon_i+\varepsilon_j\}\), where \(\varepsilon_i\in\{1,2\}\) records the parity of \(N-n_i\).
