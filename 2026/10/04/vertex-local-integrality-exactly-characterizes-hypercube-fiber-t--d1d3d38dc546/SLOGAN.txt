For \(d\ge2\), antipodal perfect transfer inside the \(g\)-fiber of \(G[Q_d]\) occurs exactly when the adjacency eigenvalue support of \(g\) is integral; then every positive transfer time is an odd multiple of \(\pi/2\). A seven-vertex nonintegral tree has local support \(\{-2,0,2\}\) at its middle vertex, giving connected nonintegral lexicographic products with exact transfer.
