# Review

## Correctness
**PASS.** Wan proves that the complete analytic semiuniversal base is exactly the germ of \(V(ab,bc,ca,de,ef,fd)\). Each three-axis factor has an explicit Hilbert--Burch resolution. Tensoring the two minimal resolutions gives the stated length-four minimal resolution over six variables. The source gives dimension \(2\); Auslander--Buchsbaum then gives depth \(2\), so the ring is Cohen--Macaulay. The final free rank is \(4\), all in one shift, giving level type \(4\) and hence non-Gorenstein. Independent Hochster enumeration reproduces every graded Betti number. The Hilbert series, multiplicity, singular locus, and projective \(K_{3,3}\) curve follow exactly from the squarefree monomial ideal.

## Originality
**PASS.** The full initiating proof was inspected. It states the exact Kuranishi equations, reducedness, nine coordinate-plane components, and the relation to Aspinwall's three-axis critical locus, but not the accepted local-algebra profile. Aspinwall's cited primary source was also inspected and covers only the one-factor noncompact superpotential. Exact-ideal, alias, invariant, and published-finding searches did not locate a source stating the type-four Cohen--Macaulay/non-Gorenstein result, minimal Betti table, multiplicity-nine statement, six-axis singular stratification, or \(K_{3,3}\) projectivization for Wan's compact germ. The shortness of the algebraic derivation remains an explicit residual risk.

## Value
**PASS.** The computation identifies structural invariants of the paper's principal obstructed deformation germ, not an arbitrary numerical specialization. In particular, the type-four non-Gorenstein conclusion is a substantive local-algebra distinction for a reducible Kuranishi space arising from Calabi--Yau tangent-bundle deformation. The full resolution and tangent-cone graph provide reusable data for comparison with future deformation germs.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is Wan, arXiv:2609.27271v1, Theorem 1.5 and Section 5.3. The closest predecessor is Aspinwall, arXiv:1110.2524v3, Section 7, where a single three-axis critical locus arises from \(W=XYZ\). The present result profiles the semiuniversal fixed-base germ only; it does not compute the coarse quotient by bundle automorphisms or \(S\)-equivalence.
