Wan's six-parameter Igusa tangent-bundle Kuranishi cone is a two-dimensional Cohen--Macaulay level singularity of type \(4\), hence non-Gorenstein, with Hilbert--Samuel multiplicity \(9\). Its projectivized tangent cone is the nine-line Stanley--Reisner curve of \(K_{3,3}\), and its singular locus consists of six coordinate axes.
