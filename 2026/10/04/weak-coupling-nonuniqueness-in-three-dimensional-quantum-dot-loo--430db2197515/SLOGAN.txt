In three dimensions, sufficiently weak regular attractive wells do not bind uniformly over the entire finite-array geometry: \(\inf\sigma(H_{\lambda,Y})=0\) whenever \(\lambda C_S N^{2/3}\|v\|_{3/2}\le1\). Thus the unique-maximizer clause in Exner's fixed-length-loop conjecture needs a binding hypothesis; without it, every admissible geometry ties at zero in the weak-coupling regime.
