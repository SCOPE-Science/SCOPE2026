# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In a complete multipartite graph, a positive support dominates exactly when it meets at least two parts or fills one whole part. This makes the one-guard transfer rule depend only on the number of supported parts, the total guard weight in each supported part and the number of zeros left there. The three support cases in RESULT.md follow directly. I independently compared the literal transfer rule to the theorem criterion on every ordered multipartite composition through order seven, totaling 164682 labelings, with zero mismatches; an independent polynomial reconstruction also matched every brute-force weight histogram through order six. The packaged verifier extends the finite stress test through order eight.
