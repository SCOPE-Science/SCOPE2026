On a connected complete multipartite graph, quasi-total double Roman functions are governed entirely by how many parts support labels \(3\) and \(2\); the only nonautomatic cases occur when all defenders are concentrated in at most two parts. This yields a closed weight enumerator for every part profile, with minimum value \(3\) for \(K_2\), \(4\) when a part has size at most two and \(N\ge3\), and \(6\) when every part has size at least three.
