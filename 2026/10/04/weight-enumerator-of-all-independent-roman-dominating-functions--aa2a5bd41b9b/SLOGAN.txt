For a connected complete multipartite graph \(G=K_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}\), every independent Roman dominating function has all positive labels on one entire part, with at least one label \(2\). Hence \(I_R(G;x)=\sum_i((x+x^2)^{n_i}-x^{n_i})\), and this polynomial recovers the multiset of part sizes and therefore the graph within this class.
