For a complete multipartite graph, a covering Italian function either has no zeros or places every zero in one part; the only exceptional defense rule occurs when that part has a single vertex outside it. This gives \(\mathcal C_G(z)=P_N(z)+\sum_i B_{N-n_i}(z)T_{n_i}(z)\) and \(\gamma_{cI}(G)=\max\{2,N-\max_i n_i\}\).
