The Petrzela–Gotthans conic-equilibrium flow preserves the smooth volume density \(e^{ax}\): its finite-time Jacobian is exactly \(e^{-a(x(t)-x(0))}\). Consequently every periodic orbit has transverse Floquet product \(1\), and the exact ODE cannot support a compact asymptotically stable attractor with a bounded open trapping neighborhood.
