Finite-parameter 1-types of the generic {1,2,3}-metric space contain the two-particle Widom–Rowlinson model exactly: on a graph-metric A_G, the nonalgebraic type polynomial factors as a vacancy-set sum over connected components, so the scalar type count is hom(G,P_3^circ), is #P-complete already at distance-1 degree 3, and ranges sharply from 2^(n+1)-1 to 3^n.
