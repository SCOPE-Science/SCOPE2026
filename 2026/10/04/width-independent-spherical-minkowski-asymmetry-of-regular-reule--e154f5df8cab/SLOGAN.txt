For every regular spherical Reuleaux odd-\(n\)-gon of width at most \(\pi/2\), the Hou–Jin spherical Minkowski asymmetry is \(\bigl(2\cos(\pi/(2n))-1\bigr)^{-1}\), completely independent of the spherical width. The formula is exactly the Euclidean regular-Reuleaux value and decreases from the Reuleaux-triangle extremum to the disk value \(1\).
