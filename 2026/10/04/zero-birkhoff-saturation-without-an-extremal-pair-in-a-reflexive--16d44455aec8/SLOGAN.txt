A reflexive strictly convex \(\ell_2\)-sum of two-dimensional \(\ell_{p_n}\) blocks can have \(J_B^{\varepsilon}=J=2\) for every \(0\le\varepsilon<1\), hence zero Birkhoff saturation, while no unit pair attains the value \(2\). The new saturation parameter can therefore be purely asymptotic in infinite dimensions.
