# Review

## Correctness

PASS. For a finite local nonfield, the zero divisors form the maximal ideal. The published total-graph structure theorem gives clique components in residue characteristic two and balanced complete-bipartite components paired by opposite residue classes in odd characteristic. Deleting zero changes only the maximal-ideal clique to \(K_{s-1}\). Zero forcing is additive over components; the exact values and minimum-set structures for \(K_t\), \(K_{s,s}\), and \(K_1\) are proved directly. Explicit block matrices have rank \(q\) for \(s\ge3\) and rank \(1\) in the \(s=2\) boundary. The standard inequality \(M(G)\le Z(G)\) then gives equality and the stated minimum ranks.

Sources and risks: the exact object and its primary classification were checked in the full 2012 paper, and the component theorem was checked in the full 2008 paper. The packaged checker reconstructs representative rings directly. The minimum-rank field is explicitly restricted to the reals.

## Originality

PASS. The exact \(T_0(\Gamma(R))\) paper studies connectivity, diameter, girth, and path notions, and its full text contains no occurrence of “zero forcing,” “minimum rank,” or “nullity.” A 2016 survey likewise contains neither “zero forcing” nor “minimum rank.” Targeted web and semantic-database searches did not locate the stated formulas, equality \(Z=M\), residue-field recovery, or minimum-set transversal classification for \(T_0(\Gamma(R))\).

The closest later source is Mishra–Patra, which studies a nil-ideal total graph on \(\mathbb Z_n\), retains zero in the underlying graph, and computes minimum zero forcing sets there before constructing a graph on those sets. For prime-power \(\mathbb Z_n\) this is genuine nearby full-total-graph coverage, so the accepted statement is specifically about zero deletion, arbitrary finite local rings, maximum nullity/minimum rank, and the exact boundary behavior.

## Value

PASS. The result converts an algebraic residue invariant into an inverse-eigenvalue invariant: for every nonboundary ring, \(\operatorname{mr}(T_0)=|R/\mathfrak m|\), and graph order then recovers \(|\mathfrak m|\). It also identifies a sharp boundary at maximal-ideal size two and classifies every minimum forcing set as a coset-transversal complement. This is more structural than another isolated zero-forcing count.

Risk: after the published component theorem is invoked, the componentwise forcing calculations are short. The value is in the exact equality with maximum nullity, algebraic recovery, boundary analysis, and all-minimizer classification.
