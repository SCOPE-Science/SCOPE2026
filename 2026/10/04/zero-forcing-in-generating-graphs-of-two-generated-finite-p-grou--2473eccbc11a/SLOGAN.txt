For every two-generated noncyclic finite \(p\)-group, the generating graph is a Frattini-isolate set plus an equal-part complete multipartite graph, and its minimum zero forcing sets are exactly the complements of unordered generating pairs. The zero forcing polynomial recovers \(p\) and \(|\Phi(G)|\), while real symmetric minimum rank is \(1,2,3\) according as \((p-1)|\Phi(G)|\) is \(1,2,\ge3\).
