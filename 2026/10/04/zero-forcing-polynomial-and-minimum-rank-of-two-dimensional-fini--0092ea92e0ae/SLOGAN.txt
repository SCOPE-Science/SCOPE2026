For \(q\ge3\), the two-dimensional finite-field total dot product graph has \(\mathcal Z(u)=u^{(q+1)(q-2)}(u+q-1)^{q+1}\), real minimum rank \(q+1\), and \(Z=M=q^2-q-2\). Its minimum zero forcing sets are exactly complements of projective transversals, one omitted nonzero vector from each line of \(\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_q)\).
