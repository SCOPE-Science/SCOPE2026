For two cliques of orders \(a,b\ge3\) joined by a single bridge, zero forcing is controlled by two local clique thresholds plus one global dead configuration. This gives the exact polynomial \(\mathcal Z(B_{a,b};x)=x^{a+b-3}(x^3+(a+b)x^2+(ab+a+b-2)x+(2ab-a-b))\), hence \(Z(B_{a,b})=a+b-3\) and exactly \(2ab-a-b\) minimum sets.
