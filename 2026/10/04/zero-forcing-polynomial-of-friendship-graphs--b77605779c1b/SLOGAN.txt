A zero forcing set of a friendship graph either contains the center and meets every outer pair, or omits the center, meets every outer pair, and fully contains at least one pair. Thus \(\mathcal Z(F_k;x)=x^k((1+x)(2+x)^k-2^k)\), giving \(Z(F_k)=k+1\), \(2^{k-1}(k+2)\) minimum sets, and \(2\cdot3^k-2^k\) zero forcing sets in total.
