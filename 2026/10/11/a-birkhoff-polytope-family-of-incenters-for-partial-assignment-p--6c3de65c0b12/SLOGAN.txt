For fixed-cardinality nonattacking-rook polytopes, the optimal Euclidean inball has a sharp threshold at \(k=N+1-\sqrt{N+1}\): below it the uniform center is unique, while above it the **entire** incenter set is a positive scaled copy of the \(N\)-by-\(N\) Birkhoff polytope. At every rank the enclosing sphere instead has the unique uniform center and touches every partial permutation matrix.
