# Assessment of the monomial composition counterexample

## Correctness — PASS

The coefficient norm explicitly defines an analytic weighted Hardy Hilbert space with admissible radius of convergence: the interpolant is \(1\)-Lipschitz, giving \(n^{-1}\le\beta(n)\le n\). It follows both that multiplication by \(z\) is bounded and that \(C_{z^2}\) is bounded with norm exactly \(2\). The monomial chain \(1,2,4,8,\ldots\) is reducing, and its weights are precisely \(2^{(-1)^{\lfloor\log_2(j+1)\rfloor}}\). The polar-decomposition formula for a positive weighted shift yields binomial averages; the explicit Hoeffding estimate rigorously separates even and odd dyadic subsequences. Hence a fixed weak-operator matrix coefficient has limits \(2\) and \(1/2\). The accompanying finite exact-arithmetic checks corroborate coefficients, but the analytic estimate proves the infinite assertion.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

The originating manuscript (Lahiri--Ojha--Birbonshi, arXiv:2604.27762v1, Section 5, Question 5.2) asks whether *all* bounded composition operators on *some weighted Hardy space* enjoy strong convergence of their Aluthge iterates. Its proved results concern specific linear-fractional maps and leave this general question open. Chō--Jung--Lee (2005, Section 3, Proposition 3.2) already prove nonconvergence for abstract weighted shifts, including the binomial averaging mechanism. That antecedent is explicitly credited. The present statement additionally realizes the obstruction as the **unweighted analytic composition operator with the fixed symbol \(z^2\)** on an explicit highly regular admissible weighted Hardy space. An earlier general representation theorem or an unindexed construction could conceivably give the same example. The complete 2015 Jung--Kim--Ko article was not available for direct full-text comparison, although its accessible abstract and the 2026 manuscript's description concern two different linear-fractional symbols. This leaves a bounded bibliographic risk rather than a contradictory coverage result.

## Value — PASS

The construction supplies an explicit negative answer to a natural, published 2026 operator-theoretic question. The obstruction persists despite polynomial bounds on the Hardy coefficient weights, asymptotically unit neighboring ratios, bounded multiplication by \(z\), a bounded non-weighted composition map, and a fixed elementary symbol. These hypotheses rule out the interpretation that the example is merely an arbitrary pathological Hilbert-space weighted shift. The result does not address the restricted two-symbol Question 5.1 or standard unweighted Hardy spaces.
