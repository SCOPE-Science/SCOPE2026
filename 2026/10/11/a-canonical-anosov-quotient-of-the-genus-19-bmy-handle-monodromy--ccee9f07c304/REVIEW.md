PASS. The source supplies the exact characteristic polynomial, irreducibility of the degree-\(17\) factor, the two-dimensional kernel \(K=\ker(S^2+I)\), an integral basis of \(L=K\cap H_1\), and isotropy of \(L\). From these premises the proof reconstructs every claimed step: coprimality of \(h\) and \(h^*\); exclusion of unit-circle roots from \(hh^*\); the rank-\(34\) symplectic quotient; the quotient characteristic polynomial; the \(17+17\) stable/unstable split; and the unique nonsplit \((t^2+1)^2\) rational primary block. The bundled exact-arithmetic script replays the polynomial and rank bookkeeping. No finite experiment is used to infer an infinite statement.

## Value
PASS. The quotient \(L^\perp/L\) is canonical: it removes exactly the finite-order isotropic lattice singled out by the source and leaves a genuinely hyperbolic symplectic action. This yields an explicit rank-\(34\) Anosov torus automorphism attached to a newly constructed genus-\(19\) pseudo-Anosov handle map and exposes a nonsemisimple four-dimensional neutral primary sector that is invisible if one records only the finite-orbit plane. The statement is structural rather than a recomputation of a table entry or an arbitrary parameter slice.
