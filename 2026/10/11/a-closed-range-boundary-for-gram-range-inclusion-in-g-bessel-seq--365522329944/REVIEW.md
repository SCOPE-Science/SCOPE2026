# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The family
\[
\Lambda_nx=\frac{x_n}{n}
\]
is \(g\)-Bessel, with
\[
T=T^*=\operatorname{diag}(1/n)
\]
and
\[
G=T^*T=\operatorname{diag}(1/n^2).
\]
The vector
\[
(1/n^2)_{n\ge1}
\]
belongs to \(R(T^*)\) but not \(R(G)\). The exact closed-range boundary follows from Douglas factorization and a positive spectral gap for \(T^*T\).

## Originality
PASS. The discrete 2019 predecessor and the continuous 2020 successor were both inspected, so the inherited statement is included in the claim. Exact-source, lemma-number, Gram-range, analysis-range, and closed-range searches were compared with nearby literature.

The abstract identity
\[
R(T^*)=R(T^*T)
\]
under closed range is standard operator theory and is not claimed as new. The original contribution is the source-specific correction of both published \(g\)-frame lemmas with one explicit witness.

## Value
PASS. The erroneous lemma sits directly inside the synthesis/Gram range program of both papers. The repair gives the exact missing condition and explains why the later frame-sequence range criterion works once closed range is available.

## Limitations
The finding corrects the two auxiliary lemmas, not every downstream theorem. Results that independently assume or prove closed range can survive unchanged. A nonindexed correction may exist.
