For the positive periodic MERS-CoV/DPP4 model, phase-volume contraction is cohomologous to a strictly negative observable: \(\det\Phi(\omega)=\exp[-\int_0^\omega(d_1+c+\lambda/T+\lambda_1/D)\,dt]\). Every positive locked cycle therefore has at least one rigorously contracting Floquet direction, although the identity alone does not prove full stability.
