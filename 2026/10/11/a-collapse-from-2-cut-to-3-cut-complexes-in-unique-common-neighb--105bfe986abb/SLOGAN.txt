For every connected triangle-free, \(C_4\)-free graph of diameter at most \(2\), the ordinary \(2\)-cut complex simplicially collapses onto the ordinary \(3\)-cut complex. Hence \(\Delta_3(G)\simeq\bigvee^{e-n+1}S^{n-4}\), with the zero-fold wedge contractible; in particular \(\Delta_3(P)\simeq\bigvee^6S^6\) for the Petersen graph.
