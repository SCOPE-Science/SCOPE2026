For finite nontrivial Bourgain–Morrey triples, the Calderón–Lozanovskiĭ product always embeds contractively into the Bourgain–Morrey space obtained by harmonically interpolating \(p\), \(u\), and \(r\). This turns a recent abstract off-diagonal fractional Gagliardo–Nirenberg target into a concrete Bourgain–Morrey target without compatibility assumptions.
