The specified Gaussian-rational witness lies strictly in the disk; substituting it into the source's actual Definition 1 at \(\alpha=1/2\) gives a strictly negative real part, with nonvanishing denominators. The bundled standard-library checker verifies the full integer certificate and checks the derivatives by a second algebraic route. No finite test is extrapolated to a universal theorem: one rigorously checked counterexample suffices to falsify a universal claim.

## Mathematical value: PASS

This is a direct counterexample to an explicitly proposed universal complex-analysis conjecture, not a routine restatement of an endpoint case. It changes the logical status of the proposed positivity principle and removes a stated premise for a potential Graham–Kohr extension argument.
