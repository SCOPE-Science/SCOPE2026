# Review of A coordinate-subspace dichotomy inside a strongly unique-predual renorming of \(\ell_1\)

## Correctness
PASS. The exact norm formula from arXiv:2609.24392v1 is sufficient to check every coordinate projection directly. Coordinate deletion decreases both components of the defining maximum, proving contractivity; nonzero ranges force norm exactly \(1\). Weak-* closedness follows because missing coordinates are kernels of vectors in the specified predual. The two infinite-subspace cases are exact: omitting coordinate \(1\) leaves the ordinary \(\ell_1\) norm, while retaining coordinate \(1\) and infinitely many tail coordinates gives the original norm after a tail permutation.

## Originality
PASS. The closest source proves only one bad codimension-one hyperplane in this construction. Targeted searches for finite-codimension inheritance, coordinate-subspace formulations, continuum families, and norm-one complemented copies did not locate a result implying the full dichotomy. The new claim is not the source counterexample itself; it is the classification of all infinite canonical coordinate subspaces and the simultaneous every-finite-codimension consequence.

Residual risk: an older equivalent formulation could exist under different terminology for coordinate renormings or predual inheritance.

## Value
PASS. The focal problem is precisely whether strong uniqueness survives weak-* closed finite-codimensional passage. The result shows that, even within one fixed explicit ambient space and under norm-one complementability, the answer can be either yes or no at every positive finite codimension. The continuum-sized two-sided family gives a structural picture of the counterexample rather than a routine one-step extension.

## Closest literature and limitations
Aliaga--Vico, arXiv:2609.00970v1, first made the codimension-one counterexample public. Aliaga--Cúth--Vico, arXiv:2609.24392v1, contains the exact norm and proof used here. Brown--Ito, DOI 10.4153/CJM-1980-006-2, verifies the classical nonuniqueness of the isometric predual of \(\ell_1\).

The result is limited to canonical coordinate subspaces of this specific renormed \(\ell_1\) and does not classify arbitrary complemented subspaces.
