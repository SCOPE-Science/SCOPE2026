Plurisubharmonicity can be detected by a fixed countable subfamily of the new Skoda converse: it is enough to test the tuples \(\bigl(A(z-q),M\bigr)\) with Gaussian-rational \(q\) and \(A\), and positive-integer \(M\) and Skoda exponent. On bounded pseudoconvex domains this countable testing property is equivalent to plurisubharmonicity for any fixed nonzero \(f\in A^2(D,\varphi)\).
