# Mathematical review

## Correctness
The infected-prey equilibrium balance forces \(X_S>d_2/\beta\). The predator balance and monotonicity of the saturating square-root response yield a strict positive lower bound on \(X_I\), while the susceptible-prey balance gives the incompatible strict upper budget. At the Section 7.2 terminating decimals, exact arithmetic gives \(X_I>80/261\) and a contradiction margin \(-1156/6525\). This excludes every interior equilibrium for every delay; the nonzero infected-prey residual at the reported point is an independent check.

## Originality
The source's Eq. (2.10) and Theorem 2.5 establish equilibrium balances and discuss an existence test, but Section 7.2 reports a positive equilibrium rather than proving the necessary exclusion criterion. Searches for corrections, equivalent formulations, and the exact numerical tuple did not find earlier coverage. Related herd/refuge and published equilibrium-correction results use different vector fields. This is a qualified literature comparison, not an exhaustive priority certification.

## Scientific value
The negative result concerns the principal published numerical coexistence/Hopf scenario, not just rounding of a displayed state. Its simple structural inequality rules out the entire interior equilibrium set at those printed parameters and shows that Hopf analysis about the stated point does not apply to the printed system.

## Scope and literature limits
Wu and Meng, *AIMS Mathematics* 6 (2021), DOI 10.3934/math.2021218, supplies the exact equations and Section 7.2 data. Saha and Samanta, *International Journal of Biomathematics* 12 (2019), DOI 10.1142/S1793524519500074, concerns a different strong-Allee model according to its abstract; its full text was not accessible for a complete priority comparison. An unindexed correction may exist. This review neither reconstructs the authors' code nor extrapolates to a corrected model.
