# Review

## Correctness
PASS. At a positive equilibrium the delay drops out. The infected-prey equation forces \(X_S>d_2/\beta\); the predator equation converts monotonicity of \(G\) into a strict lower bound on \(X_I\); and the susceptible-prey equation converts positivity of \(Y\) into a strict upper-budget inequality. The exact Section 7.2 fractions violate that necessary inequality. The direct residual at the reported point is a separate exact consistency check and is not used as the main proof.

## Value

It gives a reusable delay-independent necessary condition for positive coexistence and proves that the entire positive equilibrium set is empty for the source's principal Section 7.2 parameter example. Because that example is used to support local stability and Hopf conclusions, ruling out their required equilibrium is a substantive boundary check on the model's bifurcation claims.
