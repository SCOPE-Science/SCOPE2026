The sharp Bohr radius of the normalized pluriharmonic polydisc class changes scale at \(1-\alpha\asymp n^{-1}\). For fixed \(0<\alpha<1\), \(r_{n,\alpha}=(1-\alpha)^{-1/2}n^{-3/2}-\frac{5}{6(1-\alpha)}n^{-2}+O_\alpha(n^{-5/2})\), while the endpoint window has the larger \(n^{-1}\) scale governed by an explicit profile.
