A six-point equilateral Lipschitz-free space has a unit vector whose pointwise Daugavet constant is exactly \(3/4\) but whose \(\Delta\)-constant is at least \(7/8\). Every convex representation by molecules nevertheless places weight at least \(1/4\) on a molecule at distance \(3/4\), so distant molecules alone miss a genuine quantitative convex-hull witness.
