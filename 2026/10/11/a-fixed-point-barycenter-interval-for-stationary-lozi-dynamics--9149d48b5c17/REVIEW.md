# Correctness
The stationary budget follows directly from first-coordinate and second-coordinate invariance. Decomposition into positive and negative parts gives the two endpoint-defect formulas exactly. Endpoint rigidity is valid because equality removes one sign of \(X\); with \(b>0\), the invariant support then lies on one affine branch, and after translation the branch matrix is invertible and hyperbolic, so no nonzero compact invariant set exists there.

# Originality
The same-map literature inspected emphasizes strange attractors, fixed points, invariant manifolds, bifurcations, symbolic dynamics, entropy, and applications. Searches using barycenter, invariant-measure, time-average, first-moment, and \(|x|\)-moment formulations did not locate the sign-weighted stationary budget or its sharp fixed-point interval. The closest mathematical facts are the standard fixed-point formulas, which are only the two equality cases and do not imply the statement for arbitrary invariant measures.

# Value
The interval is sharp, parameter-explicit, and applies uniformly to periodic and chaotic compact stationary states. More than a mean bound, its two exact defects recover the positive and negative first-moment visitation from the barycenter. This gives a rigorous obstruction to one-sided non-equilibrium recurrence and a simple exact benchmark for numerical invariant statistics of a canonical piecewise-linear chaotic map.
