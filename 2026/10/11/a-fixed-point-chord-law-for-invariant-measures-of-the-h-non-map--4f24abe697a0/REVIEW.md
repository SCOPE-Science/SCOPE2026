## Correctness
PASS. Invariance of the second coordinate gives the exact marginal pushforward
\[
y_\#\mu=(bx)_\#\mu.
\]
Invariance of the first coordinate then gives
\[
a\mathbb E[x^2]=1-(1-b)m.
\]
Factoring the fixed-point quadratic proves
\[
\operatorname{Var}(x)=(x_+-m)(m-x_-).
\]
The Euclidean chord identity follows after adding the two coordinate variances. Endpoint rigidity and the sharp midpoint bound are exact, and the equal fixed-point mixture attains the bound.

Risk: compact support is stronger than necessary, but safely guarantees every moment used in the proof.
