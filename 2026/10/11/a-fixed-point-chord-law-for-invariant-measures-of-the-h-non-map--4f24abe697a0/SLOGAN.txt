Every compact invariant Hénon measure has its barycenter on the segment joining the two fixed points, and its total coordinate variance is exactly the product of the barycenter's two distances to those endpoints. Equivalently, \(\operatorname{Var}(x)=(x_+-m)(m-x_-)\), so a chaotic invariant state and the two-fixed-point mixture with the same barycenter have exactly the same total coordinate variance.
