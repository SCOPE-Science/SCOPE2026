For the radial mean bodies \(R_p([-1,1]^2)\), the Euclidean longest directions switch sharply from the four coordinate axes to the four diagonals at \(p=2\), where exactly eight directions tie. The shortest directions remain diagonal through \(p=0\), then split into eight oblique directions with coordinate ratio \(1/(p+1)\); the complete profile holds for every \(p>-1\).
