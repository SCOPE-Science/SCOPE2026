# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof gives a complete all-graph argument. In girth at least six, distance-two pairs have unique midpoints; a descending free-face matching retracts all centered simplices to the graph. Every noncentered triangle is exactly an alternating triple of an induced six-cycle; a four-vertex clique containing one would force overlapping six-cycles. Thus the full Rips complex attaches exactly two disks per hexagon. Each pair kills one primitive cycle and adds one two-sphere; different hexagons have disjoint edge supports. The standalone exact verifier checks all cliques, the local collapse and mod-two homology in nine finite examples. Limits: finite experiments do not prove the universal case, and the proof—not the output log—supports it.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

Adamaszek (arXiv:1104.0433) treats the stronger girth-seven hypothesis and the known one-hexagon example but not the hexagon packing formula. Adamaszek et al. (arXiv:1712.06224) cover short-path graph gluings, including some special cases, but their stated criteria do not apply to all theta graphs in this theorem. Saleh et al. (arXiv:2302.14388) classify specific Platonic-solid vertex metrics, not this general class. Their actual theorems and hypotheses were compared in the inspected primary HTML texts. An unindexed or implicit equivalent remains a residual risk; absence of a search result is not a mathematical uniqueness proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Girth six is the first sharp obstruction to the known collapse of square-graph clique complexes to the original graph. The exact formula gives an infinite-family mixed-dimensional homotopy classification and a full integral persistence transition for arbitrary branching, vertex-sharing and noncactus members. Both the sharp graph hypothesis and the survival/killing of homology classes are structurally motivated; the conclusion is not a routine numerical increment.

## Boundaries

Only simple connected finite graphs with girth at least six, at least one hexagon and pairwise edge-disjoint **all** six-cycles are classified. No claims are made about higher scales, arbitrary overlapping hexagons, a continuous metric graph, or symmetry representations. The included code is finite cross-checking; an independent scientific review remains necessary before treating originality and correctness as externally certified.
