When a finite graph has girth at least six and its distinct six-cycles are edge-disjoint, its inclusive scale-two vertex Rips complex turns each hexagonal loop into exactly one two-sphere: \(\operatorname{VR}_{\leq2}(G)\simeq\bigvee^{b-h}S^1\vee\bigvee^{h}S^2\), where \(b\) is the cycle rank and \(h\geq1\) counts six-cycles. The classification applies even when hexagons share vertices or sit inside noncactus graphs.
