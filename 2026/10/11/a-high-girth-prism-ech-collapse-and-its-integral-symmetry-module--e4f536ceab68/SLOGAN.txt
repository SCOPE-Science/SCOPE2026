For every connected graph \(H\) without triangles or four-cycles, the scale-two intrinsic Čech nerve of its prism \(H\square K_2\) collapses onto one tetrahedral two-sphere per edge, giving \(H\vee\bigvee^{|E(H)|}S^2\). Its integral second homology is the unsigned edge-permutation representation, while the scale-one-to-two first-homology kernel is the oriented edge representation with a sign change under layer swapping.
