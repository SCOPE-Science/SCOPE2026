For a decreasing sequence \(t_n\downarrow0\), every Lipschitz map from \(\{0,t_n:n\ge1\}\) into every nonzero real Banach space attains its pointwise norm exactly when \(t_{n+1}/t_n\to0\). Otherwise the nonattainers, together with zero, contain a closed isometric copy of \(\ell_\infty\), with a uniform quantitative gap at the accumulation point.
