There is a norm on real Hilbert space within a factor \(\sqrt2\) of its usual norm such that both the space and its dual are smooth and locally uniformly rotund, and every vector is individually \(C\)-approximately left- and right-symmetric for Birkhoff--James orthogonality, yet the sharp global \(C\)-symmetry defect equals \(1\) and is never attained. Two-coordinate witnesses approach the obstruction with deficit asymptotic to \(p^{-1/2}\).
