In Volokitin's cubic Darboux flow, the original-time escape delay next to the invariant line \(x=-y\) is logarithmic for \(p<0\), double-logarithmic for \(p=0\), and approaches a finite limit with a sharp \(\varepsilon^p\) cusp for \(p>0\). An exact two-scale crossover \(p\log((2+\varepsilon)/\varepsilon)=\delta\) interpolates uniformly between these regimes, while the blow-up order jumps from a simple pole off the line to a square-root pole on it for \(p>0\).
