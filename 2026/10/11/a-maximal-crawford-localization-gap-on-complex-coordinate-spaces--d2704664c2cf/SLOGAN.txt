On every complex coordinate space \(c_0(I)\) with at least two coordinates, surjective diagonal isometries realize the **largest possible** gap between the full Crawford number \(r\in(0,1)\) and the property-\(\beta\) coordinate-restricted value \(1\). The gap remains positive under sufficiently small operator-norm perturbations, giving a sharp positive-Crawford counterexample to Choi--Lee Question 3.4.
