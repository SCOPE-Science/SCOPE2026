Deleting just one point from a nonsplit quadratic conic over \(\mathbb F_q\) changes the sharp fourth-moment extension problem: for every odd \(q\ge5\), the extremizers must vanish at the retained antipode of the deletion. The exact sharp norm is \(3-3/(q-1)\) in the normalized fourth-power inequality, while the exceptional field \(\mathbb F_3\) instead has sharp constant \(5/3\) and flat-amplitude extremizers.
