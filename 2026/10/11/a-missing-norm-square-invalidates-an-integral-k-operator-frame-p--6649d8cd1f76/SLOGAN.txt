The perturbation theorem for integral \(K\)-operator frames loses a square: the proof needs \(\|L\|^2\int|a_\omega|^2<A\), not \(\|L\|\int|a_\omega|^2<A\). A one-point scalar frame cancels to zero while satisfying the printed hypothesis, and the same defect was propagated to the later Hilbert-\(C^*\)-module generalization.
