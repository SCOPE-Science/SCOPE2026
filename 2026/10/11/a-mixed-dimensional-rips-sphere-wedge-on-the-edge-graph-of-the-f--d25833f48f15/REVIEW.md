# Same-model scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The object, metric, and inclusive parameter inequalities are explicitly specified. At scale one, the cover by cube-vertex star simplices has nerve \(Q_4\). At scale two, a fully enumerated \(13{,}896\)-simplex face poset admits the supplied \(6{,}943\)-pair acyclic matching with one zero-, one three-, and eight five-dimensional critical cells. The full directed Hasse graph has \(66{,}304\) arrows and its topological sort visits every vertex. An exact cup-one calculation constructs a nonzero degree-three class and proves \(\mathrm{Sq}^2=0\), which rules out all eight possible suspended-Hopf attachments. At scale three, the sole missing edges are a perfect matching, giving a join of sixteen zero-spheres. The claimed persistence maps follow from the absence of equal positive-dimensional homology at consecutive thresholds. Finite exact arithmetic and the standard named topological theorems together support the homotopy claim; checks alone are not substituted for topological implications.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** Adamaszek's graph-power treatment covers general vocabulary and the ordinary line-graph clique complex but does not compute the square of this particular line graph. Goyal–Shukla–Singh cover unsquared line-graph clique complexes. Adamaszek–Adams cover the hypercube **vertex** metric, not its **edge** line-graph metric. Kozlov's collapse work is methodological. Exact-object and equivalent-formulation searches of the published research index and relevant literature found no covering statement, including after comparing implications rather than only titles. This is not proof of universal uniqueness; an unindexed equivalent computation remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** The four-cube is the first small hypercube for which radius two is a genuine intermediate scale in the edge line-graph metric. Its 32-edge filtration is a natural complete finite classification, not a chosen parameter merely for a favorable answer. The eight five-dimensional spheres together with the three-sphere give a mixed-dimensional homotopy type, and the exact test of \(\mathrm{Sq}^2\) resolves a genuine attaching-map ambiguity left by a Morse-cell or Betti-number computation. The result supplies a reproducible full persistence barcode and an informative benchmark for Rips complexes of graph powers.

Scope is only this finite metric; a synonymous earlier graph-power calculation in unindexed sources remains a residual originality risk. Exact finite enumeration does not imply a general hypercube theorem.
