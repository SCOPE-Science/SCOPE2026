The graph-metric Vietoris–Rips filtration on the 32 edges of the four-cube passes through \(\bigvee^{17}S^1\), the mixed-dimensional wedge \(S^3\vee\bigvee^8S^5\), and \(S^{15}\) before becoming contractible. A complete discrete-Morse matching and a vanishing Steenrod-square calculation together certify the intermediate wedge, not merely its homology.
