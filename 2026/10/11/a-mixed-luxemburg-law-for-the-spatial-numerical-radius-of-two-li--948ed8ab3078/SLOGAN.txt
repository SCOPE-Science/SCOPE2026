On real or complex \(\ell_p^3\), the spatial numerical radius of every two-link weighted nilpotent shift is exactly a fixed scalar multiple of the mixed \((p,q)\) Luxemburg norm of its ordered edge weights, with \(q=p/(p-1)\). Conjugate exponents reverse the weights without changing the radius, while for \(p=4\) the larger first weight gives a strictly larger radius than its reversal.
