# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For each integer m>=1, Lemma 3.1 of arXiv:2609.28917v1 gives integral 2-component surgery coefficients 2m+15 and 0 and linking number m+2; Claim 3.2 identifies the filling with Sigma_2(D(1-m)). The integral surgery presentation is [[2m+15,m+2],[m+2,0]]. Its entry gcd is gcd(11,m+2) and absolute determinant is (m+2)^2, so the two Smith factors are d and (m+2)^2/d with d|((m+2)^2/d). Exactly m≡9 mod 11 gives d=11 and a noncyclic group. All other m have cyclic H1. A two-bridge link has a lens-space cover with cyclic H1, so only the noncyclic class is excluded. The argument proves all m by Smith theory; sample computations only corroborate.

This is an elementary corollary, not a new technique; priority remains qualified by the risk that the two-line computation was previously recorded elsewhere.
