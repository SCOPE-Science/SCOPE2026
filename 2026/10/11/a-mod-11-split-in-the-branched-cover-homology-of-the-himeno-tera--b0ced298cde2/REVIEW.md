# Review

## Correctness
The source gives the surgery coefficients (2m+15,0), linking number m+2 and the double-branched-cover identification for D(1-m). The two-by-two integral surgery presentation has determinant -(m+2)^2 and Smith invariants gcd(m+2,11) and (m+2)^2/gcd(m+2,11), which agree with the branch-link determinant. The noncyclic residue class and one-way two-bridge obstruction follow.

## Originality
The primary family paper supplies every surgery-matrix entry and the branch-link determinant, but does not state the first-homology decomposition or mod-11 cyclicity split. Targeted statement-level searches did not identify an earlier theorem for these links. The calculation is an elementary application of standard Smith theory, so priority of this explicit corollary remains subject to unindexed literature.

## Value
The cover's integral first-homology group is a natural refinement of determinant, and its complete all-parameter structure gives an infinite congruence class of quasi-alternating links excluded from the two-bridge class. The scope is this exact family and this elementary topological obstruction, not a manifold classification or a converse lens-space criterion.
