For the Himeno--Teragaito quasi-alternating branch links \(D(1-m)\), the double-cover homology is exactly \(\mathbb Z/d\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/((m+2)^2/d)\mathbb Z\) with \(d=\gcd(m+2,11)\). The cover is therefore noncyclic precisely for \(m\equiv9\pmod{11}\), giving an infinite residue class of these links that cannot be two-bridge.
