For the synchronous binary parity rule on the distance-two graph of an \(n\)-dimensional Boolean cube, the entire state dynamics switches with \(n\bmod4\): either every state reaches zero in at most two updates or every state is fixed or in a two-cycle. A closed formula for the rank of the shifted binary adjacency matrix gives the exact number of states in every transient or periodic class for all \(n\ge2\).
