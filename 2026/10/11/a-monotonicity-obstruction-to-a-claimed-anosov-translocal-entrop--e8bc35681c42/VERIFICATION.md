# Verification

For \(0\le\omega_1<\omega_2\), the defining initial balls satisfy \(B(z,e^{-\omega_2 n})\subseteq B(z,e^{-\omega_1 n})\) for every \(n\). Therefore every maximal separated-set cardinality for \(\omega_2\) is bounded above by the corresponding cardinality for \(\omega_1\). Passing through logarithms, normalized limits, and \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\) proves the required nonincreasing dependence without an experiment.

For fixed positive rates \(\chi_i\), the disputed expression \(h_{\mathrm{top}}(f)-\sum_{\chi_i\ge\omega}(\chi_i-\omega)\) is piecewise affine with slope equal to the number of active rates and is continuous when a rate leaves the active set. Hence it is nondecreasing.

For \(A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}\), the characteristic polynomial is \(t^2-3t+1\), so \(\Lambda=(3+\sqrt5)/2\) is the expanding eigenvalue and \(\chi=\log\Lambda\). The source's Theorem 2.2 gives \(h_\omega=(\chi-\omega)_+\); the disputed expression gives \(\min\{\omega,\chi\}\). The contradiction is exact.

The author-hosted preprint was inspected for its date, MSC classification, definition, and theorem, and the journal full text was inspected for the same statements and the post-theorem extension. The conclusion is limited to falsity of that extension as written; no corrected non-affine theorem is asserted.
