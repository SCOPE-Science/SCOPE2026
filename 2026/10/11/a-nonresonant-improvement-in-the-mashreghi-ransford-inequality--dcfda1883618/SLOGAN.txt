When \(\arccos(1/\beta)/\pi\) is irrational, the Mashreghi–Ransford binomial-transform inequality admits the improved universal-on-this-class constant \(\frac4\pi(2\cos(\pi/9)-1)=1.119668\ldots\), strictly below the globally sharp \(2/\sqrt3\). The improvement follows from Poisson angular equidistribution and an exact first-plus-third-harmonic absolute integral; its sharpness at fixed \(\beta\) is not asserted.
