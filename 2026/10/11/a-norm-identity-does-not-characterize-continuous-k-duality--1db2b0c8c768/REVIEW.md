# Review

## Correctness
PASS. On the two-point counting space with
\[
f=g=\{e_1,e_2\}
\]
and
\[
K=P_{\operatorname{span}\{e_1\}},
\]
both scalar identities in Theorem 3.2 reduce exactly to
\[
|a|^2=|a|^2.
\]
The reconstruction operator is nevertheless
\[
T_fT_g^*=I,
\]
not \(K\).

The structural derivation is exact. If
\[
M=T_fT_g^*,
\]
the first scalar identity is equivalent by polarization to
\[
K^*(M-K)=0,
\]
so it certifies only
\[
P_{\overline{R(K)}}M=K.
\]

## Originality
PASS. The exact preprint, journal theorem, norm-identity aliases, projected-reconstruction formulation, correction searches, and broader continuous \(K\)-dual literature were compared.

The closest published dual-frame result has a different invisible subspace, namely a frame-operator eigenspace. The closest range-compression result concerns a \(K\)-frame operator acting on \(R(K)\), not a scalar test for dual-pair reconstruction. Neither subsumes this theorem-specific counterexample.

## Value
PASS. The source presents Theorem 3.2 as a scalar characterization of duality. The failure occurs already for two orthonormal vectors and a rank-one projection, and the exact operator formula shows precisely what information the scalar test loses. This yields a usable repair rather than only a negative example.

## Limitations
The result concerns Theorem 3.2 only. The added range hypotheses are sufficient repairs and are not claimed to exhaust every possible special case. A nonindexed correction remains possible.
