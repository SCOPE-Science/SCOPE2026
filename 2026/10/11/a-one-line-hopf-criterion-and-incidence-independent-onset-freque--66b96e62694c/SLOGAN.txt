For the normalized nonlinear-incidence SIRS family, the source's two Hopf branches collapse to one criterion, and the original-time onset frequency is \(\omega_H^2=rs\widetilde X/X_1-1\). Under the generalized-Hopf reparametrization, the auxiliary variable \(D\) is exactly \(\omega_H^2\), so at fixed demographic ratios and endemic prevalence the onset frequency does not depend on the incidence exponents.
