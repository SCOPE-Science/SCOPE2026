For outer \(SU(2)\) nonlinear-Fourier data, \(\|b\|_{A_1}=B<1\) forces the explicit inverse bound \(\|a^{-1}\|_{A_1}\le(1-B^2)^{-2}\). Combined with the published quantitative Baxter estimate, this raises the known general radius in the new open problem from \(1/\sqrt3\) to \(1/\sqrt2\), leaving \(1/\sqrt2\le\tau\le1\).
