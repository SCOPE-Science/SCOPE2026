# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Exact integral certificates prove \(H_1\cong\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/4\) and \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z/2\). Independent mod-two elimination gives \(H_2\cong\mathbf F_2^2\), and the coefficient Bockstein is computed directly from halved integral boundaries. Every support through eight vertices is exhausted. The unique zero-Bockstein class first occurs on seven vertices with seven carriers, all induced orientable tori; the two nonzero-Bockstein classes first occur on eight vertices with fifteen and twenty carriers, and every minimum support has one-dimensional ambient image.

## Originality
PASS. The exact-object literature and data tables provide the triangulation, homology, and face vector but not the induced-support filtration or coefficient-Bockstein spectrum. Focused literature searches under exact-object, Bockstein, induced-torus, and support-minimum formulations did not locate the one-vertex gap or the \(7,15,20\) carrier census. An unindexed historical finite computation remains a residual risk.

## Value
PASS. The theorem isolates a precise combinatorial cost for nonzero Bockstein: the integral-lift class appears on seven vertices, while both nonlift classes require eight. The seven integral minimizers are themselves vertex-minimal tori, and the two nonlift minimum layers are completely counted, giving a structural refinement of the known torsion groups rather than a routine homology calculation.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest exact-object sources are Lutz's 2003 flat-space survey, the Manifold Page facet and homology tables, and the later f-vector table. They establish the ambient object but not the support theorem. The result is specific to the published seventeen-vertex triangulation and does not classify larger supports or alternate triangulations.
