In the seventeen-vertex triangulation of \(\mathcal B_4\), the unique mod-two \(H_2\)-class that lifts integrally already has seven-vertex torus carriers, while both classes with nonzero coefficient Bockstein require eight vertices. The liftable class has exactly \(7\) minimum carriers, whereas the two Bockstein-nonzero classes have \(15\) and \(20\), giving an exact one-vertex Bockstein support gap.\n
