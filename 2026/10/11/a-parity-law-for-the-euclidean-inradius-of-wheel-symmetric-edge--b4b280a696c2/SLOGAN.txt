For wheel symmetric edge polytopes with outer cycle length \(m\ge5\), the Euclidean inradius obeys a sharp parity law: \(1/\sqrt{m-2}\) for even \(m\), and \(\sqrt{(m+1)/(m^2-m-3)}\) for odd \(m\). The centered inball touches exactly \(m\) facets in the even case and \(2m\) in the odd case, represented by two-zero words separating maximally balanced sign blocks.
