The number of vertices in the cyclic two- and three-term simplex-sum deformation of dimension \(n-1\) is exactly \((1+\sqrt2)^n+(1-\sqrt2)^n-2L_n\), where \(L_n\) is the \(n\)th Lucas number, for every \(n\ge4\). Its normal fan is simplicial exactly when \(n=4\); the correction term counts two opposite directed-cycle obstructions.
