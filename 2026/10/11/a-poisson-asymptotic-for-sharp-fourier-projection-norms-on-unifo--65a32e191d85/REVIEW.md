# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The pair-incidence map has only the constant and irreducible \((N-2,2)\) images because every vertex has exactly \(k-1\) co-block partners. Schur orthogonality and the diagonal trace determine the exact normalized projector. A connected-component classification of ordered pair patterns proves every fixed factorial moment converges to the Poisson moment \(\lambda_k^r\). The second moment controls the absolute-deviation limit, and Poisson recursion gives the leading coefficient. Exact rational histograms and finite idempotence computations from the included checker corroborate the normalization. The proof explicitly fixes \(k\) before sending \(n\) to infinity.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
Meagher–Moura (2005) examines intersecting uniform partitions, rather than these harmonic-projection endpoint norms. Lindzey (2014) provides the perfect-matching spectral analogue. Meagher–Shirazi–Stevens (2021, Corollary 5.3), citing Bender (1974), proves the prior zero-overlap asymptotic \(\Pr(S=0)\to e^{-\lambda_k}\), a strong special case of the distributional assertion. Filmus–Lindzey (2022, Section 9) uses exactly the same pair-overlap statistic to describe the hypermatching spherical projector; this structural dependence is prior work and is not claimed as new. Their displayed results address largest intersecting families, without an explicit fixed-\(k\) Poisson law for common pair overlaps or a sharp normalized endpoint norm limit. The finite kernel is independently normalized by trace and checked by idempotence. The printed Proposition 22 coefficient in the source, evaluated at \(k=3,n=2\) with its stated overlap definition, yields trace \(12\) instead of the irreducible dimension \(9\); the corrected trace normalization is therefore not simply assumed from that formula. Bender’s more general regular contingency-table enumeration might imply full Poisson convergence. The originality claim is restricted to the sharp spectral-amplification norm constant and iterated asymptotic, not to the older zero-event limit or the spherical-function dependence. A prior equivalent projection-norm identity under another terminology remains a residual risk.

## Value — PASS
The leading endpoint norm of a canonical irreducible Fourier projection is a natural quantitative measure of harmonic amplification on \(S_{kn}/(S_k\wr S_n)\). Its exact \(k\)-dependent Poisson constant and iterated Gaussian limit distinguish finite blocks from large blocks and offer a concrete asymptotic counterpart to the previously established representation-theoretic kernel. This is not a claim of a uniform joint-limit theorem.
