# Review

## Correctness

PASS. The update map was reconstructed directly from the primary source. Its Jacobian at the origin is lower triangular with discrete multipliers \(0,0,\alpha_0\), so \(0<\alpha_0<1\) gives local asymptotic stability. More strongly, the admissible parameter point \((\rho_1,\rho_2,\alpha,\alpha_0)=(2,1,1/2,1/2)\) has an explicit forward-invariant positive neighborhood on which \(V=3H+P+G\) contracts by \(3/4\). Positivity is preserved, so these are genuine positive solutions converging to the origin. This directly contradicts a universal positive permanence lower bound.

## Value

PASS. Permanence is a global qualitative property central to the biological interpretation of a population model. Exhibiting an open set of strictly positive solutions that converges to extinction at the origin invalidates the theorem at its stated parameter assumptions, not merely a numerical example or a cosmetic formula. The one-way coupling also clarifies that the environmental parameters cannot alter the host–parasitoid trajectory in the printed equations.
