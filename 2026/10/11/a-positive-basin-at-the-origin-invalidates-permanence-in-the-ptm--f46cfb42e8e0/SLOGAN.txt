The published PTM host–parasitoid map cannot be permanent under its stated theorem: the origin has discrete multipliers \(0,0,\alpha_0\), so for \(0<\alpha_0<1\) it attracts a positive neighborhood. At \((\rho_1,\rho_2,\alpha,\alpha_0)=(2,1,1/2,1/2)\), the exact Lyapunov weight \(V=3H+P+G\) contracts by at least a factor \(3/4\) whenever \(V<1/16\).
