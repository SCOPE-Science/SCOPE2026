Predator release strengthens the prey-reset clock: every positive order-one cycle satisfies \(L<F(T)=\rho T-(\lambda/\beta)\log((h+\tau)/(h+\tau e^{-\beta T}))\), with \(L=\log(1/(1-p))\). Under the natural monotonicity condition \(\rho\ge\lambda\tau/(h+\tau)\), this gives a unique explicit numerical period floor that is substantially stronger than the release-blind bound \(T>L/\rho\).
