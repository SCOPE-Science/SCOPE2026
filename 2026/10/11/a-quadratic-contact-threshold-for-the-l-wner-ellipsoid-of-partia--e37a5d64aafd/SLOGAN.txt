For every \(n\ge3\), the canonical two-orbit covariance ellipsoid of the partial permutohedron \(\mathcal P(m,n)\) becomes its unique Löwner ellipsoid at the sharp dimension \(m=n(n+1)-1\). At that boundary the second-to-full support orbit still contacts; one dimension later only the origin and full-support permutations remain in contact.
