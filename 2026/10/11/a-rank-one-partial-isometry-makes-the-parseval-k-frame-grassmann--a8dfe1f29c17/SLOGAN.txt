The Grassmannian map in Sadri--Rahimlou Theorem 3.3 is not well-defined for general closed-range \(K\): one fixed Parseval \(K\)-frame can admit two permitted unitary factorizations whose proposed images are different subspaces. A rank-one partial isometry on \(\mathbb C^2\) gives the failure exactly, while \(R(T_F^*)\) is a canonical choice-independent subspace invariant.
