# Scientific assessment

**Correctness: PASS.** The first-chaos projection is invariant under contractive oddification; the eight-coordinate expansion covers every odd input. Exact symbolic expansion of the Walsh fourth moments matches a sum of quadratic forms, whose three rational matrices have all positive leading principal minors. The resulting upper bound holds universally, and the explicit three-coordinate witness provides the lower bound. The exact norm is deliberately **not** claimed.

**Originality: PASS (subject to independent bibliographic review).** Skrzypek's 2009 full paper derives a three-coordinate exact norm and analyzes complementary projections but does not present this rational 4-coordinate SOS bound. Semantically relevant mathematical-record searches did not identify a stronger directly covering four-coordinate estimate. Search absence is not proof of novelty; historical finite Walsh projection constants remain a risk.

**Value: PASS.** The four-character Rademacher projection is the first case beyond the analytically solved three-coordinate setting. The exact rational certificate substantially improves the classical fourth-moment norm estimate and traps this natural operator invariant inside an interval of width less than \(0.00573\), a relative separation near one half of one percent. This is a quantitative enclosure, not an exact evaluation.

**Closest literature:** Lesław Skrzypek, *On the \(L_p\) norm of the Rademacher projection and related inequalities* (2009), doi:10.1090/S0002-9939-09-09875-X. A source-specific unresolved risk is unindexed estimates for higher finite Rademacher projections.
