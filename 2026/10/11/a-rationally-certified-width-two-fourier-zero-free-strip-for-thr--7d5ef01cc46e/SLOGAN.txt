For three-dimensional convex bipyramids \(\Omega_\alpha=\{(x,y):|x|+\alpha\|y\|_2\le1\}\), every real Fourier zero lies beyond Euclidean radius \(2\alpha\). An exact-rational interval certificate raises the established zero-free transverse width from approximately \(1.256\) to \(2\), proving that every \(\alpha\ge23\) beats its equal-volume ball in first-Fourier-zero distance.
