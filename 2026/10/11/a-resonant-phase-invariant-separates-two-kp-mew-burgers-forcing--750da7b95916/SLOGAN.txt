At the \(2:1\) forcing resonance, the autonomous KP-MEW-Burgers embedding has an exact relative-phase first integral \(K=V\overline W^2\). The oscillator initial state used for the published Lyapunov diagram has \(K=(117-44i)/125\), whereas the printed periodically forced equation and all of its time translates have \(K=1\), so the two Figure 11 diagnostics are not for the same forcing phase.
