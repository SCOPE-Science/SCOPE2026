For the resonant Poisson-kernel densities \(\rho_{r,n}\), the equal-frequency Wasserstein sectional curvature is exactly \(3n^2/(1+r^2)\). Hence \(\int\rho_{r,n}^{-1}\to\infty\) while the curvature tends to \(3n^2/2\), giving an explicit obstruction to the universal curvature-decay clause in Corollary 3.12 of arXiv:2610.00625.
