Uniform perfectness plus doubling forces a quantitative reverse-doubling exponent \(\kappa>0\), and that exponent pushes the Campanato-to-Hölder necessity theorem from \(\lambda\le p\) to \(\lambda<p+\kappa\). If \(\kappa\ge Q\), this resolves the full open range \(Q<\lambda<Q+p\) for that quantitative subclass.
