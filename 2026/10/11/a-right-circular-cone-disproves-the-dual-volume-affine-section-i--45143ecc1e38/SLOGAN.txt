A truncated right circular cone in \(\mathbb R^3\) reverses the conclusion of the 2009 “Dual volumes and affine sections” problem: every plane through two explicit axial points prefers the lower point, while the full three-dimensional radial integral prefers the higher point. Exact derivative certificates at \(23/35\) and \(33/50\), combined with strict concavity of the radial-volume functional, make the reversal rigorous.
