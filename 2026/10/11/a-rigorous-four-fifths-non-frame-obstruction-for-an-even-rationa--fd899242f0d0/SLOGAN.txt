A rigorously signed \(3\times3\) fiber determinant proves that the even rational Gabor window \(g(x)=(x^2-1)/((x^2+1)(x^2+4)(x^2+9))\) has a non-frame rectangular lattice of density \(4/5\). This settles the first previously unproved index in the proposed \(p/(p+1)\) obstruction family; it does not resolve the remaining indices.
