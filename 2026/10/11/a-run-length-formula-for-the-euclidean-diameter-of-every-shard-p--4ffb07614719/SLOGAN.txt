For every shard polytope, the Euclidean diameter is determined by the arc's alternating run pattern: \(\operatorname{diam}^2=2(f+g)\), where \(f\) is the number of \(U\)-to-\(D\) runs and \(g\) is a greedy matching number of the runs having spare coordinates. In particular, run lengths beyond the distinction \(1\) versus \(\ge2\) do not affect the diameter.
