The seven-petal word \((0,2,4,6,1,3,5)\) is a trefoil whose exact reduction excess under the standard petal moves is \(2\): it cannot reach petal number \(5\) without passing through \(9\) petals. Thus monotonic petal simplification fails already for a nontrivial trefoil diagram, answering Vernitski's Question 7.4 affirmatively.
