For every fixed positive pair \(s,t\), the numerical radius of the four-periodically modulated geometric shift loses exactly \(a_{\mathrm{Ai}}[2stL/((s+t)\sqrt{4+(s-t)^2})]^{1/3}(1-q)^{2/3}\) from its periodic edge \(L=(s+t+\sqrt{4+(s-t)^2})/4\) as \(q\uparrow1\). The universal Airy zero and the explicitly homogenized four-edge resistance determine the sharp fractional-power constant.
