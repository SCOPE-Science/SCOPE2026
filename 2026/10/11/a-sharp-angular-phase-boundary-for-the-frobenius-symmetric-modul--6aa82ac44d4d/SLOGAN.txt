For the smallest real rank-one pair with orthogonal input supports and one positive corner projection, the squared Frobenius symmetric-modulus triangle defect admits an exact two-parameter formula. Strict failure occurs precisely at nontrivial alignment \(1/2<x<1\) and coupling \(0<t<\sqrt{x^2+2x-1}-x\), establishing a sharp \(\pi/3\) angular phase boundary.
