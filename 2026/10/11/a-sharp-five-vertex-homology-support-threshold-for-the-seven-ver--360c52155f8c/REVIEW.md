PASS. The published face formula for \(T_{7,1,2}\) is reconstructed directly. The complete one-skeleton reduces induced-image computation to the quotient classes of triangle boundaries. Exact \(\mathbf F_2\) elimination gives global first-homology dimension \(2\), and the packaged verifier exhausts all \(128\) vertex subsets. The \(21\) empty triangles occupy all three nonzero classes with multiplicities \(7,7,7\), and every four-set is checked against the face status of its complementary triple.

## Originality
PASS. The exact-object sources define and classify the torus but do not state this local homology-support spectrum. General tightness literature gives injectivity of induced-subcomplex homology, not the ranks of the global images. Focused searches over aliases and equivalent formulations found no covering statement. The closest published computation on the same torus concerns simplicial self-map contiguity; another published finding gives an empty-triangle count criterion for a cyclic family but not homology classes or induced-image ranks.

## Value
PASS. The invariant is structurally motivated by the unique vertex-minimal torus and by tightness. The result gives a sharp local-to-global threshold and a complete rank classification, including the nontrivial face/complement switch on four vertices.
