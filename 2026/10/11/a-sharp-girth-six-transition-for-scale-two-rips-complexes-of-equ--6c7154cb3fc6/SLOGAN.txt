The second graph-power complex of an \(m\)-arm equal-length theta graph is contractible for arm length two, a wedge of \(m-1\) two-spheres at length three, and a wedge of \(m-1\) circles at every longer arm length. At the exceptional six-girth boundary, reversing the two endpoints switches from acting by \(-I\) on first homology to \(+I\) on the new second homology.
