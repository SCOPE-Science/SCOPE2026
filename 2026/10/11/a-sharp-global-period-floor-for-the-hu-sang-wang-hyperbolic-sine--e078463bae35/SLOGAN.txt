For the Hu–Sang–Wang hyperbolic-sine jerk family, every compact stationary state obeys \(\int z^2\,d\mu=b^2\int y^2\,d\mu\), forcing every nonconstant periodic orbit to have \(T>2\pi/b\). The constant is sharp because the source's nondegenerate Hopf cycles approach \(2\pi/b\), but the nonlinear \(\sinh x\) term prevents equality at finite amplitude.
