The second partial permutohedron \(\mathcal P(m,2)\) has an explicit, unique minimum-volume enclosing ellipsoid in every dimension \(m\ge2\), certified by a rational three-orbit vertex distribution. Every vertex is tangent through \(m=5\), whereas for \(m\ge6\) precisely the origin and the \(m(m-1)\) two-support vertices are tangent.
