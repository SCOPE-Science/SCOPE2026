The symmetric edge polytope of an \(m\)-page book graph has an explicit unique Euclidean Löwner ellipsoid for every \(m\ge2\). When \(m\ge3\), adding the common hub edge to \(K_{2,m}\) does not change that ellipsoid, and for \(m\ge4\) both newly added root vertices are strictly interior; at \(m=2\), the optimal enclosing volume instead increases by exactly \(16/(9\sqrt3)\).
