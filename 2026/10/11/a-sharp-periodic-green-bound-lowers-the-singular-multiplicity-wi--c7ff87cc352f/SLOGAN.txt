For the periodic singular multiplicity theorem of arXiv:2610.04501v1, the forcing window \(B-A\) can be reduced from \(\frac{\pi}{3}\lVert e-\bar e\rVert_1\) to \(\frac{\pi}{4}\lVert e-\bar e\rVert_1\). The gain comes from the sharp oscillation norm of the periodic Green corrector rather than two separate sup-norm estimates.
