For the four-character rectangle \(a+bz+cw+dzw\) on the two-torus, every nonzero coefficient magnitude pattern has exactly one optimizing cross phase: aligned for every \(p>2\), opposite for every \(0<p<2\), and arbitrary at \(p=2\). In fact, the normalized \(p\)-moment varies strictly monotonically with the angular distance between those two phase extremes.
