On the critical scale \(q_n^{1/n}\to2\), a dyadic one-parameter Hénon family realizes every lower quotient limit \(L\in(0,2)\) while keeping the upper limit equal to \(2\). Its derivative-normalization series is summable exactly for \(L\ge1\); throughout that summable side the basin is biholomorphic to \(\mathbb C^2\), while the \(L<1\) side is parabolic but nonsummable and remains a concrete unresolved test family.
