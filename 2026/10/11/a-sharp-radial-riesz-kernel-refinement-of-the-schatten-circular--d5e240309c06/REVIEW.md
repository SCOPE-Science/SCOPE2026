The proof was reconstructed from the local regularized Hessian estimate, the exact Poisson--Jensen normalization, monotone convergence in the regularization parameter, and finite-rank compression. The coefficient was cross-checked by recovering the published frozen-radius theorem exactly and by equality on rank-one rays.

## Originality
PASS. The focal source states only the outer-radius frozen inequality. The closest classical Riesz-measure source gives the Poisson--Jensen mechanism but a different local Schatten-norm estimate with coefficient \(p^2/8\) and only \(1\le p\le2\). Targeted semantic searches for the radial operator-norm/Hilbert--Schmidt kernel and its rank-one equality case found no covering statement.

## Value
PASS. The claim is a sharp structural refinement: for \(0<p<2\) it strictly improves the published lower bound at every positive radius and nonzero direction, at \(p=2\) it becomes exact, and on rank-one rays from the origin it is attained for the full exponent range.
