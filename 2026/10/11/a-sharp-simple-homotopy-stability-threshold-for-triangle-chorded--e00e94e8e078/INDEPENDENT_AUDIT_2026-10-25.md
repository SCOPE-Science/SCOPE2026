# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For all n>=7 exactly four disjoint families of new faces occur under the single length-two chord: T_j,A_j,B_j,U_j, totaling 4n-18 faces. Each B_j is a free codimension-one face only of U_j and after those removals each T_j is free only in A_j, so 2n-9 elementary simplicial collapses leave B_3(C_n). The n=6 complex has 56 faces including the empty one and 27 verified free-face collapses to a vertex. The known S^3 homotopy type of B_3(C_n) for n>=6 is an external premise. Finite enumeration corroborates but is not the proof of the all-n statement.

Best-of-knowledge only; unindexed equivalent results remain possible.

PASS

An edge perturbation creating an odd triangle is natural in the robust-clique/total-cut setting, and the result provides a parameter-uniform, explicit simple-homotopy stability mechanism with exact cancellation count and a sharp n=6 contractible exception. It is stronger than a homology equality, a renaming or an arbitrary isolated census.
