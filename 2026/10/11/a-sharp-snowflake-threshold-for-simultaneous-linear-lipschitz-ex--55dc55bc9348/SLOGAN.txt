For a regular \(n\)-gon together with its center in the chord-distance snowflake metric, the optimal real-linear simultaneous Lipschitz extension is vertex averaging, with exact norm \(\max\{1,\frac1n\sum_{j=1}^{n-1}(2\sin(\pi j/n))^\alpha\}\). A norm-one linear extension exists precisely below a unique threshold \(\alpha_n\), and \(\sqrt n\,\alpha_n\to2\sqrt6/\pi\).
