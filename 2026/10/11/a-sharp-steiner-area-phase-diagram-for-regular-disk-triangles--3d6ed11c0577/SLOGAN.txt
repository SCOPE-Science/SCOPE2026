Within the exact regular disk-triangle family connecting the Reuleaux and equilateral triangles at fixed width, minimizing the area of a circular outer parallel body has three sharp regimes. Small offsets favor the limiting equilateral triangle, large offsets uniquely favor the Reuleaux triangle, and every intermediate offset between \(w/3\) and \(w(3+2\sqrt3-2\pi)/(2\pi-6)\) has a unique genuinely intermediate disk radius.
