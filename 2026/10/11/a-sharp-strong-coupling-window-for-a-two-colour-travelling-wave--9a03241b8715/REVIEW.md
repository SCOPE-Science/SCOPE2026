# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The claim concerns the explicitly defined nearest-neighbour map on every even cyclic lattice. The two-site symbolic orbit identity is exact for every coupling because each neighbour is the image of the local Chebyshev map. Differentiation, a two-sublattice Fourier transform, and the relation \(f'(u)f'(v)=-4\) yield all two-step multipliers and the exact spectral radius. The existence of the zero Fourier mode proves the maximum without an approximation. The inequalities and unique optimal coupling follow by factorization and convexity. The finite-size calculations pass the standalone 997-check script; the all-size proof is algebraic. The infinite \(\ell^2\) extension follows from a bounded Fourier multiplier and a quadratic differentiability bound. At neutral endpoints no nonlinear classification is claimed.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
The closest recent paper (Lippolis 2026, arXiv:2510.12532) gives spectral analysis for spatially **synchronized** orbits: it does not provide the explicit travelling checkerboard or its strong-coupling stability interval. The older Dettmann–Lippolis (2005) source considers a two-site family that includes the same coupling convention, but the inspected complete manuscript treats weak-coupling periodic-orbit numerics and cycle expansions, not this uniform all-even-ring Floquet law. The 2002 synchronized-state paper has only an abstract accessible for comparison; arXiv and publisher/open-access retrieval were attempted and full-text access remained unavailable. Its title and accessible description are not sufficient to rule out a discussion of related patterns, so this is a concrete residual risk rather than a novelty certificate. A separate earlier 2001 paper covers general reduced-matrix methods for certain coupled lattices without establishing this concrete exact result. Independent priority verification would be valuable.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The theorem identifies an entire *strong-coupling* stable travelling phase beyond the parameter at which the previously studied synchronized temporal two-cycle ceases to exist. The exact spectral cancellation gives a size-independent stability boundary and the unique coupling that minimizes the full Floquet spectral radius; these are motivated dynamical properties of coherent spatial patterns, not an arbitrary numerical slice. Both finite rings of every even size and the infinite square-summable perturbation setting are covered. No global attractor or generic-model claim is made.
