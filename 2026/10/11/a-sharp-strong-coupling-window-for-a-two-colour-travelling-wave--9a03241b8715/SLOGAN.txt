An exact two-colour travelling wave persists at every coupling of the nearest-neighbour quadratic Chebyshev lattice on any even ring. Its complete two-step Floquet spectral radius is \(4(1-a)^2+a^2\), making it locally attracting precisely for \(3/5<a<1\) in the strictly hyperbolic range, with optimally rapid asymptotic contraction at \(a=4/5\), independently of lattice size.
