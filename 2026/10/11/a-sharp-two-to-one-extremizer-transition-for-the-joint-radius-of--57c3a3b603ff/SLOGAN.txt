The balanced Euclidean joint radius of two rank-one orthogonal projections has an exact two-branch formula and a sharp symmetry-breaking transition at overlap \(c=1/2\): below it, exactly two maximizing complex rays compete; at or above it, the bisector ray alone maximizes. The angular splitting is asymptotic to \(2\sqrt{3(1/2-c)}\), while the gain over the bisector is \(3(1/2-c)^2\) to leading order.
