# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The single boundary kernel has unnormalized disk integral exactly 4. The pointwise triangle deficit for positive charges is nonnegative. Scaling \(z=\cos\delta+(\sin\delta)w\) maps poles to \(\pm i\) and factors the total deficit into \(|\sin\delta|\) times the same fixed integrand on a domain tending almost everywhere to the left half-plane. Near either pole the deficit is bounded by twice the other kernel, hence locally bounded; at infinity rationalization plus the \(O(|w|^{-1})\) relative phase gives \(O(|w|^{-3})\), integrable in two dimensions. Dominated convergence proves the two-sided cusp; a positive-area set with non-aligned nonzero summands proves positivity. Confocal elliptic coordinates correctly have Jacobian \((\cosh u-\cos v)(\cosh u+\cos v)\), yielding the positive integrand. Equal weights give the central-binomial identity by Tonelli and two explicit beta integrals, with summands \(O(m^{-5/2})\). Independent evaluation: 200,000-term series \(2.87108003758\), independent confocal midpoint integral \(2.87105782\) (quadrature approximation only).

## originality

PASS

Full primary papers on the same positive weighted Coulomb/Cauchy fields provide configuration-uniform lower bounds and global minimization, not the precise derivative at colliding positive boundary charges. The nearby-pole estimate in the weighted Chui paper bounds a *signed difference* of kernels by an upper order \(\delta+\delta\log(1/\delta)\), a different observable. Bezrukov gives a global equispaced minimum for equal-pole families and generalized functionals but no first-order coalescence coefficient. The local universal half-plane blow-up and the central-binomial series are additional exact data. Residual unindexed two-charge analyses and incomplete published-archive search are acknowledged.
