Two positive boundary charges approaching one another have a strict \(L^1\) Coulomb-energy cusp: their deficit from the coincident field is \(\mathcal C(\alpha,\beta)|\sin\delta|+o(|\sin\delta|)\), with an exact, finite, positive half-plane coefficient. For unit charges, that sharp coefficient is the central-binomial series \(\pi^2\sum_{m\ge1}b_m^2b_{m-1}/(2m-1)\approx2.87108004\).
