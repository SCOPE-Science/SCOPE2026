# Verification of the harmonic trace theorem

The central identity is checked analytically in four independent stages: the published diagonal interrupter proves unshifted positivity; the exact finite-compression identity equals the off-corner squared entry sum; Riemann-sum scaling with an \(O_m(R^{-1})\) tail gives an exact double integral; and a row-nuclear estimate makes every fixed real parameter shift a trace-class perturbation. Cyclic trace cancellation therefore preserves the trace. The external hyponormality classification then characterizes equality in the trace-norm inequality.

The exact evaluations for orders \(m=1,2,3,4\) are respectively \(1\), \(22/9\), \(411/100\), and \(1452/245\). They follow directly from rational harmonic sums and provide arithmetic spot checks, not proof for all orders.

No finite experiment is used to prove the infinite-dimensional conclusion; no external independent audit was performed. Positivity and its sharp parameter boundary rely on the published theorems cited in RESULT.md. The exact negative-shift Schatten-1 norm, fractional orders, and any generalization to different spaces are not established. The available literature comparison does not certify global uniqueness.
