# Verification

The focal source was checked for its definitions of the ordinary and minimal Shalit–Shamovich radii and for the exact all-powers statement in Theorem 3.1. The proof here uses that stronger statement before taking a countable direct sum.

For a completely bounded norming sequence normalized by \(\|X^{(r)}\|_{\mathcal E_1}=1\), each coordinate is uniformly bounded because coordinate functionals on a finite-dimensional operator space are completely bounded. Thus the direct-sum tuple is a genuine bounded operator tuple on a separable Hilbert space.

At every positive integer power, the defining word operator of the direct sum is block diagonal. Its norm is exactly the supremum of the block norms, which are \(\|X^{(r)}\|_{\mathcal E}^{m}\) by the focal theorem. Therefore both radii equal \(\sup_r\|X^{(r)}\|_{\mathcal E}\). This is \(1\) for the source structure and the full completely bounded norm for the target structure.
