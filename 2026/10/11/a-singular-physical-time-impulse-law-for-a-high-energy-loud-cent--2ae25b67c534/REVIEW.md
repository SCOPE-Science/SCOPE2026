# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The claim concerns exactly one Hamiltonian level family of a fixed reversible Loud center. The change \(q=-\log(1-x),\ p=-y\) preserves physical time and yields the strict-convexity energy \(H=p^2/2+q+e^{-q}-1\). Turning-point identities and the uniform positive-side passage-time inequality establish the normalized parabola, while the negative excursion occupies only \(O((\log E)/E)\) of the normalized period. The bounded momentum converges in \(L^1\), its distributional derivative converges, and the exactly zero mean of \(x\) bounds total variation by two. This reconstructs the singular signed-measure limit for every continuous test function and the limiting probability density. The separate numerical checker succeeds but establishes none of the limiting statements.

## Originality — PASS with priority risk

Marín–Villadelprat (arXiv:2203.12966v1, 2022) consider outer-boundary period-function criticality in this Loud family. Kouznetsov–Bisson–Li–Ueda (J. Phys. A, 2007, DOI 10.1088/1751-8113/40/9/016) treat the same undamped exponential-potential oscillator and provide exact energy integrals, asymptotic period scaling, and high-quality *approximations* to the phase waveform. Chouikha (2005) supplies period-function methods for Loud centers. The current claim is the exact full-phase limit together with the physically normalized signed-force impulse and time-sampling distribution. Those statements were not found in the inspected original results. Known quadratures and leading period laws have not been presented as original. A general hard-impact theorem or a transformed result elsewhere could imply this conclusion; a finite search does not rule that out.

## Value — PASS

The measurable polynomial force does not tend to its almost-everywhere background in an integrable sense: it has an exactly balanced negative pulse whose width vanishes and whose impulse is nonzero. This is a structural singular limit of an entire natural family of periodic orbits, not a numerical sample or an arbitrary parameter slice. The explicit universal phase waveform and time-sampling density expose how trajectories allocate original physical time near the hard wall, connecting the classical conservative oscillator description with its high-amplitude pulse geometry. This precise normalized impulse cannot be read off from period scaling alone and may be useful when comparing perturbations or measured cycle averages.

## Scope and risk

The theorem is restricted to the undamped parameter \((D,F)=(0,0)\), initial phase at the positive logarithmic turning point, and the invariant half-plane \(x<1\). It gives weak-* signed-measure convergence but no universal finite-energy error bound and makes no independent-priority or independent-validation claim. The exact energy quadrature, existence of periodic orbits, and high-energy period leading term are credited prior results.

Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
