At high energy, the reversible Loud center \(\dot x=-y(1-x),\ \dot y=x\) develops a universal parabolic logarithmic-coordinate waveform while its original-time force converges as a signed measure to \(ds-\delta_{1/2}\) over one normalized period. The limiting distribution of logarithmic energy share has density \(\mathbf1_{(0,1)}(u)/(2\sqrt{1-u})\), and the delta impulse has exact mass \(-1\).
