For \(L_t=\widehat{(\sigma_1\sigma_3\sigma_2^4)^t\sigma_1\sigma_3}\), the Kauffman brackets satisfy an explicit sixth-order Laurent-polynomial recurrence, and hence so do the Jones polynomials after the standard writhe normalization. The proof is the exact Temperley--Lieb state identity \(P_A(B)S=0\), not an extrapolation from finitely many examples.
