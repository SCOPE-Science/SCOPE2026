For weighted-shift numerical radius as a norm on \(\ell_\infty\), every nonvanishing sequence whose moduli converge to a positive limit \(b\) is a Fréchet-smooth point exactly when its numerical radius exceeds \(b\); at equality it is not even Gâteaux smooth. For \((t,1,1,\ldots)\), the exact smoothness transition occurs at \(t=\sqrt2\).
