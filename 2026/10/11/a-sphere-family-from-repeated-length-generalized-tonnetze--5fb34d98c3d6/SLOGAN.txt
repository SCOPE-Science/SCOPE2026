For every \(k\ge4\), the generalized Tonnetz with length vector \((1,\ldots,1,2)\) on \(k+1\) cyclic vertices is not a torus: it is exactly \(\partial\Delta^k\), hence a sphere \(S^{k-1}\). This gives an all-dimensional repeated-length degeneration extending the known triadic tetrahedron-boundary case.
