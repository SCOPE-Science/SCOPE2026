Every compact stationary Michelson state has the same exact \(x\)-RMS amplitude \(\sqrt2c\), so its mean and variance lie on \(\operatorname{Var}(x)=2c^2-m^2\). Any non-equilibrium stationary state must place positive mass on both sides of the equilibrium amplitude \(|x|=\sqrt2c\), and every nonconstant periodic orbit crosses that threshold at least twice per period.
