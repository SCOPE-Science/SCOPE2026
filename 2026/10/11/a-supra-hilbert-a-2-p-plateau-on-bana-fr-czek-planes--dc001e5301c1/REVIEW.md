# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The unit ball is the Euclidean disk cut by the strip \(|u|\le\lambda^{-1}\). The proof exhausts all four combinations of Euclidean and strip branches in the two numerator norms. The only nontrivial mixed branch is optimized exactly with the support function of that cut disk. The identity
\[
(\lambda^2-1)(\lambda-t)^2-(1-t^2)
=
\lambda^2-2+(-\lambda^2+\lambda t+1)^2
\]
certifies the needed support regime for every \(t\in[-1,1]\) when \(\lambda\ge\sqrt2\). The explicit pair \((\lambda^{-1},b),(-\lambda^{-1},b)\), \(b=\sqrt{1-\lambda^{-2}}\), attains the profile. An exact derivative calculation then forces the normalized maximum to \(\tau=1\) for every finite \(p\ge2\), and \(p=\infty\) follows directly.

## Originality
PASS. The defining generalized-Baronti paper was inspected at its reduction theorem and concrete examples; it contains no Banaś--Frączek example, and its main closed-form hypothesis of linear Day modulus fails here. Same-object searches returned Ptolemy and Euclidean-distance calculations, while the same-family 2016 paper treats James-type and von Neumann--Jordan constants. No checked statement implies the all-\(p\) plateau or the exact chord profile.

## Value
PASS. This is a full \(p\ge2\) parameter theorem on a classical named deformation, not an isolated numerical evaluation. It shows that every generalized Baronti constant from the Hilbert exponent to \(p=\infty\) collapses to the same value, and it identifies the geometric mechanism and extremizing pair.

## Closest literature and limitations
Banaś--Frączek provides the object; Baronti--Papini defines \(A_{2,p}\) and supplies the closest general formulas; Yang--Yang studies the same generalized max-norm family for different constants. The theorem is restricted to \(\lambda\ge\sqrt2\) and does not classify the lower-\(\lambda\) or generalized \(\ell_r\) regimes.
