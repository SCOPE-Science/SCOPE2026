For every \(\lambda\ge\sqrt2\), the generalized Baronti constants of the classical Banaś--Frączek plane are independent of \(p\) throughout \(2\le p\le\infty\): \(A_{2,p}=1+\sqrt{1-\lambda^{-2}}\). The plateau comes from an exact mixed Euclidean/strip chord profile whose normalized maximum is always attained at equal unit scales.
