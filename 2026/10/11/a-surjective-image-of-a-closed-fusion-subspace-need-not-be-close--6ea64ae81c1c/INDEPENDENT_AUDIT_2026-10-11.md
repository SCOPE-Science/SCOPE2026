# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The interlacing unitary identifies W⊂ℓ² with the graph of the closed unbounded diagonal D(x_n)=(n x_n), whose domain is dense and nonclosed: (1/n) lies in ℓ² but not dom D, and its finite truncations converge to it. U sends even basis vectors e_(2n) to e_n and annihilates odd vectors; UU*=I and U is surjective. It sends W precisely onto dom D, so UW is nonclosed despite W closed. Taking K=I, W1=W,W2=H, Λ1=π_W, Λ2=I, weights 1 gives the frame inequality ||f||²≤||π_W f||²+||f||²≤2||f||², and UK=KU. Thus all hypotheses of Huang--Yang Theorem 3.5(1) hold but UW1 violates the definition of a fusion subspace; the coisometric Corollary 3.6(1) is also affected. The quotient isomorphism H/N(U)→H gives the exact criterion UW closed iff W+N(U) closed. Replacing UW with its closure may repair the projection domain, but is not claimed to prove every other theorem convention.
