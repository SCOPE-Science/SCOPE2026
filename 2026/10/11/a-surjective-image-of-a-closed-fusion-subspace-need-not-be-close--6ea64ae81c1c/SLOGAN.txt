Surjectivity does not preserve closed fusion subspaces: a coisometry can send a closed graph subspace onto the nonclosed domain of an unbounded diagonal operator. Consequently Huang--Yang Theorem 3.5 can fail to define a \(K\)-\(g\)-fusion frame at all, and the exact closed-image boundary is \(UW\) closed if and only if \(W+N(U)\) is closed.
