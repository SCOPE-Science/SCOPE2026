# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Cauchy--Schwarz over the six distances and the exact zero-sum fourth-power identity give the universal upper bound \(8\sqrt6\); the explicit centered tetrahedral four-tuple attains it exactly.

## Originality
PASS. The defining 2021 article contains no \(\ell_4\) computation. The closest exact published result is planar and yields \(8(1+\sqrt2)\), so it does not imply the strictly larger three-coordinate value. Exact-symbol, alias and value searches located no covering result.

## Value
PASS. The result completely resolves the invariant on a canonical sequence space from dimension three onward, proves a genuine dimension jump, and identifies a natural nonplanar extremizer.

## Closest literature and limitations
Ahmad, Liu and Li introduced the invariant in *Symmetry* 13 (2021), article 1294. A later planar collapse theorem gives the exact \(\ell_4^2\) value. The present theorem is specific to exponent \(4\), does not classify all maximizers, and does not solve the general \(\ell_p\) case.
