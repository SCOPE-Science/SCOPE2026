For every integer \(n\ge7\), the scale-two Vietoris–Rips complex of the rectangular torus grid \(C_4\square C_n\) simplicially collapses onto a cyclic necklace of \(n\) three-spheres and has homotopy type \(S^1\vee\bigvee^nS^3\). The collapse has exactly \(16n\) elementary pairs and the full complex has face vector \((4n,22n,40n,27n,4n)\).
