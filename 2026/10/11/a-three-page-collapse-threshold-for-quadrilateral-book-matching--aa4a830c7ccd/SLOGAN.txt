For the quadrilateral book \(B_n\) formed by \(n\) four-cycles sharing one edge, the ordinary matching complex has a sharp three-page transition: \(S^0\) for \(n=1\), \(S^1\vee S^1\) for \(n=2\), and a simplicially collapsible complex for every \(n\ge 3\). The collapse follows because three pages always leave an unblocked middle edge whose star captures any matching.
