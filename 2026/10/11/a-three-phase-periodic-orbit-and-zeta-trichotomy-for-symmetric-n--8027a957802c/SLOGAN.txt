For the negative-doubling map with an open symmetric hole \((a,1-a)\), every survivor is eventually periodic throughout \(0<a\le2/5\), yet the exact rational dynamical zeta function changes twice: fixed points appear at \(a=1/3\) and an additional primitive four-cycle appears exactly at \(a=2/5\). All these systems have zero topological entropy, so the zeta jumps distinguish phases invisible to entropy.
