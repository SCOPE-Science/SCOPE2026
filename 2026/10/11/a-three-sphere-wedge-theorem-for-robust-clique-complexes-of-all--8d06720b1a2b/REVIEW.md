# Mathematical assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** For every finite connected bipartite graph, the bounded-independence complex at level three is at most three-dimensional and simply connected. Deleting leaves yields a contractible complex on a spanning tree. For each missing cross-part edge, the relative two-faces are precisely the triangles indexed by vertices adjacent to neither endpoint. The boundaries of newly admitted tetrahedra are signed incidence columns of old edges meeting that vertex set. Every connected component reaches a vertex outside the set because the old graph is connected, so paths give integral preimages of all relative two-generators. The resulting surjective relative differential preserves vanishing of integral second homology under every edge insertion. Hurewicz and Whitehead give a wedge of three-spheres; exact face counting gives its multiplicity as the binomial coefficient of cycle rank plus one, choose two, minus the number of four-cycles. The one-vertex and tree boundaries are covered.

**Originality — PASS (best of knowledge).** Filakovský's grid and square-sequence results cover proper graph subclasses. Kim–Lew establishes general bounded-independence collapsibility bounds; Carnero Bravo proves connected-graph simple connectivity and separate cycle, chordal and multipartite homotopy results. A previously published all-connected-bipartite equivariant Euler character supplies the exact alternating invariant but does not prove integral homology concentration. Searches and material reading did not identify a prior theorem establishing the unrestricted integral three-sphere wedge classification. An unindexed equivalent or stronger theorem remains possible; priority is not certified.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The classification resolves a natural infinite-family topological problem with no degree, girth, planarity or square-attachment restriction. The integer edge-insertion lemma explains why a universally determined three-dimensional homotopy type persists beyond the previously studied graph families. This is structural mathematical information, not merely a graph census or restatement of an Euler number.

**Verification and limits.** An independent bounded check verified chain-rank predictions over characteristics 2, 3 and 5 for 231 connected small bipartite graphs, 673 possible missing cross-edge insertions, and additional cycles and complete bipartite graphs. These examples do not replace the universal integral proof. No formal proof assistant certification or expert attestation is claimed. No result is asserted for nonbipartite graphs or robust parameter greater than three.
