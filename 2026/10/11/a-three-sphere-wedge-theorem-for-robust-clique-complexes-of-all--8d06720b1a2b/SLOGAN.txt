Every finite connected bipartite graph has a three-robust clique complex homotopy equivalent to a wedge of three-spheres, with precisely \(\binom{b+1}{2}-c_4(G)\) summands, where \(b\) is the cycle rank and \(c_4(G)\) counts four-cycles. A relative-chain edge-insertion argument proves the integral vanishing required to upgrade an Euler-characteristic identity to a homotopy classification.
