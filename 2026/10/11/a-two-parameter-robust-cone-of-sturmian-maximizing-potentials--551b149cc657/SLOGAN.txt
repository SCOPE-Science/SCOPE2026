Independently weighting the digit and distance defects in a Sturmian beta-shift produces an explicit open convex cone of potentials with the same nonperiodic maximizing measure. For \(p,q>0\), the potential \(p\Phi-q\Psi\) tolerates every perturbation with Lipschitz seminorm below \(\min\{p(1-\theta)^2,q/[1+2(1-\theta)^{-3}]\}\).
