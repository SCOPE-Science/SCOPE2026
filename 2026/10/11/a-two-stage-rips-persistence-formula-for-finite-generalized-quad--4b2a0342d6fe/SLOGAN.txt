For every finite thick generalized quadrangle of order \((s,t)\), the Rips complex of its point–line incidence graph collapses to a wedge of \((st)^2\) circles at scale two, then becomes a wedge of \((st)^4\) three-spheres at scale three, and is contractible at scale four. Its entire positive-dimensional barcode over any field is precisely \((st)^2\) degree-one intervals \([1,3)\) and \((st)^4\) degree-three intervals \([3,4)\).
