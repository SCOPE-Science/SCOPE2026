# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For every infinite set Γ (including noncountable Γ) over complex scalars, the isometric direct limit T of c0(Γ) even duals has canonical positive layer E=ℓ∞(Γ). The norm-one fiber lemma is exact: for a unital norm-one projection C(L)→π* C(K), evaluation on u gives a positive probability µ supported in π^-1(u). On a fiber with three points one point y has µ{y}≤1/3, and ||δ_y−µ||=2(1−µ{y})≥4/3. The decomposition C(βΓ)*=ℓ1(Γ)⊕1 M(U), U=βΓ\Γ, dualizes to the central C*-direct sum E**=ℓ∞(Γ)⊕∞ C(U)** with canonical JE(f)=(f,J_C(U)(f|U)). The induced spectrum surjection K⊔L_C(U)→K fixes K and maps second-spectrum fibers onto U. C(U) contains an isometric c0 via infinitely many pairwise disjoint nonempty clopen subsets of U, hence is nonreflexive; the second-spectrum map has a non-singleton fiber, which together with the fixed K point produces ≥3 points. Restricting a tower P to E** transfers the complement-norm lower bound. Compatible canonical adjoints (J_X(2n−1))* supply a norm-one retraction onto E, so the bounded class is nonempty. No finite test is used as infinite proof; the 4/3 is a lower bound and may not be sharp for the tower.

## originality

PASS

Qualified best-of-knowledge originality for the full quantitative tower-layer statement, not for the general bicontractive obstruction or projection construction. Liu–Uglešić Proposition 4.1 only prohibits the stronger M-projection and expressly says the weaker bicontractive property is unsettled. Friedman–Russo gives a powerful prior order-two-isometry classification of bicontractive projections on C*-algebras: combined with newly identifying a fiber of size at least three, it already implies qualitative non-bicontractivity; the qualitative mechanism is thus strongly anticipated. Neither inspected theorem supplies the uniform numerical bound ||I−P||≥4/3 for every norm-one tower-layer projection. The contribution that survives these logical implications is the explicit positive quantitative norm gap for the canonical c0(Γ) tower and the structural triple-fiber proof, for all infinite Γ. Priority remains qualified.

## value

PASS

The natural structural question singled out by Liu–Uglešić is whether a canonical positive bidual-tower layer can be both contractively and bicontractively complemented. The explicit 4/3 complementary operator-norm gap, valid for every infinite Γ, quantitatively separates contractive complementation from bicontractivity and therefore resolves the c0-family instance of that open distinction. The spectrum-fiber geometry and uniform cardinality independence explain a genuine Banach-space obstruction. The general existence of norm-one projections and qualitative involution theorem are not counted as novel independent contributions. The exact optimal gap for the full tower, higher positive layers, real analogues, and arbitrary X are left open.
