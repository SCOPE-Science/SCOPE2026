# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The prey ceilings follow from exact two-sided maxima on a compact invariant set. The predator conclusion follows from the exact logarithmic growth identity and a backward-time compactness contradiction. The predator-free invariant-measure statement follows from a strict Bendixson–Dulac sign, one-dimensional boundary dynamics, planar recurrence, and Poincare recurrence.

## Originality
PASS. The exact source gives boundedness, equilibrium conditions, local stability (including the predator-free condition (4.8)), and Hopf/chaos analysis, but not this uniform compact-recurrence exclusion theorem or the compact invariant-measure support conclusion. Searches by exact DOI, extinction/recurrent aliases, invariant-measure language, and the explicit threshold combination found no equivalent statement. The closest related papers concern different deterministic, stochastic, delayed, or lower-dimensional models.

## Value
PASS. A global parameter region excluding all predator-positive compact recurrence is directly relevant to a model whose motivating paper studies Hopf bifurcation and chaos. It separates a regime in which recurrent predator dynamics are mathematically impossible from regimes in which local bifurcation and chaos remain possible.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest statement in the target paper is equation (4.8), which makes the predator-free equilibrium locally stable when its predator invasion eigenvalue is negative. The present threshold is stronger in hypothesis but global in conclusion. It is sufficient rather than necessary and is not claimed to be an optimal extinction boundary. Related stochastic extinction thresholds do not cover this deterministic compact-invariant-set statement.
