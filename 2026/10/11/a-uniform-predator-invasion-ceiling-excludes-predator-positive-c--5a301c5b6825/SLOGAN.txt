For the weighted two-prey/one-predator fear–refuge system, a simple uniform invasion ceiling rules out every compact invariant set with positive predator density whenever \(C_1(1-m_1)M_1+C_2(1-m_2)M_2<C_3+q_3E_3\). In that regime, every compactly supported invariant probability measure is supported on predator-free equilibria.
