The inverse-power innovation map \(x\mapsto ax+b/x^p\), for \(p>1\), has exactly one positive primitive two-cycle if and only if \(0<a<(p-1)/(p+1)\), with an explicit cycle ratio. In the quadratic-inverse case its multiplier is \(9a^2+6a-2\), so the unique cycle changes from repelling to locally attracting at the sharp boundary \(a=(\sqrt2-1)/3\), independently of \(b\).
