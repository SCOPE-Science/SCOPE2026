Among all three-dimensional degree-two lattice zonotopes, the maximum Euclidean inradius is \(1/\sqrt2\), attained by exactly one Euclidean-congruence class: the rhombohedron with generator Gram matrix \(4I_3-J_3\). Every lattice-width-one member has radius at most \(1/2\); the exceptional maximizer has a unique insphere tangent to all six facets.
