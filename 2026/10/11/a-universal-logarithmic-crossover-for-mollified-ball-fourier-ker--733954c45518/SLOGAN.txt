At the Fourier-ball kernel's critical integrability index \(p_c=2d/(d+1)\), changing the exponent by \(-\lambda/\log(1/\delta)\) produces the universal sharp logarithmic crossover \(C_d\int_0^1e^{(d+1)\lambda t/2}\,dt\). The law is uniform in bounded \(\lambda\) and uniformly regularized normalized radial mollifiers in every fixed dimension \(d\ge2\), and concerns kernel norms rather than multiplier-operator norms.
