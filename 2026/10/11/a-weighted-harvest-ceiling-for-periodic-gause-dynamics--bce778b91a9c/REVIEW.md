PASS. Differentiating \(x+q(t)y\) with \(q=s/p\) cancels the two Holling-III interaction terms exactly and leaves the stated production, harvest, competition, mortality, and \(-\dot q\) contribution with the correct signs. Periodicity makes the mean derivative zero. Under \(0<m<K\), the cubic prey-production term has its unique positive maximum in \((m,K)\) at the stated \(u_*\). Strict positivity of \(qb y^2\) yields the strict harvest ceiling, and coefficient minima plus Jensen's inequality yield the predator-mean bound. The packaged symbolic replay checks the nontrivial algebraic identities.

## Originality

PASS. The motivating Yu--Liu--Lin full text was inspected through the model definition and the coincidence-degree existence proof; it treats the interaction and harvest terms separately and does not state this weighted cancellation or its nonexistence consequence. The closest structural predecessor inspected in full, Etoua--Rousseau, is autonomous, harvests only prey, has no Allee factor, and develops a bifurcation/MSY analysis rather than the periodic weighted budget. Indexed searches by model name, coefficient ratio, harvest ceiling, varying conversion efficiency, and equivalent balance-law language returned only different dynamical systems. The claim is therefore limited to the exact source-specific identity and consequences, without asserting an exhaustive literature census.

Residual risk: related food-chain energy balances may occur under different notation in older ecological literature. This does not affect correctness, but it limits the breadth of the originality claim.
