# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The example satisfies the theorem's printed assumptions:
\[
R(U)=R(K)
\]
and
\[
\|U^*f\|=\|K^*f\|
\]
for every \(f\). The original one-component family is Parseval relative to \(K\), but
\[
KU^*=0,
\]
so the transformed family is zero and cannot have a positive lower \(K\)-\(g\)-fusion bound.

The proof obstruction is exact: \((K^\dagger)^*K^*\) is the orthogonal projection onto \(R(K)\), and the source has not shown that \(U^*f\) lies in that range.

## Originality
PASS. The exact theorem, transformed-family aliases, Moore--Penrose formulation, lower-comparison formulation, and positive-operator formulation were searched and compared.

The closest indexed results concern range compression, perturbation, scaling, woven reconstruction, or operator-orbit independence. A later woven theorem uses \(R(U^*)\subseteq R(K)\), which supports the repair but does not state the counterexample or the exact correction of the 2020 theorem.

## Value
PASS. The source claims an iff construction theorem. The counterexample satisfies its quantitative condition with equality and nevertheless sends a Parseval input to the zero transformed family. The exact replacement
\[
US_\Lambda U^*\ge cKK^*
\]
gives a complete criterion rather than a merely qualitative objection.

## Limitations
The finding concerns Theorem 4 only. The adjoint-range hypothesis is a simple sufficient repair of the published proof, while the operator inequality is the exact criterion. A nonindexed correction remains possible.
