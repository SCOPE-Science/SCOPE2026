A Parseval frame for \(End_{\mathcal A}^*(\mathcal H,\mathcal K)\) need not be a \(g\)-frame for \(\mathcal H\): over \(\mathcal A=\mathbb C\oplus\mathbb C\), a coefficient module supported on one central summand cannot see the other. The missing converse hypothesis is adjoint reachability, which holds in particular when \(\mathcal K\) contains a unit vector.
