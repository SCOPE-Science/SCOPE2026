The verification uses the mixed operator \(R=T_CU^*\). From \(\|I-RA\|<1\), the Neumann lemma gives invertibility of \(RA\); multiplying by the inverse of \(A\) gives invertibility of \(R\). Conversely, \(R^-1\) makes the defect exactly zero.

For the scalar witness \(H=\mathbb C\), \(C=I\), \(f_1=1\), \(g_1=3\), the mixed operator is \(R=3I\). With \(A=I/3\), the adjustable defect is zero. The ordinary approximate-dual defect is \(\|I-R\|=2\), so the strict bound fails. The Neumann factor is \(I-R^*=-2I\), so the terms do not tend to zero and the displayed series diverges.
