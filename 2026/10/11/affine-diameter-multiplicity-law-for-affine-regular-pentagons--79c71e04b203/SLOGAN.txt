For a uniformly chosen point in any affine-regular pentagon, the number of affine diameters through that point is almost surely \(1\), \(3\), or \(5\), with exact probabilities \(5-2\sqrt5\), \((7\sqrt5-15)/2\), and \((7-3\sqrt5)/2\). The fivefold region is exactly the central pentagon of the pentagram.
