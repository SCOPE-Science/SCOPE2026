For the radius-one Lee cross \(\{0,\pm e_j\}\subset\mathbb F_p^{(p-1)/2}\), every Fourier spectrum is an affine line for each \(p\in\{5,7,11,13\}\); the exact numbers of spectra are \(10,392,5622144,1425765120\). At \(p=13\), four pairwise orthogonal characters can nevertheless be noncollinear, making the full-basis rigidity genuinely global.
