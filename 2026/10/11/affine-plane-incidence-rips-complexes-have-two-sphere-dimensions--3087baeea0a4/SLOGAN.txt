The graph-metric Vietoris–Rips complex of every finite affine-plane incidence graph of order \(q\ge3\) is, at scale two, a wedge of \((q-1)^2(q+1)\) two-spheres and \((q-1)^{q+1}\) \(q\)-spheres. The second sphere dimension comes from the affine parallel classes and disappears for projective planes; at order three the Hesse configuration produces sixteen spheres in each of dimensions two and three.
