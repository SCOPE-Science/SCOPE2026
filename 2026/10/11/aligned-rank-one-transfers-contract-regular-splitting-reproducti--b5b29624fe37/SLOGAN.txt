For a regular rank-one splitting, an admissible common-row transfer obeys the exact law \(R_t-1=(R_0-1)/(1+ts)\), so it moves the reproduction number toward \(1\) without crossing the threshold. The two reproduction numbers in Johnston--Cupons Example 3.12 satisfy the concrete contraction \(R_a-1=((C+D)/(C+2D))(R_c-1)\).
