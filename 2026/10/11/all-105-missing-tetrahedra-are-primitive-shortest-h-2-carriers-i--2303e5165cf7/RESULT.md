# All 105 missing tetrahedra are primitive shortest \(H_2\)-carriers in the minimal twelve-vertex \(S^3\times S^2\)
## Finding
Let \(K\) be Lutz's twelve-vertex triangulation \((S^3\times S^2)^a_{12}\), with face vector
\[
(12,66,220,390,336,112).
\]
The source identifies \(K\) as a vertex-minimal triangulation of \(S^3\times S^2\), so
\[
H_2(K;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z.
\]

Among integral simplicial \(2\)-cycles representing nonzero homology classes, the minimum vertex-support size is \(4\). Exactly \(105\) four-vertex supports attain the minimum: the four-sets that are not tetrahedra. Since all \(\binom{12}{3}=220\) triangles are present, each missing four-set carries the full tetrahedral boundary.

Every one of these \(105\) tetrahedral spheres represents a primitive generator \(\pm g\) of \(H_2(K;\mathbb Z)\). With all triangles and four-sets increasingly oriented, the explicit integral cocycle used in the verification pairs to \(+1\) with \(57\) of the carriers and to \(-1\) with \(48\).

The minimum-support family is vertex-regular: each of the twelve vertices belongs to exactly \(35\) of the \(105\) carriers.

## Assumptions and scope
The complex is the labelled 112-facet triangulation printed by Lutz and independently reproduced by Datta. Vertices \(a,b,c\) denote \(10,11,12\). A missing tetrahedron means a four-vertex set whose four triangular faces are present but whose tetrahedron is absent.

Support size counts vertices occurring with nonzero coefficient in a simplicial chain. The claim classifies minimum supports of nonzero integral degree-two classes; it does not classify all larger-support representatives or the other known twelve-vertex triangulations of \(S^3\times S^2\).

## Proof
The facet list reconstructs the exact face vector
\[
(12,66,220,390,336,112).
\]
In particular, all \(\binom{12}{2}=66\) edges and all \(\binom{12}{3}=220\) triangles occur. Hence the complex is three-neighborly.

There are
\[
\binom{12}{4}=495
\]
four-vertex sets and exactly \(390\) tetrahedra. Thus exactly
\[
495-390=105
\]
four-sets are missing tetrahedra. On fewer than four vertices there is no nonzero simplicial \(2\)-cycle. On exactly four vertices, the integral cycle group supported on the four possible triangles is generated by the alternating tetrahedral boundary. Therefore the missing tetrahedra are the only possible four-vertex supports of nonzero homology classes.

To prove primitivity, define an integral two-cochain \(w\) on increasingly oriented triangles. Give coefficient \(+1\) to
\[
146,147,14b,156,28b,369,389,38b,468,469,46a,478,568,569,56a,56b,578,789,78a,78b,
\]
coefficient \(-1\) to
\[
124,134,248,249,24a,258,25a,279,27a,348,34a,358,35a,679,67a,
\]
and coefficient zero to every other triangle.

The verifier checks the cocycle condition directly on all \(390\) tetrahedra: \(w(\partial \sigma)=0\) for every tetrahedron \(\sigma\). It then evaluates \(w\) on every one of the \(105\) missing tetrahedral boundaries and obtains only \(\pm1\), with multiplicities \(57\) and \(48\). Hence no carrier can represent \(m g\) with \(|m|>1\), because every integral cocycle would then pair with it by a multiple of \(m\). Each missing tetrahedral boundary is therefore primitive.

As an independent mod-two check, exact boundary ranks are
\[
\operatorname{rank}(\partial_2)=55,\qquad
\operatorname{rank}(\partial_3)=164,
\]
so
\[
\dim_{\mathbf F_2}H_2(K;\mathbf F_2)=220-55-164=1.
\]
All \(105\) missing tetrahedral boundaries reduce to that unique nonzero class, while all \(390\) tetrahedral boundaries reduce to zero.

Finally, direct incidence counting gives \(35\) minimum carriers through every vertex. Since \(105\cdot4/12=35\), this verifies exact vertex-regularity of the complete carrier family.

## Verification
The standalone `verify.py` contains the complete 112-facet presentation and uses only the Python standard library. It reconstructs every face, checks the full face vector and three-neighborliness, computes the mod-two boundary ranks by exact bit-vector elimination, enumerates all 495 four-sets, checks every missing tetrahedron, verifies the integral cocycle on all tetrahedra, evaluates all 105 primitive pairings, and recomputes the carrier incidence at every vertex.

Its exact successful output is:

`VERIFY_OK f=12,66,220,390,336,112 H2_F2_dim=1 missing4=105 all_missing4_mod2_nonzero=yes integral_cocycle_support=35 primitive_pairings=+1:57,-1:48 carrier_vertex_degree=35`

The integral conclusion does not rely on a finite-field experiment: primitivity follows from the explicit integral cocycle pairings \(\pm1\). The published topology is used only to identify the ambient rank-one group as \(H_2(K;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z\).

## Relationship to prior work
Lutz gives the exact 112-facet complex, proves that it triangulates \(S^3\times S^2\), records its face vector, and proves vertex minimality by the Brehm--Kühnel bound. Datta later republishes the same labelled complex as a vertex-minimal example. Lutz also notes that this particular twelve-vertex triangulation has no nontrivial simplicial symmetries.

The inspected sources do not state that all \(105\) missing tetrahedral spheres are primitive integral generators, nor do they provide the complete minimum-support carrier census or the \(57/48\) integral pairing split. Focused searches using the exact object, missing or empty tetrahedra, primitive generators, and shortest \(H_2\)-support formulations did not locate a covering indexed statement.

## Limitations
The result is specific to \((S^3\times S^2)^a_{12}\). Lutz records at least twenty-five combinatorially distinct minimal twelve-vertex triangulations of \(S^3\times S^2\), and the present computation does not assert the same carrier census for the others.

The \(57/48\) sign split uses the displayed labeling, increasing simplex orientations, and the particular integral cocycle above; replacing the cocycle by its negative swaps the two numbers. The basis-free claim is primitivity of all \(105\) carriers.
