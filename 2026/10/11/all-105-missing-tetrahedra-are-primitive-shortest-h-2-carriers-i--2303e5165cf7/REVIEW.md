## Correctness
PASS. The exact 112-facet presentation reconstructs \(f=(12,66,220,390,336,112)\), so the complex contains every triangle and has exactly \(105\) missing tetrahedra. Four vertices are the smallest possible support of a nonzero two-cycle. An explicit integral two-cocycle is checked on all \(390\) tetrahedra and pairs by \(\pm1\) with every one of the \(105\) missing tetrahedral boundaries, proving each carrier is primitive. Independent mod-two ranks give one-dimensional \(H_2\) and place all \(105\) carriers in its unique nonzero class.

The inspected primary full text gives the facet list and minimality theorem but not the \(105\)-carrier primitive integral census. ## Value
PASS. This is the complete shortest-support layer of the rank-one middle homology in a vertex-minimal five-manifold triangulation. The fact that every missing tetrahedral sphere is not merely nonzero but primitive is an inclusion-sensitive integral statement, and the explicit cocycle makes the result reusable without requiring a large Smith-normal-form computation.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest exact-object literature is Lutz's Theorem 44 and Datta's Example 3.14. They establish the triangulation and its minimality. Lutz also records that the complex is asymmetric. The new claim is limited to minimum integral \(H_2\)-support and does not compare the other known twelve-vertex \(S^3\times S^2\) triangulations.
