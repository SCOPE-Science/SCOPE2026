For every Euclidean orthotope in dimension \(d\ge3\), the area and perimeter of the **same random planar shadow** have an exact gamma-function mixed moment and strictly positive covariance. The sharp bound \(\operatorname{Cov}(A,P)\ge c_d e_3\) has equality precisely for a cube, and \(c_d\sim\frac32\sqrt{2/\pi}(16-5\pi)d^{-5/2}\).
