# All empty triangles are integral one-systoles in Lutz's 14-vertex \(L(4,1)\)
## Finding
Let \(K\) be the 14-vertex triangulation of the lens space \(L(4,1)\) whose 70 tetrahedra are listed in `facets.txt`. Then
\[
H_1(K;\mathbb Z)\cong \mathbb Z/4.
\]
Exactly 143 triples of vertices span all three edges of \(K\) but do not span a triangular face. Every boundary of one of these empty triangles is nonzero in integral homology. Of the 143 classes, 105 have order four and 38 have order two. Hence the minimum edge length of a simple cycle representing a nonzero integral first-homology class is 3, and these 143 empty-triangle boundaries are exactly all minimizers.

## Assumptions and scope
The object is the asymmetric 14-vertex \(L(4,1)\) triangulation printed by Frank H. Lutz, with face vector \(f=(14,84,140,70)\). The statement concerns simple cycles in the one-skeleton and integral simplicial homology. A simple cycle has length equal to its number of edges. The order-four/order-two split is invariant; a choice of orientation and generator only exchanges the two primitive residues.

The earliest inspected source containing the exact facet list is Lutz's 1999 dissertation. Its inspected bibliographic evidence resolves only the year. The first inspected day-resolved public source for the same 14-vertex \(L(4,1)\) triangulation is the preprint `arXiv:math/0311116`, submitted on 2003-11-07; that is the date recorded in the structured metadata rather than inventing a day in 1999.

## Proof
Build the one-skeleton from the 70 tetrahedra and choose the deterministic breadth-first spanning tree used by `verify.py`. Since the graph has 84 edges on 14 vertices, its integral cycle lattice has rank \(84-14+1=71\). Contracting the spanning tree identifies this cycle lattice with \(\mathbb Z^71\), using the 71 non-tree edges as coordinates.

For every one of the 140 triangular faces, express its oriented boundary in these 71 chord coordinates. This gives a \(71\times140\) integral relation matrix \(R\). Exact rational elimination finds 71 independent columns. The determinant of the resulting \(71\times71\) integer minor is \(4\). Therefore the subgroup generated by all triangular boundaries has full rank and index dividing 4 in the cycle lattice, so \(H_1(K;\mathbb Z)\) is finite of order at most 4.

Independently, the verifier solves the cocycle equations modulo 2 and lifts the unique nonzero mod-two solution to a \(\mathbb Z/4\)-valued 1-cocycle. This lifted cocycle vanishes on all 140 triangular boundaries and takes an odd value on a cycle, so it induces a surjection
\[
H_1(K;\mathbb Z)\twoheadrightarrow \mathbb Z/4.
\]
The upper and lower bounds force \(H_1(K;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z/4\).

There are 143 vertex triples for which all three edges exist but the triangle is absent. Exhaustive evaluation of their oriented boundaries under the cocycle gives 38 residues equal to 2 and 105 odd residues. Because the cocycle is an isomorphism on the order-four homology group, the former are exactly the order-two class and the latter are exactly the primitive order-four classes. No empty triangle has residue 0.

Finally, a simple three-edge cycle in a simple graph is exactly a three-vertex clique. The verifier finds 283 such cliques: 140 are triangular faces and hence bound simplices, while the remaining 143 are precisely the empty triangles just shown to be homologically nonzero. Simple cycles of length 1 or 2 do not exist. This proves both the systolic value and the complete classification of its minimizers.

## Relationship to prior work
Lutz's dissertation gives the exact 70 tetrahedra, the face vector, asymmetry, and the homology of the underlying \(L(4,1)\). The 2003 geometric-manifolds preprint gives \(L(4,1)\) the same smallest-known face vector \(f=(14,84,140,70)\) in its lens-space table. The later 2009 \(f\)-vector paper again records the same smallest-known face vector while studying face-number bounds. In the inspected relevant portions, none states the number of empty triangles, proves that every empty-triangle boundary is homologically essential, or gives the 105/38 order spectrum.

Searches also used the equivalent formulations “missing triangle,” “induced three-cycle,” “simple integral homology one-systole,” and numerical formulations involving 143, 105, and 38. No published statement located in those searches implies the claimed complete spectrum.

## Limitations
This is an exact statement about one labeled 14-vertex triangulation, not a theorem about all triangulations of \(L(4,1)\) and not a claim that 14 vertices are globally minimal. The finite census is exhaustive for the supplied complex, but it does not classify longer representatives or optimize geometric lengths under a metric. An unindexed historical computation could still contain the same finite census; the inspected exact-object sources do not.
