For the cubic graph \(\{(t,t^3):t\in\mathbb Z/p^2\mathbb Z\}\), every orthogonal exponential basis is determined by one of \((p+1)/2\) permitted reduction slopes and an independently chosen horizontal or allowed vertical lift line over each residue point. The complete family has exactly \(\frac{p(p+1)}2[p(1+\mathbf1_{p\equiv2\pmod3})]^p\) distinct frequency spectra.
