In Lutz's seventeen-vertex flat manifold \(\mathcal G_6\), every one of the \(305\) empty triangles is an integral homology \(1\)-systole, and all \(15\) nonzero classes of \(H_1\cong(\mathbb Z/4)^2\) already occur at length \(3\). Up to orientation, the six order-\(4\) inverse-pair classes occur \(52,51,51,48,45,38\) times, while the three nonzero order-\(2\) classes occur \(13,5,2\) times.
