For every \(m,n\ge2\) and every \(1\le p,q\le\infty\), the injective tensor product \(\ell_p^m\widehat\otimes_\varepsilon\ell_q^n\) has maximal von Neumann--Jordan constant \(2\). In the reciprocal-sum region above one, the extremal two-by-two witness is the normalized identity/swap pair, whose sum and difference are rank-one operators of exactly twice the norm.
