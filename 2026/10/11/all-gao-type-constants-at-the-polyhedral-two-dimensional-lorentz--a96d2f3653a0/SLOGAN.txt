For the polyhedral Lorentz endpoint \(N_\omega(s,t)=\max\{|s|,|t|\}+\omega\min\{|s|,|t|\}\), every finite-exponent Gao-type constant has a closed form, with a phase transition at the same parameter \(\omega=\sqrt2-1\) for all \(p>1\). This resolves the \(r=1\) endpoint of the Lorentz range left open for generalized Gao-type constants.
