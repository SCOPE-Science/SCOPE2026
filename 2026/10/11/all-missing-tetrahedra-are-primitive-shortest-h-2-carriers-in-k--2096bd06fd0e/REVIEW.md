# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The exact facet presentation gives \((9,36,84,90,36)\), so every three-set is a triangle and exactly \(36\) four-sets are missing tetrahedra. A nonzero integral two-cycle cannot use fewer than four vertices. On any four-set, the local integral cycle group is generated by its tetrahedral boundary; for present tetrahedra this is a global boundary, while for all \(36\) missing tetrahedra an explicit integral cocycle evaluates \(\pm1\). Hence exactly those \(36\) supports carry nonzero four-vertex classes and every such class is primitive. Exhaustion of all \(9!\) relabelings gives \(54\) automorphisms, carrier orbits \(9+27\), and a balanced \(27+27\) sign action on rank-one integral middle homology. Mod-two boundary ranks independently check that every missing tetrahedral boundary is nonzero.

## Originality
PASS, with explicit residual risk. published-finding corpus searches covered exact-object, missing-tetrahedron, shortest-support, primitive-generator, automorphism-orbit, and integral-sign formulations. The closest indexed finding proves that proper induced nonfaces collapse to tetrahedral spheres, but that is an intrinsic simple-homotopy statement and does not imply the inclusion class of those spheres in ambient integral homology. Bagchi--Datta's accessible abstract states complementarity and full-automorphism structure, not the primitive carrier theorem or the induced sign character. The older literature was not all fully accessible, so unpublished tables or inaccessible passages remain a stated risk.

## Value
PASS. The minimal nine-vertex complex is a canonical extremal triangulation of \(\mathbb{CP}^2\), whose middle homology is its defining nontrivial integral invariant. The result identifies the complete shortest layer of actual integral generators and shows that every combinatorial missing tetrahedron is a primitive projective-line-class carrier up to sign. The symmetry calculation then determines how the full combinatorial symmetry sees orientation of that generator, linking complementarity, homology, and automorphism structure.

## Closest literature and limitations
Kühnel--Banchoff introduce the complex; Bagchi--Datta prove uniqueness/complementarity and explicate the automorphism group; Köhler--Lutz give vertex-transitive classification context. The closest indexed result on the same exact complex gives collapses of proper nonfaces to tetrahedral spheres. The new statement is limited to ambient integral middle-homology classes, their minimum four-vertex supports, and the induced sign action.
