In Kühnel's nine-vertex \(\mathbb{CP}^2\), the \(36\) missing tetrahedra are exactly the minimum four-vertex carriers of nonzero integral middle homology, and every one represents a primitive generator up to sign. The \(54\)-element simplicial automorphism group has carrier orbits of sizes \(9\) and \(27\) and acts on \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z\) through a surjective sign character with kernel of order \(27\).
