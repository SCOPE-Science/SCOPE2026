# Review of All positive \(\tau\) are admissible in the fractional half-space Hardy–Maz’ya remainders

## Correctness
PASS. The source remainder weight factors exactly as \(x_d^{-\beta}(x_d/R)^\tau\) with \(R=(x_{d-1}^2+x_d^2)^{1/2}\) and \(0<x_d/R\le1\). Hence every remainder inequality valid at \(\sigma>0\) remains valid, with the same positive constant, at every \(\tau\ge\sigma\). The source supplies a nonempty interval of positive \(\sigma\) for both its Gagliardo and Sobolev--Bregman theorems, so choosing a smaller source-admissible \(\sigma<\tau\) proves the asserted all-positive-\(\tau\) extension. The monotonicity of the optimal constants follows from the same order relation.

## Originality
PASS. The focal paper was inspected at Theorems 1 and 2 and at the paragraph in which the authors explicitly state that they could not determine whether their \(\tau\)-range assumptions are optimal. The paper does not state the pointwise parameter monotonicity, the all-positive-\(\tau\) extension, or monotonicity of the best remainder constants. Targeted source-ID, equivalent-formulation, broader-coverage, and implication searches found no published fractional theorem covering these statements. The strongest residual risk is that the one-line weight comparison may have been observed informally or implicitly elsewhere without being indexed.

## Value
PASS. This is a complete classification of the admissible exponent range for the exact remainder family in a current theorem: the admissible set is all \((0,\infty)\), not merely the sufficient intervals displayed in the source. It directly resolves the source's explicit range-optimality question and applies simultaneously to both main nonlocal remainder inequalities. The contribution is structural rather than a numerical tweak: it identifies an order relation that makes every upper exponent restriction a proof artifact.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest literature is arXiv:2609.10832v1 itself, which proves the two inequalities only on restricted seed intervals and explicitly leaves range optimality unresolved. Tidblom's classical half-space Hardy--Maz’ya inequality treats the analogous local remainder, while Frank--Seiringer provide the sharp fractional Hardy term without this codimension-two remainder. None of these inspected statements implies the fractional all-positive-\(\tau\) theorem as stated. The sharp numerical remainder constants remain open, and no uniform positive lower bound in \(\tau\) is claimed.
