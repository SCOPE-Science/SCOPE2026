In the Köhler--Lutz twelve-vertex triangulation of \(S^3\widetilde{\times}S^1\), every one of the \(76\) empty triangles is a primitive integral homology \(1\)-systole. The full \(96\)-element simplicial symmetry group splits them into exactly three orbits of sizes \(12\), \(48\), and \(16\), and realizes both signs on \(H_1\cong\mathbb Z\).
