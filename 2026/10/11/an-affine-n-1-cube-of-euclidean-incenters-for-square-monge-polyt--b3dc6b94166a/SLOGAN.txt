The Euclidean inradius of the normalized nonsymmetric \(n\times n\) Monge polytope is exactly \(\bigl[n^2\sqrt{1-n^{-2}}+n^2(n^2-1)/6\bigr]^{-1}\) for every \(n\ge2\). Its entire set of optimal ball centers is an affine \((n-1)\)-cube, with every adjacent Monge facet tangent and a precisely classified additional set of tangent coordinate facets.
