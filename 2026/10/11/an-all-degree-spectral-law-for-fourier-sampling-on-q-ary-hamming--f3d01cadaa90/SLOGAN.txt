The complete rational spectrum of a Fourier band of every interior interaction degree on a nonbinary Hamming sphere is given by explicit harmonic Krawtchouk-coefficient sums. In particular, the rank is \(\binom nd(q-1)^d\), and the entire kernel is generated by the single shell equation \((\sum_i\mathbf1_{\{x_i\ne0\}}-k)\mathcal H_{\le d-1}\).
