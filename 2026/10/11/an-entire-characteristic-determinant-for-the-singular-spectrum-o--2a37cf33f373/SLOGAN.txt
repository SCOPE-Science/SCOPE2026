The complete nonzero singular spectrum of the Hardy–Volterra commutator \(MC-CM=CJ\) on real \(L^2(0,1)\) is characterized exactly by the positive zeros of a concrete entire \(2\times2\) boundary determinant built from convergent hypergeometric-type series. The construction resolves the specific spectral equation beyond the previously known order \(\sigma_n\asymp n^{-2}\), without claiming elementary formulas for individual roots.
