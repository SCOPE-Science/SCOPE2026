For the twelve-vertex Chvátal graph, the independence complex collapses onto its ordinary open-neighborhood complex by a *single free dihedral orbit* of eight elementary simplex pairs. Both spaces have homotopy type \(S^2\); the neighborhood sphere makes Lovász's four-chromatic lower bound sharp, and the full dihedral group acts on top integral homology with character \(\deg(r^a s^b)=(-1)^{a+b}\).
