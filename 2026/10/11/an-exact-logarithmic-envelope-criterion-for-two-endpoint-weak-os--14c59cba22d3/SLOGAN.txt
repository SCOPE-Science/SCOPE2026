For a broad, regular monotone class of compositions of the canonical one-sided logarithmic cusp, the exceptional \(\gamma=-1\) BSVY weak test and the \(p=1\) ball mean-oscillation weak test are finite **exactly when** the nonlinear profile \(\Phi(s)\) is at most linear near zero. Logarithm-plus-loglog profiles have the sharp common boundary \(\alpha>1\) or \(\alpha=1,\ \beta\ge0\).
