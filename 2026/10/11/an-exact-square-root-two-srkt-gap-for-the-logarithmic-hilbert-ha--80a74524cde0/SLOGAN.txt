The canonical unbounded BMOA symbol \(\psi(z)=-\log(1-z)\) generates the Hilbert Hankel matrix, whose operator norm is \(\pi\) but whose exact reproducing-kernel testing/Garsia norm is only \(\pi/\sqrt2\). This gives an explicit sharp-for-the-symbol square-root-two separation and a quantitative negative benchmark for the unbounded strong reproducing kernel thesis question.
