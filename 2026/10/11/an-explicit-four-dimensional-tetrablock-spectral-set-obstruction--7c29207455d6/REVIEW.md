# Scientific review: explicit tetrablock spectral-set obstruction

## Correctness
The commuting 4×4 tuple is jointly square-zero. Using the published scalar derivative-ball theorem, the chosen normalized map from ℓ¹₂ ⊕∞ ℂ to M₂ has norm exactly one, giving the scalar spectral-set bound by a disk Möbius transformation and block Schur complement. The displayed matrix rational function is holomorphic on a neighborhood of the closed tetrablock. Its explicit positive defect on the distinguished boundary gives a uniform norm at most one, attained by boundary unitaries. Tensor amplification has eigenvalues 1+ρ, 1−ρ, ρ, −ρ, yielding exact norm (1+ρ)/φ. For scalar off-diagonal Parrott blocks every contractive functional is completely contractive, so the block-minimality statement is restricted to the specified centered equal-square-block family. The finite checker is diagnostic, not the basis for the universal inequalities.

## Originality
Mandal and Ray, arXiv:2609.37698v1, establish the complete cotangent operator-space identification, the general rational interpolation formula, and existential finite-dimensional failure. All these broader results are credited as prior. They do not display this concrete 4×4 coefficient tuple, the chosen M₂ witness or its exact golden-ratio amplification threshold. Earlier tetrablock work of Pal and Ball–Sau addresses different constructions and dilation criteria; Bhowmik's 2026 2×2 examples admit dilations. The present claim is a narrow concrete realization rather than discovery of general non-dilation, and an unindexed older equivalent construction remains a priority risk.

## Scientific value
An explicit four-dimensional matrix triple together with a two-dimensional rational matrix certificate gives a small reproducible benchmark for the previously existential rational-dilation failure. The exact sharp ρ transition and minimal block size within the centered Parrott template are mathematically motivated concrete boundary facts. The calculation is elementary once the derivative geometry and rational interpolation theorem are available; it does not claim a global minimum dimension or general new dilation principle.
