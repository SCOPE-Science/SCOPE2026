# Mathematical review: explicit tetrablock spectral-set obstruction

## Correctness (C): PASS
The full claim concerns one explicit \(4\times4\) commuting tuple, one explicitly parameterized \(2\times2\) rational function, and minimality restricted to equal-block Parrott form. The exact scalar bound \(\|u\|=1\) is proved via the singular values of a \(2\times2\) endpoint matrix, combined with the established tetrablock derivative ball [1, Proposition 2.1]. Scalar contraction follows by a disk Möbius transform and a block Schur complement. The rational function is holomorphic near the closed tetrablock; the Shilov boundary formula and a positive contraction-defect identity prove its uniform norm is exactly one. The amplified block has four explicitly computed real eigenvalues and norm \((1+\rho)/\varphi\). The one-dimensional-block restriction follows from complete boundedness of scalar functionals on operator spaces. The exact rational checker supplements but does not replace these proofs.

## Originality (O): PASS with specified literature risk
Mandal--Ray [1] establish the derivative structure, rational interpolation template, and an existential finite-dimensional failure. Their printed Corollary 3.3 leaves both the matrix dimension and coefficients unspecified, and gives no concrete rational witness or exact norm gap. This finding supplies a size-two matrix map, the smallest Parrott blocks, an explicit degree-two rational matrix test, and the exact \(\rho\)-threshold. Pal [3] uses an infinite-dimensional shift model, and Ball--Sau [4] explain why an earlier purported necessary condition is not necessary. The original tensor amplification and algebraic scalar normalization were checked against aliases and possible stronger prior statements. The theoretical possibility of a pre-existing unindexed explicit version is a documented residual risk. An absence of search hits is not treated as proof of novelty.

## Value (V): PASS
The direct construction replaces a nonconstructive existence result in a spectral-set/dilation problem with a four-dimensional finite witness and two-dimensional rational certificate. It yields exactly computable amplification and a sharp phase threshold for the test parameter. The dimension statement is carefully restricted to the natural Parrott family, avoiding an unsupported global minimality claim. The result allows deterministic independent reproduction and gives a small concrete example for testing dilation criteria rather than a renaming of the known abstract existence assertion.

## Literature scope and limits
Sources [1] and [3] were inspected in full text for the relevant statements and constructions; [4] was inspected in its open preprint. The argument uses [1, Proposition 2.1] and the established tetrablock Shilov boundary. No claim about all possible \(3\times3\) tuples, an overall cb norm, or a universal optimal counterexample dimension is made.
