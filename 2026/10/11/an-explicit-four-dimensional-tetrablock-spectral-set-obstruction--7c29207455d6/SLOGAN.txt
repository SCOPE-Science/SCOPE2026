A concrete commuting \(4\times4\) triple has the closed tetrablock as a scalar spectral set, yet an explicit \(2\times2\) rational matrix function of supremum norm one attains operator norm \(7/(4\varphi)>1\), where \(\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\). Its rational test family has the exact failure threshold \(\rho>\varphi^{-1}\), and block dimension two is the smallest possible within the centered equal-block Parrott construction.
