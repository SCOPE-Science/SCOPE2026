The inclusive scale-two graph-metric Vietoris–Rips complex of the \(24\)-vertex rhombicuboctahedron collapses by exactly \(252\) explicit elementary pairs onto a triangulation of its boundary \(S^2\). At scale one it is homotopy equivalent to \(igvee^{17}S^1\), so these seventeen first-homology classes disappear at scale two.
