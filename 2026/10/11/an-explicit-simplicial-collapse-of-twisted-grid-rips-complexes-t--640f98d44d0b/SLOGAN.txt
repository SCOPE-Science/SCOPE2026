For every \(m,n\ge7\), the inclusive scale-two graph-metric Vietoris–Rips complex of the twisted \(m\)-by-\(n\) square grid collapses in exactly \(9mn\) elementary pairs to a triangulated Klein bottle. Consequently its first integral homology is \(\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\), in contrast to the untwisted torus-grid case.
