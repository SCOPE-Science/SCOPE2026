# Same-model review

## Correctness

PASS. The proof evaluates the quadratic continued fraction exactly, checks the source threshold \(A>8\omega_3\), and verifies the source's bounded-tail condition uniformly for every \(m\ge2\). The only remaining indices admit exact closed formulas for both forbidden-window functions. Their strict inequalities follow from elementary sine estimates, including the single transition index where the odd-multiple distance crosses an integer. The source's Theorem 14 and rigidity theorem then apply with the correct quantifiers.

## Originality

PASS. The primary paper gives two related isolated quadratic examples and keeps all of its subsequent large families inside continued-fraction cylinders near one interior point. The closest published follow-up proves positive Lebesgue measure inside such a bounded cylinder, which does not imply unboundedness. Searches for the exact quadratic family, endpoint approach, and arbitrarily large admissible section areas found no covering result.

## Value

PASS. The admissible rotation interval has the geometrically singular endpoint \(\gamma=1/2\), corresponding to divergent radius and tangent-section area. The theorem shows explicitly that admissibility reaches that endpoint through a quadratic sequence, proving unboundedness and providing exact asymptotic scales. This complements, rather than merely enlarges, the existing interior measure results.

## Closest literature and limitations

The closest source is Haddad--Ryabogin, arXiv:2605.00299v1. The closest later result proves positive Lebesgue measure for the same admissible set but only from a bounded cylinder near an interior admissible rotation number. A separate global ellipsoid theorem has no arithmetic restriction but assumes the unknown bodies are ellipsoids.

The theorem does not characterize all admissible quadratic irrationals, prove positive measure near infinity, estimate the density of admissible areas, or remove the source's four-dimensional symmetry and body-of-revolution hypotheses.
