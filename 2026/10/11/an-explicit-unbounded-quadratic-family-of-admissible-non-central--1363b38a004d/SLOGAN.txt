The Haddad--Ryabogin admissible area set is unbounded: for every integer \(m\ge2\), the quadratic rotation \(\gamma_m=[0;2,m,1,\overline2]\) is admissible and gives \(A_m=\frac{4\pi}{3}\cot^3\!\bigl(\frac{\pi}{2(2m+1+\sqrt2)}\bigr)\). These explicit rigidity areas satisfy \(A_m\sim\frac{256}{3\pi^2}m^3\).
