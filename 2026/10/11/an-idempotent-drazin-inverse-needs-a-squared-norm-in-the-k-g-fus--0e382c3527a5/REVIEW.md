# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The displayed matrices satisfy the three Drazin identities exactly, with \(S^2=S\) and \(K^2=S\). The one-component system has energy
\[
E(f)=\|K^*f\|^2.
\]
At \(f=(1,0,-1)\), the printed lower constant \(1/\|S\|\) requires \(\sqrt2\le1\), which is false. The corrected estimate follows from
\[
S^*=S^*K^*
\]
and is attained exactly when \(z=-x\) in the displayed family.

## Value
PASS. The corollary is intended to sharpen the general Drazin-inverse lower bound under idempotence. Its printed constant is false, whereas the corrected constant
\[
A/\|S\|^2
\]
is independent of \(r\), stronger than the general theorem's bound, and sharp as a universal norm estimate.

## Limitations
