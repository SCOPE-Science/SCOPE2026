A whole Kohn–Nirenberg tower, not just one isolated model, carries the weighted holomorphic inward flow needed for continuous hypersurface pluricomplex Green functions. For every \(m\ge3\) and \(1<\beta<m^2/(2m-1)\), the nonconvexifiable \(B\)-regular domain \(\Omega_{m,\beta}\) admits the flow \(S_\tau(z,w)=(e^{-\tau}z,e^{-m\tau}w)\), giving continuity for the natural reduced divisor family \((w-1)^2-\lambda z^2=0\).
