The normalized rank-one separable-field automaton contains an exactly invariant stripe subsystem which evolves by Rule 54 as long as a cyclic triple of live rows exists, and can then move irreversibly into Rule 18 or extinction; the transition is selected by the maximal cyclic triple occupancy. At the studied threshold parameters, a \(13\)-row stripe realizes a primitive \(169\)-cycle for every width at least \(3\), transferring an already-known Rule-54 orbit to this nonlocal model.
