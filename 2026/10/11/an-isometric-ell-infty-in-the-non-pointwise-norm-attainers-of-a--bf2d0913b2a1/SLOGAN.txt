For every positive nonincreasing null sequence \(\alpha_n\le1/3\), the countable metric \(d(n,m)=2-\alpha_{\min(n,m)}-2\alpha_{\max(n,m)}\) admits a closed isometric copy of \(\ell_\infty\) consisting, apart from zero, entirely of Lipschitz functions that fail pointwise norm attainment. This includes the asymmetric metric singled out in Choi–Jung–Lee–Roldán's Example 4.10.
