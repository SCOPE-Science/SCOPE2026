For an explicit weak-star closed isometric \(\ell_\infty(\kappa)\) space of nonattaining Lipschitz functions on any infinite equilateral metric, weak-star continuous projection norms have infimum \(1\) but never equal \(1\). Ordinary bounded projections achieve norm \(1\), while explicit weak-star continuous projections have norms \((k+1)/k\), tending sharply to that unattained threshold.
