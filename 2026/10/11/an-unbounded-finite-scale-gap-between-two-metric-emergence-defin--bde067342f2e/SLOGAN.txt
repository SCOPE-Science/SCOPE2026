In the binary full shift, \(k\) mutually staggered nonconvergent orbit statistics admit a single ordinary-emergence prototype exactly for accuracies \(\varepsilon>1/k\), while Berger's second metric emergence is exactly \(\lfloor(2\varepsilon)^{-1}\rfloor+1\). Hence the two metric notions can differ by arbitrarily large factors at the same resolution even for a finite-support reference measure.
