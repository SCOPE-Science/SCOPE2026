Every \(C^2\) convex plane curve has a positive-curvature point where a V-dent of Hausdorff size \(O(t^2)\) raises the cell's average absolute curvature by a positive term of order \(t\). A homothetic inner copy changes its average curvature only by \(O(t^2)\), so all sufficiently small dents in this family violate the DNA inequality.
