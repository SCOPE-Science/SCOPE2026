At a Gaussian CAZAC chirp of length \(N\), every sufficiently small phase-preserving CAZAC deformation is exactly periodic with period \(d=\prod_{p^v\parallel N}p^{\lfloor v/2\rfloor}\). An explicit Fourier-diagonal Hessian proves transverse rigidity, so the local moduli dimension is \(d-1\) after removing global phase; in particular all squarefree-length Gaussian chirps are locally isolated up to phase.
