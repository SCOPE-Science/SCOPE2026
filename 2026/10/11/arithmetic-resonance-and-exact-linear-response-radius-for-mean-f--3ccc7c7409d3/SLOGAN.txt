For mean-field expanding circle maps with one cosine observable and one sine forcing harmonic, the uniform-density linearization has spectral radius exactly \(d^{-1}\) at every coupling strength unless the forcing frequency is an integer power \(d^j\) of the expansion degree. Precisely at these arithmetic resonances, the radius becomes \(\max\{d^{-1},(\pi d^{j-1}|\varepsilon|)^{1/j}\}\), giving a sharp linear instability threshold.
