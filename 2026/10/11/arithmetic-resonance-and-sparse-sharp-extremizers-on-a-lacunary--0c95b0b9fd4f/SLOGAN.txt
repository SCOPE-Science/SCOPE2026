For the lacunary curve \(t\mapsto(t,t^3,t^4)\) over finite fields of characteristic greater than \(3\), the sharp counting-to-probability Fourier synthesis \(\ell^2\to L^4\) norm jumps from fourth power \(2-q^{-1}\) to \(9/4\) exactly when the field contains fourth roots of unity. The complete maximizers change from arbitrary phases at constant magnitude on the whole field to four-point multiplicative orbits with one opposite-pair phase constraint.
