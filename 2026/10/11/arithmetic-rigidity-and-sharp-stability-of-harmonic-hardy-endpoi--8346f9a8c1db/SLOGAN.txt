For every finite set \(S\) of coefficient indices, all simultaneous equality cases for the sharp harmonic \(\mathbf h^1\) coefficient bound are phased positive Poisson mixtures on exactly one cyclic coset, whose possible size is the gcd of \(2n_0\) and the index differences. Near-extremizers obey an explicit \(O_S(\sqrt\varepsilon)\) bounded-Lipschitz stability theorem, and the square-root exponent is sharp.
