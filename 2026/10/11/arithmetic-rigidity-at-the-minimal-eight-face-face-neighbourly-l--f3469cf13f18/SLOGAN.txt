Every trivalent equivelar face-neighbourly surface with eight faces must use at least nonagons; at the minimum it is forced to have \(36\) edges, \(24\) vertices, genus \(3\), and exactly eight units of repeated face adjacency. If no face pair meets in more than two edges, the doubled-pair graph can only have cycle type \(8\), \(5+3\), or \(4+4\).
