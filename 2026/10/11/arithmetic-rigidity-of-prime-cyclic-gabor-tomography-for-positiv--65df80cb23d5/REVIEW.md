# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The prime-cyclotomic lemma forces a rational zero-frequency polynomial at a nontrivial character to have constant coefficients. Positivity makes this equivalent to empty overlap for a proper support, while the full-support cyclic product identity forces global constancy. The standard Weyl Fourier diagonalization (Bojarovska--Flinth, §2.2) converts ambiguity zeros into exact rank and Weyl-basis kernel dimensions with normalization \(\sqrt p\). Explicit rank-two signal ambiguities prove the necessity of the support-cover condition for phase retrieval. Cauchy--Davenport gives the sharp support-size bound; elementary differences confirm the non-difference-set example. The exact proof handles every odd prime; numerical tests are illustrative only.

## Originality — PASS with residual risk

The closest primary source, Bojarovska--Flinth (arXiv:1503.05800, §§2.2--2.4), already gives the general Gabor ambiguity nonvanishing condition, singular-value bounds, classical difference-set constructions and missing-band obstructions. None of its examined results directly states the arithmetic *all nonnegative rational weights* rank-and-kernel classification, nor the positive nonconstant full-support dichotomy. Feichtinger--Kozek--Luef (arXiv:math/0703228) gives general finite Weyl calculus but not that coefficient-specific rigidity. Related published-result searches for rational windows, cyclotomic ambiguity, difference covers and exact lifting ranks revealed no covering statement. The conclusion is not a claim to invent the known Weyl spectral reduction. An unlocated earlier specialization involving rational windows remains possible.

## Value — PASS

A canonical p-by-p Fourier/Gabor measurement system admits a complete exact deficiency classification under a natural dense arithmetic family of weights. Unlike a single construction, it yields a uniform operator-kernel formula, determines precisely when *all signals* can be phase retrieved, exposes the rational-versus-real boundary, and covers asymmetric difference covers with arbitrary weights rather than only cyclic difference sets. The support combinatorics are quantified sharply by Cauchy--Davenport; the result is relevant to exact arithmetic frame and tomography design, though it makes no robustness claim.

## Remaining limits

The theorem requires nonnegative rational weights; its zero-free conclusion fails for general positive real weights. It does not quantify noise conditioning. Full historical priority has not been independently certified.
