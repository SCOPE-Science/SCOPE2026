A triangle-free graph with an aspherical two-dimensional square completion has second discrete cubical homology equal to ordinary cellular second homology: sphere boundaries force every embedded square to be monophobic. Consequently all toroidal product grids \(C_m\mathbin\square C_n\) with \(m,n\ge5\) have \(H_2^{\mathrm{cub}}\cong\mathbb Z\).
