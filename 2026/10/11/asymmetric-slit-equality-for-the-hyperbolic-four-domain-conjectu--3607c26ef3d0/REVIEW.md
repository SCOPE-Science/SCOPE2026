# Review of Asymmetric slit equality for the hyperbolic four-domain conjecture

## Correctness

PASS. For each one-slit disk, the source's inversion-capacity argument gives
\[
\lambda_{D_{+}(a)}(0)=\frac{a+a^{-1}+2}{4}
\]
and, by reflection,
\[
\lambda_{D_{-}(b)}(0)=\frac{b+b^{-1}+2}{4}.
\]

For the intersection, inversion produces the closed unit disk with one positive and one negative radial segment attached. The Joukowski map is injective on the exterior disk and maps the two attached segments to
\[
[2,a+a^{-1}]
\]
and
\[
[-(b+b^{-1}),-2].
\]
The exterior domain therefore maps conformally onto the complement of one interval. Its capacity is exactly one quarter of that interval's length, yielding
\[
\lambda_{D_{+}(a)\cap D_{-}(b)}(0)
=
\frac{a+a^{-1}+b+b^{-1}}{4}.
\]
The union is the unit disk, whose density at the origin is
\[
1.
\]
The conjectured four-domain equality follows identically.

## Originality

PASS. The full primary source was inspected through its logarithmic-capacity lemmas, Joukowski lemma, sharpness construction, Conjecture 6.1, and equality remark. Its equality example uses one common slit parameter and is reflection symmetric. The inspected text does not state independent positive and negative slit depths.

Searches by source identifier, asymmetric-slit language, independent parameters, and equivalent four-domain equality formulations did not locate this two-parameter family. The multiplicative four-domain inequalities in the cited earlier literature are distinct statements and do not imply the additive equality calculation.

## Value

PASS. Equality structure is mathematically relevant because the source explicitly formulates an open four-domain inequality and separately points out that equality can occur. The new family shows that the visible symmetry of the source example is not rigid: the equality locus already contains a two-dimensional asymmetric family.

This substantially changes the shape of any prospective equality classification for the open conjecture. It also supplies an exact two-parameter benchmark with a closed Fuchs-quotient defect, rather than a cosmetic reparametrization of one symmetric curve.
