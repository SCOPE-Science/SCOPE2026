For
\[
D_{+}(a)=\mathbb D\setminus[a,1),
\]
inversion produces
\[
K_{+}(a)=\overline{\mathbb D}\cup[1,a^{-1}].
\]
The source's Joukowski map sends the unbounded complementary component conformally onto
\[
\widehat{\mathbb C}\setminus[-2,a+a^{-1}]
\]
with derivative
\[
1
\]
at infinity. Hence
\[
\lambda_{D_{+}(a)}(0)
=
\frac{a+a^{-1}+2}{4}.
\]

Reflection gives
\[
\lambda_{D_{-}(b)}(0)
=
\frac{b+b^{-1}+2}{4}.
\]

For the intersection, the inverted compact set is
\[
\overline{\mathbb D}
\cup[-b^{-1},-1]
\cup[1,a^{-1}].
\]
Because the Joukowski map is injective on the exterior disk, its unbounded complement maps onto
\[
\widehat{\mathbb C}
\setminus
[-(b+b^{-1}),a+a^{-1}].
\]
Therefore
\[
\lambda_{D_{+}(a)\cap D_{-}(b)}(0)
=
\frac{
a+a^{-1}+b+b^{-1}
}{4}.
\]

The union is
\[
\mathbb D,
\]
so its density at the origin is
\[
1.
\]
Substitution makes the four-domain equality an exact identity.

No numerical approximation or finite search is used in the proof.
