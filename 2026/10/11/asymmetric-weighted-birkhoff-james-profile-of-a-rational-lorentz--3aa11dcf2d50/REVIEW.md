# Review: exact weighted Birkhoff–James profile of a rational Lorentz octagon

## Correctness — PASS

The claim is quantified over all real \(\lambda\in[0,1]\) and both unit vectors in the real normed plane. The four path parameterizations are exhaustive by the norming-functional criterion for Birkhoff–James orthogonality. The three explicit norm-one orthogonal pairs supply lower bounds. On the three weight intervals, an upper-bound violation implies strict positivity of two of eight supporting facets on one path; the complete feasibility partition is determined by endpoint and degree-two determinant roots. The supplied exact-rational verifier checks all 768 branches, 3840 potential critical polynomials, and 2488 exact critical and intermediate weights, proving the upper bound through the explained root-completeness argument. The breakpoint calculation proves the asymmetry maximum. No analytic continuation from finite samples is used.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The example distinguishes weight-reflection symmetry of an invariant from signed-permutation symmetry of a norm. The complete exact three-phase profile, including the central plateau, and sharp quantitative asymmetry give a structural counterexample for the newly defined invariant; it is not a mere generic two-point normalization or an unrelated numerical slice. The rational Lorentz octagon is a canonical, easily reproducible absolute-symmetric test space, and the certificate establishes the full weight range rather than isolated parameter values. No broad all-Lorentz theorem is claimed.
