The weighted Birkhoff–James profile of the absolute symmetric Lorentz octagon \(N(s,t)=\max\{|s|,|t|,2(|s|+|t|)/3\}\) is exactly \(\max\{1-2\lambda/3,3/4,2/5+3\lambda/5\}\) for every \(0\le\lambda\le1\). It is flat on \([3/8,7/12]\) and exhibits exact weight-reflection asymmetry \(\max_\lambda|J^\perp_\lambda-J^\perp_{1-\lambda}|=1/40\), despite the norm's signed-permutation symmetry.
