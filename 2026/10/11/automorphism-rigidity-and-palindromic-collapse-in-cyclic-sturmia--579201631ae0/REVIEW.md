# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The continuous circle factor and its precisely two-valued fibers follow from mechanical coding and irrational orbit density. Asymptotic pairs force automorphisms to induce circle rotations, and preservation of the two-valued fiber set restricts them to shift powers and constant symbol translations. Breakpoint counting yields the factor complexity, and the prohibition of reverse increments for cyclic alphabets of size at least three yields the palindrome cutoff. Exact-rational finite tests supplement but do not replace this proof.

**Originality — PASS, with priority risk.** Kang–Jäger (arXiv:2609.01140v1) supply the general permutative-lift framework and selected substitution results. Cyr–Kra give general low-complexity automorphism restrictions; Rote covers the binary complexity-two case; rotation palindrome research predominantly treats binary codings. None of the inspected statements implies the full automorphism and palindrome classification for every irrational slope. An equivalent older theorem in specialized rotation-coding literature remains possible; some sources were accessible only in limited depth.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** This gives an exact and non-routine automorphism group over an entire natural family (including non-substitutive slopes), and a sharp algebraic cutoff for palindromes that changes at alphabet size three. The result provides test examples for structural theory of symbolic extensions.

**Limitations.** Not a theorem about all permutative block maps or binary palindrome complexity. Computational rational models are finite consistency checks only, and independent validation has not been performed.

Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
