Among all axially stretched octahedra \(\operatorname{conv}\{\pm e_1,\pm e_2,\pm\lambda e_3\}\), the regular octahedron \(\lambda=1\) uniquely maximizes the probability that a uniformly oriented planar shadow has six vertices. An explicit all-\(\lambda>0\) arctangent law and a strict derivative factorization establish the optimum, while a finite trihedral-cone formula computes the four-vertex probability for every affine octahedron.
