For the spanning-tree polytope of \(K_{p,q}\), Minkowski asymmetry equals \(\max\{p,q\}-1\) for unequal part sizes, but has the strictly larger value \(\frac{(2r-1)(r-1)^2}{2r^2-4r+1}\) at equal sizes \(p=q=r\). The uniform spanning-tree incidence average is an optimal center, and the balanced excess tends to \(1/2\).
