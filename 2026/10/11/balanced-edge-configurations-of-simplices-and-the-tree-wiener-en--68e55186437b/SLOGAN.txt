Every equal-weight set of \(n-1\) points on simplex edges balancing its vertex centroid corresponds uniquely to a labeled spanning tree, giving exactly \(n^{n-2}\) configurations with explicit component-size edge weights. For a regular simplex, the total squared separation of these points is an affine function of the tree's Wiener index, minimized precisely by paths and maximized precisely by stars (with the natural coincidence at \(n=3\)).
