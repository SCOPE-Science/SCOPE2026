Every balanced complex Grassmannian \(\operatorname{Gr}_{\mathbb C}(k,2k)\) with \(k\ge2\), after scaling to \(\operatorname{Ric}=g\), has diameter \(\pi k=\pi\sqrt{\dim_{\mathbb C}M}\) but is not a product of projective lines. Thus the proposed Kähler diameter inequality survives these tests, while its equality-only-for-\((\mathbb{CP}^{1})^m\) rigidity clause is false even for irreducible homogeneous examples.
