PASS. The proof checks all prototype families under the published exhaustive support classification. PASS. The two-phase alphabet is a natural flat-spectrum/perfect-autocorrelation restriction. The theorem completely separates the four-point extreme Sidon geometries that admit real signs from those that necessarily use genuinely nonbinary phases.
