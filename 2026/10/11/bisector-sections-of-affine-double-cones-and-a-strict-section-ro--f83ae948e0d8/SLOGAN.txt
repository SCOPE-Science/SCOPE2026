For every dimension \(d\ge3\), tilted bisector sections of an affine circular double cone have an exact beta-function profile. In three dimensions this yields \(\mathcal S(C_3)\ge2(\Gamma(5/4)/\Gamma(7/4))^2>3\pi^2/16+1/100\), a strict benchmark for Saroglou's section-root conjecture on the full centered affine double-cone family; the maximizing direction is not determined.
