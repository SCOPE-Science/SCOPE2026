For Dutch windmills, permuting the \(n\) identical cycle blades acts on top matching-complex homology by a parity-controlled sign twist: the sphere case is \(\operatorname{sgn}_n^{\otimes k}\), while the \((2n-1)\)-sphere wedge carries \(\operatorname{sgn}_n^{\otimes k}\otimes(\mathbf1\oplus2\,\mathrm{Std}_n)\).
