The Lovász open-neighborhood complex of any connected block graph with \(b\ge1\) clique blocks of order at least three is a wedge of one sphere \(S^{|C|-2}\) for each such block and exactly \(b-1\) extra circles. In degrees at least two, these clique-boundary spheres give canonical integral generators with a signed block-permutation automorphism action; line graphs of trees inherit a formula determined solely by branch degrees.
