# Same-model review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The proof reduces the parameter dependence to the abelianization of the pure braid group. The source's Theorem 4.6 makes every bond image a pure conjugate of \(A_{ij}^p\); after transport through braid blocks, each bond contributes one permuted standard basis vector multiplied by \(p\). The bond degree is preserved by the defining presentation, so the coordinate-sum identity is exact. Distinct parameters then have distinct total pairwise linking numbers.

## Originality
**PASS.** The primary source was inspected at its definitions, closure background, pure-braid presentation, and Theorem 4.6. It does not state a linking-number formula or the infinite closure-distinctness consequence. Searches using the exact family, affine-linking, bond-degree, total-linking, and closure-distinctness formulations found no covering statement. Classical pure-braid literature supplies only the abelianization input.

## Value
**PASS.** The parameter \(p\) is converted from an algebraic choice in a new family of epimorphisms into a measurable topological speed: each bond contributes exactly one unit to the total-linking slope. Thus every positive-bond pure input yields infinitely many distinct oriented classical closures, a natural and reusable structural property of the construction.

## Closest literature and limitations
