For a boundary zero of a holomorphic function on the disk, the universal half-multiplicity energy law has a first nonuniversal term governed exactly by the signed curvature \(\kappa_f(q)\) of the normalized boundary germ. This yields explicit corrections of orders \(\epsilon^{1+1/\ell}\) for the level integral and \(\epsilon^{2+1/\ell}\) for the sublevel energy.
