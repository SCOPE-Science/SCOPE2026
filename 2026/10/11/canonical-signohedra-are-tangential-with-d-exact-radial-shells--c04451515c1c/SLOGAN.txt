The canonical signohedron \(\Sigma_d=(C_d+\Diamond_d)^\circ\) is tangent to one origin-centered sphere along all \(d2^d\) facets, with exact inradius \(1/\sqrt{d+3}\) and circumradius \(1/2\). Its \(3^d-1\) vertices form exactly \(d\) strict radial shells \(\sqrt{k}/(k+1)\), while the polar \(C_d+\Diamond_d\) has the reciprocal facet shells \((k+1)/\sqrt{k}\).
