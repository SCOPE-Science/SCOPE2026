For \(0\le\lambda<1/2\), a centrally symmetric convex body keeps central symmetry after one \(\lambda\)-Steiner step in direction \(u\) exactly when it is an affine prism in that direction. Since a convex body has only countably many such prism directions, no centrally symmetric body can be an intermediate \(\lambda\)-invariant; this verifies the higher-dimensional invariant-body conjecture on the entire centrally symmetric class.
