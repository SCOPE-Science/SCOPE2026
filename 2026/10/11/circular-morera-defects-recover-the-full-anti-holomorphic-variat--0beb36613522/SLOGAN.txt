The normalized circular Morera defect detects exactly when the distributional \(\bar\partial\)-derivative is a Radon measure, and its spatial \(L^1\) mass converges to the derivative's full total variation. Subtracting the almost-everywhere pointwise limit recovers precisely the singular mass, which can persist in continuous nonholomorphic examples with modulus \(O(t\log^\beta(1/t))\) for every \(\beta>0\).
