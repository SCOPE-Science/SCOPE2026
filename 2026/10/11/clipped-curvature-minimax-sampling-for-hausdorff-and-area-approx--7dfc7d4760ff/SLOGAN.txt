For a smooth noncircular convex plane curve, the unique sampling law balancing normalized Hausdorff and missing-area errors is a **clipped curvature law**: \(f_*(s)=\sqrt{\kappa(s)}\max\{t_*,c_*\kappa(s)^{-1/6}\}/V\). The threshold \(t_*\in(0,1)\) is uniquely determined by equality of the two error ratios, yielding a strict but exactly computable compromise beyond either separate optimum.
