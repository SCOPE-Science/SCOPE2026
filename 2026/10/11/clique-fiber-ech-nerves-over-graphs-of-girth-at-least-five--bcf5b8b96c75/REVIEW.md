# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** For each closed-neighborhood generator, pairs of off-fiber vertices belong uniquely to that generator because the base has no triangles or four-cycles. Matching those faces with the designated fiber pivot yields valid elementary free-face collapses in decreasing size. The residual edge blocks are unions of two contractible pieces intersecting in a complete bipartite graph, hence wedges of \((m-1)^2\) two-spheres. Gluing edge blocks along full fiber simplices reproduces the cycle rank of the base and sums the sphere groups. An explicit base-graph section proves surjectivity of integral first homology and determines the kernel rank. The standalone checker validates finite collapse and chain computations but does not replace the general proof.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** The graph Čech source establishes the neighborhood construction and studies hypercubes, not the asserted clique-fiber products over arbitrary high-girth bases. A later closed-neighborhood paper treats Cartesian products of two complete graphs, and in particular covers the single-edge base \(H=K_2\) as a special case. It does not imply the classification for connected noncomplete high-girth bases. Previously established width-two prism results are a proper special case, explicitly excluded by the bound \(m\ge3\). A published Vietoris–Rips classification for cyclic clique strips concerns a distinct nerve and does not imply the scale-two Čech sphere summands. Primary-source inspections, targeted literature queries and published-result comparisons did not reveal an equivalent or stronger coverage; unindexed work remains a risk.

**Value — PASS.** This is a structural all-parameter classification for branching and cyclic high-girth bases and arbitrarily wide clique fibers. The exact collapse formula reduces high-dimensional simplices to natural edge blocks and computes complete integral homotopy and the persistence kernel. The new quadratic \((m-1)^2\) two-sphere contribution per base edge is not determined by earlier width-two prism or Vietoris–Rips calculations.

Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
