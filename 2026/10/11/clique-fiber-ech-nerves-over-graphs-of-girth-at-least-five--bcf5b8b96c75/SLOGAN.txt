For every connected graph \(H\) with no triangles or four-cycles and every clique width \(m\ge3\), the scale-two graph Čech nerve of \(H\square K_m\) collapses to an edge-block complex with homotopy type \(H\vee\bigvee^{|E(H)|(m-1)^2}S^2\). The first Čech thickening preserves precisely the base graph's integral first homology and adds a quadratic number of two-spheres per base edge.
