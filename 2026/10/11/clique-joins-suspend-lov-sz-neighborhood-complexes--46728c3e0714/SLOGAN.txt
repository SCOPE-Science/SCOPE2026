For every finite nonempty simple graph \(H\) without isolated vertices and every \(r\ge1\), joining \(H\) with \(K_r\) suspends its Lovász neighborhood complex exactly \(r\) times up to homotopy. Hence \(\mathcal N(K_r\vee(nK_m))\simeq(\bigvee^nS^{{m+r-2}})\vee(\bigvee^{{n-1}}S^r)\) for \(n,m\ge2\).
