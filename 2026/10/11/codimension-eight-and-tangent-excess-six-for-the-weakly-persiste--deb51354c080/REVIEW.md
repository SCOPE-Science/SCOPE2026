PASS. The exact tangent excess is mathematically informative: at the linear system the locus has local dimension \(6\) but tangent dimension \(12\). Thus a codimension-eight nonlinear condition presents only two first-order parameter obstructions, a useful structural warning for perturbative or computational searches for isochronicity.
