On the balanced hypergeometric surface \(b+c=3a+\tfrac12\), a single gamma quotient \(R\) controls every sufficiently distant zero of \({}_1F_2(a;b,c;-z^2/4)\): two simple real strings for \(|R|<1\), but two simple off-real-axis strings for \(|R|>1\). The nonreal strings approach the heights \(|\operatorname{Im}z|=\operatorname{arcosh}|R|\).
