The Hilbert matrix has a sharp compactness transition on logarithmically weighted Bergman Hilbert spaces: it is compact into \(A^2\) for \(\alpha>2\), bounded but noncompact at \(\alpha=2\), and fails to map the whole space into \(A^2\) below \(2\). The endpoint noncompactness is witnessed by weakly null logarithmic block polynomials whose Hilbert images retain fixed \(A^2\)-mass.
