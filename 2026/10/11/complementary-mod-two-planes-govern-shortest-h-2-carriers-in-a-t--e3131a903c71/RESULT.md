# Complementary mod-two planes govern shortest \(H_2\)-carriers in a twelve-vertex \((S^2\times S^2)\#(S^2\times S^2)\)
## Finding
Let \(K\) be the twelve-vertex triangulation of
\[
(S^2\times S^2)\#(S^2\times S^2)
\]
listed in the public small-four-manifold facet table. Direct reconstruction gives
\[
f(K)=(12,63,192,225,90)
\]
and
\[
H_2(K;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^4.
\]

The minimum vertex support of a nonzero integral simplicial \(2\)-cycle is exactly \(4\). Exactly \(153\) four-vertex supports attain this minimum. Every such support induces the boundary of a tetrahedron, and its fundamental \(2\)-cycle represents a primitive integral homology class.

Up to reversing orientation, the \(153\) minimum carriers represent exactly \(12\) integral classes. Nine of these classes have \(16\) carriers each and three have \(3\) carriers each.

Choose the integral basis \(e_1,e_2,e_3,e_4\) represented by the oriented tetrahedral boundaries on
\[
\{1,7,11,12\},\quad
\{1,2,3,9\},\quad
\{1,2,3,8\},\quad
\{1,4,7,9\},
\]
respectively. Write the same symbols for their reductions in
\[
H_2(K;\mathbf F_2)\cong\mathbf F_2^4.
\]
Define
\[
U=\langle e_2+e_3,\ e_2+e_4\rangle
\]
and
\[
W=\langle e_2+e_3+e_4,\ e_1+e_3\rangle.
\]
Then
\[
H_2(K;\mathbf F_2)=U\oplus W.
\]

The complete minimum-carrier multiplicity rule is:
\[
m(u)=3\quad(0\ne u\in U),
\]
\[
m(w)=0\quad(0\ne w\in W),
\]
and
\[
m(u+w)=16\quad(0\ne u\in U,\ 0\ne w\in W).
\]
Consequently exactly \(12\) of the \(15\) nonzero mod-two classes are represented at minimum support. Moreover, each represented mod-two class has exactly one primitive integral lift up to sign among the minimum carriers.

## Assumptions and scope
The labelled complex is the `(S^2xS^2)#(S^2xS^2)` entry in the published `small_4_manifolds.txt` data table. Vertices are \(1,\ldots,12\), and all \(90\) four-simplices are embedded verbatim in `verify.py`.

The vertex support of a simplicial chain is the union of all vertices occurring in simplices with nonzero coefficient. Integral carrier classes are compared up to reversing the orientation of the tetrahedral sphere.

The displayed basis is a computational integral basis certified by the chain reduction. No claim is made that it is canonical with respect to the intersection form. The basis-independent assertions are the minimum support size, the number of carriers, primitivity, the \(16^9\,3^3\) multiplicity multiset, and the existence of the complementary-plane description after a suitable integral basis is chosen.

## Proof
The \(90\)-facet presentation reconstructs \(63\) edges, \(192\) triangles, and \(225\) tetrahedra.

The integral boundary matrix
\[
\partial_2:C_2\longrightarrow C_1
\]
has size \(63\times192\). The verifier performs only unimodular integral row and column operations and reduces it to an identity block of rank \(52\) with zero residual block. Hence the integral \(2\)-cycle lattice is a primitive free lattice of rank
\[
192-52=140.
\]

The tetrahedral boundary map
\[
\partial_3:C_3\longrightarrow\ker\partial_2
\]
is then expressed in that exact integral cycle basis. A second unimodular reduction gives an identity block of rank \(136\) with zero residual block. Therefore
\[
H_2(K;\mathbb Z)
\cong
\mathbb Z^{140}/\operatorname{im}\partial_3
\cong
\mathbb Z^4
\]
without torsion. The same reduction gives exact integral coordinates for every \(2\)-cycle.

A nonzero simplicial \(2\)-cycle cannot be supported on fewer than four vertices. On a fixed four-vertex set, a nonzero \(2\)-cycle exists only when all four triangular faces are present, and then the cycle space is generated by the oriented tetrahedral boundary. If the tetrahedron itself is present, that boundary is null-homologous.

The verifier exhausts all
\[
\binom{12}{4}=495
\]
four-vertex subsets. Exactly \(153\) have all four triangular faces present while the tetrahedron is absent. Every one of their boundary cycles has a nonzero primitive coordinate vector in \(\mathbb Z^4\). These are therefore exactly all minimum-support nonzero integral \(H_2\)-classes.

After identifying a coordinate vector with its negative, the \(153\) cycles give exactly \(12\) vectors: nine occur \(16\) times and three occur \(3\) times. The four displayed representative spheres give the standard coordinate vectors \(e_1,e_2,e_3,e_4\).

Reduction of all \(153\) exact integral coordinates modulo \(2\) gives \(12\) represented nonzero classes. Exact enumeration verifies that the three nonzero vectors of \(U\) occur three times each, the three nonzero vectors of \(W\) do not occur, and every sum of a nonzero vector of \(U\) with a nonzero vector of \(W\) occurs sixteen times. Since \(U\cap W=0\) and both have dimension two, they form a direct-sum decomposition of \(\mathbf F_2^4\).

Finally, the \(12\) integral sign-classes reduce to \(12\) distinct mod-two classes, proving uniqueness of the primitive integral lift up to sign within the minimum layer.

## Verification
The standalone `verify.py` uses only the Python standard library. It embeds the complete \(90\)-facet presentation, reconstructs every face, performs both integral unimodular chain reductions, exhausts all \(495\) four-vertex subsets, checks primitivity of every minimum carrier, and verifies the integral and mod-two multiplicity spectra and the direct-sum plane rule.

Exact successful output:

`VERIFY_OK f=12,63,192,225,90 H2_Z=Z^4 min_vertex_support=4 carriers4=153 all_primitive=yes integral_classes_up_to_sign=12 integral_multiplicities=16x9+3x3 mod2_carried_classes=12 mod2_missing_classes=3 planes=U+W_direct_sum=yes multiplicity_rule=U_nonzero:3|W_nonzero:0|mixed:16`

The proof of \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z^4\) and of primitivity is integral. The mod-two plane structure is computed only after the integral carrier classes have been certified.

## Relationship to prior work
Lutz's 2005 survey records a twelve-vertex triangulation of
\[
(S^2\times S^2)\#(S^2\times S^2)
\]
with homology
\[
(\mathbb Z,0,\mathbb Z^4,0,\mathbb Z)
\]
and links the public small-manifold facet data. The same survey explains that small connected-sum triangulations were obtained by bistellar simplification.

Köhler and Lutz independently enumerate two vertex-minimal, vertex-transitive twelve-vertex triangulations of the same topological manifold. Those two examples have face vector
\[
(12,66,204,240,96),
\]
so they are different triangulations from the \(90\)-facet complex studied here.

The inspected sources therefore establish the topology, vertex-minimality context, ambient homology, and nearby vertex-transitive examples. They do not state the \(153\)-carrier theorem, the \(16^9\,3^3\) integral spectrum, or the complementary-plane rule in mod-two homology. Focused literature searches using the exact manifold name, missing-tetrahedron terminology, minimum \(H_2\)-support terminology, and the numerical fingerprints \(153\), \(16\), and \(3\) did not locate a covering statement.

## Limitations
The result concerns minimum vertex support in one fixed labelled triangulation. It does not classify larger supports or assert the same spectrum for the two vertex-transitive twelve-vertex triangulations of the same manifold.

The chosen integral basis is computational rather than intersection-form canonical. The complementary-plane statement is an explicit normal form for the minimum-support spectrum, not a claim of a canonical decomposition attached to the topological manifold.

An unindexed historical finite computation could contain part or all of the same carrier census.
