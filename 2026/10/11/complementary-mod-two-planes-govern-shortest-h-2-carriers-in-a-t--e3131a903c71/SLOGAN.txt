In a twelve-vertex triangulation of \((S^2\times S^2)\#(S^2\times S^2)\), exactly \(153\) induced tetrahedral spheres are the minimum-support nonzero integral \(H_2\)-carriers, and every one is primitive. Modulo \(2\), complementary two-planes \(U\oplus W\) control the whole minimum layer: nonzero \(U\)-classes have \(3\) carriers, nonzero \(W\)-classes have none, and the nine mixed classes have \(16\) each.
