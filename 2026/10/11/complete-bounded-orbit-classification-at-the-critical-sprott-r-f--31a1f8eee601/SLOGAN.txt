On the zero-restoring Sprott-R plane \(a=0\), nonzero forcing \(b\) precludes all bounded forward-complete orbits. At \(a=b=0\), each positive invariant-height leaf contains exactly two equilibria and one heteroclinic orbit up to translation, whose total dissipation is exactly \(\int_{\mathbb R}y(t)^2\,dt=\frac23(2h)^{3/2}\).
