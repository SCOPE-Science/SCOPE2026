On every anisotropic two-dimensional finite-field paraboloid with \(q\equiv3\pmod4\), all nonzero complex \(L^2\to L^4\) extremizers are precisely scalar multiples of quadratic additive characters \(u\mapsto\psi(\alpha u_1+\beta u_2+\gamma(u_1^2+u_2^2))\). Exactly \(q^3\) extremizer classes occur modulo nonzero complex scaling.
