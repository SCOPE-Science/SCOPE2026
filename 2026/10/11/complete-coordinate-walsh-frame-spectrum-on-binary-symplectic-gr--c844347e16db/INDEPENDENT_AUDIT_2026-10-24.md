Matching involution decomposition U⊕V⊕W has dims 1,m-1,m and simultaneous eigenvalue triples (d,1,d-2),(0,1,-2),(0,-1,0). Exact determinant polynomials give s<ell-, and the maximum r for d=4,6 versus ell+ for d>=8; d=4 and d=6 are checked directly, and for d>=8 the polynomial signs are immediate from positive coefficients. Expansion gives kappa²=1+(d²+O(d))/2^d. Independent generation of all 720 Sp(4,2) elements matched all 289 exact Gram entries; a second independent script checked 18 representative pair correlations in dimensions 4,6,8, both symbolic determinant identities and 128 even-dimension sign instances. These finite checks corroborate rather than prove the universal case. The submitted code was inspected but not used as a substitute for proof.

## value

PASS

The standard coordinate Walsh band restricted to uniform symplectic matrices is a natural stable-sampling/finite-group Fourier question: imposing the symplectic constraint breaks ordinary GL rank invariance into paired versus nonpaired coordinate orbits. The complete sharp Gram spectrum, equality-mode subspaces and strict m=4 crossover solve an all-rank conditioning problem whose answer cannot be read off a generic frame theorem or small table. This is a motivated and quantitatively exact spectral classification, not merely renaming a familiar frame or enumerating arbitrary instances. No claim is made of reconstruction of all group functions, higher Walsh degrees, or a new symplectic Fourier theory.
