For every bijective additive-affine Yang–Baxter map over \(\mathbb F_2^m\), the complete helical site-and-carrier dynamics has only primitive cycles of length one, two or four, with explicit counts at every lattice length. Four-cycles fill the entire state space exactly when the lattice length is even and \((I+L)c\ne0\); the rule-family cardinalities are given by square-zero matrix ranks.
