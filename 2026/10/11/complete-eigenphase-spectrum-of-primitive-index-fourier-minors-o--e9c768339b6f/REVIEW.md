# Mathematical review: primitive-index Fourier eigenphases

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem fixes the positive-sign normalized discrete Fourier matrix restricted on both axes to the invertible residues of every odd modulus N≥3. The local exact equalities AA*=A*A=K and A²=KR hold by the Ramanujan sum, with K a residue-fiber projection at repeated prime powers. On ranK the operator is unitary and has fourth-root phases; both its trace and the trace of its square vanish, forcing equal fourth-root multiplicities. At first-power odd primes the constant eigenline has eigenvalue −p^(−1/2), while the standard signed quadratic Gauss sum resolves the other four phases. The Chinese remainder theorem must use local additive twists b=(N/q)^(−1) (mod q), not a blanket twist b=1. Polynomial spectral inversion gives genuine orthogonal projectors; the rank and positive-singular-spectrum condition number follow.

Independent finite matrix diagonalizations and projector identities agreed with the formulas for 16 odd moduli and 10 local projector cases, with maximum eigenvalue mismatch below 6.0×10^(−15). The theorem for all odd moduli follows from the symbolic argument and the classical Gauss-sum evaluation, not from finite tests.

## Originality — PASS, best of knowledge

The complete complex eigenphase and eigenspace classification is mathematically stronger than known Gram/singular concentration results, rank statements, and the full Fourier-transform four-eigenphase decomposition. Horn's full Fourier projectors do not restrict automatically to Euler units; Caragea–Lee's small principal-minor results and squarefree conjecture do not enumerate complex eigenvalues. Complete nearby mathematical findings on valuation-shell concentration and primitive-row frame-potential optimization address positive concentration and pairwise correlation energy, respectively. Herr–Wiegand's primitive-difference-set classification addresses complex Hadamard submatrix existence, not the present eigenspectrum. The similarly named interval-supported restricted DFT has a different invariant space.

A potentially important older source, Delvaux–Van Barel's *Rank-deficient submatrices of Fourier matrices* (TW470; LAA 429, 2008), was not available for a full-text comparison. Its abstract alone cannot establish noncoverage, and a hidden equivalent arithmetic phase formula remains a real priority risk. The assigned record's exact reappearance in a published index is a self-match, not independent novelty evidence. This is a qualified best-of-knowledge verdict, not an assertion of historical priority certification.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The Euler-unit principal Fourier compression is a canonical arithmetic operator. Explicit root-of-unity phase splitting, local Legendre-character dependence, Chinese-remainder twists and orthogonal spectral projectors provide a complete all-odd-modulus structural invariant that singular values alone cannot supply. The result does not establish arbitrary principal-minor spectra, even-modulus formulas or a new proof of classical quadratic Gauss sums.
