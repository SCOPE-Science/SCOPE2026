For every odd modulus, the discrete Fourier matrix restricted on both axes to invertible residues has a complete, explicitly factorized complex eigenvalue spectrum and computable orthogonal eigenspace projectors. At each repeated prime factor, its local nonzero eigenphases are \(1,-1,i,-i\) with equal multiplicities, while first-power primes contribute arithmetic Gauss-phase splitting and one isolated eigenvalue of magnitude \(p^{-1/2}\).
