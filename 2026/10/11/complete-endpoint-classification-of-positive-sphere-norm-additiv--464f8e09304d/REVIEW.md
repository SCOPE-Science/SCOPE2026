# Review of the complete exponent-one classification

## Correctness — PASS
The map \(Hf=f(0)+f'\) is an onto map to the nonnegative sup-norm unit sphere with exactly the interval fibers in the theorem. The sum-norm identity is exact. An Urysohn bump separates distinct positive-sphere profiles, making the map induced by a surjective norm-additive \(T\) well-defined. Ezumi–Miura's inspected Theorem 2.1 forces composition by a homeomorphism. Surjectivity of \(T\) forces each induced fiber map to be onto; conversely the stated formulas preserve all positive-sum norms and hit every target fiber. No regularity on the interval maps is silently assumed. Singleton fibers and equality cases are treated.

## Originality — PASS
Ezumi–Miura, arXiv:2610.02862v1, fully inspected through Theorem 2.1 and Section 6, give \(p>1\) rigidity and a single affine-fiber counterexample for \(p=1\), but no classification of all exponent-one norm-additive surjections. Identifying the exact positive-sum fibers by itself does not constrain the induced quotient action. The present theorem proves the missing all-maps implication by applying the published positive-sphere Banach–Stone theorem to an induced map and then classifies arbitrary onto fiber actions. Enami et al., arXiv:2606.30952v1, concern surjective isometries, not this unrestricted norm-additive class. Targeted semantic, alias, theorem, and source searches did not identify a covering published theorem. Residual risk: a formulation in unindexed literature may already combine these ingredients; no exhaustive novelty certification is asserted.
