The Hu--Ivaki facet network of the regular \(n\)-cross-polytope is exactly a uniformly weighted Boolean hypercube, so its generalized eigenvalues are \((n-1)k\) with multiplicity \(\binom nk\). On even facet functions only even Walsh levels survive, giving \(\lambda_{1,e}=2(n-1)\) with multiplicity \(\binom n2\).
