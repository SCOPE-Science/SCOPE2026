# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

Character orthogonality and the rank-one factorization give the exact normalized Fourier shell kernel (independently reproducing Delsarte's rank-one character formula). Frequency differences within the selected band have rank only zero, one, or two. A blown-up rook decomposition accounts for all eigenvalues and multiplicities; a nonconstant support-product statistic shows the exceptional two-dimensional Gram block is strictly positive. Rayleigh's principle establishes the sharp energy fraction and all equality spaces. The square-dimension asymptotic follows from the explicit block trace and determinant. Twelve independently constructed finite-field examples were checked, including a nonprime field. These finite computations are diagnostic, not proof of the unrestricted theorem.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Delsarte 1978, Appendix equation A7, supplies the necessary rank-one Fourier transform, so that ingredient is explicitly prior art. The new statement assessed here is the *complete* coordinate-band compression spectrum, orthogonal eigenspace classification, sharp band concentration, and cubic condition law. Delsarte's full text treats rank schemes and code enumerators but does not give that selected-band spectrum. Schmidt 2014 treats symmetric bilinear rank schemes; Tan–Balzano–Draper 2011 prove stochastic low-rank measurement thresholds rather than exact character-band concentration. Targeted literature and published-result searches found no covering equivalent; an equivalent implicit consequence in older rank-metric or association-scheme work remains possible. A failed search is not a novelty proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The rank-one (Segre) shell and complete degree-one coordinate-character band are canonical, independently motivated objects. Determining all singular values and equality directions settles exact stability of this low-degree Fourier model, including full injectivity over every finite field and an explicit deterioration \(\kappa^2\sim d^3/(2q^2)\) in square dimension. This is not a parameter substitution into a rank-shell character table: it adds a specified sparse-frequency compression and its spectral geometry. The contribution is a bounded exact structural classification, not a general result for arbitrary bands.
