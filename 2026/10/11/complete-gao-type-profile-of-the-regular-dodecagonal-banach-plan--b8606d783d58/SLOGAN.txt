The Gao-type lower von Neumann--Jordan constant of the regular dodecagonal plane has an exact all-exponent profile. For \(1<p<2\) the minimizer slides along two adjacent edges according to \(q_p=(2-\sqrt3)^{1/(p-1)}\), while for \(p\ge2\) the balanced coordinate pair takes over, with the two branches meeting exactly at \(p=2\).
