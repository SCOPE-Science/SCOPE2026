For the fractional stable-set polytope of a complete graph, the unique minimum enclosing sphere is supported by the singleton vertices up through dimension six, but the all-half fractional vertex becomes an additional contact exactly from dimension seven onward. For \(n\ge7\), the squared-radius gap from the true stable-set simplex is \((n^2-8n+8)^2/(16n(n-2)^2)\), and the fractional radius grows asymptotically as \(\sqrt n/4\) rather than remaining bounded.
