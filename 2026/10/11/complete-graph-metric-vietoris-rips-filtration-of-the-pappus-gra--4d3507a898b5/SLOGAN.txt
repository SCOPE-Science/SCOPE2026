The shortest-path Vietoris–Rips filtration of the Pappus graph passes through \(\bigvee^{10}S^1\), \(\bigvee^{26}S^2\), and then jumps to \(\bigvee^{64}S^5\) before becoming contractible. The \(64\)-fold penultimate wedge comes from the exact decomposition of the farthest-distance graph into six disjoint triangles.
