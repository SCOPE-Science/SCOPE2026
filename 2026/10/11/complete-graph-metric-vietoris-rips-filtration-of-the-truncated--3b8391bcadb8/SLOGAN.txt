Let \(T\) be the 24-vertex truncated-octahedral graph whose vertices are the signed coordinate permutations of \((0,1,2)\), with two vertices adjacent exactly when their squared Euclidean distance is \(2\), and give \(V(T)\) the shortest-path metric. For the inclusive Vietoris--Rips complex \(\operatorname{VR}_{\le r}(T)\), the complete homotopy filtration is \(\bigvee^{23}S^0\) for \(0\le r<1\), \(\bigvee^{13}S^1\) for \(1\le r<2\), \(\bigvee^{9}S^2\) for \(2\le r<3\), \(S^2\) for \(3\le r<4\), \(\bigvee^{5}S^4\vee\bigvee^{2}S^5\) for \(4\le r<5\), \(S^{11}\) for \(5\le r<6\), and a point for \(r\ge6\). The scale-\(4\) stage is the mixed wedge \(\bigvee^{5}S^4\vee\bigvee^{2}S^5\).
