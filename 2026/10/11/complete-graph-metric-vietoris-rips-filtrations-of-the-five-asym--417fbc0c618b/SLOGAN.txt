The five asymmetric cubic Hamiltonian graphs on twelve vertices have complete graph-metric Vietoris--Rips filtrations with only sphere-wedge stages: at radius \(1\) their circle ranks are \(6,6,5,6,4\), and at radius \(2\) they are respectively \(\bigvee^2S^2,S^2,S^2,S^2,S^2\). Every filtration is contractible from radius \(3\) onward.
