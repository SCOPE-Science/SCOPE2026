At the zero-damping boundary of the Sprott J flow, two explicit polynomial integrals reduce every invariant plane to an exactly classified cubic Hamiltonian: all bounded complete motions are equilibria, periodic cycles or one separatrix. The oscillation period diverges at that separatrix with the exact law \(T(E)=L^{-1/2}\log(72L^3/(E_+-E))+o(1)\), while invariant probabilities are mixtures of equilibrium and periodic-orbit measures.
