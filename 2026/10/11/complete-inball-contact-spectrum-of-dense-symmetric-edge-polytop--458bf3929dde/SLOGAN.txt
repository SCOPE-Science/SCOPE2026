Deleting a maximum-degree-two edge set from a complete graph changes the canonical symmetric-edge polytope's Euclidean inball only below a sharp threshold: dimension \(n\ge12\) if the deleted graph has no four-cycle, and \(n\ge16\) in general. The exact inradius and full count of facets tangent to the origin-centered optimal ball depend only on \(n\), the number of deleted degree-two vertices, and the number of deleted four-cycle components, with a striking contact-facet transition.
