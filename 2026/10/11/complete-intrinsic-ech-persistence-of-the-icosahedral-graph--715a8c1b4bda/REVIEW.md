# Scientific review of complete intrinsic Čech persistence of the icosahedral graph

## Correctness
**PASS.** The metric is the intrinsic shortest-path metric of the unit-edge geometric realization, not the ambient Euclidean metric. The parity-dependent generating-face formula of Adams–Shukla–Singh, Lemma 2.2, is applied at its stated integer scales, and their scale-constancy observation covers every real \(r\geq0\). The explicit pole-and-rings presentation determines the graph without hidden dependencies. The two stored discrete-Morse matchings are independently replayed and checked against the complete face posets: 446 faces, 222 matched pairs, 1572 Hasse incidences and critical cells in dimensions zero and two at scale two; 2037 faces, 1013 pairs, 9924 incidences and one critical zero-cell plus ten critical four-cells at scale three. Full directed acyclicity makes Forman's CW theorem applicable. Because the Morse CW models have no other positive-dimensional cells, their attaching maps are necessarily constant and give the stated integral sphere wedges. The unique-antipode property gives the boundary of a simplex at scale four and the full simplex at scale five. The adjacent nonzero reduced integral homology degrees are disjoint, proving all inclusion maps and the claimed barcode. Independent mod-two boundary ranks provide corroboration, not an invalid substitute for the homotopy proof.

## Originality
**PASS subject to unindexed-literature risk.** The owning paper proves the Čech face formula and hypercube results, not the complete icosahedral filtration. Previte's distance-neighborhood construction encompasses the even-scale definition but does not give the present icosahedral calculations. Matsushita proves general closed-neighborhood structural relationships and some graph-family examples, not the specific scale-two homotopy or the scale-three to scale-five persistence. The published icosahedral **open**-neighborhood computation yields twenty-three two-spheres; its definition and face vector differ from the closed-neighborhood scale-two nerve, whose second Betti number is one. It also does not treat the edge-generated scale-three nerve. Euclidean cuboctahedron Rips results do not imply intrinsic icosahedral graph Čech persistence. Relevant published-search queries and own historical titles were checked; this cannot rule out all unpublished or unindexed calculations.

## Value
**PASS.** The result is the complete integral homotopy and persistent-homology classification for the canonical five-regular, twelve-vertex Platonic graph, not an isolated numerical slice. Successive nontrivial scales produce homology in dimensions one, two, four and ten, making a natural example of large jumps in degree and finite-lived Čech features outside the girth-controlled reconstruction regime. The exact CW descriptions establish torsion-freeness and non-shellability at the two intermediate pure, high-dimensional complexes. It is a reproducible structural benchmark for symmetric graph thickenings, without claiming a general classification of all Platonic graphs.
