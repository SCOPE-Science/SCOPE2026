For every complete multipartite graph \(K_{n_1,\ldots,n_k}\) with \(k\ge2\) and all \(n_i\ge2\), the intrinsic graph Čech filtration passes from a graph to \(\bigvee^{\prod_i(n_i-1)}S^{2k-2}\) at scale two and becomes contractible at scale three. Its full integral barcode and the \(\prod_i S_{n_i}\)-module on the top homology are determined exactly by the graph parameters and augmentation kernels.
