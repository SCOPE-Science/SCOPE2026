# Review of complete intrinsic graph Čech persistence of the Heawood graph

## Correctness
**PASS.** The graph and metric are defined explicitly. Adams–Shukla–Singh's even/odd neighborhood-generation formula reduces all real parameters to six integer regimes. The scale-two complex is proved to collapse onto the original graph via fifty-six explicit, relative free-face pairs. The scale-three matching is a fully determined finite certificate: 847 faces, 399 matched incidences, one critical vertex and forty-eight critical triangles, with the complete 3,010-incidence directed Hasse graph tested acyclic. This yields a wedge of forty-eight two-spheres, not merely its homology. Independently, the scale-four complex is a union of two contractible joins intersecting in the join of two Fano triple complexes; each triple complex collapses to a graph of rank eight. This gives sixty-four four-spheres, also corroborated by 3,927 verified discrete-Morse pairs and exact boundary ranks. The scale-five nerve is a simplex. Distinct-degree homology and the relative scale-one-to-two collapse determine every integral persistence interval. Replaying `verify.py` from its actual package path produced `VERIFY_OK`.

## Originality
**PASS subject to residual bibliographic risk.** The full text of Adams, Shukla and Singh (arXiv:2212.05871v2) covers intrinsic graph Čech constructions and computes hypercube cases, not the Fano incidence graph at thresholds three or four. The published projective-plane incidence theorem c3740b96b3c3 is about the distance-two **Vietoris–Rips clique complex**, not a nerve of metric balls. The full 2014 Hughes thesis mainly studies distance sets \(\{0,1\}\) on Cayley graphs and does not state the displayed Heawood filtration. Published Heawood matching, independence and total-cut complexes are different simplicial functors with no implication to the present Čech barcode. Searches under Heawood, Fano-plane Levi graph, closed distance-neighborhood, Čech, and edge-ball nerve formulations identified no exact or stronger covering calculation. A non-indexed prior computation cannot be excluded.

## Value
**PASS.** The Heawood graph is the smallest cubic graph of girth six, and threshold three is exactly where the standard girth-based reconstruction guarantee ceases. The jump from eight one-dimensional classes to forty-eight two-dimensional sphere classes, followed by sixty-four four-dimensional sphere classes, is a natural complete topological and persistence classification of this canonical object. The constructive Morse witness and independent structural join proof expose why two successive higher-dimensional homotopy types occur. This is not a normalization check, a restatement of known Betti numbers, or an arbitrary isolated parameter slice.

## Limitations and literature relationship
The finite computation proves this precise fourteen-vertex graph only. It does not imply a formula for all projective-plane orders or for all girth-six graphs. The strongest related graph-specific result inspected computes a different complex, the scale-two Rips filtration of finite projective-plane incidence graphs. The main Čech reference does not analyze incidence graphs. An equivalent statement may exist under neighborhood-complex terminology that was not indexed by the searches.
