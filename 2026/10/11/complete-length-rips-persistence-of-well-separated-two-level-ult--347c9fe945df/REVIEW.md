# Scientific assessment

**Correctness (PASS).** Every nonempty face has its claimed exact Hamiltonian-path weight: pure faces use only \(\delta\)-edges, and an optimal mixed ordering crosses exactly once. During a mixed interval the complex is precisely a complete-simplex skeleton union two full cluster simplices. The integral Mayer–Vietoris top-homology map is split injective by explicit simplicial retractions, so the only reduced homology is free of rank \(b_t\) in degree \(t-1\). A connectivity argument and Hurewicz–Whitehead give the wedge type, with a separate graph-of-spaces argument for \(t=2\). Skeleton inclusions kill the preceding homology, proving the barcode rather than merely its ranks. The embedded standard-library checker independently confirms every interval for 48 small parameter instances, without substituting finite tests for proof.

**Originality (PASS; residual risk).** Ivanov–Xu's arXiv:2411.01857 supplies the \(\ell^1\)-complex definition and general stability/homotopy machinery, not a two-level-ultrametric full persistence classification in the inspected sections. The related monoidal-Rips full-text discussion and semantically searched related indexed homology calculations do not determine these binomial mixed-scale wedge multiplicities or persistence maps. Ordinary ultrametric \(\ell^\infty\) results have contractible components and therefore do not imply the \(\ell^1\) classification. An unindexed, unpublished or unnoticed equivalent calculation remains a possibility; novelty is not claimed as absolutely certified.

**Value (PASS).** The theorem is an exact, all-size natural class computation for a filtration recently introduced in generality, identifying *every* nonzero dimension and lifetime. A finite ultrametric has no positive-dimensional ordinary Vietoris–Rips persistence, but its length–Rips variant can have a degree-\(N-2\) interval and explicit families of higher-degree bars; this distinguishes two fundamental constructions on the same metric. The restriction to a strong two-cluster gap limits generality without making the classification an arbitrary slice.

Closest known literature and scope are detailed in the result and references. Cases of smaller intercluster separation, more hierarchy levels and the different ordered simplicial-set construction are excluded. Independently checked scientific validity or priority is not asserted.
