For two well-separated equilateral clusters, the symmetrized \(\ell^1\)-Vietoris–Rips filtration has an explicit wedge-of-spheres homotopy type at every mixed scale and a complete binomial-coefficient barcode in every homological degree. Unlike the ordinary \(\ell^\infty\)-Rips filtration of an ultrametric, it has positive-dimensional persistence in arbitrarily high degree.
