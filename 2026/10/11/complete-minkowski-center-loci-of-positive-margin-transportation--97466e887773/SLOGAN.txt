For any positive-margin transportation polytope, Minkowski asymmetry is exactly one less than the largest row-or-column sum of individual cell maxima divided by its prescribed margin, and **every** minimizing center forms a translated residual transportation polytope. In the square \(n\times n\) case, asymmetry is universally \(n-1\) regardless of margins, while center uniqueness and dimension are determined by the positive residual margins.
