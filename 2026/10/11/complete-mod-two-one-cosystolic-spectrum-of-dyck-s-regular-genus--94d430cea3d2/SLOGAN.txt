Dyck's regular twelve-vertex genus-three map has mod-two one-cosystole \(6\), and its \(63\) nonzero cohomology classes split into exactly five automorphism orbits with minimum-support data \((6,16,1),(10,24,4),(12,6,8),(14,16,18),(16,1,12)\). The unique invariant nonzero class is the weight-\(16\) class, while each of the sixteen weight-\(6\) classes has a unique minimizing cocycle.
