For the axis-aligned elliptical four-center cubic Kolmogorov Hamiltonians, the original-time period on every annulus is an explicit complete elliptic integral and is strictly completely monotone in absolute energy: every derivative has its prescribed alternating sign. The period has no critical orbit, tends to \(\sqrt2\pi/C\) at its center and grows as \((2/C)\log(C^2/(h\sqrt{ab}))\) at the separatrix.
