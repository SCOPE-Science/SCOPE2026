The five uniform solid Platonic polyhedra have an exact, two-parameter scale-free fourth-moment spectrum: the regular tetrahedron and cube share one angular quartic profile, the octahedron has the opposite anisotropy, and the dodecahedron and icosahedron have no quartic angular anisotropy. Their five distinct radial moment ratios quantify information that the previously known qualitative rotational-invariance classification does not determine.
