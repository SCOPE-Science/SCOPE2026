For the planar Duffing map \(F(x,y)=(y,ay-y^3-bx)\) with \(a\ge0\) and \(0<b<1\), all prime-period-two orbits can be classified exactly: one antipodal saddle always exists, and two further cycles exist precisely when \(a>2(1+b)\). Those two are attracting exactly below the sharp bound \(a=\sqrt{[9(1+b)^2+(1-b)^2]/2}\), with an eigenvalue \(-1\) at equality.
