# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The ordinary Morse complex of K3,3 is the acyclic vertex-edge matching complex, equivalently outdegree≤1 directed rooted forests. An independently written enumerator exhausted 4^6 per-vertex choices and found exactly 1791 nonempty simplices, face vector (18,126,432,729,486). Augmented mod-2 boundary ranks (1,17,109,323,406) saturate successively the rational upper bounds given by ∂²=0, proving H̃4(Q) has dimension 80 and all other reduced rational homology vanishes. Exact signed Lefschetz enumeration of all 72 automorphisms reproduced nine conjugacy traces (80,0,2,0,0,5,-4,0,-1), with orientation parity cross-check, and rational character inner products gave irreducible multiplicities (1,2,1,2,4,5,3,4,4). Nine irreducible characters are orthonormal, squared dimensions sum to 72 and weighted multiplicities sum to 80; invariant dimension = (sum traces)/72 =1. Pure side interchange has −4 trace and eigendimensions 38,42. Independent finite enumeration proves this one finite group/complex, not a family. This agrees with the inspected analytic proof and the included verifier design; a VERIFY_OK log alone was not relied on.

## originality

PASS

The full nine-class rational K3,3 character and nine rational irreducible multiplicities survive best-of-knowledge literature comparison. Jojić (2012) already determines the non-equivariant K3,3 80-sphere homotopy type; Lin–Scoville identifies the abstract automorphism group; the published all-m balanced-Kmm theorem already proves the K3,3 pure swap trace −4 and resulting 38/42 eigenspaces. Those components are excluded from any novelty claim. Unlike these earlier or parallel results, the remaining eight class traces and complete module decomposition were not found in read full texts or semantic searches. Related K2,n and K2,4 representation results do not dominate K3,3. No chronology or priority is inferred; unindexed equivariant calculations remain possible.

PASS

K3,3 is the smallest balanced complete bipartite graph with substantial 80-dimensional reduced top homology, a canonical test object for ordinary discrete Morse complexes. Its natural full 72-element automorphism action has a complete rational module and invariant line, beyond the previously known Betti number/group identification and one involution trace; this offers exact equivariant topological data for naturality and comparisons with other graph Morse complexes. The complete module is a motivated natural finite classification rather than an arbitrary isolated statistic.
