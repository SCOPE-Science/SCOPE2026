# Scientific review: rational symmetry module of the \(K_{3,3}\) Morse complex

## Correctness — PASS
The face definition is the ordinary acyclic-gradient-field complex, not a graph matching complex or a chain complex chosen for a configuration space. Two distinct exhaustive constructions both give \((18,126,432,729,486)\) faces by dimension. Full augmented \(\mathbb F_2\)-boundary ranks are \((1,17,109,323,406)\). By induction using \(\partial^2=0\), these saturate the rational upper bounds, proving that only reduced rational \(H_4\) survives and has dimension \(80\). Signed fixed-simplex Lefschetz traces, checked for all \(72\) automorphisms in two ways, produce the nine-class character. The stated nine rational irreducibles are orthonormal and complete by squared-dimension count \(72\). Their multiplicities are exact nonnegative integers and sum dimensionally to \(80\). Standard homological Lefschetz–Euler–Poincaré explains the subtraction of the ordinary \(H_0\)-trace.
