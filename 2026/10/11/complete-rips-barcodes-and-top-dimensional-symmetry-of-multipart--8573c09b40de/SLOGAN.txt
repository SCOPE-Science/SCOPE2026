For every complete multipartite graph \(K_{a_1,\ldots,a_k}\) with \(k\ge2\) and \(a_i\ge2\), its two-layer prism has a full graph-metric Rips barcode given explicitly by part sizes: \(2B\) spheres in degree \(k-1\) at scale one give way to \(B\) spheres in degree \(2k-1\) at scale two, where \(B=\prod_i(a_i-1)\). The scale-two top homology carries the tensor-product augmentation representation, with layer interchange acting as \((-1)^k\).
