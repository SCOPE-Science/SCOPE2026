# Scientific review: Nauru-graph Rips filtration

## Correctness — PASS
The graph is given by explicit cyclic edge formulas and the homotopy statement uses inclusive graph distance. The exact finite checker reproduces its 24 graph-distance rows; the radius-two full face vector \((24,108,120,24)\); both 12-vertex orientable, connected, closed torus triangulations; and the complete 108-step relative reduction. In particular, 96 **actual sequential elementary** collapses leave 12 critical two-cells over a torus, and each critical attaching one-cycle has an explicitly checked integral two-chain filling in that torus. Since \(\pi_1(T^2)=\mathbb Z^2\) and the Hurewicz map in degree one is an isomorphism, these attachments are nullhomotopic. The exact bipartition-distance argument identifies the next scale with the join of the two tori. Standard splitting and inclusion arguments establish the full homotopy and all-field barcode, not only Betti numbers. The reproducible check returned `VERIFY_OK`. Residual correctness limitation: the finite certificate is specific to this 24-vertex graph and does not extend to arbitrary graph families.

## Originality — PASS, with residual search risk
The closest geometric source, Eppstein's *The many faces of the Nauru graph* (2007), already gives the graph's toroidal embedding and its generalized-Petersen and star-transposition descriptions. The known torus drawing is not an original contribution. Kawamura (2011), Theorems 2.2 and 3.3, supplies general bipartite graph-square fundamental-group and homology comparisons; in particular the degree-one torus fundamental group is consistent with existing theory. That source does not exhibit the twelve nullhomotopic two-cell attachments, the exact wedge, the next-scale join, or the barcode. Adamaszek (2011), *Clique complexes and graph powers*, treats graph-power theory and certain high-girth or cyclic families, not this girth-six graph at all radius thresholds. Adamaszek–Adams (2015 preprint) treats circular metrics, not this 24-vertex graph metric. Published index and original-document checks were augmented with equivalence, graph-alias, star-transposition, graph-square, and exact-multiplicity searches. The nearest published calculations of other graph metrics do not imply the stated result. An equivalent proof in unpublished, unindexed, or alternatively named literature remains possible; searches alone cannot certify absolute novelty.

## Value — PASS
This is a complete topological and persistent-homology classification for a canonical cubic symmetric graph, not an arbitrary graph-slice enumeration. The radius-two torus with twelve additional \(S^2\) summands is not determined by knowing that the graph admits a torus embedding or that the bipartite graph square preserves \(\pi_1\). The radius-three join yields homology in three consecutive nonzero degrees, and the bars identify exactly which original graph cycles persist through scale two. The finite collapses and integral attaching certificates can be checked independently and are mathematically informative about how graph-power topology changes.
