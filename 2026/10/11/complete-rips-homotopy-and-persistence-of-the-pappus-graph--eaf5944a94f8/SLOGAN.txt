The graph-metric Vietoris–Rips filtration of the Pappus graph passes through \(\bigvee^{10}S^1\), \(\bigvee^{26}S^2\), and \(\bigvee^{64}S^5\) at successive integral scales, before becoming contractible. A two-color null-attached homotopy pushout accounts for the twenty-six two-spheres, while six distance-four triples account for the sixty-four five-spheres.
