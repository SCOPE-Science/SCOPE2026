For every nondegenerate \(n\)-simplex times a parallelogram, the \((n+3)\)-vertex simplex determinants have only three absolute classes, with exact multiplicities \(H_0,H_1,H_2\) and \(H_2=n(n+1)4^{n-1}\). The largest contained simplex occupies exactly \(2/((n+1)(n+2))\) of the product's volume; all vertex-supported equality cases use complementary square diagonals over two distinct simplex vertices.
