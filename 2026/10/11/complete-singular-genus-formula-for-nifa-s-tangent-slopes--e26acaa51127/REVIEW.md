# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The lower bound follows from the explicitly reconstructed image of \(\lambda\) in Nifa's circle-action representation and the strict Eisenbud--Hirsch--Neumann commutator criterion. The upper bound is obtained by adapting Nifa's branched-cover monodromy construction to boundary cycle type \(N^a r^1\). The Euler-characteristic calculation gives exactly the same integer as the lower bound. The cases \(m=0\), \(|m|=1\), \(r=0\), negative exponents, and low covering degrees are handled separately or explicitly. The arithmetic verifier agrees with the symbolic proof but is not used to replace it.

## Originality
PASS. Nifa's Theorem 6.5 covers only powers of \(\lambda^N\), and Remark 6.8 explicitly says that no formula is asserted for the other primitive tangent slopes. The previously derived stable seminorm for this family gives only stable commutator length and explicitly leaves the finite nondivisible case unresolved. Targeted searches for the source, powers of the preferred longitude, primitive tangent slopes, commutator length, singular genus, and equivalent covering formulations found no statement implying the all-power formula. The closest prior result is therefore strictly weaker.

## Value
PASS. Exact singular slope genus is a natural finite invariant in the foundational slope-genus framework, and the source itself isolates the nondivisible primitive slopes as the portion without a formula. The result gives a complete classification for that natural family and exhibits the finite residue correction lost under stabilization. It is not a parameter renaming or routine recomputation of a published table.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest sources are Nifa's 2026 construction and Liu--Ni--Sun--Wang's general slope-genus framework. Bertram's permutation factorization is proof infrastructure rather than a competing topological statement. The result does not address embedded slope genus, and an unindexed earlier observation of the same mixed-cycle refinement remains a residual originality risk.
