For every prime \(p\ge5\), all nonzero equality cases of the sharp finite-field parabola \(L^2\!\to\!L^6\) inequality are exactly the scalar quadratic characters \(f(t)=\lambda\exp(2\pi i(At+Bt^2)/p)\). There are precisely \(p^2\) extremizers up to nonzero scalar, extending the known sharp norm and constant-modulus necessity to a complete phase classification.
