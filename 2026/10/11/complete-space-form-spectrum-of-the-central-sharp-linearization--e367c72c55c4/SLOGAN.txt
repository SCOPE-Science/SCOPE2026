At a constant-curvature tensor \(R_\kappa\), the central Clifford channel is diagonal on the scalar, traceless-Ricci, and Weyl curvature pieces with eigenvalues \(2(n-2)\kappa\), \((n-4)\kappa\), and \(-2\kappa\). Thus the sharp quadratic linearization has a unique dimension-four traceless-Ricci cancellation, while its Weyl eigenvalue is always \(4\kappa\).
