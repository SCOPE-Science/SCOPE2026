The 495 four-vertex subsets of a regular icosahedron have exactly five tetrahedral determinant levels, with multiplicities 75, 30, 180, 180 and 30. Every contained tetrahedron has volume at most \(2\varphi^3/3\), or \(\varphi/10\) of the icosahedron, and the full equality locus consists of 20 opposite-facet apex families rather than only the 30 vertex-supported maximizers.
