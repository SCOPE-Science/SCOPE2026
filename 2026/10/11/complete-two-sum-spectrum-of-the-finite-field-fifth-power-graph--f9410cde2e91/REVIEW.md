Its collision factorization has two components, \(x=\pm y\) and a nonzero-radius quadratic circle; explicit intersection counting yields \(3(q-1-\chi(-1))\) ordered collisions per nonzero sum. The zero-sum and characteristic-five cases are dealt with separately.
