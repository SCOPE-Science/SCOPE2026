For every finite field of odd order, the twofold sum of the fifth-power graph \(\{(t,t^5)\}\) has a completely explicit representation-multiplicity spectrum governed by \(\chi(-1)\) and \(\chi(-2)\), including a distinct Frobenius subgroup regime in characteristic five. The spectrum yields closed formulas for the exact sumset size, additive energy, and constant-input fourth Fourier-extension moment.
