# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For unit-base right circular cones use V=πt/3, S=π(1+√(1+t²)), M=π(π+t−arctan t), with m=M/π, s=S/π, q=√(m²−4s)>0 (strict 3D Minkowski equality applies only to balls). The cited 2007 Theorem 1 translates its φ± to Ψ±=m³−6ms+8t±q³. With x=t/√(1+t²), m′=x², s′=x and q′=x(mx−2)/q, direct symbolic algebra yields the all-t identities Ψ±′=2+(3/2)[x(m±q)−2]²>0. Independently implemented exact rational interval arithmetic (Fractions, integer-isqrt enclosures, with 333/106<π<355/113) proved Ψ+(1)<0<Ψ+(√3) and Ψ−(tan(2π/5))<0<Ψ−(tan(5π/12)). Thus exactly one zero t2 of Ψ+ occurs in (1,√3) and exactly one zero t1 of Ψ− occurs in (tan(2π/5),tan(5π/12)), with t2<t1. The 2007 published sign theorem then yields Type 2 complex, Type 2 double, three simple real, Type 1 double, Type 1 complex in that order. Independent high-precision numerical root solutions t2≈1.1523939523482100792 and t1≈3.3710843620880922306 have negligible residuals but do not prove uniqueness. The exact symbolic derivative and interval sign certificates, not sampling, establish the claim.

## value

PASS

The right circular cone is a standard, naturally one-parameter family in three-dimensional convex geometry, and the 2009 Hadwiger/Steiner literature explicitly uses it to exhibit contrasting root regimes. A complete exact classification of that family is mathematically motivated: it establishes both unique root-type crossings, rigorously identifies the previously unstated lower Type 2 boundary, and completely describes the intermediate three-real-roots window. The fact that both complicated transition functionals have a universal positive-square derivative is a structural certificate rather than a routine evaluation or an arbitrarily sampled numerical table. The result is a useful self-contained exact family theorem without pretending to settle global Steiner-root geometry or all cones.

The dated certificate retains the supplied scientific assessment, sources and limitations.
