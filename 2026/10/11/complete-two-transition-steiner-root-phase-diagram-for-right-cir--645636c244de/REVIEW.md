# Review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The published cone formulas for \(V\), \(S\), and \(M\) reduce the general three-dimensional root classifiers \(\phi_\pm\) to two scalar functions \(\Psi_\pm(t)\). Differentiation gives the exact identities
\[
\Psi_\pm'(t)=2+\frac32\left(\frac{t}{\sqrt{1+t^2}}(m(t)\pm q(t))-2\right)^2,
\]
so both are strictly increasing on the entire nondegenerate cone family. Exact rational interval arithmetic certifies one sign change for each function using four special aspect ratios with algebraic trigonometric data. The sign classification of \(\phi_\pm\) then yields all five root regimes. The packaged checker independently replays the derivative identity, sign certificates, and numerical root approximations.

## Originality
**PASS, with a residual terminology/indexing risk.** The 2007 full text supplies the general \(\phi_\pm\) classification and explicitly classifies cylinders, but contains no right-circular-cone phase theorem. The 2008 thesis and 2009 full text give the cone coefficients, state qualitatively that all three regimes occur, and quote only the upper threshold near \(3.37108\) for the Type 1 boundary. Searches by both transition decimals, cone/Steiner aliases, Type 1/Type 2 terminology, and the transition functionals found no statement of the lower threshold or the complete two-transition monotonic phase diagram. The residual risk is an unindexed or differently worded derivation.

## Value
**PASS.** This is a natural complete classification of the canonical cone family used in the motivating literature. It replaces a qualitative diagram plus one quoted endpoint by a rigorous all-aspect-ratio theorem, exposes a simple square identity forcing both transition functionals to be monotone, and identifies the previously unstated Type 2 boundary. The result also clarifies exactly which cone aspect ratios have three real Steiner roots.
