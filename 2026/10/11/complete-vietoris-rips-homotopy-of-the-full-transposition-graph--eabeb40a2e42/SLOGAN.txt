For the 24 permutations of \(S_4\), distance measured by the minimum number of arbitrary transpositions yields an inclusive Vietoris–Rips filtration passing through \(\bigvee^{49}S^1\) and \(\bigvee^{33}S^3\) before becoming contractible. A complete 9,904-face acyclic discrete-Morse matching proves the three-sphere wedge.
