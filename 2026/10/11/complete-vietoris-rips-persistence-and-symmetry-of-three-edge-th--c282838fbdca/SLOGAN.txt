For \(m\ge3\), the graph consisting of \(m\) length-three paths between two common poles has a graph-metric Rips filtration that moves from \(m-1\) circles at scale one to \(m-1\) two-spheres at scale two and becomes contractible at scale three. Arm permutations act through the integral augmentation lattice in both degrees, whereas pole interchange acts by \(-1\) on the circles and by \(+1\) on the two-sphere homology.
