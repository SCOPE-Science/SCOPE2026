# Verification

Let
\[
N(s,t)=\max\{|s|,|t|,|s-t|\}.
\]
For active functionals \(f\ne g\), choose their signs so that the third defining functional is \(f+g\). If
\[
u=\frac{x}{N(x)},\qquad v=\frac{y}{N(y)},
\]
and
\[
a=f(u),\quad b=g(u),\quad c=f(v),\quad d=g(v),
\]
then
\[
|a|,\ |b|,\ |a+b|\le1,
\qquad
|c|,\ |d|,\ |c+d|\le1.
\]

For \(r=N(x)\) and \(s=N(y)\), the active branch is the quadratic form with
\[
M=
\begin{pmatrix}
ab&(cb-ad)/2\\
(cb-ad)/2&-cd
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Direct expansion gives
\[
\det M=-\frac{(ad+bc)^2}{4}
\]
and
\[
(\operatorname{tr}M)^2-4\det M
=
(a^2+c^2)(b^2+d^2).
\]
Thus
\[
\rho(M)
=
\frac{|ab-cd|+\sqrt{(a^2+c^2)(b^2+d^2)}}2.
\]

If \(ab\ge cd\),
\[
\rho(M)
\le
\frac14\bigl((a+b)^2+(c-d)^2\bigr)
\le\frac54.
\]
If \(cd\ge ab\),
\[
\rho(M)
\le
\frac14\bigl((a-b)^2+(c+d)^2\bigr)
\le\frac54.
\]
Same-functional branches are bounded by \(1\), so this proves the global upper bound.

Equality in the first branch forces
\[
a=b=\pm\frac12,\qquad c=-d=\pm1,
\]
and equality in the second branch swaps these roles. The equality eigenvector of \(M\) has positive coordinate ratio \(2:1\), giving the forced norm ratio. The dihedral isometries generated by
\[
R(s,t)=(s-t,s),\qquad S(s,t)=(t,s)
\]
reduce all equality configurations to
\[
x=r(0,1),\qquad
y=r\left(-\frac12,-\frac14\right).
\]

For this representative,
\[
N(x)=r,\qquad N(y)=\frac r2,
\qquad
N(x+y)=N(x-y)=\frac{5r}{4},
\]
so the quotient is exactly \(5/4\).

The determinant and discriminant identities were independently expanded symbolically. No finite enumeration, numerical optimization, or external certificate is used as proof.
