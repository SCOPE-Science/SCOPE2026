The harmonic cubics \(z^3+az+b\overline z-1\) with \(a,b>0\) have exactly \(3\), \(5\), or \(7\) distinct zeros, determined everywhere—including singular transition curves—by explicit cubic discriminant and real-axis collision inequalities. The seven-zero phase is open and occurs even for \(a>b\); the full classification extends beyond previously established coefficient-separated regimes.
