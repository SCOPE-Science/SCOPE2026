# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof was reconstructed from the lead source's Steiner parameterization, joint convex support, Borell--Brascamp--Lieb marginal step, and reflection identity. Keeping the actual \(\gamma\)-concavity of the density through the same marginalization over \(nm\) variables gives the exact exponent \(s_\gamma=\gamma/(1+nm\gamma)\). The critical, logarithmic, and \(\gamma=\infty\) limits agree with the standard Borell--Brascamp--Lieb cases. Evenness then converts power concavity into monotonicity in \(|t|\). The centered-ball specialization is legitimate because restricted Lebesgue measure has constant density on a convex rotationally invariant support. No finite experiment substitutes for proof.

## Originality
PASS. Marín Sola's theorem states the endpoint generalized Petty inequality and his proof applies the marginal argument after lowering to the critical density exponent. The checked text does not state the resulting \(s_\gamma\)-concavity of the complete Steiner parameter profile. Lin's earlier theorem is likewise an endpoint Steiner comparison for the classical operator. Checked Brunn--Minkowski inequalities for polar projection bodies concern Minkowski addition rather than the Steiner chord. Targeted searches over concavity, shadow-system, \((L_p,Q)\), and truncation-profile aliases found no checked result implying the present theorem.

## Value
PASS. The theorem upgrades a family of endpoint inequalities to a quantitative interpolation principle with the natural Borell exponent. For Lebesgue measure it gives a genuine Brunn--Minkowski-strength law: the \(1/(nm)\)-th root of the generalized polar projection volume is concave along the full Steiner chord. The same statement simultaneously controls every centered radial truncation, giving distribution-level information useful for stability and equality analysis rather than only a total-volume comparison.

## Closest literature and limitations
The principal source is F. Marín Sola, arXiv:2503.00949 / DOI:10.1112/blms.70187. Y. Lin, arXiv:2102.07103, supplies the closest classical endpoint Steiner comparison. D. Zou, W. Li, and D. Li, DOI:10.1007/s12220-026-02365-5, gives a Brunn--Minkowski inequality for polar projection bodies under a different input operation. The present result is limited to the source's Steiner linear parameter system and does not establish strictness, equality cases, or set inclusion. A differently indexed historical shadow-system formulation remains a residual originality risk.
