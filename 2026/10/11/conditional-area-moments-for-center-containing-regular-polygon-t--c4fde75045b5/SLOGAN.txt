For triangles sampled uniformly from distinct vertices of a regular polygon, the conditional first and second area moments given strict containment of the center have exact closed forms for every order where the conditioning event is nonempty (the square is excluded). The conditional area variance approaches \(3/8-3/\pi^2\), with a leading \(-3/(4n)\) correction for even orders but only an \(n^{-2}\) correction for odd orders.
