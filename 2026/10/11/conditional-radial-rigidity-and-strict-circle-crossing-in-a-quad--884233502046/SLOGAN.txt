For the Gotthans–Petržela equilibrium-circle flow, every compact stationary state has exact conditional radial mean square \(\mathbb E[x^{2}+y^{2}\mid z-dx^{2}/(2a)]=r^{2}\), independent of its other coefficients. Unless supported entirely on the equilibrium circle, it must occupy both radial sides and has \(\operatorname{Cov}(x,x^{2}+y^{2})=-a\mathbb E[z^{2}]\ne0\); every bounded forward orbit also has radial time-mean square tending to \(r^{2}\).
