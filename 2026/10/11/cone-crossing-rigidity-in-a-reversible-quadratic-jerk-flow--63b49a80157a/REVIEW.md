# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The polynomial identity \(\dot P=(a+1)x^2-y^2\), unique equilibrium, and obstruction to a nonstationary trajectory lying on the cone were independently reconstructed and symbolically checked. The convergence and sector-crossing results follow from compact limit-set and monotonicity arguments; the conditioned stationary law follows by testing \(H(P)\) against any compact invariant probability. No simulation is used as an infinite-time certificate.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** The complete 2015 original paper (DOI 10.1142/S0218127415500789, Eq. (11)) and 2014 related full text (DOI 10.1142/S0218127414500096) were inspected. The original presents a numerical chaotic attractor at \(a=1.3\), but not the all-parameter exclusion theorem or conditional moment statement. Other located quadratic and jerk balance laws apply to different vector fields, and their statements do not imply these conclusions for Eq. (11). Unindexed or conjugate earlier work remains a possible priority risk.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The negative-parameter half-line is rigorously excluded from nonstationary bounded recurrence, while all surviving bounded complete motions must cross both sectors of a parameter-determined cone. The conditioned RMS identity offers an exact independent mathematical benchmark for stationary distributions of the very same flow. The result makes no claim that nontrivial compact recurrence exists for \(a>-1\).
