For Sprott's reversible quadratic jerk flow, \(P=z+xy\) has derivative \((a+1)x^2-y^2\), forcing every bounded forward orbit to converge to the origin when \(a\le-1\). Above that boundary, every nonstationary bounded complete orbit crosses both sides of the exact quadratic cone, and every compact stationary state satisfies a height-conditioned mean-square identity.
