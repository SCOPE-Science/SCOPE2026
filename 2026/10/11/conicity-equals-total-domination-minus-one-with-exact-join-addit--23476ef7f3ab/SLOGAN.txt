For every finite nonempty simplicial complex \(K\), the maximal conicity satisfies \(\kappa(K)=\widetilde\gamma(K)-1\), where \(\widetilde\gamma\) is simplicial total domination. Consequently maximal conicity is exactly additive under joins in the shifted form \(\kappa(K*L)=\kappa(K)+\kappa(L)+1\), upgrading the standard join lower bound to equality.
