**PASS.** The claim follows from two applications of Hanner's inequality for \(q\ge2\), followed each time by the sharp two-coordinate norm comparison \(a^2+b^2\le2^{1-2/q}(a^q+b^q)^{2/q}\). The explicit Hanner pair \(2^{-1/q}(e_1+e_2)\), \(2^{-1/q}(e_1-e_2)\) attains both estimates simultaneously. Endpoint checks at \(q=2\), \(\lambda=0,1\), and the symmetric parameter \(\lambda=1/2\) are consistent. Risk: the proof is restricted to finite \(q\) and real scalars; these restrictions are explicit.

The result also joins continuously to the Hilbert and symmetric-parameter benchmarks. Risk: it does not classify all extremizers or extend beyond \(\ell_q\).
