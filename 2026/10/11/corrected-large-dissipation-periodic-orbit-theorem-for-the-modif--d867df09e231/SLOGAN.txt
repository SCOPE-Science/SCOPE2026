The published large-\(\varepsilon\) reduction of the modified Nosé–Hoover oscillator uses a \(z\)-nullcline that is not invariant; its only complete full-system trajectory is the equilibrium. A Fenichel-plus-averaging repair proves that for all sufficiently large \(\varepsilon\) the full system nevertheless has an asymptotically stable periodic orbit converging to \(x^2+y^2=4/3\), with slow-manifold correction \(z=\varepsilon^{-1}(y^2-1)+2xy/(\alpha\varepsilon^2)+O(\varepsilon^{-3})\).
