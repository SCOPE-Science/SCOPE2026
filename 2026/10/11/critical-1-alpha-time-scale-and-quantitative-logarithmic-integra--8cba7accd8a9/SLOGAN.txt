For \(\dot y=C(1-y^\alpha)\), the critical observation scale is \(t\asymp1/\alpha\): slower power scales collapse to \(0\), the critical scale converges to the logarithmic-integral flow, and faster power scales saturate at \(1\). At the critical scale the approximation has an exact inverse-function bracket and a first correction \(-\frac{\alpha}{2}Y(-\log Y)\).
