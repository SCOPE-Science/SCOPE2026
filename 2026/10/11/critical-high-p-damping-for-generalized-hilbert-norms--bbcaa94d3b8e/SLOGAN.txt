In the exact weighted-Bergman norm regime, the high-\(p\) generalized Hilbert norm has a uniform all-\(b\) digamma law. The parameter \(b\) cancels the usual linear high-\(p\) growth only near the critical scale \(b\asymp p^{\,p/(\alpha+2)}\), with a one-parameter finite-norm transition across exponential-in-\(p\) factors.
