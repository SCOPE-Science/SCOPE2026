In the exponential-memory power blow-up system, a three-term energy subtraction has a sharp resonance at the quintic power \(p=5\): its remaining drift is a universal nonzero multiple of \(\log v\), whereas it is polynomial for \(3<p<5\) and convergent for \(p>5\). Subtracting that last drift yields a finite trajectory invariant \(K\), and an exact positive comparison formula shows that \(K\) strictly orders initial phase velocities at each fixed positive memory level.
