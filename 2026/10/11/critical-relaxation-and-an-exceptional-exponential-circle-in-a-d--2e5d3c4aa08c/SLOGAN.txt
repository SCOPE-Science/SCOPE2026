At the critical weak-edge weight of a repulsive three-oscillator triangle, the discrete phase map has a full-measure attracting phase class for sufficiently small steps, but an exact invariant circle converges exponentially while all other attracted states relax algebraically. Their centered inverse-square distance has the sharp step-dependent law \(u_n^{-2}=hn+(3h-1)\log(n)/4+C+o(1)\), with cancellation of the logarithmic correction at \(h=1/3\).
