# Scientific review of critical gestation-delay predator extinction

## Correctness — PASS

The specified positive-parameter three-level food chain has global positive bounded solutions for every nonnegative continuous history with strictly positive present values and every fixed finite nonnegative delay. When the intermediate predator's conversion parameter obeys beta<d, comparison with a stable scalar delay equation gives exponential decay of that predator and then of the top predator. At equality beta=d, the nonnegative critical mass

\[P(t)=y(t)+d\int_{t-\tau_1}^{t}y(s)\,ds\]

has the exact derivative

\[P'=-\frac{dm\,y(t-\tau_1)^2}{x(t-\tau_1)+my(t-\tau_1)}-\frac{\theta yz}{v+py+qz}\le0.\]

The resulting square-integrability and bounded derivative imply y(t)→0; scalar delay comparison then gives exponential top-predator extinction. Under r>b+alpha/m, logistic upper and lower bounds imply x(t)→X=(r-b)/a>0. The normalised shifted solutions y(t+s)/P(t) have bounded entire limits; a scalar-delay Liouville theorem gives y/P→1/(1+d*tau1). The reciprocal mass derivative therefore tends to dm/[X(1+d*tau1)^2], proving the exact tail t*y(t)→X(1+d*tau1)/(dm), including tau1=0. The given beta>d rational equilibrium has Jacobian stable on its positive-prey/intermediate-predator coordinates and negative top-predator eigenvalue. Exact algebraic checks corroborate these computations but finite diagnostics do not establish the infinite-time theorem.

## Originality — PASS (best-of-knowledge)

Wang, Shao and Chai (AIMS Mathematics, 2023, DOI 10.3934/math.2023378) introduce the exact model. Their Theorem 5.2 establishes the strict beta<d local predator-free stability criterion; Appendix (A.1) yields a neutral characteristic root at beta=d. Neither statement entails the global equality-case extinction mechanism, a universal positive t^-1 tail, or its exact gestation-delay coefficient. The full primary article and its relevant equations, theorem, and appendix were compared materially. Tian and Xu (2011, DOI 10.15388/NA.16.2.14109) study a distinct nondelayed stage-structured system, whose global extinction theorem does not imply the present delayed result. A separate result on the same exact food-chain equations controls top-predator-weighted means of hypothesized positive periodic cycles; it says nothing about global critical trajectories. Another related global extinction result applies to a different stage-structured Crowley–Martin model at a strict threshold. The exact mathematical-record search match is the present finding and supplies no independent prior priority evidence.

Published Shao–Kong (2022, DOI 10.3390/math10183378) and Shao (2022, DOI 10.1016/j.matcom.2022.04.013) abstracts describe other two-species delayed systems. Their full mathematics was not accessible in this comparison, so no whole-document claim of noncoverage is made. A differently titled or unindexed equivalent treatment cannot be ruled out. This is a bounded best-of-knowledge statement, not a guaranteed priority or originality certification.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The original local characteristic equation becomes nonhyperbolic precisely at beta=d. The nonnegative delay-compensated mass closes this endpoint for every strictly positive initial history and every finite fixed delay. The rigorous universal t^-1 coefficient is positive, depends linearly on the intermediate predator's gestation delay, and is insensitive to separate fear and top-predator parameters at leading order. The stable beta>d example shows the extinction threshold is sharp as a parameter-uniform sufficient condition. This is a mathematically motivated exact critical-relaxation theorem, not a numerical approximation or a restatement of the known strict local stability criterion.

## Scope and evidence

The exact coefficient additionally assumes r>b+alpha/m. No prey limit outside that margin, supercritical global classification, stochastic forcing, dispersal, or time-varying delay statement is made. Proof components are the exact nonlinear mass identity, scalar logistic/delay comparisons, the bounded-entire delay equation, reciprocal-mass asymptotics, and the exact rational stable example. Model sources include Wang–Shao–Chai (2023) DOI 10.3934/math.2023378; Tian–Xu (2011) DOI 10.15388/NA.16.2.14109; Shao–Kong (2022) DOI 10.3390/math10183378; and Shao (2022) DOI 10.1016/j.matcom.2022.04.013. The last two full texts were not inspected, and accessible source limitations remain explicit.
