For the signed-chain polytope of the Boolean inclusion lattice \(B_d\), every center of a largest Euclidean ball is constant on each subset-rank, and the rank-values form exactly the central cube section \(\sum_{k=0}^{d}t_k=0\), \(|t_k|\le1-\sqrt{d/(d+1)}\). The inradius is \(1/\sqrt{d+1}\), and the \(d\)-dimensional incenter locus has an exact parity-dependent vertex count.
