# Review
## Correctness
PASS. The source's small-\(a\) boundary formula is valid on a neighborhood of \(0\). The spectral equations give analytic principal Lambert-\(W\) branches, and exact series substitution yields
\[
\beta(a)=\sqrt2-\frac13a^3-\frac{\sqrt2}{24}a^4+O(a^5).
\]
The sign comparison for \(b(a)-\beta(a)\) then follows directly. The existence statement on the lower side uses the source's sharpness theorem, not a numerical extrapolation.
