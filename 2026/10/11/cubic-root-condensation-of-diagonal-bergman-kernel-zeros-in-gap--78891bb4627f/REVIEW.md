# Scientific assessment

**Correctness.** The exact Edholm–Mathew diagonal numerator formula and three interval sums give the gap-three reciprocal sextic in both coprime residue classes. Its Joukowski cubic has the stated strictly negative discriminant and a real root below −2. The double-root rescaling at u=−1 gives the nonreal root perturbation ±i√2/k, yielding the sharp radial factor √(2/3)/k and n(1−|s|)→√6. These identities are algebraic and analytic; finite numerical checks are corroborative, not proofs.

**Originality, qualified.** The previously established gap-three unit-circle nonvanishing and the count of three interior zeros are not original contributions. The refined root-type classification and, especially, the sharp first-order boundary-migration coefficient were not found in the inspected Edholm–Mathew paper or other relevant published sources. A separate prior gap-three proof mentioned in its Section 4.3 was not available in full; it could overlap the finer claim.

**Scientific value.** The exact rate separates two zero levels converging to the punctured-disk boundary from one root persisting in the interior as the rational domain parameter approaches one. This is a natural quantitative question about a fixed diagonal slice of a Bergman kernel, not a classification of its entire zero variety.

**Sources and limitations.** Edholm–Mathew, *Arithmetic properties and zeros of the Bergman kernel on a class of quotient domains*, arXiv:2505.20489 / DOI 10.1007/s00209-026-04059-8, particularly Theorems 1.3, 1.5, 1.7 and Section 4.3; MathDB problem 368572. The full status of the unlinked gap-three proof remains an explicit priority risk. No expert certification or formal proof verification is asserted.
