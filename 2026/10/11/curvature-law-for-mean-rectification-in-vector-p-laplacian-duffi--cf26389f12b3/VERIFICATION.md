# Verification

The cited Proposition 3.1 gives \(u_\varepsilon-\alpha=\varepsilon^{1/(p-1)}w+o(\varepsilon^{1/(p-1)})\) in \(C^1\), with \(\bar w=0\). Uniform convergence therefore gives the corresponding second- and third-moment orders componentwise.

For \(g_j(s)=b_js^3+c_js^2+d_js\), the Taylor identity through degree three is exact. Substitution into the compatibility identity and division by the simple-root slope \(g_j'(\alpha_j)\neq0\) yields the stated coefficient with no omitted term of the same order.
