Let \(C\) be a smooth connected compact curve and let
\[
K_C\subset\mathcal M(C)
\]
be generated by ambient meromorphic restrictions.

If ambient meromorphic functions separate points of \(C\), then \(K_C\) separates points. Choose nonconstant
\[
u\in K_C.
\]
Then
\[
[\mathcal M(C):\mathbb C(u)]<\infty,
\]
hence
\[
[\mathcal M(C):K_C]<\infty.
\]
The corresponding finite map
\[
\pi:C\to Y
\]
has degree
\[
[\mathcal M(C):K_C].
\]
If this degree were larger than one, a generic fiber would contain distinct points indistinguishable by every element of \(K_C\). Therefore
\[
K_C=\mathcal M(C).
\]

Every
\[
h\in K_C
\]
is a rational expression in finitely many ambient restrictions. The same rational expression in the ambient meromorphic functions has restriction \(h\), so equality of fields gives genuine meromorphic extension.

For Picard positivity, a nonconstant ambient restriction has a regular fiber meeting \(C\) in a nonempty effective divisor. The associated ambient divisor line bundle therefore has strictly positive degree on \(C\).

These arguments use exact function-field and divisor facts; no numerical experiment or finite sampling is involved.
