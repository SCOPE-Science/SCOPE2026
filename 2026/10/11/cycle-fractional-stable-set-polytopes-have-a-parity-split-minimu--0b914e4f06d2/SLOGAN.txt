The minimum enclosing sphere of \(\operatorname{FRAC}(C_n)\) has a sharp parity split: for even cycles every vertex is a contact on the sphere centered at \(\mathbf1/2\), while for odd cycles only maximum stable sets are contacts. The odd cycle\'s unique half-integral vertex \(\mathbf1/2\) lies strictly inside, only \(1/(2\sqrt n)\) from the center.
