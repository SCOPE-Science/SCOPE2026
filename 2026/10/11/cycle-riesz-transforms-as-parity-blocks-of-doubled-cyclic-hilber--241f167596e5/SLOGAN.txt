On a finite cycle, the forward difference--Laplacian Riesz transform is an exact square root of a translated negative identity on mean-zero functions, and the cyclic Hilbert transform on the doubled cycle is precisely its two-by-two parity block extension. This gives a half-grid cotangent kernel and the exact common endpoint norm \(\frac{2}{N}\sum_{r<\lfloor N/2\rfloor}\cot((2r+1)\pi/(2N))\), with a parity-dependent \(N^{-2}\) correction.
