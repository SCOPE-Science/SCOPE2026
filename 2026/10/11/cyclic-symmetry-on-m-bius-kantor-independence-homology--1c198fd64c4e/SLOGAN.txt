Let \(r\) be the order-eight automorphism \(u_i\mapsto u_{i+1}\), \(v_i\mapsto v_{i+1}\) of the Möbius–Kantor graph \(M=G(8,3)\). As \(\mathbb Q[C_8]\)-modules, \(H_3(\operatorname{Ind}(M);\mathbb Q)\cong \mathbf 1\oplus\varepsilon\oplus V_4\) and \(H_4(\operatorname{Ind}(M);\mathbb Q)\cong\varepsilon\), where \(\varepsilon(r)=-1\) and \(V_4\) is the two-dimensional rational irreducible on which \(r\) has minimal polynomial \(x^2+1\). Equivalently, for \(k=0,\ldots,7\), the two characters on \(r^k\) are \((4,0,0,0,4,0,0,0)\) and \((1,-1,1,-1,1,-1,1,-1)\), respectively.
