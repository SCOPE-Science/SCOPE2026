The convex hull of a polynomial's critical points equals the intersection of zero hulls sampled at its critical values for every quadratic and cubic, but not for all quartics. The explicit monic quartic \(p(z)=z^4+\frac{32}{15}z^3+2z^2+\frac{32}{5}z\) has \(\frac2{21}\) in every critical-value fiber hull although all of its critical points lie in the closed left half-plane.
