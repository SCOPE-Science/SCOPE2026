Among monic polynomials whose zeros are confined to the fixed antipodal pair \(\{-1,1\}\), equal multiplicities minimize the filled unit-lemniscate area through degree five, but degree six is the first failure: the unique minimizing allocations are \(2:4\) and \(4:2\), with area strictly below the balanced value \(2\). The same imbalance obstruction persists in every degree divisible by six.
