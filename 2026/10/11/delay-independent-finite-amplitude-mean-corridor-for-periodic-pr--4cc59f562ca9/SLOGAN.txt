Every strictly positive nonconstant periodic orbit of the Wu–Zheng delayed predator–prey model obeys the exact finite-amplitude law \(\bar v=((m+\alpha\beta+\bar u)(1-\bar u)-\operatorname{Var}_T(u))/s\) and a delay-independent corridor for \(\bar u\). In the source's Case 2, every such orbit has \(18/25<\bar u<(29+\sqrt{14806})/175=0.8610280454586158\ldots\), while the coexistence prey level is \((11+\sqrt{5441})/100=0.8476313442364011\ldots\).
