# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reconstructs the printed delayed equations, fixes all quantifiers and positivity assumptions, and derives the identities by exact period integration. Translation invariance of the period average handles the delayed prey term. Two rational algebra identities give the strict mean defect and phase relation. The zero-defect case is checked against both differential equations and collapses to the equilibrium. The bundled checker reproduces the exact numerical coefficients.

## Originality
PASS. The closest statement in the source is equation (15), a period-integral relation for the nondelayed planar model. The delayed section instead develops characteristic roots, stability switches, Hopf normal forms and simulations. Searches for the source DOI/title together with period mean, variance-defect, covariance, autocovariance and delay aliases did not locate the all-amplitude delayed identity. Related 2024 and 2025 additive-predation extensions change the governing equations and do not imply this exact statement. Search coverage is not exhaustive, which remains a stated residual risk.

## Value
PASS. The paper’s delayed model is used precisely to generate and classify oscillatory coexistence. The finding constrains every positive periodic coexistence state, not only near a Hopf point: prey mean is fixed at \(d/\beta\), predator mean is strictly depressed from equilibrium, and the deficit is exactly the weighted sum of current predator–prey covariance and lagged prey autocovariance. This gives a direct finite-amplitude diagnostic for simulated or computed cycles and a delay-specific interpretation of phase changes.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is Bai–Zhang (2022) itself because its nondelayed equation (15) already contains part of the period-average mechanism. A prior own-ledger theorem for a different nondelayed predator–prey system also uses a rational mean-defect identity; it does not contain a delay or lagged autocovariance. The present finding is therefore accepted for the exact delay-specific phase–yield coupling, not for the mean-defect motif alone. The accepted claim is narrower in object but stronger for the delayed system: it adds the exact variance defect and lagged covariance relation for every positive periodic orbit. Later additive-predation papers with Smith growth or predator self-competition use different equations. The theorem does not establish periodic-orbit existence, stability, uniqueness or bifurcation direction and does not cover boundary cycles with zero predators.
