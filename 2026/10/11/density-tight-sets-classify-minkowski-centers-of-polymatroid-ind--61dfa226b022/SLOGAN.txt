For every finite full-dimensional polymatroid independence polytope, Minkowski asymmetry is the maximum ratio of summed singleton capacities to a subset capacity; all minimizing centers are an explicit residual submodular packing polytope indexed by the unique largest density-tight set. In particular, a loopless matroid has asymmetry equal to its already-known fractional arboricity, and its minimizing center is unique exactly when the matroid is uniformly dense.
