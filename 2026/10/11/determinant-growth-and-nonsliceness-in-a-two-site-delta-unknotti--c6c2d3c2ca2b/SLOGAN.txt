Nakamura's two-site delta-unknotting family \(K_b=M(0;(3b+5,-3),(8b+13,8),(8b+13,8))\) has exact determinant \((8b+13)(24b+41)\) for every \(b\ge-1\). The determinants strictly increase and are never squares, so the family is pairwise nonisotopic and every member is algebraically and topologically nonslice.
