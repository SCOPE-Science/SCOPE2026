For every cardinality-weighted star hypergraphic polytope with positive edge weight, the squared ambient Euclidean diameter is exactly \(T^2+\sum_j a_j^2\), and every diameter pair joins the all-hub vertex to a permutation of the full leaf-weight vector. Throughout the natural \(m\)-graph interpolation from zonotope to stellohedron, the number of diametral pairs jumps from one to \(r!\) at \(m=2\), while the diameter follows one explicit binomial-sum formula.
