For every coprime pair of positive integers with difference five, the degree-ten palindromic diagonal Bergman numerator of the corresponding rational Hartogs triangle is strictly positive after its unit-circle phase is removed. A four-residue cubic identity and an exact rational Bernstein certificate give five zeros inside the punctured disk and hence genuine Bergman-kernel zeros, settling the difference-five case of the Edholm–Mathew nonvanishing conjecture.
