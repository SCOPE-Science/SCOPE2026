For the discrete-Morse complex of every wheel \(C_k*K_1\) with \(k\ge5\), the entire top rational \(D_k\)-character is determined: it is \(k^3\) at the identity, zero at nonidentity rotations and even-rim reflections, and \((-1)^{(k-1)/2}\) at odd-rim reflections. Equivalently, the representation is a multiple of the regular module, plus an odd-rim sign-twisted permutation summand when \(k\) is odd.
