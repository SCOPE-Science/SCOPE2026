For measure-induced Cesàro operators on weighted Bergman spaces of the complex unit ball of dimension \(n\ge2\), Hilbert–Schmidt membership has a sharp three-regime transition at \(\beta=n/2\), with a new logarithmic penalty exactly at the transition. The membership test depends only on the moment sequence, \(n\), and \(\beta\), not on the Bergman weight exponent; power-logarithmic measures exhibit an exact double-critical endpoint.
