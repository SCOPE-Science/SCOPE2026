# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The unique support functional at each real \(L_p\) unit vector gives the exact reverse residual. For \(p>2\), an arbitrary orthogonal unit pair induces a zero-mean scalar variable whose positive and negative size-biased moment inequalities compress it to a two-point mean-zero law. Conditioning away any zero atom can only raise the normalized reverse residual. Two disjoint measurable sets attain every scalar ratio, and strict equality in the Jensen steps characterizes all maximizers. Hölder conjugacy transfers the result and equality classification to \(1<p<2\). The Hilbert and cubic specializations, endpoints of the scalar maximization, and normalizations were checked. Finite symbolic/numerical tests corroborate, but do not replace, the proof.

## Originality
PASS for the **dimension-free real \(L_p\) reduction and full equality classification**, not for the already obtained two-dimensional cubic constant. The primary 2020 approximate-symmetry full text and the 2022/2023 sequence-space/direct-sum full texts were inspected for same-object parameter implications. They treat the C framework, exact orthogonality, qualitative pointwise symmetry and approximate smoothness, without deriving the arbitrary-distribution two-atom inequality. Literature-record searches included the defining C term, aliases, exact p=3 value, two-coordinate reductions, and competing Dragomir symmetry. A plausible 2018 paper introducing global approximate symmetry and a related constant was available only through its abstract; this remains an unresolved bibliographic risk rather than a claim of full-document noncoverage.

## Value
PASS. The proof resolves a natural infinite-dimensional quantitative invariance question: neither additional coordinates nor nonatomic support can worsen the optimal approximate symmetry parameter. It gives a sharp explicit scalar variational formula, realizes the bound on any nontrivial measure space, and classifies equality configurations by two opposite-sign ratio values. The measure-theoretic moment compression is an independent structural theorem beyond a planar calculation.

## Closest literature, limits and risks
Chmieliński, Stypuła and Wójcik establish the real support-functional criterion; Chmieliński and Wójcik introduce the global approximate-symmetry constant; Chmieliński, Khurana and Sain develop local/global symmetry criteria. Bose, Roy and Sain characterize orthogonality and exact left/right symmetry in sequence spaces, and Bose develops the support geometry of direct sums. These results were compared by implication, not only by wording. The theorem assumes real sigma-finite \(L_p\) with \(1<p<\infty\); no complex, endpoint, or vector-valued extension is claimed. The full 2018 predecessor was not accessible in this run, and unindexed equivalent formulas remain an originality risk.
