For every real \(L_p\) space of dimension at least two with \(1<p<\infty\), the optimal global Chmieliński \(C\)-approximate Birkhoff-symmetry parameter is the same as in \(\ell_p^2\) and is given by one explicit scalar maximum. A size-biased two-atom reduction proves that every non-Hilbert equality pair has exactly two opposite-sign ratio levels, even on nonatomic measure spaces.
