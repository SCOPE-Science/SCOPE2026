For the two-species annular long-range segregation limit, increasing the ambient dimension always moves the inner free boundary strictly toward the inner wall. Quantitatively, with \(p=n-2\), its offset is asymptotic to \(\frac{M_1}{M_2}\frac{R_1}{p}(\frac{R_1}{R_1+R})^p\), so the interface is exponentially pinned in high dimension.
