# Same-model scientific review
## Correctness
PASS. The Dini condition implies finiteness and small-scale vanishing of the dyadic tail \(\Omega(r)=\sum_{n\ge0}\omega(2^{-n}r)\). Reconstructing Wen's Section 3 shows that Hölder regularity is used in exactly two ways: a one-step interior estimate in Lemma 3.1 and summability of stable/unstable roof differences in Lemma 3.2 and the final product estimate. Continuity with modulus \(\omega\) gives the first, while \(\Omega\) gives the second. The uniqueness argument then carries over unchanged after the generalized stable/unstable coordinate formulas are established.

The quantifiers, positivity of the roof, the lower roof bound \(m>0\), the Bowen--Walters normalization, and the forward/backward tails were checked. No finite computation is used as an infinite proof.

## Originality
PASS. Wen's 2026 theorem assumes a Hölder roof and does not state a Dini or summable-modulus extension. The closest published finding about the same example establishes a cohomological obstruction for the logarithmic roof and gives examples showing that non-Hölder regularity alone is not decisive; it does not imply a general Dini sufficient theorem. published-finding corpus searches for Dini roofs, summable modulus, summable variation, Walters conditions, stable time holonomy, and local product structure found no statement implying the final theorem.

The main residual risk is terminological: an equivalent sufficient criterion may exist in symbolic-flow literature under Walters or summable-variation language without being surfaced by the searches.

## Value
PASS. The source places a Hölder sufficient theorem next to a logarithmic continuous counterexample. The new result identifies the quantitative mechanism between them: uniform summability of stable and unstable roof variations. Dini continuity is a natural, strictly weaker regularity class than Hölder continuity, so the result enlarges the positive theorem beyond a cosmetic reparameterization and explains why the source counterexample fails at the level of time-holonomy tails.

## Closest literature and limitations
Closest literature is Xiao Wen, “Local Product Structure of Expansive Flows with Shadowing Property,” arXiv:2608.26561v1, together with the published published-finding corpus finding “A cohomological obstruction behind a non-Hölder suspension without local product structure.” The theorem here is a sufficient-condition extension only; it does not claim Dini continuity is necessary or classify all continuous roofs with local product structure.
