Every finite connected nonbipartite square-free graph \(G\) has a Lovász neighborhood complex that simplicially collapses, for any chosen neighbor root at each vertex, to an explicit connected graph on \(V(G)\). If \(G\) has \(n\) vertices and \(e\) edges, this gives \(\mathcal N(G)\simeq\bigvee^{2e-2n+1}S^1\).
