For the dodecahedral graph \(D\), the two-particle unordered configuration space has rational Betti numbers \((1,12,36)\), and its full \(A_5\times C_2\)-action is determined explicitly. In particular, \(H_1\cong V_6^+\oplus(\mathbf 1\oplus V_5)^-\) and \(H_2\cong(V_4\oplus V_5\oplus V_6)^+\oplus(V_4\oplus V_5\oplus2V_6)^-\).
