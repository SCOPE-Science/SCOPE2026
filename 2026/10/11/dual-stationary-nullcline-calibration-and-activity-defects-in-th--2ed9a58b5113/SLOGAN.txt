Every compact stationary positive Gray–Scott kinetic state satisfies the exact fiberwise nullcline laws \(\mathbb E[v^2\mid u]=F(1-u)/u\) and \(\mathbb E[u\mid v]=(F+k)/v\). Their residuals are exactly kinetic speeds, and the two calibrations confine every such stationary state to the classical positive-equilibrium side of the saddle-node discriminant.
