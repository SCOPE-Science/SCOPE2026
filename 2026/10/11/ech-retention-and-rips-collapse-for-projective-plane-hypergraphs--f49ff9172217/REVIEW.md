# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The order complex is exactly the point-line incidence graph, whose connected cycle rank is \(q^3\). For the Čech side, the associated incidence complex admits an explicit linewise acyclic matching because every face with at least two point vertices belongs to one unique line; the critical cells form a connected one-dimensional Morse model with cycle rank \(q^3\). For the Rips side, every singleton hyperedge is a dominated vertex and deleting all of them leaves the simplex on the line hyperedges. The proof applies to every finite projective plane, not only Desarguesian examples.

## Originality
PASS. Exact searches under projective-plane, point-line-hypergraph, order-complex, Čech, pairwise-intersection Rips, incidence-complex, and contractibility formulations found no checked source stating the theorem. Jost–Zhang provide the general framework and warn that Čech and Rips need not preserve hypergraph topology, but do not compute this family. Cook–Migliore–Nagel–Zanello study the same associated incidence complex algebraically but do not give this homotopy comparison. The closest database result is a graph-metric Rips theorem on the incidence graph, which is not the same construction and does not imply the present claim.

## Value
PASS. The family gives a natural, all-orders boundary example for two standard hypergraph complexes: Čech retains all \(q^3\) one-dimensional homotopy generators while pairwise Rips collapses to a point. This quantifies the qualitative distinction emphasized in the foundational hypergraph-topology source and is not an arbitrary finite slice.

## Closest literature and limitations
The primary source is Jost–Zhang, arXiv:2003.06021v1, whose Section 5 supplies the hypergraph realization and the order/Čech/Rips framework. Cook et al., arXiv:1503.04335, define the associated projective-plane incidence complex. The theorem is restricted to the singleton-plus-line hypergraph and does not address graph-metric Rips persistence or embedded homology. Search cannot exclude every unindexed source.
