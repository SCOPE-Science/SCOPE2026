The ordinary four-cut complex of the triangular graph \(J(5,2)\) is a non-shellable pure five-dimensional complex homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{11}S^3\). Its natural \(S_5\)-action on rational third homology splits as \(S^{(3,2)}\oplus S^{(2,2,1)}\oplus\operatorname{sgn}\), so no nonzero homology class is fixed by all graph automorphisms.
