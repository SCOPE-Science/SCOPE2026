# Review of Equal-product classification of four partially shared derivative values

## Correctness

PASS. The inspected source proof identifies two partially shared values
\[
a,b
\]
as the Picard exceptional values and derives
\[
f'=\kappa(f-a)(f-b),
\qquad
\kappa\ne0.
\]
The other two partially shared values
\[
c,d
\]
are attained, hence they are the two roots of
\[
\kappa(y-a)(y-b)-y=0.
\]
Vieta gives
\[
cd=ab.
\]

Conversely, if
\[
ab=cd,
\]
then
\[
\kappa=(c+d-a-b)^{-1}
\]
is well defined for four distinct values. The displayed Möbius--exponential formula satisfies
\[
f'=\kappa(f-a)(f-b)
\]
by direct differentiation, omits exactly
\[
a,b,
\]
and has
\[
\kappa(y-a)(y-b)-y
=
\kappa(y-c)(y-d).
\]
Thus its finite partially shared values are exactly the prescribed four.
