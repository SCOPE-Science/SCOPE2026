# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For 0<a<c, d_N=c+(1-c)/(N+1) lies in (c,1), and H_N=(a,d_N) has positive μ measure because μ and Lebesgue are equivalent. By ergodicity and invariance, the union of backward images of H_N has μ-measure 1 (equivalently Birkhoff: almost every orbit visits H_N); hence μ(S_f(a,d_N))=0. Since Leb<<μ, each survivor is Leb-null, so their countable union is null. For b>c with Φ_f(b)∈E_{-2}(m), Proposition 5.2 for negative doubling implies Φ_f(b) survives (m,Φ_f(b)); its orbit avoids 1/2, giving a unique itinerary τ_f(b+). For a<c<b, Proposition 5.1 gives exact symbolic survivor equality, so that itinerary is realized by x∈S_f(a,b); injectivity of the strict alternating-order EDLM itinerary on distinct points implies x=b. For sufficiently large N, d_N<b, hence b∈S_f(a,d_N). The b=c endpoint is one singleton. This reproduces the source's equation (5.2) without using its affine expansion, proves (P), and Corollary 3 then provides nonincreasing continuous entropy locally constant a.e. (possible constant). Critically, μ<<Leb alone would NOT transfer μ-nullity to Leb; full equivalence supplies Leb<<μ. No assumption of existence for all EDLM maps is asserted. No finite experiment is needed.

## originality

PASS

Best-of-knowledge qualified PASS for the explicit general EDLM implication, not for the underlying ergodic-null lemma or symbolic cover. Ding–Steiner–Sun (arXiv:2610.03327v1) give Proposition 5.2, conditional Corollary 3 and the general symbolic step (5.2) within their two-slope example, but their only proven fixed-hole nullity there uses affine expansion; they do not state the invariant-equivalent-measure sufficient hypothesis for arbitrary EDLM. Sun–Li–Ding (arXiv:2311.02465v1) prove the same ergodic measure mechanism for increasing expansive Lorenz maps (Theorem 1.4, p.15 proof), not decreasing orientation; changing orientation is not a conjugacy within the one-discontinuity increasing class. The inference is an elementary and close synthesis of those published components, so historical independent precedence or implicit-coroIlary interpretation is a genuine residual risk. Resultary semantic searches returned only this exact finding and no separately published same-class equivalent.

## value

PASS

This supplies a natural and interpretable sufficient hypothesis for the very pullback-null condition explicitly left open for general decreasing EDLM by the focal source. It decouples a measure-theoretic recurrence assumption from the nontrivial negative-doubling symbolic parameter transfer, applies to every strict fixed left endpoint simultaneously, and is not limited to affine slopes. The hypothesis is nonvacuous beyond piecewise linear coordinates: conjugating the full two-slope negative-doubling map by an increasing smooth non-affine diffeomorphism of [0,1] produces nonlinear EDLM with an ergodic invariant measure of strictly positive finite density (pushforward of Lebesgue). This is a modest structural criterion, not an independent new entropy proof or a solution for arbitrary EDLM. The Birkhoff null-hole step is textbook, and the symbolic cover is prior work; value lies specifically in identifying and verifying their application to the named missing P hypothesis, not in claiming these ingredients as novel.
