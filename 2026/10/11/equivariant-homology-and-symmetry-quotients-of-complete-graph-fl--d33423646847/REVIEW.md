# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The result rests on the explicitly cited path/cycle decomposition of the complete-graph flex complex, whose component parametrization is natural under relabeling. A direct pair-orbit argument counts invariant tournament orientations, and inclusion-exclusion excludes unique sources and sinks. Cyclic-order stabilizers act by orientation-preserving rotations. Path stabilizers fix both endpoints and act trivially on the interval; their tournament automorphism groups have odd order. These facts establish the two character formulas and both quotient decompositions for every integer \(n\ge3\). Complete finite checks for \(3\le n\le6\) independently agree.

## Originality — PASS, with a residual risk
Kozlov (2026), Theorem 3.4, supplies the underlying component decomposition, counts, and circular-order labels but does not state the full graded \(S_n\)-representation, its fixed-path character, or the two orbit-space descriptions. Royle et al. (2023), §3, already proves the odd-order tournament-automorphism lemma and the unrestricted fixed-tournament enumeration; those inputs are explicitly credited. Searches of indexed published mathematical records and relevant primary-source formulations revealed no stronger statement covering the complete flex-complex equivariant theorem. An equivalent unindexed specialization of orientation-flip theory remains possible.

## Value — PASS
The graph construction is newly introduced in the cited 2026 topology paper, and its natural relabeling symmetry is an intrinsic invariant not determined by any nonequivariant component count. The theorem gives a uniform complete graded character, a computable closed cycle-type formula, an exact decomposition of both natural quotient graphs, and a sharp parity change of first Betti number in the alternating quotient. The fixed-path count and alternating quotient require structural arguments beyond merely substituting a parameter in Kozlov's enumeration.

## Relationship and limits
The closest primary sources are Kozlov, DOI 10.1007/s41468-026-00235-1 (complete-graph flex components), and Royle et al., DOI 10.1007/s10801-022-01197-0 (tournament automorphisms and fixed-orientation counts). The proof claims no new general graph homotopy classification, no integral equivariant decomposition, and no generalization to other flip complexes. Finite enumeration is corroboration, not proof of the all-parameter assertion.
