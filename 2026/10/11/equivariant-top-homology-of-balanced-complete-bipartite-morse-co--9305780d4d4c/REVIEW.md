The directed-tree model is exactly the ordinary Morse-matching complex after an equivariant reversal of arrows. Jojić's complete-multipartite shellability theorem supplies free top homology concentrated in degree \(2m-2\). The fixed-arc-orbit bijection for the diagonal exchange is one-to-one with rooted forests on \(K_m\), and the orientation sign of each two-arc orbit is negative. The matrix-forest identity consequently yields the displayed trace, with all quantifiers \(m\ge2\). The twenty-class finite certificate enumerates every possible rooted forest of \(K_{4,4}\), not a sample, and reproduces the class-weighted dimension-six invariant subspace.

A complete inspection of every older equivariant treatment was not possible, so an unindexed prior equivalent character formula remains possible.
