The ordinary Morse complex of a triangular book with \(k\ge2\) pages has a complete rational \(C_2\times S_k\) top-homology character: \(\chi(1,\sigma)=\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)(f+1)^2\) and \(\chi(s,\sigma)=(-1)^{k+1+e}(1-f+2t)\), where \(f,t,e\) count fixed pages, two-cycles, and even page cycles. Its top Betti number \((k+1)^2\) alone does not determine this symmetry action.
