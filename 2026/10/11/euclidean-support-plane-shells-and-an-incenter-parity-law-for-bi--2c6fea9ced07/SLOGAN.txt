The standard bipartite-cycle root polytope has an explicit \(k^2\)-facet Euclidean support-plane distance spectrum and a sharp inradius \(\sqrt{2/[k(k^2-2)]}\) for even \(k\), or \(\sqrt{2/[k(k^2-1)]}\) for odd \(k\). A maximum-radius ball has a unique center exactly for even \(k\); for odd \(k\), the optimal-center set has affine dimension \(k-1\).
