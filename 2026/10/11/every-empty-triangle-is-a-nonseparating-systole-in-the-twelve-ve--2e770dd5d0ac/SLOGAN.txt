In the maximally symmetric twelve-vertex genus-six triangulation, every one of the \(176\) empty triangles is a nonseparating mod-two one-systole. They represent \(110\) homology classes with multiplicities \(1^{\times56},2^{\times48},4^{\times6}\), and the six fourfold classes form one orbit of the full order-\(12\) simplicial symmetry group.\n
