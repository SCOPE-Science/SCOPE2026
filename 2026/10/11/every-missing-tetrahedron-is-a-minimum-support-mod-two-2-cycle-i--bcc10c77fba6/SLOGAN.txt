In Lutz's vertex-minimal 11-vertex triangulation of \(S^2\times S^2\), all 58 missing tetrahedra are homologically essential over \(\mathbf F_2\). Their boundaries are exactly the minimum four-vertex representatives of nonzero \(H_2\), with the three nonzero classes supported \(6\), \(24\), and \(28\) times.
