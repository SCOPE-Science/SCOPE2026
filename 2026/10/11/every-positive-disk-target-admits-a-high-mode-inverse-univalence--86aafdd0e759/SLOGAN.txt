For the algebraically damped Hadamard preimage class, the exact inverse-safe radius of the \(n\)-th monomial slice is \(\delta_n=\Omega_n(n-1)/(n-\Omega_n)\), and these radii tend to zero. Hence every fixed positive disk target contains sufficiently high single-mode members whose transforms are disk-starlike while the original functions are not even locally univalent.
