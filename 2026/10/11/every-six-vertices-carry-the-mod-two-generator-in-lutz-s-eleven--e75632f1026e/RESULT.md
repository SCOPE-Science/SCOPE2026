# Every six vertices carry the mod-two generator in Lutz’s eleven-vertex \(\mathbb{RP}^{3}\)
## Finding
Let \(K\) be the eleven-vertex triangulation \(\mathrm{RP}^{3}_{11}\) whose forty tetrahedra are listed by Lutz. A three-set \(T\) is called a generator triangle when all three of its edges lie in \(K\), \(T\) itself is not a triangle of \(K\), and its three-edge cycle represents the nonzero class of \(H_1(K;\mathbf F_2)\).

There are exactly 55 generator triangles. More strongly, for every vertex subset \(S\subseteq V(K)\),
\[
H_1(K[S];\mathbf F_2)\longrightarrow H_1(K;\mathbf F_2)
\]
has nonzero image if and only if \(S\) contains a generator triangle. Hence the numbers of supporting subsets by cardinality are
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrrr}
|S|&3&4&5&6&7&8&9&10&11\\ \hline
\#\text{ supporting }S&55&242&442&462&330&165&55&11&1.
\end{array}
\]
Equivalently, the 55 generator triangles form a three-uniform hypergraph whose independence polynomial is
\[
1+11t+55t^2+110t^3+88t^4+20t^5.
\]
Thus every six-vertex induced subcomplex carries the global mod-two generator, while exactly 20 five-vertex induced subcomplexes do not. Under the 48-element automorphism group generated by the permutations stated by Lutz, the generator triangles have orbit sizes \(3,4,12,12,24\), and the 20 exceptional five-sets have orbit sizes \(8,12\).

## Assumptions and scope
The object is exactly the forty-facet complex printed in the primary source, with vertex set \(\{1,\ldots,11\}\). The statement concerns simplicial homology with coefficients in \(\mathbf F_2\), induced subcomplexes \(K[S]\), and the inclusion-induced map to the homology of the full complex. No assertion is made for other eleven-vertex triangulations or for coefficients other than \(\mathbf F_2\).

## Proof
The facet list generates 11 vertices, 51 edges, 80 triangles, and 40 tetrahedra, agreeing with the published face vector. Exact binary linear algebra gives \(\dim Z_1(K;\mathbf F_2)=41\) and \(\operatorname{rank} B_1(K;\mathbf F_2)=40\), so \(H_1(K;\mathbf F_2)\cong\mathbf F_2\).

A concrete one-cocycle \(w\) is supported on the following 17 edges:
\[
\begin{aligned}
&(1,4),(1,8),(1,10),(2,5),(3,6),(3,9),(3,10),(4,7),(4,9),\\
&(5,7),(5,8),(5,9),(5,10),(6,7),(6,8),(7,10),(8,9).
\end{aligned}
\]
It evaluates to zero on the boundary of each of the 80 triangles. The vertex-coboundary space has rank 10, while adjoining \(w\) raises the rank to 11, so \(w\) represents the nonzero class of \(H^1(K;\mathbf F_2)\).

Exhausting the \(\binom{11}{3}\) three-sets finds exactly 55 with all three edges present but the triangle absent. The cocycle \(w\) evaluates to one on every corresponding three-edge cycle; hence all 55 represent the unique nonzero global homology class.

For each of the \(2^{11}\) vertex subsets \(S\), the verifier computes a basis of the cycle space of the induced one-skeleton and evaluates \(w\) on that basis. Independently, it computes
\[
\dim\frac{B_1(K)+Z_1(K[S])}{B_1(K)}.
\]
The two tests agree for every \(S\). The resulting image is nonzero exactly when \(S\) contains one of the 55 generator triangles. Counting such subsets gives the stated support profile; taking complements within the subset lattice gives the stated independence polynomial. Finally, the three published generating permutations close to a group of order 48, preserve all forty facets, and produce the stated orbit decompositions.

## Relationship to prior work
Lutz identifies this exact forty-facet complex as combinatorially isomorphic to Walkup’s minimal eleven-vertex triangulation, and gives its face vector and full automorphism group. The inspected exact-object section does not tabulate induced-subcomplex homology maps, empty-triangle homology classes, or the support hypergraph above. Bagchi's tightness criterion uses injectivity of homology maps from all induced subcomplexes as a central structural notion, so the complete support profile of the unique nonzero mod-two class is a natural local-to-global invariant rather than an arbitrary subset census. Focused searches for the exact object together with empty triangles, induced homology, and generator supports found no statement implying this profile.

## Limitations
This is an exhaustive finite result for the published triangulation \(K\). The computation does not claim an analogous threshold for all triangulations of \(\mathbb{RP}^3\), nor an integral-coefficient analogue. The originality assessment is bounded by the accessible indexed and primary literature; an older unindexed computation could in principle contain an equivalent census.
