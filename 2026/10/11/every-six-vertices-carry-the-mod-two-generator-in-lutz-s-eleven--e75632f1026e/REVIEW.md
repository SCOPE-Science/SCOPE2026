# Same-model review
## Correctness
PASS. The proof is finite and exhaustive on the exact forty-facet complex. The embedded verifier independently computes the global homology dimension, validates an explicit nonzero cohomology class, checks all 55 minimal three-vertex witnesses, and compares two independent inclusion-image calculations on all 2048 induced subcomplexes. It also reconstructs the 48-element symmetry group generated by the published permutations and verifies the orbit counts.

## Originality
PASS. The closest exact-object literature prints the triangulation, face vector, and automorphism group but not the induced-subcomplex support profile. Broader tightness literature explains why induced homology maps are structurally natural but does not imply the exact 55 minimal supports, the polynomial, the every-six-vertices threshold, or the exceptional five-set orbits. The closest indexed finite-complex result found concerns the six-vertex triangulation of \(\mathbb{RP}^2\), so it has neither the same object nor a stronger covering statement.

## Value
PASS. The claim localizes the unique global mod-two one-dimensional homology class across every induced vertex subset of a vertex-minimal three-manifold triangulation. The sharp transition from 20 exceptional five-sets to all 462 six-sets, together with the minimal-support hypergraph and symmetry decomposition, is a natural structural invariant rather than an arbitrary finite slice.

## Closest literature and limitations
The historical source is D. W. Walkup, DOI:10.1007/BF02392331; Lutz, arXiv:math/0506372, supplies the exact forty-facet presentation and symmetry data. B. Bagchi, arXiv:1406.4299, provides the broader induced-subcomplex homology framework. The main residual originality risk is an older unindexed computation of the same local support data. The claim is limited to this triangulation and \(\mathbf F_2\) coefficients.
