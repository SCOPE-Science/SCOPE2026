In Lutz’s eleven-vertex triangulation of \(\mathbb{RP}^{3}\), exactly 55 empty triangles represent the unique nonzero class in \(H_1(-;\mathbf F_2)\), and an induced subcomplex maps nontrivially to global \(H_1\) exactly when it contains one of them. Their independence polynomial is \(1+11t+55t^2+110t^3+88t^4+20t^5\), so every six-vertex induced subcomplex carries the generator while exactly 20 five-vertex ones do not.
