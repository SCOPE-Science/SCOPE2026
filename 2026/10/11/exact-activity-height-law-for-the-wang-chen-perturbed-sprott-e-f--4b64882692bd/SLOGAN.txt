For every compact stationary state of the Wang–Chen perturbed Sprott-E flow, the mean-square vertical activity \(A=\int \dot z^{\,2}d\mu\) fixes the \(y\)-weighted mean height exactly as \(\int z\,d\nu=-16a/(1+A)\). Zero activity occurs only at the stable equilibrium, so for \(a>0\) every non-equilibrium recurrent branch lies strictly between the equilibrium height \(-16a\) and \(0\) in this weighted statistic.
