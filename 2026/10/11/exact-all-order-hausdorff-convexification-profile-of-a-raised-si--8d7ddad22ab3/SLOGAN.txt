For the regular-simplex base plus its elevated vertices, the Hausdorff distance to the convex hull after \(k\) Minkowski averages is exactly \(\varepsilon\min\{1,(n-1)/k\}\) for every \(k\ge1\) when \(0<\varepsilon<1/(n-1)^2\). This single family attains the Schneider-index convexification bound at every order, with the first strict decrease occurring at \(k=n\).
