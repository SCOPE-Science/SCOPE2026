# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The final claim is for synchronous Boolean AND on an N-site periodic binary ring, p>=1, N>=3, z=e^mu>0, and initial states satisfying F^(p+1)x=F^p x (AT MOST p steps to a fixed state). Induction gives F^p(x)_i=product_{j=-p}^p x_{i+j}, with repeated cyclic positions harmless. A fixed point containing a 1 forces both neighbors to be 1 and hence is the homogeneous all-ones state, while all zeros is the only other fixed point. F^p x=1 occurs only for x=1; F^p x=0 iff the circular initial word avoids 1^(2p+1), even for 2p+1>N. The disjoint basin sum is z^N plus the cyclic avoidance partition. For a non-all-ones periodic word the run-length automaton state is uniquely determined by its preceding zero; closed walks yield trace(T_m(z)^N) exactly. T_m(z) has a strongly connected graph with a self-loop at state 0 for each z>0 and is primitive. Its Perron eigenvalue solves sum_{r=0}^{m-1} z^r/lambda^(r+1)=1. Strict decrease in lambda gives one positive solution. Evaluating at lambda=z gives m/z, hence the unique Perron/isolated-state crossing at z=m=2p+1, and P=max(log lambda,log z). Implicit differentiation at lambda=z=m gives rho=(sum r)/(sum(r+1))=(m-1)/(m+1)=p/(p+1), whereas the all-ones pressure slope equals 1. The two analytic branches therefore have a single first-order kink and jump 1/(p+1). Since Perron eigenvalue is simple with a uniform local spectral gap, trace(T(z_N)^N)=m^N exp(rho*t+o(1)) for z_N=m exp(t/N). The isolated term is m^N exp(t), yielding the logistic all-ones probability (1+exp(-t/(p+1)))^-1 and the density mixture; fixed p and t. Nothing is claimed for simultaneous p,N growth or for weighted time trajectories rather than weighted initial states. Independent implementation, not the submitted script, enumerated actual Boolean updates and independently formed matrix traces for p=1..4, N=3..10 and z=1,2,5 (96 exact equalities); eigenvalue checks at z=m for p=1..4 yielded rho=1/2,2/3,3/4,4/5, and numerical N=240 window checks approached the predicted sigmoid. Finite checks corroborate but do not prove the all-parameter analytic conclusions.

## originality

PASS

The structurally identical rule-128 chemical-potential trajectory ensemble and an observed p=1 first-order-like jump are already in Koller–Hudcová (2025), Fig. 4 and its discussion; p=1 qualitative coexistence and generic space-time transfer matrices are therefore covered and NOT credited as new. Lind (1989) covers the unweighted forbidden-word shift/zeta construction, and general Perron–Frobenius spectral facts are classical. The inspected primary paper does not state the explicit p-uniform transition mu_c(p)=log(2p+1), critical left slope p/(p+1), or N^-1-window logistic limit. These require special algebraic reduction of the AND basin to an m-state run automaton and identification of competition with a single homogeneous trajectory. An unindexed exact specialization of general transfer/pressure results is possible, so historical priority is qualified, not certified. The Bóna–Maga–Richey full text could not be inspected (arXiv PDF/HTML and lawful open-access search failed; authorized Oxford returned vpn_disconnected); its abstract concerns density inside one forbidden-word subshift and does not by itself resolve whether any hidden formula overlaps. No decisive prior theorem directly supplies the full statement in the inspected material.

## value

PASS

The rule-128 fixed-point-basin ensemble and its unexplained sharp density jump are already motivated experimentally by Koller–Hudcová, rather than being an arbitrary engineered example. The result analytically locates and quantifies the transition for EVERY fixed transient length p>=1; the nontrivial exact m=2p+1 reduced matrix gives a uniform explanation of why one all-ones configuration competes against exponentially many zero-basin words. The exact density jump and N^-1 logistic crossover quantify finite-size rounding of this physically natural constrained initial-state ensemble beyond plotted short-p cases. These sharp formulas are not mere restatements of the generic transfer-matrix recipe or of p=1 numerical data. Value does not rely on a new general Perron–Frobenius technique, a dynamic bifurcation, or a joint p,N asymptotic.
