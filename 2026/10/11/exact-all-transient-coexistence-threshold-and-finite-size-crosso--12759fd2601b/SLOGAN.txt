For elementary cellular automaton rule 128, trajectories that reach a fixed state by time \(p\) have an exact all-transient first-order chemical-potential threshold \(\mu_c=\log(2p+1)\), with density jump \(1/(p+1)\). At \(\mu_c+t/N\), the all-ones basin weight has the explicit logistic limit \((1+e^{-t/(p+1)})^{-1}\).
