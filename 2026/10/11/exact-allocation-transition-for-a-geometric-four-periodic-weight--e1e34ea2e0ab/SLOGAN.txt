The geometric four-periodic shift has a sharp total-modulation threshold \(c_*(q)=q^{-3}\sqrt{(1-q^4)(1-q^8)}\): below it the best numerical radius is \(1/2\) but no strictly positive allocation attains it, while above it a unique positive split minimizes the radius. The large-budget optimal split and the numerical radius both have explicit two-term asymptotic formulas.
