Contracting planar homogeneous star-node flows with nonvanishing angular drift have an exact affine Poincaré return in the radial power \(r^{2p}\), and every attracting cycle has transverse Floquet exponent exactly \(-2p\lambda\). The original-time period satisfies \(T=-\lambda^{-1}\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{f(\theta)}{|g(\theta)|}\,d\theta\), even for an explicit noncircular cubic family whose period and multiplier are independent of anisotropy.
