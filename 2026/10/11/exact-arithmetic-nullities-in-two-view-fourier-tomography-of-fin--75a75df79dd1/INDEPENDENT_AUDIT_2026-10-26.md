# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For every odd prime power q, the quadratic etale algebra F_q[J]/(J^2+1) has cyclic norm-one group U of order n=q-chi_q(-1). Fourier inversion on the additive group converts each full frequency line into all line-fibre sums. For nonisotropic u, fibres are exactly orbits of R_u(z)=a_u/z, a_u=u/conj(u). Two vanishing slices impose anti-invariance under R_u and R_v, hence invariance under r=a_v/a_u. On the quotient U/<r> of order h, anti-invariance under w->a_u/w contributes one dimension per two-cycle and zero per fixed coset. Solving w^2=a_u gives 1 fixed point for odd h, 2 for even h with chi_q(Q(u))=+1, and 0 otherwise, proving the nullity formula and explicit signed-coset basis. The nonisotropic projective directions biject with U, so enumerating rotation orders n and n/2 yields n*phi(n)/2+n*phi(n/2)/4 injective unordered distinct-direction pairs. Split-case isotropic directions are factor projections and each single view is injective. An independent bipartite-incidence graph cycle-rank checker validated 1914 pairs over q=3,5,7,9,11,13,17,19,25,49; these finite checks are corroboration, not the all-q proof.
