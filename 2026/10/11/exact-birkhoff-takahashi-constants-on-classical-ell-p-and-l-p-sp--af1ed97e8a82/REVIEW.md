# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The upper bound follows from the focal paper’s monotonicity in \(t\), its comparison with the unrestricted Takahashi constant, and Clarkson’s exact von Neumann–Jordan formula. The lower bound is attained by explicit Birkhoff-orthogonal pairs in both parameter branches. The cases \(p=1\) and \(p=\infty\) are checked without using smoothness. No finite experiment substitutes for an infinite proof.

## Originality
PASS. The focal full text gives only a balanced-family lower bound and asymptotic convergence for \(\ell_p^2\); it does not state the exact finite-\(p\) formula or the \(L_p[0,1]\) result. The closest exact later result concerns direct isosceles orthogonality, which is a distinct admissibility condition and does not imply Birkhoff attainment. Targeted source, alias, exact-formula and implication searches in the publication index and available prior research record found no covering Birkhoff statement. The main residual risk is an unindexed short equivalent argument in older orthogonality literature.

## Value
PASS. The theorem supplies the complete classical \(\ell_p\)/\(L_p\) benchmark for the new Birkhoff-restricted scale throughout the natural quadratic-or-lower regime. It explains exactly when the orthogonality restriction leaves the classical extremal geometry untouched and replaces the focal paper’s asymptotic information with a closed formula across both sides of \(p=2\).

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is arXiv:2608.07146v1 itself; the closest numerical analogue is DOI 10.3390/axioms15090684 for isosceles orthogonality. Clarkson’s DOI 10.2307/1968512 is the classical unrestricted input. The theorem does not address \(t>2\) for \(1<p<\infty\), \(p\ne2\), complex spaces, or arbitrary measure spaces.
