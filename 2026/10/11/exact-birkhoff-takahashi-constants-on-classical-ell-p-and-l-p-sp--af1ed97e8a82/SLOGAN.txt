For every \(1\le p\le\infty\) and \(-\infty\le t\le2\), the Birkhoff-orthogonal Takahashi constant on real \(\ell_p\) and \(L_p[0,1]\) is exactly \(2^{|2/p-1|}\). Explicit Birkhoff-orthogonal extremal pairs show that the new orthogonality restriction does not lower the classical von Neumann–Jordan value anywhere in this full parameter regime.
