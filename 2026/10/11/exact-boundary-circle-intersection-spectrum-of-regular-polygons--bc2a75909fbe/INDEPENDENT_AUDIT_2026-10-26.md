Reconstructed for n>=3 and R>0: normalization R=1, squared distance on every edge strictly convex, angular test bounds strict distance peaks by two even or three odd. The opposite even side yields two crossings plus two on complement for all nonvertex centers; vertex chains are monotone. For odd n, an interior projection supplies at least four crossings; both opposite-edge projections have interior feet when t<tau_n. The weak-peak/strong-valley squared gap factors as c²(t-t_-)(t-t_+), where t_-=(2s/c)(1-cos(alpha/2))² and t_+>s. It is positive exactly |t|<t_-; at equality two doubled crossings degenerate into one tangent/vertex each, so the maximum is four. The normalized perimeter ratio equals tau_n/s and its Taylor leading term is pi^4/(32n^4). Independent exact symbolic difference factorization passed, and fresh radius-chamber enumeration checked 80 representative centers across n=3..101, including both sides of the threshold. Finite tests supplement, not replace, the all-n geometric proof.
