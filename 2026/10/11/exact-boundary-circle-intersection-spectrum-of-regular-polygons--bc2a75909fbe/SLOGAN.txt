For circles centered on the boundary of a regular polygon, the largest intersection count is completely classified: even polygons permit two at vertices and four elsewhere, while odd polygons permit six precisely within an explicit short interval around each edge midpoint and four elsewhere. The six-center perimeter fraction is \(2(1-\cos(\pi/(2n)))^2/\cos(\pi/n)\), asymptotic to \(\pi^4/(32n^4)\) along odd \(n\).
