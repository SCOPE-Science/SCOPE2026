# Same-model review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The proof fixes an arbitrary projection plane, computes the exact complex third-harmonic coefficient of \(u_1u_2u_3\), converts its magnitude to \(\frac1{16}\prod_i(1-n_i^2)\), and applies the standard planar support-function area identity. The AM--GM extremum argument is exact. A symbolic replay independently checks the diagonal case and several exact frames. No finite experiment is used to infer the universal statement.

## Originality
**PASS.** Targeted published-finding corpus searches for the exact brightness formula, its cubic aliases, and the extremal directions found only nearby records on the threefold-projection classification and the sharp convexity threshold. The lead paper's full text was inspected through its exceptional-case proof: it derives only the allowed first/third harmonics and projection symmetry, not their amplitude or projection area. General brightness theory and recent mixed-volume/Wigner work do not state the directionwise product formula.

## Value
**PASS.** The lead paper's exceptional family is geometrically important because it is the obstruction to a ball-characterization theorem. The exact brightness function supplies a natural tomographic invariant of that counterexample, shows that threefold symmetry of every projected shape does not flatten shadow area, identifies three disk shadows and four least-area projection planes, and quantifies the full anisotropy in one closed formula.
