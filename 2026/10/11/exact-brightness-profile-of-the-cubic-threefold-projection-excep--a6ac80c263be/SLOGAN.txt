For the cubic constant-width family with support \(1+\varepsilon u_1u_2u_3\), every planar shadow has area \(\pi-(\pi\varepsilon^2/4)\prod_i(1-n_i^2)\). For nonzero admissible \(\varepsilon\), exactly the coordinate planes give disk shadows and exactly the four body-diagonal planes minimize area. At \(\varepsilon=0\), every shadow is a unit disk of area \(\pi\), so all directions are extremal.
