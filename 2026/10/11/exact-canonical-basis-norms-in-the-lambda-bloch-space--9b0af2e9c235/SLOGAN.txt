For the canonical \(\lambda\)-analytic basis, the new \(\lambda\)-Bloch norm is exactly
\[
(n+\lambda+1)\epsilon_n^{-1}\frac{2}{n+2}\left(\frac{n}{n+2}\right)^{n/2}
\]
for \(n\ge1\), attained at \(r=\sqrt{n/(n+2)}\). Consequently the norm grows as \(2n^\lambda/[e\sqrt{\Gamma(2\lambda+1)}]\), separating a universal \(1/n\) boundary layer from the reflection-dependent amplitude.
