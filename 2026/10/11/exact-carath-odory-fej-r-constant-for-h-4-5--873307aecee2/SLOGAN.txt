For the sparse nonnegative cosine problem with allowed higher harmonics \(4\) and \(5\), the exact first-harmonic coefficient is \(2\cos(\pi/18)/\sqrt3\), the unique root greater than \(1\) of \(3A^3-3A-1=0\). A two-point positive certificate gives the upper bound, and an exact factorization gives the unique extremizer.
