The optimal two-body Bézout mixed-volume coefficient of a Cartesian product is exactly \(\frac{n}{n-1}\max\{1,\max_i\frac{d_i-1}{d_i}\beta_{d_i}(K_i)\}\), omitting one-dimensional factors from the inner maximum. Every product of planar convex bodies and segments therefore has the sharp, shape-independent coefficient \(n/(n-1)\), attained by test segments in two different factors.
