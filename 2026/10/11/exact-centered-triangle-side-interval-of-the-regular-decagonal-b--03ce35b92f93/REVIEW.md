# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The centered triple \(u+v+w=0\) is exactly converted by \(x=u\), \(y=-v\) into \(N_{10}(x)=N_{10}(y)=N_{10}(x-y)=1\) with side objective \(N_{10}(x+y)\). Every admissible pair belongs to a face obtained by choosing one tight facet from each of those three sphere constraints. The exact \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\) checker exhausts all \(1000\) triples, finds \(60\) nonempty segment faces, and minimizes/maximizes the piecewise-affine output exactly on every segment. It verifies the global values \((9-\sqrt5)/4\) and \(4-\sqrt5\) and checks explicit attaining pairs.

## Originality
PASS. Searches covered the original \(J_L,Y_J\) notation, the later \(g_1,J_1\) notation, regular-decagon and affine-regular-decagon aliases, and the exact radical \(4-\sqrt5\). The highly relevant 2024 Martini--Papini--Wu article could not be retrieved in full; its accessible abstract and metadata state general characterizations and relations but no decagonal value, so this is retained as a specific residual risk rather than treated as a negative full-text check. Targeted published-finding corpus searches returned no covering same-invariant decagonal finding.

## Value
PASS. \(J_L\) and \(Y_J\) were introduced precisely to quantify centered inscribed unit-triangle side lengths. The affine regular decagon is a canonical polyhedral Banach plane, and the two exact endpoints give its complete centered-triangle side interval. This complements known decagonal James and Banach--Mazur data and supplies a reusable exact polyhedral method.

## Closest literature and limitations
Fu, Liu and Li introduced \(J_L\) and \(Y_J\) in 2021. Martini, Papini and Wu later studied the same constants as \(g_1,J_1\) in Annals of Functional Analysis; the accessible bibliographic record lists MSC 46B04 and 46B20. The closest public same-space exact result is instead a Banach--Mazur-distance computation. The present result does not classify all regular \(2n\)-gons, and the inaccessible 2024 full text remains a bibliographic residual risk.
