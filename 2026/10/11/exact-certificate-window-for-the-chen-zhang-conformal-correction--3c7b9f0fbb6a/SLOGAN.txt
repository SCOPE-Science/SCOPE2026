The Chen--Zhang quadratic conformal correction on \(S^2\times T^2\) belongs to an explicit open one-parameter family whose published curvature estimate certifies positivity exactly on a closed-form coefficient window. Within that certificate, the unique best coefficient is \(\tau_*(\varepsilon)=1/24+\varepsilon^4/864+O(\varepsilon^6)\), explaining the source coefficient \(1/24\) as the asymptotically optimal choice.
