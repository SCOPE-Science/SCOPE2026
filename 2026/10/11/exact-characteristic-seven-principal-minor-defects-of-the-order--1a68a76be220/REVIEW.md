# Review: characteristic-seven principal-minor defects of the order-fifteen Fourier matrix

## Correctness — PASS
The claim concerns exactly the nonempty principal minors of the unnormalized order-fifteen Fourier matrix over a field of characteristic seven containing primitive fifteenth roots. The factorization of the cyclotomic polynomial has two irreducible degree-four factors; the exact finite-field program verifies invertibility of every nonzero field element and the primitivity of the chosen roots. It checks all \(32767\) principal masks in each of the two Frobenius orbits and finds precisely fifteen singular size-six translates and their fifteen size-nine complements, all nullity one. A separate integer-polynomial permutation expansion verifies the exceptional seed determinant as \(35\) times a specific nonzero polynomial of degree seven in a primitive root. Translation scaling and complementary-minor duality supply structural explanations for all thirty cases. These are finite exhaustive calculations with exact arithmetic, not floating-point evidence for an infinite assertion.

## Originality — PASS (same-model assessment, residual risk remains)
Caragea–Lee–Malikiosis–Pfander, *Principal minors of Fourier matrices of square-free order* (arXiv:2505.24326v3), Theorems 1.3–1.4, prove complex nonsingularity for certain square-free orders and formulate a conditional characteristic-lifting criterion. Their statements do not enumerate **singular** principal matrices of order fifteen in characteristic seven. Gu–Zhou–Wang (arXiv:2608.17746) report exact finite-field certificates for bases of orders ten and eleven in other questions, not the present classification; only abstract-level material of that manuscript was inspected. W. Zhou (arXiv:2608.06335v1), Section 2.3, discusses the complex principal-minor conjecture, not a finite-characteristic defect census. Published mathematical record searches for characteristic-seven order-fifteen matrices, translation-orbit defects, and characteristic lifting to order 105 did not identify an equivalent or stronger classification; this absence is evidence of search coverage, not proof of novelty. A specialized or unindexed computation may predate the result.

## Value — PASS
The order-fifteen matrix is the natural base for the characteristic-seven sufficient lifting criterion at the first odd three-prime product \(105=3\cdot5\cdot7\). A single negative example would be a limited observation; instead this result completely describes the defect locus, its translation/complement symmetries, both primitive-root orbits, and the exact one-dimensional kernel defect at every singular principal set. This is a structurally motivated complete finite classification useful for determining precisely where a naive lifting argument is unavailable and what exceptional configurations a refined method must address. It does not claim that the complex order-105 conjecture fails or that all other proof strategies are impossible.

The two verification programs are included so the exact finite analysis is reproducible.
