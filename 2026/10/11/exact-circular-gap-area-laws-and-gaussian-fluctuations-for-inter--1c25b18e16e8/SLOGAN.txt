For fixed \(b>1\), the area of an intersection of congruent radius-\(b\) disks whose centers lie on the unit circle is exactly a strictly convex sum of circular-gap scores, making equally spaced centers the unique area minimizer. Uniformly random center angles have an explicit mean area, a factor-six leading expected-area penalty relative to optimal placement, and Gaussian fluctuations with sharp variance asymptotic \(40\pi^6(b-1)^4/(b^2m^5)\).
