Every partial permutohedron has an exactly computable unique smallest enclosing Euclidean ball, with rational center and squared radius and at most two consecutive ranks of contacting vertices. Its last contact transition is sharp: for \(m>n(n+1)\), only vertices using all \(n\) positive labels touch, and the squared radius is \(\sum_{j=1}^n j^2-(\sum_{j=1}^n j)^2/m\).
