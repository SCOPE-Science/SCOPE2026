In the SIXP leaf-brown-spot model, the pathogen-free equilibrium has the exact closed global threshold \(R_0\le1\). The Lyapunov quantity \(V=I+(R_x/\varepsilon)X\) satisfies \(\dot V=-(R_xX+R_cI)I/(S+I)-c(1-R_0)I\), which proves global extinction even at the nonhyperbolic boundary \(R_0=1\); for \(R_0>1\), the infected linear block has negative determinant and the equilibrium is unstable.
