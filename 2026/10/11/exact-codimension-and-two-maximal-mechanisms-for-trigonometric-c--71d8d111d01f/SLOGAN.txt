For zero-mean pairs of trigonometric coefficients of harmonic cutoff \(n\), the common-composition global-center family has exact codimension \(2n-1\); its only maximal-dimensional mechanisms are proportional coefficients and a common odd time-reflection axis. At harmonic cutoff two, every such composition center is in one of those two families, giving an exact Fourier-coefficient decision test and revealing a nonproportional branch absent at cutoff one.
