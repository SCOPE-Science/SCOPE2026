For the complex coordinate-sum-zero hyperplane of \(\ell_4^n\), the exact fourth power of its Banach--Mazur distance to complex Hilbert space is \((n^2-3n+3)/(n-1)\), in every dimension \(n\ge2\). Complex roots of unity attain the equal-modulus radial minimum, while a real one-outlier vector attains the maximum; permutation symmetry makes the ambient Euclidean norm optimal.
