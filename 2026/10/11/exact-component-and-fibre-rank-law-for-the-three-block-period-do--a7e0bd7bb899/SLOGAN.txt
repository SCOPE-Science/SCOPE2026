The three-block difference lift of the period-doubling subshift modulo \(\kappa\) splits into exactly \(2^{\nu_2(\kappa)}\) minimal components, each of uniform fibre rank \(\kappa^2/2^{\nu_2(\kappa)}\). A two-return-word subgroup calculation and a component-stabilizer argument show that only the power of two dividing the alphabet modulus governs the splitting.
