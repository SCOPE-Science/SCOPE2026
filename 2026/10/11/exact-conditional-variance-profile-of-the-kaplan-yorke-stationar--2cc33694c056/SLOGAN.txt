For the classical Kaplan–Yorke map with \(|\alpha|<1\), every stationary state with Lebesgue expanding-coordinate marginal has the exact fiberwise law \(\mathbb E[Y\mid X=x]=\cos(2\pi x)\) and \(\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid X=x)=\frac{\alpha^2}{2}(\frac{1}{1-\alpha^2}+\cos(2\pi x))\). The familiar global vertical variance \(1/(2(1-\alpha^2))\) is therefore the integral of a completely explicit heteroscedastic profile.
