For the apple-like Ma–Minda starlike class with target \(e^w\sqrt{1+w}\), the sharp classical convexity radius is the unique \(R\in(1/3,17/50)\) satisfying \(e^{-R}\sqrt{1-R}=R(1+1/[2(1-R)])\), namely \(R=0.3338769322513574\ldots\). The exact radial order of convexity is \(e^{-r}\sqrt{1-r}-r(1+1/[2(1-r)])\) for every \(r\le R\), with equality at the negative radius for one canonical extremizer.
