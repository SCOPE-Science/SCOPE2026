The nearest-predecessor basis of a strictly decreasing comb ultrametric has exact sign and suppression constants \(U=1+\sup_{\ell}\sum_{i>\ell}a_i/a_\ell\) and \(K=\sup_{\ell}\sum_{r\geq0}a_{\ell+2r}/a_\ell\). It is unconditional exactly when the distance sequence decreases by a uniform fixed factor within a bounded number of steps; otherwise a monotone retractional basis can be conditional even though the free space is isomorphic to \(\ell_1\).
