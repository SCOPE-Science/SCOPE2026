# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The rank-one Fourier coefficient is \(-1/(q^m-1)\) by uniform nonzero columns; the rank-two coefficient is \(1/((q^m-1)(q^{m-1}-1))\) by two-column cancellation. The rook-graph decomposition gives every multiplicity and eigenline. Positivity is established by an explicit positive row-contrast value and a nonzero-variance Schur complement; no experiment is treated as an infinite proof. Comparing the characteristic polynomial at the doubly centered eigenvalue gives a rational expression whose sign changes exactly at \(m=2q\). The checker enumerates eight independently constructed finite cases, including characteristic two and \(\mathbb F_4\), and performs 576 exact rational sign comparisons.

## Originality — PASS, with a defined historical risk
Delsarte (1978), Sections 2–3 and Appendix, provides the general rank-dependent Fourier transform and covers the rank-one/two coefficients, but does not state this exact coordinate-restriction spectral decomposition, endpoint eigenspaces, or sharp threshold. The rank-one matrix and determinant-one matrix coordinate-frame classifications are for different supports and do not imply the all-dimension invertible-matrix result; the claimed range begins at \(m=3\). Searches included formulations in terms of restricted characters, Gram spectra, invertible-matrix coordinate covariance, rook graphs, and exact conditioning. A possible unlocated equivalent older association-scheme specialization remains a material priority risk.

## Value — PASS
Conditioning independent coordinate characters on matrix invertibility is a natural finite harmonic-analysis measurement problem: it asks how much orthogonality is lost on the full-rank locus. This gives the entire sharp Riesz spectrum, all equality spaces, and a non-obvious strict change of maximizer at the arithmetic boundary \(m=2q\), rather than a numerical table or an isolated small case. It also quantifies the disappearance of the conditioning defect in growing dimension and growing field size.
