For the ball obstacle \(\varphi(z)=-|\langle z,v\rangle|^{2q}\) with \(0<q\le1\), the Perron–Bremermann envelope is exactly \(-\left(1-\|P_{v^\perp}z\|^2\right)^q\). Its minimum fills the boundary minimum circle to the full complex diameter, and \(q=1\) is the sharp upper threshold for this orthogonal-shadow formula to remain plurisubharmonic.
