Every compact stationary Bogdanov state satisfies \(\operatorname{Cov}(x,y)=\tfrac12\operatorname{Var}(y)\). In the supercritical-Hopf sign regime \(k>0\), \(\mu<0\), every non-fixed stationary state has mean-square displacement from the midpoint of the two fixed points strictly larger than \(1/4\), forcing recurrent mass outside their strip; saturation of the covariance bound is possible only on period-two dynamics.
