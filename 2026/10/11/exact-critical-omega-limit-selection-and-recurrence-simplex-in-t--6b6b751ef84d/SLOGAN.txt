For the Linz–Sprott absolute-value jerk, every bounded forward orbit at the critical values \(A=\pm1\) has as its entire omega-limit set exactly one of an explicit radius-at-most-one family of harmonic circles or the opposite equilibrium, and these orbits completely determine the compact stationary measures. Outside \(|A|\le1\), every bounded forward orbit converges to an equilibrium; the critical circles also realize the universal \(2\pi\) least-period edge.
