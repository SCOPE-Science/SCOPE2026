For the \(k\)-fold Minkowski average of the vertices of a regular Euclidean \(n\)-simplex, the exact distance to the simplex is \(\ell\sqrt{r_*(n+1-r_*)/[2(n+1)]}/k\), where \(r_*=\min\{k,\lfloor(n+1)/2\rfloor\}\). The centroid is the unique farthest point through that threshold; thereafter the full farthest-point set and its cardinality have an explicit integer-composition classification.
