For every nonzero planar nondegenerate quadratic level set over an odd finite field, the sharp \(L^2\to L^4\) deficit splits exactly into four nonnegative pieces measuring pair-mass variance, opposite-point modulus imbalance, cross-pair imbalance, and antipodal phase misalignment. This gives explicit quantitative stability beyond the sharp constants and equality classifications of the split and anisotropic cases.
