The degree-two primitive projection of every \(q\)-ary Hamming scheme, for \(q\ge3\), has an exact endpoint norm given by only four binomial boundary masses. As \(n/q\) moves from infinity to a finite positive value, its normalized norm changes from the universal constant \(\sqrt{2/(\pi e)}\) to an explicit Poisson–Charlier profile, with a genuine derivative jump at \(2-\sqrt2\).
