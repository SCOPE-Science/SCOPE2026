Every point of a finite real cube has an exact Choi–Jung \(\Delta\)-constant determined by opposite-face capacities above its coordinate-escape floor; the full formula resolves even the previously difficult three-dimensional case. The sharp value on the unit sphere of \(\ell_\infty^n\) is \(2-2/(n-1)\) for \(n\ge3\), with one signed-permutation orbit of maximizers for \(n\ge4\).
