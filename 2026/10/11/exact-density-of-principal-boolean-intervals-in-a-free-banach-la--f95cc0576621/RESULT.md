# Exact density of principal Boolean intervals in a free Banach lattice
## Finding
Let \(\Gamma\) be a set and let \(L=\mathcal{P}(\Gamma)\) be the Boolean lattice ordered by inclusion. For every nonempty \(A\subseteq\Gamma\),

\[
\operatorname{dens}\bigl([\delta(\emptyset),\delta(A)]_{\operatorname{FBL}\langle L\rangle}\bigr)
=\max\{\aleph_0,2^{|A|}\}.
\]

Consequently, the interval is separable for every finite nonempty \(A\), and for every infinite \(A\) its density is exactly \(2^{|A|}\).

## Assumptions and scope
The lattice \(L\) is the full power-set lattice of an arbitrary set \(\Gamma\), with meet given by intersection and join by union. The map \(\delta:L\to\operatorname{FBL}\langle L\rangle\) is the canonical lattice embedding. The set \(A\) is assumed nonempty; the degenerate case \(A=\emptyset\) is excluded because then the order interval is a singleton.

Density character is the least cardinality of a norm-dense subset. No separability, countability, or cardinal-arithmetic hypothesis on \(\Gamma\) or \(A\) is imposed.

## Proof
Ben Rjeb and Tradacete prove for every distributive lattice and every strict lattice interval that

\[
\max\{\aleph_0,|[a,b]_L|\}
\leq
\operatorname{dens}([\delta(a),\delta(b)])
\leq
\max\{\aleph_0,|L|\}.
\]

Taking \(a=\emptyset\) and \(b=A\) gives \([\emptyset,A]_L=\mathcal{P}(A)\), hence

\[
\operatorname{dens}([\delta(\emptyset),\delta(A)])
\geq \max\{\aleph_0,2^{|A|}\}.
\]

It remains to prove the matching upper bound without using the potentially much larger cardinality of \(L\).

Put

\[
U=\{x^*\in L^*:x^*(A)>x^*(\emptyset)\}.
\]

The restriction construction in Example 4.4 of the source gives a lattice homomorphism \(r:\operatorname{FBL}\langle L\rangle\to X_U\) that is isometric on \([\delta(\emptyset),\delta(A)]\): outside \(U\), the two endpoint functions agree, so every function between them agrees there as well.

Fix \(B\subseteq\Gamma\) and \(x^*\in U\). Write \(B_0=B\cap A\) and \(B_1=B\setminus A\). Let \(\alpha=x^*(\emptyset)\) and \(\beta=x^*(A)\), so \(\beta>\alpha\). Since \(B_1\cap A=\emptyset\) and \(x^*\) preserves finite meets,

\[
\min\{x^*(B_1),\beta\}=x^*(B_1\cap A)=\alpha.
\]

Order preservation gives \(x^*(B_1)\geq\alpha\). Because \(\beta>\alpha\), the displayed equality forces \(x^*(B_1)=\alpha\). Since \(B=B_0\cup B_1\) and \(x^*\) preserves finite joins,

\[
x^*(B)=\max\{x^*(B_0),x^*(B_1)\}
=\max\{x^*(B_0),\alpha\}
=x^*(B_0),
\]

where the last equality uses \(\emptyset\subseteq B_0\). Thus, for every \(B\subseteq\Gamma\),

\[
r(\delta(B))=r(\delta(B\cap A)).
\]

The free Banach lattice is the norm closure of the vector lattice generated by its canonical generators. Therefore the whole restricted range \(r(\operatorname{FBL}\langle L\rangle)\) is contained in the closed vector sublattice generated by

\[
\{r(\delta(C)):C\subseteq A\},
\]

a family of cardinality at most \(2^{|A|}\). Finite lattice-linear expressions with rational coefficients form a dense set in the closed vector sublattice generated by any given family, so that sublattice has density at most \(\max\{\aleph_0,2^{|A|}\}\). Because \(r\) is isometric on the interval,

\[
\operatorname{dens}([\delta(\emptyset),\delta(A)])
\leq \max\{\aleph_0,2^{|A|}\}.
\]

Combining the two inequalities proves the formula.

## Verification
The proof uses only finite meet and join preservation of each lattice homomorphism \(x^*\), the source's restriction-isometry argument, and the standard density estimate for a closed vector lattice generated by a family of prescribed cardinality. The crucial collapse identity \(x^*(B)=x^*(B\cap A)\) was checked directly for arbitrary \(B\subseteq\Gamma\) and arbitrary \(x^*\in U\); no enumeration or finite experiment substitutes for the cardinal statement.

The endpoint \(A=\emptyset\) is not included. For finite nonempty \(A\), the formula reduces to density \(\aleph_0\), which agrees with the nondegenerate line segment lower bound in Proposition 4.3. For singleton \(A\), it recovers Example 4.4.

## Relationship to prior work
Ben Rjeb and Tradacete's Proposition 4.3 gives the lower bound \(\max\{\aleph_0,|[a,b]_L|\}\) and the ambient upper bound \(\max\{\aleph_0,|L|\}\). For \(L=\mathcal{P}(\Gamma)\), Example 4.4 proves separability for a singleton upper endpoint, while Remark 4.5 records only the lower bound \(2^{|A|}\) for infinite \(A\). The argument above extends the restriction-map mechanism from a singleton to every nonempty \(A\), producing the missing matching upper bound.

The earlier work of Avilés, Martínez-Cervantes, Rodríguez Abellán, and Rueda Zoca studies lattice embeddings and local complementation for free Banach lattices over lattices. Its results concern when inclusions of generating lattices induce isometric or isomorphic embeddings; they do not state this power-set principal-interval density formula.

## Limitations
The theorem concerns principal intervals whose lower endpoint is \(\delta(\emptyset)\) inside the free Banach lattice generated by a full power-set lattice. It does not classify arbitrary intervals \([\delta(B),\delta(C)]\), nor intervals in general distributive lattices. Remark 4.6 of the focal source shows that no upper bound depending only on the cardinality of the generating lattice interval can hold in full generality, so the Boolean-lattice structure is essential to the proof.

A literature search did not identify an implication-equivalent published statement, but this is not a proof of historical uniqueness. An older equivalent calculation under different Boolean-lattice or free-lattice terminology remains a residual possibility.
