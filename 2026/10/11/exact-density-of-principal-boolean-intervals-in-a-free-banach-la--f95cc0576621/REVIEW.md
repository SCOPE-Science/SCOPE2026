# Review of Exact density of principal Boolean intervals in a free Banach lattice

## Correctness
**PASS.** The proof reconstructs the focal source's lower bound and restriction-isometry mechanism, then proves the new identity \(x^*(B)=x^*(B\cap A)\) for every \(x^*\in U\) directly from finite meet and join preservation. This reduces the restricted generating family to \(\mathcal{P}(A)\), and the cardinal density estimate follows from rational finite lattice-linear expressions. The excluded case \(A=\emptyset\) is handled explicitly as a singleton outside the theorem.

## Originality
**PASS.** The focal source's Proposition 4.3 does not give the desired upper bound when \(\Gamma\) is larger than \(A\); Example 4.4 proves only the singleton case, and Remark 4.5 gives only a lower bound for infinite \(A\). The inspected embedding paper arXiv:2107.11358 concerns induced embeddings and local complementation rather than exact order-interval density. Ten statement-level database searches using power-set, Boolean-lattice, density-character, and restriction-map formulations found no implication-equivalent result. The closest same-framework database record concerns a factor-two \(C(K_L)\) representation constant, a different invariant.

Residual risk remains that an older equivalent observation is phrased under different Boolean-algebra or free-lattice terminology. Search failure is not used as a novelty proof.

## Value
**PASS.** The result resolves the exact density of every nontrivial principal interval from the canonical minimum in the focal paper's central Boolean example. It generalizes the source's singleton calculation and closes the lower-bound-only statement for infinite \(A\) by identifying a structural generator-collapse mechanism.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is Ben Rjeb--Tradacete, arXiv:2604.27841v1, Section 4. The result is limited to \(L=\mathcal{P}(\Gamma)\) and lower endpoint \(\delta(\emptyset)\); it does not classify arbitrary intervals or arbitrary distributive lattices. The earlier embedding paper arXiv:2107.11358 is structurally related but does not cover this density claim in the inspected full text.
