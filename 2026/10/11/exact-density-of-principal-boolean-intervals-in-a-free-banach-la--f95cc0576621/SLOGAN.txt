For the free Banach lattice over the Boolean lattice \(\mathcal{P}(\Gamma)\), every nontrivial principal interval from \(\delta(\emptyset)\) has exact density \(\max\{\aleph_0,2^{|A|}\}\). The proof sharpens the source's lower bound by showing that, on the restriction set controlling the interval, every generator indexed by \(B\subseteq\Gamma\) collapses to the generator indexed by \(B\cap A\).
