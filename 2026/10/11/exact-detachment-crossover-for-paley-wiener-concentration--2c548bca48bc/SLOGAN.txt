For a Paley–Wiener interval with a piece of length \(\varepsilon\) detached by a vanishing distance \(b\ge\varepsilon\), the optimal spectral concentration loss is asymptotic to \(-u_s'(s/2)\varepsilon b\). This gives the exact crossover from the adjacent \(\varepsilon^2\) regime to mesoscopic displacement and identifies the prolate boundary slope as the governing constant.
