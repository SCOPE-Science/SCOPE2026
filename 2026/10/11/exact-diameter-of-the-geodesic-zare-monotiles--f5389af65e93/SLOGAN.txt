The geodesic Zare monotiles of Liao--Wang have an exact two-branch diameter formula: the lower endpoints are farthest only for \(q=2\), while opposite lower--upper endpoints are farthest for every \(q\ge3\). Their diameter satisfies \(q\operatorname{diam}(T_q)\to\sqrt2\), sharpening the construction's published \(3/q\) bound to the exact asymptotic constant.
