For every regular odd \(n\)-gon, a line through the centroid cuts a shorter boundary arc whose fraction is exactly \(1/2-\tan\alpha\tan(\pi/n-\alpha)/(2n)\) in a symmetry sector. The unique minimizing direction in each sector bisects the gap between adjacent reflection axes, giving the sharp coefficient \(1/2-\tan^2(\pi/(2n))/(2n)\); every regular even polygon is bisected by every central line.
