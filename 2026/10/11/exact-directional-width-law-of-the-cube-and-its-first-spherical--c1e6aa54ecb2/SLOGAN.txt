The random directional width of a unit cube in three dimensions has an explicit elementary density, with an exactly uniform upper tail on \([\sqrt2,\sqrt3]\). In every dimension \(d\ge3\), its high-width tail is a diagonal spherical-cap probability above \(\sqrt{d-1}\), and the first correction below that threshold has sharp order \((\sqrt{d-1}-s)^{d/2}\) with an explicit coefficient.
