Every generalized Lawson band has exact Dirichlet invariants \(\operatorname{ind}_D(\bar\tau_{1,2k})=2k\) and \(\operatorname{null}_D(\bar\tau_{1,2k})=1\). The entire count is controlled by a sharp Fourier threshold: odd modes up to \(2k-1\) are negative, while the explicit positive Jacobi profile at \(2k+1\) forces all higher odd modes to be positive.
