# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The finite-abelian Fourier normalization and dihedral averaging reduce the unrestricted problem to a three-parameter trigonometric positivity program. Exact pentagonal and two-contact sampled Fourier inequalities force mass at most \(5\) when \(5\mid n\) or \(12\mid n\), and the cross autocorrelation attains \(5\). The original Lam–Leung short vanishing sum theorem classifies every zero of the baseline Fourier polynomial. Two distinct supported positive-definite perturbations prove strict excess otherwise, including the delicate case \(6\mid n\) but \(12\nmid n\). All indices and boundary cases \(n\ge5\) are accounted for. Exact rational witness at \(n=6\) and supplementary grid checks confirm the algebra. No finite enumeration is used to prove the infinite statement.

## Originality — PASS, subject to bibliographic limitations
Berdysheva–Révész (arxiv:1904.03614) and Kolountzakis–Révész (arxiv:math/0312218) provide the general positive-definite Turán framework and conditional packing/spectral upper bounds, but their inspected theorems do not establish the precise square-cyclic Lee-ball equality/strictness criterion. The closest publicly checked one-dimensional cyclic five-point Turán calculation and Boolean weight-two shell result optimize different supports and group laws; neither mathematical statement dominates this criterion. The Lam–Leung classification of five-root vanishing sums supplies a crucial lemma but no Turán optimization. Searches also addressed circulant semidefinite, finite-grid, and Lee-ball terminology. Unindexed older exact tables remain a risk; search non-detection is not certainty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The thirteen-point Lee support is the difference set of the canonical five-point cross, so its autocorrelation gives a distinguished benchmark \(5\). Determining precisely when this benchmark is sharp, for every finite square cyclic torus, is a natural exact extremal problem. The surprising arithmetic resonance at \(12\) (alongside the pentagonal resonance at \(5\)) and the strict tangential improvement in the sixfold-only regime are substantive structural information, not an isolated computation.

## Limitations
The theorem characterizes equality with \(5\), not exact mass values when the inequality is strict, nor all extremizers. The nonstandard minimal vanishing-sum classification is cited from the original mathematical source and checked against its theorem statements. Independent mathematical and bibliographic assessment remains outstanding.
