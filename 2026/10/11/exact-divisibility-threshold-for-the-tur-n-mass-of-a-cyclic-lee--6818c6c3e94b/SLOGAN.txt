For the thirteen-point Lee ball of radius two on the finite square torus \((\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)^2\), the normalized positive-definite Turán mass equals its canonical five-point cross-autocorrelation benchmark \(5\) precisely when \(5\mid n\) or \(12\mid n\). For every other \(n\ge5\), an explicit type of supported positive-definite perturbation gives a strict excess.
