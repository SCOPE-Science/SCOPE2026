For diagonal polydisc quotient modules with finite transverse multiplicity, the absolute coordinate self-commutator has an exact logarithmic singular-value coefficient \(2D_i(d-1)\sqrt{1-|\eta_i'(0)|^2}\). Its trace-class endpoint occurs exactly when \(\eta_i\) is a unimodular multiple of \(z\), and the trace norm there is \(D_i\).
