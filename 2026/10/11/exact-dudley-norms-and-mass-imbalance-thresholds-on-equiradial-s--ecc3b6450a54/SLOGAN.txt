On every finite or countable equal-arm star, the real dual bounded-Lipschitz norm of an absolutely summable, zero-total-mass atomic measure is the maximum of two explicit expressions involving the leaf total variation and net signed mass. When both signs occur, the optimizing branch switches sharply from a two-sided sign potential to a one-sided potential as their mass ratio crosses the radius-dependent threshold \(1+2/\rho\), where \(\rho\) is the common arm length.
