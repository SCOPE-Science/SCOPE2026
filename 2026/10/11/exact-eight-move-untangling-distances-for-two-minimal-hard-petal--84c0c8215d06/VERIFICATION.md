# Verification

The finite checker `artifacts/verify_petal_distance.py` implements cyclic word normalization, the published \(\mathsf T^{\pm}\) and \(\mathsf X\) moves, and the dihedral action generated by \(\mathsf R\) and \(\mathsf S\). It first verifies every transition in the two eight-move paths.

For the lower bound, petal counting and hardness reduce every hypothetical path of length at most \(7\) to one addition, five deletions, and at most one preserving move. The checker strengthens the adversary by assigning zero cost to any number of \(\mathsf R\) and \(\mathsf S\) moves, then exhaustively enumerates all states obtainable with one \(\mathsf T^+\), five \(\mathsf T^-\), and zero or one \(\mathsf X\). The one-petal word is absent in both cases for both starting words.

A successful replay prints `VERIFY_OK alpha_distance=8 beta_distance=8`. The run used no nonstandard Python packages. The checker does not certify that the starting words are unknots or that they are hard; those are explicit prior results of arXiv:2610.01596v1 and are stated as assumptions in the proof.
