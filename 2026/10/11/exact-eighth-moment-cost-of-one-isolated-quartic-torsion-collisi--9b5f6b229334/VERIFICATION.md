The bundled checker reconstructs the fourth-convolution moment polynomial from multinomial coefficients, verifies the isolated-collision correction, derives the one-variable polynomial and derivative, computes the exact resultant, and isolates the relevant root. It also compares the formula with direct finite sampling on a concrete realization of the collision pattern.

The checker is corroborative. The theorem for arbitrary compact abelian groups is proved by Parseval, the explicit collision hypothesis, pair smoothing, and one-variable monotonicity; finite sampling is not used as an infinite proof.

The isolated-collision assumption is essential. If another degree-four exponent triple collides, additional cross terms appear and this verification does not cover that spectrum.
