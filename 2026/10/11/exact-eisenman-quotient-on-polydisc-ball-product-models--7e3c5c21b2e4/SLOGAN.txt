For every mixed product \(\Delta^k\times\mathbb B^m\), the Kobayashi–Eisenman volume at the origin is \(1\), while the Carathéodory–Eisenman volume and quotient invariant are exactly \(m^m/(k+m)^{k+m}\). In the scaling model \(\Delta\times\mathbb B^n\), the missing quotient constant is therefore \(n^n/(n+1)^{n+1}\sim1/(en)\).
