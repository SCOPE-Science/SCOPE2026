The unit regular tetrahedron has an exact incomplete-elliptic-integral formula for the mean product of a random planar shadow's area and perimeter: \(\mathbb E[AP]=4\sqrt3/(3\pi)+\sqrt2(2E(\theta\mid-1/2)-F(\theta\mid-1/2))/\pi\), where \(\theta=\arctan\sqrt2\). A rational interval certificate proves its area–perimeter covariance is strictly negative, refining the previously known numerical estimate.
