Every centrally symmetric genuine hexagon has an exactly computable minimum enclosing parallelogram: if \(X,Y,Z\) are its three generator-pair determinant magnitudes and \(M=\max\{X,Y,Z\}\), the unrestricted area ratio is \(1+XYZ/(M^2(X+Y+Z))\). A minimizer uses two hexagon edge directions, and the deficit from the classical \(4/3\) extremum satisfies a sharp affine-invariant stability inequality that forces near-balanced determinants.
