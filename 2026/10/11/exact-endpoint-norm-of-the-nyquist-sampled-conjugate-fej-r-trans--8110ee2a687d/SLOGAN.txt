At the critical \(2n\)-point grid, the first Cesàro-smoothed cyclic Hilbert kernel collapses exactly to cotangent samples, so its endpoint norm is \(C_n=n^{-1}\sum_{r=1}^{n-1}\cot(\pi r/(2n))\). These constants satisfy an exact dyadic bridge to the untapered cyclic Hilbert norm and have a sharp logarithmic expansion through the \(n^{-2}\) term.
