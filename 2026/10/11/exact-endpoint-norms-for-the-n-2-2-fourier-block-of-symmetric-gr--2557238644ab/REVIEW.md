# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof identifies the irreducible character as the difference between the unordered-pair permutation character and the trivial and standard characters, verifies the sign analysis for every nonnegative fixed-point and two-cycle count, and uses exact character orthogonality to replace the full absolute-value sum with twice the mass of the negative set. Class-counting and the exponential cycle formula establish the finite and limiting sharp endpoint norms. The packaged exact checker independently exhausts all 28,622 cycle classes for degrees 4 through 30 and directly enumerates 46,224 permutations through degree 8. Finite checks are corroboration, not a general proof.

## Originality — PASS
The exact character polynomial and its no-short-cycle counting series are present in Barvinok–Stephen (2001) and are credited; Scarabotti–Tolli (2007) includes the same irreducible type within homogeneous-space decompositions. The compared statements do not provide the regular-group central isotypic \(\ell^1/\ell^\infty\) projection norm, the precise negative-support classification as a character-total-variation identity, or the sharp quadratic limiting amplification. Searches under Fourier block, irreducible-character \(L^1\), fixed-point/2-cycle aliases, asymptotic probability coefficients, and published-finding corpus equivalents disclosed no dominating result. OEIS A214418 concerns unweighted sums of all character-table entries, whereas the operator norm uses weighted conjugacy-class multiplicities. The principal risk is a previously published one-line consequence of these classical ingredients under an unfamiliar normalization; absence from the inspected sources does not prove absolute novelty.

## Value — PASS
This is a complete all-degree evaluation of a canonical nonabelian regular-representation Fourier projection, including its sharp endpoint growth coefficient and a strikingly sparse, rigid negative kernel support. The precise invariant controls endpoint amplification of the entire \((n-2,2)\) matrix-coefficient block and is not the Johnson-scheme projector on 2-subsets. Its concise derivation is not used as evidence of significance; value comes from the exact spectral invariant and the uniform structural sign classification rather than from merely reproducing a character polynomial.
