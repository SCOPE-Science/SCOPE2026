The \((n-2,2)\) Fourier block of \(S_n\) has exact sharp endpoint norm \(n(n-3)(r_{n-1}+r_{n-2}/2)\), where \(r_m\) is the density of permutations without 1- or 2-cycles. Its character is negative precisely at permutations with one or two fixed points and no 2-cycles, and its normalized amplification tends to \(\tfrac32e^{-3/2}\).
