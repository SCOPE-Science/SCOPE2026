For every nonlinear irreducible block of a finite generalized dihedral group, the sharp \(\ell^1\) and \(\ell^\infty\) Fourier-projection norms depend only on the order \(L\) of the inducing character and equal one explicit cyclic absolute-cosine average. The block is contractive exactly when \(L=4\), so such a group has a contractive nonlinear isotype exactly when its abelian kernel has exponent divisible by \(4\).
