For the irreducible \(SU(2)\) block of dimension \(m\), the Peter--Weyl projection has exact endpoint norm \(\frac{4m^2}{\pi(m^2-1)}\cot(\pi/(2m))\) for \(m\ge2\), with the trivial block having norm \(1\). Thus one Fourier block already amplifies \(L^1\) and \(L^\infty\) by asymptotic factor \((8/\pi^2)m\).
