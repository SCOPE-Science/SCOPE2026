For the classical Hardy--Littlewood pair \(1+e(x)\pm e(3x)\), the majorant defect has exact algebraic-logarithmic endpoint slopes: \(\Delta'(2)=0.0948207920539202998\ldots\) and \(\Delta'(4)=-0.5424530170069415436\ldots\). Thus the known positive defect on \(2<p<4\) opens and closes linearly with explicitly determined transversality constants.
