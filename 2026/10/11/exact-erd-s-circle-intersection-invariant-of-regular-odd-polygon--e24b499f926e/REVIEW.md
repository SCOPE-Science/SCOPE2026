# Review of Exact Erdős circle-intersection invariant of regular odd polygons

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reduces the upper bound to a one-dimensional distance profile on half of the polygon boundary. The squared distance is strictly increasing edge-by-edge from a chosen vertex to a farthest vertex and strictly decreasing along the final half of the opposite side. Therefore a fixed radius appears at most twice on each reflected half-boundary, giving at most four intersections. The open interval \(V_0M<r<V_0V_k\) gives four, and the published all-boundary lower-bound fact rules out \(N<4\).

Risk: the lower bound is imported from the primary source rather than reproved here; the source statement was inspected directly.

## Originality
PASS. Bárány and Roldán-Pensado state the four-intersection lower-bound property for regular odd polygons but do not state the exact invariant. The closest published published-finding corpus record (hash `ab61f433b81d`) gives only the general estimate \(N(K)\le2n-4\) for convex \(n\)-gons, plus exact triangle values. For odd \(n\ge5\), that estimate is strictly weaker than \(4\). Exact-form published-finding corpus and web searches found no implication-level coverage.

Residual risk: an unindexed elementary note may contain the same observation under different terminology.

## Value
PASS. The foundational paper itself highlights regular odd polygons as the canonical family forcing the conjectured bound above \(2\). The exact value \(4\) completes that natural benchmark family and explains geometrically why a vertex is already an optimal center.
