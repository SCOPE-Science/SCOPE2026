For generalized Hilbert matrices on \(\ell^p\), multiplicatively slowly oscillating coefficient magnitudes have essential norm exactly \(\pi\limsup|g_k|/\sin(\pi(1/p+\gamma))\), even when the usual local block means never converge. This gives the sharp value for the oscillatory example \(g_k=1+\sin(\log\log(k+e))\) and shows compactness is exactly decay of the magnitudes.
