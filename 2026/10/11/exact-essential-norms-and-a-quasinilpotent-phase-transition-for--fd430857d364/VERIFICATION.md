# Verification of the stated commutator theorem

The proof uses exact unitary mappings: differentiation, the Cayley weighted-Bergman transformation, and the weighted Laplace transform onto \(L^2((0,\infty),t^{-\alpha-1}dt)\). The identity \(U_s f(t)=s^{-\alpha/2}f(st)\) is checked by the substitution \(v=st\). The two product orders give \(c_1=1-a+(a/b)(1-b)\), \(c_2=(2-a-b)/b\), and \(c_2-c_1=2(1-a)(1-b)/b>0\). The exact norm maximizer is \(t_* = \log(c_2/c_1)/(c_2-c_1)\) and remains in \((0,\infty)\). The non-atomic-space essential-norm equality is established with infinitely many orthonormal disjointly supported tests, not a finite enumeration. For off-diagonal slopes, \(n\) iterates are bounded by \(B^nq^{-\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor}\) for some finite \(B\) and \(q>1\), uniformly over inputs. For diagonal slopes, the full essential range gives the interval spectrum.

The analytic arguments are complete within the stated strict parameter range. No numerical run is presented as proof or as independent validation. The research comparison excludes any claim of independent certification, and the general linear-fractional problem is not addressed.
