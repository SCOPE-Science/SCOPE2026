For every conjugate Day–James plane \(X_p=\ell_p^2-\ell_{p/(p-1)}^2\) with \(p>2\), the exact Euclidean Banach–Mazur distance is determined by a unique one-variable balance point \(s_p\), and the optimal ellipse has quadratic form \(x^2+y^2+2\rho_pxy\). In particular, \(d_{\mathrm{BM}}(X_4,\ell_2^2)^2=6-2\sqrt6\).
