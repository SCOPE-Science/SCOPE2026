# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Coordinate-swap averaging is applied before the one-parameter optimization, so arbitrary Euclidean pullbacks are covered. The same-sign ratio is \(1+ts^2\); the opposite-sign ratio is
\[
2^{4/3}\frac{t+u}{(1+3u)^{2/3}},
\]
with logarithmic derivative
\[
\frac{1+u-2t}{(t+u)(1+3u)}.
\]
This gives the complete distortion profile and unique optimum \(t=2^{-4/3}\).

## Originality
PASS. The isometric reversed notation \(\ell_3-\ell_1\) was checked explicitly. The closest same-family archival source treats orthogonality constants rather than Euclidean Banach--Mazur distance. Closest exact-distance records concern different exponent pairs or endpoints and do not imply the cubic \(\ell_1-\ell_3\) value.

## Value
PASS. The cubic member is a canonical first nonquadratic odd-power case in the archival \(\ell_1-\ell_p\) Day--James family. Its cubic identity makes the optimal-ellipse problem exactly solvable and produces
\[
d_{\mathrm{BM}}(X,\ell_2^2)^2=1+2^{-4/3}.
\]

## Closest literature and limitations
Nilsrakoo--Saejung (2006) supplies the archival Day--James setting, Alonso (2011) the explicit general definition, and Mizuguchi (2016) a highly relevant treatment of the same \(\ell_1-\ell_p\) family for different constants. The theorem is restricted to \(p=3\).
