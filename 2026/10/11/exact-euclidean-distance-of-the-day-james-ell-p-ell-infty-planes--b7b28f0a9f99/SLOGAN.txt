For every \(2<p<\infty\), the Day--James plane \(\ell_p-\ell_\infty\) has exact squared Euclidean Banach--Mazur distance \(1+2^{-2/p}\), attained by the diagonal-coordinate ellipse \((x+y)^2+2^{-2/p}(x-y)^2\). Together with the known formula \(J(\ell_p-\ell_\infty)=1+2^{-1/p}\), this gives \(d_{\mathrm{BM}}^2=1+(J-1)^2\).
