# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The norm representation as the maximum of two reflected quadratic norms is exact on all sign quadrants. Coordinate-sign averaging reduces every positive-definite Euclidean pullback to a diagonal form without worsening its two-sided comparison constants. The remaining Rayleigh quotient is solved exactly, and the normalized lower bound is global on the whole parameter domain. The diagonal pullback \(H_{a,c}(x,y)=\sqrt{a x^2+c y^2}\) meets that lower bound. The Hilbert and weighted-\(\ell_1^2\) endpoints are included.

## Originality
PASS. The closest archival primary source is Zuo's 2012 paper on Ptolemy constants of absolute normalized planes. Its Cesàro example is one member of the present family, but the inspected full text does not state an all-parameter Banach--Mazur-distance theorem. Searches under the canonical normalized form, piecewise quadratic norms, maxima of Hilbert norms, and optimal ellipses found the isolated Cesàro exact distance and several different planar families, not a result that implies the present claim. A residual risk remains that an equivalent older convex-body formulation is unindexed.

## Value
PASS. The theorem turns a named special case into a complete exact law for a natural family of absolute piecewise-Hilbertian norms, identifies the normalized coupling as the only Euclidean-distortion parameter, and gives an explicit optimal ellipse. The family continuously connects a Hilbert plane to weighted \(\ell_1^2\) and provides a reusable benchmark for planar absolute norms.

## Closest literature and limitations
The principal inspected source is Z. Zuo, *The Ptolemy constant of absolute normalized norms on R^2*, DOI 10.1186/1029-242X-2012-107. The theorem is restricted to real dimension two and the single quadratic absolute coupling. It does not claim uniqueness of all optimal pullbacks, higher-dimensional extensions, or a formula for arbitrary absolute normalized norms. No independent audit has been performed.
