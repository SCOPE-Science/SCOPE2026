For the real zero-sum plane in \(\ell_p^3\), the exact Euclidean Banach--Mazur distance is \(\max\{(1+2^{p-1})^{1/p}/\sqrt3,\sqrt3/(1+2^{p-1})^{1/p}\}\) for every finite \(p\ge1\), with limit \(2/\sqrt3\) at \(p=\infty\). The plane is isometrically Hilbertian exactly at \(p=2\) and \(p=4\), and the two extremal symmetry orbits exchange roles across these exponents.
