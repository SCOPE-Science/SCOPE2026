The zero-sum hyperplane of real \(\ell_4^n\) has an exact Euclidean Banach--Mazur distance whose fourth power is \((n^2-3n+3)/(n-1)\) for even \(n\), and \((n^2-3n+3)(n+1)/(n^2+3)\) for odd \(n\). Symmetric-quartic reduction determines its sharp Euclidean radial extremes, while symmetry averaging proves that no tilted ellipsoid improves the distance.
