For \(n\ge3\), the harmonic polytope has unique incenter \(\bigl((\frac{n+1}{2}+\frac1n)\mathbf1,(\frac{n+1}{2}+\frac1n)\mathbf1\bigr)\) and inradius \((n^2-n+2)/(2\sqrt{n(n-1)})\), with only \(2n\) of its \(3^n-3\) facets touching the maximal ball. At \(n=2\), the radius drops to \(1\) and the incenter becomes a nontrivial line segment.
