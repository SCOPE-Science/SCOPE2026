Ardila's bipermutahedron has a unique Euclidean circumcenter at the origin, and every vertex radius is determined by one Dyck-area statistic. Its vertices occupy exactly \(n(n-1)/2+1\) concentric shells, with circumradius \(\sqrt{2n(n-1)(3n^2+7n+1)/3}\) and \(n((n-1)!)^2\) vertices on the outer shell.
