For the affine-regular hexagonal plane \(N(s,t)=\max\{|s|,|t|,|s-t|\}\), the Liu–Li Euler–Lagrange constant has the exact ratio profile \(L_{\mathrm{YJ}}=\frac{2+r^2+r\sqrt{r^2+4}}{2(1+r^2)}\), where \(r=\min\{\lambda,\mu\}/\max\{\lambda,\mu\}\). Its maximum is \(4/3\), attained exactly when one parameter is \(\sqrt2\) times the other.
