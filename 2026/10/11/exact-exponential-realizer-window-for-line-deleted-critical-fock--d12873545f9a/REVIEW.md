# Review of Exact exponential-realizer window for line-deleted critical Fock zero sets

## Correctness

PASS. The source Hadamard lemma forces every exact realizer to be
\[
C e^{Az^2+Bz}g_N.
\]
Its shifted-cell lower bound makes every nonzero quadratic coefficient impossible, because some sector has positive quadratic real growth. The same lower bound on vertical cells forces the linear coefficient to be real.

For real \(t\), the source upper bound reduces the Fock integrand to exponential damping in the horizontal direction times \((1+|z|^2)^{-N}\). At \(|t|=\sqrt\pi\), the damping cancels on one half-plane; the residual two-dimensional power tail is integrable exactly when \(N\ge2\). For \(N=1\), the source lower cells give a harmonic-series divergence. Outside the window, a horizontal cell sequence gives exponential divergence. These arguments cover all parameters and both endpoints.

## Originality

PASS. The recent source explicitly exhibits \(e^{tz}g_N\) only for the strict interior range \(|t|<\sqrt\pi\), using that family to prove infinite dimensionality. It does not classify all exact realizers or state whether either endpoint belongs.

Searches under exact-realizer, exponential-multiplier, line-deleted-lattice, insertion-index, and endpoint formulations found no covering published statement. Earlier Fock zero-set papers concern dependence on the Fock exponent, hereditary zero sets, or uniqueness-set stability rather than this new explicit critical family.

Residual risk remains that the endpoint classification could have been observed informally or in a very recent unindexed note following the September 2026 preprint.

## Value

PASS. The source's infinite-dimensionality proof leaves the geometry of its exact realizers unresolved. The classification converts an exhibited continuum into the complete exact-realizer family and identifies a genuine endpoint transition: the parameter interval is open for the maximal-under-finite-additions example and closed as soon as one finite insertion is allowed.

This is a natural structural invariant of the source's canonical examples, not a routine recomputation of the insertion index, and it isolates the precise balance between the line-deletion exponential decay and the polynomial decay created by the extra deleted points.
