For the critical square-lattice zero sets obtained by deleting one lattice line and \(N\) extra points, every exact Bargmann--Fock realizer is \(C e^{tz}g_N\) with real \(t\). The multiplier window is \(|t|<\sqrt\pi\) for \(N=1\) and \(|t|\le\sqrt\pi\) for \(N\ge2\), so the two endpoints enter exactly when the source insertion index becomes positive.
