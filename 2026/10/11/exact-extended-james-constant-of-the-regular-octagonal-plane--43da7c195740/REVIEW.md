# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reduces the modulus-of-smoothness supremum exactly to five dual-octagon support separations, proves which affine branch dominates throughout \(0\le t\le1\), and then converts the \(J_4\) optimization to a one-variable bound. The identity
\[
2(1+q^2)-\frac{2(1+qt)^2}{1+t^2}=\frac{2(t-q)^2}{1+t^2}
\]
with \(q=\sqrt2-1\) proves the global upper bound, and the displayed pair attains it. The exact checker independently replays the algebraic identities used by the proof. Risk: the dual-support reduction must use all eight support functionals; RESULT.md does so explicitly.

## Originality
PASS. The 2022 defining full text introduces \(J_4\), gives general comparisons, and does not calculate the regular-octagon value. The closest inspected regular-octagon paper computes \(L_{YJ}\) and \(L'_{YJ}\), not the minimum defining \(J_4\). Targeted published-index and web searches for the exact radical, \(J_4\) aliases, the smoothness profile, and broader polygon coverage returned no dominating statement. Available prior-result ledgers contain many octagonal constants and one result about another constant from the same 2022 source, but none states or implies this \(J_4\) value. Residual risk is unindexed literature, not a decisive unresolved comparison.

## Value
PASS. The regular octagon is repeatedly used in the literature as a benchmark non-Hilbert plane with \(J(X_8)=\sqrt2\). The exact value supplies a natural missing benchmark for a recently introduced James-type invariant and, more structurally, exhibits a strict separation \(J_4(X_8)>J(X_8)\) with a short exact mechanism through the smoothness profile. This is not a parameter renaming or a table recomputation.
