For a regular \(n\)-simplex, the abstract face-angle constant in Kenderov--Mushkarov--Nikolov's far-observer theorem is \(\sqrt{rs/((r+1)(s+1))}\), where \(r=\lfloor n/2\rfloor\) and \(s=\lceil n/2\rceil\). This makes their sufficient exceptional radius explicit and shows that the worst face pair is a balanced pair sharing one vertex and together using every simplex vertex.
