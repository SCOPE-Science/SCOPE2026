For every \(k\ge3\), the first conjecturally shellable squared-cycle diagonal \(\Delta_k(W_{k+6})\) has an explicit six-entry face vector and reduced Euler characteristic \(-\frac{k^4+2k^3-21k^2+18k+8}{8}\). If the published shellability conjecture holds there, the complex is forced to be a wedge of exactly \(\frac{k^4+2k^3-21k^2+18k+8}{8}\) five-spheres.
