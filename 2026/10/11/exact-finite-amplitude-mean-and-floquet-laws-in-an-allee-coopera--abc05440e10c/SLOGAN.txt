In Xue's Allee–cooperation predator–prey model, every positive periodic orbit has mean prey density exactly \(e/(\varepsilon c)\), while its mean predator density falls below the coexistence-equilibrium value by an explicit positive weighted-variance defect. The same nonlinear predator factor also cancels from the period-integrated divergence, leaving an exact one-dimensional formula for the orbit's nontrivial Floquet multiplier.
