# Review: finite-atom fourth-norm mean reflection

**Correctness — PASS.** The real induced norm is the optimum of a symmetric homogeneous quartic ratio. Riener's stated and proved half-degree principle permits a global two-value reduction, including vectors with zero coordinate values through the projective endpoint. The two-value rational function and its quartic stationary polynomial are expanded exactly. The eight-atom calculation checks the three nonconstant multiplicities, with a strict rational-polynomial separation of the winning branch. The Pearson inequality and its equality conditions independently establish the sharp unrestricted bound and rule out equality on uniform finite atoms. The accompanying checker reproduces the critical polynomial identities; its finite diagnostic sweep is not used as an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS, with residual bibliographic risk.** Riener (arXiv:1001.4464, Theorem 1.3) proves the general two-level criterion, not the finite fourth-norm reflection constants. Blekherman--Riener also states that criterion (Proposition 3.1). Osękowski (DOI 10.1155/2013/646012) treats restricted weak-type Haar projections rather than this one-sign strong \(L^4\) norm. Shargorodsky--Sharia (DOI 10.1002/mana.202100217), especially Section 1, treats the distinct centering operator \(I-E\), including finite uniform-atom results; its formula does not provide the constants for \(I-2E\). No covering source was identified in the targeted indexed and published-result comparisons, which is not itself proof of novelty. The 2016 Lewicki--Skrzypek hyperplane-projection paper and 1990 Franchetti paper could not be fully inspected and remain residual risks.

**Value — PASS.** The family is an intrinsic sharp stability invariant for a canonical rank-one involution on finite uniform \(L^4\) spaces. The all-dimension algebraic profile and exact first nontrivial eight-atom sharpness calculation are motivated by the gap between infinite probability-space and finite-data norms. The strict nonattainment in *every* finite dimension, explained by an irrational extremal mass, and recovery of the unrestricted value as dimension grows reveal a structural boundary, not a generic computation or parameter substitution. Claims about arbitrary Haar signs, complex scalars, and weighted atom probabilities are excluded.
