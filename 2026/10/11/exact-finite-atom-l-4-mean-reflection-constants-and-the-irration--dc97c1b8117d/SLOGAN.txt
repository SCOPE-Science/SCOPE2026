The exact real \(\ell_4^n\) norm of the rank-one involution that reverses the constant-coordinate direction and fixes the mean-zero hyperplane is determined, in every finite dimension, by finitely many explicit quartic stationary equations. Every finite uniform-atom constant is strictly below \(\sqrt{1+\sqrt2}\), although the constants converge to that bound; for eight atoms the exact algebraic value is \(1.5445210906900528884\ldots\).
