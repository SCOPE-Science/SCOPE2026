For the canonical roots-of-unity lemniscate, the finite-degree vertical-width obstruction is attained exactly: \(\rho(z^n-1)=(2\sin\alpha_n)^{1/n}\sin\alpha_n\) with \(\alpha_n=\pi/[2(n+1)]\). An explicit largest disk is tangent at the two boundary points of arguments \(\pm\alpha_n\), and the exact radius has a logarithmically enhanced \(n^{-2}\) deficit from \(\pi/(2n)\).
