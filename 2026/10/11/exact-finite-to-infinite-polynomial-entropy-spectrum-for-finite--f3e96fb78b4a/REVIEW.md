# Review

## Correctness
PASS. On regular curves the checked source records \(\mathcal{CR}(f)=\mathrm{NW}(f)\); finite chain recurrence and nondegeneracy force a wandering point. The regular-curve entropy theorem then gives the base value \(1\), and the finite-chain-recurrence suspension theorem multiplies it by \(n\) for every \(\mathcal{S}F_n^{m}\). The full-hyperspace value follows from the regular-curve hyperspace corollary. All hypotheses and integer ranges were matched directly to full text.

## Originality
PASS. published-finding corpus searches for regular-curve, finite-chain-recurrence, generalized-suspension, full-hyperspace, and both source identifiers found no implication-equivalent result. The 2025 paper gives the finite-layer multiplier but not the all-regular-curve/full-hyperspace classification; the 2026 paper gives regular-curve base, \(F_n\), and \(2^f\) values but not suspension quotients. Earlier generalized-suspension work studies qualitative dynamical properties rather than polynomial entropy.

## Value
PASS. The claim gives a complete exact spectrum over a natural hierarchy of induced systems, including a sharp transition from finite linear levels \(n\) to infinite polynomial entropy on \(2^X\). It simultaneously covers all finite \(n\) and all quotient levels \(m<n\), rather than a routine single-parameter instance.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest sources are arXiv:2507.10126v1 and arXiv:2602.19256v1. The result does not extend the classification to arbitrary continuous maps, to infinite chain recurrent sets, or beyond regular curves. An equivalent synthesis under different terminology remains a residual bibliographic risk.
