# Review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The proof expands the base energy into torus characters, separates the deterministic diagonal, classifies every equality among nontrivial character monomials by the sole interchange \(d\leftrightarrow k\), and counts the multiplicity-one diagonal \(d=k\). Haar orthogonality then converts multiplicity counts directly into the variance. The endpoint and parity sums are explicit, and the relation \(X_n=n^2+2E_n\) transfers the result to the fourth norm.

The exact enumerator independently checks all exponent-vector multiplicities for \(2\le n\le40\). This computation is corroborative rather than the justification for the universal quantifier.

## Originality
**PASS.** The closest primary harmonic-analysis source, arXiv:1302.2766, supplies the fourth-norm/autocorrelation identity and deterministic extremal context but not a random finite-variance theorem. The closest directly inspected random-unimodular average-norm paper, Choi–Mossinghoff (2011), gives the classical mean and asymptotic moment concentration, including vanishing variance after normalization, but not the exact finite-\(n\) parity law. Searches under variance, fourth-norm, autocorrelation-energy, merit-factor, and random-phase aliases did not reveal a stronger statement that implies the claim.

Residual risk remains that an equivalent covariance count appears in older random-sequence or radar literature under different notation.

The result converts qualitative asymptotic concentration into an exact all-\(n\) law, exposes a genuine even/odd correction, and gives an explicit benchmark for random-phase autocorrelation energy.
