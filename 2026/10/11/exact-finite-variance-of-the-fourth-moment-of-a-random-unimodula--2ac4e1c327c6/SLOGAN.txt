For independent Haar phases, the fourth-power \(L^4\) norm of a length-\(n\) unimodular polynomial has an exact parity-sensitive finite variance: \(\frac{2n(n-2)(4n-7)}3\) for even \(n\), and \(\frac{2(n-1)(4n^2-11n+3)}3\) for odd \(n\). The formula identifies the fluctuation scale \(\operatorname{Var}(\|P_n\|_4^4)\sim \frac83 n^3\) by classifying the only collisions among the underlying torus characters.
