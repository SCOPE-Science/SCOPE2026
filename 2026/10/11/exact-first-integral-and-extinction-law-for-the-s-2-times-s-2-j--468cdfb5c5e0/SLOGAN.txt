The unnormalized \(J\)-flow on \(S^2\times S^2\) has the exact first integral \((A-B)/(A+B)^3\), which reduces the full product trajectory to one scalar equation. Its two factors become extinct simultaneously at \(T=(A_0^2+4A_0B_0+B_0^2)/4\), with anisotropy decaying linearly in the remaining time.
