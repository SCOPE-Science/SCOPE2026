# Review of Exact first-two inverse logarithmic coefficient bodies for Sakaguchi symmetric classes

## Correctness

PASS. The source identities reduce the first two inverse logarithmic coefficients to the first two coefficients of one Carathéodory function. The exact two-coefficient Schur parameterization gives
\[
c_1=2u,
\qquad
c_2=2\left(u^2+(1-|u|^2)v\right),
\qquad
|u|\le1,
\quad
|v|\le1.
\]
Substitution yields the two claimed disk fibers exactly.

The converse is also proved rather than assumed. Every admissible
\[
(u,v)
\]
is realized by a Schur function, and the associated positive-real-part function is lifted to an element of each Sakaguchi class by solving the even and odd parts of the defining differential identity. Thus the inequalities describe exact attainable bodies.

The moduli-difference endpoints are obtained by computing the minimum and maximum modulus of a point in each disk fiber and then optimizing the remaining scalar radius. The lower extrema occur at the unique transition where the fiber first reaches the origin.

## Originality

PASS. The fully inspected 2026 lead preprint displays only sharp upper inequalities for the two symmetric-point classes, although its abstract uses broader upper-and-lower language. A fully inspected 2025 paper on the same inverse logarithmic coefficients gives sharp individual bounds for
\[
\Gamma_1,
\quad
\Gamma_2,
\quad
\Gamma_3,
\]
but not their joint attainable body.

A September 2026 article on logarithmic coefficients of the same classes was checked at the public abstract level because the complete text is subscription-only. Its abstract does not state a joint first-two inverse-logarithmic coefficient region. Searches for coefficient bodies, variability regions, both lower constants, and equivalent Schur-fiber formulas located no implication-equivalent statement.

## Value

PASS. The recent lead paper is explicitly organized around the functional
\[
|\Gamma_2|-|\Gamma_1|,
\]
but its first two theorem statements provide only the upper endpoint. The exact joint bodies solve the stronger natural classification problem for the same coefficient pair and automatically supply both missing lower endpoints.

This is not merely a recomputation of separate coefficient bounds: separate sharp values of
\[
|\Gamma_1|
\]
and
\[
|\Gamma_2|
\]
do not determine their dependence. The disk fibers give the complete dependence geometry and identify exactly where the lower moduli-difference extremizers occur.
