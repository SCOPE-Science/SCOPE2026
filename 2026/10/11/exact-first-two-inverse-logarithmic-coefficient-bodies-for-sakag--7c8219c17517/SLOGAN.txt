For the two classical Sakaguchi symmetric-point classes, the first two inverse logarithmic coefficients form exact disk-fiber bodies rather than merely satisfying separate sharp bounds. The bodies yield the missing sharp lower moduli-difference endpoints \(-1/\sqrt6\) for \(\mathcal S_S^*\) and \(-1/(3\sqrt2)\) for \(\mathcal K_S\), while recovering the known upper endpoints.
