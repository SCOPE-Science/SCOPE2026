For the canonical Euclidean \(r\)-disk-triangle perturbation used to prove optimal order in ball-convex Pál stability, the full area deficit is an explicit circular-segment formula and its exact slope is \(L(r,w)=\frac{s}{2c}\sin(2\arcsin u+\pi/3)\). This yields the explicit necessary Hausdorff-stability bound \(C\ge c/(2L)\), interpolating from \(\sqrt3/(2w)\) at \(r=w\) to \(1/w\) in the classical limit.
