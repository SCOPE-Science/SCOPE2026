On the finite parabola over \(\mathbb F_7\), every exactly five-supported complex Fourier sum has at most five zeros, strictly improving the motivating general odd-support bound of six in this first five-support case. The bound is attained by exactly \(3234\) projective full-support coefficient vectors per support, arranged in \(66\) quadratic-linear character orbits; an exhaustive exact-arithmetic certificate proves both counts.
