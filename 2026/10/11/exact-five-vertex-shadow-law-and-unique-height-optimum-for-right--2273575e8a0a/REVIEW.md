## originality

PASS

Finch 2012 provides only the symmetric regular-octahedral special probability. A separate Resultary published affine-octahedron solid-angle theorem provides a more general cone-sum identity. Completing the pyramid by the reflected apex yields an affine octahedron; six-vertex shadows of that completion correspond to five-vertex pyramidal shadows. Hence a general conic-angle expression for the entire probability family is covered by prior work and is NOT claimed novel as an angle-sum method or generic exact probability. Crucially, that result does not determine the strict unique height optimizer, fixed-area aspect monotonicity, or 4/pi inverse-sqrt asymptotic, which require independent nontrivial scalar differentiation and limit analysis. Other triaxial-octahedron optimization assumes perpendicular axes and does not cover this nonorthogonal completion. Surviving mathematically original claimed optimization qualifies; priority caveat is explicit.
