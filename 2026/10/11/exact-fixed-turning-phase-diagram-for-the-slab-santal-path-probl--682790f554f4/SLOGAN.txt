The fixed-turning causal-path problem behind the slab Santaló theorem has a unique momentum phase: below the closing threshold the source momentum \(p_a\) is optimal, while above it a unique larger momentum is selected by an explicit scalar closing equation. As the turning parameter approaches \(1\), the optimal action collapses sharply like \(2^{(n+2)/2}(1-a)^{1/2}/\sqrt{n+1}\).
