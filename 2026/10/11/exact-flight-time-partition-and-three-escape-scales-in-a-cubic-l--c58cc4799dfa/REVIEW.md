# Mathematical assessment

## Correctness — PASS

For all real parameters \(\mu,\kappa>0\), the rational first integral
\[
H(x,y)=\frac{x^2+y^2}{1+2\mu y-\kappa y^2}
\]
reduces the non-invariant-line trajectories to exact conics. Both center annuli, the two invariant horizontal lines, the invariant parabola, and the remaining hyperbolic sectors are separated by the sign and level of \(H\). The exact physical periods are \(T_0=2\pi\) and \(T_1=2\pi\mu/\sqrt{\mu^2+\kappa}\); Riccati and quartic integrations give the complete noncompact flight times \(F_\pm=\pi(1\pm\mu/\sqrt{\mu^2+\kappa})\) and \(F_{\rm p}=2\pi\mu/\sqrt{\mu^2+\kappa}\). Therefore \(T_0=F_-+F_+\) and \(T_1=F_{\rm p}=F_--F_+\), exactly in the original physical time. The Riccati, quartic and hyperbolic equations yield sharp finite-time escape exponents \(1\), \(1/3\), and \(1/2\), respectively, with the constants in the unchanged scientific result. Symbolic identities and finite parameter quadratures corroborate, but do not replace, these universal analytic arguments.

## Originality — PASS, best-of-knowledge

Zhou (2023) proves local origin isochronicity for this exact cubic family, and earlier cubic/quartic bi-center papers classify center coexistence, local normal forms and global phase portraits. These are established prior results. The surviving contribution concerns the complete original-time noncompact flight/period sum-and-difference relation and its sharp global escape exponents and coefficients; such information is not logically forced by local linearizability or orbital topology. Comparable published findings for other cubic systems were checked, and the identical published entry was excluded as a self-match. The complete 2020 symmetric-cubic and 2022 symmetric-quartic manuscripts could not be examined; any older equivalent exact physical-time calculation remains a priority risk. No absolute novelty or priority certification is asserted.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result describes exact original-time observables on natural boundaries of two period annuli of a classical cubic family, and distinguishes all escaping strata through precise singularity coefficients. This is a motivated global classification rather than an isolated low-order computation. It makes no general claim about perturbations, parameter axes, complex dynamics or other families.
