In a natural cubic \(\Lambda\)–\(\Omega\) family, two distinct isochronous centers have periods equal to the sum and difference of the finite infinity-to-infinity flight times on two invariant lines; the difference also equals the exact flight time along an unbounded parabolic separatrix. Every nonperiodic orbit blows up in finite physical time, with three sharp geometric escape exponents \(1\), \(1/2\), and \(1/3\).
