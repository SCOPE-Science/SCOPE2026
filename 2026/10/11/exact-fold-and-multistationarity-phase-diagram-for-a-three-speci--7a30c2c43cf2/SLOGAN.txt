For the four-reaction three-species mass-action example, the fixed-parameter equilibrium diagram is controlled by the sharp threshold \(T=(\theta_2-2\theta_1)^3/(216\theta_2^2)\) when \(\theta_2>2\theta_1\). Below that threshold two folds bound the exact three-equilibrium window in \(K\); equality is the unique cusp boundary, and above it every admissible class has exactly one positive equilibrium.
