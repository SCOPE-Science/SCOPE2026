The three-parameter positive Cantor–Bernstein proof has an exact one-sided guarantee frontier: the best inverse-regular bound under a forward budget \(R>2\) is \(9/(R-2)\) up to \(R=3\), then \(2+5/(R-2)+2/(R-2)^2\). Its unique product minimizer is the published \(9+6\sqrt3\) point, while the full profile exposes a sharp regime change at \(R=3\).
