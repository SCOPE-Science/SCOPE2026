For every bounded weighted composition operator on a real or complex weighted quasi-Banach \(\ell_p\) sequence space with \(0<p\le1\), the approximation, Bernstein, Gelfand and Kolmogorov numbers all equal the same generalized decreasing rearrangement of the input-fiber norms. A strict block-dominance argument proves the Kolmogorov equality without assuming that the operator range admits a contractive projection.
