# Review of Exact Fourier-algebra norm from induced edges in symmetric rank-three actions

## Correctness
PASS. The proof fixes the normalization, identifies the rank-three permutation module as \(\mathbf1\oplus V_\theta\oplus V_\tau\), computes each Fourier block from right-coset averaging, and evaluates the two rank-one trace norms through the strongly regular spectral projectors. Boundary cases are consistent: \(J=\varnothing\) gives norm \(0\), a singleton gives norm \(1\), and \(J=\Omega\) gives norm \(1\). An exhaustive exact-rational replay for all \(64\) subsets in the \(S_4\) action on two-subsets confirms the actual convolution singular-value spectrum.

## Originality
PASS. The closest inspected harmonic-analysis source, arXiv:0912.0308, defines the norm, proves coset norm \(1\), and gives general inverse structure, but not an exact formula for unions in rank-three actions. arXiv:0705.4277 treats Fourier algebras of coset spaces at the structural and amenability level, not this indicator-norm calculation. arXiv:1805.02168 gives decision-tree bounds rather than exact spherical trace norms. Searches under rank-three, Gelfand-pair, strongly-regular, point-stabilizer, and coset-union aliases did not locate a statement implying the formula. Residual risk remains from older association-scheme or spherical-function terminology.

## Value
PASS. This is an infinite-family structural identity, not a finite table: it converts a nonabelian Fourier-algebra norm problem for every union of point-stabilizer cosets in every symmetric rank-three action into the elementary pair \((r,e_X(J))\). It therefore links quantitative idempotent norms to induced-subgraph statistics in strongly regular orbital graphs and supplies exact benchmarks substantially sharper than the triangle bound from summing coset indicators.
