The sharp fourth-power Fourier extension constant for the parabola over \(\mathbb Z/p^k\mathbb Z\) (odd \(p\)) is \(p^{k/2}\) at even exponents and \((2-1/p)p^{(k-1)/2}\) at odd exponents. Every extremizer concentrates on one specific prime-power coset, and its complete phase structure is classified by cyclic characters and, in odd exponents, independent daughter-coset phases.
