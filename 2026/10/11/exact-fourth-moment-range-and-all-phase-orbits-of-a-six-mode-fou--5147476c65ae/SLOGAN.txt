The six-mode unimodular Fourier rectangle has exactly the fourth-moment range \([50,114]\): its minimum has three phase-modulation orbits and its maximum has only character-phase coefficients. The optimal aperiodic merit factor is \(18/7\), and the minimum admits \(q\)-phase coefficients exactly when \(q\) is even, with \(q^3\) or \(3q^3\) minimizers according as \(q\equiv2\pmod4\) or \(4\mid q\).
