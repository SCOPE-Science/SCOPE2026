For the first nontrivial odd cyclic order, the real zero-mean cyclic Hilbert transform has exact fourth-power norm \(X_*\), where \(X_*=2.0896890045699924\ldots\) is algebraic and \(\|H\|_4=X_*^{1/4}=1.2023209494390236\ldots\). Its extremizers form a single orbit under real scaling and cyclic translation.
