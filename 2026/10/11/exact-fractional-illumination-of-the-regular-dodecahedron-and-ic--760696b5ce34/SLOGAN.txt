The regular dodecahedron has ordinary illumination number \(5\) but fractional illumination number \(4\), while the regular icosahedron also has fractional illumination number \(4\). A symmetry formula \(i^*(P)=f_0(P)/\alpha(P)\) for vertex-transitive polytopes reduces both exact evaluations to finite central-halfspace certificates in the polar Platonic solid.
