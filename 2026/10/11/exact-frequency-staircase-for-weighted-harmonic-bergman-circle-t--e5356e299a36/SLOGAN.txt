At \(p=2\), the weighted harmonic Bergman circle-trace problem has an exact spectral staircase: the sharp constant is the largest beta-weighted Fourier diagonal ratio, and the extremizing degree changes only at \(r_m^2=m/(m+\alpha+1)\). This makes the recent universal circle coefficient strictly nonsharp at every fixed radius while showing it remains asymptotically unimprovable as \(r\downarrow0\).
