The Fu--Yang--Liu--Li skew James constant of the affine-regular hexagonal plane has the full exact profile \(J[t,H]=(t^2+t+1)/(t+1)\) for every \(t>0\). This fills the minimum-type endpoint left open by the published hexagonal skew-constant calculations and makes the source's general James-comparison bound strictly nonsharp for every \(t\ne1\).
