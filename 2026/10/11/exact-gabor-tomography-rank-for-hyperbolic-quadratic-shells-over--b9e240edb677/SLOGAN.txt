The full Gabor intensity-lifting operator generated by the indicator of \(\{(x,y)\in\mathbb F_q^{2r}:x\cdot y=1\}\), for odd prime powers \(q\) and \(r\ge2\), has an explicit arithmetic rank and a completely classified Weyl-basis kernel. The zero pattern of its ambiguity function is controlled exactly by isotropic versus anisotropic shifts and one quadratic-character test; for fixed \(q\), the surviving operator fraction tends to \((q-1)(q+2)/(2q^2)\).
