For the regular octagonal Banach plane \(N(s,t)=\max\{|s|,|t|,(|s|+|t|)/\sqrt2\}\), Gao's lower von Neumann--Jordan constant is exactly \((2+\sqrt2)/4\). A sharp Euclidean comparison gives the lower bound, and the coordinate/diagonal unit pair attains it even though the published sufficient maximizer-matching condition is not met.
