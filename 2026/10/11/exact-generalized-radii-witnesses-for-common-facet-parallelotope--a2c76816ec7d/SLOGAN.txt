Xi's three-dimensional generalized-radii conjecture is exactly sharp for every equal-volume origin-symmetric pair of common-facet parallelotopes. If the three facet-distance ratios are \(a_1,a_2,a_3\) with \(a_1a_2a_3=1\), any two of them give an explicit generalized-radius witness, and all four mixed-volume inequalities become equalities.
