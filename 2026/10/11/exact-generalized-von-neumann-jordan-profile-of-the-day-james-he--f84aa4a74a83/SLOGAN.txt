For the affine-regular Day--James hexagon, the full exponent-\(p\) generalized von Neumann--Jordan constant reduces to one scalar maximum. For every \(p>1\), the unique optimizing scale \(\tau_p\) satisfies \(\tau_p^{1-p}-(1+\tau_p)^{1-p}=1\), giving \(C_{\mathrm{NJ}}^{(p)}=((1+\tau_p)/(2\tau_p))^{p-1}\), while \(C_{\mathrm{NJ}}^{(1)}=2\).
