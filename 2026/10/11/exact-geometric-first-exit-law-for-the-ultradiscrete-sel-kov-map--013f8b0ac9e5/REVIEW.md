# Review of the exact Sel'kov survivor-area and first-exit theorem

## Correctness

**PASS.** On each strict positive-branch itinerary, the centered map is multiplication by the integer matrix \(A\). The first seven exact row vectors give a bounded seven-vertex polygon of area \(79B^2/128\). The inequalities at shifted indices \(j=1,7\) make the earliest test redundant after shifting the surviving itinerary by one step from the sharp threshold \(n=6\), yielding exact set renormalization by \(A^{-1}\). The determinant gives the area decay and the normalized area ratios give the first-exit distribution. The intersection of all survivors is the fixed point because \(A^{-8}=I/16\). All strict branch boundaries are treated and have zero area. An exact-rational checker reproduces the critical finite identities, but the infinite conclusions rely on explicit induction.

## Originality

**PASS, with residual priority risk.** The closest primary results are Ohmori–Yamazaki's 2021 map and 2022 Poincaré/basin papers, which derive the map, seven-cycles, their stability and one-dimensional backward-basin curves. The 2022 journal paper's heptagon describes the invariant limit-cycle geometry in transformed coordinates, not the set of planar initial points surviving several consecutive \(X>0\) tests. Its equations (2)–(4), and the 2024 generalized model's affine branch matrix, provide ingredients; neither gives the exact all-\(n\) planar survivor sets, coefficient, or geometric exit law. Searches for survival area, residence-time law, first passage, equivalent parameterizations, and exact polygon coefficients did not find a covering result in the inspected literature or mathematical records. Priority remains conditional on unlocated specialized literature and independent verification.

## Value

**PASS.** The original work analyzes limit cycles and singular basin sets; this result computes a complementary **two-dimensional transient statistic** for arbitrary initial states. The sharp inverse-image-renormalization onset occurs after seven branch tests (the first six-step survivor set). Its nontrivial area coefficient and the failure of the same law at the preceding time determine an exact memoryless first-exit law for every subsequent time, not just a local linearization or a finite numerical experiment. The theorem is specific rather than a general claim for arbitrary ultradiscrete models.

## Limits and sources

The result concerns strict first exit from \(X>0\), not final attracting-cycle capture. Sampling must be uniform in area on the seven-tested-coordinate survivor heptagon. Primary sources: Ohmori–Yamazaki, arXiv:2107.02435, arXiv:2204.00745, *JSIAM Letters* 14 (2022), 127–130, and arXiv:2402.06230. A broader published result could supersede the exact planar law, although the inspected sources do not do so. Independent mathematical assessment has not been performed.
