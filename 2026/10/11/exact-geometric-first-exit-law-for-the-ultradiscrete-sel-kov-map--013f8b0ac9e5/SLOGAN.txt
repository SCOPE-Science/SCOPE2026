For the ultradiscrete Sel'kov map, the full-plane strict expanding-branch survivor area is exactly \(79B^2/2^{n+1}\) at every \(n\ge6\), with the six-step cutoff being the earliest exact affine-renormalization stage. Uniform planar initial states conditioned on surviving through time six then have an exact geometric first-exit delay: first exit at time \(7+k\) has probability \(2^{-k-1}\), for every \(k\ge0\).
