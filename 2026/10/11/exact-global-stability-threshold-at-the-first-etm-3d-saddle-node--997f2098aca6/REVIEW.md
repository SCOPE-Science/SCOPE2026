PASS. Cubic dominance forces generalized energy preservation for any globally strict centered quadratic certificate; coefficient comparison leaves only \(\operatorname{diag}(a,1,2-a)\). The weighted derivative matrix is negative definite exactly when an explicit cubic \(F(a,\rho)\) is positive. Its discriminant and the equilibrium-quartic discriminant share the same degree-eight factor, and exact Sturm counts certify the root topology. A strict quadratic inequality gives global exponential stability below the first root.
