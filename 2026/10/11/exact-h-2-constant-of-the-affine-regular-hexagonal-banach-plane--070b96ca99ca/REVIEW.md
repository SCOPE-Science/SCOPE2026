# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The defining source and pointwise symmetry were inspected directly. The new argument gives an exhaustive exact facet decomposition of every unit isosceles pair in the affine-regular hexagon, reduces the eight resulting segments to two canonical families, and proves a sharp lower bound on \(N(x+\lambda y)/\sqrt{1+\lambda^2}\). The minus family reaches \(\sqrt{32/45}\) exactly at \(u=1/3\), \(\lambda=3\); hence \(H_2=3\sqrt{10}/8\). The bundled checker reproduces the finite classification and all factor identities.

## Value
PASS. The affine-regular hexagon is a canonical nonsmooth Banach plane, and \(H_2\) is a recently introduced Hilbert-deviation constant. The exact interior extremizer \(\lambda=3\) and the two-family classification supply structural information beyond a numerical evaluation and provide a reusable method for polygonal spaces.
