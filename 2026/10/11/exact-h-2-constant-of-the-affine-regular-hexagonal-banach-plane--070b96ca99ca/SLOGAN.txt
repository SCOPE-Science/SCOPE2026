For the affine-regular hexagonal plane \(N(s,t)=\max\{|s|,|t|,|s-t|\}\), the Baronti--Bertella quadratic isosceles constant is exactly \[H_2=\frac{3\sqrt{10}}8.\] The sharp pair is \(x=(1,1/3)\), \(y=(-1/3,-1)\) with \(\lambda=3\); an exact facet decomposition reduces every unit isosceles pair to two one-parameter families.\n
