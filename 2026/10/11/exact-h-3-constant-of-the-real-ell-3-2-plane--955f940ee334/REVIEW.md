# Review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The proof starts from the source definition of the \(p\)-isosceles constant and classifies every real isosceles-orthogonal unit pair in \(\ell_3^2\) up to signed coordinate isometries and interchange of the two vectors. Both ordered branches required by the source symmetry reduction are retained. The inactive branch is bounded by \(1\) through exact concavity endpoint checks. The active branch is optimized globally: the stationarity resultant factors exactly into one strictly increasing quartic, a factor with a positive Bernstein certificate, and boundary factors; symmetry leaves one interior critical point, and the boundary and diagonal comparisons are exact. The bundled standard-library checker independently replays the critical resultant and sign certificates.

## Originality
**PASS.** The defining 2024 Baronti–Bertella manuscript was inspected through the definition, reduction, universal-bound section, Hilbert computation, \(\ell_\infty^2\) computation, and concluding equality discussion. It does not state \(H_3(\ell_3^2)\). Targeted searches for the exact invariant, \(\ell_3^2\), aliases involving isosceles orthogonality, and the algebraic parameter found no statement implying the result. The closest public records concern \(H_4\) on a different hexagonal plane, \(T_\perp\) on \(\ell_3^2\), Dragomir approximate symmetry on \(\ell_3^2\), and a different generalized James constant on \(\ell_p\). None dominates or specializes to the present claim.

## Value
**PASS.** The pair \((p,X)=(3,\ell_3^2)\) is a natural first smooth non-Hilbert classical test case for the new \(p\)-isosceles invariant, with the invariant exponent matching the ambient cubic norm. The exact answer is not a routine substitution into a known table: it requires a complete isosceles-pair classification and a nontrivial interior algebraic optimization. It supplies a sharp benchmark for future study of \(H_p(\ell_q^2)\), and shows that the extremal geometry can be genuinely interior rather than inherited from the source's Hilbert or square examples.

## Closest literature and limitations
The primary source is Baronti–Bertella, DOI 10.1007/s00009-024-02654-9. The closest public comparison records are the \(H_4\) equality-conjecture counterexample on a hexagonal norm, the exact \(T_\perp\) value on \(\ell_3^2\), the Dragomir-symmetry value on \(\ell_3^2\), and the generalized James profile on \(\ell_p\). These use different exponents, spaces, orthogonality notions, or objective functionals. No formula beyond the real two-dimensional \(p=3\) case is asserted, and an unindexed prior computation under different notation remains possible.
