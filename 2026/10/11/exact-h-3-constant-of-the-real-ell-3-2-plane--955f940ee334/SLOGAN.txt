For the real cubic plane \(\ell_3^2\), the Baronti–Bertella \(3\)-isosceles constant is exactly \(H_3=\bigl((1+t_*^3)^2/(8t_*^3+(1-t_*^2)^3)\bigr)^{1/3}=1.0290679924451534\ldots\), where \(t_*\) is the unique root in \(0<t<1\) of \(t^4+6t^3+4t^2+2t-1=0\). The extremizer is an interior isosceles-orthogonal configuration, not an axis or diagonal pair.
