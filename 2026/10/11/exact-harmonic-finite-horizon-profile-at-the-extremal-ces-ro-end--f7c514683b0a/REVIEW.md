# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The exact iterate formula is obtained by telescoping the weights. At the critical exponent \(r=q_0/p=1/\beta\), Minkowski reduces the full vector supremum to the scalar quantities \(A_{j,N}\). Their supremum is exactly \(NH_N\): for \(j\le N\), use \(jH_j\le NH_N\); for \(j\ge N\), each term \(j/(j-k)\) decreases with \(j\). The vector \(e_N\) attains the resulting bound. The countable sum is justified by finite-coordinate truncation and monotone convergence.

## Originality
PASS. Arnold's focal example gives the same shift and the basis-vector identity \(H_j\), using it only to prove failure of strong endpoint boundedness. Abbar--Arnold--Coine give a global weighted-shift characterization, but at this critical exponent their global constant is infinite and they do not state the finite-horizon sharp profile. Targeted published-finding corpus searches for finite-horizon, truncated, harmonic-number, and endpoint formulations returned the related weak-endpoint and feasible-exponent findings but no exact \(N\)-step formula. Full inspection of those closest records shows that neither implies the equality \(\mathcal C_N(W,q_0)=H_N\).

## Value
PASS. The focal paper's endpoint obstruction is a single-vector lower bound. The new result identifies the complete sharp finite-horizon behavior over all vectors, with exact coefficient and every-horizon attainment. This is a natural endpoint invariant of the extremal witness, and it distinguishes the exact harmonic law from the weaker information “strong endpoint fails” or “strong moments grow at most logarithmically”.

The closest primary sources are arXiv:2610.00271v1 and arXiv:2609.24601v1. The closest published database findings are *Weak-\(L^{q_0}\) endpoint for the extremal Cesàro self-improvement shift* and *Exact feasible region for absolute Cesàro self-improvement endpoints*.
