Under \(2^{\lambda}\le\kappa\) for every \(\lambda<\kappa\), coherent initial hereditary types form a single hereditary family that contains every cardinal-\(\kappa\) combinatorial Banach space as a \(1\)-complemented isometric coordinate subspace. This also gives a norm-one isometric universal host for all real spaces with monotone unconditional bases of size \(\kappa\), while avoiding the hereditary-closure collapse in the recent universal-hypergraph construction.
