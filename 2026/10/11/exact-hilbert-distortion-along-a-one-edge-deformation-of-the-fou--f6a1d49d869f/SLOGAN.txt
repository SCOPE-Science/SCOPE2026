The real Lipschitz-free space over four points with five unit distances and one edge of length \(0<t\leq2\) has exact squared Euclidean Banach–Mazur distance \((7-2t-t^2)/(3-t^2)\) for \(t\leq1\) and \(1+4/((t+1)(3-t))\) for \(t\geq1\). The uniquely most-Hilbertian member is the equilateral metric, at distance \(\sqrt2\).
