For the balanced two-cluster four-point ultrametric with intra-cluster distance \(t\in(0,1]\) and cross-cluster distance \(1\), its real Lipschitz-free space has exact squared Euclidean Banach--Mazur distance \(2+(1-t)^2/(1-t^2/2)\). The optimal Hilbert distortion increases continuously from \(\sqrt2\) to a limiting \(\sqrt3\) as the two clusters collapse internally.
