The exact Euclidean distortion of a real four-point ultrametric with three mutually \(t\)-close points and one point at distance \(1\) is \(d_{\mathrm{BM}}^2=(7-2t-t^2)/(3-t^2)\) for every \(0<t\le1\). This sharp formula, optimized over all real linear maps, interpolates strictly from limiting squared distortion \(7/3\) to \(2\).
