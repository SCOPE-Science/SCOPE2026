For every real dimension \(n\ge3\), the Euclidean Banach–Mazur distance of \(N_t(x)=(\|x\|_\infty^2+t\|x\|_1^2)^{1/2}\) has an exact rank-two-eigenvalue divided by sparse-support minimum formula, with sharp support transitions. Optimal parameter tuning improves the endpoint distance from order \(\sqrt n\) to the exact asymptotic \(n^{1/4}/\sqrt2\).
