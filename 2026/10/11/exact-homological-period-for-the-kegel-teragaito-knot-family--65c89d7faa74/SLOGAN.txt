For the uniquely strongly invertible Kegel–Teragaito knots \(K_n\), the pseudo-Anosov fiber monodromy has exact homological period \(\operatorname{lcm}(8n+10,8n+4)\). Equivalently, this is the smallest positive power of the monodromy that lies in the Torelli group, with a factor-of-three drop exactly when \(n\equiv1\pmod3\).
