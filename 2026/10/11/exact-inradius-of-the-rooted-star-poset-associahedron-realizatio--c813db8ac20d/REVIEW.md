# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Specializing Sack's exact halfspace realization to the rooted-star Hasse diagram gives leaf-difference coordinates \(y_i=p_i-p_0\) with
\[
\sum_i y_i=n^{2n}
\]
and one inequality
\[
\sum_{i\in S}y_i\ge n^{2(|S|+1)}
\]
for every nonempty proper leaf subset \(S\). The root coordinate is constant on this affine slice, so these coordinates preserve the inherited Euclidean metric. Singleton facets give the sharp global upper bound and force the uniform difference center. Every larger subset facet is then proved strictly farther away by an explicit normalized-distance inequality. The closed radius and original-coordinate incenter follow exactly.
