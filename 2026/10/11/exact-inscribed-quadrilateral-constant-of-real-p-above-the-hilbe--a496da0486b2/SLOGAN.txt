For every real \(\ell_p(I)\) with at least three coordinates and \(2\le p<\infty\), the inscribed-quadrilateral constant is exactly \(24(2/3)^{2/p}\), with one centered tetrahedral configuration sharp for every exponent. For each finite \(p>2\), this strictly exceeds the two-dimensional value, giving a full super-Hilbert dimension jump.
