Every consecutive coordinate block of the canonical finite-dimensional Schauder decomposition of a real weighted-Cantor Lipschitz-free space has exact norm \(\max\{1,2\min(1,B_{n,m}/a_n)\}\), where \(B_{n,m}\) is its total coordinate weight and \(a_n\) is the smallest earlier weight. The decomposition is bimonotone precisely when every remaining tail weighs at most half of the smallest preceding coordinate, a sharp threshold stronger than contractivity of individual blocks.
