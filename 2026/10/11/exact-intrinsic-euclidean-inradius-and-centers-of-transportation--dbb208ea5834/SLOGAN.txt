For every positive-margin transportation polytope, the intrinsic Frobenius inradius is the smallest normalized marginal multiplied by the universal factor \(\sqrt{mn/((m-1)(n-1))}\), and every largest-ball center is obtained by subtracting that common entry floor and solving one residual transportation problem. In particular, the \(d\times d\) Birkhoff polytope has inradius \(1/(d-1)\) and unique center \(J/d\).
