The convex hull of three equal centered disks in pairwise perpendicular planes has exactly eight equilateral triangular facets and twenty-four ruled patches, with volume \(\left(8-10\sqrt2/3\right)R^3\) and surface area \((12-4\sqrt3+2\pi)R^2\). Its inradius is \(R\sqrt{2/3}\), while its circumradius is \(R\).
