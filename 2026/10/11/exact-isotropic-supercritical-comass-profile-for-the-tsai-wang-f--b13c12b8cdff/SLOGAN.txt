On the isotropic affine family \(F_\lambda(x)=\lambda x\), the Tsai--Wang form has an exact comass profile in every rank \(r\ge2\). Beyond the sharp threshold \((r-1)\lambda^2=1\), the maximizing graph slope switches from the source-scale \(-\lambda\) witness to the reciprocal slope \(-1/((r-1)\lambda)\), yielding a closed formula for the full supercritical comass.
