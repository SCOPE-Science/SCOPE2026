# Scientific review: Exact John ellipsoid of balanced two-layer order polytopes

## Correctness — PASS
The proof gives the exact eigenvalues, center, volume, and all facet contacts in the standard \(2k\)-coordinate Euclidean metric, including the boundary case \(k=1\). Positivity holds for every integer \(k\ge1\). The matrix of weighted contact normals equals the inverse proposed shape matrix and the weighted normal sum vanishes. John’s contact theorem therefore certifies global volume optimality and uniqueness; no finite computation is used in place of the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
Mori (arXiv:2404.00263, full Sections 1–3) determines structural relationships of triangular two-faces of maximal-ranked-poset polytopes. Ahmad–Fourier–Joswig (arXiv:2309.01626, Sections 1–4) determine face injections and \(f\)-vectors. Neither scientific conclusion implies the claimed Euclidean center, three semiaxis values, all-facet contact pattern, or explicit John weights. John’s general contact characterization is prior mathematics, not a novelty claim. A synonymous exact computation in another metric/optimization terminology remains possible; indexed search alone cannot prove uniqueness of the discovery.

## Value — PASS
The statement classifies a natural geometric extremal invariant for all balanced two-rank order polytopes, not just sample dimensions. The \(k^2+2k\)-facet simultaneous contact and exact quadratic radical are substantial structural features; it is not a relabeling of order-polytope face counts. The theorem does not address arbitrary or unbalanced posets.
