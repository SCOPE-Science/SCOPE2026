For every \(k\ge1\), the standard-Euclidean order polytope of two comparable \(k\)-element antichains has an explicit unique John ellipsoid, with center \( (a\mathbf1_k,(1-a)\mathbf1_k) \), where \(a=1/(2+\sqrt{2-1/k})\); its squared semiaxes follow three spectral formulas (two for \(k=1\)). All \(k^2+2k\) facets are tangent, with exact positive John weights certifying global optimality.
