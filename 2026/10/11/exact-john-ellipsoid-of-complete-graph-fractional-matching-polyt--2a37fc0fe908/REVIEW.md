# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The defining halfspaces have slacks \(a\) and \(b\), matched exactly by the two support variances. The radical satisfies a unique positive quadratic root and yields an explicit positive-weight John certificate with zero first moment and identity second moment. This proves global maximum-volume containment and uniqueness for every integer \(n\ge3\). The standalone reconstruction script tested the critical matrix and contact identities for \(3\le n\le10\); these tests are supplementary, not an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

Blanchard, De Loera and Louveaux (arXiv:2001.09575, §3) examine edge-monotone paths of the *same complete-graph fractional matching polytope*, including its inequality description, but do not provide its John ellipsoid. Sanità (arXiv:1806.03397) determines its combinatorial one-skeleton diameter and half-integral vertex structure, rather than its inscribed Euclidean ellipsoid. Behrend (arXiv:1301.7356) addresses related fractional *perfect* matching polytopes with degree equalities, and does not imply the edge-coordinate John ellipsoid for the degree-inequality polytope. General John's contact theorem is not claimed as new; the evaluated contribution is the full explicit result for this family. Earlier equivalent calculations under optimal experimental design or network ellipsoids remain a residual risk; no independent literature-priority certification is claimed.

## Value — PASS

The fractional matching polytope of \(K_n\) is a canonical half-integral relaxation. Determining its extremal inscribed ellipsoid exactly across all dimensions is a natural metric classification. The spectrum splits into the constant, star, and cycle edge-representation spaces, and the theorem proves that all \(\binom n2+n\) distinct facets are active. The explicit formulas and positive contact weights make this classification reproducible and geometrically useful, beyond a generic convex-optimization formulation.

## Limits

The proof does not extend automatically to noncomplete graphs or the integral matching polytope with blossom inequalities. Finite computations are not substituted for the John-contact proof. Independent validation and priority certification have not been performed.

Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
