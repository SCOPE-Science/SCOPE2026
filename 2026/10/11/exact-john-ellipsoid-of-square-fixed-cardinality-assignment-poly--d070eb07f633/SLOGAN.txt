The unique John ellipsoid of every square fixed-cardinality \(k\)-assignment polytope is explicit: it is round for \(k\le n+1-\sqrt{n+1}\) and otherwise has two different squared-semiaxis eigenvalues with a closed volume formula. Every coordinate facet is tangent; row and column facets join the contact set exactly at the transition, and the anisotropic ellipsoid has strictly greater volume than the largest inscribed ball.
