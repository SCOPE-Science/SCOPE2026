For every compact stationary Chirikov standard-map state on the cylinder, the kick \(\Delta p\) has exact covariance \(\operatorname{Cov}(p,\Delta p)=-\operatorname{Var}(\Delta p)/2\), so momentum variance is at least one quarter of kick variance. If a nonzero-kick ergodic state reaches that boundary, the full map is forced onto a nontrivial two-cycle; invariant-circle and chaotic states must lie strictly above it. The boundary is attained at \(K=2\pi\) by the explicit cycle \((\pi/2,-\pi)\leftrightarrow(3\pi/2,\pi)\).
