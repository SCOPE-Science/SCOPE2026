The largest centrally symmetric subset of every regular Reuleaux odd-gon is attained by intersecting the shape with its point reflection about the dihedral center; its exact Kovner–Besicovitch area ratio is \(F(2n,r_n)/F(n,r_n)\), where \(F\) is an explicit elementary disk-intersection formula. The ratio approaches one as \(1-\pi^2/(8n^2)-25\pi^4/(1536n^4)+O(n^{-6})\).
