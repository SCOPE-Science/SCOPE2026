For the stable triangular shear \(M_{a,b,c}=\begin{pmatrix}-a&b\\0&-c\end{pmatrix}\), prefactor-one \(L^p\) contractivity is governed exactly by \(\beta_p(a,c)=p\,a^{(p-1)/p}c^{1/p}/(p-1)^{(p-1)/p}\). This threshold peaks at \(a+c\) when \(p=1+a/c\), so \(\max\{a,c\}<b<a+c\) produces a genuine two-transition, reentrant contractivity window in \(p\).
