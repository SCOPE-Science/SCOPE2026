For every complete bipartite graph \(K_{p,q}\) with \(p,q\ge2\), the intrinsic minimum-volume enclosing ellipsoid of its centered graphical zonotope has shape \((pq/4)L\), where \(L\) is the graph Laplacian. Exactly \(2^p+2^q-2\) vertices touch that ellipsoid, distinguished by monochromatic rows or columns of the edge-sign matrix.
