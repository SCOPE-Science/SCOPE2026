For every \(a\ge2\), \(b\ge1\), the symmetric edge polytope of \(K_{a,a,b}\) has an explicit unique Löwner ellipsoid with four principal-eigenvalue classes and a closed radical design parameter. Cross-part roots are all contacts, while the edges between the two equal parts become entirely screened once \(b>2a+1\): from that threshold onward the ellipsoid equals that of \(K_{2a,b}\).
