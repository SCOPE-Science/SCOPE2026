For every full-dimensional rank-selected Boolean polytope \(P_{n,S}\), the exact Löwner ellipsoid is determined by a planar polygon of first and second Hamming-weight moments: it is either the cube's circumball or is supported on exactly two allowed weight layers chosen from the extreme pair and consecutive pairs. Missing ranks can change the optimizer: for \(n=8\) and \(S=\{0,1,5\}\), only weights \(1\) and \(5\) contact, while the origin is strictly interior.
