Every compact positive stationary state of the harvested Hutchinson–Wright equation is exactly mean-locked to \(K_\delta=K(1-\delta/r)\), and its present population is uncorrelated with the population one feedback delay earlier. For every non-equilibrium state, the lag-\(\tau\) Pearson correlation is exactly zero while population variance equals \(K^2/r^2\) times mean-square per-capita growth.
