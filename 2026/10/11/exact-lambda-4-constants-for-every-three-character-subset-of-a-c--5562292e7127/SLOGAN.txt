Every three-character subset of a cyclic group has a sharp \(L^4/L^2\) constant from exactly five values: \(3^{1/4}\), \((7/3)^{1/4}\), \(2^{1/4}\), \((15/7)^{1/4}\), or \((5/3)^{1/4}\). The value is determined completely by whether the three frequencies form an order-three coset, an order-four fragment, an isolated antipodal pair, one ordinary three-term progression, or no pair-sum collision.
