For the canonical low-pass Peter--Weyl projection on \(SU(2)\), retaining highest weights \(0,1,\ldots,L\), the sharp \(L^1\) and \(L^\infty\) norms grow as \(\frac{8}{\pi^2}(L+1)\). The constant comes from an exact trigonometric decomposition of the central Dirichlet kernel and periodic averaging of \(|\cos|\).
