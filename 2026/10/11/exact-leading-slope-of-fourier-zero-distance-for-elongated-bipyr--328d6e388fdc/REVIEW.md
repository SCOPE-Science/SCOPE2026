# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The exact Fourier rescaling reduces the nearest-zero problem to minimization of \(\sqrt{u^2+\alpha^2s^2}\) over one fixed zero set \(Z_d\). Both monotonicity statements follow pointwise before taking the infimum. The limit uses a lower bound by \(\rho_d\) and an \(\varepsilon\)-minimizer for the transverse coordinate; it does not assume that \(\rho_d\) is attained. The source's positive strip and zero-set nonemptiness give \(0<\rho_d<\infty\), and its equal-volume-ball formula yields the final normalized limit.

## Originality
PASS. The lead paper states a positive normalized liminf bound, not a full limit. Its 2009 precursor proves only qualitative divergence of the same three-dimensional elongation. The closest published comparison gives an explicit Bessel lower strip, which implies only a lower bound on \(\rho_d\). Targeted semantic searches found no indexed statement of the exact variational slope or the monotonicity of \(\kappa/\alpha\). The inaccessible full package behind that comparison record remains a stated residual risk.

## Value
PASS. The coefficient \(\rho_d\) is the sharp transverse zero threshold of the fixed Fourier profile driving the source's principal higher-dimensional counterexample. The result therefore identifies the exact first-order growth law and separates the conceptual asymptotic problem from the harder numerical task of evaluating \(\rho_d\) or finding finite crossing thresholds.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is Gómez-Serrano--Levitin--Platt--Polterovich, arXiv:2609.10517v1. Benguria--Levitin--Parnovski, DOI 10.1016/j.jfa.2009.06.022, contains the qualitative three-dimensional precursor. A published comparison record gives an explicit Pólya--Bessel lower strip but not an exact asymptotic identity. The present result does not evaluate \(\rho_d\), prove its attainment, locate a minimizing zero, or certify a finite \(\alpha\) threshold.
