For the elongated Fourier bipyramids, the nearest real Fourier-zero distance has an exact linear leading term: \(\kappa(\Omega_{\alpha,d})/\alpha\to\rho_d\), where \(\rho_d\) is the least transverse coordinate of the fixed rescaled zero set. This upgrades the known positive liminf bound to an exact asymptotic coefficient without assuming that the transverse infimum is attained.
