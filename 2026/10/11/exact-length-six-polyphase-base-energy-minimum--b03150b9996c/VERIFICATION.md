The universal result is proved analytically in `RESULT.md`. The accompanying `verify.py` performs exact replay checks for the algebraic certificate:

1. For each of the four corner values \(p_i\), it verifies the tangent-half-angle identity \(2Dp_i=\|z_i\|^2\) as a bivariate polynomial identity.
2. It verifies \(z_1\cdot z_4-z_2\cdot z_3=-4DB\), the identity used in the Cauchy--Schwarz step.
3. It verifies in the quadratic integer ring generated by \(\zeta\), with \(\zeta^2-\zeta+1=0\), that the classical length-six witness has five autocorrelations of squared modulus \(1\).

The polynomial checks use exact integer evaluation on a product grid. The checked differences have degree at most four in each variable, so vanishing on five distinct values of each variable proves the bivariate identities exactly. No random or floating-point computation is part of the certificate.

Limit: this verification does not classify all minimizers, because the finding does not claim such a classification.
