In every compact stationary Lorenz–96 regime, the nonlinear advection at site \(i\) has exact conditional mean \(\mathbb E[A_i\mid x_i]=x_i-F\), despite depending quadratically on neighboring sites. Its unexplained variance is exactly coordinate-acceleration energy, yielding a site-time identity \(\operatorname{Var}(A)=\operatorname{Var}(X)+N^{-1}\sum_i\mathbb E[\dot x_i^2]\).
