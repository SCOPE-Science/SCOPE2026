# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reduces every boundary point by signed-coordinate isometry to \((1,a)\), \(0\le a\le1\). The explicit choice \(y=(1,-1)\) gives the universal lower bound \(M_\nu(2,1)\). At the candidate minimizer \(x=(1,0)\), every \(y=(u,v)\in S_{\ell_\infty^2}\) yields an unordered norm pair dominated coordinatewise by \((2,1)\); monotonicity and symmetry of the Heinz mean give the matching upper bound. Endpoints \(\nu=0,1\) are handled by the continuous arithmetic-mean extension and \(\nu=1/2\) gives the geometric mean.

## Originality
PASS. The defining 2019 paper states only \(\sqrt2\le h_\nu(\ell_\infty^2)\le3/2\) for the square plane, while its exact calculations concern other cases and the upper constant. The 2022 absolute-normalized-norm paper explicitly develops \(H_\nu\), not the lower infimum-supremum quantity. Targeted semantic-database and public-web searches using the exact notation, defining infimum-supremum expression, the square-plane alias, and the candidate formula returned no statement implying the claimed profile. The residual risk is ordinary literature incompleteness, not a known competing theorem.

## Value
PASS. The result closes an explicit exact-value gap in the paper that introduced \(h_\nu\). It simultaneously recovers the known arithmetic endpoint \(3/2\) and geometric midpoint \(\sqrt2\), showing how the full Heinz interpolation behaves on the canonical non-uniformly-nonsquare square plane. The formula is structural rather than a table lookup: it identifies the axis as the minimax location and the opposite corner as a universal extremal response.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is Dinarvand (2019), Definition 1 and Example 2, which introduce \(h_\nu\) and leave exactly the interval \(\sqrt2\le h_\nu(\ell_\infty^2)\le3/2\). Zuo--Huang (2022) studies exact upper Heinz constants under absolute normalized norms, but not this lower minimax constant. The proof here is specific to the two-dimensional square; no higher-dimensional claim is included.
