On the line-equilibrium slice of the Bao–Chen–Liu–Deng five-dimensional dynamo, \(H=z+g(1+m)x^2/(2r)\) obeys the exact law \(\dot H=-gx^2\), forcing every bounded exact trajectory to converge to the equilibrium line. At the published line-equilibrium parameters the origin is stationary, and its reported positive finite-time growth rates match transverse Jacobian instability rather than hyperchaotic state-space motion.
