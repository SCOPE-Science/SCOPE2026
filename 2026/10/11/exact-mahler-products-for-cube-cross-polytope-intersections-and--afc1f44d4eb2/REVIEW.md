The polar support is obtained by fractional knapsack, and exact integration over all signed sorting chambers gives the closed polar volume. These steps prove the all-dimensional formula without relying on finite computation. Differentiation gives the positive integer-knot jumps. The finite verifier independently exercises the volume and support formulas and exact cubic identities. Boundary parameters, multiplicities, and normalization are covered.

## Value — PASS
A cube–cross-polytope intersection is a canonical interpolation between two equality cases of the volume-product inequality. Determining the entire profile is a motivated invariant question rather than an arbitrary parameter slice. The all-dimensional closed expression enables exact evaluation, while the three-dimensional bimodality, strict cuboctahedral cusp minimum and sharp global extremizer distinguish substantially different intermediate shapes. The result is neither a new proof of the known unconditional Mahler bound nor a claimed universal optimization.
