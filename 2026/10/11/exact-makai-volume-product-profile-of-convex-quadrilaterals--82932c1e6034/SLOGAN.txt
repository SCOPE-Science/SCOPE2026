For every convex quadrilateral, the affine-invariant Makai product \(\operatorname{area}(Q)\operatorname{area}((Q-Q)^\circ)\) has an exact two-parameter formula determined solely by where its diagonals meet. Its attainable range is precisely \((3/2,2]\), with maximum only at parallelograms and the lower endpoint approached by degenerate trapezoids.
