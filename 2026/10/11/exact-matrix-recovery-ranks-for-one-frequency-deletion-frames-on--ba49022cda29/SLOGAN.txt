For one-frequency Fourier deletions on an arbitrary finite abelian group, the rank-one intensity measurements have an exact matrix-recovery rank determined by the orders of all dual-group elements, with one missing matrix direction per nontrivial even-order translation coset. They recover every zero-mean signal up to global phase precisely for odd group orders at least five; for groups of order eight, the matrix ranks are \(44\), \(36\), and \(28\) according to the abelian group structure.
