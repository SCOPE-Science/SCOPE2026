# Review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The proof first computes the diameter of the minor-axis half-ellipse exactly from a two-parameter chord identity, reducing the maximization to one concave quadratic in \(u=\sin^2d\). This gives the two branches and the \(\sqrt2\) threshold. Global optimization over center-line directions is then proved without sampling: for the rounder regime the cut chord itself gives the sharp lower bound; in the elongated regime, orientations before \(d_*\) are excluded by their cut chord and later orientations by one explicit endpoint-to-arc pair. The published center-line reduction theorem transfers the result to arbitrary bisections. The packaged checker independently replays every algebraic identity and representative numerical cases.
