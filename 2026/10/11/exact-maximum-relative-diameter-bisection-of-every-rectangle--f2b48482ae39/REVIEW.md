# Same-model review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The reduction to center-passing straight lines is a published theorem for centrally symmetric planar convex bodies. The remaining rectangle optimization is exhaustive because a line through the center meets either the horizontal pair or the vertical pair of sides. In each class an explicit boundary pair gives the required lower bound; the midpoint cut attains it. Equality conditions were checked separately, including the square boundary case.

## Originality
**PASS.** The closest exact database result treats regular even polygons and therefore covers the square only. The archive-era source explicitly notes that the precise optimal straight line was not characterized in the general bisection framework. The later full-text bisection paper supplies the reduction and general criteria but has no rectangle, square, or parallelogram specialization and no aspect-ratio formula. Residual risk remains for an unindexed elementary note or an uninspected journal-only example in the 2004 article.

## Value
**PASS.** Rectangles form a canonical complete family in this fencing problem. The theorem determines the exact optimum for every aspect ratio and the complete center-line equality pattern, rather than solving an arbitrary numerical instance. The square is only a consistency boundary case; the substantive content is the full non-square family.

## Closest literature and limitations
The foundational 2004 work provides the center-line reduction in the equal-area setting; the 2018 paper extends that reduction to arbitrary bisections. The present result does not classify all curved minimizing bisections, and it does not address general parallelograms.
