Every compact stationary state of the axisymmetric Langford torus model that stays off the symmetry axis has exact mean height \(\mathbb E[z]=\beta\) and, more strongly, \(\mathbb E[z\mid x^2+y^2]=\beta\). Its height variance is exactly one quarter of the mean-square logarithmic radial speed, so every noncircular recurrent state must visit both sides of the level \(z=\beta\).
