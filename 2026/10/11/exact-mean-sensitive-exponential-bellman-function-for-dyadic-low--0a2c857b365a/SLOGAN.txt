The exact binary-dyadic bounded-lower-oscillation exponential Bellman envelope at local mean excess \(a\in[0,1]\) is \(1+a(e^\lambda-1)/(2-e^\lambda)\) for \(0<\lambda<\log2\), while at or above \(\log2\) it is infinite whenever \(a>0\). A geometric staircase, placed on dyadic blocks of arbitrary total measure, attains the envelope for every admissible \(a\).
