Every unit-edge uniform \(n\)-gonal prism has an explicit mean-square directional width, with one closed branch for even \(n\) and one for odd \(n\). The parity split comes entirely from the fact that the planar difference body of a regular \(n\)-gon has \(n\) sides when \(n\) is even and \(2n\) sides when \(n\) is odd.
