For every strictly positive nonconstant periodic coexistence state of the delayed hunting-cooperation model, maturation delay enters an exact mean–variance law: \(\bar p=\sigma e^{-\beta\tau}[\bar n(1-\bar n/\kappa)-\operatorname{Var}(n)/\kappa]\). Hence \(\bar p<\sigma\kappa e^{-\beta\tau}/4\), and positive periodic coexistence is impossible when \(h_1e^{\beta\tau}\ge1\).
