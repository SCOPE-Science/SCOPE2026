# Scientific review: minimum enclosing ball of simplex-sum stellohedra

## Correctness — PASS

The exact family and inherited Euclidean normalization are fixed before the theorem. Every exposed vertex is obtained from the Minkowski-sum support function: a prefix of leaves dominates the hub and produces powers-of-two coordinates; every rank/order is realized and distinct. Direct expansion shows squared-radius clearance \(\frac23(2^k-2^{k-r})(2^{k-r}-1)\), nonnegative for all \(k\ge1\) and zero exactly in the two terminal ranks. The hub contact and uniform measure on all full-rank contacts have the proposed center as a strictly positive barycenter, so the variance identity proves minimax optimality and uniqueness even against nonsymmetric centers. There are no unresolved nonstandard auxiliary lemmas; finite rational checks corroborate but do not replace the all-parameter proof.

## Originality — PASS with recorded residual risk

Devadoss–Smith (arXiv:2011.08169), inspected in full, establishes combinatorial/abstract graph-associahedron structures, not a metric enclosing sphere. Pešović–Stojadinović (arXiv:2209.12053), likewise inspected, supplies the actual connected-subset simplex-sum realization and investigates enumerative invariants, not its Euclidean circumradius. Searches under stellohedron, star graph associahedron, circumsphere, circumradius, Chebyshev center, minimal enclosing ball, and contact-class aliases returned no covering statement. Existing discussions of other metrics, other graph families, or abstract minimum-sphere existence do not imply the precise exponential radius and contact-clearance identity. The remaining risk is an unindexed or alternatively normalized source or an implicit instance of a more general exact optimization theorem. Search absence alone is not proof of novelty; no independent bibliographic certification is claimed.

## Value — PASS

Graph associahedra have canonical combinatorial and metric realizations, yet ordinary facet/vertex enumerations do not determine the minimum enclosing Euclidean ball. The exact formula spans a natural infinite family, identifies just \(1+k!\) contacting vertices among \(\sum_{r=0}^k k!/(k-r)!\), and gives a quantitative strict-interiority gap at every other rank. The exact barycenter certificate makes the radius reproducible without optimizing over exponentially many vertices. This is an invariant of a mathematically motivated standard realization, not an arbitrary finite parameter slice.

## Limitations and evidence

The result is not affine-invariant under general nonsimilarity transformations. The package includes an independently implemented rational verifier for sample dimensions, whereas the universal claim is established by the written proof. Two primary original texts were read, and missing coverage under differently named embedding conventions remains a priority risk.
