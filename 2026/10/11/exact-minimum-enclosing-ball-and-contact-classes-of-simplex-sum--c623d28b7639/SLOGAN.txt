For the standard unweighted simplex-sum stellohedron of the star \(K_{1,k}\), the unique smallest enclosing Euclidean ball has radius \(\frac{2^{k+1}-1}{3}\sqrt{\frac{k}{k+1}}\). Precisely the hub vertex and the \(k!\) full-leaf-order vertices contact it; every intermediate combinatorial rank has an explicit, strictly positive squared-distance clearance.
