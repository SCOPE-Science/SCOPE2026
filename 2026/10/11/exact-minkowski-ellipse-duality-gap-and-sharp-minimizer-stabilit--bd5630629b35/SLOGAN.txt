For the dual cube–crosspolytope pair in dimension \(n\ge3\), the Minkowski-ellipse lower constant has a strict gap \(A_1(\ell_1^n)-A_1(\ell_\infty^n)=1/2-1/n\), even though both upper constants equal two. Exact pointwise formulas identify uniformly distributed crosspolytope minimizers versus coordinate-vertex cube minimizers, with sharp quantitative stability in both geometries.
