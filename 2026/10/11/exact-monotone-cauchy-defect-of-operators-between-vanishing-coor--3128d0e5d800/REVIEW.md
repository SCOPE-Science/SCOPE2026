# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
Every coordinate row is absolutely summable. The upper bound reduces each increasing sequence to finitely many convergent scalar row pairings plus a uniform tail estimate. The lower bound inductively chooses escaping output rows and finite disjoint input blocks on the maximizing sign, with exact losses bounded by the stated regulator; it constructs one monotone sequence witnessing all tail scales. Coordinate projections give the independent essential-norm identity. Infinite-dimensional identity and signed-difference operators attain the two sharp constants. The proof is analytic for arbitrary index sets, not an extrapolation from computation.

## Originality — PASS (subject to residual bibliographic risk)
Emelyanov 2023, Definition 2 and Example 2, develops qualitative quasi-Levi classes and compact positive examples, while Emelyanov 2024 studies collective \(\sigma\)-Levi families. Emelyanov 2025, Lemma 2.3 and Proposition 2.4, provides further qualitative inclusion and separation. Alpay–Emelyanov–Gorokhova 2021 addresses qualitative KB/Levi operators. Previously known quasi-KB and Schur arguments can explain the qualitative compactness corollary; originality is specifically asserted for the exact sign-sensitive monotone-Cauchy *defect* formula and its optimal \(1/2\) and \(1\) comparison to essential norm. No inspected theorem contains that formula or a stronger statement implying it. Unindexed literature remains a risk, not proof of novelty.

## Value — PASS
The natural quasi-Levi requirement asks for convergence of images of bounded increasing vectors, making its maximal norm-Cauchy failure a motivated quantitative invariant. The formula gives the complete answer on the canonical coordinate lattices of arbitrary cardinality; the two sharp constants reveal that positive and signed operators can have substantially different monotone defects at the same essential norm. The gliding-hump construction is also an explicit witness extraction principle for noncompactness. These are structural and quantitative statements, not an arbitrary finite slice or renaming of the usual essential norm.
