For the regular octagonal Banach plane, the complete Gastinel--Joly generalized rectangular profile is \(\mu_p=\max\{(1+[2(\sqrt2-1)]^p)^{1/p},(1+(2\sqrt2)^p)^{1/p}/(1+\sqrt2)\}\). The stronger denominator profile \(d_8(\lambda)=\max\{1,\sqrt2-1+\lambda/\sqrt2,\lambda-(\sqrt2-1)\}\) explains the two competing extremal scales.
