For any convex \(2r\)-gon with antipodally paired outward edge normals, the exact \(k\)-fold illumination number is \(\lceil2rk/(r-1)\rceil\), regardless of side lengths or central symmetry. Every optimal fractional illumination has a unique direction-cell mass pattern when \(r\) is even and an alternating one-parameter family when \(r\) is odd.
