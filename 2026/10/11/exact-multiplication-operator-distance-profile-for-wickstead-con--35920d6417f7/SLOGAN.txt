For a positive unital \(C(K,\mathbb R)\)-projection with nonzero Wickstead constant diagonal \(\alpha\), the exact distance to multiplication operators is \(1-\alpha\), with all best multipliers characterized pointwise. Along the scalar axis, \(\|P-cI\|=1-\alpha+|c-\alpha|\), so \(\alpha I\) is uniquely best and \(\|I-P\|=2(1-\alpha)\).
