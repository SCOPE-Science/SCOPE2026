# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS

The claim is quantified over both scalar fields and every \(0<\delta<1\). Coordinate evaluations give the summable atomic sequence, finite-support perturbations establish the exact additive norm splitting, and the second adjoint is computed on the canonical dual basis. One inclusion of the hull follows from the contractive singular projection; explicit unit near-attainers prove the reverse inclusion with a separate boundary approximation. Scaling the singular part attains the stated distance. A norm-one quotient functional yields witnesses excluding every positive generating radius. No unverified computational extrapolation is used.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

Kadets, Martín, Merí and Quero (arXiv:2306.02645v1, Example 2.4(5)) already prove the underlying inclusion is generating. Their Proposition 2.22 establishes only the implication from the second adjoint to the original operator and explicitly asks for the converse. Their Example 2.23 and the journal version's Example 2 concern a diagonal operator on \(c_0\), not this inclusion. The complete near-attainment hull, exact distance, and uniform failure of positive bidual generating radii are not supplied by the statements and proofs inspected. Relevant published search records revealed no covering result; absence from such searches cannot rule out equivalent unindexed literature.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result resolves a precise question explicitly raised by the motivating paper, using its very own canonical generating example. Its sharp formula determines the entire norm-closed convex geometry of near norm-attainment sets and the quantitative distance to them, rather than only producing a negative witness. The full positive-radius obstruction is structurally explained by the singular annihilator of \(c_0\).

## Scope and risks

The proof establishes a concrete counterexample, not a classification for general second adjoints. The critical topological distinction is norm closure, not weak-star closure. Possible independently discovered versions or dual-space reformulations outside the inspected source collection remain a residual originality risk. The scientific finding has not received independent validation.
