For the canonical generating inclusion \(J:\ell_1\to c_0\), the second adjoint has the exact norm-closed near-attainment hull \(\overline{\operatorname{conv}}(\operatorname{att}(J^{**},\delta))=\{\varphi\in B_{\ell_\infty^*}:\|\varphi_{\mathrm{sing}}\|\le\delta\}\) for \(0<\delta<1\). Thus \(J^{**}\) is not \(r\)-generating for any \(r>0\), resolving negatively the published second-adjoint converse question.
