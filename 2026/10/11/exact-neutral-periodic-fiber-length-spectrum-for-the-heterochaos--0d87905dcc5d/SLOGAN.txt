Neutral fixed-point sets in generalized heterochaos baker maps split into center-parallel intervals with an exact length histogram determined by reflected Dyck bridges. Their total Euclidean \(1\)-dimensional measure is asymptotic to \(\frac{M(M+1)}{(M-1)^2\sqrt\pi}\frac{(4M)^m}{m^{3/2}}\), while length weighting selects shallow excursions that are rare when components are counted uniformly.
