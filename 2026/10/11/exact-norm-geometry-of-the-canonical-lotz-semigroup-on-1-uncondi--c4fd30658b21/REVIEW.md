# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. Under the stated 1-unconditional multiplier normalization, every bounded diagonal multiplier has norm exactly the supremum of its scalar coefficients: the assumed inequality gives the upper bound and any single nonzero block gives the matching lower bound. This proves the operator-norm metric immediately. The positive-time maximum is then a complete one-variable optimization, the zero-time case is handled separately because the supremum is not attained, and norm holomorphy follows from uniform coefficient differentiation on right half-planes. In the finite-dimensional-block case, the displayed truncations are genuinely finite rank and their tail norm is exactly \((N+1)^{-t}\).

The focal 2026 paper was inspected at the theorem and proof level: it supplies the unconditional-decomposition Lotz construction and a multiplier estimate with an unspecified constant, but it does not state the all-pairs exact operator-norm metric, positive-time derivative norms, or exact finite-rank tail law. The remaining risk is an older equivalent diagonal-multiplier calculation under different terminology.

## Value
PASS. The focal Lotz argument hinges on the distinction between strong and uniform continuity. The result identifies that boundary completely for the natural 1-unconditional normalization: the norm jump at zero is exactly one, every positive-time pairwise distance is explicit, the same path is norm-analytic away from zero, and finite-dimensional decompositions have an exact compact approximation rate. This is a structural profile of the source's witness rather than a numerical recomputation of one example.
