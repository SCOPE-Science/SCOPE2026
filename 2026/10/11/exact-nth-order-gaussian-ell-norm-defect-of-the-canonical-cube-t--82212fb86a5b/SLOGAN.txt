For the canonical all-vertex truncation of \(B_\infty^n\), the Gaussian \(\ell\)-norm defect is exactly \(c_n\varepsilon^n+o(\varepsilon^n)\), with \(c_n=2^{\,n-1/2}n^{(n-1)/2}\Gamma((n+1)/2)/(n!\pi^{n/2})\). Thus the sharpness family used in the recent stability theorem has genuinely \(n\)-th order, not merely bounded-above, contact with the cube.
