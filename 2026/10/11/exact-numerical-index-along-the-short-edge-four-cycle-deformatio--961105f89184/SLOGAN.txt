For the real Lipschitz-free space over a four-cycle with consecutive edge lengths \(1,1,1,t\), the exact numerical index is \(1/(2-t)\) for every \(0<t\le1\), attained by a rank-one operator. The full short-edge deformation connects the equilateral triangle's limiting index \(1/2\) to the unit square's index \(1\).
