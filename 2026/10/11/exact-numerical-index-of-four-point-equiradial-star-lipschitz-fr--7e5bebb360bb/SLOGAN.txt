For a four-point metric consisting of three equilateral leaves at distance \(t\in[1/2,1]\) from a common center, the real Lipschitz-free space has the exact numerical index \(n=(1+3(2t-1))/(1+6(2t-1)+(2t-1)^2)\). This strictly interpolates between the tree value \(1\) and the equilateral value \(1/2\).
