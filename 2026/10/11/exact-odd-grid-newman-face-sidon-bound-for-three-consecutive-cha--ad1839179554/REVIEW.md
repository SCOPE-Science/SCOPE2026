PASS. The proof reduces the normalized sign-face problem to a concave quadratic on the finite cosine grid. The optimizer is certified by the two samples adjacent to zero and a strictly convex weighted lower bound; the stationarity equations reduce to a quartic with a unique root. The finite replay corroborates, but does not replace, the all-odd-modulus argument.

## Value
PASS. The face contains the translated classical Newman extremizer and is therefore structurally motivated. Its exact all-odd-modulus solution supplies an explicit, sharp lower bound for the unrestricted odd finite-cyclic Sidon problem and identifies the magnitude retuning responsible for the leading finite-grid gain.

## Closest literature and limitations
The result is deliberately narrower than the unrestricted odd-order Sidon problem. It neither rules out complex phases outside the Newman face nor claims equality with the full odd-order constant.
