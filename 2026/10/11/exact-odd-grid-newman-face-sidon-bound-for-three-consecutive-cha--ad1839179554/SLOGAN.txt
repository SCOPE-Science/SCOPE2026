For every odd \(N\ge5\), the canonical Newman sign face \(a+bz-cz^2\) has an exact finite-grid Sidon ratio determined by one strictly monotone quartic root. It gives the rigorous lower bound \(\sqrt2+3\sqrt2\,\pi^2/(16N^2)+O(N^{-4})\) for the unrestricted three-consecutive-character Sidon constant.
