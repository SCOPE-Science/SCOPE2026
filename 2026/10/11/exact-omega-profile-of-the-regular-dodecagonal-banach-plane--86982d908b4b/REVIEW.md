# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The regular dodecagon satisfies the hypotheses of the published symmetric-Minkowski-plane lemma, so every unit isosceles partner is a signed quarter-turn. The sign choices give the same objective. Dihedral symmetry reduces the base vector to one edge and reflection reduces the parameter to \(0\le a\le1/2\). Exact comparison of all six facet functionals gives the formulas for \(A(a)\), \(B(a)\), and \(C(a)\), with the sole switch at \(q=(5-2\sqrt3)/13\). The two symbolic derivative identities are positive on their intervals, so the unique reduced maximum is the edge midpoint. Exact substitution yields \(\Omega=14/5-\sqrt3\).

## Value
PASS. The finding resolves the full reduced profile rather than merely reporting one scalar value: it identifies the unique facet transition, monotonicity, and complete maximizing orbit. This gives a natural exact model for an isosceles-orthogonality invariant on a canonical symmetric polyhedral Banach plane.
