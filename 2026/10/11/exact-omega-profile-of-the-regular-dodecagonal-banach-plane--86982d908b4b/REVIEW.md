# Mathematical review

## Correctness
The regular dodecagon has coordinate sign and interchange symmetries, so the published symmetric-Minkowski-plane theorem reduces unit isosceles-orthogonal pairs to quarter turns up to sign. Dihedral and reflection symmetries reduce the extremum to one half-edge. On the complete interval, comparison of all twelve signed supporting functionals yields affine functions A and C and a two-branch function B, with the unique switch at (5-2√3)/13. Differentiation gives a nonnegative derivative on the first branch and a positive derivative on the second. Exact evaluation at the edge midpoint yields Ω=14/5−√3; the strict increase gives the complete maximizer orbit.

## Originality and literature
Liu, Yang, Fu and Li, *New geometric constants of isosceles orthogonal type*, arXiv:2111.08392, introduced this invariant and supplied the symmetric-plane reduction, but did not compute its regular-dodecagonal profile. Balestro, Martini and Teixeira, *Optimal Constants in Normed Planes* (2019), established the rotation description of unit isosceles partners and evaluated distinct geometric constants of regular polygonal norms; their paper predates this invariant. Published computations for the regular octagon concern a different unit ball. Other published dodecagonal evaluations concern the James, angle, rectangular and equilateral-triangle constants, whose definitions do not imply the present three-chord quotient. The arithmetic and historical priority of a specialized calculation remain subject to possible differently named earlier results.

## Mathematical value and scope
The regular dodecagon is a canonical polyhedral Banach plane. The calculation determines the entire reduced functional profile, the sole active-facet transition and the sharp equality orbit, not just an approximate scalar. The computation is specific to this plane and does not supply a general law for regular polygons.
