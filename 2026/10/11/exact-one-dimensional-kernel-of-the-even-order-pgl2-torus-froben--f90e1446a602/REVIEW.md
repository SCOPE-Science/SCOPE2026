# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The source gives a basis consisting of the unit, the web element \(w\), and the loop-basis elements \((k,l)_Y\). For even \(n\), Theorem 5.6 sends \(w\) to zero, the unit to the unit, and \((k,l)_Y\) to \((nk,nl)_Y\). Since multiplication by \(n\) is injective on the nonzero lattice and the target has the same basis type, every non-\(w\) coefficient in a kernel vector must vanish.

## Originality
PASS. The inspected source states the parity dichotomy “noninjective for even order, injective for odd order” but does not identify the even-order kernel. Full-text inspection found no “kernel” statement, and targeted literature-database searches using exact-kernel, one-dimensional-kernel, torus, PGL2, Frobenius, and skein terminology found no covering statement. The closest retrieved records concern unrelated torus-exterior group kernels and slope seminorms and do not imply this skein-theoretic claim.

## Value
PASS. The source emphasizes that this is the first known noninjective surface Frobenius example in its setting. Determining that the failure is exactly one-dimensional classifies all information lost by the map on the torus, rather than merely exhibiting one killed element. This is a natural structural refinement of the source's injectivity dichotomy.

## Limitations and closest literature
The conclusion is confined to the torus and the source's even-order root-of-unity regime. It does not assert a corresponding kernel description for other surfaces. The closest primary source is arXiv:2610.01881v1 itself: Theorem 5.6 supplies the full basis formula, while Corollaries 1.4 and 5.7 stop at noninjectivity.
