For \(t(z)=\sum_{j=1}^{s}p_jz_j\) with fixed positive weights summing to one, the optimal degree-\(N\) approximation of \(1\) by \((1-t)P(t)\) in radial Dirichlet polydisk norm has an exact inverse-multinomial-sum error and a sharp vanishing threshold \(\beta\le(s+1)/2\). In the bidisk this yields a strict gap \(3/2<\beta\le2\): the symmetric single-generator approximation fails while unrestricted bivariate polynomial approximation succeeds.
