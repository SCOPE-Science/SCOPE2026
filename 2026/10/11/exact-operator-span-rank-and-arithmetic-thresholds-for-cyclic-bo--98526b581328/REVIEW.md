# Same-model scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Goldberger–Kang–Okoudjou, Theorem 1, identifies projector-span dimension with nonzero cyclic ambiguity coefficients. Here the overlap geometry gives exactly zero, one, or two ordinary intervals at each lag. For one interval, geometric sums count roots by a gcd; for two intervals the root-of-unity factorization is \((1-z^k)(1+z^a)\), and inclusion–exclusion gives the exact intersection \(\beta_d(a)\). The closed arithmetic count is then proved for every dimension. The least-prime-factor threshold follows from counting nonunits in a contiguous interval; the power-of-two optimum follows by counting zeros separately for each character order. A standalone exact cyclotomic-divisibility program independently checks all lengths and lags through dimension \(24\), without substituting finite tests for an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS with stated residual risk.** The rank/ambiguity equivalence of Goldberger–Kang–Okoudjou [1] is foundational rather than novel here. Bendory–Eldar [2, Corollary 3.2] already give the sufficient coprimality criterion for recoverability of rectangular windows, which in the odd case *includes all lengths newly classified as informationally complete*; that sufficiency is expressly not presented as a novel recovery result. Bendory–Eldar–Boumal [3] also treat rectangular-window admissibility and nonlinear reconstruction. The checked sources do not state the exact rank at every window length, including failure strata, or the exact power-of-two maximum and all maximizing lengths. Specialized two-sparse multiwindow results concern a different family and do not imply these full boxcar rank profiles. Searches for the explicit rank, alias terminology, prime-divisor criterion and capacity formula found no more comprehensive covering result. Unindexed equivalent computations remain possible.

**Value — PASS.** Rectangular sliding windows are a canonical structured probe. The exact deficiency quantifies how many matrix observables a lifted STFT intensity map cannot distinguish, including on composite cyclic groups and at windows too short for full linear tomography. This is stronger information than a yes/no sufficient frame condition. The classification reveals a sharp parity divide, dependence on the least prime divisor, nonmonotonic rank as window length grows, and a precise logarithmic power-of-two rank defect together with all optimal lengths. These quantify a natural design tradeoff without claiming a downstream theorem or noise guarantee.
