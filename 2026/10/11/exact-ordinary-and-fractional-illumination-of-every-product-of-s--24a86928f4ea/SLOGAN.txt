For every Cartesian product of simplices, ordinary and fractional illumination coincide exactly with the number of vertices:
\[
i(P)=i^*(P)=\prod_j(d_j+1).
\]
Thus every non-cubical product of simplices has illumination number at most \(3\cdot2^{n-2}\), with equality exactly for a triangle times an \((n-2)\)-cube.
