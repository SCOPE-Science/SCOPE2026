All five Platonic solids have exact, scale-free variances for their areas of uniformly oriented shadows, with the cube the most variable and the icosahedron the least; the tetrahedral--octahedral equality follows from a known projection-body equivalence. For independent uniform views, the sharp counts needed to estimate surface area to \(1\%\) relative RMS precision are \(104,57,57,16,6\), respectively.
