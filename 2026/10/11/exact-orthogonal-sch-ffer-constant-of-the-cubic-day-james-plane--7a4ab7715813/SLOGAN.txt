For the real cubic Day--James plane \(\ell_3-\ell_1\), the orthogonal Schäffer constant is exactly
\[
g_\perp=\left(1+\frac{3}{2^{4/3}}\right)^{1/3}=1.298726719564592\ldots .
\]
This closes the same-space value previously reported only as \(g_\perp\approx1.2987\); the extremal pair is the diagonal cubic unit vector and the balanced opposite-sign taxicab unit vector.
