# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Signed norming facets for the two symmetric chords reduce the weighted quantity to dual norms. Exhaustive exact evaluation of the ten signed facets proves \(A+rB\le3-r\), and sign reversal gives \(rA+B\le3-r\). Together with \(A,B\le2\), these bounds produce a six-vertex polygon containing every feasible chord pair. The quadratic form equivalent to the P-angle bound is convex and is at most \(4\) at every vertex. The adjacent-vertex pair has chord norms \(2\) and \(r\), giving equality.

## Originality
PASS. The defining 2022 preprint was inspected at the definition, universal bounds, \(\ell_p\) example, Hilbert characterization, and later comparison and geometric-property results; it has no regular-polygon or decagonal computation. Searches covered the named invariant, regular-decagon aliases, the candidate radical, adjacent-vertex wording, exact-value tables, and stronger-looking von Neumann--Jordan formulations. The closest same-invariant indexed result concerns real \(\ell_p\) sequence spaces, while the closest same-object result is a Banach--Mazur distance. Exact P-angle values for other regular polygons and decagonal unit-distance or triangle invariants do not imply the new weighted chord envelope.

## Value
PASS. The central output is a sharp two-inequality chord envelope for every unit pair in the affine-regular decagon. The exact P-angle constant is a consequence of that structural statement, not a routine table lookup. It supplies a natural golden-ratio benchmark for a canonical non-Hilbert polygonal plane.

## Closest literature and limitations
Yang and Li, arXiv:2208.11239, introduce \(S_P\) and develop its general theory. Exact values are known for several other classical norms, but the inspected literature does not cover the affine-regular decagon. The result is restricted to this decagonal plane and does not classify every maximizing ordered pair. The defining preprint has a standing dimension restriction in its preliminaries; the present theorem uses its displayed P-angle supremum directly in dimension two.
