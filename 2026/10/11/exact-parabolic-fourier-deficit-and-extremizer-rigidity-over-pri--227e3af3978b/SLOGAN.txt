For every odd prime \(p\), parabolic Fourier extension over \(\mathbb Z/p^2\mathbb Z\) has sharp fourth-power norm \(p\), with an exact nonnegative deficit splitting residue-class mixing from within-class Fourier dispersion. Its nonzero maximizers are exactly the \(p^2\) orthogonal residue-character packets, and the same identity gives a quantitative distance-to-packet bound.
