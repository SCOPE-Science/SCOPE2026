# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The claim concerns the unordered two-particle restricted configuration space, not an ordered cover. A two-stage global strong deformation retract maps the actual polygon-and-triangle model onto a graph containing one subdivided complete core on long arms, pendant arms, and isolated short-short pairs. The proof verifies all gluing maps, parameter inequalities, equality cases, and natural inclusions; persistent ranks then follow by elementary graph homology and component mergers. Integral torsion and higher homology vanish by the graph retraction. Exact arithmetic checks on 7,065 polygonal cases and 10,000 graph-filtration cases support but cannot replace the proof.

## Originality — PASS, with stated risk
Li–Özaydın (arXiv:2603.00914v1, §§3–4 and Appendix A) treat the **ordered** space and its persistent graph with vertices \((x,y)\), and derive ordered homology, not the unordered quotient barcodes. The ordered filtered graph is swap-symmetric and its quotient is already the incidence graph used in the proof, so we make **no originality claim about this graph by itself**; the precise new assertion is the closed-form unordered barcode with natural inclusion maps and equality conventions. The 2011 Deeley paper and 2013 Dover–Özaydın paper concern ordered configurations and general topology of restricted spaces; the 2023 Li paper also treats ordered configurations. Indexed published findings for finite crowns, generalized theta configurations, and spider curvature do not imply the distance-constrained unordered star statement. The earlier ordered graph construction is the closest structural predecessor and may enable an unstated equivariant quotient derivation, so overlap risk remains. No absent-source result or absence of an unindexed equivalent is asserted.

## Value — PASS
Unlabeled particles are the appropriate configuration object for interchangeable agents. The result resolves the exact lifetime and number of every connected component and fundamental cycle for all real arm lengths, showing a separation of control parameters: short-short component births depend on pair sums, while cycle births depend only on individual arm lengths. The full barcode and equality conventions supply a usable classification beyond a pointwise Betti calculation or one special-length example.

## Scientific limitations
Only a fixed-length, one-parameter minimum-separation filtration of two indistinguishable points on a star is covered. Computer enumeration is finite and field-specific; the all-real, integral conclusion follows from the proof. Equivariant reformulation of the closest ordered model is a residual originality risk. External independent audit has not occurred.
