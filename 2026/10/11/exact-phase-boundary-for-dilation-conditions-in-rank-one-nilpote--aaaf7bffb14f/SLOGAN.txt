For rank-one nilpotent \(\Gamma_n\)-contractions, the two fundamental-operator conditions used in conditional dilation theory have an exact common boundary: all relevant two-by-two minors \(\lambda_i\overline{\lambda_{n-j}}-\lambda_j\overline{\lambda_{n-i}}\) vanish. Equivalently, the coefficient vector is invariant under reversal-conjugation up to one unimodular phase.
