The common-delay linearization in the published fractional Lotka--Volterra example collapses to a quadratic in \(z=s^q e^{s\tau}\), giving the exact first crossing \(\tau_*=1.147739992150082\ldots\) at \(k=-1/2\), not the printed \(1.0229\). The corrected value falls exactly between the paper's stable run at \(\tau=0.93\) and oscillatory run at \(\tau=1.16\).
