The exact second moment of a random planar orthogonal shadow of any \(d\)-dimensional orthotope is an explicit quartic polynomial in its side lengths, derived from three universal Haar correlations of absolute Plücker minors. For the unit cube, \(\operatorname{Var}(A_d)=(d-2)(4/\pi-5/4)\); the new content is the full finite-dimensional orthotope tensor and a dimension-five covariance sign transition, not the previously known three-cube special case or asymptotic coefficient.
