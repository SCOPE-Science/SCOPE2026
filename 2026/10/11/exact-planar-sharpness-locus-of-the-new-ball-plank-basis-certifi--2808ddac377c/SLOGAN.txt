In dimension two, the orthonormal-basis minimization introduced in the new proof of Ball's plank theorem reaches its universal \(\sqrt2\) radius exactly for positive-semidefinite correlation matrices. On the symmetric line \(A=\begin{pmatrix}1&q\\q&1\end{pmatrix}\), the exact squared radius is \(2/\max\{1,q^2\}\), while every nonsymmetric matrix gives a strict improvement.
