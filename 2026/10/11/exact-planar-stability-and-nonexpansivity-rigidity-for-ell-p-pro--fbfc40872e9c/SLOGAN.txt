For the real \(\ell_p\) nearest-point map onto \(\{(t,u):t\ge\kappa\|u\|_2\}\), the exact planar Lipschitz constant is \((1+\kappa^p)^{1/p}(1+\kappa^{q(q-1)})^{1/q}/(1+\kappa^q)\), where \(q=p/(p-1)\). The map is nonexpansive in dimension at least three exactly at \(p=2\), while in the plane the additional non-Hilbert exception is \(\kappa=1\).
