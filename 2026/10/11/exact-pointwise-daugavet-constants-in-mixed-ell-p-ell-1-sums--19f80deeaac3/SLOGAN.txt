In a real \(\ell_p\)-sum of a Daugavet space and an atomic \(\ell_1\) space, the pointwise Daugavet constant is determined exactly by the Daugavet-component norm, total atomic mass, and largest atomic mass through one scalar minimization. At \(p=2\), pure atomic unit vectors exhibit a sharp change in the optimal slice at largest-atom mass \(1/2\), including a strictly mixed optimum for five equal atoms.
