In every real scalar–Daugavet \(\ell_p\)-sum with \(1<p<\infty\), the entire two-constant profile at a unit point \((t,bx)\) is \(\operatorname{dc}=((1-|t|)^p+b^p)^{1/p}\) and \(\delta c=2b\), where \(b=(1-|t|^p)^{1/p}\) and \(x\) is a Daugavet point. Hence mixed unit points exhibit a strict, sharp quantitative separation between Daugavet and Delta behavior; if the second summand has the Daugavet property, the only Delta points are on its unit-sphere axis and no Daugavet points survive.
