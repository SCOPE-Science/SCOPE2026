For \(x\in S_{\ell_\infty^n}\) and every finite \(t\ge1\), the entire pointwise James-type profile is controlled by one statistic: the second-largest coordinate modulus \(s_2(x)\). Precisely, \(J(x,\ell_\infty^n,t)=\left((2^t+(1+s_2(x))^t)/2\right)^{1/t}\), so the \(p=\infty\) endpoint never becomes pointwise constant at a finite power parameter.
