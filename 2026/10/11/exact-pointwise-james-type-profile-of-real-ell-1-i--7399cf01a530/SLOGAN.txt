For real \(\ell_1(I)\), the entire pointwise James type profile is controlled by one coordinate statistic: \(J(x,\ell_1(I),t)=2M_t(1,1-m(x))\), where \(m(x)=\inf_i|x_i|\). In particular, every point of infinite-dimensional \(\ell_1(I)\) has the maximal value \(2\) for every \(-\infty\le t<\infty\), while finite-dimensional \(\ell_1^n\) has a sharp profile ranging from \(2M_t(1,1-1/n)\) to \(2\).
