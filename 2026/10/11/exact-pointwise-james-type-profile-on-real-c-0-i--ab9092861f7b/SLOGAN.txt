For every real \(c_0(I)\) and every point \(x\) of its unit sphere, the full Rincón--Villamizar pointwise James profile depends only on the second-largest coordinate modulus: \(J(x,c_0(I),t)=M_t(2,1+s_2(x))\) for all \(-\infty\le t<\infty\). Thus the complete pointwise range is \([M_t(2,1),2]\), from signed coordinate vectors to points with at least two coordinates of modulus \(1\).
