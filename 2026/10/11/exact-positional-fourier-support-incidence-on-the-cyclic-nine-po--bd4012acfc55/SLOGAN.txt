On the cyclic group of order nine, exactly \(92{,}236\) ordered pairs of nonempty time and Fourier support sets are simultaneously realizable, with the complete positional incidence matrix determined by exact cyclotomic rank computations. The realizable pairs form \(302\) affine orbits, or \(158\) orbits after Fourier duality, and the \(486\) realizations at boundary sizes \((4,5)\) constitute one affine orbit.
