For the capacitor-only memristive Colpitts model, \(R=e^{u/(2n)}\) is exactly a stable linear filter of \(\cosh(x/(2n))\). Every compact recurrent state therefore lies in \(u\ge0\), and every compact stationary measure satisfies the exact observable identity \(\langle e^{u/(2n)}\rangle=\langle\cosh(x/(2n))\rangle\).
