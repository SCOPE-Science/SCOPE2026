# Review of Exact positivity interval for the canonical half-plane Carathéodory component

## Correctness

PASS. The primary source gives the exact rational expression for the component of
\[
f_\lambda(z)=z/(1-\lambda z).
\]
After the rotation
\[
w=\lambda z
\]
and substitution
\[
\beta=1-\alpha,
\]
the boundary real part for
\[
0<\beta<1
\]
reduces to a rational function of
\[
x=\operatorname{Re}w
\]
whose derivative is strictly negative. Its smallest finite boundary value is
\[
\beta/2
\]
at
\[
w=1.
\]
A partial-fraction identity controls the boundary pole at
\[
w=-1,
\]
and the harmonic minimum principle transfers the boundary lower bound to the disk. The endpoint reductions are exact Cayley-type identities. Outside
\[
[0,1],
\]
either a denominator zero lies in the disk or a negative real part occurs near
\[
w=i.
\]

## Originality

PASS. The full primary article was inspected at the conjecture definitions, the canonical and rotated examples, the explicit rational formula, and the numerical discussion. It reports very small sampled positive values but does not derive the exact infimum, the maximal real parameter interval, or the endpoint failure of the positive-minimum formulation.

Related extension-operator literature was checked for stronger coverage. It gives endpoint inclusion and extension results but not this exact component profile. Targeted literature searches for the source formula, the half-plane map, the exact floor, the endpoint, and the maximal parameter interval did not locate an implication-equivalent theorem.

## Value

PASS. The half-plane map is the source's canonical test case, so this is not an arbitrary specialization. The exact calculation replaces numerical sampling by a closed theorem, determines the maximal real parameter interval, and exposes a genuine logical distinction between pointwise positivity and a positive uniform floor at the endpoint.

That distinction matters directly to the source's claimed equivalence of its formulations while leaving the main pointwise conjecture intact. The result therefore gives both rigorous positive evidence for the central conjecture and a precise boundary correction to one auxiliary formulation.
