For the canonical half-plane maps \(f_\lambda(z)=z/(1-\lambda z)\), the new \(\alpha\)-Carathéodory component has positive real part exactly for \(0\le\alpha\le1\), with sharp floor \((1-\alpha)/2\) for \(0<\alpha<1\). At \(\alpha=1\) it remains pointwise positive but its infimum is \(0\), so the source's positive-minimum formulation is not equivalent to its pointwise conjecture.
