For the critical dyadic \(c_0\)-sum backward shifts, the exact power norm is \(\|T_p^n\|^p=n+1\) at every positive power except \(n=1\) and \(n=5\), where it is \(17/6\) and \(37/6\), respectively. All norm-attaining unit vectors and the sharp global growth multiplier \((17/12)^{1/p}\) are classified explicitly.
