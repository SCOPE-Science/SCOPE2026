For the complete graph and its self-looped analogue, coupled Arnol'd cat maps have exact finite-torus permutation orders determined solely by the site count modulo four and the modulus \(2^s\). The loopless period is \(3\cdot2^{s-1}\) in two residue classes and \(3\cdot2^{\max(0,s-2)}\) in the other two; self-loops yield \(2^s\) for even site count.
