For every compact recurrent statistical state of the classical QPP flow supported in \(z>0\), the pressure distribution fixes kinetic activity exactly through \(\int x^2\,d\mu=\bar z+h\int z^{-1}\,d\mu-(h+1)\). The defect vanishes exactly on equilibrium-supported measures, so every non-equilibrium ergodic state has a strictly positive arithmetic–reciprocal pressure excess.
