# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Adding both slow-variable increments forces the two fast coordinates to sum to 2 sigma. Writing them sigma±u (u≠0) and eliminating y yields a single scalar polynomial P(u²)=4 alpha sigma/(2+eta). Reverse substitution reconstructs a genuine two-cycle from each strictly positive root and shows that u and -u are the same orbit. For sigma≤0 none exist; monotonicity for 0<sigma≤1 and the unique positive minimum at sigma²−1 for sigma>1 yield the exact 0/1/2 census, with q=0 excluded and double-positive-root counted once. At the example, an independent implementation directly iterated both reconstructed pairs and computed two-step multipliers approximately (0.74498042,1.00314407) and (-0.24842272,0.99679836), plus stable fixed-point eigenvalues (-0.79944427,0.99944427). These confirm the analytically stated rational Schur inequalities; the proof of all-parameter root counts is algebraic, not numerical. The hyperbolic strict inequalities persist locally; no basin classification or longer-period assertion follows.

PASS

The original Rulkov model treats chaotic bursts and the frozen one-dimensional fast map; Wang–Cao and Sun–Ma–Cao address fixed-point bifurcations/numerical periodic windows. Zhou–Cao (2026) proves all periods for a newly introduced auxiliary ONE-dimensional subsystem, explicitly not the full original two-dimensional map. Those statements do not imply the exact global two-cycle count, saddle branch or coexistence wedge of the full map. The 2014 and 2025 same-model full papers remain uninspected and materially relevant; best-of-knowledge originality passes with this risk.
