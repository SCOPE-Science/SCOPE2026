For every unitary operator, the squared \(L^2\)-Cartesian radius equals one minus an explicit sum of projective spectral-gap deficits \((\ell-\sin\ell)/(2\pi)\). Among unitaries on an \(n\)-dimensional complex Hilbert space, its exact maximum is \(\tfrac12+\tfrac{n}{2\pi}\sin(\pi/n)\), attained exactly by equally spaced antipodal eigenvalue classes.
