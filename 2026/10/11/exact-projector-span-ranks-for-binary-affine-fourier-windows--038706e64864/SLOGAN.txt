For every binary extension field \(\mathbb F_{2^n}\) with \(n\ge2\), a one-frequency complex perturbation of a flat Fourier window has an exact parameter-stratified affine projector-span rank. The choice \(z=2+i\) enables complete complex matrix recovery in dimension \(2^n-1\), while \(z=2\) spans only \(2^{n-1}(2^n-1)\) matrix dimensions.
