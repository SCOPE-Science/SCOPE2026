# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The dual support-function optimization over x1>=x2>=0 gives N*(M,m)=(M^p+omega^(1-p)m^p)^(1/p) for m<=omega M and (M+m)/(1+omega)^(1/q) otherwise (p=q/(q-1)). The ratio to Euclidean norm has unique first-branch minimum at rho=omega^(1/(2-q)), and maximum at the axis or diagonal. Euclidean Ptolemy yields (b/a)^2, attained by explicitly constructed triples in both weight regimes. Parameter endpoints and the phase boundary are treated as stated.

## Originality
PASS on the basis of the inspected primary statements. ScienceAsia 44 (2018), Theorems 7-8 and Remark 2, computes the primal constant for 1<=q<2 and the dual at q=1 and q>=2; it explicitly leaves the dual 1<q<2 case unknown. Zuo 2012 midpoint-extremal comparison theorems and Zuo 2018 MIA q>=2 example do not yield the interior-minimizer dual formula. No independently examined source establishes the complete missing-range dual formula; unindexed coverage remains possible.

## Scientific value
PASS. The exact formula completes the dual side of an explicit natural open parameter range from the primary literature, determines the weight phase boundary and produces sharp attaining triples; it yields the full primal-dual equality for this two-dimensional Lorentz family. It does not assert a general duality theorem for arbitrary Banach spaces.

## Boundaries and provenance
The new proof addresses exactly the real two-dimensional 1<q<2 range. General cases, dimensions above two, and arbitrary Banach-space duality are not claimed. Prior mathematical evidence and literature comparisons are retained in AUDIT.json; no formal or external certification is implied.
