For \(1<q<2\), the dual two-dimensional Lorentz space \(d^{(2)}(\omega,q)^*\) has the same exact Ptolemy constant as its primal:
\[
C_{\mathrm{Pt}}=(1+\omega^{2/(2-q)})^{(2-q)/q}\max\left\{1,\frac{2}{(1+\omega)^{2/q}}\right\}.
\]
Together with the known endpoint and \(q\ge2\) cases, this proves \(C_{\mathrm{Pt}}(d^{(2)}(\omega,q))=C_{\mathrm{Pt}}(d^{(2)}(\omega,q)^*)\) for every \(0<\omega<1\) and \(1\le q<\infty\).
