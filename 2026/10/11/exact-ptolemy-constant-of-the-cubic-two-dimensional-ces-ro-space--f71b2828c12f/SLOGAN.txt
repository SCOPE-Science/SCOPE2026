For the cubic two-dimensional Cesàro space \(\mathrm{ces}_3^{(2)}\), the Ptolemy constant is exactly \(\bigl(8+(1+\tau)^3\bigr)^{2/3}/(3^{4/3}+\tau^2)\), where \(\tau>0\) uniquely solves \(3^{4/3}(1+\tau)^2=\tau((1+\tau)^2+8)\). The extremum is attained by the explicit triple \((-1,0),(0,-\tau),(-1,-\tau)\).
