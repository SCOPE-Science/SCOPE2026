# Exact pure-braid image on \(H_3(\exp_3 D_n;\mathbb Z)\)
## Finding
For every integer \(n\ge 2\), let \(D_n\) be the disc with \(n\) punctures and
\[
\rho_n:P_n\longrightarrow \operatorname{Aut}H_3(\exp_3D_n;\mathbb Z)
\]
be the induced pure-braid action. Then
\[
\operatorname{im}\rho_n\cong\mathbb Z^{\binom{n}{2}-1}.
\]
For the standard pure generators \(A_{ij}\), set \(N_{ij}=\rho_n(A_{ij})-I\). Then \(N_{ij}N_{kl}=0\) for all pairs, and the only integral linear relation among the \(N_{ij}\) is
\[
\sum_{i<j}N_{ij}=0.
\]
Equivalently, the induced map from \(P_n^{\mathrm{ab}}\cong\mathbb Z^{\binom{n}{2}}\) has kernel generated by the all-ones vector. The case \(n=2\) gives the trivial image, while \(n=3\) recovers the published rank-two calculation.

## Assumptions and scope
The finite subset space \(\exp_3X\) consists of nonempty subsets of \(X\) of cardinality at most three. The punctured disc \(D_n\) deformation retracts to the bouquet \(\Gamma_n\) of \(n\) circles, and the source computes the action using a cellular model of \(H_3(\exp_3\Gamma_n;\mathbb Z)\).

In degree three the source's basis can be grouped by support size:
\[
U=\langle u_a\rangle,\qquad W=\langle w_{ab}\rangle,\qquad V=\langle v_{abc}\rangle,
\]
with ranks \(n\), \(\binom n2\), and \(\binom n3\), respectively. The theorem concerns only this degree-three representation.

## Proof
Let \(\tau_i\) be the standard Artin generator. Equation (4.1) of the source says that its cellular action is the signed permutation of bouquet coordinates, except for one correction: when the entries at \(i,i+1\) are even and the second is positive, a term \(-2\) times the corresponding cell with two additional odd coordinates appears.

Specialized to degree three, this means that \(\tau_i\) permutes \(U\), acts on \(V\) by signed permutation, and acts on \(W\) by permuting pair labels together with a correction into \(V\). For
\[
A_{ij}=\tau_{j-1}\cdots\tau_{i+1}\tau_i^2\tau_{i+1}^{-1}\cdots\tau_{j-1}^{-1}
\]
define \(N_{ij}=\rho_n(A_{ij})-I\). Conjugating the local formula gives three facts: \(N_{ij}\) vanishes on \(U\oplus V\); it vanishes on \(w_f\) unless the edge \(\{i,j\}\) and the pair \(f\) share exactly one endpoint; and in the remaining case it sends \(w_f\) to \(\pm2v_t\), where \(t\) is their three-label union.

Hence every \(N_{ij}\) maps \(W\) into \(V\) and kills \(V\), so
\[
N_{ij}N_{kl}=0
\]
for all pairs. Products of pure-generator images therefore add their nilpotent parts.

Now fix \(w_{ab}\). For every third label \(c\), exactly \(N_{ac}\) and \(N_{bc}\) contribute to the \(v_{abc}\)-coordinate, with opposite signs. Thus
\[
\sum_{i<j}N_{ij}=0.
\]

Suppose \(\sum c_{ij}N_{ij}=0\). Fix \(a<b<c\), restrict to the three pair vectors on those labels, and project to \(\mathbb Zv_{abc}\). Only \(N_{ab},N_{ac},N_{bc}\) remain. Their action is the published three-strand calculation: they span rank two and their only integral relation is their sum. Therefore
\[
c_{ab}=c_{ac}=c_{bc}.
\]
The triangles of the complete graph connect all edges when \(n\ge3\), so every \(c_{ij}\) is equal. Thus the global relation module is exactly the diagonal copy of \(\mathbb Z\). For \(n=2\), the unique correction operator is zero. Consequently
\[
\operatorname{im}\rho_n\cong
\mathbb Z^{\binom n2}/\mathbb Z(1,\ldots,1)
\cong\mathbb Z^{\binom n2-1}.
\]

## Verification
The accompanying `verify.py` reconstructs the degree-three basis and the cellular action, verifies the Artin braid relations, constructs all standard \(A_{ij}\), and checks \(N_{ij}N_{kl}=0\), \(\sum N_{ij}=0\), and the predicted operator rank for every \(2\le n\le8\). It returns ranks \(0,2,5,9,14,20,27\), respectively.

The finite replay is not the proof for arbitrary \(n\); the arbitrary-\(n\) step is the triangle-restriction argument above.

## Relationship to prior work
Tuffley's paper supplies the cellular basis, the braid-generator formula, and a general nilpotency bound for the pure-braid image. It then treats \(n=3\), \(k=3\) as the smallest nontrivial example and proves a free-abelian image of rank two. The inspected paper does not state the all-\(n\) rank formula or determine the complete relation lattice of the standard pure generators for \(n>3\).

## Limitations
No exact-image claim is made for higher finite-subset degree. The literature comparison used the exact primary source and targeted searches, but an obscure unindexed later computation could conceivably contain the same all-parameter rank under different terminology. The finite checker covers \(n\le8\) only and is supplementary to the symbolic proof.
