For the degree-one Fourier band on a \(q\)-ary Hamming cube, restriction to any nontrivial fixed-weight shell has exactly one forced zero singular value and three explicit positive spectral sectors. All positive singular values coincide precisely when \(n\equiv1\pmod q\) and \(k=((q-1)n+1)/q\), yielding infinitely many exact tight sampling frames after quotienting by the shell equation.
