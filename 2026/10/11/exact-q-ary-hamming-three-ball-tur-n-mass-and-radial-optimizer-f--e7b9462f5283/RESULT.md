# Exact q-ary Hamming three-ball Turán mass and radial optimizer faces

## Finding
For every prime power \(q\ge3\) and every integer \(d\ge3\), let \(G=\mathbb F_q^d\) and \(B_3=\{x\in G:\operatorname{wt}(x)\le3\}\), with counting measure. Define
\[
p=\frac{q-1}{q},\quad r=\lfloor(d-1)p\rfloor,\quad \theta=(d-1)p-r,\quad
\Delta=(d-1)p(1-p)-\theta(1-\theta).
\]
Then \(\Delta>0\), and the maximal mass of a positive-definite \(f:G\to\mathbb C\) with \(f(0)=1\) and support contained in \(B_3\) is
\[
\boxed{T_q^{(3)}(d)=\frac{r(r+1)}{(1-p)\Delta}.}
\]
A radial extremizer has unnormalized Fourier transform
\[
\widehat f(\xi)=\frac{(d-t)(t-r)(t-r-1)}{d(1-p)\Delta},\qquad t=\operatorname{wt}(\xi).
\]
Among Hamming-radial extremizers this is unique unless \(d\equiv2\pmod q\). In that exceptional congruence class, every radial extremizer lies on the line segment joining the binomial-expectation-normalized spectra
\[
P_r(t)=(d-t)(t-r)(t-r-1),\qquad
P_{r+1}(t)=(d-t)(t-r-1)(t-r-2).
\]
No classification of nonradial maximizers is asserted. Examples: \(T_3^{(3)}(5)=27\), \(T_3^{(3)}(6)=81/2\), \(T_3^{(3)}(9)=405/7\), and \(T_4^{(3)}(7)=640/7\).

## Assumptions and scope
Fourier transforms are unnormalized: \(\widehat f(\xi)=\sum_{x\in G}f(x)\overline{\chi_\xi(x)}\) for a fixed nontrivial additive character and the induced pairing. All quantities use counting measure on \(G\); a radial function depends only on Hamming weight. Positive definiteness means \(\widehat f(\xi)\ge0\) for every \(\xi\). The assertions hold for arbitrary prime powers \(q\ge3\), not merely primes. The classification pertains only to radial extremizers.

## Proof
The Hamming isometry group generated by coordinate permutations and independent nonzero scalar multiplications acts transitively on each Hamming-weight layer, preserves \(B_3\), and acts by group automorphisms. Averaging any admissible \(f\) over this group preserves positive definiteness, \(f(0)\), and \(\sum f\). Thus an unrestricted maximizer can be taken radial.

For radial \(f\) with support in \(B_3\), \(\widehat f(\xi)=P(t)\), where \(t=\operatorname{wt}(\xi)\), and \(P\) is a real polynomial of degree at most three. Indeed, the transform of the Hamming-weight-\(j\) sphere is the q-ary Krawtchouk polynomial
\[
K_j(t)=\sum_{s=0}^j(-1)^s(q-1)^{j-s}\binom{t}{s}\binom{d-t}{j-s}\qquad(0\le j\le3).
\]
Conversely the first four Krawtchouk polynomials form a basis of real polynomials of degree at most three, so nonnegative \(P(0),\ldots,P(d)\) determine an admissible radial function supported on \(B_3\). With \(X\sim\operatorname{Bin}(d,p)\), Fourier inversion gives \(f(0)=\mathbb E P(X)\) and \(\sum_x f(x)=P(0)\). The extremal problem is therefore to maximize \(P(0)\) among cubics nonnegative on \(\{0,\ldots,d\}\) with \(\mathbb E P(X)=1\).

Write \(\mu=(d-1)p=r+\theta\). Since \(p=(q-1)/q\), \(0\le\theta\le p\), and \(1\le r\le d-2\). The integer-grid cubic \(P_r(t)=(d-t)(t-r)(t-r-1)\) is everywhere nonnegative for \(0\le t\le d\). The binomial size-bias identity \(\mathbb E[(d-X)h(X)]=d(1-p)\mathbb E[h(Y)]\) for \(Y\sim\operatorname{Bin}(d-1,p)\) yields
\[
\mathbb E P_r(X)=d(1-p)\bigl((d-1)p(1-p)-\theta(1-\theta)\bigr)=d(1-p)\Delta>0.
\]
Positivity also follows directly from the strictly positive binomial probability at \(X=0\), where \(P_r(0)>0\).

For sharpness, interpolate any cubic \(P\) at \(0,r,r+1,d\). Taking its binomial expectation produces the exact quadrature formula
\[
\mathbb E P(X)=w_0P(0)+w_rP(r)+w_{r+1}P(r+1)+w_dP(d),
\]
where the Lagrange weights satisfy
\[
w_0=\frac{(1-p)\Delta}{r(r+1)},\qquad
w_r=\frac{dp(d-1)(1-p)[r-(d-2)p]}{r(d-r)},
\]
\[
w_{r+1}=\frac{dp(d-1)(1-p)[(d-2)p-(r-1)]}{(r+1)(d-r-1)},\qquad
w_d=\frac{p[(d-1)p(1-p)+\theta(\theta+1)]}{(d-r)(d-r-1)}.
\]
All four are nonnegative: \(r-(d-2)p=p-\theta\ge0\), \((d-2)p-(r-1)=1+\theta-p>0\), \(w_d>0\), and \(w_0>0\). Consequently \(1=\mathbb E P(X)\ge w_0P(0)\), giving the claimed sharp upper bound. The normalized \(P_r\) attains equality because it vanishes at \(r,r+1,d\).

For equality among radial polynomials, when \(\theta<p\) all three nonzero-contact weights are strictly positive, so \(P(r)=P(r+1)=P(d)=0\). Degree at most three forces \(P\) to be a positive multiple of \(P_r\). When \(\theta=p\), equivalently \(d\equiv2\pmod q\), exactly \(w_r\) vanishes. Equality requires \(P(r+1)=P(d)=0\). Hence \(P(t)=(d-t)(t-r-1)(at+b)\). Nonnegativity at all integer points, in particular at \(r\) and \(r+2\) (which is strictly below \(d\) in this congruence class), implies that its last linear factor is positively oriented with root in \([r,r+2]\). Thus \(P\) lies in the cone spanned by \(P_r\) and \(P_{r+1}\); imposing \(\mathbb E P=1\) produces exactly the asserted segment.

Run `python checker.py` using standard Python. The checker performs 297 exact-rational parameter checks over \(q\in\{2,3,4,5,7,8,9,11,13\}\) and \(3\le d\le35\), including all four quadrature weights, moment reproduction through degree three, the exceptional congruence, attainment by both endpoint cubics, and direct Krawtchouk inverse vanishing beyond weight three in smaller instances. The proof above, not the finite checks, establishes the unrestricted statement.

## Relationship to prior work
The 2003 study of Kolountzakis and Révész and the 2019 framework of Berdysheva and Révész establish the group-theoretic Turán context and general bounds, not the displayed nonbinary radius-three Hamming formula or its equality face. The argument uses classical Krawtchouk reduction and exact positive quadrature; such tools by themselves do not constitute a prior statement of this parameter-explicit extremal value. Formula equivalence to an older Hamming-scheme linear-programming evaluation remains a bibliographic uncertainty. The theorem concerns radius three and nonbinary alphabets; smaller radii and Boolean alphabets do not logically determine it.

## Limitations
No complete classification of nonradial extremizers is supplied. The calculation is for radius exactly three, finite fields of prime-power cardinality, and the unnormalized counting-mass objective. The algebraic optimality proof is exact, but the claim of novelty remains conditional on the inspected literature and does not exclude unindexed equivalent forms.
