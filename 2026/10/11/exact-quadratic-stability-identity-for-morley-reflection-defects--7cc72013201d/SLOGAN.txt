Tran's Morley reflection defect has an exact quadratic stability form: after centering the weighted difference of the two relevant \(K\)-rows, its regular-simplex energy is exactly \(\frac{L^2}{2}(q-1)^2\varepsilon_{ij}\). Therefore small reflection defect forces both the scale parameters and the weighted row data to approach the transposition-symmetry locus at the sharp square-root scale.
