The quantitative illumination parameter of every pair-constraint polar type-\(D\) root polytope \(Q_d=\{x:|x_i|+|x_j|\le2\text{ for }i<j\}\), for \(d\ge3\), equals its number of vertices, \(2^d+2d\). In particular the rhombic dodecahedron has parameter \(14\), exceeding the previously conjectured universal three-dimensional upper bound \(12\).
