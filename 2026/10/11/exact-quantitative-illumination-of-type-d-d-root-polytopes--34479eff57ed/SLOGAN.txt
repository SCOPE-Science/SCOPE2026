For every dimension \(d\ge3\), the type-\(D_d\) root polytope has quantitative illumination parameter exactly equal to its \(2d(d-1)\) vertices, because any one point source illuminating \(k\) vertices must cost more than \(k\) in the polytope's own gauge. This proves the previously only upper-bounded value \(12\) for the cuboctahedron and gives the exact value \(24\) for the regular \(24\)-cell.
