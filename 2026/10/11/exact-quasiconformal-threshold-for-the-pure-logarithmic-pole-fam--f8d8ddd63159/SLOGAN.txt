For the pure logarithmic pole map, the optimal quasiconformal-extension coefficient is exactly \(k_{\min}=s/(2-s)\), where \(s=(1-p^2)|A|\). Hence membership in \(\Sigma_H^k(p)\) is equivalent to \(|A|\le2k/((1+k)(1-p^2))\), and the optimal extension simply drops the logarithmic term outside the unit disk.
