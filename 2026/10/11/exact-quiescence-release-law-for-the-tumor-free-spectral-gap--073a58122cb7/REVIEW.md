# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The source characteristic polynomial at the tumor-free equilibrium factorizes into three scalar factors and three quadratic tumor-sector factors. Rewriting the latter as \(\lambda^2+(g+\beta_1+m)\lambda+gm\) is exact. Positivity of the two identities comparing the discriminant with the derivative numerators proves monotonicity in both \(g\) and \(m\), while direct differentiation gives strict concavity in \(g\). The minimum over the three tumor blocks and the three scalar modes therefore gives the stated full spectral gap.

## Originality
PASS. The motivating source establishes the tumor-free stability threshold but does not extract a convergence-rate formula from the retained \(\beta_2+\gamma_1\) dependence of its characteristic factors. The closest inspected chemoradiotherapy predecessor uses impulsive recruitment, while the earlier CAR-T/TMZ model lacks the same proliferative–quiescent block. Searches for spectral-gap, decay-rate, recruitment, and quiescence aliases did not identify an implication-equivalent statement. An equivalent observation in older literature under a different normalization remains a residual risk.

## Value
PASS. The result separates two mathematically and biologically distinct effects that the threshold alone cannot distinguish: whether tumor-free decay occurs and how fast its slowest local mode decays. The exact concavity and crossover formula quantify diminishing returns from quiescence release and identify when a non-tumor mode becomes rate-limiting.
