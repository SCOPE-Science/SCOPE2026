For the non-injective family used to prove the harmonic inner-radius upper bound, the published Schwarzian estimate is exact:
\[
\|S_{f_\alpha}\|_{\mathbb D}=2\alpha^2+2\alpha-\frac52.
\]
On every circle the unique maximizer lies on the positive real radius, and every global extremizing sequence converges to the cusp point \(1\).
