For the regular nested permutohedron, every centered vertex radius splits into a common \(M\)-dependent baseline plus \(N^2\) times the endpoint-zero quadratic roughness of the inner permutation. This gives an exact tight-frame covariance and squared-radius variance \(N^4(d-2)(d+1)^2(d+2)(5d+11)/180\): the twelve vertices are cospherical in dimension \(2\), but no higher-dimensional member is.
