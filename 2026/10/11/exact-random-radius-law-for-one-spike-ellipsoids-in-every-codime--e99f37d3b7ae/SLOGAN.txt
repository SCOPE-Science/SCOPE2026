For a one-spike ellipsoid with semiaxes \(a>b\), the radius of every Haar-random codimension-\(n\) section is exactly a monotone transform of \(\operatorname{Beta}(n/2,(N-n)/2)\). This gives the full finite-dimensional CDF, all positive moments, and an exact random-versus-optimal success curve whose high-anisotropy proportional-dimensional limit is the sharp factor \(\sqrt{N/n}\).
