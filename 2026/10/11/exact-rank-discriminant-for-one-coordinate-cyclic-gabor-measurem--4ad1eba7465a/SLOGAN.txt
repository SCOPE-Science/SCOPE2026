The projector span of the one-coordinate cyclic Gabor window \(g_{n,z}=(z,1,\ldots,1)/\sqrt{n-1+|z|^2}\) has an exact rank-defect formula determined by two real loci and a divisor-counted family of phase lines. In particular, the complex displacement \(z=1+it\) with real nonzero \(t\) is informationally complete precisely for prime \(n\), whereas the fixed parameter \(z=2+2i\) works in every dimension \(n\ge3\).
