For quadratic polynomials on real and complex Euclidean balls, the exact unweighted \(\ell_2\) coefficient constants are \(\sqrt{2n}\) in every even dimension; real odd dimensions instead have \(\sqrt{2n-1/n}\), while the complex field reaches \(\sqrt{2n}\) in all dimensions at least four. The sole extra complex exception is dimension three, with sharp constant \(\sqrt{17/3}\), proved by a symmetric-unitary cofactor obstruction.
