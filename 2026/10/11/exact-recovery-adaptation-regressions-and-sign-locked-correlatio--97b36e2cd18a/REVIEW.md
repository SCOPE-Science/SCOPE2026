# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Arbitrary antiderivative tests in \(x\), \(y\), and \(z\) give the three exact stationary regressions. Their residuals are exactly the three coordinate speeds, yielding orthogonal variance decompositions. The recovery and adaptation regressions give
\[
\operatorname{Cov}(x^2,y)=-\frac{\operatorname{Var}(y)}d,
\qquad
\operatorname{Cov}(x,z)=\frac{\operatorname{Var}(z)}s.
\]
Nonnegativity of \(x^2\) gives the support constraint \(y\le c\). Vanishing of any variance defect forces the corresponding coordinate constant on bounded complete support trajectories, after which the remaining equations force equilibrium support.

Risk: compact support is used for complete bounded equality-rigidity trajectories and unrestricted polynomial test integrability.
