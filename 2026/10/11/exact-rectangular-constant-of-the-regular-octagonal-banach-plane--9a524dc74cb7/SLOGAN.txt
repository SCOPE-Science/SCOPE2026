For the regular octagonal Banach plane \(N(s,t)=\max\{|s|,|t|,(|s|+|t|)/\sqrt2\}\), the Joly rectangular constant is exactly \(2\sqrt2-1\). The maximum occurs on a tangent direction at a vertex, and an exact support-line reduction proves that no Birkhoff--James orthogonal pair gives a larger value.
