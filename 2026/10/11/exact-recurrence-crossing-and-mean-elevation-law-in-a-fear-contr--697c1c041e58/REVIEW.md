# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. On compact support inside the strict positive octant, \(\log u_3\) is a valid smooth observable and its derivative along the flow is \(-c_3+w_3u_2/(d_3+u_2)\). Invariance therefore gives the exact mean Holling-II balance. The rational identity used to convert this balance into the displayed mean-elevation formula is checked symbolically. Equality forces \(u_2\) to be constant almost surely; for an ergodic positive invariant measure, the remaining two equations then force every trajectory in the support to be an equilibrium, so a non-equilibrium ergodic measure has strict positive defect. Strict monotonicity of \(u_2/(d_3+u_2)\) then forces support on both sides of \(u_{2*}\).

Risk: the invariant-measure argument requires compact support bounded away from \(u_3=0\); no boundary-support extension is claimed.

## Originality
PASS. The introducing article was inspected at equation and statement level. It gives the same top-predator equation, the positive-equilibrium coordinate, local stability and bifurcation analysis, and numerical limit-cycle/chaotic regimes, but the inspected text does not state the invariant-measure balance, the exact concavity-defect law, or the support-crossing consequence. Searches using the source DOI/title, the Holling-II balance, period-average, mean-elevation, and recurrence-crossing aliases returned no equivalent published statement. A later generalist-predator paper was inspected in full text and uses a different top-predator growth law. The 2018 fear-chain predecessor was only available at metadata/abstract level during this review and is retained as a residual literature risk rather than treated as disproved coverage.

Risk: the core logarithmic balance is short and could have appeared under different terminology in unindexed ecological-dynamics literature; the accepted contribution is the combined exact finite-amplitude defect and recurrence-crossing theorem for this source model.

## Value
PASS. The source uses fear to move the model among stable, periodic, period-doubled, and chaotic regimes. The result gives an all-amplitude diagnostic for those recurrent states: the intermediate predator must oscillate across its coexistence-equilibrium level while its arithmetic mean is biased strictly upward, with the bias given exactly by a weighted variance. At the source's standard parameters the universal crossing level is \(140/13\). This links the source's equilibrium geometry to its nonlinear recurrent regimes without relying on a local Hopf approximation or a finite simulation.

Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
