# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For natural signed-permutation representation χ(g)=Σ_{σ(i)=i} ε_i, Eχ²=1 for B_n (all n≥1) and even-sign D_n (n≥3), using proper marginal uniformity, proves irreducibility by real character orthogonality. Central projection kernel nχ(g^-1)/|G| has identical absolute row/column sums, so sharp l1 and linfty norm=n E|χ|. Fixed point count P(K=k)=derangement_proportion(n-k)/k! and signed-walk absolute moments s_{2m}=2m*C(2m,m)/4^m, s_{2m+1}=(2m+1)*C(2m,m)/4^m give generating function z e^-z(I0(z)+I1(z))/(1-z). Integral for e^-z(I0+I1) gives exact alternating coefficients a_j=C(2j,j)/[2^j(j+1)!], hence nA_n. D conditioning changes the character distribution only for identity permutation; the alternating binomial moment is zero odd n and (-1)^(n/2-1)C(n-2,n/2-1)/2^(n-2) even n, divided by n!, giving the stated signed norm defect. The ratio a_(j+1)/a_j=(2j+1)/[(j+1)(j+2)]<1 yields the strict alternating error and common limit c=e^-1(I0(1)+I1(1)). Independently enumerated all signed permutations and even-sign cases at ranks n=1..6, confirming n3 B=D=9/4, n4 B=31/12 D=5/2, n5 B=D=655/192, n6 B=1289/320 D=129/32 and Eχ²=1. This bounded verification is not the proof for arbitrary rank.

## originality

PASS

Character central projection kernels and wreath-product representation classification were known and are not presented as new. The inspected 69-page Scarabotti--Tolli arXiv paper, especially Corollary 2.15 and Section 5, gives the general projection and wreath machinery, but no explicit l1 absolute natural signed-trace evaluation or even-sign parity correction in those relevant results. Foata--Han's 1997 basic Bessel generating functions concern different signed-permutation statistics rather than this ordinary modified-Bessel fixed-point absolute norm in the material available. No implication-equivalent B/D sharp endpoint formula was located. The 2014 Cambridge wreath-products monograph remained inaccessible in full, a material priority risk. Resultary was unavailable; no negative novelty inference from that failure.

## value

PASS

The results form a natural complete sharp endpoint-norm classification for canonical reflection isotypic projections for both classical hyperoctahedral and even-sign Weyl families, including an exact odd-rank coincidence, alternating even-rank defect and common Bessel limit. These are motivated group-harmonic norms, not an arbitrary small-rank enumeration or a mere substitution into abstract kernel bounds. The class is explicitly limited to natural representation endpoints, not all irreducibles or intermediate p.
