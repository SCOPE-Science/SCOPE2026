# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** On each finite group, character orthogonality establishes irreducibility of the natural representation in the stated rank range and identifies the sharp endpoint norm with its dimension times the average absolute trace. The signed-fixed-point distribution reduces this average to a derangement convolution. A directly evaluated Bessel integral yields the exact alternating coefficients; conditioning the sign product reduces the type-D difference to the identity permutation, and an alternating binomial sum supplies its exact parity-dependent value. The coefficient-ratio proof controls the infinite tail, and the finite checker independently replays the relevant small-rank identities. No numerical evidence is used to infer a theorem at unrestricted rank.

Foata–Han's signed-permutation Bessel treatment uses q-basic generating functions for other statistics and does not state the projector-norm claim in the inspected relevant material. Published-result searches covered character-\(L^1\) norms, signed fixed-point terminology, Coxeter type B/D, and modified Bessel and wreath-product formulations; no decisive dominating statement was located. The 2014 monograph on wreath-product harmonic analysis could not be compared in full and remains a specific access risk, as does an unidentified equivalent result in older character-norm literature. Search absence alone is not a novelty proof.

**Value — PASS.** These are the canonical full reflection-isotypic spectral projections of two nonabelian Weyl-group families. The rank-parity rigidity across an index-two subgroup is structural, and its exact signed correction is not a formal renaming of a type-A norm. The explicit Bessel constant and sharp alternating error give a useful certified asymptotic profile, with genuinely different all-rank finite consequences for the two families.
