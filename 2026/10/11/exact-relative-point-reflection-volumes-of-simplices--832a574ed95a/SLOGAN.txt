For every \(n\)-simplex, Lángi's four relative point-reflection volumes are explicit: the Busemann, Holmes--Thompson and mass values have closed elementary formulas, while mass* is exactly \(4^nD_n/n!\), where \(D_n\) is the zero-one maximal determinant. Thus the simplex benchmark for the normed Rogers--Shephard problem already encodes a classical determinant problem.
