Near a concentric outer disk, the Barker–Larman tangent-chord differential is the two-shift operator \(f\mapsto \frac{R}{\sqrt{R^2-1}}(f(\cdot+\beta)+f(\cdot-\beta))\). Its kernel appears exactly at even-denominator rational angles \(\beta/\pi\), and the fundamental resonant mode becomes visible again at quadratic order.
