# Scientific review

**Claim assessed.** The exact continuous \(\Phi_2\) profile for central five-cube hyperplane sections with normal \((1,1,1,1,s)\), its strict lower infimum \(96\), sharp upper endpoint and local extrema; the prior-known endpoint at \(s=0\) is not represented as novel in isolation.

## Correctness — PASS

A four-dimensional section's two-faces are precisely positive-area intersections with the forty coordinate three-faces. The count has six pairs of free weights \((1,1,s)\) and four pairs of free weights \((1,1,1)\), with explicitly enumerated section levels. Uniform-sum densities and the coarea formula give the exact slice areas. The derivative sign proof, including elimination of the spurious squared root and the \(s=2,4\) boundaries, establishes the entire range analytically. Independently programmed rational polygon reconstruction matches the decomposed coefficients on eighteen parameters. Finite tests are not substitutes for the continuum proof. No undefined limiting shape is treated as a finite equality case.

## Originality — PASS, with identified uncertainty

Section 5 of Zwierzyński (2026, arXiv:2609.27798v1) explicitly states the ridge conjecture and gives special classes, a weaker bound, and an octahedral-section skeletal polynomial. That polynomial **already implies the new family's \(s=0\) endpoint**, and is cited as such; it does not supply the profile for \(0<s<\infty\), transition regimes or nonmonotone extrema. Karasev (2025, arXiv:2505.16247v3) proves results on volumes and ordinary surface areas, not ridge areas. Related indexed results on a three-dimensional parallelotope section-width functional and on other four-dimensional central sections use different invariants and do not imply the stated formula. Burton (1980, DOI:10.1112/S0025579300009980) discusses skeletal measures under integral-geometric averaging, but its complete text was inaccessible. Prior identical parameterizations with a hidden equivalent formula cannot be categorically excluded, so this remains a literature risk rather than an assertion that search proves novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The 2026 ridge conjecture first becomes genuinely open beyond the classical three-dimensional edge bound in four dimensions. The fixed family is natural: it joins a product of the central octahedron with an interval to progressively cube-like four-dimensional sections while retaining fourfold coordinate symmetry. The exact ridge-area law proves the target bound sharply on an infinite continuous family not directly covered by the two existing special-class results. Its change-of-combinatorics locations and nonmonotone local extrema give structural information unavailable from a single benchmark or an expectation calculation. It does not establish the full conjecture.

**Primary literature and risks.** Zwierzyński (2026), Section 5; Karasev (2025); Burton (1980) abstract only. Residual uncertainty is concentrated in older integral geometry and unindexed exact cubical-section calculations, not the numerical proof.
