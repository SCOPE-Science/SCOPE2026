For four-dimensional sections of the five-cube with normal vector \((1,1,1,1,s)\), the complete Euclidean ridge-area profile is explicit and satisfies the sharp, strictly unattained bound \(\Phi_2>96\) for every finite \(s\geq0\). Its exact range is \((96,48\sqrt2+32\sqrt3]\), and the profile has a genuine interior dip and rebound before decreasing to \(96\).
