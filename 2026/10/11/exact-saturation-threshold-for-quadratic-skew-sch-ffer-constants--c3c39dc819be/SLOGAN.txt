For the real Banaś–Frączek plane, the quadratic skew Schäffer constant meets its natural Euclidean lower envelope **exactly** at and above a sharp parameter \(\tau_c\), smaller than the previously known sufficient threshold for every \(\lambda>1\). The formula for \(\tau_c\) switches branches at \(\lambda=\sqrt{(1+\sqrt5)/2}\), distinguishing flat-side contact from an interior circular-arc optimizer.
