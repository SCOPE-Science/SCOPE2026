# Review: exact scalar annulus for super-asymptotic expansivity

## Correctness — PASS

Definition 3.3 of arXiv:2610.01196v1 has a *uniform* time \(N\), independent of the unit vector, even though the small/large branch may depend on that vector. At \(\varepsilon=1/2\), \(M=2\), a continuous norm image of the connected unit sphere cannot occupy both branches. This yields a power with operator norm strictly below one or minimum modulus strictly above one. The converse follows from submultiplicativity/supermultiplicativity and the exact division of each exponent into a fixed-power multiple and remainder. Spectral-radius and superadditive-limit formulas justify both strict scalar inequalities, including cases with zero lower modulus. There are no extrapolations from finite experiments or unproved lemmas.

## Originality — PASS, subject to independent literature confirmation

The focal source [1] gives a different scalar rule in Proposition 5.3, whereas the exact result here is a two-radius forbidden annulus for all bounded operators. The shift \(2S\) shows that avoidance of the unit circle by the *full* spectrum is not necessary. The published uniform-positive-expansivity characterization of [2] accounts for only the uniform expansion branch and does not contain the two-sided alternative or parameter classification. An argument restricted to hyperbolic operators also does not imply the arbitrary-operator theorem or the nonsurjective shift boundary. Targeted searches for the definition, aliases, minimum-modulus radii, and scalar formulations found no stronger published coverage. Remaining risk: analogous connectedness reductions may occur under older terminology.

## Value — PASS

This is a complete natural parameter classification for the newly defined dynamical property, not a reformulation of a single counterexample. It replaces a unit-circle spectral test with exact intrinsic operator-growth thresholds, describes all scalar perturbations at both endpoints, and clarifies the decisive difference between hyperbolic mixed directions and uniformly expansive nonsurjective dynamics. No claim of optimality for other expansivity definitions is made.

## Limits and closest literature

Bag–Mandal–Das [1], Definition 3.3, Theorems 4.5 and 4.13, Proposition 5.3, and Example 4.1, are the closest same-definition source. Bernardes Jr. et al. [2], Section 2, Proposition 5, covers classical positive/uniform positive expansivity but not the new exact scalar annulus. The conclusion concerns the precise uniform quantifiers of [1] and is not an assertion about asymptotic or strong asymptotic expansivity.
