For every bounded operator \(T\) on a nonzero complex Banach space, the scalars \(c\) for which \(cT\) is super-asymptotically expansive are exactly \(\{c:|c|r(T)<1\}\cup\{c:|c|\rho_-(T)>1\}\), where \(\rho_-(T)\) is the minimum-modulus growth radius. When both radii are positive the forbidden set is a sharp closed annulus, and the criterion also detects nonhyperbolic expanding shifts.
