The first-coordinate restricted Fourier characters on the finite-field paraboloid have an explicit seven-level Gram spectrum, with a cubic controlling its three isolated levels. This natural family is independent exactly for odd field sizes \(q\ge7\); its squared condition number tends to \(2\) for \(q\equiv1\pmod4\), but grows asymptotically like \(q\) for \(q\equiv3\pmod4\).
