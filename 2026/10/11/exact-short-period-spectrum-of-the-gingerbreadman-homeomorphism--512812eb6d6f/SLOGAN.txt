The gingerbreadman map \(F(x,y)=(1-y+|x|,x)\) has least periods \(\{1,5,6,11\}\) through period \(11\), with exactly two distinct five-cycles and two distinct eleven-cycles. The eleven-cycles are both hyperbolic, with exact return traces \(-5\) and \(+5\); a rational sign-itinerary census includes every branch boundary.
