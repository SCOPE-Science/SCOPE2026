# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reduces the bidisk supremum exactly in one variable, evaluates the remaining function at two phase-adapted antipodal points, and obtains the sharp coefficient bound from a triangle inequality plus an exact square decomposition. The Hadamard-sign polynomial supplies equality. The continuity step covers vanishing coefficients.

## Originality
PASS. Direct searches covered Sidon terminology, the equivalent \(2\times2\) bilinear formulation, exact finite-frequency variants, stronger/general coefficient inequalities, and prior-result aliases. Neuwirth's three-frequency result, Cavalcante--Pellegrino's Littlewood \(4/3\) optimization, and homogeneous-polynomial asymptotics do not imply the claimed coefficient-\(\ell^1\) constant. The closest indexed rectangle result is a finite-group phase-only zero classification, a different invariant.

Residual risk: an older exact \(2\times2\) bilinear-form or unconditional-basis calculation may exist under terminology not exposed by the searches. That risk was not strong enough to identify a covering statement.

## Value
PASS. This is an exact four-character Sidon calculation in a natural Cartesian support. The product geometry gives a structural two-point certificate, while the analogous one-variable four-frequency problem is still nontrivial. The result supplies a reusable sharp model rather than an arbitrary finite computation.
