# Review: exact singular-value coefficient of generalized Cesàro operators

**Correctness — PASS.** The central analytic steps reconstruct the compact factorization \(C_t=D(I-tS)^{-1}\), the finite-band block decomposition, a trace-class within-block error, the vanishing normalized cost of rank-controlled boundary crossings, the Toeplitz-to-circulant trace-norm comparison, and the weak-Schatten perturbation extension. Monotone counting inversion and the elliptic-integral identity complete the theorem. Finite singular-value computations were supplementary checks, not proof of an asymptotic law.

**Originality — PASS (qualified).** The qualifying 2023 Albanese–Bonet–Ricker study gives the same operator definition and extends spectral and compactness analysis to nonnormable spaces, not the singular-number asymptotic. Curbera–Ricker had studied spectral and norm estimates in a journal article already public by 2021, and Rhaly proved Hilbert–Schmidt membership in 1984; neither result determines the first-order singular-number coefficient. Widom's 1989 theorem determines finite Toeplitz-section singular distributions, which is insufficient alone for the diagonal-weighted infinite operator. The 2025 Rhaly paper studies a different row-constant family and \(q>1\) membership, not the claimed coefficient. Search comparisons included the published records for Mellin Cesàro trace defects and Toeplitz Schur multipliers, neither of which implies this result. The possibility of a broader unindexed weighted Toeplitz law remains.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The first-order coefficient is a natural sharp invariant of a motivated operator family. It determines exactly the logarithmic singular-trace growth, distinguishes the weak trace endpoint from trace class, and encodes the parameter dependence through an elliptic integral. The statement neither renames a general norm estimate nor rests on a sampled finite matrix.

**Limits.** Only fixed \(0\le t<1\) is addressed; no \(t=1\) extension or second-order singular asymptotic is asserted. Independent audit has not yet occurred.
