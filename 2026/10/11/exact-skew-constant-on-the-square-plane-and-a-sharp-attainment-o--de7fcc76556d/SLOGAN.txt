For the real square plane \(\ell_\infty^2\), the skew constant is exactly \(B_{\alpha-\beta}=\min\{1,2\beta/\alpha\}\). Hence upper-bound attainment starts at \(\beta/\alpha=1/2\), while the two-boundary-segment condition stated as necessary in Zhou--Ni--Liu (2024) cannot hold until \(\beta/\alpha\ge1\).
