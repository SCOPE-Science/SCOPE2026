# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** Six exact polygonal edge cases suffice because explicit linear isometries act transitively on the edges and identify reflected pairs. Two cases give the bound \(1+\tau\); in the remaining two, three directly checked norm facets and nonnegative barycentric weights \((1,\tau^2,\tau)/(1+\tau+\tau^2)\) or \((\tau,\tau^2,1)/(1+\tau+\tau^2)\) cancel the free edge coordinates. The explicit pair attains the same value for all \(\tau\ge0\). Endpoint \(\tau=0\) and vertices are included. The proof requires no computational extrapolation.

**Originality (PASS, qualified).** Xu–Liu, arXiv:2610.03050v1, defines the skew Schäffer invariant and proves universal bounds and exact Hilbert/Lebesgue cases; its inspected relevant sections do not contain this hexagonal all-parameter endpoint formula. The closest published mathematical records about this very hexagonal norm compute a *different* parameterized von Neumann–Jordan invariant; other records examine a skew geometric-mean constant of \(\ell_4\) or distances to Hilbert norms, none of which implies the present infimum of two skew norms. Formula-specific searches identified no covering result, but unindexed prior equivalent work remains possible. A negative search alone is not treated as proof of priority.

**Value (PASS).** The regular affine hexagon is a canonical nonsmooth, uniformly nonsquare test space. Its complete all-parameter skew profile is an exact structural benchmark beyond existing Hilbert and Lebesgue formulas, with explicit optimizers, a nontrivial \(4/3\) midpoint, and an unexpected cubic rather than quadratic departure at zero. The result treats a natural whole-parameter invariant, not a chosen finite computation or a renamed general bound.

**Scientific scope and risk.** No result is claimed for finite outer exponents or arbitrary polygonal norms. The comparison with unpublished and unindexed mathematics is necessarily incomplete. No independent validation or novelty certification is claimed.
