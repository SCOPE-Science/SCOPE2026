Every compact stationary Rulkov neuron state has exact fast-variable mean \(\mathbb E[x]=\sigma-1\) and exact slow–fast covariance \(\operatorname{Cov}(x,y)=(\mu/2)\operatorname{Var}(x)\). The resulting variance bound is rigid: for an ergodic state with nonzero fast variance, equality can occur only on a two-cycle.
