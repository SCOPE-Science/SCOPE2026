Every compact stationary state of the classical piecewise-linear Chua flow obeys exact conditional regressions and the speed-slope law \(\mathbb E[\dot y^2]=\mathbb E[h^{\prime}(x)\dot x^2]\). In the classical double-scroll slopes, every genuinely recurrent state reaches beyond \(|x|=3/2\) and more than one third of its \(x\)-speed energy lies on the outer branches \(|x|>1\).
