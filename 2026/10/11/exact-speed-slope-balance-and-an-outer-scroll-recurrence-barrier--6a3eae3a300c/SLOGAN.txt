For every compact invariant probability measure of the three-segment Chua flow, the exact new stationary speed-slope law is \(\mathbb E[\dot y^2]=\mathbb E[h'(x)\dot x^2]\). Every non-equilibrium state consequently concentrates strictly more than \(-m_0/(m_1-m_0)\) of its squared \(x\)-speed energy on \(|x|>1\), more than one third for the classical slopes. The previously known equilibrium-amplitude excursion is not asserted to be new.
