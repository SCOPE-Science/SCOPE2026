Every compact stationary state of the Cui–Li four-dimensional flow lies on the exact moment shell
\[
\int\!\left(w+\frac{a}{2e}\right)^2d\mu=\left(\frac{a}{2e}\right)^2+\frac{a}{be}\int u^2d\mu.
\]
Except for the origin Dirac mass, such a state must place positive mass outside the slab \(-a/e\le w\le0\).
