A later paper sharing the name “Sprott B” was checked and is a different thermostatic vector field. Closest stationary-shell results in indexed literature concern other flows and do not imply this theorem.

This is a structural restriction on recurrent dynamics, not a parameter renaming or a table recomputation.

## Closest literature and limitations

The theorem is limited to compactly supported invariant probability measures; it does not establish existence or uniqueness of a chaotic invariant measure and does not control unbounded trajectories. Absolute novelty cannot be proved by search, so obscure or unindexed prior work remains a residual risk.
