Every compact stationary state of the classical Sprott B flow has the exact shell \(\mathbb E[y^2]=1\), while \(\mathbb E[x^2]-1=\mathbb E[\dot y^2]\) measures departure from the equilibrium mixtures. Any non-equilibrium compact stationary state must occupy both \(|y|<1\) and \(|y|>1\), forcing every nonconstant periodic orbit to cross \(|y|=1\) at least twice per least period.
