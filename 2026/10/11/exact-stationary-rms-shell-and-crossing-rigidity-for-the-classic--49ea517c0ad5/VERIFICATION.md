# Verification

The classical vector field was checked against the original Sprott catalogue and against a later mathematical full text that writes the same case B equations. Primary MSC ownership was checked from that later source as \(34C05\) and \(34C45\).

For a compact invariant probability measure, the generator identity was applied to arbitrary continuously differentiable functions of \(y\) and of \(z\). This yields the conditional laws \(\mathbb E[x\mid y]=y\) and \(\mathbb E[xy\mid z]=1\). Their integrated consequences give \(\mathbb E[y^2]=1\). Expanding \((x-y)^2\) then gives the exact defect \(\mathbb E[x^2]-1=\mathbb E[\dot y^2]\).

The equality case was checked geometrically: if the defect is zero then the invariant support lies in \(x=y\); tangency of the flow and compactness exclude the \(y=0\) translating orbit and leave only \(E_+\) and \(E_-\). Conversely their probability mixtures attain equality.

The crossing conclusion was checked from the exact shell. If either \(|y|<1\) or \(|y|>1\) had zero stationary mass, then \(y^2=1\) almost surely, which by invariance collapses the support to the equilibria. A nonconstant periodic orbit therefore visits both sides, and continuity on its time circle forces at least two crossings per least period.

Full-text comparison covered the original catalogue source, the Sprott BC mathematical study, and a later same-name but inequivalent thermostatic system. Focused literature and indexed-record searches were also performed for aliases, exact equations, moments, RMS identities, defects, and crossing statements. No numerical experiment or incomplete enumeration is used to certify the theorem. Remaining uncertainty is bibliographic: search cannot exclude an obscure unindexed prior derivation.
