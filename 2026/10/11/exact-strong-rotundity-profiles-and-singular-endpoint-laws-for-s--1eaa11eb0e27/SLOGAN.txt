For every nonzero real Banach space \(Y\), the local strong-rotundity index at the scalar \(\ell_p\)-summand vector in \(\mathbb R\oplus_pY\) is exactly the inverse of \(d^p+1-|1-d|^p\) for \(1<p<\infty\), with explicit formulas at \(p=1,\infty\). Its full profile exhibits a universal finite-\(p\) value \(\eta(1)=1/2\), an \(\epsilon^p/p\) small-scale law, and genuine endpoint jumps at \(p=1\) and \(p=\infty\).
