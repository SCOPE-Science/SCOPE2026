For real \(\ell_p(I)\), the two-parameter geometric-mean constant satisfies \(\mathcal T_1(\kappa,\tau,\ell_p(I))=(\kappa^p+\tau^p)^{1/p}\) throughout \(1\le p\le2\), and equals \(\kappa+\tau\) at \(p=\infty\). Combined with the known \(p>2\) formula, this completes the exact classical \(\ell_p\) phase diagram and identifies \(p=2\) as the extremal-geometry transition.
