For the body-centered cubic optimizer of the regular-octahedron lattice covering problem, the entire pre-covering volume profile is explicit: for \(1\le r\le3/2\), the uncovered fraction is \(r(3-r)(3-2r)/3\) and the double-covered fraction is \((r-1)^3\). At the sharp radius \(r=3/2\), exactly \(7/8\) of space is singly covered and \(1/8\) doubly covered almost everywhere, so the density excess \(9/8-1\) is precisely the double-overlap mass.
