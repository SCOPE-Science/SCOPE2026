On cyclic groups of twice an odd prime, the exact-support Fourier uncertainty profile throughout the first half of support sizes follows a sharp parity law with two competing even-support constructions. For primes \(p\ge11\), forcing a fifth nonzero time coefficient after four makes the optimally concentrated Fourier spectrum larger by exactly \(p-7\), revealing an unbounded nonmonotonicity invisible to the usual at-most-support uncertainty function.
