For the canonical non-radial weights \(\mu_{i,j}=\omega_{i+j}\sqrt{\binom{i+j}{i}}\), the quadrant backward shift becomes, after a degreewise Hadamard rotation, an orthogonal sum of ordinary unilateral weighted shifts. This yields the full exact singular spectrum of every diagonal transfer \(B^n:X_{q+n}\to X_q\), not merely an asymptotic smallest singular value.
