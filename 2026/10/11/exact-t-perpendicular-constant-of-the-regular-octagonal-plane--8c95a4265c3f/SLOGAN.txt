For the regular octagonal norm \(N(s,t)=\max\{|s|,|t|,(|s|+|t|)/\sqrt2\}\), the Ahmad--Xie--Li Birkhoff-orthogonal constant is exactly \(T_{\perp}(X_8)=\sqrt{(12+11\sqrt2)/8}\). The maximizer occurs at an octagon vertex with the orthogonal partner in the interior of an adjacent opposite-sign support arc.
