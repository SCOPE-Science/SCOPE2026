For the 18 ternary cellular automata defined by three-element semigroups, the number of possible length-\(n\) single-site time histories has an exact formula for every \(n\ge1\). Exactly five have positive temporal trace entropy (one equal to \(\log3\) and four equal to \(\log2\)); the remaining thirteen have polynomial or bounded history growth, even though some of the former have absorbing states.
