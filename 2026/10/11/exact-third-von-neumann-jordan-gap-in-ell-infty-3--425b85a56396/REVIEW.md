## correctness

For three real l_infinity^3 vectors, convexity on each fixed-radius coordinate box reduces the 3x3 matrix numerator to sign rows. Four projective sign classes exist; one class has quotient 1, two classes at most 2, three classes reduce to (r^2+2r+4)/(r^2+2), globally at most (3+sqrt(3))/2 by an exact positive square, attained at radii sqrt(3)-1,1,1. For the unit-sphere modified constant, three coordinate rows cover at most three of four sign classes, giving numerator 3*9+1=28 and denominator 12, or 7/3. Independent enumeration confirmed all 64 projective row choices and exact symbolic equality; continuous radius proof is analytic.
