For every ellipsoid \(E\subset\mathbb R^3\), the sole nontrivial three-dimensional Janson coefficient is exactly \(W_{2,E}(1,1,1)=\frac{\pi^2}{160}|E|^2\). Thus ellipsoids occupy exactly the fraction \(\pi^2/48\) of the universal Janson upper bound, providing an affine-invariant benchmark for the unresolved extremal problem.
