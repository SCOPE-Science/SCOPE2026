Every unweighted three-tap convolution on an extraspecial ternary Heisenberg group is singular, and its complex nullity belongs to exactly three levels: \(2Q\), \(2Q/3\), or \(2Q/9\), where \(Q=3^{2n}\). The nullity level is determined by the quotient triangle's symplectic geometry and cyclic-coset structure, with an exact closed count for every level and every \(n\geq1\).
