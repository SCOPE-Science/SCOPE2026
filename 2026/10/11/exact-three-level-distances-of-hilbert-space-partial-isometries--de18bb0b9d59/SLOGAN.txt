For partial isometries on a separable infinite-dimensional complex Hilbert space, the exact distance to the absolutely norm-attaining operators is always \(0\), \(1/2\), or \(1\), determined by initial rank, kernel dimension, and cokernel dimension. An infinite-rank projection and a surjective coisometry can have the same modulus but different distances, \(1/2\) and \(1\).
