For every \(0<p<1\), the sharp universal inverse-norm constant for canonical embeddings of real three-point Lipschitz-free \(p\)-spaces into arbitrary \(p\)-metric superspaces is \(\bigl(4/(4-2^{p+1}+2^{2p})\bigr)^{1/p}\). A four-point weighted star attains the constant, matching and completing the previously known lower bound.
