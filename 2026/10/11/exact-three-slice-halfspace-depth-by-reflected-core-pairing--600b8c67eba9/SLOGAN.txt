For three convex slices consisting of one reflected-included homothetic copy and two identical larger copies, the exact maximum normalized halfspace depth is \((\delta(K)+r^d)/(2+r^d)\). In the canonical planar triangle sharpness family, this settles the full finite-mass profile \(2/9+7\varepsilon/9\) for \(0<\varepsilon\le1/9\), with the middle centroid attaining the bound.
