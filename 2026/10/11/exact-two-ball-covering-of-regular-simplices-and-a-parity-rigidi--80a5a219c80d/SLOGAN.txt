For every regular Euclidean \(d\)-simplex with \(d\ge3\), the least radius of two equal balls covering its *entire body* is exactly \(s\sqrt{(k-1)/(2k)}\), where \(s\) is the edge length and \(k=\lceil(d+1)/2\rceil\). Odd dimensions have only finitely many, fully classified optimal center pairs, while even dimensions admit continuous families of optimal centers.
