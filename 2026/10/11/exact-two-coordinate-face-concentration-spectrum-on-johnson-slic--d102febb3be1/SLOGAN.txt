For Johnson-scheme functions of degree at most \(L\), concentrating on the face of all \(k\)-subsets containing two prescribed elements has a completely explicit spectrum: apart from \(0\) and \(1\), only two rational boundary eigenvalues can occur, with exact multiplicities. This determines the face-restriction rank and all entropy-type spectral traces for every \(n\ge2k\ge4\) and \(0\le L<k\).
