For the canonical upper-half-plane Gram kernel generated by the value-and-first-derivative sequence \(\xi=(\xi_0,\xi_1,0,\ldots)\), the Lu Qi-Keng phase is exactly projective: when \(\xi_0\ne0\), zero-freeness holds precisely for \(\operatorname{Im}(\xi_1/\xi_0)\ge0\). If \(\xi_0=0\), the pure first-derivative kernel is also zero-free.
