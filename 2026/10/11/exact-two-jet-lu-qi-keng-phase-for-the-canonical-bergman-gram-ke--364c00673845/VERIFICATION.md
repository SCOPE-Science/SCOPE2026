For the classical upper-half-plane Bergman kernel,
\[
K_{\mathbb H}(z,w)
=-\frac1{\pi(z-\overline w)^2},
\]
put
\[
u=z-\overline w.
\]
Direct differentiation gives
\[
K^{(m,n)}_{\mathbb H}(z,w)
=
\frac{(-1)^{m+1}(m+n+1)!}{\pi u^{m+n+2}}.
\]

For the Gram kernel generated by
\[
\xi=(\xi_0,\xi_1,0,\ldots),
\]
expansion of the inner product gives
\[
B_\xi(z,w)
=
\sum_{m,n=0}^1
\frac{\overline{\xi_m}\xi_n}{m!n!}
K^{(m,n)}_{\mathbb H}(z,w).
\]
Thus, for
\[
t=u^{-1},
\]
its zero set is controlled by
\[
F_\xi(t)
=
|\xi_0|^2
+2(\overline{\xi_0}\xi_1-\overline{\xi_1}\xi_0)t
-6|\xi_1|^2t^2.
\]

When
\[
\xi_0\ne0,
\qquad
\frac{\xi_1}{\xi_0}=x+iy,
\]
the normalized factor is
\[
1+4iyt-6(x^2+y^2)t^2.
\]
For a nonzero ratio its roots are
\[
t_\pm
=
\frac{2iy\pm\sqrt2\sqrt{3x^2+y^2}}{6(x^2+y^2)},
\]
so
\[
\operatorname{Im}t_\pm
=
\frac{y}{3(x^2+y^2)}.
\]
This establishes the exact root-location trichotomy.

The parameter
\[
t=(z-\overline w)^{-1}
\]
ranges over exactly the lower half-plane. Inclusion follows from
\[
\operatorname{Im}(z-\overline w)>0.
\]
For surjectivity, if
\[
t_*^{-1}=p+iq,
\qquad q>0,
\]
then
\[
z=p/2+iq/2,
\qquad
w=-p/2+iq/2
\]
belong to the upper half-plane and satisfy
\[
z-\overline w=t_*^{-1}.
\]

If
\[
\xi_0=0,
\]
then
\[
F_\xi(t)=-6|\xi_1|^2t^2,
\]
and its only zero is
\[
t=0,
\]
which is outside the attained parameter region. If
\[
\xi_1=0,
\]
then the factor is constant.

All continuum assertions are analytic and exact; no finite experiment is used. The primary full text was checked at the Gram definition and upper-half-plane zero reduction, and the proof here independently reconstructs the coefficient conjugations from the Gram product itself.
