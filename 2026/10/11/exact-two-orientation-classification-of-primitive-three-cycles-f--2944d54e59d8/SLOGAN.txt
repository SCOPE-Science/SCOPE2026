For Everett's farthest-orthogonal-projection map on three complete triangle supporting lines, there are exactly zero, one, or two primitive three-cycles according to two explicit triples of strict polynomial inequalities in the normalized vertex coordinates. In the isosceles family, exactly two such cycles exist precisely for \(1/2<v<\sqrt{3/4+\sqrt3/2}\); the endpoints are excluded because ties are fixed points.
