# Verification

The universal proof is algebraic. For a two-point signal, rank loss equals the multiplicity of one eigenvalue of the separation element in the left regular representation. Restricting that representation to the cyclic subgroup generated by the separation gives exactly one copy of every cyclic character per left coset, hence total multiplicity \(N/m\).

The included checker constructs regular permutation matrices for representative finite groups and confirms numerically that every tested resonant phase has rank \(N-N/m\), while a nonresonant coefficient ratio has full rank. These tests only corroborate the exact proof and do not establish the general theorem.

Limits: no verification or claim is made for support size at least three or for unweighted representation support.
