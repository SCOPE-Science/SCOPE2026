For the starlike subordination classes generated by \(e^z(1+z)^q\), \(0\le q\le1/2\), the exponential class embeds for free in every interpolant, but the sharp reverse dilation radius is \(R_q=1-qW(1/q)\) for \(q>0\). The logarithmic image has exact centered inradius \(1+q\log2\), while \(1-R_q\sim q\log(1/q)\); in particular the apple-like endpoint has sharp radius \(1-W(2)/2\).
