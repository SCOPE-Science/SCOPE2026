Every quadratic polynomial graph \(\Gamma_p=\{(z,p(z))\}\subset\mathbb C^2\) has an exact uniform-flatness scale: its reach is \(1/(2|a|)\) when \(p(z)=az^2+bz+c\) with \(a\ne0\), and is infinite in the affine case. Thus the first nonlinear class in the recent polynomial-graph theorem admits a complete sharp tubular-radius formula.
