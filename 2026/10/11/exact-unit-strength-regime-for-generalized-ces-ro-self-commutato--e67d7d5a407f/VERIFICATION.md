# Verification

The published identities used in the proof were checked in the full text of arXiv:2610.00193v1. Equation (2.2) gives a finite-support right inverse and therefore dense range. Theorem 2.3 gives the exact decomposition \(I-B^*B=R+\sum_r\lambda_rv_rv_r^*\), the coefficient formula, and the polynomial-degree information needed for linear independence. Theorem 1.1 gives the uniform coefficient \(1/36\) and explicitly states that it is not asserted optimal.

The new proof was checked algebraically in four stages: dense-range transfer of the quadratic form; the orthogonal-complement obstruction proving no coefficient above \(1\); equivalence between positivity of the finite-rank correction and the signs of its diagonal coefficient matrix; and the exact sign classification of \(\alpha\prod_{{j=1}}^r(\alpha^2-j^2)\). No numerical approximation or finite sampling is used.

The order-two historical comparison was checked in the primary PDF DOI:10.7153/oam-2018-12-08, including the rendered page containing Corollary 2.4. That result proves hyponormality for \(\alpha\in\{0\}\cup[1,\infty)\) and does not provide the optimal weighted coefficient classified here.

The exact values of \(c_m(\alpha)\) inside the noninteger bands are not proved and are not claimed.
