# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof defines a precise finite domain (four affinely independent lattice vertices, one marked vertex) and derives the exact point-to-closed-triangle distance through barycentric plane projection or closest-edge projection. An independent convex-quadratic minimum over interior, edges and vertices checks all 58,528 finite cases in exact `Fraction` arithmetic. Every candidate is generated once, the complete 88-level frequency table sums to the total, and all 48 cube symmetries are used to classify the first three levels. The conclusion is finite and no infinite extrapolation or heuristic probabilistic proof is made.

## Originality — PASS
Deza and Pournin's Theorem 1.1 and Proposition 4.1 establish the minimum and uniqueness for point–triangle separation (arXiv:2601.03183v1), while their tables concern extremal values. Deza–Liu–Pournin (arXiv:2502.19554v1; arXiv:2412.03479v1) concern smallest distances between lattice polytopes and their least-squares models. None of the inspected relevant passages supplies the 88-level closed-triangle distance distribution or the sharp runner-up classification. The known first level is explicitly not treated as new. Residual risk: an equivalent small-grid enumeration may exist outside indexed literature.

## Value — PASS
The three-level lattice cube is the first nonbinary setting in the all-grid family, and vertex-to-opposite-face distance is the motivating flatness invariant in the primary source. Classifying **every** value and its multiplicity gives an exact benchmark on a natural complete finite model, while identifying the first positive stability gap and two geometrically different runner-up orbits gives a sharp structural separation beyond the published unique minimizer. This is not an arbitrarily selected subfamily, a table recomputation of the published minimum, or a routine one-line corollary of extremal uniqueness.

## Comparison and limitations
The nearest published paper proves the first rank for all side lengths, not the distribution or the second rank at side length two. The classification excludes coplanar configurations and supplies no generalization to larger cubes. Exact enumeration replaces neither mathematical analysis of general parameters nor an independent audit. Source coverage was assessed using the full primary manuscripts cited in the references and a further published-papers search.

Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
