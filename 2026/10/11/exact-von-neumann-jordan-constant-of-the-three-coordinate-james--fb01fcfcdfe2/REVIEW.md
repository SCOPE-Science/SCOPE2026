# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reduces the exact three-coordinate James norm to four explicit quadratic forms, proves a global upper bound for every pair of vectors, and gives an exact extremizing pair. The exceptional matrix-difference case is handled symbolically through the spectrum \(\{1,-1/2,-1/2\}\).

## Originality
PASS. The focal James-tree paper, general von Neumann--Jordan literature, and targeted exact-constant searches were compared at statement and implication level. Nearby exact results concern Day--James planes, \(\ell_4\), Lorentz, or other norms, and no inspected result covers this \(J_3\) equality. The residual risk is older equivalent terminology for a finite James section.

## Value
PASS. The invariant is standard and the section is canonical: three successive branch coordinates are the smallest branch block exhibiting all four contiguous James partitions. The formula gives a sharp quantitative benchmark for the non-Hilbert geometry underlying current work on copies of \(J\) inside \(JT\).

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest current source is Argyros--González--Motakis, arXiv:2603.17886v1, which identifies branch sequences with the James basis and studies complemented copies of \(J\) and \(\ell_2\), but does not compute this invariant. The result is limited to \(J_3\) and does not determine the full-space constant.
