# Verification

The proof is exact. For three successive James coordinates, the only contiguous interval partitions give the four squared norms displayed in RESULT.md. Writing the correction forms as symmetric matrices yields operator norms at most \(1/\sqrt2\) for all pairwise differences except the full-interval correction against zero. That exceptional correction has eigenvalues \(1,-1/2,-1/2\), and the displayed scalar two-case estimate proves the needed bound for all vectors.
