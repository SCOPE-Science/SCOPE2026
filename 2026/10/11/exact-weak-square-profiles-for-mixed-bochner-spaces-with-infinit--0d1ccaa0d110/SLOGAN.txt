In real mixed atomic–nonatomic \(L_1(\mu;\ell_p)\), \(1<p<\infty\), the exact weakly null two-sided perturbation profile is \(1\) up to the nonatomic mass and then grows as \(b+(a^p+(t-b)^p)^{1/p}\). At unit perturbation norm, the sharp excess is \((2^{1/p}-1)a\), where \(a\) is the atomic mass of the base unit vector and \(b=1-a\).
