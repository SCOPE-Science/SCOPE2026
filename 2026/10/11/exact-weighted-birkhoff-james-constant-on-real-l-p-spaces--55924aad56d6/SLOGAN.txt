For every real \(L^p(\mu)\) containing two disjoint finite positive-measure pieces, the new weighted Birkhoff orthogonal James-type constant has an exact two-branch Hanner--Clarkson formula, with the transition at \(p=2\). The sharp unrestricted weighted James value is already attained by Birkhoff--James orthogonal two-piece witnesses, so the orthogonality restriction is inactive on this class.
