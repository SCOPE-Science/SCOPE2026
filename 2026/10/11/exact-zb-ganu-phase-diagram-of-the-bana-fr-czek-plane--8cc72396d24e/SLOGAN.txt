The Zbăganu constant of the Banaś--Frączek plane has the exact phase diagram
\[
C_Z=\lambda\quad(1<\lambda\le\sqrt2),\qquad
C_Z=2\sqrt{1-\lambda^{-2}}\quad(\lambda\ge\sqrt2).
\]
At the transition, a continuum of Euclidean-orthogonal maximizing pairs collapses to one orbit and then continues as the circle--strip transition-ray orbit.
