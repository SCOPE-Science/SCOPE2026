For the canonical unit-disk Bergman Gram kernel of the normalized \(m\)-th derivative evaluation, exactly \(\lceil m/2\rceil\) distinct negative-product zero fibres occur when \(m\ge1\), while order \(0\) remains zero-free. The numerator is a Cayley transform of the Jacobi polynomial \(P_m^{(0,1)}\), giving an exact all-orders count and a contrast with the zero-free pure-jet kernels on the upper half-plane.
