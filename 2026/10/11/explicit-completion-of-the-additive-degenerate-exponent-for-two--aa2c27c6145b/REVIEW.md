# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The final claim follows from the exact momentum equation \(y'=-a\). Zero mean is necessary under both boundary conditions. In the mean-zero two-step case, the Neumann momentum is forced to be \(-A\), while periodic closure reduces exactly to \(|c|^q=|c-M|^q\), hence \(c=M/2\). The oscillation formulas follow from exact power integrals over the two linear branches of \(A\). Positivity is obtained precisely by taking sufficiently large additive translates, and every solution is in the corresponding additive orbit.

PASS. The closest source, arXiv:2610.06068v1, explicitly excludes \(\gamma=0\) from its main theorem, records translation degeneracy, and identifies zero mean as the compatible scalar condition. It does not state the full necessary-and-sufficient existence classification, uniqueness modulo translation, the exact periodic momentum, or the oscillation ratio. The 2021 predecessor covers nondegenerate exponent ranges. Targeted published-finding corpus searches for source, aliases, boundary conditions, momentum formulations, and the oscillation invariant returned no implication-equivalent result.

## Value
PASS. This is a natural complete classification of one of the two exponents expressly excluded from a recent classification theorem. It replaces failure of ordinary uniqueness by exact uniqueness modulo the correct symmetry and quantifies the difference between Neumann and periodic shapes through a universal oscillation ratio. It does not claim broader nonlinear consequences.
