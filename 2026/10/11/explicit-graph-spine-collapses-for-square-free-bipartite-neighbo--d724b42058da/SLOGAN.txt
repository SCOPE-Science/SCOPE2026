For every finite connected bipartite square-free graph \(G\), the Lovász neighborhood complex simplicially collapses onto two explicit root-selected graph spines. Each spine is connected with cycle rank \(e-|V(G)|+1\), so \(\mathcal N(G)\simeq(\bigvee^{e-|V(G)|+1}S^1)\sqcup(\bigvee^{e-|V(G)|+1}S^1)\).
