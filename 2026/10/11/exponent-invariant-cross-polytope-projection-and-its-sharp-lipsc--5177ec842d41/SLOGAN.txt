The nearest-point map onto a real cross-polytope is the same soft-threshold map in every \(\ell_p\) geometry with \(1<p<\infty\), yet it is globally nonexpansive only in dimensions at most two or at \(p=2\). Its exact Lipschitz modulus is the largest induced norm of an active-coordinate centering projection; in the first expansive case \(n=3,p=4\), the sharp constant is \(\bigl((1+\sqrt[3]2)^3/9\bigr)^{1/4}\).
