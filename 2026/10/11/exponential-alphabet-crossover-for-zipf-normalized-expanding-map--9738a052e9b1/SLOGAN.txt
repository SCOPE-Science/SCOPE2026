For a Zipf-normalized countable expanding map, restricting the alphabet to \(\lfloor e^{\tau/(q-1)}\rfloor\) branches produces an explicit nontrivial first-order Hausdorff-dimension bias as \(q\downarrow1\). The finite survivor crosses the natural convergence exponent \(1/q\) at the sharp exponential cutoff phase \(\tau=1/\lambda\), while fixed cutoffs and the full countable alphabet have noncommuting dimension limits.
