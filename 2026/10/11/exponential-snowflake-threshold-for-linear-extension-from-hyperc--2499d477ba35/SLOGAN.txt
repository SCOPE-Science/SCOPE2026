For a Euclidean \(d\)-cube's vertices plus its center, the optimal real-linear simultaneous Lipschitz extension is the vertex mean, and the exact norm is \(\max\{1,2^{-d}\sum_{k=1}^d\binom dk(2\sqrt{k/d})^\alpha\}\). Its sharp norm-one snowflake threshold is exponentially small: \(2^d\alpha_d=2/\log2+1/(d(\log2)^2)+O(d^{-2})\), while the unsnowflaked optimal norm tends to \(\sqrt2\).
