For the 1994 Sprott G system, the exact slice \(a=-1/2\) collapses all bounded long-time behavior onto equilibria through an exponentially contracting quadratic manifold. When \(b\ne0\), its entire bounded complete nonstationary dynamics is one unique saddle-to-sink heteroclinic with exact dissipation \(4|b|^3/3\); when \(b=0\), only the equilibrium line remains.
