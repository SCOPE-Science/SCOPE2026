For the max-plus reset oscillator started at \((1,1)\), adjacent parameter thresholds near \(R=4\) enclose exponentially thin gaps of width \(\pi^2/(2^nn^3)(1+O(n^{-1}))\). The neighboring reset-window threshold separation is only polynomially small, \(8\pi^2/n^3(1+o(1))\), so the exact gap-to-window ratio is asymptotic to \(2^{-n}/8\).
