For finite-dimensional real Banach spaces, \(T_G(X)=2\) exactly when one unit decomposition \(u=v+w\) is supported by two exposed faces meeting at \(u\). The Day--James \(\ell_\infty-\ell_1\) plane satisfies this criterion while \(J=3/2\), so \(T_G\)-saturation can coexist with uniform non-squareness.
