# Facet complements are exactly the minimum \(H_3\)-carriers in the sixteen-vertex \(\mathbb{RP}^4\)
## Finding
Let \(K=\mathbb{RP}^{4}_{16}\) be Lutz's sixteen-vertex triangulation of real projective four-space in Datta's \(S_6\)-equivariant labeling. Its face vector is
\[
(16,120,330,375,150).
\]
Over \(\mathbf F_2\),
\[
H_3(K;\mathbf F_2)\cong\mathbf F_2.
\]

For a simplicial \(3\)-cycle \(z\), let its vertex support be the union of the vertices of the tetrahedra occurring in \(z\). The unique nonzero class in \(H_3(K;\mathbf F_2)\) has minimum vertex support \(11\).

Exactly \(150\) eleven-vertex subsets support the nonzero class. They are precisely
\[
V(K)\setminus \sigma,
\]
where \(\sigma\) ranges over the \(150\) facets of \(K\). If \(S=V(K)\setminus\sigma\), then
\[
H_3(K[S];\mathbf F_2)\cong\mathbf F_2
\]
and the inclusion
\[
K[S]\hookrightarrow K
\]
induces an isomorphism on third mod-two homology. Every other eleven-vertex induced subcomplex has zero third mod-two homology.

The full simplicial automorphism group is \(S_6\). It splits these \(150\) minimum supports into two orbits of sizes
\[
30\quad\text{and}\quad120.
\]
Representatives may be chosen as
\[
S_{30}=\{1,2,3,4,6,8,11,12,14,15,16\}
\]
and
\[
S_{120}=\{1,2,3,4,5,6,7,12,13,14,16\}.
\]
Their induced face vectors are, respectively,
\[
(11,55,92,52,4)
\]
and
\[
(11,55,95,58,7).
\]

## Assumptions and scope
Datta's labeling uses the subgroup of \(S_{16}\) generated by
\[
(2\,7)(4\,10)(5\,6)(11\,12)
\]
and
\[
(1\,2\,3\,4\,5\,10)(6\,8\,9)(11\,12\,13\,14\,15\,16),
\]
acting on the two facet representatives
\[
\{1,2,4,5,11\},\qquad \{1,2,4,11,13\}.
\]
This generates exactly the \(150\) facets of \(K\).

All homology in the finding is over \(\mathbf F_2\). The support invariant counts vertices, not tetrahedra, geometric volume, or metric length. The result concerns this specific vertex-minimal triangulation.

## Proof
Expanding the two published facet orbits under the published \(S_6\) action gives exactly \(150\) facets and reconstructs
\[
f(K)=(16,120,330,375,150).
\]

Let \(\partial_3\colon C_3(K;\mathbf F_2)\to C_2(K;\mathbf F_2)\) and
\(\partial_4\colon C_4(K;\mathbf F_2)\to C_3(K;\mathbf F_2)\).
Exact row reduction gives
\[
\operatorname{rank}\partial_3=225,\qquad
\operatorname{rank}\partial_4=149.
\]
Therefore
\[
\dim_{\mathbf F_2}H_3(K;\mathbf F_2)
=375-225-149
=1.
\]

To establish the support minimum, the verifier exhausts all
\[
\binom{16}{10}=8008
\]
ten-vertex subsets. For each subset \(S\), it computes the kernel of the restricted tetrahedron-to-triangle boundary map and tests whether any local \(3\)-cycle survives modulo the global boundary space \(\operatorname{im}\partial_4\). None does. Any cycle supported on fewer than ten vertices is contained in some ten-vertex subset, so no nonzero class has support at most \(10\).

Next the verifier exhausts all
\[
\binom{16}{11}=4368
\]
eleven-vertex subsets. Exactly \(150\) contain a local \(3\)-cycle whose global class is nonzero. Independently, taking complements of the \(150\) facets produces exactly the same family of \(150\) subsets.

For each facet complement \(S\), direct local rank computation gives
\[
\dim_{\mathbf F_2}H_3(K[S];\mathbf F_2)=1,
\]
and the surviving local cycle is nonzero in global homology. Since both source and target have dimension one, inclusion induces an isomorphism. For each of the other \(4218\) eleven-vertex subsets, the local third homology is zero.

Finally, the published \(S_6\) action has order \(720\). Acting on the \(150\) facet complements gives precisely two orbits, of sizes \(30\) and \(120\). Direct face counting in orbit representatives gives the two displayed induced face vectors.

## Verification
The standalone `verify.py` uses only the Python standard library. It reconstructs the group and the complex from the two published facet representatives, computes the global chain ranks, exhausts every ten-vertex and eleven-vertex subset, checks equality with the family of facet complements, computes every local third-homology dimension, and verifies the two symmetry orbits and their induced face vectors.

Its exact successful output is:

`VERIFY_OK f=16,120,330,375,150 H3_F2_dim=1 min_vertex_support=11 supports11=150 facet_complements=yes other11_local_H3=0 S6_order=720 support_orbits=30,120 rep30_f=11,55,92,52,4 rep120_f=11,55,95,58,7`

The computation is exhaustive over every finite family used in the statement. No absence result is inferred from a timeout or from sampling.

## Relationship to prior work
Lutz constructed the sixteen-vertex triangulation and established the vertex-minimal setting. Datta gives the exact \(S_6\)-equivariant facet-orbit presentation and the face vector. Balagopalan studies the same complex in detail, records the full \(S_6\) automorphism group and the two facet orbits of sizes \(30\) and \(120\), and develops further constructions related to the Witt design.

Those sources provide the object, minimality, symmetry, and facet-orbit structure. The new statement concerns a different invariant: the minimum vertex support of the unique mod-two \(H_3\) class, the exact classification of all minimum induced carriers, and the fact that the carrier family is precisely dual to the facet family under vertex complementation.

A previously recorded result on this same complex classifies the shortest nonzero \(H_1(-;\mathbf F_2)\) cycles among missing triangles. It does not imply the degree-three support theorem: the dimensions, chain groups, support sizes, and facet-complement classification are different.

## Limitations
The result is mod-two and does not classify integral \(3\)-cycles, larger-support representatives, or minimum supports in other triangulations of real projective four-space. It also does not assert a general facet-complement theorem for arbitrary projective-space triangulations.

The originality assessment is literature-based. An unindexed historical computation or auxiliary table could contain the same induced-subcomplex census.
