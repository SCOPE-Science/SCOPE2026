In the sixteen-vertex \(\mathbb{RP}^4\), the unique nonzero class in \(H_3(-;\mathbf F_2)\) first appears on \(11\) vertices, and its \(150\) minimum supports are exactly the complements of the \(150\) facets. The full \(S_6\) symmetry splits these carriers into the same \(30+120\) pattern as the facets.
