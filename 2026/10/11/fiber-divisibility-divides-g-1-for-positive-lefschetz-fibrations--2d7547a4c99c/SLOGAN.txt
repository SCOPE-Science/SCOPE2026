For every positive Lefschetz fibration over \(S^2\) of fiber genus \(g\ge2\), the fiber divisibility \(m(f)\) divides \(g-1\). Consequently every genus-\(2\) or genus-\(3\) fibration admits a positive \(2\)-section, every genus \(g\ge3\) admits a positive \(g-1\)-section, and in genus \(2\) every degree \(d\ge2\) occurs.
