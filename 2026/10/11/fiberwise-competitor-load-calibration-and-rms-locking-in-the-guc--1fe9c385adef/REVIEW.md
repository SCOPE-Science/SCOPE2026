# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Cutoff logarithmic generator tests are valid because compact support gives bounded coefficients and each coordinate plane has zero measure. They yield zero conditional mean per-capita growth on every squared-amplitude fiber. The resulting circulant mean system is nonsingular exactly when \(a,b,c\) are not all equal. Conditional orthogonality gives the covariance and variance-speed identities, and summing the per-capita rates gives the aggregate squared-amplitude defect.

Risk: measures charging invariant coordinate planes are outside the theorem and require separate boundary statements.

## Originality
PASS. The complete open 2015 same-object review was inspected through the exact equations and heteroclinic-cycle construction and searched across the full article for invariant-measure, moment, covariance, and average formulations. A later coupled-cell review was also compared. Broader generalized Lotka–Volterra global mean balances are treated as prior background; the accepted conditional disintegration is strictly stronger because it yields all weighted balances against functions of one squared amplitude.

Risk: the cutoff argument is short and could have appeared under different terminology in unindexed population-dynamics or heteroclinic-cycle literature.

PASS.
