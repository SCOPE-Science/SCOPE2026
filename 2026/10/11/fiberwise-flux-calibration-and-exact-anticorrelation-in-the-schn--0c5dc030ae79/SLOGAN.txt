Every compact stationary Schnakenberg state calibrates the autocatalyst exactly on total-concentration fibers: \(\mathbb E[u\mid u+v]=a+b\). This forces the universal mean \(\mathbb E[u]=a+b\), the exact anticovariance \(\operatorname{Cov}(u,v)=-\operatorname{Var}(u)\), and a crossing of the equilibrium autocatalyst level on every nonconstant periodic orbit.
