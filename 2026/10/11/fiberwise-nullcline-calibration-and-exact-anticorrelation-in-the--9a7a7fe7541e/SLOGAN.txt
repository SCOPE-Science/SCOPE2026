Every compact positive stationary Brusselator state is exactly calibrated on two natural fibers: \(\mathbb E[x\mid x+y]=a\) and \(\mathbb E[y\mid x]=((b+1)x-a)/x^2\). These laws force the universal identity \(\operatorname{Cov}(x,y)=-\operatorname{Var}(x)\), so every non-equilibrium stationary state has strictly negative species covariance and larger \(y\)-variance than \(x\)-variance.
