# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The exact facet list reconstructs \((11,51,80,40)\), with four missing edges and \(135\) three-vertex cliques. Mod-two reduction proves exactly the \(55\) nonfacial cliques are nonzero cycles. A separate determinant-two lattice certificate, together with the one-dimensional mod-two quotient, proves integral first homology is exactly \(\mathbb Z/2\). Therefore all \(55\) nonfacial triangles are integral torsion generators and no shorter simple cycle can exist. Exhaustive symmetry enumeration gives \(48\) automorphisms and systole orbits \(3,4,12,12,24\).

## Originality
PASS. The exact-object sources already give the triangulation, its face vector, minimality, full automorphism group, and the later fact that it is the unique eleven-vertex projective-space triangulation with this face vector. Those facts are not claimed as new. Required semantic searches and statement-level source inspection found no result asserting the complete \(55\)-triangle torsion census or its five automorphism orbits. Residual risk remains from inaccessible or unindexed historical material.

## Value
PASS. The unique vertex-and-facet-minimal triangulation of real projective three-space is a canonical extremal complex, and its order-two first homology is the fundamental nontrivial loop invariant. The result completely identifies the shortest combinatorial carriers of that class and resolves their symmetry structure, rather than reporting an arbitrary finite statistic.

## Closest literature and limitations
Lutz's 2005 survey is the closest exact-object source: it prints the same facets and determines the full automorphism group. Sulanke--Lutz later enumerate all thirty eleven-vertex triangulations of the manifold and prove Walkup's complex is uniquely facet-minimal. The new claim is limited to the shortest homology layer of this one complex and does not extend to the other twenty-nine minimal triangulations or to geometric systoles.
