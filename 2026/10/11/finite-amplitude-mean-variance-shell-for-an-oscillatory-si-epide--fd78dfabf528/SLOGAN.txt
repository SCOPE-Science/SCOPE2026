For the nonlinear-incidence SI model, every nontrivial compact recurrent state obeys an exact variance-gap law \(V_X+nV_Y=\frac{n+1}{n}(x_+-\bar X)(\bar X-x_-)\) whenever two endemic equilibria exist. Thus every positive nonconstant cycle has mean infective density strictly between the two endemic-equilibrium infective coordinates, even at finite amplitude.
