Every positive nonconstant periodic orbit of the Fan–Wolkowicz delay model lies on an exact mean–variance shell: \(\operatorname{Var}_T(x)=(\bar x-x_*)(1-\bar x)\) and \(\operatorname{Cov}_T(x,y)=-\operatorname{Var}_T(x)\). Consequently its mean prey lies strictly above the coexistence value, its mean predator lies strictly below it, and predator variance strictly exceeds prey variance.
