On \(\mathbb R^n\), the bounded semiconjugacies supplied by Tikhomirov's generalized-hyperbolicity theorem are automatically proper surjections, because every bounded continuous perturbation of the identity has Brouwer degree \(1\). Thus the perturbed and unperturbed systems are mutual factors and have the same supremum of topological entropy over compact invariant subsystems, although conjugacy is not implied.
