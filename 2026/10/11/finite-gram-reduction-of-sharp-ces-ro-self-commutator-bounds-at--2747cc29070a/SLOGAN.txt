For every positive integer order, the sharp weighted self-commutator lower coefficient of a generalized Cesàro matrix is exactly the smallest spectral value of an explicit finite Gram perturbation. At order two this gives a digamma closed form on \(0<\alpha<1\), with optimal coefficient \(261/560+(3/4)\log2\) at \(\alpha=1/2\), never attained by a nonzero input.
