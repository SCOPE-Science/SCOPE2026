# Same-model mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof reconstructs the source equation with \(d>0\) and arbitrary fixed \(a,\varepsilon\), \(b,c>0\). The exact stretched-time change, four strict inward-pointing rectangle faces, and finite integral of \(e^{-x/3}\) establish a nonempty open set of finite-time escaping trajectories. The positive quadratic error energy yields convergence to the planar profile, and direct integration of \(\dot q=-Y/3\) determines the three physical-time coefficients. The reflection argument applies to the opposite escape sign. The algebraic checker verifies identities, not the infinite-time assertions; those follow from the analytic inequalities.

## Originality — PASS
The inspected Hu–Sang–Wang full paper reports attractors, Hopf/pitchfork bifurcations and the divergence \(-c\), without a finite-time singularity classification. The closest published theorem for this precise jerk equation excludes or classifies **bounded** recurrent dynamics and explicitly leaves unbounded trajectories outside its conclusion. General positively homogeneous blow-up asymptotics do not directly imply the result for exponential positional forcing. Search-based novelty claims remain fallible; the strongest residual risk is an unindexed general singularity theorem applicable after a nontrivial transformation. A later different-jerk-family article was only accessible through its publisher abstract.

## Value — PASS
Finite-time noncompleteness for an open set of initial states qualifies an unbounded confinement claim in a standard volume-contracting chaotic circuit flow. The theorem also provides a parameter-uniform limiting profile, rigorous two-sided life bounds and a clear line between escaping and bounded recurrence without asserting a full basin partition. The exact singularity coefficients are natural features of the same flow, rather than arbitrary measurements.
