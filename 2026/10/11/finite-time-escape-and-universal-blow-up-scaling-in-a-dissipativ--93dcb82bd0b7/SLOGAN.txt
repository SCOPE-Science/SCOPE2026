The positive-feedback hyperbolic-sine jerk flow has open sets of finite-time escaping trajectories even with constant negative divergence. On every constructed positive escape, \(x(t)=-3\log(T-t)+\log(12/d)+o(1)\), while \((T-t)\dot x(t)\to3\) and \((T-t)^2\ddot x(t)\to3\); reflected open sets have the opposite signs.
