The Zhou--Ni--Liu--Li parallelogram quotient is finite on the real \(\ell_p\) scale only at the Hilbert exponent: \(P(\ell_2)=-1\), while its defining supremum is unbounded above for every \(p\ne2\). Thus unboundedness persists even in smooth uniformly convex \(\ell_p\) spaces and is not a polyhedral artifact.
