For a hyperelliptic fibered-knot branch surface in \(T^2\times S^2\), the orbit lengths \(r_1,\ldots,r_c\) of the Weierstrass permutation determine the entire first homology of the complement: \(H_1\cong\mathbb Z^{c+1}\oplus\mathbb Z/\gcd(r_1,\ldots,r_c)\mathbb Z\). The torsion is therefore exactly the gcd of the orbit lengths.
