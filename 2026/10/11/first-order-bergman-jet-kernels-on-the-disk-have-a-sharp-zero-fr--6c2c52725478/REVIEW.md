I independently symbolically expanded N_t(z,v)=(1-zv)^2+2t(z+v)(1-zv)+2t^2(1+2zv) and P_t=N_t(z,-1): A=1+2t,B=2-4t^2,C=1-2t+2t^2, discriminant 8t^2(2t^2-2t-1). For 0<t<sqrt(2), P_t(±1)>0, A>C>0: real roots cannot both lie beyond either endpoint, nonreal roots have squared modulus C/A<1. For t=sqrt(2), one strictly interior root persists; for larger t, P(-1)>0>P(1) gives one real interior root. Continuity of roots under v=-r as r tends to 1 gives an interior zero, not a false boundary evaluation. Pure derivative has zero at z conjugate(w)=-1/2; b=0 leaves K. Complex phase rotates away by disk symmetry. This covers all a,b not both zero.
