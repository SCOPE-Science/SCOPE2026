For every fixed singular-value rank of \((I-(1+s/n)J_n)^{-1}\), the first-order critical-window correction has an explicit Robin–Dirichlet spectral formula and changes sign at \(s=1\). At that unique cancellation point every fixed-rank remainder improves to \(O(n^{-1})\), while the largest singular value equals exactly \(n\) in every dimension.
