# Review

## Correctness
**PASS.** Strict convexity is exactly the positivity of \(\rho=p+p''\). Positivity at opposite normals forces \(R>3|a|\), placing the normalized parameter \(\lambda=-3a/R\) in \((-1,1)\). The Wigner branch has signed support \(b\sin(5t)\), so its cusp equation and ordinary-cusp nondegeneracy are immediate. The centre-symmetry-set cusp criterion reduces exactly to \(2xQ_\lambda(x^2)=0\). The endpoint values and the factored discriminant of \(Q_\lambda\) prove that two and only two simple roots remain in \((0,1)\) for all \(|\lambda|<1\); together with \(x=0\) this gives exactly five simple cusp parameters. The packaged symbolic checker independently reconstructs every algebraic identity used in this argument.

## Originality
**PASS.** The closest literature is unusually strong prior art: a later paper uses the concrete support \(31+2\cos(2t)+\sin(5t)\) in a figure displaying its affine equidistants and centre symmetry set, and earlier work gives examples where both relevant curves have five cusps. Those examples are therefore not claimed as new. What was not found in the inspected sources is the all-parameter theorem for every strictly convex support \(R+a\cos(2t)+b\sin(5t)\), the reduction to the explicit cubic \(Q_\lambda\), or the discriminant certificate showing that the five-versus-five equality cannot change anywhere in the full admissible family. Exact-form, alias, and implication searches found no covering statement. Residual risk remains that a similar Fourier-family calculation appears in an unindexed note or exercise.

## Value
**PASS.** The literature proves only a general lower comparison between the cusp numbers and exhibits isolated examples. The present result identifies a natural family already used illustratively in that literature and proves that equality persists on its entire strictly convex parameter chamber. The fact that the centre-symmetry-set cusp locations are independent of the odd-mode amplitude \(b\) after normalization, and that the global count is protected by one explicit cubic discriminant, gives a structural explanation of the published picture rather than a routine recount of one example.
