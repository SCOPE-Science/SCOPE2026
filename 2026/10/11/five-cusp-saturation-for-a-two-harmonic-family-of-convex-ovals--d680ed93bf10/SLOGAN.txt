Every strictly convex oval with support \(p(t)=R+a\cos(2t)+b\sin(5t)\) and \(ab\ne0\) has exactly five Wigner-caustic cusps and exactly five centre-symmetry-set cusps. The centre-symmetry-set count reduces to a cubic whose discriminant never vanishes in the convexity-forced range \(|-3a/R|<1\), so the general cusp inequality is saturated throughout the family.
