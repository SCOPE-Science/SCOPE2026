A globally damped degenerate Liénard oscillator has a universal inverse-square relaxation law whose logarithmic coefficient is \(d/c-b/a+3c/a^2\), except on its exponentially fast strong-stable curve. For cubic–quintic restoring force, the zero-log condition exactly coincides with an algebraic invariant slow graph; the graph's existence itself follows from established Darboux-curve theory.
