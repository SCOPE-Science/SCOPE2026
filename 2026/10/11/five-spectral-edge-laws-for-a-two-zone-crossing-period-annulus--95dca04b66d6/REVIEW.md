# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

For \(a,D,\kappa>0\) and every real \(\tau\), the left excursion is exactly \(v=r u-aW\), with \(u\) the second-order impulse response. Strict concavity of \(\log u\) forces the flight-time parameter \(r(t)\) to increase on its maximal positive interval, and \(v(s)>0\) before the first return. A time-reversed, spatially rescaled left excursion solves the right equation exactly and closes the cycle. Closed forms for the impulse response establish the five independent asymptotic regimes, including both repeated-eigenvalue transitions; dimensionless logarithmic arguments and one-sided ranges are explicit. Finite checker diagnostics support but do not replace the proof.

## Originality — PASS, with publication-priority risk

The 2022/2023 Carmona–Fernández-Sánchez–Novaes characterization already proves existence of annuli satisfying the balanced conditions, and an elementary single-zone affine-focus analysis gives the focus-flight endpoints; these are not claimed as new. The new content is the full real-node spectral edge classification in original time, especially the different defective-node \(\log\log\) and reciprocal laws. The 2026 Liu–Liu–Wang abstract states an upper bound on critical periods, which does not by itself yield the sharp edge expansions. Its full text was inaccessible under authorized routes, however, so equivalence or prior derivation of part of the theorem cannot be eliminated decisively. The 2026 Rezende–Santos–Torregrosa abstract addresses continuous fields and centers at infinity, not this discontinuous-node family.

## Value — PASS

The exact return-map identity alone cannot reveal whether the maximal period is finite, diverges logarithmically, acquires a logarithm-of-logarithm correction, or blows up at a finite crossing speed. These distinct boundaries occur naturally as the subsystem eigenvalues change type. The amplitude-existence threshold, physical-time growth law, and fixed residence fractions give a complete quantitative benchmark for the canonical balanced two-zone annulus; this is more informative than a textbook linear-system period calculation or a small parameter increment.

## Residual risks and limitations

The strongest recent general period-function paper is available only by abstract; its full text might contain related edge formulas. The theorem does not extend to unbalanced discontinuous systems, nontransverse origin solutions, or limits varying multiple parameters jointly. The finite checker is not an infinite proof, and no independent audit has been performed.
