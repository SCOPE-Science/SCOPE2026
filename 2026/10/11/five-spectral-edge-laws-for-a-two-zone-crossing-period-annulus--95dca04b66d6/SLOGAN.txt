For a balanced two-zone planar piecewise-affine center, the actual period grows strictly with crossing speed but exhibits five sharply different spectral edge laws: stable-node logarithmic divergence, a stable double-node \(\log\log\) correction, a finite focus ceiling, an unstable double-node pole, and unstable-node logarithmic divergence at a finite-speed cutoff. The real-node boundary laws and the exact left/right residence-time ratio separate physical-time information from the pre-existing annulus-existence and focus-flight results.
