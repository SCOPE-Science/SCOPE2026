Every compact stationary Genesio state has barycenter \((m,0,0)\) on the segment joining its two equilibria and obeys the sharp law \(\operatorname{Var}(x)=m(c-m)\). At the same time, the nonlinear vertical forcing is calibrated fiberwise by \(\mathbb E[x^2-cx-by\mid z]=az\), with the loss of perfect height coherence exactly equal to mean-square vertical speed.
