# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Variation of constants gives the exact exponential past-memory formula for \(z\). Arbitrary one-coordinate antiderivative tests give the three conditional regressions. Stationarity of \(xy\), together with the heightwise regression, yields
\[
\operatorname{Var}(z)
=
m(b-m)+\mathbb E[x^2].
\]
The residual
\[
y^2-z=\dot z
\]
is conditionally orthogonal to height, giving the exact variance and covariance decomposition. Vanishing of either dynamical defect forces compact complete support trajectories to be equilibria.

Risk: the sharp chord inequality is a useful nonnegative lower envelope only for means in \(0\le m\le b\), although the exact identity itself has no such restriction.

## Originality
PASS. The complete 2013 same-system paper was inspected through its exact equations, equilibria, Hopf analysis, Lyapunov coefficients, and conclusion, with document-wide searches for the principal stationary-statistics aliases. The complete open 2021 same-system article was also compared through its chaotic dynamics, return maps, periodic-orbit extraction, symbolic encoding, and bifurcation analysis. A previously indexed same-system recurrence result was checked at statement level and concerns a different damping/sign balance.

Risk: the generator argument is short, so an equivalent conditional formulation could remain in unindexed convection literature.

## Value
PASS. The theorem links the physically meaningful convective amplitude and mean-temperature variables through an exact conditional law, and then turns the fixed-point height \(b\) into a sharp statistical boundary. The excess above the fixed-point chord is exactly \(\mathbb E[x^2]\), while the additional \(y^2\)-variance excess is exactly vertical-temperature-speed energy. For \(b>0\), every non-equilibrium stationary state must put positive mass above \(z=b\), which is directly relevant to the return sections used in chaotic-orbit studies.
