Every compact stationary Rucklidge state obeys the heightwise convective-energy law \(\mathbb E[y^2\mid z]=z\) and the exact fixed-point chord excess \(\operatorname{Var}(z)=m(b-m)+\mathbb E[x^2]\). For \(b>0\), every non-equilibrium stationary state must place positive invariant mass above the nonzero-equilibrium height \(z=b\), while fixed-point mixtures give the sharp lower variance envelope.
