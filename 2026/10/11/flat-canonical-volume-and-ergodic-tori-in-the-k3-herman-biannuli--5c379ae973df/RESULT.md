# Flat canonical volume and ergodic tori in the K3 Herman biannuli
## Finding
For the projective K3 automorphism constructed in Cao's Theorem B, let \(W_\ell=j_\ell(\mathcal A_r)\), with \(\ell\in\{0,1\}\), be one of the two invariant biannuli. The source gives
\[
T\circ j_\ell=j_\ell\circ R_{(-1)^\ell\alpha},
\]
where \(\alpha\) is Diophantine, and it constructs a nonzero holomorphic two-form \(\Omega\) satisfying \(T^*\Omega=\Omega\).

There is a nonzero constant \(c_\ell\) such that
\[
j_\ell^*\Omega=c_\ell\,\frac{dz_1}{z_1}\wedge\frac{dz_2}{z_2}.
\]
Writing \(z_k=e^{s_k+i\theta_k}\), with \(-r<s_k<r\), this gives
\[
j_\ell^*(\Omega\wedge\overline{\Omega})=4|c_\ell|^2\,ds_1\wedge d\theta_1\wedge ds_2\wedge d\theta_2.
\]
Thus the canonical volume conditioned on \(W_\ell\) is exactly the product of normalized Lebesgue measure on \((-r,r)^2\) in the logarithmic radii and normalized Haar measure on \(\mathbb T^2\) in the angles.

Every ergodic \(T\)-invariant probability measure whose support is contained in \(W_\ell\) is the pushforward by \(j_\ell\) of Haar measure on a unique invariant torus
\[
\{|z_1|=e^{s_1},\ |z_2|=e^{s_2}\},
\qquad (s_1,s_2)\in(-r,r)^2.
\]
Conversely each such Haar measure is ergodic. Every one of these ergodic measures has metric entropy zero and all Lyapunov exponents equal to zero. Consequently the two explicit biannuli make zero contribution to the positive-Lyapunov-exponent integral appearing in the Pesin formula; any positive canonical-volume entropy for this constructed automorphism must arise on the complement of these biannuli.

## Assumptions and scope
The statement concerns only the specific projective K3 surface and automorphism of Cao's Theorem B and the two embedded biannuli supplied there. It uses the source hypotheses that \(\alpha\) is Diophantine, that the displayed conjugacies hold, and that the constructed automorphism preserves the chosen nonzero holomorphic two-form. The invariant-measure classification is for probability measures whose support is contained in one displayed biannulus. No assertion is made about all Fatou components, about the complement of the two biannuli, or about whether the global canonical-volume entropy is positive.

## Proof
Set \(\eta=j_\ell^*\Omega\). Pulling back \(T^*\Omega=\Omega\) through the conjugacy gives \(R_{(-1)^\ell\alpha}^*\eta=\eta\). The Diophantine condition implies that the cyclic subgroup generated by \(\alpha\) is dense in \(\mathbb T^2\): for every nonzero \(k\in\mathbb Z^2\), the character \(e^{i k\cdot\alpha}\) is not \(1\). By continuity, \(\eta\) is therefore invariant under every coordinatewise phase rotation.

Write \(\eta=h(z_1,z_2)\,dz_1\wedge dz_2\). Then \(g(z_1,z_2)=z_1z_2h(z_1,z_2)\) is a holomorphic function on the Reinhardt biannulus \(\mathcal A_r\) invariant under all phase rotations. Its Laurent expansion can contain only its constant coefficient, so \(g=c_\ell\). Since a nonzero holomorphic two-form on a K3 surface has no zeros and \(j_\ell\) is a holomorphic embedding of equal complex dimension, \(c_\ell\neq0\). This proves the logarithmic form of \(j_\ell^*\Omega\).

Now \(dz_k/z_k=ds_k+i\,d\theta_k\). Direct exterior multiplication yields
\[
\left(\frac{dz_1}{z_1}\wedge\frac{dz_2}{z_2}\right)\wedge
\left(\frac{d\overline z_1}{\overline z_1}\wedge\frac{d\overline z_2}{\overline z_2}\right)
=4\,ds_1\wedge d\theta_1\wedge ds_2\wedge d\theta_2.
\]
Normalization therefore gives the claimed product law for canonical volume on \(W_\ell\).

For the ergodic classification, pull an invariant ergodic probability measure on \(W_\ell\) back to \(\mathcal A_r\). The map \(z\mapsto(\log|z_1|,\log|z_2|)\) is invariant under the rotation, so ergodicity forces it to be almost surely constant, say \((s_1,s_2)\). The measure is therefore supported on the corresponding modulus torus. On that torus the map is translation by \(((-1)^\ell\alpha_1,(-1)^\ell\alpha_2)\). The same nonresonance condition implies unique ergodicity of this Kronecker translation, so the measure is Haar. Conversely Haar measure on each fixed-modulus torus is ergodic.

Finally, on any fixed-modulus torus, for every integer \(n\),
\[
D T^n=D j_\ell\big|_{R^n z}\circ D R^n\circ
\left(D j_\ell\big|_z\right)^{-1}.
\]
The middle factor is unitary and the first and last factors are uniformly bounded, together with their inverses, on the compact torus. Hence \(\|DT^n\|\) and \(\|DT^{-n}\|\) are bounded independently of \(n\), so every Lyapunov exponent is zero. The conjugate Kronecker rotation has metric entropy zero. The same derivative argument holds pointwise on every fixed-modulus torus in the biannulus, so the positive Lyapunov exponent vanishes throughout \(W_0\cup W_1\), proving the stated localization of the Pesin integral.

## Verification
The proof uses only the source's explicit conjugacy, its Diophantine nonresonance, preservation of the holomorphic two-form, Laurent uniqueness on a Reinhardt domain, elementary ergodic facts for torus translations, and a bounded-derivative conjugacy calculation. No finite experiment, numerical approximation, or extrapolation is used. The exterior-product coefficient was independently recomputed from \(dz_k/z_k=ds_k+i\,d\theta_k\), giving the factor \(4\).

## Relationship to prior work
Cao constructs the positive-entropy projective K3 automorphism, its two invariant biannuli, their Diophantine rotation conjugacies, and the positive canonical volume of the biannuli, and asks whether the canonical-volume metric entropy is positive. The paper does not state a classification of all ergodic invariant probabilities supported in a biannulus, the exact logarithmic-polar density of canonical volume there, or the resulting zero contribution of those biannuli to the Pesin integral.

Bedford and Kim's earlier rotation-domain theory establishes broad structural facts for conservative surface automorphisms, including torus actions on rotation domains. That general theory does not provide the above Cao-specific logarithmic canonical-volume formula or the explicit ergodic decomposition of the canonical volume on these newly constructed biannuli. The present statement is therefore a local quantitative refinement tied to the explicit 2026 construction rather than a rephrasing of the general rotation-domain theorem.

## Limitations
The result does not decide the source's global canonical-volume entropy question. It shows only that the two displayed positive-volume Herman biannuli are entropy-silent in the Lyapunov/Pesin sense, thereby localizing any possible positive contribution to their complement. An equivalent local formulation may exist as folklore for Diophantine rotation domains; no such formulation was located in the checked literature, so that possibility remains a residual originality risk.
