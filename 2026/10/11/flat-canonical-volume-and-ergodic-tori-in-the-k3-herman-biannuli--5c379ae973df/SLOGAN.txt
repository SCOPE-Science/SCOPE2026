On each explicit K3 Herman biannulus, the preserved holomorphic two-form becomes exactly \(c\,(dz_1/z_1)\wedge(dz_2/z_2)\), so canonical volume is flat in logarithmic radii and angles. Its ergodic components are precisely Haar measures on fixed-modulus Diophantine tori, all with zero entropy and zero Lyapunov exponents; any positive canonical-volume entropy must therefore come from outside the two biannuli.
