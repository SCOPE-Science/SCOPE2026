For a simplex with distinct John-ellipsoid semiaxes, Eydelzon's focal quadrics meet exactly in nonconsecutive index patterns: their nerve is \(\operatorname{Ind}(P_{n-1})\). Hence at most \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\) focal degeneracies occur simultaneously, and the indexed \(r\)-fold intersections are counted by \(\binom{n-r}{r}\).
