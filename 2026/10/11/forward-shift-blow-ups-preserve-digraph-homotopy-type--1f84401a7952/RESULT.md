# Forward-shift blow-ups preserve digraph homotopy type
## Finding
Let \(G\) be a finite loopless digraph. Assume there is a digraph endomorphism \(\tau:G\to G\) satisfying \(v\to\tau(v)\) for every vertex \(v\). Replace each vertex \(v\) by a nonempty independent fiber \(F_v\), and replace each arc \(v\to w\) by all arcs from \(F_v\) to \(F_w\). Write \(B\) for this complete vertex blow-up and \(\pi:B\to G\) for the projection.

Then \(\pi\) is a digraph homotopy equivalence. Hence every homotopy-invariant version of standard path homology takes the same value on \(B\) and \(G\). In particular, for the consistently oriented directed cycle \(\overrightarrow C_n\), \(n\ge3\), every such blow-up satisfies
\[
H_0(B;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z,\qquad H_1(B;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z,\qquad H_k(B;\mathbb Z)=0\quad(k\ge2).
\]

## Assumptions and scope
A digraph map is understood in the standard path-homology sense: an arc may be sent either to an arc or collapsed to a single vertex. Digraph homotopy is the line-digraph homotopy reviewed by McGuirk and Park. The fibers \(F_v\) are nonempty and have no internal arcs; between two fibers there are exactly the complete sets of arcs prescribed by the base digraph.

The forward-shift hypothesis on \(\tau\) is sufficient, not asserted necessary. No conclusion is claimed for arbitrary vertex blow-ups of a digraph lacking such an endomorphism.

## Proof
Choose one representative \(s(v)\in F_v\) for each base vertex. This gives a section \(s:G\to B\) of \(\pi\), so \(\pi s=\operatorname<built-in function id>_G\).

Define \(q:B\to B\) by
\[
q(x)=s\bigl(\tau(\pi(x))\bigr).
\]
Because \(\tau\), \(s\), and \(\pi\) are digraph maps, so is \(q\). If \(x\in F_v\), the hypothesis \(v\to\tau(v)\) and completeness of the blown-up arc imply
\[
x\to q(x).
\]
Thus \(\operatorname<built-in function id>_B\) and \(q\) are related by a one-step forward homotopy. Likewise
\[
s\pi(x)=s(v)\to s(\tau(v))=q(x),
\]
so \(s\pi\) and \(q\) are related by a one-step forward homotopy. Hence \(s\pi\simeq q\simeq\operatorname<built-in function id>_B\). Therefore \(s\) and \(\pi\) are homotopy inverses.

For \(G=\overrightarrow C_n\), take \(\tau(i)=i+1\) modulo \(n\). This is an endomorphism and \(i\to\tau(i)\), so every complete vertex blow-up is homotopy equivalent to \(\overrightarrow C_n\).

It remains only to identify the path homology of the base directed cycle. There are \(n\) vertices and \(n\) allowed directed edges, and the incidence boundary has rank \(n-1\), so \(H_0\cong\mathbb Z\) and the kernel in degree one is generated by the directed cycle sum. For every allowed path of length at least two, deleting its second vertex creates a unique nonallowed skip from \(i\) to \(i+2\); for \(n\ge3\) that skip is neither an allowed edge nor a repeated adjacent vertex. Distinct allowed paths give distinct such skip terms. Consequently no nonzero linear combination of allowed paths of degree at least two has allowed boundary, so \(\Omega_k=0\) for \(k\ge2\). Therefore \(H_1\cong\mathbb Z\) and all higher path homology groups vanish.

## Verification
The accompanying script `verify_forward_shift_blowup.py` checks the proof template exhaustively for every loopless digraph on two and three vertices and every endomorphism satisfying the forward-shift condition. Across these bases it verifies 53 separate endomorphism instances: the projection and chosen section are maps, the projection-section composite is the identity on the base, the auxiliary map \(q\) is a map, and both required one-step homotopies are valid pointwise.

For directed cycles of lengths \(3\) through \(7\), the script additionally constructs nonuniform blow-ups with alternating fiber sizes, forms the degree-one boundary and an explicit basis for \(\Omega_2\), and recomputes the rational ranks. Each sample has Betti numbers \(b_0=b_1=1\). It separately checks the unique-skip argument for base cycles of lengths \(3\) through \(12\). These finite computations corroborate, but are not substituted for, the all-parameter proof above.

## Relationship to prior work
McGuirk and Park review the standard digraph homotopy relation and homotopy equivalence in their 2022 work on digraph cofibers; the inspected homotopy section provides the exact framework used in the proof above. Grigor'yan, Lin, Muranov, and Yau established homotopy invariance of path homology. Those results imply the homology consequence once a homotopy equivalence is supplied, but they do not by themselves give the forward-shift blow-up criterion proved here.

Targeted searches for digraph blow-ups, lexicographic blow-ups, vertex duplication, and path-homology blow-up invariance did not locate a published statement equivalent to this criterion. General graph-theoretic blow-up literature defines the same independent-fiber substitution but does not address digraph homotopy. The closest prior result checked for overlap concerns first path homology of cyclic tournaments; it is neither a blow-up theorem nor a homotopy-equivalence statement and does not imply the present claim.

## Limitations
The result gives a sufficient condition only. It does not classify which digraphs remain homotopy equivalent to all of their complete vertex blow-ups, and it does not assert that a blow-up is homotopy equivalent to its base when no forward endomorphism exists. The cycle corollary concerns the standard consistently oriented cycle with \(n\ge3\); two-cycles and blow-ups with extra intra-fiber arcs are outside the stated scope.
