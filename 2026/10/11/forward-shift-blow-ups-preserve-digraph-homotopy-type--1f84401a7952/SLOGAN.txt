A complete vertex blow-up does not change the digraph homotopy type whenever the base admits a forward endomorphism \(\tau\) with \(v\to\tau(v)\) at every vertex. In particular every nonempty complete layer blow-up of \(\overrightarrow C_n\), \(n\ge3\), has the same integral path homology as the directed cycle: one class in degrees \(0\) and \(1\), and none above.
