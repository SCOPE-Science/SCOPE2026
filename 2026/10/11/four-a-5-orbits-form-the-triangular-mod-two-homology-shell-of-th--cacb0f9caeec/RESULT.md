# Four \(A_5\)-orbits form the triangular mod-two homology shell of the ten-vertex genus-seven surface
## Finding
Let
\[
K={}^{2}10^{7}_{1}
\]
be the ten-vertex vertex-transitive triangulation of the closed nonorientable surface of genus \(7\). Its face vector is
\[
f(K)=(10,45,30),
\]
so its one-skeleton is the complete graph on ten vertices. The full simplicial automorphism group is \(A_5\).

Exact mod-two computation gives
\[
H_1(K;\mathbf F_2)\cong\mathbf F_2^7.
\]
Among the \(120\) three-vertex cliques, \(30\) are triangular faces and \(90\) are nonfacial triangular cycles. Every one of those \(90\) nonfacial triangles is nonzero in \(H_1(K;\mathbf F_2)\). Therefore the simple mod-two homology \(1\)-systole has edge length \(3\), with exactly \(90\) minimizers.

Those \(90\) minimizers represent exactly \(41\) of the \(127\) nonzero mod-two homology classes. Their class multiplicities are
\[
10\text{ classes with multiplicity }1,
\]
\[
25\text{ classes with multiplicity }2,
\]
and
\[
6\text{ classes with multiplicity }5.
\]

Under the full \(A_5\)-action, the minimizers have four orbits of sizes
\[
20,\ 30,\ 30,\ 10.
\]
Their images in homology are four distinct \(A_5\)-orbits of sizes
\[
10,\ 6,\ 15,\ 10,
\]
with constant carrier-to-class fiber sizes
\[
2,\ 5,\ 2,\ 1,
\]
respectively.

## Assumptions and scope
The labelled complex is the public realization of \({}^{2}10^{7}_{1}\), with the thirty triangles embedded verbatim in `verify.py`. The surface is nonorientable of genus \(7\): every edge has degree two, every vertex link is a cycle, and
\[
\chi(K)=10-45+30=-5=2-7.
\]

A simple mod-two homology \(1\)-systole means a simple edge cycle of minimum edge length whose class in \(H_1(K;\mathbf F_2)\) is nonzero. The result concerns simplicial edge length in this fixed triangulation, not a Riemannian metric.

## Proof
Contract the spanning tree consisting of the nine edges joining vertex \(1\) to the other vertices. The graph-cycle space then has dimension
\[
45-10+1=36.
\]
The thirty triangular faces give mod-two boundary relations of rank \(29\). Hence
\[
\dim_{\mathbf F_2}H_1(K;\mathbf F_2)=36-29=7.
\]

Because the one-skeleton is complete, every three-vertex set is a triangular graph cycle. There are \(120\) such triples. The verifier removes the thirty facial triples and reduces each of the remaining ninety cycles modulo the full facial boundary space. No reduced class is zero.

A simple cycle in a simplicial graph has at least three edges. Since nonzero three-edge cycles occur, the simple mod-two homology \(1\)-systole is exactly \(3\). Exhaustive reduction of the ninety nonfacial triangles gives exactly forty-one distinct nonzero homology classes and the stated multiplicity profile.

The verifier independently exhausts every vertex permutation preserving the thirty faces and obtains exactly sixty automorphisms. This group agrees with the group generated by the two published permutations and hence is the published \(A_5\). Acting on the ninety minimizers gives four carrier orbits of sizes \(20,30,30,10\). The induced action on the seven-dimensional homology quotient is computed directly from graph cycles. The four carrier orbits map to four disjoint class-orbits of sizes \(10,6,15,10\), and division of the carrier-orbit sizes by the corresponding class-orbit sizes gives the constant fiber sizes \(2,5,2,1\).

## Verification
The standalone `verify.py` uses only the Python standard library. It reconstructs the complete surface from the thirty triangles, checks the closed-surface links and Euler characteristic, computes the mod-two quotient, exhausts all \(120\) graph triangles, enumerates the full simplicial automorphism group, verifies the published generators, and checks the induced homology action on every minimizer.

Exact successful output:

`VERIFY_OK f=10,45,30 surface=N7 H1_F2_dim=7 graph_triangles=120 facial3=30 empty3=90 all_empty3_nonzero=yes simple_F2_1_systole=3 distinct_triangular_classes=41 class_multiplicity_profile=1x10,2x25,5x6 aut=A5_order60 carrier_orbits=20,30,30,10 class_orbits=10,6,15,10 fibers=2,5,2,1 all_nonzero_class_orbits=15`

No sampling is used in the triangle, automorphism, or homology-orbit counts.

## Relationship to prior work
Altshuler and Brehm classify all neighborly maps with fewer than twelve vertices; the later vertex-transitive census identifies this exact map as \({}^{2}10^{7}_{1}\), records its face vector, its full automorphism group \(A_5\), and a single thirty-face orbit. The same census supplies explicit group generators, while the public manifold data supply the complete labelled triangle list.

Those sources determine the combinatorial surface and its symmetry but do not state the complete shortest mod-two homology shell: the facts that every nonfacial triangle is essential, that exactly forty-one nonzero classes occur, the multiplicity profile, or the four carrier-to-class orbit fibers above.

Focused searches under the exact census notation, the nonorientable genus-seven description, neighborly-map terminology, shortest-homology terminology, and the numerical fingerprints did not locate a covering published statement.

## Limitations
The theorem is specific to mod-two homology and to this labelled ten-vertex triangulation. It does not classify longer simple cycles, integral homology classes, or shortest curves in a geometric metric.

The later exact-object census cross-references older map-coloring and design literature. The earliest day-resolved exact neighborly-map classification verified here is the 1992 Altshuler--Brehm paper; older bibliographic references were not established at comparable day resolution or inspected deeply enough to exclude an unindexed historical computation of the same finite shell. That remains the principal originality risk.
