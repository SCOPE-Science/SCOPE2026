The four-column Gram spectra of quadratic-residue Fourier frames over primes \(p\equiv3\pmod4\) fall into exactly three tournament classes with explicit multiplicities. The exact worst four-column spectral deviation occurs precisely for transitive tournaments when \(p\ge11\), and for strongly connected tournaments when \(p=7\), with asymptotic size \((1+\sqrt2)p^{-1/2}\).
