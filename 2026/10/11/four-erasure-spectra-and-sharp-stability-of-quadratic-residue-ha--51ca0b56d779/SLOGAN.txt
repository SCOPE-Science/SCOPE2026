Four deleted coefficients of a quadratic-residue harmonic Parseval frame have one of three explicit algebraic Gram spectra. The uniquely worst four-erasure geometry is the strongly connected Paley subtournament: its sharp retained lower frame bound is \((p+1-y_p)/(2p)\), where \(y_p\) is the largest root of a stated quartic, and precisely \(p(p-1)(p-3)(p+1)/64\) erasure sets attain it.
