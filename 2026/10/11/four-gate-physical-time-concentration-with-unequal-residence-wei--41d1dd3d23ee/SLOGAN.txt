A contracting cubic oscillator has four slow angular gates whose original-time residence weights alternate between \((\kappa+\eta)/(4\kappa)\) and \((\kappa-\eta)/(4\kappa)\), with standard Cauchy boundary layers. Remarkably its exact total period is \(2\pi\kappa/[\lambda\sqrt{\varepsilon(\varepsilon+\omega)}]\), independent of the parameter that redistributes time among the gates.
