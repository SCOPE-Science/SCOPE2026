In the published sixteen-vertex triangulation of \(\mathcal G_4\), the simple integral homology \(1\)-systole has length \(3\) and \(259\) carriers. Up to orientation, they split as \(112\) pure torsion cycles, \(69\) primitive free cycles, and \(78\) cycles in the torsion-shifted primitive free class; \(37\) other empty triangles are null-homologous.
