The constant and all one-coordinate additive Fourier characters, restricted to determinant-one \(2\)-by-\(2\) matrices over any finite field, form a uniformly well-conditioned Riesz family with exactly four Gram eigenvalues. The sharp upper frame constant is \(q/(q-1)\), its extremizer space has dimension \(2q-3\), and the exact lower constant and every equality space follow from an explicit two-dimensional block.
