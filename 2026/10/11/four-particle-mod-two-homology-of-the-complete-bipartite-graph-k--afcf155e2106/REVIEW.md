# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The reduced Świątkowski model is the standard homology chain complex for unordered configurations, explicitly stated in An [1, §2]. The proof enumerates the full weight-four basis (degrees zero through four) and gives the local differential and a completely executable finite-rank calculation. Every boundary-squared relation is checked exhaustively. The two distinct local reference edge selections agree on all ranks. The alternating sum agrees with the independent graph-configuration Euler series. The result claims only mod-two homology, not an integral computation.

## Originality — PASS, subject to recorded search limitations
An's 2026 primary paper [1] explicitly leaves the lower Betti numbers for four particles unresolved in Question 8.1. Its Theorem 3.10 determines only the top group and torsion one degree below, neither of which determines the degree-two/three mod-two dimensions. Maciążek–Sawicki [2] tabulate four-particle homology for \(K_{4,4}\), but the graph here is \(K_{5,4}\), for which no analogous entry or general covering theorem was found. The indexed search did not yield an equivalent statement. One residual risk is an unindexed computation of these exact mod-two groups; lack of retrieval is not proof that one cannot exist.

## Value — PASS
The graph is the first \(m=4,r=1\) instance of the precise four-particle complete-bipartite family singled out in [1, Question 8.1], and is also the three-particle minimal odd-torsion host described there. The middle-degree dimensions 281 and 353 are substantial, nontrivial inputs for assessing free-rank and 2-primary ambiguities in this concrete test case; the top-homology theorem, previously computed smaller graphs and the Euler characteristic alone do not determine them. The calculation is an exact field-valued benchmark, not a replacement for the unsolved integral and infinite-family questions.
