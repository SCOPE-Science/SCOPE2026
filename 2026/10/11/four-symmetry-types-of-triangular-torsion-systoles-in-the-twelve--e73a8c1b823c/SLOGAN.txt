In the twelve-vertex \(24\)-cell quotient triangulation of \(\mathbb{RP}^3\), all \(64\) nonfacial triangles are shortest representatives of the unique integral torsion loop class. The full order-\(24\) simplicial symmetry group splits them into four orbits of sizes \(4,12,24,24\), with the smallest orbit forming a vertex-partition into four disjoint systoles.
