For the Day–James hexagonal plane \(N(s,t)=\max\{|s|,|t|,|s-t|\}\), the four-term von Neumann–Jordan profile is exactly \(C'''_{\mathrm{NJ}}(\lambda)=1+\frac32m-2m^2\), where \(m=\min\{\lambda,1-\lambda\}\). Its maximum is the off-center value \(41/32\), attained at \(\lambda=3/8\) and \(\lambda=5/8\).
