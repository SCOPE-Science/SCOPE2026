For finite-dimensional complex \(\ell_1^m\), every joint numerical index with \(2\le p<\infty\) and \(k\le m\) attains the universal lower bound \(k^{-1/p}\); real spaces attain it whenever a dyadic Walsh block of size at least \(k\) fits. In contrast, \(n_{(2,3)}(\ell_1^3(\mathbb R))>3^{-1/2}=n_{(2,3)}(\ell_1^3(\mathbb C))\), and dimensions smaller than the tuple length cannot attain the bound over either field.
