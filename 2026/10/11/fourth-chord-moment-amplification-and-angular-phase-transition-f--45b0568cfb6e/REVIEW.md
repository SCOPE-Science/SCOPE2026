## Originality — PASS, with specified residual risks

The July 2026 convex-order result of Tokmachev gives a one-sided *lower* comparison for the moments of boundary versus interior endpoints, not an exact *upper* fourth-moment quotient or its angle-dependent extremizers. Kukushkin's 2026 arbitrary-planar-body result concerns first-power mean distances. A published precise second-moment triangle formula involves a distinct shape family and moment order. Targeted searches for the exact fraction, fourth-moment chord formulations, parallelogram/square aliases, and dominating upper bounds revealed no claim implying the complete result. The complete Tokmachev text was not accessible; its possible examples or appendices, and unindexed older literature, remain priority risks. The negative search outcome is not treated as proof of global uniqueness.

## Value — PASS

The quartic distance moment is the first even chord moment sensitive to nontrivial covariance anisotropy beyond the second-moment comparison. Optimizing over all Euclidean parallelograms yields a sharp, nontrivial finite bound whose only equality shape is a square. The algebraic aspect ratio at which angular monotonicity reverses is a structural classification of the entire two-parameter family, not a one-off numerical estimate. This makes the result a concrete quantitative benchmark for boundary-versus-interior stochastic geometry, without claiming a bound for all convex bodies.
