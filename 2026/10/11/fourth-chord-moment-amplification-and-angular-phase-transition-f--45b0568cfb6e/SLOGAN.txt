For independent Euclidean chords sampled either from area-uniform interior points or arclength-uniform boundary points of any parallelogram, their fourth-moment ratio lies in \(1<\mathcal R_4\le61/17\), with the maximum attained only by squares. At fixed side-length ratio, the ratio's angular monotonicity reverses at one exactly defined algebraic aspect ratio \(r_*\approx1.236326\), where it becomes angle-independent.
