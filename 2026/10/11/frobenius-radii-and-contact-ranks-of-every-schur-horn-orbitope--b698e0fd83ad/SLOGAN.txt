For every nontrivial Schur-Horn orbitope, the intrinsic Frobenius inradius is the minimum normalized majorization slack \((A_k-k\bar a)/\sqrt{k(d-k)/d}\), while the circumradius is the centered spectral variance \(\sqrt{\sum_i(a_i-\bar a)^2}\). The minimizing ranks give exactly the supporting-hyperplane orbits touched by the canonical inball.
