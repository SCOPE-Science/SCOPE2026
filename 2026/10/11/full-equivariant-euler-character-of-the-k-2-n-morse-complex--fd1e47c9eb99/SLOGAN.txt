For every \\(n\\ge3\\), the exact rational equivariant Euler character of the \\(K_{2,n}\\) Morse complex is determined by fixed points and two-cycles of graph automorphisms. Its parity-dependent virtual-module decomposition uses a sign-twisted symmetric square and a sign-twisted exterior-square-plus-two-standards; under the separately reported degree-\\(n\\) homology concentration, this also gives the full actual homology representation.
