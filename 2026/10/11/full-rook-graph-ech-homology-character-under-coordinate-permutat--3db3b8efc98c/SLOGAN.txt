For every \(q\ge3\), the complete rational homology character of the radius-one Čech complex of the rook graph \(K_q\square K_q\) is determined for all coordinate permutations and their interchange. Its \(S_q\times S_q\)-invariant second homology is one-dimensional, but the coordinate interchange negates that line, leaving no invariants under the full automorphism group.
