# Full \(S_2\times S_4\) homology representation of the \(K_{2,4}\) Morse complex

## Finding
Let \(X=\mathcal M(K_{2,4})\) be the ordinary simplicial complex of acyclic discrete vector fields on the complete bipartite graph with parts of sizes two and four. Its full automorphism group is \(S_2\times S_4\). Let \(\varepsilon\) be the nontrivial rational character of \(S_2\), and \(S^\lambda\) the rational Specht module of partition \(\lambda\vdash4\). Then all reduced rational homology is concentrated in degree four and, as an \(S_2\times S_4\)-module,
\[
\widetilde H_4(X;\mathbb Q)\cong (\mathbf1\boxtimes(S^{(3,1)}\oplus2S^{(2,1,1)}))\oplus (\varepsilon\boxtimes(S^{(1,1,1,1)}\oplus S^{(2,1,1)}\oplus S^{(2,2)})).
\]
In particular the \(S_2\)-invariant and anti-invariant dimensions are respectively 9 and 6. The non-equivariant homotopy type \(X\simeq\bigvee^{15}S^4\) is also verified here but is not the novelty asserted.

The trace of \((s,\pi)\in S_2\times S_4\) on degree-four reduced rational homology depends only on \(s\in\{1,\mathrm{swap}\}\) and the cycle type of \(\pi\). In the cycle-type order \((1,1,1,1),(1,1,2),(1,3),(2,2),(4)\), the two character rows are \((15,-3,0,-1,1)\) for \(s=1\), and \((3,1,0,-5,1)\) for \(s=\mathrm{swap}\).

## Assumptions and scope
Let the bipartition be \(A=\{a_0,a_1\}\) and \(B=\{b_0,b_1,b_2,b_3\}\). The graph has exactly the eight edges \(\{a_i,b_j\}\). A vertex of \(X\) is a primitive matching \((v,e)\) between a graph vertex \(v\) and an incident graph edge \(e\). A face is a set of these primitives such that no graph vertex or graph edge appears twice, and directing every chosen graph edge from its chosen vertex to the other endpoint gives no directed cycle. This is the ordinary Morse complex, not the matching complex of graph edges or the generalized Morse complex admitting directed cycles. All homology groups below have rational coefficients unless explicitly stated otherwise. The group \(S_2\times S_4\) permutes the two vertices in \(A\) and the four in \(B\).

## Proof
Each of the eight graph edges has three possible states: unchosen, chosen at the \(A\) endpoint, or chosen at the \(B\) endpoint. Enumerating all \(3^8\) states, rejecting repeated chosen vertices and directed cycles, produces precisely the 944 nonempty simplices with face vector \((16,96,272,368,192)\). Face-toggle matching in the fixed order of the 16 primitive vectors pairs each still-unpaired face without its next primitive with its enlargement, if that enlargement is still available. The augmented computation has 465 matched pairs and 15 unmatched four-simplices; removing the matched empty-face pair leaves 464 genuine pairs, one critical vertex, and 15 critical four-simplices. The associated reversed-cover directed graph has 944 nodes and 3,440 edges and admits a topological ordering; hence this is an acyclic discrete-Morse matching. Forman's theorem therefore gives \(X\simeq\bigvee^15S^4\), so reduced homology vanishes except in degree four, where it has dimension 15.

For any graph automorphism \(g\), each \(g\)-fixed simplex contributes the sign of the induced permutation of its ordered primitive vertices times \((-1)^d\) to the ordinary cellular Lefschetz number, where \(d\) is the simplex dimension. Subtracting the trace 1 in unreduced degree zero gives the reduced Lefschetz number. Since reduced rational homology occurs only in even degree four, this number equals \(\operatorname{tr}(g\mid\widetilde H_4)\). Exhaustive exact enumeration of the signed fixed faces for all 48 group elements produces the two character rows recorded above. Consequently the \(S_2\)-positive and negative character rows on \(S_4\) are respectively \((9,-1,0,-3,1)\) and \((6,-2,0,2,0)\). Comparing against the irreducible \(S_4\) character table gives \(S^(3, 1)\oplus2S^(2, 1, 1)\) and \(S^(1, 1, 1, 1)\oplus S^(2, 1, 1)\oplus S^(2, 2)\), respectively, proving the displayed decomposition. Character theory over \(\mathbb Q\) suffices because all \(S_4\) irreducibles are rational Specht modules.

## Verification
Run `python3 verify.py` (Python standard library only) from the unpacked package. It reconstructs all 944 nonempty simplices from the graph rather than importing a face table; checks the complete face vector, all 3,440 Hasse covers and acyclicity by topological sorting; verifies the 464 matching pairs and remaining critical cells; and enumerates the signed face characters for every one of the 48 automorphisms. It independently compares both eigenspace characters to explicit rational Specht characters and prints `VERIFY_OK` if every assertion holds. No numeric floating-point arithmetic or external dependencies are used.

## Relationship to prior work
Donovan and Scoville, *Star clusters in the Matching, Morse, and Generalized Morse complex* (arXiv:2207.13780, public 2022-07-27, primary MSC 57Q70), study Morse complexes using star clusters and compute path and extended-star families; their results and definitions do not specify this \(S_2\times S_4\)-homology module. A separate publicly recorded complete-bipartite calculation supplies the nonequivariant wedge-of-spheres formula for \(K_{2,n}\), which at \(n=4\) gives the already-known rank 15; neither its nonequivariant homotopy statement nor that dimension determines the irreducible constituents. This result is the explicitly computed full symmetry representation, not a new nonequivariant wedge assertion.

## Limitations
This is a finite exact classification for \(K_{2,4}\), not a formula for all \(K_{2,n}\), a claim of integral equivariant decomposition, or a classification for arbitrary bipartite graphs. An equivalent unindexed equivariant calculation could exist; no independent audit or external proof certification has been performed. The explicit computation is finite and exhaustive for the specified complex and group only.
