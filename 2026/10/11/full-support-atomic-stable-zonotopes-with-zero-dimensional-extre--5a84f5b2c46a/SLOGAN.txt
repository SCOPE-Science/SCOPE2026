A countable stable zonotope may have a directional law supported densely on the entire sphere and uncountably many exposed points, yet its extreme-point set has zero Hausdorff, packing, and upper box dimensions. An exponentially decaying tail of atomic directional masses forces an explicit polylogarithmic covering bound in every dimension \(d\ge2\), contrasting the positive \((d-1)\alpha\) dimension for uniform directions.
