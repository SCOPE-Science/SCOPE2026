For the cuboctahedral graph \(C\), the two-particle space \(\operatorname{UConf}_2(C)\) has torsion-free homology of ranks \(14\) and \(43\) in degrees \(1\) and \(2\). Its full \(S_4\times C_2\) symmetry action splits into explicit irreducible rational modules, refining the classical nonequivariant planar-graph calculation.
