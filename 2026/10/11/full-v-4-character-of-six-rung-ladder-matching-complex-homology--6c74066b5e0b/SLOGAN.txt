For the six-rung ladder \(L_6\), the full automorphism group \(C_2\times C_2\) acts on \(H_3(\mathcal M(L_6);\mathbb Q)\) with character \((5,-1,1,3)\). The two natural reflections therefore have different homological traces, a symmetry refinement invisible in the known nonequivariant wedge \(\bigvee^5S^3\).
