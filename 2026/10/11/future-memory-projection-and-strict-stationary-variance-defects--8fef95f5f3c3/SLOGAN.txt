In the four-dimensional Rössler hyperchaos flow, bounded stationary recurrence turns the linearly expanding channel into the exact future-memory law \(z(t)=-c\int_0^\infty e^{-ds}w(t+s)\,ds\) while simultaneously enforcing \(\mathbb E[w\mid z]=-(d/c)z\) and \(\mathbb E[x\mid w]=-b/w\). The resulting two variance defects are exact squared-speed budgets and vanish only on equilibrium mixtures; at the classical hyperchaotic parameters they are therefore strict for every genuinely recurrent non-equilibrium state.
