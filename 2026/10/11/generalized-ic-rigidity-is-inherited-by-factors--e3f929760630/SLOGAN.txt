For locally compact second-countable amenable group actions, the generalized IC-rigidity of arXiv:2609.36397v1 passes to every compact-metric topological factor. The key compatibility is \(\pi_{\mathcal K}(\mathcal K_G(X))\subseteq\mathcal K_G(Y)\), so the discrete factor theorem for invariant random compacts extends to the non-discrete orbit-segment definition used for flows.
