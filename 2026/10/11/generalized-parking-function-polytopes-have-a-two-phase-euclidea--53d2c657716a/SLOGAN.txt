For the two-parameter parking-function polytope \(\mathfrak X_n(a,b)\), every upper-facet orbit collapses metrically to two competitors: singleton coordinate facets and the full-sum facet. The unique inradius is the smaller of their two explicit values, with a sharp phase transition at \(a-1=b(\sqrt n+1)\) and a corresponding change from \(2n\) to \(n+1\) tangent facets.
