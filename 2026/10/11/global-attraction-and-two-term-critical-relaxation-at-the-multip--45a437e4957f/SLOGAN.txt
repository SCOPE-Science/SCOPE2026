At the multiple-exposure disease-free codimension-two threshold, the Li–Ma planar epidemic system is globally attracted to \(E_0=(A/d,0)\), and every interior orbit has an explicit two-term algebraic relaxation. The physical infection coordinate satisfies \(\dot I=-(\rho^3/A^2)I^3+[\rho^4(d-\rho)/(A^3d)]I^4+O(I^5)\), yielding \(I(t)=A/(\sqrt2\,\rho^{3/2})t^{-1/2}+A(d-\rho)/(2d\rho^2)t^{-1}+O(t^{-3/2}\log t)\).
