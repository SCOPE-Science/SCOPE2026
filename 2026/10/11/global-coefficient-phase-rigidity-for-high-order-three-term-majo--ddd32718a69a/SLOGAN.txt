For the three-frequency polynomial \(1+e^{it}+u e^{i(k+2)t}\), every exponent strictly between \(2k\) and \(2k+2\) with \(k\ge23\) has a unique norm-minimizing coefficient phase \(u=1\) and a unique norm-maximizing phase \(u=-1\). The entire phase response obeys an explicit uniform cosine law with geometric error in \(k\); the threshold 23 is sufficient rather than optimal.
