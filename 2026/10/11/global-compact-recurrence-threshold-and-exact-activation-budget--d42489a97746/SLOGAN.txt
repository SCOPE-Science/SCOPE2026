In the zero-bias Chialvo neuron map, the equilibrium fold \(c_*\) is also a rigorous global boundary: below it no nontrivial compact invariant dynamics exists, and at the boundary compact stationary measures are supported only on the two equilibria. Above it, every compact positive stationary state satisfies the exact activation-deviation budget \(\int [x/x_*-1-\log(x/x_*)]d\mu_+=(c-c_*)/(1-a)\).
