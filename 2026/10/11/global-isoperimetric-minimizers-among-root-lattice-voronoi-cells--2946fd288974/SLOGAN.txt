Among rank-\(d\) root-lattice Voronoi cells, the isoperimetric minimum depends only on \(d\bmod8\): \(\mathcal I_{d,\min}=d\sqrt2\,q_{d\bmod8}^{1/(2d)}\) for \((q_0,\ldots,q_7)=(1,2,3,4,4,4,3,2)\). All equality lattices are classified explicitly by \(E_8\)-sums with one residual \(A\), \(D\), \(E_6\), or \(E_7\) factor.
