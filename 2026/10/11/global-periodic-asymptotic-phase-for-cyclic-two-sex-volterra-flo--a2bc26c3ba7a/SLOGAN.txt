For cyclic sex-symmetric three-type Volterra heredity, every initial pair of populations with full collective type support converges either to the diagonal equilibrium or to one uniquely phased diagonal periodic orbit. An exact exponentially decaying sex difference drives a positive invariant-product limit with a quantified \(e^{-2t}\) tail, and the mean follows its limiting periodic orbit within \(O((1+t)^2e^{-2t})\).
