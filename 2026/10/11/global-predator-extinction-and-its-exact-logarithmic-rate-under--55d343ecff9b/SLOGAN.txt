In the delayed cooperative-hunting predator–prey model, the sharp parameter-uniform handling-time barrier \(h\ge e^{-\beta\tau}\) forces extinction from every positive history, including at equality. The predator's exact logarithmic extinction rate is the unique root \(\rho_*\in(0,1)\) of \(\rho_*+\frac{e^{-\beta\tau}\kappa}{1+h\kappa}e^{\rho_*\tau}=1\), independent of cooperation strength and of the initial history.
