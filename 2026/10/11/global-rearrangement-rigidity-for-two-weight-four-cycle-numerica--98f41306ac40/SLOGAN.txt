For every real or complex \(\ell_p^4\) with \(1<p<\infty\), positive weights \(a>b\) placed twice each on a directed cycle give strictly larger spatial numerical radius when the equal weights are adjacent than when they alternate, regardless of the size of the contrast. The alternating radius has an exact one-variable formula and the strict global gap admits explicit positive certificates, extending the known Hilbert-space arrangement law to all non-Hilbert exponents.
