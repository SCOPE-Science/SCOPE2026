At the saddle-node of the negative-forcing Sprott M flow, the known affine-conjugate global convergence and 1/t law admit an explicit finer statement: every bounded non-exponentially relaxing orbit satisfies -b/(x-b/2)=t+(2/b)log(t)+C+o(1). The (2/b) coefficient is the model's first logarithmic critical correction in original time.
