For the classical parking-function polytope in \(\mathbb R^n\), the unique minimum-volume enclosing ellipsoid has a golden-ratio limiting center and an asymptotic squared axial-to-transverse semiaxis ratio of exactly \(3/5\). Every sequence of contacting vertex layers accumulates only at the all-one layer or at the proportion \((3-\sqrt5)/2\) of non-one entries.
