For every good symmetric polynomial, the minimum enclosing Euclidean sphere of its Newton polytope is determined only by the two endpoint partitions. Its unique center is \(c_*\mathbf1\), where \(c_*\) comes from an \(O(m)\) scan of endpoint midpoints and layer means, and its radius is \(\left(\sum_j\max\{(\alpha_j-c_*)^2,(\beta_j-c_*)^2\}\right)^{1/2}\).
