For the four-dimensional Jafari–Sprott–Molaie plane-equilibrium flow, a single exact quadratic balance proves that \(b\leq-1\) is gradient-like: every bounded forward orbit approaches the equilibrium plane and all compact stationary measures live there. On the other side, every nonconstant periodic orbit, if it exists, must satisfy the strict universal duration barrier \(T>2\pi/\sqrt{1+b}\), which diverges at the boundary.
