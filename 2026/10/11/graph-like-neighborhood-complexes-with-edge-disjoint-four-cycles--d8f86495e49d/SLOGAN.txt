For every connected simple graph whose distinct four-cycles are pairwise edge-disjoint, the Lovász open-neighborhood complex has the homotopy type of a disjoint union of wedges of circles, with each four-cycle removing exactly one independent circle in each sheet of the bipartite double cover. Its exact circle multiplicities are \(e-v+1-k\) per component for bipartite graphs and \(2e-2v+1-2k\) for non-bipartite graphs, where \(v,e,k\) count vertices, edges, and four-cycles.
