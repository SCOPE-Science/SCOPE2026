For any graph on \(n\ge4\) vertices, sampling the full standard Fourier block of \(S_n\) on its edge transpositions has full row rank and an explicit concentration spectrum controlled by the graph's unsigned incidence matrix. On regular graphs this spectrum encodes the adjacency spectrum exactly; for \(n\ge6\) and degree at least three, its sharp sampling condition number depends only on the order and degree, not on graph topology.
