For a regular \(m\)-gon approximated by the convex hull of \(n\) arclength-uniform boundary points, the \(n\to\infty\) rescaled Hausdorff error has a sharp Gumbel transition as \(m\to\infty\), with a necessary \(\frac12\log\log m\) centering. Its limiting mean is \(\frac{\pi}{2m}(\log m+\frac12\log\log m+\frac12\log\pi-\log2+\gamma+o(1))\), sharpening the previously known order of magnitude.
