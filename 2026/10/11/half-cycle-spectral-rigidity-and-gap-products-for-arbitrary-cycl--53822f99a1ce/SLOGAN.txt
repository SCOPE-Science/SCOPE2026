For every Fourier-frequency mask on an even cyclic group, concentration on half the cycle has exactly \(\bigl|\#F_{\mathrm{even}}-\#F_{\mathrm{odd}}\bigr|\) eigenvalues equal to \(\tfrac12\). All other eigenvalues pair around \(\tfrac12\), and the complete product of their distances from that threshold has an exact Cauchy--Vandermonde chord formula.
