On the uniform Boolean cube, concentration onto any strict homogeneous weighted halfspace has exactly \(\binom{n-1}{L}\) half-energy Walsh low-pass modes, regardless of its orientation. Every other concentration eigenvalue occurs in a pair \((\lambda,1-\lambda)\) and remains at least \(\min_x|\sum_i a_ix_i|/(2\sum_i|a_i|)\) away from \(1/2\).
