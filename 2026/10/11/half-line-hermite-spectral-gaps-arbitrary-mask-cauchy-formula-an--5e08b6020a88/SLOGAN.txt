For any finite Hermite-frequency mask, concentration on the positive half-line has exactly \(\big||E|-|O|\big|\) half-energy eigenvalues, and the product of all other spectral gaps is an explicit Cauchy-minor sum. For the first \(2m\) levels this product equals a finite gamma product and decays as \(\sqrt\pi G(1/2)^2m^{-1/4}\).
