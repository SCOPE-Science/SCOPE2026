In the canonical pure-linear several-variable Herglotz--Nevanlinna model, the representing vectors generate exactly \(\widehat{\bigotimes}^{\,n-1}H^2(\mathbb C_+)_{\partial}\), not the full ambient \(L^2(\mathbb R^{n-1})\). Thus for every \(n\ge2\) the construction has an explicit infinite-codimensional vector-cyclic redundancy, while the stronger minimal self-adjoint-relation problem remains open.
