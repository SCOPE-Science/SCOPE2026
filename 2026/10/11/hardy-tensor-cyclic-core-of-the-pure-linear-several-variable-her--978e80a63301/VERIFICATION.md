# Verification

The focal formulas were checked directly in arXiv:2610.04637v1. In the pure-linear Case 3, with only \(b_j=b>0\), all hyperplane summands except \(R_j\) vanish and the surviving vector is the product of \(n-1\) one-variable resolvent kernels. In Theorem 6.3 the corresponding \(h_{\boldsymbol z}\) formula gives \(\prod_{k\ne j}(s_k-\overline{z_k})^{-1}\).

One-variable Cauchy kernels are reproducing kernels for \(H^2(\mathbb C_+)\), so their span is dense in that RKHS; finite tensor products of dense factor sets are dense in the Hilbert tensor product. Properness follows from the standard \(L^2(\mathbb R)\) Hardy decomposition, whose opposite Hardy factor is an infinite-dimensional orthogonal complement. No finite experiment is used as proof.

Literature checks included the focal full text, DOI 10.4153/S0008439522000406, DOI 10.1007/s11785-021-01155-x, eight targeted published-finding corpus queries, and source/alias searches in the current and prior owned ledgers. No covering statement was located. The residual risk is an unindexed equivalent observation under cyclicity terminology.

The verified limit is that this establishes only the exact closed span of the representing vectors; it does not establish a full minimal relation realization.
