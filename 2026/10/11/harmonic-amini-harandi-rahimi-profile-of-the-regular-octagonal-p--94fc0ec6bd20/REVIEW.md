# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The harmonic mean is an admissible member of the defining class. For arbitrary unit \(x,y\) and \(0\le t\le1\), norming functionals for \(x+t y\) and \(x-t y\) reduce the chord sum to one of five exact dual-support pairs. Each pair gives at most \(2+2(\sqrt2-1)t\). Harmonic mean is at most arithmetic mean, and the fixed vertices \((1,\sqrt2-1)\) and \((1-\sqrt2,1)\) make both chord norms exactly \(1+(\sqrt2-1)t\). Hölder duality then gives the full formula and the optimizing scale, including both endpoint exponents.

## Originality
PASS. The complete defining 2019 article was inspected. It introduces the general family, proves the one-parameter reduction, lists minimum/power/geometric specializations, and computes the geometric-mean member on a different hexagonal plane; it contains no harmonic-mean specialization and no regular-octagonal computation. A later full-text application of the family was also checked. Exact-symbol, formula, object-alias, and implication searches found only different regular-octagon objectives. The recent skew extension changes the vector pair and does not dominate the symmetric claim.

## Value
PASS. The finding resolves a canonical classical-mean member across the entire exponent scale rather than at one parameter. Its exact answer is the Hölder-dual norm \(\|(1,\sqrt2-1)\|_q\), with a single pointwise-sharp vertex pair for every exponent. The stronger arithmetic chord envelope explains the formula structurally and provides a reusable geometric fact.

## Closest literature and limitations
Amini–Harandi and Rahimi, *Mediterranean Journal of Mathematics* 16 (2019), article 99, is the defining source and computes a geometric-mean member on the \(\ell_\infty-\ell_1\) hexagon. Dinarvand's 2022 preprint applies the same general family to normal-structure estimates. The theorem here is specific to the harmonic mean and the regular octagonal plane; it does not classify other means or regular polygons.
