For positive contractive \(C_0\)-semigroups on monotonically complete complex Banach lattices with the Fatou property, every imaginary generator eigenspace has dimension no larger than that of any integer harmonic: \(\dim\ker(A-i\beta I)\leq\dim\ker(A-ik\beta I)\). A periodic fixed-space reduction and one irrational sampling time turn a discrete lattice-homomorphism dimension theorem into this semigroup inequality, which may be strict.
