For odd \(N\ge5\), the height-rotation-fixed petal words are exactly the \(\varphi(N)\) unit-step arithmetic permutations; none permits a crossing exchange. Exactly \(\varphi(N)-2\) also forbid deletion and remain in their cyclic class under both standard symmetries, so reducing their petal count requires first adding two petals; this is local move rigidity, not a claim of nonminimal knot presentation.
