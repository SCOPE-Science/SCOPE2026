# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Arbitrary one-coordinate antiderivative tests give
\[
\mathbb E[yz\mid x]=\mu x,
\qquad
\mathbb E[x(z-a)\mid y]=\mu y,
\qquad
\mathbb E[xy\mid z]=1.
\]
The residuals are exactly the coordinate speeds, so conditional orthogonality gives the three variance defects and the complete orthogonality of \(xy-1\) to functions of \(z\). Zero vertical defect forces \(z\) and \(xy\) constant on support trajectories; Poincaré recurrence reduces the remaining motion to a scalar autonomous equation, whose non-equilibrium solutions are monotone and nonrecurrent. This yields the exact equilibrium-support equality case.

Risk: compact support and invariant-probability recurrence are used in the equality classification.

## Originality
PASS. The closest same-object published stationary-law result was inspected in full. It already contains the scalar balances
\[
\int xy\,d\nu=1,
\qquad
\int xyz\,d\nu=\int z\,d\nu,
\]
plus mean-height rigidity, and those facts are excluded from the originality claim. They are two consequences of, but do not imply, the stronger family
\[
\mathbb E[(xy-1)g(z)]=0.
\]
A complete eighteen-page same-object integrability article was also inspected and does not contain an invariant-measure disintegration theorem.

Risk: the generator proof is short and an unindexed equivalent observation remains possible, especially in older dynamo literature.

## Value
PASS. The result resolves the reversal-driving product \(xy\) on every height fiber, rather than only in two global moments. It identifies vertical speed energy exactly with product variance, gives exact height–product decorrelation for every non-equilibrium compact stationary state, and forces nonconstant cycles to cross the surface \(xy=1\) at least twice per period.
