Every compact stationary Rikitake state calibrates the reversal-driving product on every height fiber: \(\mathbb E[xy\mid z]=1\). Away from equilibrium-supported measures, \(z\) and \(xy\) are exactly uncorrelated even though \(\operatorname{Var}(xy)=\mathbb E[\dot z^2]>0\), forcing recurrent states to sample both \(xy<1\) and \(xy>1\).
