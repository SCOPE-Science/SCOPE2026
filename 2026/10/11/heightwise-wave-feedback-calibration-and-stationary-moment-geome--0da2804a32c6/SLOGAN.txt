Every compact stationary Lorenz–Stenflo state calibrates the quadratic wave feedback height by height: \(\mathbb E[xy\mid z]=bz\). Its mean height \(m\) then obeys the exact geometry \(\mathbb E[y^2]+b\operatorname{Var}(z)=bm(r-m)\), while the variance defect in the heightwise regression is exactly the mean-square vertical speed.
