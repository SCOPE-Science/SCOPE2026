# Review of the hexagonal-prism total three-cut calculation

## Correctness — PASS
The definition and indexing match the original total-cut construction in arXiv:2209.13503. An explicit exhaustive program enumerates all 76 independent triples and all 3762 simplices and verifies the complete discrete-Morse matching and acyclicity of all 21576 Hasse incidences. The surviving CW cells consist of one vertex and 22 six-cells, proving the integral wedge-of-spheres homotopy type. All 24 symmetry traces and every exact rational character inner product are checked. The proof is limited to this finite graph; mod-two boundary ranks are an independent corroboration, not an unjustified integral inference.

## Originality — PASS, subject to identified literature risk
The original 2022 total-cut paper covers complete-graph prisms with independence number two and cannot imply a nonempty total three-cut complex for the hexagonal prism. The 2024 grid result concerns rectangular path grids, while later square-attachment theorems do not include cyclic closure along two disjoint edges; other published cases are different graph complexes or different graphs. Targeted searches found no covering theorem, but unindexed or implicitly equivalent results remain a risk. The exact finite object, invariant and implication comparisons are recorded in the structured review.

## Value — PASS
The hexagonal prism is a canonical closed two-row square grid with natural dihedral and layer-exchange symmetries, rather than a parameter chosen to optimize a numeric count. The result supplies both a complete integral homotopy type and a nontrivial full rational symmetry character in a case not covered by the open-strip formulas. Non-shellability follows from the gap between purity dimension eight and homology degree six. The representation distinguishes homology sectors invisible in Betti numbers alone.
