The total three-cut complex of the hexagonal-prism graph \(C_6\square K_2\) is homotopy equivalent to a wedge of \(22\) six-spheres, although it is pure of dimension eight. Its full rational sixth-homology representation is determined under the natural \(D_6\times C_2\) symmetries, with layer-swap eigenspaces of dimensions \(16\) and \(6\).
