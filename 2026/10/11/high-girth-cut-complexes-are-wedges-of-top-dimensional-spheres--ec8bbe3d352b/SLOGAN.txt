If \(G\) has girth at least \(k+2\) and \(N\ge k+4\), its ordinary \(k\)-cut complex is a wedge of exactly \(\binom{N-1}{k-1}-c_k(G)\) spheres of dimension \(N-k-1\). Each connected \(k\)-set has a private codimension-one face in the Alexander dual, forcing an integral homotopy classification even when shellability is unknown.
