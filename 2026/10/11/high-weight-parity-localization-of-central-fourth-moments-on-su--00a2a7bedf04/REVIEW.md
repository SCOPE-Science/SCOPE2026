# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
Normalized irreducible characters are orthonormal, and a direct multiplicity-one Clebsch–Gordan count gives the exact fourth-moment functional for all fixed offsets at sufficiently high weight. The parity-leading quartic is globally maximized by two Cauchy–Schwarz inequalities and a completely solved one-variable quadratic; the bound and compactness argument establish the asserted rate and all limiting maximizers. The bundled executable checks 216 direct fusion identities and rational boundary values. The finite checks support but do not replace the arbitrary-offset proof.

## Originality — PASS (bounded comparison)
The inspected primary papers concern either weighted torus reductions and boundedness of individual characters (Travaglini) or noncentral matrix coefficients of one representation block (Dooley–Gupta). Neither directly determines the sharp central high-weight fixed-packet \(L^4\) asymptotic, its parity-only dependence, or the limiting phase geometry. Published related comparisons involving spherical harmonic norms and Fourier projection norms concern different objects and do not imply the packet asymptotic. Earlier Chebyshev-polynomial and compact-group norm literature remains a priority risk; absence from a search index is not a novelty certificate.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The arbitrary-offset theorem gives a natural sharp asymptotic spectral embedding invariant on a nonabelian compact group and a complete structural classification of limiting extremizers, including a sharp \(3:1\) transition. The exact moment formula provides a reusable finite-weight starting point. This is substantially different from recomputing an individual character norm or optimizing one handpicked frequency packet.
