## Correctness
PASS. The source gives
\[
z_J=\binom{|A|+|C|+1}{2}+|B|.
\]
Writing \(u=|A|+|C|\), \(b=|B|\), and \(s=u+b\), the doubled slack at the uniform point is exactly
\[
s(d-s)+b(s+u-1).
\]
The projected subset normal has squared norm \(s(d-s)/d\), yielding the exact shell formula. Its correction term is nonnegative, while \(s(d-s)\ge d-1\). The singleton tight inequalities give the matching global upper bound, and equality in their sum forces the uniform center uniquely.
