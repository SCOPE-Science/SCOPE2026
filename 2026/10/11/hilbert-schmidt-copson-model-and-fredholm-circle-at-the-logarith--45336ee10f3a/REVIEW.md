# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The three Hilbert–Schmidt comparisons are supported by explicit square-summability estimates. The exponential-half-line resolvent yields the Fredholm index, while weakly null test vectors prove the exact essential norm and non-Fredholm boundary. The origin receives a separate proof on the original sequence space; zero extension alone would not justify it.

**Originality — PASS, with uncertainty.** Norrbo's critical weighted map is the direct starting point, but its Theorem 3.4 proves boundedness rather than this quantitative Calkin invariant or normalized Fredholm classification. The two closely related essential-norm papers work with Bergman endomorphisms or different radial spaces. Searches for the exact logarithmic Hilbert/Copson equivalence and the precise normalized spectrum identified no covering result. This is not proof that unpublished or unindexed work lacks the theorem; a general result on asymptotic weighted Hankel matrices could imply it.

**Value — PASS.** The critical endpoint had boundedness but no determined invariant in the examined sources. The exact essential norm and winding-index boundary distinguish compactness obstruction from Fredholm behavior, and the explicit Hilbert–Schmidt equivalence explains the mechanism rather than giving a numerical accident.

**Scope and risk.** The value \(2\) depends on the exact coefficient norms. The circle belongs to the explicitly normalized endomorphism and is not an invariant of the map between different Hilbert spaces without a chosen identification. Prior literature may contain an equivalent result under Wiener–Hopf or Mellin-transform terminology.
