# Review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The proof fixes the generator convention from the primary source: three fixed points on the genus-\(19\) fiber have tangent multiplier \(\omega^2\), and the quotient has genus \(6\). Invariant holomorphic forms therefore have dimension \(6\). Holomorphic Lefschetz gives trace \(\omega^2\) on \(H^{1,0}\), forcing multiplicities \(6,6,7\). Complex conjugation then gives homology multiplicities \(12,13,13\). The resulting homological trace \(-1\) independently matches the source's ordinary Lefschetz computation. No finite experiment is used to infer an infinite statement.

## Originality
**PASS.** Full-text inspection of arXiv:2610.01467v1 found the fixed-point data, quotient genus, and the scalar trace \(-1\), but no statement of the full homology characteristic polynomial, no \(H^{1,0}\) eigenspace decomposition, and no Prym Hodge signature. The older arXiv:1412.4137 establishes the genus and fixed-point geometry but likewise does not state this character calculation. Targeted searches for the characteristic polynomial, the \(6,6,7\) Hodge decomposition, the Prym interpretation, and generic genus-\(19\) triple-cover formulations found no equivalent claim. A related recent result about Cartwright–Steger critical values concerns arithmetic on the elliptic base, not the fiber homology representation.

## Value
**PASS.** The \(6,6,7\) Hodge character is a natural invariant of the newly studied order-three symmetry, not an arbitrary parameter slice. It identifies the entire rational \(C_3\)-module on first homology and the dimension/signature data of the Prym part. These data constrain equivariant homology and Hodge-theoretic analyses of the monodromy problem that the source leaves open, and are strictly stronger than the source's single trace value.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is Akhmedov–Yeung, arXiv:2610.01467v1, because it supplies exactly the rotation data and quotient genus used here. Cartwright–Koziarz–Yeung, arXiv:1412.4137, is the geometric predecessor. The result does not determine the integral module structure or Prym polarization. Residual originality risk remains that a general cyclic-cover reference may encode the same eigenspace dimensions without mentioning the Cartwright–Steger fiber explicitly.
