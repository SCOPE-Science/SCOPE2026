For the invariant genus-\(19\) Cartwright–Steger fiber, the order-three symmetry has \(H^{1,0}\)-multiplicities \(6,6,7\) for \(1,\omega,\omega^2\). Hence its rational homology characteristic polynomial is \((t-1)^{12}(t^2+t+1)^{13}\), and the nontrivial Prym part has Eisenstein Hodge multiplicities \(6\) and \(7\).
