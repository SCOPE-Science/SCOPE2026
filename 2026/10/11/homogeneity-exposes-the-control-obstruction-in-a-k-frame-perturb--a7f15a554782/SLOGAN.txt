In the 2018 controlled \(K\)-Bessel perturbation theorem, scaling the coefficients shows that the nonhomogeneous \(\alpha\|c\|\,\|T_Fc\|\) term is completely redundant and yields the sharp ordinary Bessel bound \(B_G\le((\sqrt{B_F}+\gamma)/(1-\beta))^2\). The published controlled conclusion is nevertheless false without the exact compatibility \(CS_G=S_GC\), as a two-dimensional example shows.
