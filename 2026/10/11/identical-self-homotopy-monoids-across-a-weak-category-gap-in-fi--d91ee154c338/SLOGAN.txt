For every \(m\ge2\), the weakly contractible finite space \(P(9)\oplus D_m\) and the finite graph model \(D_2\oplus D_m\) have isomorphic full self-homotopy endomorphism monoids of size \(1+2(m^m-m)\), yet their unreduced weak LS-categories are \(1\) and \(2\). The common monoid remembers neither weak contractibility nor the unbounded first-homology rank \(m-1\).
