Deleting any sufficiently low-degree bipartite forbidden graph \(H\) from a complete bipartite graph makes the inclusive scale-two graph-metric Rips complex **exactly** \(\operatorname{Ind}(H)\), with no surviving degree-one bars from scale one. When \(H\) is a spanning union of even cycles, this gives a complete three-scale persistence barcode and a mod-three law for the dimension and multiplicity of its sphere wedge.
