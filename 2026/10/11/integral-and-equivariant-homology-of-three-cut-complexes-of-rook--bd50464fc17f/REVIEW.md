# Same-model scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The all-parameter geometric classification of the Alexander dual's maximal faces follows by choosing any nonadjacent pair of vertices: additional vertices must be the two other corners of its rectangle. The relative pair of the union of row/column simplices and all rectangle tetrahedra has a canonical degree-two boundary map with domain the tensor product of exterior-square permutation lattices, target the tensor product of augmentation lattices, and image generated by every elementary four-cycle. The long exact sequence, integral Alexander duality with ambient orientation twist, and universal coefficient theorem prove exactly the stated integral module, degree, rank, and rational character. The positive below-top homology obstructs pure shellability. The independent brute-force verifier reconstructs the complexes and matches 192 complete group-action traces for three small graph sizes; it is corroboration, not an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS, with literature risk.** The 2023 primary cut-complex paper proves statements for clique prisms with one factor of order two, while its complete-multipartite family is not a rook graph. The 2024 follow-up treats Cartesian products of paths (rectangular grid graphs), not products of complete graphs of both orders at least three. Neither inspected full text includes this integral-coordinate-action theorem. Searches for rook/Hamming graph, line graph of a complete bipartite graph, Alexander dual of connected triple hypergraph, and relevant ordinary versus total cut-complex synonyms did not yield a stronger or equivalent statement. An unindexed algebraic or equivariant translation remains a priority risk.

**Value — PASS.** This is a natural all-parameter classification on the line graphs of complete bipartite graphs, not a single low-dimensional experiment: it gives the complete free integral homology and rational group character, provides a computable integral lattice presentation, and rules out shellability for every rook graph with both factors of order at least three. The earlier prism case and the nonequivariant path-grid computations do not imply the new family.
