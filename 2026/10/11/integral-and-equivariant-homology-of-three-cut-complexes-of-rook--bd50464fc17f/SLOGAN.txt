For every \(m,n\ge3\), the ordinary three-cut complex of the rook graph \(K_m\mathbin{\square}K_n\) has free reduced integral homology of rank \(\binom m2\binom n2-(m-1)(n-1)\) only in degree \(mn-5\), and is therefore not shellable. Its full coordinate-permutation homology action is the orientation-twisted integer dual of an explicit rectangle-boundary kernel, yielding a closed character formula for \(S_m\times S_n\).
