For every connected simple cactus graph, allowing two labeled particles to collide exactly at vertices gives torsion-free homology with ranks \(1,E+b,b(b-1)\) in degrees \(0,1,2\), where \(E\) is the edge count and \(b\) the cycle rank. Label exchange splits rational first homology into eigenspaces of dimensions \(b,E\), and second homology into two equal eigenspaces of dimension \(\binom b2\).
