Two twelve-vertex projective-plane connected sums have the same face vector and the same \(33,33,6\) mod-two shortest \(H_2\)-spectrum, yet their integral lifts differ. The \(72\) primitive tetrahedral carriers split \(33,33,3,3\) in \(\mathbb{CP}^{2}\#\mathbb{CP}^{2}\) but \(33,33,6\) in \(\mathbb{CP}^{2}\#-\mathbb{CP}^{2}\).
