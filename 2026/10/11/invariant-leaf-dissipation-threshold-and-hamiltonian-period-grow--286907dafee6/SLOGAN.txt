Removing the \(b\)-coupling in the Jafari–Sprott LE2 flow exposes an exact polynomial foliation: on \(y>-1\), its nonpositive-coupling leaves are globally attracted to equilibrium except at a Hamiltonian threshold. At that threshold all positive-energy leaves are periodic with \(T(h)\sim2\sqrt{2h}\), while below \(y=-1\) the same system has finite-time escape.
