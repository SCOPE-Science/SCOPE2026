For every centrally symmetric polygon tangent to a circle, the complete uniform-direction width distribution recovers exactly the inradius and the unordered set of tangent-normal angle gaps. It determines a quadrilateral or hexagon uniquely up to congruence but admits exactly \((m-1)!/2\) noncongruent \(2m\)-gons whenever \(m\ge4\) and all half-cycle gaps are distinct.
