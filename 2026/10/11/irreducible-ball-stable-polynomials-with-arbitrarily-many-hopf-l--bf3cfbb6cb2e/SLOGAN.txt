In \(\mathbb C^3\), irreducible polynomials that avoid zeros in the open ball can have arbitrarily many separate boundary-zero circles, all smooth and pairwise Hopf-linked in an equatorial \(S^3\). The explicit family \(1-(z_1^2+z_2^2)^m-\varepsilon z_3^2\), for \(m\ge1\) and \(0<\varepsilon<1\), realizes exactly \(m\) such circles.
