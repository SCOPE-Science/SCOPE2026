A centered \(d\)-dimensional isocanted cube has exactly \(d\) Euclidean vertex shells with binomial multiplicities, but its circumradius stays fixed at \(\ell\sqrt d/2\) as the cant varies. Its inball changes regime sharply at \(a=\ell(1-1/\sqrt2)\): beyond that threshold the Chebyshev center expands from one point to a diagonal segment.
