A positive-coefficient analytic disk function makes the two-logarithm Wiman–Valiron endpoint fail at every radius sufficiently near the boundary: the quotient by \(A(r)\sqrt{\log A(r)}\log_2 A(r)\) diverges. This shows that the \(\delta>0\) hypothesis cannot simply be dropped at the first iterated-logarithm level in the 2026 finite-logarithmic-measure theorem.
