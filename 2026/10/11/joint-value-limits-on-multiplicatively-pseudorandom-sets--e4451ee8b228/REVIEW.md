# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reduces every compact-group character to a scalar unimodular completely multiplicative function, exactly within the hypotheses of the focal paper's Definition 1.2 and Lemma 2.1. The auxiliary measures therefore have the Haar Fourier coefficients in the same iterated limit used in the paper's Theorem 6.1. Standard compact-abelian harmonic analysis promotes this to convergence against continuous test functions, and the source's tail estimate (6.3) guarantees arbitrarily large hits rather than merely mass from a finite initial segment.

Risk: the proof is qualitative and depends on compact abelian harmonic analysis; no quantitative hitting rate is asserted.

## Originality
PASS. The closest inspected statement is the focal paper itself. Its Theorem 6.1 is scalar, while Corollary 1.2 only gives finite simultaneous approximation to the identity. The new theorem identifies the full joint compatible subgroup. Searches also checked compact-group, vector-valued, tuple, Piatetski--Shapiro, nil-Bohr, and joint-limit aliases; no implication-equivalent statement was located. Related arXiv:2608.27418 is scalar, and arXiv:2608.26814 concerns shifted correlations.

Risk: an older unindexed compact-group formulation or a later revision of the very recent focal preprint could cover the statement.

## Value
PASS. Knowing all scalar coordinate limit sets does not determine the joint one: relations among coordinates can force a proper subgroup of \(\mathbb T^r\). Identifying exactly that subgroup is a natural structural strengthening and gives a usable criterion for when arbitrary simultaneous targets occur on the new recurrent families.

Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
