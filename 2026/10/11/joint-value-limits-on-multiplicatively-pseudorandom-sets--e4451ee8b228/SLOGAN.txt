For any multiplicatively pseudorandom set, a compact-abelian-valued completely multiplicative map visits every neighborhood of every point in the closed subgroup generated by its values at arbitrarily large indices. Hence a finite tuple of unimodular completely multiplicative functions has exactly its algebraically compatible subgroup as its joint limit set, with the same conclusion on the recent Piatetski--Shapiro and regular irrational nil-Bohr families.
