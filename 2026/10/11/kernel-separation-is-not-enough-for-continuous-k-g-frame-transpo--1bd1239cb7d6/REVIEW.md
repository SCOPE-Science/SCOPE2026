PASS. The block operator is a direct sum of rank-one norm-one matrices. Its range is the closed first-coordinate subspace, while \(R(U^*)\) has the orthonormal basis \(v_n=\lambda_ne_n+a_nh_n\). The kernel intersection is exactly zero because every \(\lambda_n>0\). With \(K=U\) and the one-point analysis operator \(\Lambda=K^*\), the original family is Parseval relative to \(K\). The transformed lower ratio on \(e_n\) is exactly \(\lambda_n^2\to0\), so no positive lower bound exists. The repaired universal criterion is proved in both directions and the stronger inclusion \(R(K^*)\subseteq R(U)\) correctly recovers the source's pseudoinverse lower bound.
