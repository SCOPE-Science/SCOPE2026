For fixed Schwarz-map order \(m\) and power \(p\), the sharp powered Bohr–Rogosinski radius approaches \(1\) with the exact boundary layer \(N(1-R_{m,N}^{(p)})=2W(N/\sqrt{pm})+o(1)\). Equivalently, its deficit is \((2\log N-2\log\log N-\log(pm)+o(1))/N\), sharpening the previously qualitative large-tail convergence.
