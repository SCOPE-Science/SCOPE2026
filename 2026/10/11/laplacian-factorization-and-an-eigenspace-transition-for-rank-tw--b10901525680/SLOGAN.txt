The basis-exchange graphs of rank-two matroid polytopes with one parallel class of size \(p\) and \(q\) singleton classes admit a complete all-parameter Laplacian factorization and an explicitly evaluated spanning-tree count. Their algebraic connectivity changes eigenspace type sharply at \(q=p(p-1)\), with multiplicity jumping from \(q-1\) to \(p-1\) through a coincident eigenspace of dimension \(p+q-2\).
