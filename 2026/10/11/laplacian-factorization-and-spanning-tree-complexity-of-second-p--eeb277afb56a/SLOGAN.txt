The second partial permutohedron \(\mathcal P(m,2)\) has a one-skeleton with a four-factor Laplacian characteristic polynomial, despite having \(m^2+1\) vertices. Its exact spanning-tree count is \(m^{m(m-3)/2}(m+2)^{(m-1)(m-2)/2}(m^2+2m+2)^{m-1}\) for every integer \(m\ge3\), and its algebraic connectivity tends to \(1\).
