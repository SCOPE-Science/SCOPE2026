The one-skeleton of the symmetric edge polytope of \(K_{p,q}\) is a signed antipodal two-cover of \(K_{pq}\), with an explicit integral Laplacian spectrum and exact spanning-tree complexity. Its spectral gap is \(pq-2\max\{p,q\}+2\), revealing a Fiedler-space symmetry enhancement at \(p=q\) and a constant gap when \(\min\{p,q\}=2\).
