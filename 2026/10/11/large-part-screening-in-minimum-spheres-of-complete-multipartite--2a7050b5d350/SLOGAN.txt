The minimum enclosing Euclidean sphere of a complete multipartite stable-set polytope is determined by a rational threshold \(h\) satisfying \(\sum_i(1-h/a_i)_+=2\), with full-class contacts exactly when \(a_i\ge h\). Adjoining arbitrarily many new vertex classes no larger than \(h\) leaves the sphere radius and all old center coordinates unchanged.
