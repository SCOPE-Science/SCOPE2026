For every Latin hypercube with \(q\) symbols and \(n\ge3\) coordinates, all face-wise assignment restrictions are surjective—even uniquely extensible from \(n-1\) coordinates—yet the associated sheaf is not flabby. Its simplicial inflation is a wedge of exactly \((q-1)(q^{n-1}-(q-1)^{n-1})\) spheres of dimension \(n-2\), revealing a uniform topological obstruction to replacing global flabbiness by local lifting.
