At the higher-dimensional Monge–Ampère critical power \((-\log|f|)^{1/n}\), each successive iterated logarithm creates a new exact repair threshold: membership is decided lexicographically against \((1/n,\ldots,1/n)\). All these singularities still lie in \(W^{1,2}_{\mathrm{loc}}\) with \(L^1\) Laplacian and all fail \(W^{2,1}_{\mathrm{loc}}\), isolating a genuinely pluripotential hierarchy invisible to the Sobolev tests.
