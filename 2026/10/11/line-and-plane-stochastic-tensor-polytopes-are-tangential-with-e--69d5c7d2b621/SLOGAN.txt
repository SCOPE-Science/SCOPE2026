The standard order-\(3\) line- and plane-stochastic tensor polytopes are intrinsically tangential: every coordinate facet touches one common Frobenius ball. The unique radii are \(\sqrt n/(n-1)^{3/2}\) and \(1/\sqrt{n(n^3-3n+2)}\), respectively, and the common projected-facet-normal square is simply affine dimension divided by \(n^3\).
