Every common orthogonal four-character spectrum for a family of four-point sets in \(\mathbb F_2^d\) exists alongside an affine-plane spectrum, and the total number is exactly \(2^{d+h-2}T\), where \(T\) counts common admissible frequency planes and \(h\) is the joint annihilator dimension. Three explicit four-point planes in \(\mathbb F_2^3\) have common spectra pairwise, but no common spectrum jointly.
