For any smooth link with \(n\) components, the full integral Betti polynomial of the space of \(k\) ordered free points in its three-dimensional complement is \(\prod_{j=0}^{k-1}(1+nt+(n+j)t^2)\), independent of knotting or linking. Yet configurations in the unknot and trefoil complements have nonisomorphic fundamental groups for every \(k\ge1\).
