# Same-model review

## Correctness

PASS. The first primary theorem states that, for fixed \(n\), the embedded constant-\(S\), constant-\(f_3\) value set is finite in every bounded interval. Therefore its subset in
\[
(2n,2n+1]
\]
has a positive minimum distance from \(2n\), after adjoining \(1\) if the subset is empty. The second primary theorem states for \(n\ge5\) that
\[
S>n\Longrightarrow S\ge2n
\]
and classifies equality exactly as the minimal Cartan \(g=3\) images, which occur in dimensions \(6,12,24\). These statements combine directly to give the claimed \(\delta_n>0\).

## Originality

PASS. The local-finiteness paper does not locate the spectrum near \(2n\). The later second-gap paper cites that theorem but states only the sharp lower endpoint and, outside the Cartan dimensions, the strict conclusion
\[
S>2n.
\]
Its inspected full text does not state the stronger uniform separation
\[
S\ge2n+\delta_n.
\]
Targeted source-ID, spectral-isolation, post-Cartan, and \(\varepsilon_n\) searches found no covering result.

Residual risk: the deduction is concise and may be regarded as implicit in the juxtaposition of the two recent theorems even though no explicit statement was located.

## Value

PASS. The admissible \(S\)-values are the central spectrum in Chern's conjecture. The sharp second-gap theorem identifies the Cartan level \(2n\); local finiteness then shows that this model level is genuinely isolated from above, uniformly over topology and the constant value of \(f_3\). This rules out sequences of embedded constant-cubic-trace examples with curvature values descending to the Cartan endpoint and supplies a natural next-gap statement, although without an explicit numerical constant.

Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.

## Closest literature and limitations

The closest sources are Tan--Tang--Xie--Yan, arXiv:2609.03711v1, for local finiteness, and Ge--Tan--Yan--Zhang, arXiv:2609.27661v1, for the sharp second gap and Cartan endpoint classification.

The new gap constant is existential and dimension-dependent. No explicit lower bound, next admissible curvature level, or immersed analogue is claimed.
