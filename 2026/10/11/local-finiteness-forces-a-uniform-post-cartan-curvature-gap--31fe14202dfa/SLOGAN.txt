For every \(n\ge5\), local finiteness of the embedded constant-cubic-trace curvature spectrum turns the sharp second gap into a genuine post-Cartan gap: there is \(\delta_n>0\) such that \(S>n\) forces either \(S=2n\) or \(S\ge2n+\delta_n\). The level \(S=2n\) occurs only for the Cartan hypersurfaces in dimensions \(6,12,24\).
