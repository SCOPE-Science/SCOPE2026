# Same-model review

## Correctness
**PASS.** For each \(x\in E\), the positive orbit \(T^k|x|\) generates a norm-closed ideal \(F_x\). The explicit series defining \(e_x\) converges in norm and dominates every orbit generator up to a scalar, making \(e_x\) a quasi-interior point of \(F_x\). Positivity proves \(T(F_x)\subseteq F_x\); closedness plus ambient mean ergodicity gives \(P(F_x)\subseteq F_x\); and an ideal of an order-continuous Banach lattice inherits order-continuity of the norm. Dobrick's weak-unit individual ergodic theorem therefore applies in \(F_x\), after which regular-sublattice invariance transfers uo-convergence to \(E\). The proof treats arbitrary \(x\), including \(x=0\), and uses no finite experiment.

## Originality
**PASS.** The focal source assumes a weak unit in the ambient lattice. The present statement removes that global hypothesis by a vector-dependent invariant-ideal localization. Searches for equivalent formulations, stronger coverage, weak-unit removal, orbit-ideal localization, and regular-sublattice variants found no covering published finding. The closest relevant older source, Gao--Troitsky--Xanthos, supplies only the regular-sublattice transfer theorem. Residual risk remains that an older positive-operator paper contains the same localization observation under different terminology.

## Value
**PASS.** Removing the global weak-unit hypothesis is mathematically substantive: it extends the individual ergodic conclusion to arbitrary order-continuous Banach lattices and isolates a reusable mechanism showing that each individual orbit already lives in a suitable weak-unit environment. This is structural rather than a routine special case or numerical refinement.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is Alexander Dobrick, *Banach's principle in vector lattices*, arXiv:2608.14272v1, which proves the weak-unit version of the positive mean-ergodic uo-convergence theorem. Niushan Gao, Vladimir G. Troitsky, and Foivos Xanthos, arXiv:1509.07914v1, provide the regular-sublattice invariance of uo-convergence used for the final transfer. The result still requires positivity, power boundedness, mean ergodicity, and order continuity of the norm; it does not assert order convergence in the original lattice.
