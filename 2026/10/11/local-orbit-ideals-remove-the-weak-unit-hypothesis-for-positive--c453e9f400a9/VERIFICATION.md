# Verification

The proof was reconstructed from its definitions and hypotheses. For a fixed \(x\), the series
\[
e_x=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}2^{-k-1}\frac{T^k|x|}{1+\|T^k|x|\|}
\]
converges in norm and satisfies
\[
T^k|x|\le 2^{k+1}(1+\|T^k|x|\|)e_x.
\]
Consequently the principal ideal generated by \(e_x\) is dense in the closed ideal generated by the positive orbit. Positivity verifies invariance of this ideal under \(T\). Mean ergodicity restricts because every Cesàro average remains in the closed ideal and its ambient norm limit therefore lies there. The restricted lattice has order-continuous norm.

The local application uses the weak-unit individual ergodic theorem from arXiv:2608.14272v1. The return to the ambient lattice uses the theorem from arXiv:1509.07914v1 that uo-convergence is stable under passage to and from regular sublattices, together with the fact that ideals are regular.

No computational certificate is needed. No finite sampling, truncation, or numerical experiment is used as evidence for the infinite statement. The proof does not establish order convergence inside the ambient lattice, and it does not treat nonpositive operators.
