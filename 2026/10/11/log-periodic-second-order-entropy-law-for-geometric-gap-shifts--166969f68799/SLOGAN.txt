For geometric \(S\)-gap shifts with return distances \(1,b,b^2,\ldots\), the maximum invariant entropy at marker density \(t\downarrow0\) is \(t\log\log_b(1/t)\) to first order, with an explicit **nonconstant log-periodic second-order correction**. The correction has a precisely characterized nondegenerate interval of subsequential values for every integer \(b\ge2\).
