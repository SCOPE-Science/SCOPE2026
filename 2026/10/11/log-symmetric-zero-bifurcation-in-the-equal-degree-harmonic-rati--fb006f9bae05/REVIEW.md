# Review of Log-symmetric zero bifurcation in the equal-degree harmonic rational family

## Correctness

PASS. The primary theorem reduces all possible radii in the equal-degree case to the two positive roots of
\[
x^2-Ax+|a|^2=0,
\qquad
x=|z|^{2n}.
\]
Inside the zero-existence region,
\[
A\ge2|a|.
\]
Writing
\[
A=2|a|\cosh\eta
\]
therefore gives exactly
\[
x_\pm=|a|e^{\pm\eta}.
\]
Taking positive \(2n\)th roots proves the radius formula and the product invariant.

The exact identity
\[
\sinh\eta=\sqrt{\Delta}/(2|a|)
\]
proves the logarithmic-gap formula. The source phase equation shows that each fixed admissible radius carries the \(n\)th roots of one unimodular number, hence a regular \(n\)-gon. The source itself identifies the discriminant-zero case as \(n\) double zeros. The square-root law is the Taylor expansion of the exact formula.

## Originality

PASS. The full open-access 2026 source was inspected through Theorem 3.1 and its proof. It gives the coefficient parabola, the two quadratic radial roots, and the count trichotomy, but it does not state the product invariant, the logarithmic normal form, the exact arsinh separation, or the square-root radial bifurcation.

The preceding pole-family paper provides zero-count context but does not state this equal-degree radial geometry. The earlier trinomial zero-location paper concerns the inclusion-sector machinery from which the 2026 paper departs when it treats the limiting equal-degree rational case.

Targeted published-finding and literature searches for geometric-mean radii, reciprocal/logarithmic symmetry, discriminant splitting, and square-root coalescence found no implication-equivalent statement.

## Value

PASS. The equal-degree case is the source's distinguished limiting regime because it becomes completely solvable, and the boundary of its coefficient parabola is exactly where the zero count changes from \(2n\) distinct zeros to \(n\) double zeros.

The new normal form identifies the geometry of that transition rather than merely restating the quadratic formula: the two zero rings are reciprocal in logarithmic radius about one invariant circle, their exact separation is an arsinh of the discriminant, and the coalescence has a universal square-root exponent. This gives a compact bifurcation law for a natural solvable harmonic-rational model.
