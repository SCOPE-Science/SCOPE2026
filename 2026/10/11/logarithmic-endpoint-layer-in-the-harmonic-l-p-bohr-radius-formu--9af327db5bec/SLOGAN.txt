The harmonic \(L^p\) Bohr-radius formula is strictly increasing in the boundary exponent, from \(1/3\) at \(p=1\) to \(\pi/(\pi+4)\) at \(p=\infty\). Its lower endpoint has a logarithmic boundary layer: the first gain above \(1/3\) is \((p-1)\log(1/(p-1))/9\), while the upper endpoint is approached at inverse-linear speed.
