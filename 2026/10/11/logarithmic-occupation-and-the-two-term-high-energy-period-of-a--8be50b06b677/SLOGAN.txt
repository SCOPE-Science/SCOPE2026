For a natural two-parameter cubic global center, the original-time high-energy period has a sharp logarithmic correction with a discriminant-dependent constant: \(\sqrt{kE/2}\,T(E)-\tfrac12\log(4E/k)\to\log(8k^2/(k-a^2))\). Unlike a classical large-amplitude sinusoidal oscillator, its physical-time position has a uniform distribution on logarithmic distance scales, with equal weight on positive and negative positions.
