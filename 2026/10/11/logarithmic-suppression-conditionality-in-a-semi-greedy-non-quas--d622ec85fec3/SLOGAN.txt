For Berná's semi-greedy but non-quasi-greedy Markushevich basis, the suppression conditionality constants satisfy \(k_m(\theta)\asymp_\theta\log(m+1)\), with explicit bounds \(\max\{1,(1+\theta\log m)/2\}\le k_m(\theta)\le1+\theta(\log m+1/e)\). Thus the counterexample has only logarithmic coordinate-projection growth despite the failure of quasi-greediness.
