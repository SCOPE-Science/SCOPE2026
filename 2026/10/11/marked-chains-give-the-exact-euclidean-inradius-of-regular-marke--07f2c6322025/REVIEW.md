# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Pegel's facet theorem makes every cover relation an actual facet for a regular marked poset. Facets incident with a marked endpoint have unit normal, while an unmarked cover has normal length \(\sqrt2\). Summing shifted clearances along a marked interval chain gives the upper bound. A longest-path potential construction proves the converse: if every marked-chain barrier is respected, the shifted cover inequalities are jointly feasible. This also proves the complete incenter description. Zero total slack along a minimizing chain forces every facet on it to be tangent.

## Value
PASS. Marked order polytopes are a broad class that includes prominent representation-theoretic polytopes. The exact invariant is not a renamed height: unequal prescribed marking gaps compete with different saturated-chain lengths. The theorem solves the Euclidean inball problem throughout the regular bounded class, gives every optimal center, produces canonical lower and upper centers, and identifies forced tangent chains.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source supplies the exact facet correspondence; the later marked-poset paper supplies deformations and facet comparisons. The theorem is realization-specific and deliberately excludes nonregular presentations until their actual facets are identified.
