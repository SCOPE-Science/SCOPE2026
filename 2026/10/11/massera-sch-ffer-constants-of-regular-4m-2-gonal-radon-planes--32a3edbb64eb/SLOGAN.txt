For every regular \(N\)-gonal Radon plane with \(N\equiv2\pmod4\), the Massera--Schäffer Birkhoff constant has an exact modulo-\(8\) formula determined by one longer orthogonal vertex chord. The regular decagon gives the golden ratio \((1+\sqrt5)/2\), while both residue classes converge to the Hilbert value \(\sqrt2\).
