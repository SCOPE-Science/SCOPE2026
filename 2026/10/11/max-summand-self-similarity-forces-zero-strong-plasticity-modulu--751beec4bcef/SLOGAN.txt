Any real Banach space containing an isometric max-summand copy of \(\mathbb R\oplus_\infty X\) has zero strong-plasticity modulus on \((0,2)\). Thus the newly proved plastic spheres \(S_c\) and \(S_{\ell_\infty}\) are plastic but not strongly plastic, with a single non-contractive map sending arbitrarily close pairs to distance \(2\).
