For every unit-edge complete bipartite graph \(K_{m,n}\) with \(m,n\geq3\), its real Lipschitz-free transportation space has exact Euclidean Banach–Mazur distance \(\sqrt{m+n-1}\), the dimension-maximal value. This sharpness persists although these graph-polytope balls are not Hanner polytopes; an explicit permutation-invariant Euclidean form and a dual-edge certificate prove optimality.
