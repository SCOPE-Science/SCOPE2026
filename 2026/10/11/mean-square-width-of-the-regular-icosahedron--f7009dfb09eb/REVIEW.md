# Review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The exact unit-edge coordinate model, the vertex-normal spherical pentagon, its inradius, and the five-sector integral reconstruct the claimed moment. The packaged checker verifies the algebraic geometry exactly and replays the integral numerically as an independent regression check.

## Originality
**PASS, with residual unindexed-classical-literature risk.** Finch's full text treats the tetrahedron and cube, not the icosahedron. The later higher-moment Sudakov paper gives a general representation and explicit cube/simplex/crosspolytope applications, but no icosahedral evaluation. Object, alias, exact-constant, decimal, and projection-length searches found no covering formula.

## Value
**PASS.** The regular icosahedron is a canonical omitted Platonic-solid case in the literature that introduced exact mean-square width calculations. The closed form is a natural benchmark for width variance and random one-dimensional projections, and the normal-fan integration is structurally reusable.
