The third symmetric Fourier partial sum is not a contraction from complex-valued zero-mean \(L^\infty(\mathbb T)\) to \(L^4(\mathbb T)\): an explicit odd unimodular input exhibits a rigorously positive fourth-moment excess. This rules out a direct endpoint extension of the Fourier-section argument for Poisson Schwarz-contractivity, without deciding the Poisson endpoint conjecture.
