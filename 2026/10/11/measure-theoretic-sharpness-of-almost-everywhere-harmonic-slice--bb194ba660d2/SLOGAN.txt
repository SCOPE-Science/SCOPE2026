The almost-everywhere harmonic-slice theorem for finite plurisubharmonic functions has a genuinely sharp measure threshold: zero exceptional measure cannot be replaced by any positive tolerance. The explicit bounded family \(\phi_a(z)=\max\{0,\log|z_1|+a\}\) is nonpluriharmonic while its bad-direction measure is exactly \((1-e^{-2a})^{n-1}\to0\).
