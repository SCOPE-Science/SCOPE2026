The minimum enclosing sphere of the minimal-matroid base polytope \(T_{r,n}\) has a sharp rank-corank transition. When \(r(n-r)\le n\) every vertex is a contact and the center is \(r\mathbf1/n\); when \(r(n-r)>n\), the exceptional basis vertex moves strictly inside and the \(r(n-r)\) one-exchange vertices alone support the sphere.
