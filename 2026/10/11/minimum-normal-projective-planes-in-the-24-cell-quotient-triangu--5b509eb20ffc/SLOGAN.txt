In the \(24\)-cell quotient triangulation of \(\mathbb{RP}^3\), every normal projective plane meets the edge set at least \(21\) times, and exactly \(60\) attain equality. These minima form symmetry orbits of sizes \(12,24,24\), equivalently giving mod-two one-cosystole \(21\) for the unique nonzero class in \(H^1\).
