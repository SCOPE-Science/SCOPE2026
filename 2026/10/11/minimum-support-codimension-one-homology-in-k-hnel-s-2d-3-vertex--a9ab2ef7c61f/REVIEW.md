## correctness

PASS

PASS: The actual Bagchi-Datta construction identifies K^d_{2d+3} with the boundary of the (d+1)-dimensional complex on cyclic length-(d+2) facets. For d>=3, adjacent facets meet in interior d-face Q_i of d+1 consecutive vertices. K[Q_i] is the boundary of a d-simplex and its (d-1)-cycle is the mapping-torus fiber, a nonzero class in H_{d-1}(K;F2)=F2 by the sphere-bundle Wang sequence and the fact that mod-2 monodromy on top sphere homology is identity. Any nonzero (d-1)-cycle requires >=d+1 vertices, and on exactly d+1 vertices it is the full simplex boundary. For any other candidate Q, positive cyclic gaps g_j sum 2d+3, no facet containment entails each g_j<=d+1; removing each vertex forces adjacent gap sums>=d+2, hence 2(2d+3)>=(d+1)(d+2), false for d>=3. Thus the only minimum supports are exactly n cyclic Q_i. Independently built boundary complexes and found precisely 9,11,13,15 cyclic candidates for d=3,4,5,6. The universal proof is the gap contradiction and Wang sequence, not a finite extrapolation.
