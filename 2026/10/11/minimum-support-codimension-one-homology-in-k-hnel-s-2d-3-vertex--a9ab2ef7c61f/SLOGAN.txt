For every \(d\ge3\), Kühnel’s \(2d+3\)-vertex sphere-bundle triangulation has mod-two codimension-one homology systole \(d+1\) in vertex support. Its unique nonzero class has exactly \(2d+3\) minimum supports, namely the consecutive cyclic \(d+1\)-blocks, which are precisely the missing \(d\)-simplices.
