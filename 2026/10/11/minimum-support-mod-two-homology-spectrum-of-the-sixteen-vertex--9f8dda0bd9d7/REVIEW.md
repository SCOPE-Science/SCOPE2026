# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The exact labeled triangulation is reconstructed from the published group generators and two facet seeds. Exact binary row reduction gives \(\operatorname{rank}\partial_2=105\), \(\operatorname{rank}\partial_3=433\), and \(\dim H_2=22\). Every one of the \(1100\) missing four-sets is tested, yielding \(508\) nonzero quotient classes with multiplicities \(1^{320},3^{80},5^{108}\). The minimum-support conclusion follows because no two-cycle exists on at most three vertices and the four-vertex two-cycle space is generated by the tetrahedral boundary. Orbit and spanning claims are exhaustively recomputed.

## Originality
PASS. Searches covered empty tetrahedra, missing tetrahedra, four-vertex induced spheres, minimum-support homology, induced-subcomplex Betti data, Hochster/Stanley--Reisner formulations, exact numbers, and automorphism orbits. The exact-object literature gives three-neighborliness, tightness, the order-\(240\) action, and qualitative use of empty tetrahedra, but no inspected source gives the \(508\)-class quotient, the \(1/3/5\) collision spectrum, or the six homology-class orbits. Tightness implies nontriviality of the induced classes but not their equality relations. The principal residual risk is an unindexed computation or a statement in the unavailable full text of the 2001 paper.

## Value
PASS. Four vertices are the smallest possible support for middle-dimensional homology in this three-neighborly minimal K3 triangulation, so the exact quotient of all empty-tetrahedron boundaries is a canonical local-to-global invariant rather than an arbitrary subset census. The collision spectrum shows how \(1100\) minimal local spheres encode the \(22\)-dimensional global middle homology, and the full symmetry-orbit decomposition makes the result structurally usable.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest exact-object source is Spreer--Kühnel, arXiv:0909.1453v1, which gives the labeled triangulation and discusses empty tetrahedra in slicings. Casella--Kühnel establishes the minimal, tight, highly symmetric K3 triangulation. Codenotti--Santos--Spreer supplies the broader induced-subcomplex framework. The result is restricted to mod-two homology of this exact triangulation.
