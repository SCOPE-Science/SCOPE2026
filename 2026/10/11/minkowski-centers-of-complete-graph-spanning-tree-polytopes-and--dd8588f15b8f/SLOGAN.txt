For the spanning-tree polytope of every complete graph \(K_n\), Minkowski asymmetry is exactly \(n-1\), and all minimizing centers correspond to nonnegative weighted-degree-\((n-1)\) graphs with edge caps \(n-2\). An additional triple-cut bound is essential **only** for \(n=6\); in every dimension the center locus has affine dimension \(n(n-3)/2\).
