For \(m\ge4\) and \(n\ge5\), the Lovász open-neighborhood complex of \(K_m\square C_n\) is a wedge of one circle, \(n\) spheres of dimension \(m-2\), and \(n(m^2-3m+1)\) two-spheres. A uniform local simplicial collapse and a connected crown-graph interface relation yield the full integral homotopy classification.
