# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The graph is defined explicitly and all graph edges and pairwise disjoint edge sets are regenerated, yielding \((27,219,613,510,72)\) simplices in dimensions zero through four. The included acyclic partial matching leaves one critical zero-cell, 49 critical two-cells, and 67 critical three-cells; the full orientation of 4677 face incidences is verified acyclic. The signed integer \(49\)-by-\(67\) Morse boundary is reconstructed from the critical cells and the matching, has exact rational rank 19 and a rank-19 minor of determinant 1. Therefore its nonzero Smith invariants are all units. Since \(\pi_2\) of a wedge of two-spheres is freely generated by its spheres, the integer attaching-map reduction proves the homotopy equivalence \(\bigvee^{30}S^2\vee\bigvee^{48}S^3\). A separate direct mod-two boundary computation confirms the reduced Betti numbers; it is supplementary rather than an integral proof.

## Originality — PASS
Engström (arXiv:math/0512420) and Barmak (arXiv:1007.0418) supply general claw-free/independence-complex techniques and bounds, not the graph-specific integral wedge decomposition. The outerplanar matching-complex theorem (arXiv:2411.04601) is not applicable: the Goldner–Harary graph has 27 edges on eleven vertices, exceeding the outerplanar limit of 19. The manifold matching-complex classification (arXiv:1906.03328) does not apply to this nonpure complex. Queries for the graph's proper and equivalent names and the exact edge-matching homotopy decomposition revealed no covering statement among inspected published sources. A possibly equivalent, unindexed computation remains a bibliographic risk; search absence does not establish priority.

## Value — PASS
The graph is the canonical smallest non-Hamiltonian maximal planar graph and a natural non-outerplanar planar example in a research area motivated by the planar matching-complex wedge question. A complete mixed-dimensional integral homotopy type of this standard graph is not an automatic consequence of the existing general results. Its exact two-dimensional and three-dimensional sphere multiplicities and attaching-map simplification can serve as a concrete planar benchmark. This finite result makes no unsupported broader conjectural claim.

## Limitations
The proof is a reproducible finite calculation for one explicitly defined graph; no result for all planar graphs is claimed. Bibliographic completeness and independent scientific validation remain open. Full signed integer computations, not only field homology or execution summaries, underlie the conclusion.
