For every finite affine plane of order \(q\ge2\), the scale-two Vietoris–Rips complex of its incidence graph is \(\bigvee^{(q-1)^2(q+1)}S^2\vee\bigvee^{(q-1)^{q+1}}S^q\). The extra high-dimensional spheres arise precisely from parallel classes of lines, distinguishing affine from projective incidence geometry.
