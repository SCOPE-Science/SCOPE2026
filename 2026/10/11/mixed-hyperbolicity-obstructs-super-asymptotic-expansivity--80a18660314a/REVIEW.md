# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The defining quantifiers were reconstructed from arXiv:2610.01196v1. In a mixed hyperbolic Riesz splitting, stable and unstable unit vectors have image norms on opposite sides of \(1/2\) and \(2\) for every sufficiently large time. The normalized segment joining them lies continuously in the unit sphere, so an intermediate unit vector has image norm exactly \(1\) at each such time. This directly defeats any proposed common cutoff. The pure stable and pure unstable cases follow from the spectral-radius formula, and the finite-dimensional unit-circle case is blocked by a unit eigenvector.

The concrete \(\operatorname{diag}(1/2,2)\) certificate was replayed with exact rational arithmetic for sample indices, while the displayed identities prove it for every \(N\ge1\). No finite experiment is used as an infinite proof.

## Originality
PASS. The motivating paper states the opposite hyperbolicity-to-super implication in Theorem 4.5 and Corollary 4.6 and uses it in its finite-dimensional and surjective-Hilbert equivalences. Its proof does not control mixed stable/unstable vectors. The exact definition, the full relevant source sections, the older linear-expansivity background, the earlier asymptotic-expansivity literature, eight semantic/alias searches, and the closest indexed scientific findings were compared. No implication-equivalent correction was found. The closest earlier result based on the same motivating source concerns asymptotic and strong asymptotic expansivity for normal operators, not the super property.

Residual originality risk remains that an equivalent mixed-dichotomy observation exists under older unrelated terminology; the exact “super asymptotic expansivity” terminology is introduced by the motivating source.
