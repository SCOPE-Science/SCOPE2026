For the source-defined super asymptotic expansivity, a hyperbolic operator with both stable and unstable spectral subspaces fails the uniform small-or-large orbit alternative. In finite-dimensional complex spaces, super asymptotic expansivity therefore holds exactly when the entire spectrum lies strictly inside the unit disk or entirely outside its closure; \(\operatorname{diag}(1/2,2)\) is an explicit hyperbolic counterexample to the broader equivalence.
