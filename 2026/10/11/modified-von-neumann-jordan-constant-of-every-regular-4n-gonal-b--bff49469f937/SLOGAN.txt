Every regular \(4n\)-gonal Banach plane has the exact modified von Neumann--Jordan constant \[C'_{\mathrm{NJ}}(X_{4n})=\sec^2\!\left(\frac{\pi}{4n}\right).\] The sharp pair is formed by adjacent vertices symmetric about a facet normal, and the constants converge quadratically to the Hilbert value \(1\).
