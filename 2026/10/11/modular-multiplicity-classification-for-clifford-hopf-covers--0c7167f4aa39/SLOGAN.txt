For the Hopf immersion over an \(m\)-fold great circle, the marked modulus is \(im/2\) for even \(m\) and \((1+im)/2\) for odd \(m\), while lattice covolume, Willmore energy, and varifold mass all equal \(2\pi^2m\). The conformal class determines the covering multiplicity except for the unique collision between the once- and twice-covered Clifford torus.
