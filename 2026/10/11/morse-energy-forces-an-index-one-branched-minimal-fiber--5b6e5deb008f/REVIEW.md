# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The cyclic pseudo-Anosov action on contractible Teichmüller space is free and properly discontinuous, so the quotient is a \(K(\mathbb Z,1)\) with nonzero first homology. Verjovsky proves that the descended energy is proper, that its critical points are exactly the zeros of the Hopf differential, and that its global minima are least-area fibers. Under the stated Morse hypothesis, the weak Morse inequality forces an index-one critical point, which cannot be a minimum and therefore yields a non-least-area branched minimal immersion.

## Originality
PASS. The recent primary paper develops the harmonic-energy framework but does not invoke Morse theory or state an index-one/non-minimizing critical-orbit result. Goldman--Wentworth prove the needed quotient properness but likewise do not state this implication. Targeted exact, alias, and implication searches found no covering statement, and the available current and prior owned records contain no Cannon--Thurston/harmonic-energy finding.

## Value
PASS. The result turns the topology of the natural cyclic Teichmüller quotient into a geometric existence statement: nondegeneracy of the unperturbed energy cannot leave only least-area fibers as critical orbits, but forces a genuine index-one branched minimal representative. This is a structural consequence for the new variational framework rather than a parameter substitution or finite computation.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest sources are Verjovsky's recent fibered-hyperbolic harmonic-energy paper and Goldman--Wentworth's properness theorem. The conclusion is conditional on the descended energy being Morse; no genericity theorem for that condition, embeddedness of the saddle immersion, or quantitative gap above \(A_0\) is claimed. A logically equivalent but unindexed observation remains a residual originality risk.
