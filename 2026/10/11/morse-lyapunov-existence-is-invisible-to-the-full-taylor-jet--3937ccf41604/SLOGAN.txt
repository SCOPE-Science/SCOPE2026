Two smooth globally asymptotically stable planar vector fields can have the same complete Taylor jet at the equilibrium while only one admits a Morse Lyapunov function. A flat positive time change transfers Jongeneel's no-Morse example to a zero-jet field, whereas \(F_+(x)=-e^{-1/\|x\|^2}x\) retains the quadratic Morse Lyapunov function \(\|x\|^2\).
