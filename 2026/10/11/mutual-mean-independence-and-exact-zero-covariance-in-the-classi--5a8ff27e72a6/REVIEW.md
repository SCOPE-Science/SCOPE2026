# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Compact positive support permits arbitrary logarithmic one-coordinate generator tests. These give
\[
\mathbb E[y\mid x]=\frac{\alpha}{\beta},
\qquad
\mathbb E[x\mid y]=\frac{\gamma}{\delta}.
\]
The covariance cancellation is then exact, and the defining differential equations identify each regression residual with the opposite species' per-capita growth rate. Vanishing of either variance forces invariant support to be tangent to a nullcline and hence to collapse to the coexistence equilibrium.

Risk: boundary extinction measures are outside the stated domain because the logarithmic tests are singular there.

## Originality
PASS. The familiar time-average-equals-equilibrium theorem is treated as prior work, not as a new claim. The complete foundational article and a complete modern periodic-solution paper were inspected at statement and implication level. Neither gives the reciprocal conditional expectations, zero covariance theorem, or variance–growth identities. Direct semantic searches covered conditional, covariance, zero-correlation, and weighted-average aliases.

Risk: the proof is short enough that an equivalent weighted identity could remain in unindexed literature.

## Value
PASS. A nonconstant Lotka–Volterra orbit is a strongly coupled nonlinear cycle, yet its time-weighted prey and predator coordinates have exactly zero Pearson correlation at every amplitude. The stronger flat-regression statement explains this cancellation and supplies exact data-consistency identities for the canonical predator–prey oscillator.
