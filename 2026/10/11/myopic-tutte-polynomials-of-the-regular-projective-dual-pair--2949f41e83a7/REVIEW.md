Exhaustively independently enumerated all 32768 edge subsets per graph in NetworkX and recomputed all coefficients with signed parity and binomial expansion; results precisely match full displayed tables: 7204/25564 local subsets, 17/20 nonzero monomials, and 1296/2000 spanning-tree evaluations. This is exhaustive for two graphs only.
