For homogeneous nearest-neighbor Dirichlet energy on \(\mathbb Z^d\), \(d\ge3\), the sharp \(\ell^q\) embedding constant approaches its \(\ell^\infty\) endpoint with the exact exponentially small correction \((2d/q)\rho_d^q\), where \(\rho_d\) is the nearest-neighbor normalized Green value. The prefactor counts the \(2d\) closest lattice sites; the result does not assert exact finite-exponent constants.
