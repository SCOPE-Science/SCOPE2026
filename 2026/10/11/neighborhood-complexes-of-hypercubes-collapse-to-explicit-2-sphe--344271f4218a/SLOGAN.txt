For every \(d\ge4\), each parity component of the Lovász neighborhood complex of the hypercube \(Q_d\) collapses to a simply connected \(2\)-complex and is homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{\mu_d}S^2\), where \(\mu_d=2^{d-3}(d^2-5d+8)-1\). Thus a complex built from \((d-1)\)-simplex neighborhoods has homotopy concentrated in dimension two with an explicit exponentially growing sphere multiplicity.
