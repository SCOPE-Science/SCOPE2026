For every \(d\ge2\) and every finite \(p>2\), no Euclidean-equivariant selector can be associated with the \(L_p\) support-function metric on convex bodies. A two-harmonic perturbation of the ball makes the \(L_p\)-nearest translation of a large ball drift by a nonzero multiple of \((p-2)R^{-1}\), resolving the 2009 selector problem negatively.
