For every \(n\ge2\), the complex body \(\{z:|z_1|+|z_2|\le1,\ |z_j|\le1\ (j\ge3)\}\) has fractional illumination number exactly \(2^n\) but is not a complex-linear image of the polydisc. Thus polydisc-only equality in the complex fractional illumination conjecture fails in every dimension, without contradicting its proposed upper bound.
