**Mathematical value — PASS.** The result settles a natural thermodynamic boundary problem: although the marker-free full \(q\)-shift has entropy \(\log q\), every finite marker penalty still yields strictly higher pressure and a unique marker-using equilibrium state. The gap is doubly exponentially small with a nonconstant universal discrete-scale correction. That exact scale profile cannot be read off from an entropy value or a simple pointwise pressure inequality, and provides a precise invariant of sparse return geometry.
