In the 16-vertex Björner–Lutz Poincaré homology sphere, vertex \(16\) is the unique vertex of tetrahedron-incidence degree \(8\), and its link is an octahedron whose three opposite pairs already occur as edges outside the star. Therefore its star cannot be replaced by any 3-ball on the same six boundary vertices while leaving the rest of the triangulation fixed; deleting the vertex gives a 15-vertex homology \(3\)-ball with octahedral boundary and binary-icosahedral fundamental group.
