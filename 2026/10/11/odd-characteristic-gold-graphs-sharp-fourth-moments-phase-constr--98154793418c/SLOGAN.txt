The sharp fourth-moment extension norm of an odd-characteristic Gold graph has an exact arithmetic jump: its fourth power is \(2-q^{-1}\) in the planar regime and \(p^d+1-p^d/q\) in the nonplanar regime, where \(q=p^m\) and \(d=\gcd(m,k)\). An exact orbit-square deficit identity characterizes all extremizers by flat amplitude and root-of-unity phase relations, with genuinely nonlinear quadratic-trace examples in the nonplanar branch. In fact, modulo global phase, the planar optimizer space is a continuous torus whereas every nonplanar optimizer-phase group is finite.
