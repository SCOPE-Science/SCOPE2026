For identical repulsive sine-coupled phase networks, every odd size admits an explicit exponentially attracting \(|Z|\le1/2\) balanced neighborhood, whereas every even size admits nearby orbits relaxing only as \(|Z_n|\sim (2hn)^{-1}\). The exact even-size inverse-square phase law has logarithmic correction \((3h/2-1/4)\log n\), absent at the admissible step \(h=1/6\) only for four nodes.
