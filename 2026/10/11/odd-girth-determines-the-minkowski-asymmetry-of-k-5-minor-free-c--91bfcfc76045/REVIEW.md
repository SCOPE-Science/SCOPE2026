# Same-model mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS

For every finite simple \(K_5\)-minor-free graph with at least one edge, the proof establishes full-dimensionality of the cut polytope from cut-vector identities, interiority of the half-edge center from positive averaging, and global optimality of that center from affine switching and convexity of the set of centers feasible at a fixed dilation. The facet-dilation quotient follows directly from reflected containment. The exhaustive cycle-and-edge facet description in Barahona–Mahjoub, Corollary 3.10, is checked in the primary full text. Evaluating each chordless cycle inequality over realizable even-parity edge selections gives exactly \(\ell/(\ell-2)\) for odd cycles and \(1\) for even cycles. The decreasing quotient identifies shortest odd-cycle length as the unique controlling parameter. The all-graph statement is analytical, not inferred from experiments. Exact-rational finite tests passed 1,288 graph cases and 1,867,540 reflected inequalities.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

The closest full texts inspected are Barahona–Mahjoub's complete polyhedral facet description, Deza–Dutour Sikirić's switching and face-enumeration work, and Nikolaev's graph-metric study of cut-polytope one-skeleta. The first theorem supplies a nontrivial input that permits, but does not explicitly state, the reflection-dilation optimization; the second concerns automorphisms and facets; the third concerns adjacency, clique number and graph distance. Thus the odd-girth asymmetry formula is not a restatement of a conclusion found in those texts. Result-index searches under Minkowski asymmetry, symmetry measures, parity polytopes, and odd-cycle girth returned only different invariants. Searches do not prove publication priority; the remaining principal risk is an equivalent older measure-of-symmetry formulation.

## Value — PASS

The result provides a complete exact affine-invariant metric classification for a broad and natural minor-closed graph class, including every planar graph. It reveals a sharp structural transition between bipartite graphs (central symmetry) and nonbipartite graphs, and quantitative convergence of the asymmetry to one with increasing odd girth. The dependence solely on shortest odd cycle is substantially more informative than checking all facets for each graph; neither correctness nor computational reproducibility alone is used as the value argument.

## Scope and limitations

No claim is made beyond the \(K_5\)-minor-free class, about uniqueness of an optimal center, or about independent confirmation of scientific priority. The finite checks test arithmetic and representative boundaries but do not prove universal statements. All literature comparisons reflect genuinely inspected sources; alternative naming conventions may still hide prior coverage.
