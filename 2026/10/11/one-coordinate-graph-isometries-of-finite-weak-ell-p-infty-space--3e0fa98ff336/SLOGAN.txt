For the canonical map that keeps \(n\) weak-\(\ell_p\) coordinates and appends one linear functional, isometry is equivalent to the exact coefficient inequality \(\sum_{k=1}^n k^{-1/p}u_k^*\le(n+1)^{-1/p}\). This completely describes the one-coordinate graph family and makes the published two-to-three-dimensional nondisjoint example the sharp balanced case.
