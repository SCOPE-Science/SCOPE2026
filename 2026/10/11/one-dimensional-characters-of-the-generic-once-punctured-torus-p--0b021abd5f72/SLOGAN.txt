For generic \(q\) with \(q^4\neq1\) and \(q^6\neq1\), the one-dimensional characters of the once-punctured-torus \(\mathrm{PGL}_2\) skein algebra lie in four diagonal families of formula-points plus two three-point permutation orbits. The finite list follows by factoring the scalarized presentation, and every listed point satisfies all defining relations exactly.
