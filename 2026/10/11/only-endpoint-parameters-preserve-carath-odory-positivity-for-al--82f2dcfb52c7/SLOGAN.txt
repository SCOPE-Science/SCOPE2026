For Grigoriciuc's \(\alpha\)-Carathéodory component, universal positive real part on normalized convex disk maps holds exactly at \(\alpha=0\) and \(\alpha=1\). The same convex sector map \(f(z)=\sqrt{(1+z)/(1-z)}-1\) has \(\inf_{\mathbb D}\operatorname{Re}h_\alpha[f]=-\infty\) for every \(0<\alpha<1\).
