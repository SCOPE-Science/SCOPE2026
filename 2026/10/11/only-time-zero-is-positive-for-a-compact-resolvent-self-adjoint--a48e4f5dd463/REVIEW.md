# Same-model mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem addresses the explicitly defined real self-adjoint operator on \(\ell^2(\mathbb Z)\), not an arbitrary semigroup. The diagonal \(D\) is positive with compact resolvent; the rank-one perturbation \(K\) and compact antisymmetric perturbation \(B\) are bounded and self-adjoint. The reference generator is nonpositive by a \(D^{1/2}\)-Cauchy–Schwarz identity, its nullspace is precisely the strictly positive span of \(u\), and it has a spectral gap. The original construction's exact reducing-subspace formula is rederived in the proof. A Dyson–Phillips estimate bounds every symmetric \((-n,n)\) entry by \(n^2e^{-3n^2}(e^{2t}-1)\), without finite-dimensional truncation. The antisymmetric correction is exactly \(-h_n(t)/2\), with \(h_n(t)\geq nt e^{-2n^2-nt}\). The analytic inequality \(e^{n^2-nt}>4ne^{2t}\) for \(n\geq\lceil t+3\rceil\) gives strict negativity for every real \(t>0\). Separate spectral and Duhamel estimates establish individual eventual strong positivity for all nonzero positive inputs. The proof handles the endpoint \(t=0\) separately: the identity is positive. The executable verifies the rational constants and selected parameter checks; the infinite assertion follows from the proof, not the computations.

## Originality — PASS, with identified residual risk

The identical underlying operator and its individual eventual strong positivity were already published by Dobrick (arXiv:2609.00425v1, 2026-08-31). Its Theorem 1.1(3) and Proposition 3.5 identify nonpositive operators at sufficiently large integer times, whereas the present statement identifies **every** strictly positive real time and supplies the explicit uniform-in-coordinate sufficient cutoff. Selected integer times do not imply absence of all other positive times merely by taking semigroup powers. The full first-version construction and relevant assertions were inspected, not only its abstract. Daners–Glück–Kennedy (2016), Example 5.7, already establishes the *abstract nowhere-positive phenomenon* for an individually eventually strongly positive semigroup on \(C([-1,1])\), but its bounded generator and Banach lattice are different; Example 5.8 adds compact resolvent on a \(C(K)\)-space but checks selected times. Neither is a self-adjoint Hilbert-lattice compact-resolvent instance. General smoothing and domination theorems impose hypotheses absent here. Targeted mathematical-record and primary-literature searches found no result determining the entire positivity-time set for this particular self-adjoint generator. This is not a claim to have discovered the general nowhere-positive phenomenon. Residual risk: an unindexed note, an independently derived equivalent inequality, or another revision might already contain the all-time comparison.

## Value — PASS

Identifying the entire positivity-time set \(\{0\}\), rather than only its failure to contain an eventual ray, gives a qualitative extremal boundary in the most rigid concrete class relevant to the source question: real self-adjoint Hilbert-space contraction semigroups with compact-resolvent generator. The explicit \(n\geq\lceil t+3\rceil\) witness is uniform in coordinates for each time, and the short Dyson estimate replaces the original selected-time convolution bound. Prior \(C(K)\)-examples show the qualitative phenomenon but do not solve this precise Hilbert-lattice question. The statement neither claims a general classification nor a new construction of the underlying generator.
