A real self-adjoint contraction semigroup with compact-resolvent generator can make every nonzero positive orbit eventually strongly positive while having no positive operator at any strictly positive time. For the explicit diagonal-plus-rank-one construction on \(\ell^2(\mathbb Z)\), every \(t>0\) and integer \(n\geq\lceil t+3\rceil\) satisfy \(\langle e^{tA}e_n,e_{-n}\rangle<0\).
