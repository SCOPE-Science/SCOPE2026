# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The claim follows from exact maximal-neighborhood faces, elementary collapses of both one-sided covers to simplex boundaries, an exact intersection identified as the matching-deleted complete bipartite graph, and a homotopy-pushout splitting. For integer m at least four the two one-sided covers are simply connected, so the intersection inclusions are null-homotopic. The proof distinguishes ordinary open from closed neighborhoods, accounts for the coincident sphere dimensions at m=4, and proves non-shellability by lower-dimensional integral homology of a pure complex. A standalone exact mod-two chain-complex checker reconstructs the graph from edges and agrees for m=4..8. The finite computations alone are not an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS

The closest same-graph theorem is Bayer et al., arXiv:2304.13675, Definition 7.16 and Theorem 7.18: it describes cut complexes, not the open-neighborhood complex. Matsushita, arXiv:2511.12608, Example 3.5, describes closed-neighborhood complexes of Cartesian products of complete graphs; the rank of their homology differs from the present open-neighborhood result. The 2022 hypercube Čech paper explicitly separates closed Čech from open Lovász neighborhoods. Shukla's 2018 open-neighborhood theorem has bounded-degree and circulant hypotheses that do not give this unbounded clique-prism family. Targeted external and mathematical-result searches and own record deduplication found no result implying the stated exact wedge. An equivalent unindexed theorem is a residual novelty risk.

## Value — PASS

A canonical Cartesian graph family explicitly studied in cut-complex topology has a natural, distinct open-neighborhood invariant with mixed homology degrees from m=5 onward, an exact quadratic rank, and a uniform non-shellability obstruction. The result clarifies why closed-neighborhood computations and nearby cut-complex classifications cannot answer the open-neighborhood question, and supplies a useful benchmark for graph homotopy and neighborhood complexes rather than an arbitrary parameter slice.

## Limits

Only m at least four is proved. No independence or external validation is claimed. The independent status remains unperformed, and finite checks are supporting evidence, not a proof of the uniform range.

Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
