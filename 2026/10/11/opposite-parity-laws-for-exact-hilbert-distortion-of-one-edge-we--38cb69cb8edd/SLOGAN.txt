For a cycle with one edge of length \(\lambda\in(0,1]\) and all other edges of length \(1\), the real Lipschitz-free space has a closed-form optimal Euclidean Banach--Mazur distance in every dimension \(n\ge3\). Remarkably, the distance increases strictly with the shortened edge's length when \(n\) is even, but decreases strictly when \(n\) is odd.
