In a \(P\)-controlled dual g-frame, the canonical factor must be ordered as \(\Lambda_j P S_{P\Lambda P}^{-1}\), not \(\Lambda_j S_{P\Lambda P}^{-1}P\). The reversed published order works exactly when \(P\) commutes with the ordinary frame operator \(S_\Lambda\), and a two-dimensional noncommuting example disproves it otherwise.
