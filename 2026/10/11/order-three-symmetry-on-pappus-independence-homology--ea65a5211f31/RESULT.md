# Order-three symmetry on Pappus independence homology
## Finding
Let \(P\) be the standard Pappus graph with LCF presentation \([5,7,-7,7,-7,-5]^3\), and let \(r\) be the graph automorphism \(r(i)=i+6\pmod{18}\). Then \(\widetilde H_i(\operatorname{Ind}(P);\mathbb Q)=0\) for every \(i\neq4\), while, as a \(\mathbb Q[C_3]\)-module for \(C_3=\langle r\rangle\),
\[
\widetilde H_4(\operatorname{Ind}(P);\mathbb Q)\cong 2\mathbf 1\oplus4V,
\]
where \(V=\ker(\mathbb Q[C_3]\xrightarrow{\sum}\mathbb Q)\cong\mathbb Q(\zeta_3)\). Equivalently, the character on \(1,r,r^2\) is \((10,-2,-2)\).

## Assumptions and scope
The graph has vertex set \(\{0,1,\ldots,17\}\). Its edge set is reconstructed from the Hamiltonian cycle together with the LCF offsets \([5,7,-7,7,-7,-5]^3\). The independence complex \(\operatorname{Ind}(P)\) consists of all vertex subsets containing no graph edge. The cyclic group considered here is only the order-three subgroup generated by the period-six translation \(r\); no claim is made about the full automorphism-group representation. Homology and characters are over \(\mathbb Q\).

## Proof
The supplied verifier reconstructs \(P\), obtaining \(18\) vertices, \(27\) edges, and degree \(3\) at every vertex. It exhaustively enumerates every nonempty independent set. The nonempty face vector in simplex dimensions \(0,\ldots,8\) is
\[
(18,126,438,801,756,348,90,18,2),
\]
so there are \(2597\) nonempty faces.

A sequential toggle matching using the vertex order
\[
(10,5,16,17,9,0,15,14,3,6,11,13,12,7,1,8,2,4)
\]
pairs \(1293\) faces. The verifier checks each pair is a Hasse cover and checks the complete Forman orientation on all \(11430\) nontrivial Hasse covers by topological sorting. The orientation is acyclic. Exactly one critical \(0\)-cell and ten critical \(4\)-cells remain. Forman's theorem therefore gives
\[
\operatorname{Ind}(P)\simeq\bigvee^{10}S^4,
\]
and in particular reduced rational homology vanishes outside degree \(4\) and has dimension \(10\) there.

The map \(r(i)=i+6\pmod{18}\) has order \(3\), and the verifier checks directly that both \(r\) and \(r^2\) preserve the reconstructed edge set. For each \(r^k\), it computes the signed trace on each oriented simplicial chain group by summing the orientation signs of setwise fixed simplices. In degrees \(0,\ldots,8\), the chain traces are
\[
(18,126,438,801,756,348,90,18,2)
\]
for the identity and
\[
(0,0,6,0,0,9,0,0,2)
\]
for each of \(r\) and \(r^2\). Hence the Lefschetz numbers are \(11,-1,-1\). Since the complex is connected, \(H_0\) has trace \(1\), and all remaining homology is in degree \(4\). Therefore the character of \(H_4\) is \((10,-2,-2)\).

Over \(\mathbb Q\), the irreducible representations of \(C_3\) are the trivial representation \(\mathbf 1\), with character \((1,1,1)\), and the two-dimensional cyclotomic representation \(V\), with character \((2,-1,-1)\). Solving \(a+2b=10\) and \(a-b=-2\) gives \(a=2\) and \(b=4\), proving the claimed decomposition.

## Verification
Run `python3 verify_pappus_c3.py`. It uses only the Python standard library. In addition to the complete face-poset matching and chain-trace computation, it independently constructs all simplicial boundary maps and checks the ordinary Betti vector \((1,0,0,0,10,0,0,0,0)\) over each of \(\mathbb F_2\), \(\mathbb F_3\), and \(\mathbb F_5\). These finite-field calculations are cross-checks; the homotopy concentration follows from the verified acyclic matching, and the rational character follows from the exact Lefschetz trace calculation.

## Relationship to prior work
Matsushita's topology-primary work on graph matching complexes treats homotopy types of polygonal line tilings and records independence complexes among its keywords; it does not treat the Pappus graph or equivariant homology. Berghoff develops a spectral sequence for homology of independence complexes of arbitrary finite graphs, with explicit applications to paths and cycles; inspection of the full text found no Pappus example and no equivariant or graph-automorphism computation. Tsukuda proves a general suspension description for independence complexes of finite bipartite incidence graphs, directly relevant because the Pappus graph is the Levi graph of the Pappus configuration, but the inspected theorem does not state a Pappus homology representation or an equivariant character formula. The standard Pappus graph identification and LCF presentation are independently documented by MathWorld.

The nonequivariant fact that the fourth homology has dimension \(10\) cannot determine the \(C_3\)-module: distinct rational \(C_3\)-modules can have the same total dimension. The character \((10,-2,-2)\), or equivalently the decomposition \(2\mathbf1\oplus4V\), is therefore strictly finer symmetry information.

## Limitations
The result concerns one canonical order-three subgroup coming from the period-six LCF translation. It does not determine the action of the full automorphism group of the Pappus graph, the integral \(\mathbb Z[C_3]\)-module structure, or torsion phenomena for other coefficient systems. Literature searches and full-text inspections cannot exclude an obscure or unindexed equivalent computation; no such computation was found in the checked sources.
