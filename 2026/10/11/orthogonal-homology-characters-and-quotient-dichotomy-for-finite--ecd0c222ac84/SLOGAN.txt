For every \(n\ge2\) and \(d\ge2\), the complete rational orthogonal action on the homology of finite subsets of \(S^d\) is a sum of explicitly determined powers of the orientation character. Every finite orthogonal quotient with an orientation-reversing symmetry is a rational homology sphere when \(d\) is even, but for odd \(d\ge3\) it becomes rationally acyclic precisely when \(n\equiv1,2\pmod4\).
