# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Dihedral reduction leaves only a smooth reference-edge point or its endpoint vertex. In the smooth case Birkhoff--James orthogonality forces the perpendicular edge midpoint, and the two chord norms are \(\sqrt2\pm t/(\sqrt2c)\). In the vertex case the Birkhoff sector is exactly the arc between the two perpendicular adjacent facet normals; each half-edge gives \(\sqrt2\pm(s-r)/(\sqrt2c)\), up to interchange. These formulas exhaust the sphere and prove the pointwise threshold. Quarter-turn vertex and edge-midpoint pairs attain equality.

## Originality
PASS. The defining Baronti--Papini paper was inspected in full at the definitions, general results, and examples. It contains the regular-octagon identity \(J_{\perp}=g_{\perp}=\sqrt2\), but no \(16\)-gon, \(24\)-gon, or all-\(8m\) extension. published-finding corpus searches covered the invariant names, exact \(\sqrt2\) value, \(8m/8n\) aliases, and the \(16\)- and \(24\)-gon specializations. Current and immediate-prior own ledgers were also checked; nearby own results concern the decagon and ordinary James constants in a complementary residue class.

## Value
PASS. The result gives an infinite non-Hilbert family with Hilbert-valued orthogonal James and Schäffer constants and explains the phenomenon by a stronger pointwise chord-separation theorem. This is structural information beyond another isolated polygonal constant evaluation.

## Closest literature and limitations
Baronti and Papini introduced \(J_{\perp}\) and \(g_{\perp}\) and computed the regular octagon as one example. The present theorem extends that isolated example to every regular \(8m\)-gon. A previously recorded decagonal result has a different value and belongs to another residue class. The complementary regular-polygon classes are not classified here.
