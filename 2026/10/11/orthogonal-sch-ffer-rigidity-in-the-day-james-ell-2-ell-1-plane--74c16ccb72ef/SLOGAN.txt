For the real Day--James plane \(\ell_2-\ell_1\), the orthogonal Schäffer constant is exactly \(g_\perp=\sqrt{3/2}\), equal to the classical Schäffer constant. The minimizing Birkhoff pairs are exactly the balanced diagonal/taxicab pair and its norm symmetries, even though the same space has \(J_\perp<J\).
