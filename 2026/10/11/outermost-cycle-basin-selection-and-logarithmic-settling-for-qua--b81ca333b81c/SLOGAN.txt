For every negative-slope quantized affine contraction \(f(x)=\lfloor\mu-rx\rfloor\), \(0<r<1\), every initial state outside the periodic core is captured by its outermost two-cycle, while every inner cycle has an exact one- or two-cell basin. The time for a remote integer to settle is \(\log|n|/(-2\log r)+O(1)\), uniformly up to a parameter-dependent additive constant.
