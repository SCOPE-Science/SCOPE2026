Pairwise-distinct vector norms do not force a unique \(K\)-frame scaling: \(F=(e_1,2e_1,3e_2)\) has a continuum of strict Parseval scalings already for \(K=I\). For a finite-dimensional frame with a strict scaling, uniqueness is governed by real-linear independence of the outer products \(f_i f_i^*\), not by the norms.
