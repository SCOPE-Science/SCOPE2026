For every prime \(p\equiv3\pmod4\), Fourier energy concentration between the nonzero quadratic residues has an exact multiplicative-character spectrum with one distinguished constant mode and complementary pairs \(\lambda,1-\lambda\) for every nonconstant mode. The character formula gives the sharp localization constant and every optimizer, a closed centered second moment, and the absence of a central \(1/2\)-eigenvalue whenever \((p-1)/2\) is prime.
