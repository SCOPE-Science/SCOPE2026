For the Li–Zhao–Geng Lorenz-like flow, whenever \(b>0\) and \(\kappa=f+g-bg/(2a)>0\), every bounded complete non-equilibrium trajectory lies strictly above the paraboloid \(z=gx^2/(2a)\). The exact memory law \(z-gx^2/(2a)=\kappa\int_0^\infty e^{-bs}x(t-s)^2\,ds\) makes the confinement pointwise rather than merely statistical.
