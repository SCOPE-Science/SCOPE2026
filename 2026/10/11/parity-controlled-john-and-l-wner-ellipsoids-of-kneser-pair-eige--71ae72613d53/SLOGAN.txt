The orthonormally realized \(1\)-eigenpolytope of \(KG(n,2)\) has exact spherical John and Löwner ellipsoids, with a parity-dependent sharp inradius. Its tangent facets are indexed by perfect matchings when \(n\) is even and by a triangle plus a matching when \(n\) is odd, via an affine polarity with fractional perfect matchings.
