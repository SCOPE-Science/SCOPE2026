For the Euclidean numerical radius of the first two powers of a nilpotent Jordan shift, even matrix sizes never saturate the lower bound \(t\,w(J_n^2)\) at a finite parameter, while odd sizes saturate sharply after a finite threshold. The first thresholds are \(\tau_3=2\) and \(\tau_5=\sqrt{5+7\sqrt2/2}\), and odd-dimensional thresholds have exact growth order \(n^{3/2}\).
