For \(W_{n,d}=\bigvee_{i=1}^n S^d\), the summand-permuting \(S_n\)-action on the based third finite-subset space has a complete integral homology description: diagonal classes are permutation copies of suspended projective-space homology, while the free off-diagonal degree-\(2d\) classes are ordinary pair permutations for even \(d\) and \(\bigwedge^2\mathbb Z^n\) for odd \(d\). The parity switch is the degree \((-1)^d\) of interchanging the two sphere factors in a smash product.
