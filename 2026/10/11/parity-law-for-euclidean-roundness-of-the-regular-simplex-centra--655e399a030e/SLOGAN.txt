For the largest centrally symmetric kernel of a unit-circumradius regular \(n\)-simplex, the inradius is always \(1/n\), while the circumradius has a sharp parity split: \(1/\sqrt n\) for odd \(n\) and \(1/\sqrt{n+1}\) for even \(n\). The split is exactly the difference between a central cube section reaching cube vertices and reaching only edge midpoints.
