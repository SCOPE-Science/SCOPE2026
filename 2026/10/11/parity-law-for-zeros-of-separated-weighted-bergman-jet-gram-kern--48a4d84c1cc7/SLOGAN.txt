For every power-weighted Bergman space on the upper half-plane, every pair of derivative orders separated by at least two produces a Gram kernel with zeros. The exact number of attainable scalar zeros is the gap \(d\) when \(d\) is even, and \(d+1\) or \(d-1\) when \(d\) is odd, determined solely by the sign of the higher-jet coefficient's imaginary part; every such zero is simple.
