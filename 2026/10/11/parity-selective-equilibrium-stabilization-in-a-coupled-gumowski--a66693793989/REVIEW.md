The exact odd-even stability boundary for slopes below \(-1\) resolves an otherwise hidden finite-size effect of a natural coupled-map model and yields a sharp quadratic vanishing rate for its coupling window. The source's two independently studied numerical parameter bands both contain two provably attracting homogeneous fixed points, which explains a mathematically meaningful limitation of single-attractor numerical phase maps without asserting anything about dominant basins. This is a complete exact local classification for three canonical states, not an arbitrary isolated numerical point.
