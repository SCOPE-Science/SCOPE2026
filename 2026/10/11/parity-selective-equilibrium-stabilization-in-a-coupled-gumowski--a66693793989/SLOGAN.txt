For the nearest-neighbor coupled Gumowski–Mira ring, the three explicit homogeneous equilibria have an exact, all-parameter Schur-stability classification; an odd ring can stabilize a fixed point with local slope below \(-1\), while an even ring never can. The odd-ring stabilization window is sharp and shrinks like \(N^{-2}\); the published numerical parameter regimes also contain open basins of two homogeneous sinks whenever coupling exceeds a precise threshold.
