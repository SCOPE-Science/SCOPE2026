For the quadratic Fourier graph over \(\mathbb Z/2^m\mathbb Z\), the exact sharp fourth-moment constant is \(2^{(m+1)/2}\) when \(m\) is odd and \(3\cdot2^{m/2-1}\) when \(m\) is even. Explicit coset indicators attain both bounds, whereas constant coefficients cease to be optimal from \(m=4\) onward.
