For the total three-cut complex of every \(n\)-rung ladder with \(n\ge3\), the top rational homology representation is the permutation module on pairs of ladder squares, twisted by row-exchange sign when \(n\) is odd. Its complete \(C_2\times C_2\) character is \(\big(\binom{n-1}{2},(-1)^n\binom{n-1}{2},\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor,(-1)^n\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor\big)\), distinguishing symmetry information invisible to the previously known Betti number.
