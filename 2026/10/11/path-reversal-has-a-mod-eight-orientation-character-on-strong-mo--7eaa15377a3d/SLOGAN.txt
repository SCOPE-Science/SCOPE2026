For the full strong discrete Morse matching complex of a path, reversal acts on its unique nonzero reduced rational homology group by \((-1)^k\) for \(n=4k\) edges and by \((-1)^{k+1}\) for \(n=4k+1\) edges; the other residue classes have zero reduced homology. Thus the orientation character is periodic modulo eight, refining the known nonequivariant sphere classification.
