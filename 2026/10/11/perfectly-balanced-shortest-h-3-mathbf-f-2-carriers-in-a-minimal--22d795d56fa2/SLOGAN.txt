In Lutz's minimal thirteen-vertex \(S^3\times S^3\), the \(273\) missing four-simplices are exactly the shortest nonzero mod-two \(H_3\) carriers. They split perfectly evenly among the three nonzero classes of \(H_3\cong\mathbf F_2^2\), with \(91\) carriers for each class, despite the triangulation being asymmetric.
