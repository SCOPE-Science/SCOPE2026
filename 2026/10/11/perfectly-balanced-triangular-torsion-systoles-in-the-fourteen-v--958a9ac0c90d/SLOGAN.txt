In the fourteen-vertex triangulation of \(L(5,2)\), all \(164\) nonfacial triangles are shortest integral homology generators, split perfectly \(82/82\) between the two unoriented classes of \(\mathbb Z/5\). The full simplicial symmetry group is \(C_4\) and acts on homology as the entire unit group \((\mathbb Z/5)^\times\).
