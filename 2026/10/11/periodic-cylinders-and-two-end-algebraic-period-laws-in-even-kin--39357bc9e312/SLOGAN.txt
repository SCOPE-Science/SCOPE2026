For every even \(p\ge4\), the polynomial skew flow \(\dot x=u-1\), \(\dot u=v^{p-1}\), \(\dot v=u(u-2)\) has a nonempty finite set of periodic cylinders, characterized exactly by a zero-mean axial-drift integral. Its projected periods diverge at both the degenerate center and the saddle separatrix with the same power \(h^{-(1/2-1/p)}\) and \((4/3-h)^{-(1/2-1/p)}\), but different explicit beta-function constants.
