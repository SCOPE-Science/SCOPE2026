# Review: Periodic Jacobian rigidity for holomorphic expanding maps

## Correctness
PASS. Zhang's Theorem A supplies a biholomorphic conjugacy to an affine expanding endomorphism induced by an expanding Lie-group automorphism on a complex infra-nilmanifold. A positive left-invariant top form descends because the finite holonomy acts complex linearly with real determinant \(1\). The inducing automorphism scales this form by one positive constant; integration identifies that constant with the covering degree \(d\). Pullback gives \(f^*m=d\,m\). The general Jacobian identity is then a smooth coboundary, periodic products telescope, and Oseledets gives the invariant-measure Lyapunov sum. No finite computation is used to infer an infinite statement.

Risk: the only delicate geometric point is descent through infra-nilmanifold holonomy. Finite holonomy plus holomorphicity makes every real determinant positive of finite order, hence exactly \(1\), which resolves it. No claim is made about individual return eigenvalues.

## Originality
PASS. Targeted database searches used the source title, holomorphic/infra-nilmanifold aliases, periodic Jacobian, degree, Livšic/coboundary language, periodic determinants, and Lyapunov-sum language. No located record states this combined determinant-rigidity theorem. Zhang states the smooth affine classification but not the constant-Jacobian volume or its periodic-data consequences. Shub--Franks--Gromov only provide topological classification in the general expanding setting. Spatzier--Yang concern genuinely higher-rank commuting actions. Bowen treats entropy of affine systems, not this periodic Jacobian criterion.

Residual risk: an equivalent scalar periodic-data consequence may appear as folklore or in unindexed Livšic literature. This risk is retained rather than converted into a novelty assertion stronger than the searches support.

## Value
PASS. Periodic derivative data are a central obstruction to smooth rigidity, explicitly motivating Zhang's discussion of why generic expanding maps are not smoothly conjugate to their algebraic models. The finding extracts an exact, all-period scalar rigidity law from the holomorphic case and gives a concrete obstruction: one periodic orbit with determinant different from \(d^n\) rules out every compatible holomorphic expanding realization. The same construction fixes the total Lyapunov expansion for every ergodic invariant measure. This is a structural invariant, not an arbitrary numerical slice or a recomputation of a table.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is Zhang's arXiv:2610.01525v1, whose full HTML text was inspected through Theorem A and its proof. Related smooth-classification literature for higher-rank expanding actions and Bowen's affine entropy work were compared at statement level. The result does not classify individual Lyapunov exponents, does not prove a converse holomorphic-realizability criterion, and does not claim a new entropy theorem.
