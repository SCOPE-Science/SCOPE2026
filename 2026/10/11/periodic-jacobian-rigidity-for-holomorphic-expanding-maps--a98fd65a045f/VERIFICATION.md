The finding was checked by reconstructing the volume form on the affine infra-nilmanifold supplied by arXiv:2610.01525v1. The finite holonomy acts holomorphically, so its real determinant is positive and of finite order and therefore equals \(1\). This lets a positive left-invariant top form descend. The inducing automorphism scales that form by a constant; integration over the degree-\(d\) covering identifies the constant with \(d\).

Pullback by the biholomorphic conjugacy gives \(f^*m=d\,m\). Writing \(m=e^u m_0\) verifies the smooth cohomology identity \(\log J_{m_0}f-\log d=u-u\circ f\). Summing it on a period-\(n\) orbit gives the exact determinant \(d^n\). Oseledets then gives the Lyapunov-sum identity for every ergodic invariant probability.

No numerical experiment is used. The verification does not extend to individual Lyapunov exponents or return eigenvalues, and it does not prove a converse holomorphic-realizability theorem. The main originality risk is an equivalent unindexed Jacobian-Livšic formulation.
