The cycle splitting argument proves the universal content inequality, and direct exact-distance comparisons classify all periodic orbits in the planar system. Harmonic-number bounds establish the full exponent dichotomy, not merely subsequences or finite experiments. A finite exact rational checker passed 420 diagnostics. The proof, rather than the checker, supports all universal and infinite claims.

These do not logically imply the inequality under time iteration or the explicit countable compact strict-jump construction. Related positive-critical-content and finite-index dimension findings concern different classes/invariants. Search of several equivalent phrasings found no dominating statement. The later part of the motivating preprint could not be inspected, and a result in older/unindexed work remains possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Iteration is a fundamental change of time sampling; an invariant introduced to quantify periodic geometric packing ought to be tested against it. Here the one-sided law is sharp in kind: a zero-entropy compact planar homeomorphism has dimension zero, yet its \(q\)-th power has dimension one with zero critical content. The construction gives a complete precise test case and a structural explanation via splitting clustered cycles.
