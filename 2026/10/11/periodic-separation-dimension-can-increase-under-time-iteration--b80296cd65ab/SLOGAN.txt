For every integer \(q\ge2\), periodic separation dimension is monotone under \(T\mapsto T^q\), but an explicit compact countable planar homeomorphism satisfies \(\mathfrak s(T)=0\) and \(\mathfrak s(T^q)=1\), even though its topological entropy is zero. The construction separates large individual periodic orbits into well-spaced suborbits under iteration.
