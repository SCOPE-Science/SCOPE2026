# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The four-set gap criterion is an equivalence, not a heuristic. A signed twelve-tetrahedron join chain has zero boundary, the distinguished tetrahedron belongs to no four-dimensional simplex, and its indicator cochain evaluates by a unit on the cycle. The clamping map is verified to preserve every four-set connectivity condition, proving an actual simplicial retraction at all parameters. Integral combinatorial Alexander duality transfers a split free summand to the next-to-top homology, which obstructs shellability of a pure complex. The standalone checker independently recomputes the base certificate and exhaustively checks smaller retractions.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** The closest published work proves shellability for three-cuts of cycle powers (Chauhan--Shukla, 2026) and for cut complexes of squared paths (Bayer et al., 2024/2025; Zhang--Yang, 2026). Those assertions neither imply nor contain a nonzero next-to-top integral homology class in four-cuts of cubed paths. Bayer et al.'s example of a nonshellable four-cut complex of a chordal graph is not a classification of this two-variable graph family. Direct searches for the path-power family, its Alexander dual, the degree-three witness, and shellability obstructions found no equivalent published statement. Unindexed independent results remain possible.

**Value — PASS.** This is a structural all-length obstruction at the natural transition from squared paths to cubic paths, not a routine numerical restatement or one finite counterexample. The integral direct summand and explicit retract provide a reusable homological reason for failure of higher-cut shellability. The result does not determine the remaining Betti numbers or any complete homotopy type.
