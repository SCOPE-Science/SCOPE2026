**PASS.** Invariance of the first moment gives \(Rc=m-a\). Invariance of intensity and \(|u(g)|=1\) give \((1-R^2)q=2a\operatorname{Re}m-a^2\). Subtracting \(|m|^2\) and completing the square yields the stated variance-disk identity. The packaged symbolic checker independently simplifies the difference between the two forms to zero. Boundary equality is equivalent to zero variance, hence to a Dirac invariant measure, which is invariant exactly at a fixed point.
