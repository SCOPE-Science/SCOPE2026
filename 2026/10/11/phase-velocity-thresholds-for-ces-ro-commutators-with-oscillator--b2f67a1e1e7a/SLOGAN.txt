For unimodular multipliers whose positive logistic-coordinate phase velocity is eventually monotone, and asymptotically locally unchanged when tending to zero, the commutator with the real-variable Cesàro operator is compact exactly at zero limiting velocity and Hilbert--Schmidt exactly when that velocity is also square-integrable on a tail. Power-chirps split into Hilbert--Schmidt \(0<\theta<1/2\), compact non-Hilbert--Schmidt \(1/2\le\theta<1\), and noncompact \(\theta\ge1\), with critical logarithmic energy coefficient \(1/2\).
