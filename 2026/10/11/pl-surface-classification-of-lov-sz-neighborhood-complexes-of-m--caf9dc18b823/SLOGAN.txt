The open Lovász neighborhood complex of the Möbius ladder \(M_{2n}\) is an annulus when \(n\) is even and a pair of Möbius bands when \(n\) is odd, apart from two classified small cases. The circular ladder \(P_n\) instead gives an annulus for odd \(n\), a pair of annuli for even \(n\ge6\), and two spheres at \(n=4\).
