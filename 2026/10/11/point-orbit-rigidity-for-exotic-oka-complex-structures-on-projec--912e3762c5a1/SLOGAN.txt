The one-point Oka blow-up \(\operatorname{Bl}_pX_a\) remembers its centre exactly up to automorphisms of the underlying torus-fibred threefold. Consequently, every fixed \(X_a\) produces uncountably many pairwise non-biholomorphic exotic Oka complex structures on the smooth manifold \(\mathbb P^3\), classified by the point-orbit space \(X_a/\operatorname{Aut}(X_a)\).
