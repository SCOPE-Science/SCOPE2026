For every smooth, positively curved, centrally symmetric prism \(B\times[-h,h]\), the intersection body's axial facets are exactly the scaled polar base \(h\operatorname{Vol}(B)B^\circ\), not copies of \(B\). Its side surface meets each facet along a universal \(n/2\)-order contact law with an explicit Gauss-curvature coefficient, giving the sharp dimension-dependent boundary regularity \(C^{\lceil n/2\rceil-1,\,n/2-\lceil n/2\rceil+1}\).
