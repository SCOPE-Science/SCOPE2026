In the coupled-radius angular class \(\dot s=2s(1-s)U(\theta)\), \(\dot\theta=sU(\theta)-V(\theta)\), every angularly regular annulus contains at most one off-circle periodic orbit on each side of \(s=1\). A neutral invariant circle is necessarily double, is the only periodic orbit in that annulus, and has an exact sign-definite quadratic return coefficient.
