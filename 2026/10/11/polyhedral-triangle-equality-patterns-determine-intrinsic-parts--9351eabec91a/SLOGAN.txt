For finite-dimensional real polyhedral norms, the qualitative triangle-equality relation is already a complete invariant of intrinsic-part membership: \(p\) and \(q\) share an intrinsic part exactly when \(\Delta_p\) and \(\Delta_q\) vanish on the same pairs. Finite piecewise-linearity upgrades that zero-set agreement to the uniform defect comparability required by the intrinsic Hilbert geometry.
