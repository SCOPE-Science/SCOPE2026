A positive operator \(U\) need not preserve a \(K\)-frame under the atomwise map \(f_i\mapsto f_i+Uf_i\). With \(S=I+U\), preservation of every \(K\)-frame is equivalent to \(R(K)\subseteq S R(K)\), and the optimal universal lower-bound multiplier is the largest \(\eta\) with \(\eta KK^*\le SKK^*S^*\).
