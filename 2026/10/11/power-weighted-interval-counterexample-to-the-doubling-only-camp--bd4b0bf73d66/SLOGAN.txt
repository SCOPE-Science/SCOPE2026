For the doubling power weight \(d\mu_\beta(x)=x^\beta\,dx\) on \([0,1]\), with \(1<Q<\beta+1\), the Campanato-to-Hölder embedding in the range \(Q<\lambda<Q+p\) holds exactly when \(\lambda>p+1\), even though lower \(Q\)-regularity always fails. This gives a negative answer to the doubling-only extension in Question 5 of Górka--Stępień and identifies the sharp transition on a natural model family.
