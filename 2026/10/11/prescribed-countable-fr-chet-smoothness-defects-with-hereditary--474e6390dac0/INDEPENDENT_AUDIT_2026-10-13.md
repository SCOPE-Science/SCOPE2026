## correctness

PASS

Borwein–Fabian Thm4 gives a single-defective-line Gâteaux-smooth equivalent norm q. Rotating q via Hilbert isometries gives q_j with uniform bounds m||x||≤q_j(x)≤M||x|| and prescribed exceptional lines; P=Σw_j q_j is a norm and Gâteaux smooth because derivative series uniformly bounded by M. Bregman remainders R_j(x,h)=q_j(x+h)−q_j(x)−Dq_j(x)h≥0 and ≤2M||h|| show exact union of non-Fréchet loci: any bad summand persists, and if all are good dominated tail convergence makes P good. Finite-head norms Q_N=Σ_{j≤N}w_jq_j+2^(-N)||·||_2 have finite bad locus, hereditary MIP via Bandyopadhyay–Gothwal Thm6, and uniform norm/derivative gap ≤(M+1)2^(-N). On every closed Y dim≥2, dense Fréchet support functionals for Q_N|Y approximate any P|Y dual unit functional; continuity at a Q_N smooth support point and Baire avoidance of countably many lines choose z where P is Fréchet smooth; derivative uniform control transfers density, implying MIP via BG Thm2(a). One-dimensional subspaces trivially have MIP. Radial normalization is a sphere homeomorphism, hence a countably dense prescribed direction set remains dense on P-sphere. This is an infinite analytic proof, not extrapolation from finite N.
