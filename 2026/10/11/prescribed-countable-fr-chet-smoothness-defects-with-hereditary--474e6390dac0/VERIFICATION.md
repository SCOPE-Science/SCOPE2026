# Verification of the mathematical statement

The reference norm is the real Hilbert norm. The 1993 source theorem provides a globally Gâteaux-differentiable equivalent norm with one non-Fréchet-differentiability line; the 2026 source's finite-exception and norming-density results were inspected in their full primary text.

The proof independently checks the exact defect set using nonnegative convexity remainders and a uniform summable majorant. For the infinite case, it derives the dual-map bound \(\|DP(x)-DQ_N(x)\|_{2,*}\le(M+1)2^{-N}\) for **every** nonzero \(x\), and uses continuity of the finite-head duality map at a Fréchet-smooth point. The complement of a countable collection of proper closed lines is dense by Baire; the perturbed point is therefore Fréchet smooth for the limiting norm. Convergence of the dual unit norms and the derivative bound yield the required norming-functional density on each closed subspace.
