For the four-point affine simplex in \(\mathbb F_p^3\), the smallest number of distinct character frequencies giving an exact tight frame is \(4\), \(6\), or \(p\), according as \(p=2\), \(p=3\), or \(p\ge5\). In the exceptional ternary case, all \(27\) six-frequency optimizers are translates of one explicit set: a complete rigid classification of the smallest redundant Fourier design.
