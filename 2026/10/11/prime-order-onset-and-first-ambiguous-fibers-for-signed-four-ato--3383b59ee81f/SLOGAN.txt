For four-atom signals with alphabet \(\{-1,0,1\}\) on prime cyclic groups, Fourier magnitudes determine the signal up to shift, reflection, and global sign precisely at prime orders \(5\) and \(7\), while every prime order \(p\ge11\) admits a genuine ambiguity. At the sharp onset \(p=11\), all five ambiguous power spectra are explicitly classified and each has exactly two inequivalent signal classes.
