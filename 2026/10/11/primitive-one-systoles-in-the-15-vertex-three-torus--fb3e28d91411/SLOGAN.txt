In the Kühnel--Lassmann 15-vertex three-torus, the integral simple one-systole has length \(3\), and all \(275\) minimizers are primitive missing-triangle boundaries. Up to sign they occupy exactly seven classes of \(H_1\cong\mathbb Z^3\), with multiplicities \(45,45,45,45,45,45,5\); the exceptional five-cycle class is fixed by the full order-\(120\) symmetry group.
