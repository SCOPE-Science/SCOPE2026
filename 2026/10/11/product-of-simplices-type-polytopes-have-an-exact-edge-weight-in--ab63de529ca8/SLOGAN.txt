For the normalized edge-weight type polytope of \(\Delta_{d_1}\times\cdots\times\Delta_{d_k}\), the unique incenter weights factor \(i\) by \(\sqrt{(D-d_i)/d_i}\), and the exact inradius is \(D^{3/2}\sqrt{V/2}/\sum_i\sqrt{d_i(D-d_i)}\). With at least three factors, the type simplex is Euclidean-regular exactly when all factor dimensions agree.
