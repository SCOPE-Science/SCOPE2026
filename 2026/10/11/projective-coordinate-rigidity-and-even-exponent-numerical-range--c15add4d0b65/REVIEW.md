# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof identifies the unique normalized duality map of complex \(\ell_p^n\), reduces multivalued blow-up to a scalar annihilation condition, and proves independence of distinct projective coordinate restrictions outside the even integers by real-analyticity at the kernel of one form. At even exponents, monomial coefficient comparison yields the exact nullspace rank. The binomial construction uses a valid polynomial finite-difference cancellation, with strict positivity at the larger even exponents. Dimensions one and zero coordinate restrictions are included. Finite symbolic tests supplement, but never replace, the general proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
The numerical-range definition, compact/full-plane alternative, and adjoint spectral inclusion are explicit prior results of Boubaker–Gernandt–Selmi (2026). The nonpolynomial ridge-function uniqueness principle and even-exponent special behavior are also known themes; no originality is assigned to them in isolation. The new contribution is the exact multivalued-part classification and dimension formulas, together with a fixed relation whose bounded numerical-range exponents are exactly the first \(k\) positive even integers. Targeted published-record and primary-literature comparisons identified no covering claim. The full Pinkus and Ammar–Jeribi chapters were not available and remain an access risk.

## Value — PASS
The result answers a natural, previously undecided quantitative question left by the finite-dimensional relation dichotomy: which multivalued subspaces are permitted, and of what maximal dimension? The contrast between projective-coordinate rank and polynomial rank at even exponents, along with arbitrarily long exact exceptional lists and the all-spectrum example, gives a structural rather than cosmetic answer. There is no assertion for arbitrary renormings, endpoints, or all realizable exponent subsets.
