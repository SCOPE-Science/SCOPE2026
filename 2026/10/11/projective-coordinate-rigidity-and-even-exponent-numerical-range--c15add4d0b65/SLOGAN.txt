For finite-dimensional complex \(\ell_p\)-relations, the maximum multivalued dimension compatible with a bounded numerical range is exactly computable from projective coordinate classes when \(p\) is not even, and from a finite polynomial rank at even exponents. A binomial finite-difference family has numerical range \(\{0\}\) exactly at \(p=2,4,\ldots,2k\), and the whole complex plane at every other \(1<p<\infty\).
