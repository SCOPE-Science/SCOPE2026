For a cellular graph \(G\subset\mathbb{RP}^2\), the Boyle--Spyropoulos projective rank is exactly the frustration index of the ambient cohomology signing and exactly the shortest homologically nontrivial cycle length in the dual graph. Equivalently, it is the minimum distance from a lifted dual vertex to its deck mate in the spherical double cover, turning the alternating-link Jones-piece breadth deficit into a dual systole.
