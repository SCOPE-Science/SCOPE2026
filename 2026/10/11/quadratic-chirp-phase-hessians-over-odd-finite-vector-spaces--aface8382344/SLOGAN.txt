For every nondegenerate quadratic chirp on an odd-prime vector space, the complete real phase-autocorrelation Hessian has eigenvalues \(8p^d\sin^2(2\pi Q(h)/p)\); its nullity is exactly the number of isotropic vectors of the quadratic form. Such chirps are locally rigid modulo global phase exactly in anisotropic ranks, while every isotropic line produces a genuine \((p-1)\)-torus of perfect phase deformations.
