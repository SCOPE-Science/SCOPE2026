In every symmetric Minkowski plane, the Baronti–Bertella constant \(H_2\) is exactly the largest norm distortion of the coordinate rotations through angles up to \(\pi/4\). For every affine regular \(4n\)-gonal unit circle this gives \(H_2=\sec(\pi/(4n))\), including \(H_2=\sqrt{4-2\sqrt2}\) for the regular octagon.
