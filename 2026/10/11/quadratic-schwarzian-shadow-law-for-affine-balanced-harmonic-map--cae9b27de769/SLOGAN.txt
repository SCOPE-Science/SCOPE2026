For affine-balanced harmonic mappings with a fixed positive pre-Schwarzian budget, the optimal Schwarzian gap between the harmonic map and its analytic part is of exact order \(k^2\) as the balanced dilatation \(k\to0\). An explicit universal upper bound and the balanced cubic shear \(z+(k/3)\overline z^{\,3}\) give matching quadratic order, partially resolving the Schwarzian-shadow problem posed for affine balancing.
