Every transcendental linearly non-degenerate holomorphic curve of order \(\rho<1\) has a positive quantitative Wronskian-zero density: \(\limsup N_1/T\ge J(\rho)^{-1}\), where \(J(\rho)=\int_0^\infty\max\{1,u^\rho\}(1+u)^{-2}\,du\). At order \(1/2\), this gives the explicit bound \(4/(4+\pi)\), while order zero recovers the recent sharp constant \(1\).
