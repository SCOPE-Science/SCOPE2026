The rectangle mechanism behind the symmetric dual Brunn--Minkowski counterexamples does not become merely second-order neutral at the sharp endpoint: it stabilizes exactly at fourth order, with \(\widetilde V_4(R_t)/\widetilde V_4(R_0)=1-t^4\). Under the source's thin-box reduction this yields the dimension-\(n\) endpoint deficit \(1-(1-t^4)^{1/(n+2)}\) in the degeneration limit.
