The regular representation of \(H(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)\) splits by representation degree into central Ramanujan projectors: the degree-\(m\) layer has kernel \(c_m/n\) on the center and sharp endpoint norm \(2^{\omega(m)}\varphi(m)/m\). Hence a degree layer is endpoint-contractive exactly when \(m\) is a power of two.
