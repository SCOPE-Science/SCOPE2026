# Review

## Correctness
PASS. A good triangulation of the coarse space exists by the primary source. In top degree, every codimension-one simplex contributes either a two-generator relation \(x_i=\pm x_j\) or, at a reflection wall, a one-generator relation \(x_i=0\). Componentwise signed-graph reduction therefore gives only \(\mathbb Z\), \(\mathbb Z/2\), or zero summands in \(H^5\), so its \(2\)-primary torsion exponent is at most \(2\). The source's Proposition 8.7 then gives rank at most \(8\). The free-point connected sum preserves the source's rank-two obstruction and allows the rank-four bundle to glue across trivialized sphere boundaries.
