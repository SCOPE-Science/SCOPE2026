Every closed smooth five-orbifold with stabilizers of order at most two admits a complex regular bundle of rank at most \(8\): the coarse top cohomology has only free and elementary \(2\)-torsion. For the source's open nonorientable both-stabilizer problem, the sharp bound is therefore either \(4\) or \(8\), with rank \(4\) attained by a free-point connected-sum example.
