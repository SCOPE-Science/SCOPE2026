For a bounded positive operator and a rank-one positive input, crossed-Heinz operators have at least the ordinary Heinz trace norm, but no greater Hilbert–Schmidt norm; their squared defects are exactly a pair of spectral covariance formulas. If the supporting spectral measure has at least three points and \(t\ne1/2\), this comparison reverses at a unique Schatten exponent \(p_*\in(1,2)\), with an exact three-dimensional witness.
