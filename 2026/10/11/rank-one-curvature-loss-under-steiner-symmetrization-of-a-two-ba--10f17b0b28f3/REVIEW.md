PASS. Each positive-length vertical lens fiber has endpoints from two spherical caps. On a pure branch the symmetral has spherical curvature 1/R. On a mixed upper branch h=(f1+f2)/2+C, -D²fi=(Wi/R)(I+pi pi^T) and first/second fundamental forms are I(v,v)=|v|²+(p·v)² and II(v,v)=-D²h[v,v]/W. Reflection handles lower boundary and C² continuity handles regular branch seams and vertical tangencies. Published 3D explicit deficient normal curvature example proves the one-direction allowance sharp, with higher-dimensional embeddings retaining that direction. Numerical 16,000-sample logs were NOT substituted for this analytic proof.
