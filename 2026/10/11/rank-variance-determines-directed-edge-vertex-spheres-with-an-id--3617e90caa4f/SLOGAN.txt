For every connected graded quiver, the centered rank variance determines the unique Euclidean sphere through all directed-edge vertices: its center is \(-r/\|r\|^2\) and its squared radius is \(2-1/\|r\|^2\). This sphere is the minimum enclosing sphere exactly when every lower ideal has nonpositive centered-rank sum, equivalently when the sphere center is realized by a nonnegative unit flow on the arrows.
