For the ordinary open-neighborhood complex of the fifteen-vertex Kneser graph \(\mathrm{KG}(6,2)\), the natural \(\mathfrak S_6\)-action on rational second homology splits into the two distinct irreducible Specht representations \(S^{(3,1,1,1)}\oplus S^{(2,2,1,1)}\). This refines the previously known non-equivariant homotopy type of nineteen two-spheres by specifying how all six-point coordinate permutations act on those nineteen homology generators.
