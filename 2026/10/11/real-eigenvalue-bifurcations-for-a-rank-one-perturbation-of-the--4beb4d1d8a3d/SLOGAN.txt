For the normal real-variable Cesàro operator \(C\) on \(L^2(0,1)\), the canonical rank-one perturbation \(C+\tau P\) has a completely classified real point spectrum with exact coupling transitions \(\tau_-=-8/(\pi^2-4)\), \(-2\), and \(\tau_+=8/(\pi^2+4)\). At \(\tau=-2\), the eigenvalue crosses through the essential-spectrum point \(0\), with eigenfunction \(1-x\) and sharp slope \(3/2\) on both sides.
