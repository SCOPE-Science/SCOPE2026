For every finite connected simple cactus graph \(X\) with \(b_1(X)\ge2\), the new degree-one algebraic gnarledness is exactly reciprocal girth: \(G_1^F(C_*^\Delta(X))=1/g(X)\) for every subfield \(F\subset\mathbb R\). Since geometric degree-one gnarledness is \(1\), the algebraic/geometric gap factor is exactly \(g(X)\).
