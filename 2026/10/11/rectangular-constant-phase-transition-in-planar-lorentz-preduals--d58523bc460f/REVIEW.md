# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The unit sphere has only axial faces, diagonal faces, and vertices; all three cases are treated. Two affine supporting-function lower bounds give a sharp pointwise envelope for each of the two orthogonality cones at a vertex. The exact one-variable quotients are respectively strictly increasing and strictly decreasing in the direction parameter, and the two maximum values are attained at explicitly verified unit vertex-to-vertex moves. Smooth face interiors have strict suboptimal bounds, establishing the complete ordered-triple classification.

## Originality
PASS. Full primary texts for Paul--Ghosh--Sain (2014/2017) and Baronti--Casini--Papini (2021/2022) were inspected. The former reduces all two-dimensional polyhedral rectangular computations to vertex starting points but gives no explicit Lorentz-predual profile; the latter treats hexagonal geometry and ell-p bounds. Kim (2013) studies symmetric bilinear forms on exactly the same Lorentz-predual norm, not the Joly quotient. An unavailable 1977 two-dimensional study remains an explicit residual risk, as does an unindexed differently named calculation. Searches and comparisons of related records distinguish invariants rather than merely titles or matching numbers.

## Value
PASS. The result completely identifies the family’s classical rectangular constant, its unique minimizing parameter, and the change of the sharp Birkhoff--James geometry at the regular octagon. The exact normalized extremizer classification provides information unavailable from a general vertex-reduction theorem or a numerically coincident neighboring constant.

## Closest literature and limits
The closest prior theory is the 2014 general polyhedral vertex-reduction theorem and the 2021 exact-constant survey. Kim’s 2013 bilinear-form study concerns the same norm but a different functional. The theorem is real and two-dimensional only; the 1977 original body was not accessible. Numerical tests are only exploratory and not part of the proof.
