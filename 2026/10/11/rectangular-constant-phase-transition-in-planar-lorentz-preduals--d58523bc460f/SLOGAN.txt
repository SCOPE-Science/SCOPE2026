For the planar Lorentz-predual norm \(N_w(u,v)=\max\{|u|,|v|,(|u|+|v|)/(1+w)\}\), Joly's rectangular constant equals \(1+2\max\{w,(1-w)/(1+w)\}\) for every \(0<w<1\). Its unique minimum \(2\sqrt2-1\) occurs at the regular octagon \(w=\sqrt2-1\), where the maximizing configuration switches between diagonal and axial edges.
