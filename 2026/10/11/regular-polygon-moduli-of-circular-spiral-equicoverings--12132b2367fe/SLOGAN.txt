For normalized arclength on the unit circle, every convex \((3k,k+1)\)-spiral equicovering is encoded by a regular \(3k\)-gon of feasible centers. Its inradius is \(\cos(\pi(k+1)/(3k))\), its circumradius is \(\frac12-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\tan(\pi/(3k))\), and these center polygons converge to the disk of radius \(1/2\).
