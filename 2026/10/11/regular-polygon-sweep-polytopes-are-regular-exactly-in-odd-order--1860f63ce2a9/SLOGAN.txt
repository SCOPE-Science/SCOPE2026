The sweep polytope of a unit regular \(n\)-gon is a regular \(2n\)-gon exactly when \(n\) is odd, in which case the sweep construction doubles the Euclidean symmetry order. For even \(n\), it is instead a cyclic equiangular \(2n\)-gon with alternating side lengths \(2\cot(\pi/n)\) and \(2\csc(\pi/n)\), with exact perimeter and area.
