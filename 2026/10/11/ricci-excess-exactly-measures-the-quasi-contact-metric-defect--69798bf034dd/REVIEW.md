# Same-model review

## Correctness

PASS. The primary source proves
\[
d\eta-2\Phi=-2g(\phi h\,\cdot,\cdot)
\]
under the Killing hypothesis and also
\[
\operatorname{Ric}(\xi,\xi)=2n+\|h\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2.
\]
The metric norm of a two-form represented by a skew operator \(K\) is
\[
\|\omega_K\|^2=\frac12\|K\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2.
\]
Since \(\phi\) is orthogonal on the horizontal distribution, this gives
\[
\|d\eta-2\Phi\|^2=2\|h\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2.
\]
The defect is \(\phi\)-anti-invariant while \(\Phi\) is \(\phi\)-invariant, so they are orthogonal. Together with \(\|\Phi\|^2=n\), this proves both norm identities and the angle law with the stated factors.

## Originality

PASS. The full motivating preprint states the defect tensor identity and the characteristic Ricci identity separately, and uses the latter as a qualitative \(K\)-contact criterion. It does not state the exact norm--Ricci identity, the total norm of \(d\eta\), or the compatibility-angle formula. Exact-source and equivalent-formulation searches found no covering published statement. The closest earlier Killing quasi-contact paper was only partially accessible directly; the 2026 source compares its equations in detail and identifies an error in its qualitative theorem.

Residual risk: the final derivation is concise, so an older equivalent observation could exist under a different exterior-derivative normalization or unindexed terminology.

## Value

PASS. The new strict examples make the size of contact-metric failure a natural invariant rather than a formal exercise. The theorem turns the source's qualitative Ricci equality criterion into an exact quantitative identity valid pointwise in every dimension under the Killing hypothesis. It also gives the source deformation parameter a direct intrinsic angular meaning.

Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.

## Closest literature and limitations

The closest source is Cappelletti-Montano--De Nicola, arXiv:2610.02073v1. The closest earlier same-hypothesis paper is Bae--Jang--Park--Sekigawa, DOI:10.4134/BKMS.b190981. Rovenski's 2024 weak quasi-contact paper provides a broader curvature framework but not the displayed norm identity.

The theorem requires a Killing characteristic field and uses the ordinary quasi-contact equations. It does not extend automatically to non-Killing or weak quasi-contact structures.
