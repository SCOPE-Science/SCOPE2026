# Verification

For the counterexample, let \(S\) be the unilateral right shift on \(\ell^2(\mathbb N)\). Then
\[
S^*S=I
\]
so \(S^*\) is a norm-one left inverse, while
\[
SS^*=I-P_{\operatorname{span}\{e_1\}}
\]
so \(S\) is not surjective. With \(F=(e_j)\), \(m=2\), and \(U_1=U_2=S\), the source perturbation size is exactly zero. Yet
\[
\sum_{j\ge1}|\langle e_1,Se_j\rangle|^2=0,
\]
so \(SF\) is not a frame.

For the repaired theorem, \(U_kV=I\) implies
\[
V^*U_k^*=I
\]
and hence
\[
\|U_k^*f\|\ge\|V\|^{-1}\|f\|.
\]
For an arbitrary partition, the baseline coefficient vector generated by \(U_kF\) has norm at least
\[
\frac{\sqrt A}{\|V\|}\|f\|.
\]
The difference between the actual weaving coefficient vector and this baseline satisfies
\[
\|\text{error}\|^2
\le
B(m-1)\delta^2\|f\|^2.
\]
Applying the reverse triangle inequality gives the claimed lower bound, and the ordinary triangle inequality gives the claimed upper bound.
