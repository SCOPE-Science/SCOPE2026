In the classical octagonal family \(N_\beta(u,v)=\max\{|u|,|v|,\beta(|u|+|v|)\}\), the known modified von Neumann--Jordan value has a rigid equality set that changes type at \(\beta=1/\sqrt2\). Below the threshold extremizers are coordinate-reflected vertex pairs; above it they are coordinate-swapped vertex pairs, and at the regular octagon both classes occur.
