The focal square proof was reconstructed separately for even and odd orders.

For even \(m\), choosing \(b=\sqrt{A/B}\) turns equality in the sharp product inequality into equality in the entire chain
\[
0\le\|\mathcal E_{b,c_e}v\|_2^2\le A+b^2B-b(2c_e-N+2)C=0.
\]
The difference between the two square expressions is the nonnegative angular term generated by
\[
|x|^2|\nabla v|^2-(x\cdot\nabla v)^2.
\]
Its vanishing forces radiality.

For odd \(m\), equality gives \(\mathcal O_{b,c_o}v=0\). Defining \(w=\Delta v+bc_ov\) yields \(\nabla w=-bx\Delta v\); tangential projection forces \(w\), then \(\Delta v\), then \(v\) to be radial because \(c_o>0\). With \(v'=ry\), direct symbolic differentiation gives
\[
y''+\left(\frac{N+1}{r}+br\right)y'+b(N+c_o)y=0.
\]
The confluent-hypergeometric parameters reduce exactly to
\[
\frac{N-c_e}{2}=\frac{N-s}{4},\qquad
\frac{2-c_o}{2}=\frac{N+2-s}{4},
\]
where \(s=\sqrt{N^2+4(m-2)(N-1)}\).
