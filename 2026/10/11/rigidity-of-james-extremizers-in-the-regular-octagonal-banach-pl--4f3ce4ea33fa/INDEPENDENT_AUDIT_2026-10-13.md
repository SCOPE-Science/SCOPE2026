## correctness

PASS

The unit sphere is the eight edges of |u|<=1, |v|<=1, |u|+|v|<=sqrt(2). The dihedral group sends the first unit x to (1,t), 0<=t<=sqrt(2)-1; y may be put in the upper half-plane by whole-vector sign. For y on right vertical, upper-right diagonal, left vertical and upper-left diagonal pieces, one of the two chord norms is <sqrt(2), except the common endpoint that belongs to top edge. On top y=(a,1), -r<=a<=r, the two conditions N(x+y)>=sqrt(2) and N(x-y)>=sqrt(2) simplify respectively to a+t>=0 and a+t<=0. Therefore a=-t and y=Qx. Dihedral conjugation sends Q to Q or -Q, and direct substitution gives both norms sqrt(2). This is exhaustive continuous boundary algebra rather than finite sampling.
