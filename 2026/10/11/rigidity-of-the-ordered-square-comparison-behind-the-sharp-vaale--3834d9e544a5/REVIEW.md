# Same-model review

## Correctness

PASS. The proof reconstructs the source ordered-square substitution and tracks both possible losses. For \(q_1>1\), the normalized Jacobian is strictly below one throughout the positive ordered chamber. If \(q_1=1\), the exact summation-by-parts identity expresses the exponent excess as a sum of nonnegative prefix defects times strictly positive ordered-square gaps. Equality therefore forces all prefix defects to vanish, hence every \(q_j=1\). The source orthoscheme reduction has \(q_j=\beta_j^2\), so the local equality shape is the standard cube flag orthoscheme. The non-rigid \(n=1\) case is excluded explicitly.

Karasev's precursor says only that the cube attains equality in dimensions two and three. Targeted published-finding corpus, primary-literature, and exact-term searches found no statement implying the local if-and-only-if result.

## Closest literature and limitations

The closest primary sources are arXiv:2609.27798v1 and arXiv:2505.16247v1. The first supplies the inequality and the transformation used here; the second supplies the earlier low-dimensional surface theorem.

The result is local to the Gaussian/orthoscheme comparison. It does not prove that the cube is the unique global equality polytope, and it gives no quantitative deficit estimate. The normalized one-dimensional comparison is an identity for every admissible parameter.
