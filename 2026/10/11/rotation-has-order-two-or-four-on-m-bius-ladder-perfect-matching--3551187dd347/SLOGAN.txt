For every even \(n\ge4\), one-step rotation of the Möbius ladder acts on the two-dimensional rational homology of its perfect-matching complex with trace zero and square \((-1)^{n/2-1}I\). The induced rotation has exact order two when \(n\equiv2\pmod4\) and exact order four when \(n\equiv0\pmod4\), although the underlying graph rotation has order \(2n\).
