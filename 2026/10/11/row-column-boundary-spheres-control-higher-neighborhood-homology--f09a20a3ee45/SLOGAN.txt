For every rook graph on an \(m\)-by-\(n\) board with \(m,n\ge3\), collapsing its complete-edge, row-boundary and column-boundary core leaves precisely \(mn((m-1)(n-1)-1)\) two-spheres. All integral homology in degrees at least three comes from oriented row and column boundaries, with an explicit \(S_m\times S_n\) sign-permutation action.
