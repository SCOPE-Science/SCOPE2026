For every finite connected triangle-free graph \(H\), the scale-three vertex-centered intrinsic Čech nerve of \(L(S(H))\) collapses simplicially onto its scale-two Čech nerve; both have the homotopy type of \(H\). The scale-one-to-two map kills exactly the local clique-cycle lattices, whereas scale two to three preserves every integral homology class.
