For the incidence graph of every finite thick generalized quadrangle of order \((s,t)\), the inclusive scale-three Vietoris–Rips complex is \(\bigvee^{s^4t^4}S^3\). The scale-three complex splits as a join of two clique complexes, and each factor retracts to the original incidence graph with cycle rank \(s^2t^2\).\n
