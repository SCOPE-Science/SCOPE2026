# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
For every integer \(n\ge5\), the proof enumerates the entire closed-neighborhood complex, gives \(4n\) elementary collapses, identifies a cyclic graph of tetrahedral boundaries, and derives the integral homotopy type. A spanning-tree argument proves the precise integral cycle-kernel statement. The standalone verifier checks every free face for ten sample orders and separately recalculates mod-two boundary ranks. The sample computations corroborate, rather than establish, the general theorem.

## Originality — PASS, with bibliographic limitation
The closest article Adams–Shukla–Singh, arXiv:2212.05871v1, supplies the construction and hypercube results. Matsushita, arXiv:2511.12608, treats the same closed-neighborhood functor, but the inspected general theorems do not classify this cyclic family. Shen et al., arXiv:2512.09674, treat open Lovász neighborhoods of circular ladders and thus different simplicial complexes. Prior scale-two circular-ladder Vietoris–Rips work has different facets and a different homotopy type. Published-result comparisons did not reveal an exact or dominating statement, but an unindexed equivalent may exist.

## Value — PASS
The cycle of square faces is the first canonical obstruction to high-girth Čech recovery, making the graph family and scale mathematically natural. The complete integral \(S^1\)-plus-\(n\)-spheres classification and the exact integral inclusion kernel explain homological creation and destruction for all \(n\ge5\), beyond a finite list of Betti numbers.

## Scope and limitations
Only intrinsic closed balls centered on graph vertices are included. This is not a theorem about all-point metric-graph Čech or Euclidean Čech complexes. No all-scales barcode is claimed, and no independent audit has been conducted. Possible equivalent literature not retrieved remains a risk.

Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
