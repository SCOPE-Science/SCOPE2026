For every circular ladder \(C_n\square K_2\) with \(n\ge5\), its scale-two intrinsic graph Čech nerve admits \(4n\) elementary collapses and has homotopy type \(S^1\vee\bigvee^{n}S^2\). The inclusion from scale one kills exactly the \(n\) independent quadrilateral face cycles in integral first homology, and preserves the longitudinal circle.
