The scale-two intrinsic Čech complexes of the two strongly regular graphs with parameters \((16,6,2,2)\) have homotopy types \(\bigvee^{23}S^2\) (Shrikhande) and \(\bigvee^{63}S^2\) (rook). Thus their topological Čech invariant distinguishes these cospectral graphs, although their standard strongly regular parameters coincide.
