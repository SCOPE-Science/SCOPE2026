The included code verifies exact free-face deletions for six instances, including width five; the proof supplies all quantifiers.

The metric-gluing theorem does not yield the explicit current scale-two collapse. The closest same-direction width-two circular-ladder finding, which is acknowledged, fixes the fiber width and does not cover any case of the new domain \(m\ge3\). Targeted exact and alias searches did not locate a stronger theorem, but this cannot rule out an unindexed or implicit description.

This unbounded-dimensional reduction and the exact \(n(m-1)\)-rank death in persistence are structurally motivated by persistent topology of cyclic graph products, not a fixed numerical example. The variable-width result requires a separate domination and exceptional-edge analysis and is not logically implied by width-two behavior.

**Limits and closest literature.** The theorem uses graph-vertex metrics, \(n\ge7\), and thresholds one and two. It makes no assertion on larger thresholds or smaller cycles. Direct supporting literature is Saleh–Titz Mite–Witzel (2023), Adamaszek et al. (2018), and the prior width-two finding.
