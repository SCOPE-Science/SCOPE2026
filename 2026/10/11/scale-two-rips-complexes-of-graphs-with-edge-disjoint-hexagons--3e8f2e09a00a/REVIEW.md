# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The full all-parameter proof classifies the two alternating triangles per edge-disjoint hexagon as the only faces outside the closed-neighborhood union, proves none lies in a larger clique, collapses the union onto the graph, and cancels one of the two resulting disks against a distinct edge of each hexagon. This produces the claimed graph wedge with two-spheres. Exact finite characteristic-two and characteristic-three checks verify sample homology and full simplex decompositions; they are supplementary only.

## Originality — PASS, subject to literature coverage

Adamaszek (arXiv:1104.0433) proves the girth-seven theorem and calculates individual six-cycle examples. The SoCG 2018 metric-gluing decomposition has path-length restrictions failing the noncactus theta graph with three- and four-edge branches; Kawamura's square-graph results concern distinct bipartite homological comparisons. Indexed mathematical searches did not identify the unrestricted edge-disjoint-six-cycle wedge formula. Equivalence in unindexed literature cannot be categorically excluded.

## Value — PASS

A natural class of graphs with multiple sparse six-cycles and arbitrarily many long cycles admits a complete integral homotopy classification. The mixed-dimensional theta example is structurally outside known girth-seven graph-power collapses and sufficiently short metric-gluing regimes. This identifies how each six-cycle replaces one independent loop by a 2-sphere.

## Limitations

No statement is made for graphs with two six-cycles sharing an edge, for scales other than two, or for an entire Rips filtration. The finite computations do not certify all possible parameter values. Independent review remains outstanding.
