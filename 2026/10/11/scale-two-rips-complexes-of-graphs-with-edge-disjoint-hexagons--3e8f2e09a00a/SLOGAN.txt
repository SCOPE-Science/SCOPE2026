For a finite connected graph of girth at least six whose simple hexagons share no edges, the inclusive scale-two graph-metric Rips complex is \(\bigvee^{b-t}S^1\vee\bigvee^t S^2\), where \(b\) is the graph cycle rank and \(t\) its number of hexagons. Unlike the girth-seven collapse theorem, this covers graphs with simultaneous six- and seven-cycles and can produce mixed-dimensional homotopy, as for the theta graph with path lengths \(3,3,4\).
