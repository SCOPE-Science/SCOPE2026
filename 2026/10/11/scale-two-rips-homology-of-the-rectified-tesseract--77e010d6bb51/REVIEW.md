# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The exact certificate enumerates all faces, verifies the complete Hasse-cover orientation is acyclic, and leaves one critical \(0\)-cell, one critical \(3\)-cell, and eight critical \(5\)-cells. Since no critical cells occur in adjacent dimensions, the integral Morse cellular differentials vanish. The full mod-two boundary calculation independently reproduces the two nonzero reduced Betti ranks.

## Value
PASS. This gives the exact integral homology at the first interior scale for a canonical uniform four-polytope and identifies a mixed-dimensional behavior absent from the scale-two Rips complexes of the original hypercube graphs. The result is a concrete structural boundary for proposed extensions of known polyhedral and hypercube Rips calculations.

## Closest literature and limitations
