Let \(R=L(Q_4)\) be the line graph of the four-dimensional hypercube graph, equipped with its shortest-path metric, and let \(K=\mathrm{VR}_{\le 2}(R)\). Then \(\widetilde H_3(K;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z\), \(\widetilde H_5(K;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^8\), and all other reduced integral homology groups vanish. In particular, the scale-two Rips complex of the rectified tesseract has genuinely mixed-dimensional homology and cannot be homotopy equivalent to a wedge of spheres all in one dimension. This gives a natural four-dimensional rectification benchmark at the first interior graph-distance scale, contrasting with the single-degree scale-two homology of the original four-cube.
