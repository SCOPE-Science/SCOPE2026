The inclusive scale-two Rips complexes of the finite prism graphs \(C_n\square K_2\) pass through a point, \(S^3\), \(S^2\), and a threefold wedge of \(S^2\) for \(n=3,4,5,6\), then collapse simplicially onto one rim circle for every \(n\ge7\). A nine-orbit free-face sequence proves the stable case uniformly in \(n\), while exact small-case collapses establish the exceptional threshold.
