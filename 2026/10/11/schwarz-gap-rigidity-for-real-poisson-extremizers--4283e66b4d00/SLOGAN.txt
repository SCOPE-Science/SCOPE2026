For real zero-mean boundary data, the sharp finite-radius Poisson \(L^\infty\to L^p\) norm has no hidden extremizers: every normalized extremizer is a rotation of \(\operatorname{sgn}\cos\theta\). More sharply, the norm is bounded by the strip extremal at the contracted radius \(q_r(f)r\), where \(q_r(f)\) is the maximal Schwarz quotient of the subordinate disk map.
