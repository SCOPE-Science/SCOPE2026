For the Tutte–Coxeter graph, every ordered distance-\(3\) endpoint pair contributes exactly four dimensions to \(\operatorname{MH}_{3,5}(-;\mathbb F_2)\), while adjacent endpoint pairs contribute zero. Consequently \(\dim_{\mathbb F_2}\operatorname{MH}_{3,5}=1440\), exactly at the girth-eight boundary of the known second-off-diagonal vanishing range.
