The Koumandos--Ruscheweyh critical curve has an explicit second-order law at the small-parameter endpoint: \(\nu(\rho)=\kappa\rho+\beta\rho^2+O(\rho^3)\), with \(\kappa=\pi/\operatorname{Si}(\pi)\) and an exact integral formula for \(\beta\). This quantifies the first departure from the limiting root ratio proved in the recent all-parameter theorem.
