## Correctness
PASS. The source layer functional and connected semi-balanced dimension theorem give the affine hull exactly. The centered layer vector is orthogonal to its translation space, so its normalized form is the projection of the ambient origin. Every root vector has squared norm \(2\) and layer increment \(1\), giving the common radius \(2-1/s\). A self-contained common-sphere lemma proves minimum-enclosing optimality exactly when the center is in the convex hull, and strict separation proves strict improvement otherwise. In the standard bipartite orientation, convex-hull membership is a uniform-marginal transportation problem whose max-flow/min-cut inequalities are precisely \(p|N(X)|\ge q|X|\).

PASS. The result gives a clean metric structure for the entire semi-balanced family rather than for a small example. It also identifies exactly when the obvious all-vertex sphere is genuinely the smallest one, connecting Euclidean root-polytope geometry to balanced flow feasibility. The proportional Hall specialization is a natural graph-theoretic boundary, and the biregular corollary yields a broad exact-radius class.
