PASS. The proof uses Beeson's exact formulas in Theorems 18--21 and Lemma 15. Additivity of \(\Omega\) gives the first two lower bounds immediately. In the third case, the published integrality of \(\lambda^2/b\) makes the \(\Omega=2\) equality criterion exact. The arithmetic examples are checked only as points satisfying the necessary formulas, not as tilings.

Targeted published-finding corpus, web, primary-full-text, and own-ledger searches found no covering statement.
