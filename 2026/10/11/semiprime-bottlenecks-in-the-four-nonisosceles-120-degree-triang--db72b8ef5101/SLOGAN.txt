Among the four nonisosceles incommensurable \(120^\circ\)-tile families in Beeson's prime-tiling theorem, two cannot have semiprime tile counts at all, while the other two admit semiprimes only through exact arithmetic gates. The surviving gates are \(\lambda^2=b\) with \(\Omega(a+b)=2\), or \(\lambda=1\) with both \(a+2b\) and \(2a+b\) prime.\n
