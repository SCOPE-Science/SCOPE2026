A complete, acyclic discrete-Morse certificate upgrades the previously tabulated homology of the seven initial circular-ladder total three-cut complexes to exact wedge-of-spheres **homotopy types**. For \(4\le n\le10\), the nonzero wedge ranks are \(9,14,22,29,37,46,56\) in dimension \(2n-6\); no infinite-family classification is asserted.
