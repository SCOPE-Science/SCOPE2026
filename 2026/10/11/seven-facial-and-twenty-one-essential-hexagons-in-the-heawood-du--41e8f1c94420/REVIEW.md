# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The exact fourteen published facets give \(21\) edges. Binary row reduction gives \(\dim Z^1=8\) and \(\dim B^1=6\), so \(H^1(K;\mathbf F_2)\cong\mathbf F_2^2\). Exhaustion of all \(256\) cocycles gives the two displayed coset weight enumerators and seven weight-six minimizers in each nonzero class. Independently, the dual graph is reconstructed as the Fano incidence graph, all \(28\) six-cycles are enumerated, and their supports are checked to equal the \(28\) weight-six cocycles. The published generators produce an order-
\(42\) group preserving the facets and cycle-orbit sizes \(7\) and \(21\). The Poincaré-duality step is standard for closed orientable surfaces.

## Originality
PASS. Exact-object, cosystole, code-weight, Heawood-cycle, regular-map and homology-class searches found prior coverage of the triangulation, the abstract count of \(28\) Heawood six-cycles, and the seven hexagonal faces, but not the complete cohomology-coset enumerators, the statement that exactly the other \(21\) six-cycles are essential, their \(7,7,7\) homology split, or their single orbit under the embedding-preserving order-
\(42\) group. The closest prior computation on this exact triangulation classifies simplicial self-maps and cohomology actions, not cocycle supports. The closest primal systolic computation concerns a different chain condition and does not imply the dual classification. The inaccessible full text of Szilassi's regular-toroid paper is retained as a residual risk rather than treated as negative evidence.

## Value
PASS. The seven-vertex torus is the vertex-minimal triangulation of \(T^2\), and its dual is the canonical Heawood map. The finding refines the familiar abstract count of twenty-eight shortest Heawood cycles into embedding-sensitive topology and simultaneously gives the complete support-weight distribution of every mod-two first-cohomology class. The sharp facial-versus-essential split and its symmetry orbit provide a compact benchmark linking minimal triangulations, dual systoles and cohomological codes.

## Closest literature and limitations
Köhler--Lutz supplies the exact triangulation and order-
\(42\) symmetry data. Standard Heawood-map sources supply the abstract six-cycle count and seven-face embedding. Neither inspected source supplies the cohomological support enumerators or essential-cycle split. The result is specific to mod-two coefficients and the fixed triangulation; no retriangulation-invariant claim is made.
