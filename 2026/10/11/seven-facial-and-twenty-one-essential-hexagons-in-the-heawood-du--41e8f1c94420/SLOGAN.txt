For Möbius' seven-vertex torus, every nonzero class in \(H^1(K;\mathbf F_2)\) has edge cosystole \(6\) with exactly seven minimizers. In the dual Heawood map, its \(28\) hexagons split into seven facial cycles and one orbit of twenty-one essential cycles, with seven essential cycles in each nonzero mod-two homology class.
