In Bagchi--Datta's fifteen-vertex tight \(4\)-manifold, all \(225\) missing triangles are primitive integral homology \(1\)-systoles, but up to orientation they occupy only seven classes. In a cyclically permuted basis \(g_1,g_2,g_3\), these are exactly the seven nonzero binary subset sums, with multiplicities \(46^3,18^3,33\).
