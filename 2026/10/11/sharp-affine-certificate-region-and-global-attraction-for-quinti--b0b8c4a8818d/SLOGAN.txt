In the quintic Liénard family, the Gasull–Giacomini affine no-cycle certificate exists exactly when \(a_3\geq a_4^2/4-2\sqrt{a_1}\), for \(a_1>0\), with no restriction on the quadratic damping coefficient. Every system in this entire sharp certificate region has a globally asymptotically stable origin, even when the ordinary quadratic energy increases on part of the plane.
