The first angular Fourier coefficient of the harmonic order operator is exactly minus the radius minus a positive hyperbolic-energy integral of the analytic dilatation; the resulting circlewise lower bound is sharp even for nonconstant dilatation. It also shows that the 2026 Efraimidis–Hernández Schwarzian criterion admits only affine harmonic Möbius maps, a rigidity consequence independently obtainable from the known global order bound.
