Prescribing a constant hyperbolic-area multiplier on a degenerating annulus forces the sharp rotationally equivariant quasiconformal distortion to blow up like \(\varepsilon^{-2}\), while a map with the same boundary values but no area constraint needs only logarithmic distortion. The exact constrained minimizers include a nonunique family of small interior twists, and the gap has a sharp critical boundary layer when \(k-1\) is on the scale \(\varepsilon^2\).
