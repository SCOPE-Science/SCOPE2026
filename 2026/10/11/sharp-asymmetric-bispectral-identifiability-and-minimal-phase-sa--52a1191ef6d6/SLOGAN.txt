For integers \(n>r+s\), \(r>s\ge1\), and \(r\ge3\), a sharp threshold \(2r+s=n\) separates a circle of bispectral phase ambiguities from exactly the cyclic translations for complex signals with Fourier support \(\{-s,\ldots,-1,1,\ldots,r\}\). Given Fourier magnitudes, respectively \(r+s-1\) or \(r+s\) individual bispectral samples are necessary and sufficient to determine the entire phase fiber.
