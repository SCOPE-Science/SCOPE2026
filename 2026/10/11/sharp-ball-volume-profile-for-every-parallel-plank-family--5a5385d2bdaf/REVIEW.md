**PASS for the repaired sharp-position-deficit theorem.** Fubini gives the exact cross-sectional density \(\kappa_{d-1}(1-t^2)^{(d-1)/2}\). When \(|E|=W\), the sets removed from and added outside the centered trace both have measure \(\delta\). ## Originality
**PASS, best of knowledge, for the repaired claim only.** Bakaev and Polyanskii, *Optimal Partial Plank Coverings* (arXiv:2607.27483v1, 29 July 2026), already prove the centered-plank volume optimum for all finite plank directions, a stronger theorem than the original all-dimensional aligned optimization. That original novelty attribution is withdrawn; the centered optimum and incomplete-beta value remain acknowledged background. Their full primary paper was inspected; it does not specify the exact sharp symmetric-difference displacement penalty for full-utilization aligned traces. The separately published Bakaev–Yehudayoff *Volume stability of plank covers* has a different full-covering/annular hypothesis, not the same positional partial-coverage deficit. The repaired claim is only the exact, attaining sharp deficit law; because its proof is a classical bathtub exchange, an unindexed equal-measure rearrangement formula remains a real priority risk.

## Value
**PASS for the narrowly scoped repaired result.** Geometric displacement in symmetric-difference distance is a natural way of measuring failure to achieve the known optimal centered arrangement, independently of overlap and direction changes. The formula is exact and attained throughout its entire natural feasible parameter domain, so it is a potentially useful stability benchmark. The accepted scope does not pretend to advance the unrestricted plank optimum already solved by others.
