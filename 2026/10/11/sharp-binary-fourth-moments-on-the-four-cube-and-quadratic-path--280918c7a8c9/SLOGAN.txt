Every binary sixteen-mode Fourier polynomial on the four-dimensional unit cube has fourth moment at least \(336\), with equality exactly for the \(384\) sign patterns generated by Hamilton-path quadratic phases and affine sign changes. For quadratic Boolean phases through seven variables, labeled paths remain the unique graph minimizers of a naturally associated weighted induced-Eulerian-subgraph invariant; the latter finite-range conclusion does not assert an unrestricted complex-phase optimum.
