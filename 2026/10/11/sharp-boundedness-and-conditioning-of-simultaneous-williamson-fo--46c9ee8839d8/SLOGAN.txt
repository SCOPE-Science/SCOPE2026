For aligned pairs of positive symplectically commuting multiplication operators, a bounded simultaneous Williamson normalizer exists exactly when the position--momentum multiplier ratio stays uniformly comparable. The least possible operator-norm condition number among **all** bounded symplectic normalizers is \(\sqrt{\operatorname*{ess\,sup}\max\{p/q,q/p\}}\), and it diverges for a concrete pair of bounded injective operators with noncoercive continuous spectrum.
