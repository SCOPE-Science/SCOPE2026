For a regular even \(N\)-gonal Banach plane, the optimal Chmieliński \(C\)-approximate Birkhoff-symmetry defect is \(0\) when \(N\equiv2\pmod4\) and exactly \(\tan(\pi/N)\) when \(4\mid N\). Thus regular \(4m\)-gons have a sharp defect that decays as \(\pi/N\), attained by a vertex and the perpendicular edge midpoint.
