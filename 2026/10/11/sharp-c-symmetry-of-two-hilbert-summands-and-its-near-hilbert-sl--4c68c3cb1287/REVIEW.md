# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Formula (5) is the exact Hilbert-sum norming functional. Under Birkhoff orthogonality, the two block pairings cancel; two Cauchy–Schwarz bounds lead to a scalar envelope. The envelope is strictly increasing up to \(\omega=1/(1+\tau)\) and strictly decreasing afterward, with positive derivative factors even for \(1<p<2\). A blockwise collinear witness attains the envelope at every positive ratio. The finite-ratio maximum is positive and below one when \(p\ne2\); equality in Hilbert Cauchy–Schwarz proves extremizer rigidity. A hyperbolic substitution proves conjugate-exponent invariance. The uniform tail bound \(2z e^{-9z/14}\) proves the near-Hilbert coefficient and convergence of maximizing parameters. Endpoint blocks and \(p=2\) were addressed separately. The numerical checker is supplementary.

## Originality
PASS after the bounded comparison. The entire original arXiv:2309.14252v1 (2023) was inspected, including its support-functional, Birkhoff and pointwise-symmetry theorems; none computes this sharp global reverse symmetry defect. The complete open-access §5 of Chmieliński–Khurana–Sain's direct-sum approximate-smoothness paper was compared and likewise provides input identities, not the extremal statement. The older global-approximate-symmetry paper (2018) could only be checked through its abstract: it remains an explicit, material bibliographic risk. Actual indexed searches covered support-functional, duality, Hilbert-fiber and hyperbolic formulations. No matching result was found, which alone would not establish novelty.

## Value
PASS. An exact dimension-independent answer across arbitrary Hilbert-fiber pairs, all \(1<p<\infty\), and all maximizers is a structural rigidity theorem, motivated by the known qualitative direct-sum classifications. The sharp linear rate of decay as \(p\to2\) measures approach to Hilbert symmetry, rather than supplying an arbitrary isolated numerical value.

## Closest literature and limitations
Bose's preprint *Geometry of \(\ell_p\)-direct sums of normed linear spaces* (2023), Theorems 9, 19 and 25, and Chmieliński–Khurana–Sain's *Approximate smoothness in normed linear spaces* (2021 preprint, 2023 publication), §5, are closest in mathematical objects. The former proves exact Birkhoff orthogonality/pointwise symmetry, the latter support-functional identities/approximate smoothness. Neither inspected text states the global \(C\)-symmetry defect, the Hilbert-fiber extremal alignment or the uniform sharp near-Hilbert coefficient. The earlier 2018 paper defining a related constant remained inaccessible in full and is a residual originality risk. No claim is made for complex fields, nonsmooth exponents or general Banach fibers.
