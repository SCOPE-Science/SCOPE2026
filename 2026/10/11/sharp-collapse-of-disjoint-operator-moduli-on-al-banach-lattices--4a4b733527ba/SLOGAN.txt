For every bounded operator from an infinite-dimensional abstract AL Banach lattice to any Banach space, the three disjoint moduli satisfy \(\beta(T)=\Gamma_d(T)=\kappa_d(T)\). This supplies the sharp coefficient-one AL-space case of a published comparison question and the stability bound \(|\beta(T+S)-\beta(T)|\leq\Delta_d(S)\), including invariance under disjointly strictly singular perturbations.
