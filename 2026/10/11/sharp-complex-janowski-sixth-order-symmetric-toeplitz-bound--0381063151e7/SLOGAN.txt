The sharp modulus of the sixth-order symmetric Toeplitz determinant over the full complex-coefficient odd Janowski starlike class is \(\bigl[(8+P_1)(8+P_2)/64\bigr]^2\), where \(P_1=(A-B)(A-3B)\) and \(P_2=(A-B)(3A-B)\). The only maximizers arise from the two Schwarz functions \(v(\zeta)=\pm i\zeta\), and the sharp bound strictly exceeds the real-coefficient maximum throughout \(-1\le B<A\le1\).
