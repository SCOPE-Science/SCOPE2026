For every odd Legendre degree \(\ell\ge3\), the axial constant-width support \(1+\varepsilon P_\ell(u\cdot e)\) is convex exactly for \(|\varepsilon|\le2/(\ell(\ell+1)-2)\); its endpoint volume is \(4\pi/3-8\pi/((2\ell+1)(\ell(\ell+1)-2))\). The unique lowest-volume single nontranslational zonal mode is degree \(3\), with volume \(128\pi/105\) at width \(2\), and it alone among these endpoint modes has a non-polar ring of zero meridional curvature.
