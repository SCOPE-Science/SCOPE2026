The constant and all single-entry additive Fourier characters restricted to \(\mathrm{GL}_m(\mathbb F_q)\) form a linearly independent family with a complete five-block Gram spectrum for every prime power \(q\ge2\). Its sharp upper Riesz eigenmode changes strictly at \(m=2q\), while the lower mode is always simple, giving an exact condition number and its asymptotic decay.
