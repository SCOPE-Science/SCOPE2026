On the finite-field Plücker cone of decomposable bivectors, all one-coordinate additive Fourier characters form a linearly independent system with five explicitly determined spectral blocks. Its sharp least-Riesz-eigenvalue mode changes at \(m=10\) for \(q=2\), at \(m=4q+3\) for \(q=3,4\), and at \(m=4q+4\) for \(q\ge5\).
