In the global quintic Liénard family \(\dot x=y,\ \dot y=-x-a x^3-x^5-bxy\), the isochronous-cubic balance \(b^2=9a\) leaves the universal normalized-period defect \(-5A^4/16+O(A^6)\), independently of \(b\). Crossing to \(\mu=b^2-9a>0\) creates exactly one small-amplitude period maximum, at \(A^2=\mu/15+O(\mu^2)\), while every fixed admissible system has large-amplitude period of order \(A^{-2}\).
