# Scientific review: Sharp defect-norm thresholds for band projections on \(L_p\)

**Correctness — PASS.** The disjoint-band norm identity reduces the claim to a homogeneous function \(F_{p,c}\) on the positive quadrant. Exact differentiation factors the second derivative into a positive scalar and \(H_p-c\); the derivative of \(\log H_p\) determines its global infimum for every \(1<p<\infty\). Convex-perspective and coordinate-monotonicity arguments prove sufficiency on all of \(L_p\), while two nonzero disjoint-band unit vectors convert any negative curvature into an explicit triangle-inequality failure. The \(p=1\), \(p=2\), \(t=0\) and sharp-equality boundaries are separately proved. Symbolic algebra was checked independently, but is not an infinite proof substitute.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** Jana–Pal–Roy, arXiv:2407.15112, Section 7 was inspected in full at its relevant assertions: their Theorem 7.13 supplies a general equivalence, Example 7.18 treats a Hilbertian sequence sum, and Example 7.20 supplies unrelated non-Hilbert \(L_p\) counterexamples. None fixes the cutoff for the positive projection multipliers \(tP_E\). Fackler–Glück, arXiv:1709.08547, Definition 2.7 and Theorem 7.1 applies a weaker dilation notion and supplies no Pythagorean norm classification. Searches for equivalent absolute-norm convexity, scaled-projection defect, and stronger multiplier results found no covering statement; an unindexed equivalent result remains possible.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The exact phase diagram answers a natural unresolved quantitative question arising from the general defect/dilation criterion: unlike the Hilbert exponent, \(1<p<2\) admits a sharp, nonzero interval of scaled band projections while \(p>2\) admits no nontrivial scale. The threshold is independent of the number of atoms, measure type, and the dimensions of the two bands. This supplies a substantive sharp structural boundary, not a numerical estimate or a renaming of the general theorem.

**Limitations.** The result does not classify arbitrary positive operators or all dilation notions, and literature indexing is incomplete. All scientific certification here is internal to the mathematical proof and checks; independent verification has not occurred.
