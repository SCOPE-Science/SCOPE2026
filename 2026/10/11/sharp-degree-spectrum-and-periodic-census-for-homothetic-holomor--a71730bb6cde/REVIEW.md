# Scientific review: sharp degree spectrum and periodic census

**Correctness — PASS.** The integral companion of \(t^{2d}+k\) has determinant \(k\), mutually distinct conjugate pairs of eigenvalues of common modulus \(k^{1/(2d)}\), and admits an invariant complex structure and flat Hermitian norm for which it is an exact homothety. The induced complex torus map is holomorphic of covering degree \(k\). The roots-of-unity multiplicity calculation establishes the stated periodic-point formula for every positive iterate, and the volume Jacobian identity rules out degree one expanding maps on any positive-dimensional compact complex manifold. Exact integer tests verify bounded examples but do not replace these arguments.

**Originality — PASS (residual risk acknowledged).** Zhang's classification does not explicitly realize every degree with a Hermitian homothety in every dimension. Bauer--Herrig's generic fixed-point determinant identity *covers that ingredient*, so the census formula is not presented as a distinct novelty claim. Hughes--de Jong's nearby work concerns smooth Markov partitions. Targeted searches did not find this specific all-degree homothetic realization; an uncatalogued elementary companion example is a genuine remaining risk.

**Value — PASS.** The complete degree spectrum answers a naturally constrained existence question. The sharp realization of degree two in arbitrarily large complex dimensions while preserving exact flat metric homothety contrasts with the high degrees obtained by multiplication endomorphisms. The explicit family permits exact enumeration of periodic data, while neither a fixed-torus degree classification nor new determinant formalism is claimed.

**Literature and limitations.** Zhang, arXiv:2610.01525v1, Theorem A and its proof; Bauer--Herrig, arXiv:1508.05958v1, Proposition 1.1; Hughes--de Jong, arXiv:2604.00257v1, Theorems 1.2–1.3. The older general determinant identity dominates only the fixed-count computation, not the degree realization. The complex structure depends on the selected degree, and possible older folklore remains unexcluded.
