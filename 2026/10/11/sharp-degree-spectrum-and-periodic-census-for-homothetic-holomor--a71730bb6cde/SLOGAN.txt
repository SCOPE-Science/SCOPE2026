In every positive complex dimension, every integer degree \(k\ge2\) is realized by a holomorphic expanding endomorphism of a complex torus that scales a flat Hermitian metric exactly by \(k^{1/(2d)}\). An explicit companion-matrix construction also gives \(\#\operatorname{Fix}(f^n)=|1-(-k)^{n/\gcd(n,2d)}|^{\gcd(n,2d)}\) for its iterates.
