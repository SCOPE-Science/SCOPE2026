# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The argument begins with the exact uniform exit-distance distribution in each coordinate, giving the survival polynomial \(\prod_i(1-|v_i|t)\). Its moment identity is integrable for every \(p>-1\), including negative exponents, and has a continuous logarithmic case at \(p=0\). The moving-endpoint integral has an exact nonvanishing leading term of order \(m+1\) at an \(m\)-fold maximum; the sign and coefficient survive the smooth logarithmic normalization. This establishes the high-order Peano jet and obstruction. Across two-way ties, the same endpoint estimate glues Hessians and bounded third derivatives to obtain local \(C^{2,1}\). At coordinate zeros a strictly positive one-sided derivative in absolute coordinate magnitude proves nondifferentiability. Independent rational and high-precision checks of representative dimensions and exponents reproduce the defect coefficient; the universal quantifiers rest on the analytic proof, not these tests.

**Originality — PASS, with explicit close precedent.** The most relevant source is Langharst, Manui, and Zvavitch, *On the Fourier Mean Bodies of a Convex Body*, arXiv:2601.20117, Proposition 34, which already gives the complete integral representation of \(R_pQ\) and the axial/diagonal radial values. That formula is not claimed here. Its following Proposition 35 concerns Banach–Mazur distance, not differentiation at coordinate-dominance ties. Langharst's March 2026 all-parameter convexity theorem concerns convexity, not a sharp regularity stratification; Haddad addresses planar convexity. The dimension-two case is excluded from the new originality claim. The new work proves a simultaneous all-dimensional local \(C^{2,1}\) bound, exact maximal-coordinate multiplicity stratification, and the nonzero leading contact coefficient across the full range \(p>-1\). The published explicit cube integral makes this a derived structural theorem, but no inspected statement supplies the claimed sharp jets or their necessary boundary-layer analysis. Older unidentified sources remain a genuine risk; the original 1998 article could not be fully inspected using authorized access.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** This is a sharp regularity classification for a natural and currently active affine-geometric construction. It identifies two distinct geometric singularities that the convexity theorem alone does not distinguish: nonsmooth coordinate-zero seams versus locally twice-differentiable maximum-tie seams with exactly quantified higher-order failure. The positive coefficient proves that all finite parameters exhibit the obstruction, rather than merely numerical examples. Multiplicity-dependent contact orders and affine transport produce a structural statement for all parallelotopes, not an arbitrary finite list or isolated parameter value.

**Limitations.** The claim concerns gauges of parallelotope radial mean bodies for finite \(p>-1\), not general convex bodies, the limit \(p=\infty\), or neighborhood higher-order regularity near triple ties. The earlier integral representation remains prior art, and independent assessment is outstanding. The original Gardner–Zhang full text was not accessible during the literature comparison; no negative assertion about its unseen contents is made.
