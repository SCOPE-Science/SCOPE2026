For every finite \(p>-1\), the Gardner–Zhang radial mean body of an \(n\)-cube, \(n\ge3\), has a gauge locally \(C^{2,1}\) in each coordinate orthant but nonsmooth across coordinate-zero hyperplanes. At an \(m\)-fold maximum-coordinate tie it has exactly an \(m\)-th order Peano Taylor jet, with an explicit nonzero \((m+1)\)-st order boundary-layer defect, so generic ties are \(C^2\) but not \(C^3\); the classification is affine-invariant for parallelotopes.
