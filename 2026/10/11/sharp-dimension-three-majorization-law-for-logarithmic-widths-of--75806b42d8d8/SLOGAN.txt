With squared semiaxes summing to one, the geometric mean of directional widths of a planar or three-dimensional orthogonal crosspolytope increases strictly whenever its axes are balanced. The majorization law breaks sharply in every dimension at least four: the explicit profiles \((1/3,1/3,1/3,0)\) and \((8/25,8/25,8/25,1/25)\) reverse the predicted order, even after a small full-dimensional perturbation.
