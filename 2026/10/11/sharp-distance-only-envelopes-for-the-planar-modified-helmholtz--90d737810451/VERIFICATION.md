# Verification

The upper bound was checked by solving the radial comparison problem on the maximal centered inscribed disk and applying the strong maximum principle. The lower bound was checked by constructing the exact exponential solution associated with a supporting line at a nearest boundary point.

Sharpness was checked symbolically. The centered disk gives the upper value exactly. For the tangent disks \(B_R((0,R))\) observed at \((0,d)\), the exact value is \(I_0(\mu(R-d))/I_0(\mu R)\), and the classical asymptotic of \(I_0\) gives the lower limit \(e^{-\mu d}\).

No numerical experiment or finite enumeration is used. No claim is made for nonconvex domains or higher-dimensional sharp profiles.
