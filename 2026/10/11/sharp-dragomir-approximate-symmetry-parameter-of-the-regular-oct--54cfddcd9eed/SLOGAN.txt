The real regular octagonal Banach plane has sharp global modified-Dragomir Birkhoff-symmetry parameter
\[
\varepsilon_D=\sqrt{\frac{5-2\sqrt2}{8}}.
\]
Equivalently, the smallest reverse line-distance over unit Birkhoff--James pairs is \((2+\sqrt2)/4\), attained at a vertex with a coordinate-axis orthogonal direction.
