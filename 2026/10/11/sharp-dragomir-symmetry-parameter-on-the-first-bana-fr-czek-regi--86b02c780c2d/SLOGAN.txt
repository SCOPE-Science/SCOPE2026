On the first Banaś--Frączek regime \(1<\lambda\le\sqrt2\), the sharp global modified-Dragomir Birkhoff-symmetry parameter is
\[
\varepsilon_D(R_\lambda^2)=\sqrt{1-\lambda^{-2}}.
\]
Equivalently, the smallest reverse line distance is \(1/\lambda\), attained at a clipped-disk corner against the vertical Birkhoff direction.
