The golden-mean self-similar measure has an exact dyadic renormalization law: its doubling-map orbit converges to the stationary Parry measure at sharp total-variation distance \(q^{n+2}/(1+q)\), with \(q=(3-\sqrt5)/2\). An outward-certified base calculation and the exact recurrence prove \(\operatorname{Im}\widehat\mu(2^n)>1/5\) at every nonnegative dyadic exponent, an infinite zero-free lacunary set.
