For every finite order, the sharp real \(\ell_p\) Lipschitz constant of unweighted isotonic regression depends only on the size \(N\) of its largest connected component: it is \(1\) for \(p=2\) or \(N\le2\), \(N^{1/p}\) for \(1<p<2\), and \((N/2)^{1/p}\) for \(p>2\) when \(N\ge3\). Full-pooling perturbations prove optimality on every connected order, regardless of its branching.
