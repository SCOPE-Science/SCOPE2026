For real unweighted isotonic regression on an \(n\)-point chain, the exact \(\ell_p\)-Lipschitz constant of nearest-point projection is \(1\) in dimension at most two or at \(p=2\), but is \(n^{1/p}\) for \(1<p<2\) and \((n/2)^{1/p}\) for \(p>2\) when \(n\ge3\). The sharp transition comes from the concentration of the sensitivity of pooled \(p\)-means at zero or extreme residuals.
