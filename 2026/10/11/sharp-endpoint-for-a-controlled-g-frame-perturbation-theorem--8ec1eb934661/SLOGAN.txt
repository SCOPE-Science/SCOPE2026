Li--Leng's controlled g-frame perturbation theorem needs the strict threshold \(\lambda<A\): its stated endpoint \(\lambda=A\) fails for the complete diagonal family \(\Gamma_n f=n^{-1}\langle f,e_n\rangle\). With \(\lambda<A\), the original argument is valid and gives the explicit controlled lower bound \((A-\lambda)^2/B\).
