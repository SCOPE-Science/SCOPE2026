For every complex two-periodic first-order recurrence on bilateral \(\ell_2\), the sharp residual-error constant is \(\sqrt{(S+\sqrt{S^2-4\delta^2})/(2\delta^2)}\), where \(S=2+|a|^2+|b|^2\) and \(\delta=||ab|-1|>0\). At fixed positive, nonunit Floquet-product modulus, balanced coefficients uniquely minimize this constant, arbitrary imbalance makes it unbounded, and a nonzero extremizer exists exactly when the product vanishes.
