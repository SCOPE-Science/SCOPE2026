For every stationary binary shift process, entropy rate determines a **sharp universal upper envelope** for Ornstein's \(\bar d\)-distance to the fair Bernoulli process: \(\bar d\le\tfrac12-H_{\mathrm b}^{-1}(h)\). Biased iid Bernoulli processes attain the bound at every entropy level, making the full nonlinear profile exact rather than merely square-root accurate.
