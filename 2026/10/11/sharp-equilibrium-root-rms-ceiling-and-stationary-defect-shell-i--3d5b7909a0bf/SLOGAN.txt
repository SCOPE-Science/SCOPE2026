Every compact stationary Ehrhard–Müller state satisfies the exact calibration \(\mathbb E[z]=\mathbb E[x^2]\) and the defect shell \(\operatorname{Var}(z)+\mathbb E[(\dot x/\sigma)^2]=\mathbb E[z](r-1-\mathbb E[z])+c\mathbb E[x]\). For nonsymmetric heating \(c>0\), this forces the sharp RMS ceiling \(\mathbb E[x^2]\le\xi_+^2\), where \(\xi_+\) is the positive equilibrium amplitude, with equality only at that equilibrium atom.
