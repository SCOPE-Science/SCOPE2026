For every real Banach-space map \(T\) and \(1\le p\le\infty\), the canonical induced free \(p\)-convex lattice operator is finite rank when \(\operatorname{rank}T\le1\), but otherwise its distance to all compact operators is exactly \(\|T\|\). Thus rank-two maps arbitrarily close to rank one induce lattice homomorphisms a full unit away from their rank-one limits in a normalized example.
