For every real \(m\)-point equilateral metric space with \(m\ge2\), the squared Euclidean Banach--Mazur distance of its Lipschitz-free space is exactly \(2\lfloor m^2/4\rfloor/m\). Permutation symmetry makes the centered Euclidean ellipsoid globally optimal, and the parity dependence comes from the most balanced two-level Lipschitz potentials.
