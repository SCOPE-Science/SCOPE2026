For fan-graph symmetric edge polytopes, a four-state transfer matrix gives every Euclidean facet distance with its multiplicity. The largest inscribed ball has radius \(\left(m-1-\varepsilon_m/(m+1)\right)^{-1/2}\), touches exactly two or four facets according to parity, and has a family of centers of affine dimension \(m-1\) or \(m-2\), respectively.
