## correctness

PASS

For Q_d, the graph Laplacian eigenvalues are 2|S| on Walsh modes. The Poincare bound 2||f||²<=sum_edges |df|²<=d2^(d-1) gives ||f||²<=d2^(d-2). Equality is exactly first Walsh level with all coordinate coefficients ±1/2, giving 2^d polar vertices; their span is d-dimensional. Pairwise opposite contact constraints force all optimal centers perpendicular to that subspace. Each noncontact vertex has integer-edge potential and squared norm in (1/2^d)Z strictly below the maximum, providing a positive noncontact margin, so the affine hull is precisely E_1-perp within H, of dimension 2^d-d-1. Independently checked Laplacian eigenvalues and explicit contacts for d=2,3,4,5. Source verifier fully specifies exact enumeration of 6,38,990 polar vertices for d=2..4, not an infinite proof.
