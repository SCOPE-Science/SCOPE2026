For the symmetric edge polytope of the Boolean \(d\)-cube graph, the exact largest Euclidean inball has radius \(2/\sqrt{d2^d}\), with exactly \(2^d\) facets touching the origin-centered ball. The space of all optimal ball centers nevertheless has affine dimension \(2^d-d-1\), so no dimension \(d\ge2\) has a unique incenter.
