Every circle-covariant Schatten operator satisfies a sharp all-even weighted numerical-radius bound with coefficient \(\sum_{k=0}^m\binom mk^2t^{2k}(1-t)^{2(m-k)}\), asymptotically attained by long nilpotent shifts but strictly unattainable by nonzero compact operators in the nondegenerate regime. At fourth order the entire deficit is exactly \(2t^2(1-t)^2\|T^*T-TT^*\|_2^2\).
