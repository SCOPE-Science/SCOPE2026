In mass-action networks, the first nonzero Taylor order of each newly appearing species is a rate-independent min-plus invariant of the productive reaction hypergraph. With reactant molecularity at most \(m\) and \(s\) activated initially absent species, the maximum possible onset order is exactly \((m^s-1)/(m-1)\) for \(m\ge2\) (and \(s\) for \(m=1\)); serial bimolecular networks attain \(2^s-1\) even though all activated species are positive immediately after time zero.
