At the zero-input border of the generalized max-plus oscillator, the map is globally annihilated in exactly \(2+\lceil\pi/\arccos(T/(2\sqrt D))\rceil\) iterates if \(T^2<4D\), and in no finite uniform time otherwise. In the nonoscillatory region the complete persistent set is a two-piece cone; exact extinction times exhibit an algebraic critical law and a logarithmic overdamped law.
