For the real Lipschitz-free space of the complete bipartite metric with cross-distances \(1\) and same-part distances \(t\), the exact number of facets of the unit ball is \(2^{m+n}-2\) at \(t=1\), \(2(2^m-1)(2^n-1)\) for \(1<t<2\), and \(2^m+2^n-2\) at \(t=2\), whenever \(m,n\ge2\). Thus snowflaking a complete bipartite graph metric has a sharp and completely classified change of facet count at both endpoints.
