For complete multipartite metrics with at least three nonempty parts, the real Lipschitz-free unit ball gains exactly \(\sum_i(2^{m_i}-2)(2^{N-m_i}-2)\) facets above the equilateral count throughout \(1<t<2\). The classification identifies the extra extreme potentials and the jumps to the already-known graph-metric endpoint at \(t=2\).
