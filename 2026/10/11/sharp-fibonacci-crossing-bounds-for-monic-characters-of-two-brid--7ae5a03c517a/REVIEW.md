# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof covers all positive rational continued-fraction sequences by a reversible unit-weight recursion. The greedy path is Fibonacci-maximal. In the sole parity-obstructed congruence class, the first non-greedy step has a quantified loss bounded below by \(F_{c-4}\), and explicit rational-knot sequences attain equality. The character-count corollary then follows from the published \(16p^2\) estimate. The finite verifier is supplementary and agrees through \(c=18\).

## Originality
PASS. The closest recent paper states \(\mathcal N\le16p^2\) for two-bridge \(K(p,q)\) and a separate general crossing bound, but does not optimize \(p\) at fixed crossing number. The closest classical determinant paper gives the rational-knot continuant recursion and the Fibonacci all-ones specialization, but not the parity-corrected odd maximum. Searches using determinant, Schubert numerator, rational/two-bridge knot, Fibonacci, continuant, fixed crossing number, and monic-character formulations found no equivalent or stronger statement. A residual literature risk is that an older specialized continuant result may use different language.

## Value
PASS. The result turns a parameter-dependent estimate into a sharp, uniform crossing-number specialization for all two-bridge knots and changes the exponential scale from base \(8\) to base \(\varphi^2\). The correction in the class \(c\equiv2\pmod3\) records a natural knot-versus-link parity boundary and is not a routine numerical increment.

## Closest literature and limitations
Kim--Morifuji is the source of the monic-character estimates. Kauffman--Lopes is the closest determinant-recursion source. The sharpness proved here is for the determinant bound; no sharpness is asserted for the count \(16M_c^2\). The character-variety hypotheses and the restriction to two-bridge knots remain essential.
