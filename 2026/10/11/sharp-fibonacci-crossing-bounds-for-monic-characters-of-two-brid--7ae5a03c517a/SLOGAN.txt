For a two-bridge knot with \(c\) crossings, the largest possible Schubert numerator is exactly \(F_{c+1}\), except when \(c\equiv2\pmod3\), where knot parity lowers it to \(F_{c+1}-F_{c-4}\). Combined with the two-generator character-variety estimate, this gives \(\mathcal N\le16M_c^2=O(\varphi^{2c})\).\n
