For the eight-coefficient cellular automata invertible at every finite size under null boundary conditions, reversibility on the bi-infinite full shift holds exactly when both neighbor coefficients are divisible by \(\operatorname{rad}(d)\), rather than merely their product. Otherwise noninjectivity already has a periodic witness of length dividing \(p-1\) for some \(p\mid d\); in the reversible case the optimal uniform inverse radius is \(\max_{p\mid d}v_p(d)-1\).
