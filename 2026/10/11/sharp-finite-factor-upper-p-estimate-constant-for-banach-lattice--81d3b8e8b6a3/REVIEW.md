# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof was reconstructed from the focal source's exact structural statements rather than inferred from experiments. Proposition 3.14 gives the closed solid-convex-hull model of the free-product unit ball, which yields factorwise positive majorants for arbitrarily close approximants. Proposition 3.10 makes the norm of the resulting positive sum exactly the sum of the factor norms. Applying each factor's upper \(p\)-estimate and Hölder across the \(r\) factors gives \(r^{1-1/p}\). Norm continuity of finite lattice suprema passes from approximants to the original vectors. Proposition 3.13 gives an exact scalar model in which the lower bound is attained.

## Originality
PASS. The closest result is Proposition 8.5 of Martínez-Fernández--Tradacete, which proves only qualitative preservation for two factors. Proposition 8.3 and Remark 8.4 give a sharp constant for the distinct and stronger property of \(p\)-convexity. Earlier literature on free Banach lattices with upper \(p\)-estimates studies free objects and push-outs rather than the ordinary free product. Exact-claim, alias, factor-count, and implication searches found no covering statement. A prior indexed result for two \(C(K)\) factors computes their norm from a slice profile but does not imply the arbitrary-factor upper-\(p\) estimate.

## Value
PASS. The theorem turns a new qualitative closure statement into an exact finite-factor law and proves universal sharpness on the canonical scalar test family. The dependence \(r^{1-1/p}\) is mathematically motivated by the coproduct's \(\ell_1\)-span geometry and is the correct benchmark for how upper \(p\)-estimates deteriorate under finite free products.

## Closest literature and limitations
The focal paper is the decisive comparison. The theorem is limited to finite factor families and \(1<p<\infty\), and no optimal unequal-factor-constant formula is claimed. Literature searches cannot exclude an older equivalent result indexed under substantially different terminology.

Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
