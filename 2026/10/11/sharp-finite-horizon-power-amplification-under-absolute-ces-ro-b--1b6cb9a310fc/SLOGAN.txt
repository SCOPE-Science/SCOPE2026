The exact largest \(p\)-th power of the \(n\)-th operator norm under an optimal \(p\)-absolute Cesàro bound \(K\) is \((K(n+1)-1)\prod_{j=2}^{n}(1-1/(Kj))\). A single positive weighted shift on \(\ell^p\) attains this bound for every positive integer \(n\), including the optimal asymptotic coefficient.
