For derivatives subordinate to \(e^z(1+\sin z)\), an explicit Blaschke formula gives the sharp hyperbolic pre-Schwarzian envelope at every radius and for every prescribed finite Taylor-jet gap. Despite arbitrarily many vanishing initial coefficients, the sharp global norm remains \((1-r_*^2)(1+\sec r_*+\tan r_*)=2.14565700788682\ldots\), where \(r_*\) is a uniquely characterized critical radius.
