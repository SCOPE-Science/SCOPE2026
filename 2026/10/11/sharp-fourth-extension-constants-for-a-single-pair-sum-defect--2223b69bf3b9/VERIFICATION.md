# Verification

The universal proof is algebraic. Character orthogonality converts the fourth extension norm into the exact ordered pair-sum energy. The checker independently reconstructs that convolution for finite cyclic examples rather than reusing the closed formula.

`verify.py` enumerates all subsets of \(\mathbb Z_N\) with exactly one double unordered pair-sum fiber for \(N\in\{5,7,9,11,13\}\) and subset size at most \(6\). It verifies that every defect is either a proper parallelogram or a midpoint relation, checks the corresponding exact Gaussian-integer energy identity for two deterministic coefficient vectors, and verifies the scalar sharp values with rational arithmetic.

Expected successful output:

`VERIFY_OK P_sets=199 M_sets=613 exact_energy_checks=1624 cyclic_orders=5,7,9,11,13`

The enumeration is a finite corroboration only. It does not certify the theorem for arbitrary groups or sizes; that scope comes from the proof in `RESULT.md`. The checker does not search the literature and provides no independent audit.
