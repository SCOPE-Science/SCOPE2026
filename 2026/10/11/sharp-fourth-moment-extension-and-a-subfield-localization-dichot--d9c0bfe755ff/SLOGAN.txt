For the zero-completed inverse graph over \(\mathbb F_{2^m}\), the sharp fourth-power Fourier extension norm is \(3-2^{1-m}\) when \(m\) is odd but exactly \(4\) when \(m\) is even. All odd-degree extremizers have full support and binary phases, whereas every even-degree extremizer localizes to one scaled copy of \(\mathbb F_4\), with four projective sign patterns per copy.
