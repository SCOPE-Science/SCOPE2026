On the split finite-field hyperbola \(\{(t,t^{-1}):t\ne0\}\), the sharp normalized \(L^2\to L^4\) Fourier extension fourth-power constant is \(3q^2(q-2)/(q-1)^3\) for every odd prime power \(q\). Equality is characterized by common-modulus coefficients with a common antipodal-pair product, and an exact four-term nonnegative deficit identity quantifies every departure from equality.
