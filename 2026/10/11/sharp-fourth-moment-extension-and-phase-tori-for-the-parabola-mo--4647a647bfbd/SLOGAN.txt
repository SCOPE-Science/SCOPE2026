For the parabolic Fourier extension operator over \(\mathbb Z/p^3\mathbb Z\), every odd prime has the exact sharp fourth-power norm \(2p-1\). Extremizers are precisely one-residue-class packets whose fine-digit Fourier frequencies agree, with arbitrary equal-magnitude middle-digit phases; modulo scaling they form \(p^2\) tori of dimension \(p-1\), and an exact positive deficit detects every failure of equality.
