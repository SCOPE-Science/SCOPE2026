For the six unimodular frequencies in the degree-two triangular aperture \(\{1,z,w,z^2,zw,w^2\}\), the fourth Fourier moment ranges exactly from \(48\) to \(102\), and its minimizing phases form exactly two cubic-root orbits. Binary phases cannot do better than \(54\), whereas a \(q\)-phase alphabet reaches \(48\) precisely when \(3\mid q\); these sharp statements also hold on every nonaliasing finite rectangular torus.
