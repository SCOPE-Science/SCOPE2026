For every \(n\ge6\), the complete real quadratic Walsh chaos on \(n\) signs has sharp fourth-moment ratio \((15n^2-63n+68)/(n(n-1))\), with switching-equivalent uniform coefficients as its only nonzero extremizers and an explicit sum-of-squares stability remainder. Its exact Banach–Mazur distance to Euclidean space is the fourth root of this ratio.
