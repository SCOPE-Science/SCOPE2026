For a real quadratic Walsh space supported on a finite graph whose four-cycles share no edges, the sharp fourth-moment quotient is \(3-2/m\) if there is no square, and exactly \(4\) otherwise; the latter case has precisely one-square extremizers. Every \(\delta\)-near-extremizer for \(0\le\delta<1/2\) places at least \((1+\sqrt{1-2\delta})/2\) of its squared coefficient mass on one square, and this threshold is sharp.
