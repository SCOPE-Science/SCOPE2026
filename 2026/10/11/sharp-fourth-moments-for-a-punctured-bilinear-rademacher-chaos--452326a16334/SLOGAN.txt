Deleting one corner character from the real \(n\times n\) bilinear Rademacher chaos changes its sharp \(L^2\)-to-\(L^4\) fourth-moment ratio to \(3-1/(n-1)+(6n-11)^2/(6n^2-11n+1)\) for every \(n\ge2\). All extremizers are row/column sign switchings of an explicit positive two-level coefficient array, and the deletion incurs an exact deficit asymptotic to \(6/n^2\) relative to the full bilinear chaos.
