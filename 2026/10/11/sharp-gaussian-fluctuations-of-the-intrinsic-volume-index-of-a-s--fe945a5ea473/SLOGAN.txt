For the cone tangent to a vertex of a regular \(m\)-simplex, the conic intrinsic-volume index has fluctuations \((J_m-2\log m)/(2\sqrt{\log m})\Longrightarrow N(0,1)\). Its variance is asymptotic to \(4\log m\), saturating the sharp universal bound \(\operatorname{Var}(J_m)\le2\mathbb E J_m\) at leading order.
