The conic index is simulated using the exact Gaussian projection face-count formula. The standalone checker validates deterministic normal-tail and truncated-normal moment identities numerically; compares a sorting-based face count to bisection of the Karush–Kuhn–Tucker scalar equation; checks a small independent quadratic minimization reconstruction; and reports finite-sample moments for several dimensions. The analytic proof, including Lyapunov convergence, local empirical-process control, and exponential uniform integrability, is recorded in RESULT.md. Finite samples do not establish asymptotic rates or novelty.
