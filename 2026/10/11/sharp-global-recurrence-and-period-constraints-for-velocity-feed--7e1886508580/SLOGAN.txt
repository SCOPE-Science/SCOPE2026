For the full velocity-feedback jerk class \(\dddot x+A\ddot x+x=f(\dot x)\), compact non-equilibrium recurrence is impossible when \(A\le0\); when \(A>0\), stationary activity obeys an exact secant-slope law at the imaginary-pair threshold and every nonconstant cycle has least period at least \(2\pi\sqrt A\), with an explicit sharpness criterion. In Sprott's original quadratic system every non-equilibrium compact stationary state must visit velocities below \(-1/A\) and above zero.
