For finite weak \(\ell_p\), every codimension-one graph map \(x\mapsto(x,L_a x)\) is isometric exactly when \(\sum_{j=1}^m a_j^*j^{-1/p}\le(m+1)^{-1/p}\). The Fang--Gao--Huang--Sukochev example is the two-dimensional equal-coefficient boundary case, and disjointness fails precisely when the added functional uses at least two coordinates.
