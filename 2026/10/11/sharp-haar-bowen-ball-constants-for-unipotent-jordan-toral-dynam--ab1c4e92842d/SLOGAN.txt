For the unipotent Jordan map on \(\mathbb T^d\), normalized Haar mass of an \(n\)-step Bowen ball has a sharp \(n^{-d(d-1)/2}\) asymptotic whose exact coefficient is the volume of a bounded-polynomial coefficient body. In dimension three this gives the explicit curvature-sensitive constant \(\lim_{n\to\infty}n^3m_3(B_n(z,\varepsilon))=544\varepsilon^3/9\) for \(0<\varepsilon<1/4\); the exponent itself was known previously.
