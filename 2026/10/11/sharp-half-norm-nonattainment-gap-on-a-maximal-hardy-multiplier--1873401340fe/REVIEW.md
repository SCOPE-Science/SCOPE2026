# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The Poisson-kernel concentration establishes both extremal operator bounds; Hardy boundary uniqueness proves that ordinary norm or minimum attainment forces constant boundary modulus. The maximal-ideal argument uses the codimension-one kernel of boundary evaluation. Comparison at a boundary zero and a boundary modulus maximum proves the sharp half-norm lower bound, with an explicit equality witness. Weak-null kernels give the exact approximation/Gelfand/Kolmogorov numbers, and an asymptotically orthogonal collection of localized kernels gives all Bernstein numbers. The infinite-dimensional and all-index quantifiers are proved analytically, not inferred from computations.

## Originality — PASS, with literature risk
The exact quantitative gap on the maximal boundary-vanishing analytic Toeplitz ideal, coupled with both ordinary nonattainment notions and all four flat width profiles, is not a mathematical consequence explicitly stated in the inspected Ramesh–Sequeira or Dantas–Falcó–Jung–Rodríguez-Vidanes results. Ramesh–Sequeira establish much of the **absolute** attainment background; the elementary symbol classification here is acknowledged as prior-related rather than counted on its own. Closely ranked work on Toeplitz Schur multipliers and one-sided multipliers on Schatten ideals treats different operators, so its flat-width formulas cannot be directly substituted. Yoshino (2002) is plausible prior art on ordinary Toeplitz norm attainment; its full text was not obtained, and hidden equivalent quantitative results remain a risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The norm-attaining set is closed nowhere dense **inside a canonical closed Hilbert-space operator algebra**, while a maximal codimension-one subalgebra has uniform sharp relative separation from it. The exact \(1/2\) barrier quantifies the robustness of nonattainment, rather than merely exhibiting examples. The simultaneous failure of minimum attainment and complete finite-dimensional width rigidity exposes different behaviors of norm optimization, range geometry and approximation in a natural family. No broader statement for arbitrary Toeplitz symbols is inferred.
