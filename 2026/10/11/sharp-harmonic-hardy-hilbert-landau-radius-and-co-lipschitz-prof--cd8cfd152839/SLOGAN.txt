For normalized planar harmonic Poisson maps with boundary \(L^2\)-norm at most \(M\), the sharp universal Landau radius is the unique \(R_M\) satisfying \(2(M^2-1)\sum_{n\ge2}n^2R_M^{2n-2}=1\) (and \(R_1=1\)). The exact co-Lipschitz profile is \(\max\{0,1-\sqrt{2(M^2-1)S(r)}\}\), attained in the limiting chord sense by explicit Hilbert-space extremals.
