For planar convex sets, the radial weighted perimeter with power \(-1<p<0\) has a diameter-independent Hausdorff modulus of sharp exponent \(p+1\), including degeneration to segments and points. At \(p=0\) it becomes globally \(2\pi\)-Lipschitz, while every \(p>0\) admits a sharp-exponent Lipschitz bound on bounded families.
