For every bounded nonconstant real random variable, the best constant \(L^p\) approximant has an exact derivative at \(p=2\), with sharp universal bound \(\left|m_p'(2)\right|\le K\sigma\), where \(K\approx0.662743419349182\). The optimal bound is attained exactly by a specified asymmetric two-point distribution (or its reflection), and equally weighted samples approach it within \(O(n^{-2})\).
