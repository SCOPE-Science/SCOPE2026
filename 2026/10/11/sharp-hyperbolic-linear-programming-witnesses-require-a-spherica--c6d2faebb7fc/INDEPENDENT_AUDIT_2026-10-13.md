# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Use Wackenhuth reduced spherical measure eta_hat>=0 and eta_plus(F)=eta_hat(Fhat)+i^2 Fhat(1)<=iF(e). Equality i=F(e)/Fhat(1) forces the nonnegative spectral integral to vanish and thus its measure is supported on zeros of continuous Fhat. Small nonnegative K-bi-invariant h supported in distance <2r with h(e)=1 has eta_plus(h)=i and Haar mass arbitrarily small, so eta(h)=i-i^2 mG(h)>0, showing eta_hat nonzero. To establish a genuinely NONTRIVIAL zero (rather than possibly only zero at trivial spherical character), strengthen that argument: if eta_hat were supported only at the trivial character, its inverse transform would be c mG; h shrinking toward K would force eta(h)=c mG(h)->0, contrary to eta(h)->i>0. Hence eta_hat charges nontrivial spectrum; the equality support constraint forces at least one nontrivial zero. This latter implication is essential but not written out in the package proof; it follows rigorously from the same arbitrary-shrinking family and spherical uniqueness. Compact support, i>0, r>0 and direct transform identity are critical, and no existence of attaining packing is claimed.
