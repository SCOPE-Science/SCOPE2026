# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact Lyapunov identity was reconstructed for every real parameter, with a separate identity for the exceptional case \(a=0\). Uniform boundedness of the transverse coordinates and the linear bound on vertical speed prove two-sided global existence inside the cylinder. Positive lower bounds of the Lyapunov derivative rule out compact invariant subsets; boundedness plus square integrability and uniform continuity gives the radial convergence branch. Exact equilibria prove the threshold cannot be enlarged. The standalone symbolic script reproduced the critical identities and endpoint substitutions. The proof does not claim pointwise convergence on the cylinder.

## Originality — PASS (subject to historical limits)

The full introducing paper [1] and the closely relevant full 2021 follow-up [2] were inspected, including their ES9 tables and analysis. Both treat the original cylinder and examples at fixed parameters, while the new theorem characterizes **all real parameters** using an explicit globally strict Lyapunov function, rules out any compact invariant interior dynamics up to the sharp threshold, and proves the complete bounded-orbit alternative. Searches for the exact differential equations, cylinder interior recurrence, equivalent energy inequalities, and stronger global statements yielded no covering theorem in checked material. Other published analyses not found by those searches remain a residual risk; absence from a search is not priority proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The original system was motivated by chaotic dynamics protected by an equilibrium surface. It is not automatic that absence of isolated interior equilibria excludes recurrent trajectories. The theorem gives a complete sharp existence boundary for interior compact invariant dynamics and a global fate dichotomy throughout the excluded parameter region, including a nontrivial interval in which vertical velocity changes sign. This supplies a qualitative parameter phase diagram rather than a numerical recurrence diagnostic or a mere equilibrium census.
