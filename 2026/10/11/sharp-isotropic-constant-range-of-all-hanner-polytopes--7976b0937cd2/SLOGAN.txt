Every \(d\)-dimensional Hanner polytope has isotropic constant between the cross-polytope value
\[
\frac{(d!)^{1/d}}{\sqrt{2(d+1)(d+2)}}
\]
and the cube value \(1/\sqrt{12}\), with both equality classes rigid for \(d\ge3\). The exact recursion shows that Cartesian products preserve the weighted isotropic scale while genuine higher-dimensional \(\ell_1\)-sums strictly decrease it.
