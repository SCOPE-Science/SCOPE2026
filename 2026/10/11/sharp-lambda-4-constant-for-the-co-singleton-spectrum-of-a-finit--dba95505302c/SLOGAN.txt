For every finite abelian group \\(G\\) of order \\(N\\ge4\\), the co-singleton spectrum \\(\\widehat G\\setminus\\{1\\}\\) has the exact sharp constant \\(K_4^4=N-2+1/(N-1)\\). Its nonzero extremizers are precisely translated point-mass defects \\(c(N\\mathbf 1_{\\{x_0\\}}-1)\\), so the constant and equality class are independent of the group structure.
