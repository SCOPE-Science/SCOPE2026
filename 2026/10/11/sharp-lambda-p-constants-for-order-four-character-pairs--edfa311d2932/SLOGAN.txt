For two characters whose relative character has order \(4\), the sharp \(\Lambda(p)\) constant has two exact phase transitions: balanced extremizers are axis-aligned for \(2<p<4\), diagonal for \(4<p<6\), and axis-aligned again for \(p>6\), while every phase is extremal at \(p=4\) and \(p=6\). The five-regime formula for \(K_p^p\) is explicit.
