For every finite equilateral set of \(n\ge4\) leaves, adjoining their metric-star center has the exact universal real \(p\)-Banach Lipschitz extension factor \(\min\{2,2(n-1)^{1/p}/n\}\). The sharp transition \(p_n=\log(n-1)/\log n\) tends to one, so every fixed quasi-Banach exponent below one reaches the one-point obstruction \(2\) for sufficiently many leaves.
