# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The claim is proved directly for every real \(r\ge1\). The truncation net gives the matching lower bound \(r\), the full dual decomposition of \(c\) identifies the order-continuous part, finite-support sign vectors determine its induced norm, and the Lorentz-gauge computation is two-sided. A norm-one extension of the ordinary limit is proved positive before it is used to define the lattice norm on \(\ell_\infty\). No finite experiment is used as a substitute for an infinite proof.

## Originality
PASS. The focal preprint was inspected at its canonical \(c\)-renorming, its general \(r^2\)-norming theorem, its Lorentz extension/completion theorem, and the explicit question about improving those constants. Targeted searches under demicontinuity, weak-Fatou, convergent-sequence, Lorentz-completion, Banach-limit, and order-continuous-dual terminology did not locate a result implying the combined exact theorem. The closest indexed related result gives finite-stage adherence bounds for general demicontinuous lattices, not these exact formulas. The principal residual risk is an older equivalent calculation under different weak-Fatou notation.

## Value
PASS. The result supplies the exact benchmark requested by the source's open constant question on the source's own canonical family: the quadratic \(r^2\) loss simultaneously collapses to the optimal linear constant \(r\), and both the Lorentz completion and a sharp extension norm are explicit. This is a structural calibration of the general theory, not a numerical recomputation.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is E. Bilokopytov, *Variants of order semicontinuity in Banach lattices*, arXiv:2609.03070v1 (2026), especially Example 3.2, Proposition 3.10, Theorem 6.1, and Question 6.2. The present theorem does not claim a universal improvement for arbitrary \(r\)-demicontinuous lattices, nor does it classify all possible extension norms.
