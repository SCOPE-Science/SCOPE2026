For the canonical convergent-sequence renormings \(\|x\|_r=\max\{\|x\|_\infty,r|\lim x|\}\), the exact demicontinuity constant and the exact order-continuous-dual norming constant are both \(r\). The Lorentz completion is \(\ell_\infty\) with its sup norm, and a norm-one extension of the limit yields a sharp \(r\)-demicontinuous extension, so the general quadratic \(r^2\) bounds collapse to the optimal linear constant on this family.
