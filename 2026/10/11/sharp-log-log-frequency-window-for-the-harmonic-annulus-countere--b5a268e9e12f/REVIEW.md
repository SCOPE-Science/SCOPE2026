# Review of Sharp log–log frequency window for the harmonic annulus counterexample

## Correctness

PASS. The source's exact Fourier representation gives the same perturbation term in the inner-boundary quantities
\[
P
\]
and
\[
Q.
\]
Under
\[
m s^{-m}/t\to\lambda,
\]
the
\[
j=\pm1
\]
pair contributes
\[
SD\lambda t^2+o(t^2)
\]
to the maximal real leakage, while every higher pair is
\[
o(t^2).
\]
The quotient expansion has linear coefficient
\[
1/(2a(0)^2),
\]
and the source's first radial mode has deficit
\[
-c_{s,S}t^2.
\]
Combining them yields the stated coefficient
\[
c_{s,S}(2D^2\lambda-1).
\]

The outer boundary remains a fixed distance below the conjectural level, because its first-mode ratio tends to
\[
\kappa/s^2
\]
and its perturbation is
\[
o(1).
\]
The source's nonvanishing and degree argument then remains valid, so both the norm asymptotic and the diffeomorphism assertion are justified.

## Originality

PASS. The fresh source chooses an intentionally oversized frequency that forces the high-frequency leakage to be
\[
o(t^2).
\]
It does not retain the leading leakage term, identify a balancing parameter, or state a log--log frequency boundary.

The original Iwaniec--Kovalev--Onninen annulus theory predates this non-radial construction and therefore cannot imply the perturbative frequency threshold.

Searches covering the exact preprint, high-frequency annulus perturbations, minimal oscillation frequency, and the balancing quantity
\[
m s^{-m}/t
\]
did not locate an implication-equivalent statement.

## Value

PASS. High frequency is the central design parameter that makes the new counterexample work. Determining how high the frequency actually needs to be is therefore a structural question about the mechanism, not an arbitrary coefficient refinement.

The result exhibits a sharp two-effect balance: the radial improvement is quadratic in the perturbation size, while the first leaked Fourier pair is exponentially damped in the frequency. Their competition produces an unavoidable
\[
\log\log(1/t)
\]
correction and an exact phase constant. It also identifies paths for which the same construction provably succeeds and paths for which it provably fails to cross the conjectural threshold.
