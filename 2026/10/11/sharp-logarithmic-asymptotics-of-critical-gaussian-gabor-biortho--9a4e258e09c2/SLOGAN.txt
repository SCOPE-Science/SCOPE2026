The unique biorthogonal vectors of the critical-density Gaussian Gabor system, after deletion of any one lattice atom, have squared norms \(C_{\mathrm G}\log R+O(1)\), where \(R\) is their lattice distance from the hole and \(C_{\mathrm G}=4/(\pi|\theta_1'(0,e^{-\pi})|^2)\approx1.54852449\). This proves the recently stated logarithmic-norm conjecture with a sharp leading coefficient and an error uniform in direction and the deleted atom.
