At the exact bullying-index threshold \(R_0=1\), every strictly positive trajectory relaxes algebraically: \(t\,y(t)\to\beta/(bc)\) and \(t(x(t)-c/\alpha)\to-1/\alpha\). More sharply, \(1/y(t)=(bc/\beta)t+b(c-\beta)\log t/\beta^2+o(\log t)\), so the first logarithmic correction records the mismatch between bully-removal and ordinary student-departure rates.
