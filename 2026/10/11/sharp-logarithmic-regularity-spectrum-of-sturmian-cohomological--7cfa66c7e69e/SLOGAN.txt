Every continuous transfer of a centered Lipschitz observable on an irrational Sturmian shift has cylinder oscillations equal to \(|c_f|\) times the largest rotation gap, up to an exponentially small error. When \(c_f\ne0\), its exact logarithmic regularity thresholds are \(\sup_k q_{k+1}^{s}/q_k<\infty\), with critical exponent \(1/(\mu_{\mathrm{irr}}(\alpha)-1)\); bounded-type slopes alone attain the full inverse-logarithm endpoint.
