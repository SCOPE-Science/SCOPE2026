For the unit disk weighted by the actual Euclidean boundary distance \((1-|z|)^t\), the Bergman kernel is zero-free exactly when \(-1<t\le2\). Above that sharp threshold its zeros are all simple and negative in \(z\overline w\), explicitly at \(-\tan^2(\pi j/(t+2))\), and a new zero enters whenever \(t\) crosses \(4j-2\).
