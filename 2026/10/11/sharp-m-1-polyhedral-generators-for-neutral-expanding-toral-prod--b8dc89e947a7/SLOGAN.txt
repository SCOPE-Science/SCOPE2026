For the Haar system \((\theta,y)\mapsto(\theta,my\bmod1)\), the exact minimum number of symbols in a one-sided generating partition is \(m+1\), achieved by polygonal cells for every \(m\ge2\). Splitting just the zero-digit strip at \(y=\theta/m\) makes the neutral coordinate recoverable from a symbol frequency, while maximal entropy rules out any \(m\)-symbol generator.
