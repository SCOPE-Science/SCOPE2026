The Davis–Wielandt radius of every nonzero rank-one Hilbert-space operator has a unique explicit cubic optimizer away from nilpotency, but at zero generating-vector overlap its maximizing rays undergo a sharp transition at squared norm \(1/2\). At the critical norm, infinitesimal overlap creates the universal fractional response \(z_*(c)\sim c^{1/3}\) and \(\operatorname{dw}(T)=1/2+(3/4)c^{4/3}+o(c^{4/3})\).
