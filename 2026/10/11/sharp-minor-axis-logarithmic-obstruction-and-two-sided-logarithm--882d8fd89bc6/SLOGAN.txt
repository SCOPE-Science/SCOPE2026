For every noncircular ellipse, the minimum number of equal-Euclidean-length translates needed to cover the ellipse diverges logarithmically as the required length approaches its major semiaxis. The covering obstruction on its minor-axis segment has the exact leading coefficient \(1/\log((1+\kappa)/(1-\kappa))\), where \(\kappa\) is the minor-to-major semiaxis ratio; the full covering number has matching logarithmic upper and lower orders.
