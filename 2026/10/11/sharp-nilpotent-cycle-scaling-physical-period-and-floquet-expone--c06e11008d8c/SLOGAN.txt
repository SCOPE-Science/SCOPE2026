For \(\dot x=y\), \(\dot y=-x^3+\alpha x^2y+y^3\), the unique unstable cycle shrinks at rate \(\sqrt{-\alpha}\) as \(\alpha\uparrow0^-\), while its original-time period diverges like \(T_*/\sqrt{-\alpha}\) and its logarithmic Floquet multiplier vanishes like \(J_*(-\alpha)^{3/2}\), with explicit beta-function constants. Exact parameter-reversing symmetry cancels the first correction in each quantity.
