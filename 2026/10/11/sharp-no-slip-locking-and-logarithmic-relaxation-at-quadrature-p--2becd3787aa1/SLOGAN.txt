For two internally synchronized unequal clusters in the discrete-time Kuramoto phase-lag model at quadrature coupling, the sharp no-phase-slip window is \(K(p-q)/N\le B_*=1.380050139689300948\ldots\), where \(B_*\) is determined by \(\tan(t_*/2)=t_*\). Throughout this window every non-finite-hitting relative phase locks with the exact reciprocal law \((2\pi-\delta_n)^{-1}=(B/2)n+(B/2)\log n+C+o(1)\), a logarithmic correction absent from the corresponding continuous-time flow.
