For the finite nilpotent shift on real or complex \(\ell_p^n\), the sharp spatial numerical-radius deficit obeys \(1-v_p(S_n)\sim 2(p-1)\pi^2/[p^2(n+1)^2]\) at every fixed \(1<p<\infty\). Its coefficient is the local Young-defect curvature times the first Dirichlet eigenvalue, extending the Hilbert shift's cosine law.
