# Sharp nonexpansive boundary and endpoint global attraction for the saturating Ikeda map

## Correctness — PASS
The Jacobian is a radial-tangential shear with exact singular norm; its maximum is attained on the unit circle. The boundary strict distance reduction follows by integrating over chords that intersect that circle at finitely many points. Global convergence follows from a forward-invariant disk, existence/uniqueness of a fixed point, and compactness. A SymPy checker reproduces the algebra; it is not a substitute for the full analytic argument.

## Originality — PASS
Direct published-finding corpus searches on aliases, shear singular values, sharp contraction thresholds, exact tables, and stronger global Ikeda stability coverage returned no statement dominating the finding. A prior stationary Ikeda variance result is logically weaker and incomparable regarding pairwise distances. Hansson–Wabnitz 2015 full text treats a different Kerr phase law and local multistability. Original 1979/1980 articles and 1998 intervention analysis were only accessible at abstract/bibliographic level; residual coverage uncertainty remains explicit.

## Value — PASS
An exact, naturally parameterized robustness frontier separates uniform Euclidean contraction, boundary nonuniform strict contraction, and loss of Euclidean nonexpansiveness for a canonical optical-cavity map. Unlike a raw differential-norm computation, the endpoint establishes global convergence for every initial state in a setting where the standard \(q<1\) contraction theorem does not directly apply. This does not claim a sharp global stability threshold beyond the certified region.
