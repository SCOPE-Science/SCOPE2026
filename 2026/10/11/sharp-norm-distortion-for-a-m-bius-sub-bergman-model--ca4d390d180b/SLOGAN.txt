For the positive-contraction Möbius model \(B_A(z)=(zI-A)(I-zA)^{-1}\), the set equality \(\mathcal A_\alpha(B_A)=A^2_{\alpha-1,\mathcal E}\) has exact two-sided distortion \(\sqrt{(1+\|A\|)/(1-\|A\|)}\) for every \(\alpha\ge0\). In Luo--Shen's diagonal nonrigidity example this constant is exactly \(\sqrt7\).
