For a real norm-one functional on \(L_1\), the adjusted Bhatia–Šemrl property holds exactly when its essential maximum-modulus level has positive measure and is isolated from all remaining values. This is equivalent to a finite global norming-face error bound, whose sharp constant is \(2/(1-r)\) when the complementary essential supremum is \(r<1\), and equals \(1\) when there is no complement.
