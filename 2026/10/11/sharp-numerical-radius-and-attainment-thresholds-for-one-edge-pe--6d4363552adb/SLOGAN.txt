For one exceptional weight \(t\) in an otherwise unit unilateral shift on real or complex \(\ell_p\), the forward spatial numerical radius remains one exactly up to \(p^{1/p}\), while the backward radius remains one exactly up to \(q^{1/q}\), with \(q=p/(p-1)\). In either direction the radius is attained precisely when it exceeds one, and the two thresholds differ away from the Hilbert exponent.
