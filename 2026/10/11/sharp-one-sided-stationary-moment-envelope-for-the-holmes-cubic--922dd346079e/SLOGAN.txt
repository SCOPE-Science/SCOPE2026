For every compact first-quadrant stationary state of the Holmes cubic map, the mean \(m\) and variance obey the sharp fixed-point envelope \(0\le m\le\rho\) and \(\operatorname{Var}(x)\le m(\rho-m)\), where \(\rho=\sqrt{a-b-1}\). Equality occurs exactly for mixtures of the origin and the positive fixed point, so every one-sided chaotic stationary state lies strictly inside the envelope.
