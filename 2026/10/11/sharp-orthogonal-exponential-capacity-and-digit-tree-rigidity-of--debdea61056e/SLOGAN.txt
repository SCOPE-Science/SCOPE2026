For every odd prime \(p\), the modular hyperbola over \(\mathbb Z/p^k\mathbb Z\) supports at most \(p^{k-1}\) pairwise orthogonal restricted additive characters, and this bound is sharp. All orthogonal frequency sets are characterized by a local quadratic-character compatibility rule on base-\(p\) digit trees, yielding a complete structural description of every extremal family.
