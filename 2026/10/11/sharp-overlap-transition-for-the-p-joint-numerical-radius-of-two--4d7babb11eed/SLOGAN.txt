For two rank-one orthogonal projections with ray overlap \(c\), the balanced \(p\)-joint numerical radius has one maximizing ray exactly when \(c\ge1-1/p\), and two symmetry-related maximizing rays below that sharp threshold. For \(p=2\) the exact squared radius changes from \((1-c^2)^2/[2(1-2c^2)]\) to \((1+c)^2/4\) at \(c=1/2\).
