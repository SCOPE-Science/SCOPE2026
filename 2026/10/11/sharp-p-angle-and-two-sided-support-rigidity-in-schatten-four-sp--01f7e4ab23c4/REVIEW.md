# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Exact trace polarization for the fourth Schatten power gives the nonnegative operator term \(16-(a^4+b^4)\). The scalar identity with \(k=2-\sqrt2\) gives a second nonnegative term, so their sum is the claimed exact P-angle deficit. Both denominators are positive for the permitted pairs. Equality forces balanced chords and two-sided disjoint supports; the right-support projection argument upgrades the trace conditions to vanishing of both mixed adjoint products. The converse is verified by explicit two-dimensional disjoint projections. The bundled exact checker confirms algebraic trace and scalar identities independently of floating-point optimization.

The full Alrimawi--Hirzallah--Kittaneh paper gives matrix Clarkson refinements but no angular profile. Targeted searches of the named constant, Pythagorean-angle synonyms, Schatten-four matrices, the exact radical, and the support-overlap decomposition yielded no covering record. The closest indexed exact P-angle computation is on commutative \(\ell_4\); this yields the lower bound only and not the noncommutative sharp inequality/equality locus. Historical own ledgers were searched for the same object and claim without finding overlap.

## Value
PASS. The result is a structural operator inequality on every Schatten-four class, not a standalone scalar evaluation: it identifies all maximizing ordered pairs and furnishes a positive exact defect separating two-sided support overlap and chord imbalance. The theorem connects a natural Banach-space angular invariant to genuinely noncommutative support geometry.
