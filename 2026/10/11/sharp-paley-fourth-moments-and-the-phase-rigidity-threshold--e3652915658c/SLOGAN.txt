The sharp normalized fourth moment for the Paley quadratic-residue Fourier spectrum is \((m^2+1)/(2m)\) times the squared coefficient energy, where \(m=(p-1)/2\) and \(p\equiv3\pmod4\). Equality phases form a projective two-torus at \(p=7\), but for every allowable prime \(p\ge11\), the only nonzero extremizers are scalar multiples of additive characters restricted to the residue spectrum, giving exactly \(p\) projective extremizers.
