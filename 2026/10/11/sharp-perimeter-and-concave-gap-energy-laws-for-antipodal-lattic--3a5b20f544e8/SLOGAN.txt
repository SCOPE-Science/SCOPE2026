For every even circular lattice, selecting one endpoint of every diameter gives a sharp perimeter spectrum: the minimum uses consecutive vertices, while the maximum alternates except for one antipodally forced \(1,3\) gap pair. The same exact extremizers optimize **every** strictly concave edge-gap energy; the even-order perimeter loss from the unconstrained regular polygon is \(4R\sin(\pi/n)(1-\cos(\pi/(2n)))\).
