For ellipses avoiding a planar lattice, the sharp supremum of \(P-\lambda R\) is infinite below \(\lambda=4\), equals \((2\pi-\lambda)\rho(\Lambda)\) between \(4\) and \(2\pi\), and is zero thereafter. In the positive finite regime, exactly the largest lattice-free disks centered at deepest lattice holes are optimal; in particular, for \(\mathbb Z^2\), \(P-4R\le\sqrt2(\pi-2)\) sharply.
