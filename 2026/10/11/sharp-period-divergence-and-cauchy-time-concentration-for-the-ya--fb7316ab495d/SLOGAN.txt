On the classical Yablonskii quartic oval, the original-time period increases strictly and diverges as \((c_*-c)^{-1/2}\) with the explicit coefficient \(\pi/[x_*\sqrt{b-a}\,((3a-b)(3b-a))^{1/4}]\). Near arrest the trajectory spends asymptotically all its time at one lower-branch point, while its normalized position fluctuations obey a universal Cauchy law.
