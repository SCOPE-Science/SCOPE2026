For the three consecutive sites of \(\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z\), the fewest distinct positively weighted Fourier characters forming a tight frame is exactly \(3\), \(4\), or \(5\), according as \(3\mid N\), \(2\mid N\) but \(3
mid N\), or \(\gcd(N,6)=1\). For every prime \(p\ge7\), positive weighting reduces the necessary number of samples from \(p\) with equal weights to just five; all five-node rules admit explicit unique weight tests.
