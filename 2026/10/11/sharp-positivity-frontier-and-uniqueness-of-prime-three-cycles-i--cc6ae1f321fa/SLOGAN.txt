For the rational recurrence \(z_{n+1}=h+bz_n/(z_{n-1}-1)\), \(b>0\) and \(0<h<1\), a strictly positive prime-three orbit exists **exactly** when \(0<b<1\) and \(h>1-b+b^2\). It is then unique up to rotation, with all three entries in \((0,h)\); the boundary has only the previously known nonnegative three-cycle.
