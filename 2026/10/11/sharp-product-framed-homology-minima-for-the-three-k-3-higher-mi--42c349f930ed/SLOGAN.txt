For the three \(k=3\) model higher mixed sums, the minimum first-homology orders among equatorially product-framed full-rank gluings are exactly \(64\), \(27\), and \(24\), attained by \((\mathbb Z/4)^3\), \((\mathbb Z/3)^3\), and \(\mathbb Z/6\oplus(\mathbb Z/2)^2\). The key lower bound is the product-framed divisibility \(pq\gcd(p,q)\mid |H_1|\), and explicit symplectic transvection certificates attain it in all three model cases.
