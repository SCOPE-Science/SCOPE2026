For the real hybrid norm \(N_t(z)=\bigl(\|z\|_1^2+t\|z\|_2^2\bigr)^{1/2}\), the sharp quadratic midpoint-convexity coefficient in every dimension \(n\ge2\) is \(c=(s-1)/(s+1)\), where \(s=\sqrt{(1+t/\lfloor n/2\rfloor)(1+t/\lceil n/2\rceil)}\). The classical modulus of convexity equals \(1-\sqrt{1-c\varepsilon^2/4}\) exactly through \(\varepsilon=2/\sqrt{1+c}\) and is strictly larger afterward.
