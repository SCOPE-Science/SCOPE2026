# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The cubic factor in the primary source is exactly the characteristic polynomial of the symmetric \(3\times3\) block used in the proof. The signed substitution at \(L=\sqrt{x_1^2+(x_2+x_3)^2+(x_2-x_3)^2}\) covers all admissible real weights and all regular boundary strata. Equality is classified algebraically; Cauchy interlacing gives the lower-eigenvalue bound. The finite checker is supplementary only.

## Originality
PASS. The primary source already reports from plots/Maple that the regular icosahedron globally minimizes the second-largest eigenvalue, so that observation is not claimed as new. The accepted claim is the stronger pointwise quadratic stability inequality with equality line and a formal proof. Targeted searches against the same object, earlier spectral-representation theory, fastest-mixing symmetry literature, the published-finding index and the prior own ledger found no implication covering that quantitative statement. Residual publication risk remains because search failure is not a novelty proof.

## Value
PASS. The source explicitly notes the absence of a formal proof for its tetrahedral-group observations. The inequality resolves that gap and adds a natural weighted-distance stability term with an immediate two-sided mixing interpretation.
