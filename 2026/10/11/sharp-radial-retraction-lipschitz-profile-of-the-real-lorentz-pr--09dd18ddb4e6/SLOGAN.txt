The canonical radial projection onto the ball of the real weighted Lorentz-predual octagon has sharp Lipschitz constant \(\max\{1+w^2,2(1+w^2)/(1+w)^2\}\). Its nonmonotone distortion is uniquely smallest for the regular octagon, where the exact constant is \(4-2\sqrt2\), strictly improving the universal constant \(2\).
