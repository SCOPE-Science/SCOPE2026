## Mathematical value — PASS
The exact, naturally posed question is the chance that at least one of polynomially many independent, untrained observations has greater variance contribution from the slowing mode than from all stable modes together. The derived probability law resolves the transition, including its nontrivial critical window and joint lag-correlation behavior. It provides quantitative sensor-budget guidance for a linear surrogate, not a guarantee of detecting nonlinear tipping events from finite datasets.
