For polynomially many independent random observation directions in a high-dimensional Ornstein–Uhlenbeck system with one slowing mode, the chance that any sensor's slow-mode variance dominates the stable-mode variance has an exact Poisson/Gumbel critical window at \(\epsilon_n=(2\log m_n-\log\log m_n-\log\pi+2x)/n\). The same extreme-value geometry gives a sharp three-regime law for maximum conditional population variance and lag autocorrelation.
