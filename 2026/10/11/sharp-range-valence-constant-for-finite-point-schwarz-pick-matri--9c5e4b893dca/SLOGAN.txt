For disk maps of valence at most \(m\) whose images lie in a radius-\(\rho\) disk, the optimal \(n\)-point Bergman Schwarz–Pick matrix constant is exactly \(\rho^2\min\{n,m\}\), without requiring a proper map or a Blaschke product. Sharpness moves to configurations collapsing onto an interior branch point when \(\rho<1\).
