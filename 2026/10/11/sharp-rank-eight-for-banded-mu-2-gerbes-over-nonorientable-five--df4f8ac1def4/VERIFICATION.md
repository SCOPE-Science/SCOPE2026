# Reproduction and limits

The proof is mathematical, based on the Wang sequence, Pontryagin-square naturality, integral Poincaré duality, the Antieau--Williams index theorem and the Becerra--Lupercio gerbe realization theorem. These statements are explicitly referenced in `RESULT.md`.

Run `python3 proof_check.py` in a directory containing the standalone script. It checks:

* Integral monodromy \(f^*:H^2(S^2\times S^2;\mathbb Z)\to H^2(S^2\times S^2;\mathbb Z)\), its Smith invariants, and the expected \(\mathbb Z/2\) cokernel.
* The one-dimensional \(\mathbb F_2\)-fixed subspace generated by the sum of the sphere classes.
* The degree-four monodromy-invariant group \(\{0,2uv\}\) modulo four, and that the integral lift of \(\xi|_F\) has square \(2uv\).
* The algebraic implications of the nonzero coefficient Bockstein \(\beta_4(P_2(\xi))\), index four, and regular rank eight.

The executable checks are finite matrix and coefficient arithmetic. They **do not** independently establish the general topological theorems, existence of the gerbe bundle, or global originality, and they should not be misinterpreted as formal proof certificates for those points.

Exact inputs: `A = [[0,-1],[1,0]]`, 4-dimensional orientation sign \(-1\), and the ordinary cup-product relation \((u+v)^2=2uv\). Expected output: `VERIFY_OK`.
