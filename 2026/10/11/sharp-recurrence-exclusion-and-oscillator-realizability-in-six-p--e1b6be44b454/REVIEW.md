# Scientific review of the sharp quadratic-jerk recurrence criterion

**Correctness — PASS.** The exact Lie-derivative identity reduces the recurrence obstruction to a globally nonnegative sum of squares. For a bounded forward orbit, compactness of the omega-limit set and constancy of the limiting monotone observable force its largest invariant zero set to be the unique equilibrium, including the equality boundary. Bounded complete trajectories and compact invariant probability measures are handled separately and without an assumed global absorbing set. The opposite-sign oscillator is an explicit solution for every prescribed pair of opposite-sign coefficients; an executable symbolic checker independently confirms the algebra and source-table substitutions. The claim does not infer infinite dynamics from finite simulations.

**Originality — PASS with disclosed historical risk.** Wei–Sprott–Chen (2015), Eq. (4) and Table 1, are precisely the primary object and parameter list and were read in full. They establish numerical examples and equilibrium spectra, not the global sign sector, equality-boundary convergence, measure classification, or a sharp realization construction. Liu–Sang–Wang–Ahmad (2021), Sec. 4, derive an analogous monotone polynomial for a *different* jerk system containing linear position and quadratic acceleration terms, not this unique-nonhyperbolic-equilibrium family. The 2015 Wei–Zhang–Yao zero-Hopf study is closer historically, but its accessible abstract concerns periodic-orbit bifurcation of one flow; full text could not be checked, leaving a genuine risk. Similar published recurrence-balance results concern different vector fields and do not imply this six-coefficient theorem. Searches alone cannot prove historical uniqueness.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The 2015 source classified nine numerically observed chaotic regimes by eigenvalue type, but offered no coefficient-level theorem limiting where *any* compact recurrence is mathematically possible. This result supplies a sharp closed semialgebraic exclusion region across all six coefficients, including its boundary, and explicitly constructs oscillatory witnesses on the other side. It simultaneously fixes a parameter-independent stationary moment ratio for possible recurrent regimes, a diagnostic applicable to all nine source models. The natural coefficient cone and its sharpness, rather than a single isolated numerical identity, motivate the assertion; no paper-sized extension or proof of chaotic existence is claimed.

**Residual limits.** Arbitrary forward trajectories need not be bounded; an opposite-sign pair does not force every remaining-coefficient choice to be recurrent. Historical priority requires further checking of the inaccessible 2015 bifurcation study and possible unindexed equivalent results.
