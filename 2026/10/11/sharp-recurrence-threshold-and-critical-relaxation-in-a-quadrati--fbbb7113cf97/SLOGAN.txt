For the Zhao–Lin–Dai quadratic chaotic flow, the equilibrium discriminant \(\Delta=d^2+4bc\) is the **exact threshold for every compact invariant set**, not just for equilibria. At \(\Delta=0\), all bounded forward-complete orbits converge to one equilibrium and, outside its global strong-stable manifold, satisfy the sharp law \(t(z(t)-d/(2c))\to1/c\).
