For Sprott's case-N quadratic flow with \(a\ne0\), a centered Euclidean ball permits arbitrarily long solution segments exactly when it contains the unique equilibrium, at radius \(\sqrt{b^{-4}+b^{-2}}\) for \(b\ne0\); when \(b=0\), no bounded forward trajectory exists. Every compact stationary state additionally satisfies an exact height-conditioned mean law and a strict radial-moment defect away from the equilibrium measure.
