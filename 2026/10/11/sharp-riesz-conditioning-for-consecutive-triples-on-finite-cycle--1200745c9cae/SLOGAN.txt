For every cyclic order \(N\ge3\), the unique optimal gap geometry for the exponential Riesz basis of \(\{0,1,2\}\subset\mathbb Z_N\) is an almost-equilateral frequency triangle: its three cyclic gaps differ by at most one. The sharp squared spectral condition number has an explicit trigonometric radical formula, with exactly \(N/3\) optimal spectra when \(3\mid N\) and \(N\) otherwise.
