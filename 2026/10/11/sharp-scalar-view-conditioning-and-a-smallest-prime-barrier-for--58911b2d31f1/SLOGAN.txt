For complete projective line tomography on every cyclic square grid, no positive scalar rescaling of whole projection directions can beat uniform weights on the mean-zero subspace: the sharp squared condition number is \(N\prod_{p\mid N}(1+p^{-1})/(p_*+1)\), attained only uniformly. Exact weighted spectral endpoints and the entire unweighted singular spectrum follow from Fourier orthogonality and prime-power reduction.
