For the expanding circle map \(x\mapsto bx\pmod1\) with integer \(b\ge2\), the critical-window orbit pair-correlation count has an exact asymptotic variance \(4s(b+1)/((b-1)N)\), although its typical-orbit first-order limit is Poissonian. The variance exceeds the independent-point benchmark by precisely \((b+1)/(b-1)\).
