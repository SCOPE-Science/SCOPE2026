For three distinct parabola frequencies over every odd finite field, the sharp sixth-moment extension coefficient is \(31/(9q)\) outside characteristic three and \(11/(3q)\) in characteristic three. Every optimizer has equal coefficient magnitudes; ternary characteristic imposes the additional rigid condition \(f(t_0)^3=f(t_1)^3=f(t_2)^3\), yielding nine projective extremizers and the global sharp constant \(11/9\) on \(\mathbb F_3\).
