# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof reconstructs the discrete cubic convolution and its phase majorant, establishes reflected RMS symmetrization by two-dimensional Cauchy--Schwarz on cyclic autocorrelations, and solves each one-variable rational maximization. The derivative factorizations and positive-root uniqueness arguments cover every integer \(r\ge3\). The equality analysis forces affine coefficient phases, adds the root-of-unity constraint exactly for \(r\le6\), and shows that a sharp group extremizer localizes on a single annihilator coset. Symbolic identities and finite instances were replayed from the embedded checker. No finite test is treated as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual historical risk
The finite-cyclic semigroup hypercontractivity results of Junge--Palazuelos--Parcet--Perrin concern the length-weighted time threshold, not an unweighted sharp \(L^2\)-to-\(L^6\) subspace constant. Nursultanov--Ruzhansky--Tikhonov provide general support-based Nikolskii bounds without this exact quotient-order classification. The known continuous analytic-quadratic case supplies the stabilized \(r\ge7\) branch, not the finite aliasing cases or localized three-coset extremizers. Searches for sharp cyclic sixth moments, finite-grid Nikolskii constants, equality phases and subgroup-coset formulations did not identify a dominating theorem. Older specialized tables remain a realistic residual priority risk, so a failed search is not counted as proof of novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is an exact classification for a natural infinite family of Fourier subspaces, not an arbitrarily selected numerical sample. The quotient order \(r\) controls an explicit sharp norm with distinct torsion constants and a rigid-to-continuous phase transition at order seven. The full equality classification supplies concrete localization of optimizers on the annihilator cosets, which is useful when reconstructing sharp Nikolskii behavior of structured frequency unions. The ordinary circle case is acknowledged as existing rather than recycled as new.
