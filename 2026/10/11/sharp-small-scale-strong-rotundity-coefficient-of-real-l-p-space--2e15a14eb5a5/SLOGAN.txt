For every real \(L_p\) space with two disjoint positive-measure pieces and \(p>2\), the global strong-rotundity index has the sharp leading term \(c_p\varepsilon^p\), where \(c_p\) is the minimum of a one-dimensional scalar convexity remainder. In the quartic case its exact coefficient is \(c_4=1/12\), attained asymptotically by a two-coordinate boundary perturbation.
