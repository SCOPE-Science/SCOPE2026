For the alternating sine initial state of the even-species biased cyclic spatial replicator, nonconstant frozen waves persist **exactly when** \(-aL^2>4\pi^2ND\); otherwise all species homogenize, including at equality. The surviving waves have explicit Jacobi-elliptic profiles and an exact positive periodic instability eigenvalue, while \(N=52\), \(a=-1/2\), \(D=1/100\), and \(L=2\pi\) give a concrete large-cycle homogenization counterexample to an unconditional frozen-wave conjecture.
