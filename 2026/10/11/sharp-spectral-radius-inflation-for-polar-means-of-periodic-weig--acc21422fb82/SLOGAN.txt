For positive \(n\)-periodic weighted shifts of weight contrast at most \(K\), the sharp spectral-radius amplification under the polar \(\lambda\)-mean equals \(\left(1+\lambda(1-\lambda)(K-1)^2/K\right)^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor/n}\). Extremizers alternate between the two extreme weights on as many cycle edges as possible; the unavoidable equal edge for odd periods produces an exact parity defect.
