For finite diagonal heat generators with geometrically spaced eigenvalues, the endpoint \(L^1(\ell_r^N)\) maximal-regularity norm grows as \(\kappa_r(b)N^{1-1/r}\) with an explicit error bound and an exact log-periodic integral for the coefficient. The coefficient also has sharp dense- and sparse-spectrum limits, quantifying how spectral spacing controls endpoint blow-up.
