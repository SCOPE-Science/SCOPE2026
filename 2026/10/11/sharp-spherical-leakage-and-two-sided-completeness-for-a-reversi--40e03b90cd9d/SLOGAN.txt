For a reversible quadratic flow with coexisting reported chaos and invariant tori, an exact radial identity proves complete real-time existence and identifies every outward-pointing patch on large spheres. Their surface fraction is \(1/(3\sqrt3R^2)+O(R^{-4})\), while the total outward normal flux is \(2\pi/(9\sqrt3R)+O(R^{-3})\); this does not prove eventual boundedness.
