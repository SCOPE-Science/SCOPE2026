The true fixed-center Bohr radius for bounded complex harmonic disk maps obeys \(R_H(a)\ge\sqrt{(1-a)/(3+a)}\), with the **sharp boundary law** \(R_H(a)\sim\frac12\sqrt{1-a}\) as \(a\uparrow1\). This disproves the claimed sharpness of the earlier linear radius while explaining why the radius jumps to \(1\) at the exactly constant endpoint \(a=1\).
