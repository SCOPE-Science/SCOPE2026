For every bounded, possibly nonnormal \(T\) with \(|\operatorname{Re}T|=\|\operatorname{Re}T\|P\) for a projection \(P\), its imaginary part obeys the sharp dimension-free bound \(\|\operatorname{Im}T\|^2\le d(T)(2\|\operatorname{Re}T\|+d(T))\), where \(d(T)=\|(|T|-|\operatorname{Re}T|)_+\|\). For every fixed eligible real part this is the exact optimal profile, with best small-defect exponent \(1/2\).
