In the canonical six-blocker chamber of the sharp planar lattice-transference inequality, a deficit \(\varepsilon\le1/90\) forces Euclidean Hausdorff distance at most \(10\sqrt\varepsilon\) from the extremal hexagon. An explicit centrally symmetric hexagon deformation achieves distance of order \(\sqrt\varepsilon\), proving that the exponent \(1/2\) is optimal within this chamber.
