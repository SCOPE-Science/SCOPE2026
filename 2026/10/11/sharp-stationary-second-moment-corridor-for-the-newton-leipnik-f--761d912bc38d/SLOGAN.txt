For the nominal Newton–Leipnik flow, every compact stationary state has an exact energy-plane identity and a sharp interval for \(\int x^2\,d\mu/\int z^2\,d\mu\), with endpoints \((637\pm35\sqrt{257})/928\). Endpoint equality is rigid: only mixtures of the origin and one corresponding sign-pair of nonzero equilibria attain it, so every non-equilibrium ergodic state lies strictly inside the corridor.
