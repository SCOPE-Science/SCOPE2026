PASS. The pointwise identity follows by substituting the second-coordinate update into the lifted first-coordinate increment. ## Value
PASS. Ballistic and anomalous transport are central observables for this map. The theorem supplies a parameter-explicit hard transport window for every compact stationary regime, converts every finite-time lift fluctuation exactly into an integrated-\(y\) fluctuation, and classifies saturation by arithmetic resonance. Thus it gives a reusable structural constraint on periodic, quasiperiodic, and chaotic stationary states rather than a parameter-specific numerical observation.
