For the reflection-symmetric Sprott flow, an exact squared-defect identity forces \(\operatorname{Var}(x^2)\ge a^2\operatorname{Var}(y)\), with equality only for mixtures of the two off-plane equilibria. Along the stable cycles emerging from the Hopf point \(a=2\), the strictly positive defect is asymptotic to \((2-a)^2\), making this fluctuation bound asymptotically sharp. Off the reflection plane, the flow Jacobian is exactly \(e^{-at}|x(t)/x(0)|\).
