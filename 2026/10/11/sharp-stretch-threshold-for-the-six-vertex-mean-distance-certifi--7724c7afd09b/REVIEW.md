# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reconstructs the exact surface-coordinate parameters from the source, derives the identity
\[
g_{{L,\\varepsilon}}-\\frac4{{15}}=\\frac{{\\alpha}}{{45S^2}}\\left(6S^2+2S-\\alpha(3S^2-3S+2)\\right),
\]
uses the exact denominator to obtain \(0<\\alpha<3/2\), and proves strict positivity for every \(S>1\). The matching \(\\varepsilon\\downarrow0\) limit and exact fraction comparison give the iff threshold. The checker only confirms algebraic manipulations.

## Originality
PASS. The closest source, arXiv:2608.06470, gives the exact family, the \(L=1000\) example, and a sufficiently-large-\(L\) asymptotic criterion; it does not state a finite sharp \(L\)-threshold for the same certificate. arXiv:2608.14848 gives an independent but different centrally symmetric stretching family. Targeted searches for the exact constant, the equivalent infimum statement, and the two characteristic Gini constants found no covering source. The main residual risk is an unindexed independent optimization of this elementary certificate.

## Value
PASS. The result answers a natural quantitative question left by the explicit counterexample: how elongated must Shen's own projection-diameter proof make the six-vertex body? The exact answer for that certificate is \(L>2625/19\), a substantial reduction from the exhibited \(L=1000\), together with a proof that improving below that cutoff requires a genuinely sharper estimate rather than parameter tuning.
