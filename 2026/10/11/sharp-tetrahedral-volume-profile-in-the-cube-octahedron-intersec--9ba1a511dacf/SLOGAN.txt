For the full cube–octahedron intersection family \(K_c=[-1,1]^3\cap\{\|x\|_1\le c\}\), \(1\le c\le3\), the maximal inscribed tetrahedron volume has an exact three-phase piecewise-quadratic profile. The normalized volume fraction is uniquely minimized at the regular truncated octahedron \(c=\tfrac32\), where it equals \(1/6\), and it exhibits a second, higher local minimum beyond the cuboctahedron.
