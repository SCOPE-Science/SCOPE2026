For positive operators with the same two-point spectrum in any finite tracial von Neumann algebra, the exact universal coefficient in the crossed-Heinz trace-norm deficit is \(\min\{(a^t-b^t)(a^{1-t}-b^{1-t}),\,(\sqrt a+\sqrt b)^2/2\}/(a-b)^2\). In the high-contrast regime it strictly, and by an unbounded factor asymptotically, improves the sharp coefficient obtained from the corresponding Loewner-order inequality.
