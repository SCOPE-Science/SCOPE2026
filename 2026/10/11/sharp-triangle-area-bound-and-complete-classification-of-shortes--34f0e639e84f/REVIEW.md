# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
Every perimeter-halving chord of a nondegenerate triangle is controlled by the unique vertex shared by its endpoint edges. With the two arc lengths from that vertex summing to the semiperimeter, the cosine-law identity gives the exact shortest-chord formula and the complete equality classification. Heron’s formula and the minimum-angle half-angle identity reduce the area bound to two uniform inequalities on ordered positive tangent lengths. Equality in both forces an equilateral triangle. Exact-rational and independent direct boundary-minimization checks corroborate the proof without substituting for an all-triangle argument.

## Originality — PASS, best of knowledge
Dumitrescu and collaborators established the convex-cycle inequality A/h² ≥ 1/√3 and the single equilateral ratio 4/(3√3). Their WADS 2005 result δ(T) ≥ 2 for triangles is weaker than the full angle-based chord formula and does not imply the sharp triangle area comparison. The inspected relevant sections do not state an all-triangle sharp area theorem or classify all shortest chords. The 2006/2008 Grüne dissertation and 2007 related article were not materially available in full; they remain acknowledged priority risks. Existing results for other triangle-roundness measures and isosceles trapezoids concern different objectives.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The area/minimum-halving-chord ratio is an explicitly motivated convex-geometric invariant. Its sharp lower bound and unique equilateral equality shape for all nondegenerate triangles resolve a natural restricted extremal family with more information than the previously known equilateral example. The classification of all minimizing chords gives a constructive geometric description. No claim is made for arbitrary convex cycles.
