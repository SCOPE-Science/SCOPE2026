For every nondegenerate Euclidean triangle, the shortest perimeter-halving chord has length \(s\sin(\alpha/2)\), where \(s\) is the semiperimeter and \(\alpha\) the smallest angle; its endpoints lie halfway along the two edges from a smallest-angle vertex. The sharp area ratio is \(A/h^2\ge4/(3\sqrt3)\), with equality precisely for equilateral triangles.
