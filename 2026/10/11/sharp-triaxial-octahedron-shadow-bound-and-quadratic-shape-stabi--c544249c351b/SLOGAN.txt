For every three-axis stretch of a Euclidean octahedron, a random planar shadow is hexagonal with probability at most \(\frac4\pi\arctan(\sqrt2/5)\), and equality holds only for the regular octahedron. The optimal probability drops by \(\frac{1}{\sqrt2\pi}\sum_i(\ell_i-\bar\ell)^2\) to second order in centered logarithmic axis lengths.
