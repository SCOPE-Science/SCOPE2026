For the radius-three Hamming ball in \(\mathbb F_2^d\), the exact positive-definite Turán mass is \(2d+2\) in odd dimensions and \(2d\) in even dimensions. Every odd-dimensional optimizer for \(d\ge5\) lies in an explicit \((d-1)\)-simplex of nonradial extremizers, while even-dimensional radial optimizers form an exact interval.
