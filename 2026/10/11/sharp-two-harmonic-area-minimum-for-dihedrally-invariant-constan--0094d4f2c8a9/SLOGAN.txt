Among dihedrally invariant planar bodies of constant width \(w\), restricting the support function to the first two admissible harmonics \(n\) and \(3n\) gives an exact sharp area minimum \(\pi w^2/4-\pi w^2F_n/[8(n^2-1)]\), with two explicit minimizing shapes. The extra harmonic increases the optimal disk-area deficit by the sharp factor \(F_n>4/3\) over the one-harmonic model, uniformly for every odd \(n\ge3\).
