# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Set \(z=x-y\). The equilateral condition becomes \(x,z,x-z\in S_{X_8}\). Dihedral symmetry puts \(x=(1,a)\) with \(-(\sqrt2-1)\le a\le\sqrt2-1\), and exchanging \(z\) with \(x-z\) swaps the two target lengths. Direct boundary intersection gives three exact branches. On the two outer branches one target is constantly \(2\sqrt2-1\); on the middle branch both are explicit affine functions and the interval endpoint \(3-2\sqrt2\) gives the same bound. The pair \(x=(1,1-\sqrt2)\), \(y=(3-2\sqrt2,-1)\) attains both bounds, so the three mean constants follow exactly.

## Originality
PASS. The defining 2022 full text was inspected at Definitions 5, 6 and 8, its general inequalities, examples and conclusion; no regular-octagonal evaluation is present. Public searches under exact notation, octagon aliases and the candidate value found no covering \(M_1,M_2,M_3\) statement. The closest octagonal result computes a geometric mean on the same equilateral domain, which does not imply separate upper bounds on both factors. The current and immediate-prior ledgers were also checked; the closest own midline calculation is for the regular dodecagon, not the octagon.

## Value
PASS. The structural content is the simultaneous two-coordinate envelope, not just three derived numbers. It controls every admissible equilateral triangle, resolves three different mean objectives at once, and identifies a common sharp configuration. This gives a natural exact benchmark for a canonical regular polygon and a precise comparison with the known geometric-mean invariant.

## Closest literature and limitations
Chen, Yang, Liu and Li introduced \(M_1\), \(M_2\) and \(M_3\) in *Symmetry* 14 (2022), article 348. A later exact result for the same regular octagon computes the equilateral geometric-mean constant \(T_G\), but a product bound does not imply the coordinatewise theorem proved here. The present result does not classify every maximizing pair or extend the formula to all regular polygons.
