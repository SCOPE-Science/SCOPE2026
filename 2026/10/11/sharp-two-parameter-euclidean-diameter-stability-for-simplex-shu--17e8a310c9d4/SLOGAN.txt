For the Euclidean shuffle of two standard simplices of dimensions \(m-1\) and \(n-1\), weighted by \(\alpha,\beta\ge0\), the squared diameter is exactly \(mn(m+n)+2\alpha^2+2\beta^2\) precisely in the sharp rectangle \(\alpha\le m-1\), \(\beta\le n-1\). Beyond either boundary, a single flipped row or column gives a quantitatively strict increase.
