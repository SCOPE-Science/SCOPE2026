For two sectorial operators with half-angles \(\alpha,\beta<\pi/2\), the sharp universal ratio \(w(A+B)/\max\{w(A),w(B)\}\) is \(1\) when \(\alpha+\beta\le\pi/2\), and \(\sin(\alpha+\beta)\) otherwise. Already one-dimensional complex scalars prove sharpness, so the common-angle phase boundary is exactly \(45^\circ\).
