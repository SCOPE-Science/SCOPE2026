For \(m\) invertible operator images of one orthonormal basis, with one operator fixed to the identity and the others within distance \(t\), the **exact** worst-case weaving-energy envelope is \((1-\sqrt{m-1}\,t)_+^2\) below and \((1+\sqrt{m-1}\,t)^2\) above. At the critical radius, rank-one changes to individually invertible sources already produce an incomplete weaving whenever \(m\ge3\).
