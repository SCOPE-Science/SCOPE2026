For the endpoint quadratic Ma–Minda class \(1+zf''/f'\prec1+z+z^2/2\), the sharp uniform-convexity radius is the algebraic constant \(\rho=0.646983632244827\ldots\) determined by \(\rho^6-4\rho^4+23\rho^2-9=0\). Sharpness comes from a nonreal tangential contact with the uniform-convexity parabola, attained by \(f_0'(z)=\exp(z+z^2/4)\).
