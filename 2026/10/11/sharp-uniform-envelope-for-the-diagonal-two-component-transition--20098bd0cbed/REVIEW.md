# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The source formulas were reconstructed at the critical value \(\lambda=1\). The proof separates the singular \(m=1\) mode exactly, proves strict termwise monotonicity for every remaining positive odd mode, uses summable domination for both endpoint limits, and evaluates the upper endpoint by an exact partial-fraction identity. No finite truncation is used to prove the infinite-series claim.

## Originality
PASS. The motivating source gives the series, two endpoint asymptotics, rounded constants, and a heuristic \(0.72/(d-1)\) approximation, but not the global normalized monotonicity, exact upper constant, or sharp all-parameter envelope. The closest same-author scalar transition papers were inspected and do not contain the two-component coefficient. Exact-constant, series, alias, and implication searches did not locate a published equivalent. A public author page mentioning ongoing closed-form-transition-number research remains a residual originality risk because it supplies no public covering theorem.

## Value
PASS. The coefficient controls the source's cubic transition-boundary slope. A sharp global envelope therefore gives a rigorous all-diffusion substitute for the source's numerical rule of thumb and shows precisely how little the normalized boundary coefficient can vary.

## Closest literature and limitations

The result is restricted to the diagonal two-component example with \(d>1\) and does not address non-diagonal matrices or higher-order degeneracy after \(\mathcal{P}_3=0\).
