For a common \(K_i\)-\(g\)-fusion frame with lower bounds \(A_i\), the sharp universal lower bound after replacing the operators by \(\sum_i a_iK_i\) is \(\left(\sum_i |a_i|/\sqrt{A_i}\right)^{-2}\). Huang--Yang Theorem 3.4(1) remains true, but its printed proof uses a false weighted-to-unweighted norm comparison and misses this optimal constant.
