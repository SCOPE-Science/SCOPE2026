Among all reflection-symmetric equilateral convex pentagons, the sharp ratio of the sum of all ten vertex-pair distances to minimum width is \(4+6\sqrt3\), attained only in the weakly convex completion by the marked \(2:1\) isosceles trapezoid. This proves the five-vertex reflection-symmetric case of the trapezoidal optimizer predicted by Audet–Ninin's pairwise-distance conjecture, without resolving their unrestricted problem.
