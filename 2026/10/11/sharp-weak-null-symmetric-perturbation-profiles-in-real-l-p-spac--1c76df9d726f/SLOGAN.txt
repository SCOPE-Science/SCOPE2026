For real infinite-dimensional \(L_p\) with \(1<p<2\), the least attainable symmetric weak-null perturbation radius of a unit vector depends exactly on the \(L_p\)-mass of its nonatomic component through the sharp Hanner two-point formula. At \(p=2\) and above, the profile collapses to \((1+t^p)^{1/p}\) for every unit vector, revealing a sharp structural phase transition.
