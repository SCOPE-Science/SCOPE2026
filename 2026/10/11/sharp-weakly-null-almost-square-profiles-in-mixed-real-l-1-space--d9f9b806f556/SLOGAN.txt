For a mixed real \(L^1\) space, a unit vector's optimal weakly-null two-sided square profile is \(\Lambda_x(t)=\max\{1,t+\|x_{\mathrm{at}}\|_1\}\). Consequently, the weakly almost-square points are exactly the unit functions supported on the nonatomic component, and the sharp tolerance radius is \(\|x_{\mathrm{dif}}\|_1+\varepsilon\).
