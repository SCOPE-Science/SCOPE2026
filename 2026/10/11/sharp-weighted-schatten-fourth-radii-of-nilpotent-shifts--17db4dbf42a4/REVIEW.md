# Scientific review: sharp Schatten-fourth radii of nilpotent shifts

## Correctness — PASS
The analytic trace expansion has been derived directly from the diagonal and separation-two entries of \(B^*B\), with no spectral-norm normalization ambiguity. The finite sharp constant follows from the complete simple spectrum of the Dirichlet path matrix, and the equality classification uses positivity of the leading sine vector. The second-eigenvalue gap is sharp among positive squared-weight vectors, because small perturbations by the second eigenvector stay strictly positive. Identity (6) gives strict nonattainment for all nonzero infinite Schatten-fourth shifts. The embedded exact-rational code and supplementary complex-phase singular-value computations agree, but finite checks are not substituted for the analytic proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The central comparison is with Sahoo, arXiv:2504.04693v1: its inspected general inequalities for Schatten-\(p\) numerical radii and \(2\times2\) operator blocks neither state nor imply a sharp all-dimensional one-step shift Rayleigh quotient or its equality cases. Ghosh–Birbonshi–Ojha, arXiv:2608.17486v1, examines the different operator-norm radius for a prescribed geometrically decaying weight pattern. Alrimawi–Kawariq–Abushaheen (2022) analyzes \(2\times2\) block inequalities, while Pal–Chandok, arXiv:2610.11336v1, analyzes generalized-Aluthge bounds and is later supporting comparison literature. Targeted semantic and phrase searches found no covering identity or domination. This is evidence of noncoverage, **not proof of global novelty**. Gao–Hou–Zhao (2026) could not be inspected in full after open-access searches and an institutional-download attempt, and older or unindexed equivalent trace-moment formulations remain possible.

## Value — PASS
The exponent-four case is the first non-Hilbert even Schatten moment in which the interaction of two neighboring shift weights survives the trace. The sharp constant depends nontrivially on dimension through a path-graph eigenvalue; every extremizing weight modulus is classified and phase freedom is exact. The spectral-gap deficit is quantitatively sharp even under positivity, and the infinite-dimensional optimal constant is never attained at interior parameters. These are structural finite/infinite operator-ideal facts, not an arbitrary isolated numerical evaluation.

## Scope and uncertainty
The theorem is confined to one-step nilpotent and unilateral Schatten-fourth shifts; it is not a bound for arbitrary matrices or exponents. Lawfully inaccessible plausible full text and potential unindexed equivalences are retained as originality risks. The review is same-model only and does not imply independent verification.
