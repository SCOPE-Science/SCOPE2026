A quadratic cohomology converts the damped Nosé–Hoover oscillator's sign-changing Euclidean divergence into a globally sharp weighted-volume contraction law: uniform contraction holds exactly for \(b>1\), with the nonexpanding boundary at \(b=1\). Every compact stationary state has an exact Lyapunov-sum deficit, and only mixtures of the two equilibria attain the sharp bound.
