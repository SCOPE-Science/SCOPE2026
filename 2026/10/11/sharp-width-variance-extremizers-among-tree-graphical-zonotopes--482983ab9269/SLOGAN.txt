For a connected simple graph, the variance of uniform-direction Euclidean widths of its graphical zonotope is an explicit affine function of the number of incident-edge pairs. Among all tree graphical zonotopes of the same order, every one has identical volume and mean width, but paths uniquely minimize and stars uniquely maximize the variance, with exact gap \(\delta(n-2)(n-3)/(n-1)\), where \(\delta=1/3+(2\sqrt3-4)/\pi>0\).
