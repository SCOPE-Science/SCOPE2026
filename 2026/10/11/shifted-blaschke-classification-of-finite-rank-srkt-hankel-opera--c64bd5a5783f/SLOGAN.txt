A nonzero finite-rank Hardy Hankel operator obeys the strong reproducing kernel thesis exactly when its analytic symbol is a shifted finite Blaschke product \(C(B-B(0))\). Its norming kernels are precisely the zeros of \(B\), and \(B(0)\ne0\) yields the strict examples \(\|\psi\|_G=\|\psi\|_*<\|\psi\|_\infty\) requested in the recent SRKT paper.
