For the Holling-II predator response in the nutrient–prey–predator chemostat, every compact interior invariant measure has shifted harmonic mean \(H(\alpha_2+P)=\alpha_2+p_*\), where \(p_*\) is the predator break-even prey density. Every non-equilibrium recurrent state therefore has \(\bar P>p_*\), with an exact positive variance defect, and every positive nonconstant periodic orbit strictly crosses \(P=p_*\).
