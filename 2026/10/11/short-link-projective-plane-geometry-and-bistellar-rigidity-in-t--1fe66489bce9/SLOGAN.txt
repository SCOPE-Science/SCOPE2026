The 13-vertex Wu-manifold triangulation has exactly 91 short-link tetrahedra, split by its affine symmetry into orbits of sizes \(39,39,13\); the 13-orbit is the line design \(2\text{-}(13,4,1)\) of a projective plane of order \(3\). Despite these exceptionally short links, the triangulation has no positive-index bistellar move.
