Among prime fields of characteristic at least five, the fourth-power value set is additive Sidon exactly at \(p=5\) and \(p=13\). At precisely these two fields, the sharp phase-only quartic-graph Fourier fourth moment has every equality phase of the form \(f(t)=\zeta_p^{At}u_{t^4}\), giving five two-dimensional and thirteen four-dimensional torus components, respectively.
