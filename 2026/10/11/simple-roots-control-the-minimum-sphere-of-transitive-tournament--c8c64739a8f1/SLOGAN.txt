For the directed edge polytope of the transitive tournament, the unique minimum enclosing sphere is supported by only the \(n-1\) simple roots, despite the polytope having \(n(n-1)/2\) vertices. Vertices with gap \(d=j-i\) lie on the exact squared-radius shell \(2-(4d-2)/S_n\), where \(S_n=n(n^2-1)/6\).
