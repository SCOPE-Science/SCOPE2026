The odd-order no-cancellation statement, even-order congruence solution counts, and divisor-product formula are verified directly. The packaged standard-library checker independently recomputes finite sums. The analytic argument, not those tests, covers every \(N\ge3\).

## Mathematical value — PASS
A single-coordinate defect is a natural minimal intervention to break the extremely sparse self-ambiguity of a periodic chirp. Its exact impact on *all* lifted intensity singular values is quantified, with an arithmetic zero-count for every even modulus, an odd-order recovery certificate, and the unique optimal positive defect amplitude and exact condition number. These are useful diagnostics for cyclic Gabor tomography and explain why a seemingly tiny defect cannot remove the even-modulus obstruction. The theorem does not compete with globally optimized Gabor windows.
