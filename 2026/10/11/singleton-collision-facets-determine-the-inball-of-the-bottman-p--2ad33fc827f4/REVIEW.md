# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof uses the published affine-sum equation and all three Bottman–Poliakova facet types. Translating by the singleton baseline converts each mixed collision facet into an offset bounded by a two-parameter function \(F(R,Q)\). Its normalized density is strictly coordinatewise increasing whenever both collision directions are present, while one-direction interval facets have density \(1/2\). This proves every nonsingleton facet lies strictly farther from the uniform point than the singleton facets. Summing the singleton ball constraints gives the matching global upper bound and forces uniqueness of the center.

Published-result and literature searches found no covering statement.

## Value
PASS. Constrainahedra are a recent family designed to encode collisions of horizontal and vertical lines and have applications to higher algebra and configuration-space compactification. Their facet structure contains many composite collision patterns. The theorem gives the exact Euclidean inball of the full genuinely two-directional Bottman–Poliakova family and proves that all composite collision facets are metrically inactive: only singleton collisions touch the maximal ball.
