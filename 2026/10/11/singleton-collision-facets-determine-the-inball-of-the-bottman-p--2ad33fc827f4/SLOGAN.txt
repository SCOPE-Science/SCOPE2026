For the Bottman–Poliakova constrainahedron with \(m,n\ge3\), the unique incenter is the uniform point and
\[
r_{\mathrm{in}}
=
\frac{\binom n2\binom m2+\binom n2+\binom m2-(m+n-2)}
{\sqrt{(m+n-2)(m+n-3)}}.
\]
Only singleton collision facets touch the maximal ball; every larger collision pattern has strictly smaller translated facet density and therefore lies farther away.
