For every genuine biclique \(K_{p,q}\) with \(p,q\ge2\), the translation-normalized standard graph associahedron has
\[
r_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{(2^p-1)(2^q-1)}{\sqrt{(p+q)(p+q-1)}}
\]
and a unique uniform incenter. Only the \(p+q\) singleton facets touch the maximal ball, even though the polytope has \(p+q+(2^p-1)(2^q-1)-1\) facets.
