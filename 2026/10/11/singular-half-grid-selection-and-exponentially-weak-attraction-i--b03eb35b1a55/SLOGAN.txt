For a steep logistic smoothing of any rational circle rotation, the attracting cycle selects the half-grid and its first positional correction consists of signed shifts of size \(\tfrac12e^{-a/(2n)}\), with the sign pattern determined by the rotation numerator. Its period-return multiplier is \(1-2a e^{-a/(2n)}+O(a^2e^{-a/n})\), so attraction becomes exponentially weak even though the orbit attracts almost all initial points.
