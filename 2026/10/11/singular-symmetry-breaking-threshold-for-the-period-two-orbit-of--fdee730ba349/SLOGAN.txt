A two-strategy replicator map has a unique transverse loss of its period-two orbit's stability for sufficiently small asymmetry \(\delta=|b-1/2|>0\), at coupling \(a_*(\delta)=1/[\delta(1-2\delta)]+O(\delta^{-2}e^{-1/(20\delta)})\). At exact symmetry, by contrast, that period-two orbit remains attracting at every finite coupling above its birth threshold.
