For every odd prime \(p\), all singular three-by-three minors of the Fourier matrix on \(\mathbb Z_{2p}\) are exactly the rank-two parity-pure/prime-coordinate-collision configurations, numbering \(\frac43p^2(p-1)^2(p-2)\). Consequently three-frequency signals have precisely the attainable zero counts \(\{0,1,2,4\}\), yielding the sharp exact-support uncertainty value \(\Theta^{\mathrm{ex}}_{2p}(2p-3)=4\) for \(p\ge5\).
