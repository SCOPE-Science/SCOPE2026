For any three sites in \(\mathbb Z_{2p}\) consisting of one antipodal pair and a third site, with \(p\) an odd prime, precisely six distinct Fourier characters suffice for a positively weighted tight frame and fewer never do. The \(\big(p(p^2-1)/24\big)^2\) optimal frequency sets are pairs of origin-containing triangles in the two parity classes, each with a unique positive weighting up to common scale; equal weights instead require all \(2p\) frequencies.
