For every \(q\ge3\), the Fourier-degree-at-most-two concentration operator on an interior \(q\)-ary Hamming shell has exactly six explicit positive spectral branches, and its kernel is precisely \(\bigl(\sum_i\mathbf1_{x_i\ne0}-k\bigr)\mathcal H_{\le1}\). Its rank is always \(\binom n2(q-1)^2\), independent of the chosen interior shell radius, and the exact quotient sampling condition number follows from the six rational levels.
