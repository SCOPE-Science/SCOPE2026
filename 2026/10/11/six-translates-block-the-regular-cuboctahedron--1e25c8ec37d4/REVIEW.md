# Review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The proof uses the standard centrally symmetric boundary-cover characterization of blocking configurations. The six proposed half-scaled centers lie on the cuboctahedral boundary, and every pairwise difference lies outside the interior, so the corresponding touching translates are pairwise non-overlapping. The only nontrivial step is strict coverage of the boundary. This is certified with positive rational clearance \(1/13\) by an exhaustive exact half-plane decomposition of all fourteen facets. The packaged checker uses rational arithmetic throughout and leaves no surviving uncovered branch on any facet.

A later blocking-number treatment gives the boundary-cover criterion and several three-dimensional six-translate results, but searches in the inspected text found no cuboctahedron or cube--octahedron-intersection case. Targeted searches under cuboctahedron, rectified cube, blocking configuration, six translates, and norm-intersection aliases found no statement implying the explicit rational-clearance construction. A published result on ordinary illumination of the same cuboctahedral family is not covering because the comparison between illumination and blocking does not turn an illumination upper bound into the blocking upper bound proved here.

## Value
**PASS.** Blocking numbers are a named geometric invariant with an explicit open-problem history. The regular cuboctahedron is a canonical centrally symmetric Archimedean solid, and six is the conjectured dimension-three minimum. Producing a concrete six-blocker arrangement with positive exact clearance therefore gives a natural benchmark for the conjectural lower-bound regime and is more informative than a numerical or near-touching configuration.
