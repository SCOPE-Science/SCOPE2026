On the boundary where the known quadratic endpoint term for the planar isotropic \(L_p\) dual Minkowski period vanishes, the first nonzero term is generically sixth order: \(\Theta-\pi/\sqrt{q-p}=-\pi(q-p)^{5/2}(4-q+p)(q-p-1)^2(r-1)^6/368640+o((r-1)^6)\). Its coefficient vanishes exactly at the three constant-period exceptional parameter points.
