The proof was checked against the stated generator and smoothness definitions in arXiv:2610.01642v1. The key exact identities are the binary recursion \(D^n y\mapsto D^{n+1}y\) and the supremum reconstruction of each invariant coordinate from a dense doubling orbit. The supporting Mihailescu result was checked in full text and applies only to hyperbolic nonexpanding toral endomorphisms with generating Rokhlin partitions.

No numerical calculation is required for the theorem. Finite-prefix numerical tests were used only as a diagnostic and are not part of the proof. The result is limited to the explicit family \(\operatorname{diag}(I_q,2)\); no classification of all neutral toral endomorphisms is claimed.
