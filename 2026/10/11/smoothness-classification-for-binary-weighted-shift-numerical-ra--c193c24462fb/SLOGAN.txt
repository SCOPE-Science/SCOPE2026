For binary weighted shifts on \(\ell_2\), the numerical-radius norm is Fréchet smooth precisely when the one-edges contain a unique finite longest consecutive run; every tie or unbounded run length destroys even Gâteaux smoothness. The exact norm is \(\cos(\pi/(m+2))\) for largest run length \(m\), and the derivative is the sine-eigenvector edge pairing on that run.
