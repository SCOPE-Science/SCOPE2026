PASS. At each word length \(n\), the coordinate identity amplifies the defining tensor by at most \(\|\operatorname{id}\|_{\mathrm{cb}}^n\); taking \(n\)-th roots gives the universal upper bound for both spectral-radius definitions. Tornes's Corollary 3.2 gives the matching lower bound because its universal tuple simultaneously realizes every matrix-level operator-space norm for every operator-space structure. The proof handles zero radii and does not assume that the cb norm is attained.

PASS. It therefore supplies a natural sharp stability interpretation of the focal universal-witness construction, simultaneously for the two spectral radii.
