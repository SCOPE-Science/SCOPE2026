For operator-space structures \(E_1,E_2\) on \(\mathbb C^d\), the worst multiplicative distortion of the Shalit--Shamovich spectral radius under the coordinate identity is exactly \(\|\operatorname{id}:E_1\to E_2\|_{\mathrm{cb}}\). The same exact formula holds for the minimal spectral radius, and Tornes's universal tuples realize every matrix-level norm ratio simultaneously for both radii.
