The claim was checked from the defining tensor formulas and the universal witness theorem. For the ordinary radius, the \(n\)-fold Haagerup tensor functoriality gives a factor at most \(\|\operatorname{id}\|_{\mathrm{cb}}^n\) before the \(n\)-th root. For the minimal radius, the same estimate follows from functoriality of the spatial operator-space tensor product. Tornes's Corollary 3.2 then supplies, for each nonzero matrix tuple \(X\), one operator tuple \(T_X\) satisfying \(\rho_E(T_X)=\rho_E^{\min}(T_X)=\|X\|_E\) for every operator-space structure \(E\). Taking the supremum over matrix levels therefore gives exactly the cb norm.

Limits checked: the supremum is over arbitrary operator tuples; no finite-dimensional detection theorem or attainment statement is asserted. No numerical computation is part of the proof.
