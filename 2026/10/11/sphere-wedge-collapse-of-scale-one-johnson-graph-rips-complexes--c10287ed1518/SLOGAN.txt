For every \(n\ge6\) and \(3\le k\le n-3\), the scale-one Vietoris–Rips complex of the Johnson graph \(J(n,k)\) simplicially collapses to a two-dimensional complex and has homotopy type \(\bigvee^{\beta(n,k)}S^2\), where \(\beta(n,k)=\binom nk-1-\tfrac12\binom nk k(n-k)+\binom n{k-1}\binom{n-k}{2}+\binom n{k+1}\binom k2\). The proof combines canonical star/top clique collapses with the simply connected two-skeleton of the hypersimplex.
