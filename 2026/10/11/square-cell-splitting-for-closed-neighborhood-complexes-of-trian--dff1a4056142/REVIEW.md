# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The descending free-face pairing is uniform for every finite connected triangle-free graph with at most two common neighbors per vertex pair. Unique-center counting proves all paired lower faces are free, with no hidden large cofaces; exactly the four cycle-tetrahedron boundaries survive. The second-disk attachment along a square is null-homotopic in the chosen filled-square subcomplex, producing the wedge splitting. Complete finite collapse checks on 118 examples, including 90 independently seeded admissible graphs, corroborate the critical combinatorial premises. These computations are finite evidence, not a proof of the universal assertion.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** Matsushita's 2025 full paper develops the closed-neighborhood complex and identifies its fundamental group; it does not assert this all-admissible-graph collapse-to-square complex and sphere-splitting formula. Adams–Shukla–Singh's full 2022 paper proves a hypercube scale-two formula, which our theorem subsumes in that special family, and a girth-five lemma, which does not address four-cycle-rich rectangular grids. The earlier metric-gluing theorem does not establish arbitrary connected overlapping-square examples such as grids. published-finding corpus searches covering closed neighborhood/Čech aliases, four-cycle boundaries, and grid and hypercube reductions returned only adjacent but non-covering objects. Both current and preceding own topical ledgers were searched for implication overlap. Equivalent unpublished or unindexed literature remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** The theorem is a structural, checkable infinite-family classification that reduces a high-dimensional simplicial complex to a familiar square-filled graph, identifying one additional sphere for each four-cycle. It naturally explains both the previously known hypercube pattern and a new rectangular-grid family; it is not merely a single computed Betti number, naming substitution, or a trivial consequence of the existing girth-five case.

**Limitations.** Triangle-free and no-\(K_{2,3}\) are essential for the stated construction. No assertion is made about general graphs, other Čech scales, shellability, or arbitrary homotopy groups of the square-filled graph. All mathematical acceptance is same-model review; independent validation remains outstanding.
