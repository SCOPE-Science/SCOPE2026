For triangle-free graphs with no pair of vertices having three common neighbors, the closed neighborhood complex collapses to the graph decorated by one tetrahedral sphere boundary per four-cycle, and its realization is the graph's square-filled CW complex wedged with one \(S^2\) per four-cycle. In particular, a rectangular \(m\)-by-\(n\) grid has a closed neighborhood complex homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{(m-1)(n-1)}S^2\).
