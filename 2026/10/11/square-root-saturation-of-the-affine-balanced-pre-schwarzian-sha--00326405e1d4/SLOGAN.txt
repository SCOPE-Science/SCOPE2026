The sharp affine-balanced pre-Schwarzian shadow constant does not merely tend to \(1\) at high distortion: its deficit is \(2\sqrt2\,K_{\mathrm{aff}}^{-1/2}+O(K_{\mathrm{aff}}^{-1})\). The sharp Schwarz-Pick model realizes this saturation in a matched unit-disk boundary layer whose radial deficit is asymptotic to \(\sqrt2\,K_{\mathrm{aff}}^{-1/2}\).
