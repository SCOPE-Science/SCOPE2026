For every \(n\ge6\), the natural symmetric-group action on the only nonzero reduced homology of the graph domination complex \(D_{n,n-2}\) has exactly four irreducible rational Specht constituents, with multiplicities \(1,1,2,2\). The result also determines the exceptional \(n=4,5\) representations and refines the previously known nonequivariant sphere-wedge theorem.
