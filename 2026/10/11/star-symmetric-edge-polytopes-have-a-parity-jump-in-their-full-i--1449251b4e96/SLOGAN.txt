The canonical symmetric-edge polytope of a star with \(m\) leaves has inradius \(\bigl(m-\delta_m/(m+1)\bigr)^{-1/2}\), where \(\delta_m\) is the parity of \(m\). When \(m\) is odd its incenter is unique, but when \(m\) is even the full incenter set is an explicitly bounded line segment whose endpoints acquire additional tangent facets.
