At a generic Camassa–Holm first breaking, the sharp \(C^{3/5}\) cusp coefficient \(C_*\) fixes the newborn anticuspon–cuspon geometry: the Eulerian width is \(\sqrt{216/3125}\,C_*^{5/2}(t-t_*)^{5/2}+o((t-t_*)^{5/2})\), while the profile-height gap is exactly \(5/2\) times that coefficient at order \((t-t_*)^{3/2}\). Equivalently, \((t-t_*)H(t)/W(t)\to5/2\).
