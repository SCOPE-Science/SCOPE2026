The generic condition \(\tau'(a_*)=0\), \(\tau''(a_*)>0\) gives a quadratic label-time opening, while \(Q_t(a,\tau(a))=1/2\) fixes its leading normalization. The identities \(\eta_a=Q^2R/(1+Q^2)\) and \(U_a=QR/(1+Q^2)\) produce the \(5/2\) and \(3/2\) temporal powers by integration over the newborn interval.

\[
C_*^5=\frac{20^3}{6^5}\frac{R_*^2}{\kappa},\qquad A^2=\frac{16}{225}\frac{R_*^2}{\kappa},
\]
so \(C_*^5/A^2=3125/216\). The height coefficient is \((5/2)A\).
