# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

PASS. A stationary law on entire unit-speed geometric paths equals the unit-roof suspension of a stationary bi-infinite edge shift; shift phase is Lebesgue and its one-edge weights are a normalized nonnegative Kirchhoff circulation. Conversely a circulation induces a stationary vertex-Markov path law. Transient inter-SCC edges have zero circulation; cycle decomposition and incidence rank give extremal simple cycles and affine dimensions. In each SCC an all-backward invariant set containing any internal edge point must contain all internal edge points; conversely upstream closure of a reachability-minimal positive SCC distinguishes cross-component mixtures. Conditional entropy chain rule bounds all lifts by H(w), attained by vertex-Markov lift. Perron eigenvectors and KL nonnegativity give maximum log rho; simple cycles attain 0 and connected circulation polytope gives all intermediate values. Independently checked three-loop bouquet and double-edge/return example, not universal proof. Only unit edge lengths, no waiting, all vertices on cycles, marginal ergodicity rather than lift ergodicity.

A stronger general nondeterministic graph suspension theorem might subsume this natural special case.
