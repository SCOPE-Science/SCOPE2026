Every compact stationary van der Pol state satisfies the parameter-independent weak identity \(\mathbb E[\phi'(x)v^2]=\mathbb E[x\phi(x)]\), which reconstructs the position distribution of kinetic energy from the position marginal alone. It forces the exact fourth-moment law \(\mathbb E[x^4]=3\mathbb E[x^2]\) and the strict non-equilibrium bound \(0<\mathbb E[x^2]<3\).
