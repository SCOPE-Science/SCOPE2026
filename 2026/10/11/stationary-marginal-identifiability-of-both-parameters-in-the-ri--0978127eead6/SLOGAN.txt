For every compact positive invariant law of the deterministic Ricker map, the stationary mean is \(K\); unless the law is the equilibrium atom, the same one-time marginal also gives \(r=2\,\mathbb E[X]\operatorname{Cov}(\log X,X)/\operatorname{Var}(X)\). Thus a non-equilibrium stationary marginal identifies both parameters exactly without lagged observations.
