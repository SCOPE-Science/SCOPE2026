Every compact stationary Glukhovsky–Dolzhansky state lies on the exact temperature shell \(\mathbb E[(y-\rho/2)^2+z^2]=\rho^2/4\) and satisfies the heightwise convection law \(\mathbb E[xy\mid z]=z\). Away from equilibrium-supported measures, the loss of perfect \(z\)-to-\(xy\) coherence is exactly the normalized mean-square vertical speed.
