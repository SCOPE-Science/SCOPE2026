For the canonical Bézout test consisting of a segment and an overlapping lens cut from a Euclidean ball, the normalized mixed-volume violation increases strictly with the separation in every dimension \(n\ge2\). Its unattained limit \(\frac{n}{n-1}\frac{\kappa_n\kappa_{n-2}}{\kappa_{n-1}^2}\) equals the sharp coefficient for two zonoid auxiliary bodies, attained by perpendicular segments.
