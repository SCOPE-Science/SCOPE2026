The norm obtained by taking half the Perron root of a finite graph's absolute edge-weight adjacency matrix is strictly convex exactly for stars and triangles (ignoring isolated vertices). Its complete real-smooth locus is determined by a uniquely Perron-dominating positive-edge component that induces its vertex set, yielding a sharp one-or-three-nonzero-weights smoothness law for three-periodic weighted-shift numerical radii.
