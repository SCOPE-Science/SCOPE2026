For odd normalized quasiconformal harmonic maps with one univalent analytic combination, the published growth envelope \(T(k,r)\) is genuinely sharp only at \(k=0\): for every \(0<k<1\) and \(0<r<1\), the true best constant is strictly smaller. The envelope becomes sharp when global injectivity is dropped, because its unique local equality shear has two preimages of every point \(iy\) with \(y>1/2\).
