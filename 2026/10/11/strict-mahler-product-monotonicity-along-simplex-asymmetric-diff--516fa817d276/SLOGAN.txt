For every nondegenerate simplex in dimension \(d\ge2\), the Santaló volume product of \((\Delta-c)+t(c-\Delta)\) has a closed rational formula and is strictly increasing until the symmetric difference body at \(t=1\), then strictly decreasing. Its unique maximum is \(\frac{d+1}{d!}\binom{2d}{d}\), and its exact logarithmic second-order drop is determined.
