On the complete global-center locus \(\dot x=y,\ \dot y=-x-\alpha x^3y^2-\beta x^5\), the physical period is constant only for the harmonic oscillator and otherwise decreases strictly from \(2\pi\) to zero as amplitude grows. Its exact leading high-amplitude law is \(2\pi/(\sqrt{\alpha}\,A^2\sqrt{1+\beta A^4})\) when \(\alpha>0\), with a different beta-function constant on the pure-quintic axis \(\alpha=0<\beta\).
