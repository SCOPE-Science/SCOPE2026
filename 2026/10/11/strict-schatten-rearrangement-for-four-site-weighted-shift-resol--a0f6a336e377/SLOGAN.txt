For four-site nilpotent weighted shifts, placing the largest edge magnitude at the center strictly maximizes every Schatten \(S_p\) norm of the resolvent \((I-N)^{-1}\) simultaneously for all \(p\ge1\); the smallest center strictly minimizes them. The rule actually holds for every positive singular-value power sum, even though the second and fourth singular values move opposite to the first and third.
