The cube uniquely maximizes the expected number of vertices in a uniformly oriented central hyperplane section among positive orthotopes with fixed squared diagonal, in every dimension at least four. The exact expectation is \(2^d\sum_i\arcsin(a_i/\sqrt{\sum_j a_j^2})/\pi\), strictly increases under balancing squared side lengths, and equals \(32/3\) for a four-dimensional cube.
