The packaged `verify.py` uses only the Python standard library and exact rational arithmetic.

It checks the orthogonal-regression algebra generated by
\[
L=\delta y+\dot y
\]
with
\[
\mathbb E[\dot y\mid y]=0.
\]

It verifies the exact Cauchy-bound conversion
\[
\delta q
=
f\mathbb E[y\cos\theta],
\qquad
\mathbb E[\cos^2\theta]=\frac12
\]
to
\[
q\le\frac{f^2}{2\delta^2}.
\]

For exact rational positive test parameters it verifies that the Cauchy equality ansatz produces the cubic coefficient
\[
-\alpha
\left(
\frac{f}{\delta\omega}
\right)^3,
\]
which is nonzero.

It also checks the sign changes of
\[
x
\left(
x^2-\frac{\beta}{\alpha}
\right)
\]
on the two sides of the positive and negative unforced potential minima.

The stored output in `verification_output.txt` is `VERIFY_OK`.

The phase-Haar argument, conditional-expectation quantifiers, endpoint rigidity, and periodic crossing count are analytic arguments in `RESULT.md`; no finite trajectory calculation is used.
