For the Day--James hexagon at exponent \(p=4\), the strongly skew range has a second exact extremal branch that is absent from the published example. The phase transition occurs at the unique \(\rho\in(0,1)\) with \(\rho^3+2\rho^2-2=0\), and for \(r\le\rho\) the exact constant is attained by an adjacent-vertex pair with scale determined by \((t^3-r)(1+rt)^3=t^3\).
