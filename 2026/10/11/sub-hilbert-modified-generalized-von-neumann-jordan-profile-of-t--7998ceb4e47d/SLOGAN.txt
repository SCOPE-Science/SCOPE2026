For the regular octagonal Banach plane, the modified generalized von Neumann–Jordan constant has the exact sub-Hilbert profile \(\widetilde C_{\mathrm{NJ}}^{(p)}=1+(\sqrt2-1)^p\) for \(1\le p\le2\). Comparing with the published unrestricted profile shows that equal-radius normalization is genuinely restrictive exactly for \(1<p<2\), with the two constants meeting at \(p=2\).
