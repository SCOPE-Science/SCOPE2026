For the quadratic Sprott case-F flow, the entire positive-damping boundary \(a=0\) is supercritical: \(\ell_1=-2b/[(b^2+1)(b^2+4)]\), with a small stable cycle for \(a>0\). Precisely at criticality, generic nearby trajectories decay with the sharp law \(t(x^2+y^2)\to(b^2+1)(b^2+4)/b\), while the strong-stable exceptional set decays exponentially.
