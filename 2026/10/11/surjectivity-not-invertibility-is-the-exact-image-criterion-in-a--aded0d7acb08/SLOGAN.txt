A published tensor controlled-frame theorem requires \(U_1\otimes U_2\) to be invertible, but surjectivity is the exact criterion. A backward-shift example gives a noninjective \(U_1\otimes U_2\) whose transformed family is still Parseval, and the repaired bounds are \(\alpha m((U_1\otimes U_2)^*)^2\) and \(\beta\|U_1\otimes U_2\|^2\).
