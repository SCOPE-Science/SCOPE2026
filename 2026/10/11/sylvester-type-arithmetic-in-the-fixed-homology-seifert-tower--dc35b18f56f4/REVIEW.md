# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The source operation adjoins a reduced coefficient whose denominator is \(P_kH+1\) and proves that the first homology group is unchanged. Hence \(H\) is constant, the exceptional-order product satisfies \(P_{k+1}=P_k(P_kH+1)\), and \(Q_k=HP_k\) obeys \(Q_{k+1}=Q_k(Q_k+1)\). The product-plus-one identity then gives pairwise coprimality, coprimality with \(HP_0\), and the stated doubly-exponential lower bound. All quantifiers and the construction-specific scope are explicit.

## Originality
PASS. Full-text inspection of the source's construction shows preservation of first homology and infinite iteration, but not the induced Sylvester-type recurrence, pairwise-coprime exceptional-order law, or growth estimate. Targeted published-findings searches and broader web searches for the exact recurrence in Seifert/taut-foliation settings found no equivalent statement. The arithmetic recurrence itself is elementary and classical in flavor; the originality claim is its exact structural identification inside this recent topology construction.

## Value
PASS. The source uses the iteration only qualitatively to produce infinitely many examples. The refinement determines how singular-fiber complexity must evolve while homology is frozen: every new cone order avoids all previous ones and the initial homological product, and the orders grow at least doubly exponentially. This supplies a natural quantitative invariant of the construction and prevents the infinite tower from being viewed as an uncontrolled repetition of comparable Seifert data.

The claim is limited to the printed iteration and does not classify other fixed-homology constructions.
