# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
For every integer \(n\ge4\), the proof directly establishes simple connectivity by replacing long inclusion edges with Boolean saturated chains and filling adjacent-letter swaps with the two small triangles. The count of every face dimension follows from choosing one or two added letters, and Hurewicz plus Whitehead establish the stated wedge homotopy type. A permutation-fixed chain is fixed vertexwise since the ranks distinguish its vertices; the counts of fixed rank-one edges, rank-two edges, and triangles give the complete character by reduced Lefschetz. The orbit quotient is the rank-span-two complex on ranks and strongly collapses via dominated endpoints. The supplied independent-in-method combinatorial computation checks every conjugacy class of \(S_n\) for \(4\le n\le8\), with exact finite mod-two boundary ranks. These tests are not used as infinite proofs.

## Originality — PASS, with explicit residual risk
The closest directly relevant source is Kitajima's 2025 poster and 2026 preprint on filtered order complexes; the public abstract supplies homotopy and shellability results, not a cycle-type character formula or a contractible quotient. Hersh–Sundaram's 2026 rank-selected homology representations concern a different, fixed-rank construction and do not imply the moving rank-window character. Searches of mathematical research records for the exact cycle-type expression, equivalent chain-poset descriptions and orbit quotients returned no covering statement. The Kitajima preprint's full text was not accessible, which leaves a real uncertainty; the conclusion is based on examined materials and implication comparisons, not an assertion that the unexamined text is silent or that all unpublished work is absent.

## Value — PASS
The complete degree-two \(S_n\)-character is a natural structural invariant of the new rank-filtered complexes, which cannot be recovered from their known wedge-of-spheres homotopy types or Betti numbers. The contractibility of the full orbit quotient is a second substantive global consequence, holding uniformly despite unbounded upstairs second homology. These results describe all parameters \(n\ge4\), rather than an arbitrary small slice or a recomputation of a known table.

## Closest literature and boundaries

The theorem says nothing about integral module splittings or the higher-rank-span filtration.
