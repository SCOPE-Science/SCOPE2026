# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
Every finite \(n\ge5\) is handled uniformly. The Johnson facet partition, equivariant nerve, relative pairs \((\Delta^3,K_4)\), triangle-relator proof of first-homology vanishing, and equivariant Euler identity reconstruct the theorem. An independent finite implementation verifies exact mod-two homology and all conjugacy-class character traces for \(n=5,6,7,8\). These computations cannot substitute for the proof.

## Originality — PASS (residual risk)
The available full text of Shuldiner–Oldford gives the star/top clique input but neither its topological representation nor the character calculation. The Galetto–Montaño–Wellner paper studies the full cube, while the result here studies one induced Hamming-weight layer; homology of the latter is not a formal representation-theoretic restriction of homology of the former. A recent partition-graph wedge theorem concerns different combinatorial objects. Indexed searches found no equivalent character, but the unavailable full hypercube paper and unindexed work remain real residual risks.

## Value — PASS
The theorem computes a complete and natural \(S_n\)-representation for all \(n\ge5\). Its signed traces distinguish symmetry information that the degree and homology dimension do not detect. The fixed-weight shell is a natural Rips subspace connected to the Johnson graph's two maximal-clique types.

## Limitations
No integral \(S_n\)-lattice splitting or full Rips persistence is claimed. The virtual formula specifies a genuine character, not a direct sum of its signed terms. No independent verification was performed.
