The inclusive distance-two Vietoris–Rips complex on the constant Hamming-weight-three binary layer has reduced rational homology only in degree two, of dimension \(3\binom n4-2\binom n3+\binom n2-1\) for \(n\ge5\). Its entire coordinate-permutation homology character is determined by an explicit five-term signed combination of induced and exterior-power representations.
