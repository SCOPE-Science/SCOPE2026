# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The maximal cliques of J(n,3) are stars indexed by 2-subsets and 3-simplex tops indexed by 4-subsets. The star union has a good-cover nerve N whose vertices are pairs, edges intersecting pairs, and triangles the triples {ab,ac,bc}. The relative attachment (Delta^3,K4) for each top contributes H_2(X,A)=Ind_{S_{n-4}xS4} H1(K4); it contributes no other relative homology. Filling top triangles kills the star-nerve H1 because the complete graph K_n cycle space is normally generated by 3-cycles. H_i(X) is thus concentrated at i=2, and the exact sequence plus reduced Euler character of N produces the five-term virtual S_n character. The edge orientation sign is the transposition of the two outer endpoints and the N triangle orientation sign agrees with wedge^3 P_n. Independent GF2 boundary rank calculations on actual full clique complexes for n=5,6,7 give H2 dimensions 4,19,55 and no other reduced homology, supporting the theorem but not replacing its n>=5 proof.

## originality

PASS

Shuldiner–Oldford classify maximal Johnson cliques, not topological homology representations. Galetto–Montaño–Wellner study full Hamming cube Rips complexes with hyperoctahedral symmetry, not the induced *exact weight three* J(n,3) layer. A separate published result treats the weight<=3 function–Rips sublevel with H3 carrying wedge^3 P_n plus wedge^4 P_n, not this H2 and not a formal direct restriction. The character formula is a distinct natural representation even though known cliques supply its facets.

## value

PASS

The exact rational S_n-character of the natural Johnson clique complex is a uniform equivariant refinement of the homological dimension. It identifies how the nontrivial degree-two cycles transform under every coordinate permutation rather than providing a tabulated small-n rank; the relative top-attachment decomposition is a reusable structural description.
