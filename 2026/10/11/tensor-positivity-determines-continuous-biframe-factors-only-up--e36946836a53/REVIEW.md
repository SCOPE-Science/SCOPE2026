# Review

## Correctness
PASS. On one-point measure spaces, the two factor quadratic forms are exactly \(-\|z\|^2\), so neither factor pair satisfies a positive biframe lower bound. Their tensor second components multiply to \(+1\), making the tensor quadratic form exactly \(\|z\otimes w\|^2\). The reciprocal-phase repair follows algebraically from the positivity of \(q_1(f)q_2(g)\) and does not use approximation.

## Originality
PASS for the source-specific theorem correction and its exact phase classification. Exact-source and semantic searches covered the theorem, sign cancellation, reciprocal phase, and equivalent tensor-factor formulations. The closest indexed frame findings concern different convergence, duality, range, or operator-orbit questions.

The target full text was inspected at both definitions and the complete theorem proof. A later tensor-biframe theorem for Hilbert \(C^*\)-modules was also inspected and repeats an analogous unrotated equivalence, so it does not supply the correction.

## Value
PASS. The false direction is one half of the source's advertised tensor-product characterization. The counterexample is minimal, while the repair identifies exactly what tensor positivity forgets: a reciprocal unimodular phase. In the real case this reduces to the transparent common-sign ambiguity.

## Limitations
The corrected equivalence assumes the scalar factor integrals exist and that the tensor integral factors as their product. It does not settle any independent Fubini or integrability issue under weaker hypotheses. A nonindexed correction remains a literature risk.
