# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For each k, the normalized real ternary vectors of uniform support k represent exactly C(n,k)2^(k-1) projective lines over both R and C. Different supports of size k have mutually exclusive norming coordinates x_i=±1,y_i=0 and y_j=±1,x_j=0, giving both Birkhoff–James inequalities for all complex λ. For equal supports, distinct normalized patterns agree at the first nonzero coordinate and disagree at another; their perturbation norms dominate max{|1+λ|,|1−λ|}>=1, symmetrically, for every complex λ. Thus a clique. The consecutive layer ratio a_(k+1)/a_k=2(n-k)/(k+1) fixes a mode floor((2n+2)/3), with ties n≡2 mod3. Expressing a_k=(3^n/2) P(Binomial(n,2/3)=k) and using Stirling at the mode gives asymptotic 3^(n+1)/(4 sqrt(pi n)). Independent enumerations for n=1..7 verified all pairs and expected sizes [1,2,6,16,40,120,336]. This proves a lower bound, never the full orthograph clique maximum.

## originality

PASS

The 2021 Arambašić–Guterman–Kuzma–Rajić–Zhilina Example 3.7 uses only full-support ±1 corners, yielding 2^(n−1) projective lines. The new uniform fixed-size k supports add genuinely different pairwise mutually orthogonal subspaces, improving the exponential base from 2 to 3. A 2024 orthograph classification addresses general graph/norm recognition rather than this signed ternary layer construction. Resultary search found a distinct real l1^n probabilistic lower bound, not the same l_infinity^n result; the specific 2022 overview full text remained inaccessible. Best-of-knowledge originality with explicit residual risk, not novelty based on failed search.
