In real and complex \(\ell_\infty^n\), all signed ternary vectors with the same number of nonzero coordinates form a clique of mutually Birkhoff–James orthogonal projective lines. Choosing the best support size yields \(\omega_n\ge (1+O(n^{-1}))3^{n+1}/(4\sqrt{\pi n})\), exponentially strengthening the established \(2^{n-1}\) construction without claiming the lower bound is exact.
