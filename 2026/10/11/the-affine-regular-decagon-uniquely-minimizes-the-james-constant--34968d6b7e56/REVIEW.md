# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. Mizuguchi's Theorem 4.1 supplies the exact three-branch James profile. The derivative signs show that the first branch decreases to the golden-ratio junction, the second branch has one interior maximum and therefore minimizes at an endpoint, and the third branch increases. Exact endpoint comparison gives the strict global minimum \((3\sqrt5-1)/4\). At the minimizing parameter the decagon vertices satisfy the regular-decagon recurrence with coefficient \(2\cos(\pi/5)\), proving affine regularity. The bundled exact verifier checks the algebraic identities and inequalities.

Searches used both source parameters, regular/affine-regular decagon aliases, the golden-ratio threshold, and the exact value. The closest separate public record has the same number for Banach--Mazur distance, not for James-profile minimization. Its public summary was compared, while lack of complete text is retained as a residual risk rather than treated as proof of noncoverage.

## Value
PASS. This is a natural global optimization over a complete published family of decagonal Radon planes. It identifies the unique least-James member with the affine-regular decagon and reveals an exact numerical coincidence with an independently computed affine invariant of the same decagon. The result is therefore a structural equality/rigidity statement, not a routine evaluation at an arbitrary parameter.
