For three uniform points in any smoothly strictly convex planar body \(K\), the probability of a circumradius at most \(t\) is \(6\pi^2t^4/A(K)^2-512P(K)t^5/(15A(K)^3)+O_K(t^6)\). Thus a single small-radius law encodes both area and perimeter, with the disk eventually maximizing the probability among each fixed equal-area nondisk comparison.
