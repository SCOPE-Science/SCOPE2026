For Cervone's unique 1133 eight-vertex Klein-bottle triangulation, the three nonzero classes in \(H^1(-;\mathbf F_2)\) have exact minimum-support data \((w,m,q)=(5,1,1),(6,5,0),(6,3,1)\), where \(q\) is the cup-square evaluation. Every minimizing cocycle is a single simple dual cycle, and the unique weight-five support is \(\{BC,BG,CE,DE,DG\}\).
