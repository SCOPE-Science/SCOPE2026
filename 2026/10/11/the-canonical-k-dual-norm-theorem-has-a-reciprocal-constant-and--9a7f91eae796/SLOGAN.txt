Li--Leng--Huang's canonical \(K\)-dual theorem uses the optimal lower frame bound on the wrong side of a reciprocal: the sharp minimum is \(\|T_\Gamma\|^2=A^{-1}\), not \(A\). Even after that correction, operator-norm minimizers need not be unique; a three-vector frame in \(\mathbb C^2\) has an interval of distinct duals all attaining \(A^{-1}\), while the Douglas reduced-range condition singles out one of them.
