For the graph associahedron of the balanced complete bipartite graph \(K_{4,4}\), every vertex has eccentricity exactly \(15+s\), where \(s\) is the number of successive search-tree roots until one color class is first exhausted. The exact polytope diameter is \(22\), with eccentricity multiplicities \(48,768,5760,23040\) at distances \(19,20,21,22\).
