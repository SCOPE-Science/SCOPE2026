For the dodecahedral graph, the complete automorphism-group action on rational first homology of its open-neighborhood complex is \(((W_6\oplus V_5)\boxtimes\mathbf1)\oplus((2V_5)\boxtimes\varepsilon)\). The antipodal symmetry splits its \(21\) dimensions into \(11\) even and \(10\) odd, with no rotation-invariant class; the previously known nonequivariant homotopy type is not the new result.
