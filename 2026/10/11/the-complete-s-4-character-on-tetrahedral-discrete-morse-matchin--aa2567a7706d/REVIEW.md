The literature supplies the independently established concentration of its rational homology in degree four (rank 99). A first exhaustive program enumerates each of 14,720 possible matchings exactly once, tests acyclicity, and obtains the published face vector. The full tetrahedral simplex is the first higher-dimensional simplex whose discrete Morse matching complex has a large, recently classified wedge homotopy type. The complete \(S_4\)-module is a natural symmetry invariant of the entire 99-dimensional fourth homology and is not recoverable from the 99-dimensional Betti number or from the abstract automorphism group. It records, for example, precisely four invariant classes and four sign classes, and asymmetry between the two three-dimensional Specht constituents. This is a complete finite representation classification of an intrinsically motivated benchmark, not a random numerical slice. No unproved extension or broad consequence is claimed.
