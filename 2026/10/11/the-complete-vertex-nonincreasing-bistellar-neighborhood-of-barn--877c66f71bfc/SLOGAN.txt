Barnette's eight-vertex nonpolytopal \(3\)-sphere has exactly two legal \(2\)-to-\(3\) moves and six legal \(3\)-to-\(2\) moves, with each set forming one automorphism orbit. The upward orbit reaches the Brückner--Grünbaum nonpolytopal sphere, while the downward orbit reaches a single polytopal \(18\)-tetrahedron sphere.
