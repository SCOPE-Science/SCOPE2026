For every square grid \(P_n\square P_n\) with \(n\ge3\), the rational dihedral \(D_4\)-representation on the top homology of its ordinary three-cut complex has character \((B_n,0,H_n,X_n,0)\), explicitly distinguishing half-turns and axis reflections while all quarter-turn and diagonal-reflection traces vanish. The complete irreducible multiplicities follow from these formulas, refining the previously known nonequivariant Betti number without claiming a new homotopy classification.
