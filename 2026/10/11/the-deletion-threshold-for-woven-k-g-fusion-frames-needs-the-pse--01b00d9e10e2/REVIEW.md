# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The source theorem and its proof were reconstructed from the publisher PDF. The proof controls deleted energy by
\[
C\|f\|^{2}
\]
and, on \(R(K)\), uses
\[
\|f\|^{2}\le \|K^\dagger\|^{2}\|K^*f\|^{2}.
\]
Therefore the surviving lower coefficient is
\[
A-C\|K^\dagger\|^{2},
\]
not \(A-C\).

The one-dimensional example satisfies \(m>1\), uses a proper deleted subset, satisfies the printed hypothesis, and leaves only zero operators after deletion.

## Originality
PASS. The exact theorem, pseudoinverse-scaling aliases, erasure terminology, the foundational \(K\)-\(g\)-fusion paper, and a later continuous controlled weaving removal theorem were compared. The closest literature either uses orthogonality or controls removed energy directly in the \(K\)-scaled norm. No checked indexed source supplies the theorem-specific counterexample and corrected scalar threshold.

## Value
PASS. The theorem is intended to quantify when erasing part of a woven system preserves its lower \(K\)-frame control. The wrong placement of \(\|K^\dagger\|^{2}\) can turn a completely destructive erasure into one certified as safe. The corrected estimate is immediate to apply and is exactly what the published proof supports.

## Limitations
The correction gives a sufficient scalar criterion. It is not a necessary-and-sufficient characterization of all deletions, and sharper bounds may exploit the actual compressed operators.
