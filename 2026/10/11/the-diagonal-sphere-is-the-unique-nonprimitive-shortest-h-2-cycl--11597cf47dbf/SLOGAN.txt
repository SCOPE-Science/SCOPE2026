In Bagchi--Datta's natural ten-vertex \(\mathbb{CP}^{2}\), exactly \(35\) four-vertex supports realize nonzero integral \(H_2\), so four vertices are the sharp support minimum. Thirty-four are primitive, while the induced diagonal sphere on \(\{x_{11},x_{22},x_{33},x_{44}\}\) is the unique shortest representative of twice a primitive generator; the full \(A_4\)-orbit sizes are \(1,4,6,12,12\).
