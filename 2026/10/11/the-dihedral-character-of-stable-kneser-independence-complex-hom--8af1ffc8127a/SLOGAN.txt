For every \(m\ge8\), the complete rational dihedral character on the second reduced homology of the stable Kneser graph independence complex is explicit: generic nonidentity rotations have trace \(-1\), order-two and order-three rotations have exceptional traces, and reflection traces depend on the parity and axis type. This refines the previously known nonequivariant wedge-of-two-spheres homotopy classification.
