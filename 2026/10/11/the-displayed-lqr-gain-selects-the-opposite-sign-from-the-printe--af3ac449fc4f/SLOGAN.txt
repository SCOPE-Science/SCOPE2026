The Fu–Xu–Xiao synchronization section's displayed matrix is a stabilizing standard LQR gain: it nearly satisfies \(A^{\mathsf T}P+PA-P^2+100I_4=0\), while the article's printed-sign Riccati residual is \(221.28644292\). Literal use of the printed negative gain changes the closed loop from Hurwitz \(A-P\) to an open-right-half-plane matrix \(A+P\), so the numerical gain itself identifies the intended sign convention.
