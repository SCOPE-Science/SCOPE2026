In the Benedetti--Lutz ball \(B_{15,66}\), the double-trefoil spanning edge \(\{2,3\}\) is the unique interior edge of tetrahedron-incidence degree \(3\), the smallest possible degree for an interior edge of a triangulated \(3\)-manifold. The other \(53\) interior edges have degrees \(4\) through \(7\), with multiplicities \(14,21,13,5\).
