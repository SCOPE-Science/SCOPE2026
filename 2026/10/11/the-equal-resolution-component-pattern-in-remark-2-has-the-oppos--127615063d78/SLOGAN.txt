In an ordinary unoriented skein triple, the three component counts always have the form \(\{c,c,c+1\}\). Therefore the equal-resolution hypothesis \(|L_0|=|L_1|=|L|+1\) in Huang's Remark 2 is empty; equal resolutions force \(|L_0|=|L_1|=|L|-1\), which is also the sign compatible with the localized rank constraints.
