The Euclidean diameter of \(\mathcal P(m,n)\) is obtained by splitting the \(s=\min\{m,2n\}\) largest entries of two copies of \(n^2,(n-1)^2,\ldots,1\) as evenly as possible between two disjoint vertex supports. In particular the diameter stops growing exactly at \(m=2n\), where its square becomes \(n(n+1)(2n+1)/3\).
