For the normalized close-to-convex harmonic class \(\mathcal P_{\mathcal H}^0(\alpha)\), the areas of centered subdisk images fill an exact closed interval with a new sharp lower endpoint \(r^2-(1-\alpha)^2r^4/2\) after division by \(\pi\). The only lower extremizers are quadratic harmonic shears, whereas analytic extremizers give the previously known upper endpoint; the entire unit-disk area spectrum is also determined.
