The canonical second-eigenpolytope of the rhombic-dodecahedron graph is tangential but not inscribed: its six degree-\(4\) vertices lie on radius \(1/2\), its eight degree-\(3\) vertices lie on radius \(\sqrt3/4\), and all twelve facets lie at distance \(1/(2\sqrt2)\) from the origin. This gives an exact metric signature for the exceptional spectral polytope that is not vertex-transitive.
