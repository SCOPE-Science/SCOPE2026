# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The finite \(\ell_\infty\)-sum proof is symbolic. The pointwise \(\Delta\)-criterion is reconstructed from slices and the rank-one characterization, including active and inactive coordinates. The convex-hull converse uses Hahn--Banach separation in each factor and a summed support functional with a strict finite gap.

## Originality
PASS. The closest recent source proves factor inheritance for every absolute normalized norm except \(\ell_\infty\), and explicitly identifies that norm as exceptional. The foundational direct-sum result proves only that convex-DLD2P in both factors is sufficient for every absolute sum. Targeted literature, published-finding corpus, and owned-ledger checks did not locate the exact finite \(\ell_\infty\) equivalence. The known pointwise coordinate behavior of \(\Delta\)-points is not claimed as novel.

## Value
PASS. The result completes the natural missing case in the recent absolute-sum theorem with an exact criterion and shows that the exceptional norm obeys an opposite ``one good factor'' law rather than factor inheritance.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest sources are Guerrero-Viu--Markowicz (arXiv:2607.01875v1) and Abrahamsen--Haller--Lima--Pirk (arXiv:1812.02450). The statement is restricted to finite real \(\ell_\infty\)-sums; no infinite-coordinate analogue is asserted. An unindexed older corollary remains a residual originality risk.
