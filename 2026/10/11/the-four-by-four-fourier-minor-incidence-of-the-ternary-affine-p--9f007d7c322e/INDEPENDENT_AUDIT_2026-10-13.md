The 9×9 character table of F3² has 126 four-point row and 126 four-point column sets. Independent exact 24-term Leibniz determinant calculation in Z[omega] using integer coefficient pairs and omega²+omega+1=0 exhausts all 15,876 positional minors without numeric tolerances. It reproduces 54 affine caps, 72 line-rich sets, exactly 5,670 singular ordered pairs (45 per fixed row) and type quotient 18/27 for each line-rich A, 36/9 for each cap A. Affine transitivity of caps and line-plus-point configurations justifies row type uniformity. Nonsingular pairs=10,206. This proves only finite F3² 4x4 incidence, not exact-support kernel existence with all coefficients nonzero.
