The twelve-vertex asymmetric Frucht graph has \(\operatorname{Ind}(F)\simeq\bigvee^{3}S^{2}\). Exactly four vertex deletions preserve this homotopy type via collapsible links; the other eight split into six deletions equivalent to \(\bigvee^{2}S^{2}\) and two equivalent to \(\bigvee^{4}S^{2}\), with the corresponding links classified exactly.
