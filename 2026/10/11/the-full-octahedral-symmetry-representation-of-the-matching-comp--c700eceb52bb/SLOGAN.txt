The ordinary matching complex of the octahedral graph is a wedge of eleven circles. Its full rational octahedral symmetry module is \((S^{(3,1)}\otimes\varepsilon)\oplus S^{(2,1,1)}\oplus(S^{(2,1,1)}\otimes\varepsilon)\oplus(S^{(2,2)}\otimes\varepsilon)\), revealing three inversion-even and eight inversion-odd homology dimensions and no globally invariant class.
