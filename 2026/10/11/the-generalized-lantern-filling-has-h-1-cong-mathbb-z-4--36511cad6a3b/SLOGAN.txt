For the six-factor generalized-lantern PALF on \(\Sigma_{0,6}\), the vanishing-cycle matrix has Smith form \(\operatorname{diag}(1,1,1,1,4)\). Thus \(H_1\cong\mathbb Z/4\), \(H^2\cong\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/4\), and Mori's nonzero order-two \(c_1\) is exactly \(2\tau\) in the cyclic four-torsion summand.
