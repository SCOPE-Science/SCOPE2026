For the intrinsic scale-three Čech nerve of the four-cube, the symmetry group \(B_4\) acts on its one-dimensional rational third homology by the determinant and on its 24-dimensional fourth homology as \(V_{(2),(2)}\oplus V_{(2),(1,1)}\oplus V_{(3),(1)}\oplus V_{(2,1),(1)}\). Neither nonzero homology degree admits a class fixed by every cube symmetry.
