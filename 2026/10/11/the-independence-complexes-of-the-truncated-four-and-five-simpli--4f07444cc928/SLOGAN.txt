The ordinary independence complexes of the truncated four- and five-simplices have integral homotopy types \(\bigvee^5 S^3\vee\bigvee^{23}S^4\) and \(\bigvee^6 S^4\vee\bigvee^{967}S^5\), respectively. Explicit acyclic discrete-Morse matchings and unit integral attaching maps establish both mixed-dimensional sphere-wedge decompositions.
