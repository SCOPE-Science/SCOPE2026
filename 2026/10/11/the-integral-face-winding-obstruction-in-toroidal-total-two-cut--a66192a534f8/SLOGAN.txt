For every toroidal grid \(C_m\square C_n\) with \(m,n\ge4\), the natural face–winding extension underlying the top homology of its total two-cut complex has **exact integral extension-class order \(\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)\)** under translations, although it splits rationally. The complete rational character under rotations, reflections and coordinate interchange is \(\operatorname{sgn}_{V}(g)[\det(A_g)(f(g)-1)+\operatorname{tr}(A_g)]\).
