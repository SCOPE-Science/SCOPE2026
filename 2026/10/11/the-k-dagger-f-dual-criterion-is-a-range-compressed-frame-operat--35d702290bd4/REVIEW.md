# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The source definition and Lemma 3.2 were reconstructed from the publisher PDF and public preprint. Under the printed \(A=B=1\) convention, the inequalities do not imply
\[
S_F=KK^*.
\]
For closed-range \(K\), direct substitution shows that the proposed dual \(K^\dagger F\) reconstructs through
\[
S_F(K^\dagger)^*.
\]
Moore--Penrose identities prove that this equals \(K\) exactly when
\[
S_FP_{R(K)}=KK^*.
\]
The one-point example satisfies the printed bounds and violates the dual equation exactly.

## Originality
PASS. Exact-source searches, the standard discrete \(K^\dagger F\) dual theorem, the later continuous exact-Parseval convention, operator-factorization duality, semantic database matches, and the available prior finding corpus were compared. A later 2024 paper already adopts the stronger sufficient identity
\[
S_F=KK^*,
\]
but no checked source states the one-point counterexample or the weaker exact equivalence
\[
K^\dagger F\text{ is a }K\text{-dual}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
S_FP_{R(K)}=KK^*.
\]

## Value
PASS. Lemma 3.2 supports the paper's canonical-dual construction, so the distinction between mixed numerical bounds and exact \(K\)-Parseval energy is structural rather than cosmetic. The range-compressed criterion identifies precisely which portion of the frame operator is relevant and avoids imposing unnecessary equality on \(R(K)^\perp\).
