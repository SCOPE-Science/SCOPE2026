For the classical sixteen-point Kummer biplane, the simplicial complex consisting of every subset of a block has homotopy type \(\mathbb{RP}^{2}\vee\bigvee^{55}S^{2}\), including genuine integral two-torsion. It is also the open-neighborhood complex of both the four-by-four rook graph and, under an explicit simplicial isomorphism, the Shrikhande graph.
