Any continuous normalized solution of the proposed Morley-dihedral limiting equation has an infinite Fourier spectrum. More precisely, its nonlinear term has nonnegative nonzero Fourier modes \(4\tau\lvert\widehat\gamma(k)\rvert^2\), and the cubic moment obeys \(\int_0^1\gamma^3=3\int_0^1\gamma\), ruling out every exact trigonometric-polynomial ansatz.
