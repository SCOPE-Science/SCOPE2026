# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The complete face poset is reconstructed from the thirteen open neighborhoods. All 286 Morse pairs are codimension-one and disjoint, and the complete oriented Hasse diagram on 598 faces and 2,067 covers is acyclic. The unique critical 0-cell and 25 critical 2-cells prove the asserted wedge decomposition. Finite-field boundary ranks over \(\mathbb F_2\), \(\mathbb F_3\), and \(\mathbb F_5\) independently agree with the resulting homology.

## Originality
PASS. Exact and alias searches used Paley \(P_{13}\), \(QR(13)\), circulant \(C_{13}(1,3,4)\), and strongly regular \((13,6,2,3)\). Shukla's directly relevant full text classifies maximum-degree-at-most-\(3\) graphs and \(4\)-regular circulants, so it does not cover this \(6\)-regular graph. Matsushita's broader paper identifies the fundamental group of a neighborhood complex but does not determine this full homotopy type. No searched database record implied the claim.

## Value
PASS. The graph is a canonical highly symmetric Paley/conference graph and a natural first \(6\)-regular circulant beyond the published \(4\)-regular classification. The result gives a full homotopy type with an explicit certificate, not merely a numerical homology profile.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest direct paper is Shukla, arXiv:1802.04526, which stops at \(4\)-regular circulants. The result here is finite and specific to order \(13\); no general Paley-graph pattern is claimed. Bibliographic searches cannot eliminate every possible unindexed equivalent formulation.
