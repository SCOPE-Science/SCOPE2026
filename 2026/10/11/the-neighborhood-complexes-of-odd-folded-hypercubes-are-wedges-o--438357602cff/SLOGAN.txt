For every odd \(n\ge3\), the open-neighborhood complex of the \(n\)-dimensional folded cube collapses to dimension two and has integral homotopy type \(\mathcal N(FQ_n)\simeq\bigvee^{2^{n-3}(n^2-5n+8)-1}S^2\). In particular, the Clebsch graph case is a wedge of \(31\) two-spheres, and the formula holds uniformly for all odd dimensions.
