The regular nine-vertex torus has exactly nine triangular integral homology systoles, split evenly among three primitive unoriented directions \(\{\pm g_1\}\), \(\{\pm g_2\}\), and \(\{\pm(g_1-g_2)\}\). Its full order-\(108\) simplicial symmetry group is transitive on all nine carriers, while the order-\(9\) kernel of the homology action separates them into the three direction orbits.\n
