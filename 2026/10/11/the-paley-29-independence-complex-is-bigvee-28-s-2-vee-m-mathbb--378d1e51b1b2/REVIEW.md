# Same-model review of The Paley-\(29\) independence complex is \(\bigvee^{28}S^2\vee M(\mathbb Z/2,2)\)

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reconstructs the complete finite independence complex, verifies an acyclic 397-pair Morse matching on all 2436 Hasse covers, and computes the resulting \(37\times9\) integral Morse boundary. A unit \(8\times8\) minor, a determinant-2 \(9\times9\) minor, and mod-2 rank 8 force Smith invariant factors \(1^8,2\). The absence of critical 1-cells makes the Morse 2-skeleton a wedge of 37 two-spheres, and standard cell slides plus unit cancellations yield \(\bigvee^{28}S^2\vee M(\mathbb Z/2,2)\). The original simplicial mod-2 chain complex supplies an independent consistency check.

## Originality
PASS. Exact searches covered the Paley, quadratic-residue, circulant, self-complementary clique-complex, Moore-space, and torsion formulations. The most relevant inspected source, arXiv:1807.05755v1, treats the 2-dimensional Paley-\(17\) case and does not imply the 3-dimensional \(P_{29}\) result. The middle-window topology anchor arXiv:2207.10363v1 treats square grids. House of Graphs 51215 fixes the target graph and aliases but gives no topology. An unindexed equivalent finite computation remains a residual risk.

## Value
PASS. The target is a canonical symmetric graph, and the result detects integral 2-torsion through an explicit Moore summand. This is a qualitative topological feature not obtainable from the standard graph parameters alone and provides a compact exact test case for independence complexes of Paley graphs.

## Limitations and closest literature
The claim is only for \(P_{29}\). It does not extrapolate to a Paley family. The finite verifier certifies the combinatorics and boundary algebra; the last topological reduction uses standard discrete Morse and CW cell-slide facts. Closest inspected literature is the Paley-\(17\) vertex-decomposable result in arXiv:1807.05755v1 and exact grid-family independence-complex homotopy calculations in arXiv:2207.10363v1.
