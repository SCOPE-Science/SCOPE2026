## Correctness — PASS
The graph is fixed by its explicit LCF representation and checked against the point–line incidence presentation of the Pappus configuration through a complete vertex permutation. The verifier enumerates all 2,597 nonempty independent faces, constructs 1,293 ordinary matched Hasse edges and topologically sorts all 2,597 faces across 11,430 directed incidences. Exactly one critical vertex and ten critical four-faces remain. Forman discrete Morse theory reduces the complex to a point with ten four-cells, all attaching to that point, giving the integral sphere wedge. A separately written incidence-based chain computation verifies all boundary ranks modulo two and three. Both scripts were executed successfully from the complete artifact set. Risks are the standard finite-verifier implementation and input-identification risks, mitigated by independent graph presentations and exhaustive checks; these are not independent external validation.

The complete primary texts were inspected for the relevant statements, and neither supplies this exact Pappus calculation. The most directly named indexed published record concerns the Pappus **matching** complex, which has a different ground set and cannot imply the stated independence-complex homotopy equivalence. Searches covered Pappus aliases, complement clique complexes, nine-point incidence hypergraphs and homological ranks. Unindexed equivalent work remains possible; the published matching-complex record's repository full text was inaccessible, and its search-index abstract was not treated as evidence of whole-document noncoverage.
