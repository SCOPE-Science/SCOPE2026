For the Petersen graph \(P\), the ordinary matching complex has exact homotopy type \(\operatorname{Match}(P)\simeq\bigvee^{5}S^2\vee\bigvee^{5}S^3\). A complete discrete-Morse certificate leaves five critical \(2\)-cells and five critical \(3\)-cells, and the integral Morse attaching matrix is zero.
