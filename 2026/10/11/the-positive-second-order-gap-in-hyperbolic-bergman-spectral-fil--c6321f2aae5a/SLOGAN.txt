For hyperbolic-distance Toeplitz forms on weighted complex-ball Bergman spaces, the known spectral filling bound is asymptotically sharp at leading order but has a strictly positive, exactly calculable second-order deficit of size \(N(\log N)^{p-1}\). Its coefficient is \(\frac{p}{(2d)^p}[\log\Gamma(\alpha+d+1)-\log\Gamma(\alpha+1)-d\psi(\alpha+1)]\) for every fixed \(d\ge1\), \(\alpha>-1\), \(p>0\).
