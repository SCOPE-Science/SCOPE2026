In the printed hybrid-controlled predator–prey delay model, \(\alpha_1=0.6\) changes the positive equilibrium, so the reported point \(E(0.1975,0.2674)\) is not an equilibrium of the controlled equations. Recomputing at the true equilibrium gives the first Hopf crossing \(\delta_c=2.96355390887214958\ldots\), slightly later than the uncontrolled \(\delta_0=2.92415281225183456\ldots\), reversing the paper's claimed controller effect.
