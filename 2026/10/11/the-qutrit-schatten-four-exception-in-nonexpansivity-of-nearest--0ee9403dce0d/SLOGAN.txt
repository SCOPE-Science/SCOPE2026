For trace-one Hermitian matrices, nearest-density-matrix projection is independent of the Schatten exponent \(1<p<\infty\), but it is nonexpansive exactly in dimensions \(n\le2\), at the Hilbert exponent \(p=2\), and at the exceptional qutrit exponent \((n,p)=(3,4)\). Explicit noncommuting perturbations show strict expansion in every remaining case.
