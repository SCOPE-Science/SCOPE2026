For \(T=\mathrm{Tonn}^{11,4}(1,2,3,5)\), the integral homology is one copy of \(\mathbb Z\) in each degree \(0,1,2,3\), exactly as for \(S^1\times S^2\), but every vertex link is \(\bigvee^3S^2\). This next all-distinct nongeneric four-length case is therefore globally homology-manifold-like while being singular at every vertex.
