For every nilpotent Jordan block \(J_n\), the affine operator-norm pencil \((1-\lambda)I+\lambda J_n\) has the **unique complex** optimizer \(\lambda=(n+1)/(2n+1)\), yielding an exact finite tridiagonal formula for its minimum. The same minimum is exactly the largest \(q\) for which the scaled \(q\)-numerical range is a \(1\)-spectral disk for \(J_n\), sharpening the universal threshold beyond dimension two.
