# Review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The source's variational identity gives \(M_n=\max I_n(T)\), while its cone profile supplies \(M_n\ge2^{{n/2+1}}/(n+1)\). For every admissible \(T\), the pointwise bound \(1-tT(t)\le2\) and Cauchy--Schwarz give \(I_n(T)\le2^{{n/2+1}}\). The beta-integral formula for \(B_n\) and the standard gamma-ratio asymptotic then imply the two stated root limits. All quantifiers are over the complete admissible transport class, not a sampled subclass.

## Originality
**PASS.** The lead paper was inspected at its sharp geometric theorem, sharp functional theorem, transport-action reduction, path-maximization proposition, comparison with earlier functional inequalities, and cone-profile appendix. It states the strict comparison \(M_n>B_n\) and the cone lower bound, but it does not state the matching exponential upper bound, \(M_n^{{1/n}}\to\sqrt2\), or \(R_n^{{1/n}}\to2\). Searches for the profile constant, the ball comparison, the functional quadratic comparison, and equivalent exponential-rate formulations found no checked source that implies the claim. The closest neighboring records concern different Santaló functionals or local Hessian questions.

## Value
**PASS.** The source's new sharp inequality changes the extremizer away from the Euclidean ball/quadratic benchmark but leaves the dimensional size of that improvement qualitative. The finding identifies the exact exponential scale of the improvement with a short global argument valid on the full transport class. This distinguishes a genuinely dimension-amplified phenomenon from a bounded or merely polynomial gap and gives an immediately reusable normalization for future asymptotic study of the maximizer.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is arXiv:2609.38041v1 itself; Bobkov's 2010 convex-measure inequality is the principal earlier functional comparison discussed there. The result does not determine the polynomial prefactor, a full asymptotic equivalent, or the maximizing parameter.
