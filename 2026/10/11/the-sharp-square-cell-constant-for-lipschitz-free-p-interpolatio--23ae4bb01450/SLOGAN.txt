The canonical bilinear vertex retraction of the \(\ell_1\)-unit square into its four-point Lipschitz-free \(p\)-space has exact Lipschitz constant \(\operatorname{Lip}(r)=(2+2^p)^{1/p}/4\) for every \(0<p\le1\). The sharp tangent balances three transport loads \((1/2,1/4,1/4)\), improving the known dimensional lower bound strictly whenever \(p<1\).
