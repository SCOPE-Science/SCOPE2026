For Rhaly (terraced) matrices with nonincreasing coefficient moduli, trace-class membership is exactly \(\sum_{n\geq0}|\eta_n|\log(n+2)<\infty\), with two-sided equivalence of the trace norm to that series. At the canonical critical coefficients \(|\eta_n|\asymp((n+1)\log^r(e+n))^{-1}\), the sharp endpoint is \(r>2\), revealing a logarithmic loss absent from the established \(\mathcal S_q\) criteria for \(q>1\).
