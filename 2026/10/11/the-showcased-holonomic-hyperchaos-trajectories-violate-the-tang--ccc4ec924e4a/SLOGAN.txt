For the Li–Cui holonomic Hamiltonian model, the three initial conditions used to exhibit hyperchaos and sensitivity lie on \(\|q\|^2=L^2\) but not in its tangent bundle because \(q\cdot p\ne0\). Since \(\dot{\|q\|^2}=2q\cdot p\), integrating the printed equations from those states immediately leaves the constraint sphere, so those numerical trajectories do not establish hyperchaos of the stated constrained system.
