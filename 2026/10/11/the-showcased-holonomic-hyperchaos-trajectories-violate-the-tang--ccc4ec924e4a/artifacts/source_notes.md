Primary-source facts used in the proof:
- Li–Cui equation (3.1): \(q_1^2+q_2^2+q_3^2=L^2\).
- Li–Cui equation (3.2): \(q\cdot p=0\).
- Li–Cui equation (3.5): \(\dot q=p\) with the three displayed polynomial numerators for \(\dot p\).
- The source sets \(L=0.01\), reports the holonomic Lyapunov exponents from \((0,0,0.01,0.011,0.051,0.051)\), and lists two further sensitivity states \((0,0.01,0,0.011,0.051,0.051)\) and \((0.01,0,0,0.011,0.051,0.051)\).
- Udwadia's constrained-Hamiltonian formulation requires constrained initial data to satisfy the constraint equations and its spherical example differentiates \(q^Tq-L^2=0\) to \(q^Tp=0\).
