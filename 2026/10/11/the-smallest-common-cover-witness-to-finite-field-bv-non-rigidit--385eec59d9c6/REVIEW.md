# Review of “The smallest common-cover witness to finite-field BV non-rigidity”

## Correctness
PASS. The source classification applies for every odd prime because the two kernels have order \(4\), invertible in \(\mathbf F_p\), and the universal-cover factors have torsion-free integral homology. The group-algebra decompositions are exact, and the rank lower bound follows from the centre classification and rank additivity.

## Originality
PASS. The closest source gives the general classification and the abstract \(C_4\) versus \(C_2\times C_2\) group-algebra coincidence, but not an explicit smallest common-cover semisimple realization or a rank threshold. Searches for the explicit quotient pair, rank-minimal witnesses and equivalent finite-field BV statements found no prior statement implying the rank-\(4\) minimality claim. Residual risk is an unindexed contemporaneous note containing the same elementary minimal realization.

## Value
PASS. The result turns the source's abstract finite-field loss of fundamental-group rigidity into its smallest common-cover semisimple witness and identifies the exact coefficient-characteristic boundary. The sharp rank cutoff is a natural finite invariant of the phenomenon.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest primary source is arXiv:2610.04164v1, especially Corollary 9.3, Remark 9.6 and Example 9.7. The claim does not assert rank minimality without the common-cover restriction and does not treat characteristic \(2\).
