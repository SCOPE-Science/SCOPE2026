The common cover \(K=\mathrm{SU}(4)\times\mathrm{SU}(2)\) is the smallest-rank semisimple realization in which \(\pi_1\cong C_4\) and \(\pi_1\cong C_2\times C_2\) can become indistinguishable to finite-field loop BV homology. For odd primes the two BV algebras coincide exactly when \(p\equiv1\pmod4\), and rank \(4\) is sharp.
