# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The standard cycle graph-associahedron is the tube-simplex Minkowski sum. Every proper tube of size \(k\) is a cyclic interval, and the exact right-hand side of its facet is the number of connected subintervals, \(k(k+1)/2\). Projection of the subset normal to the affine tangent space gives norm \(\sqrt{k(n-k)/n}\). The resulting squared facet-distance difference factors into a strictly positive expression for every \(k>1\). The singleton facets then give a matching all-center upper bound and force every coordinate at equality, proving uniqueness.

## Value
PASS. The cyclohedron is a classical family and the cited modern source explicitly places it among the fundamental graph associahedra. The finding supplies an exact all-dimensional Euclidean size parameter for its standard nestohedral realization, proves that its vertex barycenter is the unique incenter, and identifies all tangent facets. These are intrinsic metric data not determined by the combinatorial type alone.
