# Scientific review: three-point configurations of finite crowns

## Correctness — PASS
The theorem uses the precise ordered deleted cube of the \(2n\)-point finite crown, with a sharp \(n=2\) exception. The classes \(L_1,U_1,L_2,U_2\) partition precisely the configurations containing a comparable coordinate pair. In the stated order, their unique strict predecessor/successor witnesses prove each point is a beat point at deletion time. The remaining pairwise-incomparable triples have multiple incomparable extreme neighbors or none, so no beat point remains. Stong's theorem identifies the exact core. The count follows from four exhaustive class counts. The standalone verifier reconstructs these checks for \(2\le n\le12\). Finite testing is not used as the proof of the universal assertion.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
The closest same-object published result, *Two-point configurations of finite crowns retain the circle weak type*, applies to two labeled particles and supplies neither a three-particle reduction nor the minimal finite-space classification here. The general results of Barmak–Minian and Minian provide the reduction framework, not this family-specific classification. The known models of configurations in geometric simplicial complexes are not the deleted cube of the finite crown. Public literature and published-result searches did not reveal a broader theorem that implies the exact triple Stong core or its size. An unindexed equivalent and a yet-unidentified general deleted-power reduction remain possible.

## Value — PASS
This is a natural complete finite-space homotopy-core classification for **all** crowns and three labeled particles, with an explicit sharp exceptional case and closed core cardinality. The classification is stronger than merely finding Betti numbers or the weak type and is not implied by the known two-particle weak homotopy equivalence. The four-stage reduction exposes the combinatorial obstruction from configurations with one versus two incidences and gives a reproducible normal form for follow-up three-particle topology.

## Scope and access limitations
The result does not determine the general homology of the core or prove equivariance of the deformation-retraction homotopy. The published two-particle comparison was inspected in full. The finite-poset foundational arXiv texts were inspected as HTML; the geometric simplicial-configuration literature was distinguished by its object and scope. Novelty is provisional in the usual literature-search sense, and the independent audit has not been performed.

Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
