For every crown \(C_n\) with \(n\ge3\), the exact Stong core of its ordered three-particle configuration space consists of the pairwise incomparable triples and has \(2n(2n-4)(2n-5)\) points; a four-stage beat-point reduction removes everything else. The four-point crown is exceptional: its ordered three-particle space is already a beat-point-free union of two twelve-point crowns.
