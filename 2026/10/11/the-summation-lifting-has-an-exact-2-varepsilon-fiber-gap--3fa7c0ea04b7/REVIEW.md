# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The final claim is the statement in the Finding section of `RESULT.md`. The key new step is exact: for fixed \(x\) and \(N\), every sufficiently late local lifting in the directed system has first support index \(m_1>N\), because the finite-dimensional datum contains \(e_{N+1}\) and the source requires \(\|(I-P_{m_1})|_F\|<1\). Hence the weak-star ultralimit annihilates all finitely supported vectors of \(Z\), and therefore all of \(Z\), proving \(PS=0\).

The remaining norm identity uses standard M-ideal structure. Since \(Z=(\bigoplus E_m^*)_{c_0}\) is M-embedded, \(P=J_Z^*\) is an L-projection and \(Y^{**}=J_Y(Y)\oplus_1\ker P\). This makes the norm additive between \(Sx\) and every \(J_Yy\). The focal paper supplies both \(\|Sx\|=\|x\|_\infty\) and the norm-one quotient property of \(Q\), so the exact unrenormed distance follows. Proposition 2.4 of the focal paper then multiplies that distance by \(\varepsilon\) on the kernel of \(Q^{**}\). No finite experiment is used.

It gives an upper estimate corresponding to \(M+1=2\) but does not identify the ultralimit lifting as lying in \(\ker P\), nor does it state an exact affine-fiber distance. The standard M-ideal reference proves that \(c_0\)-sums preserve M-embeddedness and that the natural third-dual projection is an L-projection, but it contains no statement about this summation quotient or this lifting.

Searches using the aliases “summation quotient,” “bidual lifting,” “M-embedded,” “L-summand,” “singular summand,” “affine fiber,” and “factor two” did not locate an implication-equivalent statement. This negative evidence is not a novelty proof. Because the focal preprint is recent and the M-ideal ingredients are classical, an equivalent special-case observation under different terminology remains possible.

## Value
PASS. The source's quantitative argument leaves a factor-two gap and explicitly uses a generic \(M+1\) displacement estimate with \(M=1\). The exact formula here shows that the loss of \(2\) at that step is structural for the source's own ultralimit lifting: no better choice of representative in the genuine quotient fiber can improve it. This isolates a concrete boundary for attempts to sharpen the lower-bound mechanism and identifies the geometric reason for the factor, namely complete separation of the lifting into the singular L-summand.
