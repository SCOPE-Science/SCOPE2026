For the independence complexes of all generalized Mycielskians \(\mu_{\ell}(K_n)\), the natural \(S_n\)-action on reduced rational homology is completely determined by tensor powers of the standard representation \(U_n\). In particular, at \(\ell=3k\) the unique nonzero module is \(U_n^{\otimes(k+1)}\oplus U_n^{\otimes k}\), and at \(\ell=3k+1,3k+2\) it is \(U_n^{\otimes(k+1)}\).
