# Review

## Correctness
PASS. In the scalar two-by-two specialization, every coefficient array literally prescribed by Note 3.2 is an outer product and therefore has determinant \(0\). Two such allowed arrays sum to the identity, whose determinant is \(1\). This proves that the displayed coefficient set is not a vector space.

The repaired domain
\[
\bigoplus_{i,j}^{\ell^2}(H_i\otimes K_j)
\]
is a Hilbert space. The canonical map from
\[
\left(\bigoplus_i^{\ell^2}H_i\right)
\otimes
\left(\bigoplus_j^{\ell^2}K_j\right)
\]
is isometric on simple tensors and onto by density of finite-support elementary tensors. The corrected synthesis and adjoint identities follow on a dense subspace and extend by boundedness.

## Originality
PASS. Exact-title, arXiv-identifier, DOI, Note 3.2, synthesis-domain, direct-sum, simple-tensor, non-simple-tensor, and correction searches were compared with the relevant tensor-frame literature.

The closest prior tensor-product frame papers establish tensor-product frame properties in standard Hilbert spaces. They do not state the two-by-two nonlinearity counterexample or the unitary domain correction to Note 3.2 and Theorem 3.6.

## Value
PASS. The disputed object is the coefficient Hilbert space on which the synthesis and analysis operators are supposed to act. A non-linear domain prevents those objects from being bounded linear operators and makes the published synthesis identity ill-typed. The repair is structural, canonical, and preserves the intended frame-operator identity.
