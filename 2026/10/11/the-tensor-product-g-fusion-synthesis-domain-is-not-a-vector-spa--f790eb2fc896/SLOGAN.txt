The coefficient object in Note 3.2 consists only of globally separable arrays and is not closed under addition, so it cannot be the Hilbert domain of the tensor-product \(g\)-fusion synthesis operator. Replacing it by the full \(\ell^2\)-direct sum yields a canonical unitary \(U\) and the type-correct identity \(T_{\Lambda\otimes\Gamma}U=T_\Lambda\otimes T_\Gamma\).
