For the explicit \(\ell=0\) same-Alexander Cappell–Shaneson pair of Endo–Iwaki–Pajitnov, cyclic-cover first homology agrees through degree \(9\) and first separates at degree \(10\). The degree \(10\) torsion groups have the same order but Smith types \((1,1,1,N)\) and \((1,1,11,N/11)\), so the distinction detects structure invisible to the common Alexander polynomial.
