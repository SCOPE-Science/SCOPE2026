# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The finite vertex set and inclusive threshold are specified precisely. The first nontrivial graph has 27 triangular facets and 81 edges; collapsing one unique free edge per triangle leaves a connected graph with 54 edges, so its cycle rank is 28. The radius-two complex is precisely the independence complex of the categorical triple product. The standalone exact code reconstructs the 5949 nonempty faces, checks a complete 2951-pair discrete-Morse matching, confirms the 47 unmatched cells have dimensions zero (one) and three (46), and tests all 27189 directed Hasse cover relations for acyclicity. Discrete-Morse CW theory then proves a wedge of 46 three-spheres; independent mod-two ranks corroborate but are not mistaken for a proof of the integral homotopy type. The maximum Hamming distance is three, yielding the terminal simplex. Persistence endpoints follow without an unverified connecting-map assumption because the nonzero positive homology occurs in different degrees at the two nontrivial thresholds.

## Originality — PASS

The closest full-text source is Antolín Camarena–Carnero Bravo, arXiv:2307.12401. Proposition 25 proves the family containing a \(K_2\) factor; Conjecture 26 explicitly *predicts* the three-factor wedge formula and the value 46 at \((3,3,3)\), but supplies no proof of that case. The matched-face construction supplies a missing finite proof and identifies the full associated ternary Hamming Rips filtration. Goyal–Shukla–Singh, arXiv:1905.06926, establish two-factor results and the general toggling method, without the required 46-cell classification. Circle and binary-hypercube results are not stronger covering results for this ternary setting. Targeted searches found no other covering published statement; an unindexed equivalent proof or inaccessible journal-version material remains a risk. Numerical 46 was anticipated, and is not claimed as a novel prediction.

## Value — PASS

The graph is a natural, highly symmetric 27-point metric space and the first fully symmetric three-factor categorical product with no \(K_2\) factor. The calculation verifies a genuinely conjectural special case by an exact finite homotopy certificate, not just a Betti count, while simultaneously determining integral homotopy types and persistence at every radius. It does not purport to prove the all-parameter conjecture; the value is in this motivated precise case and its graph-metric interpretation.

## Limitations and nearest literature

Key comparisons are arXiv:2307.12401, Proposition 25 and Conjecture 26; arXiv:1905.06926, Section 3; and the distinct circle and binary-hypercube Rips references arXiv:1503.03669 and arXiv:2103.01040. The code verifies a finite case and cannot establish the general \((n,m,l)\) conjecture. Possible independent precedence remains the principal scientific risk.
