The 27-point ternary Hamming cube has inclusive Vietoris--Rips types \(\bigsqcup^{27}\mathrm{pt}\), \(\bigvee^{28}S^1\), \(\bigvee^{46}S^3\), and a point at successive integer thresholds, with a complete field-independent persistence barcode. An exhaustive acyclic matching proves \(\operatorname{Ind}(K_3\times K_3\times K_3)\simeq\bigvee^{46}S^3\), establishing the \((3,3,3)\) case of a previously published conjecture, not the conjecture in general.
