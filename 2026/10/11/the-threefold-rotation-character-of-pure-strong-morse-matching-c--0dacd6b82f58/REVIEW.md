The central 2026 primary source gives the pure strong-cycle homotopy type and the exact facet count but does not give rotation Lefschetz characters or group representations. The resulting virtual rotation module and nontrivial cubic rational summands are not implications of the previously known nonequivariant Betti numbers. Targeted searches of mathematical literature and indexed published research found no equivalent statement, but absence of indexed hits is not proof of novelty and unindexed equivalent calculations remain possible.

The cubic-divisibility dichotomy is a structural, all-length equivariant invariant. Unlike the nonequivariant homotopy type, it determines how the cyclic group acts on the top homology for every length divisible by three, revealing a forced two-copy cyclotomic representation. It also gives a uniform virtual representation in all other lengths. This is neither a narrow numerical sample nor a mere renaming of the published sphere multiplicities.
