For the pure strong discrete Morse matching complex of every cycle of length \(n\ge6\), rotation through \(a\) steps has reduced homological Lefschetz number \(5\) precisely when \(3\mid\gcd(n,a)\), and \(-1\) otherwise. In particular, at lengths divisible by three, its complete rational rotation-homology representation is governed by two copies of the nontrivial two-dimensional rational representation of the cubic quotient.
