In the seventeen-vertex Nil triangulation \(M_2=\{Oo,1\mid2\}\), every one of the \(261\) empty triangles is an integral homology \(1\)-systole. The infinite-order minimizers project to exactly three primitive directions \(\pm g_1\), \(\pm g_2\), and \(\pm(g_1-g_2)\), with both order-two torsion lifts represented in every direction.
