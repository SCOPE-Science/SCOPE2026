In the Datta--Upadhyay triangulation \(Q_{5,3}\), the three nonzero classes of \(H^1(-;\mathbf F_2)\) have exact cosystolic profiles \((5,1,1),(5,1,1),(10,1,0)\), where the entries are minimum support, minimizer count, and cup square. The two unique weight-five carriers are exactly the two exceptional five-vertex blocks used in the published non-weak-regularity analysis, and their disjoint union is the unique minimum representative of \(w_1\).
