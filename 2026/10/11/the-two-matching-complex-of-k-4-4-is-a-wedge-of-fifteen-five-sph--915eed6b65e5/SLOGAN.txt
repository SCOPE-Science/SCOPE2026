For the complete bipartite graph \(K_{4,4}\), let \(M_2(K_{4,4})\) be the simplicial complex whose vertices are the sixteen edges of \(K_{4,4}\) and whose faces are the edge sets in which every graph vertex has degree at most \(2\). Then \(M_2(K_{4,4})\simeq\bigvee^{15}S^5\). A complete acyclic discrete-Morse matching leaves one critical vertex and exactly fifteen critical \(5\)-cells.
