For the complex function plane \(E_\theta=\operatorname{span}\{1,z\}\) on a circular arc, the unique best-conditioned Hilbertian quadratic form is \(|a|^2+|b|^2+2\max\{\cos\theta,0\}\operatorname{Re}(a\overline b)\), up to scale. Its sharp Banach–Mazur distortion is the previously known \(\sqrt2\), but the exact optimal metric has a new semicircle threshold: it stops changing at \(\theta=\pi/2\) even as the Banach spaces remain nonisometric.
