The verifier enumerates all \(105\) nonempty faces, all \(244\) Hasse covers, verifies all \(49\) matched pairs, and proves the directed Hasse graph acyclic. Residual risk remains from unindexed literature and from the inaccessible full text of the 2026 same-family paper.

**PASS.** The Wagner graph is the canonical eight-vertex Möbius ladder, a standard highly symmetric cubic graph. Exact ordinary matching-complex topology is a natural invariant for the same family in which independence and perfect-matching complexes are already being studied. The result supplies a sharp finite base case and a reproducible discrete-Morse certificate rather than a routine numerical recomputation.
