For every nontrivial thin slice of an \(n\)-dimensional rectangular prism with \(n\ge3\), the intrinsic Euclidean inradius is controlled by only three quantities: total mass, total co-mass, and the narrowest coordinate interval. Explicitly, \(r=\sqrt{n/(n-1)}\min\{k/n,(C-k)/n,c_{\min}/2\}\), and the full incenter set is obtained by shifting only the coordinate inequalities that are actual facets.
