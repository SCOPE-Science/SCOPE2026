For every \(n\ge13\), the Lovász open-neighborhood complex of the third power of the cycle \(C_n\) collapses in exactly \(10n\) elementary steps onto a triangulated \(S^2\)-bundle over \(S^1\). The bundle is untwisted for even \(n\) and twisted for odd \(n\), giving integral two-torsion \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\) precisely in the odd case.
