For the real quadratic-character harmonic fusion planes, every three-plane Gram matrix has the same exact spectrum. For the \(13\)-plane family, all four-plane Gram spectra split into four classes of sizes \(78,390,195,52\), and the sharp block restricted isometry constant \(\delta_4=\sqrt3/2\) is attained exactly by affine images of \(\{0,1,3,9\}\).
