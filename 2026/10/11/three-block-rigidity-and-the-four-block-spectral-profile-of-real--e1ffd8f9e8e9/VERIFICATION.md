# Mathematical verification

The provided `checker.py` has no external dependencies and works with integer matrices and the formal quadratic ring generated by \(i\sqrt{11}\). Run it with Python 3. It enumerates all \(715\) subsets of size four of \(\mathbb F_{13}\), builds the matrices \(C\), \(S\), and \(H=(C+i\sqrt{11}S)(C-i\sqrt{11}S)\), evaluates Newton power sums through degree four, and compares the resulting four characteristic polynomials against factored expressions. Exact integer equality verifies multiplicities \(78,390,195,52\) and the affine orbit of all exceptional quartets.

The same program evaluates every \(286\) triple at \(q=13\), and every \(10\) triple at \(q=5\). Its expected final line is `CHECK_PASS 715 quartets, 286 triples, 10 five-field triples, four exact profiles`. This cross-check does not replace the general all-prime-power reflection argument for triples. A direct symbolic determinant for the exceptional four-index representative gives \(\det(tI-M)=(t^2-108)(t^2-12)^3\); its equality class is also tested without numerical approximation.

The theorem for quartets is restricted to \(q=13\); there is no computational extrapolation to larger fields or block sizes. Bibliographic equivalences outside the inspected sources and independent verification remain open.
