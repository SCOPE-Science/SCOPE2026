# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For finite simple graph n>=7, containing a triangle and no 4-cycle (not merely no induced 4-cycle), Alexander dual B of ordinary Delta_3 has all vertices/edges and 2-faces exactly connected triples (P3 or triangle). Any 4-set all of whose triples are connected would induce min degree>=2 and therefore a 4-cycle, so B is at most 2-dimensional. For a P3-face its endpoint nonedge has unique common neighbor else a 4-cycle; this is a private free 1-face. Collapse all path 2-faces along those distinct edges. Every surviving graph triangle shares no graph edge with another triangle (else C4), so remaining 2-faces collapse along a free edge. B retracts to a connected graph with E=C(n,2)-p-t, and p=sum C(d(v),2)-3t, hence rank beta=C(n-1,2)-sum C(d(v),2)+2t. Combinatorial Alexander duality gives reduced homology only in degree n-4 of free rank beta; full (n-5)-skeleton gives simple connectivity and vanishing lower homotopy, so Hurewicz/Whitehead gives wedge of beta S^(n-4), including beta=0 contractible. Independent graph checks: truncated tetrahedron n=12,m=18,t=4 yields beta=27, triangle necklaces 3 to 6 blocks match formula. Infinite-family theorem is symbolic, not inferred from these examples.
