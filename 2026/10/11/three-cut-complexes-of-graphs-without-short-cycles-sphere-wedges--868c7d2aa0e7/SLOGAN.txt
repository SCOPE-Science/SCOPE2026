For every finite simple graph on \(n\ge7\) vertices with no triangles or four-cycles, the ordinary three-cut complex is a wedge of \(\binom{n-1}{2}-\sum_v\binom{\deg(v)}{2}\) spheres of dimension \(n-4\), including the contractible case. The full automorphism character is determined by the permutation of vertices and signed fixed two-edge paths, refining the previously known Euler-characteristic formula into an integral homotopy classification and symmetry-module description.
