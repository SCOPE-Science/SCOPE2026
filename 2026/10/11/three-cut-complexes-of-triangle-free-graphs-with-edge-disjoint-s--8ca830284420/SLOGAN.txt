For every triangle-free graph on \(n\ge8\) vertices with pairwise edge-disjoint four-cycles, the ordinary three-cut complex is a wedge of \(\binom{n-1}{2}-\sum_v\binom{d(v)}2+q\) spheres of dimension \(n-4\), where \(q\) is the number of squares. An explicit collapse of its Alexander dual explains the contribution of each square and proves the result integrally.
