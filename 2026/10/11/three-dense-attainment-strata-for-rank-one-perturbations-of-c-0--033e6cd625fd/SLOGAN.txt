For the positive rank-one perturbations \(T_{a,t}x=\bigl(\sum_k a_kx_k\bigr)\mathbf1-tx\) of \(c_0\hookrightarrow\ell_\infty\), finite support means norm attainment, infinite support with a zero means only weak-star quasi norm attainment, and full support means no weak-star quasi norm attainment. All three strata are norm dense with fixed \(\sum_k a_k\) and \(t\), but generic members of this same-norm family fail weak-star quasi norm attainment.
