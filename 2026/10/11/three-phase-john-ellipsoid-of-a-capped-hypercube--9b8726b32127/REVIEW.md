# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The convex body is full-dimensional for every \(n\ge2\) and \(0<\tau\le n\). The proof uses uniqueness of the maximal-volume inscribed ellipsoid to reduce its symmetry, evaluates exactly the support of every defining halfspace, and globally optimizes the determinant on two explicitly resolved branches. Both regime thresholds, eigenvalues, facet contacts, and endpoint agreements are derived rather than extrapolated. A bundled exact-rational verifier passed 5,520 support/phase cases and 248 independent grid optimizations. Finite checks do not replace the general calculus proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

Ferroni (arXiv:2012.04711v1, Sections 1–3) gives the exact uniform-matroid independence-polytope description and Ehrhart positivity; Ferroni (arXiv:2105.04465v3, Sections 1, 3–4) treats matroid polytopes and Ehrhart data, not maximal-volume ellipsoids. Jochemko–Ravichandran (2022, Sections 3–4) treat Minkowski functionals and Ehrhart coefficients. Goemans–Harvey–Iwata–Mirrokni (2009, Sections 2–3) give an ellipsoidal-approximation framework for centrally **symmetrized** polymatroids, a different body. Published result searches for capped simplex, truncated cube, and uniform matroid John ellipsoids did not reveal a statement covering the explicit intermediate branch and its contacts. Previously available results cover the central halfcube slice \(\tau=n/2\), the largest inscribed Euclidean balls at integer ranks, and the elementary endpoint regimes, but not the complete anisotropic interval. An equivalent earlier presentation under a different name cannot be excluded.

## Value — PASS

The three-phase analytic classification answers a natural geometric optimization problem for an entire family of cube truncations, including all uniform-matroid independence polytopes. The interval between the simplex-fit and cube-fit thresholds has a genuinely anisotropic optimal ellipsoid and simultaneously contacts \(2n+1\) facets; this is not fixed by known insphere radii or Ehrhart data. Exact eigenvalues, centers, contact patterns, and sharp thresholds are reusable structural invariants. The claim does not purport to solve ellipsoids of arbitrary matroid polytopes.
