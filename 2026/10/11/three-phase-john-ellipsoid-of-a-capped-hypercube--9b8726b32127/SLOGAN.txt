The exact John ellipsoid of \([0,1]^n\cap\{\sum_i x_i\le\tau\}\) has three sharp regimes separated by \(\tau=(n+1)/2\) and \(\tau=(n+\sqrt n)/2\). Between these thresholds its center is the cube center but its two semiaxis eigenvalues are unequal, and every coordinate facet and the rank facet are tangent.
