Every rotated odometer obtained by permuting three equal intervals is pointwise aperiodic and uniquely ergodic, even though a two-interval swap produces an entire fixed interval. Their first returns to the first quarter fall into exactly three laws with height triples \((4,4,4)\), \((2,2,8)\), and \((10,1,1)\), giving variances \(0\), \(8\), and \(18\).
