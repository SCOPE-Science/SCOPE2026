# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Cauchy-Schwarz and Plancherel give S(E)<=sqrt3; equality enforces unimodular coefficient magnitudes and constant dual Fourier modulus. Every nonzero support difference must have at least two ordered representations; with E={0,a,b} the possible patterns reduce to an order-3 or order-4 progression or a Klein-four triple. For Klein-four, flatness requires p+conj(p)=q+conj(q)=p*conj(q)+conj(p)*q=0, which cannot hold since p,q=±i imply p*conj(q)=±1. Explicit coefficients (1,1,omega) for order 3 and (1,e^{-pi i/4},i) for order4 make every nonconstant Fourier coefficient vanish. The finite group checks in the package are only corroboration; the difference argument is valid for every G.

## originality

PASS

Graham general extremality criterion and explicit C3/C4 witnesses are correctly treated as prior art, as is Neuwirth torsion-free three-frequency analysis. The global only-if classification ruling out every other group pattern, particularly Klein-four, is the surviving claim. The complete relevant Graham section was consulted, but possible equivalent general combinatorial characterization elsewhere remains a real originality risk.
