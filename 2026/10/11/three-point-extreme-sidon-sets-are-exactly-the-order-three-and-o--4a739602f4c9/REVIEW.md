# Review

## Correctness
PASS. Equality in the universal Cauchy--Schwarz/Plancherel bound forces equal coefficient moduli and a flat Fourier transform. The difference-pattern case split is exhaustive, explicit witnesses establish the order-three and order-four cases, and the Klein-four phase equations are inconsistent.

## Originality
PASS. Neuwirth treats integer triples on the continuous circle. Graham supplies general extremal-measure machinery but the inspected statements do not give the complete three-point support classification. The closest finite-cyclic theorem treats only consecutive characters. Residual risk remains that older extremal-measure literature contains the same small-support classification implicitly.

## Value
PASS. This is the complete classification of the smallest nontrivial supports attaining the maximal Sidon constant, and it isolates the two torsion geometries responsible for extremality.

## Closest literature and limitations
The theorem uses the standard flatness characterization of extreme measures and a new complete three-point difference-pattern analysis. It does not compute nonextremal three-point constants.
