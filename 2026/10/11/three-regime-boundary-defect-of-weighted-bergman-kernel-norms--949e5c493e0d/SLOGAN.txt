After the leading Forelli--Rudin singularity is removed, weighted Bergman kernel \(L^p\) norms have a sharp boundary-regularity transition at \((p-1)(\alpha+2)=1\): fractional-power below, \(s\log(1/s)\) at the threshold, and linear above. The Hilbert exponent \(p=2\) is the exact zero-defect exception.
