For every centered ellipsoid \(A\mathbb S^{n-1}\) satisfying \(\operatorname{tr}(A^2)=1\), the tight Fuss parallelotopes have exactly the volume interval \([2^n\det A,\,2^n n^{-n/2}]\); principal-axis boxes minimize, while a tight regular cube always exists and maximizes. The polar tight cross polytopes have the reciprocal volume interval, with \(\operatorname{Vol}(P)\operatorname{Vol}(P^\circ)=4^n/n!\).
