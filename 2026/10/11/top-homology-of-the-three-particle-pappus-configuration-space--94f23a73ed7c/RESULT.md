# Top homology of the three-particle Pappus configuration space
## Finding
Let \(P\) be the Pappus graph and let \(B_3(P)\) denote the unordered configuration space of three distinct points on \(P\). Then
\[
H_3(B_3(P);\mathbb Z)\cong \mathbb Z^7.
\]

## Assumptions and scope
The graph \(P\) is the simple cubic graph on vertices \(0,\ldots,17\) obtained from the Hamiltonian cycle by the LCF chord sequence \([5,7,-7,7,-7,-5]^3\). This is the standard Pappus graph; the construction gives \(18\) vertices and \(27\) edges. The space \(B_3(P)\) consists of unordered triples of pairwise distinct points of the topological realization of \(P\).

The computation uses the Świątkowski chain model. At every cubic vertex \(v\), fix one incident half-edge as a reference and use the two reduced generators \(h_1-h_0\) and \(h_2-h_0\). Their boundaries are the corresponding edge differences. The cited graph-configuration-space literature states that the reduced and unreduced Świątkowski complexes have the same homology.

## Proof
In particle weight \(3\), a degree-\(d\) basis vector of the all-vertex-reduced complex consists of \(d\) distinct vertices carrying one of the two half-edge-difference generators, together with an edge monomial of degree \(3-d\). Hence
\[
\operatorname{rank} C_3=\binom{18}{3}2^3=6528,
\qquad
\operatorname{rank} C_2=\binom{18}{2}2^2\cdot27=16524.
\]
There is no degree-four chain group in particle weight \(3\).

The boundary \(\partial_3:C_3\to C_2\) is generated by the graded Leibniz rule and \(\partial(h_i-h_0)=e_i-e_0\). The attached verifier constructs this matrix directly from the LCF graph. Reduction modulo \(3\) has rank \(6521\), so the rational rank is at least \(6521\). The attached certificate contains seven integral vectors in \(\ker \partial_3\); their distinct leading column indices make them linearly independent over \(\mathbb Q\), so the rational nullity is at least \(7\), and therefore the rational rank is at most \(6528-7=6521\). Thus
\[
\operatorname{rank}_\mathbb Q(\partial_3)=6521,
\qquad
\dim_\mathbb Q\ker(\partial_3)=7.
\]
Since \(C_4=0\), one has \(H_3=\ker\partial_3\). This kernel is a subgroup of the free abelian group \(C_3\), hence is itself free abelian, and its rank equals the rational nullity. Therefore \(H_3(B_3(P);\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^7\).

## Relationship to prior work
Maciążek and Sawicki develop graph-configuration-space homology computations using Abrams and Świątkowski models and explicitly solve families including wheel graphs and certain complete bipartite graphs. Their full text describes the reduced Świątkowski complex and states that it has the same homology as the unreduced complex. The same paper does not contain the word “Pappus” and does not list a Pappus-graph configuration-space computation. Exact searches for the Pappus graph together with three-particle configuration homology, graph braid homology, and Świątkowski terminology did not locate a published statement implying the rank-seven top-homology result.

A prior computation of a matching-complex invariant of the Pappus graph concerns a different simplicial complex and does not imply this configuration-space homology. A prior three-particle result for theta graphs concerns a different graph family and likewise does not determine the Pappus case.

## Limitations
The claim is only the integral top homology in particle number \(3\). It does not determine \(H_1\), \(H_2\), the ring structure, braid-group presentations, higher particle numbers, or stabilization. The literature search cannot exclude unpublished or poorly indexed duplicate computations; no such duplicate was found in the checked sources.
