# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The claim reduces to an exact finite boundary-rank calculation in the all-vertex-reduced Świątkowski complex. Particle weight \(3\) gives \(6528\) degree-three generators and no degree-four group. Seven attached integral kernel vectors bound the rational rank above by \(6521\); exact elimination modulo \(3\) gives rank \(6521\), bounding it below. The integral kernel is therefore free of rank \(7\). The verifier reconstructs the matrix and checks the chain condition.

PASS. The Pappus graph is a canonical highly symmetric cubic graph.
