For every square-grid quotient with no nonzero period of Manhattan length below five, its Lovász open-neighborhood complex simplicially collapses onto one or two PL tori, according to whether the period lattice contains an odd-parity translation. This includes all twisted toroidal grids \(\mathbb Z^2/\langle(m,0),(k,n)\rangle\) with \(m,n\geq5\), and supplies a uniform exact collapse beyond the previously treated cyclic special cases.
