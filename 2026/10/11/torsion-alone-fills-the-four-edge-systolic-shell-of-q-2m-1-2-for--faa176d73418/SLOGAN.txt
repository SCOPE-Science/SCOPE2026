For every \(m\ge4\), the Datta--Upadhyay Klein-bottle triangulation \(Q_{2m+1,2}\) has simple integral homology systole \(4\) with exactly \(6m+3\) minimum carriers. Every minimum carrier represents the unique order-two class, and cyclic translation splits them into orbits of sizes \(4m+2\) and \(2m+1\).\n
