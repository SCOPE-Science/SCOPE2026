For a faithfully trace-localized imprimitivity bimodule, the localization norm forces the Parseval module-frame length through \(m_{\mathrm{pf}}\ge\lceil\tau(1)/\|\iota_\tau\|^2\rceil\). On every rectangular matrix Morita module \(M_{r,s}(\mathbb C)\), this lower obstruction is exact and gives \(m_{\mathrm{pf}}=\lceil s/r\rceil\).
