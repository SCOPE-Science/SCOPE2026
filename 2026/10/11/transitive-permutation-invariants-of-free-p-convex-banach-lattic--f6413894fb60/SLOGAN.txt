On \(\operatorname{FBL}^{(p)}[\ell_1^n]\), every transitive coordinate-permutation fixed-point sublattice is isometrically \(C(S_p/G)\): its full norm collapses exactly to \(n^{1/p}\) times the uniform norm on the \(\ell_p\)-unit sphere. For finite \(p\), this produces a norm-one-complemented AM-space inside an ambient free lattice that is not an AM-space.
