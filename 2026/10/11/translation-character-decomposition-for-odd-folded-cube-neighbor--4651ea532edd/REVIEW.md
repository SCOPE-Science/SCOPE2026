# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
For every odd \(n\ge3\), the proof checks the quotient graph and every neighborhood intersection, constructs a dimension-two acyclic matching, establishes simple connectivity using Matsushita’s full-text fundamental-group theorem, and computes reduced degree-two rank by Euler characteristic. Fixed-simplex signed traces and exact Fourier inversion reconstruct every rational translation-character multiplicity. Packaged verification programs were executed with complete face and chain checks at dimensions three, five and seven; finite checks are corroboration, not the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS
The claim is the full rational translation representation, not the known nonequivariant rank. The closest primary foundation, Matsushita’s *Fundamental Groups of Neighborhood Complexes* (arXiv:1210.2803), determines fundamental groups and does not give this representation. Csorba’s 2008 equivariant graph-complex comparison (doi:10.4153/CMB-2008-053-9) does not state the formula in its accessible abstract, but its full text was not available for definitive comparison. Literature and record searches used folded-hypercube, Clebsch, quotient-cube, character and Fourier aliases; no covering published statement was identified. Unindexed priority risk remains.

## Value — PASS
These canonical Cayley graphs have a natural translation action. Distinct weight classes carry different character multiplicities, structural information invisible to their Betti numbers and nonequivariant homotopy types. The proof treats an infinite, mathematically natural family. It does not assert equivariant homotopy equivalence or integral module classification.
