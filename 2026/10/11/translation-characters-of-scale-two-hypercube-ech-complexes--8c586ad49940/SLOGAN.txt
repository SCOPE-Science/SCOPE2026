For every dimension \(n\ge2\), the complete translation representation on the second rational homology of the scale-two intrinsic hypercube Čech complex has multiplicity \(\binom n2\) for the trivial character and \(\binom n2+1-k(n+1-k)\) for each nontrivial character of weight \(k\). In particular its translation-invariant homology has rank \(\binom n2\), and every translation character appears once \(n\ge4\).
