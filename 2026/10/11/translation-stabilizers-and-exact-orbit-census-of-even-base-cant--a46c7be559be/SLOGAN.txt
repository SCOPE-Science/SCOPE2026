For the finite two-digit Cantor set at every even base, the complete spectrum translation stabilizers undergo a sharp modulo-four dichotomy: all spectra are aperiodic when the base is divisible by four, whereas for bases congruent to two modulo four their entire power-of-two stabilizer distribution has an exact formula (and every order occurs if the base exceeds two). Explicit fixed-subgroup formulas determine the exact number of spectra and of their frequency-translation orbits at every finite level.
