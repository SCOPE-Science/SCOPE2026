Every standard-weight Loday operahedron on \(n\) internal tree vertices has the same exact Euclidean inradius \(\frac12\sqrt{(n-1)(n-2)}\), with unique uniform incenter. More strongly, a facet indexed by an \(s\)-edge nest always lies at distance \(\frac12\sqrt{(n-1)s(n-1-s)}\), so the entire centered facet-shell spectrum is independent of the tree.
