All tree Laplacian simplices on \(n\) vertices are lattice-unimodularly equivalent, yet in their canonical Euclidean embedding the inradii range across the sharp scales \(n^{-5/2}\) and \(n^{-1}\). The path has an exact quartic pseudoinverse-column profile with \(n^{5/2}\rho\to10.17084684\ldots\), whereas the star has \(n\rho\to1\); universal bounds prove that neither exponent can be improved for all trees.
