For every finite simple \(K_5\)-minor-free graph with at least one edge, the unique maximum-volume inscribed ellipsoid of its cut polytope is centered at the half-vector and has diagonal squared semiaxes determined by a strictly concave optimization using **only triangle incidence**. All longer induced-cycle inequalities are redundant for computing the ellipsoid; the theorem also identifies exactly which triangle, coordinate and four-cycle facets it touches, with explicit nonuniform formulas for wheels and triangle books. For triangle-free graphs in this class the ellipsoid is exactly the radius-\(1/2\) Euclidean ball.
