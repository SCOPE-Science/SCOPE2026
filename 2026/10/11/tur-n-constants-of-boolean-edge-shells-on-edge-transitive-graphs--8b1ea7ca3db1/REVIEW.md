# Review

## Correctness — PASS
The Fourier/cut proof was reconstructed from the definitions. The critical steps are the realness of coefficients on an exponent-two group, character parametrization by sign vectors, conversion of Fourier positivity to cut inequalities, automorphism averaging with incidence fraction \(M/m\), and the explicit uniform extremizer. The denominator is positive because every nonempty graph has a cut larger than \(m/2\). Odd-cycle uniqueness follows from tightness for every complement-of-one-edge maximum cut.

## Originality — PASS
The closest harmonic source gives the general positive-definite Turán framework, while the closest graph source gives fractional cut-cover/cubical coloring and its edge-transitive formula. Their inspected statements do not give the Boolean-edge-shell identity. Exact odd-cycle cubical-chromatic values are prior and are not claimed as new; the harmonic bridge and normalized extremizer rigidity are the assessed contribution. Residual risk remains for older literature using Cayley-kernel or cut-polytope terminology.

## Value — PASS
The result links two natural invariants across harmonic analysis and graph cut theory for an infinite class, transfers exact cut information to Turán extremals, and gives a reusable proof mechanism plus a nontrivial equality classification for odd cycles.
