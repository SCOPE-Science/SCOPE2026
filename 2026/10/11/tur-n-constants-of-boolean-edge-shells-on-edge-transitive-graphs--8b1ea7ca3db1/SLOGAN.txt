For every finite nonempty edge-transitive graph \(H\), the positive-definite Turán extremum on the Boolean shell formed by its edge vectors equals its cubical chromatic number: \(\tau(H)=2\operatorname{MAXCUT}(H)/(2\operatorname{MAXCUT}(H)-|E(H)|)=\chi_q(H)\). For odd cycles \(C_{{2k+1}}\), the normalized extremizer is unique and has every edge coefficient \(1/(2k-1)\).
