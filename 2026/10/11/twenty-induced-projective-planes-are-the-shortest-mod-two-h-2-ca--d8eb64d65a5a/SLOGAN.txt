In Walkup's eleven-vertex \(\mathbb{RP}^3\), the nonzero mod-two \(H_2\)-class first appears on six vertices, and it has exactly \(20\) minimum carriers. Every minimum carrier is an induced six-vertex \(\mathbb{RP}^2\), and the full simplicial symmetry group splits them into orbits of sizes \(12\) and \(8\).
