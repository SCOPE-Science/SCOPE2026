The sharp anisotropic finite-field paraboloid extension deficit has exactly two positive quadratic curvature bands: \(2-2/q\) for mean-zero phase perturbations and \(2-2/q+4/q^2\) for mean-zero amplitude perturbations. The only zero modes are complex scalar changes, and the lower band is the exact transverse local stability constant for every \(q\equiv3\pmod4\).
