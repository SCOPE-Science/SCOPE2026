A single adjacent sign exchange in a two-block orientation of an even cycle causes an exact Euclidean minimum-sphere contact transition: all six vertices touch for \(k=3\), but for every \(k\ge4\) precisely the two exchanged-edge vertices are strictly interior. The exact squared radius is \(2-3(2k-1)/[(k-1)k(k^2-k+1)]\) for \(k\ge4\), with rational contact weights and a strict, explicit clearance for both screened vertices.
