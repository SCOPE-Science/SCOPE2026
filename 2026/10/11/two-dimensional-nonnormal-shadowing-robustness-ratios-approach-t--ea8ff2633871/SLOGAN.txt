A diagonalizable but nonnormal complex \(2\times2\) hyperbolic matrix can have shadowability times distance-to-nonhyperbolicity strictly greater than the sharp normal-operator bound \(\sqrt2\). For the explicit upper-triangular family \(A_{\varepsilon,K}\), this product converges to \(2-\varepsilon\) as \(K\to\infty\), yielding ratios arbitrarily close to \(2\) without asserting any universal upper bound.
