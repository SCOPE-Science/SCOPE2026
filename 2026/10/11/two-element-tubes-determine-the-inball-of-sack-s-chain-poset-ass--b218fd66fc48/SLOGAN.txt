In Sack's explicit associahedron realization of an \(n\)-element chain, the exact inball is controlled only by the \(n-1\) two-element tubes. Its radius is \(\bigl(n^{2n}-(n-1)n^4\bigr)/\bigl(\sqrt6+(n-3)\sqrt2\bigr)\), and the unique incenter has adjacent gaps \(n^4+r_n\sqrt{3/2}\) at the two ends and \(n^4+r_n\sqrt2\) in the interior.
