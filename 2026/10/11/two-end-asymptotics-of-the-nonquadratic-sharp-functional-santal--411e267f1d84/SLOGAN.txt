The new nonquadratic sharp functional Santaló extremizer is conical at both ends with reciprocal exact slopes: \(c_{0,n}=k_n^{1/n}/\sqrt2\) at the origin and \(c_{\infty,n}=\sqrt2/k_n^{1/n}\) at infinity. Its first nonlinear corrections have powers \(n+1\) and \(1/(n+1)\), forcing \(a_n(1+a_n)^n<2^{n-2}\) and fixing the zero-ball and domain radii of its Legendre dual.
