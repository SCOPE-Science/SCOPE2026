For the first two harmonic degrees of every finite \(q\)-Grassmannian, restriction to a fixed-point Schubert star has exactly two nonzero singular values: \(1\) and \(\sqrt{[k-1]_q/[n-2]_q}\), with explicitly determined multiplicities. Its rank is \([n-1]_q\), its nullity is \(q^{n-1}\) independent of the subspace rank, and it gives the exact optimal bandlimited interpolation cost.
