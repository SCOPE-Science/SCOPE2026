Every two-lineup polytope of \(\Delta_{d-1}\times\Delta_{e-1}\) is optimally cospherical: its unique minimum enclosing sphere has squared radius \(1+\alpha^2+\beta^2-1/d-1/e\), and every lineup vertex is in contact. A cyclic family of only \(de\) row-sharing lineups already has the sphere center as its equal-weight barycenter.
