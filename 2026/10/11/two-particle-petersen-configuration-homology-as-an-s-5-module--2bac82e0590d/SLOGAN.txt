For the Petersen graph \(P\), the unordered two-particle configuration space has \(H_1\cong\mathbb Z^6\oplus\mathbb Z/2\) and \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z^5\). Under \(\operatorname{Aut}(P)\cong S_5\), its rational homology is the irreducible pair \(S^{(3,1,1)}\) in degree one and \(S^{(2,2,1)}\) in degree two.\n
