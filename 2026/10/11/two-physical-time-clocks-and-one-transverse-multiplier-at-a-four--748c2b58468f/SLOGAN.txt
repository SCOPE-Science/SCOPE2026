In the four-oval chemical oscillator of Craciun and Erban, all four attracting cycles share one exact transverse Floquet multiplier, although their original physical-time periods split into unequal pairs. As the four ovals approach their nodal merger, these periods diverge with logarithmic coefficients in the exact ratio \(13/7\), while the common multiplier converges to \(\exp(-I_c/\varepsilon)\) with explicit \(I_c>0\).
