# Review of the exact primitive-period-two census

## Correctness — PASS
The period-two reduction forces every two-cycle onto the horizontal axis and then to a two-cycle of the one-dimensional rational function. The sign obstruction rigorously excludes all parameters \(\mu\ge-1\), with the degenerate \(\mu=1\) case separately computed. For \(\mu=-1-t\) and every \(t>0\), the exact polynomial \(Q_t\) has at least two negative roots by explicit opposite endpoint signs; Descartes’ rule bounds their number, with multiplicity, by two. Every such root produces one and only one prime two-cycle. The small- and large-coordinate scalings are expansions about simple roots. Symbolic replay passed all stated polynomial and asymptotic identities.

## Originality — PASS
The primary published Gumowski–Mira recurrence contains exactly the same nonlinearity but discusses a nonzero dissipative term and different parameter ranges, without the present global area-preserving census. Numerical later studies use other parameters. Named integrable Gumovski–Mira/KAM analyses address distinct rational recurrence families (and the published quartic-integrable numerator-degree restriction cannot subsume the present cubic-numerator function for \(\mu<-1\)). Searches of mathematical research records and the accessible primary works showed no statement implying the all-parameter count together with the two threshold scalings. Residual uncertainty concerns earlier historical full texts that were not accessible; unexamined sources cannot be ruled out.

## Value — PASS
The conservative map is a named natural planar dynamical system, and primitive period-two counts are basic bifurcation and resonance data. The exact sharp onset produces two cycles with sharply different geometries: one collapses locally at square-root scale, while the second diverges to infinity at reciprocal fourth-root scale. A global finite-but-complete orbit census and nonlocal branch asymptotic are not a standard normalization check or a recomputation of a supplied table; they provide exact landmarks for bifurcation study of this model.
