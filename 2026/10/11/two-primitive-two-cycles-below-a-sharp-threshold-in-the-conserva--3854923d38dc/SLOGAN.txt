The conservative Gumowski–Mira plane map has exactly two primitive period-two orbits when \(\mu<-1\), and none for \(\mu\ge-1\). As \(\mu\uparrow-1\), one collapses onto the origin at square-root scale while the other escapes to infinity at fourth-root reciprocal scale; the global count follows from a sharp sign-and-Descartes argument.
