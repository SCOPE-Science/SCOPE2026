For the coefficient-series Landau radii of \(\mathcal W_{\mathcal H}^{0}(\alpha)\), increasing the differential parameter improves both the guaranteed univalence disk and the guaranteed schlicht disk strictly. Their large-\(\alpha\) stabilization is two-scale: \(1-\rho_\alpha\sim e^{-\pi\alpha/(8M)}\), while \(R_\alpha=\pi/(4M)-2/\alpha+O(\alpha^{-2})\).
