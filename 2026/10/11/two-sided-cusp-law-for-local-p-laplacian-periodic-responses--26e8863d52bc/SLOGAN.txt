For the recent vector \(p\)-Laplacian Duffing averaging problem with \(p>2\), every local periodic response has the same two-sided leading law \(u_\varepsilon-\alpha\sim\operatorname{sgn}(\varepsilon)|\varepsilon|^{1/(p-1)}\mathcal K(e-\bar e)\). If the forcing is not constant, this exact fractional-power displacement forces an infinite parameter difference quotient, so no local solution selection can be differentiable through \(\varepsilon=0\).
