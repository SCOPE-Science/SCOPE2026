For the residual exceptional pretzel knots \(P_{2m-1}\) with odd \(m\ge3\), the Alexander polynomial has exactly two negative real roots, a simple reciprocal pair \(-e^{\pm x_m}\). Their logarithmic displacement satisfies \(x_m=\sqrt3/(2m^2)+9\sqrt3/(40m^4)+O(m^{-6})\), so the pair collapses sharply onto \(-1\).\n
