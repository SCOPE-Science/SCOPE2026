# Review of: Two symmetry orbits exhaust the mod-two one-systoles of the 15-vertex quaternionic space

## Correctness
PASS. The exact labeled 75-facet complex is reconstructed. The verifier obtains boundary ranks \(14\) and \(74\), so \(H_1(K;\mathbf F_2)\cong\mathbf F_2^2\). It exhausts all \(455\) vertex triples and finds exactly \(120\) missing triangles, all essential, split \(40+40+40\) among the three nonzero classes. Because every simple cycle has at least three edges and every three-edge cycle is a triangle boundary, this proves the systolic statement. The published symmetry generators produce a \(180\)-element facet-preserving group and the exact \(90+30\) orbit split with class balances \(30+30+30\) and \(10+10+10\).

## Originality
PASS. The closest exact sources give the 15-vertex model, \(S^3/Q=P(2)\), the face vector, \(H_1\), the full group label \(\mathrm{GL}(2,4)\), tetrahedral orbit data, and explicit group generators. None of the inspected full-text statements gives the complete missing-triangle homology classification or its two symmetry orbits. published-finding corpus searches and overlap-only checks of the available owned ledgers found no implying claim. The 1986 construction article was readable only at abstract/bibliographic level, so its inaccessible full text remains a stated residual risk rather than being certified as noncovering.

## Value
PASS. This is a complete shortest-cycle classification for a natural highly symmetric triangulation of a classical spherical \(3\)-manifold. It identifies every simple mod-two \(1\)-systole, resolves all three nonzero homology classes, and relates the systoles to the full combinatorial symmetry in a way not forced by the known homology group alone.
