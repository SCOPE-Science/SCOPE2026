In the 15-vertex vertex-transitive triangulation of \(S^3/Q\), all \(120\) missing triangles are exactly the shortest nonzero mod-two \(1\)-cycles, with \(40\) representatives for each nonzero class of \(H_1\). Under \(\mathrm{GL}(2,4)\), they form two symmetry orbits of sizes \(90\) and \(30\), each balanced across the three nonzero homology classes.
