For the cubic global-center family \(\dot x=v\), \(\dot v=-x(1-\alpha v+\kappa v^2)\) with \(4\kappa>\alpha^2\), the physical period has an explicit two-term inverse-amplitude law and the positive-to-negative peak-speed ratio tends to \(\exp(-\pi\alpha/\sqrt{4\kappa-\alpha^2})\). Combined with an already known local period coefficient, the global law forces an interior period maximum throughout \(3\kappa<\alpha^2<4\kappa\).
