For ellipsoidal open billiards with physical flattening \(\beta\), the top per-collision Lyapunov exponent grows as \(2\log\beta+C_0+\beta^{-2}C_1+O(\beta^{-4})\), with explicit Gibbs-measure averages \(C_0\) and \(C_1\) and a uniform error bound. The leading factor two comes from the exact equatorial normal curvature \(\beta^2/r\).
