# Same-model review

## Correctness
**PASS.** Proposition 5.1 of arXiv:2610.04382v1 simultaneously realizes the coordinates \(c,c^2,\ldots,c^m\) for one homeomorphism with trivial loop braid, where \(c=[x_1,x_2]\). These coordinates all abelianize to zero. With trivial loop braid, Theorem 4.6 reduces Nielsen equivalence to ordinary conjugacy in \(F_n\); the cyclically reduced lengths \(4,8,\ldots,4m\) are distinct, so the coordinates are pairwise nonconjugate. The proof is symbolic for arbitrary \(m\), not an extrapolation from finite testing.

## Originality
**PASS.** The primary paper's Remark 5.2 and Example 5.3 show only a two-class collision of linking data. Its full text was checked for unbounded/arbitrary-fiber formulations and none was found. The March 2026 predecessor explains the abelianized linking data but does not supply the October paper's simultaneous realization theorem. Claim-level literature searches found no covering statement. The closest published result is therefore the source's own two-class example, which does not imply any finite-fiber bound is impossible without the additional construction.

## Value
**PASS.** The result identifies a sharp limitation of the new linking-data framework: failure of completeness is unbounded, not merely exceptional. For every requested finite multiplicity, one homeomorphism in the trivial-loop-braid sector realizes that many distinct Nielsen classes in the zero-linking fiber. This is a motivated structural boundary on what abelianized linking information can recover.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is arXiv:2610.04382v1, especially Proposition 5.1, Remark 5.2, and Example 5.3. arXiv:2603.06233v2 supplies the generalized-Lefschetz/linking interpretation. The result does not assert that the displayed classes are essential, does not bound the Nielsen number, and does not claim one homeomorphism has infinitely many distinct Nielsen classes.
