# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The definition and Pythagorean-orthogonality exclusion were reconstructed from the 2023 primary source. For the explicit family, every norm support used in the proof is verified on the full interval \(0<t<12/35\). The numerator and denominator factor exactly, the denominator is nonzero on every admissible member, and the quotient diverges to \(+\infty\) from positive values. No finite experiment is used to infer the infinite conclusion.

## Originality
PASS. The primary source treats the identical regular-octagonal norm but states only a lower bound for \(P(X_8)\). Searches using the DOI, definition, octagonal norm, Pythagorean orthogonality, denominator singularity, and the explicit parameter \(12/35\) did not locate the unboundedness statement. Closest indexed same-object results concern different geometric constants and do not imply this quotient blow-up.

## Value
PASS. This is not a routine recomputation of a known table entry: it changes the qualitative status of a recently introduced geometric quantity on the exact benchmark space used by its defining paper. Showing that the defining supremum can fail to be finite in a natural two-dimensional uniformly non-Euclidean setting is directly relevant to how the quantity can be used in subsequent geometric estimates and motivates a finite or regularized variant.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is Zhou--Ni--Liu--Li (2023), which defines \(P(X)\) and gives the same octagonal space as an example but only a lower estimate. A 2024 paper by overlapping authors introduces the distinct skew constant and does not cover this statement. The result does not classify all spaces where \(P(X)\) is finite, and obscure unindexed prior observations remain a bibliographic risk.
