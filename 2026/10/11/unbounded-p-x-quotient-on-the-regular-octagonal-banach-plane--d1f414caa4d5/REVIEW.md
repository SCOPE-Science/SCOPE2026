## correctness

PASS

For octagonal N(s,t)=max(|s|,|t|,(|s|+|t|)/sqrt 2), take x=(1,sqrt2−1), y_t=(−t/6,t), 0<t<12/35. Exact facet inequalities establish N(x)=1, N(y_t)=t, N(x−y_t)=1+t/6, N(x+y_t)=1+5t/(6sqrt2) throughout. The denominator is t(35t−12)/36<0 and numerator t(47t−60sqrt2)/72<0; their quotient (47t−60sqrt2)/(2(35t−12))→+infinity as t increases to 12/35 from below. Every prelimit pair is allowed by nonorthogonality. The boundary t=12/35 has zero denominator and is excluded. The symbolic factors and limit were independently confirmed with SymPy; no finite sampling is used for the proof.
