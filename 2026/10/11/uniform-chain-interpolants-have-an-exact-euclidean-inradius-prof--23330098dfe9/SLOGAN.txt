For the canonical uniform interpolation between the marked order and chain simplices on an \(m\)-element chain, the Euclidean inradius is exactly the reciprocal of an explicit sum of \(m+1\) transfer-normal lengths. Every \(m\ge2\) has a strictly better interior inradius than either endpoint; for \(m=2\) the unique optimum is \(t=1/2\) with radius \(1/(1+\sqrt5)\).
