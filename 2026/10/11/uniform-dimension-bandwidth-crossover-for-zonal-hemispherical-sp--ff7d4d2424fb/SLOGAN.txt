For zonal hemispherical concentration with Gegenbauer weight \((1-x^2)^\beta\), the product of all centered spectral gaps has an exact gamma-product formula and a sharp asymptotic, uniform even when the weight parameter grows with the bandwidth. Its leading coefficient interpolates between \(\sqrt\pi G(1/2)^2\) and \(2^{1/4}\sqrt\pi G(1/2)^2\), exhibiting an exact dimension-to-bandwidth crossover and noncommuting limits.
