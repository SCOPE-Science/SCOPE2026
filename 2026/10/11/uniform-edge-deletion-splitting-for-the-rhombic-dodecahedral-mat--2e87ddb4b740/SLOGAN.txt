For the rhombic dodecahedral graph \(R\), the ordinary matching complex is \(\bigvee^{39}S^4\). Every one of its 24 graph edges \(e=\{u,v\}\) yields a uniform homotopy splitting \(\mathcal M(R)\simeq\mathcal M(R-e)\vee\Sigma\mathcal M(R-\{u,v\})\), whose two summands are wedges of 26 and 13 four-spheres.
