# Review

## Correctness
PASS. Two exact diagonal examples separate the two missing uniformity assumptions. Coordinate operators give a Parseval outer weave while local vectors \(1/n\) produce the non-frame family \(e_n/n\). Conversely, outer operators scaled by \(1/n\) fail the lower \(g\)-frame bound while local vectors \(n\) produce the Parseval family \(e_n\).

With uniform local bounds
\[
0<a\le b<\infty,
\]
the local frame inequalities integrate to
\[
aE_g(f)\le E_{\mathrm{vec}}(f)\le bE_g(f),
\]
which yields the stated bound transfers in both directions.

## Originality
PASS. The exact 2020 theorem, its proof, the later 2023 Hilbert \(C^*\)-module extension, an earlier analogous continuous fusion-frame construction, and source-specific correction searches were compared. No checked indexed source supplied the two counterexamples or the repaired quantitative equivalence.

The later extension repeats the same infimum/supremum step and therefore does not cover the correction.

## Value
PASS. The equivalence is intended to transfer weaving between coefficient-space operators and ordinary vectors. The failure occurs even for coordinate Parseval systems, and either a vanishing local lower bound or an unbounded local upper bound can independently destroy one direction. Uniform local conditioning gives a practical exact repair of the proof.

## Limitations
The repair is a uniform sufficient condition and is separately necessary for an unrestricted theorem of this form. It is not a classification of every nonuniform local-frame field for which accidental compensation may preserve the equivalence.
