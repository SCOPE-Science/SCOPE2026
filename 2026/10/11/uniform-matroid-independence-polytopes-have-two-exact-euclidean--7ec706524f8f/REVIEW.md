# Same-model review

## Correctness
PASS. The inradius proof uses only genuine facets and begins with global upper bounds valid for an arbitrary center. Lower coordinate facets plus the rank facet give \(r/(n+\sqrt n)\); opposite coordinate facets cap the radius at \(1/2\). The displayed centers attain the appropriate bound, and equality conditions force uniqueness. For the minimum-enclosing ball, uniqueness and permutation symmetry reduce the center to \(t\mathbf1\). The exact squared distance to a cardinality-\(k\) vertex is affine in \(k\), so the farthest layer and the two outer regimes follow without approximation.

## Value
PASS. Uniform-matroid independence polytopes are canonical full-dimensional matroid polytopes and a central application in the source literature. The result provides a complete Euclidean ball profile and identifies two genuinely different phase transitions: the outer sphere becomes cube-centered at half rank, whereas the inball reaches the cube radius only at the later threshold \(2r=n+\sqrt n\). The contact sets explain the mechanisms behind both transitions.
