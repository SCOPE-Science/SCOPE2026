For every \(a>0\), the positive-height equilibrium of Sprott's quadratic S flow undergoes a nondegenerate **supercritical** Hopf bifurcation at exactly \(b=1/4\), with first Lyapunov coefficient \(-\sqrt a(8a+1)/((a+1)(4a+1))\). Orbitally stable small cycles are rigorously born for \(b>1/4\), and their periods tend to \(2\pi/\sqrt a\).
