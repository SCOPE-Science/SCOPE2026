For the discontinuous scalar affine return family of arXiv:2607.13519, every \(a\) with \(|a+1/3|\leq10^{-6}\) admits a thirteen-step invariant Cantor repeller conjugate to the full two-symbol shift. An exact-rational two-branch covering certificate proves the explicit bound \(h_{\mathrm{top}}\geq(\log 2)/13\) on a compact continuous invariant subsystem, without asserting a lift to the original planar map.
