Among all calibrated positive three-direction estimates of a three-dimensional convex body's surface area from ordinary planar projection areas, the unique minimax scheme uses three orthogonal directions with equal weights and has worst relative error \(1/3\). Every rule with excess error at most \(\varepsilon\) has weights within \(O(\varepsilon)\) and pairwise direction cosines within \(O(\sqrt\varepsilon)\) of this optimum, and both stability orders are sharp.
