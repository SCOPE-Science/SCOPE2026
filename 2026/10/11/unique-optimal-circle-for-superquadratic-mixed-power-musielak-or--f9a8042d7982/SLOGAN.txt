For every \(2<p<q<\infty\), the Euclidean circle is the unique Banach--Mazur optimal ellipse, up to scale, for the mixed-power Musielak--Orlicz plane with unit ball \(\{(x,y):|x|^p+|y|^q\le1\}\). Its squared distance to \(\ell_2^2\) is \(\max_{0\le t\le1}[t^{2/p}+(1-t)^{2/q}]\), attained at the unique interior solution of \(\frac{2}{p}t^{2/p-1}=\frac{2}{q}(1-t)^{2/q-1}\).
