Every uniquely quasi norm-attaining operator \(c_0(\Gamma)\to Y\) is compact, for any index set \(\Gamma\) and any real or complex Banach space \(Y\). Thus \(\overline{\operatorname{UQNA}(c_0(\Gamma),Y)}=\mathcal K(c_0(\Gamma),Y)\): distance to this class equals essential norm, and the identity on infinite-dimensional \(c_0(\Gamma)\) has exact distance \(1\).
