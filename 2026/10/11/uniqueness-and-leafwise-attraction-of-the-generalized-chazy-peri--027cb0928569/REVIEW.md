# Same-model scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof constructs the exact negative-energy cylinder, derives its strict coarea area contraction, and uses Stokes on disks/annuli to exclude additional periodic orbits. An exact Liouville identity produces the transverse multiplier; energy scaling preserves its logarithmic integral. Poincaré–Bendixson establishes convergence precisely for bounded positive semiorbits. The symbolic program is supplementary: no extrapolation from finite computations is used to establish the general theorem. Prior published existence is explicitly distinguished from the new uniqueness proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk
The 2023 Llibre–Novaes–Valls article's Theorem 1, Corollary 1, proof and appendix were checked against the stronger leafwise statement. They establish existence through trapping/root counts and do not visibly classify all cycles or compute the energy-independent Floquet invariant. The later 2024 Zhuang–Liu–Luo abstract establishes broader existence, not a corresponding uniqueness claim. Its full text remained inaccessible and might contain a covering argument; this and incomplete mathematical indexing remain genuine risks. Searches of published research records and previous claims identified no identical Chazy theorem, which is not a proof of publication priority.

## Value — PASS
For a studied third-order system with a noncompact cylinder of periodic orbits, uniqueness, attraction of every bounded leaf trajectory, and an invariant transverse multiplier determine qualitative behavior which an existence result alone leaves open. The result is a natural global phase-portrait strengthening, not a small numerical refinement.

## Limitations
The new proof does not show all positive semiorbits bounded; stability is only within a fixed energy surface, and no numerical value of the multiplier is asserted. Existence for the cited finite range rests on prior published theorems, whose numerical certificate was not independently reproduced. The inaccessible closely related 2024 full text leaves nonzero novelty risk.

Same-model review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
