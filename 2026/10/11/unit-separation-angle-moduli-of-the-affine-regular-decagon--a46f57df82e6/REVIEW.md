PASS. Dihedral symmetry reduces the first vector to one edge, and the translated-boundary equation gives three exact branches plus exchanged companions. Their affine formulas yield the unique lower endpoint \((9-\sqrt5)/4\) and upper endpoint \(4-\sqrt5\), both attained explicitly. The two angle-modulus values then follow exactly.

## Originality
PASS. The defining 2022 full text contains the two moduli and their general theory but no affine-regular-decagon computation. Searches covered exact notation, decagon aliases, the accepted radicals, and equivalent chord-extremum formulations. The nearest exact record is the octagonal unit-separation smoothness value; own angle-modulus findings concern other polygons.

## Value
PASS. The structural result is the complete sharp interval of sum-chord lengths at unit separation, which resolves both angle moduli at once and identifies distinct equality configurations on a canonical non-Hilbert polygon.
