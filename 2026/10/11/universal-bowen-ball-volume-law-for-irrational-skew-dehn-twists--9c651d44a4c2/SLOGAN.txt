For every irrational-base \(C^1\) skew Dehn twist of nonzero winding \(k\), the normalized Haar area of an \(n\)-step Bowen ball satisfies \(n\lambda(B_n(z,\varepsilon))\to 4\varepsilon^2/|k|\), uniformly over all centers and independently of the periodic shear perturbation. The associated rescaled orbit-distance balls converge to a universal parallelogram, giving polynomial entropy exactly \(1\).
