In the mass-conserving Wilhelm–Heinrich chemical oscillator, **every positive supercritical trajectory has the same time-averaged concentration vector as its unique equilibrium**, even when stable and unstable limit cycles coexist; the average error is \(O(T^{-1})\) for each initial state. Exactly at the total-mass threshold \(C=r\), every positive trajectory instead approaches extinction with a universal \(t^{-1}\) concentration profile and an exact logarithmic cumulative-production correction.
