# Scientific review: universal escape asymptotics for the even-degree secant model

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof handles all real initial points and both signs of \(a\): a monotone-coordinate dichotomy rules out bounded nonconvergence; exact difference and sum recurrences force a projective limit for escaping orbits; and a convergent weighted logarithmic telescoping series yields a strictly positive height and exact universal prefactor. The included integer checker tests recurrence identities and examples, but the analytic argument—not finite iterations—covers every even degree.

**Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk.** Fontich, Garijo, and Jarque (arXiv:2405.08791v1) are credited for introducing the model, the even-degree compact basin and stable-manifold boundary, and coordinate monotonicity. Those results do not state the all-exterior quantitative escape ray, positive normalized height, or exact multiplicative prefactor. The same authors' arXiv:2405.08812v1 deals with odd degrees and homoclinic dynamics. Broader polynomial escape theory could imply related conclusions, and equivalently targeted work may remain unidentified; literature priority is not independently certified.

**Value — PASS.** The sharp exterior escape classification complements the previously studied interior basin of an explicitly motivated, two-dimensional nonhyperbolic polynomial model. The universal ray, double-exponential growth law, and orbit-dependent height distinguish directional collapse from loss of initial-state information; these are natural global dynamical invariants that are not supplied by a topological basin description alone.

**Limitations.** Even degree and the polynomial model only. No statement for the original secant algorithm, odd degrees, or an elementary seed formula for the escape height. Finite checker tests are illustrative, not an exhaustive proof.
