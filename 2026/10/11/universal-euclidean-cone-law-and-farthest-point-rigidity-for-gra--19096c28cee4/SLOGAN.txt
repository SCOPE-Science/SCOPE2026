For every finite simple graph \(H\) with \(m\ge2\) vertices, adjoining one universal vertex increases the **squared Euclidean diameter** of its graphical zonotope by exactly \(m(m+1)\), regardless of the original edges. A complete-cut equality criterion identifies every farthest vertex, and for bipartite \(H\) gives exactly \(2^{c+1}\) farthest vertices, with two additional contacts precisely when \(H\) itself is complete bipartite.
