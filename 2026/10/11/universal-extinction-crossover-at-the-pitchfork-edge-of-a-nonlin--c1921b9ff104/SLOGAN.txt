At the disease-free pitchfork edge of the nonlinear SIRS model, infection decays as \(t^{-1/2}\), whereas immediately on the forward side it decays as \(t^{-1}\); both rates have explicit coefficients valid for every strictly positive state on the invariant population simplex. The entire singular transition has the parameter-independent physical-time profile \(1/Y-\log(1+1/Y)=\tau\) on the exact scale \(t\asymp C_0/\varepsilon^2\).
