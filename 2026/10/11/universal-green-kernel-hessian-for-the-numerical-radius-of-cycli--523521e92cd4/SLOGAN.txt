The spatial numerical radius of a positive weighted cyclic shift on real or complex \(\ell_p^n\) has an exact, universal second-order perturbation law governed by the cycle Laplacian’s Green operator. The resulting Hessian detects precisely one exceptional flat mode at \(p=2\) in even dimension, and predicts that clustering two raised weights on a four-cycle is locally more expansive than alternating them for every \(1<p<\infty\).
