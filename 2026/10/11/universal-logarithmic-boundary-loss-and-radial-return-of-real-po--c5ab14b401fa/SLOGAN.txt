For every fixed finite \(p\ge1\), the sharp real mean-zero Poisson \(L^\infty\)-to-\(L^p\) norm has a universal logarithmic boundary loss: \(N_p(r)/r=1-(4/\pi^2)(1-r)\log(1/(1-r))+O_p(1-r)\). Even at exponents where Schwarz contractivity fails near the origin, the sharp norm becomes strictly smaller than \(r\) sufficiently close to the boundary.
