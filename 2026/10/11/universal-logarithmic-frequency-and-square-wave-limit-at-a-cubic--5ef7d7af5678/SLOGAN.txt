Every global cubic center \(\dot x=y\), \(\dot y=-x+(a-\kappa x)y^2\) with \(\kappa>0\) has high-energy minimal period \(T(E)\sim2\pi/\sqrt{\log E}\). After scaling, its canonical position approaches a cosine while its original physical-time position approaches a square wave, spending asymptotically half the period at each normalized extremum.
