For the classical Brusselator, holding the feed parameter \(A>0\) fixed and crossing the Hopf threshold \(B=1+A^2\) always slows the newly emerging stable oscillation: its period has strictly positive first derivative with respect to \(B\). The exact coefficient is \((2\pi/A)(4A^4-7A^2+4)/(6A^2(A^2+2))\), positive for every \(A>0\).
