For every \(n\ge3\) and \(m\ge2\), equally spaced rotations of \(m\) concentric congruent regular \(n\)-gons maximize the proportion of every concentric circle covered by their union, and hence simultaneously maximize every positive radial mass, with uniqueness up to a common rotation. The exact maximum union area is \(nmR^2\cos^2(\pi/n)[\tan(\pi/n)-\tan((1-1/m)\pi/n)]\), and its deficit from the circumdisk is asymptotic to \(\pi^2R^2\tan(\pi/n)/(nm)\).
