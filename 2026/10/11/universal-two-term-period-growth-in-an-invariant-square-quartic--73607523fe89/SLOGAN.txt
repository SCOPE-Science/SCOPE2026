For a natural two-parameter quartic center with an invariant square, every noncentral point inside the square is periodic, and its original-time period obeys \(T(E)=4E+2[J(p)+J(-p)+J(q)+J(-q)]+o(1)\) as the normalized energy diverges. The leading period slope \(4\) is independent of the parameters, while the full finite offset is explicit.
