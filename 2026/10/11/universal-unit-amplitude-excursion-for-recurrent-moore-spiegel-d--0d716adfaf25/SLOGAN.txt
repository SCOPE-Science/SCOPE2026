Every nontrivial compact stationary state of the Moore–Spiegel oscillator with \(T,R>0\) satisfies \(\int x^2y^2\,d\mu>\int y^2\,d\mu\), forcing positive stationary mass beyond \(|x|=1\). The unit barrier is parameter-independent and strictly strengthens the previously available \(|x|>\sqrt{1-T/R}\) excursion bound.
