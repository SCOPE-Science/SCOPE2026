Every compact positive radial spectrum and every countable prescribed set of radial eigenvalues containing its isolated points can be realized simultaneously by one positive self-adjoint operator, giving a complete catalogue of the scalar asymptotic-expansivity, strong asymptotic-expansivity and hyperbolicity failure sets. On the Cantor spectrum, strong asymptotic expansivity and its failure both occur densely at nonhyperbolic radii even though every scalar multiple is asymptotically expansive.
