# Review of Unrestricted quadratic-graph Dirichlet continuity beyond the half-curvature threshold

## Correctness

PASS. The source's deformation preserves
\[
F_{\varepsilon,a}
=
(z_2-\varepsilon z_1^2)^2-a^2
\]
exactly. Its graph criterion reduces the singular-boundary check to the normal quantity
\[
N_\varepsilon
=
|\xi_1|^2+
\operatorname{Re}
\left(
2\varepsilon\xi_1^2\overline{\xi_2}
\right).
\]
On either graph and on the unit sphere, exact substitution gives
\[
N_\varepsilon
=
1-a^2+
|\varepsilon|^2|\xi_1|^4
>
0
\]
for every complex \(\varepsilon\). The source theorem therefore applies without a curvature restriction.

The separate sphere-wide minimization is exact:
\[
\min N_\varepsilon
=
x\left(
1-2|\varepsilon|\sqrt{1-x}
\right).
\]
It proves that
\[
|\varepsilon|=1/2
\]
is exactly the global outwardness threshold, while for larger parameters the first variation has both signs and the deformed domains are nonnested.

## Originality

PASS. The primary paper states and proves its quadratic-graph example only under
\[
|\varepsilon|<1/2,
\]
using a uniform sphere estimate. It does not substitute the graph and sphere equations to obtain the unrestricted positive expression on the singular set, nor does it identify \(1/2\) as the sharp global-outwardness rather than continuity threshold.

Published-finding searches under exact-source, quadratic-graph, arbitrary-curvature, and deformation formulations returned no covering result. Current and prior own ledgers were checked; the only same-source finding concerns a finite coordinate-power obstacle and does not imply this graph-deformation statement.

## Value

PASS. The result removes the only curvature bound from the source's principal curved-graph test family, enlarging a small-parameter example to the full complex parameter plane. It also cleanly separates the geometric phenomenon of whole-domain outward motion from the analytic condition actually needed at the singular boundary.

That separation is useful for applying the new deformation theorem: beyond the exact threshold
\[
|\varepsilon|=1/2,
\]
the domains become genuinely nonnested, yet the singular obstacle remains admissible. The example therefore demonstrates the intended strength of the source theorem in a regime excluded by its own displayed parameter range.
