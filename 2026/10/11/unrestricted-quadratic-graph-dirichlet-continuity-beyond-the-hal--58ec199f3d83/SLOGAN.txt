The bound \(|\varepsilon|<1/2\) in the recent two-quadratic-graph Dirichlet example is geometric rather than analytic: it is exactly the threshold for global outward motion of the unit sphere. On the singular graphs themselves the normal term is \(1-a^2+|\varepsilon|^2|\xi_1|^4>0\), so the unbounded Perron–Bremermann envelope remains continuous for every \(\varepsilon\in\mathbb C\), including the genuinely nonnested regime.
