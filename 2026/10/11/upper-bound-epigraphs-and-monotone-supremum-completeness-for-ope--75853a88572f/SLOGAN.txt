For the operator-gauge order \(C_T=\{(t,x):t\ge\|Tx\|\}\), every order-bounded increasing net has an exact upper-bound epigraph \(u\ge s+\|Tw-y\|\), and it has a supremum precisely when its image limit \(y\) lies in \(T(X)\). Thus monotone supremum completeness of these cones is equivalent to closed range, with explicit increasing bounded sequences lacking suprema whenever the range is nonclosed.
