## Checks performed

- Reconstructed the cone inequalities and proved that the image net is norm Cauchy by comparing two indices above a common predecessor.
- Derived the epigraph formula in both directions, rather than assuming any candidate bound is least.
- Verified the leastness obstruction with upper-bound test points, proving no least upper bound is possible for a completion limit outside the range.
- Constructed a summable-increment increasing sequence for every nonclosed image and verified its finite order upper bound.
- Checked the diagonal \(\ell_1\) example symbolically: increment gauge \(2^{-(n+1)}\), scalar limit \(1\), image limit \((2^{-k})\), and uniquely required nonsummable preimage \((1,1,\ldots)\).
- Compared the new quantified upper-bound statement with the relevant 2024 monotone-supremum theorem and 2013 quasi-lattice treatment.

## Limits

The proof is analytic and needs no computer enumeration. The individual-net criterion is not a claim about convergence in the ambient \(X\)-norm. Possible equivalent prior results under different cone terminology are an acknowledged originality risk. Independent audit has not been performed.
