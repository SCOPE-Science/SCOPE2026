For the three-cube, the upper ordinary cut-complex filtration has \(\Delta_4(Q_3)\simeq\bigvee^3S^2\) and \(\Delta_5(Q_3)\cong S^2\sqcup S^2\). The inclusion \(\Delta_5(Q_3)\hookrightarrow\Delta_4(Q_3)\) is null-homotopic because the two sphere components bound the parity tetrahedra already present at level \(4\).
