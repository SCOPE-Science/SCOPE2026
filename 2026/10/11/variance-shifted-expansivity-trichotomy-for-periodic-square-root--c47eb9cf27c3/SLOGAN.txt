For zero-mean periodic perturbations \(c_j/\sqrt j\) of unilateral shift weights, the exact orbit-growth exponent is \(\delta=\alpha-\frac{1}{2p}\sum_{r=1}^{p}c_r^2\): positive drift produces strong asymptotic expansivity, negative drift gives asymptotic but not strong asymptotic expansivity, and zero drift gives nondecaying bounded orbits. The full, point and approximate-point spectra remain identical across all three regimes.
