## Correctness — PASS

Ramos's equivariantly natural sink cubical model identifies the relevant pair as the product of the graph with itself and its complete one-skeleton with only the diagonal edge-squares deleted. The resulting cellular relative group is exactly free on those squares. For each edge the two triangles using distinct extra vertices yield an oriented pair of cycles whose Hadamard product is a signed unit edge vector, proving surjectivity integrally at all sizes. Exactness and the integral Künneth formula give both Betti numbers and torsion-freeness; the edge, vertex, and swap traces derive the four character formulas. The attached standalone script reconstructs the complex and verifies every vertex permutation and label exchange through six vertices, checking all computed Betti ranks over four primes. The symbolic proof, rather than finite samples, handles the infinite parameter range.

The closest foundational sources [1,2] construct general sink models and calculate particular families, without identifying the complete-graph all-vertex-sink second-homology kernel and its full two-factor character. The especially plausible complete-graph pair computations in [3,4] concern ordinary collision-free configurations, not vertex-permitted collisions: already at five vertices their second Betti number is one rather than twenty-six. The literature search is finite, so unindexed equivalent treatments remain a residual originality risk.

## Value — PASS

Complete graphs are a canonical family with growing automorphism groups and many overlapping cycles. A closed integral answer for every size is strengthened by the entire \(S_m\times C_2\) representation: the label-swap eigenspaces distinguish an exterior square of graph cycles from the kernel of a natural symmetric edgewise pairing. The sharp threshold of four vertices and the contrast to ordinary graph braid homology make the collision convention mathematically consequential, rather than a changed name or a one-off small numerical invariant.
