For two labeled particles on the complete graph \(K_m\) with collisions allowed only at original vertices, \(m\ge4\), the integral homology is torsion-free with Betti ranks \(1,\,2\binom{m-1}{2},\,\binom{m-1}{2}^{2}-\binom m2\). The full vertex-permutation and label-swap characters follow from an explicit surjective edgewise multiplication map on graph cycle lattices, with a natural exterior-square positive-swap summand in second homology.
