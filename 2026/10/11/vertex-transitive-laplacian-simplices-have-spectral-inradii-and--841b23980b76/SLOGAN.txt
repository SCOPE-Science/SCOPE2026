For the canonical Euclidean Laplacian simplex of any connected vertex-transitive graph, the degree fixes the entire circumsphere while the Laplacian spectrum fixes the insphere. Specifically, \(R=\sqrt{k(k+1)}\) and \(r=\bigl(n\sum_{j=2}^{n}\lambda_j^{-2}\bigr)^{-1/2}\), with the origin as the unique common center and every facet tangent.
