For a bounded injective operator \(T:X\to Y\) between real Banach spaces, the cone \(\{(t,x):t\ge\|Tx\|\}\) has weak-to-norm convergence for every increasing weakly convergent net exactly when \(T\) has closed range. Every nonclosed-range operator admits an explicit increasing weakly null net whose norms tend to \(1\), resolving the net-versus-sequence ambiguity for the classical weighted \(\ell_1\) cone.
