For the generalized Lorenz flow, compact recurrent statistics obey an exact weighted-height defect law: the displacement of the \(x^2\)-weighted mean of \(z\) from the nonzero-equilibrium height equals a signed derivative-energy term. Every non-equilibrium ergodic recurrent state, and hence every nonconstant periodic orbit, must cross strictly beyond that equilibrium height on the side selected by the sign of \(s_2\).
