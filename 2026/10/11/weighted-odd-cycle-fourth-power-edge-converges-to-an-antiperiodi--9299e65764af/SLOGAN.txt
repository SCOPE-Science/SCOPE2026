For any positive smooth periodic edge profile \(a\), the sharp fourth-power energy of a weighted odd cycle approaches its bipartite bound \(16\) with deficit \(6\mu(a)/n^2+o(n^{-2})\), where \(\mu(a)\) is the first antiperiodic eigenvalue of \(-(aW')'=\mu aW\). Thus an inhomogeneous nonlinear graph extremum has a weighted *linear* Sturm–Liouville spectral limit.
