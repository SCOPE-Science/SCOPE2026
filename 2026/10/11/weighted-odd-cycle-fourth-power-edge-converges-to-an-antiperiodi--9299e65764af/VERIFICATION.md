# Verification record

The proof is based on an exact quartic defect identity with incident-edge mass normalization, a global coercivity inequality for the signed-square transform, localization of the improved inequality away from its zeros, and convergence to the first antiperiodic eigenvalue of a weighted Sturm–Liouville operator. Simple zeros of the eigenfunction control the fourth-power recovery remainder.

The standalone `verify_weighted.py` replayed exact identities for several positive rational edge-weight profiles and rational vertex vectors, along with even-cycle equality and the constant-weight recovery examples; it returned `WEIGHTED_CYCLE_CHECK_OK`. These are finite checks. They do not by themselves certify the universal asymptotic or establish bibliographic uniqueness. The proof does not cover nonpositive weights, nonsmooth profiles, complex scalars, or other exponents. Independent audit has not been performed.
