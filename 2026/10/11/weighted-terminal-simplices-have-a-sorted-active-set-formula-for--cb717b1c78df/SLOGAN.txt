For every weighted terminal simplex \(S(\omega)=\operatorname{conv}\{-\omega_0\mathbf1,\omega_1e_1,\ldots,\omega_de_d\}\), the Euclidean minimum enclosing sphere is obtained after sorting the coordinate weights: its positive base contacts are always a tail, with explicit closed formulas in both the base-only and apex-active regimes. For the standard terminal simplex the center is \(-\frac{d-1}{2(d+1)}\mathbf1\) and the radius is \(\frac{\sqrt d(d+3)}{2(d+1)}\).
