Every weighted disk Bergman kernel with radial weight \(1-q|z|^{2\gamma}\), for real \(\gamma>0\) and \(0\le q\le1\), is zero-free. The stronger half-plane bound \(\operatorname{Re}((1-s)^2\mathcal K_{\gamma,q}(s))\ge1\) holds even though the nonconstant weights have strictly superharmonic logarithm, beyond a familiar log-subharmonic nonvanishing criterion.
