For the three-qubit mixed-state \(R_2\) obtained by taking separate convex roofs for each bipartition and only then a geometric mean, a symmetric mixture of three Bell-pair-times-product states has \(R_2>0\) even though every component has \(R_2=0\). The state is biseparable by construction but NPT across every fixed cut, giving an exact strict convexity failure.
