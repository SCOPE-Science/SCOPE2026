# Review: A canonical elliptic quotient for the \(Q_8\)-sextic stratum



## Correctness
**PASS.** The source supplies the invariant plane-sextic equation and the two projective generators of the quaternion action. The central element \(z=A^2=B^2\) has projective fixed locus \(X=0\) together with \([1:0:0]\), and only the line meets the curve. Its intersection with the curve is exactly \(YZ(Y^4+Z^4)=0\), giving six distinct points in the stated characteristics. Since every nontrivial subgroup of \(Q_8\) contains \(z\), this exhausts ramification. The induced transformations on \(t=Y/Z\) partition the six points into three size-two orbits with \(C_4\) stabilizers. Tame Riemann--Hurwitz then forces quotient genus \(1\). The exact symbolic checker independently confirms the matrix relations, invariance, fixed-point equations, and arithmetic.

## Value
**PASS.** The quotient by the distinguished automorphism group is a canonical object attached to this newly classified quaternionic stratum. Showing that it is elliptic, with a uniform three-point \(C_4\)-ramification pattern, gives a structural invariant of the entire family rather than an arbitrary parameter computation. It also identifies exactly where the group action fails to be free, which is useful for moduli and Jacobian questions.
