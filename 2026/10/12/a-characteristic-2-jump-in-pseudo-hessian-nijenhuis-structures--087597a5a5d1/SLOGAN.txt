For \(e_2\cdot e_1=-e_1\), \(e_2\cdot e_2=e_2\), the characteristic-zero pseudo-Hessian–Nijenhuis family of Wang–Bai–Liu–Sheng is exhaustive whenever \(2\) is invertible. In characteristic \(2\), every nondegenerate symmetric form is pseudo-Hessian and every invertible selfadjoint endomorphism is compatible, raising the finite-field count from \(q^2(q-1)^2\) to \(q^4(q-1)^2\).
