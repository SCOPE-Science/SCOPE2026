The packaged checker `artifacts/verify.py` reconstructs all defining conditions from
\[
e_2\cdot e_1=-e_1,\qquad e_2\cdot e_2=e_2.
\]

For a symmetric form
\[
B=\begin{pmatrix}x&y\\y&z\end{pmatrix},
\]
the cocycle system reduces to
\[
2x=0.
\]

For
\[
N=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix},
\]
the Nijenhuis torsion is generated by
\[
2c^2,\qquad2bc,\qquad2b(a-d).
\]

The checker independently verifies the selfadjointness equation and the first diagonal entry of \(BN\), which supplies the \(B_1\)-cocycle condition once selfadjointness makes \(BN\) symmetric.

Direct exhaustive enumeration over
\[
\mathbf F_2,\quad\mathbf F_3,\quad\mathbf F_5
\]
gives respectively
\[
16,\quad36,\quad400
\]
pseudo-Hessian–Nijenhuis structures. For every accepted characteristic-\(2\) form, exactly
\[
q^2(q-1)
\]
invertible selfadjoint endomorphisms are found.

The checker returns `VERIFY_OK`.

Finite enumeration is a replay check. The arbitrary-field classification and prime-power counts are proved symbolically in `RESULT.md`.
