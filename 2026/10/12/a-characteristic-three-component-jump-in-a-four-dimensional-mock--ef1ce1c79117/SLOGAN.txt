For the four-dimensional mock-Lie algebra \(e_1\bullet e_1=e_2\), \(e_1\bullet e_3=e_4\), the complete skew Yang–Baxter solution locus jumps from an affine three-space plus plane to **three three-dimensional components** in characteristic \(3\). Over odd \(\mathbf F_q\), this changes the exact count from \(q^3+q^2-q\) to \(3q^3-2q^2\), with a corresponding jump in nondegenerate solutions and distinct coboundary maps.
