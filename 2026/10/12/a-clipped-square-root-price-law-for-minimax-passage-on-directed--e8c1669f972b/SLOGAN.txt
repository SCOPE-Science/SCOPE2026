On a one-way continuous-time Markov ring with arbitrary positive edge-rate prices, the unique allocation minimizing the worst directed mean first-passage time obeys a clipped inverse-square-root price law: cheap arcs have a common rate, and expensive arcs receive rates proportional to \(c_i^{-1/2}\). A single uniquely determined threshold gives every rate and the exact optimum, with a sharp uniform-rate criterion.
