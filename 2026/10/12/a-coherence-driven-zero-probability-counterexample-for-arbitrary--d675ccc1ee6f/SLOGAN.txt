A three-photon input with spatial occupation \((2,1,0,0)\) and coherent internal-state entanglement has \(P_{1110}(\phi)=(1+\cos\phi)/24\), so the event is exactly suppressed at \(\phi=\pi\) even though every relevant fully indistinguishable one-, two-, and three-photon transition is nonzero. This gives an explicit counterexample to the arbitrary-input zero-probability conjecture of arXiv:1410.1506 as stated, while leaving open product-internal-state variants.
