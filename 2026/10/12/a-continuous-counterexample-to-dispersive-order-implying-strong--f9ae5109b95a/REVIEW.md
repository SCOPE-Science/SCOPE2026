# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The quantile functions are strictly increasing and continuous. Their difference is nondecreasing on the whole unit interval, which proves the classical dispersive order for every pair of quantile levels. Exact quantile integration gives the expectile-level maps on the only interval needed. At \(u=24/25\) and \(v=159/164\), the two expectile gaps are explicit and reverse the strong spacing inequality. The strict comparison is certified by the positive integer square difference \(1241240\).

## Originality
PASS. Eberl and Klar explicitly leave open whether dispersive order implies their strong expectile-dispersive order. Their own counterexample is for the opposite implication. The later Zeng–Zhuang–Hu paper concerns the median-straddling weak order, proves it equivalent to dilation, and shows only that dilation does not control all same-side expectile spacings. Targeted statement-level and exact-expression searches found no covering resolution. Independent appearance under different terminology remains a residual risk.

## Value
PASS. This answers a named open implication negatively inside the exact regularity class of the source. It also pinpoints why the known weak/dilation theorem cannot be upgraded to the strong all-level order: a violation can occur entirely in the upper expectile tail even while every quantile spacing is ordered.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is Eberl and Klar, arXiv:2108.06138v1 / DOI 10.1007/s00362-022-01331-x. Zeng, Zhuang, and Hu, DOI 10.1017/jpr.2022.49, is the closest later comparison. The present result is a counterexample rather than a characterization and uses positive piecewise-constant densities.
