For the elliptic curve underlying Burek's rank-twelve Calabi–Yau family, its coarse level-four parameter admits a cyclic quartic descent with exact Mordell–Weil rank profile \(6\), \(8\), \(12\). The order-four action on the rational Mordell–Weil group has character \((12,4,4,4)\), with rational module \(\mathbb Q^{6}\oplus\mathbb Q_{\mathrm{sign}}^{2}\oplus\mathbb Q(i)^{2}\).
