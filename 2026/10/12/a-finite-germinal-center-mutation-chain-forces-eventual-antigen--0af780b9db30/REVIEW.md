# Scientific review

## Correctness

The completed theorem is restricted to the source's well-defined finite chain with \(n\ge2\). For \(n=1\), the source's distinct intermediate and terminal equations would refer to the same clone, so no canonical single ODE is specified. For every \(n\ge2\), direct cancellation yields \(\dot Q=-d(B_0+\sum_i\alpha^{-i}B_i)\le0\) and a dissipative \(N+H+G\) estimate. Positive forcing successively propagates through \(H,G,B_1,\ldots,B_n\), and \(P'=\kappa B_n>0\). Integrating \(V'=-\mu VP\) gives exponential decay after every positive time, hence \(V(t)\to0\).

## Originality

The underlying variation-of-constants and comparison tools are standard, and the claim does not advertise them as new. The source-specific correction is that the published deterministic \(n=50\) chronic-persistence interpretation cannot describe a positive asymptotic antigen level under its own non-mutating-antigen equations. The accessible primary article does not state this corrective conclusion. General germinal-center models with chronic antigen evolution do not imply it for these equations. An unindexed independent correction remains possible.

## Mathematical value

The paper's acute-versus-chronic interpretation is a primary modeling conclusion. Demonstrating an obligatory eventually-clearing limit, while distinguishing arbitrarily long transients from actual persistence, changes the interpretation of the exact ODE. The result does not assert fast or experimentally observable clearance.

## Scope

All mathematical assertions refer to the well-defined chain length \(n\ge2\). The ambiguous \(n=1\) boundary is expressly excluded; later mutating-antigen systems are not covered.
