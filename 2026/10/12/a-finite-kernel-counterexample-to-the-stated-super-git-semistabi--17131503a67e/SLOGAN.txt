For the weight-\(m\) action of \(\mathbf G_m\) on \(\mathbf A^1\), the ineffective kernel \(\mu_m\) acts trivially while the identity character restricts with exact order \(m\), yet \(\mathbf A^1\setminus\{0\}\) is \(\chi\)-stable. Thus semistability forces only torsion of \(r_\Delta(\chi)\), not the triviality asserted in the stated criterion.
