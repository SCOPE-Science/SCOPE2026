If \(C\) is c.e., \(A,B<_T C\), \(A\nleq_T B\), and \(\bigoplus_n E_n\leq_T B\) with every \(E_n\) noncomputable, then two fixed witnesses \(X_0,X_1\leq_T C\) can both cup \(A\) above \(B\) while no prescribed \(E_n\) is computed by both. This strengthens one-target avoidance to an arbitrary uniformly \(B\)-computable countable family without claiming that the pair is minimal.
