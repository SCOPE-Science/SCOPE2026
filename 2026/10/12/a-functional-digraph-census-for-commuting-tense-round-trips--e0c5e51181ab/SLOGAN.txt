The two event round trips in a deterministic tense frame commute for every valuation exactly when the transition map’s range is a union of its complete fibers. This admits an exact labelled functional-digraph census \(A(z)=1/(1-z\exp(u(z)))\), \(u=z(\exp z+\exp u-2)\), and the fraction of qualifying \(n\)-point frames is asymptotic to \(0.9459396379\ldots\,(0.8228042439\ldots)^n\).
