For a small-shape Weibull law, standardized anti-concentration does not merely vanish at the essential-exponential rate: after the natural renormalization it converges to the explicit threshold profile \(y^{-2/e}\). In particular, fixed-threshold tail ratios converge to \((y_2/y_1)^{2/e}\), recovering nontrivial threshold geometry that the zero anti-concentration function itself erases.
