# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Euclid gives primitive triples uniquely up to leg swap from coprime m>n with m−n odd and r=n(m−n); square r forces n=u²,m−n=v², gcd(u,v)=1 and odd v. Thus c=(u²+v²)²+u⁴. Counting positive coprime lattice pairs in dilation X^(1/4) Omega with odd v gives A*T²/2 times odd-divisor Mobius density 8/pi² and O(T log(2T)), hence kappa=(2/pi²) integral_0^(pi/2) [1+cos⁴ theta]^(-1/2) dtheta and error O(X^(1/4)log(2X)). Monotone boundary justifies O(T) fixed-grid error. Independent Euclid vs quartic enumeration agrees at X=100,10000,10^6,2*10^6 (2,29,280,396).

## originality

PASS

Robbins fully handles number of primitive Pythagorean triangles for a prescribed fixed inradius, but not the square-inradius subfamily counted by variable hypotenuse X. The new coprime quartic lattice-area asymptotic with explicit positive constant and uniform error is not a restatement of fixed-radius formula or the recorded OEIS excess lists.

An explicit asymptotic for primitive triples with a natural square integer inradius and a hypotenuse size cutoff is a genuine counting problem.
