The number of primitive integer right triangles of hypotenuse at most \(X\) whose inradius is a perfect square is \(\kappa\sqrt X+O(X^{1/4}\log(2X))\), with explicit \(\kappa=(2/\pi^2)\int_0^{\pi/2}(1+\cos^4\theta)^{-1/2}\,d\theta\approx0.27836513281\). The exact bijection to coprime quartic lattice points supplies the growth law and error term.
