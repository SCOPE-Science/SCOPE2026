Heydari's 2005 \(W^3\) concurrence changes by a factor \(1/\sqrt2\) when a Hadamard rotation is applied only to a separable spectator qubit. Because the rotation can be chosen as \(iH\in SU(2)\), this is an exact local-unitary counterexample to the paper's claimed SLOCC invariance and entanglement-monotone status.
