Inside the crossover model stated in arXiv:2610.01704v1, combining its displayed equation for \(N_*(p)\) with \(T(N)\sim\log^2N\) forces \(N_*(p)=\Theta(1/[p(\log(1/p))^4])\), with an explicit leading constant when \(T(N)=\kappa(\log N)^2(1+o(1))\). This is a conditional asymptotic consequence of the model, not a universal hardware law.
