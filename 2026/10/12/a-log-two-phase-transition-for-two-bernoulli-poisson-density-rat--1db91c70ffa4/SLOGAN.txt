For a Poisson-binomial law with at most two active Bernoulli parameters and fixed \(\Delta=\lambda^{-1}\sum p_i^2\), the exact worst density ratio to the matching Poisson law is \(e^{\Delta}\) up to \(\Delta=\log2\) and \(e^{2\Delta}/2\) afterward. The extremizer switches at \(\log2\) from one active Bernoulli to two equal Bernoulli parameters, and the resulting envelope is strictly below \(1/(1-\Delta)\).
