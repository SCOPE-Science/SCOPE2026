For adjacency quantum walks on an unweighted cycle, any nonzero potential at one vertex forces the infinite-time average-mixing rank to \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor+1\), independent of the potential's size or sign. Relative to the known unmarked cycle, this unexpectedly *lowers* the rank on odd cycles and *raises* it on even cycles; an exact odd-moment determinant \((-1)^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}\tau^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}\) certifies the change.
