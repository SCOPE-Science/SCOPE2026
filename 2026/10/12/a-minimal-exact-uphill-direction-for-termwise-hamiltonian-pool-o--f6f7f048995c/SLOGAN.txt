A two-term Hamiltonian pool already lets exact OVQITE point uphill: for an explicit one-qubit instance, the directional energy derivative is \(s_E=1/(5+14\lambda)>0\) for every \(\lambda\ge0\), despite exact imaginary-time energy rate \(-1\). One nonconstant Hamiltonian term can never do this, so two terms are the minimal exact obstruction.
