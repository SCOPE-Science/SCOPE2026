The October 2026 Plotkin-recursive characterization of the full \(\mathrm{R}(1,m)\) service-rate region is missing the facet \(3\lambda_1+2\sum_{i=2}^{m+1}\lambda_i\le2^m+2\). The corrected region is exactly the 2020 Kazemi--Kurz--Soljanin polytope after relabeling, and \(((2^m+2)/3,\,2^{m-1}-(2^m+2)/3,0,\ldots,0)\) is a uniform counterexample to the 2026 display for every \(m\ge3\).
