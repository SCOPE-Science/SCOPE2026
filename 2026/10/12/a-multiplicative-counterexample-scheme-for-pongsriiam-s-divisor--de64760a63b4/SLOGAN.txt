Pongsriiam’s divisor-sum progression converse fails for every real \(s\): a common coprime progression can be multiplied by fixed factors \(u\) and \(v\) with \(\sigma_s(u)<\sigma_s(v)\) while \(u\nmid v\). Explicit choices give permanent strict inequalities with \(ad=bc\) but with neither \(a\mid c\) nor \(\gcd(a,b)\mid\gcd(c,d)\).
