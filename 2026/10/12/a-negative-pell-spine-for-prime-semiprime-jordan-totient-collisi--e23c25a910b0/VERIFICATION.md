# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For odd primes p,q, J_2(p)=p^2-1 and J_2(2q)=3(q^2-1), hence the collision is exactly p^2-3q^2=-2. The exceptional q=2 produces J_2(4)=12 and p^2=13, impossible; p=2 likewise impossible. Every positive solution of x^2-3y^2=-2 is (P_t,Q_t) generated by (1+sqrt(3))(2+sqrt(3))^t: for y>=2, (2x-3y,2y-x) is strictly positive, norm-preserving and decreases y to 1. t>=1 is forced for prime pairs. Cubing the unit gives recurrence P_{t+3}=26P_t+45Q_t and Q_{t+3}=15P_t+26Q_t, so t congruent 1 mod3 forces 5|P_t and 3|Q_t; except t=1 both are composite. Fresh integer checks t=0..6 confirm norm and first collisions; finite checks are corroboration, not proof of exhaustive Pell orbit.
