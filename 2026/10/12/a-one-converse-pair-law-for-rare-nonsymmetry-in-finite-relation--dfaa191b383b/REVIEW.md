# Review

## Correctness
PASS. Koussas states the same uniform Maddux asymptotic for every admissible number of self-converse atoms. Substituting \(s=n-2k\) gives the exact exponent loss \(k(n-1)\) and the exact automorphism-factor ratio. Their quotient yields the displayed \(R_{n,k}\). The consecutive-defect quotient is exact and is uniformly geometrically small after the first defect, so summing over all \(k\ge2\) is legitimate. Uniformity of the source relative error is essential and is explicitly present in Proposition 14.

The conditional \(O(n^2 2^{-n})\) statement does not require a convergence rate for the source theorem: the unknown uniform relative errors appear only as bounded multiplicative factors in the ratio of the tail to the one-pair stratum.
