# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The two-component ideal differences force y=0 and xz=0, so singularities occur only at [1:0:0] and [0:0:1]. Local branches have expansions z=-a y^2. Any embedded analytic isomorphism preserves the unique common tangent; its 2-jet Y=alpha*y+beta*z+..., Z=delta*z+gamma*y^2+... yields b=(delta*a-gamma)/alpha^2, an affine action. Conversely Y=y, Z=uz-vy^2 realizes all affine changes, preserving the transverse y=0 line. A global projectivity sends the conic span <xz,y^2> to itself, preserving its unique rank-one and rank-two singular members and therefore acts on the parameter by scaling only. Symbolically checked determinant -lambda^2*mu/4, affine identity, and explicit translated but non-scaled triple. The proof covers all m>=3, not merely the witness.

## originality

PASS

Both extremal minimal-Tjurina families and their nearly-free status were already published by Beorchia--Miró-Roig; Aluffi--Faber already describe the multiplicative parameter stabilizer for the bitangent pencil. Those premises are credited. The extra exact local affine quotient, its mismatch with global scaling, and the one-dimensional generic fiber are not stated or entailed by the inspected original full-text sections. Search of published mathematical records returned conic-pencils with different singularity configurations, not this same local/global quotient. The conclusion is best-of-knowledge; standard singularity theory may contain a stronger implicit affine-branch classification.
