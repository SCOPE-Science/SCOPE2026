For the minimal-Tjurina bitangent families \(\mathcal C_2\) and \(\mathcal{CL}_6\), embedded local analytic type remembers the parameter set only up to affine transformations \(a\mapsto ua+v\), while global projective type remembers it up to scaling \(a\mapsto ca\). Thus projective classes have a genuine one-dimensional modulus invisible to all local analytic singularity germs.
