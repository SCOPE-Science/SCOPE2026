The proposed upper alignment bound allows a learned correction to reverse the gradient completely: on \(f(x)=\tfrac12x^2\) over \([-1,1]\), the admissible choice \(b(x)=-2x\) makes the published diminishing-step iteration converge to the boundary instead of the minimizer. The contraction proof needs the opposite-sided bound \(\langle b,x-x^\star\rangle\ge-\eta\langle Q,x-x^\star\rangle\).
