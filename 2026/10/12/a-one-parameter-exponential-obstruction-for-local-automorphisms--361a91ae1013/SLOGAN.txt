For the five-dimensional associative algebra \(\pi_3\), the plus local-automorphism component is not the exponential image of its local derivations: the automorphisms \(\Phi_\tau(e_4)=\tau e_3-e_4\) with \(\tau\ne0\) lie outside that image. The obstruction is the exact resonance \((\exp A)_{3,4}=0\) whenever the shared diagonal parameter satisfies \(e^x=-1\), while \(\Phi_0\) remains exponential.
