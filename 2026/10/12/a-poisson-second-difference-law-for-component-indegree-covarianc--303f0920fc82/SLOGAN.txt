For a uniform random self-map, the covariance of its component count with the full in-degree-count polynomial converges to \(-\tfrac12(z-1)^2e^{z-1}\). Its fixed-degree covariances therefore approach a signed Poisson second-difference profile: positive exactly for in-degrees \(1\) and \(2\), and negative for in-degree \(0\) and every fixed degree at least \(3\).
