Burek's 96-node elliptic threefold admits a projective crepant small resolution over \(\mathbf Q\): the sum of twelve conjugate section graphs is a rational Weil divisor whose absolute local class is \(2\) at 64 nodes and \(4\) at 32 nodes. Its divisorial Proj supplies a smooth projective \(\mathbf Q\)-model whose geometric elliptic Mordell–Weil rank is twelve; no rank over \(\mathbf Q(v,t)\) is asserted.
