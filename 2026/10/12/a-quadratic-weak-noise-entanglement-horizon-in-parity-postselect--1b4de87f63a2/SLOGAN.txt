In an edge-erased, parity-postselected double-rail graph state, increasing the even layer count as \(n\sim c/p^2\) produces an exact two-qubit crossover: the conditional Bell-pair negativity is \(\max\{0,(2e^{-2c}+e^{-7c/2}-1)/4\}\), with entanglement disappearing at \(c\approx0.458661867647\). The rare heralding event has \(p\log P_{\mathrm{acc}}\to-3c\), exposing a quantitative limit behind the previously known length-independent first-order noise susceptibility.
