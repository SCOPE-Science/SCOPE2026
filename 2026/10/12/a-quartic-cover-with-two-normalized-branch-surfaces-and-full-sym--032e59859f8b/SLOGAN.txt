The Shi–Xu–Zuo polynomial map \(\mathbb A^3\to\mathbb A^3\) is finite free of degree \(4\) and has trace discriminant \(256B_1H\) with two irreducible branch surfaces, both normalized by \(\mathbb A^2\). One surface is a Whitney umbrella, and the connected four-sheeted cover has full geometric monodromy \(S_4\).
