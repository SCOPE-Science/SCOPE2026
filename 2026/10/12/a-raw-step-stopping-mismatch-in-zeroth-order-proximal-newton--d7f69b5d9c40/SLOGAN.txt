The printed ZOPN test \(\|d_k\|\leq\varepsilon\) does not by itself certify \(\varepsilon\)-stationarity at the returned point: on \(f(x)=x^2/2\), an exact-gradient first step can stop with \(\|d_0\|=\varepsilon/2\) yet return a point whose stationarity residual is \(3\varepsilon/2\). The source's own stationarity lemma gives the natural scaled stopping repair.
