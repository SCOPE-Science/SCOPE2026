For scalar delayed gradient descent with the fixed step \(6/5\), stationary zero-or-one-step Markov staleness has an exact, bounded interval of mean-square stability in the delay-persistence probability \(p\), between approximately \(0.426776\) and \(0.791226\). Even though every such chain uses fresh and stale gradients equally often, both stronger alternation and stronger persistence cause exponential mean-square divergence outside that interval.
