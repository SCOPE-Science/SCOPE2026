The joint chance-constrained LCP feasible set can already be nonconvex in one dimension with Gaussian uncertainty: for \(M=1\) and \(p=1/2\), the positive-\(z\) boundary has \(g(z)/z\to+\infty\) at the feasible origin, forcing line segments from the origin to boundary points to exit the feasible set. This gives an exact negative answer to universal convexity of the joint model while leaving the source's separate-chance convexity result untouched.
