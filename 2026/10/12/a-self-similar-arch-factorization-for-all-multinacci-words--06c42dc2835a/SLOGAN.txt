For every multinacci word, complete scattered-subsequence universality arches are exactly the intervals terminating at the rarest letter: the first has length \(2^{m-1}\), and subsequent arch lengths encode the original word by \(j\mapsto 2^m-2^j\). Thus every prefix has universality index equal to its count of the letter \(m-1\), with limiting density \(\lambda_m^{-m}\), where \(\lambda_m\) is the multinacci constant.
