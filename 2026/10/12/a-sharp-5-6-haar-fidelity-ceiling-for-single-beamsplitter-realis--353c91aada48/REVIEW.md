# Review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The claim is reduced to two exact facts. First, a polarization-preserving beamsplitter cannot convert the relative sign between the all-horizontal and all-vertical components of \(\lvert\phi^+\rangle\) and \(\lvert\phi^-\rangle\) into a photon-count probability, so their likelihoods are identical outcome by outcome. This yields the sharp Bayes/MAP label bound \(p_{\mathrm{MAP}}\le3/4\), attained by the standard 50:50 analyzer. Second, every wrong Bell-label correction differs from the correct one by a nonidentity Pauli, whose Haar-average pure-state fidelity is \(1/3\), giving \(\bar F=(1+2p_{\mathrm{MAP}})/3\le5/6\). The proof covers all beamsplitter settings in the source model and does not rely on finite sampling.
