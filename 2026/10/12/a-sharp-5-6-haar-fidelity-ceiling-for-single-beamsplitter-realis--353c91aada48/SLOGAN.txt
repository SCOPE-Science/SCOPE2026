For the single polarization-preserving beamsplitter protocol of arXiv:quant-ph/0010113v1, maximum-posterior Bell-label decoding succeeds with probability at most \(3/4\), and the sharp Haar-averaged teleportation fidelity is therefore \(5/6\), not the reported \(\approx0.88\). The standard 50:50 analyzer attains the bound, while the corrected value still exceeds the classical \(2/3\) benchmark.
