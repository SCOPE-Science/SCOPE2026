The classical common-shock Poisson vector is jointly \(\mathrm{MTP}_2\), and every pair is likelihood-ratio monotone when conditioned on its pair sum, yet conditioning a coordinate on the full sum fails stochastic monotonicity from dimension three onward. For the first three-coordinate comparison, the sharp likelihood-ratio and stochastic-order cutoffs are respectively \(b=2a^3\) and \(b=6a^3\).
