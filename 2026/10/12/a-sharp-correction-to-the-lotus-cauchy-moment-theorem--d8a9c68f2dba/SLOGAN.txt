For a nondegenerate Cauchy law, the recently claimed positive-integer complex moments do not exist as ordinary expectations: the proof loses the negative branch when inverting the density. The formula \(\mathbb E[X^z]=(\mu+i\sigma)^z\) is valid instead exactly on the principal-power strip \(-1<\operatorname{Re}z<1\), so its positive-integer values are analytic continuation, not Cauchy moments.
