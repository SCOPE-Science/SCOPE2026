On the ED discriminant of \(\operatorname{pGr}(k,n)\), every finite critical set has size at most \(\binom{n-2}{k}+\binom{n-2}{k-2}\), and this is sharp. Thus the generic count \(\binom{n}{k}\) cannot degrade by a small finite amount: the first possible finite drop is at least \(2\binom{n-2}{k-1}\).
