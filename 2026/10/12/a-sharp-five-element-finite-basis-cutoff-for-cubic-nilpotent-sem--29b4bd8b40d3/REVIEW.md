# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The R1 unary part of N(S) in Wang–Yin–Wang is full nonzero domain and all nonzero elements in S² are binary-isolated. The atom graph on S\S² is homomorphically equivalent to N(S), so finite duality reduces correctly. A transitive ≤3-vertex nonempty loopless digraph is hom-equivalent to T_h; a directed path of h arcs is its finite obstruction. If not transitive, mutual-pair, complete bidirected triangle, directed 3-cycle, and oriented path-without-shortcut cases admit unbounded vertex-critical bidirected odd cycles, odd wheels, directed cycles length nonmultiple3, and alternating-sign odd cycles. Those give lack of finite duality rather than a finite failure test. At size 5 the one-output case consists of 15 nonempty 3-vertex digraph iso classes, four transitive; the two-output two-atom case adds one nonfinite class. The order-six given 4-atom example is equivalent to T3 but not transitive. Independently enumerated 15 graph classes and four transitive and checked corresponding size-six homomorphisms.

## originality

PASS

Wang–Yin–Wang prove finite-basis↔finite-duality for all nilpotent semigroups and finite examples; the new result is the explicit complete five-element, elementwise-zero-square 16/4 classification and sharp failure of a simple graph criterion at six, rather than the known general decision theorem. Distler–Mitchell give a semigroup census but not the finite-basis stratification; older digraph-duality classification remains priority risk.

PASS

The full-power-semiring finite-basis decision is a central structural property; its exact first nontrivial small-order cutoff, complete isomorphism census, and sharp six-element exception are naturally motivated finite classifications, not arbitrary enumeration.
