The ordinary average quantum mixing matrix of the Johnson triple graph \(J(n,3)\) has full rank for every \(n\ge7\), while the antipodal boundary \(J(6,3)\) has rank exactly \(10\) on \(20\) vertices. Strict positivity for \(n\ge7\) is witnessed by a single lowest-eigenspace Schur square, with an explicit positive rational lower bound on its least eigenvalue.
