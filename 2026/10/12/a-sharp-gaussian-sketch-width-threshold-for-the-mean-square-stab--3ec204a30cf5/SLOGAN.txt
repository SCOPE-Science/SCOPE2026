For one-step Gaussian-sketch randomly pivoted LU on nonsingular \(2\times2\) matrices, a uniform normalized mean-square residual bound holds for every sketch width \(b\ge3\), but fails sharply at \(b=1\) and \(b=2\), with exact diagonal witnesses diverging as \(e^{-1}\) and \(-2\log(e)/(1-e^2)\). Even for matrices with condition number at most \(2\), all higher-than-second residual moments are unbounded for every sketch width, and the exact worst tail along an explicit family is of order \(T^{-2}\).
