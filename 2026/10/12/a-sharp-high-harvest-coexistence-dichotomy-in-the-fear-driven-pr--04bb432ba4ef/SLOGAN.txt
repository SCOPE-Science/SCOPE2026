In the harvested fear-driven predator–prey model, the exponent comparison is decisive: if \(r>m\), a locally stable positive equilibrium survives at every sufficiently large finite harvesting effort with \(v_q\sim(eK^p/q)^{1/(r-m)}\). At \(r=m\), coexistence instead ends at the finite threshold \(q=eK^p\) for \(m<1\), or \(d+q=eK^p\) when \(m=1\).
