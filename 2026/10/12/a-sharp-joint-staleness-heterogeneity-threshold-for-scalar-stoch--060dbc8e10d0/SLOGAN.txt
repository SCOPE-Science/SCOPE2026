For scalar stochastic gradient descent with independently sampled positive curvatures and a one-step stale-read probability \(p\), the exact all-initialization mean-square stability boundary is \(\vartheta(s+ps^2)<2\), where \(s\) is the mean-curvature-scaled stepsize and \(\vartheta\) is the normalized curvature second moment. Staleness and curvature heterogeneity can each be harmless alone but destabilize the same stepsize when combined.
