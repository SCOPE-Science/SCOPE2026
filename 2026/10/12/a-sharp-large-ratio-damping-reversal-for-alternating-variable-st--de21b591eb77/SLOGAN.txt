For standard alternating-step BDF3 on \(y'=-\lambda y\), the two-step Floquet spectral radius tends to \(2/(1+\lambda h)\) as the ratio of alternating timesteps grows without bound. Thus every fixed \(0<\lambda h<1\) eventually becomes unstable, although \(\lambda h>1\) is eventually stable; an exact finite witness already has a real multiplier below \(-1\).
