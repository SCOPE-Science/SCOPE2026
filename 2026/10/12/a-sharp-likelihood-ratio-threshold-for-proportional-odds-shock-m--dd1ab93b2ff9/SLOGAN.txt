For homogeneous portfolios with Bernoulli claim occurrence and common-baseline proportional-odds severities, an exact two-branch inequality characterizes likelihood-ratio ordering of the maximum, including its probability atom at zero. If the second portfolio has smaller severity odds \(\beta<\alpha\), stronger ordering nevertheless begins at the sharp integer size \(1+\left\lceil2(\alpha-\beta)/(\beta q-\alpha p)\right\rceil\), provided \(\beta q>\alpha p\).
