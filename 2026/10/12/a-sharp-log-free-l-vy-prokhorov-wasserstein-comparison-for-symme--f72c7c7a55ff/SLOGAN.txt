For symmetric geometric laws with \(\mathbb E|X|\le B\le1\), the exact sharp comparison is \(W_1\le(1+B^2+B\sqrt{1+B^2})d_{\mathrm{LP}}\). At the unit moment normalization this becomes the sharp log-free constant \(2+\sqrt2\), so the logarithmic loss in the general discrete log-concave bound is not forced by this canonical log-affine family.
