For scalar heavy-ball optimization with positive autoregressive gradient-noise persistence \(r\), adding momentum reduces stationary error at a fixed normalized stepsize \(t\) precisely when \(t>\tau(r)\), where the sharp frontier rises from \(1\) to \(\sqrt2\) as persistence grows. Above the frontier a unique crossover momentum separates strict improvement from strict deterioration; below it every positive momentum is worse than plain SGD.
