On a dense binomial random graph with independent uniform vertex colors, ordinary modal formulas of depth one distinguish all vertices with high probability when the color alphabet is asymptotically larger than \(pn/\log n\), but fail with high probability when it is asymptotically smaller by a fixed factor. The sharp constant is the edge probability \(p\), arising from rare missing neighbor colors under random occupancy; the critical window and unrestricted modal depth are not classified.
