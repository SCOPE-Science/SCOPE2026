On every full-rank \(2\times2\) matrix with unequal singular values, Rademacher subspace iteration has an exact, sharp orientation-dependent **expected squared** rank-one error law. At the worst singular-frame orientation, an excess of \((a^2-b^2)2^{-\ell}\) survives any number of power steps, while a two-column Gaussian sketch has zero excess almost surely.
