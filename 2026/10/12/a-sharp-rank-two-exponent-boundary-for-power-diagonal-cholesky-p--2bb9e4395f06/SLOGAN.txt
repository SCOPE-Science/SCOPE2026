For power-diagonal Cholesky sampling, every exponent \(0\le\beta\le2\) preserves the sharp universal one-step Frobenius-square contraction \(1-1/n\). On a natural rank-two isolated-column-versus-redundant-cluster family, the exact safe range extends to \(\beta\le4\), while every \(\beta>4\) loses that dimension-uniform guarantee.
