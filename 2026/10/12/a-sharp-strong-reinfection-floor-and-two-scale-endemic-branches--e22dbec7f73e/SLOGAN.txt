In a five-compartment SEIRV model, arbitrarily strong recovered-class reinfection cannot push the subcritical endemic-existence threshold below \(1-\delta/K>0\): it approaches that sharp floor at an explicitly determined \(B^{-1/2}\) rate. The coexisting endemic equilibria separate into one with infected prevalence of order \(B^{-1}\) and another with a strictly positive limit.
