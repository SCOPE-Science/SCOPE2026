For exact-arithmetic MINRES on SPD Stieltjes systems with nonnegative right-hand side, dimensions one and two cannot produce a negative iterate, but dimension three can do so already at step two. For \(A=\operatorname{diag}(1,2,4)\) and \(b=(1,1,\varepsilon)^{{\mathsf T}}\), the third component of the second iterate is negative exactly for \(0<\varepsilon<1/\sqrt{{50}}\), despite a strictly positive exact solution.
