At fixed mean-half input singlet conversion probability and any prescribed variance on \([0,1/4]\), the worst two-link entanglement-swapping success law is uniquely uniform on a central interval at low variance and becomes a uniform-plus-two-endpoint-atoms distribution at high variance. This exact envelope yields a sharp distribution-independent triangular-lattice supercriticality guarantee precisely below variance \(3(1/2-2\sin(\pi/18))^2\).
