# Self-review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The proof is exhaustive for \(\omega(N)\le3\). It first converts the unitary divisor sum into pairwise-coprime prime-power blocks. The one-block case is immediate; the two-block case is forced to \(M=2\) and an impossible factorization; and the three-block case is forced to \(M=2\), after which the exact linear equation for the largest block is eliminated in all possible ranges. The finite checker is not used as a substitute for the proof.

## Originality
**PASS.** The closest literature was compared at the statement and implication level. Yamada gives quantitative bounds for \(\sigma^*(N)=MN\pm1\), while Subbarao and Hasanalizade treat the plus-sign equation. Targeted exact-form, congruence-form, database, and published-finding-index searches did not identify a checked source implying the exact minus-sign classification for \(\omega(N)\le3\). Older or poorly indexed unitary-Lehmer literature remains a residual risk.

## Value
**PASS.** This is a natural complete low-support classification for the signed companion of a longstanding unitary Lehmer problem. It separates the first three support layers exactly, without exponent bounds, and complements the known plus-sign support restrictions and the modern analytic bounds.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is T. Yamada, arXiv:2303.16853v1, which treats the same family of equations with both signs but does not close the low-support minus-sign cases. M. V. Subbarao's 1998 full text and E. Hasanalizade's 2023 paper cover the plus sign. The theorem does not classify \(\omega(N)\ge4\) and does not assert existence of a nontrivial minus-sign solution.
