For a uniform random permutation, cycle count has covariance with any permutation linear statistic equal to the array's diagonal mean minus its grand mean. Applied to squared rank displacement, this gives the exact Cayley–Spearman covariance \(-1/n\) and correlation \(-\sqrt{n-1}/(n\sqrt{H_n-H_n^{(2)}})\), revealing the finite mechanism behind their asymptotic decoupling.
