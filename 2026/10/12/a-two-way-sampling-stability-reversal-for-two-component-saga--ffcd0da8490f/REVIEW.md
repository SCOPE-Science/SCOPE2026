# Mathematical review

**Correctness — PASS.** The stated algorithm is the original pre-update-gradient-memory SAGA for scalar strongly convex quadratics; the two linear update matrices are obtained directly. Exact six-monomial lifts distinguish iid single steps from randomly permuted two-step epochs. For the stable schemes, the supplied integer Lyapunov matrices and their expected residuals are strictly positive definite by the displayed exact Sylvester minors. For the unstable schemes, the rational generating functions arise from the initialized quadratic-moment recurrences, have denominator roots strictly between zero and one, and have numerators with no zeros there. Nonnegative expectation coefficients yield exponential-subsequence expected position-error growth. Independent exact path enumeration corroborates each recurrence through six grouped rounds. The theorem does not infer infinite-time behavior merely from finite experiments.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** The original SAGA algorithm defines the pre-update memory rule; the 2017 reshuffling SAGA analysis displays a different post-update memory convention and only sufficiently small-step convergence. The 2019 arbitrary-sampling SAGA treatment develops iid sampling and sufficient bounds, not an exact bidirectional iid-versus-reshuffle stability reversal. Targeted published mathematical-index searches and earlier relevant finite-horizon reshuffling results did not show an equivalent stronger claim. This is a priority assessment, not an assertion of guaranteed global uniqueness; unindexed specialized analyses are the material residual risk.

**Value — PASS.** The two-direction obstruction is an actionable stability distinction for a well-established variance-reduced algorithm: either sampling strategy can converge while the other has growing expected objective error, even with individually smooth strongly convex components. The identical-component witness isolates sampling correlation from curvature heterogeneity, and strict rational inequalities show robustness to perturbation. A sharp general threshold or broad optimization guarantee is not claimed or required for this counterexample classification.

The closest literature is Defazio–Bach–Lacoste-Julien (2014), Ying–Yuan–Sayed (2017), and Qian–Qu–Richtárik (2019). The algorithm update convention differs from the 2017 reshuffling algorithm; no result about that distinct convention is claimed. No independent validation, generalized parameter frontier, or almost-sure instability theorem has been produced.
