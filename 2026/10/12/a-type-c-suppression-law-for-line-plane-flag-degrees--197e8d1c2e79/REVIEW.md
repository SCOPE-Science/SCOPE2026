\[
\frac{\deg C_n}{\deg A_n}=\frac{3(n-1)}{4n-3}
\]
and
\[
\frac{\chi(C_n)}{\chi(A_n)}=\frac{2(n-1)}{2n-1}.
\]
The final normalized factor decreases strictly because its consecutive difference is positive.
