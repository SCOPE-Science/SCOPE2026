Inside the ordinary line-plane flag \(F(1,2;2n)\), imposing symplectic isotropy multiplies the Segre-Pluecker degree by \(3(n-1)/(4n-3)\) but the Euler characteristic by \(2(n-1)/(2n-1)\). The normalized degree-to-Euler ratio is therefore suppressed by a strictly decreasing factor tending to \(3/4\).
