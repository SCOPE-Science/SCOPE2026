For the Chow variety of degree-\(d\) products of linear forms in \(n+1\) variables, projective degree and topological Euler characteristic are \((nd)!/(d!(n!)^d)\) and \(\binom{n+d}{d}\), respectively. Away from trivial projective-space boundaries, equality occurs only for reducible quadrics in \(\mathbb P^3\), where both invariants are \(10\).
