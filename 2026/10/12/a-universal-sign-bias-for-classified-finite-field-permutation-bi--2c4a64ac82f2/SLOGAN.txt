For every admissible odd-characteristic permutation binomial \(X^r(X^{q-1}+a)\), its sign is a fixed power-map factor times the quadratic character of \(\operatorname{Norm}(a)-(-1)^e\). Among eligible coefficients the even and odd counts differ by exactly \((q^e-1)/(q-1)\), so a fixed-exponent family is never parity-balanced.
