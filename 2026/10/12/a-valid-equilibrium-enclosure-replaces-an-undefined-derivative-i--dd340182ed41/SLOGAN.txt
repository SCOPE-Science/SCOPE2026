In the convex adaptive vaccination model, the published upper endpoint \(\pi'^{-1}((1-r)/\epsilon)\) is undefined because its argument is positive while \(\pi'\le0\). A valid and sometimes sharp replacement is \(P_0\le\min\{p_*,(1-r-\epsilon\pi'(0))/(1/p_*-\epsilon\pi'(0))\}\), with a tighter implicit chord bound in between.
