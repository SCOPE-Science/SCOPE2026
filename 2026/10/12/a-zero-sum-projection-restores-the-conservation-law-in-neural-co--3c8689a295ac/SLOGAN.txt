In the published neural-corrected SIR equation, total population obeys \(\dot M=\lambda\,\mathbf 1^\top f_{\mathrm{NN}}\), so an unconstrained three-output residual does not structurally preserve the SIR simplex. Projecting residual targets or outputs with \(P=I-\frac13\mathbf 1\mathbf 1^\top\) restores exact conservation, and in the paper's same-structure parameter-bias experiment this projection cannot increase Euclidean error to the exact residual.
