For the unique class-three stem nilpotent Lie algebra of breadth type \((0,2m)\) over any odd \(\mathbf F_q\), all maximal abelian subalgebras form \(\mathbf P^2(\mathbf F_{q^m})\), with pairwise intersection equal to the center. An exact Gaussian-binomial formula counts every abelian subalgebra and abelian ideal by dimension; the derived algebra is the unique maximal abelian ideal.
