If a strongly pseudoperfect integer \(n>1\) satisfies \(\sigma(n)\le3n\), then the pair \((1,n)\) is forced into every representation and all powers \(n^k\) are strongly pseudoperfect. More generally omitted divisor pairs lie in an explicit central band, and \(720\) is the first integer with a strong representation that can omit \((1,n)\).
