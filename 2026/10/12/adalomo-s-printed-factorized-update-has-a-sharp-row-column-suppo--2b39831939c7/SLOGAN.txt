For the printed AdaLomo matrix update, the first raw adaptive step is finite exactly when the gradient has no all-zero row and no all-zero column; otherwise the factorized preconditioner creates an explicit \(0/0\) support singularity even if the full gradient is nonzero. The cited Adafactor rule and current first-party AdaLomo implementation contain denominator or squared-gradient safeguards that avoid this exact boundary.
