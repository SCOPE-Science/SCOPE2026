Every maximum rank-distance-\(2\) code in \(\mathbb F_2^{2\times3}\) is affine: there are exactly \(384\) labeled maxima, arising as the eight cosets of each of \(48\) linear MRD three-spaces. Those \(48\) linear spaces form a single \(\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb F_2)\times\operatorname{GL}_3(\mathbb F_2)\) orbit with stabilizer order \(21\).
