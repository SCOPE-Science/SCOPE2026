For every nonempty closed convex \(C\) and every \(r>0\), the truncated normal map \(y\mapsto N_C(y)\cap B_r\) is Hausdorff-Lipschitz exactly when \(C\) is affine; every genuine convex boundary causes a jump of at least \(r\). Applied to the recent inexact linearized ADMM hypothesis, this means its general-set normal truncation covers affine convex constraints but excludes every non-affine closed convex constraint set.
