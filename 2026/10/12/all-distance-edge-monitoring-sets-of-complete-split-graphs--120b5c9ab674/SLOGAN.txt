For \(S_{m,n}=K_m\vee\overline{K_n}\), every nonempty set monitors all edges when \(m=1\); for \(m\ge2\), distance-edge-monitoring sets are exactly the vertex covers. Hence \(\mathcal D_{m,n}(z)=z^m(1+z)^n+mz^{m+n-1}\) for \(m\ge2\), giving every feasible sensor placement rather than only the known minimum size.
