For a crown-graph perfect matching \(M_\pi\), every forcing subset has size at least \(n-2\), and the minimum ones are obtained exactly by omitting an adjacent pair in the cycle decomposition of \(\pi\). Consequently \(\Phi_\pi(x)=x^n+n x^{n-1}+(n-t_2(\pi))x^{n-2}\), so the full forcing-set family detects the number of transpositions even though every perfect matching has the same forcing number \(n-2\).
