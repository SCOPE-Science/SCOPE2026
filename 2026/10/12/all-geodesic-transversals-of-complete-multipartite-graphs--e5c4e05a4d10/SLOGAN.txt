A geodesic transversal of a connected complete multipartite graph is exactly the complement of either a set contained in one non-singleton part or a sparse set taking at most one vertex from each non-singleton part and at most one singleton-part vertex. This yields the complete size distribution and \(\operatorname{gt}(G)=N-\max\{M,q+\mathbf 1_{\{s>0\}}\}\).
