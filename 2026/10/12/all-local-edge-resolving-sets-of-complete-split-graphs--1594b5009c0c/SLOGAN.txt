For the complete split graph \(S_{m,n}=K_m\vee\overline{K_n}\), every local edge resolving set omits at most one vertex when \(m\ge2\); for the star boundary \(m=1\), it may omit the center and at most one leaf. Hence \(\operatorname{edim}_{\ell}(S_{m,n})=m+n-1\) for \(m\ge2\), while \(\operatorname{edim}_{\ell}(S_{1,n})=n-1\) for \(n\ge2\).
