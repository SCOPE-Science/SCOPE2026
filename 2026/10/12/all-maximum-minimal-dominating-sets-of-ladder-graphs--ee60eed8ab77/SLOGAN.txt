For every two-row Cartesian ladder \(P_2\square P_n\) with \(n\ge2\), every largest inclusion-minimal dominating set has size \(n\) and belongs to four explicit single-vertex-column patterns or, when \(n\) is even, two additional periodic full/empty-column patterns. Thus the exact number of largest minimal dominating sets is \(4\) for odd \(n\) and \(6\) for even \(n\).
