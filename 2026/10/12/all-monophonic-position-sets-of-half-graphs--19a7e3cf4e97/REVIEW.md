# Review of All monophonic position sets of half graphs

## Correctness
PASS. Half graphs are \(2K_2\)-free, hence have no induced path on five vertices. Independent sets therefore meet every induced path in at most two vertices. Conversely, every selected edge together with any third selected vertex lies on one of four explicit induced \(P_3\)/\(P_4\) constructions, so larger admissible sets are exactly independent. The index-cut bijection gives the enumerator and maximum sets. Exhaustive verification for \(2\le n\le8\) agrees throughout.

## Originality
PASS. The defining 2020 source gives a triangle-free upper bound and several exact families but no half-graph or chain-graph theorem in the inspected searchable text. The scalar value is not used as the originality basis. Exact, alias, semantic, and own-ledger searches did not locate the all-set classification, enumerator, or maximum-set description.

## Value
PASS. The result upgrades a maximum-size question to a complete feasible-set description on the canonical twin-free chain graph. The sharp boundary between unrestricted pairs and larger sets forced to be independent yields every coefficient in closed form and gives a natural benchmark for extensions to general chain graphs.
