On the two-dimensional affine Lie algebra, all NDB forms are \(\begin{psmallmatrix}a&b\\-b&0\end{psmallmatrix}\) with \(b\ne0\), giving a one-parameter family of form orbits. Their induced left-symmetric algebras nevertheless collapse to exactly two isomorphism classes, represented over \(\mathbb R\) by \(\mathcal A_2\) and \(\mathcal F(-1)\).
