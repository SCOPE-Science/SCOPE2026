# Review of All nonlocal resolving sets of complete multipartite graphs

## Correctness
PASS. The only nonadjacent pairs in a complete multipartite graph lie inside one part. For any same-part pair, every third vertex has equal distance to the pair, so one endpoint itself must be selected. Therefore each non-singleton part contributes the exact independent condition that at most one vertex may be omitted. The minimum, product enumerator, and basis count follow without interaction between parts. Exhaustive direct distance checks on every connected complete multipartite isomorphism type through order \(10\) agree with the full classification and every enumerator coefficient.

## Originality
PASS. The defining 2022 source was inspected in full searchable text at the definition, subject classification, complete-bipartite characterization, and family coverage. It proves the complete-bipartite extremal case but contains no arbitrary complete-multipartite treatment. Targeted searches under complete multipartite, complete \(r\)-partite, Turán, and nonlocal-resolving terminology found no equivalent theorem. Related complete-multipartite results concern distinct resolving invariants and do not imply this nonlocal all-set classification.

## Value
PASS. The result upgrades a known complete-bipartite scalar endpoint to a natural arbitrary-part-size theorem and gives substantially more than the minimum: every feasible set, a factored generating polynomial, and all bases. The product formula gives exact benchmark data for stars, Turán graphs, and arbitrary complete multipartite graphs in the nonlocal metric-dimension theory.
