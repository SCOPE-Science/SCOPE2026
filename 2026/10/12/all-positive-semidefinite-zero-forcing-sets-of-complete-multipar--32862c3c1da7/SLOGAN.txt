For a complete multipartite graph, positive semidefinite zero forcing is governed by a sharp white-support rule: whites may lie in one part, or in exactly two parts with a single white vertex on one side and a blue vertex remaining on the other. This yields a closed enumerator for every positive semidefinite zero forcing set and recovers \(Z_+(G)=N-\max_i n_i\) as a corollary.
