For the linear-in-leadership opinion–leadership model, strictly same-sign nonzero anchor opinions imply exactly one equilibrium in that sign sector for every positive feedback ratio and every row-stochastic influence network, even reducible ones. An implicit scalar-root iteration converges to it under an explicit strict logarithmic contraction, and the equilibrium depends monotonically and smoothly on the anchors; these are equilibrium results, not claims of dynamical convergence.
