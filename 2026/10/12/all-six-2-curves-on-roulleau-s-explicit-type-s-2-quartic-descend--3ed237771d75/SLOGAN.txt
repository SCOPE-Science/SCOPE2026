For Roulleau’s explicit type \(S_2\) quartic over \(\mathbb Q\), the two printed quadric sections split over \(\mathbb Q\) into their four smooth rational quartics. These classes generate the geometric Néron–Severi lattice, so its Galois action is trivial; consequently all six \((-2)\)-curves and the third distinguished quadric descend to \(\mathbb Q\).
