For \(n\)-qubit GHZ states subjected to two independent amplitude-damping stages, flipping any proper nonempty subset of qubits between stages gives an exact binomial expression for genuine multipartite concurrence and a unique sharp first-stage damping threshold separating finite sudden death from survival through every incomplete second stage. For a fixed number \(m\) of flipped qubits, that threshold decays as \(\left(a/b\right)^{2/m}2^{-2(n-1)/m}\) as the number of qubits increases.
