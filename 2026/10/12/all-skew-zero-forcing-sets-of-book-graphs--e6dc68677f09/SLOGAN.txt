For the book graph \(B_n=K_{1,n}\mathbin{\square}P_2\), skew zero forcing is exactly a two-side hitting condition: an initial set works precisely when it meets both bipartition classes. Hence \(\mathcal Z_-(B_n;x)=((1+x)^{n+1}-1)^2\), with \(Z^-(B_n)=2\), \((n+1)^2\) minimum sets, and \(F^-(B_n)=n+1\).
