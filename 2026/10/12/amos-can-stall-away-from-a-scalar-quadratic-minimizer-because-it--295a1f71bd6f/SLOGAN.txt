At the source-permitted memoryless second-moment endpoint, Amos's own decay buffer grows quadratically on a scalar strongly convex quadratic, making its normalized-gradient displacement decay as \(t^{-2}\). The resulting total step mass is finite, and an explicit threshold \(X_\star\) separates boundary convergence to the minimizer from a whole half-line of initializations that converge to positive nonstationary limits.
