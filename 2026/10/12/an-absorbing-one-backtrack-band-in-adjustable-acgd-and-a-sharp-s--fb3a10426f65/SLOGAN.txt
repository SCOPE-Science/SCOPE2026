On a scalar quadratic, adjustable AdaStep has a whole absorbing one-backtrack band \(\gamma\in(\tau/\beta,\tau)\), with exact Q-factor \(1-\tau/(\beta\gamma)\); the feedback therefore balances backtracking work without identifying a unique curvature or rate. With the source defaults, it moves from two backtracks and an almost exact step to one backtrack and factor \(0.0669852397\ldots\), an exact cost-versus-contraction tradeoff.
