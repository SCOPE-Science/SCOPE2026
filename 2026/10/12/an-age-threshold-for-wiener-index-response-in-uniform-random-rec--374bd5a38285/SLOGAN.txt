For uniform random recursive trees, conditioning a new vertex to attach to an older parent changes the expected global Wiener index according to an exact harmonic formula; the effect reverses sign when the parent age is about \(i/e\), where \(i\) is the arriving label. The root degree and Wiener index have a strictly negative exact covariance from size four onward, with limiting normalized constant \(3/2-\pi^2/6\).
