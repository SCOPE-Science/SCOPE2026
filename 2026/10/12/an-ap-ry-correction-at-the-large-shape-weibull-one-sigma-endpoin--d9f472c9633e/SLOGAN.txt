For large Weibull shape \(k\), the one-standard-deviation central mass tends to the reflected-Gumbel value \(0.7237512675\ldots\) from below. Its exact first correction is \(-A/k\), where \(A=0.3754669303\ldots\) is an explicit positive constant containing \(\zeta(3)\), which gives an analytic right-endpoint recovery of the Gaussian comparison.
