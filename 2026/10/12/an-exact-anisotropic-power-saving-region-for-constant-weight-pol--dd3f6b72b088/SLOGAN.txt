For \(A=p^{a+o(1)}\), \(B=p^{b+o(1)}\), and \(C=p^{c+o(1)}\), the constant-weight bound of Fouvry--Shparlinski--Xi has an exact power-saving phase condition: with \(M=\max\{ka+b+c,2b+2c\}\) and \(S=b+\min\{a,b\}+2c\), some fixed moment parameter gives a power saving exactly when \(S>1/2+\max\{M-1,0\}\). This recovers their diagonal \(1/8\) threshold and reveals anisotropic tradeoffs invisible in the equal-range corollary.
