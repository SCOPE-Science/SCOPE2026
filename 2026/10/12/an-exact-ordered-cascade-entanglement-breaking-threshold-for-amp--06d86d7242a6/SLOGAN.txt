For two serial zero-temperature qubit amplitude-damping channels with survival probabilities \(b\) followed by \(a\), inserting a unitary \(U\) makes the cascade entanglement breaking exactly when \((1-a)(1-b)|\langle1|U|0\rangle|^4\ge b\). The full rotation threshold is sharp and direction-sensitive: one ordering of two damping strengths can be entanglement breaking while the reverse cannot, regardless of the inserted unitary.
