For real invertible planar sketch-and-project with a conditioned Bernoulli–Rademacher one-column sketch, the uniquely optimal probability of using a dense two-sign sketch is \(t_*=\sin^2(2\theta)\), where \(\theta\) is the leading right-singular-vector angle; it is independent of the singular-value ratio. Except at special aligned angles, this strict interior optimum improves the exact uniform expected-squared-error contraction guarantee over both fully sparse and fully dense sign sampling.
