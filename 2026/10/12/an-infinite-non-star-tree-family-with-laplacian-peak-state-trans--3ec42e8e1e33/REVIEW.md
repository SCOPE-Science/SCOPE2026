# Review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The proof decomposes the Laplacian into symmetry sectors and shows that only the fully symmetric sector contributes to the root-to-child mutual eigenvalue support. The resulting three eigenvalues, projector signs, gcd, and parity conditions are all explicit. The argument establishes the claim for every integer \(b\ge2\); the finite replay is corroborative only.

## Originality
**PASS.** The closest primary source, arXiv:2505.11986v4, reports only one non-star Laplacian-peak tree through order eighteen and explicitly asks whether infinitely many such trees exist. The older generalized-Bethe spectral literature gives spectrum machinery but no peak-transfer result. Searches over source identifiers, object aliases, parameter relations, equivalent parity formulations, and the current literature located no equivalent infinite-family theorem. A 2026-10-06 graph-operations preprint was available only at abstract level and remains a documented residual risk.

## Value
**PASS.** The theorem resolves a stated existence problem, identifies the isolated ten-vertex example as the first member of an infinite family, and gives exact transfer times and probabilities with an even/odd classification. The result is limited to root-to-child Laplacian transfer; the transfer distance is one and the probability decays with \(b\).

## Closest literature and limitations
Coutinho--Guo--Schmeits provide the peak-transfer criterion and the open problem. Rojo--Medina provide generalized-Bethe spectral infrastructure. Neither states this family or its parity theorem. The result does not address long-distance transfer, other pairs, adjacency dynamics, or a classification of all depth-two trees.
