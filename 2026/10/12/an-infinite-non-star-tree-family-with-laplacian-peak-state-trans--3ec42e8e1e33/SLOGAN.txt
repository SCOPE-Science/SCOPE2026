For every even integer \(b\ge2\), the depth-two tree with \(2b-1\) children at the root and \(b\) leaves below each child has Laplacian peak state transfer from the root to every child at every odd multiple of \(\pi\). These non-star trees form an infinite family, and the peak probability is \((4b/((b+1)(2b+1)))^2\).
