A vertex subset resolves the dodecahedral graph exactly when it meets at least three of the graph's ten antipodal vertex pairs. This determines the entire resolving polynomial, \(R_D(z)=(1+z)^{20}-1-10(2z+z^2)-45(2z+z^2)^2\), and classifies its \(960\) metric bases as one vertex from each of three distinct antipodal pairs.
