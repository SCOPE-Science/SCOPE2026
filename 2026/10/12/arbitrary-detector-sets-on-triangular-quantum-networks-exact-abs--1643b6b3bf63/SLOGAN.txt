For a continuous-time quantum walk on the triangular graph \(J(N,2)\), the eventual absorbing-subspace dimension for **any** nonempty detector set is determined exactly by the bipartite-component counts of the detector graph and its complement. Complete capture requires only \(\binom{N}{2}-N\) detectors for \(N\geq5\), two fewer than the ordinary zero-forcing number, and the theorem characterizes every placement that works.
