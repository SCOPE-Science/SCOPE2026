For the Fermat cubic, the nine canonical free arrangements obtained by adjoining an entire three-conic hyperosculation block have explicit sextic factors \((u-\omega^jv)^6-16w^3F\). Precisely three such free degree-nine arrangements are defined over \(\mathbb Q\), although each rational sextic is an irreducible polynomial whose three geometric conic components split only over the \(S_3\) field \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt[3]{2},\omega)\).
