The determinant fibers of all four-by-four Hankel matrices over \(\mathbb F_q\) are counted exactly by \(q^6+q^3[\rho_4(t)-1+(q-1)(\rho_2(t)-1)]\), where \(\rho_m(t)\) counts \(m\)-th roots of \(t\). This gives uniform fibers in characteristic two and a square/fourth-power stratification in odd characteristic, refining the classical singularity count.
