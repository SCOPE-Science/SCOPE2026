For opposite \(2\times d\) rectangular monomial ideals, the plane Hilbert-scheme torus edge exists over \(\mathbb Q\) exactly when \(2\mid d\) or \(3\mid d\), but exists over \(\mathbb R\) for every \(d\ge3\). For odd \(d\), the smallest possible degree of a number field defining an edge is exactly \((p-1)/2\), where \(p\) is the smallest prime divisor of \(d\).
