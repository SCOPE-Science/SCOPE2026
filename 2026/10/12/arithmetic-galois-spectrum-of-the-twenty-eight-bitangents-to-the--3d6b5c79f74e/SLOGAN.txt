The \(28\) bitangents of \(X^4+Y^4+XZ^3=0\) form arithmetic Galois orbits of sizes \(1,1,2,8,16\), with common field \(\mathbb Q(i,\sqrt3,\sqrt[4]{(3+2\sqrt3)/12})\) of degree \(16\). For every prime \(p\ge5\), exactly \(2\), \(4\), or \(28\) of them are rational, with natural prime densities \(1/4\), \(11/16\), and \(1/16\), respectively.
