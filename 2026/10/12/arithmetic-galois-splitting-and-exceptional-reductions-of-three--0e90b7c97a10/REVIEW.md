Quadratic-form determinants \(-256\) prove component smoothness, and third-conic evaluations \(2457=3^3\cdot7\cdot13\) and norm \(-3^5\cdot13\) prove that the resulting pair intersections remain transverse, disjoint and exhaustive for every \(p\ge5\) outside \(\{7,13\}\). Direct Galois action on the twelve explicit points yields the orbit structure and fixed-point spectrum; Chebotarev gives densities. The embedded verifier independently confirms the exact equations and checks the Jacobian point counts over twenty odd prime fields. Limitations: odd characteristic at least five only, exact exceptional baskets unspecified.

## Value — PASS
The node scheme is a canonical finite arithmetic object attached to the classical osculating-conic construction. The exact biquadratic field and complete fixed-point character reveal why only two nodes descend to \(\mathbb Q\), while prime reductions yield four distinctly sized rational node sets and two exceptional incidence characteristics. Those answers are not automatic consequences of the published geometric count of twelve nodes; the explicit Galois action and all-prime collision exclusion are needed. The fact is a complete natural arithmetic classification rather than an arbitrary finite test.
