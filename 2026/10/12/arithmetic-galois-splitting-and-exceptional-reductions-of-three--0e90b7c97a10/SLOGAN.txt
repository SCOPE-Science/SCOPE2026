The twelve nodes of the three hyperosculating conics of a distinguished nodal cubic split over the exact biquadratic field \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3},\sqrt{13})\), with arithmetic Galois orbits of sizes \(1,1,2,2,2,4\). Outside the exactly two exceptional primes \(7\) and \(13\) among primes at least five, the rational-node count is always \(12,6,4\), or \(2\), according to two quadratic characters, each case of natural density \(1/4\).
