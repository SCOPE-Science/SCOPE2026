For an odd open SSH chain with arbitrary positive losses at even-site indices \(2k\), the number of undamped one-particle energy modes is exactly \(2g-1\), where \(g=\gcd(M+1,\{k\})\) and the chain has \(2M+1\) sites. An excitation initially at even site \(2\ell\) survives with long-time probability precisely zero when \(g\mid\ell\), and \(g/(M+1)\) otherwise, independently of the positive hoppings and loss rates.
