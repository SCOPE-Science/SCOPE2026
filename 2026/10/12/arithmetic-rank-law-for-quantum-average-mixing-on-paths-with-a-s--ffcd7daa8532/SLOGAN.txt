A single nonzero on-site potential on a path changes its continuous-time quantum average-mixing rank according to \(\gcd(i,n+1)\): away from the center of odd paths, the exact rank is \(n-\lfloor(\gcd(i,n+1)-1)/2\rfloor\), independently of potential strength and sign. The central site on an odd path retains rank \((n+1)/2\); otherwise, full rank occurs exactly when the gcd is at most two.
