For every generalized Thue–Morse digit-sum word \(T_{b,m}\), ordinary reflected factors have an arithmetic sharp cutoff: the language is reversal-closed for \(m\le2\), while for \(m\ge3\) the cutoff is \(4\) if \(\gcd(b-1,m)\le2\) and \(2\) otherwise. In every larger-modulus case, reflection complexity agrees with factor complexity at every length at least \(3\), with an explicit gcd-dependent correction at length \(2\).
