# Scientific assessment

**Correctness.** The associative Nijenhuis identity on the unital square-zero extension forces the radical to be invariant. The unit identity gives a marked eigenvector of the restriction, and the remaining basis pairs prove sufficiency, in every characteristic. For fixed restriction, centralizer orbits on the distinguished eigenvector are indexed by the distinct primary Jordan block lengths, with explicit stratum sizes. Rational canonical theory gives the centralizer order; partition generating functions yield the cumulative similarity-class formula. Independently enumerated cases over F_2 and F_3 agree, but finite checks do not prove universal quantifiers.

**Relationship to existing mathematics.** The associative Nijenhuis identity and deformation framework are classical (Cariñena–Grabowski–Marmo; Uchino). Zou–Gao–Kang–Lü classify operators on complex associative algebras of dimension three but expressly do not classify their automorphism orbits. Achar–Henderson (Proposition 2.3), also citing Travkin, gives a broader bipartition classification of the enhanced nilpotent cone over general fields; this covers the marked-matrix orbit machinery after the restriction to an eigenspace. Nishiyama–Ohta studies enhanced adjoint representations and relevant generic/null-cone geometry. The classical marked-vector strata are not independently new. The new claim assessed here is the natural-algebra Nijenhuis reduction together with the all-rank, all-finite-field complete operator count and moduli census. No exact earlier combined theorem was identified in the inspected sources. Equivalent results may exist under pointed-module terminology; historical priority is not guaranteed.

**Value and scope.** These are motivated exact invariants of a canonical family of split local algebras: the raw number of solutions, the complete orbit number and every orbit size. They provide a reproducible baseline for comparison with deformed-product and other local-algebra operator classifications. The theorem does not classify arbitrary local Artinian algebras, higher nilpotency radical algebras or general enhanced adjoint actions.

**Literature.** Cariñena–Grabowski–Marmo, *Quantum Bi-Hamiltonian Systems*, arXiv:math-ph/0610011; Uchino, *Twisting on associative algebras and Rota-Baxter type operators*, arXiv:0710.4309; Zou–Gao–Kang–Lü, *Nijenhuis operators on 2D pre-Lie algebras and 3D associative algebras*, arXiv:2308.12121; Nishiyama–Ohta, *Enhanced adjoint action and their orbits for the general linear group*, arXiv:1703.08641; Achar–Henderson, *Orbit closures in the enhanced nilpotent cone*, arXiv:0712.1079.
