For the unital square-zero algebra \(\mathbb F_q\oplus V\), every associative Nijenhuis operator is a matrix with a distinguished eigenvector, and this parametrizes all such operators in every characteristic. Its complete automorphism-orbit count is \(q\sum_{j=0}^{\dim V}c_j(q)\), where \(c_j(q)\) counts ordinary matrix similarity classes, and every individual orbit size has an explicit Jordan-stratum formula.
