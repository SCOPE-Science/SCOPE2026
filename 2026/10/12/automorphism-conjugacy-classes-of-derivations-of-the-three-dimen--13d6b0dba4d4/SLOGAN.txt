For the three-dimensional null-filiform left Leibniz algebra over every \(\mathbb F_q\), the ordinary derivations have exactly \(2q+2\) automorphism-conjugacy classes when \(q\) is odd and \(3q\) when \(q\) is even. Explicit normal forms, orbit sizes, and the characteristic-two invariant \(ac+b^2\) completely explain the difference.
