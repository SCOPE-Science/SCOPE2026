# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In A_n(k)=k[x1,...,xn]/(xi^2) with char k=2, Frobenius squaring kills each element of the maximal ideal. Thus arbitrary radical images of generators define endomorphisms; invertible map on m/m^2 is equivalent to an automorphism by Nakayama and finite dimensionality. For a composition-idempotent F, its induced linear map T has splitting im(T)⊕ker(T). Lift an im-basis using F(ell_i), lift ker-basis using h_j-F(h_j); these are radical elements with a basis of linear parts, hence define an algebra automorphism conjugating F to P_r. Unique r follows from cotangent rank and P_r has vector-space rank 2^r. For q even, |Aut|=|GL_n(q)| q^{n(2^n-1-n)}; its centralizer at P_r imposes r image generators in B and s kernel generators in J, with block-diagonal invertible linear parts. The resulting exponent eta_{n,r}=r(2^n-2^r-2s)+s(2^r-1) and Gaussian binomial factor give every labeled rank count, extremes both 1. Independent exact orbit-stabilizer calculations produced n=2,q=2 [1,12,1], n=3,q=2 [1,448,3584,1], n=2,q=4 [1,80,1]. These bounded validations do not replace the arbitrary-n proof.

## originality

PASS

Epstein–Nguyen Theorem 6.4 concerns graded algebra retract monomial bases for irreducible monomial ideals. It provides preexisting graded retract isomorphism types, which the submission correctly credits; it does not classify non-graded endomorphism conjugacy under the full ungraded automorphism group or count all labeled idempotent substitutions. Berkesch et al. work on reduced toric face rings and differential-operator techniques, not these characteristic-two nonreduced rank distributions. A Resultary semantically close hit concerns Rota–Baxter operators of the 4D exterior algebra over F2; these are different operator identities. The present precise full orbit/rank polynomial was not covered in inspected primary statements.
