# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The radical-squaring identity in characteristic \(2\) guarantees that every choice of nilpotent generator images is a homomorphism. An invertible linear part guarantees an automorphism by Nakayama's lemma. Every idempotent endomorphism is conjugated to a unique coordinate retraction by images of basis lifts and corrected kernel lifts. The stabilizer is counted by the exact first-variable subalgebra and remaining-variable ideal, yielding the orbit-stabilizer rank polynomial. The executable finite-field checks independently match the claimed distributions for dimensions \(1\) through \(3\) over \(\mathbb F_2\) and dimension \(2\) over \(\mathbb F_4\). The proof, rather than the finite checks, covers arbitrary dimensions.

## Originality

**PASS with residual literature risk.** The closest direct prior theorem, Epstein–Nguyen Theorem 6.4 (arXiv:1301.3967v2), already covers **graded** retract isomorphism types for irreducible monomial quotients; those types are expressly excluded from the novelty claim. The surviving result includes arbitrary non-graded retractions, an exact full-automorphism orbit classification, and a labeled finite-field rank polynomial. Berkesch et al. (arXiv:2112.00266v2) analyze retract-based differential-operator structures of reduced toric face rings, rather than this nonreduced algebra's idempotent endomorphism counts. Other operator or automorphism calculations on different truncated algebras do not imply the stated rank coefficients. Equivalent results under a different name cannot be ruled out absolutely.

## Value

**PASS.** Algebra retracts encode split quotient maps and embeddings, and the precise number of such maps is not determined by the known graded isomorphism classification. The result gives a complete natural invariant in every embedding dimension, including all higher-degree non-graded corrections, with only powers of two as possible ranks and strongly asymmetric multiplicities already at dimension \(8\). This is an infinite-family classification and enumeration, not an isolated low-dimensional calculation.

## Boundaries

The classification depends essentially on characteristic \(2\). Computations are exhaustive only in the listed small cases; no infinite-range novelty or external certification is asserted.
