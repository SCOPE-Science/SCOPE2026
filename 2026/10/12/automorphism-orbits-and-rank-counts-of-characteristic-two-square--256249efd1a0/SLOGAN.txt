Over every field of characteristic \(2\), every unital retraction of \(k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]/(x_1^2,\ldots,x_n^2)\) is automorphism-conjugate to a coordinate projection and has linear rank \(2^r\) for a unique \(r\). For finite even fields, all labeled rank multiplicities have a closed Gaussian-binomial formula, including the unrestricted non-graded maps.
