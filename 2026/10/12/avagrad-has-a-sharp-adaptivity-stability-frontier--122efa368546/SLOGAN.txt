AvaGrad's root-mean-square normalization removes common scaling of its adaptive-rate vector, but not coordinate concentration. On an isotropic quadratic with bounded delayed second moments, the exact robust learning-rate ceiling is \(\alpha\lambda\le(2/\sqrt d)\sqrt{1+(d-1)[\varepsilon/(G+\varepsilon)]^2}\), interpolating sharply from the SGD ceiling \(2\) to the concentrated-rate ceiling \(2/\sqrt d\).
