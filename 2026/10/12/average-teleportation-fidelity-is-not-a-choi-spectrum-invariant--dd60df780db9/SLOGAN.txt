Average teleportation fidelity is target-relative, not a Choi-spectrum invariant: unitary channels all have the same Choi eigenvalues, yet \(F_{\mathrm{avg}}=(|\operatorname{Tr}U|^2+d)/(d(d+1))\). This gives an all-dimensional counterexample to the Choi-eigenvalue formula printed in Appendix B.4 of the 2026 wormhole-simulation review under either standard Choi normalization.
