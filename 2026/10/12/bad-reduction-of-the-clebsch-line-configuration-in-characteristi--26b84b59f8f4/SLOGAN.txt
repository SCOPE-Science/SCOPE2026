In characteristic \(5\), the Clebsch diagonal cubic acquires one \(A_1\) node and its \(27\)-line configuration folds to \(21\) lines in two natural \(S_5\)-orbits of sizes \(15\) and \(6\). The rational \(15\)-orbit survives, while the golden-ratio double-six specializes two-to-one onto the six node lines.
