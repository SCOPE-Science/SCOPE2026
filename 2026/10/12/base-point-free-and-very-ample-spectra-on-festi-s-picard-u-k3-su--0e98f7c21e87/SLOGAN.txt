On Festi's Picard-\(U\) order-three K3 surface, primitive ample classes of square \(2d\) are exactly the coprime factor pairs \(d=xy\) with \(x<y\); they are base-point free exactly for \(x\ge2\) and very ample exactly for \(x\ge3\). Hence the source's uniform class \(e+df\) always has the fixed section \(O\), while the first base-point-free and very ample squares are \(12\) and \(24\).
