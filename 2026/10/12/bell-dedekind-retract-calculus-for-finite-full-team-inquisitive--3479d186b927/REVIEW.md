## Value
The structural question is natural: how many distinct semantic subalgebras can arise by retraction, how are they nested, and how does their size depend on collapsed worlds? The exact answer links three familiar combinatorial families—partition lattices, Dedekind numbers, and Stirling-number rank profiles—inside one well-motivated logic-algebra construction. Unlike a small isolated census, it holds uniformly for every finite number of worlds and resolves all embedded retracts and all endomorphism-image ranks in the designated core-preserving category.

## Limits
The result is relative to a discrete underlying Kripke frame and a designated core. It does not classify all core-forgetting Heyting homomorphisms, and it does not generalize the established finite-poset duality.
