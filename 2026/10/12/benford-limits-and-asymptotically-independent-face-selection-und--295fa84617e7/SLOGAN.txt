For fixed-dimensional box fragmentation, independent coordinate cuts with the same logarithmic mean and variance—but not necessarily the same distribution—produce strong Benford laws for both the largest \(d\)-face product and the total \(d\)-frame volume under only a second-moment and nonresonance condition. More precisely, the maximizing face labels become uniformly distributed and asymptotically independent of the leading digits, while the two logarithmic digit phases differ by the explicit rotation \((m-d)\log_B2\).
