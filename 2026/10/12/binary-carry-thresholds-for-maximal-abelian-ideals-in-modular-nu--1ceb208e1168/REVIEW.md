# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

For the canonical binomial Zinbiel product, the integer coefficient identity verifies the Zinbiel law in all characteristics. Since left multiplication by \(e_1\) is one nilpotent Jordan block, every ideal is a tail. Lucas parity proves that the least degree of a nonzero product within the tail beginning at \(e_j\) is precisely \(j+t(j)\), with \(t(j)\) the least power of two at least \(j\). Solving that inequality yields the claimed unique largest abelian ideal and exact dimension. The bundled checker supplies independent finite replay, not an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS

Ceballos–Towers Proposition 4.2 is the closest exact object and invariant, but it assumes complex scalars, and its nonvanishing argument does not survive in characteristic two. Camacho and collaborators recall the arbitrary-field binomial normal form, but the inspected theorem does not give modular abelian-ideal dimensions. Targeted equivalent-formulation, broader-coverage, and exact-formula searches found no dominating statement. An equivalent result under modular shuffle-algebra terminology remains possible.
