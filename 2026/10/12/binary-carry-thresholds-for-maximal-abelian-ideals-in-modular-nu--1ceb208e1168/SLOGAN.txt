For the canonical \(n\)-dimensional null-filiform Zinbiel algebra in characteristic \(2\), the maximal abelian ideal is a uniquely determined tail, with dimension \(\min\{2^r,n-2^{r-1}\}\) for \(r=\lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor\). Binary carries explain the cutoff and produce an unbounded gap from the classical complex-field value \(\lceil n/2\rceil\).
