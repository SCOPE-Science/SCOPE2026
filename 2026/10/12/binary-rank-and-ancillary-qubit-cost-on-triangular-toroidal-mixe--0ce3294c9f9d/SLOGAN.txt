For the six-neighbor triangular torus on \(\mathbb Z_m\times\mathbb Z_n\) with \(m,n\geq3\), the binary adjacency nullity is exactly \(m+n+\gcd(m,n)-2\). Every orientation of its edges therefore requires precisely \(\bigl(mn-m-n-\gcd(m,n)+2\bigr)/2\) ancillary qubits in the standard mixed-graph commuting-stabilizer extension, with a greatest-common-divisor correction arising from diagonal winding orbits.
