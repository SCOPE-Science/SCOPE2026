For every smooth rational member \(C_\lambda\) of the Ciani pencil, the exact common field of its 28 bitangents is \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-\lambda-1},\sqrt{\lambda+2})\); generically the splitting cover is biquadratic and branches only at \(-2,-1,\infty\). This determines all rational members with 28 rational bitangents, the entire degree-\(1/2/4\) Galois stratification, and the real counts \(16/28/4\) across the pencil.
