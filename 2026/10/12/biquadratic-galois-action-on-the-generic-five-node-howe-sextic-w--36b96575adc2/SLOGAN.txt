The generic five-node Moriya–Kudo Howe sextic has a regular biquadratic Galois orbit of four nodes and one rational node. For parameters \((2,3,4,5,6)\), the splitting field is \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt{385},\sqrt{14})\), and good primes yield five rational singular nodes with density \(1/4\), otherwise exactly one with density \(3/4\).
