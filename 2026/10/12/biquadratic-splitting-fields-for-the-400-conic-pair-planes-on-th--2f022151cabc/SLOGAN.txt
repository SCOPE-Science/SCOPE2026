The 400 embedded planes cutting the Barth–Bauer quartic into pairs of conics have exact minimal fields \(\mathbb Q\), \(\mathbb Q(i)\), \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\), and \(\mathbb Q(i,\sqrt5)\) with multiplicities \(16,144,48,192\). Their complete Galois permutation action has \(16\) fixed points, \(96\) two-element orbits, and \(48\) four-element orbits.
