For a complete bipartite graph with a matching deleted, every induced forest with at least two chosen vertices in each bipartition class is precisely a four-vertex path, two disjoint edges, or a five-vertex path; a closed bivariate polynomial counts all induced forests by size and number of components. In balanced graphs with a nonempty deleted matching, equality of the numbers of induced \(P_4\) and \(P_5\) occurs exactly when the deleted matching is perfect.
