On the boundary-only system \(x^2\le0\), the exact I-LMM keeps \(x_k=0\) forever but can never satisfy its transformed zero-residual stopping test at finite slack. Its auxiliary coordinate obeys \(t_k\to-\infty\) and the residual tends to zero, exposing the exponential-slack equation as a strict-feasibility encoding rather than an equivalent representation of all non-strict feasible points.
