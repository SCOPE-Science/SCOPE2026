On a complete two-level finite S4 frame, bounded maps obey a sharp top-level rigidity law: with at least two maximal worlds, every nonconstant map permutes that level, whereas with one maximal world the lower level maps freely. This gives closed, distinct one-step, idempotent and eventual-image rank polynomials for the associated Boolean modal algebra, including exact retraction supports and a rooted-forest refinement.
