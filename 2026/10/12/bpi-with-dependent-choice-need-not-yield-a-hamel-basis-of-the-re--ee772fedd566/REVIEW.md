# Review

## Correctness

PASS. Zapletal proves, relative to \(\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC})\), the existence of a model of \(\mathrm{ZF}+\mathrm{DC}+\mathrm{BPI}\) with no \(E_0\)-selector, and explicitly uses \(E_0\) eventual equality for the Vitali relation. Larson--Zapletal prove in \(\mathrm{ZF}+\mathrm{DC}\) that a Hamel basis of \(\mathbb R\) over \(\mathbb Q\) yields a total \(E_0\)-selector. Applying the contrapositive inside Zapletal's model is valid because that model satisfies DC. The Hahn--Banach corollary uses the standard implication BPI \(\Rightarrow\) Hahn--Banach.

## Originality

PASS. The 2026 source itself does not formulate the Hamel-basis consequence, and focused literature searches for BPI/Hamel-basis, no-Vitali/Hamel-basis, and Hahn--Banach/Hamel-basis formulations did not locate an equivalent published separation. The older Larson--Zapletal implication supplies the bridge but not a BPI model with no selector. Earlier no-Vitali/no-Hamel models did not establish BPI. A 2025 discussion still described BPI \(\Rightarrow\) “the reals have a Hamel basis” as open, which is consistent with the absence of prior coverage found here.

## Value

PASS. This closes a natural weak-choice implication problem connecting three classical consequences of choice: BPI, Hahn--Banach, and Hamel bases. The result is stronger than merely producing another choiceless model without a basis: the model retains BPI and DC, so a substantial compactness/ultrafilter fragment of choice survives while the linear-basis conclusion fails.

## Closest literature and limitations

The closest exact ingredients are Zapletal's 2026 BPI-plus-no-\(E_0\)-selector model and Larson--Zapletal's 2017 implication from Hamel bases to \(E_0\)-selectors under DC. Beriashvili--Schindler provide related no-Vitali/no-Hamel phenomena without the BPI separation.

The conclusion is a relative-consistency nonimplication, conditional on \(\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC})\), and it uses DC in the known bridge. It does not decide the exact implication over bare ZF.
