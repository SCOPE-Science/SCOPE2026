On a constant biregular signed matrix gradient G=gQ with g nonzero, CAME's clipped raw update is exactly sgn(g)Q/sqrt(p); its residual-confidence factor is spatially uniform. The pre-learning-rate global RMS is independent of the support density p and tends to epsilon_2^(-1/2) for positive epsilon_2, while active-coordinate magnitudes gain the factor p^(-1/2) over dense support. The direction retains the sign of g; no stochastic-training convergence is implied.
