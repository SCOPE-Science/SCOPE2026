For any finite reflexive chain of S5 clusters, modal term functions are counted exactly by subset-noninclusion words that embed into the ordered cluster capacities and end at the final cluster. This yields an explicit full-clone census, including exactly \(4^h\) unary operations on the \(h\)-point reflexive chain and different unary clone sizes for the two three-point capacity orders \((2,1)\) and \((1,2)\).
