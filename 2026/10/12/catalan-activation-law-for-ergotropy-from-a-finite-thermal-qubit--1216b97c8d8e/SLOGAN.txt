For one energy-preserving interaction between a ground-state qubit and \(n\) identical thermal bath qubits, optimal ergotropy is governed by an exact Catalan sum: each even bath qubit adds no benefit, and each subsequent odd qubit contributes a positive Catalan term. Population inversion becomes attainable at finite size exactly above Boltzmann factor \(1/2\), with a sharp logarithmic-minus-logarithmic minimal-size law near that threshold.
