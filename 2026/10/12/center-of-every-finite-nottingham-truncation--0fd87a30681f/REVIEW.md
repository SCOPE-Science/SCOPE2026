# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reduces centrality to the first visible coefficient of a commutator. The coefficient \((r-s)a_rb_s\) is reconstructed directly from composition, including its degree and truncation boundary. The two test elements \(x+x^2\) and \(x+x^3\) detect every possible first nonlinear degree below the top two layers, and the boundary cases \(n=2\) and \(n=3\) are included. Exact exhaustive calculations over several small primes and truncation levels agree with every branch of the formula.

## Originality
PASS, with a stated residual risk. The recent source supplies the filtration commutator expansion and a power-commutator presentation through level \(15\), but does not state this center theorem. Johnson's lower-central-series result does not imply the exact first upper-central term. The inspected conjugacy-class and normal-subgroup metadata likewise do not state the congruence-dependent center formula. Searches under center/centre, Nottingham quotient, filtration, and the exact congruence pattern found no covering result. A prior truncated-brace socle result is not equivalent: at the congruence jump the multiplicative center is strictly larger than the first socle layer described there.

## Value
PASS. The center is a basic structural invariant for the same finite Nottingham quotients whose power-commutator presentations are under active study. The theorem gives a complete all-level formula and isolates a sharp arithmetic jump \(n\equiv3\pmod p\), immediately yielding the center size and top central layers without constructing a full presentation. This is a natural structural consequence rather than an arbitrary finite slice.
