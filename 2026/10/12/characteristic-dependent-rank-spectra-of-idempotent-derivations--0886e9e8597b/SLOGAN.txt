For the algebra of upper triangular \(n\times n\) matrices, composition-idempotent derivations have a complete diagonal-label classification; their exact finite-field rank enumerator is \(1+\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}q^{k(n-k)}t^{k(n-k)}\) in odd characteristic and \(1+\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}\binom{n-1}{k}q^{k(n-k)}t^{k(n-k)}\) in characteristic two. The extra binary labelings in characteristic two create an explicit, dimension-uniform jump in every rank multiplicity.
