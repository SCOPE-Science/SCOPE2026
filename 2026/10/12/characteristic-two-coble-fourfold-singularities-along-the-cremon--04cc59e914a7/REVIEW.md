# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The lead source gives the exact characteristic-two equation of \(\mathcal V\), the involution, the lifted Cremona–Richmond configuration and the \(\mathfrak S_6\)-action. In characteristic \(2\), differentiation gives \(G_w=q\) and \(G_{{y_i}}=wq_{{y_i}}+b_{{y_i}}\). A representative lifted curve annihilates the equation and all first derivatives identically; symmetry carries this to all fifteen curves. The normal quadratic form along the representative curve is computed exactly, and its polar determinant is \(t^2(t+1)^2\), proving nondegeneracy away from the three incidence parameters. At a representative incidence point the quadratic form factors into two distinct linear forms, and three explicit singular-curve branches have distinct tangents. The packaged verifier reproduces these calculations over \(\mathbb F_2\).

## Originality
PASS. Dolgachev states that the fixed locus is singular over the fifteen-line configuration but does not state that these lifted curves are singular curves of the ambient fourfold or compute their transverse local types. The classical Coble-fourfold literature establishes the Cremona–Richmond singular locus and small resolutions in characteristic zero, but that does not imply the local characteristic-two statement because the double-cover equation and its differential behavior change essentially when \(2=0\). Searches using the exact source, \(P_2^6\), Coble-fourfold, Cremona–Richmond, transverse ordinary-double-point, tangent-cone and characteristic-two formulations found no covering statement. A residual risk remains from older invariant-theory terminology.

## Value
PASS. The fifteen-line configuration is a canonical modular stratum in the source, not an arbitrary slice. Determining how the ambient GIT quotient is singular along it, and locating the precise jump from nondegenerate transverse quadratic cones to reducible tangent cones at the \((15_3)\) incidence points, provides local information directly relevant to resolution and degeneration questions for the characteristic-two Coble model.

Closest literature and limitations: Dolgachev's arXiv:2302.13101 supplies the characteristic-two model and fixed-locus configuration; Cheltsov–Kuznetsov–Shramov's arXiv:1712.08906 supplies the characteristic-zero Coble comparison. The claim deliberately does not assert a complete global singular-locus classification or a resolution in characteristic two.
