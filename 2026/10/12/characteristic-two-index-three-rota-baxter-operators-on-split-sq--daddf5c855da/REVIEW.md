# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The coefficient calculation is reversible for every field of characteristic two. The centralizer block form gives every stratum orbit and its exact size. The independent brute-force basis-pair check supplied with the package agrees for the first three radical dimensions over the prime field. The general case follows algebraically, not from finite sampling.

## Originality

PASS within the inspected scope. Guo–Rosenkranz–Zheng (arXiv:1402.3702v1) classify semigroup cases in characteristic zero. Farooq (arXiv:2605.15670v1, Theorem 3.4) treats the identical square-zero algebra only in characteristic zero and obtains nilpotency index at most two. The new characteristic-two index-three operators and complete orbit-size census are not covered by those results. Targeted published-result searches found a Rota–Baxter classification of a different exterior algebra over the binary field, not the same object. The result remains at risk of equivalent results under different terminology; no global novelty certification is claimed.

## Value

PASS. The characteristic-two index jump is a structural boundary for a natural algebra family; exact closed rank-stratified counts and complete group orbits identify its scope for every dimension and finite field, not merely a one-example counterexample.

## Limits

No claim is made for nonzero weights, nonsplit local algebras, or characteristic-zero novelty. Only self-review and reproducible finite tests have been conducted.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
