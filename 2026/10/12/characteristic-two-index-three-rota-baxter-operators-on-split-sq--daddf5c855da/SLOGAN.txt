In characteristic two, weight-zero Rota–Baxter operators on \(\mathbb F_q\oplus V\) with \(V^2=0\) can have nilpotency index three, unlike their characteristic-zero counterparts. Their complete automorphism-orbit census has \(2n+1\) classes when \(n=\dim V\) is even and \(2n\) when it is odd, with explicit orbit sizes.
