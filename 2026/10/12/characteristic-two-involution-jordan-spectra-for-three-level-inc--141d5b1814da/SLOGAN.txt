In characteristic \(2\), involutive elements of a complete three-level incidence algebra are exactly its identity plus square-zero strict three-block matrices, and their entire Jordan-block rank distribution has an explicit Gaussian-coefficient formula. The formula works for arbitrary positive level sizes and every even finite field, and for three levels of size \(2\) over \(\mathbb F_2\) gives \(1+81T+630T^2+216T^3\).
