# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The characteristic-two proof is symbolic and field-uniform. Four basis-pair identities force \(P(t^2)=0\), \(P(t)\in\mathbb F_qt^2\), and \(P(1)\in\mathbb F_qt^2\); bilinearity makes the basis check exhaustive. The automorphism group and conjugation action are derived explicitly. The standalone verifier exhausts all linear maps over \(\mathbb F_2\) and \(\mathbb F_4\), recomputes the full solution sets and orbits, and independently checks \(\mathbb F_3\) as a characteristic boundary.

## Originality
PASS. The strongest same-object prior source is Li–Hou–Bai (2007): its C12 algebra is exactly \(\mathbb C[t]/(t^3)\), and its table gives the complex weight-zero families. This is treated as genuine prior coverage of the nonmodular analogue. The paper's standing field is \(\mathbb C\), so it does not imply completeness in characteristic two, where a displayed coefficient \(2\) vanishes. Farooq's 2026 truncated-polynomial classification assumes characteristic zero and radical square zero. Targeted searches found no inspected source giving the characteristic-two \(q^2\) classification or the \(q+2\) automorphism-orbit classification. Residual indexing risk remains.

## Value
PASS. The cubic truncation is the first one-variable truncation beyond the square-zero radical case, and characteristic two is a natural singular boundary of the known complex C12 table. The result provides not only a count but complete normal forms, rank profile, square-zero operator structure, and the exact finite-field conjugacy quotient with invariant \(a/b^2\). This is a compact structural boundary theorem useful for positive-characteristic Rota–Baxter classification.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest exact-object source is Li–Hou–Bai (2007), over \(\mathbb C\). The closest broader truncation source is Farooq (2026), for characteristic-zero quotients by \(\mathfrak m^2\). The archive anchor Zheng–Guo–Rosenkranz begins the polynomial-algebra line and has primary MSC 16W99.
