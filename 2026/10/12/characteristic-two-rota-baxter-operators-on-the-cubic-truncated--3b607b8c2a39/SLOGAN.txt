For \(A_q=\mathbb F_q[t]/(t^3)\) with \(q\) even, every weight-zero Rota–Baxter operator lands in the one-dimensional socle \(\mathbb F_qt^2\), so there are exactly \(q^2\) operators and every nonzero one has rank one. Conjugation by algebra automorphisms reduces them to exactly \(q+2\) classes, with the mixed classes parametrized by \(a/b^2\in\mathbb F_q^\times\).
