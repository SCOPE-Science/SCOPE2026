On the radical-square-zero algebra \(K\oplus V\), weight-zero Rota–Baxter maps are exactly \(P(a+v)=au+L(v)\) with \(L^2=0\) and \(2L(u)=0\): in characteristic two the extra freedom produces \(P^3=0\) with \(P^2\ne0\), first possible in radical dimension two. An exact Gaussian-binomial formula counts every rank and nilpotency-index stratum over every finite field.
