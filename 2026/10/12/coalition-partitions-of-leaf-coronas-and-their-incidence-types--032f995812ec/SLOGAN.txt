For any \(n\ge2\), every leaf corona \(G\circ K_1\) has ordinary coalition number \(4\), independently of its core edges. Its maximum coalition partitions have exactly the three private-pair incidence types \(2K_2\), \(P_4\), and \(C_4\), and number \(2^{n-3}(4^n-5\cdot2^n+6)\) as unordered partitions.
