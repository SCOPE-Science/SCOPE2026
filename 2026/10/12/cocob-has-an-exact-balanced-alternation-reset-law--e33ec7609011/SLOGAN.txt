On perfectly balanced alternating outcomes, theoretical COCOB returns exactly to its initialization every other iterate, while the intervening excursion decays as \(0.4535905112\ldots\,n^{-3/2}\). Its source average telescopes exactly to \((1-P_{\lfloor T/2\rfloor})/T\sim1/T\), and the entire law is independent of the gradient bound \(L\).
