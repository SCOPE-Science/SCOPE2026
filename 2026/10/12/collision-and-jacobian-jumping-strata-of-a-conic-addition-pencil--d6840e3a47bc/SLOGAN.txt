In the smooth-conic pencil obtained from Măcinic–Pokora's four-syzygy example, the only singularity collisions occur at \(t=\pm1\) and \(t=\pm2i\), and these trigger different, exactly determined jumps in the degree-four and degree-five Jacobian syzygy spaces. Every nonzero member still has \(\operatorname{mdr}=4\), so no member is free, nearly free, or plus-one generated.
