On a complete quantum network, two walkers that react on meeting have exact capture dimension \(3n-1\) at unequal hopping rates but only \(2n\) at equal rates, for every \(n\ge4\). The exchange-symmetric input's capture probability stays \(2/(n-1)\) across the resonance, while the exchange-antisymmetric input switches sharply from \(2/n\) to zero.
