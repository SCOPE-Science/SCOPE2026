For a smooth rational normal scroll with smallest twist at least two, the gcd of every higher-dual degree before the first osculating break is exactly \(\gcd(d,2n)\), where \(d\) is the total degree and \(n\) the scroll dimension. Thus the entire higher-dual tower is primitive exactly when \(d\) is odd and \(\gcd(d,n)=1\), independently of the splitting type.
