For every fixed finite collection \(\mathcal A\) of integer bases at least \(2\), there are common blocks of length \(\gg_{\mathcal A}\log\log X\) up to \(X\) containing no integer \(p+a^n\) for any \(a\in\mathcal A\). The proof synchronizes the single-base sieve by placing one translated block in a macroscopic gap of the union of all relevant power sequences.
