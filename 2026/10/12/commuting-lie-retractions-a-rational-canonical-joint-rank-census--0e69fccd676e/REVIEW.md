# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem is a finite module-theoretic identity with an explicit classification of all idempotent Lie homomorphisms. Since the derived ideal equals the module \(V\), any endomorphism is triangular; the Lie condition is \(TN=cNT\), idempotence forces either the zero map or \(c=1\), \(T^2=T\), \(TN=NT\), \(Tu=0\). For two such maps, commutation is equivalent to commuting \(T,S\) and \((I-S)u=(I-T)v\). Four simultaneous module summands and orbit–stabilizer prove the bivariate formula. The primary-module endomorphism-ring radical calculation supplies the automorphism orders. The proof treats characteristic two, repeated primary factors and nonsemisimple actions; no empirical step is promoted to an infinite theorem. The included checker replays exact prime-field examples and prints VERIFY_OK.

## Originality — PASS, with limitations

Avetisyan's arXiv:1610.05365v2 (Section 4) establishes general homomorphism structure for almost-abelian algebras; arXiv:1811.01252v1 treats their Jordanable actions and commutants. These are prior coverage of much of the *normal-form machinery*. General commuting idempotents correspond to simultaneous splittings, and finite module automorphism orders are classical. Bergman–Nahlus arXiv:0910.5181v2 Section 11.1 discusses commuting projections but its Boolean-lattice theorem assumes \(A=AA+Z(A)\), which does not hold for the centerless Lie algebras under consideration. Huang arXiv:2404.19483v1 concerns counting commuting endomorphisms of modules, not Lie idempotent maps or the affine compatibility constraints. The 2021 finite-ring paper addresses single idempotent counts. The distinctive assertion not found in those inspected statements is the all-rational-canonical **joint rank polynomial of commuting Lie retractions** and its affine correction, with noncommuting pairs even when all induced module idempotents commute. This is an originality assessment, not a guarantee of worldwide priority; equivalent work under other names or a non-obvious implication of older enumerations remains possible.

## Value — PASS

A retraction is a split Lie-homomorphic projection, and commuting retractions encode compatible split decompositions. Their commutation statistics give a natural structural invariant of the endomorphism monoid, refining counts of single retractions. The theorem is complete across every dimension, every finite characteristic, every invertible rational-canonical action, and every ordered rank pair. It detects affine obstruction to simultaneous splitting even over a simple module, a distinction not visible from ordinary commuting idempotent matrices. The explicit squarefree specialization demonstrates how irreducible factorization affects exact commuting probability.

## Verification and risks

Direct exhaustive enumeration in small finite fields agreed with every checked bivariate rank coefficient. The accompanying proof, not the test data, justifies arbitrary dimensions and prime powers. The singular-action case is not covered. No external independent audit has been performed.
