For every finite almost-abelian Lie algebra with invertible defining action, the complete joint rank distribution of commuting idempotent Lie endomorphisms is an explicit rational-canonical module sum with an affine factor \(q^{\dim M-\dim M_{11}}\). Even when the action is irreducible, the induced module projections all commute but exactly \(q^{2d}-q^d\) ordered Lie-retraction pairs do not.
