Every abelian subalgebra of the two-step graph Lie algebra of a complete bipartite graph arises from a pure-part subspace or a mixed line, together with a central subspace and a linear lift. This gives an exact dimension enumerator over every finite field and shows that there are \(2+(q^a-1)(q^b-1)/(q-1)\) inclusion-maximal abelian subalgebras, many of them smaller than the maximum possible dimension.
