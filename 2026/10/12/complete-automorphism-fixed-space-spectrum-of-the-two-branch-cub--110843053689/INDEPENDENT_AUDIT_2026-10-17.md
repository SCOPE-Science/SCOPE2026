# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Automorphisms have P monomial 2x2 linear part, Q induced squares, and arbitrary socle B; dimension fixed equals 1+dim ker(P−I)+dim ker(Q−I)−rank(B|ker(P−I) modulo im(Q−I)). Enumerating possible (r,s) for diagonal and anti-diagonal P distinguishes ±1 in odd characteristic from characteristic two, and counting finite s×r matrix ranks yields coefficients and Burnside totals. Boundary q=2 and odd q=3 show nonnegative counts, all counts sum to group order.

## originality

PASS

Prior exact published same-object general automorphism normal form was read and credited. It does not contain the fixed subspace distribution N_j or element orbit number; these require classification of P,Q unit eigenvalues, rank of induced B:ker(P−I)→coker(Q−I), and Burnside averaging. Díaz–Lucchini Arteche studies same ideal but structural characteristic-zero derivations.

## value

PASS

The exact orbit/fixed-space fiber distribution of a canonical finite Artinian coordinate-cross algebra is a natural invariant distinct from automorphism-group order. It quantitatively distinguishes even/odd characteristic and is useful for enumeration of conjugate fixed modules, not an arbitrary small-case computation.
