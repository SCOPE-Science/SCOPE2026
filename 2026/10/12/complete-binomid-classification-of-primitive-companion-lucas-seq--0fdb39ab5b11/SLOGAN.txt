For the primitive companion Lucas recurrence \(V_{n+2}=PV_{n+1}-QV_n\) with \(V_0=2\), every generalized factorial binomial is integral exactly when \((|P|,Q)=(1,0),(1,1)\), or \((2,1)\). In each case the sequence also satisfies ordinary index divisibility, settling this complete companion subfamily of a published order-two binomid classification question.
