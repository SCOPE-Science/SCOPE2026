# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. Two independent exhaustive generators produce exactly the same \(2048\) order-\(5\) rotation classes. Two independent discrepancy calculations agree cycle by cycle. The exact histogram is \(5:224\), \(6:688\), \(7:912\), \(8:224\). The \(224\) minimizers decompose into \(60\) complement/reversal orbits, with \(8\) of size \(2\) and \(52\) of size \(4\).

## Originality
PASS. The minimum value \(5\) itself is prior work and is excluded from the novelty claim. The 2024 minimum-discrepancy paper reports attainable minima, not equality-layer counts or the complete distribution. The 2022 discrepancy paper studies selected constructions and explicitly lists the all-sequence distribution as future work. Exact-count and equivalent-formulation searches found no published order-\(5\) histogram.

Residual risk remains that unindexed or unpublished experimental output contains the same small-order census.

## Value
PASS. This directly advances a future direction stated in the discrepancy literature and refines the new minimum-value theorem from existence to abundance and symmetry. Order \(5\) is a natural complete finite layer: it contains \(2048\) cycles, enough for a nontrivial distribution while remaining exactly enumerable.
