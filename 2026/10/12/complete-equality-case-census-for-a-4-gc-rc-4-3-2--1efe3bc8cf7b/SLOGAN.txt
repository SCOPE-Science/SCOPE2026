For length-four DNA codes of GC-content \(2\) with Hamming and reverse-complement distance at least \(3\), the known optimum \(A_{4}^{GC,RC}(4,3,2)=6\) has exactly \(512\) labeled equality cases. Every maximum has a universal three-edge perfect matching of distance-four pairs, and the maxima form twelve \(32\)-orbits and two \(64\)-orbits under the stated \(64\)-element symmetry subgroup.
