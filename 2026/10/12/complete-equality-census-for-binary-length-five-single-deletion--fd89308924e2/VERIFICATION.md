# Verification

The package contains `verifier.py`, `expected.txt`, and `census.json`. Running `python verifier.py` reconstructs the binary length-five compatibility graph in two independent ways, exhaustively checks every six-subset and every seven-subset, independently enumerates maximal cliques, and verifies the six-code census, the three natural-symmetry orbits, the two perfect VT classes, and all shadow-size/coverage profiles.

The computation is exhaustive only for the finite domain of \(32\) binary words of length \(5\). It is not evidence for longer block lengths. The symmetry computation concerns the group generated by complement and reversal and does not certify that this group is the full automorphism group of the compatibility graph.
