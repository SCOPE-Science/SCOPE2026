# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof exhausts the six factorization shapes \(p,p^2,pq,p^3,p^2q,pqr\). Each divisor-count denominator is \(2,3,4,4,6,8\), respectively, and the corresponding numerator is reduced exactly modulo those denominators. The only mixed repeated-prime formula needed is \(\sigma(p^2q)+\varphi(p^2q)=q(2p^2+1)+2p+1\); its parity and modulo-\(3\) analysis is exhaustive. The squarefree three-prime branch is settled exactly modulo \(8\).

## Originality
PASS. A055464 is the exact public database object, but its inspected entry contains terms, a direct computational test, and an equality theorem rather than a classification by multiplicative complexity. The inspected De Koninck–Luca full text treats a different composition quotient of the same functions. Exact-condition, alias, prime-power, semiprime, repeated-prime, and squarefree-three-prime searches returned no statement equivalent to or stronger than the complete \(\Omega(n)\le3\) classification.

## Value
PASS. The first three multiplicity layers are a natural structural slice of an exact long-standing divisibility sequence. The result replaces raw table membership by a complete unbounded residue criterion for every possible prime-factorization shape. In particular, it exposes the different local moduli governing repeated and squarefree support, so it is more informative than a bounded census.
