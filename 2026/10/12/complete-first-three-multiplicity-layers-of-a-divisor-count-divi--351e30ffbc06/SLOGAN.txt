The first three \(\Omega\)-layers of \(\tau(n)\mid\sigma(n)+\varphi(n)\) admit a complete residue classification: prime squares are excluded only at \(3^2\), semiprimes are exactly the odd squarefree ones together with all prime squares except \(9\), and every \(\Omega(n)=3\) shape is decided by explicit congruences modulo \(3\), \(4\), \(6\), or \(8\).
