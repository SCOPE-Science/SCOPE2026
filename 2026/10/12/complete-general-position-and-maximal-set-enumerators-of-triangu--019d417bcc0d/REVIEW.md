# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** In a diameter-two triangular graph, the forbidden selected geodesic triple is exactly an induced three-vertex path. The edge-intersection cliques of \(K_n\) are exactly stars or triangles; disconnected cliques have disjoint vertex supports. Maximality is established by explicit legal additions in the three excluded cases and impossibility of additions in the two allowed cases. The labeled exponential formula counts each support partition exactly once, with center multiplicities handled separately for two-, three-, and larger-vertex stars. Independent direct enumeration through \(n=6\) agrees coefficientwise; finite computation is supplementary to the proof.

**Originality — PASS, with close prior structural coverage.** The 2019 line-graph theorem already gives the maximum cardinality, and the 2023 lower-general-position theorem explicitly identifies the shapes of maximal sets and their smallest size. The 2024 general-position polynomial paper treats the invariant on other families but does not provide the triangular graph's all-set or maximal-set enumerator. The proposed contribution is specifically the evaluated exponential generating functions and exact cardinality multiplicities, not the known structural shapes. Consequently originality is limited in scope: it is an enumerative refinement of prior classifications, and a prior unindexed enumerator remains possible. Searches of published mathematical records and exact/alias web searches found no evaluated equivalent.

**Value — PASS.** Full cardinality enumerators are canonical refinements of previously studied extremal general-position numbers, and the maximal-set polynomial quantifies the entire landscape of inclusion-minimal cluster-vertex deletion solutions, not merely an optimum. The paired generating functions give reproducible exact counts for arbitrary order without enumerating the exponentially many subsets of the triangular graph. Exact extremal multiplicities illustrate information invisible in the known minimum and maximum cardinalities.

**Closest literature and limitations.** Boral et al. (2013) establish the cluster-vertex deletion perspective; Ghorbani et al. (2019) and Di Stefano et al. (2023) provide direct geometric/structural predecessors; Iršič et al. (2024) introduce the enumerative polynomial framework. No independent mathematical audit has been performed. The structural ingredient is prior, and analogous enumerations may be differently indexed.
