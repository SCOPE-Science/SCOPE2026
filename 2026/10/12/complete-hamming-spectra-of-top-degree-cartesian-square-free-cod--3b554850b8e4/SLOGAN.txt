For the degree-\(m-1\) homogeneous square-free code evaluated on every point of \(\mathbb F_q^m\), each codeword's weight depends only on the support size \(r\) of its coefficient vector, giving the explicit complete enumerator \(W_C(z)=1+\sum_{r=1}^m\binom mr(q-1)^r z^{(q-1)^m-(q-1)^{m-r}N_r+r(q-1)^{m-1}}\), where \(N_r=((q-1)^r+(q-1)(-1)^r)/q\). For \(q>2\), exactly the first two support sizes share a weight, and all later weights strictly increase.
