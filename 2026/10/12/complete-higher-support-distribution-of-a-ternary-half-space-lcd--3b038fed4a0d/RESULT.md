# Complete higher-support distribution of a ternary half-space LCD family
## Finding

For every
\[
n\ge 3,
\]
consider the ternary LCD code
\[
C'_n=C'_{D_{F^{(2)}}}
\]
of Mondal--Lee Theorem 3.9, generated by
\[
[I_n\mid G_{D_{F^{(2)}}}],
\]
where
\[
F^{(2)}(x_1,\ldots,x_n)
=
(1+x_1)\prod_{i=2}^n(1+x_i+x_i^2)-1.
\]
Its length is
\[
N=n+2\cdot 3^{n-1}-1.
\]

The complete subcode-support distribution has a closed form.

Fix
\[
1\le r\le n,
\qquad
h=n-r.
\]
For integers \(m,h,s\), define
\[
E_{m,h}(s)
=
\binom{m}{s}
\sum_{j=0}^{m-s}
(-1)^j
\binom{m-s}{j}
\left[{m-s-j\atop h-s-j}\right]_3,
\]
where
\[
\left[{a\atop b}\right]_3
\]
is the Gaussian binomial coefficient, interpreted as zero when \(b<0\) or \(b>a\).

For every
\[
0\le s\le h,
\]
there are exactly
\[
E_{n-1,h}(s)
\]
\(r\)-dimensional subcodes with support size
\[
n+2\cdot3^{n-1}-3^h-s.
\]

When
\[
h\ge1,
\]
there are also exactly
\[
E_{n,h}(s)-E_{n-1,h}(s)
\]
\(r\)-dimensional subcodes with support size
\[
n+2\cdot3^{n-1}-2\cdot3^{h-1}-s.
\]

If the same numerical support size arises from both displayed families, the two multiplicities are added. These formulas account for every \(r\)-dimensional subcode.

In particular, the complete generalized Hamming-weight hierarchy is
\[
d_r(C'_n)
=
r+2\cdot3^{n-1}-3^{n-r},
\qquad
1\le r\le n.
\]
The number of \(r\)-dimensional subcodes attaining \(d_r(C'_n)\) is exactly
\[
\binom{n-1}{r-1}.
\]

For the source's Example 3.10,
\[
n=3,
\]
the published code has parameters
\[
[20,3,10]_3.
\]
The formulas refine its published ordinary weight enumerator to the complete distributions
\[
r=1:\quad
10:1,\quad13:2,\quad14:6,\quad15:4,
\]
\[
r=2:\quad
17:2,\quad18:3,\quad19:8,
\]
and
\[
r=3:\quad20:1.
\]
Thus its generalized Hamming-weight hierarchy is
\[
(10,17,20).
\]

## Assumptions and scope

The source defines, for a polynomial
\[
F=\sum_{g\in\mathbb F_3^n}\alpha_g
\prod_{i=1}^n x_i^{g_i},
\qquad
\alpha_g\in\{0,1\},
\]
the defining set
\[
D_F=\{g:\alpha_g=1\}.
\]

Expanding \(F^{(2)}\) over the integers shows directly that
\[
D_{F^{(2)}}
=
\{x\in\mathbb F_3^n:x_1\in\{0,1\}\}\setminus\{0\}.
\]
Write
\[
A=\{x\in\mathbb F_3^n:x_1=0\}.
\]
Then
\[
D_{F^{(2)}}=(A\setminus\{0\})\cup\{x:x_1=1\}.
\]

The code \(C'_n\) has one coordinate for every column of \(G_{D_{F^{(2)}}}\), together with one additional coordinate for each standard basis column
\[
e_1,\ldots,e_n
\]
coming from \(I_n\). Repeated columns are separate code coordinates and are counted with multiplicity.

## Proof

Let
\[
U\le\mathbb F_3^n
\]
be an \(r\)-dimensional message subspace, and put
\[
W=U^\perp,
\qquad
\dim W=h=n-r.
\]
A generator column is zero on the whole subcode generated by \(U\) exactly when that column lies in \(W\).

Let
\[
s(W)=|\{i:e_i\in W\}|.
\]
The number of zero coordinates of the corresponding subcode is therefore
\[
|D_{F^{(2)}}\cap W|+s(W).
\]

There are two geometric cases.

If
\[
W\subseteq A,
\]
then
\[
D_{F^{(2)}}\cap W=W\setminus\{0\},
\]
so
\[
|D_{F^{(2)}}\cap W|
=
3^h-1.
\]
Hence the support size is
\[
N-(3^h-1+s(W))
=
n+2\cdot3^{n-1}-3^h-s(W).
\]

If
\[
W\not\subseteq A,
\]
then the first-coordinate map
\[
W\to\mathbb F_3
\]
is a nonzero linear functional. Each fiber therefore has size
\[
3^{h-1}.
\]
The defining set retains the first-coordinate-zero fiber except for the origin and the entire first-coordinate-one fiber. Thus
\[
|D_{F^{(2)}}\cap W|
=
(3^{h-1}-1)+3^{h-1}
=
2\cdot3^{h-1}-1,
\]
and the support size is
\[
n+2\cdot3^{n-1}-2\cdot3^{h-1}-s(W).
\]

It remains to count subspaces by \(s(W)\).

For an \(m\)-dimensional coordinate space, fix a set \(S\) of \(s\) coordinate axes. The number of \(h\)-subspaces containing every axis in \(S\) and no other coordinate axis is, by inclusion-exclusion,
\[
\sum_{j=0}^{m-s}
(-1)^j
\binom{m-s}{j}
\left[{m-s-j\atop h-s-j}\right]_3.
\]
There are
\[
\binom{m}{s}
\]
choices of \(S\). Therefore the number of \(h\)-subspaces containing exactly \(s\) coordinate axes is \(E_{m,h}(s)\).

Inside the hyperplane \(A\), there are \(n-1\) coordinate axes, so the number of \(W\subseteq A\) with \(s(W)=s\) is
\[
E_{n-1,h}(s).
\]
Among all \(h\)-subspaces of \(\mathbb F_3^n\), the number with \(s(W)=s\) is
\[
E_{n,h}(s).
\]
Hence the number not contained in \(A\) is
\[
E_{n,h}(s)-E_{n-1,h}(s).
\]
This proves the complete support distribution.

For the generalized Hamming weights, note that
\[
s(W)\le h.
\]
If \(W\subseteq A\), equality holds exactly when \(W\) is spanned by \(h\) standard basis vectors chosen from
\[
e_2,\ldots,e_n.
\]
Thus the largest zero-coordinate count in the contained case is
\[
3^h-1+h.
\]

For \(h\ge1\), the largest zero-coordinate count in the noncontained case is at most
\[
2\cdot3^{h-1}-1+h,
\]
which is smaller by
\[
3^{h-1}.
\]
Therefore every generalized-weight minimizer lies in the contained case with \(s(W)=h\). There are exactly
\[
\binom{n-1}{h}
=
\binom{n-1}{r-1}
\]
such annihilators, and
\[
d_r
=
N-(3^h-1+h)
=
r+2\cdot3^{n-1}-3^{n-r}.
\]

## Verification

`artifacts/verify.py` uses only the Python standard library.

For
\[
n=3,4,5,
\]
it reconstructs the defining set directly as the coefficient support of
\[
(1+x_1)\prod_{i=2}^n(1+x_i+x_i^2)-1,

forms the systematic generator
\[
[I_n\mid G_{D_{F^{(2)}}}],

For every subspace it computes the support directly from the actual generator-column multiset and independently evaluates the closed distribution formula above. The two histograms agree in every dimension for all three tested values of \(n\).

## Relationship to prior work

Mondal and Lee introduce this ternary LCD family, prove its parameters, and determine its ordinary weight distribution. Their Theorem 3.9 gives the family
\[
[n+2\cdot3^{n-1}-1,n,3^{n-1}+1]_3
\]
and Example 3.10 gives the \([20,3,10]_3\) member. Their article does not discuss generalized Hamming weights or subcode-support distributions.

The same paper also contains a different systematic family obtained from the full ternary simplex defining set. The present family is not that construction: its defining columns occupy only the two first-coordinate slices \(0\) and \(1\), with the origin removed. This produces two distinct annihilator-intersection regimes rather than the single full-space regime of a simplex extension.

Li, Chen, and Qu give a general framework for generalized Hamming weights of defining-set codes and evaluate selected Bent and Semi-Bent constructions. A generic defining-set intersection formula does not itself evaluate the present systematic extension, whose generator additionally contains the identity columns and whose complete distribution depends on exact counts of coordinate axes inside annihilator subspaces.

Pan, Chen, Liu, and Liu determine complete subcode-support distributions for several Griesmer, modified Solomon--Stiffler, and simplex-complement constructions. Their explicit families are based on projective simplex matrices and prescribed subspace deletions. The present generator instead consists of a nonprojective half-space defining set plus systematic identity columns; no specialization of their displayed families to this code was located.

Focused searches using the source DOI, Theorem 3.9, the half-space description, the closed hierarchy formula, and subcode-support terminology found no prior statement of the formulas above.

## Limitations

The result concerns the exact family \(C'_{D_{F^{(2)}}}\) of Theorem 3.9. It does not automatically extend to the other binary or ternary LCD families in the source.

The formula classifies subcodes by support size, not by monomial-equivalence or automorphism-group orbit.

The source's ordinary weight distribution remains prior work. The new claim is the all-dimensional support distribution, generalized Hamming hierarchy, and exact minimizer multiplicities.

A differently presented equivalent construction could exist in unindexed literature; no such coverage was found in the inspected sources.
