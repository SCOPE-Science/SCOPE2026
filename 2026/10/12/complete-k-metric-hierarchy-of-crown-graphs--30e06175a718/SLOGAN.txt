For the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M\), the entire \(k\)-metric ladder is controlled by the deleted matched pairs: the dimensions for \(k=1,2,3,4\) are \(n-1,n,2n-1,2n\). The minimum bases have pair-occupancy profiles respectively ‘one hole’, ‘one from every pair’, ‘all but one pair doubled’, and ‘all vertices’, giving exact counts \(n2^{n-1},2^n,2n,1\).
