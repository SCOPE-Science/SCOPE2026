Every maximal matching of the labeled prism \(C_n\square K_2\) is counted by an explicit bivariate rational/logarithmic generating function for all \(n\ge3\). The smallest ones have \(\lceil2n/3\rceil\) edges and occur in exactly \(6\), \(2n\), or \(n(5n-1)/3\) configurations according as \(n\equiv0,1,2\pmod3\); the ungraded total was previously known.
