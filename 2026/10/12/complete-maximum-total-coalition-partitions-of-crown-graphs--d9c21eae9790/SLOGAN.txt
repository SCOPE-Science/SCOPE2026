For every \(n\ge5\), all maximum total-coalition partitions of the crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M\) have exactly two nontrivial triple blocks of opposite bipartition profiles and all remaining blocks singleton. They number \(\binom n2\,n(n-2)\binom{n-1}{2}\), and every corresponding coalition graph is the same double star; the scalar optimum was already known through a different domination parameter.
