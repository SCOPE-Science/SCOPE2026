Every complex six-line–conic arrangement with exactly three nodes and four ordinary quadruple points belongs to a single \(j\)-line of complete-quadrangle configurations, and every member is free with exponents \((2,5)\). Projective symmetry jumps from \(C_2\times C_2\) to \(D_4\) at \(j=1728\) and to \(A_4\) at \(j=0\).
