# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The claimed variables and rate normalization agree with [1]. The key published level-size expectation has the exact binomial form used in the proof. Because the coalescent holding times are independent of all merger pair choices, only their own variances contribute to covariance with tree height. Pascal's identity yields every alternating finite difference, including a strictly positive first surviving summand; neither positivity nor higher-order monotonicity rests on finite data. Uniform stagewise domination by \(8/[k(k-1)]\) verifies the full macroscopic limit, including endpoint sequences. An independent rational merger-state enumeration agrees for \(n=2,\ldots,8\); it also checks the two deterministic tree consistency identities. The proof does not extend to non-Kingman demographic structures.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

The finite branch-size expectation, the standard \(2/r\) mean, the external-height covariance \(4/n\), and general phase-type numerical moment methods are already known [1–4]. Those results are explicitly excluded from the proposed novelty. The positive, strict complete-monotonicity hierarchy for all descendant-size indices and the nontrivial macroscopic \(r/n\) covariance profile do not appear among the material inspected from [1] and [2]. Sections of [3] and [4] describe much more general finite moment computation, but no identified statement classifies the sign of every frequency difference or supplies this continuum profile. A broader unpublished or historical derivation could still contain it; neither failed searching nor domain partition establishes global uniqueness. The original finite covariance formula is not independently novel where it is a direct consequence of the earlier expectation identity.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Age is a global timescale, while the entire branch-order sequence is the natural site-frequency decomposition of a genealogy. The theorem resolves a structural question for *all* frequency classes rather than a hand-picked finite value, establishing a hierarchy of sign constraints much stronger than positivity and a sharp macroscopic scale whose coefficient varies continuously from \(4\) to \(2\). The exact endpoint bounds and the profile remain meaningful without any claimed downstream inference theorem. Computation and correctness alone are not the basis of this value judgment.
