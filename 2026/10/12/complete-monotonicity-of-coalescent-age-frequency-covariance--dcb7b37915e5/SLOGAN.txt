In the Kingman coalescent, covariance between genealogy age and branches subtending exactly \(r\) leaves has strictly alternating finite differences of *every* order as \(r\) ranges over all descendant frequencies. When \(r/n\to\alpha\), the scaled covariance \(n\operatorname{Cov}(H_n,L_{n,r})\) follows an explicit decreasing profile from \(4\) to \(2\).
