Omitting one CCZ gate from a complete three-uniform hypergraph state has an exact five-term binary-rank spectrum, giving the entire Pauli-expectation magnitude distribution and every stabilizer Rényi moment in closed form. For at least six qubits the omitted gate strictly increases order-two magic, whose large-size limit is \(\log_2(256/11)\), rather than the intact state's \(3\); the isolated second moment is already implied by earlier ensemble results, while the full rank histogram is the new classification.
