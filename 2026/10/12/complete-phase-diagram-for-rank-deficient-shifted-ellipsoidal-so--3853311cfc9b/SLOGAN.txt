For a rank-deficient shifted ellipsoidal SOCP, the decisive test is \(b\in\operatorname{Range}(H)\): if it fails, a nullspace ray makes the objective unbounded for every \(a\); if it holds, the positive-definite phase diagram survives with \(H^{-1}\) replaced by \(H^\dagger\), while strict-case optimizers form an affine nullspace family. The nonzero attainment boundary still has dual attainment and zero gap but no primal optimizer.
