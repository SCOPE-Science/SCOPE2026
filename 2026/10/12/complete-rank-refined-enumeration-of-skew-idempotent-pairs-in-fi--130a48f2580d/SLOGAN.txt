For every finite full transformation monoid \(T_n\), an explicit finite binomial sum gives the exact number of ordered skew idempotent pairs in every pair of image ranks, with all orientation choices specified. The rank-\((2,2)\) stratum has exactly \(\frac{n(n-1)(n-2)(n+1)}4\,4^{n-3}\) pairs for \(n\ge3\); the total at \(n=4\) is \(204\).
