For ordinary pentagonal numbers \(P(n)=n(3n-1)/2\), the semigroup generated by \(P(n),P(n+1),P(n+2)\) is symmetric exactly for \(n=2,3,5,40\). The last exception \(\langle2380,2501,2625\rangle\) arises from a non-obvious common factor \(35\) of its outer generators; every index \(n\ge41\) is nonsymmetric.
