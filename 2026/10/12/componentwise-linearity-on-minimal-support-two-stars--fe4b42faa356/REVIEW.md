# Review of Componentwise linearity on minimal support-two stars

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reduces necessity to exact two-generator restrictions using the published generator-deletion proposition for componentwise linear monomial ideals. For two generators, the degree of the later generator is the only new critical component: its intersection with the padded earlier principal ideal is itself principal, and the exact degree computation forces \(a=1\) and the monotonicity of the center exponent. Sufficiency is stronger than required: the exponent condition gives an explicit nondecreasing-degree ordering with every colon generated by leaf variables, hence linear quotients and componentwise linearity. The standard fact that \(\mathfrak m J\) preserves a linear resolution is used only to pass between adjacent homogeneous components.

The bundled replay checks the exact two-leaf arithmetic and the global pair-to-star reduction on thousands of bounded exponent tuples. It ends with `CHECK_OK`. The finite run is not extrapolated to the infinite theorem.

## Originality
PASS. The closest 2026 source proves only a co-chordality necessary condition for componentwise linear support-two ideals and classifies the stricter single-linear-resolution problem; its minimal-star linear family has one common center exponent. The 2023 and 2025 weighted-oriented papers do characterize componentwise linearity for several graph classes, including bipartite graphs, but their generators have vertex-consistent weights and therefore do not contain arbitrary exponent pairs \((a_i,b_i)\) on the edges of a star. Targeted published-finding corpus and web searches for the exact arbitrary-exponent star statement did not locate a covering theorem. Dao--Suresh-Babu's sum criteria are broader in method but do not state this classification.

Residual risk remains because an equivalent criterion might appear under a different weighted-monomial formalism, and the motivating paper is very recent.

## Value
PASS. The recent support-two paper explicitly identifies componentwise linearity as the harder direction and gives only a graph-theoretic necessary condition in full generality. The star is the first natural co-chordal base graph on which the edge exponents remain genuinely free. The theorem closes that entire infinite family with a field-independent criterion and reveals a sharp structural boundary: arbitrary center exponents are allowed when all leaf exponents are \(1\), while only one heavier leaf is possible and it must sit on a maximal center exponent. The linear-quotient order also provides a usable certificate rather than only a yes/no test.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest sources are arXiv:2609.39165v1, arXiv:2309.16810v1, and arXiv:2509.14637v1. The result is limited to minimal support-two star ideals and does not claim a classification for general co-chordal graphs or non-minimal ideals.
