In uniform set partitions with \(k/n\to\alpha\in(0,1)\), singleton and size-\(j\) block counts have an explicit limiting covariance whose sign crosses once in \(\alpha\) for each fixed \(j\ge3\); for pairs \(j=2\) it remains negative. The critical fractions strictly decrease with \(j\), starting at \(\alpha_3=0.5464068225505325\ldots\), and satisfy \(j\alpha_j\to1\).
