For every equiprobable pure-state unit-norm tight frame of \(n\) states in \(\mathbb C^d\), the conditional-subentropy conjecture holds with an exact gap: \(H_{\min}(X|B)-Q(X|B)=H_n-H_d\). The result is strict for every overcomplete frame \(n>d\) and needs neither equiangularity nor group covariance.
