For every non-crown graph obtained from a biclique by deleting a nonempty matching, every constant sequence \(S_q=(q^\infty)\) with \(2\le q\le m\) has spectrum exactly \(\{2\}\), while \(q>m\) is not realized. Mixed-shore color classes can only be deleted matched pairs, and requiring at least two color-dominating vertices forces such a class to propagate to a full balanced matching, which is precisely the excluded endpoint.
