Equal loss and gain rates at each fermionic site do not protect entanglement clocks when the balanced rates differ between sites: the two-site Kitaev chain has a sharp contrast threshold at \(|\gamma_2-\gamma_1|=4J\), beyond which only the faster-damped site has a finite parity crossing. The first twisted-negativity spectrum closing nevertheless survives every finite contrast, moves strictly earlier, and has the exact \(\arccos/\operatorname{arccosh}\) law derived in the accompanying theorem.
