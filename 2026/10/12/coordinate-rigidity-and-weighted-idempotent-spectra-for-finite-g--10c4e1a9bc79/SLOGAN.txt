Every homomorphism between finite products of Gödel-chain EARS reducts is coordinatewise either constant \(1\) or a nonconstant homomorphism of one input chain, giving an exact product hom-set formula and \(|\operatorname{End}(A_m^r)|=(1+r2^{m-2})^r\). Idempotents are weighted rooted-star forests, with exact exponential generating function \(\sum_r I_{m,r}z^r/r!=\exp(z+z\sum_{p=0}^{m-2}e^{w_{m,p}z})\), where \(w_{m,p}=\sum_{k=0}^p\binom{m-2}{k}\).
