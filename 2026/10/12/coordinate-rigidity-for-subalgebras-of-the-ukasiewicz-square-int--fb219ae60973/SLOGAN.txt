For the Łukasiewicz-square reduct \(([0,1],\vee,\neg,\ast)\), every homomorphism between arbitrary standard-interval subalgebras fixes each truth value: the entire hom-set is either one inclusion or empty. Thus even exotic finite subalgebras have no nonidentity endomorphisms, and finite full-chain morphisms exist uniquely exactly when one denominator divides the other.
