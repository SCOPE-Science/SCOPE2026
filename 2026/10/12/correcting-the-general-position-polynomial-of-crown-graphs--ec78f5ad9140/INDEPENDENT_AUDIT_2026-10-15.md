# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For connected Cr_n=K_{n,n} minus matching (n≥3), any ordinary general-position set of size at least 3 in a bipartite graph cannot contain an edge: for an edge uv and any third selected w, shortest-path parity forces one endpoint onto a geodesic. A mixed independent triple cannot exist in Cr_n because any cross-part nonedge is a matched pair a_i,b_i and every third vertex is adjacent to one of them. All one-sided subsets and every two-set therefore give exact psi=2(1+z)^n−1+n²z². For 2-position, common neighbors of each pair of selected same-side vertices restrict the opposite-side indices to the two selected labels. The complete mixed admissible patterns are (1,1), (2,1) with singleton matched to one of two indices, its symmetric case, and (2,2) with identical two index sets. Thus gp2 adds 2n(n−1)z³+binom(n,2)z⁴. Independent all-pairs shortest-path and all-subset enumeration for n=3,4,5,6 verified both polynomials coefficientwise. The published 2024 T*(n,2) polynomial is exactly gp2, not ordinary gp. Extremal gp2 size max(n,4), and the n=3,4,n≥5 descriptions follow.
