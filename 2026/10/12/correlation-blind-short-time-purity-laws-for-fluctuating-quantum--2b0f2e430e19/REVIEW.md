# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The source's effective generator is a covariance-weighted Hermitian double commutator. For a pure state, differentiating purity converts that dissipator exactly into \(2J^2\Delta t\operatorname{Tr}(C K_\psi)\). For a localized state, all exchange expectations vanish, squared edge operators contribute exactly on incident edges, and cross-edge anticommutator diagonal elements vanish. For Haar-random pure states, the standard two-copy moment identity combined with \(\operatorname{Tr}(V_eV_f)=2\delta_{ef}\) gives \(\mathbb E K_\psi=2I/(N+1)\). The quantifiers and switching-order scope are explicit.

## Originality
PASS. The anchor derives the covariance-controlled Lindbladian and gives a special pair of covariance orientations with identical finite-time purity, but it does not state a general localized-state or Haar-averaged initial-slope no-go theorem. General channel-purity Haar formulas are older and credited, and random-Lindbladian decoherence-rate work studies Gaussian/Ginibre operator ensembles rather than edge-covariance geometry. Claim-specific published-result searches found no statement implying the two topology-specific laws. The principal residual risk is indexing lag for an anchor only days old.

## Value
PASS. The source's central distinction is between covariance strengths and covariance geometry. The finding identifies a measurement boundary for that distinction: even access to every localized initial purity slope, or to a basis-independent Haar-averaged slope, cannot recover any off-diagonal topology correlation. This gives a concrete reason to use coherence- or sector-resolved observables when the target is fluctuation geometry, while carefully avoiding a false generalization to finite-time purity.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest same-object source is arXiv:2610.03194v1; its exact global/community example has equal finite-time purity but does not imply the arbitrary-covariance slope identities. Zanardi--Lidar (2004) supplies general Haar channel-purity machinery, and Yang--Xu--del Campo (2024) supplies random-Lindbladian decoherence-rate context. The present result is limited to the source's leading rapid-switching generator and one-excitation exchange operators; higher-order switching corrections and generic coherent probes remain outside the claim.
