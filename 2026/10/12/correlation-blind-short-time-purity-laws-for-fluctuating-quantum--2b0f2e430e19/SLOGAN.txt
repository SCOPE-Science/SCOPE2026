For the rapid-switching fluctuating-network Lindbladian, every localized one-excitation probe has initial purity loss \(R_v=2J^2\Delta t\sum_{e\ni v}p_e(1-p_e)\), while the Haar mean is \(\mathbb E R=4J^2\Delta t\operatorname{Tr}(C)/(N+1)\). Both observables erase all off-diagonal edge correlations, so short-time purity alone cannot identify covariance geometry in these probe classes.
