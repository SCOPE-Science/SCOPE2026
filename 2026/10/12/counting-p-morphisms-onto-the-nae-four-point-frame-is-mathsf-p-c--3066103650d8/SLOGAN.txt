For the fixed four-point Kripke frame \(r\to c\to\{t,f\}\), surjective p-morphisms from a monotone-\(\operatorname{NAE}_3\) incidence frame are in exact bijection with satisfying Boolean assignments. Thus counting those p-morphisms is \(\#\mathsf{P}\)-complete even on rooted irreflexive acyclic inputs whose longest directed path has two edges.
