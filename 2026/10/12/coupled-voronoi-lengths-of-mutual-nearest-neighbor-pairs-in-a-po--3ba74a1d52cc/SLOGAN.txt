For mutually nearest neighboring sites of a stationary Poisson process on the line, their complete *Voronoi-cell lengths* are two independent rate-\(2\lambda\) exponentials plus one shared rate-\(3\lambda\) exponential, giving correlation \(4/13\) rather than the ordinary adjacent-cell correlation \(1/2\). A Palm-typical site's probability of being in such a pair, conditional on cell length \(v\), is exactly \((1-e^{-\lambda v})/(\lambda v)\), a strictly decreasing function.
