For ferromagnetic Ising stars with nonidentical positive bonds bounded uniformly away from zero, the empirical Lee–Yang zeros converge to an explicit Poisson-kernel mixture that uniquely determines the limiting law of the exponential interaction parameter \(q=e^{-2J}\). Finite zero spectra recover every fixed moment of this law with exponentially small unnormalized error, and the closest zeros obey an exact asymptotic spacing law.
