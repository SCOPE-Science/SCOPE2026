For the eight three-letter patterns in a biased iid coin sequence, all twenty-eight pairwise first-occurrence-time covariances have an exact sign phase diagram: seven stay positive, three stay negative, fourteen change sign once, and four change sign twice. The HHT/HTT pair reverses from positive to negative and back at precisely the two roots in \((0,1)\) of \(-p^3+6p^2-5p+1=0\).
