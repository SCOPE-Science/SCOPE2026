Under every fully specified continuous null, Cramér–von Mises and Anderson–Darling statistics satisfy \(\operatorname{Cov}(W_n^2,A_n^2)=(2n-1)/(18n)\) exactly. Their correlation converges to \(\sqrt5/[3\sqrt{2\pi^2/3-6}]=0.9789229548\ldots\), quantifying the strong redundancy of the two classical quadratic EDF tests.
