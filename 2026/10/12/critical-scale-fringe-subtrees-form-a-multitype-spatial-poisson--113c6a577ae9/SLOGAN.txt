At the critical \(\sqrt n\) subtree-size scale, any fixed collection of distinct fringe-subtree sizes in a uniform random binary search tree has an independent marked spatial Poisson limit, even when the size ratios coalesce. A uniform \(O(n^{-1/2})\) total-variation bound holds against the matching discrete Poisson field, resolving the critical-range joint-independence conjecture of Dennert and Grübel (2010).
