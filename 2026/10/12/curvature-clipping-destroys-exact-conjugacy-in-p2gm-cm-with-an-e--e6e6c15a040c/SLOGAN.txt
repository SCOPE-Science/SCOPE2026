In the latest P2GM_CM implementation, clipping the directional Rayleigh quotient generally breaks the exact Hessian conjugacy that motivates the two-dimensional subspace decomposition: \(v^\top H\widetilde s=(s^\top Hv)(1-r/q)\). An accepted two-dimensional quadratic trajectory realizes the defect explicitly, while using the unclipped sampled curvature only in the orthogonalization coefficient restores exact positive-curvature conjugacy.
