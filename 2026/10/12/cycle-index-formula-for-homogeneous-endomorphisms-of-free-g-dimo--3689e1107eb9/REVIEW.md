# Self-review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The proof reconstructs the fixed-point count from the explicit free-object model and the full automorphism action. A \(d\)-cycle of generators contributes one freely chosen length-\(k\) image fixed by the \(d\)-th power of the permutation, giving \(2^{k-1}F_d^k\) choices. Multiplying over source cycles and applying Burnside's lemma gives the partition formula. The finite checker independently enumerates the six cases \(1\le r\le3\), \(1\le k\le2\) and agrees exactly.

The finite enumeration is not evidence for the infinite theorem; the symbolic argument supplies all quantifiers and all \(r,k\ge1\).

## Originality
**PASS.** The closest primary source gives the exact same free g-dimonoid model, proves \(\operatorname{Aut}(D_r)\cong S_r\), and studies determinability from the endomorphism semigroup, but does not enumerate homogeneous endomorphism orbits. Targeted searches covered conjugacy classes, automorphism orbits, homogeneous length layers, cycle indices, Burnside formulas, and the functional-digraph specialization. The related commutative g-dimonoid paper studies automorphisms of the endomorphism semigroup itself and does not imply this result.

Residual risk remains that an equivalent formula may appear in older transformation-semigroup terminology or in the 2014 construction paper, whose separate full text was not required for the proof and was not fully inspected.

## Value
**PASS.** The count is attached to a canonical grading of the free object and to its full automorphism action, not to an arbitrary parameter slice. It gives a closed cycle-index enumerator for symmetry types of endomorphisms, recovers the classical unlabeled functional-digraph problem at \(k=1\), and proves that for fixed rank almost every long-image endomorphism has trivial relabeling symmetry.
