For the rank-\(r\) free g-dimonoid, homogeneous endomorphisms whose generator images all have length \(k\) have an exact automorphism-conjugacy count given by a partition cycle-index formula. At \(k=1\) it reduces to unlabeled functional digraphs, while for fixed \(r\) almost all large-\(k\) endomorphisms have trivial relabeling symmetry.
