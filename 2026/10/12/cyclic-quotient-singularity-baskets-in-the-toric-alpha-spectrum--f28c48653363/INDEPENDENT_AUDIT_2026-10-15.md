# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The six primitive rays are permutations of (-A,B,A-B) (three rays only at the coprime endpoint (2,1)). For A<2B, each facet cone is generated by the two explicit integer vectors in RESULT.md. Their positive determinants are m_-=A(2B-A) and m_+=B(2A-B). The Bezout basis gives q=-p mod m, hence both quotient weights and exact cyclic group orders. Unimodular coordinates give 1/3(1,2) at (2,1). Coprimality makes (1,1) the unique smooth pair. The HJ continued fraction of m/q then determines the full resolution chains. Independently checked all 1967 coprime pairs with B<=80, including all six facet determinants, q-coprimality and edge counts; this finite test supplements, not replaces, the symbolic proof.

## originality

PASS

Best-of-knowledge originality of the full parameterized cyclic quotient types and HJ baskets survives. Wu realizes all alpha values and studies coarse singularity conditions; Liu–Zhu and the published local class-group computation determine the (A,B)=(3,2) orders 3 and 8 but not q types nor all coprime B<=A<=2B. The standard toric quotient algorithm computes types from a given cone but does not already evaluate this family. Full Wu paper access was unavailable, so undiscovered Section 5 coverage remains a specific risk.
