The K-polystable toric surfaces realizing \(\alpha=B/(A+B)\) have a complete cyclic-quotient basket: six fixed-point charts with explicitly calculated orders and weights, except for the smooth \((1,1)\) member and a three-point \(A_2\) boundary. Along \((A,B)=(B+1,B)\), quotient-group orders grow quadratically while the alpha invariant tends to the smooth boundary value \(1/2\).
