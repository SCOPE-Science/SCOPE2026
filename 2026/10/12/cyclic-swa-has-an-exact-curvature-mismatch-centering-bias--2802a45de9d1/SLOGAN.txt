On a two-component scalar quadratic, constant-step cyclic SGD has a stable two-cycle whose ordinary SWA center is biased from the finite-sum minimizer by \(\frac{ab\eta(a-b)(u-v)}{2(a+b)(a+b-ab\eta)}\). Weighting the post-\(f_2\) and post-\(f_1\) phases by \(a\) and \(b\), respectively, removes that bias exactly.
