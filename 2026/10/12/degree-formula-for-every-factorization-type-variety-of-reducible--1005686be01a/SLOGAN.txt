For every factorization type \(\lambda=(d_1,\ldots,d_r)\) of forms in at least three variables, the projective degree is the multinomial intersection number of the multiplication parametrization divided exactly by the permutations of equal-degree factors. In particular, for \(d\ge3\), the full reducible-form locus has degree \(\binom{\binom{m+d-2}{m-1}+m-2}{m-1}\).
