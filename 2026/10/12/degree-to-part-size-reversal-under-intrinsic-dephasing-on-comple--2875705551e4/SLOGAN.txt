Under positive intrinsic energy dephasing, every genuinely unbalanced complete bipartite network \(K_{m,n}\) with \(2\le m<n\) switches its maximally quantum–classically distinguishable initial vertex class from the smaller part at short times to the larger part at long times. An exact weighted-graph short-time law and complete bipartite spectral-pinching formula prove this reversal for every positive dephasing rate, with stars and balanced bipartitions giving the sharp fixed-size long-time extremes.
