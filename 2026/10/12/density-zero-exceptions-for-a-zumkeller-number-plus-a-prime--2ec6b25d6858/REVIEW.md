# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The four-step proof establishes the unrestricted limiting density for all positive integers. The coprime-divisor partition and reduced-residue Dirichlet steps have their coprimality and positivity hypotheses checked. The cited published prime-block lemma is used only for sufficiently large indices. The telescoping product yields the strict uniform lower bound on \(\varphi(z)/z\); Chinese remainder independence and arbitrariness of the number of blocks prove the limit. Finite checker output tests illustrations only, not the theorem's infinite cases.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected material
Somu, Kukla and Tran explicitly conjecture the density-zero prime-summand conclusion (Conjecture 4.21) and prove infinitely many exceptional numbers (Theorem 4.19), but do not settle the density. Their congruence-class proposition and practical-summand theorem do not dominate this statement. OEIS A368082 lists finite data without the limit. Related published mathematical records on Zumkeller partitions and prime support address different invariants. Original published ingredients are credited; no priority claim beyond inspected literature is made.

## Value — PASS
The conclusion settles a named infinite asymptotic problem, despite the proven existence of infinitely many failures of individual representation. The proof introduces a density-transfer mechanism from mutually coprime odd Zumkeller blocks to prime summands. It does not claim an effective rate or any assertion about squares.
