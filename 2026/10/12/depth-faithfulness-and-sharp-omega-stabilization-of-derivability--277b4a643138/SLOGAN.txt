At the \(n\)-th derivability-box approximation of normal modal logic \(\mathsf K\), every theorem of modal depth at most \(n\) is already present, and every finite depth-\(n\) interpretation can be simulated at a finite Kripke-tree root. The bound is exact: \(\Box^{n+1}\top\) first appears one stage later, so the iterative construction stabilizes for the first time at \(\omega\).
