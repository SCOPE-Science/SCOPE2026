The complete linear-operator rank distribution of derivations of every finite-field height-two incidence algebra from a simple bipartite graph is an explicit graphic-matroid Tutte specialization. The first difference from the forest polynomial \((1+(q^2-1)z)^m\) occurs in degree \(g-1\) and has coefficient \(c_g(q-1)^g\), recovering the graph's girth \(g\) and its number \(c_g\) of shortest cycles.
