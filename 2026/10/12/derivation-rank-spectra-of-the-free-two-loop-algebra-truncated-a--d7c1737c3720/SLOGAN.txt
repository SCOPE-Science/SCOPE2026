The full rank distribution of every derivation of the seven-dimensional noncommutative algebra \(\mathbb F_q\langle x,y\rangle/(x,y)^3\) has an explicit formula for every prime power \(q\). In odd characteristic all ranks \(0,\ldots,6\) occur, but in characteristic two the largest possible rank is \(4\); the complete formulas distinguish the two regimes.
