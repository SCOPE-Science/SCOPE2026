For a finite product of fields with at least one nonbinary factor, every determining set of the reduced comaximal graph is obtained independently by omitting at most one vertex from each support fiber, giving \(\operatorname{Det}=|V|-(2^r-2)\) and a closed determining polynomial. For the Boolean ring \((\mathbb F_2)^r\), the mechanism changes completely: determining sets are exactly coordinate-separating subset families, so \(\operatorname{Det}=\lceil\log_2 r\rceil\).
