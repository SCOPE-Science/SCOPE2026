On every even magnetic cycle, tuning the total flux through \(\pi\) creates a dark antipodal channel; detuning by \(\delta\) restores uniform long-time quantum mixing but forces the finite-time uniformity threshold to grow as \(\Theta_n(|\delta|^{-1})\). The antipodal probability has an explicit finite-sum sinc crossover when observation time scales as \(1/|\delta|\).
