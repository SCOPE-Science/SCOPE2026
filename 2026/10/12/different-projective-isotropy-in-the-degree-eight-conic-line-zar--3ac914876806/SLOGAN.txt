For the explicit degree-eight conic-line Zariski pair of Amram--Shwartz--Sinichkin--Tan--Tokunaga, the two members have different full projective stabilizers: \(C_2\) for the first and \(C_2\times C_2\) for the second. The difference comes from how their two tangent lines sit inside the common order-eight dihedral stabilizer of the three conics.
