The Schur rank-threshold decision \(\lambda'_1\le r\) can be implemented on permutation-invariant \(n\)-qudit inputs with pairwise SWAP tests at cost \(\widetilde O(nr^2)\), independent of the ambient local dimension. The improvement comes from stopping after the first column reaches \(r+1\) and exploiting the exact harmonic waiting-time bound \(n((r+2)H_r-r)\), rather than certifying every Young-diagram column.
