Relative improvement accuracy of a local linear oracle has a sharp dimension divide on isotropic convex quadratics: the worst solution-distance factor is \(\eta\) in one dimension but jumps to \(2\) for every \(\eta>0\) in dimension at least two. The optimal absolute-gap certificate \(\|y-x_\star\|^2\le2E/\mu\) gives a way to recover strict distance contraction.
