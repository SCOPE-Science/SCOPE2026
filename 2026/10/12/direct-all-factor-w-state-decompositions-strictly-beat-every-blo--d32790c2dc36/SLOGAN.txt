For the recent generalized \(W\)-state rank bound, merging any two nonempty factor blocks strictly shortens the certified decomposition by exactly \(2^{|I|+|J|-2}(\sum_{i\in I}(d_i-2))(\sum_{j\in J}(d_j-2))\). Hence the direct all-factor construction strictly beats every decomposition length obtained by applying the same bound blockwise over a nontrivial partition and tensoring the results.
