For the canonical toric contraction from the permutohedral variety to a connected graph-associahedral variety, the exceptional prime divisors are pairwise disjoint **exactly when the graph's missing edges form a matching**. For \(r\) such edges the contraction blows up \(r\) disjoint smaller permutohedral centers, and the integral even-cohomology polynomial is \(A_n(t)-rtA_{n-2}(t)\).
