For every crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n\) with \(n\ge3\), every vertex subset of size at least \(4\) is disjunctive-total-dominating, while the valid triples have an exact one-sided-or-unmatched-support classification. Consequently \(\gamma_t^d(\operatorname{Cr}_n)=3\), there are \(\frac{4}{3}n(n-1)(n-2)\) minimum sets, and the full cardinality enumerator is explicit.
