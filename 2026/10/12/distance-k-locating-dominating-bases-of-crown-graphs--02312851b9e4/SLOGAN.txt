For every crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n\) with \(n\ge5\), the distance-\(k\) location-domination number is \(n-1\) for every \(k\ge1\). Minimum sets always omit one deleted-matching pair and choose one endpoint from every other pair; at \(k=1\) both bipartition classes must contribute at least two selected vertices, while for \(k\ge2\) every such transversal works.
