Every two-digit cubic happy fixed point is controlled by the three-consecutive-integer product \(c(c-1)(c+1)\): for \(c\ge2\), each divisor \(a\mid c^3-c\) gives exactly one valid base \(b=a^2+(c^3-c)/a\). Fixing the leading digit instead produces an exact periodic residue law with density \(r(\nu_2(a))3^{\omega(a/2^{\nu_2(a)})}/a\).
