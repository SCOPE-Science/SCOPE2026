For any finite deterministic modal Boolean algebra, the number of \(r\)-ary term operations is \(2^{\sum_{d\in D_f}2^{rH_f(d)}P_{2^r}(d)}\), where \(D_f\) is the set of divisors of cycle lengths and \(H_f(d)\) is the greatest tail height feeding a cycle divisible by \(d\). The complete arity spectrum is controlled by this divisor–tail skyline, regardless of multiplicities of identical branches or cycles.
