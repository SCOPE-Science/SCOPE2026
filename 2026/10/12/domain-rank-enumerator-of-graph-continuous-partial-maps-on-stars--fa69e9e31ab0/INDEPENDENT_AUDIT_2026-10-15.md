# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Inverse-image-of-face continuity has a precise combinatorial consequence: every nonempty target singleton fiber must be one source vertex or source edge, and inverse images of any occupied target edge must also be faces. When the image avoids target center, three mutually exclusive source-center cases contribute P_n, n uv Q_n, and n²u²v P_{n-1}. If target center is occupied, its preimage is source center alone (uvP_n), a single source leaf (nuv+n²u²v²), or a source edge (nu²v). The six cases are mutually disjoint, exhaustive and satisfy the face constraints conversely; their sum is the displayed all-n bivariate polynomial. P_n and Q_n are exact binomial-partial-injection enumerators. An independently written exhaustive checker generated every partial function and tested all target faces for n=1..5, reproducing totals 9,36,185,1278,10857 and every domain/rank coefficient. These bounded checks corroborate, not replace, the six-case proof.

## originality

PASS

Margolis--Rhodes arXiv:2005.02606 defines the inverse-face-continuous monoid M(G) and provides its general structural foundations and examples. This definition and general theory are not new. Their recent arXiv:2609.39370 advertises explicit enumerations for paths and cycles; n=1 and n=2 stars are paths, but the genuinely branching K_(1,n), n≥3, and its full bivariate enumerator are not shown in the accessible abstract. The 2024 Dimitrova--Fernandes--Koppitz star paper counts *forward* partial graph endomorphisms, an inequivalent condition (edge images versus inverse images of faces), and thus does not imply F_n. The 2026 Margolis--Rhodes full text remained unavailable after arXiv/OA attempts and an authorized institutional VPN check; this is material originality risk expressly retained, not a FAIL shortcut.

## value

PASS

This is a natural exact two-statistic census of the foundational graph-continuous transformation monoid on the simplest branching tree. It reveals how domain size and image rank interact and specializes to explicit all-order counts. Path/cycle and forward-star monoid enumerations do not give the branching inverse-face case. It is a complete finite-object family classification motivated by established semigroup theory, not arbitrary small-data recomputation.
