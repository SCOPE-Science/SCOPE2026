For every star \(K_{1,n}\), the full graph-continuous partial-transformation monoid has an explicit bivariate polynomial counting each domain size and image rank. A disjoint six-case fiber classification gives the exact polynomial \(F_n=(1+uv)P_n+nuvQ_n+n^2u^2vP_{n-1}+nuv+n^2u^2v^2+nu^2v\), where \(P_n\) and \(Q_n\) count partial injections between the indicated leaf sets.
