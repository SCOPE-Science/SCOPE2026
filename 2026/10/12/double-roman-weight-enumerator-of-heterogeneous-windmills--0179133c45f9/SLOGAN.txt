For a heterogeneous windmill \(W(m_1,\ldots,m_r)=K_1\vee(K_{m_1}\cup\cdots\cup K_{m_r})\), the complete double Roman weight enumerator factors into three products according to whether the center receives label \(3\), label \(2\), or label \(0\) or \(1\). This gives every weight coefficient and the exact total number of feasible functions, while the known scalar value \(\gamma_{dR}(W)=3\) appears only as a corollary.
