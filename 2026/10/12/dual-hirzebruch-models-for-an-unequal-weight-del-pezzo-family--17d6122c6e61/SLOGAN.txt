For the unequal-weight Kim--Won surfaces \(tz+f(x,y)=0\), the full minimal resolution is a ruled surface with exactly one two-component \((-1)\)-fiber over each root of \(f\). Contracting opposite halves of those fibers gives the dual Hirzebruch models \(\mathbb F_{{m+1}}\) and \(\mathbb F_{{n+1}}\), so the resolution is a marked blow-up of either ruled surface.
