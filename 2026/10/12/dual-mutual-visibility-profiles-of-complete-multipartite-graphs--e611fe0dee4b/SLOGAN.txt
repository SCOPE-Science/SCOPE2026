For a complete multipartite graph, a set \(X\) is dual mutual-visible exactly when every part containing two vertices on either side of \(X\) sees a vertex of the complement outside that part. This yields an exact dual visibility polynomial and \(\chi_{\mu_d}(G)=2\) for every noncomplete case except \(K_{1,n}\) with \(n\ge3\), where no dual mutual-visibility coloring exists.
