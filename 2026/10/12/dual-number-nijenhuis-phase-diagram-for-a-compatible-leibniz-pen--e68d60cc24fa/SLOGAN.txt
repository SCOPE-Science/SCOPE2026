The Nijenhuis operators of the compatible Leibniz pencil \(\mu_{u,v}(e_2,e_1)=ue_1,\ \mu_{u,v}(e_2,e_2)=ve_1\) have a one-fiber phase jump: one projective parameter gives a single affine plane, while every other parameter gives two planes meeting in the scalar line. Any two independent brackets have exactly the dual-number algebra \(K[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)\) as common Nijenhuis operators.
