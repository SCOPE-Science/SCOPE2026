The entire edge-domination polynomial of an arbitrary clique windmill \(K_1\vee\bigsqcup_iK_{m_i}\) is a three-product inclusion-exclusion expression, separating hub-covered and hub-uncovered edge sets. Its minimum edge-domination number jumps by exactly one when all blade sizes are odd, and the theorem counts every optimal edge set—including nonmatching optima—by explicit parity-sensitive products.
