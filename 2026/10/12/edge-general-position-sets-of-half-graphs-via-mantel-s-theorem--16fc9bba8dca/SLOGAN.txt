Half-graph edges are naturally the pairs of an ordered \(K_{n+1}\), and every edge general-position set maps to a triangle-free graph. Mantel's theorem is sharp: \(\operatorname{gp}_{e}(H_n)=\lfloor(n+1)^2/4\rfloor\), and the maxima are exactly the balanced bipartitions whose ordered color string has at most three runs.
