# Scientific review: edge-graded Catalan age of a set-homogeneous three-hypergraph

## Correctness

PASS. The hypotheses identify the strongly dense ordered 2-regular \(C\)-set and its particular 3-hypergraph reduct \(M_3\). The critical ambient facts are established in Assari–Hosseinzadeh–Macpherson, Theorem 3.1.1 and Proposition 3.1.2: homogeneity of the ordered \(C\)-set, set-homogeneity of the reduct, and equality of their automorphism groups. Finite restrictions of the ordered binary branching geometry are exactly reduced full plane binary trees; all finite shapes occur by dense splitting. Isomorphism classes of reduct hypergraphs coincide with these tree shapes by set-homogeneity and group equality, despite the fact that finite hypergraph automorphisms need not preserve the globally definable order. The root-split hyperedge rule gives precisely \(e(T)=e(L)+e(R)+a\binom b2\); thus the weighted recurrence has no missing cross terms. Mirroring complements every sorted triple. The six-vertex polynomial has all positive coefficients, and two singleton-root grafts shift consecutive edge-count intervals that overlap for all larger sizes, proving no other gaps. The stated finite specializations and tuple-orbit formula follow. A standalone checker independently reconstructs tree edge sets and recurrences and exhaustively tests vertex-permutation isomorphism through six leaves. The infinite theorem is proved combinatorially, not inferred from finite checks.

## Originality

PASS for the **edge-graded age polynomial and its interpretation**; not an originality claim for Catalan numbers, the bare ordered-tuple integer sequence, the source's constructions, or the source's four-vertex examples. Full scientific text of the original preprint and the journal article's relevant Sections 2–3 was checked for an all-order edge-distribution recurrence. The source proves the decisive structure and explicitly notes the five four-vertex examples but does not present the degree-refined Catalan recurrence or identify the forbidden five-edge class at order five. OEIS A052886 explicitly covers the ungraded Stirling–Catalan convolution, which is therefore treated only as corroborating context. Searches for the specialized weighted recurrence and for edge-graded induced members of \(M_3\) found no equivalent. Residual risk: the formula may appear in unindexed notes or arise as a previously unstated elementary consequence of the cited tree presentation.

## Value

PASS. The original paper exhibits a rare countable 3-hypergraph which is set-homogeneous but not homogeneous and studies how the finite induced structures encode its hidden ordered branching geometry. The edge-graded age is a natural and complete orbit-counting invariant of exactly this example; it is not an arbitrary finite slice. The all-order recursion distinguishes edge frequencies among types and gives the concrete missing half-density five-vertex level, while specializing to the Catalan profile and allowing exact computation in any finite arity. This captures both the combinatorial richness and the structural constraints of a canonical nonhomogeneous example.

## Closest prior work and limitations

The underlying \(M_3\) and all structural model-theoretic statements belong to Assari–Hosseinzadeh–Macpherson (arXiv:2202.09613, DOI:10.1112/jlms.12796). Their proof already contains a root-cone decomposition, so the current theorem should be understood as an exact combinatorial enumeration derived from this representation, not an independent representation theorem. Catalan tree enumeration and OEIS A052886 are classical. The proof covers all \(n\ge1\); the finite program verifies only the enumerated sizes. Independent mathematical review remains outstanding.
