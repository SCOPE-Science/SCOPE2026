# Review of Edge-open-packing polynomial of square-page book graphs

## Correctness
PASS. The proof explicitly determines every compatibility relation among the four edge types. The spine is compatible with side edges and conflicts with middle edges; each of the left, right, and middle families is internally compatible; every left edge conflicts with every right edge; and a side edge is compatible with exactly the middle edge on its own page. These relations make the four-case classification exhaustive. Their disjoint generating contributions sum to the displayed polynomial, whose top coefficient gives the edge open packing number and maximum-set count. Exhaustive verification of \(599{,}184\) edge subsets for \(1\le m\le6\) matches the structural test and every polynomial coefficient.

Square-page books are a standard highly structured cyclic family for which the common spine creates a nontrivial three-way interaction among two star-like side families and a transversal middle family.
