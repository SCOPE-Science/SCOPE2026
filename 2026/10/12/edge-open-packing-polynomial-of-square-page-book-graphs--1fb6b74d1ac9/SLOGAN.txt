For the square-page book \(B_m\), every edge open packing falls into four explicit compatibility types, giving \[\mathcal E_o(B_m;z)=3(1+z)^m-2+2mz^2+z\bigl(2(1+z)^m-1\bigr).\] Hence \(\rho_e^o(B_m)=m+1\); for \(m\ge2\) exactly two maximum packings exist, one using the spine with all left page edges and one using the spine with all right page edges.
