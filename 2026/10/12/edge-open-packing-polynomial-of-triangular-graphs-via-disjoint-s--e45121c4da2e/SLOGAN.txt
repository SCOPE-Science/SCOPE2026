Every edge-open packing of the triangular graph \(T_n=L(K_n)\) is a uniquely determined collection of vertex-disjoint two-edge and three-edge paths in the underlying complete graph. The resulting exponential generating function is \(\exp(u+u^3z/2+u^4z^2/2)\); maximum packings have size \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor-\mathbf1_{\{n\equiv2\pmod4\}}\), with two structural types exactly when \(n\equiv2\pmod4\) and \(n\geq6\).
