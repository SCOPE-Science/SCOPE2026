# Scientific review: edge-open packing profiles of matching-deleted bicliques

## Correctness — PASS
The theorem covers all integers \(m,n\ge2\) and \(0\le t\le\min\{m,n\}\), with no connectivity assumption. The definition uses a third **distinct** edge. For two disjoint selected bipartite edges, the only potential common edges are the two crossed edges; both are missing precisely for the reversed-edge pair indexed by two deleted matching edges. A third packing edge is impossible for such a pair. Otherwise selected edges are pairwise incident and, by bipartiteness, share a unique center if at least two edges are present. Distinct star subsets have unique centers and the crossed pairs are disjoint from this type. The coefficient calculation follows from exact degrees. Direct checks examine 144444 subsets in 32 small parameter cases, a further 1048576 subsets for the five-by-five full-deletion case, and 5062 independently scanned edge-pair comparisons. No finite experiment is treated as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, subject to literature risk
Chelladurai–Kalimuthu–Soundararajan (2022), Proposition 2.3, covers scalar edge-open packing maxima for complete multipartite graphs; it does not imply the coefficient profile after a matching is deleted. Brešar–Samadi (2024), Theorem 4.2, provides single-edge-deletion bounds for maxima rather than a full size enumerator for matching deletions. Brešar–Dravec–Hedžet–Samadi (2024), Section 4, gives direct-product bounds involving related matching-deletion constructions, but not an all-cardinality profile. Cardoso–Cerdeira–Dominic–Cruz (2019), Proposition 2.2, gives a general induced-star-forest characterization of color classes but not the exact specialization and crossed-pair count. The central mathematical overlap is made explicit, not bypassed. Search results and material inspected do not establish absolute absence of prior equivalent work. The 2022 publisher PDF did not open directly, but a publicly readable full-text transcription, including Proposition 2.3 and later sections, was examined; this access limitation, restricted full-text access to a recent general algorithmic preprint, and the possibility of unindexed work remain residual risks.

## Value — PASS
The object is natural: edge-open packings are individual injective edge-color classes, and complete bipartite graphs with a deleted matching interpolate between the well-studied biclique and the crown family. The theorem resolves all feasible edge subsets, not just a scalar optimum. The exceptional \(\binom{t}{2}\) term isolates the precise new non-star phenomenon caused by matched nonedges; every coefficient is determined for arbitrary and unequal part sizes. This profile is useful for enumerating permitted monochromatic classes and distinguishing dense bipartite interference structures. No downstream theorem or entire coloring-count result is assumed.
