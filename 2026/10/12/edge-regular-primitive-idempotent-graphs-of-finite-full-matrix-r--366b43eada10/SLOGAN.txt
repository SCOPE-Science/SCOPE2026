For every prime power \(q\) and \(n\ge3\), the primitive-idempotent subgraph of the indecomposability graph of \(M_n(\mathbb F_q)\) is an explicit edge-regular antiflag graph with degree \(q^{n-2}[n-1]_q\) and adjacent common-neighbor count \(q^{n-3}[n-2]_q\). In dimension three every neighborhood is a perfect matching, every edge lies in a unique triangle, and the triangles are exactly the noncollinear projective point triples.
