For binary single parity-check product codes, an eight-erasure pattern permits eight completions exactly when its bipartite graph is \(K_{2,4}\), \(K_{4,2}\), or \(K_{3,3}\) minus one edge. The exact number is \(\binom m2\binom n4+\binom m4\binom n2+9\binom m3\binom n3\), simultaneously counting the third-weight minimum-support subcodes.
