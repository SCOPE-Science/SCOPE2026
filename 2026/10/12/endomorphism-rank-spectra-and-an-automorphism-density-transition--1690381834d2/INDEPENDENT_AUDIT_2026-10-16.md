## correctness

PASS

For H_m=V+Kz with xy=omega(v,w)z and nondegenerate alternating omega (all characteristics), H_m^2=Ann(H_m)=Kz. A K-linear endomorphism has block (A,f,c) with omega(Av,Aw)=c omega(v,w). c!=0 gives symplectic similitudes and rank 2m+1. c=0 means image A is isotropic of dimension r<=m; f vanishing on ker A gives total rank r (q^r choices), otherwise r+1 (q^{2m}-q^r). Counting isotropic r-spaces I_{m,r}={m bracket r}_q product(q^{m-i}+1), surjections product(q^{2m}-q^i), and GSp_{2m}(q) order yields stated rank generating polynomial. Noninvertible ranks exactly 0..m+1 and full rank 2m+1. Singular degree (5m^2+5m)/2 versus auto degree 2m^2+3m+1 gives density 1,1/2,0 for m 1,2,>=3. Independent complete enumeration of all 2^9 linear maps at (m,q)=(1,2) found rank polynomial 1+21T+18T^2+24T^3. Arbitrary-field proof is symbolic, not finite enumeration.
