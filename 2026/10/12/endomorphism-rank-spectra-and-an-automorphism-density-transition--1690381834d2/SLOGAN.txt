The endomorphism rank polynomial of every alternating two-step algebra \(V\oplus Kz\) over \(\mathbb F_q\) is explicit, including the contribution of singular maps with totally isotropic image. Its automorphism density has a dimension-driven three-way limit: \(1\), \(1/2\), or \(0\) as \(q\to\infty\), depending on whether the symplectic half-dimension is \(1\), \(2\), or at least \(3\).
