An arbitrarily small nonzero potential at just one endpoint restores positive-definite infinite-time quantum average mixing on every positively weighted path. For \(n\ge2\), the rank jumps from \(\lceil n/2\rceil\) at zero potential to \(n\) otherwise, with an exact determinant equal to an explicit coupling monomial times \(\tau^{2\lfloor n/2\rfloor}\) divided by the Hamiltonian's characteristic discriminant.
