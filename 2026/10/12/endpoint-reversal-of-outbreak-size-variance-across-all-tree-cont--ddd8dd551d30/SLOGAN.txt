For a single uniformly seeded outbreak transmitted along independently open contacts in any \(n\)-vertex tree, weak-transmission variance is ordered by the number of adjacent edge pairs, while near-certain-transmission variance is ordered by the Wiener distance sum. Consequently, the star and the path exchange their roles as unique global variance maximizer and minimizer, with an explicit common window \(1/(100n^6)\) at either endpoint for every \(n\ge4\).
