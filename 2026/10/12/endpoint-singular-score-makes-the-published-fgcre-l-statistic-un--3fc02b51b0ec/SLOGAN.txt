The score used by a 2026 FGCRE exponentiality test satisfies \(J_\beta(u)\to-\infty\) as \(u\uparrow1\), yet the published empirical L-statistic evaluates it at \(u=1\). Therefore the printed statistic and every coefficient in its exact-null formula are undefined; an explicit endpoint regularization and a new coefficient-distinctness argument are required.
