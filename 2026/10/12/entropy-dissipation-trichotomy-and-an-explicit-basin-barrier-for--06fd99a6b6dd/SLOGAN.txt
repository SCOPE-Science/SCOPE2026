For the mean-field incremental voter model with any finite odd number of opinion levels, an exact entropy-dissipation identity forces every initial distribution to converge exponentially to left consensus, right consensus, or a unique saddle. The strict sublevel \(\mathcal F<L\log2-\log(L+1)\) additionally certifies the winning consensus from the sign of \(M-L/2\), extending the previously known attraction regions without claiming a complete basin classification.
