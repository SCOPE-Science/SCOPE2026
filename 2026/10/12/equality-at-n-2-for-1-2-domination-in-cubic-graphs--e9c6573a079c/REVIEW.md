# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof tracks the cut between a \((1,2)\)-dominating set and its complement. Cubicity supplies the upper cut bound, domination supplies the lower cut bound, and equality forces crossing degree one at every vertex. The converse follows immediately from the internal degree two left by a perfect matching cut.

## Value
PASS. The result gives a complete structural characterization of the equality layer of a sharp general bound and links it to perfect matching cuts. It is not a numerical recomputation or an arbitrary finite slice.
