# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

González–Hernando–Mora Proposition 1 forces every equalizer set to contain an entire bipartition class A or B. B has 2q vertices and equalizes every pair in A using an arm vertex (requires q>=3 for two noncentral v_i). For S containing A, if two u_i are omitted, each corresponding w_i must be selected: for unselected u_i_prime and w_i the sole equidistant vertex is the unselected middle u_i of their length-four path, so no valid equalizer otherwise. This yields >=2q and forces either B or exactly A plus q-1 of the u_i. Directly checked sufficiency of these q extra bases; independent brute subsets q=3,4 gave precisely 4 and 5 optima of size 2q. All-q proof is the bipartite dichotomy plus unique midpoint, not finite extrapolation.

## originality

PASS

Best-of-knowledge original all-basis classification for natural twice-subdivided stars. The foundational full text treats bipartite lower bounds, stars/bistars, paths and cycles but not length-three q-arm spiders; the later paper treats lexicographic products and NP-completeness, not the tree family. The smaller partite class has q+1 vertices, yet eqdim is 2q, showing unbounded gap q-1.

## value

PASS

The smallest non-bistar symmetric spider is a natural test family for the explicitly open tree equidistant-dimension problem. Exact dimension, all q+1 bases, and unbounded gap from the generic bipartite lower bound are jointly structural and useful; the midpoint obstruction is a motivated nontrivial classification.
