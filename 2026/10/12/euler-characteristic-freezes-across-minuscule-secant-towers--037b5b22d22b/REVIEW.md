# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. A minuscule representation has one-dimensional weight spaces and a single Weyl orbit of weights. A generic cocharacter separates all of those weights, so the ambient projective fixed locus consists exactly of the weight lines. Every weight line is a Weyl translate of the highest-weight line and therefore lies on the closed homogeneous orbit.

Every secant variety is torus-stable and contains that orbit. Hence every secant has the same fixed locus as the entire ambient projective space. Euler localization for algebraic \(\mathbb C^*\)-actions, valid for singular varieties, gives
\[
\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(\sigma_r(X))=\dim V_\lambda.
\]
Constructible additivity then gives
\[
\chi_c(\sigma_r(X)\setminus\sigma_{r-1}(X))=0
\]
for every \(r\ge2\).

The bundled checker independently verifies weight separation in principal classical minuscule models. Finite computation is regression evidence only.

## Value
PASS. Minuscule secant varieties are central border-rank loci in homogeneous and tensor geometry. Their geometry changes strongly along the secant tower, so complete constancy of Euler characteristic is a substantive structural limitation of this invariant. The theorem simultaneously explains all higher exact secant layers as Euler-invisible.
