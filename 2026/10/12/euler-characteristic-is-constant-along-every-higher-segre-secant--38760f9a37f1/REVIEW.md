## Correctness
PASS. A base-\(B\) one-parameter subgroup makes every ambient tensor-coordinate weight distinct. Therefore the ambient fixed locus consists exactly of coordinate tensor lines, all of which are decomposable and lie already on the Segre variety. Every higher secant is invariant and contains the Segre variety, so its fixed locus is exactly the same finite set of
\[
\prod_i a_i
\]
points.

Euler characteristic localizes to the fixed locus for an algebraic \(\mathbb C^*\)-action. The proof included in the result uses Rosenlicht stratification and the fact that every nonfixed orbit piece is a locally trivial \(\mathbb C^*\)-bundle with compactly supported Euler characteristic zero. No smoothness assumption is used.

Hence every projective secant has Euler characteristic
\[
\prod_i a_i,
\]
and additivity gives compactly supported Euler characteristic zero for every exact border-rank layer above rank one.

The bundled checker replays the separating-weight construction across a broad finite family. Finite computation is regression evidence only.
