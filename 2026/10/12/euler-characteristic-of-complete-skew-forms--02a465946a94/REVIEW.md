# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. Thaddeus gives the exact sequence definition and a smooth projective blow-up model for complete skew forms. Under a diagonal \(\mathbb C^*\)-action with binary-separated basis weights, every projective coordinate wedge has a distinct weight. A fixed complete skew form must therefore choose one coordinate wedge, then one coordinate wedge on its coordinate kernel, and so on. This gives a bijection with ordered matchings of the basis labels.

The ordered-matching count is
\[
\binom n2\binom{n-2}2\cdots
=
\frac{n!}{2^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}}.
\]
Bialynicki--Birula decomposition for the smooth projective variety identifies this isolated fixed-point count with ordinary Euler characteristic. The bundled checker independently enumerates finite cases and verifies the recurrence. Finite computation is regression evidence only.

## Value
PASS. The theorem computes a canonical topological invariant of the complete-skew-form compactification in every dimension and explains it by a natural ordered-matching model. The simple factorial quotient contrasts sharply with the different Euler laws of complete quadrics and complete collineations, exposing genuinely different fixed-point combinatorics in the antisymmetric compactification.
