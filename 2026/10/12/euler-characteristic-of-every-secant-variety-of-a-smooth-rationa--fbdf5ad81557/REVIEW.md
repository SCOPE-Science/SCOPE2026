PASS. A generic one-parameter subgroup of the scroll torus makes every tautological coordinate a distinct weight line. Projection to a directrix block shows that the scroll border rank of a coordinate monomial is exactly its border rank on the corresponding rational normal curve.

For
\[
s^{d-j}t^j,
\]
the relevant catalecticant has rank
\[
\min\{j,d-j\}+1,
\]
giving the lower bound on border rank. A finite-difference degeneration of pure powers gives the matching upper bound. Thus the coordinate lies in the \(r\)-th secant exactly when
\[
\min\{j,d-j\}+1\le r.
\]

Counting fixed coordinates in each block gives
\[
\min\{d_i+1,2r\},
\]
and Euler localization gives the claimed sum. The bundled checker independently verifies the catalecticant ranks, finite-difference identities, and finite fixed-point counts.
