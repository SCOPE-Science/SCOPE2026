# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

A one-parameter subgroup combining base binary weight and separated block offsets makes all ambient coordinate points isolated, and secants are invariant. Projection of the affine scroll cone to the ith binary-form block gives the lower comparison of border ranks; inclusion of ith directrix gives the reverse, hence scroll border rank of x_ij equals binary monomial symmetric border rank. The catalecticant order min(j,d-j) has q+1 independent distinct derivative monomials (lower bound); an explicit (q+1)-term finite-difference limit of powers gives matching upper bound, even at endpoints. Thus the fixed coordinate count is min(d_i+1,2r). For projective complex possibly singular secants, compact-supported Euler equals fixed-locus Euler under C* action. Independently verified 299 binary monomial catalecticant-rank cases and coordinate counts for representative 2-,3-,4-directrix scrolls; proof applies all parameters and does not assume expected dimension or smooth secants.

## originality

PASS

Best-of-knowledge original exact full secant-tower Euler invariant for arbitrary positive splitting degrees. The closest full source Lin–Shen focuses on blow-up/Rees/fiber cone, regularity and singularities of balanced scroll secant varieties; De Poi and Catalano-Johnson address dimensions/determinantal structures, not explicit topological Euler numbers. Standard C* localization and binary monomial border rank are prior ingredients, and the formula may be folklore under those techniques; the new claim is their all-scroll quantitative synthesis.

## value

PASS

The Euler characteristic is a canonical topological invariant of a natural projective secant tower, and the directrixwise additive law gives all steps for every smooth scroll, including defective levels. This supplies previously unavailable exact topology with an unexpectedly simple saturation threshold. Coarseness of chi and known localization ingredients limit scope but do not render the complete all-parameter invariant arbitrary.
