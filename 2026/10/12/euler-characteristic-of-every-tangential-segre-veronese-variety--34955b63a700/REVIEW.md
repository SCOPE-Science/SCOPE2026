# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. A generic diagonal one-parameter subgroup makes ambient coordinate monomials the only fixed points. Since the tangential variety lies in the second secant variety, every multigraded catalecticant has rank at most two. Local monomial catalecticants show that only pure monomials and one-variable near-pure monomials survive; tensor-product ranks exclude simultaneous excitations in two factors. Every survivor is visibly a tangent direction. Euler localization therefore turns the exact fixed-point count into the claimed formula.

The bundled checker reconstructs local catalecticant ranks and verifies mixed one-, two-, and three-factor grids. Finite computation is regression evidence only.

## Originality
PASS. The primary archival source studies the same tangential Segre--Veronese varieties, including arbitrary numbers of factors, but computes tensor rank and decomposition structure rather than Euler characteristic. Targeted full-text and literature searches did not locate the mixed-multidegree Euler formula.

The one-factor Veronese and pure Segre specializations are already covered and are not the novelty claim. The genuinely mixed formula, including the quadratic correction and the stabilization from degree three onward, is not implied by those cases.

Residual risk remains because the fixed-point proof is short and may be folklore or implicit in a stronger motivic calculation.

## Value
PASS. The result gives a uniform global topological invariant for a central family of partially symmetric tensor varieties and reveals a clean three-regime dependence on each multidegree entry. It is structural rather than a small numerical slice.
