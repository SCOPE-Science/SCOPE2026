For a Segre--Veronese tangential variety, ordinary Euler characteristic is an explicit fixed-monomial count: \(\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(\tau(X))=\prod_i n_i+\sum_i a_i\prod_{j\ne i}n_j\), with \(a_i=0\), \(\binom{n_i}{2}\), or \(n_i(n_i-1)\) according as \(d_i=1\), \(d_i=2\), or \(d_i\ge3\). Thus every multidegree coordinate stabilizes topologically after degree three.
