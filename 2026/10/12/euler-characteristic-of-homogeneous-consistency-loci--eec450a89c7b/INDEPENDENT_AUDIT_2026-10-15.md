# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

A diagonal C* action with separated monomial weights fixes precisely coordinate monomial lines in each projective coefficient space. For a monomial tuple, absence of a common projective root is equivalent to covering each variable by at least one pure power; necessity follows by evaluation at coordinate points and sufficiency by choosing a nonzero coordinate at an arbitrary point. Inclusion-exclusion counts fixed tuples as sum_{t=0}^n (-1)^t C(n,t) prod_i(N_i-t). Compact-support Euler localization under C* equates this to chi_c(U); additivity in the proper ambient product gives chi(R). Finite differences prove vanishing for m<n, n! for m=n and n! S(m,n) for linear forms. Independent direct enumeration verified 14 small monomial tuples and exact finite differences across n,m<=9.

## originality

PASS

The square case m=n and n! Euler invariant overlaps classical resultant complement theory, and Vassiliev gives finer topology for n=2 in a differently normalized coefficient space. D'Andrea--Dickenstein compute multivariate resultant determinant complexes, while Monin studies generic consistent overdetermined linear-series systems. Their inspected statements do not give the all-n, arbitrary m>n separately-projectivized Euler characteristic formula, though a differently worded torus-localization calculation remains possible.

## value

PASS

The closed overdetermined consistency-locus Euler characteristic in every multidegree is a natural global topological invariant of a fundamental family of algebraic-system parameter spaces. It cleanly distinguishes m<n, square m=n and m>n cases and is not a gratuitous tiny-instance computation.
