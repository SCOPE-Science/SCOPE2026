# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. A generic diagonal one-parameter subgroup isolates monomial coordinate systems. Such a monomial system has no common projective zero exactly when its chosen pure powers cover every coordinate variable. Inclusion--exclusion over missing variables gives
\[
\sum_{t=0}^{n}(-1)^t\binom nt\prod_i(N_i-t).
\]
Algebraic \(\mathbb C^*\)-localization gives the compactly supported Euler characteristic of the complement, and additivity gives the Euler characteristic of the closed consistency locus.

The packaged checker independently enumerates actual homogeneous monomials on a finite grid and verifies the covering criterion and all three threshold specializations. Finite computation is regression evidence only.

## Originality
PASS. The archival resultant source treats the square resultant and determinant formulas. Vassiliev treats affine non-resultant systems in two variables. Later overdetermined work defines consistency varieties and studies their generic zero sets. Targeted semantic and web searches did not locate the arbitrary-\(m\), arbitrary-multidegree separately projectivized Euler formula.

The closest previous square formula is precisely the \(m=n\) specialization. The new \(m>n\) statement is not degree-independent and replaces permutation fixed points by general covering tuples.

## Value
PASS. The theorem gives one closed expression across the full underdetermined, classical resultant, and overdetermined regimes. It exposes a sharp transition from empty complement, to the universal value \(n!\), to a degree-sensitive consistency invariant, with the linear case recovering the surjection count \(n!\,{m\brace n}\).
