# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof uses Vainsencher's published iterated blow-up construction. For a rank-\(r\) center, kernel and image choices give the Grassmannian factors, while the already-resolved quotient-to-image datum is the square complete-collineation space \(C_r\). Its codimension is the usual determinantal value.

The smooth blow-up Euler formula gives an exact recurrence. In the square case and then in the general rectangular case, the recurrence reduces to explicit telescoping factorial identities. No finite experiment is used as an infinite proof.

The bundled checker independently evaluates both recurrences and the closed formulas over broad parameter ranges.
