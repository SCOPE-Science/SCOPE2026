For every positive rank-four Euler matrix in the Loginov--Orlov minifold classification, the Euler degree \(D(A)\) is exactly the order of the torsion in \(\operatorname{coker}(T-I)\), where \(T=-A^{-\mathsf T}A\). The five Smith groups are cyclic except in the quadric and projective-three-space classes, where the torsion is \(\mathbf Z/3\oplus\mathbf Z/18\) and \(\mathbf Z/2\oplus\mathbf Z/2\oplus\mathbf Z/16\), respectively.
