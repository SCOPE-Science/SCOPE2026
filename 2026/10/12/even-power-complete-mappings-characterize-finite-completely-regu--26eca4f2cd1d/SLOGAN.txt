For any finite semigroup, a permutation \(\alpha\) such that \(x\mapsto x(\alpha(x))^k\) is bijective with even \(k\geq2\) exists exactly when the semigroup is a union of groups. Every such semigroup has an explicit witness \(\alpha(x)=x^a\), while any \(k\geq2\) forces the union-of-groups structure.
