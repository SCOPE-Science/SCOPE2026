# Review

## Correctness

PASS. For a fixed finite target \(H\), membership sets are downward closed because Wang proves \(\mathcal H_j\subseteq\mathcal H_i\) whenever \(i<j\). Hence only empty, finite-initial, or full profiles can occur. The empty profile is realized by \(K_3\), which is absent from \(\mathcal H_2\), and therefore from every later class. Proposition 7.7 gives \(M_{D,L}\in\mathcal H_D\setminus\mathcal H_{D+1}\), which nestedness upgrades to the exact profile \(\{2,\ldots,D\}\). Theorem 1.4 gives \(K_4\in\mathcal H_d\) in every dimension. Nonbipartiteness of the finite-cutoff witnesses follows from their continuous membership together with the Borel bipartite/nonbipartite characterization.

## Originality

PASS. Wang explicitly proves strict nestedness and supplies adjacent-dimension separators, but does not define a target-wise dimension spectrum or state the complete realization theorem. Searches under dimension spectrum, exact cutoff, threshold, initial segment, the explicit \(M_{d,L}\) family, and Borel-versus-continuous behavior did not locate an equivalent formulation. Earlier work treats the dimension-two problem and Borel hom-shifts rather than the complete target-wise dimension-profile classification.

## Value

PASS. The result turns a class-level strict hierarchy into a complete invariant-shape theorem for individual finite targets. More importantly, it shows that the full range of continuous dimension dependence survives inside one Borel-indistinguishable class: nonbipartite finite targets all have positive Borel behavior in every \(d\ge2\), yet their continuous profiles can have any possible cutoff. This isolates dimension sensitivity as a genuinely continuous phenomenon rather than a shadow of the Borel obstruction.

## Closest literature and limitations

Wang (2026) contains all decisive continuous ingredients: nestedness, explicit separators \(M_{d,L}\), the \(K_4\) positive theorem, and the recalled \(K_3\) obstruction. Gao--Jackson--Krohne--Seward supply the earlier dimension-two continuous theory, while Chandgotia--Unger supply the Borel positive theory for nonbipartite targets.

The theorem does not determine the cutoff of an arbitrary graph and does not minimize witness size.
