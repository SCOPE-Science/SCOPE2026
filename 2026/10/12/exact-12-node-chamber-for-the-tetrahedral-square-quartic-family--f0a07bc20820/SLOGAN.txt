For the marked characteristic-two family \(x_1x_2x_3x_4+q^2=0\), the vague “generally 12 nodes” condition is exactly the coefficient torus: all four square coefficients and all six mixed coefficients of \(q\) must be nonzero. On this locus the Jacobian scheme is twelve disjoint doubled points of total length \(24\), each supported at an \(A_1\)-node.
