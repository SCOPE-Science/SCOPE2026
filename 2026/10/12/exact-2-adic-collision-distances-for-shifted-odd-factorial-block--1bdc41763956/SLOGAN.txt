The complete shifted odd-factorial block \(P_e(A)\) obeys the sharp collision law \(\nu_2(P_e(A)-P_e(B))=3e-2+\nu_2((A-B)(A+B+1))\) for every \(e\ge2\) and all integer shifts. At precision \(2^{3e-2+s}\), the shifts modulo \(2^s\) form exactly two-element fibers \(\{A,-A-1\}\), upgrading a known uniform congruence to a complete valuation and fiber classification.
