For the cycle \(C_n\), classical 2-WL stabilizes in exactly \(0\) rounds for \(3\le n\le5\) and \(\lceil\log_2(\lfloor n/2\rfloor-1)\rceil\) rounds for \(n\ge6\): each round doubles the largest graph distance already separated. The counting-logical width is exactly \(2\) for \(n\le5\) and \(3\) for \(n\ge6\).
