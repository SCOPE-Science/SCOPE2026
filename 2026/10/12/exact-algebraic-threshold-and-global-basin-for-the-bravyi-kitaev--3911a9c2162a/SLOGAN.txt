The Bravyi–Kitaev H-state 15-to-1 error map has an exact algebraic threshold, not merely the quoted numerical value \(0.141\): \(\epsilon_*=0.1414802926561672383\ldots\), obtained from the unique \(z_*\in(2,3)\) satisfying \(z_*^3+z_*^2-2z_*-16=0\). Below \(\epsilon_*\) the recurrence converges monotonically to zero error, while above it in the twirled physical interval it converges monotonically to \(1/2\).
