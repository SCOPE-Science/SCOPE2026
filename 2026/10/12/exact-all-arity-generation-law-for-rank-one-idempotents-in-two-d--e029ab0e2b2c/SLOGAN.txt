The ordered \(r\)-tuples of rank-one idempotents generating \(M_2(\mathbb F_q)\) are counted exactly by \([q(q+1)]^r-(q+1)(2q)^r+\binom{q+1}{2}2^r\) for every prime power \(q\) and \(r\ge1\). They generate precisely when their image–kernel line pairs share no projective point; this requires three projectors over \(\mathbb F_2\) but only two over every larger finite field.
