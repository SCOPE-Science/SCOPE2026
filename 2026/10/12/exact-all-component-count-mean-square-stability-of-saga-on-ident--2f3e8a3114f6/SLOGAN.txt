For \(n\) identical strongly convex quadratic components, the exact all-initialization mean-square stability ceiling for SAGA is \(\eta\lambda_{\max}(H)<\tau_n\), where \((2n-1)(\tau_n-1)(1+(n-1)\tau_n)=1\). The sharp threshold decreases from \(2/\sqrt3\) at \(n=2\) toward \(1\) with excess \(1/(2n^2)+O(n^{-3})\), revealing memory-induced instability despite identical components.
