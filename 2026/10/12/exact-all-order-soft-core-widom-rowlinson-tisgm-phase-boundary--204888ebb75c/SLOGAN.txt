For every tree order \(k\ge4\), the ferromagnetic soft-core Widom–Rowlinson TISGM phase diagram has the exact one-versus-three boundary conjectured in 2019: \(\theta_c=(k-1)/(k+1)\) and, below it, \(\lambda_c=((k+1)/k)^k/[k-1-(k+1)\theta]\). A global ratio parameterization proves that the nonsymmetric branch is unique up to spin exchange, replacing the source’s finite-order numerical evidence.
