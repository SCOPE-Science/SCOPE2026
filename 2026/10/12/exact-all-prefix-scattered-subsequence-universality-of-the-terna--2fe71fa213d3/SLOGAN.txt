For the ternary Thue difference word, the number of universally embeddable subsequence letters in every prefix of length \(16k+r\) is \(5k+\lfloor r/3\rfloor\), with one explicit Thue–Morse-dependent correction at residue \(r=2\). Its universality density is exactly \(5/16\), with sharp bounded discrepancy \(-10\le16U(N)-5N\le6\).
