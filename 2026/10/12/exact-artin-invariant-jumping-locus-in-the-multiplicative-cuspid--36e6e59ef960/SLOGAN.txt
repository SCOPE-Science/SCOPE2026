In the multiplicative cuspidal Kummer family in characteristic \(2\), the Artin invariant drops from \(3\) exactly on \(\lambda_4\mu_2^3+\mu_4\lambda_2^3=0\): it is \(2\) off the origin of that locus and \(1\) at the origin. For fixed leading coefficients, the \(\sigma=2\) locus is three punctured lines meeting at the unique \(\sigma=1\) point.
