For the equilateral standard Kirchhoff loop chain, the exact Bloch relation is \(\cos\theta=2\cos k-1\), so the dispersive spectrum is precisely \(\{k^2:\cos k\ge0\}\). Every \((n\pi)^2\) is also an infinite-multiplicity flat band: even modes live on one loop, while odd modes require one backbone edge and its two endpoint loops, piercing every forbidden Bloch interval with a gap eigenvalue.
