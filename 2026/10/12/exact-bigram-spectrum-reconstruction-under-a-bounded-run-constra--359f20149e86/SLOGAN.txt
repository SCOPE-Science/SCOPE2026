For binary words with every constant run of length at most \(h\), the number of reconstructible bigram-multiset codewords has an exact rational generating function and leading term \(3(h-1)^2n^2/[4h(h+1)]\). When \(h=2\), a period-twelve quadratic formula gives the sharp code size, yet every word is ambiguous whenever its length is \(1\pmod6\).
