# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** Full exact finite enumeration of every one of the 16,384 binary words at length fourteen. Both independently implemented signature constructors partition the domain into 128 singleton, 8104 two-word and 12 four-word observation fibers; all twelve four-word member lists coincide exactly. The proof reduces observational equivalence to equality of integer substring count vectors. No infinite theorem is inferred from finite testing.

**Originality — PASS, with bibliographic limitations.** The 2014 full-text original defines the identical model and gives a lower bound of four and an upper bound of eight at this arithmetic parameter, while its special exact theorems do not include length fourteen. The finite sharpening and complete equality census are not stated there. Searches for synonymous observations, earlier exact parameter tables, published related results, and available predecessor records did not locate this exact count. Abstract-only access to the 2010 predecessor and later same-object papers limits the completeness of this comparison; failed searching is not evidence of global uniqueness.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Length fourteen is the smallest natural case in the model where the length plus one has two distinct odd prime factors; the exact ambiguity bound is unresolved by the cited general formulas. The complete count of every maximally ambiguous fiber and the exact uniform nonreconstructability probability provide a useful finite reconstruction benchmark. The claim is not presented as an asymptotic result or as a new general algorithm.
