For binary strings with no three identical consecutive bits, the exact number of distinguishable full three-gram multiplicity profiles has generating function \(\frac{1+6z+8z^2+5z^3+4z^4+2z^5}{(1-z^2)(1-z^3)(1-z^4)}\). Its sharp growth is \(\frac{13}{24}n^2+O(n)\), with an exact period-twelve quadratic formula.
