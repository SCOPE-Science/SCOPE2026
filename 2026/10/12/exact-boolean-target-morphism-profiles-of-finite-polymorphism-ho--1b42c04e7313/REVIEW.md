# Review of Exact Boolean-target morphism profiles of finite polymorphism-homogeneous semilattices

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reduces every scalar meet-homomorphism to either the empty \(1\)-fiber (constant zero) or a principal filter. Coordinatewise decomposition gives the homomorphism count. Injectivity is equivalent to occurrence of every join-irreducible threshold, because a missing join-irreducible \(j\) collapses \(j\) with its unique lower cover, while the full set of join-irreducible incidence coordinates determines every finite-lattice element. Inclusion--exclusion then gives the embedding formula. A direct brute-force checker confirms the formulas on chains, a Boolean lattice, \(M_3\), and \(N_5\).

## Originality
PASS with residual folklore risk. The closest source, Tóth--Waldhauser, classifies polymorphism-homogeneous semilattices but does not state the Boolean-target homomorphism or embedding counts. Classical Birkhoff representation supplies a canonical embedding but not the full enumeration in the located sources. Targeted published-finding corpus and web searches for the exact formulas, principal-filter encoding, and embedding count did not return an equivalent statement. Because the proof is short and uses standard facts, an uncatalogued earlier occurrence remains plausible.

## Value
PASS. Boolean powers are the canonical finite targets in semilattice representation and are directly relevant to the injectivity viewpoint in the classification theorem. Replacing mere existence of a Boolean representation by the full homomorphism profile, the full embedding profile, the exact minimal dimension, and the \(r!\) minimal-embedding multiplicity is a natural quantitative refinement. The dependence only on \(n\) and \((n,r)\) respectively is a structural invariance, not an arbitrary finite slice.

## Closest literature and limitations
The classification premise is Theorem 4.1 of Tóth--Waldhauser, arXiv:2007.04405. Classical Birkhoff representation is the closest broader framework. The result uses the binary meet signature without named constants and does not enumerate morphisms between arbitrary distributive-lattice meet reducts.
