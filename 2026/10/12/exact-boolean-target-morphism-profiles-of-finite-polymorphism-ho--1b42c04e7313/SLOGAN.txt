For a finite lattice meet-reduct of size \(n\) with \(r\) join-irreducibles, Boolean-cube targets have the exact profiles \(|\operatorname{Hom}(-,B_e^\wedge)|=(n+1)^e\) and \(|\operatorname{Emb}(-,B_e^\wedge)|=\sum_i(-1)^i\binom{r}{i}(n+1-i)^e\). Hence every finite polymorphism-homogeneous semilattice has minimal Boolean embedding dimension \(r\), with exactly \(r!\) minimal embeddings.
