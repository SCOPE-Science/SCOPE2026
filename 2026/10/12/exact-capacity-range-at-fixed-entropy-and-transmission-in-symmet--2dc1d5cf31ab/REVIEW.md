# Scientific review: Exact capacity range at fixed entropy and transmission in symmetric double-brane conformal interfaces

## Correctness
PASS. Substitution of the symmetric radii into the primary source's exact formulas gives \(c_{\mathrm{eff}}/c=r\), \(c_{LR}/c=2/(2+x+y)\), and \(c_{\mathrm{cap}}/c=2r^2(1+x)(1+y)/(2+x+y)\). Fixing \(t=c_{LR}/c\) fixes \(x+y\); writing \(d=(x-y)/2\) yields the exact quadratic \(c_{\mathrm{cap}}/c=r^2/t-r^2td^2\). The strict Israel bounds give both the feasible transmission interval and the exact open interval for \(d\). The resulting optimization is therefore complete, and the source's two numerical examples are recovered exactly. No finite computation is used to establish an infinite statement.

## Originality
PASS. The 2026 primary source states that the capacity depends on the tension split even when the entropy and energy-transmission data are fixed, and it illustrates this with two choices. It does not state the full feasible capacity interval, the exact quadratic asymmetry coordinate, or the unique equal-tension maximum. Claim-specific searches for those formulations found no covering published result. The 2022 double-brane paper predates the capacity coefficient and therefore cannot contain this range theorem.

## Value
PASS. The result replaces two examples demonstrating independent interface data by a complete sharp classification in the same natural symmetric-radius family. It identifies exactly how much capacity freedom survives after entropy and transmission are fixed, gives the unique maximizer in invariant form \(c_{\mathrm{cap,max}}=c_{\mathrm{eff}}^2/c_{LR}\), and determines the sharp unattained lower edge imposed by the Israel bounds.
