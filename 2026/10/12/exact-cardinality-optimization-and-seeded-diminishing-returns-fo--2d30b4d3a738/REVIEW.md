# Scientific review: Rao diversity and grounded-tree resistance

**Correctness — PASS.** The complete proof explicitly identifies root-to-MRCA ancestry covariance with a positive sum of edge-mass squares, converts minimization of this quadratic energy into series/parallel effective resistance, derives the exact binary tree-knapsack recursion, and proves strict nonempty-set diminishing returns from concave conductance composition. The strict assumptions matter: positive pendant edge lengths ensure a unique strictly positive abundance optimizer, and common tree height is needed for the Rao-to-resistance identity. The actual standalone verifier reports 3,690 independently solved exact-rational convex quadratic instances, 405 exhaustively compared cardinality optima and 83,970 strict marginal triples. This is finite corroboration, not a substitute for the general algebraic proof.

**Originality — PASS, subject to explicit residual uncertainty.** Pavoine–Ollier–Pontier (2005, DOI `10.1016/j.tpb.2005.01.004`) proves, according to its inspected abstract, that maximizing Rao quadratic entropy with ultrametric dissimilarities retains all categories. The full paper was inaccessible, so this is not a claim of full-text noncoverage. Steel (2005, DOI `10.1080/10635150590947023`), whose actual author-posted theorem, proof and extensions were read, gives a greedy optimizer for the different edge-union objective Faith PD, not optimal Rao reweighting. Pavoine–Bonsall (2009, DOI `10.1016/j.tpb.2009.01.008`) discusses maxima of fixed dissimilarity matrices, including nonunique maxima in other metrics. Existing Rao software computes the objective or its fixed-set maximum. The new joint theorem concerns rooted resistance, cardinality-constrained exact tree optimization and the strict seeded set-function law. A general preexisting theorem on grounded-tree conductance or a stronger result in the unavailable 2005 full text could encompass parts of the finding; that risk has not been eliminated by unsuccessful searches.

**Value — PASS.** Conservation planning naturally compares exactly a fixed number of candidate taxa while allowing idealized reallocation of relative abundances. The theorem provides a transparent exact optimum rather than heuristic selection, recovers unique optimal weights, and distinguishes a nonempty-seed diminishing-returns regime from genuinely non-submodular behavior at the empty set. It clarifies why greedy guarantees for Faith PD cannot simply be reused for Rao's entropy. This is an idealized structural benchmark rather than a claim about abundance feasibility in field populations.

**Scientific limitations.** Only positive-length ultrametric rooted binary trees with unconstrained abundance probabilities are proved. Rational lengths permit exact output. Species-specific costs, bounded weights, nonultrametric trees and empirical performance are outside scope. Novelty risk remains in inaccessible or unindexed broader grounded-resistance/ultrametric quadratic optimization literature.
