For every path \(P_s\) and star \(K_{1,m}\) with \(s\ge2\) and \(m\ge3\), all maximum general-position sets in their strong product use exactly two copies of each leaf and occupy at most four path layers. Their number is \(\binom{s}{2}+(3^m-3)(s-2)+(2^m-2)(s-2)(s-3)+(2^m-2)^2\binom{s-2}{2}\), a quadratic in \(s\) that refines the known scalar optimum \(2m\).
