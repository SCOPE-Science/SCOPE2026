# Exact central support of the trace quotient in the Taft Auslander example
## Finding
Let \(k\) be a field of characteristic \(0\), and let \(\lambda\in k\) be a primitive third root of unity. Consider the Taft algebra \(H\) generated by \(g,p\) with
\[
g^3=1,\qquad p^3=0,\qquad gp=\lambda pg,
\]
and \(\Delta(p)=p\otimes 1+g\otimes p\). Let
\[
S=k\langle x,y,z\rangle/(yx-\lambda xy,\ zx-\lambda^2xz,\ zy-\lambda^2yz)
\]
with the action
\[
g\rightharpoonup(x,y,z)=(\lambda^2x,\lambda y,z),\qquad
p\rightharpoonup(x,y,z)=(0,x,y).
\]
Write \(B=S\# H\),
\[
t=3^{-1}(1+g+g^2)p^2,
\]
and let \(T=B(1\#t)B\). For the central polynomial subalgebra
\[
C=k[x^3,y^3,z^3]\subseteq Z(B),
\]
one has
\[
\sqrt{T\cap C}=(x^3,y^3).
\]
Consequently the reduced central support of \(B/T\) over \(C\) is exactly the \(z^3\)-axis. Moreover,
\[
\operatorname{GKdim}(B/T)=1.
\]
In particular, the bound \(\operatorname{GKdim}(B/T)\le 1\) used in the motivating Auslander-isomorphism example is sharp.

## Assumptions and scope
The statement concerns exactly the Taft action appearing in Example 5.7 of Chen--Li--Wu, over characteristic \(0\). The symbol \(\sqrt{T\cap C}\) denotes the ordinary commutative radical inside \(C\). No claim is made about the full noncommutative radical of \(T\), nor about the exact generators of \(T\cap C\).

## Proof
Chen--Li--Wu prove in their Example 5.7 that \(C=k[x^3,y^3,z^3]\) is central in \(B\) and that
\[
x^6\in T,\qquad y^{18}\in T.
\]
Set \(X=x^3\), \(Y=y^3\), and \(Z=z^3\). Then
\[
X^2,Y^6\in T\cap C,
\]
so
\[
(X,Y)\subseteq\sqrt{T\cap C}.
\]

For the reverse inclusion, define a homomorphism
\[
\phi:B\longrightarrow k[z]
\]
by
\[
\phi(x)=\phi(y)=\phi(p)=0,\qquad \phi(g)=1,\qquad \phi(z)=z.
\]
The defining relations of \(S\) and \(H\) are preserved. The smash-product cross-relations are also preserved: they are
\[
gx=\lambda^2xg,\quad gy=\lambda yg,\quad gz=zg,
\]
\[
px=\lambda^2xp,\quad py=x+\lambda yp,\quad pz=y+zp,
\]
and both sides have the same image under \(\phi\). Since \(\phi(t)=0\), the ideal \(T\) is contained in \(\ker\phi\).

If \(F(X,Y,Z)\in T\cap C\), then
\[
0=\phi(F)=F(0,0,z^3).
\]
The substitution map \(k[Z]\to k[z]\), \(Z\mapsto z^3\), is injective, so \(F(0,0,Z)=0\). Hence \(F\in(X,Y)\), and therefore
\[
T\cap C\subseteq(X,Y).
\]
Because \((X,Y)\) is prime,
\[
\sqrt{T\cap C}\subseteq(X,Y).
\]
Together with the opposite inclusion above this gives
\[
\sqrt{T\cap C}=(X,Y)=(x^3,y^3).
\]

Finally, \(\phi\) factors through a surjection \(B/T\twoheadrightarrow k[z]\), so
\[
\operatorname{GKdim}(B/T)\ge 1.
\]
Chen--Li--Wu prove \(\operatorname{GKdim}(B/T)\le1\) by exhibiting central powers in \(T\). Thus equality holds.

## Verification
The proof uses only the displayed presentation and action, together with two inclusions established in the motivating source: \(x^6\in T\) and \(y^{18}\in T\). The lower bound is independently checked by the explicit quotient homomorphism \(\phi\). The map is verified against all generator relations of the smash product, and its restriction \(k[Z]\to k[z]\) is injective. No finite experiment or numerical approximation is used.

## Relationship to prior work
Chen--Li--Wu introduce quotient Morita criteria and in Example 5.7 prove the Auslander map is an isomorphism for this Taft action. Their argument establishes the upper bound \(\operatorname{GKdim}(B/T)\le1\) by proving suitable central powers lie in \(T\); the inspected text does not identify the radical of \(T\cap C\) or prove equality of the GK dimension. Cline--Gaddis classify the underlying Taft actions on quantum affine spaces and supply the action family used here, but do not study this trace ideal. Bao--He--Zhang develop the general pertinency framework for Hopf actions; the present statement is an exact support calculation for this specific nonsemisimple action.

## Limitations
The result determines only the reduced contraction of \(T\) to \(C\), not the full ideal \(T\cap C\), its primary structure, or the structure of \(B/T\) as a noncommutative algebra. It also does not assert that analogous Taft actions have the same support. The originality comparison is limited by ordinary literature indexing: an unindexed computation of this exact trace contraction could still exist.
