For a spider tree, ordinary color-refinement depth depends only on the longest leg and the second-largest distinct leg length: it is \(\lceil L/2\rceil\) when all legs have the same length, and otherwise \(\max\{\lceil L/2\rceil,S+1\}\). Among \(d\)-leg spiders on \(N\) vertices, the sharp maximum is \(\lfloor(N-d)/2\rfloor+1\).
