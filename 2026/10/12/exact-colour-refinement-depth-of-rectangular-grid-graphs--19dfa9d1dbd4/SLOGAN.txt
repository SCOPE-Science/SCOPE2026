Ordinary colour refinement on \(P_m\square P_n\) stabilizes in exactly \(\lfloor(m-1)/2\rfloor\) rounds for square grids and \(\max\{m-1,\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor\}\) rounds for unequal rectangular grids, starting from a uniform colour. At stabilization its colours coincide with the vertex-automorphism orbits, so even a one-column change from square to non-square can nearly double the required depth.
