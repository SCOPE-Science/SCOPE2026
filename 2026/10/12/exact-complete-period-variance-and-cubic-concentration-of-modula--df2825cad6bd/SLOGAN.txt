For the modular-energy function \(E_m(n)=\sum_{k\le m}(n\bmod k)\), the exact variance over a full \(\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,m)\) period is \(\frac1{12}(\sum_{d\le m}J_2(d)\lfloor m/d\rfloor^2-m^2)\), asymptotic to \(\frac{2\zeta(2)-\zeta(3)}{36\zeta(3)}m^3\). Hence \(E_m(n)/m^2\) converges in probability to \(1/4\) over complete periods, with deviation proportion \(O_\varepsilon(m^{-1})\) for each fixed threshold \(\varepsilon>0\).
