For every multiplicity \(r\ge2\), the uniform coupon collector has an exact finite transform \(\mathbb E[K_{m,r}x^{T_m}]\) obtained from a Stirling-number generating function. For doubletons this yields an explicit covariance with completion time that is strictly negative for every \(m\ge3\) and has leading order \(-m(\log m)^2/2\).
