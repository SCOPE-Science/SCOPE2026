For a stable two-mode diagonal discrete-time system with one freely oriented scalar actuator, the Gramian condition number is minimized exactly by equalizing the two modal diagonal Gramian energies. The optimal value is \(\coth^2(|\operatorname{artanh}(a)-\operatorname{artanh}(d)|/2)\), so near-coincident modes force quadratic ill-conditioning no matter how the actuator is rotated.
