All six length-three binary block-parity words whose defining pattern contains both digits have completely classified maximal constant-run lengths and rational limiting frequencies. The pairs `001`/`110`, `010`/`101`, and `011`/`100` have identical run-length frequency distributions, while only `001`, `010`, and `011` exhibit unique anomalously long runs starting at index \(0\), of lengths \(9\), \(10\), and \(11\), respectively.
