For any finite prime set \(S\), every target \(y\) coprime to \(\prod_{p\in S}p\) has an exactly parametrized arithmetic-subderivative fiber indexed by subsets \(T\subseteq S\) with \(H(T)\mid y\). This gives exact minimum and maximum fiber sizes on the coprime-target stratum, with at least \(|S|\) preimages for every such target.
