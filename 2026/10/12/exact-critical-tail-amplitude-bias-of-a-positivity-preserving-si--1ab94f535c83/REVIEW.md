# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The claim is exactly the zero-diffusion, constant-step critical restriction of the source recurrence, whose denominators were checked against equations (12)–(13) and (16)–(17). All admissible initial conditions with positive infection are covered, for every positive step. The proof establishes positivity, boundedness, extinction, convergence of the susceptible-to-infected deficit ratio, then a reciprocal-infection Cesàro limit. A separate ODE calculation establishes the physical-time normalization. The arithmetic checker passes the recurrence identities and reproduces the asymptotic coefficient. The finite computation is not invoked as a proof of infinity.

**Originality — PASS, within the precise stated scope.** The complete, openly accessible Schurz–Tosun paper contains unconditional positivity and fixed-finite-horizon convergence, not a critical-regime \(1/t\) leading-coefficient theorem or its step-size bias. Related stochastic SIS work analyzes linearized stochastic thresholds rather than the deterministic SIR nonlinear critical tail; neither type of statement directly entails the other. Zhou–Hu–Zhao study a transformed explicit–implicit stochastic SIR method, not the same update map. General critical slowing and numerical consistency principles do not entail the particular factor \(1+kh\) without analyzing the exact recurrence. Full access to a related, different numerical-method paper could not be obtained, so unindexed same-coefficient results are a residual risk.

**Value — PASS.** At exact invasion criticality, prevalence has a universal algebraic tail; using its amplitude to compare critical relaxation is mathematically motivated, not an arbitrary narrow numerical table entry. The source emphasizes unconditional dynamic consistency, yet the method's asymptotic leading coefficient differs from the ODE by a precise factor and the bias persists as time tends to infinity for each fixed step. This isolates which quantitative property is not guaranteed by positivity and fixed-time convergence, relevant to critical-regime numerical interpretation.

**Closest literature and limits.** Schurz–Tosun (2026), DOI 10.1007/s00285-026-02392-4, provides the model and algorithm. Zhou–Hu–Zhao (2024 online), DOI 10.1007/s11075-024-01986-7, analyzes a distinct stochastic SIR method; only abstract and source preview were available. The theorem is deterministic, exactly critical, constant-step, and asserts neither stochastic nor uniform-in-horizon convergence failure.
