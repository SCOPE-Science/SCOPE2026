At the deterministic epidemic threshold, the unconditional-positive SIR balanced implicit method has \(\lim_{n\to\infty}nhI_n=\mu(1+kh)/(\beta k)\), whereas the exact SIR flow has \(\lim_{t\to\infty}tI(t)=\mu/(\beta k)\). The asymptotic infected-amplitude bias is exactly \(1+kh\) for every fixed step \(h>0\), despite convergence on fixed finite horizons.
