For a general-position arrangement of \(n\ge4\) lines, an explicit nonlinear change of reciprocal-plane coordinates identifies each singular completed local ring with the full Veronese cone \(\mathbb C[[s,t]]^{\mu_{n-2}}\), not merely with its tangent cone. Its local divisor-class group is \(\mathbb Z/(n-2)\), its Gorenstein index is \( (n-2)/\gcd(n-2,2)\), and its minimal log discrepancy is \(2/(n-2)\).
