For the explicit gradient-aligned Cauchy trust-region step, the exact quarter-descent threshold is \(b_*(L,M)=M^2/(4M-2L)\) when \(L\le M/2\) and \(b_*(L,M)=2L/3\) otherwise. It is universally sharp, even against connected two-agent quadratic-consensus limits, and can be nine times smaller than the previously sufficient \(2(L+M)\).
