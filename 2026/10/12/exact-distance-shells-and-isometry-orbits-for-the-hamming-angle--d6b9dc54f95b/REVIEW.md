# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof normalizes the center by an explicit monomial isometry, partitions a second vector into outside-support nonzeros and support symbol multiplicities, and counts every scalar-invariant distance class exactly. The zero vector is removed before dividing by the uniform \(q-1\) orbit size. The orbit argument separately proves within-weight transitivity and excludes cross-weight isometries from distinct shell profiles. Exact replay over \(\mathbb F_2\), \(\mathbb F_3\), \(\mathbb F_4\), and \(\mathbb F_5\) confirms all shell coefficients for dimensions through four.

The closest broader sphere-size literature under the name “projective metric” uses a different translation-invariant vector-space definition. Targeted searches and the checked prior records did not locate a stronger statement or exact table covering this claim.

Residual risk: the scalar-minimized Hamming quantity predates its angular formulation in specialized coding contexts, so an older differently named distance-distribution computation could exist without having appeared in the searches. No decisive unresolved comparison was found.

The formula covers every prime power, dimension, radius, and center weight, while the shell profiles expose the precise nonhomogeneity of the metric through its vertex-orbit partition.
