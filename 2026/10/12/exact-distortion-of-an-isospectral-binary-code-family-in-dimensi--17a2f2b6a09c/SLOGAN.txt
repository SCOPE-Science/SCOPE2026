There are permutation-inequivalent binary dimension-three codes with identical ordinary weight enumerators whose exact minimum linear distortion is \(\bigl((2t+2)/(2t+1)\bigr)^2\) at every length \(6+7t\), for all integers \(t\ge0\). A dependent-versus-independent Fano-plane triple of minimum-weight messages forces the optimal distortion and makes it tend sharply to \(1\) without ever reaching it.
