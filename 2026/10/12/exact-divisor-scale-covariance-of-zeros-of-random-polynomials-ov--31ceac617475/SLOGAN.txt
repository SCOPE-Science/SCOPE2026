Uniform random polynomials over \(\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z\) have an exact, degree-independent zero-indicator covariance \(\operatorname{Cov}(I_a,I_b)=(\gcd(m,a_1-b_1,\ldots,a_n-b_n)-1)/m^2\), which decomposes into positive contributions from every nontrivial congruence scale. Summing these contributions proves the prime-power univariate variance \(k(1-1/p)\) conjectured by Jain, Moon and Wu and gives exact variances for every modulus and number of variables.
