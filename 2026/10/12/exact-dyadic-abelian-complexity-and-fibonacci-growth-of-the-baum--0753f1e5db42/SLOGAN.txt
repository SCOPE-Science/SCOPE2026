Every binary factor of the Baum–Sweet word with length \(2^k\) has at most \(F_{k+2}\) ones, and the initial factor attains the bound; every intermediate one-count occurs, so its exact abelian complexity at this length is \(F_{k+2}+1\). Across all lengths the abelian complexity grows on the scale \(n^{\log_2\varphi}\), where \(\varphi\) is the golden ratio.
