The exact power of \(2\) dividing the Euler characteristic of the complete-quadrics variety is \(\left\lceil n/2\right\rceil-s_2(n)\), with one extra spike \(\nu_2(n+1)\) exactly when \(n\equiv3\pmod4\). Consequently this Euler characteristic is odd only in dimensions indexed by \(n=1\) and \(n=2\).
