For the regular paperfolding word, every Dyck factor of length \(2^K-2^r\), \(1\le r<K\), starts exactly at boundary offsets congruent to \(2^r-1\) or \(2^K\) modulo \(2^{K+1}\), and no other positive lengths occur. Exactly one distinct factor occurs at lengths \(2\) and \(4\), exactly two at each other permitted length, and the starting-position density for fixed \((K,r)\) is \(2^{-K}\).
