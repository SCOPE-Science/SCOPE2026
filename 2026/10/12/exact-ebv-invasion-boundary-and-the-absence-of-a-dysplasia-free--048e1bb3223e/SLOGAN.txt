For the six-compartment nasopharyngeal carcinoma model, the claimed dysplasia-free equilibrium is not stationary, and the actual carcinoma-free equilibrium is stable exactly below the EBV feedback threshold \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{EBV}}=\beta ea_3D_0/[d_6(d_3+\gamma-a_3)]\) under positive removal assumptions. Exact positive parameters show that apoptosis rates can dominate proliferation rates while the purportedly stable carcinoma-free state is nonetheless unstable.
