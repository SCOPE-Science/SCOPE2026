For the noisy Werner–Holevo qutrit channel, the exact entanglement-breaking depth is the first integer \(k\) satisfying \(5(1-3x/2)^k+3(1-x/2)^k\le2\) and \(3(1-x/2)^k-5(1-3x/2)^k\le1\). The zero-private-capacity example at \(x=1/2\) breaks reference entanglement only at the fourth power, and the depth grows as \(1.463405430167/x\) for weak noise.
