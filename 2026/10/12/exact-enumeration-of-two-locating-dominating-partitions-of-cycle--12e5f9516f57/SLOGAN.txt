For the labeled cycle \(C_n\), \(n\ge5\), an ordered two-part locating-dominating partition is exactly a cyclic binary word avoiding \(000\), \(111\), \(00100\), and \(11011\). Its all-orders rational enumerator has denominator \(1-x^2-x^4-2x^5-x^6-x^8\) and counts \(a_n\sim(1.51287639686409)^n\) such partitions, with a separately determined four-vertex boundary.
