For Atanassov's modified functions \(\Phi(n)=\prod(p^a-1)\) and \(\Psi(n)=\prod(p^a+1)\), three published inequalities have exact equality loci governed only by factorization type. The mixed difference vanishes exactly on prime powers and squarefree integers, the modified sum equals \(2n\) exactly on prime powers, and the modified product meets \(\varphi(n)\psi(n)\) exactly on squarefree integers.
