For the published rational weak Ziegler pair, the first integral conic-line arrangement has precisely two bad incidence-reduction primes \(p\ge7\): at \(7\), two pairs of lines coalesce, and at \(11\), a line becomes tangent to the conic at a four-branch point. The second arrangement preserves its complete ordinary eleven-point incidence structure at every prime \(p\ge7\).
