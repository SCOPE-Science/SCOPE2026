# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The all-length proof uses unambiguous length-eight marker synchronization (`0000` and `11`), a bijective desubstitution of every long bispecial factor under \(u\mapsto01\beta(u)0\), an exhaustive seven-triple base of all possible short contexts, and the standard bilateral-order double-difference identity. The formula and exact accumulation bounds follow algebraically. A standalone standard-library checker reproduces the finite base and tests all factor lengths through \(260\), but the infinite statement rests on the structural argument.

**Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk.** The exact original object is Andrade–Mol's 2024 rich additive-power-free fixed point (arXiv:2408.15390v1); the primary first and revised versions do not state its exact factor-complexity function or normalized complexity oscillation. Shallit–Shur (arXiv:1801.05376v1) treats factor complexity for other power-free words and supplies thematic motivation, without covering this particular morphism. Searches for the exact image words, subword/factor aliases, OEIS-style sequences, and indexed results found no equivalent or dominating statement. An unindexed equivalent remains possible.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The result completely determines a natural invariant of an independently motivated extremal avoidability construction and finds two exact distinct accumulation slopes. Neither the basic recognizability of uniform substitutions nor the original richness proof alone implies the piecewise-5-adic formula.

**Limits.** This is ordinary contiguous complexity of the specific fixed point, not an extremal bound for all power-free or all rich words. Computational enumeration is finite corroboration; no independent audit is asserted.
