# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof treats every positive modulus, every target residue, and every positive moment order. The dyadic doubling map is proved precisely two-to-one using opposite parity in the difference factorization; the odd-prime-power cases follow from explicit valuation classes and unit-root lifting. CRT combines these independent factors. The moment formula follows from exact counts of discriminant valuations and the collision formula has a separate product-congruence proof. The verifier checks 1,000 whole moduli and several moment orders, but is not substituted for the proof.

## Originality — PASS relative to checked literature
Ulness (2022), Theorem 5, already gives the qualitative square-discriminant criterion. The source explicitly labels the two exact saturation statements as Conjectures 1 and 2 rather than proving them. This result proves those statements and gives a complete numerical formula for each fiber and for moments of every order across arbitrary moduli. The checked 2019 triangular-lacunary paper addresses Gauss-sum curves rather than the full arithmetic fiber moment hierarchy. Searches using aliases for quadratic residues, prime-power saturation, and modular collisions did not yield a dominating closed-form theorem. Important limitation: the primary paper's relevant text was read through indexed open-access HTML, but direct complete-page retrieval was rate-limited; a parallel equivalent formula, especially in classical quadratic-residue counting references, cannot be excluded.

## Value — PASS
The paper explicitly highlights multiplicity and saturation as principal invariants and conjectures its prime-power and dyadic laws. The full fiber theorem handles all residue classes and moduli, its all-moment product summarizes multiplicity concentration at every scale, and it resolves both motivated conjectures without numerical truncation. This is a complete classification of a natural distribution, not an arbitrary finite numerical extension. The fact that classical Hensel lifting provides a short proof is disclosed rather than presented as a new method.

## Limitations
No independent audit has been performed. Full-document access to one directly relevant source was constrained; its pertinent conjecture statements and structural results were inspected. A literature equivalence not captured by the searches remains possible. Finite executable checks verify boundary cases, not the infinite theorem.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
