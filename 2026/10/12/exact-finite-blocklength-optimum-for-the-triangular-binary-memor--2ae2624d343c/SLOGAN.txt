For the triangular binary channel with memory one, the exact maximum length-\(n\) zero-error code is the set of binary words avoiding \(111\) and boundary \(11\), with sizes \(3,5,9,17,31,\ldots\) obeying the tribonacci recurrence. The proof identifies an exact confusability partition after eliminating \(111\), upgrading the known asymptotic capacity to an all-length optimum.
