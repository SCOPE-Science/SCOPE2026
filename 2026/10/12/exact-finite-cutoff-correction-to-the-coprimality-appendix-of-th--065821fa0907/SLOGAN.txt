The finite-cutoff coprimality bounds used in the continuous-variable Shor appendix are not valid for every \(N\ge a\): explicit counterexamples occur already at small \(a\) and \(N\). Exact Möbius formulas replace the failed bounds and recover the intended \(\Omega((\log\log a)^{-2})\) scale only after taking a large-cutoff limit for fixed \(a\).
