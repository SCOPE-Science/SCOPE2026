For the standard filiform Lie algebra \(L_n(\mathbb F_q)\), \(n\ge4\), the fixed-point-free automorphisms are counted exactly by a divisor sum over orders in \(\mathbb F_q^\times\). They exist precisely when \(q>n\), or when \(q\le n\) and \(q-1\) is composite; in dimension four the only exceptional fields are \(\mathbb F_2,\mathbb F_3,\mathbb F_4\).
