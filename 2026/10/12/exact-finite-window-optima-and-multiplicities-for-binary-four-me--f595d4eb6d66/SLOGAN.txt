For binary four-mer hitting sets, the exact minimum sizes and numbers of labeled optimizers at window lengths \(4,5,6,7,8,9,10\) are respectively \((16,1),(10,2),(9,50),(7,8),(7,52),(6,10),(6,14)\); at all larger lengths the pair is \((6,30)\). The thirty previously known minimum decycling sets split by shortest hitting-window threshold into groups of sizes \(10,4,16\) at lengths \(9,10,11\), refining the previously known extremal path-length range.
