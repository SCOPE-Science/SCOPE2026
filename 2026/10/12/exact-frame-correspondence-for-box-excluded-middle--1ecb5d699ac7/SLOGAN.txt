On standard birelational frames, \(\Box\varphi\vee\neg\Box\varphi\) is valid exactly when \(x\le y\wedge xRz\) always admits \(u\) with \(yRu\wedge u\le z\). This exact condition is strictly weaker than the published \(\mathsf{BEM}\) condition, and the gap already appears on a minimal two-point frame.
