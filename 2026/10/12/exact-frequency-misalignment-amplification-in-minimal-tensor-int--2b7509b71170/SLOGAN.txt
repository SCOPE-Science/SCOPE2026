For rank-one t-DEIM with two Fourier frequencies, a single shared T-ARP pivot has an exact worst-case squared-error factor \(K=\max_\ell\{q/t_\ell+(1-q)/(1-t_\ell)\}\), with \(q=(t_1+t_2)/2\). Anti-aligned frequency leverages force \(K=1/(2t(1-t))\), making the published \(1+\eta\) alignment penalty asymptotically sharp even though every sampled basis is invertible; independent per-frequency pivots retain factor two.
