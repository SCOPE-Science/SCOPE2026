For every additive map \(f:\mathbb F_p^r\to\mathbb F_p\), its least normalization-free Frobenius degree is the sum of the standard representatives \(0,\ldots,p-1\) of its coordinate coefficients. Thus every additive map on the split algebra has finite degree at most \(r(p-1)\), with exact degree enumerator \((1+t+\cdots+t^{p-1})^r\).\n
