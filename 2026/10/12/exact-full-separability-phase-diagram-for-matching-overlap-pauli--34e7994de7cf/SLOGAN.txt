For every Pauli Hamiltonian whose term-overlap graph is a matching, full separability of the Gibbs state at **any** temperature is characterized exactly by the parity and number of locally incompatible sites in each two-term component. The three-qubit \(X^{\otimes3}+Z^{\otimes3}\) component loses full separability above an explicit threshold even though its Gibbs state is PPT across every bipartition.
