Under Gaussian sampling, sample mean absolute deviation and sample variance satisfy the exact identity \(\operatorname{Cov}(M_n,S_n^2)=\sigma^2\mathbb E M_n/(n-1)\). More generally, every degree-\(p\) homogeneous statistic of the Gaussian residual vector has covariance \(p\sigma^2\mathbb ET/(n-1)\) with sample variance, and the resulting MAD–variance correlation has an explicit finite-sample formula.
