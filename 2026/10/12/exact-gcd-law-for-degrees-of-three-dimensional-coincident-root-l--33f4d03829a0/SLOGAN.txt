For binary forms of degree \(d\), the gcd of the projective degrees of all three-dimensional coincident root loci has no prime factors beyond \(2\) and \(3\). Its exact value is \(2^{\alpha(d)}3^{\beta(d)}\), with the two-primary exponent determined by \(d\bmod4\) and the factor \(3\) disappearing exactly when \(\nu_3(d)=1\).
