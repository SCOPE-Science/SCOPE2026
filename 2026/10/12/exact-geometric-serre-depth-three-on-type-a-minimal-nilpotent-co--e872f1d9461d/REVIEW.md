# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. For \(n\ge3\), the punctured minimal type-\(A\) cone is the complement of the zero section in \(T^*\mathbb P^{n-1}\). Its sphere-bundle model has simply connected base and fiber, so the punctured cone is simply connected; the Gysin sequence gives \(H^1=0\) and \(H^2\cong\mathbb C\). Juteau's full integral cohomology table independently gives the same low-degree groups. Since the affine cone is contractible, the first nonzero vertex local cohomology of the constant sheaf occurs in degree \(3\), where it is one-dimensional. Xu's Proposition 3.3 therefore gives exact \(S_3\) and failure of \(S_4\). Proposition 3.5 then forces every \(S_2\) sheaf to be the direct image of a local system from the smooth stratum; simple connectivity makes it a direct sum of constants, and the same degree-three obstruction excludes every nonzero one from the geometric MCM category.

## Originality
PASS. The initiating paper explicitly treats only the \(3\times3\) rank-one nilpotent cone. The full type-\(A\) minimal-orbit cohomology was known, but it predates and does not discuss geometric Serre conditions. Targeted searches for the exact uniform \(S_3\)-not-\(S_4\) statement, constructible MCM vanishing, cotangent-bundle aliases, and minimal-nilpotent terminology found no statement implying the new \(n\ge4\) conclusion. A highly relevant 2026 paper by the same author studies coherent algebraic MCM sheaves on the same orbit closures; it does not concern the constructible geometric MCM category and therefore does not cover this claim.

## Value
PASS. The result identifies a stable, dimension-independent defect across an infinite family of increasingly high-dimensional symplectic singularities: geometric constructible depth remains exactly \(3\). The categorical consequence is stronger than a one-number calculation: the natural two-stratum constructible MCM category is zero, in sharp contrast with the rich coherent MCM theory on the same spaces. This gives a clean test family for the newly introduced geometric Serre formalism.

## Closest literature and limitations
Xu's Example 3.4 is the exact \(n=3\) base case. Juteau supplies the classical topology needed for all type-\(A\) ranks. Xu's earlier coherent-MCM paper is the closest categorical comparison but lives in a different sheaf category. The conclusion is restricted to complex coefficients and the natural two-stratum orbit stratification. An unindexed note could contain the same synthesis.
