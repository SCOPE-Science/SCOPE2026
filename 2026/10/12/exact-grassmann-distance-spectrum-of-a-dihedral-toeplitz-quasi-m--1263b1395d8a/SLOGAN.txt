The direct row spaces of the Lee--Park dihedral Toeplitz code \(E_1(m,n)\) form a projective constant-dimension code whose pairwise intersections are controlled exactly by ordinary polynomial gcds. If \(t=\max(\deg f,\deg g)-\deg\gcd(f,g)\), then the distance is \(2\min(m,t)\), and the complete ordered \(t\)-spectrum is \(N_t=(q+1)q^{2t-1}(q^{s-t+1}-1)/(q-1)\).
