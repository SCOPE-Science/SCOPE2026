For a directed hierarchical population chain with identical birth and death rates and strictly positive predation, every nonempty deterministic community converges exponentially: its survivors are exactly the left-to-right greedy subset of the species present initially. For three initially present species, the final ratio of species 3 to species 1 equals \(\frac{x_3(0)}{x_1(0)}\exp[-\frac{p_2x_2(0)}{p_1x_1(0)}]\), giving an explicit permanent ecological memory of the extinct intermediate species.
