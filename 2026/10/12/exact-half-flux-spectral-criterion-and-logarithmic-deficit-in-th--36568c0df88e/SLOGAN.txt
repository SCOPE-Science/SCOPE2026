At half flux, the magnetic preferred-orientation square lattice has an exact two-branch momentum-spectrum criterion in \(\sin^2 k\). Its known density \(1/2\) is approached with the sharper law \(K/2-2(2-\sqrt2)\pi^{-1}\log K+O(1)\), because narrow integer-resonance bands are outweighed by \(k^{-1}\)-scale gaps around half-integer momenta.
