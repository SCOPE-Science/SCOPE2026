For the actual ordered one-tree `SampleClique` replacement of the Schur clique of an unweighted \(n\)-leaf star, the random Laplacian is unbiased but its expected pseudoinverse is exactly \(n/2\) times the exact clique pseudoinverse. Every realized tree also has a fixed leaf pair whose resistance is inflated by \(n/2\), showing a dimension-growing inverse distortion despite connectedness and perfect primal unbiasedness.
