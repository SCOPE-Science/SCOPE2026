# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The model uses the exact sign-rule update from Hughes et al., not a smooth mean-field surrogate. At every node, the intralayer field magnitude is at most its degree; once inhibitory coupling exceeds the maximum degree the cross-layer sign dominates. This proves the full-map involution and excludes ties. Explicit all-positive states establish failure of the identical deterministic map at and below the boundary (with fair tie handling at equality). The complete orbit census and biased product-law probabilities then follow by exact independent site counting. The packaged finite enumerator independently checks the identities on all graph pairs with up to three vertices. It is a finite check, not a substitute for the unrestricted proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

The motivating 2026 article studies polarized absorbing states and metastability numerically at cubic degree and coupling negative one, not the exact strong-coupling microstate phase. The classical Goles–Olivos threshold theorem gives an eventual period-at-most-two conclusion for symmetric threshold systems; that implication does not specify an immediate involution, the precise sitewise invariants, or counts and biased-ensemble law. Published findings index comparisons and literature searches for sign dominance, two-layer negative coupling, and exact orbit counts found no same-model theorem covering these sharper statements. The 1980 full text could not be inspected; its accessible abstract does not settle every possible special case. Failure to locate another source is not treated as proof of uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This is a natural complete classification of the finite microscopic dynamics in a strong-antagonism parameter region of a model introduced to explain selection between polarized outcomes. The classification identifies exponentially many genuine oscillatory attractors and reveals that, beyond a sharp network-degree barrier, the typical initialized system never reaches a stationary polarized state at all. Exact enumeration of all fixed and period-two orbits plus an all-bias binomial law are model-level structural consequences, not a calculation on an arbitrary microstate subset. The regime is distinct from the paper's baseline simulations and supplies a useful exact boundary for interpreting simulation outcomes.

## Scope and risks

The sharp threshold concerns only the all-state involution identity; it is not a global phase-transition criterion outside this regime. Fixed graph topology and a uniform scalar negative cross-coupling are assumed. No claim is made about the finite-time three-category numerical entropy statistics or about full noise perturbations at nonzero fields. Potential overlap with a specialized unpublished or unindexed exact enumeration remains possible. The classical 1980 paper was available in bibliographic abstract form only, so its stronger unexamined details remain a source-access risk.
