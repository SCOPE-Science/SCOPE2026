# Scientific review
## Correctness — PASS
The quotient is Zariski-locally a product with the weighted plane \(\mathbb P(1,1,d)\). On its singular chart the exact quadratic Hankel presentation gives all \((d-1)\)-minors degree at least \(d-1\). The selected triangular minor is \(z_0^{d-1}\); characteristic-zero \(\operatorname{GL}_2\) polarization then supplies every degree-\(d-1\) Veronese monomial, proving equality of the Fitting and maximal-ideal powers. Smooth-factor Fitting identities glue the result and the Hilbert series gives the length. A standalone exact checker confirms four illustrative degrees but is not the proof.

This isolates meaningful infinitesimal structure invisible to the global \(K\)-regularity statement and is not a mere alternative presentation of non-smoothness.
