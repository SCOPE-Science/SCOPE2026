For the Tran–Nguyen realization domains \(D_{m,n}\), the valuative dimension is exactly \(n-1\): the base domains have Jaffard defect \(n-m+1\), their polynomial rings are Jaffard exactly on the diagonal \(m=n\), and their integer-valued polynomial rings are always Jaffard. In the base family, the weak coefficient transform itself is Jaffard of dimension \(n-1\), isolating the full hidden valuative dimension.\n
