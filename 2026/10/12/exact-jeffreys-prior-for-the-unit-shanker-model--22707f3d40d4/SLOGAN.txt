For the unit Shanker model, Jeffreys' rule gives \(\pi_J(\alpha)\propto\sqrt{\frac{2(3\alpha^2+1)}{\alpha^2(\alpha^2+1)^2}+\frac{\alpha^2}{\alpha^2+1}e^{\alpha^2}E_1(\alpha^2)}\), not \(1/\alpha\). The source's \(1/\alpha\) analysis remains a proper-posterior log-uniform-prior analysis for every nonempty interior sample, but it is not a Jeffreys-rule analysis.
