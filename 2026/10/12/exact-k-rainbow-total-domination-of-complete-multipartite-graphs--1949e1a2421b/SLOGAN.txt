For \(G=K_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}\), \(k\ge2\), order \(N\), and smallest part size \(s\), the \(k\)-rainbow total domination number is \(\min\{N,2k,k\}\) when \(s\le\lfloor k/2\rfloor\), and \(\min\{N,2k,s+\lfloor k/2\rfloor+1\}\) otherwise. This strictly extends the published complete-bipartite formula to arbitrary complete multipartite graphs and therefore gives \(\gamma_t(G\square K_k)\) as well.
