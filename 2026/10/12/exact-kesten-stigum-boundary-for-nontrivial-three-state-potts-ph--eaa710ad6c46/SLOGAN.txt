For the three-state ferromagnetic Potts model on the order-four Cayley tree, the Kesten–Stigum test for a nontrivial TISGM is exact in the root parameter \(t=z^{1/4}\): it holds precisely for \(0<t<\rho\), where \(\rho^3+\rho^2+\rho=1\). Equivalently, the \(z<1\) branch is certified non-extreme exactly for \(\theta>2(1+\rho)=3.087378025384152\ldots\), while no \(z>1\) branch ever satisfies the test.
