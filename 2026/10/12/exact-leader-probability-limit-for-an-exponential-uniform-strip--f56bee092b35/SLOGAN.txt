For \(Z=(X,X+V)\) with \(X\sim\operatorname{Exp}(1)\) and \(V\sim\operatorname{Unif}(0,1)\), the leader probability converges exactly to \(\int_0^1[1+w(e^w-1)]^{-1}\,dw=0.7323592996919\ldots\). This is strictly below \(\pi/4<0.8\), correcting the motivating paper's simulation suggestion for this exact model.
