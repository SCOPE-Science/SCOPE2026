For every rectangular rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\), the \(k\)-limited domination number is exactly \(\min\{m\max(1,n-k),n\max(1,m-k)\}\), making the Cartesian-product upper bound sharp for all dimensions and capacities. When \(1\le k<\min(m,n)\), every optimum is a slab of complete parallel rows or columns, with an exact binomial count of minimizers.
