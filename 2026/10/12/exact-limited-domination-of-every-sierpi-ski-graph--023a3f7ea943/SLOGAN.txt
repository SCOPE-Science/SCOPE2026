Every Sierpiński graph satisfies \(\gamma_k^{\mathrm L}(S(n,m))=(m-k)m^{n-1}\) for \(n\ge1\), \(m\ge2\), and \(1\le k\le m-1\), confirming the full conjecture posed in October 2026. A uniform construction selects vertex words according to a symmetric regular relation on their final two symbols, and a two-level block inequality proves optimality.
