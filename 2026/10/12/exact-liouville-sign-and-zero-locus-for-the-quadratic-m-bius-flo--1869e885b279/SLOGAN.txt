For the quadratic Möbius-floor recurrence, the sign of every nonzero term is the Liouville sign \((-1)^{\Omega(n)}\), and the zeros occur exactly when the exponent of \(2\) exceeds the odd-part prime-factor count by at least three. The sequence is congruent to \(\mu\) modulo \(2\) and equals \(-1\) precisely at the primes.
