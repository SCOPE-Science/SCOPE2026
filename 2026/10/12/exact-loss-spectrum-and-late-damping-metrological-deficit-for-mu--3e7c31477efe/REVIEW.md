# Mathematical assessment: damped multi-excitation Dicke probes

## Correctness
The exact Kraus output decomposes by surviving-excitation number into disjointness-incidence Gram matrices. Complementing columns gives Boolean-inclusion operators. Their harmonic-chain eigenvalues, multiplicities and adjacent collective-spin matrix elements yield the exact total symmetric-logarithmic-derivative Fisher formula, including transitions to zero eigenspaces. The first-order expansion near complete damping is \(F_{N,k}(1-\epsilon)=N-2k(N-k+2)\epsilon+O(\epsilon^2)\), and the direct endpoints are \(N+2k(N-k)\) and \(N\). The analytical proof works for all \(N\ge4\), \(2\le k<N\); numerical checks do not replace that proof.

## Historical comparison
One-excitation W-state results (Ozaydin, arXiv:1403.2376) are outside the excitation range. General pure-Dicke metrology (Tóth–Apellaniz, arXiv:1405.4878) and observable-based QFI witness bounds (Apellaniz et al., arXiv:1511.05203) provide neither the binomial Kraus output spectrum nor the full reset derivative. Sudha et al. (arXiv:2601.23043) study overlapping local damping and balanced eight-qubit numerical probes; that finite numerical subcase is already covered by earlier work. Hartmann (arXiv:1201.1732) provides broader permutation-symmetric Lindblad machinery rather than the specific exact eigenvalue-and-Fisher formula. He et al. (arXiv:2512.14821) estimate a different field parameter with damping after encoding. The complete all-excitation claim appears distinct on inspected evidence, with a real residual risk from unknown stronger permutation-representation analyses. A literature search cannot certify global priority.

## Value and limitations
The exact rank-by-rank spectrum and polynomial-size spin-QFI evaluation provide an all-excitation analytical benchmark for independent identical zero-temperature loss. The universal below-shot-noise interval immediately before full reset is a motivated sharp endpoint distinction, not an extrapolation of finite curves. No heterogeneous decay, optimal measurement, damping-after-encoding or uniqueness of Fisher threshold crossings is claimed. Formal proof checking and external expert attestation are not claimed.
