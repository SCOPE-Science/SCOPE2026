# Scientific review: multi-excitation Dicke probes under loss

## Correctness
**PASS.** The proof derives the Kraus output entries for each fixed surviving-excitation block, not just the total excitation weights. Complementing the lost-set incidence matrix converts the block into an ordinary Boolean-lattice inclusion Gram matrix. The raising/lowering commutator, primitive-chain multiplicities, and exact singular values prove the full spectrum for every specified particle number. Inserting these eigenvalues into the spectral symmetric-logarithmic-derivative definition, with support-to-kernel terms retained, proves the collective-spin formula. Endpoint calculations and a complete first-order small-survival expansion establish the strict sub-shot-noise window. A separate dense computational-basis Kraus calculation matches the formula in 75 finite test cases, and 95 inclusion Gram spectra match independently. Numerical checks corroborate but do not certify the infinite proof.

## Originality
**PASS with residual priority risk.** The closest direct literature includes Ozaydin's single-excitation W-state QFI, Tóth–Apellaniz's general Dicke-metrology exposition, Apellaniz and collaborators' QFI witness bounds for noisy Dicke states, and Sudha and collaborators' explicit eight-qubit numerical comparison with local amplitude damping. The single-excitation W-state case is explicitly excluded. Pair concurrence concerns reduced two-qubit entanglement, not collective-spin Fisher information. The modern balanced-state numerical plot overlaps the same physical process at a finite size but does not give the all-size rank-resolved spectrum, exact all-excitation Fisher formula, or universal late-damping coefficient. Targeted published-finding corpus and external literature searches disclosed no mathematically equivalent or stronger complete formula; that negative search is not proof of publication priority. The possibility of a stronger undetected representation-theoretic theorem remains.

## Value
**PASS.** The output is an exact nontrivial classification of a physically motivated noisy metrology benchmark for every multi-excitation Dicke family member. A priori, independent loss generally fills many permutation irreducibles and has an exponentially sized density operator. The binomial-chain spectral resolution reduces the computation to polynomially many terms and yields a uniform, negative first derivative at the complete-relaxation endpoint. This proves a concrete and somewhat counterintuitive loss-induced regime strictly worse than the shot-noise reference, even though full reset achieves exactly the reference. It does not merely rename a known single-excitation formula or infer a quantum Fisher invariant from a concurrence invariant.

## Closest literature and limits
Relevant inspected primary materials: arXiv:1403.2376, arXiv:1405.4878 (Dicke, QFI and noise sections), arXiv:1511.05203 (Dicke bounds), and arXiv:2601.23043 (Sections III and IV.2 on noisily encoded Dicke probes). The last work supplies numerical same-parameter overlap at eight qubits, and unlocated analytic priority remains possible. The proof does not classify unequal damping rates, optimize input states, determine all threshold crossings, or certify experimental implementation.
