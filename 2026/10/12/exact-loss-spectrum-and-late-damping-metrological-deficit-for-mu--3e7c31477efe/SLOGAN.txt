For multi-excitation symmetric Dicke probes under independent amplitude damping, an exact Boolean-lattice spectrum yields the total collective-spin quantum Fisher information from only \(O(N^2)\) terms. Although the undamped probe exceeds shot noise, the information approaches the reset value \(N\) strictly from below, with deficit \(2k(N-k+2)(1-p)+O((1-p)^2)\).
