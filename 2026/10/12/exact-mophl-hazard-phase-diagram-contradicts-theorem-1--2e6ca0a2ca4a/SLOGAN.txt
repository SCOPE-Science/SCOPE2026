The MOPHL hazard is not determined by \(\theta\) alone: the exact monotonicity boundary depends jointly on \(\alpha\) and \(\theta\), with two-turning-point hazards on both sides of \(\theta=1\) for sufficiently extreme Marshall–Olkin distortion. The paper's own \((\alpha,\theta,\gamma)=(0.03,2.52,0.03)\) simulation lies in the corrected non-monotonic region, contradicting its Theorem 1.
