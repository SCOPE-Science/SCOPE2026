For any alphabet of size \(q\ge2\), the maximum multiplicity-sensitive overlap of two distinct length-two, exact-two-insertion spheres is exactly \(8q-5\), attained precisely when one center is constant and the other differs in one position. The sharp multiset-reconstruction threshold is therefore \(8q-4\) distinct insertion vectors; all \(2q(q-1)\) labeled unordered extremal center pairs are classified.
