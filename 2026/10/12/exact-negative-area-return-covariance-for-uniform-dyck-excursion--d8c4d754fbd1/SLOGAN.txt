For uniformly sampled Catalan excursions, total walk area and the number of completed excursions have strictly negative covariance at every semilength \(n\ge2\): \(\operatorname{Cov}(A_n,R_n)=[6\,4^n/C_n-2(2n+1)(n+3)]/(n+2)\). The covariance is asymptotically \(-4n\), yet their correlation tends to zero at the sharp rate \(-2(10/3-\pi)^{-1/2}n^{-1/2}\).
