The role-reversal model has an exact predator invasion number \(\mathcal R_P\) that decreases strictly with juvenile predation \(g\) and increases affinely with predator reproduction \(k\), so its local predator-free boundary cannot re-enter as \(g\) increases. In the exact sharp-maturation limit, \(\mathcal R_P^\sharp=e^{-g\bar x\tau^*}Q(\tau^*,k)\) with \(Q\) strictly decreasing in maturation age, giving an explicit strictly decreasing threshold \(g_c(\tau^*;k)\) wherever invasion is possible at \(g=0\).
