The twelve hyperflex tangents of the Edge quartic keep 66 ordinary double intersections in every odd characteristic at least seven, but in characteristic three they acquire six quadruple points, and in characteristic five they collapse in threes to the quartic's four component lines. Their exact prime-field rational-intersection count is 66 when \(p\ge7\) and \(p\equiv1\pmod4\), and six in all other odd characteristics.
