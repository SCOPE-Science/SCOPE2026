Among integers with \(\Omega(n)\le3\), the divisibility \(n\mid\varphi(n)\sigma(n)\) holds exactly at \(6\), \(18\), and the cyclotomic family \(p^2(p^2+p+1)\) when both \(p\) and \(p^2+p+1\) are prime. In particular, the squarefree three-prime layer is empty and the repeated-prime layer collapses to a single \(\Phi_3\)-prime condition.
