For \(n>\ell\ge2\), the exact maximum size of a single-edit-correcting binary sum-channel code equals the maximum size of a classical length-\(\ell\) one-error-correcting code, multiplied by \(2^{\ell(n-1)}\); even single-substitution correction cannot improve on this number. The result closes the channel's one-bit optimality gap at the cardinality level and yields a \(25\%\) gain over its shortened-Hamming construction for \(\ell=8\).
