On a finite cycle, the edge-Hamming Wasserstein distance and total variation between a biased independent one-out graph and the uniform spanning tree have exact closed forms. Their optimal asymptotic sparse biases differ: \(n\min\{p,1-p\}\to\log2\) minimizes transport, whereas \(n\min\{p,1-p\}\to1\) minimizes total variation, with respective limiting distances \(\log2\) and \(1-e^{-1}\).
