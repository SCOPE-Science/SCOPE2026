For an acyclic evolution algebra whose every natural-basis square is either zero or a nonzero scalar multiple of one basis vector, the ordinary power \(E^k\) consists exactly of basis vectors at incoming path depth at least \(\lceil\log_2 k\rceil\). Thus a longest directed path of \(L\) edges gives nilpotency index \(2^L+1\), and the entire power filtration ignores the nonzero edge weights.
