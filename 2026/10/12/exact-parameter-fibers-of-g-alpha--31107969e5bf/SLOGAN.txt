For fixed \(n=\prod p_i^{e_i}\), Mittou's least-common-multiple exponent function has exactly the parameter collisions detected by \(\alpha/\gcd(\alpha,h)\), where \(h=\gcd(e_1,\ldots,e_r)\). Each normalized parameter \(s\) has an explicit finite fiber of size \(\tau(h^{(s)})\), so the parameterization is injective exactly when \(h=1\).
