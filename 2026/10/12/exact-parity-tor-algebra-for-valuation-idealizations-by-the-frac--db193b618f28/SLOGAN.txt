For \(R=D\ltimes(Q/D)\), the canonical kernel is \(N=0\ltimes(Q/D)\), and its self-Tor is exactly \(N\) in every odd degree and zero in every even degree. The reduced quotient has the same odd-degree Tor pattern, so \(\operatorname{Tor}^{R}_{*}(D,D)\) has a square-zero positive-degree ideal supported precisely in odd degrees.
