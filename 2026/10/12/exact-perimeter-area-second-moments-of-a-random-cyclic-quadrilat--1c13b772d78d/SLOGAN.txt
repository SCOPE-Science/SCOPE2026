Four uniform points on a unit circle produce a cyclic quadrilateral whose perimeter and area have an exactly evaluable bivariate second-moment matrix, including \(\operatorname{Cov}(P,A)=4(216-19\pi^2)/(3\pi^4)>0\). The calculation also establishes, without an earlier conjectural angle-density assumption, the value \(\mathbb EA^2=1/2+105/(16\pi^2)\).
