For classical AB2 on a scalar decay equation with two alternated physical steps, every trajectory decays exactly when the sum of the two dimensionless steps is below \(2\), regardless of their ratio. Equal steps uniquely minimize the period spectral radius at fixed total length, and the global minimum is \(1/3\) at two steps of length \(2/3\); nevertheless, stable unequal steps can produce arbitrarily large one-period transients in the stored-value state.
