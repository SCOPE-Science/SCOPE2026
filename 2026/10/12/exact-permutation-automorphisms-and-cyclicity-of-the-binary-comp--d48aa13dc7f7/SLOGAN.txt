For every even \(k\ge6\), the binary complement-pair code \([I_k\mid I_k+J_k]\) has exactly the even-signed pair permutations as its coordinate automorphisms, a group of order \(2^{k-1}k!\). It follows that the code is never equivalent to a cyclic code; among all positive even \(k\), only \(k=2\) is cyclic-equivalent.
