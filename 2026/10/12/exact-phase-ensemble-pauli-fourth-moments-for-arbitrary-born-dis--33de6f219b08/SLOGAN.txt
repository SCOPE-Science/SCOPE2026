For any fixed Born distribution, independently randomizing computational-basis phases gives an exact XOR-autocorrelation formula for the mean fourth Pauli moment \(\mathbb E(2^{-M_2})\). For a flat Born distribution, the phase-randomized states satisfy \(\mathbb E M_2\ge n-2\) and \(\Pr\{M_2\le n-2-t\}\le2^{-t}\), despite the positive-amplitude member having zero magic.
