For a signed star \(\Gamma=(K_{1,m},\sigma)\) with \(m\ge3\), the plurality number depends only on the number \(p\) of positive centre edges: it is \(m\) for \(p=0\), \(\max\{p+2,m-p+1\}\) for \(1\le p\le m-1\), and \(m+1\) for \(p=m\). Across the switching class its minimum is \(\lceil(m+3)/2\rceil\), giving reachable-code covering radius \(\lfloor(m-1)/2\rfloor\).\n
