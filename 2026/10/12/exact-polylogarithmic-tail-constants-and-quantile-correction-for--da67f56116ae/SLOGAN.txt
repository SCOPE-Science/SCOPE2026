The LGF survival tail has the exact coefficient \(A=(\lambda/\theta)^\lambda\operatorname{Li}_{\lambda+1}(\eta)/[-\log(1-\eta)]\), and its first correction is governed by \(B=(\lambda^2/\theta)\operatorname{Li}_{\lambda+2}(\eta)/\operatorname{Li}_{\lambda+1}(\eta)\). This makes the source's extreme-quantile asymptotic explicit and adds the correction \(Q(p)=(A/(1-p))^{1/\lambda}-B/\lambda+O((1-p)^{1/\lambda})\).
