The original three-dimensional Douglas–Rachford heat scheme is unconditionally stable but not unconditionally positivity preserving. On an \(n^3\) Dirichlet cube its completed step is nonnegative exactly for \(\Delta t/h^2\le\tau_n\), where \(\tau_n\uparrow(3-\sqrt6)/2\), and on rectangular grids the full sign test collapses to three pairwise inequalities between boundary diagonals of one-dimensional resolvents.
