Every component of the undirected power graph of the full transformation semigroup \(T_n\) has an exact size determined by its idempotent's kernel-fiber sizes, through a permutation sum of rooted-forest determinants. The first same-rank size split occurs at \(n=5\): the two rank-two fiber profiles \((4,1)\) and \((3,2)\) yield components of orders \(17\) and \(11\).
