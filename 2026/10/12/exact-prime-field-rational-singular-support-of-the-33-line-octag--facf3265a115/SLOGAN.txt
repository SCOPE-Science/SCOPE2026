The 169 intersection points of the 33-line regular-octagon arrangement have exactly nine \(\mathbb Q\)-rational members, forming the dual \(B_3\) reflection configuration; all others require \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)\). Away from three exceptional characteristics, the exact number of rational intersections is 169 or 9 according as 2 is a quadratic residue or nonresidue, and the exceptional prime fields have 6, 13, and 57 rational singular points in characteristics 2, 3, and 7.
