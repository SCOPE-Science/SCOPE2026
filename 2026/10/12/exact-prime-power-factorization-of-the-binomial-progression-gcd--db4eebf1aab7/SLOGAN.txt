For the natural binomial progression gcd \(D(k)=\gcd_{2\le q\le k+1}\binom{qk}{k}\), every prime-power factor is determined exactly by the factorization of \(k+1\): if \(p^a\parallel k+1\) and \(t=\lfloor\log_p((k+1)/p^a)\rfloor\), then \(\nu_p(D(k))=\max\{0,a-t\}\). Explicit binomial coefficients attain every positive minimum valuation, strengthening the known test for \(D(k)=1\).
