# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reconstructs the elimination from the source's Eq. (3.7), treats the singular full-compliance case separately, checks both pure endpoints, and reconstructs positive fast variables on the full mixed branch. The general result is algebraic; the exact-rational script is only a reproducibility check for a representative source parameter set.

## Originality
PASS. The motivating article gives pure pairwise and pure triplewise formulas but discusses their combination numerically. Searches by title, aliases, rate variables, threshold language, convexity, and synergy did not find an equivalent published or indexed statement. The closest classical and clustered-network papers use different tracing mechanisms and do not imply the quadratic mixed-threshold law.

## Value
PASS. The result turns a qualitative numerical reinforcement into an exact mechanism: incomplete compliance creates a strictly positive quadratic synergy gap, while full compliance makes the mechanisms exactly additive at threshold. This is a natural parameter-plane classification for the model rather than an arbitrary numerical slice.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is de Meijere et al. (2026), doi:10.1007/s00285-026-02455-6. Eames and Keeling (2003), doi:10.1098/rspb.2003.2554, is the classical pairwise antecedent; Rizi et al. (2024), doi:10.1103/PhysRevE.109.024303, studies clique-based contact tracing. The result is exact for the source's reduced fast-variable threshold equations, not for every finite-seed trajectory of the unreduced ODE system.
