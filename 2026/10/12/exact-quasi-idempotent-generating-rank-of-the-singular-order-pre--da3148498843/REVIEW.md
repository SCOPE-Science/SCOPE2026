# Scientific review: quasi-idempotent generating rank

**Correctness — PASS.** The top-rank Rees-style multiplication rule is derived from the omitted-image and collapsed-pair indices. It makes generation equivalent to strong connectivity of a finite directed bipartite graph. The local cut conditions are necessary; their sufficiency follows from the interval property of sink strongly connected components, proved in the result. The independent-set transfer states encode every local exclusion and boundary condition. An exact 256-entry max-plus identity yields a five-step recurrence, and the checked base cases imply the closed formula for every \(n\ge4\). The standalone checker executes successfully and covers direct finite cases. Risk: correctness of the finite 16-state matrix construction is a critical premise, made inspectable and reproducible in the included code.

**Originality — PASS.** The 2023 primary paper gives an upper bound and explicitly conjectures its sharpness in Remark 1; it does not contain the present exact rank. The explicit construction at \(n=9\), with proof of optimality, contradicts the suggested sharpness. The 2024 partial-map analogue addresses a distinct semigroup, while the inspected 2026 ideals paper develops generation and factorization length without the exact minimum-size formula. No indexed finding or searched published item established an equivalent general rank theorem. Residual risk: an earlier independent graph-theoretic or semigroup treatment under different terminology may have implied the same result.

**Value — PASS.** The invariant is a natural semigroup generating rank previously bounded but not uniformly determined in the motivating paper. The result gives an exact closed formula for every size, refutes a published sharpness conjecture at a small explicit instance, and explains the period-five phenomenon through a finite weighted graph-state identity. This is structural, rather than an isolated numerical improvement.
