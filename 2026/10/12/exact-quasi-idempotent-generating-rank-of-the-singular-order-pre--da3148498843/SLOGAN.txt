The quasi-idempotent generating rank of the singular order-preserving transformation semigroup on \(n\ge4\) points is exactly \(\lfloor6n/5\rfloor-\mathbf1_{n\equiv1\pmod5}\). In particular, ten quasi-idempotents suffice for \(n=9\), disproving the published suggestion that the earlier upper bound of eleven is sharp.
