In the uniform-legal-move general-position process on \(K_m\times K_n\), \(m,n\ge3\), the first three choices determine which of four terminal geometries occurs, with exact rational probabilities. Square boards have a rare four-corner outcome with probability asymptotic to \(4/(3n)\), yet its expected loss from maximum size tends to \(4/3\); highly rectangular boards exhibit a different Bernoulli scaling limit.
