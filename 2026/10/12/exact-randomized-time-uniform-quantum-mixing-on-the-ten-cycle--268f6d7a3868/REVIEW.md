# Mathematical review: randomized-time uniform mixing on the ten-cycle

## Correctness
The exact ten adjacency eigenvalues reduce the transition probabilities to a two-dimensional golden-ratio phase torus. The nonnegative full-support density proportional to \(1-\cos(x-2y)\) cancels each nonconstant displacement Fourier character: the relevant exact frequency counts are \((0,0,0)\) for odd indices and \((2,2,2)\) for even indices. Fourier inversion gives the flat expected mixing matrix. An irrational phase orbit is dense after every cutoff; a relative-interior convex-hull argument upgrades phase averaging to exact physical-time averaging. The symmetric probability vectors have affine dimension at most five, giving at most six distinct times. Numerical checks corroborate the exact finite character arithmetic and do not prove density or existence on their own.

## Relationship to earlier results
Baptista, Coutinho and Marques, *Unexpected averages of mixing matrices* (arXiv:2308.16378), establish the arbitrary observation-time law framework, spectral formulas and short-path constructions. Godsil, Mullin and Roy (arXiv:1301.5889) exclude **instantaneous** uniform mixing on even cycles other than the four-cycle. Adamczak and collaborators (arXiv:0708.2096) exclude the **ordinary long-time Cesaro average** on Abelian circulants. These different sampling quantifiers do not cover the ten-cycle law. Kang, *Arithmetic of Bohr Frequencies Governs Uniform Mixing in Randomly Timed Quantum Spin Chains* (arXiv:2609.14463), establishes an arithmetic classification for open **paths**, not the cycle under consideration. The exact positive result for the eight-vertex path is another different graph. Priority beyond the identified material remains uncertain.

## Mathematical value and limitations
The result is a motivated exact ten-cycle construction: one nonnegative Fourier harmonic defeats two usual mixing obstructions and yields a small finite-support sampling guarantee after any desired observation cutoff. No explicit closed-form physical times, minimality of six samples, or other-cycle classification is asserted. The analytic proof, not a numerical grid, establishes the universal statement.
