For an iid continuous-uniform sample, the exact conditional projection of unbiased sample variance onto the range is \(\mathbb E[S^2\mid R]=((n+1)(n+2)/(12n(n-1)))R^2\). The resulting range-only unbiased variance estimator has variance only asymptotically \(10/n\) times that of \(S^2\), while \(\operatorname{Corr}(R,S^2)\sim\sqrt{10/n}\).
