# Review of exact mixing rank on heterogeneous clique-cored windmills

## Correctness — PASS
The adjacency \(-1\)-eigenspace consists exactly of independent zero-sum vectors inside the core and peripheral cliques; this is proved from the cell-sum equations, not guessed from a quotient spectrum. Its spectral-projector Schur square has an explicit block spectrum. The PSD kernel-intersection argument and a positive Perron projector eliminate the sole additional possible hub direction, while cell transposition symmetry supplies the converse inclusion for all size-two differences. The sharp \(1/3\) positive gap follows from the same block spectrum, with internal three-cell contrasts attaining it. The embedded exact and numerical calculations corroborate all critical steps but do not replace the unrestricted proof.

## Originality — PASS, with limitations
Godsil (arXiv:1103.2578) and Coutinho–Godsil–Guo–Zhan (arXiv:1709.03591) give the underlying projector formula and general rank theory. Coutinho et al. (arXiv:2208.08971) give exact arithmetic for general walks. Kimmerling (2023 dissertation, DOI:10.7273/000006322) proves full rank for equal French-windmill clique blades with a single hub; this special case is **prior work**. Comparison of statements shows the present heterogeneous blade sizes, arbitrary positive core size, exact size-two nullspace threshold and sharp universal positive spectral gap are not consequences explicitly stated in those accessible claims. Related earlier complete-multipartite and blow-up classifications concern different adjacency constructions and do not imply this claim by graph isomorphism. An indexed but inaccessible full dissertation and a possible general theorem on equitable projector Schur squares present residual originality risk. The web-accessible dissertation abstract, relevant indexed section excerpt, and available original paper sections were inspected; the full dissertation was not represented as read.

## Value — PASS
The defined graphs are a natural family of clique-sunflower networks: many complete subnetworks share a possibly nontrivial complete core, with freely varying blade sizes. A complete kernel classification identifies the exact obstruction to retaining every long-time quantum-mixing mode; the exceptional hub of size two is different from the familiar one-vertex windmill. The dimension-free lower spectral bound gives quantitative stability for arbitrarily many heterogeneous blades and is attained exactly by three-vertex clique modes. This is more than routine recomputation of fixed small graph tables and gives an exact useful theorem across an infinite variable-size class.

## Scope and residual risks
The proof assumes at least two peripheral cliques of size at least two, unit edge weights, and the adjacency Hamiltonian. It does not cover general weighted gluing, singleton blades, or alternate sampling schemes. The 2023 dissertation PDF remained inaccessible through available lawful sources; its abstract specifically addresses equal French windmills, but unexamined parts could overlap the broader classification. General spectral-idempotent theory may offer another route to the result. No independent audit is claimed.
