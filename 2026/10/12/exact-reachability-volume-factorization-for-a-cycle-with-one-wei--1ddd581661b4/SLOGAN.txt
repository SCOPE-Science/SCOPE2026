For a cycle whose closing edge has weight \(w\), the exact single-endpoint Kalman determinant is \((-1)^{n+1}(w-1)^{\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor}(1+(n-1)w)\). Any positive nonunit closing weight restores controllability, but the reachability volume collapses to order \(\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor\) as the edge weight approaches one.
