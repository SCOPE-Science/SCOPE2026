The binary word whose \(1\)'s lie exactly at the nonnegative perfect squares has \(r(n)=2+\lfloor(\lfloor n/2\rfloor+1)^2/3\rfloor+(n\bmod2)\) reversal classes of length-\(n\) factors for every \(n\ge2\). Its reflection complexity is asymptotic to \(n^2/12\), exactly two thirds of its ordinary factor complexity in the limit.
