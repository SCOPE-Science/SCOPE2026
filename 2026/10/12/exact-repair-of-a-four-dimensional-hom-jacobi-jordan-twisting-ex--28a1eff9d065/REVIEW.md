# Mathematical assessment

## Algebraic correctness

Consider the commutative multiplication with \(e_1^2=e_2\) and \(e_1e_4=e_4\), all other basis products zero. Its ordinary Jacobiator has \(J(e_1,e_1,e_4)=2e_4\), so the printed base is not Jacobi–Jordan when the characteristic is not two. The displayed linear map in Example 3.4(ii) also fails multiplicativity: \(\alpha(e_1)^2-\alpha(e_2)=-a_{12}e_1-2e_4\).

The complete algebra-endomorphism equations split into the branches \(\lambda=0\) (six free coefficients, with \(\alpha(e_2)=\alpha(e_4)=0\)) and \(\lambda\ne0\) (four free coefficients, \(\alpha(e_2)=\lambda^2e_2\), \(\alpha(e_4)=te_4\), \((1-\lambda)t=0\)). For any endomorphism, the twisted Hom-Jacobian is \(\alpha^2\) of the ordinary Jacobiator. The twist is therefore valid precisely when \(\alpha^2(e_4)=0\), equivalent to \(t=0\) in the second branch. No invertible endomorphism yields a valid twist. Over odd \(\mathbf F_q\), the exact count is \(q^6+q^5-q^4\), and its extension-field counts give the indicated zeta function.

Independent exhaustive finite-field reconstruction at \(q=3\) found 1053 endomorphisms, including 891 valid twists. The unrestricted conclusion follows from the relation-based proof, not finite sampling.

## Relationship to previous results

Attan, *Representations and O-operators of Hom-(pre)-Jacobi-Jordan algebras* (arXiv:2105.14650v1), Definition 3.1 and Example 3.4(ii), provides the printed multiplication and map. Its general twisting proposition presupposes a true Hom-Jacobi–Jordan algebra and a self-morphism, whereas the displayed example violates both premises. The publicly indexed 2025 journal version repeats the same relevant equations, although the complete journal PDF could not be inspected directly.

Burde and Fialowski, *Jacobi-Jordan algebras* (arXiv:1404.5435), Proposition 3.1, establish that genuine Jacobi–Jordan algebras of dimension at most four satisfy \(A^3=0\) under their field assumptions; this independently excludes the printed base, but does not classify its endomorphisms or valid Hom twists. No searched source gave the exact two-branch salvage and finite-field counts. That negative search cannot establish absolute historical novelty; a separate erratum or equivalent unindexed result remains possible.

## Significance and limits

A full correction of a concrete example used for twisting constructions is useful beyond the elementary Jacobiator countercheck. The classification identifies every genuine replacement map and shows that all valid twisted products eliminate the original \(e_1e_4\) interaction. It does not reconstruct the author's intended example, cover characteristic two, or claim an exhaustive classification of all four-dimensional Hom-Jacobi–Jordan algebras.
