The four-dimensional multiplication and four-parameter self-morphism printed in Attan Example 3.4(ii) cannot satisfy the stated hypotheses: the base Jacobiator is \(2e_4\), and the displayed map already fails on \(e_1^2=e_2\). The printed multiplication can nevertheless be salvaged exactly: its genuine Hom–Jacobi–Jordan twists form two explicit endomorphism branches, with \(q^6+q^5-q^4\) valid twists over every odd \(\mathbf F_q\).
