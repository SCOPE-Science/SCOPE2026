On scalar quadratics, MPG-NGD is exactly a threshold-reset controller: it expands its stepsize until \(2\eta_0/L\), then resets to \(2\eta_1/L\). This yields a finite reset budget and an exact post-reset asymptotic rate window; with the paper's \((\eta_0,\eta_1)=(0.5,0.49)\), one reset forces the terminal factor below \(0.02\).
