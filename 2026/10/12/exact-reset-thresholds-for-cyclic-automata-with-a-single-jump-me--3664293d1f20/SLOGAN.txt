For a directed \(n\)-cycle with one idempotent letter merging state \(0\) into state \(h\), the shortest reset word has exact length \(1+(n-2)(n-h+1)\) when \(\gcd(n,h)=1\), and synchronization is otherwise impossible. An inverse-interval argument shows the familiar construction is optimal, sharply interpolating from the quadratic Černý value to linear length.
