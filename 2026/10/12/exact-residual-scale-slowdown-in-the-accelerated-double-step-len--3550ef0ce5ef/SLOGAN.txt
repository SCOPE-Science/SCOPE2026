ADSM learns the exact scalar Jacobian after one nonterminal step, yet its own line search can still make the calibrated method arbitrarily slow under the harmless residual rescaling \(F_a(s)=a s\): \(1-\rho(a)=\Theta(a^2)\) while every scalar Jacobian has condition number one. The obstruction is the unscaled search-direction penalty, which also forces \(\Theta(\log(1/a))\) eventual backtracking trials per accepted step.
