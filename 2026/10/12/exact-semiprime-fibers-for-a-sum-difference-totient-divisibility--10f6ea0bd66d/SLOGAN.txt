The complete \(\Omega(n)=2\) layer of \(\sigma(n)-\varphi(n)\mid\sigma(n)+\varphi(n)\) consists of odd squarefree semiprimes \(pq\) with \(p+q\mid p^2-1\). Equivalently, each quotient \(k\) is generated by a factor pair \((p-k)(q-k)=k^2-1\), giving a finite exact divisor parametrization of every semiprime fiber.
