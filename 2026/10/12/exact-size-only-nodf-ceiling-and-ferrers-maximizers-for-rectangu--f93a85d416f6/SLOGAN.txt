For classical NODF on a nonisolated binary ecological network with guild sizes \(m,n\ge2\), put \(a=\min(m,n)\), \(b=qa+r=\max(m,n)\) with \(0\le r<a\). When interaction count is unrestricted, the exact NODF ceiling is \(1-[(a-r)\binom q2+r\binom{q+1}2]/[\binom m2+\binom n2]\), achieved precisely by the \(\binom ar\) balanced Ferrers degree types.
