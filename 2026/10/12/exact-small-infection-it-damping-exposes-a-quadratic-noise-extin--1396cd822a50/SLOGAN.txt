For the source's nonlinear-noise SEI Lyapunov function, the exact uniform Itô damping is \(\sigma_{21}^2\sigma_{31}^2/[2(\sigma_{21}^2+\sigma_{31}^2)]\), so the quadratic coefficients \(\sigma_{22},\sigma_{32}\) cannot supply the claimed boundary-uniform damping. In the paper's Example 5.2 this changes the guaranteed damping from \(0.25\) to \(0.01\), removing the published negative extinction certificate without asserting that the simulated system actually persists.
