For two distinguishable quantum walkers with positive hopping strengths on a star with \(L\ge3\) leaves, positive-rate reaction at every coincidence site has absorbing dimension \(3L+1\) for equal speeds but \(5L\) for unequal speeds. A hub–leaf pair reacts with probability \(1/2\) exactly at equal speeds and with probability one at every nonzero speed mismatch.
