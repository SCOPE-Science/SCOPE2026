For the homogeneous \(N\)-state pure-birth chain, the classical RK4 end-step is stochastic exactly up to the dimensionless step \(\rho_2\), \(\rho_3\), \(4/3\), and \(1\) for \(N=2,3,4,\ge5\), respectively. Five states are the first pure-birth dimension that realizes the RK4 stability polynomial's absolute-monotonicity radius \(1\).
