For crown graphs \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M_n\), the strong-majority edge-colouring index is \(2\) only at \(n=4\) and is \(3\) for every other \(n\ge3\). A row-column rigidity argument rules out two colours outside the unique exception, while balanced one-factor colourings give the uniform three-colour construction.
