For the four-dimensional standard filiform Lie algebra over every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\), the probability that two uniformly selected subalgebras have a Lie-closed sum is exactly \(\frac{11q^4+27q^3+39q^2+30q+25}{(q^3+3q^2+3q+5)^2}\). Its decay as \(11/q^2\) contrasts with the nonzero limiting permutability of a four-dimensional Heisenberg central stabilization.
