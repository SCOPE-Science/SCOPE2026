For a finite product of bi-Heyting chains, every endomorphism is controlled by a weighted transformation of its chain factors, with weights given by binomial counts of monotone surjections. The complete endomorphism and retraction image-rank spectra coincide and consist precisely of products \(\prod_{j\in J}(a_j+1)\) for supports \(J\) containing a shortest dual chain; their exact multiplicities are different and explicit.
