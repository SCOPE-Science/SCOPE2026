For an ordinary size-\(n\) bootstrap resample, conditioning on exactly \(k\) represented source observations multiplies the original sample variance by the universal factor \(1-\left\{\begin{smallmatrix}n-1\\k\end{smallmatrix}\right\}/\left\{\begin{smallmatrix}n\\k\end{smallmatrix}\right\}\). Consequently the distinct-support count and bootstrap sample variance have the exact positive covariance \((1-1/n)^nS^2/(n-1)\).
