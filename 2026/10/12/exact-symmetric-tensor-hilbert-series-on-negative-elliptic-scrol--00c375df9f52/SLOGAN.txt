For every negative-degree split ruled surface over a complex elliptic curve, the symmetric tangent tensors have the exact Hilbert function \(h^0(M,\operatorname{Sym}^kT_M)=1+d\binom{k+2}{3}\) for all \(k\ge0\). Its tautological divisor is big with volume \(d\) but has vanishing cubic self-intersection, even though the graded section ring is not finitely generated.
