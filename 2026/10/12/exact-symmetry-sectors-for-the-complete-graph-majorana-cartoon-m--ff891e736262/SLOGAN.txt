The vertex-star symmetries of the complete-graph Majorana cartoon matrix model split the Hilbert space into exactly \(2^{N-1}\) equal sectors of dimension \(2^{(N-1)(N-4)/4}\). The raw star product in the source needs a phase \(i\) to be a Hermitian involution, and the corrected symmetries enforce block structure rather than energy degeneracy by themselves.
