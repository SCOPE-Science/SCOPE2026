# Exact ternary length-five non-overlapping codes detecting one substitution

## Finding
For a set \(C\subseteq\{0,1,2\}^{5}\), require **non-overlap**: for all \(u,v\in C\) (also when \(u=v\)) and each \(1\le j\le4\), the first \(j\) symbols of \(u\) are not the last \(j\) symbols of \(v\). Require also \(d_H(u,v)\ge2\) for all distinct codewords. The largest such code has exactly **9 words**. There are exactly **24 labeled nine-word codes**, forming **two orbits of size 12** under the twelve transformations generated by a common alphabet permutation and reversal of every codeword. Their unordered-pair Hamming-distance histograms at distances \(2,3,4,5\) are respectively \((21,10,5,0)\) and \((22,8,6,0)\).

Representatives (each comma-delimited token is a five-symbol word) are:

* \((21,10,5,0)\): `00002,00122,01022,01202,10022,11102,11222,12002,12122`.
* \((22,8,6,0)\): `00002,00122,01022,01202,10022,10202,11222,12002,12122`.

## Assumptions and scope
The alphabet is the **fixed labeled** set \(\{0,1,2\}\); length is exactly five and the Hamming minimum is at least two (one-substitution **detection**, not correction). Prefix/suffix lengths are exactly the proper nonempty lengths \(1,2,3,4\), including self-overlap. Labeled codes are unordered subsets of the 243 words; coordinate permutations other than whole-word reversal are **not** regarded as symmetries of non-overlap. No assertion about larger alphabets, other block lengths, or minimum distance three is made.

## Proof
**Structural reduction.** Let \(S\) be the set of first symbols and \(T\) the set of last symbols of the nonempty code. The length-one non-overlap condition forces \(S\cap T=\varnothing\), with both sets nonempty. With three symbols, either the cardinalities are \((1,1),(2,1)\), or \((1,2)\). If both are singletons, alphabet relabeling reduces to words beginning in `0` and ending in `2`. If the cardinalities are \((2,1)\) or \((1,2)\), relabeling and, in the latter case, reversal reduce to the **canonical class** of words beginning in `0` or `1` and ending in `2`. Reversal preserves the non-overlap condition because it swaps all prefixes and suffixes; relabeling also preserves it and preserves Hamming distance.

**Complete finite certificate.** Discard from the 243 words each word having an equal-length proper prefix and suffix. Exactly 144 survive, computed by the direct four-length predicate in `verify.py`. Make an undirected graph on the survivors, adjacent precisely when two words have Hamming distance at least two and no proper prefix of either is an equal-length suffix of the other. A code as defined above is **exactly a clique** of this graph, not merely contained in a clique. This equivalence follows by testing each ordered pair and each of the four proper lengths.

The canonical induced graph has 48 vertices and 706 edges. A complete maximal-clique enumeration with the Bron–Kerbosch recursion yields the following exact histogram of maximal-clique sizes: \(3:2,\ 4:18,\ 5:406,\ 6:1294,\ 7:726,\ 8:170,\ 9:4\). A second recursion independently enumerates **every clique** rather than just maximal cliques, with size histogram \(0:1,\ 1:48,\ 2:706,\ 3:4226,\ 4:11320,\ 5:13850,\ 6:7752,\ 7:1946,\ 8:204,\ 9:4\). Both identify the same four canonical optima and prove that the canonical maximum is nine. The singleton/singleton induced graph (words beginning `0` and ending `2`) has 24 vertices, 152 edges, and maximum clique size five. Thus any size-nine code must belong to **exactly one** of six classes having the two possible orientations and three choices of the distinguished singleton symbol: the two-symbol end support is necessarily used in full. Hence there are \(6\cdot4=24\) different size-nine codes, and no larger code exists.

For a check not using the six-class argument, an independently constructed 144-vertex graph has exactly 3324 edges. Exhaustive Bron–Kerbosch enumeration on this **global** graph gives maximal-clique size histogram \(3:12,\ 4:108,\ 5:2436,\ 6:7764,\ 7:4356,\ 8:1020,\ 9:24\), confirming both the bound and the labeled count without relying on the structural partition. Its 24 maxima agree exactly with the alphabet-permutation/reversal images of the four canonical maxima.

Finally apply the six alphabet permutations and both reversal choices to each global optimum. The 24 codes form exactly two orbits, each of size twelve. Their stated distance histograms are checked directly over all \(\binom{9}{2}=36\) unordered word pairs; distinct histograms show the orbits cannot merge under the action. This exhausts all equality cases.

**Completeness of the finite enumeration.** The attached Python checker explicitly constructs all length-five words and every graph edge from the definitions, with a second independently formulated pair predicate. Its Bron–Kerbosch recursion maintains a current clique \(R\), vertices \(P\) that can still extend it, and already considered vertices \(X\); the pivot rule only removes redundant branches, and a maximal clique is output exactly when \(P=X=\varnothing\). Its forward recursion maintains the larger-index common-neighbor candidates and counts every clique exactly once by increasing vertex sequences. Neither routine uses random search, stopping thresholds, or an optimization solver that could return a mere lower bound.

## Relationship to prior work
Blackburn's *Non-overlapping codes* (arXiv:1303.1026, publicly posted 2013-03-05) established the synchronization framework and general constructions, without this joint Hamming-distance-two equality classification. Stanovnik, Moškon, and Mraz (*In search of maximum non-overlapping codes*, 2024) determine the **unconstrained** ternary length-five maximum as 17 with 12 labeled unconstrained maximizers; those are different optimization constraints and neither number entails the present maximum nine or its 24 equality cases. Their later work on balance/run-length constraints explicitly motivates simultaneous channel restrictions. The full published length-five exact-optimization theorem establishes an all-alphabet result for unrestricted non-overlap; its complete RESULT.md has no minimum-distance-two constraint or equality classification, so neither its 17-word ternary optimum nor 12 maximizing unrestricted codes implies this result. Wang, Fang, and Feng's treatment of partial overlap with Hamming restrictions was located; its accessible abstract advertises general existence bounds, not the exact strongly non-overlapping ternary equality census, but its full text was not verified and remains a literature-comparison risk.

## Limitations
The argument is a transparent exhaustive finite proof, not a closed-form structural classification valid for arbitrary \(q,n\). The numerical novelty depends on a literature comparison, which cannot rule out unindexed or inaccessible historical enumerations. In particular, a plausibly relevant later paper on partially overlap-free distance-constrained codes was not available in reliable full text; no claim of its absence of related results is made. Independent external mathematical validation has not been performed.
