For four equally weighted pure qubit encodings, the Hilbert--Schmidt geometric discord is maximized exactly by signed regular tetrahedra in the Bloch sphere, with \(D_G=1/12\). Every such maximizer has uniform-average \(2\to1\) QRAC success at most \(1/2+1/(2\sqrt3)\), strictly below the optimal QRAC value \(1/2+1/(2\sqrt2)\).
