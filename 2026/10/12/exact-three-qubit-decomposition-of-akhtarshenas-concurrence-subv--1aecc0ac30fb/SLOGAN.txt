For every pure three-qubit state, Akhtarshenas's pair-labelled concurrence subvector obeys \(A_{ij}^2=c_{ij}^2+\tau/2\): it contains the ordinary reduced-pair concurrence plus exactly half the three-tangle. Summed over the three pairs, the construction collapses to \(A_{\mathrm{tot}}^2=3Q/2\), a fixed rescaling of Meyer-Wallach global entanglement.
