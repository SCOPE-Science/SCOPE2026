The six-Kraus finite-temperature qutrit model of Guo et al. is trace preserving only up to \(t=(\log2)/2\); beyond that exact boundary its analytic continuation has an explicit positive trace defect. On the paper's separable and PPT-entangled Horodecki inputs, the two-local output remains PPT for every time, so late-time positivity of the unnormalized trace-norm-minus-one expression is a normalization artifact rather than a birth of distillable entanglement.
