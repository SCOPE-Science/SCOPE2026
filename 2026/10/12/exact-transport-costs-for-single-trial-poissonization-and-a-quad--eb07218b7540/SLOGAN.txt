For a single uniformly assigned count across \(d\) cells, independent mean-matched Poissonization has exact \(\ell_1\)-Wasserstein cost \(2/e\), while its exact squared Euclidean transport cost is determined by the maximum of \(d\) independent Poisson counts. The latter exceeds \(2/e\) at every finite dimension, but the leading excess is only \(7/(12d^2)\): the first-order collision term cancels.
