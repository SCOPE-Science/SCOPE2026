For the real two-eigenvalue one-Gaussian-vector Nyström preconditioner and an independent Gaussian log-determinant probe, the complete unconditional mean-squared error has an explicit dilogarithm formula. Its excess over ideal rank-one spectral preconditioning is strictly positive for unequal eigenvalues and decays at the sharp rate \(8[\log(1+b)+2\log2]\sqrt{b/a}\) as the top eigenvalue \(a\to\infty\) with \(b>0\) fixed.
