In the edge-pair-initiated two-particle Grover walk on every \(K_{m,n}\), the complete particle-reduced spectrum is two copies of a Bernoulli tensor power of order \(0\), \(1\), or \(2\), depending only on time modulo four. Every Rényi entropy is explicit, and entanglement rank is at most eight regardless of graph size, with maximal entropy \(\log8\) attained only on \(K_{2,2}\) at time \(2\pmod4\).
