For volume-sampled exact minimization over all but one coordinate of a positive-definite quadratic, the exact mean-square contraction factor is the Perron root of a normalized Hadamard square of the inverse Hessian. An inverse-Hessian off-diagonal zero forces almost-sure finite absorption, while a dense inverse gives a different, explicit almost-sure Lyapunov exponent even though mean-square error always decays geometrically.
