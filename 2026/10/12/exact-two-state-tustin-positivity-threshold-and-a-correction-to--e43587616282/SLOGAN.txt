For a two-state Hurwitz Metzler system, the Tustin step is positive exactly up to an explicit coupling-sensitive threshold \(h_\star=4/(|a-d|+\sqrt{(a+d)^2-4bc})\). This corrects a determinant-only sufficiency sentence in the original Padé-positive-systems analysis and shows that its later safe diagonal bound can be arbitrarily conservative.
