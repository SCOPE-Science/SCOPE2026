For \(f(x,y)=(\lambda+x^2+y^2)^2-(\gamma_1x+\gamma_2xy)^2\), the origin is the unique global minimizer exactly when \(0<\gamma_2\le1\) with \(\gamma_1^2\le2\lambda\), or \(1<\gamma_2<2\) with \(\gamma_1^2<2\lambda\gamma_2(2-\gamma_2)\). This completely resolves the first genuinely two-variable case of the arbitrary-positive-parameter minimization question arising from the discrete Terrell problem.
