For finite undirected paths, two-variable counting logic has exact depth \(D^2_{\#}(P_1)=1\), \(D^2_{\#}(P_2)=2\), and \(D^2_{\#}(P_n)=\lfloor(n+1)/2\rfloor\) for \(n\ge3\). The slow rivals are precisely a shorter path plus cycles, and their first color-refinement separation occurs in round \(\lfloor m/2\rfloor+1\).
