For every odd prime power \(q\), the number of decompositions \(A=U+V\) with \(U\in\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb F_q)\) and \(V^2=I_2\) is given exactly by a quadratic-character formula in the trace, determinant, and scalarity of \(A\). Only \(A=\pm I_2\) have a unique decomposition; the sharp count for all other matrices is \(q^2-q+1\), attained precisely by nonscalar involutions and nontrivial Jordan blocks at eigenvalues \(\pm1\).
