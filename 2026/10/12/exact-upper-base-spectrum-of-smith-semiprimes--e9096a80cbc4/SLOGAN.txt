For a semiprime \(pq\) with both prime factors one-digit in base \(B\) and \(pq\) two-digit, the Smith bases have an exact divisor spectrum determined by \(K=pq-p-q\). Every odd semiprime except \(9\) has the canonical Smith base \(B=(p-1)(q-1)\), while no even semiprime occurs in this upper-base stratum.
