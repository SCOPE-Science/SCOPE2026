## correctness

PASS

Using Wei Xie's published if-and-only-if description of basic profile vectors, define left-half symmetric partners a_k=f_k and b_k=n-f_{n-k}; the reflected halves are forced and their ordered inequality a_k>=b_k recovers the original vector. Consecutive symmetric profiles vary by -1,0,1, so the pairwise difference is a nonnegative walk with increments -2,-1,0,1,2 and respective multiplicities 1,2,3,2,1. For n=2m both central values equal m, giving W_{m-1}(0); for n=2m+1 three permissible central ordered pairs have difference 0,0,1, giving 2W_{m-1}(0)+W_{m-1}(1). The 9-ary normalized symmetric random walk and Sparre--Andersen survival identity produce G(t)=(1-9t)^(-1/2) exp(0.5 sum_{r>=1} a_r t^r/r), a_r=[u^0](u^-1+1+u)^(2r). Its characteristic function ((1+2cos theta)/3)^2 and exact log-cosine integral yield the analytic factor 3 sqrt(2-sqrt3), then the square-root singularity gives the claimed m-1 asymptotic. Independent brute-force enumeration of all Xie-inequality vectors for n=2..9 matched the recurrence sequence 1,3,6,20,43,149,331,1169; finite match does not replace the all-order pairing and survival theorem. Caveat: the published description of Xie's canonical factor construction is an indispensable premise, verified against accessible abstract and internal proof reconstruction rather than complete external paper.
