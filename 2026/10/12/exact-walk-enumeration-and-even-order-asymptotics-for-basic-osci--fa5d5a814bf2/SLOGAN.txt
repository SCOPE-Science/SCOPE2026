The distinct exponent profiles of basic oscillatory matrices are counted exactly by a one-dimensional weighted nonnegative-walk model with step weights \(1,2,3,2,1\). For even order \(2m\), their number is asymptotic to \(3\sqrt{2-\sqrt3}\,9^{m-1}/\sqrt{\pi(m-1)}\), quantifying how much larger the full basic class is than the symmetric-profile subclass.
