For the arbitrary-dimensional extremality test over \(\mathbb F_{p^2}\), the cited width argument needs the exact width \((p+1)^{2g}-(p-1)^{2g}\), which is strictly larger than \(4gp^{2g-1}\) for \(g\ge2\). Using that exact width repairs the one-sign false-positive bound to \(\min\{1,((p+1)^{2g}-(p-1)^{2g})/(p-1)^{2g}\}\), preserving the claimed \(O(p^{-1})\) decay.
