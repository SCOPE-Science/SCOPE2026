The divisor-sum defect \(B(n)=\#\{d\mid n:\sigma(d)\nmid\sigma(n)\}\) vanishes exactly on squarefree integers and equals \(1\) exactly for \(p^2m\) or \(p^3m\), where \(m\) is squarefree and all its prime factors are \(-1\) modulo \(p+1\) or \(p^2+p+1\), respectively. The lone failing divisor is always \(n/p\); this one-defect class is infinite but has density zero.
