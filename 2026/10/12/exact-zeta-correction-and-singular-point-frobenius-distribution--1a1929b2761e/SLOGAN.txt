The complete singular-model zeta correction for disjoint-branch genus-five Howe sextics over odd finite fields is \((1-T)\prod_{\mathcal O}(1-\varepsilon_\mathcal O T^{|\mathcal O|})\), with each sign given by an explicit quadratic character at a cubic-difference root. A rational \(S_3\) specialization has exactly \(1\), \(2\), or \(4\) rational singular points on prime sets of natural densities \(1/3\), \(1/2\), or \(1/6\).
