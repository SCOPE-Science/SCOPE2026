The closure of the reciprocal-fourth-power divisor-sum range \(\sigma_{-4}(\mathbb N_{>0})\) consists of exactly 140 closed intervals separated by 139 strict gaps. All endpoints are given exactly as rational multiples of the prime tail constant \(T=\frac{\pi^4}{90}\prod_{p\le13}(1-p^{-4})\), with a reproducible rational-arithmetic certificate.
