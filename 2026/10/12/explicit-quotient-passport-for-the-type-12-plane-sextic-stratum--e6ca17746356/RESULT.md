# Explicit quotient passport for the Type-12 plane-sextic stratum
## Finding
For \(\beta\in\mathbb C\setminus\{{0,\pm2,\pm10/3\}}\), consider
\[
C_\beta:\quad X^6+Y^5Z+YZ^5+\beta Y^3Z^3=0.
\]
Badr--Bars prove that the full projective automorphism group is
\[
G=\langle\sigma,\tau\rangle\cong C_3\times D_8,
\qquad |G|=24,
\]
with
\[
\sigma=[X:\zeta_{{12}}Y:\zeta_{{12}}^7Z],
\qquad
\tau=[X:Z:Y].
\]
The full quotient by this group is rational, and an explicit quotient coordinate is
\[
u=\frac{{Y^4+Z^4}}{{Y^2Z^2}}.
\]
The map \(u:C_\beta\to\mathbb P^1\) has degree \(24\) and branch values
\[
\infty,\quad -\beta,\quad 2,\quad -2,
\]
with inertia orders
\[
12,\quad 6,\quad 2,\quad 2,
\]
respectively. Thus the quotient signature is \([0;12,6,2,2]\). The corresponding ramification-orbit sizes are \(2,4,12,12\).

## Assumptions and scope
The ground field is \(\mathbb C\). The exclusions \(\beta=\pm2\) remove the singular members of the displayed family, \(\beta=0\) is the separate Type-24 stratum in the source, and \(\beta=\pm10/3\) are the parameters where the curve becomes isomorphic to the Fermat sextic and acquires a larger automorphism group. The statement concerns the full automorphism group on the remaining Type-12 stratum.

## Proof
Work on \(Z\ne0\) with
\[
t=Y/Z,\qquad x=X/Z.
\]
Then the equation is
\[
x^6=-t(t^4+\beta t^2+1).
\]
The source identifies the normal subgroup \(N=\langle\sigma^2\rangle\) of order \(6\). In the affine function field, \(N\) fixes \(t\) and multiplies \(x\) by sixth roots of unity. Hence
\[
\mathbb C(C_\beta)^N=\mathbb C(t).
\]
The quotient \(G/N\cong V_4\) acts on \(t\) by
\[
t\longmapsto -t,
\qquad
t\longmapsto t^{-1}.
\]
Its invariant field is therefore generated by
\[
u=t^2+t^{-2}.
\]
Consequently \(\mathbb C(C_\beta)^G=\mathbb C(u)\), proving that \(u\) is the full quotient map and that its degree is \(6\cdot4=24\).

The Kummer cover \(C_\beta\to\mathbb P^1_t\) branches with index \(6\) at \(t=0\), at \(t=\infty\), and at the four roots of
\[
t^4+\beta t^2+1=0.
\]
For \(\beta\ne\pm2\), these four roots are distinct. At each of them,
\[
u=t^2+t^{-2}=-\beta.
\]
The map \(\mathbb P^1_t\to\mathbb P^1_u\) has degree \(4\). It has branch value \(u=\infty\) at \(t=0,\infty\), branch value \(u=2\) at \(t=\pm1\), and branch value \(u=-2\) at \(t=\pm i\), each with local index \(2\). The Kummer cover is unramified at \(t=\pm1,\pm i\) when \(\beta\ne\pm2\). Therefore the composite quotient has inertia orders \(12,6,2,2\) at \(u=\infty,-\beta,2,-2\).

The orbit sizes are \(24/e\), hence \(2,4,12,12\). The first orbit consists of the two points \(C_\beta\cap\{YZ=0\}\); the second consists of the four other points on \(X=0\); and the last two are the points lying over \(t=\pm1\) and \(t=\pm i\).

Finally, the tame Riemann--Hurwitz formula gives
\[
24\left(-2+\left(1-\frac1{{12}}\right)+\left(1-\frac16\right)+2\left(1-\frac12\right)\right)=18=2\cdot10-2,
\]
which agrees with the genus \(10\) of a smooth plane sextic and confirms that the quotient genus is \(0\).

## Verification
The standalone checker `artifacts/verify_type12_quotient.py` verifies symbolically that \(u\) is invariant under \(t\mapsto-t\) and \(t\mapsto t^{-1}\), that the two order-\(2\) branch fibers are exactly \(u=\pm2\), that the quartic Kummer branch polynomial has repeated roots only at \(\beta=\pm2\), that its four roots map to \(u=-\beta\), and that Riemann--Hurwitz closes with signature \([0;12,6,2,2]\). The checker was freshly replayed using SymPy 1.14.0.

## Relationship to prior work
Badr--Bars give the family, the generators, the exact full automorphism group \(C_3\times D_8\), and the normal subgroup \(N=\langle\sigma^2\rangle\), but the inspected Type-12 section does not state the quotient coordinate, its four branch values, or this labeled ramification passport. Broughton--Shaska--Wootton explain that abstract signatures for curves of genus at most \(48\) have been tabulated in Breuer's work; therefore novelty is not claimed for the unordered abstract signature by itself. The new content is the explicit quotient function and the identification of the branch divisor \(\{\infty,-\beta,2,-2\}\) for this plane-sextic stratum.

## Limitations
This result does not classify isomorphisms between different parameter values \(\beta\), nor does it claim that the abstract signature \([0;12,6,2,2]\) was previously unknown. The statement is restricted to the generic Type-12 locus where the Badr--Bars full-group theorem applies and the displayed sextic is smooth.
