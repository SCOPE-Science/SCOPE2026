For the generic Badr--Bars Type-12 plane sextic, the full \(C_3\times D_8\)-quotient is given explicitly by \(u=(Y^4+Z^4)/(Y^2Z^2)\). Its four branch values are \(\infty,-\beta,2,-2\) with inertia orders \(12,6,2,2\), so the parameter appears directly as the moving branch value of the quotient cover.
