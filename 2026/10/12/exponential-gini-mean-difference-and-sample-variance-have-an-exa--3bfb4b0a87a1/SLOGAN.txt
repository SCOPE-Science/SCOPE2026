For iid exponential samples, the sample Gini mean difference \(G_n\) and unbiased sample variance \(S_n^2\) satisfy \(\operatorname{Cov}(G_n,S_n^2)=(3n-2)/(n(n-1)\lambda^3)\), giving an exact closed correlation. That correlation rises with \(n\) and tends to \(3\sqrt6/8\), so the two classic scale summaries stay strongly but not perfectly aligned.
