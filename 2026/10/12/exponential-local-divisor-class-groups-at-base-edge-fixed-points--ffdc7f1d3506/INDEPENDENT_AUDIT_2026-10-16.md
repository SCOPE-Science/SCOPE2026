# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The projective friendship graph F_m has m triangles sharing a central vertex and dimension 2m; the fixed base-edge chart at W_i yields u_j v_j=xy w_j for the other k=m-1 triangles. The k leading monomials u_j v_j are pairwise coprime and form a regular sequence and Gröbner basis. Quotient has no xy torsion and becomes a Laurent-domain after xy inversion, proving primeness and exactness of the chart ideal. Serre S2 holds by complete intersection, while generic points of torus-invariant divisors are smooth (nonvanishing relevant Jacobian entries), giving R1 and normality; Gorenstein follows. The saturated chart cone has inequalities alpha,beta,gamma_j,delta_j>=0, gamma_j+delta_j>=alpha+beta. Its primitive extremal rays are exactly 2k coordinate rays and 2^(k+1) alpha/beta binary-choice rays; a slack-removal convex decomposition establishes completeness. The toric invariant-divisor exact sequence has cokernel torsionfree by explicit unit minor, giving local Cl rank 2^(k+1)-2=2^m-2. Initial ideal (u_jv_j) is squarefree, with 2^k coordinate components and Hilbert series (1+z)^k/(1-z)^(2m), multiplicity 2^k. Independently enumerated cone rays by hyperplane intersections for k=1,2,3, obtaining class ranks 2,6,14, in accord with general proof; finite enumerations are not used to infer exponential all-m formula.

## originality

PASS

The general affine toric class-group exact sequence and edge-ring divisor facts are classical. Higashitani--Matsushita (arXiv:2108.01347) Theorem 3.6 gives class groups of the *homogeneous affine graph edge rings* satisfying the odd-cycle condition, not local groups of distinguished *projective chart* fixed points. The source's graph has multiple odd cycles glued at one vertex and these varieties have different local/global constructions. The explicit base-edge chart, universal ray pattern, exponential local class-group rank, and reduced 2^(m-1)-component tangent cone were not stated in inspected general theorem or nearest related Resultary local-toric record (which treats Liu--Zhu examples). Best-of-knowledge originality applies to this family-specific local theorem, not a new toric class-group theory.

## value

PASS

Friendship graphs are a canonical block-glued odd-cycle family, and local divisor class group measures Q-factoriality and singularity complexity at geometrically natural torus-fixed strata. The exponential free rank 2^m-2 against linear ambient dimension 2m, alongside normal Gorenstein singularity and 2^(m-1) reducible tangent-cone components, is a precise structural growth phenomenon rather than arbitrary numerical slices. It clarifies local versus global invariants and supplies an all-parameter singularity classification at base-edge points, without claiming a census of all singular strata.
