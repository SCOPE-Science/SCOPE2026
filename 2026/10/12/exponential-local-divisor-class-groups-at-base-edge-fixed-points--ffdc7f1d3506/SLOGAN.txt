At each base-edge torus-fixed point of the projective friendship-graph edge variety formed by \(m\ge2\) triangles, a normal Gorenstein local complete intersection has multiplicity \(2^{m-1}\) but local divisor class group \(\mathbb Z^{2^m-2}\). Its tangent cone has \(2^{m-1}\) reduced linear components, giving exponential local non-\(\mathbb Q\)-factoriality despite linearly growing dimension.
