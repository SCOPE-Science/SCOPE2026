A complete \(q\)-ary finite tree can be completely rigid under ordinary two-way modal bisimulation using exactly \(q\) vertex colors, but uniformly random fixed-alphabet colorings achieve rigidity with exponentially small probability. The exact positioned-coloring census is \(G_{q,a}(h)=R_{q,a}(h)(q!)^{(q^h-1)/(q-1)}\) with \(R_{q,a}(0)=a\) and \(R_{q,a}(h+1)=a\binom{R_{q,a}(h)}q\), yielding an explicit convergent-series entropy rate.
