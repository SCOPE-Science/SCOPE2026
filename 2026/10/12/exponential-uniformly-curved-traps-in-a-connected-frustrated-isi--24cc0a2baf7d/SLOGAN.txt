A connected maximum-degree-three frustrated Ising family has exactly \(3^m\) nondegenerate local minima under a \(3m\)-parameter product-rotation VQE ansatz, with a unique accessible ground state and an open region of uniformly positive Hessian and nonglobal energy gaps. Its mean squared gradient per coordinate stays above \(1/2\), making an exact all-size benchmark for exponentially many optimization traps without barren-gradient suppression.
