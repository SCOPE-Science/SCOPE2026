For an exterior-localized quantum walker on a uniformly weighted split join, adjacency and combinatorial-Laplacian measurements agree for all destinations and all times exactly when \(\alpha(r-1)=\eta|r-s|\); matching just the fourth-order coefficient of a single center transition already forces this global equivalence. The criterion rigorously characterizes the previously observed unweighted irregular family and its continuously tunable weighted extension, while separating the trivial weighted-regular branch.
