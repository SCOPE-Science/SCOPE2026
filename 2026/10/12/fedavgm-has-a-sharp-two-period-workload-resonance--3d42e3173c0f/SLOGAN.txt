FedAvgM can be unstable under a two-period alternation of local workloads even when every local gradient step and every frozen-workload FedAvgM system is stable. For \(\beta=1/2\), local factor \(q=-4/5\), and alternating one versus two local steps, FedAvgM has a two-round eigenvalue of magnitude greater than one while vanilla FedAvg contracts by \(64/125\) every two rounds.
