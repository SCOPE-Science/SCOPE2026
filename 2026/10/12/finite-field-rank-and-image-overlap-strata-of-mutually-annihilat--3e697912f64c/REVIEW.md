**PASS.** For the exact matrix relations \(X^2=Y^2=XY=YX=0\), the two maps vanish on the sum of their images. The converse construction uses independently chosen quotient surjections, so every matrix pair appears once. The Gaussian intersection count accounts for all choices of images, including degenerate and maximal-rank boundaries. Independent exhaustive enumerations over field orders \(2,3,4\) agree with every predicted triple. These finite checks do not replace the general bijective proof.

The present exact \((r,s,u)\) finite-field point counts require an additional full subspace-intersection and quotient-surjection enumeration. Some Hall-algebra formulation could already give equivalent values; priority is not independently established.

Its nonvanishing conditions and rigid even-dimensional maximal-rank case are uniform across all finite fields. The finite verifier checks six small dimension/field combinations, and no claim is made about finite experiments proving all dimensions. The nearest earlier literature establishes structural facts, not an explicit priority verdict for finite-field overlap frequencies.
