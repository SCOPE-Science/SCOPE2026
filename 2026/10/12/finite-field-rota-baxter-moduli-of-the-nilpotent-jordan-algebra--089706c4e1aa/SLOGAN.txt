For the integral \(J_5\) model \(e_1\circ e_1=2e_2\), odd finite fields have exactly \(2q^2-q\) weight-zero Rota–Baxter operators in \(2q\) automorphism classes. In characteristic \(2\) the multiplication collapses to zero, so all \(q^4\) endomorphisms are Rota–Baxter and form \(q^2+q\) similarity classes.
