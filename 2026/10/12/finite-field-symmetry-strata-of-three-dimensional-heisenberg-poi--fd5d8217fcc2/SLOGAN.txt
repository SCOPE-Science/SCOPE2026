For three-dimensional Poisson algebras with Heisenberg bracket and nonzero associative square contained in the Lie-derived line, the entire finite-field isomorphism and symmetry profile is governed by similarity of a nonzero traceless \(2\times2\) matrix. There are exactly \(q\) isomorphism classes when \(q\) is odd and \(2q-1\) when \(q\) is even; precisely \(q-1\) even-characteristic classes have Poisson-derivation dimension \(6\) instead of \(4\).
