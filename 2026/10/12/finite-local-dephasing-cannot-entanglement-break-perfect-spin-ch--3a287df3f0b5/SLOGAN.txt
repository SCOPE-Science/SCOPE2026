For independent equal-rate local pure dephasing, a vacuum-initialized number-conserving spin chain loses the transferred qubit coherence only by the exact factor \(e^{-\kappa t}\); the transfer channel is entanglement breaking exactly when the corresponding noiseless one-excitation amplitude vanishes. Hence the engineered mirror-inverting chain remains non-entanglement-breaking at its perfect-transfer time for every finite dephasing rate and every chain length.
