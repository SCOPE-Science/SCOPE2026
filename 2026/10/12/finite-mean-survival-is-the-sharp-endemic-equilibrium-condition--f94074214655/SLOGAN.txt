In the age-structured HIV transactivation model, \(\mathcal R_0>1\) produces a positive equilibrium in its \(L^1\) phase space exactly when the unactivated infection-age survival has finite mean; the published integrable-hazard class instead yields a nonintegrable formal endemic profile. A power-law transactivation hazard leaves \(\mathcal R_0\) unchanged while producing an exact finite-mass existence transition at tail exponent \(k=1\), and the characteristic formula quantifies the infection-age mass retained when influx persists.
