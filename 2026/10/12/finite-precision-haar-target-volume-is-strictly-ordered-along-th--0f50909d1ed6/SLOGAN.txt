For the Watts–O’Connor–Vala coordinate-cube Haar target, every controlled two-qubit gate at larger Weyl coordinate \(c\) has strictly larger finite-precision target volume for every admissible \(0<a<\pi/2\). Thus CNOT/CPHASE at \(c=\pi/2\) uniquely maximizes this target volume along the controlled-gate edge, with no finite-precision reversal.
