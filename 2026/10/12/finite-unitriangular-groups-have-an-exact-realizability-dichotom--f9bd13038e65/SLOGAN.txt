For \(n\ge2\), the finite unitriangular group \(\mathrm{UT}_n(p^f)\) is realizable as the unit group of a finite ring exactly when \(p=2\), except that in size \(2\) every Mersenne prime characteristic also works. For even \(q\), the explicit local ring \(\mathbf F_2I_n\oplus N_n(\mathbf F_q)\) has unit group \(\mathrm{UT}_n(q)\) and order exactly twice the group order.
