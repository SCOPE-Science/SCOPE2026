For three consecutive centered-square denominations \(C_n,C_{n+1},C_{n+2}\), the greatest integer having at most one representation is exactly \(506\) at \(n=3\), \(6n^3+26n^2+43n+22\) at \(n=4,5\), and \(8n^3+20n^2+16n-2\) for all \(n\ge6\). A complete residuewise second-Apéry formula explains the sharp transition at \(n=6\).
