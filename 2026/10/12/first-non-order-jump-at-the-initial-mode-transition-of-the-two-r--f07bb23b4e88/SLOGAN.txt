For the two-run order-\(k\) negative-binomial waiting-time laws, the first transition away from the minimum produces modes \(\{4,6\}\) at \(k=2\), \(\{6,9\}\) at \(k=3\), but \(\{8,15\}\) at \(k=4\). Thus order four is the first order whose initial positive modal competitor jumps farther than the order itself; its sharp parameter is the unique root of \(-2q^7+18q^5-32q^4+18q^3+6q^2-1=0\) in \((0.3315,0.3316)\).
