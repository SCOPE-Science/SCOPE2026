For any fixed \(k\ge4\), the minimum span of \(k\) consecutive uniform points on a circle has a Weibull-type extreme limit with an explicit strictly negative first finite-sample correction of order \(N^{-1/(k-1)}\). The coefficient is \(-e^{-t^{k-1}/(k-1)!}(k+1)t^k/k!\), arising from overlaps between neighboring short scan windows.
