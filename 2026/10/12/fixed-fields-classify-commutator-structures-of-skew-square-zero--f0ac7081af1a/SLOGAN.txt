For the skew square-zero finite rings \(R_\sigma=\mathbb F_q\oplus\mathbb F_q u\), the fixed field of the twist classifies the entire additive commutator structure: it determines a pointwise commutator-preserving equivalence, every centralizer, every commutator fiber, and the commuting graph. Distinct, even non-anti-isomorphic rings over \(\mathbb F_{32}\) have identical such structures, despite being sharp examples for the finite-zero-divisor bound.
