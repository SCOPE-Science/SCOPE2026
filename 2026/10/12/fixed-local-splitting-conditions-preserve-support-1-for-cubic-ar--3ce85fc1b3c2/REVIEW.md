# Review

## Correctness
PASS. In the explicit formula, every prescribed prime contributes only \(O_{\Sigma,\phi}(1/\log X)\). For varying primes outside the fixed support of \(\Sigma\), the extra local indicator tensors with Ahlquist's local test function. The residues and the field-counting denominator acquire the same fixed mass; local Fourier bounds change only by fixed constants; Ahlquist's Section 7.4 supplies the local maximality-sieve changes; and the hard dual \(S_3\) term already uses a counting theorem with arbitrary splitting conditions. Therefore the exponent inequalities giving support \(<1\) are unchanged. The \(75\%\) bound follows from the nonnegative squared-sinc test function and even central multiplicity.

## Originality
PASS. Ahlquist's Theorems 1.10--1.11 state support \(<1\) and \(75\%\) nonvanishing for the unconditioned family, while his splitting-condition results are stated for counting functions. Shankar--Södergren--Templier Theorem 5 is the direct local-family predecessor and has support only \(<2/5\). Indexed and web searches for the combined conditioned support-1 / 75%-nonvanishing statement found no matching theorem. The claim is therefore not implied by the nearest printed statements without the local-factor stability argument.

## Value
PASS. Fixed splitting conditions define natural arithmetic subfamilies. Extending the support threshold from \(<2/5\) to \(<1\) in these families crosses the range needed to force a positive nonvanishing proportion; the resulting \(75\%\) lower bound is a concrete strengthening of the local-specification theory rather than a routine parameter substitution.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest sources are Ahlquist, arXiv:2610.01837v1, and Shankar--Södergren--Templier, arXiv:2107.10900v3 / Invent. Math. 243 (2026). The local conditions must be fixed as \(X\to\infty\); no uniformity for growing local moduli or equality of lower-order terms is asserted. The result is conditional on GRH exactly as Ahlquist's support-\(<1\) theorem is.
