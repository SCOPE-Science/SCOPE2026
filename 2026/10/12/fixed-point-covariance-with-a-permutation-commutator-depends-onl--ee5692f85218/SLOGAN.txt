For a uniform random permutation, the covariance between its number of fixed points and the number of fixed points of its commutator with a fixed permutation depends only on the latter's \(f\) fixed points: it equals \(f(n-f)/[(n-1)(n-2)]\) for \(n\ge3\). Every fixed-point-free reference permutation makes the two counts uncorrelated but not independent; the covariance can be positive when the reference permutation has both fixed and moved points.
