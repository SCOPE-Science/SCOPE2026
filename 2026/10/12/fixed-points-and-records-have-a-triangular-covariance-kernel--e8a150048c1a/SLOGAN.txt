In a uniform random permutation, a record at position \(j\) has covariance \(1/[n\binom{n-1}{j-1}]\) with the total fixed-point count, even though the same-position fixed-point contribution is usually negative. The full positional kernel is triangular, and the total fixed-point/record-count correlation is asymptotic to \(1/(n\sqrt{\log n})\).
