For the four-dimensional model filiform Lie algebra over every odd finite field, the full automorphism fixed-space spectrum has a closed formula and its Burnside sum is exactly \(5\). The five element-orbits are precisely the five intrinsic layers cut out by \(0\subset Z\subset[\mathfrak f_4,\mathfrak f_4]\subset C_{\mathfrak f_4}([\mathfrak f_4,\mathfrak f_4])\subset\mathfrak f_4\).
