Among citation profiles with the same number of papers and the same total citations, the \(h\)-index and \(g\)-index cannot rank two profiles in opposite strict orders below nine total citations. The first reversal occurs at four papers and nine citations: \((3,2,2,2)\) has \((h,g)=(2,2)\), whereas \((7,1,1,0)\) has \((h,g)=(1,3)\).
