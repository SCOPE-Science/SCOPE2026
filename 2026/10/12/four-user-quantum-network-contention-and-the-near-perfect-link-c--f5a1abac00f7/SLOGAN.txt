For a quantum-repeater chain with four uniformly sampled users and two random sessions, the perfect-output one-slot capacity has an exact finite-size Bernoulli formula whose competition loss is strictly positive and of sharp order \(N^{-3}\) at fixed imperfect link probability. When the link failure probability instead scales as \(\lambda/N\), the normalized capacity obeys an explicit, strictly decreasing crossover from \(1/2\) toward zero.
