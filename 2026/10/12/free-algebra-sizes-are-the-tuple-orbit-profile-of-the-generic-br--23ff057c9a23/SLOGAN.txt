For the countable universal ultrahomogeneous Brouwerian semilattice \(\mathbb B\), ordered \(n\)-tuple orbits are exactly congruence kernels of the free \(n\)-generated algebra, and finiteness makes every congruence principal. Hence the exact orbit count is \(|F_n|\): \(1,2,18,623662965552330,\ldots\), with arity four equivalent to the still-unknown \(|F_4|\).
