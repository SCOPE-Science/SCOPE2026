# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof identifies every idempotent weight-minus-one Rota–Baxter map with an ordered complementary pair of subalgebras. Primitive-idempotent support blocks over the splitting field yield a proper two-colored rooted tree exactly when the full incidence matrix has rank equal to the algebra dimension. Frobenius-invariant parent maps reduce to weighted directed arborescences; their Laplacian determinant has the stated block factorization. These arguments are uniform in characteristic. The standalone verifier reproduces the predicted counts for fifteen small finite étale algebras over \(\mathbb F_2\). Those tests are supporting evidence, not an infinite-field proof.

## Originality — PASS, with risks
Gubarev (arXiv:1811.08219, Theorem 2) proves the tree encoding for split algebras \(F^n\); Table 1 reports the split count formula with question marks, alongside numerical counts. An–Bai (arXiv:0711.1389) treat operators on complex associative algebras through dimension three. The new theorem's claimed advance is an explicit formula for general finite étale finite-field forms, with arbitrary Frobenius cycle structure, proved by a directed weighted determinant. The classical graph-theoretic theorem and pre-existing split-case data are credited. An equivalent result under Galois-invariant trees or finite-étale subalgebra language may exist and has not been ruled out exhaustively.

## Value — PASS
The algebra-family parameterization by extension degrees is intrinsic, and counting its splitting decompositions is a natural invariant. The theorem resolves all extension degrees simultaneously, gives a closed arithmetic product, and exhibits independence from the underlying finite field's size. It has mathematical content beyond a small-field recomputation or a restatement of the split case.

## Limits
The result does not count non-idempotent Rota–Baxter operators or classify their conjugacy classes. Symbolic proof supplies the general quantifiers; small-field enumeration is only a consistency check. Publication priority and independent proof checking remain open.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
