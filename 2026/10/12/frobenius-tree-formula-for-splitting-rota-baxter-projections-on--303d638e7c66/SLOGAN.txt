The idempotent weight-minus-one Rota–Baxter operators on every finite étale algebra \(\prod_d\mathbb F_{q^d}^{\,r_d}\) are counted by \(\prod_{d:r_d>0}(2+s_d)(2+s_d+d r_d)^{r_d-1}\), where \(s_d=\sum_{e\mid d,\,e<d}e r_e\). The answer is independent of \(q\) and follows from a Frobenius-equivariant two-colored tree classification.
