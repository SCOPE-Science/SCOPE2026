In the higher-degree Asgarli–Ghioca plane-filling family with \(p\mid r\), every rational singularity has multiplicity exactly \(\min(p^{v_p(r)},q)+1\), with a complete three-regime tangent-cone classification. The ordinary multiple points undergo a sharp collision boundary at \(p^{v_p(r)}=q\); above that boundary all \(q+1\) local branches have rational tangent directions.
