The full automorphism group of Kai's universal quandle \(Q_n=[n]\times\mathfrak S_n\), for every \(n\ge3\), is \(\mathfrak S_n\times\prod_{\lambda\vdash n}Z(C_{\mathfrak S_n}(g_\lambda))\). Every automorphism is inner at \(n=3\), but the first outer quotient occurs at \(n=4\), where it is \(C_2^3\) and the full group has order \(2304\).
