Every integral five-nodal even sextic in the Moriya–Kudo form has full embedded projective symmetry group exactly \((\mathbb Z/2)^2\) or the dihedral group of order eight; the jump occurs precisely when its three paired coefficients satisfy \((G,D,H)=(-A,-B,C_4)\). Two explicit genus-five Howe-type sextics have identical five-node configurations but realize the two different full projective groups.
