For the Płoski moustache \(Q_m=\prod_{j=1}^m(y^2+xz+jx^2)\), the full projective stabilizer is \(\mathbb G_a\rtimes\mu_4\): a one-parameter shear fixes every conic while an order-four symmetry reflects \(j\leftrightarrow m+1-j\). The same shear differentiates to the linear Jacobian syzygy \(x(Q_m)_y-2y(Q_m)_z=0\).
