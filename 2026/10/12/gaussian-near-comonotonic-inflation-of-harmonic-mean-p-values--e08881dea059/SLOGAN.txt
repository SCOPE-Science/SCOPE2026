For fixed \(p\in(0,1)\), the weighted harmonic mean of equicorrelated Gaussian-copula null p-values has excess rejection probability \((1-r)(1-\sum_iw_i^2)\phi(q)[q+\phi(q)/p]+o(1-r)\) as pairwise correlation \(r\uparrow1\), where \(q=\Phi^{-1}(p)\). The coefficient is strictly positive for every nondegenerate weight vector, giving a fixed-level local positive-correlation result but not a uniform-in-level or global-correlation theorem.
