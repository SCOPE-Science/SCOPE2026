For the uniform Dirichlet mixture of all \(k!\) permutation copulas, the normalized checkerboard fluctuations \(\sqrt{(k!+1)(k-1)}\,(\widehat Y_k-uv)\) converge as a continuous random field to a Brownian pillow. An exact separable covariance kernel, a discrete-sine eigenspectrum, and almost-sure uniform collapse before normalization complete the fluctuation description.
