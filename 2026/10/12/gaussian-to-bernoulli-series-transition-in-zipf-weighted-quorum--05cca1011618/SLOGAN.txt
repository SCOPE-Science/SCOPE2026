Under homogeneous Poisson discovery clocks and Zipf quorum weights \(w_i\propto i^{-s}\), weighted-threshold hitting times have Gaussian fluctuations for \(s\le1/2\) (with a logarithmic correction at equality), but a non-Gaussian weighted Bernoulli-series limit for \(1/2<s<1\). The sharp second-order phase boundary is invisible to the known universal first-order quorum-time law.
