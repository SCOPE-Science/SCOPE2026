For every finite abelian odd-order group \(A\), the pseudo-algebra of \(\operatorname{Dih}(A)\) is the element-order spectrum of \(A\), with every nontrivial multiplicity halved, plus the two linear codegrees \(1\) and \(2\). Consequently the group pseudo-algebra determines \(A\), and therefore determines the generalized dihedral group within this entire family.
