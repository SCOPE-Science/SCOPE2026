# Review

## Correctness

PASS. The channel is trace preserving by direct summation of the Kraus squares. The proof uses the exact transmittability criterion from arXiv:2609.40054v1. Each rank-one branch projector lies in the channel operator system, so a quantum block of dimension at least two must annihilate every branch coordinate and therefore lies in the unique protected qubit sector. Cross terms \(E_0^*E_j\) then force every classical block coexisting with that qubit to lie in the \(z\)-dimensional zero-overlap branch subspace. The classical bound \(m+1\) follows from pairwise orthogonal nonzero output supports. The final embedding comparison is exact and covers all boundary cases \(0\le z\le m-1\).

## Originality

PASS. The closest source, Salzmann--Singh arXiv:2609.40054v1, supplies the general criterion and the single \(m=2\) Hadamard example with maxima \(\mathbb M_2(\mathbb C)\) and \(\mathbb C^3\). Targeted published-finding corpus and web searches for rotated branch bases, generic domination failure, \(\mathbb M_2\oplus\mathbb C^z\), and equivalent hybrid-code formulations did not locate the all-\(m\) classification, the exact \(z=m-1\) domination threshold, or the Haar-generic corollary. Earlier operator-algebra QEC and noncommutative-graph sources give the general language and criteria but not this family-level statement.

## Value

PASS. Failure of domination is a central phenomenon in the recent source. The new theorem shows that its smallest explicit counterexample is not fine-tuned: the same mechanism extends to arbitrarily many rank-one branches, and domination is recovered only on the sharply characterized aligned locus. The result therefore supplies a structural robustness theorem and a complete exact phase diagram for a natural family generated by the source example.

## Closest literature and limitations

The closest literature is arXiv:2609.40054v1, with foundational background from arXiv:quant-ph/0608071v2 and DOI 10.1109/TIT.2012.2221677. The theorem is exact and one-shot; approximate recovery, tensor powers, entanglement assistance, and more general branch ranks remain outside its scope.
