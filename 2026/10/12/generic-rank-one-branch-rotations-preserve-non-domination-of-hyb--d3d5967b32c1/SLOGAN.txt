For a protected qubit followed by \(m\) rank-one branches into an arbitrary orthonormal output basis, the maximal exact hybrid codes are controlled by a single integer \(z\): the number of branch vectors orthogonal to the qubit-output overlap direction. Domination occurs only at the fully aligned value \(z=m-1\); Haar-generically \(z=0\), so the incomparable maxima are \(\mathbb M_2(\mathbb C)\) and \(\mathbb C^{m+1}\).
