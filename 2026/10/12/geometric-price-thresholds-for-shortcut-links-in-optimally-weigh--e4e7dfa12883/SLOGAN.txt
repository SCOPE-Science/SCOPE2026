For optimally budgeted weighted paths, the price at which a single shortcut becomes worthwhile has a sharp geometry-dependent limit: an end-to-end link has critical price asymptotic to \((\pi^2/4-2)n^2\), whereas a short chord's coefficient rises from \(2/3\) at an endpoint to \(1\) in the interior. The finite activation test follows classical convex network design; the contribution is the explicit macroscopic and boundary-layer price law.
