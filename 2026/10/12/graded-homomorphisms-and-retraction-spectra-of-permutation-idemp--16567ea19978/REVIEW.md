# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The degree-two PBW coordinates in Gateva-Ivanova–Majid, Theorem 3.4, are linearly independent. This makes the defining-relation test equivalent to equality of column sums, including the zero linear map. Multiplication in every positive degree yields the exact formula for all higher graded components. Idempotence reduces to a block idempotent, with the two scalar choices and eigenspace restriction on the shear vector. Every image has been checked via independent PBW monomials in its own grading. Projection counts include all complements; no characteristic assumptions are hidden. The included direct small-prime-field brute-force verifier succeeds. It is supporting evidence, not proof of the unbounded statement.

## Originality — PASS, subject to the stated bibliographic risk
The closest sources are the 2023 canonical-presentation paper arXiv:2308.11427 (Theorem 3.4 and Sections 6–7), its broader 2024 successor arXiv:2409.02939 (Proposition 5.3 and Sections 6–7), and the 2022 structural precursor arXiv:2212.07361 (Example 3.15 and Corollary 3.17). Their examined claims yield the algebra and PBW basis but do not state the all-graded-homomorphism criterion, retraction orbit classification, finite-vs-infinite image dichotomy or exact rank spectrum. Morphisms of solutions and degree-changing Veronese maps are not the same statement. Closest returned mathematical-result records instead cover automorphisms of different finite-dimensional monomial algebras and retractions of weak orders; neither implies these conclusions. The possibility of an equivalent older result under another algebraic description remains unresolved.

## Value — PASS
The whole class of permutation-idempotent Yang–Baxter algebras is represented by these canonical algebras, so the result gives the complete degree-preserving homomorphism behavior rather than an isolated matrix invariant. The split between square-zero finite graded retracts and infinite canonical graded retracts, uniform \(2n\) orbit classification, and all-prime-power rank distribution are natural structural data about a family previously treated through PBW bases, Veronese constructions and Segre products. The classification is not merely a single low-rank exercise.

## Scope and risks
Only *graded unital* maps are included. General ungraded maps and morphisms of underlying Yang–Baxter solutions may have other structures. General all-field theorems rest on the proof; finite enumeration is a cross-check. Equivalent coverage in literature missed by the queried terminology is a residual uncertainty, and no independent external audit has been performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
