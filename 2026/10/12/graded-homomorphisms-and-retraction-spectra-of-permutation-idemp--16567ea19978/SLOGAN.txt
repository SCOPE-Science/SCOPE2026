For the canonical permutation-idempotent Yang–Baxter algebra \(A_n\), every graded algebra homomorphism is governed by one augmentation scalar and a linear map. Graded retractions have exactly \(2n\) automorphism-conjugacy types, with either a finite square-zero image or another canonical algebra; over \(\mathbb F_q\), their exact degree-one rank polynomial is \(\sum_{r=0}^{n}q^{r(n-r)}({n-1\brack r}_q+{n-1\brack r-1}_q)T^r\).
