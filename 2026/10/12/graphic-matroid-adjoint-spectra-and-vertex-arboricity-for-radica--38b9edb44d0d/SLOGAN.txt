For the radical-square-zero path algebra of any oriented simple graph, the complete inner-commutator rank polynomial is determined by graphic-matroid flats, contractions, and the rank function, independently of arrow orientation. Forests have the universal spectrum \(q^{c(G)}(1+(q^2-1)T)^{|E|}\), and an element surjects by commutators onto the whole radical exactly when the graph's vertex arboricity is at most \(q\).
