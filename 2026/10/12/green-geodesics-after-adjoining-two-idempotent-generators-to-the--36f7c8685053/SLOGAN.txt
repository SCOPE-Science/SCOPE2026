For the monogenic free inverse monoid, adjoining the unit-cost idempotents \(xx^{-1}\) and \(x^{-1}x\) changes exact Green-geodesic multiplicities but preserves deterministic context-freeness of the \(\mathcal R\)- and \(\mathcal L\)-languages and regularity of the \(\mathcal D\)-language. Every nonidentity \(\mathcal R\)-class now has exactly two shortest words, except balanced two-sided intervals, which have four; their complete length distribution is rational.
