For uniform spacings, Greenwood's quadratic concentration \(G_m=\sum_iD_i^2\) and the Shannon spacing entropy \(H_m^{\mathrm{sp}}=-\sum_iD_i\log D_i\) have exact covariance \(- (m-1)/((m+1)^2(m+2))\). Their raw covariance vanishes, but their correlation tends to the strong universal limit \(-1/(2\sqrt{\pi^2/3-3})\approx-0.92869\).
