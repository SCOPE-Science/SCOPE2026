For a continuous-time quantum walk on the \(n\)-cube with an absorber at every nearest neighbor of one vertex, the exactly captured dimension is \(n^2-n+2-\mathbf1_{\{2\mid n\}}\), and every site's capture probability has an explicit Hamming-distance formula with a single even-dimensional Krawtchouk correction. The antipodal neighbor shell adds no new captured directions, while installing one more absorber at the original vertex repairs exactly the parity-protected direction for even \(n\).
