Given Kolmogorov distance eta of unitary output-probability marginals from Haar and a controlled q-th output-probability moment, the expected Shannon-entropy gap is at most a dimension-free multiple of eta^(1-1/q). When such moments are controlled through order k (as for ordinary relative k-design moment comparison), even q<=k yield an O(eta log(1/eta)) bound once k is of logarithmic order in 1/eta, sharpening the prior square-root estimate under these explicit hypotheses.
