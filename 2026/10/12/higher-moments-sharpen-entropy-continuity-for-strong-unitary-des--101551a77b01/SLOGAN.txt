For a strong unitary design, the higher even moments already guaranteed by the design condition sharpen the expected-output-entropy error from the source's \(O(\sqrt\eta)\) to \(O(\eta^{{1-1/q}})\) for every even \(q\le2k\). Choosing \(q\asymp\ln(1/\eta)\) gives \(O(\eta\ln(1/\eta))\), reducing the sufficient generic design-order scale for \(O(1/n)\) expected entropy error from \(n^4\) to \(n^2(\ln n)^2\).
