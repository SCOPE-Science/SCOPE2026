For any nonnegative degree-\(q\) homogeneous statistic of a centered normal sample, weighting samples by that statistic shifts \((n-1)S^2/\sigma^2\) exactly from \(\chi^2_{n-1}\) to \(\chi^2_{n-1+q}\). Hence \(\operatorname{Cov}(T,S^2)=q\sigma^2\mathbb ET/(n-1)\), covering the sample range, Gini mean difference, and every other admissible homogeneous residual dispersion statistic at once.
