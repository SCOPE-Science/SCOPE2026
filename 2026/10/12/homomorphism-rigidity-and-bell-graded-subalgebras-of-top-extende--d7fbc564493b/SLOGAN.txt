For the finite Heyting algebra formed by adjoining one top point to a Boolean algebra on \(m\) atoms, every homomorphism to its \(n\)-atom counterpart is either injective or two-valued, with exact hom-set count \(m+m!\left\{\begin{smallmatrix}n\\m\end{smallmatrix}\right\}\). Its subalgebras are indexed by set partitions plus one Boolean minimum, yet only that minimum and the whole algebra are retract subalgebras.
