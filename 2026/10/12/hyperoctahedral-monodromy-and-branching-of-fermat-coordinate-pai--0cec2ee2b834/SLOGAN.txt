Every coordinate-pair quasi-Galois projection of a complex Fermat hypersurface of degree \(d\ge4\) and dimension at least two has the full hyperoctahedral normal-closure group \(C_2\wr S_{\lfloor d/2\rfloor}\), with an explicit three-type branch-cycle portrait. In particular, the 24 non-Galois quasi-Galois centers on the Fermat quartic surface all have dihedral \(D_4\) normal closures.
