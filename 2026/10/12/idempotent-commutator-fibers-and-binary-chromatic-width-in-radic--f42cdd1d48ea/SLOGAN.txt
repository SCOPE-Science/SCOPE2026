For radical-square-zero quiver algebras, the complete prescribed commutator fibers of pairs of idempotents are a four-state weighted graph sum, and a target is one such commutator exactly when its nonzero-arrow support graph is bipartite. The exact number of idempotent commutators needed is \(\lceil\log_2\chi(H_z)\rceil\), independent of field size and potentially much larger than the unrestricted commutator width.
