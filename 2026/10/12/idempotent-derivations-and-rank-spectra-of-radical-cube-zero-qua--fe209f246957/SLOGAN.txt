For radical-cube-zero quantum exterior algebras with all skew parameters different from \(\pm1\), composition-idempotent derivations have explicit quadratic-shear normal forms, and their conjugacy classes are the parameter-symmetry orbits of subsets of generators. The complete finite-field rank polynomial is independent of the chosen admissible parameters, while characteristic two admits \(2^n\) classes in the constant-parameter family versus \(n+1\) in odd characteristic.
