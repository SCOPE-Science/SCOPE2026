# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact domain is the commutative cubic-truncated symmetric algebra over an arbitrary field. In characteristic not two, the derivation-of-a-product identity forces the square of each generator image to vanish; the degree-zero and degree-one parts therefore vanish, after which idempotence kills every generator image. In characteristic two, the cubic relations force generator images to have zero scalar part, and every linear-plus-quadratic assignment is a derivation. Equality \(D^2=D\) reduces exactly to \(P^2=P\) and \(Q=QP+\delta_PQ\); the symmetric-square decomposition gives explicit and independent constraints. Quadratic conjugacy shears establish the normal form and preserve full linear rank. The Gaussian-binomial complement count and dimension of the admissible quadratic maps give the exact distribution, including \(r=0,n\). Exhaustive checks over small prime fields and \(\mathbb F_4\) and systematic quadratic-shear checks agree with the formulas. The infinity/general-field conclusion rests on the algebraic proof, not enumeration.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risks

Cojuhari–Gardner (Example 2.3) attributes the characteristic-two correspondence of idempotent derivations and binary gradings to Bahturin–Parmenter; that equivalence is explicitly excluded from novelty. Bahturin–Kochetov classify gradings of different divided-truncation coordinate algebras \(O(m;1)\) over algebraically closed fields, not the symmetric cube-jet algebra and not the finite-field rank census. Suárez-Álvarez studies derivations on a noncommutative Ore-type algebra in characteristic zero, not these operators. Searches for direct polynomial, orbit, and characteristic-two equivalents, including published mathematical-result records, did not identify a statement covering the three simultaneous claims of normal form, exact ranks, and finite-field multiplicities. A broader classification of gradings of arbitrary finite local algebras, if available, might imply the normal form; equivalent explicit enumerations remain possible. A negative search by itself is not proof of novelty.

## Value — PASS

Composition-idempotent derivations encode binary gradings in characteristic two and determine a natural operator invariant of a standard Artin local algebra. The proof supplies a sharp characteristic boundary, unrestricted automorphism-orbit normal forms, and every finite-field rank fiber in all embedding dimensions. The quadratic freedom changes strongly with the projection rank, and the resulting multiplicities are not the ordinary counts of idempotent matrices or algebra retractions. These are motivated, complete classification/counting questions, not a finite arbitrary slice.

## Limitations and most relevant literature

The grading correspondence is classical, and similar results may be derivable from broader grading theory. The truncated coordinate algebra of Bahturin–Kochetov uses relations on individual \(p\)-th powers, not the whole cubic maximal-ideal power. The present result does not address general local algebras, divided powers, or derivations of higher-order jets. The verifier exhausts only declared small finite cases.
