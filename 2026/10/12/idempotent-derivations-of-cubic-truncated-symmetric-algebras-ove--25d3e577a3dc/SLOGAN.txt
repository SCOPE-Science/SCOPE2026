For the cubic jet algebra \(K[x_1,\ldots,x_n]/(x_1,\ldots,x_n)^3\), every composition-idempotent associative derivation vanishes outside characteristic two. In characteristic two there are exactly \(n+1\) automorphism classes, and every finite-field rank multiplicity is given by an explicit Gaussian-binomial polynomial from the dimension of admissible quadratic shears.
