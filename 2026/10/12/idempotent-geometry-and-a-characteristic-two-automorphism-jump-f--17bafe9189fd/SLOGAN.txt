For the four-element dihedral quandle algebra, the full automorphism group changes from \((K^\times)^2\rtimes C_2\) in odd characteristic to \((K^4,+)\rtimes C_2\) in characteristic two. At the same transition, the nonzero idempotent locus changes from three affine lines to two affine planes, giving exact finite-field counts and a transitive automorphism action in characteristic two.
