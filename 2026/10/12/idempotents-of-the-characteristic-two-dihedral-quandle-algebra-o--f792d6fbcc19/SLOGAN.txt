Over every characteristic-two field, the dihedral quandle algebra \(K[R_8]\) has exactly four disjoint affine three-spaces of nonzero idempotents, plus zero. Thus \(\mathbb F_q[R_8]\) has exactly \(1+4q^3\) idempotents, and reduction modulo four reveals that only the four basis idempotents downstairs admit nonzero lifts.
