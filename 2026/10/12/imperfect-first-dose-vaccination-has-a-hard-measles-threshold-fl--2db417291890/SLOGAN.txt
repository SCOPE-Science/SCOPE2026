With second-dose vaccination and treatment turned off, the measles model has the hard first-dose floor \(\inf\mathcal R_F=K\epsilon\). At the paper's fitted parameters this floor is about \(1.4022\), so the reported critical newborn coverage \(u_1^*=1.02\) is not only outside \([0,1)\): no combination of newborn and adult first-dose vaccination can make the first-dose-only reproduction number subunit.
