For multiplicative Hilbert cubes whose every subset product becomes a perfect \(k\)-th power after the same positive shift, the published dimension ceiling \(9\) drops to \(7\) for \(k\in\{4,5\}\) and to \(6\) for \(k\ge6\). A positive-shift four-product identity removes the size condition from the usual gap step and lets a short tail force the contradiction.
