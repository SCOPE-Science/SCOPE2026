The 2026 discrete Wiener-range paper truncates to inclusive integer intervals but normalizes as though their lower endpoints were excluded; its printed bivariate mass totals \(86.7321789531\), not one, at the very bounds used in its market illustration. The error becomes arbitrarily severe for short observation times, with an explicit at-least-exponential divergence, and disappears only after replacing each denominator \(F(b)-F(a)\) by \(F(b)-F(a-1)\).
