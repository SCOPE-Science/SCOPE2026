For distinct primes \(p,q\), every maximal independent set of \(\operatorname{AG}(\mathbb Z/(p^a q)\mathbb Z)\) is one of \(a+1\) explicitly indexed facets, yielding a closed independence polynomial and \(\alpha=a\). The graph is well-covered exactly for \(a\le2\), while for \(a\ge2\) its independent domination number is always \(2\).
