With independent Wiener drivers, \(\sigma_S=-\sigma_I\) does not create the anticorrelation of a single noisy transmission rate: the printed model has \(d\langle S+I\rangle_t=0.04(1-\alpha)^2S^2I^2dt\), whereas genuine transmission noise cancels from \(S+I\). The same nonzero normal diffusion rules out every finite positive rectangle as an invariant domain, so the simulated zero-flux problem on \([0,1]^2\) is a confined PDE model rather than the unrestricted SDE law.
