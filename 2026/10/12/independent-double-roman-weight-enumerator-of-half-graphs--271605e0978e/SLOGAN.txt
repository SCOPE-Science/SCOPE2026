Every independent double Roman dominating function of the half graph \(H_n\) is supported on one of the \(n+1\) canonical cut independent sets. This gives the exact enumerator \(\mathcal I_{dR}(H_n;z)=2z^{2n+1}(1+z)^{n-1}+(n-1)z^{2n+2}(1+z)^{n-2}\), so \(i_{dR}(H_n)=2n+1\) and exactly two minimum functions exist.
