For rectangular rook graphs \(R_{m,n}=K_m\square K_n\), every induced forest is encoded by a vertex-disjoint collection of alternating paths in \(K_{m,n}\), giving a closed bivariate exponential generating function. The maximum induced-forest size is \(\min\{m+n-1,2\min(m,n)\}\), and an explicit factorial/binomial formula counts all maximizers by connected-component number, with disconnected maximizers first appearing when the part sizes differ by at least two.
