The \(144\) \((-1)\)-curves on the index-\(2\) Hesse-type Halphen surface have global pairwise intersections \(0,1,2,3,4\), with exactly \(72\) pairs of intersection \(4\). Those \(72\) pairs are precisely cubic–quartic pairs in which the quartic's triple base point is one of the cubic's two omitted base points.
