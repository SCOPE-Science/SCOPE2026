If \(L_A=\mathbf F_q t\ltimes_A\mathbf F_q^n\) has invertible almost-abelian action, its noncommuting graph is always \(K_{q^n-1,\underbrace{q-1,\ldots,q-1}_{q^n\text{ parts}}}\), regardless of \(A\). The graph recovers \(q\) and \(n\) but, from dimension \(3\) onward, systematically forgets the projective-similarity class that distinguishes the Lie algebra.
