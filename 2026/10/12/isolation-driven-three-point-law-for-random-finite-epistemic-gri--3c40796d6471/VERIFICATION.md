# Direct mathematical verification

Execute \(`python3 verify_random_epistemic.py`\) with a standard Python 3 interpreter; the verifier requires no installed third-party libraries or external data. It implements a direct iterative modal partition refinement and separately checks the deterministic one-round classification under its exact hypotheses.

The \(66{,}064\) exhaustive cases are all \(2^{4}+2^{9}+2^{16}\) binary square valuations in sizes \(2,3,4\), respectively, not an exhaustive test of all finite matrices. Of these, \(32{,}440\) satisfy the deterministic sufficient hypotheses; every one matches the exact type-count prediction. In \(43\) small cases a separate closure enumerator constructs the Boolean algebra generated by the atom and both row/column modalities and confirms its size equals the corresponding power of two. The additional \(480\) seeded Bernoulli matrices undergo direct refinement; the reported sample counts are illustrative, **not** claimed as exact limiting frequencies. Thirty-six finite joint factorial-moment evaluations check the explicit finite formula and approach to its limit.

The proof's asymptotic assertions depend on mathematical factorial-moment convergence and maximum-degree bounds, not on simulation. The same-source finite checker is a reproducibility aid and does not constitute independent scientific audit.
