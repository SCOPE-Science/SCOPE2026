# Review

## Correctness

PASS. For \(P_H\), strict upper cones are exactly \(x+H_+\). A negative displacement cannot give reverse upper-cone inclusion because the least positive element of \(H\) produces an explicit missing witness. For a nonnegative displacement \(d\), reverse inclusion is equivalent to \(d+H_+\subseteq H_+\), which holds exactly when \(d\in H\) or \(d\) is pseudo-Frobenius. Standard pseudo-Frobenius adjunction preserves numerical-semigroup structure, so the identity iterates. Strict growth at every nonterminal stage follows because the Frobenius number is pseudo-Frobenius. The genus identity is a finite telescoping count of the newly adjoined gaps.
