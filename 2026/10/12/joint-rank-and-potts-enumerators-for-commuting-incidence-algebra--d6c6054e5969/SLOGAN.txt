For every finite poset, ordered commuting idempotent tuples in its incidence algebra have explicit Boolean-color affine charts and a complete finite-field joint-rank enumerator. Their total is a Fortuin--Kasteleyn evaluation at \(Q=2^k\), and its degree first reaches the number of strict comparabilities exactly when \(2^k\) reaches the poset height; simultaneous diagonalizability alone was already known.
