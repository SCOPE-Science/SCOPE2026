Ježek's simultaneous calibration-and-discrimination log-likelihood is not jointly concave: on a valid commuting-qubit slice its Hessian at \(p=q=1/2\) is \(\begin{pmatrix}-4&4\\4&0\end{pmatrix}\) with determinant \(-16\). Therefore the source's convexity statement cannot certify global uniqueness or convergence of the joint state-and-POVM iteration; a separate global argument is required.
