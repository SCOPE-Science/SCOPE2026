Practical L4-SGD on a scalar quadratic eventually locks its adaptive loss reference to the current loss, after which any fixed positive denominator regularizer \(\varepsilon\) forces the sharp tail \(tL(x_t)\to\varepsilon/[2\alpha(1-\gamma)\lambda]\). In the formal unregularized case the same post-lock dynamics are geometric, so the numerical-stability constant changes the exact late convergence class.
