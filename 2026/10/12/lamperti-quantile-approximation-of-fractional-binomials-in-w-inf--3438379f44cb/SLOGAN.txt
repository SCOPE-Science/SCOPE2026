The Lamperti stable-ratio law supplies explicit heterogeneous Bernoulli parameters whose entire Cramér rate function matches that of a fixed fractional binomial. Consequently, the resulting Poisson-binomial sums approximate \(\alpha\mathrm{Bin}(N,1/2)\) in \(W_\infty=o(N)\) for every fixed \(0<\alpha<1\), answering the sublinear part of the known \(2/3\)-scaling question.
