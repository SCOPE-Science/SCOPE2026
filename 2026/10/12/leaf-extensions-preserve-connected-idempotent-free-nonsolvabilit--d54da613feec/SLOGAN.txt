Leaf extension by new natural-basis vertices satisfying \(f_j^2=e_1\) preserves non-degeneracy, graph connectedness, idempotent-freeness, and nonsolvability of an evolution algebra. Applied to the Hu--Wen exceptional algebra, this yields connected indecomposable counterexamples in every dimension \(n\ge3\) over every field of characteristic different from \(2\).
