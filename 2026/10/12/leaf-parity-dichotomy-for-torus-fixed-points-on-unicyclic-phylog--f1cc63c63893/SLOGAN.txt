For every simple trivalent unicyclic graph with \(n\ge3\) leaves, the associated phylogenetic toric polytope has two torus-fixed points over each even-parity leaf labeling and one half-cycle point over each odd-parity labeling. Therefore \(\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(X(G))=3\cdot2^{n-1}\), independent of cycle length and attached-tree shape.
