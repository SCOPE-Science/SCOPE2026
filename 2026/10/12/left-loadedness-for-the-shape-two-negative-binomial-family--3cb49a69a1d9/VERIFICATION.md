# Verification

The proof is symbolic and universal. The survival function is obtained by summing a differentiated geometric series. The first endpoint is reduced to exact rational checks for \(n=2,3,4,5\) and the monotonicity of \((1+1/x)^x\) afterward. The last endpoint is reduced to exact checks for \(n=3,4\) and \((1+1/x)^x<e<3\) afterward. The internal sign structure follows from the exact consecutive-ratio identity for \(\beta_i\).

No exhaustive finite range, floating-point comparison, or timeout is used to establish the theorem. The proof does not cover negative-binomial shapes \(r\ge3\) and does not assert condition \(L_2\).
