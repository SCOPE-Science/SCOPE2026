For the triangular graph \(T_n=L(K_n)\), the liar's vertex-edge domination number is exactly \(\lceil3n/4\rceil\) for every \(n\ge3\). For \(n\ge8\), all minimum sets correspond precisely to forests of trees on at least four vertices, with one narrowly specified triangle exception, yielding an explicit Cayley-tree enumeration of every optimum set.
