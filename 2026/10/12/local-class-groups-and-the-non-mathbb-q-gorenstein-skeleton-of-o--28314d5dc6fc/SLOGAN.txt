For every odd cycle of length \(2m+1\) with \(m\ge3\), the stable-set projective toric variety has identical non-Gorenstein and non-\(\mathbb Q\)-Gorenstein loci: exactly \(2m+1\) linearly embedded copies of \(\mathbb P^m\), with explicitly classified pairwise intersections. Its generic local canonical obstruction is the primitive infinite-order class \((m-2)g\); all torus-fixed local class groups and canonical classes have closed formulas.
