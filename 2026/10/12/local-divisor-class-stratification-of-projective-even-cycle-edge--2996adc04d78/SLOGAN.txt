For every even-cycle edge toric hypersurface, the rank of the Zariski local divisor class group at a projective point with \(a\) vanishing edges of one parity and \(b\) of the other is exactly \((a-1)(b-1)\) when both counts are positive. The worst projective non-\(\mathbb Q\)-factorial defect is \((m-1)(m-2)\), attained at precisely the \(2m\) coordinate torus-fixed points of \(X_m\).
