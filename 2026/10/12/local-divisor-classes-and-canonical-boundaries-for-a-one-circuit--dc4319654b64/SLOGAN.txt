Deleting one basis from the rank-\(k\) uniform matroid produces a natural projective toric variety whose torus-fixed local class groups jump integrally from \(\mathbb Z\) to \(\mathbb Z^2\) exactly at the \(k(n-k)\) bases adjacent to the deletion. Its fixed-point canonical class is \(\mathbb Q\)-Cartier precisely when Cartier, and the entire variety is Gorenstein only for \((n,k)=(6,3)\).
