Deleting the coordinate \(x_0x_1\) from the quadratic Veronese map of \(\mathbb P^n\), for \(n\ge3\), gives a projective variety with one singular line, a reduced conductor supported on that line, and two higher-dimensional pinch-type endpoints. Every point of the line has depth \(2\) and the same full local Hilbert--Samuel series \(2/(1-T)^n-1/(1-T)\), while the two completed-local-ring types describe precisely how the branches change along the line.
