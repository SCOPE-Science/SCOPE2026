For \(n\ge5\), locating-dominating sets of the crown graph are exactly the omission patterns with at most one doubly omitted matching index, apart from two explicit singleton-side domination obstructions. This gives a closed bivariate polynomial for every locating-dominating set and yields \(\gamma_L(\operatorname{Cr}_n)=n-1\) with exactly \(n(2^{n-1}-2n)\) minimum sets.
