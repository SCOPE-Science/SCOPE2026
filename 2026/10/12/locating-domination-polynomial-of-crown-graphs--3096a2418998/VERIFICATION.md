The symbolic proof is self-contained. A standalone verifier reconstructs the crown graph and checks, for every subset at \(5\le n\le7\), equivalence between the graph-theoretic locating-dominating definition and the omission-index classification. It independently compares every bivariate coefficient to the closed formula and checks the minimum size, number of minimum sets, and total count.

Finite verification does not prove the all-order theorem; it is a stress test for the symbolic classification and enumeration. The all-order proof is the matching-index argument in RESULT.md.
