For robust unidirectional binary cycles under standard replicator dynamics, the interior flow preserves volume in logit coordinates and the mixed-equilibrium Jacobian satisfies \(\chi(\lambda)=\lambda^N-\prod_i q_i(1-q_i)[\Delta_i(1)-\Delta_i(0)]\). Inverter parity gives the exact unstable dimension; the sharp exception is \(N=2\) with odd parity, where the mixed equilibrium is a nonlinear center with an explicit conserved quantity.
