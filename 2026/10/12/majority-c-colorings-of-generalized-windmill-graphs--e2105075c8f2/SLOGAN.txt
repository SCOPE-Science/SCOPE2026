Every majority C-coloring of \(W_{n,m}=K_1\vee nK_m\) with \(m\ge2\) makes each clique blade monochromatic, and at least half of the blades share the center color. Hence the feasible color counts are exactly \(1,\ldots,1+\lfloor n/2\rfloor\), with an exact Stirling-number formula for the number of labeled colorings at every color count.
