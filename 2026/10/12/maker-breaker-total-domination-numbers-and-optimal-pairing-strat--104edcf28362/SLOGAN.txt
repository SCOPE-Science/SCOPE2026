For crown graphs, the Maker–Breaker total domination game has a sharp threshold at \(n=4\): both starting conventions are unwinnable for Dominator on \(\operatorname{Cr}_3\), but for every \(n\ge4\) both game numbers equal \(4\). All optimal pairing certificates are exactly two disjoint within-shore pairs on each side, giving \(\bigl(3\binom{n}{4}\bigr)^2\) minimum systems.
