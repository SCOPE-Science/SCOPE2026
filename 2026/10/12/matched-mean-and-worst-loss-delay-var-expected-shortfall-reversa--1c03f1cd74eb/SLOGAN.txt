If two empirical loss distributions have the same mean and the same worst loss, VaR and Expected Shortfall cannot strictly reverse until the tail probability covers two scenario masses. The sharp threshold is \(\lceil2/(1-\alpha)\rceil\) scenarios: \(40\) at \(95\%\) confidence and \(80\) at \(97.5\%\), with the equal-mean witness \((0,\ldots,0,1,3,4)\) versus \((0,\ldots,0,2,2,4)\).
