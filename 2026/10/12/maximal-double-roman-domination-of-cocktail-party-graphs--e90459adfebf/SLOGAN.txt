For the cocktail party graph \(CP_n=K_{2,2,\ldots,2}\), \(n\ge2\), maximal double Roman domination has the exact value \(\gamma_{dR}^{m}(CP_n)=2n+1\). Every optimum has one zero, two label-\(2\) defenders outside its partner pair, all other vertices labeled \(1\), so there are exactly \(2n(n-1)(2n-3)\) minimum functions.
