For every \(n\ge4\), maximum Grundy dominating sequences of \(C_n\) are exactly binary frontier-growth sequences followed by one of three terminal choices, so there are \(3n2^{n-4}\) ordered maxima. Their vertex sets omit exactly an adjacent pair or a distance-two pair, giving \(2n\) maximum sets for \(n\ge5\) and \(6\) for \(n=4\).
