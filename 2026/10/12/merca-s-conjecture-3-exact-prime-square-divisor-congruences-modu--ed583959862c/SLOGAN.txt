For every odd prime \(p\) and \(r\in\{3,5,7\}\), Merca's set \(\mathcal B_{r,p}\) has size \((p-1)/2\) when \(p\equiv r\pmod8\) and size \(p-1\) otherwise. More precisely, membership is governed by whether \((8\beta+r)/p\) avoids the square classes modulo \(8p\), giving an exact quadratic-nonresidue criterion and proving the published Conjecture 3.
