# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. Exact ultrahomogeneity makes the labeled finite diversity a complete automorphism-orbit invariant for injective tuples. The free-coordinate count is \(2^n-n-1\). The explicit point \(\delta_0(A)=1+\varepsilon|A|\) for \(|A|\ge2\) has strict slack in every non-tautological monotonicity and overlapping-subadditivity inequality, so the diversity parameter space contains a full-dimensional open box. Fixing the \(\binom n2\) pair coordinates leaves exactly the displayed number of higher coordinates, giving both the upper bound and a matching local lower bound for fiber dimension. The three-point interval follows directly from monotonicity and the three overlapping-edge inequalities, with the converse checked against the original diversity axiom.

## Originality
PASS with a substantial folklore caveat. Prior work already supplies all three qualitative ingredients separately: a universal ultrahomogeneous diversity, the dimension of the finite diversity cone, and examples showing that induced metrics do not determine diversities. The searched literature did not locate the exact automorphism-orbit fiber formula, its open-stratum statement, or the fact that the first positive-dimensional metric fiber occurs already at arity three. The accepted claim is restricted to that synthesis and its exact quantitative consequences.

## Value
PASS. The result gives a compact quantitative measure of how much orbit information is destroyed by passing from the universal diversity to pairwise metric data. It also provides an explicit minimal witness on three points and a clean comparison point for the much more rigid \(L_1\)-diversity subclass.
