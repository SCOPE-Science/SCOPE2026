On arbitrary quadratic clients, deterministic Mime with SGD exactly reproduces centralized gradient descent after one and two local steps. The first possible averaged client-curvature defect appears at the third step and is exactly \(-\eta^3V_Hc\), with \(V_H=m^{-1}\sum_i(H_i-\bar H)^2\), so its inner product with the broadcast gradient is always nonpositive.
