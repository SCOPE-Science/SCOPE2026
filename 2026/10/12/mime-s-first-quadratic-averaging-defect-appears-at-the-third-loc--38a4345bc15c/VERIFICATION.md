The package verifies the deterministic quadratic Mime identities directly from the defining client correction.

`verify.py` compares direct client-level Mime execution with centralized gradient descent on random noncommuting symmetric positive-definite client Hessians. It verifies exact agreement after one and two local steps, the cubic third-step defect, the Hessian-variance identity, and the broadcast-gradient sign formula.

It also checks an exact rational scalar example in which the third-step Mime average differs from centralized gradient descent by exactly \(-1/500\).

Finite computations are transcription guards only. The identities are proved algebraically in `RESULT.md`.
