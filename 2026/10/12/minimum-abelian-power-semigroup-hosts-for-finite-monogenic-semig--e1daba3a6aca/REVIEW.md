# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof uses the exact ascending chain of translated sumsets in a finite abelian group. Strictness before stabilization proves the index, the eventual quotient cosets prove the period, and the bound and equality classification follow by cardinality and Lagrange's theorem. The explicit cyclic construction attains the bound for every positive parameter pair, including all boundary cases. A standalone, independently implemented subset enumeration checks 13,309 nonempty subsets across 17 small abelian-group profiles and 130 additional construction cases; these finite checks corroborate but do not substitute for the proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual bibliographic risk
The first-public motivating preprint arXiv:2604.04763 sets qualitative power-semigroup embeddability problems, and Lau (1979) gives a reduction for qualitative representations. The examined 2026 survey does not formulate the minimum finite abelian host order indexed by the exact transient and period, nor the classification of equality cases. Targeted searches for minimal hosts, equivalent cyclic-semigroup terminology, and additive sumset stabilization revealed no equivalent statement. A 2025 general power-semigroup paper and Pedersen–Sizer (1980) were not available in full; they may contain related statements, and the absence of a search hit is not treated as a uniqueness proof. This risk does not supply a known dominating statement.

## Value — PASS
The minimum host order is a natural resource invariant for representing every finite cyclic semigroup, and the sharp formula and all equality cases give a complete infinite-parameter classification. The result quantifies a class relevant to finite-group power-semigroup embeddability rather than merely producing isolated examples or a finite table. The classification also shows that cyclic hosts are already optimal among all finite abelian hosts.

## Scope and limitations
Only nonempty subsets, monogenic targets, and finite abelian hosts are considered. The result is not an answer to the full commutative-semigroup embedding programme, and older equivalent results cannot be categorically excluded without full texts.
