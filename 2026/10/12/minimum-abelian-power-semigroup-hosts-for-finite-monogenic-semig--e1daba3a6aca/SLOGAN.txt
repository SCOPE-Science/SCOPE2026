Every finite monogenic semigroup of index \(\mu\) and period \(\lambda\) embeds in the nonempty-subset power semigroup of a finite cyclic group of smallest possible abelian-host order: \(\lambda\) if \(\mu=1\), and \((\mu+1)\lambda\) otherwise. All minimum-order representations have singleton or two-point normal forms determined by a cyclic subgroup and cyclic quotient.
