For the subgroup-intersection graph of \(C_p^3\), the determining number is \(3\) at \(p=2\) and \(4\) for every odd prime. All minimum bases are projective-frame configurations or their precise point-line mixed analogues; there are \(56\) at \(p=2\) and \(\frac{5}{12}p^3(p^3-1)(p^2-1)\) for odd \(p\).
