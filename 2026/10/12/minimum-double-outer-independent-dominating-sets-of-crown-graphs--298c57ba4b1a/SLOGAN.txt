Deleting a perfect matching from \(K_{n,n}\) raises double outer-independent domination from \(n+1\) to \(n+2\) for every \(n\ge4\). For \(n\ge5\), all minima are one whole bipartition class plus exactly two vertices of the other, giving \(n(n-1)\) minima; \(\operatorname{Cr}_4\) has four additional tied minima.
