For the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M_n\), the Italian domination number is \(3\) only at \(n=3\) and is \(4\) otherwise for \(n\ge2\). For \(n\ge5\), exactly \(\binom{n+1}{2}\) minimum functions exist, arising from one matched \(2,2\) pair or two matched pairs whose four endpoints all receive \(1\).
