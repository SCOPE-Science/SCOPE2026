For \(2\le m\le n\), the minimum perfect Italian functions on the rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) are completely rigid: permutation matrices in the square case, and one full \(2\)-column or two full \(1\)-columns in the rectangular case, with one additional row-column-cross family exactly when \(n=m+1\). The corresponding counts are \(n!\), \(n+\binom n2+mn\), and \(n+\binom n2\) in the three regimes.
