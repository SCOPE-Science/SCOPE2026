For the half graph \(H_n\), quasi-total Italian domination has value \(3\) only at \(n=2\) and value \(4\) for every \(n\ge3\). The optimum is unique for every \(n\ge4\): only the two universal opposite-side vertices receive label \(2\), and all other vertices receive label \(0\); consequently \(\partial_s^*(H_n)=2n-4\) for \(n\ge3\).
