For every connected chain graph with both bipartition classes of size at least \(2\), the published minimum quasi-total Roman weight is \(4\), and every optimum belongs to one of four explicit support patterns. If the universal classes have sizes \(u\) and \(v\), the exact number of minima is \(uv+\mathbf 1_{s=2}ut+\mathbf 1_{t=2}vs+\mathbf 1_{s=t=2}\); in particular every half graph \(H_n\) with \(n\ge3\) has a unique optimum.
