# Review

## Correctness

PASS. For an \(r\)-subcode with annihilator \(W\), the support is exactly
\[
N-p^{n-r}+p^{\dim(W\cap A)}+\#\{j:b_j\in W\}.
\]
The intersection dimension has the sharp lower bound \(\max(0,m-r)\), and the graph constructions in both dimension ranges attain that bound while avoiding all exceptional vectors. Counting the allowed graph maps gives the stated formulas. Five exhaustive ternary and quinary replays independently verify every minimum and multiplicity.

## Originality

PASS. The primary source gives the family, ordinary weights, and classical Griesmer equality, but no higher-support minimizer counts. The generalized-Griesmer theorem already covers the generalized-weight values, so those values are expressly excluded from the novelty claim. A full subcode-support paper on modified Solomon--Stiffler constructions was inspected and found to use a different projective multiplicity pattern: whole subspaces are deleted from repeated simplex codes, whereas the present family also has a partial defect on selected line representatives. No same-family multiplicity formula was found.

## Value

PASS. The family is newly introduced and structurally distinguished by one nonprojective line defect. The formulas classify all subcodes realizing every generalized-weight minimum and quantify exactly how the defect parameter \(t\) changes those extremal incidence counts. This gives natural higher-dimensional information not present in the ordinary few-weight enumerator.
