# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

At p odd, n≥3, 2≤m≤n−1 and 1≤t≤p−1 the support of an r-message subspace U is N−p^(n−r)+p^a+s, with W=U^perp, a=dim(W∩A), and s the number of exceptional vectors in W. The dimension inequality forces a≥max(0,m−r), and graph parametrization yields minimizers exactly at equality and s=0. For r≤m, quotient A/A0 gives p^r possible images of the first complementary basis vector; the t forbidden values coalesce precisely when α∈A0. Gaussian-subspace counts yield the displayed M_r. For r>m the graphs Y→A omit exactly t p^(m(h−1)) graphs for each Y containing e. r=n is unique. A separately reconstructed small exact enumeration (p=3,n=3,m=2,t=1,2) matched formula pairs (11,8),(15,8),(17,1) and (10,5),(14,7),(16,1). The packaged certificate and verifier source were inspected, but their log alone is not proof.
