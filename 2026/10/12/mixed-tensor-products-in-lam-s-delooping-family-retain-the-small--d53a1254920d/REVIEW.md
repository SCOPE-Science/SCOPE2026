# Review

## Correctness
PASS. Lam’s full text gives the family \(A_r\), the identity \(\operatorname{dell}(A_r)=1\), the minimal projective resolution of the endpoint simple \(T_r\), the Ext profile concentrated in degrees \(1\) and \(r\), the successor-closed support lemma, and the three-step calculation of \(\Sigma^3\Omega^2\) in the diagonal case. Replacing the two equal parameters by \(m\) and \(n\) changes neither the Künneth vanishing through degree \(q=\min\{m,n\}\) nor the local projective types used in the three-step calculation. The minimal total tensor resolution supplies the explicit outside-support vertex \((m+2,q+1)\), completing the stable-retract obstruction. The finite checker verifies the supporting degree/support combinatorics for all \(2\le m,n\le12\).

## Originality
PASS. The closest source, Lam’s arXiv:2609.39745v1, treats \(A_n\otimes_k A_n\) and does not state a mixed \(A_m\otimes_k A_n\) theorem. Targeted searches for the mixed family, the \(\min\{m,n\}\) bound, and equivalent rectangular formulations returned no covering result. Guo’s tensor-product theorem is for derived delooping with a finite-global-dimension factor and therefore does not imply the claim for Lam’s infinite-global-dimension factors. The main residual risk is an extremely recent or poorly indexed independent derivation.

## Value
PASS. The result separates the two parameters in the newest unbounded counterexample family and shows that the pathology is not an artifact of self-tensor symmetry. It gives a natural two-parameter lower bound controlled by the smaller arm and produces unbounded defects along unequal pairs such as \((r,2r)\). This directly sharpens the structural interpretation of the motivating tensor-product counterexample.

## Closest literature and limitations
The motivating source proves only the diagonal family and provides the local support mechanism used here. Earlier derived-delooping tensor results cover a materially different regime. The present theorem is a lower bound rather than an exact formula, retains the algebraically closed field hypothesis, and does not claim an exhaustive literature proof of novelty.
