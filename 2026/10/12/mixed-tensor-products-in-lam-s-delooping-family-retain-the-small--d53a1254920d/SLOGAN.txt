Lam’s diagonal delooping obstruction persists for mixed parameters: for \(m,n\ge2\), the simple endpoint tensor in \(A_m\otimes_k A_n\) has delooping level greater than \(\min\{m,n\}\). Thus the nonadditivity defect is at least \(\min\{m,n\}-1\), so it is unbounded even when the two tensor factors are different.
