# Modal operations destroy the sole lattice symmetry of the free positive S4 algebra

## Finding
Let \(F=\mathrm{Fm}_{\mathrm{PS4}}(x)\) be the free one-generated positive S4-algebra described by Moraschini. It has \(37\) elements. Its bounded-distributive-lattice reduct has exactly two automorphisms:
\[
\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathrm{BDL}}(F\!\upharpoonright\!\{\wedge,\vee,0,1\})\cong C_2.
\]
The nonidentity lattice automorphism interchanges
\[
\Diamond\Box x\quad\text{and}\quad\Box\Diamond x
\]
and fixes each of
\[
x,\quad \Box x,\quad \Box\Diamond\Box x,\quad \Diamond x,\quad \Diamond\Box\Diamond x.
\]
In particular it fixes the free generator \(x\). Hence the modal expansion destroys the only nontrivial lattice symmetry:
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathrm{PS4}}(F)=1.}
\]
It follows that \(x\) is the unique element of \(F\) that generates the full positive S4-algebra. Since freeness gives one endomorphism for each possible image of \(x\),
\[
\boxed{|\operatorname{End}_{\mathrm{PS4}}(F)|=37,}
\]
and the identity is the unique surjective endomorphism.

## Assumptions and scope
A positive S4-algebra is a bounded distributive lattice equipped with the positive modal operations \(\Box,\Diamond\) satisfying the positive S4 equations. Moraschini proves that the algebra in his Figure 1 is the free one-generated member \(F\) and that its lattice reduct is the free bounded distributive lattice generated by the seven one-variable modal terms
\[
P=\{x,\Box x,\Diamond\Box x,\Box\Diamond\Box x,\Diamond x,\Box\Diamond x,\Diamond\Box\Diamond x\}
\]
subject exactly to the order relations in Fact 5.2. The claim here concerns this specific free algebra and its reduct; it does not assert rigidity for arbitrary finite positive S4-algebras.

## Proof
Write
\[
a=x,\ b=\Box x,\ c=\Diamond\Box x,\ d=\Box\Diamond\Box x,\ e=\Diamond x,\ f=\Box\Diamond x,\ g=\Diamond\Box\Diamond x.
\]
Reading the covers in Fact 5.2 gives
\[
b<a<e,
\]
\[
b<d<c<g<e,
\qquad
 d<f<g.
\]
No other comparabilities among these seven generators are imposed except those forced transitively.

A finite concrete model of the free bounded distributive lattice on this ordered generating set is obtained as follows. Let \(Q=\operatorname{Up}(P)\), the poset of upsets of \(P\) ordered by inclusion. There are exactly \(14\) such upsets. Evaluating a generator \(p\in P\) at a two-valued isotone valuation identifies it with
\[
\widehat p=\{U\in Q:p\in U\}.
\]
The free bounded distributive lattice is therefore \(\operatorname{Up}(Q)\). Direct enumeration gives
\[
|Q|=14,
\qquad
|\operatorname{Up}(Q)|=37,
\]
recovering the size of Moraschini's Figure 1.

For a finite poset \(Q\), automorphisms of \(\operatorname{Up}(Q)\) are exactly the automorphisms of \(Q\), via the action on principal join-irreducibles. The inclusion poset \(Q\) has exactly two automorphisms. Besides the identity, the only one is induced by the symmetry of \(P\) exchanging \(c\) and \(f\). On the free lattice this gives
\[
c\leftrightarrow f
\]
while fixing \(a,b,d,e,g\). The accompanying verifier enumerates all rank-preserving permutations of the fourteen points of \(Q\) and finds exactly these two order automorphisms. The nonidentity lattice automorphism fixes \(23\) of the \(37\) lattice elements and exchanges the remaining \(14\) in seven pairs.

Now let \(\alpha\) be an automorphism of the full positive S4-algebra \(F\). Its lattice reduct is one of those two lattice automorphisms, and both fix \(x=a\). But Moraschini's theorem says that \(F\) is generated by \(x\) in the full signature. Therefore a positive-S4 homomorphism fixing \(x\) fixes every term in \(x\), hence every element of \(F\). Thus \(\alpha=\mathrm{id}\).

Finally suppose that \(y\in F\) generates \(F\) as a positive S4-algebra. By the universal property of \(F\), there is a unique endomorphism \(h_y:F\to F\) with \(h_y(x)=y\). Its image is exactly the subalgebra generated by \(y\), hence all of \(F\). Because \(F\) is finite, \(h_y\) is an automorphism; rigidity forces \(h_y=\mathrm{id}\), so \(y=x\). Conversely \(x\) generates by construction. The same universal property gives a bijection
\[
F\longleftrightarrow \operatorname{End}_{\mathrm{PS4}}(F),
\qquad
y\longmapsto h_y,
\]
so the endomorphism monoid has \(37\) elements and only \(h_x\) is surjective.

## Relationship to prior work
Moraschini proves that the free one-generated positive S4-algebra is finite, gives its Figure 1, and proves its universal property. In the earlier public version the algebra is explicitly described as having \(37\) elements. The paper also states that the lattice reduct is the quotient of the free seven-generated bounded distributive lattice by the order relations of Fact 5.2. Those ingredients determine the lattice used above.

The inspected paper does not state its automorphism group, does not classify its generating elements, and contains no occurrence of the word “automorphism.” Targeted published-finding corpus and exact web searches likewise found no statement that the lattice reduct has exactly one nontrivial symmetry, that the modal expansion is rigid, or that the free generator is unique. The closest indexed results concern automorphism groups of other free algebras, such as free Gödel algebras, and are mathematically distinct.

Thus the new content is the symmetry calculation and its free-algebra consequences, not the published construction or its cardinality.

## Limitations
The argument is specialized to the one-generated free positive S4-algebra. It does not determine the full multiplication table of the \(37\)-element endomorphism monoid, nor does it address automorphism groups of higher-generated free positive S4-algebras. The originality check cannot exclude an unindexed folklore observation based on Figure 1. Independent audit has not been performed.
