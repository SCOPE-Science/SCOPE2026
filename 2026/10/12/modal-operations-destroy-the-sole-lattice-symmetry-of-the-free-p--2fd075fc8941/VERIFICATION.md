The verifier reconstructs the ordered seven-element generating set from Fact 5.2. It forms all \(14\) upsets \(Q\) of that poset and then all \(37\) upsets of \(Q\), which realize the free bounded distributive lattice subject to those generator inequalities.

It then exhaustively enumerates every rank-preserving permutation of the \(14\)-point inclusion poset \(Q\). Exactly two preserve order. The nonidentity automorphism fixes the lattice generator \(x\), swaps \(\Diamond\Box x\) with \(\Box\Diamond x\), fixes the other five canonical generators, fixes \(23\) of the \(37\) lattice elements, and swaps the remaining \(14\) in seven pairs. The script checks the induced automorphisms on every lattice element and on every pairwise meet and join.
