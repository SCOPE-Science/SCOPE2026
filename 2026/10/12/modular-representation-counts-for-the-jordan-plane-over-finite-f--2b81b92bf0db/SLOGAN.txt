For the Jordan plane \(k\langle x,y\rangle/(xy-yx-y^2)\) over every finite field of characteristic \(p\), the matrix equation \([X,Y]=Y^2\) is solvable exactly when every nonzero-eigenvalue rational-canonical block has length divisible by \(p\). This gives an explicit all-dimensions Euler product for the exact ordered representation counts and shows that nonnilpotent second matrices first occur in dimension \(p\).
