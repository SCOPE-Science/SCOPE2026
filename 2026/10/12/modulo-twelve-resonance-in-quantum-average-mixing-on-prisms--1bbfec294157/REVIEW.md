# Scientific review: prism average-mixing resonance

## Correctness — PASS

The normalizations follow directly from Fourier inversion on the group of order \(2n\); each average-mixing eigenvalue is an integer equal-energy pair count divided by \(2n\). The cyclotomic rigidity lemma is independently proved via the algebraic-integer norm argument and Kronecker's root-of-unity lemma, giving a genuinely exhaustive classification of pairs whose cyclic eigenvalues differ by \(2\). The equal-layer and opposite-layer counts yield each eigenvalue and the sharp rank, including \(n=3\) and \(12\mid n\). Independent real-symmetric projector calculations over \(3\le n\le24\) and pair enumeration over \(3\le n\le18\) agree. Numerical experiments do not establish the infinite statement; the proof does.

Baptista–Coutinho–Marques (arXiv:2308.16378, Section 5) treat product mixing and a covariance/trace formula, not the complete rank. Coutinho et al. (arXiv:2208.08971) provide a general exact algorithm, not the prism spectrum. Semantic searches including prism, circular ladder, cycle Cartesian product, rank and equivalent trigonometric resonance did not locate a result implying the full indexed spectrum or its exceptional rank formula. A general spectral counting result may imply the formula after additional analysis, and another independently derived prism computation may be unindexed; these are real residual risks, not assertions of absolute novelty.

## Value — PASS

The circular ladder is a natural regular finite quantum-transport geometry with two coupled rings. Conventional time averaging retains an unexpectedly sparse set of coherent modes, and the sharp exceptional counts separate even cycles into different residue classes modulo \(12\). The exhaustive algebraic-integer resonance obstruction is the essential structural ingredient; the result gives a complete spectral classification for all circumferences, rather than a routine numerical table or an arbitrary parameter slice.

No generalization to arbitrary rung strengths or claims concerning transient mixing rates are made. There has been no independent human audit or external proof certification.
