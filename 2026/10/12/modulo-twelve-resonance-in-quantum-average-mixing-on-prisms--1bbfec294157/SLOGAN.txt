The ordinary continuous-time quantum walk on a prism \(C_n\square K_2\) has an exactly computable long-time average-mixing spectrum: its rank is \(n\) for odd \(n\), and \(n/2+2\mathbf{1}_{4\mid n}+3\mathbf{1}_{6\mid n}\) for even \(n\). A cyclotomic-integrality argument proves that transverse-layer resonances occur only at the modulo-four and modulo-six cycle lengths, producing a sharp modulo-twelve classification.
