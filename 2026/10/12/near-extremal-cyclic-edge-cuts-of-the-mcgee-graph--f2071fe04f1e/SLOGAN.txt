The McGee graph has exactly 362 cycle-separating eight-edge cuts, in 21 automorphism orbits: 34 isolate an eight-cycle, 224 isolate a seven-cycle with one leaf, 80 isolate a \(\Theta(3,4,4)\) core, and 24 split the graph into paired twelve-vertex subdivisions of \(K_4\). The balanced splits divide into \(AA\), \(AB\), and \(BB\) types with multiplicities 6, 16, and 2, revealing the full first layer beyond the previously known minimum cyclic cut size seven.
