# Nijenhuis geometry of an involutive Hom-Heisenberg algebra

## Finding

Let \(K\) be a field with
\[
\operatorname{char}K\ne2.
\]
Let \(H_K\) have basis
\[
\{e_1,e_2,e_3\},
\]
bracket
\[
[e_1,e_2]=e_3
\]
with all other basic brackets zero, and involutive twist
\[
\alpha(e_1)=e_1,\qquad
\alpha(e_2)=-e_2,\qquad
\alpha(e_3)=-e_3.
\]

This is a multiplicative Hom-Lie algebra. A Nijenhuis operator is a linear map \(N:H_K\to H_K\) satisfying
\[
N\alpha=\alpha N
\]
and
\[
[Nx,Ny]
=
N\!\left([Nx,y]+[x,Ny]-N[x,y]\right)
\]
for all \(x,y\in H_K\).

Every Nijenhuis operator is exactly
\[
\boxed{
N=
\begin{pmatrix}
a&0&0\\
0&b&0\\
0&c&e
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
(a-e)(b-e)=0.
}
\]

Consequently the Nijenhuis variety is
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal C_1\cup\mathcal C_2,
}
\]
where
\[
\mathcal C_1
=
\left\{
\begin{pmatrix}
e&0&0\\
0&b&0\\
0&c&e
\end{pmatrix}
:e,b,c\in K
\right\},
\]
and
\[
\mathcal C_2
=
\left\{
\begin{pmatrix}
a&0&0\\
0&e&0\\
0&c&e
\end{pmatrix}
:a,e,c\in K
\right\}.
\]
Each component is an affine \(3\)-space and
\[
\mathcal C_1\cap\mathcal C_2
=
\left\{
\begin{pmatrix}
e&0&0\\
0&e&0\\
0&c&e
\end{pmatrix}
:e,c\in K
\right\}
\cong\mathbf A^2.
\]

Over every odd finite field \(\mathbf F_q\),
\[
\boxed{
|\operatorname{Nij}(H_{\mathbf F_q})|
=
2q^3-q^2.
}
\]
The exact rank distribution in ranks \(0,1,2,3\) is
\[
\boxed{
\left(
1,\,
(q-1)(q+2),\,
(q-1)(3q-1),\,
q(q-1)(2q-3)
\right).
}
\]

The induced Nijenhuis bracket is especially rigid:
\[
[e_1,e_2]_N=(a+b-e)e_3,
\]
and all other basic brackets vanish. Hence exactly
\[
\boxed{2q^2-q}
\]
Nijenhuis operators over \(\mathbf F_q\) induce the zero bracket, while the remaining
\[
\boxed{q(q-1)(2q-1)}
\]
induce a nonzero Hom-Lie algebra isomorphic to \(H_{\mathbf F_q}\).

The full Hom-Lie automorphism group is
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Aut}(H_K)
=
\left\{
\begin{pmatrix}
s&0&0\\
0&p&0\\
0&r&sp
\end{pmatrix}
:
s,p\in K^\times,\ r\in K
\right\}.
}
\]
For a Nijenhuis operator with parameters \((a,b,c,e)\), conjugation sends
\[
c\longmapsto
sc+\frac{r}{p}(b-e),
\]
while \(a,b,e\) remain fixed.

Therefore, over \(\mathbf F_q\), the Nijenhuis operators have exactly
\[
\boxed{3q^2-q}
\]
automorphism-conjugacy classes. They admit the following disjoint normal-form strata:

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{diag}(e,b,e),
\qquad b\ne e,
}
\]
giving \(q(q-1)\) classes;

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{diag}(a,b,b),
}
\]
giving \(q^2\) classes; and

\[
\boxed{
\begin{pmatrix}
a&0&0\\
0&b&0\\
0&1&b
\end{pmatrix},
}
\]
giving another \(q^2\) classes.

Finally, over every extension field \(\mathbf F_{q^m}\),
\[
|\operatorname{Nij}(H_{\mathbf F_q})(\mathbf F_{q^m})|
=
2q^{3m}-q^{2m}.
\]
Thus the Hasse--Weil zeta function of the reduced Nijenhuis variety is
\[
\boxed{
Z(T)=\frac{1-q^2T}{(1-q^3T)^2}.
}
\]

## Assumptions and scope

The Nijenhuis identity used here is the standard multiplicative Hom-Lie identity appearing in the Hom-Lie Nijenhuis literature:
\[
N\alpha=\alpha N,
\qquad
[Nx,Ny]=N([Nx,y]+[x,Ny]-N[x,y]).
\]

Das and Sen's 2021 article develops the general theory of Nijenhuis operators on Hom-Lie algebras. Baishya and Das's 2024 preprint gives the same defining identity explicitly as Definition 4.8 and describes the induced bracket
\[
[x,y]_N=[Nx,y]+[x,Ny]-N[x,y].
\]

The characteristic restriction is needed because the \(+1\) and \(-1\) eigenspaces of \(\alpha\) must be distinct in the twist-centralizer calculation.

The theorem concerns this one involutive Hom-Heisenberg algebra. It does not classify all twists of the Heisenberg Lie algebra.

## Proof

The Hom-Jacobi identity is immediate because
\[
[H_K,H_K]=Ke_3
\]
is central. Multiplicativity follows from
\[
\alpha([e_1,e_2])=-e_3=[e_1,-e_2]=[\alpha(e_1),\alpha(e_2)].
\]

Because
\[
\alpha=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1)
\]
has distinct \(+1\) and \(-1\) eigenspaces, every endomorphism commuting with \(\alpha\) has the block form
\[
N=
\begin{pmatrix}
a&0&0\\
0&b&d\\
0&c&e
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The only potentially nonzero Nijenhuis defects on ordered basis pairs are generated by
\[
D(e_1,e_2)
=
-d(a-e)e_2
+
\bigl(ab-ae-be+cd+e^2\bigr)e_3,
\]
and
\[
D(e_1,e_3)
=
-d^2e_2+d(a-e)e_3.
\]

Since \(K\) is a field,
\[
d^2=0
\]
implies
\[
d=0.
\]
The remaining scalar equation becomes
\[
ab-ae-be+e^2
=
(a-e)(b-e)
=
0.
\]
This proves the two-component classification.

For finite-field ranks, consider the two components separately. On \(\mathcal C_1\), the rank-one operators are exactly those with
\[
e=0,\qquad (b,c)\ne(0,0),
\]
giving \(q^2-1\) points. On \(\mathcal C_2\), the rank-one operators outside the origin number \(2(q-1)\). Their rank-one intersection has \(q-1\) points. Hence the union has
\[
(q^2-1)+2(q-1)-(q-1)
=
(q-1)(q+2)
\]
rank-one points.

The rank-three count on \(\mathcal C_1\) is
\[
q(q-1)^2,
\]
and the same holds for \(\mathcal C_2\). Their invertible intersection has
\[
q(q-1)
\]
points. Therefore
\[
2q(q-1)^2-q(q-1)
=
q(q-1)(2q-3)
\]
operators have rank \(3\). Subtracting ranks \(0,1,3\) from the total gives
\[
(q-1)(3q-1)
\]
rank-two operators.

For the induced bracket,
\[
[e_1,e_2]_N
=
[Ne_1,e_2]+[e_1,Ne_2]-N[e_1,e_2]
=
(a+b-e)e_3.
\]
On \(\mathcal C_1\), this vanishes exactly when \(b=0\), giving \(q^2\) points. On \(\mathcal C_2\), it vanishes exactly when \(a=0\), also giving \(q^2\) points. Their overlap has \(q\) points. Thus the zero-bracket fiber has
\[
2q^2-q
\]
points.

If
\[
a+b-e\ne0,
\]
scaling \(e_3\) by the inverse of this scalar gives an isomorphism from the induced Hom-Lie algebra back to \(H_K\), and this scaling commutes with \(\alpha\).

Now let \(P\) be a Hom-Lie automorphism. Commutation with \(\alpha\) gives a \(1+2\) block form. Bracket preservation forces
\[
P=
\begin{pmatrix}
s&0&0\\
0&p&0\\
0&r&sp
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
s,p\ne0.
\]
Direct conjugation sends
\[
(a,b,c,e)
\longmapsto
\left(
a,\,
b,\,
sc+\frac{r}{p}(b-e),\,
e
\right).
\]

If \(b\ne e\), the Nijenhuis equation forces \(a=e\), and \(r\) can kill \(c\), producing the first normal-form stratum. If \(b=e\), translation by \(r\) no longer changes \(c\); multiplication by \(s\) leaves \(c=0\) fixed and acts transitively on \(c\ne0\), giving the second and third strata. Their class counts sum to
\[
q(q-1)+q^2+q^2
=
3q^2-q.
\]

## Relationship to prior work

Das and Sen established the general Nijenhuis-operator theory for Hom-Lie algebras, including its cohomological and deformation framework. The public bibliographic record and abstract available for that 2021 article do not expose a Heisenberg classification; complete article text was not available through the noninteractive lawful access routes used for this comparison, so this remains a specific access risk.

Baishya and Das's 2024 preprint is openly available in full. Its Definition 4.8 gives exactly the Nijenhuis equation used here, and Proposition 4.9 gives the induced Hom-Lie bracket. The inspected full text has no occurrence of “Heisenberg”; its displayed three-dimensional example instead uses a different twist and a four-parameter bracket.

A separate calculation of adjoint embedding tensors on a one-parameter Hom-Heisenberg family contains an isomorphic copy of the present Hom-Lie algebra at the involutive parameter, but embedding tensors satisfy a different quadratic operator identity. That classification therefore does not imply the Nijenhuis equations, rank strata, deformation fibers, or conjugacy normal forms above.

Likewise, a complete Nijenhuis census for Yau twists of the two-dimensional affine Lie algebra concerns a different base algebra. In that family the Nijenhuis torsion is automatic after twist commutation, whereas here it imposes the genuine reducible equation
\[
(a-e)(b-e)=0.
\]

Focused searches for the involutive Hom-Heisenberg object, its Nijenhuis variety, the equivalent matrix equation, finite-field counts, deformation fibers, and automorphism orbits did not locate a covering result.

## Limitations

Characteristic \(2\) is excluded because the twist eigenspaces coalesce.

The theorem concerns one canonical involutive Hom-Heisenberg algebra, not every Heisenberg twist.

The full 2021 primary article could not be inspected through a noninteractive lawful full-text route; this is the main residual bibliographic risk. The later 2024 same-framework preprint was inspected in full and supplies the exact defining identity.

Failed searches do not prove absolute historical novelty.
