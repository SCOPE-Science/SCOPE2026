For the involutive Hom-Heisenberg algebra \([e_1,e_2]=e_3\), \(\alpha=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1)\), the entire Nijenhuis variety is the union of two affine \(3\)-spaces meeting in an affine \(2\)-space. Over odd \(\mathbf F_q\) it has \(2q^3-q^2\) operators but only \(3q^2-q\) automorphism-conjugacy classes, and every induced deformation is either zero or isomorphic to the original Hom-Lie algebra.
