The closure of the range of the alternating reciprocal-cube divisor sum \(s_{-3}\) has exactly nineteen connected components and eighteen strict gaps. Every component has explicitly determined endpoints \([L_jT,U_j]\) with rational \(L_j,U_j\) and \(T=1/(\zeta(3)\prod_{p\in\{2,3,5,7\}}(1-p^{-3}))\).
