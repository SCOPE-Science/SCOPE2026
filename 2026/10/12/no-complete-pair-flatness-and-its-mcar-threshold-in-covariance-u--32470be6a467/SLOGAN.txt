When at most one row is fully observed, the covariance-updated all-active-pair Mahalanobis imputation objective is exactly \(n(n-1)p\) on every nonsingular completion, even with a full-rank frozen-covariance imputation design. Under independent cellwise MCAR missingness the exact probability of this blind regime is \((1-r)^n+nr(1-r)^{n-1}\), where \(r=(1-q)^p\), with a sharp logarithmic dimension threshold.
