In the resource-noise model for distributing tree graph states, the star uniquely maximizes and the path uniquely minimizes fidelity below depolarization \(p=3/4\), but their roles reverse for every \(3/4<p\le1\); phase damping never reverses the ordering. For degree-three trees, an exact branching factor determines the optimum solely from the count of trivalent vertices, subject to the distinction between abstract degree constraints and actual hardware embeddability.
