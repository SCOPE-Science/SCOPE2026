The projective stabilizer of Kollár's characteristic-two unicuspidal curve jumps from \(\mathbb G_a\rtimes\mu_3\) in degree four to the finite cyclic group \(\mu_{q+1}\) in each higher degree \(q+2\). Nevertheless its stabilizer **scheme** always has a three-dimensional tangent space, so every member has genuinely nonreduced infinitesimal projective symmetries.
