In Hall's universal locally finite group, marked tuples from any group variety are classified by finite-index kernels in the corresponding relatively free group. For commuting exponent-\(m\) tuples this turns simultaneous conjugacy into the subgroup lattice of \((\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z)^n\), giving exact Birkhoff/Galois counts and growth \(m^{n^2/4+o(n^2)}\).
