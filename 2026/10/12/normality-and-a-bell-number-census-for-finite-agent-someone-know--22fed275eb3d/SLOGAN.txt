For finitely many \(S5\) agents, the someone-knows disjunction becomes a normal box exactly when each world has an agent whose information cell is the intersection of all agents' cells; it then coincides with distributed knowledge under arbitrary nesting. For two agents this is precisely a laminar-intersection condition on their partitions, and the ordered labeled-frame census has exponential generating function \(\exp(2\exp(\exp z-1)-\exp z-1)\).
