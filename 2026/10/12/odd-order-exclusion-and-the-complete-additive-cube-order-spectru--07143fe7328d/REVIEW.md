# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The digit recurrence guarantees parity-opposite letters at each even-aligned adjacent pair for every even base. Centered prefix heights at even indices are multiples of four; an odd-order cube would force an odd common height difference and simultaneously an even one. For base four, all aligned length-four heights vanish, and the explicit digit-complement construction supplies both additional nonaligned zero heights for every length congruent to two modulo four. The checker additionally tests the critical finite identities and witnesses, but the proof does not rely on finite extrapolation.

## Originality

**PASS with residual risk.** Popoli–Shallit–Stipulanti study additive complexity and additive powers of automatic words, including generalized Thue–Morse words, but their inspected statements do not specify an all-even-base odd-order exclusion or the exact additive-cube order set of the base-four word. Chen–Wen classify possible Parikh vectors of individual factors, which do not determine three consecutive equal-sum factors. Targeted bibliographic and public semantic-index searches and comparisons with previously reported scientific claims found no equivalent or stronger statement. The remaining risk is an unindexed earlier equivalent derivation, not an identified dominating theorem.

## Value

**PASS.** A natural additive-power problem has a sharp and complete order spectrum in the first nonbinary even base, together with a uniform obstruction for every even base. The arithmetic construction supplies witnesses for infinitely many orders, making this more than a short forbidden-pattern check or a finite census.

## Scientific limitations

The theorem does not classify positive even orders for bases greater than four, odd bases, or higher additive powers. Independent validation is not performed.
