For every odd exponent \(k\ge3\), a finite Clifford semigroup with nilpotent maximal subgroups admits a \(k\)-complete mapping exactly when no maximal subgroup has a nontrivial cyclic Sylow-\(2\) subgroup. Every such mapping preserves each group fiber, and an explicit construction combines ordinary Hall--Paige mappings on \(2\)-groups with CRT power permutations on odd-order factors.
