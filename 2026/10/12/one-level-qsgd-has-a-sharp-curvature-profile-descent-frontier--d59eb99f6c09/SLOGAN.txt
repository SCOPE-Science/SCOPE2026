For one-level QSGD on \(f(x)=\tfrac12x^\top\operatorname{diag}(a_i)x\), strict expected descent from every nonzero state holds exactly when \(0<\eta<2/\|(a_i)\|_2\). On \(A=LI\), the sharp worst one-step factor is \(1-2h+\sqrt d\,h^2\), so aggressive quantization turns the uncompressed exact-solve step \(h=1\) into an expected expansion on dense directions for \(d\ge5\).
