For the standard base-\(q\) digit-block count modulo \(q\), the intrinsic automaton has exactly \(q|w|+\mathbf1_{\{w_0=0\}}\) states. Every binary pattern of length at least two is transparent under prefix-metric opacity, whereas every pattern over any base at least three is maximally opaque; the binary one-digit patterns \(0\) and \(1\) have normalized opacities \(1/2\) and \(1\), respectively.
