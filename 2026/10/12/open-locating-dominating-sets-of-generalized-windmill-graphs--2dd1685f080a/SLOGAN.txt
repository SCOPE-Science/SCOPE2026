For \(m\ge3\), an OLD-set of \(W_{r,m}=K_1\vee rK_m\) is exactly a set omitting at most one vertex from each blade, so \(\mathcal O_{r,m}(x)=(1+x)x^{r(m-1)}(m+x)^r\) and there are \(m^r\) minimum sets. The friendship boundary \(m=2\) has \(\mathcal O_{r,2}(x)=(2r+1)x^{2r}+x^{2r+1}\); its scalar minimum was already known, while the retained result classifies and counts every OLD-set.
