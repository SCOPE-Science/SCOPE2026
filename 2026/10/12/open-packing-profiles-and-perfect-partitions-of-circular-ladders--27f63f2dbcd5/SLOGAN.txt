Every circular ladder \(C_n\square K_2\) has a complete open-packing polynomial obtained from the independent-set polynomial of one or two squared cycles, with an equivalent compact rational length-generating function. All maximum-packing multiplicities follow six residue classes, and its vertices can be partitioned into maximum open packings exactly when \(3\mid n\) or \(n\in\{4,8\}\), with explicit counts of all such partitions.
