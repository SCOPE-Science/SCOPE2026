For diagonal factorized linear systems, pairing the two Kaczmarz row selections through a maximal diagonal coupling minimizes expected squared solution error at every finite iteration, without changing either stage's prescribed sampling probabilities. On the identity system, independent pairing shifts the absolute mean-square threshold from \(m\log m\) to \(m(\log m+\log\log m)+o(m)\); partial synchronization has an exact crossover determined by \((1-\theta_m)\log m\).
