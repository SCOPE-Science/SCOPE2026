For the affine Grassmann code \(C^{\mathbb A}(2,4)\), exactly \(60\) three-dimensional subcodes attain third generalized Hamming weight \(q^4-q^2\) over \(\mathbb F_2\), compared with \(2q^2(q+1)\) over every larger finite field. The exception is geometric: binary extremizers correspond to symplectic-isotropic affine-plane directions, while all larger fields have only the two rank-one rulings.
