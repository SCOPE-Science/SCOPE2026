For every binary extended Hamming code of length \(2^m\) with \(m\ge2\), a primitive-field-power ordering makes every syndrome obtainable within a three-coordinate window, and no ordering can do so within two. More sharply, an optimal two-window ordering realizes exactly \(2^{m+1}-1\) of the \(2^{m+1}\) syndromes, leaving one explicit, three-window-repairable gap, with identical bounds for cyclic and noncyclic windows.
