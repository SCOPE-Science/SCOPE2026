For coprime \(q,e\ge2\) with \(\operatorname{ord}_e(q)=4\), the least length \(m(q,e)\) of a zero-sum of powers of \(q\) equals \(3\) exactly when \(e=15\) and \(q\equiv7\) or \(13\pmod{15}\). Equivalently, the order-four subgroup \(\{1,4,7,13\}\) of the units modulo \(15\) is the unique such subgroup with minimum three-term additive zero-sum.
