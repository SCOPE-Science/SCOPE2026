At every nonconcurrent triangle of three distinct lines, the locus of plane cubics admitting a linear factor has completed local ring \(\mathbb C[[t_1,\ldots,t_6,u,v,w]]/(uv,uw,vw)\): three smooth branches with a common six-dimensional stratum. The transverse three-axis singularity has multiplicity \(3\), Cohen--Macaulay type \(2\), and an explicitly determined normalization conductor.
