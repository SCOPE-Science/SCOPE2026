# Review of Outer multiset bases of square-page book graphs

## Correctness
PASS. Exact distance-count signatures show that at most two \(a\)-vertices and at most two \(b\)-vertices can lie outside an outer multiset resolving set. Six outside vertices would therefore force both spine vertices and exactly two vertices of each internal side outside, which makes the two spine signatures identical. This proves the lower bound \(2r-3\). A five-vertex complement gives the matching upper bound. Equality then leaves only two omitted-spine patterns, and the explicit signature tables prove exactly when each pattern resolves. Exhaustive direct checks for \(3\le r\le9\) agree with the dimension, complete basis classification, and counts.

## Originality
PASS. The 2019 foundational paper and the 2022 continuation were inspected at statement level and under book/theta aliases. The first develops the invariant and several basic exact families; the second treats the order-minus-one class, value two, lexicographic products, and rectangular grids. Neither inspected source contains a book or theta result. Semantic published-results searches and exact web searches under book, square-page book, and generalized-theta terminology found no equivalent \(2r-3\) formula or basis classification.

## Value
PASS. Book graphs are a standard, highly symmetric generalized-theta family. The theorem identifies a stable codimension-five outer multiset regime, not merely a single small example, and it completely classifies and counts all minimum landmark sets. The result therefore supplies a natural exact family beyond the paths, cycles, grids, diameter-two extremals, and join constructions emphasized in the existing literature.
