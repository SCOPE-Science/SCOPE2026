# Review of Outer multiset dimension and all bases of half graphs

## Correctness
PASS. Exact formulas for the distance multisets of omitted vertices reduce same-side collisions to repeated cumulative landmark counts. They give the lower bound \(n-1\). At equality, prefix/suffix counting forces \(a_1\) and \(b_n\) outside the basis and exactly one landmark in every shifted pair \(\{a_{k+1},b_k\}\). The converse calculation gives distinct same-side signatures; a cross-side collision occurs exactly when the two side landmark counts are equal. This proves the parity-dependent basis count. Exhaustive verification through \(n=10\) agrees with every claimed basis.

## Originality
PASS. The 2019 foundational primary paper was inspected in searchable full text and contains no half-, chain-, or Ferrers-graph occurrence. A later primary paper, which lists AMS classifications \(05C12\) and \(05C76\), was also inspected in full text and likewise contains no such family occurrence. Its broad theorems do not imply the half-graph cumulative-count classification. Targeted exact and semantic searches found no equivalent half-graph value or basis enumeration. The nearest exact same-parameter family found is complete multipartite graphs, whose structural rule is different.

## Value
PASS. Half graphs are the canonical twin-free chain graphs and provide a natural test for unordered landmark information. The theorem gives both the exact invariant and every minimum set, with a genuine parity obstruction and closed basis counts. The result is structural rather than a small finite computation.
