For the half graph \(H_n\), the outer multiset dimension is exactly \(n-1\), and every basis chooses exactly one vertex from each shifted pair \(\{a_{k+1},b_k\}\). All \(2^{n-1}\) choices work when \(n\) is even; when \(n\) is odd, precisely the balanced choices fail, leaving \(2^{n-1}-\binom{n-1}{(n-1)/2}\) bases.
