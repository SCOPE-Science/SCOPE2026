For every crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n\) with \(n\ge3\), the outer multiset dimension is \(2n-3\), and all resolving sets are classified by the three possible omission sizes. There are exactly \(2n(n-1)\) bases, and deleting one perfect matching from \(K_{n,n}\) lowers the invariant from \(2n-1\) to \(2n-3\).
