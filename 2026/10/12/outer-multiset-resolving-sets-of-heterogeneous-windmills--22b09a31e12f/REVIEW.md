# Review of Outer multiset resolving sets of heterogeneous windmills

## Correctness
PASS. Twin symmetry forces at most one omission from each peripheral clique. The exact multiset representation of an omitted peripheral vertex contains \(k_i-1+\varepsilon\) copies of distance \(1\), so two omitted peripheral vertices collide exactly when their clique orders are equal. If the center is omitted, its representation contains only distance \(1\), while every omitted peripheral vertex sees a selected vertex in another nontrivial clique at distance \(2\). These observations prove the classification and the product formulas. Exhaustive direct-distance verification over all heterogeneous windmills of peripheral orders \(4\) through \(10\) agrees with every statement.

## Originality
PASS. The 2019 foundational source defines the target invariant and contains no windmill, friendship, clique, or join treatment in the inspected searchable text. The closest primary result is the 2025 joined-graphs paper, whose full text was inspected. Its Theorem 2.3 treats only uniform windmills \(K_1\vee rK_k\) and proves the scalar value \(rk-1\). Targeted searches under heterogeneous windmill, clique bouquet, block graph, unequal clique sizes, and join-of-cliques aliases did not locate an equivalent theorem. The present result strictly extends the uniform scalar theorem and adds a complete all-set classification and product enumerator.

## Value
PASS. Variable peripheral clique sizes change the invariant in a structured way: every distinct clique order permits one additional omission, while repeated orders compete for a single omission. The exact dependence on the number of distinct clique orders is invisible in the uniform theorem. The classification yields the whole resolving-set profile and all bases for a natural block-graph family.
