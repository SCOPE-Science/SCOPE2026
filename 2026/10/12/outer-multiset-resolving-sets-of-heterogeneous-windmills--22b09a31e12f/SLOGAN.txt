For a clique bouquet \(K_1\vee(K_{k_1}\cup\cdots\cup K_{k_r})\) with all \(k_i\ge2\), an outer multiset resolving set may omit at most one vertex from each peripheral clique, and omissions may occur only from cliques of pairwise distinct orders. Thus \(\operatorname{dim}_{\mathrm{ms}}(G)=\sum_i k_i-|\{k_i\}|\), with a complete product enumerator for all resolving sets.
