For every diameter-two graph and every nondecreasing \(S\) with \(s_1=1\) and \(s_2\ge2\), the complete packing \(k\)-domatic chromatic profile is determined exactly by the cardinalities of maximal independent sets. Above the optimal-color threshold \(n-\alpha+1\), the value is \(k\) precisely when a maximal independent set has size \(n-k+1\), and is \(k+1\) otherwise.
