The normal projective wheel edge-toric variety has torsion-free spoke and rim fixed-point divisor-class groups of ranks \(a_n\) and \(2a_n-2+\mathbf1_{\{n\text{ odd}\}}\), where \(a_n\) counts compositions of \(n\) into twos and threes. Its spoke charts are Gorenstein exactly at \(n=4\), while its rim charts have the exceptional Gorenstein indices \(n=4,5,7\); otherwise their canonical classes are not even \(\mathbb Q\)-Cartier.
