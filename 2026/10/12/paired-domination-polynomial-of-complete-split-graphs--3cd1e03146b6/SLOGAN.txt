In the complete split graph \(K_r\vee\overline{K_n}\), a vertex set with \(a\) selected clique vertices and \(b\) selected independent vertices is paired-dominating exactly when \(a\ge1\), \(b\le a\), and \(a+b\) is even. This gives the full paired-domination polynomial, and every inclusion-minimal paired-dominating set is just an edge.
