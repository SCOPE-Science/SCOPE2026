For even reduced symmetric perfect-state-transfer chains, Chebyshev parity cuts the published early-state-exclusion root budget from \(2\lambda_n-n\) to \(\lambda_n-n+\tfrac12\). After the compulsory \(2n-1\)-fold transfer-time zero is removed, the residual return-amplitude polynomial is even, so only half of its remaining degree can support positive-time exclusions.
