For the connected crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M_n\), every perfect dominating set is either a deleted-matching pair, the whole graph, or one of six exceptional four-vertex sets occurring only for \(n=3,4\). Hence \(D_p(\operatorname{Cr}_n;x)=nx^2+x^{2n}\) for \(n\ge5\), with a single extra term \(6x^4\) at \(n=3,4\).
