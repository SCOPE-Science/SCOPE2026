For the binary simplex weak-phase sensing construction with one persistent quantum-memory pair and \(Q=2k\), the exact-message success under a fixed BSC noise level \(p<1/2\) converges to \(e^{-\pi^2p(1-p)}\cos^2(\pi p)>0\), while every same-resource classical-memory QC receiver has success \(O(1/k)\). The separation closes sharply at \(p=1/2\), where the QQ success is exactly random guessing.
