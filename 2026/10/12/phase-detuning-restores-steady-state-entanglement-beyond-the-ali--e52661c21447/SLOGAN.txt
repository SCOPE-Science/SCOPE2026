For two resonantly coupled oscillators with equal independent phase-locked squeezed baths, optimizing the relative bath phase removes the high-squeezing entanglement cutoff present at aligned phase: beyond a finite squeezing threshold the best logarithmic negativity is \(\max\{0,\log_2(\sqrt{1+\mu}/y)\}\), independent of squeezing magnitude. Positive entanglement at very large squeezing requires an exponentially narrowing relative-phase window of width \(\Theta(e^{-2r})\).
