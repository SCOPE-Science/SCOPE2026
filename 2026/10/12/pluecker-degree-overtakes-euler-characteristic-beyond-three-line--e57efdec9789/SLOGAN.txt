For every non-projective complex Grassmannian, the Pluecker degree and topological Euler characteristic are never equal. The degree is smaller only for \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,4)\), \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,5)\), and \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,6)\) and their dual descriptions; everywhere else the number of maximal Schubert chains exceeds the number of Schubert cells.
