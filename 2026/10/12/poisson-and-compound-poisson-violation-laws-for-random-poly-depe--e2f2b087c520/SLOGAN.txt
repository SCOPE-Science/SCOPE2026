Independent Bernoulli sampling of two tables produces an exact generating function for violations of a cross-team poly-dependence atom. At equal limiting mean \(ab\), balanced sparsity gives ordinary \(\operatorname{Poisson}(ab)\) violations while asymmetric sparsity gives a compound-Poisson law with satisfaction probability \(\exp\{-b(1-e^{-a})\}\), strictly larger than \(e^{-ab}\).
