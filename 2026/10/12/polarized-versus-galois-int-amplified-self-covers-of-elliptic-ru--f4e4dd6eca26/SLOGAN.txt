Every decomposable degree-zero elliptic ruled surface \(\mathbf P_E(\mathcal O_E\oplus L)\) has explicit Galois int-amplified self-covers of degree \(m^3\) with deck group \((\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z)^3\), but a polarized self-map exists precisely when \(L\) is torsion. For torsion \(L\), a polarized Galois cover of degree \(m^4\) has deck group \((\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z)^2\times\mathbb Z/m^2\mathbb Z\).
