For a star with arbitrary positive edge prices, the fastest-mixing conductance allocation is uniform exactly when the square roots of the prices satisfy the polygon inequality. If one square-root price exceeds all the others combined, every optimal rate has an explicit nonuniform formula and the best spectral gap is \(1/[\sum_i c_i-\delta^2/(m+1)]\), where \(\delta=(2\max_i\sqrt{c_i}-\sum_i\sqrt{c_i})_+\).
