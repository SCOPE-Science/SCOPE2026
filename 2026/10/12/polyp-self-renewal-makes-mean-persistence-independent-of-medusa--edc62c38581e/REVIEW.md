# Self-review

## Correctness

PASS. A positive stochastic integrating factor converts the polyp SDE and a same-noise stochastic-logistic comparator into random ordinary differential equations. Their difference has zero initial value and nonnegative forcing \(bRM\), so the polyp process dominates the comparator pathwise. The comparator's explicit solution gives its exact long-time mean when \(a-d-\sigma_1^2/2>0\). For the source's weighted total, the linear medusa transfer cancels exactly; an Itô estimate for \(\log W\), together with the comparison lower bound, gives a finite upper time average. These two estimates meet the source's definition of persistence in mean.

## Originality

PASS. Stochastic logistic thresholds and cooperative-population comparison methods are established prior work. The surviving source-specific contribution is that the exact additive stage structure in the jellyfish SDE allows the source's medusa-noise hypothesis to be removed completely from persistence in mean. Searches by exact source identity, stage-specific aliases, logistic comparison, and medusa-noise independence found no published statement of this one-condition result.

## Value

PASS. The source presents small noise in both life stages as a sufficient route to population persistence. The new theorem isolates a robust biological mechanism that the original criterion misses: polyp self-renewal alone can guarantee positive long-time mean population even when medusa noise is arbitrarily large. At the source's own biological rates, \(\sigma_2=1\) violates its medusa condition strongly, while the theorem still gives an exact polyp mean lower bound of \(14480\).

## Closest literature and limitations

General stochastic cooperative-system theory establishes componentwise persistence effects under noise, while stochastic-logistic literature supplies the scalar baseline used in the proof. Those results do not directly contain the additive medusa-to-polyp recruitment equation or the source's weighted total. The deterministic jellyfish predecessor separately motivates polyp-only survival as a biologically meaningful regime.

The result concerns persistence in mean, not two-stage stochastic permanence, and it does not force the medusa time average to remain positive.
