For a symmetric continuous finite-variance random walk, the number \(A_n\) of positive partial sums satisfies \(\operatorname{Cov}(A_n,S_n)=\frac12\sum_{k\le n}\mathbb E|S_k|\), so its correlation with terminal displacement converges universally to \(4/(3\sqrt{\pi})\). The same constant is the exact occupation-time/endpoint correlation for Brownian motion.
