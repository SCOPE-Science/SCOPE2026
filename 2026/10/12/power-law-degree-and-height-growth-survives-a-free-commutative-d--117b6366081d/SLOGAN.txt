A free-commutative direct factor preserves a power-law weighted count and multiplies its leading constant by \(\prod_j(1-e_j^{-\rho})^{-1}\). For Hindes’s explicit elliptic family \(F(n)\times A(m)\) with \(n\ge2\), this proves the degree and Weil-height asymptotics that Remark 1.9 had supported only numerically, with factor \(\prod_j(1-q_j^{-2\rho})^{-1}\).
