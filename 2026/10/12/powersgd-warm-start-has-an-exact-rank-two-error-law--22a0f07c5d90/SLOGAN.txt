For rank-one PowerSGD on a fixed rank-two matrix, the \(t\)-th warm-started compression has exact expected squared-error factor \(1+(\kappa^2-1)/(\kappa^{2t-1}+1)\) relative to the best rank-one approximation. A fresh random start is a factor \(\kappa\) suboptimal, but one reuse already reduces the large-gap excess to \(O(1/\kappa)\).
