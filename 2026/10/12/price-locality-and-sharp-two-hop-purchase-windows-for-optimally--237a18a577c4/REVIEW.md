# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The definition of the Kirchhoff index, normalized shared budget, connected-support boundary, and oriented nested cuts are explicit. Tree series resistances give the base minimizer; a rank-one Laplacian derivative and centered cut-indicator identity give the exact threshold. Global strict convexity and the equality case supply the if-and-only-if and uniqueness, while elementary integer extrema prove the two-hop range. The finite exact-arithmetic checker independently corroborates the critical identities for representative trees but is not an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS, with residual historical uncertainty.** Ghosh–Boyd–Saberi (2008, Sections 2.3, 4.2, 5.1) provide the general convex design and tree baseline; neither their theorem statements nor checked examples state the arbitrary-price tree-cut formula, its off-path-price locality, or the complete two-hop topology extremizers. Those novel explicit consequences require deriving and analyzing the nested-cut covariance. Predari and collaborators (arXiv:2309.08271v1) treat fixed-weight link insertion by greedy methods and therefore do not imply the shared reweighting threshold. Path-only unit-price cases are not counted separately. An earlier equivalent algebraic specialization may exist; the 2008 general KKT makes independent rediscovery particularly plausible.

**Value — PASS.** A design with arbitrary branching and arbitrary positive edge prices admits an exact yes/no new-link purchase decision from path-cut sizes alone, without solving the augmented graph optimization. Counterintuitive independence from off-path prices is exact. The attained finite-order extrema for two-hop shortcuts quantify how topology alone changes what one should pay for the same proposed link, and the sharp path-length bound controls prices for longer links. These are natural network investment comparisons, not sampled tables or numerical gains.

**Limitations.** One optional link only; the installed positive conductance is not explicitly determined. Historical coverage is not exhaustively established, and one contemporary relevant abstract could not be inspected fully. General convexity, derivative formula, and tree optimum are recognized prior art, not claimed discoveries. Independent audit remains unperformed.
