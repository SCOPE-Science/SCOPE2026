For the prime dihedral quandle algebra \(K[R_p]\) in characteristic \(p\), the derivation algebra has dimension \(2(p-1)/\operatorname{lcm}(\operatorname{ord}_p(2),2)\). An explicit basis is indexed by the odd exponents \(t\) with \(2^{t+1}\equiv1\pmod p\), so modular derivations are controlled exactly by the arithmetic of \(2\) modulo \(p\).
