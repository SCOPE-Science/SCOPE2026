# Scientific comparison and limitations

## Correctness — PASS

The counting inequalities are exact for every integer \(n\ge2\): forbidden subsets necessarily consist of multiples of their gcd, and subsets of multiples of the least forbidden gcd are always forbidden. The additional unit in each deficit accounts for the empty subset. The log error is bounded uniformly, the residue-class fibers are exact, and truncation of their density tails proves a genuine probability limit. The two parity bounds follow globally. The standalone integer checker confirms the finite formulas without being used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

Pongsriiam's 2013 paper defines the functions and poses the extremal-ratio question explicitly (Question 3.3). Its partial sums and the exact gcd interpretation do not state the new atom-and-weight limit law. Pongsriiam's later Theorem 9 establishes sign alternation of the difference but neither exponential separation nor the joint prime-power/prime law. Łydka's 2019 treatment concerns log-inequalities for different subset-count functions. OEIS A224840 provides sample values and definitions rather than the distribution theorem. These are mathematical non-implication comparisons of the stated results; unindexed elementary derivations remain a risk.

## Value — PASS

The exact limiting measure gives a complete quantitative description of the logarithmic scale of an explicitly posed arithmetic-subset ratio, including all possible atoms, their arithmetic densities, and a sharp parity-level divergence dichotomy. It applies to every input size and does not rely on an arbitrarily selected finite slice. It does not settle separate summatory questions for the unscaled ratio.
