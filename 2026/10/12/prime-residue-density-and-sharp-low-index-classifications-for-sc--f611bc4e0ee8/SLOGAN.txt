For every fixed even Schemmel parameter \(m\), sufficiently large prime bases whose every positive prime power is \(D_m\)-abundant have exact relative prime density \(\prod_{q\le m,\,q\nmid m}(q-2)/(q-1)\). At \(m=2\) and \(m=4\), abundance is completely classified with no exceptions: precisely \(p\ge7\), and respectively \(p\ge17\) with \(p\equiv2\pmod3\), for every positive exponent.
