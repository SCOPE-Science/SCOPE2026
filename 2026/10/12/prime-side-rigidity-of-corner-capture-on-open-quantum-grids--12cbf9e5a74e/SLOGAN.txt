A single corner absorber on the open quantum grid \(P_{p-1}\square P_{q-1}\), for odd primes \(p,q\), captures every state when \(p\ne q\), but leaves an exactly \((p-1)^2/2-1\)-dimensional dark space when \(p=q\). The incoherent-state capture probability drops sharply from \(1\) to \(1/2+1/(p-1)^2\), a cyclotomic spectral-collision effect rather than a dependence on absorber strength.
