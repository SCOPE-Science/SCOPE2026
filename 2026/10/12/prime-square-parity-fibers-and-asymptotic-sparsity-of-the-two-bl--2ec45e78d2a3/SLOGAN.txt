The signed two-block odd partition coefficients are odd exactly on two explicit prime-factorization families: odd-square parts governed by the quadratic character \(\chi_8\), and odd nonsquares with one distinguished prime \(p\equiv3,5\pmod8\) to exponent \(1\pmod4\). The number of odd coefficients up to \(X\) is asymptotic to \(\pi^2X/(8\log X)\), despite their natural density being zero.
