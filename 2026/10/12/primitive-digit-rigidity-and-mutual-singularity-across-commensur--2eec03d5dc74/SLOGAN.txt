For distinct multiplicatively dependent integer bases \(q=b^m\) and \(r=b^n\) with \(\gcd(m,n)=1\), two strictly positive iid digit-expansion laws are either identical or mutually singular. They coincide exactly when both dice are groupings of a single iid \(b\)-digit law; incompatible laws are singular even though both have full support on \([0,1]\).
