## Correctness

**PASS.** The source's Theorem 11.4 says exactly that principal finite types are the types whose realizations lie on one piecewise segment from the distinguished point \(p\). On such a segment, the ordered radial coordinates \(t_i=d(p,a_i)\) determine every pairwise distance by \(d(a_i,a_j)=|t_i-t_j|\). Quantifier elimination and the source's pseudometric description therefore give bijectivity of radial coordinates. The lower metric bound follows from the triangle inequality, and the upper bound is attained by realizing both tuples on the same ray. Hence the principal locus is exactly \(([0,r]^n,\|\cdot\|_\infty)\).

The density statement is also sound. Lemma 8.7 gives the separable one-type interval, while Proposition 8.8 gives continuum metric density in arity \(2\). Repeating one coordinate is an isometric embedding from \(S_2(T)\) into every \(S_n(T)\) with \(n\ge2\), and the finite-pseudometric description of types gives the matching continuum cardinality upper bound. Compactness of the principal cube then forces the complement to retain continuum density.
