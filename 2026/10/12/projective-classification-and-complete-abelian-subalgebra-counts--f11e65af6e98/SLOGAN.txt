For the seven-dimensional Saha–Towers class-three algebra, every maximal abelian subalgebra has dimension \(4\); they consist of a single distinguished ideal and a line-bundle family over \(\mathbf P^2\). Over each odd finite field the entire distribution is \(A_d(q)={4\brack d}_q+q(q^2+q+1)({4\brack d}_q-{3\brack d}_q)\).
