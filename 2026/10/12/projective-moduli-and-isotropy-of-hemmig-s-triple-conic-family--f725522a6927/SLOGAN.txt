Hemmig's auxiliary triple of conics has a complete one-parameter projective moduli description: the parameter is identified by six anharmonic transforms, equivalently by \(J(\lambda)=256(\lambda^2+\lambda+1)^3/(\lambda^2(\lambda+1)^2)\). Its unordered projective stabilizer is generically trivial, jumps to \(C_2\) at \(\lambda\in\{1,-1/2,-2\}\), and to \(C_3\) at \(\lambda^2+\lambda+1=0\).
