The free degree-eight union of a smooth cyclic quartic and its four concurrent hyperflex tangents has complete projective moduli given by the cross-ratio \(j\)-line. Its full projective stabilizer jumps from order \(16\) to \(32\) or \(48\) at the harmonic or equianharmonic values, and the harmonic marked union retains only one third of the Fermat quartic's automorphisms.
