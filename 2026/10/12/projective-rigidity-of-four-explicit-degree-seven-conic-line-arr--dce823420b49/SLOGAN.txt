The four explicit degree-seven conic-line representatives of Amram--Bannai--Shirane--Sinichkin--Tokunaga are four distinct \(\operatorname{PGL}_3(\mathbb C)\)-orbits, and each has trivial projective automorphism group. Their common conic pair has stabilizer \(V_4\), but adjoining the distinguished weak-contact conic kills every nontrivial symmetry.
