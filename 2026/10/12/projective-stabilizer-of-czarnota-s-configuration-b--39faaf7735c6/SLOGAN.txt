Czarnota's Configuration B has exactly four projective symmetries: independent sign changes of \(x\) and \(y\), so \(\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathbb P^2}(A)\cong C_2\times C_2\). The unique secant line fixes one pair of points on the conic and cuts the conic-point symmetry down to the four Möbius maps \(t,-t,t^{-1},-t^{-1}\).
