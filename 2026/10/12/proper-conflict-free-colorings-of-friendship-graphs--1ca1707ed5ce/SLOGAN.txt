A proper coloring of the friendship graph \(F_k=K_1\vee kK_2\) is proper conflict-free exactly when some leaf color is used once. Hence \(\chi_{\mathrm{pcf}}(F_k)=4\) for every \(k\ge2\), every color count from \(4\) through \(2k+1\) occurs, and the number of labeled minimum colorings is \(96\) for \(k=2\) and \(24k\,2^k\) for \(k\ge3\).
