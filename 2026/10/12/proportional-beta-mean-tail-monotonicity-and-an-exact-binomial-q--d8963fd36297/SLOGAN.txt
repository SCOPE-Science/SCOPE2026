For beta distributions whose two shape parameters grow proportionally, the probability of falling below the fixed mean moves strictly toward one half. This resolves the exact binomial-tail threshold conjecture of Babichenko, Feldman, Holzman, and Narayan: for every integer \(n\ge2\), the infimum over \(t\ge1\) of \(\Pr\{\operatorname{Bin}(nt-1,1/n)<t\}\) equals \((1-1/n)^{n-1}\), attained at \(t=1\).
