Every extended boundary sequence of the aperiodic period-doubling word is **purely periodic**: at window width \(\ell\), shifting the separation by \(2^{1+\lceil\log_2\ell\rceil}\) leaves the complete set of endpoint pairs unchanged. A two-hole valuation argument also gives an exact finite construction and at most \(4\cdot2^{\lceil\log_2\ell\rceil}\) pairs per boundary set.
