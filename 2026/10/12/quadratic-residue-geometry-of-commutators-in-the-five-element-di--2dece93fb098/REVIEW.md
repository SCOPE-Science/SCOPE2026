# Review: quadratic-residue geometry of dihedral-quandle commutators

## Correctness — PASS
The characteristic-five truncated-logarithm is an invertible basis change for the cyclic group algebra, and it sends the quandle product to \(U(-t)V(2t)\). Coefficient calculation gives the stated five-dimensional alternating bilinear map. Its image is the projection of decomposable \(2\)-vectors in a four-space, characterized *exactly* by the single Plücker equation, including the zero case. Eliminating free minors gives the square-discriminant criterion without assumptions on finite cardinality. The direct commutators spanning the augmentation ideal, sharp width, and finite-field counting are then algebraic deductions. The bundled verifier checks all prime-field pairs and independently checks the extension-field arithmetic; finite checks do not prove the general case. No unresolved proof step has been identified.

## Originality — PASS (historical residual risk)
Sangare–Ta's theorem and ensuing text contain the base \(\mathbb F_5\) width and count of 40, which this claim acknowledges as prior. They test a finite list of coefficient fields and do not give the pointwise quadratic-form criterion, all extension degrees, or arbitrary-field width criterion. Bardakov–Passi–Singh's earlier width-one results do not imply them. Related recently posted group-algebra work by Zhang describes an ideal/subalgebra, not necessarily the set of single additive commutators; its full text was not available, so a statement-equivalence risk persists. Targeted published-result and bibliographic comparisons revealed no covering formulation, but absence from search is not proof of global uniqueness.

## Value — PASS
The first known width-two dihedral-quandle example had only an enumerative explanation at the ground field. The new theorem gives an explicit *membership criterion* for the commutator image over every characteristic-five field, explains its exceptional locus as an arithmetic square obstruction arising from the Plücker relation, and computes the exact size of that locus over every finite extension. The dependence on the field's square classes shows a meaningful algebraic boundary, not a routine restatement of the known 40-element enumeration.

## Limitations
The proof assumes coefficient characteristic five and quandle order five, and makes no claim of an all-characteristics classification. The Zhang full-text comparison remains unresolved; a stronger or equivalent theorem in an uninspected source would affect originality. Independent validation has not been performed.
