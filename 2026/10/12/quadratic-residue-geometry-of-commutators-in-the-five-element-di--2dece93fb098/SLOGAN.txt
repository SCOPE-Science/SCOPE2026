For the five-element dihedral quandle algebra in characteristic \(5\), an augmentation vector is one additive commutator exactly when its linear truncated-logarithm coefficient is nonzero or an explicit binary quadratic discriminant is a square. Over \(\mathbb F_q\), \(q=5^e\), precisely \(q(q-1)^2/2\) vectors require two commutators; the criterion holds over arbitrary characteristic-five fields.
