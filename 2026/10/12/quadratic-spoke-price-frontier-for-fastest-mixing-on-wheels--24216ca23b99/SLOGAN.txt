For a continuous-time wheel network with independently chosen edge conductances and a unit price budget, the unique fastest-mixing design deletes every rim edge when its spoke-price ratio is below \(1/[4\sin^2(\pi/n)]\), but retains the entire rim above that price. At equality there is exactly one interval of symmetric optimal allocations, with no asymmetric extremizers, and the transition price grows quadratically with the number of rim vertices.
