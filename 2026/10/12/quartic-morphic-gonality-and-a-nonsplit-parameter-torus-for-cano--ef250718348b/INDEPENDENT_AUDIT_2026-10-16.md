# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Assume exactly the stated canonical one-nonsplit-node Gorenstein curves over odd F_q with hyperelliptic normalization y²=f(x), genus g>=4, and a rational nonsplit node with two conjugate preimages over k2 whose distinct x-coordinates are alpha,alpha^q and f(alpha)≠0. For a nonconstant morphism C'->P1 of degree d<=4, the induced rational function h on smooth normalization has degree d. If h∉k(x), the (x,h) map to P1×P1 is birational onto its image (since [k(C):k(x)]=2); that image has bidegree (2,d) and arithmetic genus (2−1)(d−1)=d−1<=3, which cannot have smooth normalization of genus g>=4. Hence h=R(x) and d=2 deg R; degrees 1,3 are impossible. At the node, a morphism descends exactly when values on two conjugate branches are equal and k-rational (including infinity); Möbius degree-one R cannot identify alpha and alpha^q, giving no degree2. For degree2 the rational quadratic pencil must have a section vanishing on both conjugate points, i.e. phi=(x−alpha)(x−alpha^q), and a complementary linear section a+bx. All such pencils are base-point free over geometric closure because phi is irreducible over k and a+bx has no nonzero root at alpha; there are q+1 distinct [a:b]. Their quotient F_{q²}×/F_q× identifies the parameter group with the nonsplit torus of the generalized Jacobian normalization sequence. Independent arithmetic in finite field F3 confirms phi=x²+1 is irreducible and f=x^9−x+1 has squarefree derivative −1 and f(alpha)=1, yielding genus4 example and q+1=4 pencil classes; the genus/descent proofs, not these finite checks, give the theorem.

## originality

PASS

The near-exact Hilario–Salomão–Martins preprint arXiv:2609.22557 constructs singular curves with nonsplit nodes and claims to determine gonality of its curves; its abstract and bibliographic record were inspected, but full text was inaccessible through attempted arXiv HTML/PDF, OA mirrors, and authorized institutional access. Its word 'gonality' may denote generalized trigonal linear series of degree three with a non-removable base point, distinct from a base-point-free finite morphism, as indicated by Rosa–Stöhr; nevertheless the preprint's claimed determination of gonality is a serious residual risk, and whole-document noncoverage is NOT asserted. The assessed result distinguishes degree4 morphic gonality, classifies all minimal morphisms and exhibits q+1 nonsplit-torus classes, features not explicitly present in the accessible abstract. Best-of-knowledge originality PASS is conditional on this precise recorded literature risk, not a proof by failed search.

## value

PASS

The distinction between torsion-free generalized trigonal pencils and actual finite maps on singular curves is a natural geometric question with different numerical answers. Here degree4 minimality, enumeration of every degree4 morphism modulo target automorphisms, and generalized-Jacobian torsor structure provide a complete intrinsic geometric invariant across a naturally constructed family; q+1 is not an arbitrary finite cutoff. It complements rather than simply renames the source’s gonality-three pencil. No claim is made for all possible singular hyperelliptic curves, genus<4, split nodes, or characteristic two.
