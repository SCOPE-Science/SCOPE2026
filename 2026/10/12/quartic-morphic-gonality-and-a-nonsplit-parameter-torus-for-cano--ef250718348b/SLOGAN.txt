For the canonical one-nonsplit-node Gorenstein curves of Hilario–Salomão–Martins with normalization genus \(g\ge4\), the least degree of a genuine finite map to \(\mathbb P^1\) is \(4\), despite the generalized trigonal pencil. All minimal covers up to target coordinates form the nonsplit torus \((\operatorname{Res}_{\mathbb F_{q^2}/\mathbb F_q}\mathbb G_m)/\mathbb G_m\), giving exactly \(q+1\) classes.
