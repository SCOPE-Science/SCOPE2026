On the symmetric two-sample quadratic, constant-step random reshuffling has an exact epoch-scale Bernoulli-convolution stationary law. Its support avoids the true minimizer on the sharp band \(1-1/\sqrt2<h<1+1/\sqrt2\), becoming Cantor-like except at the exact-local-solve point, while at the two boundary steps the invariant law is exactly uniform on an interval.
