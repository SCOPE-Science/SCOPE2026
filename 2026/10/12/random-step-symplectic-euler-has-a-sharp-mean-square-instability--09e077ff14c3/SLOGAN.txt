Randomizing individually stable symplectic Euler steps for the harmonic oscillator produces **exponential mean-square energy growth for every nonconstant independent step law** supported in \((0,2)\); constant steps are bounded. The exact growth factor is the spectral radius of a three-dimensional covariance recurrence determined by four step moments, and its small symmetric-noise correction is \(2h^2\varepsilon^2/(4-h^2)+O_h(\varepsilon^4)\).
