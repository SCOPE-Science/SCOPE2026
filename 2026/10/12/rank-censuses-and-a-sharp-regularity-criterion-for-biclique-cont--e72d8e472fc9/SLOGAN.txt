In the Margolis–Rhodes monoid of a complete bipartite graph \(K_{m,n}\) with \(m,n\ge2\), nonregularity occurs exactly when the image lies on one side but forced singleton-fiber representatives occupy both source sides. This criterion yields closed rank-generating polynomials for every map, every nonregular map, and every idempotent, with a sharp nonregular rank cutoff at \(\max(m,n)\).
