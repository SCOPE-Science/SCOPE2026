For every finite Heisenberg Lie algebra \(H_m(\mathbb F_q)\), the complete endomorphism-rank distribution is an explicit symplectic-product polynomial; all proper ranks lie between \(0\) and \(m+1\), with a gap before the full rank \(2m+1\). As the field order grows with \(m\) fixed, the fraction of endomorphisms that are automorphisms approaches \(1\), \(1/2\), or \(0\) according as \(m=1\), \(m=2\), or \(m\ge3\).
