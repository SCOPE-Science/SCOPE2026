# Self-review

## Correctness

PASS. Choosing one outside element as a baseline and conjugating it into the complement converts every remaining generator into one kernel vector after cancelling its quotient exponent. Since \(\mathbf F_p(\alpha)=\mathbf F_q\), generation is exactly \(\mathbf F_q\)-spanning. This proves \(d(G)=t+1\) and makes pair extension explicit. Kernel pairs are adjacent exactly when linearly independent; every nonzero kernel element is adjacent to every outside element; outside pairs are adjacent exactly when they lie in distinct conjugate complements. The multipartite-join formula and degree counts follow. The packaged checker reconstructs the \(48\)-element case directly from all three-element generating sets.

## Originality

PASS. Lucchini's 2020 paper explicitly asks whether element order divides degree in \(\Gamma_{d(G)}(G)\) and notes that the \(d(G)=2\) case is already known. The later rank-graph theorem proves connectivity for \(d(G)\ge3\), not degrees. Database searches found a nilpotent-family theorem and the complement-\(2\) generalized-dihedral boundary, but no odd-prime-complement scalar Frobenius formula. Targeted searches under equivalent graph and module formulations found no covering theorem. A specialized older generation paper using different terminology remains the main bibliographic risk.

## Value

PASS. The family is nonnilpotent and has unbounded minimum generator number, so it tests the motivating question in a genuinely different regime. The theorem gives the complete graph structure and two closed degree strata rather than a yes/no divisibility statement.
