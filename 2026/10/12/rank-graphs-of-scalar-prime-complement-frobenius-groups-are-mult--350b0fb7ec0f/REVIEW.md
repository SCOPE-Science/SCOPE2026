# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

With e=ord_ℓ(p), α∈F_{p^e} of prime order ℓ and t≥2, an outside generator normalizes to h^a. Multiplying the other generators by suitable powers cancels their quotient exponents and extracts t vectors in the homogeneous irreducible F_q-module V; generation iff those vectors span F_q^t. Thus d(G)=t+1. Two nonzero kernel elements are adjacent iff independent; kernel/outside pairs always adjacent; two outside elements iff their conjugate-complement labels differ. Therefore the graph is the join of stated complete multipartite factors, and degrees are ℓq^t−q for nonzero kernel, ℓ(q^t−1) outside, zero for identity. Orders p/ℓ respectively divide these degrees. Independently enumerated all 3-generator triples in F4²⋊C3, obtaining degrees 0^1,44^15,45^32.
