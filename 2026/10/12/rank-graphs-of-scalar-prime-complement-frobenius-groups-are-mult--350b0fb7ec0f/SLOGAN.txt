For \(G=\mathbf F_q^t\rtimes C_\ell\) with odd prime \(\ell\), irreducible scalar Frobenius action, and \(t\ge2\), the minimum-size independence graph is the join of a projective-line multipartite graph on the kernel with a conjugate-complement multipartite graph off the kernel. Its two nonzero degrees are \(\ell q^t-q\) and \(\ell(q^t-1)\), so Lucchini's order-divides-degree question holds on a nonnilpotent family with unbounded \(d(G)=t+1\).
