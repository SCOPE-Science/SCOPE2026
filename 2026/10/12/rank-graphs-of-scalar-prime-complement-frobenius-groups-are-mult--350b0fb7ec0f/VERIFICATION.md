The proof is symbolic.

The packaged checker `artifacts/verify.py` constructs
\[
G=\mathbf F_4^2\rtimes C_3
\]
using
\[
\mathbf F_4=\mathbf F_2[\omega]/(\omega^2+\omega+1).
\]
It enumerates all \(\binom{48}{3}\) three-element subsets. For each triple it computes the subgroup generated by the triple from the Cayley multiplication table. The checker then marks every pair occurring in a generating triple, thereby reconstructing \(\Gamma_3(G)\) directly from the defining extension condition.

It verifies the degree histogram
\[
0^1,\qquad44^{15},\qquad45^{32},
\]
and checks the predicted adjacency condition on all unordered pairs. It also verifies five kernel projective-line parts of size \(3\) and sixteen conjugate-complement parts of size \(2\).

The checker returns `VERIFY_OK`.

Finite computation is not used to prove the infinite family.
