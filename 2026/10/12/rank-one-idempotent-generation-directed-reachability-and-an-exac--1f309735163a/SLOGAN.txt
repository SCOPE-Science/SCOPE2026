An ordered \(n\)-tuple of rank-one idempotents generates the full \(n\times n\) matrix algebra precisely when its overlap matrix is invertible and its nonzero-entry digraph is strongly connected; the sharp minimum is \(n\) for \(n\ge3\), with a unique two-dimensional binary-field exception. For \(M_3(\mathbb F_q)\), the exact number of ordered generating triples is \(\frac{|\operatorname{GL}_3(\mathbb F_q)|}{q-1}(q^4+q^3-6q^2+2q+5)\).
