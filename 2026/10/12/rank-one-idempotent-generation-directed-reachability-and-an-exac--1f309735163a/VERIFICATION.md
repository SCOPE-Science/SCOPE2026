# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Dimension count for a unital algebra generated by n rank-one idempotents forces both factor matrices U,V invertible: when image span or dual row span has dimension <n, positive words span at most n(n-1) dimensional space; adjoining 1 cannot reach n^2. After U-conjugation P_i=e_i c_i^T and words are scalar multiples of e_i c_j^T along directed C-support walks. Because C^-1 is a polynomial in C, it has zero entries on unreachable pairs and the identity is already in the reachable span. Hence generation is equivalent to strong connectivity. The n=3 seed det=-1 and Schur-complement extension prove n>=3 existence, n=2 field obstruction ab!=1. Symbolic subspace Möbius count plus 63-cut inclusion-exclusion yields S3(q)=(q-1)^2(q^4+q^3-6q^2+2q+5); divide by (q-1)^3 factor rescalings to obtain G3(q). Independent normalized enumeration gave q=2:9, q=3:260, q=5:9840; these are finite checks, not an infinite proof.

## originality

PASS

The rank-one-constrained minimal generator number, directed reachability criterion for n-tuple idempotents, and exact three-dimensional count over every finite field were not implied by the inspected literature. Petrenko–Sidki analyze unconstrained matrix pairs and special rank-one projector paired with a full-rank shift, while Kravchenko–Mazur–Petrenko count unrestricted tuples, whose generating probability cannot be conditioned to rank-one idempotents by substitution. Krupnik (1993) is a plausible predecessor on arbitrary idempotent generators; full text unavailable after lawful OA search and an authorized institutional attempt returned vpn_disconnected. This limitation is a real priority risk, not evidence of coverage or automatic failure.
