For \(f(X)=X^3-X^2+c_1X+c_0\) over \(\mathbb F_q\), the exact number of rank-one idempotents \(E\) with \(C_f-E\) nilpotent is a polynomial-Euler-totient term plus two explicit corrections supported at \(X\mid f\) and \(f=X^2(X-1)\). This turns qualitative nonuniqueness of cubic nil-clean companion decompositions into a factorization-sensitive closed formula.
