Every rank-one weight-zero Rota–Baxter operator on a nondegenerate spin-factor Jordan algebra is a square-zero map parameterized by a point on a smooth projective quadric and a nonzero annihilating functional. Over odd finite fields, the rank-one count is \((q^{n-1}-1)\) times the quadric's point count, with a discriminant-sensitive correction exactly when \(n\) is odd.
