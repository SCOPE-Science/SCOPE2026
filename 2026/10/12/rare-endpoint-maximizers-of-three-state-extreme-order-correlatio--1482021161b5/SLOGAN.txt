Among symmetric three-point laws, the largest Pearson correlation between the sample minimum and maximum is asymptotically \(c_*(2-c_*)/n\), where \((2-c_*)e^{c_*}=2\) and \(c_*\simeq1.593624\). Every maximizing endpoint probability is asymptotic to \(c_*/n\), in sharp contrast with uniform three-point probabilities, whose extreme-order correlation decays exponentially.
