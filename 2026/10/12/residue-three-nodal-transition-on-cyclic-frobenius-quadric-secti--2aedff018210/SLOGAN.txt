For the cyclic Frobenius surface of degree \(p+1\) intersected with its canonical split quadric, the curve is smooth precisely when \(p\equiv2\pmod3\); for \(p\equiv1\pmod3\) it has exactly \(4p\) ordinary nodes, four rational. Its normalization has \((p+1)^2\) rational points in the smooth case and \((p+1)^2+4\) in the nodal case.
