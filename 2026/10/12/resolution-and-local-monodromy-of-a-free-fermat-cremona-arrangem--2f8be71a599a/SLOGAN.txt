For the free degree-\(4m\) Fermat–Cremona union with two added tangent-line pencils, each of its two nonquasihomogeneous vertices has \(m\) smooth branch pairs of contact \(m+1\), a resolution with \(m\) length-\(m\) tails, and local first-homology monodromy polynomial \((1-t)(1-t^{2m})^{m-2}(1-t^{4m})^m\) for every \(m\ge2\). The complete singularity basket has a global Milnor–Tjurina defect of \(2(m-1)^2\).
