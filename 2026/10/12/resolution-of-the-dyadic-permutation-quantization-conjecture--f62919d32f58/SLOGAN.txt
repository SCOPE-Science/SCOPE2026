For every allowed permutation of the initial dyadic masses, any optimal \(n\)-point quadratic quantizer with \(n\ge k+2\) must isolate \(1,2,\ldots,n-3\) as singleton Voronoi cells. A uniform adjacent-pair distortion floor therefore proves Conjecture 1.5 of arXiv:2305.02372v1 and makes its conditional tail classification unconditional.
