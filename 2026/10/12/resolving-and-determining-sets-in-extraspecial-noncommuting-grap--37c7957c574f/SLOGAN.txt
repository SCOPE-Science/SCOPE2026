For an extraspecial \(p\)-group of order \(p^{1+2n}\), resolving sets and determining sets in the noncommuting graph are exactly the sets containing all but at most one vertex from each projective commutator twin class. Consequently both minimum sizes equal \(\frac{p^{2n}-1}{p-1}(p(p-1)-1)\), and both cardinality enumerators equal \((p(p-1)x^{p(p-1)-1}+x^{p(p-1)})^{(p^{2n}-1)/(p-1)}\).
