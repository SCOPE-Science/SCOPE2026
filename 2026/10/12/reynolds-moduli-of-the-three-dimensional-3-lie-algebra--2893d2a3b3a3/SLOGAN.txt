For the canonical three-dimensional \(3\)-Lie algebra \([e_1,e_2,e_3]=e_1\), the Reynolds identity collapses to \(\det(R)e_1=(\sigma_2(R)-\det(R))R(e_1)\), yielding a complete characteristic-free classification. Over \(\mathbf F_q\) there are \(q^7+3q^6-3q^5-2q^4+2q^3\) Reynolds operators, including exactly \(q^3(q-1)(q^2-q-1)\) invertible ones.
