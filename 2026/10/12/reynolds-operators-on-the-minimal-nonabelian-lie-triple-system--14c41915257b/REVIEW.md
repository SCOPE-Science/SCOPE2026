## Correctness

PASS. The complete Reynolds defect on the two-dimensional Lie triple system reduces to four polynomial equations. The \(c=0\) case is exactly \(a^2d(d-2)=0\); the \(c\ne0\) case forces both trace and determinant to vanish and hence \(R^2=0\). Direct substitution proves the converses. Inclusion--exclusion and determinant tests give the stated finite-field total and rank profile. The packaged checker exhaustively replays every matrix over four odd prime fields.

PASS. The 2023 source supplies the Reynolds identity but no two-dimensional census. At weight \(-1\), the complete calculation shows that this known plane is only one of four structural pieces. Targeted searches found no complete decomposition or exact Reynolds-Lie-triple finite-field count. A differently indexed low-dimensional calculation remains a residual risk.
