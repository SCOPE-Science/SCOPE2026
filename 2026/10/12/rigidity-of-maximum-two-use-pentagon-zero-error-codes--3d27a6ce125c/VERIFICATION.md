# Verification

`verify.py` reconstructs the full strong square of the five-cycle on \(25\) vertices from the coordinatewise confusability rule. It enumerates every \(5\)-subset and \(6\)-subset, checks independence directly, and verifies that the maximum is \(5\), with exactly \(10\) maximum sets.

It then constructs the ten claimed affine transversals \(\{(i,a\pm2i)\}\) and checks exact set equality with the exhaustive census. Finally it enumerates the natural group generated by a dihedral isometry in each coordinate and coordinate swap, obtaining group order \(200\), one orbit of the ten maxima, and stabilizer order \(20\).

Expected terminal output:

`VERIFY_OK maximum=5 labeled_maxima=10 natural_orbits=1 orbit_size=10 stabilizer=20`

The finite replay does not replace the proof. The proof supplies the structural necessity of the affine forms; the script checks the entire finite instance and the stated orbit computation. No claim is made about higher block lengths.
