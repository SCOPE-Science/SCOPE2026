The Roman-domination polynomial of every sunlet graph \(C_n\circ K_1\), for \(n\ge3\), has an explicit cubic-denominator generating function and a positive third-order polynomial recurrence. Its minimum-weight Roman functions are counted exactly by \(3\), \((3k+1)(k+6)/2\), or \(3k+2\), according as \(n\) is \(3k\), \(3k+1\), or \(3k+2\); the minimum weight itself was previously known.
