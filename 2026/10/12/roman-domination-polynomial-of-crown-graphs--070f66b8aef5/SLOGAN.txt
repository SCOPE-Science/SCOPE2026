For every connected crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M_n\) with \(n\ge3\), Roman dominating functions admit an exact seven-regime decomposition by the numbers of label-\(2\) vertices on the two sides, yielding a closed Roman domination polynomial. The minimum weight is \(4\), attained by exactly the \(n\) functions that place label \(2\) on one deleted-matching pair and \(0\) elsewhere.
