For \(K_{m,n}\) with any matching of edges deleted, every initially one-cup target has an exact stackability criterion: a vertex on the \(m\)-side succeeds precisely when \(m\le n+1\), with one extra unit of imbalance allowed exactly at endpoints of deleted edges. At imbalance two, deleting a single edge creates a new successful target although the graph as a whole remains non-stackable.
