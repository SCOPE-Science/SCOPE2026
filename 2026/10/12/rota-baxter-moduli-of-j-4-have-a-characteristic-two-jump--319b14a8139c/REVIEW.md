Over finite q, counts q² odd and 2q²−q even, orbit types respectively three and q+2. Independent direct basis-law and conjugation enumeration over GF2, GF4, GF3 and GF5 corroborated all counts; arbitrary-field proof is symbolic, not prime-field extrapolation.
