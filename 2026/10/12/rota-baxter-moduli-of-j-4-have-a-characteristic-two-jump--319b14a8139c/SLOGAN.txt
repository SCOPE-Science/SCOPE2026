For the two-dimensional Jordan algebra \(J_4\), weight-zero Rota–Baxter operators are exactly square-zero endomorphisms away from characteristic \(2\), and they collapse to only three automorphism orbits. Characteristic \(2\) creates a new \(K^\times\)-family of orbit types, giving \(q+2\) classes over \(\mathbf F_q\) instead of three.
