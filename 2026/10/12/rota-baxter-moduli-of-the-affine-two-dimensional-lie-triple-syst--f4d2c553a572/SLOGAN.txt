The affine two-dimensional Lie triple system has \(3q^2-2q\) weight-zero Rota–Baxter operators over every odd \(\mathbf F_q\), all nonzero operators have rank \(1\), and they form exactly \(2q+1\) automorphism classes. One entire affine-plane branch is genuinely ternary: it is absent from the Rota–Baxter operators of the inducing affine Lie algebra.
