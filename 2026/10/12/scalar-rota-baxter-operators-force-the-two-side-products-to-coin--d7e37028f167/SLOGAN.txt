For the cross-coupled Rota–Baxter definition used in arXiv:2304.06781, the scalar map \(R=-\lambda I\) works at nonzero weight exactly when the two side products coincide. The paper's Example 2.10 has \(e_2\dashv e_1=e_1\) but \(e_2\vdash e_1=0\), so its displayed scalar operator fails for every nonzero \(\lambda\).
