A set in the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n\) is secure total dominating exactly when each bipartition side contributes at least two vertices and every nonfull opposite side contributes at least three. This yields the complete enumerator \(\operatorname{ST}_n(x)=\left(\sum_{k=3}^{n}\binom{n}{k}x^k\right)^2+2\binom{n}{2}x^{n+2}\), and in particular \(\gamma_{st}(\operatorname{Cr}_n)=6\) for every \(n\ge4\).\n
