For a faithful finite semisimple module \(M=\bigoplus_i\mathbb F_{q_i}^{d_i}\) over a product of fields, its full-spectrum Zariski topology-graph remembers exactly the multiset of coordinate proper-subspace counts \(a_i\), and nothing finer. Its vertices form false-twin support cells with disjointness adjacency, which yields the full automorphism group and explicit nonisomorphic modules having the same graph.
