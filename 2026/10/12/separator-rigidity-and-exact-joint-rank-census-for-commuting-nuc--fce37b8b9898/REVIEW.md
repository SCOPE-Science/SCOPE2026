# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The classification is proved from fixed-point ceiling maps and a strict witness to noncommutation when both exclusive kinds occur within a common-fixed-point interval. The block decomposition yields an exact coefficient count \(R_{n,k}\) and closed-form total \(c_n\), while finite-meet preservation establishes product factorization. All 349,525 ordered pairs for chains through ten points, every joint-rank profile, independent low-order nucleus classifications, and small direct products were checked. Exhaustive finite verification does not by itself establish the unrestricted theorem.

## Originality — PASS, with explicit historical limitations
Cīrulis (2020), Proposition 3.5, gives a generic commutation criterion in terms of both composites being modal, while Erné (2022), Lemma 3.6, proves general composition implications; Shallit–Willard (2011) consider different closure/complement monoids. Their inspected statements do not imply the separator obstruction and exact joint-rank profile. OEIS A007070 already lists the same total-count sequence and recurrence, which are **not claimed to be new in isolation**. Searches of published results and relevant indexed records did not find the particular realization, but an implicit earlier equivalent result remains possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This resolves a natural compatibility and joint-quotient census for finite Gödel-modal algebras, with a finite-word rigidity criterion, complete image-rank distribution, asymptotically vanishing chance of commutation, and extension to finite products. These structural and rank-refined statements provide information absent from the plain OEIS list.

## Limits and closest evidence
The proof is restricted to finite nonempty chains and finite direct products; it does not characterize nuclei on all finite Heyting algebras. The computational verifier is finite and an independent historical/mathematical audit has not been performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
