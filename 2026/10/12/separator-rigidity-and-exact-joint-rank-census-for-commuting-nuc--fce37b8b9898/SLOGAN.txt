Two nuclei on a finite Gödel chain commute exactly when no gap between consecutive common fixed points contains both kinds of private fixed points; their composite is the nucleus fixed on the intersection. The full ordered-pair count is \(c_n=((2+\sqrt2)^n-(2-\sqrt2)^n)/(2\sqrt2)\), with a coefficient formula for every composite-image rank and multiplicative extension to products of chains.
