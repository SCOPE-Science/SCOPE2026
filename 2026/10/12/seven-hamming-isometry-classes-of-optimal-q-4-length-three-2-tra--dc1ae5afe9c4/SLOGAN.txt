At the last alphabet where the exact length-three \(2\)-traceability maximum remains \(q\), the optimum is structurally nonunique: \(q=4\) has \(27{,}696\) literal maxima in exactly seven Hamming-isometry classes. Representatives range from minimum distance \(1\) to the diagonal minimum-distance-\(3\) code, so optimal traceability does not force large Hamming distance.
