The complete expected rooted phylogenetic-diversity rarefaction curve determines the unlabeled topology of every positive-edge binary tree through six species, but the sharp threshold is seven—even among strictly ultrametric trees with integer branch lengths. Two explicit seven-tip trees have the same full mean curve and different two-tip variances, \(128/147\) and \(142/147\), and the ambiguity persists at every larger number of tips.
