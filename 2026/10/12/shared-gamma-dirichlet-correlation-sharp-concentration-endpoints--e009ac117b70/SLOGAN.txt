For equal Dirichlet marginals generated with shared Gamma fractions \(s\), the cross-correlation is strictly below \(s\) at every finite concentration and converges to \(s/(2-s)\), rather than \(s\), in the low-concentration limit. That limit is a categorical coupling that copies one draw with probability \(s/(2-s)\) and otherwise draws the two categories independently; at high concentration the correlation converges to \(s\).
