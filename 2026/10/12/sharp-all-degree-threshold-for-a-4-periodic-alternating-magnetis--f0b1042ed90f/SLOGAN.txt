In the alternating-magnetism SOS tree model, the source's \(u=1,\ w=v\) 4-periodic branch exists exactly for \(\theta>2/(k-1)\) at every tree order \(k\ge2\). Its reciprocal solutions form one gradient Gibbs measure and open as a supercritical square-root pitchfork with explicit amplitude \(\sqrt{6k(k-1)/(k+1)}\) in \(|\log r|\).
