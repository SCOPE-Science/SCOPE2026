For one-step preconditioned CG on a two-mode positive-definite system, uncertainty of less than \(45^\circ\) in a rank-one spectral direction permits a unique, explicitly optimal robust correction; at or beyond \(45^\circ\), doing nothing is minimax. The usual exact-eigenvector coefficient can already lose to no preconditioning at substantially smaller angular error, with a sharp condition-number-dependent threshold.
