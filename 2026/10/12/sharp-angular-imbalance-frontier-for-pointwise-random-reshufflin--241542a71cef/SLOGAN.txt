For two noncommuting strongly convex quadratic components, a full two-step reshuffle improves every initial vector’s expected squared error over independent component sampling exactly when the row-angle correlation satisfies \(c\le\min\{a/b,b/a\}\). Above this sharp boundary some initial directions favor independent sampling, even though reshuffling always improves the worst-direction mean-square error; its possible pointwise disadvantage is strictly less than a sharp factor of \(2\).
