For two separate one-node Lanczos log-determinant probes of a real \(2\times2\) positive-definite matrix, perpendicular Haar directions minimize mean-squared error among all couplings with the same uniform marginals. Their exact variance is \(2\operatorname{Li}_2(\tanh^4(\tfrac14\log\kappa))\), versus \(4\operatorname{Li}_2(\tanh^2(\tfrac14\log\kappa))\) for independent probes; the irreducible positive quadrature bias is unchanged.
