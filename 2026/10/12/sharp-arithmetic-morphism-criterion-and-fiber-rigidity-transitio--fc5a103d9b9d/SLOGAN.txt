Finite global-quorum neighborhood algebras admit a homomorphism precisely when the source and target cardinality thresholds can be reconciled by an almost-equal partition of the underlying worlds; their exact hom-set sizes are partition-weighted multinomial sums. All endomorphisms are permutations, but at a fixed block multiple \(q\ge3\), interior target quotas admit genuinely unequal fibers whereas both quota endpoints force perfect equipartition.
