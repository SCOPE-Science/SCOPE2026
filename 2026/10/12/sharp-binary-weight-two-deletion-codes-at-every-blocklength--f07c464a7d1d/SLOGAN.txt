For every length \(n\ge2\), a binary weight-two single-deletion-correcting code has at most \(\lfloor(3n-2)/4\rfloor\) words, and an explicit periodic support construction attains the bound. This optimum is asymptotically \(3/2\) times the largest class of the classical modified Varshamov--Tenengolts coloring in the same weight layer.
