For every SPD matrix with spectral condition number \(\kappa\), every unpivoted Gaussian-elimination multiplier is at most \((\kappa-1)/(2\sqrt\kappa)\) in magnitude, and this bound is sharp in \(2\times2\). Consequently \(3+2\sqrt2\) is the exact condition-number frontier below which classical partial pivoting is forced to make no row swaps, while above it a two-dimensional SPD system can force a swap.
