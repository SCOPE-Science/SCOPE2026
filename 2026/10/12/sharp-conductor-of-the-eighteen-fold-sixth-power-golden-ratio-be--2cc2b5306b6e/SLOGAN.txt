The eighteen-fold sumset of the Beatty sequence \(\{\lfloor n\varphi^6\rfloor:n\ge1\}\) has exact Frobenius number \(93041\) and conductor \(93042\). A Pell approximant of denominator \(323\) rules out the final candidate, proving the \(m=6\) case of Pongsriiam's golden-ratio Beatty-sumset conjecture.
