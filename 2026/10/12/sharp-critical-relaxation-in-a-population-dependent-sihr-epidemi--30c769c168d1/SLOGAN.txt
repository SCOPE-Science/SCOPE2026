At the critical threshold \(\mathcal R_0=1\), the Jiao–Huang SIHR model is still globally disease-free but relaxes algebraically: infected, hospitalized, recovered, and population-deficit components have explicit \(t^{-1}\) limits. The same coefficient forces logarithmic cumulative infection and confirmation tails, quantifying a nonhyperbolic regime omitted by the source's strict-threshold theorem.
