For independent equal-rate Poisson counts weighted by jumps \(1,\ldots,k\), the entire probability tail from index \(k\) is strictly decreasing exactly when \(\sum_{j=0}^k\binom{k}{j}\lambda^j/(j+2)!<1\). A factorial-convolution inequality shows that the first neighboring crossing at indices \(k+1,k+2\) determines the sharp all-order threshold, resolving a published conjecture.
