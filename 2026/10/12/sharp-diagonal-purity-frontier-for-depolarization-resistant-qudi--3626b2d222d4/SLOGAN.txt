At a fixed computational-basis collision purity, a pure qudit input has an exact sharp global-depolarization NPT survival threshold, with unique optimal population spectra changing from two equal large levels plus a flat tail to a two-level state at collision purity \(1/2\). Strikingly, among all inputs, equal occupation of only two levels survives stronger global depolarization than a maximally coherent \(d\)-level input for \(d\ge3\).
