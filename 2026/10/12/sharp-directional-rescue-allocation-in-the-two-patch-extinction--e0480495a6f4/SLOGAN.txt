For a two-patch extinction-and-rescue Markov model with fixed total directed colonization rate \(C\), the unique mean-extinction-time-maximizing flow split is \(x^*=\operatorname{clip}_{[0,C]}((C+a w_{10}-b w_{01})/2)\). Symmetric allocation is always optimal from full occupancy, even with unequal local extinction rates, while singly occupied starts induce exact and sometimes one-direction-only investment thresholds.
