In Mondal–Lee's systematic ternary simplex LCD codes, coordinates split into three classes with distinct exact minimal two-helper repair counts \(12L+4(n-1)\), \(12L+4n+1\), and \(12L+4n\), where \(L=(3^{n-1}-1)/2\). The maximum number of pairwise disjoint helper sets of size at most two is \(3L+2\) for projective weight one or two, and \(3L+1\) otherwise; these sharpen the coarse observation that every coordinate has locality one.
