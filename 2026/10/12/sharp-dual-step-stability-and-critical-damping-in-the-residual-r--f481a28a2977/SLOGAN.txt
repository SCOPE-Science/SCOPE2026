On the minimal scalar feasibility mode, b-ASADMM is stable exactly for \(\sigma>3/(4a+3\beta)\) and has a unique critical-damping optimum \(\sigma_\star=2/(a+\beta/2+\sqrt{a(a+\beta)})\). The source proof coupling \(\sigma=2/\beta\) is rate-optimal only at \(a/\beta=1/8\), and its scalar factor tends to one while the optimal factor tends to zero when the proximal metric dominates the penalty.
