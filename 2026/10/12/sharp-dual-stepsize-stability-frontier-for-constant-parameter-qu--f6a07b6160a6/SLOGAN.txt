For constant-parameter APAPC on strictly convex isotropic primal–dual quadratics, the exact all-initialization convergence condition is \(\gamma\tau\|K\|_2^2<(1+a\gamma\mu)(2+a\gamma\mu)(2+\tau/(a\lambda))/(3+a\gamma\mu)\). Sufficiently strong dual curvature can make *every finite* dual stepsize stable, while equality produces an exact alternating eigenmode.
