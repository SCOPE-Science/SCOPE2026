For Hermitian positive-definite matrices of condition number \(\kappa\), all sketched harmonic Ritz values stay in the open right half-plane under subspace-embedding distortion \(\varepsilon<2\sqrt{\kappa}/(\kappa+1)\), and this universal threshold is sharp. At equality an explicit two-mode harmonic pencil becomes singular; above it a valid embedding creates a negative harmonic Ritz value.
