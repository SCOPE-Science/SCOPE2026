For any number of identical fair-Bernoulli weights and one arbitrary real exceptional weight, the support size times the squared largest atom is strictly below one: \( |\operatorname{supp}(S)|\,\|\Pr(S=\cdot)\|_\infty^2<1 \). This settles the sharp endpoint Rényi-entropy comparison \(H_0(S)<2H_\infty(S)\) throughout this natural infinite family, and the coefficient \(2\) is asymptotically optimal.
