For the last-snapshot, two-component scalar-quadratic SVRG algorithm, lengthening an epoch from two to three updates increases the exact mean-square stable stepsize interval precisely below a sharp curvature-heterogeneity threshold and decreases it above. The threshold is the unique \(z_c\in(0,1)\) satisfying \(128z_c^3-41z_c^2+6z_c-1=0\).
