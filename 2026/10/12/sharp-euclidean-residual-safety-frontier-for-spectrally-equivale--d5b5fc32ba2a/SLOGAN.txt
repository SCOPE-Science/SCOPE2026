Spectral equivalence does not by itself make the ordinary residual monotone under SPD preconditioned Richardson: the exact worst one-step Euclidean amplification is \(\delta\sqrt{\kappa_2(A)}\) when the symmetrically preconditioned spectrum lies in \([1-\delta,1+\delta]\). The bound is sharp in \(2\times2\), yielding the exact safety frontier \(\delta\sqrt{\kappa_2(A)}\le1\).
