For a fixed Pauli-axis CHSH test, the exact largest Bell value at two-qubit exchange trace distance \(d\) is \(2(d+\sqrt{1-d^2})\), across every pure or mixed state. Both zero and maximal exchange asymmetry forbid violation, while the unique optimal exchange defect \(d=1/\sqrt2\) attains \(2\sqrt2\); the formula also gives sharp inverse bounds on symmetry breaking from any observed violation.
