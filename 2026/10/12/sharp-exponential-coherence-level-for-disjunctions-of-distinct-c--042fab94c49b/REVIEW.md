# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Lax splitting is exactly equivalent to selection of one constant value per coordinate. The proof establishes finite obstruction for arbitrary (including infinite) teams by a separate induction and then produces evaluation-diagonal, linearly independent multilinear polynomials for any finite inclusion-minimal counterexample. This yields a universal \(2^r\) bound. The binary cube fails, but every proper subset satisfies, proving exactness. The independent finite checker exhaustively examined 66,332 team masks and supports the argument without replacing the symbolic proof. Risks: none identified under the stated distinct-coordinate, lax-semantic hypotheses; the theorem makes no claims about other operators or disjunction semantics.

**Originality — PASS, with documented residual risk.** Fröhlich–Kolaitis–Meier (arXiv:2508.16146), Corollary 23, proves exactly the \(r=2\) subcase; its inspected higher-arity section still concerns **two** disjuncts. Barlag et al. (arXiv:2605.31269) establish general logical equivalences involving existence of coherence levels according to their abstract, not this quantified sharp exponential value. Published-result searches and comparisons did not show a dominating full all-\(r\) formula. A previous proof under geometric coordinate-hyperplane/Helly terminology remains possible. The recent Barlag et al. full text could not be retrieved, so that unresolved access limitation is material and not negative evidence.

**Value — PASS.** This gives a structurally explained, sharp obstruction threshold for an unbounded natural formula family, locating the precise exponential growth of coherence and supplying a direct finite witness test for every fixed formula. In contrast to merely knowing each fixed formula is FO-rewritable, it identifies the optimal locality threshold and an extremal binary-cube obstruction. The proof employs a multilinear interpolation mechanism beyond the published two-disjunct case. Risks: the result does not classify arbitrary dependence atoms or minimal witnesses up to isomorphism, and an older equivalent combinatorial result may exist.
