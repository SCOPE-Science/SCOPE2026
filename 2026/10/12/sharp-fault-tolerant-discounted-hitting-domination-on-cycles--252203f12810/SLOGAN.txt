For cycles, survival after any \(f\) sources fail is possible exactly when \(\tau\le\psi_{f+1}\), and the sharp minimum source count is \(\max\{f+1,\lceil(f+1)n/L\rceil\}\) with \(L\) the ordinary discounted gap threshold. All optimum placements are characterized by cyclic \((f+1)\)-gap inequalities, and their exact number is a finite transfer-matrix coefficient.
