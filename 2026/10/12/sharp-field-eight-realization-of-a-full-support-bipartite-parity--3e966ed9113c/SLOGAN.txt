The complete bipartite incidence mask \(K_{6,6}\) has an explicit full-support \([36,24,6]_8\) parity-check realization, and no finite field with fewer than eight elements admits a distance-six realization when every allowed coefficient must be nonzero. A prime-order root-of-unity construction extends the distance-six phenomenon to all \(K_{r,s}\) with \(3\le r,s\le p\mid(q-1)\) and \(p\) odd prime.
