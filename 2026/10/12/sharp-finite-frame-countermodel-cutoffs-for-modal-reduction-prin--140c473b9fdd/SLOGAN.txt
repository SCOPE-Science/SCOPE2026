For every \(m,k\ge1\), the first finite Kripke frame refuting \(\Box^{m+k}p\to\Box^mp\) has exactly \(\min\{m+1,d(k)\}\) worlds, where \(d(k)\) is the least integer at least two not dividing \(k\). Restricting to serial frames changes the exact minimum to \(\min\{m+2,d(k)\}\), producing a sharp one-world separation for every \(k\) divisible by \(\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,m+1)\).
