For the standard rectangular Dirichlet heat grid, Peaceman–Rachford positivity has an exact finite-size threshold: \(\Delta t/(2h_x^2)\le\sigma_m\) and \(\Delta t/(2h_y^2)\le\sigma_n\), where \(D_k(\sigma_k)=2D_{k-1}(\sigma_k)\). On square grids the sharp frontier rises to \(4-2\sqrt2\), so unconditional stability does not imply unconditional positivity.
