For the six-state quantum stochastic random-transposition walk, a unique optimal cosine of the coherent angle cancels the three-cycle probability deviations and gives the exact minimum \(D_{\min}=(2+x-\sqrt{4+4x-2x^2})/3\) for \(x=e^{-\gamma t}\), where \(c_*=(2-\sqrt{4+4e^{-\gamma t}-2e^{-2\gamma t}})/(2e^{-\gamma t})\). Its sharp long-time mixing envelope is \(D_{\min}\sim e^{-2\gamma t}/4\), versus classical \(D\sim 2e^{-\gamma t}/3\), when the coherent coupling may be retuned for the observation time.
