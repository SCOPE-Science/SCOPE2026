A finite mate-limited population has a sharp first habitat-capacity switch: reaching three or four individuals from two founders always favors the smallest eligible habitat, but reaching five favors either five or six sites according to an exact mortality-to-reproduction threshold \(\gamma_*=(113+\sqrt{23901})/253\). For every higher finite recovery target \(K\), capacities above \(2K-3\) can never maximize the recovery probability in the specified uniform-sampling model.
