For every alphabet \(q\ge2\) and fixed duplication length \(k\ge2\), correcting one tandem duplication buys no more codewords than correcting arbitrarily many until length \(3k+1\). Exactly there, the optimum first increases by \(q^k(q-1)\), and the complete structure and number of all optimal codes follow from forced and freely chosen zero-signature fibers.
