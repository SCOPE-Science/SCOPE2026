Among connected \(n\)-vertex trees of diameter at most three, the random-order greedy maximal-independent-set law has a completely classified least deviation from uniformity, with a unique minimizer at every order \(n\ge3\). The sharp global positive bias gap is \(1/15\), uniquely attained by the five-vertex fork; the all-order formula identifies diameter four as the first nontrivial diameter permitting arbitrarily small greedy bias.
