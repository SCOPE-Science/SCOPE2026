For every positive symmetric two-atom transport kernel, the fixed-parameter Acc-Sinkhorn dual-state linearization has an explicit unique rate-optimal tuning, with a repeated positive eigenvalue. Even after this tuning, Acc-Sinkhorn is locally slower than ordinary Sinkhorn when the nontrivial Sinkhorn eigenvalue is below \(1/4\), ties at \(1/4\), and is locally faster above \(1/4\).
