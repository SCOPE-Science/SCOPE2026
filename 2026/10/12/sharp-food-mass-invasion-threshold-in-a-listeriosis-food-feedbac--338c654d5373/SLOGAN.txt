In a recent listeriosis–food model, the conserved food mass changes the invasion boundary to \(\mathcal T_F=\widehat{\mathcal R}(1+a_3F/(p-a_4F))\) when \(p>a_4F\); below that boundary there is no positive endemic equilibrium, and above it there is exactly one. If \(p\leq a_4F\), a unique positive endemic equilibrium exists at every positive transmission rate, and an exact five-compartment example has the published \(\widehat{\mathcal R}=2/3<1\) despite positive endemic infection.
