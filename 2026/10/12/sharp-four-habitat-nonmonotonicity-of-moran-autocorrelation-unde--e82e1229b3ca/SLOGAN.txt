Under equal-edge dispersal and homogeneous local turnover, four connected habitat sites are the smallest network on which global Moran autocorrelation of a binary habitat-source equilibrium can decrease as dispersal increases. The unique minimal example is a four-site path with two adjacent favorable sites at one end: its index peaks exactly at \(d/\mu=(1+\sqrt5)/4\), then declines while Geary's index continues to decrease.
