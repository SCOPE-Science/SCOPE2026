Risk: failed search is not proof of bibliographic uniqueness. An unindexed historical table or software enumeration could contain the same finite census.

The smallest player number at which they disagree is therefore a natural structural boundary. The complete first layer shows that disagreement is neither ubiquitous nor a dummy artifact: it is concentrated in three weighted-game symmetry types, with two types surviving after all dummy players are excluded.
