For every strictly positive \(2\times2\) transport kernel with uniform two-point marginals, fixed-parameter overrelaxed Sinkhorn scaling converges from every positive initialization exactly for \(0<\omega<2\); the endpoint \(2\) conserves an explicit log-cosh potential and fails globally. This sharp interval replaces a potentially near-unit Birkhoff-based sufficient bound, even for highly imbalanced kernels.
