On every binary-colored simple cycle \(C_n\), ordinary modal types at depth \(t\) are exactly centered cyclic factors of length \(2t+1\) modulo reversal, and they coincide with color-preserving dihedral orbits by the sharp depth \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor-1\). Consequently the one-atom Boolean modal algebra is the full powerset exactly for dihedrally asymmetric colorings, yielding a bracelet-based generator census for disjoint unions of cycles.
