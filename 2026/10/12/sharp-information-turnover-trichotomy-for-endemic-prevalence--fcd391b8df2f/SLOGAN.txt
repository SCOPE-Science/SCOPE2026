In the reduced information-dependent SIRM model, the endemic infective level falls strictly as the information growth-to-decay ratio \(k=a/b\) rises. Saturation creates a sharp boundary: prevalence decays like \(k^{-1}\), slows to \(k^{-1/2}\) at critical balance, or approaches a positive floor according as \(u_1/u_2\) is above, equal to, or below \((p+\mu)(\mathcal R_0-1)\).
