For scalar continuous-time LQR, constant-step policy gradient preserves every stabilizing gain up to the sharp normalized threshold \(t<3\sqrt3/2\), yet the optimal gain repels for \(t>1\). In the nonempty interval \(1<t<1.25819035934\ldots\), a unique nonoptimal and locally attracting two-cycle coexists with universal gain stabilization; exact radical formulas locate its points and its sharp loss-of-attraction boundary.
