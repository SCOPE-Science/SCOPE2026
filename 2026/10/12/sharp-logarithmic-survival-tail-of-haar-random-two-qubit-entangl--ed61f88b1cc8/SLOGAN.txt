For two independently dephased qubits initialized in a complex Haar-random pure state, the fraction retaining entanglement has the sharp near-complete-dephasing law \(8s^4\log(1/s)-2s^4+O(s^6\log(1/s))\). Explicit finite-visibility upper and lower bounds identify the logarithmic enhancement as the competition between two independent products of Gaussian amplitudes.
