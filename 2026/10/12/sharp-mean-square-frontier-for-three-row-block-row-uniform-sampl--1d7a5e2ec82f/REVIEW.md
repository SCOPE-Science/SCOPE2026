# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** Four exact subset-transfer calculations yield the explicit \(2\times2\) moment matrix. Self-adjointness of the Euclidean covariance operator transfers the result to every deterministic initial error. The second-order Schur criteria prove geometric stability below \(\mu_*\); Perron--Frobenius theory proves persistence or divergence above it. Independently derived one-step eigenvalues give the sharp \(1/2\) boundary. The exact-rational verifier passed all twelve selected coherence parameters. The finite checks support, but do not replace, the all-parameter analytic proof.

**Originality — PASS, with priority risk.** Du--Sun's defining BRUS algorithm is exactly the one studied; their mean-square theorem supplies a sufficient stepsize condition corresponding to \(\mu<1/2\), whereas the exact threshold is larger. In the equivalent Zhang--Li optional formulation, relaxation \(3\) is not covered by their projection theorem. Zhang--Li's separate greedy block algorithm and earlier block sampling study use pseudoinverses, whereas Necoara's weighted-average framework gives general bounds and different step prescriptions. No inspected primary result gives the exact two-threshold classification for this unaveraged four-row family. Unindexed related results remain possible.

**Value — PASS.** The proof settles a natural stability question for parallel row-correction sums: independent randomization can stabilize every initial error even when every individual fixed-block map has spectral radius greater than one and expected error initially expands along a natural mode. It distinguishes per-step safety from asymptotic mean-square convergence by two exact, algebraically different thresholds. This is a substantive boundary, not a numerical improvement or a reformulation of the source's projection theorem.

**Limitations.** The result is restricted to real four-row equicorrelated systems, uniform independent sampling of three rows and the *unit-coefficient* BRUS step. It refines but does not contradict the sufficient mean-square guarantee for BRUS, and does not apply to the distinct pseudoinverse-based Zhang--Li algorithm. The equality/nonconvergence example uses a specified initial vector. Unindexed specialized literature remains a residual priority risk.
