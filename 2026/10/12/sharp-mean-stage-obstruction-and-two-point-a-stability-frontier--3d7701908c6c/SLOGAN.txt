For the scalar stability map of randomized implicit RK2, every nonconstant stage law with mean \(1/2\) loses mean-square A-stability, with the sharp stiff-limit penalty at least \(12\operatorname{Var}(T)\) above one. Yet a genuinely random two-stage law supported on \(\{a,1\}\), with \(0<a<1/2\), is mean-square A-stable exactly when the mass at the individually unstable stage is at most \((a/(1-a))^2\).
