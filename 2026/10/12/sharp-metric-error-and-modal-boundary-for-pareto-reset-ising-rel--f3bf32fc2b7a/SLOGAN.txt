For Pareto-reset Glauber Ising relaxation, the published cutoff-atom approximation changes the exact stationary conditional-mean law by total variation \((\alpha-1)/\alpha\), but only by \(W_1=m_0(\alpha-1)[(1-e^{-b})/b-e^{-b}]/\alpha\), where \(b=(1-\tanh(2\beta J))\tau_0\). The exact law has no atom, and its finite interior peak exists precisely for \(b<\alpha\).
