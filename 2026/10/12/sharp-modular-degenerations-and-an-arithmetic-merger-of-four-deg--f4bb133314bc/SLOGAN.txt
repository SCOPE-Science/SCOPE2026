Four explicit degree-seven conic–line arrangements forming a complex Zariski pair have sharply classified singularity degenerations at precisely the primes \(7\), \(11\), and (for one arrangement) \(109\). At characteristic \(7\), two representatives from different complex realization-space components reduce to exactly the same divisor; the remaining good-prime singular-point counts have a biquadratic Frobenius distribution.
