The inversion-defect polynomial of maximal consecutive-\(321\)-avoiding Lehmer codes is unimodal exactly for lengths \(2\) through \(10\) and length \(12\); it fails first at \(11\), recovers once at \(12\), and fails at every length from \(13\) onward. An exact block-composition coefficient formula and two infinite families of strict coefficient valleys prove this sharp transition.
