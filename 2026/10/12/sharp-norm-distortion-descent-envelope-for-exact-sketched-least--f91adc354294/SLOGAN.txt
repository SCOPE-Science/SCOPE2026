Exact sketched least-squares minimization gives the sharp true-objective decrease envelope \(\Psi_\varepsilon(\delta)=[\delta-\varepsilon\sqrt{\delta(\delta+4)}]/(1-\varepsilon^2)\) from observed sketched improvement \(\delta\). For \(\delta>0\), universal true-objective nonincrease holds exactly when \(\delta\ge4\varepsilon^2/(1-\varepsilon^2)\); zero improvement is a tie, and two-dimensional counterexamples sharply witness failure below the frontier.
