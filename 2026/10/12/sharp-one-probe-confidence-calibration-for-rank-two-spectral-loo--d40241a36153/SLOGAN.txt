For a rank-two residual, one fresh Gaussian projected spectral-norm look-ahead has exact worst-case relative underestimation probability \(\frac2\pi\arctan(t^2/(2\sqrt{1-t^2}))\); its least-favorable singular-value ratio is \(t/\sqrt2\). Inverting this law gives a sharp one-sided confidence factor for stopping checks without assuming a particular residual spectrum.
