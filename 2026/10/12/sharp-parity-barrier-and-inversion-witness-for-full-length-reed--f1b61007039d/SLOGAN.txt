For every odd prime power \(q\ge5\), full-length unscaled Reed–Solomon \([q,(q+1)/2]_q\) subspaces have exact maximum pairwise subspace distance \(q-3\), attained by coordinatewise field inversion; a one-dimensional-center sunflower consequently cannot have two distinct petals. At \(q=5\), the six distinct codes form a nonsingular conic incidence pattern: pair intersections have dimension two, but every triple intersects only in the constants.
