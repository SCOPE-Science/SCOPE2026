For uniformly phased weighted graph states on \(K_{1,1,c}\), the exact entanglement negativity of the two terminal qubits is positive at every nontrivial phase, yet its globally optimized value decays as \(1/(2c)-3^{2/3}/(4c^{5/3})+o(c^{-5/3})\). All maximizing phases approach integer multiples of \(\pi\) at the sharp distance \(3^{1/6}c^{-2/3}(1+o(1))\).
