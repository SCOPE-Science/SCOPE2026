For four unit-variance equicorrelated Gaussian variables, the gap between their joint fourth-power moment and the product of fourth moments has a sharp quadratic lower bound \(C_*\rho^2\), with \(C_*=2510.51265\ldots\). The nontrivial sharpness point is uniquely located at an interior *negative* correlation \(\rho=-0.1655387555\ldots\), rather than at a singular covariance endpoint.
