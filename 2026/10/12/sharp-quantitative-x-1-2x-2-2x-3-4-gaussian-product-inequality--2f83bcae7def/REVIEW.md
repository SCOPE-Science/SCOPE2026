# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. Isserlis' formula gives the exact eighth-order moment polynomial. The sign-positive region is immediate. In the sign-negative region, arithmetic-geometric mean reduces the problem without loss to equal magnitudes for the two correlations incident to the fourth-power coordinate. Positive semidefiniteness then leaves a two-variable feasible region whose complete calculus minimization yields
\[
C_*=\frac{7-\sqrt{41}}2.
\]
Every equality step is reversible at the stated rank-two extremizer, and positive-definite sharpness follows by approximation from the elliptope interior.

## Originality
PASS. The direct source explicitly asks for quantitative three-dimensional Gaussian product inequalities. The closest full-text paper proves the same qualitative exponent family but gives no sharp correlation-weighted remainder. Searches using the exact moment, exponent pair, weighted quadratic form, and radical constant found no covering statement. The principal residual risk is equivalent coverage in older Gaussian-moment or polynomial-positivity language.

## Value
PASS. The result answers the motivating quantitative question in the first unequal-exponent integer case and gives the optimal constant and equality geometry. The anisotropic weights and rank-two extremizer reveal structure that qualitative nonnegativity does not capture.
