The first unequal-exponent three-dimensional Gaussian product inequality admits a sharp quadratic correlation remainder:
\[
\mathbb E[X_1^2X_2^2X_3^4]-3
\ge
\frac{7-\sqrt{41}}2\left(6\rho_{12}^2+12\rho_{13}^2+12\rho_{23}^2\right).
\]
The optimal constant is attained by an explicit rank-two correlation geometry with one negative correlation-sign product.
