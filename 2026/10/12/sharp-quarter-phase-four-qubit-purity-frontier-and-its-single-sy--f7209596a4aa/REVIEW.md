# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The nonnegative spin-flip overlap gives the unrestricted four-qubit sum-of-balanced-purities bound; exact Gaussian-integer parity quantizes every quarter-phase reduced purity in steps of \(1/64\). The displayed phase vector attains the resulting \(11/32\) lower bound on each cut. Unique five-coefficient gauge fixing reduces the finite classification to exactly \(4^{11}\) candidates. The standalone C++17 checker examines every candidate with exact integers and shows all \(96\) minimizers constitute the same symmetry orbit. A separate complex-matrix Python implementation confirms the representative, orbit, single-qubit marginals, and real-sign restriction. No finite sampling is used to infer an infinite statement: this classification has precisely \(4^{11}\) gauge representatives.

## Originality
**PASS, with residual risk.** The most relevant earlier results are Pauwels–Gühne (arXiv:2608.21185v1) on locally generated iso-entangled bases, Gour–Wallach (arXiv:1006.0036v2) on global four-qubit linear-entanglement bounds, and Chen–Wang (arXiv:1409.0928v1) on real-sign hypergraph states. Their materially inspected statements do not determine the \(\mathbb Z_4\) worst-cut purity optimum or the count/symmetry classification. Related published-result searches address Fourier phase flatness or four-level stabilizer states, not this constrained two-versus-two qubit invariant. Equivalent unpublished or unindexed specializations remain possible; no absolute novelty certification is claimed.

## Value
**PASS.** This is a natural constrained design problem for equal-amplitude states generated by diagonal quarter-phase gates, tied to locally encoded measurement bases. The exact optimum quantifies a nontrivial finite-phase penalty over the unconstrained all-state bound, and the \(96\)-class orbit classification eliminates ambiguity in selecting optimal four-qubit fiducials. The real-sign comparison shows the extra phase levels have a strict operational effect on worst-cut linear entanglement. The result is not a claim of universal physical advantage or general LU classification.

## Closest literature and limits
The general purity lower bound, local basis construction for flat states, and real-sign hypergraph framework are prior work. The new contribution is specifically the discrete complex-phase constrained optimum and all equality classes under specified discrete symmetries. The full quantum-state landscape, device noise, and resource cost beyond these phase gates are not treated.
