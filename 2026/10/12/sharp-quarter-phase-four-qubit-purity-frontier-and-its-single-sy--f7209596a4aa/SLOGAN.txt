For four-qubit states whose sixteen equal-magnitude computational amplitudes carry only phases \(1,i,-1,-i\), the smallest achievable worst two-versus-two marginal purity is exactly \(11/32\), attained by precisely \(96\) local-phase-gauge classes forming one bit-flip/permutation/conjugation orbit. Every optimum yields a sixteen-outcome iso-entangled projective measurement basis; restricting phases to real signs raises the optimum to \(3/8\).
