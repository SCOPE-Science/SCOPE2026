For sampling without replacement at fraction at most \(1/4\), whenever the expected number of marked objects lies strictly between one and two inclusive, the chance of seeing at least two is **strictly greater than \(1/4\)**; that uniform constant is sharp. A fifth-order Bonferroni polynomial with an all-positive 36-coefficient certificate establishes this infinite-family case of the conjectured four-to-one hypergeometric mean-tail bound.
