For three steps of a symmetric continuous random walk, the covariance of the new-record count and positive-occupation count is exactly \(109/96-\Pr(B_1>B_2+B_3)/8\), where the \(B_i\) are iid jump magnitudes. Although both marginal laws are universal, their covariance fills the sharp interval \((35/32,109/96]\); at one and two steps the covariance is universal.
