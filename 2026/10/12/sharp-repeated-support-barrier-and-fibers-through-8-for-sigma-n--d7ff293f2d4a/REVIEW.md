# Review

## Correctness
PASS. Prime powers satisfy the exact formula \(z(p^a)=1+p+\cdots+p^{a-2}\). For fixed prime support, the normalized gap \(z(n)/n\) strictly increases with every exponent, while composite positivity follows from the exact divisor identity \(z(n)=\sum_{1<d<n,\ d\mid n}(d-\varphi(d))\). Thus nonsquarefree two-prime support reduces to \(p^2q\) and \(pq^2\), whose exact values have minimum \(8\), uniquely at \(12\). Three distinct primes already give \(2(p+q+r)\ge20\), and further support or exponents increase the value. These cases exhaust all integers.

## Originality
PASS. The 1999 puzzle already contains the prime-power and squarefree-triple formulas, and A051709 records the known fibers \(0,1,2\), so those ingredients are not claimed as new. The exact range and smallest-preimage tables also reveal the initial candidate values and witnesses. What they do not establish is uniqueness of the fibers \(3,4,6,7,8\), global absence of \(5\), or a structural exclusion of all larger factorization shapes. Targeted exact-form and semantic searches found no statement supplying those implications.

## Value
PASS. A051709 explicitly conjectures finite fibers for every value at least \(3\). Determining the next six fibers is therefore a natural first exact segment of that fiber problem. The result is not a table recomputation: the sharp two-support and three-support barriers explain why no arbitrarily large additional preimages can occur in the classified range.
