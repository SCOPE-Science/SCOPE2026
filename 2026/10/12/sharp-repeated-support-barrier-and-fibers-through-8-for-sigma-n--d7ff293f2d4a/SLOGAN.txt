After the known prime, prime-square, and squarefree-semiprime fibers \(0,1,2\), the next fibers of \(\sigma(n)+\varphi(n)-2n\) are rigid: \(3,4,6,7\) occur uniquely at \(8,27,125,16\), \(5\) never occurs, and \(8\) occurs exactly at \(12\) and \(343\). The structural reason is a sharp \(8\)-barrier for nonsquarefree two-prime support and a \(20\)-barrier once three distinct primes occur.
