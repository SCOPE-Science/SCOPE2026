In the quadratic multiclass graph-label penalty of Faccio et al., the strict local-integrality threshold improves from the full spectral bound to the sharp rowwise bound \(\varepsilon<2/\max_i((L_s)_{ii}+\omega_i)\). Exact categorical rounding certifies the soft-to-hard gap, and a supervised two-vertex example develops nonglobal fractional local minima exactly at the threshold.
