For a two-coordinate strictly convex quadratic, two relaxed coordinate updates sampled without replacement beat two uniform independent draws in expected objective for every initial state exactly through the sharp relaxation limit \(\omega\le 2/(1+c)\), where \(c\) is the normalized coupling. Above this limit, the better sampling protocol depends on the initial error mode, and random reshuffling can have a worse one-epoch worst-case factor even though every individual update decreases objective.
