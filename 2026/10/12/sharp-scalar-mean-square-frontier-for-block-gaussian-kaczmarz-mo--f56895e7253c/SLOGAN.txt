For the source's unnormalized scalar Gaussian-block Kaczmarz update, heavy-ball momentum has the sharp mean-square stability ceiling \(2p(1-\omega^2)/(p+2+(2-p)\omega)\). Over all fixed positive stepsizes and \(0\le\omega<1\), the fastest asymptotic mean-square rate is \(2/(p+2)\), uniquely achieved **without** momentum, even when the expected iterate converges under mean-square-unstable parameters.
