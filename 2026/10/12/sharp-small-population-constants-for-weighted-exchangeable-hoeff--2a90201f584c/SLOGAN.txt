The optimal projected-variance Hoeffding inflation constants for bounded finite-exchangeable populations are exactly \(C_3^\star=1/\log2\), \(C_4^\star=4/3\), \(C_5^\star=1/[2\log(3/2)]\), and \(C_6^\star=6/5\). At odd sizes, nonzero exponential tilts are essential for sharpness; at even sizes, the variance limit suffices.
