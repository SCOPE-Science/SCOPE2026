# Scientific review: sharp support-cap phases for two-summand small deviations

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem covers every real cap \(L\geq1\), arbitrary independent marginals supported on \([0,L]\), exact means one, and the closed event \(X+Y\geq3\). The full moment-preserving mixture of two-point laws is reproduced in the proof and remains inside the cap. For pairs of two-point laws, the low-low event never qualifies; the other three outcomes give exhaustive high-high-only, exactly-one-cross, and both-cross patterns. The first is bounded by a sharp product minimization, the second by \(1/2\), and the third by a monotone two-parameter rational function. Their competition gives the five branches and the exact internal threshold. Each branch has an explicit admissible attaining law. Independent exact-rational finite checks corroborate all boundary formulas and sample cases, without being substituted for a universal proof.

## Originality — PASS, subject to residual priority risk

Ling's arXiv:2608.18392v1 proves the moment-only optimum \(5/9\) at threshold three and the two-atom decomposition used here; these are not claimed as new. Neither that full article, the independently inspected Feige papers arXiv:2607.23980v1 and arXiv:2607.24528v1, nor the bounded-support Chernoff-bound paper arXiv:2503.17594v1 supplies the exact cap-dependent optimum. In particular, an unrestricted sharp inequality cannot imply the strictly stronger cap-dependent inequalities when \(L<3\), and a Chernoff upper bound is not an exact tail-probability maximization. Actual targeted semantic-record and literature searches for the phase points, support caps and two-point formulation yielded no covering statement. Unindexed older equivalents remain possible; lack of a hit is not a uniqueness certificate.

## Value — PASS

This is the exact naturally parameterized finite-support strengthening of a recently resolved small-deviation extremal problem, not a generic restatement of a known tail bound. The sharp discontinuous onset at \(L=3/2\), persistent plateau through \(2+1/\sqrt2\), and final transition to the moment-only value at \(L=3\) identify distinct extremizing mechanisms and quantify how much support information strengthens Feige-type guarantees. No theorem for larger numbers of summands is asserted or required.

The event here is inclusive and atoms are allowed; changing either the support or the event convention can change the phase structure. The optimizer is exhibited, not classified exhaustively.
