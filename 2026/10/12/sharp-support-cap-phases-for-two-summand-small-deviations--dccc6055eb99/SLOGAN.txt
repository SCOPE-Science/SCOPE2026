For two independent nonnegative mean-one variables capped at \(L\), the exact probability of exceeding their common mean by one has five sharp support-cap phases. A discontinuous onset at \(L=3/2\), a \(1/2\) plateau, and the transition \(L=2+1/\sqrt2\) precede the unrestricted \(5/9\) maximum at \(L=3\).
