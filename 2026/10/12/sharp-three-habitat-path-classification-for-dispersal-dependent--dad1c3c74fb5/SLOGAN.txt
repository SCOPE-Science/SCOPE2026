On the three-habitat path, placing the medium reproductive rate at the center always maximizes the linear population growth exponent; nevertheless, the other two placements exchange rankings exactly once when \(2m>h+\ell\), at dispersal \(d_*=(h-m)(m-\ell)/(2m-h-\ell)\). This is the smallest connected habitat network supporting such a ranking reversal among permutations of a fixed set of local growth rates.
