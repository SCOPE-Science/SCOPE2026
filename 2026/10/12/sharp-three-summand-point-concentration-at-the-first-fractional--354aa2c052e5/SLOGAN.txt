For three independent integer-valued summands whose individual point masses are at most \(\alpha\in[1/3,1/2]\), the sharp maximal point probability is \(\alpha(7\alpha^2-6\alpha+2)\) below \(\alpha=2/5\) and \(3\alpha^2(1-\alpha)\) above it. Explicit three-site distributions attain both branches, proving a finite odd-summand case of the signed extremal-concentration conjecture and revealing an optimal-orientation transition.
