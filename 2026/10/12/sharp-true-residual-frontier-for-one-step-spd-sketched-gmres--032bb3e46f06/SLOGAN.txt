For one sketched GMRES step on a real SPD system of condition number \(\kappa\), an \(\varepsilon\)-embedding of the residual plane gives the **sharp** true-residual factor \((\kappa-1)/((\kappa+1)\sqrt{1-\varepsilon^2})\). Universal nonexpansion holds exactly for \(\varepsilon\le2\sqrt\kappa/(\kappa+1)\), and explicit two-dimensional sketches attain equality and cause growth above that threshold.
