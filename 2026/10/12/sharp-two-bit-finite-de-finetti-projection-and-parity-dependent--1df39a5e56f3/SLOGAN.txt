Every negatively correlated exchangeable binary pair has a unique closest infinitely exchangeable pair in total variation: an independent Bernoulli pair whose parameter is the projection of \(1/2\) onto \([\sqrt{P(1,1)},1-\sqrt{P(0,0)}]\). Among binary pairs extendible to \(N\) exchangeable observations, the exact worst-case distance is \(1/[2(N-1)]\) for even \(N\), and \(1+1/N-\sqrt{1+1/N}\) for odd \(N\), with balanced fixed-count urns attaining equality.
