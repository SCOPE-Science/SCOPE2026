# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof fixes the exact outside-neighbour condition and uses the part-occupancy equations \(s-s_i=2\), with a justified no-full-part step requiring \(n_i\ge3\). Their sole admissible solution is one vertex from each of three parts; with four or more parts no proper solution exists. For three parts the block-excess counting obstruction supplies \(N-3\), and three explicit cross-part pairs attain this bound. For all part sizes at least four, the remaining singletons force exactly one pair of each cross-part type and justify the binomial product count and coalition graph. Full exhaustive checks of up to eleven vertices and an independent twelve-vertex optimum-matching enumeration agree. Finite computation is not an infinite proof.

## Value — PASS
The theorem supplies a sharp and unbounded numerical separation between three and four partite classes for a recently defined coalition invariant. It identifies all extremizers, their exact count, and their coalition graph whenever each of the three parts has size at least four. The lower part-size assumption is mathematically motivated by the exact-neighbour obstruction, and the claim concerns entire natural multi-parameter graph families rather than selected small instances.
