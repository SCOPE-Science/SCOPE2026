# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Every proper exact-two-fair dominating set S on r>=3 parts, each size>=3, cannot contain an entire part: an outside vertex from another part would see >=3 chosen vertices. Consequently every part has an outside vertex, the occupancies satisfy |S|-s_i=2 and all s_i=t, giving (r-1)t=2. Therefore proper eligible sets are precisely three-part one-per-part transversals, and none exists for r>=4. For r>=4 every coalition union must be V, permitting at most two blocks; any bipartition attains two by vacuity of V. For r=3, a hypothetical >=N-2-block partition has at most two cross-part pairs yet necessarily singleton vertices of all three part types requiring three pair types. Three AB/AC/BC pairs attain N-3. At ni>=4, every maximum consists of one of each pair type, 8 product binom(ni,2) choices, coalition graph three disjoint stars. Independent direct exhaustive partition enumeration finds maximum six among 21,147 partitions for K333 (558 optima); direct fair-set mask enumeration finds exactly 27 proper transversals for K333 and no proper 2-fair sets for K3333. Finite computations are not the general proof.

## originality

PASS

The initiators define exact-two-fair coalitions but work with complete/bipartite families; the 2026 Kaneshiro ordinary coalition requires at least two outside neighbors, mathematically weaker than exactly two. Those results do not force the four-part collapse or the exact extremizer count; full Kaneshiro text remained inaccessible despite arXiv, OA and authorized institutional attempts.

## value

PASS

The sharp N-3 versus 2 drop between r=3 and 4 is a genuine structure transition on an infinite natural multipartite family. Full maximizer structure, counts, and three-star coalition graph solve more than a scalar threshold and can serve follow-on enumeration.
