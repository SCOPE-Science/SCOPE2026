For complete multipartite graphs whose parts all have at least three vertices, the two-fair coalition number falls sharply from \(N-3\) with three parts to \(2\) with four or more. With three parts of size at least four, every maximum coalition partition has exactly one two-vertex block of each cross-part type, giving \(8\prod_i\binom{n_i}{2}\) optima and a uniquely determined three-star coalition graph.
