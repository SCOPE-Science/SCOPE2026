For three i.i.d. draws from any \(q\)-letter distribution, the loss in expected distinct rearrangements relative to uniformity lies between two explicit **optimal** multiples of squared Euclidean distance from uniformity. For \(q\ge3\), the lower coefficient \(5-4/q\) is attained at point masses, while the upper coefficient \(9-12/q+4(q-2)/(q(q-1))\) is attained by uniform distributions missing exactly one letter.
