For the rational prime-representing iteration \(T(x)=\lfloor x\rfloor(1+\{x\})\), the smallest reduced numerator of exact entrance time two is determined explicitly for *every* prime-power denominator \(p^k\). Its formula has an exponent-parity transition and a distinct binary odd-power branch, answering the first nontrivial case of a published minimum-entrance question over all prime powers.
