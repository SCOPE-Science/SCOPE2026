For the normalized sum of any \(n\ge3\) independent symmetric signs, the sharp Gaussian convex-order dilation uniform in \(n\) is \(\sqrt{6\pi}/4\), attained at \(n=3\); for \(n\ge2\) the sharp uniform constant is \(\sqrt2/[4\varphi(\Phi^{-1}(3/4))]\), attained at \(n=2\). Both strictly improve the optimal universal one-sub-Gaussian comparison constant \(\sqrt{\pi/2}\).
