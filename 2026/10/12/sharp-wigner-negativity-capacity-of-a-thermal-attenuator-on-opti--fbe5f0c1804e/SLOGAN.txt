For any phase-insensitive thermal attenuator, a pure vacuum–one-photon superposition uniquely maximizes integrated optical Wigner negativity at every fixed nonzero excitation population, among all mixed single-rail inputs, whenever negativity survives. The full single-rail capacity is \(\frac{2\eta}{A}e^{-1+A/(2\eta)}-1\) for \(A=\eta+(1-\eta)(2b+1)<2\eta\), uniquely attained by \(|1\rangle\); otherwise every input has zero negative volume.
