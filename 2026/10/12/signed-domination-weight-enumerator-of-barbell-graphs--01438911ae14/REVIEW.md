# Review

## Correctness
**PASS.** For each non-bridge vertex, the closed-neighborhood sum equals its whole-clique sum. Each bridge endpoint adds exactly the opposite endpoint label. This gives the necessary-and-sufficient four-inequality criterion. Parity then makes the bridge inequalities automatic for even \(n\), while conditioning on both endpoint signs gives the four disjoint odd-\(n\) contributions. The minimum layers follow from the same thresholds. Exhaustive enumeration for \(2\le n\le8\) agrees coefficientwise with the formulas.

## Originality
**PASS.** The closest inspected same-invariant full text gives scalar signed-domination results for complete bipartite graphs, not barbells. A broader same-invariant paper gives general bounds rather than an all-function enumerator. The closest inspected same-object result concerns the ordinary domination polynomial and proves bridge irrelevance there; that statement does not imply the signed-majority inequalities and, in fact, the signed case distinguishes even and odd clique order. Searches under “barbell,” “dumbbell,” “two complete graphs joined by an edge,” “clique bridge,” and weight-enumerator formulations did not locate a statement implying the theorem. Residual risk remains for unindexed literature and for the inaccessible full text of the 1995 foundational chapter.

## Value
**PASS.** The result gives a complete weight distribution, not merely the minimum signed-domination number, for a standard graph family. The parity-sensitive bridge effect supplies a structural comparison with the known ordinary-domination bridge-irrelevance result: a single bridge is invisible to all signed dominating functions for even clique order but deletes an exactly classified layer for odd clique order. This makes the formula useful as a test family for signed-domination enumerators and for understanding edge sensitivity of majority-type domination.

## Closest literature and limitations
The checked sources were the 1995 foundational bibliographic record (MR1405819), Zelinka's full-text complete-bipartite paper (DML-CZ:128088), the full-text general-bounds paper arXiv:1409.2755, and the full-text barbell ordinary-domination paper arXiv:1511.00159. No claim is made for unequal barbells or path-barbells, and the literature search cannot exclude every unindexed source.
