On a heterogeneous windmill \(K_1\vee(K_{m_1}\cup\cdots\cup K_{m_r})\), signed domination separates blade by blade: the complete weight enumerator factors into one product for each center sign. If \(o\) blades have odd size, then \(\gamma_s=1+o\), and the minimum functions are exactly those with center label \(+1\) and a smallest majority in every blade.
