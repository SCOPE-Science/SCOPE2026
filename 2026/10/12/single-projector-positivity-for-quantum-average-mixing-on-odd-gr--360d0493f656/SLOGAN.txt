For every odd graph \(KG(2k+1,k)\), the Schur square of one adjacency spectral projector is already positive definite, so the continuous-time average mixing matrix has full rank \(\binom{2k+1}{k}\). An explicit signed-transversal construction proves this simultaneously for arbitrarily large \(k\), providing an infinite-family case of the primitive distance-regular average-mixing rank problem.
