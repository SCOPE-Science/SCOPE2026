For the projective edge-monomial hypersurface of two odd cycles joined by a path of length \(\ell\), the reduced singular locus has exactly \(\lfloor\ell^2/4\rfloor\) divisorial components, independent of both cycle lengths, and all its remaining irreducible components have explicit codimension-two and -three counts. Their generic transverse germs are, respectively, a two-branch crossing, a surface \(A_1\) singularity, and a threefold node; normality holds exactly when \(\ell\le1\).
