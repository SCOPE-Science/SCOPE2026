# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The determinant expansion \(t\prod a_i-\sum b_i^2\prod_{j\ne i}a_j\) is exact. Since all diagonal entries vary independently, the adjugate has a nonzero diagonal entry at every corank-one point, so the singular set is precisely the corank-at-least-two locus. Partitioning by the number of zero leaf diagonals gives an exhaustive description: none gives full possible rank, one gives \(Y_i\), two gives an intersection of two \(Y\)-components, and three or more gives an \(L\)-component. Each component is irreducible and generically distinct. The local equations \(a_i\tau-b_i^2\) and \(\tau a_ia_ja_k-b_i^2a_ja_k-b_j^2a_ia_k-b_k^2a_ia_j\) have the claimed transverse nondegenerate Hessians; formal Morse reduction is valid over \(\mathbb C\). The complement has no divisorial singularities, so Serre's \(R_1+S_2\) criterion proves normality. Exact gradient enumerations support the proof but are not used as infinite certification.

The calculation distinguishes the singularity geometry of sparse symmetric determinant hypersurfaces from the generic determinant: a single natural family develops a cubic-growth count of irreducible codimension-two singular components, comprising both nonlinear determinantal varieties and linear spaces. Their common generic transverse node, normality, and aggregate reduced degree give structural birational and intersection-theoretic information throughout the infinite family; these are not consequences of the basic determinant formula alone.
