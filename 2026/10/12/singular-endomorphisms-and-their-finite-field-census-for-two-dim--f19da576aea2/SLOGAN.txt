For every two-dimensional perfect evolution algebra, each singular nonzero endomorphism is idempotent or square-zero, and every such map is explicitly controlled by a triangular structure matrix and a quadratic equation. Over every \(\mathbf F_q\), as the labeled perfect algebra structure varies, there are exactly \(2q(q-1)^2\) rank-one retractions and exactly as many nonzero square-zero endomorphisms; the full endomorphism-pair count is \(2q(q-1)^2(q+1)+(q-1)^2(5q-\gcd(2,q-1)+\gcd(3,q-1))\).
