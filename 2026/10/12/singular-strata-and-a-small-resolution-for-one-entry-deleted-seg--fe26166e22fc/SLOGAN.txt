Deleting one coordinate from a projective Segre embedding produces a normal toric variety whose singular locus is at most two disjoint coordinate-linear spaces, with explicit transverse rank-one determinantal cones and local class groups \(\mathbb Z\). Blowing up the omitted Segre point resolves the projection; when both matrix dimensions are at least three, the morphism is a projective small resolution, crepant exactly in the \(3\times3\) case.
