The complete critical scheme of the Corrêa--Kollár--Schreieder--Wang fibration from a smooth projective fivefold is six disjoint split elliptic ribbons, each with a nontrivial conormal line bundle of exact order \(3\). Their nilpotent directions are invisible even to structure-sheaf cohomology: reduction induces an isomorphism on every \(H^i(\mathcal O)\).
