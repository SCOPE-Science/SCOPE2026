With three candidates and two seats, bloc Approval Voting and Satisfaction Approval Voting cannot choose different unique committees with fewer than \(6\) nonempty approval ballots. At \(6\) voters the boundary is completely rigid: up to relabeling, \(3\times\{A,B\}+\{B\}+2\times\{C\}\) makes AV elect \(\{A,B\}\) and SAV elect \(\{B,C\}\).
