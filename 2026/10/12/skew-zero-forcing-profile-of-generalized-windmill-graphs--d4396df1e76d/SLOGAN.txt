For \(k\ge2\) and \(m\ge3\), skew zero forcing on \(W_{k,m}=K_1\vee kK_m\) is controlled exactly by blade occupancies: each blade may leave at most two white vertices, with only the all-two profile forbidden when the center is white. This yields \(\mathcal Z_-(W_{k,m};x)=(1+x)(x^m+m x^{m-1}+\binom{m}{2}x^{m-2})^k-\binom{m}{2}^k x^{k(m-2)}\) and \(Z_-(W_{k,m})=k(m-2)+1\).
