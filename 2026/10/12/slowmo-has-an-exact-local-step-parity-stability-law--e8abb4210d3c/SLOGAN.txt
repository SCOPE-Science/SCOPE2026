On a scalar quadratic, SlowMo with \(\alpha=1\) has an exact parity split: even local-step counts are stable only for the ordinary SGD range \(0<\gamma\lambda<2\), while odd counts are stable up to \(\gamma\lambda<1+(1+2\beta)^{1/\tau}\). Thus positive slow momentum can stabilize an individually unstable fast SGD step only when the synchronization period is odd.
