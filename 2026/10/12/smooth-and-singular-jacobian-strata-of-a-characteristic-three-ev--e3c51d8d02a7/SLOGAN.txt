The even-octic genus-three family \(y^2=t^8+b^3t^6+a^3t^2+1\) in characteristic \(3\) has constant smooth Jacobian \(3\)-rank \(2\) and \(a\)-number \(1\), while its boundary \(ab=1\) consists of two-cusp integral curves and four two-tacnode split fibers. The cuspidal normalizations are elliptic, with exactly four supersingular parameters, and the reducible fibers have connected generalized Jacobian \(\mathbf G_m\times\mathbf G_a^2\).
