The adjacency quantum walk on the \(2n\)-vertex sunlet graph has long-time average-mixing rank exactly \(2n\) for odd \(n\), exactly \(n\) for \(n\equiv2\pmod4\), and exactly \(n-1\) when \(4\mid n\). An explicit splitting into clone-symmetric and clone-antisymmetric spectral sectors identifies the unique extra zero mode at multiples of four and gives the full Fourier-indexed spectrum.
