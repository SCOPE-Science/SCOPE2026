In the nondegenerate multiplicative slice of Baishya–Das's three-dimensional Hom–Lie example, Nijenhuis operators are governed by the single block equation \((xI_2-B)(AB-BA)=0\). Over odd \(\mathbf F_q\), square and nonsquare values of \(\beta\gamma\) give sharply different moduli: \(3q^3-2q^2\) versus \(q^3\) operators, with explicit rank distributions.
