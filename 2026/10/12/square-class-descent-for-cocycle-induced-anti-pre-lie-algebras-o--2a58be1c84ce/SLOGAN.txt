For the two-dimensional affine Lie algebra over a field of characteristic other than \(2\), anti-pre-Lie products induced by nondegenerate symmetric commutative \(2\)-cocycles are classified by one exceptional type and the square classes \(K^\times/(K^\times)^2\). Over every odd finite field \(\mathbf F_q\), this gives exactly three isomorphism types, \(q^2\) distinct induced products with orbit sizes \(q,q(q-1)/2,q(q-1)/2\), but \(2q-1\) orbits if the original cocycles are retained.
