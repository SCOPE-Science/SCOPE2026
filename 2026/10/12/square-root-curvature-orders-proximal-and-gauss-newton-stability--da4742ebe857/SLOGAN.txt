For nonnegative convex radial losses, the sign of the one-step stability-index difference between nonnegative Gauss–Newton and exact proximal minimization is determined by whether the square root of the radial loss is convex or concave on the current radial segment. For power losses \(\|x\|^p/p\), the ordering reverses sharply at \(p=2\) for every positive stepsize, with explicit small- and large-stepsize gap limits.
