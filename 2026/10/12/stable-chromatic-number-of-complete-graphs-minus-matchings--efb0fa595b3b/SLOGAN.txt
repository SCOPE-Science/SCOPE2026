Deleting any nonempty matching from \(K_n\) reduces its stable chromatic number by exactly one: \(\chi_{\mathrm{stable}}(K_n-M_r)=n-1\) for every \(1\le r\le\lfloor n/2\rfloor\). By contrast, ordinary chromatic number drops to \(n-r\), and a reverse-preference construction forces a blocking exchange whenever two deleted pairs share colors.
