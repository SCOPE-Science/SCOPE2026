In the four-player opinion–action public-goods game, **fully synchronous best responses can converge to a strict attracting two-cycle even on a complete, primitive influence network**. An exact rational example alternates the opinions \((77/79,77/79,2/79,2/79)\) and \((2/79,2/79,77/79,77/79)\), while both consensus equilibria remain locally attracting; the cycle persists under small parameter changes.
