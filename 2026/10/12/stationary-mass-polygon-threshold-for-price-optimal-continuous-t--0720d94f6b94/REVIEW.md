# Review

## Correctness — PASS
The exact generator normalization gives the covariance matrix \(V=\operatorname{diag}(p)-pp^\mathsf T\) and variational gap. Strict semidefinite feasibility gives equality of primal and Gram dual optima. The minimum weighted polygon resultant is \(\delta\); constructions in both regimes attain \(S-\delta^2\), including the equality boundary. The unbalanced Schur complement proves positive semidefiniteness, and complementarity yields global uniqueness and gap multiplicity. The 321 exact instances and numerical eigenchecks only supplement the proof.

## Originality — PASS
Göring–Helmberg–Wappler (2005) proved the basic unweighted-vertex embedding dual and tree geometry. Boyd–Diaconis–Xiao (2004) and the later symmetry paper treat a discrete-time SLEM objective. Jafarizadeh (2020) explicitly solved an arbitrary-mass star for discrete-time reversible SLEM but did not give the jointly arbitrary-price, arbitrary-mass **continuous-time** budget theorem. Equal-mass priced-star and arbitrary-mass birth-death path cases are already separate specializations, not this joint multi-leaf theorem. The retrieved published published-finding corpus hits did not cover this exact claim. An inaccessible historical equivalent cannot be ruled out; weighted-tree perturbation literature poses residual priority risk.

## Value — PASS
The exact purchase-price and stationary-mass geometry determines the optimal rates and the gap on all stars with at least three leaves. It pinpoints a nontrivial mass-induced phase transition even when all edge prices are equal and identifies when the optimal gap changes multiplicity. The result is a complete parameter classification, not a fixed-instance certificate or mechanical rescaling of the equal-mass case.

## Scope and limits
Strictly positive stationary masses and prices, one linear edge budget, finite reversible continuous-time star only. No result is claimed for capped transition rates, discrete-time SLEM, or arbitrary graphs. The work has not undergone independent review.
