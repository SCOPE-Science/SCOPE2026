For a reversible continuous-time Markov chain on a star with arbitrary positive stationary masses and independently priced edges, the unique budget-optimal spectral gap is \(1/(S-\delta^2)\), where \(S=\sum_i\pi_i c_i\) and \(\delta=(2\max_i\pi_i\sqrt{c_i}-\sum_i\pi_i\sqrt{c_i})_+\). The optimal rates exhibit a sharp polygon-imbalance transition even when every edge has the same price.
