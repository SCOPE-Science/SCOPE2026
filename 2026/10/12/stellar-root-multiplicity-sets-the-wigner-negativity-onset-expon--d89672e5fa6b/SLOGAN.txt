For every nonvacuum pure state with finite Fock support, the Wigner-negative volume emerging just above 50% pure-loss transmissivity is asymptotic to an explicit positive constant times \((2\eta-1)^{m_*+1}\), where \(m_*\) is the smallest multiplicity of a zero of its stellar polynomial. Squarefree states exhibit quadratic onset, whereas the \(n\)-photon number state has onset order \(n+1\).
