For every \(0<p\le1/3\), the tails \(\Pr\{\operatorname{Bin}(3k,p)\ge k\}\) strictly decrease with \(k\). Hence at threshold \(1/3\) the three-bet average is the unique best equal-stake strategy, settling the full one-third slice of the generalized Chaundy–Bullard candidate problem while leaving arbitrary unequal stakes open.
