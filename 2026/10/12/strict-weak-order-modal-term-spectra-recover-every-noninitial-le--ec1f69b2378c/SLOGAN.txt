For finite strict weak-order frames, the exact number of \(r\)-ary Boolean modal term operations is \(2^{q\sum_i\prod_{j>i}\sum_{k=1}^{n_j}\binom{q}{k}}\), with \(q=2^r\). Across all arities, these counts reconstruct every ordered level width except the first, and that loss of information is sharp.
