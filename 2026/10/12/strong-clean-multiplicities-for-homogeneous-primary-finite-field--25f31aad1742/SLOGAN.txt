For homogeneous primary finite-field matrices, every neutral \(f\)-primary block \((\mathbb F_q[t]/(f^m))^k\) contributes exactly \(\sum_r {k\brack r}_{q^{\deg f}}(q^{\deg f})^{(2m-1)r(k-r)}\) strongly clean choices, while the \(0\)- and \(1\)-primary blocks are forced. For arbitrary finite-field matrices, the strongly clean decomposition is unique exactly when \(A-A^2\) is nilpotent.
