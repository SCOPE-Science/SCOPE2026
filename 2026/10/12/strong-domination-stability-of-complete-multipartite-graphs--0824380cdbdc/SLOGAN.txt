For a connected noncomplete complete multipartite graph, strong-domination stability is determined only by the smallest part size and its multiplicity: it equals the number of singleton parts when the minimum is \(1\), equals \(2\) exactly when the minimum is \(3\) and occurs at least twice, and equals \(1\) otherwise. In particular, balanced \(K_{p,\ldots,p}\) has stability \(2\) only at \(p=3\).
