Every negative-binomial law with integer shape \(r\ge3\) and failure probability \(1/(t+1)\) for integer \(t\ge1\) is left-loaded in the strong, full-vector sense: its centered left-minus-right tail probabilities have exactly one positive-to-negative sign transition. A Vandermonde-propagated endpoint bound and a one-turn mass-ratio argument prove the statement for all parameters, not just finitely many tested cases.
