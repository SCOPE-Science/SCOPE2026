For every \(m,n\ge2\), the strong-majority coloring game on \(K_{m,n}\) is strategy-free once the palette has enough shore capacity: every legal play completes exactly when \(k\lfloor m/2\rfloor\ge m\) and \(k\lfloor n/2\rfloor\ge n\). Consequently \(\operatorname{Maj}_g(K_{m,n})=2\) for even-even shore sizes and \(3\) otherwise.
