For \(1\le m\le n\), every complete bipartite graph except \(K_{1,2}\) has strong majority index in \(\{1,2,3,4\}\): two colors occur exactly for \(K_{2,2r}\), while four colors occur exactly for \(K_{1,4}\), \(K_{3,4}\), and \(K_{4,5}\). All remaining admissible complete bipartite graphs need exactly three colors, except \(K_{1,1}\), which needs one.
