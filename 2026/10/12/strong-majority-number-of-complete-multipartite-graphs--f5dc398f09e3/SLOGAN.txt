For every colorable complete multipartite graph \(G=K_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}\), the strong majority number is \(2\) exactly when all parts are even, is \(4\) exactly for \(K_4\) and \(K_{2,1,1}\), and is \(3\) otherwise. The two-color criterion was already known through neighborhood-balanced coloring; the new step is the exact three-versus-four classification.
