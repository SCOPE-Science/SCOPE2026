For every connected complete multipartite graph \(G=K_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}\), a proper coloring is strong odd exactly when every color class has odd size. Thus \(\chi_{\mathrm{so}}(G)=r+|\{i:n_i\text{ is even}\}|\), sharpening the previously stated four-color upper bound for complete bipartite graphs to an exact parity law.
