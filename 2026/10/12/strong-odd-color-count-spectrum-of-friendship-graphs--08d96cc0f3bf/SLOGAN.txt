For the friendship graph \(F_k\) with \(k\ge2\), the possible numbers of colors in strong odd colorings are exactly \(\{3,5,\ldots,2k+1\}\) when \(k\) is odd and \(\{5,7,\ldots,2k+1\}\) when \(k\) is even. The parity law comes entirely from the common center: every color on the outer vertices must occur an odd number of times.
