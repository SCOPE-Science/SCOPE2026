Every complex linked projective space has the same structure-sheaf cohomology as projective space: \(H^0(X,\mathcal O_X)\cong\mathbb C\) and \(H^i(X,\mathcal O_X)=0\) for all \(i>0\). Its simple-normal-crossings dual complex is contractible, giving a cohomological strengthening of the known diagonal Chow-class and Hilbert-polynomial coincidences.
