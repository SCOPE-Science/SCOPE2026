The complete lattice of unital subalgebras of \(\mathbb F_q\oplus V\oplus\mathbb F_q z\), with \(uv=B(u,v)z\), is classified by ordinary subspaces and graphs over totally isotropic subspaces. Its dimension polynomial determines the algebra in odd characteristic, but in characteristic two it identifies exact pairs of nonisomorphic algebras of adjacent nonalternating form ranks whose **entire subalgebra lattices are isomorphic**, even when one algebra is Gorenstein and the other is not.
