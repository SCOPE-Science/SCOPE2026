# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The six governing equations, strict fractional-order range and reproduction threshold were reconstructed directly. A positive Metzler comparison yields global decay for \(\mathcal R_0<1\) without a rational-order assumption. Nonnegative scalar Mittag–Leffler kernels and their nonintegrable homogeneous tails, followed by Tonelli transfer across positive incidence, progression and treatment links, establish divergence of every stated time integral. The integer-order comparison is exponentially stable. All analytic quantifiers, strict inequalities and zero-seed/zero-treatment boundaries are explicit; the exact checker corroborates matrix algebra but is not treated as an infinite proof.

## Originality
PASS with residual indexing risk. The closest six-class paper (Oumedjber et al., 2026) establishes common-order global disease-free convergence but does not handle unequal orders or infinite ordinary-time burden; the closest incommensurate SI result (Aloui et al., 2026) is two-class and does not include exposed, asymptomatic or treated transfer. The motivating full source gives local rational-order stability and finite-horizon burden. Pollard's positive-kernel theorem is established prior art. The joined clearance-versus-infinite-burden theorem for this multi-order six-class nonlinear network is not covered by their stated results.

## Value
PASS. The theorem directly changes the mathematical interpretation of extending the source's cumulative treatment-load and active-burden calculations to unbounded horizons: instantaneous extinction need not produce finite literal lifetime occupancy or incidence. The result has a sharp classical-order comparison and explicit substantive boundaries.
