In the six-compartment incommensurate Caputo epidemic model, \(\mathcal R_0<1\) forces every infected and treated occupancy to vanish, yet any nonzero infection seed makes the literal all-time integrals of exposed, symptomatic, asymptomatic, treated and new-infection terms diverge. Those integrals are finite for the corresponding ordinary differential model, so finite-horizon treatment load cannot be extrapolated to an unweighted infinite horizon without accounting for fractional tails.
