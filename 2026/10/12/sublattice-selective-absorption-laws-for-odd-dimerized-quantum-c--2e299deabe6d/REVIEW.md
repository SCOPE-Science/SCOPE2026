# Scientific assessment: odd dimerized chain absorption

## Correctness — PASS

The normalized singular vectors of the bidiagonal intersublattice hopping give simple eigenvalues \(\pm E_\ell\) and one exact chiral zero mode. Dissipativity proves that the asymptotic surviving weight is the orthogonal projector onto all eigenvectors invisible to every monitored site. Bézout divisibility counts the \(2g-1\) surviving minority-monitor modes; a finite Fourier sine-square identity gives their exact sitewise capture values. For one majority monitor, nonzero-energy modes vanish only at a finite list of positive hopping ratios, with no endpoint resonances. These arguments hold for every finite \(m\ge2\) and all strictly positive hopping and loss strengths. The standalone verifier corroborates the proof with 1135 rank, 2260 probability, 32 lossy-spectrum, 1 exact-polynomial and 4 dynamical cases. Finite calculations are not substituted for proof.

## Originality — PASS (qualified priority risk)

Exact clean odd SSH eigenvectors are prior results of Sirker et al. (arXiv:1406.7832); qualitative sublattice-protected nondecay is demonstrated by Poli et al. (*Nature Communications* 2015); and Wang and Zhang (arXiv:2607.10218) derive a rank-one local-loss secular criterion on an even-site chain. The evaluated arbitrary-subset gcd count, exact hopping-independent capture from all minority sites, and isolated majority-monitor coupling-ratio classification are distinct asserted observables from the inspected sources. Sirker et al.'s full public arXiv HTML was inspected, particularly the odd-chain eigenvectors in Section 2.2 and the topics of subsequent sections; no absorber classification is given. An unidentified broader spectral theorem could nonetheless imply the finding. The literature comparison does not certify priority.

## Value — PASS

Exact detector placement determines whether a wave packet decays or remains protected. The full gcd classification identifies redundancy of multiple monitored sites and a coupling-independent capture fraction for each localized minority input. The exceptional majority-detector ratio classification gives a distinct tunable boundary. This is a motivated structural finite-chain classification, not an arbitrary small numerical example.

## Limitations and closest work

The claim assumes strictly positive alternating nearest-neighbor hopping on finite odd open chains, perfect localized absorbers, and either a nonempty subset of minority monitors or a single majority monitor; arbitrary mixed monitored sets, finite-time decay rates, disorder and interactions are not classified. Earlier clean-chain eigenvectors, sublattice-protection demonstrations and single-impurity resolvent techniques are credited. Publication priority and independent audit remain unverified.
