On an odd-length dimerized SSH chain, absorbers confined to the minority sublattice leave exactly \(2g-1\) dark modes, where \(g\) is the gcd of the monitored site indices and half the chain length rounded up; the capture probability from any minority-sublattice site has an exact, hopping-independent two-level arithmetic law. A single majority-sublattice absorber instead captures every state except at an explicit finite set of hopping ratios, where paired modes become perfectly dark.
