For the projective toric variety of the complete-graph spanning-tree polytope, every torus-fixed local divisor-class group is torsion-free, with rank exactly the number of proper connected induced subsets of its tree minus \(n-2\). The least and greatest ranks among all tree vertices are \(\binom{n-1}{2}\) (paths) and \(2^{n-1}-n\) (stars), so every fixed point is non-\(\mathbb Q\)-factorial for \(n\ge4\).
