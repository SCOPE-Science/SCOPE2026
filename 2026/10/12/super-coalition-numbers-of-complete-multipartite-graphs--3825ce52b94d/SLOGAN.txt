For a connected complete multipartite graph, the super coalition number is never above \(4\): it equals \(4\) exactly for \(K_{2,2}\) or when at least three partite classes have size at least \(2\), and otherwise equals \(3\) for order at least \(3\). Thus \(K_{1,2,2,2}\) is an explicit universal-vertex counterexample to the initiating paper's Theorem 3.6.
