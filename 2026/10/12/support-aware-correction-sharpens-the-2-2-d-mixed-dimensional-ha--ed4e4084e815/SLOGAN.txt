For \(\mathbb C^2\otimes\mathbb C^2\otimes\mathbb C^d\) with \(d\ge5\) and dimensional distance \(5\), the full two-qubit operator algebra is guaranteed correctable, so the non-degenerate Hamming count is \(16K\le4d\), not the example's \(7K\le4d\). This removes the example-specific numerical gap used to infer forced impurity from the Hamming and pure-Singleton bounds.
