All group Rota–Baxter operators on odd exponent-\(p\) extraspecial groups of order \(p^{2n+1}\), \(n\ge2\), admit a unique symplectic-matrix parametrization. The two exceptional actions \(0\) and \(-1\) on the center have identical, exactly counted isotropic-rank spectra, and the balanced-rank locus for every other center action consists precisely of complementary Lagrangian projections.
