For even-cycle edge evaluation codes of dimension \(2k\), every generalized Hamming weight from \(r=k\) through \(2k\) has an explicit formula. Every minimum-support subcode in the nontrivial range is classified and counted by partitions of the cycle edges whose odd–even occupancies balance modulo \(q-1\), with a simpler closed multinomial formula for \(q\geq k+2\).
