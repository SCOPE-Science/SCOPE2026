# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Any nontrivial tensor factor of a split local algebra is again split local, and the radical of B⊗C is J(B)⊗C+B⊗J(C); quotienting by its square gives e(B⊗C)=e(B)+e(C). Since e(A)=2 and every nontrivial split local factor has e≥1, a nontrivial decomposition has e(B)=e(C)=1. For split local R with e=1, nilpotent J and a generator x of J/J² ensure every nonzero layer J^j/J^{j+1} is generated by x^j and has dimension exactly one, hence R≅k[t]/(t^m). Tensoring two such algebras gives the rectangular quotient; uniqueness of unordered irreducible factors follows from Sakurai Theorem A, with no characteristic restriction. Independent algebraic boundary checks: e=2 but k[x,y]/(x,y)² has dimension 3 so cannot be a tensor product of factors each dimension at least 2, whereas k[u,v]/(u²,v³) has Hilbert series 1+2t+2t²+t³. No numerical tests are offered as an infinite proof.
