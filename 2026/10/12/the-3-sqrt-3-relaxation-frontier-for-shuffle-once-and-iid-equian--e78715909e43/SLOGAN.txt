For the three equiangular rows of a perfectly conditioned planar linear system, relaxed Kaczmarz with one row order reused every epoch beats IID row sampling in asymptotic expected squared error exactly when \(0<\eta<3-\sqrt3\); above that sharp threshold and below \(2\), IID is exponentially faster. At \(\eta=3/2\), the order already reverses between the first and second epochs.
