The \(81\) three-secants through the \(27\) sextactic points of the Fermat cubic are not projectively homogeneous: the full order-\(54\) plane automorphism group splits them into orbits of sizes \(9\), \(18\), and \(54\). The split is detected canonically by the doubling images in \(F[3]\): a point, a coset of the complex-multiplication fixed subgroup, or a coset in one of the other three directions.
