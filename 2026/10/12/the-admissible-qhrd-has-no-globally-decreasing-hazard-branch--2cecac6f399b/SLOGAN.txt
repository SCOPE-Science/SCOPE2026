Under the QHRD admissibility condition \(\beta\ge-2\sqrt{\alpha\lambda}\), setting \(\lambda=0\) forces \(\beta\ge0\), so a globally decreasing linear hazard is impossible. The 2026 paper's Figure 3 decreasing example \((\alpha,\beta,\lambda)=(3,-0.05,0)\) therefore lies outside the probability model: its hazard becomes negative after \(y=60\) and its CDF becomes negative after \(y=120\).
