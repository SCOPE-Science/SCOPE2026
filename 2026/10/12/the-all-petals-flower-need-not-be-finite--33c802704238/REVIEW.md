# Mathematical review

## Correctness

**PASS.** In the specified preprint version, Definition 8.2 defines simultaneous grafting for finite families of canonical sprouts, while the proof of Proposition 12.3 grafts all bounded petals up to ordinary isomorphism. An m-world complete reflexive modal cluster can map constantly to one reflexive canonical bud representative for every m≥1. It is a finite canonical sprout with zero strict modal height and width and an atomic complete label, so these examples remain eligible petals; distinct m give distinct ordinary isomorphism types. The finiteness needed for the displayed graft is therefore absent.

## Originality

**PASS, best of knowledge.** The paper's own Definitions 3.4, 7.1, 8.2, 8.4, 12.1 and Proposition 12.3 show the mismatch. Its Theorem 14.2 uses a coarser simulation representation by sets of cluster labels; it does not prove finiteness modulo ordinary isomorphism. No equivalent published diagnostic was identified in focused literature searches, though an unindexed erratum or revised preprint could change the comparison. A separate finite-model proof for IK4 does not address this specific construction.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The flower is a substantive step in the source proof's internal-defect resolution. The example identifies an exact obstruction and the structural target of a repair: bound cluster multiplicity or choose finitely many adequate reduced or simulation classes. It is not a refutation of the flower proposition or the finite model property of IK4.
