Bounded petal height and width do not bound modal-cluster multiplicity: the all-petals family used in Proposition 12.3 of arXiv:2609.27078v2 can contain pairwise nonisomorphic \(m\)-point reflexive clusters for every \(m\ge1\). Because the paper defines simultaneous grafting only for finite families, the displayed flower construction needs an additional finite quotient or selection argument; this diagnoses that proof step without disputing \(\mathsf{IK4}\)'s independently known finite model property.
