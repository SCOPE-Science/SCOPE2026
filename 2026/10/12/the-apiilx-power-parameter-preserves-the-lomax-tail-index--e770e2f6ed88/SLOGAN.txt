The APIILx alpha-power parameter changes the tail constant but leaves the Lomax tail exponent \(\theta\) unchanged: \(S(x)\sim C_\alpha\lambda^\theta x^{-\theta}\). Thus \(\mathbb E[X^q]\) is finite exactly for \(q<\theta\), and \(\mathbb E[e^{tX}]=\infty\) for every \(t>0\); the printed moment and MGF formulas therefore need explicit domain restrictions.
