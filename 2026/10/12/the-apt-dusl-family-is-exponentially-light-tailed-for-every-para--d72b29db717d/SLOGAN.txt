The APT-DUSL survival always satisfies \(1-F(x)\sim K_{\alpha,\xi}x e^{-\xi x}\), so its MGF is finite exactly for \(t<\xi\) and its hazard tends to \(\xi\). The alpha-power parameter changes the tail constant and pre-asymptotic shape, but it cannot create a heavy tail or change the asymptotic exponential rate.
