Iwasaki's canonical class-\((4,2)\) quartic deformation in \(\operatorname{Bl}_{\Lambda}\mathbf P^3\) stays unramified for every parameter and jumps from \(\mathcal O(6)^{\oplus2}\) to \(\mathcal O(5)\oplus\mathcal O(7)\) exactly at \(\varepsilon=0\) and \(\varepsilon=1/4\). The pullback of the jumping divisor is the reduced degree-two divisor \(V(\varepsilon(1-4\varepsilon))\).
