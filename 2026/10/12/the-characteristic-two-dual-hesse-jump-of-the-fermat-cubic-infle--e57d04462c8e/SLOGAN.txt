The fixed Fermat cubic with its nine flex tangents has 39 geometric singular points in every characteristic at least five, but only 21 in characteristic two: its nine lines jump to the classical dual-Hesse twelve-triple configuration. For every prime other than three, the number of rational singular points is \(7\), \(9\), or \(39\), according as the prime is two, is odd and congruent to two modulo three, or is congruent to one modulo three.
