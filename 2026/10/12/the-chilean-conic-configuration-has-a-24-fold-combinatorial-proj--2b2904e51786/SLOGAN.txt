The Chilean \((12_6,9_8)\) point–conic incidence structure has full abstract symmetry group \(\operatorname{AGL}(2,3)\) of order \(432\), but only the order-\(18\) kernel \(G_{18}\) is realized projectively. The quotient \(S_4\) is exactly the permutation group of the four conic triples that are recoverable from incidence as the pairs sharing only three base points.
