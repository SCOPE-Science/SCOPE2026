For independently phase-damped one-axis-twisted qubits, visibility \(\eta_n=c n^{-1/5}\) and twist \(\tau_n=a n^{-3/5}\) yield the exact limiting Wineland squeezing \(c^{-4}a^{-2}+a^4/(6c^2)\). Within this critical-time family the sharp limiting metrological threshold is \(c>(3^{2/3}/2)^{3/10}\); no unrestricted time-optimality is asserted.
