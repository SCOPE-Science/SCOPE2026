On an SPD quadratic, the published discrete PID optimizer is exactly heavy ball with mode-dependent momentum \(\beta(\lambda)=\alpha+K_d(1-\alpha)\lambda\). Its all-mode stability requires \(K_dL<1\); at or above that derivative-gain ceiling, no positive learning rate can stabilize the highest-curvature mode, and the best possible isolated-mode rate floor is \(\sqrt{\beta(\lambda)}\).
