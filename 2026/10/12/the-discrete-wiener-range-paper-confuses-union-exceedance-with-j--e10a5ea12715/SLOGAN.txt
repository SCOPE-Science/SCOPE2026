For independent Wiener-range coordinates, \(1-F_{\rm joint}\) is the probability that at least one range exceeds its threshold, not that all ranges do. The correct co-exceedance survival is the product of marginal survivals; for two identical margins with tail \(s\), the paper's quantity is \(2s-s^2\) instead of \(s^2\), an unbounded relative overstatement in the far tail.
