# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. A binary-separated diagonal one-parameter subgroup has one fixed point for each dual Plücker coordinate. For every \(k\)-subset \(I\), the assumptions \(k\ge2\) and \(n-k\ge2\) permit a \(k\)-subset \(J\) with
\[
|I\cap J|=k-2.
\]
At the coordinate point \([e_J]\), the affine tangent cone to the Grassmannian is
\[
\bigwedge^{k-1}J\wedge\mathbb C^n,
\]
so it is spanned by coordinates \(e_K\) with \(|K\cap J|\ge k-1\). Therefore \(p_I\) vanishes on the tangent space, making the coordinate hyperplane \(p_I=0\) tangent.

All ambient fixed points consequently lie on the dual. Euler localization gives
\[
\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(X^\vee)=\binom nk.
\]
The bundled checker exhaustively replays the subset/tangent condition and coordinate-weight separation for \(4\le n\le14\).

## Originality
PASS. The direct archival literature inspected here studies fundamental groups of complements, hypersurface status, degrees, singularities, and normality of Grassmannian dual varieties. Targeted full-text searches in the direct singularity source found no Euler or fixed-point statement, and broader searches for Euler, Poincaré, Pfaffian, CSM, and torus-fixed formulations did not locate an equivalent formula.

An analogous Euler computation for dual Segre varieties is a different family and does not imply the Plücker tangent criterion. Residual risk remains because the fixed-point argument is short and may be folklore.

## Value
PASS. Projective duals of Grassmannians are classical singular discriminantal varieties. The result computes a natural global topological invariant uniformly for every nonprojective Plücker Grassmannian and shows that the dual, the original Grassmannian, and the ambient dual projective space have the same Euler characteristic. The vanishing Euler characteristic of the complement is an immediate global consequence.
