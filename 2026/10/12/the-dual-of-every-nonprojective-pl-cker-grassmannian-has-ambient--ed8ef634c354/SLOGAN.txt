For every nonprojective Plücker Grassmannian \(X=\operatorname{Gr}(k,n)\), the projective dual has \(\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(X^\vee)=\binom nk\), exactly the Euler characteristic of both \(X\) and the ambient dual projective space. A generic diagonal torus action leaves every coordinate hyperplane fixed, and each one is tangent to a coordinate Grassmannian point obtained by replacing two indices.
