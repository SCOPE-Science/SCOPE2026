For the binary quadratic Reed–Muller family \(\operatorname{RM}(2,m)\) in increasing binary-integer coordinate order, every cyclic window width \(5\cdot2^m/8<b\le3\cdot2^m/4\) has exactly eight nonzero codewords with nonfull window weight, each of weight \(b+2^{m-2}-1\). All remaining nonzero words have full window weight; for larger \(b\) every nonzero word has full weight.
