Every unital algebra endomorphism of a radical-square-zero directed-cycle Nakayama algebra is uniquely a vertex coloring plus independent idempotent-lift and forward-edge scalars. This gives the complete finite-field linear-rank polynomial and the closed total count \(\bigl(q^2+(n-2)q+1\bigr)^n+(q-1)^n\bigl[n(q^n+(-1)^n)-(q-1)^n\bigr]\), and asymptotically almost all these maps are automorphisms at fixed cycle length.
