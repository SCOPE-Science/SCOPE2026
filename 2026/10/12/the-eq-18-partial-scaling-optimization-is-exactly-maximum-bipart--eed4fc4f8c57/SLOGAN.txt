For every finite-dimensional multipartite state \(\rho\), the Lupo–Man’ko–Marmo–Sudarshan Eq. (18) tensor-product partial-scaling trace-norm optimization is attained at a cube vertex; when all but one party are scaled, its value is exactly twice the largest bipartition negativity. Therefore the continuous scaling parameters add no entanglement-detection power beyond the corresponding ordinary PPT tests.
