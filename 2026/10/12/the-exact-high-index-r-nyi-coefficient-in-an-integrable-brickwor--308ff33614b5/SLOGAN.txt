In a four-state integrable brickwork circuit with square-root von Neumann operator entanglement, every fixed Rényi order \(\alpha>1\) has the exact logarithmic law \(S_\alpha(t)=\frac{\alpha-1/2}{\alpha-1}\log t+O_\alpha(1)\). The same coefficient holds for its companion product-state domain-wall quench, by difference-diagonal Schatten bounds and count-sector pinching.
