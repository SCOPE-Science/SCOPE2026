# Scientific review — WDLE modal staircase

## Correctness — PASS

Starting directly from the parent survival/PMF in Alharbi–Bengalath–Abd El-Bar (2026), geometric summation yields \(f(r)=Kq^r(b+r)\) and the exact adjacent comparison \(f(r+1)>f(r)\) if and only if \(r<H_m(\varphi)\). The proof reconstructs the normalization, handles \(m=2\), derives strict global decrease of \(H_m\) from explicit hyperbolic inequalities, proves its two endpoint limits, and treats equal-mass boundary cases. Therefore all quantifiers and the sharp fixed-origin modal ceiling follow analytically. Executing the included checker verifies independently computed mass sums and finite examples. The checker is not used to promote experiments to an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with bounded priority risk

The 2026 primary paper already states the WDLE mass and its mixture form. Its reviewed construction, moments, figures, estimation and angular-origin analysis do not state the universal strict modal staircase or all sharp transition shapes. Shakhatreh–Al-Mofleh (2024/2025) discuss unimodality for a different weighted-geometric family; Srihari–Girija–Rao (2018) describe a different discrete wrapped exponential law. General unimodality or the shared adjacent-ratio method does not imply the particular WDLE transition classification without the shape-specific monotonicity proof. Source-title, equivalent formulation, source-parameter, and broader weighted-geometric searches found no covering statement. An inaccessible complete publisher PDF/possible unindexed treatment remains a genuine priority risk, not evidence that the entire literature was exhaustively cleared.

## Value — PASS

A mode is a directly interpretable directional category. The source applies a one-parameter wrapped model to animal orientations and documents sensitivity to the chosen angular origin, with no independently fitted location parameter. The theorem establishes a sharp and complete restriction on which fixed-origin sectors can become most probable, the precise order in which modes shift, and every critical parameter. That is more informative for interpretation, design and diagnosis of this concrete model than generic unimodality or a finite numerical scan. No general methodological superiority or conclusion about freely rotated models is claimed.
