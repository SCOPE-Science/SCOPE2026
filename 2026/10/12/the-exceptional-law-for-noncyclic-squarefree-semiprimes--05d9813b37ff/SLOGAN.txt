Among squarefree semiprimes, failure to be a cyclic group order has the sharp asymptotic \(E(x)\sim\mathcal A x/\log x\), where \(\mathcal A=\sum_p1/(p(p-1))=0.7731566\ldots\). Consequently the exceptional proportion is asymptotic to \(\mathcal A/\log\log x\), so almost every squarefree semiprime is cyclic.
