The expectile profile order \(\le_{s_2}\) does not imply the mean/MAD stop-loss skewness order \(\le_{sf}\), even for atomless distributions with interval support. An exact rational finite-support seed has \(s_{2,X}(\alpha)<s_{2,Y}(\alpha)\) for every \(0<\alpha<1/2\) but reverses the \(sf\) inequality at \(t=4/5\), and the strict separation survives smoothing.
