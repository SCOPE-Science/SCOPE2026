Direct algebra gives strictly positive gaps \(T(0)-T(1)\) and \(T(2)-T(1)\) for every integer \(p\ge2\); the Fano lines attain all 21 pairwise minima. The converse forces a \(2\!-(7,3,1)\) design and its unique isomorphism type. ## Value — PASS

The exact global optimization answers the natural first seven-point case of an explicitly proposed subsystem-incidence inverse problem. It identifies not just a low-variance example but all minimizing designs and quantifies a \(44.6753\%\) variance penalty for the cyclic design in the qubit case.
