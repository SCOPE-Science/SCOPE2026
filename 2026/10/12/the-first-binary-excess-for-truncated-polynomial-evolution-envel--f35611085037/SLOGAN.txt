The minimum evolution-envelope dimensions of the binary truncated-polynomial algebras \(\mathbb F_2[t]/(t^n)\) for \(n=1,2,3,4\) are exactly \(1,3,5,8\). The fourth case is the first to exceed \(2n-1\); an explicit eight-term symmetric bilinear decomposition and an exhaustive 6,435-case obstruction prove optimality.
