For the order-\(k\) Poisson law, the first change of global mode is from \(0\) to \(k\) for every \(2\le k\le14\), but for \(k=15\) it jumps directly from \(0\) to \(25\). Exact rational coefficients and a uniform infinite-tail bound prove fifteen is the smallest order where the proposed \(0\)-then-\(k\) rule fails.
