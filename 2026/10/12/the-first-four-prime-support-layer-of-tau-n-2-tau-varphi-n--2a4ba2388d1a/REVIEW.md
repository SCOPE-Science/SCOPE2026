# Scientific review: the first four-prime-support layer

## Correctness — PASS
The multiplicative partitions of 81 and the 2-adic lower bound give a universal minimal totient of 57600 for squarefree four-support solutions. Exhausting 33 eligible prime candidates yields 37 distinct strictly increasing four-prime products and a least product of 75361. The separately exhaustive sieve excludes all 11386 four-support integers below the exhibited witness, whether squarefree or not. Both certificates rely on exact integer arithmetic and deterministic primality/factorization rather than probability or timeout. Full unrestricted four-support classification is not claimed.

## Value — PASS
The focal article develops a natural classification by number of distinct prime factors. The first missing support size is four. The extremal answer identifies both its least integer and its sharp, non-arbitrarily selected smallest totient, then completes the entire corresponding 37-element fiber. This supplies a concrete first-support benchmark and a reproducible starting boundary for further Diophantine classifications; the contribution is an exact motivated invariant, not a claim to a general classification or a mere isolated large computational example.
