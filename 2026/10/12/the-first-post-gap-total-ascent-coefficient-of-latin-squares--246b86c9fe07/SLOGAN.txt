For every order \(n\ge3\), the first non-extremal Latin Eulerian coefficient is exactly \(T_n(n+1)=n!\sum_{d\mid n,\,3d\le n}d\), equal by reversal to \(T_n((n-1)^2-2)\). Every square attaining the lower-boundary coefficient has a unique three-block cyclic-arc decomposition indexed by an admissible divisor and one residue class.
