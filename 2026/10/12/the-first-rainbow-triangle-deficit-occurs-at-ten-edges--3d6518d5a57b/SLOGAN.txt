For finite simple graphs with \(m\) edges and three available edge colors, the maximum number of rainbow triangles at budgets \(0\le m\le11\) is \((0,0,0,1,1,2,4,4,5,7,7,8)\). The first strict loss against the uncolored Kruskal--Katona bound occurs at \(m=10\), where the optimum is seven rather than ten.
