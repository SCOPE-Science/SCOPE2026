For the new characteristic-\(5\) genus-\(2\) semistable fibration with four singular fibers, the generic Jacobian has exact Mordell–Weil group \(\mathbf Z/12\mathbf Z\). The order \(12\) is the last Smith invariant of the full vertical-divisor lattice inside the rank-\(14\) Picard lattice of the resolved rational surface.
