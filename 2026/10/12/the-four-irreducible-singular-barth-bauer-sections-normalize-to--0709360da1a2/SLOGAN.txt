The four geometrically irreducible singular members of Naskręcki's \(C_\alpha\) hyperplane-section family all normalize to the same genus-two curve \(y^2=x^6-5x^4-5x^2+1\). Its reduced geometric automorphism group is \(S_4\), so these four nodal quartics specialize to one highly symmetric genus-two moduli point.
