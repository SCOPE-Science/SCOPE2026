For Tufo's Fano fourfold \(Y\cong\operatorname{Bl}_S(\mathbf P^2\times\mathbf P^2)\), the free middle cup-product lattice is exactly \(I_{11,2}\). The blow-up pairing splits it as \(U\oplus\langle1\rangle\) from the ambient product plus the negative Enriques lattice, giving rank \(13\), odd unimodularity, and signature \((11,2)\).
