For the unique-point Hermitian-relative curve \(C_\eta\) over \(\mathbb F_{s^2}\), every field-defined abstract automorphism is an explicit triangular matrix and the complete group is a Heisenberg semidirect product of order \(s^3(s+1)\). Its normal order-\(s^3\) subgroup gives a one-branch-point wild Galois cover, with invariant \(X^{s^2}-X\).
