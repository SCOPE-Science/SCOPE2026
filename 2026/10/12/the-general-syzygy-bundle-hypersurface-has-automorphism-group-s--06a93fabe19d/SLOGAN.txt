For the general Bunnett--Namanya syzygy-bundle threefold \(X_W\subset\mathbf P^2\times\mathbf P^3\), the finite automorphism group is not merely discrete: it is exactly \(S_4\). The four base points of the annihilator conic pencil form a projective frame, so their \(24\) permutations give all automorphisms.
