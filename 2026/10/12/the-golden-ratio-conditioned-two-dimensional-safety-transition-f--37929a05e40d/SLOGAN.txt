For the Gaussian projected-gradient control-variate step on SPD quadratics, the exact one-step expected-objective safety discount is \(\frac54-\frac{5-\sqrt5}{8}(1-1/\kappa)\) in dimension two but \(1+1/(2d\kappa)\) in every higher dimension. Consequently, using the undiscounted oracle step is universally safe in dimension two exactly at condition numbers \(\kappa\ge(5+\sqrt5)/2\), but at no finite condition number in dimensions at least three; positive finite-difference radius adds an exact nonnegative noise floor.
