The recruited-immune delay virus model's infection-free equilibrium is globally asymptotically stable on the closed threshold \(\mathcal R_0\le1\), extending the source's strict \(\mathcal R_0<1\) theorem. At equality the infection linearization has a simple zero characteristic root, so the result is a genuinely nonhyperbolic Lyapunov-LaSalle endpoint theorem rather than a strict-stability limit.
