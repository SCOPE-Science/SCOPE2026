The inverted ZLindley mean-inactivity formula has the wrong sign on its exponential-integral term: \(E_1(\alpha/t)=-\operatorname{Ei}(-\alpha/t)\), not \(+\operatorname{Ei}(-\alpha/t)\). The printed expression is negative for all sufficiently small \(t\), while the true mean inactivity time satisfies \(m(t)\sim t^2/\alpha>0\).
