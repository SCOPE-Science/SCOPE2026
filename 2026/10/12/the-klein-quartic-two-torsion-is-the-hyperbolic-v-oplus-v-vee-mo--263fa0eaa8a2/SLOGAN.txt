For Klein’s quartic, the two-torsion of the Jacobian is not merely a set with orbit sizes \(7,7,21,28\): as an automorphism-group module it is \(V\oplus V^\vee\), with Weil pairing given by evaluation. The four nonzero orbits are exactly the two coordinate orbits and the two mixed evaluation strata, and their full addition fusion law is explicit.
