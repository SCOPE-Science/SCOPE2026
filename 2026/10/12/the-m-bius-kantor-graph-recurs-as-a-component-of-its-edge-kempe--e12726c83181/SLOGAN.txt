For the Möbius–Kantor graph \(G=GP(8,3)\), the simple Kempe-switch reconfiguration graph of proper three-edge-colorings **modulo color permutations** has the exact form \(\mathcal K(G)\cong G\sqcup K_{1,3}\). An explicit finite isomorphism certificate identifies the sixteen-coloring component with its own base graph; the other four colorings form a three-leaf star.
