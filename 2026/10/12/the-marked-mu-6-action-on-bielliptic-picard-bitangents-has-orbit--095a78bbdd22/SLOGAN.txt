For a smooth marked bielliptic Picard quartic, the 28 bitangents split under the distinguished \(\mu_6\)-action as \(1+3+4\cdot6\): the line at infinity is fixed, the three horizontal bitangents form one three-cycle, and the other 24 bitangents are free. The 12 nontrivial \(\tau\)-pairs therefore become four free three-cycles on \(A[2]\setminus A[\widehat\lambda]\).
