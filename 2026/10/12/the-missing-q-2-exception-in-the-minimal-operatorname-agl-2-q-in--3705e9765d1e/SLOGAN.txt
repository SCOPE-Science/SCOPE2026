In the \(q=2\) specialization of the Borges--Korchmáros--Speziali \(\operatorname{AGL}(2,q)\) pencil, \(\lambda=1\) is the unique non-irreducible member: it splits into two smooth \(\mathbb F_4\)-conjugate conics meeting transversely in four points. Every \(\lambda\ne1\) member is smooth and geometrically irreducible.
