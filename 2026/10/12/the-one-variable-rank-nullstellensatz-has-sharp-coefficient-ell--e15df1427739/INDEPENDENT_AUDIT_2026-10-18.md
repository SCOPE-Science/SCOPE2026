## correctness

In K[x], the ideal generated by nonzero f_i is (d) for monic gcd d. If d divides g, write g=h sum_i a_i f_i and apply rank inequalities for every n and matrix X to bound rank g(X)<=ell max_i rank f_i(X). Conversely if d does not divide g, choose a root lambda whose multiplicity in d exceeds that in g; its Jordan block J_a(lambda) annihilates each f_i yet not g. For sharpness take distinct lambda_1,...,lambda_ell, f_i=product_(j != i)(x-lambda_j), g=1 and X diagonal of those scalars; ranks are 1 for every f_i and ell for g. The all-zero generator case reduces to g=0. Independently checked diagonal sharpness for ell<=5; the general proof is algebraic, not finite checking.
