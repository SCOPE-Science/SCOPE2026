# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For smooth projective geometrically connected curves of genus g≥2 over algebraically closed characteristic p≥3 admitting a cyclic wild subgroup of exact order N in {2g,2g−1}, Theorem 4.8 of Kazemifard–Tafazolian supplies eight exhaustive normal forms (cited premise, not reproved). For five Artin–Schreier covers of P¹ with r=2,1,4,5,3 distinct poles, Deuring–Shafarevich γ(X)=pγ(base)+(p−1)(r−1) gives 2,0,6,8,8. For remaining three Kummer quotient bases, explicit function-field inverses reduce to v²=u³−u in char3, v²=u³−1 in char5, v²=u⁵−1 in char3. The elliptic Hasse coefficients vanish and the genus-two Hasse–Witt matrix [[0,0],[1,0]] has square zero, so ALL base p-ranks are 0; each Artin–Schreier extension has a single reduced pole and hence rank0. All eight ranks are thus 2,0,6,0,0,0,8,8 in listed order, each either 0 or genus. The char3 genus6 order12 pair has distinct intrinsic rank, so cannot be isomorphic. Independently checked three Kummer identities, two elliptic Hasse coefficients, nilpotent genus-two matrix, eight genus/rank formulas. No claim of Newton polygons or reclassifying the eight cases.

Pries–Zhu and Booher–Cais supply general Deuring–Shafarevich formulas, so once input equations and quotient ranks are known many rows become direct applications; five rational-base rows are immediate. The positive-genus quotient identifications and stable Cartier calculations, the eight-row invariant package and separation of the two genus6 N12 families are the distinct result. ## value

PASS

Geometric p-rank is a natural intrinsic Jacobian invariant for a complete, finite, geometrically motivated list of wild cyclic boundary curves; the exact ordinary-versus-zero dichotomy constrains arithmetic moduli behavior and distinguishes two families with the same (p,N,g). The genus-two stable-rank calculation avoids the common error of reading raw Hasse–Witt matrix rank 1 as p-rank 1. Although not a new global curve classification, this is a complete unknown natural invariant for a published classified locus, worthwhile independent of a downstream theorem.
