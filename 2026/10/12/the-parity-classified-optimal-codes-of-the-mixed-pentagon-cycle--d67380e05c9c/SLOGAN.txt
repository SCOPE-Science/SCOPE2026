For every \(n\ge6\), every optimal zero-error code for the mixed pentagon–cycle channel \(C_5\boxtimes C_n\) is either a cyclic walk code with one word per column or, when \(n\) is even, an alternating-column code with independent pairs. Their exact labeled count is \(5\sum_{2k\equiv n\ (\mathrm{mod}\ 5)}\binom nk+\mathbf1_{2\mid n}\,2\cdot5^{n/2}\), exhibiting different exponential growth rates for even and odd \(n\).
