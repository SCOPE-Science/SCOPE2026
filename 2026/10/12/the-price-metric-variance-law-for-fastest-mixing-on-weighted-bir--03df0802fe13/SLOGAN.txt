For every finite reversible birth-death chain with prescribed positive stationary masses and arbitrary positive edge prices, the unique unit-budget conductance allocation maximizing the continuous-time spectral gap is explicit: the optimum is the reciprocal of the stationary-mass variance of the path positions separated by \(\sqrt{c_i}\), and each optimal conductance is a weighted cumulative flux divided by that variance and \(\sqrt{c_i}\). A strict sum-of-squares certificate proves global optimality and simplicity, with all optimal rates strictly positive and computable in linear time.
