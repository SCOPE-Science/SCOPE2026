The 2026 Gompertz–Lindley competing-risks paper's printed posterior is not the posterior of its own model: it is missing the parameter-dependent factor \(\alpha^{4(n-m)}(1+\alpha)^{2m}\). Its expanded classical log-likelihood is consistent, but a Metropolis–Hastings or Laplace calculation that literally targets Eq. (4.2) targets a different Bayesian model.
