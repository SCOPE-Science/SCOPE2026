The 2026 PTS-XL count law is exactly the sum of two iid Poisson XLindley counts with the same parameter, so its PGF is the square of the PXL PGF and its Fano factor is unchanged by the two-sum construction. It is strictly overdispersed for every finite \(\theta>0\); the advertised equidispersion occurs only in the degenerate boundary \(\theta\to\infty\), where the mean tends to zero.
