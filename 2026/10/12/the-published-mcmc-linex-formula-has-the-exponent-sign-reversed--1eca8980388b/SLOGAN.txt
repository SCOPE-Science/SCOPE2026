The paper’s theoretical LINEX estimator correctly uses \(e^{-h g}\), but its MCMC Eq. (4.11) reverses the exponent to \(e^{+h g}\) while keeping the prefactor \(-1/h\). With the paper’s main choice \(h=0.5\), the printed formula is negative for every positive parameter, survival, or hazard target and therefore cannot produce the reported small-MSE simulation tables.
