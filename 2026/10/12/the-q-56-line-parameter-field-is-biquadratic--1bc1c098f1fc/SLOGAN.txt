For Veniani's \(Q_{56}\) model, the field \(\mathbb Q(p)\) used to define all 56 lines is exactly \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt5,\sqrt{-3})\), with Galois group \(C_2\times C_2\). The source's surface field \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-15})\) is the fixed field of the relative involution \(p\mapsto-1/p\), which acts simultaneously by \(q\mapsto-1/q\).
