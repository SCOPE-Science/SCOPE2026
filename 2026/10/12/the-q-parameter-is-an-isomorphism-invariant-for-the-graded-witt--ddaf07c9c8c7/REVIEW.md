# Review of The \(q\)-parameter is an isomorphism invariant for the graded Witt \(q\)-pre-Lie family

## Correctness
PASS. The commutator of every \(S(q,\lambda)\) is exactly the Witt bracket, so an algebra isomorphism must lie in the complete published automorphism group of the Witt Lie algebra. Substitution of \(W_n\mapsto\varepsilon a^nW_{\varepsilon n}\) into the two products produces an identity affine in \(m,n\). Its \(m\)-coefficient gives \(q=q'\), its constant term gives \(\mu=\varepsilon\lambda\), and the remaining coefficient then agrees identically. The converse maps are explicit. The automorphism-group statement is the specialization \(\mu=\lambda\). The verifier checks the coefficient algebra exactly on a finite regression set; the proof, not the regression, supplies the infinite statement.

## Originality
PASS, with a recency risk. Lu--Hong Theorem 3.11 gives \(\lambda\sim-\lambda\) only after fixing a common \(q\). The inspected source does not compare underlying algebras belonging to different \(q\)-categories. The earlier anti-pre-Lie paper is the single slice \(q=-1\). published-finding corpus searches for cross-\(q\) isomorphism, global moduli, and recoverability of \(q\) found no equivalent or stronger statement. The cross-parameter rigidity is therefore not implied by the cited fixed-parameter classification without the additional Witt-automorphism comparison carried out here.

## Value
PASS. The recent paper introduces \(q\) as a deformation/scaling parameter and classifies a one-parameter Witt family separately for each \(q\). Determining whether different \(q\)'s describe genuinely different binary algebras is the natural global moduli question. The result shows that \(q\) is not presentation gauge: the fixed-\(q\) moduli spaces remain disjoint after forgetting the \(q\)-label, and the symmetry jump at \(\lambda=0\) identifies the unique orbifold-type stratum. This is a structural completion of the new classification rather than a numerical or cosmetic reformulation.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is Lu--Hong, arXiv:2607.08389v1, whose Theorem 3.8 supplies the family and whose Proposition 3.10/Theorem 3.11 solve the fixed-\(q\) isomorphism problem. Bai--Gao, arXiv:2507.09293v1, solves the analogous sign quotient in the special anti-pre-Lie slice. Bavula's 2016 theorem supplies the complete Witt Lie automorphism group. No claim is made for \(q=1\), \(q=0\), ungraded structures, or noncomplex ground fields. A later revision of the recent preprint could reduce the originality margin if it explicitly adds cross-\(q\) comparison.
