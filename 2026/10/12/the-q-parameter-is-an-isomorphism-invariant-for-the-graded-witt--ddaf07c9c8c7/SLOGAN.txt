Across the newly classified graded Witt \(q\)-pre-Lie family, the scaling parameter \(q\) is an intrinsic isomorphism invariant: \(S(q,\lambda)\cong S(q',\mu)\) exactly when \(q=q'\) and \(\mu=\pm\lambda\). The special point \(\lambda=0\) is also the unique stratum where the automorphism group enlarges from \(\mathbb C^\times\) to \(\mathbb C^\times\rtimes C_2\).
