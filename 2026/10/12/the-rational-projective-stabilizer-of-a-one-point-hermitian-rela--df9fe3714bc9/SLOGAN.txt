Every one-rational-point Hermitian-relative plane curve \(X^{s+1}+\eta Y^{s+1}+Y^sZ+YZ^s=0\), with \(\eta\in\mathbb F_{s^2}\setminus\mathbb F_s\), has full \(\mathbb F_{s^2}\)-rational projective stabilizer \(H_s\rtimes\mu_{s+1}\) of order \(s^3(s+1)\). Its nonabelian unipotent radical has order \(s^3\), and in the smallest case it is quaternionic.
