In the RPSL construction \(\psi=u g(\psi)\), one necessarily has \(\psi(0)=0\), so the paper's extra atom \(\Pr(X=0)=g(0)\) cannot belong to the stated Lagrange law. The positive coefficients already sum to one when \(g'(1)\le1\); when \(g'(1)>1\), they instead have the Galton-Watson extinction mass \(q<1\) and require conditioning to form a finite-count law.
