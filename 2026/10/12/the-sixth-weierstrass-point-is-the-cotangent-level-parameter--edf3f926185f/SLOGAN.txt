For every nondiscriminant cotangent-level direction \(e\) on a smooth degree-four del Pezzo surface, the associated Kummer K3 surface is the Kummer surface of the genus-two curve branched at the five pencil discriminant points together with \(e\) itself. Thus Kim--Lee's undetermined sixth Weierstrass point is exactly \(e\), and the level is \(\operatorname{Jac}(C_e)\setminus\operatorname{Jac}(C_e)[2]\).
