For the fixed power-sum integral model of Bring's curve, the only exceptional positive characteristics are \(2,3,5\): the first two fibers become smooth surfaces, whereas the characteristic-five fiber is exactly six rational concurrent lines with a single \(\delta=9\) singularity. Its full point count is \(6\cdot5^r+1\) over \(\mathbb F_{5^r}\), with zeta function \(1/((1-T)(1-5T)^6)\).
