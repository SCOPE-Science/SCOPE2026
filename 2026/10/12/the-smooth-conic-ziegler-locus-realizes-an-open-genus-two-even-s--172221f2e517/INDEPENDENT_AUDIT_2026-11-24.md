The inverse reconstruction is algebraic: on an ordered-line cover of this incidence stratum the concurrent triples are locally constant as incidence labels, their points are obtained by intersections, and the conic is the rank-five linear interpolation kernel. The subgroup H=(S3×S3)⋊C2 of S6 orders the same data on both sides; via the Veronese conic the common coarse quotient is the incidence-open subspace of M_{0,6}/H. For the double cover, 2W_i∼K, total ramification∼3K, and the theta characteristic of a triple is sum(W_i)−K, equal for the complementary triple. It is not effective: an effective degree-one theta is a Weierstrass point, but |K+W_i| consists of W_i plus a hyperelliptic fiber, never three distinct Weierstrass points. Distinct partitions can be oriented so the representative triples share two points, and equality of characteristics would imply equivalence of distinct degree-one points. This also verifies the classical dictionary using the six odd and sixteen total characteristics.
