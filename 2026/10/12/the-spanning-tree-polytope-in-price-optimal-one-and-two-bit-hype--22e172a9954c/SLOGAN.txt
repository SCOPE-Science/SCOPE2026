For the hypercube with one-bit and two-bit flips, priced at one and \(t\), the maximum continuous-time spectral gap changes formula exactly at \(t=2\). Below that threshold its entire translation-invariant optimal-design family is a degree-constrained fractional spanning-tree polytope on coordinate indices, containing both fully dense and Hamilton-path-sparse designs; above it the ordinary hypercube is the unique globally optimal network.
