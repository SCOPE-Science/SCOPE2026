On two fermionic modes, every physical state is already a convex combination of pure Gaussian states, so the paper's stated local convex-Gaussian channel property is automatically satisfied by every parity-superselection-preserving channel. Noiseless SWAP therefore gives a direct counterexample to Eq. (35): the local property holds while the paper's own formula gives \(C_+=1\) and \(C_-=0\).
