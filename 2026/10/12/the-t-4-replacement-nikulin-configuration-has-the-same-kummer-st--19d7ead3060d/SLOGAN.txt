For the generic \(t=4\) Kummer surface of Roulleau--Sarti, the four-curve replacement configuration is not a new Kummer structure. The genus-two class \(F=\frac12(L-A_1-A_2-A_3-A_4)\) defines a double-plane involution exchanging each \(A_i\) with its replacement \(B_i\), so the two Nikulin configurations are automorphic.
