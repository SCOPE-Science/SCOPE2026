The three nontrivial two-dimensional anti-Leibniz models printed in the original classification all reduce to the single algebra \(u^2=v\). The surviving parameter family is killed by the basis change \(f_1=e_1\), \(f_2=e_1^2\), and the canonical algebra has automorphism group \(\mathbf G_a\rtimes\mathbf G_m\).
