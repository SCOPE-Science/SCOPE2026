For scalar combinations of the three Hom-trialgebra products, universal Hom-associativity occurs only on the three coordinate axes and the signed line \(K(1,1,-1)\). Thus \(\dashv+\vdash+\perp\) is not universally Hom-associative in characteristic zero, while \(\dashv+\vdash-\perp\) is the unique nontrivial mixed universal direction.
