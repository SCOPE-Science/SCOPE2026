The UPGWD stress–strength gamma series is valid exactly when \(eta_2<eta_1\); for \(eta_2=eta_1\) its terms fail to vanish, and for \(eta_2>eta_1\) they grow without bound. The integral remains valid everywhere, and the symmetry \(R(eta_1,eta_2)+R(eta_2,eta_1)=1\) gives a convergent piecewise repair for every positive shape pair.
