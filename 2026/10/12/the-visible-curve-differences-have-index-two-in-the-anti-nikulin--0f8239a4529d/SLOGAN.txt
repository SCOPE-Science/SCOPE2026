For the generalized Inose quartic of Picard rank sixteen, the canonical two-torsion translation pairs the nineteen distinguished rational curves as nine explicit transpositions and one fixed curve \(a_7\). The nine curve-difference classes span the anti-invariant space but generate a determinant-\(2^{10}\) lattice, hence have exact index \(2\) in the Nikulin lattice \(E_8(-2)\).
