The sixty all-Weierstrass tritangents of Bring's curve turn its sixty Weierstrass points into the clique geometry of the arrangement graph \(A_{5,3}\). The type-preserving incidence symmetry is \(S_5\times S_3\): the geometric \(S_5\) is enlarged by the Galois \(S_3\) that permutes the three roots used to label the Weierstrass points.
