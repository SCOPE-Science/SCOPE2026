The pairwise rule \(J_x>J_y\Rightarrow D_x\) is the more effective equal-strength DM direction fails once the third exchange coupling is allowed to intervene. On the exact family \(J_x=0\), \(J_y=-a\), \(J_z=a\), \(D_x=D_y=a\), the \(D_x\) thermal concurrence vanishes for every \(T>0\), while the \(D_y\) concurrence is positive exactly for \(0<T<a/x_*\), where \(x_*\) is the unique positive root of \(e^{3x}-e^x-e^{\sqrt5x}-e^{-\sqrt5x}=0\).
