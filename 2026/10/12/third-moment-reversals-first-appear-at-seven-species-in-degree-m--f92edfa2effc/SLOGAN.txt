For three-plant coextinction networks with matching plant degrees, the difference of unstandardized third central moments always equals \(3(1-\mu)\) times their variance difference under the same exchangeable pollinator-loss law. With equal degrees on both guilds, a moment-order reversal first becomes possible at seven total species; two explicit seven-vertex trees cross at \(q\approx0.5436890127\) even though their variance order never changes.
