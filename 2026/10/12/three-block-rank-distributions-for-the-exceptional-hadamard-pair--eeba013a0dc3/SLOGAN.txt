For the exceptional families \(H^{1,1,t-4}\) and \(\bar H^{2,0,t-5}\), the complete two- and three-kernel-block rank distributions admit closed formulas for every \(t\ge5\). In particular, every three-block union has rank at most \(7\) in the first family, while the second has rank-\(8\) triples, resolving the third level of the hierarchy for this infinite pair.
