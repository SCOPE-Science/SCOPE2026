Symmetric Jacobi equilibration can never worsen the spectral condition number of a \(1\times1\) or \(2\times2\) SPD matrix, but three dimensions are enough for failure at every \(\kappa>1\). An explicit rank-one family has \(\kappa_2(A)=\kappa\) and a strictly larger equilibrated condition number, with relative inflation tending to \(4/3\).
