For optimally tuned Heavy-Ball on an SPD quadratic, two dimensions impose a sharp finite second-step objective ratio and stay stepwise monotone exactly through \(\kappa=9+4\sqrt5\). A single interior curvature in dimension three changes the picture completely: for every \(\kappa>1\), the first update can hit the minimizer while the second leaves it, and nearby problems have arbitrarily large \(f(x_2)/f(x_1)\).
