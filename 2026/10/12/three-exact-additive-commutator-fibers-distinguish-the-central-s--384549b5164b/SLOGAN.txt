For every prime power \(q\), the additive commutator \((X,Y)\mapsto XY-YX\) of strictly upper triangular \(4\times4\) matrices has exactly three fiber sizes: \(2q^9+q^8-2q^7\) at zero, \(2q^9-2q^7\) at nonzero central targets, and \(q^9-q^7\) at all other derived targets. Thus the nonzero fiber distribution alone detects the third lower-central layer: every nonzero central target occurs exactly twice as often as any noncentral derived target.
