# Three markers suffice for every finite affine-direction S5 frame

## Finding

Let \(q\) be any prime power, let \(d\ge 2\), and put \(W=\mathbb F_q^d\). For every one-dimensional linear subspace \(L\le W\), give \(W\) its own *named* equivalence relation
\[
 x\mathrel{R_L}y\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad y-x\in L,
\]
and let \(\Diamond_L U=\{x\in W:(x+L)\cap U\ne\varnothing\}\). These are ordinary existential \(\mathsf{S5}\) modalities; none counts successors. Write \(\langle P\rangle\) for the Boolean subalgebra of \(\mathcal P(W)\) generated by a single set \(P\) and closed under every \(\Diamond_L\), and define the minimum support
\[
 \mu(q,d)=\min\bigl\{|P|:\langle P\rangle=\mathcal P(W)\bigr\}.
\]

**Theorem.** For every prime power \(q\) and dimension \(d\ge2\),
\[
 \boxed{\mu(q,d)=\begin{cases}1,&q=2,\\3,&q>2.\end{cases}}
\]
When \(q=2\), *every singleton* generates. When \(q>2\), *every noncollinear three-point set* generates. For these generators, every singleton of \(W\) has a defining formula of modal nesting depth at most \(1\) when \(q=2\), and at most \(3\) when \(q>2\), independent of both \(q\) and \(d\).

In particular, if \(q>2\), at least
\[
 \boxed{\frac{q^d(q^d-1)(q^d-q)}{6}}
\]
distinct minimum-support valuations generate the full modal algebra: these are the noncollinear three-point sets. This is a guaranteed family, **not** asserted to exhaust all generating three-point valuations for larger fields.

## Assumptions and scope

All fields are finite, all affine directions are individually named, and there are no additional constants, nominals, graded counting operations, successor counts, or point labels except the single truth set \(P\). The signature includes Boolean operations and one unary modal operator for each one-dimensional \(\mathbb F_q\)-linear subspace of \(W\). There are \((q^d-1)/(q-1)\) such modalities. The result concerns a specified finite frame and its full powerset algebra, not the validity or axiomatization of the logic over all affine frames. Coordinate choices used in the proof are descriptions of field elements, not additional named propositions.

The requirement \(d\ge2\) is essential to the three-point noncollinearity construction. For an infinite field, defining every singleton would not alone imply generating the entire powerset by finite Boolean combinations, which is why finiteness is part of the theorem.

## Proof

**1. Automorphism obstruction and sharp lower bounds.** Every affine homothety \(T(x)=b+\lambda x\) with \(b\in W\) and \(0\ne\lambda\in\mathbb F_q\) preserves every *named* relation: if \(y-x\in L\), then \(T(y)-T(x)=\lambda(y-x)\in L\). If \(T(P)=P\), structural induction on modal formulas shows that the value of every formula built from \(P\) is invariant under \(T\). A nonidentity such automorphism therefore prevents \(\langle P\rangle\) from being the full powerset, since some singleton is not invariant.

An empty truth set is preserved by a nontrivial translation. A one-point set \(P=\{a\}\) is preserved when \(q>2\) by a nonidentity homothety about \(a\), namely \(x\mapsto a+\lambda(x-a)\) with \(\lambda\ne0,1\). Every two-point set \(P=\{a,b\}\) is preserved by \(x\mapsto a+b-x\), which interchanges \(a\) and \(b\), even in characteristic two. Thus \(\mu(q,d)\ge3\) for \(q>2\) and \(\mu(2,d)\ge1\).

**2. The two-element field.** Take \(P=\{a\}\). For any \(x\ne a\), the line \(a+\mathbb F_2(x-a)\) consists of exactly \(\{a,x\}\). Consequently
\[
 \{x\}=\Diamond_{\mathbb F_2(x-a)}P\setminus P.
\]
The singleton \(\{a\}\) is already \(P\). Thus all singletons are definable by modal depth at most one.

**3. Isolating a fourth point from an arbitrary triangle.** Suppose \(q>2\) and \(P=\{a,b,c\}\) is noncollinear. Set \(u=b-a\), \(v=c-a\). These vectors are linearly independent. Abbreviate \(D_u=\Diamond_{\mathbb F_qu}\), \(D_v=\Diamond_{\mathbb F_qv}\). The \(u\)-parallel lines meeting \(P\) are \(a+\mathbb F_qu\) and \(c+\mathbb F_qu\), and the \(v\)-parallel lines meeting \(P\) are \(a+\mathbb F_qv\) and \(b+\mathbb F_qv\). Independence gives the four unique pairwise intersections \(a,b,c,a+u+v\). Therefore
\[
 Q=(D_uP\cap D_vP)\setminus P=\{a+u+v\}.
\]
The new point defines two of the original marked points separately:
\[
 B=P\cap D_vQ=\{b\},\qquad
 C=P\cap D_uQ=\{c\},\qquad
 A=P\setminus(B\cup C)=\{a\}.
\]
The formulas for \(Q\) have depth one and those for \(A,B,C\) depth at most two.

**4. Defining an arbitrary point from two noncollinear anchors.** Let \(x\notin\{a,b,c\}\). At least one pair \(s,t\) among the three now definable anchors has \(s,t,x\) noncollinear. Indeed, if \(x\) lay on the three pair-lines through \(a,b,c\), it would equal one of their intersection vertices; this is excluded. The distinct affine lines through \(s,x\) and through \(t,x\) intersect exactly in \(x\). Hence
\[
 \{x\}=\Diamond_{\mathbb F_q(x-s)}\{s\}\ \cap\
        \Diamond_{\mathbb F_q(x-t)}\{t\}.
\]
The right side has modal depth at most three. Since \(W\) is finite, all subsets are finite unions of the definable singleton sets; therefore \(\langle P\rangle=\mathcal P(W)\).

**5. Counting the explicit optimal family.** Let \(N=q^d\). There are \(N(N-1)\) ordered choices of two distinct points. Their affine line contains exactly \(q\) points, leaving \(N-q\) choices of a third point off the line. Every noncollinear unordered three-point set is counted in six orders. The displayed number of explicit minimum-support generators follows. These constructions and the automorphism obstruction establish the exact minimum without classifying the remaining three-point supports.

## Verification

A standalone standard-library Python checker, `verify.py`, independently implements finite fields of orders \(2,3,4,5,7,8,9,11\), enumerates all one-dimensional direction relations, tests the geometric singleton formulas, and computes ordinary modal partition refinements without referring to the constructive proof. It additionally exhausts all valuations with at most three marked points on the planes over \(\mathbb F_3\) and \(\mathbb F_4\), and all valuations with at most one mark over \(\mathbb F_2^2\).

The reproducible output is:

```text
VERIFY_OK triangles=9 refinements=11 exhaustive_labelings=832 fields=11
```

In the two exhaustive three-marker plane cases, the number of generating supports agrees with the noncollinear count: \(72\) over \(\mathbb F_3^2\) and \(480\) over \(\mathbb F_4^2\). Further constructive and refinement checks include dimensions three, and the nonprime prime-power fields of orders \(4,8,9\). These finite checks test the implementation and small instances; the result for all fields and dimensions is established by the preceding proof.

## Relationship to prior work

The literature on spatial and vector-space modal languages provides the motivating framework. Van Benthem and Bezhanishvili study modal definability, dependence, and axiom systems for groups and vector spaces; their scalar-multiple modality and their modal-language operations differ from the present *individually named affine-direction equivalence operators*. The examined full preprint, including Sections 2, 5, and 6, does not state a minimum-support theorem for this frame family.

Earlier modal analyses of affine planes, including Hodkinson and Hussain's two-sorted point/line language with incidence and line parallelism, investigate axiomatizability rather than a complete one-sorted Boolean modal algebra generated by a sparse marking. Our result neither implies their non-finite axiomatizability theorem nor follows from its statement.

Related finite three-equivalence grid results establish generation from three markers in particular two-dimensional *cyclic* nets. That special-case framework neither supplies the dimension-uniform depth-three construction here nor covers noncyclic additive groups of nonprime finite fields in higher dimensions. No exact equivalent was located in the examined literature; absence from those sources does not establish global priority.

## Limitations

The relation signature is intentionally rich: it names **every** affine direction. The theorem does not assert anything analogous with a bounded fixed selection of directions, in dimension one, or with an unnamed relation aggregating directions. The lower bound exploits the scalar homothety automorphisms of Desarguesian affine spaces and need not hold in arbitrary non-Desarguesian affine planes. Depth three is a uniform upper bound, not a claim of optimal worst-case depth. The noncollinear-support formula gives a lower bound on the census of all minimum generators, except that the checker confirms equality in the stated small finite cases. Mathematical and priority review by independent experts remains outstanding.
