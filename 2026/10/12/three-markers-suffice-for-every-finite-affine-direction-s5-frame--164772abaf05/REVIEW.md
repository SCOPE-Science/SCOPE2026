# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The statement explicitly quantifies over all prime powers and dimensions at least two with every one-dimensional direction as a named ordinary S5 modality. The lower bound follows from valuation-preserving affine homotheties, including the characteristic-two translation that swaps a pair. The upper bound constructs a fourth singleton from an arbitrary noncollinear triple, isolates two original marks, and expresses each remaining world as an intersection of two different named directional lines. Finiteness then converts singleton definability into full Boolean-algebra generation. The bound on modal depth is obtained from the displayed formulas and is not inferred from experiments. `verify.py` independently checks finite-field arithmetic, relational modalities, explicit formulas, and small-support exhaustiveness in test cases. The uniform proof does not rely on those tests.

## Originality — PASS, with residual literature risk

The closest inspected contemporary source is van Benthem and Bezhanishvili's 2022 preprint on modal structures in groups and vector spaces (ILLC PP-2022-03; later Journal of Logic and Computation). It studies scalar multiples, group operations, and enriched modal languages, but the present one-sorted frame with a separate S5 modality for **every affine direction**, its minimum size of a one-predicate generator, and uniform depth-three construction were not stated in the inspected complete preprint. Hodkinson and Hussain's earlier two-sorted affine-plane axiomatization has a different signature and concerns non-finite axiomatizability. Prior cyclic two-dimensional three-equivalence grid generation results overlap in particular prime-sized plane examples but do not imply the extension to all dimensions and nonprime fields or the constant-depth bound. Priority in older affine geometry, modal algebra, or epistemic-grid literature is not ruled out globally.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The minimum support needed to generate the full powerset under named line-equivalence modalities is a natural, exact observability invariant, and its value is independent of dimension despite the exponentially growing state space. The result pairs a uniform sharp obstruction with explicit depth-three formulas; it highlights how naming the full set of affine directions changes generation compared with restricted two- or three-direction epistemic nets. The constructive family contains exactly \(q^d(q^d-1)(q^d-q)/6\) noncollinear three-point supports, giving a useful certified family for testing finite-modal refinements. Neither a complete census of other three-point supports nor optimality of the depth bound is asserted.

## Limits and risks

The language is stronger than fixed-modal-signature treatments of affine planes; the finding should not be transferred to those languages. The proof needs Desarguesian finite affine spaces for the scalar-homothety lower bound. An equivalent special case may appear implicitly in older geometric modal-logic or finite algebra literature. Independent mathematical and originality audits have not been carried out.
