In \(\mathbb F_q^d\), \(d\ge2\), equip every affine line direction with its own ordinary \(\mathsf{S5}\) modality. Exactly one marked point is necessary and sufficient to generate the whole Boolean modal algebra when \(q=2\); exactly three are necessary and sufficient when \(q>2\), and every noncollinear triple works, with all points definable at modal depth at most three.
