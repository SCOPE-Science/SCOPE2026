Among every triple of qubit projective measurements, the pairwise mutually unbiased triple is uniquely extremal for both state-optimized Shannon bounds: it maximizes \(B_{\min}\) at \(2\log2/3\) and minimizes \(B_{\max}\) at \(h((1+1/\sqrt3)/2)\). A Bloch-sphere sign-vector argument and strict entropy concavity prove the full \(N=2\), \(L=3\) case of the published certainty conjecture.
