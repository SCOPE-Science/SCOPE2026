Every maximum edge open packing of the two-rail ladder \(L_n=P_2\square P_n\), for \(n\ge2\), comes from exactly one checkerboard class and a partition of its rung indices into centered intervals of length two or three. Thus \(\rho_e^o(L_n)=n\), and the number of distinct maximizing edge sets is exactly \(4\) when \(n\equiv2\pmod3\) and \(2\) otherwise.
