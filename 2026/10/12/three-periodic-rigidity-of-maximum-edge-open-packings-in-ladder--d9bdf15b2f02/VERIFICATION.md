# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

An induced disjoint star forest with m selected edges has m pairwise independent leaves, at most one per rung; thus m≤n. Equality forces one of two alternating checkerboard sets of n leaves, whose complementary prospective centers a_j have full B-neighborhood I_j=[j−1,j+1] cropped to [1,n]. All selected edges at a center are forced by induced-star condition; equality packings biject with indexed interval partitions. Boundary intervals of length two and interior length three force one tiling for residues 0,1 and two for residue2, and the two leaf classes give disjoint edge sets (otherwise all vertices present, impossible star forest since ladder has cycle). Independently enumerated induced isolate-free star forests for n=2..8: maxima n and counts 4,2,2,4,2,2,4. This is finite corroboration, not proof of all n.
