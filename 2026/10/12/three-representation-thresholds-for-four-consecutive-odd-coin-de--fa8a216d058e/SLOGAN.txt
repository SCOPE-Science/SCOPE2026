The next representation layer after the classical two-representation threshold is explicit for every fixed four-term consecutive-odd progression \(\{a,a+2,a+4,a+6\}\) with odd \(a\ge9\): the largest integer with at most two coin representations and the complete three-representation Apéry set have closed formulas. The extremal residue is uniquely \(2\pmod a\) for \(a\equiv1\pmod3\), and \(4\pmod a\) otherwise.
