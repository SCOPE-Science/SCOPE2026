# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof uses exact global genus defect and the source's proven irreducibility, normalization, central node, sign symmetry, and singular-point classification. In each three-point case the symmetric pair must contribute \(\delta=2\) per point. Formal splitting plus parity determines \(A_3\); the discarded infinity alternative would instead yield \(A_2\), contradicting the same exact defect. The packaged checker verifies the coefficient identities and the published finite-field Case-II example.

## Originality
PASS. The primary source gives only multiplicity-two points, and in the Case-III local calculation it proves only an exponent \(\alpha\ge4\), with tacnode mentioned as an example rather than established. Its infinity criterion retains two coefficient systems. The direct companion paper restates only the count and multiplicity for this family. Exact and alias searches did not find the basket \(A_1+2A_3\) or the exclusion of the triple-root system. Residual risk remains from older differently normalized literature and an in-preparation related work.

## Value
PASS. The exact ADE type is a natural invariant of the nongeneric three-singularity stratum, and eliminating the unrealizable coefficient branch clarifies the geometry of the source's singularity criterion. This sharpens resolution and degeneration data without relying on a finite census.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is Moriya--Kudo, arXiv:2310.15993v1, especially Lemmas 4.2.2 and 4.2.4 and Proposition 4.2.6. Kudo's companion arXiv:2401.00760v1 is the closest later treatment. The claim is restricted to characteristic \(0\) or at least \(7\) and to the three-singularity cases; it does not classify the generic five-singularity case.
