# Verification

The claim is proved deductively, with no finite experiment standing in for an infinite assertion.

For the descent step, the initiating source's dimension-at-least-three proof contracts every \((-1)\)-hypersurface and descends automorphisms. A complex abelian variety contains no rational curves, hence no \((-1)\)-hypersurface; on its point blow-up the only such hypersurfaces are the exceptional divisors, so the contraction is the original blow-down.

For the stabilizer step, an automorphism is uniquely \(x\mapsto g(x)+a\), with \(g\) a group automorphism. The group of possible \(g\)'s is countable because \(\operatorname{End}(A)\) is a finite-rank free abelian group. For fixed \(g\) and \(\sigma\in S_3\), the stabilizer equations are two homomorphism equations in \(A^3\). Their common kernel is all of \(A^3\) only when \(\sigma=1\) and \(g=1\); otherwise it is proper closed. A very general triple avoids the countable union of these loci.

The lower bound is exact: \(x\mapsto2p-x\) fixes one center point, and \(x\mapsto p+q-x\) swaps two distinct center points, so both lift to the corresponding blow-ups.
