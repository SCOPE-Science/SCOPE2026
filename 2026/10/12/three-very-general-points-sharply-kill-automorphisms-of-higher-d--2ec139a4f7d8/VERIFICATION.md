# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The final claim is over C, abelian varieties of dimension d>=3, and very general triples (outside a countable union of proper closed subsets). Every morphism P^{d-1}->A is constant because abelian varieties have no rational curves. Thus exceptional divisors are the only P^{d-1} exceptional loci and each blow-up automorphism permutes exceptional components, descending through the blow-down (consistent with the source Sarkar contraction argument). Conversely automorphisms preserving the reduced center lift. Any automorphism of A is translation plus g in Aut_gp(A), countable because End(A) is a finite-rank Z-module. After eliminating the translation, a fixed (g,sigma in S3) preserving a triple obeys the two difference equations g(p2-p1)=p_sigma(2)-p_sigma(1), g(p3-p1)=p_sigma(3)-p_sigma(1). If they held identically on A^3, variable support forces sigma=id then g=id. All other closed loci are proper, and their countable union is avoided by a very general triple. Involutions x->2p-x for one point, and x->p+q-x for two points, prove optimality. No finite experiment stands in for genericity.

## originality

PASS

The claimed sharp threshold three is not implied by the general d+1 sufficient bound claimed in Sarkar; the affine stabilizer argument strengthens it uniformly for all d>=3. No Resultary result observed matching blow-ups of complex abelian varieties at very general triples. The relevant Sarkar primary manuscript could only be inspected at abstract/preprint-description level, NOT full text; earlier theorem coverage is therefore retained as a real residual risk, not concealed or used as automatic failure.
