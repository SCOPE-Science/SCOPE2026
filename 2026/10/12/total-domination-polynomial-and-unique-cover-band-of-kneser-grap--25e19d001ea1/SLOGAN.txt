The complete total-domination polynomial of \(KG(n,2)\) is an explicit difference of the unrestricted edge-subset polynomial and three disjoint, exactly enumerated low-transversal classes. Every non-total-dominating set of cardinality at least \(n+1\) has a unique two-point obstruction, giving the simple coefficient formula \(\binom{\binom n2}{k}-\binom n2\binom{2n-3}{k}\) throughout the high-cardinality band.
