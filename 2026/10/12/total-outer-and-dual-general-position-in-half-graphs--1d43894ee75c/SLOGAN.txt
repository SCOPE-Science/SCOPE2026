For every half graph \(H_n\) with \(n\ge4\), the total, outer, and dual general-position numbers all equal \(2\), but their maximum sets behave differently. The total and dual maxima are uniquely \(\{a_n,b_1\}\), whereas the outer maxima are exactly the \(\binom n2\) diametral nonedges \(\{a_i,b_j\}\) with \(j<i\).
