For the star whose every edge is subdivided twice, \(q\ge3\), total perfect Roman domination drops from the once-subdivided extremal regime to \(\gamma_{tR}^{p}=2q+3\). Every optimum has center label \(2\), one exceptional arm of type \((1,1,1)\) or \((1,2,0)\), and all other arms of type \((0,1,1)\), so there are exactly \(2q\) optima.
