# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For any complete multipartite graph D is TRD iff it meets at least two classes and its complement is empty or meets at least two. r>=4 has two untouched classes after selecting a cross-part pair; r=3,N>=4 has a non-singleton class and a partner for each singleton. K3 and K1,m (m>=2) admit only the whole graph as TRD and thus exactly two coalition blocks; K2 has two, and Kp,q with p,q>=2 has the singleton partition. No graph with N vertices admits more than N partition blocks.

## originality

PASS

Prior total-restrained-coalition paper establishes complete, bipartite and star cases and characterizes maximal triangle-free graphs; it does not by that theorem imply the triangular multipartite (r>=3) classification. The new exact remaining regime and unified formula are not found among the relevant Resultary hits; this is best-of-knowledge, not absolute priority.

## value

PASS

Total restrained coalition number was recently introduced; exact determination on the basic and natural complete multipartite class is a useful structural classification beyond old triangle-free cases, not simply a redefinition of domination.
