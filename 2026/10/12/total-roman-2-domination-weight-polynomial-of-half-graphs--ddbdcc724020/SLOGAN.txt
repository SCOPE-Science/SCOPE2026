The complete weight polynomial for total Roman \(\{2\}\)-dominating functions of every half graph \(H_n\), \(n\ge2\), has an explicit finite-sum formula and a rational size-generating function. Its total number of feasible labelings is \(\bigl(121\cdot9^n+(189n-72)2^n\bigr)/441\), and the unique minimum-weight labeling for \(n\ge3\) places label two on the two universal endpoints.
