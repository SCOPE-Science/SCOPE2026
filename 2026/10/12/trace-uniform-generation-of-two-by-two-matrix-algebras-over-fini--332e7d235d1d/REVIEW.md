# Scientific review: fixed-trace matrix-algebra generation

## Correctness (PASS)
A complete maximal-proper-subalgebra dichotomy leaves rational-line stabilizers and nonsplit quadratic field subalgebras. Trace-fiber counts are surjective on all of these and their intersections, including the field-trace map in characteristic two. The scalar-fiber term cancels in inclusion-exclusion because \(1-(q+1)+\binom{q+1}{2}-q(q-1)/2=0\). The result is the stated exact fiber formula. Unital simple-ring Goursat and freely acting inner automorphisms prove the direct-product consequence. Exhaustive independent word-closure tests verify every target trace fiber for several small fields; the infinite theorem is symbolic.

## Originality (PASS, with specified residual uncertainty)
Florentino's 2008 structural similarity and triangularization classifications concern the same matrix tuples, but do not count generating tuples at fixed traces. Quick--Ruškuc 2010 establishes the growth context. Reineke 2025 explicitly counts *unconditioned* absolutely irreducible tuples in dimension two, and hence subsumes the unconditional count; that count and its simple orbit corollary are not presented as autonomous new results. In odd characteristic the per-trace statement follows from the known unconditional count via scalar translations, and is not claimed as independently original. The distinct contribution is **characteristic-two** equidistribution of each prescribed trace fiber, where scalar translations preserve traces and cannot yield the result. Targeted searches did not identify a previous proof of this refined law. Its possible earlier occurrence in finite-field character-variety literature is a real residual risk.

## Value (PASS)
Exact trace-constrained generator counts are natural finite-field invariants of free-algebra representations with fixed character data. The characteristic-two case is an informative boundary: scalar translations preserve trace, yet all trace fibers still have identical generator counts by a nontrivial Borel-versus-nonsplit-field cancellation. The theorem supplies a completely explicit refined distribution; the sharp direct-power cutoff connects the refinement with finite generation, while already-known unconditional statements are properly attributed.

## Checked literature and limitations
Inspected primary material: Florentino 2008 (arXiv:0809.3032, similarity/triangularization and trace invariants); Quick--Ruškuc 2010 (Cambridge full PDF, asymptotic direct-power generation); Reineke 2025 (arXiv:2510.06959, Section 3 and Example 4.6 including exact unconditional formulas). Remaining issue is possible independently established trace-refined enumeration in other historical terminology. The result does not cover higher dimensions, arbitrary rings or nonunital generators.
