A pure translation already breaks the stated weighted dispersive-order theorem: for \(X\sim\operatorname{Uniform}(0,1)\), \(Y=X+2\), and \(w(x)=e^{-x}\), the source's premise \(X\leq_{\mathrm{disp}}Y\) holds while \(\Phi_{Q_X}^{w}(u)<\Phi_{Q_Y}^{w}(u)\) for every \(0<u<1\). The obstruction is structural because dispersive order ignores translations but the value-dependent weight does not.
