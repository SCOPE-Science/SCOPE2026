# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reduces singleton SU-rank to Mirabi's explicit dividing criterion. Each forking step must absorb at least one previously unresolved infinity-node on the path, while adjoining the first unresolved infinity-node realizes a dividing extension that removes exactly one. Induction therefore gives the exact local rank. The polynomial and moment identities then follow from Mirabi's exact fiber counts.

## Originality
PASS, with a narrow scope. Mirabi's later coordinatization theorem already gives an additive SU-rank formula in a more general tree-product setting, so that component is treated as prior art. The retained claim is the bridge to the earlier asymptotic counting machinery: local degree equals SU-rank, normalized exponent equals normalized SU-rank, and the global counting polynomial is the coefficientwise SU-rank enumerator of parameter-free 1-types. Targeted semantic and web searches did not locate that combined statement or the induced finite rank-distribution formulas.

## Value
PASS. The result turns the counting polynomial from a finite-size device into a direct partition function for a standard model-theoretic rank. It also gives exact finite rank strata and moments, so it makes the relation between asymptotic dimension and SU-rank quantitative rather than merely bounding.
