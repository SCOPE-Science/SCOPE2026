For every finite loopless quiver, the complete inner-conjugacy class-size spectra of its radical-square-zero algebra and unit group depend only on the undirected multigraph. Their class counts are \(q^{c(G)}T_G(q+1,q)\) and \((q-1)^{c(G)}T_G(q,q)\), so these full spectra cannot distinguish arbitrarily large finite-global-dimension algebras from infinite-global-dimension ones.
