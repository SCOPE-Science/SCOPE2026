With sample size and true prevalence fixed, strictly positive confusion matrices with positive MCC and positive Cohen kappa cannot receive opposite strict rankings before \(N=12\). At twelve cases, \((2,3,2,5)\) versus \((3,5,1,3)\) has \(\kappa:2/17>1/10\) but \(M:1/\sqrt{70}<1/8\), exposing the first positive-regime ordinal failure.
