For positive integer incomes at fixed household count and fixed total, Gini and the coefficient of variation first split a tie at total \(10\) and first reverse strictly at total \(12\). The unique first strict reversal is \((1,2,4,5)\) versus \((2,2,2,6)\): Gini ranks the first as more unequal, while the coefficient of variation ranks the second as more unequal.\n
