Uniform expansion of a symmetric graph does not force simplicity of its evolution algebra: independently doubling each vertex preserves a positive Cheeger bound but creates a full quotient-subspace interval of ideals, with at least \(q^{\lfloor N^2/4\rfloor}\) ideals over \(\mathbb F_q\). The eight-dimensional \(K_{2,2,2,2}\) algebra over \(\mathbb F_5\) already has \(1121\) ordinary ideals and a proper basic evolution ideal.
