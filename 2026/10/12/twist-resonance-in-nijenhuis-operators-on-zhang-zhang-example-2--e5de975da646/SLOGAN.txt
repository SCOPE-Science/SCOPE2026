Zhang–Zhang Example 2.6 has a sharp Nijenhuis resonance: away from \(\mu=\pm1\) the operator variety is two affine planes meeting in a line, while at \(\mu=\pm1\) it jumps to an affine line times the singular \(2\times2\) determinantal cone. Over odd \(\mathbf F_q\), the raw count jumps from \(2q^2-q\) to \(q^4+q^3-q^2\), while the resonant automorphism quotient has only \(q(q+2)\) classes.
