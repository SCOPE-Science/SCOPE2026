# Review: paired absorbing vertices of a coherent hypercube

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof establishes the reducing dark/bright decomposition of a finite dissipative Hamiltonian, excludes real spectrum from the bright part for every pair of positive loss strengths, and calculates exact ranks from Walsh characters and strictly positive two-vector Gram determinants. The Krawtchouk projector formula and the complete-absorption classification follow from explicit inverses and the degree-one Fourier eigenspace. The independent exact Walsh-versus-Krawtchouk checker matches 640 localized cases, with five unequal-rate matrix exponential comparisons. Computations support rather than replace the unrestricted proof.

**Originality — PASS, subject to bibliographic uncertainty.** Novo et al. (2015) and Razzoli et al. (2020/21) provide the key one-trap spectral reduction, which is not new. Their one-target results do not imply the exact two-target rank plateau or pointwise two-target Krawtchouk inversion without further eigenspace-rank reasoning. Novo et al. (2016) study a single trap with disorder/dephasing; Lugão et al. (2024) analyze Hermitian multimarked search rather than irreversible absorption. Checked published research database matches concern different graph or Hamming invariants. A potentially equivalent multi-target calculation outside inspected works remains a priority risk.

**Value — PASS.** The theorem exactly answers a natural absorber-placement problem: antipodal pairing creates no additional capture directions, whereas *every* other distance adds precisely \(d-1\), almost doubling capture from the maximally mixed preparation at large dimension. All starting sites are classified, including the remote antipodal shadows of the traps, and the rational finite formula is reproducible. This is a full structural transport classification, not a parameter substitution or isolated numeric gain.

Limits: only binary hypercubes, uniform hopping, exactly two local positive absorbers, and infinite observation time; independent literature audit not performed.
