For odd bases with \(\gcd(b-1,n)=2\), the digit sums of multiples of \(n\) are *all* positive even integers exactly when a single negative-one power of the base exists modulo the prime-to-base part of \(n\); equality is classified by synchronized \(2\)-adic valuations of local multiplicative orders. The theorem also counts all admissible base residue classes modulo every even \(n\) and disproves a proposed necessary digit-sum divisibility condition.
