For \(n=2^a p\) with \(a\ge2\) and odd prime \(p\), every solution of \(D(\sigma(n)-n)=n\) forces the aliquot sum to contain exactly two factors of \(2\), and forces a parity rule for the remaining prime-factor count. When that odd cofactor has at most two prime factors, all solutions are \(284\) or a fully specified paired-prime family.
