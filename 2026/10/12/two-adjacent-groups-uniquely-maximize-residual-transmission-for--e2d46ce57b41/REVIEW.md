# Review of the five-cycle small-budget anti-Pareto theorem

## Correctness — PASS
The domain is the full five-dimensional vaccination cube intersected with the exact-cost hyperplane at cost \(\varepsilon/5\), with \(0<\varepsilon<\varepsilon_0\) for an existential positive threshold. The proof identifies the isolated simple Perron eigenvalue \(2\), derives its uniform second-order expansion under the budget-simplex normalization, and checks the exact reduced-resolvent matrix. The edge/nonedge quadratic has global maximum \(1/25\) attained only at the five equal adjacent pairs. The decisive further step uses a uniform \(C^2\) eigenvalue expansion: no allocation with support outside its adjacent pair can be optimal near that vertex of the maximizing set, while reflection symmetry and strict concavity on its edge face force the exact split. This proves global uniqueness up to rotation at all sufficiently small positive budgets, not just limiting optimality. The packaged exact symbolic verifier confirms the resolvent, polynomial identities and second-order coefficients; the support-shifting and compactness steps are justified in prose and do not depend on experimental enumeration. A numerical cutoff is neither assumed nor claimed.

## Originality — PASS, subject to stated literature risk
The source's *fully symmetric circle* section uses precisely the undirected cyclic next-generation matrix, states that only the full-suppression cost is obtained analytically for this model, and reports a numerical twelve-group anti-Pareto one-group-first pattern without proof. Its Proposition 2.5 treats minimization to zero rather than maximization at a small fixed dose. The earlier same-authors paper on cordons sanitaires excludes disconnected worst allocations but does not identify the small-budget optimum while all groups remain susceptible. Their general convexity/concavity sufficient theorem does not apply to the mixed-sign, two-positive-non-Perron spectrum of \(C_5\). The relevant 2018 finite-population theorem optimizes the **minimum** under an inverse-\(M\)-matrix condition absent here. Published cross-topic result listings and targeted literature searches did not identify a stronger matching five-cycle anti-Pareto theorem; this absence is not proof of absolute novelty. An unindexed equivalent graph spectral-radius result, a newer specialist article, or uninspected portions of a related preprint remain residual risks.

## Value — PASS
The 2021/2023 source singled out five or more symmetric-circle groups as the first range with no general closed optimization formula. The undirected five-cycle is the **smallest** such case and the first odd cycle beyond the elementary triangle. The result computes an explicit second-order term and, more importantly, proves the exact anti-Pareto optimizer in an entire nonzero budget interval. It distinguishes the worst allocation from a natural one-group-first heuristic and explains the distinction through the positive edge versus negative nonedge interaction in the Perron expansion. These are a motivated complete local classification and a structural perturbation mechanism, not an arbitrary numerical slice. It does not purport to predict the behavior of a real vaccination campaign.
