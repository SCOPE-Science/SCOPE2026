For stationary Gaussian AR(1) samples, the covariance between adjacent zero crossings and mean-centered energy is positive near independence and extremely strong positive persistence, but negative in between for every sample size \(n\ge7\). Its two asymptotic sign-change layers are \(\rho\sim1/n\) and \(1-\rho\sim20/(3n^2)\), obtained by a new sign analysis of existing exact mixed-moment identities; uniqueness of the zeros is not claimed.
