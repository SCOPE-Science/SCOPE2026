For a stationary Gaussian AR(1) sample, the covariance between the discrete zero-crossing count and the square of the sample sum has two distinct negative-unit-root boundary layers: the even-size sign changes on scales \(1+\rho\asymp n^{-2}\) and \(1+\rho\asymp n^{-1/2}\). This produces at least two sign reversals for every sufficiently large even sample size, whereas odd sizes have a different near-endpoint sign.
