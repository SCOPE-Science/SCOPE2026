The dual-number pre-Lie algebra \(A_2\) and the nonunital one-idempotent algebra \(A_3\) are nonisomorphic, yet their Nijenhuis operators have exactly the same automorphism-orbit space \(k^2\sqcup k\). Over \(\mathbf F_q\), each has \(2q^2-q\) Nijenhuis operators in exactly \(q^2+q\) conjugacy classes.
