Optimal binary dimension-four four-server PIR encoders of length \(9\) have exactly two equivalence types under coordinate permutation and \(\operatorname{GL}(4,2)\). One type is projective and four-recovers every nonzero target in \(\mathbb F_2^4\); the other has one doubled column and four-recovers exactly \(14\) of the \(15\) nonzero targets.
