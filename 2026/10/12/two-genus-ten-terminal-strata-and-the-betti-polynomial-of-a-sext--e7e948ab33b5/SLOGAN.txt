The involution quotient of every smooth Shioda sextic fourfold has exactly two genus-\(10\) singular curves, with transverse terminal Veronese-cone singularities \(\frac12(1,1,1)\) and canonical index \(2\). Its rational Betti polynomial is \(1+t^2+1283t^4+t^6+t^8\), so \(\chi_{\mathrm{top}}=1287\).
