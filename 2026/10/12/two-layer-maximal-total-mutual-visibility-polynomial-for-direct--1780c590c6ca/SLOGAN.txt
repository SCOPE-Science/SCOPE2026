Every inclusion-maximal total-mutual-visibility set in the direct product \(K_m\times K_n\), for \(m,n\ge4\), omits either four nonattacking cells or six cells arranged as three distinct two-cell row patterns (or the transposed patterns). These are the only two possible maximal-set sizes, and their complete counting polynomial is explicit; in particular the lower total-mutual-visibility number is \(mn-6\).
