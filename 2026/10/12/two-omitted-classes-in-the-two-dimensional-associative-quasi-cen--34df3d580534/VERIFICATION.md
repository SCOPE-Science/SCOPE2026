# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For the seven pairwise non-isomorphic two-dimensional complex associative multiplication tables in Rikhsiboev–Rakhimov–Basri Theorem 4.1, I independently imposed φ(e_i)e_j=e_iφ(e_j) for all four ordered basis pairs with column-image matrix [[a,c],[b,d]]. Exact kernel dimensions are 4,3,3,1,1,2,2 for zero, idempotent-plus-null, nilpotent e1²=e2, left-unit, right-unit, dual numbers, and C×C, respectively. In particular A0 has M2(C), and Ae has all lower-triangular matrices, each absent from the five-row 2023 table. All seven products are associative; the cited classification supplies exhaustiveness, not finite sampling. The corrected dimension multiset is {4,3,3,2,2,1,1}; the source theorem omits both A0 and Ae.

## originality

PASS

This is a correction of the explicit universal completeness claim in Fiidow–Zahari–Mosbahi Theorem 5.1, not an assertion that its five listed values are all wrong. Original Thm5.1 visibly lists precisely five rows and the complete 2010 primary classification lists seven; the later quasi-centroid classification therefore fails to include the zero and idempotent-plus-null classes. Searches did not find a pre-existing corrigendum or seven-row quasi-centroid table; the seven base isomorphism classes themselves are prior and not claimed original.
