A Bernoulli random ternary team satisfies the relational independence atom \(x\perp_z y\) with two distinct Poisson critical windows: \(p\asymp n^{-5/2}\) at the sparse end and \(1-p\asymp n^{-3}\) at the dense end. An exact rectangular-fiber reliability formula and a uniform intermediate-regime bound show that satisfaction disappears between those windows and reappears near the complete team.
