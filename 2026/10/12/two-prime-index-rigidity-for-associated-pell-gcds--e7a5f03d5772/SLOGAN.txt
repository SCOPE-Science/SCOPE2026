For the associated Pell numbers, every prime-power index has trivial index gcd, and every index \(p^a q^b\) with \(p<q\) satisfies \(\gcd(Q_{p^a q^b},p^a q^b)\in\{1,q^b\}\), with the second value occurring exactly when \(q\mid Q_{p^a}\). The infinite family \(\gcd(Q_{3\cdot2^a},3\cdot2^a)=1\) for \(a\ge2\) disproves the proposed sufficient rank criterion in Mbirika–Schrader–Spilker's Conjecture 32.
