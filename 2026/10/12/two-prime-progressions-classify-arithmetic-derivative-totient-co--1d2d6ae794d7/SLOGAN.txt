For primes \(r>7\), the equality \(D(5^a7^b r^c)=\varphi(5^a7^b r^c)\) holds exactly in two prime-progressions: \(r\equiv2\pmod{35}\) with \((a,b,c)=(1,1,(12r-24)/35)\), or \(r\equiv12\pmod{35}\) with \((a,b,c)=(1,3,(2r-24)/35)\). Each progression contains infinitely many primes, yielding two infinite families of exact three-prime-support coincidences.
