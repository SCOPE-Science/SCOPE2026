# Review

## Correctness

PASS. For prime powers, the exact identity \(D(p^a)/p^a=a/p\) forces \(a=p-1\). For exact support \(\{p,q\}\), the equation is equivalent to \((a+1)q+(b+1)p=pq+1\). The \(p=2\) branch contradicts parity and size. For odd \(p\), the coefficient \(a+1\) is forced into \(\{2,\ldots,p-1\}\) and is the unique inverse of \(q\bmod p\); the other exponent is then forced and is proved positive. The converse substitutes back into the same identity. The squarefree corollary reduces to \((p-2)(q-2)=3\). The density corollary is the standard prime number theorem in arithmetic progressions applied to all nonzero classes except \(1\bmod p\).

PASS. Equality between the arithmetic derivative and Euler's totient is a natural coincidence problem between two canonical multiplicative-factorization invariants.
