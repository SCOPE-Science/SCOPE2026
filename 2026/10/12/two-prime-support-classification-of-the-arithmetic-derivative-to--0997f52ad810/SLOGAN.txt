For \(\omega(n)\le2\), the equation \(D(n)=\varphi(n)\) is completely rigid: prime powers are exactly \(p^{p-1}\), while every admissible odd two-prime support \(\{p,q\}\) carries one explicitly determined solution via the inverse of \(q\bmod p\). No support containing \(2\) works, and \(15\) is the unique squarefree semiprime solution.
