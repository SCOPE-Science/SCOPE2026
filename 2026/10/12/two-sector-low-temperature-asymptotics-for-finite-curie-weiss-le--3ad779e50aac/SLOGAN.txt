For every fixed finite Curie--Weiss system, each moving positive Lee--Yang zero approaches \((2k-1)\pi/(2n)\) at the exact one-spin-flip scale \(e^{-2(n-1)\beta/n}\), with amplitude \(\sin((2k-1)\pi/n)\). The next correction is governed by the two-spin-flip sector, with an explicit \(n=4\) central-sector exception.
