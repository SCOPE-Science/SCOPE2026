For every even cycle length \(2k\ge6\), all maximum zero-error codes in \(C_7\boxtimes C_{2k}\) split into dense–empty and singleton–pair column types, and their exact labeled count is \(2(7^k+\operatorname{tr}((3I_7+A(C_7))^k))\). The dense–empty type comprises asymptotically all optima, with relative error \(O((5/7)^k)\).
