For two tasks where the mean gradient harms the weaker task, CAGrad first crosses a sharp radius \(c_{\rm cd}\) that restores common descent and only later reaches a distinct radius \(c_{\rm bal}\) that equalizes taskwise progress. If the weaker gradient has at most one third of the stronger norm, exact equalization is impossible for every convergence-safe \(c<1\), although common descent remains attainable.
