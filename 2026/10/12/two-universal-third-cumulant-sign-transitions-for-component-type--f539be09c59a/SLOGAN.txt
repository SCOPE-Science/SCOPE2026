For any three disjoint connected-component types of fixed orders \(a,b,c\) in \(G(n,\lambda/n)\), the normalized mixed third cumulant has the universal limit \(abc[(a+b+c)(\lambda-1)^2-1]\). Its sign changes twice, at asymptotic parameters \(\lambda=1\pm(a+b+c)^{-1/2}\), independently of the component topologies; uniqueness of all finite-sample zeros is not asserted.
