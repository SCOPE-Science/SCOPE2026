# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The construction is uniform for every \(m\ge1\). The piecewise graph function is continuous and semialgebraic on the slit disk. The displayed homotopy preserves its graph closure, fixes the limiting two-branch graph pointwise, and retracts the closure onto a graph with \(m+1\) vertices and \(2m\) edges. Its cycle rank is therefore exactly \(m\). Michel's equiregularity and section-transfer propositions move this construction to a bounded CAD cell in \(\mathbf R^4\) without changing the closure pair.

## Originality
PASS. The closest source proves the rank-one case: its explicit bounded noncontractible CAD-cell closure retracts onto one circle. It also remarks that infinitely many counterexamples can be produced, but gives no arbitrary-rank or unbounded-Betti statement. The strongest older regularity theorem applies under stronger well-based hypotheses. Exact, alias, broader-coverage, and implication searches did not locate a prior result realizing \(H_1\cong\mathbf Z^m\) for every \(m\) in closures of bounded CAD cells in fixed ambient dimension four.

## Value
PASS. The statement strengthens a single topological pathology to unbounded topological complexity while keeping the ambient dimension fixed. The exact invariant \(b_1=m\) has a direct geometric mechanism—each consecutive pair of zeros contributes one independent boundary loop—and gives a natural benchmark for how far arbitrary CAD-cell closures can lie from regular closed balls.

The construction does not classify all realizable closure homotopy types and does not establish the minimal ambient dimension for unbounded first Betti number.
