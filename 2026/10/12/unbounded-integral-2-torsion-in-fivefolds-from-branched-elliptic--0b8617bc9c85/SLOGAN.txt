For every even branch count \(r\ge2\), the smooth projective fivefold from the elliptic double-cover blow-up has fixed Betti vector \((1,2,3,6,9,10,9,6,3,2,1)\), but its integral cohomology has \((\mathbb Z/2)^{r-2}\) torsion in each degree \(4\), \(5\), \(6\), and \(7\), and no torsion elsewhere. Thus these Aomoto-exact fivefolds support unbounded integral two-primary torsion invisible to their rational cohomology.
