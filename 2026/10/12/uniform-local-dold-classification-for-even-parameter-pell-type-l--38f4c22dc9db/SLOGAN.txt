For every even \(P\ge2\), the Lucas sequence \(U_{n+2}=PU_{n+1}+U_n\) has nonnegative local Möbius transforms with a completely explicit least repair factor at every prime, including ramified primes and \(2\). Its locally realizable primes are exactly the split odd primes of \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt{P^2+4})\), giving the same universal four prime densities \((1/2,1/2,1,1)\) throughout the even-parameter family.
