Every unital map between two multi-arrow Kronecker path algebras is an arbitrary arrow-linear map with a shear, a radical-annihilating vertex reversal, or one of two scalar augmentations. Over \(\mathbb F_q\), the Hom count is \(q^{s(r+1)}+q^s+2\); composition-idempotent endomorphisms have a complete Gaussian-binomial rank polynomial and exactly \(n+3\) automorphism-conjugacy classes.
