The polynomial \(pF_{m,p}(X)/X^{h_m}\) has a \(p\)-unit leading coefficient, \(p\)-divisible remaining coefficients and constant term of exactly \(p\)-valuation one. Exact independent checks at moments 2–20 corroborate each algebraic step; they are not treated as an infinite proof.
