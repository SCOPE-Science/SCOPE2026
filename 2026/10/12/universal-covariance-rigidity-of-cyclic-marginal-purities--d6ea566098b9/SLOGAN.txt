For cyclic length-\(k\) marginal purities of a pure \(n\)-qudit state, all affine dependencies are forced by complementary intervals: the independent count is \(n\), except that it is \(n/2\) at the balanced even-size cut. The same exact rank holds for the purity covariance of every full-support pure-state ensemble, and translated Bell-pair probes witness it without Haar integration.
