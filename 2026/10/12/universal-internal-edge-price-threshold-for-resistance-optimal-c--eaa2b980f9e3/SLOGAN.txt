For any bouquet of complete graphs sharing a hub, with arbitrary clique sizes and positive hub-edge/internal-edge prices, the unique Kirchhoff-index-minimizing conductances have an explicit blockwise formula. Internal edges in every block are purchased exactly when their per-edge price relative to hub spokes is below the same universal threshold \(2N/(N-1)\), independent of the partition into clique sizes at fixed total vertex count and of all other blocks' prices; optimal expenditures across blocks follow a square-root allocation rule.
