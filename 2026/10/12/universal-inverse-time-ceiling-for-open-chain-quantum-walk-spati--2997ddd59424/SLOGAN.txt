For the independent many-walker spatial-entanglement prescription of arXiv:0901.0671, the local purity factorizes into one-particle terms, yielding the universal ceiling \(E_{\mathrm{MW}}(t)\le \frac{2^M}{2^M-1}\frac{2M}{2t+M+1}\). Hence the open-chain Meyer--Wallach quantity must vanish at least as \(O(t^{-1})\) for every fixed number of walkers, independently of the coin parameters within the source's product model.
