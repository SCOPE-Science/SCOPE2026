# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The four quotient identities and conic return identity are exact polynomial consequences of the defining conic. Nonvanishing reduces to absence of integer roots of the four endpoint polynomials. For \(P_0,P_2\), the discriminant equation is \((c-2)^2-d^2=4\), whose only positive same-parity factorization at \(c\ge2\) yields \(c=4\). For \(P_1,P_3\), \((c+2)^2-d^2=4\) would force \(c=0\). Thus one cycle is nondegenerate for every \(c\ge2\) with \(c\ne4\), and conic preservation closes the induction. The exceptional point \((c,x,y)=(4,-2,4)\) is exact.

## Originality
PASS. The closest source, arXiv:2610.00808v1, constructs the four-cycle and proves an infinite positive subfamily from the residue chamber \(x\equiv y\equiv1\pmod c\); it does not state that every nonzero integral conic point lifts when \(c\ne4\), nor identify \(c=4\) as the unique parameter obstructing that universal statement. Exact formula searches and semantic searches over published findings found no stronger statement covering this implication.

## Value
PASS. The result turns an existence construction for a recurrent nonunit cycle into an all-points lifting theorem on its natural conic for every nonexceptional parameter, and isolates the unique sharp parameter where zero quotients can occur. This directly clarifies the arithmetic lifting locus for the paper’s central recurrent example and removes sufficient congruence/coprimality hypotheses rather than merely producing another orbit.

The closest literature limitation is that \(c=4\) remains only partially described: the universal statement fails, but its surviving infinite-lift points are not classified here.
