For a Bernoulli random binary team, the entire probability distribution of violations of unary inclusion \(x\subseteq y\) has an exact factorial-moment formula, and the single intensity \(\lambda_n=n(1-p_n)^n[1-(1-p_n)^{n-1}]\) controls all asymptotic truth probabilities. This yields two distinct Poisson windows—an extremely sparse window and an almost-column-covered window—with near-certain satisfaction at either extreme but asymptotic failure between them.
