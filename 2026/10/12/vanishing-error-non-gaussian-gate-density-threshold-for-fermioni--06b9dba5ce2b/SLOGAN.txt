For positive-rate, positive-relative-distance fermionic code families, vanishing codeword-preparation error in the bounded-weight doped-Gaussian model requires asymptotic non-Gaussian gate density at least \(\Delta/(q-2)+R/q\). Below \(\Delta/(q-2)\) the source fidelity bound is exponentially small, while the intermediate density band still has a nonzero asymptotic fidelity gap.
