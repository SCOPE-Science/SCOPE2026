For a fixed-inoculum, two-host-type virulence model, simultaneously weakening within-host competition and mortality coupling makes the globally selected pathogen phenotype unique and uniformly \(10+O(c)\) for every vulnerable-host proportion. The selected mortality is \(c\,10m\int_0^{1/m}e^{9t/2}\,dt+O(c^2)\), and the competing local optima of the finite-competition fitness landscape disappear on every fixed bounded phenotype interval.
