On every complete bipartite quantum network \(K_{p,q}\) with \(p,q\ge3\), an arbitrarily small mismatch in two walkers' hopping strengths raises the eventual collision-absorbing dimension from \(3(p+q)-2\) to \(5(p+q)-4\). Exact sitewise capture laws show that opposite-class initial configurations double their eventual reaction probability off resonance, independently of all positive coincidence-loss rates.
