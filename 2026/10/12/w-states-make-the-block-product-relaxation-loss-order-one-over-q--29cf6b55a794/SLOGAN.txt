For the \(n\)-qubit W state, the best relaxation from fully product states to states product across \(q\) blocks has exact squared-overlap loss \(A_q-A_n\) when \(q\mid n\), where \(A_m=((m-1)/m)^{m-1}\). Hence the recent random-coloring additive \(O(1/q)\) loss scale is asymptotically sharp, with unavoidable leading size \(1/(2e\,q)\).
