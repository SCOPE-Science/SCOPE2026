For heterogeneous clique windmills, weak Roman feasibility depends only on the center label and the number of empty blades: none are allowed when the center is \(0\), at most one when it is \(1\), and any number when it is \(2\). This gives a closed factorized weight enumerator for every weak Roman dominating function and shows that for at least three blades the unique optimum places weight \(2\) at the center.
