For \(CP_n=K_{2n}-nK_2\), every total Roman \(\{2\}\)-labeling of weight at least \(4\) is feasible exactly when its positive support meets at least two deleted-matching pairs; only two explicit low-weight correction families remain. This yields the complete enumerator \(\mathcal T_n(x)=(1+x+x^2)^{2n}-1-n((1+x+x^2)^2-1)-2n(n-1)x^2-4n(n-1)x^3\), with exactly \(\binom{2n}{3}\) minimum functions.
