A cycle in the smaller ground-set block and three disjoint edges in the larger block give weight \(4\) signed Roman domination on \(K(n,2)\) for every even \(n\ge10\), extending the previously isolated \(n=10\) case and improving the general even-order upper bound \(5\). The weight is best possible within the natural two-block signing class; unrestricted equality is not claimed.
