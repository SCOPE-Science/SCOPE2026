For a weighted directed-cycle evolution algebra over \(\mathbf F_q\), all \(n\) edge weights collapse to one class in \(\mathbf F_q^\times/(\mathbf F_q^\times)^{2^n-1}\), and isomorphism is exactly orbit equivalence under squaring. This gives a Burnside formula for all isomorphism classes, exact automorphism orders, and a sharp criterion showing that exactly one class has nonzero idempotents.
