The identity \(\det(v,J_dv)=Q_d(v)\) gives both Witt cases and shows why local maps over nonsplit fields may fail locality after the form splits. Pairwise witnesses on \((e_1,e_2)\) and \((e_1,e_1+z)\) force any **linear** 2-local derivation to be a derivation. The checker verified these symbolic statements and the full \(\mathbf F_3\) local-witness enumeration, including both Witt classes; larger finite fields independently confirm the parameter counts.
