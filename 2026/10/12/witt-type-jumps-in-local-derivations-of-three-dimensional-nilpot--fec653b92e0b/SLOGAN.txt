For the three-dimensional nilpotent associative algebra with \(e_1^2=z\) and \(e_2^2=d z\), the space of linear local derivations has dimension \(7\) when \(-d\) is nonsquare but dimension \(5\) when it is square, while ordinary derivations always have dimension \(4\). Every linear 2-local derivation is ordinary, yet a local derivation from the nonsplit form can cease to be local after separable scalar extension.
