# Review

## Correctness

PASS. The dihedral presentation gives the exact generating conditions: a rotation and reflection generate precisely when the rotation exponent is a unit modulo \(p^k\), and two reflections generate precisely when their exponent difference is a unit. This yields \(p^{k-1}\) isolated rotations and an active complete multipartite graph with one part of size \((p-1)p^{k-1}\) and \(p\) parts of size \(p^{k-1}\). False-twin classes force the complete zero-forcing-set classification. The rank witnesses have the exact graph zero pattern, and the rank-two impossibility for \(k\ge2\) follows from the fact that a nondegenerate symmetric form on \(\mathbb R^2\) has at most two isotropic lines.

The packaged verifier exhaustively reproduces the complete polynomial for the small prime and first nontrivial prime-power cases and checks larger structural profiles and exact rational ranks.

The closest same-invariant result for generating graphs concerns two-generated finite \(p\)-groups. Restricting to odd \(p\), the present dihedral groups are not \(p\)-groups, and their active multipartite component has one exceptional part together with \(p\) equal reflection parts.
