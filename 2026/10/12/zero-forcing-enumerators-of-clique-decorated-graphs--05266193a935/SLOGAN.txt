Attaching a private clique of size at least two to every vertex admits a complete local characterization of zero-forcing sets. For a uniform clique corona, the entire zero-forcing polynomial transforms by \(\mathcal Z(G\odot K_s;x)=s^n x^{(s-1)n}\mathcal Z(G;x(x+s+1)/s)\); arbitrary private-clique sizes obey a weighted sum over base zero-forcing sets.
