For distinct primes \(p,q\), the zero-divisor graph of \(\mathbb Z/(p^2q)\) has \(\mathcal Z(u)=u^{N-4}(u+(p-1)(q-1))(u+p-1)(u+q-1)(u+p(p-1))\), where \(N=p^2+pq-p-1\). Its minimum zero forcing sets omit one vertex from each gcd class, while \(Z=M=N-4\) and the real symmetric minimum rank is \(4\).
