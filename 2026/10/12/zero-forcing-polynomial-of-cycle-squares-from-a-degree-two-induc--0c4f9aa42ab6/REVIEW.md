# Scientific review: cycle-square zero-forcing polynomial

**Correctness — PASS.** The unrestricted biconditional is established by an initial legal-force criterion followed by propagation from five consecutive selected vertices. The 16-state cyclic-word bijection and an exact determinant calculation yield the evaluated rational generating function. A standalone integer-polynomial determinant verifier and direct independent exhaustive simulation of every ordinary forcing process on all subsets for orders 7 through 14 agree. This finite census corroborates but does not replace the proof. The degree-four assumption requires the stated \(n\ge7\) boundary, and skew forcing is excluded.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risks.** The original AIM work (DOI 10.1016/j.laa.2007.10.009, online 2007-11-26) defines ordinary zero forcing and emphasizes minimum size. Boyer and coauthors (arXiv:1801.08910) introduce the polynomial and compute ordinary cycles, not their squares. The targeted circulant paper of Johnson and coauthors (DOI 10.3390/appliedmath5020032) studies skew forcing instead. Bermond–Peyrat's 1989 induced-subgraph paper (DOI 10.1137/0402039) is relevant by object but its abstract poses a minimum-degree size-bound problem, while Gomez and coauthors (DOI 10.20429/tag.2024.110105) study maximum failed cardinalities. The latter two full articles were inaccessible for complete comparison, and a differently named forbidden circular-word enumeration might exist. The minimum and maximum forcing cardinalities alone are not claimed as independently original. Published literature and indexed findings revealed no directly covering full polynomial; non-discovery is not certification.

**Value — PASS.** The result gives the complete, closed bivariate rational enumerator for every subset cardinality across an infinite connected four-regular family. Its local induced-degree criterion converts an iterative process into exact structural enumeration. The evaluated denominator and coefficient recurrence capture information strictly beyond either extremal number.

**Scientific limits.** Orders at most six, skew forcing, higher cycle powers, and formal independent mathematical validation are not within the claim. Earlier equivalent enumerations under degree-bounded induced subgraphs or local forbidden circular words remain possible; the 1989 and failed-circulant papers require follow-up full-text inspection by an independent literature checker.
