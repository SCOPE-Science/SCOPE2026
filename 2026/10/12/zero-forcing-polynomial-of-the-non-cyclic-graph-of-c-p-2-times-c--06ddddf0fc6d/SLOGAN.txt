For \(G=C_{p^2}\times C_p\) with \(p\ge3\), the non-cyclic graph has a five-term zero forcing polynomial and \(Z(\Gamma_G)=p^3-5\). Every minimum set is the complement of one vertex from the common order-\(p\) core, one vertex from each of two distinct maximal cyclic order-\(p^2\) branches, and one vertex from a different order-\(p\) line; there are \(p^4(p-1)^5/2\) such sets.\n
