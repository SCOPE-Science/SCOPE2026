# Review

## Correctness

PASS. The central quotient gives \(p+1\) maximal subgroups meeting in the center, while each maximal subgroup contains exactly \(p\) noncentral order-\(p\) subgroups belonging to no other maximal subgroup. Hence the graph is a clique \(K_{p+2}\) with \(p\) private leaves at each maximal vertex. The necessary-and-sufficient forcing criterion is: at most one white leaf per block, and if the center is white then at least one block is entirely blue. Counting these profiles gives the stated polynomial, \(Z=p^2\), and \((2p+1)p^p\) minimizers. The packaged checker exhausts every subset at \(p=3\) and independently rebuilds subgroup intersections at \(p=3,5\).

Public subgroup-intersection work inspected for the graph definition and structural parameters does not supply the accepted forcing classification. The full zero-forcing-polynomial article was inspected through its family-characterization section; no corona/private-leaf-block formula or subgroup-intersection specialization covering this graph was found. Searches under extraspecial, Heisenberg, clique-with-private-leaves, and polynomial aliases did not locate an implication-equivalent result.

## Value

PASS. The result gives a closed enumerator for an infinite nonabelian group family rather than a single finite graph. The center creates a genuine two-regime forcing mechanism: with the center white, one maximal-subgroup leaf block must be entirely blue; with the center blue, that extra condition disappears. This yields both a sharp forcing threshold and a complete two-type classification of every minimum set.
