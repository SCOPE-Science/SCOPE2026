For an odd-prime extraspecial group of order \(p^3\) and exponent \(p\), the subgroup-intersection graph has \(\mathcal Z(u)=u^{p^2-1}(1+u)^{p+1}((1+u)(u+p)^{p+1}-p^{p+1})\). In particular \(Z=p^2\), with exactly \((2p+1)p^p\) minimum zero forcing sets, all arising from two explicit leaf-block patterns.
