For every prime \(p\), the subgroup intersection graph of \(C_{p^2}\times C_p\) is the \((p+2,p)\)-pineapple and has \(\mathcal Z(u)=u^{2p-1}(u+p)(u+p+1)(u+1)\). Its minimum zero forcing sets are exactly the complements of the center, one pendant order-\(p\) subgroup, and one noncentral clique subgroup, so there are \(p(p+1)\) of them.
