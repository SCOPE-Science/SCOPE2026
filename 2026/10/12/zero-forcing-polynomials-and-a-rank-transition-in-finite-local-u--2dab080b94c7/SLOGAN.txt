For every finite commutative local nonfield with residue-field order \(q\), maximal-ideal size \(s\), and \(N=qs\), the unit graph has \(\mathcal Z(u)=\binom{q}{2}s^2u^{N-2}+Nu^{N-1}+u^N\), so the polynomial recovers \(q\) and \(s\). Real minimum rank is \(2\) for odd \(q\) and for \(q=2\), but \(3\) for even \(q\ge4\), giving the exact boundary where \(Z=M\) fails by one.
