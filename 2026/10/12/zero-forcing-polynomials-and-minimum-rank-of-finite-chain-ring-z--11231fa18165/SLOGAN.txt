For every finite commutative chain ring except the one-vertex boundary, the zero forcing polynomial factors with one linear factor for each valuation layer: \(\mathcal Z(u)=u^{N-(\ell-1)}\prod_{i=1}^{\ell-1}(u+(q-1)q^{\ell-i-1})\). Thus \(Z=M=N-(\ell-1)\), real minimum rank is \(\ell-1\), and the polynomial recovers both the residue-field order and nilpotency length.
