For a finite product of fields with both binary and nonbinary factors, the standard zero-divisor graph has \(\mathcal Z(u)=u^{N-(2^r-2)}\prod_{\varnothing\ne S\subsetneq[r]}(u+\prod_{i\in S}(q_i-1))\), with \(Z=M=N-(2^r-2)\) and real minimum rank \(2^r-2\). The polynomial recovers every field order, so it determines the finite ring within this mixed class.
