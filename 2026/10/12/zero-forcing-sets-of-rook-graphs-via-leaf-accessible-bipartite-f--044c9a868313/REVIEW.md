# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The line-graph identification makes the force criterion exact: a blue edge has the sum of endpoint white degrees as white neighbors. Cycle edges cannot ever be deleted, while a forest with the stipulated isolate-and-leaf accessibility can be peeled to completion. The bipartite Prüfer-word decomposition counts tree components with marked leaves; labeled exponential composition counts forests. Six-term inclusion–exclusion selects exactly the accessible ones, and a tree with one isolated vertex gives the Stirling-number count of minimum sets. A standalone program independently checks direct forcing versus the static rule, all polynomial coefficients, and the extremal count for eight boards (104272 subsets). The proof, rather than the finite census, covers all orders.

**Originality — PASS with residual risk.** Direct comparison with the AIM foundational zero-forcing work, Fallat–Soltani (2016) Theorem 3.4, Boyer et al. (2018) full polynomial paper, and Pelayo–Abara (2022) Theorem 3.7 distinguishes the known minimum number and largest failed-set number from the present all-set forest classification and complete cardinality enumerator. Searches for rook graph, line graph of complete bipartite graph, leaf forest forcing and Cartesian complete-graph forcing in the relevant article texts and published research record did not locate a dominating equivalent theorem. This is evidence of noncoverage by the inspected sources, not proof of priority among all literature. Possible equivalent indexing under unfamiliar terms is the main residual risk.

**Value — PASS.** Describing all initial forcing sets converts a dynamical propagation question on an infinite two-parameter graph family into a test on an associated bipartite forest. The full polynomial computes every coefficient and the explicit nontrivial minimum-set count refines a known extremal parameter. The leaf-accessibility obstruction is structural, not merely a restatement of the minimum zero-forcing number. The result is self-contained and mathematically motivated independent of computational speed.

**Scope and limits.** The result concerns ordinary zero forcing and two complete Cartesian factors of order at least two; no variant forcing rules or wider graph classes are claimed. Finite verification is not an infinite proof. Earlier equivalent all-set theorems outside the inspected terminology cannot be excluded by searching alone.
