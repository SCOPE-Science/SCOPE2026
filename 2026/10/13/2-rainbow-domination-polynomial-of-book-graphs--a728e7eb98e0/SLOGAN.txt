The full \(2\)-rainbow domination polynomial of the square book \(B_n=K_{1,n}\mathbin{\square}P_2\) collapses to seven explicit \(n\)-th-power terms obtained from the two spine labels. Equivalently, this gives the ordinary domination polynomial of \(K_{1,n}\mathbin{\square}C_4\), with a unique minimum function for every \(n\ge4\).
