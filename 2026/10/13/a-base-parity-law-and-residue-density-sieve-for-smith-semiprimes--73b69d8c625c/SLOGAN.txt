Odd bases have essentially no Smith semiprimes: base \(3\) has none, and every odd base \(B\ge5\) has only \(4\). In every even base, the universal digit-sum congruence confines distinct-prime Smith semiprimes to an explicit residue involution whose semiprime density is \(c_B=|A_{B-1}|/\varphi(B-1)^2\); in decimal this sieve has density \(1/12\).
