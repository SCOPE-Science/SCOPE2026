On a \(3\times3\) SPD family with fixed condition number \(2\), expanding exact Lanczos from \(\mathcal K_1\) to \(\mathcal K_2\) strictly improves the largest Ritz value while the associated residual ratio diverges like \(2\sqrt6/(9\varepsilon)\). The effect is an exact-arithmetic near-breakdown and cannot occur in dimensions below three.
