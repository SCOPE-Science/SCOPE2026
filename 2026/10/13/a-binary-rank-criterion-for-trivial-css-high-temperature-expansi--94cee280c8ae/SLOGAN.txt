For the CSS high-temperature expansion of Stengele et al., the exact local obstruction to a two-term expansion is excess nullity of a binary qubit-interaction incidence matrix: \(\dim\ker B_R=\mathbf1_{\{B_R\mathbf1=0\}}\). This strictly sharpens the paper's degree-two sufficient condition and turns the admissibility test into exact \(\mathbb F_2\) Gaussian elimination.
