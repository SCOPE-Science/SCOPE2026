For every \(m\ge1\), the nonmonomial algebra \(B_m=\operatorname{End}_{A_m}(T_m)\) from Liu--Xin Example 6.3 is a one-dimensional socle quotient of a three-edge Brauer tree algebra. Hence the entire family is representation-finite, giving infinitely many affirmative instances of the source's open representation-finiteness problem outside the G-condition.
