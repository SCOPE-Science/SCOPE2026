For a double-star network whose two clusters have \(k\ge\ell\ge1\) leaves, the fastest symmetric continuous-time mixing rate under a shared edge-price budget has an explicit two-branch formula. A sharp bridge-price threshold \(t_c=((k-\ell)/(\ell+1))^2\) switches both the unique optimal edge-rate allocation and the spectral-gap multiplicity, which is \(k\) below the threshold and one above it.
