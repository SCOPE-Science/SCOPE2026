# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. Wu's discriminant forces \(\alpha\gamma\neq0\). The simultaneous sign involution can be fixed only at the four combinations of \(0\) and \(\infty\) in \(\mathbf P^1\times\mathbf P^1\); substitution into the bihomogeneous equation excludes all four. The action is therefore free and tame. The invariants \(u=x^2\) and \(v=xy\) generate the fixed field and satisfy
\[
\alpha u^2+(v^2+\beta v+\gamma)u+\alpha v^2=0.
\]
Riemann--Hurwitz gives genus one for the quotient. With a rational origin, the degree-two quotient morphism is an isogeny and its nontrivial kernel point is \(\tau(O)\), hence a rational point of order \(2\).

## Originality
PASS. The initiating full text gives the symmetric QRT equation, smoothness criterion, genus-one model, and standard fiber-switching involutions, but it does not state the free simultaneous-sign quotient, its invariant-field equation, or the pointed two-isogeny and rational two-torsion consequence. General QRT literature checked supplies horizontal/vertical involution and translation theory rather than this central quotient. Exact and alias searches found no covering statement. A related 2026 isogeny paper by the same author was available only at abstract level and remains an explicit residual risk.

## Value
PASS. The finding exposes a uniform arithmetic structure across every smooth member of the full three-parameter family. The degree-two quotient and, after pointing, the rational two-torsion point provide reusable structure for arithmetic and dynamics and are materially stronger than observing a formal symmetry of the affine equation.

The pointed isogeny conclusion requires a rational origin. The unpointed fixed-point-free degree-two quotient does not. No classification of all isogenies or torsion is asserted.
