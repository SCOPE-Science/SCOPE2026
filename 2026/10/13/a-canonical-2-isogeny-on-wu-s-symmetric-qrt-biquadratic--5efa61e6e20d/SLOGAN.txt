Every smooth member of Wu's symmetric QRT family has the fixed-point-free involution \(\tau(x,y)=(-x,-y)\), whose quotient is explicitly given by \(\alpha u^2+(v^2+\beta v+\gamma)u+\alpha v^2=0\) under \(u=x^2, v=xy\). After choosing a rational origin, this becomes a degree-two isogeny and \(\tau\) is translation by a nonzero rational two-torsion point.
