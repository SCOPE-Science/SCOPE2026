For a natural rank-two diagonal matrix whose optimal Frobenius approximation rank is one, Gaussian two-column QB row pruning retains rank two with asymptotic probability \(2\arctan(1/\sqrt{c^2-1})/\pi\) at tolerance \(\tau=c\epsilon\). The probability equals one half at \(c=\sqrt2\), and the actual matrix-free look-ahead branch has the same limit; a rotation of the final block to singular directions would remove the excess rank.
