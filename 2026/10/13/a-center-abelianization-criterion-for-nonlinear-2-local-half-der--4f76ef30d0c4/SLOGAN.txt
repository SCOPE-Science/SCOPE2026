Every Lie algebra \(L\) over a field of characteristic different from \(2\) with \(Z(L)\ne0\) and \(\dim L/[L,L]\ge2\) admits a nonadditive 2-local \(\frac12\)-derivation. Two abelianization coordinates interpolate a two-parameter family of central half-derivations, explaining the recent totally graded filiform construction in intrinsic terms.
