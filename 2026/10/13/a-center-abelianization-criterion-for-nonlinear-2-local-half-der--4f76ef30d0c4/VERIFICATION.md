# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For any z in nonzero center and independent forms λ1,λ2 on L/[L,L], each D_ab=Id+(aλ1+bλ2)z is a linear half-derivation: the extra term vanishes on every bracket and does not affect the right brackets. f(s,t)=s when t=0 and 0 otherwise obeys f(cs,ct)=cf(s,t), even at c=0. Two-point interpolation is solvable when projection pairs are independent, and compatible by homogeneity when dependent; the surjective k² projection gives f(1,0)+f(0,1) != f(1,1), hence Δ not additive. Exactly the stated sufficient condition, not necessity. The optional H3(F5) finite sanity script does not replace the abstract proof.

## originality

PASS

The general sufficient intrinsic center/abelianization hypothesis removes all eleven totally graded filiform family presentations of Arzikulov–Shodiev. Their Theorem 24 already uses a homogeneous function and two free last-row entries in special filiform algebras, so the interpolation mechanism and those cases are NOT claimed as new. Earlier local/2-local half-derivation classifications and rigidity in Khudoyberdiyev–Yusupov do not imply sufficiency for arbitrary algebras with two-dimensional abelianization and a nonzero center. Search did not identify a stronger general lemma; older 2-local homogeneous-function constructions could coincide.

## value

PASS

A basis-free sufficient criterion using only two intrinsic invariants simultaneously unifies eleven classified complex filiform families and covers infinite-dimensional and nonnilpotent cases and arbitrary characteristic different from 2. It supplies a reusable construction for future classifications, not an isolated exercise or a claim of a new two-point interpolation technique.
