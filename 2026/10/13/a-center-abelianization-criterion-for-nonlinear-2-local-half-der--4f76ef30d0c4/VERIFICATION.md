The theorem is verified by a direct symbolic proof in `RESULT.md`. The critical checks are:

1. For every \(a,b\), centrality of \(z\) and \(\lambda_i([L,L])=0\) give the \(\frac12\)-derivation identity for \(D_{a,b}\).
2. For each pair of coordinate vectors in \(k^2\), the two interpolation equations are solvable: invertibility handles rank two and degree-one homogeneity handles rank at most one.
3. Independence of \(\lambda_1,\lambda_2\) makes the coordinate map onto \(k^2\), so the chosen homogeneous function yields an explicit nonadditivity witness.
4. For the motivating totally graded filiform families, the displayed brackets show that the abelianization is generated by the first two homogeneous classes and the top homogeneous component is central.

`verify.py` is an auxiliary finite sanity check on the Heisenberg Lie algebra over \(\mathbb F_5\). It exhaustively verifies all \(25\) members of the central half-derivation family, all \(15{,}625\) ordered interpolation pairs, and a nonadditivity witness. Its output is stored in `verification_output.txt` and terminates with `CHECK_OK`.

The finite computation is not an exhaustive proof of the general theorem and is not used to justify infinite or arbitrary-field quantifiers. The criterion is only sufficient, and characteristic \(2\) is outside scope.
