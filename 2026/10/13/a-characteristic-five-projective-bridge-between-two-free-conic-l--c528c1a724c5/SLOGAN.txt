The seven-line–conic arrangement \(\mathcal{CL}_{10}\) of Pokora retains all twelve ordinary intersection points over every characteristic \(p\ge7\), but at \(p=5\) its reduced support collapses to five lines and a smooth conic with just six ordinary intersections. An explicit matrix over \(\mathbb F_5\) identifies this exceptional reduction projectively with Pokora's separately constructed five-line–conic arrangement \(\mathcal{CL}_4\).
