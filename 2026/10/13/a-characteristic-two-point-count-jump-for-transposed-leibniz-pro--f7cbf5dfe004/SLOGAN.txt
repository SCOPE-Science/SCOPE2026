For the fixed Lie algebra of upper triangular two-by-two matrices, polynomial transposed-Leibniz-compatible commutative associative products number \(q^4+q^3-q^2\) over odd finite fields and \(q^6+2q^5-q^4-q^2\) over even finite fields. The characteristic-two jump comes from a trace-zero two-dimensional quotient and its square-zero central extensions, raising the parameter-variety dimension from \(4\) to \(6\).
