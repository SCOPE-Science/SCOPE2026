For every characteristic-two field \(K\), the four-element dihedral quandle algebra has full \(K\)-linear automorphism group \((M_2(K),+)\rtimes C_2\), where the involution acts by simultaneous row-and-column swaps. Over \(\mathbb F_{2^m}\), the group has exactly \(2^{4m+1}\) elements, including \(32\) over \(\mathbb F_2\).
