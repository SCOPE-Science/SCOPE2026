For \(N\ge3\) independently prepared pure photons whose internal states span at least \(N-1\) dimensions, every lossless \(N\)-port interferometer has strictly positive all-ports coincidence probability unless some pair of photons has proportional internal states. For a unique full-support dependence, the probability has an explicit positive lower bound, uniform over all interferometers, controlled by the photon permutation-Gram spectral gap.
