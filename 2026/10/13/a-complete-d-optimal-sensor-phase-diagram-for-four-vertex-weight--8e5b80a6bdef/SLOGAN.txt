On a weighted four-node diffusion path, the exact D-optimal single-sensor vertex is governed by an explicit two-threshold phase diagram with an outer-conductance bifurcation at \(p/r=2+\sqrt3\); the optimal vertex can be the first, second, or third node, and precise boundary ties occur. Four nodes are the smallest weighted path allowing a uniquely optimal interior sensor; the general horizon-independent determinant factorization is established prior work.
