On a weighted diffusion star with distinct positive conductances, D-optimal single-node sensing at every positive horizon is determined exactly by the leaf scores \(w_j^{m-1}/\prod_{i\ne j}|w_j-w_i|\): the hub and the smallest-conductance leaf never win, while every other conductance rank can win uniquely. Repeated conductances exhibit a complete observability bifurcation: a sole duplicate pair gives two tied observable leaves, but more repetition leaves every single-node Gramian singular.
