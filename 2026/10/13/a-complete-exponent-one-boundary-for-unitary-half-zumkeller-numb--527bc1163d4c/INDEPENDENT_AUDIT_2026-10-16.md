# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Proper unitary divisors of 2^a3^bq^c are exactly seven block products {1,A,B,AB,C,AC,BC}; balanced partition iff the seven signs yield N+C D=0. For a=1,b>=2, |D|>=B-3, |N|<=3(B+1) forces B<=9,C=5, giving 90. For b=1,a>=3, |D|>=A-4 and |N|<=4(A+1) force A<=24; further bound leaves A=8,C in {5,7} and A=16,C=5. At a=2,b=1, D=0 entails N!=0, otherwise |D|>=2 and C<=10 yielding A=4,C in {5,7}. The a=b=1 branch has an explicit partition for arbitrary prime q>3 and exponent c; all six exceptional witnesses are explicit. The full unitary-Zumkeller distinction for {84,120,168,240} follows from sigma*(n)<2n, and 60,90,6q^c have explicit full partitions. Independent subset tests independently corroborate bounded parameters without proving the infinite exclusion.

## originality

PASS

Das 2024 introduces unitary half-Zumkeller and examples including 84,120 but does not classify all exponent-one {2,3,q} numbers. OEIS A290467 records finite membership not exclusion for arbitrary exponents. The full Git text of the earlier Resultary three-prime-support even unitary Zumkeller theorem covers only full divisor equality, with list 6q^c,60,70,90; it cannot imply exclusion of additional HALF-only numbers and fails to include 84,120,168,240. The new theorem gives an exact necessary-and-sufficient classification of the larger proper-only condition on two natural infinite exponent-one faces.
