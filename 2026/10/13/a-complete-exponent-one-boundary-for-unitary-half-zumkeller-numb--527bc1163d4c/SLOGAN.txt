For \(n=2^a3^bq^c\) with \(q>3\) prime and \(\min(a,b)=1\), equal-sum partitions of the proper unitary divisors occur exactly for \(6q^c\) or \(n\in\{60,84,90,120,168,240\}\). Exactly four of the sporadic cases, \(84,120,168,240\), are not unitary Zumkeller, and the proof excludes all larger exponents by a signed-coefficient separation bound.
