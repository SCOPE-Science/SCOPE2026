Every progression \(An+B\) has \(4\mid\tau(An+B)\) universally exactly when its forced prime exponents already give a factor of \(4\), or give a single factor of \(2\) and forbid the quadratic congruence \(p t^2\equiv B\pmod A\). This classification yields \(\lvert\mathcal B_{1,109}\rvert=108\), contradicting the previously conjectured value \(54\).
