In Chtioui–Mabrouk–Makhlouf Example 2.2, the printed map \(R(e_1)=b e_2,\ R(e_2)=0\) satisfies the Hom-Jordan Rota–Baxter product equation for every parameter but satisfies the required twist commutation only when \((\lambda-1)b=0\). For the entire two-dimensional family and \(\operatorname{char}K\ne2\), the only nonzero Rota–Baxter families occur at \(\lambda=1\) and \(\lambda=0\), giving \(3q-2\) parameter-operator pairs over every odd \(\mathbf F_q\).
