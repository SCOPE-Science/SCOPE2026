PASS. The result corrects the mathematical domain of a central stability lemma: the equilibrium is stable transversely on a fixed-mass invariant leaf, not asymptotically stable in an ambient space that allows total-population perturbations. The neutral mode is biologically meaningful because it is exactly the population-mass direction.
