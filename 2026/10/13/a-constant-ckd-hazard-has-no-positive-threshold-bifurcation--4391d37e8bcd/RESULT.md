# A constant CKD hazard has no positive-threshold bifurcation
## Finding
Consider the CKD-only subsystem
\[
\dot S=\Lambda-(\lambda_k+\mu)S,\qquad
\dot I_k=\lambda_k S+\gamma_h H-\phi_k I_k,\qquad
\dot H=\zeta_k I_k-\psi H,
\]
where \(\Lambda,\mu>0\), \(\lambda_k,\zeta_k,\mu_k,\gamma_h,\mu_h\ge0\), \(\phi_k=\zeta_k+\mu_k+\mu\), and \(\psi=\gamma_h+\mu_h+\mu\). Define
\[
D=\phi_k\psi-\gamma_h\zeta_k
 =\zeta_k(\mu_h+\mu)+(\mu_k+\mu)(\gamma_h+\mu_h+\mu)>0.
\]
For every \(\lambda_k\ge0\), there is exactly one equilibrium,
\[
S^*=\frac{\Lambda}{\lambda_k+\mu},\qquad
I_k^*=\frac{\lambda_k\Lambda\psi}{(\lambda_k+\mu)D},\qquad
H^*=\frac{\lambda_k\Lambda\zeta_k}{(\lambda_k+\mu)D},
\]
and it is globally exponentially stable. Thus \(I_k^*>0\) for every \(\lambda_k>0\), while \(H^*>0\) whenever additionally \(\zeta_k>0\). No eigenvalue reaches zero as \(\lambda_k\) varies through a positive value, so the printed system has no transcritical or other local bifurcation at the paper's \(C_k=1\) value.

## Assumptions and scope
The statement concerns exactly the three-dimensional CKD-only subsystem obtained in the source after removing the COVID-specific compartments. The parameter \(\lambda_k\) is used as the source defines it: a constant per-capita noncommunicable CKD hazard, not an infection force generated by CKD prevalence. The conclusion is about this affine autonomous subsystem. It does not by itself determine equilibria, bifurcations, or optimal controls in the full seven-compartment COVID-19--CKD system.

## Proof
The equilibrium equations are triangular. The first gives \(S^*=\Lambda/(\lambda_k+\mu)\). From \(0=\zeta_k I_k^*-\psi H^*\), one has \(H^*=\zeta_k I_k^*/\psi\). Substitution into the \(I_k\) equation gives
\[
0=\lambda_k S^*-\left(\phi_k-\frac{\gamma_h\zeta_k}{\psi}\right)I_k^*,
\]
which yields the displayed formulas because \(D>0\). This also proves uniqueness.

After subtracting the equilibrium, the homogeneous coefficient matrix is
\[
M=\begin{{pmatrix}}
-(\lambda_k+\mu)&0&0\\
\lambda_k&-\phi_k&\gamma_h\\
0&\zeta_k&-\psi
\end{{pmatrix}}.
\]
Its characteristic polynomial factors exactly as
\[
(r+\lambda_k+\mu)\left(r^2+(\phi_k+\psi)r+D\right).
\]
The first root is negative. The quadratic has positive linear coefficient and positive constant term, so both of its roots have negative real parts. Hence \(M\) is Hurwitz for every \(\lambda_k\ge0\). Because the system is affine linear, every trajectory converges exponentially to the unique equilibrium.

The source defines
\[
C_k=\frac{\lambda_k\Lambda(\psi+\zeta_k)}{\mu D}.
\]
At the parameter value for which \(C_k=1\), one has \(\lambda_k=\mu D/[\Lambda(\psi+\zeta_k)]>0\). The actual equilibrium formula therefore gives \(I_k^*>0\), whereas the source's later proportionality \(I_k^*\propto C_k-1\) would give zero. Equivalently, the constant term of the actual characteristic polynomial at this parameter remains \((\lambda_k+\mu)D>0\), so no zero eigenvalue exists. The claimed transcritical exchange cannot occur in the printed system.

Finally,
\[
\frac{dI_k^*}{d\lambda_k}=\frac{\Lambda\psi\mu}{D(\lambda_k+\mu)^2}>0,
\qquad
\frac{dH^*}{d\lambda_k}=\frac{\Lambda\zeta_k\mu}{D(\lambda_k+\mu)^2}\ge0,
\]
so the CKD burden changes smoothly rather than switching on at a positive threshold.

## Relationship to prior work
The motivating article explicitly states that \(\lambda_k\) is a noncommunicable constant per-capita hazard, writes the three CKD-only equations above, observes that its CKD-free reference configuration is not a true equilibrium for \(\lambda_k>0\), and nevertheless introduces \(C_k\), claims an equilibrium proportional to \(C_k-1\), and claims a transcritical bifurcation at \(C_k=1\). The exact solution of the printed affine system gives a different implication: the unique stable equilibrium exists for every nonnegative hazard and is CKD-positive for every positive hazard.

Earlier COVID-19--kidney co-infection papers inspected for comparison use a prevalence-dependent kidney-disease force of infection rather than this source's constant noncommunicable hazard. Their reproduction-number thresholds therefore do not imply the present correction for the affine CKD-only subsystem.

## Limitations
This finding does not assess the medical appropriateness of modeling CKD by a constant hazard, and it does not reanalyze the full co-infection model. It also does not claim that threshold phenomena are impossible in other CKD models with nonlinear feedback or prevalence-dependent incidence. The literature search cannot exclude every unindexed comment or later correction, so the originality conclusion is specific to the inspected source, closely related open literature, and targeted database searches.
