In an infection-age SIS renewal model, any nonincreasing residence-time density makes the endemic characteristic determinant strictly positive-real in the closed right half-plane, whatever the nonnegative age-dependent infectivity profile and however large \(\mathcal R_0>1\) is. Hence endemic Hopf bifurcation is impossible; exponential-tailed durations give local exponential incidence stability, including gamma shapes \(0<j\leq1\).
