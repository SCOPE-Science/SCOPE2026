In the general heterogeneous network SIS model, high-degree targeting beats equal-budget uniform immunization exactly when vaccination intensity is positively correlated with the epidemic weight \(\phi(k)f_k'(0)\). An admissible two-degree example has \(\mathcal R_0^{\mathrm U}=15/16<1\) but \(\mathcal R_0^{\mathrm T}=9/8>1\), so degree targeting can fail to control an epidemic that uniform coverage suppresses.
