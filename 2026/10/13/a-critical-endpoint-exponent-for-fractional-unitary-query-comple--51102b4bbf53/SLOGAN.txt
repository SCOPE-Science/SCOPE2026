For fractional powers approaching an integer as \(c\varepsilon^\alpha\), the exponent \(\alpha=1\) is a sharp query-complexity boundary: \(0<\alpha<1\) still costs \(\Theta(\delta^{-1}\log(1/\varepsilon))\), while \(\alpha>1\) is eventually handled by zero queries near \(t=0\) and one query near \(t=1\). The critical \(\alpha=1\) constant-scale crossover remains open.
