For a uniform labelled Cayley tree, the expected total number of leaves conditioned on the distance between two fixed vertices increases and then decreases, with the first maximum at distance \((2n)^{1/3}+O(1)\). The exact finite-size transition is controlled by \((k^2+1)(k+2)\ge2n\), and the maximum exceeds the adjacent-vertex mean by \(e^{-1}+o(1)\).
