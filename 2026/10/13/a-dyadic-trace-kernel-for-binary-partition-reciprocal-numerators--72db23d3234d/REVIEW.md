# Scientific review

Correctness — PASS. Dyadic binomial expansions modulo four, multiplicity-series parity and even/odd binary-partition pairing give a complete all-index proof. The characteristic-two kernel is nonzero at one, proving exact root order; the standalone verifier checks indices 2 through 100 coefficientwise. Its finite tests do not replace the proof.

Originality — PASS, with residual risk. Ballantine–Beck–Feigon–Maurischat establish the same numerator, low-order coefficient congruences and an exact recurrence. Chen–Ono–Zhang establish coprimality and discuss shape conjectures. Neither inspected primary full text states the closed dyadic trace kernel or its exact modular root multiplicity. The existing exact recurrence can in principle determine all coefficients and may admit an equivalent unpublished simplification.

Value — PASS. A uniform trace-polynomial compression of all mod-four coefficients and an exact unbounded modular root multiplicity give a natural arithmetic structural invariant of a polynomial family studied for shape, factorization and coprimality, rather than a small-n table or low-degree coefficient exercise.

Limitations: No proof of the remaining shape conjectures or of irreducibility, and no claim of independent validation.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
