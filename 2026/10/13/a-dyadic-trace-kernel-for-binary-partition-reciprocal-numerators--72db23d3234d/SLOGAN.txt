The entire binary reciprocal subsum numerator admits a closed congruence modulo four governed by a finite characteristic-two dyadic trace kernel. After dividing by two, its exact \((1+x)\)-root multiplicity modulo two is \(d(n)-2^{\nu_2(2\lfloor n/2\rfloor)}\), exposing arbitrarily high modular degeneracy from just the binary partition index.
