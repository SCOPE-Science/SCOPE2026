For three distinct odd primes \(p<q<r\), the positive-shift Carmichael equation \(\lambda(pqr)\mid pqr+1\) admits an exact divisor sieve forcing \(q\le2p^2+p\), so each fixed least-prime fiber is finite and explicitly computable. The fibers \(p=3\) and \(p=5\) contain exactly two and five triples, respectively.
