For \(U_\chi=\exp[-i(\frac{\pi}{4}X\otimes X+\chi Y\otimes Y)]\), one fixed local rotation \(R_y(\pi/2)\) makes the inverse image of every rank-three MEMS PPT, with exact spectrum \(\{0,1/3,1/3-\gamma/2,1/3+\gamma/2\}\) independent of \(\chi\). Together with the analogous rank-two spectrum, this gives an explicit analytic all-purity certificate for the second global perfect-entangler family that was reported numerically in 2013.
