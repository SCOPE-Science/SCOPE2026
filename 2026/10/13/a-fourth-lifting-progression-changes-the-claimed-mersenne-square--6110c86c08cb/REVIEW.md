# Review of the four-progression Mersenne density bound

**Correctness (PASS).** The four exact prime-square congruences are directly checked; geometric-series divisibility propagates each through all its exponent multiples. Inclusion-exclusion gives density \(47/210\) for the first three. For multiples \(110k\), the complement of those first three is exactly \(\gcd(k,6)=1\), with density \(1/330\). The union therefore has density \(262/1155\), a proven lower density bound for the full nonsquarefree set. A standalone exact-arithmetic checker verifies the residue arithmetic.

**Originality (PASS, subject to literature-access risk).** The published abstract of arXiv:2609.00039v1 asserts an exact density \(47/210\) for the full Mersenne exponent set. The disjoint \(11^2\)-lifting progression supplies a strictly larger lower bound, not a corollary of that asserted equality. Targeted published-record searches for the four-period calculation and its numerical constant returned no matching result. The original preprint's full text was unavailable; it may contain explanatory context or a restriction that is not in the abstract.

**Value (PASS).** A claimed exact natural density is contradicted by an infinite family of explicitly characterized counterexamples having positive density \(1/330\). This is not an isolated numerical exception: it identifies the structural source of a missing contribution, namely independent prime-square lifting at \(11\), and supplies a corrected rigorous lower bound relevant to the Mersenne family.

**Closest literature and limits.** Laniewski (2026) is the directly relevant and conflicting abstract; the factorization and lifting observations themselves are classical. The full density may exceed \(262/1155\), and the work does not establish its existence. Only the abstract, not the full preprint, was materially readable.
