For singleton-coordinate randomized subspace Nesterov acceleration on a diagonal strongly convex quadratic, the exact long-run expected-objective contraction has a positive fractional-power correction: \(1-2\sqrt\kappa/d+[2(d-1)/d^4]^{1/3}\kappa^{2/3}+O_d(\kappa^{5/6})\). A three-cycle in its exact second-moment dynamics explains this nonanalytic rate and the factor-of-two leading improvement over the general certified contraction on this family.
