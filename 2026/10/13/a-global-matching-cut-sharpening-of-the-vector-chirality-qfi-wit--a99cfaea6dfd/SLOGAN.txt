For the vector-chirality QFI witness on an even periodic spin-\(1/2\) chain, retaining the global matching cut count sharpens the published finite-size bound to \(F_Q\le qk^2+s^2+N-\max\{0,2k-N\}\). The improvement is strict for every \(k>N/2\) and reaches the natural \(F_Q\le N^2\) endpoint at \(k=N\).
