# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The scheme isomorphism \(V(F_i-1)\times(\mathbb G_m)^r\cong D(\prod_iF_i)\) has explicit mutually inverse regular morphisms because each \(F_i\) has a **primitive basis character**. Since the principal open is integral, the quotient by the unsaturated equations is a domain. Weighted Euler identities give Jacobian rank \(r\), and the nonzero dense Cox-torus point removes all coordinate saturation. The exact example checker returns `VERIFY_OK`, but the universal argument does not depend on this finite computation. The source's Assumption 1 provides precisely the needed integral basis via its unimodular matrix.

## Originality — PASS

Calvo Cortes–Telen explicitly state the unsaturated ideal generation as Conjecture 3.11, after proving only its coordinate saturation in Corollary 3.10; their Example 4.4 has an inconclusive saturation calculation. Cox's foundational quotient/graded-ring results supply background, not this ideal equality. The 2025/2026 finite-representation-type paper's Theorem 5.3 decomposes a ring *after every coordinate has been inverted*, and its Theorem 9.2 establishes unsaturated primeness only for its particular representation-theoretic family. The present scheme-level product decomposition over \(D(\prod_iF_i)\) extends through all coordinate boundaries for every unimodular toric chart, which neither comparison gives. A differently indexed general semi-invariant slice theorem remains a historical originality risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The theorem supplies an exact general solution to a published conjecture, replacing potentially prohibitive symbolic saturation with a uniform structural argument. It also proves smooth complete-intersection structure and identifies the precise primitive-character mechanism; the nonprimitive example distinguishes its indispensable hypothesis. This is a motivated answer to an explicitly recorded algebraic-geometric gap, not a new name for an example or a numerical rerun.
