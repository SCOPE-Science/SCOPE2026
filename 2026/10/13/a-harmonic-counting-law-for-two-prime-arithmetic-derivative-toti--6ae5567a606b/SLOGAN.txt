For each fixed odd prime \(p\), the number of integers \(p^a q^b\le X\) with \(q>p\) prime and arithmetic derivative equal to Euler’s totient is asymptotic to \(\frac{pH_{p-2}}{p-1}\frac{\log X}{(\log\log X)^2}\). The harmonic coefficient comes from exactly \(p-2\) admissible prime residue classes, weighted by their forced exponents.
