For prime-power \(m\), retaining the Euler-constant term in the harmonic estimate sharpens Yudin's affine factorial perfect-power construction to \(N\le m[1+(m-1)(R+\pi(R))]\) with \(R=\lceil e^{m-\gamma}\rceil\). Its leading factor-count coefficient becomes \(e^{-\gamma}\) instead of \(2\), and this coefficient is asymptotically optimal within the harmonic-majorant parameter relaxation of the same zero-sum proof.
