The proposed five-message, length-eight recursive Z-channel codebook from Chen–Lin–Moser is strictly suboptimal whenever the probability \(p\) of a \(1\to0\) flip exceeds the unique root of \(2p^3+p-1=0\). A disjoint-support codebook drawn from the same published weak-flip column family has a rigorously larger maximum-likelihood success probability throughout that interval.
