# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The half-plane argument is exact: for \(\operatorname{Re}s\ge0\) and \(0<\alpha\le1\), the principal power \(s^\alpha\) has nonnegative real part, so the common distributed-order symbol cannot equal an eigenvalue of a Hurwitz Jacobian. The source's own root criterion then gives local asymptotic stability. The baseline endemic state is independently reconstructed and its quartic satisfies strict Routh-Hurwitz inequalities with comfortable positive margins.

## Originality
PASS. The general characteristic-root criterion is established in prior distributed-order literature and is explicitly treated as prior work. The 2026 source derives the same characteristic structure but only searches selected memory distributions numerically. The inspected 2011 theorem and 2013 review do not state the positive-real Hurwitz corollary or certify the 2026 SEIRS baseline. The claimed novelty is limited to that robust corollary and its exact source-specific application.

## Value
PASS. The result upgrades the paper's finite memory scan into an all-memory statement at its principal endemic parameter set. It shows that support and density can alter transient relaxation there but cannot change local asymptotic stability, eliminating an otherwise infinite family of oscillatory-crossing checks.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest general source is Saberi Najafi, Refahi Sheikhani, and Ansari (2011), DOI 10.1155/2011/175323, which supplies the distributed-order characteristic-root criterion. Rivero et al. (2013), DOI 10.1155/2013/356215, reviews that framework. The result is local, assumes a common scalar distributed-order operator with orders in \((0,1]\), and does not preclude stability changes when epidemiological parameters alter the Jacobian or when compartments use different memory operators.
