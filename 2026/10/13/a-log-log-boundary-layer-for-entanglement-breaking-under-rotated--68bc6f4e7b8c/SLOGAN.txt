For repeated qubit phase damping composed with a fixed, small transverse rotation, the direct entanglement-breaking index is finite at every nonzero misalignment but diverges like \(2\log(1/\theta)/|\log\lambda|\) with a sharp negative \(\log\log(1/\theta)\) correction as the axes align. An exact two-term recurrence decides breaking at every iteration and yields the universal rescaled transition profile.
