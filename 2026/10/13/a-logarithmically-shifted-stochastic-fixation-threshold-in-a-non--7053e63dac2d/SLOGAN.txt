A nonlinear pair/triple public-goods game admits a sharp finite-population fixation trichotomy: cooperation fixation from its stable interior equilibrium can be exponentially unlikely, polynomially unlikely, or overwhelmingly likely within the same deterministic bistability phase. At equal quasipotential heights fixation is only of order \(N^{-1/2}\); a nontrivial logistic crossover occurs after a \((\log N)/N\) shift of the benefit parameter.
