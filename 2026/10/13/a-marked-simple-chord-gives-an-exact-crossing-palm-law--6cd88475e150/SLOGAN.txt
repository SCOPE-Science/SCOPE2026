Size-biasing a uniform chord diagram by its number of simple chords and then forgetting the marked chord exactly reduces the crossing count from \(X_n\) to \(X_{n-1}\). This forces \(\operatorname{Cov}(S_n,X_n)=-2n(n-1)/(3(2n-1))\) and the exact correlation \(\operatorname{Corr}(S_n,X_n)=-\sqrt{5/(n+3)}\).
