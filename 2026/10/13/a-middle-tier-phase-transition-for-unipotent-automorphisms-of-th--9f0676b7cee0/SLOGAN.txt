For a three-antichain ordinal-sum chain-polytope toric variety with outer sizes \(a,b\ge2\), a singleton middle tier yields a nonabelian unipotent radical of dimension \(2^a+2^b+a+b-1\) and nilpotency class \(\max(a,b)+1\). With two or more middle elements, that radical becomes abelian of dimension \(a+b+c\); the complete Demazure root systems give both group classifications.
