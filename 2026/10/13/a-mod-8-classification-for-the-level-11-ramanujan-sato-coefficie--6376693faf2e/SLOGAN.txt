For the level-\(11\) Ramanujan–Sato coefficients \(c_{11}(n)\), the exact minimum dyadic valuation occurs on only two rays: \(\nu_2(c_{11}(n))=2\) exactly when \(n=2^k\) or \(n=3\cdot2^k\). Equivalently, the coefficients are \(4\pmod8\) on those indices and \(0\pmod8\) everywhere else for \(n\ge1\).\n
