In \(G(n,m)\), the covariance between degree-one vertices and copies of any fixed motif of minimum degree at least two has an exact sign threshold depending only on the motif's numbers of vertices and edges. For triangles it switches from negative to positive precisely between \(m=n\) and \(m=n+1\) for \(n\ge5\), whereas in \(G(n,p)\) the same covariance is always negative.
