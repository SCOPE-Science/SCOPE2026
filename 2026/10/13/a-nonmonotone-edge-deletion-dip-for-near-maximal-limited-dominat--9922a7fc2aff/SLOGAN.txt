For \(K_{n,n}\) with \(t\) edges of a matching deleted and fixed capacity \(k=n-2\), the limited domination number is three exactly for \(2\le t<n\) and four for \(t\in\{0,1,n\}\). All minimum sets are classified, with \(2(n-t)\binom t2\) minimizers in the three-vertex regime: deleting more edges first lowers and then raises the optimum.
