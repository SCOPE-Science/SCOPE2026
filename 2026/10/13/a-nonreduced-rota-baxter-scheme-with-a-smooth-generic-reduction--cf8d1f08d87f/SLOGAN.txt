The complete three-dimensional skew-nilpotent Rota–Baxter operator **scheme** is nonreduced at every weight, and it supports a first-order deformation that cannot be lifted to second order. Yet its reduction is geometrically integral, becomes smooth at nonzero weight, and has Grothendieck class \(\mathbb L^6\) at weight zero versus \(\mathbb L^6+\mathbb L^4\) at nonzero weight.
