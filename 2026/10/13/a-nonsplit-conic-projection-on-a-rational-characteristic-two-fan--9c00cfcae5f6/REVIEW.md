# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Verified coefficient quadrics x^2+yz, y^2, z^2 have no common projective zero, making X smooth by partial derivatives in base parameters and giving the pulled-back Euler kernel E (rank two) and rho:X=P(E). In affine z=1 the map has target P=r^2+w,Q=w^2, with w^2=Q,r^4=P^2+Q, hence a purely inseparable function-field extension of degree four; ample pullback O(2) confirms finite morphism. For K=k(a,b), valuation r=a^{-1} in k(b)((r)) and primitive coordinates force y in rR, then modr x^2+bz^2=0 forces x,z in rR since b is nonsquare, contradiction. Thus no K-point despite all affine fibers being smooth. Explicit coordinate change gives quaternion reduced-norm conic [ab,1/b)=[ab,b); the latter nontrivial 2-torsion class extends as Brauer-Severi smooth conic over A^2. Direct substitution checks points [sqrt(a):1:0] and [0:theta/a:1] over the two degree-2 splitting extensions. An independent characteristic-two polynomial substitution checked the splitting identity; the valuation, not numerical sampling, is the proof.

## originality

PASS

Tanaka Example 7.5 constructs exactly this smooth Fano (1,2) threefold and triple-line discriminant, which are expressly excluded from new claims. Its inspected example does not compute generic conic anisotropy, Brauer class or minimal separable/inseparable splitting fields, nor the opposite projection’s explicit pulled-back Euler-bundle structure. Auel et al. Section 2 discuss an anisotropic but totally singular generic conic ux^2+vy^2+wz^2=0; inserting the mixed yz term creates a smooth generic conic and their specific argument does not imply its anisotropy. Their general quaternion correspondence is used as background, not claimed as novel.

## value

PASS

The same rational Fano total space has one split projective-line bundle projection and one nonsplit generically smooth conic projection, a geometric distinction invisible from total-space rationality or discriminant support. The exact Brauer class and degree-two splitting extensions are useful invariants for birational conic-bundle geometry. This is a motivated explicit example, not an arbitrary symbolic rewrite of the published discriminant.
