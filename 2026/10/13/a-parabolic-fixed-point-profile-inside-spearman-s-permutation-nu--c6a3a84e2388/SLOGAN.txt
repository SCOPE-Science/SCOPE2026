Under Spearman's uniform permutation null, a fixed point at rank \(i\) raises the conditional mean of \(\rho\) by an exact parabolic amount \(3(2i-n-1)^2/((n-1)^2(n+1))\). The total fixed-point count has \(\operatorname{Cov}(F,\rho)=1/(n-1)\) and \(\operatorname{Corr}(F,\rho)=1/\sqrt{n-1}\), so its exact least-squares projection is \(1+\rho\).
