On the one-dimensional Dirichlet Poisson matrix, the second exact conjugate-gradient iterate changes its sign response to an arbitrarily weak isolated positive source depending on whether a separated discrete hat has one peak or a two-point plateau. The plateau case has a unique sharp source-amplitude threshold for every width \(k\ge2\), approaching \(1-2/k\) asymptotically, even though the exact Poisson solution is strictly positive.
