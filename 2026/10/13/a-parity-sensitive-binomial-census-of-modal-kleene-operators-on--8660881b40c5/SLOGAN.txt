On an \(m\)-element Kleene chain, meet-preserving modal operators satisfying positivity and consistency have a unique low/high midpoint normal form, yielding closed parity-dependent binomial counts for all chain sizes. The same classification counts exactly those operators preserving top, with the two families giving \(1,2,5,13,40,115,\ldots\) and \(1,2,3,9,21,70,\ldots\), respectively.
