# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact four-environment failure decomposition follows by conditional independence of seeds, not by independence of years. The strict-convexity and finite-budget allocation bounds follow by elementary exponential comparisons; the rate and bounded-deviation conclusion hold uniformly for all admissible fixed environmental laws with positive exclusive-good masses. The phase correction is a rigorous translated-lattice minimization with an exponentially smaller joint-good term, while the endpoint cases reduce to a coercive one-integer minimization. We checked the cutoff where the unrestricted lattice minimizer is feasible, sign directions in the discrete difference, positivity and boundary conventions. Finite exact-rational enumeration independently checks identities and optimizer inequalities but is not used to claim an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with specified coverage uncertainty

The directly inspected Snyder (2006) full text treats evolutionary dispersal/dormancy fitness, not finite-cohort at-least-one-establishment rescue. The full open analysis of ten Brink–Gremer–Kokko (2020) is similarly a long-run evolutionary and within-season timing analysis. Valleriani (2006) is closely motivated but only abstract/material identifying the fitness objective was accessible; it remains a coverage risk. Bai–Yun–Chung (1991) studies common-cause reliability with cost-rate optimization and selectable redundant population size, rather than fixed-budget two-season allocation. The claims do not follow simply from these published objectives. The generic exponential-balancing argument belongs to classical asymptotics, so independent general allocation coverage may still imply some components; we restrict originality to the precise joint theorem and list this risk rather than claiming a new general principle.

## Value — PASS

Finite assisted regeneration frequently requires the chance of at least one recruit rather than long-run geometric-mean population growth. Shared bad-year shocks are ecological mechanisms that invalidate independent-year calculations. The exact theorem identifies when positive banking is structurally mandatory, gives a sharp success-improvement rate, quantifies fixed-budget integer rounding effects (including a nonconvergent oscillatory prefactor), and identifies a qualitative support threshold when one exclusive-good-year outcome becomes impossible. This supplies a mathematically interpretable benchmark for the reliability objective; it does not by itself demonstrate field applicability or an evolutionary stable strategy.

## Relationship to literature and limitations

Further earlier reliability-allocation theorems could dominate the explicit two-channel asymptotic classification. Exact formulas assume a precommitted split of one fixed cohort, shared binary seasonal environments, and conditional independence between seed outcomes. Nonadaptive finite-horizon success is distinct from evolutionary fitness.
