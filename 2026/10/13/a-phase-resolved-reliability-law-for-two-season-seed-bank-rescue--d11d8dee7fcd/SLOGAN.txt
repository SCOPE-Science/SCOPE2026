When both seasons have a positive chance of being the only viable season, an optimal two-year seed-cohort split converges to a universal fraction \(\log(1/b)/(\log(1/a)+\log(1/b))\), regardless of temporal environmental correlation, and its avoidable failure probability obeys a sharp phase-oscillating exponential law. Remove either exclusive-good-season possibility and the optimal allocation fraction jumps to the corresponding endpoint.
