For iid Bernoulli samples, the strongest attainable Pearson correlation between the two smallest order statistics approaches \(\sqrt{c_*}/(1+c_*)\approx0.479413\), where \(c_*\) is the unique positive root of \(e^c=1+c+c^2\). Every optimizing success probability lies in the sharp rare-zero window \(n(1-p_n)\to c_*\).
