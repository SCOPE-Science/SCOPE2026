For every base \(b\ge8\) with \(3\nmid b\) and \(b\), \(b-2\), \(2b-3\) squarefree, the squarefree integers \(b(2b-3)\) and \((b-2)(2b-3)\) collide under \(H_b(n)=nS_b(n)\). Such bases have lower natural density at least \(7/900\), giving a positive-density partial resolution of the squarefree noninjectivity question.
