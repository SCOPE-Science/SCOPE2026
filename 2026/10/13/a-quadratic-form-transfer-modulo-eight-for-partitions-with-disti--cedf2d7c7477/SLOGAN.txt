For partitions with distinct even parts, the odd-minus-even part-sum statistic has a coefficientwise modulo-eight correction counted by representations of \(8n+1\) as \(x^2+y^2\) or \(x^2+2y^2\). An odd valuation of any prime congruent to \(5\) or \(7\) modulo \(8\) annihilates the correction, yielding ten new infinite weighted congruence progressions modulo \(25\) and \(49\).
