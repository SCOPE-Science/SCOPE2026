# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. A separating diagonal one-parameter subgroup reduces the Euler characteristic to monomial fixed points. Catalecticant ranks classify those fixed monomials exactly: from degree three onward only pure powers and tangent monomials can lie in the second secant; in degree two every quadratic monomial does. The resulting counts are \((n+1)^2\) and \(\binom{n+2}{2}\), respectively. Constructible additivity then gives the exact border-rank-two values.

The bundled checker independently enumerates exponent vectors and the relevant monomial catalecticant ranks over a finite grid. Finite computation is regression evidence only.

## Originality
PASS. Full-text inspection of the closest archival Veronese-secant sources found rank-stratification and flattening results but no Euler-characteristic statement. Targeted searches in secant, symmetric-tensor, fixed-point, and characteristic-class language did not locate the sharp degree-two phase break in higher dimension. Nogueira's later rational-normal-curve work covers the \(n=1\) specialization, and the quadratic symmetric-determinantal boundary is also prior knowledge; neither implies the uniform \(n\ge2\), \(d\ge3\) formula.

Residual risk remains because an unindexed characteristic-class computation could imply the same Euler values.

## Value
PASS. Second Veronese secants are canonical symmetric border-rank varieties. The theorem gives a complete dimension-and-degree-uniform Euler classification and identifies an exact structural discontinuity between quadrics and every higher Veronese embedding.
