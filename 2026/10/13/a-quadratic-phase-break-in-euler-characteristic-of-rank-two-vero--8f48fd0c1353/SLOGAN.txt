The second secant of the quadratic Veronese has Euler characteristic \(\binom{n+2}{2}\), while every higher-degree Veronese second secant has the degree-independent value \((n+1)^2\). Equivalently, exact symmetric border rank two has compactly supported Euler characteristic \(n(n+1)/2\) in degree two and \(n(n+1)\) from degree three onward.
