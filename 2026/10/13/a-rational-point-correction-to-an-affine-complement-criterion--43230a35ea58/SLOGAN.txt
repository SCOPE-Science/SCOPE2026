Over a characteristic-zero field, the affine-complement criterion for two fibre-hyperplane divisors on a projective bundle needs the unique collision point to be \(k\)-rational, not merely a single closed point. On \(\mathbf P^1\times\mathbf P^1\) over \(\mathbf Q\), graphs of degrees \(1\) and \(2\) can collide in one closed cubic point while their complex complement has \(\chi_c=3\), so it is not \(\mathbf A^2\).
