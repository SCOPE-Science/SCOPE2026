At fixed endpoint \(n\ge2\), iterating the harmonic-number construction \(j\) times gives the exact asymptotic \(\prod_{i=1}^{j}h_i(n)\sim\frac{j}{n}\prod_{r=1}^{n-2}\log_rj\). For each fixed \(n\ge3\), the iterate itself satisfies \(h_j(n)=1+\frac1{j+1}+\frac{1+o(1)}{j\log j}\), a reverse-index asymptotic complementary to the previously studied endpoint limit.
