The lower-plus-upper Wythoff representation count has a deterministic golden-rotation shape: \(r(n)=n\Lambda(\{n/\varphi\})+O(\log n)\) for an explicit triangular-with-plateau profile \(\Lambda\). Consequently \(r(n)/n\) has an explicit limiting law with a continuous density and an atom at \(\varphi^{-2}\), and \(\sum_{n\le N}r(n)\sim(\sqrt5-2)N^2/2\).
