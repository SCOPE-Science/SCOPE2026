# Review

## Correctness
PASS. For independent exponential components the CDNCEx definition reduces exactly to the one-dimensional integral \(J_\lambda(t)\). Direct integration and differentiation prove \(J_\lambda'(t)>0\) for every positive threshold. The map \(\psi(x)=2x\) satisfies the source's dispersive-order dilation condition. Exact evaluation at \(\lambda=\log2\) gives
\[
\mathcal{CJ}^{\mathbf Y}_i(2,2)-\mathcal{CJ}^{\mathbf X}_i(2,2)
=\frac{57-80\log2}{36\log2}>0,
\]
so the theorem's asserted order is contradicted. The positivity proof uses only an elementary Taylor lower bound for \(e^{7/10}\), not numerical evidence.
