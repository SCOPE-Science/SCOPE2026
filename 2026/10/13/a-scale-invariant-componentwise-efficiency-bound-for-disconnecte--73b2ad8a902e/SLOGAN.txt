For disconnected exact detailed-balance α-potential games, componentwise coefficient comparison gives a scale-invariant social-efficiency bound \(B_{\rm comp}=\max_k M_k/m_k\) without changing the potential-minimizer set. It is exactly the optimal normalization of the interaction-restricted coefficient certificate, not of the source's all-pair Corollary-4.8 expression: two components with weights \((1,10)\) show the latter may optimize to \(20\) while \(B_{\rm comp}=11\).
