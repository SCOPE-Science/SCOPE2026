In disconnected exact \(\alpha\)-potential games, the detailed-balance weights have an arbitrary positive scale on each interaction component, so a global efficiency certificate can depend on a gauge that does not change the selected policies. Componentwise coefficient comparison removes that artifact and yields the scale-invariant bound \(\max_k M_k/m_k\), which is exactly the best normalization of the source's Corollary-4.8 coefficient bound.
