Because every infectious strain in the 2026 model feeds one shared exposed class, the correct threshold is \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{pool}}=C_0+\sum_j A_j\), not \(\max_j\sqrt{C_0+A_j}\). An exact two-strain example has the published threshold \(9/10<1\) while the true threshold is \(27/20>1\), and the disease-free Jacobian has a positive eigenvalue.
