For every \(n\ge2\), approximating \(\frac23\operatorname{Bin}(3n,1/2)\) by any Poisson-binomial law in \(W_\infty\) costs at least \(2/3\), and this lower constant is attained at \(n=2\). The uniform obstruction comes from an explicit log-concavity violation in the only integer rounding permitted by a smaller transport radius.
