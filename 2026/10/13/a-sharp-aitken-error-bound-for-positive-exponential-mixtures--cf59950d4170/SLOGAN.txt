For every positive mixture of decaying geometric modes with rates in \([\lambda_-,\lambda_+]\), Aitken's \(\Delta^2\) estimate stays on the same side of the limit and contracts the current error by at most \(\bigl((\lambda_+-\lambda_-)/(2-\lambda_+-\lambda_-)\bigr)^2\), with equality on a two-endpoint mixture. In the one-sided case \([0,q]\), its sharp factor \((q/(2-q))^2\) is strictly better than the raw two-step factor \(q^2\).
