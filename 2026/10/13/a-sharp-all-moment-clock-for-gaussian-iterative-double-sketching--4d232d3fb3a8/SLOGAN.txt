The published Gaussian nested-sketch IDS recurrence has a sharp moment clock: after \(t\) updates, its source-initialized scalar least-squares error has finite \(p\)th moment exactly when \(r>p(2t+1)\). In particular the unconditional prediction mean-square becomes infinite at \(r\le4t+2\) even though the same fixed Gaussian sketch inverse exists almost surely and every gradient sketch has more rows than the Hessian sketch.
