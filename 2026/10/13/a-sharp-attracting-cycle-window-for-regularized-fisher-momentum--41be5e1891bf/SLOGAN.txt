The published regularized-Fisher momentum update has a sharp attracting-two-cycle window \(2<\eta h(1-\beta)/[\rho(1+\beta)]<4\) on a scalar strongly convex quadratic. With the actual bias correction and zero source moment, a nondegenerate interval of initial parameters converges geometrically to a strictly nonoptimal alternating orbit for every \(0\le\beta\le1/100\) at \(\eta h/\rho=3\).
