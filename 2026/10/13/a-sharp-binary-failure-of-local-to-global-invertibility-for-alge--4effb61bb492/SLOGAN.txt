Over \(\mathbf F_2^3\), a three-dimensional operator space can consist entirely of singular maps even though its algebraic reflexive hull contains the identity; the displayed example has \(\operatorname{Ref}_a(E)=E\oplus\mathbf F_2I_3\). Ambient dimension \(3\) is minimal over \(\mathbf F_2\), and the obstruction persists in every higher binary dimension.
