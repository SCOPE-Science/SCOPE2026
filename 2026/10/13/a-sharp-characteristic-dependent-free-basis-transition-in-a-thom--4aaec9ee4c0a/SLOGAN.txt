For the reduced Thom–Sebastiani plane divisor \(F=\prod_j\ell_j\bigl(\prod_j\ell_j^{k_j}-z^d\bigr)\), the full logarithmic-derivation basis changes sharply from coefficient degrees \((1,m-1,d)\) when the characteristic does not divide \(d\) to \((0,1,d+m-1)\) when it does. In the latter case, the nonlinear component has every smooth tangent through one fixed exterior point, although the reduced divisor remains free.
