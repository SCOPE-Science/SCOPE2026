In a chemostat with two identical obligate mutualists, equal population removal rates still permit stable oscillations: a positive-equilibrium Hopf crossing is possible for some parameters **exactly when** biomass removal \(d\) exceeds twice nutrient dilution \(D\). Every positive cycle is synchronized, and \(d\le D\) excludes them all; a fully rational Monod example has a rigorously negative first Lyapunov coefficient.
