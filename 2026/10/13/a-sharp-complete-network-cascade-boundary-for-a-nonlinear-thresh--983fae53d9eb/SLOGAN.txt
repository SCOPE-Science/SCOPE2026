On a unit-weight complete network with one seed, the clipped nonlinear opinion relaxation has an exact full-cascade threshold \(\tau_c=(1+(n-2)\sqrt\gamma/2)^2\), exceeding the strict discrete threshold \(1\) whenever \(n\ge3\). At the discrete equality threshold, the extra cascade persists for every admissibly small positive coupling, but its half-activation time diverges sharply as \(\pi\gamma^{-3/4}/\sqrt{n-2}\).
