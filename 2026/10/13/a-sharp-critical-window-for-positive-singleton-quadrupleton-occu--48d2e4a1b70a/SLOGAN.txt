For independent uniform allocations of \(n\) balls to \(m\ge3\) boxes, the covariance of the counts of singleton and four-ball boxes is positive on exactly one consecutive integer interval centered near \(n=2m\). This interval has endpoints \(2m\pm\sqrt{2m}+o(\sqrt m)\), and throughout its square-root scaling window the covariance converges to \(\tfrac43e^{-4}(1-z^2/2)\), even though its first-order fixed-load covariance density is never positive.
