# Scientific review — sharp four-step renewal minorant

## Correctness — PASS

The exact recurrence with step law supported on \(\{1,2,3,4\}\) reduces the all-time renewal minimum to its first three positive indices: every later renewal mass is a convex combination of four earlier masses, including \(u_0=1\). Each of the three slack polynomials between \(P=Q_4+2p_1p_2p_4\) and these masses is given by an explicit sum of nonnegative monomials or squares. The cumulative-coordinate truncations are exactly \(x\), \(xy\), and a cubic, satisfying the source's restricted degree requirements. A one-dimensional face identity proves that coefficients larger than \(2\) fail; exact symbolic identity replay supplies a reproducible cross-check. No infinite conclusion is inferred from finite enumeration.

## Originality — PASS

Nikolov–Savov, *Properties and conjectures regarding discrete renewal sequences* (arXiv:2307.00545, 2023; *Mathematics and Informatics* 67, 2024), state the **same** polynomial \(Q_k\), the two comparison classes, and Conjecture 3.7 for every \(k\geq3\). Their Proposition 3.5 proves \(Q_k\) is a minorant; Proposition 3.8 settles only \(k=3\). Inspection of the full source preprint and journal text found no admissible dominating cubic at \(k=4\). The present construction contradicts both their proposed maximality and largest-element properties, rather than merely rephrasing their lower bound. Targeted searches for the source, equivalent envelope language, and the explicit perturbation did not identify a published dominating theorem. This is a same-model bibliographic assessment, not independent priority certification. Risk: an unindexed or differently phrased subsequent source could still contain the example.

## Value — PASS

The finding resolves a concrete, explicitly formulated open conjectural direction at its **first unproved support size** and disproves both clauses using a simple uniform certificate valid on the entire probability simplex. The coefficient-sharp perturbation identifies a substantive boundary phenomenon and motivates a revised extremal classification. It does not present a full classification of maximal minorants for support four or larger.

## Closest literature and limitations

Direct source: N. Nikolov and M. Savov, arXiv:2307.00545, Conjecture 3.7 and Propositions 3.5 and 3.8; DOI:10.53656/math2024-2-1-pro. Renewal-age/residual-life correlations and renewal-tail statistical bias concern different functionals, not competing cubic minorants. The result is confined to four possible step lengths and to coefficient sharpness within \(Q_4+\lambda p_1p_2p_4\). Further independent bibliographic verification is outstanding.
