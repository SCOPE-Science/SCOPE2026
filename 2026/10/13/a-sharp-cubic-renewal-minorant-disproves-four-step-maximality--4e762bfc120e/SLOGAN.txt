For renewal steps supported on \(\{1,2,3,4\}\), the conjecturally maximal cubic minorant \(Q_4\) is globally dominated, and strictly improved when \(p_1p_2p_4>0\), by the admissible polynomial \(Q_4+2p_1p_2p_4\). The improvement lies even in the restricted cumulative-degree class, disproving both clauses of Nikolov–Savov Conjecture 3.7 at \(k=4\); the coefficient \(2\) is sharp within this perturbation family.
