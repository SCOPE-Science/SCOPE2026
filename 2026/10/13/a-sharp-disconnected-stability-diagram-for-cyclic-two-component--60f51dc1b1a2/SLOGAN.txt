A cyclic two-component scalar heavy-ball method can be stable, then unstable, and then stable again as its fixed stepsize increases. The sharp phase diagram has disconnected stability precisely above the curvature contrast \(\bigl(r+\sqrt{1+r^2}\bigr)^2\), with \(r=(1-\beta)/(1+\beta)\), and at equality loses exactly one isolated stepsize. The two resonant choices \(\eta=(1+\beta)/a\) and \(\eta=(1+\beta)/b\) attain the smallest possible per-cycle spectral radius \(\beta\), even for arbitrarily mismatched curvatures.
