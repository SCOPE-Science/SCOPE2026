For two habitats with unequal regulation rates and independent fixed-intensity environmental noise, regional stationary variance has a unique finite optimal dispersal rate exactly when the noise-intensity ratio lies between two explicit thresholds; otherwise it is monotone. When isolated habitat variances are equal, the optimum is \(d=\sqrt{ab/2}\), and the maximum possible relative reduction is strictly below one half but approaches that bound in a strongly heterogeneous limit.
