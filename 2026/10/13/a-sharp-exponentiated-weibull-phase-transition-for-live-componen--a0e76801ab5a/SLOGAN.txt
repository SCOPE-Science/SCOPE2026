For exponentiated-Weibull components, the cumulative residual extropy of the last live component after coherent-system failure is asymptotic to \(-\lambda^\beta t^{1-\beta}/(4\beta)\). This produces a sharp phase transition at \(\beta=1\): the CRJ vanishes, stabilizes at \(-\lambda/4\), or diverges to \(-\infty\) according as \(\beta>1\), \(\beta=1\), or \(0<\beta<1\), independently at leading order of \(\alpha\) and the system signature.
