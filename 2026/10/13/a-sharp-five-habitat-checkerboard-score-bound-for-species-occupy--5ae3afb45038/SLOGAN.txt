For species found in exactly two of five habitats, the classical total checkerboard score never exceeds \(12s^2/11\), where \(s\) is the number of species. Equality occurs exactly at species counts divisible by eleven: optimal incidence patterns have site-pair multiplicities \((5t,2t,2t,2t)\) on one pair and the three edges of its complementary triangle; no other pattern attains equality.
