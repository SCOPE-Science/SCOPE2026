For three independent three-point laws with probability vectors \((a,a,1-2a)\), \((b,b,1-2b)\), and \((c,c,1-2c)\), where \(1/3\le a\le b\le c\le1/2\), the maximum point probability over all sign orientations has an exact two-branch formula. In the strict interior, whether to align all variables or reverse only the one with largest parameter depends precisely on the phase boundary \(5ab=a+b\), independent of \(c\).
