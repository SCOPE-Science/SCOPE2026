In the fear–media epidemic model, arbitrarily strong infection-driven advertising has three sharply different equilibrium outcomes: \(I_r\asymp r^{-1}\), the critical law \(I_r\asymp r^{-1/2}\), or a strictly positive infection floor. The boundary is exactly \(\lambda=(\mathcal R_0-1)(\lambda_0+d)\), and the source's Table 1 parameters lie in the \(r^{-1}\) regime.
