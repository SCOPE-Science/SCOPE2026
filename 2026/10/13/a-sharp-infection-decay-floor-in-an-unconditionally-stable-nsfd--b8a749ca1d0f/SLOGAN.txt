The all-step NSFD SIR scheme preserves disease-free stability, but its infected multiplier can never fall below \(\mathcal R_0\). Beyond the sharp step \(h_*=-\log(\mathcal R_0)/[c(1-\mathcal R_0)]\), exact continuous infection decay is unreachable within the source update, and its physical-time decay rate tends to zero as the step grows.
