A single affine function \(\log\beta(t)=\log\beta_0+at\), specified by two endpoint values on any finite horizon, can generate arbitrarily many successively smaller SIR epidemic waves. An exact Lotka–Volterra transformation proves that infinitely many prevalence maxima occur precisely when \(0<a<\gamma\) and the conserved energy exceeds \(\gamma+a\), while the incidence also peaks in every transformed period.
