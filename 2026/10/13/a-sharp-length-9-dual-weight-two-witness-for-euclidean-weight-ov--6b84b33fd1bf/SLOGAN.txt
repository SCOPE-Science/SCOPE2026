For the Euclidean weight on \(\mathbb F_5\), two explicit dimension-\(2\) codes of effective length \(9\) have the same primal enumerator but satisfy \(A_2(C^\perp)=14\) and \(A_2(D^\perp)=10\). Exhaustion of every normalized effective dimension-\(2\) code through length \(8\) proves that \(9\) is sharp for failures visible in dual Euclidean weight \(2\).
