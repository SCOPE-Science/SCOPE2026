For any irreducible birth–death chain on at least three states, every \(\theta\)-method step with \(0\le\theta\le\frac12\) is impossible to embed in a homogeneous continuous-time Markov chain, even though Crank–Nicolson steps are strictly positive and stochastic at small stepsizes. The boundary is sharp: every \(\theta>\frac12\) admits a positive-rate embedding for sufficiently small steps, and every \(\theta\ge1\) is embeddable at every positive stepsize.
