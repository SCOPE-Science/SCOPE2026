A largest-element Jacobi rotation can make the new largest off-diagonal entry bigger even while the classical total off-diagonal energy decreases. The exact one-step law is \(m^+/m\le\sqrt2\,\tau\), where \(\tau\) is the second-largest-to-pivot ratio, and the threshold \(\tau=1/\sqrt2\) is sharp at every prescribed SPD condition number \(\kappa>1\).
