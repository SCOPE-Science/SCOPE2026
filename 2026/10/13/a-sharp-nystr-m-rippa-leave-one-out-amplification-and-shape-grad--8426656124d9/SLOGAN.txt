In two-site inverse-multiquadric interpolation, a one-landmark ridge-Nyström surrogate has a sharp leave-one-out residual discrepancy asymptotic to \(1/(2\sqrt\lambda)\), even as the full kernel condition number tends to one. For a unit data vector it reverses the entire shape-optimization gradient beyond the exact correlation threshold \(t=\sqrt\lambda\), creating a false objective maximum of height \(1+1/(4\lambda)\).
