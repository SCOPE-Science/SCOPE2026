For two strongly convex scalar quadratic components, random reshuffling reverses its exact one-epoch expected-error advantage over independent sampling precisely at \(\eta(\lambda_1+\lambda_2)=2\) when begun at the finite-sum optimum. The sharp uniform cutoff \(\eta=1/L\) is attained by equal curvatures, where the reversal identity holds for **every** initial point even though both component steps remain stable below \(2/L\).
