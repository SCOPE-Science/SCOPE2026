For common-Hessian strongly convex quadratic finite sums, random reshuffling beats independent with-replacement sampling in expected **terminal** objective precisely on modes with \(\eta\lambda<1\), while independent sampling wins on modes with \(1<\eta\lambda<2\). This gives an exact all-horizon reciprocal-curvature boundary and, when the spectrum crosses that boundary, a data-dependent reversal of the ordering; it does not concern averaged iterates.
