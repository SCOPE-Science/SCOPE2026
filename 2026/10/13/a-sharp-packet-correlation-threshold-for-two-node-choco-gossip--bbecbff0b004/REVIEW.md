Shi–Johansson–Johansson address independent versus dependent unreliable exchange, but use a memoryless asynchronous gossip matrix, without the cache state or the parameter \(q\). Suresh and collaborators use correlated quantizers for mean estimation and optimization, not the dynamical operator and exact threshold. The new theorem requires a four-dimensional symmetric second-moment reduction and compares all exchangeable couplings with the same marginal. No inspected indexed or searched formulation implied the exact \(q=1/3\) boundary. Risk: unindexed analyses or less obvious reductions may preempt it; absence of a search hit is not a proof of priority.

**Value: PASS.** Cached-state compressed consensus is a substantive algorithm family. Holding each sender's marginal reliability and mean message budget fixed while switching coupling can change mean-square convergence into exponential-subsequence divergence. The intermediate critical floor and explicit Lyapunov certificate give sharp, operationally meaningful dependence limits. The small two-node setting is motivated by isolating the minimal coupling effect, rather than by an arbitrary numerical slice. Limits: no claims for arbitrary graphs, other gains, or almost-sure outcomes.
