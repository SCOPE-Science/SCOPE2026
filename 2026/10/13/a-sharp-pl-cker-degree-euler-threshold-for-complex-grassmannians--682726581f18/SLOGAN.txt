For a complex Plücker Grassmannian, projective degree never equals Euler characteristic. Degree is smaller only for projective spaces and for the two-plane cases \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,4)\), \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,5)\), and \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,6)\) up to duality; every other Grassmannian has strictly larger Plücker degree.
