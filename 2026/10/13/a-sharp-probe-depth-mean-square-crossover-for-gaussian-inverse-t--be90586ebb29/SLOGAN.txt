For independent Gaussian inverse-trace Lanczos quadrature on a \(2\times2\) positive-definite matrix, spending an even fixed matrix–vector budget on many one-node probes beats fewer exact two-node probes precisely below the sharp threshold \(B_*(u)=4(u^4-u^3+u^2-u+1)/(u-1)^4\), with \(u\) the square root of the spectral condition number. Every spectrum favors shallower probes at budgets \(2\) and \(4\); for each even budget at least \(6\), there is exactly one condition-number crossover.
