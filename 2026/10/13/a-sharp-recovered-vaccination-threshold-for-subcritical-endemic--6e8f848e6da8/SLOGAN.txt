At the fixed subthreshold value \(\mathcal R_0=1067/1080\), an eight-compartment reinfection model admits both a stable disease-free state and a stable endemic state, alongside a one-unstable-direction endemic saddle. Vaccinating recovered individuals removes both endemic equilibria at the sharp exact rate \(\theta_c=(127-4\sqrt{858})/150\), even though \(\mathcal R_0\) never changes.
