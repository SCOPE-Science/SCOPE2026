For a four-cycle with three unit-cost edges and one edge priced at \(t>0\), the maximal continuous-time spectral gap is \(4/(t+2\sqrt t+5)\) for \(t\le9\) and \(1/5\) for \(t\ge9\). The expensive edge receives a unique positive optimal rate below \(t=9\), zero rate above it, and belongs to an entire optimal line segment exactly at the transition.
