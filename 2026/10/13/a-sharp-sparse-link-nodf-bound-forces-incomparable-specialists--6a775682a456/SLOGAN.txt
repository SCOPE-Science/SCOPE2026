For every \(3\times n\) ecological network with \(n\ge4\), exactly \(n+2\) interactions and no isolated species, the sharp classical NODF maximum is \((2n-2)/(\binom n2+3)\). Every maximizing network is a uniquely shaped bipartite tree with a universal generalist and two specialists choosing different partners; this strictly outperforms every Ferrers network at the same number of links.
