The polynomial Kimeldorf–Sampson copula has a sharp **full-validity** Spearman cap \(\rho_S=(\sqrt{57}-3)/12\approx0.379153\), attained by one explicitly admissible parameter pair. The 2026 paper’s smaller cap \(25/96\) is correct inside its imposed rectangular parameter box, but that box is not necessary for positivity of the polynomial copula density.
