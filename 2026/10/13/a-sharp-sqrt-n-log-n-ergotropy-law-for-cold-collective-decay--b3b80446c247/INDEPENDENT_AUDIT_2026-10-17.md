# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The exact cold collective decay Lindbladian preserves spin-sector multiplicities. From initial maximally mixed state the sector k has g(n,k)=C(n,k)-C(n,k-1) nonzero eigenvalues (n-2k+1)/2^n at excitation energy k. These eigenvalues decrease with k and their cumulative ranks equal C(n,k). Passive rearrangement assigns these ranks to the lowest binomial-degenerate energy levels; the rank-tail asymptotic B_n(c)=2^n n^{-2c^2+o(1)} yields passive energy n/2-(1/2+o(1))sqrt(n log n). Casimir trace E[J(J+1)]=3n/4 gives initial stationary energy n/2-O(sqrt n). Hoeffding largest-eigenvalue tails ensure uniform integrability and hence W_n/(omega sqrt(n log n))->1/2. Recomputed compressed finite-size binomial eigenspectrum and passive energy: W_4/omega=1/16, n=10 about .26562, n=100 about 2.78061, n=1000 about 14.90948. Slow finite-size approach and computations are not an infinite proof.
