In a hybrid host-commuting/vector-migration epidemic model, the smallest achievable squared reproduction-number ratio at fixed stationary vector distribution is exactly \(1+\operatorname{Var}_{h}(q)\), the weighted variance floor of host-group vector exposure. If that variance is positive the floor is approached only under arbitrarily fast vector mixing, whereas zero variance is the exact criterion for attaining the homogeneous single-patch reproduction number at every finite migration rate.
