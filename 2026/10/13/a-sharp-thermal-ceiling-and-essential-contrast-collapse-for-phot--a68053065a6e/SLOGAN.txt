For balanced multiplexed click detection, arbitrarily many photon additions cannot make a thermal state sub-binomial once the seed occupancy reaches \(\beta_*=1/(1-2\eta/N)\), except at the ideal two-detector endpoint. Below this exact ceiling the optimal negative witness contrast exists but collapses as \(\exp[-\kappa/(\beta_*-\beta)+o((\beta_*-\beta)^{-1})]\); photon additions eventually change the sign, yet cannot prevent this essential loss of contrast.
