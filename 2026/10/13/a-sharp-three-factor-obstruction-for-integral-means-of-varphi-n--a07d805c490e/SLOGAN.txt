If all three classical means of \(\varphi(n)\) and \(\sigma(n)\) are integers for \(n>1\), then \(\Omega(n)\ge3\), sharply attained by \(418\) and \(477\). The key is a universal integer-pair obstruction: the geometric and harmonic means of \((x-1)(y-1)\) and \((x+1)(y+1)\) cannot both be integral for integers \(x,y\ge2\).
