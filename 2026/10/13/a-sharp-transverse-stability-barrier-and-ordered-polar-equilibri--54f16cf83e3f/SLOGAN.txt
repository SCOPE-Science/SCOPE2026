The two-community cubic amplification model has no stable interacting polarized equilibrium unless the confidence threshold exceeds \(1/\sqrt3\); the sharp stable interval is \(3\beta<\alpha<2\beta/(1-\varepsilon^2)\) for \(\varepsilon<1\). Its polarization branch is a full-system saddle immediately above \(\alpha=2\beta\), and the only other interacting polarized equilibria are two asymmetric saddles.
