For a coupled two-dimensional positive affine contraction, the raw squared SqS3 stage preserves the nonnegative cone exactly up to \(s=2/\operatorname{tr}(I-J)\). The adaptive SqS3 rule generically exceeds what cone preservation can tolerate near the Perron ray, and a strictly positive \(2\times2\) contraction gives an exact completed-cycle sign failure.
