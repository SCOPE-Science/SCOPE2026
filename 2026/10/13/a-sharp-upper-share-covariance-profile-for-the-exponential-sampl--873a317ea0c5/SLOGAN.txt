Under an iid exponential model, the finite-sample covariance between the Gini inequality coefficient and the share held by the largest \(k\) observations is a positive, explicitly harmonic function of rank with a sharp maximum. The maximizing upper population fraction converges to \(-W_0(-2e^{-2})/2=0.2031878699\ldots\), not to one-half or to the single-largest observation.
