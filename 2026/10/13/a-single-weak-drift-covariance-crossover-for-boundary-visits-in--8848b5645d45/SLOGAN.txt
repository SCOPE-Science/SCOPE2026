For a nearly fair gambler's-ruin walk, the covariance between absorption time and the number of visits one step from ruin has a sharp, starting-site-dependent large-barrier limit. Only a start at site \(2\) yields a drift-induced sign reversal, at the unique critical scaled drift \(b_*\approx1.49888421886734\); site \(1\) stays positive and all starts at site \(3\) or farther stay negative for positive drift.
