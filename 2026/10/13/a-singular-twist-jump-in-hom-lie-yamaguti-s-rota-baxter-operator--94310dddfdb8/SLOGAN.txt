In Teng–Jin–Long Example 3.4 with nonidentity twist \(\alpha=\operatorname{diag}(k,1)\), the complete \(s\)-Rota–Baxter condition is \(R=\operatorname{diag}(a,d)\) and \(k^sa^2=0\). Thus the operator locus is normally a line, but it jumps to the full diagonal plane exactly at the singular twist \(k=0\) for positive order \(s\), while all descendants still depend only on \(d\).
