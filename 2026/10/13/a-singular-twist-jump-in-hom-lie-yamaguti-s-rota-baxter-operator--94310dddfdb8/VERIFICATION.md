The bundled checker reconstructs the two-dimensional Hom–Lie–Yamaguti structure from Example 3.4 and verifies all defining Hom–Lie–Yamaguti identities on basis elements for symbolic \(k\).

For a general matrix \(R\), it reconstructs twist commutation and all binary and ternary \(s\)-Rota–Baxter defects. Under \(k\ne1\), commutation forces the off-diagonal entries to vanish and the complete remaining defect ideal is generated by
\[
k^sa^2.
\]

The checker also derives the descendant coefficients
\[
k^sa+d
\]
and
\[
2k^sad+d^2,
\]
which reduce on the solution locus to \(d\) and \(d^2\).

It exhaustively tests all \(2\times2\) matrices over \(\mathbf F_5\), \(\mathbf F_7\), and \(\mathbf F_{11}\) for \(k=0\) and \(k=2\) at \(s=0,1,2\). The zero twist has \(q\) solutions at \(s=0\) and \(q^2\) at positive order; the nonzero nonidentity twist always has \(q\).
