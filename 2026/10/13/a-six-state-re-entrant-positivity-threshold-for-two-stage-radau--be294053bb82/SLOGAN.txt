For two-stage third-order Radau IIA on the homogeneous absorbing pure-birth chain, \(2\) through \(5\) states are Markov-positive exactly for \(0\le h\le3\), while \(6\) states exhibit a small-step positivity hole. The six-state update is positive exactly at \(h=0\) or \(\eta\le h\le3\), with \(\eta=0.253946561637\ldots\) the unique positive root of \(h^5+30h^4+180h^3+360h^2+180h-72\).
