# A smooth Archimedean PQD copula that is not PMI

## Finding

Set
\[
f(t)=\frac{t}{4}-\frac{t^2}{4}+\frac{t^3}{9}-\frac19,
\qquad
\varphi(t)=-\log t+f(t)\quad(0<t\le1),
\]
with \(\varphi(0)=+\infty\). Let \(C\) be the bivariate strict Archimedean copula generated by \(\varphi\):
\[
C(u,v)=\varphi^{-1}\!\bigl(\varphi(u)+\varphi(v)\bigr).
\]
Then \(C(u,v)\ge uv\) for all \(u,v\in[0,1]\), so \(C\) is positively quadrant dependent (PQD). Nevertheless its smooth density \(c\) satisfies
\[
\boxed{c(1/4,1/4)-2c(1/4,3/4)+c(3/4,3/4)<0.}
\]
More quantitatively, exact rational interval arithmetic proves that this reflection defect lies strictly between \(-0.019002196\) and \(-0.019002192\). By the density characterization of positive measure inducing (PMI) copulas, \(C\) is **not** PMI. This is a counterexample to the PQD-implies-PMI direction of Fuchs and Tschimpke's Conjecture 1 for Archimedean copulas.

## Assumptions and scope

The domain is the unit square and all copulas are bivariate. A strict Archimedean generator is convex and decreasing, equals zero at one, and diverges at zero. For a continuously differentiable density, the PMI condition is equivalent to nonnegativity of
\[
\Delta_c(u,v)=c(u,v)-c(1-u,v)-c(u,1-v)+c(1-u,1-v)
\]
at every \((u,v)\in(0,1/2)^2\). This is the density test in Remark 1(2) of Fuchs--Tschimpke, arXiv:2306.09676v1. Symmetry of \(C\) makes \(\Delta_c(1/4,1/4)\) the three-density combination above. The result concerns this explicit, absolutely continuous copula; no condition on complete monotonicity in higher dimensions is asserted.

## Proof

**Generator validity.** Elementary differentiation gives
\[
\varphi'(t)=-\frac1t+\frac14-\frac t2+\frac{t^2}{3},\qquad
\varphi''(t)=\frac1{t^2}-\frac12+\frac{2t}{3}.
\]
Since \(0<t\le1\), the polynomial part of \(\varphi'\) is at most \(1/4\), and \(\varphi''(t)\ge1/2\). Therefore \(\varphi'<0\) and \(\varphi''>0\). Also \(\varphi(1)=0\) and \(\varphi(t)\to+\infty\) as \(t\downarrow0\). Thus \(\varphi\) is a valid smooth strict Archimedean generator and \(C\) has a positive smooth density in the open unit square.

**Global PQD.** Write \(h(t)=f'(t)+tf''(t)\). Direct algebra gives the sum of squares
\[
h(t)=t^2-t+\frac14=\left(t-\frac12\right)^2\ge0.
\]
For all \(0<u,v\le1\), the fundamental theorem of calculus applied twice gives
\[
f(uv)-f(u)-f(v)+f(1)
=\int_u^1\!\int_v^1h(st)\,dt\,ds\ge0.
\]
The logarithmic part of \(\varphi\) is additive under multiplication, so \(\varphi(uv)\ge\varphi(u)+\varphi(v)\). Because \(\varphi\) is decreasing, applying its inverse yields \(uv\le C(u,v)\). Continuity extends this to the boundary.

**Density and the negative reflection defect.** Define
\[
\ell(t)=-\varphi'(t)=\frac1t-f'(t),\qquad
k(t)=\varphi''(t)=\frac1{t^2}+f''(t).
\]
Differentiation of \(\varphi(C(u,v))=\varphi(u)+\varphi(v)\) twice gives
\[
\boxed{c(u,v)=\frac{\ell(u)\ell(v)k(C(u,v))}{\ell(C(u,v))^3}.}
\]
Both \(\ell\) and \(k\) are positive and strictly decreasing for \(0<t<1\): \(\ell'=-k<0\), while \(k'(t)=-2/t^3+2/3<0\). Consequently, if \(L<C(u,v)<U\), then the rigorous enclosure is
\[
\frac{\ell(u)\ell(v)k(U)}{\ell(L)^3}
<c(u,v)<
\frac{\ell(u)\ell(v)k(L)}{\ell(U)^3}.
\]
The three needed copula values have the following exact rational enclosures:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\frac{6433697255}{10^{11}}&<C(1/4,1/4)<\frac{6433697257}{10^{11}},\\
\frac{18897106192}{10^{11}}&<C(1/4,3/4)<\frac{18897106193}{10^{11}},\\
\frac{56538957970}{10^{11}}&<C(3/4,3/4)<\frac{56538957971}{10^{11}}.
\end{aligned}
\]
These are not rounded roots assumed exact: each is certified by testing the signs of the strictly increasing function
\[
G_{u,v}(x)=\log\frac{x}{uv}-f(x)+f(u)+f(v)
\]
at rational endpoints. To bound the logarithm using rational arithmetic, put \(z=x/(uv)\), \(r=(z-1)/(z+1)\), and use, for \(z\ge1\),
\[
2\sum_{j=0}^{7}\frac{r^{2j+1}}{2j+1}
\le\log z\le
2\sum_{j=0}^{7}\frac{r^{2j+1}}{2j+1}
+\frac{2r^{17}}{17(1-r^2)}.
\]
Here the root-bracketing and density formulas are exactly evaluated with rational numbers. The resulting enclosure is
\[
-\frac{19002196}{10^9}
<\Delta_c(1/4,1/4)
<-\frac{19002192}{10^9}<0.
\]
Thus \(C\) fails the PMI criterion. The exact-code certificate is supplied in `verify_pqd_not_pmi.py`.

**Independent local analytic check.** In the generator family \(\varphi_\varepsilon(t)=-\log t+\varepsilon f(t)\), the first-order copula perturbation at independence is
\[
\left.\frac{\partial C_\varepsilon(u,v)}{\partial\varepsilon}\right|_{\varepsilon=0}
=uv\bigl(f(uv)-f(u)-f(v)\bigr)
=\sum_{m=1}^{3}a_m(u-u^{m+1})(v-v^{m+1}),
\]
where \((a_1,a_2,a_3)=(1/4,-1/4,1/9)\). Differentiating its density and taking the same reflection combination gives the exact first-order defect
\[
\left.\frac{d}{d\varepsilon}\Delta_{c_\varepsilon}(1/4,1/4)\right|_{\varepsilon=0}
=\sum_{m=1}^3 a_m(m+1)^2\bigl((3/4)^m-(1/4)^m\bigr)^2
=-\frac{11}{576}.
\]
This independently explains the obstruction near independence, although the exact rational certificate above establishes the claimed negative defect at \(\varepsilon=1\).

## Verification

Execute `python3 verify_pqd_not_pmi.py` using the Python standard library. The script checks the three root brackets by exact rational bounds for logarithms, propagates them through the exact copula-density formula, proves the strict negative defect, and verifies the first-variation fraction. Every decisive assertion operates on `fractions.Fraction`, not floating-point approximations. Displayed decimal values are supplementary only. The global PQD conclusion is supplied by the preceding analytic integral identity, not by finite numerical tests.

## Relationship to prior work

Fuchs and Tschimpke, *A novel positive dependence property and its impact on a popular class of concordance measures*, arXiv:2306.09676v1 (first public posting 2023-06-16), define PMI in Section 2, prove the reflected-density test in Remark 1(2), and in Conjecture 1 propose that an Archimedean copula is PQD if and only if it is PMI. Their explicit counterexamples for non-Archimedean families, including selected extreme-value copulas, do not settle the Archimedean restriction. The present generator satisfies that restriction and disproves one direction of their proposed equivalence. Their article appeared in *Journal of Multivariate Analysis* 200 (2024), 105259.

The same authors' *Total positivity of copulas from a Markov kernel perspective*, arXiv:2205.01914v1 (Section 4, Theorem 4.6), characterizes stronger properties such as stochastic increase and Markov-kernel total positivity for Archimedean copulas; it does not assert this reflected-density obstruction or establish the disputed PQD-to-PMI implication. No independent priority assessment has been carried out; an unlocated equivalent counterexample remains a literature risk.

## Limitations

The theorem falsifies the PQD-implies-PMI half of Conjecture 1 for bivariate Archimedean copulas; it does not determine the reverse PMI-implies-PQD direction, the NMI analogue, or a complete characterization of generators with PMI. The specific reflected-density interval is computer-assisted but rigorously certified by exact rational arithmetic and an explicit logarithm remainder bound. The first-order negative defect is a separate symbolic check, not a substitute for the finite-parameter certificate. Reuse of the scientific finding warrants independent review.
