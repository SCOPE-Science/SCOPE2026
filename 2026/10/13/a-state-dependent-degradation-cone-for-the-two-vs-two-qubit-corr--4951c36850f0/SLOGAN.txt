For a pure two-qubit state of concurrence \(C<1\), a Schmidt-unbiased measurement on one side admits an exact same-basis degradation to the corresponding Bell-state statistics whenever the other measurement satisfies \(|b_z|\le\sqrt{(1-C)/(1+C)}\), with the zero-correlation case automatic. This yields the 2013 two-vs-two complementary-correlation bound on a continuous nonmaximally-entangled measurement sector and exactly characterizes that degradation mechanism when the equatorial correlation is nonzero.
