For a multiclass quantum classifier with known readout, intersecting tie-priority-adjusted spectral half-spaces for \(M_k-M_j\) produces a subspace whose every normalized state is classified as the chosen target \(k\). Measuring the projector onto that subspace gives the certified target-specific upper bound \(\varepsilon_k^*(\psi)\le 1-\sqrt{p_k}\) whenever \(p_k>0\).
