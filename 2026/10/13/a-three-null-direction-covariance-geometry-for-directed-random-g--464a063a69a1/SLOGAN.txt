In a loopless independent-arc directed random graph, the jointly binomial-whitened in- and out-degree histograms have a covariance eigenvalues \(0\) (three times), \(n\) (\(2n-4\) times), and \(2n\) (once), independent of the arc probability \(p\). The exact mean squared Pearson discrepancy between the two histograms is \(n-2\), reflecting three deterministic degree constraints.
