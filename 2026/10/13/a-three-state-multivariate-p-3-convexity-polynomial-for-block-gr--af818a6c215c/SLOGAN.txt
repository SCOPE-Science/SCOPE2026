Every block graph has an explicit, positive three-state vertex–block recurrence for its full multivariate \(P_3\)-convex-set polynomial, giving a linear-size arithmetic circuit and every cardinality count. For the clique bouquet \(K_1\vee tK_m\), the entire polynomial simplifies to \(1+tm\,x+x(1+x^m)^t\); the novelty is this concrete recurrence, not the polynomial-time tractability already implied by bounded-clique-width theory.
