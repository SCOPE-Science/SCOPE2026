For continuous-time transport on a perfect rooted \(b\)-ary tree of height \(h\), a single absorbing leaf sees exactly \(\sum_{q=2}^{h+2}\varphi(q)\) spectral directions, while a root absorber sees only \(h+1\). For a uniform incoherent initial particle, dividing these numbers by the total vertex count gives the exact eventual capture probabilities, independent of positive loss strength.
