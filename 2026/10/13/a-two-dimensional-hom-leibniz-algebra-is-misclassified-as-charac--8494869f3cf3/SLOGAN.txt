For the Hom-Leibniz algebra \(L_2^1\) with \([e_2,e_2]=e_1\) and \(\alpha=\operatorname{id}\), the published derivation space is the two-dimensional affine Lie algebra: with basis \(H,E\), one has \([H,E]=E\). Therefore the table entry \(\mathrm{CN}=\mathrm{Yes}\) must be corrected to \(\mathrm{CN}=\mathrm{No}\).
