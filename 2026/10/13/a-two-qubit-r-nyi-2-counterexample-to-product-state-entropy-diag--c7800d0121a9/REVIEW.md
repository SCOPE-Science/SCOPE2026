# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The product-state ceiling follows for every local density matrix from the Bloch-ball inequality \(C_ZC_X\leq9/16\), and tensor-product factorization gives \(81/256\). The explicit state is normalized, entangled, and has exact collision probability \(7/12\) in both measurements, yielding the strict rational separation \(49/144>81/256\).

## Originality
PASS. The primary 2015 paper states Conjecture V.6 but does not provide this obstruction. The closest later full-text source proves the analogous product-measurement result only for Shannon entropy; it notes that arbitrary Schur-concave extensions can fail but gives no public Rényi-2 statement or exact witness. Source, alias, exact-constant, broader-coverage, implication, and public-result comparisons revealed no covering public result. Residual risk remains from differently phrased literature and an unpublished private communication cited in 2018.

## Value
PASS. The example occurs in the smallest composite Hilbert-space dimension and for the canonical local Hadamard pair. It converts a published factorization conjecture into an exact boundary statement: entanglement can beat the entire product-state collision-entropy sum region at order \(2\), even though Shannon linear uncertainty is later known to be additive.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest positive theorem is the 2018 Shannon additivity result for product projective measurements. The present finding does not challenge that theorem and does not address a version of Conjecture V.6 explicitly restricted to dual Rényi pairs. It only establishes failure of the conjecture as written at \((2,2)\).
