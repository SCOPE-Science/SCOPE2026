For two local Hadamard qubit measurements, an explicit entangled state attains the Rényi-2 entropy pair \(\bigl(\log_2(12/7),\log_2(12/7)\bigr)\), while every product state obeys the stricter sum bound \(H_2^Z+H_2^X\geq\log_2(256/81)\). Thus the product-state entropy-diagram conjecture of arXiv:1509.00398 fails as written at Rényi orders \((2,2)\).
