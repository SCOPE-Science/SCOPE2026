For single-photon-added squeezed thermal light, the negative Wigner volume obeys a path-uniform high-temperature/large-squeezing crossover controlled by \(s=t e^{2r}\): it changes from a quadratic thermal decay at fixed squeezing to \(\kappa t^3\) under sufficiently strong squeezing, with an exact Bessel-integral profile. The strong-squeezing cubic coefficient lies in a sharp range whose high-temperature endpoint is \(\kappa=0.2309681778\ldots\).
