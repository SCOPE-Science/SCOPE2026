Under the Ewens permutation law, covariance between total cycle count and the count of cycles longer than a given fraction has exactly one positive-to-negative transition. Its limiting critical fraction equals \(0.253192933\ldots\) for uniform permutations, tends to \(1/2\) as the Ewens parameter vanishes, and is asymptotic to \(0.434818204\ldots/\theta\) as that parameter grows.
