In Wang's full-dimensional weak-Fano Laurent setting, the monodromy at infinity has exactly one maximal Jordan block of size \(d+1\), and it lies in the eigenvalue-\(1\) summand. Thus the normalized unipotent logarithm satisfies \(N_\infty^d\ne0\) but \(N_\infty^{d+1}=0\), even for the nonsimplicial and non-vertex-supported cases newly covered in 2026.
