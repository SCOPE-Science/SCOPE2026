# Review

## Correctness
PASS. On the affine chart \(A=[I_k\ B]\), Cauchy--Binet gives the isotropic Plücker equation as \(\det(I_k+BB^{\mathsf T})\). The determinant derivative proves that a point is singular exactly when the restricted quadratic form has corank at least \(2\): corank at least \(2\) kills the adjugate, while at corank \(1\) the identity term in \(I_k+BB^{\mathsf T}\) rules out vanishing of \(2\operatorname{adj}(M)B\). The exact-radical stratum is then parameterized by a totally isotropic \(r\)-plane \(R\) and a nondegenerate \((k-r)\)-plane in \(R^\perp/R\), giving codimension \(\binom{r+1}{2}\). The boundary range \(2\le k\le n-2\) is explicitly enforced.

## Originality
PASS. The initiating preprint states a general upper bound \(\binom{k+1}{2}\) for the singular-locus codimension and isolates exact codimension \(3\) only when \(k=2\). Targeted searches for the all-\(k\) radical formulation, the determinant-chart formulation, the corank-two formulation, and the exact codimension formula found no statement covering the accepted theorem. Classical sources do contain the local symmetric-matrix fact that corank at least \(2\) is codimension \(3\), and the earlier Grassmannian-distance paper treats only \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,n)\); neither checked source supplies the global all-\(k\) radical-stratum theorem.

Residual risk remains that older orthogonal-degeneracy literature contains the same global formula under different terminology.

## Value
PASS. The initiating paper makes the singular codimension a structural input and gives a bound growing as \(\binom{k+1}{2}\). Showing that the exact codimension is the constant \(3\) in every nontrivial Grassmannian dimension identifies the geometry uniformly rather than merely improving a numerical example. The full radical-stratum formula \(\binom{r+1}{2}\) explains the constant and supplies a reusable description of all higher degeneracy levels.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest current source is arXiv:2610.02018v1; the closest earlier metric-algebraic source is arXiv:2601.22843v1, which is confined to lines. Classical symmetric-degeneracy results give a local analogue but not the checked global statement on the ordinary Grassmannian. No stronger ED-generality dimension cutoff is claimed.
