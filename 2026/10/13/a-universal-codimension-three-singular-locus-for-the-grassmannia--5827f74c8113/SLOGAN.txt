For every \(2\le k\le n-2\), the isotropic Plücker quadric cuts \(\operatorname{Gr}(k,n)\) in a hypersurface whose singular locus is exactly the set of \(k\)-planes with at least a two-dimensional radical, and this locus has codimension \(3\). More generally, radical dimension at least \(r\) has codimension \(\binom{r+1}{2}\).
