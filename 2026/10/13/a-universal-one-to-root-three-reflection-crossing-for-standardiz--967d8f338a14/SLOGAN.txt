For a standardized Gamma density, the local left-right imbalance about the mean has no interior reversal at shapes \(0<\alpha\le1\), but for every \(\alpha>1\) it reverses exactly once at a radius \(\kappa_\alpha\in(1,\sqrt{3})\). The crossing solves \(\operatorname{artanh}(\kappa_\alpha/\sqrt{\alpha})/(\kappa_\alpha/\sqrt{\alpha})=\alpha/(\alpha-1)\) and tends to \(\sqrt{3}\) standard deviations with first correction \(-2\sqrt{3}/(5\alpha)\).
