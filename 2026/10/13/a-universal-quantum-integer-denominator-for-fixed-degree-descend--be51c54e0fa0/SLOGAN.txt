For each fixed degree vector \(p\), the all-genus descendant series in the quantum-mirror correspondence has a universal denominator \(D_p(q)=\prod_{\ell\ge2}[\ell]_q^{\sum_j\lfloor p_j/\ell\rfloor}\). This gives an explicit root-of-unity pole-order envelope depending only on \(p\), while allowing cancellations to make the actual denominator smaller.
