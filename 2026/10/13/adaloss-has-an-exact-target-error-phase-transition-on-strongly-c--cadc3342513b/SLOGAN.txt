A fixed error in AdaLoss's optimum-loss target changes convergence on every strongly convex quadratic, within an explicit basin, from geometric to a sharp stretched-exponential law. Each active Hessian mode has decay \(t^{-\eta^2\lambda^2/(2\alpha\delta)}\exp[-2\eta\lambda\sqrt{t/(\alpha\delta)}]\), with \(\delta\) the target error; an exactly calibrated target instead gives geometric decay.
