Compressing \(N\) coordinates into \(B\) Adam-mini mean-square blocks has an unavoidable sparse-impulse price: the sharp worst-coordinate amplification relative to coordinatewise Adam is \(\sqrt{\lceil N/B\rceil}\). Within a block of size \(d\), a \(k\)-coordinate equal-magnitude impulse approaches the exact factor \(\sqrt{d/k}\), so balanced block sizes are minimax for this robustness criterion.
