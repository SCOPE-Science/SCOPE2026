On alternating gradient norms \(A>B\), AdaNorm never changes the high step but eventually raises every low step toward \((\gamma A+B)/(1+\gamma)\). The resulting asymptotic high-to-low contrast is \((1+\gamma)(A/B)/(\gamma A/B+1)\), with the sharp universal cap \((1+\gamma)/\gamma\); at \(\gamma=0.95\), even arbitrarily large raw contrasts are compressed below \(39/19\approx2.05263\).
