The first explicit finite-plateau Lie ring \(L_1\) has additive group \(\mathbb Z^5\oplus\mathbb Z/2\oplus(\mathbb Z/4)^{22}\oplus\mathbb Z/8\oplus(\mathbb Z/16)^{15}\oplus(\mathbb Z/64)^7\oplus(\mathbb Z/256)^3\). Its torsion therefore has order \(2^{174}\) and exponent \(256\), four times the exponent of \(\gamma_4(L_1)\).
