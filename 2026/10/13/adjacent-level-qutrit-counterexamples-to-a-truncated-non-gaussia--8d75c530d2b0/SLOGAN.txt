In the qutrit truncation of the Memarzadeh--Mancini channel at \(N=3/2\), pure dephasing admits an adjacent-level input \((|1\rangle+|2\rangle)/\sqrt2\) whose output entropy is strictly below both conjectured candidate families for every \(0<t\le\log(4/3)\). Thus the published truncated-space minimum-output-entropy conjecture already fails in dimension three.
