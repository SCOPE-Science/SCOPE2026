The map Phi from the matrix centralizer into V/im(A) has rank the number of zero-primary Jordan blocks no longer than the largest occupied block: a module homomorphism cannot map an L-block top onto a longer-block top, and explicit homomorphisms to every shorter-block top show surjectivity. This establishes the general rank formula for all fields. Finite-field vector fibers are counted by independent top coordinates; summing over rational-canonical classes gives the rank polynomial. All seventeen n=3 characteristic-polynomial/Jordan strata have been symbolically enumerated and checked. Independent full direct ad-matrix enumeration over F2 in dimension three recovered 4096 elements and 86080 commuting ordered pairs. The arbitrary-dimension assertion depends on the proved module-homomorphism lemma, not on finite tests. The symbolic and direct verifiers both passed. Limits: finite experiments do not certify arbitrary dimensions.
