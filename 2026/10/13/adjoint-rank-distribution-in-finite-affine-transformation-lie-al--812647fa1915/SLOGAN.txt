For the Lie algebra \(\mathfrak{gl}_n(\mathbb F_q)\ltimes\mathbb F_q^n\), an element’s adjoint rank and its exact frequency are determined by the centralizer dimension and the zero-eigenvalue Jordan-block sizes of its matrix part; this yields an all-dimensional rational-canonical rank enumerator and commuting-pair count. In dimension three, exactly the seven ranks \(0,3,5,6,7,8,9\) occur, with explicit polynomial multiplicities valid over every finite field.
