For a centrally symmetric continuous parent, an affine conditional regression of the three-sample midrange on the sample median forces the quantile \(Q(p)=\mu+s(2p-1)[p(1-p)]^{1/(2a)-1}\) with the sharp slope constraint \(a\ge\frac12\). The slope interpolates from the uniform law at \(a=\frac12\) through Student \(t_2\) at \(a=1\) and directly determines the power-tail index.
