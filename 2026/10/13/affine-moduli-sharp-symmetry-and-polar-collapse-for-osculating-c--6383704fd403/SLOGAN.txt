For unions of at least three conics from the pencil \(z^2-xy+t xz=0\), projective equivalence is exactly affine equivalence of parameter sets. The sharp automorphism bound \(m(m-1)\) occurs precisely for affine copies of finite subfields; there the polar map has total degree \(2\), purely inseparable in characteristic two, and geometric classes over finite fields admit a closed Burnside formula.
