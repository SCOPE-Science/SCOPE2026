# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The two-error parity requirement forces each hypercube neighbor's check word into a six-element antipodal Hamming ball. The data-distance condition makes these images injective, proving the six-direction upper limit. In the equality case the square-XOR lemma makes each edge color constant along every coordinate direction, proving affinity; all six allowed check columns are then forced. A complete pairwise-distance proof establishes the converse. The package's executable verifier checked the \(720\) fixed-origin candidates, all relevant local squares, and the \(65536\) shorter-check maps at the two-bit boundary. Those finite checks are supplemental, not a replacement for the all-message algebraic argument.

**Originality — PASS.** The 2025 function-correcting paper already proves a general lower bound of five check bits and constructs the four-bit parity example; the March 2026 sequel reiterates it. Van Gils's 1982 linear unequal-error-protection tables include a length-eleven, dimension-six separation vector matching the intended protection levels in an appropriate information basis, so existence at these classical parameters is prior art. The claim is restricted to a different and stronger statement: **all arbitrary nonlinear systematic optimal maps at six message bits are affine**, with exact labeled enumeration and a sharp dimension cutoff. Inspected prior primary texts, relevant exact-parameter tables, scholarly searches and the available published-record search did not identify that equality classification. This negative search finding is not an independence or novelty certificate.

**Value — PASS.** The five-check-bit budget is the already established minimum for the protection pair, so asking for its maximal systematic message dimension and describing its equality cases is a natural coding-design problem. A rigidity result for every nonlinear assignment is qualitatively stronger than another short construction: it excludes hidden nonlinear improvements at the boundary and characterizes the full design space. The number \(23040\) follows from the structural classification, rather than an arbitrary census.

**Closest literature and risks.** The 2025 general bound and four-bit construction, the March/April 2026 strict-code discussions, and the 1982 linear separation-vector table were inspected and distinguished by implication, not title alone. An earlier unpublished or unindexed classification of nonlinear unequal-error-protection systematic maps remains a residual originality risk. This result does not determine optimal redundancy for seven or more message bits and does not classify subcritical encoders.
