On a scalar quadratic with curvature \(h>\lambda\), source-form AGC with any positive parameter-norm floor \(\epsilon\) is globally convergent exactly for \(0<\eta h<2\), not for the wider clipped-curvature range \(0<\eta\lambda<2\). Between the two ceilings it has an explicit continuum of exact two-cycles, so the small norm floor can determine asymptotic stability.
