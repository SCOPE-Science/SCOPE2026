Aida's mutual-projection depth acts as an angle-selective filter: in the zero-regularizer projection limit, the layerwise squared momentum-gradient discrepancy after \(K\) projections is exactly \( |\cos\theta|^{2K} \) times its AdaBelief value. At the source default \(K=2\), the factor is \( |\cos\theta|^4 \), so orthogonal pairs are annihilated while collinear pairs receive no projection suppression.
