On the split square-zero local algebra \(A=K1\oplus V\), every weight-zero Rota–Baxter operator is governed by \(T^2=0\) and \(2T(u)=0\); its sharp nilpotency exponent jumps from two to three in characteristic two when \(\dim V\ge2\). Over every finite field, the complete rank distribution and every algebra-automorphism orbit size follow from explicit Gaussian-binomial formulas.
