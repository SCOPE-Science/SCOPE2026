For the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M\), a set is connected dominating exactly when it selects at least two vertices from each bipartition class, except that selecting the same two matching indices on both sides disconnects the induced subgraph. Thus \(C(\operatorname{Cr}_n;x)=((1+x)^n-1-nx)^2-\binom{n}{2}x^4\), and every inclusion-minimal connected dominating set has size \(4\).
