For the lollipop graph \(L(k,\ell)\), edge resolvability is controlled by a single clique-omission rule: all clique vertices away from the attachment are selected, or exactly one is omitted and at least one tail vertex is selected. This yields the complete edge-resolving-set polynomial, \(1+(k-1)\ell\) minimum bases, and identifies the attachment vertex as the unique vertex lying in no edge metric basis.
