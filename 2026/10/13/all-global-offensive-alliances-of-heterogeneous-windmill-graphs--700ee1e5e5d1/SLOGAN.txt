For \(K_1\vee(K_{m_1}\cup\cdots\cup K_{m_r})\), global offensive alliances are governed independently blade by blade: with the center chosen, blade \(i\) needs at least \(\lfloor m_i/2\rfloor\) vertices, and without it one extra vertex is required in every blade. Consequently the entire cardinality enumerator factors, and every minimum alliance contains the center.
