For every complete multipartite graph with at least three parts, \(k\)-limited domination is governed exactly by how many vertices are omitted from every occupied part: each selected part may see at most \(k\) omitted vertices outside itself. This gives the full cardinality enumerator and an exact support-by-support formula for \(\gamma_k^{\mathrm L}\), extending the previously known complete-bipartite scalar result.
