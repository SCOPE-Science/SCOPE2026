For a path-barbell made from \(K_p\) and \(K_q\) with \(p,q\ge3\), ordinary resolving sets are exactly those omitting at most one non-attachment vertex from each clique, while 2-metric generators are exactly the supersets of all non-attachment clique vertices. Thus the handle length contributes only a free factor \((1+x)^{\ell+1}\) to the all-set enumerators, and the unique minimum 2-metric generator is independent of that length.\n
