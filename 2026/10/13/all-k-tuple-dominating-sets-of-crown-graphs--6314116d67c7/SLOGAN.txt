For the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M\), every \(k\)-tuple dominating set is controlled only by the two side sizes and two boundary inclusions: when one side has exactly \(k\) selected vertices it must be contained indexwise in the other. This yields a closed bivariate enumerator for every \(1\le k\le n\), with \(\gamma_{\times k}(\operatorname{Cr}_n)=2k\) and exactly \(\binom nk\) minimum sets.
