For the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n\), every \(k\)-tuple total restrained dominating set is controlled by the two omitted index sets and one sharp disjointness condition at the boundary size \(k\). This yields the full cardinality enumerator and the phase transition \(\gamma_{\times k,t}^{r}(\operatorname{Cr}_n)=2n\) for \(n\le2k\) and \(2k+2\) for \(n\ge2k+1\).
