# Review of All solid-resolving sets of crown graphs

## Correctness
PASS. For \(i\ne j\), exactly \(a_i\) and \(b_j\) are strictly closer to \(a_i\) than to \(a_j\). This forces every mixed-side complement to lie in one matched pair. The three permitted complement types are then checked explicitly against every ordered-pair type. The largest complement has size \(n\), attained only by the two sides, and direct complement counting gives the enumerator. Exhaustive checks for \(3\le n\le9\) agree with every statement.

## Originality
PASS. The foundational and later primary solid-resolution papers were inspected at their characterization theorems, family coverage, and searchable terminology; neither treats crown graphs or matching-deleted complete bipartite graphs. Targeted searches under crown, complete bipartite minus a perfect matching, bipartite double cover, solid resolving, and solid-metric dimension found no equivalent theorem. Retrieved crown results concern non-equivalent edge, strong, mixed, domination, or locating variants.

## Value
PASS. The theorem determines the entire feasible set system and its cardinality enumerator, not only a scalar dimension. It shows that a standard symmetric family has exactly two minimum robust locator configurations and identifies the precise directional obstruction controlling every larger feasible set.
