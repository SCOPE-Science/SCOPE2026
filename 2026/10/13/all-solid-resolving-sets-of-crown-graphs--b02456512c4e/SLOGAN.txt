For every connected crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n\), \(n\ge3\), the solid-metric dimension is \(n\), and the only bases are the two bipartition classes. More generally, a set is solid-resolving exactly when its complement lies in one bipartition class or one deleted matched pair, so \[F_s(\operatorname{Cr}_n;z)=2z^n(1+z)^n-z^{2n}+nz^{2n-2}.\]
