For the generalized wheel \(W_{m,n}=C_n\vee\overline{K_m}\), every strong resolving set is obtained by omitting at most one center and, for \(n\ge4\), at most one rim edge. Hence \(\mathcal R_{m,n}(x)=(x^m+m x^{m-1})(x^n+n x^{n-1}+n x^{n-2})\), giving exactly \(mn\) strong metric bases when \(n\ge4\), with the triangular-rim cases classified separately.
