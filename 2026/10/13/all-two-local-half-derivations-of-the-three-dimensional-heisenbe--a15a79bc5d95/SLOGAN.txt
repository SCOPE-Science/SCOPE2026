For the three-dimensional Heisenberg Lie algebra over any field of characteristic different from two, a map is two-local \(\frac12\)-derivation exactly when its quotient-coordinate map \(g:K^2\to K^2\) and central-coordinate map \(h:K^3\to K\) are independently homogeneous of degree one. Over \(\mathbb F_q\) for odd \(q\), this gives exactly \(q^{q^2+3q+3}\) maps, of which \(q^7\) are linear and only \(q^6\) are genuine half-derivations.
