For \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M\), every unique-response strong Roman dominating function belongs to one of only three rigid families, so \[U_{\operatorname{Cr}_n}(z)=z^{2n}+2n z^{n+h}+n z^{2h},\qquad h=1+\left\lceil(n-1)/2\right\rceil.\] In particular there are exactly \(3n+1\) feasible functions and exactly \(n\) minima, one for each deleted matched pair.
