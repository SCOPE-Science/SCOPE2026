For the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M\), an initial set is zero forcing exactly when it omits at most two vertices from each side and does not omit the same two matched indices on both sides. Hence \(\mathcal Z(\operatorname{Cr}_n;x)=(x^n+n x^{n-1}+\binom n2x^{n-2})^2-\binom n2x^{2n-4}\), so \(Z(\operatorname{Cr}_n)=2n-4\).
