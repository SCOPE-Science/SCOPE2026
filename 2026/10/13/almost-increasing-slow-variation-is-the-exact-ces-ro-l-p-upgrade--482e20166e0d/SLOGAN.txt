For independent triangular arrays, Cesàro uniform integrability of \(|X|^pL(|X|^p)\) forces normalized maximal \(L^p\) convergence for every array exactly when the slowly varying weight \(L\) is eventually almost increasing, for \(1\le p<2\). Outside this class, symmetric finite-support rows can still satisfy the weak law while their normalized maximal \(p\)-moments diverge; this failure can occur even with \(L\ge1\).
