# Scientific assessment

## Correctness: PASS
The nilpotent symmetric-tensor representation proves a unique length-N Jordan chain, not merely repeated eigenvalues. Strict positivity of all local traps and noise follows precisely from the displayed parameter inequality. The Lyapunov equation proves the unique product-Gaussian invariant measure and the stationary current, while its weighted squared integral gives the full entropy-production expression. A Gaussian mean-shift calculation and an exact tensor norm prove the all-time KL law and strict contraction. Reproducible exact symbolic checks support the chain of identities for \(N=2\) through \(N=8\), but the all-N conclusions depend on the tensor proof rather than finite sampling.

## Originality: PASS with explicit residual risk
The full text of Pal and Gupta (arXiv:2609.05391v1) was compared at its uniform-trap chain exceptional point, disordered-dimer exceptional points, and pseudo-equilibrium sections. It establishes both themes but at different parameter regimes, not the simultaneous heterogeneous-noise family and exact KL law here. Higher-order spin exceptional points already appear in Du et al. (arXiv:2205.07566v1). Drift--diffusion Lyapunov separation, and symmetric powers of Jordan blocks, already appear in Rodríguez (arXiv:2601.16121v1). Barrera, Högele and Pardo (arXiv:2009.10590) also cover generic polynomial decay factors from drift Jordan blocks in small-noise thermalization, not the present exact closed KL law in its factorized irreversible model. Those established ingredients are credited; no uniqueness or priority is inferred just from unsuccessful searches. The residual risk is an unindexed or implicitly equivalent construction combining known spin matrices with the Gaussian stationary covariance condition.

## Value: PASS
The question of whether an arbitrarily high-order drift exceptional point can coexist with a factorized, irreversible steady state is motivated directly by the distinct regimes in the recent nonreciprocal OU analysis. The theorem gives an all-dimensional physically positive local-bath realization, an exact finite-size dissipation observable, and a fully explicit transient nonequilibrium information distance. It is more than a generic Lyapunov identity or a renamed non-Hermitian matrix: it imposes sitewise local fluctuation--dissipation consistency and derives a sharp dimension-dependent positivity boundary. The result does not claim generic robustness or a new SU(2) exceptional-point mechanism.
