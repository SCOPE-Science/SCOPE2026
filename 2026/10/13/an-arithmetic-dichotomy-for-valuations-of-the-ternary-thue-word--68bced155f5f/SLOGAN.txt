For the ternary Thue word \(0\mapsto012,\ 1\mapsto02,\ 2\mapsto1\), an integer valuation preserves abelian complexity at every factor length exactly when \(A=a_0+a_2-2a_1\ne0\) and \(A\nmid(a_0-a_2)\). Every other valuation loses an unbounded number of abelian classes along the explicit lengths \((2\cdot4^k+1)/3\).
