An explicit weak-entanglement qubit strategy makes the asymmetric \(I_{3322}\) detector threshold tend to exactly \(3/7\), replacing the original approximate \(43\%\) construction by closed measurement angles. Within the natural scaling \(\alpha_1=O(\theta)\), \(\alpha_2=O(\sqrt{\theta})\), \(\alpha_3=\pi/2+O(\sqrt{\theta})\), \(\beta_1,\beta_2=O(\theta)\), \(\beta_3=O(\sqrt{\theta})\), a completed-square identity shows that \(3/7\) is the sharp limiting threshold.
