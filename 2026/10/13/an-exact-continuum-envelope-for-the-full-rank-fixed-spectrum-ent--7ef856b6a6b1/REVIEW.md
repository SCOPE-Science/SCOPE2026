# Review

## Correctness

PASS. The source's exact necessary constraints were checked in the full preprint, including the continuum family in Eq. (39) and the rank-four first-row reduction in Theorem 8. The new step reduces the full second-row continuum to a two-dimensional copositive quadratic inequality, derives the exact bound \(F_{23}\le 1/2+\sqrt{(1/2-F_{22})(1/2-F_{24})}\), and then performs an elementary concave quadratic maximization. Boundary cases, both discriminant branches, and the ordered-spectrum assumptions are covered. The strict-extension spectrum was checked exactly.

## Originality

PASS. The closest source is Camacho--de Vicente, arXiv:2511.08285v1, which introduces the continuum constraint and numerically finds an infeasible region larger than its analytic Theorem 8 regions A, B, and C. Its analytic proof does not state the closed-form continuum envelope, the discriminant split, or the threshold \((1+\sqrt2)/2\). The 2024 predecessor concerns rank-two spectra. Targeted statement-level searches found no source implying the new envelope. A residual risk remains that an equivalent later derivation exists under different notation.

## Value

PASS. The source explicitly identifies a gap between the numerically infeasible full-rank region and its smaller analytic region. The new result converts part of that gap into a closed-form theorem, strictly subsumes all three published analytic full-rank regions, and provides an exact spectral witness outside them. This is a structural strengthening rather than a routine recomputation.

## Closest literature and limitations

The closest literature is arXiv:2511.08285v1 / Phys. Rev. A 113, 022416, together with arXiv:2402.05673 and arXiv:quant-ph/0011110. The result remains only a necessary no-go criterion under \(2\lambda_2+\lambda_3<\lambda_1\); it does not settle the complete full-rank feasibility problem.
