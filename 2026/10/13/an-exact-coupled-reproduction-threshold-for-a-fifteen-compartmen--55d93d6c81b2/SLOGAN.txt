In the printed fifteen-compartment COVID–Mpox system, the disease-free invasion threshold is the Perron root of an explicitly coupled human co-infection block together with the rodent threshold—not the maximum of the three separately published reproduction numbers. A fully positive exact parameter choice gives published maximum \(7/8<1\) but a true full-system reproduction number \(47/45+\sqrt{27049}/180>1\), reversing the claimed local-stability verdict.
