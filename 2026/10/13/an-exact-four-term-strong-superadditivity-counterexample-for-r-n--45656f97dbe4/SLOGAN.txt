The four-term state \(\bigl(3|0000\rangle+2|0101\rangle+6|1010\rangle+3|1111\rangle\bigr)/\sqrt{58}\) gives an exact four-qubit violation of Rényi-\(2\) strong superadditivity. Its residual is \(\log_2(611618/619817)<0\), replacing a previously numerical existence result by a hand-checkable certificate.
