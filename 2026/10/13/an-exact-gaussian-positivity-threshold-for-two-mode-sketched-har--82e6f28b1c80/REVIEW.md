## Correctness — PASS
Equation (2.13) of Güttel--Liu--Nyman specializes to the stated rank-one harmonic quotient. Restriction to the eigenplane and an exact \(2\times2\) Sylvester determinant prove the positive-half-plane event for every starting direction. Gaussian orthogonal invariance gives the squared-cosine Beta distribution, and its stochastic ordering and normal limit give the sharp row-budget law. The rational checker constructs actual negative witnesses as well as positive cases. Simulations only corroborate the proof.

## Originality — PASS
The originating paper, arXiv:2607.10354v1, proves half-plane-based convergence statements without this exact Gaussian all-directions probability. The related full texts arXiv:2601.10248v1 and DOI:10.1002/nla.70029 treat corrected or reorthogonalized Ritz constructions. Gaunt's arXiv:2408.04101 handles scalar products of correlated Gaussians, a known component for a single fixed starting vector. The claim is simultaneous positivity under one shared sketch, not novelty of Beta laws or elementary linear algebra. An equivalent formula in older Wishart or antieigenvalue work remains a residual risk; a broad 2026 eigenproblem thesis could not be read in full.

## Value — PASS
The half-plane hypothesis is directly relevant to restarted sketched harmonic matrix-function algorithms. For the natural extremal two-eigenmode plane, the finding determines the exact probability of satisfying that hypothesis uniformly and the sharp \(\Theta(\kappa)\) Gaussian sketch budget, including its confidence-dependent constant. It warns against replacing a uniform recycled-sketch guarantee with fixed-start probabilities. It claims neither global convergence nor arbitrary sketches.
