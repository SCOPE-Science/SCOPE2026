For a single real Gaussian sketch shared across every start in a two-eigenmode invariant plane, the probability that all one-dimensional sketched harmonic Ritz values stay positive is exactly \(I_{4\kappa/(\kappa+1)^2}(1/2,(s-1)/2)\). Achieving any fixed success confidence requires asymptotically \(s\sim\kappa[\Phi^{-1}(1-\delta/2)]^2/4\) sketch rows, quantifying a sharp uniform-stability cost absent from fixed-start guarantees.
