For complex Haar-random pure two-qubit inputs under independent identical amplitude damping, the exact probability of still having entanglement at positive visibility \(q\) is \(\frac{1}{2[1+(1-q)^2]}+\frac{\arctan(1-q)}{2(1-q)}\). Consequently an exact fraction \(\frac{6-\pi}{8}\) undergoes entanglement sudden death at finite time, while the remaining \(\frac{2+\pi}{8}\) loses entanglement only asymptotically.
