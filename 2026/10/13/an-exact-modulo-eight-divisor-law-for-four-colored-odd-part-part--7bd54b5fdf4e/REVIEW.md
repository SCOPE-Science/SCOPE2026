# Scientific review: a four-colored odd-part divisor congruence

## Correctness — PASS

The proof establishes the full odd-index congruence, including the exact normalization between the pentagonal-square count and representations by \(x^2+3y^2\). Modulo-eight reduction is valid in integer formal power series; the potentially exceptional zero-coordinate representations are excluded by \(12n+1\equiv13\pmod{24}\). The Eisenstein-integer norm calculation has exactly two representations per ideal of odd norm, and the prime-power residue cases follow from its Euler factors. Independent direct coefficient computations to index \(1200\) and finite norm enumeration matched. The density argument uses diverging reciprocal primes in the split residue class, not a finite heuristic.

## Originality — PASS, with access risk

Hirschhorn–Sellers, arXiv:2507.09752, supplies the same family and an \(a_4\) congruence modulo seven, but not an odd-index modulo-eight characterization. Jameson–Sellers, arXiv:2609.19440, announces a general modulo-three method; its full text was unavailable, so an equivalent secondary statement cannot be entirely excluded. Hirschhorn's quadratic-form identity and OEIS A002324 supply individually known mathematical ingredients, but not the new coefficient identity. Searches for alternative product, divisor-sum, quadratic-norm and colored-partition formulations found no covering statement. The comparison is about implications, not word matching.

## Value — PASS

The theorem determines every odd-index residue of a natural colored partition sequence modulo eight, turns it into a direct prime-factor criterion, proves density-one divisibility, and exhibits infinitely many sharp exceptions. A structural two-adic theta reduction, rather than an arbitrary finite modulus search, makes the parity restriction mathematically motivated.

## Scope and residual limitations

Only positive odd indices and residues modulo eight are covered. The analytic density conclusion is qualitative. The uninspected September 2026 full text and unindexed equivalent results remain genuine originality risks. Finite checks corroborate but cannot replace the general proof.
