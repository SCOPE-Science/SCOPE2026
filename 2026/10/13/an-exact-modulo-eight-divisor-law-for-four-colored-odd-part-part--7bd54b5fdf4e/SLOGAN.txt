For four-colored odd-part partitions with one color on even parts, every positive odd-index coefficient satisfies the exact rule \(a_4(n)\equiv2\sum_{d\mid(12n+1)}\chi_{-3}(d)\pmod8\). Thus the odd-index residues are always zero or four modulo eight, with a complete prime-factor test, density-one divisibility by eight, and infinitely many four-residue exceptions.
