For every continuous scalar filter \(f\) with \(f(0)=1\), positivity on **all** unweighted connected complete multipartite graph Laplacians at **all** positive scales holds exactly when \(0\le f(s)\le1\) and \((1-f(s))/s\) is nonincreasing. The fourth-order two-stage Gauss–Legendre/\([2/2]\) Padé diffusion step satisfies this sharp criterion and is strictly positive, symmetric, and doubly stochastic at every step size on every graph in the family, despite its zero unrestricted absolute-monotonicity radius.
