For the \(k=3\) three-state hardcore-SOS model, the numerically reported critical coupling \(\widehat\theta_c\approx0.4812\) is exactly the square root of the unique root in \((23/100,6/25)\) of \(2187z^5-2916z^4+1940z^3-660z^2+96z-4\). A rational reconstruction of the tangency variables certifies that this root lies on the physical upper branch.
