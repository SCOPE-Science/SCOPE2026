For a squarefree semiprime \(n=pq\) with \(p<q\), the divisor-sum increment equation \(\sigma(n+1)-\sigma(n)=\sigma(n)/\tau(n)\) forces \(p\le3\): a \(2q\) solution requires \(q\equiv15\pmod{16}\), while the \(3q\) solutions are exactly those with \(q=12r+11\) prime and \(\sigma(18r+17)=20(r+1)\). This gives an unrestricted prime-support obstruction and an exact odd-argument reduction, without claiming the remaining even branch is empty.
