For two-mode squeezed thermal states, the optimized covariance-to-fidelity Schmidt-number witness has an exact thermal boundary at \(\beta_c=\log(2+\sqrt3)\): at and below it the fidelity lower bound is identically zero for every squeezing, while above it the certified dimension grows like \(C_\beta e^{2r}\). The same covariance matrix still yields an EoF-based Schmidt-number lower bound of order \(e^{2r}\) at every fixed \(\beta>0\), isolating a sharp limitation of the fidelity conversion rather than of the measured second moments.
