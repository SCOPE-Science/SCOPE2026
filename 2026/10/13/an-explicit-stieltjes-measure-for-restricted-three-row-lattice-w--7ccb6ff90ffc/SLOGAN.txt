The restricted three-row lattice-word sequence \(B_k=\binom{3k}{k}-2\binom{3k}{k-1}+\binom{3k}{k-2}\) has an explicit strictly positive probability density, parametrized on \([0,27/4]\), whose moments are exactly \(B_k\) for every \(k\geq0\). Consequently every minor of its infinite Hankel matrix is strictly positive, proving the \(B\)-sequence half of a 2026 Stieltjes-moment conjecture.
