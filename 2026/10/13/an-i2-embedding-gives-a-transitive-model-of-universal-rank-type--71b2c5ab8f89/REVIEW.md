# Review

## Correctness

PASS. Lücke--Poveda Theorem 1.3 gives, from an \(I2\)-embedding, a transitive set-sized model \(M\models\mathrm{ZFC}\) with a proper class of exacting cardinals. Internally, this means exacting cardinals are unbounded in \(\operatorname{Ord}^M\). Osinski Corollary 5.9 is a ZFC theorem and states that unboundedly many exacting cardinals are equivalent to the displayed universal rank-type compactness scheme. Applying that theorem inside \(M\) yields the claim. No external absoluteness step is required.

## Originality

PASS. Osinski's paper contains the universal compactness characterization but no occurrence of \(I2\); Lücke--Poveda provide the \(I2\) transitive-model theorem but their paper contains no occurrence of “compactness” and does not cite Osinski's preprint. Targeted searches for the combined implication and for its equivalent formulations returned no statement of the synthesis. The claim is not a special case of either theorem alone: both are needed.

## Value

PASS. The synthesis supplies a natural relative-consistency upper bound for a universal model-theoretic compactness principle ranging over every logic. This directly connects a new forcing consistency theorem to a new Makowsky-style compactness characterization and answers a consistency-strength question that is mathematically motivated by Osinski's discussion of how far compactness principles can reach in the large-cardinal hierarchy.

## Closest literature and limitations

The closest source on the compactness side is Osinski, Corollary 5.9, which is an equivalence with unbounded exacting cardinals. The closest source on the consistency side is Lücke--Poveda, Theorem 1.3, which constructs the transitive set-sized model with a proper class of exacting cardinals from an \(I2\)-embedding.

The conclusion is internal to the constructed model. No lower consistency bound, equiconsistency, or ambient-universe compactness assertion is made.
