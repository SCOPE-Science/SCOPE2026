In the displayed Theorem 4 cost model of arXiv:2510.09911v1, the amplitude-amplification product is indexed over the bottom \(\ell\) recursion levels, so for \(k=2^m\) its exponent sum is \(2^\ell-1\), not \(k(1-2^{-\ell})\). The printed product and the following exponential simplification therefore cannot both be correct; retaining the printed product even makes the displayed cost subexponential for a suitable growing \(\ell\), which is a proof diagnostic rather than an algorithm claim.
