# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The all-size proof is independent of the finite checker. For a symmetric operator, a repeated characteristic root yields an isotropic eigenvector either in a two-dimensional eigenspace or as the endpoint of a nontrivial Jordan chain; the converse follows from symmetry and the orthogonal-complement description of the image. The incidence equations form a surjective vector-bundle morphism with smooth rational kernel bundle. A single nilpotent symmetric two-block supplies the generically unique marked eigenline and a simple-zero discriminant perturbation. The size-two exception is factored explicitly. Risks are restricted to interpreting the incidence as a resolution of the *reduced* affine locus, not stronger singularity or crepancy assertions.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** The initiating preprint’s relevant primary sections, Friedman--Hoşten--Rosana (arXiv:2610.02018v1), states the coincidence of two discriminant sets, not the vector bundle resolution, birationality, rationality and polynomial irreducibility. Breiding--Kozhasov--Lerario (2018) analyzes a different real codimension-two repeated-eigenvalue variety, not a proper birational model of the complex hypersurface. published-finding corpus searches for eigenline, isotropic quadric, discriminant, resolution and rationality formulations gave no covering result. The strongest unresolved risk is a pre-existing algebraic invariant-theoretic treatment under another title or terminology, not a directly located counterexample.

**Value — PASS.** The result geometrically resolves the previously set-theoretic ED discriminant for all projection-Grassmannian ranks with one construction, proves that its function field is purely transcendental, and determines the exact reduced degree and low-dimensional reducibility boundary. This addresses a natural global geometric invariant and provides a parameter space for studying exceptional eigenvalue collisions. It does not assert a new ED-degree formula or classify exceptional fiber schemes.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
