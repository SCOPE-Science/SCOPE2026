For every \(n\ge3\), the characteristic repeated-eigenvalue hypersurface of complex symmetric \(n\times n\) matrices, which is the ED discriminant of every projection Grassmannian \(\operatorname{pGr}(k,n)\), admits a proper birational smooth rational resolution by marking an isotropic eigenline. Its defining discriminant polynomial is irreducible of degree \(n(n-1)\); the size-two discriminant instead splits into two hyperplanes.
