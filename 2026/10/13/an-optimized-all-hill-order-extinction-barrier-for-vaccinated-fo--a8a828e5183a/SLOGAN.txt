For every Hill order \(n\ge2\), the vaccinated fomite SVEIR system has a computable, vaccination-weighted global extinction guarantee \(\mathcal T_n\le1\), including the neutral boundary of its optimal uniform-linear comparison matrix. The constant \(L_n=(n-1)^{(n-1)/n}/(n\kappa)\) is sharp for the environmental response inequality, but the epidemic extinction criterion is only sufficient, not necessary.
