On the fixed one-dimensional direction sequence \(+1,-1,+1,-1,\ldots\), AngularGrad's paper angular multiplier still changes with the common gradient magnitude. Its cosine and tangent variants have reciprocal-amplitude symmetry but opposite extrema at \(G=1\): \(\phi_{\cos}=1/2\) and \(\phi_{\tan}=1\), with exact crossovers at \(G=0.3460143392\ldots\) and its reciprocal.
