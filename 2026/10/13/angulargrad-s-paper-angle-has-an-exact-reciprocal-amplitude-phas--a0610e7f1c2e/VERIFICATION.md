The package verifies the paper-form AngularGrad angular coefficient on a persistent sign-alternating one-coordinate gradient.

`verify.py` checks the direct source angle, the closed cosine and tangent formulas, reciprocal-amplitude symmetry, the exact crossover amplitudes, the unique extrema, and the unit-amplitude separation.

The numerical checks are transcription guards. The phase diagram and crossover equation are proved algebraically in `RESULT.md`.
