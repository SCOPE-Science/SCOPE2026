# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof uses two verified statements from the motivating paper: every minimal nonzero two-sided ideal is one-dimensional and annihilated from both sides, and the free binary Zinbiel algebra is spanned by left-normed products in generators and degree-three defects. Iterating the minimal-ideal statement through quotients gives a complete ideal flag; the flag forces the left-normed power bound \(A^{n+1}=0\). Homogeneous degree counting in the free spanning set then forces every word of degree at least \(3n+1\) to expand into left-normed products with at least \(n+1\) factors. No finite experiment is being promoted to an infinite proof.

## Originality
PASS. The closest source, arXiv:2610.02852v1, proves qualitative nilpotency but leaves its exponent unspecified. The foundational defining-identities paper explicitly posed binary-Zinbiel nilpotency as open. Searches for annihilator flags, left-normed exponent \(n+1\), and arbitrary-bracketing exponent \(3n+1\) found no covering statement. Ordinary Zinbiel and symmetric Zinbiel results apply to strict subclasses and therefore do not dominate the claim.

## Value
PASS. The flag is a structural refinement of the newly proved nilpotency theorem, and the two explicit bounds give dimension-controlled cutoffs for induction, classification, and computation in the full finite-dimensional binary-Zinbiel class. The result is not merely a renaming or a parameter substitution.

The coefficient \(3\) is not claimed optimal, and characteristic zero is essential to the cited binary-Zinbiel structure theory. The main residual originality risk is an equivalent quantitative corollary in poorly indexed literature.
