For a crown graph, the anti-forcing number of a perfect matching depends only on the number of cycles in its derangement: \(af(\operatorname{Cr}_n,M_\pi)=n(n-3)/2+c(\pi)\). Hence the full anti-forcing distribution is a shifted derangement cycle enumerator, with continuous spectrum from \(n(n-3)/2+1\) through \(n(n-3)/2+\lfloor n/2\rfloor\).
