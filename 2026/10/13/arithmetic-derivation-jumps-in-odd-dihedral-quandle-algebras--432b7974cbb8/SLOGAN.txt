For every odd dihedral quandle order prime to the coefficient characteristic, the full derivation Lie algebra is abelian and its dimension is an explicit divisor sum controlled by the multiplicative orders of \(2\) and the residue class of \(-1\). Positive-characteristic derivations first occur at order \(11\) in characteristic \(3\), where a Legendre-symbol circulant gives a nonzero derivation.
