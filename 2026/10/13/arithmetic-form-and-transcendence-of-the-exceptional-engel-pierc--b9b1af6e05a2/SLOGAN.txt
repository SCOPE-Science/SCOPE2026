Ahn's countable dense exceptional set \(C\) has a rigid arithmetic form: every point is a rational affine expression in \(e^{-1}\) with nonzero \(e^{-1}\)-coefficient, obtained explicitly from its finite Pierce prefix and consecutive tail. Hence every point of \(C\) is transcendental, so every algebraic irrational in \(0,1\) lies in the image of the Engel–Pierce transfer homeomorphism.
