## correctness

PASS

The sextic f(x,y)+f(z,w) with f=uv(u^4-v^4) has 6 octahedral root points. The projection/Boissière–Sarti lemma partitions its lines into 36 joining lines and graph lines M=sA, where A permutes the six roots and s^6 c_A=-1. Independently enumerating all 120 image triples gives exactly 24 valid projectivities and 96 excluded by Gaussian norm gcds 50, 3200 or 204800, supported only at primes 2 and 5; this excludes new projectivities for every p>=7. Exact multiplier and normalized scalar conditions place all lines in Q(zeta24), and a primitive-zeta24 graph forces minimality.
