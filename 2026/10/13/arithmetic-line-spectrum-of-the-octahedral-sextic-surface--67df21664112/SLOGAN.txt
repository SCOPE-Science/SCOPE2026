The 180 geometric lines on the octahedral sextic have minimal common field \(\mathbb Q(\zeta_{24})\), but only 20 are individually rational over \(\mathbb Q\). For every prime \(p\ge7\), the exact \(\mathbb F_p\)-rational line count is determined by \(p\bmod24\); the exceptional smooth reduction at \(p=5\) has 756 geometric lines, 156 rational.
