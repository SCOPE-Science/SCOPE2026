The rigid 57-line Hesse closure reduces in characteristic seven to the *entire* projective plane \(\mathbb P^2(\mathbb F_7)\), its line product becoming the degree-57 Moore determinant. Exactly primes \(7,13,19\) change its incidence lattice among characteristics at least five, and its good-prime rational intersection-point count alternates between \(453\) and \(31\) according to the prime modulo three.
