For the six-conic, six-line XR arrangement, the 21 geometric singular points have minimal common field \(\mathbb Q(i,\sqrt3)\) and precisely seven rational points over \(\mathbb Q\). In every characteristic \(p\ge5\), its fixed integral model preserves all nine sixfold points and twelve nodes, while its \(\mathbb F_p\)-rational singular-point count is \(21\), \(13\), or \(9\), according to \(p\bmod12\).
