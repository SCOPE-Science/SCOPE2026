The printed Lévy Milstein discretization of a 2026 six-compartment measles model cancels its own infection-incidence gain, forcing exposed-plus-infectious mass to decay geometrically at every transmission rate in its noise-free specialization. At exact positive parameters where the intended model has an unstable disease-free state and a strictly positive endemic equilibrium, the printed scheme instead eradicates infection for all nonnegative starting states at sufficiently small steps.
