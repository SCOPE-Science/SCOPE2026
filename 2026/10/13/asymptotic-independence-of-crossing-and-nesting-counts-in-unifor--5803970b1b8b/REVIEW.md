# Scientific review: crossing and nesting fluctuations

**Correctness — PASS.** The uniform matching model is represented exactly by independent continuous chord endpoints. The proof computes conditional crossing and nesting probabilities and explicitly integrates their squares and product. It establishes the nonstandard key cancellation \(\operatorname{Cov}(q_C,q_N)=0\), then counts overlapping and nonoverlapping chord pairs without approximation to derive exact finite covariance. A standard two-dimensional U-statistic Hoeffding decomposition and the bounded degenerate remainder give the joint Gaussian limit, whose diagonal covariance means independence. Exact recursive enumeration through seven chords, three-label interleaving enumeration, and symbolic projection integration passed. Limits: the checker alone is not an infinite proof, and the theorem does not address rates of convergence.

**Originality — PASS, with specific historical uncertainty.** Kasraoui–Zeng, arXiv:math/0601081v1, Proposition 3.8, already give the full bivariate finite enumerator. Their result dominates finite covariance formulas; those are supporting identities, not proposed original ones. Flajolet–Noy (2000) supply the univariate crossing CLT and exact crossing variance. Khare–Lorentz–Yan derive exact matching-statistic moments, particularly marginal crossing/nesting second moments, without a two-dimensional independent weak limit. The identified new statement is the independent bivariate Gaussian limit, based on vanishing covariance between the first chord projections; it does not follow from marginal normality plus finite correlation convergence alone. Another historical treatment could possibly extract the same limit from the earlier finite generating function, and this is a residual priority risk. Search did not establish an independently certified novelty claim.

**Value — PASS.** Crossing and nesting counts quantify geometrically distinct, mutually exclusive pair interactions in a natural random matching. Their strictly negative covariance at every finite order could suggest lasting antagonism; the exact cancellation of shared-chord covariance and the resulting asymptotic independent Gaussian channels show a nontrivial scale separation. The complete leading two-dimensional fluctuation law is useful for simultaneous calibration, and the exact correlation gives a finite-size correction. The mathematical substance is an explicit orthogonality mechanism and nondegenerate joint limit, not only reproducibility or relabeling a known one-dimensional theorem.

**Residual risks.** The bivariate finite enumerator is a very strong earlier source, and an unpublished or unindexed derivation could contain the same joint weak limit. Independent priority and proof verification are outstanding. None of the finite computations is offered as an infinite certificate.
