For every uniform chord diagram with at least two chords, the numbers of crossing pairs and nesting pairs are negatively correlated for every finite size, with exact correlation \(-5/[2(n+3)]\), yet their centered \(n^{3/2}\)-scaled fluctuations converge jointly to **independent** Gaussians of variance \(1/45\). Orthogonality of their first-order chord projections explains the cancellation; the finite bivariate generating function was already known.
