For the nilpotent algebra \(e_1e_2=e_2e_1=e_3\), the Nijenhuis variety is the union of two affine \(5\)-spaces meeting in an affine \(3\)-space. Over odd \(\mathbf F_q\), automorphism conjugacy has exactly \(q(4q+1)\) classes with four normal forms for each distinct-eigenvalue pair and five on each repeated-eigenvalue fiber.
