PASS. The group is defined by an explicit faithful action of \(D_{10}\) on the additive group of \(\mathbb F_{16}\). The proof identifies \(V=O_2(G)\) and \(G'=V\rtimes C_5\) as characteristic, partitions the group into five automorphism-invariant strata, and derives their sizes \(1,15,64,20,60\) from fixed-space and order calculations. An exact finite enumeration of all \(20160\) matrices in \(\operatorname{GL}_4(2)\) finds a \(60\)-element linear normalizer; including translations yields a \(960\)-element affine normalizer whose conjugation action is transitive on each stratum. Thus a subgroup of \(\operatorname{Aut}(G)\) is already transitive on every stratum, while full automorphisms cannot fuse strata because \(O_2(G)\), \(G'\), and element order are invariant.
