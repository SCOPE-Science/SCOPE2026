The unital subalgebras of \(\mathbb F_q[t]/(t^6)\) split into exactly \(12\) automorphism-orbits in characteristic above three, \(18\) in characteristic three, and \(16+\gcd(3,q-1)\) in characteristic two; every orbit size is explicitly determined. The characteristic-two jump distinguishes odd and even field-extension degrees, although the total number of subalgebras is given by the same previously known polynomial in \(q\).
