For odd \(q\), the three-dimensional total dot-product graph over \(\mathbb F_q\) is a \((q-1)\)-vertex twin blow-up of the orthogonal polarity graph of \(\operatorname{PG}(2,q)\), with \(\operatorname{Aut}\cong S_{q-1}^{\,q^2+q+1}\rtimes\operatorname{P\Gamma O}(3,q)\). Its determining number and metric dimension both equal \((q^2+q+1)(q-2)\), and every minimum set omits exactly one nonzero vector from each projective point.
