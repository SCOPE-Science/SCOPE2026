The least extremal restricted-binomial-GCD row is already the smallest admissible row \(2m\) exactly when doubling \(m\) carries through every base-\(p\) digit: \(2d_0\ge p\) and \(2d_j+1\ge p\) thereafter. For odd \(p\), this gives exactly \(\frac{p-1}{2}(\frac{p+1}{2})^{r-1}\) such \(r\)-digit parameters, while for \(p=2\) the only ones are \(m=2^r-1\).
