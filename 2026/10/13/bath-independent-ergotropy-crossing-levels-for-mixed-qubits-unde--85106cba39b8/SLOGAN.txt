For two arbitrary mixed qubit preparations undergoing the same thermal amplitude-damping semigroup, ergotropy order reverses exactly once if and only if the initially higher-work state has lower transverse coherence. The work at this crossing is exactly \(h(b_2-b_1)/(2(z_1-z_2))\), independent of bath temperature and damping rates; the crossing time is explicit and advances as the bath gets colder at fixed relaxation rate.
