A uniformly random \(n\)-world S5 frame with independent uniformly colored propositions becomes fully modally observable at the Bell-scale palette size \(Q\asymp n\log n\). At \(Q/(nW(n))\to\lambda>0\), the number of indistinguishable world pairs tends to \(\operatorname{Poisson}(1/(2\lambda))\), while an exact finite squarefree-palette coefficient counts every successful colored frame.
