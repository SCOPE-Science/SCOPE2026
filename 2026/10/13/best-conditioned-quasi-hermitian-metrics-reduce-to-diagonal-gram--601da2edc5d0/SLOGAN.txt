For a finite-dimensional quasi-Hermitian Hamiltonian with simple real spectrum, optimizing the spectral condition number of the physical metric is exactly a positive diagonal-scaling problem for the eigenvector Gram matrix. Any pair of normalized eigenvectors with overlap \(c\) forces \(\kappa_2(\eta)\ge(1+c)/(1-c)\), and in two dimensions this is the exact optimum, diverging as \(4/\theta^2\) when the eigendirections coalesce.
