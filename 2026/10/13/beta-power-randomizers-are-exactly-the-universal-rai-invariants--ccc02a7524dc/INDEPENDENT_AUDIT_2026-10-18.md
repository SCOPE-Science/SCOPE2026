# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For any nonnegative absolutely continuous parent X and strictly increasing absolutely continuous randomizer cdf G with a.e. g>0, the exact cdf composition H=G∘F_X and density g(F_X)f_X give RAI ratio u log(u)g(u)/(G(u)log(G(u))). Choosing an exponential parent covers every 0<u<1. Universal ratio 1 forces d log(−log G(u))/du=1/(u log u) a.e.; absolute continuity and 0<G<1 imply G(u)=u^a with a>0. Conversely every Beta(a,1) yields exact invariance. Thus separate a,b>0 transformations preserve any valid pointwise RAI order, and integer a,b give iid sample maxima. Independently verified the exact multiplier and exponential-parent formula symbolically, respecting log((1−e^−t)^a)=a log(1−e^−t) for positive arguments. This is valid only where cdf in (0,1) and densities defined, not singular cdfs.

## originality

PASS

Kayid and Alshehri (2024) already derive random-quantile cdf composition, discuss Beta(n,1) maxima and RAI preservation with same randomizer under additional stochastic-order premises, and flag two randomizers as a future problem. Their inspected full text does not characterize all universally RAI-invariant randomizers or prove arbitrary distinct beta parameters preserve order without stochastic-order hypotheses. Buono et al. treat generalized RAI, not this universal if-and-only-if randomizer class. The chain-rule observation may appear elsewhere under hazard terminology: explicit residual risk.

A necessary-and-sufficient family classification of all admissible randomizers preserving the entire RAI functional is a motivated exact structural theorem, and eliminating stochastic-order side conditions for all pairs of Beta(a,1) quantile randomizers addresses a clearly identified natural open subproblem. Its ODE proof is short but the iff classification and cross-parameter order consequence are useful; it is not asserted to settle arbitrary randomizer pairs.
