Finite Bochvar algebras whose canonical Płonka index is a chain have a binary normal form: there is exactly one isomorphism type of every positive finite size, with Boolean-fiber sizes given by its set bits. Its automorphism group is a product of symmetric groups determined by gaps between the bit positions, and it is rigid exactly at sizes \(2^t-1\) and \(2^t-2\) (within positive ranges).
