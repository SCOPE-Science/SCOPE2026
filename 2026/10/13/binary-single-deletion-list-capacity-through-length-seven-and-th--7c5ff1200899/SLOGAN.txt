For one deletion from binary length-seven words, the exact maximum codebook sizes for list limits \(L=1,\ldots,8\) are \(16,33,50,65,81,96,112,128\). In particular, a 33-word list-two code exists but cannot be a union of two single-deletion-correcting codes, and length seven is the smallest block length with \(M(n,2)>2M(n,1)\).
