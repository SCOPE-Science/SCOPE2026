The two-adic valuation of the step-three Pascal-row GCD is determined for every row by the binary weight of \(3n\): it is \(2\) at weight \(2\), \(1\) at weight \(3\), and \(0\) at every larger weight. This classifies the entire small-prime exceptional case left outside the classical congruence criterion, including exactly when the GCD is divisible by \(4\).
