Finite S4.3 cluster-chain endomorphisms have a complete block-surjection normal form, giving exact algebraic image-rank and retraction polynomials for arbitrary unequal cluster sizes. When all clusters have size \(r\), a uniformly random endomorphism has image-cluster length \(1+\operatorname{Binomial}(h-1,r!/(r!+r^r))\), and the idempotent proportion is exponentially small in \(h\).
