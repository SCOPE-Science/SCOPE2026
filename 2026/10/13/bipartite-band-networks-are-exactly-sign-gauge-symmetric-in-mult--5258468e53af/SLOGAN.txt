For the multiband BCS linearized operator \(K_T+\lambda V^{\mathrm d}+\lambda\kappa V^{\mathrm{od}}\), any bipartite graph of nonzero inter-band blocks admits a bandwise sign gauge sending \(\kappa\) to \(-\kappa\). Hence the entire linearized spectrum and \(T_c\) are exactly even in \(\kappa\) for all coupling strengths; an odd cycle is precisely the obstruction to this gauge, though not by itself proof of sign-dependent \(T_c\).
