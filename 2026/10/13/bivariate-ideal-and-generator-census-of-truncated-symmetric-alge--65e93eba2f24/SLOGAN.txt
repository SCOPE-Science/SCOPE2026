The complete joint distribution of ideal dimension and minimal generator number in \(\mathbb F_q[x_1,\ldots,x_d]/(x_1,\ldots,x_d)^3\) is an explicit double Gaussian-binomial sum for every \(d\ge1\) and finite field. It counts nonhomogeneous ideals and distinguishes, already in two variables, same-dimensional ideals requiring one versus three generators.
