# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof differentiates the exact published hazard. Before the exponential-power derivative changes sign, both derivative contributions are nonpositive and the Weibull contribution is strictly negative. After that point, the ratio of the positive and negative contributions has a strictly positive logarithmic derivative, rises from zero to infinity, and therefore crosses one exactly once. Endpoint divergence makes this crossing the unique global hazard minimum. The checker independently reproduces the formulas and the extreme transition scale of the source's plotted example.
