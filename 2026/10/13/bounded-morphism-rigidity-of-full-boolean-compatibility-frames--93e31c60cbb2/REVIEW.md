# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The proof reconstructs a source-atom map using singleton worlds. Back-lifting to disjoint target singleton worlds rules out multisized singleton images; forth/back then force all other world images to be direct images. Back-lifting from a source world covering the target proves onto-ness, and the direct-image converse uses the preimage of the target world. These steps cover arbitrary nonempty infinite or finite sets without a choice assumption. A standalone finite checker exhaustively matches morphisms and surjective atom maps on the declared small cases. Finite checks are not used as proofs for infinite sets.

## Originality — PASS, with identified risk

The motivating work supplies the co-consistency frames and a partition-induced Boolean algebra embedding (Lemma 6.13), but the inspected sections do not contain an exhaustiveness theorem for bounded morphisms of full frames. Standard inverse-image/direct-image adjunction and partition counting are acknowledged inputs. The two-atom codomain result is already a restricted special case; the claimed advance is the full arbitrary-target, arbitrary-cardinality characterization, including a categorical equivalence, and not an isolated new count. Bounded morphisms are stronger than relation-preserving maps, and the result concerns compatibility rather than reverse-inclusion frames. Prior formulations in Boolean-algebra duality or set-functor language may be equivalent and are a genuine residual risk; unsuccessful searches alone cannot certify novelty.

## Value — PASS

This settles a natural *complete classification* question for the full class of compatibility frames: which modal quotients between these frames can actually occur? The answer determines all morphism sets, forbids morphisms to larger finite atom sets, identifies composition, and gives exact finite counts. This is an invariant of the frame construction, not an arbitrary choice of finite slice, and applies without a cardinality bound. The elementary combinatorial enumerator is a corollary rather than the whole result.

## Limits

Restricted to full nonzero powerset frames; no extension to arbitrary Boolean algebras or subframes is claimed. One source may already imply the theorem in another formalism. The small-computation replay is a consistency check, not independent validation of the unrestricted proof.
