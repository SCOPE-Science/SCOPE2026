For full nonzero Boolean powerset frames equipped with the compatibility relation \(A\cap B\ne\varnothing\), every Kripke bounded morphism is exactly direct image along a unique surjection of atoms. Thus their entire bounded-morphism category is the category of nonempty sets and surjections, with precisely \(m!\,{n\brace m}\) morphisms from \(n\) atoms to \(m\) atoms when \(m\le n\).
