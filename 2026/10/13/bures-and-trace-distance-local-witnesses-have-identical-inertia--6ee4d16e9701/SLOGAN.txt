For every unital qubit Bloch dynamics, the infinitesimal Bures/Fisher growth matrix is a positive-definite congruence of the trace-distance damping matrix, so the two local witnesses have exactly the same inertia. This converts the numerical Bures check in Hall--Cresser--Li--Andersson's example into an analytic all-state result and proves a general equivalence of the two local witnesses in the unital qubit regime.
