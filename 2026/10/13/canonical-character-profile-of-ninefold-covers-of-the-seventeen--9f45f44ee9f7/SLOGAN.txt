For the seventeen-cusp quintics carrying two intersecting twelve-cusp divisibility relations, a simultaneous smooth \((\mathbb Z/3)^2\)-cover has \(K^2=45\), \(c_2=87\), \(p_g=10\), \(q=0\), and deck-subgroup fixed-point profile \((15,15,15,6)\). Its complete canonical representation has six additional non-invariant sections, so the ninefold covering map to the quintic is not its canonical morphism.
