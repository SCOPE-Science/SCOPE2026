Every maximal independent set in the generalized Petersen graph \(P(n,2)\) is counted by an explicit tenth-degree logarithmic generating function, for every \(n\ge5\). Exact residue-class formulas count both smallest and largest maximal independent sets; the minimum has \(\lceil3n/5\rceil\) vertices and the maximum has \(\lfloor4n/5\rfloor\).
