Every inclusion-minimal dominating set of the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n\), \(n\ge3\), is either a whole bipartition side, one deleted-matching pair, or a disjoint-index \(2+2\) set. Thus its minimal-dominating-set size profile is \(n x^2+6\binom{n}{4}x^4+2x^n\), refining the previously tabulated total count.
