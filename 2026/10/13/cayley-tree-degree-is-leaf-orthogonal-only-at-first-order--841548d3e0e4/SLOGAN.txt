In a uniform Cayley tree, the degree of any fixed vertex is exactly uncorrelated with the total number of leaves, even though the conditional leaf mean visibly depends on that degree. The cancellation is only first-order: every higher falling-factorial moment of \(D_{n,v}-1\) has positive covariance with the leaf count, tending to \((r-1)/e\) at each fixed order \(r\ge2\).
