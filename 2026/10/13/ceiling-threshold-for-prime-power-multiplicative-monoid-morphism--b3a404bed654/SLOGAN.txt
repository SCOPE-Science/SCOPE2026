For the nonconstant prime-power multiplicative monoid morphisms of arXiv:2610.02393v1, zero preservation forces \(v\ge\lceil f/e\rceil\), not \(v\ge\lfloor f/e\rfloor\). For \(\mathbb Z/9\mathbb Z\to\mathbb Z/27\mathbb Z\), the correction changes the printed parameter count from \(55\) to the exhaustive exact count \(19\).
