For \(L_r(q)=\mathfrak h_3(\mathbb F_q)\oplus\mathbb F_q^r\), the subalgebra commutativity degree has an exact Gaussian-binomial formula for every finite field and every \(r\ge0\). As \(q\to\infty\) with \(r\) fixed, it tends to \(0\) for even \(r\) and \(1/4\) for odd \(r\), so central stabilization produces a persistent parity oscillation.
