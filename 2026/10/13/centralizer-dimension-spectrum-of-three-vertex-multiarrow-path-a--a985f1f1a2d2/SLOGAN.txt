For the full path algebra of the three-vertex quiver with \(r\ge2\) parallel arrows followed by \(s\ge2\) parallel arrows over any finite field, a fourteen-stratum formula gives the exact dimension distribution of every element centralizer. The tensor-product layer of length-two paths changes the commuting probability in ways invisible to radical-square-zero Kronecker formulas; in particular, two algebras of dimension \(26\) over \(\mathbb F_2\) have distinct commuting probabilities.
