On an ordered ten-site quantum ring with antipodal excitation and trapping, homogeneous dephasing can *improve* eventual capture: a complete exact-integer certificate proves an efficiency gain exceeding \(0.01385039\) at \(\mu=1/1000\), \(\kappa=30\), and \(\gamma=1/5\). This refutes the previously stated universal exclusion of environment-assisted transport for antipodal sites, without claiming a global optimum.
