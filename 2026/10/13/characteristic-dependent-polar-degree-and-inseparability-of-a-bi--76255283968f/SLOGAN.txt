For the bitangent conic-pencil arrangement \(x\prod_i(xz+a_i y^2)\), the total polar-map degree is \(2m-1\) in ordinary characteristic, drops to \(m\) if an odd characteristic divides \(m\), and drops to \(m-1\) if it divides \(m+1\); characteristic two collapses the image to a line. In the \(p\mid m\) regime, an exact differential condition on the pencil polynomial decides inseparability, which occurs for admissible squarefree pencils even while their reduced component geometry remains unchanged.
