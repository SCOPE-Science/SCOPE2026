For every \(m\ge2\), the cyclic root-of-unity arrangement on a bipartite four-cycle has an explicit cubic-factor characteristic polynomial with negative discriminant, hence is never free. Its projective complement has complete Betti polynomial \(1+(4m-1)t+(6m^2-3)t^2+(3m^3+2m^2-4m-1)t^3\); the twelve-plane Purdy-counterexample member has Betti numbers \((1,11,51,86)\).
