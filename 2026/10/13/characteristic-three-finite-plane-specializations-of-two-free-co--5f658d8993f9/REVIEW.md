## Value — PASS

These are the largest adjacent free examples in the source's classification, differing by one line and sharing a conic. Their characteristic-three geometry is not just a changed local node count: the conic splits into the two particular missing pieces of a nearly complete finite projective-plane line arrangement. The result gives a natural complete arithmetic boundary, a global combinatorial identification with \(\mathbb P^2(\mathbb F_3)\), all thirteen singular points, and simultaneous freeness with changed logarithmic exponents proved by explicit bases.
