In characteristic \(3\), the conic of Pokora's two nested free arrangements splits so that their reduced supports become all \(13\) lines of \(\mathbb P^2(\mathbb F_3)\) minus one or two lines. Their free logarithmic exponents jump to \((1,3,8)\) and \((1,3,7)\), with exact Saito bases and complete \(13\)-point singularity spectra.
