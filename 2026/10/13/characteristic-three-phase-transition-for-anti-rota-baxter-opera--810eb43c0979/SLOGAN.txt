Anti-Rota–Baxter operators on the dual numbers undergo a sharp characteristic-\(3\) jump: a single affine line outside characteristic \(3\) becomes two affine planes meeting at zero. Over \(\mathbf F_q\) this changes the count from \(q\) to \(2q^2-1\), creates exactly \(q(q-1)\) invertible operators, and yields \(4q-1\) automorphism-conjugacy classes in characteristic \(3\).
