# Self-review
## Correctness — PASS
The explicit \(2r+1\)-dimensional natural-basis multiplication embeds the split local algebra with \(u^2=u\), \(uz_i=z_i\), \(z_i z_j=0\), and image containing every natural-basis square. For an arbitrary ideal embedding, every coordinate square restricts to a decomposable symmetric bilinear form. The multiplication has an \((r+1)\)-dimensional span of coordinate forms vanishing on \(V\times V\), while injectivity on \(V\) forces an additional \(r\) independent restrictions. Thus at least \(2r+1\) natural coordinates are needed. The direct proof includes arbitrary fields of characteristic two; the included finite checks confirm the upper construction without being used as infinite proofs.

## Originality — PASS
The closest complete paper, Costoya–Fernández Ouaridi–Viruel, arXiv:2609.32784v1, has the all-characteristic embedding-invariant bridge but explicitly restricts its squaring/Waring correspondence to characteristic different from two. The closest publicly available numerical result *Exact evolution dimension of split square-zero local algebras* proves the same envelope dimension only in characteristic different from two, with polarization essential in its proof. The present result supplies the omitted characteristic-two case by a nonpolarization bilinear restriction argument and quantifies the unbounded mismatch between the naive square-map rank and the ideal-embedding invariant. Search and statement-comparison do not establish absolute publication uniqueness; an older equivalent characteristic-two tensor decomposition remains a residual risk.

## Value — PASS
Split square-zero unital local algebras are a canonical infinite family. The theorem completes their minimal evolution dimension across characteristic two for every radical dimension, with explicit optimal envelopes, and exhibits the structural reason why a characteristic restriction in the motivating Waring correspondence cannot be dropped. The gap grows linearly with radical dimension; this is not an isolated small-field anomaly. It does not address nonsplit local rings or arbitrary higher nilpotency.

## Closest literature and limitations
Primary full text: arXiv:2609.32784v1, especially §§3–5. Closest numerical result: *Exact evolution dimension of split square-zero local algebras* (2026), whose theorem excludes characteristic two. Adler–Strassen-type bilinear lower bounds are related background and not treated as a novelty proof. Potential earlier equivalent bilinear-tensor normal forms remain a risk. The finite-field verifier checks constructions, not all possible embeddings.
