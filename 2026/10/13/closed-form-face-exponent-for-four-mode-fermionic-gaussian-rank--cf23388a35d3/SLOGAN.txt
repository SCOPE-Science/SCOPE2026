On the natural \(w_3=0\) face of the weighted-Gram exponent for \(|M_t\rangle\), the entropy maximization is exactly solvable: \(B_t\ge\widehat B_t=8(3N_t-2+N_tD_t)/(1+D_t)^4>1\). As \(t\to0\), this explicit certificate obeys \(\log\widehat B_t\sim(16/3)t^4\log^2(1/t)\), quantifying how the guaranteed exponential Gaussian-rank rate collapses near the Gaussian vacuum.
