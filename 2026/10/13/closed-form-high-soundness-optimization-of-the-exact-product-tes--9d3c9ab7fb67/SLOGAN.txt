The full exact product-test curve improves the high-soundness two-Merlin conversion beyond the uniform quadratic constant: for \(1-1/(2\sqrt2)\le\sigma<1\), the Remark 7.3 maximization equals \(1-[(1-\sigma)^2-(1-\sigma)^4]/2\), attained symmetrically. For \(\sigma=1-1/q\) with \(q\ge3\), this doubles the leading rejection constant from \(1/(4q^2)\) to \(1/(2q^2)\).
