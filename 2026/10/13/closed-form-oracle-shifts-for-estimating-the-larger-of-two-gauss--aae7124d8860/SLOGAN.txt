For two Gaussian means, every constant-shift estimator \(X_{(2)}-b\) has an exact quadratic regret around the oracle shift \(b_\Delta=\sigma[\sqrt2\,\phi(\Delta/\sqrt2)-\Delta\Phi(-\Delta/\sqrt2)]\). The source's boundary shift \(\sigma/\sqrt\pi\) beats the usual estimator exactly up to the unique standardized separation \(\Delta_*=0.699454801905019\ldots\), after which the usual estimator is better.
