For the two integral spike-code families \([I_k\mid J_k-I_k]\) and \([I_k\mid J_k-I_k\mid\mathbf1]\) over every residue ring \(\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z\), one binomial-gcd sum determines the complete Hamming-weight distribution in a closed expression. The result refines previously available minimum-distance, full-support and small-table information without claiming those earlier results as new.
