The automorphism orbits of finite subsets of every homogeneous bounded-arity meet-tree are exactly unlabelled, partly marked rooted trees, and their closure-overhead polynomials satisfy a uniform Pólya recurrence. Arity \(k\) first becomes visible on \(k+1\) selected points, in exactly one extra unmarked-meet orbit; all intermediate closure overheads occur for arity at least two.
