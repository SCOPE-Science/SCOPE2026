On every rectangular bilinear zero-sum game, exact Competitive Gradient Descent converges for every \(\eta>0\) to the Euclidean projection of the initialization onto \(\ker(C^\top)\times\ker(C)\). Each positive singular mode rotates by \(\arctan(\eta\sigma)\) and contracts by \((1+\eta^2\sigma^2)^{-1/2}\), so the step changes the transient rate but not the selected Nash equilibrium.\n
