Kudo's genus-five Howe sextic has a completely rigid ADE refinement: the always-present point at infinity is an \(A_3\) tacnode, while an affine root of multiplicity \(m\in\{1,2,3\}\) gives \(A_{2m-1}\). Across all seven strata the baskets are built only from \(A_1,A_3,A_5\) and always have total \(\delta=5\).
