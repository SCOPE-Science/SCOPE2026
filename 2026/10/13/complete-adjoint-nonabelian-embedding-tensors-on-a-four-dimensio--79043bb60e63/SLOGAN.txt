For the central four-dimensional \(3\)-Lie algebra \([e_1,e_2,e_3]=e_4\), adjoint nonabelian embedding tensors are exactly a free-covector thickening of all singular \(3\times3\) blocks at \(t=0\), together with one scalar invertible branch satisfying \(t(\lambda^2+1)=\lambda^3\). The arXiv-v1 diagonal family is valid in characteristic zero only at \(k=0\) and \(k=\tfrac12\); the later journal version already retains only the valid \(k=0\) example.
