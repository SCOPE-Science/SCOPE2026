The entire finite-field alternating-rank distribution of every complete tripartite graph \(K_{a,b,c}\) with \(a,b,c\ge2\) has an exact finite sum in rectangular matrix-rank counts and symplectic pullback counts. For every odd prime power, multiplication of its rank-\(2t\) count by \(q^{a+b+c-2t}\) gives the exact number of degree-\(q^t\) irreducible characters of the corresponding graphical group.
