For odd \(q\) and \(a,b\ge2\), the degree-\(q^i\) irreducible characters of the graphical group of \(K_{a,b}\) are counted by \(q^{a+b-2i}\) times the number of rank-\(i\) \(a\times b\) matrices over \(\mathbf F_q\). This gives a closed Gaussian-product answer to Rossmann's character-enumeration question for every non-tree complete bipartite graph.
