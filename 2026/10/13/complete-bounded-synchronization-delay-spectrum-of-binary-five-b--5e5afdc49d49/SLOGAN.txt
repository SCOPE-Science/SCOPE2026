Among all binary five-bit codes attaining the maximum six codewords with finite block-count synchronization delay, the exact counts at delays \(1,2,3,4\) are \(86,5546,3116,456\); none has larger finite delay. The \(86\) comma-free cases were previously known; the new result classifies every one of the \(9{,}118\) delayed, non-comma-free optimal codebooks into \(2{,}293\) complementation-and-reversal classes.
