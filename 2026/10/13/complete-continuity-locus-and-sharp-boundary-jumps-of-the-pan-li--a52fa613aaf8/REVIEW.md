# Self-review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The source definition was reconstructed from the full text. The proof identifies the nongenuine pure states with a finite union of closed rank-one flattening varieties, proves the genuine set is open dense, and uses continuity of the average one-qubit entropy to classify all continuity points. The exact jump maxima follow from the Schmidt form for a biseparable three-qubit state and the explicit \(|\mathrm{Bell}\rangle\otimes|\mathrm{GHZ}_{N-2}\rangle\) witness for \(N\ge4\). No numerical experiment is promoted to an infinite proof.

## Originality
**PASS.** The defining paper was inspected through Eqs. (9)–(13) and does not state the iff continuity locus or the sharp jump law. published-finding corpus searches used author/source names, continuity and biseparability aliases, the hard-zero mechanism, the sharp jump witnesses, and the final implication statement; no implication-equivalent result was found. The closest own findings address strong monotonicity of different functionals. The 2007 comparison paper is a genuine residual risk because only indexed metadata was accessible.

## Value
**PASS.** This is a structural classification of a published multipartite entanglement quantifier. It shows exactly where its hard zeroing changes a continuous entropy average into a discontinuous functional, and proves that the jump can be the entire normalized range for every \(N\ge4\). That is stronger and more reusable than a single counterexample.

## Closest literature and limitations
The direct source is Pan–Liu–Lu–Draayer, arXiv:quant-ph/0405133. Cao–Liu–Bai (zbMATH:1107.81302) is the closest later comparison located; its metadata was read but full text was unavailable. Recent GME work such as arXiv:2505.05998 explicitly values continuity but concerns a different measure. The present finding is restricted to pure qubits and does not infer failure of LOCC monotonicity merely from discontinuity.
