The cyclic ternary Golay code has exact \(b\)-symbol spectra \(1+44z^7+88z^8+110z^9+220z^{10}+266z^{11}\), \(1+22z^8+44z^9+132z^{10}+530z^{11}\), \(1+22z^9+44z^{10}+662z^{11}\), and \(1+22z^{10}+706z^{11}\) for \(b=2,3,4,5\). These exact distributions strictly refine the known minimum-distance hierarchy for the same cyclic coordinate order.
