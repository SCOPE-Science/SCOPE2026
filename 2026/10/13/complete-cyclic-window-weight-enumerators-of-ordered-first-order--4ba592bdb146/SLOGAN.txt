The complete cyclic \(b\)-symbol weight enumerator of the binary first-order Reed–Muller code is determined for every \(m\ge1\) and every read width \(1\le b\le2^m\), when codeword positions use increasing binary-integer order. In particular, the symbol-pair enumerator is \(1+4\sum_{r=2^{m-1}+1}^{2^m-1}z^r+3z^{2^m}\).
