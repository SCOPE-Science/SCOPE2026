For a standard Segre product \(\mathbb P^{a_1}\times\cdots\times\mathbb P^{a_r}\), projective degree versus Euler characteristic has a complete phase diagram controlled by one coordinate-growth ratio. Equality occurs only for \(\mathbb P^2\times\mathbb P^4\) and \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^2\).
