For every cycle prism \(C_n\square K_2\), \(n\ge3\), the complete dissociation-set cardinality polynomial has a rational logarithmic-derivative generating function. Its maximum sets have size \(n\), with exactly \(L_n+2\) or \(L_n-1\) according as \(3\mid n\) or not, plus two additional sets when \(n\) is even; the same count enumerates minimum three-vertex-path covers.
