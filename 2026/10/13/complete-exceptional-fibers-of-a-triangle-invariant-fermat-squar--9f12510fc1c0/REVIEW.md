# Scientific review: complete exceptional fibers of a triangle-invariant Fermat-square pencil

## Correctness — PASS
The proof classifies every geometric point by whether it lies in the coordinate torus, on exactly one coordinate side, or at a vertex. The torus gradient equation is equivalent to \(u=v=w\) and \(3\lambda+1=0\); the side binary quadratic gives \(4\lambda+1=0\) in odd characteristic, while the vertices lie only on \(\lambda=0\). Exact polynomial factorizations determine component structure and intersection multiplicities, including characteristic two and three. A quadratic Cremona transformation supplies the normalization at \(\lambda=0\). The executable finite-field tests independently check many small fields but are not a proof for all fields.

## Originality — PASS, with limitations
The central prior work is Borges–Korchmáros–Speziali, arXiv:2202.05765v1, §4.7 and Theorem 4.22, which introduces the pencil and its symmetry motivation but does not give the listed complete singular-fiber classification. Its blanket nonsingularity assertion, as printed in the accessible preprint, does not cover the explicit exceptional factorizations. Ng Kwing King–Vallès (DOI 10.13137/2464-8728/34099) treats a different Fermat arrangement pencil. Searches for the equations, special parameters, and equivalent Kummer/conic descriptions did not locate dominating published coverage. Residual bibliographic risk: the 2024 journal version was not read completely and historical equivalent formulations may have escaped indexing. These failed searches do not establish absolute novelty.

## Value — PASS
This is a complete and natural degeneration theorem for a published invariant family: it isolates every singular parameter and yields geometric component counts, normalization genus, local singularity types, and Frobenius descent across all finite-field sizes. The formula-specific classification distinguishes legitimate smooth members from degenerations and clarifies the limits of a cited irreducibility statement. The result is not a routine computation of a preexisting table and does not claim unrelated global moduli conclusions.

## Limits and status
The theorem does not classify the full automorphism groups of smooth fibers or compute their zeta functions. Its proof and computational checks await independent scrutiny.
