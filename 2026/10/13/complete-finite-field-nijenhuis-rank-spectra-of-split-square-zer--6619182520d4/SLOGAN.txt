On every unital split square-zero algebra \(K1\oplus V\), associative Nijenhuis operators are exactly the block maps determined by \((a,u,T)\) with \(Tu=au\). Over every finite field \(\mathbf F_q\), a closed Gaussian-coefficient polynomial counts these operators in each linear rank, and exactly \(2(q-1)|\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbf F_q)|\) are invertible.
