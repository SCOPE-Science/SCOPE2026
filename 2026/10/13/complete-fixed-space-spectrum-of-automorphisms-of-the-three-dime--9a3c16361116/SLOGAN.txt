For every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\), the automorphisms of the three-dimensional Heisenberg Lie algebra have a complete four-fiber fixed-space spectrum with multiplicities \(q^3(q+1)(q-2)^2\), \(q(2q^4-q^3-4q^2-q+1)\), \((q^2-1)(q+1)\), and \(1\). Its Burnside average is exactly \(3\), matching the three element-orbits: zero, nonzero central, and noncentral.
