# Review

## Correctness

PASS. The proof reduces the \(e\)-Galois pairing of Moore rows to Ho's exact correlation identity \(S_0=\lambda^{-1}\), \(S_r=0\) for nonzero residues. Because \(s\) is invertible modulo \(N=2m\), each row pairs with exactly one cyclicly shifted Moore row. The orthogonal complement is therefore the complementary cyclic interval, giving the displayed generalized-Gabidulin dual. Intersecting two length-\(k\) cyclic intervals with \(k\le N/2\) gives the hull formula. The Euclidean and Hermitian boundary cases follow directly.

## Originality

PASS. Islam--Horlemann gives the complete Galois-hull formula for ordinary Gabidulin codes with a trace-self-dual basis; Ho gives the scaled trace-self-dual identity and proves only the Hermitian endpoint for generalized steps. The inspected sources and focused searches did not state the combined all-\(e\), all-\(s\) right/left dual formulas or its circular-distance hull profile. The result is not a restatement of the ordinary formula because the Galois twist must be converted through the inverse generalized step \(s^{-1}\pmod{2m}\).

## Value

PASS. The source's scaled basis removes the parity obstruction to trace-self-dual bases but records only Hermitian self-orthogonality. The new formula supplies the complete family of Galois hulls and explicit right/left Galois duals, simultaneously identifying exactly which twists are LCD or self-orthogonal and showing that changing the generalized step permutes the profile. Hull dimensions are a natural invariant in duality and entanglement-assisted code constructions.

## Closest literature and limitations

The closest exact predecessor is Islam--Horlemann, arXiv:2211.05068, Theorems 2.2 and 3.1, for \(s=1\) and ordinary trace-self-dual bases. The primary 2026 source is Ho, arXiv:2605.20109v1, especially Lemma 4.1 and Theorem 4.2. A residual bibliographic risk is that a less-indexed treatment of skew or generalized Gabidulin duality may contain an equivalent specialization, but no inspected source or focused search produced the complete formula claimed here.
