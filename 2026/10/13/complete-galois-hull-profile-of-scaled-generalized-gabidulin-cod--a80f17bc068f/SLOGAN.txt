For a full-length scaled-basis generalized Gabidulin code \(G_{k,s}(\alpha)\), every \(e\)-Galois hull has dimension \(\min\{k,\delta_{2m}(es^{-1})\}\), while the right and left \(e\)-duals are explicitly shifted generalized Gabidulin codes. Thus the generalized step \(s\) only permutes the complete Galois-hull profile, while the Euclidean and Hermitian endpoints are respectively LCD and self-orthogonal.
