For the two-block simplex extension of Li--Chen--Jin--Qu, every \(r\)-subcode support is determined solely by its containment in two distinguished hyperplanes of the coefficient space. This gives the complete support spectrum and shows that \(d_r=2^m-2^{m-r}\) for \(1\le r\le m-2\), while only the top two generalized Hamming weights feel the appended blocks.
