Cycle graphs have two previously overlooked nonproper irreducible FAT colorings whenever \(4\mid n\): the paired patterns \(0011\cdots\) and \(0110\cdots\). Consequently the exact irreducible count is \(I(C_n)=1+2\mathbf 1_{4\mid n}+\mathbf 1_{6\mid n}\), correcting the earlier cycle classification while preserving the known \(C_6\) case.
