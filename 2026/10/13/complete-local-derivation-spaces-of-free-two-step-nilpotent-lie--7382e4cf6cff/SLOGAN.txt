For every field of characteristic other than \(2\) and every rank \(n\ge4\), the local derivations of the free two-step nilpotent Lie algebra are exactly the independently parametrized triples \((P,Q,A)\) acting by \(Pv+Qv+R(A)z\). Their space exceeds the ordinary derivations by exactly \(n^2\) dimensions; the proof uses an infinitesimal Grassmannian tangent-rigidity lemma.
