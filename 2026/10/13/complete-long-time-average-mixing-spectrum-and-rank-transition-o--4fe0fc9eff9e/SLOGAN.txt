For the unweighted Hamming graph \(H(d,q)\), every eigenvalue of the standard long-time quantum-walk average mixing matrix is an explicit constant Laurent coefficient of \((q-2+z+z^{-1})^j/q^j\), with full multiplicities. Its rank is exactly \(q^d\) when \(q\ge3\), but exactly \(2^{d-1}\) when \(q=2\), giving the sharp binary-to-nonbinary rank transition for all dimensions.
