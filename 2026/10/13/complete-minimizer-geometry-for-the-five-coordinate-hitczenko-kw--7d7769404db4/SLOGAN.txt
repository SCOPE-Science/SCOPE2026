For normalized Rademacher sums with at most five nonzero coefficients, the known sharp tail value \(1/4\) has a complete equality description: after sorting and padding the absolute coefficients, equality is exactly the gap condition \(u\le\tau<v\) determined by \(b_3\), \(b_4\), and \(b_1+b_2\). Every vector outside that minimizer region has tail probability at least \(5/16\).
