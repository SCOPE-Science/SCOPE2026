For every level \(\ell\) and every index \(n\), a single-carry digit formula determines the multinomial-square sum \(S_{\ell}^{(2)}(n)\) modulo eight; at every larger even height the correction disappears. For each \(\ell\equiv3\pmod4\), its four odd residues occur equally often on every initial dyadic block \(0\le n<2^k\) with \(k\ge2\).
