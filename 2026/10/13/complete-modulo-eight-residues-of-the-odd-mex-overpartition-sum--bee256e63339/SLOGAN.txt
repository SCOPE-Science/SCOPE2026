The sum of odd-valued minimal excludants over overpartitions admits an explicit, coefficientwise formula modulo \(8\) using only square roots and two square indicators. For every positive \(n\), it is divisible by \(8\) exactly when \(n\) is a square; the other coefficients are congruent to \(2\) or \(6\), each on a set of natural density \(1/2\).
