Every nonzero-weight relative Rota–Baxter operator on the central four-dimensional \(3\)-Lie algebra \([e_2,e_3,e_4]=e_1\) preserves its derived line, and all operators are exactly the blocks \(\begin{psmallmatrix}a&h\\0&A\end{psmallmatrix}\) with \(\det A=a(\sigma_2(A)+\lambda)\). Over \(\mathbf F_q\) their number is \(q^{12}+q^{10}-q^8-(1+\chi_q(\lambda))q^7\), where \(\chi_q\) is the quadratic character for odd \(q\) and is set to zero for even \(q\).
