Every smallest feedback vertex set of the cycle prism \(C_n\square K_2\) is classified by one or two cuts in a cyclic pattern of deleted rungs; all optimal complements are trees or two-component forests. Their exact number is \((2r+1)2^r\) for \(n=2r+1\) and \((3r^2+2r)2^{r-1}\) for \(n=2r\), with a separate closed formula for the tree complements.
