The characteristic-five genus-two fibration with just four singular fibers has exactly two smooth superspecial fibers, at \(t^2+2=0\), and exactly four further smooth fibers of \(5\)-rank one and \(a\)-number one, at \((t-2)(t+1)(t^2-2t-2)=0\). An explicit pair of elliptic quotients turns its complete \(5\)-rank and \(a\)-number stratification into two factored Hasse invariants.
