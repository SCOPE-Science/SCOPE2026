For lexicographically ordered binary first-order Reed–Muller truth tables of length \(n=2^m\), the cyclic pair enumerator is exactly \(1+4\sum_{j=1}^{n/2-1}z^{n/2+j}+3z^n\). The low-pair-distance codeword graph becomes connected exactly at threshold \(3n/4\), a distance-threshold gain of \(n/4\) over Hamming connectivity.
