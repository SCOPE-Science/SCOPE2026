# Review

## Correctness

PASS. A degree-one realization is exactly a scalar parity-check map with kernel \(C\). Passing to \(V=\mathbb F_2^n/C\) turns the map into an injection. Conversely, every injection sends the nonzero, pairwise-distinct coordinate syndrome classes to nonzero, pairwise-distinct field elements, whose inverses and an arbitrary anchor produce a valid support with exactly kernel \(C\). The constructions are inverse, and the injection count is the standard ordered-frame product. Exhaustive finite-field replay verifies representative cases directly.

## Originality

PASS. The September 2026 source proves existence and minimum extension degree but does not classify or count all realizations. The closest older Goppa enumeration literature counts inequivalent irreducible or extended Goppa codes under group actions, not labeled degree-one support-and-anchor descriptions of one fixed code. Searches for the quotient-space bijection and exact formula found no prior statement.

## Value

PASS. The recent source makes degree-one Goppa representation a general support-synthesis mechanism; the natural structural question is how nonunique that representation is. The theorem gives the complete parameter space and an exact universal count for every extension degree, with direct relevance to representation generation and equivalence analyses.
