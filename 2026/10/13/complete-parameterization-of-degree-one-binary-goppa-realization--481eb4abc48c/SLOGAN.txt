A binary code with redundancy \(s\) and minimum distance at least \(3\) has exactly \(2^M\prod_{j=0}^{s-1}(2^M-2^j)\) anchored ordered degree-one Goppa realizations over \(\mathbb F_{2^M}\). Every realization is exactly an anchor plus a linear injection of the intrinsic syndrome quotient into the extension field.
