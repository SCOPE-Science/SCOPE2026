# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS

For every odd prime power the proof applies finite additive-character orthogonality to the trace pairing, computes all four Sylvester-kernel strata, and evaluates the nonzero-trace singular contribution by rank-one projectors. The projector trace condition is counted line by line, with the scalar cases handled separately. This gives all seven values. Exhaustive enumeration at prime orders \(3\) and \(5\) confirms every individual target fiber; it does not substitute for the general argument.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

The closest primary full texts are Bourgeois, arXiv:1210.0766v1 (fixed-matrix Sylvester map, determinant and invertibility) and Huang, arXiv:2110.15570v1 (homogeneous \(XY=\zeta YX\), including the zero anticommutator). The zero-target count follows from Huang and is expressly not claimed as new. Neither of those texts supplies the *nonzero prescribed-target* fiber decomposition, whose seven-stratum profile uses the rational eigenline count. Relevant research-record searches concerning anticommutator fibers, Jordan products, Sylvester operators, and finite matrix rings found no exact duplicate; absence from a searched index does not establish priority. A presently unidentified inhomogeneous quantum-plane or Jordan-word enumeration could overlap.

## Value — PASS

The first noncommutative full matrix algebra is a natural test case for the Jordan multiplication word map. The exact prescribed-value multiplicities distinguish five nonscalar conjugacy patterns and two scalar patterns, giving a complete distribution that is substantially finer than the previously known single homogeneous count. This is an intrinsic finite-field invariant rather than a parameter slice or textbook normal-form exercise.

## Limitations

Only odd finite characteristic and matrices of size \(2\) are proved. No claim is made about larger matrices or bibliographic exhaustiveness. Arithmetic verification uses prime fields only; the proof covers all odd prime powers.
