For every finite field and matrix size at least two, the full distribution of products of arbitrary length drawn from nonzero rank-one square-zero matrices has a two-eigenvalue closed form distinguished only by target rank and trace vanishing. Conditional on a nonzero product, the law converges to uniformity over nonzero rank-one matrices at an exact geometric total-variation rate, with alternating trace bias and a sharp two-versus-three-factor boundary in dimension two.
