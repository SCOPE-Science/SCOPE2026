For the two-dimensional Lie–Yamaguti algebra \([e_1,e_2]=e_1\), \(\llbracket e_1,e_2,e_2\rrbracket=e_1\), the source's two-parameter Rota–Baxter family is exactly one affine-plane component of the full locus. The complete locus is that plane union the square-zero cone; over \(\mathbf F_q\) it has \(2q^2-q\) points and \(q+2\) automorphism classes.
