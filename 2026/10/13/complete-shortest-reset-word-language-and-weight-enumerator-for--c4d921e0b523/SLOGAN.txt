Every shortest reset word of the \(n\)-state Wielandt automaton is obtained by fixing two explicit disjoint patterns of mandatory input letters and choosing the other \((n-2)(n-3)\) letters freely. This gives exactly \(2^{(n-2)(n-3)}\) shortest words and the complete letter-count polynomial \(z^{n-1}(1+z)^{(n-2)(n-3)}\), rather than only the previously known shortest length.
