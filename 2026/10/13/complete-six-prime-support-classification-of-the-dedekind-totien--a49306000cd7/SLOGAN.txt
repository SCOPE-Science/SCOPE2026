Sándor's inequality \(\varphi(\psi(n))\le n\) has no counterexamples with fewer than six distinct prime factors; at exactly six factors, every exception belongs to precisely eleven prime-support templates with arbitrary positive exponents at \(2\) and \(3\). The largest possible final prime in the \(11\)-branch is \(383\), yielding the sharp ratio \(147456/147455\).
