For the simultaneous prime-value condition \(\sigma(n-1),\sigma(\varphi(n))\in\mathbb P\), the **only composite solution with at most two distinct prime factors is \(10\)**. Prime solutions in that range are precisely Fermat primes \(2^{2^j}+1\) for which \(2^{2^j+1}-1\) is also prime; any other composite solution must involve at least three distinct prime factors.
