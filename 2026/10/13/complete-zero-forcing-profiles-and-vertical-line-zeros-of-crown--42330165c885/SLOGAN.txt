For every crown graph \(H_n=K_{n,n}-M\) with \(n\ge3\), standard zero-forcing sets are exactly the complements of two side-index sets of sizes at most two, excluding identical two-sets; this gives its complete five-term zero-forcing polynomial and shows every inclusion-minimal forcing set is minimum. For every \(n\ge5\), all four nonzero polynomial zeros are simple and lie on \(\operatorname{Re}x=-n/2\).
