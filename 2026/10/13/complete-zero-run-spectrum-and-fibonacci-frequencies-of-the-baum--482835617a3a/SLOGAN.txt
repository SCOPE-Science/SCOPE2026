The maximal nonempty zero runs in the Baum–Sweet automatic word have exactly the lengths \(\{1,2,5,8,17,32,65,128,257,\ldots\}\), with every listed length appearing infinitely often. At dyadic prefix lengths their exact counts are Fibonacci expressions, and the normalized long-run frequencies are \(\varphi^{-1}\) for length \(2\) and \(\varphi^{-j}\) for length \(2^{j-2}+\mathbf{1}_{\{j\,\mathrm{even}\}}\), \(j\ge4\).
