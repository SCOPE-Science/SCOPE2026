For positive diagonal systems started from the origin, exact conjugate gradients preserve componentwise nonnegativity in every dimension whenever \(\kappa\le2\), and this condition-number threshold is sharp. Every two-dimensional system is safe for arbitrary conditioning, whereas for every \(\kappa>2\) a three-dimensional system can cross the orthant at the second iterate; exact-line-search BFGS and the well-defined one-parameter Broyden family inherit the same result because they generate the same quadratic iterates.
