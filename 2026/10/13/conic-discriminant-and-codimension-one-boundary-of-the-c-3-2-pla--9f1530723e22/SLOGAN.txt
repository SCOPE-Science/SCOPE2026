For the Badr--Bars \(C_3^2\)-invariant plane-sextic normal form, smoothness is controlled exactly by \((4-a^2)(4-b^2)(4-c^2)(4-a^2-b^2-c^2+abc)\ne0\), and the coordinate-cube quotient is a conic branched at six inertia-three points. Its generic discriminant boundary has two geometric types: one side tangency produces three \(A_2\) cusps, while conic degeneration produces two smooth diagonal cubics meeting in nine nodes.
