PASS. The construction is valid for every \(k,m\ge1\). Nonnegative coefficient multiplicities make a genuine square-zero graded algebra; the complete off-diagonal degree-two support proves connectedness. The Cartan matrix is exactly \(f_m I_r+p_-J_r\), and a constant rational basis change isolates one unit local factor and \(k\) factors of exact \(t\)-adic valuation \(m\). Inversion gives the stated signed profile. No finite experiment is used to support the universal quantifiers.
