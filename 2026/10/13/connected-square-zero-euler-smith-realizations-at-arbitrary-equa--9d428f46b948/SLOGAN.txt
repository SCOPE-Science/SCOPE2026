For every \(k,m\ge1\), a connected radical-square-zero graded algebra realizes the signed Euler–Smith profile \((\underbrace{-m,\ldots,-m}_{k\text{ times}},0)\). A complete-graph Hilbert matrix reduces the local Smith calculation to one unit eigenvalue and \(k\) copies of a scalar of exact \(t\)-adic order \(m\).
