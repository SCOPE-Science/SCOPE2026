# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The claim is an exact equivalence for all positive integers with power-of-two totient. The proof excludes totient \(1\), uses the prime-power totient formula to obtain a squarefree odd part consisting of distinct Fermat primes, and turns the derivative equation into the integral identity \(a-1+T=2^{2-T}P\) in the even case. Since \(P\) is odd, this forces \(T\le2\), exhausts all allowed prime supports, and verifies each candidate by direct substitution. The odd case is excluded separately. Exact integer tests through \(10^6\) corroborate but are not substituted for the proof.

## Value — PASS
This is a complete, unlimited classification of a naturally distinguished subdomain of an existing arithmetic-function fixed-point problem. Power-of-two totients are exactly the indices of straightedge-and-compass constructible regular polygons. The proof eliminates all hypothetical higher Fermat primes without relying on their unknown distribution, turning a short numerical list into an exact structural assertion. It does not attempt to classify the unrestricted equation.
