Every homomorphism between finite regular \(L\)-algebras is, in Young-diagram coordinates, a tuple of zero maps or positively rescaled coordinate projections; when the target is a box, the exact hom-set count depends only on the source coordinate maxima. For homogeneous boxes, the endomorphism monoid is the opposite of a pointed full transformation monoid, giving closed Stirling-number rank spectra and exactly \(B_{m+1}\) distinct embedded retracts in \(m\) dimensions.
