On a uniformly coupled Dirichlet path, every single-node sensor whose index is coprime to \(n+1\) has exactly the same positive observability-Gramian determinant at every horizon \(H\ge n\); all other sensors have determinant zero. At the shortest full horizon, the common determinant is exactly \(\beta^{n(n-1)}\), independent of diagonal damping and of which coprime node is observed.
