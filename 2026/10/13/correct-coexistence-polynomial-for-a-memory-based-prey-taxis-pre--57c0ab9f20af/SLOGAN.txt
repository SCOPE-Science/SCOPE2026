For the Liu–Tan memory-based prey-taxis predator–prey equations, the correct coexistence reduction is a strictly convex quadratic, not the quadratic printed in Section 3. An exact positive-parameter example has the equilibrium \((u,v)=(1,1/3)\) while the published polynomial has no root in the physically admissible interval; the paper's own numerical coexistence value also matches the corrected, rather than the printed, polynomial.
