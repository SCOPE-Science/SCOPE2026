For the weak-noise stochastic SIRS model at its published baseline, the total linearized covariance is affine in the three Brownian correlations and ranges sharply from 11.4947945907 to 23.3606091279 over every valid correlation matrix, attained at correlation patterns (-1,-1,1) and (1,1,1). The correct correlated-covariance equation was established in earlier work and is not claimed as new here.
