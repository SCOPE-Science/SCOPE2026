For \(G=K_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}\), the injective-edge conflict graph is the join of the graphs \(\overline{L(K_{n_i,n_j})}\), so \(\chi'_{\mathrm{inj}}(G)=\sum_{i<j}\min\{n_i,n_j\}\). In particular \(\chi'_{\mathrm{inj}}(K_{2,2,2})=6\), correcting the published value \(2\) implied by Proposition 2.7 of the 2023 Filomat join paper.
