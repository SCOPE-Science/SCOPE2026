The EDBuE tail is geometric with a \(1/n\) correction, so its MGF is finite exactly for \(t< -\log\theta\) and the model is light-tailed for every admissible \((\alpha,\theta)\). The paper's printed raw-moment series diverges; the correct moments are tail sums \(\sum_{k\ge1}[k^r-(k-1)^r]\Pr(X\ge k)\).
