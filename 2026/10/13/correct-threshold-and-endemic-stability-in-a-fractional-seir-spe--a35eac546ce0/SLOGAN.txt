In the source's \(\psi=\theta=0\) Caputo epidemic specialization, the exact threshold is \(\mathcal R_*=n\Lambda\omega/[\mu(\omega+\mu)(\tau+\mu)]\); the printed denominator drops the \(\omega\tau\) cross term and can reverse the invasion classification. The biologically feasible endemic branch exists for \(\mathcal R_*>1\) and is locally asymptotically stable for every \(0<\alpha\le1\).
