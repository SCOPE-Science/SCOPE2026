For every even \(k\ge2\), the gcd of all length-\(k\) sums of Fibonacci cubes is \(m_k\) when \(6\nmid k\), but \(m_k/2\) when \(6\mid k\), where \(m_k=\gcd(F_{k+1}^{3}-1,F_{k+2}^{3}-1)=\gcd(F_{k+2}-1,3(F_{k+1}-1))\). This proves the corrected all-length classification and reverses the two case labels printed in the motivating cubic-window conjecture.
