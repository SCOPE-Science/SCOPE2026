For the order-\(2\) ferromagnetic Blum--Kapel tree, the translation-invariant symmetry-breaking threshold is the unique positive root of \(4\lambda^4+7\lambda^3-14\lambda^2-33\lambda-12=0\), namely \(\lambda_c=2.102815843291536\ldots\). This corrects the previously reported value \(2.1132163\): exactly one positive translation-invariant boundary law exists up to \(\lambda_c\), and exactly three exist above it.
