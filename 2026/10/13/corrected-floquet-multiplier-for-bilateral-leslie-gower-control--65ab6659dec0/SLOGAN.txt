For a transversal order-2 cycle in the 2026 bilateral Leslie–Gower model, the exact Poincaré multiplier uses the lower post-jump coordinate \(y_{D_4}-\tau_1\), not \(y_{D_4}-\tau_2\). At the published Example 4.3 parameters, the corrected multiplier is about \(0.19233\) rather than \(0.40352\) from the printed substitution, so stability survives but the explicit margin is quantitatively wrong.
