The printed second-delay crossing formulas use the wrong quadratic signs, an incompatible sine coefficient, and a phase \(\arg(U_2+iU_2)\) that drops \(V_2\). Direct modulus expansion restores the correct \(K_2,U_2,V_2\) and \(\arg(U_2+iV_2)\); with the paper's own coefficients, the printed criterion even rejects \(\omega=1.4\) although the source reports \(1.4\in\Omega\).
