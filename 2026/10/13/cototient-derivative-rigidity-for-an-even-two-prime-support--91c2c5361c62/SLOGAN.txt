Every solution of \(D(n-\varphi(n))=n\) with prime support \(\{2,p\}\) for odd prime \(p\) has \(n=4p\), and its existence is characterized exactly by one of two arithmetic-derivative identities on the odd part of \(p+1\). The two branches connect this cototient equation to primary pseudoperfect numbers and a distinct Egyptian-fraction port, without claiming either branch is completely enumerated.
