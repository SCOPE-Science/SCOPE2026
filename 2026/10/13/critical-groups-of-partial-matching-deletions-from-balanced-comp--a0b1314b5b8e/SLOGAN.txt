The critical group of \(K_{n,n}\) after deleting any strictly partial matching of size \(t\) has explicit invariant factors: for \(2\le t<n\), exactly \(2(n-t)-2\) factors equal \(n\), \(t-2\) equal \(n(n-2)\), and one equals \(n(n-2)[n(n-2)+t]\). A separate formula covers \(t=1\), giving a complete arithmetic interpolation between the known no-deletion and perfect-deletion endpoints.
