In the slashed inverse-Rayleigh family, the critical tail-shape window \(q=2+2w/\log(z^2/\theta)\) has an exact two-term survival crossover governed by \(\Phi(w)=(1-e^{-w})/w\), continuously extended at zero. For samples with \(q_n=2+2\kappa/\log n\), maxima scaled by \(\sqrt{\theta n\log n}\) converge to a Fréchet law with exponent coefficient \(\Phi(\kappa)\), with an explicit logarithmic finite-size correction.
