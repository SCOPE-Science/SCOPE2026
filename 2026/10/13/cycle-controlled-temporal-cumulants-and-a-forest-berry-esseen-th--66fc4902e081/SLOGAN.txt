For quasifree-fermion temporal statistics with rationally independent relative one-particle gaps, triangles and four-cycles of the active coherence graph give the complete third- and fourth-cumulant corrections. Forest support is exactly the case of independent active edge phases, yielding an exact independent-cosine law and a finite-size Berry–Esseen bound controlled by \(\max_e r_e/(\sum_e r_e^2)^{1/2}\).
