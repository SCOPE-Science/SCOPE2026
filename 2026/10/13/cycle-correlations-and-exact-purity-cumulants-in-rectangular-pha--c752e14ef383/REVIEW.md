# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The pure state and reduced density matrix are exactly normalized. The proof decomposes purity into squared row overlaps and establishes their first three moments by integer-phase orthogonality. Rooted-forest edge-ratio coordinates give full joint independence; an oriented cycle has exactly \(m(m-1)\) surviving off-diagonal phase assignments. The diagonal edge-cubic and triangle terms exhaust the third centered moment. Exact rational Laurent-polynomial checks reproduce both cumulants for six finite dimension pairs and two cycle lengths; the mathematical proof, not the finite checks, establishes arbitrary dimensions. No infinite-limit claim is used.

## Originality — PASS
The inspected 2014 paper's purity computation is a mean/single-trace spectral-moment result, not the third *centered* moment of the random purity and not an edge-graph independence theorem. The 2011 phase-random-state paper supplies mean entanglement and concentration estimates; it also mentions a standard-deviation bound, so the existence of purity fluctuation controls is prior. The 2018 traffic paper supplies broad combinatorial machinery for square unimodular random matrices, potentially allowing further derivations, but it does not state this cycle identity, forest iff characterization, or rectangular third cumulant. Focused indexed searches for exact formulas, graphs, aliases and implied statements did not retrieve a covering result. General traffic formalism and differently indexed graph-probability results remain genuine prior-art risks: this review does not equate unsuccessful searches with proof of universal novelty.

## Value — PASS
The equal-amplitude phase ensemble arises naturally from diagonal quantum gates and phase-random entanglement, so exact purity fluctuations have a motivated object and statistic. The graph theorem identifies a clear qualitative boundary between pairwise and full independence, while the triangle contribution gives a structural account of nonzero skewness in every nontrivial dimension pair beyond \((2,2)\). The result treats the full rectangular family, not a fabricated or arbitrary finite slice; it neither assumes a Gaussian limit nor repackages an unrelated constant.

## Closest literature and limitations
The 2014 source is prior for ensemble construction and mean purity. Nakata–Turner–Murao is prior for phase-random mean entanglement/concentration. Garza Vargas is prior for traffic-moment graph enumeration of the square ensemble and is the chief possible implicit-coverage risk. This theorem is confined to iid uniform phases and exact moments through order three; it proves neither an asymptotic normal law nor higher mixed-cumulant formulas. Independently adjudicated originality and correctness remain open.
