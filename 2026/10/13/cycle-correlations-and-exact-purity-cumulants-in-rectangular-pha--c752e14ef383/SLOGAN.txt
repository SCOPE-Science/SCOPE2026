For equal-amplitude bipartite states with independent uniform phases, reduced purity has exact finite-dimensional variance \(2(d-1)(m-1)/(d^3m^3)\) and third centered moment \(8(d-1)(m-1)(d+m-4)/(d^5m^5)\). Squared overlaps of distinct rows are jointly independent exactly on forest graphs; every cycle has the same positive centered mixed moment \(m(m-1)\).
