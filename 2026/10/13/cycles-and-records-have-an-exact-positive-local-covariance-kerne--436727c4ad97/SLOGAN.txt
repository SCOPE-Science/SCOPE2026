For a uniform permutation, every nontrivial upper-record indicator is positively correlated with the number of cycles: \(\operatorname{Cov}(C_n,R_{n,j})=(j-1)/(n j\binom{n-1}{j-1})\). Although cycle count and record count are classically equidistributed, their same-permutation correlation is positive and decays as \(1/(n\log n)\).
