PASS: Independently followed the all-word converse, not merely existence of a two-permutation representation. Rotate at a symbol x of a part A to xUxV. Every outside symbol occurs once in U and V, two outside parts maintain interpart order to alternate, whereas symbols within a part reverse order; an interleaving of the same part with an outside part would contradict these requirements, forcing part-contiguous blocks. Extra A symbols occur twice in U or V and must bracket every outside symbol and nest within A, which yields the displayed cyclic normal form. The converse follows pairwise. The word is rotationally primitive unless every pair alternates (excluded noncomplete graph); each orbit has exactly 2r part-boundary cuts yielding normalized forms. Counting r! product n_i! block choices gives both formulas.
