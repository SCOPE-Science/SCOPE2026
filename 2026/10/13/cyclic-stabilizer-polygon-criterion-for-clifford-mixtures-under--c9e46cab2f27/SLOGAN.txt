A tilted-dephasing qubit channel mixed with any preceding unitary is a convex mixture of Clifford unitaries exactly when one complex number lies in the regular point, segment, triangle, or square determined by its Clifford axis stabilizer. For a genuinely non-Clifford phase rotation and any strictly intermediate dephasing probability, every nonvertical noise axis is excluded, while body-diagonal dephasing alone has the sharp \(p=3/4\) boundary.
