For every prime power \(m=\ell^a\ge3\), Robu’s generalized totient attached to the cyclotomic polynomial \(\Phi_m\) equals Euler’s \(\varphi\) exactly at \(n=\ell^b\) with \(b\ge1\). The proof reduces the comparison to exact local root counts and a parity obstruction for every extra prime divisor of \(n\).\n
