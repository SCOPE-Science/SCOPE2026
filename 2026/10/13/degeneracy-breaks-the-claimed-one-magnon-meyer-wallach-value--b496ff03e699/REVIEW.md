PASS. The correction is structural rather than numerical: translation symmetry of a Hamiltonian guarantees a simultaneous momentum eigenbasis but does not make every vector in a degenerate energy eigenspace a momentum eigenvector. The exact interval \([1/2,3/4]\) quantifies how much the published spectral value can vary already in the smallest nontrivial periodic example and supplies the needed qualification for using \(Q\) as a function of energy eigenstates.
