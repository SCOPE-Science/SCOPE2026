In the four-site periodic Heisenberg chain, the degenerate zero-energy one-magnon eigenspace has Meyer–Wallach entanglement ranging exactly from \(1/2\) to \(3/4\), not the single value \(3/4\) asserted for one-excitation eigenstates. The discrepancy comes from mixing degenerate opposite-momentum eigenvectors into standing waves with nonuniform site occupation.
