For every forest \(F\), the complete number \(SO_F(q)\) of proper strong-odd colorings from \(q\) named colors factorizes into local degree factors, so any two forests with the same degree multiset have identical strong-odd coloring polynomials. For a nontrivial tree \(T\), this gives the exact three-color count \(SO_T(3)=6\prod_{v:\,\deg_T(v)\text{ even}}2^{\deg_T(v)-2}\).
