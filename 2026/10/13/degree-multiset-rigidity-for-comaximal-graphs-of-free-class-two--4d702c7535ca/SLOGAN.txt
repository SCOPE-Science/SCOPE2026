For every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\) and every \(n\geq3\), the complete degree multiset of the comaximal graph of \(\mathbb F_q^n\oplus\bigwedge^2\mathbb F_q^n\) has an explicit Gaussian-binomial formula with exactly \(n\) distinct degrees. Within this free class-two nilpotent Lie-algebra family, the degree multiset uniquely determines the rank \(n\) and field size \(q\).
