For a graph state prepared with one shared Gaussian controlled-phase calibration error across all edges, a broad high-degree class has the universal fidelity law \(F_G(c/\sqrt{\sum_v\deg(v)^2})\to(1+c^2/8)^{-1/2}\). The complete graph has a sharper theta-function lattice crossover and loses fidelity at noise scale \(c/n\), whereas the local-Clifford-equivalent star retains asymptotic fidelity \((1+e^{-c^2/8})/2\) under that same physical error amplitude.
