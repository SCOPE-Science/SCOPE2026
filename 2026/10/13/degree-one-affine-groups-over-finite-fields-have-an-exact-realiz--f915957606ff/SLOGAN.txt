For a finite field \(\mathbf F_q\), the affine group \(\operatorname{AGL}_1(\mathbf F_q)\) is the unit group of a finite ring exactly when \(q\) is even or \(q=3\). Every even \(q\) is realized uniformly by a twisted dual-number ring of order \(q^2\), while the Jacobson radical and Artin--Wedderburn force the unique odd exception.
