For every finite field and every standard graded commutative Gorenstein local algebra with Hilbert vector \((1,d,1)\), \(d\ge2\), the ordinary zero-divisor graph's degree multiset determines the ring up to isomorphism. The degree split of non-socle vertices detects isotropic vectors and thereby recovers the finite-field bilinear-form type, including the alternating boundary in characteristic two.
