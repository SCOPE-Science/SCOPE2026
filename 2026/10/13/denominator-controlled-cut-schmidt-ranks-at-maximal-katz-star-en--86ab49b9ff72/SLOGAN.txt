At the first maximal single-site entanglement time of a Katz-weighted star, every bipartition has an exact compressed entanglement spectrum and purity, yet its Schmidt rank exhibits an arithmetic resonance. For \(\beta=p/q\) in lowest terms the rank is \(\min\{b+1,2(a+1),2q\}\), while for irrational \(\beta\) it is \(\min\{b+1,2(a+1)\}\), even though the one-site entanglement distance is maximal in both cases.
