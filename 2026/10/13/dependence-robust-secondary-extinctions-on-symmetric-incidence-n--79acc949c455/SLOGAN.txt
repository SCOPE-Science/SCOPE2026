For symmetric ecological incidence networks, the smallest expected number of secondary extinctions under arbitrary correlated pollinator losses with a common marginal is the convex envelope of a deterministic hyperedge-loss profile. On odd pollination cycles, an unavoidable sharp \(1/2\)-plant expected loss survives at marginal \(q=1/2\), whereas even cycles permit zero; the full dependence-robust curve is explicit for every cycle size.
