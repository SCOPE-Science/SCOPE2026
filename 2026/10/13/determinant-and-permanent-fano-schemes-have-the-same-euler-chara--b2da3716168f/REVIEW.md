# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. A generic one-parameter subgroup of the row-column torus isolates the coordinate points of the ambient Grassmannian. Chan--Ilten Proposition 2.3 proves that such a coordinate point lies in the determinantal Fano scheme if and only if it lies in the permanental Fano scheme, and this occurs exactly when the associated bipartite graph has no matching of size \(r\).

Both Fano schemes therefore have the same finite fixed locus. Algebraic \(\mathbb C^*\)-localization gives
\[
\chi_{\mathrm{top}}=B_{m,n,r}(k+1).
\]
The edge threshold and the \(r=2\) star formula are separately elementary and are replayed in the bundled checker.

## Originality
PASS. The direct archival source develops the same Fano schemes, compression components, dimensions, smoothness, connectedness, and coordinate fixed points, but targeted full-text searches found no topological Euler, Poincaré, Betti-number, or topology formula. Targeted semantic-database and web searches found no equivalent all-parameter equality.

The closest related results concern Euler characteristics of the underlying matrix varieties, not the positive-dimensional Fano schemes of contained linear spaces. The closest external database hit computes a Plücker degree for one compression component and therefore does not imply the present global Euler equality.

## Value
PASS. The theorem shows that determinant and permanent Fano schemes, despite their different scheme geometry, share an exact global topological invariant for every parameter choice. It also gives that invariant a finite combinatorial interpretation as a bounded-matching graph count and produces an explicit closed formula in the first nontrivial rank case.
