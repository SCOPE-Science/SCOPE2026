The Fano schemes of \(k\)-planes on corresponding determinant and permanent varieties have exactly the same ordinary Euler characteristic: it is the number of \(k+1\) edges in \(K_{m,n}\) that contain no matching of size \(r\). For \(r=2\), this becomes the closed star count \(m\binom{n}{k+1}+n\binom{m}{k+1}\).
