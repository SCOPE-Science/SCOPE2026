# Self-review

## Correctness

PASS. The degree-two representations in both groups have the same rotation eigenvalues. Their kernels contain no outside element and have exactly \(\gcd(2n,k)\) rotations, so the codegree is \(2n/\gcd(2n,k)\). Exact-order residues modulo \(2n\) occur in inverse pairs, giving \(\varphi(d)/2\) nonlinear characters of codegree \(d>2\). The dihedral abelianization is always \(C_2\times C_2\); the dicyclic abelianization is \(C_2\times C_2\) for even \(n\) and \(C_4\) for odd \(n\). These facts give the two formulas and the exact parity criterion. The packaged checker verifies all multiplicities through \(n=80\).
