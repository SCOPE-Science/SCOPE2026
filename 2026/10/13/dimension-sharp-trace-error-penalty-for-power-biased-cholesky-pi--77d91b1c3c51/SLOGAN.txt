For one-pivot Cholesky, sampling residual diagonals to any fixed power \(q>1\) can inflate expected trace error relative to ordinary diagonal-proportional sampling by the dimension-sharp order \(\Theta(n^{1-1/q})\), even for rank-two kernels. For squared-diagonal sampling, the exact worst ratio within the two-orthogonal-equal-entry-block family is \(1/2+n/(4\sqrt{n-1})\).
