## originality

PASS

The NPT maximal dimension result and negative-eigenvalue upper bound were established by Cubitt–Montanaro–Winter, Rana, and Johnston; they are not attributed to this work. What is new to our best knowledge is the complete exact sign/inertia classification within the natural diagonal mixture simplex, including necessity/sufficiency of local discrete log-concavity, binomial maximizing weights, and geometric zero-mode boundary. A generic block-diagonal proof is elementary once this specific state family is selected, but neither the general maximal count nor its attainment implies this entire weight-dependent classification.
