## correctness

For projective edge monomial map of K_{a_1,...,a_r}, dehomogenize by a nonzero edge uv joining parts i,j. Ratios x_{u′}=t_{u′}/t_u and y_{v′}=t_{v′}/t_v, plus p_w=t_w/t_u and q_w=t_w/t_v for each outside vertex, generate the chart. Equality of Laurent monomials fixes x/y exponents, each outside-vertex total, and the total number of p factors; allocation exchanges are generated by the 2-by-2 minors p_w q_w′−p_w′ q_w. Thus the complete kernel is the minors and chart A^{a_i+a_j−2}×D_{2,m}. D_{2,m} is smooth iff m≤1; when m≥2 its only singularity is vertex p=q=0, yielding pairwise disjoint coordinate Segre strata. Normality follows by saturated rank-one semigroup. Projective Segre P¹×P^{m−1} degree m yields vertex multiplicity m; class group Pic / O(1,1)=Z and canonical class (2,m) mod diagonal yields Gorenstein/Q-Cartier iff m=2. Codimension m+1 follows dimensions. r=2 has no singular component. I independently checked Hilbert-series numerator 1+(m−1)t for m=2..9.

Determinantal cone and Segre local invariants are classical separately, and Ohsugi–Hibi 2000 studies complete multipartite edge-ring ideals and polytope enumeration.
