The affine coordinate algebra is reconstructed, generator by generator, in every projective coordinate-edge patch. Every kernel relation is a row-allocation swap and hence follows from rank-one minors; the converse is immediate from the Laurent monomial map. This proves an isomorphism with a smooth affine factor times a normal rank-one determinantal cone for every graph in the stated infinite family. The only singular point of each nontrivial determinantal factor is the zero matrix. The Segre strata are supported in disjoint coordinate blocks. Multiplicities and class groups follow from the degree, toric divisors and canonical bidegree of the Segre projectivization. The limited checker is regression evidence, not an infinite proof.
