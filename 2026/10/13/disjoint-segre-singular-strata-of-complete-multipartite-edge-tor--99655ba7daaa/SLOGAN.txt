Every singular stratum of a complete multipartite graph's projective edge toric variety is a disjoint Segre subvariety, with a transverse cone of rank-one \(2\times m\) matrices determined solely by the number \(m\) of vertices outside its two parts. The transverse multiplicity is \(m\), the local class group is \(\mathbb Z\), and its canonical class is \(\mathbb Q\)-Cartier exactly at \(m=2\).
