The complete dissociation polynomial of the \(2n\)-vertex Möbius ladder is a signed ratio of two small transfer determinants, rather than the ordinary cyclic trace. Its maximum dissociation sets (equivalently, minimum three-vertex-path covers) have size \(n\) and number \(L_n+1+2\mathbf1_{2\mid n}-3\mathbf1_{3\mid n}\), an exact Lucas-periodic twist correction for every \(n\ge3\).
