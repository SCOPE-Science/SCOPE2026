For the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n\) with \(n\ge3\), distance \(2\) fills the classical gap in the fall spectrum: \(\operatorname{Fall}_2(\operatorname{Cr}_n)=\{2,3,\ldots,n\}\), and a proper coloring works exactly when it has no singleton color class. At distance at least \(3\), every proper color count from \(2\) through \(2n\) becomes feasible.
