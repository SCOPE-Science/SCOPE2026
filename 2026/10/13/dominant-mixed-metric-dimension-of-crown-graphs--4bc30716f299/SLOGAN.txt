For the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M\), dominant mixed metric dimension equals \(3\) at \(n=3\), equals \(n\) for \(4\le n\le6\), and stabilizes at \(n-1\) for \(n\ge7\). In the stable range, the bases are exactly balanced transversals of all but one deleted matched pair, giving \(n(2^{n-1}-n^2+n-2)\) minimum sets.
