For the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M\), the doubly resolving number has a sharp transition: \(\psi(\operatorname{Cr}_n)=n\) for \(3\le n\le6\) and \(\psi(\operatorname{Cr}_n)=n-1\) for \(n\ge7\). Every basis is classified by how it meets the deleted matched pairs, yielding exact closed formulas for the number of bases.
