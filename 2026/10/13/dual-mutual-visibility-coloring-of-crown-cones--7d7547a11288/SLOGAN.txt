For \(G_n=K_1\vee(K_{n,n}-M_n)\), the dual mutual-visibility chromatic number is infinite exactly at \(n=3\) and equals \(2\) for every other \(n\ge2\). Hub-containing dual sets are exactly the one-per-matched-pair choices whose two shore counts are nonzero and are not \(2\), giving an exact binomial count.
