For every connected complete multipartite graph, dual-server total domination is determined exactly by stars, singleton parts, and whether the smallest part has order at most two, giving only the values \(2\), \(3\), or \(4\) away from stars. Total dual-server domination exists exactly for nonstars of order at least \(4\), and every such graph attains the universal lower bound \(\gamma_{\mathrm{tds}}=4\).
