The EBSH right tail is asymptotically \(C_\theta e^{-x^\alpha}\), so \(\alpha=1\) is a sharp heavy-tail boundary: \(0<\alpha<1\) has no finite positive exponential moment, \(\alpha=1\) is exponential-order, and \(\alpha>1\) is super-exponential. Meanwhile \(h(x)\sim\alpha x^{\alpha-1}\), so \(\theta\) changes the tail constant but not the terminal decay exponent or hazard scale.
