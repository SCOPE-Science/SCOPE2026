For every crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M\) with \(n\ge3\), \[\operatorname{gp}_{\mathrm e}(\operatorname{Cr}_n)=\left\lfloor n^2/2\right\rfloor.\] Crown edges encode arcs on \([n]\), and the edge-general-position condition becomes exactly the exclusion of a transitive directed triangle; a balanced bidirected cut is sharp.
