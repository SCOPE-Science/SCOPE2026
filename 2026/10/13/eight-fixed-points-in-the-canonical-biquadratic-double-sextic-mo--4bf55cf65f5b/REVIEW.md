# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The six smooth fixed points follow directly from the weighted-projective fixed equations and the smoothness of the source's genus-two curve. At the unique branch singularity the even local equation admits an equivariant \(A_{2n+1}\) normal form; the induced involution on \(UV=z^{2n+2}\) reverses the exceptional chain and has exactly two fixed points on its central component. The bundled checker independently replays the finite chain and involution calculations for every allowed \(n\).

## Originality
PASS. The lead source states the canonical equation, the three involutions, the genus-two fixed curve of one antisymplectic factor, and the \(A_{2n+1}\) branch singularity, but not the location of the symplectic fixed points. Van Geemen--Sarti supplies the general eight-fixed-point theorem, which does not imply the six-plus-two localization. Exact-source and alias searches found no covering statement. The main residual risk is an older equivalent computation in different coordinates.

## Value
PASS. This identifies a natural geometric decomposition of the Nikulin fixed set in a canonical projective model: six visible Weierstrass points and two resolution points. It connects the genus-two fixed curve and the ADE singularity to the quotient geometry and is useful for explicit double-cover and quotient calculations.
