In the canonical irreducible biquadratic double-sextic models with \(\rho_L>10\), the symplectic involution's eight fixed points split as six Weierstrass points of \(y^2=c_6(u,v)\) and two points on the central exceptional curve over the unique \(A_{2n+1}\) branch singularity. The exceptional chain is reversed, and its central component carries the only two resolution fixed points.
