The six-dimensional rank-three free class-two nilpotent Lie algebra \(\mathbb F_q^3\oplus\bigwedge^2\mathbb F_q^3\) has exactly eight ideal-orbits under its full automorphism group, with orbit sizes \(1,1,1,Q,Q,Q,Q,qQ\), where \(Q=q^2+q+1\), over **every** finite field. Its total ideal count is \(q^3+5q^2+5q+7\), and the exact orbit decomposition refines aggregate ideal-enumeration results.
