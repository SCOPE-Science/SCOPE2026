For every finite field, every commutative four-dimensional split local algebra of Hilbert type \((1,2,1)\) has one of six explicit automorphism element-orbit-size spectra, determined by the rank and finite-field type of its symmetric multiplication form. The total orbit count is \(3q\), \(4q\), or \(5q\); in characteristic two, a Gorenstein and a non-Gorenstein algebra can have identical full orbit-size spectra despite being nonisomorphic.
