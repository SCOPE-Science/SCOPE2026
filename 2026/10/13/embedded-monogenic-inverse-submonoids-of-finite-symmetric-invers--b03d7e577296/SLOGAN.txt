For each finite symmetric inverse monoid, the number of **distinct embedded** one-generator inverse submonoids has an exact joint distribution by longest directed-chain size and cyclic-component order. Every chain-bearing submonoid has exactly two generators (mutual inverses), while cycle-only submonoids have \(\varphi(k)\) generators; the resulting totals for \(n=1,\ldots,6\) are \(2,6,24,126,838,6841\).
