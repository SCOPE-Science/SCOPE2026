## Correctness

**PASS.** The proposed relation is bitotal by identities. Atomic and element back-and-forth clauses follow from the witnessing isomorphism. Inclusion-to-embedding is mirrored by composing the inclusion with the inverse isomorphism. Embedding-to-inclusion is mirrored by transporting the target structure to a renamed isomorphic copy whose domain literally contains the old source. Isomorphism-closure is exactly what guarantees that renamed target remains in the world class.

## Value

It gives, in one step, exact-cardinality layers, arbitrary cardinal spectra, and other invariant subcollections, which are not obtained merely by Adam-Day's printed \(<\lambda\) restriction.

The new claim weakens the target notion from iso-bisimulation to ordinary bitotal bisimulation while broadening the world class to every isomorphism-closed class. It does not claim a coherent iso-bisimulation for arbitrary subclasses.
