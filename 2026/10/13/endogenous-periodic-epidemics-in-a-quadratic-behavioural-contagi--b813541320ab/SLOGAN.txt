The two-variable SIS model with quadratic behavioural contagion has a rigorous autonomous periodic epidemic regime: at \(\mathcal R_0=640/437\), \(\tau=39/10\), \(\chi=1\), \(\alpha=2\), and \(q_c=1\), its sole endemic equilibrium repels, and every other interior trajectory approaches a nonconstant periodic orbit. This behaviour also persists under sufficiently small reductions in transmission protection, without seasonal forcing.
