# Scientific review: endpoint-degree cycle–adjacency covariance

**Correctness — PASS.** For any pair of specified distinct adjacent-position images, fixing the two functional arrows leaves exactly \(n-2\) open paths and up to two already closed cycles. A uniform bijection of path ends to starts gives the contraction lemma and the precise indicators for one-cycles and two-cycles. Summing their contributions over the two orientations of each allowed graph edge gives the stated identity. Boundaries \(n=2\), empty/complete graphs and both star labelings are included. Exact-rational exhaustive permutation/graph tests verify every labeled graph through five vertices, plus sampled larger cases. Finite tests are not the proof of all sizes.

**Originality — PASS, with specified residual risk.** Xu–Alain–Sankoff give the marginal one-line common-adjacency law for the path graph; Deutsch–Kitaev–Remmel give marginal adjacency equidistributions; Diaconis–Evans–Graham give a set-valued equality in distribution for oriented one-line successions and fixed points. None of these inspected claims gives covariance with the same permutation's disjoint cycles, because marginal equidistribution does not preserve that joint dependence. Mansour–Shattuck's exact joint cycles/successions enumeration concerns directed arrows *inside cycles*, rather than neighboring values in one-line notation. Their joint results thus neither cover nor automatically imply the all-graph formula. Earlier general mixed-moment enumerations might contain an equivalent identity implicitly; this remains an originality risk, not asserted absent from all literature.

**Value — PASS.** Allowed neighboring pairs are the central local statistic in genome adjacency/breakpoint comparisons and graph-constrained orderings, while total cycles controls transposition/Cayley distance. The exact interaction between them collapses from arbitrary graph structure to an unexpectedly small set of graph invariants: total edge count, overlaps with the numerical path, and the two boundary degrees. It settles the sign for every labeled graph. Relabeling an abstract star from center at an endpoint to center at an interior vertex reverses the sign for all \(n\ge3\), establishing substantive structural sensitivity rather than a mere numeric special case.

**Scope and risk.** This is a covariance theorem, not a full joint-law or independence result. Uniformity and fixed numeric labels are essential. The underlying contraction and harmonic-cycle identities are standard; originality is assigned only to their all-graph mixed consequence and sign classification. The 2016 cycle-succession enumeration is the closest strong mathematical comparison, but acts on a non-equivalent statistic. General historical implicit coverage cannot be ruled out solely by searches.
