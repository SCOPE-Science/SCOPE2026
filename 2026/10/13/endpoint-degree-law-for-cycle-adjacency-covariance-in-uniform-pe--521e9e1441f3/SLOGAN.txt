For a uniform random permutation, the covariance between its number of disjoint cycles and the count of neighboring one-line values joined by any fixed graph is exactly \(\bigl(|E(H)\cap E(P_n)|+2|E(H)|/n-\deg_H(1)-\deg_H(n)\bigr)/\bigl(n(n-1)\bigr)\). Its sign is fully determined by this endpoint-degree law: two isomorphic labeled stars can give strictly opposite signs.
