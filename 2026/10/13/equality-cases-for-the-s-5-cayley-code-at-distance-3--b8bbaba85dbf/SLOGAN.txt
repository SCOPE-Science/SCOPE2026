For the ordinary Cayley metric on \(S_5\), the exact maximum at distance \(3\) is \(10\), with exactly \(144\) labeled maxima. After normalization, every maximum is an order-\(5\) subgroup together with one generator coset in its normalizer quotient \(C_4\), and all maxima lie in one orbit of an explicit \(28{,}800\)-element isometry group.
