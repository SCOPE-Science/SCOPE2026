Equality in the sharp bound \(\operatorname{Ivol}(V)\ge p_g(V)-1\) for a smooth projective threefold with \(\kappa(V)=1\) and \(p_g(V)\ge2\) forces the canonical Stein base to be \(\mathbf P^1\) and the canonical image to be the rational normal curve of degree \(p_g(V)-1\). The resolved canonical map is therefore the Iitaka fibration followed by that rational-normal embedding.
