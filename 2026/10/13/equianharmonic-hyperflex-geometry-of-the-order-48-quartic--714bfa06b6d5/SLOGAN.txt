For \(C_{48}:x^4+y^4+xz^3=0\), the four hyperflex points are the equianharmonic divisor \(C_{48}\cap\{y=0\}\), and their four tangent lines are concurrent. The action on this divisor geometrically realizes \(1\to C_4\to\operatorname{Aut}(C_{48})\to A_4\to1\): the quotient is the full tetrahedral symmetry group of the four hyperflexes.
