At every coordinate vertex of the elementary-symmetric quartic hypersurface \(V(e_4)\subset\mathbb P^{n-1}_{\mathbb C}\), for \(n\ge5\), an equivariant, coordinate-support-preserving formal change of variables takes the full singular germ to its cubic tangent cone \(V(e_3)\). All remaining singular points lie on coordinate lines and have transverse ordinary quadratic nodes; this is an all-order local classification, not merely a tangent-cone calculation.
