# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. A diagonal \(\mathbb C^*\)-action separates all degree-\(d\) binary monomials. Unique factorization shows that
\[
x^{d-s}y^s
\]
lies in \(\Delta_\lambda\) exactly when \(s\) is a subset sum of the parts of \(\lambda\). Thus the fixed locus has precisely
\[
|\Sigma(\lambda)|
\]
points. Euler localization for algebraic \(\mathbb C^*\)-actions applies to the possibly singular projective locus and proves the formula.

The criterion for attaining \(d+1\) is independently proved by interval induction on sorted positive parts. The bundled checker replays subset sums and the criterion across all integer partitions in a finite test range. Finite computation is regression evidence only.

## Originality
PASS. Full-text inspection of sources on the same coincident or multiple root loci found equations, normalization, degree, singularities, equivariant Poincaré duals, quotient cohomology, conormal duality, and ED geometry, but no ordinary Euler-characteristic subset-sum formula. In the Fehér--Némethi--Rimányi paper, the word “Euler” occurs in the distinct context of equivariant normal Euler classes.

Targeted literature searches did not locate the complete theorem. Residual risk remains because the torus-localization argument is short and older literature uses several names for the same strata.

## Value
PASS. The result gives an exact topological invariant for every multiplicity partition and identifies the invariant with a natural additive-combinatorial object. The ambient-equality criterion is a complete structural classification and shows that proper multiple-root loci can have the same Euler characteristic as the full coefficient projective space.
