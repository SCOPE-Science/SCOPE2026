For every \(n\ge2\) and \(d\ge3\), the tangential variety of the degree-\(d\) Veronese embedding of \(\mathbb P^{n-1}\) has Euler characteristic \(n^2\). Its generic diagonal fixed locus consists exactly of the monomials \(x_i^d\) and \(x_i^{d-1}x_j\), so the invariant is independent of \(d\) throughout the higher-degree range.
