For a projective matrix Schubert variety, the Euler characteristic is the number of matrix coordinates whose northwest rank bound is positive, equivalently \(\sum_{j=1}^n(n-m_j+1)\) for the prefix minima \(m_j\) of the indexing permutation. It ranges sharply from \(n(n+1)/2\) at the long permutation to \(n^2\), with the maximum occurring exactly when \(w(1)=1\).
