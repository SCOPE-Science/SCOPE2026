PASS. A diagonal \(\mathbb C^*\)-action with binary-separated basis weights gives pairwise distinct weights to all ladder coordinates. Hence the ambient fixed points are exactly the coordinate points.

At any such point the represented skew matrix has rank \(2\). Every Pfaffian of size at least \(4\) therefore vanishes. A size-\(2\) Pfaffian is exactly one coordinate function, so it removes precisely the coordinate points lying in its square subladder. Thus the fixed locus is the set of coordinates outside all \(t_a=1\) blocks.

Euler localization for algebraic \(\mathbb C^*\)-actions applies without smoothness assumptions, and projectivity converts compactly supported Euler characteristic to ordinary topological Euler characteristic. The bundled checker directly evaluates Pfaffians in mixed examples.
