For pairs of nonzero binary forms of degrees \(m\le n\), the locus with gcd degree at least \(k\) has Euler characteristic \((m+1)(n+1)-k(k+1)\). Exact positive gcd-degree strata have the linear compactly supported Euler staircase \(2(k+1)\), while the coprime stratum always has Euler characteristic \(2\).
