For projective \(n\times n\) complex matrices with at most \(r\) distinct eigenvalues, the Euler characteristic is a Stirling-weighted sum of equality-partition strata plus the universal nilpotent contribution \(n(n-1)\). The locally closed stratum with exactly \(r\ge2\) eigenvalues has \(\chi_c={n\brace r}(-1)^{r-2}(r-2)!\).
