For \(k\ge3\), nonnesting \(k\)-canon permutations have a joint descent/major-index polynomial that factors as \(A_n(u,q^k)\prod_{i=1}^{n-1}(1+uq^{ki})\). The bijection adds independent weighted boundary swaps to ordinary permutations and yields every possible major index as a multiple of \(k\) between \(0\) and \(kn(n-1)\).
