# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

With V=C^n and 1<=s<=d<n, the higher Kalman locus is the projectivization of matrices admitting an invariant s-plane in a fixed d-plane L. Choose torus weights w_i=2^i (or any integral powers of two with ordered differences distinct); 2-adic valuations show all n(n-1) offdiagonal conjugation weights are distinct. The fixed points are the projective diagonal subspace P^(n-1), Euler n, and offdiagonal matrix units E_ij. When s<d every E_ij annihilates an s-plane inside L∩ker E_ij (dimension at least d-1). At s=d the only plane is L, and precisely d(n-d) units E_ij with j<=d<i fail to preserve it. For any projective complex algebraic variety, compactly supported Euler characteristic localizes to its algebraic C*-fixed locus, and equals ordinary topological Euler characteristic. Therefore chi(K_s)=n^2 for s<d and chi(K_d)=n^2-d(n-d); subtraction yields adjacent compactly-supported layers chi_c=0 for 1<=s<=d-2 and d(n-d) for the last layer. The proof does not use smoothness, nor treat Betti numbers. Independently checked weight uniqueness and fixed-locus counts for all 2<=n<=16, d<n, 1<=s<=d; that bounded test is not the all-n proof.

## originality

PASS

Ottaviani–Sturmfels define exactly the same higher Kalman filtration K_{s,d,n}, compute codimensions, equations, singular strata and degrees, but their inspected Sections 4–5 do not give Euler characteristics of the projective varieties. Sam's full arXiv paper similarly studies normalization, syzygies and the singularity chain but not the displayed Euler-characteristic formulas. A general C*-fixed-locus localization theorem is classical and needed as a proof tool, but its statement alone, without identifying these particular fixed loci and their membership, does not assert the n² rigidity or filtration differences. Searches produced no equivalent published same-object formula. This is best-of-knowledge novelty, not a guarantee of nonexistence of folklore observations.

## value

PASS

The higher Kalman loci are a natural nested family of determinantal/singularity varieties. A uniform Euler characteristic across all proper nonlinear filtration levels despite varying codimension and singularity, together with a single nonzero open-layer Euler jump, is a motivated topological invariant and a diagnostic constraint on later cohomology calculations. The argument is short but not a mere reformulation of the known degree formulas.
