# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. Generic diagonal conjugation preserving \(L\) fixes the diagonal projective space and the off-diagonal matrix-unit points. For \(s<d\), every matrix unit admits an invariant \(s\)-plane inside \(L\); for \(s=d\), exactly the \(d(n-d)\) matrix units mapping a basis direction of \(L\) outside \(L\) fail. Algebraic \(\mathbb C^*\)-localization therefore gives
\[
\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(\mathcal K_{s,d,n})=n^2
\]
for \(s<d\) and
\[
\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(\mathcal K_{d,d,n})=n^2-d(n-d).
\]
Additivity gives the layer formulas.

The packaged checker independently enumerates all torus-fixed coordinate cases on a broad finite grid and verifies generic weight separation. Finite checks are regression evidence only.

## Originality
PASS. The archival sources define exactly these higher Kalman varieties and develop their codimensions, equations, nested singular loci, desingularizations, degrees, normalizations, and syzygies. Full-text searches found no Euler- or Poincaré-type formula. Targeted semantic and web searches using the standard object names, invariant-subspace formulation, projectivization, topology, and torus fixed points found no covering statement.

Older homotopy work mentioned in the original paper concerns spaces of observable pairs with varying observation data, not the fixed-\(L\) projective Kalman filtration.

## Value
PASS. The theorem gives a closed global invariant for every level of a classical iterated singularity filtration. The complete constancy \(n^2\) across all nonlinear levels, followed by the exact terminal drop \(d(n-d)\), yields a sharp cancellation law for the locally closed singularity layers that is not visible from the published degree and codimension formulas.
