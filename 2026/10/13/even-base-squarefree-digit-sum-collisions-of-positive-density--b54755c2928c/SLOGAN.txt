For a positive-density set of even bases \(b\ge12\), the two distinct squarefree integers \((2b-1)(b^2-2)\) and \((2b-3)(b^2-2)\) have identical products with their base-\(b\) digit sums. The bases certified by this quadratic-factor construction have an exact natural density given by a positive Euler product (approximately \(0.2696\)), extending a published squarefree-noninjectivity problem into the even-base regime.
