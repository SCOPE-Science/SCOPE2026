# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
For every core vertex absent from a purported paired-dominating set, its private leaf must be selected to dominate itself but would be isolated inside the selected subgraph. Thus every core vertex is forced. With selected private leaves indexed by \(L\), the induced selected graph has a perfect matching exactly when the core graph induced by \(V(G)\setminus L\) has one. This gives the entire set-counting polynomial without overcounting. The unsigned matching polynomial instead counts each induced perfect matching, producing a coefficientwise nonnegative difference. Two distinct perfect matchings yield an alternating even cycle, and every even cycle supplies two matchings, proving both implications of the equality criterion. The forest determinant identity follows by a direct Leibniz expansion: only disjoint edge transpositions survive, each with positive sign. The real-symmetric adjacency matrix gives imaginary roots. The bundled checker exhausts all simple core graphs of order at most five (1,099 graphs), plus four instances at orders six or seven; `VERIFY_OK` confirms the formulas, but finite enumeration is not the proof for arbitrary order.

## Originality — PASS, with stated residual risk
A full reading of the relevant corona theorem in Dettlaff–Raczek–Yero (2016, Lemma 3.2) finds the scalar minimum \(2n-2\nu(G)\), which is acknowledged as known, but not the full coefficientwise comparison, exact even-cycle criterion, or spectral forest identity. Xu (2013, Section 8.2) surveys paired bondage and the paired-domination definition, not the present polynomial. The accessible conference abstract of Puttaswamy–Alwardi–Nayaka (2016) introduces the polynomial and reports general examples; its full body was not inspectable and could conceal overlap. Matching-polynomial reconstruction work studies a different polynomial's recoverability, without by itself supplying the bijection or its failure criterion. Published related studies of paired domination on complete multipartite, chain, and algebraically defined graphs involve non-whiskered objects and do not imply the asserted all-graph equivalence. No published inspected result was found implying the entire theorem; incomplete access remains an explicit risk, not a proof of universal novelty.

## Value — PASS
The theorem isolates an intrinsic, non-arbitrary obstruction: even cycles are *exactly* when counting matchings differs from counting paired-dominating sets under full whiskering. It converts the entire paired-domination polynomial, not just a minimum, into a classical matching invariant for all even-cycle-free graphs; for forests this further identifies an exact adjacency-characteristic polynomial and its full zero geometry. Thus vertex-set domination enumeration detects the base forest's spectrum and counts every maximum matching through minimum paired dominating sets. These structural and spectral consequences are not just a new name for the known scalar corona formula.
