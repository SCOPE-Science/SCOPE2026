Denominator arguments force the odd-prime exponent congruence, \(\nu_2(p-1)\le2\), and squarefreeness of the odd part of \(p-1\). All cases with at most two odd prime factors are then solved by an elementary rational-integrality classification. Every implication is reversible, and the proof identifies the unrestricted domain.
