For even integers \(2^a p^b\) whose odd part of \(p-1\) has at most two prime factors, the equation \(D(\varphi(2^a p^b))=2^a p^b\) has exactly four explicitly classified cases. Two explicit parameter families arise from primes \(p\ge7\) with \((p-1)/2\) prime, and the only two-odd-factor exception is \(244\).
