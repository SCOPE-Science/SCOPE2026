# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The theorem quantifies over each integer \(r\ge3\). A \(2\)-adic valuation proves unique positive differences in the designated Golomb-ruler set; disjoint modular difference ranges prove injectivity of the cross-sum map. Consequently each allowed edge label occurs once and the graph is simple, unweighted, connected, locally finite, and regular. The exact Laurent identity factors \(H_r(z)+rI\) as a rank-one positive-semidefinite matrix for every unit-modulus \(z\). The explicit Laurent kernel vector gives a finite-support eigenfunction in real space. Nonconstancy of the other band proves the flat band is unique. The stand-alone exact verifier checks the construction for \(2\le r\le8\); the unrestricted proof is analytic and combinatorial, not numerical.

## Originality — PASS
The closest primary source, Sabri and Youssef (2023), Section 5.2, explicitly asks whether two-orbit flat-band energies beyond \(0,-1,-2\) exist. Its general flat-band generator enlarges the fundamental-cell size rather than keeping two sites. This finding supplies, for the first time found in checked literature, the complete negative-integer existence family \(-3,-4,\ldots\) with only two sites per cell and zero-one hopping coefficients. Faust and Kachkovskiy (2025) prove generic absence upon changing onsite potentials, which does not exclude a special zero-potential construction. A weakly indexed equivalent family remains possible, and originality is a literature comparison rather than a proof of global uniqueness.

## Value — PASS
This answers an explicit structural existence question for the smallest nontrivial periodic motif and shows that the possible negative energies are unbounded in magnitude despite the two-site restriction. The additive-combinatorial construction gives exact finite-range hoppings, a compact localized state, and a spectral-bottom classification for every integer parameter. It does not classify positive flat-band energies or impose uniform degree/range restrictions.
