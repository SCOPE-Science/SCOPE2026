# Review

## Correctness

PASS. Theorem 1.2 of the primary source gives a special class \(\mathcal P\subseteq\mathrm{GL}_1\) such that every nonempty slice \(\mathcal P\cap[\sigma]\) has the Join Property. Proposition A.2 applies to an arbitrary class with the Join Property, so it applies separately to each such slice and yields countably infinitely many generalized-low Turing degrees with prescribed jump \(A\geq_T0'\). Because the entire slice lies in \(\mathrm{GL}_1\), the generalized-low fibre is exactly the ordinary jump fibre there. Cylinders form a basis, giving density, and \(B'\equiv_TA\) implies \(B\leq_TA\), giving countability of the fibre as a set of reals.

## Originality

PASS. The source states local Join Property and countably infinite jump-inversion fibres as separate results. Its concluding discussion asks for further structure of the fibres but does not formulate the basic-open saturation consequence. Candidate-specific published-result and literature searches for dense jump-inversion fibres, local Join Property fibres, and countably degree-rich basic slices did not locate an equivalent statement. The earlier Jananthan--Simpson work establishes class-restricted join and pseudojump phenomena but does not provide this local density conclusion.

## Value

PASS. The source itself identifies the structure of jump-inversion fibres as a natural next question. The finding supplies a first topological structural property: every prescribed jump degree above \(0'\) is represented not merely globally but degree-infinitely often in every nonempty basic neighborhood of one fixed special class. This separates local abundance from the more delicate unresolved order structure and shows that the new local Join Property has a simultaneous fibre-saturation consequence.

## Closest literature and limitations

Cintioli (2026) contains both ingredients: Theorem 1.2 gives the local Join Property and Proposition A.2 gives countably infinite fibres for any Join-Property class. Jananthan--Simpson (2021) is the principal earlier class-restricted inversion reference.

The result is a corollary rather than a new priority construction, and no effective uniform choice of local fibre representatives is claimed.
