# Verification

The primary source gives two independent ingredients.

Theorem 1.2 provides a special class
\[
\mathcal P\subseteq\mathrm{GL}_1
\]
such that every nonempty basic slice
\[
\mathcal P\cap[\sigma]
\]
has the Join Property.

Proposition A.2 says that if a class \(\mathcal R\) has the Join Property, then for every
\[
A\geq_T0'
\]
its generalized-low jump-inversion degree fibre
\[
\mathcal F_{\mathcal R}(A)
\]
is countably infinite.

Substituting
\[
\mathcal R=\mathcal P\cap[\sigma]
\]
is valid for every nonempty slice. Since every member of the slice belongs to \(\mathrm{GL}_1\),
\[
B'\equiv_TB\oplus0'
\]
holds automatically, so the fibre condition becomes simply
\[
B'\equiv_TA.
\]

Thus every nonempty relatively basic open part of \(\mathcal P\) contains countably infinitely many distinct Turing degrees from the prescribed jump fibre.

Because cylinders form a basis, this proves density. Because
\[
B'\equiv_TA
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
B\leq_TA,
\]
the whole fibre contains at most countably many reals. Proposition A.2 supplies infinitely many degrees, so the fibre is countably infinite.

## Limits

The verification gives no uniform selector for local witnesses and no classification of the order among their Turing degrees.

The conclusion is specific to the special local-Join class supplied by the source.
