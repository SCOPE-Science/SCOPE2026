For \(1\le m\le n\), the ordinary 2-domination number of the Hamming rectangle \(K_m\mathbin{\Box}K_n\) is exactly \(\min\{n,2m\}\). Minimum sets switch sharply at \(n=2m\): below the threshold they are surjective column transversals, above it they are exactly the sets with two chosen cells per row, yielding closed minimizer counts.
