For complete multipartite graphs, ordinary \(2\)-domination stability is governed entirely by the counts of parts of sizes \(1\) and \(2\), except for one smallest-large-part threshold. The resulting exact formula also shows that complete bipartite stability is not uniformly \(2\): for example \(\operatorname{st}_{\gamma_2}(K_{2,3})=1\) and \(\operatorname{st}_{\gamma_2}(K_{2,2})=3\).
