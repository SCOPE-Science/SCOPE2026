# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. Direct shore counting reduces every proper \(2\)-fair dominating set of \(K_{s,t}\) with \(s,t>2\) to occupancy \((2,2)\). The complementary-type analysis is exhaustive, the four-unit excess lower bound is uniform for \(s\ge4\), and explicit partitions attain every stated value. Small-order exhaustive enumeration independently matches the theorem.

## Originality
PASS. The directly inspected initiating source, arXiv:2509.11358v1, gives upper bounds for the nontrivial complete-bipartite ranges and exact values only on several boundary cases. Targeted searches for the invariant, notation, and the \(K_{3,t}\) and \(K_{4,t}\) subfamilies found no exact formula. The ordinary \(k\)-coalition complete-bipartite theorem and prior \(1\)-fair coalition results use different predicates and do not imply the claim.

## Value
PASS. The result closes a natural exact-value gap left in the paper introducing the parameter. It shows the published \(K_{4,t}\) upper bound is not tight, identifies the small-shore phase transition, and gives a reusable occupancy-type method for exact-fair coalition partitions.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is A. Jafari and S. Alikhani, arXiv:2509.11358v1, Theorem 3.3. The result here is restricted to \(k=2\) and does not classify every extremal partition or solve arbitrary \(k\). A poorly indexed equivalent formulation remains a bibliographic residual risk.
