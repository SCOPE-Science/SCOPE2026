For \(1\le s\le t\), the complete bipartite \(2\)-fair coalition number is \(4\) for \(K_{2,2}\), is \(3\) for \(K_{3,3}\) and \(K_{3,4}\), is \(s+t-4\) for \(s\ge4\), and is \(2\) otherwise. The proof reduces every nontrivial coalition union to shore occupancy \((2,2)\), turning the extremum into a complementary block-type problem.
