For every finite connected distance-regular graph of diameter \(D\), ordered-pair 2-WL reveals graph distances by exact doubling: after \(r\) strict rounds it has separated distances through \(2^r\). Hence its strict-refinement depth is \(0\) for \(D\le2\) and \(\lceil\log_2(D-1)\rceil\) for \(D\ge3\).
