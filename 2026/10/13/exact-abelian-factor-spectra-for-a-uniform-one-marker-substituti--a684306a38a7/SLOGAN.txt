For the Parikh-constant substitutions \(0\mapsto0^{b-1}1\) and \(1\mapsto10^{b-1}\), the full spectrum of one-counts in every factor length is an explicit interval, and Abelian complexity is periodic from length \(b\) onward. At \(b\ge4\), the value \(4\) occurs exactly for lengths \(n>b\) with \(2\le n\bmod b\le b-2\), giving the sharp balance constant \(3\).
