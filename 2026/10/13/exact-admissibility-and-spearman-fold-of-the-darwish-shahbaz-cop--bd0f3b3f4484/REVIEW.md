# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof explicitly establishes all copula axioms by groundedness, uniform margins and the sign of the rational mixed density. For negative parameters an elementary decomposition makes the density strictly positive; for the nonnegative range a bilinear lower bound has four nonnegative corner values. Parameters above \(1/2\) are excluded by an interior density-sign violation or a genuine pole. For Spearman correlation the exact integral representation, differentiation and strict logarithmic monotonicity of \(H(t)\) prove a unique extremum; a strict covariance inequality locates it beyond \(t=2\). Uniform convergence and the two-preimage assertion follow from a pointwise bound and strict monotonicity on either side. Symbolic identities and three quadrature resolutions reproduce illustrative numbers. Numerical cross-checks are not treated as proofs.

**Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk.** The closest source, Darwish and Shahbaz (2026), introduces this rational family on \([-2,1/2]\). Its Section 4.2 gives restricted-range Spearman values and its Appendix A explicitly chooses the negative bound to keep dependence/concordance values unique. Neither provides the unrestricted validity interval, proves its optimality, or locates the unique global Spearman fold at a parameter below \(-2\). The 2024 Darwish–Shahbaz power-polynomial model and the 2022 piecewise negative-dependence copula have different defining families, so their correlation results do not imply this family-specific theorem. Targeted published and mathematical-record searches did not locate a covering result. Unindexed equivalent treatments may remain; search absence is not a priority certificate.

**Value — PASS.** Exact admissible parameter domains are needed before fitting or calibrating a copula family. The new analysis distinguishes copula feasibility from the source's preference for a one-to-one dependence calibration, identifies the precise loss of uniqueness, and shows that extreme negative parameters paradoxically return the copula to independence. The gain in maximal negative Spearman magnitude is small, and that numerical increase is not offered as the sole motivation.
