The Darwish–Shahbaz rational formula is a copula for every \(\alpha\le1/2\), not only on its originally selected parameter interval, and its unrestricted Spearman correlation has a unique minimum at \(\alpha\approx-2.33900178182\). Beyond that point the copulas approach independence uniformly as \(\alpha\to-\infty\), and each strictly intermediate negative rank correlation is attained by two different parameters.
