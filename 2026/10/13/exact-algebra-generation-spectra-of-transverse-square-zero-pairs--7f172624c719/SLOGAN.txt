Every transverse pair of maximal-rank square-zero matrices in \(M_{2m}(\mathbb F_q)\) generates an algebra isomorphic to \(M_2(\mathbb F_q[t]/(\mu_C))\) for one invertible similarity class \(C\), and all dimensions \(4,8,\ldots,4m\) have exact rational-canonical counting formulas. The largest generated dimension, \(4m\), occurs precisely when \(C\) is cyclic; its exact frequency is the classical cyclic-matrix count multiplied by an explicit Grassmannian factor.
