# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The defining feasibility problem is reduced to the intersection of a strictly increasing function \(A(\varepsilon)\) and a strictly decreasing function \(B(\varepsilon)\) on the full interval where positive feasibility is possible. The crossing parameterization is algebraically reversible. The derivative of \(\Gamma_d\) has a positive denominator and a quadratic numerator whose minimum on the relevant interval is positive for every integer \(d\ge2\), so the boundary is single-valued. The fixed-degree asymptotic follows from the endpoint expansion \(x\to1/(2d)\). The \(d=2\) elimination reproduces the source's quartic.

## Originality
PASS. The primary source defines the same \(G_d\), explicitly solves its boundary only for \(d=2\), and labels the \(d=3\) upper boundary in its figure as numerically computed. Its large-degree theorem addresses a different asymptotic existence question. The inspected 2024 follow-up develops a different strong-coupling criterion for finite localization sets and does not state the all-degree parametric boundary here. Targeted searches for the exact source, aliases of the good set, the two defining inequalities, and the parameterization found no statement covering the claim. A residual risk remains that the elementary elimination appears in weakly indexed notes.

## Value
PASS. The set \(G_d\) is not an arbitrary auxiliary slice: the primary source uses it as a model-independent criterion for localized Gibbs measures and again for large-period gradient Gibbs measures. A complete fixed-degree boundary makes membership analytic in every degree, replaces numerical boundary tracing at \(d=3\), recovers the known binary-tree case, and exposes the general fixed-degree tail scale. This is a natural exact classification of a criterion that directly controls the source's existence theorems.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is Henning--Külske, arXiv:2002.09363 / DOI:10.1214/20-AAP1647. The later Abbondandolo--Henning--Külske--Majer paper, arXiv:2302.05398 / DOI:10.1007/s10955-024-03278-9, treats a broader localization-set construction through a different criterion. The present claim does not upgrade a sufficient condition to a necessary phase boundary.
