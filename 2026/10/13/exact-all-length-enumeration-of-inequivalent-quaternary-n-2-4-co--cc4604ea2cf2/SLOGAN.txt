For monomial-equivalence classes of quaternary \([n,2]_4\) codes, the five points of \(\operatorname{PG}(1,4)\) reduce the Hermitian hull to the parity pattern of five projective multiplicities. The resulting \(A_5\) cycle index gives exact all-length generating functions and proves \(I_{n,0}>I_{n,1}>I_{n,2}\) for every \(n\ge4\).\n
