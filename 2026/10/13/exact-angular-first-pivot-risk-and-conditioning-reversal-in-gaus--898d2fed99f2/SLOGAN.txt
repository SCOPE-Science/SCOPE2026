In Gaussian-sketched two-column QR, the exact probability that the first pivot mistakenly selects the shorter column is \(I_z(d/2,d/2)\), where \(z=[1-(\kappa^2-1)/\sqrt{(\kappa^2+1)^2-4\kappa^2\rho^2}]/2\). Remarkably, at fixed norm ratio and sketch size this risk decreases as the columns become more correlated, even though their spectral condition number increases.
