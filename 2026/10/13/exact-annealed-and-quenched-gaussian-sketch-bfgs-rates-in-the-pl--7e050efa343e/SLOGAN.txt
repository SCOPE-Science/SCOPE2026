For planar fixed-Hessian randomized inverse-BFGS with independent Gaussian rank-one sketches, the exact typical squared-error contraction is \(\bigl((1+r^2)/2\bigr)^4\), while the exact mean-square contraction is the Perron root of an explicit \(3\times3\) matrix. In the isotropic case these are \(1/16\) and \(3/8\), respectively: Gaussian sketches converge much faster on almost every path than in mean square.
