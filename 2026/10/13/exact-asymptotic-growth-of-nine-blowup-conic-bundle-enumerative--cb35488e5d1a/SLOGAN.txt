For the nine-point blow-up real conic-bundle invariants, the wall-crossing-invariant signed count in bidegree \((2,2n)\) grows like \(e^{2\pi\sqrt{n+1}}/[8\pi(n+1)^{5/4}]\), while its Gromov–Witten counterpart grows like \(e^{2\pi\sqrt{n+1}}/[2(n+1)^{7/4}]\). The unique dominant cusp and an exact Jacobi-theta cancellation at the apparent competing cusp force both leading constants and the square-root ratio.
