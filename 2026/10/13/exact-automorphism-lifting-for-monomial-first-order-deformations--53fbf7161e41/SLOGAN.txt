For the square-zero monomial deformation \(x^n=\varepsilon x^r\) of a truncated polynomial algebra over any finite field, liftable automorphisms are exactly those satisfying an explicit power congruence; their number and the size of every lift fiber have closed formulas. The image has sharp Frobenius-dependent jumps governed by the characteristic's divisibility of \(n\) and \(n-r\).
