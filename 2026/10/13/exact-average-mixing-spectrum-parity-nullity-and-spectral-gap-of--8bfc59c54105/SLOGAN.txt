For the unweighted folded hypercube of dimension \(d\ge3\), the exact continuous-time Cesàro quantum-mixing spectrum is given by central binomial coefficients indexed by even Hamming weights, with a single zero mode exactly when \(d\equiv2\pmod4\). Its smallest positive eigenvalue is the sharp value \(\binom{2k}{k}/4^k\), where \(k=\lfloor(d-1)/2\rfloor\), so all odd-dimensional folded cubes have full-rank average mixing.
