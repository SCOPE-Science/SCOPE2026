On a strongly convex quadratic, multiple greedy PSB is exactly a batching mechanism for a predetermined finite Hessian-identification process: if \(s\) basis directions have positive initial deficits, exactly \(s\) nontrivial PSB updates recover the Hessian, while batch size \(t\) reduces the required completed outer iterations to \(\lceil s/(t+1)\rceil\). Thus batching reduces outer solves but not the total nontrivial PSB update count.
