For a product of \(K\) Bell pairs, the exact mean purity over all \(m\)-qubit subsystems is \(\binom{2K}{m}^{-1}[x^m](1+x+x^2)^K\). At the balanced scale this is a central-trinomial ratio asymptotic to \((\sqrt3/2)(3/4)^K\), so Scott's normalized balanced quantity tends to \(1\) even though the state has entanglement depth exactly \(2\).
