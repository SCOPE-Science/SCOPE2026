For Hamming boxes whose coordinate alphabet sizes include both an even and an odd integer, the binary adjacency nullity is exactly \((Q+H)(P+(-1)^dR)/4\), with \(Q,H,P,R\) the explicit parity-sector products. Every full orientation of the box therefore requires exactly \([PQ-(Q+H)(P+(-1)^dR)/4]/2\) ancillary qubits in the mixed-graph stabilizer completion.
