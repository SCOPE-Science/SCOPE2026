The four-parameter FEPW map is identifiable only when both scale parameters are strictly positive. At \(\tau=0\) the shape \(\eta\) disappears, at \(\kappa=0\) the shape \(\varphi\) disappears, and \(\tau=\kappa=0\) yields \(R(x)\equiv1\), so the source-stated closed scale domain must be stratified into lower-dimensional boundary models.
