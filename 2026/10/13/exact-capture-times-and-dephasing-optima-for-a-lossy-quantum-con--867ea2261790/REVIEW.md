# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
For precisely the stated homogeneous real-coupling model, positivity and the loss identity exclude loss-free invariant density operators when the leaf dephasing rate is positive. The permutation-reduced Lindblad equations and their time integrals give the exact unconditional capture-time formula. Direct differentiation proves the unique dephasing optimum; separate elementary minimizations establish the joint resonant optimum. A separate full complex-Liouvillian computation verifies 40 localized-leaf cases to maximum absolute error \(7.11\times10^{-14}\) and eight optimization configurations. It is corroboration only; the proof covers arbitrary allowed system sizes and parameters.

## Originality — PASS, with priority risk
Alterman, Berman and Strauch (arXiv:2309.00164) analytically solve transfer and optimal dephasing for all-to-all **equal hopping** with **dephasing also at the trapping site**. Their optimizer mechanism and spectral detuning are credited and cannot be described as new; their printed master equation is not the same as selective leaf-only noise with independently adjustable hub–leaf and leaf–leaf hoppings. Pepe, Pouthier and Yalouz (arXiv:2308.15857) study a different extended-star model and compute a median absorption time with an approximate rate mapping, not this exact mean for all positive noise strengths. Novo, Mohseni and Omar (arXiv:1312.6989) study numerical dephasing/disorder efficiency on binary trees and hypercubes. An inaccessible paper by Pouthier (DOI 10.1007/s11128-014-0891-y) studies dephasing on stars and remains an explicit priority uncertainty, as does any unlocated broader theorem. No independent priority audit has been performed.

## Value — PASS
This is an exact all-size, all-positive-noise mean-time classification for a natural graph family joining a star to a weighted clique. It quantifies three distinct mechanisms—noise-released dark sectors, strong-noise Zeno slowing, and hub/bright-mode detuning—in one formula. The unique minimum and the quadratic hopping/detuning penalty supply an interpretable network-design criterion, not simply code reproducibility or a renamed known formula.

## Scientific limitations
No result is asserted for heterogeneous donors, hub dephasing, arbitrary initial coherent superpositions, correlated noise, additional loss channels, or exact finite-time convergence rates. The \(\delta=0\) endpoint is outside the positive-noise theorem. Full-text priority comparison with Pouthier (2015) remains incomplete.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
