For a lossy quantum cone with independent dephasing on its leaves, the mean hub-capture time from each leaf is exactly \((n+1)/\kappa+(n-1)/\delta+(\kappa+\delta)/(4g^2)+[\varepsilon-(n-1)h]^2/[g^2(\kappa+\delta)]\). Resonance removes the interleaf-coupling penalty and gives the unique optimal dephasing rate \(2|g|\sqrt{n-1}\), while zero dephasing leaves permanent trapping.
