For every non-invariant, unreduced \(k\)-step Lanczos subspace with \(k\ge2\), Gaussian-sketched Galerkin inversion has a terminal coordinate equal in law to a reciprocal shifted Student-t variable with \(s-k+1\) degrees of freedom. Its exact \(R^{-1}\) tail forces every unconditional error moment of order at least one to be infinite, regardless of finite sketch width, while an invariant subspace gives the exact answer.
