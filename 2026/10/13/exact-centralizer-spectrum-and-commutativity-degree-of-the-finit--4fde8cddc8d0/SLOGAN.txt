For \(W=\operatorname{Der}_{\mathbb F_q}(\mathbb F_q[x]/(x^p))\) with \(p>3\), the Lie-centralizer dimension of \(f\partial\) is determined exactly by the \(x\)-adic valuation of \(f\). This yields the closed commutativity degree \(\operatorname{cd}(W)=(q^3+\frac{p-1}{2}(q^2-1))/q^{p+2}\) and a complete centralizer-size distribution.
