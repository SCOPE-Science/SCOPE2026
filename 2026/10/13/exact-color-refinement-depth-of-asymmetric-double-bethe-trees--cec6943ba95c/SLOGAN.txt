Joining two perfect \(d\)-ary trees at their roots gives a branching-independent three-regime color-refinement law. Equal heights stabilize at the common height, heights differing by one stabilize at the taller height, while a gap of at least two gives \(\max\{2m+3,\lfloor(m+M+2)/2\rfloor\}\), with stable colors exactly equal to automorphism orbits.
