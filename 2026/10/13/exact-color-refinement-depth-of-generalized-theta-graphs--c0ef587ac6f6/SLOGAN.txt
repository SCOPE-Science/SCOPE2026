For a generalized theta graph with path lengths \(\ell_1,\ldots,\ell_d\ge2\), color refinement stabilizes after exactly
\[
\max\left\{\left\lfloor\frac{\max_i\ell_i}{2}\right\rfloor,\ S\right\}
\]
rounds, where \(S\) is the second-largest distinct path length (or \(0\) if all lengths coincide). At fixed order \(n\) and path count \(d\), the exact maximum is \(\lfloor(n-d+1)/2\rfloor\).
