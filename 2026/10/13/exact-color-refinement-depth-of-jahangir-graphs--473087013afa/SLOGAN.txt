For the standard Jahangir graph \(J_{n,m}\), ordinary color refinement stabilizes in \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\) rounds when \(m\ge4\), and in \(\max\{2,\lfloor n/2\rfloor\}\) rounds when \(m=3\). The sole exception comes from the center and rim attachments both having degree \(3\); in every case the stable colors recover the automorphism orbits.
