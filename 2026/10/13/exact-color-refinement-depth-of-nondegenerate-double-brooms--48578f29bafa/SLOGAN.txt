For a nondegenerate double broom with \(p,q\ge2\) leaves at the two ends of an \(L\)-edge spine, ordinary color refinement stabilizes in \(\max\{1,\lfloor L/2\rfloor\}\) rounds when the two broom heads are balanced and in \(\max\{2,\lceil L/2\rceil\}\) rounds when they are unbalanced. In both cases the stable colors are exactly the automorphism orbits.
