For a rose graph, color-refinement depth is controlled only by the longest petal and the second-largest distinct petal length. If \(\lambda_s\) is the largest distinct cycle length, the depth is \(\lfloor\lambda_s/2\rfloor\) when all petals have that length, and otherwise \(\max\{\lfloor\lambda_s/2\rfloor,\lambda_{s-1}\}\); the stable colors also recover the automorphism orbits.
