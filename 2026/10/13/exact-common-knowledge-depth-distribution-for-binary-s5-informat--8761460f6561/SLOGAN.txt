For two-agent S5 frames with information cells of size at most two, the exact number of mutual-knowledge rounds needed to obtain common knowledge is the largest diameter of an alternating path or cycle in the union of the agents' information graphs. Their depth-at-most-\(d\) labelled census is \(n![z^n]\exp(\sum_{j=1}^{d+1}z^j+\sum_{j=1}^{d}z^{2j}/(2j))\); for \(n\ge3\), exactly \(n!\) frames attain the maximum depth \(n-1\).
