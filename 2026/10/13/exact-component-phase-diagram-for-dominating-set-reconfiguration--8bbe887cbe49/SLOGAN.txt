For crown graphs \(K_{n,n}-M_n\), the entire component count of the dominating-set add–delete reconfiguration graph is explicit at every vertex budget. At \(k=4\) there are \(\binom n2\binom{n-2}2\) isolated mixed obstructions; they vanish by \(k=5\), but for \(n\ge6\) two new isolated whole-side dominating sets reappear at \(k=n\), before connectivity returns at \(k=n+1\).
