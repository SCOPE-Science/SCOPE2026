# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The claim is proved for every prime power and then combined by the Chinese remainder theorem. The modulo-\(9\) necessity is exact. For \(p>3\), the finite-field conic has a nonsingular point after choosing \(t(t+1)\ne16n\); for \(p=3\), each admissible residue regime has an explicit simple Hensel seed; and for powers of \(2\), the triangular residue map is bijective on a complete index range. Finite computations are used only as checks of the symbolic proof.

## Originality
PASS. The closest source, Bagchi--Zhou-Zheng, proves the same obstruction classes and local solvability away from \(3\) but stops before the \(3\)-power classification. Full-text inspection of the recent integral triangular-pair literature does not supply the all-modulus statement. Targeted published-finding searches for equivalent formulations, broader coverage, exact tables, and direct implication found no covering result.

Residual risk: a narrowly phrased local-solubility note outside the searched literature could exist, but no decisive unresolved comparison remains.

## Value
PASS. The theorem closes a natural, explicitly isolated local gap and shows that every admissible parameter is free of congruence obstructions. This is mathematically useful because it proves that any failure of an admissible parameter to produce an integral triangular pair must be genuinely global rather than detectable by a modulus.
