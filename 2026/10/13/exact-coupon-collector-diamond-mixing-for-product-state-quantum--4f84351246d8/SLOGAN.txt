Randomly refreshing one site at a time to a prescribed pure product state has normalized diamond mixing distance *exactly equal* to the probability that at least one site has never been selected, for arbitrary positive site weights and local dimensions. With uniform updates the quantum cutoff is at \(n\log n\), with Gumbel profile \(1-e^{-e^{-c}}\), twice the leading classical hypercube total-variation mixing time.
