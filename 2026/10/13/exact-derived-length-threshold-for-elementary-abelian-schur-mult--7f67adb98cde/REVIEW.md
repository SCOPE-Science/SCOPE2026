# Review

## Correctness
PASS. For a finite abelian \(p\)-group with cyclic-factor exponents \(a_1,\ldots,a_r\), the standard formula gives one multiplier factor \(C_{p^{\min(a_i,a_j)}}\) for every unordered pair. An elementary-abelian multiplier therefore has exactly \(\binom{r}{2}\) rank, proving the full abelian obstruction and its converse. For nontriangular \(k\), Holt's Section 2 supplies a realization with abelian normal subgroup and cyclic quotient, hence derived length at most \(2\); the explicit exceptional target \(C_2^2\) is covered by the order-\(5\) complement construction in the source. No finite experiment is used to infer an infinite statement.

## Originality
PASS. The inspected 2026 source proves universal solvable realizability and gives the elementary-abelian construction, but searches of its full text found no occurrence of “derived length” or “metabelian,” and it does not state the triangular criterion. Targeted searches for triangular ranks, abelian realization, elementary-abelian Schur multipliers, and minimum derived length did not locate an equivalent or stronger statement. The closest database result involving exterior squares concerns a different kernel-wreath abelianization formula and does not imply the claim.

## Value
PASS. Minimum derived length is a natural structural complexity of a realization, and the theorem gives a complete infinite classification for every prime and every elementary-abelian rank. It identifies exactly when the new existence theorem genuinely requires noncommutativity, rather than merely shaving a construction parameter or recomputing a known table. The triangular/nontriangular boundary is sharp and field-independent.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is Holt, arXiv:2610.01312v1: Proposition 1.2 supplies the exterior-square formula and Section 2 supplies metabelian realizations of all elementary-abelian targets. Classical sources already contain the abelian multiplier formula, so the triangular obstruction alone is not claimed as new; the accepted claim is the exact minimum-derived-length dichotomy obtained by combining it with Holt's 2026 construction. An older paper could have recorded the same optimization under different language, which remains the principal residual originality risk.
