For every prime \(p\), the minimum derived length of a finite group with Schur multiplier \(C_p^k\) is \(1\) exactly for triangular ranks \(k=\binom{r}{2}\), and is \(2\) for every other \(k\). Thus Holt's universal solvable realization is already metabelian on elementary-abelian targets, and nonabelianity is forced precisely off the triangular ranks.
