# Exact descent field of the explicit Nikulin configuration on \(X_{\mathrm{BH}}\)
## Finding
Let \(K_0=\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\), \(\phi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\), choose \(r=\sqrt\phi\), and put \(L=K_0(r,i)=\mathbb Q(r,i)\). In the fixed complete-intersection model \(X_{\mathrm{BH}}/K_0\subset\mathbb P^5\) of Bonnafé--Sarti, let \(C\) be the conic of Remark 5.8 and let \(\mathcal N=A\cdot C\) be its 16-conic Nikulin configuration.

The unordered embedded configuration \(\mathcal N\) has exact field of definition \(\mathbb Q(r)\). Equivalently, inside \(\operatorname{Gal}(L/K_0)\cong C_2\times C_2\), its stabilizer is exactly the subgroup generated by complex conjugation \(i\mapsto-i\). The involution \(r\mapsto-r\) sends \(\mathcal N\) to a second 16-conic Nikulin configuration with no component in common with \(\mathcal N\).

Exactly eight members of \(\mathcal N\) are individually defined over \(\mathbb Q(r)\). The remaining eight have exact field \(\mathbb Q(r,i)\) and occur as four complex-conjugate pairs over \(\mathbb Q(r)\). In particular, no member of this displayed 16-conic configuration is individually defined over \(K_0\), and the configuration itself does not descend to \(K_0\).

Finally, \(r\) satisfies \(r^4-r^2-1=0\), this polynomial is irreducible over \(\mathbb Q\), and hence \([\mathbb Q(r):\mathbb Q]=4\) and \([L:\mathbb Q]=8\).

## Assumptions and scope
The statement concerns the specific embedded surface and the specific 16-conic \(A\)-orbit displayed in Bonnafé--Sarti, Remark 5.8. It is a statement about the fields of definition of these embedded conics and their reduced unordered union. It does not assert that every abstract Nikulin configuration on \(X_{\mathrm{BH}}\), or every configuration obtained after an arbitrary isomorphism over \(\overline{\mathbb Q}\), has the same minimal field.

A conic is identified with its intrinsic projective plane together with the nonsingular quadratic equation cut out there. Since the source conic is smooth and the group \(A\) preserves \(X_{\mathrm{BH}}\), the same holds for all 16 orbit members.

## Proof
Bonnafé--Sarti define the initial conic by
\[
x_5=rx_1,\qquad x_4=rx_2,\qquad x_3=rx_6,\qquad x_1^2-x_2^2-x_6^2=0,
\]
and print four monomial matrices generating a subgroup \(A\) of order \(32\), with scalar center \(\mu_2\) and elementary-abelian projective quotient \(A/\mu_2\). Exact closure of those four matrices modulo scalars gives \(|A/\mu_2|=16\), and their action produces 16 distinct projective planes. Restricting the three defining quadrics of \(X_{\mathrm{BH}}\) to the initial plane gives scalar multiples of the single smooth conic equation \(x_1^2-x_2^2-x_6^2=0\); invariance of the span of the three quadrics under each generator propagates this to every orbit plane.

Because \(N_{K_0/\mathbb Q}(\phi)=-1\), the element \(\phi\) is not a square in \(K_0\). Thus \(K_0(r)/K_0\) is quadratic. Since \(K_0(r)\subset\mathbb R\), adjoining \(i\) is an independent quadratic extension, so
\[
\operatorname{Gal}(L/K_0)=\langle\sigma_r,\sigma_i\rangle\cong C_2\times C_2,
\]
where \(\sigma_r(r)=-r\), \(\sigma_r(i)=i\), \(\sigma_i(r)=r\), and \(\sigma_i(i)=-i\).

For each of the 16 planes, compute its Plücker coordinates exactly in \(\mathbb Z[i,r]/(r^4-r^2-1)\), normalized only up to a nonzero scalar. Exhaustive comparison of the four Galois images gives the following facts. The involution \(\sigma_i\) preserves the 16-plane set and acts with cycle type \(1^8 2^4\). By contrast,
\[
\mathcal N\cap\sigma_r(\mathcal N)=\varnothing,
\qquad
\mathcal N\cap\sigma_r\sigma_i(\mathcal N)=\varnothing
\]
as sets of components. Hence the setwise Galois stabilizer of \(\mathcal N\) is exactly \(\langle\sigma_i\rangle\), whose fixed field is \(K_0(r)=\mathbb Q(r)\). This proves the exact descent field of the reduced unordered configuration.

For an individual component fixed by \(\sigma_i\), its stabilizer in \(\operatorname{Gal}(L/K_0)\) is exactly \(\langle\sigma_i\rangle\), because neither \(\sigma_r\) nor \(\sigma_r\sigma_i\) maps any component back into \(\mathcal N\). Its exact field is therefore \(\mathbb Q(r)\). For each of the other eight components the stabilizer is trivial, so its exact field is \(L=\mathbb Q(r,i)\). The same exhaustive comparison shows that these eight components form four \(\sigma_i\)-pairs.

Finally, \(r^2=\phi\) and \(\sqrt5=2r^2-1\), so \(\mathbb Q(r)\) contains \(K_0\). Since \(\phi\) is not a square in \(K_0\), the degree is \(4\) over \(\mathbb Q\), and the displayed quartic \(T^4-T^2-1\) is the minimal polynomial. Adjoining \(i\) doubles the degree.

## Verification
The standalone verifier uses only exact integer arithmetic in the quotient ring \(\mathbb Z[i,r]/(r^4-r^2-1)\). It reconstructs the four source generators, checks their projective closure has size \(16\), verifies preservation of the defining three-quadric span, verifies the initial conic restriction, constructs all 16 planes, and exhausts the four elements of \(\operatorname{Gal}(L/K_0)\) on exact Plücker coordinates.

A fresh replay of the packaged verifier returns:
`VERIFY_OK projective_A=16 planes=16 ci_fixed=8 ci_pairs=4 r_conjugate_overlap=0 both_overlap=0 config_field=Q(r) component_fields=8xQ(r)+8xQ(r,i) minpoly_r=r^4-r^2-1`

## Relationship to prior work
Bonnafé--Sarti construct \(X_{\mathrm{BH}}\), identify it as a Kummer surface, and in Remark 5.8 give the explicit conic \(C\), the four matrices generating \(A\), and the fact that the \(A\)-orbit consists of 16 pairwise disjoint conics. Their statement does not determine the minimal field of the unordered configuration or the individual component fields. The later work of Comparin--Demelle revisits projective models with an \(M_{20}\)-action, while Brandhorst--Hashimoto classifies finite extensions of maximal symplectic actions; the inspected relevant material does not state the descent calculation above.

The new point is not the existence of the 16 curves. It is the exact Galois stabilizer of the displayed configuration and the resulting \(8+8\) split of component fields.

## Limitations
The result is relative to the fixed Bonnafé--Sarti projective model and their particular \(A\)-orbit. It does not classify all Nikulin configurations on the surface, the full 80-conic orbit of the source conic, or fields of definition after arbitrary projective changes of model. It also does not claim that a component of \(\mathcal N\) is geometrically disjoint from every component of its \(r\mapsto-r\) conjugate; only the two 16-element component sets have empty intersection.

An originality risk remains that older or unindexed arithmetic work on this Kummer surface may encode an equivalent field-of-definition statement under a different marking of the exceptional curves.
