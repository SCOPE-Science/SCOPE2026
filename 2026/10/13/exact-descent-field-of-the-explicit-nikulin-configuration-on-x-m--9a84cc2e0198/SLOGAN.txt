For Bonnafé--Sarti's explicit sixteen-conic Nikulin configuration on \(X_{\mathrm{BH}}/\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\), the unordered configuration descends exactly to \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt\phi)\) and not to \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\). Eight conics are individually defined over \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt\phi)\), while the other eight require adjoining \(i\) and form four conjugate pairs.
