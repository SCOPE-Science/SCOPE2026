From two identical diagonal qubit copies, the normalized square state \(\rho^2/\operatorname{Tr}(\rho^2)\) has exact best deterministic physical approximation error \(\frac{\sqrt5-1}{4}\sqrt{\sqrt5-2}=0.1501415530\ldots\) in uniform trace distance. Discarding one copy is minimax optimal, so arbitrary deterministic processing of both copies cannot uniformly beat doing nothing on this family.
