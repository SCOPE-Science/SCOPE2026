In the block-transposition metric on \(S_5\), diameter-three codes have maximum size \(4\), with exactly \(540\) labeled maxima in two full-isometry orbits of sizes \(180\) and \(360\). Exactly \(120\) of the maxima are right cosets of four cyclic order-\(4\) subgroup maxima; the remaining \(420\) are non-coset extremizers.
