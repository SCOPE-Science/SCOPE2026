# Scientific review

**Correctness: PASS.** With a homogeneous total jump hazard, each no-jump trajectory segment is an exactly known coherent walk, and its even/odd weighted probabilities have Bessel-transform kernels. The nonnegative two-state renewal matrix has a unique Perron-root crossing; the claimed determinant is expanded directly. Analytic differentiation at the zero tilt gives the displayed drift and variance growth coefficient, including the nontrivial parity-contrast subtraction. Independent full-density-matrix integration checks the asymptotic slope. The infinite model is established by a mathematical renewal argument, not by extrapolating the 91-site calculation.

**Originality: PASS, qualified by literature priority.** The 2009 graph-local quantum stochastic walk paper does not give the parity-staggered full cumulant law. The 2016 QSW spreading-exponent analysis treats uniform local or global Lindblad operators. The 2019 Shapira–Cohen analysis studies an Ohmic-chain model and disorder, but does not present this constant-escape parity-contrast formula. The closest result is Eq. (31) of Avnit–Cohen (2023): it **does** cover the homogeneous-rate spectral dispersion, and the homogeneous part of the new formula is acknowledged as known. The new term requires a genuinely two-parity weighted renewal operator. An older dynamic-disorder paper was accessible only in abstract form, leaving nonzero priority risk.

**Value: PASS.** Alternating directional rates generate a classical parity-anticorrelation penalty that can strongly suppress transport. The theorem quantifies exactly how coherent hopping erodes this suppression, while the drift remains unchanged, for every strictly positive parameter set; the all-tilt equation also determines the complete large-deviation rate. The equal-escape restriction is structural because it creates uniform Poisson renewal epochs rather than an arbitrary numerical parameter slice. Limitations: strictly positive rates; localized input; homogeneous coherent hopping; no additional baths or arbitrary rate periods.
