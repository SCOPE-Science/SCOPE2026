For a quantum stochastic walk with equal total escape rates but alternating directional bias, the exact asymptotic positional variance rate is \(\Gamma+4h^2/\Gamma-(d_0-d_1)^2/[2(\Gamma+\sqrt{\Gamma^2+16h^2})]\), while the drift is \((d_0+d_1)/2\). Coherent hopping strictly raises dispersion by weakening the sublattice anticorrelation, and a two-parity renewal determinant determines the entire large-deviation law.
