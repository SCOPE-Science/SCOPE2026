# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The full-stabilizer invariant coefficient bundle is the trace-free commutant of the stable-isotypic multiplicity algebra. Its rank is \(\sum_\alpha d_\alpha^2-1\) and its degree is \(0\). Riemann--Roch therefore gives Higgs-complex Euler characteristic \(-2(g-1)(\sum_\alpha d_\alpha^2-1)\). Cao's Lemma 5.5 gives endpoint dimensions \(s-1\), so the invariant degree-one dimension is exactly \(2(g-1)(\sum_\alpha d_\alpha^2-1)+2(s-1)\). Theorem B identifies this with the vertical tangent, and Theorem A adds the \(3g-3\)-dimensional Teichmüller base. The non-stable maximum follows from convexity under \(\sum_\alpha d_\alpha m_\alpha=r\), with repeated single-block types handled separately.

## Originality
PASS. Cao leaves the invariant degree-one rank abstract and does not state the closed dimension formula, its independence from multiplicities, or the extremal non-stable codimension. Fan's fixed-curve orbit-type paper was inspected and does not state the formula. Tirelli's closest dimension calculation concerns \(\mathrm{GL}_n\), not determinant-one trace-free orbit-type strata, and proves a coarser strictly-polystable-locus estimate. Exact, alias, source, and implication searches found no covering statement.

## Value
PASS. This is a complete dimension formula for every orbit-type stratum in a newly constructed joint moduli space. It exposes a non-obvious multiplicity cancellation and gives an exact extremal codimension for the largest non-stable numerical type. Those are natural structural invariants for singular and Poisson-stratum geometry, rather than an arbitrary finite slice.

The result does not classify the connected components themselves or their transverse singularities, and equivalent formulas may be implicit in unindexed literature.
