For a smooth cubic \(d\)-fold, the Fano variety of lines has \(\nu_2(\chi)=\nu_2(3d-4)-1\) in even dimension and \(\nu_2(\chi)=\nu_2(3d+5)-1\) in odd dimension. Hence its Euler characteristic is odd exactly for \(d\equiv2,3\pmod4\), with every higher dyadic valuation occupying exactly two residue classes.
