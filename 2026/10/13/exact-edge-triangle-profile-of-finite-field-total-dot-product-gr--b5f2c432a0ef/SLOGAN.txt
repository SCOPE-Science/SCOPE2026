For every finite field and every dimension at least three, the number of triangles through an edge of the total dot product graph is determined exactly by the span rank and isotropy of its endpoints. Every edge lies in a triangle except exactly six isotropic-anisotropic edges of \(TD(\mathbb F_2,3)\), and in dimension three the total triangle count is \((q-1)(q+1)(q^2-3)(q^2-2)/6\).
