For \(W_{r,m}=K_1\vee rK_m\), every \(\ell\)-forcing set is determined by the white counts inside the blades and, once \(\ell\ge m\), only by how many blades are completely white. This gives the full forcing-set polynomial, the exact minimum \(F_\ell(W_{r,m})\), and the number of all minimum sets across the entire range \(1\le\ell\le rm\).
