In a reversible-confinement epidemic model, every interior point of the stable disease-free equilibrium segment is the **exact** endpoint of some genuinely infected trajectory; consequently that segment is the smallest closed attractor of the seeded-infection sector. A balanced susceptible state \((u,qu)\) perturbed by infectious mass \(\delta\) loses \(\frac{ru}{(1+q)(1-ru)}\delta+o(\delta)\) unconfined susceptible mass, explicitly quantifying the weak-outbreak response.
