For every odd base \(B\ge5\), the complete first-entrance distribution into the four-digit Kaprekar map's stable odd region is an explicit parity-sensitive polynomial in \((B-1)/2\), both for difference states and uniformly chosen four-digit strings. Exactly one difference state needs three steps; a random word needs three steps with probability asymptotic to \(3/B^2\), while the zero- and one-step probabilities tend to \(1/4\) and \(3/4\).
