**PASS.** The source generator is read directly from the primary paper. Exact additive closure reproduces the published size and weight distribution. The \(21\) minimum words have all \(21\) zero pairs. This makes the target minimum word under any coordinate permutation unique and therefore determines every local nonzero-symbol map. Exhausting all \(5040\) coordinate permutations and then checking all \(32\) codewords yields exactly \(21\) full source-equivalence automorphisms and exactly \(7\) pure coordinate automorphisms. The projected element-order census \(1^1,3^{14},7^6\) and closure establish the stated nonabelian order-\(21\) group.

## Value
**PASS.** The source's classification isolates this code as the unique additive code at an extremal length-seven parameter and explicitly highlights its cyclic symmetry. Determining the entire stabilizer under the same equivalence is therefore a natural classification invariant, and the result shows that cyclicity accounts for only one-third of the full symmetry. The zero-pair incidence gives a compact structural reason for the rigidity and is useful for equivalence testing; this is more than an arbitrary numerical fingerprint.
