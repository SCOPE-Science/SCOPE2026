For every fixed offset \(r\ge4\), solutions of \(\sigma(n)=(1+1/k)n+r\) with \(k\ge2\) are either the explicit prime-semiprime family \((k,n)=(r-1,(r-1)q)\) or lie below the sharp cutoff \(n\le(r-1)^3\). Solutions occur infinitely often exactly when \(r-1\) is prime; the small offsets \(r=1,2,3\) are completely classified as well.
