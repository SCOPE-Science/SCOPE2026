For prime-factorization tower height \(h\), the first height-\(k\) factorial occurs at index \(E_{k-1}+2\) for \(k\ge3\), while the first height-\(k\) least common multiple of an initial interval occurs at \(E_k\), where \(E_1=2\) and \(E_{j+1}=2^{E_j}\). Every \(k\ge3\) then has an exact lcm-height plateau \(E_k\le N<2E_k\), followed by a one-level drop at \(2E_k\).
