A one-column Gaussian signed Nyström correction to an exactly positive-definite double-saddle-point Schur complement can make its approximation indefinite with a precisely computable probability and a sharp global failure-probability supremum of \(0.142154976126\ldots\). Its spectral norm has a \(z^{-1}\) tail and infinite mean; adding any fixed positive ridge to the signed core does not eliminate positivity failure or the infinite mean.
