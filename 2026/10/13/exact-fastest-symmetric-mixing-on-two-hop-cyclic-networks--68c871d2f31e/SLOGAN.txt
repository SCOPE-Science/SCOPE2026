The fastest symmetric stochastic walk on the two-hop cycle \(C_n^2\) has a closed-form transition rule for every \(n\ge5\), globally optimal even when individual edge probabilities may differ. Its sharp SLEM satisfies \(1-\rho_n^*=8\pi^2/n^2+O(n^{-4})\), a fourfold leading spectral-gap gain over the best nearest-neighbor-only walk.
