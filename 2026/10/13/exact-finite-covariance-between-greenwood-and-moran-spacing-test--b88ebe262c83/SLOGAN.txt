Greenwood's sum of squared uniform spacings and Moran's log-spacing statistic have the exact null covariance \((m-1)/(m+1)^2\), with a closed finite correlation whose squared limit is the classical spacing-efficiency constant \(0.3876365241\ldots\). The same Dirichlet calculation gives an exact covariance between Moran's statistic and every power-spacing sum \(\sum_iD_i^a\).
