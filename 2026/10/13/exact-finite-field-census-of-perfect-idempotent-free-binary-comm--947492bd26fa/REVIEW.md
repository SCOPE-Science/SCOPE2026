# Review of the finite-field idempotent-free census

**Correctness — PASS.** The squaring eigenline cubic is explicitly derived from the six structure constants. The surjective cubic map has a two-dimensional radial kernel, proving its exact fiber cardinality. In a perfect binary algebra two independent square-zero directions are impossible. The only surviving cubic factorizations and all rank-one exclusions are proved in RESULT.md. Direct exhaustive counts over \(\mathbb F_3\) and \(\mathbb F_5\) match every stratum; they are not substitutes for the general proof.

**Originality — PASS with residual historical risk.** Ahmed–Bekbaev–Rakhimov Proposition 4.1 treats idempotents in each given algebra by a cubic equation; the new statement counts every labeled perfect commutative multiplication with none, for every odd prime power, split into three Frobenius types. Kaygorodov–Volkov classify isomorphism types over algebraically closed fields, and Petersson–Scherer count general isomorphism classes over finite fields, neither implying the stated labeled finite-field filtered counts without new analysis. Verhulst gives general Burnside enumeration methods, not this filter. A literature search is not a uniqueness proof; an unindexed equivalent formula remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** The fraction of perfect binary commutative algebras lacking a nonzero *rational* idempotent approaches \(1/3\), in marked contrast to algebraically closed classifications. The exact split into irreducible cubics, a linear-times-irreducible-quadratic branch, and triple-root degenerations identifies the arithmetic causes rather than only reporting a number. This finite-field census answers a motivated and natural structural counting question arising from contemporary idempotent-free evolution-algebra work; it is not a recomputation of known isomorphism-class tables.

The formulas do not apply to characteristic two or measure idempotents after scalar extension. The finite verifier is a separate computational consistency check; no independent review has been performed.
