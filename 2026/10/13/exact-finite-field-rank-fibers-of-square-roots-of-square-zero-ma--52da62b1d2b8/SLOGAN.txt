For every fixed square-zero matrix over any finite field, the complete matrix-square-root rank distribution is an explicit positive-integer sum of nilpotent centralizer-order ratios. A root exists precisely when its rank is even or its target has a nonzero one-dimensional Jordan block, and every possible root rank from \(\lceil3r/2\rceil\) to \(\lfloor(n+r)/2\rfloor\) occurs.
