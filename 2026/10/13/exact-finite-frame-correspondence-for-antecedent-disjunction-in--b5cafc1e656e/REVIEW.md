# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The flat conditional clause reduces to the local contractive operator \(I_w\). A direct valuation argument forces finite-join preservation; finiteness of the preorder permits exactly the principal-upset decomposition used to derive and reverse condition (PC). The three-world fork and two-world minimality are proved directly. The independent checker exhaustively compares semantic valuations and the first-order condition on all \(14914\) labelled frames with one, two or three worlds. Computations do not replace the arbitrary finite-size proof.

## Originality — PASS
The closest source is de Groot–Litak (2026), Definition 3.1 and Lemma 3.12. It gives only sufficient pointwise downward directedness, while the theorem establishes both directions and exhibits a non-directed valid frame. Litak–Visser (2018) concerns sharp/constructive strict implication, and Dufty–de Groot (2025) uses antecedent-indexed conditional relations; the inspected portions do not supply (PC). Targeted mathematical-record searches found no equivalent theorem. An older reformulation using finite union-preserving interior operators remains a specific originality risk.

## Value — PASS
An exact polynomial-time first-order structural characterization for a natural modal distributivity axiom resolves the gap between semantic validity and the paper’s sufficient hypothesis on finite frames. The three-world separation explains why global downward directedness is too strong. This is a natural recognition theorem, not a table-only or cosmetic strengthening.

## Scientific limitations
Only finite nonempty preorders are covered; no infinite-frame necessity is claimed. Reproducibility checks are exhaustive only through three worlds. The similarity search and inspected literature do not establish absolute priority.
