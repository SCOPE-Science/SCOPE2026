For the two-neighbor rank-modulation constraint with tolerance one, the exact number of admissible length-\(n\) permutations is a factorial-weighted coefficient sum of a polynomial sequence satisfying a second-order recurrence. Its bivariate generating function is \(\frac{z(1+(3u-2u^2)z+u^2z^2)}{1-uz-(2u-u^2)z^2}\), giving exact finite-length counts with quadratic integer-arithmetic work.
