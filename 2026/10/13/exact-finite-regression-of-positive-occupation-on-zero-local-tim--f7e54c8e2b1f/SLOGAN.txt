For a simple symmetric random walk, the strict positive occupation time has the exact finite regression \(\mathbb E[A_{2n}\mid Z_{2n}]=n-Z_{2n}/2\) on the zero-visit local time. Consequently \(\operatorname{Cov}(Z_{2n},A_{2n})=-\operatorname{Var}(Z_{2n})/2\), with an explicit central-binomial variance and asymptotic covariance \(-(1-2/\pi)n\).
