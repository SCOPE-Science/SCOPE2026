For a fully specified continuous null, Watson's centered goodness-of-fit statistic and the tail-weighted Anderson–Darling statistic have exact covariance \((n-1)/(36n)\) for every integer \(n\ge1\). Their null correlation is positive and strictly increases with sample size \(n\ge2\), approaching \(\sqrt{15}/[6\sqrt{\pi^2-9}]\); the calculation rests on a constant Watson diagonal and an exact mixed bridge-kernel integral.
