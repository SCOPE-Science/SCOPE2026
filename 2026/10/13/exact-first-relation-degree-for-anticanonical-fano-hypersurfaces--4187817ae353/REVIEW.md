## Correctness
PASS. Proposition 7.17 supplies the exact graded structure needed: the ambient section ring is normally presented, the quotient ideal vanishes below degree \(M\), is nonzero in degree \(M\), and is generated there. For \(M>2\), all lower-degree relations belong to the ambient quadratic ideal, so they cannot generate a relation with nonzero image in the degree-\(M\) quotient; a minimal degree-\(M\) generator is forced. For \(M\le2\), the anticanonical embedding is nondegenerate and its ideal is nonzero, forcing \(t_1\ge2\). The source upper bound gives equality in both cases.
