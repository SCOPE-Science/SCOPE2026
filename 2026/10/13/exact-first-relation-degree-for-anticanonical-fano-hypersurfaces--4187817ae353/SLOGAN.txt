For every smooth positive-dimensional Fano hypersurface in a product of projective spaces, Quedo's anticanonical first-relation bound is attained exactly: \(t_1=\max\{2,\lceil\max_i a_i/(n_i+1-a_i)\rceil\}\). The degree-\(M\) quotient relation cannot be generated away by the ambient quadrics because the quotient ideal vanishes in every smaller degree.
