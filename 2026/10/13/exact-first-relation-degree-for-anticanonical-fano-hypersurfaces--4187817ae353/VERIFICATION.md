# Verification

The universal proof is algebraic and does not depend on finite computation.

The source facts checked directly in arXiv:2610.00709v1 are:

1. Definition 2.10 identifies \(t_1\) with the largest degree of a minimal homogeneous ideal generator.
2. Proposition 7.17 defines \(M=\lceil\max_i a_i/(n_i+1-a_i)\rceil\) and proves \(t_1\le\max\{2,M\}\).
3. In that proof, the quotient ideal \(J\) is zero in degrees below \(M\), nonzero in degree \(M\), and generated in degree \(M\).
4. The ambient line bundle \(L=\mathcal O(n_i+1-a_i)_i\) is normally presented, so the ambient defining ideal is generated by quadrics.

From (3)--(4), when \(M>2\), lower-degree relations map to zero in \(J\), while a degree-\(M\) relation maps nontrivially; hence a minimal degree-\(M\) generator is unavoidable. When \(M\le2\), the complete anticanonical embedding has no linear equations and has a nonzero ideal in positive dimension, forcing a quadratic minimal generator.

`verify.py` only checks representative arithmetic instances of the Fano inequalities and the threshold formula. Its output is a regression aid, not an infinite proof.
