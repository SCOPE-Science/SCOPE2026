For two independent generalized matching games with at least four objects, a uniformly most powerful test at the first nontrivial match-total tie prefers \((1,1)\) to \((0,2)\) or \((2,0)\), for every positive skill parameter. The exact power lost by discarding that split has a positive triangular significance profile and, at sparse skill \(\theta=\lambda/n\), a peak of \(\lambda^2e^{-2(1+\lambda)}/(2n)+o(n^{-1})\).
