For every connected bipartite forbidden graph \(H\), exact \((2,H)\)-avoiding coloring on a complete multipartite host depends only on the two bipartition sizes of \(H\). The optimum is the minimum of a uniform cap \(t-1\) on every color class and, when \(s\ge2\), a second regime with one exceptional host part and cap \(s-1\) everywhere else.
