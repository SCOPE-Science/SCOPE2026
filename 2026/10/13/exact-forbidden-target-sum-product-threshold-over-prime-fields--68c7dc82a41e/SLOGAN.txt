The maximum size of a subset of \(\mathbb F_p\) whose sumset and product set both omit a prescribed nonzero target \(a\) is exactly \((p-1)/2\), except that it drops by one precisely when \(a\) is a square and the projective transformation \(x\mapsto a-a/x\) has even order. This explains the target-dependent obstruction beyond the sharp construction for target \(1\).
