For positive-parity pure states of four fermionic modes, the standard two-copy Bell Gaussianity test has the exact rejection law \(r_4=\tfrac12F_{\mathrm G}(1-F_{\mathrm G})\). Hence the sharp rejection floor at Gaussian trace distance \(\varepsilon\) is \(\tfrac12\varepsilon^2(1-\varepsilon^2)\), giving an exact finite-sample round count.
