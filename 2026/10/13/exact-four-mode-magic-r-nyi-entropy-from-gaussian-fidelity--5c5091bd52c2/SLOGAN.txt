For four complex fermionic modes, the order-two fermionic magic Rényi entropy is determined exactly by Gaussian fidelity: \(\nu_2=1-3F_{\mathrm G}(1-F_{\mathrm G})+\frac32F_{\mathrm G}^2(1-F_{\mathrm G})^2\). Its exact maximum is \(\ln(32/11)\), attained exactly at \(F_{\mathrm G}=1/2\), so the recent universal bound is already strict at \(N=8\) Majoranas.
