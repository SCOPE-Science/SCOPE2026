For up to four independent identically biased Bernoulli bets, every upper-tail probability with threshold at least the coin bias is maximized by equal stakes among one, two, three, or four bets. An explicit five-piece \((p,t)\) phase diagram gives the sharp value and exhibits a three-bet majority phase for \(1/2<p\le t\le2/3\); the unrestricted-number-of-bets conjecture remains open.
