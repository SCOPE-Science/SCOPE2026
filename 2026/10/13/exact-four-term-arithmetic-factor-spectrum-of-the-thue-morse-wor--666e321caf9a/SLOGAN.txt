For every prescribed positive difference \(d\), the Thue–Morse word exhibits exactly \(10\), \(14\), or \(16\) binary four-term arithmetic patterns, according as the odd part of \(d\) equals \(1\), equals \(3\), or exceeds \(3\). The unique nonmonochromatic exception occurs at odd part \(3\), where precisely \(0110\) and \(1001\) are absent; an exact binary-carry automaton certifies the classification for all differences.
