For Olshanskii's four-element nonsimple prime ring \(A\), the \(n\)-generated free algebra has exact dimension \(4^n+n-1\) over \(\mathbb F_2\), hence exact order \(2^{4^n+n-1}\). The product part is precisely the full codimension-one Boolean-function space vanishing at the zero input, giving an exact model of maximal potential \(\operatorname{pt}(A)=4\).
