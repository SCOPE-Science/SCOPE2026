For each finite field \(\mathbb F_q\), extending the affine Lie algebra by one unipotent Jordan block of length \(s\ge2\) gives relatively free rank-\(n\) dimension exactly \(n+s(n-1)(q^n-1)\). The resulting Lie varieties form a strictly ascending chain separated by explicit adjoint-polynomial identities, even though all share exponential free-dimension growth rate \(q\).
