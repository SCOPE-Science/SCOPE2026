For the unadjusted random-coordinate Langevin Euler sampler on any positive-definite Gaussian precision matrix, a Gaussian stationary law exists exactly when coordinate probabilities are proportional to precision diagonals within every connected precision-graph component and each component's effective step is below two. In the connected case the sole Gaussian invariant is an explicit scalar inflation of the target, while every other strictly stable coordinate-probability choice has a non-Gaussian invariant.
