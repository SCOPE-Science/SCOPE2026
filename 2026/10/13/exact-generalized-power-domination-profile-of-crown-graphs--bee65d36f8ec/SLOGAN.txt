For every crown graph, generalized power domination is governed only by whether the selected set meets both sides and, for one-sided selections, by the sharp threshold \(\max\{1,n-k-1\}\). This yields the full enumerator \(\mathcal P_k(\operatorname{Cr}_n;x)=((1+x)^n-1)^2+2\sum_{t=h}^{n}\binom nt x^t\) and replaces the published same-object value \(n-k\), which fails under the standard generalized propagation rule.
