The nilpotency-index-two ideal of the Catalan transformation monoid has an exact factorization criterion and unique minimal generating set: a nonconstant map factors inside the ideal precisely when its initial one-fiber is at least its maximum output plus the number of distinct nonunit outputs. Its rank is \(2^{n-2}-b_n\), where \(b_n\) is the known sequence OEIS A005251; at \(n=6\), the rank is \(9\), strictly exceeding the earlier lower bound \(8\).
