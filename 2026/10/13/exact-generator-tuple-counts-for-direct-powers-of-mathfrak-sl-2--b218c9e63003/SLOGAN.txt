The number of ordered \(r\)-tuples generating \(\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb F_q)^{\oplus k}\) has an exact closed product formula in every finite field. Odd characteristic admits \(q^2(q-1)\) simultaneously generated factors with just two elements, but in characteristic two the same direct power requires at least \(2k\) elements, even though the one-factor generating-tuple counts agree in all characteristics.
