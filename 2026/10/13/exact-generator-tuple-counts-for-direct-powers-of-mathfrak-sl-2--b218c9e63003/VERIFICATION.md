# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For every q, exactly t=q+1 of the q²+q+1 planes of sl2(F_q) are Lie subalgebras; in odd characteristic this is a nonsingular trace conic, in even characteristic sl2 is Heisenberg and the closed planes contain its center. Counting spanning triples plus nonclosed spanning planes yields one-factor G=q^r(q^r-1)(q^r-q). Odd-characteristic sl2 is simple with |Aut|=q(q²-1), whose action on generating r-tuples is free; Lie Goursat shows a k-factor tuple generates precisely if all coordinate tuples lie in different Aut-orbits, hence the falling product. Even-characteristic Heisenberg direct sums generate iff projected tuples span their 2k-dimensional abelianization; lifting gives q^{kr} times the finite-field full-rank factor. Independent exact bracket closure checked all ordered two-generator pairs for q=2 and q=3, yielding 24 and 432 respectively.

## originality

PASS

The broad Lie-generator varieties of Bois guarantee existence for sums over algebraically closed fields of characteristic zero or >3 (Cor.2.2.5), not finite-field all-arity exact multiplicities. The published three-dimensional plane-count theorem already gives the q+1 ingredient, explicitly credited. Standard Goursat and free automorphism orbits imply a falling product after supplying the one-factor count, so no claim to novelty of these lemmas; an older general Hall formula could cover the synthesis. With the matched finite-field full prime-power characteristic dichotomy, not found as one published formula in inspected sources, best-of-knowledge O passes.

## value

PASS

Determining all ordered generating-tuple counts and maximal number of simultaneously generated direct factors is a natural finite-field Hall-type invariant. The exact characteristic-two degeneration from q²(q−1) odd-characteristic factors generated by two elements to only one even-characteristic factor is meaningful structure, even though the Goursat principle itself is classical.
