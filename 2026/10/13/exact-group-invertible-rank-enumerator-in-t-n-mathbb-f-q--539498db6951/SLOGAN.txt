For every fixed diagonal in \(T_n(\mathbb F_q)\) with exactly \(k\) zeros, precisely \(q^{\binom n2-\binom k2}\) upper-triangular completions are group invertible, independent of the zero positions. Consequently the rank-\(r\) group-invertible stratum has size \(\binom nr(q-1)^r q^{\binom n2-\binom{n-r}2}\).
