For Haar random \(d\)-dimensional sections in \(\mathbb R^n\), the sharp worst-case expected Frank–Wolfe ball-oracle efficiency is \((d-1)/(n-1)\), approached by nearly aligned boundary-normal and gradient directions. This gives a positive, optimal uniform comparison-ball coefficient already at \(d=2\) and sharpens the expected convergence constant of the 2026 random-subspace Frank–Wolfe method.
