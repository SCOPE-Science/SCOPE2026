# Exact in-gap guided-state crowding law at the critical Dirac line shell
## Finding
Consider the massive two-dimensional Dirac operator with a purely electrostatic \(\delta\)-shell interaction supported on the straight line. Assume \(m
eq0\) and \(\eta\in\mathbb R\setminus\{0,\pm2\}\), and define
\[
a_\eta=rac{|\eta^2-4|}{\eta^2+4}.
\]
The electrostatic fiber calculation of Behrndt--Holzmann--Tušek gives exactly one guided eigenvalue for each tangential momentum \(k\), whose magnitude is
\[
e_\eta(k)=a_\eta\sqrt{m^2+k^2}.
\]

\[
N_\eta(E)=rac1{2\pi}\,igl|\{k\in\mathbb R:e_\eta(k)\le E}igr|.

\]

\[

Thus the critical flat zero-energy band is approached through a quantitative \(|\eta-\eta_c|^{-1}\) accumulation of in-gap guided states, while its moving edge has a square-root van Hove singularity with coefficient growing like \(|\eta-\eta_c|^{-1/2}\).

## Assumptions and scope
The statement concerns only the single guided fiber branch generated by the straight electrostatic shell; it is not the full two-dimensional bulk density of states. The normalization \(dk/(2\pi)\) is the standard density of tangential Fourier modes per unit physical length. The free mass gap is \((-|m|,|m|)\). At \(\eta=0\) there is no shell-guided branch, so that value is excluded. The critical cases \(\eta=\pm2\) are excluded from the finite-density formulas and treated as limits; there the cited operator has a flat zero-energy band and zero is an eigenvalue of infinite multiplicity.

## Proof
For \(\eta
eq\pm2\), the source determinant equation is
\[
p^2=rac{(\eta^2+4)^2}{(\eta^2-4)^2}z^2-m^2
\]
with the source sign condition selecting one branch. Equivalently, the later full straight-line analysis writes the unique electrostatic fiber eigenvalue as
\[
z_\eta(k)=\operatorname{sgn}(\eta)rac{\eta^2-4}{\eta^2+4}\sqrt{m^2+k^2}.
\]
Taking magnitudes yields \(e_\eta(k)=a_\eta\sqrt{m^2+k^2}\).

For \(E>a_\eta|m|\), the inequality \(e_\eta(k)\le E\) is equivalent to
\[
|k|\le \sqrt{E^2/a_\eta^2-m^2}.
\]
The allowed momentum interval therefore has length twice the right-hand side. Dividing by \(2\pi\) proves
\[
N_\eta(E)=rac1{\pi a_\eta}\sqrt{E^2-a_\eta^2m^2}.
\]
Below the band edge the set is empty, giving the positive-part form.

Set \(E=|m|\) from below. Since
\[
1-a_\eta^2
=rac{16\eta^2}{(\eta^2+4)^2},
\]
one obtains
\[
N_\eta(|m|-)
=rac{|m|}{\pi a_\eta}\sqrt{1-a_\eta^2}
=rac{4|m\eta|}{\pi|\eta^2-4|}.
\]
If \(\eta_c=2\) or \(\eta_c=-2\), then \(|\eta^2-4|=4|\eta-\eta_c|+O(|\eta-\eta_c|^2)\) and \(|\eta|=2+O(|\eta-\eta_c|)\), which gives the stated critical crowding law.

Differentiating \(N_\eta(E)\) on the open band gives \(ho_\eta(E)\). Writing \(E=a_\eta|m|+arepsilon\) and expanding the square root proves the threshold asymptotic. Finally,
\[
a_\eta=rac12|\eta-\eta_c|+O(|\eta-\eta_c|^2),
\]
so at fixed \(E>0\), the exact density formula gives \(ho_\eta(E)\sim 2/(\pi|\eta-\eta_c|)\).

## Relationship to prior work
Behrndt--Holzmann--Tušek establish the spectral transition and derive the momentum-energy relation for the electrostatic straight-line shell. Their later general straight-line paper states the guided dispersion explicitly, displays its flattening at \(\eta=\pm2\), and proves that the critical flat band gives an eigenvalue of infinite multiplicity. Those sources do not state a guided-band density of states, an in-gap state-count formula, or a critical crowding exponent. Borrelli--Carimati--Fermi later give a detailed description of the critical straight-line eigenspace and contrast it with the circular geometry; their treatment likewise does not state the noncritical density formulas above.

The present finding is therefore not a new dispersion relation. It is the exact state-count consequence of the known branch, including the closed total in-gap density \(4|m\eta|/(\pi|\eta^2-4|)\), its universal \(|\eta-\eta_c|^{-1}\) divergence, and the simultaneous square-root edge singularity.

## Limitations
No claim is made about the full bulk density of states, local density of states in the transverse coordinate, nonlinear perturbations of the shell, curved supports, or disorder. The counting density separates the unique guided fiber branch from the bulk direct-integral spectrum. The critical formulas describe approach to \(\eta=\pm2\); at criticality itself the finite counting density is replaced by an infinitely degenerate zero-energy eigenspace.
