# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The argument starts from the published electrostatic fiber relation and the later explicit one-eigenvalue dispersion. Counting tangential momenta with density \(dk/(2\pi)\) gives the closed counting function directly; the total in-gap formula follows from an exact algebraic identity. Differentiation gives the density, and first-order expansion of \(a_\eta\) at \(\eta=\pm2\) gives the two critical exponents. The critical source theorem independently identifies the limiting zero-energy flat band as an eigenvalue of infinite multiplicity. No finite computation is used as an infinite proof.

The closest prior statement is the explicit band \(z_\eta(k)\), which is an input rather than a covering implication: a dispersion curve does not itself state the normalized state count, its exact simplification, or its critical asymptotics.

## Value
PASS. The cited spectral-transition papers emphasize that the entire dispersive in-gap contribution collapses to an infinitely degenerate point at \(\eta=\pm2\). The exact line density \(4|m\eta|/(\pi|\eta^2-4|)\) supplies a natural quantitative measure of that transition, while the simultaneous \(|\eta-\eta_c|^{-1/2}\) growth of the edge singularity separates local band-edge crowding from the stronger total in-gap crowding. This is a structural spectral invariant of the model rather than an arbitrary parameter evaluation.

The main residual risk is terminological: an equivalent density calculation may exist in unindexed physics literature under line-defect, guided-mode, or flat-band language. No such statement was found in the inspected primary and closest follow-up sources.
