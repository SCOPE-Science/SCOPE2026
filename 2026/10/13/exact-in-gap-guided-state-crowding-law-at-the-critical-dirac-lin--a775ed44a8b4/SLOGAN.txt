For the electrostatic Dirac line shell, the number of guided states per unit length that remain inside the free mass gap is exactly \(4|m\eta|/(\pi|\eta^2-4|)\). It diverges as \(2|m|/(\pi|\eta-\eta_c|)\) at either critical coupling \(\eta_c=\pm2\), quantitatively resolving how the dispersive guided band collapses into the infinitely degenerate zero-energy flat band.
