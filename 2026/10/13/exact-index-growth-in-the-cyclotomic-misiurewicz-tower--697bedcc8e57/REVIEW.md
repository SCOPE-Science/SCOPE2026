Squarefreeness of \(\Phi_p(2j)\) makes the radical polynomial Eisenstein at an odd prime over its radicand, proving irreducibility, and it removes all odd index contributions. At primes above \(2\), exact binomial valuations yield a regular Newton polygon and the index lattice count \(2^k-2^{k-\min\{\nu_2(j),k\}}\). Residue-degree summation over \(2\) contributes the factor \(p-1\). The finite checker agrees with every Newton-polygon identity it tests and is not used for the infinite quantifier.
