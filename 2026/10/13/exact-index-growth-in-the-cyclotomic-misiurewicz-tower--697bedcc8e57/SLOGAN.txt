For the cyclotomic Misiurewicz tower \(F_k(x)=\Phi_p(h_j^{\circ k}(x))\) with squarefree \(\Phi_p(2j)\), the exact index of the order generated by a root of \(F_k\) is \(2^{(p-1)(2^k-2^{k-\min\{\nu_2(j),k\}})}\). This order is maximal exactly for odd \(j\); even \(j\) produces dyadic index growth from the first level onward.
