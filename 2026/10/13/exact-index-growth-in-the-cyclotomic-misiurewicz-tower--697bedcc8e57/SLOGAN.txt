For the cyclotomic Misiurewicz tower \(F_k(x)=\Phi_p(h_j^{\circ k}(x))\) with squarefree \(\Phi_p(2j)\), the exact order index is \(2^{(p-1)(2^k-2^{k-\min\{\nu_2(j),k\}})}\). The odd-\(j\) monogenic family is precisely the zero-index boundary; even \(j\) produces explicit dyadic index growth from the first level onward.
