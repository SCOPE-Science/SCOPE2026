For every finite unital ring with semisimple quotient \(\prod_i M_{n_i}(\mathbb F_{q_i})\), the probability that a product of \(k\) independent uniform elements is nilpotent is exactly \(\prod_i[1-(1-q_i^{-n_i})^k]\). Thus the full sequence of nilpotent-product probabilities is blind to the Jacobson radical and cannot distinguish \(M_n(\mathbb F_q)\) from \(\mathbb F_{q^n}\).
