For the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M_n\), deleting the perfect matching lowers \(\gamma_k^{\mathrm L}\) from the complete-bipartite value \(2(n-k)\) by exactly two precisely when \(\lceil(n-2)/2\rceil\le k\le n-3\). The optimum sets are exactly disjoint equal-size shore supports in that window and arbitrary equal-size shore supports outside it, giving closed counts in both regimes.
