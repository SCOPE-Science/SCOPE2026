For a complete multipartite graph, \(k\)-tuple total dominator coloring reduces exactly to a color-count occupancy problem across the parts. This yields a feasibility threshold \(k\le N-L\), an exact water-filling formula \(\chi_{k,d}^t=k+\tau\), and a Stirling-number enumeration of all optimal colorings; in particular \(\chi_{k,d}^t(K_{m,n})=2k\) whenever \(1\le k\le m\le n\).
