# Mathematical review: Exact kernel of quantum average mixing on Kulli path windmills

## Correctness — PASS
The average mixing matrix is the sum of Schur squares of mutually orthogonal adjacency projectors, and its real quadratic form is exactly the sum of squared Frobenius norms of the diagonal-matrix compressions. Path sine eigenmodes split by reversal parity. The proof separately establishes multiplicities, absence of coincident coupled and path energies, and the nonzero hub coordinate of each coupled eigenvector. For even path indices the individual blade compressions vanish; for odd indices a diagonal form vanishing on the blade-sum-zero space forces every diagonal coefficient to be zero when there are at least three blades. The discrete Fourier inverse proves precisely blade-reflection antisymmetry, and a coupled eigenvector forces the center coordinate to vanish. The converse checks all spectral compressions. Thus the kernel and rank are proved for every integer blade count at least three and path order at least two. The 40 finite checks are supporting tests, not proof of quantification over all parameters.

## Originality — PASS, subject to named uncertainty
The 2023 doctoral dissertation of Paula R. Kimmerling, *Continuous-Time Quantum Walks on Windmill Graphs*, Section 5.1.3, Table 5.3 and Conjecture 5.1.5, records experimental Kulli-path ranks and explicitly leaves the at-least-three-blade case open. Its established Dutch-windmill result is for a different attachment and does not imply this kernel. Godsil (2013) and Coutinho–Godsil–Guo–Zhan (2018) supply the general spectral-projector/average-mixing identities, not the path-windmill-specific nullspace calculation. The proof of Kimmerling's previously predicted rank, strengthened to the exact nullspace, is the asserted contribution; the rank formula was already conjectured and the finite examples were already known. A later proof under alternative vocabulary or an implicit graph-join corollary remains possible. The publicly indexed dissertation section was read, but its full official repository-hosted PDF could not be retrieved, so its unseen portions remain an access risk. published-finding corpus comparisons with windmill, graph-cone, and quantum-mixing searches found no implication covering the theorem; lack of a database hit alone is not novelty evidence.

## Value — PASS
This settles the at-least-three-blade branch of a specifically posed infinite-family spectral-rank conjecture and identifies an exact physically interpretable kernel: differences of mirror-symmetric sites along each path blade. The discrete Fourier and spectral-compression mechanism explains why all other modes survive, not merely a numerical pattern. It gives a natural complete classification for an infinite family of quantum transport networks with extensive degeneracy. The two-blade conjecture remains outside scope.
