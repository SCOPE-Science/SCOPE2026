For the symmetric three-state \(p\)-SOS phase at \(p=1/10\), the anti-ferromagnetic Kesten--Stigum boundary is the algebraic value \(\theta_{\mathrm{KS}}=6.8818943763536516\ldots\), not the public preprint's reported numerical value near \(1523.4\). The proof isolates a unique degree-40 elimination root and shows the second nontrivial eigenvalue is uniformly too small to trigger Kesten--Stigum.
