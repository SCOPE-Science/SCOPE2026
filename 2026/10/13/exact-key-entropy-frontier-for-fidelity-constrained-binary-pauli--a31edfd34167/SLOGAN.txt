For a pure binary qubit pair on the fidelity line, imposing sensitive contraction \(|\lambda_x|\le t\) and the source utility constraint costs exactly \(h_2((1-t)/2)\) Shannon bits of Pauli randomness whenever feasible. A two-gate \(I/Z\) mixture attains the bound, giving an exact key-randomness/privacy/utility frontier for the full adapted Pauli class.
