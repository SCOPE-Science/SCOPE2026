For four independent binomial populations with two trials each, selecting the largest two counts with fair tie breaking has a unique least-favorable configuration for every separation \(0<\delta<1\): the two smaller probabilities equal \((1-\delta)/2\) and the two larger equal \((1+\delta)/2\). Its exact worst-case correct-selection probability is \((1+\delta)^4(\delta^3-4\delta^2-7\delta+16)/96\).
