For every odd prime \(p\), the canonical tetrahedral TQD state of a standard elementary nontrivial type-I or type-II cocycle has minimum \(X\mathcal C_d\) stabilizer hierarchy level exactly \(p\). The lower bound comes from a flat-support slice on which the star amplitude ratio is an affine copy of the degree-\(p\) carry function, so no alternative lower-level stabilizer presentation can generate the same state.
