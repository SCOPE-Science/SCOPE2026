For a fixed shortest binary residue embedding with appended block \(B\) of rank \(t\), the An--Kim--Ling lift space has exactly \(2^{k(r-t)+t(t+1)/2}\) shortest self-orthogonal \(\mathbb Z_4\) generator-matrix lifts. Thus the size of the quaternary lift search is determined solely by \(k,r\), and \(\operatorname{rank}B\).
