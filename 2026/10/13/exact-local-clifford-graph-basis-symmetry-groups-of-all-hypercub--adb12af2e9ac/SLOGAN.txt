For the \(2^d\)-qubit graph state of a \(d\)-dimensional hypercube, the full group of graph-basis-preserving tensor-product Cliffords modulo local Pauli corrections is exactly \(S_3\) when \(d\) is odd and the Klein four group when \(d\) is even. This parity law completely classifies the allowed one-qubit Clifford patterns and their affine action on all graph-basis labels, for every \(d\geq1\).
