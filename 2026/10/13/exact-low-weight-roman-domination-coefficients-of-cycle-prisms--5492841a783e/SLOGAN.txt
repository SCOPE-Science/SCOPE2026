For cycle prisms \(C_n\square K_2\), the minimum Roman-domination multiplicity is exactly \(4\) when \(4\mid n\), \(2n\) when \(n\equiv2\pmod4\), and \(4n\) for odd \(n\geq5\), with the triangular-prism exception \(15\). The next weight above the optimum on a length divisible by four occurs exactly \(6n\) times; both laws follow from a finite exact \(81\)-state matrix-periodicity identity.
