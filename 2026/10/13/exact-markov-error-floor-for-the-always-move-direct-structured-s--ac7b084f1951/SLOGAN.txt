For the always-move direct structured search of arXiv:2609.17759v1, a scalar strongly convex quadratic with Rademacher rank-one candidates becomes an exact birth--death chain. With \(q\ge2\), its almost-sure time-average objective is \(\eta^2/[4(1-2^{1-q})^2]\), so even infinitely many candidates leave the fixed-step floor \(\eta^2/4\); with \(q=1\), the method is a simple random walk.
