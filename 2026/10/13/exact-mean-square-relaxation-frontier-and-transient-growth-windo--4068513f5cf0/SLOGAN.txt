For two equally weighted correlated rows, averaged randomized Kaczmarz converges in mean square exactly for \(0<\alpha<4/[1+\sqrt{c^2+(1-c^2)/q^2}]\), strictly beyond its sharp one-step nonexpansion range when \(q>1\). This gives a provably convergent window with transient expected-error growth and a unique infinite-horizon rate-optimal relaxation.
