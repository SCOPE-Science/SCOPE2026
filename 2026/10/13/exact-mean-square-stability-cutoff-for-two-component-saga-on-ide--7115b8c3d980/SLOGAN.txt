For standard SAGA on only two **identical** scalar quadratic components, the exact all-initialization mean-square convergence interval is \(0<\eta L<2/\sqrt3\), strictly narrower than gradient descent's \(0<\eta L<2\). An explicit positive-definite cached-state energy has conditional drift exactly \((3(\eta L)^2-4)x_k^2\), proving both the sharp cutoff and a nonnegative martingale at its boundary.
