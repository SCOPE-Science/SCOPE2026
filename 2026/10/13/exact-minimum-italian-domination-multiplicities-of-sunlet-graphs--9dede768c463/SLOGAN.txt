For the \(2n\)-vertex sunlet \(C_n\odot K_1\), the number of minimum-weight Italian dominating functions has a closed formula depending on \(n\bmod3\): \(3\cdot2^q\), \((3q+1)(q+3)2^q\), or \((3q+2)2^{q+1}\), respectively. The already-known minimum weight is refined into a complete structural enumeration of its optimizers.
