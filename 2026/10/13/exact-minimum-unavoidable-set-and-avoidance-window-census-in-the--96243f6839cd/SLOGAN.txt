In the golden-mean binary shift forbidding `11`, the minimum unavoidable-set cardinalities are classical, but their exact numbers of realizations for lengths two through eight are 2, 1, 4, 1, 40, 2, and 240. For length eight, exactly 16 of the 240 optimal-size sets achieve the sharp minimum guaranteed-hit length of 23 symbols; the complete residual-path distributions and all optimal sets are provided.
