For every prime \(p\ge5\) and odd \(m\ge3\), the minimum-weight words of the primitive BCH code \(C_{1,2}\) span exactly the cyclic ideal generated by \((x^h-\beta)(x^h-\beta^2)\), not merely the previously known containing subcodes. This gives the exact distance-three component count \(p^{2(h-m)}\).
