The twenty-vertex Desargues graph has exactly \(13\,520\) minimum ordinary zero-forcing sets, in \(67\) automorphism orbits of sizes \(20\), \(60\), \(120\) (eighteen times) and \(240\) (forty-seven times). Every optimum set splits four-and-four across the two incidence classes, a restriction explained by the rank-six simplicial boundary operator; the already known minimum cardinality is \(8\).
