For its canonical \(n=2\) counterexample, the first lower-arity layer is therefore a natural invariant rather than an arbitrary slice. The result upgrades qualitative finiteness to a complete local spectrum with a sharp bound and exact multiplicities, exposing how the Esakia geometry constrains one-variable behavior.
