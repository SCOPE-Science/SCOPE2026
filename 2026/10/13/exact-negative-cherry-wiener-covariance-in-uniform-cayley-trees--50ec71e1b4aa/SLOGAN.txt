In a uniformly random labelled Cayley tree, the number of unordered leaf pairs sharing a neighbor is negatively correlated with the Wiener sum of all pairwise distances for every order \(n\ge4\). The covariance has an explicit finite-sample formula and is asymptotic to \(-\frac{e^{-2}}4\sqrt{\frac\pi2}\,n^{5/2}\).
