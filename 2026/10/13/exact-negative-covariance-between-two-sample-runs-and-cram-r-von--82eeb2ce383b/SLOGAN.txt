Under the common continuous two-sample null, the Wald–Wolfowitz run count \(R\) and the classical Cramér–von Mises discrepancy \(T\) satisfy \(\operatorname{Cov}(R,T)=-(2mn-N)/(3N^2)\), with \(N=m+n\). Thus every nontrivial pooled sample of size at least three has strictly negative finite-sample covariance between local label alternation and global quadratic empirical separation.
