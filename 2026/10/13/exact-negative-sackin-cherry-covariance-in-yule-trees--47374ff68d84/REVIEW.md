# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The claimed \(n\ge3\) rational covariance follows from exact uniform-leaf splitting recurrences for Sackin depth, cherry count, and the depths of cherry leaves, including the nontrivial mixed product term. The deterministic \(n=2,3\) cases and the strict negativity boundary are checked. Exact rational full-shape enumeration through ten leaves reproduces all displayed formulas. The finite enumeration is not used to justify arbitrary \(n\).

**Originality — PASS, with historical priority risk.** Cardona–Mir–Rosselló (2012) prove Sackin moments and a different mixed covariance; Wu–Choi (2015) give cherry–pitchfork joint moments. Dearlove–Frost (2015) study Sackin and cherries together, explicitly describing weak correlation but not stating the claimed exact size-dependent mixed moment. The 2020 depth-variance paper likewise concerns different cross statistics. The displayed original results were inspected for implication/coverage, with attention to the unrelated use of \(C\) for Colless's index. An unlocated older general joint-moment identity might imply the exact formula; comprehensive independent historical verification remains outstanding.

**Value — PASS.** The two indices measure complementary levels of phylogenetic tree balance; their exact joint dependence matters to joint model calibration. This supplies a full all-size sign result and linear covariance scale where previous descriptive work was qualitative. It does not claim a new marginal law or a complete joint distribution.

**Limitations.** Unit-edge Yule tree shapes only, not chronological branch lengths, other tree laws, or sample-time conditioned trees. The analytic proof does not establish monotone dependence or an extremal coupling.
