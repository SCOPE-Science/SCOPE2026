For a uniformly chosen labeled Cayley tree, its first Zagreb degree-square index and Wiener total-distance index have the exact covariance \(2n(n-1)-(n+6)\mathbb E[W]/n\), which is zero only at sizes \(2\) and \(3\) and strictly negative thereafter. A distance-conditioned square-degree regression explains the sign and shows the covariance grows in magnitude like \(\sqrt{\pi/8}\,n^{5/2}\).
