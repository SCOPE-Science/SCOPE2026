The all-order extremality-window conjecture for the source's disordered mixed-spin Ising phase reduces to a monotone polynomial \(H_k(x)=2(k-1)x^{k+1}-2kx^{k-1}-1\) under \(x=(\theta+\theta^{-1})/2\). Its unique roots give reciprocal critical parameters and prove that the certified extremality windows shrink strictly with the Cayley-tree order.
