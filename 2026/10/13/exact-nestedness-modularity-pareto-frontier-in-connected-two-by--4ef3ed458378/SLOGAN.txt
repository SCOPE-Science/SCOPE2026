In connected two-by-\(n\) binary ecological networks with \(n+c\) links, the full achievable NODF–Barber-modularity locus and its Pareto frontier have closed forms. The Ferrers network uniquely maximizes NODF and also maximizes global Barber modularity exactly when \(2c\ge n-1\); below that threshold, a genuine nestedness–modularity tradeoff is unavoidable.
