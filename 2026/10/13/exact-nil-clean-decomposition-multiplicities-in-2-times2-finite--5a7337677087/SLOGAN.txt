For every \(A\in M_2(\mathbb F_q)\), the exact number of nil-clean decompositions is determined by nilpotency of \(A\) and \(A-I\), together with \(\operatorname{tr}A\), \(\det A\), and the number of rational eigenlines. This yields a complete multiplicity profile and shows that the nil-clean locus has size \(q^3+2q^2\) for even \(q\) and \(q^3+2q^2-1\) for odd \(q\).
