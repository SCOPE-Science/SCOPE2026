For the irreducible matrix pattern formed by a weighted directed cycle and nonzero loops at every vertex, the exact finite-field nilpotent realization count is \(\frac{(q-1)^n}{m}\frac{n!}{(h!)^m}\) when \(n=hm\), \(h\) is the characteristic-power part of \(n\), and \(m\mid(q-1)\), and is zero otherwise. All such realizations have one maximal Jordan block, while their complete pattern-preserving monomial-orbit spectrum is governed by the rotation periods of balanced necklaces.
