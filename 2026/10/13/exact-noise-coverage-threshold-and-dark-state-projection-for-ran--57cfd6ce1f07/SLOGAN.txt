In a complete bipartite quantum network with arbitrary nonzero rank-one weighted couplings, selective positive-rate dephasing leaves exactly \((r-1)_++(s-1)_+\) dark directions, where \(r,s\) count undephased, nonabsorbing sites in the two vertex classes. Complete eventual absorption of every initial state requires exactly \(a+b-3\) optimally placed dephasers, and the exact limiting state is the explicit coupling-weighted projection \(Q\rho_0Q\), independent of all positive dephasing and absorption strengths.
