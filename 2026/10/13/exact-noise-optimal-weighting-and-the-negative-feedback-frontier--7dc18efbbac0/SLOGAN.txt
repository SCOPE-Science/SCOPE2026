For a noisy two-lag linear fixed-point iteration, the unique variance-optimal convex weight is \(p_*(a)=\sqrt{1-a}/(1+\sqrt{1-a})\) whenever \(-3<a<1\) and \(a\ne0\). Optimizing the weight extends the exact negative-feedback stability frontier from \(-2\) to \(-3\), but the best stationary variance still blows up quadratically at the new boundary.
