Tafazolian's first-layer normalizer can be computed exactly: it has order \(4p^2\) except on the second normal form's locus \((a/b)^2\in\mathbf F_p\), where it jumps to \(2p^2(p+1)\). On that exceptional locus the quotient by \(C_p^2\) is dihedral of order \(2(p+1)\), arising from the norm-one torus of \(\mathbf F_{p^2}/\mathbf F_p\).
