# Verification

The initiating full text was checked at Theorem 1.7, Corollary 1.8, and Remark 9.4.

Theorem 1.7 gives \(S\cong C_p^2\) and the two normal forms for \(V\). Corollary 1.8 gives
\[
N_{\operatorname{Aut}(X)}(S)/S
\cong
\operatorname{Stab}_{k^*\rtimes C_2}(V)
\]
with
\[
D_\lambda(a,b)=(\lambda^{-1}a,\lambda b),
\qquad
J(a,b)=(b,a).
\]
Remark 9.4 supplies the universal subgroup generated by \(D_{-1}\) and \(J\), hence the lower bound \(4p^2\), but does not give the exact classification below.

For the first normal form \(V=A(1,1)\), one has
\[
D_\lambda(1,1)=(\lambda^{-1},\lambda).
\]
This lies on the same coefficient line only when \(\lambda^2=1\), so the stabilizer is exactly the Klein four group after adjoining \(J\).

For the second normal form, set
\[
v_1=a(1,1),\qquad v_2=b(1,-1),\qquad r=a/b.
\]
Direct substitution gives the matrix
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
c&d/r\\
rd&c
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
c=\frac{\lambda+\lambda^{-1}}2,\quad
d=\frac{\lambda^{-1}-\lambda}2.
\]
Preservation of the \(\mathbf F_p\)-span requires
\[
c,\ rd,\ d/r\in\mathbf F_p.
\]
If \(d\ne0\), division of the last two quantities gives \(r^2\in\mathbf F_p\). Thus outside that locus only \(\lambda=\pm1\) occur.

When \(r^2=\delta\in\mathbf F_p\), the assumption \(r\notin\mathbf F_p\) makes \(\delta\) a nonsquare. Writing \(d=ur\) gives
\[
c^2-\delta u^2=1.
\]
Since
\[
\lambda=c-ur,
\]
the allowed \(\lambda\)'s are precisely the norm-one elements in \(\mathbf F_{p^2}^*\), a cyclic group of order \(p+1\). The relation
\[
JD_\lambda J=D_{\lambda^{-1}}
\]
then makes the full quotient dihedral of order \(2(p+1)\).

This is a uniform proof for all odd \(p\); finite experiments are not used as evidence. The result does not determine automorphisms outside the normalizer.
