For the Markovian supercoupon collector displaying \(n-1\) of \(n\) elements each round, all uncollected \(s\)-subsets are exactly those containing every element omitted so far. Consequently, for every \(1\le s\le n-1\), its entire uncollected-count process reduces to ordinary occupancy on \(n-1\) symbols, and its complete collection time is one plus a sum of \(s\) independent geometric waiting times, with exact finite laws and a Gaussian proportional-size limit.
