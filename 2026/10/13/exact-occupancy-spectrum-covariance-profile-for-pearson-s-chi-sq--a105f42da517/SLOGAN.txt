For an equiprobable multinomial sample, the covariance between Pearson's chi-square and the number \(K_r\) of cells observed exactly \(r\) times is governed by one explicit quadratic \(\Delta_{n,m,r}\). In particular, singleton count is exactly orthogonal to Pearson's statistic at \(n=2m\), while in the fixed-load limit the sign profile is controlled by \((\lambda-r)^2-r\).
