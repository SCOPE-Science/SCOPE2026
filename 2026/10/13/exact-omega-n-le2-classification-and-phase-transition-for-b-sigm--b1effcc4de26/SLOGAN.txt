For coprime \(a>b\ge1\) and \(k\ne0\), every solution of \(b\sigma(n)=an+k\) with \(n\ge2\) and \(\Omega(n)\le2\) is given by one prime equation, one quadratic prime-square equation, or the factorization \(((a-b)p-b)((a-b)q-b)=ab-(a-b)k\). The low-multiplicity solution set is infinite exactly for \((a,b,k)=(r+1,r,r(r+1))\) with \(r\) prime, when the solutions are precisely \(rq\) with \(q\ne r\) prime.
