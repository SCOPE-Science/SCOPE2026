For a faithful qubit arm and a rank-one reward projector, the Umegaki divergence to a higher-reward half-space reduces exactly to one strictly convex scalar minimization. For the qubit instance in arXiv:2609.40339v1 this gives \(D_{\mathrm{inf}}=1.117330054762\ldots\) nats and an analytic maximum exploration-coefficient saving of \(25.6830513176\ldots\%\) for the paper's quantum-memory converse.
