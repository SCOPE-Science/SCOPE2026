For a two-eigenvalue positive-definite matrix, the one-Gaussian-probe FlexTrace effective-dimension estimator has the exact negative bias \(ab(a-b)^2/[(a+\zeta)(b+\zeta)(\sqrt{a(a+\zeta)}+\sqrt{b(b+\zeta)})^2]\). If the weaker eigenvalue \(b\) and regularization \(\zeta=\gamma b\) jointly vanish, that bias tends to \(1/(1+\gamma)\), asymptotically losing the weak mode's entire nonzero effective-dimension contribution and yielding noncommuting rank/regularization limits.
