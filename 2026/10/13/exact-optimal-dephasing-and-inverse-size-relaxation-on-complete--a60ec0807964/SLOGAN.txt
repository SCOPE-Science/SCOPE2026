Uniform site dephasing on a coherent complete quantum network has an exact cubic relaxation spectrum and a unique spectral-gap-optimal rate \(\gamma_*=\sqrt n\,(n-1)|g|/\sqrt{n-2}\). At fixed hopping and noise, its gap instead falls as \(2\gamma/n\), so adding complete-graph connections paradoxically slows the asymptotic equilibration of localized populations.
