# Review
## Correctness
PASS. Conditioning on the \(k\)-th order statistic gives independent lower and upper uniform groups. Their exact first and second sample-sum moments produce a quadratic conditional mean for the unbiased variance. Beta moments through order three give the covariance exactly, and standard marginal variance formulas give the correlation. Independent Dirichlet-spacing and conditional-beta replays reproduce the formulas and all sign boundaries.

## Value
PASS. The result gives a complete rank-resolved dependence law between two basic same-sample summaries. It identifies the exact negative/zero/positive transition, proves median orthogonality for odd samples, and yields a nontrivial bulk correlation curve whose maximal magnitude is \(\sqrt5/4\).

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest literature supplies exact moments of uniform order statistics and broad dependence/bound theory for order statistics, rather than this same-sample quadratic-dispersion coupling. The theorem is specific to a uniform parent distribution and to sample variance rather than standard deviation.
