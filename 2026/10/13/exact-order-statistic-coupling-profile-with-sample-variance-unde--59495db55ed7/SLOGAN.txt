For a uniform sample, the covariance between the \(k\)-th order statistic and the same-sample unbiased variance is \(k(n+1-k)(2k-n-1)/(6n(n-1)(n+1)(n+3))\). The profile changes sign exactly at the median rank, and bulk correlations converge to \(\sqrt5(2u-1)\sqrt{u(1-u)}\), whose largest magnitude is \(\sqrt5/4\).
