Mass-triggered repair intervals have an exact overlap law: finite multiplicity occurs exactly when \(L>U\), and the sharp bound is \(2+\lfloor U/(L-U)\rfloor\). The strongly convex silver-rate repair constants \(L=S_0\), \(U=S_0/4\) fall in the sharper \(L>2U\) regime, which forces consecutive separation and recovers the source's twofold counting bound.
