For a cycle permutation combined with a single idempotent that simultaneously merges \(0\to1\) and \(2\to3\), every unordered state-pair has an explicit constant-time shortest-merger formula. The maximum pair-merger distance is \(k^2+k-2\) for \(n=2k\) and \(k(k+1)+\lceil k/2\rceil\) for \(n=2k+1\), valid for every \(n\ge5\).
