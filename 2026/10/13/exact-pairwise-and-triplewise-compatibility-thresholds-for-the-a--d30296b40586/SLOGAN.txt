For the three Pauli measurements distorted by qubit amplitude damping with survival parameter \(q\), every pair becomes jointly measurable exactly at \(q\le1/2\), but the entire triple only at \(q\le1/3\). An explicit eight-outcome joint POVM and a rank-one-effect symmetrization prove both thresholds, creating a sharp nonunital Specker window \(1/3<q\le1/2\).
