For fixed \(n=\prod p_i^{e_i}>1\) and \(L=\operatorname{lcm}(e_i)\), the value of \(f_\alpha(n)=\prod p_i^{\gcd(e_i,\alpha)}\) depends on \(\alpha\) exactly through \(\gcd(\alpha,L)\). Hence the parameter map has least period \(L\), exactly \(\tau(L)\) values, and fiber multiplicities \(\varphi(L/d)\) for \(d\mid L\).
