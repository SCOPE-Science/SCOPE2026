For fixed \(n=\prod p_i^{\alpha_i}\), Bouderbala–Karras’s parameter sequence \(f_k(n)=\sum_i\gcd(k,\alpha_i)\) is controlled exactly by \(\gcd(k,L)\), where \(L=\operatorname{lcm}(\alpha_i)\). This gives the exact least period \(L\), every value’s multiplicity in one period, and sharp formulas for the extrema and mean.
