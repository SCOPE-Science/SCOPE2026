The seven-point set-homogeneous AGL1(7) hypergraph fails partial-isomorphism extension only at sizes 3 and 4: it becomes homogeneous again on every 5- and 6-point induced substructure. At the bad sizes the exact extension fractions are 1/2 for edge triples, 1/3 for nonedge triples, and 1/2 for both four-point types.\n
