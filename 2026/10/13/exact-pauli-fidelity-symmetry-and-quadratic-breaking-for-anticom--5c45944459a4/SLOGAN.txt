For a Clifford rotation generated by a Pauli \(L\), a static mutually anticommuting Pauli Hamiltonian error preserves *all* gate-conjugate Pauli fidelities exactly unless it drives both quadratures of an \(L\)-rotated Pauli pair; the only other escape is an exact sine-zero time. The mean-squared symmetry defect is exactly \(8s^4\sum(a b)^2\), revealing quadratic interference invisible to first-order coherent-noise selection rules.
