# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof reduces the new content to a sharp arithmetic statement about \(n|\sin(\pi\sqrt3n)|\). The integer identity \(m^2-3n^2\ne0\) gives the lower bound, while the Pell sequence generated by \(2+\sqrt3\) attains it separately in both parity classes. Inserting this into the source's two published band-width asymptotics gives the claimed constants. The finite checker is corroborative only.

## Originality
PASS. The closest literature is the primary Baradaran–Exner paper itself: it derives the two reciprocal-sine width formulas and concludes boundedness from quadratic irrationality, but it does not state an exact limsup, identify Pell resonances, or separate the sharp envelopes by parity. Targeted searches under equivalent spectral and Diophantine formulations found no stronger covering statement. Residual risk is an unindexed equivalent calculation.

## Value
PASS. The source highlights that this family has fluctuating high-energy widths. The exact limsup shows that arbitrarily high bands retain order-one energy width along sparse arithmetic resonances and quantifies the maximal envelope, including its parity factor. This is a structural refinement of the source's high-energy picture rather than a routine numerical increment.

## Closest literature and limitations
The claim depends on the family-(iii) high-energy expansion proved in arXiv:2312.13827v1 and does not rederive the underlying Floquet determinant. It is a limsup theorem, not convergence of all widths. The strongest remaining originality risk is an equivalent result hidden under different terminology.
