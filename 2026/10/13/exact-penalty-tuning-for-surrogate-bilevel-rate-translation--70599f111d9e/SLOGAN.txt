For the affine-in-\(\rho\) surrogate rate certificates identified by Shtern--Taiwo, a uniform Lipschitz certificate turns the translated outer bound into a strictly convex one-variable tuning problem exactly when \(a_1>0\) and \(b_0>0\). Its unique optimum is the positive root of \(b_1\rho^4+b_0\rho^3=\ell^2b_0^2/(2a_1^2)\), with a cube-root closed form when \(b_1=0\); the remaining cases have only boundary infima or a flat bound.
