For Das's map that adds \(k\) at primes and otherwise takes the largest prime divisor, all exact two-cycles have the explicit divisor criterion \(p\mid k\) and \(P(k/p+1)\le p\). Exact three-cycles occur precisely at Fermat-prime parameters \(k=2^{2^t}-1\), where the unique cycle is \(2\to k+2\to2k+2\to2\).
