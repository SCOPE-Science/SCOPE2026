The ordinary two-step Lanczos exponential approximation has an exact all-time positivity phase diagram on two independent reversible Markov links: it stays nonnegative if and only if \(R^3\varepsilon^4+2R(R^2-2R+2)\varepsilon^2+2-R\ge0\). Every rate contrast \(R>2\) permits a rare fast-state probability to become negative after a unique finite time, even though the exact evolution remains stochastic and nonnegative.
