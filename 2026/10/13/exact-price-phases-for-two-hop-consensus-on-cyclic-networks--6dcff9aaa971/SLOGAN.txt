The exactly optimal continuous-time consensus rate on the two-hop square of a cycle has three explicit price phases, with sharp boundary optimizer families, even when every edge conductance is independently chosen. An odd cycle can optimally discard all nearest-neighbor edges when second-neighbor edges are cheap, but an even cycle cannot; for each fixed price \(0<t<4\), the large-cycle rate improves over nearest-neighbor-only consensus by a factor approaching \(4/t\).
