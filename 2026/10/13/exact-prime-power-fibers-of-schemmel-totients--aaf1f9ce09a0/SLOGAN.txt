For every Schemmel order \(r\), every prime-power fiber \(S_r^{-1}(\ell^t)\) is exactly a finite subset-sum construction from the primes \(r+\ell^j\), with one explicit exceptional factor when \(\ell=r+1\). At prime targets this makes infinitude for even \(r\) equivalent to the prime-pair problem at gap \(r\).
