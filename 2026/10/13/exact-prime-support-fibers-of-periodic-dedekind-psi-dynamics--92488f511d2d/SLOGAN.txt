For the Dedekind-psi pseudo-aliquot map \(T(n)=\psi(n)-n\), every prescribed periodic word of prime-divisor supports has an exact finite feasibility test: its rooted periodic points form either the empty set or one canonical point multiplied freely by powers of primes present at every step. The least dynamical period equals the least support-word period, and every nonempty fiber has an explicit polylogarithmic counting law.
