# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The only degree-three irreducible component is the smooth Fermat cubic; its preservation forces preservation of the three-line union defined by x^3+y^3 and its unique concurrence point [0:0:1]. Any point stabilizer fixing that point has first two coordinates depending only on (x,y) and third coordinate ell(x,y)+mu z. Comparing coefficients of z^2 in Fermat equation forces ell=0, and cubic coefficient forces both binary-cubic and z^3 multipliers equal. The binary cubic has 3 distinct roots in P^1, full projective permutation group S3. Its lifts to PGL3 preserving the total Fermat equation have a 3-element kernel, producing exactly 18 monomial/swap maps, structure C3 x S3. Independently enumerating phase-normalized monomial elements reproduced 18. The degree-seven unique extra line not through concurrency point must be fixed, which imposes equal y/z exponents and excludes swaps, leaving precisely 3 maps. This establishes completeness over C rather than only a finite sample.
