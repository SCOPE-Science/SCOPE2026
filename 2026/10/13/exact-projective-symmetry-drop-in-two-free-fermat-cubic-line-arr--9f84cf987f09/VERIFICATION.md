# Verification

The proof supplies the infinite upper bound: preservation of component degrees and of the unique triple concurrence point forces a block form, and comparison of coefficients in the Fermat cubic eliminates every shear term. The remaining binary cubic has three distinct roots, whose projective stabilizer is \(S_3\).

The standalone verifier enumerates the explicit monomial lifts modulo common scalar. It checks that there are \(18\) stabilizers for \(D_6\), that their action on the three line components has image of order \(6\) and central kernel of order \(3\), and that a complementary subgroup has the \(S_3\) relations. It then imposes preservation of \(y+z=0\) and obtains exactly \(3\) elements, all diagonal.

The finite enumeration is not used as an exhaustive search over \(\operatorname{PGL}_3(\mathbb C)\); exhaustiveness comes from the symbolic reduction in the proof. No claim is made about automorphisms of complements or about arbitrary subsets of Fermat flex tangents.
