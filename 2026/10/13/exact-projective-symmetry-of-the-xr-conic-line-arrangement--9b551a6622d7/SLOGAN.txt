For the explicit free conic-line arrangement \(\mathcal{XR}\) of Măcinic–Pokora, the full projective stabilizer is exactly \(S_3\): the six-line skeleton has \(S_4\)-symmetry, and the six conics cut it to one point stabilizer. This symmetry expands the source's single free added line to a three-line orbit and its single plus-one generated added line to a six-line orbit of projectively equivalent examples.
