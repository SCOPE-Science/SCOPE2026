A diagonal spin-1 mixed state has an exact Hilbert--Schmidt quantumness formula: outside the classical parabola \(x^2+2b\le1\), its unique closest classical state is determined by the single cubic \(3y^3+(6b-1)y-2x=0\). The optimizer is a rank-three equal mixture of three azimuthally spaced coherent states, and three coherent states are provably minimal.
