For every deterministic qutrit measure-and-prepare channel whose classical map has exactly two image points, the qutrit-Clifford randomized-benchmarking second moment has a unique unit-modulus eigenvalue and largest nontrivial modulus exactly \(1/6\). The full spectrum depends only on the number \(r\in\{0,1,2\}\) of fixed basis labels, even though the channel is nonunital and its unital projection is not positive.
