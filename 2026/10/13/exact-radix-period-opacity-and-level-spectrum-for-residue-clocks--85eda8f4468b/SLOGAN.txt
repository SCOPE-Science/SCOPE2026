For the distinct-symbol periodic residue clock \(u(n)=n\bmod m\), normalized radix-\(b\) prefix opacity vanishes exactly when \(b\mid m\); otherwise it equals \(2^{2-L}\), where \(L\ge2\) is the smallest integer with \(b^L-b\ge m\). Every positive level \(2^{2-L}\) is realized by exactly \((b-1)^2b^{L-2}\) positive periods.
