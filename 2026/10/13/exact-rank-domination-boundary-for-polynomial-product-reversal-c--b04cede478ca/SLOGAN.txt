For \(f=p(xy)\) and \(g=q(yx)\), universal rank domination is completely rigid: it holds exactly when \(p\mid q\) and either \(p(0)\ne0\) or \(tp\mid q\), and it automatically holds with constant \(1\). Within this family, rank domination fails to imply \(g\in(f)\) exactly when \(p(0)\ne0\), \(p\mid q\), and \(q(0)\ne0\).
