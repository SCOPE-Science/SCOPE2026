On the centered \(2\times2\) Frobenius quadratic, ERSO-FW with \(\eta_t=2/(t+1)\) identifies the full optimal rank exactly at iteration three and then follows an explicit gamma-product trajectory with \(f(X_t)\sim\Gamma(-2\sqrt2)^{-2}t^{-8-4\sqrt2}\). With exact line search, the same family reaches the optimizer in one iteration.
