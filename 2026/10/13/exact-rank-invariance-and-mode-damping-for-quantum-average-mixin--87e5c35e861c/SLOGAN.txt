For a uniform nonzero pendant extension of any finite symmetric Hamiltonian, quantum average mixing splits exactly into a fixed base block and a monotonically damped block, so its rank never changes with the pendant coupling. This yields rank \(m\) for every \(2m\)-vertex path comb and a sharp positive mixing gap \(\frac{n-2}{n(1+4g^2)}\) for \(K_n\circ_gK_1\), \(n\ge3\).
