Independent random reflection of a uniform response with retention probability \(p\) transforms Chatterjee's directional rank correlation by the exact law \(\xi(W_p\mid U)=(2p-1)^2\xi(V\mid U)+p(1-p)\xi(2\min(V,1-V)\mid U)\), for every bivariate copula. Its sharp attainable range is \([0,1-3p(1-p)]\); at a fair coin, both concordance correlations vanish while up to one-quarter of directional rank dependence can remain, with equality precisely when the folded response is determined by the predictor.
