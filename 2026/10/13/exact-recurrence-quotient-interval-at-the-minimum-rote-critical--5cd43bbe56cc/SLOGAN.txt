For each of the two complementary-symmetric Rote languages attaining the minimum critical exponent \(2+1/\sqrt2\), the normalized recurrence values \(R_v(m)/m\) have exactly the accumulation interval \([3+\sqrt2,\,5+3\sqrt2]\). In particular their recurrence quotient is \(5+3\sqrt2\), while the lower recurrence quotient is \(3+\sqrt2\).
