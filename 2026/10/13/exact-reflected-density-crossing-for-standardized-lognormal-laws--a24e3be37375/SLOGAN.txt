For a standardized lognormal law, the densities at equal distances to the right and left of the mean cross exactly once. The crossing radius is \(\kappa_\sigma=\sqrt{e^{-\sigma^2}+e^{-2\sigma^2}+e^{-3\sigma^2}}\), decreasing from \(\sqrt3\) to \(0\), with \(\kappa_\sigma=1\) at \(\sigma=0.78062658386453\ldots\).
