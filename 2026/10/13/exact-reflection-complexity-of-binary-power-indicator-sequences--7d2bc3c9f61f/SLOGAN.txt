For the binary word with ones exactly at integer powers \(b^k\), the number of distinct factors up to reversal has a closed formula on every geometric length interval. Its normalized reflection complexity converges only for \(b=2\); for every \(b\ge3\), the exact liminf is \(\frac12+\frac1{b(b-1)}\) and limsup is \(\frac12+\frac{b}{(b-1)(b+2)}\).
