The aperiodic increasing-run binary word \(0^1 1^1 0^2 1^2\cdots\) has exact factor complexity \(n(n+1)/2+1\) and reflection complexity \(\lceil n(n+1)/3\rceil+1\) for every positive \(n\). Its reflection-to-factor ratio tends to \(2/3\), disproving the aperiodic repair of a published dichotomy while distinguishing the already-known periodic counterexample.
