In the detailed-balance signaling network of Franco and Velázquez Example 3.8, species \(X_2\)—not \(X_3\)—is the robustly adapting product: \(N_2=e^{-E_2}\) is signal independent, while \(N_3=e^{E_1-E_3}s\) tracks a constant signal level \(s\). Under the paper's admissible signals, \(X_2\) converges to its set point and has \(n_2''(0)=K_1>0\), so the transient response is nontrivial for every positive parameter choice.
