For a cyclic \(n\)-state automaton whose second letter advances the first \(k\) states along a successor chain and fixes the rest, the shortest reset word has length exactly \(n(\lceil(n-1)/k\rceil-1)+k\) for every \(2\le k<n\). A greedy inverse-interval argument gives explicit optimal words and the quadratic-to-linear crossover as \(k\) grows.
