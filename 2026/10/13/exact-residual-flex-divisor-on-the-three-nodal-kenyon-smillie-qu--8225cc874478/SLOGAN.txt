On the three-nodal Kenyon–Smillie cusp quartic, the three smooth weight-one flexes that were previously listed only numerically form one irreducible cubic divisor over \(\mathbb Q\). Their \(x\)-coordinates satisfy \(106x^3+3x^2-6x-2=0\), their \(y\)-coordinates are \(-(106x^2+23x+3)/19\), and the cubic has Galois group \(\mathrm S_3\).
