For the adjacency quantum walk on \(C_n\square K_q\), \(n\ge5\), \(q\ge3\), the infinite-time average mixing spectrum has an explicit rational Fourier formula and fails to factor exactly at two arithmetic resonance families. Its rank is \(nq\) when \(n\) is odd, \(nq/2\) for generic even \(n\), and \(nq/2+3\) precisely when \(q=4\) and \(n\equiv2\pmod4\).
