For odd prime powers, every multiplicative-monoid retraction is either a zero-collapse power map or a valuation-preserving power map with one explicit root-of-unity parameter. Their exact number is \(1+2^{\omega(N)}+\prod_{\ell\mid N}(1+\ell^{\nu_\ell(N_-)})\), with \(N=p^{e-1}(p-1)\) and \(N_-=p^{e-2}(p-1)\).
