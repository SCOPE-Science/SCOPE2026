For a Pitman–Yor sample partition, the covariance between observed richness and empirical pair diversity is exactly \((1-\alpha)(\theta+\alpha)R_n/(\theta+1)^2\), where \(R_n=\prod_{j=2}^{n-1}(\theta+j+\alpha)/(\theta+j)\). In the Ewens/Dirichlet case this collapses to the sample-size-invariant value \(\theta/(\theta+1)^2\), and the stronger identity \(\mathbb E[K_n\mid1\not\sim2]-\mathbb E[K_n\mid1\sim2]=R_n\) explains the covariance structurally.
