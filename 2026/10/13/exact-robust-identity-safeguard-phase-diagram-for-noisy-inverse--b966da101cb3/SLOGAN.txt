For mode-aligned bounded errors in an inverse preconditioner, the unique best reliable identity safeguard is \(\omega=(\sqrt{1+(\mu+L)^2\delta^2}-1)/(\mu+L)\) in dimension two, but \(\omega=\delta\) in every dimension at least three, as long as \(\delta<(L-\mu)/(2\mu L)\). At that exact noise threshold finite safeguard tuning ceases to improve the worst first-step GMRES residual over the unpreconditioned system; above it, no finite safeguard is minimax-optimal.
