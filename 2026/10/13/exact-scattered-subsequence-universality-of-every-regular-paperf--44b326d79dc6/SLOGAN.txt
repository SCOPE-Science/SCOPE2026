For the regular paperfolding word, the scattered-subsequence universality index of every prefix has an exact eight-residue formula: at length \(8T\) it is \(3T-p_T\), while the other seven residue classes depend only on \(T\bmod2\). Its universal-prefix density is exactly \(3/8\), with a sharp discrepancy confined to \([-1,1/8]\) and an explicit ten-point limiting distribution.
