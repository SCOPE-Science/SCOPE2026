The binary Rudin–Shapiro prefix of length \(N\) contains every scattered binary word through length \(U(N)=5N/16+O(\log N)\), with the explicit error \(|U(N)-5N/16|\le s_2(N)\). At length \(2^m\), its universality index is exactly \(5\cdot2^{m-4}-1\) for even \(m\ge4\) and \(5\cdot2^{m-4}\) for odd \(m\ge5\).
