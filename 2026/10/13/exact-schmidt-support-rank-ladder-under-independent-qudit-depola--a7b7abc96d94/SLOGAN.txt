Independent local depolarization creates an exact Schmidt-support rank ladder for uniform-on-support coherence inputs: the negativity is \(\mathcal N_k=(k-1)[\Delta-kpq]_+/(2d^2)\), and increasing symmetric noise makes the best support visit \(d,d-1,\ldots,2\) at explicit thresholds \(t_k=d/(d+1+\sqrt{2k+1})\). This is an optimization over flat-support inputs, not arbitrary Schmidt spectra; the final all-input entanglement-death endpoint is known prior work.
