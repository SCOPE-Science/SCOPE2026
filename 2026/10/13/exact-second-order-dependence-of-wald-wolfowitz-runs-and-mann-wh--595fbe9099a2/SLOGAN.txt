The Wald–Wolfowitz run count is exactly uncorrelated with the signed Mann–Whitney rank-sum direction, but it is not second-order independent: under the continuous two-sample null, \(\operatorname{Cov}(R,Z^2)=-4(mn-1)/[(m+n)(m+n+1)]\). For proportional sample sizes this covariance stays nonzero while the correlation decays universally as \(-\sqrt{2/(m+n)}\).
