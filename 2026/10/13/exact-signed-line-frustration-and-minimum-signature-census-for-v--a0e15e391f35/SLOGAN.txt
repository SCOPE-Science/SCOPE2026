For a connected graph with pairwise vertex-disjoint cycles, the signed antisymmetric line graph has frustration \(\ell=\sum_{C\text{ odd}}(\min_{v\in C}d(v)-1)\). Every minimizing labeled signature is counted by the product \(\prod_{C\text{ odd}}\sum_{v\in C:\,d(v)=\min_{w\in V(C)}d(w)}(1+\mathbf1_{\{d(v)>2\}})\), giving an exact classification of all minimizing signings rather than just a bound.
