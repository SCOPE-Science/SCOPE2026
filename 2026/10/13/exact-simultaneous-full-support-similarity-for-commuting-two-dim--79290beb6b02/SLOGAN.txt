A commuting family of nonscalar \(2\times2\) matrices over \(\mathbb F_q\) admits exactly \((q-1)(q-|E\cup Z|)\) fully-nonzero simultaneous conjugates, where \(E\) records forbidden diagonal values and \(Z\) the characteristic-polynomial roots. Every such \(k\)-tuple has a common fully-nonzero basis for \(q>2k+2\), but infinite families at \(q=2k+2\) and a commuting pair over \(\mathbb F_5\) show that individual full-support bases need not combine into one.
