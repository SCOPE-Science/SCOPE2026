Among all communities with \(S\) species and \(N\) individuals, the largest expected number of species seen exactly once in a uniform size-\(k\) sample is attained with one possibly dominant species and \(S-1\) nearly equal abundances. At any nonreciprocal limiting sampling fraction \(\rho\in(0,1)\), the unique optimal abundance multiset eventually has one dominant species and \(S-1\) species of size \(\lfloor1/\rho\rfloor\), producing a sharp sampling-fraction staircase.
