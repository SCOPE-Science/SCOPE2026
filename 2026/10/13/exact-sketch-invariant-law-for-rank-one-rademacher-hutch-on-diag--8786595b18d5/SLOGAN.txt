With one Rademacher sketch column, Hutch++ on **every** positive diagonal matrix has an output distribution independent of its sketch signs; the exact law reduces to two weighted Rademacher sums and gives a positive, closed-form variance. On an isotropic \(2\times2\) matrix at the original three-query budget, Hutch++ misses the true trace by a fixed nonzero amount on every run even though ordinary Rademacher Hutchinson is exact.
