# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. For an affine pencil there is a single singular point, so the anti-admissibility condition reduces to one total-sum test. In rank two over \(\mathbf F_2\), the two directions not generated by the total sum must both occur an odd number of times, which is equivalent to even \(n\). For every odd prime, an explicit two-direction assignment gives both total-sum coordinates nonzero. For every rank at least three and every prime, a basis assignment with repeated \(e_1\) leaves the total sum with nonzero \(e_2\)- and \(e_3\)-coordinates. These constructions are surjective and prove every asserted threshold.

## Originality
PASS. The closest source defines the smooth spectrum and computes it for general-position arrangements and near pencils, while leaving the pure affine-pencil spectrum unstated. Its odd-multiplicity obstruction and rank-extension lemma do not by themselves determine the missing low-prime entries. Exact-statement, alias, broader-coverage, and implication searches found no covering statement. A closely related 2025 thesis was inspected in its near-pencil and abelian-cover sections and does not state this affine spectrum.

## Value
PASS. The result gives a natural complete benchmark for the new invariant, answers the spectrum question for the basic one-singular-point incidence type, and exhibits a parity reversal relative to near pencils. It also identifies the exact target-rank jump at which the characteristic-two obstruction disappears for odd pencils.

The result is restricted to affine pencils and elementary abelian targets; no general supersolvable-arrangement classification is claimed.
