For an affine pencil of \(n\) concurrent complex lines, the entire elementary-abelian smooth spectrum is explicit: it is \((2,\ldots,2)\) for even \(n\) and \((3,2,\ldots,2)\) for odd \(n\). The characteristic-two obstruction disappears as soon as the target rank reaches \(3\), and the pencil parity is the reverse of the near-pencil parity.
