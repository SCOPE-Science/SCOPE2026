On the scalar soft-threshold model \(h(x)=\lambda|x|\), the proximal nonlinear conjugate-gradient method can compute the exact minimizer \(x_k^+=0\) at every iteration yet deliberately step past that proximal hit. In an explicit basin the accepted iterates obey \(x_{k+1}=(1-1/\mu)x_k\), so one-step proximal identification becomes an arbitrarily slow geometric tail as \(\mu\) grows.
