On SPD quadratics, Hessian-corrected Heavy Ball has curvature-dependent momentum \(m_\lambda=\alpha-\theta\lambda\) and is stable on the whole spectral interval exactly when \(\alpha-\theta\mu<1\) and \(L(\beta+2\theta)<2(1+\alpha)\). The same quadratic calculation gives the exact complex-, positive-real-, negative-real-, and opposite-sign-root regions, exposing a sharp tradeoff between curvature damping and stability margin.
