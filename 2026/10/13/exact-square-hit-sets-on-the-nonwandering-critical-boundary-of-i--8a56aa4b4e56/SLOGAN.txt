On the full nonwandering boundary \(4ac-b^2+2b\in\{0,-4,-8\}\), the critical square-hit set of an integral quadratic is given by explicit coefficient tests. If the quadratic is irreducible, the only possible patterns are \(\varnothing\) or all indices for \(0\), \(\varnothing\) or exactly one parity for \(-4\), and \(\varnothing\), \(\{1\}\), or the full tail \(\{k\ge2\}\) for \(-8\); only the \(0\)-class can have every critical iterate square.
