For a uniform-tolerance star and a unit-weight clique on its leaves, the exact triangle-strengthened elliptope optimum differs from the cut-polytope slice optimum by \(\{n\varepsilon\}(1-\{n\varepsilon\})\) once \(n\ge4\) and \(\varepsilon\ge1/n\), and by zero otherwise. The sharp additive gap is \(1/4\), first possible on five vertices, whereas the bare elliptope retains a quadratic value throughout the initial tolerance range.
