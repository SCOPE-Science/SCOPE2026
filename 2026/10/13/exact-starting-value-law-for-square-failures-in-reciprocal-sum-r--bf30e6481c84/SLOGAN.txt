For fixed \(h\), the number of failed-square terms is exactly periodic in the starting value \(x_0\), with period dividing \(\prod_{p\mid h,\ p=2\text{ or }p\equiv1\,(4)}p^{v_p(h)+1}\). The exact densities are finite Chinese-remainder sums of first-hit counts for \(t\mapsto t+t^{-1}\) on \(\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_p)\), including collisions where several primes spoil the same term.
