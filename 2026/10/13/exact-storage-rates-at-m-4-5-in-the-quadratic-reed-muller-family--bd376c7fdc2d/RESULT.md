# Exact storage rates at \(m=4,5\) in the quadratic Reed--Muller family
## Finding

Let
\[
X_m=\operatorname{Sym}_m(\mathbb F_2),
\qquad
D=\frac{m(m+1)}2,
\]
and define the binary matrix
\[
M_m(S,T)=\mathbf 1\{\operatorname{rank}(S+T)\le1\},
\qquad S,T\in X_m.
\]
This is the full-parity matrix of the quadratic Reed--Muller storage family in the recent reformulation.

For the first two parameters in the original open range,
\[
\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_2}M_4=448,
\qquad
\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_2}M_5=13658.
\]
Therefore the exact storage-code dimensions are
\[
576\quad\text{and}\quad19110,
\]
and the exact rates are
\[
R_4=\frac9{16},
\qquad
R_5=\frac{9555}{16384}\approx0.58319091796875.
\]

Using the cut--cycle interpretation from the recent source, the corresponding bicycle-space dimensions are
\[
575\quad\text{and}\quad19109.
\]

For context, the complete exact rank sequence through this cutoff is
\[
1,4,28,448,13658
\]
for
\[
m=1,2,3,4,5.
\]

## Assumptions and scope

Zabokritskiy identifies the quadratic Reed--Muller construction with the Cayley graph on the additive group
\[
X_m=\operatorname{Sym}_m(\mathbb F_2)
\]
generated by the nonzero rank-one symmetric matrices. Every such matrix is uniquely
\[
xx^{\mathsf T}
\]
for a nonzero
\[
x\in\mathbb F_2^m.
\]
Thus the full-parity matrix is exactly the displayed \(M_m\), of order
\[
N_m=2^{m(m+1)/2}.
\]

The original Barg--Zemor open formulation starts at \(m\ge4\). The finding resolves only the finite cases \(m=4\) and \(m=5\); it does not settle the asymptotic question whether the rate tends to one.

## Proof

Fix an ordering of the \(D\) upper-triangular coordinates of a symmetric matrix and identify \(X_m\) with \(\mathbb F_2^D\). Under this identification, addition of symmetric matrices is bitwise xor.

For
\[
x=(x_1,\ldots,x_m)\in\mathbb F_2^m,
\]
write \(\nu(x)\in\mathbb F_2^D\) for the upper-triangular vectorization of \(xx^{\mathsf T}\). The diagonal entries of \(xx^{\mathsf T}\) are the coordinates of \(x\), so \(\nu\) is injective. Hence the rank-at-most-one symmetric matrices are exactly the \(2^m\) vectors
\[
\{\nu(x):x\in\mathbb F_2^m\}.
\]

Consequently the row of \(M_m\) indexed by \(t\in\mathbb F_2^D\) is the indicator vector of
\[
\{t+\nu(x):x\in\mathbb F_2^m\}.
\]
This gives every row of \(M_m\) without any omitted cases or probabilistic sampling.

The bundled verifier constructs all \(N_m\) such rows and performs exact Gaussian elimination over \(\mathbb F_2\). Rows are stored as arbitrary-precision integers and xor is the row operation. A new pivot is inserted only when its leading bit has not appeared previously; therefore the number of stored pivots is exactly the matrix rank.

For \(m=4\), the exhaustive elimination of all \(1024\) rows produces \(448\) pivots. For \(m=5\), the exhaustive elimination of all \(32768\) rows produces \(13658\) pivots. Thus
\[
\dim\ker M_4=1024-448=576,
\]
\[
\dim\ker M_5=32768-13658=19110.
\]
The storage-rate formula
\[
R_m=1-\frac{\operatorname{rank}M_m}{N_m}
\]
then gives the stated exact rates.

The recent source also proves, for this connected odd-degree graph,
\[
\operatorname{rank}M_m=N_m-1-\dim(\mathcal B_m\cap\mathcal B_m^\perp),
\]
where \(\mathcal B_m\) is the cut space. Substitution yields bicycle dimensions \(575\) and \(19109\).

## Relationship to prior work

Barg and Zemor introduced this second-order Reed--Muller candidate and explicitly left determination of its storage rate open for \(m\ge4\). Their numerical table concerns a different double-error-correcting BCH family, not the Reed--Muller family.

Zabokritskiy's October 2026 paper gives the exact symmetric-matrix reformulation, proves a general polynomial-method rank bound, and states the asymptotic rank problem as open. It does not give exact finite ranks for this family. Its bound at \(m=4\) is \(562\), compared with the exact rank \(448\); at \(m=5\) it is \(14893\), compared with \(13658\).

Schmidt's association-scheme paper supplies the natural rank relations and complex eigenvalue theory for symmetric bilinear forms. Its inspected treatment concerns valencies, character-sum eigenvalues, and \(d\)-codes; it does not state the modular \(\mathbb F_2\)-rank of the rank-at-most-one relation. Focused searches for the exact values, binary rank, bicycle-space dimensions, and the symmetric rank-one Cayley graph found no prior table containing these two ranks.

## Limitations

This is a finite exact result. It gives no recurrence and does not imply that the storage rate tends to one.

The exhaustive computation grows with
\[
2^{m(m+1)/2},
\]
so the same direct method is not claimed to be practical at the next parameter.

The finding concerns the binary rank of the full-parity matrix and its kernel code. It does not determine the nonlinear storage capacity of the graph.
