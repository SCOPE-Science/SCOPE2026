For a semisimple matrix over \(\mathbb F_q\), the exact number of strongly clean decompositions factors over the irreducible primary summands, with each nonspecial \(f\)-summand contributing the full idempotent count of \(M_{m_f}(\mathbb F_{q^{d_f}})\). In particular, such a matrix has a unique strongly clean decomposition exactly when it is idempotent.
