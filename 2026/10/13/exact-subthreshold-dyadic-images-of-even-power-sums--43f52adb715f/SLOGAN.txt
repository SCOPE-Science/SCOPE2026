For every even exponent \(k\ge6\), all subthreshold sums of \(k\)-th powers modulo \(2^a\) form an explicitly classified union of residue cylinders and recursively scaled copies, with exact counts and a periodic counting discrepancy. Over \(\mathbb Z_2\), the attainable set has Haar measure \(t/[2^{\nu_2(k)+2}(1-2^{-k})]\) and exactly one boundary point.
