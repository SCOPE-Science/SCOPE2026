# Review: exact target-collision counts for geometric exponent sets

## Correctness — PASS
The proof eliminates all noncanonical lower-prefix sums by a base-\(b\) carry lower bound. It then accounts exhaustively for the two alternative allocations of the upper pair, plus the one-split ternary representations. The small case \(n=2\) is enumerated separately. A directly enumerating verifier passed 1911 finite cases; universality is supplied by the written argument rather than the computation.

## Originality — PASS, with access risk
The closest full-text primary source is Fonollosa, arXiv:2607.08366, which determines the least valid modulus in base two. The present theorem instead gives exact counts for all \(b\ge3\) and every factor \(q\) in moduli \(q b^{n-2}\). The related companion arXiv:2607.09949 describes a general transform criterion, but only its abstract was accessible; it was not judged noncovering on the basis of snippets alone. Published-record and prior-claim searches did not identify a stronger statement, though unindexed equivalent work remains a risk.

## Value — PASS
The result classifies every target fiber in a natural two-top-digit family of cyclic moduli, exactly identifies which moduli isolate the target, and explains the sudden ternary exceptional multiplicity. This supplies a structural base-adic classification rather than a routine parameter substitution in the existing binary minimum-modulus theorem. It does not address globally least moduli.
