For the geometric exponent set \(\{b^i-1:0\le i<n\}\) in every base \(b\ge3\), the exact number of target-summing indexed \(n\)-multisets modulo \(q b^{n-2}\) is \(1+2\mathbf1_{q\mid(b-1)}+(n-3)_+\mathbf1_{b=3,\ q\mid4}\). Thus uniqueness is governed entirely by divisors of \(b-1\), except for one sharply delimited ternary splitting obstruction.
