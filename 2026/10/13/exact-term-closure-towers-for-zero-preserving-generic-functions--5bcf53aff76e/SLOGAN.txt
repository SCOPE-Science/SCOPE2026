For a free finite-field vector space expanded by zero-preserving generic operations of arities \(r_1,\ldots,r_s\), the function-depth dimensions satisfy the exact recurrence \(d_{n+1}=m-s+\sum_j q^{r_jd_n}\). A single unary operation over \(\mathbb F_2\) on one generator therefore produces the tower \(1,2,4,16,65536,\ldots\).
