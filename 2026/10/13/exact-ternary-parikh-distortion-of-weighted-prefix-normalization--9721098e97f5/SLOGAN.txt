For ternary standard weights, weighted prefix normalization can change a Parikh vector by at most \(4\lfloor(n-2)/2\rfloor\) in \(\ell_1\)-distance, and this is attained for every length \(n\ge2\). More generally, every factor-weight-equivalent pair differs by \((t,-2t,t)\) with \(|t|\le\lfloor(n-2)/2\rfloor\).
