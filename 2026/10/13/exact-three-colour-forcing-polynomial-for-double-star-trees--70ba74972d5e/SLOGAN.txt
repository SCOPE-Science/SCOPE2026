For every double-star tree with at least two leaves at each centre, the complete three-colour forced-colouring probability is \(\operatorname{FC}_3(D_{m,n};p)=6p^{m+n}\bigl[2^{m+n}p^2+(2^{m+n+1}-2^m-2^n)p(1-3p)+(2^{m+n}-2^m-2^n)(1-3p)^2\bigr].\) The coefficient for two initially blank centres corrects for conflicting simultaneous forcing, giving a closed formula for the entire two-parameter family.
