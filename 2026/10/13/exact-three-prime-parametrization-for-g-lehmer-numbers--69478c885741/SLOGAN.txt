Every odd \(G\)-Lehmer number with exactly three prime factors already satisfies \(\Phi(n)=\mathcal F(n)\), and the whole layer is indexed by factor pairs of either \(t^2+t+1\) or \(s^2-s+1\) with explicit congruence-prime constraints. Hence an odd example with \(\Phi(n)\) a proper divisor of \(\mathcal F(n)\) must have at least four distinct prime factors.
