For every regular graph, perfect state transfer between the apexes of its double cone under the symmetric normalized-Laplacian quantum walk is governed by an explicit \(2\)-adic inequality in the base order, degree, and presence of the apex edge; all transfer times are determined exactly. With nonadjacent apexes the condition reduces to \(k\equiv0\pmod4\), while adding the apex edge can eliminate transfer or create it for previously ineligible bases.
