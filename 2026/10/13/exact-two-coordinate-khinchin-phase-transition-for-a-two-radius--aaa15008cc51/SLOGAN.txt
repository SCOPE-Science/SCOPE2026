For the variance-one symmetric four-point perturbation \(X_d=\varepsilon R_d/\sqrt{1+d^2}\) with \(R_d\in\{1-d,1+d\}\), the optimal two-coordinate Khinchin coefficient vector changes sharply at the unique root \(d_\ast\approx0.813606502648331\) of \(d^3+4d^2+d-4=0\). Equal weights minimize below the threshold, while above it the unique minimizing ratio is \(b/a=(1-d)/(1+d)\), resolving this natural two-coordinate instance of the complementary coefficient regime.
