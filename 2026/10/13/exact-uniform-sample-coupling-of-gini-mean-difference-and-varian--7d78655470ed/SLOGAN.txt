For iid uniform data, the sample Gini mean difference and unbiased sample variance have exact correlation \(\sqrt2(n+2)/\sqrt{(n+3)(2n+3)}\), increasing to one. More strongly, the normalized contrast \(G_n/\ell-2S_n^2/\ell^2-1/6\) is a degenerate order-two U-statistic with exact variance \(1/(90n(n-1))\).
