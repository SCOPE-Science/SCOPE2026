# Review
## Correctness
PASS. Conditioning on both observed extremes leaves a uniform without-replacement sample from the interior lattice. Exact finite-population moments of its sum and sum of squares give the displayed regression. The result does not depend on the minimum, so it remains valid after conditioning only on the range. Exact subset enumeration supplies an independent finite replay but is not used as the all-parameter proof.

PASS. Sample range and sample variance are basic dispersion measures, and conditioning on observed extremes is a natural finite-population calibration question. The exact disappearance of the ambient population size and the explicit lattice correction provide a concise structural distinction between finite without-replacement and continuous-uniform sampling.
