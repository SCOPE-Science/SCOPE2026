For binary cyclic three-symbol read codes of length \(n\ge6\), with exactly two ones in every word and read distance at least six, the optimal size is \(m(3m-1)/2\) when \(3\mid n\), and \(\lfloor nm/2\rfloor\) otherwise, where \(m=\lfloor n/3\rfloor\). A sharp graph-theoretic degree defect at multiples of three and explicit constructions settle every length.
