For a star-Gramian linear system, the exact mean number of Gramian-selectable Kaczmarz projections until a complete post-center leaf sweep is \(\binom{m+w}{m-1}+\binom{m+w-1}{m-1}-1\), versus \((m+w)(p_R^{-1}-1)/w\) for ordinary norm-weighted randomized Kaczmarz, where \(p_R=\prod_{r=1}^{m}r/(r+w)\). This verifiable exact-solution certificate is sensitive to row scaling even though the underlying projection maps and Gramian zero pattern are unchanged; it is not necessarily the earliest exact-solution time.
