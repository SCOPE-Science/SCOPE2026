For every \(n\ge3\), the maximum number of binary length-\(n\) words having exactly the same four consecutive two-gram counts is \(\frac{n-1}{\lceil(n-1)/4\rceil}\binom{\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor-1}{\lceil(n-1)/4\rceil-1}\binom{\lceil(n-1)/2\rceil-1}{\lceil(n-1)/4\rceil-1}\), asymptotic to \(2^{n+1}/(\pi(n-1))\). All maximizing profiles have equal numbers of \(01\) and \(10\) transitions, and their exact loop-count and parity-dependent equality structure is classified.
