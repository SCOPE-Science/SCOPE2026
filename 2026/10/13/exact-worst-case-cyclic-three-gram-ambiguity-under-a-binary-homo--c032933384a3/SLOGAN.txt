The largest number of indexed homopolymer-free cyclic binary words sharing an identical three-gram multiplicity profile is given exactly, at every length, by a one-dimensional maximum of products of two binomial coefficients. Every profile fiber has size \(\frac{n}{s}\binom{s}{a}\binom{s}{b}\), and the largest fiber at fixed \(s\) occurs when the two detour counts differ by at most one.
