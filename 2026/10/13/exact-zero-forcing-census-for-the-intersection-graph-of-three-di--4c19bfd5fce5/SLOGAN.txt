The intersection graph of the nonzero proper subspaces of \(\mathbb F_3^3\) has \(Z=M=\operatorname{mr}=13\) and exactly \(1{,}246{,}986\) minimum zero forcing sets. Its complete zero forcing polynomial is explicit, and those minimum sets split into exactly \(254\) orbits under the natural \(\operatorname{PGL}(3,3)\)-action.
