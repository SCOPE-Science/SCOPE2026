For the Hermitian-relative curves \(X^{s+1}+\eta Y^{s+1}+Y^sZ+YZ^s=0\) with \(\eta\in\mathbb F_{s^2}\setminus\mathbb F_s\), the complete zeta numerator is \((1+sT)^{s^2/p-s}(\sum_{j=0}^{p-1}(-sT)^j)^{s^2/p}\), where \(s=p^f\). This determines their point counts over every finite extension from a characteristic-\(p\) Hermitian descent and is independent of \(\eta\).
