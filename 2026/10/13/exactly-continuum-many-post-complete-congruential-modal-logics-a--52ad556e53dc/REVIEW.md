# Review of Exactly continuum many Post-complete congruential modal logics are neighborhood-unsound

## Correctness
PASS. Krawczyk’s base Boolean algebra is the finite-cofinite algebra on \(\omega\); its atoms and middle elements are countably infinite, and the construction ranges over all bijections between them. The set of such bijections has cardinality exactly \(2^{\aleph_0}\). Krawczyk proves both minimality and pairwise distinctness of the resulting varieties and proves that no neighborhood frame validates the corresponding logic. Fritz’s theorem gives exactly \(2^{\aleph_0}\) C-Post complete congruential modal logics in total, so the lower and upper bounds coincide.

## Originality
PASS. The closest source, Krawczyk 2026, formulates the new family as “uncountable” and proves injectivity of the family indexed by all bijections; it does not state the exact continuum cardinality of the neighborhood-unsound subclass. Fritz 2016 proves the continuum total count but did not establish the existence of unsound C-Post complete logics. The companion Gyenis–Krawczyk preprint treats neighborhood-complete logics rather than the unsound subclass. Searches for the exact cardinality formulation and its standard aliases did not locate a covering result.

## Value
PASS. The exact number is the quantity asked for in Fritz’s open problem. Replacing an unspecified uncountable lower bound by the maximum possible cardinality \(2^{\aleph_0}\) gives a CH-independent exact answer for the unsound branch and removes a genuine cardinality ambiguity left by the source’s wording. The step is short, but it closes the explicit “how many?” question rather than introducing an arbitrary parameter slice.

## Closest literature and limitations
Krawczyk’s 2026 article supplies the construction and unsoundness theorem; Fritz’s 2016 theorem supplies the global continuum upper bound. The companion 2026 complete-side preprint is adjacent but does not subsume this statement. The exact size of the neighborhood-complete C-Post complete subclass remains outside this claim.
