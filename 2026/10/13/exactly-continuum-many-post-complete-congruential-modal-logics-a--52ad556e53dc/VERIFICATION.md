The exact-cardinality proof was checked from the definitions and theorem statements in the cited sources.

1. Krawczyk’s Boolean algebra is the finite-cofinite algebra on \(\omega\); its atom set is the countably infinite singleton family, and its middle set is countably infinite and bijective with the atoms.
2. The index set of all bijections between two countably infinite sets has size \(2^{\aleph_0}\). An explicit lower-bound injection swaps the pair \(2n,2n+1\) according to membership of \(n\) in a chosen subset of \(\omega\); the graph of each permutation gives the upper bound.
3. Krawczyk proves that distinct indices give distinct generated varieties and that each generated variety is minimal. He also proves that no neighborhood frame validates the corresponding logic.
4. Fritz’s theorem, reproduced in the 2026 article, gives exactly \(2^{\aleph_0}\) C-Post complete congruential modal logics in total.

These points yield matching lower and upper bounds for the neighborhood-unsound subclass. No computation or finite enumeration is used. The result does not settle the exact cardinality of the neighborhood-complete subclass.
