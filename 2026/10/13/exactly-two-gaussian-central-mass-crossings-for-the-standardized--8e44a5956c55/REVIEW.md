# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The centered exponential has two exact support regimes. On \(0<c\le1\), the derivative sign of the central-mass difference is governed by a ratio whose logarithmic derivative is \(c+\tanh c>0\); on \(c>1\), it is governed by a ratio whose logarithmic derivative is \(c-1>0\). Mills bounds show positivity at the support breakpoint and negativity in the far tail. These facts force exactly one nonzero crossing in each regime and establish the complete sign pattern. The numerical root brackets are supplementary.

PASS. The closest source proves the radius-one Gamma comparison and explicitly gives shape-one examples at radii \(1/2\) and \(2\) with opposite outcomes, but does not state the all-radius sign classification. The earlier broad variation-comparison paper is likewise fixed at one standard deviation. Candidate-specific semantic searches, exact-value searches, and comparison with the closest previously recorded probability results found no covering statement. Because the argument is elementary, an older formulation under different terminology remains a residual risk.

## Value
PASS. The source itself identifies the general-radius comparison as a point where the Gamma-versus-normal ordering can fail and chooses the exponential boundary member as an example. Resolving that member for every radius, with exactly two transitions and explicit root equations, is a natural complete classification of the first nontrivial case rather than an arbitrary parameter evaluation.
