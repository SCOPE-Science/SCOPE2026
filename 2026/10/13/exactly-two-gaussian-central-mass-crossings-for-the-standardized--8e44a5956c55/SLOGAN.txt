For the standardized rate-one exponential, Gaussian central-mass ordering changes sign exactly twice: at \(c_-=0.4961685495956\ldots\) and \(c_+=1.9329872875286\ldots\). The exponential is less concentrated than the normal below \(c_-\), more concentrated on \((c_-,c_+)\), and less concentrated again above \(c_+\).
