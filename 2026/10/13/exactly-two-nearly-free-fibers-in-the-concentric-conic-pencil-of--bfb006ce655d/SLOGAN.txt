In the fixed four-line square arrangement, the concentric smooth conic gives a nearly-free degree-six curve at exactly two parameters: the inscribed conic \(t=8\), with exponents \((3,3)\), and the previously known circumscribed conic \(t=16\), with exponents \((2,4)\). Every other smooth member has fourteen nodes and minimal Jacobian relation degree four, so is neither free nor nearly free.
