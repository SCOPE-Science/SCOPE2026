## correctness

PASS

For odd p<q the source squarefree Φ formula gives Φ(pq)=(p−β(p))(q−β(q)). Neither p nor q divides its own factor, and q>p+1≥p−β(p) excludes cross-divisibility from q to the p-factor, so failure occurs iff p|(q−β(q)). CRT yields two distinct reduced classes modulo 4p, each fixed-p progression contributes Li(X/p)/(2(p−1)). Independent exhaustive local check 328564 pairs pq≤2e6 found 45757 non-G-cyclic and no mismatches. Independently summing primes p≤1e7 gives C lower 0.27315666318973836 and rigorous integer telescoping tail <1e−7, so six decimals 0.273157; finite checks corroborate only, analytic argument proves asymptotic.
