Among odd squarefree semiprimes, G-cyclic numbers have density \(1\): the exceptional count is asymptotic to \(C X/\log X\), where \(C=\sum_{p\ge3}1/(p(p-1))=0.273157\ldots\). The obstruction is exact: for odd primes \(p<q\), the product \(pq\) is non-G-cyclic precisely when \(p\mid q-\left(\frac{-1}{q}\right)\).
