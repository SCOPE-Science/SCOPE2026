For ordered positive \(D(n)\)-triples, the split-cubic curve has \(j=1728\) exactly when the middle entry is the harmonic mean of the outer two, equivalently when the three defining squares form an arithmetic progression. The family \(\{6s,8s,12s\}\) with \(n=-47s^2\) realizes this locus at every scale, and its good reductions have order \(p+1\) for every \(p\equiv3\pmod4\).
