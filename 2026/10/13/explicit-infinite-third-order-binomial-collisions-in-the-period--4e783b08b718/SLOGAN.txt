The period-doubling factors \(\mathtt{0101010001}\) and \(\mathtt{1000101010}\) are distinct but \(3\)-binomially equivalent, and every pair of their substitution iterates remains so. This gives explicit collisions at every length \(10\cdot2^t\) and proves \(\mathsf b_{\mathbf{pd}}^{(3)}\prec\mathsf p_{\mathbf{pd}}\).
