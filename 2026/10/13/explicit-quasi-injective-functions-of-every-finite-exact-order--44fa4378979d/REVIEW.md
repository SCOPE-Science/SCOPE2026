## Correctness
PASS. The odd part of an integer is invariant under the constructed map, while the \(2\)-adic exponent evolves exactly under the finite-state map \(g_m\). Before the \(m\)-th iterate, the two tail states remain distinct and record whether the original exponent was a square. Equality on all multiples would therefore force a nonzero shift to preserve the square indicator eventually, which is impossible because \(t^2+d=u^2\) has only finitely many solutions for fixed \(d>0\). At the \(m\)-th iterate the two chains both hit zero, and the explicit scalings \(2^{2m-1}\) and \(2^{2m}\) then agree on every multiplier.
