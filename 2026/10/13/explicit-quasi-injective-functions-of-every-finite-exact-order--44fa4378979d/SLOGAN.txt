Every finite quasi-injectivity order occurs: for each \(m\ge2\), an explicit \(2\)-adic function \(f_m\) has quasi-injective iterates \(f_m,\ldots,f_m^{(m-1)}\) but a non-quasi-injective \(m\)-th iterate. The construction answers Pongsriiam\'s Question 17 by coupling two length-\(m-1\) chains to the non-eventually-periodic square indicator.
