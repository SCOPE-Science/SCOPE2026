Since \(h_0-s=r-1\), the first allowed count is \(h_0\). Positivity makes the first \(h_0\) powers the smallest possible such sum, so \(M_k-s\) is bad whenever positive and the lower bound follows. The inspected primary theorem gives equality at \(k=1\), and \(M_k\ge M_1\).
