With one hypercube direction priced \(t\) times as much as the others, the unique resistance-optimal conductance ratio grows linearly in dimension for every fixed \(t<1\), equals one at \(t=1\), and collapses as \((d-1)/(2\sqrt{(t-1)2^{d-1}})\) for every fixed \(t>1\). Every finite optimum retains all edges; the sharp price-one boundary is asymptotic.
