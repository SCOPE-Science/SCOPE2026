For iid exponential data, the sample maximum \(M_n\) and unbiased sample variance \(S_n^2\) have the exact positive covariance \(\bigl(H_n^2+H_n^{(2)}-2H_n/n\bigr)/((n-1)\lambda^3)\). Their correlation nevertheless decays as \((\sqrt3/(2\pi))(\log n)^2/\sqrt n\), quantifying asymptotic separation between extreme and quadratic dispersion.
