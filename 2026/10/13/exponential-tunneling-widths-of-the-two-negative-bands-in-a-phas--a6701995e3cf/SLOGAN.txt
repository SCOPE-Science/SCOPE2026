In the phase-twisted square quantum graph, the two negative Bloch bands have exact leading tunneling widths \(8t^2e^{-t\ell}\) and \(8u^2e^{-u\ell}\), with \(t=\tan(\mu/2)\) and \(u=\tan(\mu/2+\pi/4)\). The quasimomentum splitting is already of order \(e^{-\kappa\ell}\), correcting the stronger remainder order printed in the source.
