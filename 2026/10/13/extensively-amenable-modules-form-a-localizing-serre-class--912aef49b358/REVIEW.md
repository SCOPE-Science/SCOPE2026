# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The finite-support step in the directed-union proof was reconstructed directly from the symmetric-algebra Følner definition: every finite-dimensional test subspace of the smash product involves only finitely many vectors of the union, so directedness places the complete test in one stage. The source's exact-sequence two-of-three theorem then gives finite coproducts, submodules, quotients, and extensions. Arbitrary coproducts and the radical construction follow without additional unproved hypotheses.

## Originality
PASS. The closest source, arXiv:2610.00364v1, supplies the definition and exact-sequence theorem but the inspected text does not state the arbitrary-module directed-union/direct-sum closure or the localizing Serre and maximal-radical package. Older group-action work proves direct-union permanence for actions, covering a permutation-module special case after Bartholdi's comparison, but not arbitrary modules. published-finding corpus and targeted searches using categorical aliases did not locate a stronger covering statement.

## Value
PASS. A localizing Serre structure is materially stronger organizational information than isolated finite-extension permanence: it produces a functorial largest extensively amenable submodule \(T_{\mathrm{EA}}(M)\) and a quotient with no nonzero extensively amenable submodule for every \(M\). This supplies a canonical torsion-theoretic decomposition for the new notion and can be used without restricting to permutation modules.

## Closest literature and limitations
The direct-union portion has a known group-action analogue, so no novelty is claimed for that special case. The result does not address products, inverse limits, general non-injective filtered colimits, noncocommutative Hopf algebras, or non-field bases. An unindexed categorical restatement remains a residual originality risk.
