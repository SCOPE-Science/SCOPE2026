# Verification

The universal proof was checked symbolically. For a directed union, each finite-dimensional testing space in the smash product has finite polynomial support and therefore lies in one directed stage. The stage Følner witness embeds unchanged in the union. The short-exact-sequence theorem then yields finite direct sums and all Serre operations; arbitrary direct sums are directed unions of finite partial sums.

For the radical, the sum of all extensively amenable submodules is the image of their external direct sum, hence is extensively amenable by quotient closure. Functoriality follows from image closure. Any extensively amenable submodule of the quotient would lift to an extensively amenable extension strictly containing the radical.

No computational enumeration is used. The proof is limited to cocommutative Hopf algebras over fields and does not establish preservation by products, inverse limits, or arbitrary non-injective filtered colimits.
