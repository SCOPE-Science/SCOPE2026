Extraspecial groups of order \(p^{1+2n}\) have an edgeless noncommuting–nongenerating graph at \(n=1\), but for \(n\ge2\) the graph is an independent blow-up of the projective symplectic nonorthogonality graph. Its four-eigenvalue spectrum recovers \(p\) and \(n\) while remaining blind to the two extraspecial isomorphism types.
