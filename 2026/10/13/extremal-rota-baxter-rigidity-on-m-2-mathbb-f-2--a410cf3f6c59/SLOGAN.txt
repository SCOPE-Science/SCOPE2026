On \(M_2(\mathbb F_2)\), the maximal Rota–Baxter minimal-polynomial degree is \(3\) for both weights: there are exactly \(6\) extremals at weight \(0\) and \(24\) at weight \(1\). Automorphism conjugacy splits them into \(2\) and \(4\) orbits, but transpose and, at weight \(1\), \(\phi(R)=R+\operatorname{id}\), collapse each weight to the single extremal class predicted by the characteristic-zero pattern.\n
