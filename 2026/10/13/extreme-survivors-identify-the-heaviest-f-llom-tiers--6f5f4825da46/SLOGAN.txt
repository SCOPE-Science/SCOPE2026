In F-LLoM, sufficiently extreme survivors asymptotically reveal only the tier or tied tiers with the smallest Lomax shape: \(\Pr(M=m\mid Y>y,x)\) converges to explicit tail-constant weights and collapses to one under a unique minimum. At the same time \(h_Y(y\mid x)\sim\lambda_*/y\), so covariates affect the leading tail constant but not the first-order terminal hazard.
