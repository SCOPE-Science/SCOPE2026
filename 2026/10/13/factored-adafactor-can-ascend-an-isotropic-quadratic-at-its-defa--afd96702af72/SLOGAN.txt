Factored Adafactor's matrix-level RMS clip can spend almost its entire update budget on a coordinate whose gradient is vanishingly small. On \(f(X)=\tfrac12\|X\|_F^2\), this makes the first-step objective ratio approach \(1+\rho^2>1\) for every positive relative step \(\rho\); the defining paper's default first-step constants already give a concrete \(2\times2\) ascent witness.
