# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. Choose a diagonal one-parameter subgroup with variable weights \(1,B,B^2,\ldots\), where \(B>d\). Every degree-\(d\) monomial then has a distinct weight by uniqueness of base-\(B\) expansion, so the ambient projective fixed locus is exactly the monomial coordinate set.

Every degree-\(d\) monomial can be partitioned into monomial factors of any prescribed positive degrees \(d_1,\ldots,d_r\) summing to \(d\). Hence every ambient fixed point lies in every reducible-form variety \(X_\lambda(V)\). The restricted fixed locus therefore consists of exactly
\[
\binom{d+m-1}{m-1}
\]
points.

Euler characteristic localizes to the fixed locus for an algebraic \(\mathbb C^*\)-action; the result includes a Rosenlicht-stratification proof valid for singular projective varieties. This proves the theorem for every partition.

The bundled checker independently verifies weight separation and prescribed-degree monomial factorization over a finite all-partition grid. Finite computation is regression evidence only.

## Originality
PASS. The inspected primary archival source defines the same arbitrary-partition factorization varieties and studies their secants, but targeted full-text searches found no Euler-characteristic, torus, or monomial-localization statement. Broader semantic and web searches did not locate a partition-independent ambient-Euler theorem.

The all-linear completely decomposable case is a known special family and is not treated as evidence of novelty for that special case. Residual risk remains because the general fixed-point proof is short.

## Value
PASS. Reducible-form varieties vary strongly with factorization type in dimension and singular geometry. The theorem gives a complete and unexpected opposite behavior for Euler characteristic: every factorization type has the same value, equal even to the ambient projective space. This is a natural structural statement about a classical family, not a finite numerical slice.
