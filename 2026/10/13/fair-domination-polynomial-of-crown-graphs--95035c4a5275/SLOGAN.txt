For every \(n\ge3\), a fair dominating set of the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n\) is governed exactly by whole-side selection, equal matched index sets, or equal-size index sets whose union is all of \([n]\). This yields a closed fair-domination polynomial, \(\operatorname{fd}(\operatorname{Cr}_n)=2\) with exactly \(n\) minimum sets, and total count \(3\cdot2^n+[z^n](1+z+z^2)^n-4\).
