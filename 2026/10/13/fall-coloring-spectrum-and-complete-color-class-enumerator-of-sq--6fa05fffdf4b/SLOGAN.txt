The fall-coloring spectrum of every squared cycle \(C_n^2\) with \(n\ge7\) is determined exactly: three colors occur precisely when \(3\mid n\), five precisely when \(5\mid n\), and four in every order except \(7\) and \(11\). An explicit degree-\(48\) determinant produces the complete cardinality polynomial counting partitions into fall color classes.
