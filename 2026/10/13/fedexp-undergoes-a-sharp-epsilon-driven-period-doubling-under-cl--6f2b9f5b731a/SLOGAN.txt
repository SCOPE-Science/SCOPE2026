On two symmetric quadratic clients, FedExP has the sharp local-stability threshold \(\epsilon=s^3b^2/4\): below it the global optimum is repelling and an explicit symmetric two-cycle appears. With exact one-step local solves and \(\epsilon=1/8\), FedAvg reaches the optimum in one round while FedExP has the attracting cycle \(\{\pm1/\sqrt6\}\) with multiplier \(25/49\).
