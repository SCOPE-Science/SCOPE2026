With two equal-curvature clients and the locally exact step \(\eta=1/a\), default vanilla FedNova has round multiplier \(-(\tau_1-\tau_2)^2/(4\tau_1\tau_2)\), so its sharp local-step-ratio stability frontier is \(3+2\sqrt2\). A \(1:6\) imbalance therefore makes the global round diverge even though both clients reach their own local minimizers in one step.
