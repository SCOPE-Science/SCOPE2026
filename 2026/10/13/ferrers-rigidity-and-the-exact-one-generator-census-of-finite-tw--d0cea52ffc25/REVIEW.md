# Mathematical assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The result concerns labelled finite Cartesian two-equivalence frames with exactly one proposition; no hidden row names or counting modalities are allowed. The core argument shows a checkerboard in any non-Ferrers marking can be saturated into a concrete colour-respecting nontrivial two-modal bisimulation. Duplicate profiles give genuine automorphisms. Conversely, Ferrers plus distinct profiles permits a definable peeling of one uniquely pure row or column at a time, preserving profile distinctness on the surviving rectangle. Finally, the integer row-length argument gives the four canonical cases and exact permutation-orbit sizes, including one-by-one and one-by-two boundaries. The independent modal-refinement enumerator agrees on all 74,954 matrices with at most four rows and four columns; 648 core certificates and seven direct algebra-closure cases provide distinct semantic checks. These tests support rather than replace the unrestricted proof.

**Originality — PASS, with stated historical uncertainty.** The 2023 full-text study by Figallo and Mónica-Gomes classifies finite diagonal-free two-dimensional cylindric subalgebras by compatible atom partitions, but does not supply this binary single-generator iff and exact labelled census. Erdős–Faber–Larson's 1981 infinite upper-triangular generating lemma establishes part of the staircase intuition and is expressly acknowledged; no new claim is made for upper-triangular existence. Prior finite Ferrers work assumed the Ferrers valuation from the outset and studied modal depth, not the necessity of the Ferrers property for arbitrary valuations. The more specialized 1982 Bergman paper on cylindric rank was identifiable bibliographically but inaccessible in full text; a potentially implicit antecedent remains an unresolved originality risk, not a certification of priority. Queries in several equivalent formulations and own-record comparisons identified no directly equivalent complete census. A separate historical-priority audit is needed.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** This is a complete structural classification and exact formula for a natural invariant of finite Boolean modal algebras: the number of *single* generating truth-sets on a two-agent grid. It explains why an overwhelming majority of binary valuations cannot generate all labelled worlds despite there being only two colours, identifies every exceptional valuation by a familiar Ferrers forbidden pattern, and obtains a sharp quadratic minimum support. The mixed-core bisimulation obstruction and definable peeling are reusable mathematical mechanisms; the conclusion is not a routine re-expression of the previously known sufficient triangular construction. No generalization beyond the specified class has been verified.
