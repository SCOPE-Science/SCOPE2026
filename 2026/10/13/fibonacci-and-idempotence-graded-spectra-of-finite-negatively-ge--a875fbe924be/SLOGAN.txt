For finite negatively generated De Morgan chains, each odd order has one isomorphism type, while order \(2t\) has exactly \(F_{t+1}\) types; the non-idempotent-element spectrum is \(1+\sum_{d=1}^{t-1}F_du^d\). A reflection–fiber classification also gives a rational three-variable generating function and a geometric limiting law for outer shell thickness.
