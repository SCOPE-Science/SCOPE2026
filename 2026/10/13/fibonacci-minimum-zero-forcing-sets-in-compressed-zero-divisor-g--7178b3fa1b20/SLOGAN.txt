For a finite chain ring of nilpotency length \(\ell\), the compressed zero-divisor graph is the antiregular graph \(A_{\ell-1}\), independent of residue-field size. For \(\ell\ge3\), its zero forcing number and real maximum nullity are \(\lfloor(\ell-1)/2\rfloor\), while the number of minimum zero forcing sets is \(F_\ell\) for odd \(\ell\) and \(2F_{\ell-2}\) for even \(\ell\).
