# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The exact tensor-population calculation identifies all possible strict energy/probability inversions: writing the occupancy differences as \((-p,p+q,-q)\) proves the necessary lower bound on tensor-copy count, and an explicit two-vector exchange proves sufficiency. The golden-ratio bounds use adjacent Fibonacci fractions with determinant one, furnishing a global denominator-and-numerator minimality argument for *all* candidate fractions. Binet estimates yield the two sharp unequal accumulation constants. Exact-integer checks corroborate examples and determinant identities, but the proof is analytic and infinite in scope.

## Originality — PASS, with qualifications

The archival virtual-temperature criterion already provides sufficient rational inequalities, and the 2017 qutrit engine paper gives related activation windows. A 2019/2021 geometric passivity theorem already gives a necessary-and-sufficient general copy-by-copy test. This work therefore does **not** claim the first discovery of the underlying inversion criterion or first-copy algorithm. Its assessed additional claim is the closed Fibonacci classification for the specified irrational-gap family, with strict breakpoints and a nonconvergent asymptotic scaling envelope. Searches for corresponding formulas under Farey, Fibonacci, \(N\)-passivity, virtual-temperature, and rational-approximation terminology yielded no equivalent calculation in the inspected primary texts. Equivalent arithmetic specialization elsewhere remains a substantive priority risk; no independent priority audit has been performed.

## Value — PASS

The first activated tensor-copy count is a natural resource invariant in finite-size quantum thermodynamics. For a canonical badly approximable irrational level spacing, the result supplies every exact threshold and demonstrates that even after the leading square-root copy-cost scaling is removed, a persistent finite multiplicative oscillation remains. This refines broad qualitative eventual-activation results and the existing generic copy-by-copy criterion without claiming a wider thermodynamic protocol.

## Closest literature and limitations

Alicki–Fannes (2012) supplies passivity and global-unitary work extraction; Skrzypczyk–Silva–Brunner (2014) gives sufficient virtual-temperature inequalities; Sparaciari–Jennings–Oppenheim (2017) considers reversible ancillary machine cycles; Koukoulekidis–Alexander–Hebdige–Jennings (2019/2021) establishes geometric \(N\)-passivity. This result is for full-rank, three-level diagonal states, unrestricted identical-copy global unitary operations, and a fixed irrational energy ratio. It proves first onset, not the optimum extracted energy or extension to other nonidentical-copy resources.
