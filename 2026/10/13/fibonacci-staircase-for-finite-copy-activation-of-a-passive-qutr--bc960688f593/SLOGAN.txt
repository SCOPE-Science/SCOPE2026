For a passive qutrit with a golden-ratio energy gap, the exact smallest number of identical copies that permits unitary work extraction follows the Fibonacci staircase \(3,8,21,55,\ldots\), with all transition thresholds given by successive rational approximants. Near the Gibbs family the copy requirement is of order \(\delta^{-1/2}\), but its scaled coefficient oscillates between the distinct sharp accumulation values \(\varphi^2/5^{1/4}\) and \(\varphi^4/5^{1/4}\).
