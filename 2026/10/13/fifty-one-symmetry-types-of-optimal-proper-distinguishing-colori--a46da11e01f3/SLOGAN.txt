The Heawood graph has exactly 328608 proper distinguishing colorings on a fixed labelled four-color palette, or 13692 partitions after forgetting color names; three colors cannot distinguish it. These optimal color partitions form exactly 51 orbits under its full automorphism group of order 336, including exceptional orbit sizes 56, 84 and 112 arising from automorphisms that permute, rather than fix, color classes.
