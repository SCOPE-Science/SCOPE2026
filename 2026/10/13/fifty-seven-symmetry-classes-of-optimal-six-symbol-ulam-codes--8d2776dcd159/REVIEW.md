# Scientific review: six-symbol Ulam equality census

**Correctness — PASS.** The directed-triple criterion is logically equivalent to Ulam distance at least four. Full enumeration starts with all \(720\) permutations, finds \(132\) identity-compatible candidates, enumerates exactly \(298\) compatible triples, and checks that none extends to a fifth word. Two implementations use distinct representations (direct longest common subsequences and directed-triple masks), while a third explicitly expands the labeled population to \(53\,640\). The orbit canonicalization is complete because any relabeling taking a designated word to the identity is unique. Every representative in the bundled table is among the enumerated codes. The Erdős–Szekeres argument establishes equidistance without relying on numerical output.

**Originality — PASS, with explicit bibliographic uncertainty.** The 2015 primary source tabulates the maximum size four, but not the equality census or orbit classification. The 2022/2023 covering-array study imposes an exact uniform-cover condition rather than the present pairwise-disjoint triple-incidence condition. The 1999 Mathon–van Trung paper addresses related directed designs; its publicly available abstract emphasizes perfect four-subsequence packings, while its entire text could not be checked, leaving a real possibility that subsidiary partial-packing data overlap. Search results did not identify a statement implying the \(57\)-class, \(53\,640\)-code census, but failed search is not itself evidence of novelty.

**Value — PASS.** The natural first non-Singleton equality case at blocklength six has a complete, reusable structure classification. The \(57\) symmetry classes quantify genuine heterogeneity of optimal rank-modulation designs, and their stabilizer distribution distinguishes high-symmetry and generic optima. This is stronger than simply recomputing the known maximum size four; it exhausts the equality cases for a classical exact coding parameter.

**Closest literature and limitations.** Göloğlu et al. (2015, arXiv:1504.05100v1) provide the known optimum; Mathon–van Trung (1999, DOI 10.1023/A:1008353723204) address related directed packings but remain incompletely inspected; Gentle–Wanless (2022/2023, DOI 10.1007/s00026-022-00610-6) prove results on complete uniform coverings. The result is specific to six distinct symbols and natural relabeling/reversal equivalence; computational enumeration is exhaustive and reproducible but not independently audited.
