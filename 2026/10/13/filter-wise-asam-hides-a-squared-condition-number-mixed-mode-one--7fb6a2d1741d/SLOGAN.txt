On a two-curvature quadratic, one-filter ASAM can require a one-step learning-rate scale of order \(\kappa^{-2}\) on a balanced-gradient mixed state even though both pure eigendirections remain contractive at order \(\kappa^{-1}\). The hidden factor comes from filter-wise normalization turning a \(1/\kappa\) high-curvature parameter component into an order-one gradient component before the Hessian acts again.
