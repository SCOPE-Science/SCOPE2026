The minimal well-determined logic is complete for finite Boolean powerset semilattices under its \(\mu\)-extension semantics: each non-theorem has an explicit countermodel with at most \(2^s\) elements, where \(s\) counts its distinct variable and implication subformulas. A simple nested-implication formula is valid on every two-element semilattice but refutable on a three-element chain, so this is more than a two-valued reduction.
