Even for one finite-order orthogonal generator in \(\mathbb R^3\), the real isotypic block code cannot be computed from the generator matrix alone: \(\operatorname{diag}(1,R_{2\pi/n})\to I_3\) while the isotypic dimensions jump from \(1,2\) to \(3\). Thus the finite-group data in Burnik's computable isotypic block-diagonalization theorem cannot simply be discarded.
