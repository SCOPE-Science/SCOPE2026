On SPD quadratics, one Entropy-SGD inner step is exactly a scalar multiple of the raw gradient and therefore gives no conditioning improvement. With \(K\ge2\) nonoscillatory inner steps, the expected estimator condition number lies strictly between the raw value \(\kappa\) and the ideal local-entropy value \(\kappa(\mu+\gamma)/(M+\gamma)\), reaching the latter only as the inner loop resolves the Gibbs mean.\n
