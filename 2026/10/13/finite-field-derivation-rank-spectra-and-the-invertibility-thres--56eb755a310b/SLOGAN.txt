For the four-dimensional model filiform Lie algebra over every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\), the complete rank distribution of all \(q^7\) derivations has an explicit characteristic-dependent polynomial. An invertible derivation exists precisely when \(q\ge4\); characteristics two and three have different rank-stratum mechanisms, and neither \(\mathbb F_2\) nor \(\mathbb F_3\) admits an invertible one.
