A nonzero weighted directed \(m\)-cycle over \(\mathbb F_q\) is classified by one \((2^m-1)\)-power residue invariant, modulo doubling from cyclic rotation. The exact number of forms is \(m^{-1}\sum_{k=0}^{m-1}\gcd(\gcd(2^m-1,q-1),2^k-1)\), and the familiar collapse to unit weights occurs exactly when \(\gcd(2^m-1,q-1)=1\).
