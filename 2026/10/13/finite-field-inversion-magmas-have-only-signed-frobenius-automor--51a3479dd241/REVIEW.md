# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The field-by-field group formula follows from a symbolic proof valid for all prime powers: collision fibers of the inverse-difference magma recover subtraction outside at most two exceptional ratios; at most six excluded parameters permit an additive-repair chain; additivity and commutation with inversion then recover multiplication via Hua's identity. Small fields are treated individually and converses checked. The socle exception is proved algebraically. The packaged finite-field checker tests the claimed formulas independently through order sixteen, exhaustively enumerating all zero-fixing bijections through order nine, but these finite checks are not a universal proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual historical risk
Byott–Jasko's complete 32-page 2026 paper was inspected with particular attention to its equation (1.6), Propositions 2.5, 2.6, 2.14, Theorem 3.2, examples and Section 6. The authors already give the construction and the specific order-five inversion transposition case; those are excluded from novelty claims. Their general commutation lemma and finite computational investigations do not imply an exact all-prime-power inversion-group classification. Searches for equivalent inversion-difference and fractional-linear functional equations, broader pointed-magma coverage and existing tables found no definitive stronger result. An older field-incidence rigidity theorem in different terminology remains possible; searching does not certify independence.

It distinguishes the finite exceptional additive cases from a uniform trivial-socle family and proves that even over non-prime fields the full magma symmetry is just signed field Frobenius. The step recovering field addition from binary-operation fibers is the substantive mechanism beyond a parameter substitution or a finite table extension.
