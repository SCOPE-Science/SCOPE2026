For the \(3\)-Leibniz algebra with \([e_3,e_2,e_3]=-e_2\) and \([e_3,e_3,e_2]=e_2\), the exact finite-field Rota–Baxter count is \(q^6+2q^5-q^4-q^3\) in odd characteristic and \(q^6+3q^5-3q^4\) in characteristic \(2\). The jump is caused by one boundary stratum where an extra diagonal parameter becomes free only in characteristic \(2\).
