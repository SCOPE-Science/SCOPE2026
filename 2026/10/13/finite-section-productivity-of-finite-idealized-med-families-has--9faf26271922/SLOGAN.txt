For a finite \(\mathcal J\)-eventually-different family of size \(k\), finite-section productivity is equivalent to the exact-fiber condition \(\{n:F(n)=k\}\in\mathcal J^+\). Thus finite FSP maximality needs only a positive \(k\)-fiber, while finite Van Douwen maximality needs that same fiber to lie in \(\mathcal J^*\).
