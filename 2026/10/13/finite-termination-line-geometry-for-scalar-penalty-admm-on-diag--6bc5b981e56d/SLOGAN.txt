For diagonal quadratic consensus, scalar-penalty over-relaxed ADMM is finite for every initialization exactly when the curvature points \((a_i+b_i,a_ib_i)\) lie on one line \(p=\tau s-\rho^2\) with \(0<\tau\le\rho\); then \(\alpha=1+\tau/\rho\). The heterogeneous example \(A=\operatorname{diag}(1,2)\), \(B=\operatorname{diag}(5,6)\) terminates with \(\rho=4\) and \(\alpha=15/8\), so finite termination is not confined to an isotropic quadratic block.
