For every fixed \(\sigma>1/2\) in the source's unconditional support range, the weighted zeta one-level density has an explicit first finite-height bias: its \(1/\log T\) coefficient is \(\widehat\phi(0)(\log(2/\pi)-1+2\zeta'(\sigma+1/2)/\zeta(\sigma+1/2))\). The coefficient blows up like \(-2\widehat\phi(0)/(\sigma-1/2)\) at the critical transition.
