For the ternary \(2\)-shifted Sloane map, the least non-fixed periodic point is \(64\), belonging to the cycle \(64\leftrightarrow72\). The least periodic point of exact period at least \(3\) is \(4096\), in the cycle \(4096\to10368\to4608\to4096\); below \(4096\), the only other periodic points are the fixed points \(36,144,288,1152\).
