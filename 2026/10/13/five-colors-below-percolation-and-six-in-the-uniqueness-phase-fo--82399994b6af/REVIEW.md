# Review

## Correctness

PASS. In the finite-cluster regime, amenability makes the Bernoulli graphing hyperfinite; quotienting by the smooth finite percolation-component relation preserves hyperfiniteness; planarity of the base gives a Borel-planar quotient; finite clusters preserve local finiteness; and Proposition 3.5 makes the quotient pmp. The source's Theorem 6.4 therefore supplies a measurable \(5\)-coloring, and Corollary 3.6 transfers it to an fiid coloring. In the unique-infinite-cluster regime the same argument applies after restricting to finite clusters. Proposition 3.5(3) transfers that coloring to a partial factor, and assigning one fresh color to the invariant measurable infinite cluster yields a proper full \(6\)-color factor.

## Originality

PASS. The primary paper proves the measurable \(5\)-color theorem and the quotient correspondence, but its stated factor-of-iid conclusions are the square-grid theorem and a general amenable \(7\)-color bound. It does not state the general planar amenable \(5\)-color subcritical consequence or the \(6\)-color uniqueness-phase extension. Candidate-specific published-result and web searches under percolation-quotient, subcritical, uniqueness-phase, amenable-planar, factor-of-iid, and hyperfinite-pmp formulations found no equivalent theorem.

## Value

PASS. The conclusion converts a descriptive-combinatorial theorem into a broad factor-of-iid statement for random planar maps beyond the square grid. It improves the source's general amenable \(7\)-color bound to \(5\) whenever all percolation clusters are finite and to \(6\) when there is a unique infinite cluster. The two-regime result is a natural structural use of the source's quotient formalism rather than an arbitrary parameter specialization.

## Closest literature and limitations

Hsu--Sium--Thornton (2026) is the decisive source. Its Corollary 3.8 gives a general amenable \(7\)-color bound, Theorem 6.4 gives measurable \(5\)-colorability for hyperfinite locally finite pmp planar graphs, and Proposition 3.5 identifies quotient graphing colorings with factors. The present theorem composes those ingredients with the finite/unique-infinite quotient definitions and adds the explicit sixth-color extension.

No sharpness claim is made, and phases with multiple infinite clusters are outside the statement.
