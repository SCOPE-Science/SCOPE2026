With at most four firms, \(CR_4\) is constant; with five rational-share firms, a strict \(CR_4\)–HHI reversal first appears on common integer scale \(13\). The unique HHI-heavier boundary market \((5,2,2,2,2)\) is ranked less concentrated by \(CR_4\) than exactly two rivals, \((4,3,3,2,1)\) and \((3,3,3,3,1)\).
