# Review of the five-point unary-equation theorem

## Correctness
**PASS.** The signature and evaluation domain are fixed, and the proof proves the four-choice induction in the five constant-order strata. Explicit crisp terms isolate all strata, realizing all \(192\) independently selectable normal forms. Each strict half-region admits exactly four distinct outputs, the center three, and the endpoints two; sample points distinguish every profile. The normal-form argument yields all finite-chain counts and proves the if-and-only-if equational test; separate characteristic terms exhibit failure for each omitted size/parity. Recomputed finite closures for sizes \(2\) to \(17\) agree exactly with the predicted sets. Numerical enumeration is corroboration, not the basis for the infinite proof.

## Originality
**PASS, with residual risk.** The 2025 paper of Bílková, Ferguson, and Kozhemiachenko defines the same nonmodal operations but develops many-valued epistemic tableaux and their complexity; no unary term census or parity-sensitive single-variable equation test was identified in the inspected full text. Coniglio–Esteva–Gispert–Godo explicitly construct five-valued truth-cell formulas in 2021, which are prior ingredients, but their intermediate-logic classification does not assert the claim. Carai treats ordinary Gödel algebras without the involution. The 2023 \(L_{PG}\) free-algebra result is for a distinct perfect-MV/Gödel-fusion signature and underlying chains. These comparisons do not eliminate the possibility of an equivalent result hidden under an older functional-clone formulation.

## Value
**PASS.** The theorem gives an exact full classification of all unary formulas, an explicit smallest finite identity test for continuum semantics, and a sharp parity law: every sufficiently large even equally spaced chain still misses a genuine continuum unary identity, whereas every odd chain with at least five points captures all of them. This is useful for equational reasoning in the nonmodal core of involutive Gödel epistemic logic, without suggesting that five truth degrees suffice for modal validity.

## Limitations and sources
The central inputs are Bílková–Ferguson–Kozhemiachenko (arXiv:2510.04642, Sections 1–2 and 4–5), Coniglio–Esteva–Gispert–Godo (2021, Section 4.2), Carai (arXiv:2406.05480, free Gödel algebras), and *Involutive symmetric Gödel spaces, their algebraic duals and logic* (2023, Sections 2 and 7). The cited 2021 characteristic formulas could yield an implicit proof of the new identity test when combined with elementary order arguments; no claim of exhaustive novelty is made.
