One graded-modal counting round on an independently marked \(\lfloor n^a\rfloor\times n\) epistemic grid yields five sharp distinguishability regimes, with critical aspect exponents \(a=1/2\) and \(a=2\) governed by an explicit Gaussian occupancy integral. In every regime, the number of distinguishable cell types is asymptotic to twice the product of the numbers of distinct row and column degrees.
