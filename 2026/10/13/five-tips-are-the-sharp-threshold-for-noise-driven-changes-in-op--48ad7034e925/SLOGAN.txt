With a known Brownian phylogeny and equal independent trait-measurement noise, the best fixed-size set of taxa for estimating the root mean can depend on noise strength for the first time at five tips: no rooted binary ultrametric tree with at most four tips permits such a change. A five-tip tree has an exact unique switch at a positive error variance determined by \(2x^3+205x^2-1225x-198500=0\), even though its topology, branch lengths, and sampling budget never change.
