# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The outbreak objective is precisely the original-population-normalized expected infected count. The deterministic mixed-difference identity follows by classifying residual components by attachment to two vaccinated sites; all square terms from one-sided residual pieces cancel. For four or fewer vertices, at most two residual vertices rule out a negative mixed difference. A unique-path expansion establishes forest submodularity for independent nonidentical edge probabilities. For the five-cycle, a fully explicit polynomial and cycle symmetry cover all vaccination sets and vertex pairs; the cubic has a unique root in the relevant interval. Exact integer enumeration of 83,506 deterministic checks and 32 edge states independently supports the finite portions. Limits: enumeration is not the general proof; nonstationary infection is not covered.

## Originality — PASS, subject to recorded risks

Schneider et al. (arXiv:1102.1929) study immunization and network reachability, not this complete set-function classification. Srivastava and Teng (arXiv:2410.19205) expressly discuss the existence of immunization complementarity and non-submodularity, which are *not* claimed as new. The first universal four-vertex guarantee, exact five-cycle polynomial threshold, and attachment-class mixed-difference formula were not found in inspected source results. Holme (arXiv:1708.06456) investigates different exact epidemic importance metrics on small graphs. Li et al. (2023) address a different fixed-source, delayed-immunization SI model and do not imply the complete cycle threshold. Brightwell and Winkler (doi:10.1137/07069078X) study a modular-lattice chain theorem, not epidemic vaccination. Literature searches and a research index are not proofs of novelty; unindexed reliability and immunization results are the residual risk.

## Value — PASS

Submodularity is the structural condition underlying provable greedy vaccine prioritization. The theorem identifies a sharp and natural graph-order boundary for that condition in a standard static transmission model and gives a complete transmissibility phase transition on the minimal cyclic witness. The exact formula separates alternate-route synergy from component-fragmentation gains, explaining when and why the property fails. The fact is structural, not merely a computation at a chosen probability. Perfect vaccination, static contacts, fixed original-population seeding, and no independent audit limit applicability.
