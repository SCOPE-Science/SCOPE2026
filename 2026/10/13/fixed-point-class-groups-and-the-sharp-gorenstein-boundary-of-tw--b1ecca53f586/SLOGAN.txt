For the toric Segre model with two disjoint missing monomials, every torus-fixed local divisor-class group is \(0\), \(\mathbb Z\), or \(\mathbb Z^2\), with rank two occurring exactly at the two cross vertices. The projective variety is Gorenstein (equivalently \(\mathbb Q\)-Gorenstein) precisely for the \(3\)-by-\(3\) model.
