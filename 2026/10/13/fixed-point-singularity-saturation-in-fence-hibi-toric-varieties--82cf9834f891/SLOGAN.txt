For the projective Hibi toric variety of the \(n\)-element fence, there are \(F_{n+2}^{\mathrm{Fib}}\) torus fixed points but, from \(n=3\) onward, exactly four are smooth. Its projective degree is the Euler zigzag number \(E_n\), which is smaller than the Euler characteristic through \(n=4\) and larger for every \(n\ge5\).
