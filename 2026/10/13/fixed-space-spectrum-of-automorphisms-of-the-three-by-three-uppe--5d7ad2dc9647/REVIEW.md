# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof explicitly derives the three commutator equations, converts them to a rank problem, and handles every diagonal-equality pattern. The stratum counts sum to \(q^3(q-1)^2\), the exact automorphism-group order. Independent finite enumeration of the packaged formulas succeeds for \(q=2,3,5,7,11\), and direct orbit construction succeeds for \(q=2,3\). The computations are checks, not substitutes for the symbolic proof.

## Originality
PASS. Classical triangular-ring papers determine the automorphism structure but do not supply the finite-field fixed-space spectrum. The closest Borel-orbit papers located treat square-zero or nilpotent loci, while the final theorem covers every element of \(T_3(\mathbb F_q)\). Targeted searches for the exact polynomial orbit count and equivalent formulations did not locate a dominating statement. A residual risk remains from unindexed finite-field orbit tables.

## Value
PASS. A complete fixed-space distribution is a natural refinement of knowing the automorphism group, and Burnside turns it into the exact number of upper-triangular similarity classes. The result is a structural finite-field classification for the full algebra, rather than a routine substitution into a known table.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest structural sources are Ánh–van Wyk and Jøndrup on automorphisms of triangular matrix rings. Melnikov and Fresse provide the closest orbit-theoretic comparisons, but only on restricted nilpotent loci. The theorem is limited to size three; no higher-rank extrapolation is claimed.
