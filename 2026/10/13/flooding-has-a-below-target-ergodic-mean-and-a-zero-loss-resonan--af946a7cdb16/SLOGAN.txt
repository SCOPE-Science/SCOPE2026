On a scalar quadratic, deterministic Flooding is exactly a rigid rotation in log-radius for \(0<h<1\); irrational steps spend less than half their time above the flood level and have mean raw loss \(bh/\operatorname{artanh}(h)<b\). At the natural exact-solve step \(h=1\), every nonzero start outside a countable boundary-hit set reaches zero loss in finite time.
