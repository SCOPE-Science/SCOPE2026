In a finite rhombic quantum chain with one lossy upper-rim site, the exact detector-visible dimension is \(2N+3\) at generic flux, \(2N-2\gcd(j,N+1)+3\) at zero flux, and only \(3\) or \(5\) at half flux. Dividing by \(3N+1\) gives the exact eventual absorption probability from a uniform incoherent initial state, for any positive absorber strength.
