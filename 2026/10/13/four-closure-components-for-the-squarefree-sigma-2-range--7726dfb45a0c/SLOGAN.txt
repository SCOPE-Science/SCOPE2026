On the squarefree domain, \(\overline{\sigma_{-2}(S_1)}\) consists of exactly four explicit disjoint intervals, with endpoints determined by \(54/(5\pi^2)\), \(10/9\), \(5/4\), and \(25/18\). This gives \(L=4\) at \(k=1,r=2\) in Defant's restricted-domain component problem, even though the unrestricted \(\sigma_{-2}\) range has three components.
