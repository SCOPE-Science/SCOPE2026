# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. Expanding the commutator gives three coordinate equations. After quotienting two irrelevant common diagonal shifts, the first and third simple-root equations are determinant fibers in \(\mathbb F_q^2\). The only exceptional case in solving the middle-root coordinate is when both linear coefficients vanish; recasting that locus as three vectors with prescribed pairwise determinants gives the stated correction terms. The four fiber sizes sum to \(q^{12}\) with their target multiplicities. Independent finite enumeration in the supplied verifier agrees for \(q=2,3,5\), including a direct full-matrix check for \(q=2\).

## Value
PASS. A fixed-target commutator distribution is a natural refinement of commuting probability. Dimension three is the first triangular size where multiplication inside the radical contributes a second-superdiagonal term, and the theorem identifies exactly how that interaction breaks the uniform nonzero-fiber behavior of the two-vertex radical-square-zero case. The result is a complete, characteristic-free classification rather than an arbitrary finite census.
