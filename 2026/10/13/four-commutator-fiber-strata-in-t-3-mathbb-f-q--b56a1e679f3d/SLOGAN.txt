For \(T_3(\mathbb F_q)\), the additive commutator map is onto the strictly upper triangular radical and its fibers fall into exactly four explicit strata, determined by whether the two simple-root coordinates vanish. The zero fiber gives \(\operatorname{Pr}(T_3(\mathbb F_q))=(q^4+3q^3-2q^2-2q+1)/q^7\).
