# Review: exact spanning-tree shapes of \(K_{4,m}\)

## Correctness — PASS

The degree-excess identity confines all nonleaf vertices on the larger shore to one, two, or three vertices. Counting four surviving core shapes and their full hub automorphism actions produces the rational Burnside series for **every** \(m\ge5\). An exhaustive independent bipartite Prüfer census at \(m=5,6\) agrees with the proof, and the rational-series expansion is checked through degree forty. The finite checks support rather than replace the all-parameter argument.

## Originality — PASS

Johnson and Nochumson, arXiv:2602.06867, give general partition bounds for the same invariant. Yan and Zhang, arXiv:2609.30201, already prove the core reduction and the leading \(29/144\) cubic coefficient; those are not claimed as original. Their asymptotic proof ignores non-generic orbit stabilizers in its error term; the complete four-core classification and all-coefficient formula require and supply their exact treatment. Bicoloured-tree synonyms, possible Pólya coverage and exact tabulations were compared in targeted searches; none located was logically covering. Residual risk: an uninspected general enumeration may specialize to the same rational series.

## Value — PASS

The formula turns an explicitly studied extremal family from an asymptotic into a complete exact infinite enumeration. It records the symmetry classes of every spanning tree and establishes a degree-three quasipolynomial with period dividing twelve, information missing from the cited bounds.

## Limits and evidence

Claims are restricted to the unequal-shore case \(K_{4,m}\), \(m\ge5\); the known leading coefficient is credited, not newly asserted. The proof and elementary verification program are self-contained. The Pippenger equicolourable-tree source was compared at abstract level only; unindexed equivalent work cannot be excluded. No external assessment is represented.
