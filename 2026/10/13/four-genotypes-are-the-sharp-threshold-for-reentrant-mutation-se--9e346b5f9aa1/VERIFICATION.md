The standalone `verify_reentrant.py` was extracted from the same science package and run directly with SymPy. It recomputed the determinant, polynomial remainder, resultant and crossing-eigenvalue identity from the four-by-four mutation–selection matrix. It verified three exact disjoint rational brackets for the cubic roots, and two independent limiting-frequency calculations. Optional NumPy eigenvector samples and root approximations are not proofs of the all-rate statement.

The universal lower bound for graphs with at most three vertices is proved by an explicit complete shape classification and a strictly decreasing principal eigenvalue on a three-vertex path. The admissible four-vertex crossings follow from a resultant plus spectral bounds; no numerical root search is used to assert exhaustiveness. The result does not address different genotype graphs, nonlinear frequency-dependent fitness, or stochastic finite populations.
