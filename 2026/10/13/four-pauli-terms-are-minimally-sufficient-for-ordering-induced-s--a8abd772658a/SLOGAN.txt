A nominal one-pass first-order Trotter sweep can be genuinely second order for four suitably related Pauli terms: \(P,Q,QR,PR\) ordered as \(P,Q,QR,PR\) becomes Strang splitting on one \(R\)-sector and exact identity on the other. Four is sharp: with at most three distinct Pauli directions, the nonzero quadratic BCH commutators occupy distinct orthogonal Pauli directions and cannot cancel.
