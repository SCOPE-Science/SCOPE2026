For the six-dimensional nilpotent algebra of strictly upper-triangular \(4\times4\) matrices over \(\mathbb F_q\), the exact ordered-factorization count \(\#\{(X,Y):XY=Z\}\) assumes precisely four positive values on its three-dimensional square, with a closed formula for every target and every prime power \(q\). This refines the known image classification by distinguishing zero, nonzero central, single-supported, and doubly-supported product fibers.
