Two communities with \(s\) positive species proportions can have at most \(s-2\) finite positive-order ties between distinct Hill-diversity profiles. Four species are the sharp minimum for two reversals: abundance vectors \((994,2014,2989,4003)\) and \((1000,2000,3000,4000)\) reverse their Hill-diversity ranking once between orders \(2\) and \(3\), and again between orders \(3\) and \(4\).
