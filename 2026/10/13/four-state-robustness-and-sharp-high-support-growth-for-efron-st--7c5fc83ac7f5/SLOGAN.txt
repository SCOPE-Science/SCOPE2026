For identically distributed but potentially nonexchangeable coordinate pairs, the classical Efron–Stein telescoping factor remains exactly \(1\) through four-point support, first rises to \((3+\sqrt5)/4\) at five points, and equals \(3/2\) at six. Across arbitrary numbers of coordinates, the sharp factor divided by support size tends to \(\sin^2\tau/(\pi\tau)=0.2306509575\ldots\), where \(\tan\tau=2\tau\).
