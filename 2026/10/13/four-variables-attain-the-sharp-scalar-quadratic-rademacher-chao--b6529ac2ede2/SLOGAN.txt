Every real scalar quadratic Rademacher chaos on four signs satisfies \(\|h\|_2\le2\|h\|_1\), with equality exactly for products of two signed two-point sums on a partition of the four coordinates. The best constants on \(2,3,4\) variables are \(1,\sqrt2,2\), so four variables are the first dimension attaining the conjectured degree-two constant.
