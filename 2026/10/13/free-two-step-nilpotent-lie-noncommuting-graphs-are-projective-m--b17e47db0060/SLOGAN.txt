For the free two-step nilpotent Lie algebra \(N_r(q)=V\oplus\Lambda^2V\), the noncommuting graph is an equal-part complete multipartite graph indexed by the projective points of \(V\). Its parts have size \(q^{\binom r2}(q-1)\), so the graph has closed independence, chromatic, spectral, and automorphism formulas and classifies every maximum commuting set.
