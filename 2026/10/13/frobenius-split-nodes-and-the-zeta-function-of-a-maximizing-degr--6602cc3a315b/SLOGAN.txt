The degree-nine maximizing free curve \(V(xyz((x^2+y^2+z^2)^3-27x^2y^2z^2))\) has an explicit zeta function over every prime field of characteristic greater than three: \(Z(T)=(1-T)^2(1-\epsilon_pT)^4(1-\eta_pT)^3/(1-pT)^4\), with \(\epsilon_p=(\frac{-3}{p})\) and \(\eta_p=(\frac{-1}{p})\). The formula comes from a cubic-coordinate normalization of its sextic by a smooth conic and an exact Frobenius accounting of four nodes and six cusps.
