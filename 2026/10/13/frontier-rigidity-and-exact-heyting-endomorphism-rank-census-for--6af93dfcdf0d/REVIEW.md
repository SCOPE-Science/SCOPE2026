The suffix-image equation yields a complete dynamic census for arbitrary weak-order level sizes. The accompanying independent brute-force and implication-table checks corroborate all small cases; they are not substitutes for the proof.

Related published literature on stars, products of chains and modal cluster chains has different dual structures or operation signatures and does not imply the present uniform frontier rigidity and rank census. Equivalent results in older unindexed order-algebra literature remain possible.
