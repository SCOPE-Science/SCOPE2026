For the Heyting algebra of upsets of a height-\(h\) ordinal sum of equal \(m\)-point antichains (\(m\ge2\)), every endomorphism is either a string of level permutations followed by a single collapse or has permutations above an arbitrary bottom-level map. This gives an exact Stirling-number image-rank polynomial and, through a suffix-image recurrence, an exact enumeration for all nonuniform finite weak orders.
