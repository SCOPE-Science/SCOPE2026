# Full projective symmetry of the \(49\)-line dodecagon arrangement
## Finding

Let \(C_{12}\subset\mathbb P^2_{\mathbb C}\) be the arrangement formed by the twelve side-lines of a regular dodecagon and let
\[
\mathcal O_{49}=\Lambda_{\{2\},4}(C_{12}).
\]
Pokora--Roulleau show that this construction gives a rigid free arrangement of forty-nine lines with singularity numbers
\[
n_2=204,\quad n_3=96,\quad n_4=6,\quad n_5=24,\quad n_6=6,\quad n_7=12,\quad n_{12}=1.
\]
Its full projective stabilizer is
\[
\operatorname{Stab}_{\operatorname{PGL}_3(\mathbb C)}(\mathcal O_{49})
\cong D_{12},
\]
the dihedral group of order \(24\).

In coordinates with dodecagon vertices
\[
v_j=[\cos(j\pi/6):\sin(j\pi/6):1],\qquad 0\le j<12,
\]
the stabilizer is generated by
\[
R=
\begin{pmatrix}
\sqrt3&-1&0\\
1&\sqrt3&0\\
0&0&2
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
S=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&0\\
0&-1&0\\
0&0&1
\end{pmatrix},
\]
viewed projectively. They satisfy \(R^{12}=S^2=1\) and \(SRS=R^{-1}\).

The induced action on the forty-nine lines has orbit sizes
\[
1,6,6,12,12,12.
\]
The unique fixed line is \(z=0\), the line at infinity.

## Assumptions and scope

The statement is over \(\mathbb C\) and concerns the specific arrangement \(\mathcal O_{49}\) obtained from a regular dodecagon by the operator \(\Lambda_{\{2\},4}\). The calculation uses the standard projectively equivalent realization above over \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt3)\).

The result concerns the projective stabilizer in \(\operatorname{PGL}_3(\mathbb C)\). It does not assert that the abstract automorphism group of the intersection lattice has the same order.

## Proof

Start with the twelve side-lines of the regular dodecagon. Their \(\binom{12}{2}=66\) pairwise intersections are all distinct double points. Applying \(\Lambda_{\{2\},4}\) means taking every line containing at least four of those sixty-six points. Exact arithmetic over \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt3)\) gives forty-nine distinct lines. Their pairwise intersections reproduce the source census
\[
( n_2,n_3,n_4,n_5,n_6,n_7,n_{12} )
=(204,96,6,24,6,12,1).
\]

The Euclidean rotation through \(\pi/6\) and reflection in the \(x\)-axis preserve the regular dodecagon. The operator \(\Lambda_{\{2\},4}\) is incidence-defined and hence equivariant under projective transformations, so the matrices \(R,S\) above preserve \(\mathcal O_{49}\). They generate a dihedral subgroup of order \(24\).

It remains to exclude accidental projective symmetries. The singularity census has a unique point \(P\) of multiplicity \(12\), so every projective stabilizer element fixes \(P\). It must also permute the twelve points of multiplicity \(7\). Choose three of those sevenfold points so that, together with \(P\), they form a projective frame. A projective transformation is uniquely determined by the ordered images of that frame. Consequently every possible stabilizer element occurs among the
\[
12\cdot11\cdot10=1320
\]
ordered choices of distinct images for the three selected sevenfold points.

For each of those choices, exact projective-frame reconstruction over \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt3)\) gives the unique candidate transformation when the target quadruple is a frame. Testing its action on all forty-nine line covectors leaves exactly \(24\) transformations. Therefore the visible dihedral subgroup already exhausts the full projective stabilizer.

As a check independent of the chosen generators, the element-order distribution of these \(24\) transformations is
\[
1^1,\quad 2^{13},\quad 3^2,\quad 4^2,\quad 6^2,\quad 12^4,
\]
which is the order profile of \(D_{12}\). Their line orbits have sizes \(1,6,6,12,12,12\), and the singleton is represented by the covector \([0:0:1]\), namely \(z=0\).

## Verification

The accompanying `verify.py` reconstructs the arrangement from the regular dodecagon using exact arithmetic in \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt3)\). It verifies the number of lines, reproduces the complete singularity census, performs the exhaustive projective-frame stabilizer enumeration, checks the dihedral relations for \(R,S\), computes the element-order profile, and computes the six line-orbit sizes.

## Relationship to prior work

Pokora--Roulleau introduce \(\mathcal O_{49}=\Lambda_{\{2\},4}(C_{12})\), prove its freeness and rigidity, and record its full singularity census. Their paper also emphasizes regular-polygon constructions as a source of highly structured arrangements. The inspected current arXiv text does not state a projective automorphism group or stabilizer for \(\mathcal O_{49}\).

The present statement is narrower than an abstract classification of simplicial arrangements: it determines the full projective symmetry group of this explicit rigid free arrangement and shows that the evident dodecagonal symmetries are all the projective symmetries. Focused searches for the exact arrangement notation, singularity signature, regular-dodecagon construction, and projective-stabilizer terminology found no statement implying this result.

## Limitations

The computation proves the projective stabilizer of this realization, not the automorphism group of the abstract intersection lattice. An older simplicial-arrangement catalogue or an unindexed computation could encode the same projective group without using the notation \(\mathcal O_{49}\); this remains the principal originality risk.

No independent audit has been performed.
