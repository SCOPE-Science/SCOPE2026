IMTL-G's positive-rescaling theorem gives a collinearity factor \(\lambda=(\sum_i\alpha_i/k_i)^{-1}\); its orientation is invariant for every positive scale vector exactly when all task coefficients are nonnegative. A full-rank three-task example with one negative coefficient has a finite scale singularity at \(k=1/\sqrt2\), beyond which the IMTL-G aggregate reverses and becomes first-order conflicting with every task.
