For the arbitrary-dimensional functional pre-Lie algebra \(x\cdot y=\varphi(x)y\), every linear endomorphism is Nijenhuis, and its induced product is simply \(x\cdot_Ny=(\varphi\circ N)(x)y\). Over \(\mathbf F_q\), the full Nijenhuis space has \(q^{n^2}\) points while every induced-functional fiber has exactly \(q^{n(n-1)}\) points.
