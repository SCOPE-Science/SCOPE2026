With refresh period \(T\), rank-\(r\) GaLore can spend exactly \(T-1\) consecutive steps with zero projected gradient even while the full gradient is nonzero. On the isotropic quadratic \(F(W)=\tfrac12\lVert W\rVert_F^2\), a diagonal \(m=Kr\) instance takes exactly \((K-1)T+1\) GaLore updates although full-gradient descent solves it in one.
