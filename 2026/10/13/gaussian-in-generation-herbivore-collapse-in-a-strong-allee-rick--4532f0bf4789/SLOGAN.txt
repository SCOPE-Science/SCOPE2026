In the strong-Allee Ricker plant–herbivore model, every strictly positive extinction orbit has plant biomass \(x_n\sim Ce^{-rbn}\) but herbivore biomass \(y_n\sim D(aC)^n e^{-rbn(n+1)/2}\), with explicitly convergent positive prefactors. Consequently plant detection thresholds take order \(\log(1/\eta)\) generations to cross, versus order \(\sqrt{\log(1/\eta)}\) for herbivores, a structural rate contrast that persists for a class of shared-biomass maps.
