For the parity hard-core graph studied only at binary-tree order in the source's non-uniqueness section, every nonsymmetric mass fixed point at arbitrary order \(k\ge2\) lies on one explicit hyperbolic branch \(L=\Lambda_{k,\ell}(s)\). Above the explicit bending threshold \(\ell>(k+1)(k+3)/(3(k-1)^2)\), that branch forces a nonempty activity interval with at least five translation-invariant Gibbs measures.
