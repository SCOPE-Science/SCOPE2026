General-position subsets of a rectangular rook graph correspond exactly to spanning star forests of a complete bipartite graph, and their full bivariate exponential generating function is \(\exp(u e^{zv}+v e^{zu}-zuv)\). The number of maximum subsets is \(mn\) when \(m,n\ge3\), \(2^{\max\{m,n\}}\) when exactly one dimension is two, and six for the four-cycle.
