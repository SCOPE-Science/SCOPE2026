For every smooth general-type cyclic cover \(z^r=F(x)\) of \(\mathbf P^n\) satisfying \((r-1)m>n+1\), \(m\ge2\), and \(\gcd(rm,n+1+m)=1\), the full graded matrix-factorization category determines the branch divisor up to projective linear equivalence. The key extra rigidity is that a graded coordinate change cannot translate the weight-\(m\) cover coordinate: the \(z^{r-1}\)-coefficient forces that translation to vanish.
