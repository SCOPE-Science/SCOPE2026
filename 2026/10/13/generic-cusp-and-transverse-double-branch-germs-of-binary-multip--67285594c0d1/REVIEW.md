# Review: generic collision germs of multiple-root loci

## Correctness — PASS

The reduced-image convention, precise root partitions, and characteristic-zero assumption eliminate ambiguities about multiplicities and branches. Coprime analytic factorization makes the ambient polynomial coefficient germ a product of cluster spaces. Explicit expansion of \((z-t)^m(z-v_0)\) recovers the centered coordinate and both \(v^2\) and \(v^3\), proving the cusp algebra. At two disjoint multiplicity-\(m\) clusters, straightening the smooth power curves yields the union ideal \((a_i b_j)\). A standard fiber-product exact sequence gives depth \(d-2m+3\), versus dimension \(d-m+1\), and the generic-point localization gives depth one. The checker verifies finite coefficient, tangent-space and Hilbert-function instances; the proof is independent of enumeration.

## Originality — PASS, with risk

Chipalkatti (2001 preprint, §5) and Kurmann (2011 preprint, §2) already classify the underlying singular support and distinguish multi-preimage from ramified-normalization mechanisms; these are acknowledged as prior. Kurmann uses qualitative nodal/cusp language but does not compute the completed germs or the general depth defect. The exact local equations and the resulting non-Cohen--Macaulay and non-\(S_2\) range are not entailed by singular-support lists or generic tangent-rank tests alone. Comparisons included primary arXiv full text, Macaulay2 singular-locus documentation, and a published degree-focused coincident-root result. A complete comparison with Weyman's 1993 Hilbert-function paper remains unavailable, so equivalent prior work is possible; the risk does not currently provide affirmative coverage.

## Value — PASS

Higher multiple-root loci are natural and classical projective discriminant varieties. Knowing the formal transverse germs distinguishes the universal cuspidal divisor from an additional, higher-codimension two-branch stratum. The exact depth defect \(m-2\) identifies an infinite family of failures of Cohen--Macaulayness, rather than merely enumerating singular strata. This local structural obstruction informs permissible resolutions, local duality, and global ideal-theoretic investigations. No global classification beyond these exact strata is asserted.
