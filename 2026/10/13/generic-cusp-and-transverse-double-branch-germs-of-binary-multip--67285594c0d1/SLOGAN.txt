At a generic collision of a multiplicity-\(m\) root with one simple root, the binary multiple-root locus has a transverse ordinary cusp for every \(m\ge2\). At two distinct multiplicity-\(m\) roots the completed germ is a transverse union of two smooth branches, with local depth defect \(m-2\); hence these higher loci are not Cohen--Macaulay there whenever \(m\ge3\) and \(d\ge2m\).
