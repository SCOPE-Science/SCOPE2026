For every crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M_n\), a set is geodetic exactly when it contains a deleted-matching pair, equals one whole bipartition class, or selects at least three vertices from each side on disjoint matched indices. This yields the complete polynomial \(\mathcal G_n(x)=(1+x)^{2n}-(1+2x)^n+2x^n+\sum_{k=6}^{n}\binom{n}{k}(2^k-k^2-k-2)x^k\) and shows that the minimal geodetic sets are precisely the matched pairs, the two full sides, and the disjoint \(3+3\) selections.
