For a uniform random \(q\)-ary word, the number of matched block pairs in its largest block-palindrome factorization converges to a geometric law with continuation parameter \(s_q=\sum_{m\ge1}u_m(q)q^{-2m}\). The resulting exact mean \(E_q=(1+s_q)/(1-s_q)\) has coefficients \(2\,\mathrm{A274199}\), proving the conjectured expansion.
