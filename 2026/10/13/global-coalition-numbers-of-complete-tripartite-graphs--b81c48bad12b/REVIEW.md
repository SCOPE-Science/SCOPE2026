# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof reduces global domination in \(K_{a,b,c}\) to the necessary-and-sufficient condition of meeting all three partite sets. The resulting support-union criterion for coalition partners is then used to prove matching lower and upper bounds in all three regimes. The defect arguments were reconstructed case by case, including the boundary cases \(c=1\) and \(c=2\). A definition-level enumerator independently corroborates every sorted profile through order \(10\), but the finite computation is not used to establish the infinite theorem.

It identifies two structural thresholds in the smallest part and provides upper bounds that are not consequences of the existing general lower bound.
