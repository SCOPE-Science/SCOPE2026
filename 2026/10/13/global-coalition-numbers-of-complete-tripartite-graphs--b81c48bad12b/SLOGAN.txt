For a complete tripartite graph \(K_{a,b,c}\) with \(a\ge b\ge c\ge1\) and \(N=a+b+c\), the global coalition number is \(a+1\) for \(c=1\), \(N-2\) for \(c=2\), and \(N-3\) for \(c\ge3\). The proof reduces global domination to meeting all three partite sets and converts coalition partitions into a sharp support-defect argument.
