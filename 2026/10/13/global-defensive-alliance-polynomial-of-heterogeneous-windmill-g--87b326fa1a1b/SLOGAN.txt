For any clique bouquet \(K_1\vee(K_{m_1}\cup\cdots\cup K_{m_r})\) with \(r\ge2\) and all \(m_i\ge2\), global defensive alliances have an exact truncated-product cardinality polynomial and minimum size \(1+\lfloor(\sum_i m_i)/2\rfloor\). All minimum sets contain the center except in the two-odd-blade case, which has an additional explicitly counted family of disconnected minimum alliances.
