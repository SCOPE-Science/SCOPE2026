For crown graphs \(\operatorname{Cr}_n\), every defensive alliance is automatically global and connected once \(n\ge5\), and all such sets admit an exact four-state index classification. This yields the full size enumerator and shows that deleting a perfect matching from \(K_{n,n}\) lowers the global defensive-alliance number by two exactly for even \(n\ge6\), while leaving odd \(n\ge5\) unchanged.
