For IID \(\operatorname{Exp}(1)\) data with the Gaussian kernel and \(b_n=n^{-1/2}\), the generalized past-entropy estimator has infinite global Lebesgue-time MISE for every \(n\ge1\). The obstruction is a nonvanishing random tail entropy level, so the published claim that the MISE on \((0,\infty)\) tends to zero is false as stated.
