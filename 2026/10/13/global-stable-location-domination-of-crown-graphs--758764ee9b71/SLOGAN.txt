For every crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n\) with \(n\ge3\), global stable locating-dominating sets admit an exact omitted-pair classification and a closed cardinality enumerator. In particular, \(\lambda^s_{gl}(\operatorname{Cr}_n)=4\) for \(n=3\), equals \(6\) for \(n=4,5\), and equals \(n\) for \(n\ge6\); in the last range, every minimum set is a matching transversal with at least three selected vertices on each side.\n
