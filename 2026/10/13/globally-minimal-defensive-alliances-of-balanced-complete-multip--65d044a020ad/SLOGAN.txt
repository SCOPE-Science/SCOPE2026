For a complete \(r\)-partite graph with equal part size \(n\), \(r\ge3\) and \(n\ge2\), a defensive alliance is globally inclusion-minimal precisely when at least two parts attain its sharp occupancy cap. This yields the exact upper defensive alliance number \(n+\lfloor(r-1)n/2\rfloor\), a complete counting polynomial for every globally minimal alliance, and an explicit formula for the number of maximum-size examples.
