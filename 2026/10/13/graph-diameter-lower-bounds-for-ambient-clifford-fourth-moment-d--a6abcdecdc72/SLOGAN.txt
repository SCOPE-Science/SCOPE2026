For arbitrary edge-local interaction graphs, the ambient-circuit obstruction to a Clifford 4-design is controlled exactly by graph-ball volume: \(\|\Phi_{\mathcal E_L}^{(4)}-\Phi_{\mu_{\mathrm{Cl}_n}}^{(4)}\|_\diamond\ge1-(4^{b_G(L)}-1)/(4^n-1)\). Consequently every approximation error \(\varepsilon<3/4\) forces depth at least \(\operatorname{diam}(G)\), recovering the one-dimensional linear-depth lower bound as the path case.
