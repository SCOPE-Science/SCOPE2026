For radical-square-zero path algebras whose nonempty ordered arrow classes each have two arrows, the finite-field commuting probability of the Lie algebra \(\mathrm{HH}^1\) is an exact graph-partition evaluation in characteristic two, but depends only on the number of arrow classes in odd characteristic. The triangle/forest distinction over \(\mathbb F_2\) demonstrates a cycle-sensitive modular obstruction to commuting outer derivations.
