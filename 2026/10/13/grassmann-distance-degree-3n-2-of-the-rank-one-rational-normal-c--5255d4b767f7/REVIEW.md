# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The model is a smooth open rational normal curve of nonisotropic rank-one projections. For generic rank-one data \(Q=aa^{\mathsf T}/(a^{\mathsf T}a)\), the objective is \(A(t)^2/S(t)\) and its derivative is \(A(t)(2SA'-S'A)/S^2\). The excluded cut locus consists exactly of the \(n\) simple roots of \(A\). Since \(S\) has \(2n\) distinct roots, the remaining critical polynomial has degree \(3n-2\), is coprime to \(A\) and \(S\), and is squarefree for generic coefficients. The squarefreeness proof uses independent first- and second-jet conditions and a proper incidence projection, not finite testing. The parameter infinity is noncritical because the local derivative equals \(2a_na_{n-1}\ne0\). Riemann--Hurwitz independently checks the counts. Exact finite-field polynomial checks through \(n=20\) corroborate, but do not prove, the universal result.

## Originality — PASS, with a bibliographic qualification

Friedman--Hoşten--Rosana (arXiv:2610.02018v1) calculate ED degree \(4n-2\) for a single-block rational-normal-scroll ruling curve and define cut-locus-excised GD degree; their relevant inspected Theorem 2.2 and Section 4 do not give the present exact GD count. Friedman--Rosana--Sturmfels (arXiv:2601.22843v1) focus on rank-two projection models. Published-finding searches under rational normal curve, Veronese curve, rank-one projections, Grassmann-distance degree, and the formula yielded no covering claim. The closest related findings compute different coisotropic or secant degrees, or GD for a rank-two ruling family. An earlier implicit deduction or an inaccessible thesis could still contain the same formula; database silence alone is not a novelty proof.

## Value — PASS

The exact sequence \(3n-2\) evaluates a natural metric-algebraic invariant for an unbounded family of classical embedded curves, in the setting where source literature explicitly contrasts ED and GD degrees. It explains quantitatively why specializing data to ambient rank-one projections loses precisely \(n\) extraneous critical points, rather than merely giving a numerical example. This is a self-contained structural count, not a claim of a more general theory.

## Closest literature and limits

The explicit overlap point is ED degree of the same parametrization in arXiv:2610.02018v1. The new count concerns the cut-locus-excised GD invariant, not its already available ED counterpart. The range is \(n\ge2\), complex generic rank-one data only. Potential older reformulations through rational-function ramification remain the principal originality risk.
