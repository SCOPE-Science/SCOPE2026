# Review

PASS. Conditioning one uniform-simplex coordinate above the threshold gives an affine-rescaled Dirichlet vector, from which the conditional Greenwood mean follows exactly. Exchangeability yields the covariance with the exceedance count, and one- and two-coordinate tail probabilities give the empirical-CDF variance. The asymptotic and zero-integral statements follow from explicit expansions and a pathwise identity. Finite exact computation only checks the algebra and does not replace the all-parameter proof.
