# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The exact inductive semantic equivalence was reconstructed at every constructor, including empty teams, contradictions and lax splitting. At a disjunction the proposed canonical overlapping split covers precisely the rows satisfying at least one child flattening, and every guarded child witness lies in its selected side; this is the key nonstandard step. The canonical maximal-subteam deduction follows by preservation of witnesses under enlargement within the flattening. The deterministic logarithmic-space argument uses recomputation of classical Boolean formula values and syntax positions rather than storing constructed guards or sets. An actual standalone checker exhaustively examined 45,584 small pairs and checked 4,800 larger seeded pairs. No finite experiment is offered as an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS, subject to stated risk.** Anttila–Kontinen–Yang (arXiv:2510.08122, §3 Proposition 4 and §6) prove only a polynomial-time bound using iterative fixed-point labelling, conjecturally observe stabilization by step four without proving it, and explicitly ask whether a tighter complexity result holds; their classical flattening and maximal-team lemmas are not claimed new here. A detailed comparison of implications identifies the effective guarded witness family and uniform \(\mathrm L\) decision as the contribution. Searches across published related findings, primary literature and primary literature found no directly covering theorem; negative searches are not proof of novelty. The closely related *Convex Team Logics* (arXiv:2503.21850) could be checked only through its abstract/bibliographic description, leaving an identifiable risk of a stronger equivalent result in its unread content. An existing expressive completeness result, without effective bounds and logspace evaluation, would not itself imply this theorem.

**Value — PASS.** The 2025 source itself identifies improved model-checking complexity for positive nonemptiness team semantics as a substantive open direction. The derived \(\mathrm L\) bound is a qualitatively sharper classification than \(\mathrm P\), and the transparent guarded witnesses give a canonical maximal-team construction that avoids the prior fixed-point presentation. This is not merely a normalization sign check or a quantitative micro-optimization. It remains valuable even without a matching lower bound.

**Closest work and limitations.** Anttila–Kontinen–Yang, *Complexity Results in Team Semantics: Nonemptiness Is Not So Complex*, arXiv:2510.08122; Anttila–Knudstorp, *Convex Team Logics*, arXiv:2503.21850; Buss, *The Boolean Formula Value Problem Is in ALOGTIME* (1987). Explicit finite teams, lax disjunction, and no team-level contradictory negation are essential. Full examination of the second preprint was not possible and could change the originality assessment. No claim of independent review or priority is made.
