For a Haar-random \(n\)-qubit pure state fully dephased in a fixed product energy eigenbasis, global ergotropy has an exact finite-size binomial–harmonic formula and concentrates at \(c_*\omega\sqrt n\), where \(c_*\approx0.451598642784\). Thus complete basis dephasing makes the typically extractable fraction of extensive stored energy vanish as \(2c_*/\sqrt n\).
