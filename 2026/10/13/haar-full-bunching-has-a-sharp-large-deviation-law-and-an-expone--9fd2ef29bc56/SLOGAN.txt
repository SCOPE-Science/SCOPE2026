For \(N\) photons from distinct inputs of a Haar-random \(m\)-mode interferometer, the full-bunching probability into one fixed output port has an exact Gamma-product moment law and, when \(m/N\to\alpha\), a sharp logarithmic large-deviation rate. The Haar mean overestimates a typical realization by the exponential factor \(\exp(N\Delta_\alpha+o(N))\), independently of partial distinguishability between normalized pure product input photons.
