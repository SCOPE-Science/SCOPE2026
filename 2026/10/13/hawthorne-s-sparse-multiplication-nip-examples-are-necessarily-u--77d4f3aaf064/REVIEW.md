# Review

## Correctness
The two NIP premises are explicit theorems of Hawthorne. The new step is immediate but exact: on the named set of powers \(P\), the restricted multiplication relation satisfies \(R(a^i,a^j,a^k)\) exactly when \(i+j=k\). Hence \(x\prec y\) iff \(x\ne y\) and \(y=xz\) for some \(z\in P\) is precisely exponent order. The sequence \(a^0,a^1,\ldots\) therefore witnesses the order property, so the theories are unstable. There is no dependence on ambient addition in this instability argument.
