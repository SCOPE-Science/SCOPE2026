For the three-periodic SOS gradient-Gibbs branch on a \(k\)-regular Cayley tree, the critical inverse temperature has the explicit high-degree law \(\beta_c(k)\sim1.65699836352747325122\,k^{-1/2}\). A two-term expansion of \(\tau_c(k)=2\cosh\beta_c(k)\) follows from a universal rescaled minimization problem whose unique minimizer solves \(e^c+1-2e^{-c}=3c\).
