For the two \([95,7]\) affine-half partial-spread codes, the generalized Hamming-weight hierarchies are \((18,54,74,84,90,93,95)\) and \((22,56,74,84,90,93,95)\). The exact numbers of minimizing subcodes are respectively \((1,7,126,168,280,32,1)\) and \((1,12,36,144,256,32,1)\); only the first two generalized weights distinguish their two normal classes.
