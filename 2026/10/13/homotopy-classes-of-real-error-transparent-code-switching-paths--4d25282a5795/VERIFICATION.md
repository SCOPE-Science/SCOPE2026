The proof is analytic. The source provides the projective null-quadric model
\[
\mathcal Q_n\cong(S^{p-1}\times S^{q-1})/\{\pm1\},
\]
and the verification checks each universal-cover case directly.

For \(\min(p,q)=1\), the quotient is explicitly homeomorphic to \(S^{m-2}\). For \(p=q=2\), the universal cover is \(\mathbb R^2\) with a rank-two deck lattice. For exactly one of \(p,q\) equal to \(2\) and the other at least \(3\), the universal cover is \(\mathbb R\times S^d\) and the deck group is generated by one combined half-turn/antipodal transformation. For \(p,q\ge3\), the product of spheres is simply connected and the diagonal antipodal quotient has deck group \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\).

The standalone checker only confirms the piecewise classification and the transcription of the signatures in the source survey. It does not certify the covering-space theorem and does not replace the proof.

Limits: the result concerns real trajectories inside \(\mathcal Q_n\). It does not establish that distinct real path classes remain distinct in the full complex distance-three set, nor that they yield distinct switching holonomies or endpoint unitaries.
