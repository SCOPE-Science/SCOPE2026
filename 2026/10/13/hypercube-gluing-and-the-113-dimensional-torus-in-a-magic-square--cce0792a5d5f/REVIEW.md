# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Six diagonal quadric relations reduce the center-zero section to sixteen sheets over a smooth genus-one complete intersection. Pairwise gluing occurs only at simple zeros of one base coordinate, giving exactly four ordinary nodes per edge of the four-cube and none elsewhere. The local \(A_1\) surface equation restricts to a nodal curve; there are 128 nodes and graph Betti number 113. The standard nodal Picard exact sequence then gives the torus, while oriented edge induction gives character multiplicities \(4|S|-1\). The independent adjunction arithmetic-genus check yields 129. The bundled exact checker corroborates the incidence and character arithmetic. No computation substitutes for the geometric proof.

**Originality — PASS with explicit residual risk.** Auel--Singer, Proposition 3.3, establishes sixteen elliptic components but does not state the 128-node hypercube gluing or the equivariant generalized-Jacobian character. Their Section 5 and associated repository compute extensive intersection data on the resolved surface; such data may encode aspects of the gluing implicitly, so the associated complete machine-readable tables are an important residual comparison risk. Caporaso--Viviani establishes a general exact sequence for arbitrary nodal curves but does not compute this specific graph. Searches for equivalent torus ranks, signs, and hypercube formulations did not identify a covering claim; negative searches do not prove absolute novelty.

**Value — PASS.** The distinguished center-zero section is intrinsic to the magic-square surface and independently motivated by the initiating paper's study of its elliptic curves and the arithmetic of the complement. Computing its nodal gluing upgrades normalization data to a specific 129-dimensional semiabelian Jacobian with a 113-dimensional torus and an explicit 15-character isotypic spectrum. This is a natural exact boundary invariant, not an arbitrary parameter slice or a routine recomputation of the sixteen-component count. The extension class and other boundary divisors remain uncomputed.
