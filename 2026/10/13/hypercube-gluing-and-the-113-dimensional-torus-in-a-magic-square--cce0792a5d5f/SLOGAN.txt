The center-zero hyperplane section of the magic-square-of-squares surface glues sixteen elliptic quartics along 128 ordinary nodes, with four parallel edges on every edge of a four-cube. Its generalized Jacobian has torus rank 113, and the sign-change group acts on its rational torus character lattice with multiplicity \(4|S|-1\) for every nontrivial sign character \(\chi_S\).
