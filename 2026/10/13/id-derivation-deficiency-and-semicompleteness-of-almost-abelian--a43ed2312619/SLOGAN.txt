For every finite-dimensional almost-abelian Lie algebra \(Ke\ltimes_T V\) over any field, the outer ID-derivations are exactly \(\operatorname{Hom}_{K[t]}(V,tV)/(KT)\), with an explicit elementary-divisor dimension formula. Such an algebra is semicomplete precisely when \(T=0\), or \(T\) has rank one and either \(T^2\ne0\) or \(\dim V=2\).
