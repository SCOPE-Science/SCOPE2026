For the free class-two Lie algebra on \(n\ge3\) generators over any field, composition-idempotent derivations have exactly two automorphism orbits outside characteristic \(2\), but \(n+1\) orbits in characteristic \(2\). Their complete labeled finite-field rank distributions are Gaussian-coefficient polynomials with an explicit characteristic jump; for \(n=3\) over \(\mathbb F_2\), the polynomial is \(1+1408T^3+448T^4\).
