# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The derivation condition reduces to a conformal-symplectic block equation, and idempotence adds precisely the matrix projection and central-functional equations. In characteristic different from two the center-annihilating branch is zero. In characteristic two that branch is classified by orthogonal nondegenerate symplectic summands. In every characteristic the center-fixing branch is equivalent to an ordered transverse Lagrangian decomposition. Automorphism shears eliminate central lifts for the orbit classification, while these lifts must be retained in the labeled count. Standard symplectic and Lagrangian counts yield the displayed rank polynomial. The complete finite checks listed in VERIFICATION.md agree; the arbitrary-field proof does not rely on them.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
Ochoa Arango and Rojas, arXiv:1811.09948v1 (2018-11-25), give a complex-characteristic derivation block framework for the Heisenberg algebra but not a complete idempotent-orbit enumeration or finite-field rank polynomial; see their Section 4 and Theorem 4.1. Di Bartolo, La Rosa and Mancini (2024), DOI 10.1007/s00009-024-02679-0, treat derivations of a different nonnilpotent non-Lie Leibniz algebra. Classical symplectic projection and Lagrangian-incidence formulas are antecedents, not new contributions. An equivalent idempotent census may appear under the language of derivation gradings or symplectic projections: this is a residual novelty risk. The claim is the combined, all-characteristic orbit classification with the full labeled rank polynomial, not the familiar ambient derivation matrix.

## Value — PASS
Idempotent derivations encode binary eigenspace decompositions compatible with the Lie bracket. The rank polynomial and complete conjugacy classification cover every Heisenberg rank and every finite-field cardinality, including the sharp extra family in characteristic two. Central lifts contribute nontrivial multiplicities and orbit/rank effects; the result is a natural complete classification, not a single small-dimensional calculation.
