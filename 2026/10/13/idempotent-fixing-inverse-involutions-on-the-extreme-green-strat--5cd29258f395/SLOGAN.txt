For every \(n\ge4\), the rank-two and rank-\(n-1\) strata of the monoid of isotone maps on an \(n\)-chain have explicit involutive mutual-inverse matchings fixing **exactly all idempotents**. Their fixed-point and two-cycle counts are respectively \(\bigl(\binom{n+1}{3},\binom n3\bigr)\) and \(\bigl(2(n-1),\binom{n-1}{2}\bigr)\), and the numbers of fixed points are optimal.
