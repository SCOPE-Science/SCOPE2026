The individual idempotents of the complete-graph evolution algebra admit an exact count for every odd finite field and every dimension, governed by the quadratic characters of \(1-4k(n-k)\), with an explicit boundary when the adjacency matrix is singular. All geometric idempotents are defined over \(\mathbf F_{p^2}\), so the extension-field point counts and zeta function follow from just two finite-field counts.
