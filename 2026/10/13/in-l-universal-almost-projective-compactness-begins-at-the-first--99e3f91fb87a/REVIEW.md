# Review

## Correctness

PASS. At the first weakly compact cardinal \(\kappa_*\), Calderoni--Yanowitz's Theorem 1.1 gives the positive projectivity conclusion, so the restricted property \(\mathsf{PC}(\kappa_*)\) holds. For each regular uncountable \(\lambda<\kappa_*\), Shelah--Strüngmann construct in \(L\) an indecomposable group \(G\) of cardinality \(\lambda\) as a continuous union of free groups of size below \(\lambda\). This is exactly a \(\lambda\)-filtration by projective \(\mathbb Z\)-modules, so Proposition 2.7 makes \(G\) \(\lambda\)-projective. It is nonprojective because projective \(\mathbb Z\)-modules are free and an uncountable free abelian group is decomposable, contradicting the construction.

## Originality

PASS. The recent source proves the positive weakly compact theorem for almost projective modules but does not formulate a least-cardinal threshold in \(L\), does not mention the Shelah--Strüngmann construction, and does not supply counterexamples below the weakly compact point. The older source supplies sharp regular-cardinal counterexamples only in the abelian-group setting. Searches for the exact universal module threshold, for \(\lambda\)-projective counterexamples below the first weakly compact cardinal, and for an \(L\)-characterization via projective modules found no equivalent statement.

## Value

PASS. The result identifies the exact regular-cardinal sharpness of a new large-cardinal compactness theorem rather than merely producing another special case. It shows that the stronger all-ring projectivity phenomenon cannot be pushed even one regular uncountable cardinal below the first weakly compact cardinal in \(L\), and that the entire lower obstruction is already visible over the canonical ring \(\mathbb Z\).

## Closest literature and limitations

Calderoni--Yanowitz (2026) provide the universal positive theorem at weakly compact cardinals. Shelah--Strüngmann (2008/2009) provide almost-free indecomposable abelian groups at every regular uncountable cardinal below the first weakly compact cardinal under \(V=L\). Classical almost-free-module texts cover the abelian-group compactness background.

The exact threshold here is conditional on \(V=L\), is limited to regular uncountable cardinals, and is formulated for rings of cardinality below the tested cardinal.
