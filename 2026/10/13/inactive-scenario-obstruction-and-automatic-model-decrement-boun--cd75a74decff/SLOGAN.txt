Active uncertainty scenarios determine robust descent, but inactive scenario gradients can point arbitrarily far uphill along the exact robust steepest-descent direction, so an all-scenario raw-gradient assumption need not hold even for a one-dimensional affine problem. The exact max-model nevertheless guarantees \(\Phi_j^*(x,v)\le-|\theta|\) and \(\|v\|^2\le2|\theta|\), which recover quantitative descent on every active scenario.
