For \(13\) complex projective lines with at most \(6\) singular points per line, the numerical multiplicity profiles \((7,17,0,2)\) and \((15,11,0,3)\) cannot occur when no point has multiplicity above \(5\). With exactly two quintuple points and no quadruple points, the triple-point count is confined to \(\{15,16,18\}\), and the middle case is realized by a subarrangement of the fifth-order Fermat arrangement.
