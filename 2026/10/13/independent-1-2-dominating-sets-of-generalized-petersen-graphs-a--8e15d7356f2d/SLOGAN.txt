For every generalized Petersen graph \(P(n,2)\) with \(n\ge5\), a rational bivariate generating function counts independent \([1,2]\)-dominating sets by size. Such a set exists at every order except \(n=8\), and its minimum size is \(\lceil2n/3\rceil\), increased by one exactly when \(n\equiv4\pmod6\).
