For identity least squares with rank-one Gaussian sketches, randomized CGLS has an exact independent-beta product law for its squared-error contractions: the first factor comes from a \(d\)-dimensional sphere and every later factor from a \((d-1)\)-dimensional one. Consequently, the later mean contraction is exactly \((d-2)/(d-1)\), strictly faster than uncorrected Gaussian line-search descent, and dimension two terminates in two steps almost surely.
