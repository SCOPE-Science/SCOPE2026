# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Positive independent Italian support in each K_n layer contains at most one vertex. For n>=3, support cannot omit a whole layer: a nonmatched zero vertex of that layer would have no positive neighbor. It is {a_i,b_j}, with i≠j by matching-edge independence. A third index k distinct from i,j has a_k adjacent to only a_i in the positive support, forcing f(a_i)=2; similarly f(b_j)=2. Every such off-matching pair of twos is feasible, giving n(n−1) functions of weight four and exact polynomial n(n−1)z^4. n=2 has eight functions and min weight two, explicitly outside theorem. Definition-level brute-force tests of all 3^(2n) ternary labels for n=2..5 independently confirm totals 8,6,12,20 and weight structure.

## originality

PASS

Best-of-knowledge: independent Italian domination is synonymous with independent Roman {2}-domination. The primary introducing/tree articles treat the general parameter, and Wei–Lu treat block graphs; n>=3 complete prisms have induced four-cycles and are not block graphs. The published Roman {3} formula on complete prisms and Roman {2} results on complete multipartite graphs impose different conditions or use nonisomorphic graph families, so do not imply this precise all-function classification. Search did not locate a same-object theorem, but absence from indices does not establish absolute priority.

## value

PASS

Complete prisms K_n□K_2 are natural Cartesian-product benchmarks for domination variants. Classifying every feasible independent-Italian labeling, not just a minimum number, identifies the sharp n=2 exception and supplies an exact multiplicity/weight enumerator for any n. Although the two-clique proof is short, its non-implied all-feasible-set classification is a mathematically usable exact invariant.
