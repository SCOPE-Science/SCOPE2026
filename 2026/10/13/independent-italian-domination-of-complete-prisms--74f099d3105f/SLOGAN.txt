For every \(n\ge3\), every independent Italian dominating function of the complete prism \(K_n\square K_2\) consists of two label-\(2\) vertices, one in each clique layer, chosen off the matching. Hence all feasible functions are minimum, \(i_I(K_n\square K_2)=4\), and the exact weight enumerator is \(n(n-1)z^4\).
