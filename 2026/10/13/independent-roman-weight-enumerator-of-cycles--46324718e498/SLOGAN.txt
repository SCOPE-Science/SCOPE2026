For every \(n\ge5\), independent Roman dominating functions on \(C_n\) are exactly cyclic concatenations of the three forced gaps \(2,0,2\), \(2,0,0,2\), and \(2,0,1,0,2\). This gives the exact all-weight enumerator \(I_n(x)=\sum_{2a+3b+4c=n} n(a+b+c-1)!/(a!b!c!)\,x^{2a+2b+3c}\) and an exact census of minimum-weight functions.
