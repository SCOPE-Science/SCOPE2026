For Gaussian-sketched two-sided Gram--Schmidt, an arbitrarily small departure from parallel input vectors imposes a universal second-moment failure on the first normalized column, irrespective of finite sketch dimension. Even two individually orthonormal input bases with a well-conditioned cross-Gram matrix can produce an output with \(\mathbb E\kappa_2(Q)=+\infty\), with a proven inverse-linear lower tail.
