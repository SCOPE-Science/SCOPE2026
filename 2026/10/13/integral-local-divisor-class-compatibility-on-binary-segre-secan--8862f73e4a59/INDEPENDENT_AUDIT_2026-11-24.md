# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The chart lattice is full: a triple minus a pair gives each (0,e_i), then a pair gives the height direction. Pairing the primitive rays with the character basis gives D_i^0=A_i-B and sum A_i=2B, a primitive relation. To justify the passage from affine to algebraic local class groups: at the vertex of the pointed affine slice the map is surjective by localization and injective by the positive grading. The rank-one graded reflexive module of an invariant divisor, if free at the homogeneous maximal ideal, has one homogeneous generator modulo that ideal and hence globally by graded Nakayama. Smooth affine coordinates at a closed point can be translated and graded as well. At the pair vertex invert its primitive monomial (1,e_i+e_j): the resulting semigroup splits into a Laurent direction and the slice having precisely the incident rays. The n coordinate rays are a basis of the integral annihilator of that monomial; the extra ray has coefficients -1,-1,+1 elsewhere. Thus the slice class group is Z, generated by D_*, with the two incident A's both equal to that generator. For a closed point on the punctured orbit the same local group follows by specializing the smooth coordinates of the split product (local freeness of the extended rank-one module specializes to local freeness at the slice vertex); at the generic point the slice is over the function field of the orbit times the smooth factor. Transitivity on rank-one and rank-two matrix strata and regularity off the singular locus give all closed-point groups. The restrictions are w_ij=a_i+a_j+b. For an integral four-cycle solution, t_i=w_ij+w_ik-w_jk is independent of auxiliary pair by the quadrilateral equations, including at n=4, and t_i+t_j=2w_ij gives a common parity. Choosing b of that parity produces integral a_i=(t_i-b)/2. The only kernel is the displayed relation, and the image is an integer kernel, so the quotient is torsion-free of the stated rank.

## originality

PASS

The prior toric chart, rays, and failure of local Q-factoriality are credited; the exact local ranks are standard deductions from those inputs. What distinguishes the result is the simultaneous integral generization map from the common Segre stratum, its saturated image, and the exact obstruction equations. The closest distinct indexed examples do not establish that map, and the primary paper's four-index binomials apply to a different object (coordinate functions, not class restrictions).

## value

PASS

The restriction lattice answers a natural local-to-generic question: which independently specified Cartier defects along the singular components come from a single class at their common Segre stratum? It gives effective integral obstruction equations, not just a list of ranks or a rational span. This is useful for studying simultaneous Weil-divisor behavior near that stratum.
