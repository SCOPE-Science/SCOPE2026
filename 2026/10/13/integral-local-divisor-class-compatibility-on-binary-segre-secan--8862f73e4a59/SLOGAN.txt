For the secant of \((\mathbb P^1)^n\), \(n\ge4\), the algebraic local Weil divisor-class rank is \(n\) on the Segre stratum and \(1\) on every other singular point. Restrictions from a Segre point to the \(\binom n2\) generic singular components form exactly the integer complete-graph edge labels satisfying all quadrilateral-balancing identities; the obstruction lattice is torsion-free of rank \(\binom n2-n\).
