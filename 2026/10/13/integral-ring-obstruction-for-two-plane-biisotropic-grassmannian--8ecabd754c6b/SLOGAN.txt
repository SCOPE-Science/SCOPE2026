For every \(n\ge2\), the odd two-plane biisotropic Grassmannian and the ordinary Grassmannian \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,2n)\) have identical graded integral cohomology groups but nonisomorphic graded integral cohomology and Chow rings. Their primitive Plücker top powers are the unequal Catalan numbers \(C_{2n-1}\) and \(C_{2n-2}\), giving a uniform multiplicative obstruction.
