The \(800\) conics on the Mukai quartic have only two local intersection profiles: \(480\) conics have \((179,440,179,1)\) neighbors at multiplicities \(0,1,2,4\), while the other \(320\) have \((183,432,183,1)\). The \(480\)-class is exactly the unique projective orbit of that size, and the \(400\) multiplicity-\(4\) pairs are the residual conic pairs in plane sections.
