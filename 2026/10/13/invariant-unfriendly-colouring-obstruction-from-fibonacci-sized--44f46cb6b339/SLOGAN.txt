A Fibonacci-growth replacement of the odd majority fibres in a recent Borel unfriendly-colouring counterexample admits a finite **invariant** counting probability of exact normalizing mass \(11/3\), yet no measurable unfriendly two-colouring exists even almost everywhere. The graph is closed, hyperfinite, bipartite and one-ended componentwise, has minimum degree \(3\), and has infinite mean degree: this proves the necessity of finite average degree in the invariant-probability unfriendly-colouring theorem.
