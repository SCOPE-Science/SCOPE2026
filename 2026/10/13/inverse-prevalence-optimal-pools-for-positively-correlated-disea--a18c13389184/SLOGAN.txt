When within-pool disease-status correlation remains positive as prevalence \(p\) vanishes, the optimal two-stage Dorfman group size is asymptotic to \(1/(\tau p)\), rather than the independent-model \(p^{-1/2}\). The sharp per-person test cost is \(\tau p[\log(1/(\tau p))+1-\psi(\tau)]+o(p)\), and a monotone adjacent-cost ratio determines the exact integer optimum.
