The critical-distance discriminant for \(\operatorname{pGr}(k,n)\) has exactly \(\min(k,n-k)\) irreducible factors in universal spectral-coefficient space: the known eigenvalue discriminant and one new equal-disjoint-\(r\)-sum resonance factor for every \(2\le r\le\min(k,n-k)\). Their degrees and discriminant multiplicities are explicit, and generic points of every resonance component retain distinct eigenvalues while exhibiting a prescribed number of double critical values.
