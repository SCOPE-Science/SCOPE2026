For every crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n\) with \(n\ge3\), ordinary irredundant sets are exactly the one-sided sets, deleted-matching pairs, and four small disjoint mixed index patterns, giving closed all-set and maximal-set enumerators. Deleting the perfect matching from \(K_{n,n}\) leaves \(\operatorname{ir}=2\) and \(\operatorname{IR}=n\) unchanged while creating new mixed layers in degrees \(3\) and \(4\).
