For every \(n\ge4\), the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M\) has exactly two isolate secure dominating sets: its two bipartition classes. Thus \(\gamma_{0s}(\operatorname{Cr}_n)=n\), whereas the initiating isolate-secure paper shows that the undeleted complete bipartite graph admits no isolate secure dominating set.
