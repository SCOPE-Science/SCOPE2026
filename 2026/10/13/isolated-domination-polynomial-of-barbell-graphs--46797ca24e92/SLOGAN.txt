For the barbell graph \(B_n\) formed from two copies of \(K_n\) joined by one bridge, every isolate dominating set is characterized by a singleton on at least one clique side, with one bridge-endpoint exception. This gives an exact bivariate enumerator and shows that all minimal isolate dominating sets are the \(n^2-1\) cross-clique pairs other than the bridge pair, so \(\gamma_0(B_n)=\Gamma_0(B_n)=2\) for every \(n\ge2\).
