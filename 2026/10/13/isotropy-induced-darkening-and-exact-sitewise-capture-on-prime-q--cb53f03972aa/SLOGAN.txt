On an odd-prime square torus with one lossy vertex, equal horizontal and vertical hopping yields exact capture probability \(1/8\) at generic sites and \(1/4\) on the four coordinate/diagonal directions; every unequal positive rational hopping ratio instead gives \(1/4\) off the coordinate axes and \(1/2\) on them. The distinction follows from a prime cyclotomic resonance and holds at every positive loss rate, with bright-subspace dimensions \((p+1)(p+3)/8\) and \((p+1)^2/4\), respectively.
