Jaccard ranks binary community pairs by \(u=(b+c)/a\), while squared Ochiai ranks them by \(u+bc/a^2\); they reverse exactly when those two keys order a pair of comparisons oppositely. No strict reversal is possible through gamma richness \(4\), and gamma richness \(5\) is sharp with the unique first pair \((1,0,4)\) versus \((1,1,2)\).
