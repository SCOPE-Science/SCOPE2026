For every general divisor \(D\in|kH+mL|\) in the residual range on a very general quartic K3 containing a line, its intrinsic Jacobian singular scheme has ideal \(\mathcal I_L^{r-1}\) for even \(k\), and \(\mathcal I_L^{r-1}\mathcal I_q\) for odd \(k\), where \(r=m-\lfloor k/2\rfloor\). The odd case adds exactly one embedded point of length one when \(r\ge2\).
