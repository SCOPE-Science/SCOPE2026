# Verification

The proof gives the exact local identities \((\partial_u u^r,\partial_v u^r)=(u^{r-1})\) and \((\partial_u(u^rv),\partial_v(u^rv))=u^{r-1}(u,v)\) for all integers \(r\ge1\) over characteristic zero. It also establishes the forced fixed part by intersection theory and invokes Castorena–Vite for existence of a smooth residual curve. The standalone `checker.py` performs finite symbolic sanity checks; those checks alone are not a proof of the universal family. No independent audit has been performed.
