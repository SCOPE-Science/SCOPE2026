Implicit \(\operatorname{collapse}(n)\) checks have pure-readout distance \(n\), but their effective joint patch-\(X\)/readout detector has distance \(2\), and an accepted wrong inferred logical-\(X\) sign first appears at total fault weight \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor+1\). An exact two-state transfer matrix gives the acceptance and conditional sign-error probabilities for independent data and readout faults.
