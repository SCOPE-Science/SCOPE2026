For every finite field and every rank-\(r\) rectangular sandwich matrix, the complete bivariate ordinary-rank enumerator of commuting ordered idempotent pairs is a Gaussian multinomial sum with a single lift factor \(q^{(m+n-2r)(b+c+d)}\). The simultaneous four-eigenspace decomposition yields exact counts and a sharp large-rectangle commutation-probability asymptotic; previously known single-idempotent enumerations are explicitly credited.
