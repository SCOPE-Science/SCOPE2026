Pairs of commuting finite equivalence modalities admit an exact generating function simultaneously recording both individual partition ranks and the image cardinality \(2^c\) of their composite. The sharp feasibility threshold for fixed individual ranks \(a,b\) jumps from \(n\geq ab\) for one composite class to \(n\geq a+b-c\) for \(c\geq2\), refining previously tabulated unweighted commuting-pair totals.
