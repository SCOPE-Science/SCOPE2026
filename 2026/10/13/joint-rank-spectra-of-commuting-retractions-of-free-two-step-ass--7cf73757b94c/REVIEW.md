# Scientific review: joint rank spectra of commuting retractions

## Correctness — PASS
Every algebra endomorphism preserves the square ideal \(V\otimes V\); its action there is forced to be \(A\otimes A\), while its generator lift can be any linear map into that ideal. The commuting composition-idempotent hypothesis makes the degree-one and degree-two actions simultaneous modules for the split algebra generated by commuting projections. Splitting the short exact sequence gives a **single** algebra-automorphism shear that eliminates all lifts at once. The stabilizer of that shear is exactly the space of joint-eigenspace-preserving maps, with dimension \(\sum_\epsilon n_\epsilon m_\epsilon\). The multiplicity and rank formulas follow with no assumptions on field characteristic. The separately implemented exhaustive binary enumeration agrees for \(n=1,2\), including all \(868\) ordered pairs at \(n=2\) and the two different product-rank strata \(384\) and \(96\) among pairs with both individual ranks \(2\). Finite tests are only corroboration, not the proof.

## Originality — PASS with residual bibliographic risk
The closest inspected primary work on idempotent endomorphisms (Facchini–Heidari Zadeh, arXiv:2210.08230, Theorem 1.2 and Remark 4.1) describes split morphisms/augmentations but not this algebra, its ordered-tensor lifting fibers or the joint ranks of all products. Kanel-Belov–Berzins–Lipyanski (arXiv:math/0512273, Theorems 4.1–4.2) concern automorphisms of the entire endomorphism semigroup of an *untruncated* free algebra, not these finite-field tuple counts. Drensky–Makar-Limanov (arXiv:1909.13262, Theorems 3.3 and 4.4) concern locally nilpotent derivations of a free algebra, not endomorphism retractions. Published treatments of commuting projections provide an underlying linear-algebra reduction, but do not alone supply the nonlinear algebra endomorphism count; the exact exponent comes from the tensor-square action and its shared-shear stabilizer. Equivalent enumeration in older terminology remains a risk; negative searches are not proof of absolute priority.

## Value — PASS
The object is the initial nonunital associative algebra on \(n\) generators subject to cubic nilpotency. Classifying every commuting retraction family of arbitrary length \(k\) is a natural problem about its endomorphism monoid; joint ranks of all products are intrinsic labeled statistics. The exact finite-field exponent couples every eigenspace color to the coordinatewise-AND distribution on degree two, and the polynomial determines all attainable product rank patterns and their multiplicities. It is neither a finite isolated example nor a renamed single-retraction count. The concrete six-dimensional result provides a fully reproducible first nontrivial instance.
