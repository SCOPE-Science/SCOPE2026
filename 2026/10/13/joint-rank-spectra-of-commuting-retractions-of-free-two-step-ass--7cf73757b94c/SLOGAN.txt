Every ordered commuting family of algebra retractions of the free nonunital two-step associative algebra \(V\oplus(V\otimes V)\) is simultaneously conjugate to projections on its generators. The resulting \(2^k\)-color decomposition gives exact finite-field multiplicities for the ranks of all products of the \(k\) retractions; for \(k=n=q=2\), there are exactly \(868\) commuting ordered pairs.
