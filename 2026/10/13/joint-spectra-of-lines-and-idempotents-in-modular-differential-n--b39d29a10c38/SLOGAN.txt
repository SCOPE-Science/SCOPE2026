For every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\) of characteristic \(p\), the full \(q^p\)-parameter truncated differential Novikov family \(f\circ g=f(g'+ag)\) has exactly four joint possibilities for its numbers of one-dimensional subalgebras and nonzero idempotents. Two canonical top-coefficient invariants have uniform \(q^{p-2}\)-element fibers, producing a complete characteristic-uniform joint distribution and explicit representatives of every line subalgebra.
