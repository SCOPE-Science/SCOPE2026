# Scientific review notes

**Correctness.** Level recovery and the missing-comparability graph reduce the problem to permutations of paths with identical level intervals. Exactly the paths passing through every retained level can have multiplicity greater than one. This gives both the necessity and the sufficiency of the group formula, including the first rigid depth.

**Scope and relationship to existing results.** The canonical Esakia spaces and their generating properties come from the original construction. A separate result, *Automorphism rigidity of the canonical strictly finite Heyting witnesses*, proves the infinite and dual-algebra rigidity. The distinct claim here concerns the exact finite groups and sharp finite cutoff; it does not assign priority between the two rigidity arguments.

**Value.** The finite profile distinguishes qualitative rigidity from the quantitative amount of adjacent-level information needed to destroy every symmetry, and identifies all the symmetries before that threshold.
