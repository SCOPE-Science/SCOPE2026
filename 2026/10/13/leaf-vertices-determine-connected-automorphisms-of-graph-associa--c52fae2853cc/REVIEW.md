# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The fan and dual lattice are stated explicitly. Every possible Demazure root is exhausted using the singleton rays and zero-sum equation, not inferred from experiments. The critical two-element tubes prove the graph-theoretic necessity; the leaf condition proves the converse. The Cox derivation formulas prove commutativity, and the standard complete-toric automorphism structure theorem supplies the group decomposition. The excluded two-vertex case is essential. The bounded exact checker covers all 770 connected labeled graphs on three through five vertices, but general validity rests on the symbolic proof.

## Originality

PASS, with residual priority risk. The closest source for the objects, Ferreira da Rosa--Jensen--Ranganathan (arXiv:1411.0537, Construction 1/Definitions 5--7), builds the fan but does not classify its connected algebraic automorphism groups. Nill (arXiv:math/0407491, Section 2) provides the general Demazure criterion but not its specialization to graph leaves. Eur--Huh--Larson (arXiv:2207.10605, Section 3) covers the star case via an additive-group action; that special case is explicitly prior art. The claim is the **complete graph-wide characterization**, including the exclusion of all nonleaf roots and abelianity of the full unipotent radical. No search can exclude unindexed equivalent specializations, which remain a literature risk.

## Value

PASS. This is a natural structural classification of the full connected algebraic automorphism group for every connected graph associahedron, not an arbitrary parameter sample or a restatement of a computational recipe. It identifies exactly when the geometric symmetry group is reductive, determines its unipotent radical and dimension, and exhibits a sharp graph-theoretic boundary between torus-rigid and nonreductive varieties. The explicit root dictionary applies across paths, trees, cycles, stars and mixed graph structures.

## Boundaries

Only the identity component over the complex numbers is determined, not the disconnected group or field forms. The two-vertex graph is excluded. Finite tests are regression checks, not independent or exhaustive validation of all graph orders.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
