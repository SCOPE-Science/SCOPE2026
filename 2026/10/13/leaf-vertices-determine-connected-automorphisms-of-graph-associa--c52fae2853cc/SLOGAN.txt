For every connected simple graph \(G\) with at least three vertices, each leaf contributes exactly one commuting additive automorphism to its graph-associahedral toric variety, and no other Demazure roots occur. Thus \(\operatorname{Aut}(X_G)^\circ\cong\mathbb G_a^{\ell(G)}\rtimes\mathbb G_m^{|V(G)|-1}\), and this connected group is reductive exactly when \(G\) has no leaves.
