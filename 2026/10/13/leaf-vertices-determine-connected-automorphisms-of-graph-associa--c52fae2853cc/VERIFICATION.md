# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For the fan with primitive rays v_I for proper connected tubes and character lattice of zero-sum integer vectors, a negative nonsingleton distinguished tube is impossible because singleton-ray pairings would all be nonnegative. With distinguished singleton i, sum-zero forces exactly one other +1 coordinate j. All proper two-vertex tubes {i,k} then demand k=j, hence i is a leaf with neighbor j; conversely any leaf satisfies all tube inequalities. In a connected graph with >=3 vertices no two leaves are adjacent, so the Cox derivations for distinct leaves commute and cannot have opposite roots. Standard Demazure-Cox theorem yields (G_a)^ell semidirect (G_m)^(n-1). I checked the n=2 exception where opposite roots occur. An independent new graph enumeration tested all 4+38+728 connected labeled graphs on 3,4,5 vertices and all possible root inequalities (3+28+475 graphs possess leaf roots); the finite check is not substituted for the infinite proof.
