In a uniform random recursive tree, leaf count \(L_n\) and total path length \(P_n\) satisfy the exact law \(\operatorname{Cov}(L_n,P_n)=-(n-2)/4\), so every growth step contributes exactly \(-1/4\) to their covariance. The corresponding least-squares search-cost slope tends to \(-3\), while the correlation itself vanishes like \(n^{-1/2}\).
