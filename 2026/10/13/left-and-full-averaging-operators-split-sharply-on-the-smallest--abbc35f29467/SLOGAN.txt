For the canonical two-dimensional non-Lie Leibniz algebra \(\{e_1,e_2\}=e_2\), left averaging operators form two affine planes, while full averaging operators collapse to three lines. Over \(\mathbf F_q\), the counts drop from \(2q^2-q\) operators in \(q^2+q\) conjugacy classes to \(3q-2\) operators in \(2q\) classes, and the full descendants have only four di-Leibniz isomorphism types.
