In the unit-rate Kingman coalescent, cherry count is positively associated with the number of two-descendant ancestral lineages at every nondegenerate lineage level. Integrating this exact levelwise covariance gives \(\operatorname{Cov}(C_n,L_{n,2})=5/18\) for every sample size \(n\ge5\), despite the growth of expected cherry count with \(n\).
