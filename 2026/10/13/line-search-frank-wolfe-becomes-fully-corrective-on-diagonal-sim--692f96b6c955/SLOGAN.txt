For \(f(x)=\tfrac12\sum_i a_i x_i^2\) on the probability simplex with \(a_i>0\), exact-line-search Frank–Wolfe with the standard vertex oracle is already fully corrective whenever it starts from a face minimizer. Each step adds one unseen vertex and lands exactly at the minimizer on the enlarged face, so a vertex start reaches the global inverse-curvature weights in exactly \(m-1\) nonstationary steps.
