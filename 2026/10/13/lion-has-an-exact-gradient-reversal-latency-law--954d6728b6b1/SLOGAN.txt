After a persistent gradient reversal, Lion keeps the old update sign exactly while \(\beta_2^{\,n-1}>r/[\beta_1(1+r)]\). At the defining defaults \(\beta_1=0.9\), \(\beta_2=0.99\), an equal-magnitude reversal produces \(59\) stale-sign updates, while an immediate strict sign flip requires a reversal larger than nine times the previous gradient.
