# Scientific review notes

**Correctness.** The floor-to-divisor conversion and coefficientwise finite Dirichlet inverse give the signed polynomial. Inclusion-exclusion counts exactly the occupied squarefree slots, including when several primes repeat. The extremal coefficients give the degree and exact vanishing order. Finite exact checks supplement the symbolic argument.

**Scope and relationship to existing results.** The arbitrary-input recurrence and divisor reformulation are due to Andrews, Kauffman and Sahoo. The sharp affine prime criterion is independently stated and proved in the separate *Sharp affine slope threshold for fixed-function Möbius primality* record linked in `RESULT.md`; no distinct contribution is claimed for that threshold. Its squarefree and single-prime formulas and general congruence are narrower than the full ordered-squarefree coefficient formula for mixed exponents. This comparison does not establish historical priority or exclude other equivalent work.

**Value.** The full polynomial determines both the Liouville sign for slopes at least two and the way zeros of the classical Möbius function disappear when the slope changes. The maximal prime exponent is encoded in the exact multiplicity at slope one. No computational-speed advantage is claimed.
