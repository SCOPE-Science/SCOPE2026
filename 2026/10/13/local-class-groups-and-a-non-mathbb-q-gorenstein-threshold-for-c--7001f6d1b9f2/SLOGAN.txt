For the projective edge toric variety of the crown graph \(K_{n,n}\setminus M\), every coordinate fixed point has local class group \(\mathbb Z^2\), but its canonical class is \((n-3,n-3)\), hence is never \(\mathbb Q\)-Cartier for \(n\ge4\). The explicit normal affine cones show a sharp change from the Gorenstein six-cycle boundary at \(n=3\).
