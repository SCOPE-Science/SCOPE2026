For Nakamura–Shibata elliptic-cone threefolds, the full local Weil class group is \(\operatorname{Pic}^0(E/G)\) at the distinguished singular point and \(\operatorname{Pic}^0(E)\) at every other closed point of the singular axis. The two maps from the global class group differ by the degree-\(m\) dual isogeny; its kernel is the canonical torsion class, and no singular-axis point is \(\mathbf Q\)-factorial.
