# Verification notes

The proof has no finite-computation dependency. Reproduce it by checking the following exact steps using the definitions in RESULT.md: descend the equivariant degree-\(m\) bundle along the free isogeny and verify the descended degree is one; descend the primitive affine-line character to an exact-order-\(m\) degree-zero line bundle; identify the smooth modification as a rank-two vector-bundle total space; use \(\mathcal O(F)=\pi^*A^{-1}\) in divisor localization; and test local principal divisors by restriction to the contracted fibers \(B\) at zero and \(E\) away from zero. The kernel of pullback on \(\operatorname{Pic}^0\) is the character group of the connected cyclic cover and is generated by the canonical divisor class. Finite-étale descent proves that the latter is locally Cartier away from the distinguished point.
