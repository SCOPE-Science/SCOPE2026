# Review of Local fractional strong metric dimension collapses to local fractional metric dimension

## Correctness
PASS. For adjacent \(u,v\), triangle inequality gives \(|d(w,u)-d(w,v)|\le1\). Hence unequal distances are equivalent to a difference of one. Equality \(d(w,u)=d(w,v)+1\) means a shortest \(w\)-\(v\) path followed by \(vu\) is a shortest \(w\)-\(u\) path, so \(v\) lies on a geodesic and \(w\) strongly resolves the pair; the converse is immediate from the geodesic equality. Therefore the two edge neighborhoods are identical, so the feasible functions, all optimizers, and optimum values coincide. The published local-fractional characterization of value one then gives bipartite graphs exactly. Exhaustive direct checks on all connected labeled graphs through six vertices agree.

## Originality
PASS. The 2018 local-fractional paper and the 2021 paper introducing the local-strong fractional parameter were compared at the definition and theorem level. The latter states only an inequality between the parameters and gives a path-only characterization at value one, rather than the universal equality. The 2023 continuation retains the separate local-strong terminology and computations. Targeted exact-phrase and semantic searches found no source stating that the two local neighborhoods coincide for every edge or that the two fractional parameters are universally identical.

## Value
PASS. The result collapses a separately introduced fractional graph invariant to an existing one on every connected graph, not merely on a special family. It removes the need for a second linear program, transfers the complete local-fractional theory immediately, and corrects the basic value-one characterization under the published definitions. This is a structural equivalence with consequences for all subsequent computations of the local-strong fractional parameter.
