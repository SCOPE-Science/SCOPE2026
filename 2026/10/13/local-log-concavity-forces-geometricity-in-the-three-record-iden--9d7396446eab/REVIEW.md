# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The record-transition formula and the two lowest totals give exact formulas for \(p_3\) and \(p_4\). The two adjacent log-concavity defects are exact negatives up to a strictly positive factor, forcing equality. The resulting scalar equation has the unique positive root that fixes \(p_2=p_1(1-p_1)\). A triangular coefficient argument then proves the full infinite geometric law. The included checker confirms the algebra and representative exact identities but is not used as an exhaustive proof.

## Originality
PASS. Arnold and Villasenor leave their general Conjecture 2 open and, for \(m=3\), derive only the first recurrence relation before speculating that non-geometric solutions may exist. Targeted searches for the exact identity, aliases, local log-concavity conditions, and geometric conclusion found no covering result. A recent paper citing the source was inspected and concerns a different martingale characterization of \(\delta\)-records. Residual risk remains that an equivalent argument could be present in poorly indexed characterization literature.

## Value
PASS. The result resolves the full \(m=3\) conjectural identity on the natural class of strictly positive log-concave mass functions, and in fact shows that only log-concavity at two adjacent indices is needed. It directly addresses the source's stated uncertainty about whether the three-record identity can force geometricity.

## Closest literature and limitations

The claim does not settle unrestricted \(m=3\), does not construct or exclude non-log-concave counterexamples, and does not address \(m>3\).
