Every perfect matching of the Coxeter graph has forcing number \(4\), exactly \(198\) minimum forcing four-sets, and a local forcing polynomial with eleven nonzero coefficients; the \(16{,}632\) minimum four-sets form \(51\) symmetry orbits. Only \(2\) vertex-disjoint alternating cycles can coexist, giving a uniform forcing-versus-packing gap of \(2\).
