For the published nearly-free degree-nine conic–line example, the complete singularity profile is one \(A_3\), two \(D_4\) points, and two five-branch non-quasihomogeneous germs with \((\mu,\tau)=(20,18)\). The total defect \(\mu-\tau=4\) is exactly two equal local defects, and each exceptional germ has monodromy \((t-1)(t^5-1)(t^7-1)^2\).
