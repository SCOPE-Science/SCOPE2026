For the Thom--Sebastiani free curves \(D=V(z(\prod_j\ell_j^{k_j}-z^d)\prod_j\ell_j)\), the only singularities are one ordinary \(m\)-fold point and \(m\) weighted-homogeneous germs \(uv(u^{k_j}-v^d)=0\). Their global threshold is \(\min\{2/m,(d+K)/(dK+d+K)\}\) with \(K=\max_j k_j\), so fixed degree and fixed free exponents can conceal different log-canonical thresholds.
