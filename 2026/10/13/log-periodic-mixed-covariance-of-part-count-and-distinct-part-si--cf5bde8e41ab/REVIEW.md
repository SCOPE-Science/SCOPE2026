# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The separator-bit influence identity follows from the independent fair Bernoulli representation, and the local split indicator formula accounts for coincident child sizes and removal of the parent size. For rare central lengths, Arratia–Goldstein–Gordon's verified Poisson void bound applies to locally dependent separator words; incompatible close overlaps and the single possible shared endpoint imply the stated dependency-neighborhood bounds. Deleting the vicinity of the changed cut isolates independent remote indicators and provides a uniform local conditional error. Small-size void probabilities decay superpolynomially, while large-size pivotal events have summable geometric tails. Their errors remain \(o(1)\) after summing over all sites and sizes, and the Mellin/Fourier step has absolutely convergent tails. The exact finite recurrence and separate enumeration agree for all directly tested sizes. The full asymptotic relies on the proof, not on a finite computation.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

The closest detailed earlier analyses are Hwang–Yeh (1997), Hitczenko–Stengle (2000/2001), and Hitczenko–Louchard (2001): their inspected conclusions establish distinct-size expectations, marked occupancy generating functions, marginal distributions, or related moments, not the displayed two-term mixed covariance with the part count. Bender–Canfield (2005) give general central/local limit results for fixed-size part counts and first-order distinct-size growth, not this growing-size mixed covariance. Finch (2020) studies part count versus maximum part size, which is not the statistic here. Public mathematical-record searches found analogous digital oscillation for distinct substrings but no stronger or equivalent result for this joint composition statistic. Broad marked generating functions may encode the joint covariance implicitly, and unlocated historical work could contain the asymptotic; these remain genuine risks, rather than proofs of originality from absence of matches.

## Value — PASS

The number of parts measures fragmentation, whereas distinct part sizes measure diversity. Their dependence is not determined by either marginal expectation. The theorem finds a negative order-\(\log n\) mixed covariance, resolves a nonconstant logarithmic-scale modulation that is invisible to a constant-coefficient limit, and supplies its constant-order periodic correction with absolute \(o(1)\) accuracy. The rare-size mechanism and exact finite checker make the asymptotic both interpretable and reproducible.

## Limits

No global finite-size covariance sign theorem is claimed, and a numerical finite census is not an infinite proof. The Gamma Fourier coefficient proves nonconstancy analytically. Bibliographic priority is not independently certified; a stronger older joint formula may be derivable to overlap this result.
