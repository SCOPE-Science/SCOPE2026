The greedy coprime permutation's record values have unequal natural densities in the two possible residue classes modulo \(6\): the class \(1\) exceeds class \(5\) by more than \(1/35\) of all integers. Each class count has logarithmic discrepancy, \(C_a(X)=\delta_a X+O(\log X)\), and the density gap has a certified 15-digit rational enclosure.
