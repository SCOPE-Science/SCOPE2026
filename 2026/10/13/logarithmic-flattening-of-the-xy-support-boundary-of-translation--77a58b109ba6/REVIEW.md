# Review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The complementary modulus in the source energy formula is exactly \(|\gamma|\), so DLMF 19.12.2 gives the displayed endpoint expansion. Differentiation yields \(\epsilon''(\gamma)\to-\infty\) and \(\epsilon'''(\gamma)\sim(4\pi\gamma)^{-1}\). The local exposed point is \((a,b)=(\epsilon',\epsilon-\gamma\epsilon')\), hence \(g'=-\gamma\) and \(g''=-1/\epsilon''\). This gives \(g''(a)\sim4\pi/\log(1/|a|)\), \(g''(0)=0\), and divergent \(g'''=\epsilon'''/(\epsilon'')^3\).

## Originality
**PASS.** The elliptic expansion and XY anisotropy criticality are credited prior facts. The primary paper instead asserts analyticity and does not analyze the dual projected boundary. Searches in source-title, correction, logarithmic, Legendre-transform, curvature, and quantum-marginal language found no implication-equivalent statement of the \(C^2\)-not-\(C^3\) boundary law or its logarithmic flattening asymptotic.

## Value
**PASS.** The theorem converts a known critical support singularity into precise geometry of the reduced-state projection that motivates the source, while correcting a theorem-level regularity error and preserving the main non-semialgebraicity conclusion by a local mechanism.

## Closest literature and limitations
Blakaj--Wolf supplies the exact support function and semialgebraicity framework; DLMF supplies the elliptic endpoint expansion; classical XY literature supplies the criticality background. None of those sources states the projected-boundary regularity theorem. The result applies only to the explicitly defined two-observable projection and its graph coordinates.
