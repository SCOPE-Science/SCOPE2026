In the natural two-observable projection of \(\operatorname{Red}_2\), the anisotropic-XY lower boundary is \(C^2\) but not \(C^3\) at the isotropic point, with \(g''(a)\sim4\pi/\log(1/|a|)\). The dual support function has a \(\gamma^2\log|\gamma|\) singularity, correcting the published analyticity claim and locally obstructing semialgebraicity.
