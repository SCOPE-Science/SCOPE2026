At half-integer flux, the symmetric magnetic ring chain has zero limiting momentum-band density but not finite total band length: the occupied momentum measure grows as \(4(\pi\ell)^{-1}\log K+O(1)\). In energy, each high band has asymptotic width \(8/(\pi\ell)\), so the cumulative non-flat band measure grows as \(8(\pi\ell)^{-1}\sqrt E+O(1)\).
