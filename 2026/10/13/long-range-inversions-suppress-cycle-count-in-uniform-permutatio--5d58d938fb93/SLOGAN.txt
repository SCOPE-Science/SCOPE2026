For a uniform random permutation, an inversion spanning distance \(d\) has covariance \(-(2d-1)/(2n(n-1))\) with total cycle count. Summing over all pairs gives \(\operatorname{Cov}(C_n,\operatorname{Inv}_n)=-(2n-1)/12\), which is also the exact first derivative of expected Mallows cycle count at \(q=1\).
