A lovely pair of real closed fields has arbitrarily large finite dp-rank already in one object variable: finite families of \(P\)-linear cosets give explicit inp-patterns of every depth. Therefore every fixed finite unary dp-rank cutoff is excluded from Kim's larger class \(\mathfrak D^h_{n\text{-var}}\), because RCF has unary dp-rank \(1\) while its lovely-pair expansion violates every such cutoff.
