Independent reconstruction: let B be the sum of J_i J_j O_i O_j over distinct unordered commuting pairs. Then H²=P₁I+2B and tr B=0. In tr B² only two copies of the same edge survive; in tr B³ the only even-degree multiset of three loopless edges is a triangle. Each of its six orders contributes x_i x_j x_k because all three vertices commute. Thus M₄=P₁²+4 times the weighted commuting-edge sum, and M₆=P₁³+12P₁ times that sum +48 times the weighted commuting-triangle sum. The two sums are (P₁²−P₂)/2−E_x and (P₁³−3P₁P₂+2P₃)/6−(P₁E_x−Q_x)+W_x−T_x, by inclusion-exclusion. This gives both displayed moments and, by expansion of the logarithm at Z(0)=1, both cumulants. Odd moments vanish by the stated redundancy condition. For m>0 with unit magnitudes, 12(−c₄)/m−1 gives d and c₆ then gives t in the stated regular family. Isospectrality would force all normalized trace moments to agree; if physical coupling scales are allowed to differ between equal-magnitude m-term instances, equality of M₂ already forces equal scales. The weighted recovery is with the couplings specified (P₂ known), not blind identification of E_x from one number with unknown couplings.
