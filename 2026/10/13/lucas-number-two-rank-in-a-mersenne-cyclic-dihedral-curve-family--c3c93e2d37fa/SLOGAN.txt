For odd \(r\ge3\), the smooth characteristic-two curve \(y^{2^r-1}=x^3+x^{-3}\) has Jacobian \(2\)-rank exactly \(2(L_r-1)\), where \(L_r\) is the Lucas number. The formula comes from a Cartier-operator classification by cyclic binary words with no adjacent zeros, refining the previously known nonordinarity bound.
