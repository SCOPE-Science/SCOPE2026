On a deterministic alternating gradient, practical M-SVAG asymptotically reports corrected variance \(2G^2/(1+\beta)\) and attenuates the update to \((1-\beta)^2/[(1+\beta)(3-\beta)]\) of the current gradient, despite the input having no stochastic variance. At the source value \(\beta=0.9\), the exact alternating-to-constant update gain is \(1/399\).
