For an \(n\)-cycle equipped with arbitrary selected adjacent one-state merging letters, the exact shortest reset word is the shortest backward cyclic scan visiting marked sites \(n-1\) times. With exactly two merging letters the reset length is \(n(n-2)/2+1\) for even \(n\), but \(n(n-3)/2+1+d\) for odd \(n\), where \(d\) is their shorter circular gap.
