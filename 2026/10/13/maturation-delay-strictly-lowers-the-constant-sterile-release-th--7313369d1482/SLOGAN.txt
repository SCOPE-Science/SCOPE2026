In the constant-release delayed mosquito model, the critical sterile-release rate decreases strictly with maturation delay and collapses quadratically at \(\tau_c=\mu_0^{-1}\log(a_0/\mu_1)\). For the paper's Figure 1 parameters the correct threshold at \(\tau=0.2\) is about \(21.0433\), not \(21.93\), and with \(b=21\) positive equilibria disappear already at \(\tau\approx0.209893\).
