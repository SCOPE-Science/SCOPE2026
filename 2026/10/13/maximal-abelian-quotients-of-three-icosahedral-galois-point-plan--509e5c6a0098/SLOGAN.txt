For the three icosahedral Galois-point plane curves of degrees \(12,20,30\), the commutator subgroup of the full automorphism group is \(\operatorname{SL}(2,5)\). Their maximal abelian quotient curves have genera \(1,2,4\) and residual cyclic signatures \( (2,3,6)\), \( (2,5,10)\), and \( (3,5,15)\), respectively.
