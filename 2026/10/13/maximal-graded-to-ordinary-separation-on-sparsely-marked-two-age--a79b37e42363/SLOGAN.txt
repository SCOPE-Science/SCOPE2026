A single propositional bit marking only \(2n-1\) cells of an \(n\times n\) two-agent S5 grid leaves ordinary modal logic with exactly two types, while graded modal logic distinguishes all \(n^2\) cells. The graded depth spectrum is exactly \(2,(t+1)^2+3,n^2\) in its respective ranges, giving the sharp possible separation ratio \(n^2/2\) and first full distinction at depth \(n-1\).
