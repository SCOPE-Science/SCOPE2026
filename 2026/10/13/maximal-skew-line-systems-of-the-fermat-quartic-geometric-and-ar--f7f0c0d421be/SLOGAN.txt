The \(48\) lines of the standard Fermat quartic admit exactly \(128\) maximum twelve-skew-line systems, forming two projective symmetry orbits of sizes \(32\) and \(96\). All unordered systems descend to \(\mathbb Q(i)\), and exactly \(16\) descend to \(\mathbb Q\), although each individual line requires \(\mathbb Q(\zeta_8)\).
