For a heterogeneous windmill \(K_1\vee(K_{m_1}\cup\cdots\cup K_{m_r})\) with \(r\ge2\) and every \(m_i\ge2\), every maximum Grundy total dominating sequence either takes two vertices from every blade, in arbitrary order, or takes two from all but one blade, one from that blade, and the center last. This yields exact product-sum formulas for both the maximum sets and all of their legal orderings.
