For \(R=\prod_i\mathbb F_{q_i}\) with every \(q_i\ge3\), maximum independent sets of the zero-divisor graph are determined one complementary support pair at a time: always take the heavier support cell, and choose either side only when the two cell sizes tie. Thus \(\alpha(\Gamma(R))=\sum_{\{S,S^c\}}\max\{w(S),w(S^c)\}\), and the number of maximum independent sets is \(2^\tau\).
