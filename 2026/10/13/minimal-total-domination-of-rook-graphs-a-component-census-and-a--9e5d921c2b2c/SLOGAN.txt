Inclusion-minimal total dominating sets of \(K_m\square K_n\) decompose into three regimes of labeled stars, subdivided stars, and two-edge paths, yielding every coefficient of their cardinality polynomial. On an \(n\times n\) board with \(n\ge4\), the largest minimal sets have size \(2n-2\) and number exactly \(2n\,n!\); when \(n\ge5\), size \(2n-3\) is impossible.
