For \(n\ge5\), every minimum 2-rainbow dominating function of the crown graph has weight \(4\) and is forced onto either one deleted-matching index with two double-color labels or one common two-index support with singleton colors on both sides. Thus \(\operatorname{Cr}_n\) has exactly \(2n^2-n\) minimum functions, equivalently exactly that many minimum dominating sets in \(\operatorname{Cr}_n\square K_2\).
