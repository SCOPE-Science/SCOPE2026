# Scientific review: minimum-cluster nuclei and S5 retraction ranks

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem is fully quantified for all nonempty finite sets partitioned into positive-size equivalence classes. The core lemma follows directly from elementary finite Stone duality: a Boolean endomorphism of \(\mathcal P(X)\) is inverse image along one unique function, and preservation of relational \(\Diamond\) is equivalent to the forward-and-back successor-image equality. On an equivalence frame that equality forces the image of **each complete source cluster** to be **one complete target cluster**; merely hitting one target point would be insufficient. This makes the surjection factor \(q_{ij}=a_j!S(a_i,a_j)\) both necessary and sufficient.

The inclusion–exclusion derivation of the unrestricted image polynomial counts exactly those target clusters used, not merely their containing set. Each target cluster is occupied surjectively, so it contributes its full cardinality to the dual image. The image cardinality of the inverse-image Boolean map is exactly the number of subsets of the dual image, \(2^{s(J)}\). Idempotence is equivalent to \(f^2=f\), which forces every point of every selected target cluster to be fixed. Omitted source clusters can independently surject onto any selected cluster no larger than themselves, yielding the displayed product. A globally smallest source cluster cannot map to a larger selected target, and having one smallest selected target suffices for every omission; this proves the minimum-cluster criterion, complete support count, and equality of unrestricted and idempotent rank supports.

The proof handles singleton clusters, one-cluster frames, repeated sizes, empty products, and merging colliding algebra-image ranks. The formula reduces to the classical full transformation-monoid idempotent count when all clusters are singletons and to \(I_X(z)=z^{2^{a_1}}\) when \(m=1\). Finite exhaustive checks independently evaluate \(197{,}086\) candidate point maps over sixteen partitions, compare \(48\) full spectra/support results, and test the Boolean modal operation directly on \(10{,}874\) subset instances. No computer experiment is used in place of an all-size proof.

## Originality — PASS (provisional literature qualification)

The closest accessible primary paper, Bezhanishvili–Fernández-Duque, *The Baire closure and its logic* (arXiv:2102.03564v1; DOI: 10.1017/jsl.2024.1), explicitly introduces \(\mathsf{S5}\) cluster decompositions and lower cluster cardinalities in Definition 2.10, then studies completeness and the Baire quotient. Its inspected sections do not state a rank spectrum or minimum-cluster retraction criterion. The 2007 Adams–Dziobiak paper is more directly about monadic Boolean endomorphism monoids, but the publisher provides only its abstract and preview; the accessible abstract asserts recoverability of **finite algebra isomorphism types** from endomorphism monoids rather than a rank-graded idempotent census. The 2006 Monteiro–Abad–Savini–Sewald–Zander paper counts **subalgebras** of finite monadic Boolean algebras, an invariant not equivalent to counts of idempotent endomorphisms or dual-frame retraction supports. The novel claim is intentionally restricted to the stated combined minimum-cluster obstruction, exact unequal-size endomorphism/idempotent rank polynomials, and image-type formula—not to the classical cluster representation, finite duality, or Stirling identities.

Important residual risk: the full text of the particularly relevant 2007 article and the 2006 subalgebra paper could not be inspected; either may implicitly contain the same numerical refinement. The surveyed abstract-level evidence does not conclusively establish global priority. Dedicated searches of published literature, semantic mathematical finding records, and an existing local research-claims ledger did not return an explicit equivalent all-cluster-size retraction-rank theorem. An unindexed equivalent antecedent remains possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result gives a complete positive-size-parameter solution to a natural structural question for finite \(\mathsf{S5}\) modal algebras: which dual-frame unions admit modal retractions, and exactly how many endomorphisms/idempotents have each algebra-image size? In ordinary finite Boolean algebras, there is no equivalence-cluster obstruction; here a globally minimum-size component is a necessary and sufficient **retraction nucleus**. The fact that unrestricted and idempotent maps have exactly the same possible image cardinalities, despite very different coefficients, is a sharp qualitative conclusion not obtainable from a count of ungraded endomorphisms. The heterogeneous formulas distinguish frames that have the same total number of worlds and clusters but different class-size profiles, and specialize naturally to weighted transformation-monoid spectra. The work does not assert a sweeping new duality or external application; its value is a precise complete structural and enumerative classification.
