For a finite \(\mathsf{S5}\) frame, a union of clusters is a bounded-retraction image exactly when it contains a globally smallest cluster; hence a frame with \(m\) clusters, \(r\) of minimum size, has exactly \(2^m-2^{m-r}\) such images. The complete endomorphism and idempotent image-rank polynomials of its powerset modal algebra admit exact Stirling-surjection formulas for arbitrary unequal cluster sizes.
The set of attainable image ranks is identical for all endomorphisms and for idempotents, despite sharply different coefficient distributions.
