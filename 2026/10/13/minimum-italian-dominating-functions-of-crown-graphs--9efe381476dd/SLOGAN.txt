For the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M_n\), Italian domination has minimum weight \(3\) at \(n=3\) and \(4\) for every \(n\ge4\). For \(n\ge5\), every minimum function is forced to align its two side supports across the deleted matching, giving exactly \(n+\binom{n}{2}=\binom{n+1}{2}\) optimum functions.
