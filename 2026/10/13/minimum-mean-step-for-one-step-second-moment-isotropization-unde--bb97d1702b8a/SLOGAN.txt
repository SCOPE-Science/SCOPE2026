For random-time implicit midpoint on a harmonic oscillator, annihilating every initial second-moment anisotropy in one step requires mean duration at least \(1/\omega\), and equality uniquely chooses \(H=0\) or \(H=2/\omega\) with equal probabilities. Every larger mean admits a strictly positive two-point isotropizing law, while a maximum permitted duration below \(2/\omega\) makes exact isotropization impossible.
