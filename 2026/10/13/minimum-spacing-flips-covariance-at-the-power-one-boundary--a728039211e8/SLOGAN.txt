For uniform spacings, the minimum spacing is positively correlated with every concave power sum \(\sum_iD_i^a\) for \(0<a<1\), uncorrelated at \(a=1\), and negatively correlated with every convex power sum for \(a>1\). For Greenwood's statistic the finite correlation is exactly \(-\sqrt{(m+1)(m+3)/(m^2(m+2))}\), and conditioning all spacings to exceed a threshold drives its mean quadratically toward equal spacing.
