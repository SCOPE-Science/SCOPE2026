Deleting a perfect matching from \(K_{n,n}\) raises total vertex-edge domination from \(2\) to \(3\) for every crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n\), \(n\ge3\). Every minimum set uses two vertices on one side and one on the other with three pairwise distinct matching indices, giving exactly \(n(n-1)(n-2)\) minima.
