For every generalized helm \(H(m,s)\) with \(m\ge3\) and \(s\ge2\), the minimum zero forcing sets are exactly the center-type sets and the sets with one saturated pendant bundle. There are \(s^{m-1}(s+m)\) of them; exactly \(s^m\) are efficient with propagation time \(2\), while the remaining \(m s^{m-1}\) have propagation time \(3\).
