For the standard comb \(C_n=P_n\circ K_1\), every minimum zero forcing set is an endpoint transversal of an explicitly described minimum path cover. There are \(2^m\) minimum sets when \(n=2m\), and \((3m+4)2^{m-1}\) when \(n=2m+1\ge3\); the odd case requires resolving overlaps between different minimum path covers.
