For the fan-with-horn graphs \(F_n(m)\) appearing in the finite Yu–Wu annihilating-ideal classification, every minimum zero forcing set is classified explicitly: for \(m\ge2\) there are \(m2^n\), while for \(m=1\) there are \(2^{n-1}(n+4)\). Together with the nine minimum sets of \(K_3(1,1,1)\), this determines zero forcing and all minimizers for every finite triangle-core annihilating-ideal graph.
