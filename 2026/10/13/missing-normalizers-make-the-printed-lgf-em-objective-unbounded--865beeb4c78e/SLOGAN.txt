The LGF paper's printed complete-data likelihood drops the log-series and gamma normalizers, and its Eq. (4.5) therefore makes the frailty-shape EM objective diverge to \(+\infty\). Restoring \(-m\log[-\log(1-\eta)]\) and \(-m\log\Gamma(\lambda)\) yields the source's own \(\eta\)-score and a finite frailty-shape M-step with the required digamma term.
