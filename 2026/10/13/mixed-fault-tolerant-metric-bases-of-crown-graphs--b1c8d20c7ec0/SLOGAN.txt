For every crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n\) with \(n\ge4\), \(\operatorname{mftdim}(\operatorname{Cr}_n)=n\). Minimum mixed fault-tolerant generators are exactly deleted-matching transversals that are either entirely one-sided or use at least four vertices from each bipartition class, producing mixed bases precisely from \(n=8\) onward.
