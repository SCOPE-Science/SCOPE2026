For the prime-base binomial congruence on two-prime-support indices \(n=q^tp^r\), an exact p-adic initial segment is intersected with a base-\(q\) digit-sum filter. The classification includes a sharp non-Wieferich cube \(n=31\cdot5^3\) and an example in which the digital condition deletes an entire nonempty p-adic tower.
