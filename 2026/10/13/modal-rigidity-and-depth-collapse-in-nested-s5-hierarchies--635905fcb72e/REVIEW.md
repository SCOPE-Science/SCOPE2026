# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The signature characteristic formulas have a direct depth induction, equality of complete signature vectors forms a color-respecting bisimulation via recursive successor matching, and the rigidity implication is proved by induction on colored rooted subtrees. The regular census follows from canonical unordered child-type sets and their assignments to labeled child positions. The binary sharpness family has a separate three-type induction; direct algebra closures and exhaustive small hierarchies were checked by `verify.py`. The proof is restricted to finite, *nested*, named S5 relations with a universal top relation.

**Originality — PASS for the modal claim, not for the rooted-tree recursion.** Wißmann, Milius and Schröder provide generic modal witnesses and partition refinement, while Cheng's Theorems 3.1–3.2 already supply the distinguishing-rooted-tree recursion. Neither inspected work states the nested S5 signature-vector characterization, its collapse at depth \(H-1\), or its sharpness in complete binary hierarchies. The recurrence itself is openly attributed to Cheng and is treated only as a corollary through the modal-rigidity bridge. Residual risk: older modal-logic work may implicitly contain the specialization.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** A finite hierarchical S5 modal-algebra generation problem is reduced to rigid tree coding, giving a full arbitrary-hierarchy criterion and a uniform, sharp expressive-depth cutoff. The result separates a modal phenomenon from the pre-existing combinatorial enumeration and yields an exact, reusable rank/count calculation in homogeneous cases. It exhibits arbitrarily large nested epistemic models generated by two propositions. General crossing epistemic partitions are not addressed.
