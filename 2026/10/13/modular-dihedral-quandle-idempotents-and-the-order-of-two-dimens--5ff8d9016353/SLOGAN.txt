In characteristic \(p\), all idempotents of the odd-prime dihedral quandle algebra \(K[R_p]\) form one isolated zero plus an explicit affine space whose dimension is determined by \(\operatorname{ord}_p(2)\). The nonzero locus has dimension \(2\) at \(p=17\) and \(3\) at \(p=31\), so its exact finite-field idempotent count is \(1+q^{m_p}\), rather than merely the number of basis elements.
