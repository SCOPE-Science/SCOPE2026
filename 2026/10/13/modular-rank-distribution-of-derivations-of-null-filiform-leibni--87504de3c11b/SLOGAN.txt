For the \(n\)-dimensional null-filiform Leibniz algebra over every finite field \(\mathbf F_q\), the full derivation-nullity distribution has a closed formula with an explicit characteristic-dependent resonance stratification. Its derivations are invertible precisely in dimensions \(n<p\), where \(p=\operatorname{char}\mathbf F_q\); once \(n\geq p\), every derivation is singular.
