For odd dihedral quandle algebras in characteristic coprime to their order, every derivation is circulant and its dimension is controlled exactly by multiplicative-order resonance of \(2\) and \(-1\). The first modular exception to characteristic-zero rigidity is \(K[R_{11}]\) in characteristic \(3\), where the derivation space is one-dimensional and generated by a quadratic-character convolution.
