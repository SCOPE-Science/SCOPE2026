With unstandardized symmetric adjacency weights, every regular graph forces \(C=(n-1)(1-I)/n\), so Moran and Geary give exactly the same autocorrelation ordering. Binary strict ranking reversal first becomes possible on five vertices: on \(P_5\), an endpoint singleton and an adjacent internal pair are ranked oppositely, and \(11\) of the \(21\) connected five-vertex graph classes admit some reversal.\n
