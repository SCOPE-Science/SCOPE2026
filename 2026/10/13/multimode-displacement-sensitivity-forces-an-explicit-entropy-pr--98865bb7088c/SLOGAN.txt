In a uniform \(N\)-mode thermal Gaussian steady state, the best displacement-QFI gain \(R\) above bath equilibrium obeys \(\dot\Sigma/\gamma\ge 2N\nu\operatorname{arcoth}(2\nu)(R-1)^2/[R(2N-R)]\). Hence a gain proportional to \(N\) necessarily costs entropy production proportional to \(N\), with a divergence as \(R\) approaches the trace-imposed ceiling \(2N\).
