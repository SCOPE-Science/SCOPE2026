For the elementary-symmetric cubic in \(m\geq4\) variables, every graded layer of its Jacobian saturation defect is multiplicity-free: the only constituents are the two-row Specht representations \(S^{(m-j,j)}\), one copy for each \(2\leq j\leq\min(d,m-d)\). The decomposition refines known numerical nodal-cubic Hilbert-function results and identifies exactly which symmetry types account for the embedded affine-vertex defect.
