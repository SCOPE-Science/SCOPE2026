Connected crown graphs have finite multiset dimension only at the six-cycle boundary: \(\operatorname{md}(\operatorname{Cr}_3)=3\), while \(\operatorname{md}(\operatorname{Cr}_n)=\infty\) for every \(n\ge4\). The obstruction is a four-state membership bottleneck on the deleted matched pairs, with the fourth state forcing a cross-part collision.
