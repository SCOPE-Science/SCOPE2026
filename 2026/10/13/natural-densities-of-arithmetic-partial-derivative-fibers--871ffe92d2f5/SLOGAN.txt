For every prime \(p\), each possible number of positive integral anti-partial derivatives has an explicit, strictly positive natural density, and the entire distribution is computable by rational series with geometric tail bounds. The image density differs from \(-(p-1)\log(1-1/p)\) by at most \(p^{1-p}\), yielding the sharp leading density deficiency \(1/(2p)\) as \(p\) grows.
