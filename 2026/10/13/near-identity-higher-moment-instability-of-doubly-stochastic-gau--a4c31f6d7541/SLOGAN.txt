For entry-weighted doubly stochastic Gauss–Seidel on a positive-definite matrix arbitrarily close to the identity, the squared error contracts geometrically in expectation and the iterates converge almost surely, while the error moment of any prescribed order \(p>2\) can grow exponentially at an arbitrarily large prescribed rate. The separation occurs even at the original two-column recommended relaxation \(\alpha=1/2\) and follows from rare off-diagonal updates rather than ill-conditioning.
