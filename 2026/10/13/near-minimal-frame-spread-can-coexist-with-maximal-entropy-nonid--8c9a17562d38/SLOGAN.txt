Among fixed rank-one measurements, frame spread obeys the sharp rank-sensitive bound \(\kappa_\nu\ge(d^2-1)/r_\nu\), and every subset of a complete MUB family attains it. Yet using all but one of the \(d+1\) MUBs gives \(\kappa=1+1/d\to1\) while the exact entropy minimax risk remains \(\frac14\log^2d\) for every sample size.
