In a uniformly grown binary search tree, total internal path length and the number of nodes visited to find the maximum key have the exact covariance \(3n+H_n-2(n+1)H_n^{(2)}\), strictly negative for every \(n\geq3\). Its magnitude is linear in \(n\), yet the correlation decays as \(-0.4471358767/\sqrt{\log n}\).
