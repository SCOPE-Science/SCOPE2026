For a uniformly chosen Dyck excursion, the number of peaks and its step-height area have strictly negative covariance for every semilength \(n\ge2\): \(\operatorname{Cov}(P_n,S_n)=(n+1)/2-(n+1)4^{n-1}/\binom{2n}{n}\). The exact least-squares slope of area on peak count is asymptotic to \(-2\sqrt{\pi n}\).
