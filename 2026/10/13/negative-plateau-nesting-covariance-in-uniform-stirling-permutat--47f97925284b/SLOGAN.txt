For a uniform Stirling permutation of order \(n\), the covariance of the number of plateaux with the total number of entries nested between equal labels is exactly \(\frac{2\cdot4^n}{3\binom{2n}{n}}-\frac{8n+4}{9}\). It is strictly negative for all \(n\ge2\) and its ratio to \(n\) tends to \(-8/9\).
