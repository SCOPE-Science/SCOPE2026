# Self-review

## Correctness

PASS. All Lie endomorphisms of the affine algebra are derived explicitly. Their Yau-twisted brackets are scalar multiples of the affine bracket, and direct substitution shows that the Nijenhuis torsion vanishes for every linear endomorphism. Thus the problem reduces exactly to matrix commutation. The endomorphism variety and all matrix-centralizer sizes are counted over arbitrary finite fields, and exhaustive checks over four prime fields replay the result.

## Originality

PASS. The primary Hom-Lie Nijenhuis source supplies the general framework. A full public follow-up source explicitly gives the Nijenhuis identity and Yau-twist construction, but no complete finite-field census of all affine two-dimensional twists. Searches across low-dimensional, affine, Yau-twist, centralizer, exact-polynomial, and finite-field formulations found no covering statement. The unavailable full text of the primary source is retained as a specific residual risk rather than treated as proof of noncoverage.

## Value

PASS. The affine Lie algebra is the unique nonabelian two-dimensional Lie algebra, and its full Lie-endomorphism space is the canonical complete parameter space of Yau twists. The theorem gives the full Nijenhuis fiber distribution, identifies the exact two twists where the fiber dimension jumps, and determines a closed extension-field zeta function.
