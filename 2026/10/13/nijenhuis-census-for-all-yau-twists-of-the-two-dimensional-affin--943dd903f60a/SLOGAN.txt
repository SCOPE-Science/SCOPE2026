Across all Yau twists of the two-dimensional affine Lie algebra over \(\mathbf F_q\), Hom-Lie Nijenhuis operators are exactly the matrices commuting with the twist. There are \(4q^4-q^3-2q^2\) twist-operator pairs: only the zero and identity twists have \(q^4\) operators, while every other twist has \(q^2\).
