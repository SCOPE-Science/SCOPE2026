For the Leibniz algebra \([e_3,e_2]=e_2,\ [e_3,e_1]=e_1+e_2\), the nilpotent weight-zero Rota–Baxter locus is exactly two affine \(2\)-planes meeting in a line, and every nonzero point is square-zero of rank \(1\). Modulo algebra automorphisms, the normal forms are \(dE_{21}\), \(dE_{21}+E_{23}\), and \(E_{13}\); over \(\mathbf F_q\) this gives \(2q+1\) classes.
