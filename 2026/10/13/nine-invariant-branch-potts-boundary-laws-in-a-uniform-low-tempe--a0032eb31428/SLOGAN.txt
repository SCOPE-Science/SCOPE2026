For the three-state Potts model, the natural coupled two-value matrix \(M=\begin{pmatrix}k&0\\1&k-1\end{pmatrix}\) has exactly nine \(I_1\)-boundary-law solutions for every integer \(k\ge4\) and every \(\theta>24\). The count is branch-local: it yields nine splitting Gibbs measures without claiming a global classification.
