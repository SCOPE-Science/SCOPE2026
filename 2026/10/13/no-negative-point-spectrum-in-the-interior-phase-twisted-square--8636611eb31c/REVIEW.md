# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The negative-energy Floquet determinant in the primary source is affine in \(Q=\cos\theta_1+\cos\theta_2\). Its exact coefficient is \(2\kappa(1+\kappa^2)\sinh(\kappa\ell)\), which is positive for every \(\kappa>0\) and \(\ell>0\). Thus a fixed negative energy can occur only on a measure-zero Bloch level set and cannot be a point eigenvalue of the periodic direct integral. No negative Dirichlet singularity is omitted because \(\sinh(\kappa\ell)\neq0\).

The 2010 general square-lattice theorem allows flat bands for general couplings and does not settle this specialized family. Closest same-source results concern positive point spectrum, positive high-energy edges, or large-edge negative-band widths; none implies the all-\(\ell\) negative-sector statement. ## Value
PASS. The source treats phase-induced transport changes and derives both positive and negative dispersion laws. The result gives a global structural boundary for the negative sector: localized infinitely degenerate levels are impossible at every edge length and every interior phase, so all negative spectral states must come from dispersive Bloch sets. This complements, without duplicating, the known positive flat-band behavior.
