The sixth cyclotomic polynomial has no reciprocal divisibility pairs other than \((1,1)\): both \(a\mid(b^2-b+1)\) and \(b\mid(a^2-a+1)\) force \(a=b=1\). Consequently the gcd-reciprocal sum cannot be integral at any nontrivial squarefree input with fewer than three distinct prime factors, eliminating an entire putative two-cycle case.
