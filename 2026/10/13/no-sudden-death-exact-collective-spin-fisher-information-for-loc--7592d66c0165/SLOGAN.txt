For every \(N\ge2\), independent local phase damping of a one-excitation \(W_N\) state has maximal collective-spin Fisher information \(N+2(N-1)(1-p)^2\), strictly above shot noise until complete damping. Amplitude damping instead gives \(N(1-2p)^2+2(N-1)(1-p)\), which never vanishes: both exact laws exclude the published sudden-death and discontinuity claims for these channels.
