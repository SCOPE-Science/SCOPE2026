The projective scheme of hollow symmetric matrices of rank at most two is reduced and locally a product of ordinary crossing nodes: a point with \(z\) zero rows has exactly \(2^z\) branches and Hilbert--Samuel multiplicity \(2^z\). Its irreducible components are the \(2^{n-1}-1\) bipartition-indexed Segre varieties, and their ideals intersect to the saturation of all \(3\times3\) minors.
