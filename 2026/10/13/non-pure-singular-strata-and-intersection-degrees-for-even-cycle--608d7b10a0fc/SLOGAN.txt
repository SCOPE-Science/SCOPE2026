For the stable-set toric variety of every even cycle \(C_{2m}\), \(m\ge3\), the reduced singular locus has \(2m\) codimension-three components and an additional singular line with transverse rank-one \(m\times m\) determinantal type. All intersections of the large components are controlled by independent sets of the cycle, and their projective degrees are explicit multinomial products of Euler zigzag numbers.
