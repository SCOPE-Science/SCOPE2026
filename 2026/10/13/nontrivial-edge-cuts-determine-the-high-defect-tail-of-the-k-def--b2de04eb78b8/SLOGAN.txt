For every graph, the high-defect tail is rigid once the number of bichromatic edges drops below the smallest nontrivial edge boundary: only the all-one-color value and singleton-cut values can survive. For crowns \(\operatorname{Cr}_n\), this gives a sharp gap above \(n^2-3n+4\), with the sole interior exception \(\phi_{(n-1)^2}=2\).
