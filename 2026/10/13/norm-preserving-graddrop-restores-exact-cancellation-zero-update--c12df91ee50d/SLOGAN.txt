Pure GradDrop escapes exact task-gradient cancellation, but its norm-preserving postprocessing cannot: if \(\sum_i g_i=0\), the returned gradient is exactly zero for every mask realization. More generally, the norm-preserved update is bounded by \(\lVert\sum_i g_i\rVert_2\), so its conflict-induced stochastic escape collapses as ordinary gradient cancellation is approached.
