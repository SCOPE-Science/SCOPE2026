A finite antichain in a group's normal-subgroup lattice becomes a finite family of reachable homomorphic states that can never lead to one another. Thus every non-chain normal lattice forces failure of the \(\mathsf{S4.3}\) axiom \(.3\) with parameters; for \((C_p)^n\) the construction yields \((p^n-1)/(p-1)\) pairwise future-incompatible branches.
