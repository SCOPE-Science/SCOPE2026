For the normalized-Laplacian quantum walk on every double star \(D_{p,q}\), pretty good state transfer occurs precisely between sibling leaves at a hub with two leaves, unless the other hub's leaf count satisfies the Pell equation \(q(q+1)=6h^2\). These exceptions form \(\{2,24,242,2400,\ldots\}\), and no genuine double star admits perfect state transfer.
