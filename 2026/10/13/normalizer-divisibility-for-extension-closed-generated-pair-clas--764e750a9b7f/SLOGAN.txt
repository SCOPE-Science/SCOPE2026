Sabatini's normalizer divisibility extends from solubilizers to every isomorphism-closed finite-group class containing cyclic groups and closed under subgroups and extensions. In particular, the normalizer of \(\langle x\rangle\) inside \(K\) divides the number of \(g\in K\) for which \(\langle x,g\rangle\) is \(\pi\)-separable, including the \(p\)-solvable case.
