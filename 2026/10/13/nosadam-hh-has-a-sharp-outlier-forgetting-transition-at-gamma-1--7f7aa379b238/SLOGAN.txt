NosAdam-HH has a sharp memory transition at \(\gamma=1\): a fixed gradient outlier is forgotten with a power law for \(0\le\gamma<1\), only logarithmically at \(\gamma=1\), and leaves a permanent second-moment bias for \(\gamma>1\). At the source comparison value \(\gamma=0.1\), the outlier weight decays as \(0.9\,\tau^{-0.1}t^{-0.9}\).
