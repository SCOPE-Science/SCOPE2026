For the NOT-Exp law, \(h(y)\sim\gamma[((1+\alpha)\theta)/\alpha]^\gamma y^{\gamma-1}\) as \(y\downarrow0\), while \(h(y)\to\theta\) as \(y\to\infty\). These endpoint limits rule out the source's displayed increasing, decreasing, bathtub, and upside-down-bathtub parameter examples under the global shape descriptions used in the paper.
