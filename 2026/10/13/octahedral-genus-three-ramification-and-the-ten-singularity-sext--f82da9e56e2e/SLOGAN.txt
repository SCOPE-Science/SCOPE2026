The factor-permutation quotient of Nukui's smooth symmetric K3 surface is a degree-\(24\) cover of \(\mathbf P^2\) branched along an explicit rational sextic with six cusps and four nodes. Its six reduced ramification components are copies of the octahedral genus-three curve \(y^2=t^8+14t^4+1\); the generic sextic singularity count was already known, but this special geometric realization identifies the ramification curves.
