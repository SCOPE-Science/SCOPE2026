The odd biisotropic symplectic Grassmannian \(\mathrm{S}_2\mathrm{Gr}(k,2n+1)\) and \(\mathrm{Gr}(k,2n+2-k)\) have noncanonically isomorphic integral Chow motives. Their common dimension-by-dimension affine-cell count also forces equality of their Grothendieck classes, while leaving the cohomology-ring question open.
