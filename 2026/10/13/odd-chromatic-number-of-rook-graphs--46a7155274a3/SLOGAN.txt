For \(2\le m<n\), every optimal proper coloring of the rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) is already odd, so \(\chi_o=n\). For square rook graphs \(K_n\square K_n\) with \(n\ge3\), row-column omission balance forbids \(n+1\) colors while an explicit cyclic construction uses \(n+2\), giving \(\chi_o=n+2\).
