For odd \(p\) and \(n\ge2\), the pseudo-algebra of \(\operatorname{Hol}(C_{p^n})\) has an explicit codegree tower: the pure \(p\)-power codegrees have multiplicities \(p^t\) below the top and \(p^{n-1}\) at \(p^n\), while mixed codegrees come only from the cyclic quotient. The top codegree and its multiplicity recover \(p\) and \(n\), so the invariant is rigid on this holomorph family.
