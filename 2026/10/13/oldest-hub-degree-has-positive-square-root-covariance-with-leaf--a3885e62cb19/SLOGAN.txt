In the one-edge Barabási–Albert tree, the degree \(D_n\) of an initial vertex and the global leaf count \(L_n\) are exactly uncorrelated only through size \(3\), after which their covariance increases at every step. The exact recurrence gives \(\operatorname{Cov}(D_n,L_n)\sim 2\sqrt n/(3\sqrt{\pi})\), quantifying a positive oldest-hub/global-boundary coupling.
