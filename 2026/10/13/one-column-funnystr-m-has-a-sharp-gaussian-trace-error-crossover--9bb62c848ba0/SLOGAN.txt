A single Gaussian funNyström column has an exact sharp spectral-contrast trace-risk law for matrix powers: its excess is asymptotic to \(C_\theta\kappa^{\theta-q+1/2}\). At one pass the square-root exponent \(\theta=1/2\) separates mean-optimality from divergent mean error, while two passes restore asymptotic mean-optimality for every \(0<\theta\le1\), despite the one-pass error already being typically optimal for \(\theta<1\).
