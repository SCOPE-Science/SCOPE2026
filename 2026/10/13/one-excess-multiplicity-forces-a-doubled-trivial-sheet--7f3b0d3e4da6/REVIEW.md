# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. Chen's full proof supplies the exact source input: on a reduced affine base, the nilradical is locally free of rank \(r-s\) and the reduction is finite étale of rank \(s\). At \(r-s=1\), a local generator \(\varepsilon\) satisfies \(\varepsilon^2=c\varepsilon\); nilpotence and reducedness force \(c=0\). The multiplication action on the resulting square-zero line gives a retraction of the reduced finite étale algebra, hence a clopen trivial sheet. Lifting the complementary idempotent and using its trivial action on the nilradical gives the stated product decomposition. All quantifiers and boundary cases, including \(s=1\), were checked.

## Originality
PASS. Full-text inspection of arXiv:2602.21708v3 found the locally free nilradical and finite-étale reduction but no rank-one, square-zero, or forced-section classification. The superseded arXiv:2502.05834v3 contains the double-line prototype but no general statement. Searches over the exact source, finite-flat degree excess one, invertible nilradicals, square-zero thickenings, and the connected-cover obstruction found no equivalent or stronger statement. The remaining risk is an older general finite-algebra formulation under different terminology.

## Value
PASS. The result identifies the complete scheme structure at the first nonreduced boundary of the covering criterion. It shows that one unit of multiplicity excess is not merely small: it forces a globally trivial reduced sheet and therefore rules out connected reduced covers of degree greater than one. This is structural information about the obstruction, not a recomputation of the source theorem.

Closest literature is Chen's reduction theorem and its 2025 predecessor. General idempotent-lifting facts are proof tools rather than covering results. The claim is intentionally limited to excess one and a reduced base.
