Alternating the classical left-to-right and right-to-left Saul'yev heat sweeps is pointwise positivity preserving on every Dirichlet grid with at least two interior nodes exactly when \(D\Delta t/\Delta x^2\le1\), despite unconditional Fourier stability. Above that threshold the completed pair has the explicit negative corner \(2r^{\,n-1}(1-r)/(1+r)^{n+1}\), while the one-node grid remains nonnegative for all \(r\).
