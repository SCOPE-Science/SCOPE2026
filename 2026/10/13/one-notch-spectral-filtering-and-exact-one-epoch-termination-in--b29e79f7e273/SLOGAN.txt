On deterministic quadratics, one MNMPSVRG epoch is exactly the spectral filter \(P_m(q;\theta)=[(1-\theta q)^m-(1-\theta)]/\theta\), with at most one admissible spectral notch. For \(H=\lambda I\), a unique negative-momentum parameter solves the quadratic exactly in one epoch if and only if \(m\eta\lambda>1\) while \(\eta\lambda<1/2\).
