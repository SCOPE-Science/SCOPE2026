In symmetric Dirichlet–multinomial sampling, unseen-category and doubleton counts have exactly one covariance sign transition as sample size grows, for every number of categories \(k\ge3\) and every positive fixed Dirichlet shape \(a\). Its unique continuous crossing is \(\frac{a}{a+1}k+\frac{a+3}{2(a+1)}+O_a(k^{-1})\), and the covariance has an explicit nonzero-scale linear profile within the constant-width critical window.
