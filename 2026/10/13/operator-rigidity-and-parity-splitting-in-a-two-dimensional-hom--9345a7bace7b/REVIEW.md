# Self-review

## Correctness

PASS. Because the homogeneous components are one-dimensional, every even operator is diagonal. Direct substitution into the four defining operator identities gives complete polynomial systems, not sample tests. The Rota–Baxter system has only \(0\) at weight \(0\) and \(0,-\lambda I\) at nonzero weight; the Nijenhuis system is \((a-b)^2=0\); the centroid is \(K\alpha^k\); and the averaging system has one line for odd \(k\) and two lines for even \(k\). The finite-field replay independently checks every even operator over four odd prime fields.

## Originality

PASS. The primary source exhibits \(R=-\lambda I\) but does not establish uniqueness or classify the other three operator classes. A later open-access paper reuses the exact object and operator without giving the complete profile. Searches under exact multiplication, exact twist, example number, operator aliases, low-dimensional classification, and parity formulations found no covering statement. Residual risk remains that an equivalent classification is written under different Hom-color notation.

## Value

PASS. The algebra is a published operator example later reused as a construction seed, so determining its full operator geometry is mathematically motivated rather than an arbitrary coordinate exercise. The result upgrades an isolated example to a complete operator inventory and separates three rigid operator classes from the parity-sensitive averaging locus.
