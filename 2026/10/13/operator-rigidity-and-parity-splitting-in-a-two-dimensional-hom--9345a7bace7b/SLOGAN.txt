On Bakayoko–Attan Example 1.12, the source operator \(R=-\lambda I\) is the unique nonzero even weight-\(\lambda\) Rota–Baxter operator for every \(\lambda\ne0\), and every Nijenhuis operator is scalar. The \(\alpha^k\)-averaging locus is one affine line for odd \(k\) but two lines meeting at the origin for even \(k\), giving an exact parity split absent from the other operator classes.
