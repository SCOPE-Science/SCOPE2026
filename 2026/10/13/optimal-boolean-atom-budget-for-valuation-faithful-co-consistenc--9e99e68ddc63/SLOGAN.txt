For a finite reflexive symmetric frame with \(m\) worlds and underlying edge clique cover number \(\theta_e\), the smallest power-set Boolean algebra whose co-consistency semantics faithfully realizes **every** valuation on that same frame has exactly \(m+\theta_e\) atoms. The extra \(m\) private markers are unavoidable, while optimal clique tokens suffice; this compresses the complete-frame construction from quadratic to linear atom count.
