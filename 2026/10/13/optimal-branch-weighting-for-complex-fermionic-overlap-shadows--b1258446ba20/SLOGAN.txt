The equal coherent reference used for complex orbital-rotation overlap shadows is uniquely minimax, but it is not average-variance optimal for a nontrivial known target ensemble. Reweighting the trial branch to \(\lambda_*=1/(1+\sqrt{\bar S})\) lowers the exact ensemble-average complex variance by \(b_{N,\eta}(1-\sqrt{\bar S})^2\) while preserving one shared shadow data set.
