For every integer \(t\ge2\), the coefficient gcd of the Nekrasov--Okounkov square-parameter series minus the \(t\)-core series is exactly \(p^{a+1}\) if \(t=p^a\), and is \(1\) otherwise. The first coefficient preventing a further \(p\)-adic lift occurs sharply at index \(p^{a-1}\), completing the optimal modulus classification for all \(t\).
