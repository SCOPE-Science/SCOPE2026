For \(G=C_{p^a}\oplus C_{p^b}\) with \(a<b\), the \(p+1\) order-\(p\) Acentralizers have only two fiber sizes: one for the distinguished long-factor socle line and one shared by the other \(p\) lines. The exact formulas show a sharp \(p=2\) collapse, where only the long-factor line can occur and only for adjacent exponents.
