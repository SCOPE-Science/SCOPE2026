For every finite graph, ordinary zero forcing of its private-leaf corona is equivalent to a uniquely restricted matching saturating the initially empty vertex pairs in a donor-to-empty cut; this gives an exact formula for the full zero forcing polynomial. In particular, the zero forcing polynomial of \(K_n\circ K_1\) is \((2x+x^2)^n+n((2x+x^2)^{n-1}-x^{n-1})\), and exactly \(1+n(2^{n-1}-1)\) of its zero forcing sets are inclusion-minimal for \(n\ge2\).
