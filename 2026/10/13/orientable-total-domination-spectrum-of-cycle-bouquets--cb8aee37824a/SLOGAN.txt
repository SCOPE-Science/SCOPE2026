For a bouquet of at least two cycles sharing one vertex, the full distribution of minimum directed total-domination numbers over all positive-indegree orientations factors as \(z^{\sum_i(\ell_i-3)+2}(\prod_i(\ell_i-1+2z)-\prod_i(\ell_i-1))\). It gives exact counts for every possible minimum and confirms that every intermediate value between the orientable lower and upper extrema occurs.
