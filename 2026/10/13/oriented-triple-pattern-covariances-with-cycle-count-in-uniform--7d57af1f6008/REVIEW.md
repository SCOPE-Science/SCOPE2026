# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

For fixed images at three positions, forced-edge contraction gives a bijection with permutations on \(n-3\) supervertices and yields the conditional cycle expectation \(H_{n-3}+G\). The proof counts all one-, two-, and three-cycle contributions and obtains four exact affine covariance numerators. Direct exhaustive enumeration of every permutation through size \(9\), plus exact-rational substitution through size \(500\), agrees. These computations are supplementary; the bijection establishes the full quantifiers, including \(n=3\) and both positional boundaries.

## Originality — PASS

The precise object is **orientation-resolved and position-specific** covariance with the total number of disjoint cycles. Gessel–Reutenauer and Gessel–Zhuang cover joint **descent-set statistics** with cycle structure, and Fulman–Kim–Lee cite strong aggregate peak/cycle generating functions. Those imply some coarser sums but do not, by their stated statistics, distinguish local \(132\) from \(231\), or \(213\) from \(312\). Fu–Li explicitly distinguishes those peak orientations but studies their pattern counts without cycle structure. Searches for orientation-by-cycle joint laws, explicit positional covariances, and equivalent generating functions found no covering published statement in the inspected materials. Residual risk: a different unlocated multivariate generating function might encode the formulas, and a cited 1996 historical paper was not available in full text.

## Value — PASS

The four cases naturally exhaust the oriented local peaks and valleys. Their covariance profiles expose sign changes at asymptotic positions \(1/4\) and \(3/4\), and a full leading-order cancellation on removing orientation. That is a structural distinction between local rank geometry and global cycle topology; it is not a relabeling or a marginal peak-count evaluation.
