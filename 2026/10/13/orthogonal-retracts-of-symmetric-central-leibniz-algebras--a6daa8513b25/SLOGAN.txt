For a nondegenerate symmetric central Leibniz algebra in characteristic not two, every nonidentity idempotent endomorphism retracts onto the graph of a functional on a totally isotropic subspace. Over any odd finite field, the complete rank-\(k\) count is \(I_B(k)q^{k(n-k+1)}\), whereas the number of distinct retract images is only \(I_B(k)q^k\), with \(I_B(k)\) explicitly determined by orthogonal geometry.
