For the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M_n\), every outer-connected dominating set can be counted by subtracting exactly three complement-disconnection types from the domination polynomial. This yields the complete polynomial and the sharp transition \(\gamma_{\mathrm{oc}}(\operatorname{Cr}_3)=4\) but \(\gamma_{\mathrm{oc}}(\operatorname{Cr}_n)=2\) for every \(n\ge4\).
