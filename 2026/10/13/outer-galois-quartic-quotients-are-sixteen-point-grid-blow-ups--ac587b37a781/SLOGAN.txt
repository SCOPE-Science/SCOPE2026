For a smooth quartic \(X^4+Y^4+F_4(Z,W)=0\) with two outer Galois points, the minimal resolution of the simultaneous order-four quotient is exactly \(\operatorname{Bl}_{U\times V}(\mathbf P^1\times\mathbf P^1)\), where \(U\) and \(V\) are the two four-point root sets. The eight quotient-resolution curves form the Coble boundary \(-2K\), and the sixteen invariant lines become the sixteen grid exceptional curves.
