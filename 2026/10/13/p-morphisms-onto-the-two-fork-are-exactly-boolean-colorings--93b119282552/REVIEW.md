# Self-review

## Correctness

PASS. The proof derives the map uniquely from maximal-point colors using only monotonicity and the p-morphism back condition, then checks that every coloring yields a p-morphism. The count and surjectivity criterion follow immediately. Exhaustive checks at \(n=1\) and \(n=2\) agree with the symbolic result.

## Originality

PASS. The closest inspected sources use the same two-fork powers and p-morphism notion. Xiao's 2026 paper defines \(\mathbf A_n\cong\mathbf V_n\) and constructs several specific p-morphisms to other targets; Fontaine's thesis develops p-morphic image constructions for \(\mathsf{Cheq}\). Neither inspected full text classifies all maps \(\mathbf A_n\to\mathbf A_1\) or gives the exact count \(2^{2^n}\). Targeted searches for equivalent bounded-morphism/Boolean-coloring formulations and broader \(\mathbf A_n\to\mathbf A_m\) classifications found no dominating result.

## Value

PASS. The target \(\mathbf A_1\) is the canonical generating two-fork for the frame family that characterizes \(\mathsf{Cheq}\). A complete hom-set classification at this target is mathematically natural and exposes a structural fact not visible from existence constructions: arbitrary Boolean colorings of maximal completions, not merely coordinate projections, occur.

## Closest literature and limitations

The closest literature is Han Xiao, arXiv:2609.39577v1, together with Gaëlle Fontaine's MoL-2006-08 thesis and the original two-fork semantics of van Benthem, Bezhanishvili, and Gehrke. The result is restricted to target \(\mathbf A_1\). The included exhaustive checker covers only \(n=1,2\) and is supplementary; the proof is general.
