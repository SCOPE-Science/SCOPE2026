For every double-star tree \(D_{a,b}\), the packing \(k\)-domatic chromatic number is \(3\) for \(1\le k\le3\); for \(4\le k\le a+b+2\), it equals \(k\) only at \(k\in\{a+2,b+2\}\) and equals \(k+1\) otherwise. The two exceptional palette positions encode the pendant-leaf counts of the two centers.
