For the square book \(B_n=K_{1,n}\mathbin{\square}P_2\) with \(n\ge2\), paired-dominating sets obey a four-type spine/page classification, yielding the exact polynomial \(D_{\mathrm{pr}}(B_n;x)=x^2(1+x^2)^n+n(n-1)x^4(1+x^2)^{n-2}+(2n+1)x^{2n}\). In particular, the spine edge is the unique minimum paired-dominating set.
