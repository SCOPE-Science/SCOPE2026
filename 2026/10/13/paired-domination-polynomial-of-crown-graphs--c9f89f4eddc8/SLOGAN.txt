For the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M\), a vertex set is paired-dominating exactly when it selects the same number \(t\ge2\) of vertices from each bipartition class. Hence \(D_{\mathrm{pr}}(\operatorname{Cr}_n;x)=\sum_{t=2}^{n}\binom{n}{t}^2x^{2t}\), and all inclusion-minimal paired-dominating sets have size \(4\).
