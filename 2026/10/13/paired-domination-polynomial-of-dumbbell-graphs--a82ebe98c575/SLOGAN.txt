For the dumbbell graph \(D_{r,s}\) formed by two cliques joined by one bridge, paired domination has an exact parity split: selected clique sizes are both even, or they are both odd and the bridge endpoints are selected. This gives \(D_{\mathrm{pr}}(D_{r,s};x)=E_r(x)E_s(x)+x^2Q_{r-1}(x)Q_{s-1}(x)\), with the bridge pair as the unique minimum set.
