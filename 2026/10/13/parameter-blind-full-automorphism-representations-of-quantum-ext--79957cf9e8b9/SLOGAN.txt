For all generic parameters \(\lambda\ne\pm1\), the nonisomorphic quantum exterior algebras \(A_\lambda=\mathbb F_q\langle x,y\rangle/(x^2,y^2,yx-\lambda xy)\) have literally the same full four-dimensional automorphism matrix group and exactly the same element and ideal actions. The indistinguishable family has \((q-3)/2\) isomorphism classes for odd \(q\) and \((q-2)/2\) for even \(q\), even though its Nakayama eigenvalues retain the parameter.
