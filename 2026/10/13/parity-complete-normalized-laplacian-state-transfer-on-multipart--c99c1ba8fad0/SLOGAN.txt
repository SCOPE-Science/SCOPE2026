For the symmetric normalized-Laplacian walk on \(K_{\underbrace{2,\ldots,2}_{r\text{ parts}},m}\) with \(r\ge2\), distinct-vertex perfect transfer occurs exactly when \(r\) is odd and \(m\equiv2\pmod4\). At time \(\pi(m+2r-2)/2\), every two-vertex part is swapped simultaneously while the last part undergoes the exact reflection \(2J_m/m-I_m\); the irregular graph \(K_{2,2,2,6}\) realizes three swaps at \(5\pi\).
