For the crossed two-layer ring \(C_n\boxtimes K_2\), every positive three-price conductance-budget problem has an exact fastest-mixing spectral gap obtained from four explicit candidate allocations, or five when \(n\) is odd. A sharp parity effect lets sufficiently cheap crossed diagonals form the entire optimal connected network for odd \(n\), but never for even \(n\); all edge-class-symmetric optimizers are explicitly classified, with no claim of nonsymmetric uniqueness.
