Every nonempty bipartite cubelike graph \(\operatorname{Cay}(\mathbb F_2^d,S)\) has strong odd chromatic number exactly \(2\) or \(4\): the value is \(2\) for odd \(|S|\) and \(4\) for even \(|S|\). The even-valency case follows from an explicit odd-fiber coordinate projection and a characteristic-two obstruction to three colors.
