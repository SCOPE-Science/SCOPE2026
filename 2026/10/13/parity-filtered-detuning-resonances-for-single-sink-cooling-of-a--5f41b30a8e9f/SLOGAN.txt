For a periodic XX spin chain with one lossy qubit and one adjacent on-site detuning, the vacuum attracts every initial state except at the exact isolated ratios \(\delta/J\in\{0\}\cup\{2\cos(k\pi/(n-2)):k\text{ odd},\ 1\le k\le n-3\}\). An exact fermionic parity filter explains why the other spectral resonances do not prevent physical cooling.
