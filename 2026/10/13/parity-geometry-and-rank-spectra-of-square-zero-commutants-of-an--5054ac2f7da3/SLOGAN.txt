For a nilpotent Jordan matrix of type \((n,1)\), its square-zero commuting locus changes from two intersecting affine spaces to an irreducible quadratic cone as \(n\) switches from even to odd. The complete finite-field rank enumerator is explicit in every characteristic, with total point counts \((2q-1)q^{n/2}\) for even \(n\) and \(q^{(n+3)/2}\) for odd \(n\).
