A single antiferromagnetic bond can preserve every Lee–Yang zero on the unit circle of a ferromagnetic Ising ring, but only up to a sharp parity-dependent strength. The exact defect boundary is \(L=-K\) on even rings and \(L=-K-\tfrac12\log(1+(1-e^{-4K})/(n-1))\) on odd rings, with the first off-circle pair emerging from \(z=-1\).
