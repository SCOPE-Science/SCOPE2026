For the cycle prism \(Y_n=C_n\mathbin{\square}K_2\), the complete cardinality polynomial of **total** dominating sets is \(D_{2n}(z)\) when \(n\) is odd, and \(D_n(z)^2\) when \(n\) is even, where \(D_m\) is the ordinary domination polynomial of a cycle. The odd case is a weight-preserving open-to-closed-neighborhood bijection onto one doubled cycle; the even case splits into two independent cycle constraints.
