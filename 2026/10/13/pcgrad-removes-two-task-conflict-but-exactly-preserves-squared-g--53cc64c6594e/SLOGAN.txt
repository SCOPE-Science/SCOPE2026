For two conflicting tasks, PCGrad guarantees common first-order descent whenever the gradients are not exactly antiparallel, but it attenuates both tasks by the same angular factor \(1-\rho^2\). The resulting task-progress ratio remains exactly \((\|g_1\|/\|g_2\|)^2\), while ordinary gradient summation is common descent only inside the sharp norm-ratio window \(-\rho<\|g_1\|/\|g_2\|<-1/\rho\).
