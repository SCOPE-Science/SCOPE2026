Under a uniform multinomial null, Pearson's \(X^2\) has an exact covariance with every additive cell statistic equal to a binomial average of that statistic's discrete curvature. Consequently Pearson's statistic is strictly positively correlated in covariance with every finite Cressie--Read power-divergence statistic \(D_\lambda\) for \(\lambda>-1\), including the log-likelihood ratio.
