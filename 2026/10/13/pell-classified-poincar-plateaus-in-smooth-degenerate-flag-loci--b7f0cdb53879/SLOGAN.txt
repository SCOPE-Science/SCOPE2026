For the smooth locus of the type-\(A\) PBW degenerate complete flag variety, the shifted Schröder Poincaré polynomial has a unique largest coefficient except at square-triangular dimensions. Exactly at \(n=1,8,49,288,1681,\ldots\), a two-term maximum plateau occurs, equivalently along the Pell equation \((2n+1)^2-8s^2=1\).
