For a connected simple graph, continuous-time search from the degree-stationary state using the random-walk Laplacian and a single marked-vertex oracle reaches the target with certainty exactly when the target is universal and its deletion is regular. If the remaining vertices have induced degree \(k\), the unique tuning is \(\gamma=(k+1)/(k+2)\), and the first perfect time is \(t=\pi\sqrt{k+2}/2\).
