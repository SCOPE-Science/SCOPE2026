For every \(n\ge3\), the square rook graph \(K_n\square K_n\) has \(\gamma_{dI}^{p}=2n\), and its minimum perfect double Italian functions are exactly the \(n\times n\) label matrices with every row sum and every column sum equal to \(2\). The minimum-function count is \((n!)^2[z^n]e^{z/2}/\sqrt{1-z}\), linking the equality cases to labeled \(2\)-regular bipartite multigraphs.
