Perfect Roman domination on \(KG(n,2)\) has a sharp support transition: two disjoint \(2\)-vertices are optimal at \(n=7\), both one and two are optimal at \(n=8\), and exactly one is optimal for all \(n\ge9\). The optimum weights are \(11\), \(14\), and \(2n-2\), with respectively \(105\), \(238\), and \(\binom n2\) optimal functions.
