# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The source gives a very general fiber isogenous to a product of \(d\) Hodge-generic Abelian varieties of equal dimension, with \(d\mid g\). In rational degree two, the product Mumford--Tate group contributes exactly one invariant symplectic form from each factor and no cross-factor invariants. By Lefschetz \((1,1)\), the Néron--Severi rank is therefore exactly \(d\), and this rank is unchanged by isogeny. The source's symmetric-power construction realizes every factorization \(g=hd\), so every divisor occurs.

## Originality
PASS. The initiating paper was inspected at the exact classification and attainability statements; it does not state the Picard-number divisor classification. Searches for the exact statement, Hodge-invariant aliases, and broader Picard-number results found no covering statement. Oguiso's closely related theorem was inspected and concerns the scheme-theoretic generic fiber over the function field; it explicitly allows larger Picard number on smooth closed fibers and therefore does not cover this claim.

## Value
PASS. This is a natural complete classification of a central discrete invariant of the Abelian fibers, with sharp existence for every allowed value. It also clarifies a substantive distinction between scheme-generic and very-general-closed Picard numbers and records the role of singular primitive-symplectic models in attaining intermediate divisors.

Limitations: the statement is non-isotrivial; it is about very general smooth closed complex fibers; and the all-divisors attainability statement does not assert smooth hyper-Kähler total spaces in the intermediate cases.
