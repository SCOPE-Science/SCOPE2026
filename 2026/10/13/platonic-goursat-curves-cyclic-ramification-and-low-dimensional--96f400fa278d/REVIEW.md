# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The equivariant adjunction/Koszul sequence computes the canonical representation for every smooth \((2,d)\) curve with the invariant rotation action. Exact character products give the rational group-algebra factor dimensions; fixed-point counts follow from cyclic invariant differentials and Riemann--Hurwitz. A second exact arithmetic implementation of the finite character and branching steps returned `VERIFY_OK`. The proof does not infer a universal fact from finite samples.

**Originality — PASS, subject to residual risk.** Gegelia--van Straten give the three Goursat families, genera, and elliptic quotients, but not their canonical representation decompositions, one-branch signatures, or Jacobian factor dimensions. Paulhus--Rojas supply a general decomposition method, not these specialized families; their genus-four and genus-nine tables list other branch signatures. Focused literature and research-record searches for the Goursat source, Platonic Jacobian decomposition, genus-nine octahedral elliptic-quotient cover, and genus-twenty-five icosahedral factors revealed no statement implying the complete result. This is a supported comparison, not an absolute uniqueness certification. A prior abstract group-signature computation or older Goursat literature may contain equivalent consequences.

**Value — PASS.** The work determines all rational isotypic factor dimensions for three naturally motivated high-symmetry curve families, proves complete decomposability uniformly in the tetrahedral and octahedral smooth families, and identifies the entire ramification signature of their canonical quotient maps. These invariants connect geometric curves to their elliptic quotient and arithmetic geometry of Jacobians. No simplicity or polarizations are asserted.

**Scope and risk.** Unpolarized complex isogenies only, singular fibers excluded. Standard group-algebra theory is prior work and is not being re-claimed. The full older invariant-theory bibliography has not been exhaustively searched.
