For every smooth Platonic Goursat curve, the rotation-group map to its elliptic quotient has exactly one branch point, with inertia order \(2\), \(3\), or \(5\). Its Jacobian is a product of elliptic curves up to isogeny in the tetrahedral and octahedral cases; the icosahedral Jacobian is isogenous to one elliptic curve and powers of three abelian surfaces.
