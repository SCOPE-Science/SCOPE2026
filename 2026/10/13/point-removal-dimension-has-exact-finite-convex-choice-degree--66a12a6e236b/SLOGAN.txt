Restoring one deleted point to an \(r\)-order poset realization has exactly the Weihrauch degree of \(r-1\) independent choices from closed intervals. Globally, Marcone--Volpi's strengthened point-removal problem is equivalent to finite parallelization of one-dimensional convex choice: \(\mathsf{DB}_p^+\equiv_{\mathrm W}\mathsf{XC}_1^*\).
