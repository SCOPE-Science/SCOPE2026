# Scientific review: conditioned-fibre mapping fixed points

## Correctness — PASS

The exact probability of simultaneous fixed blocks uses uniform injective target assignment. The factorial-moment polynomial and variance follow directly. The proof verifies the tail-logarithm estimate for the sorted fibre profile, Cauchy coefficient convergence, moment determinacy, compactness, and the converse Poisson criterion using uniformly bounded fourth moments. The independent exhaustive small-size check passed 278 partitions and 1,154 exact-rational moment comparisons, plus 54 limiting-profile moment comparisons. Enumeration is a corroboration, not proof for unbounded sizes.

## Originality — PASS, subject to historical risk

The 2007 Hansen–Jaworski conditioned-indegree model is close and entails the elementary finite counting identity; no novelty is claimed for that identity alone. Its main theorems concern cyclic vertices, components, and typical component size. The 2008 local-properties article treats specified-vertex predecessor and successor statistics. The 2014 bounded-indegree paper gives cyclic/component transition results. Closest catalogued Bernoulli results use different dependence assumptions and do not directly cover fixed kernel partitions. These comparisons support the unrestricted Poisson–Bernoulli limit classification and sharp Poisson criterion as distinct, though general earlier rook or mapping theorems might imply them implicitly. Historical noncoverage is not certified.

## Value — PASS

An exactly prescribed kernel is a natural way to condition collision patterns in a functional graph. The theorem distinguishes microscopic masses (Poisson contributions) from macroscopic masses (Bernoulli contributions), identifies all possible subsequential limits, and supplies the exact if-and-only-if classical Poisson criterion despite a constant mean of one. This is a structural finite-to-infinite law rather than a restatement of an individual marginal mean or a numerical exercise.

## Limitations

No sharp convergence rate is claimed, and no statement covers the joint distribution of longer cycles. The central comparison is a self-review with potential general-theorem coverage risk; it is not independent expert verification.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
