# Scientific review: Poisson-domain-wall purity and the marginal logarithmic dephasing window

**Correctness — PASS.** The normalized state has independent Bernoulli bond walls; the XOR of two independent Born samples has wall probability \(h=2g/(1+g)^2\). Each dephasing pair contributes a squared factor exactly when it crosses unlike XOR bits. The resulting identity is exact for finite chain length. In the critical scaling \(g_n=\lambda/n\), the wall count tends to \(\mathrm{Pois}(2\lambda)\), uniformly bounded crossing counts yield the infinite product for \(\alpha>2\), and divergent crossing sums kill all nonzero-wall sectors for \(\alpha\le2\). At \(\alpha=2\), the \(1/\log n\) noise scale gives the exact factor \(e^{-4ck}\) at fixed wall count. All endpoint and vanishing-factor cases were checked, and the analytic proof is supported by 5095 finite verifier checks. The verifier is corroborative, not an infinite-volume certificate.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** Fechisin and colleagues (arXiv:2610.00480v1, Sec. II, Sec. V, Appendices G and H) introduced the state/channel and already obtained the *linear-string* crossing attenuation and the \(\alpha=2\) summability boundary. Their Eq. (74) offers a general purity measurement identity, and Appendix H an exact \(g=1,\alpha=0\) purity. These facts are acknowledged, not repackaged as novel. Neither the dilute-limit global-purity product nor the critical vanishing-strength formula appears in the inspected primary material. Related pure-MPS and measurement-feedback work does not imply the quantified result. Search coverage cannot exclude an unindexed derivation or an implicit specialization of the source's general classical partition function.

**Value — PASS.** The inverse-length domain-wall fugacity is selected by a natural finite-Poisson-wall limit, and global purity is a source-motivated observable controlling quadratic-measurement statistics. The exact limiting invariant and marginal crossover explain when substantial global purity survives as the system grows. This supplies a quantitative, motivated extension of numerical high-purity observations without making claims about thermodynamic mixed-phase preservation.

**Limitations.** This proof applies to the specified open-chain MPS and one pair-dephasing layer. The source does not define \(\alpha=1\). Results are pointwise in their stated parameters, and no mixed-state long-range-order, fault-tolerance, or experimental robustness theorem follows. Independent scientific validation remains outstanding.
