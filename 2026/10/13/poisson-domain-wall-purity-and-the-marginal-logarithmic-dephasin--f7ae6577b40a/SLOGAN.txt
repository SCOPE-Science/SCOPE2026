For an open quantum chain with \(g_n=\lambda/n\) domain-wall fugacity and fixed \(p\in(0,1]\), global purity after power-law pair dephasing converges exactly to \(\exp[-2\lambda(1-q_\alpha(p))]\), where \(q_\alpha(p)=0\) at the defined exponents below or equal to \(2\) and is an explicit nonzero-or-zero infinite crossing product for \(\alpha>2\). At the marginal exponent \(\alpha=2\), shrinking dephasing strength as \(p_n=c/\log n\) yields the sharp crossover \(\exp[-2\lambda(1-e^{-4c})]\).
