Yuan's universal single-shot conversion admits an explicit polylogarithmic-overhead regime: from any positive-rate bounded-degree CSS QLDPC seed family it produces \(k=\Theta(N/(\log N)^{1/\alpha})\) logical qubits with polynomially small failure over polynomially many noisy correction rounds. In that regime the local decoder runs in \(O(\log\log N)\) parallel time.
