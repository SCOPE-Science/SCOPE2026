Finite tree-plan generics have a sharp closure-spectrum growth dichotomy: if infinity-nodes are pairwise incomparable, finite tree-closure types have an explicit restricted-partition generating function and polynomial cumulative growth; if two infinity-nodes lie on one root path, that spectrum instead grows faster than every polynomial but subexponentially. The exact recursive series is a multiset product over infinity-labeled successors, and the simplest nested plan realizes the ordinary partition numbers.
