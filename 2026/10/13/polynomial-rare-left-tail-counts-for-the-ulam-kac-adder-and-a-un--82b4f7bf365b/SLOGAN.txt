For the Ulam–Kac adder, the number of paths ending a fixed surplus \(r\) above the minimum is an exact degree-\(r\) polynomial with nonnegative integer binomial-basis coefficients. Its relative deficit from the corresponding Mahonian inversion count is asymptotically \(r(r-1)(r-2)/n^2\) for every fixed integer \(r\ge3\), with a proved sharp constant.
