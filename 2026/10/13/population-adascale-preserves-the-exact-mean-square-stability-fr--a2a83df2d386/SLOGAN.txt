On scalar multiplicative-curvature noise, population AdaScale keeps the sharp base learning-rate stability interval \(0<\alpha<2\mu/(\mu^2+v)\) exactly unchanged at every scale \(S\), while its optimally tuned expected-objective factor improves to \(v/(S\mu^2+v)\). Fixed linear scaling instead shrinks the admissible base-rate interval to \(0<\alpha<2\mu/(S\mu^2+v)\).
