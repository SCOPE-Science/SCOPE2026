Every ordinary power of a two-row ladder in the range \(k\ge1\), \(\ell\ge k+1\) has both ordinary and positive semidefinite zero forcing number exactly \(2k\). An explicit integer Gram matrix realizes maximum nullity \(2k\), yielding exact minimum ranks \(2(\ell-k)\) and proving the full two-row case of the recently posed grid-power question.
