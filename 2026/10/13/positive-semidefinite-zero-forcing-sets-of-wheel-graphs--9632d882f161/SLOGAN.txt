For every wheel \(W_n\) with \(n\ge5\), a positive semidefinite zero forcing set either contains the hub and at least two rim vertices, or omits the hub and contains three consecutive rim vertices. Thus the minimum bases number exactly \(\binom{n}{2}\), and the full forcing polynomial is obtained by subtracting the cyclic \(111\)-avoiding transfer-matrix enumerator from all hub-free rim subsets.
