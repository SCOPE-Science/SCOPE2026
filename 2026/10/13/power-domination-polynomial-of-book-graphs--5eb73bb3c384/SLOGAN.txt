For the \(n\)-page book graph \(B_n\), \(n\ge2\), a set is power dominating exactly when it meets the spine or, without the spine, meets all but at most one page. Hence \(\mathcal P(B_n;x)=(2x+x^2)(1+x)^{2n}+(2x+x^2)^n+n(2x+x^2)^{n-1}\), giving the complete cardinality distribution of power dominating sets.
