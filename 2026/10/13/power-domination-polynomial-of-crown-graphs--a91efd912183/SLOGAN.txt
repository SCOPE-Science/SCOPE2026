For every crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n\) with \(n\ge3\), a set power dominates exactly when it meets both bipartition classes or occupies at least \(n-2\) vertices of one class. Hence \(\mathcal P(\operatorname{Cr}_n;x)=\big((1+x)^n-1\big)^2+2\left(\binom n2x^{n-2}+nx^{n-1}+x^n\right)\), giving every cardinality count at once.
