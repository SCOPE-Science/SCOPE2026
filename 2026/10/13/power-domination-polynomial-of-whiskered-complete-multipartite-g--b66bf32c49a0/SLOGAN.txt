For every complete multipartite base with at least two vertices in each part, the entire power-domination polynomial of its private-leaf corona has an explicit closed form, obtained by classifying sets according to strong core parts and vacant core--leaf pairs. In particular, its minimum power domination number is \(2\), with exactly \(\sum_{i<j}n_in_j+(2N-1)|\{i:n_i=2\}|+4\mathbf1_{N=4}\) minimum sets.
