For smooth convex minimization over any finite-dimensional compact strictly convex feasible body, exact-oracle Frank–Wolfe with curvature short steps or exact line search converges in \(o(1/t)\), without any power-type curvature assumption. A positive qualitative support-gap modulus suffices even for superflat boundary arcs \(y=e^{-1/x^2}\), where every power-type sharpness condition fails.
