PowerGrad's modified backward field is exactly the gradient of \(\Psi_\alpha(W,\theta)=\alpha^{-1}\mathcal L(\alpha W,\theta)\). Under \(V=\alpha W\), plain PowerGrad SGD is ordinary cross-entropy SGD with head rate \(\alpha\eta_W\) and body rate \(\eta_\theta/\alpha\), so one common learning rate hides an exact body-to-head rebalance by \(1/\alpha^2\).
