PowerSign and AddSign classify every finite step of an alternating gradient as momentum agreement whenever \(\beta\le A/B<1/\beta\); at the source value \(\beta=0.9\), this includes all ratios from \(0.9\) to \(1.111111\ldots\). For the balanced Nyquist signal \(A=B\), the base rules therefore collapse exactly to the constant rescalings \(e\,g_t\) and \(2g_t\).
