For every finite loopless quiver, all prescribed additive-commutator fibers of its radical-square-zero algebra over \(\mathbb F_q\) are explicit multivariate Tutte evaluations with edge weights determined by the target's zero pattern. A target is a single commutator exactly when its support graph is \(q^2\)-colorable, and the full additive commutator width is \(\min\{r\ge0:\chi(G)\le q^{2r}\}\), hence unbounded as the quiver varies.
