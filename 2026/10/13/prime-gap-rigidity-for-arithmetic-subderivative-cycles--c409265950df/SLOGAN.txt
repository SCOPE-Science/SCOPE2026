For every pair of primes \(p<q\le p^p\), the arithmetic subderivative supported on \(\{p,q\}\) has no nontrivial positive cycles: its positive periodic points are precisely the fixed families \(p^p m\) and \(q^q m\) with \(\gcd(m,pq)=1\). Any hypothetical nontrivial cycle for two supported primes therefore requires \(q>p^p\).
