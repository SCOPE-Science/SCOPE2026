For a uniform open quantum chain with \(p-1\) sites, where \(p\) is an odd prime, the mean number of endpoint-return measurements has values \(p-1\), \(p-2\), and (when \(p\ge5\)) \(p-3\). Its exceptional sampling times comprise exactly \(((p-1)/2)^2\) pairwise disjoint arithmetic resonance ladders, proved using cyclotomic rational independence.
