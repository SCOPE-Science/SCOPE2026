Among integers \(n\) with a nontrivial binomial GCD but no prime factor above \(\sqrt n\), the dominant exact prime-power component produces a complete asymptotic hierarchy: exponent \(a\ge2\) has count \(\sim\frac{4a^2}{a^2-1}X^{(a+1)/(2a)}/\log X\). The union has count \(\sim\frac{16}{3}X^{3/4}/\log X\), dominated by prime squares.
