# Review: Prime powers are the exact pointwise-injectivity locus for the additive transform

## Correctness
**PASS.** Prime powers follow immediately from the definition. For mixed support, two explicit positive completely multiplicative constructions prove failure of each implication. The only possible algebraic obstruction is \(P=2Q\) for powers of distinct primes, which is impossible by parity and unique factorization. No finite computation is used as an infinite proof.

## Originality
**PASS.** The focal source's Proposition 2.4 asserts the opposite universal pointwise equivalence. Theorem 2.1 supplies the evaluation formula, but the checked source does not state the exact prime-power validity locus or the fact that both implications fail at every mixed-support integer. Exact-phrase, correction, injectivity, and semantic searches found no covering correction. Residual risk remains from unindexed commentary.

## Value
**PASS.** This identifies the exact maximal domain of validity of a structural proposition about a newly introduced transform. Showing failure of both logical directions at every mixed-support integer is a reusable boundary theorem, not a one-off numerical counterexample.
