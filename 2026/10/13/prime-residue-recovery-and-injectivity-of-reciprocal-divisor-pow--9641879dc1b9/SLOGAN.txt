For every even \(k\ge2\), power-of-two divisor count forces all odd prime divisors of \(\sigma_k(n)\) into the single residue class \(1\pmod{2^{\nu_2(k)+1}}\). This recovers every permitted odd prime exponent from the reduced denominator of \(\sigma_k(n)/n^k\) and gives injectivity, including unrestricted powers of \(2\), on a natural infinite family related to a 2015 conjecture.
