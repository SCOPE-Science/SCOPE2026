For the Tran–Nguyen counterexample \(R=\iota(k[x,y,z])+k^{(X\times\mathbb N)}\), the spectrum is exactly one copy of \(\operatorname{Spec}k[x,y,z]\) together with countably many isolated prime points, and the minimal primes are the polynomial-component kernel plus all coordinate kernels. Its idempotents see only finite or cofinite sets of those isolated points.
