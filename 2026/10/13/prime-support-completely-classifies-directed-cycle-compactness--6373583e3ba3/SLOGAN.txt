Directed-cycle compactness sees only prime support: over \(\mathsf{ZF}\), \(K_{C_n}\) is exactly the conjunction of \(K_{C_p}\) over primes \(p\mid n\). Consequently \(\operatorname{AC}(N)\) needs only the prime-cycle principles with \(p\le N\), and, assuming consistency, \(K_{C_m}\) implies \(K_{C_n}\) exactly when \(\operatorname{rad}(n)\mid\operatorname{rad}(m)\).
