Every fixed \(b\)-row of the Wang–Zelinsky family contains an explicit arithmetic progression of primitive strongly pseudoperfect numbers: with \(a=b+t(b+1)\), all \(2^a(2^{a+1}-(2^b+1))(2^{b+1}-1)\) are primitive, and their count up to \(X\) is \(\frac{\log X}{2(b+1)\log2}+O_b(1)\). For \(b=1\), every exponent \(a\ge2\) works.
