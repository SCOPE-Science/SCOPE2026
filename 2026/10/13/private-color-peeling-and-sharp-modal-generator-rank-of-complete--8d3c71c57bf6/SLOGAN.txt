For loopless complete multipartite Kripke frames, deleting parts with a color private among remaining parts gives the exact modal-equivalence quotient, including any unsplittable residual core. If the part sizes are \(n_{(1)}\ge\cdots\ge n_{(k)}\), the minimum number of Boolean propositional generators is \(\left\lceil\log_2\max_i(n_{(i)}+i-1)\right\rceil\), and every valuation stabilizes within \(k-1\) modal rounds, sharply.
