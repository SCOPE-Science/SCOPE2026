For two commuting square-zero matrices of maximal rank in dimension \(2m\) over any \(\mathbb F_q\), the product rank is exactly one of \(0,1,\ldots,\lfloor m/2\rfloor\), and every rank has an explicit Gaussian-binomial and general-linear-group multiplicity. The positive-rank fibers extend the mutually annihilating boundary to a complete finite-field distribution on this natural representation-theoretic stratum.
