For every ladder graph with \(m\ge3\) square faces, the standard projective edge toric variety has a pure codimension-three reduced singular locus with exactly \(5(m-1)\) components. Their degrees are \(2^{m-2}\) with multiplicity \(m+3\), and \(2^{m-3}\) with multiplicity \(4(m-2)\), so the singular locus has projective degree \((3m-1)2^{m-2}\); the generic transverse singularities are ordinary threefold nodes.
