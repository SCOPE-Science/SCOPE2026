Every unordered arrangement of three smooth complex conics with two distinct \(A_7\) points has a unique parameter \(\kappa\in\mathbb C^*\) in the form \(\bigl(w^2-uv\bigr)\bigl(w^2-uv+u^2\bigr)\bigl(w^2-uv+\kappa v^2\bigr)=0\). Exactly \(\kappa=1\) is free; every other member is nearly free, and every stabilizer is \(C_2\times C_2\).
