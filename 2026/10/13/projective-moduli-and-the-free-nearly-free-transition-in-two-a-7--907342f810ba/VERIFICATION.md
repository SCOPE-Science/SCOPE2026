# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Bézout makes the two order-four contacts lie on distinct component pairs, which share a central conic. Restriction to its Veronese parametrization gives fourth powers at the marked points, with kernel spanned by q, hence leaves q+a u² and q+b v², both coefficients nonzero. Marked-conic automorphisms extend projectively and exhaust the possible projectivities after the singularity check: their diagonal scaling reciprocally rescales a,b and the reversal interchanges them, so ab is the full invariant. At fixed normalized product the pointwise marked subgroup is generated by w→−w, and the reversal by (u,v)→(sqrt(k)v,u/sqrt(k)); the two commuting involutions give precisely four elements. In v=1 the leaves intersect at u²=k, w²=u−k. This accounts for four distinct simple intersections except at k=1 where one is double, exhausting the intersection multiplicity and excluding any further A7. There is no triple intersection. The Tjurina numbers are therefore 18 and 19. Independent exact reconstruction confirmed both syzygies and the two reported minors. Independently, writing a degree-two syzygy as a derivation with linear logarithmic cofactors L_0,L_1,L_2 of the three conics, differences are L_1−L_0=alpha u and L_2−L_0=beta v. Divisibility of the solved w-component by w forces alpha=beta/k and alpha=beta. Together with L_0+L_1+L_2=0 this leaves no quadratic relation off k=1 and exactly one at k=1, so no smaller relation there. Dimca's criteria apply with r=2<d/2 in the free case and r=3=d/2 in the nearly-free case.
