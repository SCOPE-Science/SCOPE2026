Every labeled proper conflict-free coloring of the crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M_n\), for \(n\ge3\) and any palette size, is counted by an exact finite partial-Bell-polynomial formula that separates shared matching-pair colors from shore-exclusive colors. With four colors, all colorings for \(n\ge5\) use exactly two shared matched pairs, giving \(24\binom n2\) colorings; the four-by-four crown exceptionally has 384.
