Published Ranger21 can be undefined even on a smooth strongly convex quadratic: Gradient Centralization can drive its first adaptive second moment to zero while Stable Weight Decay plus Norm Loss divides by that moment scale. Near the symmetric set the published regularization displacement grows exactly like \(1/|\delta|\); the original Stable Weight Decay rule and later first-party implementation avoid the singularity by adding a positive denominator safeguard.
