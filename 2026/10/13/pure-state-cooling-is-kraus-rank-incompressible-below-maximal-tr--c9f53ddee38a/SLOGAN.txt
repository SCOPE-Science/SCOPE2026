A pure replacer channel on a \(d\)-dimensional input cannot be uniformly approximated with statewise trace-norm error below \(2\) by any channel of Kraus rank smaller than \(d\). Consequently, single-contact cooling of arbitrary \(N\)-mode fermionic inputs to one pure target with a pure coherent bath needs at least \(N\) bath modes; the recent two-bath-modes-per-system-mode construction is within a factor two of this absolute mode minimum.
