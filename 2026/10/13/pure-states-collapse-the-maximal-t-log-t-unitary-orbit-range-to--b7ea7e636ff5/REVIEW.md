# Review

## Correctness
PASS. For distinct rank-one projectors \(P,Q\), the measurement \(\{Q,I-Q\}\) sends them to \((c,1-c)\) and \((1,0)\) with \(c<1\). The classical \(t\log t\) divergence is therefore \(+\infty\), and data processing forces the maximal quantum divergence to be \(+\infty\). Equality of the projectors gives divergence \(0\). This proves the complete orbit image \(\{0,+\infty\}\).

## Value
PASS. The counterexample directly invalidates a headline conclusion stated in the source abstract and Corollary 3.3 for arbitrary quantum states. It isolates the mathematical reason: epsilon-regularized maximal divergences can be extended-valued and discontinuous at rank-deficient support changes, so connectedness of the unitary orbit does not force an interval image. The repair is conceptually useful because it separates the faithful regime, where the continuity route is available, from singular boundary behavior.
