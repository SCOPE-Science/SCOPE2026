On a constant-magnitude alternating gradient, bias-corrected QHAdam erases its entire second-moment immediate discount: \(\nu_2\) and \(\beta_2\) have no effect on the update magnitude. The asymptotic Nyquist attenuation is exactly \(1-2\nu_1\beta_1/(1+\beta_1)\), with the sharp floor \(1-\nu_1\); for the paper's WMT16 setting it is \(0.2205128205\ldots\).
