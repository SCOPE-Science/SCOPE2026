For every finite field and every \(m\ge3\), multiplication by a nonzero quadratic \(ax^2+bxy+cy^2\) in \(\mathbb F_q[x,y]/(x^m,y^m)\) has one of exactly two adjacent ranks. Their complete multiplicities depend only on explicit greatest-common-divisor expressions involving the prime-to-characteristic part of \(m\) and \(q\pm1\).
