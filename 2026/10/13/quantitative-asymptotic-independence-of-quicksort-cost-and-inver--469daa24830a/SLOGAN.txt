For first-element-pivot Quicksort on a uniform random permutation, its centered comparison cost and input inversion count have exactly zero covariance but are not independent at finite size. Their limiting order-\(n\) and order-\(n^{3/2}\) fluctuations are independent, and a uniform nonlinear covariance bound quantifies the decoupling at rate \(O(n^{-1/6}(\log n)^{2/3})\).
