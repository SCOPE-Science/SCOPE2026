Every cumulant of the minimum cost of three disjoint edges in an exponentially weighted complete bipartite graph is positive, for every pair of side lengths \(m,n\geq3\). Quantitatively, the order-\(p\) cumulant is at least \(\frac{(p-1)!}{2}(3/(mn))^p\), simultaneously for every integer \(p\geq1\).
