# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. Quarter-turn invariance intertwines the quasi-momentum fibers, so the lowest dressed-pair energy obeys \(E(U,Rk)=E(U,k)\). At every point where the source's dispersion is \(C^2\), two applications of the chain rule give \(H_U(Rk)=R H_U(k)R^{\mathsf T}\). When the Hessian is invertible, the inverse tensor has the same covariance. For a real symmetric \(2\times2\) matrix \(A\), direct multiplication gives \(A+RAR^{\mathsf T}=(\operatorname{tr}A)I_2\). No numerical experiment is used as proof. The hard-core case is inherited from the finite-repulsion covariance through the source's hard-core limit.

## Originality
PASS. The primary paper was inspected in the sections defining the symmetry assumptions, fiber decomposition, dispersion regularity, hard-core limit, and tensor mass. It does not state the general quarter-turn tensor covariance or the equal-valley isotropy identity found here. A closely related 2018 thesis contains special effective-mass calculations around \((\pi,0)\) and \((0,\pi)\), and the associated earlier isotropic exchange article was checked at abstract level; neither inspected source supplied the generalized statement. Targeted searches for Hessian covariance, mass-tensor covariance, valley averaging, and isotropic effective mass found no covering implication.

Residual risk: an older paper may express the same symmetry observation using different valley or effective-mass terminology. This is why the claim is limited to the exact generalized-model statement and does not assert priority for the underlying elementary matrix identity.

## Value
PASS. The source treats the effective dispersion and tensor mass as central observables, and the symmetry-related cuprate momenta are natural rather than chosen ad hoc. The theorem gives a parameter-independent transport consequence: single-valley anisotropy may persist, but it cancels exactly in the equal-weight quadratic average. This is useful when separating anisotropic pairing structure from quadratic center-of-mass response.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest literature is the primary generalized exchange-interaction paper, the earlier isotropic bipolaron--fermion exchange article, and the related 2018 thesis. The result does not prove that physical occupations are equal, does not prove positivity or invertibility of the mass tensor, and does not constrain higher-order anisotropy.
