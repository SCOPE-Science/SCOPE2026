At the distinguished three-state Potts-tree threshold \(\widetilde\theta_1(k)\), the source's reciprocal critical point \(v=1\) is a strict quartic local maximum for every \(k\ge2\). Just above the threshold it splits into two reciprocal strict local maxima with \(\log v_+\sim2\sqrt{C_k}\sqrt{\theta-\widetilde\theta_1}\), while the center becomes a local minimum.
