In the generic 2-nilpotent Lazard Lie algebra over \(\mathbb F_q\), ordered tuples independent modulo the commutator layer are classified exactly by subspaces of \(\Lambda^2\mathbb F_q^n\). That Galois-number stratum, together with a free-object upper encoding, forces the complete tuple-orbit profile to satisfy \(\log_q o_n=n^4/16+O(n^3)\).
