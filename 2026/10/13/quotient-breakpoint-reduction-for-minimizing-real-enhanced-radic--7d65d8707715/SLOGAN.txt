For the real enhanced radical, an exact minimizing degree can occur only at a quotient breakpoint where some \(\lfloor m_\ell/d\rfloor\) drops at the next degree. This reduces the full search through the largest valuation to at most \(2\sum_{\ell\mid bc}\lfloor\sqrt{m_\ell}\rfloor\) candidates; for the Reyssat triple only \(\{2,3,5,10\}\) remain.
