Uniform random-priority greedy selection on a clique corona \(H\odot K_s\) is exactly greedy selection on an independently \(1/(s+1)\)-thinned core, followed by independent uniform private-clique choices. The terminal law is uniform precisely when the core is edgeless, and for complete cores its total-variation bias is \(s/(s+n)-(s/(s+1))^n\).
