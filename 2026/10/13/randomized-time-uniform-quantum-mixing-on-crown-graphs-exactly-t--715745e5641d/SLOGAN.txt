For the unweighted crown graphs \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}\setminus M\) with \(n\ge3\), uniform continuous-time quantum-walk mixing under an arbitrary probability distribution of observation times is possible exactly for \(n=3\) and \(n=4\). A four-time law uniformly mixes the six-cycle \(\operatorname{Cr}_3\), while a pointwise orbit-probability bound rules out every \(n\ge5\).
