For every finite permutation \(\sigma\), a bounded exponential formula gives the complete joint rank and nilpotency-index counts of nilpotent partial injections commuting with \(\sigma\), refined by the cycle type of \(\sigma\). The attainable ranks under \(\beta^h=0\) are exactly the restricted sums \(\sum_\ell\ell a_\ell\) with \(0\le a_\ell\le m_\ell-\lceil m_\ell/h\rceil\), giving a sharp maximum.
