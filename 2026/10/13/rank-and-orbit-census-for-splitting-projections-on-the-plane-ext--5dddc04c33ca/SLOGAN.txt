The idempotent weight-minus-one Rota–Baxter operators on the four-dimensional exterior algebra form four isolated automorphism classes and four incidence families, each a single automorphism orbit. Over \(\mathbb F_q\) their rank distribution is \((1,\ 1+q^2(q+1),\ 2q^2(q+1),\ 1+q^2(q+1),\ 1)\), giving exactly \(4+4q^2(q+1)\) operators.
