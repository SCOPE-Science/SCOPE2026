For a two-step Leibniz algebra defined by a nondegenerate bilinear form, the exact finite-field number of rank-one derivations is \(q^n-1+q(q^r-1)\), where \(r\) is the radical dimension of the form's symmetrization. Every such derivation is square-zero in odd characteristic, while characteristic two can produce exactly \(q^r\) rank-one idempotent derivations, determined by a nonalternating restriction to the symmetrization radical.
