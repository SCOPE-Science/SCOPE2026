For the Lie–Yamaguti algebra \([e_1,e_2]=2e_4\), \(\llbracket e_1,e_2,e_1\rrbracket=e_4\), the complete rank-at-most-one Rota–Baxter locus is the union of maps killing \(e_4\) and maps with image in \(\langle e_3,e_4\rangle\). Over odd \(\mathbf F_q\) it has \(q^6+2q^5+q^4-q^3-q^2-q\) points, and it contains \(E_{21}\), which is outside the source’s displayed matrix family.
