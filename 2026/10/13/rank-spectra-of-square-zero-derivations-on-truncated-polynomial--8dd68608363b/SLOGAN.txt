Over every finite field of characteristic two, the entire rank distribution of square-zero derivations of \(k[t]/(t^{2s})\) agrees exactly with that for \(k[t]/(t^{2s+1})\), by an explicit rank-preserving bijection. Its coefficients form two simple regimes, \((q^2-1)q^{2r-2}\) at lower ranks and the constant \((q-1)q^{s-1}\) at higher ranks.
