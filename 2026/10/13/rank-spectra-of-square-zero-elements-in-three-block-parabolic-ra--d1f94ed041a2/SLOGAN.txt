For every finite field and every positive three-block parabolic type, the square-zero radical admits an explicit Gaussian rank polynomial, with all ranks from zero through \(\min\{\lfloor(a+b+c)/2\rfloor,a+b,a+c,b+c\}\) and no others. The coefficients refine classical upper-triangular square-zero counts by the full matrix rank under a prescribed block pattern.
