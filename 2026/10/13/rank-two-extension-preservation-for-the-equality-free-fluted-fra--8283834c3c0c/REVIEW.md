# Scientific review: rank-two fluted extension preservation

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof explicitly fixes Ding's equality-free, nonempty-domain, induced-substructure semantics and separates finite from arbitrary-model quantifiers. Structural induction gives completeness of rank-two row descriptors. The essential nonstandard step is checked constructively: compatibility of row supports implies a genuine induced extension preserving the *entire* descriptor set of a target, with at most one fresh target-copy per possible suffix type. Unary target coverage, the old pairs, both orientations of binary relations, finite model size, and higher-arity relations are handled. The resulting characteristic formulas contain only existential quantifiers and atomic negations, have depth at most two, and establish both implications. The executable bounded test exercises mixed unary/binary signatures but is not the infinite proof. No contradiction was found in the stated hypotheses or argument.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risks.** A full reading of Ding's arXiv:2607.12970v2, especially Sections 2–4 and its overview, establishes equirank homomorphism preservation and a rank-three obstruction to extension preservation; it does not state the positive rank-two induced-extension theorem. The proof here requires a different extension-amalgamation lemma with complete *signed* row types, which does not follow from the positive homomorphism result because existential sentences may have negative atoms. Yin (2026) proves extension preservation for the ordered-prefix fragment, so pure-binary overlap is acknowledged, while the fluted mixed unary/binary suffix syntax is not automatically ordered. Literature and semantic-database searches found no statement implying this theorem for the entire rank-two fluted fragment. Residual risks include older equivalent type-amalgamation theorems, a nontrivial translation of mixed-arity fluted rank two to ordered logic, and inaccessible full text of some adjacent results. No assertion of global novelty certification is made.

**Value — PASS.** This completes the exact first two ranks of a central preservation problem in an unrestricted fragment and, combined with the published rank-three counterexample, locates its first failure. The arbitrary-signature theorem and rank-preserving existential normal form are structural rather than a finite numerical curiosity. The characteristic-witness and amalgamation method may be useful in related low-rank preservation analyses; no unproved downstream theorem is asserted.

The proof does not extend to equality, arbitrary rank, or effective formula-size bounds. Same-model evidence is not independent verification.
