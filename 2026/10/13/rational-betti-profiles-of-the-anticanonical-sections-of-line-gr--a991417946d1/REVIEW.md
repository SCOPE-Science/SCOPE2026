# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The exact domain is the general \(N\)-fold Plücker hyperplane section of \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,N)\) with even \(N\ge6\). Adjunction gives trivial canonical bundle; the tangent bundle K-theory identity and normal sequence produce a precise universal top Chern polynomial. Schubert-alternant integration and Gauss–Bonnet compute the Euler characteristic for every \(N\), while weak Lefschetz, Schubert partition counts, and duality determine every rational Betti rank. The only nontrivial numerical examples were independently recomputed by coefficient extraction and torus localization. The finite checker does not substitute for the all-\(N\) proof.

## Originality — PASS, with bibliographic risks
The initiating 2026 Fano work concerns \((N-1)\)-fold sections and their Pfaffian birational geometry. Benedetti's full relevant sections classify the \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,8)\) eight-hyperplane fourfold and list the **holomorphic** Euler invariant \(\chi(\mathcal O)=2\), not this complete **topological** Betti profile. The later Calabi–Yau paper treats Pfaffian duals and focal geometry and does not state an Euler/Betti evaluation. published research index and exact public searches for the source, the Euler values, primitive ranks, and formula aliases revealed no covering item. However, the September Fano paper itself could not be read in full and a formula implied in general by classical characteristic-class machinery might have been explicitly evaluated elsewhere. Thus this is not an assertion of exhaustive bibliographic uniqueness.

The family is not an arbitrary numerical slice: anticanonical divisors of the specific index-one Fano linear sections form a naturally distinguished infinite family of Calabi–Yau varieties. The uniform profile gives the complete rational cohomology ranks, including exact primitive middle dimensions. The sixfold rank \(24910\) and the fourfold rank \(630\) provide useful exact targets for geometric and Hodge-theoretic investigations, independently of any unproved downstream application.
