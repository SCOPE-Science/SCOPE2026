For every even \(N\ge6\), the general anticanonical divisor of the index-one Fano \((N-1)\)-hyperplane section of \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,N)\) has a complete rational Betti polynomial given by one explicit Chern–Schubert coefficient. The first sixfold \(N=10\) has \(\chi_{\mathrm{top}}=24920\) and primitive middle Betti rank \(24910\).
