The rational singularities of an odd-characteristic degree-\(q+3\) plane-filling pencil are exactly ordinary nodes and cusps, with cusps confined to \(256k^7=3^7\). Across the complete pencil there are exactly \(q-1\) rational singular parameter-point pairs; the split-versus-nonsplit node census is explicit when \(7\nmid(q-1)\), and otherwise governed by a natural genus-three hyperelliptic curve.
