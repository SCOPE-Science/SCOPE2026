Each coincidence detector has a nonzero Fourier component in each sector; the bright dimension is the number of distinct H-energies per sector summed over all m. With t=|m|, energies are E=(a+b)d-2bt-2(a+b)u-2(a-b)v, 0<=u<=d-t,0<=v<=t. Equal rates yield B=2^(d-1)(d+2), irrational unequal ratio gives G=2^(d-2)(d^2+3d+4), and rational (a+b)/|a-b|=p/q in reduced p>q>=1 yields G - sum_{t=p}^{d-q} binom(d,t)(d-t-q+1)(t-p+1); the primitive coprime grid-step (q,+/-p) connects each energy class, explaining the count and exact first deficit at d=p+q of binom(p+q,p). Independently constructed direct Fourier-grid and dense two-body detector eigenspace computations corroborate d=2,3 and 3:1 speed at d=3 ->41; not an unbounded numerical proof.
