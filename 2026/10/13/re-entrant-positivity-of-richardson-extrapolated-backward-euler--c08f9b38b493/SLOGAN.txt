For homogeneous absorbing pure-birth chains, second-order Richardson extrapolation can destroy the positivity of backward Euler at arbitrarily small steps: five states are the first in this hierarchy to do so. For \(N=5\), positivity holds exactly at \(h=0\) and on \(\eta\le h\le2+2\sqrt2\), where \(\eta\) is the unique positive root of \(h^5+18h^4+52h^3+48h^2+2h-12\).
