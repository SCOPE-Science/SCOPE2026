On the coefficient-normalized affine chart of every proper higher secant variety of a rational normal curve, reciprocal-series coordinates yield an exact algebraic product \(\mathbb A^1\times\operatorname{Cone}(\sigma_{r-1}(C_{d-2}))\). The reduction works scheme-theoretically for all \(r\ge2\) and \(d\ge2r\), and identifies the entire rational normal curve in this chart with the smooth-factor axis.
