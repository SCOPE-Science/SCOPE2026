For a quantum walker on a weighted Cartesian product of two complete graphs, any rectangular pattern of positive-rate absorbers has an exact sitewise capture law and bright-space dimension \((1+\min(r,m-1))(1+\min(s,n-1))\), except that precisely one bright direction is lost when the weighted spectral gaps coincide and neither detector factor is complete. This rank-one dark resonance changes the minimum size of a completely absorbing detector rectangle from \((m-1)(n-1)\) to \(mn-\max(m,n)\).
