For a fixed product of two finite Gödel chains of sizes \(m,n\geq2\), the Frobenius-adjoint unary expansions split exactly into rectangular-core and synchronized-diagonal-core types, with total count \(2^{m+n-4}+\binom{m+n-4}{m-2}\). The theorem classifies all such expansions, not merely the relatively complete cores from earlier general theory.
