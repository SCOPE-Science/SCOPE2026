The projective Jacobian singular scheme of the hollow \(4\times4\) symmetric determinant quartic is reduced everywhere, with eight plane and three quadric components and total degree \(14\). At each coordinate vertex its singular scheme is the tetrahedral Stanley--Reisner surface germ \(\mathbb C[[b,c,d,e]]/(bcd,bce,bde,cde)\), with Hilbert--Samuel multiplicity \(6\).
