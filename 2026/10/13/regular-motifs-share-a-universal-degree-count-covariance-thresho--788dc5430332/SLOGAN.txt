In \(G(n,p)\), the covariance between the number of degree-\(k\) vertices and any fixed \(d\)-regular motif has a sign determined only by whether \((k)_d\) is below or above \((n-1)_d p^d\). Thus triangles, all cycles, and every other regular motif exhibit a universal degree-class sign switch within a constant distance of the mean degree.
