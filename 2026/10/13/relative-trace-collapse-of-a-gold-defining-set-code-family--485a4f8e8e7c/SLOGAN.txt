For the Xiang--Song Gold construction, the two defining equations depend only on the \(\mathbb F_2\)-span of the relative traces of the two parameters. In the rank-one regime of their Theorem 3.5 this collapses all three parameter cases, for fixed \(m\), to one permutation-equivalence class carrying a free cyclic norm-one automorphism of order \(2^{{m/2}}+1\).
