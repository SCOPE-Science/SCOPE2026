In the 2026 coupled avian-influenza model, endemic birds force a unique positive human equilibrium for every \(\mathcal R_0^h>0\) whenever \(\beta_{bh}>0\); the true all-positive feasibility threshold is \(\mathcal R_0^b>1\), not simultaneous bird and human thresholds. As spillover vanishes, the human endemic level is linear below \(\mathcal R_0^h=1\), square-root at criticality, and approaches the autonomous human endemic level above it.
