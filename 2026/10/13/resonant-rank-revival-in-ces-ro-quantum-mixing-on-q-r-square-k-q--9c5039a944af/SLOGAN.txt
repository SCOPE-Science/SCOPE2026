In the standard adjacency quantum walk on \(Q_r\square K_q\) for \(r\ge1,q\ge3\), the Cesàro mixing matrix has exactly half the possible rank unless \(q\equiv2\pmod4\) and \(r\ge q/2\), when synchronized spectral resonances revive explicitly counted odd-weight modes. The first revival raises the rank on \(Q_3\square K_6\) from \(24\) to \(29\) out of \(48\).
