# Self-review: retraction rank spectrum

## Correctness — PASS
Every Lie endomorphism is determined by a generator linear part \(T\) and central shear \(\phi\); its central action is forced to be \(\bigwedge^2T\). Composition idempotence yields exactly \(T^2=T\) and \(\phi T+(\bigwedge^2T)\phi=\phi\). Decomposing the generator space into image and kernel makes the permitted shear coordinates explicit. The shear automorphism eliminates them and proves a single orbit for each generator rank. Gaussian counting of images, complements, and shear fibers yields the exponent \(r(n-r)(n+2r)/2\) and image rank \(\binom{r+1}{2}\). The packaged exhaustive checker verifies three small cases; it is not used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
The universal mapping property and the description of free nilpotent automorphisms are established in Benito–Roldán-López, arXiv:1911.01897, Proposition 1 and Remark 2. Their results are treated as prior knowledge. The additional idempotence equations, conjugacy-orbit collapse and finite-field point-count formula are not supplied by its inspected statements or sections. The other inspected primary sources address uniserial or faithful representations rather than retractions. The classical unrestricted-free-Lie retract classification is a genuine nearby precursor but not an inspected covering rank table. Alternative terminology and indirect enumerative literature remain possible originality risks.

## Value — PASS
Retractions encode split quotients and direct-summand-like structure of free nilpotent Lie algebras. The complete \(n+1\)-orbit classification and exact Gaussian rank multiplicities solve a natural parameterized counting problem for all finite fields, rather than an arbitrary finite slice. The triangular-number restriction on ranks and the explicit dimensions of the central-shear fibers explain how free generator rank controls the entire operator rank. Nothing is claimed for longer nilpotency classes or nonfree quotients.
