Every Lie-algebra retraction of the free two-step nilpotent algebra on \(n\) generators is conjugate to a unique standard projection onto \(r\) generators, for some \(0\le r\le n\). Over \(\mathbb F_q\), its entire labeled rank spectrum is \(\sum_{r=0}^{n}{n\brack r}_q q^{r(n-r)(n+2r)/2}X^{\binom{r+1}{2}}\), valid in every characteristic.
