# Scientific review — retracts of the cube-zero truncated symmetric algebra

**Correctness — PASS.** The explicit decomposition \(K\oplus V\oplus\operatorname{Sym}^2V\), valid over every field, proves the normal form for every unital \(K\)-endomorphism. Squaring on generators produces exactly the two necessary-and-sufficient equations; splitting the idempotent \(P\) gives independent degree-two fibers of dimension \(d_{r,k}\). The image graph parametrization and complement count prove the claimed Gaussian formulas without interpolation or a characteristic restriction. The geometric bundle assertions follow from constant-rank fibers over smooth irreducible projection and Grassmannian varieties. The packaged `verify.py` independently checks the formulas by matrix constraints and, in small fields, directly enumerates all endomorphisms and embedded images.

**Originality — PASS, subject to the risks below.** Full primary-text comparisons with Gupta–Nagamine (arXiv:2301.12681), Chakraborty–Dasgupta–Dutta–Gupta (arXiv:1910.11023), and Chakraborty–Pal (arXiv:2504.14382) distinguish polynomial/Laurent *domain* retractions and restricted monomial/binomial families from the complete cube-zero Artinian quotient considered here. In particular, the general quotient-descent lemma in arXiv:1910.11023 does not establish that every endomorphism of the quotient arises from an enumerated polynomial retract, nor does it give these finite-field fibers or embedded-image counts. Claim/alias searches for nilpotent Artinian retractions, truncated symmetric algebras, idempotent endomorphism rank spectra, and finite-field Gaussian counts found no direct coverage. The closest related studies of short local algebras address other invariants. This is evidence of a distinct result, not a proof that no equivalent statement exists elsewhere.

**Value — PASS.** The complete natural family \(\operatorname{Sym}(V)/(V)^3\) is the universal two-step commutative local algebra with unrestricted quadratic multiplication. The theorem classifies *every* retraction and every retract image, rather than only the existence of coordinate projections, and gives exact multiplicities and moduli dimensions. The nontrivial distinction between the number of retractions and embedded images matters for counting split algebra maps and retract structures over finite fields.

**Closest literature and limitations.** The strongest nearby general-domain work is the 2023 Laurent-polynomial retract classification; the 2019 polynomial retraction article gives applicable definitions and quotient-descent principles but no classification for this Artinian quotient. The 2025 special-monomial paper is outside the source-date window but was checked as supporting comparison. Possible recodings through jet-algebra or formal coordinate-change methods remain a residual originality risk. Only total-degree-three truncations and unital scalar-preserving maps are covered. Finite computations are checks, not infinite proofs.
