For the full cube-zero truncation \(A_r(K)=K[x_1,\ldots,x_r]/(x_1,\ldots,x_r)^3\), every algebra retraction is classified by an idempotent linear map and constrained quadratic terms, and every embedded retract is itself a truncated symmetric algebra. Over \(\mathbf F_q\), both the retraction-map spectrum and the embedded-retract spectrum have exact Gaussian-binomial formulas in every characteristic.
