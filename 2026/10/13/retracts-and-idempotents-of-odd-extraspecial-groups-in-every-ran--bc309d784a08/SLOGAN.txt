The images of idempotent endomorphisms of an odd extraspecial group are exactly its full group and the elementary-abelian complements to the center above permitted isotropic subspaces. Their complete rank profiles differ between exponent \(p\) and exponent \(p^2\); already the rank-one idempotent counts differ by \(p^{4n-1}\) for groups of the same order \(p^{2n+1}\).
