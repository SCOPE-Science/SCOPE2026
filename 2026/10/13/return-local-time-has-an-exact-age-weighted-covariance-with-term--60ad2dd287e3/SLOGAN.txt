For any centered iid random walk with finite quadratic energy, a possible return at age \(k\) contributes exactly \(-k\mathbb E\|X_1\|^2\,\mathbb P(S_k=0)\) to the covariance between return local time and terminal squared displacement. For the simple symmetric walk this yields a closed central-binomial formula and the correlation limit \(-1/(3\sqrt{\pi-2})\).
