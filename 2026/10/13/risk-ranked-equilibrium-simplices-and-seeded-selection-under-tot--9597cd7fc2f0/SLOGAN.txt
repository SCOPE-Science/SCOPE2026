With infected travel completely blocked, a two-strain SIS patch system selects the smallest recovery-to-transmission ratio among **initially seeded** strain-patch cells: the disease clears if that ratio is at least \(N/k\), otherwise infection concentrates on the corresponding risk-tied equilibrium simplex. The complete equilibrium set consists of simplexes indexed by shared risk ratios, and each endemic equilibrium's unstable dimension is exactly the number of cells with lower risk.
