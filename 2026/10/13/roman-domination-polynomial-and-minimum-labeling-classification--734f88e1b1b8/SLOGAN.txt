Every coefficient of the Roman-domination polynomial of the triangular graph \(J(n,2)=L(K_n)\) follows from a no-isolated-vertices inclusion-exclusion formula. Minimum-weight labelings correspond exactly to perfect matchings after omitting one ground vertex for odd \(n\), or two for even \(n\), giving \(n(n-2)!!\) and \(\binom n2(n-3)!!\) such labelings, respectively.
