For the square book \(B_n=K_{1,n}\mathbin{\square}P_2\), conditioning on the two spine labels makes all pages independent except for two inclusion-exclusion conditions, yielding a closed Roman domination polynomial for every \(n\ge1\). The formula counts every Roman dominating function by weight and shows that the known scalar value \(\gamma_R(B_n)=4\) for \(n\ge2\) has a unique minimum function once \(n\ge3\).
