For \(W_{n,m}=K_1\vee nK_m\) with \(n,m\ge2\), all Roman dominating functions have the closed weight enumerator \(R(W_{n,m};x)=x^2(1+x+x^2)^{nm}+(1+x)((1+x+x^2)^m-(1+x)^m+x^m)^n-x^{nm}\). The formula comes from three center-label cases and gives a unique optimum of weight \(2\) together with the exact total count \(3^{nm}+2(3^m-2^m+1)^n-1\).
