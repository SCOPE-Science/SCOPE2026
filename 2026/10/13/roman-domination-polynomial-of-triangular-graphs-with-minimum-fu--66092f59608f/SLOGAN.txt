For every \(n\ge5\), the complete Roman-domination weight polynomial of the triangular graph \(T_n=L(K_n)\) is an explicit inclusion–exclusion sum over no-isolate edge supports. Its minimum weight is \(n-1\): optimal two-labeled edges form a perfect matching missing one original vertex when \(n\) is odd, or missing two vertices whose connecting edge is one-labeled when \(n\) is even; both multiplicities are determined exactly.
