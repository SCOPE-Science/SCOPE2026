For every proper component size \(2\le k<n\) in an \(n\)-point uniform circular geometric graph, the expected number of \(k\)-vertex components has an \((n-1)\)-st root that is concave in connection radius and a **unique** optimum. For fixed \(k\), the optimal radius is asymptotic to \(\log((k+1)/2)/n\), despite the exact expectation being a piecewise alternating sum inherited from classical spacing laws.
