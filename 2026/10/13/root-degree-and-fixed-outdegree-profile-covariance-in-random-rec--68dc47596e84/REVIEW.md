# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The one-step mixed-moment recurrence is reconstructed from the independent attachment rule, including the conditional probability that the root is chosen and the fact that the new vertex begins with outdegree zero. The explicit mean-profile expression and contraction estimate on \(|z|\leq\rho<1\) prove analytic convergence; Cauchy's formula then gives each fixed-class coefficient. The exact identity \(\sum_kY_{n,k}=n\) gives the complementary signs. `CHECK.py` independently compares the recurrences with exhaustive exact enumeration through nine vertices. Enumeration alone is not a proof of convergence.

## Originality — PASS with residual risk

The previously available root–leaf \(k=0\) formula is not claimed as novel. Janson's outdegree covariance matrix concerns two global degree counts. Addario-Berry and Eslava's high-degree laws and exchangeable relabeling concern unlabeled degree multisets, not the identified root's mixed degree/profile covariance. Accessible full text was checked for the latter source; Janson's full text was unavailable, leaving an explicit risk of an implicit stronger historical joint identity. The scoped originality is the positive-outdegree mixed limit and resulting noncommuting degree sum, not the elementary degree-profile expectation.

## Value — PASS

The result characterizes a specific interaction between a distinguished old vertex and degree diversity across the whole growing tree. The combination of positive fixed-degree limits and an exactly zero total covariance is mathematically structural: negative covariance mass must leave every fixed finite degree window. This information is not implied by asymptotic marginal degree frequencies alone.

## Scope and limitations

There is no uniform convergence through \(z=1\), no finite-size positivity claim for all positive degrees, and no localization of the compensating high-degree covariance. The established \(k=0\) result and literature priority uncertainties are acknowledged.
