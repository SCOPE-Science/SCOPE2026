In a uniform random recursive tree, the covariance between the root's number of children and the number of vertices of any fixed positive outdegree \(k\) converges to \(2^{-(k+1)}\). Yet the global degree-profile count is deterministic: compensating negative covariance necessarily escapes to ever-higher degree classes, and the covariance generating functions converge to \((2-z)^{-1}\) only inside \(|z|<1\).
