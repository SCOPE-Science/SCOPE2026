In a uniform random recursive tree, the covariance between root degree and the count of size-\(k\) fringe subtrees changes sign exactly once as \(k\) increases; the transition rank is asymptotic to \(\sqrt{2n}\). At \(k\sim c\sqrt n\), the scaled covariance converges to \(c^{-2}-\tfrac12\), revealing a sharp dependence crossover inside the established fringe-count Poisson regime.
