In the standard Barabási–Albert tree, root degree \(R_n\) and total root depth \(P_n\) have an exact negative covariance \(\operatorname{Cov}(R_n,P_n)=-\mathbb E R_n\,B_n\), where \(B_n\sim(1-\log2)n\). Equivalently, root-degree size bias lowers expected global depth by exactly \(B_n\), making the hub-versus-depth mechanism quantitatively explicit.
