# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The defining conditions for connected finite KD45 frames are reduced explicitly to a common nonempty reflexive successor core; this is the structural fact proved for KD45 frames in Sliusarev (2024, Theorem 3.17). The bounded-map condition is equivalent to surjectivity of the restriction from source core onto target core. Finite Boolean duality turns these exactly into modal-algebra homomorphisms. The ordinary and idempotent image polynomials follow by inclusion–exclusion and fixed-point enumeration. The eventual-image polynomial uses the periodic-point characterization of finite functional graphs and the exact rooted-forest determinant \(\det((n+r)I_n-J_n)=r(n+r)^{n-1}\). All zero-satellite and full-image cases are treated. The fresh verifier independently exhausts the bounded-map definition and selected modal-algebra preservation, including small parameter boundaries. Computation alone is not presented as a general proof.

## Originality — PASS, with an explicit antecedent risk

The primary paper establishes KD45 frame normal form and asymptotic modal validities, rather than counting homomorphisms or the three image spectra. Classical Cayley–Pitman enumerations already supply the forest factor and are excluded from the claimed novelty. The new claim is the simultaneous exact spectrum of one-step images, retract images and eventual images for the corresponding modal algebras, which is not an immediate published table found in the targeted searches. An unindexed antecedent in transformation-semigroup theory, including a general classification of maps preserving a distinguished invariant subset, remains possible. The recent source has been inspected in HTML full text at its relevant theorem and sections; the classical forest source has been inspected through its published abstract and author-hosted bibliographic record. This is not an independent originality certificate.

## Value — PASS

The three image notions are canonical finite endomorphism invariants and differ already in the five-world example. The rank laws give complete answers for every connected KD45 frame and permit exact comparison of retracts versus ordinary and eventual images. The eventual spectrum links modal-algebra dynamics with functional digraphs and Cayley rooted forests in a way not captured by the source paper's asymptotic-validity theorem. The result is a natural, non-arbitrary complete classification rather than a small case count; its semigroup ingredients are elementary and its claims deliberately limited accordingly.

## Source and scope caveats

The classification is for connected finite KD45 frames. The extension to disconnected frames is not claimed. No expert or external audit was conducted, and the originality risk above survives targeted database and literature searches.
