RotoGrad is exactly invariant to positive task-loss rescaling in its normalized backbone direction, but its shared step magnitude is multiplied by the weighted arithmetic mean \(A_p(s)=\sum_kp_ks_k\), while task-specific head and rotation update speeds are multiplied by their individual \(s_k\). Common scaling is fully removable by reciprocal rescaling of both displayed learning rates; unequal task scales are not generally reducible to one global learning-rate change.
