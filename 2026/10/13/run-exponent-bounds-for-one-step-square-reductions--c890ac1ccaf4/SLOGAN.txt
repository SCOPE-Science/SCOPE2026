Every one-step square-reduction output can be charged to a period label inside a standard run, giving \(\deg^+(W)\le\sum_R\lfloor e(R)/2\rfloor\). Current run-exponent bounds therefore imply \(\deg_k^+(n)\le\lfloor23n/20\rfloor\) in general and \(\deg_2^+(n)\le\lfloor11n/10\rfloor\) for binary words.
