# Verification

The proof depends on two source-backed ingredients: Berger--Block--Löwe's independent first-order buttons and switches, and the ordinary downward Löwenheim--Skolem theorem. The new cardinal argument is exact: for infinite \(\kappa\), an elementary submodel containing a \(\kappa\)-element subgroup has cardinality both at most and at least \(\kappa\), hence exactly \(\kappa\).

Run `python3 artifacts/verify.py`. The script checks the finite abstract frame generated by \(n\) monotone buttons and \(m\) freely resettable switches for \(0\le n,m\le3\). It verifies reflexivity, transitivity, directedness, all permitted target transitions, and the exact arrow count \(3^n4^m\). It prints a `VERIFY_OK` line with the exact assertion count.

The script does not prove downward Löwenheim--Skolem or the Berger--Block--Löwe group-theoretic construction; those are mathematical inputs inspected in the cited sources.
