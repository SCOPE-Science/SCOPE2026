For the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n\), \(n\ge3\), both solid and self location-domination have minimum size \(n\), but their complete code families differ sharply. The exact polynomials are \(z^n(z+2)^n-2nz^n+nz^{2n-2}\) for solid codes and \(2z^n(1+z)^n-z^{2n}+nz^{2n-2}\) for self-locating codes.
