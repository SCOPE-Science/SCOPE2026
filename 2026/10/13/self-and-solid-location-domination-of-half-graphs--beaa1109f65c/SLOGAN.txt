For the half graph \(H_n\), \(n\ge3\), self- and solid-location-domination both have optimum size \(2n-2\), but their optimum families are radically different. The self-locating optimum is uniquely \(V(H_n)\setminus\{a_1,b_n\}\), whereas solid location-domination has exactly \(n^2-2\) optimum codes, obtained by omitting any cross-part pair except the two leaf corners.
