For the crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n\), \(n\ge3\), every semitotal dominating set belongs to one of four elementary occupancy regimes, yielding the closed polynomial \(2z^n+((1+z)^n-1-nz)^2+2nz^2((1+z)^{n-1}-1)\). Its inclusion-minimal sets are exactly the two partite classes, matched-index triples, and disjoint-index \(2+2\) sets.
