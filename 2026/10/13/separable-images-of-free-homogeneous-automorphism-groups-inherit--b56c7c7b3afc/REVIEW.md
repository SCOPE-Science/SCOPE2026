# Review

## Correctness
The proof has four steps, each independently standard once the cited hypotheses are available. Ample generics gives automatic continuity into separable targets by Kechris--Rosendal. Continuity and density give \(\overline{\varphi(G)}=\overline{\varphi(L)}\). Homomorphic images of locally finite groups are locally finite. If the target is torsion-free, the locally finite image \(\varphi(L)\) is trivial, and Hausdorffness then forces the whole image to be trivial. Siniora--Solecki explicitly supply both hypotheses for automorphism groups of free homogeneous structures in finite relational languages.

## Originality
Targeted searches under the aliases "separable image," "torsion-free quotient," "dense locally finite subgroup," and "ample generics" did not locate this image-closure theorem or its torsion-free corollary. The two main source papers state the ingredients separately. Sági's 2024 work characterizes the dense-locally-finite hypothesis but does not state the image theorem. The main originality risk is that the deduction is short enough to be folklore.

Macpherson--Tent's simplicity theorem is stronger for transitive nonsymmetric free homogeneous structures, but it is narrower: transitivity is essential and they explicitly give a nontransitive free homogeneous counterexample to simplicity. Thus their theorem does not subsume the uniform all-free-homogeneous application or the general Polish-group statement.

## Value
The statement converts a descriptive-topological property (ample generics) and a finite-approximation property (a dense locally finite subgroup) into a restriction on arbitrary abstract homomorphisms. It shows that separable images cannot lose the finite approximation phenomenon, and yields an immediate vanishing theorem for all separable torsion-free targets, including every countable torsion-free discrete group.

## Closest literature and limitations
Siniora--Solecki are the closest source because they prove both hypotheses for the intended model-theoretic class. Kechris--Rosendal provide the automatic-continuity bridge. Macpherson--Tent give stronger simplicity in a proper subclass, while Sági broadens the study of dense locally finite subgroups. The claim remains a short corollary rather than a new deep structural theorem, and nonseparable targets remain outside its scope.
