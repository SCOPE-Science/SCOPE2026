The proposed odd-even equality of single-cell flat-band spectra fails first at seven versus six vertices: two explicit connected seven-cell periodic graphs realize the five real roots of \(\lambda^2+\lambda-4\) and \(\lambda^3+\lambda^2-4\lambda-2\). An exhaustive exact check of all \(156\) simple six-vertex graph spectra proves that none of those five energies can arise with a six-vertex cell.
