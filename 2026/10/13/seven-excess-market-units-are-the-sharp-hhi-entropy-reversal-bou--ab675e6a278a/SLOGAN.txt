For any fixed \(k\ge3\) firms with positive integer market sizes, HHI and entropy cannot rank two markets in opposite strict concentration orders until total size reaches \(k+7\). The unique first-layer reversal is \((1,\ldots,1,4,5)\) versus \((1,\ldots,1,2,2,6)\): HHI calls the latter more concentrated, while entropy calls the former more concentrated.
