Under \(\operatorname{PD}(\alpha,\theta)\), Shannon diversity \(H\) and Gini–Simpson diversity \(D\) satisfy \(\operatorname{Cov}(D,H)=(\theta+\alpha)/((\theta+1)^2(\theta+2))\), so their prior fluctuations are always positively aligned. For fixed \(\alpha\), their correlation remains nonzero as \(\theta\to\infty\), with an explicit trigamma limit.
