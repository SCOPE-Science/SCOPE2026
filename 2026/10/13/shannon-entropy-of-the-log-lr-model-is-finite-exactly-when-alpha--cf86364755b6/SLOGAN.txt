For the Log-LR model, the hidden variable \(T=-\log F_R(X)\) is exactly Lomax\((\alpha,\beta)\), and the log-Jacobian behaves like \(-T/2\) in the tail. Therefore the Shannon differential entropy is finite exactly for \(\alpha>1\); for \(0<\alpha\le1\) it equals \(-\infty\), so the published numerical-expectation treatment misses a genuine existence boundary.
