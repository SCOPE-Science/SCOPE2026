For symmetric Dirichlet vectors, Shannon entropy and Simpson concentration have the exact negative covariance \(-a(m-1)/[(ma+1)^2(ma+2)]\). As the dimension grows at fixed shape, their limiting correlation decreases strictly from \(-[2(\pi^2/6-1)]^{-1/2}\) to \(-1\); taking the sparse-shape limit first instead yields the distinct constant \(-\sqrt3/[2\sqrt{2\zeta(3)-\pi^2/6}]\).
