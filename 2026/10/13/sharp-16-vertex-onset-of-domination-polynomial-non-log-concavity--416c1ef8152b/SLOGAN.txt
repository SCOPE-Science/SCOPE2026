Domination-polynomial log-concavity holds for every trivially perfect graph on at most \(15\) vertices; at order \(16\) exactly one distinct polynomial fails, realized by \(K_1\vee(3K_5)\). All these graphs through order \(16\) still have unimodal domination polynomials, while the three-blade windmill family loses log-concavity exactly at blade size \(5\).
