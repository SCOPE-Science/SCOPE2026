For \(H(n)=\sigma_2(n)+\varphi(n)\sigma(n)-2n^2\), every integer with repeated mixed prime support satisfies \(H(n)\ge34\), uniquely sharply at \(n=12\); integers with at least three distinct prime factors are already above \(50\). Together with the known prime and squarefree-semiprime fibers, this yields the complete spectrum below \(34\): \(0,2,3,7,17,21\).
