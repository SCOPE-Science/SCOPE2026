# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The commutation-completion resource is defined by the independently established binary mixed-rank identity of Riera--Parker--Stănică, Lemma 4.1. For each admissible connected two-shift ring, multiplication of the cyclic symbol by a unit factors it as \((1+x^{b-a})(1+x^{a+b})\) in \(\mathbb F_2[x]\). Separability of the odd part of \(x^n-1\), root multiplicity truncation by its power of two, and \(\gcd(m,a,b)=1\) prove formula (1). The exhaustive parity and divisor cases bound binary nullity and the connected shifts \(1,n/2-1\) meet the bound at every even order. An independent exact bit-matrix eliminator and cyclic formula agree for 21,152 step pairs, without being used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with documented risk
The 2021 source proves the ancilla/rank reduction but no sharp cyclic two-shift optimization. Damnjanović's Theorem 14 is the strongest close comparison: it already settles maximum **ordinary real/complex nullity** of connected quartic circulants and identifies an overlapping extremal step family. Its field and invariant differ decisively. At \(n=12\), the same fixed zero-one graph has real nullity \(6\) and binary nullity \(8\), so its published real bound cannot imply the claimed binary optimum. Williams computes integral Smith forms for selected circulant families; Deaett--Meyer minimizes ranks of variable-entry Hermitian circulants. Targeted source and research-record searches found no equivalent fixed-binary-resource theorem; poorly indexed equivalents remain a risk. The previously known extremal family and mixed-rank formula are expressly credited.

## Value — PASS
The resource objective is forced by an established graph-state commutation-completion problem, not an arbitrarily selected statistic. The theorem optimizes it over a natural connected, translation-invariant fixed-degree architecture at every even size; it gives the sharp scaling and a realizable family. The characteristic-two jump from classical nullity at orders such as \(10\) and \(12\) is mathematically substantive. The proof also supplies a computable all-parameter cyclic-root formula. It does not optimize general mixed graphs or quantum gates.
