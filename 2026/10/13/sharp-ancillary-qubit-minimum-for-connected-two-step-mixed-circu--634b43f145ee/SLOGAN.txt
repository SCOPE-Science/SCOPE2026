For connected two-shift directed circulant rings with an even number \(n\ge6\) of vertices and four distinct undirected neighbors, the least ancillary-qubit commutation-completion cost is exactly \(\lfloor(n-2)/4\rfloor\), attained by shifts \(1\) and \(n/2-1\). Characteristic-two cyclic-factor multiplicities explain why this optimum differs from known real-spectral nullity bounds.
