On folded hypercubes in every dimension \(d\ge4\), unit-priced coordinate links and \(t\)-priced antipodal links have a sharp spectral-gap design transition exactly at \(t=d\), with every global optimizer explicitly classified and edgewise symmetry proved. For fixed \(t>0\) in high dimension, antipodal links consume vanishing budget fraction yet asymptotically double the optimal spectral gap.
