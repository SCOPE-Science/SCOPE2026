In a two-tier perfectly nested ecological network, exact optimization of Barber modularity produces one uniquely optimal specialist–core versus generalist–periphery split and the sharp value \(2abpq/((p+q)a+qb)^2\). As the number of specialist species increases, perfectly nested networks can have optimized modularity arbitrarily close to \(1/2\), so neighborhood nestedness alone does not force weak modularity.
