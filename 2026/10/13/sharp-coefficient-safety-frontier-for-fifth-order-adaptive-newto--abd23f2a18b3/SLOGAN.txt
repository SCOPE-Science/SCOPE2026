The fifth-degree adaptive Newton–Schulz polar iteration converges for every coefficient sequence in \([3/8,u]\) precisely for the robust subcritical range \(u<\alpha_\star=1.472229260972\ldots\), where \(\alpha_\star\) is the largest real root of \(128\alpha^3+496\alpha^2-1026\alpha+27\); the boundary is witnessed by scalar non-unit fixed points and supercritical divergence. In particular the empirically proposed PRISM interval \([3/8,29/20]\) enjoys unconditional pathwise convergence in exact arithmetic for full-column-rank inputs with singular values at most one.
