For the exact unconstrained simplex-ridge multi-gradient step, every objective has the sharp derivative margin \(\langle g_i,v\rangle\ge\|v\|^2-\rho(m-1)/(4m)\), so \(\|v\|^2>\rho(m-1)/(4m)\) guarantees common descent. Two-dimensional examples attain equality for every number of objectives, and a strongly convex biobjective example shows that a regularized step can increase an objective even when a common descent direction exists.
