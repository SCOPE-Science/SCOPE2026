Kang's parity-twisted crank detector is quantitatively separated from zero on composites: \(|h(n)|\ge120\), with equality only at \(n=4\). The positive branches are sharper, with \(h(n)\ge6144\) for odd composites and \(h(n)\ge1488\) for composites \(n\equiv2\pmod4\), attained only at \(9\) and \(6\), respectively.
