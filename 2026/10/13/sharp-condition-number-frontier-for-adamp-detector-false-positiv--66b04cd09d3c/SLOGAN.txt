AdamP's cosine detector has a sharp curvature-induced false-positive frontier on SPD quadratics: it is guaranteed inactive exactly when \(\kappa\le((\sqrt d+\sqrt{d-\delta^2})/\delta)^2\). With the source default \(\delta=0.1\), this threshold is asymptotically \(400d-2\), so sufficiently ill-conditioned but fully scale-variant convex quadratics can satisfy the detector's near-orthogonality test.
