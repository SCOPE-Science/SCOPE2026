CGS has a sharp first-step residual-spike frontier even on SPD systems: the Euclidean residual is universally nonincreasing exactly through \(\kappa=3.442815869257\ldots\). Above that point a two-eigenvalue diagonal system is already enough to make the first CGS residual grow, and the sharp worst-case growth is asymptotic to \((3\sqrt3/16)\kappa^{3/2}\).
