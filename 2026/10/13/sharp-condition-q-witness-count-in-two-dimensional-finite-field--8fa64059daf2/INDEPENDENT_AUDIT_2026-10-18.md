# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For odd q and A in M2(Fq), count U in GL2(q) such that A-U and A-U^{-1} are invertible. For invertible nonscalar A, |GL2|=q^4-q^3-q^2+q, each single bad set has cardinality q^3-2q, so w=q^4-3q^3-q^2+5q+I(A). Normalizing A to companion form, and solving both singularity constraints with determinant delta and trace x, reduces intersection I(A) to quadratic-character sums. For nonzero trace and irreducible discriminant, I=q^2-1, giving min q^4-3q^3+5q-1. Remaining discriminant, trace, rank-one, zero and scalar similarity types have larger explicit counts. Independent exhaustive F3 and F5 over all matrices/all units gives minima 14 (12 attaining) and 274 (160 attaining), but the unrestricted finite-field theorem follows from character-sum identities.

## originality

PASS

Wang–Zhou original and 2012 correction prove the qualitative existence condition (Q) for matrix rings over division rings with more than two elements. Li–Wang–Zhou's stronger two-element Goodearl–Menal condition remains existential. Karabulut's one-difference unit-graph parameters are not the inverse-coupled intersection w(A), while Călugăreanu gives qualitative per-matrix unit stable range results. Inspected primary condition-(Q) paper and compared quantifiers and numerical information. Neither qualitative existence nor single adjacency count implies the exact worst-case polynomial or irreducible nonzero-trace equality locus. A hidden equivalent Cayley-graph intersection count remains a risk.
