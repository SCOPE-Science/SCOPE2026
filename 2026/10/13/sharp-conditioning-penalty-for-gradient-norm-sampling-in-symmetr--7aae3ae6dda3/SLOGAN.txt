For a full-rank least-squares problem, the squared-gradient sampling rule of randomized symmetric Gauss–Seidel can yield less one-step progress than uniform sampling of the same reflected coordinate blocks. Its exact sharp progress ratio is bounded below by \(4\sqrt\kappa/(1+\sqrt\kappa)^2\), where \(\kappa\) is the spectral condition number of \(A^{\mathsf T}A\); a four-column family attains equality and the ratio can tend to zero.
