For one diagonal-weighted Cholesky pivot, the scalar trace loss of every real positive-definite matrix lies in a sharp convex-order interval determined by its eigenvalues; rotating the eigenvectors can make this loss completely deterministic, while diagonalizing the matrix gives its maximal variability. The exact attainable variance range is \(0\le\operatorname{Var}(T_K)\le M_3/S-(M_2/S)^2\), despite a rotation-invariant mean.
