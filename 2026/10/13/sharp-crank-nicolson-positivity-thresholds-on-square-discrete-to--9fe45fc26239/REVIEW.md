Its diagonal equals \(2G_n(s)-1\), with \(G_n\) an explicitly given finite Fourier average. Since \(G_n\) strictly decreases from \(1\) to \(1/n^2<1/2\), the threshold exists uniquely. Periodization of the infinite-lattice Green kernel proves strict monotonicity of \(G_n\) in grid size, hence strict threshold ordering; the Bessel function coordinate monotonicity has an independent cooperative-ODE proof. The square-lattice Fourier integral evaluates to the specified elliptic expression. A geometric-walk resolvent expansion and a uniform derivative lower bound establish the quantitative exponential estimate. Exact rational checks of \(3\times3\) and \(4\times4\) matrices and numerical checks of additional sizes agree with the proof but do not replace its infinite statements. Limitation: positivity of one discrete time step, not higher-dimensional, Dirichlet, nonlinear or rounded arithmetic behavior.
