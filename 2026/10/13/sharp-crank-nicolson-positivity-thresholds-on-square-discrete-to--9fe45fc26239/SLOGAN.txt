For isotropic Crank–Nicolson diffusion on the square torus \(C_n\square C_n\), the **sharp positivity timestep** strictly decreases with \(n\), from \((1+\sqrt{57})/14\) at \(n=3\) to an elliptic-integral constant \(0.588197283685628\ldots\). Each finite threshold has an exact Fourier characterization, and its excess over the limiting threshold decays exponentially with grid size.
