For fair coupon collection by random projective lines, the Fano plane is stochastically slower than uniform sampling of all triples at every draw horizon, while the order-three projective plane has exactly one reversed horizon: it finishes more often by draw \(4\), but less often by every draw \(t\ge5\). Explicit avoidance spectra and a positive grouping of exponential terms prove the complete infinite-horizon comparison.
