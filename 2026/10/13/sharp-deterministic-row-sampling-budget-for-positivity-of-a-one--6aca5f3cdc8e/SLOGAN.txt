Even with optimal basis-only selection of \(s\) coordinates, a one-vector sketched Ritz value of an SPD matrix with eigenvalues in \([\mu,L]\) can deviate from the midpoint by exactly \(\frac{L-\mu}{2}\sqrt{n/s}\). Strict positivity for every input is guaranteed exactly when \(s/n>((\kappa-1)/(\kappa+1))^2\), a sharp sampling-budget boundary rather than a heuristic oversampling guideline.
