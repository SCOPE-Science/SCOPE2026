For every \(d\)-level input and every maximally incoherent channel to a qubit, the Hilbert–Schmidt off-diagonal coherence can grow by at most \(\sqrt{\lfloor d/2\rfloor}\), and this factor is attained on full-rank states by strictly incoherent channels. The exact odd/even dimension law sharpens previously known nonmonotonicity examples by giving their global worst-case envelope.
