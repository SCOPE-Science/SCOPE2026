For the two-block odd partition function \(a(n)\), every dyadic layer \(n=2^k m\) with \(k\ge2\) has a sharp floor \(2^{k-1}-1\) at \(m=1\), followed by the sharp floor \(2^{k+1}-3\) at \(m=3\). This yields the complete coefficient fibers \(a(n)=0,1,2,3,4\), with the zero fiber agreeing with the previously known positivity boundary.
