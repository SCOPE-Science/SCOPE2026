`VERIFY_OK labeled_trees=18248 all_p_Bernstein_certificates=YES eight_vertex_profiles=8 exact_outbreak_enumerations=32 spider_endpoint_identity=YES`

The displayed algebraic Bernstein identity in RESULT.md proves that this suffices for every real \(p\in[0,1]\). The table for the eight-vertex witness is checked term-for-term. For each of its eight vaccinees and four rational probabilities, an unrelated bond-configuration and seed enumeration recomputes the mean outbreak size using exact rational arithmetic. These 32 enumerations are corroboration of the formula, not the proof of an infinite proposition.

The program checks the endpoint binomial identity for 45 spider lengths; its general validity is proved by the symbolic binomial calculation in RESULT.md. The switching equation has a unique root on \((0,1)\) by strict monotonicity, and the result never assumes a numerical approximation is a proof.
