For the two-coordinate strictly convex quadratic, fading-memory Coherent Coordinate Descent is geometrically stable from every initialization exactly for \(0<\alpha<4/[1+\gamma+\sqrt{(1+\gamma)^2-8\gamma(1-\gamma)\rho^2}]\). Partial buffer retention can even permit stable learning rates above \(2\) at strong coupling, whereas ordinary memory-free coordinate descent cannot.
