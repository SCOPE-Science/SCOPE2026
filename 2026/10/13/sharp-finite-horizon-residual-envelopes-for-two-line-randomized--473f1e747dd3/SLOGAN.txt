For two independently sampled unit Kaczmarz rows in the plane, the sharp worst-case last-iterate mean-row residual at every finite horizon has a unique coherence optimizer described by a cubic equation. The optimizer approaches parallel rows at speed \(1/t\), giving the exact asymptotic envelope \(1/(\mathrm e t)\), while initial expected residual increases are possible precisely above coherence \(\sqrt2-1\).
