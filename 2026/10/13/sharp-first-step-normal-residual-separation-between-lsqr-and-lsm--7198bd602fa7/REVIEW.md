# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The first LSQR and LSMR subspaces are the same one-dimensional space generated by \(A^{\mathsf T}b\). Direct minimization gives the two scalar steps. Expanding both resulting normal-residual norms exposes the same moment determinant, so their ratio simplifies exactly to
\[
\frac{\sqrt{s_0s_2}}{s_1}.
\]
A sharp endpoint spectral-moment inequality for \(A^{\mathsf T}A\) gives
\[
\frac{\kappa^2+1}{2\kappa},
\]
and the diagonal two-mode family attains equality. The packaged checker replays the exact formulas.

## Originality
PASS with explicit residual risks. Classical LSQR primal-residual minimization, LSMR normal-residual minimization, the CG/MINRES normal-equation equivalences, and LSMR's qualitative early-termination advantage are all treated as prior work.

The inspected full LSQR and LSMR sources do not state the exact first-step cross-method normal-residual identity or its sharp singular-condition-number envelope. Focused literature searches under LSQR/LSMR, first-step, normal-residual, CG/MINRES, and Kantorovich formulations found no implication-equivalent theorem.

A later LSMB paper directly compares LSQR and LSMR iterates and is therefore a genuine residual risk; its accessible abstract does not display the new factor, but the full body was unavailable during the bounded inspection.

## Value
PASS. The distinction between minimizing \(\|r\|_2\) and minimizing \(\|A^{\mathsf T}r\|_2\) matters directly for early stopping in least-squares solvers. The theorem turns that qualitative distinction into a sharp condition-number law and an exact equality example. At \(\kappa=3\), the two methods move the same initial normal residual in opposite directions: LSQR enlarges it by \(4/3\) while LSMR contracts it by \(4/5\).

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest full source is the original LSMR paper, which explicitly contrasts the two residual criteria but does not give the sharp ratio. The original LSQR paper supplies the primal-residual minimization derivation. The later LSMB paper is the main unresolved broader-literature risk. The theorem is restricted to the first exact-arithmetic step, full column rank, zero start, and no damping or preconditioning.
