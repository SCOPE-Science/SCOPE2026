On the first common Krylov line, LSQR's and LSMR's normal residuals differ by an exact spectral-moment factor, whose sharp worst case is \((\kappa^2+1)/(2\kappa)\). In the two-dimensional equality family, LSMR still contracts the normal residual while LSQR already amplifies it once \(\kappa>1+\sqrt2\).
