For a centered Gaussian triple with fixed correlations \(a=\operatorname{Corr}(X_1,X_2)\) and \(b=\operatorname{Corr}(X_1,X_3)\), the mixed-sign moment \(\mathbb E[|X_1|^{-\alpha}X_2^2X_3^2]\) has an exact closed lower envelope over the remaining correlation. The optimizer switches sharply from \(c_*=\alpha ab\) to the singular covariance boundary when \((1-\alpha)|ab|\) crosses \(\sqrt{(1-a^2)(1-b^2)}\), and the envelope strictly improves the earlier product-of-residual-variances factor except at \((a,b)=(0,0)\).
