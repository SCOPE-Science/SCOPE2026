For the scalar Ornstein–Uhlenbeck stochastic theta method with independent positive randomized steps, the stationary law is Gaussian exactly for deterministic steps or \(\theta=1/2\), and **each** positive-order moment exists exactly when \(\mathbb E|R(H)|^p<1\). Two explicit step distributions with identical \(\mathbb EH\), \(\mathbb EH^2\), and stationary variance \(4/3\) have radically different stationary moments: the first has all moments finite, while the second has finite absolute moments exactly for \(0<p<\kappa\) with \(3<\kappa<4\).
