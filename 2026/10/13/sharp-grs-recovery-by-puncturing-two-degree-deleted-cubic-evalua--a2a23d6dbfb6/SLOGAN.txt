For the two MDS degree-deleted cubic evaluation families \(\langle1,x,x^3\rangle\) and \(\langle1,x^2,x^3\rangle\), a six-coordinate puncturing becomes generalized Reed--Solomon exactly when its evaluation values satisfy \(e_1=0\) or \(e_5=0\), respectively; five coordinates always suffice and seven never do. Consequently the sharp GRS-recovery deletion count at length \(n\ge7\) is \(n-6\) or \(n-5\), and the exceptional six-subsets form a five-packing.
