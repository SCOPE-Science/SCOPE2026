For the published eight-compartment Mpox model, a supercritical rodent reservoir forces the human endemic infectious fraction to occupy exactly \([p z/(1+kz),p/k)\) as human-to-human contact varies over nonnegative rates; the lower endpoint is attained even when human-to-human spread is fully suppressed. The exact inverse law \(\beta_2=1/(p-kJ)-z/J\) both quantifies this irreducible zoonotic prevalence floor and calibrates every mathematically attainable target prevalence \(J\) within that interval.
