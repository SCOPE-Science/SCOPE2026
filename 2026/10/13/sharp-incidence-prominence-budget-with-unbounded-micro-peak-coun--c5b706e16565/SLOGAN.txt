In closed SIR epidemics with decreasing transmission and nonnegative susceptible–infectious incidence growth, total logarithmic peak-to-trough prominence cannot exceed the logarithmic drop in transmission, and the coefficient one is sharp. Nevertheless, analytic strictly decreasing transmission with arbitrarily small total change and a fixed bound on its logarithmic slope can produce any prescribed number of strict incidence peaks while infectious prevalence rises throughout; the extra peaks must collectively become shallow.
