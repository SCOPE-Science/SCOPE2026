The log-Pareto severity distribution is inverted concave exactly for \(0<\alpha\le2.906557937512901\ldots\), sharpening the previously established sufficient range \(\alpha\le2\). This is exactly the parameter interval on which independent compound-Poisson losses of every positive common intensity obey universal weight-majorization stochastic dominance; beyond it, two curvature switches produce explicit low-intensity counterexamples.
