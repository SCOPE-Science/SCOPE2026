For direct Crank–Nicolson integration of a stable two-mode covariance Lyapunov equation, an exact isotropic-noise floor restores positive semidefiniteness for every initial covariance of fixed trace: \(h\nu/\tau\ge E/(r+\ell+2\sqrt{k})\). The threshold is attained by a rank-one covariance and decays quadratically in step size, \(\nu_*(h)\sim\tau h^2(a-b)^2(a+b)/8\), whereas with zero noise a covariance can become indefinite after arbitrarily small steps.
