The binary Concrete density is log-concave exactly when \(\tau\ge(\sqrt\alpha+\alpha^{-1/2})/2=1/(2\sqrt{p(1-p)})\), where \(p=\alpha/(1+\alpha)\). Thus temperature above one does not guarantee log-concavity for an imbalanced Bernoulli relaxation: at \(\tau=2,\alpha=100\), midpoint logarithmic curvature is positive.
