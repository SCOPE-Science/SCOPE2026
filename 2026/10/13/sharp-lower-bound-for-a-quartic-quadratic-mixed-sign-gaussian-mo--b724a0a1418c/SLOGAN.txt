For a standardized Gaussian triple, the mixed-sign moment \(\mathbb E[|X|^{-u}Y^4Z^2]\) has the sharp universal lower bound \((1-u)(3-u)\mathbb E|X|^{-u}\) for every \(0<u<1\). Equality occurs exactly when \(Y=\pm X\) and \(Z\) is independent of \(X\), yielding a nonvanishing bound where earlier covariance-factor lower bounds can vanish.
