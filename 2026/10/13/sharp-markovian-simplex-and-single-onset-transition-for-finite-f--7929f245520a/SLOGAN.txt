For mixtures of all finite-field Weyl pinching semigroups with a common rate in prime-power dimension \(q\ge3\), CP-divisibility is characterized by the single sharp inequality \(\sum_j(1-x_j)/x_j\ge q\max_j((1-x_j)/x_j)\) when all weights are positive. Violation causes exactly one delayed transition to CP-indivisibility, while nontrivial lower-dimensional faces are CP-indivisible from every positive time.
