For exact-line-search steepest descent on an \(n\)-dimensional SPD quadratic, the sharp one-step max-norm gradient factor is \(\sqrt{n-1}(\kappa-1)/(2\sqrt\kappa)\). Hence coordinatewise gradient monotonicity is universal exactly through \(\kappa=(\sqrt n+1)/(\sqrt n-1)\), even though the corresponding Euclidean gradient can still decrease beyond that dimension-dependent frontier.
