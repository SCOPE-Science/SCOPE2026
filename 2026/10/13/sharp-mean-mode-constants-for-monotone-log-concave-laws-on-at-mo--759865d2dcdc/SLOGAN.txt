For monotone log-concave integer laws, the general \(e^{-1}\) mean-versus-mode guarantee improves sharply on supports of at most five atoms: the exact four- and five-atom constants are \(r_4=0.6572981061\ldots\) and \(r_5=0.5677373609\ldots\). Equality is attained only by the corresponding truncated geometric mass vectors, up to translation and reflection.
