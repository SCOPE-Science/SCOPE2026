For every finite group \(G\), matrix size \(N\ge2\), and \(k\ge2\), the matrix-valued \(U^k\) space has exact modulus of convexity \(\delta(\varepsilon)=1-(1-(\varepsilon/2)^{2^k})^{1/2^k}\). Constant diagonal matrix functions attain the Hanner lower bound, so the group and cube geometry cannot improve the global modulus.
