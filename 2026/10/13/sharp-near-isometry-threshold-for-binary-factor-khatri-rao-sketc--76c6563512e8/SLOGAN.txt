For a Khatri–Rao sketch made from independent two-sign Rademacher factors, full-space \(\varepsilon\)-near-isometry has a sharp sample threshold \(m\sim c_*(\varepsilon)N\log N\), where \(c_*(\varepsilon)=1/((1+\varepsilon)\log(1+\varepsilon)-\varepsilon)\) and \(N=2^d\). Its complete finite-dimensional covariance spectrum is exactly a normalized multinomial occupancy spectrum, exhibiting the exact distortion-dependent multiplicative cost beyond mere full rank.
