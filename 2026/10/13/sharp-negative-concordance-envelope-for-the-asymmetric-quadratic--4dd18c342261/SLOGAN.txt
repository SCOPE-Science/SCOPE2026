For the genuine continuous copulas in the differentiable asymmetric scheme \(C_\phi(u,v)=uv[u+(1-u)\phi(v)]\), Spearman rho fills exactly \( (-(1-e^{-2})/2,0]\), and Kendall tau is always \(2\rho_S/3\). The sharp unattained negative boundary follows from a logarithmic obstacle on admissible tuning functions and is approached by an explicit smooth CDF family; the rho/tau ratio itself was already known for broader quadratic-section copulas.
