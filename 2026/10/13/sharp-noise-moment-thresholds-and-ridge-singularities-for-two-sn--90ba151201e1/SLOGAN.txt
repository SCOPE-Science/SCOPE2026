For two fixed distinct snapshots of a scalar contraction observed with independent Gaussian errors, unsafeguarded depth-one Anderson acceleration has a finite \(q\)-th output moment exactly when \(q<d\): in particular, its mean-square error is infinite in dimensions one and two. A scalar ridge penalty restores all moments, but the mean square diverges as \(\varepsilon^{-1/2}\) in one dimension and as \(\log(1/\varepsilon)\) in two, with explicit leading constants.
