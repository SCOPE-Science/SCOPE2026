A Type-1 inexact proximal trust-region Cauchy step retains a sharp universal decrease coefficient \(1-\kappa\) whenever its error is at most \(\kappa\) times the computed step, and this is possible uniformly exactly for \(\kappa<1\). The same observable test enlarges the sufficient-accuracy interval for limiting stationarity from the source method's \(\kappa<1/2\) to \(\kappa<1\) under its original outer assumptions, without checking an unknown exact proximal step.
