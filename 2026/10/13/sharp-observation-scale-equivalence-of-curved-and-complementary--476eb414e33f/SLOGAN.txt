For a finite higher-order public-goods population, the curved triadic payoff and the all-cooperators-only payoff produce Fermi-mutation trajectory laws within \(O(T(2/3)^\gamma)\) after \(T\) updates, uniformly over hypergraph topology. Positive mutation yields a quadratic relative-entropy bound and the sharp exponential sample-scale \((3/2)^{2\gamma}\) in a resettable four-vertex experiment, while a separate explicit stationary-law bound holds for every finite hypergraph.
