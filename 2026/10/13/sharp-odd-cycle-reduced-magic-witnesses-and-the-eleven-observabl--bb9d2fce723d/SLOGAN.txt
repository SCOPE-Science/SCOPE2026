For every Pauli measurement set with odd-cycle anticommutation graph \(C_{2k+1}\), the reduced robustness of magic is exactly the largest adjacent-pair expectation sum, the total absolute-expectation sum divided by \(k\), or one. The sharp capacity is \(\max\{\sqrt2,\,k^{-1}\cot(\pi/(4k+2))\}\): the odd-hole witness dominates only for \(C_5,C_7,C_9\), with a strict switch to the pair witness at \(C_{11}\).
