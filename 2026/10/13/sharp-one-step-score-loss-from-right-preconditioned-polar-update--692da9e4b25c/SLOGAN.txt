For a quadratic with isotropic output curvature, exact-line-search Newton–Muon-style right-preconditioned polar descent has at most the one-step decrease of unpreconditioned polar descent, but always at least \(4\kappa/(\kappa+1)^2\) of it when the input curvature has condition number \(\kappa\). This factor is optimal already in the limit of invertible \(2\times2\) examples, and equality at the upper bound occurs exactly for commuting curvature and gradient modulus.
