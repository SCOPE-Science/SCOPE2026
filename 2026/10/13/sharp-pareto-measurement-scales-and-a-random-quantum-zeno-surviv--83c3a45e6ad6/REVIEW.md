Their generic formulas supply important ingredients but do not state the Poisson infinite-product limit, critical square-root-logarithmic scale, exact two moments and sharp trichotomy together. Those precise implications were compared with the full paper's model, results, conclusion and appendices. Related generic rare-event and point-process limit theorems might give a broader route to the result; other equivalent specialist work remains a priority risk. No independent literature certification is represented.

## Value — PASS

Observation-time variability is a genuine limitation of the quantum-Zeno protection mechanism. An exact answer for the natural boundary at divergence of the waiting-time second moment determines when survival is reproducible across timing samples versus non-self-averaging, and how the experimental interval scale must change at the critical exponent. The explicit Poisson law, strictly positive fluctuations, moments and sharp below/above scale alternatives offer concrete mathematical information not provided by a generic first-order decay approximation. This value is limited to the declared independent Pareto and fixed-count protocol, with no claims about fixed laboratory duration.
