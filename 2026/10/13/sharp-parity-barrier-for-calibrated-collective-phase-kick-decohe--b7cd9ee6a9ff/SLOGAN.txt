At a fixed Poisson phase-kick rate and fixed local decay ratio \(g=\Gamma_1/\Gamma\), even-sized GHZ coherences can be protected perfectly for every calibration, whereas odd-sized GHZ coherences cease to be perfectly protectable above \(1+\cos(\pi/n)\) in units of the event rate. Above that threshold, their exact best decay rate is \(\Gamma[1-T_n(1-g)]\), with a sharp quadratic onset.
