For any single optical mode limited to photon numbers \(0,\ldots,K\) and initially nonnegative in Wigner value at the origin, pure attenuation has a sharp negative origin-value floor \(-\frac{2m-1}{2m\pi}(2m)^{-1/(2m-1)}\), where \(m=\lfloor K/2\rfloor\). Equality requires equal populations on levels \(1\) and \(2m\) at one uniquely determined transmissivity; the exact bound stays constant across each adjacent even–odd cutoff pair and tends to \(-1/\pi\).
