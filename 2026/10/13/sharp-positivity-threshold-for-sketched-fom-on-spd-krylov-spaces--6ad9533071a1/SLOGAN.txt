For SPD systems, ordinary sketched FOM preserves positive-real Ritz values under an embedding distortion \(\varepsilon\) exactly below the sharp universal threshold \(2\sqrt\kappa/(1+\kappa)\), where \(\kappa\) is the spectral condition number. A three-dimensional SPD example with a two-row sketch attains a zero Ritz value at the boundary and a negative one above it, so embedding accuracy alone can fail to preserve the analytic domain needed for reciprocal matrix functions.
