In the delayed eco-epidemic system, the predator-free endemic state has a sharp **delay-independent** invasion threshold \(p=h\): above it, a positive real unstable root persists through every later Hopf crossing, yet any small mathematical Hopf offspring must have negative predator density and is biologically inadmissible. An explicit harvest bound \(h>e_1mK/[b(a+K)]+e_2n\) guarantees exponential predator extinction for all finite gestation delays and all continuous histories with strictly positive susceptible and infected prey and nonnegative predators.
