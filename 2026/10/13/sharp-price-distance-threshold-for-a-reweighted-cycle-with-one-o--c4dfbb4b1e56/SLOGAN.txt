On a cycle whose ring conductances can be fully reallocated, the unique Kirchhoff-index optimizer purchases a distance-\(d\) chord exactly when its relative unit price is below \(\frac{d(n-d)[d(n-d)+2]}{n^2-1}\). This sharp threshold is of order one for fixed chord length but of order \(n^2\) for a chord spanning a fixed fraction of the ring.
