For three independent proportional-hazards lifetimes, Kendall's tau between the first two failure times is at most \(\frac25\bigl(1-\sum_i(p_i-1/3)^2\bigr)\), where \(p_i\) are the hazard rates normalized to sum to one. The heterogeneity penalty is globally sharp; equality for strictly positive rates occurs only when the rates are equal.
