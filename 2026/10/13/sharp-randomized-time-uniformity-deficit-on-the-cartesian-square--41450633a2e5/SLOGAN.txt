On the nine-vertex graph \(P_3\square P_3\), the globally smallest squared Frobenius distance of an arbitrarily time-randomized continuous-time quantum walk from uniform mixing is \(0.0909764868554665\ldots\). It is achieved precisely by giving equal probability to the two cosine phases \(\pm\sqrt r\), where \(81r^3-81r^2+71r-15=0\), and is strictly smaller than the \(1/9\) error obtainable when the factors themselves mix uniformly on average.
