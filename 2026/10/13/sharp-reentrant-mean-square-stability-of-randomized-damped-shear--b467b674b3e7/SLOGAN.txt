For a damped planar rotation split into two individually Hurwitz shears, equal-probability random exact exponentials have expected squared-norm multiplier \(q(h)=e^{-4ah}(1+2b^2h^2)\) at every step. The sharp shear-to-damping threshold \(b/a=3.514699022529\ldots\) creates a reentrant unstable band of intermediate steps, despite stability at smaller and larger steps; at \((a,b,h)=(1,4,1/4)\), the expected state decays but its expected squared norm grows.
