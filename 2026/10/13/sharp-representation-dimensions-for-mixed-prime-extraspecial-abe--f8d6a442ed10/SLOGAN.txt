For products of extraspecial groups at pairwise distinct primes and an arbitrary finite abelian group, the smallest faithful complex representation degree is \(\sum_i p_i^{n_i}+\max(0,R-t)\), where \(R\) is the largest prime-primary rank of the center. The formula explains exactly how abelian factors are absorbed into existing nonlinear blocks, including \(\delta(\mathrm D_8\times C_3)=2\) and \(\delta(\mathrm D_8\times C_3^2)=3\).
