For the ternary fixed point \(\mathbf p\) of \(0\mapsto01\), \(1\mapsto21\), \(2\mapsto0\), reflection complexity is \(3,3,6,7,10,12\) at lengths \(1,\ldots,6\) and equals the full factor complexity \(2n+1\) for every \(n\ge7\). The transition is sharp: \(1012101\) is the last reversible factor, and no factor of length at least \(8\) occurs together with its reversal.
