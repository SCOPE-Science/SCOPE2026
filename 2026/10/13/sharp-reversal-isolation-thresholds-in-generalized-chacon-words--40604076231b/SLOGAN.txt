For the generalized Chacon fixed point \(0\mapsto0^a10,\ 1\mapsto1\), with \(a\ge3\), the first length at which every factor is absent together with its reversal is exactly \(19\) for \(a=3\) and \(a^2+3a\) for \(a\ge4\). The sharp quadratic threshold follows from a three-gap grammar and explicit extremal palindromes.
