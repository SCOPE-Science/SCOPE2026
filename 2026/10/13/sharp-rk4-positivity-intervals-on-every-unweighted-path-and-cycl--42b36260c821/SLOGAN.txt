For classical RK4 applied to unit-rate heat diffusion, every connected simple graph of maximum degree two has an exact, complete end-step positivity interval: paths stabilize at the sharp bound \(h\le1/2\) from six vertices onward and cycles from eight vertices onward, with explicit larger exceptional small-graph thresholds. Small-cycle cutoffs are nonmonotone in size (notably \(C_5\) versus \(C_4\)), while every step beyond the stated endpoint has a negative matrix entry.
