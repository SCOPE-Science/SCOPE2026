For \(n=2^m-1\) cyclic items and a defective state that is either empty or exactly one adjacent pair, \(m\) noiseless nonadaptive tests need at least \(m2^{m-2}\) total item-to-test incidences; an explicit construction meets that bound. Every optimal design has perfectly balanced pools of size \(2^{m-2}\), with no two adjacent items in any pool.
