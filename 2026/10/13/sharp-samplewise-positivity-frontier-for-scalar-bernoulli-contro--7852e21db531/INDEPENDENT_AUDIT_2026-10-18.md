# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For m>=1, finite nu>=1 and independent Bernoulli eta sampling, the estimator is unbiased and its variance quadratic has unique oracle minimizer beta=mean(p). Every 0/1 selector mask has positive probability at every finite repetition count; an affine form minimizes at a mask vertex. For beta>0 and k>m eta, the adverse k-item mask comprises k smallest p values, yielding exact beta_crit=min sum p_(1..k)/(k-m eta). Oracle negative deviation selects p_i<mean. Under p_i in [a,b] and contrast kappa=b/a, the extremal two-level population with low fraction sqrt(kappa)/(1+sqrt(kappa)) yields maximum normalized downside (sqrt(kappa)-1)/(sqrt(kappa)+1), proving exact infimum and equality/rationality boundary. Independent rational checks on populations m<=5 and square contrasts verify, but only analytic proof establishes full dimensions.

An exact deterministic positivity safeguard for a natural scalar specialization of a matrix estimator has practical structural meaning: quantifies when variance-optimal correction loses Galerkin positivity and why finite averaging cannot remove even rare counterexample masks.
