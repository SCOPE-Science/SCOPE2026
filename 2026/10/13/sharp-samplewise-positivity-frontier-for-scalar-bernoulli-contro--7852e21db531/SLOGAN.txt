For a one-vector Bernoulli-sketched Galerkin Gram matrix, a variance-optimal control variate can destroy positivity, and the exact all-mask admissible correction is \(0<\beta<\beta_{\mathrm{crit}}\). Under coefficient contrast \(\kappa\), the sharp uniform positive-margin threshold is \(\eta>(\sqrt\kappa-1)/(\sqrt\kappa+1)\), which no finite repetition count improves.
