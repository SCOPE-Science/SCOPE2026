For any commuting two-local qubit Pauli Hamiltonian with individual term strength at most \(J\), thermal entanglement lives only on isolated Bell dimers and every bipartition's logarithmic negativity is the sum of the dimers crossing it. The sharp size-independent universal full-separability threshold for every overlap-degree bound \(\Delta\ge2\) is \(\beta J\le(\log3)/4\), attained sharply by the three-term Heisenberg dimer.
