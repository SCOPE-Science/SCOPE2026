For three antiferromagnetically coupled Ising qubits at exactly the frustrated ground energy, identical local transverse marginals \((I+cX)/2\) are feasible up to \(c=2/3\) quantum mechanically, but only up to \(c=1/3\) for fully separable states. Thus \(1/3<c\le2/3\) gives a sharp, local-coherence-resolved entanglement witness without lowering the energy below the ground state.
