On a unit-rate periodic heat cycle, the L-stable TR-BDF2 end-step is positivity preserving at every timestep precisely when there are at most seven vertices. For every larger cycle there is exactly one bounded interval of timesteps where positivity fails, followed by positivity recovery; the return threshold grows as \(0.2379000\,n^2\) while the first failure threshold tends to \(2+3\sqrt2/2\).
