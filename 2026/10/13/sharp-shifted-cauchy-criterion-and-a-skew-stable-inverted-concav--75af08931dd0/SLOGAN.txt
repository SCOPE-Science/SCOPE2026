The magnitude of every centered symmetric stable law of index \(0<\alpha\le1\) is inverted concave, and the result extends to a quantitative skewness band \(|\beta|\le\tan(\pi\alpha/6)/\tan(\pi\alpha/2)\) for \(0<\alpha<1\). The underlying shifted-Cauchy generator has the exact threshold \(|m|\le1/\sqrt3\), yielding new rigorous classes of infinite-mean risks with majorization-reversed stochastic dominance.
