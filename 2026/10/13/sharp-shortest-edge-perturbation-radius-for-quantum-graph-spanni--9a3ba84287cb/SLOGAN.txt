For finite connected simple Kirchhoff quantum graphs, the shortest-edge-normalized spectral determinant recovers the spanning-tree count by integer rounding throughout an exactly sharp perturbation window; the worst graph at every vertex count is complete, with one short edge and all others long. The optimal radius is an explicit one-variable algebraic root and asymptotic to \(n^{-n}\); fixed-graph cutoffs are governed by spanning-tree edge-inclusion probabilities.
