Exact-arithmetic IC(0) cannot break down on any SPD matrix of order at most three, so four dimensions are minimal. On the Jacobi-scaled signed four-cycle containing Kershaw's classical counterexample, breakdown begins sharply at \(t=1/\sqrt3\), equivalently at condition number \(5+2\sqrt6\), while the matrix remains strictly positive definite until \(t=1/\sqrt2\).
