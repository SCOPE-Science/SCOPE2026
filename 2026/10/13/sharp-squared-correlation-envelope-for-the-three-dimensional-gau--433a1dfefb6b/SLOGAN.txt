For a standardized Gaussian triple, fixing the total squared pairwise correlation \(S\) gives an exact two-branch lower envelope for the square-product deficit, with a phase transition at \(S=3/4\) from equal-magnitude frustrated correlations to singular covariance matrices. In particular, \(\mathbb E[X_1^2X_2^2X_3^2]-1\ge(2/3)S\), and the constant \(2/3\) is sharp.
