## correctness

PASS

For transitive R, the truth-set operator T(X)={w:R[w]⊆X or R[w]∩X=∅} maps any X to a successor-forward-closed Y1. Hence Y1⊆Y2⊆Y3… . If Y1=∅ then Y2=W; if |Y1|≥2, at most n−2 further strict increments occur, so stabilization by n−1. In the remaining |Y1|=1 case, a w∈Y2\Y1 must have ≥2 differently X-colored successors and R[w] disjoint from Y1. A terminal strongly connected component of R[w] cannot be a singleton (its successor set would be homogeneous and belong to Y1); thus at least two new worlds enter Y2, again forcing stabilization by n−1. n=1 and n=2 exceptions are handled separately. The strict order iRj iff i<j with X={n−1} produces exact consecutive tails, demonstrating sharpness for every n≥3; universal two-state relation supplies n=2. Independently enumerated all 4180 transitive labeled frames n≤4 and all 65328 frame/valuation pairs, plus strict-chain witnesses through n=60; proof not inferred from finite enumeration.
