On every transitive \(n\)-world Kripke frame, repeated non-contingency \(\nabla\) has a sharp universal fixed-stage cutoff of \(n-1\) for \(n\geq3\), with exceptional cutoffs \(1\) and \(2\) at the first two sizes. The bound is attained by strict linear orders, while a terminal-component argument proves no finite transitive frame can delay stabilization longer.
