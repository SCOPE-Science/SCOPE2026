# Scientific review: sharp thermal-contact ordering law

## Correctness — PASS
The matrix-level qubit generalized amplitude-damping action fixes the off-diagonal contraction \(\sqrt R\) and yields the exact affine excited-state offset \(s_\pi\) by induction. The normalized Choi partial transpose has one potentially negative eigenvalue and its middle-block determinant is nonnegative exactly when \(s_\pi(\Gamma-s_\pi)\ge R\). The two-qubit PPT criterion converts this into an if-and-only-if entanglement-breaking statement. The adjacent-swap identity is exact, with a strictly positive multiplicative factor for all allowed damping strengths. The restriction \(0\le\nu_i\le1/2\) guarantees monotonicity of negativity in the offset. Each cubic has a unique root in the declared range; the reverse-order threshold lies below \(3/4\), so both individual channels remain non-entanglement-breaking throughout the order-sensitive interval. These analytic steps cover all finite lengths and equality cases. A numerical Choi/Kraus checker and rational permutation enumerator separately corroborate the formulas but are not the proof.

## Originality — PASS (self-review; residual risk)
The closest full-text primary sources are Aslanbaş–Alkan–Karpat (arXiv:2609.00168v1, Sec. III.E and Appendix E), Khatri–Sharma–Wilde (arXiv:1903.07747, Sec. IV), and Lami–Giovannetti (arXiv:1411.2517v4, Sec. III Example 1). **Not claimed new:** the two-contact commutator, closure of generalized amplitude-damping channels under composition, the PPT test for a single such channel, or repeated identical-channel entanglement-breaking indices. Lami–Giovannetti supply especially strong preexisting algebraic ingredients: an exact binary composition formula and entanglement-breaking inequality. The final new object is the optimization over **all permutations of a fixed heterogeneous thermal-contact multiset**, its exact negativity and entanglement-breaking phase envelope, and the sharp interval with open interior where two individually non-entanglement-breaking channels produce opposite binary outcomes under reversed definite orders. The cited papers do not state or analyze that permutation optimum or the two-root phase-window classification. There remains a real originality risk that another text may derive the sorting corollary directly from the known composition laws. It must not be described as a new composition or PPT criterion.

## Value — PASS
A multistep thermal-contact protocol normally permits exponentially many or factorially many orderings, but a direct exact rule selects the extremal ones from only stationary populations, independent of the individual damping-strength order. The phase diagram determines when zero, some, or all permutations are entanglement-breaking without enumerating the orders. The exact two-bath window exhibits a sharp irreversible loss of reference entanglement controlled solely by scheduling, even though neither constituent bath contact is entanglement-breaking. The phenomenon distinguishes definite channel ordering from the coherent-control entanglement studied in the motivating paper. The theorem has a physically interpretable model and operational parameter, rather than an arbitrary numerical slice.

## Closest literature and limitations
- Aslanbaş–Alkan–Karpat, arXiv:2609.00168v1: the full text, especially eqs. (178)–(181) and the switch concurrence analysis, was checked. Its commutator mechanism is prior; its entanglement is control–target rather than reference–target, and it does not optimize definite-order entanglement breaking.
- Khatri–Sharma–Wilde, arXiv:1903.07747: the complete accessible text contains Sec. IV's single-channel entanglement-breaking criterion and other GAD decompositions, but not fixed-multiset ordering optimization.
- Lami–Giovannetti, arXiv:1411.2517v4: its Sec. III provides binary GAD composition and an EB formula, and analyzes repeated identical maps; those formulas are genuine precursors and make derivation of the new classification feasible. The novelty therefore lies in the sharp sorting/phase theorem, not the elementary channel representation.
- Cuevas et al., Phys. Rev. A 96, 022322 (2017): unitary-filter amendment of channels differs from permuting heterogeneous thermal contacts without filters.

No claim is made for inverted equilibrium populations, higher-dimensional systems, arbitrary phase-covariant noise, or coherent quantum switches. The classification is exact at stated boundaries; independent audit has not yet occurred.
