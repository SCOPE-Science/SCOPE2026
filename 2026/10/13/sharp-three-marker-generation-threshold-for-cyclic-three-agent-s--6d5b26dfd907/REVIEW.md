# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem specifies cyclic coordinates, **three named equivalence relations**, an ordinary ungraded one-proposition Boolean modal algebra, and separate \(n=1,2\) edge cases. The constructive recurrence provides explicit selectors: an unmarked intersection of two propositional coordinate supports identifies \((1,1)\), and sum-diagonal plus zero-coordinate intersections double the range of coordinates addressable in two further modal steps. The affine reflection is a valuation-preserving nontrivial automorphism for any support of size one or two when \(n\ge3\), and the empty support has translations. The two-modality lower bound follows from swapping two unmarked coordinate classes. All boundary quantifiers have been checked. The standalone verifier reports 63 direct Boolean closures, 7,798 reflection checks, 62 inductive-size checks, 58 large set-based modal-refinement checks, and 2,773 two-modal sparse-set cases; these are finite corroboration, not a substitute for the general proof.

## Originality — PASS (with residual historical risk)

A closely related 2015 paper, *Semirigid systems of three equivalence relations* (arXiv:1505.02955), studies cyclic three-nets and an independent **triangle-closure** notion of a system generated by two points. That result cannot imply Boolean **one-proposition** generation by a two-cell truth set: the automorphism obstruction here excludes precisely that possibility. The checked full treatment of *Local finiteness in varieties of MS4-algebras* (arXiv:2312.16754) gives general modal-algebra and S5 coloring background, not the exact three-modality additive-grid marker threshold. The graph modal expressivity article arXiv:2307.05150 is further general background. Targeted semantic records and previously accepted research claims were compared for equivalence and covering implications; none found to cover the complete invariant. A niche historical antecedent or an uninspected general theorem may still exist.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This is a complete exact minimum-weight generator invariant for a natural cyclic Latin-square family, with a constructive logarithmic modal-depth upper bound. It exhibits an unbounded change from an at-least-linear support requirement with two agents to an exactly constant support requirement with the third named agent. The distinction from triangle-closure monogenicity is mathematically structural, and the outcome can inform sparse predicate design and quantitative modal-algebra generation. It does **not** claim a new general modal duality or a classification over arbitrary Latin squares.
