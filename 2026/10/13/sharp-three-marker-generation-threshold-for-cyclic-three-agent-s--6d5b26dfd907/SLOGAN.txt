For the cyclic \(n\times n\) three-agent S5 frame with row, column, and coordinate-sum modalities, the minimum number of cells marked by one propositional letter to generate the entire powerset modal algebra is exactly three for every \(n\ge3\), with explicit formulas of depth at most \(2+2\lceil\log_2(n-1)\rceil\). Removing the sum modality forces at least \(n-1\) marked cells.
