For every sample size \(n\ge4\), among all distributions assigning probability \(1/3\) to each of three distinct real atoms, the correlation of the sample minimum and maximum is uniquely maximized by equally spaced atoms. The exact maximum is \(\bigl((a_n+b_n)^2-a_n\bigr)/\bigl(3a_n+b_n-(a_n+b_n)^2\bigr)\), with \(a_n=3^{-n}\), \(b_n=(2/3)^n\), and it decays as \((2/3)^n\).
