Among integers \(n\ge2\) whose totient ratios can be written as three positive unit fractions, the largest ratio is \(6/7\), attained exactly at powers of \(7\). Thus the sharp strict nonrepresentation cutoff is \(6/7\): every \(c>6/7\) excludes all such representations when \(\varphi(n)/n\ge c\), whereas \(c=6/7\) does not.
