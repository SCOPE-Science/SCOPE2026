For any two independent three-sided dice, their five-point sum is at least \(1/5\) away from uniform in \(\ell_1\) distance (equivalently, total variation at least \(1/10\)). Equality occurs exactly, up to exchanging the dice, when one has probabilities \((1/2,0,1/2)\) and the other has \((s,2/5,3/5-s)\) with \(1/5\le s\le2/5\), resolving the first nontrivial case of a 2026 conjecture.
