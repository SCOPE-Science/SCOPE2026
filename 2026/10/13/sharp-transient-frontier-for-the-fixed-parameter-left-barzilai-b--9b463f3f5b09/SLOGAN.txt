For the fixed-\(p\) LEFT extension, the sharp one-step amplification of both squared gradient norm and quadratic objective gap is \((p\kappa-1)^2\), even after an exact-line-search warm-up. Thus its exact universal transient-monotonicity frontier is \(p\kappa\le2\), so increasing \(p\) toward the source's locally stable regime narrows rather than widens worst-case finite-step safety.
