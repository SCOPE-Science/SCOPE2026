# Self-review

PASS. The proof reduces every parameter pair to two modal-orientation patterns. In both patterns a single normalized maximum \(c\) satisfies \(c\le1+r\) and \(c\le1+s\), which is algebraically equivalent to the claimed sharp coefficient. Degenerate boundaries and all equality cases are included. The finite rational checker is supplemental only.

## Originality
PASS. The closest direct source proves general Bernoulli and integer-valued min-entropy power lower bounds and separately observes the universal upper obstruction \(1/2\) from two fair Bernoulli variables. It does not state the matching lower bound for all heterogeneous two-Bernoulli pairs. Full-text comparison with earlier majorization work and candidate-specific searches found no covering theorem. The main residual risk is an equivalent elementary result under older maximum-atom terminology.

## Value
PASS. The exact two-summand constant is a natural benchmark for the open constant gap in discrete min-entropy power inequalities. It shows that no two-Bernoulli parameter imbalance lowers the fair-pair ratio and therefore that any Bernoulli-sum failure of coefficient \(1/2\) must begin with at least three summands.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest literature gives the \(1/2\) upper obstruction and lower constants below it, not the exact two-parameter theorem. The result does not settle three or more Bernoulli summands or arbitrary integer-valued summands.
