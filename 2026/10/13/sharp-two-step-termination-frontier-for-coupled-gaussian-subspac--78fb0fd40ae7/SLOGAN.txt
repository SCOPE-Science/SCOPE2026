For planar exact subspace Newton with separately standard Gaussian sketches, the sharp probability of terminating in two steps is \(\frac4\pi\arctan(1/\sqrt\kappa)\), achieved by coupling the sketch directions. For every nonspherical Hessian no *jointly Gaussian* pair can terminate exactly in two steps with positive probability, while independent copies of an optimal nonjointly-Gaussian pair terminate almost surely after a geometrically distributed number of pairs.
