In a two-test Benjamini--Hochberg problem with one true Gaussian null and one shifted Gaussian alternative, the sharp false discovery rate supremum under strictly negative, nonsingular Gaussian correlation is \(3\alpha/4\) for \(0<\alpha<1/2\). An explicit positive-definite correlation at \(\alpha=1/20\) exceeds the proposed Gaussian negative-dependence BH bound, disproving the conjecture.
