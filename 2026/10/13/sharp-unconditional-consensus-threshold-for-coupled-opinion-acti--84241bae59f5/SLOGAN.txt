For the deterministic opinion–action model with any number of agents and \(0<\phi<1\), joint initial opinion–action diameter \(D_0\) is guaranteed to collapse to consensus whenever \(2\varepsilon\ge(1+\phi)D_0\), without assuming that interaction neighborhoods stabilize. The bound is sharp uniformly in population size by an exact polarized two-agent equilibrium below it, and convergence becomes exponential after the confidence graph becomes complete.
