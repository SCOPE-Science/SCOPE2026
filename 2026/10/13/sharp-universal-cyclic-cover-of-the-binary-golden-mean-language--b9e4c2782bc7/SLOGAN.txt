For every integer \(n\ge2\), the shortest cyclic binary word that avoids `11` and contains **all** internally `11`-free length-\(n\) words has exactly \(F_{n+2}+F_n-1\) symbols, where \(F_0=0\) and \(F_1=1\). An explicit Eulerian balancing construction attains this all-order bound; the unavoidable excess over the number of required factors is \(F_n-1\).
