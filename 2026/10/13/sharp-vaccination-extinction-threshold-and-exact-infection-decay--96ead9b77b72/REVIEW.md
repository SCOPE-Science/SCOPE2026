For imperfect vaccination, the previously sufficient global threshold ignores vaccination rate and effectiveness although they determine the reproduction number, leaving a practically important gap between predicted local extinction and guaranteed global elimination. The theorem closes that gap in the Holling-II model, including the critical nonhyperbolic boundary, and quantifies the actual logarithmic extinction rate of each nontrivial infection.
