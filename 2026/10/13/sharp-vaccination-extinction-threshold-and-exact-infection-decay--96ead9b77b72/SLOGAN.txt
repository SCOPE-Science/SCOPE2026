In the imperfectly vaccinated Holling-II environmental SVEIR model, the disease-free state is globally asymptotically stable exactly when \(\mathcal R_c\le1\), including equality; below that boundary every nonzero infection has the exact logarithmic extinction exponent \(s(B_0)\). A two-parameter exact example proves this extends beyond the prior conservative \(\mathcal J_c\le1\) global criterion.
