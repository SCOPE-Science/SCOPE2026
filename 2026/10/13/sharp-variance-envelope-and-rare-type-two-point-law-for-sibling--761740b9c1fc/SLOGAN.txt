For a fixed number of coupon types, the largest possible variance of the deficit in sibling \(j\)'s album approaches \((N-1)^2/4\) as \(j\to\infty\). A single coupon whose probability decays as \(\log(2)/((N-1)j)\) produces a balanced two-point limit, whereas the uniform probabilities produce vanishing variance even though they maximize the expected deficit.
