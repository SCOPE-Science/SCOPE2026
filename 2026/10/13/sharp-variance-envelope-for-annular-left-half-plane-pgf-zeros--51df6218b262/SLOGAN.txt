For probability-generating zeros confined to an annulus and the closed left half-plane, the exact sharp variance constant is \(C(R)=\min\{R/(R+1)^2,2R^2/(R^2+1)^2\}\) per degree. A phase transition at \(R+R^{-1}=1+\sqrt5\) switches the extremal geometry from negative-real zeros to imaginary-axis pairs.
