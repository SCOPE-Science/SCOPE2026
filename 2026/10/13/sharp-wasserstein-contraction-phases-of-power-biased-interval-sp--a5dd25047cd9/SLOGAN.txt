The optimal one-step Euclidean \(W_1\) Lipschitz coefficient of the power-biased interval splitting walk is \(1/2\) at \(p=0\), infinite for \(0<p<1\), zero at \(p=1\), and \(\max\{1/2,(p-1)/2\}\) for \(p>1\). Thus sharp \(W_1\) nonexpansiveness holds exactly at \(p=0\) and for \(1\le p\le3\), despite the source's uniform-ergodicity theorem applying for all \(p\ge0\).
