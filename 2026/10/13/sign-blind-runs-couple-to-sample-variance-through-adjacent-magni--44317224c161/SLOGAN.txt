For any ordering chosen from absolute magnitudes alone, the sign-run count is independent of every magnitude-only statistic but has exact positive covariance with centered sample variance: \(\operatorname{Cov}(R_\pi,S^2)=\mathbb E[\sum_j A_{\pi_j}A_{\pi_{j+1}}]/[n(n-1)]\). For McWilliams absolute-rank runs under \(\operatorname{Unif}[-a,a]\), this becomes \(a^2(2n+5)/[6(n+1)(n+2)]\).
