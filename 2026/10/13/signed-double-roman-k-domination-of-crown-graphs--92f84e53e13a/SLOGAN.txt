For every crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n\) with \(n\ge3\), signed double Roman \(k\)-domination attains the regular lower bound \(2k\) for every admissible \(1\le k\le3n\), except \((n,k)=(3,2)\) and \((4,1)\), where the value is \(2k+2\). Equality forces identical labels on every deleted matching pair.
