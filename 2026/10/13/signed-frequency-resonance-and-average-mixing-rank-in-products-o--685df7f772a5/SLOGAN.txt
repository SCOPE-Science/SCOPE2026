On Cartesian products of complete graphs, each quantum Cesàro average-mixing eigenvalue is an exact signed-frequency constant coefficient; an eigenmode survives precisely when its active alphabet sizes admit a constrained zero-sum. In particular, \(K_2\square K_m\square K_n\) has rank \(mn+(m-1)(n-1)\) exactly when \(|m-n|=2\) contributes the extra term (for \(m,n\ge3\)).
