# Mathematical review: signed surface representation in genus-two full-level cohomology

## Correctness: PASS
The new proof separately verifies the integral modular-lift lemma for determinant \(\pm1\), the real orientation sign \(\det(\operatorname{Sym}^2\widetilde A)=(\det\widetilde A)^3\), and the basis decomposition of the top two-dimensional simplicial cycle group. It then checks transitivity of the finite symplectic group, the determinant-one criterion for a transvection-invariant Lagrangian, the involution fixed-plane count, and the complete rational \(C_p\)-module trace calculation. The hypotheses \(p\ge3\) prime and two-dimensional symplectic eigenspaces are used explicitly. Exact arithmetic checks for \(p=3,5,7\) reproduce the predicted traces, but the infinite assertion is proved symbolically.

## Originality: PASS (subject to identified residual risk)
The closest source, Assaf--Brandt--Bruce--Chan--Vlad arXiv:2609.14895v1, explicitly determines the surface decomposition and dimensions, which are not claimed as new. Its Corollary 5.8 supplies the geometry from which an equivariant computation becomes possible, but does not itself give the determinant-sign induced representation, its character at arbitrary elements, or the exact transvection cyclotomic spectrum. Their 2025 arXiv:2505.10504v2 provides the general top-weight spectral sequence, with no corresponding \(\operatorname{Sp}_4(\mathbb F_p)\) character statement. Hoffman--Weintraub's 2003 boundary-cohomology representations concern ordinary cohomology of the geometric boundary, not cohomology of the dual complex; its top boundary permutation representation cannot equal this signed representation because the latter has negative symplectic-involution trace. Oda--Schwermer 1990 is only partially accessible in available evidence, and an implicit representation-theoretic derivation in older work cannot be excluded. This risk is reported, not taken as evidence of absolute novelty.

## Value: PASS
This is an all-odd-prime equivariant refinement of a natural moduli-space cohomology group; dimensions alone do not recover either its negative involution character or its cyclic transvection spectrum. The exact multiplicities \(\mathbb Q^{(p-1)(p+1)^2/2}\oplus\mathbb Q(\zeta_p)^{p(p^2-1)/2}\) determine natural invariant spaces for intermediate level quotients and contrast with the known boundary permutation representation. No irreducible decomposition under the full finite symplectic group and no statement for composite levels is asserted.
