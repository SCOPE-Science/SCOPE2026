For every odd prime \(p\), the degree-two cohomology of the genus-two tropical full-level link is the sign-induced representation \(\operatorname{Ind}_{H}^{\operatorname{Sp}_4(\mathbb F_p)}\varepsilon\) on oriented Lagrangian-surface labels. A transvection has trace \((p^2-1)/2\), while a symplectic involution has the strictly negative trace \(-(p-1)(p+1)^2/2\), distinguishing this equivariant invariant from the known non-equivariant Betti count.
