For every pair of real symmetric matrices of order at most three, the minimum variance of one coupled Rademacher trace-difference probe can be attained by a fixed signed permutation of its probe coordinates. In order four, explicit positive-definite matrices arbitrarily close to the identity force every such deterministic permutation to have variance \(12\varepsilon^2\) against the unrestricted optimum \(8\varepsilon^2\), a strict factor \(3/2\).
