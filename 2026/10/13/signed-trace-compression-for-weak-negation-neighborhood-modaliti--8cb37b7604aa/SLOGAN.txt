In unrestricted weak-negation two-modal neighborhood semantics, a finite set of formulas admits root-preserving compression to at most \(\max(1,2k-1)\) worlds, where \(k\) counts distinct modal operands, while preserving both verification and rejection of every subformula. This signed-trace bound is sharp for simultaneous operand-trace separation and yields coNP-completeness of finite-premise local consequence and validity.
