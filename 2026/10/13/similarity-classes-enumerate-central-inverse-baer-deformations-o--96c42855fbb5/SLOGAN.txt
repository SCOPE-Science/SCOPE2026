The central alternating-bilinear inverse-Baer deformations of one fixed extraspecial Heisenberg brace are classified, up to skew-brace isomorphism, by ordinary similarity classes in \(M_d(\mathbb F_p)\). Their class counts and the nonabelian-additive subcounts have explicit Euler-product generating functions; already for \(d=2\), these yield \(p^2+p\) and \(p^2-1\) distinct types.
