For ideal spectral deflation with a fixed matrix-vector budget, the exact Gaussian log-determinant mean-square error is single-crossing in the number of probes: one probe is globally optimal if and only if it is no worse than two probes. For geometric squared logarithmic eigenvalues, this becomes the sharp finite-size condition \(q^{-m}+q^{n-B+m}\ge2\), with limiting boundary \(q^m\le1/2\).
