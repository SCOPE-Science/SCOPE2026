If every edge of a finite simple root graph is the unique common edge of two four-cycles, the quantum average mixing matrix of its line graph is positive definite with smallest eigenvalue at least \(1/(16|E|)\). In particular, line graphs of hypercubes \(Q_d\) have full-rank average mixing for every \(d\ge3\), whereas the four-vertex example \(L(Q_2)\) has rank two.
