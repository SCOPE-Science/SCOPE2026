For a quantum walk on a prime four-regular cycle, arbitrary placements of \(r\) absorbing vertices span exactly \(1+(p-1)\min(r,d)/d\) bright dimensions, where \(d=4\) only at the arithmetic resonance \(m^2\equiv-1\pmod p\) and otherwise \(d=2\). With one absorber, every other starting vertex is captured with exactly the same probability, \(1/4\) at resonance and \(1/2\) otherwise, regardless of its distance from the absorber.
