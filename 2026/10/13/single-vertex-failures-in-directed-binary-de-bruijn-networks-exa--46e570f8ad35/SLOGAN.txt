In the directed binary de Bruijn network of any order, deleting a vertex destroys strong connectivity precisely at the four run-transition words (two distinct words at order two), and each such failure splits the network into components of sizes \(1,1,2^k-3\). Exact directed postfailure diameter histograms through order ten reveal a first three-hop worst-case penalty at order five and a smaller penalty at order six; failures of the constant words preserve diameter \(k\) at every order.
