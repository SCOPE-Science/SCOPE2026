For the projective Hibi variety of an ordinal sum of two antichains of sizes \(a,b\), the reduced singular locus has exactly \(b\mathbf1_{a\ge2}+a\mathbf1_{b\ge2}+\binom a2\binom b2\) irreducible components: boundary Segre varieties and mixed Segre joins. Their generic transverse singularities are respectively Segre-cone vertices and ordinary threefold nodes.
