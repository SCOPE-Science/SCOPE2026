The determinant hypersurface of a variable symmetric tridiagonal matrix of order \(n\ge3\) has precisely \(n-1\) reduced singular components, each a complete intersection cut out by one vanishing bridge and the determinants of its two side blocks. Component \(j\) has degree \(j(n-j)\), and its general transverse singularity is the ordinary double point \(uv-w^2=0\).
