# Review of the hollow-symmetric discriminant singularity classification

## Correctness — PASS
The two critical identities are \(\partial(\det M)/\partial x_{ij}=2\operatorname{adj}(M)_{ij}\) and the exact Schur-complement determinant factorization. The adjugate rank-one argument covers all ranks and proves the complete reduced singular support, including the coordinate-kernel exceptions. The Gram map from products of irreducible isotropic quadrics proves irreducibility and the exact dimension of the corank-two component for all \(n\ge5\). A full-rank isotropic example supplies three independent infinitesimal Schur coordinates, and the formal implicit-function argument proves the generic node normal form. The row-zero normal form follows from a nondegenerate invertible-block quadratic form. The codimension calculation implies irreducibility and normality of the hypersurface. The standalone exact checker passed the stated \(48\) families of arithmetic checks (\(36+4+4+4\)); none is substituted for the infinite argument. No statement about special intersection germs or global Jacobian radicality is made.

## Originality — PASS with specified risks
The 2016 Helmer–Sturmfels source identifies the hypersimplex discriminant and gives degree data, not the asserted singular-locus classification. The much closer 2024 El Maazouz–Sturmfels–Sverrisdóttir source already proves that the corank-two hollow locus is irreducible for \(n\ge5\); irreducibility alone is therefore NOT an originality claim. Their explicitly discussed singular-component calculation treats a distinct \(2\)-by-\(2\) block-hollow six-by-six matrix, not the \(1\)-by-\(1\) hollow determinant studied here. A published online note covers the eleven singular components of the exceptional four-by-four case, which the present claim excludes. Searches for the equivalent \(n\ge5\) singular decomposition, dual-hypersimplex formulation, and generic transverse normal forms returned no decisive prior statement; this is supporting evidence, not proof of global novelty. ## Value — PASS
This is a complete and motivated classification of singular **components** of a classical infinite family of projective \(A\)-discriminants, not a selected numerical instance. The component count \(n+1\), dimension dichotomy, and two generic transverse analytic types clarify the geometry of dual hypersimplices and the exceptional lower-size splitting; global normality follows without computing the full singular scheme. The exact generic models can support further study of resolutions, intersection strata and characteristic classes. No unproved downstream theorem or globally reduced Jacobian scheme is claimed.
