# Scientific review: a rational plane septic's singularity basket

## Correctness — PASS

The degree-seven parametrization is basepoint free and generically injective. Away from the two exceptional fibers and the unique ramification parameter, two preimages agree only through a fifth-root coincidence. The four exact coincidence cubics are all squarefree and pairwise coprime over \(\mathbb Q(\zeta_5)\): their discriminant norms are \(674041\) and all six pair-resultant norms are \(2000\). This yields twelve ordinary nodes, in addition to the transverse pair at \([1:0:0]\). At parameter \(t=0\), analytic coordinates reduce to \((t^2,t^5)\), giving one \(A_4\) cusp. Local charts also exclude further ramification and singularities. The genus and Plücker formulas independently confirm the delta total \(15\) and dual degree \(11\). A self-contained standard-library exact checker reproduces the finite certificates, the implicit nodal tangent cone and the dual tangent parametrization.

## Originality — PASS

The leading 2023 paper and its 2024 open-access final version were inspected at the exact example, Lemma 5.2. They establish nonextendibility, birationality and a multiplicity ceiling, not the claimed analytic singularity basket or dual class. In particular, an incidental smoothness assertion for the distinguished Galois point conflicts with its explicitly checked two-branch nodal tangent cone. The related Miura paper concerns an order-three extension rather than this septic. Exact equation/alias, thirteen-node, \(A_4\)-cusp and dual-degree searches in indexed mathematical records and public web sources found no equivalent or more general result implying this basket. Existing mathematical records concerning other cuspidal quintics, unexpected sextics and multiview singularities do not contain this exact curve. Search incompleteness remains a genuine bibliographic risk, not a novelty proof by itself.

## Value — PASS

The septic is the explicit counterexample at the heart of the source's Cremona nonextendibility construction, where control of singular multiplicities is essential. Its exact geometric singularities are therefore motivated independently of the finite computation: a complete \(13A_1+A_4\) basket sharpens the coarse multiplicity bound, singles out the local nature of the distinguished Galois point, and determines the geometrically meaningful projective dual degree. The fifth-root collision calculation is needed to rule out triple identifications and tangencies, not merely to evaluate an arbitrary slice.
