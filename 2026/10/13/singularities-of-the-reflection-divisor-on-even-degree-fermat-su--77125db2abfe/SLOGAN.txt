On every even-degree Fermat surface \(S_d\), the reduced divisor of the \(4+6d\) monomial-reflection plane sections has precisely \(6d\) ordinary \((d+2)\)-fold points, \(4d^3\) ordinary triple points, and \(12d^2+3d^3\) ordinary nodes, with no other singularities. For the Fermat quartic these are the fixed-plane sections of all \(28\) quasi-Galois points and yield exactly \(24\) sixfold, \(256\) triple and \(384\) nodal intersections.
