For an explicit one-parameter family of four smooth conics, the entire singularity discriminant is \(t\in\{0,2,7/2,8\}\), and only the fiber \(t=8\) is nearly free. This distinguishes the intended nearly-free \(4A_7+8A_1\) configuration from the printed \(t=9\) member, whose actual basket is \(2A_7+16A_1\).
