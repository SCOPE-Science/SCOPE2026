# Review: sharp six-line toric surface obstruction

## Correctness — PASS
A closed immersion yields a linear injection of three-dimensional tangent spaces and a closed embedding of the six-point projectivized tangent cone into that of the target. Normal toric surface singularities in characteristic zero are rational and Cohen--Macaulay. Artin's multiplicity/embedding-dimension identity and Sally's minimal-multiplicity theorem show that the projectivized tangent cone is a one-dimensional scheme defined by quadrics. The restriction of each such quadric to the source tangent plane vanishes on six points imposing six independent conic conditions, so vanishes identically. This forces a projective plane into a projective curve, contradiction. A direct exact determinant calculation gives a six-point witness and proves the required generic open condition is nonempty. The positive \(m\le5\) case follows from a smooth conic and its projective cone.

## Originality — PASS
Banks--Coskun--Tucker, arXiv:2607.18475v1, Example 3.4 covers embeddings in the particular weighted plane only through \(m=5\); Corollary 3.8 excludes all projective normal toric surface targets only for \(m\ge9\) and very general arrangements. The new full-normal-toric obstruction starts at \(m=6\) on an explicit Zariski-open locus, hence is not implied by either result. De Jong and Rossi--Valla supply rational singularity and tangent-cone lemmas, but neither formulates nor solves the intervening embedding threshold. Published-finding searches for the initiating source, six-line threshold, quadratic tangent-cone aliases, and equivalent conic-rank criterion found no dominating statement. Some differently worded earlier embedding obstruction may remain unindexed.

## Value — PASS
The source paper motivates the smallest number of tangent directions that makes a normal toric surface embedding impossible. The result identifies the exact sharp generic cutoff, replacing a previously only sufficient nine-line threshold by six, and strengthens very-generality/projectivity to an explicit open condition with no completeness requirement on the toric surface. The local mechanism applies uniformly to all rational target surface germs of minimal multiplicity; this is a structural obstruction, not an arbitrary numerical recomputation.

## Sources and remaining limitations
The initiating primary preprint was read across the tangent-cone preliminaries, line-arrangement statements and proof, and final questions. The older algebraic-singularity sources were inspected where the Artin and Sally facts are stated. Remaining risks are older, less easily indexed equivalent formulations, and the distinction between the reducible arrangement problem solved here and the source's main irreducible-curve problem. The conic-degenerate locus, nonnormal targets, and characteristic \(p>0\) are outside the finding.
