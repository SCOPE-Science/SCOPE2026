The fourth-order Gauss–Legendre step preserves positivity at every step size on periodic diffusion cycles with at most five vertices, but every cycle with at least six vertices eventually develops negative nearest-neighbor transition weights. On the first failing six-cycle, the positivity window is exactly \(0\le h\le6.36885080963102\ldots\), with the endpoint defined by \(5h^4-12h^3-102h^2-144h-72=0\), despite unconditional spectral stability.
