The Petersen candidate graph needs exactly six strict nonadaptive group tests: the six even Hamilton cycles of \(K_5\) attain the minimum, and Sperner's theorem excludes five. This replaces a seven-test step in a classical strict two-stage construction, giving \(t(n,2,2)\le m+6\) whenever \(m\ge5\) and \(n\le\binom m3+6\).
