\[
P(\lambda)=\prod_i n_i^{n_i},
\]
while Simpson ordering is exactly the reverse ordering of
\[
Q(\lambda)=\sum_i n_i(n_i-1).
\]
The complete integer-partition domains through \(N=7\) are independently generated by two constructions and compared using only exact integer and rational arithmetic. Weak-order equality holds through \(N=5\); exactly two tie splits occur at \(N=6\); and exactly two strict reversals occur at \(N=7\). The packaged verifier returns `VERIFY_OK`.

**PASS.** Shannon and Simpson are among the most widely used ecological diversity summaries and deliberately weight rare versus common species differently. The literature treats disagreement among such indices as a substantive issue and motivates diversity profiles precisely because scalar rankings can conflict.
