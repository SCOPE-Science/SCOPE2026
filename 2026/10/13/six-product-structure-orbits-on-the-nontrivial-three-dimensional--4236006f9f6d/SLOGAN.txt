On the unique nontrivial three-dimensional \(3\)-Lie algebra, every nonscalar involution is a product structure and exactly six automorphism-conjugacy classes occur in characteristic different from \(2\). Over \(\mathbf F_q\) with \(q\) odd, the \(2q^2(q^2+q+1)\) product structures split into six explicit orbits governed solely by how the derived line meets the two eigenspaces.
