Algebraic connectivity and edge connectivity cannot strictly reverse same-order, same-size network rankings through \(5\) vertices. At \(6\) vertices and \(7\) edges, a bridge-containing cone has Fiedler value \(1\), exceeding \(3-\sqrt5\) for a bridgeless triangle–\(4\)-cycle bouquet, even though their edge connectivities are \(1\) and \(2\).
