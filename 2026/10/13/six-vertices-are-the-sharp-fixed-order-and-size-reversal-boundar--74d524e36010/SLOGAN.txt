Among connected graphs with fixed order and edge count, adjacency and nonbacktracking spectral radii cannot reverse their strict ranking before six vertices. At \\(n=6,m=7\\), an explicit pair has \\(\\rho_A(G)>\\rho_A(H)\\) but \\(\\rho_B(G)<\\rho_B(H)\\), so the corresponding inverse spectral threshold surrogates prefer opposite networks.
