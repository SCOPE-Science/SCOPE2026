For positive-modularity bipartitions whose two sides each contain at least two vertices, Newman–Girvan modularity and normalized cut cannot make opposite strict choices on any connected graph with at most \(5\) vertices. At \(6\) vertices they first reverse, in exactly two graph-plus-partition-pair isomorphism classes; the mechanism is the exact balance identity \(Q=ab(1-\operatorname{Ncut})/(2m^2)\).
