For \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M_n\), a set is skew zero forcing exactly when it leaves at most two white vertices on each bipartition side, so \(\mathcal Z^-(\operatorname{Cr}_n;x)=(x^n+n x^{n-1}+\binom n2 x^{n-2})^2\). Relative to ordinary zero forcing, the skew rule adds only the \(\binom n2\) minimum configurations that omit the same two matching indices on both sides.
