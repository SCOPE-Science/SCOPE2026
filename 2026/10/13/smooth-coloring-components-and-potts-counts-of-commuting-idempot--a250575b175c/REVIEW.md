# Scientific review: commuting-idempotent incidence varieties

**Correctness — PASS.** The simultaneous spectral-idempotent decomposition gives a direct conjugator \(u=\sum_s f_s d_s\in1+J\). This supplies transitivity for every field, not merely finite-field evidence. For a fixed coloring, commuting with all diagonal idempotents cuts out exactly the coefficient positions whose endpoints have equal colors, so the stabilizer and orbit dimensions and rational-point counts follow from exact algebra. Direct linearization of the polynomial defining equations gives one tangent direction per differently colored comparable pair; the orbit has the same dimension, proving scheme smoothness. The Potts expansion is a direct edgewise identity, valid for all prime powers. The standalone verifier exhausts eight small cases (including characteristic two and \(\mathbb F_4\)) and checks all tangent dimensions. The infinite cases rest on the proof, not these experiments.

**Originality — PASS, with material residual risk.** Literature and research-record searches used the aliases simultaneous commuting idempotents, orthogonal-idempotent lifts, split-separable algebra homomorphisms, incidence/pattern-algebra projection varieties, and Potts/comparability-graph counts. The 2024 full manuscript of Dos Santos–Yasumura addresses group gradings and their Peirce blocks, rather than the ordinary commuting-idempotent scheme. Gargate–Gargate 2019 already counts odd-characteristic involutions (equivalent to the one-idempotent specialization), while Gargate–Gargate 2020 counts single potent matrices and does not, on the inspected abstract, establish simultaneous-tuple fibers. Garcés–Khrypchenko 2022 treats idempotent-preserving linear maps. Related research records concern different objects (for example Kronecker-path-algebra commutator fibers). The closest 2026 grading preprint has not been fully read and might contain an unrecognized equivalence, although its abstract does not assert the present geometry/enumerator. Conjugacy of orthogonal idempotent lifts is classical and is explicitly treated as a proof ingredient, not an original theorem; an older direct derivation from such a result is a residual risk.

**Value — PASS.** The object is a canonical moduli scheme of algebra maps from a split finite étale algebra (equivalently, mutually commuting projections). The result completely determines its geometric components and dimensions for every finite poset and every field, and its exact finite-field point counts for every length of tuple. The bridge to the two-variable random-cluster/Tutte polynomial gives a natural combinatorial interpretation and shows what invariant the joint censuses recover. This is a structural classification and natural infinite-family enumerator, not a selected small numerical gain. The proof is short because the incidence radical is tractable; the claim is correspondingly limited to finite-poset incidence algebras.
