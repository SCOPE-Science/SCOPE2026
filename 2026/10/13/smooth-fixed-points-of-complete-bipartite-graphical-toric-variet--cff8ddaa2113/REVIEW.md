# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The alternating ordered-block model gives a canonical bijection with acyclic orientations, the full Hasse cover graph is identified as consecutive complete-bipartite links, and it is a tree exactly for singleton interior blocks when both parts have size at least two. Tree edge differences give a lattice basis, upgrading the known simplicial criterion to integral toric smoothness. Exhaustive exact orientation and transitive-reduction checks pass for six graph sizes, including \(K_{4,4}\); these are corroboration, not an all-parameter proof. Odd/even block counts and the generating function are independently algebraically derived.

**Originality — PASS, with acknowledged proximity risk.** Postnikov–Reiner–Williams already give the general simplicial-chamber/tree-poset criterion, and Ardila already gives alternating blocks for the same complete-bipartite arrangement. Neither inspected source supplies the derived smooth-only enumeration, its bivariate exponential generating function, or the balanced factorial-square limit. Grujić counts all zonotope faces; Carballosa–Khera–Reyes counts acyclic orientations of other constrained types. Thus the evaluated claim is the filtered, labeled, all-parameter enumeration—not its known structural premises. The proximity to earlier combinatorial tools and possible unindexed equivalent counts remain genuine residual risks.

**Value — PASS.** Smooth torus-fixed points are the zero-dimensional torus orbits without quotient or non-simplicial singularities. The exact enumerator gives a complete census of this natural singularity-theoretic subset for every complete bipartite graphical fan. Its bivariate series and balanced scaling go beyond total f-vectors and make comparisons across both color-class sizes possible. This is a natural complete infinite-family count rather than an arbitrary selected finite example; no classification of the entire singular locus is claimed.
