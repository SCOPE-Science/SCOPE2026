For the projective toric variety of the graphical zonotope of \(K_{a,b}\), a torus-fixed point is smooth exactly when its alternating source-removal blocks have singleton interiors; these points have an explicit closed two-parameter count and bivariate exponential generating function. In the balanced case the count is \(2(m!)^2\sum_{j=1}^{m-1}(j!)^{-2}\).
