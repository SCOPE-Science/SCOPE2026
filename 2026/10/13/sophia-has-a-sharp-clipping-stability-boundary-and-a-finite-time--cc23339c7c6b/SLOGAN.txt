On a diagonal positive quadratic with exact current curvature and no moment memory, practical Sophia is globally convergent exactly for \(0<\eta<2\gamma\), and after a finite clipped phase its objective contracts by the exact factor \((1-\eta/\gamma)^2\) per step. At the resonance \(\eta=\gamma\), the update becomes coordinatewise soft-thresholding and reaches the exact minimizer in \(\max_i\lceil |x_{i,0}|/\gamma\rceil\) steps, independently of the curvature spectrum.
