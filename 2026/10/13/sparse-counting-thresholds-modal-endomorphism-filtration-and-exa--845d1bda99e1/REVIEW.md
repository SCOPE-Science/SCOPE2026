# Scientific assessment: sparse counting thresholds on finite S5 frames

## Correctness
PASS. Unital finite Boolean endomorphisms are exactly inverse images of functions. Preservation of the existential modality forces each equivalence class to surject onto an entire target class. Testing a target set of size one less than the isolated threshold eliminates every noninjective large-class surjection, while thresholds above the source-class size are vacuous. The remaining classification proves all enumerative and support formulas by independent source-class choices, inclusion-exclusion, and fixed-point characterization of idempotence. Exhaustive independent finite checks compare the original operator equations with the structural criterion, then verify counts and image ranks. The proof, not the finite tests, covers arbitrary finite frames.

## Originality
PASS with residual priority risk. Known Boolean-modal duality and all-grade multiset-coalgebra morphisms are antecedents but do not assert the single-grade filtration jump condition or exact idempotent support staircase. Earlier work covers ordinary, ungraded finite-S5 retraction spectra, and related work addresses graded term-operation spectra rather than endomorphisms. Primary graded-modal and modal Lovász literature was inspected; the relevant papers investigate counting bisimulation, graded neighbourhoods, or homomorphism-based logical equivalence rather than the stated finite algebra endomorphism census. The plausible 2022 single-modality AiML full paper was inaccessible for complete review, and equivalent older algebraic formulations remain a risk.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The result isolates a sharp structural change when a counting threshold passes an actual equivalence-class size, identifies exactly when the endomorphism monoid grows, and determines the entire cardinality distribution of endomorphism images and idempotent retracts. Such a complete classification of a natural hierarchy of counting expansions is not merely a relabeling of the ungraded case: it distinguishes sparse from cumulative graded languages and detects which block sizes must survive every retraction. The formulas cover all finite class-size vectors without a finite-order cutoff.

## Boundaries
The theorem requires a finite S5 equivalence frame and both modalities \(D_1\) and \(D_k\). It does not treat infinite frames or singleton-modal-signature languages lacking \(D_1\). Finite enumeration is corroborative, not an infinite proof. Publication priority is not independently certified.
