On a finite S5 powerset algebra, preserving existential modality and one isolated counting threshold \(D_k\) permits precisely arbitrary surjections between classes smaller than \(k\), but forces bijections for every larger class. This yields a strictness criterion for the threshold-indexed endomorphism filtration, exact Stirling-number formulas for endomorphisms and image sizes, and a complete staircase description of idempotent supports.
