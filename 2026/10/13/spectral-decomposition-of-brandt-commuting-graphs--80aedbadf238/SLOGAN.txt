For every \(n\ge3\), the commuting graph of the aperiodic Brandt semigroup \(B_n\) has a closed characteristic polynomial with two quadratic spectral blocks and three explicit integral eigenspaces. In particular it is adjacency-nonsingular only at \(n=3\), and its energy is \((n-1)(3n-5+\sqrt{n^2+2n-7})\).
