Every integer \(-r\) with \(r\ge3\) occurs as a **spectrally isolated** flat band of a connected simple unweighted periodic graph with just two vertices per cell, obtained by choosing disjoint-zero two-orbit hopping polynomials. A 15-regular example has the exact spectrum \(\{-3\}\cup[-1,15]\), giving a gap of exactly two to its compactly supported flat band.
