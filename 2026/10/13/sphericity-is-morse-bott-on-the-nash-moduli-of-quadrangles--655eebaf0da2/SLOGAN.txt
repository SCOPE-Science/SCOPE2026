For the Olberding--Walker Nash moduli of planar quadrangles, sphericity is a clean Morse--Bott function: aspect \(-1\) is a codimension-one minimum of index \(0\), aspect \(1\) is a codimension-two maximum of index \(2\), and there are no other critical points. The same statement holds after quotienting by any Nash subgroup of the similarity group.
