On a scalar quadratic, once the first SPI-Optimizer step overshoots the minimizer, its sign gate turns momentum off forever and the method becomes exactly gradient descent. Therefore \(1<r\lambda<2\) converges, \(r\lambda=2\) is a neutral two-cycle, and \(r\lambda>2\) diverges independently of the momentum coefficient, even though ordinary momentum remains stable up to \(r\lambda<2(1+\alpha)\).
