For every size \(n\ge3\), the permanent hypersurface has \(n(n-1)\) distinguished two-zero-row/column linear singular components, and on a dense open of each the hypersurface is Zariski-locally a smooth base times a nondegenerate split quadratic cone. The transverse-node degeneracy divisor is given explicitly by the determinant of the matrix of complementary \((n-2)\)-permanents and has degree \(n(n-2)\).
