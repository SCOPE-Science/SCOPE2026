For the two non-Lie two-dimensional triangular Leibniz bialgebra families in Li--Ma--Wang, quotienting the induced coalgebras by algebra automorphisms reveals cube classes on \([f,f]=e\) and square classes plus two rigid strata on \([f,e]=[f,f]=e\). Over \(\mathbf F_q\), the base-changed tables contain \(q^2+2q-1\) distinct bialgebra structures but only \(2q+1+\gcd(2,q-1)+\gcd(3,q-1)\) isomorphism classes.
