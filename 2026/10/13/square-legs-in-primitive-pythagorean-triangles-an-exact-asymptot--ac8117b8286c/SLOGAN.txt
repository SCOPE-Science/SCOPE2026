Among primitive Pythagorean triangles with hypotenuse at most \(X\), the number with a square leg is asymptotic to \(\frac{3\sqrt2\Gamma(5/4)^2}{\pi^2\Gamma(3/2)}\sqrt X\), with error \(O(X^{1/4}\log(2X))\). Triangles with an even square leg are asymptotically twice as numerous as those with an odd square leg.
