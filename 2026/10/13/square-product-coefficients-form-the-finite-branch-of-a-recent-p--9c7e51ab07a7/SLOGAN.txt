When \(ab\) is a square, the even-exponent equation \(a y^2=b(x^{2n}-c^{2n})\) is not an infinite Pell branch: it has an exact finite signed-divisor parametrization. This is precisely the \(t=1\) boundary of arXiv:2610.00119v1, where the paper's claim that every solvable auxiliary equation has infinitely many solutions fails.
