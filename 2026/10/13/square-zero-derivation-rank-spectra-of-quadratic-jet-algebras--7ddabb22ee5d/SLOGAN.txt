For quadratic jets \(\mathbb F_q[x_1,\ldots,x_d]/(x_1,\ldots,x_d)^3\), the complete rank distribution of ordinary derivations satisfying \(D^2=0\) has an explicit finite-field formula in every characteristic. In odd characteristic these derivations act only from degree one to degree two, but in characteristic two compatible square-zero linear parts raise the sharp maximum rank to \(\lfloor(d+1)^2/4\rfloor\).
