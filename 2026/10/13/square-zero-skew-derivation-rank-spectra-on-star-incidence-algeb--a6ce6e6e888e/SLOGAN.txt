For a star-poset incidence algebra with any involution of its leaves, the complete rank polynomial of square-zero \(\varphi\)-derivations factors into one explicit factor per fixed leaf and transposed pair. Each transposition contributes \((q^2-1)T^2\) beyond the untwisted factor in odd characteristic, but \(q(q-1)T^2\) in characteristic two, yielding uniform operator counts and homology dimensions over every finite field.
