For every finite commutative length-two chain ring with residue field \(\mathbb F_q\), the exact number of square-zero matrices in each pair of Smith strata is a Gaussian-binomial product depending only on \(q\) and matrix size. Conditional on reduction rank \(r\), the radical Smith rank is distributed like the rank of a random \((n-2r)\times(n-2r)\) matrix over \(\mathbb F_q\), so equal- and mixed-characteristic rings have identical distributions.
