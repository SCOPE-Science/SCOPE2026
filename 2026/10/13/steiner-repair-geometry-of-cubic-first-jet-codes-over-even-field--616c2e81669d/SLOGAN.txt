For cubic first-Hasse-jet codes over \(\mathbb F_{2^m}\), all small dual circuits split into derivative triples and a projective-Steiner family of three-value–one-derivative quadruples. Every value coordinate has exactly \(\binom{q-1}{2}\) minimal three-helper recovery sets and sharp disjoint availability \((q-2)/2\), even among repair sets of arbitrary size.
