For the maximal running average \(Z_\infty\) of iid mean-one exponentials, the known inverse CDF hides a strict reliability structure: \(Z_\infty\) is decreasing-failure-rate, with hazard falling from \(2\) to \(1\). The same transform gives every integer moment explicitly, including \(\mathbb E Z_\infty=\pi^2/6\) and \(\operatorname{Var}(Z_\infty)=\pi^2(12-\pi^2)/36\).
