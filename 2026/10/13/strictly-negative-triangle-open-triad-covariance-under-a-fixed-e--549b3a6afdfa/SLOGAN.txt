For every uniformly sampled fixed-edge graph \(G(n,m)\) with at least three present edges and at least two absent edges, triangles and induced open three-vertex paths have strictly negative covariance, with an exact closed expression for every \(n\ge4\). Under independent-edge sampling \(G(n,p)\), their covariance instead is positive below the sharp threshold \(p=2(n-3)/(3n-8)\), revealing an exact dependence reversal between the two random-graph ensembles.
