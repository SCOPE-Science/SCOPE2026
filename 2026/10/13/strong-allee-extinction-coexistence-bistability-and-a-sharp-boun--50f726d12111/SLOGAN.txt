For the strong-Allee exponential Ricker predator–prey map, the entire interval \(4<\alpha<6\) admits simultaneous hyperbolic sinks at extinction and positive coexistence, with exactly two further unstable prey-only states. The upper prey-only state changes from a source to a saddle at the exact threshold \(\alpha_F=(29+\sqrt{41})/8\), without ending the extinction–coexistence bistability.
