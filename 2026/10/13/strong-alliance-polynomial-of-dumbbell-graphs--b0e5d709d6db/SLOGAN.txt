For \(r,s\ge3\), the strong alliance polynomial of the dumbbell \(D_{r,s}\) splits into two one-clique threshold tails and one bridge-connected product. Its coefficient sequence is unimodal only for \((r,s)\in\{(3,3),(3,5),(3,7)\}\) up to interchanging the clique orders; every other dumbbell in the family has an explicit descent followed by a later ascent.
