## Correctness

**PASS.** For a blade vertex of color \(\gamma\), the neighbors of any color \(\beta\) number \(a_\beta+\mathbf1_{\{\beta=\alpha\}}-\mathbf1_{\{\beta=\gamma\}}\), with \(\alpha\) the center color. For \(m\ge3\), a blade cannot be monochromatic, so this is equivalent to the one-blade caps on center and non-center colors. Summing the caps over blades automatically meets the center’s capacity. For \(m=2\), each blade vertex instead requires its mate to avoid the center color, and the center separately forces multiplicities at most \(n\). All complete formulas and the unique \(m=3\) four-color exception follow. The independently rerun graph-level verifier confirms the structure and counts throughout its stated finite range.
