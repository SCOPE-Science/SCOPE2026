For generalized windmills \(W_{n,m}=K_1\vee nK_m\), strong majority coloring has a sharp three-versus-four-color transition: \(\operatorname{Maj}=4\) exactly for three-vertex blades and equals \(3\) for every other blade size at least two. For blades of order at least three, every allowed coloring factors independently by blade and admits an exact truncated-exponential count; two-vertex blades alone retain a global balancing constraint at the center.
