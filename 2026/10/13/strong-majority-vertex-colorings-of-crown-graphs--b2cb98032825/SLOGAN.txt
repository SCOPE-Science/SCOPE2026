For the crown graph \\(\\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M_n\\), a coloring is strong majority exactly when every color appears at most \\(\\lfloor(n-1)/2\\rfloor\\) times on each bipartition side. This gives the sharp \\(3,4,3\\) transition in the strong majority number, the full color-count spectrum, and exact multinomial formulas for all labeled colorings.
