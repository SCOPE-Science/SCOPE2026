For every crown graph \(H_n=K_{n,n}-M\) with \(n\ge3\), every proper strong-odd color class is a singleton, a deleted-matching pair, or—only when \(n\) is even—a complete bipartition side. This determines the entire named-coloring polynomial and yields \(\chi_{\mathrm{so}}(H_n)=n\) for odd \(n\), versus \(2\) for even \(n\).
