The complete strong-odd coloring polynomial of every clique bouquet \(K_1\vee(K_{s_1}\sqcup\cdots\sqcup K_{s_t})\) is an explicit signed binomial sum that counts all named-palette colorings. For uniform blades \(K_1\vee tK_s\), the chromatic number is \(s+1\) for odd \(t\) and \(1+2\lceil ts/(2(t-1))\rceil\) for even \(t\), revealing a sharp hub-parity effect.
