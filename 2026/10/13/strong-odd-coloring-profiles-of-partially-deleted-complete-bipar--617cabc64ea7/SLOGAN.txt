Deleting a nonempty nonperfect matching of size \(t\) from \(K_{m,n}\) forces all matched vertices to be singleton color classes within their sides, while unmatched vertices form odd-sized monochromatic blocks; this yields an exact polynomial counting **every** strong odd coloring. In particular, \(\chi_{\mathrm{so}}=t+2+\mathbf1_{2\mid(m-t)}+\mathbf1_{2\mid(n-t)}\), and restoring just one missing edge of an even crown graph increases this number from \(2\) to \(n+1\).
