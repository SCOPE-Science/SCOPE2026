For every complete multipartite graph \(K_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}\), strong odd colorings are exactly the proper colorings whose color classes all have odd size, so \(\chi_{\mathrm{so}}=r+e\) where \(e\) is the number of even parts. The same characterization gives a closed formula for every labeled-palette coloring count and shows that optimal colorings modulo color permutation are counted by \(2^{\sum_{i:n_i\text{ even}}(n_i-2)}\).
