For every crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n\) with \(n\ge3\), strong resolving sets are exactly the sets meeting every deleted matched pair, and domination removes precisely \(2n\) transversal exceptions. Hence \(R_{srD}(\operatorname{Cr}_n;z)=z^n((z+2)^n-2n)\), \(\gamma_{sr}(\operatorname{Cr}_n)=n\), and there are \(2^n-2n\) minimum sets.
