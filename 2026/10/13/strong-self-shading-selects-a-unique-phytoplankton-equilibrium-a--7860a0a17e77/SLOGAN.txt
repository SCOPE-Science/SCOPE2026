For the nutrient-coupled phytoplankton water-column model of Zhang–Yan–Shi, sufficiently strong Lambert–Beer self-shading forces a unique positive disease-free equilibrium even though general uniqueness was open. Its biomass is \(\kappa^{-1}u_*+O(\kappa^{-2})\) and its nutrient deficit is \(\kappa^{-1}n_*+O(\kappa^{-2})\), where both profiles are uniquely determined and strictly positive.
