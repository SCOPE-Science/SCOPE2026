For every finite complete multipartite graph \(G=K_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}\) with at least two parts and largest part size \(L\), the sub-quorum coloring number is exactly \(\psi_{sq}(G)=\max\{L,2\}\). Any sub-quorum coloring with at least three colors must have every color class meet a largest occupied part of its colored induced subgraph.
