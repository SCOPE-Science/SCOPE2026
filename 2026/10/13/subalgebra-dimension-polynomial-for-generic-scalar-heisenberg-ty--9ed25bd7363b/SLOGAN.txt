For the generic scalar Heisenberg-type Leibniz algebra with \([e_i,f_j]=\delta_{ij}z\) and \([f_i,e_j]=\lambda\delta_{ij}z\), \(\lambda^2\ne1\), every noncentral subalgebra is exactly a central graph over a subdirect product of mutually orthogonal projection spaces. This gives an exact Gaussian-sum polynomial counting embedded subalgebras in every dimension over every admissible finite field, uniformly in \(\lambda\).
