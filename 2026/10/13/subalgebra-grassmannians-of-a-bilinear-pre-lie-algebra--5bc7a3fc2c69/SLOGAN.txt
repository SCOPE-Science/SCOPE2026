For the pre-Lie multiplication \((ae+u)\cdot(be+v)=(2ab+B(u,v))e+av\), a subspace is a subalgebra exactly when it contains \(e\) or is totally isotropic for \(\langle1\rangle\perp B\). Over odd finite fields, this splits the complete subalgebra-dimension polynomial into an ordinary Grassmannian term and an orthogonal polar-space term, making the polynomial independent of the discriminant whenever \(\dim V\) is even.
