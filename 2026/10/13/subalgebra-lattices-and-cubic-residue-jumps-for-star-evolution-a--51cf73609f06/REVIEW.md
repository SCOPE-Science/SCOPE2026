# Scientific review: subalgebras of star evolution algebras

## Correctness — PASS
The multiplication relation gives a two-dimensional product space and a nondegenerate orthogonal splitting when the number of leaves is invertible in the field. Partitioning subalgebras by the dimension of intersection with the product space leads to three mutually exclusive cases. For the line-intersection case, closure forces the cubic equation and then a totally isotropic complement; for trivial intersection, closure forces zero product. The rank polynomials follow by ordinary Grassmannian and classical polar-Grassmannian counts. The quadratic-character and symplectic counting factors were independently reconstructed and checked. Exhaustive direct closure tests in `checker.py` and independent isotropic-space tests in `polar_check.py` completed successfully on their stated finite cases. Risk: the general statement has a necessary characteristic-divisibility boundary, explicitly excluded.

## Originality — PASS, subject to external-literature uncertainty
Full HTML primary texts inspected: arXiv:2111.07399v1 (graph evolution algebra construction and isomorphism focus), arXiv:1710.10516v2 (strong isotopy, isomorphisms and automorphisms), and arXiv:1601.01832v2 (distinction between arbitrary subalgebras and evolution subalgebras; ideals and decomposition). None of their inspected relevant theorem statements yields all three families and the cubic-root/polar-space dimension polynomial. Searches for star graph evolution-algebra subalgebra classifications, including equivalent multiplication and isotropic descriptions, did not reveal such a statement. Related finite-dimensional subalgebra results for other bilinear nonassociative algebras do not imply closure for this star evolution multiplication. Risk: a specialized calculation indexed under a different naming convention may exist, and the full text of arXiv:1405.7126 was unavailable for examination.

## Value — PASS
The result is a complete, field-uniform subalgebra-lattice description for an infinite natural graph-algebra family, not a selected low-dimensional slice. It connects algebraic closure to both ordinary Grassmannians and orthogonal/symplectic polar spaces plus the arithmetic of a cubic equation. The dimension distributions exhibit genuine field dependence and a zero-line phenomenon: the two-leaf star over \(\mathbb F_7\) has no nonzero one-dimensional subalgebra. The explicit count is useful when analyzing embedded subalgebras and subalgebra zeta/dimension invariants of singular graph evolution algebras. It neither classifies arbitrary graph evolution algebras nor asserts a new universal polar-space enumeration.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
