Every subalgebra of the adjacency evolution algebra of a star with \(m\ne0\) in its coefficient field belongs to exactly one of three families: arbitrary superspaces of its two-dimensional product space, cubic-root lines enlarged by totally isotropic leaf subspaces, or zero-product totally isotropic leaf subspaces. Over finite fields this yields a complete subalgebra-dimension polynomial with cubic-residue jumps; in particular, the algebra of the two-leaf star over \(\mathbb F_7\) has no nonzero one-dimensional subalgebra.
