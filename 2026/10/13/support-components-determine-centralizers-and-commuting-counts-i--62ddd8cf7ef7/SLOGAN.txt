For two-step graph Lie algebras with independent edge generators, every centralizer dimension is determined by the components of the support-induced graph and by vertices untouched by the support. Over \(\mathbf F_q\), the complete adjoint-rank spectrum is explicit and the number of ordered commuting pairs is \(q^{2|E|}Q(G;q-1,q+1)\), connecting a natural Lie invariant to the established subgraph component polynomial.
