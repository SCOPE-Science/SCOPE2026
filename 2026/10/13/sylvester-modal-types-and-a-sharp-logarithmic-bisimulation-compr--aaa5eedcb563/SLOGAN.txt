For every fixed branching factor \(q\ge2\) and palette size \(a\ge2\), the maximum possible ordinary-bisimulation quotient of a fully colored complete \(q\)-ary tree on \(N\) vertices has sharp order \(\Theta(N/\log N)\). The exact rooted-modal-type recursion is \(t_{k+1}=a\sum_{j=1}^{\min(q,t_k)}\binom{t_k}{j}\); with binary branching and one propositional bit it yields \(2,6,42,1806,\ldots\), the Sylvester recurrence in disguise.
