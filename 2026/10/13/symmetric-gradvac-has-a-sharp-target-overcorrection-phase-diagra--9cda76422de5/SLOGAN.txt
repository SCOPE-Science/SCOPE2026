When two tasks are vaccinated symmetrically toward the same GradVac target \(\tau\), the final modified pair has cosine \(C(c,\tau)=\cos(\arccos c-2\arccos\tau)\), not generally \(\tau\). The final alignment is maximized at the half-angle target \(\tau=\sqrt{(1+c)/2}\); pushing the target still closer to \(1\) makes the modified gradients cross and their mutual cosine fall back toward the original value \(c\).
