For a finite principal ideal ring whose every local factor has nilpotency length \(\ell_i\ge2\), the ideal-intersection graph has \(\operatorname{Det}=\prod_i(\ell_i+1)-2-(2^r-1)\) and distinguishing number \(D=\prod_i\ell_i-1\). Its full automorphism group is the product of the symmetric groups on the support true-twin cells, extended exactly by coordinate permutations that preserve the nilpotency lengths.
