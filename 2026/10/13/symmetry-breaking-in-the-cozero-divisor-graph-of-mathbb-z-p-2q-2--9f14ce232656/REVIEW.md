# Review

## Correctness

PASS. Principal ideals in \(\mathbb Z/(p^2q^2)\mathbb Z\) are indexed by gcd divisors, and cozero adjacency between two gcd classes is exactly incomparability in the \(3\times3\) exponent grid after deleting the unit and zero corners. This produces seven independent false-twin classes with the stated Euler-totient sizes. The seven-vertex quotient has exactly four automorphisms and no twin vertices. Each of its three nonidentity automorphisms contradicts one elementary weight equality when \(p\ne q\), so all full-graph automorphisms act independently inside the seven classes. The determining and distinguishing formulas then follow from exact symmetric-group actions. The packaged checker exhausts all quotient permutations and directly reconstructs three full modular graphs.

The later full structural article on \(\Gamma'(\mathbb Z_n)\) studies Laplacian spectra, connectivity, and Wiener-type invariants; targeted searches in the accessible full text found no automorphism, determining, or distinguishing result. Exact semantic and formula searches likewise did not locate the accepted symmetry theorem. A prior result for reduced cozero-divisor graphs does not cover \(\mathbb Z/(p^2q^2)\mathbb Z\), which is nonreduced.

## Value

PASS. The balanced two-prime-square ring is the first symmetric nonreduced two-prime divisor grid in which the quotient itself has nontrivial coordinate-type automorphisms. The theorem shows that arithmetic multiplicities destroy every accidental quotient symmetry and converts that rigidity into exact pointwise-base and symmetry-breaking-color formulas. This cleanly separates the symmetry of the divisor-poset quotient from the symmetry of the full ring graph and gives a natural benchmark for \(p^a q^b\).
