For distinct primes \(p,q\), the cozero-divisor graph of \(\mathbb Z/(p^2q^2)\mathbb Z\) has seven maximal false-twin gcd classes and no residual weighted quotient symmetry. Consequently its automorphism group is the direct product of the seven class symmetric groups, its determining number is \(p^2q^2-pq(p-1)(q-1)-8\), and its distinguishing number is \((p-1)(q-1)\max\{p,q\}\).
