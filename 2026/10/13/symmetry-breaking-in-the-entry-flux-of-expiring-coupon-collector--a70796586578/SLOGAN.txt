Uniform draws uniquely maximize the probability of complete coverage in every fixed-length coupon window, but they cease to maximize the rate of newly entering full coverage: for two types the exact transition occurs at window length \(M=4\), and for every fixed alphabet size uniformity eventually becomes a strict local minimum of that entry rate. The optimal two-type entry flux is asymptotic to \(1/(eM)\), exponentially larger than uniform sampling's flux.
