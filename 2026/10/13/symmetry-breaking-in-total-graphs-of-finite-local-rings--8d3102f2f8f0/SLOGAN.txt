For every finite commutative local nonfield with maximal ideal of size \(m\) and residue field of size \(q\), the total graph has determining number \(q(m-1)\) and distinguishing number \(m+1\). The full automorphism group is \(S_m\wr S_q\) in residue characteristic \(2\), and \(S_m\times(((S_m\times S_m)\rtimes C_2)\wr S_{(q-1)/2})\) in odd residue characteristic.
