The two 13-line arrangements in the minimal Numerical Terao counterexample have the same weak combinatorics but sharply different symmetry: the free arrangement has trivial projective stabilizer, while the plus-one-generated arrangement has projective stabilizer \(V_4\). Their full lattice automorphism groups are respectively \(C_2\) and the order-\(8\) dihedral group \(D_8\), with projective index \(2\) in both cases.
