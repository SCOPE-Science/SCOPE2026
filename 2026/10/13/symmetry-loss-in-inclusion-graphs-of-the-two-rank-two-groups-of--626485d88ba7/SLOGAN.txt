For odd \(p\), the inclusion graphs of \(C_{p^2}\times C_p\) and the nonabelian modular group of order \(p^3\) are the same balanced double star, and their group-induced graph automorphism images are the same affine subgroup of order \(p^2(p-1)\). The natural-action kernels differ sharply—\(p(p-1)\) versus \(p\)—so the amount of symmetry lost by the inclusion graph detects the abelian/nonabelian distinction even though the graph and induced image do not.
