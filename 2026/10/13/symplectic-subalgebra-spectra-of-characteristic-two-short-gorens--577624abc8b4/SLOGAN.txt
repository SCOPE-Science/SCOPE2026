For every \(2^e\)-element field and every \(m\ge1\), the short symplectic local Gorenstein algebra with Hilbert function \((1,2m,1)\) has exactly \((m+1)(m+4)/2\) automorphism-orbits of unital subalgebras, with explicit size formulas for every orbit. The entire subalgebra enumeration decomposes into ordinary Grassmannians and \(q^d\)-weighted isotropic Grassmannians, distinguishing embedded nonideal subalgebras from ideals.
