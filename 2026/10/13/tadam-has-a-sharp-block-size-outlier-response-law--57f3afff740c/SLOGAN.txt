At TAdam's exact-inlier pseudo-count equilibrium, an outlier at squared Mahalanobis distance \(D\) contributes only \(\nu/(\nu+\beta D)\) times the corresponding Adam momentum increment, and the robust-mean displacement has the sharp maximum \((1-\beta)\sqrt{\nu/\beta}/2\). With the source default \(\nu=d\), half attenuation of a \(k\)-coordinate equal-standardized outlier begins exactly at \(|z|=\sqrt{d/(\beta k)}\), quantifying the block-size penalty for sparse outliers.
