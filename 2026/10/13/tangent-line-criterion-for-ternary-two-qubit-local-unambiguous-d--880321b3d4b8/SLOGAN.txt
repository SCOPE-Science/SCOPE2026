For three linearly independent two-qubit pure states, local unambiguous failure has an exact geometry: a state is undetectable precisely when the two-dimensional orthogonal-complement line is tangent to the Segre product-state quadric at its state-orthogonal point. In adapted \(2\times2\) matrices this is exactly \(\det C=0\), \(\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{adj}(C)D)=0\), and \(\det D\ne0\).
