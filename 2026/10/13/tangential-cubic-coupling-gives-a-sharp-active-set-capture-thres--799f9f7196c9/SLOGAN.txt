For the two-dimensional strongly convex quartic \(f_\lambda(u,v)=\lambda u+(u^2+v^2)/2+cv^3+Bv^4\) on \(u\ge0\), the minimal SOS-convexified cubic Newton step captures the boundary from \((x,0)\) exactly when \(\lambda\ge3c^2x^3\). At zero multiplier, every positive iterate stays strictly interior despite cubic convergence \(x_{k+1}/x_k^3\to3c^2\): tangential third-order curvature drives a normal-direction regularization barrier.
