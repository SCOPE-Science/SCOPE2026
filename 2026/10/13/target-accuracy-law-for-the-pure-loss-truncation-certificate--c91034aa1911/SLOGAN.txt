For the finite-dimensional pure-loss capacity certificate, the published truncation penalty is asymptotically \(d e^{-\lambda d}\), not a bare exponential. Consequently a certified gap \(\delta\) needs cutoff \(d=\lambda^{-1}[\ln(1/\delta)+\ln\ln(1/\delta)+O(1)]\), and the source decoder inherits an \(O(\log^2(1/\delta))\) accuracy factor at fixed energy.
