For strictly positive equal-census ecological abundance tables, Bray–Curtis and two-row correspondence-analysis chi-square distance cannot strictly reverse pairwise dissimilarity rankings before \(N=10\). At \(N=10\), \((1,3,6)\) versus \((3,1,6)\) loses under Bray–Curtis but wins under chi-square distance against \((1,2,7)\) versus \((2,4,4)\).
