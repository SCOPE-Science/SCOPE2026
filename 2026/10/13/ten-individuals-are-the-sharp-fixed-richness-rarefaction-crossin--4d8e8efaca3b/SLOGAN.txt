At fixed census and fixed species richness, individual-based rarefaction cannot reverse the expected-richness ranking of two positive integer abundance profiles before \(N=10\). The unique first crossing is \((6,2,2)\) versus \((5,4,1)\): the latter wins at sample sizes \(2\) and \(3\), while the former wins at every sample size from \(4\) through \(9\).
