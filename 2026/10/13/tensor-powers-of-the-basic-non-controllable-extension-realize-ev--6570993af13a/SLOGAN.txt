Tensor powers of Iusenko–Primolan's three-vertex non-controllable extension give, for every \(r\ge1\), an extension with \(A_rJ(B_r)=J(A_r)\) and relative-global-dimension gap exactly \(r\). The key base fact is \(\operatorname{pd}_{(A^e,B^e)}A=1\), so the source's tensor lower and upper bounds meet exactly.
