# Verification

The verification separates the fixed base calculation from the infinite tensor argument.

For the base extension, `check_base.py` constructs the five-dimensional path algebra
\[
A=kQ,\qquad Q:\ 1\to2,\ 1\to3,
\]
and the subalgebra generated by \(e_1\), \(e_2+e_3\), and \(\alpha+\beta\). It forms all balancing relations
\[
(xb)\otimes y-x\otimes(by),\qquad b\in B,
\]
inside the \(25\)-dimensional space \(A\otimes_kA\). Exact rational elimination verifies that the balancing-relation space has rank \(16\), so \(\dim(A\otimes_BA)=9\). It then verifies that the nine tensor classes displayed in `RESULT.md` are a quotient basis and that multiplication has rank \(5\), with kernel exactly
\[
\operatorname{span}\{e_2\otimes e_3,\ e_2\otimes\beta,\ e_3\otimes e_2,\ e_3\otimes\alpha\}.
\]
The two two-dimensional blocks are the images of
\[
Ae_2\otimes e_3A
\quad\text{and}\quad
Ae_3\otimes e_2A,
\]
respectively. The checker terminates with `CHECK_OK`.

The infinite statement is proved symbolically. The source gives a module \(M\) with
\[
\operatorname{pd}_{(A,B)}M=1.
\]
The explicit base bimodule calculation proves
\[
\operatorname{pd}_{(A^e,B^e)}A=1.
\]
Theorem 4.9 and Corollary 4.10 of arXiv:2609.31642v1 therefore give matching lower and upper bounds \(r\) for the \(r\)-fold tensor power. Separability of the semisimple quotients gives the tensor radical formula and hence \(A_rJ(B_r)=J(A_r)\).

The finite checker is not used to extrapolate in \(r\). No statement in characteristic \(2\) is verified or claimed.
