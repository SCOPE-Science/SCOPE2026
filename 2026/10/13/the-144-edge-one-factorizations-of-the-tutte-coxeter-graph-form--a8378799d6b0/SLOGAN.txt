All \(144\) one-factorizations of the Tutte–Coxeter graph are symmetry-equivalent, and every proper three-edge-coloring has precisely one Hamiltonian bichromatic cycle; its other two color pairs each split into disjoint \(10\)- and \(20\)-cycles. The \(288\) perfect matchings form two symmetry orbits of size \(144\), distinguished by those complementary cycle types.
