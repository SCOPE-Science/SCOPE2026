The 24 inflection tangent lines of the Klein quartic are in general position in every prime characteristic \(p\ge5\) other than \(7\), giving exactly 276 ordinary intersections. These intersections have Galois orbit lengths \(1^{\times3},3^{\times7},6^{\times42}\), and exactly 276, 24 or 3 become rational over \(\mathbb F_p\) according to \(p\bmod7\).
