For four-part integer partitions with pairwise intersection of at least two parts, the exact maximum family size is \(\lfloor(n-2)/2\rfloor\) for every \(n\ge4\) except the already-known four-member exception at \(n=9\). For \(n\ge30\), every maximizer is a largest full two-star; an explicit thirteen-member bound for nontrivial families and an exhaustive finite census bridge all orders.
