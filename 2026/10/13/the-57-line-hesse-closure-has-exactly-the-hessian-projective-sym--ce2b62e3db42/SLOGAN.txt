The Pokora--Roulleau arrangement \(\mathcal H_{57}\) remembers the Hesse arrangement intrinsically: its twelve lines containing five eightfold points are exactly the original twelve Hesse lines. Consequently its full projective stabilizer is precisely the Hessian group \(G_{216}\cong(C_3)^2\rtimes\operatorname{SL}(2,3)\), of order \(216\).
