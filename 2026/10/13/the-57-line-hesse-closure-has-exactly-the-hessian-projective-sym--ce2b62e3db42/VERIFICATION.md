# Verification

The bundled `verify.py` performs exact arithmetic in the quadratic cyclotomic field generated by a primitive cube root of unity. It uses Python's standard-library `fractions.Fraction` and no floating-point comparisons.

It verifies all 57 source linear forms are projectively distinct; enumerates all 1596 line pairs; groups the intersections projectively; recovers exactly 252 double, 108 triple, 72 quadruple and 21 eightfold points; checks that these multiplicities account for every line pair; counts the eightfold points on every source line; and obtains precisely 12 lines with five, 9 with four and 36 with two. Finally it checks that the 12 five-point lines are set-equal to the three coordinate axes together with the nine lines \(x+\varepsilon^i y+\varepsilon^j z=0\).

A fresh replay used Python 3.13.5 and the standard-library `fractions` module. Running `python verify.py` produced the content stored in `verification_output.txt`.

The computation proves the finite incidence statement for the complete printed 57-line arrangement. The identification of the recovered Hesse arrangement's full projective stabilizer with \(G_{216}\) is supplied by the cited classical theorem, not inferred from the computation.
