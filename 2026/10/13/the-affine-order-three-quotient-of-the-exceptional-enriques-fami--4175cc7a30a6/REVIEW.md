The invariant hypersurface equation is y^2+uv+alpha*s*u^2+s^2*u+s=0. In the complete-intersection Jacobian, the u-v minor reduces to s^3 modulo u^3+sv, forcing s=0 at a singularity; the other minors successively force u=v=0 and the equation gives y=0. At the origin the Jacobian rank is only one, hence it is the unique singular point. Implicit elimination of linear variable s gives initial cubic u^3+uv^2+vy^2, so multiplicity is three, not a claim about global resolutions.
