For the blow-up of a rectangular \(r\times s\) grid in \(\mathbf P^1\times\mathbf P^1\), the sign of \(4-(r-2)(s-2)\) determines whether the anticanonical divisor is big, has Iitaka dimension zero, or is not pseudo-effective. The only zero-wall grids are \(3\times6\), \(4\times4\), and \(6\times3\), with anticanonical section rings generated in degrees \(3,2,3\) respectively; any additional point blow-up destroys all positive anticanonical sections.
