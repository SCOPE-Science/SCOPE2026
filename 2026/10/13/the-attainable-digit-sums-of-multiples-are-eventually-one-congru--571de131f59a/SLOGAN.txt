For \(D_{b,n}=\{s_b(kn):k\ge1\}\), the only eventual obstruction is congruence: if \(g=\gcd(n,b-1)\), then every sufficiently large multiple of \(g\) occurs and no other value can occur. After adjoining \(0\) and dividing by \(g\), the attainable digit sums form a numerical semigroup with an explicit conductor bound.\n
