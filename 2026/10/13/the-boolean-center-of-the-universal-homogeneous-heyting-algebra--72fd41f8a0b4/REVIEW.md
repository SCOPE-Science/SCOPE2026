# Review

## Correctness
PASS. A maximal point in a finite dual yields a Heyting map to \(\mathbf2\); the graph extension \(A\hookrightarrow A\times\mathbf2\) splits every nonzero central element, and universality plus ultrahomogeneity realizes the split over the original subalgebra. Finite center isomorphisms extend in \(L\), giving density, and Boolean-cell supports classify center tuples.

## Originality
PASS. Yamamoto's full text constructs an ambient Boolean algebra and a separate double-negation Boolean reduct, not the complemented-element center. The latter is explicitly not a substructure and has non-dense induced automorphism image. General center and atomless-Boolean-algebra literature cover ingredients but not the intrinsic-center/dense-restriction theorem located here.

## Value
PASS. The center is canonical and definable. The result isolates a fully oligomorphic Boolean locus inside a globally non-Roelcke-precompact homogeneous Heyting algebra and sharply contrasts with Yamamoto's non-dense regular-element representation; the exact tuple-orbit profile is a natural quantitative consequence.

## Closest literature and limitations
Yamamoto 2021/2023 is the closest source; Bezhanishvili 2010 treats Heyting centers generally. The result does not settle injectivity or surjectivity of the restriction map, and the Boolean orbit formula alone is not claimed as novel.
