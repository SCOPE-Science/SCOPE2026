For scalar stochastic heavy ball with iid positive quadratic curvatures, positive momentum increases the mean-square-stable stepsize ceiling exactly when the coefficient of variation is below one. When \(0<v<1\), the unique maximizing momentum is \(\beta_*=(1-v)/(1+v)\); for \(v=0\) the ceiling has an unattained supremum as momentum approaches one, while for \(v\ge1\) zero momentum is uniquely optimal.
