For odd \(n\ge5\), the dihedral quotient of \(\mathbf P^1\times\mathbf P^1\) has \(\operatorname{Cl}=\mathbf Z^2\oplus\mathbf Z/2\), but its full Cartier lattice is only \((2n\mathbf Z)^2\oplus0\). Its two quotient ruling pencils form a Picard basis and the sign-character torsion class is non-Cartier precisely at the four \(A_1\) points.
