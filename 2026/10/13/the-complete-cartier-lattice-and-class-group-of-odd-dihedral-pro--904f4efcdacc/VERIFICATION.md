# Verification and scope

The result follows from a geometric, all-odd-order argument. All nontrivial stabilizers are isolated; removal of the fixed locus gives a finite étale torsor. The class group is the equivariant Picard group, generated by two explicitly linearized ruling bundles and the sign character. On rotation-fixed corners the four eigencharacters force the two degrees divisible by the odd order. At reflection-fixed points the four independent pairs of eigenline signs force both degrees even and the sign twist trivial. Their intersection is exactly the asserted Cartier lattice.

Run `python3 verify.py` to check the character congruences for odd orders 5, 7, 9, 11, and 15 with exact integers. This finite check does not prove the theorem for all odd orders; the preceding symbolic argument does. No other external artifact is needed.
