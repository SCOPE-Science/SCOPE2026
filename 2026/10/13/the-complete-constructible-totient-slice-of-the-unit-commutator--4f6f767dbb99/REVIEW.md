# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The equation and a dyadic totient force \(\varphi(\sigma(n))\) to be dyadic. The Gauss–Wantzel prime-factorization shape is exact. Every Fermat prime \(F_j\) with \(j\ge2\) is eliminated because its \(q+1\) divisor-sum factor has a totient with an odd divisor, proved by the multiplicative-order obstruction for the odd exponent \(2^j-1\). Exhaustive remaining support \(2^a3^\varepsilon5^\delta\) is resolved: odd candidates fail directly, factor \(5\) fails a totient-ratio bound, \(15\) fails a \(3\)-adic or odd-Mersenne-exponent obstruction, and both surviving dyadic rays reduce exactly to the Fermat-prefix lemma. The lemma is proved without any assumption about undiscovered Fermat primes because \(641\mid F_5\) already excludes every longer prefix. All ten converse evaluations follow from the same formula.

## Originality
PASS. OEIS A116083 provides the ten solutions and forward dyadic constructions but not an all-constructible classification. Golomb's manuscript and A092591 provide the closely related complete dyadic-prefix obstruction and hence cover the \(2^a\) subcase. They do not exclude additional Fermat-prime factors in the Gauss–Wantzel class. A003401 classifies that class but does not impose the commutator equation. The inspected De Koninck–Luca full paper treats ratios and densities, not this unit difference. Exact-equation, constructible-index, and totient-two-power searches found no statement implying the full intersection theorem.

## Value
PASS. A116083 conjectures that the displayed ten solutions are all solutions. The Gauss–Wantzel class is a historically canonical and structurally infinite arithmetic stratum, including every known example and potentially unknown Fermat-prime factors. Settling the full conjectured ten-term classification unconditionally on that entire class supplies a meaningful necessary condition on any counterexample: its totient must have an odd prime divisor. This is more than confirming a finite OEIS prefix or specializing an existing dyadic formula.
