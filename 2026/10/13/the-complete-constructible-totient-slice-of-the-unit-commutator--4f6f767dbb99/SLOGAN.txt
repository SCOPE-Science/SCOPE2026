If \(\varphi(n)\) is a power of \(2\), then \(\varphi(\sigma(n))-\sigma(\varphi(n))=1\) holds exactly at the ten previously known examples \(2,6,8,24,128,384,32768,98304,2147483648,6442450944\). Thus every as-yet-undiscovered solution of the unrestricted equation would have a nonconstructible polygon index: an odd prime must divide its totient.
