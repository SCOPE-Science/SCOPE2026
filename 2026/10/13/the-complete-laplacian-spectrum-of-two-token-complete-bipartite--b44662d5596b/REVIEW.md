# Self-review

**Correctness: PASS.** The complete token Laplacian splits, without unstated regularity assumptions, into constant, two shore-standard, mixed-standard, and incidence-kernel sectors. The key \(M_aM_a^{\mathsf T}=(a-2)I+J\) identity is elementary and exact. Its rank discontinuity at \(a=2\) is handled explicitly; each coupled \(2\times2\) block has the tabulated eigenvalues. The dimensions sum to \(\binom{m+n}{2}\), including both size-two boundaries. The Matrix–Tree corollary follows from the simple zero eigenvalue and the product formula. Independent exact characteristic-polynomial calculations cover 36 ordered parameter pairs, and exact cofactor calculations cover five tree counts. Risk: no computational test constitutes the infinite proof; all infinite quantifiers are resolved by the invariant-subspace argument.

Verma (2026) already proves spectral integrality for all cograph token graphs, and this known consequence is explicitly excluded from the novelty claim. Reyes–Dalfó–Fiol (2024) treat quotients and spectral radii; their results do not state or imply the missing entire standard-sector multiplicities. A prior spectral-radius result for precisely this family is acknowledged rather than recycled. Searches for complete token-graph spectra, symmetric squares, and spanning-tree enumeration, including published mathematical research records and the relevant prior findings, did not expose an equivalent expression. Risk: full text of Barik–Verma (2024) was inaccessible; its abstract discusses bounds rather than a full spectrum, but unseen material could cover the claim. Unindexed equivalent results remain possible. These residual risks are real but not presently decisive against the inspected result.

**Value: PASS.** The recently active question asks for more than one extremal eigenvalue of token graphs. A complete two-parameter spectrum, with all multiplicities and sharp \(m=2\) or \(n=2\) changes, is a natural graph-invariant classification: it determines spectral moments, positive-eigenvalue products and exact spanning-tree complexity. The proof identifies the representation sectors that govern the previously missing multiplicities. This is not merely the known cograph-integrality conclusion, nor a routine rephrasing of the previously proved spectral radius. The formula covers an infinite family with arbitrarily unbalanced shores.
