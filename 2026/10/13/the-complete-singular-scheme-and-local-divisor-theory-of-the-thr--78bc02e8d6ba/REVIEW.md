# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** The six augmented permutation-matrix exponents have rank five and the unique primitive relation is the difference of the even and odd permutation triples, recovering Haase--Paffenholz's cubic. Its six partial derivatives are precisely all pair-products within the two triples. Their squarefree, saturated monomial ideal decomposes into the nine coordinate-line primes. The corresponding ring is the tensor product of two one-dimensional Cohen--Macaulay three-axis rings, with Hilbert series \((1+2t)^2/(1-t)^2\), hence degree \(9\), Hilbert polynomial \(9d-3\), arithmetic genus \(4\). Localizing a generic singular line yields an ordinary threefold node times a smooth curve, while at a coordinate point the chart is \(ab-cde=0\). The toric divisor relations compute local class groups \(\mathbb Z\) and \(\mathbb Z^2\), without torsion; normality follows from Serre's criterion. The bundled exact checker replays integer-lattice ranks, 64 support patterns, Hilbert dimensions, and the local divisor-lattice saturation. Its finite tests do not purport to prove the symbolic assertions.

**Originality (PASS; residual risk).** Haase--Paffenholz (arXiv:math/0607194v1, Section 1.3, full HTML inspected) explicitly provides the cubic binomial presentation. The present claim does not claim that equation. Paffenholz (*Faces of Birkhoff Polytopes*, DOI:10.37236/4499, full PDF searched and materially read) gives combinatorial polytope structure; his relevant discussions do not state the complete Jacobian singular scheme, arithmetic genus, or pointwise local class groups. General toric class-group formulae are standard, but they alone do not identify the scheme or its six jumping points. A publicly indexed finding about Wan's Igusa Kuranishi cone gives a different geometry with a projectivized \(K_{3,3}\) Stanley--Reisner curve; it establishes abstract incidence coincidence, not the present Birkhoff identification or local class-group jump. Targeted published and published research index searches did not reveal a stronger equivalent Birkhoff-specific statement. A differently titled or unindexed source may nevertheless contain these straightforward computations, and the Wan comparison could be incomplete because its full report was not read.

**Value (PASS).** The \(3\times3\) Birkhoff polytope is the basic non-simple transportation polytope. The claim gives the complete *scheme-theoretic* singular locus of its canonical toric cubic (not just the singular support), its Cohen--Macaulay intersection geometry and genus, and the exact local Weil-divisor obstruction at every singular point. The contrast between the ordinary node along nine lines and the rank-two class group at their six triple points is a natural invariant of the fourfold, useful when studying its \(\mathbb Q\)-factoriality, toric birational models, and degenerations. This value does not depend on treating the defining binomial or standard divisor exact sequence as novel.

**Boundaries.** The statement concerns the complex fourfold and its singular local rings. No small or crepant resolution is claimed; it does not extend to arbitrary Birkhoff polytopes.
