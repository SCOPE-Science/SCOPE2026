The projective toric fourfold of the \(3\times3\) Birkhoff polytope has a *reduced* singular scheme consisting of nine lines with \(K_{3,3}\) incidence, degree \(9\), and arithmetic genus \(4\). Generic singular-line points are transverse threefold nodes with local class group \(\mathbb Z\), while the six triple intersections have local class group \(\mathbb Z^2\).
