For the elementary symmetric cubic in characteristic \(2\), when \(n\ge5\) and \(n\not\equiv2\pmod4\), the projective singular scheme is supported exactly on indicator vectors of subset sizes \(1\pmod4\), and every point has a smooth quadratic tangent cone. The points are reduced for odd \(n\); for \(4\mid n\), there are exactly \(2^{n-2}\) curvilinear double points forming a complete intersection of \(n-1\) quadrics.
