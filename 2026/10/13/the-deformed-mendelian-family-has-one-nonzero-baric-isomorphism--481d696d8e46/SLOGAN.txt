The deformed Mendelian algebras of Bustamante–Mellon–Velasco have only one nonzero baric isomorphism type: for every \(\varepsilon\ne0\), the family is isomorphic to the fixed evolution algebra \(x^2=-x,\ xy=0,\ y^2=-x+2y\). The fixed algebra has trivial automorphism group and exactly \(2(q-1)^2\) ordered natural bases over \(\mathbf F_q\) for odd \(q\).
