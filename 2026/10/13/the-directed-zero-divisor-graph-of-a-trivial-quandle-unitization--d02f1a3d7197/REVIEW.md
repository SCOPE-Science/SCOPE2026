# Scientific review: directed zero-divisor graph of a trivial-quandle unitization

## Correctness — PASS

The proof is self-contained: the multiplication \(uv=\varepsilon(v)u\) converts under a linear change of basis into \((\lambda,\mu,w)(\tau,\nu,z)=(\lambda\tau,\mu\nu,\lambda z+\nu w)\). This yields the complete matched-block adjacency, bidegree spectrum, automorphism group, diameter, and parameter recovery. Boundaries \(q=2\) and \(n=2\) are included. Finite-field exhaustive computations cover nine small cases, including \(\mathbb F_4\); they do not serve as a general proof. The full graph automorphism groups were also directly enumerated for \((q,n)=(2,2),(2,3),(3,2)\), with orders \(2,144,768\).

## Originality — PASS, with residual historical risk

Churchill et al. (arXiv:2608.09149v1) introduce the graph, handle finite-field zero-divisor criteria, and give binary-field graph degree patterns. Li and Tucci (2013) and Fenstermacher–Gegner (2014) already treat ordinary \(2\times2\) upper triangular cases. Those boundaries are credited. The present complete general \(n\ge3\) matched-block graph and wreath-product automorphism classification, including \(q>2\), was not located in the compared primary statements or mathematical-result searches. Earlier generalized triangular-ring graph results could give equivalent statements; inaccessible full texts leave a real historical risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result settles a natural complete graph-classification problem motivated by the 2026 quandle-ring investigation. It gives all directed edges, every degree multiplicity, all graph symmetries, directed diameter and a graph-to-algebra-parameter recovery theorem for an infinite two-parameter family. The new \(n\ge3\) many-arrow case is not the classical full upper-triangular matrix algebra.

## Limits

Only finite fields and formal unitizations of trivial quandles are covered. The literature comparison is not an independent certification. No external replication or independent mathematical audit has been performed.
