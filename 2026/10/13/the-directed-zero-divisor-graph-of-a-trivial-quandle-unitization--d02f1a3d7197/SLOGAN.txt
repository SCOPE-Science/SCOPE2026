The directed zero-divisor graph of the formal unitization of a trivial-quandle algebra over \(\mathbb F_q\) is a matched-block blow-up whose entire automorphism group is \(\big((\mathfrak S_{q-1}\times\mathfrak S_{q-1})\wr\mathfrak S_{q^{n-1}}\big)\times\mathfrak S_{q^{n-1}-1}\). Its bidegree spectrum recovers both the field order and the quandle cardinality, and its directed diameter is exactly \(3\) for every \(n\ge2\).
