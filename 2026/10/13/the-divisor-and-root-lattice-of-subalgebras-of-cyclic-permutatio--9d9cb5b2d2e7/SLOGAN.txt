For the evolution algebra of an unweighted directed cycle, every nonzero linear subalgebra is a simple weighted repetition code indexed uniquely by a divisor \(d\mid n\) and a root \(c^{(2^n-1)/(2^d-1)}=1\). This yields the entire subalgebra containment lattice and the exact finite-field dimension polynomial \(1+\sum_{d\mid n}\gcd(q-1,(2^n-1)/(2^d-1))T^d\), in every characteristic.
