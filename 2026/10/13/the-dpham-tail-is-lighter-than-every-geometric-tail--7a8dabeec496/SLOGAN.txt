The DPham survival is \(\Pr(X\ge n)=\exp\{1-\mu^{n^\alpha}\}\), so its adjacent-tail ratio tends to zero and every positive exponential moment is finite. Its extremes grow only on the scale \(\{\log\log m/\log\mu\}^{1/\alpha}\), which rules out both heavy-tailed and long-tailed asymptotics for every admissible parameter pair.
