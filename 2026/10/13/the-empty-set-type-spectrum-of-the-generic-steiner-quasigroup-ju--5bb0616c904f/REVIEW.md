# Review

## Correctness
PASS. Barbina–Casanovas Proposition 2.7 gives completeness and quantifier elimination for \(T^*_{\mathrm{Sq}}\), while Theorem 3.3 gives exactly continuum many complete \(3\)-types over \(\varnothing\). In one variable every term collapses to the variable. In two variables every term collapses to \(x\), \(y\), or \(x\cdot y\); when \(x\ne y\) those are three distinct elements with their whole multiplication table forced by the Steiner identities. Hence equality versus inequality are the only two quantifier-free pair types and, by quantifier elimination, the only two complete pair types. Repeating coordinates embeds the continuum family of \(3\)-types into every higher arity.
