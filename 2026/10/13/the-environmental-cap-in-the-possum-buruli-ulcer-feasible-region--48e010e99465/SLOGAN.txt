The published Buruli-ulcer feasible region uses \(E\le\alpha_E/\mu_E\), but the model only guarantees \(I_m\le\Lambda_m/\mu_m\). The smallest rectangular environmental cap is therefore \(E\le\alpha_E\Lambda_m/(\mu_E\mu_m)\); with the paper's own parameters, a point on the printed \(E=1\) boundary has \(\dot E=1/14>0\).
