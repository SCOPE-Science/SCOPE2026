For the Lucas recurrence \(U_{n+2}=PU_{n+1}-QU_n\), the smallest positive scalar making \((M U_{n^{2s}})\) a Dold sequence is exactly \(\operatorname{rad}(|P^2-4Q|)\), simultaneously for every \(s\ge1\), whenever the discriminant is nonzero. If \(P^2=4Q\), no positive scalar works; repeated prime factors of a nonzero discriminant are entirely unnecessary.
