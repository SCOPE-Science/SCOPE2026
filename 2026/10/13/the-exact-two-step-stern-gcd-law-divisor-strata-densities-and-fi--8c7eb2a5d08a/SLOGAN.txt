For Stern's diatomic sequence, the gcd \(\gcd(s(n),s(n+2))\) on the stratum \(v_2(n+1)=r\) takes exactly the divisors of \(2r+1\), with explicit positive relative frequencies. This yields the complete positive-odd gcd distribution and a sharp first-occurrence cutoff \(3\cdot2^{(d-1)/2}-1\) for each odd \(d\ge3\).
