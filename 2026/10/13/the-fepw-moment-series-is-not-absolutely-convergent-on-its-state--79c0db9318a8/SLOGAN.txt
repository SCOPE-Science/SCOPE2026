The FEPW moment series of Eq. (3.2) is not absolutely convergent on its stated positive parameter space: at \(r=\tau=\eta=\kappa=\varphi=1\), the entire \(i=1\) row equals \(-e\). The true moment is nevertheless finite, and on \(\eta=\varphi=p\) it admits a convergent transformed integral, with \(\mu_p=e\tau^{-p}\Gamma(- (\kappa/\tau)^p,1)\).
