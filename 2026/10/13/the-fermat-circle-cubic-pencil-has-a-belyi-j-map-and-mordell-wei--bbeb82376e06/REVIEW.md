# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The genus-one surface is defined by a cubic pencil with nine base points counted with multiplicity. Three are distinct points on the line at infinity, two are simple affine points, and the remaining two proper points are double intersections, each requiring a second blow-up. Explicit local coordinates show that the \(t=-3/2\) fiber acquires two exceptional bridges, producing an \(I_4\) cycle rather than an \(I_2\) configuration. Finite critical-point elimination detects all other singular members; the line–conic factorizations and transverse intersections identify two \(I_2\) fibers and four \(I_1\) fibers. Adjunction excludes vertical exceptional \((-1)\)-curves. The binary-quartic invariant identities give the stated \(j\)-function and Belyi passport; the Picard number and Shioda–Tate formula yield geometric rank \(3\). All critical polynomial equalities were checked from the packaged script. The script is corroboration rather than a substitute for the geometric proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

The full text of Falkensteiner–Sendra, arXiv:2301.04933, was examined at Example 7.2 and by targeted whole-document searches. It states the underlying linear homotopy and a genus-specialization analysis, not the resolved elliptic surface, Belyi \(j\)-map, precise Kodaira fibers, or rank. Schütt–Shioda, arXiv:0907.0298, supplies the general formulas and background; it does not identify this particular pencil. Miranda's classification catalogues fiber combinations on some rational elliptic surfaces; it is not a parameter-resolved description of this named pencil. Searches for the curve equation, equivalent homotopy parameters, complete fiber configuration, and explicit rational \(j\)-map revealed no directly equivalent statement. Prioritization remains provisional: an unindexed pencil calculation, algebraically equivalent Weierstrass normal form, or a general-purpose catalogue could conceal partial or complete earlier coverage. An independent literature audit has not occurred.

## Value — PASS

The computation ties a published and natural deformation of two elementary plane curves to a sharply specified rational elliptic surface, producing its full degeneration configuration and geometric Mordell–Weil rank. The two infinitely near base points are essential: they change the distinguished reducible fiber to \(I_4\). The Belyi property and its complete ramification passport add arithmetic/dessins structure not implied by knowing that the generic plane member has genus one. These are exact complete invariants rather than a routine rephrasing of the source's genus statements.
