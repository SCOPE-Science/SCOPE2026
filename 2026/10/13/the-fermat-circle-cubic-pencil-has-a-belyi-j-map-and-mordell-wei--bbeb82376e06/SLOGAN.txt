Resolving the base points of the Fermat-cubic/unit-circle pencil gives a rational elliptic surface with fibers \(I_4+2I_2+4I_1\) and geometric Mordell–Weil rank \(3\). Its degree-\(12\) modular \(j\)-map is Belyi, with ramification partitions \((12)\), \((2^6)\), and \((4,2,2,1^4)\) over \(0\), \(1728\), and \(\infty\).
