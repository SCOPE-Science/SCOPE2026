For a fixed finite population in noisy utility-weighted pairwise opinion dynamics, the rigorous weak-noise mean process has Gibbs exponent \((N-1)/(1-\mu)\), not the leading large-population approximation \(N/(1-\mu)\). The one-agent shift follows from an exact random-pair disagreement covariance and a finite-time diffusion limit; no microscopic stationary-law limit is asserted.
