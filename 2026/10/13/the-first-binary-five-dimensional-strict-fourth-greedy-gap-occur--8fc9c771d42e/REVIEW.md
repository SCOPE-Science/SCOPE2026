If \(b\) is the largest multiplicity on a maximum line, the binary line through it and a global maximum point has mass at least \(M+b\) but is strictly below \(L\), while the maximum line itself has mass at most \(3b\). This forces \(M\ge3\). Rank five then requires two further positive points outside the span of the maximum point and line, yielding \(n\ge11\). Exact replay verifies the length-eleven equality construction, \(d_4=8\), and \(g_4=9\).
