For binary order-five de Bruijn cycles, reflection complexity at the first non-universal factor length, six, assumes exactly five values: \(22,25,26,28,29\), attained by \(576,960,192,256,64\) cycles respectively. A feedback-reversal identity \(r(6)=28-2A+Z\) explains the statistic, and exhaustive independent enumerations certify its complete spectrum.
