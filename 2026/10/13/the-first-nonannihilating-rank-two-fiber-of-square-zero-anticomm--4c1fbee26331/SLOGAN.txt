For a fixed rank-two square-zero \(n\times n\) matrix \(X\) over \(\mathbb F_q\), exactly \((q^2-1)q^{2n-6}S_{n-4}(q)\) square-zero matrices \(Y\) anticommute with \(X\) and have \(XY\ne0\), where \(S_s(q)\) counts square-zero \(s\times s\) matrices. Every such product has rank one, and rank two is the smallest possible rank of \(X\) admitting a nonzero product.
