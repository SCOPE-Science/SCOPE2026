# The first punctual Quot correction is determined by the tangent cone

## Finding
Let \(q\) be any prime power and let \(S=\mathbb F_q[[X,Y]]\), with maximal ideal \(\mathfrak m=(X,Y)\). Fix a nonzero series \(f\in\mathfrak m^a\setminus\mathfrak m^{a+1}\) with integer \(a\ge2\), and write \(f_a(X,Y)\) for its homogeneous initial form of degree \(a\). Put \(R=S/(f)\). For a local ring \(A\), define
\[
 Q_m(A,n;q)=\#\{L\subseteq A^n:\dim_{\mathbb F_q}(A^n/L)=m\},
\]
where submodules are understood to have finite colength and the corresponding quotient is supported at the closed point. These are the \(\mathbb F_q\)-points of the reduced punctual Quot space.

For every integer \(n\ge1\), all coefficients up to colength \(a\) agree:
\[
 Q_m(R,n;q)=Q_m(S,n;q)\qquad(0\le m\le a).
\]
At the first potentially distinguishing colength, \(m=a+1\), there is an exact universal correction:
\[
 \boxed{Q_{a+1}(S,n;q)-Q_{a+1}(R,n;q)
 =\bigl(q+1-N_q(f_a)\bigr)q^{an-1}\frac{q^n-1}{q-1},}
\]
where
\[
 N_q(f_a)=\#\{[u:v]\in\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_q):f_a(u,v)=0\}.
\]
Thus the initial discrepancy is determined by the number of rational tangent-cone directions, regardless of all higher-order coefficients of \(f\). In particular, the family of rank-dependent punctual Quot counts over all finite extensions determines the Frobenius point counts of the reduced projective tangent cone.

For coprime integers \(2\le a<b\), the torus-knot local ring \(R_{a,b}=\mathbb F_q[[X,Y]]/(Y^a-X^b)\) has exactly one rational tangent direction and hence satisfies the strict first-deviation formula
\[
 \boxed{Q_{a+1}(S,n;q)-Q_{a+1}(R_{a,b},n;q)
 =q^{an}\frac{q^n-1}{q-1}>0.}
\]
This correction is independent of \(b\), while the later coefficients can depend on both semigroup generators.

## Assumptions and scope
The field is \(\mathbb F_q\), with no restriction on its characteristic. The power series \(f\) need not be reduced or irreducible. The integers \(a\ge2\), \(n\ge1\) are arbitrary; \(m\) denotes the quotient's \(\mathbb F_q\)-vector-space length. The comparison is of sets of rational closed points, **not** an assertion that the two Quot schemes have equal nonreduced structures or equal infinitesimal deformation functors. Write \([n]_q=(q^n-1)/(q-1)\) for the number of rational points of \(\mathbb P^{n-1}\).

## Proof
**1. The earliest possible obstruction.** Any finite-length quotient \(M\) of \(S^n\), of length \(m\), satisfies \(\mathfrak m^mM=0\) by the descending \(\mathfrak m\)-adic filtration and Nakayama's lemma. For \(m\le a\), \(f\in\mathfrak m^a\) annihilates every such quotient, hence the quotients from \(S^n\) and \(R^n\) are in bijection. At \(m=a+1\), the excluded quotients are precisely those for which \(fM\ne0\), hence \(\mathfrak m^aM\ne0\).

**2. Only cyclic curvilinear quotients can be excluded.** For \(\mathfrak m^aM\ne0\), every one of the \(a+1\) successive layers
\[
 M/\mathfrak mM,\ \mathfrak mM/\mathfrak m^2M,\ldots,\mathfrak m^aM
\]
is nonzero. Since the total length is exactly \(a+1\), each layer has dimension one. In particular \(M/\mathfrak mM\) is one-dimensional, so \(M\) is generated by one element and is a local quotient algebra \(S/I\). Its embedding dimension is one; choosing a generator \(t\) of its maximal ideal gives \(M\cong\mathbb F_q[t]/(t^{a+1})\). Conversely, every quotient of this form has \(\mathfrak m^aM\ne0\). Therefore no noncyclic or noncurvilinear quotient is lost.

**3. Classifying the curve ideals and tangent obstruction.** The curvilinear colength-\(a+1\) ideals in \(S\) have a unique tangent direction \([u:v]\in\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_q)\), describing the linear coefficients of \(X\) and \(Y\) in the parameter \(t\). For the chart where \(u\ne0\), reparametrizing by \(X=t\) gives the unique presentation
\[
 X\longmapsto t,\qquad Y\longmapsto c_1t+c_2t^2+\cdots+c_at^a,
\]
with \(c_1=v/u\) fixed and \(c_2,\ldots,c_a\) arbitrary. The opposite chart has the analogous unique presentation with \(Y=t\). Thus every rational projective tangent direction supports exactly \(q^{a-1}\) curvilinear quotient ideals. On any such algebra the relation acts as
\[
 f\longmapsto f_a(u,v)t^a\pmod {t^{a+1}},
\]
up to multiplication by a nonzero \(a\)-th power arising from a choice of parameter. Consequently the quotient is excluded exactly when \(f_a(u,v)\ne0\). The number of excluded cyclic ideals is \((q+1-N_q(f_a))q^{a-1}\).

**4. Counting higher-rank framings.** Fix one excluded cyclic quotient \(M\cong\mathbb F_q[t]/(t^{a+1})\). An \(S\)-linear homomorphism \(S^n\to M\) is surjective precisely when at least one of the \(n\) chosen images is a unit. There are \(q^{(a+1)n}-q^{an}\) such ordered frames. The automorphism group of the cyclic \(S\)-module is its unit group, of cardinality \(q^{a+1}-q^a\), and acts freely by postcomposition. Hence the number of distinct quotient kernels lying over the given cyclic ideal is
\[
 \frac{q^{(a+1)n}-q^{an}}{q^{a+1}-q^a}
 =q^{a(n-1)}\frac{q^n-1}{q-1}.
\]
Multiplying by the excluded cyclic-ideal count gives the boxed identity. For \(f=Y^a-X^b\) with \(b>a\), the initial form is \(Y^a\), whose zero set in \(\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_q)\) consists of the unique direction \([1:0]\). Therefore \(q+1-N_q(f_a)=q\), yielding the torus-knot formula and its strict positivity. \(\square\)

## Verification
A standalone exact enumerator, `check_first_quot.py`, independently classifies all commuting nilpotent \(3\times3\) matrix pairs over \(\mathbb F_2\), counts generating frames of ranks one and two, divides by the \(168\)-element change-of-basis group, and checks the predicted first corrections for four initial forms. It returns `FIRST_QUOT_CHECK_OK`.

For colength three the ambient smooth-plane counts are respectively \(7\) and \(99\) in ranks one and two. The cusp initial form \(Y^2\) gives \((3,51)\); split-node initial form \(XY\) gives \((5,75)\); and the anisotropic form \(X^2+XY+Y^2\) gives \((1,27)\). These differences equal the formula with respectively one, two, and zero rational projective tangent directions. The computation is exhaustive only for those small finite-field cases; it is not a substitute for the all-\(q\), all-\(n\), all-\(a\) proof above.

## Relationship to prior work
Huang--Jiang--Oblomkov (arXiv:2608.16086v1, first public August 17, 2026) compute an affine paving and motivic series for \(\operatorname{Quot}_m^R(\widetilde R^{\oplus n})\) over torus-knot singularities. They distinguish the harder free-singular-module spaces \(\operatorname{Quot}_m^R(R^{\oplus n})\), whose individual point counts are not determined there for \(a>2\). Their functional specialization does not evaluate the coefficient addressed here.

Huang--Jiang (arXiv:2312.12528, first public December 19, 2023) prove rationality and explicit arbitrary-rank series for multiplicity-two \(Y^2=X^b\) singularities; these cover some special cases of the present formula but not its universal arbitrary-multiplicity/tangent-direction statement. Their earlier cusp paper (arXiv:2305.06411) supplies lower-rank special cases. Graffeo--Monavari--Moschetti--Ricolfi (DOI 10.1112/plms.70140, published March 20, 2026) compute initial coefficients for *smooth ambient* punctual Quot schemes, not the correction imposed by an arbitrary plane-curve equation. The present theorem determines the first relation-sensitive coefficient without assuming an evaluated formula for the ambient coefficient.

## Limitations
The equality and correction are **point-count identities over finite fields**. They do not assert equality of schematic punctual Quot functors, equality of motivic classes in arbitrary characteristic, affine pavings, or polynomiality of all subsequent Quot coefficients. In the general tangent-cone formula the first correction can vanish if \(f_a\) vanishes at every rational projective direction; positivity is proved for the torus-knot specialization, not for every plane curve. The formula records only the number of rational tangent directions at each extension degree and does not recover multiplicities of tangent lines. Earlier implicit equivalent observations in low-length Hilbert-scheme literature remain a bibliographic risk.
