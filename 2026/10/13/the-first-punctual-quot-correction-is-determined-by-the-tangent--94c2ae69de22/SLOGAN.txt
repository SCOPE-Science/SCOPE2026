For every rank, the punctual Quot point counts of a plane-curve singularity agree with those of the smooth plane through colength equal to the curve's multiplicity, and their first possible discrepancy is exactly the number of excluded rational tangent directions times a universal Gaussian-binomial factor. For the torus-knot germ \(Y^a=X^b\) with \(2\le a<b\), this first discrepancy is \(q^{an}(q^n-1)/(q-1)\), independent of \(b\).
