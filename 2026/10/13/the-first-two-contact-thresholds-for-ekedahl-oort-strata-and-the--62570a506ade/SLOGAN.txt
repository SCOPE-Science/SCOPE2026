For every odd prime and \(g\geq3\), the first Ekedahl–Oort stratum disjoint from the supersingular locus occurs at dimension \(\lfloor g/2\rfloor+1\), and it is unique at that dimension. The first stratum that meets the supersingular locus without lying entirely in it appears one dimension later, again uniquely; both boundary types are explicit in elementary-sequence coordinates.
