The general-position subsets of a rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) correspond exactly to edge-selected star forests of \(K_{m,n}\), giving the complete two-parameter enumerator \(\sum_{m,n\ge0}\Psi_{m,n}(z)u^mv^n/(m!n!)=\exp(ue^{vz}+ve^{uz}-uvz)\). An explicit polynomial recurrence determines every coefficient, and for \(m,n\ge3\) exactly \(mn\) subsets attain the already known maximum size \(m+n-2\).
