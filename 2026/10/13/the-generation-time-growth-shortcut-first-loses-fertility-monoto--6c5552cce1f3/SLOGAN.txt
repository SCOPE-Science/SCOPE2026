In a two-age positive-integer life table, the shortcut \(\widetilde r=\log(R_0)/T\) first violates fertility monotonicity at \((5,1)\to(5,2)\): true Euler–Lotka growth increases, but the shortcut decreases. The failure is exact, uniquely first at initial \(R_0=6\), and certified by \(6^{54}>7^{49}\).\n
