For the generic digraph without 2-cycles, quotienting ordered tuples by mutual bimorphism reachability turns each \(k\)-distinct-coordinate stratum into the product poset \(V^{\binom{k}{2}}\), where \(V\) is the three-state fork \(0<+,0<-\). This yields exact strong-orbit, rank, comparability, cover, and Möbius formulas in every arity.
