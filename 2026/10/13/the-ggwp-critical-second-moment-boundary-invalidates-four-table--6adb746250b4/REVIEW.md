## Correctness
PASS. The source's own survival theorem gives \(\Pr(T>t)\sim C t^{-1/\beta}\) with \(C>0\). The tail-integral identity proves that the moment of order \(r\) exists exactly for \(r<1/\beta\), and at equality its tail integral diverges logarithmically. At \(\beta=1/2\), the mean is finite but the second moment and variance are infinite. Therefore finite CV, standardized skewness, and standardized kurtosis are unavailable.
