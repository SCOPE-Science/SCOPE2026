# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In the single-operation signature, every term coefficient vector sums to 1. Translate the set of realizable vectors by the last projection. It then contains zero; finiteness and invertibility of T and 1−T give closure under the inverses of both multiplications. Applying the binary operation to the two inverse images yields addition. The resulting additive subgroup is a Z[T]-module containing all coordinate differences, so every affine vector is realizable. An equation is the kernel of the coefficient-difference map M^k → M, with image IM for the ideal generated by its coefficients. Every ideal is realized by equating a term with coefficients (r₁,…,rₘ,1−Σrᵢ) to its last variable, for generators rᵢ of the ideal. A nonzero nonunit of the faithful coefficient ring has nonzero proper image: surjectivity on the finite group would give an inverse that is a power and hence belongs to R. This gives the two-value criterion for |M|>1; the trivial quandle has just {1}. Over F_p, primary decomposition and the dimensions of the images of powers of each irreducible give the displayed spectrum. The general proof does not depend on the frozen binary-term examples. An independent bounded check at /workspace/audit_evidence/replay.py (quandles) gives principal image sizes {1,3,27} versus general ideal image sizes {1,3,9,27} on the regular module for R=(Z/9)[ε]/(3ε,ε²), T=2+ε.
