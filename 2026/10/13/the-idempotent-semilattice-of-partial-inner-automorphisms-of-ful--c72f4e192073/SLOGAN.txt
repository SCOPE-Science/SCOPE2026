For the partial inner automorphism monoid of \(\operatorname{End}_K(K^n)\), every nontrivial idempotent is uniquely the identity on a rectangular algebra \(\operatorname{Hom}_K(V/W,U)\), where \(U,W\) are arbitrary nonzero proper subspaces. Over \(\mathbb F_q\), the full idempotent count is \(2+(\sum_{r=1}^{n-1}{n\brack r}_q)^2\), and the square-zero domains are exactly those with \(U\subseteq W\).
