For the natural quotient \(Y=\operatorname{Jac}(C)/\operatorname{Aut}(C)\) of the Klein-quartic Jacobian, the reduced singular locus has five curve components with generic transverse types \(A_1,A_1,A_1,A_2,A_3\), plus two isolated \(\frac1{7}(1,2,4)\) points. Upstairs, those five curves come from \(112\) elliptic fixed curves split into \(3+1+1\) group-orbits.
