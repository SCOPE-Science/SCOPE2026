The two published conic-line arrangements share weak counts \((6,1;6,3,2)\) but not the later strong combinatorial type: their unique conic meets singular points with profiles \((4,1,2)\) and \((2,2,2)\) in multiplicities \((2,3,4)\). Thus this canonical example is a weak Ziegler pair but not a strong one in the modern \(W_C\) sense.
