In the paper's own packet-versus-complement comparison, a correlation-aware packet edge is no more expensive than its logical complement for every \(n<d\) whenever \(p_{\mathrm{net}}\ge p_{\mathrm{gate}}\); \(\lceil d/2\rceil\) is not a threshold of that argument. Any genuine cutoff below \(d\) must therefore come from other matching classes or fuller noise geometry, not from the displayed two-class weight comparison.
