In the \(M_{72}\) non-commutative Fano minifold, right duality preserves the anticanonical Hilbert polynomial for the two outer exceptional objects but changes it for the two middle ones from \(12p^3+18p^2+8p+1\) to \(48p^3+72p^2+26p+1\). Thus the Hilbert dimension stays \(3\), while the leading anticanonical growth coefficient can jump by a factor of \(4\) inside one geometric-helix mutation class.
