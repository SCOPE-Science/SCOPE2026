Martinez-Maure's minimal-degree noncircular constant-width octic is rational, and its complete complex singularity basket is \(3E_6+6A_1+2A_6\). The three \(E_6\) points are the finite real curvature-degeneracy points, while six hidden nonreal nodes and two \(A_6\) points at infinity account for the rest of the plane-octic genus defect.
