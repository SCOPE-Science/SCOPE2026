The published three-dimensional tridendriform derivation-triviality claim fails on its first model: \(\mathcal{DT}_3^1\) has the three-dimensional derivation algebra \(\left\{\begin{pmatrix}a&0&0\\b&2a&c\\0&0&a\end{pmatrix}\right\}\). Its derivation Lie algebra is \(K^2\rtimes K\), with the one-dimensional factor acting by scalar multiplication.
