In Nakamura--Shibata's threefold \(X_m\), the singular and non-klt locus is one affine line on which every closed point has minimal log discrepancy \(1\), but the canonical index is \(1\) everywhere on the punctured line and jumps to \(m\) at the origin. Thus arbitrarily large canonical index appears as an isolated jump inside an mld-constant singular stratum.
