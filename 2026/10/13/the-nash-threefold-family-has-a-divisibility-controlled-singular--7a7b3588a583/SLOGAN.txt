For the characteristic-dividing-degree triple-axis covers \(w^n=abc\), the first normalized Nash blowup changes from isolated singularities at \(n=2\) to exactly two or four singular toric curves per affine chart, with the curve indices given by \(n/\gcd(n,2)\) and \(n/\gcd(n,6)\). Its canonical divisor is Cartier exactly for \(6\mid n\), \(\mathbb Q\)-Cartier of index \(n\) exactly when \(n\) is odd and prime to \(3\), and otherwise not \(\mathbb Q\)-Cartier.
