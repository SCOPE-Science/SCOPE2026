For the Tran--Nguyen witness ideal \(I\), every power is exactly \(I^n=D+\iota(\mathfrak m^n)\), so \(R/I^n\cong k[x,y,z]/(x,y,z)^n\) while \(\bigcap_{n\ge1}I^n=D\neq0\). Consequently \(R_I\cong k[x,y,z]_{(x,y,z)}\), \(\operatorname{gr}_I(R)\cong k[x,y,z]\), and \(\widehat R^{\,I}\cong k[[x,y,z]]\): the global zero-divisor obstruction disappears under localization and every finite \(I\)-adic truncation, surviving exactly as the nonzero completion kernel \(D\).
