The characteristic-two quartic \(Y^4+XZ^3=0\) has an unexpectedly non-smooth projective stabilizer scheme: \(\operatorname{Spec}k[b,b^{-1},v,u]/(u^4)\), with matrices \(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}b^4&0&v^4\\u&b&v\\0&0&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)\). Its ordinary affine-group symmetries hide an \(\alpha_4\) infinitesimal subgroup and an extra Lie direction.
