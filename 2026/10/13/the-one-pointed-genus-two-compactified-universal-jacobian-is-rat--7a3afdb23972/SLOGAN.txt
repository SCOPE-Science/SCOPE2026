For the one-pointed genus-two fine compactified universal Jacobian, the even-degree Poincaré polynomial \(1+5t+15t^2+20t^3+15t^4+5t^5+t^6\) is irreducible over \(\mathbf Z\). Hence its rational cohomology cannot come from any nontrivial product of positive-dimensional smooth proper complex factors, even though separating-node strata admit product descriptions.
