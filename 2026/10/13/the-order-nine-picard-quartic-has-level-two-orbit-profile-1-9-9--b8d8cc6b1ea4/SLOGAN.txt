For the unique plane quartic with full automorphism group \(C_9\), the 28 bitangents split as \(1+9+9+9\), and the three free orbits are exactly the three \(u=a^3\) fibers of Liang's cubic, which generate \(\mathbf Q(\zeta_9+\zeta_9^{-1})\). The same automorphism acts freely away from zero on \(\operatorname{Jac}(C)[2]\), forcing four \(9\)-orbits of Cayley octads and seven \(9\)-orbits of Steiner complexes.
