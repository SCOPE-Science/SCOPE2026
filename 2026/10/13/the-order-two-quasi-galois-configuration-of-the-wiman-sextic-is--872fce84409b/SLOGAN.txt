For the Wiman sextic, the \(45\) order-two quasi-Galois points are exactly the \(45\) quadruple points of the Wiman reflection arrangement. Their center-axis incidence is the commuting graph of the \(45\) involutions of \(A_6\), hence the line graph of the Tutte–Coxeter graph, with the \(30\) Klein-four reflection triangles split into two \(15\)-element families.
