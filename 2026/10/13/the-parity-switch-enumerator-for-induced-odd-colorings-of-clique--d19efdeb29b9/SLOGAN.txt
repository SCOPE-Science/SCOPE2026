For induced-odd vertex colorings (color classes induce graphs of entirely odd degrees), adjoining a clique of odd order at every vertex forces an exact two-color ceiling, while an even-order clique preserves the base odd chromatic number except for a minimum of two. A complete \(q\)-coloring enumerator factors into the base even/odd coloring count and explicit hyperbolic-series clique factors; for odd clique size \(r\), the two-color count is \(2^{(r-1)|V(G)|+\nu_2(L_G)}\).
