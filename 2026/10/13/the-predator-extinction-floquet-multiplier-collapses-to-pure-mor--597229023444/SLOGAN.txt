In the Xu-Zou weighted-harvesting model, the predator-extinction cycle has the exact multiplier \(\rho=(1-p_2E_l)e^{-\mu T}\), so it is stable for every admissible predator capture rate whenever the finite cycle exists. The published threshold \(\bar p_2\) is therefore spurious, and the instability premise used in Theorem 2.2(1) does not hold.
