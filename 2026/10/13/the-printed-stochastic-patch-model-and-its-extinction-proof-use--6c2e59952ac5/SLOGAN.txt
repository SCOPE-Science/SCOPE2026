The stochastic two-patch model prints one common Brownian driver, but its survival proofs use two independent drivers. That covariance switch changes the sharp uniform noise penalty from \(\sigma_1^2\sigma_2^2/(\sigma_1^2+\sigma_2^2)\) to \(\min\{\sigma_1^2,\sigma_2^2\}\); for equal noise intensities it doubles the sufficient extinction margin.
