Hence the source's Eq. (6.17) integrates to \(\alpha\), not one, for \(0<\alpha<1\). The source's own polynomial survival asymptotic gives finite \(r\)-th moment exactly for \(r<\alpha\), so no positive integer moments exist in the fractional regime. The likelihood correction follows by removing the resulting \(n\log\alpha\) factor.
