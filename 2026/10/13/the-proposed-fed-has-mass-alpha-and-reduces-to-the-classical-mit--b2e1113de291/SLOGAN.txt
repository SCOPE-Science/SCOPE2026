For \(0<\alpha<1\), the proposed FED density has total mass \(\alpha\), not one; removing the extra factor \(\alpha\) yields the classical Mittag-Leffler distribution. The corrected law has finite \(r\)-th moment exactly for \(0<r<\alpha\), so every positive integer moment diverges in the genuinely fractional regime and the published likelihood carries an extraneous \(n\log\alpha\) term.
