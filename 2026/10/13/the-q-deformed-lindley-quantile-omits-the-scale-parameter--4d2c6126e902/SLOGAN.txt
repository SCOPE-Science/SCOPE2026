The published \(q\)-deformed Lindley quantile replaces the CDF constant \(A_\theta=1+(3-2q)\theta\) by \(A_0=1+(3-2q)\), so it inverts a different distribution unless \(\theta=1\). All four Monte Carlo designs in the paper have \(\theta\ne1\), and the corrected Lambert–Tsallis form also needs an additional real-domain restriction when \(1<q<3/2\).
