For independent inclusion-induced Boolean coefficients with per-coordinate probability \(2^{-1-\lfloor\alpha n\rfloor}\), a recycled symmetric random walk switches sharply from an independent-Brownian partner (\(\alpha<1/2\)) to eventually identical increments (\(\alpha>1/2\)). Exactly at \(\alpha=1/2\), its normalized cross-variation converges quenched to Brownian positive occupation time with the nondegenerate arcsine distribution, precluding a bivariate Brownian limit.
