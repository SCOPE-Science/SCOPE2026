For the degree-four quotient of the exterior algebra on \(n\ge4\) generators over any finite field, every one-dimensional associative subalgebra is either the scalar-unit line or a square-zero radical line. Its complete radical-depth polynomial has closed Gaussian-binomial coefficients in odd characteristic and a uniform all-radical-lines formula in characteristic two, exposing the first degree-four Plücker decomposability obstruction.
