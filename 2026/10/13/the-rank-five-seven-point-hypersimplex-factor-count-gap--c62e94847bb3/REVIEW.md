# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The rank-five symmetric factorization \(C=XBX^{\mathsf T}\) makes each order-five principal determinant a nonzero scalar times a squared maximal row minor. Nonzero off-diagonal entries force every three-row minor condition through the explicit determinant \(2c_{ij}c_{jk}c_{ki}\). Matroid duality then constrains a rank-two parallel-class partition by \(t-a_j\ge4\). All thirteen integer partitions, the resulting exact counts, and thirteen explicit integer matrix realizations were checked with the bundled exact verifier. No finite test is used to replace the universal matroid argument.

## Originality — PASS

Oldekop's 2026 source introduces the delta-matroid/principal-minor perspective and announces an ML-degree minimum through order six, but the available source materials do not state the precise seven-coordinate rank-five order-five factor-count spectrum. The author-maintained 2024 companion to Clarke–Hoşten–Kushnerchuk–Oldekop includes a seven-coordinate ML-degree computation for the rank-three minimum construction, which neither counts nor classifies the rank-five order-five support. Searches under the hypersimplex, rank-five minor, forbidden eleven, and dual-matroid formulations found no statement implying the full spectrum and its realizations. The 2026 preprint full text was not accessible despite open-access attempts; it remains a genuine residual overlap risk and should be examined independently.

## Value — PASS

This is a natural complete classification of a discriminant-factor support statistic on the first order beyond the source's six-coordinate analysis. It gives a sharp missing count, exhaustive compatible matroid patterns, and verifiable realized scalings that can constrain subsequent Euler/ML-degree stratifications. It deliberately does not claim a new ML-degree formula.
