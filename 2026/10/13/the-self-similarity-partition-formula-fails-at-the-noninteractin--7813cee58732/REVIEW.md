# Review

## Correctness

PASS. At \(J=J_p=h=0\), every Boltzmann weight is exactly one. The source fixes the root spin in \(Z_{i_0}^{(n)}\), so the exact partial partition function is \(2^{|V_n|-1}\). Using \(|V_n|=2^{n+1}-1\) gives \(2^{2^{n+1}-2}\). Substituting \(\theta=\phi=u=1\) into the printed Theorem 1 gives \(128^{2^{n-1}-1}\), already \(128\) versus \(64\) at \(n=2\). The exact and printed free-energy coefficients are \(1\) and \(7/4\) times \(\log2/\beta\).

## Originality

PASS, with a named access risk. The primary open full text was inspected at the Hamiltonian, partial-partition definition, local sum, substitutions, Theorems 1 and 2, and proofs. Candidate-specific literature and database searches covered the DOI, exact title, partition-function error, noninteracting specialization, free-energy correction, and zero-coupling aliases.

Earlier Cayley-tree free-energy papers by Gandolfo--Rakhmatullaev--Rozikov--Ruiz and Mukhamedov--Akın--Khakimov do not state this correction in the inspected material. A later same-author arbitrary-order article, DOI:10.1016/j.physb.2023.414929, is highly relevant, but only its bibliographic record and abstract were accessible; it remains the principal residual originality risk.

## Value

PASS. This is not merely a harmless convention check. Theorems 1 and 2 are presented as exact partition-function and free-energy formulas, and the proposed self-similarity method is advertised for thermodynamic calculations and extension to other lattice models. The counterexample shows exponentially incorrect finite-volume counting and a \(7/4\) error in the limiting free-energy density at the simplest exactly solvable point.

## Closest literature and limitations

The closest earlier work supplies alternative Cayley-tree free-energy constructions, not the challenged 2022 recursion. The finding does not produce a full corrected formula for interacting parameters and does not assess the source's separate fixed-point phase-transition calculation. The inaccessible body of the 2023 same-author arbitrary-order paper remains an explicit comparison risk.
