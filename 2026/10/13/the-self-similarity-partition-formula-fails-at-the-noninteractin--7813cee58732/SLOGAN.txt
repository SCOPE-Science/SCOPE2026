At \(J=J_p=h=0\), the source's root-conditioned partition formula counts \(2^{7(2^{n-1}-1)}\) states instead of the exact \(2^{2^{n+1}-2}\), already giving \(128\) rather than \(64\) at \(n=2\). Accordingly its free-energy theorem gives \(7\log2/(4\beta)\) instead of the exact \(\log2/\beta\), so the published self-similarity partition/free-energy formulas cannot represent the stated Ising model as written.
