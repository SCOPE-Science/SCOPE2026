For the rational Klein quartic \(X^3Y+Y^3Z+Z^3X=0\), the 28 bitangents have minimal common field \(\mathbb Q(\zeta_7)\) and Galois-orbit lengths \(1,3,6,6,6,6\). At every odd prime \(p\ne7\), exactly \(28\), \(4\), or \(1\) bitangents are \(\mathbb F_p\)-rational according as \(p\equiv1\), \(6\), or \(2,3,4,5\pmod7\), respectively.
