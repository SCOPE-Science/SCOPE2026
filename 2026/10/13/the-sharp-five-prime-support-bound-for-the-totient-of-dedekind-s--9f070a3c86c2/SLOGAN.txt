Every integer \(n\ge3\) with at most five distinct prime factors satisfies the sharp bound \(\varphi(\psi(n))/n\le384/385\), with equality exactly when \(n=2^a3^b\cdot5\cdot7\cdot11\) for \(a,b\ge1\). Six factors are necessary and sufficient for the inequality \(\varphi(\psi(n))\le n\) to fail, as \(39270\) demonstrates.
