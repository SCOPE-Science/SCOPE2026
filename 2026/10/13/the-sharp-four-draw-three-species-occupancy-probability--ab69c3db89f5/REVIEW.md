# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The event has exactly one duplicated pair, giving the exact fourth-order polynomial in the second, third and fourth atomic power sums. For a two-mass split the objective is affine in their product; the resulting merge-or-equalize argument forces some finite-support maximizer to be uniform. The uniform integer parameter peaks at five, with value \(72/125\). Exact polynomial limits handle arbitrary atoms and diffuse mass, and conditioning on the de Finetti directing measure handles infinite exchangeability. The finite-support equality statement has a separate strict splitting argument. The standard-library exact checker confirms 126 direct occupancy enumerations, 36 two-mass identities and 100 uniform cases, without claiming that enumeration proves the general assertion.

## Originality
PASS, with residual risk. The full text of Zhu (arXiv:2103.07518, Sections 1--4), and his 2022 thesis (Chapter 2, Sections 2.0--2.3), were materially inspected. They give the sharp \(K_3=2\) and \(K_n=2\) results and attribute to Yakubovich a theorem that the uniform-sampling points are extreme *in their own hull*. The thesis explicitly says that identifying the feasible \(K_4\) law with that hull remains open. Neither quoted theorem bounds the \(K_4=3\) probability over all i.i.d. or infinitely exchangeable distributions. Targeted published-record and mathematical finding searches found no direct covering assertion. The unpublished 2021 Yakubovich work is described in the thesis but was not independently available, so an unlocated equivalent occupancy optimization result is a real bibliographic risk.

## Value
PASS. Exactly one birthday-type collision among four samples is a natural coordinate of the exchangeable species-count problem. The bound is sharp for a non-obvious support size (five types for four draws), classifies all finitely supported maximizers and supplies a short mass-deformation principle independent of the unresolved convex-hull description. It is a precise structural occupancy extremum rather than an arbitrary numerical slice.

## Limits
This is not the full four-draw law, does not apply to arbitrary merely four-exchangeable samples, and does not classify equality in the full class of mixed and diffuse i.i.d. parents.
