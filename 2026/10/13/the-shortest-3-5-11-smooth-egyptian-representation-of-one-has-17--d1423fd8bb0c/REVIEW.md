# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof has no unbounded computational step. The first 16 smooth denominators are exhaustive below 135, and exact fraction sums give the 14-term obstruction. The 15-term prefix is forced by an explicit maximal-sum inequality; the remaining two terms reduce to three rational numbers that are nonintegral. Odd denominators forbid even lengths. The 17-term witness is checked via its exact common denominator 111375 and a direct sum of integer numerators. The standalone certificate was run from the delivered package. These finite computations support, rather than substitute for, the complete inequality argument.

## Originality — PASS, subject to indexing risk

Chen–Elsholtz–Jiang's 2011 article supplies the general admissibility theorem and special length results for different prime supports; it does not state the minimum 17 for {3,5,11}. Burshtein's 2008 11-term classification concerns {3,5,7} and its reported number 17 counts *representations*, not the number of terms. Exact-topic, pseudoperfect-equivalence, and published-result searches yielded no stronger theorem implying this minimum. Earlier unindexed equivalent work remains possible.

## Value — PASS

This closes a natural exact minimum-length invariant in restricted Egyptian-fraction theory. The family {3,5,p} has only three admissible odd-prime supports by the known feasibility criterion; {3,5,11} is the next one after the well-studied {3,5,7}. The new result is not merely a witness: it proves all smaller lengths impossible, and the precise 17 provides a boundary condition for investigating supported lengths and pseudoperfect divisor decompositions. No broad existence theorem or paper-sized development is asserted.

## Scope and residual risks

Only the minimum for support {3,5,11} is established, not counts of 17-term decompositions or analogous minima for other supports. Bibliographic search cannot exclude every unindexed prior derivation. The closest 2011 source was read as full public HTML; the distinct-prime-set 2008 paper was compared through its published title and author-supplied abstract/introduction material, without claiming complete page-by-page inspection.
