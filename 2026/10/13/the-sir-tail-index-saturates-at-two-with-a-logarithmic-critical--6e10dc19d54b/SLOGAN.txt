The SIR survival has three regimes: it is asymptotic to a constant times \(z^{-q}\) for \(q<2\), to \(\theta z^{-2}\log(z^2/\theta)\) at \(q=2\), and to a constant times \(z^{-2}\) for \(q>2\). Thus its tail index is \(\min(q,2)\), its hazard is asymptotic to \(\min(q,2)/z\), and its real positive moment of order \(r\) exists exactly when \(r<\min(q,2)\).
