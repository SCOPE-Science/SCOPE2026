# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The source gives, at every one of the six isolated total-space singularities, a lowest weighted part
\[
\lambda z^2+\mu s^4-q(x,y)
\]
for weights \((1,1,1,2)\), with \(\lambda\mu\ne0\) and with \(q=xy(x+25y)(x+9y)\) having four distinct projective roots. The Jacobian algebra of the initial form factors into the \(z\)-factor of length \(1\), the \(s\)-factor of length \(3\), and the binary-quartic Jacobian algebra of length \(9\), giving \(27\). The exact germs are semiquasihomogeneous because all omitted terms have strictly larger weighted degree. The standard semiquasihomogeneous theorem preserves the Milnor number. Milnor's bouquet theorem then gives reduced third homology of rank \(27\) and Euler characteristic \(-26\). The packaged checker independently verifies the explicit polynomial identities and finite-dimensional Jacobian calculation.

## Value
PASS. The six isolated singularities are the centers of the crepant resolution used to construct the Calabi--Yau model, so their total-space Milnor number is a natural deformation-theoretic invariant rather than an arbitrary numerical slice. The source itself computes the nearby surface-fiber Milnor number \(9\); determining that the ambient threefold singularity has \(27\) vanishing three-cycles sharply separates the intrinsic threefold smoothing topology from the singular fiber topology. This exact local datum is directly relevant to any later comparison of crepant resolutions and smoothings of the model.
