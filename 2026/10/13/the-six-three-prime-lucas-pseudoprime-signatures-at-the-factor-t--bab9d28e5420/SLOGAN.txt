For prime discriminants \(D\equiv1\pmod4\), an absolute Lucas pseudoprime with precisely three prime factors, the smallest \(3\), can have only six exact integer-and-Legendre-sign patterns: \(105,195,255,399,561,2703\) with their specified signatures. Each pattern occurs for exactly one eighth of the primes \(D\equiv1\pmod4\), in relative prime density.
