The Sine New X–Lindley hazard is strictly increasing for every \(\xi>0\), from \(\pi\xi/4\) toward \(2\xi\), with the sharp law \(z(2\xi-h_\xi(z))\to2\). More generally, the classical Sin-G transform sends every positive-density IFR baseline to a strictly IFR law because its hazard is the baseline hazard times a universal increasing multiplier along the survival trajectory.
