# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The source's Section 5.4.2 gives the exact smooth member, the projective-bundle description, and the identification of \(\operatorname{Aut}(X_W)\) with \(\operatorname{Stab}_{\operatorname{PGL}_3}(W)\). For the displayed coefficient space, the annihilator pencil is \(\langle z_0z_2,z_1z_2\rangle\), whose base ideal is \((z_2)\cap(z_0,z_1)\). Preserving the pencil is therefore equivalent to preserving the uniquely dimensional pair consisting of its line component and isolated point. The resulting block-diagonal stabilizer is \((\operatorname{GL}_2\times\mathbf G_m)/\mathbf G_m\cong\operatorname{GL}_2\). The included symbolic check gives the matching Lie-algebra dimension \(4\) in \(\operatorname{PGL}_3\).

## Originality
PASS. The primary source states only that a diagonal torus acts faithfully on this special \(X_0\), while its exact stabilizer computation is left unstated. Searches for the exact equation, annihilator-pencil formulation, special syzygy-bundle automorphisms, line-plus-point stabilizers, and \(\operatorname{GL}_2\) aliases found no checked statement of the exact group. The closest published-finding search hits concern stabilizers of unrelated arrangements, scrolls, algebras, and characteristic-two automorphism schemes. A previously recorded result from the same source concerns the general four-point pencil and explicitly does not cover special pencils, so it does not imply this claim.

## Value
PASS. The paper uses this \(X_0\) as its concrete witness that automorphisms can jump from finite to positive-dimensional in the exceptional syzygy family. Determining the full group sharpens that witness from a two-dimensional torus lower bound to a complete four-dimensional connected group and identifies the non-toric symmetries responsible for the jump. This is a structural calculation attached to a source-highlighted exceptional member, not an arbitrary coordinate slice.

The closest limitation is that the result treats one special stabilizer stratum rather than classifying all special \(W\). A later revision or an unindexed note could independently record the same exact group; no such source was found in the checked literature.
