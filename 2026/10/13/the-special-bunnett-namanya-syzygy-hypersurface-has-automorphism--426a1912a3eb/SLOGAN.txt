For Bunnett--Namanya's explicit smooth special member \(X_0\subset\mathbf P^2\times\mathbf P^3\), the full automorphism group is not merely known to contain a torus: it is exactly \(\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbf C)\). The annihilator pencil \(\langle z_0z_2,z_1z_2\rangle\) has base locus a line plus an isolated point, forcing its projective stabilizer to be \((\operatorname{GL}_2\times\mathbf G_m)/\mathbf G_m\cong\operatorname{GL}_2\).
