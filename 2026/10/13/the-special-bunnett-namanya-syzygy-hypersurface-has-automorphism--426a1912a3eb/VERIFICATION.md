1. the four quadrics define a smooth projective-bundle hypersurface when they have no common zero;
2. the source identifies \(\operatorname{Aut}(X_W)\) with \(\operatorname{Stab}_{\operatorname{PGL}_3}(W)\);
3. it permits passage to the annihilator pencil in the dual representation;
4. for its displayed \(X_0\), the coefficient space is \(\langle x_0^2,x_1^2,x_2^2,x_0x_1\rangle\).

Under the coefficient pairing the annihilator is
\[
U=\langle z_0z_2,z_1z_2\rangle.
\]
The ideal generated by the pencil is
\[
(z_0z_2,z_1z_2)=(z_2)\cap(z_0,z_1),
\]
so the base scheme is exactly a reduced line plus a reduced point. Their dimensions distinguish them intrinsically. Hence preserving the pencil forces preservation of that pair, and preserving that pair gives precisely block-diagonal projective matrices. This proves the global stabilizer is \(\operatorname{GL}_2\).
