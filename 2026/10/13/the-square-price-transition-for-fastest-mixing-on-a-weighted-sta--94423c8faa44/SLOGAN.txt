For a star with \(m\ge3\) leaves, unit total conductance-cost budget, and one edge priced \(t\) times the others, fastest continuous-time mixing uniquely uses equal conductances until the sharp threshold \(t=(m-1)^2\). Above it, the exceptional edge receives a smaller but still strictly positive rate given by an explicit formula, and the optimum mixing gap improves by an asymptotic factor \((m+1)/m\) over equal conductances.
