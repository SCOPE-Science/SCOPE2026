The three-parameter MERD profile likelihood is unbounded: after profiling the threshold at the sample minimum and the rate analytically, \(\ell_p(\sigma)=-(k/2)\log\sigma+O(1)\) as \(\sigma\downarrow0\), where \(k\) is the multiplicity of the sample minimum. Thus the proposed unconstrained numerical profile maximization has no finite global MLE for any nondegenerate sample.
