# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In the formal Boolean polynomial semiring, an ideal generated by linear supports consists precisely of unions of their monomial translates. In the absence of cancellation a support belongs iff the union of the translates entirely inside it covers it. The linear closure uniquely determines the ideal because the closure contains the original generators and lies in their ideal. I independently computed the closures by bitwise unions: without a unit generator no nontrivial translate of a nonconstant linear generator can occur in a linear support. The resulting counts were 1748 closures, 1728 missing at least one variable, and 7 coordinate-generated ideals within that class, including the zero ideal. I independently verified all 1721 remaining witnesses by anchoring translates at the lexicographic minimum of each generator, checking both factor nonmemberships and product membership, with maximum total degrees 3 for factors and 6 for products. The variable ideals are prime since a monomial avoiding the chosen variables in each factor gives such a monomial in the product. This is a complete certificate, not a bounded test for primeness.
