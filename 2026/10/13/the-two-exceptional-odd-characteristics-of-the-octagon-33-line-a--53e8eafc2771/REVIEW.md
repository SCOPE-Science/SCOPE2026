# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The claim specifies the integral line model and all odd primes, avoiding ambiguity about changing the integral presentation. Exact cross products reconstruct 33 distinct characteristic-zero lines from 28 points. The complete set of 5,456 triple determinants has 480 zeros, and the field norms of the other 4,976 have prime support only \(\{2,3,7\}\). Thus all odd primes except \(3,7\) preserve the rank-three matroid, not merely the tested sample primes. Exhaustive projective pair-incidence calculations in \(\mathbb F_9\) and in both embeddings into \(\mathbb F_7\) prove the two exceptional tables. Every row satisfies the 528 pair-incidence identity. Limitation: characteristic \(2\) and special-fiber freeness are not addressed.

**Originality — PASS (qualified bibliographically).** The first public version of Pokora–Roulleau, arXiv:2403.20024v1, Theorem B and §2, contains the eight seed factors, resulting 33-line equations, characteristic-zero counts, and freeness. Its arithmetic special fibers are not classified there or in the inspected latest full text. Focused searches on prime reduction, \(\mathcal O_{33}\), octagon 33-line arrangements, and characteristic-three/seven incidence, including published mathematical records, found no equivalent distribution. A general matroid-specialization observation alone does not imply these exact primes and the two different incidence tables. Residual bibliographic risk: unindexed modular-arrangement computations or newer work may contain the same table.

**Value — PASS.** This determines the complete odd-characteristic good-reduction set and every exceptional geometric incidence multiplicity of an established rigid free arrangement, rather than an arbitrary modular sample. Matroid specialization in small characteristic is a natural obstruction to transporting complex arrangement combinatorics. In particular, primes \(3\) and \(7\) exhibit sharply different collapses of the 169-point generic intersection scheme while all 33 components remain distinct. No conclusion about freeness of the special fibers is used to inflate the claim.

Closest reference: Pokora–Roulleau, *Construction of free arrangements using point-line operators*, arXiv:2403.20024v1, Theorem B and its proof (29 March 2024); arXiv:2403.20024v3 was also inspected.
