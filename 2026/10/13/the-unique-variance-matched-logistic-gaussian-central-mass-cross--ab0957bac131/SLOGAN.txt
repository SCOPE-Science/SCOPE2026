For a logistic law and a Gaussian law with the same mean and variance, symmetric central mass changes order exactly once: the logistic wins for \(0<c<1.74447161774732\ldots\) standard deviations and loses for every larger radius. Its exponential tail eventually dominates the Gaussian tail by a ratio growing like \(c\sqrt{2\pi}\exp(c^2/2-\pi c/\sqrt3)\).
