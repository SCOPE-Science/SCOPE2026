# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The local instrument satisfies completeness exactly. The first branch has probability \(1/4\) and is the normalized GHZ state, while the second has probability \(3/4\) and is a product state. The exact inequality \(2^{19}<7^7\) gives \(h_2(1/8)<5/8\), hence \(S_E(\psi)<125/512<1/4\). No finite experiment is used as an infinite proof, and no numerical approximation is needed for the strict inequality.

## Originality
PASS. The motivating paper was inspected at the definition and axioms: it presents the unrooted product as a good three-qubit measure, then replaces it by geometric-mean quantities for the multipartite construction. Searches using the source title, the entropy-product formula, LOCC monotonicity, counterexample terminology, and the exact generalized-GHZ/filter pattern did not locate a published statement implying this explicit violation. The closest later geometric-mean result proves monotonicity for a different, concave construction and therefore does not cover the raw product. Residual risk remains that an unindexed comment or differently phrased note observed the same counterexample.

## Value
PASS. Failure under one local measurement directly removes a defining resource-theoretic interpretation from a published three-qubit quantifier. The example is exact, minimal in party number for the cited product construction, and clarifies why multiplying individually monotone marginal entropies is not enough to inherit strong monotonicity, while geometric-mean constructions can behave differently.

## Closest literature and limitations
The closest source is Liu–Zhao–Long, arXiv:0705.3904 / DOI:10.1088/0253-6102/54/5/09, which supplies the raw product and the good-measure claim but not the counterexample. Li–Shang, DOI:10.1103/PhysRevResearch.4.023059, proves strong monotonicity for a geometric mean of bipartite concurrences, a different functional. The present result is restricted to the raw three-qubit entropy product and does not assess the geometric-mean MEMS components or mixed-state extensions.
