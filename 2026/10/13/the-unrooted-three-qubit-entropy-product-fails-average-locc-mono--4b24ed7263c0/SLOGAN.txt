The unrooted three-qubit entropy product \(S_E=\prod_i E_i\) can increase on average under a single local measurement: an explicit generalized-GHZ state rises from \(h_2(1/8)^3<1/4\) to expected value \(1/4\). This rules out that raw product as a strong LOCC entanglement monotone without addressing the distinct geometric-mean MEMS quantities.
