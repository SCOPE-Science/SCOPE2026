For a uniform sample, the empty upper boundary gap \(B_n=1-U_{(n)}\) has exact positive covariance \((n-1)/[2(n+1)(n+2)(n+3)]\) with the Cramér–von Mises statistic. The full conditional mean is quadratic, with a unique minimum at \((5n+1)/[4n(n+2)]\), revealing a sharp finite-sample endpoint profile behind the mixed moment.
