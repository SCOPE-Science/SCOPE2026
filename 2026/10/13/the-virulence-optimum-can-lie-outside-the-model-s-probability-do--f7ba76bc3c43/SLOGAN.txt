The source's optimizer \(\beta_2^*=\beta_1/p\) is feasible only when \(2\beta_1<(1+p)\sqrt{q/(pc)}\), because its own Markov model requires \(0<\gamma<1\). Outside that regime the admissible optimum moves to the probability boundary, and an exact example has every feasible symptomatic transmission rate below the asymptomatic rate.
