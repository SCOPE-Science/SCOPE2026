# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The primary article's application section and Table 5 give n=33 and 60 and the bound WLBRL quadruples (Q,AIC,BIC,AICC)=(549.37,537.65,528.92,537.80) and (410.34,411.28,399.20,410.33). The density, Table 1 and conclusion support three fitted parameters, even though Table 5 uses a different label for the second estimate and Table 6 says two. Under ordinary definitions, the displayed excesses AIC−Q and BIC−Q are (−11.72,−20.45) and (0.94,−11.14). With n>1, any positive p gives both excesses positive; the dataset-2 AIC excess is separated from any possible 2p for integral p by more than the display rounding error. Even allowing p=0 would not account for either row. Direct evaluation gives AIC/BIC (555.37,559.8595226844…) and (416.34,422.6230336867…) for p=3 and (553.37,556.3630151229…) and (414.34,418.5286891244…) for p=2. The optional conventional AICc values at p=3 are 556.1975862069… and 416.7685714286…. This proves an incompatibility conditional on the printed deviance label, not that Q is correct or that the criteria after refitting or ranks of competing models are known. The frozen checker uses fixed row inputs and is neither a fit nor independent source evidence.

## originality

PASS

The calibrated claim is a specific error in the two published WLBRL rows used for real-data model comparison. Neither the defining information-criterion identities nor a correction of a different distribution's moment table is claimed as new. Bounded Resultary and bibliographic checks found no substantive coverage of this WLBRL correction; that is not a guarantee of priority or exhaustive novelty.

## value

PASS

This is not valuable as a textbook inequality or arbitrary check. In both applied datasets the authors use the mutually incompatible model-selection numbers as evidence for WLBRL. Demonstrating a failure independent of their own two-versus-three parameter discrepancy identifies a concrete obstacle to reusing those comparisons and separates what can be fixed from the printed Q entries from what requires refitting. It does not make a new claim about model rankings.
