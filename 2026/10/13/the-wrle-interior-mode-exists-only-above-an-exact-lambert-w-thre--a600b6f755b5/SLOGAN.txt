The WRLE stationary point in Eq. (3.3) is an interior mode only when \(0<\alpha<1\) and \(\beta>\beta_c(\alpha)\), where \(\beta_c(\alpha)=[-W_{-1}(-(1-\alpha)e^{-(1-\alpha)})-(1-\alpha)]/(2\pi\alpha)\). Outside that regime the density is decreasing on the circular cut and its mode is at direction \(0\).
