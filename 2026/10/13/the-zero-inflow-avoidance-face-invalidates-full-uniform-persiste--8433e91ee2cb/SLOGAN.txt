In the published learned-avoidance epidemic model, the allowed endpoint \(p=v=0\) makes the avoidance compartment decay to zero from every positive initial value, so full-state uniform persistence is impossible even when \(R_0>1\). The same endpoint creates an invariant \(A=0\) face containing a positive-disease equilibrium, invalidating the proof step that treats the disease-free equilibrium as the only invariant boundary set.
