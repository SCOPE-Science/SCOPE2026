At fixed positive treatment capacity \(a\), the source's specialization \(b=0\) turns \(aI/(b+I)\) into a singular constant-capacity law: the positive-\(I\) vector field approaches \((a,-a,0)\) at the nominal disease-free state. Hence the disease-free equilibrium used in Theorem 3.4 is incompatible with any continuous extension of the printed \(b=0\) dynamics, and the claimed classical global-stability proof does not apply.
