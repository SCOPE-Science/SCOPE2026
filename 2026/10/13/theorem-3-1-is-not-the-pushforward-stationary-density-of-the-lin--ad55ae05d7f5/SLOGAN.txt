Theorem 3.1 is not the pushforward stationary density of the paper's own linearized Itô chemostat: the logarithmic microorganism equation misses the drift \(-\sigma^2/2\), and the return to \((S,x)\) misses the Jacobian \(1/(Sx)\). Correcting both yields an explicit bivariate lognormal approximation and closed-form microorganism moments.
