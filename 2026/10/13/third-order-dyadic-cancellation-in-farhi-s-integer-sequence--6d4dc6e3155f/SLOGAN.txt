For Farhi's integer sequence \(a_n=\frac n2a_{n-1}+(n-1)!\), the sparse non-Mersenne indices \(n=2^{2^u+u}-2^u-1\) have the exact valuation \(\nu_2(a_n)=2^{2^u+u}-2(2^u+u)+3u-1\) for every \(u\ge3\). The unbounded extra divisibility comes from the vanishing of two lower polylogarithmic layers and a unique cubic-order contribution.
