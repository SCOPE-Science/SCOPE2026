For the three-dimensional nilpotent left Leibniz algebra with \([e_1,e_1]=[e_1,e_2]=z\), the reduced endomorphism variety has three affine four-dimensional components, only one of which is the closure of its automorphism group. Over every \(\mathbf F_q\), its full endomorphism, idempotent, and nilpotent counts are \(3q^4-2q^3\), \(2q^2+2\), and \(q^2(2q-1)\), respectively.
