For the two-dimensional Lie–Yamaguti algebra \([e_1,e_2]=e_1,\ \{e_1,e_2,e_2\}=e_1\), every fixed nonzero Reynolds weight has three solution branches: the plane displayed by Teng–Guo, a second affine plane, and the full square-zero cone. Over \(\mathbf F_q\) this gives \(3q^2-2q\) operators in exactly \(2q+2\) automorphism classes.
