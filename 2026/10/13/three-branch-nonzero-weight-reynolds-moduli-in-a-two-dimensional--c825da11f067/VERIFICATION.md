The packaged checker `artifacts/verify.py` reconstructs the binary and ternary operations
\[
[e_1,e_2]=e_1,
\qquad
\{e_1,e_2,e_2\}=e_1.
\]

For a general matrix
\[
R=
\begin{pmatrix}
a&b\\
c&d
\end{pmatrix}
\]
and symbolic weight \(\mu\), it verifies that the binary Reynolds defect is generated by
\[
a^2+bc+\mu a(ad-bc)
\]
and
\[
c\bigl(a+d+\mu(ad-bc)\bigr).
\]

It then checks directly that every matrix satisfying these equations also satisfies the ternary Reynolds identity for the induced Lie–Yamaguti structure.

For every nonzero weight it exhaustively tests all \(2\times2\) matrices over
\[
\mathbf F_2,\quad
\mathbf F_3,\quad
\mathbf F_5,\quad
\mathbf F_7.
\]

The fixed-weight counts are
\[
8,\quad21,\quad65,\quad133,
\]
the rank profiles are
\[
(1,5,2),\quad
(1,14,6),\quad
(1,44,20),\quad
(1,90,42),
\]
and the automorphism-class counts are
\[
6,\quad8,\quad12,\quad16.
\]

The exact three-branch decomposition and structural orbit-size families are checked in every tested field.

The checker returns `VERIFY_OK`.

Finite exhaustive checks are replay evidence. The arbitrary-field theorem is proved by the symbolic case split in `RESULT.md`.
