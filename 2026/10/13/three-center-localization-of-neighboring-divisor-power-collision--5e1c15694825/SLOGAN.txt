For each even exponent \(d\ge6\), any coincidence \(\sigma_d(n)=\sigma_d(n+1)\) forces \(n\) to be even and to lie within \(5d\) of one of three residue-selected centers \(d2^d\), \(d2^d\pm d(4/3)^d\). Thus an unbounded search for neighboring divisor-power collisions reduces to \(O(d)\) possible starting values for each exponent.
