# Review

## Correctness

PASS. Proposition 7.1 of Hyttinen–Paolini explicitly proves that the four generators of \(\pi^4\) are algebraic over the three diagonal points and simultaneously not definable over them. Since those four generators term-generate the entire free plane, transitivity and closure under definable functions put every element of \(\pi^4\) in the same algebraic closure. Elementarity of \(\pi^4\preccurlyeq M\) gives the reverse containment.

## Originality

PASS with folklore risk. Targeted published-finding corpus searches, exact-formulation web searches, full-text inspection of the primary source, and current/prior owned-ledger searches found the weaker published \(\operatorname{acl}\ne\operatorname{dcl}\) statement but not the exact equality \(\operatorname{acl}(e,f,g)=\pi^4\). The contribution is the explicit closure computation extracted from the source's witness.

## Value

PASS. It shows that a three-point geometric configuration can algebraically generate a whole countably infinite elementary model. This is substantially more informative about the closure geometry than knowing merely that algebraic and definable closure differ.

## Closest literature and limitations

The closest source is the very Proposition 7.1 from which the result is deduced. The result should therefore be viewed as a new explicit consequence, not as a new construction of open projective planes. No claim is made that three is minimal, or that all three-element sets behave this way.
