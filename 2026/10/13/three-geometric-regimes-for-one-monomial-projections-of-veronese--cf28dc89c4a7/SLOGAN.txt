Deleting one degree-\(d\) monomial from the Veronese coordinates of \(\mathbb P^2\) yields exactly three geometries for \(d\ge3\): a smooth blowup for the three pure powers, an isolated non-Cohen–Macaulay normalization defect for the six tangent monomials, and an unchanged smooth plane otherwise. The tangent defect always has completed local ring \(\mathbb C[[v,u^2,uv,u^3]]\), normalization colength one and Hilbert–Samuel multiplicity two, independent of \(d\).
