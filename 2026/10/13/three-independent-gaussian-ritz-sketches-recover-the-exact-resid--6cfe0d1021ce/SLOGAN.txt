One unrefined square Gaussian randomized Ritz value has infinite mean-square error on a natural invariant-spectator Hermitian family, but the componentwise median of **three independent** sketches obeys the exact identity \(\mathbb E|M_3-\lambda|^2=\|(A-\lambda I)w\|_2^2\). More generally, the corresponding odd-\(k\) median has finite absolute moments exactly below order \(k+1\).
