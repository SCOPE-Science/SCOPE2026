The two-excitation symmetric Dicke state has an exact all-size Pauli-magnitude spectrum supported on zero-, two-, and four-flip sectors. Its stabilizer Rényi moments undergo a sharp transition at \(\alpha=2\), with the critical window \(\alpha=2+s/\log n\) governed by the three-term law \(60e^{-2s}+192e^{-3s}+112e^{-4s}\) after multiplying by \(n^4\).
