PASS. The proof partitions all functions by the three possible center labels. In each branch it derives necessary and sufficient local blade conditions, so the three displayed contributions are disjoint and exhaustive. Substitution at \(x=1\), the minimum weight, and the multiplicity of minimum functions follow directly. The standalone verifier independently reconstructs the graph and predicate and agrees coefficientwise on six cases.

PASS. The earliest inspected source introduces total \((a,b)\)-Roman domination but gives complexity and algorithmic results rather than an all-function windmill enumerator. The later primary total-Roman paper develops bounds and extremal structure; its readable full text was inspected, and no statement implying the heterogeneous-windmill product formula was found. Searches under windmill, friendship, join-of-cliques, total \((1,2)\)-Roman, and polynomial/enumerator formulations returned no same-object statement. The closest published-results item is an exact enumerator for complete multipartite graphs, a graph class that does not contain these windmills when at least two blades have size at least two. Residual risk remains that a family-specific result is unindexed.

## Value
PASS. Heterogeneous windmills are a natural block-graph family containing ordinary windmills and friendship graphs. The result is not only a minimum-number evaluation: it classifies all feasible total Roman configurations through a three-state center decomposition, gives the complete weight distribution, an exact total count, and the full minimum layer for arbitrary blade sizes. The factorization exposes precisely how the total-support condition interacts with the cut vertex and clique blades.
