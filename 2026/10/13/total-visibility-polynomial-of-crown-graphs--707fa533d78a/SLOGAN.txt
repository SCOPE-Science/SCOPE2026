For \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M\), total mutual-visibility is equivalent to leaving at least three vertices unselected in each bipartition class. Hence \(\mathcal V_{\mathrm t}(\operatorname{Cr}_n;z)=\left(\sum_{k=0}^{n-3}\binom nk z^k\right)^2\) and \(\mu_{\mathrm t}(\operatorname{Cr}_n)=2n-6\).
