For \\(A_Q=\\mathbb F_qQ/J^2\\), the exact commuting-pair count is the random-cluster partition function of the underlying quiver multigraph: \\(C_Q(q)=q^{|E|}\\sum_{F\\subseteq E}(q-1)^{|F|}q^{2k(F)}\\). Hence commuting probability is orientation-invariant and is an explicit Tutte-polynomial evaluation.
