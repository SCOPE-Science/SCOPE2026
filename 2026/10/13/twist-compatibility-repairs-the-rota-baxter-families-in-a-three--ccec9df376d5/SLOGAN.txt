In Ncib's three-dimensional BiHom-Lie Example 1.1, the printed weight-zero Rota–Baxter families solve the product identity but do not all commute with the two twisting maps required by the preceding \(\mathcal O\)-operator definition. The full family survives only when \(\gamma=1\); for \(\gamma\ne1\), every Rota–Baxter operator is diagonal on the even part with \(R(e_1)=0\).
