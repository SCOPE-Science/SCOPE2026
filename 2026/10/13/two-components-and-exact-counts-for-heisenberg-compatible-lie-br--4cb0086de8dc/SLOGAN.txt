Fixing the three-dimensional Heisenberg Lie bracket, all compatible second brackets form a reduced affine variety with exactly two irreducible components, of dimensions \(6\) and \(5\). Over every odd finite field \(\mathbf F_q\), the exact number of compatible second brackets is \(q^6+q^5-q^3\), and its point-counting zeta function is \((1-q^3T)/((1-q^5T)(1-q^6T))\).
