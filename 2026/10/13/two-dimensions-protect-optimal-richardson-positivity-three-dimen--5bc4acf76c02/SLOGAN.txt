For spectrally optimal fixed-step Richardson iteration from zero, every nonnegative right-hand side stays componentwise nonnegative for every \(1\)- and \(2\)-dimensional SPD Stieltjes system. A \(3\)-dimensional exact example has \(x^{(3)}_1=-1/108\) despite convergence to a nonnegative solution, making dimension three minimal for transient sign loss.
