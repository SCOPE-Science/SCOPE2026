For the exponential-weight Poisson Stein test against contiguous mean-preserving zero inflation, the locally most powerful weight among \(u^x\), \(0<u<1\), tends to \(u=1/3\) as the Poisson mean vanishes and satisfies \(\lambda u\to1\) as the mean grows. These are rigorous limiting optimization laws, not claims of global test optimality or finite-sample uniqueness.
