For an Ising star with arbitrarily heterogeneous strictly antiferromagnetic bonds, exactly two Lee–Yang zeros leave the unit circle: a simple negative reciprocal pair, while the other \(m-1\) zeros are simple and unimodular. Under uniformly positive interaction strengths, the inner outlier is asymptotic to the product of the reciprocal edge parameters, and the circular zeros satisfy an explicit finite-size discrepancy law.
