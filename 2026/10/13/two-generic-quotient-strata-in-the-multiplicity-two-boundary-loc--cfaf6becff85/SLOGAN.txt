The unresolved multiplicity-two boundary locus \(\mathcal C(-1,1,1,2)\subset\mathcal M(-1,3,7)\) already contains two natural generic quotient strata: a \(12\)-dimensional smooth-conic family and an \(11\)-dimensional skew-line family. Their dimension difference comes from a genuine \(\mathbf P^1\) choice of quotient map for a conic, versus uniqueness of the kernel for a fixed pair of skew lines.
