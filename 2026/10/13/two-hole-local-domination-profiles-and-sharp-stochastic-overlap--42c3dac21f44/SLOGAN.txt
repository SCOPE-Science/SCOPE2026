For a vertex \(v\) adjacent to all but two vertices of a connected graph, the complete polynomial counting dominating sets containing \(v\) depends only on the two exceptional degrees, their adjacency, and their shared neighbors. At fixed exceptional degrees and adjacency, decreasing their shared-neighbor overlap strictly increases the conditional dominating-set size in stochastic order, yielding sharp average-size extrema and an exact equality criterion for the degree-sum bound.
