# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The claim fixes a standard two-susceptibility SIR initial-value problem with a single infectious pool and positive admissible fractions. The cumulative-pressure representation is an exact integration of the susceptible equations; conservation gives \(I=1-s_1-s_2-\gamma x\). Differentiation establishes \(\dot C=IQ\) and \(I_x=H-\gamma\) with no unstated normalization. The independent exact-rational interval program proves the four alternating strict signs of \(Q\), and \(H(1/5)>4/5\) guarantees positive increasing prevalence and finite-time passage through every interval. Analyticity makes the forced extrema strict; open-parameter persistence follows by continuity. The proof establishes at least two, not exactly two, incidence maxima.

## Originality — PASS, scoped to the exact model-specific statement

The 2026 source provides the mathematical fixed-heterogeneity ODE/Laplace framework, not a multimodal-incidence result. **Multiple peaks in heterogeneous epidemics are already known**: Bootsma et al. (2024) exhibit peaks generated by heterogeneous latent periods and weak coupling, while Siegenfeld et al. (2022) explicitly display peaks in a several-group strongly clustered network. Comparing assumptions and conclusions—not merely titles—shows neither inspected claim supplies a two-type *susceptibility-only*, fully pooled Markov SIR example with rigorous distinct incidence extrema during one strictly rising prevalence phase. This is a narrow constructive separation of incidence and prevalence, not priority for heterogeneous epidemic multiwave phenomena generally. Risk: broader results on heterogeneity and incidence shape may entail it without identical phrasing; search alone cannot exclude that.

## Mathematical value — PASS

A recurring interpretive gap is whether several positive incidence peaks require renewed increases in transmission, weakly coupled subepidemics, latent-stage heterogeneity, or repeated rises in infectious prevalence. This example isolates the minimal fixed-susceptibility/pooled-infective mechanism and proves all incident peaks occur before the unique prevalence apex. Its exact-rational certificate and open robustness make the boundary reproducible rather than a fragile simulation. The small intervening trough limits direct empirical detectability; the result is an informative mathematical counterexample, not a claim of a separate clinical wave.
