Ohashi--Kudo--Harashita [1] prove the normal form and moduli coordinate but not the additional-hyperflex equation. Girard--Kohel [2] classify general seven-hyperflex strata and the Hessian method but do not state the two squared-parameter values or orbit decomposition within the cyclic-six locus. Hu [3] gives a general hyperflex divisor, which does not separate the exceptional curves here because every member already has one hyperflex. The new precise specialization is not an immediate statement of those sources; it requires the cubic reduction and discriminant calculation. Search results and accessible earlier findings identify no equivalent result. Vermeulen's original thesis and unindexed specialized calculations were not exhaustively inspected, so prior equivalent specialization remains a genuine risk.
