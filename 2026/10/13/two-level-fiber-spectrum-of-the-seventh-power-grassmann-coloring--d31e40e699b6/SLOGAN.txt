For the natural Moore-determinant coloring of the \(1395\) binary three-spaces in \(\mathbb F_{64}\) defined by \(\sum_{u\in U}u^7\), exactly nine norm-one colors have \(29\) spaces each and all other \(54\) colors have \(21\) each. This complete fiber census explains the two class sizes through the scalar action and its unique subfield orbit; it does not improve the separately known \(31\)-color upper bound.
