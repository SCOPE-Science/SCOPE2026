For one-dimensional uniform-sample Fermat distances, the variance-based noise threshold occurs near \((\log N+\tfrac12\log\log N)/\log4\), whereas typical normalized distances remain concentrated until the larger scale \(\alpha\approx\log N\). At the latter boundary \(\alpha=\log N+s\sqrt{\log N}\), the normalized distance converges in probability to \(\Phi(-s)\), even as its relative variance diverges.
