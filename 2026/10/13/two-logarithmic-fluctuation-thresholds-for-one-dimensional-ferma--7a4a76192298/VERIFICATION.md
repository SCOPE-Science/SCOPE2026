The analytic proof derives exact first and second moments from the uniform Dirichlet spacing law, uses a uniform gamma-ratio expansion for exponents of order \(\log N\), and separates typical events from rare large exponential spacings. The rate bounds needed for the triangular-array laws are written out in RESULT.md.

The standalone `verify.py` uses exact rational calculations to check low-degree spacings moments and asymptotic coefficients numerically for finite sizes. Such checks cannot establish the probability limits or the claimed bibliographic novelty. No independent external validation has been performed.
