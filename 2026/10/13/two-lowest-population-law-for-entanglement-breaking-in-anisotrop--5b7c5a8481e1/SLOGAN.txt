For reset channels with a faithful anisotropic stationary state having at least three distinct energy populations, entanglement breaking occurs exactly when the surviving identity weight satisfies \(s\le\sqrt{p_{(1)}p_{(2)}}/(1+\sqrt{p_{(1)}p_{(2)}})\), where \(p_{(1)},p_{(2)}\) are the two smallest stationary populations. An explicit separable Choi decomposition makes the PPT threshold sharp in every dimension \(d\ge3\), and the corresponding reset semigroup has first entanglement-breaking time \(\gamma^{-1}\log(1+1/\sqrt{p_{(1)}p_{(2)}})\).
