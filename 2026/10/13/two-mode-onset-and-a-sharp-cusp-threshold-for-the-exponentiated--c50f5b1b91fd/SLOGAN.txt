A small positive CDF-exponentiation of the Haq lifetime law creates exactly two strict interior density modes if and only if the fixed Haq parameter satisfies \(0<\tau\le\sqrt{3/2}-1\); above that threshold there is exactly one. At equality the two non-origin stationary points separate on the sharp \(\sqrt{\delta-1}\) scale, while the new near-origin mode sits at rescaled distance \((\delta-1)+O((\delta-1)^2)\).
