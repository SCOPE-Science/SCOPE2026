For friendship graphs \(F_n=K_1\vee(nK_2)\), the \(S\)-packing total chromatic number for \(S=(1,2^q,3,3,\ldots)\) has an exact three-stage transition: \(4n+1\), \(3n+2\), and \(3n+1\) as \(q\) moves from zero to one to at least two, for \(n\ge2\). The single-triangle boundary is \(5\) for every \(q\), and two distance-three-reusable colors already saturate the improvement.
