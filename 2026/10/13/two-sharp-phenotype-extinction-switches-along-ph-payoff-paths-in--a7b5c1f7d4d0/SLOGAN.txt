The four-phenotype tumor replicator game has two sharp, globally attracting extinction transitions along the straight payoff paths from its published baseline to the acidic and buffered scenarios: proliferation disappears at the unique root near \(0.256902\), while resistance disappears only near \(0.994745\). The exact endpoint logarithmic exclusion rates are \(-173/3385\) and \(-1/3340\), explaining why finite-time observations can conceal the latter extinction.
