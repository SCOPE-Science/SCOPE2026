The Levi incidence graphs of \(\mathrm{PG}(2,3)\) and \(\mathrm{PG}(2,4)\), the smallest regular girth-six graphs of degrees four and five, attain the degree upper bound for sequence b-coloring with two color-dominating vertices per color: \(\beta_2=5\) and \(\beta_2=6\), respectively. Both assertions follow from complete explicit proper-coloring certificates with exact neighbor checks; no general girth-six conjecture is claimed.
