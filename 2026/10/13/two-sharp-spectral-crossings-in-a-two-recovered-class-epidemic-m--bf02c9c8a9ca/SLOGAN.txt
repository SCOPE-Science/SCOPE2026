In the published two-recovered-class epidemic example, the unique endemic equilibrium is stable for low and high recovery rates but has exactly two unstable directions on the sharp intermediate interval \(0.6121330229\ldots<\gamma<0.9781559947\ldots\). Both transition rates are specified exactly by the two positive roots of an explicit quartic, rigorously locating the numerical outbreak example \(\gamma=0.7\) inside the unstable window.
