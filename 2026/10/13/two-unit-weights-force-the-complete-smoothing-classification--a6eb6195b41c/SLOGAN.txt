Among all weighted projective threefolds \(\mathbf P(1,1,a,b)\), the only ones admitting a \(\mathbb Q\)-Gorenstein smoothing to \(\mathbf P^3\) are \(\mathbf P^3\) and \(\mathbf P(1,1,2,4)\). The classification follows from the anticanonical volume equation alone, with no canonicality or codimension-two \(A\)-type assumption.
