A ternary \(2\)-automatic word with only linear factor complexity loses a positive linear number of abelian classes under **every** injective integer valuation: \(\rho_{\mathrm{ab}}(n)-\rho_v(n)\geq\lfloor n\gcd(\alpha,\beta)/\beta\rfloor\). For the ordinary weights \(0,1,2\), its additive complexity is exactly \(2n+1\), while its abelian complexity is at least \(3n\); three symbols are necessary for this universal valuation obstruction.
