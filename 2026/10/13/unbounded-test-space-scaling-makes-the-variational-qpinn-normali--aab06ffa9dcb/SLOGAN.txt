For the variational QPINN theorem in arXiv:2609.19239v1, the test space \(\mathcal H_M\) is an unbounded linear span while both the variational observable and its linearization error are homogeneous in the test function. Therefore A8 and the finite uniform supremum in Eq. (4.109) are nontrivial only after restricting the test vectors to a bounded normalized set.
