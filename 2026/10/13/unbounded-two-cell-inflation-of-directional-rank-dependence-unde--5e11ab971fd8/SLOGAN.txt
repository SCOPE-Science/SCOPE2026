An explicit family of smooth, strictly stochastically increasing copulas with densities uniformly bounded between \(1/2\) and \(3/2\) has a \(2\times2\) checkerboard Chatterjee rank correlation larger than its original value by a factor asymptotic to \(\sqrt{k/(2\pi)}\). Thus, despite qualitative convergence of fine-grid approximations and finite known counterexamples, no finite multiplicative checkerboard bound exists on this weaker dependence class.
