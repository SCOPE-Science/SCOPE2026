For every prime \(k\), the commutator \(\sigma(\varphi(2^{k-1}))-\varphi(\sigma(2^{k-1}))\) is negative, whereas every positive multiple of \(12\) yields a positive value. Thus both signs occur infinitely often among powers of two without assuming infinitely many Mersenne primes, with the first positive case at \(2^{11}=2048\).
