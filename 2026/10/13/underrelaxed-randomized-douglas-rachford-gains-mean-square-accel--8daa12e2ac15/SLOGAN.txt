Positive heavy-ball momentum can **strictly improve actual mean-square convergence** of underrelaxed randomized two-set Douglas–Rachford, even though the published mean-square *upper-bound factor* becomes worse. On \(A=I_2\), the sharp small-momentum transition is \(\alpha=1/2\); at \(\alpha=1/4\), \(\beta=1/10\), the actual rate is \(0.5934805\ldots<5/8\) inside the source theorem’s sufficient-stability regime.
