For two full-rank tasks with uniform weights, Aligned-MTL is common descent for every norm ratio until the cosine falls below \(-1/\sqrt2\); after that the exact safe ratio window is \(\tau(\rho)<\lVert g_1\rVert_2/\lVert g_2\rVert_2<1/\tau(\rho)\), where \(\tau(\rho)=-\rho-\sqrt{1-\rho^2}\). With equal gradient norms, every conflicting pair has an even sharper property: Aligned-MTL leaves the ordinary mean update exactly unchanged.
