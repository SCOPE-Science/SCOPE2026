The uniform lift \(A\otimes P\) in the recent Deza-digraph LDPC construction is only a relabeled block-diagonal parity check: its code is \(C^{\oplus m}\) and its Tanner graph is \(m\) disjoint copies of the base graph. Consequently \(W_{C_m}(z)=W_C(z)^m\) and every Tanner-cycle multiplicity merely scales by \(m\), so genuine mixing requires position-dependent permutation blocks.
