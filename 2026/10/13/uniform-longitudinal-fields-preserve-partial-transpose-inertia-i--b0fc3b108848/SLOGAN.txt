For any finite-dimensional bipartite Gibbs family whose uniform field couples to a conserved additive charge, every finite field is exactly an invertible local filter of the zero-field thermal state, so the full partial-transpose inertia is field independent. For the spin-one XXZ dimer this proves, for all exchange and single-ion anisotropies, that every positive-temperature negativity threshold and every zero-eigenvalue kink temperature is unchanged by a longitudinal field.
