# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The exact identity \(A^3=mnA\) gives all transition amplitudes. For both bipartition sizes at least two, one cross-part entry bounds possible uniform mixing by \(mn\leq m+n\), forcing the four-cycle. For stars, the hub-diagonal and leaf-off-diagonal moment equations force \(\mathbb EX=-(n-2)/2\) and \(\mathbb EX^2=1/(n+1)\); these cannot hold at four or more leaves. Explicit time laws realize all four other cases. The additional \(K_{1,4}\) entrywise lower bound follows from the nonnegative square \(\mathbb E(1+X)^2\). The proof quantifies over every real-valued Borel sampling law. Exact finite checks of the adjacency identities, witness laws and selected boundary errors agree, but do not replace the proof.

## Originality — PASS

Baptista, Coutinho and Marques, *Unexpected Averages of Mixing Matrices* (arXiv:2308.16378v1, Proposition 9), rule out only bicliques with more than seven vertices and recognize the four affirmative cases; they do not supply a complete negative classification for the remaining orders. Carlson and collaborators (arXiv:quant-ph/0608044v1) concern adjustable edge weights and initial-state mixing, rather than uniformity of every entry under one law at fixed unit weights. Directed searches for equivalent convex-hull/randomized-time statements and the indexed research record did not identify a stronger covering assertion. The journal version's full text was not accessible at the publisher page; poorly indexed equivalent results remain possible. The known affirmative cases are credited as prior work.

## Value — PASS

This gives a complete necessity-and-sufficiency answer for a natural infinite family explicitly investigated, but not fully classified, in the motivating article. It distinguishes common-law all-start mixing from single-start uniformity and supplies a positive lower bound on approximation error for the first excluded star. The theorem has a closed proof and fully reproducible small-case checks; it neither requires nor claims a broader downstream quantum-computation theorem.

## Limits and closest literature

All graphs are unweighted, loopless complete bipartite graphs with adjacency dynamics. The measure is common to all initial vertices; variable couplings and other graph families are outside scope. Closest literature is arXiv:2308.16378v1, Section 3.1; arXiv:quant-ph/0608044v1, Sections 2--4; and the 2024 journal record DOI:10.1007/s11128-024-04480-0, whose publisher full text was not available. The quantitative four-leaf bound is not asserted to be sharp.
