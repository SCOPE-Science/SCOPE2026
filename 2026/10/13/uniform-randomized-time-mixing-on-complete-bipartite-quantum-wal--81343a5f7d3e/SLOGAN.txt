For the ordinary adjacency quantum walk on an unweighted complete bipartite graph, one common randomized observation time gives exactly uniform mixing from every starting vertex if and only if the graph is one of \(K_{1,1}\), \(K_{1,2}\), \(K_{1,3}\), or \(K_{2,2}\). The four-leaf star is separated from uniform average mixing by an explicit entrywise error of at least \(2/45\), regardless of the sampling distribution.
