For the three-player dice race with goals \((1,2,n)\), every integer \(n\ge3\) has quantitatively ordered last-place probabilities: \(\tau_1+1/200<\tau_2\) and \(\tau_2+1/100<\tau_n\). At \(n=2\), the latter two players tie; exact Erlang integrals, rational finite checks, and uniform tail inequalities together prove the entire infinite family.
