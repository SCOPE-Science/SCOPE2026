Newton's matrix-sign iteration can have arbitrarily large intermediate error even when every eigenvalue is already exactly at the limiting sign value \(1\). On \(I+MJ_n\), the nilpotent error degree doubles each step, so convergence is exact after \(\lceil\log_2 n\rceil\) steps, while dimension three already permits a first-step spectral-norm amplification of exactly \(M/2\).
