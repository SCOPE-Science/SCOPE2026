For every \(k\ge2\) and \(n\ge3\), the half graph \(H_n\) has \(\gamma_{[kR]}(H_n)=2(k+1)\), and its minimum \([k]\)-Roman function is unique: only \(a_1\) and \(b_n\) are positive, both with label \(k+1\). The \(k=2\) scalar value was known for chain graphs; the optimizer rigidity and all \(k\ge3\) cases are the new content.
