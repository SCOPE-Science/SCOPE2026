For fixed longitudinal \(J_z\) phase sensing after independent dephasing, an \(n\)-qubit W–spin-flipped-W superposition overtakes GHZ in quantum Fisher information at exactly one noise threshold. The exact all-size crossover obeys \(np_c(n)\to1/2\); it is a directional advantage, not an optimized-over-axes or global probe-optimality claim.
