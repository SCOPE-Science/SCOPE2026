On the \(n\)-sunlet with uniform cycle-edge price and pendant-edge price \(t>0\), the conductance budget maximizing the ordinary-Laplacian spectral gap has one unique optimizer even among fully asymmetric edge allocations. Writing \(\mu=4\sin^2(\pi/n)\), its exact gap is \(\mu/[n(2+\mu t+2\sqrt{\mu t})]\), with strictly positive explicit weights on every cycle and pendant edge.
