For a coherently hopping three-site chain with one terminal Lindblad sink and fixed \(J_1^2+J_2^2=E>0\), the unique joint minimizer of the mean absorption time has \(J_1^2:J_2^2=2:1\) and \(\kappa=2\sqrt E\), with exact optimum \(3/\sqrt E\). This is strictly faster than the best equal-coupling configuration, whose mean is \(2\sqrt3/\sqrt E\).
