In the MDS-intersection EAQLRC family, locality is completely symmetric: for an erased coordinate, every \(r=n-d+1\) subset of the other transmitted qudits is a valid helper set. Thus each coordinate has \(\binom{n-1}{r}\) size-\(r\) recovery sets and exactly \(\lfloor(n-1)/r\rfloor\) can be chosen pairwise disjoint.
