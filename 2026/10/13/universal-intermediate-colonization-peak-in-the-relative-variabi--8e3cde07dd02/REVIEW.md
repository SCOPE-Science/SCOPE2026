# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The explicit backward equations prove the small-colonization coefficient \(\deg_G(v)/(2\mu)\) from the zero-rate singleton and pair moments. At high colonization, graph connectedness gives a uniform birth-path probability, geometric moment bounds for excursions from nonfull states, and an iid full-state cycle decomposition. The cycle first and second moment identities yield relative variance tending to one. Continuity then forces a finite interior maximum. Exact Markov equations give the unique two-patch peak. The standalone exact-rational checker verified every labeled connected graph through five vertices for the small-rate identities, plus two-patch and further moment evaluations. Graph enumeration is not presented as proof of arbitrary graph order.

## Originality

**PASS, with residual literature risk.** The closest inspected analytical contact-process works (arXiv:1203.2972v1 and arXiv:1509.04133v1) establish exponential extinction-time limits while graph order increases at fixed positive infection rate. They do not state the singleton-start, fixed-graph colonization-parameter profile, its degree-controlled small-rate derivative, or its necessary interior peak. The inspected ecology experiment (DOI 10.1002/ece3.713) measures variability in abundance over time, a different quantity. Known phase-type recurrences or other unindexed exact small-network studies may overlap part of the argument; a negative literature search does not establish absolute uniqueness.

## Value

**PASS.** The coefficient of variation of time to extinction is a natural, scale-free way to report persistence uncertainty alongside mean persistence. The universal theorem shows that increasing colonization can initially increase relative unpredictability even though the high-colonization lifetime becomes approximately exponential after rescaling. The exact two-patch optimum calibrates the effect in the smallest nontrivial landscape. This is a structural assertion over all finite connected habitat networks, not a numerical curiosity or a routine redescription of a mean lifetime.

## Scope and closest coverage

The claim is restricted to absorbing continuous-time occupancy dynamics with homogeneous local extinction and edgewise colonization, initially one occupied vertex. Its high-rate limit concerns a fixed finite graph and does not follow just by substituting parameters into published large-graph metastability limits. The two-patch expression does not make a claim about abundance variation or empirical conservation outcomes. Independent external validation remains outstanding.
