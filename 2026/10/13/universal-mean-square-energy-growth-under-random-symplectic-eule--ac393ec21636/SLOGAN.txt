An i.i.d. choice between any distinct stable positive symplectic-Euler steps makes the harmonic oscillator's mean-square energy grow exponentially from every nonzero initial state, although either constant step keeps all trajectories bounded. The sharp signature is the strictly negative moment determinant \(\det(I-T)=-2m_1^2\mathbb E[(H^2-(m_2/m_1)H)^2]\).
