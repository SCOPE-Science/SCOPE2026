The inverse-Gaussian Chvátal minimum has a universal Gaussian Mills-ratio coordinate: its optimizer is determined by \(zM(z)=(\kappa+1)/(2\kappa)\). This exposes a square-root cusp \(m(\kappa)-1/2\sim\sqrt{(\kappa-1)/\pi}\) at the critical point and the opposite-end law \(1-m(\kappa)\sim z_*\varphi(z_*)/\kappa\).
