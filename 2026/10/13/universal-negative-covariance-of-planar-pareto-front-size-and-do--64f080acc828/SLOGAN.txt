For iid planar samples with independent continuous coordinate marginals, the number of Pareto-maximal observations always has strictly negative covariance with the area dominated by the sample, for every \(n\ge2\). With independent uniform coordinates the exact covariance is \(-[(n+2)H_n-(n+1)H_n^{(2)}-n]/(n+1)^2\), asymptotic to \(-\log(n)/n\).
