The UWG raw-moment display in Eq. (5.9) is not the integral derived immediately before it: at \(\alpha=\beta=1\), \(p=1/2\), it gives the impossible mean \(2\log2>1\), while the true mean is \(2\log2-1\). On the complete \(\beta=1\) slice the moments have an exact hypergeometric correction, and the source's Table 2 value at \(\alpha=2\), \(p=1/2\) agrees with that correction.
