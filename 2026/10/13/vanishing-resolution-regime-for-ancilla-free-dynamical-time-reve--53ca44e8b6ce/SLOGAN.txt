Under the explicit ancilla-free adaptive-interference bounds for dynamical time-reversal testing, a vanishing signature gap \(\epsilon_n\) remains certifiable with \(o(2^n)\) queries whenever \(\epsilon_n=\omega(\sqrt{\log n/n})\). Equivalently, normalized Frobenius TRS violations \(\Delta_n=\omega((\log n/n)^{1/4})\) admit sub-dimension query certification; this is a sufficient regime, not an optimality claim.
