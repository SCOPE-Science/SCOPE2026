For \(G_{a,b,t}=K_{a,b}-M_t\) with \(1\le a\le b\), well-edge-domination occurs exactly for stars, balanced undeleted bicliques, the family \(K_{2,b}-M_2\), and the two isolated triples \((2,3,1)\) and \((3,4,3)\). The proof turns minimal edge domination into a four-pattern problem for uncovered rows and columns of a rectangular board with diagonal holes.
