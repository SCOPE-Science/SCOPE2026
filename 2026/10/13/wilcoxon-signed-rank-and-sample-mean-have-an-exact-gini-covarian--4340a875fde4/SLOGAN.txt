For iid observations from any atomless symmetric law, the exact covariance between the sample mean and the positive Wilcoxon signed-rank statistic is \(\mathbb E|X|/2+(n-1)\mathbb E|X-X'|/4\), so its limiting correlation is the Gini mean difference normalized by \(2\sigma/\sqrt3\). For a normal parent the squared limiting correlation is \(3/\pi\), while for a centered uniform parent the correlation tends to \(1\).
