For \(W\sim W_p(\nu,\Sigma)\), total Mahalanobis scatter \(Q=\operatorname{tr}(\Sigma^{-1}W)\) and log volume \(L=\log\det W\) satisfy the exact identity \(\operatorname{Cov}(Q,L)=2p\), hence \(\operatorname{Corr}(Q,L)=\sqrt{2p/(\nu V_{\nu,p})}\). At aspect ratio \(p/\nu\to r\), the correlation decreases from \(1\) to \(0\) through the explicit law \(\sqrt{r/[-\log(1-r)]}\).
