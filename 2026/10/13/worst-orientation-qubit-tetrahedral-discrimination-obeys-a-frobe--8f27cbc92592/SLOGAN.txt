For four equiprobable tetrahedral qubit states passed through any qubit channel with Bloch matrix \(T\), the least achievable optimal guessing probability over a common input rotation is exactly \(\frac14(1+\|T\|_{\rm F}/\sqrt3)\). Every minimizing rotation diagonalizes \(T^TT\) in the tetrahedral coordinate frame, and an explicit four-outcome optimal POVM exists.
