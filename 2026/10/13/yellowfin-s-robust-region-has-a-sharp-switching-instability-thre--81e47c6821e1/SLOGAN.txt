YellowFin's robust-region lemma controls each momentum operator separately, but two-period switching is uniformly stable across that region only when \(\mu<3-2\sqrt2\). At larger momentum, alternating the two robust-region curvature endpoints diverges even though every individual step has spectral radius \(\sqrt{\mu}<1\); for YellowFin's minimal robust momentum this occurs exactly once the generalized condition number exceeds \(3+2\sqrt2\).
