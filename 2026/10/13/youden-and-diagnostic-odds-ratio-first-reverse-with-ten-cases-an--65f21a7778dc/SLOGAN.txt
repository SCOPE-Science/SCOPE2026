In balanced positive-cell diagnostic studies, Youden's index ranks tests only by \(TP+TN\), while the diagnostic odds ratio can reward a more uneven sensitivity-specificity split. No strict ranking reversal occurs with fewer than \(10\) cases and \(10\) controls; at that size \( (3,7,9,1) \) is lower by Youden but higher by diagnostic odds ratio than \( (6,4,7,3) \).
