For a stationary Gaussian AR(1) segment, the covariance between zero-crossing count \(T_n\) and total quadratic energy \(Q_n\) has sign exactly opposite to the autoregressive correlation \(\rho\). More sharply, every edge-to-site covariance decays geometrically as \((\rho^2)^d\), and the finite-\(n\) total covariance is explicit.
