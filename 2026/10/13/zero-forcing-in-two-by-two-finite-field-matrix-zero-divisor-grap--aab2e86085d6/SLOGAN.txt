For the underlying zero-divisor graph of \(M_2(\mathbb F_q)\), the zero forcing number is exactly \(q^3+q^2-3q-1\). The proof converts white image-kernel types into edges of a bipartite graph on two projective lines: every force deletes a leaf edge, so a forceable white profile has at most \(2q\) edges, and an explicit profile attains that bound.
