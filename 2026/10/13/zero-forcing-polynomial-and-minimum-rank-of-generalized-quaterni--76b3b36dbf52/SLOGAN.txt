For the full cyclic graph of \(Q_{4n}\), the zero forcing polynomial is \(u^{3n-2}((u+2)^{n+1}(u+2n-2)-2^{n+1}(2n-2))\). Every minimum forcing set is the complement of one representative from all but one canonical clique class, giving \(Z=M=3n-1\), \(2^{n+1}n^2\) minimum sets, and real minimum rank \(n+1\).
