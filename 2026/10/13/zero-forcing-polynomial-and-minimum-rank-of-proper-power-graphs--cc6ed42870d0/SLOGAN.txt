For distinct odd primes \(p,q\), \(d\ge2\), and \(t=(p^d-1)/(p-1)\), the proper power graph of \(C_p^d\times C_q\) has \(\mathcal Z(u)=u^{p^d q-t-3}(u+q-1)(u+q(p-1))^{t-1}(u(u+q(p-1))+t(p-1)^2(q-1))\). In particular \(Z=M=p^d q-t-3\) and the real symmetric minimum rank is \(t+2\), with all minimum forcing sets given by one double-white line branch and one white \(q\)-element.
