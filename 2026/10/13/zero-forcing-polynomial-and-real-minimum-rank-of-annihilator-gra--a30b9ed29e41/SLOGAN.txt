For \(R=\mathbb F_q^3\) with \(q\ge3\), Badawi's annihilator graph has \(Z=M=3q(q-1)-4\) but constant real symmetric minimum rank \(4\), and its entire zero forcing polynomial has five explicit terms. Minimum forcing sets are exactly complements of one white representative in one of twelve forcing four-class profiles, giving \(3q^2(q-1)^5\) minimizers.
