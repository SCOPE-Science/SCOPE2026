For distinct primes \(p,q\), the annihilating-ideal graph of \(\mathbb Z/(p^2q^2)\mathbb Z\) has \(\mathcal Z(u)=4u^2+24u^3+33u^4+21u^5+7u^6+u^7\) and \(Z=\operatorname{tw}=\operatorname{pw}=2\). Its four minimum zero forcing sets are the two adjacent pairs along each pure-prime side together with the two pairs joining a pure prime ideal to \((pq)\).
