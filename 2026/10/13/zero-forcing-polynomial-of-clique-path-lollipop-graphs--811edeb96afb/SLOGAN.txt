For a nondegenerate clique-path lollipop \(L(k,\ell)\), every zero forcing set is controlled by the number of omitted nonattachment clique vertices and one rooted path condition: with one such clique omission and the attachment vertex white, the path must contain its far endpoint or a consecutive selected pair. This gives the full polynomial and shows that exactly \(2k-1\) minimum sets have size \(k-1\).
