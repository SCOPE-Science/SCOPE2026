# Review

## Correctness

PASS. The published blow-up theorem gives independent classes indexed by nonempty proper subsets with disjointness adjacency and product weights. False twins give the necessary at-most-one-white condition. Because every class has size at least two, an inclusion-minimal white type is forced from its complementary type, proving sufficiency for arbitrary rank. The Boolean quotient is unimodular, giving a rank witness of size \(2^r-2\), and \(M\le Z\) makes it sharp. Exact subset-product inversion recovers all local proper-ideal counts.

## Originality

PASS. The structural co-maximal literature gives the Boolean blow-up but no zero forcing theorem. Recent modular-compression work gives the generic twin-type reduction and is treated as prior coverage. The closest own three-factor result is a strict special case. Targeted searches did not locate the arbitrary-r forcing classification, minimum-rank equality, or polynomial-to-parameter inversion proved here.

## Value

PASS. The theorem makes the zero forcing polynomial a complete graph invariant on a natural algebraic class of unbounded Boolean rank. It also gives every minimum forcing set, exact maximum nullity and minimum rank, and an explicit inverse map to all local ideal counts; for chain rings those counts are nilpotency lengths.
