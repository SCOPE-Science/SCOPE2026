For every finite product of \(r\ge2\) nonfield local rings, the co-maximal ideal graph has \(\mathcal Z(u)=u^{N-(2^r-2)}\prod_{\varnothing\ne S\subsetneq[r]}(u+\prod_{i\in S}\lambda_i)\), with \(Z=M=N-(2^r-2)\) and real minimum rank \(2^r-2\). The nonzero roots recover the complete unordered multiset of local proper-ideal counts, so the polynomial determines the graph within this class.
