For \(\mathbb Z/(p^\alpha q^\beta)\), the ideal-intersection graph has a three-class form and a three-term zero forcing polynomial with \(Z=M=N-2\) and real minimum rank \(2\), outside the squarefree two-prime boundary. Within this entire two-prime family, the polynomial fails to recognize the graph in exactly two cases: exponent pairs \(\{1,9\}\) versus \(\{3,4\}\), and \(\{1,11\}\) versus \(\{2,7\}\).
