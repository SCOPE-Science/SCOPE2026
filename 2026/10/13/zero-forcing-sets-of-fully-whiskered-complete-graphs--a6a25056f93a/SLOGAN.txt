For the fully whiskered clique \(K_n\odot K_1\), zero forcing is controlled exactly by the number of empty core-leaf pairs: none is always allowed, while one is allowed precisely when an initially black leaf is present. This gives \(\mathcal Z=(2x+x^2)^n+n((2x+x^2)^{n-1}-x^{n-1})\) and exactly \(n(2^{n-1}-1)\) minimum forcing sets.
