For distinct primes \(p,q\) and \(a\ge2\), the annihilating-ideal graph of \(\mathbb Z/(p^a q)\mathbb Z\) satisfies \(Z=\operatorname{tw}=\operatorname{pw}=a-1\). A canonical forcing set consists of the \(a-1\) pure \(p\)-power ideals, while \(a\) paired ideals give a matching \(K_a\)-minor certificate.
