The published age-structured COVID-19 equations couple no distinct spatial locations, so an initially compact infection cannot propagate to uninfected sites. Their own Laplace speed formula has the form \(\mathcal L(c,\lambda)=F(c\lambda)\) with \(F'<0\), forcing \(c^*=0\) and excluding the positive-speed double roots used to support the reported kilometer-per-week speeds.
