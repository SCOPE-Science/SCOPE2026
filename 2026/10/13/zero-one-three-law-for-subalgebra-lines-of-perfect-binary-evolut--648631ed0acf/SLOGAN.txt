Over every finite field of characteristic two, a uniformly selected invertible \(2\times2\) evolution-algebra structure matrix has exactly \(0\), \(1\), or \(3\) one-dimensional subalgebras, with respective probabilities \(1/3\), \(1/2\), and \(1/6\). Each such subalgebra contains a unique nonzero idempotent, so one third of these perfect algebras have no nonzero idempotent.
