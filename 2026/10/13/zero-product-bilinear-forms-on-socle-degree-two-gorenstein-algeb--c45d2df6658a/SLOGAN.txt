For every standard graded Artinian Gorenstein algebra with Hilbert vector \((1,d,1)\), the scalar bilinear forms vanishing on zero products admit a complete \((2d+4)\)-parameter normal form, while their symmetric factorization obstruction is always one-dimensional. Over \(\mathbb F_q\), the number of nonsingular symmetric forms that do not factor through multiplication is exactly \(q^{d+1}(q-1)(q-2)\), vanishing precisely over \(\mathbb F_2\).
