The normal projective cut sixfold of \(K_{2,3}\) has exactly 156 reduced singular components: 144 copies of \(\mathbb P^3\) with generic conifold transverse germs, and twelve copies of \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\) with generic transverse cones over the Segre \((\mathbb P^1)^3\). Its singular support is non-equidimensional and equals its non-\(\mathbb Q\)-factorial support.
