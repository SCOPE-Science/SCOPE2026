# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The normal-fan lattice is the parity sublattice M of Z^6, not the ambient edge lattice; its dual consists of half-integral coordinates with integral sum. The free sum of two 3-cubes has 36 facets and exact f=(16,88,204,240,144,36,1). At each mixed face the number of active primitive normal rays is (3-a)(3-b) versus cone rank 5-a-b, and the only simplicial cones are saturated smooth ones. The maximal nonsmooth faces are 12 unmixed squares and 12x12 edge-edge joins, giving 156 irreducible reduced components; the other singular strata lie in their closures. Edge-edge local semigroups yield the conifold relation uv=wz; square local semigroup generated by 8 sign vectors is the Segre (P1)^3 cone. The face lattices give P3 and P1xP1 orbit closures. Independently enumerated 729 faces and 144+12 maximal nonsimple faces. This certifies reduced support and generic germs in all characteristics, not the Jacobian scheme or intersection germs.

## originality

PASS

The full K2,3 cut variety was already studied; Sturmfels–Sullivant give global smoothness, 19 quadratic generators and degree 72. Chimani et al classify simple/simplicial cut polytopes and stress the integral-lattice distinction. Neither those passages nor the relevant checked numerical invariants give the face-indexed 156-component singular stratification with two generic transverse toric semigroup types. Comparisons rely on actual face/component implications, not a claim that all descriptions are absent from all papers.

## value

PASS

The entire reduced singular locus and generic transverse types of a naturally small nontrivial cut toric sixfold are geometric invariants not determined by its cut-ideal generator count, Ehrhart series, or global non-smoothness. Mixed-dimensional components and their exact toric models are useful in class-group, singular-strata and resolution questions. The intrinsic parity lattice is essential rather than an arbitrary recoding.
