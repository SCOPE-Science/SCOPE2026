Tracking every \(2\)-adic valuation of \(N-1\) gives an explicit family of integers that cannot be quotients of binary antipalindromes. Its exact lower density is \(\frac16-\frac13\sum_{h\ge1}1/[2^h(2^h+1)]=0.088166593449\ldots\), more than five times the previously published lower bound for all such unrepresentable integers.
