## Correctness — PASS
The local root-lifting count is exact for odd primes: nonzero quadratic residues modulo a prime lift with one forbidden class among the lifts, and the prime-valuation-one classes contribute a separate \(p-1\) residues. A CRT root simultaneously supplies the binary-square and valuation-one conditions. The 2020 two-prime Zumkeller criterion plus the established coprime-multiplier lemma certify the resulting positive complement. The bound \(15376^2\) is valid uniformly for all ten primes, and the binary strata form a complete disjoint partition. The explicit denominator and numerator were recomputed with rational arithmetic and checked by standalone residue enumerations. No finite check substitutes for the infinite proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The paper explicitly leaves the density of failures as Conjecture 4.21, while providing a quantitative elementary modular coverage of \(5/9\). This result raises rigorously guaranteed coverage to more than \(76.43\%\), identifies a concrete finite local obstruction system, and supplies uniform constructive witnesses beyond an explicit bound. It is a genuinely quantitative partial resolution of a stated additive-density question, without claiming conjectural density one.
