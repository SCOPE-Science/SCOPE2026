In a pinned three-oscillator chain coupled to two classical Langevin baths, the unique central mass maximizing stationary heat conductance is \(m_*(b)=1+b/(1+b)^2\), independent of bath friction and temperatures. The excess inverse conductance at any other positive mass is exactly \(2\gamma(b+1)^3(m-m_*)^2/[1+\gamma^2(b+1)m]\), so a slightly heavier middle oscillator always outperforms the uniform-mass chain for positive pinning.
