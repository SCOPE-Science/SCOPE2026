For heat flow on the integer lattice, the exact \(\ell^1\)/\(\ell^\infty\) time-derivative norm follows a strictly ordered sequence of Bessel sign-change thresholds, with \(\tau_m=m^2/2+1/4+O(m^{-2})\). Its sharp large-time leading constant is \(\sqrt{2/(\pi e)}\), but the next coefficient oscillates with the distance of \(\sqrt{2t}\) from the half-integers, filling an entire interval rather than approaching a fixed constant.
