For a fixed matrix coordinate, replacing \(k\) independent Gaussian probes by their QR-orthonormalized span multiplies the self-normalized diagonal-estimation error in distribution by an independent \(\sqrt{\operatorname{Beta}((n-k)/2,(k-1)/2)}\), giving strict improvement at every positive error threshold when \(1<k<n\). The mean-square risk drops by exactly \((n-k)/(n-1)\) when \(k>2\), while the same sharp moment threshold \(p<k\) survives until a complete basis makes the estimator exact.
