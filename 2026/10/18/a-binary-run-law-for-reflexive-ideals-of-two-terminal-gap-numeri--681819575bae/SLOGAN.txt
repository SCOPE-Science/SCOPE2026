For numerical semigroups with conductor \(2m\), removing one terminal element leaves \(2^{m-1}+1\) normalized ideals, all reflexive; removing two leaves \(2^{m-1}+5\), but exactly \(a_{m-1}+1\) are reflexive, where \(a_n\) counts binary words without isolated zeroes. The reflexive proportion in the two-terminal-gap family decays like \(C(\lambda/2)^{m-1}\) with \(\lambda\approx1.754878\).
