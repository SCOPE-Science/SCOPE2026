For uniformly random labeled-tree graphical zonotopes, the expected intrinsic Euclidean surface area is asymptotic to \(\sqrt{2/\pi}\,n^{3/2}\log n\), although every tree realization has the same volume \(\sqrt n\). A uniformly chosen facet has its area divided by \(\sqrt n\) converging to the square root of a Borel(1) variable with infinite mean, explaining the critical logarithmic excess.
