For full Boolean modal valuations on countable frames with outdegrees in a fixed finite set \(S\), exactly one permitted outdegree is computably harmless, whereas allowing any two different outdegrees makes the extension principle equivalent to \(\mathrm{ACA}_0\). The unique full valuation always needs at most one oracle jump, and a single depth-one test witnesses that this bound is sharp whenever \(|S|\ge2\).
