# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** For an arbitrary pair, the first-occurrence permutation fixes the first surviving letter, and its repeated projection is the only balanced alternating word beginning there. This proves the exact projection equivalence with no boundary exceptions for \(k\ge1\). The graph consequences follow by applying the respective edge definitions. The circle-graph sharpness uses the independently published \(\mathcal G_1\) classification and classical circle-graph representation theorem. The packaged checker exhaustively tests \(4348\) small words plus \(2000\) seeded words; those tests are supporting evidence, not infinite proofs.

**Originality — PASS within examined evidence.** Adamson et al. [1] introduce projection equality and establish permutation graphs at uniformity one, cycle graphs at uniformity two, and a different inclusion for \(12\)-representability. The present arbitrary-\(k\) first-occurrence construction and complete circle-graph inclusion are not statements in the examined full text. Fleischmann et al. [2] establish circle graphs as the classical \(2\)-uniform class, but do not address the newer projection-equality model. Targeted primary-literature, published-finding corpus, and research-literature searches found no covering statement. The short construction could be latent folklore, so historical priority is not certified.

**Value — PASS.** The all-\(k\) canonical embedding relates two otherwise distinct graph representations without any increase in multiplicity or length. At uniformity two it proves membership of the entire circle-graph class and determines the sharp one-versus-two minimum, a natural class-level question motivated by the new graph model. This is a structural classification rather than merely another cycle witness.

**Limitations.** The construction is one-way and gives no classification of all two-uniform projection graphs. The illustrative \(C_5\) membership is already covered by the origin paper and is not advanced as new. A covering earlier formulation could have escaped the searched literature; independent audit remains unperformed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
