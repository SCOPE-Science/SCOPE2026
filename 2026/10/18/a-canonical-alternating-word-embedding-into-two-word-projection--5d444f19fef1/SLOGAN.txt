Every classical \(k\)-uniform word representation is converted, without increasing word length or multiplicity, into two \(k\)-uniform words whose equal two-letter projections encode exactly the same graph: pair the original word with the \(k\)-fold repetition of its first-occurrence permutation. Consequently all circle graphs admit two \(2\)-uniform projection words, and their minimum multiplicity is \(1\) exactly for permutation graphs and \(2\) otherwise.
