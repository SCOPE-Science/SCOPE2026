For every rank-\(r\) sparse-paving matroid of corank at least three, normal-fan toric fixed charts have torsion-free class group \(\mathbb Z^{1+t(B)}\) and are \(\mathbb Q\)-Gorenstein precisely when they are Gorenstein, with a sharp balanced rank-three exception. The Vámos matroid yields \(17\) Gorenstein and \(48\) non-\(\mathbb Q\)-Gorenstein singular fixed points, partitioned into six symmetry orbits.
