A single shared habitat-catastrophe clock can generate an environmentally induced species-covariance matrix of full rank: its exact Cauchy kernel has rank equal to the number of distinct recovery rates. In the independent exponential-recovery null model, all pairwise occurrence correlations are strictly below the sharp ceiling \(1/2\), and any positive local extinction rate makes the correlation peak at one unique intermediate catastrophe frequency. The ceiling fails for deterministic recovery delays, even without species interactions.
