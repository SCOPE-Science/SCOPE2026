# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The claim fixes the real space, sphere, Yost index, scalar kernel, and finite LP exactly. The fresh-coordinate limit provides the upper bound, while the \(\pm e_1\) construction and Jensen convexity give an exact reverse inequality for every finite empirical scalar distribution. Weak compactness of probability measures and continuity of payoff justify the max/min rather than a formal supremum. Reflection symmetrization and the piecewise angular derivative estimate prove the \(\pi/(4N)\) guarantee for every positive integer \(N\). Whitley's index is exactly \(\sqrt2\), and the strict index gap is proved analytically without appealing to numerical experiments. The critical endpoint \(s=\pm1\) and potential duplicate vectors are handled by continuous perturbations.

Search absence alone was not treated as a novelty proof. The uninspected 1998 full text and imperfect bibliographic coverage remain real originality risks.

A classical natural index on an elementary mixed-sum Banach space is computed in the sense of a complete convergent finite-game prescription with a proved uniform error guarantee. The proof also supplies a quantitative strict separation from the corresponding thinness index. Finite LP approximants address the original invariant to arbitrary precision and are not arbitrary parameter slices; this is stronger than a mere floating-point guess or standalone elementary inequality.
