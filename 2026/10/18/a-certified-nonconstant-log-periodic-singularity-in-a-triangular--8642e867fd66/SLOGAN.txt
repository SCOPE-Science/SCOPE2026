For the cubic-fiber planar map \(T(x,y)=(x(4-xy)/2,\,y(1+xy)/3)\), the stable projection onto \(xy=2\) diverges at the repelling boundary \(xy\downarrow1\) with exponent \(\log(3/2)/\log(7/6)\) and an exact, analytically defined, genuinely nonconstant log-periodic prefactor. A 1000-step interval certificate proves that two phase values differ by more than \(1.2\times10^{-24}\); a two-term relative correction is \(1+2(xy-1)+O((xy-1)^2)\).
