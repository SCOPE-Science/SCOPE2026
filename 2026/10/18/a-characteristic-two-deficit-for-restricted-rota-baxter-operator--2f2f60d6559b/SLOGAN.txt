For a split toral almost-abelian restricted Lie algebra \(Ke\ltimes_T V\), the derived-ideal-valued weight-zero Rota–Baxter operators correspond to square-zero endomorphisms \(F\) of \(V\) and arbitrary vectors \(u\). The restricted graph condition is automatic in odd characteristic, but in characteristic two it forces \(Fu=0\), producing explicit rank-resolved Gaussian-binomial counts and a strict finite-field deficit whenever \(\dim V\ge2\).
