The first category to reach occupancy \(r\) is also first to reach \(r+1\) with exact probability \((\mathbb ET_{r+1}-\mathbb ET_r)/m\). For fixed \(r\ge2\), this rare lineage event has probability asymptotic to \((r+1)!^{1/(r+1)}\Gamma(1+1/(r+1))m^{-1/(r+1)}\) and changes the normalized next-hit time from a Weibull law to its equilibrium distribution.
