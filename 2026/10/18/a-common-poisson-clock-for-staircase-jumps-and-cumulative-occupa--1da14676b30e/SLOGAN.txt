For the discrete hyperbolic staircase, jump count and cumulative height admit a shared marked-Poisson approximation with \(O_{\mathbb P}(\log\log(np))\) error whenever \(np\to\infty\), including vanishing jump parameters. Their centered fluctuations have a bivariate Gaussian limit with covariance matrix \(\begin{psmallmatrix}1&1\\1&2\end{psmallmatrix}\), revealing dependence that neither marginal law determines.
