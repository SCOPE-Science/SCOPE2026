A Fourier-endpoint family of explicit entire weights makes weighted composition operators on the classical Gaussian Fock space compact for every \(\gamma>0\), yet Hilbert--Schmidt exactly when \(\gamma>1/2\), negatively answering the 2026 compact-versus-Hilbert--Schmidt question. The exact Hilbert--Schmidt energy and the operator-norm rate of compact approximation reveal a sharp logarithmic critical transition.
