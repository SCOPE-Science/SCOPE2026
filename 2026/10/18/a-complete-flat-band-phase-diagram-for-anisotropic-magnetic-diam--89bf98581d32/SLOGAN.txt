For positive-weight magnetic diamond chains with arbitrary periodic onsite potentials, the number of flat energies is exactly \(0\), \(1\), or \(3\): unequal side potentials require an explicit zero- or half-flux tuning, while the three-band cage occurs exactly at half flux with equal arm-hopping products. Every flat energy has a two-cell compact state; a one-cell state occurs exactly at zero flux, equal side potentials and \(ad=bc\).
