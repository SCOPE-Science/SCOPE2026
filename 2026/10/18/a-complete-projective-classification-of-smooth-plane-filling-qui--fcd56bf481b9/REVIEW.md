# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Seven independent evaluation conditions give a fourteen-dimensional binary vector space. An exhaustive enumeration of all 16383 nonzero forms produces explicit extension-field singular-point witnesses for 14871 forms. The 1512 survivors split into ten group orbits; exact Gröbner computations certify the Jacobian gradient unit ideal on all three standard affine charts for each representative. These last certificates establish geometric smoothness without relying on a finite search cutoff. The executable standalone verification was run successfully.

**Originality — PASS (best knowledge).** Asgarli–Ghioca, arXiv:2307.03072v1, studies this degree regime and explicit families but does not tabulate all smooth binary quintics or ten rational projective equivalence classes. Homma–Kim, arXiv:0903.1918, concerns minimal-degree quartics at the binary field. Published-record searches for the complete count, Fano-plane aliases and projective orbits returned no covering claim. The closest source in the same area is the Asgarli–Ghioca paper. An older or poorly indexed classification remains a residual bibliographic possibility; search absence is not uniqueness proof.

**Value — PASS.** This is a complete classification in the first field and the first nonminimal degree of the natural plane-filling problem, showing that the parameter family of prior constructions is only part of a ten-orbit landscape. It determines the full rational projective stabilizer distribution, a geometric feature neither existence theorems nor a single example can supply. This is a motivated finite structural classification, not an arbitrary isolated coefficient computation.

**Limitations.** The classification is over the binary field and up to rational projective linear transformations. It does not address abstract isomorphism classes. Geometric smoothness depends on independently rerunnable exact Gröbner computations. No independent audit has been performed.
