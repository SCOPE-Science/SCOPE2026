The lower-plus-upper Wythoff representation count, divided by its exact maximum, has the limiting law \((3-\sqrt5)\operatorname{Unif}[0,1]+(\sqrt5-2)\delta_1\). A positive density \(\sqrt5-2\) of integers attains that maximum, and both the full cumulative distribution and saturation count have \(O((\log X)^2)\) counting errors.
