For invariantly sampled affine hyperplanes meeting a regular \((N-1)\)-simplex, the logarithm of its random section's vertex count, divided by \(\log N\), converges to the continuous law \(2-U^2\), where \(U\) is uniform on \([-1,1]\). The exact mean is \(N(N-1)/(\sqrt\pi\,M_N)\sim N^2/[2\sqrt{2\pi\log N}]\), even though the count divided by its mean tends to zero in probability.
