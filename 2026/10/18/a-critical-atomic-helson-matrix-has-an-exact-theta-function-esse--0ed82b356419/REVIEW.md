# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The positive-form phase transition follows from rank-one zeta norms and trace-norm summability. At the critical exponent, the column Gram is a half-line Toeplitz operator with summable hyperbolic-secant diagonals plus an absolutely summable, hence trace-class, error. Plane-wave Weyl sequences yield the Toeplitz essential interval; the Jacobi Fourier series and theta identities determine both endpoints. Positive nonzero essential spectra are preserved under interchanging the two bounded operator products. Finite computations check the critical arithmetic without substituting for the proof.

**Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk.** The two closest Helson papers address general moment representations, boundedness and trace-class criteria, not the specified explicit essential band in their accessible descriptions. An earlier compact geometric-atomic spectral asymptotic requires positive polynomial damping and cannot entail the noncompact endpoint via substitution. Searches by atomic measure, Gram kernel, hyperbolic-secant symbol, Jacobi theta formulation and essential spectral band found no directly covering result. The motivating paper was available only in abstract, and broader Toeplitz or sampling literature could contain an equivalent theorem; independent priority review remains necessary.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The result identifies a noncompact critical boundary in a natural moment-operator family and computes its entire positive essential spectrum, not just a boundedness criterion or approximate norm. The exact theta endpoints distinguish geometrically sampled atomic moment matrices from the continuous-measure Hilbert model. The phase trichotomy explains the nature of the boundary, while the critical band is a quantitatively reusable spectral invariant.

**Limitations.** The essential spectrum at zero and possible discrete outliers are not determined. The primary motivating preprint's full text could not be inspected, leaving a real residual originality risk; no independent audit has been performed.
