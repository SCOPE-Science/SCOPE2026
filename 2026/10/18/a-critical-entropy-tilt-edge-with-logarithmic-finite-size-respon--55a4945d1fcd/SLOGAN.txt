In an open antisymmetric Ornstein–Uhlenbeck chain whose stationary Gaussian factorizes, the entropy-production generating function at a critical tilt is exactly \(\kappa[N-\cot(\pi/(2(N+1)))]/2\). Its tilted mean grows as \((8J^2L_*/(\pi\kappa))N\log N\) rather than remaining extensive, with a sharp next-order correction.
