For the expanding circle maps \(x\mapsto bx+\varepsilon\sin(2\pi x)\), setting \(\varepsilon=\tau/\sqrt n\) makes the logarithmic multipliers of uniformly chosen period-dividing-\(n\) points converge, after subtracting \(n\log b\), to a Gaussian with mean \(\pi^2\tau^2/b^2\) and variance \(2\pi^2\tau^2/b^2\). Exact finite-period derivative identities identify the nonzero curvature and the initial fluctuation variance before taking the joint limit.
