For commutators of two monomial Toeplitz operators on every power-weighted Bergman disk, the generic singular values decay as \(j^{-2}\), but parallel noncommuting exponent vectors force the sharper and exact \(j^{-3}\) law. Their leading coefficients, critical weak-Schatten residues, and approximation rates are explicit, with the Bergman weight entering as the universal factor \(\alpha+1\).
