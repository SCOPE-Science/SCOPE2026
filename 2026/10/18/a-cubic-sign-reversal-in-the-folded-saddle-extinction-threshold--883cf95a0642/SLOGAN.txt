For the singular reduced strong-Allee predator–prey tipping threshold, the previously undetermined cubic correction is \(\frac{(2-a)^4[4(1-a)^3+(1-a)^2-1]}{8(1-a)}\delta^3\). Its sign flips at exactly one Allee strength \(a\approx0.443306905\), so the published quadratic critical-rate approximation changes from underestimating to overestimating the true threshold as \(a\) crosses that value, for sufficiently small positive \(\delta\).
