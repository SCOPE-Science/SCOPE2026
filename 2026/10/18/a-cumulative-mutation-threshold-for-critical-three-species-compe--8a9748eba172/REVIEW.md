# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The positive solution has logistic total biomass exactly, and its normalized composition has the skew-cyclic vector field plus nonnegative all-to-all mutation. The normalized entropy \(H\) obeys the exact derivative \(\dot H=-\mu D\), with \(3H\le D\le C(H_0)H\) on the compact interior entropy sublevel. Thus infinite accumulated exposure drives the unique uniform composition, with sharp asymptotic entropy exponent \(-6\) from the quadratic Taylor ratio. Finite exposure keeps entropy strictly positive; an asymptotically autonomous argument based on vanishing mutation tail mass and \(S\to1\) forces the omega-limit to contain every point of the nonzero-speed periodic entropy level. Exact-rational and numerical corroboration is available in `verify.py`; finite checks do not replace these proofs.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk

The full primary 2022 Jaramillo–Mrad–Stepien manuscript establishes the model and analyzes fixed mutation rates, and the full 2015 Toupo–Strogatz study likewise examines autonomous mutation patterns. The 2019 Boone–Piliouras zero-sum game result supplies prior mutation-free periodicity; the unmutated orbit structure is not claimed as new. Related published results for neutral May–Leonard stationary laws, cycle periods, and distinct forcing/chemostat mutations also do not state the all-measurable-schedule mutation-budget threshold or the universal per-budget decay exponent. An abstract nonautonomous replicator convergence theorem could conceivably imply the result and is a remaining publication-priority risk. No absence-of-search-result argument is taken as proof of uniqueness.

## Value — PASS

A finite mutation-rate snapshot cannot determine long-run stabilization: the integrated transition exposure does. The exact classification applies even to irregular, bursty schedules and distinguishes residual biodiversity cycles from full homogenization, with the asymptotic rate \(-6\) and a sharp threshold for power-law intervention decay. This addresses a motivated structural question in population dynamics without relying on finite simulations.

## Limits

The theorem is deterministic, well mixed, exactly critical and symmetric; it makes no claim for stochastic extinctions, other competition lines or an asymptotic orbit phase. The positive-initial-data assumption is essential to entropy and periodic interior levels. Publication priority is subject to future independent checks.
