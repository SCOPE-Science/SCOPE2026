# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The observed component of isotropic Poisson-refreshed propulsion obeys a closed Markov polynomial generator. Stationary monomial identities yield the fourth-order Laplace transform; the executable checker reconstructs it from the displayed generator. Its inverse transform is a strictly positive convolution times a linear function of a strictly increasing convolution ratio, furnishing all-parameter signs and uniqueness, including the critical equality case. Independent matrix-exponential comparisons corroborate but do not prove the global theorem.

## Originality — PASS (self-review)

Frydel (arXiv:2309.12537) supplies the oscillator setup and stationary moment hierarchy. Sun–Ye–Podgornik (arXiv:2409.00578) handle finite-duration tumble one-time moment dynamics. Guéneau–Majumdar–Schehr (arXiv:2602.08436) give arbitrary-dimensional stationary marginal laws. Knight–Pruessner (arXiv:2605.29201) study multitime irreversibility for one-dimensional active dynamics. The assessed contribution is the explicit **all-dimensional stationary two-time fourth-order ordering and its sharp, unique zero-crossing phase diagram**, which is not a stated result in inspected source material. Common generator methods may implicitly contain intermediate formulas, and independent priority analysis is necessary. A failed literature search alone is not counted as novelty evidence.

## Scientific value — PASS

The sign and uniqueness theorem identifies an exact qualitative change in time-ordered higher statistics, starting at the natural dimension-five boundary. It separates temporal-order information from equal-time stationary moments and gives a motivated parameter classification of the harmonic active-particle model. This remains useful without a new general algorithm or a claim about total entropy production.

## Restrictions

The proof treats stationary harmonic dynamics with fixed-speed independently isotropic Poisson orientation refresh and no translational thermal noise; it does not classify other reorientation mechanisms or conclude reversibility when this one contrast vanishes. Symbolic and numerical validation is same-model only; no external independent audit has occurred.
