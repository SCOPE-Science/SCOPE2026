For a harmonically confined run-and-tumble particle whose orientation is independently redrawn on the unit sphere, the stationary fourth-order time-order contrast is positive in every dimension \(d\le4\), but in each dimension \(d\ge5\) it changes sign **exactly once** above an explicit sharp refresh-rate threshold. The critical dimension-five ratio is \(\gamma/\kappa=6\), while higher-dimensional thresholds follow a closed piecewise formula; equality never causes a finite-lag crossing.
