For the centrally symmetric \(d\)-cube with exactly two opposite corners truncated by a depth \(t\in[0,2]\), the Mahler volume product has an exact all-dimensional formula. The **unique maximizing depth** is interior precisely in dimensions \(3,4,5\), but equals the maximum admissible depth \(t=2\) in every dimension \(d\geq6\): an exact dimension-six transition.
