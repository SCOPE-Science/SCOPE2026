# Review of the dominance criterion for zonotopal central cube sections

## Correctness

**PASS.** The exact domain is all nonzero real normals in every ambient dimension at least two, with any number of zero coordinates. The critical full-dimensional off-center-section lemma is proved by uniqueness of restricted coordinate-normal directions, compulsory opposite facet pairs under central symmetry, and the necessity of at least \(r-1\) active coordinate families for boundedness. Its equality cases are included. Projecting the largest-coordinate face onto the remaining nonzero coordinates gives an off-center section to which the lemma applies. The positive cases are explicit affine cubes or products of centrally symmetric planar polygons with cubes. Exact rational checks cover 289 sampled non-dominant four-coordinate normals and six five-coordinate cases, and 896 off-center three-cube cases; the universal claim rests on proof, not finite testing.

## Originality

**PASS, subject to residual bibliographic risk.** Anantharaman's full accessible paper states the dominant-coefficient condition as sufficient, gives special necessary cases, and explicitly leaves the full four-coordinate converse open. Winter (2021) supplies a general zonotope characterization but does not classify cube sections. The closest identified research record concerns the face vector of Haar-random four-cube sections, with the same generic dominance phase boundary, not the all-dimensional characterization including nongeneric and zero-coefficient normals. Targeted searches for zonoid, zonotope, cube-section, hyperplane, and finite-dimensional \(\ell_\infty\)-subspace formulations found no covering theorem. The closest four-cube record was available at abstract level only; an unpublished or differently indexed general result remains possible.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** This supplies an exact necessary-and-sufficient characterization for a natural geometric preservation problem, rather than one more numerical example. It settles the unclassified parameter region explicitly described in a primary article, extends beyond dimension four, and distinguishes zonotopes from nonzonoids (not merely nonzonotopes) with an exposed-face obstruction. The critical inequality is sharp at its equality boundary and handles lower-support degeneracies without exclusions.

## Limits

The result does not address noncentral sections, arbitrary codimension, or all sections of general zonotopes. The checker is finite corroboration only. The paper and record comparisons do not constitute an exhaustive global bibliographic guarantee.
