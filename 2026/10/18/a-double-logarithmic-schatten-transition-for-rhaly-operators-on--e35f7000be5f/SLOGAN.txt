On the classical Dirichlet Hilbert space, the logarithmically damped Rhaly operator has two sharp critical exponents: boundedness begins at \(\beta=1\), compactness and membership in every Schatten class \(\mathcal S_p\) with \(p>1\) begin at \(\beta>1\), and trace class begins only at \(\beta>2\). Consequently \(1<\beta\le2\) is a genuine all-nontrace-Schatten interval.
