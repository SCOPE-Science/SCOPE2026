For the Fano-plane Heawood graph, the \(16\)-dimensional rational second homology of the perfect-matching complex is the **bipartition-sign twist** of the ordinary matching complex's fourth homology under all \(336\) graph automorphisms. Thus the ordinary complex has one automorphism-invariant homology class, whereas the perfect-matching complex has none; a point-line duality has respective traces \(+4\) and \(-4\).
