For every finite unweighted cactus graph, the covariance of a uniformly selected, canonically normalized facet normal of its real Lipschitz-free unit ball is *exactly* the Green kernel \(L_c^+\) of a conductance network: even-cycle edges receive conductance \((r-1)/r\), while all other edges retain conductance one. The identity gives every expected squared potential difference as an effective resistance, an explicit covariance determinant, and reconstruction of the labeled cactus graph from second moments alone.
