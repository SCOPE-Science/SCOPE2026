In the 28-vertex Coxeter graph, every minimum total dominating set has ten vertices and is precisely the union of distance shells \(0,1,4\) around one unique center, yielding exactly \(28\) optima. Individual exchanges cannot connect them: the sharp simultaneous replacement threshold is \(4\), at which the reconfiguration graph is the connected distance-four graph of the Coxeter graph, of diameter \(3\).
