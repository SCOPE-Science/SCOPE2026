For the classical Bernstein–Kantorovich integral operator on \(L^2(0,1)\), the Hilbert–Schmidt energy minus the sum of squared eigenvalues converges to \(1/6\), with sharp first correction \(-5\sqrt\pi/(96\sqrt{n+1})\). This quantifies a persistent, finite spectral–singular energy discrepancy in a family that converges strongly to the identity.
