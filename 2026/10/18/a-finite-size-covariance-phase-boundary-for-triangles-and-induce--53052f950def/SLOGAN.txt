In \(G(n,p)\), triangle counts and induced chordless \(\ell\)-cycle counts have an exact covariance that changes from positive to negative once, at \(p=6(n-\ell)/((\ell-1)(3n-2\ell-2))\) whenever \(n>\ell\ge4\); at \(n=\ell\) their covariance is always negative. The threshold approaches the induced hole's edge-density fraction \(2/(\ell-1)\) from below, with an explicit finite-size correction.
