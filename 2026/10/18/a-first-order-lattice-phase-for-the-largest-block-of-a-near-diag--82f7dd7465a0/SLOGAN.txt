For a uniformly random partition into \(n-r\) blocks with \(r\asymp\sqrt n\), the probability that every block has size at most two admits an explicit first-order expansion in the moving ratio \(r^2/n\). Substituting \(r=\lfloor t\sqrt n\rfloor\) produces a nonvanishing fractional-part phase, disproving the existence of the single first-order coefficient proposed in a published set-partition conjecture.
