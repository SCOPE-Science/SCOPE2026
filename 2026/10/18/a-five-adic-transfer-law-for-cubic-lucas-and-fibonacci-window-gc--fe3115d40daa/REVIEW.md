# Scientific review: five-adic cubic-window transfer

## Correctness — PASS
The proof derives an explicit integral shift transformation from Fibonacci cubes to Lucas cubes with determinant \(5^6\), establishing identical valuations at every other prime. The prime-five argument proves the exact period obstruction, gives finite residue certificates modulo \(25\) and \(125\), and uses a five-adic invertible block-sum operator to lift the result to every multiple of twenty. This closes all positive window lengths. The stand-alone checker validates 320 parameters and each stated certificate; finite computation is corroboration rather than the unrestricted proof.

## Originality — PASS relative to inspected literature
Mbirika–Spilker (arXiv:2204.12626), inspected through its principal theorems and Section 6, covers square-window formulas and raises cube-window conjectures. The cited formulas do not prove the new cubic Fibonacci-to-Lucas ratio, and the cubic display is only conjectural. Guyer–Mbirika (arXiv:2104.12262) treats first powers. A search of equivalent formulations and published-result summaries located no stronger applicable result. Unindexed prior work may exist; an editorial issue in the published conjecture is not claimed as a wholly new mathematical result.

## Value — PASS
The ratio answers a natural comparison for two central recurrence sequences and reveals a sharp \(4\mid k\) boundary that differs from the known even-length square case. Its determinant and five-adic lifting mechanism explain the complete infinite-family classification, not just numerical coincidence or a small gain.

## Limits and risks
No higher-power or arbitrary-initial-condition generality is asserted. An equivalent result absent from searchable literature remains possible. Independently checked residue computations supplement, but do not replace, the proof.
