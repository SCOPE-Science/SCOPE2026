An explicit hyperbolic three-torus map has fair binary observations that are jointly independent at any three distinct times and on every four consecutive times, yet its first five-symbol law has the exact non-Bernoulli interaction \(\Pr(S_0=s_0,\ldots,S_4=s_4)=2^{-5}(1+s_0s_1s_2s_4/8265)\). A primitive-relation parity formula classifies the fourth-order dependence at arbitrary distinct times.
