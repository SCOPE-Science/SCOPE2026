In homogeneous-anchoring Friedkin–Johnsen networks with binary innate opinions, an added undirected edge cannot raise equilibrium polarization on four or fewer vertices, but it can on a five-vertex path. For an explicit five-person path and shortcut, the edge raises polarization when \(0<a<1.320180425\ldots\) and lowers it when \(a>1.320180425\ldots\), with a unique exact crossover.
