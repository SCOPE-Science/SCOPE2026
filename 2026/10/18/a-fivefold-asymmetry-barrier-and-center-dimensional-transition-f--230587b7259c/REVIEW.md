# Mathematical review

## Correctness
PASS. The lower bound is an exact sum of sixteen cut-switching reflection inequalities whose total forces \(s\ge5\). At equality the sixteen signed normal vectors have orthogonal Walsh columns, forcing a unique half-edge center on each \(K_5\) projection. The cited complete maximal-graph facet classification has only pentagonal and triangle facets; their center reflection ratios are exactly \(5\) and \(3\). Surjective coordinate projection transfers the factor-five upper bound to nonmaximal graphs and the lower bound from each retained \(K_5\). For maximal graphs the remaining four inequalities per triangle have \(1/6\) slack at the half center, establishing the complete center locus and its claimed affine dimension. The embedded rational checker replays support bounds, orthogonality, and a nonunique-center example.

## Originality
PASS, with qualification. The exact \(K_5\) base case and its unique half-center are already available in a known edge-transitive-graph asymmetry classification and are **not** presented as independent novelty. The 2019 primary paper classifies facets of all \(K_{3,3}\)-minor-free cut polytopes without calculating Minkowski asymmetry or the minimizing-center locus. The earlier known odd-girth asymmetry formula concerns \(K_5\)-minor-free graphs, so it excludes every graph claimed here. The new claim combines a sharp class-wide factor-five law beyond edge transitivity, mandatory center-coordinate rigidity, and a complete affine-dimensional/halfspace description for all maximal members. No equivalent statement was identified in the inspected primary texts or searched mathematical records. A different-language or unindexed prior formulation remains possible.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The result gives a graph-minor-controlled geometric invariant on an infinite nonplanar graph class, and a structural rather than merely numerical classification of centers. Even within graphs of fixed asymmetry \(5\), the full set of optimal centers changes from one point to polytopes of unbounded affine dimension as edges outside the \(K_5\) cores are introduced. The exact triangle-halfspace description makes that contrast constructive, as illustrated by the explicit two-dimensional family from a strict \(K_5\)-triangle clique-sum. This is not an extra entry for the already-known \(K_5\) case.

## Prior literature and limits
Closest full texts: Chimani–Juhnke-Kubitzke–Nover–Römer (2019), Theorem 3.3/Corollary 3.4; Barahona–Mahjoub (1986), Sections 1–2. The claims do not extend to arbitrary graphs with only a \(K_5\) minor; the exact full-center classification assumes fixed-vertex-set edge-maximality. The results depend on the published facet theorem. The listed computations are checks of critical finite algebra and examples, not proofs for all graph sizes.
