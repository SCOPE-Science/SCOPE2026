Every \(K_{3,3}\)-minor-free graph containing an actual \(K_5\) has a cut polytope of exact Minkowski asymmetry \(5\), with all \(K_5\)-edge coordinates pinned at \(1/2\) in every optimal center. In maximal such graphs the dimension of the entire center locus is exactly the number of edges outside all \(K_5\) subgraphs, giving a sharp uniqueness-versus-freedom criterion.
