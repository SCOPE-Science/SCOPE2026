The unit-level cubical hyperbolic-cosine Orlicz body has a high-dimensional Euclidean-volume ratio tending to \(\sqrt e\), not one, even though its defining function has an exactly quadratic tangent. At every fixed positive level \(\beta\), the limit is \(e^{\beta/2}\) and its sharp first relative correction is \(-(\beta+\beta^2/4)/n\).
