# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS
The upper bound is an explicit global \(2d\)-translate cover. For the lower bound, the \(2d\) vertices require different copies at all scales \(\lambda<(d-1)/d\). Every facet barycenter is at least \(2(d-1)/d\) from every vertex, so no such barycenter can occur in a vertex copy. With only three copies remaining, the sign-vector facet barycenters would three-color \(H_2(d,d-1)\); Theorem 3.3 of Harney–Gluesing-Luerssen gives chromatic number four. All inequalities and strict endpoints were checked. The exact finite checker corroborates the geometric identities but does not substitute for the published infinite-family coloring theorem.

## Originality — PASS
The 2023 Joós paper explicitly leaves \(d\ge6\) at \(2d+3\) copies as Conjecture 2; its earlier theorem handles only \(d=4,5\). The preexisting Hamming graph coloring result is not a covering theorem. The key new implication is the crosspolytope cover-to-coloring reduction, which lifts the low-dimensional obstruction to all \(d\ge6\). The 2021 Lian–Zhang analysis is three-dimensional, and the 2026 Lian–Xia–Xue–Zhang work targets \(2^d\) copies, not this minimum-size sharp lower bound. Searches for parameter aliases and stronger covering results revealed no complete prior implication. Bibliographic risk remains from unavailable full texts and unindexed literature; search absence is not a uniqueness certificate.
