For the natural skew cyclic quotient plane with coordinate weights \((1,2)\), the Euler lattice has a four-dimensional radical precisely at multiples of six. At order \(6k\) its integral cokernel is exactly \(\mathbb Z^4\oplus\mathbb Z/k\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/(3k)\mathbb Z\), exposing a growing torsion obstruction invisible from the Euler coefficients alone.
