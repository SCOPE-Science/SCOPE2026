# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof checks the Rota–Baxter identity by splitting image and kernel, classifies all central and noncentral Heisenberg subalgebras, and proves that a complement has exactly one central summand. Symplectic isotropic-frame counting yields the exact rank enumerator for all finite fields. A uniform bound on the product correction to the quadratic exponent gives the fixed-field discrete-Gaussian concentration. Direct exhaustive computations independently match all small configurations stated in `VERIFICATION.md`. The computations alone do not establish the general formula or asymptotic.

## Originality — PASS (self-review)
The Lie idempotent/projection correspondence is classical, and the dimension-three Heisenberg case was already classified. The novel claims are the higher-dimensional finite-field rank spectrum and its four-fifths concentration law. The primary full texts of Han–Khudoyberdiyev–Xie–Zhang (arXiv:2610.14838), Rathee (arXiv:2402.15428), and Cairns–Nikolayevsky (arXiv:2207.03068) were compared by statement and implication. The first supplies general factorization principles, the second classifies three-dimensional operators over complex numbers, and the third studies respectful decompositions with an additional cross-bracket condition. No inspected result gives the higher-rank formula or asymptotic. An equivalent result in unindexed work remains possible; the 2018 Ji–Hua source was available at abstract level only.

## Value — PASS
A complete formula for the rank distribution of a natural infinite operator family provides more than a finite example. Its two equiprobable rank phases and dimension-independent fluctuations expose a nontrivial typical geometry of complementary Lie subalgebras, independent of the finite-field characteristic.

## Limits
The theorem is limited to idempotent weight-minus-one linear maps, fixed finite fields in the asymptotic, and Heisenberg Lie algebras. It does not count affine translations, nonidempotent maps or group Rota–Baxter maps. There was no independent mathematical audit.
