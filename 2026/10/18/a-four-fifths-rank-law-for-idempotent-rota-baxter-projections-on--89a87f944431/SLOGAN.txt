For the \((2m+1)\)-dimensional finite-field Heisenberg Lie algebra, idempotent weight-\(-1\) Rota–Baxter projections have exact rank counts given by isotropic-subspace and complement enumeration. For each fixed field, their ranks concentrate on the two proportions \(2/5\) and \(3/5\), with bounded-width discrete-Gaussian fluctuations.
