The full relevant sections of the 1994 Andreev–Matheson paper establish local maximality along fixed Hilbert-sphere directions, including the exceptional second Fourier mode, rather than allowing the two coupled higher-order Fourier corrections. Targeted semantic and bibliographic searches for higher-order Chang–Marshall stability and near-extremal Taylor formulas did not identify a covering result; a negative search is not a uniqueness proof. Older specialized perturbative studies remain a residual historical-priority risk.

## Value

Completing the quartic and sextic squares identifies the forced nonlinear corrective modes and their exact residual coefficient. In particular, a natural quadratic- or quartic-order stability strengthening fails even locally, so any sharp quantitative stability theorem must handle this nonlinear degeneracy. This is more than a numerical accuracy refinement or arbitrary parameter slice.
