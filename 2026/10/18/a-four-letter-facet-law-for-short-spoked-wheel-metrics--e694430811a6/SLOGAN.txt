For each wheel with at least four rim vertices, shortening all spokes to any common length strictly between one-half and one produces a single stable new facet regime for the real Lipschitz-free unit ball. Its exact number of facets is \(2\cos(\pi m/3)+\sum_{z^3-z^2-3z-2=0}z^m\), so it grows like \(2.51154714169453^m\), rather than following the known unweighted-wheel count.
