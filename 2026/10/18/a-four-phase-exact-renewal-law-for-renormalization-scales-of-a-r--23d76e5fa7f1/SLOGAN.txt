For a uniformly chosen parameter in the Lowenstein–Vivaldi rhombus piecewise-isometry family, the canonical nested base-triangle scale hits the exact ratio \(q^m\), with \(q=\sqrt2-1\), at probability \(\frac14+\frac14(-q^2)^m+\frac{q^m}{2}(\cos(\pi m/2)-\sin(\pi m/2))\): a sharp four-phase modulation of the renewal limit \(1/4\). The first scale crossing below \(q^m\) has an explicit nondegenerate limiting lattice overshoot law.
