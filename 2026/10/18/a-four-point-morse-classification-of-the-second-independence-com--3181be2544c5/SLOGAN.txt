For every \(n\ge6\), the second independence complex of the pair-disjointness Kneser graph is exactly \(\operatorname{Ind}_2(KG(n,2))\simeq\bigvee^{\binom{n-1}{4}}S^3\). An explicit four-point-block discrete-Morse matching proves this integral homotopy classification in all orders, rather than extrapolating from finite computations.
