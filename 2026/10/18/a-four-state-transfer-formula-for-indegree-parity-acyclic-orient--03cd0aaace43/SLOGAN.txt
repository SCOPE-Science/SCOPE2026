For every labelled wheel \(W_m=K_1\vee C_m\), a four-state cyclic transfer product counts acyclic orientations with any prescribed vertex indegree-parity signature using \(O(m)\) arithmetic operations. Every feasible signature has \(2^m\) unrestricted orientations, but precisely two signatures have no acyclic realization when \(m\) is odd, and none are missing when \(m\) is even.
