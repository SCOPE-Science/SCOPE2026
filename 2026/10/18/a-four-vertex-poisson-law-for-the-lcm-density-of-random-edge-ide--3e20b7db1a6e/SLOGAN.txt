For Erdős–Rényi squarefree edge ideals, normalized LCM-lattice size has a sharp threshold at \(p\asymp n^{-4/3}\), governed by the first four-vertex paths rather than the giant component. At \(p\sim cn^{-4/3}\), its entire limiting law is \((7/8)^Z\) with \(Z\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(c^3/2)\); deterministically, maximal LCM lattices occur exactly for disjoint unions of stars.
