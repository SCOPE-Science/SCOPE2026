The inhabited four-world structures share every propositional valuation (p true only at b, q true only at c, all other letters false) but have convex constant neighborhood families {U,V} and {U,T,V}, U={a}, T={a,b}, V={a′,b,c}. U is covered by T, V is incomparable, hence both satisfy convexity. A structural induction on ALL unary-fragment formulas preserves support as a downward-closed predicate on the three propositional valuation types. All subsets of a realized type-profile have representing substates, so implication preserves this representation; universal neighbor quantification is determined by maximal profile π(V), existential by minimal π(U), even with unlimited nesting. The strict binary conditional ¬q ⇒ (¬p∧¬q) holds in the smaller neighborhood family, fails in the larger because T supports ¬q but not ¬p, proving indefinability over convex frames. Independently enumerated 19 downsets and tested both modalities and binary discriminator; no arbitrary depth cutoff assumed by proof.
