Even on convex inhabited neighborhood frames, the inquisitive binary conditional \(\Rrightarrow\) cannot be defined using both unary neighborhood quantifiers \(\boxplus\) and \(\mathsf E\). Two explicitly constructed convex models with four worlds agree on every formula of the unary fragment yet disagree on \(\neg q\Rrightarrow(\neg p\wedge\neg q)\), answering the second question of Ciardelli's Open Problem 4.6 negatively.
