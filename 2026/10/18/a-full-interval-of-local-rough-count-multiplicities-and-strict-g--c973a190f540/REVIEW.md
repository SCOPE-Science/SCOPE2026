# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof independently reconstructs the endpoint-admissible CRT residues, separates all possible interior positions into composite/small prime/large prime cases, and checks the potential cross-killing by another toggled large prime. The key parity and size observation is that an odd prime greater than half the interval cannot divide a nonzero even integer at most the interval length. It proves the infinite full-support claim; finite tests corroborate it (4,042 CRT patterns, five complete small local models).

**Originality — PASS, subject to identified literature risk.** The closest direct paper, Uğurdoğan (2026, arXiv:2610.10582v1), defines the same local count and cumulative constants, proves their finiteness and growth and conditional prime-gap asymptotics, and tabulates limited counts. Its inspected theorems, proofs, and numerical appendix do not state the full-interval support theorem or strict growth of every adjacent constant. Gafni–Tao (2025, arXiv:2508.06463v1) treat the earlier zero-count case. The complete 20-page 2025 primary manuscript was inspected, including Section 4, Table 1, and Remark 4.1; it treats the zero-rough-number case rather than all exact multiplicities. A residual risk remains for unindexed equivalent CRT constructions. Indexed adjacent-prime residue statistics concern other parameters; searches are evidence, not a mathematical novelty proof.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The new exact structure resolves whether the source’s consecutive cumulative multiplicity constants can have equal neighbors. Uniform positivity of every exact-count atom is not implied by positivity or large-threshold estimates for cumulative constants. A natural family \(h=P-1\) supports arbitrarily long unbroken intervals of multiplicity values, with a transparent binomial lower bound. This directly strengthens the conditional interpretation for prime gaps with *exactly* a given number of rough integers.

**Scope and risks.** No unconditional asymptotic for actual prime gaps follows. The result does not estimate the size of the positive gaps sharply, or provide the smallest anchor for a specified multiplicity; earlier equivalent constructions might be unindexed. The test program supplies finite corroboration, not infinite certification.
