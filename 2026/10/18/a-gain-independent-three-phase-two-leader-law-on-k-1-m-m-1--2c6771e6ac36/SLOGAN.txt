For two equal-strength noisy leaders on the almost-balanced complete tripartite network \(K_{1,m,m+1}\), the exact optimal pair has three gain-independent regimes: the two vertices in the largest part for \(m=1\), two vertices in the middle part for \(m=2,3\), and the universal vertex plus one middle-part vertex for every \(m\ge4\). The sharp inclusion threshold for the universal vertex is a structural one, independent of every finite positive feedback gain.
