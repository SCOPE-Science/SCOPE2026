Iterated mean transforms of positive weighted shifts with power-chirp weights have a sharp strong-versus-non-weak convergence transition at exponent \(\beta=1/2\), with Gaussian damping \(e^{-\vartheta^2/16}\) exactly at the boundary. Above the transition the iterates converge strongly to the unilateral shift, yet remain at operator-norm distance \(\varepsilon\) at every finite stage.
