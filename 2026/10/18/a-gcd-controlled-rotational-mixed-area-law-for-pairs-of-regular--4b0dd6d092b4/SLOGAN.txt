The area of the Minkowski sum of a regular \(n\)-gon and a rotated regular \(m\)-gon is exactly one cosine arch per angular cell of width \(2\pi\gcd(n,m)/(nm)\). The sharp maximum has \(n+m\) sides, the sharp minimum has \(n+m-\gcd(n,m)\) sides, and a random rotation follows an explicit truncated-arccosine area law.
