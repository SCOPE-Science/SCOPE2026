The published ordinary genus-four Artin–Schreier curve \(y^3-y=x^2/(x^3+2x^2+1)\) has a perfect-square Hasse–Weil numerator \((1+T+T^2+3T^3+9T^4)^2\). Its Jacobian is \(\mathbb F_3\)-isogenous to the square of the simple ordinary genus-two Jacobian of \(v^2=u^5+u^4+u^2+1\), and has \(225\) rational points.
