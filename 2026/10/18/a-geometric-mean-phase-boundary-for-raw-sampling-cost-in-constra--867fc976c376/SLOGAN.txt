In diagonal subspace-constrained Kaczmarz with null-proposal rejection, the Frobenius-best pinned row can require arbitrarily more random probes than an alternative although both finish in the same number of accepted steps. For three ordered singular values, the raw-probe-optimal endpoint pin switches exactly at \(s_2^2=s_1s_3\); the Frobenius sacrifice can be arbitrarily small in higher dimensions while the probe penalty diverges.
