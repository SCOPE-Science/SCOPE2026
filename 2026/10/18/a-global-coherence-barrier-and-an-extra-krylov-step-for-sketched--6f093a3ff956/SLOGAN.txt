On normalized equicorrelation kernels, the sketched block-Jacobi kernel-ridge preconditioner preserves the entire original spectral condition number for every nontrivial equal-block partition, independent of the ridge penalty. With nontrivial blocks it also turns a two-eigenvalue system into a three-eigenvalue system, so generic exact CG takes three steps instead of two; the result pinpoints a global-coherence obstruction to block-local acceleration.
