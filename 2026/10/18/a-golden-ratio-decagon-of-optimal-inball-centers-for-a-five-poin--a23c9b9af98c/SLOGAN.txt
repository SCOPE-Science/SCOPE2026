For the five-point cyclic metric with unit neighbor distances and diagonal distance \(t\in[1,2]\), the largest Euclidean ball in its real transportation-norm unit ball has a **unique center**, except at the golden ratio \(t=(1+\sqrt5)/2\), when its complete center set is a regular decagon in a two-dimensional Fourier plane. The exact inradius and decagon size follow from a full parametric facet classification; the already-known generic five-point facet total is not claimed as new.
