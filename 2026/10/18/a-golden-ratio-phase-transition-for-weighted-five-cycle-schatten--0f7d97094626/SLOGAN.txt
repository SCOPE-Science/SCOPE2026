For weighted five-cycle shifts of unit Hilbert–Schmidt norm, the exact minimum of the weighted Schatten-fourth numerical radius undergoes a golden-ratio transition at \(q=\sqrt5-1\): the optimizer changes from uniform weights to exactly three nonzero weights. At the critical parameter, the complete minimizing family is a two-dimensional Fourier slice of the probability simplex; at \(q=2\), a further continuum of three-or-two-support minimizers appears.
