For the three-dimensional parabolic map \(F(x,y,z)=(ay^2,by(1-z),c(1-x))\), the total number of primitive real two-cycles is exactly \(0\), \(1\), or \(3\), with sharp parameter boundaries. Its symmetric two-cycle is an attracting sink only in the shorter golden-ratio window \(-\frac{3+\sqrt5}{4}<b(1-c)<-1\); beyond the second cycle-creation threshold, both extra cycles have two-step Jacobian determinant \(4\) and cannot attract.
