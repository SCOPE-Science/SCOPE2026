For the normalized \(p\)-by-\(q\) Monge polytope, its exact Frobenius inradius contains the arithmetic correction \(\gcd(p,q)^2/6\), and the set of all largest-ball centers is an affine cube of dimension \(\gcd(p,q)-1\). Every Monge facet touches each ball; the number of contacting coordinate facets is determined by the endpoints of the cube parameters. The previously published square case is acknowledged as prior work.
