For products of \(r\) large odd primes, the first prime bases clear every quadratic-character obstruction to generating the unit group according to an exact binary-matrix rank law. When \(r\to\infty\) as slowly as \(r\log(r+2)=O(\log\log X)\), the number of prime bases needed beyond \(r\) has a universal nondegenerate limiting distribution, with probability \(0.288788\ldots\) of needing no extras.
