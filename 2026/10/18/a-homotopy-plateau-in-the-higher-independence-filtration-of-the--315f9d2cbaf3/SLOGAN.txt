The higher-independence filtration of the Möbius–Kantor graph has a genuine homotopy plateau: \(\operatorname{Ind}_2(M)\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Ind}_3(M)\) is a homotopy equivalence between wedges of three six-spheres. At the next stage, \(\operatorname{Ind}_4(M)\simeq\bigvee^{21}S^7\), and the sixth-homology classes all die; the integral persistence map is certified by a determinant-one minor.
