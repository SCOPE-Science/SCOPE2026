For the perturbed Euler partition reciprocal \(1/(P(z)-a_jz^j)\) with \(a_j=e^{\gamma\sqrt j}\), the growth exponent has the sharp crossover \(\sqrt j\,t_j\to(\gamma+\sqrt{\gamma^2+2\pi^2/3})/2\). Its marked-size fraction has a continuous profile symmetric around \(1/2\), while the scaled Gaussian fluctuation coefficient is symmetric and largest at the balanced point; fixed perturbation weights also have an explicit logarithmic correction and coefficient amplitude.
