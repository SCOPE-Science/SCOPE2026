For uniformly sampled union-closed team properties generated by singleton and doubleton teams, the number of singleton generators is typically \(\log_2 n-\log_2\log_2 n+O(1)\) with a discrete, lattice-phase-dependent limiting law. An explicit normalized first-order correction approximates its whole probability distribution with \(\ell^1\) error \(O((\log n)^{-2})\); the coefficient counts and underlying lacunary polynomial are previously known.
