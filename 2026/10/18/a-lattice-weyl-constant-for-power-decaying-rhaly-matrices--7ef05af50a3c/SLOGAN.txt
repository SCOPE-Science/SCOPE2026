For the power-decaying Rhaly matrix \((R_\gamma x)_j=j^{-\gamma}\sum_{k\le j}x_k\) on \(\ell^2\), every \(\gamma>1\) has a sharp lattice Weyl law \(s_n(R_\gamma)\sim\mathsf C_\gamma^\gamma n^{-\gamma}\), where \(\mathsf C_\gamma\) is the mean of \((2\sin(\theta/2))^{-1/\gamma}\) over \([0,\pi]\). The same coefficient gives the critical logarithmic singular-value residue and the exact Schatten threshold \(p>1/\gamma\).
