# Mathematical verification record

The direct proof identifies the Rhaly matrix with an embedding from discrete finite-energy prefix sums to a power-weighted sequence space. The spectral counting function of its reciprocal squared singular values is reduced, to within two boundary modes, to a long weighted string. Edge cutting loses only one eigenvalue per cut; path eigenvalues, weighted endpoint comparison, and a uniform high-frequency tail bound give the exact phase integral. Tonelli and the Euler beta integral compute the gamma-function constant.

The executable `check_rhaly_weyl.py` completed finite Sturm recurrence checks at exponents \(\gamma=1.5,2,3\), finite Hilbert--Schmidt summation checks, and the exact constant \(\mathsf C_2^2\approx1.393203929685678\). These calculations concern finite truncations. They are not a computer-assisted proof of convergence, independence, uniqueness in the literature, or a certification of publication readiness.
