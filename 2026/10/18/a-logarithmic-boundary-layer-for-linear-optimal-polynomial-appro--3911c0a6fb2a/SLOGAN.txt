For the degree-one optimal Hardy approximant to \(1/(1-z)\), the unique zero approaches \(-1\) from the exterior at the sharp logarithmic rate \(w_p=-1-\log(4p)/p+o(p^{-1})\) as \(p\to\infty\). The optimal residual has moment \(E_p^p\sim(2\pi p)^{-1/2}\), with both constants arising from an exact balance of the two ends of an arcsine integral. The coefficientwise limiting residual has twice the optimized leading moment, so the logarithmic correction remains quantitatively essential.
