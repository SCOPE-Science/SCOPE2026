The continuous-time Ehrenfest chain has an exact, strictly increasing counting-\(\ell^1\) semigroup norm given by a central-binomial integral. Its time-weighted derivative norm in counting-\(\ell^1\) exceeds its \(\ell^\infty\) counterpart by at least a logarithmic factor in the even-dimensional limit, although the process is a lumping of a symmetric Boolean-cube walk.
