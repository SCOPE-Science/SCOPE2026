For the degree-one Hardy \(H^p\) optimal polynomial inverse of \(1-z\), the unique zero lies at \(-1-t_p\) for every finite real \(p>1\) and approaches the unit circle with the sharp logarithmic law \((p-1)t_p=\log(4(p-1))+o(1)\). A two-endpoint Laplace balance establishes the exact approach rate, beyond previously published finite-exponent examples.
