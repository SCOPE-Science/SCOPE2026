For the critical path matrix with off-diagonal entries \(1/|i-j|\), the top eigenvalue has the exact second-order law \(\lambda_N=2\log N-\lambda_1^L((0,1))+o(1)\), and the rescaled positive eigenvector converges strongly to the Dirichlet logarithmic-Laplacian ground state. An exact positive cell-averaging remainder explains the limit.
