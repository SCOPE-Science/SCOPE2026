For a uniformly random partition of \(n\), the integral of its normalized partition polynomial decays like \(1/\log n\), but the rescaled integral has a nondegenerate limit: \(2\sum_{j\ge1}E_j/[j(j+1)]\) for independent unit exponentials. Its exact Laplace transform is an infinite product with a closed Gamma-function form, and the limit has mean \(2\) and variance \(4\pi^2/3-12\).
