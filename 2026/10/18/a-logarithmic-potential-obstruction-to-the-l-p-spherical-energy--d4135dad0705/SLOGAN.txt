For every sufficiently small positive energy exponent \(\beta\), the circular \(L_{3-\beta}\)-spherical copula fails to maximize the expected distance power \(\mathbb E\|X-X'\|_2^\beta\). A fixed smooth marginal-preserving density perturbation improves it, because its \(\beta\downarrow0\) logarithmic potential cannot be additively separable: its Laplacian is the nonseparable \(L_3\)-spherical density.
