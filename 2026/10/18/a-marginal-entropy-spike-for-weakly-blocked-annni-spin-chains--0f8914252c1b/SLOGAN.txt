For the periodic axial next-nearest-neighbor Ising chain, weak one-spin-blocked configurations have an exact, nonmonotone entropy: the marginal coupling \(\kappa=1\) has exponential growth \(1.666301937\ldots^n\), versus \(1.555187226\ldots^n\) for every \(\kappa>1\). A finite-state proof identifies the respective Perron rates as roots of explicit quintic and septic polynomials, explaining an extensive spike caused by neutral-energy spin motifs.
