It improves the general diameter-two upper estimate from \(16r+8\) to \(4r\) throughout a natural non-complete-multipartite infinite family and all its spanning supergraphs. The bound addresses the new invariant in a direction not handled by the existing cycle/path/complete-multipartite evaluations. Only the upper bound is asserted; equality for all orders is not implied by finite tests.

The construction requires no probabilistic assumptions, and exact lower bounds for the infinite family and any analogous clearing guarantee remain open here.
