Every \(P_3\)-convex set in the one-subdivision of a finite graph decomposes uniquely into selected original vertices, a matching of remaining edges, and independent boundary-edge choices, yielding an exact all-coefficient generating polynomial. For subdivided complete graphs, the polynomial has exactly \(\lfloor(n-1)^2/4\rfloor\) missing cardinalities; the largest proper convex-set size for any connected original graph is its subdivision order minus the original minimum degree.
