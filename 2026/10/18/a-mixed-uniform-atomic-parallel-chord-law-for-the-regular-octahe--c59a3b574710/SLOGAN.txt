For parallel chords of a regular octahedron along any threefold symmetry axis, uniform sampling of perpendicular offsets gives an exact law: two-thirds of chords have uniform length on \([0,2/\sqrt3]\), while one-third attain the maximal length \(2/\sqrt3\) exactly. The directional overlap volume is \(4/3-\sqrt3\,r+r^2/2\) until separation \(2/\sqrt3\), then zero.
