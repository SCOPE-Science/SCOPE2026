**Correctness — PASS.** The proof derives an exact trace-fiber count from a characteristic-two linear map and a normalized Artin--Schreier system. The critical field-reduction step is proved via the equation \(b^2+(h+1)b+h^3=0\) and an absolute-trace obstruction; the remaining four roots are counted explicitly in \(\mathbb F_{16}\). Boundary conditions \(s\ge2\), zero trace, and nonzero trace are individually handled. Exact complete enumeration for \(q=4,8,16\) separately corroborates these formulas but is not used as an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS with risk.** Fernando--Kousik arXiv:2606.01037v1, Question 6.1 explicitly asks for the permutation criterion and gives the polynomial formula; the relevant sections do not supply the joint trace-fiber count. Fernando--Hou--Lappano arXiv:1208.2942 provides general trace methods for other parameters, not the same specific field-size congruence and exact count. Searches of mathematical-result records and accessible historical claims did not identify the formula, its Artin--Schreier equivalent, or stronger coverage. The possibility that a general unpublished or implicit result implies the identity remains a residual risk; this is not a claim of global uniqueness.

**Value — PASS.** The formula provides a complete uniform answer to a natural intermediate invariant directly tied to an explicit 2026 open permutation-classification question, and rules out an infinite congruence class of parameters. It is a genuine structural arithmetic separation arising from \(\mathbb F_{16}\), not an arbitrary finite table or a routine numerical check. The claim does not assert full classification in other classes.

**Limitations.** Only the zero-output-trace fiber is classified; permutation status in the remaining classes and higher even extension degrees remains open. Independent mathematical validation remains necessary.
