For the \(e=4\) reversed-Dickson polynomial in Fernando--Kousik's permutation question, the zero-output-trace fiber has exactly \(q^3+4q(q-1)\) elements when \(q=2^s\) and \(s\equiv3\pmod4\), but exactly \(q^3\) elements in all other cases \(s\ge2\). Consequently the polynomial cannot permute \(\mathbb F_{q^4}\) for infinitely many parameters, with the obstruction explained by four explicit \(\mathbb F_{16}\) witnesses.
