In a symmetric two-site conditionally reset quantum walk, consecutive reset-location signs retain a memory invisible to the stationary one-point position law. At fixed distance–rate product \(z\), the exact persistence grows strictly with site separation \(a\) toward \((2/\pi)\int_0^zK_0(s)\,ds\), with a universal strictly negative \(a^{-2}\) correction and uniform \(O(a^{-4})\) remainder.
