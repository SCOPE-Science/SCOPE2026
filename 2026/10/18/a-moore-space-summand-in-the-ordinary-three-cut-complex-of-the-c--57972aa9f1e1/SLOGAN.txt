The ordinary three-cut complex of the Clebsch (folded five-cube) graph has integral homotopy type \(M(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z,11)\vee\bigvee^{15}S^{11}\). Its natural degree-eleven torsion shows that the torsion-free sphere-wedge conclusion for triangle-free graphs with edge-disjoint four-cycles does not extend to this symmetric graph with overlapping squares.
