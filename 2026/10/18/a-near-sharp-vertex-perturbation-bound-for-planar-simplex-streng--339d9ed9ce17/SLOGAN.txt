For the squared-area-over-semiperimeter-cubed strength of an arbitrary planar triangle, moving one vertex by \(\varepsilon\) changes ordinary or signed strength by at most \(3\varepsilon/32\), and an explicit triangle proves the coefficient is within \(0.4\%\) of optimal. Moving all three vertices by at most \(\varepsilon\) costs at most \(9\varepsilon/32\), a substantial sharpening of the earlier general Lipschitz bound.
