For a motion-invariant random affine section of the \(n\)-cube, the logarithmic number of vertices divided by \(n\) converges to the explicit nonconstant law \(\log2-I(\sqrt{2/\pi}U)\), where \(U\) is uniform on \([0,1]\) and \(I\) is a half-normal Rademacher rate function. Although the already-known exact expected vertex count is \(2^{n-1}\), its ratio to the actual count diverges in probability: typical sections have a strictly smaller random exponential growth rate.
