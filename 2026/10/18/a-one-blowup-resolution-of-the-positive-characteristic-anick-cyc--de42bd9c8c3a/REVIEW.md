# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The invariant hypersurface follows by direct substitution and normality, consistent with the earlier general Nagata-type theory. Smoothness is certified on all three affine charts of the blowup by unit Jacobian partial derivatives; the exceptional divisor is smooth but has inseparable nonreduced geometric fibres. Factoriality follows from the UFD localization at the prime \(z\), and the discrepancy is computed by ambient blowup adjunction. Finite-field symbolic identities were replayed. Remaining checks not implied by finite experiments are proved algebraically.

## Originality — PASS, with qualifications
Kuroda (arXiv:2202.00262), Section 6, *does* subsume the hypersurface presentation, which is explicitly excluded from the new contribution. Miasnikov–Shestakov (arXiv:2610.11013) identify the map but do not provide the characteristic-\(p\) cyclic quotient resolution. The new claim is the complete birational-geometric package: smooth one-step blowup, Frobenius-thick exceptional fibres, discrepancy \(2-p\), UFD quotient, and finite-map nonflatness. Targeted mathematical literature and published-finding corpus searches found no equivalent package. An unindexed consequence of the general invariant theory remains possible.

## Value — PASS
An explicit uniform resolution in every positive characteristic, together with nonreduced geometric exceptional fibres and variable discrepancy, addresses genuinely geometric behaviour of an intrinsically defined wild quotient. The factoriality and degree-\(p\) versus length-\(p^2\) nonflatness further distinguish this canonical fourfold from familiar smooth or tame quotients. These structural features require a nontrivial blowup analysis beyond applying the known invariant presentation.

## Limits
No claim of novel invariant-ring generators, unique or minimal resolution, or generality beyond the Anick cyclic action. Geometric novelty still carries unindexed-literature risk.
