For every nonlinear two-site ribosome-flow chain driven by a single weak sinusoidal rate modulation, the leading gain-of-entrainment coefficient changes sign **at most once** as frequency varies. A strictly increasing analytic hindrance law attains the bound: its gain crosses zero at \(\omega=4\sqrt{11}/3\) and has a unique positive optimum at \(\omega=(4/3)\sqrt{11+12\sqrt5}\).
