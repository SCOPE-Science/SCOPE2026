For the real Lipschitz dual ball of the complete split graph \(K_p\vee\overline{K_q}\), an exact one-dimensional disintegration yields its volume and all three covariance eigenvalues. As \(p\to\infty\), the class-contrast and clique-internal eigenvalues exchange order asymptotically at \(q/p=1/3\), while a second transition at \(q/p=1\) separates vanishing spectral splitting from a positive limiting gap.
