# Scientific review: stationary diffusivity–displacement time ordering

**Correctness — PASS.** The stationary law exists uniquely via first-reset coupling. At exponential backward age, Gaussian fourth moments and conditional Itô isometry give exact values for the simultaneous mixed moment and the volatility variance. Reset-free conditional expectations, followed by independent Poisson survival, give both ordered lag covariances for every dimension and positive lag. The resulting ratio and unique normalized peak have direct algebraic proofs. Standalone generator and age/quadrature calculations independently agreed in 321 finite numerical checks. These checks supplement, rather than substitute for, the analytic all-parameter proof.

**Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk.** The closest source, Santra–Basu–Sabhapandit (arXiv:2205.00549v1), is the model's original work: its Sections 4–6 explicitly derive and study stationary spatial marginals and their time evolution, not the evaluated opposite-order mixed square covariances and dimension-independent rate-recovery ratio. The earlier Sposini et al. stochastic-volatility analysis does not impose simultaneous complete resetting. Sokolov's resetting fluctuation–dissipation analysis assumes ordinary Brownian motion rather than the stochastic-diffusivity auxiliary coordinate. Different reset models with higher-order temporal asymmetries do not dominate this two-way positive ratio or its parameter cancellation. General renewal and joint-generator equations are acknowledged as existing methods; an as-yet-unlocated equivalent specialization is a residual risk, and independent priority examination has not occurred.

**Scientific value — PASS.** The source emphasizes one-time distributions in a natural model of heterogeneous diffusion. Here the **order** of squared-displacement and instantaneous-diffusivity measurements gives a sharp, explicit time-arrow observable, a universal normalization independent of the amplitude and dimension, and an exact inverse formula for the reset clock. A full ordinary coordinate covariance matrix cannot recover this temporal structure. The result is mathematically motivated by the causal diffusion-amplitude coupling rather than by an arbitrary parameter slice. It does not claim finite-sample performance, entropy production, or extensions to other reset protocols.

**Scope and risks.** The formulas require stationarity, simultaneous complete Poisson resets, independent Wiener noises and access to instantaneous diffusivity. Their all-time positivity rests on exact analytic moments, not on finite simulation. Broad generator methods may imply additional corollaries; the reviewed original content is the evaluated universal ratio and its identification interpretation, not the model or renewal identities.
