In a simultaneously reset Brownian model with stochastically diffusing diffusivity, the forward-to-reverse stationary covariance ratio between squared displacement and instantaneous diffusivity is exactly \(1+5rt/6\) at every positive lag, independent of spatial dimension and diffusion scale. This strictly directed temporal statistic identifies the Poisson reset rate even though all ordinary coordinate two-time covariances match those of independent Gaussian Ornstein–Uhlenbeck processes.
