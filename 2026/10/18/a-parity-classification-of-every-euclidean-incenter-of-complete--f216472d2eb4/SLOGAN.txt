For the canonical symmetric-edge polytope of every complete bipartite graph with both parts of size at least two, the full set of Euclidean incenters is explicitly determined by the part sizes and parity: its dimension is \(p-1\) or \(p\) for unequal parts and either zero or one for equal parts. A unique incenter exists exactly for equal odd part sizes; equal even part sizes give an explicit segment with additional tangent facets at its endpoints.
