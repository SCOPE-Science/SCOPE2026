For every \(m,n\ge2\) and \(2\le r<m+n\), the integral degree-\(r-1\) homology coinvariants of the higher independence complex of \(K_{m,n}\) under \(S_m\times S_n\) form an explicitly classified elementary two-group, even though the homology before taking coinvariants is torsion-free. In the stable range \(m,n\ge r+1\), the two-rank jumps sharply between \((r+1)/2\) for odd \(r\) and \((r-2)/2\) for even \(r\).
