For the one-subdivision of a clique, every induced matching is a partial map from its distinct original tails to complementary heads, and inclusion-maximality means this map is onto. The reconfiguration graph of minimum inclusion-maximal induced matchings is a disjoint union of hypercubes for every even clique order at least four, but is connected and regular for every odd order at least five; complete size enumerators follow from Stirling-number surjections.
