For the standard \(48\) Euclidean \(F_4\) roots, the first nontrivial Vietoris–Rips complex is the facet-stellar subdivision of the \(24\)-cell boundary, hence a \(3\)-sphere. At radius \(\sqrt2\), despite \(3408\) new simplices up to dimension six, the enlarged complex collapses in \(1704\) elementary pairs onto that sphere, preserving an integral degree-three class on which the Weyl group acts by the determinant character.
