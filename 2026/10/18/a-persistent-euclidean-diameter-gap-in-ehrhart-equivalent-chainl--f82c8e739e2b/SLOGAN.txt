For every integer \(m\ge3\), two complementary Ehrhart-equivalent chainlink polygons with consecutive chain lengths \((2m,2m+1,2m+2)\) have squared Euclidean diameters \(7m^2/2+5m+2\) and \(7m^2/2+11m/2+7/2\). Their diameter difference tends to the strictly positive constant \(1/(2\sqrt{14})\), even though their Ehrhart quasipolynomials coincide.
