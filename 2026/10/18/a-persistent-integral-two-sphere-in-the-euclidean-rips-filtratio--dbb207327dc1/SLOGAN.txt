For the thirty unit vertices of the regular icosidodecahedron, the inclusive Euclidean Rips complex is homotopy equivalent to \(S^2\) throughout \(1\le r<\sqrt3\), with every inclusion from radius one a simple-homotopy equivalence. Exact certificates give \(330\), \(1690\), and \(7802\) relative collapse pairs at the three intervening distance levels.
