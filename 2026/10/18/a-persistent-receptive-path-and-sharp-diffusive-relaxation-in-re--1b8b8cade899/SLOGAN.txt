In the fully antagonistic bounded-confidence opinion model, an open family of initial configurations keeps exactly the same receptive path at every finite update, although that path vanishes when receptivity is evaluated at the limiting state. Exact positive tridiagonal deficit dynamics yield a sharp slowest relaxation rate \(1-\rho_n=\pi^2/(3n^2)+O(n^{-3})\), with explicit three- and four-agent complete-confidence examples.
