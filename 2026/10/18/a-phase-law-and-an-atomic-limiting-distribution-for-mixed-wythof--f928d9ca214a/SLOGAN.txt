The number of representations of an integer as a lower Wythoff number plus an upper Wythoff number follows a three-piece linear function of its golden-ratio rotation phase, with a uniform \(O(\log n)\) error. The normalized counts converge empirically to density \(2\) on \((0,\varphi^{-2})\) together with an atom of mass \(\sqrt5-2\) at \(\varphi^{-2}\).
