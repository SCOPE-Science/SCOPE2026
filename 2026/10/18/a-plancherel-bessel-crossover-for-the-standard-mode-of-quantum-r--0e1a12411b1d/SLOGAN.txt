The standard-character mode of a random-transposition quantum stochastic walk has an exact finite Plancherel branching formula and, on coherent times \(s n^{3/2}\), converges to the Bessel-square law \(\bigl[J_1(4s)/(2s)\bigr]^2\). With dissipative rate \(g/\sqrt n\), the corresponding open-system profile is \(e^{-2gs}\bigl[J_1(4s)/(2s)\bigr]^2\); this is a mode-specific statement, not a full mixing theorem.
