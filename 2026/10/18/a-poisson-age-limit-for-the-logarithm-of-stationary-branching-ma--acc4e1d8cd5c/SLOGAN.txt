For the stationary immigration–branching process with Gamma\((\alpha,1)\) marginals, the logarithmic mass \(-\alpha\log Z_{t/\alpha}\) converges jointly at fixed times as \(\alpha\downarrow0\) to the age since the last point of a rate-one Poisson process. An explicit coupling inequality shows exponentially accurate tracking of the most recent immigration age on the logarithmic mass scale.
