This graph is claw-free; rational-weight clique blow-ups plus continuity rigorously transfer the published Chudnovsky–Seymour real-rootedness theorem to all nonnegative weights. The polynomial is normalized with nonnegative coefficients, so real-rootedness yields independent-Bernoulli factors. For any bordered motif, the earliest compatible overlap gives an exact two-occurrence probability and quadratic discriminant, supplying both a sharp threshold and a constructive failure of universal representability. The claw-free graph-polynomial theorem is expressly prior art. Closely related established two-letter motif classifications are subsumed, not represented as independently novel. Searches for unbordered words, cross-bifix-free code counts, Poisson–binomial representability, weighted real-rootedness and first-overlap discriminants located no dominating statement. Residual risk: unindexed literature may derive this consequence of the graph theorem, and only portions of some pattern papers were accessible.

These link a probabilistic dependence model, pattern combinatorics and real-rooted graph polynomials, rather than merely computing isolated coefficients.
