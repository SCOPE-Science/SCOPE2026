For the modal density axiom \(\Box\Box q\to\Box q\), a sparse random directed frame has a sharp Poisson transition at \(n p_n^2=\frac32\log n+\frac12\log\log n+t\), with limiting validity probability \(\exp(-\sqrt{3/2}\,e^{-t})\). Frame validity is reentrant: it occurs for vanishingly few arcs, disappears at intermediate density, and returns above the critical window.
