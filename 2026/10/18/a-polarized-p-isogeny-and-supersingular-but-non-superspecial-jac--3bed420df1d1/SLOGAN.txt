For every odd prime \(p\), a natural \(p\)-power-degree sum-of-pullbacks isogeny identifies the Jacobian of a prime-square Artin--Schreier cover with \(p+1\) copies of one superspecial hyperelliptic Jacobian, and its precise polarized degree is \(p^{(p^2-1)/2}\). The cover is supersingular but not superspecial in every admissible parameter case.
