A single chord of cyclic span \(d\) adds too few new sums to overcome multiplication-table sparsity unless \(d\) grows: every sum-completing chord has \(d\gg(\log n)^{\delta/2}(\log\log n)^{3/4}\), where \(\delta=1-(1+\log\log2)/\log2\). In fact, if \(d=o((\log n)^{\delta/2}(\log\log n)^{3/4})\), the chorded cycle represents a vanishing proportion of all sums, as proved via an exact two-gap identity.
