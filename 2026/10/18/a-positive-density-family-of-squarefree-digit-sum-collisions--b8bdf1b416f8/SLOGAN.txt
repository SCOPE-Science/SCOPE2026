For a set of bases of exact positive density \(\frac5{18}\prod_{p\ge5}(1-3/p^2)\), the distinct squarefree numbers \(b(2b-3)\) and \((b-2)(2b-3)\) have the same value under \(n\mapsto nS_b(n)\). This gives a quantitative infinite-family answer to the squarefree-collision question for base-dependent digit sums, without asserting noninjectivity for every base.
