For one-letter-head binary marker rules \(A*\omega\), the fraction of length-\(m\) tails yielding involutive full-shift automorphisms converges to a strictly positive constant \(\rho\in(0.2883289988874315,0.2883290137885928]\). Every even-length count is exactly \(N_{2J+2}=2^{2J+2}q_J-1\), where \(q_J\) is a finite, explicitly enumerable overlap-avoidance probability.
