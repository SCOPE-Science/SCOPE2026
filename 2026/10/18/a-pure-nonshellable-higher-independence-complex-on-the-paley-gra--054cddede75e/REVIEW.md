The standard simply connected three-complex attaching-map argument yields precisely one two-sphere and two three-spheres.

**Value — PASS.** This is the integral homotopy type of a pure complex naturally attached to a standard highly symmetric graph. Its nonzero second homology lies below its top dimension, certifying nonshellability and failure of Cohen–Macaulayness over every field. The claim does not generalize beyond this particular graph and parameter.
