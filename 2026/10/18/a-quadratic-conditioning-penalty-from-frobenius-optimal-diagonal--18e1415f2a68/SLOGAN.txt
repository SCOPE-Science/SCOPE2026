For a positive-definite star matrix, the globally Frobenius-optimal positive diagonal congruence always worsens spectral conditioning in dimension at least three, although leaving the matrix unscaled is condition-number optimal. In the nearly singular limit the optimized condition number grows as \(\frac{d-1}{16d}\kappa_2(A)^2\), while the squared Frobenius residual falls to half its unscaled limit.
