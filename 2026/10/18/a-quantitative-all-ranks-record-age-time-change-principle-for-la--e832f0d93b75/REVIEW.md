# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Every matched record interval differs by at most twice the maximal centered Bernoulli-clock discrepancy after activity-time rescaling; sorting is nonexpansive under matched sup-norm perturbations. Replacing a random effective horizon by \(\lfloor qn\rfloor\) has a separate deterministic two-times bound, and maximal Hoeffding concentration supplies the explicit probability and expectation estimates. The limiting Poisson–Dirichlet law uses Szabó–Vető Theorem 4 in precisely its censored-final-interval convention. Exhaustive bounded exact-rational tests were run; none are substituted for the proof.

**Originality — PASS on accessible evidence.** Majumdar–Mounaix–Schehr (arXiv:2103.14062, conclusion) studies record counts and correlations and asks for age statistics under holding; it does not prove a ranked-age convergence or a simultaneous rankwise finite-horizon error. Szabó–Vető (arXiv:1510.01152, Theorem 4) already proves the no-holding \(\operatorname{PD}(1/2,0)\) convergence, which is explicitly used rather than claimed as new. Régnier–Dolgushev–Bénichou's full 2024 paper (arXiv:2312.14885, sections II–IV and appendices) provides renewal/Laplace transforms of record counts and epochs but not an explicit pathwise insertion-of-pauses coupling across all ranked ages. The 2023 non-Markovian paper addresses a distinct stochastic-process class. Targeted literature and published-result searches located no stronger or equivalent bound. The possibility of an unindexed generic renewal time-change theorem with the same inequality remains a genuine prior-art risk.

**Value — PASS.** An open question about lazy-walk record ages is answered for the entire ranked censored-age sequence, with a quantitative all-ranks stability guarantee against a deterministic-horizon standard walk. This makes the qualitative universality robust and separates the finite-sample clock error from the previously known base-walk limit. No unsupported sharpness claim or quantitative convergence rate to the limiting law is made.
