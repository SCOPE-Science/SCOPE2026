Weakening one edge of an otherwise homogeneous cycle changes the sharp mean-zero Poisson \(\ell^\infty\) norm by \(\frac{n}{16}(w^{-1}-1)+O_w(1)\), and the worst output vertices move to quarter-cycle positions. Strengthening the edge has no positive order-\(n\) correction, giving a sharp one-sided stability transition at unit conductance.
