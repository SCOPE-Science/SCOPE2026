# Scientific assessment

## Correctness

In the four-dimensional product-band sector, the resonant dark eigenvalue is simple. The cleared rank-one secular equation gives its analytic continuation and the imaginary second-order coefficient. The remaining sector spectrum stays in a fixed lower half-plane for small detuning; a fixed resolvent contour bounds its time evolution even at repeated eigenvalues. Orthogonal permanent dark dynamics and the limiting normalized dark projection give the two contributions to the survival limit.

## Scope and comparison

The static sitewise weights and mean capture drop are already consequences of the rectangular-detector result with a singleton detector. They are used as inputs and credited, not asserted as novel. The distinct result is the explicit decay coefficient and scaled survival law for the weighted rook corner trap. The known lossy-star quadratic law gives a related mechanism for a different perturbation and geometry. The comparison does not establish exhaustive historical priority.

## Value and limitations

The coefficient sets a quantitative time scale for a metastable population whose weight can vanish for some starting sites. The limit goes beyond eventual capture by resolving how much probability persists at times proportional to the inverse square of detuning. The proof holds for a fixed finite graph and fixed positive detector and column hopping strengths, not a joint scaling of those parameters.
