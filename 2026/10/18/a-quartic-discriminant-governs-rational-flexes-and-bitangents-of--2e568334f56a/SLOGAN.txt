For the rationally smooth members of Asgarli–Ghioca's degree-\(q+3\) plane-filling pencil in odd characteristic, rational flex lines are exactly the rational points of an explicit dual-plane quartic \(a^3b+b^3c+ac^3+k(b^4+abc^2)=0\). This quartic also determines the number of rational bitangents and, when smooth, gives genus-three square-root bounds on both counts.
