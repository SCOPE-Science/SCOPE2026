For the degree-two weighted least-squares projector on a high-dimensional Euclidean ball, the sharp \(L^\infty\) norm divided by dimension tends to the larger of \(k^k e^{-k}/\Gamma(k+1)\) and \(\sqrt{2/(\pi e)}\), where \(k=\alpha+1\) is the radial weight parameter. A sharp transition at \(\alpha=-1/2\) moves worst-case amplification from the center to the boundary; at equality only these two regions remain asymptotically competitive.
