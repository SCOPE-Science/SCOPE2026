The natural inclusion \(\mathcal M_P(\partial\Delta^3)\hookrightarrow\mathcal M_P(\Delta^3)\) of pure acyclic discrete-Morse matching complexes is realized by exactly \(3428\) certified elementary simplicial collapses. This upgrades the known abstract equivalence of two \(81\)-sphere wedges to a concrete relative simple-homotopy equivalence without asserting a higher-dimensional or equivariant collapse.
