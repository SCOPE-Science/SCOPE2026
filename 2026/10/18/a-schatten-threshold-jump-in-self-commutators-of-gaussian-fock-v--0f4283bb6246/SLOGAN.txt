For Gaussian Fock Volterra operators with quadratic symbol \(az^2+bz\), the self-commutator is always trace class with trace \(8|a|^2\), but its sharp Schatten threshold jumps from \(1/2\) to \(2/3\) when both coefficients are nonzero. That same mixed-symbol regime has infinitely many positive and negative eigenvalues, unlike either pure monomial regime.
