For every odd \(n\ge5\), maximal independent \((n-2)\)-sets of the Möbius ladder form a cyclic interval-endpoint token-sliding graph with a closed Manhattan-on-a-cylinder distance formula. Every configuration has eccentricity exactly \(n\): the graph is connected, bipartite, Eulerian, and self-centered, yet nonregular for \(n\ge7\). The previously known configuration count is not claimed as new.
