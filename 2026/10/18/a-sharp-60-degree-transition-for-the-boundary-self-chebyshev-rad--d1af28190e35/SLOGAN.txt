The Euclidean boundary self-Chebyshev radius of every rhombus has an exact two-branch formula: its four minimizing edge centers collapse to the two obtuse vertices exactly at an acute angle of \(60^\circ\). Among rhombi of fixed area, precisely the \(60^\circ\) shape minimizes the squared radius at \(2A/\sqrt3\), while the square uniquely maximizes the radius at fixed perimeter.
