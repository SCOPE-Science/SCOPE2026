Three times an eight-triangle octahedral generator of \(\widetilde H_2(\mathcal M(K_{5,5});\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z/3\) requires simplicial \(\ell_1\)-filling norm exactly \(68\), even with real coefficients. An explicit integral 68-tetrahedron filling and an exact rational dual cochain give matching certificates; symmetry transfers the sharp bound to all 1800 induced octahedral spheres.
