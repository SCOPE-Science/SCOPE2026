The canonical constant-width cubic body whose every planar projection has threefold symmetry develops a sharp affine-surface-area cusp at its maximal convex amplitude. Its affine area has a finite first derivative but a divergent negative second derivative, with exact nonanalytic defect \(C\delta^{7/4}\), where \(C=\frac{256\pi}{21\sqrt6}(\frac{\sqrt6}{3})^{7/4}\).
