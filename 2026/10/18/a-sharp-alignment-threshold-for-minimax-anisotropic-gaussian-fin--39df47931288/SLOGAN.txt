For unbiased inverse-covariance-corrected Gaussian two-point gradients on an isotropic quadratic, the globally minimax probe covariance tilts toward a hinted subspace only when the entire certified alignment band lies on one side of its rank fraction \(r/d\). The same tilt is optimal at every positive finite horizon, but all fixed positive-definite covariances share exactly the same asymptotic mean-square exponent.
