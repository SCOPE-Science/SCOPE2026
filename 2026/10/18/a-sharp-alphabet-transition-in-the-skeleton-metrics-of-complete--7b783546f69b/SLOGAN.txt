The one-skeleton of a complete layered path polytope has an exact all-layer-size Laplacian spectrum and spanning-tree product, but its metric exhibits a sharp alphabet transition: binary layers produce a halved-cube graph of diameter \(\lceil k/2\rceil\), whereas all layers of any common cardinality at least three produce diameter two for \(k\ge3\). Exact distance distributions and Wiener indices are given in both regimes; the classical halved-cube invariants and general path-adjacency criterion are explicitly credited to prior work.
