A fully self-inclusive, two-token softmax dynamical system has two distinct attracting sign-flip cycles once \(b\tanh\alpha>a\) and \(\eta>2/(b\tanh\alpha-a)\); the second, antipodal cycle appears at an exact attention-temperature threshold even though the synchronized flip is already stable. The sharp Floquet multipliers are explicit, demonstrating local bistability without sparse attention or time-dependent parameters.
