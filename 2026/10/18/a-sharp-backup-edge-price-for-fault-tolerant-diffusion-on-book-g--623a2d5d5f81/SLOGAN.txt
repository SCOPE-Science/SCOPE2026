On the \(m\)-page book graph, a priced hub-to-hub backup edge is useless for maximizing intact diffusion speed but becomes compulsory at every worst-single-edge-failure optimum precisely when its price is below an explicitly determined threshold \(t_c(m)\). The sharp threshold satisfies \(t_c(2)=3+2\sqrt2\) and \(m t_c(m)\to2\), with a unique symmetric optimal conductance allocation determined by one cubic root and one scalar equation.
