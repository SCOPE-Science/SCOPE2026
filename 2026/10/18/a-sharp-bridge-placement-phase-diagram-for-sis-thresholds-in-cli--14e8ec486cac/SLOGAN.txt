In a two-community SIS contact network formed by a five-clique, one hub with \(k\) leaves, and a bridged two-clique, the bridge endpoint that minimizes the epidemic threshold has an exact phase diagram: the clique attachment always wins for \(k\le4\), the hub always wins for \(k\ge14\), and for \(5\le k\le13\) there is exactly one bridge-weight crossover. At \(k=7\) this crossover is \(w=2\sqrt{10}\), and exact parameter choices make the two placements swap between extinction and positive endemic persistence.
