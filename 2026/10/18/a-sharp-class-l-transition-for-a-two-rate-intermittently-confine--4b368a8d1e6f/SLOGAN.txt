For Brownian motion with an intermittently switched harmonic trap, the stationary position law is always infinitely divisible, but is self-decomposable exactly when \(\beta(4\mu+\alpha)\ge4\mu^2\), where \(\alpha\) is the trap-off rate and \(\beta\) is the trap-on rate. This is also the sharp condition under which an explicit Brownian-plus-compound-Poisson-driven ordinary Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process can match its stationary position marginal.
