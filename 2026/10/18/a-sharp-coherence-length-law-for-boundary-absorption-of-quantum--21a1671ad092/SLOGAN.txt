A quantum walker prepared on at most \(K\) consecutive sites has a sharp far-field boundary-absorption ceiling that approaches one with deficit \(\pi^2/[4(K+1)^2]+O(K^{-3})\), and no finite coherent preparation window attains perfect absorption. A two-site chiral packet has exact ceiling \(2/\pi\), while an explicit phase-chirped sine packet achieves the optimal large-\(K\) scaling; the finite-distance correction is uniformly at most \(K/[2\pi n(n-1)]\).
