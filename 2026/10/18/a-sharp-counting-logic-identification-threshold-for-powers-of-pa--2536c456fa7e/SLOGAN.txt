The \(k\)-th power \(P_n^{[k]}\) of an uncoloured finite path is identified among all graphs by two-variable counting logic exactly when \(k=1\) or \(n\leq k+4\). At the sharp boundary \(n=k+5\) for every \(k\geq2\), switching two edges between stable two-vertex colour classes gives a nonisomorphic but colour-refinement-equivalent graph.
