For four-point discrete renewal increments, the canonical cubic lower envelope \(Q_4\) is strictly improved everywhere \(p_1p_2p_4>0\) by the admissible polynomial \(Q_4+2p_1p_2p_4\), which belongs even to the restricted cumulative-coordinate class. This disproves both assertions of Nikolov–Savov Conjecture 3.7 at \(k=4\), and coefficient \(2\) is sharp within this one-term correction family.
