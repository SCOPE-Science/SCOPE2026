The sharp norm of the difference between consecutive radial weighted-Fock Toeplitz eigenvalue functionals is an explicit total-variation distance, attained by a radial threshold symbol. Its normalized leading constant is \(2\sqrt{g/(\pi(2g+g'))}\), so log-periodic curvature produces a complete nonconstant phase spectrum of optimal local square-root-metric slopes, even though every individual Toeplitz symbol is bounded.
