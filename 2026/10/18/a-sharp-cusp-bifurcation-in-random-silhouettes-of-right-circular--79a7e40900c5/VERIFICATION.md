# Verification record

The exact proof derives the silhouette from an ellipse and an external apex, reduces every stationary point to an extremum of an explicit scalar function, proves that extremum unique, then obtains the cubic and quadratic density exponents by Taylor inversion. These steps supply infinite-parameter arguments.

The embedded `check.py` is executable using only Python's standard library and separately approximates projected cone silhouettes by convex hulls of a 1,536-vertex base polygon and apex. It compares 64 projected areas, numerical/analytic derivatives, five means against surface-area/Cauchy normalization, and sample stationary-point sign changes. All tests passed when run from the package's source directory.

The checks are finite approximations, not proofs of all-parameter assertions. The 1989 source's publisher abstract was inspected, but not its full text. No independent external mathematical audit was performed.
