In the uniform stub-pairing model, loop counts and parallel-edge-pair counts have a completely explicit covariance for every degree sequence. For regular degree \(d=2\) it is positive of order \(v^{-2}\), whereas for every fixed \(d\geq3\) it is negative of order \(v^{-1}\): a sharp dependence transition hidden by asymptotically independent Poisson limits.
