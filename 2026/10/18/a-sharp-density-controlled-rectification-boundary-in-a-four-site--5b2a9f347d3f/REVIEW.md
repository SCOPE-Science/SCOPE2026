# Scientific review

**Claim assessed.** In the four-site departure-rate-disordered open exclusion process with rates \(1,1/4,1/2,1\), the rectification-even stationary current as a function of mean reservoir density has one sharp zero for every contrast \(0<d<d_c\); no zero in the interior at or above the unique algebraic contrast \(d_c\), with the one endpoint zero at the boundary contrast. All parameter-boundary signs are part of the same claim.

## Correctness — PASS
The entire claim follows from the exact sixteen-state Markov generator, a rational current with rigorously positive denominator, and the degree-nine transformed sign polynomial. The highest four coefficient signs hold uniformly, and the other six cross zero exactly once at rigorously ordered contrast thresholds, verified through Descartes variation of finite rational polynomials. Hence there is exactly one real density crossing below the last threshold, and none above. The limiting maximal contrast is evaluated separately as an exact positive fraction. The standalone code reconstructs all needed algebra and uses a second rational stationary solver; rational spot checks are checks, not a substitute for the global polynomial argument.

## Originality — PASS with residual risk
The primary September 2026 paper analyzes this open boundary mechanism, not the exact all-parameter four-site density sign classification; the related December 2025 paper concerns periodic field driving. A prior exact theorem for the same rates and mean density \(\rho=1/2\) varies contrast on only that section; the current theorem varies mean density at every contrast and determines a new algebraic disappearance threshold. Eight targeted research-record searches over alternate formulations, precise sample parameters, stronger implications and exact tabulations yielded no covering result. Search ranking is imperfect, and unindexed or implicit prior solutions remain a risk.

## Value — PASS
Direction and magnitude of rectification under a fixed reservoir drive are naturally controlled by the average filling. The theorem resolves the full sign-phase diagram, proving not only a sign change but uniqueness of the density crossing and a sharp contrast at which the phenomenon ceases. The exact threshold is not inferred from a parameter scan and requires a substantive polynomial root-sign certificate. Its value is specific to this finite specimen rather than a universal many-site law.

## Limitations and closest literature
Sakai–Akimoto, *Sample-specific rectification-like response in a boundary-driven exclusion process*, arXiv:2609.03560v1, is the defining open-boundary context; their *Symmetry Breaking of Current Response in Disordered Exclusion Processes*, arXiv:2512.03316v1, studies periodic external-field driving. An earlier exact four-site fixed-mean-density note is a related restricted-section result, not the same two-dimensional theorem. Other site-disorder patterns, boundary coupling conventions, chain sizes and dynamic observables are unaddressed. Independent verification has not been carried out.
