For uniform plane trees, root degree and the population at depth \(h\) have an explicit exact covariance whose sign is precisely that of \(n+1-h-2h^2\). On the square-root depth scale \(h/\sqrt n\to t>0\), the covariance converges to \(4(1-2t^2)e^{-t^2}\), revealing a unique shallow-to-deep sign reversal.
