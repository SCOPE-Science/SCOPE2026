For one-dimensional Brownian motion with rate-one Poisson contractions \(x\mapsto cx\), the stationary probability of a displacement of order \((1-c^2)^{-1}\) has an explicit **relative-error-one** asymptotic: a dilogarithmic exponential cost times a sharp \(\sqrt{1-c^2}\) prefactor. An order-one Euler–Maclaurin term contributes a nontrivial shape factor that the known logarithmic large-deviation rate alone cannot recover.
