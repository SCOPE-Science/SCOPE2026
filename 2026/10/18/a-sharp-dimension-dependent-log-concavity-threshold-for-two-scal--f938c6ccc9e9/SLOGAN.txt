A centered two-scale isotropic Gaussian mixture in \(\mathbb R^d\) is log-concave exactly when its narrow-component weight satisfies \(w\le R(q)/(R(q)+(\tau/\sigma)^d)\), with an explicit sharp constant \(R(q)\). In dimension four, \(0.9\,\mathcal N(0,4I_4)+0.1\,\mathcal N(0,I_4)\) fails log-concavity even though every three-dimensional linear projection is log-concave.
