For isotropic run-and-tumble motion with speeds refreshed at each Poisson tumble, a universal strictly decreasing fourth-cumulant threshold gives exactly three fluctuation regimes: always negative, a unique negative-to-positive crossover, or always positive. At fixed swimming speed, the crossover occurs precisely in dimension \(d\ge5\), and no such crossover exists in dimensions \(1\) through \(4\).
