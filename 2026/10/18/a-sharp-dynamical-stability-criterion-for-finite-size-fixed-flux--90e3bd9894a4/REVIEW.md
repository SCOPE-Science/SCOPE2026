**Mathematical value — PASS.** The source model's equilibrium folds do not alone reveal dynamical attractivity. This result supplies a precise bridge between stationary response slope and full size-structured linear dynamics, and gives an exact nonempty influx interval containing two stable and one unstable stationary profiles in a biologically interpretable mortality-per-size subclass. In contrast with generic delayed population feedback, this subclass cannot undergo a linear Hopf crossing. The distinction is potentially useful for interpreting hysteresis and deciding when steady-state branch continuation predicts local dynamical stability. Global nonlinear basins and general size-dependent vital rates remain open.

Only linear local stability is claimed for the present fixed-flux subclass, and this review is not an independent audit.
