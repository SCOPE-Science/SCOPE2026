In the equal-community pair-and-triangle contagion model, effective assortativities \(u=be\) and \(v=cf\) obey a sharp universal barrier: asymmetric interior equilibria are impossible when \(u\ge1\) or \(u<1\) with \(v\le V(u)\), where \(V(u)=t^3\) and \(t^2(3-2t)=u\) for \(1<t\le3/2\). For every \(u<1\) and \(v>V(u)\), some admissible choice of the remaining rates has an asymmetric equilibrium; this is not a guarantee of stability or of asymmetry for every such rate choice.
