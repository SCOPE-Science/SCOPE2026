For normalized right eigenstates of an open Hatano–Nelson chain, critical-window hopping bias \(\theta_N=e^{u/(N+1)}\) has a sharp sensing-mode switch at \(|u|=2\pi\): below it all maximizing eigenmode indices recede from the spectral edges, while above it precisely the first and last modes maximize for all sufficiently large sizes. The exact optimal quantum Fisher coefficient is \(\mathcal V(u)+2(u^2-4\pi^2)_+/(u^2+4\pi^2)^2\), where \(\mathcal V(u)=u^{-2}-(4\sinh^2(u/2))^{-1}\) is continued to \(\mathcal V(0)=1/12\).
