Among connected unicyclic graphs, the cycle uniquely maximizes the number of \(P_3\)-convex vertex subsets through order ten, but loses this property starting at the sharp threshold eleven. For every \(n\ge11\), attaching \(n-4\) leaves to one vertex of \(C_4\) gives \(2^{n-2}+2n-2\) convex subsets, strictly more than \(C_n\).
