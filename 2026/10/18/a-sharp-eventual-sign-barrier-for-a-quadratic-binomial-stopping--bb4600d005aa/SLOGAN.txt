For the quadratic zero-truncated-binomial conjugate of geometric stopping, the parameter interval \(0<\theta\le2/5\) yields strictly positive probability-generating coefficients and is closed under composition, enlarging the previously exhibited interval \(0<\theta\le1/4\). An explicit cubic determines the sharp larger threshold \(\theta_*=0.515310847\ldots\): coefficients are eventually positive at every fixed \(\theta\le\theta_*\), whereas every \(\theta_*<\theta<1\) has infinitely many coefficients of both signs and cannot define a stopping law.
