Distinct Ewens cycle-length classes have strictly positive factorial-moment dependence below \(\theta=1\), strictly negative dependence above it, and exact factorization at it, for every feasible finite collection of marked cycles. Among all positive parameters, only \(\theta=2\) produces a nonzero covariance shared by every admissible pair of distinct lengths, namely \(-4/(n+1)^2\); for fixed lengths the universal leading covariance is \(\theta^2(1-\theta)/n^2\).
