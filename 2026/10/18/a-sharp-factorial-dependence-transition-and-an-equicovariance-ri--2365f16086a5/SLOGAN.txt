For each n ≥ 4, Ewens cycle counts at distinct lengths r and s with r+s ≤ n have the same covariance for every such pair exactly at θ=1 and θ=2. The common value is zero at θ=1 and −4/(n+1)² at θ=2. The classical marked-cycle formula also gives the feasible cross-length factorial-moment sign transition at θ=1.
