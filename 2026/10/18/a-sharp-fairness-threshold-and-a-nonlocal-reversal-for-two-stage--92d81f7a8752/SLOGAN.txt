With Erlang-2 recurrent boosters and equal group sizes, the exact disassortativity threshold \(\alpha_c(c)=2-1/(1+2c+2c^2)\) determines whether equal dosing is a strict local minimum or maximum of the epidemic invasion spectral factor. At \(\alpha=3/2\), \(c=3/8\), fair dosing is locally optimal yet a completely concentrated allocation performs strictly better; switching to exponential booster intervals restores global fairness optimality whenever \(0<\alpha<2\).
