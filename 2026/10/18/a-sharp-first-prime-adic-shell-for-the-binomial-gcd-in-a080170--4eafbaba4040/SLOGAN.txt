For the binomial gcd \(D(k)=\gcd_{2\le q\le k+1}\binom{qk}{k}\), if \(p\) is prime, \(a\ge2\), and \(p^{a-1}<m<p^a\) with \(p\nmid m\), then \(D(p^a m-1)=p\) exactly. An explicit multiplier \(q=1+p^a(p^{a-1}-1)\) has exactly one base-\(p\) carry, sharpening the previously known prime-divisibility criterion.
