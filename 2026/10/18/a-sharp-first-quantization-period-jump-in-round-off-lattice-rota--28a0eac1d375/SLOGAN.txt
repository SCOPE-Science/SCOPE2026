For round-off lattice rotations \(F_\lambda(x,y)=(\lfloor\lambda x\rfloor-y,x)\), every central diamond shell of height \(r\) forms one cycle of exact period \(4r+1\) until the sharp threshold \(\lambda=1/r\). At \(\lambda=1/q\), the first activated shell merges with \(4q-3\) exterior states into one explicit cycle of exact period \(8q-2\), for every integer \(q\ge2\).
