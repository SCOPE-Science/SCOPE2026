For three independent categorical draws, the number of distinct labels is always one plus two independent Bernoulli variables whenever the alphabet has at most five labels. This is sharp: a six-label law with probabilities \(5/6,1/30,1/30,1/30,1/30,1/30\) has a quadratic richness generating polynomial with discriminant \(-1439/810000\), so no such factorization exists.
