For the central slice of a rectangular prism, the Gaussian-exposed vertex's upper-saturated-coordinate rank obeys an exact subset-sum angular law. A full six-class classification in five coordinates reveals the first possible failure of convex-order monotonicity under balancing capacities: the majorized vector \((6,4,4,2,2)\) has twice the pivot-rank variance of \((6,6,2,2,2)\).
