Every convex combination of at most five independent \(\operatorname{Bernoulli}(p)\) variables reaches its mean with probability at least \(p\) for \(0\le p\le1/3\). If every weight is below \(p\), its excess probability has the sharp, unattained uniform lower bound \(3/256\); thus any counterexample to the unrestricted conjecture requires at least six positive weights.
