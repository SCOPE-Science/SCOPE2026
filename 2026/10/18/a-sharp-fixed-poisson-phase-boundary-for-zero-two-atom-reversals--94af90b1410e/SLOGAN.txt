For a Poisson count of fixed rate \(\lambda>0\) plus independent Bernoulli counts of total mean \(0<\alpha<1\), concentrating the Bernoulli mean in one coin maximizes the probability ratio \(\Pr(S=2)/\Pr(S=0)\) exactly when \(\lambda\ge(1-\alpha)/(2-\alpha)\); at equality, all two-coin splits also tie. This phase boundary yields the sharp, unattained strict-reversal mean \(\lambda+(1-\lambda^2/2)/(1+\lambda-\lambda^2/2)\) for \(0<\lambda<\sqrt2\).
