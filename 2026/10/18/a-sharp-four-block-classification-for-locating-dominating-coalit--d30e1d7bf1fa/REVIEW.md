# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Reconstructed strict LD-coalition definition on connected complete multipartite K_(n_i), r>=2,N>=3 and proof of C_L=4 iff N>=4, all n_i<=2 and at most two singleton parts, else C_L=3. False twins in non-singleton parts and true-twin universal singleton parts permit at most one omitted representative in each LD-set. A 4+ block coalition graph cannot be a star: a hypothetical center with 3 partners is itself LD except an excluded star-graph edge case, which also has at most two repairing leaves. Thus two vertex-disjoint LD coalition edges force all parts of size <=2 and universal class <=2. These disjoint edges leave no possible fifth block with a partner; explicit four-block constructions cover s=0,1,2 and triple construction using two twins covers all other N>=3 cases. Independent direct adjacency, signature and partition enumeration checked every multipartite profile of orders 3..8 (57 profiles) without violating the theorem; larger orders governed by proof.

## originality

PASS

Best-of-knowledge new exact infinite-family LD coalition number. The initiating full paper by Chellali et al defines invariant and gives generic n-gamma_L+2 bound, path/cycle values and isolated order-4 examples; no full complete multipartite characterization. Related Resultary self/solid locating-domination of the SAME graph family analyzes a DISTINCT choice of invariant, so cannot imply this coalition partition threshold. Exact n-independent upper bound 4 is much stronger than generic order-dependent bounds.

## value

PASS

Locating-dominating coalitions are a recent natural partition invariant. Complete multipartite graphs include arbitrary independent twin classes and universal true twins, providing a fundamental test family; the exact 3/4 dichotomy and zero size dependence is a meaningful structural classification rather than a small-instance numerical gain.
