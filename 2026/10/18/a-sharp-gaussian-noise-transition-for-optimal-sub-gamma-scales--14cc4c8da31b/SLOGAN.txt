For a centered fixed-jump Poisson law smoothed by independent Gaussian variance \(s\), the sharp variance-exact sub-gamma optimizer leaves the local third-cumulant regime precisely at \(s=\lambda b^2/3\) and is uniquely interior thereafter. Its inverse scale is \((\log r+3\log\log r+1-\log2+o(1))/b\) for \(r=s/(\lambda b^2)\to\infty\); more generally, for every spectrally positive infinitely divisible law with largest positive jump \(b\), the optimally smoothed scale decays like \(b/\log(s/V)\).
