For independent Bernoulli incidence in a formal context, the stability of the top concept has an exact finite binomial expectation and a sharp almost-sure Gaussian transition when the number of attributes grows like \(\exp(({-\log p})m/2+b\sqrt m)\). Its limiting value is \(\Phi(-2b/(-\log p))\), with exact large-deviation rates on the low-stability side of the threshold.
