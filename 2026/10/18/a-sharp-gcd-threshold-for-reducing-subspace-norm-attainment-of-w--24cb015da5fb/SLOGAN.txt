For asymptotically unit-modulus unilateral weighted shifts, the implication that norm attainment on every reducing subspace for \(W^m\) forces the same for \(W^d\) holds for all shifts exactly when \(m\geq2d-\gcd(m,d)\). Every failure has an arbitrarily small compact perturbation of the unweighted shift as a witness, and all powers may enjoy this attainment even though none has a modulus with the same property.
