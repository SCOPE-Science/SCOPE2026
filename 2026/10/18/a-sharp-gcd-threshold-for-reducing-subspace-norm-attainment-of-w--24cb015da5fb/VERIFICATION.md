# Verification

The exact norm-one eigenvector criterion is verified by the weighted-shift product formula; the universal threshold is proved by a residue-class pigeonhole lemma and a complementary CRT construction. Each modulus assertion is checked by a direct diagonal reducing-subspace argument. The positive weights and convergence conditions were explicitly checked in the sharpness construction.
