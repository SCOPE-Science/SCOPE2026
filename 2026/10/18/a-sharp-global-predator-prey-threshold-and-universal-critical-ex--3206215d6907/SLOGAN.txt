For a fear–refuge predator–prey model, the sign of predator invasion growth at the prey-only equilibrium determines the **global** outcome of every positive trajectory: extinction or a unique coexistence state, with no periodic orbits even when predator self-competition vanishes. Exactly at invasion equality, predator density has the universal sharp decay \(v(t)\sim 1/(qt)\), with an explicit positive coefficient \(q\) determined by prey feedback and predator self-competition.
