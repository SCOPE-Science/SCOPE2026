If a row-split least-squares factor is inaccurate by at most \(\varepsilon\) in operator norm, applying its graph-Gram preconditioner to the **true gradient** has the sharp universal optimal relaxation \(2/(2+\varepsilon^2)\) and contraction \(\varepsilon\sqrt{4+\varepsilon^2}/(2+\varepsilon^2)\). Feeding the same inaccurate factor into the **residual** instead can turn a true least-squares minimizer into a nonstationary point and create strict ascent under arbitrarily small factor errors.
