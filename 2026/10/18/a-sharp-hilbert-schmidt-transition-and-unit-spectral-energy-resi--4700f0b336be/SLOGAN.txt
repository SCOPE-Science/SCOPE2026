The Hilbert matrix from the logarithmically weighted Bergman space \(A^2_{\log^\alpha}\) into \(A^2\) is Hilbert–Schmidt exactly for \(\alpha>2\), so its previously known compactness regime is entirely Hilbert–Schmidt. The exact quadratic spectral energy has the sharp residue \(\lim_{\alpha\downarrow2}(\alpha-2)\|\mathcal H\|_{\mathcal S_2}^2=1\).\n
