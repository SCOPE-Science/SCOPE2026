# Self-review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The entire shared-hub cyclic subspace is obtained from exact graph shell counts, with a new first Jacobi coupling \(\sqrt{2n}\). Every eigenvalue of that irreducible Jacobi matrix has positive hub weight. The positive squared spectral matrix differs from its single-cube counterpart by \(n|e_1\rangle\langle e_1|\). A Walsh-character calculation yields a strict nonintegral interval for the smallest squared frequency at odd dimension, and an interval requiring the forbidden odd integer \(5\) at even dimension. The integral-root consequence of exact periodicity is proved using the full positive-frequency trace. Low-dimensional spectral supports determine minimal times, including the zero mode at dimension two. Supporting exact symbolic and independently constructed full-matrix checks passed; neither supplies the all-dimension proof on its own.

## Scientific value

**PASS.** The joined-cube junction is a physically motivated model of scarred quantum dynamics. A complete, sharp obstruction to exact hub revival distinguishes a bottleneck-started walk from the perfect-revival parent cube and from approximate remote-corner revivals. The precise low-dimensional exceptions and all-higher-dimension proof are structural rather than arbitrary finite enumeration.

## Limitations and closest literature

The theorem uses an unweighted adjacency walk and the shared vertex as initial state. It does not preclude nearly perfect returns, and says nothing about a general remote-corner return theorem or perturbations. Closest primary source: arXiv:2112.06885, Sections II and Appendix D. General but inequivalent graph operation: arXiv:2312.06906.
