In a heterogeneous-rate voter model on a star, the identity of the slowest single-dissenter starting location switches at the exact harmonic threshold \(c_n=n(H_n-1)/H_{n-1}\), while the identity of the more influential dissenter switches only at \(c=n\). Between these thresholds, the center is more likely to win yet reaches some consensus faster on average than a dissenting leaf.
