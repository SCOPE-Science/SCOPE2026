For every rectangular rook graph with at least three rows and columns, the open-neighborhood complex has first integral homology \(\mathbb Z^2\) at \(3\times3\), \(\mathbb Z\) when exactly one side has length three, and \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\) once both sides have length at least four. Exact determinant certificates and a connected-link induction prove that the two-torsion persists at arbitrary board sizes.
