For commuting ordered tuples of rank-at-most-one square-zero matrices in dimension \(n\ge4\), the reduced commuting variety is irreducible exactly when \(n\ge2r\); beyond that threshold its components are explicit Grassmannian-incidence loci with computable, sometimes unequal dimensions. A Gaussian-binomial Möbius formula counts all such tuples over every finite field and for every tuple length.
