At equal mean reset intervals, Poisson resetting of a harmonically confined diffusive particle produces a strictly larger fourth cumulant after exact survival normalization than perfectly periodic resetting, except at a variance-matching equality. For every centered finite-fourth-moment reset law, the sign of the stationary fourth cumulant has a complete single-threshold or no-threshold classification; a binary reset gives opposite kurtosis signs under the two clocks at the same mean period.
