For SIR epidemics whose susceptible incidence is proportional to \(S^p\), the largest possible fraction infected after the prevalence peak decreases strictly with \(p\). In the vanishing-seed model its sharp high-exponent law is \(pM(p)=W((p-1)/e)+o(1)=\log p-\log\log p-1+o(1)\), rather than a fixed proportion.
