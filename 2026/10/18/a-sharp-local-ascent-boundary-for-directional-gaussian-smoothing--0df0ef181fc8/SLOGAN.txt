For exact coordinate-direction Gaussian smoothing of a smooth strongly convex ridge objective, the sharp local descent boundary is \(\delta_*=(\|u\|_2\|a\|_2-u^{\mathsf T}a)/2\), with \(a_i=u_i\mathbb E[g''(\sigma u_i Z)]\). An explicit analytic two-dimensional objective falls below this boundary: for every fixed positive stepsize, the exact nonlocal search direction increases the true objective at some points arbitrarily close to the unique minimizer.
