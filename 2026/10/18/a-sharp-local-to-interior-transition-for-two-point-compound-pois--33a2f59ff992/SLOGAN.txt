For centered compound-Poisson laws with jumps \(1\) and \(5\), the variance-exact sub-gamma scale has a unique positive tangency throughout an explicit mixture-probability interval, then switches sharply to a zero-argument optimum at \(q_c=0.056362063796\ldots\). The optimizer vanishes linearly and the improvement over the local third-cumulant bound vanishes quadratically at that transition.
