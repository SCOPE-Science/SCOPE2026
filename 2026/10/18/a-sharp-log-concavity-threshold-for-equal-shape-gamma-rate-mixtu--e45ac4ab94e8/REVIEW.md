# Review of the sharp Gamma rate-mixture curvature boundary

**Correctness — PASS.** Factoring the slow-rate Gamma density gives the exact log-curvature identity in RESULT.md. The universal sign reduces to maximizing \(t/[2\cosh((t-c)/2)]\); strict negativity of its second log derivative ensures a unique maximizer, while a positive shift in \(c\) strictly increases its maximum. Tangency yields the sharp expression and unique equality location. The proof explicitly covers positive real shapes and all boundary cases. `verify.py` reproduces 196 finite consistency checks, none of which is used as an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** Yu's inspected gamma-mixture Example 5 concerns relative log-concavity and four stochastic comparisons, not an ordinary mixture-density log-curvature classification. Levy's inspected density formulas concern sums rather than alternatives chosen by mixing. Mu's primary article treats Beta-family mixtures. The distinct common-rate Gamma shape-mixture family has a different analytic form and does not imply this rate-mixture boundary. The 2022 hyper-Erlang conference paper and one broader 2026 preprint were not available in full; an unindexed equivalent earlier theorem is possible and independent priority confirmation is still needed.

**Value — PASS.** Equal-shape Gamma rate mixtures are the smallest nontrivial scale-mixture and fixed-stage hyper-Erlang families. Their exact global log-concavity boundary is not a routine consequence of component log-concavity: the sharp normalized ratio is independent of rate separation, the failure region consists of precisely one interval, and arbitrarily slight rate heterogeneity can create far-tail failure even under total-variation convergence to a log-concave Gamma. The result is a natural complete classification, not an arbitrary parameter slice. It does not extend to unequal shapes or general mixing laws.
