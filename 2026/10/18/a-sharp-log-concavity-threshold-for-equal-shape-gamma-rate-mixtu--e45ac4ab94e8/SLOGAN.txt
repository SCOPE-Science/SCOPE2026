For a mixture of two Gamma laws of common shape \(\alpha>1\) and unequal rates, global log-concavity holds exactly when the normalized fast-component amplitude ratio is at most \(e^{2\sqrt\alpha}(\sqrt\alpha-1)/(\sqrt\alpha+1)\). Above this sharp shape-only threshold, one interval of positive log-curvature appears; even arbitrarily small rate heterogeneity can therefore destroy log-concavity in the far tail.
