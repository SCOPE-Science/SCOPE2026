Removing any fixed consecutive block of \(k\ge1\) interior samples from an equally spaced grid changes Berrut interpolation's sharp logarithmic Lebesgue coefficient to \(2/[\psi((k+3)/4)-\psi((k+1)/4)]\). Worst-case amplification concentrates at the midpoint of the missing block; even one deletion produces the coefficient \(1/\log 2\).
