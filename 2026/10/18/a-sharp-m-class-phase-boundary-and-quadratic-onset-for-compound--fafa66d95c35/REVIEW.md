# Scientific review: Beta-jump sub-gamma phase boundary

## Correctness

**PASS.** The result uses the true variance of a centered compound Poisson sum, derives the exact second-order remainder distribution, and proves the sharp coefficient ratio criterion for all real positive Beta shapes and all nonnegative transform arguments. The failure region follows from the positive linear term of the scale function, and an interior maximizer exists because the transform is entire and its scale ratio tends to zero at infinity. At criticality, the exact negative quadratic Taylor coefficient, uniform separation away from the origin, and local strict concavity prove the stated uniqueness and onset. The standalone exact-rational checker passed 2,746 finite algebraic and numerical comparisons. Such checks do not replace the analytic proof.

## Originality

**PASS on accessible evidence; independent priority audit remains outstanding.** The nearest published framework is Chen (2026), which proves the general canonical transform and optimal-scale variational formula and studies Gamma and two-point jumps. Its theorems imply the variational reduction but not the explicit Beta-jump necessary-and-sufficient shape boundary or the quadratic onset without the new coefficient argument. Chen and Wang (2026) treat signed jumps, bilateral Gamma, and Skellam rather than the two-shape Beta positive-jump family. Klar and Müller (2003) supply the M-class background and MGF-order comparisons, not this specialization. A broader stochastic-order classification not located during searching is the residual priority risk; a failed search alone is not evidence of global uniqueness.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** Two-parameter Beta jump laws form a natural family of bounded jump amplitudes whose shape varies from singular at zero to smooth and endpoint-concentrated. The result decides exactly when the sharp Bernstein pole is controlled by skewness and when the global MGF demands a larger scale. The sharp codimension-one boundary and positive quadratic onset add a full phase classification to a previously general variational method; the explicit uniform-jump benchmark \(L/4\) also improves the standard range-based scale \(L/3\). The interior optimum remains variational away from the critical regime.
