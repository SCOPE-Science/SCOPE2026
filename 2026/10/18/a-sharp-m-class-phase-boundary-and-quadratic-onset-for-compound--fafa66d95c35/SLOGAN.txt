For centered compound-Poisson sums with \(\operatorname{Beta}(\alpha,\beta)\) jump amplitudes, the optimal variance-matched right sub-gamma scale equals the local third-cumulant bound exactly when \(\alpha\ge1\), or \(0<\alpha<1\) and \(\beta\le(\alpha+2)(\alpha+3)/(1-\alpha)\). Crossing this sharp boundary creates an interior optimizer, with an explicit quadratic increase in the optimal scale above the local bound.
