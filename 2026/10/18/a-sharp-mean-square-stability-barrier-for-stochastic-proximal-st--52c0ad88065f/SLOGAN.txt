For a strongly convex average of two convex scalar clients, exact SPAM with half refresh has a sharp mean-square stepsize threshold given by the unique positive root of \(3t^5+19t^4+5t^3-54t^2-54t-12=0\), approximately \(1.7971422533\). At stepsize \(t=2\), the signed mean converges geometrically to zero while the expected squared primal iterate grows exponentially from a standard initialization.
