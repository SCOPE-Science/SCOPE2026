For the uniform-stage randomized two-stage Runge–Kutta method with one step per delay on \(y'(t)=-s\,y(t-1)\), the exact mean-square stability limit is the unique root \(s_*=1.144476047354\ldots\) of a degree-seven polynomial. At \(s=7/6\), every mean iterate converges but every nonzero initial state's expected squared norm grows exponentially; greater independent stage variance can only worsen the moment growth factor.
