A two-site positivity-preserving stochastic-heat Lie–Trotter discretization has a sharp mean-square stability frontier \(\sigma^2<\Gamma_{a,b}(h)\), with exact covariance spectral radius. Although the underlying two-site stochastic differential equation is mean-square stable below \(2(a-b^2/a)\), large time steps in the same positive discretization require the strictly stronger condition \(\sigma^2<2(a-b)\) asymptotically.
