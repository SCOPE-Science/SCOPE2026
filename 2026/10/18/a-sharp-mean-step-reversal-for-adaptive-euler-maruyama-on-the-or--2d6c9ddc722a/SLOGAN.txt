For the state-dependent Euler--Maruyama Ornstein--Uhlenbeck sampler, the stationary variance relative to a constant-step scheme at the **same mean step length** has an exact two-defect formula. Radially decreasing step policies are strictly worse whenever \(\lambda\mathbb E h\le1\), yet a smooth perturbation reverses that comparison at every baseline step above \(1/\lambda\), making the threshold sharp.
