Multiplicity-averaged broken-element mass inverses can lose mesh-grading robustness even when diagonal mass preconditioning remains uniformly conditioned: one vanishing cell creates precisely two divergent spectral modes on every fixed periodic \(P_1\) mesh with at least three cells. On the minimal three-cell ring, BRAS beats Jacobi exactly above the sharp short-cell threshold \(\varepsilon_*=0.2711405747281964\ldots\), and loses below it by an unbounded condition-number factor as \(\varepsilon\to0\).
