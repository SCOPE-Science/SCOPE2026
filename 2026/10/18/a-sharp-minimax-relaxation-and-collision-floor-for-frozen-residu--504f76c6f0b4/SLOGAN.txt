For \(k\) simultaneous frozen-residual Kaczmarz corrections sampled by squared residual on an orthogonal \(d\)-row system, the unique sharp minimax relaxation and the exact safety window \(0<\beta<2/k\) admit closed forms. Unlike fixed-uniform sampling, its best worst-case one-batch squared-error factor remains bounded away from zero as \(k\to\infty\), approaching \(((d-1)/(d+1))^2\) because of snapshot collisions.
