Every diagonal hyperplane section \(K_{n,t}=[0,1]^n\cap\{\sum x_i=t\}\) has minimum Euclidean width \(\min\{\sqrt{n/(n-1)},\min(t,n-t)\sqrt{n/\lfloor n^2/4floor}\}\). Its width has a sharp central plateau, and every least-width direction changes from a balanced bipartition to a single-coordinate split at the explicit thresholds \(t=\sqrt{\lfloor n^2/4floor/(n-1)}\) and its reflection.
