For frequent weak Poisson partial resets with positive linear growth, the second-order Gaussian diffusion approximation has exactly the correct stationary variance whenever finite but loses relative accuracy at even-moment order \(m\asymp s^{-2/3}\), with limiting moment ratio \(\exp[-2\lambda c^3/(9(\lambda-\gamma))]\) when \(ms^{2/3}\to c\). At the later scale \(p\asymp s^{-1}\), a strict interval of moment orders remains finite for the exact jump process but is already infinite for its Gaussian approximation.
