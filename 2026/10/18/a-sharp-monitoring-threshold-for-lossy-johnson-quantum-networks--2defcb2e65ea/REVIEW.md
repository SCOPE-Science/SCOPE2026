Local occupancy monitoring of a fixed-excitation all-to-all spin system is a natural detector-placement problem. Classical connected finite walks absorb eventually at any nonempty trap, whereas coherent dark states can survive even extensive monitored regions. The exact minimal number of monitored coordinates for universal absorption is a motivated structural boundary, accompanied by a robust closed probability law for all localized configurations and an explicit coherent-initialization exception. These are not merely numerical tables or arbitrary special parameter instances.
