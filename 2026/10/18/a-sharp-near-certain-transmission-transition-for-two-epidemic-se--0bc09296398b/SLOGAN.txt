For two epidemic sentinels on an \(n\)-vertex path, endpoint placement is optimal for all independent-bond outbreaks if and only if transmissibility \(p\) is at least the unique interior root of \(p^{n-3}[1+(n-2)(1-p)]=1\). The endpoint pair becomes uniquely optimal above that root, ties exactly two nearby pairs at the root, and the transition approaches full transmissibility with \((n-2)^2(1-p_n)\to2\).
