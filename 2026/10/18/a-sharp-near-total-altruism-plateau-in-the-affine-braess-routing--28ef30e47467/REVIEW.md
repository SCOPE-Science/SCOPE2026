# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The equilibrium construction is checked at both penetration branches and their shared boundary. The two route-cost differences and social-cost polynomial follow from direct edge-flow substitution. Every link satisfies the positive affine hypothesis of the aggregate-uniqueness theorem from Yi and Wei (2026); its Appendix II was examined and the result is used as an acknowledged premise. Strict inequality \(K>0\) establishes both the all-altruistic optimum and monotone quadratic decrease after the plateau. The rational asymptotic family is algebraically exact. The standalone checker verifies finite rational instances; the continuum conclusion is proved separately.

## Originality — PASS, subject to historical-priority risk
The base model, aggregate uniqueness, and the qualitative existence of penetration plateaus are prior work, not new claims. The more specific five-edge standard-Braess all-penetration law and the asymptotic near-full-penetration nonzero-gap family were not located in examined primary literature or research records. Closest comparisons: Yi–Wei (2026) full sections on six-edge modified Braess numerical experiments; Wang–He–Li (2026) weaving-ramp Stackelberg thresholds; Brown (2020) class-wide worst-case bounds; Hill–Brown (2024) Braess-resistant series-parallel guarantees. General variational-inequality theory could imply the result; search nonappearance alone does not establish priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The exact threshold gives the entire welfare-response law for a canonical non-series-parallel transportation network, not an incidental coefficient. The explicit family demonstrates no improvement from even almost-total altruistic adoption when network asymmetry increases, while keeping a nonvanishing absolute inefficiency gap.

## Limitations
Unit nonatomic demand, symmetric edge-latency pairing, strict parameter inequalities, static simultaneous choices, and positive affine costs. The external aggregate-uniqueness theorem is cited as a previously proved hypothesis, not newly proved here. Historical priority and independent validation remain open.
