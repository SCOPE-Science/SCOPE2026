The ETM-3D quadratic flow has an exact, extremely narrow negative-parameter window in which its origin and one nonzero equilibrium are simultaneously hyperbolic sinks, separated by a saddle. Its algebraic saddle-node boundary lies at \(\rho=-1.308660544\ldots\), while the origin undergoes a transcritical loss of stability at \(\rho=(5-\sqrt{105})/4\); above the saddle-node and up to zero, all trajectories converge exponentially to the origin.
