Conditionally unbiased tangent noise can stabilize otherwise repelling radial behavior of fixed-step SQP on the unit-circle constraint: the exact squared-radius map has a sharp attracting-two-cycle window \(3(5-\sqrt{13})/10<\eta<3(1-1/\sqrt5)/2\). At \(\eta=3/5\) its squared radii alternate superattractingly between \(3/5\) and \(3\), so the feasibility defect never vanishes.
