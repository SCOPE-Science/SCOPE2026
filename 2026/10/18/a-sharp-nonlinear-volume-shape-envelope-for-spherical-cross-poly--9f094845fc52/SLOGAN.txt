For every sphere-inscribed combinatorial cross-polytope, its volume is bounded sharply by \(\frac{2^d}{d!}(1-\Delta^2/(4d))^d\), where \(\Delta\) is its labelled distance from a regular cross-polytope up to orthogonal motion. Equality has an exact opposite-half-chord characterization and occurs for genuinely noncentrally symmetric polyhedra at every sufficiently small positive discrepancy.
