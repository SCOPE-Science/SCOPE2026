# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

With u=(x+z)/2 and w=(x−z)/2, equal assigned signs s give u′=asu+sy+1, y′=2bu, w′=asw, including the nonnegative branch at zero. If dist(T^n p,K)→0 for a nonempty compact K⊂D, then w_n→0. At a mixed-sign visit, |u_n|≤|w_n| and w_{n+1}=±(au_n−y_n), so infinitely many such visits force a subsequence (u_n,y_n,w_n)→(0,0,0), contradicting the exclusion of the origin from K. Hence there are eventually only equal-sign visits; |a|≥1, including equality, forces eventual exact w=0 rather than just approach. Each of the four open branches has determinant −2ab≠0 (the two determinant terms each give −ab), and the switching planes are null. Thus inverse images of null sets are null even without continuity on the switches. The basin lies in ∪_{n≥0}T^{−n}(D), which is null. This permits points (0,y,0) with y≠0 in K. The derivative cocycle for measures giving the switching set zero mass splits into a bounded invertible tangent block (determinant modulus |2b|) and signed scalar a, so the exponent identities follow. For 0<|a|<1 and 0<b<(1−|a|)/2 the stated fixed point lies strictly in the positive branch; its tangential radius (|a|+√(a²+8b))/2 and transverse modulus |a| are strictly below one, yielding an open basin. Until the tube threshold |w_j|≥δ, the reference and perturbed center agree exactly while |w_j|=|a|^j|w_0|; rounding up the crossing time gives the stated clock, provided the reference sign stays away from the switch through the preceding step.

PASS

The 3D map, symmetric invariant plane, branch Jacobian, and a worked transverse fixed-point multiplier are already in Lozi. The novel claim supported by the comparison is the nonlinear, ambient, all-compact-set null-basin criterion in the stated parameter domain, including neutral |a|=1 and switching-line intersections away from the origin. This is not a claim of novelty for block diagonalization or the derivative normal exponent alone.

The published thread-attractor example motivates distinguishing attraction in a natural invariant plane from attraction by volume in 3D.
