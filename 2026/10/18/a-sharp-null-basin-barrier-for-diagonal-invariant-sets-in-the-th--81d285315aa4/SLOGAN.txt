In the symmetric three-dimensional Lozi map, every nonempty compact invariant diagonal set avoiding the origin has zero three-dimensional attraction-basin volume whenever \(|a|\ge1\), including the neutral boundary and sets crossing the switching line. The bound is sharp in the parameter: for each \(0<|a|<1\), suitable nonzero coupling produces an ambient attracting diagonal fixed point; the mechanism is the exact normal multiplier \(a\operatorname{sgn}(x)\).
