# Review of the sharp seasonal-instability threshold

## Correctness — PASS
The mathematical object is a positive, globally bounded two-species ecological system with one-way cross-species terms alternating between seasons. Direct differentiation at the common equilibrium gives two Hurwitz Jordan-type Jacobians. Their exact exponential product has determinant \(e^{-2aT}\) and scaled trace \(2-bc\vartheta(1-\vartheta)T^2\); the hyperbolic eigenvalue identity proves the if-and-only-if local instability criterion. Derivatives of \(w/\cosh w\) and the concave duty-share product give the global uniqueness of the unstable duration band and the exact symmetric duty interval. The equality case is restricted to a simple unit negative Floquet multiplier, with no unproved nonlinear stability conclusion. The standalone `check.py` reproduces 25 exact rational matrix-product identities and 1,600 independent high-precision spectral calculations.

## Originality — PASS
The primary 2025 preprint by Swailem and Rucklidge treats sinusoidally forced predator–prey dynamics and numerical resonance tongues, not the alternating one-way ecological system or its explicit single-band duration/duty theorem. Cima–Gasull–Mañosa (2019/2020) **already establish the qualitative phenomenon** of instability induced by alternating stable planar systems; that phenomenon is explicitly acknowledged as prior. Their fixed-season nonhyperbolic elliptic construction does not give this hyperbolic positive ecological model or the exact global one-band/duty classification. Ji–Strugarek (2018) prove a sharp one-sided seasonal threshold for cooperative concave systems; their order-preserving hypotheses exclude the inhibitory season here. Gökçek (2004) supplies the more general, known matrix-exponential Floquet method; the new result **does not** claim that calculation as original. Its additional ecological family and exact all-parameter stability-phase classification are absent from the examined examples and theorems. Scientific searches covered equivalent monodromy descriptions, switched shears, larger Floquet stability criteria, seasonally switched population dynamics, and precise hyperbolic thresholds. The first preprint and the general switched-system paper were read materially; an additional 2026 ecological-network paper was accessible only by abstract and remains a specific residual risk. Search incompleteness is not evidence of absolute novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The construction distinguishes three ordinarily conflated concepts: stability of each environment, stability of the time-average, and stability under periodic switching. All frozen and average linearizations are stable, yet biological season length determines a *bounded* interval of instability. The exact universal one-band boundary and duty-cycle optimum form a complete phase diagram, not one arbitrary numerical example or a routine first-order perturbation. The result motivates precise ecological questions about seasonal interaction reversals while making no claim about global ecological basins or stochastic robustness.
