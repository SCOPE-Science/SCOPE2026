A smooth finite-field plane cubic with a rational flex and a point count divisible by \(3\) needs exactly one more covering line than Bézout predicts if and only if its rational group has a unique nonzero \(2\)-torsion point. The sharp excess-one case follows from a three-coset realization of near transversals in finite-group addition tables; the no-excess cases were already covered by zero-sum partition theory.
