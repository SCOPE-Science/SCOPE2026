The positive integers for which the reduced denominator of the reciprocal-gcd sum \(\sum_{j=1}^n1/\gcd(n,j)\) is divisible by \(3\) are counted asymptotically by \(C_3x/(\log x)^{1/3}\), with an explicit positive, absolutely convergent Euler-product constant. An exact ternary valuation criterion and a Selberg--Delange argument establish both the fractional logarithmic exponent and its leading constant.
