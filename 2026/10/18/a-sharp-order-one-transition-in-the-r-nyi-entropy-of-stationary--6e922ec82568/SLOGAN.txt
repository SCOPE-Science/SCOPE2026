For a finite reversible refresh chain with nonuniform stationary law, the Rényi entropy of stationary pairs has a unique lag maximum and overshoots its independence limit at every order \(0<\alpha<1\), even while absolute regularity decays geometrically. The overshoot disappears at the sharp Shannon threshold \(\alpha=1\), with an exact near-threshold peak-location and height law; in particular the lagged effective-support factor \(\mathcal J_\infty\) need not increase monotonically toward its limit.
