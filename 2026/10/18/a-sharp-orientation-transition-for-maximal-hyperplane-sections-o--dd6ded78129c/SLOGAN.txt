A Euclidean ball prism in every dimension \(d\ge4\) has a sharp height \(h_c=\sqrt{3/(d-2)}\) separating uniquely optimal oblique hyperplane sections from axial-plane sections. Every global affine maximizer is central, and in dimension four the exact largest three-volume is \((4\pi/3)(1+h^2/3)^{3/2}\) below the threshold and \(2\pi h\) above it.
