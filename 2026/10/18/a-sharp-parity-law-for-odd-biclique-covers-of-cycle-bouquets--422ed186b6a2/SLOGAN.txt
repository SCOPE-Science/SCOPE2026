Identifying the hubs of arbitrary cycles changes the odd biclique-cover problem from a disjoint-union obstruction to a sharp parity rule: \(b_2(G)=\sum_i\lfloor(\ell_i-1)/2\rfloor+(o\bmod2)\), where \(o\) counts the odd-length cycles. The binary-rank lower bound is attained exactly when that count is even.
