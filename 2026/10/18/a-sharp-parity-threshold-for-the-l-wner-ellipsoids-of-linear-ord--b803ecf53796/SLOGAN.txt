For the polytope of pairwise-ranking vectors of even permutations, deleting half the vertices shrinks the unique minimum-volume enclosing ellipsoid by exactly \(8/(5\sqrt5)\) in volume at orders \(n=4\) and \(n=5\), but does not change it at all for any \(n\ge6\). A four-label parity cancellation mechanism explains this sharp geometric threshold, although the minimum enclosing Euclidean sphere is unchanged at every \(n\ge4\).
