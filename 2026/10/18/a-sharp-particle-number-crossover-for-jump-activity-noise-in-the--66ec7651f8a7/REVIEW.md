# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** For every integer \(N\ge1\) and real \(d>0\), detailed balance proves the beta-binomial stationary law; fourth-order factorial-moment algebra yields the variance of the centered occupation square. A direct generator identity supplies a degree-two eigenmode and solves the activity counting Poisson equation, including boundary rates. Differentiating the finite tilted generator gives the variance, and explicit asymptotics prove the sharp \(N^{2/3}\) crossover. Exact-rational standalone comparisons independently tested 54 full generators with zero mismatches. The all-parameter result relies on the analytical proof, not enumeration.

**Originality — PASS within searched sources; residual risk.** Giardinà–Redig–Vafayi (2009) gives model and stationary measures, not evaluated jump-count second cumulants; Grosskinsky–Redig–Vafayi (2012) studies two-site condensate *motion* as independent diffusion vanishes; Pizzoferrato (2018) treats boundary-count activity with reservoirs rather than closed fixed-population total transfers; Huillet (2013) investigates discrete Moran occupation and spectral quantities, not this continuous-time jump-count Fano factor. Targeted record searches and comparison with accessible previous mathematical claims did not locate an equal or stronger statement. The use of a classical degree-two eigenmode means a general spectral-counting result might subsume the formula; that literature risk remains, and absence from a search is not a uniqueness proof.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Jump activity is a natural time-integrated kinetic observable in attractive transport. The exact rational noise law holds at arbitrary population and coupling and displays a nontrivial particle-number-dependent transition in the Fano ratio at \(N^{2/3}\), different from the static beta-binomial occupation law and previously studied weak-diffusion condensation regime. The classification is quantitative and usable as a benchmark for counting-field approximations. A Fano ratio tending to one does not imply a Poisson distribution.

**Limits and independent status.** Only two sites, fixed particle number, symmetric transfers, positive \(d\), and long-time first two total-activity cumulants are proved. No exhaustive bibliographic or independent scientific audit has been performed.
