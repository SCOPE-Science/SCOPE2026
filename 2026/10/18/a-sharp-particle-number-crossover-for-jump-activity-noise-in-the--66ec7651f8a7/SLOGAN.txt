In the stationary two-site symmetric inclusion process with \(N\) particles, total successful-jump activity has exact Fano factor \(1+(N-1)(N+2d+1)/[(2d+1)^2(2d+3)]\). Its excess crosses sharply from divergence to zero at the attraction-rate scale \(d\asymp N^{2/3}\), with limiting Fano factor \(1+1/(8c^3)\) when \(d/N^{2/3}\to c>0\).
