For every positive path-constant weighting of the five-dimensional theta-graph zonotope from a known sharp-generator counterexample, the fixed orthogonal projection inequality fails strictly. Its violation ratio is at most \(25/24\), attained exactly when the two length-two paths have equal weight and the length-three path has twice that weight; this improves the previously exhibited equal-weight ratio \(28/27\) within the specified class.
