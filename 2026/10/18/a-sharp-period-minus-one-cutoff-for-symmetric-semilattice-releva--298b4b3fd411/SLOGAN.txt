In positive semilattice relevance semantics on finite subsets of an infinite set, identifying cardinalities by exact sizes below \(k\) and residues modulo \(m\) thereafter preserves every nested formula exactly when \(m=1\) or \(k\ge m-1\). This sharp boundary yields an explicit \(k+m\)-state ternary semantics for symmetric ultimately periodic observations; below it, even one implication breaks the identification.
