The maximum of independent \(\operatorname{Bin}(n,p)\) counts retains an independent-Bernoulli-sum representation for every success probability and every sample size precisely when \(n\le2\); for each \(n\ge3\), small positive \(p\) destroys that representation. For the first non-universal case (two copies with \(n=3\)), the exact transition is the unique root \(p_*\approx0.1507324653\) of \(4p^6+8p^3+3p^2+6p-1=0\), and representation holds precisely for \(p\ge p_*\).
