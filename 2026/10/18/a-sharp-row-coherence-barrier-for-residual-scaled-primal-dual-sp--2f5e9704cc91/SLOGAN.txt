For a fixed unguarded diagonal residual correction of primal-dual splitting, convergence on invertible equality constraints holds exactly when the normalized row-Gram top eigenvalue is below \(4/3\); with two rows this is the sharp correlation bound \(|\rho|<1/3\). Orthogonal rows uniquely permit exact two-step termination among positive diagonal primal/dual residual scalings, while stronger row coherence can destabilize a trial even though ordinary PDHG converges.
