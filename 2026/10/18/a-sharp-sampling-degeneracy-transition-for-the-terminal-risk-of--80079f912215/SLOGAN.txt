For nonuniform stochastic proximal point iteration on a canonical pair of strongly convex quadratics, the optimal stationary mean-squared error undergoes a sharp transition at the unique positive root \(\gamma_\star=1.4333451253\ldots\) of an explicit quintic. Below it a strictly positive sampling distribution attains a risk below \(1/9\); above it every legal distribution has risk strictly above \(1/9\), with the infimum approached only by abandoning one component in the excluded sampling limit.
