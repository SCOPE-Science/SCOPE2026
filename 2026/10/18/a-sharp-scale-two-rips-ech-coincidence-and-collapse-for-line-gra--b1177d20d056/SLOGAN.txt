For every finite connected graph \(G\) of girth at least seven, the scale-two graph-metric Vietoris–Rips complex of its line graph equals its scale-two closed-neighborhood Čech complex and simplicially collapses onto the scale-one clique complex, recovering the homotopy type of \(G\). Six-cycles make the girth threshold sharp: \(L(C_6)\) changes from \(S^1\) at scale one to \(S^2\) at scale two.
