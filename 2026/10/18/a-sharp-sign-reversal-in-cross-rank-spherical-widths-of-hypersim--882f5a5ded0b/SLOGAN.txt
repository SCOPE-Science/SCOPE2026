Directional widths of two hypersimplices of different proportional ranks can be *negatively correlated* even when measured along the same uniformly random Euclidean direction. There is a unique sharp transition: the rank-\(\lfloor\alpha n\rfloor\) width has negative limiting covariance with the half-rank width exactly for \(0<\alpha<0.207998088885\ldots\), and positive covariance above that threshold.
