For the Cesàro averaging operator on geometrically weighted \(\ell^2\), every nonzero geometric weight has an exact singular-value Weyl constant: \(n s_n\to(2\pi)^{-1}\int_0^{2\pi}|1-\sqrt r e^{i\theta}|^{-1}d\theta\). This constant also determines the logarithmic trace and exceeds the corresponding eigenvalue-counting coefficient \(1\), exposing a quantitative nonnormality effect.
