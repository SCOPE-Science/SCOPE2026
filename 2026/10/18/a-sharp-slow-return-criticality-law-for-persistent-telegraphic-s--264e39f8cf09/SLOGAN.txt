For telegraphic first passage with constant-speed returns, the optimal Poisson alarm rate has a sharp slow-return transition at the dimensionless tumble parameter \(\varepsilon_c=0.439692620785908\ldots\): it stays positive below the threshold, vanishes as \(v^{2/5}\) at it, and as \(v^{2/3}\) above it. The proof identifies a globally strictly convex return-cost factor within an established exact renewal formula.
