For the three-mode continuous least-squares projector onto \(\operatorname{span}\{1,\cos t,\sin t\}\) on a short symmetric interval \([-h,h]\), the sharp \(L^1\) and \(L^\infty\) norms equal \(1+12\sqrt6/25-(387\sqrt6/21875)h^2+O(h^4)\). The only maximizing evaluation points are the two interval endpoints for all sufficiently small apertures, and the quadratic correction is strictly negative.
