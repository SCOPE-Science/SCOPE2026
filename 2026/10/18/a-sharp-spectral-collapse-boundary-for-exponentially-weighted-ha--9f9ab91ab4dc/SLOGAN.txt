For exponentially weighted translations \(f(z)\mapsto e^{-bz}f(z+a)\) on weighted half-plane Bergman spaces, the spectrum collapses from a full disk or interval to \(\{0\}\) as soon as both parameters are positive, although every positive power remains noncompact. Every approximation number of each power equals its operator norm, and the exact norms decay as a sharp Gaussian-in-the-power factor times \(n^{-(\alpha+1)}\).
