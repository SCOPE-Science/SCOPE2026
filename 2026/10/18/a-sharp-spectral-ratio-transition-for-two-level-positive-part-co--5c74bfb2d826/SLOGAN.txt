The spectral positive-part map on Hermitian two-by-two matrices is locally nonexpansive in operator and trace norms exactly up to the sharp spectral ratio \(\lambda_+/|\lambda_-|=1+\sqrt2\); above it, the optimal infinitesimal gain has the explicit profile \(2q^2/\sqrt{4q^2-1}\), where \(q=\lambda_+/(\lambda_++|\lambda_-|)\). Its global sharp Lipschitz constant in both norms is \(2/\sqrt3\), while the Frobenius constant is one.
