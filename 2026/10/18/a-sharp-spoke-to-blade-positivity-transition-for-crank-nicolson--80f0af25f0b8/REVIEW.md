# Scientific review

**Correctness.** The claim is a closed spectral calculation on four invariant orthogonal subspaces of a positively weighted generalized windmill. The proof gives explicit projector diagonals, proves nonnegative off-diagonals for the completed Crank–Nicolson map, verifies the hub root and the exact sign at that root, then handles both singular regimes by controlled expansions. Boundary equality is included. The packaged standard-library rational-matrix verifier corroborated the formulas in 1,290 assertion groups. Finite checks are not an infinite proof. Correctness assessment: PASS.

**Originality.** The generic resolvent criterion for Crank–Nicolson positivity and a broad theta-method positivity bound were already known and are not rebranded as contributions. The comparison specifically asked whether a source already implies the \(r,s\)-dependent crossover \(a/b=r(rs-1)/[r(s-2)+1]\), including the extension of hub control beyond the saturated-edge regime, and whether the two different singular exponents are already tabulated. A published general Markov-resolvent research record, pertinent earlier time-stepping papers and the full-text 2023 one-dimensional heat-grid study did not reveal that classification. The generic criterion entails a diagonal-root test for any graph but does not supply the new geometry-dependent simplifications without additional projector, sign and scaling analysis. Unindexed weighted-windmill work and a published research record whose link could not be opened remain originality risks. Originality assessment: PASS subject to those residual risks, not a claim of absolute uniqueness.

**Value.** This is a sharp positivity constraint for a natural all-size family of hub-and-community diffusion networks, not a single numerical table. It provides an explicit cost-free choice of the limiting vertex orbit, an exact boundary in two discrete and one continuous parameters, and a structural distinction between two-node and larger clique blades that changes the strong-conductance scaling exponent. These allow mathematically justified timestep design and identify where a hub-only rule ceases to be sharp. Value assessment: PASS.
