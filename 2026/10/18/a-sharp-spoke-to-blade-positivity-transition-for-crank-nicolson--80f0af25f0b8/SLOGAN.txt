For diffusion on the weighted generalized windmill \(K_1\vee(rK_s)\), Crank–Nicolson positivity is controlled by the hub up to the sharp spoke-to-blade ratio \(a/b=r(rs-1)/[r(s-2)+1]\), then switches to the clique vertices. As blade conductance grows, the sharp positivity step decays as \(a^{-1/2}\) for triangular blades but as \(a^{-1}\) for every larger blade, with explicit constants.
