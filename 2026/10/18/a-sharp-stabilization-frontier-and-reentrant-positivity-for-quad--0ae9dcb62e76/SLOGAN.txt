On commuting positive quadratic gradient flows, standard stabilized ETDRK2 decreases the original energy for every positive step exactly when \(\beta\ge\max\{0,\max_j(a_j-\ell_j)/2\}\), sharpening the published sufficient stabilizer in this setting. Even inside that energy-stable window, reducing stabilization can destroy and then restore positivity; the stiff-step minimax stabilizer has an exact spectral-midpoint formula.
