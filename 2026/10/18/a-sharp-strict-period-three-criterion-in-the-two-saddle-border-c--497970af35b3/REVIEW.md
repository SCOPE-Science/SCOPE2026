The claim is a necessary-and-sufficient characterization for strict Schur-attracting period-three cycles, not a claim about border-touching or neutral cycles. Affine composition yields the three orbit coordinates and the trace/determinant of the third return. The strict Jury–Schur conditions and the sign of \(N_2\) yield the exact admissibility/stability criterion. The positive numerator of the formal \(LRR\) initial state, combined with \(\det(I-M)>0\) under Schur stability, excludes the rival itinerary. Any homogeneous itinerary inherits an unstable saddle eigenvalue. Every step is algebraic; exact rational arithmetic confirms the explicit witness, and no finite experiment is used to prove parameter-wide statements.

The complete accessible Fatoyinbo–Simpson synopsis (arXiv:2210.14445; 2023 journal version), including its Fig. 7 and Appendix A, explicitly reports stable \(LLR\) behavior **outside** the two-saddle region and derives \(LRR\) stability boundaries in another offset regime. The exact two-saddle admissibility inequalities, nonemptiness witness, and \(LRR\) obstruction are not immediate corollaries of those source results. A 2014 general construction of infinite attracting cycles has a different codimension-three hypothesis. Further symbolically equivalent classifications elsewhere remain possible.

## Value — PASS

The two-saddle region is a natural focus of robust-chaos analysis; nevertheless it need not exclude an open island of locally attracting period-three dynamics. A complete symbolic classification, not merely a numerical example, determines which of the two primitive three-cycle itineraries can attract and supplies an exact parameter test. This exposes a qualitative boundary of the two-saddle description useful for separating periodic sinks from chaotic-regime hypotheses. It is a motivated natural classification rather than an arbitrary parameter slice.
