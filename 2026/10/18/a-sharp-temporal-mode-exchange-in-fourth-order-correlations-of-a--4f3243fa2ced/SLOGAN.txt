In a noninteracting Brownian gas sharing a symmetric switching harmonic trap, delayed fourth-order cross-particle correlations exchange their slowest decay mode exactly at \(r=k-h^2/k\), with a nonzero \(t e^{-2rt}\) resonance at the boundary. A strictly positive cubic time cusp identifies the hidden switching rate from the ratio of third and second short-lag derivatives, without knowing the diffusivity.
