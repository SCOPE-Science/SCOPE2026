In the convex hull of a cube and a scaled coordinate octahedron, the sharp largest-tetrahedron determinant is \(\max\{16,t^2(t+3)\}\) for \(1<t<3\). At the unique transition \(t^2(t+3)=16\), the maximizing configuration switches from two alternating cube tetrahedra to eight three-axis tetrahedra, and the occupied-volume fraction attains its unique minimum \(1/(3t)\).
