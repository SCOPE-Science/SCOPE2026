# Reproducibility and verification scope

The document `RESULT.md` contains a complete analytic proof for all real generator vectors. Its proof of the universal lower bound uses the four triple determinants, an explicit contained tetrahedron and the classical zonotope volume identity. It does not use finite experiments as an infinite proof.

Execute `python3 verify.py` in this directory. The standalone script uses Python's standard library, lists the six axial and eight cubic vertices of the canonical rhombic dodecahedron, and enumerates all \(\binom{14}{4}=1001\) vertex quadruples. It evaluates each determinant using both a direct coordinate expression and an independent permutation-sum expression, checks the complete six-column determinant histogram separated by axial-vertex count, and identifies exactly eight maximum determinant-20 tetrahedra. It also checks that different maximizers share at most two vertices, which is the finite hypothesis for the argument excluding nonvertex maximizing tetrahedra. The exact volume of the containing polytope is derived in the proof by a six-pyramid dissection.

The certificate checks 64 rational examples of the explicit universal tetrahedral witness, for diagnosis only. These finite examples are not claimed to prove the formula for all real parameters. The analytic expression and its determinant expansion appear separately in `RESULT.md`.

Expected exact output:

`VERIFY_OK vertex_sets=1001 max_determinant=20 maximizing_sets=8 max_intersection=2 parameter_samples=64`

Scientific limitations: the theorem concerns three-dimensional zonotopes generated by no more than four segments; the relative determinant imbalance inequalities are necessary near-equality conditions, not converse quantitative stability of body shapes. Verification of historical originality remains qualified by possible earlier results under different terminology.
