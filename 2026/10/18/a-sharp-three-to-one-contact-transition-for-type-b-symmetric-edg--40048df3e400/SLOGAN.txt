For the type-B symmetric edge polytope of a complete split graph with clique size \(p\ge2\) and independent-class size \(q\ge2\), the sharp Euclidean inradius is \(1/\sqrt{\max\{q,(p+q)/4\}}\). At the exact threshold \(p=3q\), tangent facets jump from \(2^q\) to \(2^q+2^{p+q}\), and the affine dimension of all inball centers collapses from \(p\) to zero; above the threshold exactly \(2^{p+q}\) facets touch.
