For binary Pólya reinforcement with fixed initial color fraction, the total-variation error of the independent binomial approximation has an exact three-regime phase diagram governed by draws divided by initial concentration. The small-reinforcement error is asymptotic to \(e^{-1/2}n/(\sqrt{2\pi}A_n)\), while comparable draws and concentration produce an explicit positive Gaussian-variance mismatch, and overwhelming draws force distance one.
