For large real orders \(\alpha\), normalized fractional Volterra integration has sharp trace norm \(\tfrac12+\tfrac1{4\alpha}+O(\alpha^{-2})\), while its optimal rank-one nuclear approximation error is \(\tfrac1{8\alpha}+O(\alpha^{-2})\). A trace-class Hankel remainder bound, rather than an ordinary operator- or Hilbert–Schmidt estimate, proves the first-order nuclear mass outside the leading singular mode.
