The largest contained 4-simplex along the natural truncation from the regular 16-cell to the regular 24-cell obeys an exact two-polynomial volume law, switching at \(t=\sqrt2-1\). At the switch, the maximizing geometry changes from 192 rigid vertex-supported simplices to 48 continuous movable-apex families, with the uniform truncated 16-cell having exact maximum volume \(1/27\).
