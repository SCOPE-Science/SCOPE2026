For the normalized even oscillator superposition \((h_0+t h_2)/\sqrt{1+t^2}\), the negative Wigner domain switches exactly at \(t=2\sqrt2\) from two disks to one annulus; at the transition its nodal set is two intersecting unit circles. The negative Wigner volume remains positive for every \(t>0\) but obeys the sharp nonperturbative law \(\lim_{t\downarrow0}t\log\mathcal N(t)=-1/\sqrt2\).
