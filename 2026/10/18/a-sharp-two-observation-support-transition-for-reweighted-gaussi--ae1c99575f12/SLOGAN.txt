For two unequally weighted observations, the exact Gaussian-mixture NPMLE switches from one to two atoms at the closed-form separation threshold \(2a_{\mathrm c}(q)\), determined by the sharp Bernoulli Kearns--Saul constant, strictly before the singleton dual's local-curvature reversal. Under independent Gamma reweighting, its two-atom probability is an exact symmetric-Beta interval probability, equaling the nonzero solution of \(\operatorname{arctanh}(x)=a^2x\) for uniform normalized weights.
