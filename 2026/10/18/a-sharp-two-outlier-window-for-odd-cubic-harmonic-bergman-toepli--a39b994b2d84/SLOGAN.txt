The harmonic-Bergman Toeplitz operator with symbol \(z^3+az\) has exactly two isolated spectral outliers, at \(\pm\frac{3a}{8}\sqrt{-a}\), precisely when \(\frac{14+2\sqrt{13}}9<|a|<4\); outside this sharp parameter window its entire spectrum is just the symbol's boundary curve. The two outliers enter and leave through distinct root-to-boundary crossings, yielding an exact zero-or-two spectral phase diagram.
