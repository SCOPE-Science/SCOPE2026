For the finite-rationality SIS–logit epidemic model, \(\sigma\max\{0,b_1-a_1,b_0-a_0\}\leq4\) excludes every interior periodic orbit, and the constant four cannot be improved. An exact admissible payoff family arbitrarily close above that bound has a unique repelling endemic equilibrium and periodic epidemic–behavior \(\omega\)-limits from every other interior state.
