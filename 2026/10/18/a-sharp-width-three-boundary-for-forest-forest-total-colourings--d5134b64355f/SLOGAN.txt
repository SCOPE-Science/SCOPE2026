In the forest–forest generalized total coloring problem, \(K_{1,a,b}\) has an exact three-step phase diagram whenever \(\min(a,b)\le3\); the sole width-three delayed transition is \(K_{1,3,5}\), which needs four rather than five colors. At that exceptional graph, every optimal coloring forces each monochromatic edge forest to be a spanning tree on all vertices allowed by its color.
