For the Markovian coupon collector that visits \((n-2)\)-subsets and collects pairs, the time to cover all pairs tends to \(5+\mathrm{NegBin}(2,1/2)\), with total-variation error at most \(17/(n-2)\). The exact probability of finishing at the earliest possible time, five, is \((n-4)(n-3)(2n-5)/(8(n-2)^3)\).
