In the first fourfold-equivariant homogeneous degree allowing both fourth and eighth angular harmonics, a second-harmonic coefficient \(c\) shifts the exact global-stability threshold \(a_*\) with the **same sign as \(c\)** throughout the strictly rotating parameter domain. The threshold has slope \(\left[b/(\omega+\sqrt{\omega^2-b^2})\right]^2\) at zero, and the unstable side admits an exact geometric escape-time formula with a sharp simple pole at the center boundary.
