For the first genus-six, positive-\(5\)-rank minimal-automorphism example \(y^5-y=x^2+x+x^{-1}\), the exact zeta numerator is irreducible: its Jacobian is simple over \(\mathbb F_5\) and has Newton slopes \(0^4,(1/2)^4,1^4\). This gives an explicit nonordinary instance relevant to the originating paper's Newton-polygon prescription question.
