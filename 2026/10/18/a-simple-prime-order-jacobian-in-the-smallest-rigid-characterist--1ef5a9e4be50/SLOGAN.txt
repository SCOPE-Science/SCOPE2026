The smallest characteristic-five, \(5\)-rank-zero Artin–Schreier example with no extra geometric automorphisms has an irreducible degree-twelve Weil polynomial and an \(\mathbb F_5\)-simple Jacobian. Its group of \(\mathbb F_5\)-rational Jacobian points is cyclic of prime order \(11731\); the complete numerator and independently reproducible extension counts are given in the proof.
