One antisymmetric hopping-pair defect opens an exactly computable zero-mode communication path through an otherwise uniform diamond channel, but its two-endpoint transfer ceiling is \(1/(1+N^2\delta^2/4)\) for every nonzero defect. For generic defect strengths the ceiling is sharp, forcing a \(1/N\) tolerance window and an order-\(N/g\) slow-beat time cost within the resonant flat-band effective model.
