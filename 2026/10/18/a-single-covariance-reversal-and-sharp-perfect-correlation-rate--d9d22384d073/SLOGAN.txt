In bond-percolated random recursive trees, root-cluster size and the number of daughter components have negative covariance at two vertices but undergo exactly one sign reversal, ultimately becoming asymptotically perfectly correlated. For fixed \(0<p<1\), the correlation deficit is asymptotic to \(n^{-p}/(2(1-p)\Gamma(1+p)V_p)\), with \(V_p=2/\Gamma(1+2p)-1/\Gamma(1+p)^2\).
