For the doubling map, observing membership in any translated semicircle gives precisely independent fair coin flips, but the observations retain almost all information about the initial point for every dyadic placement except \(a=1/4\). The exceptional placement is exactly two-to-one, whereas all other dyadic placements admit a future-only decoder with explicit synchronizing word \(0^{r-1}10^{r-1}\) and exponential waiting-time tail.
