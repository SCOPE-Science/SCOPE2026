For two damped Gaussian probes driven by a common Ornstein–Uhlenbeck source, the time-antisymmetric cross-covariance has one fixed sign at every positive lag and exactly one optimal lag, independent of local thermal noise. Near equality of all three relaxation rates, the optimal lag approaches \(\varphi/\kappa\), where \(\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\), even though the lag asymmetry vanishes at exact equality.
