The classical twelve-cubic Togliatti threefold has a normal projective model but a nonnormal homogeneous coordinate ring: its normalization adds just the degree-one monomial \(x_0x_1x_2\). This single missing section forces depth \(1\), determines its full Hilbert series, and obstructs arithmetic Cohen--Macaulayness despite a degree-\(16\) normal toric threefold.
