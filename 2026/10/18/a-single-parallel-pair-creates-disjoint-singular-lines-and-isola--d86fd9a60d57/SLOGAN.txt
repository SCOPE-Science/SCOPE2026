Deleting a single basis from the rank-two uniform matroid creates a toric variety whose reduced singular locus is exactly \(n-2\) disjoint lines plus \(\binom{n-2}{2}\) isolated Segre-cone points. The lines have generic transverse rank-one \(2\times(n-3)\) determinantal type, while the isolated points have rank-one \(2\times(n-2)\) type.
