# Reproducibility and limits
Run `python3 parallel_pair_check.py` with standard Python. The program reconstructs all bases for \(4\le n\le9\), computes exact rational linear ranks of proposed edge generators, checks that every other basis-difference character is generated by at most two adjacent swaps, checks all four-cycle minor relations and the weights exposing each special edge, and checks fixed-point/component counting identities. Its final line is `VERIFY_OK` if all checks succeed.

The general theorem is proved by explicit semigroup generators and bipartite-flow relations, the standard singular-support description of rank-one determinantal cones, toric orbit-closure gluing, and Segre degrees. Finite tests do not prove the statements for untested \(n\), nor do they verify literature priority. No outside audit was conducted. The singular subscheme beyond its reduced support is not determined.
