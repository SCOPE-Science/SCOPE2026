The John ellipsoid of a one-path-defect \(K_{2,m}\) Lipschitz ball is an explicit weighted-Laplacian energy ball, and all edge constraints remain tangent at every positive defect length. Its volume has a singular finite-size crossover: the relative response is quadratic in the defect length at fixed \(m\), linear at fixed positive length in large \(m\), and has an exact nontrivial profile at scale \(m^{-1/2}\).
