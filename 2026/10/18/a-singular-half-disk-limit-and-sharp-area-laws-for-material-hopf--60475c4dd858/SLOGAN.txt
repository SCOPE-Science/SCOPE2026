For a transported off-centre disk in the cubic Hopf flow, the co-rotated long-time shape is a full disk, a filled half-disk, or a circular arc according as the initial boundary encloses, touches, or excludes the equilibrium. An exact area formula and a critical \(e^{-t}\)-wide boundary layer show how the tangent case develops a latent diameter and switches from generic \(e^{-2t}\) area correction to \(e^{-t}\).
