For every \(n\ge3\), the three-axis nonattacking-rook complex on a \(3\times3\times n\) board splits up to homotopy into six ordinary \(3\times n\) chessboard complexes and exactly \(9n-5\) circles. At \(n=4\) the six pieces are genuine tori, while for every \(n\ge5\) they are wedges of two-spheres, leaving a free fundamental group of rank \(9n-5\) at arbitrarily large board length.
