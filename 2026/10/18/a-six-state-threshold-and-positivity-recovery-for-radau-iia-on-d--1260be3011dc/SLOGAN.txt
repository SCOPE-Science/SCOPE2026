For the third-order two-stage Radau IIA method on a uniform directed Markov cycle, five or fewer states remain strictly positive at every positive step, but six states have the exact positivity set \(\{0\}\cup[\tau,\infty)\), where \(\tau\approx0.208608859735\) is the unique positive root of \(h^5+15h^4+60h^3+90h^2+36h-12\). Every larger directed cycle also loses positivity at sufficiently small steps and recovers it for all sufficiently large steps.
