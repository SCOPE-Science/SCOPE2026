In a network epidemic where each newly infected individual contacts exactly one random neighbor before permanent recovery, adding a connection can reduce expected total infections. The smallest example has six vertices: joining the hubs of \(K_{2,4}\) lowers the uniform-seed mean from \(19/6\) to \(79/25\), and an exact formula proves the same slowdown for every \(K_{2,m}\) with \(m\ge4\).
