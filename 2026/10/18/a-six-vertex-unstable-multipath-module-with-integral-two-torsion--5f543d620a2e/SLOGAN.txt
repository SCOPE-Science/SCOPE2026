The bidirected complete bipartite digraph \(\overleftrightarrow K_{2,4}\) is an unstable dynamical module, yet its multipath complex has \(\widetilde H_2\cong\mathbb Z^7\oplus\mathbb Z/2\). An exact Smith computation and a nonorientable top-dimensional pseudomanifold certificate furnish a six-vertex counterexample to the torsion-implies-stability conjecture as stated in Caputi–Collari–Di Trani–Smith (2022).
