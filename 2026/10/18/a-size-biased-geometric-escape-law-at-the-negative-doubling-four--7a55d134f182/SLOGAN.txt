At the natural period-four boundary of the negative-doubling map with symmetric hole \((2/5,3/5)\), Lebesgue survivor mass is exactly \((2n+4)/(5\,2^n)\), and the first-hole time from uniform initial conditions outside the hole has the size-biased geometric law \(\Pr(N=n)=n/2^{n+1}\). Conditioned on surviving many iterates, the first transition from the alternating outer-cell cycle into an inner-cell channel occurs at an asymptotically uniform fraction of the observed lifetime.
