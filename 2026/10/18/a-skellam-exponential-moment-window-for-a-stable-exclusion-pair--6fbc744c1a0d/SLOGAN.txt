For a stable pair of heterogeneous exclusion walkers, the tagged particle has the local long-time exponential-moment profile of a bilateral Poisson walk with effective right/left rates \(b_1b_2/(b_1+a_2)\) and \(a_1a_2/(b_1+a_2)\), including an explicit initial-gap prefactor. This determines a non-Gaussian local large-deviation rate beyond the previously established speed and Gaussian variance; no global or finite-time Skellam law is asserted.
