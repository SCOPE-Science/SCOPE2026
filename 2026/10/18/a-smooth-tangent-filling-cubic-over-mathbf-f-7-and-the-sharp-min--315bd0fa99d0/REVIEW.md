# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The cubic \(X^3+XYZ+Y^3+YZ^2-Z^3=0\) is geometrically smooth by the explicit identity \((-2Y^2+Y+3)(3Y^4+4Y+2)+(-Y^3-2Y^2+2)(Y^3+Y^2+1)=1\) modulo \(7\), together with the \(X=0\) boundary case. Its \(12\) rational points and \(12\) distinct tangent vectors are listed explicitly. Exact incidence over all \(57\) rational projective points gives \(36,9,9,3\) point counts of tangent multiplicities \(1,2,3,5\), so there are no uncovered points. Conic polarity and the line-component argument exclude all degrees below \(3\); the proof covers singular or reducible degree-two curves.

## Originality — PASS
The primary source Asgarli–Ghioca, arXiv:2302.13420v1, posed the minimum-degree question. Its existence theorem applies to \(q\ge11\); the discussed failure of its particular degree-six construction when \(q=7\) neither constructs nor excludes the present cubic. Asgarli–Ghioca–Yip, arXiv:2208.13299v1, studies curve rational-point blocking, distinct from the dual tangent-line blocking set here. Classical 12-point dual blocking sharpness is cited, not claimed new. Indexed research-record queries and the available earlier claims disclosed no equivalent q7 smooth-cubic solution. An unindexed equivalent remains possible.

## Value — PASS
This is a sharp explicit solution to a published extremal existence question in the small prime field that the motivating construction did not address. The unexpectedly low degree \(3\) and the extremal \(12\)-tangent dual blocking configuration give structural motivation beyond an arbitrary single-equation census.

## Limitations and independent review
No assertion of uniqueness of cubic examples, of classification over larger finite fields, or of independent certification is made. The verifier certifies exact finite incidence and a polynomial identity; the mathematical proof supplies the geometric arguments. Bibliographic coverage is necessarily incomplete.
