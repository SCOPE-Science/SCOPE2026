For every finite unit-edge cactus graph, the covariance precision of a uniform continuous Lipschitz potential is exactly a weighted graph Laplacian: bridges have conductance \(3\) and each cycle of length \(r\) has an explicit rational conductance \(a_r^{-1}\). Its negative off-diagonal entries reconstruct every labeled graph edge, while a spanning-tree product gives the exact covariance determinant. In contrast, for the natural non-cactus graph \(K_{2,3}\), a missing edge has nonzero precision entry \(-3/8\).
