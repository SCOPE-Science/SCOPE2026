For the Moser–de Bruijn set \(\mathcal M\) of sums of distinct powers of \(4\), the count of integers below \(4^k\) representable as \(a+3b\) with \(a,b\in\mathcal M\) is exactly \(((2+\sqrt2)^{k+1}+(2-\sqrt2)^{k+1})/4\). The largest such representation multiplicity is exactly the Fibonacci number \(F_{k+1}\), so the sumset has density zero but unbounded multiplicities.
