For every \(p\ge2\), the full strong discrete-Morse matching complex of \(p\) unfilled four-cycles meeting at one hub simplicially collapses to the join of \(p\) six-cycles, hence is \(S^{2p-1}\). The one-petal case is instead a wedge of five circles: a shared hub changes the topology of the strong matching complex in a way the disjoint-union join formula does not predict.
