# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact group-translation identity \(A^2=(|S|\bmod2)I\) follows because the translations by distinct involutions commute, and all cross-terms cancel in characteristic two. The squaring rule therefore gives the dichotomy for *every* element, not merely a basis vector. Fixed points of the odd-valency map must have coordinates among the roots of \(z^4-z\), and the two field-intersection cases are proved exhaustively using rank–nullity and the unique decomposition in \(\mathbb F_4=\mathbb F_2(\omega)\). The square-zero relation for \(B=A+I\) proves that every listed vector is idempotent and that all choices are distinct. The hypercube rank computation uses an explicit truncated-polynomial algebra isomorphism and yields all odd dimensions. Boundary cases \(d=1\), even valency, finite and infinite fields, and nonperfect fields are covered. Direct independent multiplication over \(\mathbb F_2\), \(\mathbb F_4\), \(\mathbb F_8\) agrees in 17 exhaustive small cases, with four more exact binary-rank checks. No finite experiment is used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual historical risk

Giacomelli (arXiv:2605.12672v1, especially Sections 4 and 9.1) defines Cayley evolution algebras and studies combinatorial expansion, support growth, and persistence. It does not count idempotent elements or classify the field-extension jump. The proposed assertion that support contains a union of graph neighbourhoods is inapplicable when coordinate sums cancel; the explicit even-valency square map supplies a mathematically demonstrated counterexample to unconditional persistence/cover-time implications. Costoya–Muñoz–Tocino–Viruel study Cayley evolution algebra automorphisms, not these element equations. García-Martínez–Pérez-Rodríguez study complex-field existence of idempotents, not characteristic-two parameter sets. An earlier general binary perfect-evolution-algebra idempotent census covers part of the \(\mathbb F_2\) case: this is credited and excluded from independent novelty claims. Actual searches for characteristic-two cubelike idempotents, Frobenius-fixed-point formulations, parity laws, the \(\mathbb F_4\) jump, and equivalent module formulations found no directly covering statement; an equivalent result under a different name remains possible, particularly through characteristic-two evolution-operator or Frobenius-semilinear literature. This does not purport to certify priority or independence.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The question concerns distinguished fixed points and actual squaring dynamics in the graph-defined nonassociative algebras recently investigated via expansion. The theorem determines the entire idempotent locus for every coefficient field of characteristic two and every connected cubelike graph, not an arbitrary finite slice; it also gives a sharp field-extension transition and a nontrivial obstruction to inferring element dynamics from graph connectivity. Infinite families of hypercubes and complete cubelike graphs have contrasting, exact ranks and idempotent counts. The result is structural and reproduces a genuine low-characteristic cancellation phenomenon rather than only a software census or a merely restated degree parameter.

## Closest sources and limits

Giacomelli, arXiv:2605.12672v1 (Cayley Definition 9.1, Theorems 4.3, 4.5), is the closest motivating paper. Costoya et al., arXiv:2207.01335 (Cayley automorphisms), and García-Martínez–Pérez-Rodríguez, DOI 10.1007/s00013-026-02251-0 (complex idempotents), are the closest inspected alternatives. The result is unweighted and characteristic-two only; the rank for arbitrary generating sets is left as an explicit finite-dimensional computation. Historical equivalence through Frobenius-semilinear theory remains a risk, and some characteristic-two operator literature was not available for complete inspection.
