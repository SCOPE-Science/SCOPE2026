For the unweighted cubelike evolution algebra of a generating set on \((\mathbb F_2)^d\) over any characteristic-two field, the second iterated squaring map is either identically zero (even valency) or coordinatewise fourth power (odd valency). In the odd case, its idempotents are completely parametrized by binary vectors, and the exact count jumps from \(2^{N-r}\) to \(2^N\) precisely when the field acquires \(\mathbb F_4\), where \(r=\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_2}(A+I)\).
