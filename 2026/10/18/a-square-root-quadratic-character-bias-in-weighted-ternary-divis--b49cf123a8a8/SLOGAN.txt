For the normalized ternary ordered-divisor function \(D_3(n)=d_3(n)/3^{\omega(n)}\), every fixed reduced arithmetic progression has an explicit two-term asymptotic whose square-root coefficient comes only from quadratic Dirichlet characters, with error \(O_{q,\varepsilon}(x^{1/3+\varepsilon})\). In particular, the weighted sum over integers congruent to \(1\pmod3\) exceeds that over \(2\pmod3\) by a strictly positive constant times \(\sqrt x\), up to a power-saving error.
