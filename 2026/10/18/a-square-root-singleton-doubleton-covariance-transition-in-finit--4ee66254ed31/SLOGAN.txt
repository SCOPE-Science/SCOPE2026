In a finite symmetric Dirichlet population with fixed total concentration \(0<\gamma<1\), the numbers of species seen once and twice switch from positive to negative covariance at sample size \(\sqrt{2(1-\gamma)m}+(3+2\gamma)/2+O_\gamma(m^{-1/2})\), and for sufficiently large \(m\) there is exactly one sign transition across all sample sizes. The square-root cutoff disappears from positive square-root sample scales when \(\gamma\ge1\).
