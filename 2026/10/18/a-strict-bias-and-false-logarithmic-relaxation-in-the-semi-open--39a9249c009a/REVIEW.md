The sign and exact coefficients make this a reusable benchmark for assessing reduced-variable approximations in non-equilibrium lattice dynamics, not just an observation that two plotted curves differ. This is an exact bounded-domain crossover diagnostic, not a proposed general theorem for all boundary approximations.
