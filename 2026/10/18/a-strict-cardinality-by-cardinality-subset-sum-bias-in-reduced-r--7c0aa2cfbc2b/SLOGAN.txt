For every modulus \(N\ge2\), **no fixed-cardinality layer** of the subsets of \((\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z)^\times\) has uniformly distributed sums: for each prime \(p\mid N\), zero differs from every nonzero residue by exactly \((-1)^r[z^r](1+z+\cdots+z^{p-1})^{\varphi(N)/(p-1)}\). This positive-magnitude bias alternates with subset cardinality, explaining how pooled nonempty subset sums can nevertheless become uniform for odd moduli.
