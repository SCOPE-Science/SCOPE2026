# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** For the stated reflected diffusion with position-only Poisson resets, the exponential-local-time-budget Robin problem yields an explicit fixed-budget threshold-time transform. Differentiation reconstructs the exact first two moments; inverse-local-time independent increments give the Gaussian limit. Strict global variance-minimizer uniqueness follows by factoring the derivative and proving a unique positive zero. Coercivity transfers uniqueness to convergence of all finite-budget global minimizers. The relative-variance coefficient has an analytically strict negative derivative. The executable checks independently verify local formulas numerically but do not replace these proofs.

**Originality — PASS (same-model, not priority certification).** The closest prior paper, Bressloff (arXiv:2203.04868), already gives the half-line position-only protocol and the exact mean (its Eq. (3.17)). Its general formulas imply a transform and make the present calculations feasible, so those ingredients are not separately claimed. The inspected original does not state the variance growth function, the unique unequal variance-versus-mean global minimizing rates, or the strictly monotone relative-variance coefficient. Benkhadaj–Grebenkov (arXiv:2209.00947) studies an encounter-reset framework on bounded intervals, but no assessed directly dominating half-line result was located. Pal et al. (arXiv:1902.00907) studies forward local time rather than its threshold inverse. An unlocated equivalent specialization remains a priority risk.

**Scientific value — PASS.** A deterministic accumulated boundary-contact budget is a natural reaction mechanism in encounter-based surface chemistry. Its first-passage mean is already known, whereas the precise reliability cost and its design optimum distinguish mean-optimal from variance-optimal reset rates by a universal, nontrivial factor. The strict relative-variance monotonicity exposes a third, operationally different optimization objective. The statement is exact rather than a numerical improvement at an arbitrary parameter slice.

**Limitations.** Only the one-dimensional half-line, fixed positive budget, and position-only Poisson resets are covered. No uniqueness is asserted for finite-budget minimizers. Priority was investigated through relevant literature and a research index, but independent literature and mathematical assessment are not complete.
