On a uniform random circle, the number of mutual nearest-neighbor pairs is negatively correlated at every nontrivial connection radius with the number of isolated vertices in the geometric graph. The exact covariance is \(-\frac n3[(1-2r)_+^{n-1}-2(1-3r)_+^{n-1}+(1-4r)_+^{n-1}]\) and tends to \(-e^{-x}/3\) at the rare-isolation radius \(r=(\log n+x)/(2n)\).
