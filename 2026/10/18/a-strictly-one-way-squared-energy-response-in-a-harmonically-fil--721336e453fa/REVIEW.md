**Correctness: PASS.** The two-sided stationary construction has finite fourth moments. Generator stationarity yields the initial quadratic and quartic moments. The three-dimensional forward covariance closure, autonomous reversed covariance, Laplace factorization and positive causal kernels prove strict directionality for every \(\beta,t>0\), including coincident rates. Exact differentiation supplies the unique integrated-optimal ratio. The packaged matrix/generator checker independently passed 622 numerical assertions, but no finite testing is substituted for analytic proofs.

General polynomial process theory may imply this calculation implicitly. Independent priority assessment remains necessary.

**Value: PASS.** The harmonic resetting particle is a motivated model of non-Gaussian active forcing. The observable compares how pre-existing forcing fluctuations predict future trap energy with the reverse ordering. Strict all-lag orientation is more informative than a one-time cumulant, and the sharp unique optimal reset/relaxation balance gives a dimensionless design diagnostic and a natural exact question beyond the source paper's one-time statistics.

**Limitations.** Requires stationarity, Brownian between-reset motion, complete Poisson resetting of the drive only, harmonic filtering and squared observables. Does not establish any general entropy-production formula or arbitrary-noise universality.
