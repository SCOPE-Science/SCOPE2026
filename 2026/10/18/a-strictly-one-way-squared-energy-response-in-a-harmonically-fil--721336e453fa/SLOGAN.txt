A harmonically confined particle driven by Poisson-reset Brownian noise has \(\operatorname{Cov}(X_t^2,Y_0^2)>\operatorname{Cov}(Y_t^2,X_0^2)\) for every positive lag and every positive reset-to-relaxation ratio. Its normalized integrated asymmetry has an exact unique optimum at \(\beta=(-7+3\sqrt{33})/31\), with sharp value \((10-\sqrt{33})/3\).
