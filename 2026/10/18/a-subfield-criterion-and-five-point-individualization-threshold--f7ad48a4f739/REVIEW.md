# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof recovers every projective coordinate from joins and meets of singleton vertices, gives explicit geometric addition and multiplication in all characteristics, and establishes the reverse containment using Frobenius fixed points. The lower-bound case analysis covers arbitrary sets of four marks (collinear triples and projective quadrangles); field generators give the upper bound of five. Finite enumerations cover selected small fields and the proper intermediate subfield of \(\mathbb F_{16}\). They corroborate, but do not certify, the general proof.

## Value — PASS
The result gives an exact infinite-family natural invariant (five point-only marks) and classifies all singleton vertices with arbitrary additional marks containing a projective quadrangle. The jump from projective prime to extension fields is explained by Frobenius stabilizers rather than a numerical coincidence, and the intermediate-subfield locus supplies a reusable structural description. It is not merely the general group-base lower bound, because constructive ordinary 1-WL sufficiency is proved.
