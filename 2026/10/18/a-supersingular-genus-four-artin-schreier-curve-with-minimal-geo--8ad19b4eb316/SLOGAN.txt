The genus-four curve \(y^3-y=x^5+x^4+x^2\) over \(\mathbb F_3\) has exactly the three deck automorphisms and a supersingular Jacobian, with an explicit degree-eight zeta numerator. It realizes the smallest possible genus in characteristic \(3\) for a supersingular Artin–Schreier curve whose entire geometric automorphism group is \(C_3\).
