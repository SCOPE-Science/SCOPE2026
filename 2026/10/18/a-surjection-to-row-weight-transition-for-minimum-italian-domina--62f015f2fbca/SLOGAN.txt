Minimum Italian domination on the rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\), for \(2\le m\le n\), has weight \(\min\{n,2m\}\). Every optimum is classified and counted: surjective one-per-column placements below \(n=2m\), independent row-weight-two placements above it, and an explicitly counted union of the two at the boundary.
