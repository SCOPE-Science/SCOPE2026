For the genus-\(3^s\) hyperelliptic curves \(y^2=t^{2\cdot3^s+2}-t\) in characteristic three, every Cartier-iterate rank is exactly \(2^j3^{s-j}\) up to \(j=s\) and \(2^{2s-j}\) up to \(j=2s\), followed by zero. A ternary-shift proof gives Jacobian \(3\)-rank zero, \(a\)-number \(3^{s-1}\), and trivial Mordell–Weil groups for the associated quasi-elliptic surfaces.
