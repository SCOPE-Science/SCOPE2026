For the difference between the \(s\)-th-power sums of divisors congruent to \(0\) and \(1\pmod3\), universal strict negativity holds exactly for \(s\le-1\). Above that threshold positive values precede negative ones infinitely often; unlike parity, the ternary problem admits opposite signs at the same \(3\)-adic valuation, while for \(-1<s<0\) its cumulative sum still has a strictly negative linear bias involving a Dirichlet \(L\)-value.
