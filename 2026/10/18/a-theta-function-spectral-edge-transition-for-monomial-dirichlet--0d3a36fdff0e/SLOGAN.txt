For monomial composition–differentiation on a weighted Dirichlet space, the high-Schatten power sums at the critical scale \(p_hh^2\to\tau\) retain a precise, nonconstant Jacobi-theta dependence on the fractional part of their moving spectral maximum. The adjacent diffuse regime \(p_h\to\infty\), \(p_hh^2\to0\) has an exact Gaussian mass equivalent, revealing a sharp discrete-to-continuous spectral edge.
