# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The rational-map coordinates on a dense chart are differentiated directly. Their Jacobian and the full homogeneous Hessian identify all source critical curves; the discriminant of the cubic critical-parameter equation proves three distinct nonzero roots. Explicit resultants prove that all critical images are smooth, noncontracted conics, while a separate elimination resultant gives the complete equation. The generic map degree follows from the cubic fiber equation and unique recovery of its source coordinate. Every nonstandard symbolic identity has an exact, executable independent verification routine. The theorem excludes exceptional parameters and makes no assertion about discriminants on a resolution.

## Originality
PASS under the stated source-search limits. The closest source, Beorchia--Miró-Roig, arXiv:2303.04665, Example 3.2(4) and Proposition 4.5, provides the same family and polar coordinates but identifies only a contracted line and does not give the other critical components, their image conics, or the explicit degree-six equation. Dimca, arXiv:1704.07196, proves freeness/topological properties; Di Gennaro et al., arXiv:2306.09443, develops pencil/eigenscheme methods. The compared statements do not imply this computed branch classification. Additional indexed comparisons include Hessian curves of unrelated cubic families and singularities of other conic arrangements, neither of which determines this result. The potential existence of uncatalogued or differently titled literature is a residual risk.

## Value
PASS. The critical-value divisor is a natural invariant of the degree-three polar map singled out in the source paper as deserving study. Its complete factorization into three smoothly embedded conics of a second bitangent pencil is a structural geometric constraint, valid on a nonempty two-parameter open family. The result gives an exact foundation for studying degeneration, branching, and monodromy rather than an isolated table lookup or a routine reparameterization.
