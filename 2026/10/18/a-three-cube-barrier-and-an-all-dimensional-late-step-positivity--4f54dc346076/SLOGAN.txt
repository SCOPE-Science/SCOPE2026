The two-stage fourth-order Gauss heat update is positive at every timestep on the one- and two-dimensional hypercubes, but on **every** hypercube of dimension at least three its nearest-neighbor entries turn negative for sufficiently large timesteps. On the first failing cube, \(Q_3\), positivity holds exactly through the unique positive root \(\tau\approx8.5837634637\) of \(h^4-6h^3-20h^2-18h-6=0\).
