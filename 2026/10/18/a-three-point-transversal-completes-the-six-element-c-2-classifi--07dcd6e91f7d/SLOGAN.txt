For structures carrying three named equivalence relations, two-variable counting logic first fails to identify nonisomorphic structures at six points: exactly eight ambiguous pairs occur among 21,205 isomorphism types, in four orbits under permutations of the relation names. In every pair, one member has no common transversal triple and the other has two, so a single natural three-variable existence sentence separates all 21,205 six-point types.
