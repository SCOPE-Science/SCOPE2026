For the cubic sequence \(f_n=(n+a)(n+b)(n+c)\) with integers \(0\le a\le b\le c\), every generalized binomial coefficient is integral exactly when \(f_1\) divides both \(f_2\) and \(f_3\). This finite test holds precisely for the three root-shift patterns \((0,0,0)\), \((0,0,1)\), and \((0,1,2)\), completing the nonpositive integer-root subclass of a published cubic classification problem.
