On the long-pentagon lattice \(L_m\), obtained by adjoining one incomparable atom to an \((m+2)\)-point chain, every fundamental \(\mathsf F\)-preconditional is determined by a negation threshold and independent fixed-point activation times. The exact number is \(m!+\sum_{j=1}^m(m-j+1)^j(m-j)!\), giving \(5,17,75,407\) for \(m=2,3,4,5\).
