In the symmetric doubling family, a two-function transfer mode relaxes exactly as \((-1/2)^n\) on \(4/3\le\alpha<3/2\), while the stationary digit frequencies stay fixed at \((1/6,2/3,1/6)\) and the complete adjacent-digit law varies with the parameter. The observed digit process is not first-order Markov until the classical nonadjacent endpoint \(\alpha=3/2\), and its one-step conditional entropy decreases strictly across this interval.
