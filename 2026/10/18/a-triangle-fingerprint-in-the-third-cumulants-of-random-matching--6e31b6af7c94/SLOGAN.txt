In a uniformly random perfect matching of a regularly edge-colored complete graph, the color-count means agree with a multinomial model and their covariance matrix is an exact scalar multiple of the multinomial covariance, independent of the edge-coloring arrangement. The third mixed cumulant for three distinct colors detects exactly the negative contribution of their rainbow-triangle count, and one affine coloring of \(K_8\) produces opposite signs for color triples that have identical one- and two-color statistics.
