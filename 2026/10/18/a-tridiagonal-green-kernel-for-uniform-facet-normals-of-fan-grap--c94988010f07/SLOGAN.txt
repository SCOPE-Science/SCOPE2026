For every fan graph with at least three path vertices, a uniform random facet normal of its real Lipschitz-free transportation ball has an explicitly factored covariance whose inverse is strictly tridiagonal. Its bulk correlations decay exactly at the algebraic rate \(\rho^{-r}\), where \(\rho\) is the positive root of \(\rho^3-\rho^2-2\rho-2=0\).
