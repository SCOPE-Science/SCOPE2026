For Catalan pellytopes with depth \(d=\lfloor\alpha n\rfloor\), the number of vertices has a sharp one-half threshold at the \(4^n/n^2\) scale: the normalized limit vanishes for \(0<\alpha\le1/2\) and equals \(16(2\alpha-1)/(\pi\sqrt{\alpha(1-\alpha)})\) for \(1/2<\alpha<1\). The constant comes from two macroscopic Catalan blocks within the published vertex generating function, with all shorter blocks contributing a summable geometric factor.
