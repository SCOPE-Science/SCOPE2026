For the rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) with \(m,n\ge2\), the proper open-neighborhood conflict-free (equivalently proper odd) chromatic number equals \(\max\{m,n\}\) on non-square boards but jumps to \(n+2\) exactly on an \(n\)-by-\(n\) board. A row/column palette criterion explains the obstruction and gives explicit optimal colorings for every size.
