Two-confluent transitive modal relations on a rooted \(k\)-fork admit a complete two-colour ordinal-sum normal form, with a single complete-relation exception. The root-irreflexive frames are counted by \(\sum_{j=0}^k\binom{k}{j}2^{k-j}t_j\), and the exact total frame counts for \(k=1,2,3,4\) are \(8,38,379,7182\).
