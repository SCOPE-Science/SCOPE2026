# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof establishes an exact characterization of the at-least-two-representation ideal as the union of two translates of the ordinary two-generated semigroup. It derives exact least thresholds in every residue class; the maximum, gap count, and gap sum then follow from arithmetic progressions. The Pell specialization uses coprimality and recurrence inequalities valid for every integer recurrence coefficient at least two and every index at least three. Independently programmed finite checks pass for 2,791 general parameter triples and seven Pell instances. Residual limitation: no result is asserted for higher representation thresholds.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence
The closest published formulas concern Jacobsthal polynomials with different generating coefficients (arXiv:2304.00443) and nonconsecutive Pell-type terms (arXiv:2307.08998). Their general Apéry formulas require input thresholds and do not themselves supply the two-layer one-Apéry description for a redundant third generator. The present statement distinguishes counting parameter triples from ordinary semigroup representability and proves an explicit two-certificate lemma. Targeted literature and mathematical-record searches located no identical formula; an unindexed or implicit earlier application of standard p-Apéry machinery remains a risk.
