A redundant third denomination \(a+cb\) changes the one-representation Frobenius problem in a completely computable way: for coprime \(2\le a<b\) and \(1\le c<a\), the one-Frobenius number is \(ab-a+(c-1)b\), and the complete one-Apéry set has two explicit residue layers. The resulting formulas determine the one-genus and one-Sylvester sum, including all consecutive Pell-type triples with recurrence coefficient at least two.
