The standard star cosmological polytope has an exact two-orbit Gaussian normal-cone law: its \(3m\) vertices have probabilities \(q_m/(2m)\) and \((1-q_m)/m\), with \(q_m\to1/2\) and collision probability asymptotic to \(3/(8m)\). Its intrinsic directional width has a non-Gumbel residual law: \(\sqrt{2m}W_m-4\sqrt{\log m}\) converges to \(2|Z|\), where \(Z\) is standard normal.
