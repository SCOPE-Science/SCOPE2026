For every infinite sequence of circle points, \(\limsup_{N\to\infty}R_N(a)\ge\max\{0,4-2\sqrt2-1/a\}\) for \(0<a<1\); thus no limiting pair-correlation function can vanish at \(a>(2+\sqrt2)/4\) below unit scale. A universal inequality comparing two nested prefixes supplies the obstruction even though the published one-prefix pointwise lower bound is zero throughout that interval.
