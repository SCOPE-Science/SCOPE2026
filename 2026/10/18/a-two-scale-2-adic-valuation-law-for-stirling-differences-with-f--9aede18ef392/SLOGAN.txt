For the first Mersenne block count not covered by the earlier general Stirling-difference formulas, fifteen blocks, the \(2\)-adic valuation has two competing exact scales: \(n+1+\nu_2(a-b)\) and \(b2^n-5\), with the smaller governing except at equality. On that resonance locus the valuation is at least one unit larger, and every fixed pair \(a>b\ge1\) obeys the first scale for all sufficiently large \(n\ge10\).
