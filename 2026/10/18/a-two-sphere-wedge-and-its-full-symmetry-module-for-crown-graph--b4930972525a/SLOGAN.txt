For every \(n\ge4\), the complex of vertex sets inducing components of size at most two in the crown graph \(K_{n,n}\setminus M\) has homotopy type \(\bigvee^{\binom{n-1}{2}}S^2\). Its complete integral \(S_n\times C_2\)-homology action is the complete-graph cycle lattice, with a minus sign under bipartition exchange, and its rational character is explicit.
