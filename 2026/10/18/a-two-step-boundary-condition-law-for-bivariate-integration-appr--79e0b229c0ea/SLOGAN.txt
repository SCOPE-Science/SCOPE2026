For the tensor-square Volterra integration operator and its versions with one or both coordinates pinned at both endpoints, the \(k\)-th optimal Hilbert-space approximation error equals \(W(e^{B_r}k)/(\pi^2k)+O(\sqrt{\log k}/k^{3/2})\), with \(B_r=2\gamma+(4-2r)\log2-1\). Each extra pinned coordinate changes the sharp constant-order rank-scaled error by precisely \(2\log2\), despite all three models sharing the same leading \((\log k)/(\pi^2k)\) asymptotic.
