The two-step specialization \(H_{n,k,2k}(q)\) of the generalized q-central-binomial rational function is a polynomial with nonnegative coefficients exactly when \(n\) is odd or \(k\) is even; otherwise it has a simple pole at \(q=-1\). The exact factorization gives infinitely many counterexamples to the proposed necessity of \(\gcd(n,j)\mid k\), including every even \(k\) with \(n=j=2k\).
