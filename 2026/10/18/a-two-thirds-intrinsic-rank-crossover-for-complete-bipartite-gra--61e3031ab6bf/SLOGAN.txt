For the standard graphical zonotope of \(K_{2,q}\), the intrinsic-volume Gram weights shift the two-hub forest-connectivity threshold from \(k\asymp\sqrt q\) under uniform forests to \(k\asymp q^{2/3}\). The exact Gram-weighted critical connection profile is \(t^{3/2}/(2+t^{3/2})\), versus \(s^2/(4+s^2)\) for uniform forests.
