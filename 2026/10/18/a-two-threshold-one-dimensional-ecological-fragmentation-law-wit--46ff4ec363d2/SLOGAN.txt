On a one-dimensional ecological ring, stronger competition produces a sharp loss of surviving sites at \(\alpha=2\), while the critical coupling simultaneously extinguishes macroscopic biomass oscillations without changing occupancy. The two distinct survival regimes have different exact mean densities, \(3/8\) and \(1/8\), but the same fluctuation variance \(7n/64\) on every ring of size \(n\ge5\), with Gaussian large-ring limits.
