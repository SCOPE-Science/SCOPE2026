The proof derives the all-size covariance by a Bernoulli-tagged block-count recursion, exchangeability for earlier samples, and an exact two-future-customer predictive moment for later samples. The crucial cancellation in the latter is reproduced directly in ## Proof of RESULT.md. `verify.py` independently enumerates finite partition laws with rational probabilities and checks each of the two time orderings.

Finite enumeration does not establish the identities for arbitrary sample size. Gamma-ratio asymptotics follow from the analytic product and are not asserted on the basis of numerical evidence. The self-review is recorded in REVIEW.md and AUDIT.json; no independent audit has been carried out.
