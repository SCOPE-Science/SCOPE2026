Every binary de Bruijn cycle of order \(n\ge3\) has a cyclic subsequence-universality throughput equal to either \(\lfloor2^n/3\rfloor\) or \(\lceil2^n/3\rceil\), decided solely by whether its two alternating length-\(n\) factors overlap and by the parity of \(n\). Across all \(2^n\) cyclic cuts, the exact histogram of scattered-subsequence universality indices depends on no other feature of the cycle.
