For Kollár's characteristic-two wild cuspidal curves, the previously known three infinitesimal projective symmetry directions integrate into an explicit connected finite subgroup \((\alpha_q)^2\rtimes\mu_q\) of length \(q^3\). Adjoining all reduced geometric symmetries gives a concrete semidirect subgroup scheme of length \(q^3(q+1)\), a higher-order statement not implied by the earlier tangent-space calculation.
