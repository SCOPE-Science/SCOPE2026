In symmetric Leslie–Gower competition, the squared population-imbalance odds obey a uniform all-time logarithmic drift with first-order coefficient \(\varepsilon(a-1)/a\). Near equal intra- and interspecific competition, a vanishing initial asymmetry survives for \(2\gamma a\log(1/|\varepsilon|)/[(a-1)|\varepsilon|]\) steps before an explicit sigmoid exclusion transition; the reverse perturbation has an equally sharp logarithmic mixing delay.
