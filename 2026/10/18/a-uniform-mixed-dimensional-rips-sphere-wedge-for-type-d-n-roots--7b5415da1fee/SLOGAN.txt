For every \(n\ge5\), the inclusive Euclidean Rips complex of the type-\(D_n\) roots at scales \(\sqrt2\le r<2\) is a mixed-dimensional wedge \(\bigvee^{8\binom n3-2n(n-1)+2n-1}S^2\vee\bigvee^{2n}S^{n-2}\). Signed-coordinate star spheres and the two-skeleton of a crosspolytope explain the two independent homology degrees.
