For every \(a\ge7\), the \(p\)-representation semigroup of three consecutive denominations with \(p=\lfloor a/2\rfloor+5\) has least positive element exactly \((a+2)^2-2\), yet fails maximal embedding dimension. A uniform exact denumerant calculation produces positive members whose shifted sum is a gap, beyond the previously established uniform \(p\)-Apéry range.
