In a uniform random stick-breaking experiment, the longest gap and the count of gaps exceeding \(t\) have **negative** covariance at small thresholds and **positive** covariance at large thresholds, with exactly one interior sign change for every number of gaps. The transition is strictly between \(1/m\) and \(1/2\) for \(m\ge3\), and an explicit piecewise-polynomial formula determines the covariance exactly.
