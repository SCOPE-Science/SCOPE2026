In a two-bin occupancy model with \(L\ge2\) potential objects independently retained with probability \(\theta\), the covariance between the number of vacant bins and the maximum load changes sign exactly once, from negative to positive. Its unique transition satisfies \(\theta_L\sim (8/\pi)^{1/3}L^{-1/3}\), revealing a sharp cube-root boundary between sampling-rate and clustering effects.
