For Gaussian cold-state resetting of a harmonic Brownian particle, both periodic and Poisson protocols have a unique optimal symmetrized endpoint-KL rate at every finite reset width. When \(a=T/T_c-1\) grows, their optimal rates scale as \(\lambda\sqrt{a/2}\) and \(\lambda\sqrt a\), respectively, and the optimized periodic-minus-Poisson KL-rate gap is \(\lambda(1-1/\sqrt2)\sqrt a+o(\sqrt a)\); this is not a theorem about full implementation-dependent entropy production.
