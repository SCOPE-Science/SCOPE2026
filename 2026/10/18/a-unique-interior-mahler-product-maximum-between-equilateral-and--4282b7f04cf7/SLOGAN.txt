In a four-point metric with unit square edges and common diagonal length \(t\in[1,2]\), the real Lipschitz-free unit ball has volume product \(\mathcal P(t)=8(t+4)[4-(2-t)^3]/(9t)\). Its unique maximum occurs at \(t\approx1.12191578\), strictly above the equilateral case, before falling to the equal-edge four-cycle minimum.
