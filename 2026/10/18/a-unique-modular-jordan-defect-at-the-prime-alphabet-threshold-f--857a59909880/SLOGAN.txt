At the first modular collision, when the alphabet has exactly \(p\) letters over characteristic \(p\), the uniform single-letter operator on the entire free left-regular-band algebra has exactly one nonsimple Jordan block, of size two at eigenvalue zero. Its entire Jordan spectrum follows from the rank-one identity \(L_x^p-L_x=L_{\sum_{w\in S_p}w}\) and the characteristic-zero multiplicities.
