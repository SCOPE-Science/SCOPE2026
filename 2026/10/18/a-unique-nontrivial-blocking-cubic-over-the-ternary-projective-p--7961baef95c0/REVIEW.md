# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The proof partitions all blocking cubics by the exact thirteen-point incidence classification, interpolates the complete 40-form linear system through a six-point vertexless triangle, and excludes all extra rational zeros by a four-collinear-point argument. It leaves precisely four equations on each of 234 triangles. The characteristic-three gradient identities then exclude all geometric singular points without relying on finite testing. A separate executable census verifies the 936 count and detects rational line factors across all 29524 projective cubic equations. The proof covers all nonzero cubics up to scalar, including reducible alternatives.

## Originality — PASS, with documented search limitations
The 2022/2023 blocking-curve source gives one example at field size three but no uniqueness or 936-equation classification; the 2022 minimal blocking-set classification concerns point subsets rather than cubic equations. The 2023 tangent-filling work addresses a different incidence property. Targeted database and literature queries found no result giving the particular projective orbit, its exact equation count, and stabilizer. A full earlier equivalence search is impossible to guarantee; one potentially relevant full text was not accessible. The known point-set classification is an input, not the new cubic-equation classification.

## Value — PASS
The first odd prime field is an exceptional case of the source's sharp cubic nonblocking theorem. This gives the complete irreducible and smooth classification rather than an isolated example, with an intrinsic incidence proof, a global exact count, and a full rational projective stabilizer. The natural object and invariant are specified before any finite count; no parameter slice or arbitrary cutoff is involved.
