For classical RK4 followed by a one-invariant quasi-orthogonal energy projection, two independent oscillators have exactly one positive timestep \(h_*\) at which the resulting four-dimensional map becomes symplectic and preserves each oscillator's energy. Every other step drives the conserved total energy exponentially toward the lower-frequency or higher-frequency mode, reversing direction at \(h_*\) strictly inside RK4's usual stable stepsize range.
