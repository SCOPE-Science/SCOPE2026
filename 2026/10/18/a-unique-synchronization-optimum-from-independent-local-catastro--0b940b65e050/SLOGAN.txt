In a two-site migration model with independent whole-site Poisson clearings, the stationary occupancies develop strictly positive cross covariance and strict single-site overdispersion, with the universal amplitude ratio \(\operatorname{Cov}(N_1,N_2)/(\operatorname{Var}(N_1)-\mathbb E N_1)=h/(d+r+h)\). The induced cross covariance is maximized at exactly one clearing rate, which always lies below the per-particle death rate.
