Along an invariant chaotic diagonal curve of a two-node matrix logistic map, the infinitesimal off-diagonal logarithmic growth has the exact Lyapunov exponent \(\log(b/2)\) and a Gaussian fluctuation law with a computable Fourier-series variance. In the entire stable-channel range \(1<b<\sqrt3\), those fluctuations cancel **only** at \(b=\sqrt2\), while at \(b=2/\sqrt3\) their variance is exactly \(\pi^2/3\).
