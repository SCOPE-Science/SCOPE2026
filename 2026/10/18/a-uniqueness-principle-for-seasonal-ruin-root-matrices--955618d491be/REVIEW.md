# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The theorem specifies every hypothesis and reproduces the root-cancellation matrix from the unit-premium seasonal model. The proof checks the formal determinant, analytic root cancellation, exact reflected transition at the boundary, and uniqueness of finite signed invariant measures. The standalone exact-rational example confirms an illustrative case, not the general proof.

## Originality
PASS on accessible evidence. The 2022 primary MSC 60G50 manuscript derives the root linear system but does not prove nonsingularity for arbitrary season count. The 2024 open-access extension explicitly calls this determinant claim Conjecture 1, establishing a Vandermonde formula only for one season. published-finding corpus checks for the source, the root-matrix aliases, reflected-chain reductions, and stronger matrix results produced unrelated closest items, not a directly dominating theorem. Search cannot exclude all unindexed later literature; that is the principal residual originality risk.

## Value
PASS. The result resolves a motivated uniqueness gap for a natural exponential-moment subclass and arbitrary number of seasons, directly enabling unique initial probabilities in seasonal ruin calculations. The Markov signed-measure proof explains a structural mechanism beyond finite tests.

## Scope and limitations
The proof does not cover general positive premium, arbitrary heavy tails, multiple characteristic zeros, or zero zero-claim probabilities. It does not supply explicit numerical survival probabilities. Independent mathematical auditing is still necessary before broader claims of validation.
