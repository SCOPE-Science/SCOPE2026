For cubical fertilitopes built from every internal node of a full binary tree, the Gaussian vertex-collision probability is either exactly \(2^{-(n-1)}\) or at least \(\tfrac{10}{9}2^{-(n-1)}\). The gap is attained precisely by a root joining perfect subtrees with leaf counts in ratio two, giving sharp infinitely many-dimensional equality and a six-leaf case where an unequal root split beats an equal one.
