The John ellipsoid of the real Lipschitz unit ball on a wheel with rim lengths one and uniform spoke length \(t\ge1/2\) changes contact type at the universal threshold \(t=1/\sqrt2\). Its exact volume is \(C^1\) but not \(C^2\) there, with a second-log-volume derivative jump of \(-8m\) for every rim count \(m\ge3\).
