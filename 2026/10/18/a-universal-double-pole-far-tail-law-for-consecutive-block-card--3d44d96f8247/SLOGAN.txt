The fixed-size far tail of the successive-card-block shuffling process has a universal repeated spectral mode: for every \(n\ge3\), survival is \(2^{-t}(\alpha_n t+\beta_n)+O_n((11/24)^t)\) with \(\alpha_n>0\) and explicit rational recursions. The absorption-time generating function consequently has a genuine double pole at \(z=2\), unlike the simple pole for two starting blocks.
