For an unoversampled real Gaussian Nyström approximation to a positive-semidefinite matrix with one vanishing eigenvalue \(\varepsilon\), the rank-one residual has an exact spherical harmonic-mean law and its positive moments switch sharply at order \(1/2\): \(\varepsilon^s\), \(\sqrt\varepsilon\log(1/\varepsilon)\), then \(\sqrt\varepsilon\). In particular, the mean spectral/trace/Frobenius error is order \(\sqrt\varepsilon\) against an optimal rank-constrained error of \(\varepsilon\), with a precise rare-event coefficient.
