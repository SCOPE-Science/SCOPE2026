# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The unique finite-second-moment stationary process is a contracting past stochastic convolution under the ergodic on/off environment. Generator balance yields \(q_1=D/k\) and \(q_0=D/k+D/s\). The finite-state Feynman–Kac matrix propagates the impulse and two-time covariance, and its stationary-moment cancellation proves exactly \(-C'/D=\phi_0\). The scalar difference equation has two distinct positive rates, establishing the explicit strictly positive gap for every \(t>0\), the integral and onset identities, and the unique global maximum. Exact symbolic and 65-digit matrix-exponential tests reproduce all essential identities. The finite tests are corroborative, not substitutes for proof.

## Originality — PASS with priority risk

The accessible primary full texts of Santra–Das–Nath (arXiv:2104.00609), Alston–Cocconi–Bertrand (arXiv:2202.02630), and Frydel (arXiv:2407.12213) treat the same intermittently switched stiffness but focus respectively on one-time variance/density/first passage, steady entropy production, and stationary Gaussian mixtures/moments. Their formulas underlie the model and cannot be appropriated as new. The fluctuation-response sum-rule study of Di Terlizzi–Baiesi–Ritort (arXiv:2403.10442) studies a different switching-trap example: *trap-center* telegraph motion at fixed stiffness, rather than *stiffness* switching to zero. The assessed result is the exact sign-resolved two-time response discrepancy with universal integrated clock, kinetic reconstruction, and unique maximum. General nonequilibrium response identities could yield it after a specialized computation; a broader unlocated theorem remains a priority risk. No independent priority assessment was performed.

## Scientific value — PASS

Intermittently trapped particles are experimentally motivated by switched optical tweezers and realistic resetting. A strict universal sign in a calibrated response-correlation diagnostic is not implied by stationary variances or entropy-production rates. Moreover, its integral recovers the switching residence time without knowing the trap strength or diffusivity; the initial slope separately recovers trap strength, and the exact maximizing lag locates the strongest measurable departure from equilibrium. These are physically interpretable nontrivial consequences, not parameter substitutions or routine normalization checks.

## Limitations and near coverage

The law assumes symmetric exponential switching, harmonic confinement, independent additive thermal noise, an unperturbed trap-state clock, and a stationary ensemble. It does not cover semi-Markovian switching or a force acting on the trap state. The 2026 work *Semi-Markovian switching in a fluctuating harmonic trap* (arXiv:2607.05173) was identified through its abstract and outline; a complete manuscript inspection was not available in this review, so implicit coverage there remains an additional limited risk. The general response literature may also contain an equivalent specialized result.
