For a uniformly chosen parameter in the contracted rotation \(x\mapsto\{\lambda x+\delta\}\), the reduced rational winding fraction becomes asymptotically uniform and independent of the least attracting period scaled by \(-\log\lambda\) as \(\lambda\uparrow1\). The scaled period converges to the non-exponential density \(6t/[\pi^2(e^t-1)]\), with exact limiting moments determined by zeta values.
