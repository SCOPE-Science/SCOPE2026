For every dimension \(d\ge3\), the optimal worst relative loss from using one boundary sampling density for all intrinsic-volume errors has an exact simplex dual and, near the Euclidean ball, equals \(1+\operatorname{Var}(\partial_t\log\kappa_t|_0)t^2/[4(d+1)]+o(t^2)\). To quadratic order, only the volume and mean-width extremes matter; for an axial spheroid the sharp coefficient is \((d^2-1)/[2d^2(d+2)]\).
