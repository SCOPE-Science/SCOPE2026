For a clique bouquet with uniformly bounded peripheral clique sizes and \(S\) peripheral vertices, a uniformly random proper coloring from \(q\) named colors has a universal proper conflict-free transition when \(S/(q-1)-\log S\to c\): its success probability tends to \(1-\exp(-e^{-c})\), regardless of the proportions of clique sizes. The key is a Poisson limit for colors appearing exactly once at the shared hub.
