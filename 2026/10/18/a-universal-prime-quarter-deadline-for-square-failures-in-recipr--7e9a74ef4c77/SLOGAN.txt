For every positive integer \(h\), all reciprocal-sum sequences with parameter \(h^2\) have only perfect-square normalized terms beyond an explicit index determined by primes dividing \(h\): a prime \(p\equiv1\pmod4\) cannot cause a failure after \((p+7)/4\), and the prime \(2\) cannot cause one after index \(2\). A fourfold symmetry of the finite-field map \(z\mapsto z+z^{-1}\) proves the cutoff, which is attained for \(h=x_0=5\).
