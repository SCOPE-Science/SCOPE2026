# Scientific review: projective harmonic interpolation on nine-line SICs

## Correctness — PASS
The normalized addition theorem for \(\mathcal H_{q,q}(\mathbb C^3)\) gives diagonal evaluation kernel \((q+1)^3\) and the same off-diagonal ratio \(a_q=P_q^{(1,0)}(-1/2)/(q+1)\) at each pair. Diagonalizing \((1-a_q)I_9+a_qJ_9\) verifies the two eigenvalue multiplicities and the mean-zero range exactly. The Jacobi–Legendre identity follows by weight-\((1-x)\) orthogonality and the endpoint value; the rational recurrence gives exact finite data, and the classical integral bound \(|P_n(x)|\le1\) controls every uncomputed degree. The threshold \(q=20\) and exact inequalities \(4/63<31/473<31/256\) give the required infinite proof. The rational checker reproduces \(q=0,\ldots,19\), the unique maximum \(a_3=31/256\), and the minimum \(a_5=-47/1024\); its independent numerical SIC geometry checks are confirmatory, not an infinite proof. No claim is made that arbitrary phase-sensitive bidegrees are covered.

## Originality — PASS subject to residual literature risk
Renes–Blume-Kohout–Scott–Caves (2003) establish nine-outcome SIC constructions; Roy–Suda (2011) already provide the complex harmonic addition theorem, and the corresponding design vanishings at the two low bidegrees are not claimed as new. Tabia–Appleby (2013) discuss families of qutrit SICs. Inspected source sections did not identify the simultaneous **complete degree-wise surjectivity classification for all qutrit SICs** and **exact uniquely sharp worst-degree squared condition number**. The latter is a global optimization over all integers \(q\ge3\), not a generic consequence from naming the kernel or computing one finite level. The nearby charged-Hesse-line result for \(\mathcal H_{q+3,q}\) uses a different projective transformation law and different Jacobi polynomial parameters; it does not imply the phase-invariant claim. The existing design and Jacobi generalities might contain an unstated equivalent, and fuller literature checks and independent priority assessment remain legitimate risks; unsuccessful searches do not prove novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Nine-line SICs are canonical maximally equiangular configurations in projective signal analysis. A complete all-degree invertibility classification is an intrinsic interpolation invariant and identifies the exact first universally invertible degree. The sharp ratio \(56/25\), its unique bottleneck at \(q=3\), the two exceptional mean-zero spaces, and explicit minimum-norm kernel reconstructions turn this into a quantitative uniform spectral theorem for all SIC geometries in dimension three. The worst-degree statement needs a nontrivial finite-to-infinite cutoff rather than a one-off elementary evaluation. These objects and precise invariants are motivated independently of the computing method.
