Every nine-line qutrit SIC has the same projective-harmonic sampling spectrum: bidegrees \((1,1)\) and \((2,2)\) miss exactly the mean, but every \((q,q)\) with \(q\ge3\) interpolates arbitrary nine-point data. Across all such degrees and all qutrit SICs, the **sharp worst squared sampling condition number is \(56/25\)**, attained uniquely at \(q=3\).
