Every holomorphic hypersurface singularity germ vanishing at its isolated critical point has an exact Laurent-coefficient formula for the Tjurina numbers of all iterated separated self-sums. Unless it is quasihomogeneous, the normalized Tjurina number decays as \(\sqrt{2/(\pi\sigma_h^2 r)}\), where \(\sigma_h^2\) is an explicit cubic moment of the Milnor-multiplication Jordan blocks.
