For every nontrivial compound-Poisson active bath, a stationary harmonically confined Brownian particle has a nonexchangeable two-time position distribution at every positive lag, even if jump moments diverge. With finite fourth moments, the universal signed fourth-order asymmetry is \(-\rho\mathbb E[Y^4]e^{-\kappa t}(1-e^{-2\kappa t})/(4\kappa)\), peaks at \(t=\log3/(2\kappa)\), and its ratio across two lags identifies the trap relaxation rate.
