For the fixed two-memory iteration \(e_{k+1}=A[(1-q)e_k+qe_{k-1}]\) on arbitrary finite complex matrices, the unique convex weight whose asymptotic stability region contains every other fixed-weight region is \(q=1/3\). Its exact scalar region satisfies \(|a|^2<9\) and \(|a|^4+(18+8\operatorname{Re}a)|a|^2<27\), with sharp real stability interval \((-3,1)\).
