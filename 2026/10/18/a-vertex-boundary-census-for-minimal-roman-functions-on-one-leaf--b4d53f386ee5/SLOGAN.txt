Pointwise-minimal Roman dominating functions of every one-leaf corona are enumerated exactly by the external-vertex-boundary polynomial of its base graph. On sunlets \(C_n\circ K_1\), the complete weight census has a cubic rational generating function, its total is \(1+(1+\sqrt2)^n+(1-\sqrt2)^n\), and the number of minimum-weight functions is exactly \(3\), \(n(n+17)/6\), or \(n\) according as \(n\) is \(0\), \(1\), or \(2\) modulo three.
