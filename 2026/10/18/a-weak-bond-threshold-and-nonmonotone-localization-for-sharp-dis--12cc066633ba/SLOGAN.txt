Weakening one edge of the integer lattice changes the sharp multiplicative discrete Sobolev constant from the unattained value \(1\) to an explicitly computable attained value above \(1\); strengthening that edge leaves the constant at \(1\) without extremizers. The geometric localization of the weak-bond extremizers is strongest at the intermediate conductance \((4-\sqrt2)/7\), not at the weakest bond.
