For uniform coupon collectors whose sibling indices grow as \(\alpha\log N\) with \(0<\alpha<1\), the normalized number of missing types converges to \(W^{1-\alpha}/(1-\alpha)\), where \(W\) is standard exponential, and the normalized mean tends to \(\Gamma(1-\alpha)\). Multiple growing sibling indices share this same random amplitude but exhibit a nonlinear rather than perfectly exponential joint profile.
