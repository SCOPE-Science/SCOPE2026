Every linked symmetric snake-path delta-matroid polytope has an exact Euclidean inradius determined by maximizing a square-root-weighted endpoint barrier over maximal constant-orientation runs. Across all words of length \(n-1\), the radius ranges sharply from \(1/(n+\sqrt n)\) to \(1/(1+\sqrt n+\sqrt{n-1})\); even unimodularly equivalent snake polytopes can have different radii.
