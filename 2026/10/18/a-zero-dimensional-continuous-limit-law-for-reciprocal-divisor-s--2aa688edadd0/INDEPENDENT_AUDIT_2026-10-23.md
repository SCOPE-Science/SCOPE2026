# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Fix q>=2 prime power and n->infinity. Unique-factorization probabilities establish joint independent geometric valuations of finitely many monic irreducibles; a uniform logarithmic-tail expectation bounded by sum_d pi_q(d)q^(-2d)/(1-q^(-d)) establishes weak convergence to the finite positive Euler product. Bernoulli(2p) x fair-coin thinning (p=q^(-deg P)<=1/2), with positive weights and Sperner antichains, gives a max atom of O(M_D^(-1/2)) with M_D tending to infinity (mean 2 log D+O(1)); convolution with the independent tail preserves atomlessness. Chernoff bounds K_D=sum_{degP<=D}V_P<=L log D eventually, plus O(exp(C(log D)^2)) possible degree/exponent histogram values and a summably tiny q^(-D/2) tail, give countable liminf covers of a full-measure set of Hausdorff dimension zero. The Euler product local moments give EZ=1/(1-q^-1), EZ^2=(1-q^-3)/((1-q^-1)^2(1-q^-2)). I directly checked the geometric local second moments for q=2,3,5 with exact fractions; these checks are local and do not prove weak convergence or dimension zero. The result explicitly does not assert support dimension zero.

## originality

PASS

The analytic existence of limiting additive-function laws predates this result (Darbar–Mukhopadhyay, Mathematika 2024). The present claimed surplus is the simultaneous non-atomic and zero-Hausdorff-dimension full-measure carrier classification for the reciprocal polynomial-divisor sum, plus exact moments. The published multiplicative-function limit theorem and recent restricted divisor-function statistics do not visibly state or imply this stronger geometric classification. General infinite-convolution pure-type theorems might impose constraints but do not by themselves supply this exact dimension-zero conclusion; priority is best-of-knowledge, not absolute.

## value

PASS

An explicit, highly singular yet atomless distribution arising naturally from random monic-polynomial divisor sums is a meaningful arithmetic/probabilistic phenomenon; the zero-dimensional carrier resolves fine regularity not supplied by weak convergence alone. Exact moments identify the law quantitatively. It is not a routine renaming of general weak-limit existence.
