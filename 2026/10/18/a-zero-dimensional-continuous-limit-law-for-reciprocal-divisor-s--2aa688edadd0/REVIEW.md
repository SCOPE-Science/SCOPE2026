# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** Finite-place valuation convergence is proved from polynomial divisibility and the Chinese remainder theorem. The large-degree tail is uniformly integrable on logarithmic scale because irreducible degree counts turn it into a convergent geometric series. Conditioning on independent fair Bernoulli activations and using the Sperner antichain bound establishes absence of atoms without assuming distinct factor weights. A Chernoff bound on the total small-prime multiplicity and a degree–exponent histogram count produce a Borel carrier of Hausdorff dimension zero. Local Euler-factor identities establish the stated moments. Exact finite-degree checks of the two generating functions succeed; they are not used as infinite proofs.

**Originality — PASS, subject to literature risk.** The 2026 study of restricted polynomial divisor moments does not characterize this reciprocal-divisor random variable's limiting measure. The 2019/2024 Darbar–Mukhopadhyay distribution theorems give general existence of limits of additive functions; this may include the log reciprocal-divisor sum, so weak convergence alone is expressly not claimed original. Their full available expositions do not state the non-atomic dimension-zero classification, its degree-histogram covering proof, or the specific moment description. Targeted mathematical-result and literature searches located no stronger theorem implying the complete geometric classification. The residual risk is a general infinite-convolution theorem or differently indexed distribution work that already implies the geometry.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The problem asks for the precise type of the limit law attached to a natural weighted divisor function in the same function-field setting as current restricted-divisor statistics. Distinguishing a continuous law concentrated on a Hausdorff-dimension-zero set from either a discrete or absolutely continuous limit is a substantive structural phenomenon. The proof gives explicit, interpretable mechanisms (Sperner anti-concentration and degree-histogram entropy compression) for that sharp classification, for every fixed finite field size.

**Limits.** The theorem does not describe the closed topological support, give a convergence rate, or assert a large-field limit. Literature novelty remains subject to future external verification. Computations cover only specified finite-degree cases and do not certify the infinitary proof.
