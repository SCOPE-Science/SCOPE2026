# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For fixed integer c>=2, alpha=(c+sqrt(c^2+4))/2, F_j generalized Fibonacci numbers, reduce membership N in Beatty row floor(k alpha^j) to x_N={N alpha} in a periodic union of F_j circle arcs of measure alpha^-j. Equidistribution of irrational rotation for finite truncations and uniform row-count tail bound sum_{j>M}alpha^-j prove that each multiplicity has a natural density equal to its circle measure. Union bound gives d0>=1-1/(alpha-1)>0 and d1>=(alpha-2)/(alpha(alpha-1))>0. For v>=2 the Binet/generalized Lucas witness L_m with odd m=4v-5 belongs exactly to rows i odd <=(m-1)/2 and row m, yielding v memberships. A positive-width circle neighborhood of x_{L_m} has fixed low-row membership by quadratic-irrational Diophantine separation; periodic arc intersection and geometric tail bounds eliminate all higher rows on a positive-measure subset. Contrast c=1 zero absence follows complementary Beatty sequences. Independent integer-exact checks confirmed the rows for c=2,3,4, m=3,7,11; finite checks are not the density proof.

## originality

PASS

Ashburn's October 2026 arXiv result proves all positive occurrence multiplicities for powers of golden ratio (c=1). Metallic-mean Fibonacci Quarterly 2023 paper treats generalized Lucas numbers and their membership in selected metallic Beatty sequences. Neither establishes positive natural density of every multiplicity including zero for all integer c>=2. The new quantitative zero-frequency lower bound and controlled neighborhood argument supply a genuinely different parameter regime and conclusion; possible implicit extension remains risk.

## value

PASS

A strict transition at metallic parameter c=2 from golden complementarity to positive-density uncovered integers, together with the entire countable multiplicity spectrum at positive density, is a natural and motivated number-theoretic classification. Explicit witnesses alone would be insufficient; the positive-density reinforcement makes the phenomenon useful.
