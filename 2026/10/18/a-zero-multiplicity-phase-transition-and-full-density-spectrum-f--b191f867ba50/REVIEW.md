# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
An exact one-dimensional orbit coding transforms each row into a finite union of periodic arcs. Uniform control of the omitted tail proves existence of each natural density. The generalized Lucas witnesses realize every multiplicity at least two, and a quantified quadratic-irrationality bound together with periodic arc estimates shows the corresponding exact multiplicity has positive measure. The zero and one levels have explicit positive lower bounds. Exact finite arithmetic checks reproduced the witness identities.

It supplies explicit strictly positive zero- and one-count density bounds.
