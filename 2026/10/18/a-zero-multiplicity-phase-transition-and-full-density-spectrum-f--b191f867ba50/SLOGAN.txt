For every integer metallic mean \(\alpha=(c+\sqrt{c^2+4})/2\) with \(c\ge2\), **every** nonnegative row-occurrence multiplicity in the Beatty array \(\lfloor k\alpha^j\rfloor\) occurs with positive natural density; in particular a positive density of integers occur in no row. This creates a sharp uncovered-integer phase contrast with the golden-ratio array, in which the first two rows cover all positive integers.
