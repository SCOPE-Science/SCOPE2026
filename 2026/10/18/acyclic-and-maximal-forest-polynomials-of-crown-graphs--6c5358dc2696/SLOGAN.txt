For the crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M_n\), exact bivariate polynomials count all induced forests and all inclusion-maximal ones, whose sizes for \(n\ge4\) lie in \(\{5,n+1\}\). Its induced-forest complex is pure exactly for \(n=3,4\), while for \(n\ge6\) its maximal-face size spectrum has \(n-5\) consecutive internal gaps.
