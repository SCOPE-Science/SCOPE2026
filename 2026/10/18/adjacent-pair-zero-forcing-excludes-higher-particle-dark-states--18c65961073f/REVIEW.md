A nonempty set of spectator particles breaks the cyclic two-outward-neighbor recurrence; the local move classification was checked algebraically and against 7,633 exact finite boundary equations. Search comparisons checked aliases in particle loss, spectral unique continuation and token graph zero forcing; no dominating theorem was identified among the materials inspected. A missed publication could still contain this fact.

**Value:** PASS. This closes an explicitly numerical higher-particle classification step in a canonical open quantum reaction-diffusion model, providing a length-independent analytic boundary and a structural unique-continuation mechanism robust to diagonal disorder, flux phases, and nonuniform nonzero hopping. The two-particle exception clarifies the mechanism's sharpness.

**Limits:** The theorem concerns pure dark Hamiltonian eigenstates, not all mixed steady states or dynamics. No conclusion is claimed when loss bonds or hopping edges are removed or new hopping types added.
