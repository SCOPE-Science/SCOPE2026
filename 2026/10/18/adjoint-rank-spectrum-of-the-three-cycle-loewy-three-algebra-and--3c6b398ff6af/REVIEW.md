# Scientific review

## Correctness
The proof builds the commutator matrix from the path basis and splits all elements according to diagonal-value coincidence. For pairwise distinct values, the radical map is invertible; for one double value, a four-dimensional cross-corner has invertible commutator and the resonant square-zero corner contributes rank zero or one. For three equal values, two weighted-incidence maps and a rank-two Koszul-type map give the remaining cases. Every count and the normalization at \(t=1\) follow symbolically. Independent direct path-multiplication checks over four finite fields pass; no finite test is substituted for the proof.

A general unlocated centralizer-rank formula and older literature on quiver quantum groups remain possible coverage risks; no exhaustive literature uniqueness claim is made.

## Value

The eigenvalue-collision and radical-support transitions explain every rank, and the formula is directly usable in finite-algebra commuting-pair counts and comparisons between Loewy layers. It is a substantive stratified classification rather than a single numerical experiment.
