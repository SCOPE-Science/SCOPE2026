For the one-agent parity fragment of \(\mathsf{S5}\), every pointed model is characterized exactly by its current Boolean valuation and the affine hull of the valuations in its equivalence class. This gives an optimal \(n+1\)-world cutoff for \(n\) atoms and exactly \(2^n\sum_{d=0}^n G_2(n,d)\) pointed theories, where \(G_2(n,d)\) is the binary Gaussian binomial coefficient.
