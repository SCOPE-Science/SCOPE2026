Among all affine images of any prescribed Cartesian product of Euclidean balls, the volume-normalized first real Fourier-zero radius has one sharp optimizer: mutually orthogonal round blocks whose radii scale with their first Bessel zeros. A dimension-independent square-root stability bound quantifies this rigidity, while macroscopic block proportions incur the exact limiting factor \(\exp(-H/2)\) relative to the same-volume Euclidean ball.
