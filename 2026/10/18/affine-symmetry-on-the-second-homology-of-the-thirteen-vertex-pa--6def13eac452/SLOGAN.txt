The natural order-\(78\) affine symmetry of the open neighborhood complex of the Paley graph on thirteen vertices acts on rational second homology as \(\mathbb Q\oplus2(\mathbb Q[\mathbb F_{13}]/\mathbb Q\mathbf1)\), with character \(2|\operatorname{Fix}(g)|-1\). The previously known wedge of twenty-five two-spheres is used only as a nonequivariant input, not claimed anew.
