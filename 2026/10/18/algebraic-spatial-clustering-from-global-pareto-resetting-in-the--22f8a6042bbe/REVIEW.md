# Scientific review

**Correctness: PASS.** The resetting age law follows from the finite-mean renewal theorem; two-spin duality reduces the zero-temperature covariance to a first-hitting-time tail. The birth/death hitting-time Laplace transform yields an independently reconstructible beta integral, and the Chernoff/negative-moment bound controls the Pareto cutoff. The fixed-temperature comparison follows from the exact linear Glauber pair hierarchy and discrete parabolic comparison. The numerical Bessel evaluation reproduces the gamma-ratio amplitude in twelve cases; no computational observation is promoted to an infinite proof.

**Originality: PASS, with residual risk.** The closest full primary texts concern global power-law reset magnetization (V K–Nagar, arXiv:2602.15495v1), global Poisson reset magnetization (Magoni–Majumdar–Schehr, arXiv:2002.04867v1), and *sitewise* Poisson resetting of the voter model (Grange, arXiv:2207.08590v6). The 2026 source explicitly proposes spatial correlations under power-law resetting as an extension. Searches for zero-temperature spatial correlators, coalescing-walk gamma-ratio representations, and equivalent renewal forms located no source proving this combined sharp stationary algebraic correlation theorem; an unindexed or implicit consequence in general coalescence/renewal theory is still possible. Prior work on renewal ages and first-passage duality is acknowledged, not claimed as new.

**Value: PASS.** The precise spatial correlation exponent \(2(\alpha-1)\) and its sharp amplitude address a stated open direction of the motivating work. The result distinguishes critical coarsening under heavy-tailed global resetting from positive-temperature spatial clustering and clarifies which covariance is generated merely by a common reset age.

The scope is annealed, one-dimensional, infinite volume, initial iid spins, finite-mean Pareto resets. Verification of an infinite parameter range is analytic; computations are supplemental. Generality outside these hypotheses is unproved.
