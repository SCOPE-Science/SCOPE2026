The sixteen non-coordinate bitangents of the smooth Ciani quartic pencil have exactly five exceptional complex parameters for their line-concurrency profile: \(\lambda=0,-5,-10\) and the two roots of \(\lambda^2-\lambda+2=0\). Their complete double/triple/quadruple-point counts are determined by exact determinant factorization; all other smooth parameters give \((84,0,6)\).
