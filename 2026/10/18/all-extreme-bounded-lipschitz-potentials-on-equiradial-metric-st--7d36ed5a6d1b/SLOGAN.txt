For every real \(m\)-leaf equiradial metric star, the sum-normalized bounded-Lipschitz ball has exactly \(3\cdot2^m-2\) exposed vertices and \(2m(m+1)\) facets, with complete explicit sign and facet-inequality classifications independent of arm length. Its dual norm on **all** signed atomic measures, including nonzero-total-mass measures, has a closed formula evaluable in \(O(m)\) arithmetic steps.
