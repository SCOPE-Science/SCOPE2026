The eight-cube has exactly \(57\,600\) maximum open packings, and every such packing induces a perfect matching whose coordinate-direction multiplicities have one of just four profiles: \((16,0,0,0,0,0,0,0)\), \((8,8,0,0,0,0,0,0)\), \((4,4,4,4,0,0,0,0)\), or \((2,2,2,2,2,2,2,2)\). The respective numbers of optimal packings are \(1\,920\), \(13\,440\), \(26\,880\), and \(15\,360\), derived by a bijection with ordered pairs of length-seven perfect codes and an intersection census of Fano planes.
