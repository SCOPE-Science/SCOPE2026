Every minimum \(k\)-limited dominating set on a labelled rook board belongs to an explicit row-saturation, column-saturation or capacity-constrained fibre family. The complete optimal-set count is a piecewise formula in binomial coefficients and \(\Phi(a,b,t)=b![x^b](1+\sum_{j=t}^b x^j/j!)^a\), revealing sharp changes in the number of optimal configurations as the capacity increases.
