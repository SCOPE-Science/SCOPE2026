For every binary circular word \((10^{q-1})^m0^s\) with \(m\ge2\), \(q,s\ge1\), the minimum attractor size is exactly two and all optimal position pairs admit an explicit classification and count \(2q+(m-2)\max\{q-s+1,0\}\). Four factors suffice to check the attractor property for **every** marked set, and, for \(m\ge3\), a sharp multiplicity transition as the exceptional zero gap grows.
