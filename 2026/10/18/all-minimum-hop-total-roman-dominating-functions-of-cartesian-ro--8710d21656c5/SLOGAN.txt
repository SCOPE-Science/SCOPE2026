For the rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) with \(2\le m\le n\), hop total Roman domination stabilizes at minimum weight \(6\) once \(m\ge4\). Every minimum labeling is one of six explicitly classified geometric types, and for \(m,n\ge5\) exactly \(6\binom m2\binom n2\) optimal functions occur, six per axis-aligned rectangle.
