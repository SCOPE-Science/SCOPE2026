For outward isotropic Ornstein–Uhlenbeck escape from a ball, positive noise has one unique mean-exit-time optimum in dimension one but strictly reduces the mean exit time in every dimension at least two. At dimension two the reduction is nonperturbative: \(U_0-U_2(D)\sim D(\kappa^2r^2)^{-1}e^{-\kappa r^2/(2D)}\) as \(D\downarrow0\), despite a zero first-order response.
