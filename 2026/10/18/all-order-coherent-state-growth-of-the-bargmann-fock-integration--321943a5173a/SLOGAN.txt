The Gaussian Bargmann–Fock integration resolvent is asymptotically rank one: after multiplication by \(e^{-t^2/2}\), its norm admits the all-order factorial expansion \(\left(\sum_{j=0}^N j!t^{-2j}\right)^{1/2}+O_N(t^{-2N-2})\), while its leading output is a normalized coherent state. The same expansion yields sharp subleading coefficients and the \(-\log\log(1/\varepsilon)\) correction to its circular pseudospectral boundary.
