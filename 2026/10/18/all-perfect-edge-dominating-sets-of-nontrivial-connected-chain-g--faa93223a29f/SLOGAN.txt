Every perfect edge dominating set of a nontrivial connected chain graph is the full edge set, a star at a universal vertex, or—at precisely one sharp two-level boundary—a unique dominating singleton edge. For graphs with at least two twin-class levels, the entire enumerator is \(z^{|E|}+\alpha_p z^{|B|}+\beta_1 z^{|A|}+\mathbf{1}_{\{p=2,\alpha_2=\beta_1=1\}}z\).
