# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The identity expressing quartic Fibonacci powers in five exponential modes was checked against the Fibonacci and Lucas definitions. The five characteristic roots have discriminant \(2^4 3^2 5^{10}\); hence for each \(p\ge7\) the invertible Vandermonde matrix forces simultaneous divisibility of all translated windows exactly when \(p^e\mid k\) and the quadratic unit has \(k\)th power one modulo \(p^e\). Its order modulo \(p\) divides \(p\pm1\), and binomial lifting establishes the complete valuation. The standalone verifier checked lengths through \(400\); the symbolic proof covers all lengths.

**Originality — PASS within inspected evidence.** Adegoke (arXiv:1706.00407, Theorem 3.1) gives quartic-sum identities but no translated-window gcd; Mbirika and Spilker (arXiv:2204.12626, Section 6) present higher powers as a question while classifying squares. Relevant indexed results do not state this prime-primary theorem. Risk: a coincident statement may exist in unindexed literature.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Exact prime exponents for all primes at least seven are natural missing arithmetic invariants of the fourth-power window problem. The order condition yields unexpectedly sparse prime support and concrete nontrivial factors at selected lengths. The theorem excludes \(2,3,5\) rather than claiming a full gcd formula.
