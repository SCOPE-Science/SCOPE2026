For the gcd of every translated window of \(k\) consecutive fourth powers of Fibonacci numbers, each prime \(p\ge7\) occurs either to its full exponent in \(k\) or not at all. The deciding condition is an explicit multiplicative-order test in \(\mathbb F_p[t]/(t^2+3t+1)\); the primes \(2,3,5\) remain outside this classification.
