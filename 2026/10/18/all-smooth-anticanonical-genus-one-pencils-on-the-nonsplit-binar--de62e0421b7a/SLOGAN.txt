The nonsplit quadric over \(\mathbf F_2\) has exactly 4,020 anticanonical pencils whose three rational genus-one members are all geometrically smooth, distributed among 36 projective orbits. At most three rational points can lie in their common base locus; equality is a single free orbit of 120 pencils, each with reduced Frobenius base-point pattern \((1,1,1,5)\).
