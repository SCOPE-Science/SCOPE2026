Exactly \(5,002,905,600\) pencils of cubic surfaces over \(\mathbf F_2\) have all three members geometrically smooth, with a complete rational-base-point distribution and no pencil having exactly \(14\) rational base points. The \(56\) pencils whose base contains all \(15\) rational points form one projective orbit, and each has five pairwise skew rational lines partitioning those points.
