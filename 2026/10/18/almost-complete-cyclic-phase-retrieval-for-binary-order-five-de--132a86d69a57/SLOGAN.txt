Among the \(2048\) binary order-five de Bruijn cycles, there are exactly \(526\) different cyclic autocorrelations, and autocorrelation identifies the cycle up to reversal and complementation except for exactly two exceptional pairs of symmetry classes. Moreover, its full \(32\)-shift power spectrum is already determined by the nine lags \(5,\ldots,13\), and lag \(13\) is necessary for a consecutive-lag cutoff.
