For the Margolis–Rhodes monoid of the star \(K_{1,m}\), every noninjective partial map is regular, and the only nonregular maps are injective maps from a domain containing the centre to at least two leaves. Although the monoid is nonregular for every \(m\ge2\), a uniformly chosen element is regular and noninjective with probability \(1-m^{-1/2}+o(m^{-1/2})\); exact element, idempotent and rank counts are given by partial-bijection numbers.
