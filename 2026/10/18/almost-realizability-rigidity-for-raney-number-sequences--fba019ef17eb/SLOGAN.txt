Among all positive-integer Raney parameters, the sequence \(R_{p,r}(n)=\frac{r}{pn+r}\binom{pn+r}{n}\) is almost realizable as dynamical fixed-point counts only at \(p=r=1\). Prime-index Dold residues exclude \(p\ne r\), while twice-prime-index residues exclude the entire nontrivial diagonal \(p=r\ge2\).
