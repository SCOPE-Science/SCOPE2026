The canonical negated-biconditional candidate is cotransitive on a fraction \(1-(2^m-2)(2^m+3)/(2^m+1)^3\) of triples in the \((2^m+1)\)-element rooted-star Heyting algebra, tending to one despite global cotransitivity failure. In the same algebras, weak excluded middle holds on only \(3/(2^m+1)\) of the elements and tends to zero: qualitative equivalence of identities gives no quantitative stability here.
