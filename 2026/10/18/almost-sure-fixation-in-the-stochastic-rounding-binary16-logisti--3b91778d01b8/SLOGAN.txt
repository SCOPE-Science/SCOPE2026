At the canonical chaotic logistic parameter \(r=4\), ideal stochastic rounding of the exact polynomial to binary16 does not prevent eventual fixation: every one of the \(15{,}361\) representable starting states reaches either \(0\) or \(3/4\) almost surely. An exact complete transition-graph certificate identifies just \(44\) states with positive probabilities of both outcomes, despite the apparent benefits of stochastic rounding in finite simulations.
