For an independent Bernoulli-\(p\) formal context with \(n^{\alpha+o(1)}\) objects and \(n^{\beta+o(1)}\) attributes, the logarithm of its number of formal concepts is almost surely asymptotic to \(\alpha\beta(\log n)^2/(-\log p)\), with no independence required across sizes. In fact, all attribute sets smaller than the natural logarithmic threshold are simultaneously closed, ultimately almost surely.
