For every finite poset of height \(h\), the difference body of its standard order polytope has Euclidean inradius \(1/\sqrt h\), but its centered inball touches support hyperplanes only in the two alternating-sign directions on each longest chain. Compared with the enriched chain polytope, which has the same inradius, the number of oriented contacts falls by the exact factor \(2^{h-1}\); every discarded signed-chain normal has support distance at least twice the common radius.
