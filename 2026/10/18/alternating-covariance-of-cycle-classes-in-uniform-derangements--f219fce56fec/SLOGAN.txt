In a uniform derangement, counts of cycles of any two distinct lengths \(r,s\ge2\) have covariance with strict sign \((-1)^{n-r-s}\) whenever \(n\ge r+s\); the sign flips with every additional unused label. For fixed lengths, its magnitude decays factorially with the exact leading constant \(e/(rs)\) after multiplication by \((n-r-s+1)!\).
