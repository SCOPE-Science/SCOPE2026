For the random convex chain obtained by adjoining uniformly sampled points in a right triangle to its two non-origin vertices, the expected Euclidean length deficit has an exact absolutely convergent inverse-factorial series with rigorous alternating rational error bounds at every truncation. Its leading deficit is \(\Gamma(3/4)^2/(2\sqrt n)\), with a distinct explicitly evaluated corner correction of order \(n^{-2}\).
