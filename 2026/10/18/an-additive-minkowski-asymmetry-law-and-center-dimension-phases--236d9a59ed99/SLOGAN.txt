For the \(m\)-column straight-shape generalized Pitman–Stanley polytope with positive ceiling increments, Minkowski asymmetry is exactly \(m-1+\max_i(\sum_{k=1}^i A_k)/A_i\). Its minimizing-center locus has affine dimension \(nm-L-(m-1)|T|\), where \(T\) is the set of maximizing prefixes and \(L=\max T\); the same asymmetry can have different center-locus dimensions.
