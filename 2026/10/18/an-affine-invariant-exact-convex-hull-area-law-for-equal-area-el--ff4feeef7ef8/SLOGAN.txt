For any two centered, equal-area planar ellipses, their convex-hull area is determined exactly by one affine-invariant spectral mismatch \(D=\sqrt{\operatorname{tr}(A^{-1}B)-2}\): it equals \(s[\pi+2D-2\arctan(D/2)]\), where both ellipses have area \(\pi s\). The area excess lies sharply between \(sD\) and \(2sD\), with a strictly convex normalized profile.
