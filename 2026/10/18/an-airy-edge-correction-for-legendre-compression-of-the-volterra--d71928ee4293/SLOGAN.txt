The operator-norm error of polynomial Galerkin compression of the Volterra integration operator is \(1/(2n)-|a_1|/(2^{4/3}n^{5/3})+o(n^{-5/3})\), with \(a_1\) the first negative Airy zero. Thus this natural compression is asymptotically \(\pi/2\) times the best same-rank approximation error, and its first correction has a sharp Airy boundary-law constant.
