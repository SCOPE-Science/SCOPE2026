A single species-flipping seam in a reversible binary exclusion ring creates an exact all-time correlation sign wall: stationary spin memory is positive along the shorter arc, negative along the longer arc, and zero at antipodal sites. Its integrated pair response is the linear law \((L-2|i-j|)/4\), while the total-spin correlation time is exactly \((L^2+2)/12\) for every \(L\ge3\).
