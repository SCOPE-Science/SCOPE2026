# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS
The exact border-return identity accounts for overlapping borders and the central-length parity correction. Independent uniform outer pairs give the multinomial return probability. A first-return decomposition gives the full odd-length probability generating function, including loops at the origin. A Fourier integral with one nondegenerate saddle gives \(p_q(k)\sim q^{q/2}(4\pi k)^{-(q-1)/2}\); this is the crucial normalization. Falling-factorial moments prove the binary half-normal and ternary exponential laws, while transience yields the higher-alphabet geometric law with a quantitative tail coupling. The nonincreasing survival sequence supports the stated Tauberian step for the ternary no-border probability. Direct word, vector-state, and renewal enumerations agree through length eight; a longer independent ternary check agrees with published zero-border counts. Infinite claims rely on the proof, not the finite checks.

## Originality — PASS, residual risks disclosed
Goč, Rampersad, Rigo, and Salimov (2014) previously introduced the unrestricted-alphabet lattice-path connection and enumerated Abelian-unbordered cases, so the bijection and the zero-count problem are not asserted new. Blanchet-Sadri, Chen, and Hawes (2017) already provide the full **binary** number-of-Abelian-borders distribution; the binary law here is an antecedent and internal consistency check. Habib, Shamil, and Rahman (2021, first publicly posted 30 October 2021) enumerate possible **binary border arrays**, a different global inverse invariant from the number of return times on an arbitrary alphabet. Accessible proofs and statements in these three full texts do not give the exact unrestricted-alphabet return-count generating function, the ternary exponential constant, or the transient geometric distribution and convergence rate. Research-record and published-literature queries for their aliases did not identify dominating statements. Literature on lattice-walk local time might state equivalent probabilistic laws without Abelian-border terminology; this remains the principal originality risk. Absence from searches alone is not treated as proof of novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The sharp trichotomy distinguishes three qualitatively different ambiguity and border-density regimes of a canonical word statistic. In particular, the ternary logarithmic/exponential transition is not recovered from the known binary distribution and the count of merely unbordered words. The exact finite generating function, limiting law constants, boundary correction and quantitative transient rate together resolve the multialphabet border-multiplicity statistic; these are mathematically motivated invariants rather than arbitrary finite entries.

## Source and scope limits
The public texts inspected were the entire relevant sections and theorem statements of the 2014 arbitrary-alphabet unbordered-word paper, the 2017 binary full-border-count paper, and the 2021 binary border-array paper. The underlying random-walk renewal ideas are classical; the claim is not that those general tools are new. Only fixed-alphabet, iid uniform words are covered.
