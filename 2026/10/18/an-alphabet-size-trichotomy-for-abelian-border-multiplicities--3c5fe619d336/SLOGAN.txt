For a uniformly random \(q\)-letter word, the number of distinct proper Abelian-border lengths has three sharp alphabet regimes: a half-normal law of order \(\sqrt n\) at \(q=2\), an exponential law of order \(\log n\) at \(q=3\), and an even-valued geometric limit at every \(q\ge4\). A return-renewal generating function gives the entire odd-length distribution and explicit constants and error rates.
