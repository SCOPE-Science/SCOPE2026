The exact singular spectrum of asymmetric weighted Forelli–Rudin kernels has a sharp angular-mode staircase: as the kernel exponent approaches \(a+b+2\) from below, its norming degree diverges like \((a-b)^2/[2(a+b+2-c)]\). At the bounded critical exponent, the asymmetric norm ceases to be attained, whereas symmetric weights retain infinitely many norming modes.
