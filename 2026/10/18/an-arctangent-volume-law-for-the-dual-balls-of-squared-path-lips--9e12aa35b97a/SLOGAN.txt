The anchored-coordinate volumes \(V_n\) of the dual balls of real Lipschitz-free spaces over squared paths have an exact arctangent lattice series, \(V_n=\frac45\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z}(\arctan(3/4)+2\pi k)^{-n}\), for every \(n\ge2\). Consequently \(V_n\sim\frac45\arctan(3/4)^{-n}\), giving a sharp exponential volume loss relative to ordinary paths.
