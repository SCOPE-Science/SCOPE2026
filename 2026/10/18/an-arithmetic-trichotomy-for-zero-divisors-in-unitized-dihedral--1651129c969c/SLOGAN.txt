The extended algebra of the three-element dihedral quandle over \(\mathbb F_q\) has exactly \(q^3-1+(q-1)B(q)\) nonzero zero divisors, where \(B(q)\) takes three explicit forms according as \(q\equiv1\), \(q\equiv2\), or \(q\equiv0\pmod3\). The trichotomy arises from splitting, norm-one and characteristic-three degeneracy of a quadratic determinant factor.
