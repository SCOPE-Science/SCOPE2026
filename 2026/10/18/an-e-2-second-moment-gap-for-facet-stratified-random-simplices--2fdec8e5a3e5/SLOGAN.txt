One independent uniform point from each distinct facet of any \((n-1)\)-simplex produces an exact squared normalized-volume moment \(n!/[n^{n-1}(n-1)^n]\); relative to uniform interior sampling, the second moment grows by a factor tending to \(e^2\). The associated signed volume has an exact mean and an orientation-sign transition at \(n=4\), equivalently yielding a signed derangement-pair cancellation identity.
