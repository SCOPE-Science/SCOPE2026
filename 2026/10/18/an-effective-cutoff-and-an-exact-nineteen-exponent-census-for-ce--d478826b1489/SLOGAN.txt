The centered-power equation \((2m+1)^k=u m^k+v(m+1)^k\) has no nonnegative-coefficient solutions beyond an explicit finite bound in \(m\), for every exponent \(k\ge2\). Exhausting those proved bounds gives exactly five solutions across all nineteen exponents \(2\le k\le20\), including a rigorous all-center classification of the previously observed exponent-\(11\) and exponent-\(13\) examples.
