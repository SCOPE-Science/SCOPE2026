For the 24 truncated-octahedron vertices, exact-degree spherical-harmonic interpolation first becomes complete at degree \(7\), loses precisely one alternating sample direction at degree \(8\), and recovers permanently at degree \(9\). The degree-eight obstruction is protected by signed-permutation symmetry for every orbit of \((0,1,t)\) with \(t>1\), while full recovery at degrees \(7\) and \(\ell\ge9\) persists for all transcendental \(t>1\).
