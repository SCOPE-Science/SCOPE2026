In an even cycle whose edge-conductance prices alternate, minimizing the worst total effective resistance after any single edge outage uniquely **equalizes all edge conductances over a nontrivial interval of unequal prices**. The two exact price thresholds are \(2(m^2-1)/(2m^2+1)\) and \((2m^2+1)/(2(m^2-1))\), and the plateau width is asymptotic to \(3/m^2\).
