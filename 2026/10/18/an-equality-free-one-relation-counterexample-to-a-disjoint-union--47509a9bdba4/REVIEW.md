## Value — PASS

The result settles a specific unresolved boundary in the recent preservation-theorem literature, in the strongest small signature of one binary relation. Its extension-preservation obstruction explains why positive existential quantification plus local guarded universal quantification cannot detect the required global upper restriction, whereas unrestricted first-order logic can. It is a direct structural negative theorem rather than a parameter substitution or small numerical improvement.
