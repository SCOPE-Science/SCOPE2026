# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The stochastic coordinate change is exact, including both Brownian covariance factors. The autonomous relative generator is a reversible confining diffusion with the stated invariant density. The stationary center has an explicit stable causal representation. Stationary Stratonovich work reduces to its nonconservative center forcing because both radial and center confinement terms are exact potential differentials; independence of the relative and center Brownian noises prevents an Itô–Stratonovich cross term. The resolvent formula then follows from the stationary force autocorrelation. Spectral positivity is strict because the force has positive variance and no projection onto the constant eigenmode. The linear-limit coefficient was also reconstructed from an independent matrix Lyapunov equation; finite-rest-length radial Poisson discretizations converged on three test cases. Remaining mathematical risk is restricted to routine diffusion regularity at the direction-discontinuous origin, addressed by bounded measurable drifts and approximation of the radial potential.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

The 2026 primary dimer paper establishes zero-rest-length full distributions and treats finite rest length by numerical integration, not this exact hidden-dissipation/bond-only path identity. Zhang–Garcia-Millan (2023) already gives a two-particle equilibrium relative marginal and entropy rate in a *different*, translation-invariant periodic-ring setting; the elementary separability and general invisibility principle are expressly prior work. Loos–Klapp (2020) contains general nonequilibrium Langevin thermodynamics and linear models. Comparisons against their theorem statements and mathematical assumptions reveal no exact finite-rest-length, harmonically confined planar dimer resolvent or exact all-asymmetry energy-production coefficient. Published research-record searches on bond dynamics, stiffness difference, finite-rest-length, entropy and resolvents yielded no covering entry; an analogous finite-state circulation theorem is not a logical cover of this nonlinear diffusion. Residual risk: an unlocated general nonlinear feedforward diffusion formula may make the coefficient an immediate corollary, and literature coverage cannot prove uniqueness.

## Value — PASS

A common experimental measurement is bond length or bond displacement. This model supplies a parameter-sharp observability obstruction: even the infinite stationary bond trajectory cannot reveal arbitrarily different positive energy dissipation rates at fixed total stiffness. The coefficient remains exact for genuinely nonlinear finite-rest-length forces, a regime where the motivating paper relies on simulation for the full stationary state. It is a nontrivial steady-state thermodynamic invariant with a reproducible radial resolvent reduction, not simply a renamed Gibbs marginal or small-n numerical example.
