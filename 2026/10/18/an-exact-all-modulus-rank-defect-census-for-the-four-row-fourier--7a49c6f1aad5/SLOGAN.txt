For every cyclic Fourier order \(N\ge5\), the four-row pattern \(\{0,1,3,4\}\) has exactly \(\mathbf1_{2\mid N}N(N-2)/4-\mathbf1_{4\mid N}N/4+\mathbf1_{3\mid N}N(N-3)/3-\mathbf1_{6\mid N}N+2N\mathbf1_{30\mid N}\) rank-three maximal minors, and no smaller ranks. Two exceptional free orbits of primitive thirtieth-root configurations account for the final term, beyond the previously described antipodal and ternary ambiguity families.
