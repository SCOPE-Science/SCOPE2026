A geometrically thinned Rhaly matrix, retaining only rows \(b^k-1\) with weights \((kb^k)^{-1/2}\), has the exact nonzero singular sequence \(s_j=j^{-1/2}\), independently of every integer base \(b\ge2\). A Meixner-polynomial diagonalization explains the entire arithmetic spectrum and its sharp weak-Schatten-two endpoint.
