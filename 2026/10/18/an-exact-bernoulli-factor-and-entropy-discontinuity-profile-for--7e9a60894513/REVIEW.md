# Scientific review: scaled modulo-tent product-Bernoulli law

**Correctness — PASS.** The original modulo/scaling rule agrees on every open branch with a complete affine map onto \((0,1)\), of exactly computable inverse slope. The branch lengths sum to one, giving Lebesgue invariance. Repeated inverse substitution proves product cylinder probabilities for arbitrary lengths; geometric contraction proves the generating property. Branch entropy, metric entropy, their derivatives and their one-sided limits follow by displayed finite sums and elementary differentiation. Exact-rational reproducibility checks cover seven rational parameter values and 4,004 branch-word contributions, with 156 direct inverse identities. These finite computations are confirmatory, not substitutes for the all-parameter proof.

**Originality — PASS with specific priority risks.** Khan and Jeoti (2015) supply the map, its central normalization and qualitative uniformity, so those facts are not claimed as new. Irfan and Khan (2024) supply the half-threshold bits and numerical randomness tests; those are also not new. Neither inspected primary text gives the exact i.i.d. product decomposition together with the branch-versus-metric entropy profile and its even-parameter one-sided jump. General Bernoulli coding of full affine branches is a prior standard tool; the contribution is the explicit analysis of this map and its observables. The full earlier 2013 conference version and the later journal full text were not available; they may contain an equivalent parameter-specific result. The literature search is not a proof of priority.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** For the output observable explicitly used in an applied chaotic generator, the theorem settles all finite output-block probabilities, shows nonidentifiability of the parameter from the stationary binary process, identifies a hidden independent Bernoulli factor, and derives sharp parameter boundaries for two different entropy notions. The distinction matters mathematically because uniform output statistics remain unchanged while complete symbolic structure changes at even-parameter limits. No security claim follows, and no analogous result is asserted for finite-state implementations.

**Closest sources and boundaries.** Irfan--Khan, arXiv:2408.05580 (full v1), Section IV and Algorithms 1--2; Khan--Jeoti, DOI:10.3844/jcssp.2015.517.525 (full 2015 article, particularly the defining equations, scaling, results, and conclusion); Khan--Jeoti conference DOI:10.1109/MICC.2013.6805880 (metadata and abstract only). Independent historical and mathematical verification remain open.
