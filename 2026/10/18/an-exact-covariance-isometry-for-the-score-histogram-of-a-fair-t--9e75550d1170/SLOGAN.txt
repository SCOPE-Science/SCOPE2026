For a uniformly fair round-robin tournament, the complete Pearson-standardized score histogram has exactly two zero covariance eigenvalues and \(n-2\) identical positive eigenvalues, all equal to \(n\). Equivalently, every additive score statistic has exactly the variance of \(n\) independent binomial scores after its best affine score prediction is removed, giving \(\mathbb E X_n=n-2\) for the ungrouped Pearson score.
