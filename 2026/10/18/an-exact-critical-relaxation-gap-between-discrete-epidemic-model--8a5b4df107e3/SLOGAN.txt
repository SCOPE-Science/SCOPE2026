At the shared epidemic threshold, two discrete-time network SIS models—one permitting and one forbidding reinfection within a time step—both decay as \(1/t\), but their exact Perron-mode decay amplitudes differ by a strictly positive quadratic coefficient on every connected graph. A two-vertex network displays an unbounded disparity as the recovery probability approaches one, where a persistent swapping orbit appears only in the reinfection-permitting model.
