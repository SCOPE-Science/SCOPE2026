For \(G(n,1/2)\), the probability of an odd number of spanning trees conditioned on **every vertex-degree parity** is independent of the entire parity vector at odd orders, with exact value \(\prod_{j=1}^{(n-1)/2}(1-2^{1-2j})\). At even orders the probability is zero for the all-even vector, and otherwise has the universal value \(\prod_{j=1}^{(n-2)/2}(1-2^{1-2j})\).
