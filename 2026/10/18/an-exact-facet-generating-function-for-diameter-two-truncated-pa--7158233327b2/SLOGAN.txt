For real Lipschitz-free spaces over \(\{1,\ldots,n\}\) with metric \(d(i,j)=\min\{|i-j|,2\}\), the exact facet counts have rational generating function \(2z^2/(1-z)+2z^3(1+z)^3/((1-z-2z^2-2z^3)(1-z-z^2-z^3))\). In particular the facet count grows as \(C\lambda^n\), where \(\lambda\approx2.26953>2\), and every dual extreme is classified by a constrained binary or ternary word.
