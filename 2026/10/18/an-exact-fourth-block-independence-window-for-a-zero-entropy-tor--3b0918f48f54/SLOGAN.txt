A binary observable of the zero-entropy Anzai skew shift is three-wise independent at every parameter, but its entire four-consecutive-symbol distribution follows an exact piecewise-quadratic phase law with its only breakpoint at \(\operatorname{dist}(\alpha,\tfrac12\mathbb Z)=1/6\). The four symbols become exactly independent at the exceptional irrational distances \(1/(4\sqrt3)\), even though the full stationary process remains non-Bernoulli.
