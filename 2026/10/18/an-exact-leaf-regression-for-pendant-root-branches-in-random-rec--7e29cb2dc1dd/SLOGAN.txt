In a uniform recursive tree with \(n\ge2\) vertices, observing \(z\) pendant children at the root forces the exact conditional mean \(\mathbb E[L_n\mid Z_n=z]=(n-1+z)/2\) of the total leaf count, for every feasible \(z\). The root-singleton marginal itself is known; the finding is its exact finite-sample regression on the global leaf statistic.
