For \(2/q\) with odd \(q\ge3\), the odd greedy algorithm terminates in exactly four terms precisely for \(q\equiv7,35,47,55\pmod{60}\), a class of density \(2/15\) among odd denominators. Combining this with the two-term classes, exactly \(4/5\) of odd denominators terminate within four steps, and the four-term indicator has minimal even period \(60\).
