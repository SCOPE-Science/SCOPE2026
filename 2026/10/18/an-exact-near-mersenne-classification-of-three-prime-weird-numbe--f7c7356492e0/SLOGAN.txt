For primitive weird numbers \(2^kpq\) with \(M=2^{k+1}-1\) and \(0<p-M<\sqrt p\), the possible larger primes \(q\) are classified exactly by one open interval and \(p-M-1\) residue classes modulo \(p\). In this full near-Mersenne strip, every such weird number satisfies the one-third-scale abundance bound conjectured by Iannucci.
