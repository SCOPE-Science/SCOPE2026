# Verification and scope

The standalone `VERIFY.py` uses rational half-space clipping and computes the exact areas for each of the 16 two-letter transitions and four admissible survivor families for each integer \(m=1,\ldots,60\). All exact computed areas agree with the formulas in RESULT.md. This is a finite algebraic diagnostic, not a certification of the infinite theorem. The proof in RESULT.md derives the general intersections directly from affine branch inequalities and integrates their areas for symbolic \(m\ge1\). The first public literature date is taken from the 15 March 2023 arXiv v1 record rather than the December 2023 revision. An independent audit has not been performed.
