For any two-patch population with irreducible constant linear exchange and one fixed-strength removal pulse per patch per period, simultaneous removals strictly minimize the principal Floquet multiplier and half-period staggering maximizes it; a single closed-form hyperbolic threshold determines the entire persistence interval. A rational two-patch example has global extinction under simultaneous removal but a globally attracting positive periodic population under staggered removal with the same two pulse strengths.
