# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** The finite graph and both full face sets are explicitly reconstructed. Each of the 105 listed elementary relative collapses is checked against all remaining cofaces; the complement is exactly the ordinary independence complex. An independent exhaustive acyclic discrete-Morse matching verifies that the remaining complex has one critical vertex and one critical triangle, hence homotopy type \(S^2\). Mod-two homology is cross-check evidence only, not the integral proof.

**Originality (PASS, subject to the bibliographic risks below).** The relevant higher-independence theory classifies trees and certain other families, and reports a different generalized Mycielskian. Known ordinary Mycielski suspension results imply at most the homotopy type of the smaller complex, not the new relative-collapse conclusion. Published Grötzsch matching-complex and Čech-complex records concern different simplicial objects. Searches for \(\operatorname{Ind}_2\), dissociation, 3-path-free and relative simple-homotopy aliases found no equivalent or stronger published result.

**Mathematical value (PASS).** For the canonical eleven-vertex triangle-free four-chromatic graph, a natural relaxation from independent sets to induced matchings plus isolated vertices adds 210 simplices yet leaves the ordinary independence complex as an explicit simplicial collapse target. This is a structural inclusion theorem rather than a table recalculation.

The theorem asserts no all-Mycielskian extension and no equivariant elementary collapse. A possibly relevant older paper on ordinary Mycielski independence complexes was read at abstract/bibliographic level only; genuinely unindexed overlap remains possible.
