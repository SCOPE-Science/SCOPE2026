On every regular \(m\)-line fan in the plane, cyclic relaxed Douglas–Rachford has the exact full-cycle eigenvalues \(-\cos^m(\pi/m)\) and \(-\lambda^m\cos^m(\pi/m)\) for every \(0\le\lambda\le1\), even when arbitrary affine translations make the lines inconsistent. The asymptotic factor is independent of relaxation, but full Douglas–Rachford (\(\lambda=1\)) uniquely minimizes Euclidean error amplification at **every** positive finite-cycle horizon, relative to each method's own fixed point.
