In a conformity-driven SIRS adherence game, three locally attracting outcomes—endemic nonadherence, endemic partial adherence, and disease-free full adherence—coexist on the exact, sharp cost interval \(1/10<c<(7-2\sqrt{10})/5\) for one fixed admissible parameter family. Three is also the universal maximum number of hyperbolic equilibrium sinks for this model; the qualitative possibility of triple multistability was already indicated in the motivating literature, while the exact threshold and rigorous sharp realization are the present result.
