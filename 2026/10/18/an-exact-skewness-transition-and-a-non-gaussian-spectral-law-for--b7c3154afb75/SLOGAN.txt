In a circular random geometric graph, pairwise independence makes edge-count variance look binomial, but geometric triangles create an explicit third-cumulant correction and exactly one finite-size skewness sign reversal. Its radius is \(r_n=\frac12-\frac1{3+\sqrt{8n-15}}\), and for every fixed nontrivial radius the centered edge count divided by the vertex count converges to an explicit, positively skewed non-Gaussian exponential series.
