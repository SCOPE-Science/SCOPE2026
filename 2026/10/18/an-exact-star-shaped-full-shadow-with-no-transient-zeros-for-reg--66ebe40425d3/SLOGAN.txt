For \(P(z)=z^d-a\) with \(d\ge3\) and \(a\ne0\), every zero of every nonconstant derivative of every positive power of \(P\) lies on a spoke joining the center to a vertex, with an exact all-order multiplicity and interlacing law. The entire regular star is both the full shadow and its limiting core, so no transient zeros occur.
