No finite nonnegative mixture of positive shifted resolvents approximates the three-mode exponential on \(0,(\log3)/t,2(\log3)/t\) more accurately than \((2\sqrt3-3)/9\) in operator norm, and one resolvent attains the sharp floor. The obstruction persists on a strictly positive-definite matrix with condition number \(9\), where the exact error floor is \(3^{-1/4}(2\sqrt3-3)/9\).
