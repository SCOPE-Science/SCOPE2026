# Reproduction

The included `verify.py` uses Python standard-library rational arithmetic and `sympy`. Run `python verify.py` with SymPy installed. Successful replay prints `PASS: 7659 finite exact-rational and symbolic checks` and the exact critical parameters of the worked example. All input datasets are generated by code; no external local files, network access or prior-result paths are needed.

# Evidence and limits

The checker independently evaluates every binary solvency indicator pattern over a grid of rational model parameters, compares the maximal fixed point with the analytical regime formulas, verifies exact expected-default identities for exchangeable binary shocks, and checks the symbolic inverse and threshold identities. The finite test suite is **not** a proof of universal-integration inequalities or priority. The parameter theorem follows from the stand-alone analytical proof in RESULT.md. Every conclusion depends on maximal equilibrium selection, the proportional external-asset normalization, and strict solvency test; those boundaries are explicit.
