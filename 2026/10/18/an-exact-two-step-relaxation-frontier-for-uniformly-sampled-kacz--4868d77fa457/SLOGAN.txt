In the smallest noncommuting uniformly sampled Kaczmarz system, volume-ratio overrelaxation always worsens the first-step expected squared error, yet can strictly improve it after two steps. The exact switch occurs at the unique \(\delta_*\in(0,1)\) satisfying \(\delta_*^4+s^2\delta_*(\delta_*+2)=1\); above this threshold the overrelaxed method is worse after two steps for every nonzero initial error.
