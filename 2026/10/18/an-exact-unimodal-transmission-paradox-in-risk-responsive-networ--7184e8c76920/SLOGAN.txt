In an eight-person threshold-aware SIR network, the exact expected final number of infections is a degree-nine polynomial of transmissibility with a unique interior maximum: increasing transmission from \(4/5\) to \(1\) reduces the expectation from \(7267213/1953125\) to \(3\). The effect persists under a nonempty open range of genuine multiplex awareness mixing, independently of social-layer topology, and in connected contact networks of unbounded size.
