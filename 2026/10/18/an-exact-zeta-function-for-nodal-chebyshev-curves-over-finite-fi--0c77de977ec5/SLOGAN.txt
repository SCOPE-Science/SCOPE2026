For coprime \(m,n\ge2\), simultaneous signs in \(q\equiv\pm1\pmod{2m}\) and \(q\equiv\pm1\pmod{2n}\) determine the *entire* zeta function of the nodal affine Chebyshev curve \(T_n(x)=T_m(y)\): its \((m-1)(n-1)/2\) rational nodes are all split or all nonsplit, giving \(Z(t)=(1-\eta t)^{(m-1)(n-1)/2}/(1-qt)\).
