An explicit cyclotomic-norm barrier makes the previously qualitative prime-power stabilization of minimal vanishing power sums effective: for an order-\(n\) subgroup modulo \(p^k\), the minimum equals the least prime factor \(r\) of \(n\) as soon as \(p^k>(r-1)^{\varphi(n)}\). In particular, stabilization is guaranteed by exponent \(\varphi(n)\), and all exceptional first-layer primes are bounded explicitly.
