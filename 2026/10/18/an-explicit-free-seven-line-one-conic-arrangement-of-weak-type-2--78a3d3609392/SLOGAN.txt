The previously unresolved seven-line–one-conic weak type \( (n_2,n_3,n_4)=(2,7,2)\) has an explicit rational-coefficient realization \(xy(x+y)(x^2-z^2)(y^2-z^2)(x^2+xy+y^2-z^2)=0\). Two quartic Jacobian syzygies have Saito determinant \(-243F\), proving that this degree-nine arrangement is free with exponents \((4,4)\).
