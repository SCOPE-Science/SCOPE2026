The explicitly constructed genus-five Artin–Schreier curve \(y^3-y=x^2+x+x^{-1}+(x-1)^{-1}\) over \(\mathbb F_3\) has zeta numerator \(1+2T+6T^2+8T^3+10T^4+15T^5+30T^6+72T^7+162T^8+162T^9+243T^{10}\). Its Jacobian has \(711\) rational points and is simple over \(\mathbb F_3\) (geometric simplicity is not asserted).
