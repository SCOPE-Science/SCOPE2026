On the full real plane, the fractional sine-cubic map has an infinite lattice of equilibria whose positive Jacobian determinants form exactly paired arithmetic levels. Its number of linearly stable equilibria obeys an explicit, order-dependent staircase and grows as \(D_\nu/[\pi\sqrt{c-1}]\) near \(c=1\); genuine fractional orders enlarge the determinant stability threshold beyond the integer-order value.
