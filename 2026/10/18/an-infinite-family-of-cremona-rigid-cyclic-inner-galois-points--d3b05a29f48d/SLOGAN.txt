For every \(n\ge9\) congruent to \(1\) modulo \(4\), an explicit rational plane curve of degree \(n+2\) has exactly \((n^2+1)/2\) ordinary nodes and one \(A_{n-1}\) cusp, while the complete plane Cremona stabilizer is \(C_2\). Its distinguished inner Galois point has cyclic group \(C_n\), none of whose nonidentity elements extends to a Cremona transformation.
