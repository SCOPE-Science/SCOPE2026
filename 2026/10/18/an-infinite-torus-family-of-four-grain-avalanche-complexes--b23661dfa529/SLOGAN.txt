On every directed cycle of length \(n\geq2d+5\) with \(d\geq3\), four chips at \(\{0,1,d,d+1\}\) generate an avalanche complex that collapses by exactly \(2n\) elementary moves onto a triangulated torus. Its nonzero integral degree-one cup product gives an explicit infinite family of avalanche complexes that are not wedges of spheres.
