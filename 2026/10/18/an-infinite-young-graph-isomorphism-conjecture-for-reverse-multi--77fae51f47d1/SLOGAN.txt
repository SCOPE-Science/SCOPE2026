Kendrick's infinite Young-graph conjecture holds: for every integer \(m\ge3\), the reverse-multiple carry graph \(Y(m^2-1,m^2-m-1)\) has exactly the same eight ordinary vertices, sixteen ordinary edges, distinguished start, and pivot markings as \(Y(8,5)\). An exhaustive symbolic transition table establishes the isomorphism without reliance on finite extrapolation.
