## correctness

For all n>=2 over C and a general smooth pencil, the two isotropy equations are Plucker hyperplanes in Gr(2,2n+1), with class u^2. Integral weak Lefschetz yields low-degree ambient restriction; Fonarev--Kuznetsov affine paving gives torsion-free even cohomology, expected middle rank n and an integral Chow cycle isomorphism. For m=2n-2 the n ambient two-row Schubert classes (m-i,i) have intersection matrix diagonal 2 except terminal 1, adjacent 1, whose determinant D_(n-1)-D_(n-2)=n-(n-1)=1, proving full middle lattice surjectivity. For each m<j<=4n-4 the complement partitions yield an r=floor((4n-4-j)/2)+1 square selected Schubert pairing matrix that is lower triangular with unit diagonal; integral Poincare duality forces surjectivity in high degrees. Since [X]=u^2 and both intersection pairings are perfect, kernel of the ambient restriction is precisely Ann_(Z[u,v]/(h_2n,h_(2n+1)))(u^2), giving colon-ideal presentation. Independently implemented Pieri pairings checked middle matrices, determinants and all upper selected minors for n=2,...,35; independent two-variable Schur coefficient extraction agreed with the Pieri computation on 4653 selected pairing coefficients for n=2,...,12. These bounded calculations corroborate rather than replace the symbolic all-n proof.
