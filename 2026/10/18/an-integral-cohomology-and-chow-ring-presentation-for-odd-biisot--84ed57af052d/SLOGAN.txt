For every smooth odd biisotropic two-plane Grassmannian \(X_n\), \(n\ge2\), the complete integral Chow and cohomology ring is \(\mathbb Z[u,v]/((h_{2n},h_{2n+1}):(u^2))\), with \(h_r=uh_{r-1}-vh_{r-2}\). Its middle Schubert intersection lattice is explicitly unimodular, making ambient tautological classes sufficient in every degree.
