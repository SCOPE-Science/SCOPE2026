For the non-axial cubic constant-width body with support \(1+\varepsilon u_1u_2u_3\), the normalized random planar-shadow area deficit has an exact one-dimensional arcsine-mixture law. Its density develops a two-sided **interior logarithmic caustic** \(f(1/4+\eta)=(6/\pi)\log(1/|\eta|)+O(1)\), with independently determined finite endpoint-tail slopes \(3/2\) and \(9/2\).
