# Review of the Heawood primitive three-cycle

## Correctness — PASS
The graph and both cubical chain complexes are explicitly specified in RESULT.md. Exact elimination of the original 1680-by-644 cubical third boundary modulo three yields rank 643; a signed integer vector with 392 unit entries satisfies every integral boundary equation. The graph-theoretic support criterion is checked on all 644 top cubes, with exhaustive automorphism and orbit enumeration. Each original edge is bisected before applying the graph-configuration deformation-retraction theorem (all essential-vertex paths have at least two edges, all nontrivial cycles at least twelve). The lifted 3136-term unit-coordinate chain is verified integral-cycle-wise in the 9072-cube subdivided model, whose third boundary has mod-three rank 9071. Therefore its rational kernel is one-dimensional; integrality and a unit coordinate make the generator primitive. There are no fourth chains, and all 336 automorphisms fix it coefficientwise. These are exact computations plus a published topological comparison theorem, not an inference from isolated examples.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
Farley–Sabalka's foundational discrete configuration model and the reduced Świątkowski graph-configuration theory give general methods, but not this explicit Heawood cellular cycle or its 336-element symmetry character. Maciążek–Sawicki discuss graph-configuration product cycles and nonproduct phenomena on other graphs, without the stated Heawood classification. Most importantly, the published Heawood three-particle homology calculation (record 7524bced20b8) already proves the integral top rank one with a distinct 881-term reduced-Świątkowski chain; the new claim expressly excludes novelty for that rank and distinguishes the cubical norm, graph-theoretic matching criterion, six orbit classification, and exact automorphism action. A same-rank theorem does not imply a canonical cubical representative, its support, or the representation character. Search and full scientific-statement comparisons found no covering claim. The possibility of an unpublished or unindexed equivalent calculation remains.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This gives an explicit primitive geometric representative of the otherwise abstract top homology generator for a highly symmetric nonplanar graph-configuration space. The nonempty-induced-matching support test explains which of 644 geometric three-cubes survive in a 392-cube compression and gives an equivariant 3136-cube representative on a sufficient subdivision. The complete 336-element triviality statement and the impossibility of a disjoint product of three graph-cycle classes are structural information beyond the previously computed rank, rather than an arbitrary numerical slice. The exact cubical norm is understood only relative to the canonical specified subdivision, not claimed as a geometric systole.
