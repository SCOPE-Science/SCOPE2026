For infinitely many smooth non-projective toric threefolds \(Z''_{13}(a,2,0,0)\) with \(a\ge4\), the canonical divisor has nonzero degree on every torus-invariant curve, yet a non-invariant rational curve has canonical degree zero. The obstruction is carried by an invariant blow-up surface on which \((-K)|_S\equiv F_1+2F_2-3E\), giving an explicit negative answer to the invariant-curve-only possibility in Question 6.6 of Ito–Zeng–Zeng.
