Oppositely ranked consumer benefits and resource damage create a sharp invasion-determinant threshold \(Q=1-(a-b)(d-c)\): fully positive coexistence is stable for \(Q>0\), but two distinct consumer-exclusion states are stable for \(Q<0\). At the threshold a whole stationary segment connects the states; an explicit four-species family has exactly nine nonnegative equilibria off criticality and changes its complete local-attractor census at \(d=3/2\).
