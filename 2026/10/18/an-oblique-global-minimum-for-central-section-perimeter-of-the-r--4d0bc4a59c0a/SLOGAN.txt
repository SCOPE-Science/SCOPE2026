The smallest-perimeter central slice of the regular cuboctahedron is neither a coordinate nor a diagonal slice: its normal is a signed coordinate permutation of \((1,t_*,0)/\sqrt{1+t_*^2}\), where \(t_*\approx0.5150605281\). The exact minimum is \(7.4755344541\ldots\), with a global positive quadratic lower bound for tilts away from the optimal coordinate plane.
