The scale-four Čech nerve of the five-cube has 1,910,200 simplices, yet an explicit acyclic matching produces an optimal CW model with only one vertex, eleven 4-cells and sixty 6-cells. Its remaining homotopy ambiguity is exactly one rank \(0\le t\le11\) of \(Sq^2\), giving a wedge of \(t\) copies of \(\Sigma^2\mathbb{CP}^2\), \(11-t\) four-spheres and \(60-t\) six-spheres; that rank is not determined here.
