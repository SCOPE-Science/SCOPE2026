For the Castañeda–Rebollo-Perdomo polynomial three-flow, a squared-radius Dulac weight extends the rigorous global two-cycle classification from \(\mu\le588/6575\) to \(\mu\le1764/15625\). This cutoff is optimal among all power weights \(u^{-\alpha}\), though it is not asserted to be a dynamical bifurcation threshold.
