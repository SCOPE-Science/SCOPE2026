The smooth Frobenius-nonclassical plane quintic over \(\mathbf F_4\) displayed by Asgarli–Duan–Lai has Weil numerator \((1+3T^2+16T^4)^3\): its ordinary genus-six Jacobian is the cube of a simple ordinary abelian surface up to \(\mathbf F_4\)-isogeny, becoming a sixth power of an elliptic curve over \(\mathbf F_{16}\). Every odd-degree extension has exactly \(4^n+1\) curve points.
