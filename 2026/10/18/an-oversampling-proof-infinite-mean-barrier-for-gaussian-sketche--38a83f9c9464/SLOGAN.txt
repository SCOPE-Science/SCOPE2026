For every nonterminated Krylov degree \(k\) and every finite Gaussian sketch size \(s\ge k\), the unsafeguarded sketched Galerkin approximation to an SPD system has energy error with an exact positive \(1/T\) tail and no finite absolute mean. In contrast, the sketched minimal-residual error has a Student tail of order \(T^{-(s-k+1)}\) and finite second moment once \(s\ge k+2\); this distinction concerns rare unsafeguarded draws rather than typical convergence.
