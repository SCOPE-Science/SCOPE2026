For compact Dirichlet metric stars with \(k\ge3\) positive arms of fixed total length \(L\), the sharp \(L^\infty\) Poisson-resolvent norms fill exactly \((L^2/[8(k-1)^2],\,L^2/8)\), with neither endpoint attained. The smallest norms are approached by collapsing one absorbing arm and equalizing the remaining lengths, contrary to the equilateral minimax guess.
