For complete-bipartite graphical zonotopes, the probability that two independent random Euclidean objectives select the same vertex has an exact factorial-run generating function. With one bipartition class fixed at size \(q\), the collision probability is asymptotic to \((q!)^2\sum_{j=0}^q\binom qj^{-1}\,p^{-2q}\): exponentially many vertices have only polynomially many effective objective-stable alternatives.
