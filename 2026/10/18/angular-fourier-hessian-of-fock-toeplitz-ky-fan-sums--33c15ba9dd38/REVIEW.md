# Scientific review: angular Fourier Hessian of Fock Toeplitz Ky Fan sums

## Correctness — PASS
The trace-class Fock Toeplitz family induced by the smooth area-preserving boundary perturbation has four trace-norm derivatives because the boundary remains at strictly positive radius. The isolated top spectral block at the disc has gap \(b_N(s)>0\). Differentiating its Riesz projection gives the exact spectral second-derivative formula. The diagonal second derivative telescopes to \(-b_{N-1}(s)\|h\|_{L^2(\mathbb T)}^2\), and the cross-spectral terms diagonalize in angular Fourier modes. The cancellation for the first mode and the positive rank-dependent quadrupole coefficient follow from exact Poisson recurrences. The \(O(\varepsilon^4)\) remainder in the quadrupole follows from rotation parity and four derivatives. Exact rational checks and independent finite-matrix quadrature corroborate the coefficient identities but cannot independently establish the infinite-dimensional theorem.

## Originality — PASS, subject to independent literature review
Abreu, arXiv:2607.28376, proves the unrestricted global Ky Fan isoperimetric inequality and supplies the disc eigenvalue formula. The author-posted complete text was inspected: its Theorem 1 and Sections 2–5 do not state the rank-dependent second shape derivative or the quadrupolar curvature formula. The radial result of Nicola–Riccardi–Tilli, arXiv:2503.07069, does not itself treat angular deformations; their arXiv:2607.20965 establishes existence of optimizers, not a spectral Hessian. Earlier rank-one quantitative stability results do not directly supply the asserted \(N\ge2\) coefficients. Semantic comparisons against published mathematical results and available local findings did not identify a dominating formula. Some earlier full texts were not accessible; these are residual novelty risks rather than evidence of independent certification.

## Value — PASS
The exact nonradial Hessian addresses the local quantitative structure of a recently solved isoperimetric spectral optimization problem. It gives a sharp, explicitly positive stability coefficient for the first genuinely shape-changing angular harmonic at every rank and every positive area, together with a complete Fourier decomposition of the second derivative and a translation-symmetry zero mode. These coefficients are not determined by the global inequality and can inform quantitative stability and equality-case investigations. This is a local structural lemma; no global stability or uniqueness theorem is claimed.

## Limitations and closest literature
This work treats smooth deformations of discs at fixed rank and area. It does not prove global uniqueness of optimizers or strict positivity for all Fourier harmonics. The most relevant full source is Abreu's complete July 2026 manuscript, followed by the radial 2025 and existence July 2026 papers of Nicola–Riccardi–Tilli, and the rank-one stability theorem of Gómez–Guerra–Ramos–Tilli (2024). One older relevant full text could not be retrieved through the available access route; independent literature verification remains necessary.
