For every rank \(N\ge2\), the area-constrained second shape derivative of the Fock Toeplitz Ky Fan sum at a disc has explicit angular Fourier coefficients; its translation mode vanishes while the quadrupole has strictly positive curvature \(e^{-s}s^{N-1}(2s+N+1)/[(N-1)!(s+N)(s+N+1)]\). This supplies an exact rank-dependent local spectral stability coefficient not determined by the known global isoperimetric inequality.
