For a sparse-paving matroid base polytope, a basis's Gaussian normal-cone angle is \((1+t(B)/d)/\binom nr\), where \(t(B)\) counts neighboring circuit-hyperplanes in the Johnson graph and \(d=r(n-r)\). An explicit infinite pair of rank-three base polytopes shares its Tutte and Ehrhart polynomials, full face vector and two-direction collision probability, yet has different angular spectra and every collision probability involving at least three directions.
