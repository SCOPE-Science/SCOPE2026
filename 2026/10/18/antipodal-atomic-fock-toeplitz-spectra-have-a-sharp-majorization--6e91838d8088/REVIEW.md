# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The positive two-atom form factors through a two-coordinate weighted evaluation operator, so the nonzero spectrum is exactly a two-by-two reproducing-kernel Gram spectrum. The holomorphic and pluriharmonic kernels give diagonal entries \(1\) and \(2-u\) and off-diagonal entries \(u^2\) and \(u(2u-1)\). The normalized determinant difference factors with the sole root \(u=2-\sqrt3\) in \((0,1)\). At fixed trace, determinant ordering is exactly two-coordinate majorization. Strict convexity/concavity of powers supplies all Schatten orders. No asymptotic inference or finite enumeration is needed for the universal statement.

## Originality

PASS, subject to publication-priority assessment. Looi's 2026 general bounds and singleton sharpness examples establish the reference normalization, but not a relative spectral order for two locations. Jaguzović–Vujadinović's 2026 positive-measure results establish membership criteria; Fulsche's 2019 pluriharmonic work studies quantization and essential spectrum rather than positive atomic majorization. Searches for antipodal Fock Toeplitz Gram majorization, two-point Schatten crossover, and equivalent normalized two-atom kernels did not disclose an exact covering theorem. Because the argument is elementary once the kernels are available, an unlocated implicit computation in older reproducing-kernel literature remains a genuine priority risk.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The smallest anisotropic positive measure with two independent spatial evaluation channels already has a sharp transition in *every* convex spectral statistic, not merely an isolated norm counterexample. The single-atom amplification gives a natural reference, and the two-atom comparison changes direction exactly once; the critical Gaussian radius is independent of mass balance, dimension, and Schatten exponent. The exact isospectral critical point and reversed quasi-Schatten inequalities describe a coherent spectral phase diagram. This is an exact finite-rank classification, not a claimed global extremal theorem for arbitrary measures.

## Boundaries

The result is limited to two distinct antipodal positive atoms in Gaussian Fock spaces. The source kernel formulas are established mathematics, and the finite matrix computation is reproduced in the proof and independently diagnosed in `CHECK.py`. Publication priority for elementary special models cannot be fully guaranteed by searches.
