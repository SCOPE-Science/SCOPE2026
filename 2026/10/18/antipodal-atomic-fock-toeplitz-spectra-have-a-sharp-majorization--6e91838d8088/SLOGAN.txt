For positive Gaussian Fock Toeplitz measures supported on the antipodal pair \(\{a,-a\}\), normalizing the pluriharmonic operator by \(2-e^{-\|a\|^2/\alpha}\) creates a sharp spectral-majorization reversal at \(\|a\|^2/\alpha=\log(2+\sqrt3)\), independent of dimension and the two atomic masses. Every Schatten exponent above one follows the majorization direction, every exponent below one reverses it, and all spectra match at the critical radius.
