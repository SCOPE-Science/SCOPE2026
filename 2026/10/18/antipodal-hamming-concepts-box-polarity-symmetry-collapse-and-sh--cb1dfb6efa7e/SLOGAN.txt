For the coordinatewise-inequality context on \(q\)-ary words of length \(d\), all nontrivial concepts are proper Cartesian boxes, with exactly \(2+(2^q-2)^d\) concepts and a sharp binary symmetry collapse. When \(d\ge2\), ordinary Horn chaining needs optimal premise width \(q\), while one-pass inference needs width \(\max\{q,d\}\) for \(q\ge3\), producing an unbounded gap at fixed alphabet.
