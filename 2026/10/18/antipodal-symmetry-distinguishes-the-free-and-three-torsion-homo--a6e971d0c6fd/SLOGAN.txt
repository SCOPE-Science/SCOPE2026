For the ordinary matching complex of the icosahedral graph, antipodal inversion negates the unique \(\mathbb Z/3\) summand in integral third homology, while splitting the \(64\)-dimensional rational third homology into \(40\) even and \(24\) odd dimensions. The full graph automorphism action on three-torsion is the nontrivial order-two quotient character, so rotations fix the torsion and antipodal inversion reverses it.
