A sequential ecological succession model with \(n\) stages admits at least \(n-1\) distinct strict peaks of both Shannon and Gini–Simpson diversity as disturbance intensity varies, using one explicit robust hierarchy of forward-transition rates. Three stages are sharp for the first double peak; for three-stage Gini–Simpson diversity, the exact two-peak threshold is the ratio \(r_*\approx12.2393958693\) of the two forward-transition rates.
