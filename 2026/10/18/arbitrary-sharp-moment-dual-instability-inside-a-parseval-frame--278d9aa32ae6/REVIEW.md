# Review

## Correctness

PASS. The Parseval identity is coefficientwise exact; the universal lower bound for *all* bounded duals follows from the forced \(e_N\) coordinate in \(CU_0+DU_1=I\). The explicitly bounded dual \(U_N\) attains the bound exactly. Compactness of \(D\), positivity of all its diagonal entries, and weak nullity of the basis establish nonclosed infinite moment geometry and the sharp strong-versus-norm gap. Rational finite diagnostics agree with the exact identities but are not substitutes for these proofs.

## Originality

PASS at the scope of the joint realization claim, subject to publication-priority risk. Han–Mondal–Liu–Nour's arXiv:2610.11043v1 proves a finite-dimensional algebraic criterion; its future-work discussion leaves infinite-dimensional moment duals and optimization open. Classical positive-angle requirements are acknowledged from Koo–Lim (2015), not claimed new. Mondal–Verma–Mohapatra (2025) investigates finite erasures and minimal redundancy, a different optimization. Comparable public findings concern existence of Bessel duals or pointwise-to-global failure, not a Parseval family with every prescribed sharp finite-moment cost, an infeasible infinite moment limit and a sharp uniform-gap/strong-limit separation.

## Mathematical value

PASS. This is a quantitative boundary for an explicitly motivated extension of vanishing-moment duality: even perfect (Parseval) conditioning of the original frame gives no rate control over successively constrained duals. The theorem proves a complete arbitrary-growth realization, not merely one divergent example, and isolates the exact topological obstruction to passing from feasible finite moments to all moments. It makes a concrete trade-off relevant to infinite-dimensional structured reconstruction; no general frame classification is asserted.

The example is diagonal and coordinate structured; the underlying positive-angle principle is classical, and a broader historical theorem might subsume the realization after an additional construction. No independent mathematical audit or priority certification has occurred.
