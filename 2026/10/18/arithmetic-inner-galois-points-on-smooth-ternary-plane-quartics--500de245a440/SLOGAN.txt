Smooth plane quartics over \(\mathbf F_3\) with a rational, arithmetically Galois cubic projection comprise exactly \(24\,570\) projective equations in nineteen \(\operatorname{PGL}_3(\mathbf F_3)\)-orbits. Precisely \(234\) equations have four such centers, forming one orbit represented by \(X^4+YZ^3-Y^3Z=0\) with projective stabilizer \(\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbf F_3)\); the other \(24\,336\) have exactly one center.
