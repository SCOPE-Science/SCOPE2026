For a positive finite polynomial of the Hardy shift, every fixed-lag power difference has exactly one of three sharp arithmetic behaviors: Gaussian \(n^{-1/2}\) decay, an exact norm of \(2\), or a nonzero odd-root limit with a strictly positive \(n^{-1}\) correction. The lagged differences vanish exactly when the peripheral root order divides the lag, although the powers converge strongly to zero.
