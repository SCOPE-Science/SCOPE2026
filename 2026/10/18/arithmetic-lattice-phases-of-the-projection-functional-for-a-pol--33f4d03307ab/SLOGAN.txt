For the one-pole plus regular-\(n\)-gon weighted spherical \((2,2)\)-design in \(\mathbb R^3\), the spectral radius of its entrywise absolute Parseval Gram matrix has an explicit all-\(n\) formula. Its second-order convergence to the limiting frame projection functional oscillates with the irrational Bernoulli phase \(B_2(\{n\arccos(-1/4)/(2\pi)\})\), yielding exact distinct limsup and liminf constants.
