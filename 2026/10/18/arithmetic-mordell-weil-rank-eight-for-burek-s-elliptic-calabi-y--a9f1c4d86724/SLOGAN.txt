The elliptic Calabi–Yau threefold of Burek has arithmetic Mordell–Weil rank \(8\) over \(\mathbb Q(v,t)\), although its geometric rank is \(12\). Over \(\mathbb Q(i)(v,t)\), all twelve dimensions appear, and coefficientwise conjugation splits them into an eight-dimensional invariant part and a four-dimensional anti-invariant part.
