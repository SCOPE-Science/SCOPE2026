# Mathematical review: arithmetic resonance for floor-rotated lattices

## Correctness

**PASS.** The claim is confined to first-preimage multiplicities of coordinatewise floor quantization at primitive Pythagorean rotations. Every double fiber is a unique grid edge, because two source points in one unit target cell are at mutual distance less than \(\sqrt2\). Both edge directions have explicitly verified half-open collision rectangles. The finite phase group is a line of order \(q\); its ninety-degree invariance gives the bijection between nonzero horizontal collisions and vertical collisions, hence the odd numerator. A unimodular primitive Pythagorean parametrization produces a perpendicular pair of generators of length \(\sqrt q\) for the phase lattice, establishing its Voronoi radius and the stated uniform discrepancy estimate. The equal density of missing and double images follows from an \(O(M)\) boundary error in area-preserving rotation. All quantifiers, strict inequalities and boundary conventions are recorded in `RESULT.md`. The separate exact-arithmetic checker passes 110 ordered triples and finite target tests. Finite computations do not establish the limiting theorem; the argument does.

## Originality

**PASS for the rational-angle structural theorem, not for the generic benchmark.** Hannusch–Pethő's *Rotation on the digital plane* (DOI 10.1007/s10998-022-00480-8; sections 2–4, available full text) supplies positive lower bounds, a neighbor-pair classification, and a rational modular phase parametrization, but not an exact rational collision-density count, its universal odd numerator, or a uniform decay bound as the Pythagorean denominator grows. Vladimirov, *Quantized linear systems on integer lattices: a frequency-based approach* (arXiv:1501.04237), **already proves the generic collision/vacancy density** \((\cos\theta+\sin\theta-1)^2\) (Theorem 19(d), pp. 55–57) under nonresonance. This earlier result is explicitly acknowledged and excluded from novelty. Its hypotheses exclude the Pythagorean rational phase groups. The new structural content is the exact resonant all-triple modular law with parity and a uniform \(q^{-1/2}\) comparison, based on a square-lattice geometry not established by those sources. Related earlier discretization studies, especially Kozyakin–Kuznetsov–Pokrovskii–Vladimirov (1997), create residual priority risk, but no decisive equivalent statement was located in the material inspected.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The density of missing image pixels is an intrinsic measure of noninvertibility in a canonical quantized rotation. Both the source study and earlier frequency-based analysis ask for quantitative loss of surjectivity/injectivity. The rational-angle cases form a dense arithmetic family excluded from the generic theorem, not an arbitrary finite slice. The all-triple formula, parity obstruction and uniform square-root denominator bound describe how rational resonance affects that invariant; the \((3,4,5)\) example concretely separates the true rational frequency \(1/5\) from the neighboring nonresonant value \(4/25\). The value is this quantitative rational-resonance classification; no higher-iterate or orbit-period theorem is inferred.
