For a planar rotation discretized by coordinatewise floor, every primitive Pythagorean angle has an exact rational frequency of missing and double-hit pixels: \(\delta=(2H-1)/q\), where \(H\) is an explicit one-variable modular count, so the numerator is always odd. The exceptional density differs from the known generic law by at most \(4\sqrt2/\sqrt q+4/q\), yet the \((3,4,5)\) angle exhibits a genuine jump from the generic benchmark \(4/25\) to \(1/5\).
