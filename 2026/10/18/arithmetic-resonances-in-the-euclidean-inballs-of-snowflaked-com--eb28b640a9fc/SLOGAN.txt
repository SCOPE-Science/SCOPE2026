For balanced snowflaked complete-bipartite finite metrics, the exact Euclidean inradius of the Lipschitz-free unit ball is controlled by a finite arithmetic approximation problem \(\Delta_m(t)=\min_{a,b}|at+(2-t)b-m|\). Its tangent-facet counts and maximal-center dimensions show rational resonances: even-sized cases generically have a one-dimensional family of centers, but selected rational snowflake parameters force a unique center.
