For the rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) with \(m,n\ge2\), open-independent open-locating-dominating sets exist only on three explicit arithmetic families of rectangle sizes, and every such set consists of row/column-disjoint selected edges. Their exact labeled counts are factorial formulas; in particular, a square \(m\times m\) admits such a set exactly when \(3\mid m\), in which case all solutions have \(4m/3\) selected vertices.
