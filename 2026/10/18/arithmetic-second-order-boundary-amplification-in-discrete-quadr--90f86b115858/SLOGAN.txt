For equally weighted quadratic least squares at \(m+1\) equally spaced points, the **sharp boundary-response norm** has an exact Bernoulli-root formula and tends to \(1+12\sqrt6/25\). After removing its leading \(m^{-1}\) correction, the scaled \(m^{-2}\) remainder has the complete nontrivial limit interval \([227\sqrt6/50,377\sqrt6/50]\), caused by irrational zero-crossings in the discrete reproducing kernel.
