For positive Helson matrices from any fixed finite collection of distinct atoms shifted right by \(R\), every positive eigenvalue has an explicit asymptotic: the \(k\)-th is \(k^{-2R}\) times a ratio of consecutive leading Gram determinants, with an exponentially small relative error. The universal arithmetic decay bases \(1,2,\ldots,m\) determine the sharp rank-approximation hierarchy independently of the atom locations and weights.
