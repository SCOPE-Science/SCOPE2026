For infinitesimal chord tomography of a planar disk, finitely many offsets give a stable reconstruction exactly when their angles satisfy an integer relation with odd coefficient sum. The coupled offsets \(\cos\alpha\) and \(\cos(2\alpha)\) always suffice, with the sharp lower \(L^2\) coefficient \(1+\csc^2\alpha\) for irrational \(\alpha/\pi\), while rational offsets obey an exact dyadic kernel classification.
