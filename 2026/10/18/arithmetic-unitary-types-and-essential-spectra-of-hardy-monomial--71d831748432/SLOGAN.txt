Every Hardy-space product \(C_{z^k}^*C_{z^m}\) has one of only four exact unitary types: the identity, an infinite-multiplicity shift, an infinite-multiplicity co-shift, or an infinite-multiplicity sum of **every** finite truncated shift length. In particular, any unequal monomial exponents produce the entire closed unit disk as both spectrum and Fredholm essential spectrum, with sharp polynomial norm **and essential norm** equal to the disk supremum.
