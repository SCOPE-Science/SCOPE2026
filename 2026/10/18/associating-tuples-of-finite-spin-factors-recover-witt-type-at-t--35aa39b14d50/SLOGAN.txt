For odd finite fields, the number of ordered pairs generating an associative subalgebra of an even-dimensional spin-factor Jordan algebra distinguishes the two Witt signs, with explicit difference \(2q^{m+1}(q^2-1)(q^{2m-2}-1)\). All higher-arity counts follow from isotropic-plane incidence, while the reduced associating locus combines the rank-one determinantal variety with the previously known orthogonal nullcone.
