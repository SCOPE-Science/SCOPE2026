For four Gaussian Weyl shifts at the corners of a rectangle with small fixed symplectic area \(u/\pi\), the maximal Gram determinant is \(c_*u^4+O(u^6)\), where \(c_*=0.05282488254575485\ldots\) is the unique maximum of an explicit one-variable function. The two optimal rectangles become singularly asymmetric with one side tending to \(0.87087631712409\ldots\), whereas the balanced rectangle has determinant only \(u^6/3+O(u^7)\).
