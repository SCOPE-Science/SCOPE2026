For a uniform \(n\)-chord matching, the crossing-pair and nesting-pair counts have exact correlation \(-5/(2(n+3))\), yet their centered \(n^{3/2}\)-scale fluctuations converge jointly to independent Gaussians. The key is an exact orthogonality of one-chord influences: all mixed covariance comes from the impossibility of simultaneously crossing and nesting within one chord pair.
