In Berná's logarithmic quasi-Banach space, the distance of a positive \(m\)-coordinate block to arbitrarily late coordinate tails is asymptotically exactly \(2m\), settling the sharp end of the previously known interval from \(m\) to \(2m\). Quantitatively, the deficit from \(2m\) is at most \(O_\theta(m\log\log m/\log m)\), and the block quasi-norm is asymptotically \((\theta/2)\log m\) times its tail-distance gauge.
