A single exact ProxSPS step on a strongly convex quadratic can generate a primitive attracting period-three orbit, even with the correct loss lower bound: at \(\alpha=5\) and \(\lambda=1/20\), the iterates cycle through \(8/9\), \(17/18\), and \(35/36\). The explicit three-cycle persists on an open parameter region and forces points of every period and Li–Yorke chaos on an invariant interval; this does not imply that typical trajectories are chaotic.
