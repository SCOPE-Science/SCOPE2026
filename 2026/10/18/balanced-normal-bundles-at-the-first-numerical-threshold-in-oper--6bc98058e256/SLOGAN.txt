For every \(k\ge2\), a general degree-\(4k-1\) rational curve in \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,3k+1)\) has balanced normal bundle \(\mathcal O(2k+1)^{\oplus(5k-3)}\oplus\mathcal O(2k+2)^{\oplus k}\). An explicit pair of disjoint Veronese blocks proves this infinite series at the first integer beyond the Coskun–Larson–Vogt tangent-bundle obstruction.
