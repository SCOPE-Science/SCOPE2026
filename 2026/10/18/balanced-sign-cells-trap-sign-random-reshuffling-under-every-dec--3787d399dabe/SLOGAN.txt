When component-gradient signs balance throughout a region, unscaled sign random reshuffling with any decreasing vanishing stepsize has summable epoch drift and can remain trapped away from stationarity for every shuffle order. Two smooth strongly convex quadratics give such a nonstationary limit even under Robbins–Monro steps, with the harmonic schedule producing an exact nondegenerate Rademacher-series limit of variance \(\pi^2/6-2\log2\).
