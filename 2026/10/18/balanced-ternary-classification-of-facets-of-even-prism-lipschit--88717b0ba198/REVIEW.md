# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The unit ball of the real Lipschitz-free space for unweighted connected graph metric equals the symmetric edge polytope, since every nonedge molecule is a convex combination of unit-length edge molecules along a shortest path. For even n>=4 the prism C_n□K_2 is connected planar bipartite, so D’Alì–Delucchi–Michałek Proposition 4.33 equates facets with nowhere-zero integer 2-flows (signed ±1) on the dual n-gonal bipyramid. With ring signs c_i and two apex-spoke signs a_i,b_i, conservation is a_i+b_i=c_(i-1)-c_i; x_i=(a_i-b_i)/2 lies in {-1,0,1}, equals zero precisely at ring-sign flips. Both apex constraints are equivalent to sum x_i=0. Balanced ternary words have an even number of nonzero symbols and, because n is even, an even number of zeros, so any initial ring sign closes cyclically and produces exactly one flow; both choices give distinct facets. Thus exactly 2*[z^0](z^-1+1+z)^n facets, and Laplace/Fourier analysis gives sqrt(3/(pi*n))*3^n for even n. Independently enumerating signed bipyramid 2-flows for n=4 and 6 produced 38 and 282, and independently computing coefficients gave 38, 282, 2214, 17906, 147578, 1232454 for n=4,6,8,10,12,14; finite checks are only diagnostics, not the all-n proof.

## originality

PASS

The general planar bipartite facet/dual nowhere-zero 2-flow correspondence is explicitly prior work and is not claimed new, nor is the symmetric edge polytope identification. The surviving contribution is the complete even-circular-ladder evaluation and an explicit reversible parameterization of its dual bipyramid flows by one sign and a balanced ternary word, plus sharp growth. The 2019 D’Alì et al outerplanar facet formula does not cover circular ladders, and Chen–Davis–Korchevskaia gives general facet-subgraph descriptions rather than this explicit count. The central trinomial sequence itself is classical, but its identification with these facets via the flow bijection is mathematically additional.

## value

PASS

Facet numbers are a natural combinatorial-geometric invariant of Lipschitz-free unit balls; even circular ladders form a canonical infinite graph family with a planar dual of manageable but nontrivial cyclic topology. A closed formula, full balanced-word structural classification and n-dependent sharp asymptotic furnish independently useful data beyond general facet-flow duality and beyond a handful of small graph instances. The zero-sum/cyclic parity constraint is a mathematically meaningful distinction from open-ladder formulas.
