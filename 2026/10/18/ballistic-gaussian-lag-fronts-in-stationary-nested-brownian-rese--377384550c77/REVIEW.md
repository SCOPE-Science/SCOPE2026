# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The stationary construction traces each coordinate through finitely many independent exponential reset intervals to the anchored first site. Generator identities give the stationary first-site variance and the first-to-downstream equal-time covariances exactly, with no unsupported closure approximation. The finite triangular Markov semigroup then yields both unequal-time covariance formulas. The first derivative is controlled by a strictly decreasing positive finite sum, proving uniqueness of the maximum. A summably dominated falling-factorial expansion locates its root at \(r t_j=j-2+O(j^{-1})\); Stirling's formula and a geometrically dominated sum give the uniform compact-window Gaussian asymptotic and peak height. Direct gamma integration gives the time-integrated asymmetry. The included checker passes 1546 deterministic finite tests, which are supporting checks and not substitutes for the analytic proof.

## Originality — PASS, with specified risks

The 2025 primary source establishes stationary one-time marginals and same-time spatial correlations, including the exponential decay of \(\operatorname{Cov}(X_1,X_j)\); the claimed advance is instead the complete stationary **unequal-time**, causal cross-covariance, its unique lag optimum and algebraic/Gaussian front. The 2026 follow-up analyzes relaxation of one-time distributions toward stationarity with its own propagation delay. It does not, in the accessible abstract and indexed synopsis, assert the stationary two-time crosscovariance formula or the sharp large-distance Gaussian profile. However, its full text was inaccessible, so relevant material in the body or appendix remains a genuine risk. The general linear semigroup identity is standard and not presented as novel by itself. Searches for synonymous lagged, time-asymmetric, and directed-copying formulations and comparisons against accessible earlier findings identified no covering result; that absence is not a proof of uniqueness. No independent scientific literature review has been performed.

## Value — PASS

The motivating system is explicitly intended to model lossy downstream information propagation. The difference between an exponentially decaying equal-time correlation and a much stronger optimally delayed correlation is a natural and measurable finite-network question. The theorem determines the exact observation lag, fluctuation-scale delay profile, decay exponent, and integrated directional ratio, rather than reporting a routine fixed-index correlation. It also clearly separates stationary temporal memory from the distinct one-time relaxation question of the 2026 follow-up.
