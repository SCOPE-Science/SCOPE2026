In a stationary Brownian copying chain, the covariance between the first site now and a far downstream site later has an exact directional kernel whose unique best delay grows linearly with distance. Its best-lag signal decays only as \(j^{-1/2}\), with a Gaussian delay front, although the simultaneous covariance decays as \(2^{-j}\); the integrated downstream/upstream correlation ratio is exactly \(2^j-1\).
