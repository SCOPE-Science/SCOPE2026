Whenever an odd-order sign matrix reaches the sharp Barba determinant bound, every pair of lower Auerbach vectors of real \(\ell_1^n\) has an explicit, non-regular hexagonal two-dimensional norm, forcing every minimal-volume sufficient enlargement to be a parallelepiped. This settles an infinite family of previously untreated odd dimensions, including \(n=5\) and \(n=2p^2+2p+1\) for primes \(p\equiv3\pmod4\).
