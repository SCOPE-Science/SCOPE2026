Dense strong Kripke relations on a fixed \(n\)-world intuitionistic chain are canonically coded by partitions of \(n+1\) points: there are \(B_{n+1}\) raw frames, with \(\left\{\begin{smallmatrix}n+1\\k+1\end{smallmatrix}\right\}\) having exactly \(k\) reflexive worlds. All such frames realize exactly \(2^n\) different nuclei, and every nucleus has an explicit multiplicative fibre count determined by its reflexive worlds.
