For complete-bipartite symmetric edge polytopes, uniformly random Euclidean directional widths exhibit a sharp regime change: a fixed small part yields a Gaussian absolute-maximum limit, while two growing parts yield the maximum of two independent weighted Gumbel sums. At balanced logarithmic growth the exact limit CDF is \(4e^{-x}K_1(2e^{-x/2})^2\), whereas extreme imbalance produces a shifted Gumbel law.
