For every complete-bipartite unit-edge metric with part sizes \(p,q\ge2\), the uniform dual Lipschitz ball has an exact \(\operatorname{Beta}(p-1,q+1)\) range disintegration and a three-eigenvalue covariance with numerators \((p+1)(p+2)\), \((q+1)(q+2)\), and \(pq+2\). Its isotropic constant tends uniformly to \(1/\sqrt{12}\), while fixed-part growth produces an independent Gamma–Gaussian joint limit.
