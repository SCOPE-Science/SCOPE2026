# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The insertion ranks form an independent sequence because the inversion count is their additive inversion-code total. Each descent indicator depends on precisely two adjacent insertion ranks, while a record indicator depends on one. This proves all off-band zeros. The two nonzero bands follow from conditioning on a maximal prefix rank or on a new record. An elementary row-sum identity yields the strictly negative aggregate for every \(q>0\), including the first-row boundary and the uniform specialization. Exhaustive exact-rational enumeration of all permutations through size seven at three distinct positive parameters independently checks every entry; no finite computation is used as the proof of an infinite family.

## Originality — PASS, subject to stated residual risk
The closest established structural tools are the Mallows insertion process and its independence-of-induced-orderings theorem (Bhatnagar–Peled), and general \(q\)-exchangeability (Gnedin–Olshanski). These establish the model and generic order properties, not the signed two-index cross-covariance map. The Carlitz–Scoville and Dong–Lin–Pan Stirling–Eulerian polynomials record global counts; their displayed results do not specify the record-position/descent-position matrix. Published analyses of local inversion/descent and fixed-point/descent interactions involve different conditioning events. Searches for the record/descent pair, positional aliases, inversion-weighted parameters, and the closed uniform covariance did not produce a prior identical matrix statement. This is best-knowledge originality, not proof that no equivalent formulation exists in the literature.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Record counts and descents are fundamental, non-equivalent statistics of rankings. Their dependence under Mallows bias is not determined by the separate record and descent marginals, and the exact finite-parameter local classification distinguishes a positive same-position influence from a negative next-position influence. Its rowwise cancellation proves strict aggregate antagonism at every bias level, gives an explicit calibration formula for joint rank statistics, and separates local association from global covariance. The object and the full two-index invariant are natural, rather than an arbitrary finite parameter slice.
