For a Mallows permutation, a record at position \(j\) correlates with the descent at position \(i\) only when \(j=i\) or \(j=i+1\), with opposite signs on the two diagonals. The cancellation is always negative in total: the record count and descent count have exact covariance \(-\frac{q}{1+q}\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}([i]_q[i+1]_q)^{-1}\) for every \(q>0\).
