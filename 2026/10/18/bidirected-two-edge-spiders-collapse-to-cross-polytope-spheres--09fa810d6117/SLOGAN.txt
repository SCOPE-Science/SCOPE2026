The multipath complex of the bidirected tree with \(n\) identical two-edge arms collapses simplicially onto the boundary of the \(n\)-cross-polytope, so its integral homotopy type is \(S^{n-1}\) for every \(n\ge1\). Permuting the arms acts on top integral homology by the sign character, while reversing all arrows acts by \((-1)^n\); the genuinely branching cases begin at \(n=3\).
