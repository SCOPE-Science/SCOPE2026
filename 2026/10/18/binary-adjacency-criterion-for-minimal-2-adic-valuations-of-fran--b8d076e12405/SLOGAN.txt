A Franel number \(f_n=\sum_k\binom nk^3\) has the smallest universally guaranteed 2-adic valuation, \(s_2(n)\), exactly when the binary expansion of \(n\) contains no adjacent ones. Among \(0\le n<2^L\), precisely \(F_{L+2}\) indices achieve equality; the proof counts exactly \(2^{t(n)}\) minimum-valuation terms in Strehl's binomial identity.
