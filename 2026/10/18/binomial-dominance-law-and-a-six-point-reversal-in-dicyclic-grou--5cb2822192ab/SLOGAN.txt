For each fixed size \(k\), a random \(k\)-subset of the dicyclic group has an explicit binomial limiting law for \(|AA|-|AA^{-1}|\). At \(k=6\), quotient-dominant sets occur with limiting proportion \(22/64\), versus \(21/64\) product-dominant, and this reversal persists for every fixed \(k\ge6\), contradicting a published eventual-dominance conjecture.
