For the projective toric variety of the \(n\)-cycle's graphical zonotope, every fixed-point affine chart is the explicit binomial hypersurface \(\prod_{i=1}^k a_i=\prod_{j=1}^{n-k}b_j\). The reduced singular locus is pure of codimension three for \(n\ge4\), with exactly \(2^n-2n-2\) singular torus-fixed points; all these varieties are locally complete intersections and Gorenstein.
