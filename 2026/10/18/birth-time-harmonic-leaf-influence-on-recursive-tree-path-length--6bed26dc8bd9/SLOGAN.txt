In a uniform recursive tree, requiring vertex \(j\) to remain a leaf lowers expected total path length by exactly \(H_{n-1}-H_{j-1}\), and the covariance with its leaf indicator is \(\frac{j-1}{n-1}(H_{j-1}-H_{n-1})\). The age-resolved negative dependence is strongest near birth fraction \(e^{-1}\); summing over labels gives the exact covariance \(\operatorname{Cov}(P_n,L_n)=-(n-2)/4\).
