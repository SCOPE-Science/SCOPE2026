# Scientific review

## Correctness: PASS

The equivalence between forward/backward confluence and weak monotonicity of extremal row/column profiles is proven in both directions, including empty rows and columns. The feasible-profile conditions are necessary and sufficient and guarantee the forced skeleton is contained in the maximal envelope. Every intermediate relation has the designated extrema; the resulting disjoint Boolean-cube partition gives the entire arrow-count polynomial. The bottom-right edge proof gives the sharp uniform minimum-representative bound, and every free cell is confined to the northwest rectangle. The all-size proof is symbolic. Independently implemented finite tests exhaust 74,650 rectangular Boolean matrices against the quantified definition, all square matrices through five worlds, and profile counts through six worlds. Residual correctness risk concerns implementation of the stated semantics only; no hidden computer certificate is needed.

## Originality: PASS, with residual risk

Marin–Padhiar–Shillito (2026, Definition 2.3) supply the two confluence conditions and study proof theory, soundness, completeness and realisation; they do not supply a finite-chain profile classification or enumeration. Balbiani–Gao–Gencer–Olivetti (2024) require only forward confluence and construct an axiomatization and calculus. De Groot–Shillito–Clouston (2025) study related semantics. These primary sources were examined beyond abstracts. Semantic-result and equivalent-matrix searches and previously recorded claims were compared by the specific mathematical statements and did not reveal coverage of the enumerator and full fiber decomposition. A prior equivalent classification in binary-matrix or order-relation enumeration terminology remains a genuine risk. The confluence definition and its elementary extrema reduction are not themselves represented as novel.

## Value: PASS

Finite Kripke frames supply canonical test cases for modal semantic investigations, but a raw binary-relation search grows with the number of possible arrows. This result organizes the complete two-sided confluent frame class into uniquely indexed combinatorial Boolean cubes and provides an exact, arrow-graded generating polynomial for every finite carrier size. Every fiber has a canonical smallest admissible representative, with a sharp linear size guarantee, while its remaining variability is counted exactly. The result thus supplies usable classification and enumeration beyond isolated small-cardinality tables. It does not conflate equality of extremal profiles with modal equivalence.
