For every finite linear-word flat semiring \(A_m\), idempotent endomorphisms are precisely projections deleting selected letters: their composition is subset intersection, and there are exactly \(2^m\). Their complete image-rank generating function is \((1+B)/(1-t-tB)\), where \(B=\sum_{\ell\ge1}t^\ell z^{\ell(\ell+1)/2}\).
