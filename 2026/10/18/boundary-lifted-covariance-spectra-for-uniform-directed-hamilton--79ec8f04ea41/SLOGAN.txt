The complete covariance spectrum of the directed-edge incidence vector of a uniform Hamiltonian path has four eigenvalues; closing the path into a cycle annihilates exactly \(2(n-1)\) additional boundary-sensitive modes. For any centered directed edge-weight array, its random open-path cost has sharp variance between \(\|H\|_F^2/[n(n-1)]\) and \((n+1)\|H\|_F^2/[n(n-1)]\), with equality precisely for vertex potentials and antisymmetric circulations, respectively.
