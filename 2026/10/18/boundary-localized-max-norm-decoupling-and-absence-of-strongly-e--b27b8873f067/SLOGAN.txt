At every complex BMO function, unit-norm analytic Blaschke perturbations concentrate at a Lebesgue boundary point and obey the exact max law \(\|f+t h_r\|_G\to\max\{\|f\|_G,|t|\}\). Consequently the Garsia unit balls of BMO, BMOA, VMO, and VMOA modulo constants have no strongly extreme points, despite the existence of many ordinary extreme points.
