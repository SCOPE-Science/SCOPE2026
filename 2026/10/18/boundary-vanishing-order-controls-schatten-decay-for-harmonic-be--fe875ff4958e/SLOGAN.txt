For radial multipliers on weighted harmonic Bergman spaces, a nonzero boundary term of order \((1-|z|^2)^\beta\) forces \(s_n([M_u,P_\alpha])\sim4^\beta|v(1)|\kappa_{\alpha,\beta}n^{-\beta}\), including the fourfold angular multiplicity. This gives the exact Schatten cutoff \(p>1/\beta\) and a logarithmic critical trace residue.
