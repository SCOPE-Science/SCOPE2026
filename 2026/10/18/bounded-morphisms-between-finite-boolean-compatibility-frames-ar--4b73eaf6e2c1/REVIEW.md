# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The result is a complete theorem with all positive integer parameters quantified. It derives singleton preservation from a direct forth/back contradiction, reconstructs every subset image using both conditions, derives surjectivity without presupposing it, and verifies the converse with an explicit back witness. The unique atom map provides the counting bijection and categorical composition identity. The independently written checker exhausts the unrestricted finite function space for \(n,m\le3\), confirming \(826{,}113\) candidate maps, and verifies \(852\) induced surjection maps through six atoms. The proof, not those finite cases, covers arbitrary sizes.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge)

Jockwich, Tarafder, and Venturi, *The Internal Modal Logic of Forcing* (arXiv:2607.25977), introduce the compatibility relation and Lemma 6.13 proves existence of partition-induced Boolean embeddings. The source's accessible full-text Sections 3–7 were inspected for relevant implications; those embeddings do not classify arbitrary bounded morphisms of the full nonzero frames. The closest published finite-hierarchy study proves the maximum complete reflexive quotient size, not a classification of maps between compatibility frames. Actual specialized database and literature searches under p-morphism, locally surjective homomorphism, compatibility relation, and intersection graph terms identified no equivalent or dominating statement. Absence from those searches is not a proof of uniqueness; an unindexed equivalent general graph result remains possible.

## Value — PASS

The result resolves a natural structural homomorphism problem in a recently studied family of modal frames, beyond existence of Boolean embeddings: it identifies **every** bounded morphism, proves automatic surjectivity, computes the hom-set exactly for all sizes, and identifies the full endomorphism and automorphism monoids. These outcomes help organize modal quotient comparisons and are not an arbitrary small finite census or a mere renaming of the Stirling numbers.

## Limits and independent status

Only complete finite nonzero power-set compatibility frames are treated; induced subframes and infinite-set cases are not covered. The checker is an ancillary finite verification. Literature coverage cannot certify global priority, and no independent assessment has been performed.
