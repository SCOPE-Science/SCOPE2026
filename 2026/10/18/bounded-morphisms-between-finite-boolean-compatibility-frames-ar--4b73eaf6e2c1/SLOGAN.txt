For the finite Kripke frame of nonempty subsets of \([n]\) with intersection accessibility, every bounded morphism to the corresponding \(m\)-atom frame is induced by a unique surjection of the underlying atoms. Thus there are exactly \(m!\left\{\begin{smallmatrix}n\\m\end{smallmatrix}\right\}\) such morphisms, and every bounded endomorphism is a permutation automorphism.
