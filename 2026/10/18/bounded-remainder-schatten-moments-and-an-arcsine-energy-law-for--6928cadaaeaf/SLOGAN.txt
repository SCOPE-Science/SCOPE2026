All even Schatten moments of the finite Cesàro matrix have dimension-uniform additive errors around \(\binom{2k-2}{k-1}H_n\). Weighting each squared singular value by its contribution to the Hilbert–Schmidt energy produces a spectral probability measure converging to the arcsine law on \([0,4]\), with explicit \(O(H_n^{-1})\) errors for polynomial tests.
