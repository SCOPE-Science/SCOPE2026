When a propositional dependence atom must be resolved by a global disjunction of classical formulas only on teams of size at most \(r\), its exact minimum number of disjuncts is the binary covering-array number of strength \(\min\{r,2^k\}\) on \(2^k\) columns for \(r\ge2\); singleton teams need only one tautological disjunct. Consequently, the two-team width is \(k+\tfrac12\log_2 k+O(1)\), while unrestricted-sized teams require \(2^{2^k}\) disjuncts.
