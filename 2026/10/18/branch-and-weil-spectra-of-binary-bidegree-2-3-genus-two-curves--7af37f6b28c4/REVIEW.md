## originality

PASS

Best-of-knowledge originality of the precise final claim, with prior partial/general coverage credited. Faber–Grantham already cover the upper bound N1=6 and exhibit extremal genus-two curves; Howe–Nart–Ritzenthaler classifies abstract Jacobian isogeny/Weil possibilities. Neither counts smooth (2,3) equations modulo the 36-element ambient ruling group nor simultaneous N1,N2,geometric branch and alternate ruling ramification. Those known corollaries are credited, not claimed as original. Search failure alone is not proof of novelty, and explicit residual risks remain.
