# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Neumann symmetric and Dirichlet antisymmetric edge modes give K_ij=δ_ij g_t(x-y)+(2/m-δ_ij)g_t(x+y). Differentiate Gaussian to obtain q(z)=(z²-2)e^{-z²/4}/(8√π), scale x,y by √t, and integrate absolute kernel row: F_m(x) exactly. Symmetry gives same L1 norm. For m=2, partition of the real line yields C2=√(2/π)e^-1/2. With α=1-2/m, |a-αb|+(1+α)|b| increases strictly wherever ab<0; a=q(x-y),b=q(x+y) have an open sign-opposite y-interval for every x>0. Uniform gap ≤2C2/m gives interior maxima, compactness and monotonic constants. The difference quotient D_m(x) converges even at moving maximizing positions by dominated convergence on a compact set; optimization yields C∞-C_m=κ/m+o(1/m), κ>0. Independent Gaussian quadrature at x=0,.25,1.45,4 and m=2,3,4,8,∞ verifies monotone sample (F∞(1.45)≈0.71492), not global maximization.

## originality

PASS

Kostrykin–Potthoff–Schrader furnishes the Kirchhoff graph kernel, an acknowledged ingredient; Frank–Kovařík supplies heat kernel estimates on metric trees. Their stated results do not by themselves optimize the signed derivative row integrals or imply strict degree ordering and κ. Exact priority is provisional for other time-derivative norm literature.
