For the smooth free sign quotients of split-quadric Calabi–Yau complete intersections in every even complex dimension \(n\ge4\), the integral third cohomology and Brauer group both vanish. The Picard group is \(\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/2\), with its nontrivial torsion class given by the canonical bundle; the result contrasts with the nonzero Brauer group of an Enriques surface.
