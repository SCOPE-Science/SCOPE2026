For every complete bipartite graph \(K_{a,b}\) with \(a,b\ge2\), its symmetric edge polytope has John shape \(L/(ab)\) and Löwner shape \((a+b-1)L/(ab)\) in the intrinsic zero-sum Euclidean space. Every facet touches the inner ellipsoid, every vertex touches the outer one, and their homothety realizes the sharp symmetric John radius ratio \(\sqrt{a+b-1}\).
