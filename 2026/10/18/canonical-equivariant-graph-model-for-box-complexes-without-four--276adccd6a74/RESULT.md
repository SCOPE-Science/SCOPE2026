# Canonical equivariant graph model for box complexes without four-cycles

## Finding
Let \(G\) be a finite connected simple graph with minimum degree at least two and **no four-cycle** (four-cycles are forbidden whether or not induced). The graph-homomorphism CW complex \(X=\operatorname{Hom}(K_2,G)\) has a canonical embedded graph \(Y\) that is the once-subdivided canonical bipartite double cover \(B=K_2\times G\). Inclusion \(Y\hookrightarrow X\) is an \(\operatorname{Aut}(G)\times C_2\)-equivariant strong deformation retraction, where \(C_2\) exchanges the two input coordinates.

Writing \(n=|V(G)|\), \(m=|E(G)|\), and \(c\) for the number of connected components of \(B\) (one if \(G\) is nonbipartite, two if bipartite), the integral homology is
\[
H_0(X;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z^c,\qquad H_1(X;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z^{2m-2n+c},\qquad H_j(X;\mathbb Z)=0\quad(j\ge2).
\]

For any graph automorphism \(g\), let \(f(g)\) count its fixed vertices, \(e(g)\) count edges between fixed vertices, and \(s(g)\) count edges whose two endpoints are exchanged by \(g\). With \(\epsilon\in\{0,1\}\) denoting absence/presence of coordinate swap, let \(h_0(g,\epsilon)\) count connected components of \(B\) preserved by the combined action. The **entire first rational homology character** is
\[
\operatorname{tr}((g,0)|H_1(X;\mathbb Q))=h_0(g,0)-2f(g)+2e(g),
\]
\[
\operatorname{tr}((g,1)|H_1(X;\mathbb Q))=h_0(g,1)-2s(g).
\]
For connected nonbipartite \(G\), both \(h_0\)-terms are \(1\). For connected bipartite \(G\), each is \(2\) if and only if the coordinate-swap parity equals the bipartition-swap parity of \(g\), otherwise \(0\). The geometric orbit quotient \(X/C_2\) is homotopy equivalent to \(G\).

## Assumptions and scope
The polyhedral Hom complex has a cell \(\Delta(A)\times\Delta(D)\) for every ordered pair of nonempty vertex sets \(A,D\) with all edges between them in \(G\). It is the standard homomorphism complex, not the directed path or ordinary independence complex. Every connected finite graph meeting the stated conditions is included, with or without triangles and with arbitrary maximum degree. Automorphisms preserve the graph and act diagonally, and the extra involution swaps \(A,D\). An abstract connected graph of minimum degree at least two and no four-cycle is either bipartite or nonbipartite as usual.

## Proof
**1. Cell decomposition.** If both \(|A|\ge2\) and \(|D|\ge2\), selecting two elements of each part gives a copy of \(K_{2,2}\), hence a four-cycle in \(G\), contrary to hypothesis. Thus every cell belongs to a **left star** \(L_v=\Delta(\{v\})\times\Delta(N_G(v))\) or a **right star** \(R_w=\Delta(N_G(w))\times\Delta(\{w\})\), each a simplex. Distinct left stars, or distinct right stars, are disjoint. A left star \(L_v\) and right star \(R_w\) meet precisely in the single oriented-edge vertex \(p_{vw}=(\{v\},\{w\})\) when \(vw\in E(G)\), and otherwise do not meet. Minimum degree at least two ensures each simplex center is distinct from its peripheral vertices.

**2. Canonical invariant spine.** Within every star simplex \(S\) take the union of straight segments from its barycenter \(b_S\) to each vertex of \(S\); call this radial tree \(T_S\). The finite symmetric group permuting its vertices preserves \(T_S\). For every subgroup \(H\) of that symmetric group, the fixed set \(S^H\) is nonempty and convex; \(T_S^H\) is a star with center \(b_S\), hence nonempty and contractible. After an invariant stellar subdivision, the inclusion \(T_S\hookrightarrow S\) is a finite equivariant CW cofibration, a homotopy equivalence on the fixed sets of **every** subgroup. The equivariant Whitehead theorem and the equivariant homotopy-extension property therefore give an equivariant strong deformation retraction of \(S\) onto \(T_S\). In particular the retraction fixes all original simplex vertices. Choose the same symmetric retraction on each dimension of simplex and transport it to all left and right stars. Their intersections are original vertices, so these homotopies agree on overlaps. They combine into an \(\operatorname{Aut}(G)\times C_2\)-equivariant strong deformation retraction onto \(Y=\bigcup_v T_{L_v}\cup\bigcup_w T_{R_w}\).

**3. Identify the graph.** The vertices of \(Y\) are \(b_{L_v}\), \(b_{R_w}\), and \(p_{vw}\) for each ordered adjacent pair \((v,w)\). Each \(p_{vw}\) has exactly two edges, to \(b_{L_v}\) and \(b_{R_w}\). Suppressing the degree-two vertices gives exactly the graph with left vertices \(v\), right vertices \(w\), and edges \((v,w)\) for adjacent \(v,w\) in \(G\), namely \(B=K_2\times G\). The exchange of Hom coordinates switches left and right parts; graph automorphisms act simultaneously in both parts. Hence identification respects the full product group. Taking fixed points for any subgroup, integral homology, and orbit quotients preserves the stated consequences of an equivariant deformation retraction.

**4. Homology and character.** The cover \(B\) has \(2n\) vertices and \(2m\) edges. It is connected when \(G\) is nonbipartite, and has two components when \(G\) is connected and bipartite. Its graph-chain complex computes the integral homology as above; in particular the cycle lattice is free abelian. Over \(\mathbb Q\), the exact sequence of graph chains gives \([H_1]=[H_0]-[C_0]+[C_1]\) in the representation ring. Orient each cover edge from the left layer to the right layer. Under \((g,0)\), exactly \(2e(g)\) oriented edges remain fixed, all with sign \(+1\), and exactly \(2f(g)\) vertices remain fixed. Under \((g,1)\), fixed unoriented cover edges correspond to oriented adjacency pairs whose endpoints are exchanged by \(g\): there are \(2s(g)\) and each reverses orientation, contributing \(-1\); no cover vertex remains fixed. The \(H_0\) character counts invariant components, yielding both formulas. The quotient of \(B\) by the layer exchange is canonically the original graph \(G\).

## Verification
A standalone Python verifier builds all polyhedral Hom cells and independently computes reduced boundary ranks over \(\mathbb F_2\), the Kronecker-cover component counts, and complete chain Lefschetz characters. It checked cycles of lengths five through eight, the triangle, two triangles with a common apex (maximum degree four), the Petersen graph (all \(120\) graph automorphisms), and the Heawood graph. All recorded checks passed. Run `python verify.py`; the expected output is stored in `checks.txt`. The finite tests are not a proof of the universal assertion; the proof is the cell decomposition and invariant-spine argument above.

## Relationship to prior work
Shukla (2018 preprint; 2019 publication) classified ordinary neighborhood-complex homotopy types for graphs of maximum degree at most three, without supplying this complete product-group equivariant graph model or its general trace formula. Matsushita (2014 preprint, revised 2020) related the **isomorphism type** of neighborhood complexes to Kronecker double covers, which is distinct from the equivariant deformation retraction asserted here. Kozlov (2005) compared Hom and neighborhood complexes by simple homotopy. Walker's earlier work, reported in the subsequent literature, showed that sufficiently high-girth graph box complexes have one-dimensional free \(C_2\)-homotopy models; that statement alone neither identifies the canonical double cover as a model nor determines its simultaneous graph-automorphism module. Accessible published record summaries of high-girth neighborhood-complex circle wedges and the dodecahedral symmetry computation likewise do not assert the uniform \(\operatorname{Aut}(G)\times C_2\) model and character formulas for all four-cycle-free graphs.

## Limitations
The no-four-cycle hypothesis is essential to the star-cell decomposition: a \(K_{2,2}\) supports cells in which both Hom coordinates have cardinality two, so the argument does not extend automatically. Graphs with degree-one vertices need a modified literal embedding of the radial spine and are excluded. This establishes no representation-theoretic characterization for graphs containing four-cycles. A historical source discussing one-dimensional models is only indirectly accessible; an equivalent older equivariant model may exist and remains a literature risk. Independent assessment has not been performed.
