On finite ordered carriers, positive instantial box semantics remembers only inclusion-minimal neighbourhoods within each maximal-point trace, while the diamond only inclusion-maximal neighbourhoods within the same maximal-point trace. These signatures completely determine every formula, yield a sharp width-based arity cutoff, and give exactly \(4(n+1)^2\) observational classes of constant two-sided neighbourhood assignments on an \(n\)-element chain.
