For the split scroll \(\mathbb P_{\mathbb F_m}(\mathcal O\oplus\mathcal O(aS+bF))\), canonical tensor-generation has an explicit complete two-product-and-one-strip criterion in \((m,a,b)\). Failure along the negative section is witnessed by a smooth canonical-degree-zero multisection of least ruling degree \(1\) or \(2\), with genus respectively \(0\) or \(m-3\) determined by parity.
