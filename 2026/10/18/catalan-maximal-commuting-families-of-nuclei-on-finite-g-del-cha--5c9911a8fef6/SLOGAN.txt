Maximal pairwise-commuting nuclei of an \(n\)-element Gödel chain are classified by ordered full binary trees, giving exactly \(\frac1n\binom{2n-2}{n-1}\) maximal compatible families. Their cardinalities are frontier-cut counts; the exact number of commuting ordered pairs satisfies \(b_{n+2}=4b_{n+1}-2b_n\), so independent uniform nuclei commute with exponentially vanishing probability.
