On each finite intuitionistic chain, every centred-serial constructive neighbourhood frame is indistinguishable, for both modalities under every upset valuation and at every formula depth, from a unique singleton-interval normal form. The exact number of distinct behaviours on \(n\) worlds is the square of the Catalan number: \(\operatorname{Cat}_n^2=\big(\binom{2n}{n}/(n+1)\big)^2\).
