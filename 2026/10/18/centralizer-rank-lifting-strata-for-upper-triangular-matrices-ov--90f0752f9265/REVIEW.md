The field-linear commutator map on the 12-dimensional triangular algebra reduces to a 3-by-5 matrix over the dual numbers. The residue ranks have an exhaustive three-component description. Dual-number elimination gives \(2r+\operatorname{rank}(K)\) and direct minor differentiation gives normal ranks one or two at the stated rank-two reductions. ## Value — PASS
The seven-rank spectrum is the full centralizer-size distribution of a canonical small algebra over a first-order local thickening. The rank-lift computation detects two distinct first-order tangent behaviors on intersections of the three determinantal components, explaining exceptional frequencies and yielding an exact commuting probability. This distinguishes the result from routine substitution of the base-field formula.
