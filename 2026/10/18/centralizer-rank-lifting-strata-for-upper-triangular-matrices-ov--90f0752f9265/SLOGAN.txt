For the upper triangular three-by-three algebra over \(\mathbb F_q[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)\), every inner-derivation rank from zero to six occurs with an explicit polynomial multiplicity in \(q\). The exact commuting probability is \(\bigl(q^5+3q^4+3q^3-6q^2-3q+3\bigr)/q^{11}\), and the exceptional frequencies arise from first-order lifts along intersections of three determinantal components.
