For diagonal power endomorphisms \(a_i\mapsto a_i^{N_i}\) of a finitely generated free semigroup, every commuting nonerasing morphism has an explicit exponent-level form: arbitrary word substitutions on exponent-one letters and pure-letter powers on all other levels. Every \(k\)-fold compositional root is a weighted permutation, and its exact count is a labeled cycle-index formula with ordered-divisor weights.
