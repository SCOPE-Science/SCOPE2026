For generalized-theta graphical zonotopes with arbitrarily many internally disjoint subdivided arms, an explicit product polynomial gives the exact number of toric fixed points for every possible maximal-cone facet count. The smooth fixed points are precisely the orientations with one completely directed arm and one exceptional same-direction edge on every other arm, giving \(2\sum_j\prod_{i\ne j}\ell_i\) smooth points.
