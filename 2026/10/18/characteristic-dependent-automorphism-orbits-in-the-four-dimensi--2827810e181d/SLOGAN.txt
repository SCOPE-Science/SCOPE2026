For the four-dimensional null-filiform Zinbiel algebra over \(\mathbb F_q\), the number of element orbits under its \(\mathbb F_q\)-linear automorphism group is \(2q+2+\gcd(3,q-1)\) in characteristic two, \(2q+3+\gcd(4,q-1)\) in characteristic three, and \(4+\gcd(3,q-1)+\gcd(4,q-1)\) in all larger characteristics. Explicit rational invariants classify every orbit and explain why modular orbit families grow linearly with field size.
