When \(q-1=2^a\ell\) with \(\ell\) an odd prime, consecutive non-square non-primitive pairs are controlled entirely by a polynomial gcd in the characteristic, not by the size of the field. For \(1\le a\le4\), they occur only in the \(5\)-power family at \(a=2\) (one pair) and in \(\mathbb F_{49}\) or an admissible \(17\)-power family at \(a=4\) (two or four pairs); all other fields in these strata have none.
