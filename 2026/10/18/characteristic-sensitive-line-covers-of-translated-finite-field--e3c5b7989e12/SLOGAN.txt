For unions of distinct translated finite-field parabolas, an elementary second-moment obstruction improves the projective line-cover lower bound to \((q+3)/2\) in every odd characteristic. In even characteristic, a trace-kernel construction proves the exact optimum \(q/2+1\) whenever the translation differences span a proper binary subspace; this always holds for two components, while the odd bound is sharp over the first eight odd prime fields.
