# Scientific review: reduced sandwich loci of five-by-five niltriangular matrices

**Correctness (PASS).** The condition \(\operatorname{ad}_X^2=0\) has ten explicitly reconstructed coefficient equations. In characteristic two, disjoint divisions according to \(h\) and \(a\) give three components; in odd characteristic additional \(2\)-torsion constraints split the locus into five coordinate flats. Each of the two determinant quadrics is geometrically irreducible, no displayed component contains another, and the finite-field counts follow from independently enumerated coefficient fibers. The packaged standalone checker confirms the component predicates and four finite-field counts. Finite checks are corroborative only; the general result is established by proof.

**Originality (PASS; qualified).** The sandwich terminology and simple-Lie context are established in Cohen–Ivanyos–Roozemond (2007). The closest matrix classification is Tsai–Bogale–Huang (2020), §§1 and 5.2, which addresses Belitskii upper-triangular similarity, not this reduced quadratic adjoint-square locus. Kobal (2005) gave relevant five-dimensional similarity normal forms, but only its abstract and indirect description were inspected, leaving meaningful residual priority risk. Completed mathematical-record searches and accessible local historical-claim comparison found no statement implying these component equations or field counts. A negative search is not a uniqueness proof.

**Value (PASS).** The sandwich locus is an intrinsic, natural variety associated to a nilpotent Lie algebra. The first nontrivial quadratic interaction among length-two paths yields an exact change from a union of five linear pieces to a union of three larger components with nonlinear determinantal geometry; the dimensions and closed finite-field formulas describe a characteristic jump with mathematical content beyond a restatement of the defining equations.

**Limits.** No scheme-theoretic radicality or claims for other matrix sizes, no independent audit, and a source-access limitation for the 2005 five-dimensional orbit-classification paper.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
