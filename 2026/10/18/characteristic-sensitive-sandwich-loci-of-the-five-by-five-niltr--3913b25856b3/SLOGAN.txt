For the Lie algebra of strictly upper-triangular \(5\times5\) matrices, the reduced sandwich locus \(\operatorname{ad}_X^2=0\) has three seven-dimensional components, two determinantal, in characteristic two; in odd characteristic it has five linear components of dimensions \(6,6,6,5,5\). Over \(\mathbb F_q\), its exact cardinality is \(3q^7-q^6-q^5\) for even \(q\) and \(3q^6-2q^4\) for odd \(q\).
