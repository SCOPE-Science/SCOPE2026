# Scientific review — characteristic-three Jordan strata

## Correctness — PASS

The augmentation ideal of \(\mathbb F_q[x,y]/(x^3,y^3)\) has homogeneous dimensions \((2,3,2,1)\). Frobenius makes every augmentation element cube-zero. An element with nonzero linear part is carried to \(x\) by a filtered algebra automorphism, giving the \((6,3)\) rank pair. When the first nonzero part is quadratic, the degree-one-to-degree-three block has determinant \(\beta^2-\alpha\gamma\); the degree-two-to-socle map is onto. The same determinant is the coefficient of the square of the element. The remaining deeper strata follow from the perfect degree-one/degree-three socle pairing. Counting the nonzero cone \(\beta^2=\alpha\gamma\) gives \(q^2-1\) quadratic triples, and every coefficient is reconciled with the total \(q^8\). Direct matrix-rank checks exhaust the \(\mathbb F_3\) case and sample all leading strata in \(\mathbb F_9\). Unrestricted correctness rests on the symbolic argument, not those finite checks.

## Originality — PASS, residual risk acknowledged

The closest primary literature is Carlson–Friedlander–Pevtsova (arXiv:1106.4474), Iarrobino–Macias Marques–McDaniel (arXiv:1802.07383, Example 3.8), and Altafi–Iarrobino–Khatami (arXiv:1810.00716). The first studies rank-shifted subgroups and associated module invariants rather than distributions over all nonlinear augmentation elements. The second explicitly treats the same algebra for a special quadratic element **outside** characteristic three. The third classifies linear-form Jordan types under characteristic assumptions excluding this case. None of the inspected results supplies the exact characteristic-three six-stratum distribution. Exact statement searches and comparisons with related published mathematical findings found no implied equal or broader theorem. Residual risk: a complete census could already have been recorded in older modular group-algebra or Artinian-algebra literature under a different terminology; a negative search cannot rule this out.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The object is the smallest rank-two elementary-abelian group algebra in odd defining characteristic, and the invariant is the complete nilpotent Jordan partition of multiplication by **every** augmentation element, not a cherry-picked finite subset. The result identifies a six-part finite-field rank stratification with an actual missing rank five, and ties the nonlinear rank jump and square rank to the projective discriminant conic. These are structural, field-uniform data for a natural modular regular representation and relate directly to the studied Jordan-type loci and Lefschetz phenomena in cited work. It is a bounded small-object classification; no larger-rank or downstream theorem is claimed.
