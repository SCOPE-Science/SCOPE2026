For every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\) of characteristic three, the regular module of \(\mathbb F_q[C_3\times C_3]\) has exactly six Jordan types as an augmentation element varies, with complete closed multiplicities determined by its leading degree and one quadratic discriminant. In particular, its multiplication ranks are precisely \(0,1,2,3,4,6\), and the quadratic-leading rank-three stratum is controlled by a projective conic.
