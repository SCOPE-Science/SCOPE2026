The chordal cubic threefold has a reduced rational-normal-quartic singular scheme in odd characteristic, but in characteristic two its entire intrinsic Jacobian scheme becomes the non-split genus-five balanced canonical ribbon, a degree-eight complete intersection of three quadrics. Its conormal bundle is \(\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1}(-6)\), and the characteristic change doubles the singular-scheme degree while increasing its arithmetic genus from zero to five.
