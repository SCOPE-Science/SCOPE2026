The literal six-coordinate Segre cubic has ten nodes away from characteristics two and three, but in characteristic two it becomes a cone over a smooth Hermitian cubic surface with one length-\(16\) Jacobian singularity and exactly \(27\) projective planes. Those planes have \(135\) line-intersecting pairs and \(216\) pairs meeting only at the cone vertex; in characteristic three the cubic equation becomes redundant.
