In characteristic \(2\), a finite-dimensional evolution algebra whose nonzero natural-basis squares are independent has exactly the annihilator-valued weight-zero Rota–Baxter maps, and every linear pointwise-local map is already ordinary. For every two-dimensional evolution algebra over \(\mathbb F_{2^m}\), the only strict local-to-global gap occurs when its nonzero dependent squares span a line generated by a non-square-zero vector; then the ordinary and local counts are \(2q-1\) and \(q^2\), respectively, and exactly \(q(q-1)^2\) labeled multiplication tables exhibit this gap.
