For the free two-step nilpotent Lie algebra on \(n\) generators over \(\mathbb F_q\), idempotent derivations have precisely two automorphism-conjugacy orbits in odd characteristic but \(n+1\) in characteristic two. Every orbit has a closed Gaussian-binomial cardinality and a prescribed operator rank, yielding the complete rank distribution for all \(n\ge2\) and all finite fields.
