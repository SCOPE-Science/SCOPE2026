Over algebraically closed fields of characteristic \(2\), the singular locus of the elementary-symmetric cubic \(e_3=0\) is a finite collection of ordinary double points when \(n\not\equiv2\pmod4\), but exactly a complete arrangement of explicit projective lines meeting in one triple-multiplicity point when \(n\equiv2\pmod4\). The component counts are given by binomial sums over support sizes congruent to \(1\) modulo \(4\), and the hypersurfaces are normal for every \(n\ge4\).
