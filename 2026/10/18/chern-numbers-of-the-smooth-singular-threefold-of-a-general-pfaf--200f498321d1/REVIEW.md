# Scientific review: Pfaffian-quintic singular threefold Chern numbers

## Correctness
PASS. For a general \(9\)-plane in the projective space of ten-by-ten alternating forms, the corank-at-least-four locus is a smooth irreducible threefold and contains no forms of rank at most four. The rank-six kernel incidence gives its exact smooth model as \(35\) hyperplane cuts of \(\mathbb P_{\operatorname{Gr}(4,10)}(\bigwedge^2Q^*)\). Adjunction and the nowhere-degenerate induced six-dimensional Pfaffian show \(K_S=5H\). An exact, exhaustive localization calculation over \(3150\) fixed points gives \((H^3,c_1H^2,c_2H,c_3)=(330,-1650,7920,-31350)\), with two distinct sets of torus weights giving identical results. The size-eight specialization independently reproduces the classical genus \(169\). Limits: the finite checker certifies the stated localization sums, while the geometric reduction relies on Bertini, skew-rank stratification and Chern-class localization.

## Originality
PASS under available literature evidence, not a priority certification. The closely relevant article by Bolognesi--Ciliberto--Verra (arXiv:2610.11925v1) treats the same Pfaffian family and explicitly quotes the degree formula of Harris--Tu, so \(H^3=330\) is not novel. Its materially inspected sections do not report the canonical-class/Chern table, Euler characteristic or arithmetic genus of the general size-ten rank-six singular threefold. The corresponding size-eight singular curve has published/recorded genus \(169\), but this does not imply the size-ten threefold values. Indexed mathematical-record searches for the size-ten invariants and aliases found no equivalent explicit statement. General skew-determinantal intersection methods could implicitly supply these values; the original Harris--Tu full text was not available for inspection. Those remain residual risks, not claims of an exhaustive uniqueness search.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The singular threefold is an intrinsic, geometrically necessary degeneracy locus of the rational Pfaffian quintic hypersurfaces studied in the new literature. Its complete Chern-number table determines the topological Euler characteristic, canonical volume, holomorphic Euler characteristic and Hilbert polynomial, quantities relevant to understanding and comparing resolutions and deformations of the Pfaffian construction. This is not an arbitrary slice or a recomputation of the published degree: the new work requires exact localization of a rank-fifteen projective bundle over \(\operatorname{Gr}(4,10)\), with \(3150\) fixed-point contributions. No general theorem on resolutions, full Hodge numbers, or genericity beyond the stated open locus is asserted.

## Closest literature and limitations
Bolognesi, Ciliberto and Verra, arXiv:2610.11925v1 (8 October 2026), Sections 1--3; Harris and Tu, *Topology* 23 (1984), 71--84; Józefiak, Lascoux and Pragacz (1981) for classical skew-determinantal intersection theory. The standard degree formula and size-eight curve calculation are explicitly separated from the new size-ten Chern numbers. The Harris--Tu full paper and any unindexed preexisting numerical tables remain an originality risk. The findings apply only to general complex alternating-form systems.
