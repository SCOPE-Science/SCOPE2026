The singular locus of a general degree-five Pfaffian hypersurface in \(\mathbb P^9\) is a smooth general-type threefold with \(K_S=5H\) and Chern-number vector \((H^3,c_1H^2,c_2H,c_3)=(330,-1650,7920,-31350)\). In particular, its Euler characteristic is \(-31350\) and its canonical volume is \(41250\); the degree \(330\) was already known.
