Among connected chordal graphs with two stable color classes and quotient \(\begin{pmatrix}0&3\\1&1\end{pmatrix}\), the isomorphism types are exactly indexed by subcubic trees: color refinement sees only the order, and the first nonisomorphic pair in this restricted class has forty vertices. The number of pairwise indistinguishable nonisomorphic graphs in the class is unbounded.
