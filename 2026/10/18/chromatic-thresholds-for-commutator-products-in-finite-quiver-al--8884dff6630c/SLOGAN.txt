For the unit group of any finite radical-square-zero quiver algebra over \(\mathbb F_q\) with \(q>2\), a derived-group element is a product of \(r\) commutators exactly when its nonzero-arrow support graph can be colored with \((q-1)^{2r}\) colors. Thus commutator width is the sharp logarithmic chromatic threshold, and the entire image size of every repeated commutator word is an explicit support-graph sum.
