Among the \(2048\) binary order-five de Bruijn cycles, the number of circular Abelian-square occurrences ranges sharply from \(73\) to \(154\), with \(71\) attainable totals and a unique reversal/complementation orbit at each extreme. The full histogram is exhaustive, and in every binary de Bruijn order the difference between occurrence count and distinct Abelian-square factor count is an explicit short-factor binomial sum.
