For incidence contexts made of every \(\ell\)-vertex cyclic window, the concept lattice is exactly a circular run-length closure system: its size has a binomial census and a transfer-matrix recurrence, with a sharp crossover from \(2+n\ell\) to additional multi-run contributions. Its size recovers \(\ell\) at fixed \(n\), its maximum chain contains \(\ell+2\) elements, and modularity occurs only for singleton or co-singleton windows.
