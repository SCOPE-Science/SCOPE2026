# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The original formulas are correct. Boolean rectangular products are either zero or the rectangle of the outer supports; if distinct rectangles have both products nonzero and equal, uniqueness of supports would force equality, a contradiction. Thus distinct commuting vertices mutually annihilate. In an ordered k-clique a coordinate is absent, unilateral (one of two nonempty-label subsets), or private double membership at one label, giving 2^(k+1)+k−1 types. Inclusion–exclusion requires each private type; maximality is precisely no absent coordinate, since any new idempotent must have its overlap on an unused coordinate, and a singleton diagonal extends there. This proves both original formulas and bounds, with the exact sizes attained by partitions. The repaired result is narrower: for a fixed vertex, simultaneous membership of a neighbor is allowed only at one of s unused coordinates; u unilateral coordinates each allow two choices, so the degree is 2^u(4^s−3^s). Choosing the unused/unilateral sets and orientation yields every enumerator term, with s+u≤n−1 due to idempotence. The live vertices all attach to the diagonal clique. For n=2 that clique is the only live edge; for n≥3 the two indicated co-singleton diagonals are nonadjacent leaves attached to distinct singleton diagonals and have distance three. A separately written bounded check confirmed degrees, both original count vectors, isolates, edge counts, live connectivity and diameters for n=2..5, testing literal Boolean matrix products at n=2..4. The repaired text's three-coordinate metric witness is correct: the cross-intersections for its square-zero common neighbor vanish, but because the unused sets of the idempotents are disjoint no common idempotent neighbor exists.
