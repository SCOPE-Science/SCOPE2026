The three-clique-free complex of the fifteen-vertex triangular graph \(L(K_6)\) is homotopy equivalent to \(26\) four-spheres, and its rational \(S_6\)-homology splits as \(S^{(6)}\oplus S^{(4,2)}\oplus S^{(3,2,1)}\). An explicit integral discrete-Morse computation determines the homotopy type, while signed Lefschetz traces determine its unique invariant rational class.
