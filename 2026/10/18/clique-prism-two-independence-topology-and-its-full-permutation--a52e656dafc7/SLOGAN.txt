For every \(n\ge5\), the two-independence complex of the clique prism \(K_n\square K_2\) is \(\bigvee^n S^1\vee\bigvee^{\beta_n}S^3\), where \(\beta_n=(n^4-10n^3+31n^2-30n+4)/4\). Its full rational \(S_n\times C_2\) homology character has an explicit cycle-count formula, and swapping the two layers always has third-homology trace \(1\).
