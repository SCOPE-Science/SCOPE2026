Parshina–Puzynina (arXiv:2111.00863, §§2,4) study unrestricted closed-rich extremals and specifically use a *cube* of a de Bruijn word, not single de Bruijn cycles. Published exact binary closed-rich maxima at lengths 25–27 do not determine order-five cyclic closed-factor counts. Targeted searches for equivalent numerical tables, complete returns, and stronger coverage identified no equivalent statement. Search incompleteness and earlier unindexed results remain possible.

**Value — PASS.** Closed words are complete first returns, a natural measure of repeated local structure. The all-order \(O(N\log N)\) upper bound explains why universal cycles are far more constrained in closed-factor richness than unrestricted words, which can have quadratically many closed factors. The order-five census gives the exact first large-family distribution, showing remarkable rigidity (only four consecutive values for 2048 different cycles) and supplying exact benchmarks for return-word combinatorics.

**Limits.** The exact distribution is restricted to order five; the general upper bound is not claimed optimal. This is a self-review, not independent validation. The novelty assessment compares inspected materials and cannot rule out an unindexed earlier result.
