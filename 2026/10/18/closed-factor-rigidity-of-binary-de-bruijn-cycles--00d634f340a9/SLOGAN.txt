Every binary de Bruijn cycle of order \(h\) has at most \(2+(h-1)2^h\) distinct nonempty closed circular factors, rather than the potentially quadratic number in unrestricted words. At order five, exactly \(360,888,600,200\) of the \(2048\) cycles have respectively \(93,94,95,96\) such factors; these are the only values.
