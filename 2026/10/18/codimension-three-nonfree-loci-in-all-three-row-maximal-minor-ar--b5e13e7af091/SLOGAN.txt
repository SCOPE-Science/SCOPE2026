For every \(n\ge5\), every five-column rank-at-most-two determinantal locus is contained in the nonfree locus of the full three-by-\(n\) maximal-minor divisor. On a dense open part the logarithmic derivations require exactly \(3n+1\) generators, so in all widths \(n\ge6\) nonfreeness occurs even at full-rank matrices.
