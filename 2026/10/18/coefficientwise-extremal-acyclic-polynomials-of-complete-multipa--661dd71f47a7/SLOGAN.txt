Acyclic-set coefficients on complete multipartite graphs are minimized simultaneously by the balanced Turán graph and maximized simultaneously by the one-large-part graph at fixed order and number of parts. All such graphs through order \(15\) have log-concave acyclic polynomials; at order \(16\) the unique failure is \(K_{4,1^{12}}\), beginning an infinite family of failures.
