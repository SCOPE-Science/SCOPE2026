## correctness

PASS

The transfinite construction uses actual ordinal prefixes, not a finite-word extrapolation. With finitely many initial valuation colours, each noncofinal colour has a strict bound in the current ordinal prefix; their finite union defines a smaller prefix beta_(j+1). The strictly decreasing beta-sequence terminates after finitely many indices because ordinals admit no infinite descending omega-sequence. On each layer all present colours are cofinal. Each prefix is definable as diamond of the union of noncofinal colour classes in its predecessor; every candidate cell is therefore definable. Conversely, a nonempty union of whole cells has a highest occupied ordinal layer with a cofinal colour, so diamond sends it to the entire lower initial prefix; closure yields precisely the asserted 2^(sum|C_j|) algebra. Boundary includes zero beta, singleton successor tops, alpha=omega and omega+1, arbitrary nonzero set ordinals, q>=1. Fresh independent exhaustive Boolean/modal closure through 6,554 finite valuation words verified every predicted count.

## originality

PASS

The exact object is the least Boolean-modal algebra generated by q colour predicates on a reflexive ordinal chain. Agnew uses tuned partitions of transitive frames to establish local finiteness, but the inspected paper does not define cofinal-colour layers or the valuation-specific minimal partition. Natural aliases include minimal stable colouring, tuned partition, generated finite quotient and modal atom refinement. Agnew Proposition 3.18 covers finite products of ordinal frames, formally broader in frame family, but only as a qualitative local-finiteness claim. That theorem does not imply the identity of atoms and exact cardinality on one chain. Tuned-partition results may supply a different proof of finiteness, not automatically this formula. The stronger frame scope in Agnew does not dominate the quantitative atom identification. The predecessor result proves existence of some finite tuned refinement, while the current theorem establishes that the explicit cofinal-layer partition is exactly the generated algebra, not merely a finite bound.
