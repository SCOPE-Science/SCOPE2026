The complete bivariate induced-cograph polynomial of \(K_{a,b}\) minus a matching has an explicit three-term formula, and for \(a,b\ge3\) it decreases coefficientwise whenever another disjoint edge is deleted, apart from one exact equality case. On crown graphs, all inclusion-maximal induced cographs have sizes \(4\), \(n\), or \(n+1\), yielding arbitrarily long gaps in the sizes of inclusion-minimal cograph vertex-deletion sets.
