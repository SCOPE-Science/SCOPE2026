For two nonorthogonal unit rows sampled uniformly in randomized Kaczmarz, a small unsmoothed momentum strictly improves the asymptotic expected squared-error rate, with initial slope \(-2c^2(1-c^2)/(1+c^2)^2\) in row overlap \(c\). At exact orthogonality every positive momentum worsens that rate; near orthogonality the uniquely rate-optimal momentum is \(c^2+O(c^4)\), yielding a sharp \(c^4\) gain.
