For an unweighted two-clique barbell quantum walk with symmetric normalized-adjacency dynamics, an arbitrary launch packet on one clique's nonbridge vertices transfers only its coherent uniform component: at every time its opposite-clique population differs from that component's weight times \(\sin^2(\Delta_n t/2)\) by at most \(8n/(n-1)^2\) times the weight. The beat splitting satisfies \(\Delta_n\sim2/n^2\), so coherent uniform packets approach complete opposite-clique transfer on the quadratic time scale, while single-vertex launches have at most \(1/(n-1)\) transfer probability.
