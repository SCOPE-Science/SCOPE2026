# Collision profiles and the sharp counting-variable threshold for finite permutation systems

## Finding
Let \(r\ge1\), and interpret each binary relation \(R_i\) as the graph of a permutation \(f_i\) on a finite nonempty set \(X\). If the permutations commute, associate to each \(x\in X\) the equality partition \(\pi(x)\) of the ordered list
\[
(x,f_1(x),f_1^{-1}(x),\ldots,f_r(x),f_r^{-1}(x)).
\]
Let \(h_A(\pi)\) be the number of elements realizing partition \(\pi\) in structure \(A\). For **all** finite commuting-permutation systems \(A,B\) with the same named relations,
\[
A\equiv_{C^2}B\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad h_A(\pi)=h_B(\pi)\text{ for every partition }\pi.
\]
The profile colouring is already stable after one atomic-neighbourhood counting round.

For two named commuting permutations the first failure of \(C^2\)-identification, relative to this class, occurs on five elements. The numbers of isomorphism classes / \(C^2\)-classes on \(1,2,3,4,5,6\) elements are respectively
\[
(1/1,\ 4/4,\ 8/8,\ 21/21,\ 39/38,\ 92/82).
\]
On \(\mathbb Z/5\mathbb Z\), the two systems \(f(x)=x+1\) and \(g(x)=x+2\) or \(g(x)=x+3\) give a nonisomorphic but \(C^2\)-equivalent pair. Their distinction is expressible with three variables. More generally, **every** finite system of any fixed finite number of named permutations, even without commutativity, is identified up to isomorphism by some sentence of \(C^3\). Thus three counting variables suffice throughout the entire permutation-system class, and two do not.

## Assumptions and scope
The vocabulary contains only named binary predicates \(R_1,\ldots,R_r\), with \(R_i(x,y)\) meaning \(y=f_i(x)\); these are **not** genuine function symbols in the logical syntax. Each relation is required to be the graph of a bijection. The profile theorem additionally requires pairwise commutation. All sets are finite and nonempty; no result for infinite systems is asserted. The minimal order and numerical census concern precisely two **named** permutations. Isomorphism must preserve both names and directions. \(C^k\) denotes first-order logic with equality, counting quantifiers of arbitrary finite thresholds, and at most \(k\) distinct variable symbols.

## Proof
**1. Commuting profiles are orbit-constant.** Each generator \(f_j\) carries the entire displayed list at \(x\) to the list at \(f_j(x)\), because \(f_j\) commutes with \(f_i\) and with \(f_i^{-1}\). Therefore all points in a joint permutation orbit have the same equality partition. In particular, the points \(f_i^{\pm1}(x)\) share \(\pi(x)\).

**2. The profile is \(C^2\)-definable at a point.** Every equality between two entries in the displayed list is expressed by a formula using only \(x,y\). For example, \(f_i(x)=f_j(x)\) is \(\exists y(R_i(x,y)\wedge R_j(x,y))\); \(f_i(x)=f_j^{-1}(x)\) is \(\exists y(R_i(x,y)\wedge R_j(y,x))\); and \(f_i^{-1}(x)=f_j^{-1}(x)\) is \(\exists y(R_i(y,x)\wedge R_j(y,x))\). Equalities involving \(x\) are atomic loops, and inequalities are negations. A conjunction \(\theta_\pi(x)\) specifies one exact partition. Counting \(\#\{x:\theta_\pi(x)\}\) is expressible by \(C^2\) sentences. Thus equivalent structures have identical \(h(\pi)\).

**3. The histogram is sufficient.** Write \(\tau(x,y)\) for the complete atomic two-variable type, recording \(x=y\) and, for every \(i\), both \(R_i(x,y)\) and \(R_i(y,x)\). For fixed \(x\) of profile \(\pi\), every pair-type other than the all-false off-diagonal type is realized only among the distinct members of \(\{x,f_i(x),f_i^{-1}(x):1\le i\le r\}\). Their multiplicities and the fact that their targets have profile \(\pi\) depend only on the equality pattern \(\pi\). Every other target has the default atomic pair-type. Consequently, for every pair-type \(\tau\) and target profile \(\rho\), the number of \(y\) such that \(\tau(x,y)=\tau\) and \(\pi(y)=\rho\) depends only on \(\pi,\rho\) and the histogram \(h\). For the default type it is the total \(h(\rho)\) minus the special targets of profile \(\rho\); for a nondefault type it is the number of corresponding distinct marked targets (zero unless \(\rho=\pi\)). By symmetry, analogous counts hold when a quantifier varies the first coordinate. Induct on the formation of \(C^2\) formulas: the truth of each formula with free variables among \(x,y\) depends only on the two profiles, the atomic pair-type, and \(h\). Counting quantifiers preserve this property because the just-proved neighbour counts determine their cardinalities. Therefore equal histograms imply agreement on every \(C^2\) sentence. This also proves one-round stabilization of the profile colouring.

**4. The five-element example and minimality.** The two named shifts on five points have all five entries \(x,f(x),f^{-1}(x),g(x),g^{-1}(x)\) different at every point. Hence their histograms agree, while an isomorphism preserving the cyclic shift \(f(x)=x+1\) must be a translation and cannot conjugate \(g(x)=x+2\) to \(g(x)=x+3\). The \(C^3\) sentence asserting \(\forall x\forall y\,(R_g(x,y)\leftrightarrow\exists z(R_f(x,z)\wedge R_f(z,y)))\) holds in exactly the first system. For orders at most four, the standalone exhaustive certificate enumerates all commuting pairs of permutations, computes their orbits under simultaneous relabeling, and compares the exact profile histograms: no collision occurs. This finite exhaustive step is used only for the minimality statement.

**5. Three variables identify every finite permutation system.** We do **not** require commutation here. For a fixed finite word \(w\) in named generators and their formal inverses, its action relation \(y=w(x)\) is definable in first-order logic with just three variable symbols: the empty word is equality; prepend a letter by existentially quantifying the intermediate vertex and cyclically reusing the third symbol in the recursively defined suffix formula. With three variables, one can consequently test \(u(x)=v(x)\) for any two fixed words \(u,v\).

Fix a finite permutation system \(A\) of size \(n\). From any vertex, the orbit under generators and inverses is reached by words of length at most \(n-1\). The equalities and inequalities among the word-images of lengths at most \(n\) determine the complete rooted, labelled directed orbit graph: representatives of length at most \(n-1\) enumerate its vertices, and the extra step determines each outgoing generator edge. Hence for every rooted orbit type occurring in \(A\) there is a unary \(C^3\) formula specifying exactly that type among permutation systems of size \(n\). A \(C^3\) sentence can demand that the total domain size is \(n\) and can count the elements of every rooted orbit type. Each rooted type determines one unrooted orbit isomorphism type; dividing its total vertex count by that orbit size recovers the number of component copies. Therefore the sentence fixes the entire structure up to isomorphism. The requirement that each \(R_i\) is bijective is itself expressible in \(C^2\) by asserting exactly one outgoing and one incoming neighbour of each vertex. Conjoining these axioms also identifies \(A\) among all finite structures of the same binary signature.

## Verification
Execute `python verify_commuting.py` and `python verify_c3.py` using Python 3. Both are standard-library-only. The first completely enumerates all ordered commuting permutation pairs through size six, canonicalizes by simultaneous conjugation, and compares profile histograms. The second independently performs labelled two-dimensional Weisfeiler–Leman refinement on all systems of two named permutations through size five (including noncommuting systems), which tests the three-variable identification assertion on those sizes. Exact output is in `verification_output.txt`. Computation proves only the specified finite cutoffs; the unrestricted statements rest on Steps 1–3 and 5 above.

## Relationship to prior work
The general connection between \(C^2\), relational colour refinement and coarsest equitable partitions is established in Kiefer–Schweitzer–Selman, *Graphs Identified by Logics with Counting* (first publicly posted 2015-03-30, arXiv:1503.08792). Their more general identification theorem and the standard logical machinery are not claimed here. The present result gives a specialized closed invariant for commuting permutation systems, determines the first minimal collision by exhaustive isomorphism census, and supplies a uniform three-variable bound for permutation systems. Pratt-Hartmann (2015) treats two-variable counting logic with equivalence relations rather than pairs of commuting bijection graphs. Searches did not locate a published statement establishing these specific combined claims; the possibility of a specialized older or unindexed consequence of general coherent-configuration theory remains a priority risk.

## Limitations
The exact \(C^2\) histogram criterion is not asserted for noncommuting permutations. The minimum-order census and class totals are for exactly two labelled permutations, not arbitrary finite signatures. Three-variable characteristic sentences may be extremely long and need not be uniform in the domain size. This is a statement about expressive power, not an efficient formula-construction algorithm. The proof does not give independent certification or an absolute novelty guarantee.
