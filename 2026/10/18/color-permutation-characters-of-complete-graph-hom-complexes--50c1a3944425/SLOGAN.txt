For every \(2\le m\le n\), the target-color \(S_n\)-action on the top reduced rational homology of \(\operatorname{Hom}(K_m,K_n)\) has character \(\chi(g)=(-1)^m\operatorname{sgn}(g)\sum_{j=2}^{m}(-1)^j\binom mj(j-1)^{c(g)}\). Its full target-color quotient has no reduced rational homology, and for \(m=3\) the formula yields an explicit sign-twisted two-row Specht decomposition.
