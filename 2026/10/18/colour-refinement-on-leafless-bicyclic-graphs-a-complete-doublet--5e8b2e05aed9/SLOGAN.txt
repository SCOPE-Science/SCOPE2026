Among connected leafless bicyclic graphs, every failure of two-variable counting logic to distinguish nonisomorphic graphs comes from exactly one family: two equal-arm paths plus a third path between two poles versus two equal cycles joined by that third path. At order \(n\), the number of ambiguous doubletons is exactly \(\max\{0,\lfloor n/2\rfloor-2\}\), starting with a unique six-vertex pair.
