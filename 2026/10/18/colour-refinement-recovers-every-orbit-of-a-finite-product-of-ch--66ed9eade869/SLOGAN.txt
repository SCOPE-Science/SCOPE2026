In every finite product of nontrivial chains, counting colour refinement of the full strict-order relation recovers exactly the coordinate-permutation automorphism orbits within at most \(1+\sum_i(m_i-1)\) rounds. Their number is \(\prod_m\binom{r_m+m-1}{m-1}\), with multinomial class sizes; this does not claim unique identification among all finite posets.
