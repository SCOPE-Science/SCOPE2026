For the four-robust clique complex of the three-row grid \(P_3\square P_n\), reversing the columns has a contractible geometric fixed-point space when \(n\) is odd and a wedge of exactly \(2n-4\) two-spheres when \(n\) is even, for every \(n\ge3\). This distinguishes actual reflection-fixed topology without assuming a homotopy classification of the full complex.
