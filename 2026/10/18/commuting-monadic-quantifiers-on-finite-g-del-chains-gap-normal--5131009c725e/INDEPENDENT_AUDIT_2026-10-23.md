# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

On finite Gödel chains each monadic ∀,∃ pair is exactly lower/upper rounding L_F,U_F to an endpoint-containing subchain F, by the established monadic subalgebra representation. For ordered subchains B,C, commuting lower roundings must compose to L_{B∩C}: the common composite is idempotent, monotone, regressive with fixed points precisely B∩C. If one intersection gap contains both B-only and C-only fixed points, evaluating at the later exclusive point yields unequal lower compositions. If each gap contains exclusive points of at most one set, both lower composites round to the previous intersection point; order duality gives precisely the same condition for both upper operators and composite U_{B∩C}. The four-symbol membership word factors uniquely at common points. Valid gap words of length j number 2^(j+1)−1, giving gf 1/(1−4z+2z²), a_k=4a_(k−1)−2a_(k−2), a_0=1,a_1=4 and roots 2±√2. Independent exhaustive table checks n=2..9 verified lower/upper commutation equivalence, intersection composites and counts 1,4,14,48,164,560,1912,6528. The proof, not experiments, treats all n.
