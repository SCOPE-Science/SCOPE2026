# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem is exact for every finite labeled Gödel chain of size at least two. Bottom-preserving binary-join maps are monotone; inflationary idempotents are uniquely the upper-rounding maps to endpoint-containing fixed-point subsets. The necessity argument uses a fixed point of one map lying below a fixed point of the other inside a common gap and derives a forbidden common fixed point. The converse computes both compositions as rounding to the intersection. Positive compositions yield the counting formula, rational generating function, and recurrence. All ordered pairs up to nine chain points were exhaustively checked; the general result is deductive.

## Originality — PASS with residual priority risk

The 2026 motivating source treats polytopological intuitionistic modal semantics, not two commuting normal modalities on a finite Gödel truth-value chain. Santocanale (2019) proves broader single-idempotent representation and supplies a path product but does not state the common-fixed-gap commutation criterion or its joint census in inspected sections. The integer sequence is already OEIS A007070 with a general composition interpretation, which is expressly prior and not claimed new. No located source directly states or scientifically implies the complete modality-specific classification without an additional structural argument. Older semigroup literature may nonetheless contain a general theorem that subsumes the result.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The interchange equation between two idempotent modal operators is characterized by a local exclusion rule on shared fixed-point gaps, which identifies the allowable interactions independently of syntactic presentations. It yields an exact all-size census and a sharp exponential rarity statement within a natural finite Heyting-algebra family. The contribution is the paired structural normal form, not the preexisting sequence alone.

The work does not cover nonnormal maps, arbitrary lattices, or infinite structures, and does not establish priority beyond sources actually examined.
