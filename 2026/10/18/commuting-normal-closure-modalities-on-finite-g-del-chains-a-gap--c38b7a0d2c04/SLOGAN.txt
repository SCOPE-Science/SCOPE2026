Two normal S4-style closure modalities on a finite Gödel chain commute exactly when, between consecutive common fixed levels, extra fixed points belong to at most one modality. The number of ordered commuting pairs on an \(n\)-element chain is \(\big((2+\sqrt2)^{n-1}-(2-\sqrt2)^{n-1}\big)/(2\sqrt2)\); this is a new operator interpretation of the previously tabulated OEIS sequence A007070.
