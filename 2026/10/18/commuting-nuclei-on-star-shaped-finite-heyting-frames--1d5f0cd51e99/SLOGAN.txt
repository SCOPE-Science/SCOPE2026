On the \((2^n+1)\)-element Heyting algebra of upsets of a rooted star with \(n\) labelled leaves, maximal mutually commuting nucleus families correspond exactly to leaf subsets, and every ordered \(k\)-tuple of pairwise-commuting nuclei is counted by \(\sum_{h=0}^{k}\binom{k}{h}(2^h+2^{k-h}-1)^n\). Consequently, the probability that two independent uniform nuclei commute approaches \(1/2\), with exact correction \((1/2)(3/4)^n\).
