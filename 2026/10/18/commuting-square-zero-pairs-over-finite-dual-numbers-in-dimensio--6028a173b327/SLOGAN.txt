For every odd prime power \(q\), exactly \(q^{18}+(5q-3)(q^2-1)(q^2+q+1)q^9\) ordered pairs of \(3\times3\) matrices over \(\mathbb F_q[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)\) both commute and square to zero. Four explicit reduction strata have affine lifting dimensions \(18,10,9,8\); the proportional stratum is one dimension smaller than in the analogous anticommuting deformation problem.
