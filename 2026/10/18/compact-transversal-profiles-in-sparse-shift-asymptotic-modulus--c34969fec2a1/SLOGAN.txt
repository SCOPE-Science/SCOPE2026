Every nonempty compact \(K\subset(0,\infty)\) can be realized as the transversal parameter set of infinitely many clopen fibers in the weak/strong operator cluster set of positive roots \(|W^n|^{1/n}\) for an injective sparse-spike weighted shift. Nevertheless, the scalar asymptotic numerical range for **every** unit vector is just \([e^{-\max K},1]\), so operators with identical scalar asymptotic data can have nonhomeomorphic limit-set geometries.
