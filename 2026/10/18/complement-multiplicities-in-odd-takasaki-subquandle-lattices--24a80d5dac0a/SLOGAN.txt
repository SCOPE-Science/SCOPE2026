For any finite odd Takasaki quandle, the number of lattice complements of a given affine subquandle is an explicit sum over cyclic quotients of its underlying abelian group. In the odd cyclic case, the total depends only on the prime support of the subquandle index; for prime-power orders every proper nonempty subquandle has exactly \(n-1\) complements, while a complete size polynomial can distinguish same-size subquandles in noncyclic groups.
