Among all characteristic-two curves carrying wild cyclic automorphisms of order \(2g\), the Jacobian has \(2\)-rank exactly one if and only if the curve belongs to the two-branch Artin–Schreier family \(y^2+y=c(x^m+x^{-m})\) with odd \(m\ge3\); every other classified family has \(2\)-rank zero. The difficult genus-fifteen case reduces to a genus-four trigonal curve whose Cartier operator is nilpotent of index three.
