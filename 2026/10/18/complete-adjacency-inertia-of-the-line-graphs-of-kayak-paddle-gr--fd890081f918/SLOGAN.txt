For every graph made of two disjoint cycles joined by a path, the full adjacency inertia of its line graph depends only on the cycle lengths modulo four and the joining-path parity, via a complete twenty-case table. The signature reaches its sharp maximum \(1\) precisely for an odd joining path and cycle residues \(\{0,1\}\) or \(\{1,1\}\); adjacency nullity reaches its sharp maximum \(3\) precisely for two cycle lengths divisible by four and an odd joining path.
