# Review of the affine class-group formula

## Correctness

**PASS.** The theorem fixes the lattice, all admissible parameters, the two nondegenerate coordinate facet types, and the affine chart (not an unrelated global class group). The proof enumerates all facet vertices, proves their primitivity, computes the entire difference lattice rather than only its rational span, and deduces the Smith factors from determinantal divisors. The standard toric divisor exact sequence converts those factors into the complete chart class group. The independently generated ray matrices and their integral Smith normal forms agree with the formula in 105 parameter triples of dimensions two through six. These examples are checks, not an infinite proof.

## Originality

**PASS, with source-access priority risk.** Wu's originating alpha-spectrum construction does not claim an explicit formula for both fixed-point affine divisor class groups in its accessible abstract. A secondary detailed account reports global class-group properties but not this local invariant-factor classification. The related Liu--Zhu specialization has already been fully calculated in an earlier published-finding corpus record; our statement **does not claim novelty there**, and the general formula specializes correctly. The previously recorded automorphism-group classification of Wu's entire family controls symmetries, not Weil class groups. Broad toric-divisor theory computes class groups algorithmically, but does not supply the parameter-uniform binomial/gcd invariant factors established here. Database and literature searches revealed no same-scope published formula. The original Wu full text and some potentially relevant unindexed combinatorial toric literature were not materially inspected; absolute priority remains unverified.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** Affine divisor class groups measure both failure of \(\mathbb Q\)-factoriality (their free ranks) and finite class obstructions (their torsion). Wu's construction realizes every rational alpha value in a natural toric Fano range, so a complete fixed-point singularity-invariant formula across all parameters has a direct motivating object and use. The calculation reveals real arithmetic chamber structure: integer cube-slice versus noninteger slice, edge versus interior cases, and new higher-rank torsion patterns beyond the previously solved specialization. Its lattice-theoretic argument is reusable for other box-section toric fans. It makes no claim to global class groups, non-fixed singular strata or independent priority review.

## Closest literature and limits

- Xian Wu, arXiv:2608.14115v1: alpha-spectrum family and box-section data; only abstract/metadata and a separate secondary account inspected, not original full text.
- The published-finding corpus Liu--Zhu affine class-group result: exactly the specialization \((A,B)=(2n-1,2)\), fully subsumed as a special case and **excluded from new priority**.
- Cox--Little--Schenck, *Toric Varieties*, Theorem 4.1.3: general toric class-group exact sequence, used as an ingredient and not claimed as new.
- The full result depends on coprimality and on the exact trace-zero lattice. No statement is made for arbitrary nonprimitive parameters.
