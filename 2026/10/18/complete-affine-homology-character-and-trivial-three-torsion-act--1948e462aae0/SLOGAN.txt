For the Paley graph on seventeen vertices, the full affine group acts on the third rational homology of its open Lovász neighborhood complex as two copies of the irreducible cyclotomic representation \(\mathbb Q(\zeta_{17})\), plus the augmented regular representation of \(C_4\). Its nonzero integral \(\mathbb Z/3\) second-homology torsion is fixed by every automorphism, while no nonzero third-homology class is fixed by the full group.
