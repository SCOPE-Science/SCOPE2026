For ternary circular words with one \(b\), two \(a\)'s and \(h\) copies of \(c\), every fiber of the rotation-averaged Parikh matrix is either a singleton or one of exactly \(\max\{h-1,0\}\) explicit doubletons. Thus the exact number of observable profiles is \(\binom{h+2}{2}-\max\{h-1,0\}\), and all other necklaces in this family are uniquely recoverable.
