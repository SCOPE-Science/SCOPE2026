The formulas sum to the independently known automorphism-group order. An unlocated all-exponent table remains a residual risk, and the entire 2016 small-exponent source was inaccessible for complete-text comparison.

**Value — PASS.** This natural automorphism-invariant classification closes a coherent infinite-family portion of the exact fixed-point counting problem raised in Hayat–López-Aguayo–Abbas (2018). The invariant genuinely distinguishes fixed-subgroup cardinalities, and the exceptional top layers and characteristic-two exclusion are explained by the action on a two-dimensional residue quotient. Its unbounded interior is not a finite table or arbitrary slice.

**Limitations.** No theorem for unequal exponents both at least two, or for higher rank, follows. Finite enumeration does not establish the all-prime result; the proof does. No independent review has been conducted.
