For every prime \(p\) and integer \(m\ge4\), the complete fixed-point multiplicity spectrum of automorphisms of \(C_p\oplus C_{p^m}\) has an exact six-regime formula: its unbounded interior layers form a geometric progression, while its top two nonidentity layers do not depend on \(m\). This extends previously known isolated fixed-point layers and yields all diagonal tuple-orbit counts, without asserting priority for the already known ordered-pair case.
