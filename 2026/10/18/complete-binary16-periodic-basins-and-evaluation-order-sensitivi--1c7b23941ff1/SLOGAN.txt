For the chaotic quadratic map \(x\mapsto2-x^2\), every one of the \(32{,}769\) IEEE binary16 states has been assigned exactly to a periodic basin: one-final-rounding arithmetic yields six cycles, including a 94-cycle, whereas separately rounding the square yields five cycles, including a 44-cycle. The two protocols disagree on exactly \(1{,}842\) transitions and have distinct, explicitly rational long-run average biases despite the real map's symmetric zero-mean invariant law.
