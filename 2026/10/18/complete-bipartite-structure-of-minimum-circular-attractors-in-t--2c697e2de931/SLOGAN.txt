For each of the first twelve nontrivial finite Fibonacci words, every minimum circular string attractor is a pair, and the graph of all such pairs is a complete bipartite graph plus isolates, with exactly \(2^{m-1}\) edges at index \(m\). At length \(377\), exactly \(2{,}048\) optimal pairs form \(K_{64,32}\), and the constraint lengths that actually eliminate pairs are exactly the preceding Fibonacci lengths.
