In characteristic seven, genus-nine \(7\)-rank-zero Artin–Schreier curves have exactly three possible Cartier \(a\)-numbers: \(6\), \(7\), or \(9\), determined by the quadratic and linear coefficients of a depressed quartic defining polynomial. The classification includes the recently constructed minimal-automorphism curve and rules out \(a\)-number \(8\) throughout this natural stratum.
