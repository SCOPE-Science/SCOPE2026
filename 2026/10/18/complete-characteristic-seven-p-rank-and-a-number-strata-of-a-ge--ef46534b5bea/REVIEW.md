## originality

PASS

The positive-sign form y^2=(x^4+a x^2+1)(x^4+b x^2+1) equals Ohashi’s negative-sign standard form under parameter substitutions a->-a,b->-b. The exact object must be compared as Howe curves with Klein-four elliptic-quotient decomposition; all three elliptic Hasse invariants determine the p-divisible group, not just one direct Cartier rank. Ohashi’s all-p characteristic superspecial maximality theorem is broader in characteristic but narrower in invariant/focus. Borówka–Shatsila’s genus-three involution/Prym results cover quotient geometry in characteristic zero, not finite-characteristic Hasse-stratum intersections. General Hasse–Witt theory makes the calculation possible but does not give the six component equations or seven multiple intersections. Prior quotient isogenies imply that a-number is determined by three elliptic factors, but not where each factor is supersingular on this parameter plane or which nonordinary components meet. The new exact factors and geometric-elimination argument are necessary to derive the six rational irreducible components and exhaustive seven-point intersections.
