# Mathematical review

## Correctness

PASS. The smooth sixfold is the zero locus of two sections of the globally generated bundle \(\operatorname{Sym}^2\mathcal U^\vee\) on \(\operatorname{Gr}(3,9)\). Its codimension is \(12\), its tangent Chern series is the ambient series divided by the normal-bundle series, and its first Chern class is the Plücker class. The full top intersection table is an 84-fixed-point exact rational localization computation, independently reproduced with two unrelated torus weights. Independent Grassmannian coefficient extraction checks the volume \(3904\). No finite experiment is being extrapolated to an infinite theorem; localization applies to the whole stated geometric family.

## Originality

PASS, with priority risk. Ji–Suzuki (arXiv:2609.36233v1) supplies motivic calculations for the varieties and lines, not this second-maximal Fano scheme's intrinsic characteristic numbers. Belmans et al. (arXiv:2403.12517) and Chen–Vilonen–Xue (arXiv:1602.03052) cover Hodge/cohomological data. Consequently \(\chi_\mathrm{top}=256\) is already known and not counted as novel. Searches for the remaining exact Chern numbers, anticanonical degree and zero-locus aliases yielded no matching complete table. Standard intersection theory makes the entries computable in principle, and an older unindexed specific table might exist; the broader literature was not exhaustively checked.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The complete polarized characteristic-number table is a natural invariant of a distinguished smooth Fano sixfold, needed for numerical classification, Riemann–Roch and characteristic-class comparisons. It goes beyond the known cohomological Euler/Hodge information while keeping a self-contained geometrically motivated exact numerical goal, without asserting a new general theory of motives or Chow rings.

## Scope and limits

The result is for general smooth complex intersections of two quadrics in \(\mathbb P^8\). Known smoothness, topological Euler data and bundle formulas are explicitly distinguished from the ten remaining numerical evaluations. Generality, torsion, singular members and geometric Picard rank are not analyzed.
