On every complete split graph \(K_a\vee\overline K_b\), adjacency and negative-Laplacian continuous-time walks from one vertex have identical probabilities at all times exactly for complete graphs, or when \(b=2a-1\) and the start is in the independent part. The latter equality has a global-phase, sign-flip, and conjugation explanation, while every clique start in an irregular member has a nonzero fourth-order return-probability obstruction.
