Ordered commuting tuples in every finite Brandt inverse semigroup have an explicit exponential generating series in the commuting-tuple series of the local group, and that group series can be recovered recursively. For arbitrary finite local groups, uniformly sampling \(k_n\sim\lambda\sqrt n\) elements produces an asymptotic \(\operatorname{Poisson}(\lambda^2)\) number of noncommuting pairs, with pairwise-commutativity probability tending to \(e^{-\lambda^2}\).
