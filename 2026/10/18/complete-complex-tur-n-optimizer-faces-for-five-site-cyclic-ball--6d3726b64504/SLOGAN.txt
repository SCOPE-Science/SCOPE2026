For the five-site cyclic Turán problem the sharp scalar bound was already known, but the **entire complex equality face** is now classified: a unique real optimizer unless \(3\mid N\), then a triangle at \(N=6\) and an explicit four-vertex kite for every larger multiple of three. Its nonreal vertices have \(f(2)=e^{\pm2\pi i/N}/(1+2\cos(2\pi/N))\).
