All finite bounded rank-three Eulerian posets with \(2r+2\) elements share one complete \(C^2\) theory, although their latticehood and automorphism-orbit structure vary with a partition of \(r\) into cycle lengths. Three counting-variable names identify each such poset within the class at logarithmic quantifier rank, while latticehood first splits the two-variable theory at ten elements and orbit-refinability first fails at twelve; exact partition and divisor counts hold for every size.
