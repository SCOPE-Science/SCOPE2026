# Reproduction and limits

Execute `python3 verify_eulerian.py` and `python3 verify_incidence_enum.py` with Python 3.10 or newer. Both require only the Python standard library and have no downloaded dependencies. The supplied `eulerian_output.txt` and `incidence_output.txt` are the captured outputs of those commands. All files are self-contained and can be extracted from the enclosing package without network access.

`verify_eulerian.py` constructs every integer partition with parts at least two for each \(2\le r\le16\). Across these 230 posets it explicitly checks Eulerian interval parity, the existence of all pairwise meets and joins, the three-variable nonlattice witness predicate, stable directed counting-colour-refinement equivalence, full cycle-component recovery after logarithmically many relation squarings, the four-cell rank profile, lattice and nonlattice enumerators, the divisor formula for exact orbit-refinement, and the maximum distinct-cycle-size bound. For \(r\le5\), it independently enumerates all rank-preserving atom/coatom permutations and checks their automorphism orbits against the predicted sizes.

`verify_incidence_enum.py` independently enumerates all labeled simple bipartite incidence graphs of degree two on both sides for \(r=2,3,4,5\). Its admissible labeled-matrix counts are respectively \(1,6,90,2040\). It computes cycle-component types by graph traversal, tests the existence of pairs of atoms with two common coatoms, and checks that the atomic-type count profile depends only on \(r\).

The ten-point lattice/nonlattice pair is generated by `poset((4,))` and `poset((2,2))`. The twelve-point failure of orbit-exact refinement is generated by `poset((2,3))`. The direct general proof appears in `RESULT.md`, including quantifiers, the exact logical signature, all parameter ranges, and the limits of each computational experiment. Exhaustive tests through finite bounds are not offered as evidence of a theorem for infinitely many sizes; that theorem is supported by the mathematical argument.
