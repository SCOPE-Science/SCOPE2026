# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The covariance-kernel criterion is equivalence to constancy of linear forms on the positive-probability support. Before jamming the empty state and every one-dimer state are positive-probability witnesses, forcing exactly the alternating null direction. At jamming, the support is exactly the maximal-matchings' occupied-site vectors. Both adjacent-to-end sites are covered in every maximal matching; an explicit base-case table and the two-end induction prove that no fourth invariant is possible. All quantifiers, boundary cases and finite/infinite times are specified. The exact verifier checked 294 assertions, including genuine rational finite-time covariance calculations.

## Originality — PASS, with a substantial residual priority risk

The inspected Gerin paper establishes the probability model and vacancy statistics, not the stated time-resolved matrix-kernel classification. The inspected Klaassen–Runnenburg material addresses the total vacancy count. The 2019 maximal-matching-polytope paper supplies a general support description for trees through earlier LP results; that is a closely related broader formalism, and the terminal affine-dimension component may be derivable from it. It does not directly state the complete site-indicator covariance rank transition. An earlier implicit consequence cannot be ruled out, and the 1959 Page article could not be fully inspected. The assessment claims no independent novelty certification.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Site-occupation covariance is the covariance object for location-specific measurements of random sequential adsorption. Its full rank and kernel determine when spatial measurements retain nonredundant second-order information and which maximal principal minors can be inverted. The sharp two-dimensional loss of rank only at the terminal limit, with no interior-time transition, is a complete structural classification of a natural stochastic model; it is not merely a recomputed mean coverage fraction or a narrow arbitrary parameter slice.
