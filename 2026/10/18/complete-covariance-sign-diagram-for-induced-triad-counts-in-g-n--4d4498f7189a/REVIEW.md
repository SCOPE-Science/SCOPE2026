# Scientific review

**Correctness (C): PASS.** The exact covariance formula follows by classifying intersections of two unordered three-vertex sets and conditioning on their possible common edge. There are exactly \(3(n-3)\) second triples per first triple with one shared edge. Every displayed threshold comes from a factored polynomial with a strictly positive prefactor in \(0<p<1\). The all-parameter proof is analytic. The included exact-rational checker confirms all covariance entries by complete enumeration on three to five vertices; it does not prove the infinite statement.

**Originality (O): PASS on examined evidence, subject to historical priority risk.** The 2020 arXiv source studies sampling-based estimation of motifs, not the unsampled four-class induced triad joint-sign problem. The early directed conditioned-choice triad census literature derives covariance matrices under a different distribution; its full paper was not available for direct comparison. No equivalent all-pair sign classification was found in the targeted searches of published records and literature. A more general motif covariance result could yield this classification as a corollary, so independent priority investigation is still warranted.

**Value (V): PASS.** The four induced triad types are canonical graph summaries. Their full finite covariance-sign classification has a genuine two-switch dependence pattern for intermediate motif classes, not only isolated special-case calculations; it can inform qualitative dependence expectations when comparing network motif frequencies.

**Boundaries.** \(n\ge3\), \(0<p<1\), simple undirected independent-edge random graphs; finite-sample identities and signs only, no distributional normality or directed extension. The 1975 historical source remains a full-text access limitation.
