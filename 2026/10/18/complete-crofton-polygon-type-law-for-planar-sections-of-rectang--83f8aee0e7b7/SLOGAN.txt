A motion-invariant random plane meeting any rectangular box has an explicit four-probability law for triangular, quadrilateral, pentagonal, and hexagonal sections. Across all three-dimensional parallelotopes, the probabilities always satisfy \(p_5=p_3-2p_6\); for a cube, the Crofton hexagon probability is about \(0.04644769042\), distinct from the much larger central-section probability.
