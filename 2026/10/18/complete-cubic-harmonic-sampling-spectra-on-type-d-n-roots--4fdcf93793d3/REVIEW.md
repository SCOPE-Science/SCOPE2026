# Scientific review: cubic type-\(D_n\) root sampling

## Correctness — PASS
The independent coefficient parametrization of harmonic cubics verifies the full image and kernel, without using the spectral argument as a circular rank certificate. The Gegenbauer degree-three kernel identity is proved for every dimension, decomposed by the root-coordinate and odd-sample projectors, and yields the exact two-block singular spectrum. The pseudoinverse interpolation formula is exact. Eight finite dimension instances were checked with exact integer and rational arithmetic. Computations alone do not certify the infinite family.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
The closest full texts are Cohn–Conway–Elkies–Kumar on the 24-cell's spherical design and energy, Dai–Xu on general spherical harmonic reproducing kernels, and Hirao–Nozaki–Tasaka on lattice design properties. A separate earlier 24-cell cumulative-band calculation already implies the degree-three rank \(8\) at \(n=4\), and that numerical fact is not claimed novel. The inspected primary literature does not state or demonstrably imply the complete all-\(n\) type-\(D_n\) degree-three evaluation image, two-sector spectrum, nullspace classification, or unique dimension-10 tight frame. The foundational Delsarte–Goethals–Seidel spherical-code paper was identified but could not be inspected in full, so an earlier implicit eigenmatrix derivation is a meaningful unresolved literature risk. This verdict is not a claim of literature-wide uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Signed-coordinate root orbits are canonical finite spherical designs, and harmonic interpolation on them is a natural identifiability problem. A uniform answer identifies all invisible cubic harmonics, isolates four unexpected additional constraints on 24-cell samples, gives a closed-form minimum-norm interpolant, and singles out the unique nondegenerate root dimension with perfectly conditioned odd sampling. Exact matrix spectra support both sampling design and stability questions.

## Limits and external review
This concerns homogeneous harmonic degree three only, all sample roots and the specified spherical normalization. No assertion of independent mathematical validation or complete uniqueness is made. Inaccessible prior full text is described as a risk rather than as an excluded source.
