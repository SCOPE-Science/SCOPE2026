On the ten antipodal vertex axes of a regular dodecahedron, single-degree spherical-harmonic interpolation has ranks \(1,3,5,7,9,6,10,10,9,10,10,10,10,10,9\) in degrees \(0\) through \(14\), and full rank in **every** degree \(\ell\ge15\). A three-sector signed-Petersen Gram decomposition determines every interpolation obstruction and the minimum-norm reconstruction.
