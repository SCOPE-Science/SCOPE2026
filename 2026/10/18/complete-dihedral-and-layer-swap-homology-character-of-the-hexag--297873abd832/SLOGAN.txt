The hexagonal prism has a six-dimensional perfect-matching homology group whose natural rational dihedral and layer-exchange action splits as \(\big((A\oplus B\oplus V_1)\boxtimes\mathbf1\big)\oplus(V_2\boxtimes\varepsilon)\). In particular, the half-turn followed by layer exchange has trace \(-4\), despite every nontrivial pure rotation having trace zero.
