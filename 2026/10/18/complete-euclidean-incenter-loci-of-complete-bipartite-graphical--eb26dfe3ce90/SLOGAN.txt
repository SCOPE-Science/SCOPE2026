For the standard graphical zonotope of \(K_{p,q}\), the complete Euclidean incenter set has affine dimension \(p-1\) when \(2\le p<q\), is a singleton when \(p=q\ge3\), and is exceptionally a segment when \(p=q=2\). The \(K_{2,q}\) incenter segment and its parity-dependent binomial counts of endpoint-tangent facets are explicit; the scalar inradius itself was previously known.
