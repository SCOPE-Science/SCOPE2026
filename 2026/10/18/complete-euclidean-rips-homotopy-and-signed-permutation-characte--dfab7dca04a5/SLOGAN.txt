For the fourteen Euclidean vertices of the rhombic dodecahedron, the inclusive Vietoris–Rips complexes pass successively through fourteen points, a wedge of eleven circles, \(S^2\), \(S^3\), \(S^6\), and a contractible complex, at the sharp radii \(\sqrt3,2,\sqrt8,\sqrt{11},\sqrt{12}\). A certified eighty-pair collapse preserves the cube-vertex three-sphere, and the full octahedral symmetry acts on its top homology by the sign of the permutation of four body diagonals.
