For any cyclic planar normal grid with consecutive angular gaps smaller than \(\pi\), the vertex-normal cone has exactly \(2n\) facets and its extreme rays modulo translations are precisely duplicated antipodal pairs and origin-surrounding triples. On the regular \(n\)-grid this produces the sharp parity-dependent ray counts \(n(n^2-1)/3\) for odd \(n\), and \(n(n-2)(n-4)/3+2n\) for even \(n\).
