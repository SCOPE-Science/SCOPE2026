The matrix's checked annihilator has unique dominant eigenvalue two, and the aligned-block argument transfers its spectral error to ordinary natural density. The exact cubic recurrence for dyadic counts proves a sharp lower-bound subsequence. Finite arithmetic and independent binomial-sum replay check all critical identities; they are corroboration, not a substitute for the infinite proof.

**Originality: PASS, with residual risk.** Rowland–Yassawi Theorem 3.28 already states the pointwise formula, which is explicitly credited. Its Section 3.6 does not report four-consecutive-term frequencies, the 24-word census or sharp \(\sqrt N\) discrepancy. The inspected Rampersad–Shallit article instead studies Motzkin/Catalan automatic congruences; Malik–Straub treats Lucas congruences and periodicity. Available mathematical record searches have not identified an explicit stronger theorem covering this fixed four-term statistic, though general automatic-sequence algorithms can in principle derive it. An unindexed equivalent evaluation remains a meaningful originality risk.

**Value: PASS.** Complete neighboring-residue support is a natural arithmetic correlation invariant for Apéry's classical sequence. Eight otherwise parity-compatible quadruples are rigorously forbidden, the remaining 24 have two different exact rational frequencies, and the optimal square-root fluctuation strengthens the classification beyond a finite-state recognition exercise. The result gives the fully evaluated answer for a fixed natural word length and modulus; no general result for arbitrary lengths is claimed.
