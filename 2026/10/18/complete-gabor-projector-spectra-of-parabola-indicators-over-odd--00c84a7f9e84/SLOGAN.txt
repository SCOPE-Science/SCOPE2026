The full Gabor projector orbit of the normalized parabola indicator over every odd cyclic ring \(\mathbb Z/p^k\mathbb Z\), \(k\ge2\), has a completely explicit power-of-\(p\) Gram spectrum, including all multiplicities and null directions. Its nonzero eigenvalue exponents form an arithmetic-gap pattern, and the fraction of linearly identifiable matrix directions converges exactly to \(p^2/(p^2+p+1)\) as the ring depth increases.
