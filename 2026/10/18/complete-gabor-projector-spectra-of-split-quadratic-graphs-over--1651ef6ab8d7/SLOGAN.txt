For the normalized indicator of the split quadratic graph \(z=x\cdot y\) over any finite field, the complete Gabor-projector Gram spectrum has four nonzero levels in odd characteristic but only three in characteristic two, with exact multiplicities in every dimension \(2r+1\) for \(r\ge2\). This gives explicit all-degree matrix-recovery ranks and a sharp arithmetic discontinuity between the two characteristics.
