For a uniformly oriented plane through the center of the regular truncated octahedron, the section has exactly \(6\), \(8\), or \(10\) vertices, with respective probabilities approximately \(0.498450689917704\), \(0.316108855580191\), and \(0.185440454502106\). The exact decagonal probability is a single spherical-triangle solid angle per signed-permutation sector, and the full law follows from the exact mean \(36\arccos(4/5)/\pi\).
