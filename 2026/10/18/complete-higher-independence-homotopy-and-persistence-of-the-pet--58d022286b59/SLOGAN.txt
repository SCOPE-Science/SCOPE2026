The complete higher-independence filtration of the Petersen graph has integral homotopy types ranging from \(\bigvee^{11}S^3\) through \(\bigvee^{74}S^5\) to a final sphere and then a point. Its reduced \(\mathbb F_2\)-persistence has exactly one degree-three class surviving from parameter \(2\) to \(5\), alongside ten new degree-three classes born at parameter \(4\).
