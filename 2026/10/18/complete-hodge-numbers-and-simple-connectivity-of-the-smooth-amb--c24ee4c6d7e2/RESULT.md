# Complete Hodge numbers and simple connectivity of the smooth Ambro–Kawamata counterexample

## Finding

The smooth projective fourfold \(Y\) in Ajit's recently constructed smooth effective-nonvanishing counterexample is simply connected. Its full Hodge-number formula is

\[
h^{p,q}(Y)=\mathbf1_{3\mid(p-q)}\binom{4}{p}\binom{4}{q}+81\,\mathbf1_{p=q\in\{1,2,3\}},\qquad 0\le p,q\le4.
\]

In particular its rational Betti vector in degrees zero through eight is

\[
(1,0,97,8,117,8,97,0,1).
\]

The topological Euler characteristic is \(297\), the oriented fourfold's signature is \(-75\), and its holomorphic Euler characteristic is \(-3\). The fourfold has \(h^{3,0}=4\), \(h^{4,0}=0\), and \(h^{1,1}=97\). The numerical and topological assertions refer to the smooth quotient, not the terminal singular fourfold preceding it.

## Assumptions and scope

Work throughout over \(\mathbb C\). Let \(E\subset\mathbb P^2\) be the Fermat cubic \(u^3+v^3+w^3=0\) with origin \([0:-1:1]\). Fix a primitive cube root \(\zeta\), and let \(\rho:E\to E\) send \([u:v:w]\) to \([\zeta u:v:w]\). This automorphism fixes precisely three points and acts by multiplication by \(\zeta\) on the tangent space at the origin. Write \(A=E^4\), apply \(\rho\) diagonally, and let \(F=A^{\langle\rho\rangle}\). There are \(81\) fixed points. Denote their blow-up by \(\widetilde A=\operatorname{Bl}_F A\). Define

\[
Y=\widetilde A/\langle\rho\rangle.
\]

The action on each exceptional divisor \(\mathbb P^3\) is trivial; near that divisor it acts on a normal coordinate by a cube root of unity. Accordingly the quotient is smooth and projective; these are construction properties established in Ajit's Section 5.1. We use ordinary complex singular cohomology with rational coefficients for the Betti assertions.

## Proof

**Invariant Hodge theory.** The complex torus \(A\) has \(\dim H^{1,0}(A)=\dim H^{0,1}(A)=4\). Multiplication by \(\rho\) acts by \(\zeta\) on \(H^{1,0}\) and by \(\zeta^{-1}\) on \(H^{0,1}\), so its scalar on

\[
H^{p,q}(A)=\bigwedge^pH^{1,0}(A)\otimes\bigwedge^qH^{0,1}(A)
\]

is \(\zeta^{p-q}\). Therefore the invariant summand has dimension \(\binom4p\binom4q\) when \(p-q\) is divisible by three, and zero otherwise.

**Blow-up summands.** The ordinary blow-up formula for a smooth fourfold at a point adds one class of Hodge bidegree \((j,j)\) for each \(j=1,2,3\). There are \(81\) centers. Every center is individually fixed, and the derivative action is scalar, so the group fixes its exceptional \(\mathbb P^3\) pointwise and acts trivially on all three new Hodge classes. Thus

\[
H^{p,q}(\widetilde A)^{\langle\rho\rangle}
\cong H^{p,q}(A)^{\langle\rho\rangle}
\oplus\mathbb C^{81\mathbf1_{p=q\in\{1,2,3\}}}.
\]

For a finite group acting algebraically on a smooth projective complex variety with a smooth projective quotient, transfer/averaging identifies the quotient's rational cohomology, compatibly with Hodge structures, with the group-invariant rational cohomology upstairs. This statement does not require a free group action. Applying it to \(\widetilde A\to Y\) proves the complete formula.

**Numerical consequences.** Only the Hodge numbers \(h^{0,0}=h^{4,4}=1\), \(h^{0,3}=h^{3,0}=h^{1,4}=h^{4,1}=4\), \(h^{1,1}=h^{3,3}=97\), and \(h^{2,2}=117\) are nonzero. Taking diagonals gives the displayed Betti vector, hence

\[
\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(Y)=1+97-8+117-8+97+1=297.
\]

The signature identity for compact Kähler fourfolds, \(\sigma(Y)=\sum_{p,q}(-1)^q h^{p,q}(Y)\), gives \(\sigma(Y)=-75\). Likewise \(\chi(\mathcal O_Y)=\sum_q(-1)^q h^{0,q}(Y)=1-4=-3\).

**Fundamental group.** Write the universal cover of \(A\) as \(\mathbb C^4\), with period lattice \(\Lambda\). The scalar elliptic automorphism acts on the lattice by multiplication by \(\zeta\), so the group of deck transformations combined with the cyclic automorphism is \(\Gamma=\Lambda\rtimes\langle\rho\rangle\). Let \(\widehat U\) be the blow-up of \(\mathbb C^4\) at all lifts of the \(81\) fixed points. This center is discrete and locally finite. Blowing up finitely many points in a complex four-dimensional ball replaces them by simply connected exceptional \(\mathbb P^3\)'s, without changing the fundamental group. Exhaustion by such balls, or van Kampen, therefore gives \(\pi_1(\widehat U)=1\). The properly discontinuous \(\Gamma\)-action on \(\widehat U\) has orbit space \(Y\).

For any \(\lambda\in\Lambda\), the element of \(\Gamma\) acting on the original affine cover by \(z\mapsto\zeta z+\lambda\) fixes the point \(z=\lambda/(1-\zeta)\). Its projection to \(A\) lies in \(F\), so on the blow-up it fixes an entire exceptional \(\mathbb P^3\). The generator \(z\mapsto\zeta z\) also has fixed points. Every lattice translation is a product of two such fixed-point elements, because \((\lambda,\rho)(0,\rho)^{-1}=(\lambda,1)\). Consequently the normal subgroup of \(\Gamma\) generated by all elements having fixed points is the whole of \(\Gamma\). Armstrong's orbit-space fundamental-group theorem now yields

\[
\pi_1(Y)=\Gamma/\langle\!\langle\text{elements with fixed points}\rangle\!\rangle=1.
\]

The theorem applies to a properly discontinuous action on the simply connected locally path-connected space \(\widehat U\), with finite stabilizers.

## Verification

The independent arithmetic cross-check in `verify.py` calculates all \(25\) Hodge entries from the explicit formula; verifies Hodge symmetry, Serre duality, the Betti vector and both Euler characteristics; and checks the signature calculation. It also verifies the Euler characteristic independently by the fixed-point formula: \(\chi(\widetilde A)=0+81(4-1)=243\), each nonidentity group element fixes the \(81\) exceptional \(\mathbb P^3\)'s of total Euler characteristic \(324\), and hence \(\chi(Y)=(243+324+324)/3=297\). Run `python3 verify.py` in the directory containing the file; the expected output is `VERIFY_OK`.

This arithmetic check is not a computer proof of the transfer theorem or the fundamental-group theorem; the mathematical proof above supplies the needed arguments and hypotheses.

## Relationship to prior work

Ajit, *Counterexamples to the Ambro–Kawamata effective non-vanishing conjecture* (arXiv:2610.09270v1, first posted October 7, 2026), constructs \(Y\), proves the smooth quotient description, and studies missing global sections and related divisor positivity. Its Sections 4–5 give the fixed-point count, blow-up geometry, and canonical-bundle data used here. The inspected text does not state the complete Hodge diamond, Betti vector, signature, or simple connectivity.

The ingredients are classical: the Hodge cohomology of an abelian variety, the blow-up formula, finite-quotient averaging, and Armstrong's theorem. The contribution is their exact combined application to this newly constructed smooth counterexample, including its complete Hodge table and fundamental group; there is no claim that these general techniques are themselves new. Work on Calabi–Yau resolutions of finite quotients of abelian varieties, including Gachet's 2024 treatment, has different hypotheses and does not supply this noncrepant smooth quotient's invariants. No claim of exhaustive novelty certification is made.

## Limitations

Only the characteristic-zero smooth fourfold \(Y\) is considered. The computations do not determine the integral cohomology torsion, intersection pairing, cup-product ring, derived category, rationality, or the topology of all higher-dimensional variants. The fundamental-group argument uses standard topological orbit-space theory, not an explicit simplicial presentation. The observed lack of prior explicit tables is conditional on accessible literature; a broader theorem may contain an equivalent application.
