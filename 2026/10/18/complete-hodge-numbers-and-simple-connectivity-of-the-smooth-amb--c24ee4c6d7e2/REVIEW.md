# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof uses the explicitly defined diagonal cubic-elliptic action and its \(81\) fixed points, the standard point-blow-up Hodge decomposition, and the invariant-cohomology transfer theorem for finite quotients. The resulting Hodge formula is checked against Hodge symmetry, duality, and two independent Euler computations. Simple connectivity follows from a specific lifted group-action computation meeting Armstrong's hypotheses: the elements that fix lifted exceptional divisors generate the full semidirect product. Finite arithmetic checks do not replace the geometric proof.

**Originality — PASS, subject to the literature risks below.** Ajit's construction and divisor results do not explicitly calculate the full Hodge polynomial or fundamental group. The closest same-construction calculations in available records concern numerical Cartier classes and positivity, not topology. Armstrong gives a general orbit-space formula, and Gachet treats different crepant Calabi–Yau quotient hypotheses. Exact fingerprint, equivalent-presentation, and broader-coverage searches did not identify a complete overlapping result; this is evidence with residual incompleteness, not proof of absolute novelty.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The new smooth four-dimensional effective-nonvanishing counterexample is naturally worth classifying: the complete Hodge structure, \(b_2=97\), Euler \(297\), signature \(-75\), and simple connectivity reveal topology not supplied by its section-count or positivity data. The determination includes all rational Hodge dimensions rather than only one easily inferred invariant.

**Sources and limitations.** Rahul Ajit, arXiv:2610.09270v1, Sections 4–5.1; M. A. Armstrong, 1968, DOI:10.1017/S0305004100042845; C. Gachet, 2024, *Finite quotients of abelian varieties with a Calabi–Yau resolution* (distinct assumptions). The argument is characteristic-zero and concerns this particular smooth fourfold. Integral torsion and the rational cohomology ring are not computed, and an unindexed/implicit equivalent literature result cannot be ruled out.
