The smooth fourfold in Ajit's 2026 Ambro–Kawamata counterexample is simply connected and has rational Betti vector \((1,0,97,8,117,8,97,0,1)\). Its complete Hodge-number formula is \(h^{p,q}=\mathbf1_{3\mid(p-q)}\binom4p\binom4q+81\mathbf1_{p=q\in\{1,2,3\}}\), giving Euler characteristic \(297\) and signature \(-75\).
