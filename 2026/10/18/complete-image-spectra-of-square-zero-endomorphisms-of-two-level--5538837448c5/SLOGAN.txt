For every prime \(p\), the square-zero endomorphisms of \(C_{p^2}\oplus C_{p^b}\) have a complete cyclic-versus-noncyclic image spectrum, with sharp image-exponent cutoffs and parity-dependent total counts. The exact number is \((2p^2-1)p^{b/2+1}\) for even \(b\ge2\), and \((3p-2)p^{(b+3)/2}\) for odd \(b\ge3\).
