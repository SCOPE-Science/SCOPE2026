For the Heawood graph, the induced-forest degree filtration has successive integral homotopy types \(\bigvee^6S^3\), \(\bigvee^{13}S^5\), \(S^6\), and \(\bigvee^8S^8\), and stabilizes at degree three. The first stage is known; the subsequent stages have no persistent reduced homology classes across successive degree thresholds, giving exact interval multiplicities \(6,13,1,8\) in dimensions \(3,5,6,8\), respectively.
