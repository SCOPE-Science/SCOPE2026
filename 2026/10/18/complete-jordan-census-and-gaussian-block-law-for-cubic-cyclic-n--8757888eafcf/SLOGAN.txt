For the length-three truncated path algebra of every directed cycle over \(\mathbb F_q\), the complete Jordan-type distribution of left multiplication by radical elements is an explicit \(2\times2\) transfer-matrix trace. Its three-block count skips the value \(n-1\), and the joint counts of three-blocks and null three-dimensional summands satisfy a nondegenerate bivariate Gaussian limit with explicit covariance.
