# Scientific review

**Correctness: PASS.** Exact elimination of the double-cover quartic produces the discriminant and all exceptional parameters. The nine simple base points, Kodaira types of four reducible fibers, and uniqueness of the cuspidal collision are checked independently by geometry. The attached symbolic checker validates the polynomial identities; it does not by itself prove the analytic Kodaira identifications. Each fiber basket has total Euler contribution \(12\).

**Originality: PASS with bibliographic reservations.** The earliest verified preprint is Suluyer (9 December 2024); the 2026 full-text Section 4 states four reducible members and a single \(a=2\) nodal example, but not the uniform parameter stratification. Standard Kodaira and Persson classifications supply the category of fiber types, not this family’s explicit locus. The author's August 2024 thesis is relevant but unavailable in full, leaving an earlier-source dating and possible-coverage risk. No earlier work is represented as exhaustively excluded.

**Value: PASS.** The natural singular-fiber geometry of a parameterized rational elliptic surface is classified completely and the special parameters separate precise tangencies, triangles, and cusps. These geometric transitions are not implied merely by knowing the number of reducible fibers.

Limitations: only nine-simple-basepoint pencils; no arithmetic or Mordell–Weil classification. Relevant sources: Suluyer arXiv:2412.06928; Suluyer, Mediterr. J. Math. 23 (2026), 127; Miranda (1989, 1990).
