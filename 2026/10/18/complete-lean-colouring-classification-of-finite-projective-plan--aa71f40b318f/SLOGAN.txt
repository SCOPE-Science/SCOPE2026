For the line hypergraph of any finite projective plane of order \(q\ge2\), lean colourings are exactly the all-distinct colourings and the colourings with one monochromatic line and all other points distinctly coloured. The lean number is \(q^2+1\), with only \(q^2+q+1\) as another feasible number of colours, giving an infinite family that violates the asserted hypergraph-incidence-degree bound \(\operatorname{Lean}(H)\le\Delta(H)+1\).
