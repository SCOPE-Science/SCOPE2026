For the matched two-clique prism \(K_2\square K_n\), all locating-total dominating sets admit a five-condition classification by the occupied columns, yielding a closed exact polynomial for every \(n\ge2\). The minimum falls from \(n\) to \(n-1\) at \(n=5\), and for \(n\ge5\) exactly \(n(2^{n-1}-2n)\) optimum sets occur.
