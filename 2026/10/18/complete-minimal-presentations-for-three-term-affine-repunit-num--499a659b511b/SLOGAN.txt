For every three-term affine repunit semigroup \(\langle a,ba+d,b^2a+(b+1)d\rangle\) with \(\gcd(a,d)=1\), an explicit uniform minimal binomial presentation has two or three generators. In the genuinely three-generated range the defining ideal is a complete intersection exactly when \((b+1)\mid a\); otherwise its three exact Betti degrees are obtained by dividing \(a\) by \(b+1\).
