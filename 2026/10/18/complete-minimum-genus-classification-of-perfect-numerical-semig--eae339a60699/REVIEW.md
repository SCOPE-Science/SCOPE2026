# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof derives a forced-gap reflection inequality for every perfect numerical semigroup and analyzes equality according to the number of retained intervals. The odd case has one possible block, and the even case has exactly the listed one- and two-block patterns. Additive closure and gap lengths are verified both ways. A separate exhaustive finite checker matches every minimum-genus object for Frobenius numbers from \(3\) through \(20\), and checks all constructed members through \(300\). These computations corroborate, rather than replace, the unbounded argument.

## Originality — PASS, conditional on inspected coverage

The first public 2023 Moreno-Frías–Rosales paper gives an algorithm for \(eta(F)\) and the value \(eta(11)=7\), not the parity-dependent complete equality classification and count. Their 2019 study develops foundational perfect-semigroup algorithms. The 2026 type-two paper provides a general injection/genus inequality, closely related to the reflection estimate, but does not enumerate the perfect minimum-genus equality cases. The present theorem treats the complete genus-minimum family, including both even-Frobenius pattern types. General fixed-Frobenius genus distributions do not classify this restricted extremal family. Related inaccessible type-two material remains a residual risk, and searchable records cannot establish global uniqueness of a result.

## Value — PASS

The originating fixed-Frobenius covariety paper made the minimum genus \(eta(F)\) an algorithmically computed threshold. The closed formula, complete extremizer classification, and exact odd-versus-even enumeration replace case-by-case threshold computation with a structural result over every Frobenius number. The theorem is not merely the elementary lower genus inequality: it proves a uniform exhaustion of all equality cases, with an unbounded number of extremizers on even Frobenius numbers.
