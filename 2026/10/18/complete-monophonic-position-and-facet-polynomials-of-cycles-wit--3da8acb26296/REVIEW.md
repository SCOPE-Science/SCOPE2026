# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The independent-path localization lemma forbids private-clique vertices in path interiors. The exhaustive core-count case split (zero, one or two core vertices), the unique-common-neighbor property of cycles of length at least five, and a separate classification of inclusion-maximal configurations give the two formulas. Seven exhaustive finite instances (78,848 subsets in total) agreed with both formulas, including nonuniform private cliques. Computation is supplementary; the written argument proves the infinite families. The four-cycle exception is excluded.

## Originality

**PASS, with residual risk.** Thomas et al. prove only the scalar maximum of ordinary graph coronas, while the present theorem counts every monophonic-position set and every facet, including nonuniform private clique orders. Iršič et al. count shortest-path general-position sets and identify induced-path position polynomials as future research. Comparisons of their full relevant texts, targeted literature queries, related indexed findings and accessible previous claims identified no theorem implying the two formulas. Indexing gaps and uninspected manuscripts remain priority risks; novelty is not an independence certification.

## Value

**PASS.** The result provides the complete enumerative refinement requested in the general-position-polynomial literature for a natural graph-operation family and establishes a stable total facet count across all positive clique-size profiles. The same family realizes five distinct maximal-set cardinalities in the uniform case and arbitrarily large differences between smallest and largest facets, demonstrating structural nonpurity invisible to the known scalar maximum parameter.

## Scope and verification limits

The proof covers \(n\ge5\) with all private clique orders positive. It does not claim similar formulas for shorter cycle cores, arbitrary attachments or geodesic general position. The checks are exhaustive only on their listed finite profiles. Independent scientific validation and exhaustive bibliographic priority verification remain outstanding.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
