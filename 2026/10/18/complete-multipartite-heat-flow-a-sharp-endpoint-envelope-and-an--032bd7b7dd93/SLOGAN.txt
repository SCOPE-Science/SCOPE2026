For the unnormalized heat semigroup on any finite complete multipartite graph, the exact endpoint derivative norm is a maximum of explicit two-exponential expressions indexed by its distinct part sizes, with an irreversible transition from smaller to larger parts as time grows. Its sharp time-scaled bound is \(2(n-1)/(en)\), attained precisely when a singleton part exists, while distinct nonisomorphic graphs can have identical norm profiles despite different Laplacian spectra.
