# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof uses exact hypergeometric two-plant extinction probabilities, the degree-prescribed total of pairwise neighborhood intersections, discrete convexity, and the rigid equality case. An independent exact-arithmetic enumeration of the stated small incidence classes and direct removal-subset moments reproduced the claimed identities. The general theorem is analytic, not inferred from enumeration.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** The closest ecological model is Memmott, Waser and Price (2004), read in full; its random-removal species curves do not establish a degree-regular variance maximum. The related projective-plane result concerns a minimum, not this maximum. Zhao (2009) treats an independent-set enumerator with the same characteristic biclique extremizer but not the same random observable. Bane, Pocock and James (2018) treat a distinct integrated robustness statistic. The 2010 Kaiser-Bunbury paper was limited to its accessible abstract and listings; general incidence-moment inequalities outside inspected literature remain a bibliographic risk.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** This is an all-order, all-stage sharp variance envelope with a unique active-stage extremizer and an exact overlap-sensitive stability gap. Equal degrees imply equal means, making the contrast in uncertainty structurally meaningful, not a parameter-normalization artifact. The model allows disconnected webs and does not describe dynamic ecosystem persistence.
