In characteristic \(41\), the genus-four dihedral family \(y^2=x^{10}+s x^5+1\) has exactly eleven nonordinary geometric isomorphism classes: one known superspecial class and ten classes of \(p\)-rank \(2\) and \(a\)-number \(2\), whose coarse-moduli Frobenius orbit degrees are \(1,3,6\). Exact Cartier factorizations classify every smooth parameter and show that all remaining members are ordinary.
