For the Huynh–Ouannas–Wang–Pham–Nguyen–Alsaadi planar map without fixed points, every primitive two-cycle is parameterized by a positive root of \((c-1/2-b/4)t^2+at-d-4b/(1+b)^2=0\), giving an exact all-positive-parameter census of zero, one, or two cycles. The two-cycle branches can merge with a \(+1\) return multiplier or split as one branch escapes through infinity; explicit return-multiplier formulas identify a rational example with an attracting cycle coexisting with an unstable one.
