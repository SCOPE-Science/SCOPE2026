For every integer coefficient \(c\), the generalized Narayana recurrence modulo \(2^k\) has an exact cycle spectrum: odd coefficients yield \(7\cdot2^j\)-cycles in a full permutation, while even coefficients leave precisely \(2^k\) periodic states, with cycle lengths determined sharply by \(
u_2(c)\) and \(
u_2(c+2)\). The remaining \(2^{3k}-2^k\) states are transient for even \(c\).
