Every central planar section of the equal-radius Steinmetz bicylinder has area between \(\pi R^2\) and \(\pi\sqrt2\,R^2\), with exact and uniquely classified minimizers and maximizers; the same upper bound holds for all affine planes. An explicit inverse-tangent formula and a strict balancing principle classify the whole directional profile, including a unique interior dip along diagonal-normal tilts.
