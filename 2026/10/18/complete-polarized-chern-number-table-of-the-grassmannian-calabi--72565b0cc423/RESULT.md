# Complete polarized Chern-number table of the Grassmannian Calabi–Yau sixfold

## Finding
Let \(G=\operatorname{Gr}(2,10)\) be the Grassmannian of two-planes in \(\mathbb C^{10}\) under the Plücker embedding, and let \(X=G\cap L\) be a smooth complete intersection with a projective linear subspace \(L\) of codimension ten. Put \(H=c_1(\mathcal O_X(1))\) and \(c_i=c_i(T_X)\). Then \(X\) is a smooth Calabi–Yau sixfold, and its **complete polarized top-degree Chern-number table** is

\[
\begin{array}{c|r}
\text{characteristic monomial}&\text{integral over }X\\\hline
H^6&1430\\
c_2H^4&4004\\
c_3H^3&-4290\\
c_4H^2&9350\\
c_5H&-15180\\
c_6&24920\\
c_2^2H^2&11330\\
c_2c_3H&-12210\\
c_2^3&32480\\
c_2c_4&26720\\
c_3^2&13200
\end{array}
\]

The intrinsic numbers \(\int_X c_2^3=32480\), \(\int_X c_2c_4=26720\), and \(\int_X c_3^2=13200\) refine the already-known Hodge-theoretic information. More generally, the table gives all degree-six pairings of tangent Chern classes with the Plücker polarization. It also yields the Hilbert polynomial
\[
\chi(X,\mathcal O_X(mH))=
\frac{143}{72}m^6+\frac{1001}{72}m^4+\frac{154}{9}m^2+2
\qquad(m\in\mathbb Z),
\]
by Hirzebruch–Riemann–Roch; the degree and Hilbert polynomial themselves are standard consequences of Grassmannian intersection theory and are not claimed as separately new.

## Assumptions and scope
Work over \(\mathbb C\). The ten defining hyperplanes are chosen to have a smooth six-dimensional intersection; a general choice works by Bertini. The notation \(\int_X\) means the degree of a codimension-six Chow class, \(H\) is the restricted Plücker class, and \(c_i\) denotes the **tangent** Chern class, not a Chern character or a class of the normal bundle. The Chern monomials listed have total complex codimension six. Adjunction gives \(K_X\cong\mathcal O_X\), so \(c_1(T_X)=0\); hence the eleven displayed monomials exhaust all such products in \(H\) and the tangent Chern classes. These numerical data are constant in the smooth deformation family.

## Proof
**1. Normal sequence and Chern-root normalization.** Let \(S\) denote the tautological rank-two subbundle on \(G\), and let \(x,y\) be the formal roots of \(S^*\). Then \(H=x+y\), \(T_G=S^*\otimes (\mathbb C^{10}/S)\), and in complex \(K\)-theory
\[
[T_G]=10[S^*]-[S^*\otimes S].
\]
The normal bundle of \(X\) in \(G\) is \(\mathcal O_X(H)^{\oplus10}\). Therefore
\[
[T_X]=10[S^*]-[S^*\otimes S]-10[\mathcal O(H)],
\qquad
c(T_X)=\frac{(1+x)^{10}(1+y)^{10}}{\bigl(1-(x-y)^2\bigr)(1+x+y)^{10}},
\]
where the right side is expanded and truncated at cohomological codimension six. Its codimension-one term is zero, in agreement with adjunction. For transparent checks, the first terms are
\[
c_2=x^2+8xy+y^2,\qquad c_3=-10xy(x+y),
\]
\[
c_4=x^4+16x^3y+51x^2y^2+16xy^3+y^4.
\]

**2. Exact integration functional.** If \(F(x,y)\) is a symmetric homogeneous polynomial of total degree sixteen, integration on \(G\) is the Schubert residue
\[
\int_G F(x,y)=[x^9y^8]\bigl((x-y)F(x,y)\bigr).
\]
The ten hyperplane equations represent \(H^{10}\), so for any codimension-six class \(Q\) built from the tangent Chern classes and \(H\),
\[
\int_X Q=[x^9y^8]\bigl((x-y)(x+y)^{10}Q(x,y)\bigr).
\]
The formula is an exact finite integer/rational coefficient extraction, not a limiting argument or sampling rule. Substitution of the displayed total Chern polynomial gives all eleven table entries. As one sample calculated without any software, using \(c_3=-10xyH\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
\int_X c_3^2
&=100[x^9y^8]\bigl((x-y)x^2y^2(x+y)^{12}\bigr)\\
&=100\left(\binom{12}{6}-\binom{12}{5}\right)=13200.
\end{aligned}
\]
Every remaining row is obtained by the same explicit finite binomial expansion; all coefficients and the integration algorithm are specified in the standalone exact verifier.

**3. Completeness of the numerical table.** A degree-six monomial made from \(H,c_2,c_3,c_4,c_5,c_6\) is indexed by a partition of six with allowed part sizes one through six (the part one represents \(H\)). The possible partitions, and hence the possible monomials, are precisely the eleven rows. No unspecified top-degree product remains. The statement is about the natural subring generated by these characteristic classes and \(H\), not the full intersection pairing on all algebraic cycles of \(X\).

**4. Hilbert polynomial as a consequence.** Riemann–Roch on the sixfold with \(c_1(T_X)=0\) has Todd contributions in degrees zero, two, four and six. Substituting the rows \(H^6=1430\), \(c_2H^4=4004\), \(c_2^2H^2=11330\), and \(c_4H^2=9350\), together with \(\chi(\mathcal O_X)=2\), gives the displayed polynomial. For example \(\chi(\mathcal O_X(H))=35\). This is a consistency consequence, not an additional independent theorem.

## Verification
Run `python3 verify.py` using Python 3 and its standard library; no external data or nonstandard packages are needed. The verifier reconstructs all six truncated Chern polynomials from exact rational expansions of the root formula, evaluates all eleven Schubert residues, and independently recomputes all eleven numbers using Atiyah–Bott localization at the \(\binom{10}{2}=45\) torus-fixed points of \(G\). The two evaluation routes agree entry-by-entry. The verifier also evaluates the characteristic-class \(\chi_y\) genus by a separate exact seven-value polynomial calculation and obtains the previously published Hodge-theoretic values, making that comparison a cross-check rather than a novelty claim. The expected output starts with `VERIFY_OK fixed_points=45 degree=1430 euler=24920 middle_betti=24912`. All arithmetic is exact with Python integers and rational fractions.

## Relationship to prior work
Bolognesi–Ciliberto–Verra [1] study the general codimension-ten Calabi–Yau section of \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,10)\) as the \(n=9\) instance of their projective-geometric and Pfaffian rationality constructions; their displayed results do not give this full eleven-entry Chern-number table. Crucially, Fatighenti–Mongardi [2, Appendix B, Table 5] have **already calculated the Hodge numbers of the equivalent family** \(A(2,7)\), which they explicitly identify with a deformation of ten linear sections of \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,10)\): notably \(h^{5,1}=251\), \(h^{4,2}=5202\), and \(h^{3,3}=14004\). These Hodge numbers, the resulting Euler characteristic \(24920\), and the complete Hodge diamond are **prior work, not a new result here**. The new claim is narrower: the complete tangent Chern-number *intersection table*, in particular the polarized values and characteristic products not stated by those sources. The universal Chern-root and Schubert rules are classical tools; this table is their explicit specialization. Inoue–Ito–Miura [3] develop a broad classification method and focus their numerical classification on Calabi–Yau threefolds; no full corresponding sixfold Chern table was located in the accessible materials. This comparison does not constitute a proof of worldwide priority.

## Limitations
This is a numerical characteristic-class calculation for smooth general linear sections, not a classification of their algebraic cycles, their integral cohomology, or special singular intersections. Hodge numbers and the Euler characteristic were already known in [2]. The exact Chern numbers arise from standard general formulas; an unindexed numerical table, an unpublished computation, or a specialized general theorem may give them implicitly. Full priority requires additional historical verification. The exact finite algorithm proves the asserted integration values because it is the direct implementation of the formal residue formula; the \(45\)-point localization is a separate check, not a substitute for the normal-sequence argument.
