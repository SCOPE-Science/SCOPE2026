For a general ten-hyperplane Calabi–Yau sixfold in \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,10)\), all eleven tangent-Chern/Plücker intersection numbers are determined exactly; in particular \(\int c_2^3=32480\), \(\int c_2c_4=26720\), and \(\int c_3^2=13200\). The Hodge numbers of this family were already known and are not claimed as new.
