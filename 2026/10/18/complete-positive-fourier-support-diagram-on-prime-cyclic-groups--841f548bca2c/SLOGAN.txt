For every odd prime cyclic group, a nonzero nonnegative signal has exactly the support-cardinality pairs \((k,\ell)\) satisfying \(k+\ell\ge p+1\), \(1\le k,\ell\le p\), and odd \(\ell\). Explicit products of nonnegative cosine factors realize every permissible pair: positivity adds only Fourier-parity, with no further cardinality gaps.
