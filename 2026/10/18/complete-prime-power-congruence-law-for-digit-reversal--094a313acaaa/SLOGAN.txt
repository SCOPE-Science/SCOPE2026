For every base \(b\ge2\), the fixed-prime-power divisibility probability of the gcd of an integer and its digit reversal has an explicit limit, with the only extra correlation for \(p\nmid b\) coming from \(\nu_p(b^2-1)\). The normalized joint residues become uniform on an exactly determined congruence subgroup, while at primes dividing the base the formula stabilizes after a finite digit length.
