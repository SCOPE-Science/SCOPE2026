Every complex \(3\times3\) Fourier minor at order \(3p\), for prime \(p\ge5\), has its rank determined by four elementary residue-collision conditions. The rank-one and rank-two minors have exact polynomial counts \(p^2(p-1)(p-2)\) and \(p^2(p-1)(9p^2-21p+18)\), distinguishing all independent row-column restrictions from the previously studied principal minors.
