Over \(\mathbf F_3\), exactly \(7{,}279{,}038\) projective pencils of plane cubics have all four rational members geometrically smooth. Their common rational base locus has at most four points, sharply, with the complete counts \(2{,}577{,}744,2{,}651{,}454,1{,}493{,}856,499{,}824,56{,}160\) for base sizes zero through four.
