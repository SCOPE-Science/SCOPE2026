Every real finite quadratic combinatorial Banach space has a completely explicit isometry group: coordinate permutations respecting the maximal-set system, composed with independent Euclidean rotations inside classes of coordinates having identical maximal-set incidence. Two such spaces are linearly isometric exactly when their hereditary set systems are permutation-isomorphic, and the dimension of the continuous symmetry group is \(\sum_B|B|(|B|-1)/2\).
