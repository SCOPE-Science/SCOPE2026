For every even base \(a\ge2\), the split-root Jacobsthal recurrence represents all residues precisely modulo \(1\), \(2\), every odd modulus supported on primes dividing \(a+1\), and the isolated modulus \(3\) when \(3\mid a\). On every supported odd modulus its even and odd subsequences are separately full permutations, with an exact local \(p\)-adic isometry.
