The full \(S_4\times S_3\)-action on the Nauru independence complex has \(\widetilde H_6(X;\mathbb Z)\cong\operatorname{sgn}_{S_4}\) and \(\widetilde H_5(X;\mathbb Q)\cong(\operatorname{sgn}_{S_4}\boxtimes\operatorname{sgn}_{S_3})\otimes\operatorname{Perm}_4\), revealing two distinct natural symmetry types beyond the known sphere-wedge homology. Neither positive-degree rational homology group has any full-automorphism invariant class.
