For every wheel \(W_n\) with \(n\ge5\), the complete rank polynomial of its self-inverse partial isometries is \(J_n(z)=(1+z)F_n(z)+n(z^2+2z^3+2z^4)\), where \(F_n\) is the explicit cyclic-path-partition sum given in RESULT.md. The exceptional hub-moving involutions contribute only in ranks two, three, and four.
