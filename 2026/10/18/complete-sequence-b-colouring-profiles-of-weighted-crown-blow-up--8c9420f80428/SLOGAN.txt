Every \(b\)-colouring of a weighted crown blow-up with at least three paired blocks is either the bipartition two-colouring or the paired-block \(r\)-colouring; every vertex is colour dominating. Hence its exact sequence-\(b\) spectrum is determined by two explicit sorted CDV-multiplicity profiles, and arbitrary nontrivial profiles with all entries at least two are realized while every intermediate number of colours is excluded.
