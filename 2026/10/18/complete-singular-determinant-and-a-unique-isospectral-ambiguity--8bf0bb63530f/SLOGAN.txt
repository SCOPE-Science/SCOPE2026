For the real reflected-integration pencil \(T_a=(I+aR)J\), a single entire determinant \(D_a(z)=(\cos z-a\cos(az))/(1-a)\) (with its continuous value at \(a=1\)) determines every nonzero singular value. Exactly the two different parameters \(a=0\) and \(a=-1\) are isospectral, even though the sharp singular-value coefficient is constant for all \(|a|\le1\).
