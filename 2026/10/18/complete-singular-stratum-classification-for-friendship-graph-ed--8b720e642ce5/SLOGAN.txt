For the normal-fan toric \(2m\)-fold of a friendship-graph edge polytope, \(m\ge3\), the reduced singular locus has exactly \(2^{m-3}(m^2+3m+8)\) irreducible components: two codimension-three conifold families and a codimension-\(m+1\) rank-one \(2\times m\) determinantal family. The latter has generic multiplicity \(m\) and is not \(\mathbb Q\)-Gorenstein, while every simplicial fan cone is smooth.
