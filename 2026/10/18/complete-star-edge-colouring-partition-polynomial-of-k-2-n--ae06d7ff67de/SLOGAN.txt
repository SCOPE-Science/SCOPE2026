Every star edge-colouring of \(K_{2,n}\) corresponds to an oriented matching of its \(n\) degree-two vertices, so the number of colour partitions with \(2n-k\) classes is exactly \(n!/(k!(n-2k)!)\). This gives the complete named-palette colouring polynomial, exact multiplicities of optimal colourings, and strict log-concavity of the nonzero partition-count coefficients.
