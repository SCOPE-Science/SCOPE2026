For the Alexander quandle \(\mathbb F_{p^f}^2\) with \((a,b)\triangleright(c,d)=(a+b-d,b)\), every nonempty subquandle is an independent family of nonempty additive-coset unions over a base set. Affine-span Möbius inversion gives an explicit polynomial counting **every embedded subquandle by size** for all primes and extension degrees.
