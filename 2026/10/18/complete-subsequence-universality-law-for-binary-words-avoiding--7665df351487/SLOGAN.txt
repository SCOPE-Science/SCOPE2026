Binary words avoiding both \(000\) and \(111\) have an exact rational joint length-and-subsequence-universality-index law \(\frac{1+2t+2t^2+ut^3}{1-u(2t^2+t^3)}\). Their universality index ranges sharply from \(\lfloor n/3\rfloor\) to \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\), and under uniform sampling it satisfies a Gaussian threshold with explicit mean \(n/\sqrt5\) and variance \((1-11\sqrt5/25)n\) to leading order.
