For the centered cubic cardinal B-spline, every Zak zero throughout the continuous scale interval \(1/2\le\lambda\le1\) lies at half-frequency. Its complete zero fiber is a horizontal interval for \(1/2\le\lambda<4/7\), collapsing sharply to the unique point \( (1/2,1/2) \) at \(\lambda=4/7\); an exact rational Bernstein certificate excludes all hidden zeros.
