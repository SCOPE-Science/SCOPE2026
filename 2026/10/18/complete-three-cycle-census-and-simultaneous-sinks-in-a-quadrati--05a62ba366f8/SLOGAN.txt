For a natural quadratic member of the three-dimensional nilpotent-Jacobian family (in polynomial conjugate coordinates), the exact number of primitive three-cycles jumps through \(1,2,3,5\) at sharp parameter thresholds \(27/32\) and \(125/96\). The five-cycle regime contains an explicit rational parameter with two distinct, simultaneously hyperbolic attracting three-cycles, certified by exact return-matrix inequalities.
