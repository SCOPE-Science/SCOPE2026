The complete histogram determines the arithmetic distribution of this invariant across *every* cyclic modulus and the multiplicative total replaces enumerating \(\binom N3^2\) candidate pairs; the dependence on each prime-power valuation explains differences invisible to an existence criterion. No downstream spectral-measure theorem is claimed.
