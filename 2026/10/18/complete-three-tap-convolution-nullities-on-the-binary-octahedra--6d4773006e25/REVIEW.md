# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof is a finite and exhaustive exact computation, not a sample of supports. The explicit quaternion formula generates 48 distinct elements, a verified associative multiplication table, 1081 identity-normalized triples, and 28 closed symmetry orbits. The constant-nullity orbit principle follows from left-support translation and inner-conjugation similarity. The 28 representative regular-convolution matrices are ranked over rational numbers; separately, exact finite-field ranks at two primes were checked for all 1081 normalized supports. The 19 signatures and the aggregate 17,296-support census follow by an exact 16-to-one counting conversion. These are actual reproducible checks in `check_binary_octahedral.py`, which completed successfully.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risks
Dechant's quaternion construction is a geometric foundation, while Arezoomand–Taeri's general Cayley Fourier factorization does not compute any of the nineteen 2O signature cases. A prior 2T three-tap classification implies the within-coset portion after doubling; that part is not claimed as independently novel. It does not imply the 13,248 mixed-coset cases, the 2/5/8 nullities, or their exact counts. Published results on generalized dihedral and ternary Heisenberg groups concern different group families. A less accessible historical character-table equivalent remains possible, so publication priority is not independently certified.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This complete classification resolves a natural noncommutative convolution singularity question on a canonical group. The signature-only rule is more concise than a raw support list, the mixed-coset five-dimensional defect is not an obvious transfer from the normal binary tetrahedral subgroup, and both the full count and extremizers are exact. The work does not claim a general theorem for all finite groups or an arbitrary-weight extension.

## Literature and limitations
The key primary sources are Dechant, arXiv:1205.1451 (2012), especially Lemma 7.3, and Arezoomand–Taeri, DOI:10.37236/3105 (2013), especially Corollary 7. These were inspected in their original PDFs at the relevant sections. The two related other-group result records were compared only at their published summaries; no full-document noncoverage is asserted. The proof is executable exact enumeration and remains subject to independent mathematical audit.
