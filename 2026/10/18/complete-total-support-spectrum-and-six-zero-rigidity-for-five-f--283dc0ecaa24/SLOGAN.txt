Every complete system of five mutually unbiased bases in \(\mathbb C^4\) has exactly the total-support spectrum \(\{14,17,18,19,20\}\): the levels \(15\) and \(16\) are impossible. The forty minimizing projective states are precisely the Pauli-stabilizer eigenrays of the ten commuting triples outside the defining five-base partition, and an exhaustive exact-minor classification identifies all \(600\) dependent measurement-ray quadruples.
