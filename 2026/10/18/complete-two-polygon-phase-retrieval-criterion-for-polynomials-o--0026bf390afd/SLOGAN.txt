For every positive degree \(d\), a complex polynomial is determined up to global phase by its intensities at two regular concentric \(2d\)-gons exactly when their radii differ and \(\sin(d(\psi-\phi))\ne0\). The sharp angular obstruction is the aliasing of the top Fourier harmonic; staggered polygons with \(d(\psi-\phi)\equiv\pi/2\pmod\pi\) optimally demix that harmonic.
