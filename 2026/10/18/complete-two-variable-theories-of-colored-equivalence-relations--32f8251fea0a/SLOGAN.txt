For two-variable first-order logic with one equivalence relation and \(r\) unary predicates, two structures agree on every sentence exactly when both the numbers of each unary color inside each equivalence class and the numbers of classes of each resulting type agree after truncation at two. This yields exactly \(3^{3^{2^r}-1}-1\) sentence theories and a sharp induced-substructure bound of \(2^{r+1}3^{2^r}\) elements.
