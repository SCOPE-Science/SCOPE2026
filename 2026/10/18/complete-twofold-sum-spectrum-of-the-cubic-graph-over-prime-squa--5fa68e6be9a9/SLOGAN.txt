The cubic graph over every prime-square ring \(\mathbb Z/p^2\mathbb Z\) with prime \(p\ge5\) has an exactly determined ordered two-sum spectrum: the only positive multiplicities are \(2,p,2p,p^2\), with explicit counts for all target points. Its additive energy is \(6p^4-8p^3+3p^2\), revealing the new singular-fiber strata created by lifting from a field to a nonreduced ring.
