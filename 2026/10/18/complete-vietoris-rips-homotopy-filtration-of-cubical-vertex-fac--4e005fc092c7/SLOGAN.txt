The graph-metric Vietoris–Rips filtration of every cubical vertex–facet incidence graph has an explicit sphere stage at scale two, \(S^{2^{d-1}-1}\), and another at scale three, \(S^{2^{d-1}+d-1}\), for all \(d\ge3\). A uniform simplicial collapse proves the first stage, while an exact join of antipodal-pair complexes gives the second; at \(d=3\), these are the three- and six-spheres of the rhombic-dodecahedral graph.
