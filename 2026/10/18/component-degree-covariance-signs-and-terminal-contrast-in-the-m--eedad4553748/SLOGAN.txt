In the stationary Moran forest, component count is positively correlated with the number of isolated vertices, but negatively correlated with every nonzero undirected degree class (except the three-vertex degree-one equality). In contrast, zero-outdegree terminal-vertex count has positive covariance with component count, and the two linear-order covariance constants are exactly \(4-11/e\) and \(1-5/(2e)\).
