A probe set resolves the two-subset Kneser graph \(\mathrm{KG}(n,2)\) exactly when its corresponding ground graph avoids four explicit small-component obstructions. Independent probes at rate \(p=(\log n+c)/n\) consequently succeed with limiting probability \(e^{-e^{-c}}(1+e^{-c})\): one isolated ground vertex is harmless, whereas two are fatal.
