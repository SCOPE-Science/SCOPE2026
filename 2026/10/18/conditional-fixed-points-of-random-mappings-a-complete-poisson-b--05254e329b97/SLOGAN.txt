Conditional on any complete indegree vector of an exchangeable random self-map, the fixed-point count has mean exactly \(1\), and its full factorial moments are elementary-symmetric functions of the degrees. Every asymptotic conditional law is exactly a Poisson residual plus independent Bernoulli contributions from macroscopic indegree hubs; the \(\operatorname{Poisson}(1)\) law occurs precisely when the largest indegree is \(o(n)\).
