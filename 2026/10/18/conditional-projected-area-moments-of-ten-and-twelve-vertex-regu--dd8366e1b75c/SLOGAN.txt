For a regular dodecahedron in a uniformly random orthographic view, the exact area-weighted first and second moments split between ten- and twelve-vertex silhouettes through exact golden-ratio and spherical-triangle formulas (with \(\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\)). The twelve-vertex shadows have **smaller mean area** than the ten-vertex shadows, with strict negative area–vertex-count covariance \(6((\varphi+2)^{-1}-15\arccos(1/\sqrt5)/\pi+5)\) after normalizing area by one facet's area.
