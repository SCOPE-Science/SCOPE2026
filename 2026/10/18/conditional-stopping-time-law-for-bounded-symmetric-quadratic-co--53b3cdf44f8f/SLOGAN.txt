For the symmetric quadratic Collatz map, the bounded positive seeds up to \(2^L\) have exactly \(L^2+2L-12\) elements once \(L\ge7\), and their total zero-hitting time is an explicit quartic polynomial in \(L\). Conditional stopping time divided by \(L^2\) has a square-root limiting distribution, with the provably sharp Kolmogorov convergence scale \(L^{-1/2}\).
