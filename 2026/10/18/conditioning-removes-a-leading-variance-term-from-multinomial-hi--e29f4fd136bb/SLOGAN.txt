For a uniform multinomial histogram with \(N/n\to\lambda>0\), the adjacent-bin roughness has an explicit positive variance per bin whose Poissonized value must be reduced by a nonvanishing global fixed-sample correction. This variance is \(4\lambda^2+O(\lambda^3)\) in the sparse-load limit and asymptotically \(1.052263614148\ldots\,\lambda\) at high load.
