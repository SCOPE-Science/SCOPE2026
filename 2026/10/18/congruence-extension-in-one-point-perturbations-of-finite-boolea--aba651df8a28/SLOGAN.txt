For every finite Boolean algebra, changing the identity operation at just one subset \(S\) by adjoining a disjoint nonempty set produces a frame with congruence extension exactly when \(S\) is empty or a coatom. On an \(n\)-atom algebra this gives exactly \(2^n+n-1\) CEP operations among \(3^n-2^n\) one-point perturbations, and each operation's complete congruence lattice is explicit.
