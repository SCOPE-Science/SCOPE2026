For a finite temporal Heyting algebra built from a graph's incidence order and arbitrarily marked reflexive points, its entire congruence lattice is the downset lattice of the incidence poset obtained by contracting the nonreflexive edges and retaining the reflexive ones. The exact count is \(\sum_{W\subseteq V(Q)}2^{|E(Q[W])|}\), with loops and parallel edges retained, and vertex reflexivity does not affect the answer.
