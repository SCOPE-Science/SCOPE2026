For weak Heyting–Brouwer expansions of any named finite product of at least two chains, a weighted Ferrers-endpoint graph calculates the entire distribution of Boolean congruence-lattice ranks and exact simple-expansion counts. Uniformly random such expansions become simple with probability at least \(1-2k^2e^{-m/4}\), where \(k\) is the number of factors and \(m\) the smallest dual-chain length.
