For every finite associative quasitrivial semigroup, two-sided congruences are described by consecutive merged intervals of its weak-order levels and arbitrary partitions of unmerged levels. This yields an explicit bivariate exponential generating function counting labeled semigroups by their numbers of quotient classes, and a sharp mean-congruence asymptotic of \(0.627900141295\,(1.766516095507)^n\) as \(n\to\infty\).
