On every finite bounded chain, a weak Heyting–Brouwer algebra is uniquely reconstructed from its monotone top-preserving unary necessity operator and has a Boolean congruence lattice. Among the \(\binom{2n}{n}\) expansions of a \((n+1)\)-element chain, exactly \(\binom{2n}{n}-2^{n-2}\) are simple when \(n\ge2\), and the other congruence ranks have an explicit power-of-two distribution.
