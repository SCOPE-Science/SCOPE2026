Every congruence of a two-point-fiber McCarthy algebra \(I[\mathbf2]\) is a semilattice congruence of \(I\) plus the option to collapse just the greatest quotient fiber. For finite \(I\), this yields \(\operatorname{Con}(I[\mathbf2])\cong\operatorname{Con}(I)\times\mathbf2\), and an \(n\)-element chain produces exactly \(2^n\) congruences.
