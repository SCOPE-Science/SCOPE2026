For every even cycle \(C_{2m}\) with \(m\ge4\), the normal-fan toric variety of its stable-set polytope has exactly \(2m\) codimension-three conifold components and one additional Segre-type singular line. The conifold components intersect according to the independence complex of the cycle; the line meets the two parity families at opposite endpoints, and precisely one torus-fixed point is smooth.
