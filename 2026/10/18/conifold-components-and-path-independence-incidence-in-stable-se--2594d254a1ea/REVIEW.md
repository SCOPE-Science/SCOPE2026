# Review of the path stable-set toric singularities

## Correctness

PASS. The signed primitive facet normals form a directed-graph incidence matrix, so every independent active collection is unimodular. Every feasible circuit in the augmented-path graph forces a local \(0,1,0\) pattern, and each such pattern gives exactly one rank-three nonsimplicial cone. This proves exhaustion of the minimal singular cones and hence of the irreducible singular components, in every characteristic. The component products and intersection nerve follow directly from fixing the relevant stable-set coordinates. The dual affine semigroup has exactly four Hilbert generators with one determinant relation, proving generic transverse conifold type. The included standard-library checker exhaustively corroborates all face and circuit data for \(3\le n\le7\).

## Originality

PASS, qualified by the usual risk of unindexed or differently worded publications. Hibi--Li--Li--Mu--Tsuchiya (2015) describe order/chain polytopes and their combinatorial properties; Matsuda--Ohsugi--Shibata (2016) treat stable-set toric rings, normality, and ideal generators. Neither inspected full text classifies the singular irreducible components or nerve of the **normal-fan** varieties of paths. Lakshmibai--Mukherjee (2007) treat the different affine Hibi-cone setting. No equivalent normal-fan theorem was identified in the searched mathematical research index or the accessible comparison literature; lack of an index hit is not a uniqueness proof.

## Mathematical value

PASS. This gives a complete characteristic-independent singular-support classification of a canonical infinite family whose polytopes are simultaneously stable-set polytopes and chain polytopes of fence posets. The exact conifold codimension, product decomposition of components, and independence-complex incidence arrangement supply geometric structure not entailed merely by normality or Gröbner-basis results. Finite tests support reproducibility, not significance or infinity by themselves.

## Scope and limits

The result does not specify the singular subscheme, local equations at arbitrary component intersections, or other graph families. Independent bibliographic and proof review remain desirable.
