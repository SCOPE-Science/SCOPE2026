# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The exact lattice M of integral n×n zero-row/column-sum matrices has rank (n−1)^2. For a face support E with c balanced connected components, dim(F)=|E|−2n+c. The missing-coordinate restriction ρ:M→Z^T fits a saturated exact sequence 0→kerρ→M→Z^T→Z^(c−1)→0: the only necessary balance constraints are component net flows, and a spanning tree fills integer row/column demands within each connected component. Thus c=1 gives a free affine monoid and a smooth orbit; c>1 gives ray excess c−1 and local class group Z^(c−1). Inclusion-maximal disconnected support graphs are precisely two balanced complete bipartite blocks, giving (C(2n,n)−2)/2 components. For block sizes a,n−a the transverse semigroup has equal nonnegative masses on two q=a(n−a) rectangles; pairing generators identifies the rank-one q×q determinantal cone, its class group Z and multiplicity C(2q−2,q−1). Boundary n=4 has 34 components (16 four-dimensional, 18 two-dimensional); independently enumerated every face n=3,4, confirming 49 and 7443 faces, and the 34 n=4 maximal disconnected faces. Exact integer-flow argument, not finite census, handles all n≥4.
