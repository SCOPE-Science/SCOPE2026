For every \(n\ge4\), a torus orbit of the Birkhoff-polytope toric variety is singular exactly when its perfect-matching support graph is disconnected. The reduced singular locus has precisely \(\frac12(\binom{2n}{n}-2)\) block-partition components; each has a generic transverse cone of rank-one \(a(n-a)\times a(n-a)\) matrices, and arbitrary orbitwise local divisor-class groups are free of rank one less than the number of support-graph components.
