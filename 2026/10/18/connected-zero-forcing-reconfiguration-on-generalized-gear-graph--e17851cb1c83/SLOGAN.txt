For the generalized gear graph \(\operatorname{Gr}(m,r)\) with \(m\ge4\) and \(r\ge1\), all minimum ordinary zero-forcing sets admit a complete cyclic description, and their one-token-exchange graph is connected with exactly \(m(3r+2)\) vertices and \(m(6r+5)\) edges. Its hub-containing configurations form an induced cycle reached in one exchange from every other optimum, and the full degree distribution is explicit.
