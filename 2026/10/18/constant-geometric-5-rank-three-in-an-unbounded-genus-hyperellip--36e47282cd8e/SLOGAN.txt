The good characteristic-five reductions \(y^2=x^{2\cdot3^r+1}+x\) of a natural prime-power subfamily of Pan's hyperelliptic curves have geometric genus \(3^r\) but Jacobian \(5\)-rank exactly \(3\) for every \(r\ge1\). A full-orbit criterion for their sparse Cartier operators proves the constant rank in unbounded genus.
