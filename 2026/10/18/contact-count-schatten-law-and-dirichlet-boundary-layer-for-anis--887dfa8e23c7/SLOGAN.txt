An anisotropic diagonal trace from polydisc Hardy space to the natural weighted Bergman line has an exact singular spectrum; the number of coordinates still touching the boundary alone determines its sharp Schatten threshold. In the critical radius window \(r_j=\exp(-t_j/(2N))\), the degree-\(N\) squared singular value follows a Dirichlet-simplex Laplace profile with a nonnegative, explicitly bounded \(1/N\) correction that vanishes exactly for isotropic radii.
