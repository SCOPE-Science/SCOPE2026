# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The exact two-draw covariance identity is derived by conditioning on the remaining observations. Binomial monotone coupling and a pairwise Chebyshev covariance representation prove strict positivity for every nonaffine convex score. The frequency-sign formula follows by binomial probabilities and an explicit quadratic. Direct exhaustive rational occupancy enumeration checks 520 finite cases, including 180 strict convex hinge cases; this is supplementary, not an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS, subject to priority uncertainty.** Eaton–Godbole–Sinclair (arXiv:0806.1007, Theorems 1–3) analyze the same pair-collision and strict-tie frequency counts but establish Poisson approximations, not the convex association or all-size quadratic sign classification. Hitczenko–Wormald (arXiv:2312.04246, Sections 1, 4) prove high-moment normal limits in a bounded-capacity occupancy setting. Zhu (arXiv:2103.07518, Sections 1–2) studies feasible laws of the observed distinct-count statistic. Exact moments of such counts are classical; equivalent implicit inequalities in older occupancy literature are the main remaining risk. No uniqueness is inferred merely from unsuccessful searches.

**Value — PASS.** Pair collisions are the sample Simpson-concentration numerator; additive convex occupancy scores quantify concentration and tail weight in a frequency spectrum. The nonuniform universal sign result links entire families of such statistics, and the exact two-threshold frequency law isolates specific negative- versus positive-dependence regions and an exact zero at the singleton count. These are structural descriptions of a standard model, not an arbitrary finite slice.

**Limitations.** The result concerns iid finite categorical draws only. The sign law for frequency counts is uniform-only. Independent historical-priority and mathematical audits have not been performed.
