In a multi-type SIRS epidemic with all-at-once immunity loss, any mean-preserving increase in the convex-order dispersion of immune durations \(1/\omega_j\) cannot lower endemic infection. This proves strict monotonicity with waning heterogeneity for *every* two-type population split \(0<p<1\), including the previously unproved minority-weak-immunity regime, and gives an exact quadratic small-heterogeneity penalty.
