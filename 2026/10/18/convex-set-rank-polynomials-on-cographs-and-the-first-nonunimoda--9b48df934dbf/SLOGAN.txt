For induced-three-vertex-path convexity, the full convex-set size polynomial of every cograph satisfies an exact two-polynomial disjoint-union/complete-join recursion computable in quadratic arithmetic time. Among the corresponding convex geometries on trivially perfect graphs, the five-vertex star is the unique first nonunimodal example, and a star with \(d\) leaves has unimodal coefficients exactly when \(d\le3\).
