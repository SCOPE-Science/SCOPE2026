For every prime \(p\ge3\), the restricted \(p\)-nullcone of strictly upper-triangular \((p+2)\)-square matrices has an explicit reduced decomposition into coordinate flats and determinantal quadrics, all of codimension two. Its exact number of points over \(\mathbb F_{p^f}\) is \(q^{\binom{p+2}{2}-p-2}(q^{p+2}-(q-1)^p(q^2+pq+1))\), where \(q=p^f\).
