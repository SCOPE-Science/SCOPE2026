An explicit lacunary-block combinatorial Banach space has coordinatewise norm rigidity and therefore compactness of every bounded equinormed set, although its generating family fails every positive directedness class with \(\lambda<1/2\). Its norm simultaneously contains \(1\)-complemented isometric copies of \(\ell_\infty^N\) for every \(N\), showing that this compactness mechanism is neither a uniform-amalgamation consequence nor a disguised \(\ell_1\) geometry.
