# Scientific assessment of the mixed monomial composition commutator

## Correctness

**PASS.** Beta integration gives the exact monomial norm and the bounded composition weights. Coprime divisibility makes the difference of adjoint products a sparse orthogonal transfer; a first-order gamma expansion establishes the nonzero singular-value coefficient. Noncoprime degrees leave an infinite orthonormal family with image norm bounded below. Rearrangement and an absolutely summable error yield the Schatten and logarithmic-trace conclusions. The classical Dirichlet parameter cancels exactly. A finite numerical checker passed; the proof does not rely upon finite tests.

## Originality

**PASS, subject to stated bibliographic risk.** The 2026 linear-fractional composition product paper does not cover the nonlinear two-monomial commutator. The 2011 monomial Dirichlet paper and 2018 general weighted-Hardy monomial paper focus, according to accessible abstracts, on self-commutators; neither accessible statement provides the mixed-degree gcd classification or the sharp weak-trace residue. Related results concern individual composition spectra, affine commutators, or unrelated operator ideals. Full text of two especially plausible monomial sources was inaccessible, so hidden equivalent coverage remains a real priority risk and independent historical certification is not asserted.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** Coprimality changes an explicitly computable noncompact operator difference into a compact one without compactness of either product. The nonclassical weights then exhibit a natural sharp weak-trace-class obstruction: compactness holds, trace class fails, and the logarithmic residue is a nonzero closed-form invariant. The exact vanishing at the classical Dirichlet parameter identifies a structural exceptional point. These facts address a motivated family and a complete arithmetic classification rather than a discretionary finite test.

## Limitations

The theorem treats one normalized Dirichlet weight family and monomial degrees at least two. It makes no statement about arbitrary composition symbols. Two plausible earlier monomial papers were available only in abstract form and should be checked in full before a publication-priority assertion. The numerical checker is corroborative only.
