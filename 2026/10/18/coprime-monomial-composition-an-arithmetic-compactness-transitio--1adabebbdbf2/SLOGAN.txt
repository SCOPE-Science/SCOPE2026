For monomial composition operators on weighted Dirichlet spaces, the mixed adjoint commutator is compact exactly when the two degrees are coprime. Apart from the classical Dirichlet cancellation at \(\alpha=0\), every coprime pair has sharp singular-value decay \(s_n\sim\Lambda_{\alpha;m,k}/n\), identifying a nonzero logarithmic trace residue and the exact Schatten threshold \(p>1\).
