For automorphisms of \(\mathbb F_q\langle x_1,\ldots,x_d\rangle/(x_1,\ldots,x_d)^3\), every \(\ell\)-torsion conjugacy class with \(\ell\mid(q-1)\) is determined by the multiplicities of its linear eigenvalues. Its exact cardinality is a general-linear orbit factor times \(q^{d^3-H}\), where \(H\) counts eigenvalue resonances in quadratic words; these formulas determine all split coprime torsion and exact-order strata.
