# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The linear-noise history is exactly uniform on the image of its over-\(\mathbb F_2\) Krylov matrix. The single-site Krylov space is generated by reflection pairs without the even-ring antipode; taking orthogonal complements converts the two-input rank into an exact dihedral-orbit count modulo \(\gcd(n,2d)\). When the sites are coprime, intersection geometry proves the sharp first uniformization time. The independent executable checks verify 107,493 finite rank/time instances, 40 complete finite noise-history laws, and 65 dual-orbit counts. Infinite quantifiers rest on the proof, not these checks.

## Originality — PASS with residual risk

The inspected deterministic Rule 90 studies of Martin–Odlyzko–Wolfram (1984) and Lettieri–Stevens–Thomas (2006) address transition diagrams, minimal polynomials and periods, not fair additive noise at a spatially specified pair. Sundaram–Hadjicostis (2012/2013) establishes general finite-field control framework but its main generic result requires free self-loop entries and sufficiently large fields; it does not decide this fixed zero-diagonal GF(2) ring. Mendonça's 2015 full-text preprint studies **diluted** XOR dynamics in which the output is replaced by zero at random, rather than two fair XOR flips. Searches of prior published mathematical records for two-site noise, binary cyclic controllability, aliases such as Kalman reachability and exact finite-time uniformity did not reveal an equivalent claim. Nonetheless a differently titled binary cyclic-control theorem could imply the result; database absence alone establishes nothing.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result settles a natural finite-volume question about whether sparse external randomness can erase all initial conditions in a local stochastic lattice dynamics. Exactly two noisy sites suffice, but their placement must be arithmetically primitive; the threshold time uses the information-theoretic minimum number of noise bits. The full codimension formula quantifies the noncoprime obstruction. This is stronger than a small numerical example or a generic rank-algorithm exercise.

## Limits

The rule is additive parity evolution with unbiased, independent, fixed-site flips, not the 2015 diluted-XOR PCA and not a generic noisy spin chain. Stationary-law uniqueness outside the coprime case, biased noise, and asymptotic thermodynamic behavior are not classified. Historical bibliographic completeness and independent validation are outstanding.
