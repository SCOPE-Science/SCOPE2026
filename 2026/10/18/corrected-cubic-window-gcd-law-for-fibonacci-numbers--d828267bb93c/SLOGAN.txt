For every even window length, the gcd of all sums of consecutive Fibonacci cubes is the first-power window gcd, multiplied by three exactly when the length is divisible by eight and divided by two exactly when it is divisible by six. This proves a complete corrected version of the even-length Fibonacci-cube conjecture in Mbirika–Spilker's 2022 Question 6.1, with the printed two-adic cases reversed.
