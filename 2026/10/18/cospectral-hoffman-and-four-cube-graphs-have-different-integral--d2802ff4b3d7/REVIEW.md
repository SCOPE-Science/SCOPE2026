## Mathematical value — PASS

The Hoffman graph is the canonical connected 4-regular bipartite cospectral mate of the four-cube, so determining whether their independence-complex topology agrees is a natural spectral-versus-topological rigidity test, not an arbitrary graph enumeration. The resulting homotopy distinction is complete and integral: three versus seven \(3\)-spheres. The independence polynomials agree in every coefficient except that of \(z^5\), where they differ by four, isolating the combinatorial discrepancy that survives as a four-dimensional jump in third Betti rank. The exact finite result is independently meaningful even without a theorem for all cospectral pairs. The contribution is not solely a recomputed Betti number or a small numerical improvement; the full homotopy types are certified.
