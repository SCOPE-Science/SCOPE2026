Every finite or countable transitive Kripke frame is represented, for all modal formulas with fibre-saturated valuations, by \(\omega\)-accumulation on an explicit countably infinite \(T_0\) Alexandrov partial order. The lift is \(\omega\)-scattered exactly for conversely well-founded relations, yielding \(\mathsf{K4}\) and \(\mathsf{GL}\) completeness on these sharply restricted topological classes.
