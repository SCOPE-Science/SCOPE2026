For the real Lipschitz dual ball of \(m\) equal two-point clusters separated by distance one, the uniform covariance has exactly two explicit eigenvalues, and for every within-pair distance \(0<t<1\) its smallest eigenspace recovers the entire hidden pairing. The exact spectral gap quantifies a sharp near-equilateral transition: if \(t=1-c/m\), then \(m^2\) times the gap tends to \(c(c+1)/2\).
