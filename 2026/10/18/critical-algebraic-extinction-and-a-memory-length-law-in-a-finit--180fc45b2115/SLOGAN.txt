At the neutral zero-state threshold of the finite-memory Grünwald--Letnikov logistic map, every sufficiently small positive history relaxes as \(x_n\sim \tau_M(q)/[(1+T_M(q))n]\) with an explicit history-independent coefficient. For each fixed fractional order \(0<q<1\), that coefficient grows like \(qM^{1-q}/\Gamma(2-q)\) with retained memory length \(M\).
