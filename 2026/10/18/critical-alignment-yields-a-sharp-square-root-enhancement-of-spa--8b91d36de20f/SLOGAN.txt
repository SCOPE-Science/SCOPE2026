At the Curie--Weiss alignment threshold, a nonzero spatial Fourier mode of the run-and-tumble hydrodynamic density generically relaxes as \(C_nt^{-3/8}\exp[-t+\sqrt3\,k_n\sqrt t/\sqrt{k_n^2-1}]\), even though its global magnetization relaxes only as \(t^{-1/2}\). Exactly zero initial magnetization removes the square-root exponential enhancement.
