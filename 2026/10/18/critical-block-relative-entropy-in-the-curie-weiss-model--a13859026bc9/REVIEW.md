# Scientific assessment: critical-block relative entropy

## Correctness — PASS
The theorem specifies the zero-field, inverse-temperature-one finite Curie–Weiss Gibbs normalization, integer block sizes \(k_N/\sqrt N\to\alpha>0\), and natural-log relative entropy. Hubbard–Stratonovich conditioning gives an exact spin product-mixture identity. Quartic Laplace concentration gives convergence of the rescaled mixing field and uniform exponential square moments. The exact block likelihood ratios converge locally uniformly, while their reference-law second moments remain bounded; this establishes the uniform integrability needed to pass from weak likelihood-ratio convergence to relative-entropy convergence. The Gaussian-channel entropy identity and the established information–MMSE derivative give strict monotonicity, while explicit moment and entropy expansions give the two endpoints. The program passed 87 checks of exact finite-volume calculations and quadrature, not an infinite-size certification. The limitations in `RESULT.md` apply.

## Originality — PASS with residual priority risk
Gantert, Löwe and Schnappinger (2026, arXiv:2609.02464) supply a critical-window *total-variation* profile, not the stated limiting Kullback–Leibler information formula or its strict-monotonicity and asymptotic coefficient laws. Ben Arous and Zeitouni (1999) establish relative-entropy convergence to zero only for \(k_N=o(\sqrt N)\), while crediting Papangelou (1989) with weak non-Gaussian/Gaussian block-fluctuation limits. The latter are compatible with the present Gaussian-convolution representation, but weak convergence alone is insufficient for relative-entropy convergence. Jalowy et al. (2023) focus on total variation, and Galicza–Pete (2025) give entropy upper bounds for the full critical system. The established Gaussian information–MMSE identity is properly credited. No covering statement was identified in the actual searches and inspected accessible portions; this is not a proof of priority. Papangelou's plausible 1989 primary full text was inaccessible, and a stronger old information-convergence theorem could subsume the claimed addition. Independent priority verification remains necessary.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The model has a rigorously located physical-statistical threshold at \(k_N\asymp\sqrt N\), while relative entropy answers a different and stronger information-theoretic question than total variation. The exact positive critical profile quantifies how dependence accumulates across a growing spin block, and the derivative together with the quadratic-small/linear-large parameter transitions gives a usable information curve rather than a Boolean breakdown indicator. The \(L^2\) likelihood-ratio bound is the essential structural step absent from a weak-limit-only argument. No extension to general couplings, positive fields or joint regimes is claimed.
