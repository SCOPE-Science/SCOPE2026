For the positive gamma Helson matrix \(H_\beta(m,n)=1/[\sqrt{mn}(1+\log(mn))^\beta]\), the cubic trace has the exact critical law \(\operatorname{Tr}(H_\beta^3)\sim\pi^2/[12(\beta-1)]\) as \(\beta\downarrow1\). The coefficient is a genuine three-variable simplex integral, unlike the first and second trace residues, both equal to \(1/2\).
