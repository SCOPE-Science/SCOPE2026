For symmetric dyadic holes around the discontinuity of negative doubling, the surviving binary language is exactly the avoidance class of two complementary nearly alternating words; this yields a closed rational generating function, a sharp entropy onset at dyadic depth four, and exact finite-time escape probabilities. As the hole shrinks, its escape rate has an explicit logarithmically enhanced second-order correction, whose occurrence in related maps is known but whose exact coefficient here follows from the specialized renewal law.
