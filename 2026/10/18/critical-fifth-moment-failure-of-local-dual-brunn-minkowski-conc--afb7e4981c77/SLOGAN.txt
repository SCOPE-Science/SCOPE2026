For symmetric smooth planar convex bodies, the radial \(q\)-moment norm is Minkowski-concave on some \(C^2\) neighborhood of the disk exactly when \(0<q<5\). At the neutral endpoint \(q=5\), a fourth-harmonic deformation makes the Hessian in a second-harmonic direction positive: \(\mathcal F_5''(1+b\cos4\phi)[\cos2\phi,\cos2\phi]=-6b+O(b^2)>0\) for small negative \(b\).
