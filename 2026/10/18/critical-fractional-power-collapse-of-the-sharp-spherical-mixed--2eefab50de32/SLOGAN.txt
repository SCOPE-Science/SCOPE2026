For spherical Fourier extension in every fixed dimension \(d\ge3\), the *sharp* radial--angular global stability coefficient vanishes as an explicit positive constant times \((q-2d/(d-1))^{1/d}\) at the strong-type threshold. The coefficient is governed by a strictly positive, conditionally convergent adjacent-order Bessel cancellation integral, while the stability coefficient relative to the squared sharp norm decays linearly.
