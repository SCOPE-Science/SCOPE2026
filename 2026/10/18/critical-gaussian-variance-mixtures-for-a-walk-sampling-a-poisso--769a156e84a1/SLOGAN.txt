Sampling a stationary Poisson field of symmetric moving particles along an almost unbiased random walk produces a non-Gaussian Brownian-path variance mixture on the \(n^{3/4}\) scale, with an explicit crossover when observer drift is \(\kappa/\sqrt n\). The entire fixed first-Poisson–Charlier-rank polynomial class shares this mixture up to one explicit coefficient, while the variance profile has a closed Gaussian integral.
