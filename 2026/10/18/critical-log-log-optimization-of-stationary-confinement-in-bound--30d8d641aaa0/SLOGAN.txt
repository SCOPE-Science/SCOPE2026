In a stationary particle that tumbles only at the walls, normalized spatial variance can be reduced below the uniform level by a suitable interval length exactly when its activity-to-thermal-diffusion parameter obeys \(\beta<1\). Near that threshold, every optimal Péclet number diverges as \(H+\log H+(\log H+7)/H\) while the greatest confinement gain shrinks as \((1-\beta)/(6H)\), where \(H=\log(1/(1-\beta))\).
