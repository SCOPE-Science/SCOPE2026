For every fixed codimension at least two, the expected Euclidean intrinsic volume of a uniformly random labelled-tree graphical zonotope grows as \(n^{r-1/2}(\log n)^{r-1}/((r-1)!(2\pi)^{(r-1)/2})\), where \(r\) is the number of components after deleting \(r-1\) tree edges. Under the associated intrinsic-volume-weighted forest law, one component is giant and the logarithmic sizes of the remaining components converge to independent uniforms.
