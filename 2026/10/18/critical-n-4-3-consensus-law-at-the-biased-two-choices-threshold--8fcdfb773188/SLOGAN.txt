At the exact \(1/9\) bias threshold of asynchronous two-choices opinion dynamics, complete-graph consensus has a critical \(n^{4/3}\) expected-time bottleneck with an explicit universal gamma-function coefficient and an \(n^{2/3}\) initial-opinion crossover profile. Fixed initial fractions above \(1/4\) instead have the sharp \(n\log n\) leading law.
