# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The full frozen RESULT.md constructs the derivative of log rho_(K+ty) via differentiability of the Minkowski gauge at almost every radial boundary point. Inradius inclusions make translated log-radii uniformly Lipschitz; dominated convergence and a finite-net argument yield local-uniform convergence of p(vol(K)-g_(K,p)(y/p)). The Lipschitz radial boundary area formula rho^d du=h dH^(d-1) and divergence theorem cancel the linear term of log((1+e^s)/2), leaving the exact h log cosh integral. Its Hessian is positive definite and the projection-body lower bound gives coercivity; the ordinary covariogram chord formula bounds scaled sublevel sets so local convergence yields Hausdorff convergence. For a fixed facet polytope, the maximizing facet changes only inside finite spherical bands of measure O(1/p), giving locally uniform error O(1/p) and a radial sublevel Hausdorff O(1/p) rate. Requires d>=2, compact full-dimensional K and 0 in its interior; no global quantitative convergence rate claimed for arbitrary nonsmooth K. Independent trapezoid-midpoint radial integration for a rectangle yielded limit 0.357211880987 and errors at p=40,80,160,320 of about 0.01658,0.008296,0.004143,0.002044, confirming O(1/p) only in one planar case.

## originality

PASS

Smooth critical functional and log-cosh transition of Haddad–Jiménez–Resende are acknowledged as known. The novel assertion is its proof for every nonsmooth full-dimensional convex K and O(1/p) polytope rates. Direct comparison of smoothness hypotheses C^3 versus arbitrary K and of exact error conclusions, plus inspection of weighted-intersection and single-body radial-mean definitions, found no dominating implication. Best-of-knowledge rather than absolute bibliographic certification.
