# Mathematical review: binomial Bergman Volterra commutator

**Correctness — PASS.** The normalized Bergman monomial matrix is derived by exact integration. Direct multiplication gives one diagonal and one \(n-m\)-spaced off-diagonal. Rational asymptotic expansions yield the exact fourth-order critical terms, with strictly positive coefficient \(4n^2(n-m)^2|b|^2\) at the lower ratio and strictly negative coefficient \(-2n^3(n-m)^2|b|^2/m\) at the upper. Gauge-invariant tail quadratic forms and separate remote test blocks prove both sides of the infinite-inertia transition; min–max estimates prove the two-sided cubic singular-value law. Hilbert–Schmidt regularity of the original integration operator gives trace class and zero trace for its commutator. The packaged `verify.py` passes 5,485 finite independent matrix, exact-rational, expansion and boundary-witness checks. Finite checks do not replace the proof of infinite assertions.

They do not state the signed positive/negative eigenvalue counts for its self-commutator. The closest semantic research entries concern self-commutators of different Cesàro or integration operators, not this unweighted Bergman binomial family. A Bergman Toeplitz hyponormality result is not equivalent to a Volterra integration commutator.
