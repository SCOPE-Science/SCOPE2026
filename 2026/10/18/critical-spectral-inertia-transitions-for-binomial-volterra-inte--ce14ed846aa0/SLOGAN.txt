For a two-monomial analytic Volterra integration operator on the standard Bergman disk, the self-commutator has singular values of exact order \(j^{-3}\). Its positive spectral inertia is infinite precisely for coefficient ratios \(n/m\le |a|/|b|<(n/m)^2\), with opposite inclusion rules at the two critical endpoints; its negative spectral inertia is always infinite.
