At the critical deterministic drift scale \(\mu_T^2/T\to c^2\), Brownian motion conditioned on \(\int_0^T W_s^2ds\le\theta T\) converges on every fixed window in total variation to an Ornstein--Uhlenbeck process with rate \(\gamma\), the unique positive solution of \(2\theta\gamma^3-\gamma^2=c^2\). Thus a drift of order \(\sqrt T\) changes the limiting confinement rate, resolving the critical-scale question left open in the recent drifted-Brownian study.
