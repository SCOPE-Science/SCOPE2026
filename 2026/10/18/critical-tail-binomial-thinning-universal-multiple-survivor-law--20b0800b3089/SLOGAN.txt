At the critical \(1/k\) population-tail boundary, rare Bernoulli thinning yields a universal \((r-1)/[j(j-1)]\) survivor count conditional on at least \(r\ge2\) observations, despite an extra logarithm in the one-hit probability. For the canonical integer Pareto law, the complete finite-thinning total-variation error has an exact closed form and sharp rate \(\varepsilon\log(1/\varepsilon)/2\), with a single likelihood-ratio sign crossing.
