For backward shifts with weights \(j^\alpha L(j)\), \(0<\alpha<1/p\), every positive slowly varying factor has the same sharp strong Cesàro cutoff \(q_0=1/\alpha\), but the critical weak-\(L^{q_0}\) endpoint holds exactly when \(L\) is almost decreasing. In particular, logarithmic factors with positive powers destroy the weak endpoint without changing any subcritical strong exponent.
