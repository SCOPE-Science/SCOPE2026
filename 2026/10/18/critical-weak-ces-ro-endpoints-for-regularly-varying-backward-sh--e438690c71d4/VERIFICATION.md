1. Exact telescoping: \(\|W^kx\|_p^p=\sum_{j>k}|x_j|^p(a_j/a_{j-k})^p\).
2. For each \(\beta\in(0,1)\), cardinality-\(m\) subsets of an \(N\)-step orbit satisfy the column bound \(N^\beta m^{1-\beta}/(1-\beta)\), by direct ordering of kernel entries.
3. If \(C_L<\infty\), these two facts yield the uniform weak critical endpoint. Conversely, a block of \(s+1\) times in the basis orbit of \(e_j\) bounds \(L(j)/L(s)\), using the finite local-doubling constant.
4. Potter bounds, finite-column summation and Jensen give all strong exponents \(q<q_0\); arbitrary fixed multiplicative terminal blocks prove divergence at \(q=q_0\), and a last-coordinate term proves failure above it.
