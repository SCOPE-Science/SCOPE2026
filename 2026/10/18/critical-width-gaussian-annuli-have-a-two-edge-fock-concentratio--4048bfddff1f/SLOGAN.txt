For Gaussian Fock localization on an annulus whose radius diverges but whose physical width stays fixed, the entire critical ordered spectrum converges to a **difference of two Gaussian CDFs**, rather than to the separated-edge error-function plunge. The sharp concentration of \(\lfloor c\sqrt R\rfloor\) orthonormal states is \(\sqrt R\,Q(c,w)+O(1)\), with an explicit strict gap below both the rank and area bounds; optimal finite-rank subspaces can be chosen from consecutive monomials.
