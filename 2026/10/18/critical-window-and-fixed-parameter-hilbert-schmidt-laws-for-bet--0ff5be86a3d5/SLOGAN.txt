Finite beta-Hausdorff matrices have a sharp three-regime Hilbert–Schmidt energy transition at the smaller beta shape \(1/2\): power, squared logarithm, or logarithm. In the two-shape critical window \(a=1/2+u/\log N\), \(b=1/2+v/\log N\), the leading squared-logarithmic energy has an explicit uniform crossover profile with an \(O_R(\log N)\) remainder.
