For the power-tail alpha-Farey map, the escape rate through the Markov hole \([0,n^{-\theta}]\) crosses the finite/infinite invariant-measure boundary on the sharp scale \((\theta-1)\log n\): its normalized limit is \(\Phi(c)=c/(e^c-1)\), uniformly even for diverging moving detuning. In a bounded critical window an explicit Euler-constant correction quantifies the finite-cutoff bias.
