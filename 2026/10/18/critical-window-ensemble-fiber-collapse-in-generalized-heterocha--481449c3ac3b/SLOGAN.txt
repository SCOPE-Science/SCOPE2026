For generalized heterochaos baker maps, the expected width of a complete vertical fiber has an explicit critical-window crossover: if the narrow-branch probability is \(1/2+\eta/\sqrt n\), then \(\sqrt n\) times its mean width converges to \([2\varphi(2\eta)-4\eta\Phi(-2\eta)]/(1-1/M)\). At exactly neutral central drift, mean width decays as \(n^{-1/2}\) although almost every fiber repeatedly regains full width.
