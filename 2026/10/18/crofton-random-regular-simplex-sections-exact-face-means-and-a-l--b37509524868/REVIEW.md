# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
Under invariant affine-hyperplane measure, conditioning on intersection **width-weights** directions; the proof normalizes by integrated simplex width. A section face corresponds bijectively to a cut original simplex face, yielding exact (1). Gaussian radial independence gives expected-range ratios and expected adjacent-spacing rank probabilities. A telescoping extreme-order-statistic identity and explicit Gaussian tail inequalities prove the nondegenerate log-rank limit; the binomial face identity transfers it jointly to all fixed face indices. The standalone checker passed every finite combinatorial test and independent Crofton numerical comparisons. Experiments are auxiliary, not infinite proofs.

## Originality — PASS
The motivating Birkemeyer–Brose–Brandenburg–Prün simplex-section classification supplies combinatorial types but no invariant probability law. De Loera–Lopez-Campos–Torres analyze maximum counts and attainable gaps rather than random section frequencies. The closest indexed central Haar-simplex theorem, inspected in full, explicitly excludes affine noncentral sections and obtains a different chi-square limit. Gaussian sample/order-statistic techniques are classical and are not claimed as new. An older equivalent result remains possible, particularly given abstract-only access to Baryshnikov–Vitale (1994).

## Mathematical value — PASS
The theorem supplies an exact finite-dimensional mean face vector and exact section-type probabilities, with a nontrivial high-dimensional consequence: typical section complexity has an intrinsically random logarithmic exponent, whereas the mean is almost quadratic. The law isolates a structural sampling effect missing from central-only sections and is a natural stochastic-geometric invariant of regular simplices.

## Limitations and closest literature
The result covers regular simplices under motion-invariant conditioned affine-hyperplane measure; not nonregular simplices, nor a finite-size convergence rate. Relevant sources: arXiv:2609.16195; arXiv:2412.12419; the published *Exact mean and chi-square fluctuations of Haar central simplex-section vertex counts*; doi:10.1007/BF02574000 (abstract-only, residual priority risk). The nontrivial asymptotic follows from the analytic proof, not the finite checks.
