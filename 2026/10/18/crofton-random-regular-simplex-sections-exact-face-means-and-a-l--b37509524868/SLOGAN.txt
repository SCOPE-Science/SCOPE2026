For a motion-invariant random affine hyperplane intersecting a regular simplex with \(n\) vertices, the exact expected number of \(j\)-faces is \(\binom n{j+2}R_{j+2}/R_n\), where \(R_m\) is the expected range of \(m\) independent standard Gaussians. The typical section's logarithmic vertex-count exponent converges to the nonconstant law \(2-U^2\) for \(U\) uniform on \((0,1)\), even though its expected vertex count is nearly quadratic.
