For every \(n\ge3\), the two-independence complex of the crown graph \(K_{n,n}\) with its diagonal perfect matching removed is homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{\binom{n-1}{2}}S^2\). The integral top-homology representation of column permutations and layer exchange is precisely \(\bigwedge^2 I_n\) with a negative layer-swap action, where \(I_n\) is the permutation augmentation lattice.
