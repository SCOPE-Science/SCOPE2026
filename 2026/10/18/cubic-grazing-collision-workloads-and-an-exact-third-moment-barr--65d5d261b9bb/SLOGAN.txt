For specular billiards started uniformly inside a Euclidean ball in every dimension at least two, the number of wall reflections in any fixed positive step has a sharp \(k^{-3}\) survival tail: it is finite almost surely but has infinite third and higher moments. Rare multiple-reflection events have an exact leading cubic step-size law, with dimension-dependent constants and a finite expected recursion count; the flat interval instead has exponentially small Gaussian multi-reflection probabilities.
