For a pair of uniform-attachment trees sharing their first \(t\) vertices, their unrooted leaf-count correlation is exactly \([t(t-1)^2-12]/[n(n-1)^2-12]\) when \(t\ge4\), while the two counts are independent for \(t\le3\). If the shared fraction \(t/n\) tends to \(\alpha\), the joint standardized leaf counts converge to a bivariate Gaussian with correlation \(\alpha^3\).
