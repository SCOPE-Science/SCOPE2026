## Correctness — PASS

The normalization by \(b\), exact two-step map, normal contraction \(u(1-u)\le 1/4\), first-order transverse correction, and cubic reduced drift are derived from the rational equations. A uniform compact-interior bootstrap gives \(s_j=O(\varepsilon)\); its corrected residual is summable, yielding a rigorous Euler-limit over \(O(\varepsilon^{-1})\) iterations. Direct integration and transverse threshold crossing establish the quantitative exit clock. Exact-rational coefficient tests and decreasing-parameter trajectory checks corroborate the algebra; simulations alone do not prove convergence.

## Originality — PASS, residual priority risk acknowledged

The original 2013 paper's Section 5, especially Theorem 14 and its proof, gives equilibrium type, basin separation and boundary period-two attractors on \(c=b+3a\); it does not identify the singular \(a/b\to0\) neutral segment, its cubic reduced drift, or the exact passage-time law. The broad invariant-curve theorems of Kulenović and Merino supply qualitative manifold geometry, not the displayed coefficient. A nearby 2014 rational-recurrence article treats a different denominator family and does not imply the present slow limit. Searches of the actual recurrence, its critical-surface aliases, and mathematical conclusions identified no dominating statement. An unindexed source may nevertheless exist; literature screening is not proof of absolute priority.
