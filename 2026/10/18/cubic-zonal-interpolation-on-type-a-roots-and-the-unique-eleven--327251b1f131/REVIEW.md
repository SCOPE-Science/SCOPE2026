# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The exact probability-sphere cubic zonal kernel in dimension \(m-1\) has normalized diagonal \((m-1)(m-2)(m+3)/6\). At the root-pair inner products \(0\) and \(\pm1/2\), the kernel becomes an exact scalar diagonal term plus a multiple of the complete-graph signed incidence Gram. Since \(BB^*=mI-J\), its two eigenvalues are strictly positive for all \(m\ge4\). The pseudoinverse formula therefore interpolates arbitrary complex edge data with minimum norm. Their ratio is one only at \(m=11\), where the Gram matrix is \(210I_{55}\). Exact symbolic identities and 26775 direct checker assertions corroborate the proof.

## Originality — PASS, with explicit limits

Full material read in Dai–Xu (2013) establishes only the general zonal addition theorem and general interpolation setup, not the type-\(A\) root-specific cubic spectrum. Barg et al. (2014) concern two-distance frames of the original vectors (and an edge-*sum* orbit), not cubic harmonic data on signed edge *differences*. Bannai et al. (2017) address balanced choices of antipodal roots and harmonic-index designs, not this interpolation Gram and its unique orthogonality resonance. Searches over corresponding exact and equivalent formulations did not reveal an already asserted specialized theorem. This is not a proof of worldwide novelty; an inaccessible classical spherical-code/design article and alternative association-scheme descriptions leave genuine residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The natural oriented-complete-graph/type-\(A\) root family has a complete all-dimensional third-harmonic sampling classification. The exact cut/cycle spectral splitting yields a computable minimum-energy interpolant and noise-amplification law. More notably, its Gram has a unique arithmetic tightness point: 55 zonal harmonics are pairwise orthogonal at eleven vertices, whereas every other family member is strictly less well conditioned. The statement is about the sampling operator rather than an arbitrary finite slice or a textbook scalar identity.

## Scientific limits

The proof applies to homogeneous harmonic degree three, exact normalized root sampling, and the stated measure; it does not show injectivity into the full harmonic space. Symbolic and finite numerical tests are corroboration, not independent validation. An older equivalent result remains possible.
