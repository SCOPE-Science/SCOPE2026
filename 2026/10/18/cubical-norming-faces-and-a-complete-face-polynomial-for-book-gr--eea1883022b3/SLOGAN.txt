For the real Lipschitz-free space of an \(m\)-page book graph, every nonzero vector's dual norming face is a combinatorial cube, and the complete norming-face count is encoded by \((3+2t)(2+t)^m-t^m(1+t)\). Its \(4m+2\) extreme unit vectors each have an \(m\)-cross-polytope vertex figure and exactly \(2m\) neighbors.
