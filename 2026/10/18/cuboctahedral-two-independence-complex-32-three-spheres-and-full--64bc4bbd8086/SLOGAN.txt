For the cuboctahedral graph \(C=L(Q_3)\), its two-independence complex has the exact integral homotopy type \(\operatorname{Ind}_2(C)\simeq\bigvee^{32}S^3\). The full octahedral automorphism group fixes a two-dimensional subspace of rational third homology, while the antipodal involution has \(21\)- and \(11\)-dimensional eigenspaces.
