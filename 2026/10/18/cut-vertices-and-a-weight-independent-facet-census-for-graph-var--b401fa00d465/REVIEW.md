# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The complete finite-graph vertex theorem follows from coordinate layer cake, edge coarea and a unique norming functional for each sign vector. For a path the norm is a maximum of signed edge-linear forms; a form supports a facet precisely when the sign on each edge adjacent to the unique absolute-max coordinate points toward that coordinate. Constructing strict sign-pattern witnesses proves sufficiency and exact uniqueness of all facets. The 880 exact-rational test assertions are supplementary to the general proof.

**Originality — PASS with residual risk.** Relevant graph-TV literature develops cut-based coarea and different graph variational problems. Database searches found a graphical-zonotope Euclidean inradius result and Lipschitz-free facet enumeration, but not the graph-TV-plus-supremum facet list or its dimension-only weighted-path count. Classical coarea is acknowledged as background; the originality claim rests particularly on the complete path facet classification. An equivalent unindexed formulation under Lovasz-extension or graph-signal polytope terminology remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** This classifies all extreme points of a graph-BV Banach ball and determines every facet of the entire weighted path family, a natural sharp structural invariant robust across continuously varying positive edge weights. The dual formula supplies a cut-optimization link.

Closest literature: Hansen–Bianchi (2023), arxiv:2211.06218, graph-TV regularization and cuts; arxiv:2001.07411, a distinct maximum-gradient L-infinity variational problem; classical discrete perimeter coarea, arxiv:0902.2313. The analysis does not cover infinite graphs or nonpositive edge weights and does not establish bibliographic uniqueness.
