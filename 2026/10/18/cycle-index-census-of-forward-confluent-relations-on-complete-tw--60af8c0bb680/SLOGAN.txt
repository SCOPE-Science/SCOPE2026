For the complete two-level intuitionistic frame with \(p,q\ge2\) worlds in its lower and upper antichains, forward confluence is equivalent to a simple two-support row condition. Exact formulas count both all labelled modal relations and their isomorphism classes via cycle-index sums, with \(11{,}401\) labelled relations and \(3{,}067\) isomorphism classes already on the \((2,2)\) frame.
