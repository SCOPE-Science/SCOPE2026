For any connected graph with at least six vertices and girth at least six, the Alexander dual of its total three-cut complex is a wedge of \(\binom{b+1}{2}\) three-spheres, where \(b\) is the graph's cycle rank. The total three-cut complex has integral reduced homology of exactly that rank in degree \(N-6\) and no other degree; if \(b>0\), it is nonshellable.
