The Frobenius inradius of every connected biregular bipartite fixed-cardinality matching polytope has a sharp two-obstruction formula, with a complete uniqueness-versus-high-dimensional-incenter classification. At saturated matching rank the incenter polytope can be a homothetic graph-supported transportation polytope whose dimension equals the graph's cycle rank; this produces an exact three-dimensional-to-unique transition between the incidence graphs of \(K_4\) and \(K_n\) for \(n\ge5\).
