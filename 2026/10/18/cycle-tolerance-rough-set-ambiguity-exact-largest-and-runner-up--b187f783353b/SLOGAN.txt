For reflexive cycle-neighborhood rough sets, the completely uncertain observation is the unique largest fiber, of size \(F_{n-1}+F_{n+1}+2\) when \(3\mid n\) and \(F_{n-1}+F_{n+1}-1\) otherwise. From \(n\ge7\), precisely \(2n\) outputs with one definite vertex are runners-up, each with \((F_{n-2}+\delta(n-1))/2\) representatives (with \(\delta(t)\) equal to \(1,0,-1\) for residues \(0,1,2\) modulo \(3\)); every remaining output is strictly smaller, and an exact four-state transfer matrix counts every fiber.
