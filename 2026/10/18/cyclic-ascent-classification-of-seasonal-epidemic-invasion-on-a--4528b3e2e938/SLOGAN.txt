For a periodically switched directed ring of epidemic transmission links, the entire disease-free Floquet polynomial is \( (\lambda-1)^n-P\lambda^k\), where \(k\) counts cyclic ascents of the seasonal contact order. This gives exact order-dependent invasion thresholds, including a three-group example in which every individual season and the averaged system are stable, but one chronological ordering is invasive and its reversal is stable.
