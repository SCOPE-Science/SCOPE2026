Under a uniform superpolynomial resolvent-growth condition, taking any finite cyclic companion root preserves every individual Douglas–Yang local exponent by the exact rule \(k_T(x)=\max_j k_B(x_j)\), hence preserves the entire exponent filtration. Consequently, for every \(m\ge2\) a noncompact quasinilpotent backward shift has maximal power set \(\Lambda(T)=[0,1]\) while its image in the Calkin algebra has exact nilpotency index \(m\).
