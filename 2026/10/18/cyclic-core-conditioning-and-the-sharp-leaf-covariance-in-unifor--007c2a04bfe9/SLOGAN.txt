For a uniform random mapping, conditioning on the cyclic-core size turns the terminal-vertex count into an explicit empty-box law, and its conditional mean decreases strictly with core size. The cyclic-core/terminal-count covariance is negative for every sample size and has the sharp asymptotic \(-\sqrt{\pi/2}\,\sqrt n/(2e)\), even though the correlation decays at rate \(n^{-1/2}\).
