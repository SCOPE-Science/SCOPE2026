# Scientific review

## Correctness (PASS)

Theorem 22 of Antolín Camarena–Carnero Bravo provides concentration of the reduced homology in the odd degree \(2r-1\). The independent new calculation enumerates fixed oriented simplices by position-rotation orbits and includes the augmented empty simplex. The quotient-row transfer matrix has a \((n-1)\)-dimensional \(-1\) eigenspace and a three-dimensional block satisfying \(B^3=(n-1)I\), which gives the exact character for all parameters including \(d=1,2\). The induced-representation and necklace formulas follow by the rational character theorem, Frobenius reciprocity and Burnside's lemma. The included standard-library program independently tests direct chain traces, row sums, integer transfer powers and character multiplicities; finite tests are corroboration, not the infinite proof.

Unindexed or differently named equivariant calculations remain a residual possibility.

## Value (PASS)

This is an all-parameter equivariant refinement of a natural homotopy-classified graph family: it determines every cyclic character value, identifies the rational representation up to isomorphism with a concrete word module, and yields a closed necklace-count formula for quotient homology. These invariants are genuinely not fixed by the previously known nonequivariant Betti numbers; for example different rotations can have distinct traces. The result is structural rather than a routine parameter increment.
