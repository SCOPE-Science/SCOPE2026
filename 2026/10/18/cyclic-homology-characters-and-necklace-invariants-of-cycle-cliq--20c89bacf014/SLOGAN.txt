For the independence complexes \(\operatorname{Ind}(C_{3r}\times K_n)\), the complete rational homology character of position rotations splits into a cyclically induced word-permutation representation, with only a small trivial or sign correction. The top rational Betti number of the geometric rotation quotient is the \((n-1)\)-color necklace count of length \(r\), minus \(n-1\) when \(r\) is odd.
