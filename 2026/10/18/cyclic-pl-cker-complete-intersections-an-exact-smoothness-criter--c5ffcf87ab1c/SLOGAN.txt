For every \(n\ge4\), setting every cyclic-neighbor Plücker coordinate to zero on \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,n)\) gives a reduced Gorenstein complete intersection with trivial dualizing sheaf, whose smooth points are exactly those with nonadjacent zero columns and independent neighbors at each zero. The component count \(L_n-n-1\) follows from known binomial-edge primes, while the pointwise criterion yields a nodal five-line cycle for \(n=5\) and eight planes plus three Segre quadrics for \(n=6\).
