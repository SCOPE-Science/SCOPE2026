# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

On the smooth GL₂ frame bundle the section is the rank-two open part of the cycle binomial edge variety. Radicality descends; the rank-one prime disappears, and each surviving prime has n frame parameters (one direction per run plus n−|T| column scalars). This gives pure dimension n−4 and hence regular-sequence codimension n, making adjunction and the degree calculation valid. A pair of adjacent zero columns gives a zero Jacobian row. Otherwise a nonzero proportional run of length r supplies r−1 independent rows, by taking the transverse coordinates successively from an endpoint. Each isolated zero gives a disjoint two-coordinate block whose rank is the rank of its neighbors. Thus the total rank is n−2|Z| plus the sum of these block ranks, and smoothness descends through the frame bundle in every characteristic. In the p₃₅ chart the specified germ eliminates every constraint except st=0, with n−5 free ratios. At n=5 the five nonadjacent pairs yield five coordinate lines meeting in a cycle; at n=6 the six distance-two pairs, three opposite pairs and two alternating triples give respectively linear planes, Segre quadrics and linear planes. Fresh RREF enumeration of F₂ in sizes 4–7 and F₃ in sizes 4–6 agreed at all 282 finite-field solution points (95 full-rank).
