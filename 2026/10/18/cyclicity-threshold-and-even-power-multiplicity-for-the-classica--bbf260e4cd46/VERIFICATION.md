# Verification notes

The analytic proof can be checked directly from the published semigroup estimate, the Laplace resolvent formula, and standard Hardy coefficient bounds. No empirical finite-dimensional approximation is needed to establish any density, noncyclicity, or multiplicity assertion.

The power moment formula is an operator-norm Bochner identity obtained by repeated convolution of a semigroup exponentially decaying in norm. Odd moment vanishing gives an entire local Taylor expansion of the sine transform in a nonempty strip. Fourier uniqueness is applied to an integrable odd extension, not to a discretized transform. The dual eigenfunctional identity follows from exact telescoping with an explicitly vanishing tail, and its continuity follows from a summable gamma-product coefficient envelope. Vandermonde independence is tested analytically on the first finitely many monomials. Density for small Hardy exponents comes from continuous inclusion and polynomial density.

The known result that the original Cesàro operator is cyclic is distinguished from the new power claims. A claim of exact multiplicity for odd powers greater than one would exceed the established proof and is not made. Independent audit has not been performed.
