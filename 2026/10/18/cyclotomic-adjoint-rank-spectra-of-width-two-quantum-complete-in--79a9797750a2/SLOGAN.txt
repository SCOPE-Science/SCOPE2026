For width-two quantum complete intersections \(\mathbb F_q\langle x,y\rangle/(x^2,y^n,yx-\lambda xy)\) with \(n\ge3\), every commutator rank and its exact multiplicity are determined by two coefficient valuations and the multiplicative order of \(\lambda\). The complete commuting-pair count exhibits cyclotomic resonance, while all parameters of order at least \(n\) share a single closed spectrum.
