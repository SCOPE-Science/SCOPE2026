The full automorphisms of the six-dimensional asymmetric quantum complete intersection \(k\langle x,y\rangle/(x^2,y^3,yx-\lambda xy)\) have an explicit normal form with independent cubic-root, fourth-root, and commutative resonance coordinates. Over \(\mathbb F_Q\), their number is exactly \((Q-1)^2Q^{4+\mathbf1_{1+\lambda^2=0}+\mathbf1_{1+\lambda+\lambda^2=0}+\mathbf1_{\lambda=1}}\), including the characteristic-two and characteristic-three jumps.
