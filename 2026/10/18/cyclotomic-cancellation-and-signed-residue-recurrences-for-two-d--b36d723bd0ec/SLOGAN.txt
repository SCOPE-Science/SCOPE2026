For two-digit radix sets, the location of \(2\) in the multiplicative orbit of the radix forces an exact periodic or signed-translated recurrence for all residue counts modulo any odd modulus. In both orbit cases the absolute deviation from uniformity stays bounded independently of the digit length, and the base-\(3\) modulo-\(7\) and base-\(4\) modulo-\(9\) examples are perfectly balanced at every length.
