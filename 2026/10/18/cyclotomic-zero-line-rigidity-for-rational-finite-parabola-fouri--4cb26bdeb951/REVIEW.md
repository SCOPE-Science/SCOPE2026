Two reflection centers force nonzero translation invariance and contradict nonzero oddness. The cases \(a=0\) and the origin are handled independently. The sign census is a disjoint partition: balanced signs versus unbalanced signs, and reflection-odd balanced signs versus non-reflection-odd balanced signs. Relevant indexed finite-parabola results treat individual small fields with complex coefficients, not this coefficient-field-sensitive rigidity. Unindexed priority remains possible; a search is evidence, not certification of novelty.
