# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** At horizon equal to the number of vertices, the hub-to-state Krylov determinant factors exactly into a product of all spoke conductances and all pairwise conductance differences. Triangular binomial conversion to the stochastic transition multiplies by the specified power of the step parameter. Squaring therefore establishes the stated finite-horizon Gramian determinant, including singular boundary cases. Every maximizer has distinct positive weights. Swapping oppositely ordered price and conductance pairs and rescaling shows that conductances are inversely ordered by prices. Within the resulting ordered chamber, the log-determinant objective has a negative-definite Hessian and diverges to minus infinity on every boundary, giving an interior unique global maximum. Euler homogeneity supplies its exact Lagrange multiplier. For equal prices, the Laguerre differential equation verifies the unique stationary configuration, and classical resultant identities supply the exact maximum. The direct standalone exact-arithmetic checks recompute six weighted-star determinants and six Laguerre discriminants; they are not substituted for the universal proof.

**Originality — PASS, subject to a non-exhaustive literature search.** The pre-existing Stieltjes electrostatic description of Laguerre roots is explicitly prior art, as is generic Vandermonde factorization of structured controllability matrices. The new assessed statement is their conjunction as a *globally solved, arbitrary-positive-price, minimum-horizon D-optimal star conductance-design problem*, including the exact Gramian determinant, reverse-price ordering, unique cost-dependent optimizer, and equal-price optimal-value formula. Sundaram–Hadjicostis (DOI 10.1109/TAC.2012.2204155) studies general structured controllability, not these priced symmetric-Laplacian allocations. Pasqualetti–Zampieri–Bullo (arXiv:1308.1201) gives reachable-volume/Gramian context without the priced-star classification. Grünbaum (DOI 10.1016/S0377-0427(00)00661-0) discusses Stieltjes electrostatics and related orthogonal-polynomial zeros, not weighted-star controllability design. Gautschi (Numer. Math. 24 (1975), 1–12) concerns Vandermonde condition number rather than determinant. An equivalent result in broader optimal experimental-design or electrostatic-node literature remains a genuine priority risk.

**Value — PASS.** The minimum-horizon reachable-volume objective is a natural D-optimal sensor/actuator-design metric. The proof turns an apparently high-degree coupled graph-design problem into a unique tractable, strictly concave configuration with an actionable sorting rule for heterogeneous edge costs. The identical-cost optimum is an explicit classical zero configuration with a closed performance value. The finding applies to every star size and every strictly positive price vector; it is not simply a restatement of polynomial-zero electrostatics.

**Limitations and risks.** The objective is a *finite-horizon determinant*, not an inverse-Gramian energy bound or worst-case controllability index. Arbitrary topology, a leaf actuator, and arbitrary control horizon are not covered. Independent mathematical and historical-priority validation are not performed.
