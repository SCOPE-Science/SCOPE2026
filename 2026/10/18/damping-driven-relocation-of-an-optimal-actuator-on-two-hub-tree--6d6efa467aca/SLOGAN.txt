For a two-hub tree with \(m\ge3\) leaves on each hub, the optimal single actuator for stable diffusive dynamics moves sharply from the hub leaves to the junction's private leaf as damping crosses \(\alpha_*(m)=(\sqrt{(m+2)^2+8/(m-2)}-m-2)/2\). At the transition all leaves tie, and the critical damping scales as \(2/m^2\); when \(m=2\), the hub leaves remain optimal for every positive damping.
