For every graph without isolated vertices, the largest-volume inscribed ellipsoid in its coordinate-disk Minkowski sum has semiaxes exactly the vertex degrees. For every connected biregular bipartite graph, the unique enclosing Löwner ellipsoid also has explicit degree-controlled semiaxes, and all of its contacts form an embedded complete bipartite graph \(K_{2^p,2^q}\) with first Betti number \((2^p-1)(2^q-1)\).
