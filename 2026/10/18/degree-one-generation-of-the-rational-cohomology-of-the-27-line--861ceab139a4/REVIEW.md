# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
For the Fermat cubic, the 27 explicit line equations have 81 ordinary double intersections and 18 ordinary triple (Eckardt) intersections. Blowing up the latter produces a 45-component, 135-node SNC boundary whose dual graph has cycle rank 91. The Picard-class and Gysin calculation gives 20 degree-one residues and vanishing intermediate weights. The exact appended checker constructs the 190 wedge products and the 45-vertex incidence matrix, verifies ranks 135 and 44 modulo 101, and thereby proves that the cup products span rational degree-two cohomology. The 2-dimensional affine CW bound and the integral unimodular class basis complete the stated integral homology calculation. These are exact and theoretical arguments, not extrapolation from samples.

## Originality — PASS
Sturmfels and Telen's directly inspected arXiv:2605.11909v2, Theorem 1.1 and Lemma 5.3, concern general no-Eckardt cubic surfaces and a 109-dimensional logarithmic two-form space. They do not assert Fermat degree-one cup-generation or give the 91-dimensional multiplication certificate. Brown and Dupont's general mixed-Hodge/canonical-form formalism is background, not an identified calculation of the special case. Targeted mathematical-implication comparisons with published-finding corpus records on Fermat cubic sixers, the abstract 27-line neighborhood complex, and degree-four del Pezzo complements found no result implying this theorem. An inaccessible full reading of the 2025 mixed-Hodge paper, older unindexed literature, and independent audit remain risks, not evidence of novelty by absence alone.

## Value — PASS
The Fermat cubic maximizes Eckardt concurrence among smooth complex cubic surfaces. Computing its pure-Tate cohomology and its full rational multiplication structure addresses the special boundary regime excluded from the normal-crossing 109-dimensional calculation motivating the recent positive-geometry work. Degree-one algebra generation is not determined by Betti numbers, and it can be used to represent every rational two-dimensional cohomology class as a linear combination of wedges of explicit logarithmic classes.

## Limits
The result makes no claim about integral cup-product surjectivity, all special cubic surfaces, or the homotopy type. The finite-field line enumerations support the independently given characteristic-zero pairing argument; the modular rank certificate is exact for the rational cup statement because it has full row rank.
