For finite interval orders, the multiset of paired strict predecessor/successor counts completely reconstructs the order and its automorphism orbits; two-variable counting logic of quantifier rank two therefore distinguishes every isomorphism type within this infinite class. The one-sided successor-degree multisets form a Catalan family, and the exact multiplicity of each one-sided signature is a computable upper-triangular matrix-margin coefficient, with its first collision at four elements.
