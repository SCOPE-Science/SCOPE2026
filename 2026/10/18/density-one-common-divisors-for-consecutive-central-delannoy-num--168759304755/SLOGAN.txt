For every odd prime \(p\), the exact number of indices \(n<p^L\) for which \(p\) does not divide both consecutive central Delannoy numbers is \(1+(p-1)\sum_{h=0}^{L-1}(p-z_p)^h\), where \(z_p\) counts the zero residues among the first \(p\) Delannoy numbers. Every prime \(p\equiv3\pmod4\) divides the gcd of two consecutive Delannoy numbers on a density-one set of indices; consequently that gcd tends to infinity in natural density.
