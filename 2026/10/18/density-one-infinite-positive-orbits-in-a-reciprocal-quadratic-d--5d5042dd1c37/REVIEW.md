# Scientific review: reciprocal quadratic divisibility parameter coverage

**Correctness (PASS).** The conic identity is a polynomial identity, and the Vieta involution is an exact root swap. The seed algebra is checked in both families, and the strictly increasing recurrence turns each conic orbit into infinitely many positive, coprime, reciprocal-divisibility pairs. The least-prime-factor case split proves the precise lower bound on the trace \(H\), with equality exactly at \(k=10\). Dirichlet's theorem supplies infinitely many prime-shift examples, and the prime number theorem in progressions gives the stated upper count for uncovered parameters. Exact-arithmetic computation checks, but does not establish, the infinite theorem.

**Originality (PASS with residual risk).** The recurrence method is classical, and the 2026 Alekseyev--Khomovsky paper contains the fixed-conic mechanism: no novelty is attributed to Vieta jumping. The checked claims are instead the uniform sufficient condition \(k+2\) composite beyond the sharp threshold, the explicit prime-shift family, the density-one consequence, and the unique parabolic orbit. The materially read sections of the motivating manuscript, relevant bibliographic searches, and accessible indexed research comparisons did not disclose a stronger theorem implying this parameter-wide statement. The complete older Mills paper was not read, and the possibility of an unindexed or implicit version remains a material risk; no uniqueness or independence is asserted.

**Value (PASS).** The family depends naturally on the linear coefficient of a fixed monic quadratic. Determining for how many parameters the reciprocal system has even a single infinite positive orbit is a concrete arithmetic gap distinct from the previously investigated dynamics of individual conics. The constructions cover density one with a quantitative exceptional upper bound, exhibit prime-shift solvability beyond the naive composite condition, and separate polynomial from exponential growth in a sharp case. These are structural, infinite-family facts, not isolated numerical coincidences.

**Scope and limits.** No exact classification of prime-shift parameters outside the progression is proved; no density assertion about individual integer pairs is made. The 1954 Mills antecedent and possible implicit conic corollaries remain risks.
