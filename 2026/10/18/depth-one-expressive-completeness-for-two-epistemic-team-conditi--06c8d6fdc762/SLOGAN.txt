Over every finite propositional valuation space, adding either Barbero–Yang's epistemic indicative \(\circ\!\to\) or epistemic conditional \(\bullet\!\to\) to classical team logic defines exactly the union-closed team properties containing the empty team, with conditional nesting depth one sufficient and generally necessary. The epistemic counterfactual \(\rightsquigarrow\), by contrast, never increases classical flat expressive power, regardless of nesting depth.
