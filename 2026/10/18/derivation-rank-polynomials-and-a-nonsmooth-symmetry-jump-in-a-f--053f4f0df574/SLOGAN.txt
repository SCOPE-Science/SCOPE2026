For the four-dimensional algebra \(\mathbb F_q[X,Y]/(X^2,Y^2)\), the full linear-rank distribution of derivations is an explicit polynomial in the field size, with separate formulas in odd and even characteristic. Characteristic \(2\) produces a two-dimensional excess of infinitesimal automorphisms: the automorphism group scheme has reduced dimension \(6\) but tangent dimension \(8\), while in odd characteristic it is smooth at the identity.
