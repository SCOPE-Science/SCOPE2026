Deleting the two opposite extreme coordinates from the binary Segre embedding gives a normal projective variety whose only singularities are \(2n\) isolated rank-one \(2\times(n-1)\) determinantal cones, each with local class group \(\mathbb Z\) and multiplicity \(n-1\). The sharp canonical boundary is \(n=3\): there the six points are ordinary nodes and the variety is Gorenstein, whereas every higher-dimensional member is non-\(\mathbb Q\)-Gorenstein exactly at its \(2n\) singular points.
