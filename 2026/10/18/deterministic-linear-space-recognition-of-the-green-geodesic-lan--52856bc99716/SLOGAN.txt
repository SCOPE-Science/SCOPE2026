For every finite alphabet, membership in each of the \(\mathcal R\)-, \(\mathcal L\)-, and \(\mathcal D\)-geodesic languages of a free inverse monoid is decidable in deterministic linear space: the exact shortest lengths are twice the number of Munn-tree edges minus, respectively, the initial-root eccentricity, terminal-root eccentricity, and diameter. Consequently all three languages and their complements are context-sensitive, resolving the context-sensitivity question posed for these Green languages.
