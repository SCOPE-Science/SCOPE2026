For a uniform random permutation, the upper-record indicator at position \(i\) is independent of the inverse permutation's upper-record indicator at every different index \(j\); the complete cross-covariance matrix has diagonal entries \((i-1)/(i^2\binom ni)\) and rank \(n-1\). Consequently, their total record counts have covariance \(1/n+1/(2n^2)+O(n^{-3})\) despite each count having variance of logarithmic order.
