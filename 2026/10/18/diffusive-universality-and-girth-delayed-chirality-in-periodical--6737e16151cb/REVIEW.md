# Self-review

## Correctness: PASS
The definitions specify equal-magnitude nearest-neighbor magnetic hopping, periodic complete position dephasing, and total variation of the resulting stochastic convolution. The proof derives the exact winding-amplitude representation and the Bessel probability distribution, controls **both** the coherent tail-square and the omitted incoherent tail-square (hence the conservative term \(T_n^2\)), and telescopes the error uniformly over all fluxes. A Fourier local-limit argument establishes the full heat-kernel mixing profile, not merely weak convergence. Odd-cycle antisymmetry is traced to the shortest odd closed walk and its leading coefficient is computed directly. The bipartite case is derived separately. The all-size proof is analytic; 702 finite numerical tests are secondary checks. The quantitative bounds are not asserted for growing coherent intervals or nonuniform rings.

## Originality: PASS, with priority uncertainty
The primary full-text comparison is Longhi, *Dynamical Phase Transitions in Open Quantum Walks*, arXiv:2508.11947v1 (2025), especially its periodically dephased Markov channel and the explicit unequal-edge three-node network. It does **not** give the flux-uniform large-homogeneous-ring total-variation heat profile, factorial coupling bound, or adjacent odd-cycle first-chirality coefficient. Zimborás et al., *Scientific Reports* 3, 2361 (2013), already establish the even-bipartite transition symmetry and odd-loop directional control; these facts alone are explicitly **not** claimed as new. Finocchiaro et al. (arXiv:2507.10669, 2025) use one-target monitoring and derive first-detection results, not the fully dephased site Markov law. Levine et al. (arXiv:2605.04414, 2026) address unitary instantaneous/average mixing rather than repeated site dephasing. Fedichkin et al. (arXiv:quant-ph/0509163) use continuous environmental dephasing. Scientific search for stronger equivalent claims and public mathematical records did not identify direct coverage. Standard probability heat-kernel limits cover the flux-free reference kernel, but do not by themselves imply the uniform-in-flux quantum-to-reference approximation. Residual risk: a less visible prior work or an implicit application of known Bessel and finite-propagation identities might subsume part of the statement; independent verification of priority is outstanding.

## Value: PASS
The 2025 open-walk phase-transition literature motivates whether magnetic directionality at small ring sizes persists under homogeneous graph enlargement. The result answers that question at the natural \(n^2\) mixing scale with an explicit \(\varepsilon\)-mixing profile uniform over arbitrary total flux sequences; it also gives a factorial finite-size error and a sharp odd-girth order at which microscopic chirality first appears. The contrast between the \(n^2\) complete-dephasing mixing scale and the nearly cubic **first-detection** scale of a different monitoring scheme distinguishes physically relevant observables. This is a coherent structural law rather than a routine isolated small-parameter calculation. No generality beyond the specified model is claimed.
