For every \(n\ge5\), the two-unordered-particle configuration space of the prism \(C_n\square K_2\) has full \(D_n\times C_2\) rational first-homology representation \(\epsilon\otimes\mathbb Q[F_n]\), and integral second homology is the signed module on disjoint prism-face pairs. Its quotient by polygon dihedral symmetries retains one rational two-cycle, while adding layer exchange makes the full-symmetry quotient rationally acyclic.
