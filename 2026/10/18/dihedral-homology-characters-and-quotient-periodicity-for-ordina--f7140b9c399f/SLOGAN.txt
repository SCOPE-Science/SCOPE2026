For every wheel \(C_n*K_1\), the ordinary matching complex has an explicit full rational dihedral homology character: nontrivial rotations are governed by a six-periodic cosine, and even-length reflections share one trace regardless of axis. Its full dihedral quotient has one nonzero reduced rational homology class exactly when \(n\equiv2,5,10,11\pmod{12}\); the pre-existing nonequivariant wheel sphere counts are not new.
