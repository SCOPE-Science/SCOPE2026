# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The exact claim applies to every integer \(n\ge3\), on the specified regular Boolean cell complex, with integral top homology rationalized only for the character. Ragnarsson–Tenner's prior wedge-of-spheres theorem supplies concentration and freeness. The signed fixed-cell Lefschetz formula, quotient-orbit analysis, chromatic transfer at \(q=-1\), and reflection path recursion prove the claimed values for every group element. Special cyclic quotient sizes one and two and the boundary case \(n=3\) are handled. Two structurally different checkers independently verify the formula for finite ranges, without treating finite checks as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual bibliographic risk
The closest primary theorem (Ragnarsson–Tenner, arXiv:0806.0906) proves only the homotopy type and sphere count. Their subsequent arXiv:1005.4411 analyzes a mod-two homology basis. Jonsson–Welker's arXiv:0712.2143 studies shellability and indexing of commutation-class complexes. Their inspected results do not imply the complete signed dihedral character. Explicit graph-automorphism, reflection, Lucas, equivariant homology, related-complex and indexed-result searches found no matching or dominating statement. This is a bounded literature comparison, not proof of absolute priority; equivalent unindexed work remains a risk.

## Value — PASS
The \(n\)-cycle is the standard affine type-A Coxeter graph and its dihedral symmetries are canonical. The precise signed action of its full symmetry group on top homology gives a natural infinite-family representation invariant. In particular, the contrast between the two reflection classes for even cycles and the rotation dependence on \(\gcd(n,j)\) cannot be recovered from the previously known Betti number alone.

## Limitations
No integral lattice-isomorphism or equivariant homotopy classification is claimed. The general theorem is proved mathematically; numerical tests are finite. The bibliographic search may miss equivalent unindexed work, and the available inspection of one source used a lawfully accessible page rendering rather than an independent publisher PDF.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
