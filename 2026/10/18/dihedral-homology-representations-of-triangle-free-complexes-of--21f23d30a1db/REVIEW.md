# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The object identification is checked algebraically: for every \(n\ge7\), every triangle of the squared cycle consists of three consecutive cyclic vertices, with no other triangles. The 2021 tight-hypergraph theorem gives mod-two Betti numbers in one degree; universal coefficients restrict rational homology to that degree without asserting torsion-freeness. Signed augmented Lefschetz traces are computed from invariant orbit subsets, with the orientation of every setwise-fixed simplex explicitly accounted for. The four-state transfer matrix yields the rotation formula, and two-ended palindromic constraints yield the two reflection types. The four rational representations have the resulting characters at every dihedral element, determining them uniquely. A standalone finite checker directly enumerates oriented simplex traces, all dihedral orbit sums through \(n=13\), and mod-two homology through \(n=12\); the infinite proof is the symbolic calculation.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risks
The most directly relevant primary paper by Kim and Kim (2021), Theorem 3.1, already calculates the mod-two Betti numbers for *exactly the same cyclic triple-hypergraph independence complexes*. This known dimension statement is an input and **not claimed new**. Their Theorem 3.4 concerns the Alexander-dual noncover complex, not its rational dihedral homology character. The 2026 Ghosh–Selvaraja clique-free-complex paper studies structural shellability classes and contains no calculated squared-cycle dihedral representation. The published bounded-independence cycle orientation theorem treats faces forbidding induced independent sets of size \(d\), not consecutive cyclic triples; the dihedral action on matching complexes of cycles is likewise an action on a different simplicial complex. The indexed cyclic three-cut representation for powers of cycles concerns disconnected triples and therefore does not imply the character of the triangle-free complex here. The comparison concerns objects, homological degrees, group actions and implied characters, not title similarity. An unindexed general equivariant theorem remains a genuine originality risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Ordinary Betti numbers cannot distinguish the symmetry type of the homology. The exact character reveals a two-dimensional quarter-turn summand only for lengths divisible by four and a parity-changing reflection eigenline across neighboring lengths. This is a complete and natural equivariant refinement of a published infinite-family calculation in current clique-free topological combinatorics, not a routine re-labelling of the known rank formula.
