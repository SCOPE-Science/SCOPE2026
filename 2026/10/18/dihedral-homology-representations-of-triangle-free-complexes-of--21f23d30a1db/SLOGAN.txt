For every squared cycle \(C_n^2\) with \(n\ge7\), the triangle-free simplicial complex has a fully explicit rational dihedral homology representation: a quarter-turn two-dimensional summand appears exactly when \(4\mid n\), while its one-dimensional summand has an exact parity-dependent reflection sign. This distinguishes natural cyclic and reflective symmetries that the previously known Betti-number formula does not detect.
