The dual condition that every induced triple is connected is equivalent to having at most one nonneighbor per vertex. The key two-orbit localization lemma is proved from exact cyclic distances, the condition \(n>4p\), and the matching bound on missing edges. Equivariant Alexander duality supplies the ambient permutation-sign twist, and character averaging supplies the quotient ranks.
