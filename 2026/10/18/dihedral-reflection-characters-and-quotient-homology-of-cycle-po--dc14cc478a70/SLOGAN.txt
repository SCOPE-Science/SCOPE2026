For every \(p\ge2\) and \(n\ge6p-3\), the reflection characters on the top rational homology of the ordinary three-cut complex \(\Delta_3(C_n^p)\) have explicit parity-dependent closed forms, completing its previously known cyclic character to the full dihedral character. The full dihedral quotient is rationally acyclic for odd \(n\equiv1\pmod4\), while its nonzero top Betti numbers in the other congruence classes are determined exactly.
