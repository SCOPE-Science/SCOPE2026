For a Brownian gas with one common Poisson reset clock of rate \(\lambda/s\) and contraction factor \(1-s\), the stationary high-excursion vector \(\sqrt{s}\,\mathbf X\) obeys an explicit dilogarithmic large-deviation law. Shared resets make simultaneous rare excursions exponentially likelier than independent reset clocks, even though the fixed-scale stationary coordinates become independent Gaussian variables.
