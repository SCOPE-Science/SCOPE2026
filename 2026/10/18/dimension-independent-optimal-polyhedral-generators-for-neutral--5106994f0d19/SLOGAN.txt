An expanding circle map can encode arbitrarily many fixed torus coordinates using only **one extra symbol**: for every \(q\ge2\) and \(m\ge2\), the smallest polyhedral one-sided generator of \((\boldsymbol\theta,y)\mapsto(\boldsymbol\theta,my\bmod1)\) has exactly \(m+1\) atoms, independent of \(q\). Disjoint interrogation windows make the extra symbol carry all fixed-coordinate information, while entropy rules out \(m\) atoms.
