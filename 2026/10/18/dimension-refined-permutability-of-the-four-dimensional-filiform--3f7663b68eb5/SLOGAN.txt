For the four-dimensional filiform Lie algebra \([x,y]=z\), \([x,z]=w\) over every \(\mathbb F_q\), every failure of subalgebra permutability involves dimensions one or two, and all four ordered dimension-cell counts have explicit polynomial formulas. The exact probability of permutability is \(\bigl(11q^4+27q^3+39q^2+30q+25\bigr)/\bigl(q^3+3q^2+3q+5\bigr)^2\), asymptotic to \(11/q^2\).
