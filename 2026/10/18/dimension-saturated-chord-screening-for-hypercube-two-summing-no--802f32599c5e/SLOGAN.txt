On a binary hypercube, an arbitrary collection of added Hamming-distance chords preserves the real free two-convex lattice and absolutely two-summing edge-gradient norm exactly when each chord amplitude lies below an explicit Walsh-spectral threshold. Every nonedge threshold approaches \(1-1/(4d)\) uniformly with dimension \(d\), so adding all chords just below that threshold preserves the norm while enlarging the underlying lattice element pointwise by an exact factor asymptotic to \(d\).
