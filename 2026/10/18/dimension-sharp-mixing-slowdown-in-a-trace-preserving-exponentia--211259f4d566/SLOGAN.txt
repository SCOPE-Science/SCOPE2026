On a uniform \(n\)-level Lindblad jump cycle, a positivity- and trace-preserving exponential Euler step has a sharp dimension-dependent contraction penalty: it is strictly slower than the exact evolution after \(\gamma\tau>\log3\) for \(n=2\), after \(\gamma\tau>\log2\) for \(n=3\), and at every positive step for \(n\ge4\). The numerical relaxation time grows like \(e^{\gamma\tau}/(1-\cos(2\pi/n))\) steps while the exact evolution mixes within a large single step, and the maximum one-step trace error tends to \(2(1-1/n)\).\n
