In a lattice search with rapid Poisson resetting, the first successful route that is not shortest has exactly two extra hops, with an explicit coordinate-resolved probability of order \(r^{-2}\), and the successful duration approaches an integer-shape Gamma law at a sharp total-variation rate. For a particle starting two steps from the target and resetting at rate \(2d\), the probability of a nonshortest successful route is \(5/(8d)+O(d^{-2})\), quantifying the large-dimensional route localization observed previously.
