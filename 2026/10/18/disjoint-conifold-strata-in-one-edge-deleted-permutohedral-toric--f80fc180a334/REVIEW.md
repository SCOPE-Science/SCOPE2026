# Review of disjoint conifold strata in one-edge-deleted permutohedral toric varieties

## Correctness — PASS
The infinite argument uses the explicit deletion of one braid hyperplane: every maximal cone is either a braid chamber or the merger of exactly two chambers that differ by swapping adjacent labels. A single primitive four-ray parallelogram relation arises when there is a nonempty block on both sides of the incomparable pair. A braid-chamber lattice basis gives a saturated direct factor, and the dual semigroup calculation yields an actual ordinary-double-point equation rather than merely a numerical determinant test. All proper cone faces are unimodular, so the minimal singular cones are precisely the stated \(\tau_S\). Their stars are integrally products of two braid fans. Separate finite exact checks through seven labels agree with the formulas. The proof and computations establish reduced support and the stated local models, not a global Jacobian-scheme structure.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
Postnikov--Reiner--Williams, *Faces of Generalized Permutohedra* (arXiv:math/0609184; Sections 3 and 5), furnish the graphical orientation/normal-fan setup and general simplicity criterion. Padrol--Pilaud--Poullot, *Deformed Graphical Zonotopes* (arXiv:2111.12422; Sections 1 and 2), furnish graphical-fan and nonedge support-function relations. These results provide foundational machinery, but the inspected statements do not specify the \(2^{n-2}-2\) disjoint orbit closures, integral product-star description, transverse conifold models, or fixed-point census for the one-edge-deleted complete graph. The special four-vertex graph also belongs to the fan family \(K_1\vee P_3\), for which smooth fixed-point counts were previously recorded; that finite count is not advanced as new. Related complete-multipartite, cycle, wheel and fan graphical-zonotope results concern different graph families or invariants. Searches for deleted-braid-hyperplane singular cones, one-edge-deleted complete graphs, and permutohedral conifold strata did not identify an equivalent specific theorem. The possibility of a differently worded or unindexed classification is a residual risk, not a certified absence.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The complete graph is the canonical smooth permutohedral fan. Removing a single edge is its minimal graph-theoretic perturbation, yet it produces exponentially many *pairwise disjoint* singular components, each smooth and explicitly identified as a product of permutohedral varieties, with a uniform three-dimensional ordinary-double-point slice. This reveals precisely how simplicity fails without forcing singular-component intersections and gives closed counts in every dimension. The classification is a natural all-parameter toric-geometric structural result rather than a recomputation of a familiar sequence or an isolated small graph.

## Literature and limits
Primary material examined: Postnikov--Reiner--Williams (2006/2008), Sections 3,5 (including pages 224–225), and Padrol--Pilaud--Poullot (2021/2023), relevant fan/deformation sections. Distinguish the general structural tools in these works from the new claimed explicit classification. Results concern the normal fan over algebraically closed fields, and assert reduced singular support, affine local models, and a smooth toric stratum decomposition, not independent verification or an explicit Jacobian scheme. A targeted literature search cannot rule out every equivalent formulation.
