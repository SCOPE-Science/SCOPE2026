Deleting one edge from the complete graph turns the permutohedral toric variety into a variety with exactly \(2^{n-2}-2\) pairwise disjoint smooth singular strata, all of codimension three and all transversely ordinary-double-point singular. The strata are products of smaller permutohedral varieties, and the singular torus-fixed points number \((n-3)(n-2)!\).
