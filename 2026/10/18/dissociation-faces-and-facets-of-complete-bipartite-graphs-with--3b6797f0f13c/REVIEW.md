# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The selected-neighbor degree identities, exhaustive classification of occupancy pairs, and direct one-vertex-extension classification prove the formulas for all \(a,b\ge3\) and \(0\le t\le\min(a,b)\). Separate brute-force induced-degree tests on 78 parameter profiles and 83,456 vertex subsets recover both polynomials. The proof, not the finite check, carries the infinite quantifiers.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** Bock–Pardey–Penso–Rautenbach (arXiv:2202.01004) analyze extremal dissociation numbers and equality with matching bounds, while Tu–Li–Du (arXiv:2103.01402) give general/triangle-free counts and bounds on maximal sets. They do not imply the exact two counting polynomials for the family with a deleted matching. Published findings for dissociation polynomials of complete multipartite graphs cover the undeleted \(t=0\) boundary; published results involving the matched-deletion graph itself count different invariants. Relevant mathematical-record and web searches also used equivalent \(P_3\)-free and minimal three-path cover language. The possibility of an unindexed equivalent theorem remains, and some linked records could not be read in full.

**Value — PASS.** The result classifies all faces and facets of a natural three-parameter graph family and establishes an arbitrarily long gap in possible inclusion-maximal cardinalities in connected regular crown graphs. This gives structural information absent from size-only dissociation-number bounds.

**Limitations.** Graph parts must have at least three vertices. No arbitrary edge-deletion generalization is claimed. Computations certify only their tested finite range; exhaustive priority review and independent scientific validation remain outstanding.
