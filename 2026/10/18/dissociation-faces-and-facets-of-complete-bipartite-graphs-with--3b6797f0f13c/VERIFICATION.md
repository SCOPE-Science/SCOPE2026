The (1,1),(2,1),(1,2),(2,2) exact possibilities contribute ab,t(a+b−2),binom(t,2), with all one-sided subsets counted by binomials. Inclusion maximality: each complete part maximal, (1,1) iff both endpoints unmatched, (2,1)/(1,2) iff second selected vertex unmatched, and (2,2) always maximal.
