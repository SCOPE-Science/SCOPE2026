The Shrikhande and \(4\times4\) rook graphs have identical strongly regular parameters \((16,6,2,2)\) and spectra, yet their dissociation polynomials differ by exactly \(16z^4(1+6z+2z^2)\). They have dissociation numbers \(6\) and \(5\), respectively, so dissociation enumeration detects structure invisible to the shared spectral parameters.
