For finite ultrametrics with at most \(h\) named positive distance levels, distance-aware one-dimensional colour refinement recovers the isometry type within the ultrametric class after at most \(h-1\) rounds. At three levels, two rounds suffice; the first one-round failure occurs at eight points and consists of exactly one nonisometric pair, with four-point cluster patterns \(\{(2,1,1),(2,1,1)\}\) versus \(\{(2,2),(1,1,1,1)\}\).
