## correctness

PASS

For all fixed a>1 and ε∈(0,1), the reciprocal incumbent recurrence y_{t+1}=a^{-1}(1-ε)^{-1}(y_t+a-1) has the positive fixed point x*=1-aε/(a-1) precisely below ε_c=1-1/a; at equality x_t=[1+(a-1)t]^{-1}, and above it tends geometrically to zero. The newborn cohort of age k has limit εr^k with r=1/a below ε_c and 1-ε above; each finite-time cohort is bounded by ε(1-ε)^k, yielding ℓ¹ and dominated-entropy convergence. The resulting entropy H and Simpson concentration C are exactly the two piecewise formulas in the Git RESULT; H is strictly concave and C strictly convex on the persistent branch, while H decreases and C increases on the extinction branch. Differentiation gives ε_1=1/[a/(a-1)+a^{-1/(a-1)}], ε_2=(a²-1)/(2a²), both strictly below ε_c and ε_2<ε_1 by log(a)/(a-1)>log(1+1/a). Independent numerical reconstruction at a=1.1,1.5,2,3,10 verified both stationary values, maximum Hill effective numbers, and comparisons with off-optimum and above-threshold cases; exact rational a=2 yields ε_2=3/8, ε_1=2/5 and N_2=4,N_1=5. The analytic arguments, not finite iterations, justify all-parameter infinite-age conclusions.

The final statement is the exact noncoincident Hill-1/Hill-2 global optimizer and strict extinction-threshold ordering for the specified permanent-incumbent, deterministic fixed-novelty-mass, one-new-type-per-period law. These are not merely standard Shannon/Simpson formulas: the exact age distribution and split parameter optimization supply the claim. ## value

PASS

The natural coexistence of novelty introduction with a permanent fitness incumbent creates a genuine quantitative question: how much forced influx optimizes two standard inequivalent effective-diversity measures, and whether their optima precede extinction of the fit type. The exact answer exhibits a nonempty policy interval in which raising innovation improves Shannon but worsens Simpson diversity, which is not implied merely by the incumbent persistence threshold. It provides a deliberately idealized but transparent benchmark, not a quantitative empirical prediction. The algebra is elementary once the model is fixed, yet the precise opposing optima and phase boundary are motivated and falsifiable.
