In a deterministic culture-copying model with one permanently advantaged variant and one distinct newcomer each round, limiting Shannon and Simpson effective diversities are both maximized at finite innovation fractions, but at strictly different rates for every fitness advantage \(a>1\). At \(a=2\), Simpson favors \(3/8\) while Shannon favors \(2/5\), and both maxima precede incumbent loss at \(1/2\).
