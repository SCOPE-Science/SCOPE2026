For iid categorical data, the weighted covariance of all overlapping ordered-pair counts has exactly four probability-independent spectral levels \(0,1,L,2L-1\), with multiplicities \(1,q-1,(q-1)^2,q-1\). The known-probability Pearson statistic has exact mean \(q^2-1\) but converges to the nonstandard null law \(\chi^2_{(q-1)^2}+2\chi^2_{q-1}\), not the ordinary multinomial chi-square law.
