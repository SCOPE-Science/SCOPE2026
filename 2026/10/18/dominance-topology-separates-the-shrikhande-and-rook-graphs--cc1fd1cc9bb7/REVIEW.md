# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
For the two explicitly defined sixteen-vertex graphs, an exhaustive, deterministic standard-library checker constructs all subsets satisfying the no-closed-neighborhood containment rule, producing 59,819 and 59,747 faces including empty. Its matching partitions the face posets and validates full acyclicity by topological sorting of 461,568 and 460,672 oriented covering incidences. Removing the matched empty-face pair yields exactly one critical vertex and the asserted higher critical cells. This proves the Shrikhande wedge homotopy type and rook integral homology without inferring homotopy from finite-field calculations. Signed fixed-face Lefschetz sums and exact Fourier checks establish the translation character.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The closest primary full texts establish general dominance-complex duality and examples for cycles, strips or chordal graphs (arXiv:2312.02639v1, arXiv:2009.04145v1, arXiv:1501.06754v1). The inspected exact indexed Shrikhande–rook comparisons concern ordinary independence complexes or geometric graph Čech nerves; other indexed calculations concern the open-neighborhood complex of the graph itself, rather than the complement required by Alexander duality. Thus their statements do not logically imply this dominance-complex homotopy, rook integral-homology profile, or translation character. Unindexed equivalent calculations remain a residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The pair is canonical: exactly two nonisomorphic strongly regular graphs have parameters \((16,6,2,2)\). Dominance complexes encode the topology of the forbidden closed-neighborhood hypergraph and connect, by Alexander duality, to neighborhood complexes and graph coloring. The full Shrikhande homotopy type, contrasting rook integral homology, and natural translation character give a substantive new instance of topology beyond strongly regular parameters. The result is not claimed to be the first distinction of the graphs by any topological invariant.

## Boundaries
The rook homotopy attaching maps in dimension eleven are not computed. The group character is restricted to translations, and the classification is not asserted for other graphs or parameters. Publication-index searches do not prove absolute novelty.
