The dominance complex of the Shrikhande graph is exactly \(\bigvee^{11}S^9\), whereas the same complex for the \(4\times4\) rook graph has integral reduced homology in two degrees, \(\widetilde H_9\cong\mathbb Z\) and \(\widetilde H_{11}\cong\mathbb Z^2\). This separates the strongly regular twins with parameters \((16,6,2,2)\), and the natural Shrikhande translations act on ninth rational homology with an explicitly determined character.
