For the finite-field two-step nilpotent Lie algebra of any simple graph \(G\), the complete embedded maximal-abelian subalgebra census is \(\sum_{S\text{ dominating}}(q-1)^{|S|-c(G[S])}t^{c(G[S])}\), where the exponent of \(t\) records dimension beyond the central edge space. This yields a complete explicit classification and refines graph independence data, with different polynomials even for graphs sharing the same independence polynomial.
