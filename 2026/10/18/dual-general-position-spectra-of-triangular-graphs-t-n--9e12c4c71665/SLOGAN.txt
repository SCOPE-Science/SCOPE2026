Every nonempty dual general-position set of the triangular graph \(T_n=L(K_n)\), for \(n\geq5\), is exactly a full star: its complete size polynomial is \(1+nz^{n-1}\). Thus these highly symmetric graphs admit no dual general-position set of any cardinality \(1,\ldots,n-2\), despite having \(n\) maximum solutions.
