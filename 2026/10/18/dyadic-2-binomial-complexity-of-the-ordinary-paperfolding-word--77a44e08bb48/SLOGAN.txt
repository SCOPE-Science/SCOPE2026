For the ordinary paperfolding word, the 2-binomial complexity at length \(2^k\) is exactly \(2^{k+1}+k+1\) for every \(k\ge2\). Its three possible weight groups split into \(2^k\), \(k+1\), and \(2^k\) scattered-subsequence classes, even though ordinary abelian complexity is only three at these lengths.
