# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** Exponentiation by an odd \(k\) permutes units modulo \(2^n\), so the odd/odd and odd/even contributions can be counted exactly. The even/even contribution is a precise scaling bijection, with \(2^{2k-2}\) lifts at moduli beyond \(2^k\). The resulting recurrence stabilizes for each nonzero two-adic target and yields a geometric distribution by target valuation. Valuation class masses prove the sharp moment threshold, and a separate Haar-moment identity gives the cubic variance. Exact finite checks independently matched \(4{,}088\) residue counts and \(180\) stabilized local densities. The infinite result does not depend on finite enumeration.

## Originality

**PASS under inspected evidence, with residual risks.** Arias, Borja and Anaya (2023, Article 23.8.1, Sections 2 and 4) count solvable residue classes rather than the number of ordered representations of *each* class; the latter is not determined by image cardinality. Broughan (2003/2004, Article 03.4.6, Sections 3 and 4) classifies modular cube representability rather than local fiber multiplicities or their moment thresholds. A comparison to modular Waring-exception semigroup theory and finite-field fifth-power graph sum spectra reveals different mathematical objects and does not imply this characteristic-two result. General local zeta methods and unindexed studies remain possible sources of overlap. Search non-detection alone is not treated as novelty proof; novelty rests on the difference in mathematical statements and their implications.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The motivating literature investigates which target classes are representable; in this family *all* two-adic classes are representable, so the size of the support loses information. Exact fiber counts restore a natural quantitative invariant, and their normalized distribution displays a mathematically sharp, nontrivial variance boundary between the third and fifth powers. The moment cutoff \(k/(k-2)\) quantitatively detects singular concentration near zero and provides a reusable local statistic for comparing higher-power modular representation problems. The scope is a complete infinite family, not an arbitrary parameter slice.

## Limitations and risks

The result does not count global integer solutions or extend to even powers or odd primes. The moment law is a local two-adic claim, with the density divergent at the Haar-null target zero. No independent review has occurred. Closely related but inaccessible or unindexed local-zeta literature could change the originality assessment; the inspected primary sources do not already state or imply the explicit classification.
