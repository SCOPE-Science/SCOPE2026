For every odd exponent \(k\ge3\), the ordered representations of residues by \(x^k+y^k\) modulo powers of two admit an exact valuation-dependent count; their limiting two-adic density has finite \(q\)-moment precisely when \(q<k/(k-2)\). The cubic density has variance \(3/4\), whereas every odd exponent at least five has infinite variance, even though every residue is representable.
