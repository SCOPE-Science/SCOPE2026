Every generalized binary paperfolding word has exactly \(3\cdot2^{k-3}\) or \(3\cdot2^{k-3}-1\) universal scattered-subsequence arches in its length-\(2^k\) prefix, for all \(k\ge3\). The single possible defect occurs precisely when its first two folding instructions agree but differ from the instruction at depth \(k\), so the normalized universality index always tends to \(3/8\).
