For the binary Thue–Morse prefix of length \(2^m\), the number of shortest absent subsequences is exactly \(s_{m-1}+t_{m-1}\), where \((s_0,t_0)=(1,2)\) and \((s_{r+1},t_{r+1})=(s_rt_r,s_r^2+2)\). Its base-two logarithm grows as \(0.649537093097466\ldots\times2^{m-1}+O(1)\), giving an explicit subsequence-absence entropy for the canonical aperiodic word.
