For every smooth complex plane cubic \(E\), the split cubic threefold \(A_E(x,y,z)+u^3+v^3=0\) has exactly **12 Eckardt points**, except at the equianharmonic modulus \(j(E)=0\), where the count jumps to the sharp maximum **30**. The proof classifies all points by the ranks of the ternary Hessian and identifies the nine flex lifts, three tail points, and the eighteen exceptional points.
