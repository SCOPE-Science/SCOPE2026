The Shrikhande graph and the \(4\times4\) rook graph share strongly regular parameters \((16,6,2,2)\), but their edge-metric dimensions are respectively \(6\) and \(5\), attained by exactly \(736\) and \(576\) minimum bases. Their one-landmark-exchange graphs are both connected, yet the Shrikhande bases split into seven symmetry orbits with nonconstant exchange degree, while all rook bases form one orbit with degree \(13\).
