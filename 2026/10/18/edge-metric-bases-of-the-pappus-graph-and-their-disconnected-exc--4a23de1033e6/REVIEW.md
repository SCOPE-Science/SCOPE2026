# Scientific scope of the reconfiguration result

The edge metric dimension, census and geometric basis classification are established in the earlier Resultary record cited in `RESULT.md`. They are inputs, not originality claims. Pure-sector connectivity and the obstruction to slides follow directly from the basis types. The separate finite reconfiguration result gives exact jump connectivity and distances in the mixed sector: all 486 states are mutually reachable, and every state has eccentricity five. The complete state and transition lists make both the exact edge count and the distance claim reproducible. The result is confined to this graph, these minimum bases and the moves specified in `RESULT.md`; no general biaffine theorem or definitive publication priority is claimed.
