For each odd prime \(p\), the Legendre-symbol weighted binomial transform \(\sum_{k=0}^n (\frac{k}{p})\binom{n}{k}\) eventually follows the sign of \(\cos(n\pi/p)\) or \(\sin(n\pi/p)\), according to \(p\bmod4\), after an explicit cutoff. Its positive, negative and zero frequencies have exact natural densities, and for \(p\equiv3\pmod4\) the only eventual zeros are at multiples of \(p\).
