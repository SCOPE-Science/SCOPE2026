Focused indexed searches for the A261271 record pair correlations and the six-shift invariant found no equivalent, while the OEIS sequence entry gives initial values rather than these pair statistics. Risk remains from unindexed or differently formulated arithmetic-correlation literature.

The method gives effective limits for every fixed shift, not a one-off finite calculation.
