The toric fourfold of the graphical zonotope of \(K_5\) with two disjoint edges removed has a reduced singular locus consisting of eight pairwise disjoint projective lines and four isolated points. Its transverse singularities are conifolds along the lines and rank-one \(2\times3\) Segre cones at the isolated points, with respective multiplicities \(2\) and \(3\).
