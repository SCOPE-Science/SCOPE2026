For the ordinary matching complexes of the eight circular ladders \(C_n\square K_2\) with \(3\le n\le10\), exact acyclic matchings and integral Morse differentials give complete wedges of spheres; in particular, \(\mathcal M(C_5\square K_2)\simeq\bigvee^2S^2\vee\bigvee^2S^3\) and \(\mathcal M(C_8\square K_2)\simeq\bigvee^3S^4\vee\bigvee^7S^5\). The statement is limited to these eight graph sizes.
