For every pair of habitat trees with the same degree multiset and at most seven vertices, the expected size of the largest surviving component has a fixed ranking under independent equal-probability patch survival. Eight vertices are sharp: the degree-matched spiders with arm lengths \((3,3,1)\) and \((4,2,1)\) reverse their ranking at survival probability \(p=1/2\), with exact difference \(p^5(1-p)^2(2p-1)\).
