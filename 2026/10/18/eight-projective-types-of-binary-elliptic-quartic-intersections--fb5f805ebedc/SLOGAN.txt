Among all \(174251\) pencils of quadrics in \(\mathbf P^3(\mathbf F_2)\), exactly \(40320\) cut out geometrically smooth elliptic quartics, in eight projective orbits with completely determined point-count and quadric-type spectra. Two inequivalent four-point embeddings have the same quadric profile but are separated by rational fourth-order osculation versus coplanarity.
