The independence complex of the Dürer graph collapses onto **each** of its 18 induced octahedral two-spheres, using 72 elementary collapses for each core; those cores form dihedral orbits of sizes 6 and 12. The pure three-dimensional complex has homotopy type \(S^2\), and its full dihedral action on integral second homology sends both a generating rotation and a generating reflection to \(-1\).
