Eleven species are the first possible size of a connected plant–pollinator network pair in which every pollinator visits exactly three plants and all individual and pairwise plant coextinction probabilities agree, yet the total number of extinct plants has different skewness. An explicit six-plant, five-pollinator pair has identical mean and variance for every failure probability \(q\in(0,1)\) but a strictly positive third-central-moment difference \(6q^3(1-q)^2\).
