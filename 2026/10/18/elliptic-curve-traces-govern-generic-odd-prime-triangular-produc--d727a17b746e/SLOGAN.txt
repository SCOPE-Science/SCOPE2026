On the smooth residue locus \(p\nmid n(1+64n)\), the exact odd-prime probability that \(T(a)T(b)+n\) is a \(p\)-adic square is determined by two quadratic characters and the Frobenius trace of the elliptic quartic \(v^2=(u^2-1)(u^2-1-64n)\). The resulting uniform bias is explicit to order \(1/p\), with an \(O(p^{-3/2})\) remainder.
