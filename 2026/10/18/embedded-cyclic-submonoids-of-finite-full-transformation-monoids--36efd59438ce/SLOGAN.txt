Distinct embedded cyclic submonoids of \(T_n\), rather than merely their isomorphism types, have an exact all-degree count and a fully refined height–period enumerator, with Euler-totient generator multiplicity only at functional-digraph heights zero and one. The number of transformations possessing another generator for the same submonoid is asymptotic to \(n!\rho^{-n}/(1+\rho)\), where \(\rho e^\rho=1\), so a random transformation has a unique submonoid generator with exponentially high probability.
