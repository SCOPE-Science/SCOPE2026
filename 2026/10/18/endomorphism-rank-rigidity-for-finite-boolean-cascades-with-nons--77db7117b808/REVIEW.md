# Scientific review — Endomorphism-rank rigidity for finite Boolean cascades

**Correctness: PASS.** The classification reduces Heyting endomorphisms to the finite Esakia bounded-morphism condition and establishes a one-target-level lemma plus a no-repeated-higher-level lemma. These imply the exact two-form decomposition. Surjection, upset-count and idempotent formulas then follow without probabilistic or infinite extrapolation. The packaged standalone verifier exhausted 2,523,822 raw candidates across nine finite parameter vectors and directly checked ranks and implication where feasible. The proof covers arbitrary level number, whereas finite checks do not alone establish the general case.

**Originality: PASS, with residual priority risk.** Bezhanishvili et al. (2021, arXiv:2103.02530) established the duality and classical Boolean-cascade setting; neither is claimed as new. The closest retrieved mathematical records count all continuous weak-order maps, ordinary poset retraction images, or homotopy classes, not specifically implication-preserving bounded-morphism endomorphisms with rank multiplicities. The complete 1985 Adams–Koubek–Sichler endomorphism article was unavailable; its accessible abstract concerns general near-rigidity and does not settle the explicit census, so it remains a material priority risk. An earlier embedded-subalgebra count for two-level Heyting frames likewise does not cover the arbitrary-height endomorphism spectrum. Relevant full-text access limits and possible older equivalent work remain explicitly disclosed; absence from search results is not a priority certificate.

**Mathematical value: PASS.** The classification identifies all possible reductions of a classical finite algebraic-logical family and proves rigidity outside the bottom level. The exact image-rank and idempotent profiles are natural invariants of its endomorphism monoid and provide more than a one-off census.

**Closest literature and limitations.** The 2021 arXiv full-text sections on Esakia duality and Boolean cascades were inspected, and the 2023 frame-theoretic duality repository record was read. The latter's body and several relevant indexed record full texts were not accessible; these remain residual priority risks. The theorem requires at least two points in every antichain level and does not extend automatically to singleton levels or arbitrary posets. No independent mathematical review has occurred.
