On crisp Gödel-modal stars with one varying atomic truth degree, the root sees only the infimum and supremum of the leaf degrees: when they are distinct interior values, every formula belongs to one of exactly twelve complete semantic signatures. Attaining those extrema determines whether the model is fully witnessed, yet adjoining both missing endpoint leaves changes neither the root's truth degrees nor the complete global theory or even the full local consequence relation, so witnessedness itself is not definable by modal formulas at the fixed-model level.
