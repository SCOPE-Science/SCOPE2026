For the stationary three-dimensional Bessel process contracted by a factor \(c\) at Poisson rate \(c\), the probability of radius below \(r\) is \(K(c)r^3+o(r^3)\). Its exact small-ball coefficient has two distinct sharp endpoint laws: \(K(c)\sim(2\sqrt2/3)\sqrt c\) as \(c\downarrow0\), but \(K(c)\sim(4/(3\sqrt\pi))(1-c)^{3/2}\) as \(c\uparrow1\), forcing a positive interior optimum.
