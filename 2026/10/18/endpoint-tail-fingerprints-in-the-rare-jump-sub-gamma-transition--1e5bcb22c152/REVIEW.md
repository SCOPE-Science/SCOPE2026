# Scientific assessment: endpoint-tail sub-gamma asymptotics

## Correctness — PASS

The exact centered cumulant formula, variance normalization and variational inequality give \(c/b=\sup f_\delta\) without an unproved transform. The endpoint Laplace lemma follows from an integration-by-parts Tauberian computation under a fixed regularly varying endpoint tail; exponential tilting also proves the required first-derivative ratio. A global trial-point localization, not a local saddle assumption, places every maximizer at \(z/L\to1\). The interior derivative equation then forces \(q/z\to1\), giving both the \((\beta+3)\log L\) and additive \(1-\log(2K)\) terms. The standalone checker passes 90 deterministic assertions for three beta families. Numerical agreement is not a replacement for proof.
