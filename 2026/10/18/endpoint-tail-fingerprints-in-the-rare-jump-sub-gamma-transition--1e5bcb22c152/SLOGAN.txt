For a fixed Gaussian variance and rare bounded positive compound-Poisson jumps whose upper-endpoint probability scales as \(Ch^\beta\), the sharp variance-exact right sub-gamma scale obeys \(b/c=L+(\beta+3)\log L+1-\log(2C\Gamma(\beta+1)b^\beta)+o(1)\), where \(L=\log(a/(\lambda b^2))\). The endpoint exponent is visible in the secondary logarithmic correction, distinguishing continuous-endpoint shocks from atomic jumps.
