# Scientific review: induced triangle and open two-path covariances

## Correctness — PASS
Exact overlap classes distinguish triangles sharing zero, one, or three edges and potential wedges sharing zero, one, or two edges. Their edge-presence probabilities yield both covariance expressions directly; \(P=W-3T\) then gives the theorem. Rational algebra yields the factored fixed-edge identity; the sign follows from positivity of its remaining polynomial on the precise admissible range. The binomial-edge threshold follows by elementary factor sign, and the dense limit by dominant-power analysis. Exact enumeration independently checks all simple graphs on four through six vertices, totaling 33,856, with additional exact-rational identity checks to size 60 (775 checks). Nonstandard external lemmas are not required. Exhaustive small cases alone are not an infinite proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Triangles and induced two-paths are fundamental entries of the three-vertex triad census, so their joint fluctuations matter when interpreting motif enrichment. The theorem proves a sharp sign reversal under independent edges and a universal negative sign under fixed edge count, with distinct leading fluctuation orders under dense scaling. These are structurally different probabilistic null-model predictions, not merely re-expressions of a marginal motif expectation. The exact fixed-edge sign bound remains meaningful across the entire finite parameter domain.
