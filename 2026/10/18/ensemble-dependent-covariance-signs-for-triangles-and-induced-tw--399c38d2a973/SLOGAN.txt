Triangles and induced open two-paths can have positive or negative covariance in \(G(n,p)\), with a sharp threshold \(p=2(n-3)/(3n-8)\). Conditioning on the edge count changes the picture completely: their covariance in \(G(n,m)\) is strictly negative whenever \(3\le m\le\binom n2-2\), and zero otherwise.
