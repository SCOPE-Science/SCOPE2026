# Mathematical assessment

## Correctness — PASS
Independent block sign symmetries force the quadratic forms of unique canonical ellipsoids to split orthogonally. Exact gauge maximizations over the Euclidean sum reduce John containment and Löwner containment to the two dual Hölder constraints. The uniqueness and equality calculations establish every dimension-dependent block scale, all contact families, and the entropy factor. The endpoints and the exponent \(p=2\) are handled explicitly. The standalone checker passed 36,000 sampled scalar feasibility constraints and exact normalization identities; finite tests are not used for the universal proof.

## Originality — PASS, with risks
The primary 2022 Ivanov–Naszódi source supplies general functional John/Löwner contact conditions, not explicit canonical ellipsoid and contact formulas for \(\ell_p\) sums. The 2026 Grundbacher–Kobos source gives an exact Banach–Mazur *distance* formula for these sums, together with Hölder estimates; that objective neither equals volume extremization nor determines both canonical ellipsoids. Older fixed-ellipsoid characterizations and \(L_p\)-John literature were considered as potentially adjacent, but no checked statement entailed the dimension-entropy law and the full opposite contact classifications. Broad classical literature leaves a residual risk of an unlocated equivalent result.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The classification is universal over all symmetric convex factors, not just Euclidean balls or polygons. It supplies a complete two-sided contact-set phase change at \(p=2\) and a strict dimension-entropy law for the canonical-ellipsoid volume ratio, allowing exact geometric comparisons despite arbitrary factor shapes. The result is a structural family theorem, not a standalone routine numerical evaluation.
