For the \(\ell_p\)-direct sum of any origin-symmetric convex bodies, both canonical John and Löwner ellipsoids have explicit dimension-weighted block formulas, and their volume ratio is multiplied by \(\exp(NH(w)|1/p-1/2|)\). Their complete boundary-contact families switch in opposite directions at \(p=2\): single-block contacts on one side and all-block contacts on the other.
