# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The layer classification follows from independence and domination, and the exhaustive piece decomposition handles empty endpoints, full fibers and one- versus two-empty separators. The identity for \(3w-2n\) gives a strict nonoptimality bound for every full-fiber configuration except the directly resolved small case. The remaining minimizers have an explicit weak-composition and proper-run-color bijection. The two-color case is the disjoint union of two paths, with independently countable gap choices. The included verifier independently confirmed all sets up to the claimed optimum in 27 small cases and checked a finite-state model on 553 parameter pairs.

**Originality — PASS under accessible evidence, with bibliographic risk.** The 2022 foundational paper proves minimum sizes and gives a general layer-label characterization but does not state the all-minimizer enumeration, full-fiber rigidity, or the unique exceptional addition. The new result is not the published numerical invariant restated: it gives its extremal coefficient and an exact structural bijection. Semantic mathematical-record searches under tensor/direct/categorical-product and coefficient/count aliases found no covering claim. The earlier well-covered-product paper and a possibly relevant dissertation have limited full-text access, which remains a risk rather than proof of absence.

**Value — PASS.** Minimum independent domination was specifically investigated in this graph-product family. Knowing the optimum size does not quantify the exponentially many labeled realizations, the color-dependence, or the single exceptional full-fiber optimum. A complete structural classification across all parameters supplies that missing information and distinguishes the degenerate two-color product without narrowing to arbitrary isolated instances.

**Closest literature and limitations.** Kuenzel–Rall (2022), Proposition 6 and its layer conditions, are the closest mathematically related work; they determine \(i\) but neither the count nor all minimum-set shapes. This work concerns labeled minimum sets only; it is not a full independent domination polynomial. Unindexed equivalent literature and unread primary sources remain possible. The experimental checks are finite and do not replace the infinite proof.
