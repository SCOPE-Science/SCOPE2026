Deterministically pooling consecutive integer outcomes into equal-width blocks preserves ultra-log-concavity: if \(X\) is ULC, then \(\lfloor X/m\rfloor\) is ULC for every positive integer \(m\), with strict inequalities at interior blocks when \(m\ge2\). In particular, counts in Erlang-renewal processes with integer interarrival shape at least two are strictly ultra-log-concave and therefore strictly underdispersed.
