In every Steiner \(2\)-design incidence graph, the unit electrical current across any point–block edge is exactly a mixture of the direct edge and **equal-weight five-edge shortest bypasses**, even without edge transitivity. Consequently the canonical real cut and cycle projections have sharp endpoint norms \(5-4\rho\) and \(6(1-\rho)\), where \(\rho=(k-1)/v+1/k\), uniformly over all existing \(2\)-\((v,k,1)\) designs.
