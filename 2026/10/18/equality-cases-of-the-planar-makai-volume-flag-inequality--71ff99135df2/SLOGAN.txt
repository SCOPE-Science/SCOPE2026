For a convex \(n\)-gon, the Makai volume \(\operatorname{area}(P)\operatorname{area}((P-P)^\circ)\) equals the sharp flag bound \(n/2\) exactly for triangles and parallelograms. Every polygon with at least five vertices lies uniformly below that bound by at least \(n/2-\pi^2/4\), resolving the equality question in the planar case.
