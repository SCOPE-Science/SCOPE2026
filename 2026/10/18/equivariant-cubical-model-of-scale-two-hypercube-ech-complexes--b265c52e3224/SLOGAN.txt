For every \(n\ge1\), the intrinsic scale-two Čech complex of the \(n\)-cube is equivariantly homotopy equivalent, under its full affine symmetry group, to the cubical two-skeleton of the \((n+1)\)-cube. For \(n\ge3\), the geometric fixed space of a coordinate three-cycle is two disjoint copies of the two-skeleton of the \((n-2)\)-cube; in particular for \(n=5\), it is \(S^2\sqcup S^2\).
