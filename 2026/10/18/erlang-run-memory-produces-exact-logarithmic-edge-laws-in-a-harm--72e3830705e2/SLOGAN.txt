For a run-and-tumble particle in a harmonic trap with Erlang-\(m\) run lengths, the stationary density at the confinement edge has the exact form \(A_m(r)y^{r-1}(\log(1/y))^{m-1}\), with a finite positive coefficient given by a regenerative perpetuity. The critical rate \(r=1\) is finite at the boundary for exponential runs, but develops a logarithmic divergence of degree \(m-1\) for every non-Markovian integer shape \(m\ge2\).
