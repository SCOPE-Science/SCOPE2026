For cycle-square graphs \(C_n^2\), error-detecting open-locating sets are exactly the complements of independent sets avoiding cyclic differences \(1,2,5,6,7\), once \(n\ge12\). This yields a complete \(128\)-state enumerator, a sharp four-periodic minimum, and an exact census of all minimum sensor placements, including exceptional counts at \(n=22,23\).
