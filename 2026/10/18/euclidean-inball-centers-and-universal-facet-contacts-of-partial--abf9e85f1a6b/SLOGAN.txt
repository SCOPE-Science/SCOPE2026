For every rectangular partial alternating-sign-matrix polytope, the Frobenius inradius depends only on the longer side: \(r=1/(L+\sqrt L)\). Its entire incenter locus has affine dimension \(mn-L\) off the square diagonal and \((n-1)^2\) for an \(n\)-by-\(n\) matrix, with exactly classified universally tangent facets.
