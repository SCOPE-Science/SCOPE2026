For any finite \(\ell_1\)-metric Cartesian product, the Euclidean inball of its real Lipschitz-free unit ball has precisely the product of the factors' maximal-variance supporting facets, and the full incenter locus has an exact additive normal-rank formula. For rectangular path grids with \(N\) points and side lengths \(n_i\), the inradius is \(\sqrt{12/(N\sum_i(n_i^2-1))}\), exactly \(2^s\) facets touch, and the optimal-center locus has affine dimension \(N-s-1\).
