For the Euclidean vertices of the truncated octahedron, the Vietoris–Rips complex changes from a wedge of thirteen circles to seven circles, then to nine two-spheres, before stabilizing as a single \(S^2\) already at radius \(\sqrt8\). An explicit integral discrete-Morse certificate and a common radial-projection generator show that this sphere survives unchanged through all radii below \(\sqrt{14}\), splitting a previously studied graph-metric stage into three distinct Euclidean stages.
