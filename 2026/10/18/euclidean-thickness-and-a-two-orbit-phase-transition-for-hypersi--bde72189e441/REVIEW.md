**Correctness — PASS.** The formula follows from an exact description of the difference body, not an approximation. For a zero-sum vector, the coordinate bounds and the two total positive/negative mass bounds are both necessary and sufficient for representation as a difference of two hypersimplex points. Centered Euclidean ball inclusion then has exactly two sharp norm obstructions. Orthogonal projection proves the coordinate obstruction with equality classification; two-block Cauchy–Schwarz proves the signed-sum obstruction with equality classification. The squared-threshold Diophantine equation yields all and only the stated ties, including the even-parameter exclusion. The proof covers every integer rank and ambient dimension in its scope.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The uniform-matroid base polytope is a canonical, widely used family generalizing the regular simplex. Its exact minimum Euclidean width and the complete minimizing normal set are natural geometric invariants. The discovery of a second active constraint family, its sharp arithmetic switch at \(n=4m^2-1\), and the exact difference-body model explain a qualitative geometric change as rank and dimension vary. This is a complete all-dimensional classification, not an arbitrary fixed-parameter table or a restatement of the simplex case.

Bibliographic comparison is not exhaustive and independent mathematical validation is still outstanding.
