The intrinsic Euclidean minimum width of every hypersimplex has a two-branch exact formula, and its difference body is the intersection of a zero-sum hyperplane with a cube and a cross-polytope. Its minimizing normals form precisely coordinate or balanced two-level orbits, with a sharp arithmetic tie exactly when \(n=4m^2-1\), where \(m\) is the smaller of the rank and corank.
