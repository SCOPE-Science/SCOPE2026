# Scientific review of the even-semiprime nodal classification

## Correctness — PASS

The projective Jacobian equations are equivalent to root-of-unity ratios. In the field of \(p\)-th roots, the vanishing-sum kernel consists of constant coefficient vectors, giving a unique integer \(k\) and nonnegative multiplicities. The displayed multinomial coefficient formula counts ordered lists, and the free global scaling action has size \(2p\). The affine Hessian determinant is explicitly nonzero at every listed singularity; hence the Jacobian singular scheme is finite reduced and its residue fields are determined by the Galois stabilizers. Every nonidentity Galois element has exactly the antipodal projective configurations as fixed points. Burnside's lemma gives the orbit refinement. The separately coded exact exhaustive checks validate six finite cases, not the infinite theorem.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Lam–Leung already classify two-prime root cancellations, and Lee counts related cyclotomic critical points in a different geometric setting. Those facts are credited, not treated as new. The earlier prime-exponent diagonal-section classification has no antipodal rational-node stratum and does not imply the composite even-semiprime Galois splitting. The proposed novel result is the full finite etale singular scheme with both residue degrees, node generating function and complete coordinate-permutation orbit enumeration. Searches did not locate an equal or stronger geometric classification. An unstated corollary or differently titled treatment of this symmetric Fermat section remains a genuine risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The first two-prime cyclotomic orders introduce two geometrically meaningful cancellation mechanisms—opposite pairs and prime polygons—whose interaction yields rational singularities absent from prime-order cases. The proof gives for every prime and projective dimension the exact arithmetic singular scheme, nontrivial residue-degree phase, smoothness threshold, and full permutation-orbit count. The result is a natural infinite-family classification, not an isolated numerical test or a renaming of the root-weight semigroup.

## Scope and limitations

Characteristic zero only. The reduced special fibers in positive characteristic are not classified. General principles underlying the proof (cyclotomic kernels, Jacobian and Hessian methods) are classical; novelty is claimed only for their stated combined geometric/arithmetic consequences. External equivalences that escaped the searches are possible.
