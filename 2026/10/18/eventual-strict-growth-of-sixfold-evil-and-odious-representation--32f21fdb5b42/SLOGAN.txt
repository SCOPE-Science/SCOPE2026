For ordered six-term sums of evil or of odious nonnegative integers, each representation count has successive difference \(\binom{n+4}{4}/64+O(n^{\log_2 15})\), hence is eventually strictly increasing. A three-dimensional exact recurrence controls the sixth moment of dyadic Thue–Morse polynomials and resolves the eventual-monotonicity part of a published conjecture, without determining the sharp cutoff.
