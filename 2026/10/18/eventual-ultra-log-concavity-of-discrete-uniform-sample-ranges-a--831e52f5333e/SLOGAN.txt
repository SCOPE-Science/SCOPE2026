The range of a fair die is not ultra-log-concave after two or three independent rolls, but is strictly ultra-log-concave after every larger sample. More generally, uniform finite-alphabet ranges become strictly ultra-log-concave for all \(n\ge m^2\) when the alphabet is \(\{0,\ldots,m\}\), even though a nondegenerate discrete-uniform parent is itself not ultra-log-concave.
