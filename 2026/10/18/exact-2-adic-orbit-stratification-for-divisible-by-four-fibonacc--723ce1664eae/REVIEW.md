# Scientific review

**Correctness: PASS.** The exact integral identity \(M^2-I=cM\), the fact that \(M\) is invertible, and two-adic repeated squaring prove that every normalized iterate difference is invertible modulo two. Independently, the Smith factors of \(M-I\) are \(1\) and \(|c|\). These ingredients give every fixed-state count and every least-period multiplicity, with both \(k\le h\) and \(k>h\) boundaries checked. The executable finite tests corroborate, but do not prove, the all-exponent result.

**Originality: PASS (best knowledge; residual risk).** The closest general recurrence theory is Brent's irreducible-mod-two period theorem. Here the characteristic polynomial reduces to \((X+1)^2\), so its irreducible hypothesis fails. The inspected generalized-Fibonacci sources discuss canonical periods, subsequences or particular group periods; none of the readable material establishes this full coefficient-valuation census. The 2009 Falcón–Plaza paper remains a material-read limitation and is not asserted to be excluded by its abstract alone. An unindexed equivalent result remains possible.

**Value: PASS.** The finding gives exact natural orbit invariants at every modulus level for an entire infinite coefficient family, identifies a sharp threshold between only one- and two-cycles and unbounded longer dyadic cycles, and characterizes the dependence on coefficient valuation alone. It is a structural classification of a repeated-root regime, not a selected numerical example.

**Scientific limits.** Coefficients with \(\nu_2(c)<2\), the zero coefficient, and non-dyadic moduli are not classified. No independent verification has been performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
