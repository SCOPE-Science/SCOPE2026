For the modular Fibonacci state map \(T(a,b)=(b,a+cb)\) with nonzero \(4\mid c\), the two-adic valuation \(h=\nu_2(c)\) completely controls every state-cycle length and multiplicity modulo \(2^k\). All orbits have powers-of-two length, and new lengths beyond two begin precisely when \(k>h\), with an explicit geometric census.
