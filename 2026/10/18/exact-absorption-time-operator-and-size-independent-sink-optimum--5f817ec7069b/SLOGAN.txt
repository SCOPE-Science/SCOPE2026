For a finite coherent path with an irreversible Lindblad sink at one endpoint, the entire time-integrated survival operator has an exact closed kernel and a particle launched at site \(j\) has mean absorption time \((n-j)((j+1)/\gamma+\gamma j/(4J^2))\). The unique best sink strength for every nonabsorber starting site is \(2J\sqrt{(j+1)/j}\), independent of the total chain length.
