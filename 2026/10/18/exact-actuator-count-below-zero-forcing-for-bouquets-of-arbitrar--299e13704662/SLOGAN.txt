Every bouquet of \(k\ge2\) unweighted cycles, with arbitrary possibly unequal lengths, requires exactly \(k\) independent vertex actuators for full Laplacian controllability: one vertex adjacent to the common hub in each cycle is optimal. Its ordinary zero-forcing number is \(k+1\), demonstrating an exact one-actuator gap between fixed-Laplacian controllability and the general zero-forcing certificate.
