For a two-deme Moran flow with symmetric cosine-squared migration, the exact long-season mutant fraction is \(1/[2\sqrt{1-q}]\) for all sufficiently small positive amplitudes \(q\), strictly above the constant-migration value \(1/[2(1-q/2)]\) at the same mean migration rate. The resulting positive seasonal enhancement is \(q^2/16+O(q^3)\), although the slow-season value stays below the constant peak-amplitude value.
