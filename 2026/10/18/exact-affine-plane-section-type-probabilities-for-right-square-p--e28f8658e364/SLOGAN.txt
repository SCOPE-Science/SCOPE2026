For every right square pyramid, the complete triangle/quadrilateral/pentagon distribution of a Euclidean-motion-invariant random affine section has an exact elementary formula in the height-to-base aspect ratio. The pentagon probability is positive at every finite height, vanishes at both extremes, and has the sharp tall-pyramid asymptotic \(2(2-\sqrt2)/h\) when the base is \([-1,1]^2\).
