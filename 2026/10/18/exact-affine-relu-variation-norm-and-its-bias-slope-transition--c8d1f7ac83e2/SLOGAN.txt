On the whole real Hilbert input space, the Euclidean-parameter ReLU variation norm of an affine functional exhibits a sharp bias–slope transition: it equals \(2\sqrt{\|a\|^2+b^2}\) for \(|b|\le\|a\|/\sqrt3\), and \(|b|+\sqrt3\|a\|\) above that threshold. The optimal representing measure simultaneously changes from two opposite ReLU atoms to three atoms, the third being a constant feature.
