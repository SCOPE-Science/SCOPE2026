# Scientific review

## Correctness: PASS

The facet weights for the equilateral triangle are computed exactly. The essential all-angle lemma follows from the nonnegative-coefficient power-sum recurrence for three equally spaced normals and the strict positive sixth-order term. This yields the only six inner and six outer contacts for every positive level. The averaging argument uses the convex variational formula for matrix inversion and certifies full affine Banach–Mazur optimality, not merely round concentric bounds. Explicit Taylor expansions give the quadratic small-level constant and large-level limit. The code performs supplemental exact recurrence and finite numerical checks. The smooth approximation statement uses weak continuity of surface-area measures; it does not assume the smooth-body covariogram theorem holds directly for polygons.
