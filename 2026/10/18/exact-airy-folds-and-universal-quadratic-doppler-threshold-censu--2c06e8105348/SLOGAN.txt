A quartic phase turns the Gaussian's zero-free ambiguity function into countably many nonintersecting Airy nodal curves, each with exactly one nondegenerate Doppler minimum. Despite chirp-dependent curve locations, the number of fold thresholds below frequency \(R\) is asymptotic to \((\sqrt3/4)R^2\), with a coefficient independent of every fixed nonzero chirp strength.
