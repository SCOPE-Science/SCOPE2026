# Review of exact all-degree interaction-band spectra on multiplicative Hamming varieties

## Correctness — PASS

The normalized restricted-character Gram entry is a finite cyclic character average. It vanishes unless the index difference is constant in every coordinate; in that case its modulus is one. Thus every diagonal-shift orbit becomes a rank-one all-ones block after harmless phase correction. Exactly those shifts that turn at least \(d-k\) coordinates into zeros survive in the band. The finite free action by \(q-1\) shifts gives the stated multinomial eigenvalue counts. The two threshold inequalities follow from support coverings and sharp pigeonhole occupancies. The cases \(k=0,d\), arbitrary product level \(j\), and even characteristic are covered. Verification independently checks 124 combinatorial cases and 56 direct complex Gram matrices.

## Originality — PASS, best knowledge

Cheong–Koh–Pham (arXiv:1903.03904, 2019) study the same product hypersurfaces, proving extension estimates and additive Fourier-decay statements; their inspected arguments do not give an all-degree normalized multiplicative-character Gram spectrum or its exact sharp degree thresholds. A full published mathematical statement titled *An all-degree spectral law for Fourier sampling on q-ary Hamming spheres* instead concerns fixed-weight shells, not product constraints. Standard subgroup-character orthogonality explains the rank-one block mechanism, but does not itself state the counted all-degree eigenvalue census and dual exact thresholds. Equivalence under a quotient-character or code-theoretic formulation remains the primary bibliographic risk. The integer eigenvalues must not be transplanted to an unpreconditioned additive-frame Gram matrix.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result gives a complete infinite-parameter sampling classification: every eigenvalue and its multiplicity, the exact first degree at which the band ceases to be independent, and the distinct exact degree at which it spans all functions. It identifies an orthonormal critical band for every odd-dimensional ternary Hamming product variety. This is a natural exact reconstruction invariant motivated by the source's finite-field restriction problem, not a re-evaluation of a known numerical table. No quantitative noisy-reconstruction bound or unrestricted extension norm is claimed.
