On the multiplicative Hamming variety \(\prod_i x_i=j\) over any finite field, every interaction-band Fourier Gram eigenvalue is an integer whose exact multiplicity follows an occupancy formula. Restriction first loses injectivity at degree \(\lceil d/2\rceil\) and becomes surjective at degree \(d-\lceil d/(q-1)\rceil\); for \(q=3\) and odd \(d\), the intervening half-degree band is exactly an orthonormal basis.
