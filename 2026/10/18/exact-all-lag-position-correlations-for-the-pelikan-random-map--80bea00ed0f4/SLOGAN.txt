For the Pelikan random doubling/halving map, every stationary physical-position autocorrelation \(\mathbb E[X_0X_n]\) with \(1/2<p<1\) has an exact finite binomial formula at every lag \(n\ge0\). A reflected binary-stack count retains the noncommuting map order, and explicit dyadic fractional-position moments complete the calculation; the critical parameter \(p=1/2\) is excluded.
