Every three-site correlation of the stationary regular-paperfolding sequence has a closed dyadic formula depending only on two separation valuations and one odd residue modulo four. Its pair correlations are time-symmetric, but the three-site laws are not: the \((+1,+1,+1)\) cylinder has probability \(3/16\) at times \((0,1,4)\) and \(1/8\) at times \((0,3,4)\).
