For an anchored \(q\)-ary two-symbol read channel losing exactly one internal read, every confusability component is exactly a unit-transfer graph on the run lengths of one fixed reduced symbol itinerary. This gives exact component sizes and edge counts, an adapter-independent total edge law for \(n\ge3\), and a complete inverse-readout list-size distribution for every \(q\ge2\) and \(n\ge1\).
