# Review: fastest continuous-time mixing on two-price even cycles

**Correctness — PASS.** The all-parameter proof constructs a centered two-dimensional polygon with alternating squared side lengths exactly equal to the edge prices. Its Rayleigh trace certifies a universal upper bound for every independent, nonnegative conductance assignment. The proposed alternating rates are positive and budget-feasible, satisfy the planar eigenvector equations, and achieve the bound as the second Bloch eigenvalue. Equality forces the same planar eigenvectors for any optimizer, and the two local chord vectors are linearly independent; therefore all individual edge rates are uniquely determined. Four-cycle and equal-price degeneracies are explicitly covered. The separate verification script checked the symbolic identities and 842 finite assertions. Finite tests alone do not establish the theorem.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** Sun, Boyd, Xiao, and Diaconis (2006, DOI 10.1137/S0036144504443821), in the accessible full text, provide the continuous-time spectral-gap optimization and geometric dual and solve equal-cost cycles; their section 5.3 explicitly describes arbitrary-cost cycles as more complicated and gives a dimensionality result rather than this price-two closed form. Boyd, Diaconis, Parrilo, and Xiao (2007, arXiv:0709.0955) work with the distinct discrete-time SLEM criterion and display ordinary cycles, not this weighted-cost continuous-time classification. Focused literature and mathematical-finding searches revealed no theorem covering the explicit alternating-price rates, sharp value, and **global uniqueness**. Searches cannot prove absence from all literature, and hidden equivalences or unindexed work remain residual risks. The general variational/duality tools are prior work; they are not claimed as new.

**Value — PASS.** The exact design solves a natural alternating-tariff network optimization problem over all labelled edge variables, rather than simply computing eigenvalues of a prescribed periodic matrix. The four-cycle's price-insensitive equal-conductance design and the strict price response for all longer even cycles provide a structural finite-size boundary; the square-root large-cycle law quantitatively describes the change. The explicit globally unique allocation and objective avoid a generic numerical SDP for this entire infinite family. The result is limited to this motivated special class, not a universal formula for arbitrary tariffs.

**Scope/risks.** The objective is the continuous-time Laplacian spectral gap, not the discrete-time SLEM. Prices and budget are positive. Exact mathematical proof, not the numerical program, establishes infinite-scope correctness. Unindexed equivalent specializations remain an originality risk. Independent audit and independent validation have not been conducted.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
