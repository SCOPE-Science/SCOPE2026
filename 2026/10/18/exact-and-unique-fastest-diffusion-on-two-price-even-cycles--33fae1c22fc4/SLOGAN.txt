On an even cycle with alternating positive per-edge conductance prices, the fastest continuous-time symmetric mixing process has a unique globally optimal allocation, even when all individual edge weights are unrestricted independent variables. Its exact gap is \(2B\sin^2(\pi/m)/[m(c_A+c_B+2\sqrt{c_Ac_B}\cos(\pi/m))]\); four-cycle conductances remain equal at any price contrast, whereas large cycles approach a square-root price allocation law.
