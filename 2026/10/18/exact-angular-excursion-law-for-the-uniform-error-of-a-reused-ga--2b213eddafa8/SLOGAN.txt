For a smooth family of \(2\times2\) positive-definite matrices whose dominant eigenline sweeps angle \(\alpha\), reusing one Gaussian range-finder probe produces worst-parameter error \(1\) with exact probability \(\min\{\alpha/\pi,1\}\), even when the best rank-one error is arbitrarily small. A sharp angular tail law explains why the expected squared error vanishes at every fixed parameter while the expected uniform error need not vanish.
