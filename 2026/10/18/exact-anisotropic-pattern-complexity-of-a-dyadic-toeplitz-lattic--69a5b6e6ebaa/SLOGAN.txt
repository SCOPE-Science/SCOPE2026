For the two-dimensional dyadic Toeplitz lattice of Gómez and Petite, the exact number of patterns on a \(2^n\times2^m\) rectangle is \(5\cdot4^{n-1}\) for \(n=m\), and \(2^{n+m+1}-4^{\min\{n,m\}}\) otherwise. Its normalized pattern complexity has no shape-independent Følner limit: square windows give \(5/4\), while increasingly elongated dyadic rectangles give \(2\).
