At the chaotic logistic boundary of a Lotka–Volterra map, the typical transverse multiplier has zero exponential growth while its first two ensemble moments grow exactly like \(2^n\) and a computable combination of \((1\pm\sqrt{17})^n\). The log-multiplier has exact finite-horizon variance \(\pi^2(3n-4+2^{2-n})/3\), giving a Gaussian near-neutral amplification crossover of width \(n^{-1/2}\).
