The dodecahedral graph has two perfect-matching orbits with 8 and 22 minimum ordinary anti-forcing deletion sets per matching, but only 4 and 5 minimum edge-disjoint deletion sets. Requiring deleted edges to be pairwise nonincident raises their sharp anti-forcing size from 4 or 5 to exactly 8 on every perfect matching; the previously known ordinary spectrum is not asserted as new.
