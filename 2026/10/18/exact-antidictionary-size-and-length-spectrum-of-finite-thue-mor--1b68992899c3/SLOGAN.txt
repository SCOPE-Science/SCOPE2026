For every finite Thue–Morse iterate \(T_m\) with \(m\ge3\), the binary antidictionary has exactly \(4m-5\) minimal absent contiguous factors. Its complete length spectrum consists of two words of length \(3\), four at each length \(3\cdot2^j+2\) for \(0\le j\le m-4\), and five at the single boundary length \(2^{m-2}+2\).
