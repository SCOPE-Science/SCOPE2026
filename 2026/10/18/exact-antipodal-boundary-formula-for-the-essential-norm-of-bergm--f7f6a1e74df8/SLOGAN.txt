For a continuous disk symbol, the essential norm of the Bergman slant Toeplitz operator defined by \(Wz^{2n}=z^n\) is exactly \(\max_{|\zeta|=1}\sqrt{|\varphi(\zeta)|^2+|\varphi(-\zeta)|^2}\). The formula reveals a two-channel antipodal boundary invariant and characterizes compactness by boundary vanishing.
