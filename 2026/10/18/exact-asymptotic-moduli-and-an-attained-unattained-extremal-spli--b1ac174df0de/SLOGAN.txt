For bounded Nakano exponents \(p_n>1\) tending to \(1\), the pointwise asymptotic convexity and smoothness moduli coincide, yet the global convexity modulus is governed by the **largest** exponent while the global smoothness modulus is maximal. The convexity minimum is attained exactly on maximum-exponent coordinates; the smoothness supremum is never attained, even though these sequence spaces enjoy the fixed-point property.
