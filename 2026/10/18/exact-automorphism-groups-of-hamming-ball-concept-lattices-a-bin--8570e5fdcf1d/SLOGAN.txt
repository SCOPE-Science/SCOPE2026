The formal concept lattice of radius-one incidence on \([q]^d\) has exactly the ordinary Hamming-graph symmetry group \((\mathfrak S_q)^d\rtimes\mathfrak S_d\) for \(q\ge3\), but binary alphabets possess the larger group \(\mathbb F_{2,\mathrm{even}}^{d+1}\rtimes\mathfrak S_{d+1}\). The binary enlargement has exact index \(d+1\) and is explained by a parity lift turning the two-sorted incidence graph into the full \((d+1)\)-cube.
