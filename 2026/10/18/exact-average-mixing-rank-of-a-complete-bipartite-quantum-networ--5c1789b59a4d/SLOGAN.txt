A single nonunit bond in a complete bipartite quantum network lowers the long-time average-mixing rank by exactly \(\mathbf1_{\{a=3\}}+\mathbf1_{\{b=3\}}+\mathbf1_{\{a=b\ge3\}}\), independently of the bond's nonnegative strength. The defective network has positive-definite average mixing precisely when both parts have at least four vertices and have unequal sizes.
