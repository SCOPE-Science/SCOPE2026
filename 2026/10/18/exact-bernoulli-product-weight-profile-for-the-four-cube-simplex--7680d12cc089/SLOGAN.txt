For independent-coordinate Bernoulli weights on the four-cube, the normalized weighted four-simplex sum equals \(e_4(q)(1-4e_3(q)+18e_4(q))\), giving an exact four-parameter solution of a natural subclass of the open four-cube Hypercube inequality. Its deficit is at least \(46e_4(q)^2\), with the constant sharp precisely for the unbiased distribution.
