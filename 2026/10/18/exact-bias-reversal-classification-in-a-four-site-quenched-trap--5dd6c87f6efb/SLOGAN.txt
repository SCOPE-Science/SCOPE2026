For the four-site, three-particle quenched-trap exclusion ring, the stationary current is exactly odd under bias reversal if and only if \((a-c)(b-d)(a+c-b-d)=0\). Equal sums of opposite-site escape rates therefore preserve exact nonlinear bias symmetry even in some environments with no spatial reflection symmetry, while away from this locus the sign of rectification is fixed at every nonzero physical bias.
