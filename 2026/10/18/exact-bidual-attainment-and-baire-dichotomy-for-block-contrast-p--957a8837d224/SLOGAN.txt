For normalized disjoint signed block contrasts added to a positive rank-one map \(c_0\to c\), the second adjoint norm-attains exactly when the negative contrast mass on the core's positive support has zero limit inferior. The norm-attaining parameters are dense but meagre in the positive \(\ell_1\) simplex, despite all operators having identical norm and neither their first adjoints nor the operators themselves attaining it.
