For binary closed-neighborhood Lights Out on a Cartesian product of complete graphs with at least one even-sized factor, an explicit parity-product formula gives the exact nullity, hence the exact number of solutions to the all-on board. The same calculation exhibits an unbounded gap between the true nullity of \(K_{2r}\square K_3\), equal to \(2r-1\), and a standard characteristic-polynomial gcd lower bound, equal to \(1\).
