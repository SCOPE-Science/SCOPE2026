The exact two-adic valuation distribution of the binomial ray \(\binom{5n}{2n}\), for every dyadic index interval \(0\leq n<2^L\), is determined by an explicit eight-state carry matrix. In particular, the generating function counting valuation exactly one is \(z(1-z^2)^2/(1-z-z^2+z^3-z^4)^2\); the zero-valuation sequence coincides, after shifting, with an earlier OEIS word-count sequence.
