For every genuinely multiresidue prime-power length predicate with \(m=p^k\geq4\), at least two accepted and two rejected residues, and least translation period \(m\), the smallest unrestricted epsilon-free NFA has exactly \(m\) states, while the smallest bipartite-cactus NFA has exactly \(\operatorname{lcm}(m,2)\) states. Thus odd prime powers force an exact factor-two parity penalty, powers of two force none, and in each case a deterministic even-cycle witness is optimal.
