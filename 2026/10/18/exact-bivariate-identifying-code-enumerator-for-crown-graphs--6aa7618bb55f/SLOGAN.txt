For every crown graph \(K_{n,n}\) minus a perfect matching with \(n\ge4\), identifying codes are characterized by occupying all but at most one matched index together with two sharp one-sided coverage conditions; their complete bivariate count is \((x+y+xy)^n+n(x+y+xy)^{n-1}-n(x^{n-1}+y^{n-1}+x^{n-1}y+xy^{n-1})-n(n-1)(x^{n-2}y+xy^{n-2})\). For \(n\ge5\), the minimum size is \(n-1\) and there are exactly \(n(2^{n-1}-2n)\) minima; the \(n=4\) cube minimum count \(56\) was already known.
