For finite rooted starlike Kripke frames, the complete number of surjective bounded morphisms is an explicit inclusion–exclusion expression in the labelled arm lengths, because independently admissible arm maps glue over the roots exactly when every target arm is fully covered. For equal arm lengths, the global gluing probability has a sharp coupon-collector window with limit \(\exp(-e^{-t})\), and the uncovered-arm defect tends to \(\mathrm{Poisson}(e^{-t})\).
