On every finite Gödel chain, the unary box forgets an exactly quantifiable amount of Heyting–Lewis strict implication: its fibers have a closed product formula, and precisely \(2^n\) of the \(\binom{2n}{n}\) possible box operators uniquely determine the binary arrow. The largest fiber on an \((n+1)\)-element chain contains \((n+1)^{n-1}\) different strict implications.
