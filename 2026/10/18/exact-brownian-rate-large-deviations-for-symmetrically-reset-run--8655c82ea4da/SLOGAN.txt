For a one-dimensional run-and-tumble particle whose position and direction are both independently symmetrically redrawn at Poisson resetting times, the entire positive-occupation large-deviation rate function equals that of reset Brownian motion at the **effective resetting rate** \(R=r(r+2\alpha)/[2(r+\alpha)]\), at every finite tumbling rate \(\alpha\ge0\). This exact all-tilt equivalence, not an equivalence of finite-time distributions, gives \(I(a)=R(1-2\sqrt{a(1-a)})\) for \(0\le a\le1\).
