# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

A normalized monomial coefficient computation makes the toric composition operator an injective orthogonal-column weighted permutation. Consequently finite-coordinate truncations prove, without a finite-experiment extrapolation, that the essential norm of each power is the limsup of its column weights. Uniform gamma-ratio control deals with unbalanced index rays, so the one-variable positive-ratio optimization is global. Strict positivity of the matrix gives Perron growth of all entries and the rooted decomposition into infinitely many shifts. Their weights tend to the same positive Perron power, yielding the spectral disk and non-Fredholm interior and boundary. The constant monomial accounts for the isolated eigenvalue only when the Bergman exponent is above the Hardy endpoint. Necessary assumptions and exceptional Hardy behavior are explicitly included in the proof.

The exact Calkin profile for every iterate (not just its rate) and the simultaneous full Fredholm spectrum provide a complete operator classification for a natural nontrivial invertible-in-exponents toric family. The Hardy-to-Bergman threshold separates isometric persistent dynamics from exponentially fast operator-norm convergence; a Fibonacci instance gives a concrete closed form. These are natural quantities connected to current Bergman-composition boundedness and compactness questions, rather than arbitrarily selected computational entries.
