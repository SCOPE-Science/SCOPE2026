For positive nonsingular integral toric self-maps of the bidisc, the exact essential norm of every power of the weighted-Bergman composition operator is \((\sqrt{a_nd_n}+\sqrt{b_nc_n})^{-(\beta+1)}\), and the Fredholm essential spectrum is the disk of radius \(\rho(A)^{-(\beta+1)}\). At the Hardy endpoint this disk has radius one and norm convergence to the constant projection fails; for every Bergman exponent \(\beta>-1\), that convergence occurs at the sharp Perron rate.
