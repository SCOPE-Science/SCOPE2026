# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The original \(L^2\)-Cartesian definition reduces to the angular average of the squared Hermitian spectral norm. For the three-cycle the characteristic polynomial is exactly \(\lambda^3-S\lambda/4-\operatorname{Re}(e^{3i\theta}abc)/4\). Its trigonometric largest-absolute-root formula, followed by the uniform distribution of \(|\cos3\theta|\), gives the stated integral. Its scalar integrand is strictly increasing in the normalized determinant magnitude. Both endpoints and equality conditions follow by direct integration and equality in arithmetic–geometric mean. Complex phases and zero-weight boundary cases were checked analytically. The accompanying script supplies independent finite numerical diagnostics but is not the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with priority risk
The accessible full primary paper by Audeh, Moradi, and Sahoo defines the invariant and proves abstract comparisons, not this first nontrivial cyclic-family exact formula. Searches for equivalences, stronger coverage, indexed tables and logical implication found ordinary numerical-radius work and normal-operator special cases, but no directly dominating classification. An overlooked equivalent matrix-cubic integration theorem remains possible. Related geometric-weight work was accessible at abstract level only.

## Mathematical value — PASS
For a recently defined Hilbert-space operator functional, the first phase-sensitive finite cycle has a complete exact radius profile, a sharp normalized range, rigid extremizers, and unique recovery of the cyclic determinant magnitude at fixed Frobenius norm. The two-cycle analog is independent of the determinant. This identifies the precise first cyclic dimension where Cartesian averaging retains nonnormal spectral information, not an arbitrary parameter substitution.

## Limitations
Only complex weighted 3-cycles are classified. General 3x3 matrices and longer cycles are open here; no individual weight reconstruction, independent audit, or publication-priority guarantee is asserted.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
