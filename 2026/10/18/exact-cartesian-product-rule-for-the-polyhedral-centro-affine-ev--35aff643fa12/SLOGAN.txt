The complete even Hu--Ivaki facet spectrum of a Cartesian product of origin-symmetric polytopes consists of one zero eigenvalue, an ambient-dimension eigenvalue of multiplicity one less than the number of factors, and every factor's nonconstant even spectrum shifted by the complementary dimension. Consequently, prisms over regular cross-polytopes in dimensions at least four are non-parallelotopes with exact first even eigenvalue equal to their ambient dimension.
