Among all 688,128 geometrically smooth plane quintic equations over \(\mathbf F_2\), the Jacobian \(a\)-number takes only the values \(0,1,3\), with frequencies \(7/16,7/16,1/8\), and the full joint \(2\)-rank/\(a\)-number distribution is explicitly determined. Two smooth quintics with identical genus-six zeta functions but distinct \(a\)-numbers demonstrate that this stratification is invisible to the Weil polynomial.
