For the genus-three cyclic-six pencil \(y^2=x(x^3-1)(x^3-t)\) in characteristic \(13\), exactly two geometric parameters have \(13\)-rank one, six have rank two, and every other smooth member is ordinary. All eight nonordinary parameters are defined over \(\mathbf F_{13^2}\) but not \(\mathbf F_{13}\), and their complete Cartier strata are given by four explicit irreducible quadratics.
