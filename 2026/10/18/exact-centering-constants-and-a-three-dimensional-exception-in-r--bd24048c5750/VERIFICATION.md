The proof gives a convex-hull reduction from arbitrary real inputs to normalized signed indicators, an exact sorted-level operator-norm formula, and explicit strict witnesses in dimensions at least four. These are analytical arguments for every admissible positive nonincreasing finite weight sequence, not experimental extrapolations.

A separately executable exact-rational script, `lorentz_checker.py`, tests 72 random rational-weight samples by direct signed-support enumeration and independently evaluates the closed finite formula. It confirms the closed dimension-three formula and checks higher-dimensional strictness in finite examples. The script requires only the Python standard library.

The literature comparison is not an independent priority certification, and the cited 1999 and 2009 articles were not inspected in full. Independent audit: not performed.
