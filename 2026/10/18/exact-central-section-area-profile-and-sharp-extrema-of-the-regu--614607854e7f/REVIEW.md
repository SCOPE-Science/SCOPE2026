# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The vertex-edge characterization of the rhombic dodecahedron is explicit. Both chamber formulas follow from exact cyclic edge-plane intersection determinants and a shoelace identity. The hexagonal bounds are Cauchy–Schwarz and a two-square identity. The octagonal upper inequality has a complete nonnegative 28-coefficient rational Bernstein expansion, and the lower inequality follows from the monotonic derivative sign and exact endpoint profiles. The delivered checker independently reconstructs 2,000 sections with floating-point geometry, but the exact symbolic identities and inequalities, not those samples, prove the theorem.

**Originality — PASS relative to inspected evidence.** Full primary-text comparisons with Hausel–Makai–Szűcs (arXiv:math/9906066), Živaljević (arXiv:1307.5138), and Koca et al. (arXiv:1403.2847) identify covering, combinatorial zonotope, and lattice/root-system information, respectively. None of the inspected claims or their apparent logical implications gives the complete area formula and exact minimizer/maximizer directions. General convex-body section inequalities may give bounds of a different kind but do not yield the piecewise area identity. Relevant older section theory not completely available remains a historical-priority risk; absence from searches was not regarded as uniqueness proof.

**Value — PASS.** Plane-section area is a canonical geometric invariant of a classical lattice Voronoi cell. The formula evaluates it for every direction, gives an exact topological transition between six and eight section sides, and sharply classifies the two distinct extremizing direction orbits. This is a natural complete classification, not an arbitrarily chosen numerical slice.

**Scientific limitations.** Only central intrinsic plane sections are covered. The full affine section distribution and historical priority remain open to further investigation. Finite numerical checks are supporting evidence, not proofs.
