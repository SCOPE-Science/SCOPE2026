# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

For every nondegenerate Euclidean triangle, the proof identifies non-containment with a uniquely anchored angular half-turn, then integrates the squared counterclockwise perimeter arc over the three sides. The ray-intersection formulas have denominator bounds away from zero in their respective intervals, and the symbolic checker reproduces all rational integrals, six symmetric monomials, extreme values, and the Schur derivative identity. The convex-hull inclusion event has null boundary; repeated-side triples are included in the counting. The extremal argument applies the strictly Schur-concave polynomial to the exact feasible triangle-inequality domain, rather than optimizing over arbitrary unrelated shape parameters. The geometric half-turn argument is mathematical, not a numerical extrapolation.

## Originality — PASS with bibliographic residual risk

Ionaşcu (arXiv:1612.08619) contains a general containment integration framework and the classical equilateral-centroid probability for area-uniform interior points. The present proof explicitly credits these ingredients; their statements do not provide the closed side-weighted cubic nor its strict Schur ordering under arclength-uniform boundary sampling. Maesumi (arXiv:1804.11007) samples one point on each edge and studies area moments/distributions rather than capture, while Reitzner–Schütt–Werner (arXiv:1911.05917) obtain large-sample boundary hull asymptotics. Searches against mathematical research records and related published geometry claims found no equivalent all-triangle perimeter-capture classification. The formerly known equilateral numerical value is not advanced as an original special case. As with any bibliographic search, a not-indexed or differently named equivalent formula remains possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result resolves a natural shape-sensitive variant of the classical random-triangle point-containment problem at fixed sample count. It distinguishes area-uniform from arclength-uniform sampling, determines all shape dependence by a universal two-parameter symmetric polynomial, and proves exact optimization and rigid equality, as well as the sharp unattained degenerating configuration. It is a complete geometric family statement, not a routine point evaluation or an arbitrary numerical slice.

## Scope and limitations

Applies to three independent perimeter-arclength samples and the vertex centroid of a nondegenerate Euclidean triangle. Neither arbitrary convex polygons nor arbitrary target points follow from this computation. The sharp infimum is not achieved by a nondegenerate triangle. Symbolic checks supplement but do not substitute for the geometric proof. The historical equilateral constant is explicitly attributed to the earlier literature; independent validation has not been performed.
