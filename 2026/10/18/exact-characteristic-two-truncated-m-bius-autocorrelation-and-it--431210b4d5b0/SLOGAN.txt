For the truncated polynomial Möbius function over \(\mathbb F_2[t]\), every nonzero shift has an exact, nonzero limiting two-point correlation at fixed cutoff \(M\), with sign \((-1)^{\#\{z\in\mathbb F_2:a(z)=0\}}\). As the cutoff grows after the degree limit, the correlation is asymptotic to an explicit nonzero signed constant times \(M^{-4}\), resolving the characteristic-two boundary excluded from the motivating sieve result.
