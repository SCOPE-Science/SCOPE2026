In a rooted Yule leaf-splitting phylogeny with \(n\ge3\) leaves, the number of cherries and the Sackin leaf-depth sum have the exact covariance \(-n/9+2/[3(n-1)]\), strictly negative from four leaves onward. The optimal linear Sackin-on-cherries slope tends to \(-5/2\), while their Pearson correlation decays as \(-0.812994847/\sqrt n\).
