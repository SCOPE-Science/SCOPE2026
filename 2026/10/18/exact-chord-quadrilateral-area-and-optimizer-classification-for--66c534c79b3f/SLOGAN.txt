The regular nonagon has exact minimum chord-quadrilateral area \(\frac{5-2c+8c^2}{8\sin(\pi/9)}\), where \(c=\cos(\pi/9)\) and its circumradius is one. Exactly nine symmetry-related enclosing isosceles trapezoids attain this minimum, each touching one polygon vertex and three polygon edges; a fully edge-tangent quadrilateral is strictly worse.
