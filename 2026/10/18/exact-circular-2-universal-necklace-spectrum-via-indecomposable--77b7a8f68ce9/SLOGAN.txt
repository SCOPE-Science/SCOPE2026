For each alphabet of \(q\ge2\) letters, shortest circularly 2-subsequence-universal necklaces are classified by the direct-sum components of a permutation, giving the exact cut-refined spectrum \(N_{q,b}=q!a_{q,b}/(2b)\) except for doubled permutations. Their total number is \(\frac{q!}{2}[z^q]\log(F(z)/(1-z))\) for \(F(z)=\sum_{m\ge0}m!z^m\), and almost every qualifying necklace has exactly one antipodal witness cut-pair as \(q\to\infty\).
