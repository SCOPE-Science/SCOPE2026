For every harmonic cutoff on the round three-sphere, restriction to one Clifford torus has exactly the Fourier-diamond range, with every nonzero singular value given by an exact Jacobi sum. Its least squared singular value is exactly \(L/2^{L-1}\), so minimal-norm reconstruction amplifies worst-case errors by the sharp factor \(\sqrt{2^{L-1}/L}\), with only the four highest axial torus modes worst-conditioned once \(L\ge3\).
