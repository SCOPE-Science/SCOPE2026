For additive-noise Ornstein–Uhlenbeck test systems, a shared-noise stochastic Euler–Heun error makes the raw PI step multiplier an exact inverse power of a noncentral chi random variable, with sharp positive-moment threshold \(q=d/(k_i+k_p)\). Fixed clipping restores all moments and creates two exact boundary atoms whose probabilities decay at explicit exponential rates with dimension in the equilibrium-normalized benchmark.
