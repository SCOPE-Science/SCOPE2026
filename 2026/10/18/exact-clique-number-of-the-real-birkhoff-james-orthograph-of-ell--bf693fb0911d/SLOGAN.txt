Every real projective mutually Birkhoff–James orthogonal family in \(\ell_\infty^n\) has at most \(\max_k\binom nk2^{k-1}\) lines, and a signed-ternary support layer attains this number. A projective signed-active-face antichain and a sharp LYM chain argument establish the unrestricted upper bound missing from the previously known construction.
