## Correctness: PASS
The volume is reconstructed from the derivative of a translation-integrated cube-section volume. Because the within-cluster cutoffs are at most the unit cross-cluster distance, the event that a cube configuration violates the global range bound on a cube of side \(1+\varepsilon\) has measure at most \(N(N-1)\varepsilon^2(1+\varepsilon)^{N-2}\). This justifies replacing the globally constrained derivative by the product of elementary one-cluster volume derivatives. The explicit finite sum follows with no approximation. For equal radii, the elementary-symmetric expansion and the strictly positive two-part transfer difference prove the claimed majorization ordering for every \(N,k\); exact extreme-profile expressions prove the asymptotic. Endpoint and singleton cases are handled explicitly. The separate integral Ehrhart checker obtained the same volume in 20 configurations, six with heterogeneous radii, and verified 5,350 rational balancing transfers. These tests supplement the general proof.
