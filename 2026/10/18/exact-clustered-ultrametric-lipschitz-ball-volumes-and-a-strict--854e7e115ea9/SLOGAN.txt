The anchored-coordinate volume of every finite two-level ultrametric Lipschitz-dual ball is an explicit product-sum in its cluster sizes and within-cluster distances. For common within-cluster distance \(0<t<1\), balancing the clusters strictly *increases* volume; the balanced-to-most-unbalanced ratio grows sharply as a constant times \(N^{k-1}\) for fixed cluster count \(k\). The equilateral endpoint \(t=1\) has the same volume \(N\) for every profile.
