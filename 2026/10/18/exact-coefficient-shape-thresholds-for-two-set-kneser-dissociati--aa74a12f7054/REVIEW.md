# Scientific assessment of the two-set Kneser dissociation-polynomial result

## Correctness — PASS

The proof exhausts intersecting two-set families (stars plus exceptional triangles) and nonintersecting families (uniquely supported on four labels), then counts each class without duplication. Inclusion-maximality is verified by explicit extension arguments. The exact determinant factorizations and finite base-case table prove the all-order log-concavity threshold; the coefficient differences plus binomial tail prove the unimodality classification. The standalone checker directly verifies the defining graph condition on \(2{,}131{,}008\) subsets across \(n=4,5,6,7\), and arithmetic profiles across \(4\le n\le500\). The finite checker alone is not an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with limitations

Brešar–Dravec (arXiv:2108.10801v1, Proposition 2.1) already proves the scalar dissociation number of \(K(n,2)\) and contains the four-label localization idea. These known assertions are expressly excluded from the originality claim. The additional complete counting polynomial, maximal-set polynomial and exact isolated nonunimodality/persistent non-log-concavity thresholds were not contained in the inspected relevant statements. Tu–Li–Du (arXiv:2103.01402v1) and Tu–Xiao–Lang (DOI:10.3934/math.2023507) give generic maximal-set and cubic-graph bounds rather than a covering Kneser polynomial. The exact-claim published-finding corpus searches returned related findings on different graph families, without a stronger implication for these Kneser coefficients. Unindexed equivalent formulas, or a derivation elsewhere from the known four-label lemma, remain priority risks.

## Value — PASS

Two-set Kneser graphs are a canonical highly symmetric infinite family. The polynomial determines the distribution of every admissible low-induced-degree vertex subset, rather than merely the previously known maximum cardinality. Its coefficient sequence shows an isolated failure of unimodality sandwiched between two infinite/finite unimodal regimes while log-concavity fails from a different sharp threshold; these are mathematically distinct global features. The maximal-set enumeration additionally shows purity precisely at two orders and long gaps in maximal cardinalities. This is motivated enumeration and a nontrivial coefficient-shape classification, not a small numerical increment.

## Closest literature and residual risks

The 2021 Kneser-graph proof supplies localization, so the polynomial derivation should be credited as an elaboration of known structure, not a new foundational lemma. A complete priority claim cannot be inferred from finite search coverage. No independent review is claimed.
