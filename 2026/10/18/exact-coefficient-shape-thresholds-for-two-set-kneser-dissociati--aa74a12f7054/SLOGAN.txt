For the two-set Kneser graph \(K(n,2)\), the complete dissociation polynomial has strictly log-concave coefficients exactly for \(4\le n\le12\), but loses unimodality only at the isolated order \(n=14\) and regains it for every larger order. An explicit star-and-four-label classification also gives the complete polynomial of inclusion-maximal dissociation sets.
