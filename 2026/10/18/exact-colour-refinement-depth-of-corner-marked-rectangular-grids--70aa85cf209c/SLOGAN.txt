For every \(2\le m\le n\), ordinary colour refinement on the corner-marked grid \(P_m\square P_n\) stabilizes in exactly \(n+\lfloor m/2\rfloor-2\) rounds, producing singleton colours in nonsquare rectangles and precisely coordinate-swap orbits in squares. A boundary-Laplacian moment identity and a distant pair of reflected vertices give matching upper and lower bounds.
