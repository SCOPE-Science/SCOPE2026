For any two three-point sampling sets in \(\mathbb F_3^d\), their common orthogonal three-character Fourier bases are counted exactly by the span of the two point sums and two edge directions: the count is either zero or an explicit power of three times \(1\) or \(6\). In the ternary affine plane, \(4320\) of the \(7056\) ordered pairs are incompatible even though each individual three-point set admits an orthogonal spectrum.
