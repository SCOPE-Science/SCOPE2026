For two alternating pair-partitions around a finite cycle, the two successive lower approximations commute on a subset exactly when it is the full universe or its cyclic indicator avoids four consecutive ones. Their exact agreement probability is \((\operatorname{tr}(T^{2n})+1)/2^{2n}\) for an explicit four-state matrix, and it decays exponentially with cycle length even though commutation holds on every subset of the four-point frame.
