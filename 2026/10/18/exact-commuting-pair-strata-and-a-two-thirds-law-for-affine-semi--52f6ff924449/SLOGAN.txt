Among all labelled affine semisymmetric quasigroups on \(\mathbb F_q^n\), the full commuting-pair distribution has explicit finite-field formulas controlled by the rank of \(A+I\) for \(A^3=-I\). For every fixed prime power \(q\), the mean commuting fraction has order \(q^{-2n/3}\) and its logarithm has dimension-independent discrete-Gaussian fluctuations, even in characteristic three.
