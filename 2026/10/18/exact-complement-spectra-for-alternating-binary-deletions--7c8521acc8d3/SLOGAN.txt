For an alternating binary source \((ab)^n\), deleting an alternating scattered subsequence \((ab)^k\) leaves exactly the concatenations of \(n-k\) dominoes `ab` or `ba` with at most \(k\) runs of `ba` dominoes. Their number is \(\sum_{j=0}^{k}\binom{n-k+1}{2j}\), and every domino pattern is possible exactly when \(n\le3k\).
