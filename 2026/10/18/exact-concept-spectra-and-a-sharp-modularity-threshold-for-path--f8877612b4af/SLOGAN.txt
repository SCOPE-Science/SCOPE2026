For the reflexive radius-\(k\) adjacency context on an \(n\)-vertex path, the formal concept count has an exact two-phase formula, and its unique maximal value over radii is \(n+2+\lfloor(n-1)^2/3\rfloor\) for \(n\ge4\), attained at \(k=\lfloor(2n+1)/6\rfloor\). The lattice is a product of two chains through \(n=2k+2\), but becomes nonmodular for \(k\ge1\) once \(n\ge2k+3\).
