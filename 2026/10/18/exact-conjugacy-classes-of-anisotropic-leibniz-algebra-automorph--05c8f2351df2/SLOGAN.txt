For the anisotropic three-dimensional nilpotent Leibniz algebra over every odd finite field \(\mathbb F_q\), the full automorphism group has exactly \((q^2+3q)/2\) conjugacy classes, with a complete norm/trace-stratified class-size distribution. Its nonidentity involutions form exactly two conjugacy classes, of sizes \(q^2\) and \(q(q+1)\), and the exact commuting probability is \((q^2+3q)/(4q^2(q^2-1))\).
