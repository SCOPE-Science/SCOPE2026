For three-person majority updates on a connected sunflower of \(m\) social groups sharing one hub, agreement can fail forever: under independent unbiased initial opinions, exact eventual-consensus probability is \((3/4)^{m-1}\), regardless of positive group-selection weights. More generally, the theorem classifies every initial state and gives the exact two-color consensus probabilities, perpetual-disagreement dynamics, and coupon-collector consensus times.
