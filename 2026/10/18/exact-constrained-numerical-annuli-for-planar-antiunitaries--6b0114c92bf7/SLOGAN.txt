For every scaled antiunitary on \(\mathbb C^2\), the complete constrained \(q\)-numerical range is a closed annulus whose sharp inner radius is \((b\sqrt{1-|q|^2}-a|q|)_+\) and whose outer radius changes from \(\|A\|\) to \(a|q|+b\sqrt{1-|q|^2}\). The symmetric and skew magnitudes \(a,b\) determine two independently defined (possibly coincident) sharp thresholds for loss of the central hole and loss of full-norm attainment.
