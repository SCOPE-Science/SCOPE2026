For any fixed letter-count vector, a block-sorted word maximizes the necessary disorder budget for all jumbled scattered subsequences of length at most \(k\), with an exact finite formula for that budget. Jumbled Simon equivalence collapses to capped Parikh-vector equality at the sharp threshold \(\ell=k-\lceil k/q\rceil\), giving exactly \((k+1)^q\) classes, and has strictly more classes below that threshold.
