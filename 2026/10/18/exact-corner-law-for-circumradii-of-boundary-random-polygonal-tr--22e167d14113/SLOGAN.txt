For three independent uniform perimeter samples of any convex polygon, the probability that their triangle has circumradius at most \(t\) is **exactly cubic** for all sufficiently small \(t\), with a coefficient determined only by perimeter and corner angles. At fixed side count the scale-free coefficient is minimized exactly by equiangular polygons, and regular polygons of circumradius \(R\) have the particularly simple coefficient \(4/(m^2R^3\cos(\pi/m))\).
