# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof explicitly partitions the Lipschitz polytope into range at most one and range greater than one, the latter indexed by a unique oriented missing edge. Each chart has unit Jacobian in anchored coordinates; conditional beta integration gives the exact second moments. All normalization factors, zero-sum projection and degenerate dimensions were checked. On the four invariant subspaces, the covariance takes the four asserted values; \(B>C\) proves strict top-eigenspace recovery for all integers in scope.

The standalone exact-rational checker interpolates the volume and weighted moments directly from lattice points for all eight cases \(3\le N\le6\). This corroborates the analytic proof but is not used in place of the proof of the general case.

## Originality — PASS, with residual bibliographic uncertainty

The closest mathematically related work is Aliaga's theory of Lipschitz-free extreme points; D'Alì, Delucchi and Michałek's graph-polytope and Ehrhart theorems; and Alexander et al.'s finite-metric volume-product theorems. The prior public record on complete-multipartite facets, inradii and Euclidean distortion, and the previously recorded volume and complete-bipartite covariance findings, do not imply the full deleted-matching covariance matrix or its maximal-eigenspace graph-recovery theorem. The \(K_{2,2}\) case and scalar volume are previously covered and are not separate originality claims. An unindexed equivalent formulation remains possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The covariance is a natural second-order invariant of uniform random Lipschitz functions. The theorem identifies all spectral components across dimensions and shows that its maximal eigenspace reconstructs the complete labeled set of absent graph edges. It solves a nontrivial inverse geometry problem, not merely an isolated polytope integral.

## Limitations

Only disjoint missing edges and the canonical labeled Euclidean realization are covered. Small-dimensional exact computations are supporting checks, not a certificate of universal novelty or an independent audit. Primary-source comparisons and residual bibliographic uncertainty are documented in the structured review.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
