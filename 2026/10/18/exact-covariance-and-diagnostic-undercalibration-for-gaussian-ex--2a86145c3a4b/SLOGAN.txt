For unnormalized Gaussian FLEXTRACE on a constant-spectrum matrix, the exact leave-one-out correlation is \(1/(n-k+4)\), and the true mean-squared error exceeds the expected empirical squared-error diagnostic by the sharp factor \((n+3)/(n-k+3)\). At the square-sketch boundary the error diagnostic vanishes in expectation as dimension grows while actual variance approaches a positive constant; just one additional probe makes the estimator exact.
