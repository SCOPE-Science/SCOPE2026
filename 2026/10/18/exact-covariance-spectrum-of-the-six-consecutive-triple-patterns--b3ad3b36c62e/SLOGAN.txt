The six counts of consecutive length-three patterns in a uniform random permutation have a completely explicit finite-sample covariance spectrum, with one deterministic zero mode, one bounded nonzero eigenvalue exactly \(1/8\), and four eigenvalues growing linearly with length. The bounded mode is precisely the difference between the final and initial ascent indicators, and the sharp nonzero covariance condition number grows as \((4+2\sqrt2)n/3\).
