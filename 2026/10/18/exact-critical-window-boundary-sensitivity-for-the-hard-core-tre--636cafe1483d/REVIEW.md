# Scientific assessment: critical-window hard-core boundary response

## Correctness — PASS

The Gibbs partition-function recursion is reconstructed directly from the two possible root labels. The critical fixed point and cubic coefficient of its twice-iterated message map are obtained by explicit differentiation, including the vanishing quadratic term. The inverse-square transform gives a near-affine recurrence whose controlled cumulative error is \(o(n)\), uniformly for bounded critical-window parameter. Comparison estimates and the exact geometric solution yield the full, parity-correct, all-arity amplitude. A stand-alone rational exact-enumeration verifier independently checks the finite partition ratios and normal-form coefficients; numerical depth tests only corroborate the asymptotic.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk

The finite hard-core tree recursion and uniqueness threshold are classical, especially Galvin--Martinelli--Ramanan--Tetali (arXiv:1007.4806), and are not new. Ban--Chang (arXiv:1509.08325) study nonlinear tree-shift entropy and boundary conditions; Efthymiou--Hayes--Stefankovic--Vigoda--Yin (arXiv:1604.01422) study fixed subcritical message-passing contraction. The reviewed claim isolates a different observable, the **finite-height difference of two occupied-root marginals**, and determines the complete locally uniform double-scaling response at the threshold, including the leading constant. Search comparisons did not identify this evaluated profile. General flip normal-form theory may imply such behavior; priority against unindexed specialized computations remains unresolved, and the claim is not certified as uniquely new.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Boundary-condition sensitivity at the hard-core uniqueness threshold is a natural measure of correlation decay for tree shifts and Gibbs models. At the critical point the usual exponential contraction breaks down; a sharp, nonconstant crossover profile quantifies the entire transitional \(1/n\) activity window rather than one chosen fugacity or a restatement of the threshold. The exact function distinguishes approaches from both sides of the phase transition. It has natural computational use as a finite-height benchmark for belief-propagation recursions.

## Scope and remaining scientific limits

Fixed integer branching degree, two homogeneous leaf prescriptions, finite-height root marginal; the result does not control arbitrary heterogeneous boundary assignments, rates uniform for unbounded scaling parameter, or branching degree increasing with height. Historical-priority uncertainty persists. No external validation is asserted.
