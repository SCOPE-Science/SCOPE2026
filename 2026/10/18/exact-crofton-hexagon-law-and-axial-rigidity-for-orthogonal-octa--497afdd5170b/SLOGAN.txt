For every orthogonally stretched three-dimensional octahedron, the complete Crofton probability that a random intersecting affine plane cuts a hexagon has an explicit formula in three edge lengths and three exterior dihedral angles. Within axially stretched octahedra the probability is uniquely maximized by the regular shape, tends to \(\sqrt2-1\) in the flat limit, and decays like \(\sqrt2/\lambda\) for tall shapes.
