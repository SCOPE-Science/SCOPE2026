For every right equilateral triangular bipyramid, all four probabilities of triangular, quadrilateral, pentagonal and hexagonal sections by a Crofton-random affine plane have exact arctangent formulas. The probability of a pentagonal section is uniquely largest at height-to-base-circumradius ratio \(h_*=1.211448413582648\ldots\), with sharp maximum \(0.462970871315319\ldots\), rather than at the equilateral-faced shape.
