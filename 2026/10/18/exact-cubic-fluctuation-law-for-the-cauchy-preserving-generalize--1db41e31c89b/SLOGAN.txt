For the Cauchy-stationary generalized Boole map, the canonical bounded Cayley observable has an exact three-time correlation law and a cubic-sum cumulant rate \(3\alpha/[8(1-\alpha)^2]\). The cubic rate vanishes as \(\alpha\downarrow0\) but diverges as \(\alpha\uparrow1\), separating two zero-Lyapunov boundary limits that a mean-exponent calculation alone cannot distinguish.
