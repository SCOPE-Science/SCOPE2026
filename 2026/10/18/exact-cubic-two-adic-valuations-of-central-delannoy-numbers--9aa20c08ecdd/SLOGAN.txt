For the central Delannoy numbers \(D_n=\sum_{k=0}^{n}2^k\binom nk^2\), the exact identities \(
u_2(D_n-1)=3
u_2(n)\) for \(4\mid n\) and \(
u_2(D_n+1)=3
u_2(n+1)\) for \(n\equiv3\pmod4\) prove Lengyel's three conjectures on sharp two-adic valuations. A vanishing two-adic dilogarithm term, an odd cubic coefficient, and reflection of the two-adic interpolation provide the proof.
