The gcd of every sliding window of \(k\) cubed Pell numbers is \(1\) for odd \(k\). For \(k=2r\), it equals the half-index Pell or associated Pell number, divided by \(7\) when \(3\mid r\) and multiplied by \(3\) when \(4\mid r\); the exceptional factors come from a cubic antidifference and exact local valuations.
