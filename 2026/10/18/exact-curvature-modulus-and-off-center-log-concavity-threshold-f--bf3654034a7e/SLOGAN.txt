For a symmetric three-Gaussian location mixture, the optimal global potential-convexity modulus has two exact algebraic branches, yielding a sharp log-concavity phase diagram. Along its critical boundary, the worst curvature moves from the central mode to two off-center points at normalized spacing \(\sqrt3\); for spacing at least \(2\), arbitrarily small Gaussian-location contamination destroys global log-concavity even when the density is strictly unimodal.
