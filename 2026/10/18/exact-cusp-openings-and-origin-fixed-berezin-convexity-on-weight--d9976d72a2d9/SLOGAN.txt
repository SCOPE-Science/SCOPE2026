On each fractional weighted Dirichlet kernel space \(0<s<1\), a bounded origin-fixing composition operator has convex Berezin range exactly for real scalar dilation symbols. Every nonlinear symbol creates a sharp local cusp of half-width \(\kappa x^{1+m/2}\) near Berezin value \(1\), detecting its first nonlinear Taylor degree and coefficient modulus when the linear jet is known.
