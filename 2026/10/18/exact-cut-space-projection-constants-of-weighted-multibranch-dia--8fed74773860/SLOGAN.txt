For every weighted multibranch diamond with matching edge lengths within each branch, the smallest norm of a projection from weighted edge \(\ell_1\) onto the graph cut space is exactly \(1+(\sum_i r_i/(\sum_jr_j-r_i))^{-1}\). Equal branch lengths uniquely maximize the constant at \(2-1/m\), while one overwhelmingly long branch drives it down toward \(1\), despite unchanged cut-space codimension.
