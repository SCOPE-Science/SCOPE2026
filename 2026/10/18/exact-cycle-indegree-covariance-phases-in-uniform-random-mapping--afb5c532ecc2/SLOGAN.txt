In a uniform random mapping, longer directed cycles have negative covariance with indegree-zero and every high-indegree vertex count, positive covariance with indegree-one counts, and a single sharp sign reversal for indegree-two counts at asymptotic length \(0.584724\ldots n\). A cycle-weighted binomial–Bernoulli identity proves a more general nonpositive covariance for every discretely convex additive indegree score.
