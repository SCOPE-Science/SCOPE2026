In a single uniform random permutation, the covariance of its cycle count with a left-to-right record at position \(j\) is exactly \((j-1)/(j^2\binom nj)\). The total cycle–record covariance is positive but asymptotic to \(1/n\), almost entirely due to a record at the final position, and the correlation decays as \(1/(n\log n)\).
