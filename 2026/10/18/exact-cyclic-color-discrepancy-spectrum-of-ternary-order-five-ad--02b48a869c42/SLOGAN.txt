Of the \(786{,}432\) ternary order-five adjacency-hopping de Bruijn cycles, exactly \(23{,}700\), \(420{,}924\), and \(341{,}808\) have cyclic color discrepancies \(3\), \(4\), and \(5\), respectively. This is the first ternary adjacency-hopping order with a nonconstant discrepancy spectrum, and only \(1975/65536\) of its cycles attain the sharp minimum.
