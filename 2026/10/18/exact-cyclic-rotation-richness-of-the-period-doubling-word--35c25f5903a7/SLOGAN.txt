For the period-doubling word, the most cyclic rotations of a length-\(n=2^k m\) factor that can all occur is exactly \(n\) if the odd part \(m\) is one and \(3\cdot2^k\) otherwise. All rotations of some primitive factor occur precisely at lengths \(2^k\) and \(3\cdot2^k\), and each occurring rotation class doubles in size under the substitution at lengths at least two.
