For a fixed cyclic sweep of \(N\) completely mean-coupled scalar maps with local fixed-point slope \(-\alpha\), the exact sweep spectrum is strictly stable precisely when \(0<\alpha<N\), and at the sharp threshold \(\alpha=N\) it becomes the nontrivial \((N+1)\)-st roots of unity. Consequently a full-mean cyclic update stabilizes the otherwise unstable logistic fixed point of slope \(-2\) exactly from population size \(N=3\) onward, whereas synchronous full-mean updating does not.
