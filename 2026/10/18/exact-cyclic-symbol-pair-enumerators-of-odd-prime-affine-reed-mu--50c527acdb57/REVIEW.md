The lexicographic carry identity correctly handles the terminal cyclic step, all leading-zero coefficient positions, nonzero constants, and the entire \(p\)-choice affine intercept. The proof, not the finite test grid, covers every odd prime and every \(m\ge2\).

Binary Jacobi/Hamming-derived enumerators are not equivalent to a cyclic pair statistic. Searches of mathematical records and published literature did not identify the stated factorization or its \(2^m\)-weight corollary. The remaining risk is an unindexed equivalent classification or a derived result in a source not comprehensively accessible.

## Mathematical value (V): PASS

The theorem calculates the entire pair-weight multiplicity distribution of a natural infinite classical-code family whose distance and spectrum depend on evaluation order. The unexpectedly sparse fractal-type spectrum, exact minimum-weight multiplicity, and full-weight multiplicity answer finer coding questions than Hamming weight or pair distance alone. The statement is not extrapolated from isolated lengths or an arbitrary finite parameter slice.
