For the meet-semilattice formed by adjoining a zero to \(n\) incomparable atoms, every diagonal subsemigroup is uniquely a pair of enabled atom sets together with an arbitrary directed graph between them; its exact census is \(D_n=\sum_{L,U}2^{|L||U|-|L\cap U|}\) and its congruence proportion is asymptotic to \(2^{-n(n-2)}\). Almost every such relation contains all zero-coordinate pairs, after which its remaining directed edges behave as independent fair choices.
