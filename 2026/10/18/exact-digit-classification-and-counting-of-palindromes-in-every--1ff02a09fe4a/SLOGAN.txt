For every integer base \(b\ge2\) and exponent \(k\ge2\), simultaneous palindromes in bases \(b\) and \(b^k\) admit an exact padded-block digit classification, closed length-by-length counting formulas and a rational generating function. Their cumulative count is \(\Theta_{b,k}(X^{\lceil k/2\rceil/(2k)})\), with a parity-dependent exponent.
