For every even base \(b\) and divisor \(d\mid b-1\), the digit sums of positive multiples of \(d(b+1)\) are exactly the positive multiples of \(d\) except the odd ones at most \(b-1\). In particular, positive multiples of \(33\) in decimal notation have every digit sum divisible by \(3\) except \(3\) and \(9\).
