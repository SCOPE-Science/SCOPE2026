For the three-digit repunit \(m=b^2+b+1\) in every base \(b\ge2\), the digit sums of its positive multiples form exactly the positive part of \(\langle3,b+2,2b+1\rangle\). If \(b\not\equiv1\pmod3\), the last excluded digit sum is \(2b-2\) and there are precisely \(b\) exclusions; if \(b\equiv1\pmod3\), exactly the positive multiples of \(3\) occur.
