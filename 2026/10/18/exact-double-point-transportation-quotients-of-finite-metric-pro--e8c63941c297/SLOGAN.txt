The double-point transportation seminorm of every finite sum-metric Cartesian product is exactly the sum of the double-point seminorms of its signed coordinate marginals, so its normed quotient is an isometric \(\ell_1\)-sum of the factor quotients. For a weighted Hamming product this yields an explicit oscillation-norm quotient, an exact interaction kernel, and closed formulas for both vertices and facets of the quotient unit ball.
