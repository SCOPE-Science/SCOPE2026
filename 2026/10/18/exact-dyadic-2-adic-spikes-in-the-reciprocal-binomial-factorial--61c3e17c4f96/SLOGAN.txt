For every integer \(m\ge4\), the truncated binary logarithm has the exact valuation \(\nu_2(\sum_{k=1}^{2^m}2^k/k)=2^m+2m-2\). Equivalently, the factorial reciprocal-binomial sum at the same index has \(\nu_2(a_{2^m})=2^m+2m-3\); the proof uses two exact \(2\)-adic logarithmic cancellations and a uniquely dominant cubic term.
