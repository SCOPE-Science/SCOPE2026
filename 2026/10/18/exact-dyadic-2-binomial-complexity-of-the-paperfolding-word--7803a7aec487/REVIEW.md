# Scientific review: dyadic paperfolding 2-binomial complexity

**Correctness — PASS.** Two scattered-subsequence signatures of equal-length binary factors coincide exactly when their numbers of ones and weighted sums of one-positions coincide: the scattered `01` count is the latter minus a binomial coefficient of the former, and the remaining pair counts are determined. The dyadic interleaving formulas were derived directly from the odd-part definition of paperfolding, with one formula for each starting parity. The length-four base is explicit. Induction establishes three count layers and a mod-four separation of the outer-layer signatures, while the central layer is an explicit arithmetic progression shared by both start parities. The exact union count is therefore proved for all dyadic lengths in scope. The independent direct sliding-factor test matches complete conditional signature sets through length 512; the recurrence checker verifies its claims through length 2048. Limitations: no statement is proved about other lengths or higher binomial levels, and finite experiments do not serve as infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS, with residual literature risk.** The closest full texts inspected were Rigo–Stipulanti–Whiteland, arXiv:2201.04603 (the general binomial-complexity framework, Parikh-collinear morphisms, and other infinite words); Lü–Chen–Wen–Wu, arXiv:2112.05347 (2-binomial complexity of generalized Thue–Morse, not paperfolding); and Madill–Rampersad, arXiv:1208.2856 (abelian, not 2-binomial, paperfolding complexity). Their actual results, including a period-doubling discussion in the first, do not imply the stated paperfolding class count or complete tier decomposition. Published-result searches for dyadic paperfolding pair-count and 2-binomial signatures surfaced paperfolding attractor/prefix-normal records and general binary-multispectrum results but no covering statement; the latter do not specify factors of the paperfolding word or scattered pairs. The established ordinary factor-complexity bound does not determine the finer 2-binomial quotient. Residual risk: unindexed or otherwise missed papers might contain an equivalent formula; searches alone cannot certify uniqueness.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The paperfolding word is a canonical automatic sequence with classical abelian and factor complexities. Exactly determining the first non-abelian binomial refinement on the entire infinite dyadic subsequence is a motivated structural gap: the theorem gives an exact growing count, a complete invariant-based classification, and a sharp factor-complexity comparison. It is not a finite census or a reparameterization of the level-one result. The unresolved non-dyadic and higher-level cases define natural boundaries.
