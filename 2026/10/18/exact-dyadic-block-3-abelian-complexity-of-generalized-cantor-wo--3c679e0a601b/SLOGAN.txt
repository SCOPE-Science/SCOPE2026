For every generalized binary Cantor word with substitution width \(q\ge4\), the exact 3-Abelian complexity at length \(q^k\) is \(5\cdot2^k-4\) for \(q=4\) and \(5\cdot2^k-3\) for \(q\ge5\). All classes are classified by their numbers of ones and two-letter boundaries, with a single exceptional missing class at width four.
