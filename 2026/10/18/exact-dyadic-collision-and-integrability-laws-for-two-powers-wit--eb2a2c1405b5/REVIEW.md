# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof reconstructs the pushforward of Haar measure from a unit-group homomorphism and three mutually disjoint parity branches. The all-depth fibre recursion, moment recurrence, sharp integrability threshold, and ordered collision interpretation follow by exact measure normalization. Boundary levels \(n=1,2,d,d+1\) are treated separately, avoiding an unwarranted stable-level assumption. `verify.py` independently counted the fibres and their second moments for multiple non-power-of-two exponents through dyadic depths testing both sides of the singular threshold. Computational checks are corroborating evidence, not a substitute for the five-step infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS with bounded priority risk

Mishra (2021), Theorem 3.3 and Sections 6–7, gives intersectivity/solvability criteria for sufficiently many variables, including a four-variable start for exponent six; it does not give exact two-variable target multiplicities, the normalized four-variable collision sequence, or its integrability threshold. Pure-power-of-two exponents lie outside the claimed class; the theorem treats instead \(2\) times an odd integer at least three. Targeted literature and mathematical-record searches did not locate an equivalent all-level law. A general local zeta or diagonal-form result might yield the same formula after specialized evaluation; absence from retrieved records is not proof of absolute priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The problem asks for the local distribution of a classical two-term Waring map, a natural invariant stronger than representability. The law is exact for an unbounded natural exponent family; the collision sequence explains singular-fibre concentration by geometric growth, with the sharp critical exponent for all positive moments. It is not a numerical table, a renaming, or an arbitrary selection of moduli.
