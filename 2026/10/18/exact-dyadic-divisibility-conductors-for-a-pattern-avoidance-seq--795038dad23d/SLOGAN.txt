For the permutation-counting sequence avoiding 1342 and 2143, the exact last nonzero coefficient modulo \(2^r\) is determined by the largest positive integer \(k\) with \(k+s_2(k)\le r+1\), giving divisibility conductor \(3k-1\). The universal bound \(3r-1\) is sharp exactly when \(r\) is a power of two, and the formula settles the four forbidden-residue classes proposed by Rowland and Yassawi.
