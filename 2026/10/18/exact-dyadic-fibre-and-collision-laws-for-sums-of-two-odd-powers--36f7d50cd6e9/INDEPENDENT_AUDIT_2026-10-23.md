# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For every odd fixed d>=3, odd d-th power is an automorphism of every 2-adic unit group, so conditional odd-power distribution is uniform on the odd coset. For two independent uniform inputs, one-odd branch has mass 1/2 and density 1 on odd outputs, both-odd branch mass 1/4 gives density 1/2 on even outputs, both-even branch mass 1/4 gives 2^d-scaled copy. Its mass decays as 4^{-h}, so the pushforward is absolutely continuous and F_d(t)=1 (odd), 1/2 (even outside 2^d), or 1/2+2^{d-2}F_d(t/2^d) (multiple of 2^d) a.e. Taking conditional averages gives exact R_{d,n} and M_{d,n}=7/8+2^{n-4} for n<d and M_{d,n}=7/8+2^{d-4}M_{d,n-d} otherwise, M_{d,0}=1. The valuation shell v2(t)=dh with odd terminal u has density of order 2^{(d-2)h}, measure 2^{-dh-1}; complementary shells have corresponding upper bound, yielding sharp L^s iff s<d/(d-2), including endpoints. Conditional-expectation convergence in L^s for s>=1, and from L^1 to 0<s<1, is standard. Independent exact residue convolution and moments for d=3,5,7 and n<=8 agree; finite computation is not infinite proof.

## originality

PASS

The result describes Haar convolution/local additive energy at the prime 2 uniformly in every odd exponent and a sharp density integrability transition. Brüdern–Vaughan 2021 studies sums of positive cubes in integer arithmetic progressions and global variance, including a cubic residue exponential-sum identity; specializing their published theorem does not yield this all-depth dyadic local recursion or all odd d L^s spectrum. Standard odd-unit permutation is the classical premise, not the new theorem. Other implicit local Igusa-zeta literature could overlap, so best-of-knowledge priority is expressly conditional.
