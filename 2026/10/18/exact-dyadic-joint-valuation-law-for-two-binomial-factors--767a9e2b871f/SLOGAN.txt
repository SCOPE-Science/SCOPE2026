The complete bivariate dyadic generating function for the valuations of \(\binom{2n}{n}\) and \(\binom{3n}{n}\) is rational, with an explicit four-state carry model. Over \(0\le n<2^L\), their exact covariance is \(5L/12-4/9+(9+7(-1)^L)/(36\,2^L)\), quantifying the dependence of two natural binomial factors in an Apéry-like sequence.
