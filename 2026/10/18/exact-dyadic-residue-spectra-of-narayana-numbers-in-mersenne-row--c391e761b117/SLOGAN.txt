For every Mersenne row \(n=2^r-1\), a fixed-precision dyadic product gives the residue and exact multiplicity of every Narayana number modulo \(2^s\) whenever \(r\ge s\ge2\). In particular, modulo \(16\), only residues \(1,9,13,7\) occur: the first two each \(2^{r-1}-2\) times, while \(13\) occurs twice and \(7\) once.
