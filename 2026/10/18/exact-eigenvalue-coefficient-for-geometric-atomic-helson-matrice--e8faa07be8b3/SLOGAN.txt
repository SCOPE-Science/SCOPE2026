For a positive Helson matrix built from geometrically spaced atoms approaching the Dirichlet half-plane boundary, polynomial atomic masses \(j^{-r}\) produce the exact eigenvalue law \(\lambda_n\sim n^{-r}\bigl((2\pi)^{-1}\int f_q^{1/r}\bigr)^r\), with an explicit positive hyperbolic-secant Toeplitz symbol \(f_q\). This determines every Schatten threshold and the critical weak-Schatten residue; the leading constant is independent of the initial atomic scale.
