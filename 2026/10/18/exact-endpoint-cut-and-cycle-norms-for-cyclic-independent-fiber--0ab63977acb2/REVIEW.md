# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The Laplacian factorization into constant and mean-zero clone fibers is exact, with eigenvalues governed by the base-cycle Laplacian and scalar fiber eigenvalue \(2r\). The pseudoinverse, rooted potential, and transfer-current table are obtained by direct subtraction. All signs used in absolute values are valid over the full stated integer domain, including the boundary \(r=1\); the column sum and complementary diagonal formula yield both norms. Exact-rational grounded-Laplacian computations reconstruct 259 projector columns and incidence divergences. The computations are checks of, not substitutes for, the general proof.

## Originality

PASS under inspected evidence, with stated residual risk. Dilworth--Kutzarova--Ostrovskii (arXiv:2305.12582, Sections 2.1 and 2.6--2.7) treats tori and Hamming graphs rather than the cyclic independent-fiber lexicographic product. Polettini (arXiv:1405.0899, Sections 2--3) constructs graph-cycle oblique projectors without these endpoint orthogonal norms. Comparable known complete multipartite and bipartite calculations concern the degenerate three- and four-layer cases, not the infinite \(n\ge5\) family. Separate graph Cartesian-product and edge-subdivision transformation laws have different adjacency operations and do not imply the new formula. An equivalent presentation via electrical networks or circulants remains a possible undetected source.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The graph construction is a standard independent-vertex blow-up, and the quantities are canonical Banach-space operator norms. The exact current sign structure is determined for an infinite two-parameter family, and the sharp transition from cut dominance to cycle dominance occurs under the smallest nontrivial vertex blow-up for *every* base length. The formulas are insensitive to parity and yield optimal uniform asymptotic bounds. This contrasts structurally distinct graph operations without asserting global minimality of the orthogonal projectors.

## Limits and closest literature

The outcome concerns canonical Euclidean-orthogonal projections, not arbitrary optimal projections. No results are claimed for heterogeneous fibers or intermediate exponents. The closest checked primary paper is arXiv:2305.12582, 2023; its projection framework and specific Cartesian examples do not cover these graph adjacencies. General oblique-cycle algebra is in arXiv:1405.0899. Potential prior equivalence in electrical-current literature remains a bibliographic risk.
