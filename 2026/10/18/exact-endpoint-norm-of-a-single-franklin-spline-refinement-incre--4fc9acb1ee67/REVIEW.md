# Scientific review of the single-knot refinement increment

## Correctness — PASS

The exact cardinal-hat Gram recurrence gives a summable inverse. The orthogonalized local tent is the unique new direction, so the refinement difference has a rank-one kernel. Its squared energy, absolute integral including both infinite geometric tails, and attained spatial supremum are explicit. The rational formula has an everywhere positive derivative on the required parameter interval. Symbolic and independent finite-section checks pass 590 assertions. Infinite claims are justified by the geometric and algebraic arguments, not by finite approximations.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk

The most closely related inspected primary articles are Ciesielski–Kamont (2004, DOI 10.4064/sm164-1-4) and Passenbrunner (2011, arXiv:1103.1950). Their exact numbers are sharp norms of **whole** Franklin projectors on nonperiodic/periodic grids. Their statements neither assert the full-line one-knot **increment** norm nor the strict continuous-position midpoint maximum. Passenbrunner (2012, arXiv:1212.2777) gives inverse-Gram estimates rather than this sharp increment formula. The complete Bechler (2003, DOI 10.1016/S0021-9045(03)00030-3) wavelet paper was inaccessible: an equivalent historical formulation remains possible and needs further bibliographic assessment.

## Value — PASS

For adaptive piecewise-linear least-squares fitting, the difference between projectors controls the newly released component, not either entire fit operator. Its exact and strictly extremal amplification constant supplies an intrinsic stability measure for every single-knot placement. The midpoint being uniquely worst is a nontrivial global classification for this family. No general knot-census or higher-order extension is asserted.

## Limits

Only full-line uniform cardinal linear splines and one insertion are covered. Unseen historical equivalences remain possible; independent review has not occurred.
