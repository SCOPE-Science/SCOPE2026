For insertion of a single knot at position \(t\) in the unit-grid piecewise-linear spline space, the rank-one orthogonal projection increment has an exact sharp \(L^1\) and \(L^\infty\) norm depending only on \(t(1-t)\). Its amplification increases strictly toward the cell midpoint, where the unique maximum is \(27/26+45\sqrt3/52\), exceeding \(2.537\).
