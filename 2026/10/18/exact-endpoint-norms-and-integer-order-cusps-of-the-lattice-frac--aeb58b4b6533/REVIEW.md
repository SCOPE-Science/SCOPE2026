# Scientific assessment — lattice fractional Laplacian endpoint norms

## Correctness
**PASS (mathematical argument and bounded finite checks).** The spectral power's gamma coefficients and coefficient recurrence are identified precisely with the existing lattice fractional-Laplacian Fourier kernel. The sign of every coefficient for all positive noninteger orders follows from the finite product, including the eventually constant-sign tail. Zero total kernel mass and the exact row-sum norm on \(\ell^1\) and \(\ell^\infty\) give the finite formulas. Wiener inversion fixes the spectral radius, so the strictly positive mismatch-tail formula proves integer-only normaloidity. All coefficients outside the integer-power support have simple zeros in the order parameter, and their absolutely summable derivative beta masses give the exact jump \(2/m\). The order-zero strong-convergence dichotomy is established using density and a uniform norm bound, plus explicit counterexamples on the two endpoint spaces. The accompanying checker passed 739 finite assertions; the infinite claims do not depend on finite sampling.

## Originality
**PASS relative to investigated records, subject to historical priority risk.** The explicit gamma coefficients and the \(0<\alpha<1\) signed kernel are known. In particular Michelitsch et al. (arXiv:1412.5904, 1511.01251) already cover the all-positive-order kernel. Ciaurri et al. (arXiv:1401.2091 and 1507.04986) cover fractional-Laplacian and convergence machinery. The inspected primary texts do not state the sharp all-order \(\ell^1\)/\(\ell^\infty\) operator-norm profile, its strict normaloidity classification, the precise cusp jump at every positive integer, or the strong convergence failure at the two endpoint spaces. The assertion is these combined norm-geometric properties, **not** novelty of the gamma kernel or the basic \(0<\alpha<1\) signed-kernel norm. Related published results on fractional directed-cycle differences concern a different nonselfadjoint symbol and finite cyclic groups, and do not imply the symmetric infinite-line theorem.

A residual risk is that a classical absolute Fourier-coefficient (Wiener-algebra) identity may already encode all or part of the norm and its cusps under other terminology. That possibility could not be eliminated by the available primary searches and requires independent historical review.

## Value
**PASS.** The operator-order parameter governs a transition between local integer-order stencils and sign-changing nonlocal fractional stencils. The sharp endpoint amplification, **strict** gap from the Hilbert spectral norm except at exactly integer powers, and the universal \(2/m\) integer-order sensitivity quantify this transition. The discontinuity and strong-convergence separation at order zero clarify why pointwise convergence is insufficient for stability in the natural endpoint spaces. This is a structural all-orders classification for a canonical analytic-family problem, beyond a recomputation of isolated table entries or a finite experiment.

## Limitations
The conclusions are restricted to the unscaled, infinite one-dimensional nearest-neighbor lattice. Source-derived kernel identities are acknowledged; publication priority for all Fourier-algebra formulations is unverified. No claim is made for arbitrary graphs, negative or complex orders, or uniformly in \(p\).
