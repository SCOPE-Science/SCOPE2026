For all positive orders, powers of the nearest-neighbor lattice Laplacian have explicit sharp \(\ell^1\) and \(\ell^\infty\) norms: they exceed their spectral radius except at integer orders, where their norm profiles have exact first-derivative jumps \(2/m\). At order zero, the endpoint norms jump from \(1\) to a limiting \(2\), while strong convergence holds only on \(c_0\) and the interior sequence spaces \(\ell^p\), \(1<p<\infty\).
