The sharp \(L^1\) and \(L^\infty\) norms of orthogonal projection onto uniform cardinal exponential splines admit one elementary hyperbolic formula, with their global Lebesgue-function maxima exactly at the knots. The norm starts at \(2\), increases by \(\theta^2/120+O(\theta^4)\) under weak exponential tension, and returns to \(2\) at strong tension, forcing a nontrivial finite-tension stability maximum.
