# Scientific review: energy-covariance reversal in an inertial run-and-tumble oscillator

## Correctness — PASS

The claim concerns the unique stationary law of the linear, strictly damped, dichotomously forced oscillator with positive stiffness, mass, viscous damping, drive amplitude, and Poisson flip rate. The stable deterministic propagator provides a unique bounded stationary solution. Polynomial generator balances at degrees one through four form invertible systems. Exact symbolic elimination gives the mixed fourth moment, and subtraction of the product of two second moments yields the stated rational covariance. Its denominator is strictly positive. The threshold polynomial is strictly increasing in flip rate, with a single positive root exactly when the constant term is negative; positivity of its damping derivative proves the root curve strictly decreases. The exact independent checker reports 135 passed assertions, including 54 rational parameter solves. Neither finite cases nor numerical approximation is treated as proof of the all-parameter sign classification.

## Originality — PASS (self-review)

The primary full-text reference is Dutta, Kundu, Sabhapandit, and Basu, arXiv:2406.06120 (2024), especially Section III (general mixed-moment recurrences, stationary second moments), the sections on position/velocity marginal distributions, and its appendices. This paper supplies the same model and the general recurrence from which mixed fourth moments can in principle be derived; that underlying method is not claimed as new. The inspected sections do not state the explicit kinetic–potential energy covariance, its quartic sign boundary, or its global uniqueness and monotonicity. Patel and Chaudhuri, arXiv:2404.01107, study continuous orientation diffusion rather than a telegraph force and different marginal fourth-order quantities. Smith and Farago, Physical Review E 106, 054118 (2022), treat the harmonic active-particle nonequilibrium stationary state and rapid-noise asymptotics; their accessible abstract does not resolve the full finite-rate kinetic–potential covariance. Exact algebraic consequences of published general recurrences are a substantial residual priority risk. A 1987 dichotomous-noise-driven oscillator study could contain broader moment formulas; its full text was not inspected and equivalence is not ruled out. Searches did not identify a stronger or exactly equivalent published phase classification, but search absence is not uniqueness proof.

## Scientific value — PASS

Position and velocity have zero stationary linear covariance, so correlations between kinetic and potential energy cannot be inferred from the second moments; the sign-changing mixed fourth moment reveals a genuinely distinct aspect of the steady state. A complete monotone sign-reversal threshold, rather than isolated parameter samples or a small perturbation, provides an interpretable phase diagram for fluctuation coupling across damping and reversal rate. The exactly located damping cutoff separates an entire negative-covariance region from a mixed-sign region. The claim is limited to symmetric, exponentially switching propulsion and linear confinement without translational thermal noise; finite-data experimental sensitivity and broader stochastic forcing are unproved.
