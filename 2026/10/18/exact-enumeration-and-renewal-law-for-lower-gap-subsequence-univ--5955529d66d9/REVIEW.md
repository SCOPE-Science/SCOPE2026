# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
For a pattern beginning with the symbol first completed at the end of the initial full-alphabet arch, an admissible embedding can only continue beyond that arch plus the prescribed minimum gap. Conversely, first occurrences of every possible initial symbol precede that boundary, giving an iff reduction to the universal suffix. Iterating constructs a unique decomposition into full-alphabet minimal arches, mandatory fixed-length spacers, and an arbitrary suffix; insertion/removal of the spacers is a genuine constant-fiber bijection. Standard Stirling-number enumeration of the arches proves the rational word-count series. Differences of nested universal languages give the bivariate exact-index series; checking its specialization at the index marker \(u=1\) recovers all \(q^n\) words. Minimum lengths and the first correction follow coefficient extraction. The i.i.d. limit follows the strong law applied to disjoint coupon-collector arch lengths and deterministic spacers. Literal gap-constrained subsequence enumeration, the greedy test, exact coefficient arithmetic, and finite exact-index histograms agree in the supplied verifier.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The nearest gap-constrained primary literature is Day–Kosche–Manea–Schmid, arXiv:2206.13896, whose relevant definitions and complexity section were inspected. Its upper-bounded or mixed gap conditions differ substantively from positive-minimum-gap universal-language enumeration and do not imply the claimed canonical spacer deletion. Barker et al., arXiv:2003.04629, and Adamson, arXiv:2304.04583, give arch-factorization and algorithms for the *ordinary* zero-gap universality language. They provide important premises, but do not state the all-alphabet positive-gap bijection or distribution. Published related research results were inspected at their full `RESULT.md` texts: the binary distribution treats zero gaps, while insertion-distance optimization measures a distinct edit metric; neither has the claimed positive-gap scope. Targeted searches for gapped universality, gap-constrained enumeration, aliases, and the rational formula uncovered no equivalent statement. There is residual risk of an unindexed or simply unstated corollary of classical arch theory, so novelty is assessed against the inspected record, not proclaimed independently certified.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Uniform positive minimum gaps are a mathematically natural reconstruction and matching constraint, whereas the general gap-constrained model can entail markedly harder analysis. The new canonical reduction is exact for all alphabets and all lengths, not just a numerical example; it identifies a tractable boundary of the gap-constrained universality problem and yields the entire size/index distribution and the optimal shortest witnesses. The renewal law quantifies a natural stochastic efficiency penalty from mandatory gaps. This is a structural classification of a full language and natural statistic, not an isolated tiny calculation. No assertion is made about patterns with upper-bounded gaps.
