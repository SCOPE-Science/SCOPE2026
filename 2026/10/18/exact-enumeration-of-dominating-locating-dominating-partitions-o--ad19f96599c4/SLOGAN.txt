Ordered partitions of a labelled cycle into a dominating set and a locating-dominating set are exactly the cyclic binary words avoiding \(000\), \(111\), and \(00100\), apart from two length-four exceptions. Their full generating function is \(-z\,d\log(1-z^2-z^3-z^4-z^5-z^6)/dz-2z^2-2z^4\), and their number grows asymptotically as \(\rho^{-n}\), where \(\rho+\rho^3+\rho^5=1\).
