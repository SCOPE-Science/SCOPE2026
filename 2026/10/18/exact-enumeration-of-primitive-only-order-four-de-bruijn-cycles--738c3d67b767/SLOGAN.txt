The number of cyclic words containing every primitive four-letter word exactly once, and no imprimitive four-letter word, admits a closed formula for every alphabet size \(q\ge2\). The count is \(\mathcal U_4(q)=(q!)^{q^3}((q-1)^2+1)^{(q-1)(q-2)/2}/[q^{q^2-q+4}(q-1)^{q^2-2q+2}]\), with \(\mathcal U_4(2)=4\).
