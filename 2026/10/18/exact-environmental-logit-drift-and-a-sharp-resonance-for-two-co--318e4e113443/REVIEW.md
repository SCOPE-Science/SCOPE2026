# Review: exact environmental-logit drift

## Correctness — PASS
The original full five-dimensional equations (not a symmetry reduction) have been transcribed from Betz–Fu–Masuda, Eqs. (3)–(10). Logistic differentiation gives the exact identity \(\dot H=a+b-1\) on the whole open phase space. Both exponential face-union bounds follow from dividing by products of factors at most one and bounding the minimum using the total exponent \(1+a+b\). The proof identifies an invariant measure contradiction rigorously using the push-forward of \(H\). A concrete source-admissible payoff construction realizes interior stationary solutions precisely on harmonic resonance. Symbolic algebra and finite exact checks support but do not substitute for these arguments. The proof does not claim pointwise convergence of each environment coordinate.

## Originality — PASS
The closest directly applicable primary source is Betz, Fu, and Masuda (2024), accessible full text in arXiv:2404.17082v2. Its full model and Appendix G give equilibrium classes and local stability conditions, including a parameter combination algebraically identical to the sign of \(\sigma\): that threshold *expression* is previously present and no priority is claimed for it alone. Section 5 examines apparent cycles numerically; Section 6 notes no cycles in a different, simpler well-mixed model. None supplies the exact global log-odds drift, the uniform exponential proximity to a specified face union for every interior trajectory, or the exclusion of arbitrary interior invariant probability measures. Related single-environment feedback literature of Weitz et al. (2016) and Tilman et al. (2020) does not imply the result for three edge environments. Relevant indexed research results concerning first integrals and stationary means apply to other equations. Residual risk: unknown unindexed equivalent derivations; the present review is not an exhaustive literature census.

## Value — PASS
The model is motivated by two communities exchanging strategies through three physically distinct environmental conditions; oscillations shown in its full five-dimensional simulations raise a substantive dynamical interpretation question. The new theorem converts a delicate, model-specific global problem into a sharp, exact rate condition entirely independent of payoffs. It distinguishes merely recurrent strategies near boundary environments from genuinely persistent fully-interior environmental cycles, with exponential bounds and a constructive resonant counterregime. In the original Figure 8(c) and 8(e) the respective exact drifts are \(7/8\) and \(55/728\). The finding does not preclude boundary cycles, heteroclinic switching, or chaos, and does not claim that every resonant payoff family has interior cycles.
