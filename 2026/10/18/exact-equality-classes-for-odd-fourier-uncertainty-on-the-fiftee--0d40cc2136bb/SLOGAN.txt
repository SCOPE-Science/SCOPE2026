On the fifteen-cycle, every nonzero odd function obeys the sharp Fourier support-sum bound \(|\operatorname{supp}f|+|\operatorname{supp}\widehat f|\ge10\), and equality has exactly four complex-projective classes. These are two explicit five-character sine packets supported on \(\{\pm3,\pm6\}\) and their Fourier transforms; a complete, exact two-column sine-matrix certificate excludes all other equality cases.
