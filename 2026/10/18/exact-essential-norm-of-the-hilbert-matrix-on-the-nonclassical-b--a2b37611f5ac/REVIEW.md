# Mathematical assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The boundary rescaling gives exactly \(\operatorname B(\alpha-1,2-\alpha)\). The positive-coefficient kernel establishes a compact-approximation upper bound, while the explicitly normalized analytic spikes lie in the little Bloch space, converge weakly to zero, and meet that lower bound. The delicate weak-convergence and domination steps are proved in the main result. Numerical integral evaluations support the asymptotic, not replace the proof.

**Originality — PASS, subject to the specified access risk.** Cui, Lin and Lu determine the full operator norm and its extremizers on the same \(\alpha\)-Bloch scale; this does not directly give its essential norm. Li and Zhou determine essential norm for source Bloch-type spaces to **classical** Bloch or BMOA. Lindström, Miihkinen and Norrbo study Hardy, Bergman and weighted sup-norm spaces; the subsequent weighted-growth-space literature has the same domain mismatch. The full text of the new operator-norm preprint was inaccessible, and unindexed overlap remains possible.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The result determines the exact Calkin quotient norm for every \(1<\alpha<2\), via a sharp boundary transport coefficient, even though the unrestricted operator norm can have an interior optimizing regime. This is an independently meaningful structural invariant and exact compact-approximation barrier, not merely a boundedness restatement.

**Limits.** The proof is for the displayed additive Bloch norm and open parameter range only. It does not establish norm-attainment, finite-rank approximation numbers, endpoint behavior, or an independently verified publication-priority claim.
