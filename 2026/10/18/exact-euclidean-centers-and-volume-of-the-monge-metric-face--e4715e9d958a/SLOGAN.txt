For every integer \(n\ge3\), the intrinsic Euclidean incenter of the normalized Monge metric face is exactly the line-distance matrix \((3|i-j|/[n(n^2-1)])_{i,j}\); its minimum enclosing sphere has a different, explicit all-vertex contact center. Both radii and the face volume are exact, with circumradius-to-inradius ratio asymptotic to \(n^2/6\).
