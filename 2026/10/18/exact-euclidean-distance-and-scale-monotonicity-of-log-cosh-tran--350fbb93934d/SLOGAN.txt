For every origin-centered \(n\)-parallelotope of volume \(V\), the log-cosh critical transition body has exact Euclidean Banach--Mazur distance \(\operatorname{arcosh}(e^{1/V})/(\sqrt n\,\operatorname{arcosh}(e^{1/(nV)}))\). This sharp affine distance decreases strictly with volume, interpolating from the crosspolytope value \(\sqrt n\) to the Euclidean value \(1\).
