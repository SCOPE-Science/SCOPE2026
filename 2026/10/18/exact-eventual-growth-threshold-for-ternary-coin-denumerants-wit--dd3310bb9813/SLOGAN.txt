For the ternary coin-counting function with denominations \(2,3,c\), eventual strict coefficient growth occurs exactly when \(\gcd(c,6)=1\). For every such \(c\ge5\), the last plateau has the sharp index \(7c-7\) or \(7c-5\) according to the residue of \(c\) modulo \(6\); noncoprime parameters exhibit infinitely many strict decreases.
