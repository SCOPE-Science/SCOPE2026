Mal's first-public 2022 manuscript and 2023 journal Theorem 3.11 state the complex Hilbert formula for the Euclidean exponent and the higher-exponent two-operator case. Mal's general lower bound is \(1/(2k^{1/p})\), strictly weaker than the new low-exponent constant for \(k>1\). Moslehian–Sattari–Shebrawi (2017) provide a signed-combination Hölder inequality and related bounds, but their inspected sections do not give the sharp low-exponent joint numerical index or its equality witness. A published restricted common-source rank-one result concerns only the Euclidean exponent, so it does not imply the unrestricted \(p<2\) plateau. The 2026 Çevik–Bhunia article's abstract concerns monotonicity, convergence and inequalities; its full text was inaccessible and is explicitly an unresolved bibliographic risk. The 2013/2014 Drnovšek–Müller paper is also not verified in full, but Mal attributes to it the Euclidean-case construction rather than an all-exponent index classification. These limitations are not asserted to prove uniqueness against every unindexed source.
