For every complex Hilbert space of dimension at least \(k+1\), the exact \((p,k)\)-joint numerical index is \(1/(2k^{1/\max\{p,2\}})\) for all \(1\le p<\infty\). Thus the index is flat below the Hilbert exponent \(2\) and follows a different sharp power law above it; commuting rank-one square-zero tuples attain every value.
