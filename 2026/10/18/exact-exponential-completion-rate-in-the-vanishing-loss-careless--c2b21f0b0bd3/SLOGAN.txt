When the per-round independent coupon-loss probability is \(c/n\), the careless collector takes \(\exp(nI(c)+o(n))\) rounds on the logarithmic scale to complete, with \(I(c)=\int_0^1\log(1+(e^{cx}-1)/x)\,dx\). This strictly exceeds the independent-marginal exponent \(\log(1+c)\), and the result holds both in probability for the hitting time and for its mean.
