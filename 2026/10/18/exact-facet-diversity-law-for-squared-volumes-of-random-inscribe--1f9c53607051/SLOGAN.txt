For any \(d\)-simplex, sampling one independent point from every facet gives squared normalized hull volume with expectation \((d+1)!/((d+1)^d d^{d+1})\). Under independent iid facet choices with probabilities \(\pi_i\), the expectation is exactly this constant times \(1-\sum_i\pi_i^2\); the unique best iid choice is uniform and still loses a sharp factor \((d+1)/d\) to one-per-facet stratification.
