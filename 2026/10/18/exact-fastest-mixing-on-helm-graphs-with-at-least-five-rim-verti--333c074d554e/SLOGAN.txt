For every helm graph with at least five rim vertices, the exact fastest symmetric stochastic mixing rate is \(1-A_n/(s_n+2\sqrt{s_n}+2)\), where \(s_n=1-\cos(2\pi/n)\) and \(A_n=1/n+s_n(1-1/n)\). Its unique rotation-and-reflection-invariant optimizer saturates both hub and rim outgoing-probability constraints, and its optimal spectral gap is asymptotic to \(1/(2n)\).
