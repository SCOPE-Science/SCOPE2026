On every prism graph \(C_n\square K_2\), the globally fastest symmetric stochastic walk has exact modulus \(\Delta_n/(\Delta_n+2\delta_n)\), with rim weight \(2/(\Delta_n+2\delta_n)\) and rung weight \(\delta_n/(\Delta_n+2\delta_n)\), where \(\delta_n,\Delta_n\) are the smallest positive and largest cycle Laplacian eigenvalues. As the prism grows, the optimal rung weight decays like \(\pi^2/n^2\) despite the rim probability tending to \(1/2\).
