On the natural invariant commuting-locus measure of any quaternionic lift of the Fibonacci cat map, ordinary mixing fails because maximal-torus axes are conserved, but the optimal analytic correlation error relative to those axes is exactly \(\rho^{F_{n+3}}\) at every time \(n\ge0\). This sharp double-exponential law is attained by explicit Fourier cosines and is independent of which quaternionic Nielsen lift is chosen.
