For the quadratic single-species map with alternating multiplicative survival, the exact finite-amplitude comparison with constant mean survival is \(\overline{x}^{\,2}-x_*^2=(Q-1)(h-Q-2)/2\), where \(h\) is mean effective reproduction and \(Q=\sqrt{1+(a-b)^2/4}\). At fixed mean reproduction \(h>3\), increasing seasonal contrast provably switches from enhancing to suppressing long-run mean abundance before persistence is lost.
