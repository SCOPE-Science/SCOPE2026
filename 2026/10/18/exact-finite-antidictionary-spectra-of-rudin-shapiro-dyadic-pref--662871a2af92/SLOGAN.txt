For every dyadic Rudin–Shapiro prefix of length \(2^m\) with \(7\le m\le16\), a complete finite antidictionary enumeration gives exactly \(8m-5\) minimal absent factors, with a unique longest factor of length \(2^{m-2}+2\). Their entire length spectrum is given by five small exceptional lengths and a repeating dyadic multiplicity pattern; no claim is made for larger prefixes.
