The subsequent Ayyer--Misra inclusion-process study (arXiv:2510.09191, Section 4.2) has different rates and its transport formula in an undeformed case does not cover this theorem. Relevant index searches did not reveal an exact counterpart, but that is not proof of priority. A broader factorized current theorem may imply the coefficient identity, and a known real-rootedness comparison may imply the sign law; these are residual independent-review risks.
