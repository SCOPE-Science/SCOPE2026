For each uniform \(b\)-ary filtration with \(b\ge3\), the largest real \(L^1\) norm of a depth-\(d\) predictable sign martingale transform is exactly \(2(b-1)d/(b+1)+(b-1)(b+3)/(b+1)^2+4(-1/b)^d/(b+1)^2\). A transform is extremal precisely when it contains at least one root-to-leaf path whose signs alternate, starting negative; all off-path signs remain free.
