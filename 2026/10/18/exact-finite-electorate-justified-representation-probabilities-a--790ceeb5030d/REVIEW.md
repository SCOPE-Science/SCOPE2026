# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** The event of violating justified representation is exactly that at least one excluded candidate attracts \(\lceil n/k\rceil\) or more unrepresented voters. Conditioning on the binomial number of unrepresented voters gives the exact mixture and independent outer-candidate tails. Parameter monotonicity of the mixture establishes the finite-electorate exchange optimizer. The union bound and Bernoulli relative entropy give a uniform failure upper bound, while the equal-parameter construction and a single binomial-tail lower bound establish the sharp rate, including the \(k=1\) edge case. The proof covers \(p_c=0\) and \(p_c=1\), \(m=k\), arbitrary \(n\), and nondivisible \(n/k\). The complete rational small-instance enumeration corroborates but does not replace the analytic argument.

**Originality (PASS, subject to independent priority review).** The 2024 motivating paper proves #P-completeness with arbitrary voter-candidate probabilities and a distinct tractable special case with deterministic elected approvals. The 2021 justifying-groups paper establishes asymptotic common-probability JR and the very same elementary maximum \(k^k/(k+1)^{k+1}\). Xia (2025) proves a stronger *qualitative* exponential CORE/EJR+ property for top-probability committees. These broader guarantees are acknowledged, not relabeled as new. The presently assessed exact heterogeneous finite-\(n\) JR formula, exact finite-\(n\) MaxJR optimizer, and equality of the universal failure large-deviation exponent are not stated in the primary materials compared. Probability identities or other unexamined work could still establish equivalent coverage; that is the residual priority risk.

**Value (PASS).** Computing JR probability and maximizing it are hard under general uncertain-approval distributions. Candidate-specific independent probabilities give a natural, nontrivial tractable class in which both tasks admit explicit exact solutions; the sharp worst-case exponent offers a distribution-uniform quantitative reliability guarantee beyond the already-known qualitative high-probability assertion. The motivating object is a real committee representation axiom, rather than an arbitrary finite table or renamed standard bound.

**Limitations.** Nothing is proved for voter-specific probabilities, correlated approval decisions, PJR/EJR probability optimization, or varying \(k,m\) within the asymptotic sharpness limit. The exponential bound need not be sharp at fixed \(n\). Independent scientific and publication-priority audit remains outstanding.
