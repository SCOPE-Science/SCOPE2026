For every prime \(p\ge5\), the normalized collision count for sums of two cubes modulo \(p^n\) has an exact three-periodic geometric correction to the limit \(1+\gamma_p/p+1/p^2\), with \(\gamma_p=7\) when \(p\equiv1\pmod3\) and \(1\) otherwise. The complete limiting prime-adic fibre density is explicit and belongs to \(L^s\) exactly for \(0<s<3\).
