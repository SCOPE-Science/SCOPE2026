Two independently perturbed initial Bergman-shift weights have exact finite-order \(k\)-hyponormality and \(n\)-contractivity windows. For fixed tail mass \(0<c<1\), the \(k\)-hyponormality window collapses at sharp orders \(k^{-4}\) and \(k^{-3}\) on opposite sides, but at \(c=1\) both sides become fourth order; the corresponding contractivity powers are two orders slower.
