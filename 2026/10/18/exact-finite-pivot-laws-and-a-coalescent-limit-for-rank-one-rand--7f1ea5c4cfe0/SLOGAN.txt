For exact uniform-pair Jacobi diagonalization of \(\lambda I+\rho vv^{\mathsf T}\), the active rank-one support follows a pure-death chain: a full-support \(n\times n\) matrix takes exactly \((n-1)^2\) sampled pivots in expectation, and its stopping time divided by \(n^2\) converges to half the Kingman-coalescent absorption time. Uniform block Jacobi sampling admits an exact hypergeometric recurrence for the expected number of blocks to diagonalize.
