For a passive tracer forced by an exogenous periodic run-and-tumble driver, any \(N+1\) distinct tumbling rates recover the powers of \(N\) possible Fourier harmonics even when thermal diffusivity is unknown, and fewer measurements cannot guarantee this. The full rate-response curve determines every harmonic power and obeys a strict monotonicity law; neither Fourier phases nor feedback-coupled transport are inferred.
