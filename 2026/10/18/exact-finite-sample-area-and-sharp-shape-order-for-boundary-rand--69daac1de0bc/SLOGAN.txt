For \(N\ge3\) independent arclength-uniform boundary points of any triangle, the expected convex-hull area fraction equals \(1-N\mathbb E[(pU+qV+rW)^{N-1}]\), with independent uniform \(U,V,W\) and normalized side lengths \(p,q,r\). This exact finite-sample quantity is strictly Schur-concave: equilateral triangles uniquely maximize it, while the sharp unattained infimum \(1-(4-2^{2-N})/(N+1)\) is approached by degenerating triangles.
