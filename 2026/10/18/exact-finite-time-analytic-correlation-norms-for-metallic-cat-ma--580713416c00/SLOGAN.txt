For the metallic cat automorphisms \(A_s=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}s^2+1&s\\s&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)\), the exact normalized analytic correlation norm at every positive time is \(\theta^{G_{n+1}+2G_n+G_{n-1}}\), where \(G_{n+1}=sG_n+G_{n-1}\). Its only maximizing Fourier input frequencies are \(\pm M_s^{-n}e_1\) and \(\pm M_s^{-n}e_2\), giving the Fibonacci exponent \(F_{n+3}\) for the classical cat map.
