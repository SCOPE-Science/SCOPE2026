On the full nonzero compatibility frame of a finite Boolean powerset algebra, every pointed \(k\)-variable modal theory is completely determined by its global set of atom colors and the color support of the pointed state. This yields exactly \(\sum_{j=1}^{\min(n,2^k)}\binom{2^k}{j}(2^j-1)\) theories on \(n\) atoms and a sharp \(\lceil\log_2(n+1)\rceil\)-variable threshold for separating larger full-state stages, with modal-depth-three witnesses.
