For every sphere, the smallest possible uniform norm of a degree-two spherical Fourier reproducer fixing constants and first harmonics is exactly \((d+1)\Gamma((d+1)/2)/(\sqrt\pi\Gamma((d+2)/2))\), attained by a unique rotation-equivariant quadratic kernel. The optimal multiplier has an asymmetric stability cusp: its right-hand norm loss scales like \(\delta^{3/2}\) on the circle but quadratically in dimensions at least two.
