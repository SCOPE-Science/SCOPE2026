For any fixed odd number of at least three voters and any fixed Smith-set size \(s\ge3\), the probability of that rare size has an explicit strictly positive leading integral constant, refining the known order-of-magnitude bound. Conditional on the rare event, all voter-score coordinates of the \(s\) selected alternatives, scaled by \(n^{1/\ell}\), converge in total variation to a density determined by unions of anchored boxes and a strong-connectivity indicator.
