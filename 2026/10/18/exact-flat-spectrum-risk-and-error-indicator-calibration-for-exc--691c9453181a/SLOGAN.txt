For a rank-\(r\) flat positive spectrum, the unnormalized Gaussian FlexTrace estimator has exact squared risk \(2(r-k+1)(r+3)f(\lambda)^2/[k(r-k+4)]\) for \(k\le r\), yet becomes exact with just one more probe at \(k=r+1\). Its natural leave-one-out squared-error indicator captures only the fraction \((r-k+3)/(r+3)\) of the true mean-square risk in expectation; at the rank-saturation budget this fraction shrinks like \(3/r\).
