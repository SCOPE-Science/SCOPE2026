For a clique bouquet \(K_1\vee\bigsqcup_iK_{s_i}\) with a largest peripheral clique of size \(a\ge2\), the exact polynomial of locally forcing partial \((a+1)\)-colourings factors as \(q(a!)^Bz^m[U(1+z)(z+a)^B-aV]\). Beyond the already known single-clique case, it gives every seed count and a parity-controlled real-root census: precisely one nonzero real root when \(B\) is even, and two when \(B\) is odd.
