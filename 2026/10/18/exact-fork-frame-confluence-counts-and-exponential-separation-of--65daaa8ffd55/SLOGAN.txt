On a rooted intuitionistic fork with \(k\) labelled leaves, the exact numbers of unrestricted modal relations satisfying FIK forward confluence and IK two-sided confluence admit explicit finite sums. The proportion of FIK frames that also satisfy IK is \(2^{-k}(1+O(k2^{-k}))\), so each additional incomparable leaf asymptotically halves that proportion.
