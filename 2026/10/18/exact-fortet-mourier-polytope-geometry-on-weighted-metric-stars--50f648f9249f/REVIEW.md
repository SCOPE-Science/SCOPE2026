# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The full-dimensional star constraints reduce exactly to independent leaf intervals once the center is fixed. Every retained halfspace admits a uniquely active rationally describable witness, and all vertices are characterized by endpoint selections and center pinning. The collision count follows from inclusion–exclusion on pinning choices. The dual formula is the support function of the rectangular fibers and its finite-breakpoint optimizer. A standalone exact-rational test performs 897 assertions, including complete small-dimensional active-set enumeration. No extrapolation from the tests is used in the proof.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** Hille–Theewis (2022) gives a general optimization program, but not these star-specific count formulas or sorted-breakpoint closed dual evaluation. Hille–Theewis (2023) gives general norming/extremal constructions; it does not state this weighted-star phase classification. Gordon–Petrov (2017) treats generic strict metrics, whereas weighted stars with at least two leaves, including their augmented-metric representation, have exact triangle equalities. Farmer's earlier general extremality criterion remains a qualitative comparator; only its public abstract was accessible here. Exact equivalents in differently named polytope or flat-metric literature cannot be ruled out categorically.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** This supplies an entire finite-metric family's face structure, the sharp maximum of the vertex count, exact behavior at all coincident length thresholds, and a dimension-scalable dual norm method for arbitrary signed masses. The generic count and collision law distinguish a naturally degenerate class from generic strict-metric Lipschitz polytopes; the dual formula extends beyond the single-Dirac explicit regime noted in the motivating literature.

Limitations: real finite stars, maximum-type Fortet–Mourier norm, exact-arithmetic complexity model. Bibliographic confidence does not amount to an exhaustive novelty guarantee.
