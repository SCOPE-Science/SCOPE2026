For a finite weighted metric star, the real Fortet–Mourier ball has exactly \(2(m+1)+2k\) facets, and its complete vertex set admits a breakpoint classification that includes all length collisions; generically its maximal vertex count is \((m+2)2^m\). Every signed atomic measure has an exact dual norm given by a one-variable concave piecewise-affine maximum computable in \(O(m\log m)\) arithmetic operations.
