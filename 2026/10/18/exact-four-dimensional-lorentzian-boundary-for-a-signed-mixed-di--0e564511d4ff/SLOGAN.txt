For nonzero symmetric \(4\times4\) reference matrices \(Z\), the signed quadratic mixed discriminant \(\mathsf D(A,A,Z,I_4)\) has exactly one positive direction if and only if the two smallest eigenvalues of \(Z\) have nonnegative sum and \(e_1(Z)e_3(Z)\ge4e_4(Z)\). This exact determinant boundary strictly extends the known sufficient balance cone, while a separate positive-definite counterexample shows complementary-pair positivity alone does not guarantee the Alexandrov–Fenchel inequality.
