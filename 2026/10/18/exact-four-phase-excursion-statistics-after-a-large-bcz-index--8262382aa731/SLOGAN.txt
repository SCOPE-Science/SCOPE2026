For the strongly mixing BCZ map, a rare index \(k\ge12\) is followed by an index \(1\) and then a run of \(2\)'s with length within a constant of \(k/4\). The exact conditional length distribution has four distinct rational regimes determined by \(k\bmod4\), including a deterministic regime and limiting split probabilities \(1/6\) and \(1/2\).
