# Review: exact four-point Fourier-support spectra on ternary vector spaces

## Correctness — PASS
The Fourier transform is defined on \(\mathbb F_3^d\) with exact four-point spatial support. Affine equivalence reduces planar supports to two geometries and independent supports to one. The annihilator-coset formula gives exact scaling with dimension. The planar zero bounds follow directly from univariate linearity; the independent case has a finite *complete* row-rank closure proof because any admissible nonzero kernel forces zero-row rank at most three. All \(3304\) generating row subsets of sizes at most three are checked over exact \(\mathbb Q(\zeta)\) arithmetic, not a numerical approximation; all five allowed counts have explicit nonzero witnesses. The standalone verification program independently checks geometry, rank closure, and witnesses. No infinite claim is inferred from empirical dimension samples.

## Originality — PASS, with material residual risks
The closest classical text, Bonami–Ghobber, Section 4 (full primary text inspected), determines finite-plane uncertainty minima and equality cases, not the complete Fourier spectra for each of three exact four-point affine support types. The published exact-five-point ternary result does not imply the four-point classification. The published three-sparse odd-prime result applies to only three nonzero spatial coefficients. The published phase-only four-rectangle result assumes equal magnitudes and explicitly acknowledges prior *unrestricted* planar parallelogram computations; those planar instances are **not claimed as standalone original advances**. The new conclusion is the affine-independent \(\mathbb F_3^3\) spectrum, its forbidden intermediate value, and the resulting all-dimensional trichotomy. The 2007 Kronecker-Fourier paper could not be fully examined; its available abstract does not decide the present finer exact-support question. A historical rank-matroid encoding of the new independent-support spectrum remains possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Four spatial points are the first possible affinely independent support in dimension three. Exact support is essential in Fourier uncertainty, because allowing zero spatial coefficients changes the achievable Fourier support sizes. The result gives a natural complete classification of **all** such four-point supports on every ternary vector space and proves a nontrivial spectral hole in the affine-independent case. It complements the recent complete five-point ternary-plane spectrum without being a parameter substitution, trivial deduction, or a reprint of the already known planar parallelogram table. The finite computation is tied to a structural rank-closure theorem and explicit witnesses, not an arbitrary numerical curiosity.

## Scope and independent status
The theorem does not extend to five-point supports or general primes; it does not classify all coefficient vectors. The exact-rank script is a finite complete certificate for the local problem; the all-dimensional extension has an independent mathematical reduction. The published 2007 full text remains an originality-access risk.
