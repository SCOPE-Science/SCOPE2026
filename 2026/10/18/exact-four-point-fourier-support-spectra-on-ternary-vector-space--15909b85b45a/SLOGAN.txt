Every exactly four-point support in \(\mathbb F_3^d\) has a complete Fourier-support spectrum determined by whether it is a line-plus-point, a planar cap, or an affine-independent tetrahedron. The affine-independent type has exactly \(3^{d-3}\{22,24,25,26,27\}\) attainable nonzero Fourier counts, exhibiting a sharp forbidden \(23\) between attainable values \(22\) and \(24\) in dimension three.
