The fixed \(\{2,3,5,7,11,13\}\)-prime certificate for repeated-digit prime sandwiches admits an exact classification: even-length cores qualify precisely when divisible by \(11\), and odd-length cores fall into five explicit residue classes modulo \(3003\). Outside the trivial divisibility-by-\(11\) case, the certified prime-free cores have lower and upper natural densities \(1/33033\) and \(10/33033\), and logarithmic density \(1/6006\).
