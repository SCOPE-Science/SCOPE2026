For every modulus \(m\ge2\), the joint densities of four consecutive Stern residues are exact sums of two truncated-geometric terms, and the number of attainable residue quadruples is \(S(m)=2A(m)-J_2(m)\) with explicit multiplicative prime-power data for \(A\). The result exposes two intersecting congruence branches not visible in pair marginals.
