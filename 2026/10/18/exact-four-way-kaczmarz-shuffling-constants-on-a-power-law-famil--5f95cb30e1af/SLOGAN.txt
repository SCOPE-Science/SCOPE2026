An explicit full-rank, row-normalized paired-row Kaczmarz family with spectral edge \(F(\lambda)\sim\lambda^\beta/2\) has exact finite-horizon errors ordered SS \(\le\) RR \(\le\) FlipFlop \(\le\) IID. For every \(\beta>0\), its sharp four-method mean-square constants scale as \(4^{-\beta}:3^{-\beta}:2^{-\beta}:2^{-\beta}\), so the IID/SS advantage is exactly \(2^\beta\) in the subcritical power-law regime.
