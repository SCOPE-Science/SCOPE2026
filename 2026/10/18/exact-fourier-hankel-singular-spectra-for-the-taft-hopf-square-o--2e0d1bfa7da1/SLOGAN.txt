For the complex Taft Hopf algebra with its monomial basis orthonormal, the second Sweedler-power operator has an exact all-degree singular spectrum given by discrete Fourier transforms of explicit quantum-binomial Gauss sums. On the first nilpotent layer, even orders have perfectly flat singular values \(\sqrt2\), while odd orders have singular values \(|1+e^{2\pi i k/n}|\) and a condition number asymptotic to \(2n/\pi\).
