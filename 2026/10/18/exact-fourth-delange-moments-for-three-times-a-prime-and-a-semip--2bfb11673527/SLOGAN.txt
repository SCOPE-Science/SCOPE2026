The unnormalized fourth Delange moment for every odd semiprime \(3p\), \(p\ge5\) prime, has an explicit two-branch rational formula determined by \(p\bmod3\), with common limit \(52/405\). More generally, for each fixed prime \(q\), the full discrepancy distribution at \(qp\) converges with Kolmogorov error at most \(2(q-1)/p\) to the difference of two independent \(q\)-piece sawtooth variables.
