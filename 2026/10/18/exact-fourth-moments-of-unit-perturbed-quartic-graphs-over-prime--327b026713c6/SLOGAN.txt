The constant-input fourth Fourier moment of \((x,x^4+cx^2)\) over every odd prime-square ring, with \(c\) a unit, has an exact quadratic-character formula. Its two residue-class values reverse their ordering when \(p\) moves between \(1\) and \(3\) modulo \(4\), and no higher digit of \(c\) affects the moment.
