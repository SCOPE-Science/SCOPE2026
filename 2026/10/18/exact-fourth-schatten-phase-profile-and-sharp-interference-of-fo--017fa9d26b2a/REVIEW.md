# Review of the fourth-order nilpotent phase profile

## Correctness — PASS
The trace identity, nilpotent filtration, Schatten product inequalities, and equality rank-one rigidity yield a proof for arbitrary Hilbert–Schmidt operators. A deterministic finite matrix checker passed 4,805 diagnostic cases. Finite experiments alone do not prove the theorem. The source definition is Pal and Chandok, arXiv:2610.11336v1.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk
The exact phase-independent formulas for graded shifts in previously published research are prior-covered and excluded from the novelty claim. Full published texts for the circle-covariant moment result and one-step nilpotent shift result were inspected; both assume stronger symmetry than the noncovariant path-plus-shortcut witnesses here. The complete 2026 motivating preprint gives general Aluthge bounds, not the present formula. The complete 2025 Sahoo text was unavailable, and historical noncommutative moment literature may contain an equivalent result; publication priority is not independently certified.

## Mathematical value — PASS
An exact all-dimensional class formula and optimal nonzero oscillation determine the first nilpotent order in which the weighted Schatten-fourth phase can matter. The trace invariant, sharp bound, and equality classification are structural, beyond a single finite calculation, and distinguish the noncovariant operator class from established phase-invariant shift results.

## Scientific limitations
Only fourth Schatten moments and fourth-power-zero Hilbert–Schmidt operators are treated; no higher nilpotency or other Schatten exponent asserted. The normal numerical checker covers finitely many matrices. No independent audit was performed.
