Every Hilbert–Schmidt operator satisfying \(T^4=0\) has an exact fourth Schatten weighted-numerical-radius formula with only one nonconstant angular harmonic, controlled by \(\operatorname{Tr}(T^3T^*)\). For \(0<t<1\), its phase oscillation is sharply at most \(\frac12 t(1-t)(t^2+(1-t)^2)\|T\|_2^4\), with equality precisely for a four-site rank-one path with a closing shortcut, up to phases, unitary similarity, and a zero summand.
