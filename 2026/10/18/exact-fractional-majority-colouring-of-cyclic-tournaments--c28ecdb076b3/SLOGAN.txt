For the standard cyclic tournament on every odd \(n\ge3\), the largest majority-stable set has size \(2\lfloor(n-1)/4\rfloor+1\), and its exact fractional majority chromatic number is \(n/(2\lfloor(n-1)/4\rfloor+1)\). The optimum alternates below and above \(2\) with the parity class of \(n\pmod4\), while ordinary majority colouring takes exactly two or three colours respectively.
