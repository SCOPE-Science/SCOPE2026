For the symmetric bidirection of any unequal clique bouquet \(K_1\vee\bigsqcup_i K_{s_i}\), the exact fractional majority colouring number is \(\max_i (s_i+1)/(\lfloor s_i/2\rfloor+1)\). A single odd peripheral clique forces the value \(2\); if all are even, only the largest clique determines the smaller exact value.
