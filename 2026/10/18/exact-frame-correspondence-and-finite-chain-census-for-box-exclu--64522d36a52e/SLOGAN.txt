On intuitionistic birelational frames, \(\Box\varphi\vee\neg\Box\varphi\) holds for all persistent valuations exactly when comparable worlds have the same upward closure of their modal successor sets; testing \(\Box p\vee\neg\Box p\) already suffices. On an \(n\)-world chain this permits exactly \(1+((n+1)^n-1)/n\) labelled relations, and the previously sufficient stronger frame condition first fails to be necessary on two worlds.
