For the unit-height triangular B-spline on the near-critical Gabor lattice \(a=1\), \(b=m/(m+1)\), every integer \(m\ge2\) has exact optimal frame bounds \(A_m=2(m^2-1)/m^3\) and \(B_m=(m+1)/m\). Finite Jacobi fibers and an explicit binomial ground state show that the lower bound vanishes with sharp linear slope \(2\) as \(b\uparrow1\).
