For Gaussian time–frequency concentration on a single annulus of fixed area, the optimal Hermite mode changes exactly at \(a_m=w/(e^{w/m}-1)\), with an explicit positive corner in the concentration norm at every transition. The complete next-order large-radius norm is governed by the nonconstant periodic factor \(B_2(\{a+w/2\})\), yielding a sharp interval of oscillatory accumulation values rather than a universal constant correction.
