For complete graphs with a matching of missing edges, the graphical zonotope's entire Gaussian vertex-angle spectrum consists of exact powers \(2^s/n!\) with explicit factorial-sum multiplicities. When the number of deleted edges is proportional to dimension, Gaussian-selected and uniformly selected vertices see different Poisson laws, with parameters \(2\rho\) and \(\rho\), and the exact collision probability is asymptotic to \(e^{2\rho}/n!\).
