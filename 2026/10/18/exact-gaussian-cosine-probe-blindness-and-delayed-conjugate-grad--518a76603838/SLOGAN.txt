A flattened Gaussian cosine probe can converge to perfect apparent alignment even when a positive-definite diagonal system resists an arbitrarily growing number of conjugate-gradient steps on a suitable right-hand side. The exact two-eigenvalue loss follows a beta-transform law with a sharp visibility scale \(K\asymp\sqrt{n/r}\), unchanged by adding arbitrarily many global probes; a separate Chebyshev spectrum produces the rigorous solver obstruction.
