# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The unique gap factorization has a direct necessary-and-sufficient criterion for scattered \(a b^t a\)-avoidance: at most \(t-1\) interior letters \(b\). Counting each interior letter according to its preceding \(a\)-count gives the stated complete-homogeneous/Gaussian coefficient. In the chamber \(A>C\) and \(B\ge C\), trailing letters contribute too many \(ab\) pairs; the remaining part-index multiset is exactly a restricted partition of \(C\). The unrestricted Gaussian denominator is the ordinary finite rectangle partition count. The asymptotic explicitly uses fixed \(t\), the dominant pole of \(\prod_{j=1}^{t-1}(1-q^j)^{-1}\), and the classical Hardy–Ramanujan formula. A separate exhaustive direct word-enumeration checker agrees with every coefficient in 252 parameter triples and 320 chamber instances. No finite experiment is treated as proof of the infinite result.

## Originality — PASS, with historical-priority risk

The 2026 fixed-Parikh-matrix subsequence-probability paper studies the general question, including enumeration and approximation, without stating an all-parameter exact distribution for the target family \(a b^t a\), \(t\ge3\), or the sharp stable-chamber asymptotic. Richomme's earlier partition correspondence and Atanasiu's gap methods provide ingredients, not the conditional enumerator or its boundary law. Elementary length-four special cases are excluded by \(t\ge3\). Searches for the precise pattern, equivalent gap formulas, conditional Gaussian coefficients, and restricted-partition formulations disclosed no more general prior theorem. A potentially implicit or unindexed equivalent calculation remains a concrete risk; a failed search is not a proof of priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The parameter-dependent pattern-avoidance distribution directly addresses a natural quantitative gap in the recent theory of fixed Parikh matrices. The result gives a closed, finite Gaussian expression for every class, a surprisingly independent-of-\(A,B\) low-count region, an exact conditional probability and a sharp asymptotic constant. It goes beyond simply restating unrestricted partition enumeration, as the target pattern imposes a nontrivial cutoff on interior multiplicities. The infinite-family result supplies a benchmark for more complex scattered-subsequence laws.
