In every fixed binary Parikh-matrix class, avoidance of scattered \(a b^t a\) for \(t\ge3\) has an exact Gaussian-polynomial enumerator. When \(A>C\) and \(B\ge C\), its conditional avoidance probability is exactly \(p_{\le t-1}(C)/p(C)\), with sharp fixed-\(t\) exponential asymptotics as \(C\to\infty\).
