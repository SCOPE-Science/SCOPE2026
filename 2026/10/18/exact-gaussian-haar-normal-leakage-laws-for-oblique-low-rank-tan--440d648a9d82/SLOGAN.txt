For the two-sided oblique tangent projector used in randomized low-rank dynamics, the expected squared leakage of any fixed normal residual is exactly \((a_L+a_R+a_La_R)\) times its squared Frobenius norm, with explicit and different Gaussian and Haar row-sketch factors. The mean is infinite at minimally oversampled Gaussian sketches (even when they are square), while full Haar row-orthogonal sketches give zero leakage; the exact worst-case realized-sketch amplification is also determined.
