For unrestricted scalar depth-one Anderson acceleration, any fixed nonzero Gaussian oracle noise yields an exact \(T^{-1}\) output tail and destroys all absolute moments of order at least one, despite the underlying fixed-point map being a strict contraction. Two explicit ridge normalizations restore finite moments, but their expected squared errors diverge at the sharp rate \(\lambda^{-1/2}\) with computable, different constants as regularization vanishes.
