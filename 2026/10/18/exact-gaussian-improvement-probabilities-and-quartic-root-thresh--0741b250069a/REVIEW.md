# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** A direct orthogonal decomposition leaves a two-dimensional rank-one update and an unchanged bulk. Its generalized eigenvalues give the rational spectral condition number; a sign-preserving inequality gives the sharp improvement criterion. Independent Gaussian squares give the exact beta CDF, while the law of large numbers and continuity of the one-degree-of-freedom chi-square distribution prove all critical-window limits. The result deliberately excludes adaptive scaling and general spectra. The full proof, endpoint cases and executable small-matrix tests are supplied.

**Originality — PASS, with residual bibliographic risk.** The motivating 2026 paper develops covariance-informed right-hand-side-difference sketches, Nyström REVD and inexact spectral limited-memory preconditioners; these mechanisms are prior art. The published Nyström PCG analysis of Frangella, Tropp and Udell uses an alternate scaling and proves rank/effective-dimension condition-number bounds; the mixed-precision work of Carson and Daužickaitė and the Giraud–Gratton sensitivity analysis address other regimes. The inspected material did not imply the present exact event probability, impossibility region and contrasting high-dimensional threshold exponents. Published mathematical result queries for rank-one spiked Gaussian sketches, Nyström spectral improvement, and EDA covariance-informed preconditioners returned related but non-covering results. A differently parameterized or unindexed probability calculation remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** The paper's rationale for using an inexpensive covariance-informed sketch is precisely improved spectral capture without sequential power products. The exact criterion measures something downstream of capture—the chance that the **actual symmetrically preconditioned Hessian becomes better conditioned**—and reveals that its unscaled choice can fail for every probe even with perfect spike alignment. The two distinct critical exponents and finite success ordering quantify the sketch's value and boundaries. This is a meaningful calibration benchmark, not a claim of better finite-step CG, whose behavior is trivial on a two-eigenvalue matrix.

**Residual risk.** The mathematical model is deliberately restricted to a full-rank two-plateau Hessian, a fixed single Gaussian probe, and the unscaled inverse-Nyström preconditioner. Earlier literature may contain equivalent spectral-probability statements under different names. No independent validation has occurred.
