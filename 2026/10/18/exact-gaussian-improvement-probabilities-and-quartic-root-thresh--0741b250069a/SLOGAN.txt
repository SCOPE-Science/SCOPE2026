For one-probe unscaled Nyström preconditioning of a two-plateau Hessian, the exact chance of improving the spectral condition number is an incomplete-beta tail, and improvement is impossible at any sketch orientation unless \(1+\lambda>(1+\mu)^2\). As dimension grows, the covariance-informed Gaussian sketch crosses its improvement threshold at spike height \(m^{1/4}\), versus \(m^{1/2}\) for an isotropic Gaussian sketch.
