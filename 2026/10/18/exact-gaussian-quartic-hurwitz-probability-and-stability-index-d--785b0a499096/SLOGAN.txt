The Gaussian quartic stability probability is exactly \(\frac1{16}-\frac1{2\pi^2}\int_0^{\pi/2}\arctan(\sin^2\theta/\cos\theta)\,d\theta=0.0092838447242981189\ldots\), yielding the full fourth-order stability-index distribution. This converts a previously Monte-Carlo-estimated natural stability probability into an exact one-dimensional elementary quadrature.
