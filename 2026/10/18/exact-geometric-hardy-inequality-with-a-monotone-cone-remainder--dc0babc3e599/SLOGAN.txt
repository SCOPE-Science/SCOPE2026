For every geometric weight \(\pi_n=(1-\rho)\rho^{n-1}\) and every \(2\le p\le\infty\), the sharp cumulative-sum Hardy constant on nonnegative decreasing sequences is \(\sqrt{1+\rho}/(1-\rho)\), attained only by constants. An exact positive remainder in the successive drops quantifies the loss from nonconstancy and distinguishes this cone norm from the unrestricted weighted Hilbert norm.
