For a clique bouquet \(K_1\vee\bigsqcup_i K_{m_i}\), the vertex-order greedy maximal-independent-set law is exactly uniform if and only if \(\prod_i m_i=\sum_i m_i\), with total-variation bias \(|\prod_i m_i-\sum_i m_i|/((1+\sum_i m_i)(1+\prod_i m_i))\). An explicit infinite connected family has perfectly uniform output among sets of sizes one and arbitrarily large \(q\).
