A Haar-uniform plane through the center of a regular dodecahedron almost surely cuts out a hexagon, octagon, or decagon, with respective probabilities \(\frac5\pi[2\pi-3\arccos(1/3)-3\arccos(\sqrt5/3)]\), \(\frac{15}\pi[2\arccos(1/3)+\arccos(\sqrt5/3)-\pi]\), and \(\frac3\pi[2\pi-5\arccos(1/3)]\). An exhaustive exact ten-great-circle classification proves the law and its normal-chamber geometry.
