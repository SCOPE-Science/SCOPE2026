A Haar-random central section of the regular truncated tetrahedron is almost surely a quadrilateral, pentagon, or hexagon, with exact probabilities expressed using \(\arccos(1/11)\), \(\arccos(5/11)\), \(\arccos(7/11)\), and \(\arccos(9/11)\). A complete exact spherical-chamber census comprises \(134\) cells in six area classes, yielding the independent mean \(18\arccos(7/11)/\pi\).
