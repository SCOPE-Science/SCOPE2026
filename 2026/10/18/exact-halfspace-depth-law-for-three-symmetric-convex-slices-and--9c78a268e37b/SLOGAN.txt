For three centered, centrally symmetric convex slices, the exact maximal halfspace depth is determined by the three slice volumes and a single outer-slice halfspace-cap discrepancy; the discrepancy's excess vanishes exactly when the outer slices are nested. In every dimension \(d\ge2\), equal-volume slices have normalized maximal depth strictly above \(1/3\), and mutually transverse thin rectangles show that this constant is sharp.
