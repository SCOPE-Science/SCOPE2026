# Scientific review: exact heat-noise identity on a four-spin ring

## Correctness — PASS

The model, heat sign, and normalization are fixed explicitly: \(Q_t\) sums the system-energy changes at even-site flips divided by \(4J\). Strict positivity of every spin-flip rate for \(0\le a,b<1\) gives irreducibility. Four generator identities close the expectations of nearest-neighbor spin sums, same-sublattice pairs, and the four-spin product, yielding the exact stationary moments needed in the proof. The explicitly centered Poisson corrector \(h=(U+bV-aW)/8-\langle(U+bV-aW)/8\rangle\) satisfies \(Lh=j-f\). Differentiation of the finite-state tilted generator then gives the signed-heat variance formula; every algebraic step is verified symbolically on all 16 states. Eight independently solved exact rational generators and eigenvector-derivative systems agree. The limits are long-time cumulant rates; the finite parameter tests alone do not establish the full parameter domain.

## Originality — PASS subject to historical search limits

Schmüser and Schmittmann (arXiv:cond-mat/0206345), including their Section V, established the model and mean heat transfer. Schmittmann and Schmüser (arXiv:cond-mat/0212616), including the finite-ring appendix, established arbitrary stationary equal-time spin correlations. These prior results cover static information and the mean; those components are not counted as new. The explicit second heat cumulant, nonlinear noise identity \(\nu=(1-\bar\gamma^2)/2+j^2/4\), and exact infinite-hot-bath unit-dispersion boundary require time-correlation/Poisson information not supplied by those inspected formulas. Lebowitz and Spohn (arXiv:cond-mat/9811220) provide general fluctuation machinery, not the model-specific polynomial evaluation. Searches for heat-current variance, second cumulants, full counting statistics, and aliases of the two-bath Glauber chain did not uncover an exact equivalent. A separately indexed small-ring or tilted-generator calculation could cover it; the search does not prove novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Heat-current variance is a natural kinetic observable: equal-time spin moments and mean entropy production alone do not quantify trajectory-to-trajectory uncertainty. For the smallest simple periodic ring supporting alternating two-site temperature sublattices, the result gives an unexpectedly exact nonlinear excess-noise law with no perturbative assumption. The explicit signed-heat dispersion threshold supplies a falsifiable finite-size benchmark for counting-statistics approximations and complements known static solutions. The precise claim is finite and does not pretend to establish a many-site thermodynamic law.

## Scope and unresolved risks

The result assumes four sites, positive-rate alternating Glauber flips, and signed heat normalized by \(4J\). Only the first two asymptotic heat cumulants are evaluated. At strictly zero bath temperature irreducibility can fail; no boundary uniqueness is claimed. An equivalent historical calculation may have escaped the inspected literature; independent bibliographic and scientific review are outstanding.
