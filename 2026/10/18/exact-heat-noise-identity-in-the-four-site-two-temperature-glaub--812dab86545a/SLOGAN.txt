On the smallest simple alternating-bath Glauber Ising ring, the long-time signed heat-current variance obeys the exact nonlinear identity \(\nu=(1-\bar\gamma^2)/2+j^2/4\) for every \(0\le a,b<1\), with \(j=(b-a)/2\) and \(\bar\gamma=(a+b)/2\). If one bath is infinitely hot, the net-heat dispersion \(\nu/j\) crosses the unit-Poisson benchmark at exactly \(b=2\sqrt6-4\).
