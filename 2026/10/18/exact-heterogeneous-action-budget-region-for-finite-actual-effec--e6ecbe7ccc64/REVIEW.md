# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof treats all \(k\ge2\) and positive integer budgets. The mixed-radix pivot surjectivity argument remains valid at unequal moduli and at \(b_i=1\). The construction recovers each exact actual-effectivity neighbourhood by separate inclusion proofs; the singleton full-range frame proves the necessary complementary-product bounds. The standalone script independently recomputed the original deterministic game semantics for 1,152 generated realizations and 15,920 coordinate-fixed slices. Finite tests are not the universal proof.

## Originality — PASS with residual risks
Ciardelli's 2026 representation theorem is qualitative. The closest published quantitative results give \(r\) action copies per neighbourhood, or a sharp **common** \(\lceil r^{1/(k-1)}\rceil\) copies for every agent. Their complete publicly available mathematical statements do not imply universal sufficiency at nonuniform vectors such as \((3,4,4)\) when \(r=12\). The latter result proves the necessary product lower bound in the singleton-range example, but only constructs equal copies, leaving the mixed-budget sufficiency not covered. Searches for actual effectivity, prescribed unequal action budgets, mixed hyperplane colors and equivalent arrays found no stronger exact claim. An older coalitional source and general mixed-level-array literature remain residual priority risks, not an assertion of absolute uniqueness.

## Value — PASS
Exact action-copy feasibility under heterogeneous per-agent resources is a motivated optimization of a natural representation theorem. The complete Pareto condition plus explicit mixed-modulus construction is a structural theorem, not a routine increase in an earlier scalar constant. It subsumes the known symmetric worst-case action bound and gives strictly asymmetric feasible allocations.

## Limitations and closest literature
The condition is universally sharp, not necessary for each individual frame. Finite deterministic outcomes and equal repetition counts within each agent's individual-neighbourhood class are essential. The relevant comparisons are Ciardelli (arXiv:2606.31866), the published local-range compression result (record 4bce89f50bf5), and the published root-size result (record 114a0e0912e6).
