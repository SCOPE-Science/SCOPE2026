For deterministic finite actual-effectivity frames with \(k\ge2\) agents and \(r\) locally reachable outcomes, **every** representable frame can be realized with exactly \(b_i\) action copies per neighbourhood of agent \(i\) if and only if \(\prod_{j\ne i}b_j\ge r\) for every \(i\). A mixed-modulus hyperplane-surjective construction attains every feasible asymmetric budget vector, strictly extending the known equal-copy root bound.
