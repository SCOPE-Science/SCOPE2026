The real transportation space of the cocktail-party graph on \(2k\) vertices has exact squared Euclidean Banach--Mazur distance \(3\) at \(k=2\), \(10/3\) at \(k=3\), and \(k\) for every \(k\ge4\). The optimal symmetry-invariant Hilbert geometry becomes the canonical centered Euclidean geometry sharply at \(k=4\), while the two smaller cases require anisotropic ellipsoids.
