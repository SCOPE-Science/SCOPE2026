# Review of the exact symmetric-tensor Hilbert series

## Correctness

**PASS.** The proof fixes the quotient-projectivization convention and checks \(f_*\mathcal O_M(C_+)=\mathcal O_E\oplus L^*\) and the vanishing of the relative first direct image. The nontriviality of the extension is independently forced by its restriction to the negative section, as proved in Denisi–Höring Proposition 3.5. The Čech description separates terms by degree in the positive-base-degree summand. In each degree, the remaining two-variable translation representation is one unipotent Jordan block, yielding \((L^*)^{\otimes j}\otimes\mathcal F_{m-j+1}\). On an elliptic curve, the degree-zero block has one section and each positively twisted block has the number of sections given by its degree. Their sum equals \(1+d\binom{m+2}{3}\). Equation (10) of Denisi–Höring transfers every graded dimension from \(\mathcal F\) to the tangent bundle. The packaged exact-arithmetic checker confirms relevant finite-dimensional and combinatorial identities. Finite checks do not establish the all-degree theorem by themselves.

## Originality

**PASS, subject to residual priority risk.** The closest originating paper, Denisi–Höring, proves non-finite generation and provides the auxiliary bundle and graded-ring isomorphism, but does not formulate its all-degree direct-image splitting or exact Hilbert series. Beauville–Liu surveys symmetric-tensor algebras, including the genus-at-least-two ruled surface setting, rather than this split elliptic unipotent configuration. A targeted search for equivalent Hilbert functions, direct-image formulas, and implicit stronger statements found no exact duplicate. The formula uses classical Atiyah theory, so it may be obtainable from general treatments of unipotent extensions; an unindexed earlier explicit computation is a residual risk. Non-finite generation alone does not determine a Hilbert series, and the preceding sources do not state the new formula.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The first new geometric invariant is the complete graded dimension sequence for a natural non-finitely generated symmetric-tensor algebra, uniformly over arbitrary negative degree. The structural direct-image decomposition explains why its Hilbert series is rational and measures the cubic growth exactly. This gives a useful contrast between finite-generation behavior and Hilbert growth, and refines a recently studied ruled-surface example without claiming the algebra itself has been presented by generators and relations.

## Closest literature and limitations

Denisi–Höring, arXiv:2610.03048v1, supplies the source configuration and the graded-ring comparison. Beauville–Liu, arXiv:2309.12888, provides broader symmetric-tensor context. Atiyah's elliptic bundle theory provides the indecomposable unipotent blocks. General implicit coverage by an unlocated prior formula has not been excluded. No conclusion beyond the split complex elliptic case is established.
