For a split elliptic ruled surface \(M=\mathbb P_E(\mathcal O_E\oplus L)\) with \(\deg L=-d<0\), its symmetric-tensor algebra has the exact Hilbert series \(\frac1{1-t}+\frac{dt}{(1-t)^4}\), even though the algebra is not finitely generated. The calculation follows from a complete Atiyah-bundle decomposition of every direct image of the auxiliary symmetric power.
