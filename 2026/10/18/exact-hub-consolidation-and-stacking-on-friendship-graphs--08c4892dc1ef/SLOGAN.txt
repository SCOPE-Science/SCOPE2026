For a friendship graph of \(k\ge2\) triangles sharing one hub, an explicit sum of local parity penalties decides exactly which pebble configurations can be consolidated at the hub, and—apart from configurations already stacked—which can be consolidated anywhere. Consequently the sharp stacking number is \(\operatorname{stack}(F_k)=4k\), improving the general independence-number lower bound for every \(k\ge2\).
