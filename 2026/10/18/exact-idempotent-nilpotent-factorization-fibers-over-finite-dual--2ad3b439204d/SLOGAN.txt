For \(2\times2\) matrices over every finite dual-number ring \(\mathbb F_q[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)\), the number of ordered idempotent–nilpotent factorizations is classified exactly by the reduction rank, trace, determinant, and, for radical targets, coefficient rank. Both factor orders have identical fibers, with multiplicities ranging from zero to \(q^6(q+2)+1\).
