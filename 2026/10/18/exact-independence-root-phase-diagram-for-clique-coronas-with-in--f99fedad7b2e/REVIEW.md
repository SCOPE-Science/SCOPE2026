# Scientific review: clique-corona independence-root phase diagram

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof isolates the known corona factor \((1+z)^{k(r-1)}\), identifies all imaginary-axis crossings of \((1+z)^k+r z\) by phase and modulus, and verifies each crossing is simple with a strictly positive horizontal derivative. Constancy of degree and continuity complete the all-parameter zero count. An independent exact-arithmetic Routh implementation agrees in 940 parameter cases, and brute force matches the independence polynomial for 108748 vertex subsets; these finite checks do not replace the proof.

**Originality — PASS with residual bibliographic risk.** Brown--Cameron (2018) gives the general corona polynomial and eventual nonstability at sufficiently large degree; these do not determine the exact threshold or full count for arbitrary fixed leaf number. Hibi--Kara--Vien (2026) studies whiskering polynomials and symmetry rather than half-plane roots. Zhang--Tu (2026) proves spider stability, covering the star endpoint but not multiple joined core vertices. Targeted searches yielded no equivalent phase-count theorem. A broader, differently named result outside inspected records is a residual risk, not evidence of uniqueness.

**Value — PASS.** The result closes a two-parameter stability decision for a natural infinite graph family, including all boundary multiplicities and repeated changes in the number of roots in the right half-plane. It quantitatively refines known qualitative corona constructions rather than reasserting the known polynomial formula.

**Closest literature and limitations.** Brown--Cameron (2018) Theorem 3.2, Proposition 3.1 and Lemma 2.1; Hibi--Kara--Vien (2026) Section 3; Zhang--Tu (2026) Theorem 4. The result does not locate every complex root. Priority remains subject to independent bibliographic review, and independent mathematical validation has not been performed.
