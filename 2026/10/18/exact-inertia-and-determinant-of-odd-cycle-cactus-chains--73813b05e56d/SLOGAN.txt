For every chain of at least three odd-cycle blocks coalesced at distinct successive cut vertices, the line-graph adjacency matrix is nonsingular and has an exact inertia determined solely by the total order, number of blocks, and whether any block length is \(1\pmod4\). Its determinant is \(2^t\) if all block lengths are \(3\pmod4\), and \((-1)^{q-1}2^{t-1}(4q-2)\) otherwise, where \(q\) counts blocks of length \(1\pmod4\).
