The period-doubling word has an exact all-length first-repeat law: its first repeated factor begins at either \(3\cdot2^{k-1}\) or \(2^{k+1}\), according to the length interval, and repeats a factor beginning at \(2^k\) or at the origin, respectively. Its initial non-repetitive complexity has normalized lower and upper limit \(1\) and \(3/2\).
