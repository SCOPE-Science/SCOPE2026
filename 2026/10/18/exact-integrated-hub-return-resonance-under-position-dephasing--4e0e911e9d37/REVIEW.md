# Scientific review: integrated universal-hub return under dephasing

## Correctness — PASS

For every integer \(k\geq2\), the structural Laplacian block relation \(L_F\mathbf1=0\) closes the Lindblad evolution on a five-real-parameter operator subspace. Direct entrywise computation gives a three-variable linear system for normalized hub contrast. Its resolvent and cubic characteristic polynomial are explicit; the strict Routh–Hurwitz inequality implies exponential decay and permits evaluating the integrated correlation at zero Laplace frequency. Strict convexity proves the unique optimum. Distinct-root perturbation and residue estimates give the uniform large-degree limit. A separately constructed full density-matrix verifier passes 970 finite checks across multiple leaf topologies and noise rates. Finite numerical checks are not the argument for all graphs.

## Originality — PASS, with priority uncertainty

Razzoli, Bordone and Paris (2022; arXiv:2202.13824) prove noiseless universal-vertex dynamics; this does not imply Lindblad dephasing universality or the zero-frequency noise optimum. Bressanini, Benedetti and Paris (2022; DOI:10.1007/s11128-022-03647-x) investigate different noise-sensitive quantum-classical distances and qualitatively note a reversal in convergence behavior. Adithya L J et al. (2025; arXiv:2507.17880) numerically observe hub/complete coincidence under Haken–Strobl noise and fit coherence relaxation times. Those observations are fully acknowledged. A further full-text comparison with Luppi–Benedetti–Smirne (2025/2026) concerns a different multitime Kolmogorov metric, and Boro (2026) numerically optimizes a different transport-efficiency statistic. No exact graph-independent signed return-integral formula or all-degree optimizer was found in the inspected original texts or targeted mathematical-record and scholarly searches. Earlier unindexed or implicit derivations are still possible, and search absence is not a proof of priority.

## Value — PASS

The return integral is a natural zero-frequency stationary autocorrelation for the hub occupancy projector, not an arbitrarily chosen parameter statistic. It measures cumulative memory of localized preparation and has two competing singular regimes: coherent weak-noise slowing and quantum-Zeno strong-noise slowing. The exact optimum scales proportionally to graph degree, and the full-density operator universality applies even when the induced leaf graph has no automorphisms. The uniform exponential scaling law supplies a sharp, nontrivial asymptotic relaxation profile. The metric is a signed integrated correlation, not a total-variation mixing time or a guarantee for other quantum-walk observables.
