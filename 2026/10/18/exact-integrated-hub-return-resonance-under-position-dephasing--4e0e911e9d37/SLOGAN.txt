For any graph with a universal vertex, position-dephased Laplacian quantum dynamics launched at that vertex has the same full state trajectory regardless of the remaining edges. Its normalized signed integrated excess return is exactly \(T_k(\gamma)=\gamma/[2(k+1)]+(k-1)/(2\gamma)\), uniquely minimized at \(\gamma=\sqrt{k^2-1}\); for dephasing \(\gamma=\theta k\), the return contrast converges uniformly to \(\exp[-2\theta t/(1+\theta^2)]\).
