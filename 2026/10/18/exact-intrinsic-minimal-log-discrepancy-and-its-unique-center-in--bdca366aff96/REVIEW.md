## Correctness

PASS. The proof computes the marked pair’s Cartier index, establishes a uniform positive discrepancy floor and turns the local Cartier indices of the coordinate divisor into an all-valuation lower bound. An explicit primitive quotient-lattice valuation attains equality, and larger local bounds exclude all centers except the heaviest coordinate point. The exact verifier supports the formula in small cases; it is not extrapolated as an infinite proof.

## Originality

PASS with literature risk. Bie–Du–Yu, Proposition 9.1, and Ambro, Example 5.3, state the marked-pair threshold and give the weights but do not explicitly classify the boundary-free minimum or its unique center. General toric age formulas are prior foundations and could make the formula implicitly deducible; this risk remains. Searches for equivalent statements and stronger coverage found no exact published result.

## Value

PASS. The intrinsic global minimal log discrepancy is a standard birational singularity invariant central to the sharp-volume discussion, whereas the published threshold is attached to an auxiliary boundary. Its exact value, and identification of the unique minimizing center, determine the actual singularity scale throughout a natural two-parameter family and give an intrinsic normalization of the sharp volume growth. This is not a general theorem about all Fano varieties.
