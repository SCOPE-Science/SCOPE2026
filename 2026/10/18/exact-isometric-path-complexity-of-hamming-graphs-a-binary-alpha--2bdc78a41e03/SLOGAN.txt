For every dimension \(d\geq1\), the binary cube \(Q_d\) has isometric-path complexity exactly \(\lceil(d+1)/2\rceil\), whereas every Hamming graph \(H(d,q)\) with alphabet size \(q\geq3\) has complexity exactly \(d+1\). A two-layer zigzag forces the binary lower bound, while a diametral geodesic lying entirely in the farthest sphere forces the nonbinary bound.
