Uniform points in the real Lipschitz-free unit ball of an \(n\)-point equilateral metric have an exact hypergeometric–Dirichlet sampler and covariance \(4(2n-1)/[n(n+1)^2]\) times the identity on the canonical zero-sum subspace. Their isotropic constants tend to \(1/(\sqrt2 e)\), while \(\sqrt n((n/8)\|X\|_2^2-1)\) converges to a standard Gaussian.
