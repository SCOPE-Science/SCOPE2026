For every dimension \(d\ge2\), the unique maximal-volume ellipsoid inside a unit spherical sector has an explicit two-branch formula, with a sharp contact transition at aperture \(\theta=\arcsin(d^{-1/2})\). Below this angle its only spherical contact is the axial tip; above it, contacts form a nonaxial latitude sphere and the logarithmic optimal volume has a second-derivative jump of \(-2d\).
