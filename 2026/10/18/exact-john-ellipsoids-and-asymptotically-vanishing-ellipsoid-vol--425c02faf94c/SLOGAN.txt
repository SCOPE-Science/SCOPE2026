The unit-ball hull of two points has an exact John ellipsoid in every dimension, with contacts at the universal normalized axial latitudes \(\pm1/\sqrt m\). For fixed endpoint separation its spindle-to-John volume ratio is \(1+\sqrt{1-d^2}/(4m)+O_d(m^{-2})\), so the ellipsoid captures asymptotically all volume with a sharp first correction.
