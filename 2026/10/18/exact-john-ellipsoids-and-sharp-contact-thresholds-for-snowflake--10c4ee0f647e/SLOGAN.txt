For the real Lipschitz dual of every snowflaked complete bipartite metric with \(2\le p\le q\), the unique John ellipsoid has an exact three-regime semiaxis formula with within-class contact thresholds \(t=\sqrt{2q/(2q-1)}\) and \(t=\sqrt{2q/(p+q-1)}\). After the second threshold its shape and volume stop changing even as the underlying unit ball continues to enlarge strictly; the thresholds coincide for \(p=q\).
