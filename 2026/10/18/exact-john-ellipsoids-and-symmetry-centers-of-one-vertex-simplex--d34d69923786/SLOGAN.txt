For the simplex with one vertex truncated at total-coordinate depth \(t\), the John ellipsoid stays completely unchanged until the sharp threshold \((n-1)/(n+1)\), after which every facet touches and its axes change by exact formulas. The body simultaneously has unique Minkowski center \(\mathbf{1}/(n+1-t)\) and symmetry coefficient \(1/(n-t)\), while its exact John-centered rounding factor transitions from \(n\) to \(\sqrt{1+(n-1)^2/t}\).
