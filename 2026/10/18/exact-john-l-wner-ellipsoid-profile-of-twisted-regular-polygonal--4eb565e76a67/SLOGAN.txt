The John ellipsoid of two equal regular polygonal bases twisted through an arbitrary angle has horizontal semiaxis \(R\sqrt{\cos(\pi/n)\cos(\pi/n-\delta)}\), while the Löwner ellipsoid does not depend on the twist. The antiprism uniquely maximizes the fraction occupied by the John ellipsoid; its improvement over the prism is exactly \(3/(1+2\cos(\pi/n))\).
