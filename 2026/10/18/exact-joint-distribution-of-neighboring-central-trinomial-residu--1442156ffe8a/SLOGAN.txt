Neighboring central trinomial numbers modulo five realize every one of the sixteen nonzero residue pairs already on the interval \(0\le n<25\). Their exact joint frequencies on \(0\le n<5^L\) admit a closed sixteen-cell formula, and the sharp total-variation discrepancy from uniformity is \((4L+1)/(2\cdot5^L)\) for every \(L\ge2\).
