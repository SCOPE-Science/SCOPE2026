In a Brownian gas subjected to uniform batch resetting, the stationary probability that \(k\) tagged particles all move farther than \(z\) decays with exact logarithmic exponent \(\sqrt{rkq_k/D}\), where \(q_k\) is the chance that one reset batch hits at least one tag. Dividing by the single-particle exponent gives a strictly decreasing diagnostic of batch size, from \(k\) for independent single-particle resets to \(\sqrt{k}\) for fully synchronized resets.
