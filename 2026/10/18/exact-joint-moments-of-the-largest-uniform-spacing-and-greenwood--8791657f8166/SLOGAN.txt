For \(n\ge2\) uniform spacings, the largest gap and Greenwood's sum of squared gaps have exact positive covariance \((H_n^2+H_n^{(2)}-2H_n)/(n(n+1)(n+2))\), but their Pearson correlation decays sharply as \((\sqrt6/(2\pi))(\log n)^2/\sqrt n\). An explicit finite inclusion–exclusion polynomial also gives the mean Greenwood score after any admissible largest-gap threshold.
