# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The group-algebra/exterior-algebra isomorphism is an explicit basis identification in characteristic two. Perfect exterior duality gives the two rank-one boundary blocks and a dual pair of contraction-rank maps, yielding the exact Jordan partition. The contraction-rank gap at four follows from four-dimensional volume duality; explicit full-support forms in dimensions three and five through seven, together with direct sums, prove every remaining possible rank. Möbius inversion over subspace lattices proves every multiplicity, without relying on experiments. Exhaustion of all \(2^{20}\) binary six-variable cubic forms, 360 direct matrix checks, and recurrence checks through dimension eleven provide additional independent consistency evidence.

## Originality

**PASS subject to stated literature limits.** Carlson–Friedlander–Pevtsova study generic or constant Jordan types rather than complete distributions for near-socle elements in the regular module. Cohen–Helminck and Hora–Pudlák give fixed-dimensional trilinear-form classifications, covering their special cases but not, in the inspected statements, the all-dimensional formula. Published-result searches for trilinear contraction radicals, equivalent group-algebra formulations, exterior multiplication and Gaussian enumeration did not find the global statement, and accessible prior claims were compared by implication. Residual risk: the uninspected full text of the specialized dimension-eight classification and broader combinatorial counting literature might contain stronger implicit generalizations; search non-detection is not proof of novelty.

## Value

**PASS.** Exact multiplicities of Jordan types across an entire natural homogeneous component of a modular group algebra are a motivated representation-theoretic invariant. The all-dimension forbidden rank, complete set of attainable partitions, and frequency formulas work without the intractable orbit-by-orbit classification of alternating three-forms. The result is a global natural classification rather than an isolated case or routine small count.

## Limits and closest literature

The theorem does not classify inhomogeneous elements, other graded degrees, odd characteristic, arbitrary modules, or general trilinear-form orbits. Finite-dimensional and binary instances can be inferred from existing orbit classifications. Complete bibliographic coverage and independent mathematical validation are not claimed.
