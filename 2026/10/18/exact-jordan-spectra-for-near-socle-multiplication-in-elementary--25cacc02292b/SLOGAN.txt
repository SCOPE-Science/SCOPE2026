For every \(q=2^e\) and \(n\ge6\), the Jordan partition of multiplication by a homogeneous degree-\(n-3\) element of \(\mathbb F_q[(C_2)^n]\) is completely determined by an alternating trilinear contraction rank, with each partition occurring an explicit Gaussian-sum number of times. Multiplication rank \(10\) never occurs, whereas all permitted larger contraction ranks do.
