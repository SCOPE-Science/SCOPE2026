For an undirected cycle with one exceptional edge weight \(w\) and a single actuator at its endpoint, the exact Kalman determinant is \((-1)^{n+1}(w-1)^{\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor}(1+(n-1)w)\). Every positive edge-weight defect from the uniform value restores complete controllability, but the smallest finite-Krylov Gramian eigenvalue vanishes quadratically as \(w\to1\).
