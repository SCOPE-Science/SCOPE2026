For every order \(n\ge4\), the derangement permutation polytope has a unique Frobenius Löwner ellipsoid, exactly the ball of radius \(\sqrt{n(n-2)/(n-1)}\) centered at the uniform zero-diagonal doubly stochastic matrix. An explicit positive mixture of only two derangement conjugacy classes is isotropic on the entire affine tangent space and certifies globally optimal enclosing-ellipsoid volume, despite its two inequivalent symmetry modes; every derangement vertex contacts the ball.
