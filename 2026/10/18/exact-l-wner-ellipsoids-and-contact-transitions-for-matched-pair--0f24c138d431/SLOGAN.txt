For \(2m\) points in \(m\) metric pairs, the unique Löwner ellipsoid of their real Lipschitz dual ball stays exactly equilateral throughout \(1\le t\le2\) whenever \(2m\ge8\), despite strict growth of the ball. At six points its sharp anisotropic transition is \(t=5/3\); all ellipsoid axes and contact-vertex counts are explicit.
