For every regular \(k\)-simplex thickened by a unit Euclidean ball in dimension \(n>k\ge2\), the unique minimum-volume enclosing ellipsoid has explicit two-axis semiaxes determined by \(nt^2+(n-k)st=k\). Its entire contact set splits normalized squared radii into the universal proportions \(k/n\) and \((n-k)/n\), independent of thickening scale, and its log-volume is strictly concave in the simplex scale.
