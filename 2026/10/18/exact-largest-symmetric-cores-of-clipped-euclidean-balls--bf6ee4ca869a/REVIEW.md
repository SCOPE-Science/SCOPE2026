## Mathematical value — PASS
The Kovner–Besicovitch volume fraction is a natural invariant of asymmetry and an exact measure of how much of a convex body can be retained under central symmetry. The complete solution for all one-cut Euclidean balls includes an optimizer-uniqueness theorem, a nontrivial dimension-dependent center-locking transition, an exact integral for every cap height, and sharp hemisphere and thin-cap constants. These determine geometry not fixed by general symmetry bounds or by the classical halfspace-section formula. The dependence on dimension and the translation of the optimum are mathematically natural benchmarks for Macbeath cores and volume approximation, rather than arbitrary parameter slices.
