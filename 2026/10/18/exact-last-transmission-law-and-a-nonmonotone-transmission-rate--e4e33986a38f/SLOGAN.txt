For the continuous-time classical Maki–Thompson rumor on \(\mathbb Z\), the last newly informed site appears at a time distributed as an independent \(\operatorname{Exp}(2\lambda)\) plus the maximum of two identically distributed defective exponential clocks. Its mean has a unique, finite minimizer at transmission rate \(\lambda=1.481194304092\ldots\), so faster spreading beyond that rate *delays* the expected final new transmission.
