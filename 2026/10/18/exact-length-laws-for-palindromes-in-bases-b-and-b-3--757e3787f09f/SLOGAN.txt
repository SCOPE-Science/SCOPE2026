For every integer base \(b\ge2\), the complete set of simultaneous palindromes in bases \(b\) and \(b^3\) has three explicit digit patterns and exact length counts, with a rational generating function. Multiples-of-three digit lengths grow like \(b^{L/3}\), the other lengths like \(b^{L/6}\), and the cumulative intersection has order \(X^{1/3}\).
