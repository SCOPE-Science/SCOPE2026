For every alphabet size \(q\ge2\) and repetition count \(m\ge1\), the distinct palindromic scattered subsequences of a repeated permutation word are completely classified by equalities in half-words, with exact counts at every length and rational generating function \(quz(1+uz)/((1-z)(1-u^2z(q-1+z)))\). Their longest possible length is \(2m-1\), and their total number grows as \(\Theta_q(\lambda_q^m)\) for the stated positive algebraic root \(\lambda_q\).
