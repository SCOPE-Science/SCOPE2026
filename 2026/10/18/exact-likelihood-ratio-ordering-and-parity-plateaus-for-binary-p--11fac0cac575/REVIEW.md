# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The theorem quantifies over every integer \(n\ge2\) and all real concentrations \(0<a<b\). Exact binary-run composition counts produce a conditional TP2 kernel in the folded minority count \(J=\min(K,n-K)\). The ratio of adjacent minority-count probabilities increases strictly with the concentration. The two-by-two Cauchy–Binet expansion proves likelihood-ratio ordering, and explicit positive minors establish the strict even-to-odd steps. The odd-to-even equality steps follow from an exact universal adjacent-mass identity. An exact-rational verifier passed 1,882 finite assertions, but the universal statement rests on the analytic proof.

## Originality

**PASS on inspected evidence; independently unconfirmed.** Known conditional run distributions and the 2008 exchangeable total-run law are credited, not asserted new. Eryilmaz (2018, DOI 10.1007/s11009-017-9576-1) compares *success runs of a specified length* and provides the nearest stochastic-order setting, not in its accessible abstract/introduction the present total-change ordering across symmetric urn concentrations. Meleşteu et al. (2022, arXiv:2201.05870), Theorem 3.3, concerns convex ordering of success counts; it does not imply the claimed adjacent-switch likelihood-ratio order. The Eryilmaz (2008) and (2018) complete texts were not available for inspection. They remain explicit historical-priority risks, rather than evidence of noncoverage. No independent priority certification has been performed.

## Value

**PASS.** For detecting changes in positive reinforcement strength from observed color-switch counts, likelihood-ratio ordering supplies uniform one-sided test monotonicity at *every* sample size, substantially beyond means, variances, or isolated tail inequalities. The exact paired plateau identity explains the optimal statistic's information geometry and gives parameter-free checks of exchangeability. Symmetric Pólya sampling is a canonical model of positive dependence, so the structural result addresses a motivated statistical question rather than an arbitrary special case.

## Limits

No result about asymmetric or multicolor urns, the exact ranking of all run statistics, or historical priority is asserted. The auxiliary computation only tests finite instances.
