## Correctness
PASS — The proof is a finite-dimensional information-projection calculation with explicit normalized scores, exact covariance and cross-covariance, and a strict rank-swap obstruction. The centered parameter identifiability, boundary cases \(n=2\) and \(n\ge3\), and the order of limits are explicit. Exact rational enumerations independently reproduce the key finite identities; the asymptotics follow algebraically.

## Mathematical value
PASS — Kendall Mallows and Plackett–Luce are standard, competing ranking models. The precise unavoidable KL misspecification under best-fitting log-rates is a natural statistical model-comparison invariant. The theorem finds its first positive dimension and a universal \(n^3\) growth law, not merely an arbitrary fixed-size numerical example. It does not resolve nonlocal approximation or representations using arbitrary independent distributions.
