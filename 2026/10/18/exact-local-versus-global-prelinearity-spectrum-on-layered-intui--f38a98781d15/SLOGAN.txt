For finite intuitionistic frames formed by stacked antichains, the complete ordered-pair distribution of global comparability, locally valid Gödel–Dummett prelinearity without comparability, and failed prelinearity is given by \(N^2-\sum_iq_i\), \(q_1\), and \(\sum_{i\ge2}q_i\), where \(N=1+\sum_i(2^{n_i}-1)\) and \(q_i=4^{n_i}-2\cdot3^{n_i}+2^{n_i}\). Enlarging the bottom antichain makes prelinearity almost surely valid even though the sampled truth sets become almost surely incomparable.
