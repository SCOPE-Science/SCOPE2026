Every locally-optimal named-palette colouring of the circular ladder \(C_n\square K_2\) is either constant on every rung with no singleton cyclic colour runs, or is monochromatic on each layer with different layer colours. Its complete strict-gridlock polynomial is \(T_n(k)+(k-1)2\cos(n\pi/3)+k(k-1)-k\), where \(T_0=2\), \(T_1=1\), and \(T_n=T_{n-1}+(k-1)T_{n-2}\); this supplies a six-periodic Lucas law and a rational length-generating function.
