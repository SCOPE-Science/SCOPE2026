For every integer \(s\ge2\), replacing each vertex of a path or cycle by a clique \(K_s\) leaves its locally-optimal colouring polynomial independent of \(s\): every replacement clique is forced monochromatic, and permitted base colourings are exactly those with no singleton monochromatic run. Explicit binomial sums count all such colourings for every palette and every path or cycle, hence also for every clique blowup of a graph of maximum degree two.
