For the even-degree nonlinear Liénard oscillator, the entire subcritical damping regime has an exact geometric velocity-return law, and the reversed flow has an exactly periodic logarithmic blow-up profile with period \(2\pi n\eta/[(n+1)\sqrt{4(n+1)-\eta^2}]\). At \(\eta=2\sqrt{n+1}\), infinite oscillation stops; approaching this boundary makes physical crossing-time ratios grow at an essential-singularity scale.
