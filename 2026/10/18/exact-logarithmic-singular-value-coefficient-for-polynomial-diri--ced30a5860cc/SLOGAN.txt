For every nonconstant Dirichlet polynomial \(g\), the singular values of its Volterra operator on \(\mathscr H^2\) have the sharp asymptotic \(s_n(T_g)\sim\|g'\|_{\infty,\operatorname{Re}s>0}/\log n\). The leading constant is a finite-torus multiplier norm and can register cancellation among multiplicatively related frequencies.
