# Review of the exact two-boson onsite-loss cycle theorem

## Correctness — PASS

Contractivity forces nondecaying modes to be free-hopping eigenvectors vanishing on every doublon. Outside total momentum \(\pi\), all unordered-pair energies are simple in their momentum sector, and each corresponding bosonic plane wave has nonzero doublon amplitude. For even circumferences, the \(\pi\) sector is flat at energy zero and onsite loss is rank one, giving the stated exact dark dimensions. The translation stabilizer determines the generic \(1/n\) and antipodal \(0\) or \(2/n\) probabilities. The standalone finite-matrix script independently checks the Fock Hamiltonian, dark-space rank, and 360 survival probabilities on cycles \(3\le n\le12\).

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is an exact all-size dynamical survival classification in a canonical dissipative lattice-boson model. It separates complete absorption on odd cycles from positive residual coherent survival on even cycles and identifies the sharp modulo-four antipodal interference effect, independently of loss magnitude. The same momentum-projector method may inform studies of subradiant dynamics and loss-avoiding coherent transport.

## Scientific limitations
Assumptions include identical soft-core bosons, uniform real nonzero nearest-neighbor hopping, onsite quadratic pair loss at every site, and no elastic interaction. Numerical checks cover only the specified finite sizes; all-size statements rest on proof. The result concerns no-jump survival and gives no finite-time decay law or many-body thermodynamic statement. Primary-source access is substantial but not exhaustive; independent validation has not occurred.
