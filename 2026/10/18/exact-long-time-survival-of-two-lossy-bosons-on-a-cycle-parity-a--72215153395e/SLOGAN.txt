For two coherently hopping bosons subject to onsite pair annihilation at every site of a cycle, all localized pairs eventually decay on odd cycles, but even cycles have an exact dark momentum-\(\pi\) sector. A generic separated pair survives with probability \(1/n\), whereas an antipodal pair survives with probability \(2/n\) or zero according to \(n\pmod4\), independently of the positive loss strength.
