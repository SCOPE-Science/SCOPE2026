The finite metric assumptions are exactly \(n\ge2\), \(s\ge\tfrac12\), with the hub-based zero-mass coordinate norm derived by primal mass transfer and attaining dual potentials. Polar volume follows from the exact range density of \(n\) bounded coordinates. Primal volume follows from the disjoint signed-orthant decomposition, the two mass-imbalance sectors, beta-integral evaluation and Vandermonde's identity. These checks do not substitute for the complete analytic argument.

Aliaga's primary-MSC-46B20 theorem (arXiv:2102.01219, read in full for the statement and main proof) determines extreme molecules, not Mahler profiles. Alexander–Fradelizi–García-Lirola–Zvavitch (arXiv:1911.10642, inspected volume-product results) calculate or bound relevant endpoint phenomena but do not derive the all-radius one-hub profile. A full relevant prior note on exactly the same equiradial metric gives Euclidean Banach–Mazur distance, not the Mahler product. Research-record searches for aliases, polar-volumes, deformed type-\(A\) roots and binomial identities found no equivalent complete claim. An unindexed equivalent treatment or implicit derivation remains a bibliographic risk; this is not an assertion of absolute uniqueness.
