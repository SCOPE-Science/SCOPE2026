The real Lipschitz-free unit balls of the \(n+1\)-point equiradial hub-and-leaves metrics have an exact Mahler product \(\mathcal M_n(s)=\frac{2(2ns-n+1)}{n!s^n}\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\binom{n-1+j}{j}s^j\) for every \(n\ge2\), \(s\ge\frac12\). The product increases strictly from its tree endpoint to its **unique within-family maximum at the equilateral radius** \(s=1\), then decreases strictly as the hub recedes, as certified by an explicit positive-coefficient derivative factorization.
