For \(n\) equilateral points, the weighted bounded-Lipschitz norm \(\|x\|_\infty+\lambda(\max x_i-\min x_i)\) has an exact polar-slab and polynomial Mahler-volume profile. In dimension \(n\ge3\) its normalized volume product increases strictly from the cube value to the known anchored-equilateral limit \(n\binom{2n-2}{n-1}/4^{n-1}\), while dimension two remains constant.
