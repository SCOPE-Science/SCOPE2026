For the positive Markov semigroup \(T_t=P+e^{-t}(I-P)\) of any conditional expectation on \(L^2\), the sharp maximal norm is exactly determined by the attainable conditional probabilities through \(\Lambda(b)=(1+b+\sqrt{(1-b)(1+3b)})/2\). Its universal constant is \(2/\sqrt3\), attained at conditional mass \(2/3\); on \(n\) equally weighted states, the exact norm is \(\sqrt{\Lambda(k/n)}\), where \(k\) is nearest to \(2n/3\).
