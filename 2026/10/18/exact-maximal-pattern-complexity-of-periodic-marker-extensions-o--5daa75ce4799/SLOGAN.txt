A periodic skeleton containing one binary pattern Sturmian residue has an exactly computable maximal-pattern complexity at *every* sample size: its correction to \(2k\) is the largest number of distinct silent-phase projections of the marker template. On the same four-letter alphabet, a marker period of length \(m\) with one exceptional color gives \(p^*(k)=2k+\min\{k+1,\lceil m/2\rceil\}\), so the eventual additive excess is unbounded.
