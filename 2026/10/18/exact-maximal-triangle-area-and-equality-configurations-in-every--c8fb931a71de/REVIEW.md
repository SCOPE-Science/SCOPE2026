# Scientific review: maximum-area triangles in equal-disk lenses

## Correctness: PASS
The assertion is quantified over all disk radii \(R>0\), all nonzero half-separations \(0<d<R\), and all nondegenerate triangles contained in the intersection. Similarity reduces the proof to unit disks. Every maximizer has boundary vertices. The tangent/altitude conditions and the positive second variation exclude a smooth-only maximizer: the only possible mixed-arc stationary configuration has \(u>1/2\), and its explicit second derivative is strictly positive. Every maximizer therefore uses an arc-intersection tip. A two-variable trigonometric symmetrization shows that the other two heights must coincide, and strict concavity of the remaining scalar area function fixes that height as \(-\sin(\theta/3)\) for an upper-tip maximizer. The formula, all equality cases, and the absence of interior-vertex maximizers follow. The positive polynomial \(-1+6u^2-4u^3\) is verified by its derivative and value at \(u=1/2\), not by testing. The standalone verifier executes supplementary random and formula checks, which are not used as a replacement for the universal proof.

## Originality: PASS, with stated priority risk
Kallus (arXiv:1301.5880, full 11-page text inspected) develops general maximal-inscribed-triangle structure and convex-domain lattice-covering characterizations, but does not compute the equal-disk-lens profile. Jerónimo-Castro (The American Mathematical Monthly, 2015, available article text inspected) addresses domains with rotating maximal triangles and universal ratios, not this specific parameter family. A 2024 Mathematics Stack Exchange discussion **fully covers the special case \(d/R=1/2\)**, including the value \(2R^2\sin^3(2\pi/9)\). Its comments speculate about varying separation, but its displayed arguments and solutions do not prove or state the uniform exact theorem for every separation and the complete two-triangle classification. The published mathematical record *Mean width of a symmetric lens in every dimension* treats another geometric functional; its indexed summary does not imply the proposed triangle bound. The new claim is specifically the uniform all-separation result. A possible earlier equivalent generalization of the 2024 special case is the principal remaining originality risk.

## Value: PASS
Intersections of two equal disks form a canonical continuously varying family of centrally symmetric convex bodies. Their maximal inscribed-triangle area is a natural affine/geometric extremal invariant, relevant to critical triangles and planar covering bounds. The theorem gives one exact closed trigonometric formula and classifies all optimizers for every nondegenerate member, rather than solving an arbitrary parameter slice; the full exclusion of smooth maximizers requires a non-obvious positive second-variation argument. The previous solved symmetric-lens configuration is acknowledged, and no claim is made that its special value was unknown. No generalization to unequal radii or higher dimensions is asserted.
