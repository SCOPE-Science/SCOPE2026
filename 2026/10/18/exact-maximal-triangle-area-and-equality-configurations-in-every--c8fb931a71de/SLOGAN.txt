For two equal disks whose centers are separated by \(2R\cos\theta\), with \(0<\theta<\pi/2\), the greatest area of an inscribed triangle is exactly \(2R^2\sin^3(2\theta/3)\). Exactly two triangles attain equality, each with one vertex at a lens tip and an opposite symmetric chord at signed height \(\mp R\sin(\theta/3)\) on the opposite side of the midline.
