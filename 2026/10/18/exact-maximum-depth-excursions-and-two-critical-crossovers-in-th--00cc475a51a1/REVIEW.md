# Review: exact Pelikan excursion maximum depth

**Correctness — PASS.** The dyadic shell representation is exactly Markov. The initial upper-half uniform density yields the specified launch law. The finite Dirichlet hitting recurrence has unique explicit solutions, and the geometric sum gives an exact depth-tail formula including removable special parameters. Two uniform critical-window limits follow from the displayed factorizations. The exact-rational independent checker agrees in 112 cases; these finite tests supplement, not replace, the analytic proof.

**Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk.** Yan and coauthors' 2023 random-map paper studies invariant density and correlations. The particularly close 2026 Brevitt–Klages paper derives the Markov chain and first-return-*time* generating function, but the inspected text does not report the maximum dyadic depth or either maximum-depth crossover. Published mathematical-result searches and accessible existing claims did not produce an equivalent formula. Basic gambler's-ruin methods and the Markov construction are credited as known. Historic excursion literature could implicitly cover this specialization.

**Value — PASS.** The exact extreme-depth law measures close approach to the neutral fixed point during completed return cycles. It distinguishes recurrence from geometric reinjection by two different phase transitions and gives their finite-size crossover profiles. The result is a natural dynamical statistic with a complete exact law, not a numerical cutoff or an unmotivated special parameter.

**Limitations.** Initial positions are uniform on the upper dyadic half, not sampled from the stationary invariant law. Only \(p\ge1/2\) is included; for \(p<1/2\), an excursion might never return. The stated first-public date belongs to the motivating 2023 preprint, not to a new publication. Some further literature-priority scrutiny remains appropriate.
