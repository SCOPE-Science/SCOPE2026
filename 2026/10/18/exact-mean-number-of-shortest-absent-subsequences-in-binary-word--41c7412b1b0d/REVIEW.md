# Review: exact mean number of shortest absent subsequences in binary words

## Correctness — PASS
The quantifiers are all word lengths \(n\ge0\), the source alphabet is the fixed binary alphabet even for unary words, and missing targets are scattered subsequences. The key probability identity \(\mathbb E[s(W)]=2\mathbb E[2^K\mathbf1_{\{J=K\}}]\) follows from exact cardinality of absent length-\(K+1\) target strings, uniform independent target bits, and the standard arch-universality theorem. All phase transitions were reconstructed from their underlying source/target bit events. The only net zero-surplus excursion through a matching phase is \(F\to M\to F\); the reduced path language therefore captures all and only weighted contributions. The two renewal equations produce \(2/(1-z-3z^2+z^3)\) after converting expected counts to totals. Partial fractions yield the stated positive constant and exponential error. Independent direct definition-based enumeration through length fourteen and two distinct weighted recurrences through length one hundred agree. Limits: numerical tests alone do not prove the infinite result; the path argument does.

## Originality — PASS, subject to residual risks
Kosche et al., arXiv:2108.13968, establish shortest absent subsequences and structural tools for individual words; the inspected paper does not state the fixed-length mean. Fleischmann et al., arXiv:2202.07981, classify nearly universal words with fixed missing-count parameters; the inspected treatment does not aggregate the first missing length over all source words. Biers-Ariel et al., arXiv:1704.08661, count expected present scattered subsequences, not shortest absent ones. Publicly accessible mathematical claims on binary arch-index distribution and Fibonacci maxima concern different statistics; the arch-index marginal cannot recover this expectation since equal arch counts can have unequal shortest-absent counts. Targeted searches for the exact cubic denominator, recurrence, numerical initial sequence, and random shortest absence did not reveal an equivalent theorem. These are positive mathematical implication comparisons, not a claim that search absence proves novelty. Residual risk: an unindexed or differently parameterized equivalent recurrence, especially in the Simon-congruence literature.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The exact mean is a natural average-case size for the shortest absent-pattern output of reconstruction and string-analysis algorithms. It identifies a sharp exponential base greater than one and a nontrivial leading constant, contrasting with the linear typical shortest-absent length and the known Fibonacci worst-case maximum. The theorem is an infinite-family, fully parameterized enumerative result with a new two-state weighted path reduction, rather than a finite table extension. It does not give variance, concentration, or larger-alphabet results.
