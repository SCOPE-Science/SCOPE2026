For uniformly random binary words of length \(n\), the exact mean number of distinct shortest absent scattered subsequences has generating function \(2/(1-z-3z^2+z^3)\) after multiplication by \(2^n\). The expected count grows sharply as \(1.38760048\ldots\,(1.08504324\ldots)^n\), even though the expected length of a shortest absent subsequence grows linearly.
