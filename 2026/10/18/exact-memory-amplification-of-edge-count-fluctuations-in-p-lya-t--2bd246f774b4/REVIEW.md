# Mathematical review

**Correctness — PASS.** The full proof establishes stationarity of the beta-binomial window, irreducible finite-state mixing, the covariance recurrence and its exact convergent sum, a weighted Markov functional central limit theorem, and variance convergence. The distinction between fixed memory and simultaneous growth is explicit. Forty-two exact reproducibility checks corroborate the derivations without purporting to prove asymptotic assertions by computation.

**Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk.** The graph's finite-memory definition and original simulations appear in Yu, Alajaji and Gharesifard (arXiv:2603.18452). Alajaji–Fuja and Zhong–Alajaji–Takahara already establish the underlying finite-memory contagion law; in particular the latter supplies equivalent queue-model autocorrelation recurrences. Mori–Hisakado–Nakayama also give a general memory-kernel recurrence including the finite rectangular kernel. Those established ingredients are not claimed new. The inspected literature and mathematical-result searches did not find this specific closed integrated covariance with the weighted graph edge-count limit. A broader older channel theorem could imply a portion of the derivation; independent priority assessment remains necessary.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The all-memory exact factor identifies a quadratic memory penalty for a natural random-graph summary and gives the fixed-memory fluctuation theorem and infinite-memory contrast. This responds to finite-memory stochastic questions raised in the motivating graph paper and is not a finite-table recomputation.

**Scientific limits.** Memory is fixed before the graph-size limit; no joint growth regime is proved. The stationary initial window and positive urn parameters are essential assumptions. Earlier channel covariance theory supplies prior lemmas, and independent validation is outstanding.
