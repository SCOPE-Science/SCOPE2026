For every fixed memory length, a finite-memory Pólya threshold graph has Gaussian edge-count fluctuations on the \(n^{3/2}\) scale, with exact variance coefficient \(\rho(1-\rho)(M+\kappa)(M+\kappa+1)/(3\kappa(\kappa+1))\). The multiplier grows quadratically with memory length, while the infinite-memory graph has a random, rather than deterministic, limiting edge density.
