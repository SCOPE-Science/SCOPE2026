On the three-color antiferromagnetic Potts prism, weak one-spin local minima have entropy \(\tfrac12\log\alpha\), where \(\alpha=3.673099694\ldots\) is the dominant root of \(x^6-4x^5+x^4-x^3+7x^2-2x-1\). Although proper zero-energy colorings exist at every length, a uniformly selected weak local minimum has a positive limiting energy of \(0.2174417375\ldots J\) per rung.
