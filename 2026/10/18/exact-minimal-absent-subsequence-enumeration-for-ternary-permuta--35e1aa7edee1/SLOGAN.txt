Minimal absent subsequences of repeated ternary permutations have an exact two-step comparison test, and their total counts across all repetition numbers have rational generating function \((6x-2x^2+10x^3-3x^4+8x^5-3x^6)/(1-3x+2x^2-4x^3+2x^4-3x^5+x^6)\). A finite transfer matrix also enumerates these minimal forbidden subsequences by both length and repetition count.
