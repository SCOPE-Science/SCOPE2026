For the binary word \(B_k=(01)^k\), the exact number of minimal absent scattered subsequences of length \(k+j\) is \(\binom{k+3-j}{2j-1}\), for every \(k,j\geq1\), with zero values outside the supported lengths. Consequently the shortest layer contains only \(k+2\) words, while the total number grows as \(\Theta(\rho^k)\), where \(\rho\approx1.754877666\) solves \(\rho^3-2\rho^2+\rho-1=0\), and the longest minimal absent subsequence has length \(k+\lfloor(k+4)/3\rfloor\).
