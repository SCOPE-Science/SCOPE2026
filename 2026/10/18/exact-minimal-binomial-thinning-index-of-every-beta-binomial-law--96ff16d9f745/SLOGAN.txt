For every beta-binomial law with sample size \(n\) and positive Beta shapes \(a,b\), the smallest admissible independent Bernoulli-thinning probability is exactly \((a+n-1)/(a+b+n-1)\). A Beta integration-by-parts argument makes every inverse coefficient nonnegative at that boundary, while the coefficient of degree \(n-1\) rules out all smaller scales.
