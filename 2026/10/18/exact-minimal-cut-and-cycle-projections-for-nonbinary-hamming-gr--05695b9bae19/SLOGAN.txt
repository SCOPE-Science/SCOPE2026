For every nonbinary Hamming graph \(H(d,q)\), the exact minimal cut-projection norm in real edge \(\ell_1\) and \(\ell_\infty\) is \((d+1)(q-1)/q\); the cycle-projection norm is \(1+(d+1)(q-1)/q-4(1-q^{-d})/(d(q-1))\). The orthogonal cut and cycle projections simultaneously minimize norm for every edge \(\ell_p\) exponent, while the previously known binary specialization is excluded from the novelty claim.
