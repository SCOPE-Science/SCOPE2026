For every pair-Kneser graph \(KG(n,2)\), \(n\ge5\), the Euclidean cut and cycle projections simultaneously minimize their real edge-space \(\ell_p\) norms for every \(1\le p\le\infty\). Their exact endpoint minima are \(2(n-3)(n+6)/(n(n-1))\) and \(1+2(n-3)(n+6)/(n(n-1))-8(n+1)/(n(n-1)(n-3))\); the first peaks at \(n=8,9\), while the second peaks uniquely at \(n=12\).
