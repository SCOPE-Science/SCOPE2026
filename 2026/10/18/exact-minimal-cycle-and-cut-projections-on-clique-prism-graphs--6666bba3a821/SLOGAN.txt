For every real clique-prism edge space \(K_m\square K_2\) with integer \(m\ge3\), the exact minimum \(\ell_1\)-projection norms onto the cycle and cut spaces are \(16(m-1)^2/[m(5m-2)]\) and \(2(m^2-m+1)/(m^2-m+2)\). One-parameter symmetric projections attain both values, and the orthogonal cycle projector is optimal only at \(m=4\).
