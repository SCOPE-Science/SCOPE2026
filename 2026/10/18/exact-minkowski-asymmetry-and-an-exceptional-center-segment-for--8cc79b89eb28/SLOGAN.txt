The zero-inclusive interval-incidence polytope has Minkowski asymmetry \(1\), \(3/2\), and \(2\) in dimensions \(2\), \(3\), and at least \(4\), respectively. Every optimal reflection center is unique except in dimension \(4\), where the full center locus is the segment \(\{(1,1+t,1+t,1)/3:0\le t\le1\}\; the proof uses a sharp pair of alternating-triple inequalities.
