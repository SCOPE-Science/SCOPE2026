The Minkowski asymmetry of a free join of \(k\ge2\) positive-dimensional convex bodies is exactly the sum of their asymmetries plus \(k-1\). Every minimizing center is a fixed-weight combination of one minimizing center from each factor, so the complete center locus factors as an affine Cartesian product; in particular, joining \(k\) centrally symmetric bodies always gives asymmetry \(2k-1\), irrespective of their dimensions.
