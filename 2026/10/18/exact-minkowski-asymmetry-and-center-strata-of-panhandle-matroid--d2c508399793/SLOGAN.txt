For every panhandle matroid base polytope of rank \(r\ge3\) with \(p\ge r\) head elements and \(t\ge2\) tail elements, Minkowski asymmetry equals \(\max\{t,(p+t-r)/r,r/(p-r+1)\}\). The entire locus of optimal centers is explicit and exhibits distinct unique-center, \((p-1)\)-dimensional, and \((t-1)\)-dimensional regimes.
