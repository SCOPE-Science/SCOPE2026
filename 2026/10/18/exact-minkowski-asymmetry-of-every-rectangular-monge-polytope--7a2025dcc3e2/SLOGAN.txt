The normalized nonnegative \(p\)-by-\(q\) Monge polytope has exact Minkowski asymmetry \(\min(p,q)(\max(p,q)-1)\), a deficit of \(\min(p,q)-1\) from the full-simplex bound. A canonical optimal center is obtained by averaging complete-column vertices and one area-minimizing corner-rectangle contribution at every adjacent Monge cut; a positive grid-duality identity certifies optimality in all dimensions.
