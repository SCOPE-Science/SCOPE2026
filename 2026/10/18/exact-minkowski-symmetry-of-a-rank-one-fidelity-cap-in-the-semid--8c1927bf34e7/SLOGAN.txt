Every trace-one real or complex positive-semidefinite matrix cap defined by a single rank-one fidelity threshold has an explicit, all-dimensional Minkowski symmetry constant given by a quadratic radical, with an attaining center that is isotropic off the reference ray. The exact constant supplies a computable symmetry parameter for general ellipsoidal-rounding guarantees and remains nonzero as positive cap widths shrink toward a single pure state.
