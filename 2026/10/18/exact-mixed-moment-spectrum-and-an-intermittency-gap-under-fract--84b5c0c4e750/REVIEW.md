# Mathematical review: fractional batch-reset moment spectrum

**Correctness — PASS.** Conditioning on all Poisson marks gives independent centered Gaussian coordinates with exact random variances. Expanding their products by Tonelli, ordering the integration times, and applying the hypergeometric Poisson transform yields each denominator. Discrete concavity of the tagged reset loss proves that positivity of the final denominator is both necessary and sufficient; any nonpositive denominator makes a nonnegative Tonelli integral infinite. Strict convexity proves the interval separating the mixed and pure moments, and second-order hypergeometric expansions yield the weak-reset critical law. The included checker independently verifies 817 exact combinatorial/recurrence/separation cases and four off-boundary asymptotic checks. This is a finite corroboration of the proof, not an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** Galla's 2026 fractional-reset paper studies the frequent/weak diffusion limit and a fully synchronized example; its discussion explicitly identifies batch selection as a further problem. The 2026 de Mauro–Majumdar–Schehr batch-reset work makes selected particles jump completely to zero, unlike the present multiplicative resets, and the inspected main text and appendix address their exact marginals and low-order correlations rather than the stated all-order fractional-moment finiteness classification. Comparison of claim implications establishes that neither primary theorem covers this exact model and assertion under its hypotheses; targeted bibliographic and scientific-result searches revealed no stronger matching claim. General linear multiplicative jump-process theory may nevertheless imply the new recurrence, and the complete historical literature has not been exhausted.

**Value — PASS.** The identified finiteness thresholds separate high-order collective fluctuations from high-order individual-particle intermittency in a physically motivated two-parameter interpolation between complete batch resetting and fractional resetting. The strict nonempty divergence window, its disappearance for fully synchronized marks, and the explicit high-frequency critical scaling are mathematically meaningful model diagnostics that do not follow from the previously reported second-order covariance formulas alone.
