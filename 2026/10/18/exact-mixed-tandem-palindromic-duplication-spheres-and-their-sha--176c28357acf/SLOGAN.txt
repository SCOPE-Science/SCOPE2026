For a source word subject to one length-two tandem or palindromic duplication, the exact number of distinct outputs is determined by adjacent inequalities, distance-two inequalities, and an explicit subtraction for every nonconstant \(abba\) factor. The maximum is \(n\) for binary sources and \(2n-2\) for alphabets of at least three symbols, with binary maximizers counted by a tribonacci generating function.
