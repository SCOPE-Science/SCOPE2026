For every nonminimum cardinality \(k\) of maximal independent sets on a cycle \(C_n\), single-token sliding leaves just two frozen configurations when \(n=2k\), and otherwise connects all configurations into an Eulerian graph with an explicit degree distribution. The active graph is bipartite exactly for even \(n\); when \(n\) is odd its shortest odd cycle has length exactly \(n\).
