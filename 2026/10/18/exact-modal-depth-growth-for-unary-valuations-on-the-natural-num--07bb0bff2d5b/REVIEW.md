# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The theorem fixes the reflexive forward order and one Boolean atom. For an eventually constant valuation, the run-collapse map is a bounded morphism onto a finite alternating-colored chain. A depth-type/characteristic-formula induction establishes exact definability classes, and a further induction proves there are exactly \(\min\{r,d+2\}\) classes at depth \(d\). The other infinite case forms an immediately closed four-element Boolean subalgebra. The sharp depth is checked at the last deficient partition. A standalone program independently enumerates Boolean/modal closures and depth classes for 510 finite colored-chain cases, plus 92 longer alternating chains; this supports but does not replace the proof.

## Originality — PASS, subject to residual risk
Shapirovsky's 2019 tuned-partition proof and Agnew's 2026 general structural theorem establish qualitative local finiteness. The inspected primary full texts do not state the exact \(2^r\) unary generated subalgebra or \(2^{\min\{r,d+2\}}\) filtration. Generic bounded-bisimulation theory gives a method to compute finite depth classes but not the specialized sharp expression. Targeted searches for alternate formulations and adjacent published mathematical results did not identify direct coverage. An unlocated earlier specialized computation remains possible.

## Value — PASS
The result determines two natural invariants of an explicitly motivated model class: the exact one-generator algebra size and the minimum modal depth needed to obtain it. It demonstrates quantitatively how a locally finite complex algebra can have unbounded one-generated sizes and unbounded stabilization depth, replacing only existential finite-refinement bounds with sharp valuation-sensitive formulas. It is a self-contained structural classification rather than a table recomputation or arbitrary finite slice.

## Literature and limitations
Closest known work: I. Shapirovsky, arXiv:1903.04614, Example 1 and Theorem 3.1; G. Agnew, arXiv:2606.02941, Theorem 3.16 and Proposition 3.18; T. Bolander and A. Burigana, arXiv:2405.00480. The theorem concerns one atom on the reflexive forward natural-number order only, with unrestricted valuations. Exhaustive finite computation is not a proof on the infinite model; the bounded morphism and type induction supply that proof. Originality beyond the inspected corpus is not independently certified.
