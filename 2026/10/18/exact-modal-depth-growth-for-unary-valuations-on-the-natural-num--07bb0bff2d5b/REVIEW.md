# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For a binary valuation with both colors cofinal, Box is constant on the four-element Boolean algebra generated by p, so all modal depths have four truth sets. Otherwise finite alternating color runs give a surjective bounded morphism to the finite reflexive chain C_r, with exact preservation of modal truth. A depth-d state type consists of its color and set of accessible depth-(d−1) types; characteristic formulas define every realized type. Induction shows q_d=min(r,d+2): at depth d the last d states are individually distinguished, while the unresolved prefix remains grouped by color, and one further suffix state splits per step. The resulting Boolean algebra has 2^{q_d} elements and stabilizes sharply at max(0,r−2). Edge cases r=1,2, d=0 and both colors cofinal are consistent with the formulas. Independent exhaustive finite checks corroborate but do not prove the infinite assertion.

## originality

PASS

Best-of-knowledge original exact one-generator size/depth classification on the reflexive natural-number chain. Shapirovsky and Agnew prove broader qualitative local finiteness, not a valuation-parametric optimal atomic partition or depth-growth formula. Etessami–Vardi–Wilke Lemma 2 gives a more expressive strict future/past temporal-type recursion on words; Bolander–Burigana Proposition 2.6 supplies abstract bounded modal equivalence; neither presents the precise 2^{min(r,d+2)} law for all binary valuations of (omega,<=). The new elementary but sharp alternating-run induction evaluates that recurrence and proves the stabilization boundary. Historical specialized computations under temporal logic, suffix types or one-variable modal algebras remain a substantive priority risk.

## value

PASS

The canonical reflexive natural-number frame has qualitatively locally finite complex algebra but its single-generator sizes and depth filtration need not be uniformly bounded. The all-valuations theorem identifies exact atoms by color runs or cofinality, determines every size 2^r, and gives sharp unbounded stabilization depth max(0,r−2). This is a complete natural structural classification of a concrete invariant, not arbitrary data sampling; one can use it as an exact benchmark for bounded modal equivalence, tuned partitions and expressivity versus modal depth. It does not claim a general frame theorem or paper-sized contribution.
