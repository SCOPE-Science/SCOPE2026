For a single Boolean valuation on the reflexive natural-number order, the complete modal subalgebra is exactly the Boolean algebra of its finite color-runs, with \(2^r\) elements and sharp stabilization depth \(\max\{0,r-2\}\), unless both colors occur infinitely often, when it has only four elements. At modal depth \(d\), the eventually constant case has exactly \(2^{\min\{r,d+2\}}\) definable truth sets.
