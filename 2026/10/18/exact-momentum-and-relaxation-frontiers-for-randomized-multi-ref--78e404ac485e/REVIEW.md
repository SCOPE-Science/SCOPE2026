# Scientific review: exact momentum and relaxation frontiers for multi-reflection Douglas–Rachford

## Correctness — PASS

The claim uses the exact published momentum iteration, specialized to independently sampled uniform coordinate reflections on an identity linear system. Reflection parity yields the Bernoulli scalar coefficient with odd probability \(q=(1-(1-2/m)^r)/2\). For each coordinate, the deterministic three-component second-moment recurrence is derived explicitly. Its determinant at one factors as \(4\alpha q[1-\alpha-\beta^2+\alpha\beta(2q-1)]\). This sign by itself is insufficient: an independent nonnegative-kernel renewal argument gives necessity, sufficiency and the positive boundary limit. The discrete Lyapunov identity and its finite impulse-energy condition were checked symbolically, including all endpoint domains. For momentum at least one, instability follows from the nondecaying signed mean. Differentiating the exact moment characteristic equation gives the local-rate threshold at half relaxation. The standalone checker passed 1,007 assertions; the infinite-time conclusions are analytic, not inferred from finite tests.

## Originality — PASS, subject to the stated comparison risk

The motivating Han–Su–Xie paper (arXiv:2207.04291, Theorem 4.1 and appendix) proves a general **sufficient** mean-square stability criterion for its momentum method, with no exact identity-system three-parameter frontier or sign change of local momentum benefit. Its momentum-free bound is explicitly known to be sharp on equal-singular-value systems; that fact is not asserted as new. Prior exact Kaczmarz momentum results already imply the special \(r=1\), \(\alpha=1/2\) threshold, including \(\beta<1/\sqrt2\) on two equally weighted rows. That specialization is acknowledged as covered. What survives that comparison is the simultaneous arbitrary-relaxation, reflection-count and dimension frontier and the newly identified local acceleration sign boundary. The related minibatch-heavy-ball work studies different gradient-averaging updates. A broad unlocated recurrence theorem or an exact relaxed Kaczmarz analysis might imply the new formula, so literature uniqueness is not certified; this residual risk is substantive but no covering statement was identified in the inspected relevant sources or searched mathematical records.

## Value — PASS

The result resolves the stability threshold of an established randomized feasibility algorithm in a well-conditioned reference problem, rather than extracting a routine one-step inequality. It identifies a parameter region in which signed means converge but squared errors grow, sharpens a published usable momentum bound (for one natural test from about \(0.183\) to \(0.707\)), and makes relaxation a structural switch between local variance acceleration and deceleration. Its exact reflection-count dependence is useful for understanding whether additional random reflections buy a wider momentum stability margin; the two-row case proves they do not. It is a finite-dimensional benchmark rather than a general guarantee for nonorthogonal row systems or a claim about work-normalized efficiency.
