## Correctness
PASS. The monitored-core capacity inequality gives the minimum size and restricts optimal pendant sensors to at most two. A complete gap analysis of the selected core-cycle vertices proves each sensor-profile count, including the exceptional four-cycle. Direct exhaustive propagation checks of 90,728 subsets at orders 3–13 confirm the formulas; the proof, not this finite experiment, establishes the infinite family.

## Originality
PASS, with bibliographic qualification. The 2019 Saibavani–Parvathi source (Theorem 3.1, material text inspected) already gives the power domination number \(\lceil n/3\rceil\) and is explicitly credited; it does not classify or enumerate all optimal vertex sets. The closest retrieved previously published results about exact counts or polynomials concern connected threshold graphs, proper double stars, books, and spiders, not the sunlet. Searching alternative terms and comparing the asserted implications identified no source implying the count vector and three residue regimes. An equivalent unindexed result remains a possibility.

## Value
PASS. Optimum placement multiplicities are a natural reliability and degeneracy refinement of a classical sensor-location parameter: across adjacent residue classes they exhibit constant, linear and quadratic growth, while one exceptional small graph requires a separate classification. The classification identifies how selecting leaves changes feasible optimal placements, beyond a routine reformulation of the known scalar optimum.
