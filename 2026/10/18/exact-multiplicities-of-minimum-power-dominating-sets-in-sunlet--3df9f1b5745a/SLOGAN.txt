For the sunlet graph \(C_n\circ K_1\), the number of minimum power-dominating sets is exactly \(3\), \(3n\), or \(n(9q+4)/2\) as \(n\equiv0,2,1\pmod 3\) respectively (with \(q=(n-1)/3\) in the last case), except that the four-cycle sunlet has \(22\) such sets. The proof classifies every optimum by its zero, one or two selected pendant vertices and reveals constant, linear and quadratic solution-multiplicity regimes.
