# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. In \(P_n^k\), distances equal \(\lceil|u-v|/k\rceil\). A congruence-class triple has a unique monotone geodesic, establishing the sharp \(2k\) bound on a color class. The two-block construction explicitly checks all endpoint-offset cases \(t\ge3\), \(t=2\) with ordered offsets, and \(t=2\) with reversed offsets. Truncation preserves monotone shortest witnesses. Every boundary and small-power case is covered. Independent BFS/partition computations validate finite instances, but the proof supplies the infinite conclusion.

## Originality

PASS with residual bibliographic risk. The full 2024 introducing article studies trees, general bounds, corona constructions, some two-color graphs, and Cartesian/strong products rather than all path powers. Its exact path formula is subsumed here as the known \(k=1\) case, explicitly excluded from novelty. The inspected 2025 article on independent mutual-visibility coloring and products, and the 2025 paper on complexity and glued trees, have different objects or stronger constraints, not a statement implying the all-\(k\) result. Specific web and mathematical-record searches for path powers and squared paths revealed no exact prior formula. Neither a negative search nor restricted indexing proves absolute uniqueness.

## Value

PASS. This class is a canonical proper-interval graph family where geodesic choices are controlled by jump length. The exact two-parameter result identifies a sharp phase transition at \(n=4k\) and gives optimal colorings for every order, not merely bounds. It also yields an unbounded gap between visibility chromatic number and ordinary chromatic number for every fixed \(k\), a structural distinction within this natural graph family.

## Closest literature and limitations

The defining 2024 mutual-visibility coloring paper supplies general lower bounds and the tree base case. Later independent-visibility and product papers neither state nor directly dominate this all-path-powers formula. Unindexed equivalent work remains possible. The theorem does not address powers of cycles or other visibility variants; no independent external audit was carried out.
