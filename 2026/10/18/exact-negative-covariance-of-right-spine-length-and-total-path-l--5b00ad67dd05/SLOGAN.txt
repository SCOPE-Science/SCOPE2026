In a uniformly random binary search tree, the number of nodes on the right spine and the total internal path length have exact covariance \(3n+H_n-2(n+1)H_n^{(2)}\), strictly negative for \(n\ge3\). Its magnitude grows linearly, but their correlation vanishes like a negative constant divided by \(\sqrt{\log n}\); the formula also gives the first-order response of mean insertion cost to record bias.
