A uniform recursive tree has exact negative leaf-count/total-path-length covariance \(\operatorname{Cov}(T_n,L_n)=-(n-2)/4\), yet its centered leaf-count Gaussian limit is mixing relative to the entire growth history. In particular, the \(\sqrt n\)-scale normal leaf fluctuation is asymptotically independent of the non-Gaussian, \(n\)-scale total-path-length martingale limit.
