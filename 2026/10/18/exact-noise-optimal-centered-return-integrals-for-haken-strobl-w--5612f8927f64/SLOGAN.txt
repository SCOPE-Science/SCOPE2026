For adjacency-Hamiltonian Haken–Strobl walks on complete bipartite graphs with at least three vertices, the signed integrated excess return is exactly a positive linear function of dephasing plus a positive inverse-rate term, yielding a unique optimal noise strength. The optimum scales as \(J\sqrt b\) for a fixed initial-part size but as \(Jn\) when both parts grow proportionately; the two-vertex graph instead has only a linear term.
