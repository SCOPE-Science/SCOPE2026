For fair Bernoulli branches of the hyperbolic quadratic anti-integrable correspondence, the coordinates are linearly uncorrelated yet their squared amplitudes have the exact lag law \(r^2(1+2d)^{-1}[d/(1+d)]^k\). Two mixed lag moments recover both parameters when \(r\ne0\), although opposite values of \(r\) give identical one-time marginals.
