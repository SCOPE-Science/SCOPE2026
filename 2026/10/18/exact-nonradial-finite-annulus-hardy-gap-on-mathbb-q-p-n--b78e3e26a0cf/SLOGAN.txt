On any finite union of consecutive valuation shells in \(\mathbb Q_p^n\), the sharp quadratic fractional Hardy improvement is exactly the top-eigenvalue deficit of a finite Kac--Murdock--Szegő matrix, even without any radiality assumption. A positive radial extremizer is uniquely optimal, every nonradial deviation has an exact positive integral remainder, and the best finite-width gap has an explicit sharp \(M^{-2}\) coefficient.
