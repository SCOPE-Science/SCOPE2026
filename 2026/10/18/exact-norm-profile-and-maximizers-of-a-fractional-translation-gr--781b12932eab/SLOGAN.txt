The De Carli–Samad fractional translation group on a checkerboard-weighted sequence Hilbert space has an exact sharp norm profile \(\sqrt{1+\beta^2\sin^2(\pi t/2)}+\beta|\sin(\pi t/2)|\), where \(\beta=|a-b|/(2\sqrt{ab})\). Its norm is attained at every parameter, is periodic with period two, and exhibits nonnormal amplification whenever the weights differ and \(t\) is not an even integer.
