For a full-row-rank linear residual, Gaussian coefficient-minimal and QR-orthonormalized physical-minimal subspace corrections fit exactly the same residual in the same sampled subspace, yet their normalized squared nullspace errors follow different exact chi-square ratios: \(\chi^2_{d-r}/\chi^2_{\ell-r+1}\) versus \(\chi^2_{d-\ell}/\chi^2_{\ell-r+1}\). Their expected squared lengths are finite exactly after two redundant sketch directions, and QR reduces the expected excess by the factor \((d-r)/(d-\ell)\) without changing feasibility.
