# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

In fixed standard successor arithmetic every allowed equality is an atom E_j or a Boolean constant after cancellation. A knowing-value target is either rigid or an injective successor shift of c. Work inside the root's equivalence class: by induction a formula has the same truth at all worlds of a given tag for fixed class signature. In the K step the class is unchanged at each world, and only the set of present tags matters. In the Kv step each selected named tag supplies one value; if selected, the outside tag supplies 0, 1 or at least 2 values, and the only test is whether the total is at most one. This applies to arbitrary finite formula nesting even on infinite classes. Conversely, root tags are tested by atoms; possibility of each E_j gives the named support; possibility of E_* and Kv(E_*,c) distinguish all three outside cases, including vacuity. Consequently each realized signature and every union of signatures are definable at depth one. An all-unnamed one-value point and a two-value point give depth-zero equivalence but different Kv truth, also at k=0. One representative per named value and r distinct unnamed representatives are both necessary and sufficient, with maximum k+2. For r=0 each nonempty T contributes |T| root choices; for each of r=1,2 each T contributes |T|+1. Summing yields 3k*2^(k-1)+2^(k+1) for k>=1 and two theories at k=0. All subsets of these theories being definable gives exactly 2^N_k formula classes.

## originality

PASS

Credit Gu–Wang's knowing-value bisimulation as a direct general route to invariance, also for infinite models: one can encode the fixed numerical tests as atom labels, relate equal tags and verify the successor and unequal-value pair clauses from the signature. The finite-model converse in that source is not itself a classification of arithmetic types. The supported distinct result is the explicit necessary and sufficient bounded-observation invariant with depth-one characteristic formulas, sharp per-type and uniform world counts, and exact theory/formula census. Those quantitative and definability statements do not appear in the strongest available comparisons, and require arithmetic-label restrictions and explicit witnesses/counting, not just invoking general bisimulation invariance or the successor finite-model property.

## value

PASS

The arithmetic-observation budget is a natural finite parameter in a language with arbitrarily nested knowledge tests. The classification identifies exactly what information such tests retain, and yields both a sharp uniform representative bound and a complete finite normal form, rather than a loose satisfiability bound or just an example. The least-world formula and exact census are independently useful for finite reasoning in this fragment.
