In the one-agent, one-constant fragment of successor knowing-value logic with exactly \(k\) available numeral observations, every formula has a modal-depth-one normal form. Its pointed theories are classified by the current numeral tag, the accessible observed values, and whether there are zero, one, or at least two other distinct values; the optimal class-model cutoff is \(k+2\), with exactly \(3k2^{k-1}+2^{k+1}\) pointed theories for \(k\ge1\) and two when \(k=0\).
