With full averaging after \(E\) local-gradient steps on scalar heterogeneous quadratics, exact convergence to the original minimizer uniformly over all local centers is impossible for even \(E\ge2\), but possible at a unique stable step for odd \(E\ge3\) precisely when there are two curvature levels \(0<a<b<2a\). The exceptional step approaches the larger-curvature stability boundary at the sharp rate \(E(2-\alpha_E b)\to-\log(b/a-1)\).
