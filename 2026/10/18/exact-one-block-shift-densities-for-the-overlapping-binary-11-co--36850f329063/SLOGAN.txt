For every binary shift with exactly one run of ones, the probability that adding the shift does not decrease the overlapping \(11\)-count is one of just eight explicit rational values; its sharp minimum is \(39/64>1/2\). Once that run has length at least four, the density stabilizes exactly at \(83/128\) for odd shifts and \(39/64\) for even shifts, regardless of further run length or trailing zeros.
