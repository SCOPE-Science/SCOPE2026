# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The rank-one Laplacian perturbation retains the constant, residual \(r\)-, and residual \(s\)-eigenspaces while creating exactly two simple new eigenvalues. Their endpoint and outsider components are calculated from explicit strongly regular spectral idempotents. The nonempty set of vertices of identical adjacency signature is used to prove inherited-mode visibility at every vertex. Together with the symmetric Krylov/PBH criterion, this proves the exact rank dichotomy and count for every positive conductance. The self-contained verification checks 14846 exact finite assertions on eight graph families. Computation is corroboration, not a substitute for proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

The nearest antecedent is the adjacency/loop edge-perturbation eigenvalue theorem of Godsil, Guo, Kempton and Lippner (arXiv:1710.02181, Corollary 12); the weighted Laplacian perturbation is a special case of their family and its new eigenvalue quadratic is previously covered. Godsil's 2012 walk-matrix theorem and the PBH criterion are also prior. Neither inspected full article provides the every-vertex rank-three-versus-rank-five classification or the exact \(2(k-\lambda)\) actuator census. This claim additionally proves simultaneous visibility of the three residual eigenvalues and an all-conductance, graph-parameter-only partition of vertex actuators. The novelty is that controllability classification, **not** the underlying secular equation or original three-eigenvalue bound. Unindexed equivalent implications remain possible.

## Value — PASS

Reachable-subspace dimension is a natural invariant of one-driver network control. The result distinguishes exactly which positions gain two reachable modes after localized physical symmetry breaking, showing that conductance magnitude does not cause exceptional resonances in this spectral class. It also establishes that the fraction and count of improved vertices depend only on the strongly regular parameters, even for nonisomorphic graphs such as the rook and Shrikhande graphs.

## Scientific limits

This concerns one perturbed edge, a scalar vertex input, primitive unweighted strongly regular baseline graphs, and positive conductances. There is no multi-driver classification and no independent audit. The 2017 paper's related spectral calculation is explicitly acknowledged.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
