A rank-one-forced diagonal Lyapunov Gramian in dimension at least three admits an exact nonnegative-diagonal-plus-one-factor covariance representation only when all damping rates agree or exactly one rate is exceptional. Every other rate pattern has strictly positive Frobenius distance from this model, with a sharp cubic short-time distance law \(\delta(t)=\Theta(t^3)\), even when the true covariance is positive definite.
