For the two numerical-semigroup templates attached to prime triplets, the largest integer with at most one nonnegative three-generator representation has an explicit quadratic formula: \(12k^2+40k+23\) for \((6k+5,6k+7,6k+11)\) and \(12k^2+50k+56\) for \((6k+7,6k+11,6k+13)\), both for \(k\ge1\). At \(k=0\) the exceptional values are \(24\) and \(45\), respectively.
