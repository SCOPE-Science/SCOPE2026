For every binary Sturmian word whose ones have irrational frequency \(\alpha\), moving the first 1 to the end of each length-\(n\) factor produces exactly \(\max\{2,n+1-\lfloor1/\alpha\rfloor\}\) distinct words for \(n\ge1\). The precise complexity loss is \(\min\{n-1,\lfloor1/\alpha\rfloor\}\), with no dependence on the Sturmian intercept.
