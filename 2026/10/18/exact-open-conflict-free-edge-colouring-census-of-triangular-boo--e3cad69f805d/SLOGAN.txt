For the triangular-book graph \(T_n=K_2\vee\overline{K_n}\), open conflict-free edge-colouring requires exactly three colours whenever \(n\ge3\). With three named colours, there are \(198\) colourings for \(n=3\), \(1080\) for \(n=4\), and exactly \(18n(2n-1)\) for every \(n\ge5\); in the latter range all valid colourings differ from a uniform page-pair colouring on only one or two pages.
