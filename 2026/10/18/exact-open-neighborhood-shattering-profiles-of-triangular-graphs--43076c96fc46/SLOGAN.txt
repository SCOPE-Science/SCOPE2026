For open neighborhoods of triangular graphs \(T_n=J(n,2)\), the complete shattering polynomial has exact binomial-coefficient formulas through degree four and vanishes in all higher degrees. Every vertex pair is shattered for \(n\ge5\), but uniformly sampled triples and quartets are shattered with probabilities asymptotic to \(12/n\) and \(48/n^2\), respectively.
