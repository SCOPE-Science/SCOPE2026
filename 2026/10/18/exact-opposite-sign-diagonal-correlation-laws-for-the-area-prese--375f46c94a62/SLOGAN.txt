Two diagonal half-square sensors of the Lebesgue baker map have exact covariances \(C_\pm(n)=1/(6\cdot2^n)\pm n2^n/[8(4^n-1)]\) at every positive lag: one is always positively correlated, while the other is independent at lag one and negatively correlated thereafter. The opposite sharp \(\pm n2^{-n}/8\) leading terms arise from binary-digit reversal rather than from separate dynamical systems.
