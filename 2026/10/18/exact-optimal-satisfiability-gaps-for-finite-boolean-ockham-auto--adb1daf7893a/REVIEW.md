# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof states the nontrivial finite Boolean domain, automorphism restriction and exact sampling law. Local evaluation follows from twisted complement at an atom. Overlap-compatible blocks are in bijection with closed transfer walks even for short cycles; independent cycles multiply. A forbidden block yields a single cyclic universal test and a fixed positive failure probability. The independent-window bound forces long-cycle satisfaction probabilities to zero, so finite enumeration obtains the **attained** optimal gap. The prime-power formula is checked by matching both directions of an elementary valuation bound. Independently implemented exhaustive tests are supplied, but they are not used as infinite certificates.

## Originality — PASS (with residual priority risk)

The closest Ockham source, Marcelino–Rivieccio (arXiv:2102.05421, §2, §5), treats negation varieties and finite Hilbert axiom systems, not exact assignment probabilities. Bumpus–Kocsis (arXiv:2110.11515, §1) provides the gap concept and product formula in Heyting algebras rather than the dual-automorphism setting. Standard finite-type symbolic dynamics already provides de Bruijn/trace machinery and is not represented as original. The particular sharp-gap computation uniformly across equations and the valuation-based family are not established by the inspected sources. No equivalent statement was found in the actual research-record and literature comparisons; earlier equivalent encodings remain possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The class is mathematically natural in algebraic logic: it consists of Boolean-type Ockham algebras whose dual negation is invertible. The result **classifies quantitative equation behavior for every term of the signature**, not an arbitrary numerical instance. It supplies a uniform finite validity test, a terminating algorithm for the mathematically sharp satisfaction threshold, and an infinite explicit arithmetic family realizing different gaps. These data complement known qualitative axiomatizability questions. No claim is made for all Ockham algebras or for pseudocomplemented Heyting lattices.
