Every equation in finite Boolean Ockham algebras with bijective negation has an exact cycle-transfer formula and a computable, attained sharp satisfiability gap whenever it is not universally valid. For even negation iterates \(\sim^{a}x=x\), the optimal gap is \(1-2^{-\min_{p\ \mathrm{prime}}p^{v_p(a)}(p-1)}\).
