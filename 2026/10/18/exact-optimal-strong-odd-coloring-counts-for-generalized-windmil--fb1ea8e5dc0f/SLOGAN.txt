For the generalized windmill \(W_{r,t}=K_1\vee(tK_r)\), every optimal strong odd coloring is exactly a parity-balanced collection of \(r\)-subsets of its noncentral color palette. This yields a sharp parity-dependent chromatic number and an explicit Krawtchouk-sum count of all optimal labeled colorings, with \((r+1)(r!)^t\) for odd \(t\) and \((2r+1)!\) for \(t=2\).
