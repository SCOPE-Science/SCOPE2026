For two independent phase-only three-tap signals on every prime cycle of order \(p\ge7\), the exact short-time Fourier support spectrum is \(\{5p,5p-1,5p-2\}\). After two global phases are removed, all sharp minimizers form exactly \(p^2\) two-tori and \(4p^2\) circles, arising from two distinct patterns of time-frequency cancellation.
