For friendship graphs \(F_k=K_1\vee(kK_2)\), ordinary chromatic villainy is \(k+1\) for \(k\ge2\), while weak villainy is \(k\) at \(k=2,3\) and equals \(k+1\) thereafter; both vanish for \(F_1=K_3\). Every individual disturbance also admits an exact linear-time repair-distance formula from its coloured hub and outer-edge pairs.
