For power-one three-point curvature weighting with planar Rademacher probes, the exact worst-start, one-batch Hessian-energy risk is minimized uniquely by aligning the Hessian eigenvectors with the two probe diagonals. With \(n\) probes, the optimal step is \(2n/(n+1)\), giving the sharp expected squared-error factor \(1/(n+1)\) at every fresh-batch iteration, while axis alignment loses this guarantee as the condition number grows.
