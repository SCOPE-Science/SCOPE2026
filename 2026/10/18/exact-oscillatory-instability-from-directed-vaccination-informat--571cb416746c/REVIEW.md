The complete-graph migration operator is zero on constants and −3D on the nonuniform subspace, and acts identically on both variables. An imaginary root requires ω=∓√3Z/(2h) and k=Z/2+3Z²/(4h²); the latter is strictly increasing in Z≥0 and gives exactly the stated positive threshold.
