The image of squaring on upper-triangular \(2\times2\) matrices over \(\mathbb Z_p\) has Haar density \(7/372\) at \(p=2\), and \(p^3(p-1)(p^2+1)/(4(p+1)(p^5-1))\) at every odd prime. The dyadic exception is forced exactly by the extra factor of \(2\) in the smallest sum of equal-valuation diagonal roots; these densities are also the limits of finite-ring square-image proportions.
