The smooth \(\mathrm{PGL}(2,7)\)-invariant plane octic pencil has exactly three joint \(p\)-rank/\(a\)-number strata: \((21,0)\) generically, \((14,7)\) exactly at \(\lambda=3,4\), and \((0,21)\) at its Hermitian member. An exact \(21\times21\) Cartier-coefficient calculation detects the seven-dimensional exceptional \(a\)-number, beyond the hyperelliptic quotient's \(p\)-rank information.
