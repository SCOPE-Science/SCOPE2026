For complete tripartite graphs with all parts of size at least two and every integer \(p\ge3\), the \(p\)-strong Roman domination number is the minimum of three explicit defender-placement costs. The third placement regime is essential: \(K_{a,a,a}\) with \(p=2a-2\) has optimum five and exactly \(3a^3\) minimum functions for every \(a\ge4\).
