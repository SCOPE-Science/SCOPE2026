For length-four reverse-complement duplication over any even alphabet with a fixed-point-free complement, two source positions separated by \(d\) produce the same descendant with exact probability \(q^{-4}\) for \(d=1,2\), \(q^{-6}\) for \(d=3\), and \(q^{-d-2}\) for \(d\ge4\). These probabilities yield an exact finite-length formula for the expected number of colliding pairs of duplication sites, without conflating pair multiplicities with distinct descendants.
