## correctness

The gap after the m-th one has g_{v2(m)} zeros by W_{t+1}=W_t 0^{g_t} W_t. Consecutive gaps have different lengths, with smaller g0, so a one-centered palindrome has precisely the one-one form with zero radius at most g0. For a gap of index m with v2(m)=j, v2(m±h)=v2(h) for 1<=h<2^j forces the core W_{j+1}. If m>2^j, of the two outer gap indices m±2^j one has valuation j+1 and the other at least j+2. The shorter outer run sets the upper length M_j=q^(j+1)+2g_{j+1}, attained at m=3·2^j. The frozen reference to both outer gaps requires the endpoint exception m=2^j: there is no gap at index zero; the core starts at position zero and cannot extend, so its cap is q^(j+1), less than M_j. This confirms the frozen bound without changing the statement. All admissible shorter factors of a given type are centered subwords of the same extremal palindrome; different types are distinguished by their central consecutive ones. The all-zero contribution is always present, and intervals two levels apart are disjoint since q(q−2)>=2q−3. A one-centered contribution cannot coexist with two gap levels. Independent base-q digit generation and expansion about centers matched 560 finite counts (3<=q<=9, 1<=n<=80), and the caps at m=2^j and 3·2^j for 0<=j<=2. These finite checks are not exhaustive for the infinite language; the submitted script was inspected, not executed.
