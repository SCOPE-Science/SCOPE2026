For every generalized Cantor word \(1\mapsto10^{q-2}1\), \(0\mapsto0^q\) with integer \(q\ge3\), the number of distinct length-\(n\) palindromic factors is given exactly by a finite sum of scale intervals, for every \(n\). The center-gap classification shows the count never exceeds three; the classical \(q=3\) specialization is already implied by earlier literature, while the formula covers all larger bases.
