# Scientific assessment

## Correctness
**PASS.** The greedy-endpoint identity follows by an induction on embedded symbols. Every distinct binary palindrome has one unique half-word representation and its minimal prefix length is determined exactly by its first symbol, equality transitions, and central symbol. Summing these disjoint classes gives the rational length enumerator. The even/odd rational functions have isolated, noncancelling simple poles at the analytic root \(R(t)\); their uniformly dominant asymptotics yield the stated derivatives and Gaussian limit by characteristic functions. Finite direct and recurrence checks corroborate, but are not substitutes for, the infinite arguments.

## Originality
**PASS, with residual priority risk.** The 2021 Mahalingam–Pandoh paper establishes totals and Fibonacci-type extremal bounds. The displayed two-variable length enumerator and the limiting distribution are strictly stronger data: totals are recovered by evaluating at \(t=1\), but the converse implication does not hold. The 2019 Müllner–Ryzhikov paper concerns longest palindromic subsequences, not a length distribution of distinct palindromic words. Searches for alternating-word palindromic subsequence length spectra, generating functions, and Gaussian laws found no equivalent or stronger covering statement; possible unindexed earlier work remains a risk. The reported first-public source date is that of arXiv:2108.02729v1, not a journal or revision date.

## Value
**PASS.** The length distribution for a canonical binary extremizer is a natural refinement of a recognized counting problem. The exact rational bivariate identity makes each length multiplicity accessible and, beyond the known Fibonacci total, establishes the asymptotic typical length with explicit linear mean, linear variance and normal fluctuations. These are quantitative structural properties not implied by the prior scalar enumeration alone.

## Limitations and sources
Finite verification covers direct embeddings to length 17 and rational-polynomial comparisons to length 75. Analytic conclusions use the proof, not finite experiments. The possible existence of an unindexed earlier equivalent remains a residual originality risk. Primary source: K. Mahalingam and P. Pandoh, arXiv:2108.02729v1 (2021); additional source: C. Müllner and A. Ryzhikov, arXiv:1901.07502 (2019). Neither earlier paper is claimed to be comprehensively absent of material outside the inspected relevant sections.
