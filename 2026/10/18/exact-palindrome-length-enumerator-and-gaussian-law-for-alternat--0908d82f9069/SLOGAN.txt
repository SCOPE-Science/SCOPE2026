For the alternating binary prefix \(W_n\), the complete polynomial counting distinct nonempty palindromic scattered subsequences by length has bivariate generating function \(tz(1+z)(1+tz^2)/((1-z)(1-t^2z^2(1+z^2)))\). A uniformly chosen distinct palindrome has asymptotically normal length, with mean \(((1+1/\sqrt5)/2)n+O(1)\) and variance \((2/(5\sqrt5))n+O(1)\).
