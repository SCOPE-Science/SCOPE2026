Over \(\mathbb F_2\), the exact worst-case state cost of taking the language square root of an \(n\)-state parity automaton is \(n^3-n^2+n\), attained on a three-letter alphabet for every \(n\ge2\). The saving of \(n(n-1)\) relative to the ordinary-NFA cubic bound comes from characteristic-two cancellation in a matrix-product Hankel kernel.
