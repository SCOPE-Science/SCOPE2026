For the fan graph \(K_1\vee P_n\), a four-state recurrence counts every acyclic-orientation fibre with prescribed vertex-indegree parities in linear time. Every globally feasible signature is realized when \(n\) is odd; when \(n\) is even, exactly \(2^{n/2-1}\) signatures fail, characterized by equal consecutive interior parity pairs and odd parity at both path endpoints.
