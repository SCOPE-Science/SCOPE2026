The Pell state permutation \((a,b)\mapsto(b,a+2b)\) modulo \(2^k\) has exactly \(2^{k+1}-2\) cycles for every \(k\ge2\). Its entire least-period spectrum and every cycle multiplicity are explicit, with exactly seven cycles at period \(4\) for every \(k\ge3\), and uniform dyadic growth above that period.
