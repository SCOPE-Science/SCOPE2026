The identity-cone translated cone exchange has a complete rational primitive-period spectrum on its invariant interpolating trapezoid: if \(\lambda=p/q\), every period from \(q\) through \(p+q\) occurs, with exact planar areas \((p+q-m)w_m/(Cq^2)\). As rational denominators grow, its normalized period histogram converges to a universal linearly increasing density, while an irrational translation has no periodic points in the trapezoid.
