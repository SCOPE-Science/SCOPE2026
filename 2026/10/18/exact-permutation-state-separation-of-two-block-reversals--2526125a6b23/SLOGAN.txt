For the two-block reversal pair \(a^r b^s\) and \(b^s a^r\), the exact minimum number of states of a separating permutation automaton is determined by two low-degree divisibility tests and, above four states, the first \(d\) for which \(\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,d)\) divides neither exponent. When both exponents equal \(\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,N)\), the minimum is the first prime power greater than \(N\).
