For every \(k\ge3\), the lag-one autocorrelation of the Rudin–Shapiro word over any sliding window of \(2^k\) neighboring pairs is exactly \(-2\), \(0\), or \(+2\), with its value constant across all starts in an aligned interval of length \(2^{k-1}\). Across complete dyadic blocks of starting indices the three values occur with exact proportions \(1/4,1/2,1/4\).
