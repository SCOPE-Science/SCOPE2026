# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The cyclic form z^r Q(z^d) is preserved under differentiation; for r>0 Rolle's theorem on t^(r/d)Q(t), which vanishes at 0, gives confinement in [0,1], with multiplicity counted. For r=0 differentiate Q. Theorem 1.1 and Proposition 3.5 of Hägg–Shapiro apply to this monic polynomial without a simple-critical-point restriction. Substitution and rotation symmetry give t(dv−(d−α))=α v^d and tG(t)=αv/((d−α)(1−v)), with v→(d−α)/d at infinity. An independent check of physical-branch selection is available on t=−u<0: the signed coefficients in the exact descendant all have the same sign there. Their consecutive absolute-term ratio tends, at index ℓ/n→λ in (α/d,1), to u(1−λ)/λ times [dλ/(dλ−α)]^d. The finite ratios decrease with ℓ and their limit decreases from infinity to zero; geometric bounds away from its unique crossing concentrate ℓ/n there. The logarithmic derivative gives tG(t)=(dλ−α)/(d−α). Setting v=1−α/(dλ) yields the same algebraic equation on the negative axis; compact support then identifies the limiting transform uniquely. The positive exterior branch runs from (d−α)/d to v_c=(d−α)/(d−1), with its unique positive turning value T. On (0,T) the upper-bank v=r exp(−iθ), where r=(d−α) sin(dθ)/(d sin((d−1)θ)), t=(α/d)r^(d−1)sin(dθ)/sin θ, and 0<θ<π/d. The parametrization runs continuously from T to 0 with nonzero derivative in the interior, by the absence of another critical value; it matches the square-root branch at T. Stieltjes inversion gives positive analytic density there and analytic continuation across the exterior except for the selected pole at v=1 when α<1, whose residue is d(1−α)/(d−α). The expansions at v=0 and at v_c, using t″(v_c)=Td(d−1)/v_c², give exactly the three stated constants: for α=1 the Möbius denominator also vanishes at v_c, changing the exponent from +1/2 to −1/2. Both endpoints have weaker than simple-pole growth and the remaining support points are isolated, excluding other atoms and a singular continuous part. Substituting v=(d−α)(1+y)/d and applying Lagrange inversion to (1+y)/(1−βy) verifies the coefficient sum and first moment. Rotation invariance then gives the stated z-plane support.

## originality

PASS

Credit is limited to the explicit resolution of the continuous branch for the regular-polygon derivative family: the exact interval and density positivity, and the three calibrated endpoint laws through the critical parameter. Fixed-ratio convergence, the general transform and the atom are established elsewhere; the moment formula comes by specialization and Lagrange inversion. There is also exact equivalence of the resulting probability law to a normalized free projection product, so this is not a new abstract probability distribution. The inspected general free-semigroup theorem supplies an implicit density representation and atom criterion; the precise solved three-regime edge evaluation was not directly found in the inspected sources.

## value

PASS

The geometric support and local zero densities for regular-root descendants are natural quantitative refinements of the general shadow theorem. At the same parameter where retained root mass disappears, the continuous density changes from a vanishing soft edge to an integrable divergence. The explicit coefficients and branch selection make this transition usable for asymptotic calculations; convergence, the atom alone, and the formal algebraic transform are not the value credited.
