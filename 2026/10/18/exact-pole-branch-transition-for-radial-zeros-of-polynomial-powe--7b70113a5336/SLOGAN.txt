For the derivatives of \((z^d-1)^n\) at every fixed differentiation ratio \(0<\alpha<d\), the limiting radial zero distribution has an explicit Fuss–Catalan-type moment law and support endpoint \(T=\alpha(d-\alpha)^{d-1}/(d-1)^{d-1}\) in the variable \(t=z^d\). Its isolated root-of-unity atoms vanish exactly at \(\alpha=1\), where the outer density changes from a square-root soft edge to an inverse-square-root singularity.
