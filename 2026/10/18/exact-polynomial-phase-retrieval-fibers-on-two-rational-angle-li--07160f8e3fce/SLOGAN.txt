For a polynomial of degree at most \(d\), modulus data on two lines meeting at a reduced rational angle \(\pi p/q\) have an exactly computable phase fiber of size \(\prod_{\operatorname{Im}\lambda>0}(a_\lambda+b_\lambda+1)\), where the multiplicities count full conjugate pairs of \(q\)-point zero orbits. The sharp largest fiber has \(2^{\lfloor d/q\rfloor}\) classes, so rational angles with \(q>d\) recover **every** such polynomial uniquely up to phase.
