For a uniform permutation, the number of disjoint cycles of the original bijection has covariance exactly \(\operatorname{Cov}(C_n,I_k)=(k-1)/(k^2\binom nk)\) with the record indicator at position \(k\): every nondeterministic record is positively associated with the cycle count. The total cycle–record covariance is \(n^{-1}+(2n^2)^{-1}+O(n^{-3})\), asymptotically dominated by the final record, although cycles and records are classically only equidistributed as marginal statistics.
