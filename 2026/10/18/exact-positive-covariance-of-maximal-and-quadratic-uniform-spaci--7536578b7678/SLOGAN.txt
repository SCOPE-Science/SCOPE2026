In uniform spacings, the largest gap and Greenwood’s sum of squared gaps have the exact strictly positive covariance \(\bigl(H_m^2+H_m^{(2)}-2H_m\bigr)/[m(m+1)(m+2)]\) for every \(m\ge2\). Their Pearson correlation nevertheless tends to zero at the sharp rate \(\bigl(\sqrt6/(2\pi)\bigr)(\log m)^2/\sqrt m\).
