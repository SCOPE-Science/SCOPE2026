For every base \(b\ge3\), the number of ordered positive base-\(b\) palindrome pairs separated by \(b^k\) is exactly \((b-1)^2b^{k-1}+\tau(k)\) for every \(k\ge1\). Each pair is either a divisor-spaced short palindrome followed by a leading \(1\), or a \((2k+1)\)-digit palindrome whose central digit increases by one.
