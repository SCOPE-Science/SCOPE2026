Uniform points in the Lipschitz dual unit ball of a cycle with one exceptional edge have an exactly tridiagonal inverse covariance, with a strictly positive endpoint-pinning correction if and only if that edge is metrically nonredundant. The edge-length scale \(t\asymp\sqrt d\) yields an explicit continuum of endpoint-truncated Brownian path limits, linking a pinned bridge to free Brownian motion and determining the limiting precision stiffness.
