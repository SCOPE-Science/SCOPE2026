# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The atom-row reduction follows from axioms 1–4, and axiom 5 has only two nontrivial inner-antecedent possibilities for each atom. The listed \(k+3\) row types satisfy the projected idempotence condition, and the three choices of bottom-row value exhaust all additional restrictions. This proves the bijection used in the count. Antitone precomplementations are counted independently and recovered uniquely from the resulting operation at consequent \(0\). The elementary asymptotic is checked from the exact counts. A standalone program directly checks every quantified axiom through \(k=4\), agreeing with the formulas; this does not replace the general proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

Chen's 2026 \(\mathsf K\)-algebra paper and Holliday's original preconditional paper give general semantics and consequences of the axioms; neither inspected treatment supplies a normal form or full enumeration for finite length-two lattices. Relevant published research findings address preconditional counts on chains and the known four-element Boolean case, which is explicitly excluded from the new numerical contribution. Separate searches for rank-two/modular/atomic lattice formulas, the identical parameters, and precomplementation subclasses did not find a dominating classification. An unindexed equivalent all-\(k\) result could exist; search absence is not a uniqueness certificate.

## Value — PASS

Finite rank-two atom lattices are the canonical smallest branching bounded lattices; \(k=2\) is Boolean and \(k\ge3\) produces a systematic nondistributive modular family. The theorem classifies every \(\mathsf K\)-preconditional in this infinite family and quantifies precisely how often the standard unary precomplementation construction fails to exhaust it. The limiting induced fraction \(e^{-1}\) supplies a structural asymptotic, not just another isolated small-algebra table.

## Closest literature and limits

The relevant base facts are Holliday's preconditional axioms and precomplementation construction and Chen's identification of \(\mathsf K\)-algebras. Earlier chain classifications address a different lattice geometry, and the known four-element Boolean case equals \(k=2\). The result does not concern conditional identity, semicomplementation, or double-negation axioms required in \(\mathsf T\) and \(\mathsf F\), nor does it enumerate isomorphism classes.
