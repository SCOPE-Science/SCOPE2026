On the bounded lattice with \(k\ge2\) incomparable atoms and only bottom and top besides them, all preconditionals admit an explicit atom-row normal form: there are exactly \(2^k+2k3^{k-1}+(k+3)^k\). The fraction induced by precomplementations tends to \(e^{-1}\), so the limiting fraction not obtained from unary precomplementations is \(1-e^{-1}\).
