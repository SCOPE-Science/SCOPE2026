## correctness

Every partition into exactly four positive parts has unique coordinates (a+b+c+d,b+c+d,c+d,d) with a,b,c>=0,d>=1; its e2 is an explicit degree-two polynomial. Summing nine monomials by geometric-power generating functions yields the integer rational series F(q)=P(q)/D(q), D=(1−q)^6(1+q)^4(1+q²)^3(1+q+q²)^3, deg P<deg D and D(0)=1. If P_p/D_p is reduced in F_p(q), its coefficients are periodic from n=0 with period T iff D_p divides 1−q^T, since the numerator degree is smaller than denominator. P(1)=864, P(−1)=32, P mod Phi3=6q, P mod Phi4=−12 show no cyclotomic cancellation p>=5. Explicit Euclidean reductions for p=2,3 give D2=(q+1)^12 Phi3² and D3=(q−1)^10(q+1)^4. Frobenius multiplicities force exact T2=48, T3=54, T5=300, Tp=12p (p>=7), not merely eventual periods. Independently repeated modular gcd/polynomial divisibility tests at p=2,3,5,7,11,13 and all maximal proper factor periods; symbolic proof covers every prime.
