For the sum of pairwise part-products over all four-part partitions of \(n\), the exact least period modulo every prime is \(48\) for \(2\), \(54\) for \(3\), \(300\) for \(5\), and \(12p\) for every prime \(p\ge7\). A rational generating function and its cyclotomic pole multiplicities prove the infinite prime classification and sharpness from index zero.
