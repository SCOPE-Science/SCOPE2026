The first-zero rank of \(U_{n+1}(s)=sU_n(s)-U_{n-1}(s)\) admits a complete closed distribution as \(s\) varies modulo every odd prime power: the nonsingular counts are totient-weighted divisors of two cyclic-group orders, and the singular classes contribute an exact exceptional tower at \(p=3\). The result determines every attainable rank and its multiplicity, addressing the prime-power parameter question raised by Park.
