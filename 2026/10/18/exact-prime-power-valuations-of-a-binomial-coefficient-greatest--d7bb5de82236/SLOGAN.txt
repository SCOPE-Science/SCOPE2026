For the binomial greatest common divisor \(D(n-1)=\gcd_{2\le q\le n}\binom{q(n-1)}{n-1}\), every prime-power multiplicity is explicit: \(\nu_p(D(n-1))=\max\{0,a-\lfloor\log_p(n/p^a)\rfloor\}\) when \(p^a\parallel n\). This upgrades the known one-versus-non-one criterion to an exact formula for every \(n\ge3\).
