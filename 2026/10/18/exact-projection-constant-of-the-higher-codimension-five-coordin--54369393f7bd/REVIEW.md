# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The explicit rational rank-two projection has the required biorthogonality identities and five equal \(\ell_1\) row sums of \(845/659\). A five-row nonnegative-weight/sign dual certificate gives the matching lower bound against every real projection. Compression and blockwise extension give the result for every \(n\ge3\). All critical rational equalities are replayable with the accompanying standard-library checker. This is an analytical universal proof, not an extrapolation from small dimensions.

**Originality — PASS, with an explicit priority risk.** The closest source, Deręgowska--Lewandowska (2026), constructs the *same* obstruction spaces and an explicit \(13/10\)-norm projection (Proposition 3.4), but states no optimization of its norm. The present number \(845/659<13/10\) and its exact lower certificate are genuinely different conclusions. Checked semantic research records, the available earlier claims, and related projection literature disclosed no stronger implication; the possibility of unindexed older equivalent calculations is not eliminated. Two related papers were inspected only by accessible abstract and scope, and are not used as whole-document noncoverage certificates.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The space family is a distinguished geometric obstruction in a current Banach-space question. The exact norm measures sharp complementability uniformly across all of its higher-codimension instances, improves the previously published witness, and supplies a reusable elementary certificate technique. The result does not settle the main codimension-free Banach–Mazur conjecture.

**Closest literature:** B. Deręgowska and B. Lewandowska, *Projection Constants and Banach-Mazur Distance in Codimension Two*, arXiv:2610.06141v1, §3, Propositions 3.2–3.4.
