For the smallest nontrivial martingale Sinkhorn grid, two symmetric source atoms and three terminal atoms, exact cyclic entropy projections reduce to a scalar recurrence with sharp local factor \(\lambda=a^2(1-T)/[T(T-a^2)]\) and KL factor \(\lambda^2\). The factor approaches one as \(1-\lambda\sim2(T-a)/[a(1-a)]\), quantifying the slowdown at the convex-order boundary.
