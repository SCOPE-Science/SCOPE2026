For capacity-one public-goods sharing on a labeled path or ring, equilibria with exactly \(k\) contributors have complete explicit counting formulas, and closing a path into a ring multiplies their number by \(n/(n-k)\). Under uniform choice among ring equilibria, the contributor fraction approaches \(1-1/\sqrt5\) with Gaussian fluctuations of asymptotic variance \((1-11/(5\sqrt5))n\); the already-known unrefined counting sequence is not claimed as new.
