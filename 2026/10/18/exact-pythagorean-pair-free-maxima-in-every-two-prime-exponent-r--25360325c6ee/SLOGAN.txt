For every exponent rectangle \(\{2^a3^b:0\le a<A,0\le b<B\}\), forbidden Pythagorean-leg pairs form disjoint paths indexed by \(a+2b\), yielding exact maximum admissible cardinalities and the number of maximizing subsets. In square boxes, the maximum follows an explicit four-case formula depending on \(N\bmod4\).
