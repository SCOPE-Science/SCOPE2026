Every two-dimensional antilinear operator with \(A^2=-\rho^2I\) has an exact constrained \(q\)-numerical annulus determined solely by its two singular values; the inner and outer radii are explicit linear combinations of \(\sqrt{1-|q|^2}\) and the singular-value gap. Unequal singular values produce a sharp hole-collapse threshold \(|q|=2\rho/(a+b)\), classifying precisely when zero enters the range.
