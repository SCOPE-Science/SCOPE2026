For \(C_{p^2}\oplus C_p\), the complete parameter-free quantifier-free \(k\)-type spectrum and the automorphism-orbit spectrum admit exact closed formulas, and their difference is \((p^k-1)/(p-1)+p\binom{k}{2}_p\). The smallest finite abelian unary example separating automorphism invariance from quantifier-free definability is \(C_4\oplus C_2\).
