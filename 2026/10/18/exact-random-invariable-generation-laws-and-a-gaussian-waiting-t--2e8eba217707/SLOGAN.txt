For every elementary-abelian-kernel cyclic Frobenius group, the number of uniformly sampled ordered tuples that invariably generate it is given by a closed matrix-rank/Möbius formula. In the fixed-complement scalar family \(\mathbb F_p^n\rtimes C_m\), the invariable-generation waiting time has a Gaussian window of width \(\sqrt{m(m-1)n}\) around \(mn\), with an explicit limiting constant correction to its mean.
