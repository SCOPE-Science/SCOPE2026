For a uniformly random vertex order, FirstFit on the crown graph \(K_{n,n}\) minus a perfect matching uses two colors with probability \(1-1/n\), and its entire higher-color tail has an exact multiplicative formula. In particular its expected color count is \(2+1/n+3/(4n^2)+O(n^{-3})\), while the probability of using the worst-case \(n\) colors is exactly \(40\cdot3^{n-3}n!/(2n)!\).
