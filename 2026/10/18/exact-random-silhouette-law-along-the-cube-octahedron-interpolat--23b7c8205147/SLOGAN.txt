Every generic planar shadow of \(K_t=[-1,1]^3\cap\{\|x\|_1\le t\}\) has exactly twelve vertices throughout \(1<t<2\), even away from the single zonohedral parameter. At the cuboctahedral transition \(t=2\), the silhouette count collapses; in \(2<t<3\) it has a sharp \(12/14\) two-atom law with \(\Pr(N=14)=\frac{12}{\pi}\arctan(3-2\sqrt2)\).
