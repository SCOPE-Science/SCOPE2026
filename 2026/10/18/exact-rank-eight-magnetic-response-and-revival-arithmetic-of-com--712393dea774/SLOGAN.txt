For a single magnetic bond on any complete bipartite Grover walk \(K_{m,n}\) with \(m,n\ge2\), exactly eight spectral modes carry all finite-flux sensitivity: the four-step return defect has rank eight and an explicit sharp norm for every nonzero flux. Exact whole-walk revivals occur on a countable dense set of magnetic phases, with their minimal period determined solely by the denominator of one spectral angle.
