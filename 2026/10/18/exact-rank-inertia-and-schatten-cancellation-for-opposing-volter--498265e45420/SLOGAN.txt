For every integer integration order \(m\), the forward/backward Volterra pencil \(J^m+\lambda(J^*)^m\) collapses from infinite rank to exactly rank \(m\) at the unique parity-selected value \(\lambda=(-1)^{m-1}\). This critical operator has a completely determined real or imaginary spectral inertia and an exact eigenvalue product, while away from cancellation the singular values have sharp asymptotic coefficient \(|1-(-1)^{m-1}\lambda|/\pi^m\) at order \(n^{-m}\).
