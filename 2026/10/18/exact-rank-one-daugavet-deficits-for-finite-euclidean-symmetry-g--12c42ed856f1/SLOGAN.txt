For the full signed-permutation group on real Euclidean \(d\)-space, the worst rank-one group-Daugavet norm at perturbation size \(t>0\) is exactly \(\Phi_t(d^{-1/2})\), with only sparse/uniform vector pairs attaining it. For a finite planar symmetry group with at most \(M\) elements, the best possible profile is \(\Phi_t(\cos(\pi/M))\), uniquely achieved by \(M\) rotations, and its deficit from \(1+t\) is asymptotic to \(\pi^2t/(2(t+2)M^2)\).
