# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The determinant-one signed permutations give four independent relative sign tests X±Y±Z for each of six permutations. If both vectors are fully supported, two zero tests with one sign flipped would force that summand to vanish by subtraction; with both flipped, addition would force X=0. Thus each permutation contributes at most one zero. In the quartic sign product, each single-index term has two equally weighted assignments of opposite parity, and each two-index term is invariant under swapping those two assignments. The alternating sum is zero, excluding exactly five zero-producing permutations. For sparse coordinates, the (1,1), (1,2), (1,3) cases give 16,8,0 zeros respectively, and the (2,2) case has two contributing permutations at most, with zero or two zeros each. For (2,3) two zeros correspond to a directed edge B_j=R B_i; five edges contain both a directed 3-cycle and a reciprocal pair, forcing R³=R²=1 and hence forcing all six edges. This excludes ten zeros. The nine witnesses were independently recounted exactly over Z[ω] and give 24,23,22,21,20,18,16,12,8. For the support-21 witness the cyclic permutations give zero once each and each transposition gives equal nonzero terms with odd sign-sum. The representation is irreducible by diagonal sign eigenspaces and coordinate rotation, and Schur orthogonality with the stated normalization gives the block vuᵀ/3, nonzero of rank one, all other blocks zero. Sign twisting preserves every zero pattern. The uploaded exact checker was inspected, not executed; it is not an exhaustive certificate over C.
