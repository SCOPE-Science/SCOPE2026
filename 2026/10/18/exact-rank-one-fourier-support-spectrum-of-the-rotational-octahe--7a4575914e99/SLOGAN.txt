Rank-one matrix coefficients of either three-dimensional irreducible Fourier block of the rotational octahedral group \(S_4\) have exactly the time-support sizes \(\{8,12,16,18,20,21,22,23,24\}\). A six-permutation alternating quartic identity forbids five zeros when all vector coordinates are nonzero, while a directed three-vertex ratio obstruction rules out ten zeros when a coordinate vanishes.
