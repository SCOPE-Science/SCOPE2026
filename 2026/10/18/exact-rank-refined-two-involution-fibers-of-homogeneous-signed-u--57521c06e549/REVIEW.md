# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The statement quantifies over all odd prime powers and integers \(n\ge2\), \(m\ge1\), with one fixed product matrix. Reduction to a truncated polynomial module, the explicit lifting step \(Y=-DS/2\), fixed-layer stabilizer dimensions, orbit-stabilizer count, and trace on associated graded pieces together establish the complete joint fiber polynomial. The independently written enumerator checks thirteen finite parameter configurations, including exact residual strata. Extension fields rely on the field-uniform proof, not on those examples.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence
The 2016 Marques de Sá paper proves the orbit and allowable factor-similarity classifications for fixed involution products, including these unipotent types. Its Theorem 6.2 counts simultaneous-similarity **orbits**, not matrices in each prescribed-product fiber. The formula here gives the finite-field cardinality of each such stratum and the joint rank polynomial, requiring nontrivial stabilizer-layer counting. De Seguins Pazzis's 2023 result is qualitative existence. Praeger–Seress regular-semisimple counts do not cover the nonsemisimple target. The possibility of a numerical formula elsewhere remains a nonzero priority risk; absence of a matching search result alone is not evidence of novelty.

## Value — PASS
The prescribed-target two-involution fiber is a natural class-algebra factorization invariant. Prior qualitative orbit and rank support do not provide its probability weights: here all \(m+1\) homogeneous unipotent strata have explicit multiplicities for every odd finite field. In even block length the factor ranks coincide across signatures, yet the fiber has nonuniform residual weights, exhibiting structure invisible to rank support alone. The result applies uniformly to an infinite two-parameter family.

## Limitations
The scope excludes characteristic two and mixed Jordan lengths. The finite checks are supplements to the proof; no experiment establishes the infinite assertion. The closest prior orbit classification supplies a starting structure and deserves clear attribution. Independent audit has not been performed.
