For a fixed signed-unipotent matrix \(\varepsilon J_n(1)^{\oplus m}\) of homogeneous Jordan type over any odd finite field, the ordered factorizations into two involutions have an explicit Gaussian-binomial stratification by their residual signature. The exponents change by \(2r(m-r)\) between even and odd block lengths, and the complete joint factor-rank distribution is determined exactly.
