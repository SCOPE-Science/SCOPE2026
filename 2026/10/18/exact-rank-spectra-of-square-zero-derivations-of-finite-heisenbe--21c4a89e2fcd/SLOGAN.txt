For every finite Heisenberg Lie algebra over \(\mathbb F_q\), square-zero derivations have only ranks \(0,1,\ldots,m\), with an exact all-characteristic finite-field rank polynomial expressed through symplectic isotropic subspaces and nonsingular symmetric forms. For fixed \(m\), asymptotically all such derivations have rank \(m\) as \(q\) grows, including through characteristic-two fields.
