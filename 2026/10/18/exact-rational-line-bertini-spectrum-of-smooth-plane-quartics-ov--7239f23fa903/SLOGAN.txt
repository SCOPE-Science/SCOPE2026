Among the \(10920\) geometrically smooth plane quartics over \(\mathbf F_2\), the numbers with exactly \(0,1,\ldots,7\) transverse rational lines are \((25,168,588,2604,3885,2940,686,24)\). The complete spectrum splits into \(78\) projective orbits, and the \(25\) transverse-free curves comprise one known plane-filling orbit of size \(24\) and a single pointless invariant quartic.
