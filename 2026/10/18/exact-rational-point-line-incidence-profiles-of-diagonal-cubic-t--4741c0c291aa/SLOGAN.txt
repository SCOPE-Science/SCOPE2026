Among the seven projective-equivalence classes of smooth diagonal cubic threefolds over \(\mathbf F_7\), exactly three have every rational point on a rational line; the other four have \(189,135,135,162\) uncovered points. A complete seven-class point-line incidence census, including every incidence multiplicity and rational-line count, follows from exhaustive Grassmannian enumeration with an independent local polar check.
