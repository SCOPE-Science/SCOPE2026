For the decreasing constant-opacity atmospheric map, the unique attracting boom–bust two-cycle exists **exactly** inside a computable open interval of the post-clear-skies parameter \(p_1\), provided the response \(p_2\) exceeds an explicit sharp threshold. Outside that interval—including both neutral endpoints—every trajectory converges to one equilibrium, so increasing wind forcing produces a rigorous equilibrium–cycle–equilibrium re-entrance.
