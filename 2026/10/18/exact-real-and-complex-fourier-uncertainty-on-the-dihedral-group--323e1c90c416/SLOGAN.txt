The order-eight dihedral group has the complete complex rank-weighted Fourier uncertainty profile \((8,4,4,2,4,2,3,1)\), while the real profile is \((8,4,6,2,4,2,3,1)\). The sole real/complex gap occurs at three-point support, and a determinant-parity obstruction forces the exact five-point minimum to jump to four between two-point Fourier minima at supports four and six.
