The complete rank-weighted Fourier uncertainty profile of the quaternion group \(Q_8\) is \((8,4,4,2,3,2,3,1)\) over complex signals, but \((8,4,6,2,5,3,5,1)\) over real signals. The difference comes from a nonzero complex isotropic vector making the unique two-dimensional Fourier block rank one, which no real vector can do.
