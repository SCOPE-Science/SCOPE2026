The Heawood graph has exactly \(65\) inclusion-minimal dominating sets in four Fano-geometric families, and its add/delete dominating-set reconfiguration graphs exhibit precise successive isolation barriers at budgets \(4\), \(5\), \(6\), and \(7\). The components become connected at budget \(8\), where the graph has \(5532\) states, radius \(12\), and diameter \(14\); the sharp radius, diameter and earlier component census refine the already known general bipartite connectivity threshold.
