For every Hilbert-space contraction, the exact tolerance for syndetic returns to the starting vector is a continuous Hilbertian seminorm: \(\delta_*(x)^2=\|(I-P)x\|^2+\langle A(I-P)x,(I-P)x\rangle\), where \(P\) is the unimodular pure-point projection and \(A=\mathrm{s}\!\!\!-\!\lim T^{*n}T^n\). This formula distinguishes the sharp \(\sqrt2\) forward-isometry threshold from the stable-orbit threshold \(1\), and classifies every intermediate contraction without requiring normality.
