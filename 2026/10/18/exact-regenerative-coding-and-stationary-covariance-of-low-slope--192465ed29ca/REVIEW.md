# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The map is defined for every integer contraction depth at every step and all conclusions are annealed. The first-return calculation is exact: on each dyadic entrance subinterval, the excursion duration is \(\beta+g+1\) and the affine return map is independent of \(\beta\), with a full branch onto the base and exact normalized weight \(2^{-(g+1)}\). Uniform conditional return coordinates establish independence of successive excursion labels. A direct shellwise transfer equation proves invariance of the stated density without relying on asymptotic approximations. Tonelli's theorem evaluates its entire mass even for an infinite expected contraction depth. The induced full-branch map yields the conservative ergodic tower and uniqueness within the absolutely continuous class. The renewal equation and stationary base mass establish the autocovariance generating function, including its deterministic-depth specialization. The exact-rational replay completed 512 diagnostics. Residual correctness risk is confined to standard measure-theoretic details of tower uniqueness, spelled out in the proof; the computer checks themselves are not treated as a general proof.

Its inspected Section 4.1 states neither an exact invariant density at every shell nor an independent first-return distribution nor the stationary covariance generating function. Specific semantic searches of the published mathematical-record database and the available historical claims yielded no covering statement. Full-text access to the 2019/2020 inducing paper was unavailable; an earlier model-specific calculation remains a residual historical risk. The claim of originality concerns the exact combined classification, not the existence theorem or inducing methodology.

## Mathematical value — PASS

A central motivation of the source paper is finite versus infinite invariant mass caused by random strong contractions **despite no common indifferent fixed point**. Exact iid excursion coding identifies the underlying recurrence mechanism for every discrete contraction law, recovers exact normalization beyond earlier two-sided comparisons, and completely determines the stationary temporal covariance of visits to the natural inducing interval. The formula applies uniformly to finite- and infinite-support laws, and separates finite-mean from infinite-mean regimes without numerical approximation. In the simple deterministic-depth case the exact covariance alternates in sign. This is a natural structural/statistical invariant for this model, not an arbitrary finite computation or a generic deduction merely renamed.
