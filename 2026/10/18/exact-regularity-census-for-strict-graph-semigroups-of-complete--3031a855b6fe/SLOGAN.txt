For every complete multipartite graph with at least three parts, the edge-fiber strict transformation semigroup has exact rank-refined formulas for all elements, regular elements and idempotents, together with a complete characterization of when the semigroup is regular. With \(r\) equal parts of fixed size \(n\ge2\), the fraction of regular elements is asymptotic to \(2^n n!\,r^{-(n-1)}/n^n\), so regularity becomes polynomially rare.
