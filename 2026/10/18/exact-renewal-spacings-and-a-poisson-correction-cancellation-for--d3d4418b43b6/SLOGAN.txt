For one-dimensional Matérn-I isolation followed by independent rare thinning, the complete finite-\(n\) nearest-neighbor spacing law is an explicit renewal transform. The first correction to exponential spacing changes sign at the unique positive \(t\) satisfying \(e^{t/2}=1+t\), where the whole order-\(1/n\) Laplace-transform error cancels for every fixed frequency.
