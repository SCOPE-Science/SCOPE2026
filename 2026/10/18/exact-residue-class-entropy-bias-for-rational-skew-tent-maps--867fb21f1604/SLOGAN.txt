For rational full-branch skew tent maps, the stationary entropy of independent equal-cell coarse-graining has an exact finite-resolution error \(C_{N\bmod(p+q)}/N\), with explicit residue coefficients. In the map with turning point \(1/5\), that error is positive for every \(N\equiv4\pmod5\) and negative for every \(N\equiv1\pmod5\): even the direction of convergence alternates arithmetically.
