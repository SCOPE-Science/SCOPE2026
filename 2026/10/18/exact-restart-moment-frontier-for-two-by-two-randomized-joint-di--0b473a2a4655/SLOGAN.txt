For a natural two-by-two nearly commuting symmetric matrix pair, Gaussian best-of-trials joint diagonalization has exact expected squared error \(2\varepsilon\) after one trial, asymptotic error \((16/\pi^2)\varepsilon^2\log(1/\varepsilon)\) after two, and \((48\log2/\pi^2)\varepsilon^2\) after three. Thus three independent trials are exactly the threshold for constant-factor expected squared error relative to the best orthogonal diagonalizer on this family.
