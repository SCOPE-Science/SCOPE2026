# Mathematical assessment

## Correctness — PASS

Every Lie endomorphism sends the derived center to itself. Writing it in blocks gives \(F(v+tz)=Av+(c(v)+\lambda t)z\), with bracket preservation equivalent to \(\omega(Av,Aw)=\lambda\omega(v,w)\), and composition-idempotence equivalent to \(A^2=A\), \(\lambda^2=\lambda\), \(c\circ A+\lambda c=c\). When \(\lambda=1\), nondegeneracy forces the identity; when \(\lambda=0\), the image of \(A\) is totally isotropic, and \(c\) factors uniquely through it. Counting complements and functionals proves the rank formula and individual image multiplicity. The attached exact checker verifies small cases by exhaustive matrix enumeration and independently verifies isotropic-space counts; these finite checks are not substituted for proof.
