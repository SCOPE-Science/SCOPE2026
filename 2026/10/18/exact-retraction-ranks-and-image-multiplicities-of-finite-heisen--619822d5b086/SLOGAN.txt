For the finite Heisenberg Lie algebra \(H_n(q)\), every nonidentity retraction annihilates the center and has a totally isotropic graph as its image: its possible vector-space ranks are exactly \(0,\ldots,n\), while the identity alone has rank \(2n+1\). The exact number at rank \(r\le n\) is \(I_{n,r}(q)q^{r(2n-r+1)}\), and each specified rank-\(r\) image supports \(q^{r(2n-r)}\) retractions.
