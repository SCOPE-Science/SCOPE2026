Under independent continuous marginals, the two opposite directions of Lin–Han's two-right-neighbor rank coefficient have exact positive finite-sample correlation \(10(4n^4+n^3+11n^2-127n+147)/[n(n-1)(n+1)(4n^2+14n+35)]\) for \(n\ge4\). The correlation peaks uniquely at \(n=5\) and decays as \(10/n\), quantifying the second-moment dependence relevant to symmetrizing the statistic without assuming a joint central limit theorem.
