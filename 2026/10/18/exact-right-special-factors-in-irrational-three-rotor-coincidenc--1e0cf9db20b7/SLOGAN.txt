For every irrational free three-rotor slope \(\sigma>1\) and every \(n\geq1\), the **unique** right-special factor of the four-symbol coincidence code is the reverse of its first \(n\) symbols after the initial triple-coincidence marker. Both following symbols are \(1\) and \(3\), proving the specific reverse-prefix conjecture of Krishnaswami and Rameshan (2026).
