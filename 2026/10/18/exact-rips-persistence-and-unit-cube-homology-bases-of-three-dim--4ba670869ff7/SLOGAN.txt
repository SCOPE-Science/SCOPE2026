Every finite three-dimensional Manhattan grid box of side lengths \(a,b,c\) has scale-two Rips complex \(\bigvee^{abc}S^3\), with an integral homology basis canonically indexed up to orientation by its unit cubes. Its full Rips filtration has only the degree-one bars \([1,2)\) and degree-three bars \([2,3)\), besides degree-zero bars.
