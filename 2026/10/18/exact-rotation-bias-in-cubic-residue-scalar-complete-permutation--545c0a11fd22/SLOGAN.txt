For cubic-residue scalar complete-permutation polynomials over every prime-power field \(q\equiv1\pmod9\), the induced three-fiber rotations always outnumber transpositions by exactly \((q-4)|J(\chi,\chi)-2|^2/27>0\). All six fiber permutations occur, and the fraction of complete coefficient functions is \(2/9-5/(3q)+O(q^{-3/2})\).
