For a binary XOR-feedback shift register supplied with one fresh fair bit at a fixed site \(j\), the exact distance to full uniformity after \(t\) steps is \(1-2^{\min\{t,n+1-\gcd(j,n+1)\}-n}\). Coprime sites sample uniformly for the first time at step \(n\); other sites remain nonergodic and have explicitly classified cyclic-parity communication classes.
