For the two-particle Grover walk on every complete bipartite graph \(K_{m,n}\), the entire Schmidt spectrum is governed by a rank-two rectangular reflection, and the entropy is bounded by \(\log 8\) regardless of graph size. The exact peak is \(\log 2+2h(\mu_+)\), and maximal Hilbert-space entanglement occurs only on \(K_{1,1}\) or \(K_{2,2}\), with the regular-family saturation cases acknowledged as previously known.
