On an \(n\)-world intuitionistic chain, the full two-modal behaviour of nonempty constructive neighbourhood frames is classified by two independent nondecreasing sequences of length \(n\), giving exactly \(\binom{2n-1}{n}^{2}\) classes. Every class has a representative with at most two neighbourhoods per world, each of size at most two, and this bound is sharp for \(n\ge2\).
