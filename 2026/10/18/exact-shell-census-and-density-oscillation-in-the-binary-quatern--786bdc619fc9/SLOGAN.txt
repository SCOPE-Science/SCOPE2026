For every \(h\ge1\), the binary-versus-base-four palindrome counting race has an exact win/loss/tie census on \([16^h,16^{h+1})\), with winning count \(39\cdot16^h/56+1-2^h/14\). The set of integers where binary palindromes lead has no natural density: three explicit subsequences of initial-segment proportions converge to \(13/280\), \(3/35\), and \(3/140\).
