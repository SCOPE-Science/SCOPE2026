For every tower-height cutoff \(k\ge2\), all two-point densities at shifts \(t\ne0\) admit an explicit convergent prime product, and the minimizing shifts are exactly the integers free of \(T_k\)-th prime powers, with \(T_1=2\) and \(T_{k+1}=2^{T_k}\). The maximum is never attained, factorial shifts approach the one-point density, and the height-level indicator has both negative and positive shifted covariance.
