For two independent arclength-uniform points on any convex polygonal boundary, the small-distance probability is **exactly quadratic**: \(F_K(t)=2t/L+B(K)t^2/L^2\), with a corner coefficient depending only on exterior angles. At fixed side count, equiangular polygons uniquely minimize that coefficient, and its excess controls squared angular variation with a sharp universal factor \(1/6\).
