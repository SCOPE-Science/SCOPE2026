For the continuous one-element-per-arm linear-spline \(L^2\) projector on any metric star with at least three arms, the exact \(L^\infty\) norm is \((7S+3h)/(3(S+h))\), depending only on the total length \(S\) and shortest arm \(h\). Equal arms uniquely minimize the norm, while arbitrary arm collapse pushes it toward the unattained ceiling \(7/3\); a degree-three threshold separates the equilateral value \(2\) from strictly larger amplification.
