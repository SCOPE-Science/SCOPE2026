All sixteen length-eight binary reverse-complement palindromes fit in a cyclic word of exactly \(67\) bits, and no shorter cycle works; exactly \(1232\) optimal indexed cycles start with `00001111`. The corresponding exact (minimum length, rooted count) pairs for lengths \(2\), \(4\), and \(6\) are \((2,1)\), \((9,2)\), and \((26,14)\).
