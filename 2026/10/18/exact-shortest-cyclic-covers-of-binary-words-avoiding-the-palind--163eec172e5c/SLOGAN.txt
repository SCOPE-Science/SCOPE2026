For every \(k\ge3\), the shortest cyclic binary string covering all length-\(k\) words without `010` has repetition overhead generated by \(\frac{z^3(1-2z+2z^2)}{(1-z)^2(1-2z+z^2-z^3)}\). Every optimal cover must itself contain `010`, and complementing the bits gives the corresponding result for `101`.
