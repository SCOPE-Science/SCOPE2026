For any alphabet size \(q\ge2\) and forbidden run \(a^r\) with \(r\ge2\), the shortest legal cyclic word covering **every linearly admissible** length-\(n\) window, \(n\ge r\), has exactly \(E_{q,r}(n)+C_{q,r}(n)\) positions, where \(\sum_{n\ge r}C_{q,r}(n)z^{n-r}=\frac{(q-1)\sum_{j=1}^{r-1}jz^j}{[1-(q-1)\sum_{j=1}^{r-1}z^j][1-(q-1)\sum_{j=1}^{r}z^j]}\). An explicit reversal matching of imbalanced de Bruijn vertices constructs the optimum and an integer potential certifies it.
