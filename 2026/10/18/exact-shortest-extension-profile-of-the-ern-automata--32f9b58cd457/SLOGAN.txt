In the classical \(n\)-state Černý automaton, every proper nonempty subset has a unique shortest preimage-extending word: \(ba^r\), where \(r\) is the location of its first cyclic \(01\) boundary, starting at position zero. Exactly \(n-1\) subsets need the maximum length \(n\), whereas the average minimum length tends to \(3\); the complete distribution and each subset-size refinement have explicit formulas.
