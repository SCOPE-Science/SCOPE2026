For every alphabet of \(q\ge2\) symbols and subsequence universality index \(k\ge1\), the shortest universal palindrome has length \(qk\) for even \(k\) and \(q(k+1)-1\) for odd \(k\). All minimizers consist of mirrored permutation blocks (with a minimal all-symbol palindrome in the odd center), giving exactly \((q!)^{\lceil k/2\rceil}\) distinct optimal labeled words.
