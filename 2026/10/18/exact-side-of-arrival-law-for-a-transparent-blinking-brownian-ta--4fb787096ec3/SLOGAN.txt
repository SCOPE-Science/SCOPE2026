For a transparent two-state blinking target under Brownian motion with position-only Poisson resetting, the probability of first detection from the side opposite the reset point is exactly \(\frac{\alpha}{2(\alpha+\beta)}(1-\sqrt{r/(r+\alpha+\beta)})(1+\frac{r}{\alpha+\beta}e^{-x_0\sqrt{(r+\alpha+\beta)/D}})\). This side probability decreases strictly with resetting, increases strictly with gate-switching speed at a fixed inactive fraction, and retains a nonzero fast-gating limit despite convergence of ordinary first-passage-time statistics.
