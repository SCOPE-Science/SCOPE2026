The formerly two-valued Sierpiński domination number \(\gamma_{\mathrm S}(C_n,C_{3k+1})\) is determined for all \(n\ge3\) and \(k\ge1\): it equals \(kn\) or \(kn+1\), with exact exceptional cases classified by short closed walks on a cyclic group. In particular, for every \(k\ge3\), only \(n=3\) and \(n=6\) require the extra vertex.
