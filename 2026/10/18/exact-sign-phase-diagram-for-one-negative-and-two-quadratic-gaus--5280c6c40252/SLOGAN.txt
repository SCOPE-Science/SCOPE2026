For a three-dimensional centered Gaussian vector, the sign of the product comparison \(\mathbb E[|X_1|^{-s}X_2^2X_3^2]-\mathbb E|X_1|^{-s}\) is determined exactly by a quadratic polynomial in the three correlations. In the equicorrelated family, strictly positive correlations produce a unique exponent threshold at which the inequality reverses, whereas strictly negative correlations always give the positive sign.
