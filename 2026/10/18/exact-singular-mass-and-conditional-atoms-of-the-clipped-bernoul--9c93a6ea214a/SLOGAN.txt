Every nonzero, nonextremal parameter of the correlation-clipped Boolean-operator copula has singular mass \(-\rho^2\log(\rho^2)/(1-\rho^2)\) concentrated on two explicit non-diagonal curves, in addition to an absolutely continuous part. The conditional jumps are explicit, and at \(|\rho|=1/2\) almost 46.21% of the joint probability is singular.
