# Scientific review: exact skew-line capacity of the Yu–Zhu quintic

## Correctness — PASS
The specified smooth quintic has precisely the 85 explicitly labeled lines by Yu–Zhu's source theorem. The accompanying standard-library checker reconstructs those 85 pairs of linear equations over the exact algebraic field generated by fourth roots of 2 and 3 and by \(i\). Its 3,570 determinant tests establish the intersection graph without numerical tolerance. An explicit 18-line witness gives the lower bound. Nineteen listed meeting-line cliques cover all vertices; a hypothetical 19-skew set would be a transversal avoiding every repeated clique vertex. Eight specified residual four-element blocks have no pairwise-skew transversal, as exhaustively checked over 65,536 choices; an independent branch-and-color procedure reaches the same upper bound. The explicit witness, graph census, clique cover, and obstruction were reproduced from the actual embedded checker. Assumptions: characteristic zero and the single parameter \(t=-1/8\). The all-lines completeness theorem is sourced, not reproved.

## Originality — PASS, with stated residual risk
Yu–Zhu provide the 85-line list and lattice rank and construct 24 mutually skew lines on other, 79-line quintics; they do not claim the largest skew set for the present 85-line member. The previously published exact 85-line incidence-spectrum note provides the graph, valency, edge counts, and spectrum, but not its independence number or a maximum-skew certificate. General line-counting bounds do not imply the precise maximum 18. The current exact combinatorial certificate adds a sharp natural invariant, not an independent discovery of the source graph. Because the full graph was computable before this evaluation, an unlocated earlier exact optimization remains a real originality risk; the literature comparison is not an exhaustive historical uniqueness claim.

## Value — PASS
For a smooth surface with a record 85 projective lines, the maximum number that can be mutually disjoint is a directly motivated invariant. The exact value 18 is geometrically informative and distinguishes this surface from the different Yu–Zhu 79-line surfaces exhibiting sets of 24 skew lines. The proof offers a compact, reproducible sharp obstruction, not merely a sample selection, spectral restatement, or arbitrary graph statistic.
