On \(\mathbb Z_8\times\mathbb Z_2\), exactly \(1611\) of the \(65535\) nonempty subsets are spectral, partitioning into precisely \(43\) affine orbits. An exhaustive cyclotomic-integer classification determines the number of distinct Fourier bases and tiling complements for every subset, showing that these two multiplicities can differ even though spectrality and tiling are equivalent.
