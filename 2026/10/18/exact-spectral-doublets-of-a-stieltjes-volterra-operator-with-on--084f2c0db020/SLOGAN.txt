For strict Stieltjes–Volterra integration on \(L^2(dt+a\delta_{1/2})\), every singular value lies on one of two explicit cotangent-quantization branches, and neighboring high-rank singular values pair at the sharp rate \(s_{2m+1}-s_{2m+2}\sim(4\pi^4a^2m^4)^{-1}\). This symmetric one-impulse clock thus has a quartic intra-pair spectral gap despite the ordinary \(n^{-1}\) singular-value scale.
