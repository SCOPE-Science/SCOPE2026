# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof explicitly derives all six entries of the matrix-square map, splits the input domain into the disjoint cases where the first and fourth superdiagonal entries vanish together or not, and counts the first case by rank-one outer-product factorizations. The other case is resolved by linear elimination; adding contributions yields every pointwise formula. Summing target strata independently recovers the total input cardinality. A standalone matrix-multiplication verifier exhaustively checks all prescribed targets over \(\mathbb F_2\) and \(\mathbb F_4\), and checks selected fibers over \(\mathbb F_8\). The symbolic proof, not the finite tests, establishes the assertion for every even prime power.

## Originality — PASS (bounded examined coverage)

Dolfi–Singh–Yadav (2019) study unitriangular power images and publish small-field image sizes, not this pointwise nonzero square-root fiber distribution. Bier (2012) addresses verbal subgroup width rather than fiber cardinalities. Ekhad–Zeilberger (1995/1996) counts zero squares, hence covers the identity fiber, which is explicitly excluded from the novelty component. Semantic searches over mathematical records and comparison with accessible prior findings found no nonidentity fiber theorem with the same quantified claim. This is a same-model best-knowledge originality assessment, not a certificate of historical uniqueness. Residual risk: older root-counting formulae in unindexed or non-English literature could subsume the claim.

## Value — PASS

The first non-surjective square-map dimension in characteristic two is the natural boundary of the structural problem. The complete fiber spectrum, non-square obstruction, and all-target enumeration refine earlier existence, width and image-size results, determine an exact distribution on a standard finite nilpotent group across every even extension field, and give substantially more information than either a single zero-square count or an isolated small-field computation.

## Limits and prior sources

The formulas concern only five-by-five unitriangular matrices in characteristic two. Principal comparisons: Dolfi–Singh–Yadav, arXiv:1905.04244, Sections 1–3; Bier, DOI 10.1142/S0218196712500191, Theorems 3 and 5; Ekhad–Zeilberger, DOI 10.37236/1226, zero-square enumeration; Wu–Zhou, arXiv:2609.00533, different Cholesky fibers. The identity fiber and the already published image sizes at two small fields are not claimed as new. Independent mathematical review is absent.
