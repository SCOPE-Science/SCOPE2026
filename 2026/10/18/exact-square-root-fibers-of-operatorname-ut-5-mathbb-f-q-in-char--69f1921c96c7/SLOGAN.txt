For every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\) of characteristic two, the square map on \(\operatorname{UT}_5(\mathbb F_q)\) has exactly five nonzero fiber sizes, with a closed polynomial target multiplicity for each size and a complete prescribed-target criterion. Its image has \(q^6-q^5+q^4+q^3-q^2\) elements; the known identity fiber is distinguished from the newly assessed nonidentity distribution.
