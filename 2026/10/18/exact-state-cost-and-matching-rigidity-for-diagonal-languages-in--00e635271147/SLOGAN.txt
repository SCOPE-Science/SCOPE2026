For the finite diagonal language \(D_n=\{a_i a_i:1\le i\le n\}\), the exact minimum epsilon-free NFA state counts are \(n+2\) for planar graphs, \(1+n+\lceil n/2\rceil\) for bipartite cacti, and \(2n+1\) for trees. Every optimal cactus representation is forced by pairing alphabet symbols (with one singleton if needed), giving exactly \(n!/(2^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}\lfloor n/2\rfloor!)\) nonisomorphic optimal representations with fixed letter labels.
