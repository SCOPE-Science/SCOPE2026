For a Brownian/ballistic intermittent walker with full Poisson restart, every choice of reset-phase mixture has exactly the ordinary Brownian-resetting Laplace position law if and only if \(v^2=D(\beta+r)\). At this sharp spatial cloaking condition, the stationary all-lag covariance separates the otherwise identical position marginals strictly and recovers the hidden reset mixture from any single positive time lag.
