For any simple star-shaped polygon, a unique explicit tangential-shear choice makes nearby radial perturbations cross **every** successive vertex ray in exactly the unperturbed time, producing finite-amplitude straight-line local isochrons. The full radial return is precisely \(\delta\mapsto (\prod_i e^{-\kappa_i/\omega_i})\delta\), and a regular polygon admits a closed hyperbolic-cotangent shear formula.
