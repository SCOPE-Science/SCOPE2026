Every noncomplete power \(C_n^k\) of a cycle has strong-majority vertex-colouring number two or three: for even \(k\), two colours work precisely when \(n\) is even, while for odd \(k\), they work precisely when \(2^{\nu_2(k+1)+1}\) divides \(n\). A single explicit three-colour word works in every remaining case, and when \(\gcd(n,k)=1\) the exact number of named two-colour solutions is \(2^{\gcd(n,k+1)}\) whenever they exist.
