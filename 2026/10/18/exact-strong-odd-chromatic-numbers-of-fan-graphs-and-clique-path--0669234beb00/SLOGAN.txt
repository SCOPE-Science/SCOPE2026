For every fan \(K_1\vee P_n\), the strong odd chromatic number is exactly \(1+f(n)\): the first six paths need all-distinct colors, but thereafter \(f(n)\) takes the values \(3,4,5\) according to the path length modulo six. The same exact formula gains \(r-1\) colors when the universal vertex is replaced by a clique \(K_r\).
