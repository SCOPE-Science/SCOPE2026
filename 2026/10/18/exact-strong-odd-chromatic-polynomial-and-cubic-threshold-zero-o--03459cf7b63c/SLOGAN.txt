The complete labeled-palette strong-odd coloring polynomial of every sunlet \(C_n\circ K_1\) has a fixed five-factor recurrence in the cycle length. It has an exact chromatic-number jump from \(2\) (even \(n\)) to \(4\) (odd \(n\)); for odd \(n\), the polynomial vanishes to order exactly three at \(k=3\), with leading coefficient \(n(n^2-1)/4\).
