For the metric tetrahedron with five unit edges and one edge of length \(\lambda\), the locus \(K\sim4p\) consists of exactly two vertices for \(0<\lambda<1\) and exactly four points for \(\lambda\ge1\). At \(\lambda>1\), two subcanonical vertices move into the variable edge to distances \((\lambda-1)/4\) and \((3\lambda+1)/4\) from one endpoint; at \(\lambda=1\), all four vertices are subcanonical.
