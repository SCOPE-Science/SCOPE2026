For every fixed alphabet size \(q\ge2\), palindromic words have an explicit rational generating function recording both their lengths and their scattered-subsequence universality indices. The same enumeration gives exact shortest universal-palindrome counts and a Gaussian limit law with variance coefficient \(2(q^2H_q^{(2)}-qH_q)/(qH_q)^3\); the underlying palindrome-index symmetry lemma is credited to earlier work.
