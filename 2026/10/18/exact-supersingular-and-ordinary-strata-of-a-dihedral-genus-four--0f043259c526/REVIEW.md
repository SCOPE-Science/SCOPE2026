# Review of the characteristic-eleven dihedral stratum

## Correctness — PASS
Direct extraction of all Hasse–Witt coefficients from \(f_a(x)^5\) gives the two-block matrix. The two nonzero entry functions have no common root, so a nonordinary member has Cartier rank two and Frobenius stable rank zero. The two involution quotients each have genus two; their Jacobian isogeny decomposition forces supersingularity, which the rank condition alone would not establish. Discriminant and norm prove exact root counts, and the established normal-form isomorphism criterion proves the class count. The dependency-free verifier reproduced the coefficient identities and rational sanity checks. No unproved computational extrapolation is used.

## Originality — PASS
Ohashi–Kudo (arXiv:2312.16858, §3.2) provides the normal form, sparse Cartier framework, and classification of superspecial solutions, not the non-superspecial \(11\)-rank-zero locus at characteristic eleven. Cavalieri–Pries (arXiv:2308.14891, §8) proves a weighted nonordinary mass formula and explicitly leaves the \(a\)-number uncomputed in general. Neither assertion entails the five geometric parameters and three isomorphism classes without the new calculations. General genus-four supersingular existence and odd-abelian-cover results do not dominate this statement. There is residual risk of an unindexed equivalent computation.

## Value — PASS
Determining the entire nonordinary locus, its arithmetic type, and its moduli classes for a motivated cyclic/dihedral genus-four family supplies exact data unavailable from a weighted count. In particular all exceptional Jacobians are supersingular but not superspecial, while every other member is ordinary: there are no intermediate \(11\)-ranks in this family.

## Scope and external validation
The classification holds only in characteristic eleven and for the smooth normal-form family. The normal-form isomorphism criterion is imported from primary literature; no separate formalization or independent peer review is asserted.
