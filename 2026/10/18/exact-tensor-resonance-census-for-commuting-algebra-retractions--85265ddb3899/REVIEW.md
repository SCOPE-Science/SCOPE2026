# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof reconstructs all algebra maps from their generator images in the radical, then identifies an endomorphism with a triangular linear map on \(V\oplus(V\otimes V)\). The ordered retractions commute exactly when these maps are commuting idempotents. The crucial nonstandard step—the existence of a *common algebra-automorphism* linearizing all maps—is proved using the semisimple Boolean operator algebra \(B\cong\mathbb F_q^{2^k}\): the invariant radical layer admits a common splitting, and each splitting is realized by the actual automorphism \(v\mapsto v+C(v)\). Its solution ambiguity is precisely \(\operatorname{Hom}_B(V,V\otimes V)\), whose dimension is the explicit tensor resonance \(H(r)\). This reconstructs every factor in the formula, with all characteristics and boundary profiles covered. Independent direct finite-field row-reduction and binary exhaustive composition checks agree with the proposed counts.

## Originality — PASS within examined coverage

The motivating 2023 idempotent-pre-endomorphism paper gives kernel/image decompositions for individual maps but does not provide a simultaneous census or tensor-resonance multiplicity. The 2021 automorphism/gradation framework concerns invertible maps and group gradings, whereas the new maps form a Boolean semilattice of generally noninvertible endomorphisms. The 2024 commuting-matrix enumeration treats representations on modules over commutative rings, not the specified simultaneous retractions of a truncated *noncommutative* free algebra. Other publicly indexed tensor centralizer and short-local-algebra invariants are on different objects. The general splitting theorem for representations of a finite product of fields is classical and is **not** asserted to be original; the explicit joint orbit and embedded-map enumeration is the assessed new claim. A previously published equivalent finite-field enumeration remains a residual possibility; no historical uniqueness is asserted.

## Value — PASS

The invariant is motivated by simultaneous splitting of associative algebra extensions and records, for every tuple of retractions, the complete ranks and image sizes of all intersections of their images. Its finite formula applies to every field size, generator number and tuple length, and detects an exceptional off-diagonal tensor-resonance orbit already for two generators and two retractions. These are complete natural enumeration and orbit results, rather than isolated low-dimensional samples or routine repetitions of a single-retraction count. The fact that these values are expressed by a finite closed sum, rather than a single product, is intrinsic to the multiple joint eigenspaces.
