In a uniform rooted recursive tree with \(n\ge4\) vertices, the number of terminal vertices and the number of unordered terminal-sibling pairs have the exact covariance \(n/8\), yielding a size-independent least-squares slope of \(3/2\). The probability that an older terminal vertex \(i\) and a younger terminal vertex \(j\) are siblings depends only on \(i\), not on \(j\).
