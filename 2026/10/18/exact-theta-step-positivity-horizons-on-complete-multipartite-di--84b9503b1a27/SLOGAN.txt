For diffusion on an unweighted complete multipartite graph without singleton classes, the exact positivity-preserving \(\theta\)-step horizon depends only on the total number \(N\) of vertices and the smallest class size \(s\), with a closed radical formula and an unconditional-positivity transition at \(\theta=1-1/N\). The smallest classes determine the first loss of positivity, irrespective of every other part size.
