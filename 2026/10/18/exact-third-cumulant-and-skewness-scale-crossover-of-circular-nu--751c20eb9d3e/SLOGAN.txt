For \(n\) independent uniform points on a circle and interval width \(0<s\le1/3\), the number variance has an exact strictly positive third cumulant \(2n(n-1)s^4[(1-2s)^2+(n-2)(11s/5-4s^2)]\). Its standardized skewness changes asymptotic mechanism precisely when \(ns\) is order one: repeated pairs dominate below that scale and close-point triangles dominate above it.
