# Scientific review — central sections of the regular truncated tetrahedron

## Correctness — PASS

For generic central normals, section vertices are precisely the crossing polytope edges. The eighteen-edge graph is explicitly constructed from the twelve directed-edge vertices; the identity \(N=4+a+b+c+d-2ab\) follows by exhausting triangular-face and bridge edges. The 48 signed-permutation spherical sectors are congruent for the section count. Exact halfplane clipping exhausts all sixteen sign choices inside the fundamental cone and produces six nondegenerate regions, with vertex counts 4, 5, 6. The quadrilateral regions are two explicit spherical triangles, evaluated by a determinant/dot-product solid-angle formula. The expected count is independently determined by the common edge angular separation \(\arccos(7/11)\). No finite experiment is used to infer an all-orientations statement.
