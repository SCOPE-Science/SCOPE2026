A central plane through a uniformly oriented regular truncated tetrahedron cuts a quadrilateral, pentagon, or hexagon with exact probabilities \(0.2159360853\ldots\), \(0.5202474652\ldots\), and \(0.2638164494\ldots\). A six-chamber spherical decomposition proves the complete distribution and gives the mean number of section vertices \(18\arccos(7/11)/\pi\).
