For every right rectangular pyramid, the Crofton-random affine plane section is a triangle, quadrilateral, or pentagon with a complete exact two-parameter arctangent probability law. The mean number of sides is \(2\pi(a+b+\sqrt{a^2+b^2+h^2})/[\pi(a+b)+2J]\), where \(J\) is an explicit elementary support-gap integral.
