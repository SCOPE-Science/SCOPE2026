The complete binary Hamming-weight enumerator for positive multiples of \(2^m-1\) below \(2^{3m}\) is controlled by a fixed \(2\times2\) transfer matrix, uniformly for all \(m\ge2\). Exactly the weights \(m,m+1,\ldots,2m,3m\) occur; the next-to-minimum weight appears first at multiplier \(2^m+3\), and the coefficient at weight \(m+1\) is \(m3^{m-2}\).
