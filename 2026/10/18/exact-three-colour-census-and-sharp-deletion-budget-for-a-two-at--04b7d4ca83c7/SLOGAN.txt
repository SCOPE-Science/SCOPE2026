For three-colour teams with complete two-by-\(n\) support, the disjunction \(\operatorname{dep}(x,z)\vee\operatorname{dep}(y,z)\) holds in exactly \(3\cdot7^n+6\cdot6^n-21\cdot5^n+18\cdot4^n-5\cdot3^n\) of the \(9^n\) labelled colourings. Even the worst colouring becomes satisfying after deleting exactly at most \(\lfloor n/3\rfloor\) assignments, and this bound is attained for every \(n\).
