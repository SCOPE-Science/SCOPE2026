Among uniform permutations consisting of one \(n\)-cycle, each length-three consecutive monotone pattern has the same mean count as in an unrestricted permutation, but patterns \(132\) and \(213\) each lose exactly \(1/[2(n-1)]\) expected occurrences while \(231\) and \(312\) each gain the same amount, for every \(n\ge4\). The entire six-pattern bias is exact and follows from a path-contraction count of partial permutations.
