For the twelve vertices of a regular cuboctahedron on the unit sphere, the nearest-vertex angular distance of a uniform direction has an exact three-regime elementary distribution, with pair overlaps beginning at \(\pi/6\), triple overlaps at \(\arccos\sqrt{2/3}\), and complete coverage at \(\pi/4\). Its uncovered fraction near the covering radius has the sharp expansion \(6\varepsilon^2/\pi+2\varepsilon^3/\pi+O(\varepsilon^4)\).
