On an antiferromagnetic Ising wheel, allowing arbitrary two-spin flips eliminates no single-spin minima, but three-spin flips remove precisely the cyclic \(BBABB\) defects. The exact three-spin-stable entropy drops from the one-spin Perrin value \(\log\alpha\) to \(\log\beta\), where \(\alpha^3=\alpha+1\) and \(\beta^5=\beta^3+1\).
