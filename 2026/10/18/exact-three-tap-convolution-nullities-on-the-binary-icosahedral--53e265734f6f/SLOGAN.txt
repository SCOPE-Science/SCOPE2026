On the binary icosahedral group, the nullity of every unit-coefficient three-tap convolution depends only on the three pairwise quotient orders. Exactly \(121{,}600\) of the \(\binom{120}{3}=280{,}840\) supports are singular; the nine nullities include the exceptional value \(22\), and the maximum \(80\) occurs exactly on cosets of order-three subgroups.
