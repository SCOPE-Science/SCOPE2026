For a pair of interacting exclusion lanes with an exact Bernoulli stationary state, the density allocation maximizing summed signed current changes abruptly at repulsive coupling \(c_*=1.3570032053\ldots\): below it both lanes are equally occupied, while above it exactly one lane is half-filled and the other empty. The globally optimal switch lies between the two distinct local-stability thresholds \(4/3\) and \((5-\sqrt5)/2\), giving a mathematically exact interval of competing throughput optima.
