For a planar circular annulus, the \(n\)-th positive radial Fourier zero is always simple and trapped between consecutive zeros of \(J_0\), while its radius as the hole widens has **exactly \(n-1\) nondegenerate turning points**. Those turning points are given uniquely by an explicit pair of ordinary Bessel equations, with alternating maxima and minima and sharp disk-to-thin-ring endpoint limits.
