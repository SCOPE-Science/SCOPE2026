In a four-channel microbial cell–signal model, the stationary Gaussian **conditional** signal variance has a sharp amplification/suppression boundary and can peak at exactly one finite signal-turnover rate. An exact formula identifies when the peak exists and determines its location and height; for the published benchmark the unique optimum is \(r_*=1.122837273492\ldots\) with normalized variance \(2.014648952177\ldots\).
