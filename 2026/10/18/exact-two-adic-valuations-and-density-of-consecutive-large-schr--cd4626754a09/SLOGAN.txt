For the classical large Schröder numbers, every positive-index forward difference has exact two-adic valuation \(2\) at odd indices and \(1+\nu_2(n(n+2))\) at even indices. Thus valuation three never occurs, and each valuation \(j\ge4\) has exact natural density \(2^{-(j-2)}\); the mechanism is a pair of zeros of a continuous two-adic interpolation.
