For complete deterministic automata, every nontrivial one-symbol rotation of a word can be separated with two states, and every nontrivial two-symbol rotation can be separated with three states, over any finite alphabet. Both bounds are sharp at every admissible length: \(R_{\Sigma}(n,k)=2\) for \(\min\{k,n-k\}=1\) and \(R_{\Sigma}(n,k)=3\) for \(\min\{k,n-k\}=2\).
