# Verification of cyclic-separation theorem

Run `python3 verify.py` in the directory containing the bundled files. The verifier performs four separate scientific checks: (i) computes by closure every transformation generated by the two displayed maps on three states; (ii) checks the precise set commuting with the image of `01`; (iii) forms a complete product automaton with the three-state recognizer of the regular expression `(01)*`, and checks equivalence by reachability of its entire finite state space; and (iv) checks the constructed separators and lower-bound family on finite inputs. The output is recorded in `verification_output.txt`.

The finite product-state closure in (iii) is an **infinite-language equivalence proof** because every possible continuation corresponds to a transition within the closed graph; it is not a finite sample of words. The explicit analytic arguments in `RESULT.md` prove the remaining claims, including two-state indistinguishability for every length. Tests up to the stated finite cutoffs are corroboration, not their logical substitute.

All computations use exact integer state labels and function composition; there are no floating-point tolerances, external packages, network dependencies, or unverified downloaded certificates. No independent external audit was performed.
