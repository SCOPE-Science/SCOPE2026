For two \(\mathrm{S5}\) agents whose information cells are alternating matchings on a \(2m\)-cycle, the number of events returning after two simultaneous knowing-whether evaluations equals \(1+\sum\alpha_j^{2m}+\sum\beta_j^{2m}\), where \(\alpha_j\) and \(\beta_j\) are roots of \(z^3-z-1\) and \(z^3+z+1\). The all-true event is the only fixed point, so subtracting one from the formula and dividing by two gives the number of genuine two-cycles.
