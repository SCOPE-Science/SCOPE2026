In the zero-offset Zhang–Liu–Wei–Jiang–Bi rational map, precisely one primitive two-cycle exists for \(a<-2\), with both two-step multipliers identically \(-7-16/a\). Its attraction window is exactly \(-8/3<a<-2\), and at \(a=-16/7\) the entire return Jacobian vanishes, producing a doubly superattracting two-cycle independent of the nonzero coupling \(c\).
