For \(A\) copies of one symbol, \(B\) of another and \(r\) different singleton markers, the number of distinguishable scattered length-two decks is exactly \(r!\binom{A+r}{r}\binom{B+r}{r}(1+AB/(r+1))\). Every deck's exact source-word multiplicity is a coefficient of a product of Gaussian binomial polynomials determined by the marker gaps.
