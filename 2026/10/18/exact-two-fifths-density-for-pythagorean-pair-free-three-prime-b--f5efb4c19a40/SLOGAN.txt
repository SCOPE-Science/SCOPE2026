The largest Pythagorean-pair-free subset of \(\{2^a3^b5^c:0\leq a,b,c<N\}\) has asymptotic density exactly \(2/5\) as \(N\to\infty\). A five-cycle of smooth-number Pythagorean pairs gives the upper bound, and choosing two residue classes of the exponent of \(2\) modulo \(5\) attains the limiting density.
