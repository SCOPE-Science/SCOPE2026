The normalized two-mode axial support family \(h(u)=1+aP_2(u\cdot e)+bP_4(u\cdot e)\) is convex on an exact coefficient region bounded by two line segments and two algebraic arcs, with sharp vertical range \(-27/217\le b\le6/49\). Its boundary exhibits a transition among equatorial, polar, and two-latitude vanishing curvature radii, while surface area and volume are explicit quadratic and cubic polynomials in \(a,b\).
