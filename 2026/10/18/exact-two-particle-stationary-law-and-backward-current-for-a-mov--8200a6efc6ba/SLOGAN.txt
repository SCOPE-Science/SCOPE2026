For a cyclically moving single defect that alone enables unbiased hard-core hops, the two-particle exclusion process has an explicit stationary probability for every configuration and ring length. Its exact time-averaged current is \(J_L=-v(1-z)^2/[L(B_L(1+z^2)+z)]<0\), with \(z=e^{-p/(2v)}\) and \(B_L=(L-2)(L+1)/4\): two particles are always pumped opposite to the defect motion.
