For square normalized systems with \(m\) mutually equicorrelated rows and one exceptional row, the unique Kaczmarz row distribution maximizing sharp expected one-step Euclidean-error progress has two explicit branches separated by a spectral multiplicity transition. In a natural weak-correlation limit, the exceptional row gets probability of order \(m^{-1/2}\), and the optimal progress coefficient can exceed uniform sampling by an arbitrarily large factor.
