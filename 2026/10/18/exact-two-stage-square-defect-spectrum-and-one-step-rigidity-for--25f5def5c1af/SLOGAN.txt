For the reciprocal-sum sequence \(x_{n+1}=h^2/(x_0+\cdots+x_n)\), exact prime-local tables give the joint natural densities of square failures at indices \(2\) and \(3\) for every \(h\). All failures are confined to index \(2\) for every starting integer if and only if \(h\) has no factor \(5\) or prime factor congruent to \(1\) or \(9\) modulo \(20\), giving the complete all-index failure-count distribution on that sharp infinite family.
