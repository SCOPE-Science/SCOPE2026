The norm of the complete-bipartite edge-difference maximum in the real free 2-convex Banach lattice over \(\ell_2^{a+b}\) is exactly \((a-1)/\sqrt a+(b-1)/\sqrt b+\sqrt{1/a+1/b}\). The same formula gives the absolutely 2-summing norm of the discrete gradient, and an explicit \(ab\)-vector family attains it.
