# Self-review

## Correctness (PASS)

The finite unit group modulo \(2^n\) has a sign component and a cyclic component; for \(n\ge t+2\), the \(2^t\)-power map has exactly \(2^{t+1}\) odd preimages of each residue congruent to one modulo \(2^{t+2}\). A complete disjoint parity decomposition gives the two nonsingular congruence branches and the even/even recursion. Base moduli, the reduction factor, and the target quantifiers are explicit in the proof. The induced dyadic image is an exhaustive disjoint union of two residue balls at each valuation layer, so its Haar measure follows from an exact geometric series. The package includes finite convolution checks for forty-four complete distributions; they support but do not prove the infinite theorem.

## Originality (PASS with residual risk)

The first-public July 2022 quartic Hasse paper studies pairs of equations in at least twenty-two variables and uses the classical dyadic fourth-power unit lemma. It neither asserts exact two-term ordered target multiplicities nor the Haar image for all power-of-two exponents. The accessible extract of Ramanujam's 1963 Waring result gives a large upper bound on the number of summands, not the asserted exact two-term law. Six mathematical bibliographic queries and accessible previously recorded claims showed no equivalent result; near matches are different Fourier and dyadic objects. The 1992 Wang homogeneous zero-target Poincaré-series result may already cover the recursion restricted to target zero; that specialization is not claimed separately as novel. A separate 1993 generalization could not be read in full and remains an explicit risk, as do unindexed implicit deductions. A failed search is not a uniqueness proof.

## Mathematical value (PASS)

A complete answer to the natural dyadic two-summand Waring-image problem for an infinite exponent family determines both exact arithmetic multiplicities at every prime-power modulus and the measure of the local image, whose quartic value is \(2/15\). The result is not claimed to solve global Waring questions or to invent the structure of the dyadic unit group. The singular valuation recursion and support-measure phase are the motivated additional arithmetic facts.

## Limits

Only dyadic moduli and exponent \(2^t\) with \(t\ge2\). No claim of independent validation or exhaustive coverage of all historical literature.
