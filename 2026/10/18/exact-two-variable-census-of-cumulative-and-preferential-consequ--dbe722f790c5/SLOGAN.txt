On the four valuations of a two-variable classical propositional language, there are exactly \(336{,}852\) System C cumulative consequence relations, of which \(596\) satisfy the preferential Or rule. Up to permutation of the four Boolean atoms, the counts are \(14{,}976\) and \(44\), as established by a complete outcast-choice-function enumeration and Burnside fixed-point census.
