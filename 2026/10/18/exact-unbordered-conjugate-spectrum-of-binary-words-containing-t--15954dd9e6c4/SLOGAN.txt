For binary words with exactly three ones, the number of unbordered cyclic shifts is exactly the number of unequal neighboring cyclic zero gaps, so it can only be \(0\), \(2\), or \(3\). The complete all-length distribution is \(C_0(n)=\tfrac n3[3\mid n]\), \(C_2(n)=n(\lfloor(n-3)/2\rfloor+1-[3\mid n])\), and \(C_3(n)=\binom n3-C_0(n)-C_2(n)\); almost all such words have three unbordered shifts.
