The unconditional constant of the spanning-tree edge-molecule basis of every finite weighted cactus Lipschitz-free space with geodesic cycle edges is the maximum of explicit cycle discrepancy ratios \(\min\{S,h+S-\Delta\}/h\). In a unit-weight \(r\)-cycle it is \(r-1\), while deciding attainment of the natural weighted upper bound is NP-complete even on single cycles.
