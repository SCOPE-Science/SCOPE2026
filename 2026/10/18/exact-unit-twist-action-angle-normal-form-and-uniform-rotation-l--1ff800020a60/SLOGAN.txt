The Bastien–Rogalski genus-zero recurrence has a global canonical elliptic action–angle chart in which every invariant oval moves by the same exact unit-twist shear \((I,\theta)\mapsto(I,\theta+I-\phi_a)\). Its entire elliptic region has finite logarithmic symplectic area \(2\pi\phi_a\), and natural-area sampling makes the rotation-angle deficit exactly uniform.
