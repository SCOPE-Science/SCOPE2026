For the hook-length series \(F_{t^2}\) and the \(t\)-core series \(C_t\), the greatest common divisor of every coefficient difference is exactly \(p^{a+1}\) when \(t=p^a\), and \(1\) otherwise. In the prime-power case, lifting to modulus \(p^{a+2}\) first fails precisely in degree \(p^{a-1}\), with residue \(-p^{a+1}\).
