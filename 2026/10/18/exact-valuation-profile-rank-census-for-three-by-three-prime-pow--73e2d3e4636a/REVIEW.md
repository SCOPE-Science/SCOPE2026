# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The unrestricted complex unimodular three-by-three determinant is singular only when two normalized rows or columns coincide. This is proved directly by conjugating the dephased determinant relation, and does not assume cyclotomic special cases. The valuation conditions for proportional Fourier rows/columns and the rank-one condition follow from exact congruences; the two cases of the triple-profile census follow from ultrametric separation. Exhaustive exact cyclotomic checks for \(4,8,9,16\) and sampled checks for five larger orders agree with both the local rule and arithmetic totals. The complete general proof rests on identities, not enumeration.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge; residual risk)
published-finding corpus searches included prime-power and valuation aliases, equivalent minor-rank and harmonic full-spark formulations, and individually selected versus principal minors. The closest identified theorem, *All singular three-by-three minors of prime-square Fourier matrices*, is the strict specialization \(a=2\) for odd primes. Its full `RESULT.md` was read; the higher-exponent and dyadic census is not stated. The original full-spark criterion [Alexeev–Cahill–Mixon] is a property of an entire fixed row-selected frame, not per-minor ranks. The full text of Caragea–Lee concerns principal minors; Herr–Wiegand concerns Hadamard submatrices. An older result phrased as a generalized Vandermonde determinant criterion or rank enumeration remains a legitimate risk, but none of the material-read sources gives an all-prime-power rank census.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A complete rank stratification of every three-frequency/three-sample restriction is a natural harmonic-frame erasure invariant. The exact finite arithmetic census works uniformly at any prime power; it distinguishes rank-one from rank-two failure and identifies new binary transitions that the previously classified odd-prime-square case cannot predict. The classification settles infinitely many previously unchecked exponents rather than reporting a handpicked small case or a routine renormalization.
