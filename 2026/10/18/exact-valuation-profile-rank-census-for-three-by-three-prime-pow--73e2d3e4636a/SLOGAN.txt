Every three-by-three minor of the complex Fourier matrix of order \(p^a\) has rank determined by just four valuations: \((r,t;s,u)\), with exact rank-one, rank-two and nonsingular census formulas for every prime and exponent. The binary family exhibits successive first appearances of rank-two defects at order \(8\) and rank-one defects at order \(16\).
