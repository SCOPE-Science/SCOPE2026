For finite metrics with \(n\) mutually equidistant leaves and exactly \(k\) shortened hub spokes, the real Lipschitz-dual coordinate volume is \(1+n(2t-1)+2(n-k)(1-t^{k+1})/(k+1)\). Shortening successive incident spokes has strictly diminishing volume costs, and shortening a positive fraction produces a sharp inverse-dimension volume crossover at \(t=1/2\).
