For a complete graph with the edges of any disjoint star forest removed, the anchored Lipschitz-ball volume is exactly \(N+\sum_i2m_i/(N-m_i)\), where \(m_i\) is the number of leaves in each deleted star. With the number of removed edges fixed, a single deleted star uniquely maximizes volume, while the most numerous, most balanced deleted stars uniquely minimize it.
