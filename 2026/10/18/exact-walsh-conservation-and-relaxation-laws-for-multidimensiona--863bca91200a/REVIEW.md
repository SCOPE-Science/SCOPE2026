The underlying work identifies stable uniform pluralism and neutral manifolds but tabulates cases only for small numbers of issues. Here the exact all-issue criterion predicts **which specific correlations are conserved**, whether uniform pluralism is isolated and attracting, and its local relaxation rate under unequal issue change rates. The sharp two-versus-three issue boundary and the pair-correlation bottleneck are motivated observable consequences rather than arbitrary small-parameter exercises.
