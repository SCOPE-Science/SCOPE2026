# Scientific scope note

The corrected result is the explicit \(\sqrt2\) factorization bound through Hilbert space for noncompact operators of arbitrary injective-graph languages. The derivative supports, explicit bounded maps, and separated sequence of columns are analytic checks in the full spaces. The order-language comparison uses a classical half-graph obstruction; the flat approximation-number tail is a prior theorem. The general Boolean-matrix gap is only discussed as an implication of standard weak-compactness methods, not claimed as a separate contribution.
