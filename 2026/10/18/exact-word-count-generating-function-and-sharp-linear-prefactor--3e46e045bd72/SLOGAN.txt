The number \(A_{n,M}\) of length-\(n\) words in every generalized \(M\)-type heterochaos baker coding has the exact algebraic generating function \(\sum_{n\ge0}A_{n,M}t^n=(1-q_M(t))/([1-(M+1)t](1-Mq_M(t)))\), where \(q_M(t)=(1-\sqrt{1-4Mt^2})/(2Mt)\). Beyond the previously known entropy \(\log(M+1)\), the sharp growth law is \(A_{n,M}/(M+1)^n=((M-1)^2/[M(M+1)])n+(M+1)/M+O_M(\vartheta^n)\) for every \(2\sqrt M/(M+1)<\vartheta<1\).
