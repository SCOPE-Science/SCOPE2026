The two-node singular genus-four curve built from \(y^2=x^5-x^3-1\) over \(\mathbb F_3\) is tetragonal over its ground field but has exactly two geometric trigonal pencils, exchanged by Frobenius. They are realized over \(\mathbb F_9\) by \((y\pm c)/(x(x-1))\) with \(c^2=-1\), making the minimal trigonality extension quadratic.
