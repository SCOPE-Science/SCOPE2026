The uniform estimate for \(R_n(-y)/n\), \(y\in[1,2]\), gives the \(-\sqrt2\) limit. The parity-specific exact equations for \((1+\epsilon_n)^n\) then establish the logarithmic-over-linear rate with explicit constants. The standalone brute-force script checks every graph subset through \(n=8\); finite checks supplement but do not replace proof.

## Value
**PASS.** This is a natural root-geometry question from the study of connected domination polynomials. The infinite-family theorem gives stronger information than any coefficient list: exactly two nonzero real zeros for every order, strict location, and two different limiting mechanisms, one with an explicit logarithmic rate and even/odd constants. These require nontrivial positivity, convexity and asymptotic arguments; they are not a fixed-size table entry, arbitrary parameter slice or a mere renaming of a known enumerator. The family is the standard bipartite double cover of complete graphs and exhibits a sharp contrast between roots converging to an interior point and to the boundary \(-2\).
