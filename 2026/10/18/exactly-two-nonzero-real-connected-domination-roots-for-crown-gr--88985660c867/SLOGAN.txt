The connected domination polynomial of every crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M_n\), \(n\ge3\), has precisely two nonzero real roots, one below \(-2\) and one between \(-2\) and \(-1\). The latter tends to \(-\sqrt2\); the former approaches \(-2\) with sharp parity-dependent displacement \((\log n+\log(2(\sqrt2\mp1))+o(1))/n\), with the minus sign for even \(n\) and plus sign for odd \(n\).
