For cyclic Klein hypersurfaces \(\sum_i x_i^{d-1}x_{i+1}=0\) in characteristic \(p\nmid d\), the entire exceptional-prime singular scheme is a \(\mu_{|Q|}\)-torsor for \(Q=\sum_{j=0}^{s-1}(1-d)^j\). Its \(p\)-power thickness gives every local Tjurina algebra, while the transverse quadratic Hessian always has corank one.
