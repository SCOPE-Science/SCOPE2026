For \(n\ge5\), the determinant hypersurface of a generic symmetric matrix with zero diagonal has exactly \(n+1\) reduced singular components: one irreducible corank-two locus and \(n\) smaller linear zero-star spaces. Their intersections are explicitly determinantal or linear, and both generic transverse singularity families are nondegenerate quadratic cones; the corank-two degree and irreducibility were known beforehand.
