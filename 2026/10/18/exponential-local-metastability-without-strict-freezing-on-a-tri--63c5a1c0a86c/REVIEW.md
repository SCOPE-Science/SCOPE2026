# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The local flip energy is exactly \(4J(k-2)\), where \(k\) is the number of neighbors with opposite spins. Triangle frustration incidences give the strict-freezing parity obstruction. The unrestricted 4-state matrix's nilpotent leading coefficient supplies exact parity-dependent ground-state multiplicity, and the 16-state constrained matrix gives the weak-local-minimum polynomial and Perron limit. The stated marked characteristic factorization was evaluated symbolically; the active 14-state fifth power is strictly positive. Independent physical-graph enumeration for lengths four through eight matched both stability notions and their energy gradings. Analytic spectral perturbation, rather than finite extrapolation, proves the energy-density limit.

## Originality — PASS (best knowledge)

The closest full-text comparisons were Newman and Stein (1999) on odd-coordinate square-ladder blocking, Dhar–Chaudhuri–Dasgupta (1999) on triangular-lattice equilibrium ground-state strings and dynamics, Spirin–Krapivsky–Redner (2001) on ferromagnetic stripe metastability, and Pagnani–Parisi–Ratiéville (2002) on locally tree-like graphs. None establishes the exact open-transverse triangular-strip weak/strict/ground separation or its quintic. Previously described ferromagnetic square-ladder/Potts configurations have different graphs and local energy signs. Targeted searches did not uncover an equivalent polynomial, but unsuccessful searches do not prove uniqueness. An unindexed width-two transfer calculation could still overlap, and a relevant 1993 bulk triangular ground-state paper was inspected only at the abstract level.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The physically distinct notions of local energy minimum, true dynamical absorption under neutral Glauber flips, and ground state separate sharply in this frustrated quasi-one-dimensional system. The exponentially large local-minimum ensemble has energy density strictly above the ground state, despite only zero or four strictly absorbing configurations. The exact energy-refined transfer invariant is a meaningful benchmark rather than a fixed-size experiment.

## Limitations

The count is uniform over weak local minima and is not a distribution of dynamically reached states. Transverse width is fixed at two, and the graph has open transverse boundary conditions. An external, independent audit has not been performed.
