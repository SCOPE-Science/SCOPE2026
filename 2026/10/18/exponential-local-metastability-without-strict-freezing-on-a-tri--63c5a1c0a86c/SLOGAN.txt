On a periodic two-row antiferromagnetic triangular Ising ladder, strict zero-temperature Glauber absorbing configurations number four at even lengths and zero at odd lengths, while weak one-spin local minima grow as \(\alpha^n\) with \(\alpha=2.776562146\ldots\). Ground states number only four or \(2n^2\), and a uniform weak local minimum carries a strictly positive macroscopic energy excess.
