On the odd three-prime ray \(n_a=225\cdot49^a\), every prescribed fixed odd divisor-sum imbalance has at least \(12^{a-b(k)}\) distinct two-block realizations for all sufficiently large \(a\). The factor \(12\) is the exact number of zero-sum extensions across two new powers-of-seven layers; three distinct primes are the minimum support for such an infinite fixed-imbalance family.
