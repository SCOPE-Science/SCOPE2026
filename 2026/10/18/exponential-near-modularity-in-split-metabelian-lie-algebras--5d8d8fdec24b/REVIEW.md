# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. Classification of subalgebras into arbitrary subspaces of the abelian ideal and coset lifts of invariant subspaces is exact. Mixed pairs reduce to an invariant quotient image. Two lifted pairs reduce to invariant lines in a semisimple quotient with a provable uniform fibre size. Exhaustive small-field checks agree with the full Gaussian pair counts. For the sharp bound, a Gaussian-square-completion upper estimate is matched by noninvariant-hyperplane witnesses; the estimates are uniform because the number of distinct eigenvalues cannot exceed the fixed field size. Limits: eigenvalues must split and every eigenspace must have multiplicity at least two for the sharp law.

## Originality
PASS at self-review, with bibliographic uncertainty. Muhie--Otera--Russo (arXiv:2609.19086) studies subalgebra commutativity and exact special examples but not the split-spectrum high-multiplicity formula or its sharp exponential law. The full public result for the indexed dimension-three plane-count theorem is confined to dimension three. A prior four-dimensional two-eigenvalue result covers multiplicities \((2,1)\), expressly outside this theorem's hypothesis. Neither yields the multi-block formula or the uniform dimensional decay statement. Unindexed equivalent enumerations remain possible; the closest dimension-three and higher-Heisenberg indexed full results were read and have different domains.

## Value
PASS. This is a natural full-family probability attached to the subalgebra lattice, not an arbitrary finite slice. Its exact computable formula distinguishes modularity from *statistical near-modularity*: all these Lie algebras remain nonmodular, but their nonpermuting-pair density has a sharp multiplicity-dependent exponent. The result is useful for comparing high-dimensional metabelian examples and interpreting finite-field subalgebra statistics.
