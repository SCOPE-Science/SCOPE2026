For split metabelian Lie algebras with at least two distinct scalar adjoint eigenspaces, each of dimension at least two, the exact probability that two random subalgebras permute is a finite Gaussian-binomial expression. Its nonpermuting probability is \(\Theta_q(q^{(n-\sum_{i<j}a_ia_j)/2})\): every algebra is nonmodular, but high-dimensional multiplicities make almost all subalgebra pairs permute.
