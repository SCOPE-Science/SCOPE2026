For a fixed clockwise bias above one half, the last covered vertex of a cycle and its cover time admit a joint coupling to a drifted walk's ultimate minimum and a shifted first-passage time, with mismatch probability at most \(2((1-p)/p)^{\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor+1}\). As the cycle grows, the terminal-vertex offset is geometric and becomes independent of Gaussian-scale fluctuations in completion time.
