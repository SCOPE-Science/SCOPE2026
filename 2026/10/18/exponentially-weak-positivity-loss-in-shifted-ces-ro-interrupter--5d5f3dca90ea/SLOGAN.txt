Near the hyponormality threshold, the order-one generalized Cesàro auxiliary defect has exactly one negative eigenvalue with sharp magnitude \(\delta_\varepsilon\sim e^{\gamma_{\mathrm E}-1}e^{-1/\varepsilon}\). The first initial-coordinate compression detecting this negativity has dimension \(N_*(\varepsilon)\sim e^{1-\gamma_{\mathrm E}}e^{1/\varepsilon}\), with \(N_*(\varepsilon)\delta_\varepsilon\to1\), and the normalized unstable mode has a universal \((1+t)^{-2}\) rescaled mass law.
