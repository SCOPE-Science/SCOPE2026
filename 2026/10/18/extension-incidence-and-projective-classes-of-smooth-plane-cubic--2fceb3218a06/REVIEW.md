# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

A two-dimensional binary pencil is uniquely determined by its three nonzero cubic masks. Exact Macaulay-rank testing of every mask is equivalent to geometric Jacobian smoothness by the regular-sequence Hilbert-series argument. The 5544-pencil, extension-count, 37-orbit and rational-base enumerations cover finite, completely specified domains. The extension count divides the four successful new members per containing net, and its 6048 incidence total agrees with the 864-net count. Independent three-chart Gröbner calculations agree with the entire 336-mask smoothness set and all 12 joint strata. The Burnside fixed counts independently check the projective orbit total.

## Originality — PASS with residual priority risk

The relevant 2022 work formulates finite-field smooth pencils and proves existence; its successor proves existence and maximality for smooth linear families. Previously counted 5544 pencils and 864 nets, and an eight-orbit *net* classification, are explicitly treated as prior data. Those figures do not imply the pencil-to-net extension multiplicity, the 1680 nonextendible pencils, the 37 orbit classification, or the joint rational-base table. The 2024 geometric-irreducibility article is about a weaker property, and a separate characteristic-zero one-base-point paper studies a different base condition. Actual research-record and literature searches yielded no stronger or equivalent conclusion. Unindexed or unpublished work could still have an equivalent census; the comparisons do not establish absolute historical uniqueness.

## Value — PASS

The extension problem for partially specified smooth linear systems is natural after the existing existence/maximality theorems. A complete incidence census resolves whether and how many maximal nets contain a given pencil, showing that most pencils are neither uniquely extendible nor uniformly extendible. The 37 projective classes, stabilizers, and rational-base stratification give a reproducible structural classification, not merely a count of objects already classified in the net census.

## Boundaries and sources

The work concerns \(\mathbf F_2\)-rational members, not all geometric pencil members, and projective embeddings rather than abstract elliptic isomorphism. Geometric base-scheme cycle types are not asserted. Primary comparisons: arXiv:2203.07169 (introduction, theorem and examples), arXiv:2207.00918 (Theorem 2 and construction), arXiv:2407.18104 (Theorems 2–5 and Appendix A), and arXiv:2407.04569 (introduction and one-geometric-base-point results).
