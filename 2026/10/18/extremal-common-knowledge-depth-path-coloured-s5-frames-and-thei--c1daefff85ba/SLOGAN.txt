On an \(n\)-world classical S5 frame, the slowest possible convergence of finite common-knowledge unfoldings is exactly \(n-1\) steps, and the extremal frames are precisely spanning paths whose adjacent edges have disjoint nonempty agent-label sets. For \(n\ge3\), their complete labelled count is \(\frac{n!}{2}u^{\mathsf T}B^{n-2}\mathbf1\), giving \(n!\) extremal frames for two agents.
