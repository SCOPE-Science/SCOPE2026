For a complete two-level finite Kripke frame with at least two worlds on each level, all nuclei admit an explicit pair-of-subsets normal form and a precise commutation criterion. Pairwise-commuting families contain at most \(2^r+2^s-1\) nuclei, with exactly two extremizers; the ordered commuting-pair count is explicit, and the balanced commutation probability decays like \(2(9/16)^n\).
