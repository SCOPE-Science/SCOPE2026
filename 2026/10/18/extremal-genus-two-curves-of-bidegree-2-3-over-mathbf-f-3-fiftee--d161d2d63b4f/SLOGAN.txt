Among smooth bidegree-\((2,3)\) genus-two curves on \(\mathbf P^1\times\mathbf P^1\) over \(\mathbf F_3\), the \(6096\) models attaining eight rational points split into exactly fifteen ruling-preserving projective orbits. Their six Weierstrass branch points have precisely the Frobenius cycle types \((6)\), \((4,2)\), and \((2,2,2)\), giving three distinct zeta numerators; the eight-point bound itself was previously known.
