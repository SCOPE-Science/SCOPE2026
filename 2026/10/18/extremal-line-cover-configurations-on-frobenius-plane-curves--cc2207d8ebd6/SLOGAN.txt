For the nonreflexive Frobenius curve \(YZ^{p^2-1}=X^{p^2}\) over \(\mathbf F_{p^{2n+1}}\), optimal rational-line covers are completely controlled by finite-geometry incidence: in odd characteristic they are affine line spreads plus one arbitrary line through the singular point; in characteristic two their exact number is \(\binom{q+2}{2}(q-1)!!\). For odd \(p\) the family includes exponentially many non-pencil arrangements when \(n\ge1\).
