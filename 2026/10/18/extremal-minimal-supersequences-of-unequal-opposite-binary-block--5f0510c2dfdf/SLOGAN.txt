For the unequal opposite binary blocks \(0^a1^b\) and \(1^c0^d\) with \(a,b,c,d\ge2\), the longest subsequence-minimal common supersequences have length \(a+b+c+d-1\), and precisely \(2\binom{a+c-2}{a-1}\binom{b+d-2}{b-1}\) attain it. A complete two-family classification exhibits separate dependence on the cross-run pairs \( (a,c) \) and \( (b,d) \), with pair-swap symmetries.
