# Review of substitution-scale extremal factors

**Correctness: PASS.** For every \(q\ge3\), \(k\ge0\), a complete aligned-block classification gives five possible length-\(2q^k\) word families, the fifth occurring exactly for \(q=3\). The middle full word or two complementary end pieces account for every factor attaining \(2^k\) ones. Strict boundary losses rule out all other cases. Pairwise disjointness follows from the positions of ones and the interior zero runs. Independent substitution/digit constructions and complete finite factor enumeration agree on all 24 parameter pairs tested. These tests are corroboration, not the infinite proof.

**Originality: PASS against inspected sources.** Lü, Chen, Guo and Wen (2015 online) already establish total factor complexity; Chen, Lü and Wu (2017) already establish the ordinary Cantor word's maximum one-count. Neither inspected result specifies the complete extreme Parikh class, its size, or its sharp \(q=3\) versus \(q\ge4\) transition. Scientific search terms included extreme abelian class, densest windows, and maximal-weight factors; no equivalent classification was identified. Unindexed equivalent statements remain a genuine risk.

**Value: PASS.** Determining an extreme Parikh fiber is a natural refinement of the known abelian and ordinary factor-complexity statistics. This classification holds at every substitution depth for an entire family, proves exactly which factors realize the maximal weight, and yields limiting maximal-fiber proportions of one-half versus one-third. The presence or absence of an upper-level `101` block pattern explains the transition structurally. No all-length extremal classification is claimed.

**Scientific limitations.** The total factor counts are known independently. The maximum number of ones for the ordinary Cantor word is known. Only the exact maximizing fibers at the displayed substitution scales and their parameter dichotomy are advanced as the contribution. Other scholarly sources could contain an equivalent result.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
