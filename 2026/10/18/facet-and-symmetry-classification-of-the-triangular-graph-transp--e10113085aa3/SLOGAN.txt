The real Lipschitz-free unit ball of the ten-vertex triangular graph \(J(5,2)\) has exactly \(1032\) facets in \(17\) signed-automorphism orbits, with a completely enumerated facet-vertex incidence spectrum. Exactly \(12\) facets attain the sharp maximum of \(20\) vertices, and these correspond to decompositions of \(K_5\) into complementary Hamiltonian cycles.
