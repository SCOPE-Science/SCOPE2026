The real Lipschitz-free unit ball of the two-row rook graph \(K_n\square K_2\) has exactly \(4^n+2\cdot3^n-9\cdot2^n+8\) facets for every \(n\ge3\). Its facets admit a complete three-height-class classification, and their orbits under graph symmetries and sign have an exact parity-dependent cubic formula.
