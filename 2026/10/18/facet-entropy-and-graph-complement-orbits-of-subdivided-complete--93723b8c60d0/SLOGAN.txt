Once every edge of the complete graph \(K_n\) is subdivided, the real Lipschitz-free ball has exactly \(2^{\binom n2}+\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}\binom nk2^{\binom k2+\binom{n-k}2}\) facets, asymptotically \(2^{\binom n2}\). For \(n\ge4\), its facet orbits under graph automorphisms and negation are completely classified by unlabeled graphs, graph complementation, and pairs of graph types on opposite height classes.
