Deleting a matching of \(k\) edges from any unit complete bipartite metric with both parts of size at least three creates exactly \(2k\) new facets of its real Lipschitz-free unit ball: the full count is \(2^p+2^q-2+2k\). All facet potentials and all automorphism-plus-sign orbits (including orbit sizes) admit a complete classification for arbitrary rectangular and partially deleted bipartite graphs.
