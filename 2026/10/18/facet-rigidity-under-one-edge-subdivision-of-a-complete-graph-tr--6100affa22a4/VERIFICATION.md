# Verification

The exact reproducibility script `verify.py` uses Python 3's standard library. From the graph edges alone it enumerates every feasible integer potential normalized at a fixed vertex of the undisturbed clique; bounds on graph distances make this enumeration exhaustive. It retains only potentials whose unit-difference edge graph is connected and spanning. It counts facets and tight edges and partitions every potential into orbits generated by all clique permutations, exchange of the two subdivided-edge endpoints, and global sign reversal.

Executing `python3 verify.py` from the directory containing this file succeeded for \(n=5,6,7,8,9,10\). The respective facet counts were \(54,118,246,502,1014,2038\), the signed graph-symmetry orbit counts were \(9,12,15,18,21,24\), and the largest facet vertex counts were \(8,11,14,18,22,27\). Extremizing facet multiplicities were \(18,16,60,60,224,224\), in agreement with the proof.

These checks confirm the stated finite instances, including actual orbit decompositions; they are not a proof for every \(n\). The all-integer family follows from the explicit potential classification, group action, and quadratic maximum in `RESULT.md`. An independent external mathematical audit has not been performed.
