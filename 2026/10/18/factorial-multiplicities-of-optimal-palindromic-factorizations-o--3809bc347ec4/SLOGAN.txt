The number of minimum-length palindromic factorizations of a length-\(n\) Zimin prefix is exactly \(\lceil R(n)/2\rceil!\,\lfloor R(n)/2\rfloor!\), where \(R(n)\) counts the runs in the binary expansion of \(n\). For every \(m\), this multiplicity occurs on exactly \(\binom{m}{r}\) positive prefixes below \(2^m\) at each binary run count \(r\).
