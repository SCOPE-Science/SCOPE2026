For the toric varieties of fan-graph graphical zonotopes, the complete distribution of incident invariant curves at torus-fixed points has generating function \(2z/(1-3z+(1-u)z^2)\), with \(u\) marking degree excess over dimension. Exactly \(2F_{2m}\) of the \(2\cdot3^{m-1}\) fixed points are smooth, revealing a Fibonacci spectrum of local regularity inside an exponentially growing family.
