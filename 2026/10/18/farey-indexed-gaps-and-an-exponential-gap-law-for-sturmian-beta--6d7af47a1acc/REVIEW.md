# Scientific review: Farey-indexed Sturmian beta gaps

**Correctness — PASS.** The ceiling-summation identity is proved by shifting an absolutely convergent power series. Monotonicity is strict by termwise ceilings; continuity at irrational rotation slopes follows because each finite prefix remains constant locally. At a reduced rational, the precise one-sided ceiling difference is the indicator of multiples of its denominator. This produces both generating functions and the two algebraic endpoint equations without an arbitrary normalization. Every claimed gap is a jump of a strictly increasing slope-to-base map, and continuity at all irrational slopes excludes other gaps. Prefix changes occur only at Farey denominators at most the prefix length, giving a quadratic number of exponentially small base intervals away from one. The local normalized gap constant follows from the mean-value theorem and dominated convergence of derivative series. Endpoint cases, algebraic equations, and the exclusion of the global box-dimension assertion at one are stated explicitly. The standalone checker tests 63 rational slopes and 19 prefix levels, as well as sample normalized-gap limits; these tests corroborate, but do not establish, universal statements.

**Originality — PASS with bibliographic residual risk.** The 2026 beta-shift optimization paper identifies the irrational Sturmian parameter family and shows a failure of typical periodic optimization. The older Chi--Kwon paper supplies the beta parametrization, transcendence, and measure-related properties; no theorem in its inspected sections gives the exact collection of rational complementary beta-parameter gaps or the denominator-normalized gap asymptotics. Allouche's classification concerns unique beta expansions, and Anagnostopoulou--Jenkinson's rational Sturmian Farey construction concerns existence of a largest invariant measure. Published recent research on Sturmian maximizing potentials addresses fixed-base pressure and robustness, not the geometry of the full base parameter family. Bullett--Sentenac's older symbolic devil-staircase construction is a plausible conceptual antecedent; its abstract was inspected but the complete paper could not be read. This remains a specified risk, not proof of uniqueness or a false claim that the entire older work was checked. The proof supplies extra explicit algebraic endpoint and local asymptotic information not found in the inspected primary statements.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** A recent construction of beta-shifts with specification but without typical periodic optimization motivates determining how numerous its exceptional parameters are. The exact Farey labeling, explicit algebraic jump endpoints, zero Hausdorff dimension, and quantitative exponentially small rational-denominator gap law together classify the parameter family much more sharply than a qualitative uncountability or Lebesgue-null observation. The local prefactor depends nontrivially on the limiting irrational Sturmian series and is verified on convergents; it gives a reusable geometric scale for this natural parameter set. The contribution does not purport to classify other non-TPO beta-shifts.
