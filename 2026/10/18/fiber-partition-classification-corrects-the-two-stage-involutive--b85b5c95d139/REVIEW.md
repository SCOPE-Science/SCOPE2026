# Scientific review: two-fiber involutive-bisemilattice classification

## Correctness — PASS

Finite Boolean homomorphisms are exactly inverse images of finite-set functions; isomorphisms necessarily preserve the two components because the intrinsic values \(x\vee\neg x\) recover the component tops, and mixed joins recover the connecting homomorphism. Commuting diagrams are therefore exactly the independent relabelings of the dual function. Relabeling orbits have invariants given by nonzero fiber-size partitions, and matching partitions explicitly build relabelings. Partition enumeration and the automorphism stabilizer formula follow. The \((3,1)\) versus \((2,2)\) witness refutes rank-only equivalence at 20 points. A fresh verifier checked 1,794 maps, 48 orbits, and both witness operation tables. Limit: no computation alone proves the unrestricted classification; the proof does.

## Originality — PASS, with identified residual priority risk

The closest inspected primary paper is Bonzio–Pra Baldi–Valota (2018), full open arXiv text Sections 2–4. Its Lemma 1 uses only kernel-quotient cardinality, and Lemma 2 asserts the contrary formula \(\min(a,b)\). Our invariant distinguishes two kernels with equal quotient count; it is a correction rather than a restatement of that theorem. Targeted published-record and literature searches found no explicit two-stage correction. The bare combinatorial classification of set-function orbits is elementary and not claimed as new. A differently worded preexisting correction may exist; published full-text priority checks are necessarily incomplete. The 2019 general duality result does not supply the corrected numerical assertion in its available abstract; that alone is not treated as a full-text exclusion.

## Value — PASS

The correction affects a published algebraic census and its isomorphism test, not an arbitrary numerical slice. The result supplies an exact replacement for every two-stage shape, a sharp first counterexample, an if-and-only-if range where the previous formula is valid, and a precise automorphism-count theorem. These are natural reconstruction and enumeration facts for a standard representation of a paraconsistent logic algebra. No claim is made about more than two Boolean components or the paper's complete fine-spectrum table.
