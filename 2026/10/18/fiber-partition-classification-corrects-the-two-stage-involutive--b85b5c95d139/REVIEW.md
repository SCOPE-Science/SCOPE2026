# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For two finite nontrivial Boolean fibers B0=P([a]) and B1=P([b]) over the fixed two-point chain, x∨¬x recovers each component top intrinsically, and x∨0_1 recovers the transition map, so isomorphism of the complete Płonka algebras is exactly commuting component Boolean automorphisms. Finite Stone duality turns the homomorphism into a set map f:[b]→[a] and isomorphism into the independent S_a×S_b action. Its orbits are classified by multisets of nonzero fiber sizes, i.e. partitions λ⊢b with ℓ(λ)<=a. Target permutations of unused points, equal-size fibers and within-fiber permutations give |Aut|=(a−r)!∏_s (s!)^{m_s}m_s!, with orbit size a!b!/|Aut|. Partitions of length r are unique for b<=3 or a=1; for a>=2,b>=4, (b−1,1) and (b−2,2) give distinct equal-rank orbits. The earliest counterexample by total component size is a=2,b=4, |B0|+|B1|=20, where (3,1),(2,2) share rank 2 but yield N=3 rather than 2. Fresh independent orbit enumeration under source/target permutations for (a,b)=(2,4),(3,4),(3,5) matched partition classifications and orbit sizes, including (2,4) orbits of sizes 6,8,2. No inference from ranks alone is used.
