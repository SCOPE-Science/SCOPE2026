For a Płonka sum of two finite nontrivial Boolean algebras, isomorphism types are classified by the full partition of target-atom fiber sizes, not just the number of image atoms. This corrects a rank-only two-stage counting formula: there are three, rather than two, isomorphism types for component atom counts \((2,4)\), with the first failure at 20 total elements; an exact automorphism formula holds for every fiber partition.
