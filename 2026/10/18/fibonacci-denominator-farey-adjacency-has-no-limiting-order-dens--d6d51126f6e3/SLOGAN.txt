In the full Farey sequence, exactly two neighboring pairs have Fibonacci-number denominators precisely on the final \(F_{k-3}\) orders of each interval \([F_k,F_{k+1})\), for \(k\ge6\). The set of such orders has no natural density: its lower and upper asymptotic densities are exactly \((5-\sqrt5)/10\) and \((3-\sqrt5)/2\).
