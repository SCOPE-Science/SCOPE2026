For the golden-slope diffusion on the flat two-torus, large drift forces the squared singular-value gap to grow like \(a^{4/3}\), but its normalized coefficient oscillates nontrivially and periodically on the logarithmic drift scale. Fibonacci Fourier modes determine the exact eventual minimum and the sharp limiting coefficient interval \([0.8018986752\ldots,0.9925557164\ldots]\).
