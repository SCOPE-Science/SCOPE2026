The proof establishes the infinite assertions independently of computation. Run `python3 verify_golden_slope.py` with the Python 3.11 standard library. The program checks residuals at 33 Fibonacci indices, compares best denominators in their finite intervals, computes exact Fourier search domains from an analytic quartic bound at five drift values, and tests the proposed phase law at six phases and three geometric scales. It reports the numerical extrema and checks the two-neighbor crossing identity with 64-decimal-digit arithmetic.

These reproducible finite checks do not prove global best approximation beyond tested denominators, nor do they prove any limiting statement. The unimodular basis argument and uniform geometric-phase estimate in RESULT.md supply those claims. This package makes no assertion of independent or external validation.
