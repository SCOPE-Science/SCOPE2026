For two independent uniform points in a regular odd \(n\)-gon, the probability that their diameter disk contains the polygon center differs from \(1/2\) by \((-1)^{(n+1)/2}[5\pi^4/(192n^5)+7\pi^6/(720n^7)+O(n^{-9})]\). This is a sharp, two-term quantitative refinement of the previously known qualitative parity rule and Fourier formulation.
