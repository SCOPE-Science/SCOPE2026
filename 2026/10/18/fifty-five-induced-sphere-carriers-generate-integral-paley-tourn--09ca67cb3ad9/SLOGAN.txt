In the Paley eleven-vertex tournament, exactly 55 induced five-vertex sphere cycles form one affine orbit, and their images generate all of \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z^{10}\oplus\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\), including the torsion. The nonzero order-two class also has a canonical affine-invariant representative obtained as half the boundary of the sum of all directed tetrahedra.
