In the polynomial flow of arXiv:2610.09176, each end of the inner saddle cycle's stable basin boundary reaches infinite radius in finite backward time with universal law \(r(t)\sim(4\mu(t-t_*))^{-1/4}\) and finite height and angle limits. This gives a rigorous parameter-uniform global classification into two attracting basins separated by a proper cylindrical stable manifold, without claiming the original discovery of the two periodic orbits.
