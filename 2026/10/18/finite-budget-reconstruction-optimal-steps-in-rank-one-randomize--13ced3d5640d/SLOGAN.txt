On a rank-deficient one-row inverse problem, randomized coordinate descent has a unique finite-budget reconstruction-optimal stepsize schedule \(\eta_k=1/(N+b-1-k)\), with sharp risk \(y^2(b-1)/[b(N+b-1)]\). The optimal schedule deliberately leaves a nonzero measurement residual, whereas fixed steps converge to an exact fit while retaining a positive minimum-norm reconstruction-error floor.
