For quadratic polynomial triples with \(ab+1,ac+1,bc+1,abc+1\) all squares, non-common centers occur in characteristic at least five **only** in characteristics \(5\) and \(13\), and both exceptions occur. At every prime \(p\ge17\), existence over \(\mathbb F_{p^m}\) is governed by a cyclic cubic: one third of primes permit a triple over \(\mathbb F_p\), while the other two thirds first permit one over \(\mathbb F_{p^3}\).
