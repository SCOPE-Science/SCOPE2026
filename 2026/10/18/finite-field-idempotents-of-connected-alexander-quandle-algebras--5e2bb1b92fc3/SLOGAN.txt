Every idempotent in a connected finite Alexander quandle algebra over a finite field of nonmodular characteristic is classified by independent zero-or-Smith-kernel choices on joint character/Frobenius orbits, giving an exact count for arbitrary finite abelian groups. Midpoint quandles admit a closed gcd product, and in characteristic two their entire idempotent set is precisely the natural-basis \(\mathbb F_2\)-subspace, of size \(2^{|G|}\).
