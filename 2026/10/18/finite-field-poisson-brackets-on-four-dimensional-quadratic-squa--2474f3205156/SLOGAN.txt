For the four-dimensional nonunital quadratic-square algebra \(V\oplus Kz\) over every odd finite field \(K=\mathbb F_q\), all compatible Poisson brackets have one cross-product parameter and one central vector parameter, giving exactly \(q^4\) labeled brackets. Their full isomorphism classification has exactly \(q+2\) classes: one noncentral class, and central classes indexed by zero, nonzero isotropic vectors, and each nonzero quadratic norm.
