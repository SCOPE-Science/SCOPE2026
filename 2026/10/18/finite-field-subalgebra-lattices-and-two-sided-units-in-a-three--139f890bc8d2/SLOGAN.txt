For every finite field and every nonzero deformation parameter, the three-dimensional nonassociative algebra defined by \(i^2=j^2=-1\), \(ij=0\), and \(ji=t(i+j)\) has a completely explicit subalgebra lattice: exactly three planes and a field-arithmetic count of the smaller subalgebras. The same three quadratic planes govern exact two-sided-unit counts, idempotents, nilpotents, and all generating-tuple counts; characteristic two has a uniform ten-element subalgebra lattice.
