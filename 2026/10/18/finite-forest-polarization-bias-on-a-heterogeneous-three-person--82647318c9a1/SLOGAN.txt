For a single heterogeneous three-person opinion group, true Friedkin–Johnsen polarization is independent of who is most influential, but finite rooted-forest sampling can manufacture a contribution-dependent difference. With one intrinsic dissenter, the expected plug-in polarization is exactly \(1+h(1+\gamma_3)/T\) times its true value, a relative distortion unbounded as group influence \(h\) grows; an independent cross-forest estimator removes its expectation bias.
