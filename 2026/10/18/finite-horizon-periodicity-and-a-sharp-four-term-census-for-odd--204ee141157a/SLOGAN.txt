For every fixed numerator and number of steps, the odd-greedy Egyptian-fraction stopping test is periodic in the odd input denominator, with an explicit computable period. In particular, \(2/d\) stops at exactly four terms precisely for \(d\bmod60\in\{7,35,47,55\}\), yielding an exact stopping-by-four-terms density of \(4/5\) among odd denominators.
