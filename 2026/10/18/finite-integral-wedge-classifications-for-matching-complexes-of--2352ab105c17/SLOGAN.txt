For every integer \(2\le n\le8\), the ordinary matching complex of the \(3\times n\) Cartesian grid is integrally homotopy equivalent to a wedge of \(2,5,9,16,31,55,94\) spheres in dimension \(n-1\), respectively. A complete acyclic matching and a primitive signed Morse attaching-degree column at \(n=8\) establish the finite classification without extrapolating to larger widths.
