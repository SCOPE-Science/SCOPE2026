For any convex planar window perforated by finitely many sufficiently small disjoint equal disks, the second factorial moment of the number of components cut out by an invariant random line is an explicit sum of functions of the pairwise hole-center distances. Its first finitely many even Taylor coefficients recover the entire unlabeled distance multiset, including repeated distances, through a positive Hankel moment problem; the window enters only through its perimeter.
