For the Lebesgue-stationary doubling map, observation through any nontrivial dyadic cutoff is finite-order Markov exactly when the cutoff is \(1/2\), where the digits are independent; every other dyadic cutoff yields a finite-state hidden Markov process with genuinely infinite Markov memory. The obstruction is a universal integer word-count argument: the dominant constant-symbol cylinder counts grow exponentially at a rate strictly between one and two and therefore cannot eventually form a rational geometric progression.
