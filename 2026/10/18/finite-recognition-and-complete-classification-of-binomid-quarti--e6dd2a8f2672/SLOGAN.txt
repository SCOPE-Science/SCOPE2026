For a monic quartic \(f_n=(n+a)(n+b)(n+c)(n+d)\) with nonnegative ordered integer root shifts, integrality of just three generalized binomial coefficients—at \((2,1)\), \((3,1)\), and \((4,2)\)—is equivalent to integrality of every coefficient. Exactly five shift quadruples qualify; an exhaustive rational branch bound excludes every other choice.
