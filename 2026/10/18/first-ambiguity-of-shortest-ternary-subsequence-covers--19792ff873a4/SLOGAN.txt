Ternary subsequence-cover nonuniqueness first occurs at length \(11\): all shorter ternary words have a unique shortest s-cover, and the only length-\(11\) exceptions are the six relabelings of \(\mathtt{01012021010}\). Each has exactly the two length-\(6\) shortest s-covers \(\mathtt{010210}\) and \(\mathtt{012010}\), mutual reversals.
