On the four-vertex path, periodic one-edge donation games reverse the simple advice to schedule hub interactions last: at \(b/c=4\), an initial central-edge interaction favors a rare cooperator, whereas placing it last disfavors cooperation under weak-selection death–birth updating. A six-state exact-coalescent reduction gives all six ordering thresholds and identifies four vertices as the smallest possible connected witness for such edge-order asymmetry.
