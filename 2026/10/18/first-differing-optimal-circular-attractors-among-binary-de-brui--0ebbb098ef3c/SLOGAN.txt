Binary de Bruijn cycles of orders \(1,2,4\) all attain the circular string-attractor packing bound, but at order \(8\) two explicit cycles have different exact optima, \(32\) and \(33\). The eight possible equispaced optimal-size candidate sets all miss a shorter factor in the latter cycle, giving a certified first power-of-two-order gap between sharp window coverage and full circular attraction.
