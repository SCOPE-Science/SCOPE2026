# Scientific review

**Correctness: PASS.** The equivalence between triangular-graph geodesics and three-edge base-graph paths is reconstructed for all \(n\ge3\); the component classification and labelled enumeration prove the integer recurrence and independent rational formula. The finite certificate checks *every* coefficient and log-concavity determinant for \(3\le n\le29\), then verifies the exact negative determinant at \(30\). Exhaustive small-graph tests are supplementary rather than a substitute for the formula.

**Originality: PASS (best knowledge).** The structural star/triangle decomposition, GP number, lower GP number, and the general-position-polynomial concept are already known and receive credit. Actual searches for \(L(K_n)\), \(J(n,2)\), triangular graphs, P4-free edge families, explicit \(n=30\) coefficients and broader published coefficient theorems did not expose a covering result. The assessed contribution is specifically the *first failed log-concavity order*. The possibility of an unindexed equivalent table or theorem persists.

**Value: PASS.** The exact first breakdown of a natural coefficient-shape property for a canonical graph family is a motivated finite threshold with reproducible integer evidence. It complements known scalar general-position invariants without treating a trivial restatement as new.

Closest prior work: Ghorbani et al., arXiv:1903.04286; Di Stefano et al., arXiv:2306.09965, Section 5; Iršič et al., arXiv:2401.05696. The later arXiv:2603.06930 discusses different GP polynomial families. External confirmation of priority is incomplete, and no statement is made about log-concavity at orders beyond 30.
