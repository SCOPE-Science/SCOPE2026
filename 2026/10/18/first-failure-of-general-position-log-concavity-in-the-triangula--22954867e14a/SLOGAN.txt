The general-position polynomial of the triangular graph \(T_n=L(K_n)\) first fails log-concavity at \(n=30\): all orders \(3\le n\le29\) pass exact coefficient inequalities, while \(a_{30,29}^2-a_{30,28}a_{30,30}<0\). The finite sharp cutoff is certified by independent integer-recurrence and rational-coefficient computations; no claim is made about every higher order.
