For a uniform random chord diagram, the first matching-card closure at position \(t\) predicts its total crossing count by the exact quadratic regression \(\mathbb E[C\mid T=t]=(2n^2-6n+t^2+t-2)/12\). The regression crosses the unconditional mean sharply near \(2\sqrt n\), while the overall first-closure/crossing correlation decays as an explicit positive multiple of \(n^{-1/2}\).
