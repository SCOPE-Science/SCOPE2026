For sensitivity-adaptive Euler estimation of blow-up in \(x\prime=x^2\), a computable bias \(\varepsilon(\sqrt{2/(kx_0)}-1)\) can be cancelled by \(k=2/x_0>1\) when \(0<x_0<2\). This improves the time-error upper bound to \(O(\varepsilon^{3/2})\) without changing the leading \(\Theta(\varepsilon^{-1})\) step count.
