# Verification record

Run `python3 verify.py` alongside this document. The code is standalone standard-library Python. It checks all Boolean-cube propositional valuations through three buttons for both the Grzegorczyk and .2 schemes; exact forth/back conditions of a sample small-order-plus-capped-valuation quotient; definable prime-divisibility cube quotients; and two pp subgroup index families by direct enumeration.

The calculations are finite test cases and do not establish the general Baur–Monk reduction, the universal Smith-normal-form argument, or completeness of Grz.2 on finite Boolean lattices. Those infinite/theoretic steps are documented and cited in the proof. No independent external audit or certification has been performed.
