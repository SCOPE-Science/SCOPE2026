At the first parameter \(p=u^2\) outside the known regular range for the Pell triple \((P_4(u),P_6(u),P_9(u))\), the complete \(p\)-Apéry set changes by exactly one shortened row of \(u\) residues. This yields closed formulas for both the \(p\)-Frobenius number and \(p\)-genus for every integer \(u\ge2\), with corrections \((u^2+1)P_4(u)\) and \(u(u^2+1)\) to the naive extrapolation.
