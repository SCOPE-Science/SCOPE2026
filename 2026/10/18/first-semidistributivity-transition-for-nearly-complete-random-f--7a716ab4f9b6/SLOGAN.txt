Semidistributivity of a nearly complete random formal-concept lattice has its first sparse failure at missing-incidence probability \(q_n\asymp n^{-7/6}\): in the window \(q_n=t n^{-7/6}\), its limiting probability is \(\exp[-(\alpha^3+\alpha^4)t^6/2]\). The unique seven-or-fewer-vertex missing-incidence tree obstruction is the seven-vertex path, whose components form the governing Poisson law.
