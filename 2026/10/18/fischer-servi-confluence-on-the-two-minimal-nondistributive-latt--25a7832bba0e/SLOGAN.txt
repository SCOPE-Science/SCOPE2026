On the minimal nondistributive lattices \(M_3\) and \(N_5\), the Fischer–Servi reflexive/transitive relation counts are exactly \(1{,}395\) and \(1{,}079\); their isomorphism-class counts are \(341\) and \(1{,}079\). A relation containing both the bottom-to-top and top-to-bottom edges is necessarily universal on any bounded poset satisfying the same confluence laws.
