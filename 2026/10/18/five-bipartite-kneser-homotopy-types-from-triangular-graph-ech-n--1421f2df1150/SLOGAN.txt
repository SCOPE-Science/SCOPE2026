For each \(6\le n\le10\), the radius-one Čech nerve of the triangular graph \(L(K_n)\) is exactly a wedge of \(\binom{n-1}{4}\) three-spheres and \(\binom n4\) four-spheres. An exhaustive integral discrete-Morse certificate also determines the suspended homotopy types of five bipartite Kneser independence complexes, with no claim beyond this finite range.
