The five-cube has exactly five pairwise disjoint forts, although at most four can be open neighborhoods: \(\operatorname{ft}(Q_5)=5>\rho^{o}(Q_5)=4\). This closes the published \(4\)-to-\(6\) interval and gives the first strict fort-over-open-packing gap among binary hypercubes of dimension at least two. The one-cube is an excluded boundary case with \(\operatorname{ft}(Q_1)=1<\rho^{o}(Q_1)=2\).
