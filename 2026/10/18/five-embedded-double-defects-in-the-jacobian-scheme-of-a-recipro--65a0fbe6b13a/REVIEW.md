# Scientific review: reciprocal quartic singular scheme

## Correctness

PASS. The symbolic proof separates the set-theoretic gradient analysis from the local scheme calculation. At each vertex the Jacobian localization is generated by the four squarefree complementary quadrics; their degree-two span has codimension two inside the reduced line ideal. The exact integer determinant of the \(16\times16\) cubic multiplication matrix is \(48\), proving that all degree-three and higher reduced-line equations lie in the Jacobian ideal in characteristic zero. Away from vertices an invertible transverse quadratic Hessian identifies the Jacobian and radical ideals. The resulting five length-two skyscraper quotients give the Hilbert-polynomial shift of ten. The checker reproduces the critical determinant from integers and confirms bounded finite-field instances; these instances are not used as a universal proof.

## Originality

PASS with material residual risk. The 2004 reciprocal-circuit literature establishes the ambient hypersurface and its defining equation, and the general set-theoretic description of the elementary-symmetric singular locus is already known. Those facts are background, not novel claims. The proposed new fact is the scheme-theoretic embedded square-zero layer of exactly two units of length at *each* of five vertices. Targeted mathematical-record searches and inspected papers did not supply the local Jacobian equality or cubic determinant, or an equivalent stronger all-parameter statement. An implicit or unindexed equivalent result could still cover the claim. Closest searched records concern a different quartic discotope Jacobian defect and a different Hadamard quartic surface; neither implies this formula.

## Value

PASS. Scheme-theoretic singularities govern deformation-theoretic and intersection-theoretic behavior beyond the reduced singular support. This example shows a substantive, computable difference between the reduced singular-line arrangement and the actual Jacobian scheme of a natural reciprocal variety, and gives an exact global Hilbert polynomial shift from five uniform local defects. The result distinguishes geometric support from embedded infinitesimal structure without requiring a general theorem for all circuit lengths.

## Scope and limitations

Only characteristic zero and the five-variable quartic are covered. The result does not supply a minimal resolution of the full graded Jacobian algebra or assert novelty for the classical reduced singular-line arrangement. General Jacobian scheme theorems may contain an implicit equivalent. No external validation has been performed.
