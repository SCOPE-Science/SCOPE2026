The two-independence complexes of five consecutive Möbius ladders \(M_8,M_{10},M_{12},M_{14},M_{16}\) have exact integral homotopy types \(S^2\), \(\bigvee^9S^3\), \(\bigvee^6S^3\), \(S^4\), and \(\bigvee^{31}S^5\). Exhaustive acyclic discrete-Morse matchings prove these five cases, and signed Lefschetz counts determine their full rational dihedral homology characters; no infinite-family periodicity is asserted.
