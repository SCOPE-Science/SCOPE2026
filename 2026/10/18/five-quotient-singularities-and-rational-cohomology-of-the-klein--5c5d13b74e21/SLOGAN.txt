The quotient of the Klein cubic by its natural \(C_{11}\rtimes C_5\) subgroup has exactly five isolated singularities—one of type \(\frac1{11}(2,3,4)\) and four of type \(\frac15(1,2,3)\)—and has the rational cohomology ring of \(\mathbb P^3\). It is simply connected, yet its global Gorenstein index is \(55\) and its exact anticanonical volume is \(24/55\).\n
