For an exactly five-point support in \(\mathbb F_2^d\), the complete Walsh-support spectrum depends only on whether its affine rank is three or four: the two local spectra are \(\{4,5,6,7,8\}\) and \(\{12,13,14,15,16\}\), respectively. The sharp affine-rank gap persists in every dimension \(d\ge4\), with global sizes exactly \(2^{d-4}\{8,10,12,13,14,15,16\}\).
