# Scientific assessment

**Correctness.** The covariance of edge omissions gives an exact formula in the fixed-cardinality model. The two negative expressions for the ladder and the pair of cherries are strict on the positive branch-length domain. Since two peaks at five tips would include a peak at two sampled tips, the two shapes exhaust and exclude the singleton-root case.

**Originality.** The contribution considered here is the uniform singleton-root exclusion, not the previously reported onset or phase diagram. Nipperess and Matsen (arXiv:1208.6552) give the general rarefaction variance method. Resultary `784aa021ee67` states the same five-tip onset and the same two phase inequalities up to changing from ages to root depths, but explicitly does not classify the two other five-tip shapes. These comparisons concern mathematical coverage; they do not establish priority. Other reports on random-size extinction do not settle the fixed-cardinality claim. Unexamined comparisons, including the inaccessible relevant rarefaction article DOI 10.1111/2041-210X.12247, remain a bibliographic limitation.

**Value.** The exclusion distinguishes a genuine topological constraint from an existence example. It applies to every positive choice of branch lengths in the two shapes, so in combination with the known phase diagram it resolves which root splits can support bimodality at the minimum size.
