One added social tie can promote a threshold cascade at one common adoption threshold and suppress it at another, starting from the same single initiator: five vertices are sharp, with exactly five minimal decorated network types. On an explicit tree family the exact cascade-size change is \(0\), \(n-2\), or \(-1\) in the three successive threshold regimes, so one added tie can unlock arbitrarily many adopters yet inhibit adoption at higher thresholds.
