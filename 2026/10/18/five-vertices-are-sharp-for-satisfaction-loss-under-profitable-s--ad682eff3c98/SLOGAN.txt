In a two-type, fully occupied Schelling swap game where agents maximize their fractions of like-colored neighbors, five vertices are the sharp minimum for a mutually profitable exchange to *reduce* the number of agents meeting a half-neighbor satisfaction threshold. Exactly two connected five-vertex graph topologies permit this: a six-edge example reduces the satisfied headcount from \(2\) to \(1\) even as total utility rises from \(3/2\) to \(2\).
