The exact joint generating function for binary palindromes by length, Hamming weight, and scattered-subsequence universality index is rational: \(\Phi(x,y,u)=\frac{1-y^2x^4+x(1+y)(1+(u-1)yx^2-uy^2x^4)}{1-(1+y^2)x^2+y^2(1-2u^2)x^4+u^2y^2(1+y^2)x^6}\). It refines the previously known palindrome center rule to a composition-sensitive enumeration at every length.
