# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The domain is the fixed-point **geometric realization** of a finite flag complex, not a fixed-vertex subcomplex. For an orbit pair, the two Hamming distances are \(a+k\) and \(3-a+k\); their maximum is at most three precisely for distinct colors and lower-cube distance at most one. Since \(Q_d\) is triangle-free, every clique occurs in an eight-vertex edge block. Each such block is the join of four two-point complexes (an integral three-sphere), and the intersection pattern is exactly the smaller cube graph with contractible tetrahedral vertex fibers. Tree-edge gluing contributes a three-sphere; each additional edge contributes a three-sphere and a circle. The proof establishes an integral homotopy equivalence uniformly for \(n\ge4\), not an extrapolation from calculations. Exact finite-field chain computations on \(n=4,5,6,7\) independently corroborate the ranks.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

The direct comparisons concern the same underlying Hamming metric and scale, but a different topological object. Shukla (arXiv:2202.02756) and Feng (arXiv:2305.07084) classify ambient scale-three homology and homotopy; Adams–Virk (DOI:10.1007/s40840-024-01663-x) study ambient homology and filtration inclusions. Galetto–Montaño–Wellner (arXiv:2606.20784) compute the entire rational ambient homology representation, which does not by itself determine the integral homotopy type of a geometric fixed locus. Matched published-result searches found analogous cube, translation, and different graph-complex results but no homotopy classification for this exact fixed-point space. An unindexed treatment of orbit-clique complexes is a residual risk; independent novelty certification is not asserted.

## Value — PASS

The weight-three translation is the first nontrivial flip that uses the entire scale-three distance bound, making its fixed locus a natural equivariant geometric invariant of a well-studied metric-space filtration. The theorem gives an infinite-family integral classification and a direct geometric explanation of why independent cycles of a lower cube appear as fundamental-group generators alongside cross-polytope three-spheres. In particular the fixed locus is mixed-dimensional for every \(n\ge5\), whereas a rational character calculation of ambient homology supplies only alternating trace information about the fixed locus.

## Limitations and nearby literature

Only this conjugacy class of coordinate translations and this scale are classified. The finite scripts are checks rather than a replacement for the uniform proof. The closest relevant general work is the ambient equivariant-homology calculation of Galetto–Montaño–Wellner (arXiv:2606.20784); an equivalent unindexed fixed-space derivation remains possible.
