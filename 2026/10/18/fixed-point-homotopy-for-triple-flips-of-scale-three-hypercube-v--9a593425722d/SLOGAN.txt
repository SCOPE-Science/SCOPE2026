Under any translation flipping exactly three coordinates, the geometric fixed-point space of the scale-three binary-cube Vietoris–Rips complex is a wedge of \(d2^{d-1}\) three-spheres and \(d2^{d-1}-2^d+1\) circles, where \(d=n-3\ge1\). The circles record cycles in the smaller \(d\)-cube, and each of its edges contributes one three-sphere.
