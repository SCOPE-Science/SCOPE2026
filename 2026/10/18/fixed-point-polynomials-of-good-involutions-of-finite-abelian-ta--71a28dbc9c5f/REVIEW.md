# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The full proof derives displacement equivariance under \(2A\), order-two displacement, the necessary and sufficient involutivity equation, and the precise matching bijection. Zero versus nonzero kernel elements give exact fixed-point weights. The standalone checker independently compared all quandle identity tests to the proposed fixed-point polynomials for eleven groups and passed. Residual risk: the computer tests are finite and do not replace the universally quantified proof.

## Originality — PASS within inspected literature
Taniguchi (arXiv:2302.11138) treated connected generalized Alexander quandles; Kamada–Oshiro established the cyclic-dihedral count. Ta's 2025 work (arXiv:2505.08090, arXiv:2508.16772) already supplies the general characterization from which the displacement condition follows, and Ta's Theorem 5.11 uses matching enumeration on a different cyclic Alexander family. Neither the examined source text nor targeted equivalence searches supplied the evaluated fixed-point distribution for all finite abelian Takasaki groups and its two-rank factorization. Only this arithmetic refinement, not general characterizations or ordinary matching formulas, is claimed. Residual risk: unindexed equivalent enumerations and specializations of general classification results.

## Value — PASS
The number and fixed-point strata of good involutions quantify possible symmetric-quandle structures, a natural invariant connected to unoriented knot-coloring theory. The uniform infinite-family formula collapses dependence on the full abelian group type to two binary ranks for the unrefined count, and provides an explicit fixed-point spectrum. For \(\mathbf Z/2\mathbf Z\oplus\mathbf Z/4\mathbf Z\), the full multiplicity sequence \(9,12,10,4,1\) at fixed-point numbers \(0,2,4,6,8\) is recovered without enumerating all mappings. This is a structural enumeration, not merely a small finite table.
