Closure operators on the two-row lattice \(C_2\times C_n\) have the exact fixed-point-rank polynomial \((1+t+t^2)^n-(1+t)^{n-1}\), with every rank from \(1\) through \(2n\) attained. Via the established characteristic-map correspondence, this also refines the known enumeration of saturated transfer systems on cyclic subgroup grids by their number of fixed points, and the normalized rank obeys a Gaussian limit law.
