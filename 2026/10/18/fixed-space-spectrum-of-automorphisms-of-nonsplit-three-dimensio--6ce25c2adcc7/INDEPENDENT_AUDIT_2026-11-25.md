The derived ideal is precisely V, so any automorphism is (e ↦ ce+w, v ↦ Tv) with TA=cAT; conversely this sole bracket constraint and invertibility suffice. Determinant forces c²=1, trace forces c=1 except in the trace-zero case, and finite-field perfection excludes irreducible trace-zero quadratics in characteristic two. This also handles q=2 and q=3 without exceptions.
