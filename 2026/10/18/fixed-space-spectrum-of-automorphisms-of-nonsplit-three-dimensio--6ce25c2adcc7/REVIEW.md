# Mathematical review

**Correctness (PASS).** The derived ideal is exactly the two-dimensional irreducible adjoint module and is characteristic; this proves the exhaustive automorphism parametrization. Similarity forces the scalar on the quotient to be \(1\), with an additional \(-1\) branch precisely when the irreducible quadratic has zero trace in odd characteristic. Identifying the module with the quadratic field proves the complete centralizer and Frobenius-intertwiner descriptions. A norm-one calculation yields the fixed-space dimension and multiplicity in the second branch. The exact finite enumeration supplied in `verify.py` agrees for ten configurations over six finite fields. No finite computation is used as an infinite proof.

**Originality (PASS in self-review).** Burde–Dekimpe (arXiv:2604.27916), full author manuscript §4 and §5, treats existence over complex almost-abelian Lie algebras, not the present complete finite-field fixed-space spectrum. A published three-dimensional Heisenberg count has a different nilpotent object. A separate split characteristic-polynomial count concerns the complementary reducible hypothesis and cannot yield the nonsplit norm-one branch. Targeted research-index and literature comparisons found no dominating theorem; unindexed equivalent descriptions remain a risk. The classical finite-field group facts themselves are credited rather than claimed as new.

**Value (PASS).** The theorem gives a natural exact statistic for all nonsplit three-dimensional almost-abelian Lie algebras over every finite field, including characteristic two. It identifies the sharp existence criterion, a closed fixed-point-free density, and all other possible fixed-space dimensions; the norm-one mechanism explains a trace-sensitive jump invisible to results that ask only about existence over complex fields.

**Limits.** No extension to split, singular, or higher-dimensional adjoint operators is claimed. An independent audit has not been performed.
