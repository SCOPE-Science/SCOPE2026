For three-dimensional almost-abelian Lie algebras over \(\mathbb F_q\) whose adjoint operator has irreducible quadratic characteristic polynomial, the complete fixed-subspace distribution of automorphisms depends only on whether the adjoint trace vanishes. The number of fixed-point-free automorphisms is \(q^2(q+1)(q-2)\) in the trace-zero odd-characteristic case, and zero otherwise; a quadratic norm-one subgroup explains the dichotomy.
