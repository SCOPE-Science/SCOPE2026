For a lift \(J_n+\varepsilon C+\varepsilon^2D\) over \(\mathbb F_q[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^3)\), every nilpotency index from \(n\) to \(2n\) occurs, but above \(2n\) only the parity-spaced indices \(3n,3n-2,\ldots,3n-2(\lceil n/2\rceil-1)\) occur. A closed characteristic-independent formula gives the exact frequency of each index for every \(n\ge1\) and finite field.
