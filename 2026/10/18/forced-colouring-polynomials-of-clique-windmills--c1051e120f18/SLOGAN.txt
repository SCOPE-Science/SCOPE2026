For clique windmills \(W_{k,m}=K_1\vee(kK_m)\), the exact forced-colouring polynomial at the chromatic palette size is \((m+1)(m!)^k x^{k(m-1)}((1+x)(m+x)^k-m)\). The subtraction records a shared-hub obstruction: one blank vertex in every blade fails precisely when all omitted vertices have the same colour, and the minimum successful seed size is \(k(m-1)\).
