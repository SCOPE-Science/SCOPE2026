For a complete multipartite graph with at least three parts and at least two more available colours than parts, forced colourings have an exact all-coefficient enumeration: any part using multiple colours must be initially fully coloured, while at most one monochromatic part may be entirely unseeded. The minimum initial domain is a capacity-constrained subset-sum over part sizes, extending known one-extra-colour and bipartite cases to arbitrarily many surplus colours.
