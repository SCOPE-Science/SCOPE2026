For a weighted rectangular rook network, complete controllability from independent vertex inputs is equivalent to a forest of unactuated row–column incidences, supplemented at the single spectral-resonance equality by a sharp component-imbalance test on the actuated incidences. The minimum actuator count stays \((p-1)(q-1)\) whenever both sides have at least three vertices, while resonant two-row or two-column networks require one extra actuator.
