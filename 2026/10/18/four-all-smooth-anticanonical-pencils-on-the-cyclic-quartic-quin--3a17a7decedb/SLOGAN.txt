The cyclic quartic degree-five del Pezzo surface over \(\mathbf F_2\) has exactly four all-smooth anticanonical pencils, forming one free orbit under its order-four automorphism group. Every such pencil has one geometrically reduced, Frobenius-transitive degree-five closed base point, and its three smooth elliptic members have rational-point counts \((1,1,5)\).
