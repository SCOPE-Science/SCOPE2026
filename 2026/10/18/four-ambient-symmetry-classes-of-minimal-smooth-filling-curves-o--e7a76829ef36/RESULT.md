# Four ambient symmetry classes of minimal smooth filling curves on the binary quadric

## Finding

Let \(Q=\mathbf P^1_{\mathbf F_2}\times\mathbf P^1_{\mathbf F_2}\). Among all nonzero bidegree-\((4,3)\) sections vanishing at each of the nine points in \(Q(\mathbf F_2)\), exactly \(66\) define geometrically smooth curves. Under the factor-preserving group \(G=\mathrm{PGL}_2(\mathbf F_2)^2\), they form four orbits, of sizes \(6,6,18,36\). Their stabilizers in \(G\) have orders \(6,6,2,1\), respectively. Each curve is geometrically integral with genus \(6\). Of the nonzero filling sections, exactly \(364\) are smooth at all rational points; these form \(22\) orbits, of which only four are geometrically smooth.

## Assumptions and scope

We work over \(\mathbf F_2\), with product coordinates \(([X_0:X_1],[Y_0:Y_1])\). A section has exact bidegree \((4,3)\), and two equations differ only by a nonzero scalar, necessarily \(1\) over \(\mathbf F_2\). The group acting is \(G\) and not the abstract automorphism group of a curve. Factor interchange carries the class \((4,3)\) to \((3,4)\) and is not included. No statement is made about isomorphism of the resulting unembedded genus-six curves.

## Proof

Order the twenty monomials as \(M_{i,j}=X_0^{4-i}X_1^iY_0^{3-j}Y_1^j\), for \(0\le i\le4\) and \(0\le j\le3\), with index \(4i+j\). Encode a section \(F=\sum_{i,j}\epsilon_{i,j}M_{i,j}\) by the integer \(m(F)=\sum_{i,j}\epsilon_{i,j}2^{4i+j}\), where coefficients lie in \(\mathbf F_2\). The nine evaluation functionals at \(([1:0],[0:1],[1:1])^2\) have rank exactly \(9\), as verified by the binary elimination routine in `verify.py`. Consequently, the full filling linear system has \(2^{20-9}=2048\) sections, including zero; there are \(2047\) nonzero sections.

At each of the nine rational points, compute the two affine partial derivatives in its standard product chart. A vanishing section is nonsingular at that point exactly if at least one of these derivatives is nonzero. Binary evaluation of all \(2047\) nonzero sections gives exactly \(364\) which are smooth at every rational point. The computation is exhaustive: it spans the explicit nullspace, tests all its elements, and does not sample coefficients.

The four substitutions generated by swapping the two homogeneous coordinates on either factor and replacing \(X_0\) by \(X_0+X_1\) (or \(Y_0\) by \(Y_0+Y_1\)) generate \(G\), of order \(36\). Their monomial actions are expanded exactly in characteristic two. Breadth-first orbits of the \(364\) rationally nonsingular sections give \(22\) disjoint orbits, and their sizes sum to \(364\).

For each of the \(22\) orbit representatives, form the four dehomogenizations \(f_{ab}(x,y)\) on the charts \(X_a=Y_b=1\), where \(a,b\in\{0,1\}\). By the Jacobian criterion and the Nullstellensatz, the corresponding curve is geometrically smooth exactly when \((f_{ab},\partial_x f_{ab},\partial_y f_{ab})=(1)\) in \(\mathbf F_2[x,y]\) for all four charts. An exact Gröbner-basis computation in characteristic two tests this criterion, including singularities over every finite extension and over the algebraic closure. Exactly four orbits pass, with minimum binary-mask representatives \(396246,399606,404886,420790\), whose orbit sizes are \(6,18,36,6\), respectively. Hence the total is \(6+18+36+6=66\). Orbit–stabilizer gives the stated stabilizer orders.

Smooth divisors of bidegree \((4,3)\) are geometrically integral: if such a positive-bidegree divisor decomposed over the algebraic closure, components from both effective ruling directions would intersect because \((a,b)\cdot(c,d)=ad+bc\), causing a singularity; the only way all distinct components can be disjoint is that they lie in one ruling, inconsistent with both degrees positive. Adjunction on \(\mathbf P^1\times\mathbf P^1\) yields genus \((4-1)(3-1)=6\).

## Verification

Run `python verify.py` with Python 3.11 or newer and SymPy 1.14 or a compatible version. The script reconstructs the entire eleven-dimensional filling space, rational singularity tests, actions of the four group generators, all \(22\) orbits, and all four affine Gröbner ideals of each representative. It asserts the counts and orbit profiles, and prints the four minimal representative masks. It uses exact field arithmetic and has no random steps or external data files. The proof's finite classification depends on exhaustiveness of these loops and correctness of exact polynomial arithmetic, not on bounded extension-field searches.

## Relationship to prior work

Homma and Kim, *Filling curves for \(\mathbf P^1\times\mathbf P^1\)* (arXiv:2203.11595, first public 22 March 2022), Theorem 4.1, prove that the minimal irreducible filling bidegrees over \(\mathbf F_2\) are \((4,3)\) and \((3,4)\), giving an explicit smooth example; their text does not classify all sections or ambient automorphism orbits. Ahammed, Campbell and Moon, *Minimal algebraic space filling curves on the product of projective lines* (arXiv:2512.02796, 2025), provide abundance results and numerical statistics for other small fields; Section 2.7 recalls the binary minimum, whereas Section 4 treats \(q=3,5\), not a classification of all binary \((4,3)\) curves. Neither implies the census or four-orbit decomposition presented here.

## Limitations

The count concerns distinct equations and equivalence under factor-preserving \(\mathbf F_2\)-rational automorphisms of the ambient surface. It does not classify abstract isomorphism types, Jacobians, or higher-field twists, and does not address nonminimal bidegrees. Because this is an exact computational classification, independent replication of the accompanying exhaustive checker would be useful. Literature coverage is bounded: a differently indexed prior classification remains possible.
