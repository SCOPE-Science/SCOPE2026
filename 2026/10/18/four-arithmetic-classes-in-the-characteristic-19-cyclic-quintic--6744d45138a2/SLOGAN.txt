For the genus-four cyclic-quintic family \(y^5=x(x-1)(x-t)\) in characteristic \(19\), the Cartier operator vanishes exactly at the three harmonic parameters \(t=-1,2,1/2\); every other smooth member has \(19\)-rank \(2\) and \(a\)-number \(2\). Among the \(17\) rational parameters there are exactly four \(\mathbf F_{19}\)-isomorphism classes, and the harmonic class alone is maximal over \(\mathbf F_{19^2}\), with \(514\) points.
