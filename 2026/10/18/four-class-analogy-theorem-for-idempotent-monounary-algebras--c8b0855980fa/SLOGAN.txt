For every idempotent unary operation \(f\), original term-justification analogy partitions arrows into precisely the nonempty classes of diagonal, nonfixed-to-fixed, fixed-to-nonfixed, and unrelated arrows; hence analogical proportion is always transitive. On an \(n\)-element algebra with \(q\) nonfixed points, exactly \(n^2+2q^2+[n(n-1)-2q]^2\) of the \(n^4\) ordered quadruples satisfy the proportion, independently of individual fiber sizes.
