For every finite simple graph on \(n\ge6\) vertices with girth at least six, the ordinary four-cut complex has torsion-free reduced homology concentrated in degree \(n-5\), of rank \(\binom{n-1}3-\sum_v\binom{d(v)}3-\sum_{uv\in E}(d(u)-1)(d(v)-1)\). For \(n\ge8\), and also for \(n=6\), this is a complete homotopy classification as a wedge of spheres; in particular, all cubic high-girth graphs of the same order share this homotopy type.
