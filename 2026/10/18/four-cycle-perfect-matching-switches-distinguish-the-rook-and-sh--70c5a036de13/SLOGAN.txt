For the two strongly regular graphs with parameters \((16,6,2,2)\), alternating-four-cycle switches partition the rook graph's 2016 perfect matchings into components of sizes 1368, 288, 288 and 72 isolated states, but partition the Shrikhande graph's 1920 perfect matchings into a component of size 1908 and 12 isolated states. The 72 frozen rook matchings admit a direct description by row-assigned four-cycles on four column labels.
