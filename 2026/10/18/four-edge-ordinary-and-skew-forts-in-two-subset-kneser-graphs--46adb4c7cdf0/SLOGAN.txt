For every \(n\ge5\), the two-subset Kneser graph has ordinary and skew failed zero forcing numbers both equal to \(\binom n2-4\), yet the maximal failed-set counts differ by exactly \(3\binom n4+60\mathbf1_{\{n=6\}}\). Minimum skew white obstructions are precisely four-edge stars and four-edge matchings in \(K_n\); ordinary forcing additionally permits four-cycles and a single exceptional six-vertex shape.
