For squarefree integers, the reciprocal-square divisor-sum range has a closure of exactly four disjoint intervals: \([1,R]\), \([10/9,10R/9]\), \([5/4,5R/4]\), and \([25/18,25R/18]\), where \(R=54/(5\pi^2)\). A prime-tail subsum argument proves every point in those intervals is approximable, while the independent prime factors \(2\) and \(3\) force precisely three gaps.
