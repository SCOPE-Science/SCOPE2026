For a rational elliptic curve with all three nonzero two-torsion points rational, the density of good primes \(p\) for which \(8\mid\#E(\mathbb F_p)\) always exists and is exactly one of \(1/2,5/8,3/4,1\), with every value attained. For the split cubic from the \(D(8)\)-triple \(\{1,8,17\}\), the density is \(3/4\).
