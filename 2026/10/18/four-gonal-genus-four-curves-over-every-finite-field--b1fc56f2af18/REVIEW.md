For each of the three characteristic cases, the explicit gradient calculation proves that the Jacobian minors have no common geometric zero on the quadric-cubic intersection. The displayed rational point, anisotropic binary norm form, genus-by-adjunction calculation and canonical-quadric classification then prove both inequalities for the field gonality. ## Originality — PASS, with risk

Faber–Grantham–Howe (arXiv:2207.14307, initial 2022-07-28), Question 1.3, asks for a genus-\(g\) curve of field gonality \(g\) for each finite field. Their discussion provides an odd-characteristic nonsplit-quadric strategy but no uniform smooth cubic. Faber–Grantham (arXiv:2005.07054), Lemma 5.1 and Theorem 5.3, establish the quadric criterion and a binary example, not the all-field construction. Direct equation/alias searches and semantic research-record checks did not identify an all-field genus-four theorem. Search incompleteness and unindexed equivalent solutions remain genuine risks.

## Value — PASS

The natural genus-four instance of a published all-field existence question is fully resolved with three explicit uniform formulas and complete field-theoretic smoothness and gonality proofs. The construction handles every extension of characteristics two and three, not merely large or prime fields. Unlike an isolated example, it removes the field-size restriction in the existential genus-four question.
