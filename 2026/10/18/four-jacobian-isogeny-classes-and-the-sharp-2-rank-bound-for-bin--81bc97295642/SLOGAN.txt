Every geometrically smooth bidegree-\((4,3)\) curve over \(\mathbf F_2\) filling \(\mathbf P^1\times\mathbf P^1\) has Jacobian \(2\)-rank at least five: exactly 36 embedded equations attain five, while 30 are ordinary. Their Jacobians occupy exactly four \(\mathbf F_2\)-isogeny classes, distinguished by explicitly computed genus-six Weil polynomials.
