# Four-mode parabolic restriction: unique resonant geometry and a sharp sixth-moment dichotomy

## Finding

Let \(S=\{a,b,c,d\}\subset\mathbb Z\) with \(a<b<c<d\), and on the spacetime torus with normalized Haar measure define
\[
U_S(x,t)=\sum_{n\in S}z_n e^{i(nx+n^2t)},\qquad M=\sum_{n\in S}|z_n|^2.
\]
Here all coefficients may be complex and \(M>0\). The exact optimal sixth-power Fourier restriction constant has **only two possible values**:
\[
\sup_{z\ne0}\frac{\|U_S\|_{L^6(\mathbb T^2)}^6}{M^3}=
\begin{cases}
(97\sqrt{97}-260)/162,&S=\{h,h+\ell,h+3\ell,h+4\ell\},\quad h\in\mathbb Z,\ \ell\in\mathbb Z_{>0},\\
4,&\text{for every other four-element integer set}.
\end{cases}
\]
The exceptional value is \(4.2922173257668\ldots>4\), and the displayed support is the **only four-point shape permitting a nontrivial three-against-three quadratic Fourier resonance**.

For nonexceptional \(S\), every norm extremizer has \(|z_a|=|z_b|=|z_c|=|z_d|>0\), with arbitrary phases. There is moreover an exact globally sharp stability inequality: writing \(w_n=|z_n|^2\),
\[
4M^3-\|U_S\|_6^6\ \ge\ 4M\sum_{n\in S}\left(w_n-\frac M4\right)^2.
\]
The coefficient \(4\) cannot be increased. Equality occurs precisely for four equal squared magnitudes or for a coefficient vector supported at exactly one frequency.

On the exceptional support, every extremizer has
\[
\frac{|z_a|^2}{M}=\frac{|z_d|^2}{M}=\frac{14-\sqrt{97}}{18},\qquad
\frac{|z_b|^2}{M}=\frac{|z_c|^2}{M}=\frac{\sqrt{97}-5}{18},
\]
and the sole required relation on their phases is
\[
\arg z_a+2\arg z_c-2\arg z_b-\arg z_d\equiv0\pmod{2\pi}.
\]

The integer three-fold additive energy of the four points of the parabola, counting **ordered** triples on both sides, is exactly \(256\) in the ordinary case and exactly \(274\) in the exceptional case. Thus, among the \(\binom{N+1}{4}\) supports contained in \(\{0,1,\ldots,N\}\) with \(N\ge4\), exactly
\[
E(N)=m(N+1)-2m(m+1),\qquad m=\left\lfloor\frac N4\right\rfloor,
\]
have the exceptional energy and sharp restriction constant. In particular their proportion is \(3N^{-2}+O(N^{-3})\).

## Assumptions and scope

The phase is the integer parabola \(n\mapsto(n,n^2)\) with both spacetime variables integrated over a **full** \(2\pi\)-period and normalized measure \((2\pi)^{-2}\,dx\,dt\). Only exactly four distinct integer frequencies are considered; coefficients are allowed to vanish. The sign \(-n^2t\) gives the same conclusions after \(t\mapsto-t\). This theorem does not address partial time windows, frequencies modulo a finite period, or supports of five or more points. There is no assumption on the gaps among \(a<b<c<d\).

## Proof

**1. Every possible off-diagonal six-wave collision is forced into one pattern.** Orthogonality implies that two coefficient monomials indexed by three-element multisets \(\alpha,\beta\) interfere in \(\|U_S\|_6^6\) exactly when
\[
\sum_{n\in S}(\alpha_n-\beta_n)n^j=0,\qquad j=0,1,2.
\]
Each multiset has total cardinality three. If the two multisets differ, their multiplicity difference \(v\in\mathbb Z^4\) is nonzero and has \(\sum(v_n)_+\le3\). The real kernel of the three-by-four Vandermonde matrix with rows \(1,n,n^2\) is one-dimensional: by Lagrange interpolation it is generated by \((1/P'(a),1/P'(b),1/P'(c),1/P'(d))\), where \(P(X)=(X-a)(X-b)(X-c)(X-d)\). Hence, after possibly reversing sign, the four entries of \(v\) are nonzero and have alternating signs \((+,-,+,-)\).

The positive and negative sums both equal either two or three. In the first case the unique multiplicity shape is \((1,-1,1,-1)\); its first moment is strictly negative. In the second case the four multiplicity shapes are \((1,-1,2,-2)\), \((1,-2,2,-1)\), \((2,-1,1,-2)\), and \((2,-2,1,-1)\). The first, third and fourth have strictly negative first moments for ordered \(a<b<c<d\). Thus only
\[
v=(1,-2,2,-1)
\]
is possible. Its two moment equations are
\[
a-2b+2c-d=0,\qquad a^2-2b^2+2c^2-d^2=0.
\]
Put \(x=b-a>0\) and \(y=c-a>x\). The first equation gives \(d-a=2(y-x)\), and the second reduces to \((y-x)(y-3x)=0\). Therefore \(y=3x\) and \(d-a=4x\), proving precisely the exceptional shape. Conversely it realizes the unique collision
\[
(a,c,c)\longleftrightarrow(b,b,d).
\]
There can be no second off-diagonal fiber since the Vandermonde kernel is one-dimensional and the only admissible signed multiplicity vector is \(\pm(1,-2,2,-1)\). In particular **every three-or-fewer-frequency subset is resonance-free** because its first three moment equations form a full-column-rank Vandermonde system.

**2. Exact sixth moment and energy.** Write \(w_n=|z_n|^2\). Squaring the Fourier coefficients of \(U_S^3\) gives the diagonal multinomial polynomial
\[
H(w)=6M^3-9M\sum_{n\in S}w_n^2+4\sum_{n\in S}w_n^3.
\]
For ordinary \(S\), \(\|U_S\|_6^6=H(w)\). For exceptional \(S\), the two colliding coefficients have multinomial multiplicity three, so their interference contributes exactly
\[
\|U_S\|_6^6=H(w)+18\operatorname{Re}\bigl(z_a z_c^2\overline{z_b^2z_d}\bigr).
\]
Setting all four coefficients equal to one yields \(H(1,1,1,1)=256\), and the additional cross term is \(18\), proving the two exact ordered energies \(256,274\).

**3. The ordinary sharp norm and sharp stability.** Normalize \(M=1\), and let \(e_2\), \(e_3\) denote the elementary symmetric polynomials in the four nonnegative \(w_n\). An exact algebraic rearrangement gives
\[
4-H(w)-4\sum_{n\in S}(w_n-1/4)^2=2(e_2-6e_3).
\]
Maclaurin's inequalities show \(e_2\le3/8\) and \(e_3\le4(e_2/6)^{3/2}\). Consequently \(6e_3\le e_2\). Equality holds only when the weights are all equal or exactly one weight is nonzero: for \(e_2>0\), saturation requires equality in both Maclaurin bounds, hence equal positive weights; for \(e_2=0\) the point is a simplex vertex. This proves the stability inequality and all its equality cases. It gives the norm bound \(4\), attained by uniform squared magnitudes. A one-frequency vector makes the stability bound an equality with nonzero deficit, so its coefficient \(4\) is globally sharp.

**4. The exceptional sharp norm and all extremizers.** Set \(M=1\) and optimize the collision phase, obtaining \(H(w)+18\sqrt{w_aw_d}\,w_bw_c\). At fixed pair sums \(r=w_a+w_d\), \(s=w_b+w_c\), the diagonal polynomial is strictly increasing in each pair product \(w_aw_d\), \(w_bw_c\): its linear coefficients are \(18-12r>0\) and \(18-12s>0\). The resonant term also increases with both pair products. Thus every global maximizer has \(w_a=w_d=p\), \(w_b=w_c=q\), where \(p+q=1/2\).

The exact one-variable objective becomes
\[
\Phi(p)=18p^3-42p^2+\frac{33}{2}p+\frac52,\qquad0\le p\le\frac12.
\]
Its derivative is \(\Phi'(p)=\tfrac32(36p^2-56p+11)\), and \(\Phi''(p)=108p-84<0\) on the entire interval. Hence the unique maximum is at \(p=(14-\sqrt{97})/18\), with \(q=(\sqrt{97}-5)/18\); direct substitution yields \(\Phi(p)=(97\sqrt{97}-260)/162\). Both are strictly positive, so equality in the phase triangle inequality requires exactly the displayed phase relation. The pair-balancing steps were strict away from equality, proving completeness of the extremizer classification.

**5. Band count and rarity.** Each exceptional support inside \(\{0,\ldots,N\}\) has unique positive step \(\ell\le\lfloor N/4\rfloor\), and its left endpoint may be any integer \(h\in\{0,\ldots,N-4\ell\}\). Summing \(N-4\ell+1\) over \(\ell=1,\ldots,m\) yields \(E(N)=m(N+1)-2m(m+1)\). Since \(E(N)=N^2/8+O(N)\) and \(\binom{N+1}4=N^4/24+O(N^3)\), the fraction equals \(3/N^2+O(N^{-3})\).

## Relationship to prior work

Cushman, Demeter, and Wu (2026) prove a nearly cubic global bound for three-fold additive energy on arbitrary strictly convex curves. Deng, Fan, Guo, Guo, and Luo (2026) establish broad periodic one-dimensional Schrödinger Strichartz bounds. Neither inspected paper gives this four-point parabolic resonance classification, its exact two-level energy census, or the sharp four-mode stability statement.

A published finite-character result titled *Sharp sixth-moment norm for one balanced triple-sum defect* already proves the generic four-character sharp sixth-moment constant \((97\sqrt{97}-260)/162\) and its equality conditions under a prescribed isolated additive collision. **That algebraic constant and its generic optimization are prior results, not claimed as new here.** The present contribution identifies exactly which *integer parabolic* four-point supports have such a collision, classifies every other four-point support including sharp stability, and gives an exact arithmetic count of the exceptional supports in each finite frequency band. No distinct generic character-collision hypothesis alone supplies the parabolic classification without the new Vandermonde and signed-multiplicity argument.

## Limitations

The result is specific to four **distinct** integer frequencies and the exact quadratic dispersion on the full spacetime torus. It does not evaluate any higher-support sharp constant, long-time truncation, or nonquadratic dispersion. The finite checker cannot establish the universal statements, which are proved analytically. A comprehensive historical-priority guarantee is unavailable: an equivalent elementary classification may occur in older six-wave-resonance or Prouhet–Tarry–Escott literature under different terminology.
