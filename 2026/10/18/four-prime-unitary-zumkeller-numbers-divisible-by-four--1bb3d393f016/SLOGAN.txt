Among integers with exactly four distinct prime factors and divisible by four, the unitary Zumkeller numbers are precisely \(60r^e\) for primes \(r>5\) and \(e\ge1\), together with \(840,924,1092,1428,1596\). Every such integer is also unitary half-Zumkeller, proving the corresponding four-prime case of the even unitary Zumkeller conjecture.
