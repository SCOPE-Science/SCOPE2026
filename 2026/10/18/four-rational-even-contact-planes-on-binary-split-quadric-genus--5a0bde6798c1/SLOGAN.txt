A geometrically smooth bidegree-\((3,3)\) canonical curve on the split quadric over \(\mathbf F_2\) has at most four rational even-contact hyperplanes, and exactly 60 equations attain this bound in three ambient orbits of sizes 18, 36 and 6. The six curves in the smallest orbit have three distinct rational sextuple-contact planes, showing why nonreduced contacts cannot be discarded.
