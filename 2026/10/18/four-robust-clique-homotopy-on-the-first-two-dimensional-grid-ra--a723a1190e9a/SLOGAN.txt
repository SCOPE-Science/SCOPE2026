For all six rectangular grids with \(3\le m\le n\le5\), the four-robust clique complex collapses in discrete Morse theory to a wedge of \(\binom{(m-1)(n-1)}{3}\) five-spheres; five of these finite homotopy types extend the previously established \(4\times4\) case. The full rational dihedral homology characters are also determined for all three square cases.
