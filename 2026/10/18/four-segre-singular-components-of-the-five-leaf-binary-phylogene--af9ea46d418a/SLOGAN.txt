The five-leaf binary symmetric phylogenetic toric sevenfold has precisely four reduced singular components, each a Segre \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^3\), meeting in a four-cycle of Segre quadrics; the generic transverse germ is \(ab-cd=0\). Their union has degree \(16\) and Hilbert series \(1+4(1+3t)/(1-t)^5-4(1+t)/(1-t)^3\).
