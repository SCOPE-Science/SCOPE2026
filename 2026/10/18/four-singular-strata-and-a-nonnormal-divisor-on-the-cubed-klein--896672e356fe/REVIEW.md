# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The argument reconstructs the cubing image and irreducibility, the unique node of its plane-cubic quotient equation, a complete case analysis of gradient vanishing, primality of the irreducible complete-intersection divisor, generic node and \(A_2\) local models, and a 27-element normalization quotient. Exact symbolic checks and exhaustive point comparisons over two finite fields corroborate but do not replace the characteristic-zero proof.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** Dey--Görlach--Kaihnsa already give general equations, degree and finite quotient interpretations of coordinatewise powers; no independent novelty is claimed for those parts. The new claim is the complete reduced singular-support partition into one integral \((2,2)\) threefold and three smooth quadric surfaces, generic node/\(A_2\) local types, and this example's specific normalization. The different squared Grassmannian's eleven-surface result does not imply the cubic-image classification. A presently unidentified equivalent publication remains a material risk.

**Value — PASS.** The theorem identifies geometrically meaningful singular strata and a codimension-one nonnormality mechanism of a natural Plücker image, together with precise generic transversal ADE behavior; these features are not furnished by general image-degree formulas. The nonreduced Jacobian scheme and intersection-point germs are not treated.

The closest general reference is Dey, Görlach and Kaihnsa (arXiv:1807.03295, especially Sections 2 and 3); Scott (arXiv:math/0311148) is an archival Grassmannian reference. A published result on the square of this Grassmannian has a different exponent and component geometry. The full content of that square-specific publication was not retrieved, so comparison uses its accessible statement.
