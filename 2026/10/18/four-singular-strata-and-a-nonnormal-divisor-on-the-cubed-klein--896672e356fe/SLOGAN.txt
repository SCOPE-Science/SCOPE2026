The coordinatewise cube of the Plücker quadric \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,4)\) is a nonnormal sextic fourfold whose entire reduced singular locus is one integral degree-four complete-intersection threefold and three smooth quadric surfaces. The generic transverse singularities are a node along the divisor and \(A_2\) along the surfaces; its normalization is a 27-element diagonal quotient of the original quadric.
