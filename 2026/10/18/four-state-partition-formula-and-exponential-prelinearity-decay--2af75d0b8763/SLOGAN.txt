The fraction of pairs satisfying intuitionistic prelinearity in the Heyting algebra of any dual graph-incidence poset is an explicit four-state graph partition function divided by the square of a two-state partition function. On path-incidence frames the fraction decays exponentially at the exact spectral rate \(\rho/((3+\sqrt5)/2)^2<1\), even though every edge-point has just two maximal successors.
