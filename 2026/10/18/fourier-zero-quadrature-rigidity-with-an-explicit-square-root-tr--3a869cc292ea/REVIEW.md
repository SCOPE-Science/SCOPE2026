Endpoint continuity and all quantifiers are stated. The packaged finite checker is supplemental rather than a proof of all degrees.

Borda (arXiv:2005.04925v2, Theorem 1) gives a general Fourier smoothing transport inequality but does not derive the claimed fixed-center energy-minimizer and explicit defect inequality. Huang (2025) addresses a first zero rather than \(N-1\) prescribed zeros. Classical Gaussian quadrature covers the general interpolation principle and possibly the bare minimum; the material new candidate is the explicit, all-\(N\) nonnegative remainder and sharp-order transport stability. Neither keyword absence nor database partition proves priority; a broader specialized quadrature-stability theorem might imply parts of it.

**Value — PASS.** The exact minimizer and quantitative transport rate show that approximately minimizing the angular variance under \(N-1\) Fourier cancellations forces a nearly rigid regular configuration. The result supplies an explicit coercivity coefficient, arbitrary-measure scope, Fourier-based weight recovery, and a witness showing why the square-root rate cannot be improved, thereby connecting stable quadrature with simultaneous characteristic-function zeros.
