In the unordered two-point configuration space of the Heawood graph, fourteen products of disjoint hexagons form an integral basis of \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z^{14}\). Its full 336-element graph automorphism group acts on rational first and second homology through absolutely irreducible modules \(U_8\) and \(U_8\oplus W_6\), respectively, a distinction invisible to ordinary Betti numbers.
