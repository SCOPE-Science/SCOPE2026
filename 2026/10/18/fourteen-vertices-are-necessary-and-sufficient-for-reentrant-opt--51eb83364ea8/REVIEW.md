# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The entire claim concerns independent homogeneous one-period transmissions on a rooted finite tree, with exactly one pre-outbreak immune non-root vertex. Each vertex has a unique exposure path. The reduction in expected final infection count therefore is exactly \(G_v(p)=\sum_{u\in\operatorname{desc}(v)}p^{d(r,u)}\). Every descendant vaccine target is strictly dominated by its root-neighbor ancestor for all \(p>0\). Branch depth profiles are all positive integer compositions after the first level, and every such composition can be realized as a rooted tree.

The lower bound relies only on a necessary pairwise two-root condition: if one candidate returns to optimality after being defeated at an intermediate parameter, the fixed successful competitor at that parameter forces two roots. Exactly 6144 ordered two-profile pairs of combined size at most 12 exhaust every possible pair in every tree with at most 13 vertices. The exact standard-library rational Sturm checker verifies 150 two-sign-change candidates: 140 have zero roots in (0,1), 10 have one, none has two. Its explicit 14-vertex tree yields \(B_A-B_B=p^2(-1+3p-p^2-p^3-p^4)\). That quartic is negative at 0 and 1, positive at 1/2, and has at most two positive roots by Descartes, so exactly two simple interior crossings and the advertised unique winner intervals. A separate 8192-edge-state exact calculation checks the witness at 1/10, 1/2, 9/10. All code was executed successfully. The finite enumeration is exhaustive over *profiles*, not merely a sample of tree isomorphism classes.

## Originality — PASS, subject to named residual risks

Floyd, Kay and Shapiro's SIR-network manuscript gives the independent-transmission coupling and monotonic effect of vaccination, not cross-parameter optimal vaccine choice. DuBois's thesis studies criticality and epidemic infection-subtree vaccination heuristics under stochastic contacts, a particularly close objective. Its theorem bounds saved infection mass by a sum of expected subtree sizes, but does not state or imply the exact 13/14-node boundary or a two-switch optimality classification. Shapiro and Delgado-Eckert address infection-probability hardness rather than sharp small-tree vaccine prioritization; Yuan and colleagues study allocation among disconnected populations. Published results on root-seed reach centrality do exhibit related polynomial rank switches, but their optimization variable is *which vertex first gets infected*, not which susceptible vertex to remove with a fixed source; such results neither force a 13-node impossibility nor construct the stated 14-node intervention witness. Comparable quartics are not sufficient to infer identity of the mathematical claims. Primary full text was read for Floyd et al. and DuBois, including relevant proofs and model details. Targeted semantic and web searches found no dominating theorem. Uninspected reliability-theory literature and alternative formulations may still contain the result, so independent literature review remains necessary.

## Value — PASS

The minimum number of individuals needed for a single optimal vaccine target to disappear and subsequently regain priority as transmissibility varies is an intrinsic, motivated exact structural invariant of fixed-source epidemic intervention on trees. Both the impossibility theorem and the attaining construction are needed: a numerical two-switch example without the sharp lower bound would not answer the size-threshold question. The branch-profile reduction makes the exact cutoff reproducible without enumerating every labelled tree. It sharpens intuitive vaccine-priority heuristics and delineates where simple monotone ranking behavior must fail. The value does not depend on clinical calibration or a downstream general-graph theorem.

Distinct but nearby mathematical results concern the best *initial epidemic seed* on a tree, not the best *vaccination target* under a fixed root. The exact quartic used by one such seed-ranking construction also appears in this vaccination witness; this algebraic coincidence is disclosed rather than suppressed. It does not supply the new optimal-vaccination small-order exhaustion. Main outstanding risk is undiscovered equivalent small-tree polynomial optimization in network reliability or intervention literature. The theorem is confined to homogeneous independent tree transmission and a single perfect vaccine dose.
