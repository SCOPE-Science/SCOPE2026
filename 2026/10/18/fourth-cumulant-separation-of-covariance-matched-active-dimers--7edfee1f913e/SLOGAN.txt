A covariance-matched Gaussian Ornstein–Uhlenbeck interaction driver and a symmetric telegraph driver produce nonreciprocal Brownian dimers with identical mean-square displacement and the same source-defined entropy-production rate, yet the Gaussian-driven fourth displacement cumulant is strictly larger at every positive time. Their exact long-time fourth-cumulant-rate ratio is \(1+3\nu/\mu+(\nu/\mu)^2\), and their short-time fourth-cumulant exponents differ.
