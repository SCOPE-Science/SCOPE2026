For the fourth-order Gauss–Legendre \([2/2]\) Padé update on an irreducible directed Markov cycle with positive edge rates, every timestep preserves nonnegativity exactly for arbitrary two-state cycles and equal-rate three-state cycles; all other cycles develop negative transition entries for sufficiently large timesteps. Every cycle with at least four states has an explicit strictly negative large-step aggregate determined by nonadjacent pairs, and the uniform four-cycle has the exact positivity cutoff at the unique positive root of \(x^4-24x^2-72x-72=0\).
