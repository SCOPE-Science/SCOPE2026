For nearest-neighbor walks in iid site environments of zero mean one-step drift, independent geometric global refreshing produces an effective speed \(v(p)=4\operatorname{Var}(\omega_0)\mathbb E[(\omega_0-1/2)^3](1-p)^4+O((1-p)^6)\) near complete refresh. Thus environmental skewness selects a nonzero direction despite an exactly balanced averaged one-step rule, and the entire fifth-order term cancels.
