For every finite quadratic combinatorial Banach space defined by maxima of coordinate Euclidean norms, the **unrestricted** squared Banach–Mazur distance to Hilbert space is exactly its fractional set-cover number, attained by the ordinary Euclidean coordinates. For graph clique families this equals the fractional chromatic number of the complement, and integrality of the squared distance throughout the induced-subgraph family characterizes perfect graphs.
