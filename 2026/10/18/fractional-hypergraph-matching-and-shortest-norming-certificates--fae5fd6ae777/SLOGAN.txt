For every finite hypergraph, the \(p\)-th power of its join-of-meets generator norm in the real free \(p\)-convex lattice over \(\ell_1^n\) is exactly the hypergraph's fractional matching number. Optimal fractional matchings also determine the shortest norming test family: at most \(n\) vectors always suffice, and odd cycles and the seven-line Fano plane make this bound sharp.
