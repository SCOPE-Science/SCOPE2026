For every finite bounded integral commutative residuated chain, Frobenius-adjoint operator pairs are precisely the lower/upper roundings to full residuated subalgebras. On the \(n\)-element Łukasiewicz chain these pairs form the divisor lattice of \(n-1\), giving exactly \(\tau(n-1)\) expansions, in contrast to the previously known Gödel-chain count.
