Among ordered idempotent pairs in \(M_2(\mathbb F_{p^e})\), precisely \(q(q+1)(q-1)S_e\) generate the entire unital ring without adjoined field scalars, where \(S_e\) counts full-degree field elements excluding zero and one. More generally, the trace of the pair classifies nondegenerate simultaneous-conjugacy orbits and determines exactly which matrix subfield ring is generated.
