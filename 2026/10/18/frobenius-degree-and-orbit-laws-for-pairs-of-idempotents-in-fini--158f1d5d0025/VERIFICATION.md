# Finite verification and limits

The standalone `verify.py` performs its own polynomial-quotient construction of finite fields, enumerates all matrix idempotents and all pairs of rank-one idempotents, and checks the trace-stratified count. For every listed field it additionally computes every prime-field vector-space span generated by the identity and all words in each pair, using direct right multiplication by both idempotents. There is no sampling in these finite cases.

The program's expected full-ring pair counts are \(0,24,120,360,3024,4320\) for \(q=2,3,4,5,8,9\), respectively. It verifies all count identities and prints `VERIFY_OK`. These finite checks do not constitute an exhaustive proof for arbitrarily large fields. The uniform proof appears in RESULT.md.

The script can be rerun with Python 3 and no third-party dependencies using `python verify.py`.
