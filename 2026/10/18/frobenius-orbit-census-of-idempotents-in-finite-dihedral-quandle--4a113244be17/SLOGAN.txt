For every odd \(n\ge3\) and every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\) with \(\gcd(q,n)=1\), all idempotents of the nonassociative dihedral quandle algebra \(\mathbb F_q[R_n]\) admit an explicit bijective Fourier–Frobenius orbit parametrization, giving \(2\prod_D(1+g_D)\) idempotents with computable arithmetic factors \(g_D\). This yields the exact nonsplit-field counts and arithmetic jumps, including \(24\) over \(\mathbb F_{23}\) versus \(68\) over \(\mathbb F_{67}\) for \(R_{11}\).
