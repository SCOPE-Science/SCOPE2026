For an incidence algebra over \(\mathbb F_{p^e}\), exact generation by randomly chosen matrices as a **unital ring without adjoining the coefficient field** is governed by distinct full Frobenius orbits of the diagonal tuples together with independent nondegeneracy tests at every cover. This gives a closed formula for every finite poset and a sharp generator threshold, distinct from the known scalar-assisted algebra-generation law over nonprime fields.
