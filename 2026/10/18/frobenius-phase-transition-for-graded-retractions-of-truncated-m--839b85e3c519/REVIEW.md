# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Graded maps are uniquely determined by their degree-one matrices. The quotient relations are equivalent to the vanishing of each column's truncation power. The first nonzero mixed binomial coefficient distinguishes prime-power exponents from all others; this forces unrestricted matrices in the former case and column-monomial matrices in the latter. Rank formulas then follow from Gaussian surjection counts and inclusion-exclusion, while retraction-image counts follow from complementary subspaces or fixed weighted roots. Exact enumeration of admissible maps and idempotents in 18 finite cases supports but does not replace this proof.

**Originality — PASS within inspected coverage.** The 2022 complete-intersection paper analyzes strong Lefschetz multiplication maps, not the graded endomorphism and retraction spectra. The 2024 automorphism paper analyzes invertible maps (in characteristic zero), including a specific two-variable quadratic instance, rather than all graded endomorphisms in positive characteristic. A separate earlier classification of *all* nongraded retractions in the characteristic-two squarefree specialization already implies the retract image types and rank support in that slice; these are excluded from independent novelty. Its theorem does not count the full graded endomorphism spectra for all exponents, does not identify the prime-power/non-prime-power transition, and does not supply the stated complete all-parameter graded multiplicities. Published database searches for those complete graded spectra and exponent thresholds found no directly covering statement. A similar result in unindexed or older transformation-monoid literature remains possible; database absence is not a uniqueness certificate.

**Value — PASS.** The family is a fundamental class of Artinian monomial complete intersections. The exponent being a pure power of the characteristic changes the full graded endomorphism monoid from weighted partial functions to unrestricted linear maps, with a corresponding jump from coordinate-only retract images to all linear-subspace images. The theorem classifies every image and computes the full endomorphism and retraction rank distributions with exact image multiplicities for all field sizes, dimensions and equal truncation exponents.

The reported formulas do not address nongraded endomorphisms or unequal exponents. The automorphism subgroup in the non-power case is familiar and is not presented as an independent novelty.
