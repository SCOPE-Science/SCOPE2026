For the standard-graded complete intersection \(\mathbb F_q[X_1,\ldots,X_n]/(X_i^d)\), its graded endomorphism monoid is the full matrix monoid exactly when the exponent is a power of the characteristic, and otherwise it is a weighted partial-transformation monoid. This dichotomy yields the exact rank polynomials, all retraction images of algebra dimensions \(d^r\), and their image-by-image multiplicities.
