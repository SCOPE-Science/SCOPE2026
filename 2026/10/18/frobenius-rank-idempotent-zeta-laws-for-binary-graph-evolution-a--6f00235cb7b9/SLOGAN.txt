For every evolution algebra with binary structure matrix \(M\), the number of idempotents over \(\mathbb F_{2^f}\) is exactly \(2^{\dim\ker(M^f-I)}\); the stable invertible part of \(M\) also gives the exact geometric count, minimal splitting field, and full idempotent zeta function. The five-cycle and Petersen algebras exhibit distinct exact Frobenius periods of three and two.
