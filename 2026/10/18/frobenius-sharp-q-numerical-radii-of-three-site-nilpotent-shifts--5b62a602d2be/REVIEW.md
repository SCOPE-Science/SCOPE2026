An exact cubic determinant and simple Perron singular root prove strict monotonicity in squared edge product, and the balanced top-singular multiplicity gives the sharp \(q=1/2\) plateau. The \(q=1\) Hilbert endpoint is independently computed from the Hermitian part. The checker passes 1,645 finite rational/numerical assertions. The analytical proof, not the finite checks, establishes universal quantifiers.

The available 2010 Chien–Nakazato abstract concerns a two-sided tridiagonal family; the complete text was not retrieved. The general 2013 Chien–Nakazato discriminant paper was likewise abstract-accessible only. No compared available statement implies strict ordering under a fixed Frobenius budget, though older algebraic-pencil methods may conceal an equivalent result. No independent priority certification.

**Value — PASS.** The exact joint \(q\)/energy extremal classification singles out two natural matrix geometries, proves strict uniqueness for all \(q<1\), and locates a plateau and a sharp loss-of-rigidity endpoint. These features distinguish the claim from isolated low-dimensional numerical evaluations.

**Limitations.** Only Hilbert-space three-site one-step nilpotent shifts and real overlap \(q\in[0,1]\) are treated. The lower profile for \(q>1/2\) is implicit and the result is not asserted for larger blocks or arbitrary triangular matrices. Some older full-text comparisons remain inaccessible.
