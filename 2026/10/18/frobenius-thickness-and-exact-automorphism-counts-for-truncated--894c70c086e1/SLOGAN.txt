For every Frobenius-truncated polynomial rectangle \(k[x_1,\ldots,x_d]/(x_i^{p^{a_i}})\) in characteristic \(p\), its entire automorphism scheme admits explicit product coordinates as an affine variety, general-linear factors and infinitesimal truncated factors, revealing a uniform nonsmoothness defect. These coordinates give a closed formula for the number of automorphisms over every finite field and the exact infinitesimal thickness, but do not assert a product decomposition of the group law.
