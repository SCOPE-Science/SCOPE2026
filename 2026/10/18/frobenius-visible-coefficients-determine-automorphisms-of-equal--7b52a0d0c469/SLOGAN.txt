For \(A=\mathbb F_q[x_1,\ldots,x_r]/(x_i^d)\), the \(p\)-free part \(m\) of \(d=mp^s\) exactly controls the permitted nonlinear monomials in every algebra automorphism. If \(m=1\), the reduced automorphism variety is \(\operatorname{GL}_r\) times an affine space; if \(m>1\), it has \(r!\) torus–affine components and an explicit cardinality \(r!(q-1)^r q^{r(d^r-1-m^{r-1})}\).
