In a deterministic-weight recursive tree, the joint law of a fixed old depth and a younger depth is obtained by adding independent Bernoulli noise to the younger coordinate of the adjacent-label pair. Its distance to independence has sharp order \(V^{-1/2}\) when the noise variance \(V\) diverges, even though the covariance stays strictly positive. For power weights the sharp boundary is \(\alpha=-1\), with persistent dependence below it.
