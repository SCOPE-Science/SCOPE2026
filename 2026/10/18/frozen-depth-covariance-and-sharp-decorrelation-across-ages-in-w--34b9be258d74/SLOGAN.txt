In a deterministic-weight random recursive tree, the covariance of an old vertex's depth with every later vertex's depth stays at the same strictly positive value, yet their full joint law becomes independent in total variation at the sharp rate \(\Theta(V^{-1/2})\) whenever the Bernoulli depth-noise variance \(V\) diverges. Power-weight attachment has a sharp boundary at \(\alpha=-1\): decorrelation rates are \((\log j)^{-1/2}\) and \((\log\log j)^{-1/2}\) above and at the boundary, while below it the dependence persists.
