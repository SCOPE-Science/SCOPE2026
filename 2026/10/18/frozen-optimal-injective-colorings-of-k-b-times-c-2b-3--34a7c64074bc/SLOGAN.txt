At the critical \(2b+1\)-color palette for injective coloring of \(K_b\times C_{2b+3}\), every optimum has recoloring degree exactly \(0\), \(b\), or \(2b\), and the exact number in each class follows from a unique deficient color support. The fraction of frozen optimal colorings equals \(\frac{(b-1)(b-2)}{(b+1)(b+2)}\), tending to one as \(b\to\infty\).
