## Correctness — PASS
The argument states and proves a sufficient graph-level lemma rather than inferring measure from category. The transfer operator counts only trajectories avoiding a constant branch. Its fixed-iterate affine inverse branches have stretch at least \((\sqrt2)^r\); the endpoint-inclusive variation estimate gives contraction coefficient \(3(\sqrt2)^{-r}<1\) for \(r\ge4\). A positive survivor arclength would yield, by bounded variation compactness and Cesàro averaging, a nonzero absolutely continuous invariant measure supported outside the dense open plateau-preimage set. A nonzero nonnegative BV density cannot vanish on such a set. This is an infinite-time analytical proof; the finite exact integer derivative checks merely verify the nonzero stretch list. The cited source's graph construction and density proposition are explicit dependencies.

The nonzero residual priority risk is an unindexed general theorem about piecewise-expanding graph maps with holes.

The result settles that naturally motivated gap for every real parameter, including positive-entropy invariant graphs, by a parameter-independent structural argument. This conclusion is substantially more than a renaming or a finite table entry, while making no assertion about ambient planar measure or about the geometry of the exceptional set.
