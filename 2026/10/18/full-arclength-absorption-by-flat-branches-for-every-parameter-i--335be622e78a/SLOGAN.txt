For the invariant finite graph of \(F_{a,b}(x,y)=(|x|-y+a,\ x-|y|+b)\), every \(a<0\) and every real \(b\) has full arclength absorption into the preimages of constant-image edges: the exceptional never-absorbed set has \(\mathcal H^1\)-measure zero. A killed-transfer bounded-variation argument extends the previously known open-dense statement and the restricted-parameter full-measure results to all parameters, without claiming ambient planar full measure.
