For every dimension \(n\ge2\), the Liu–Zhu small-alpha toric Fano variety has **exactly** the algebraic automorphism group \(\operatorname{Aut}(X_n)\cong(\mathbb C^*)^n\rtimes\mathfrak S_{n+1}\). A global zero-sum identity for its primitive rays eliminates every Demazure root, and the two types of polytope facets force all discrete automorphisms to be coordinate permutations.
