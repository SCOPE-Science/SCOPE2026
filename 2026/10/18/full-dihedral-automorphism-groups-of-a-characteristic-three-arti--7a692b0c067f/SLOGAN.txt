For the ordinary characteristic-three Artin–Schreier curves \(y^3-y=\lambda/(x^m-1)\), with \(m\ge5\) and \(m\equiv2\pmod3\), the full geometric automorphism group is the dihedral group of order \(6m\). In particular, the genus-eight cyclic-order-\(15\) example in the recent wild-boundary classification has exactly \(30\) geometric automorphisms.
