The Heawood graph's graphic-matroid forest complex has \(\widetilde H_{12}\cong\mathbb Z^{3152}\), and its full 336-element symmetry group has a completely explicit eight-type rational homology character. The full-invariant top homology has dimension \(14\), while incidence-preserving symmetries fix \(18\) dimensions, four of which reverse under point-line duality.
