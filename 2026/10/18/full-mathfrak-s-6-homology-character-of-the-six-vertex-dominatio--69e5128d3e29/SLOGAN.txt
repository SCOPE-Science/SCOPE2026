The natural \(\mathfrak S_6\)-action on the six-vertex bounded-domination complex \(D_{6,3}\) has a completely determined graded rational homology character: degree four contains all nine non-linear Specht irreducibles, while degree five is \(S^{(5,1)}\oplus S^{(4,2)}\oplus S^{(4,1,1)}\). Neither degree has a trivial or sign constituent, so both the symmetric and alternating relabeling quotients are rationally acyclic.
