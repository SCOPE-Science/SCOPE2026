The rational second homology of the full proper-coset complex of \(A_5\) decomposes as \(100V_6\oplus95V_4\oplus116V_5\), with complete left-translation character \((1560,-84,-21,5,5)\). This refines the previously established nonequivariant homotopy type \(\bigvee^{1560}S^2\) without asserting a new homotopy classification.
