For the ten-rung prism, the two rational homology groups of its perfect-matching complex both have rank two but carry different, explicitly classified \(D_{10}\times C_2\) representations. Degree three splits as \(\eta_r\oplus\eta_s\eta_\tau\), while degree four splits as \(\mathbf1\oplus\eta_r\eta_s\); these are determined by exhaustive rational boundary and signed symmetry calculations.
