# Reproduction and limits

Run `python3 verification/check.py` in the extracted package root. It must exit successfully and emit exactly the bytes in `verification/expected_output.txt`. It relies only on the Python 3 standard library.

The perfect-matching recursion visits all possible edges at the least unmatched vertex. The full face universe is generated by subsets of every resulting maximal matching; this is exhaustive because the definition of the complex makes these the facets. The auxiliary independent-set complex is constructed independently from a conflict graph. All extra cross-layer restrictions are explicit and its exact union with the two exceptional simplices is checked.

A free-face collapse removes a face and its unique codimension-one coface; the full sequence leaves 713 and 483 nonempty faces in the two complexes. Exact rational oriented-chain elimination gives ranks in degrees three and four. A separate unreduced mod-two calculation provides an additional arithmetic check but does not by itself prove rational or integral homology.

For each of 40 group elements, the checker constructs and validates the permutation of all graph edges, then computes signed reduced Lefschetz numbers from complete invariant-face orbit unions. It compares those to the two predicted character tables. A second direct oriented-simplex sign enumeration checks nine distinct nonidentity elements. All arithmetic is exact; no stochastic samples, numerical tolerance, or external computational certificate are used.

The conclusion concerns only rational group representations of this finite complex. Independent scholarly validation is not claimed.
