For the ordinary matching complex of the five-crown graph, the unique rational degree-two homology line is fixed by simultaneous row-column permutations but negated by transposition. Its 13-dimensional degree-three homology splits under the full \(S_5\times C_2\) symmetry as \((S^{(4,1)}\oplus S^{(3,2)})\boxtimes\mathbf1\oplus S^{(2,1,1,1)}\boxtimes\varepsilon\).
