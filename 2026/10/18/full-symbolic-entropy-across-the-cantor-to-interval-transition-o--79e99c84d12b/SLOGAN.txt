In the Catsigeras–Guiraud–Meyroneinc–Ugalde contracting three-dimensional skew product, \(2^{n-1}\) admissible orbits remain separated at one fixed scale for every \(n\ge2\), despite overlaps between the fiber maps. The full-system separated-set entropy is exactly \(\log2\) for every contraction parameter, including the regime with connected interval attractor.
