The full 336-element automorphism group of the Heawood graph acts on the rational two-particle configuration homology with explicitly determined character: the nonzero homology representations have dimensions \(8\) and \(14\). Neither representation has a nonzero invariant vector, so quotienting the configuration space by all graph symmetries—or just the point-line-preserving symmetries—kills every positive-degree rational homology class.
