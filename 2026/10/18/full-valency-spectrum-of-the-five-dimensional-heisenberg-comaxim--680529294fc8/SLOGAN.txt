The comaximal graph of the five-dimensional Heisenberg Lie algebra over every \(\mathbb F_q\) has exactly five explicit vertex-degree classes, determined by the symplectic type of projected subalgebras. In contrast with the three-dimensional case, isotropic and nonisotropic planes have different degrees, and the graph has clique and chromatic number \(q^3+q^2+q+1\).
