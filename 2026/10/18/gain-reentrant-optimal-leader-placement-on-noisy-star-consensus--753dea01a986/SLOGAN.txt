For noisy consensus on an unweighted star, the exact optimal finite-gain \(q\)-leader placement includes the hub precisely when \(n>2q+\kappa q(q-1)/(1+\kappa)^2\), with ties at equality. The first gain-reentrant case is \(n=7\), \(q=3\): hub-inclusive placements win outside \([2-\sqrt3,2+\sqrt3]\), leaf-only placements win strictly inside it, and both tie at the two endpoints.
