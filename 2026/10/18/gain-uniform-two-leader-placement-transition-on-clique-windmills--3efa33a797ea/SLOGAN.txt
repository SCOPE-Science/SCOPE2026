For two equal finite-gain noisy leaders on the clique windmill \(K_1\vee(rK_m)\), the optimal pair changes sharply at three blades: with \(r=2\) it uses two different peripheral cliques and avoids the hub, whereas for every \(r\ge3\) it must contain the hub and one peripheral vertex. This classification holds without ties for every clique size \(m\ge2\) and every gain \(\kappa>0\), with exact rational formulas for all three competing placement types.
