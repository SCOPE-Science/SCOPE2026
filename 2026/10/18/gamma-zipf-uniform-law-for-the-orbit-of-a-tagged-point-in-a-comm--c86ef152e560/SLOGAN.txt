For Ewens-weighted random commuting permutation pairs, a uniformly tagged orbit has asymptotically Gamma-distributed size on the \(\sqrt n\) scale, an independent universal inverse-square number of first-generator cycles, and an independent uniform normalized twist. The exact finite pointed-orbit probability is \(\theta Z_{n-ad}(\theta)/(nZ_n(\theta))\) for each admissible torus dimension and twist.
