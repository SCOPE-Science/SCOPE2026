For double-star graphical zonotopes, two independent Gaussian objectives select the same vertex with an exact rational probability given by a finite factorial sum. If the two hub leaf counts tend to infinity in proportion \(\theta:(1-\theta)\), their angular collision probability is asymptotic to \(4(1-\theta(1-\theta))/(p^2q^2)\), revealing a sharp one-quarter suppression for balanced hubs relative to two uncoupled stars.
