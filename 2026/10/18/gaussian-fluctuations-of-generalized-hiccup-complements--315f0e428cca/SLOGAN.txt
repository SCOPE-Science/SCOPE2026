For every fixed generalized \((0,x,1,k+1)\)-hiccup sequence with \(x\ge3\) and \(k\ge2\), the complement-count discrepancy at a uniformly sampled index has Gaussian fluctuations of order \(\sqrt{\log_k N}\) around the explicit drift \(\bigl(x-2-(k-1)/2\bigr)\log_kN\). A tree-word decomposition couples the fluctuations to independent uniform base-\(k\) digits while controlling all stage boundaries.
