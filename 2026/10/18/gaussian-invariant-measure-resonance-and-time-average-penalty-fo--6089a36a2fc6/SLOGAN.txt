On every Gaussian target, two recently studied Langevin stochastic Runge–Kutta schemes generate exactly the same path when their Brownian data are shared. Their invariant Gaussian law is exactly correct at a positive step size only for isotropic curvature, at \(h\lambda=4/3\); even there, physical-time averaging variance is inflated by the exact factor \(7/3\) relative to the continuous diffusion.
