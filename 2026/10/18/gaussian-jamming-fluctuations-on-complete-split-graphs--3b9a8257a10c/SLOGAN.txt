On a complete split graph with an independent class of asymptotic size \(\alpha N\), irreversible random-priority dimer deposition leaves the positive unmatched fraction \(\alpha e^{-(1-\alpha)/\alpha}\), even where perfect matchings exist. Its \(\sqrt N\)-scale vacancies are asymptotically Gaussian with explicit variance \(\alpha e^{-L}[4-(L+2)^2e^{-L}]\), where \(L=(1-\alpha)/\alpha\).
