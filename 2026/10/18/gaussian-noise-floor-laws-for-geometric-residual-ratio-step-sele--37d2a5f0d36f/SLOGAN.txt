In a Gaussian observation-noise extension of geometric residual-ratio step selection, a fixed \(w\)-step window on \(d\)-component residuals has a sharp positive-moment cutoff \(q<dw\), and its noise-floor one-step unit-curvature squared gain exceeds one (or is infinite). Even though the underlying observation noise is light-tailed, half of limiting steps cross the classical normalized stability ceiling; a full-history window instead approaches that ceiling almost surely.
