Every vertex of a partial permutohedron has an exact Gaussian normal-cone angle governed by a binomial rank cap, and its Euclidean mean width has a one-integral formula for arbitrary dimensions. When the maximum label and ambient dimension grow proportionally, the normalized width has an explicit profile that is \(C^2\) but not \(C^3\) at label-to-dimension ratio \(1/2\), with a clipped-normal critical angular law.
