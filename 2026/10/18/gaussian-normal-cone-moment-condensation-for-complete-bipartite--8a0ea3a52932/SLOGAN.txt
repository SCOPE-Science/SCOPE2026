A complete-bipartite graphical zonotope has exponentially many vertices but only logarithmic Rényi entropy under Gaussian vertex exposure: for fixed \(p\ge2\) and integer \(t\ge2\), its \(t\)-direction collision probability is asymptotic to \(K_{p,t}q^{-pt}\). Remarkably, conditional on a collision almost every selected orientation has all \(q\) large-side vertices in a single block, although those orientations have vanishing unconditional exposure probability.
