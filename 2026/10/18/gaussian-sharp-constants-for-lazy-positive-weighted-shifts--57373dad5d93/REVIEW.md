# Review: Gaussian sharp constants for lazy positive weighted shifts

**Correctness: PASS.** The powers and their first differences admit exact column-sum norm identities on \(\ell^1\). The binomial local limit and the discrete derivative local limit lead respectively to the two Gaussian fractional integrals. The integrability \(0<\alpha<1\) and separate column-localization argument are crucial for passing from pointwise limits to operator norms. Positivity, the ACB bound, and peripheral spectrum are proved directly, not inferred from numerical tests.

**Originality: PASS, subject to stated risks.** The initiating papers by Abbar–Arnold–Coine and Arnold already contain weighted backward shift techniques and lazy ACB preservation; those ingredients are not claimed. The contribution is the pair of **exact sharp asymptotic operator-norm constants** for the lazy positive family and its discrete first difference. Actual primary-source full texts and semantic search comparisons found no stronger covering theorem. Closest indexed comparators treat general ACB extremal bounds, non-lazy shift moments, or unrelated Gaussian models. Unindexed equivalents remain a priority risk.

**Mathematical value: PASS.** This specifies the quantitative boundary between divergent positive-operator power growth and vanishing Katznelson–Tzafriri differences, with positive finite exact constants across an entire parameter interval. It does not settle the broader 2026 positive \(L^p\), \(p>1\), question.

Limits: fixed parameters strictly within \((0,1)\); no endpoint uniformity; optional finite checker is diagnostic only. Source comparison with the 2020 JMAA paper is restricted to accessible indexed content, not certified full-text exclusion.
