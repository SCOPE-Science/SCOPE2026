For a natural positive absolutely Cesàro bounded lazy weighted shift on \(\ell^1\), the operator powers grow at the sharp order \(n^{\alpha/2}\) while their successive differences decay at the sharp order \(n^{-(1-\alpha)/2}\). Both exact leading constants are maxima of explicit fractional Gaussian integrals, giving a quantitative positive counterpart to recent nonpositive Katznelson–Tzafriri counterexamples.
