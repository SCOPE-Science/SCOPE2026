For a motion-invariant random affine hyperplane cutting a regular simplex with \(m\) vertices, the entire section-vertex law is determined by expected spacings of ordered Gaussian samples. Its logarithmic vertex count has the nondegenerate universal limit \(\log N_m/\log m\Rightarrow2-U^2\), with \(U\) uniform on \([0,1]\), unlike the quadratic vertex counts in centroid-passing sections.
