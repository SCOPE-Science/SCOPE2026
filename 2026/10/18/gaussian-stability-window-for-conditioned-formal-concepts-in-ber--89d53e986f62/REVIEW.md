# Scientific review

## Correctness
The concept event factors into disjoint incidence blocks. Each outside-intent column restricted to the proposed extent has the independently truncated Bernoulli law; this proves the complete conditional-law reduction and exact moments by inclusion–exclusion. A uniform comparison with the binomial subset-size threshold gives the stated \(O_p(\log a/\sqrt a)\) mean-absolute Gaussian approximation. Exact rational exhaustive matrix tests cover 264 small finite instances. The infinite statement is justified analytically, not by extrapolating the finite tests.

## Originality
The 2007 Kuznetsov, 2014 Zhi, and 2022 Mouakher–Kő papers describe stability and algorithms for deterministic contexts; the 2014 Buzmakov–Kuznetsov–Napoli study performs empirical comparisons of stability across separately sampled datasets, without an analytic conditional Gaussian law. The 2021 Bernoulli formal-context article counts concepts rather than conditionally evaluating the stability of one specified concept. The new proposed distinction is the conditional moment law *together with* the uniform quenched Gaussian transition. Earlier results about random fuzzy contexts involve a different incidence model. Relevant mathematical research searches revealed no covering conditional-stability theorem; there is a residual risk of an equivalent formulation in unindexed literature or the inaccessible complete 2022 article. Search absence alone does not establish novelty.

## Mathematical value
Concept stability measures robustness of an intent to removal of objects, but computing it for arbitrary contexts is difficult. The conditional law and quantitative threshold identify, for a natural random closure-lattice model, precisely when this robustness changes from nearly complete to nearly absent; the result includes exact finite moments and a quantitative typical-instance rate. The conditional nature of the model is essential and explicitly bounded in scope.

## Closest literature and limitations
Kuznetsov (2007), Zhi (2014), Buzmakov–Kuznetsov–Napoli (2014), Mouakher–Kő (2022), and Sakurai (2021) were compared at the level of their conclusions, not only titles. The 2022 source's abstract was accessible, while complete text access was blocked; the remaining risk is explicitly recorded. The result is not a claim about unconditioned or uniformly selected concepts, changing incidence density, or simultaneous stability across the lattice.
