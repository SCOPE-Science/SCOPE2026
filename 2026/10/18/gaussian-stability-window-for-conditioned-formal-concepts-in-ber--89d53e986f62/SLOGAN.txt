Conditioned on a fixed labelled extent–intent pair forming a concept in a Bernoulli incidence context, the concept's intensional stability has exact first and second moments determined only by the extent size and number of outside attributes. In the critical window \(\log_{1/p}(m-b)=a/2+z\sqrt a/2+o(\sqrt a)\), this random stability concentrates at \(1-\Phi(z)\), with a uniform \(O_p(\log a/\sqrt a)\) Gaussian-profile mean-absolute error.
