For any fixed symmetric nondegenerate product law with endpoints at \(\pm1\), the degree-two orthogonal polynomial projector has a universal Gaussian limiting Lebesgue profile proportional to the average squared evaluation coordinates. Its sharp endpoint norm grows like \(2d/(s\sqrt{2\pi e})\), where \(s\) is the marginal variance, and every asymptotically maximizing point sequence must concentrate near the corners.
