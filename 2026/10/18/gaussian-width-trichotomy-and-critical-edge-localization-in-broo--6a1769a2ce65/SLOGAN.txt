Broom-tree symmetric edge polytopes are all crosspolytopes of the same intrinsic volume at fixed dimension, yet their random directional widths exhibit a sharp competition between a half-normal hub extreme and a Gumbel path extreme. In the critical window the centered width converges to \((|Z|+t)_+\), whose atom at zero exactly equals the limiting probability that a path edge, rather than a pendant edge, supplies the exposed vertex.
