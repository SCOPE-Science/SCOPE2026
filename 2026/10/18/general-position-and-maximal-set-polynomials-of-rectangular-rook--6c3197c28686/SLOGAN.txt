General-position sets on \(K_m\square K_n\) are precisely bipartite star forests, yielding an exact two-variable exponential generating function and a closed inclusion-maximal-set polynomial for every \(m,n\ge2\). For \(2\le m\le n\), the sizes of maximal sets are exactly \(\{m\}\cup\{n,\ldots,m+n-2\}\), creating arbitrarily long gaps as \(n-m\) grows.
