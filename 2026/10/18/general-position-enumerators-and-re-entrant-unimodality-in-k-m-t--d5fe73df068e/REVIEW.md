# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The direct-product adjacency convention and diameter-two geometry reduce general position to the absence of induced three-vertex paths. Exhaustive four-type configuration analysis proves the complete general-position and maximal-set polynomials; a one-vertex saturation argument establishes the latter without assuming that maximum size implies maximality. Two exact coefficient differences and the binomial-tail shape prove the full three-by-\(n\) re-entrant unimodality classification, including both boundaries \(n=10/11\) and \(n=17/18\). A separate direct adjacency checker reproduced both polynomial distributions in five graph orders and the integer consequences over additional parameter ranges. Finite computation is corroborative, not the proof of the infinite claims.

**Originality — PASS with residual risk.** The most relevant prior full text, Di Stefano et al., arXiv:2306.09965, Theorem 5.4, establishes the scalar minimum maximal general-position cardinality for the same direct product and exhibits several of the present structural types. Those contributions are not claimed as new. The other inspected primary work, Iršič et al., *General Position Polynomials* (2024), introduces the enumeration invariant and studies the **Cartesian** product and coefficient unimodality, but supplies neither the direct-product all-set and facet enumerators nor the sharp nonunimodal-then-unimodal window. Searches of published mathematical records did not identify a covering statement. Equivalent unindexed results, particularly under induced-cluster-subgraph enumeration terminology, remain possible.

**Value — PASS.** The two-parameter all-set polynomial and the complete facet-size spectrum answer natural enumeration questions not determined by the known lower general-position number. The re-entrant unimodality threshold gives a precise finite band of failure within an infinite standard graph-product family, while the facet enumeration proves purity only at \(K_4\times K_4\) and arbitrarily long gaps elsewhere. This supplies distinct structural and coefficient-shape information rather than a routine scalar-parameter refinement.

**Limitations.** Both factors must have order at least three. The evidence is a self-contained mathematical proof plus finite checking, with no independent audit or blanket priority guarantee. Primary source [3] was compared at abstract/metadata level only; it contributes general structural background, not a documented same-parameter enumeration.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
