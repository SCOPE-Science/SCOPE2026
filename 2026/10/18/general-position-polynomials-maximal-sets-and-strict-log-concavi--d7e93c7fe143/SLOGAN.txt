For every antihole \(\overline{C_n}\) with \(n\ge5\), the complete general-position polynomial is the original cycle's independence polynomial plus \(nz^2+nz^3\), and all its coefficients are strictly log-concave. Every inclusion-maximal general-position set is classified and counted, with \(\overline{C_6}\) the sole case having a maximal two-vertex set.
