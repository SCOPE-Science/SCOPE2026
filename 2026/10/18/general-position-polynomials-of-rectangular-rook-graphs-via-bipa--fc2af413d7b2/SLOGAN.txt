General-position vertex subsets of the rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) are exactly star-forest edge subsets of \(K_{m,n}\), giving the full exponential generating function \(\exp(xe^{zy}+ye^{zx}-zxy)\). For \(m,n\ge3\) there are precisely \(mn\) maximum general-position sets, refining the previously known maximum size.
