On complete two-level bi-intuitionistic Kripke frames, all definable upsets are determined exactly by separate binary signatures on the two levels; this yields sharp generator rank \(\max\{1,\lceil\log_2 m\rceil+\lceil\log_2 n\rceil\}\) and an explicit enumeration of generating valuations. For two levels of size \(t\), uniformly random valuations instead become expressively complete around \(4\log_2 t\), with an exact Gaussian square-root-logarithmic transition window.
