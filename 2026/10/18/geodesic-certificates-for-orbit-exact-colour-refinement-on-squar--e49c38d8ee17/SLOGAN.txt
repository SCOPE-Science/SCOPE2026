For every \(n\ge5\), marking one vertex of the square toroidal grid \(C_n\square C_n\) makes ordinary counting colour refinement recover exactly its rooted automorphism orbits. Distance, the number of neighbours one step nearer the mark, and the number of shortest paths already separate the \(\frac{(\lfloor n/2\rfloor+1)(\lfloor n/2\rfloor+2)}2\) orbit classes.
