For a positive-weight graph whose edges all lie on terminal geodesics, shortening a metrically redundant terminal shortcut by \(\varepsilon\) removes exactly \(2e(P)\varepsilon^d/d!\) of anchored Lipschitz-dual volume, where \(P\) is the geodesic reachability poset. The coefficient encodes a \(\#\mathrm P\)-hard linear-extension count even on unweighted bipartite diameter-three graphs with a single length-two shortcut.
