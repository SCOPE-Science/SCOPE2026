For any finite projective plane of order \(q\ge2\), its incidence graph has geodetic number \(2q\), and every minimum geodetic basis comprises the \(q\) points of a line other than a marked incident point together with the \(q\) other lines through that point. The \((q^2+q+1)(q+1)\) bases admit no single-vertex optimal exchange, but the exact first connected simultaneous-exchange threshold is \(\min\{q+1,2q-2\}\).
