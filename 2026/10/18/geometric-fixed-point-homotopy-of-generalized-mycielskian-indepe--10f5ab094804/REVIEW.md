# Review of geometric fixed-point homotopy classification

## Correctness
**PASS.** The fixed-point simplicial orbit model is an exact piecewise-linear parametrization of invariant barycentric coordinates. Every nontrivial label orbit yields a vertex only below the top clique. When there is a fixed label, its same-level vertex has open neighborhood contained in the nontrivial orbit-vertex neighborhood; deleting all such orbit-vertices by graph folds leaves the smaller generalized Mycielskian. With no fixed labels, full adjacency between neighboring level blocks and twin elimination leave an ordinary path. Standard path cofibration and established generalized-Mycielskian homotopy types produce the sphere formulas. The included program checked 117 exact graph-fold examples, 37 signed Lefschetz sums and 28 complete small mod-two homologies. Neither numerical tests nor rational characters are used as an infinite homotopy proof.

## Originality
**PASS, with residual literature risk.** The 2019 Goyal--Shukla--Singh theorem and the 2026 Carnero Bravo theorem determine ordinary wedge homotopy types, not symmetry-fixed geometric realizations. A closely related published result gives rational symmetric-group homology modules and their characters as functions of the number of fixed labels. Equal fixed-point Euler characteristics do not determine fixed-point homotopy equivalence. The explicit orbit-graph collapses, valid at every generalized depth, provide the missing stronger statement. A special one-step suspension case is already known and is not treated as the central novelty. The 2019 and 2026 primary texts and the relevant published character result were inspected. An unpublished or unindexed equivalent fixed-point theorem remains possible.

## Value
**PASS.** This is an all-parameter integral homotopy classification for geometric fixed sets of a natural highly symmetric graph-complex family, not a substitution in an existing Betti-number formula. It reduces arbitrary permutation cycle structures to one topological parameter, yields a nontrivial mod-three sphere/contractibility transition for every derangement, and supplies concrete data usable in equivariant obstruction and fixed-point analyses. The reduction is constructive at the level of elementary collapses.

## Scope and limits
The finding does not assert an equivariant equivalence of the original complexes, a canonical integral representation, or a classification of geometric orbit-space quotients. Independent scientific audit has not been performed.
