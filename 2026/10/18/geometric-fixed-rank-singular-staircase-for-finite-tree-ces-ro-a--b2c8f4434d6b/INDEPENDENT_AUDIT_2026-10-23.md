# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For complete fixed b-ary trees the level-radial basis and orthonormal local child contrasts give an exact unitary direct sum A_N plus trailing A_N[k:N,k:N] with multiplicity (b-1)b^(k-1). The dimension identity closes the decomposition; radial A_N(j,l)=b^((j-l)/2)/(j+1) for l<=j. After scaling (N+1)b^(-N/2), reverse the output rows and observe each fixed k block has Hilbert–Schmidt convergence, dominated by (h+1)b^(-(h+l)/2), to rank-one u_h v_l with singular value b/(b-1)b^(-k/2). All other fixed-block singular values tend to zero; compression monotonicity bounds the infinitely many deeper blocks, giving the ordered fixed-rank staircase exactly. Strict positivity and Perron–Frobenius yield the unique radial leading vector. I independently compared direct vertex-indexed SVD and contrast-block spectra in binary and ternary small trees; this corroborates, not establishes, the N→∞ limit. Scope: fixed integer j,b; not uniform in growing j.

## originality

PASS

The submitted sharp limit of every fixed Hilbert singular rank, its geometric multiplicities, and the ratio of second to first are distinct from the classical continuous-tree Hardy approximation-number estimates, general discrete-tree entropy-number statements, and one-dimensional generalized Cesàro coefficient formulas. No inspected source gives this precise double geometric height/rank scaling. Priority is qualified by possible unpublished weighted-tree width results.

An exact asymptotic s-number law for a natural hierarchical averaging operator makes branching-dependent approximation cost and spectral gaps explicit; the multiplicity law is a nontrivial invariant of tree symmetry and not an elementary recoding of a known table.
