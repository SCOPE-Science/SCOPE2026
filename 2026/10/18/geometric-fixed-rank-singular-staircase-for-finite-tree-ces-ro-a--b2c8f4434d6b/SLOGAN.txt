On a complete \(b\)-ary finite tree, averaging along each root-to-vertex path has a sharp, geometrically stepped fixed-rank singular spectrum: after scaling by \((N+1)b^{-N/2}\), the \(j\)-th singular value tends to \(\frac{b}{b-1}b^{-\lceil\log_b j\rceil/2}\). The jump multiplicities are exactly the sizes of successive branching levels, and the second-to-first singular-value ratio tends to \(b^{-1/2}\).
