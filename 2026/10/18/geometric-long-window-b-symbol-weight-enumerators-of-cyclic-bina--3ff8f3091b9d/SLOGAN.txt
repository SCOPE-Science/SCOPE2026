For the binary cyclic Hamming code of length \(n=2^m-1\), every cyclic window size \(b>n/2\) has a complete \(b\)-symbol weight enumerator with geometric nonfull-weight multiplicities \(n,n,2n,4n,\ldots\), ending at \(n-1\); precisely \(n2^{n-b-m-1}\) nonzero codewords have nonfull weight when \(b<n-m\). This follows from unique long zero runs and consecutive parity-check column independence.
