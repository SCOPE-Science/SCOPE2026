# Scientific review: quadratic Fock weighted compositions

## Correctness: PASS
The theorem is specified for \(0<|a|<1\) and arbitrary complex quadratic weight parameter \(b\), with the classical normalized Bargmann–Fock norm. Phase rotations reduce the singular-value and modulus-spectrum questions to real positive parameters. A change of variables proves boundedness at and below \(|b|=1-|a|^2\); coherent kernels prove unboundedness above it. An exact positive Gaussian kernel for \(T^*T\) is checked independently against finite Fock-monomial Gram coefficients. In the compact region, the Gaussian generating family yields every nonzero geometric eigenvalue, and equality with the directly calculated trace proves completeness. At the boundary the Gaussian kernel equals a momentum heat operator, whose Fourier multiplier proves purely absolutely continuous modulus spectrum and the flat approximation-number profile. The sharp Schatten divergence follows from an explicit quadratic-root expansion. The standard-library script checks finite identities, not the infinite arguments.

## Originality: PASS, with explicit uncertainty
The closest source [1] provides boundedness, compactness, Hilbert–Schmidt classification, and essential-norm estimates for weighted Fock composition operators, but no exact singular-sequence or critical heat-spectrum result is stated in its accessible description. Its full text could not be inspected. Johnson [2], inspected through the complete paper, includes the broader quadratic Bargmann-pullback setting, but Theorems 1–2 establish boundedness/compactness and a general exponential singular-value upper bound, not this exact two-parameter singular-value progression, spectral-type transition, or sharp blow-up coefficient. Fatehi [3], also inspected through the complete text, treats normality, closed range, and linear-exponential-weight norms; it does not contain the present quadratic-weight profile. The Putinar–Tener [5] Hardy-space weighted-composition result is not an identity for the Fock operator. Classical Mehler Gaussian diagonalization and Fourier heat functional calculus are acknowledged prior techniques. Neither semantic searching nor literature inspection establishes exhaustive novelty; potential equivalent Gaussian-semigroup formulations and unread portions of [1] remain the main bibliographic risks.

## Value: PASS
The fixed quadratic exponential weight is a natural nontrivial parameter family in current Fock weighted-composition theory. The theorem computes *all* singular values, rather than only compactness, a Hilbert–Schmidt criterion, or an upper bound. It also identifies the exact functional calculus and continuous spectral type at the noncompact boundary and quantifies the universal square-root divergence of every Schatten quasi-norm. This is a structural boundary classification with explicit measurable spectral consequences, not a mere reparameterization of the compactness condition. It is restricted to the classical one-variable Gaussian Fock norm and strict linear contractions and does not classify the full spectrum of the nonnormal operator.
