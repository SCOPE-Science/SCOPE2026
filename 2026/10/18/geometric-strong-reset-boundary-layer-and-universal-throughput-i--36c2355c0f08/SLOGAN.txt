For a fixed open exclusion chain with local return to its injection site, strong resetting leaves an exact rare one-excess-particle layer whose weights grow upstream by factors of \(3/2\). The stationary extraction current is asymptotic to \(\alpha/r\) at every length \(L\ge3\), but to \((\alpha+\beta)/r\) at length two; the nearest-boundary occupancy covariance is always \(-1/r+O(r^{-2})\).
