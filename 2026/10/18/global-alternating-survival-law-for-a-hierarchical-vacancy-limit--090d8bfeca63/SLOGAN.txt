In a vacancy-limited hierarchical predation chain with a common break-even vacancy, the eventual survivors are exactly the first, third, fifth, and subsequent alternating species in each initially occupied consecutive run, even when growth and predation rates are unequal. Every eliminated but initially present species has the sharp exponential extinction rate \(p_{i-1}\ell_{i-1}\), and the surviving densities sum to \(1-\delta\).
