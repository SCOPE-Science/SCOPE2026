At the global flip boundary of a classical rational planar map, every non-axis orbit has an exact inverse-square relaxation law with a nonzero logarithmic correction \((5+b)\log n/[2(1+b)^3]\). The transverse-coordinate amplitude \(\sqrt n\,|y_n|\to1/\sqrt2\) is universal across the entire stable multiplier interval, while the invariant axis relaxes exponentially.
