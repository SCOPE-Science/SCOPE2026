# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof reconstructs every source/destination migration flux in the six-state mean-field system, derives the conserved quantities and uniquely normalized fugacity equilibrium, and checks reversibility pair by pair. Entropy dissipation has a unique interior zero. Uniformly positive birth/death transitions rule out boundary omega limits; reaction stoichiometry supplies a positive constrained entropy gap and exponential convergence. The exponential-family Schur complement proves strict mixing-response monotonicity. Exact rational reproducibility checks are documented, but do not replace the infinite-time argument.

## Originality — PASS, with stated residual risks
Cao and Cortez, arXiv:2604.24998v1, analyze the 2026 destination-agnostic dynamics and establish the two endpoint regimes at two houses. Their simultaneous positive baseline-and-intolerance two-house regime is not solved in the inspected text. Durrett and Zhang, DOI:10.1073/pnas.1414915111, already establish the related two-house detailed-balance stationary identities for a distinct happiness-driven, destination-dependent process; these identical static identities are expressly not claimed new. The exact novelty claim is the global all-initial-condition kinetic result with exponential rate and strict parameter response for the 2026 ODE. Standard reversible-reaction entropy concepts are predecessors. The 2014 supplement was not separately inspected, and broader general kinetic theory may subsume some method; independent priority checks remain warranted.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result gives a natural complete classification of the missing positive-parameter regime for two-house destination-blind segregation: it identifies a unique asymptotically selected distribution, rules out dynamical bistability, and proves monotone suppression of mixed neighborhoods even with unequal population densities. The domain restriction is mathematically substantive: the reversibility calculation uses at most one opposite-color housemate. This is a deterministic mean-field result, not a finite-population spatial theorem.
