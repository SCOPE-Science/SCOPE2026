The singular components of the normal-fan toric variety of a cycle graphical zonotope are indexed by balanced signed four-subsets, and every nonempty reduced intersection is a product of projective lines determined exactly by sign compatibility. Their intersection nerve is a flag complex, nonpure for \(n\ge6\); for \(n=5\), the 30 singular lines have the Desargues graph as their point-line incidence graph.
