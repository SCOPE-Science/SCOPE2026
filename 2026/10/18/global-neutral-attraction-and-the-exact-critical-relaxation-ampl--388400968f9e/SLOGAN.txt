For a nonlinear two-community polarization model, neutral consensus attracts every physical initial state exactly when \(\kappa+\beta[\tau+(1-\tau)(1-\rho+\rho\alpha)]\le1\). At the sharp critical boundary, every non-antisymmetric trajectory relaxes with the explicit signed \(t^{-1/2}\) amplitude \(\sqrt{3/[2\ell(1+\beta(1-\tau)\rho\alpha^3)]}\), while between-community disagreement decays exponentially.
