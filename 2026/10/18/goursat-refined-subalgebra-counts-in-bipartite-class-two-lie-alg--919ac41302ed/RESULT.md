# Goursat-refined subalgebra counts in bipartite class-two Lie algebras over finite fields

## Finding
For each prime power \(q\), integers \(a,b\ge 1\), and finite-dimensional Lie algebra \(L=L_{a,b}(q)\) defined below, the number of \(\mathbb F_q\)-Lie subalgebras with any prescribed four-way Goursat profile has the exact formula
\[
N_{i,j,t,e}(q)=
{a\brack i}_q{b\brack j}_q{i\brack t}_q{j\brack t}_q
|\operatorname{GL}_t(\mathbb F_q)|
{d\brack e}_q q^{(i+j-t)(d-e)},\qquad
 d=ab-ij+\tfrac{t(t+1)}2.
\]
The admissible indices and the dimension associated with each stratum are explicit below. In particular this determines the complete **subalgebra**, not ideal, zeta polynomial and the incidence profile of the two natural abelian summands.

## Assumptions and scope
Let \(U=\mathbb F_q^a\), \(V=\mathbb F_q^b\), \(Z=U\otimes_{\mathbb F_q}V\). Set \(L=U\oplus V\oplus Z\) and extend bilinearly the brackets \([u,v]=u\otimes v\), \([v,u]=-u\otimes v\), while \([U,U]=[V,V]=[L,Z]=0\). This is a two-step nilpotent Lie algebra in every characteristic; in characteristic two it is still alternating because \([x,x]=0\).

For every \(\mathbb F_q\)-linear Lie subalgebra \(H\le L\), let \(S=(H+Z)/Z\le U\oplus V\) and \(T=H\cap Z\). Define the *canonical* profile
\[
i=\dim\pi_U(S),\qquad j=\dim\pi_V(S),\qquad
t=i+j-\dim S,\qquad
e=\dim T-\rho,
\quad \rho=ij-\frac{t(t+1)}2.
\]
Then \(0\le i\le a\), \(0\le j\le b\), \(0\le t\le\min(i,j)\), and \(0\le e\le d=ab-\rho\). All such integer quadruples are attainable. Let \({m\brack k}_q\) be the Gaussian binomial coefficient, with \({0\brack0}_q=1\), and \(|\operatorname{GL}_0(\mathbb F_q)|=1\); for \(t>0\), \(|\operatorname{GL}_t(\mathbb F_q)|=\prod_{h=0}^{t-1}(q^t-q^h)\). There are no additional characteristic, parity or genericity hypotheses.

The dimension of a subalgebra in this stratum is
\[
\dim H=(i+j-t)+\rho+e.
\]
Writing \(n=a+b+ab\), the complete dimension polynomial is
\[
P_{a,b,q}(z)=\sum_{i=0}^a\sum_{j=0}^b\sum_{t=0}^{\min(i,j)}\sum_{e=0}^{ab-ij+t(t+1)/2}
N_{i,j,t,e}(q)z^{i+j-t+ij-t(t+1)/2+e}.
\]
The codimension (subalgebra-index) polynomial is \(z^n P_{a,b,q}(z^{-1})\).

## Proof
**1. Goursat classification of projections.** Fix \(A=\pi_U S\le U\), \(B=\pi_V S\le V\), of dimensions \(i,j\). Define \(A_0=S\cap U\) and \(B_0=S\cap V\), identifying each with its coordinate copy. The two projection sequences give \(\dim A_0=i-t\), \(\dim B_0=j-t\) and a canonically induced isomorphism \(A/A_0\cong B/B_0\) of \(t\)-spaces. Conversely such data uniquely determine \(S\). Choose \(A,B,A_0,B_0\) and this isomorphism in succession. Their numbers multiply to
\[
{a\brack i}_q{b\brack j}_q{i\brack t}_q{j\brack t}_q|\operatorname{GL}_t(\mathbb F_q)|.
\]
There is no overcount: the four subspaces and quotient isomorphism are recovered from \(S\) itself.

**2. Commutator-image rank.** Split \(A=A_0\oplus X\), \(B=B_0\oplus Y\) and choose bases \(x_1,\ldots,x_t\) and \(y_1,\ldots,y_t\) so that \(S\) is generated by \(A_0\), \(B_0\), and the \(t\) paired vectors \((x_h,y_h)\). Its bracket image is the direct sum of the tensor pieces
\[
A_0\otimes B_0,\quad A_0\otimes Y,\quad X\otimes B_0,
\quad\text{and}\quad
\operatorname{span}\{x_h\otimes y_k-x_k\otimes y_h:1\le h<k\le t\}.
\]
Their dimensions are respectively \((i-t)(j-t)\), \((i-t)t\), \(t(j-t)\), \(\binom t2\). The last spanning family is independent: each unordered pair has an exclusive off-diagonal tensor coordinate, in particular also in characteristic two. Thus
\[
\dim[S,S]=(i-t)(j-t)+(i-t)t+t(j-t)+\binom t2
=ij-\binom{t+1}2=\rho.
\]
The notation \([S,S]\) means the image in \(Z\) of the bracket on arbitrary representatives in \(L\), which is well-defined because \(Z\) is central.

**3. Central slice and lifts.** Closure of \(H\) under its Lie bracket is equivalent to \([S,S]\subseteq T\). Set \(d=\dim Z-\rho=ab-\rho\). For any \(e\) between zero and \(d\), the number of central slices \(T\) with \([S,S]\subseteq T\subseteq Z\) and \(\dim T=\rho+e\) equals \({d\brack e}_q\). For any fixed \(S,T\), the subspaces \(H\) projecting onto \(S\) with kernel \(T\) are in bijection with linear maps \(S\to Z/T\): modulo \(T\), each is exactly the graph of such a map. Hence there are \(q^{(i+j-t)(d-e)}\) lifts. Each is automatically a Lie subalgebra because all commutators already lie in \(T\). Multiplication of the three independent counts gives the displayed formula. Every \(H\) has unique data \(S,T\), so the enumeration is exhaustive.

**4. Index extraction and endpoints.** Since \(\dim S=i+j-t\) and \(\dim T=\rho+e\), the dimension formula follows. Summing the strata according to \(\dim H\) gives the full polynomial. For \(S=0\), the formula correctly counts all central subspaces. For \(S=U\oplus V\), the commutator image is all \(Z\), forcing \(H=L\), independent of the field. Nothing in the argument assumes \(q\) prime rather than a prime power.

## Verification
The standalone script `verification/verify.py` has no third-party dependencies. Run with `python3 verification/verify.py`. It exhaustively generates reduced-row-echelon subspaces of the actual \(a+b+ab\)-dimensional Lie algebra, checks their closure under *explicit* Lie brackets, and determines each observed four-way stratum from matrix ranks; it independently compares these counts with the closed formula. All eight tested configurations passed: \((a,b,q)=(1,1,2),(1,1,3),(1,1,4),(1,1,5),(1,2,2),(1,2,3),(2,1,2),(2,2,2)\). The \(q=4\) test uses arithmetic in \(\mathbb F_2[X]/(X^2+X+1)\), not prime-field arithmetic.

For \(a=b=2\), \(q=2\), the numbers of subalgebras in dimensions \(0,1,\ldots,8\) are exactly \(1,255,2347,6147,5267,1443,251,15,1\), totaling \(15727\). All fifty-five nonzero strata in that case were checked. For \(a=1,b=2,q=3\), the total is \(531\). The exhaustive finite tests are consistency checks; the general assertion rests on the proof above.

## Relationship to prior work
This family is the base change of the free class-two nilpotent product of two abelian Lie rings \(\mathfrak g_{a,b}\), as in Carnevale–Schein–Voll [1, §5.3]. That paper establishes strong **ideal** zeta formulas, including the same family, not the present subalgebra-refined enumeration. The conditions differ: for an ideal the central slice must contain \([S,L]=\pi_U S\otimes V+U\otimes\pi_V S\), while for a subalgebra it need only contain \([S,S]\), whose dimension depends on the coupling rank \(t\). One cannot recover the four-way subalgebra profile by substituting parameters in that ideal formula.

Lee [2, §§2–3] develops finite-field subalgebra and ideal zeta methods; in particular the \(a=b=1\) Heisenberg subalgebra-count polynomial is classical and **is not claimed as new**. Lee's Grenham family treatment in §3.2.1 enumerates **ideals**, not this full subalgebra distribution. Bauer–Schein [3] likewise study **ideal** growth of related class-two families. The present contribution is the all-parameter Goursat/commutator-image refinement, valid for every finite field, together with its complete subalgebra dimension polynomial.

## Limitations
The claim counts \(\mathbb F_q\)-linear Lie subalgebras of the specified split algebra only. It does not enumerate arbitrary additive subgroups over extension fields or finite-index subalgebras over a complete discrete valuation ring; the latter have additional lattice-extension data. It does not distinguish subalgebras up to automorphism/isomorphism. While the cited primary treatments and targeted database searches did not supply this four-way formula, equivalent results in older subring-zeta or unindexed finite-geometric literature remain a priority risk. No independent mathematical audit has been performed.
