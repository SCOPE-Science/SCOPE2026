For the free class-two nilpotent product of two abelian Lie algebras over any finite field, the exact number of subalgebras is determined at every pair of projection ranks, Goursat coupling rank, and residual central dimension. The coupling rank lowers the forced commutator dimension from \(ij\) to \(ij-\binom{t+1}{2}\), producing a complete closed four-parameter subalgebra profile distinct from the known ideal zeta counts.
