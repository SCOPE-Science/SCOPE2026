For a nearly orthogonal parallelotope whose Gram perturbation is a positively weighted graph adjacency matrix, the rescaled probability of nearly maximal two-point squared distance is an explicit \(2d\)-degree spline with knots at four times the weighted graph cuts. Its atomic high derivative recovers the complete cut-weight multiset, and the fixed-deformation diameter-tail coefficient has a noncommuting cube limit with exact multiplicity ratio \(2^{c-d}\).
