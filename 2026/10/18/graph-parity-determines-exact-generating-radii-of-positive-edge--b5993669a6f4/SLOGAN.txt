For real positive edge-averaging maps \(\ell_\infty(V)\to\ell_\infty(E)\), non-bipartite graphs have generating radius \(1\), while bipartite graphs have the exact radius \(1-2/k\), where \(k\) is the number of non-isolated vertices. Every strictly internal choice of edge weights yields the same result, and an explicit norm-attainment support function explains the parity transition.
