# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The stationary two-particle weights are reconstructed by direct balance, including the doubled escape rate from a shared site. The reversible Poisson variational formula is normalized as \(V=\lim_{T\to\infty}\operatorname{Var}(H_T)/(2T)\). Rescaling its test function separates the fast vertex–edge incidence transitions from the slow nonadjacent-pair transitions and leaves the killed-walk Green coefficient. Finite-dimensional coercivity and matching upper/lower variational limits justify taking the singular limit. The complete-graph lumping, cycle hitting-time recurrence and derivative sign were checked algebraically. A standalone exact-rational checker passed 42 finite generator and singular-limit comparisons. Numerical and finite rational checks are not the proof of the infinite family.

## Originality — PASS with residual risk

Compared statements and implications against Giardinà–Redig–Vafayi (arXiv:0906.4664), Grosskinsky–Redig–Vafayi (arXiv:1210.3827), Bianchi–Dommers–Giardinà (arXiv:1605.05140), Carinci–Giardinà–Redig (arXiv:1711.11283), and Kim–Sau (arXiv:2412.01489). These works provide the generator, reversible law, condensation dynamics, infinite-line two-particle transition transforms, and finite-graph spectral-gap analysis. None of the inspected statements identifies the all-graph stationary integrated-collision Green coefficient or the noncomplete-graph criterion. The infinite-line transform may cover part of the periodic-cycle calculation after reduction; it does not give the general graph theorem. Exact duplicate claims were not located in previously recorded scientific claims or the published research-index searches. Search absence is not a proof of novelty, and uninspected historical calculations remain a risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result separates static clustering, which becomes deterministic in the low-diffusion regime independently of graph edges, from temporal clustering noise, which has a nonzero Green limit precisely when a nonadjacent pair exists. It gives the first-order graph-dependent noise coefficient in natural finite hitting-time terms. The exact cycle response additionally identifies the exceptional triangle maximum and strict higher-cycle monotonicity; these are structural, independently interpretable limits for stochastic transport.

## Limits and evidence

The model has two particles, homogeneous unit edge conductances, a fixed finite connected simple undirected graph, and stationary initial data. First take long time, then small diffusivity. There is no extension claim for larger particle count or infinite volume. The program verifies finite examples and is not an external proof certification. Independent scientific and historical bibliographic validation remain outstanding.
