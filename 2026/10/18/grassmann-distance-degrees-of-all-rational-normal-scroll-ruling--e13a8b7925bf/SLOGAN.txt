For every rational normal scroll \(S(n_1,\ldots,n_d)\) with \(d\ge2\) and all \(n_i\ge2\), the ruling curve has Grassmann distance degree exactly \(2\deg\operatorname{lcm}_i(1+t^2+\cdots+t^{2n_i})-2\), even when optimization data are constrained to rank-\(d\) orthogonal projectors. Thus its constrained GD degree equals the previously known unconstrained ED degree throughout this infinite family.
