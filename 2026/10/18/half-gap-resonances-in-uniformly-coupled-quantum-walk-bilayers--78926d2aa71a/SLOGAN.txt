For two identical real-symmetric quantum-walk layers coupled uniformly with strength \(g\), every extra average-mixing direction is selected by eigenvalue pairs separated by exactly \(2g\); the full rank is the single-layer rank plus the rank of a positive-semidefinite gap matrix. For two coupled complete graphs \(K_m\), \(m\ge3\), the rank is \(2m\) when disconnected, \(2m-1\) at \(g=m/2\), and \(m\) at every other positive coupling.
