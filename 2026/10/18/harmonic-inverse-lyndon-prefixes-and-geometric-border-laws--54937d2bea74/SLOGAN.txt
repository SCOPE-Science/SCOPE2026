For a uniform random word over a fixed \(q\)-symbol alphabet, the expected total length of its inverse Lyndon array is \(\frac{q}{q-1}n\log n+O_q(n)\), with an exact finite-length formula. Among uniformly selected length-\(k\) inverse Lyndon words, the longest-border length has an exact primitive-necklace distribution converging to a geometric law of mean \(1/(q-1)\).
