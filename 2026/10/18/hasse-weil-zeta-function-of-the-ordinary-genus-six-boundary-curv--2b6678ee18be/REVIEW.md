# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The base curve is geometrically connected and smooth after projective normalization because four distinct simple poles forbid an Artin–Schreier coboundary; Riemann–Hurwitz gives genus six. The exact six extension point counts are supported by independently implemented direct-fibre enumeration and absolute-trace histograms, with irreducibility tests for every finite-field polynomial. The Hasse–Weil functional equation and Newton identities prove that those finite data determine all twelve zeta coefficients. The factorization and quartic irreducibility are established by elementary integral-polynomial checks; the isogeny claims follow from the standard finite-field isogeny theorem. The attached checker was executed successfully from its final package.

## Originality — PASS, with identified residual risks

The originating preprint arXiv:2610.05539v1, Propositions 8.7–8.8, proves the geometry, p-rank and automorphism group of the exact family but not its zeta or Jacobian factorization. The closest full-text zeta theorem (Bouw et al., arXiv:1410.7031v2, Section 0.8) applies to supersingular polynomial one-pole covers, not this ordinary four-pole curve. Lauder–Wan's general method is an algorithm rather than an already evaluated same-curve identity, and its simple-pole correctness proof is restricted to characteristic at least five. The available finite-field genus-six census covers the characteristic-two base field, not this curve. Searches for the exact equation, its aliases, numerical polynomial, group, and broader arithmetic coverage found no matching or dominating evaluated statement. Non-indexed calculations or an unlocated table that identifies an equivalent curve remain possible; abstract-level access to Lauder–Wan is a documented limit.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The example is the natural characteristic-three genus-six equality case with an unusually large (order-72) geometric automorphism group, singled out by a recent classification. Its exact prime-field zeta function and square Jacobian isogeny decomposition connect that geometry to arithmetic and yield all extension counts and an exact Jacobian group order. The square factorization and the vanishing trace at all degrees coprime to three are structural features, not an arbitrary finite numerical slice.

## Scope and limitations

The verified theorem concerns the specified smooth projective model over \(\mathbb F_3\) and does not classify parameter twists or previously known automorphisms. The arithmetical computation is self-contained but an equivalent result in inaccessible or unindexed literature cannot be categorically excluded.
