The characteristic-three ordinary genus-six Artin–Schreier curve \(y^3-y=x+1/(x^3-x)\) has exact zeta numerator \(1+16T^3+118T^6+432T^9+729T^{12}=(1+8T^3+27T^6)^2\), a square of a polynomial in \(T^3\) with explicit elliptic and simple surface isogeny factors. Its Jacobian has \(1296\) rational points over \(\mathbb F_3\), and every extension degree prime to three has exactly \(3^n+1\) curve points.
