At the excluded \(b=4\) endpoint of a classical rational planar folding model, typical vertical trajectories acquire an explicit \(R^{-1/2}\) stationary burst tail and infinite moments of order at least \(1/2\), whereas every \(b<4\) orbit in the natural wedge has a uniform vertical bound from its second iterate. After conditioning on a burst above \(R\), the entire return-time law to the critical half-interval is exactly \(\Pr(\tau>n)=\min\{1,q_R2^{-n}\}\), revealing a logarithmic delay with nonconvergent dyadic phase corrections.
