Every finite forest order is identified among finite posets by two-variable counting logic of quantifier rank at most \(\max\{2,h+1\}\), where \(h\) is its height, and refinement also recovers its automorphism orbits. This promise matters sharply: two disjoint three-element chains have a nontransitive acyclic \(C^2\)-equivalent six-vertex companion, while no forest order on fewer vertices does.
