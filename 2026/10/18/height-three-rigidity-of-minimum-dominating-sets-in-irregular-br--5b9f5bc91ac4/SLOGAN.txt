In every finite rooted tree whose leaves have a common depth and whose internal vertices have at least two children, the **entire family** of minimum dominating sets is forced onto every third level: there is a unique optimizer except at positive heights divisible by three, when the only choice is the root or one of its children. Thus \(\zeta(T)=1+\deg_T(r)\) at those heights and \(\zeta(T)=1\) otherwise, regardless of all lower branching irregularities.
