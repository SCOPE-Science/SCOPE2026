For a reversible quadratic flow with nonzero cylinder cofactor, a hidden nonpolynomial pair of first integrals fills an entire neighborhood of the invariant cylinder with periodic trajectories. Their exact original-time period shear is \(T'(0)=\pi cI_1(c/a)/(2a^2)>0\) whenever \(c\ne0\), despite every periodic orbit having only unit Floquet multipliers.
