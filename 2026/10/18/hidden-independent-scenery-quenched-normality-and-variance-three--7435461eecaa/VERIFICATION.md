# Verification and proof limits

`checker.py` executes with standard Python 3.11 or later and no external packages. It checks the exact binary Pascal transform on all coefficient words up to ten bits; computes the expectation and variance of both the all-scenery occupation sum and actual predictable covariance sum by independent enumeration of all fair coefficient vectors and sign paths for up to seven steps; and checks that the increment-recycling map is bijective for every fixed environment of up to seven steps. The completed program reports **302 exact assertions passed**.

The general statements instead follow analytically from (i) invertibility of every finite unit-triangular Pascal matrix over \(\mathbb F_2\), (ii) independent geometric renewal blocks and the strong law, (iii) Fubini's theorem, (iv) a bounded-increment martingale functional CLT, and (v) the iid block functional CLT plus random time change and negligible geometric overshoots.

No exhaustive finite test establishes a general almost-sure or functional limit theorem. No claim of independent audit or formal external certification is made. The quenched result is limited to fair independent frozen cardinality coefficients, while the variance-three cross-variation functional CLT is annealed.
