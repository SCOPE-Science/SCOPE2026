For ideal high-order photon subtraction from a squeezed thermal mode, the central Wigner parity has two competing exponential branches, and the thermal transition at \(\nu=\sinh^2r\) develops a universal even/odd hyperbolic crossover within an \(O(1/m)\) population window. At any fixed positive thermal population in the stated regime, its origin contrast decays exponentially as the subtraction order \(m\) grows, even while odd-order negativity remains exact on one side of the threshold.
