For every fixed polynomial degree \(k\ge3\), the sharp \(L^1\) and \(L^\infty\) norms of total-degree orthogonal projection under an arbitrary compactly supported product law grow like \(d^{k/2}(B/\sqrt v)^k\mathbb E|H_k(Z)|/k!\). Asymptotically sharp evaluation points are exactly those whose coordinatewise mean squared distance from the marginal mean approaches the square of the farthest support radius; the oscillatory profile is universally Hermite even for asymmetric atomic laws.
