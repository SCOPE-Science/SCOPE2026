For every \(r\ge3\), the higher-independence complex of an arbitrary clique bouquet is an explicit wedge of spheres only in dimensions \(r-1\) and \(nr-1\), with multiplicities \(\binom{N-1}{r-1}\) and \(\prod_i\binom{m_i-1}{r}\). Its integral top homology symmetries are exterior powers of augmentation lattices, with a parity sign under blade exchanges.
