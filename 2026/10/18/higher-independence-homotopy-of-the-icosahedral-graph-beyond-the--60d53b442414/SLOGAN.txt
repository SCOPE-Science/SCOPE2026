For the icosahedral graph \(I\), the higher-independence complexes at thresholds \(r=3,4,5,6\) are integrally homotopy equivalent to wedges of \(10\) three-spheres, \(70\) three-spheres, \(350\) four-spheres and \(450\) five-spheres, respectively. Every later stage has an exact simplex-skeleton formula for \(7\leq r\leq11\) and becomes contractible for \(r\geq12\).
