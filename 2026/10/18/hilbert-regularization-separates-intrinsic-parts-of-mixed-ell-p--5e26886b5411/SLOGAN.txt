For real \(\mathbb R^n\), the intrinsic norm-cone parts of \(N_{p,a}=\|\cdot\|_p+a\|\cdot\|_2\) have a sharp transition at \(p=2\): every positive Euclidean admixture merges the super-Hilbert exponents into a single part, whereas sub-Hilbert exponents stay distinct. Approaching an unregularized super-Hilbert norm from within that part costs intrinsic Hilbert distance asymptotic to \(\log(1/a)\), despite convergence in ordinary norm distortion.
