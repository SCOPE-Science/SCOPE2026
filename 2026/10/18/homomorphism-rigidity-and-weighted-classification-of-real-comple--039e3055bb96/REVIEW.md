# Review: homomorphism rigidity for weighted complete-multipartite evolution algebras

## Correctness — PASS

The proof reduces zero products to blockwise Euclidean orthogonality, uses positivity and graph connectivity to prove nonzero images for all basis vectors, and exploits equality of dimensions to show every column has support in exactly one target part and that the map is invertible. The square algebra identifies the number of parts; coincident basis squares identify parts, orthogonality identifies isometries fixing the all-ones vector, and explicit scalar equations uniquely determine weights. Sufficiency is checked against every natural-basis product. The finite checker independently covers four rational examples; it does not substitute for the universal argument. Risk: the proof is presently self-reviewed only.

## Originality — PASS within inspected literature, with residual risk

The direct closest paper, Cadavid–Rodiño Montoya–Rodríguez, arXiv:1710.10516, states the singular-graph dichotomy as Conjecture 1, proves the nonsingular case, and gives a monomial necessity condition that does not entail this complete non-monomial weighted classification. The later singular-graph paper arXiv:2405.12341 studies other singular graph constructions and does not present this all-parameter theorem. The graph-evolution automorphism paper arXiv:2505.15604 also does not supply the asserted arbitrary homomorphism classification. Targeted searches examined equivalent weighted descriptions, dual/reversed directions, broader rigidity, and direct exact graph cases. Residual risks are differently named twin-class classifications and unindexed or unpublished literature; search failure is not proof of novelty.

## Value — PASS

The claimed result gives a complete natural morphism classification for an infinite family of finite-dimensional algebras and proves the zero-or-isomorphism conclusion of a specifically published conjecture on an infinite set of singular graphs. Nonmonomial orthogonal automorphisms appear as necessary features of the classification, rather than an arbitrary numerical refinement. The positive-real and equal-dimension restrictions are scientifically substantive.

## Scope and limitations

No claim is made over complex fields, zero/mixed-sign weights or unequal dimensions. No claim is made to solve the 2017 conjecture for arbitrary graphs. Neither independent proof checking nor independent literature audit has been performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
