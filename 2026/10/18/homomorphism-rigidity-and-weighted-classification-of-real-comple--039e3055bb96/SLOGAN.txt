Every nonzero homomorphism between equally dimensional, positive-weighted real complete-multipartite evolution algebras is an isomorphism, with all isomorphisms given by part permutations, fixed-all-ones orthogonal block matrices, and explicit positive scalings. Consequently a complete multipartite graph's adjacency and random-walk evolution algebras admit a nonzero homomorphism exactly when the graph is regular or biregular, including the singular cases.
