For every simple graph of girth at least five on \(n\ge9\) vertices, its ordinary four-cut complex is a wedge of \((n-5)\)-spheres. The pentagons' previously known Euler correction lifts to a full integral homotopy classification, without requiring the pentagons to be edge-disjoint; for the dodecahedron the complex is \(\bigvee^{841}S^{15}\).
