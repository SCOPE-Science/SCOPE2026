# Review of the rooted-tree retraction rank theorem

**Correctness — PASS.** The fixed-child argument establishes the full-height image condition in both directions, and the independent choices at each retained or omitted branch yield the exact rooted-map and retraction multiplicities. Induction on retained child counts proves that every total-vertex image rank between \(h+1\) and \(N\) is attained. The near-minimum coefficient uses the fact that two retained deepest-level leaves sharing all earlier prefixes permit arbitrary independent choices for every other source leaf. Full-map enumeration for five small parameter pairs checks all coefficients against a separately implemented composition test. Finite checks do not replace the general proof.

**Originality — PASS within examined coverage.** The full text of Ahmed–Savchuk's 2017 article concerns polynomial-induced rooted-tree endomorphisms and automorphisms, not the full finite-tree retraction rank distribution. Al-Aadhami's thesis Section 4.3.2 **already supplies** arbitrary wreath-product unweighted idempotent counts, so those totals alone are not novel. Its inspected theorem does not provide each prescribed prefix-tree image's retraction multiplicity, full vertex-rank polynomial, gap-free spectrum, or near-minimal coefficient. These are the assessed additions. Published close terminology on graph-tree regularity was checked at abstract level only; a hidden equivalent rank-refinement remains a risk.

**Value — PASS.** A rank spectrum of idempotents records the sizes of all compatible finite quotients/retracts and refines the count obtainable from a general wreath-product identity. The exact per-image multiplicities and the rank gap-free theorem give structural information that does not follow just by knowing the scalar number of idempotents. Near-minimal fibres grow as \(2^{b^h-2}\), in contrast with the unique map onto each single-leaf image; that sharp extremal difference is motivated by depth-preserving retraction geometry.

**Limits.** Labels, root and levels are fixed, height is finite, and the formula is an explicit finite recurrence. The literature search cannot establish absolute novelty, and one plausible related graph-endomorphism article was not read in full. Independent mathematical audit has not been performed.
