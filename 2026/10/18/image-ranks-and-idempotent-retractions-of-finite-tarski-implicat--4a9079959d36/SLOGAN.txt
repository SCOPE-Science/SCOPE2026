For the Tarski implication algebra of nonempty subsets of an \(n\)-set, every noninvertible endomorphism has a Boolean-cube image of rank \(2^r\), whereas invertible endomorphisms have rank \(2^n-1\). The complete rank distribution and idempotent-retraction distribution have explicit Stirling-number and binomial-power formulas for all \(n\ge2\).
