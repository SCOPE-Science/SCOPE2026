For the standard star-graph simplex sums, a positive edge-versus-pair weight ratio passes through a sharp Euclidean inball-contact transition at equal weights, although the normal fan is unchanged. In the unweighted graph-polytope interpolation, the contacting-facet count drops from \(2k+1\) to \(k+1\) between the fan-identical stages \(m=2\) and \(m=3\), while every radius and incenter is explicit.
