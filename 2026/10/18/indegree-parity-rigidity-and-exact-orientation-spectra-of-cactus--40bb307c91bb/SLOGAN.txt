A connected simple graph is a cactus exactly when indegree parity alone determines whether *all* realizing orientations are acyclic or *none* are. For a cactus with \(b\) bridges and cycles of lengths \(\ell_i\), the exact polynomial counting parity signatures by their forced number of directed cycles is \(2^b\prod_i(2^{\ell_i-1}-1+z)\).
