# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The characteristic function follows from the stationary perpetuity of independent centered Laplace increments. Independent Bernoulli/Laplace mixtures give an exact factor by factor ratio, and their variances sum. The explicit Lévy kernel is positive and valid by a summable quadratic-jump bound. Consistent independent increments give the martingale. Symmetric unimodality plus nondegenerate full-support noise establishes strict peakedness at every threshold. Every step is a universal argument; `checker.py` independently verifies finite identities with exact rational arithmetic and tests the Fourier integral numerically. The computation does not substitute for the infinite proof.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Reset strength is a natural physical parameter; a monotone variance does not entail strict tail monotonicity at all thresholds or an independent-noise ordering. The explicit parameter-increment law and Lévy measure give a meaningful, falsifiable stochastic comparison across the full parameter interval and an auxiliary independent-increment martingale representation. The result does not generalize to drifted or finite-time laws as stated.
