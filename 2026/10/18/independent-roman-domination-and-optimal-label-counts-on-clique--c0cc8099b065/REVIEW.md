# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof first forces exactly one positive vertex per clique fiber, then establishes the necessary and sufficient constraints on its color and weight. In a label-one fiber, two other vertices must be defended by two distinct adjacent label-two fibers, yielding the sharp \(q=3\) threshold. The weight identity \(2n-|S|\) makes the independence bound sharp and the two cyclic coloring bijections enumerate every minimizer. The cases \(n=3\) and \(n=4\) are explicitly handled rather than relying on non-simple cycles of lengths one or two. The two included exhaustive verification schemes agree with the formulas on twenty specified finite parameter pairs; finite checks are corroboration only.

## Originality — PASS, with residual bibliographic risk
Semantic searches of mathematical records and source/alias/parameter searches found no exact theorem that classifies the optimal independent Roman functions of \(K_q\square C_n\) and gives their closed counts. The closest accessible published-finding corpus study gives an independent Roman weight enumerator for complete multipartite graphs, not clique–cycle Cartesian products. Other near hits concern different variants, graph families, or numerical rather than enumerative questions. The full paper of Cabrera Martínez and Hernández Mira, which claims numerical results on unspecified product graphs, could not be accessed; its abstract alone cannot justify a whole-paper noncoverage conclusion. The originality assessment is based on the accessible evidence, not on database uniqueness.

## Value — PASS
The object is a classical Cartesian product and the invariant is standard independent Roman domination. The result identifies the exact qualitative boundary where label-one fibers become impossible, provides an all-parameter structural characterization and exact optimizer count, and relates optimal Roman labelings to proper cycle colorings. This is finer information than just a minimum weight and is natural for understanding multiplicity and rigidity of optimal defenses.

## Remaining scientific limitations
This concerns only clique size at least three and labeled optimum assignments; neither the two-row case nor an all-weight enumerator is claimed. A potentially relevant primary source remains accessible only by abstract and unindexed equivalents may exist. The mathematical verification is not independent review.
