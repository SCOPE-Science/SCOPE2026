In the clique–cycle graphs \(K_q\square C_n\), minimum independent Roman defenses exhibit a sharp change at \(q=3\): their weights are \(\lceil3n/2\rceil\) for three-row cliques and \(2n\) for all larger cliques. An exact coloring bijection classifies and counts every optimum, with cyclic three-coloring formulas in the exceptional case and the ordinary chromatic polynomial of a cycle thereafter.
