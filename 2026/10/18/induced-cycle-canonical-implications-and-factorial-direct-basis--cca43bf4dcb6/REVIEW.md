# Scientific assessment

**Correctness: PASS.** Each induced rooted cycle path has exactly one missing closure edge and all proper subsets are closed. An induction splitting chorded cycles shows induced-cycle implications generate the entire graphic-matroid closure. The recursive pseudo-intent condition then excludes every other nonclosed set. A closed-strict-subset argument forces every rooted induced-cycle implication in any basis; a one-pass argument forces every rooted simple-cycle implication in a direct-unit basis. Elementary cycle counts yield the cubic and factorial formulas. Independently implemented closure and exhaustive pseudo-intent enumerations agree for every graph through five vertices and seeded cases of order six. Finite evidence is supplementary, not proof of all cases.

**Originality: PASS, qualified.** Kashiwabara and Nakamura (2013) already identify chordless-cycle paths as the prime stems in graphic matroids and prove prime stems are pseudo-closed; their general optimal-basis theory and these ingredients are *not* claimed as new. The precise exhaustive graphic pseudo-intent converse, minimum-basis compulsion/uniqueness, characterization of directness, and the factorial/cubic complete-graph contrast are not directly stated in the inspected sections of that paper. Baixeries--Napoli (2024) develop general minimal/direct basis theory without these graph-specific classifications. Research-record searches for the exact formulations found no equivalent coverage. These statements may be rediscovered as consequences of earlier related work; this is a real residual priority risk, not a uniqueness certificate.

**Value: PASS.** Exact irredundant canonical premises for a natural structured closure system determine representational size, optimality and one-pass computational tradeoffs. The complete-graph family exhibits a sharp factorial/direct versus cubic/canonical size separation, and the directness characterization gives an exact graph-theoretic boundary. It is not an arbitrary isolated numerical increment.

**Scope and limitations.** Graphic edge closure only; directness is one simultaneous rule scan; no guarantee about the number of iterations for arbitrary alternative bases or total-literal minimum. A sound proof does not establish publication priority. Independent external audit is not performed.
