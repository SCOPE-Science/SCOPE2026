For every \(1/2<p<1\), the stationary leading binary digit of the Pelikan random interval map has full binary symbolic support, yet **no finite-state hidden Markov model can generate its exact law**. Its return-gap generating function is a nonrational quadratic algebraic function, with a three-regime tail transition at \(p=4/5\) that separates square-root decay, a critical collision, and pole-dominated decay.
