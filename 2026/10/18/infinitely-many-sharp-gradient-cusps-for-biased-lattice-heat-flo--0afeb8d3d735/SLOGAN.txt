For asymmetric nearest-neighbor heat flow on the integer lattice, the sharp bounded-input-to-edge-gradient norm is exactly twice the maximum Skellam heat-kernel mass, and nonzero drift forces infinitely many upward derivative cusps as its maximizing lattice site migrates. The \(k\)-th cusp occurs at \(t_k=(k+1/2)/|a-b|+1/[2(a+b)]+O(k^{-1})\) for fixed positive unequal jump rates, whereas the zero-drift norm is analytic.
