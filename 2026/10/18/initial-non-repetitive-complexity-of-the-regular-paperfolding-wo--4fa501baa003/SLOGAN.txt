In the regular paperfolding word, the first repeated length-\(n\) contiguous factor begins exactly at \(3\cdot2^{\lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor}\) for every \(n\ge8\), and it repeats the factor beginning at \(2^{\lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor}\). This gives an all-length exact formula and sharp normalized oscillation for initial non-repetitive complexity.
