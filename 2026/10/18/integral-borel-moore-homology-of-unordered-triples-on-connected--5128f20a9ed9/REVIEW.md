# Scientific review: integral Borel–Moore homology of unordered triples

## Correctness — PASS
The exact scope is the degree-two and degree-three **integral Borel–Moore homology** of the unordered configuration of exactly three distinct points on any finite connected graph. Compactification identifies the chain groups with the relative third symmetric product and its fat diagonal. Pair homotopy invariance reduces the graph to a rose of cycle rank \(b\). The unordered rose has only two relative chain degrees, indexed by weak compositions of three and two. Signed endpoint incidence gives columns either zero or precisely \(\pm2\) times one mixed lower generator; every unordered pair of distinct petals has two such source columns. Integer column operations produce \(\binom b2\) diagonal entries of magnitude two, so both the free ranks and the exact torsion groups follow without finite extrapolation. The standalone exact checker passed for \(1\le b\le15\), including independent deletion orientations, modular ranks, and the published two-particle normalization.

## Originality — PASS, with stated limitations
Khan, arXiv:2608.18143v1 (Theorem 3.4 and §4), supplies the closely motivated **two**-point integral Borel–Moore classification and specifically leaves larger particle counts as a future direction. Bibby, Chan, Gadish, and Yun, arXiv:2109.03302v6 (Proposition 2.1; Lemmas 2.4, 2.6), supply a stronger **general construction**: an integral equivariant two-term cellular model for ordered compactified configurations at arbitrary particle count. However their theorem does **not** give the integral quotient Smith invariants for three unordered particles; the exact closed forms and the degree-two two-torsion require the additional explicit analysis. Gadish and Hainaut, DOI:10.5802/ahl.213, calculate rational compact-support cohomology and representation factors on wedges of spheres; that rational theory cannot entail the integral order-two summands. Accessible related results involving three particles compute ordinary singular homology, not this Borel–Moore invariant; a two-particle exchange-sign local-system result also does not compute the three-point constant-coefficient case. Searches against equivalent terminology, more general methods, exact tables, and prior claims identified no previously stated equal or dominating integral three-particle formula. A general computational presentation is acknowledged as the main foundation, not hidden. Residual risk: additional unpublished, unindexed, or uninspected formulae might contain this particular Smith-form evaluation.

## Value — PASS
The natural next case after a 2026 complete two-particle theorem is a complete integral three-particle classification valid for **all** finite connected graphs. The precise nontrivial finding is \(\binom b2\) independent order-two classes in **degree two** for every cycle rank \(b\ge2\), which rational computations miss. The formula, its sharp torsion threshold, and explicit integral generators are useful exact topological invariants and a new structural boundary for higher-particle Borel–Moore calculations. The unrestricted graph family and a complete invariant distinguish this from an arbitrary small finite census.

## Verification and remaining limits
The proof determines the claimed integral groups, not the ordinary homology, homotopy type, four-point analogue, or the full graph-automorphism action. The independent check has not occurred, and unindexed literature is a residual originality risk; none is represented as verified independence or mathematical certification.
