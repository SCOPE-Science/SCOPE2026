For every finite connected graph with cycle rank \(b\), the unordered three-point configuration space has integral Borel–Moore homology \(H_3^{\mathrm{BM}}\cong\mathbb Z^{b(b^2+5)/6}\) and \(H_2^{\mathrm{BM}}\cong\mathbb Z^b\oplus(\mathbb Z/2)^{\binom b2}\), with all other groups zero. Thus every graph with at least two independent cycles has explicit degree-two Borel–Moore torsion, invisible to rational calculations.
