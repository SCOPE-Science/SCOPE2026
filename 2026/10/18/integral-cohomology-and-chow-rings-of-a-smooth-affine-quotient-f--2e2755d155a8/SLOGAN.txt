The smooth affine quotients in Nikitina's invariant-divisor family have an affine-space complement of a torus-cylinder divisor. Their integral cohomology is torsion-free with Poincaré polynomial \(1+t^2(1+t)^n\), every positive-degree cup product vanishes, and their entire Chow ring is \(\mathbb Z[h]/(h^2)\).
