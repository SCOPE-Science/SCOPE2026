Every Schubert compactification of a complex matrix pencil has torsion-free ordinary integral cohomology, and its entire integral cup-product ring is determined by the gaps between distinct Jordan-block sizes and whether the pencil has a singular Kronecker summand. The regular-case intersection matrix is \(G_{pq}=d_pd_q-\delta_{pq}d_p\), with discriminant \((\kappa-1)\prod_p d_p\); the singular case has diagonal pairing \(\operatorname{diag}(1,-d_1,\ldots,-d_r)\).
