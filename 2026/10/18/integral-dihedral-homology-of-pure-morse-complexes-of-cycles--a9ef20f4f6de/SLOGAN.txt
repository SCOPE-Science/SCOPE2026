For every cycle of length \(n\ge5\), the pure discrete-Morse complex has an invariant integral degree-two homology class and a top-degree integral lattice equal to the two-point dihedral permutation lattice twisted by the rotation-parity character \(\eta_n(r)=(-1)^{n-1}\). This determines the full dihedral homology action, beyond the previously known nonequivariant sphere-wedge decomposition.
