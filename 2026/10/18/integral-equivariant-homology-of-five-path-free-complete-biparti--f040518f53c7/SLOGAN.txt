For every \(m,n\ge3\), the five-vertex-path-free complex of \(K_{m,n}\) has an explicit integral \(\mathfrak S_m\times\mathfrak S_n\)-homology lattice, namely the kernel of \(d_m\otimes d_n\) on tensor products of exterior-square permutation modules. Its third rational homology decomposes into exactly three irreducible outer products involving the standard representations and their exterior squares; all other reduced homology vanishes.
