For every height-two rooted tree with at least two arms, the integral reduced homology of its \(2\)-independence complex is the tensor product of the arms' leaf-augmentation lattices in dimension \(2a-1\), with **sign-free permutation of the arm factors**. This yields the complete automorphism character and the homotopy type \(\bigvee^{\prod_i(b_i-1)}S^{2a-1}\), interpreted as contractible when the multiplicity vanishes.
