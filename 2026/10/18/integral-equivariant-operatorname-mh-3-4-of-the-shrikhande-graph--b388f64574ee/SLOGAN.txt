In bidegree \((3,4)\), the Shrikhande graph's integral magnitude homology is supported on precisely the 144 ordered distance-two endpoint pairs, with a free rank-one group at each pair. Its full automorphism action is a direct sum of two induced **nontrivial sign modules** from the two nonneighbor-pair stabilizers; hence this large free group has no automorphism-invariant vector.
