For every isolated cyclic quotient plane action with weights \(1,a\), the integral Euler polar cokernel has a complete Smith classification determined only by \(n\) and \(\gcd(n,a+1)\). Its free rank jumps from one to \(\gcd(n,a+1)+1\) exactly when \(n/\gcd(n,a+1)\) is even; then its entire torsion is cyclic of order \(n/2\).
