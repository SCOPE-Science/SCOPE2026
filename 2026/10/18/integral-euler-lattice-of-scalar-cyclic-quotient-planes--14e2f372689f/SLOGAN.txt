The scalar cyclic plane quotient has an Euler polar lattice with just one finite cyclic Smith invariant: its order is \(n\) for odd \(n\), \(2n\) for \(n\equiv2\pmod4\), and \(n/2\) for \(4\mid n\). The real radical jumps to dimension three exactly in the last case, and the full inertia is explicit for every \(n\geq3\).
