The directed-linear-forest complex of the bidirected octahedral graph has torsion-free reduced integral homology of ranks \(3,92,32\) in degrees \(2,3,4\). Its central antipodal involution splits these three rational homology groups into \((+,-)\) eigenspaces of dimensions \((2,1)\), \((49,43)\), and \((12,20)\), respectively.
