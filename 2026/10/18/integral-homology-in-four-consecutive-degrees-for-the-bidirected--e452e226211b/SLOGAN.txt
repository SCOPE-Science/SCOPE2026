The multipath simplicial complex of the fully bidirected Petersen graph has reduced integral homology \(\mathbb Z^{12}\), \(\mathbb Z\), \(\mathbb Z^{44}\), and \(\mathbb Z^{94}\) in dimensions four through seven, respectively, and zero elsewhere. An exhaustive integral discrete-Morse reduction proves torsion-freeness and three-connectivity, without asserting a wedge-of-spheres homotopy type.
