The theta complex of the five-dimensional cube’s edge hypergraph has torsion-free reduced integral homology: \(\mathbb Z^{10}\) in degree \(22\) and \(\mathbb Z\) in degree \(24\), with no other nonzero groups. An explicit perfect acyclic discrete-Morse matching on all \(254475\) independent sets resolves the previously published integral-homology gap, without asserting a full homotopy classification.
