# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The domain is exactly the 24 directed arcs of the bidirected octahedral graph; every admissible path forest on its six vertices has at most five edges. The supplied standard-library verifier enumerates all subsets within that provably exhaustive bound, confirms the 1,817 augmented faces and 6,408 Hasse covers, and validates the prescribed 844 adjacent-face pairs and directed-acyclicity. An exact rational signed-boundary reduction gives ranks \((1,23,169,444,208)\). The acyclic matching leaves one zero-cell and respectively \(3,92,32\) critical cells in dimensions two through four and none in dimension one. Because these critical-cell ranks equal the rational Betti ranks, every integral Morse differential vanishes. This proves torsion-freeness and simple connectivity, but does not prove a sphere-wedge decomposition.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The octahedral graph is a canonical, minimal symmetric nonbipartite complete multipartite graph. The result supplies full integral homology and simple connectivity for a natural bidirected multipath complex, not merely its Euler characteristic or a field-specific Betti table. The three distinct nonzero homology degrees contrast with previously classified one-way bipartite and bidirectional polygon cases, providing a motivated comparison datum and a target for subsequent structural or equivariant investigations. The claim is deliberately limited to this graph and does not infer a general family theorem.
