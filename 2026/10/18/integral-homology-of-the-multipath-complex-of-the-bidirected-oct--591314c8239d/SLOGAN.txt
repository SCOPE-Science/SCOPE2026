The multipath complex of the fully bidirected octahedral graph \(K_{2,2,2}\) is simply connected, with reduced integral homology \(\mathbb Z^3\) in degree two, \(\mathbb Z^{92}\) in degree three, and \(\mathbb Z^{32}\) in degree four, and none elsewhere. An explicit acyclic 844-pair discrete-Morse matching and exact signed boundary ranks establish the entire torsion-free integral classification without asserting a wedge-of-spheres homotopy type.
