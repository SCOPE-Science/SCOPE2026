The independence complex of the thirty-five-vertex Kneser graph on triples of a seven-point set has precisely \(\widetilde H_6\cong\mathbb Z^{29}\) and \(\widetilde H_9\cong\mathbb Z^7\), with no torsion in any integral homology group. Two exhaustive discrete-Morse reductions with unit Smith invariant factors establish the integral conclusion beyond the previously reported field Betti numbers.
