The independence complex of the thirty-six-vertex Schrijver graph \(SG_{5,2}\) has exactly two nonzero reduced integral homology groups, \(\widetilde H_6\cong\mathbb Z^6\) and \(\widetilde H_8\cong\mathbb Z^2\), established by an exhaustive acyclic discrete-Morse matching. This determines the complete integral homology and a small CW model, but not the unstable attaching maps or a wedge-of-spheres homotopy type.
