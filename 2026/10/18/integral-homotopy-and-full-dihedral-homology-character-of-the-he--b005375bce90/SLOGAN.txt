The ordinary matching complex of the Herschel graph, a canonical smallest non-Hamiltonian polyhedral graph, has integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{12}S^3\). Under the full \(S_3\times C_2\) graph symmetry, its twelve-dimensional rational third homology decomposes as \((\mathbf1\boxtimes\mathbf1)\oplus(\operatorname{sgn}\boxtimes\varepsilon)\oplus2(V\boxtimes\mathbf1)\oplus3(V\boxtimes\varepsilon)\), so exactly one rational homology direction is fixed by all symmetries.
