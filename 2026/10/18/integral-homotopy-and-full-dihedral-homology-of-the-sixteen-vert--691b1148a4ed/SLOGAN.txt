The ordinary matching complex of the sixteen-vertex Möbius ladder is integrally homotopy equivalent to \(2S^4\vee4S^5\). Its entire rational homology action under the full dihedral automorphism group is \(H_4\cong L_{+,+}\oplus L_{-,-}\) and \(H_5\cong L_{+,-}\oplus L_{-,+}\oplus E\), distinguishing a one-dimensional invariant class in degree four from no invariants in degree five.
