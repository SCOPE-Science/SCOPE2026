# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The face criterion is equivalent to the absence of three-cliques in the line graph: a triple of pairwise intersecting original edges is a three-star or a triangular cycle. Direct enumeration verifies \(14{,}430\) faces, their downward closure, and all \(175\) forbidden triples. The complete face-poset orientation admits a topological ordering over all \(69{,}762\) covers, proving that the \(7{,}018\) proposed pairs are an acyclic discrete-Morse matching. Exact signed cancellations give a \(124\)-by-\(269\) integral Morse matrix with \(123\) unit pivots and an identically zero last row. This certifies the Smith factors, torsion absence and homotopy wedge. All fifteen symmetric-group conjugacy-class traces and the complete character orthogonality calculation reproduce the stated rational module. Mod-two boundary ranks are independently reconstructed. The verifier is a mathematical finite certificate, not an infinite-case extrapolation.

## Originality
**PASS under the checked literature, with residual bibliographic uncertainty.** The most relevant recent primary manuscript is Ghosh–Selvaraja [1] (first public 2026-02-10). Its chordal/whiskered structural results do not imply the exact homotopy and \(\mathfrak S_7\)-character of the nonchordal triangular graph's three-clique-free complex. Singh [2] handles different bounded-degree regimes without this triangle-free restriction; the triangle-free two-matching optimization paper [3] studies approximation algorithms, not simplicial topology. Jonsson [4] treats a related degree-bounded complex with loops permitted, not this object. The exact seven-label claim, the graph-theoretic equivalent formulation, and the individual homology partitions were also compared against targeted mathematical-record results and accessible historical claims. Closest higher matching and neighborhood-complex results use different host graphs or different functors and imply neither the same integral wedge nor the five-part Specht character. The absence of a recorded match is not proof of universal novelty.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** This is a natural finite member of the actively studied clique-free graph-complex family, corresponding to a classical triangle-free two-matching constraint. The exact integral homotopy and full rational symmetry character detect mixed-dimensional topology that cannot be inferred from a pure top-dimensional Betti calculation. The seven-label calculation has a nonzero extra homology degree as well as multiple nontrivial irreducible character components, so it is not merely a recomputation of a smaller instance or an arbitrary graph slice.
