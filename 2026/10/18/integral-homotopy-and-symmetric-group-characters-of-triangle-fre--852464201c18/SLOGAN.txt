The three-clique-free complex of the triangular graph \(L(K_7)\), equivalently the simplicial complex of triangle-free degree-at-most-two simple graphs on seven labels, has integral homotopy type \(S^4\vee\bigvee^{146}S^5\). Its rational \(\mathfrak S_7\)-homology has a trivial degree-four line and a completely determined Specht decomposition in homological degree five, supported by exhaustive integral discrete-Morse and character calculations.
