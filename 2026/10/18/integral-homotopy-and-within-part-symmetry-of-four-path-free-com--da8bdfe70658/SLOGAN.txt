For all complete multipartite graphs with at least three nonempty parts, the four-vertex-path-free vertex complex is integrally homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{(m-1)(1-N)+e_2+e_3}S^2\). Its full rational homology representation under independent permutations within each part is an explicit nonnegative sum of tensor products of standard augmentation modules, with character determined solely by the numbers of fixed vertices.
