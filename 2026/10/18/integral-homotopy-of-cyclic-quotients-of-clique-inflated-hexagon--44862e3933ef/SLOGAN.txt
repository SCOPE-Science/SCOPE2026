The geometric rotation quotient of the independence complex of a uniformly clique-inflated hexagon has exact integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{q(q-1)/2}S^1\vee\bigvee^{((q-1)^3+2(q-1))/3}S^2\) for every \(q\ge1\). Its stabilizer-aware cell decomposition proves a free fundamental group and torsion-free integral homology, beyond the earlier rational equivariant inflation formulas.
