For every \(n\ge3\), the multipath complex of two consistently oriented \(n\)-cycles joined by any one-way perfect matching is exactly \(S^{n-1}\vee S^{2n-3}\), integrally and independently of the matching twist. Thus coherently oriented Petersen and pentagonal-prism instances both have multipath homotopy type \(S^4\vee S^7\).
