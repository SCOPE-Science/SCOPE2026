The multipath complex of the fully bidirected six-vertex wheel has complete integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{2}S^{2}\vee\bigvee^{23}S^{3}\vee\bigvee^{10}S^{4}\). Exhaustive acyclic Morse reduction together with integral cup-product calculations proves that all four-cell attachments vanish, not merely their cellular boundary degrees.
