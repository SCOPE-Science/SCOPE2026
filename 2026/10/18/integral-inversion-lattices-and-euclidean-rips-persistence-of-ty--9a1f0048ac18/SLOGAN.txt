For every \(n\ge4\), the Euclidean Rips filtration on all type-A roots has five exact homotopy phases, while its first circle stage carries two rational inversion eigenspaces whose integral eigensublattices fail to generate by the precisely determined quotient \((\mathbb Z/2)^{n(n-3)/2}\). Coordinate permutations and root inversion act with explicit homology characters at every nontrivial stage.
