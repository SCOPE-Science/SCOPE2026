For two unordered particles on the geometric complete tripartite graph \(K_{3,3,3}\), 81 selected disjoint-triangle product tori form an integral basis of \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z^{81}\). Restricting to disjoint cycles of lengths \((3,6)\) instead leaves a cyclic \(\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z\) quotient, while all six possible disjoint-cycle length types have completely computed integral quotient groups.
