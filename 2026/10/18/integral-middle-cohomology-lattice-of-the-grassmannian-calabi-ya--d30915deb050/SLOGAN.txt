For every smooth eight-hyperplane Calabi–Yau fourfold in \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,8)\), the complete integral middle cup-product lattice is the odd unimodular lattice \(I_{438,194}\). Its primitive Schubert rank-two sublattice has determinant \(84\); its orthogonal complement is an even lattice of rank \(630\) whose exact discriminant quadratic form is determined, and the second Chern class is primitive.
