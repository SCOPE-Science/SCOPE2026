The Lovász open-neighborhood complex of the triangular graph \(T_7=L(K_7)\) is integrally homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{246}S^3\vee\bigvee^7S^4\). An exhaustive acyclic discrete-Morse matching and a zero signed integral attaching matrix upgrade the previously known mod-two Betti numbers to an exact torsion-free sphere-wedge decomposition.
