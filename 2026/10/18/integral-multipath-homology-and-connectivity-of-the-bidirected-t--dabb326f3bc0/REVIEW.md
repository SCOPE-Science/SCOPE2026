# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The 24 directed arcs, 6,497 augmented faces, 29,160 incidence relations and 3,199 acyclic pairs are produced directly by the standalone verification program. The two independent face enumerations coincide. An integer signed Morse cancellation gives exactly zero differential on all 99 critical cells, proving the four integral free-homology groups and vanishing elsewhere. Unmatching the empty-face pair gives a CW Morse model with no one-/two-cells and proves 2-connectivity. Direct mod-two boundary ranks cross-check the calculation, but the integral conclusion does not rely on field sampling. The limitation is that higher attaching maps were not computed.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** The closest foundational homotopy study of multipath complexes (arXiv:2208.04656) treats paths, polygons, small directed grids and transitive tournaments. The categorical comparison paper (arXiv:2401.01248) applies its exact matching-complex bridge only to directed-acyclic inputs, which excludes the fully bidirected cube. The polygonal bidirected work (arXiv:2601.05670) treats paths and cycles. Published ordinary cube matching and discrete-Morse complex results have different face conditions. The exact fully bidirected cube integral result was not located under the binary cube, four-crown, cycle-free chessboard and directed-multipath descriptions. Residual risk: unindexed or inaccessible equivalent work may exist; absence from a search is not proof of priority.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The cube is the smallest nontrivial trivalent hypercube and a canonical cyclic graph beyond previously treated bidirected paths and cycles. Its multipath complex has four consecutive dimensions with nonzero integral homology, and determining the exact torsion-free groups plus 2-connectivity provides structural information not reducible to a face-vector exercise. This is a meaningful natural finite benchmark for broader investigations; no family theorem or homotopy wedge decomposition is asserted.
