# Scientific review: seven-vertex cyclic-tournament multipath complex

## Correctness
PASS. The precise arc set and face predicate are provided. Both programs reconstruct every relevant face. The primary check exhibits 1,415 acyclic matching pairs and critical counts \(1,24,54,17\), with exact rank \(11\) and determinant \(-1\) of an explicitly indexed full-rank minor of the signed degree-four Morse differential; the degree-five differential vanishes. This proves the integral group structure, rather than only Betti numbers over fields. The independently enumerated original signed simplicial complex has the same Betti vector modulo four primes. Rational orbit-chain ranks are independently computed over \(\mathbb Q\) and checked modulo two other primes, establishing the claimed rational rotation modules.

## Originality
PASS on the available comparisons, with an explicit residual risk of an unindexed calculation. The closest full primary study, Caputi–Collari–Di Trani–Smith (arXiv:2208.04656v1), determines the homotopy type for transitive tournaments, not this directed-cycle-containing regular tournament. Caputi–Collari–Di Trani (DOI 10.1007/s10801-022-01180-9) develops the path-poset construction and examples but not these parameters. Govc–Levi–Smith (DOI 10.1007/s41468-021-00068-0) studies vertex-based tournaplexes, which are not the arc-subset complex. Searches for regular/cyclic tournament, seven-vertex, path poset, Betti ranks, cyclic representation, and stronger general results identified no implication of the stated claim. Name similarity with directed path homology or tournament neighborhood complexes is not equivalence.

## Mathematical value
PASS. For the natural first nontrivial seven-vertex cyclic regular tournament, a complete integral answer in three distinct homological degrees and its exact rational rotation representation extends the families of multipath computations beyond the transitive class. The primitive six-dimensional rational cyclic representation is essential: the total ranks alone would not determine invariant classes. No extension to all tournaments or external consequence is needed to make this exact structural example useful.

## Limits
This is one finite complex. Higher homotopy and integral module structure are not classified. Indexed search cannot rule out all unindexed equivalent computations. Verification is reproducible but does not constitute independent mathematical audit.
