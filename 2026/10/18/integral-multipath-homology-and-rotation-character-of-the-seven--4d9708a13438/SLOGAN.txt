The arc-subset multipath complex of the regular seven-vertex cyclic tournament has torsion-free reduced homology in three degrees, with ranks \(13,43,17\) in degrees \(3,4,5\). Its complete rational sevenfold-rotation representation is \(\mathbb Q\oplus W^2\), \(\mathbb Q^7\oplus W^6\), and \(\mathbb Q^5\oplus W^2\), respectively, where \(W=\mathbb Q(\zeta_7)\).
