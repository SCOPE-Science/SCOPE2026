The multipath complex of the fully bidirected three-rung ladder has torsion-free reduced integral homology \(\mathbb Z^3\), \(\mathbb Z^4\), and \(\mathbb Z^2\) in degrees two, three, and four, and vanishes elsewhere. Its complete rational Klein-four symmetry character distinguishes row exchange, column reversal, and their product; the result follows from an explicitly checked integral discrete-Morse reduction.
