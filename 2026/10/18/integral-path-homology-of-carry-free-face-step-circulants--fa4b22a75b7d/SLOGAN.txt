For every finite simplicial complex on \(d\) vertices, its nonempty faces encoded as base-\(q\) circulant jumps with \(q\ge3\) have exactly the same integral GLMY path-homology groups: \(H_k\cong\bigwedge^k\mathbb Z^d\) at every sufficiently large modulus. Downward-closed shortcut generators can create arbitrarily many local additive relations without changing any integral Betti number or introducing torsion.
