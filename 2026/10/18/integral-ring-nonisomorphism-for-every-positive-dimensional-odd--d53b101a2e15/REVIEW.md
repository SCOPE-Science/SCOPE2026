# Scientific review of the biisotropic three-plane degree obstruction

**Correctness — PASS.** The domain consists of smooth common zeros of a general pencil of alternating forms in the tautological rank-three bundle over an ordinary Grassmannian. Regular-section intersection theory gives the codimension-six class \(c_3(\bigwedge^2 S^\vee)^2\). An exact Schur-polynomial expansion, Pieri multiplication and the three-row hook-length formula give the displayed rational Plücker-degree ratio for every \(n\ge3\). Two factorized polynomial identities give the strict inequality \(1<D_n/B_n<64\le2^d\). Since integral graded ring isomorphisms preserve primitive degree-two generators and top powers, the ratio cannot equal the required integer \(d\)-th power. The smooth cellular decompositions supply the corresponding Chow-ring conclusion. The companion standard-library checker verifies Schur expansion, two independent degree computations, and symbolic rational identities. The argument does not rely on finite enumeration for the infinite conclusion.

**Originality — PASS, with risk.** Theorem 1.1 of Fonarev--Kuznetsov, arXiv:2610.03309v1, establishes graded integral cohomology **groups**, while Remark 1.2 explicitly treats multiplication as a further problem; it neither states the rank-three degree ratio nor implies ring nonisomorphism. Benedetti, arXiv:1809.10902, develops a Chevalley rule for two-planes in even ambient dimension, not the general odd-dimensional three-plane family. Relevant Grassmannian Fano geometry in Fatighenti--Mongardi, arXiv:1904.05679, does not provide this ratio. Targeted mathematical searches identified no equivalent rank-three theorem. One relevant manuscript on the first three-plane case is cited as **in preparation** and could not be inspected; its possible unpublished contents, or a hidden equivalent calculation, remain genuine originality risks. A matching word, absence of a search result, or mere presence of a different-degree table is not treated as proof of novelty.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The distinction between integral cohomology groups and their ring structures is explicitly motivated by the originating paper. The theorem resolves that distinction for the full first higher-rank three-plane subfamily, not merely one isolated numerical example: it proves inequivalence for every admissible ambient odd dimension and supplies an exact closed top-intersection formula with limiting ratio \(64\). It does not repackage a Betti-number equality or a textbook numerical example. The method is classical Schubert calculus, but the characteristic-class expansion and global degree inequalities constitute the substantive new calculation; there is no claim of a complete Chow-ring presentation or broad general \(k\)-plane theorem.

**Nearest literature and limitations.** Fonarev--Kuznetsov (2026) is the main input and closest geometric source; Benedetti (2018) concerns a different two-plane and even-dimensional setting; Fatighenti--Mongardi (2019) concerns related homogeneous-bundle Fano constructions. The source bibliography's announced but unavailable three-plane manuscript and unindexed work leave residual overlap risk. The exact polynomial verification supplements, not replaces, the geometric proof. No external or independent validation is asserted.
