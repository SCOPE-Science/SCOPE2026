Except at the zero-dimensional point boundary \(n=2\), for every \(n\ge3\), the smooth odd biisotropic three-plane Grassmannian \(\operatorname{S_2Gr}(3,2n+1)\) has the same integral cohomology groups as \(\operatorname{Gr}(3,2n-1)\), but their integral cohomology and Chow rings are never isomorphic. A closed Plücker-degree ratio lies strictly between \(1\) and \(64\), contradicting the necessary integral top-power ratio for any graded ring isomorphism.
