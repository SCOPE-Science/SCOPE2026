The thirteen-dimensional integral second homology of the Fano matroid independence complex splits under any Singer seven-cycle as the regular lattice \(\mathbb Z[C_7]\) plus its augmentation ideal. Its coinvariants are \(\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/7\mathbb Z\), so the cyclic symmetry creates a precise order-seven integral obstruction invisible in ordinary torsion-free homology.
