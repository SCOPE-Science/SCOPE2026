The top integral homology of the complex of six-vertex graphs with no perfect matching is the sign-twisted augmentation lattice on the ten partitions into two triples: \(\widetilde H_5(\mathrm{NM}_3(6);\mathbb Z)\cong\operatorname{sgn}_6\otimes\ker(\mathbb Z^{10}\to\mathbb Z)\). Deleting ten natural triangle-pair facets exposes a four-sphere, and their primitive attaching maps identify the full integral symmetry representation rather than only its known homology rank.
