# Scientific review of the integral symmetry refinement

## Correctness — PASS
The claim is for **all finite connected simple graphs with at least one edge**, including triangles and nontrivial higher-dimensional flag homology. The proof first identifies the scale-two graph as the total graph, then decomposes the relative pair into one maximal exceptional two-cell per graph triangle. The core inclusion from the original flag complex is a homotopy equivalence. Most importantly, every exceptional cell is met with coefficient \(\pm1\) by a *canonical integral octahedral two-cycle*, and the cycles carry their orientation signs exactly under graph automorphisms. These give an actual integral equivariant section of relative homology, and the relative long exact sequence proves the direct-sum decomposition. The degree-one persistence quotient is independently derived from the incidence triangles. All six-vertex finite data remain stress tests, not infinite arguments.

## Originality — PASS, with a substantial known non-equivariant antecedent
Adamaszek's 2011/2012 Theorem 5.1 **already proves** the unrestricted nonequivariant homotopy equivalence with a two-sphere per triangle and explicitly describes its exceptional two-cells. That entire homotopy statement is intentionally excluded from the asserted novelty. The additional result identifies the actual **integral homology representation** under each graph automorphism, with canonical signed generators, natural equivariant direct-sum splitting and an exact persistent first-homology quotient. A nonequivariant wedge statement cannot itself distinguish a trivial action from a sign action, though the previously known relative-cell proof leaves a short route to this additional computation. The complete-graph case yields the nontrivial \(S_n\)-module \(\bigwedge^3\mathbb Z^n\), with zero invariant rank, rather than merely the known binomial sphere count. The full pertinent Section 5 of Adamaszek was inspected and compared, not just a title. Galetto–Montaño–Wellner studies equivariant homology of hypercube Rips complexes and not these graph subdivisions. An available complete-graph subdivision record gives ranks but not characters or generators. Possible earlier equivariant derivations or an interpretation of the refinement as immediate from Adamaszek's proof remain residual risks.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The refinement addresses an independently motivated gap: concrete integral symmetry representations, not ordinary Betti numbers, are essential for equivariant Rips persistence. It works for every finite connected base graph regardless of automorphism group, flag-complex torsion, or local degree; the signed permutation module and exact quotient identify how group symmetries act on all new second-dimensional homology and on the classes killed in degree one. The orientation signs matter: already for subdivided complete graphs, large free homology has no full symmetric-group invariants. The explicit six-vertex octahedral cycles furnish reproducible natural generators. The result is not a new nonequivariant sphere-wedge theorem or a claim to a paper-sized advance. Its independent value is the complete integral group-action information, a feature that is not contained in scalar sphere counts.
