The scale-two graph-metric Vietoris–Rips complex of any once-subdivided finite connected graph acquires a canonical **oriented-triangle permutation lattice** in integral second homology, as a direct \(\operatorname{Aut}(G)\)-summand alongside the original flag-complex homology. For subdivided complete graphs, the full \(S_n\)-homology representation is \(\bigwedge^3\mathbb Z^n\), which has no nonzero invariant classes despite rank \(\binom n3\).
