The smooth index-66 K3–elliptic threefold has torsion-free integral cohomology with Betti numbers \((1,2,3,4,3,2,1)\) and \(\operatorname{Br}(V)=0\). Its canonical bundle nevertheless has exact order 66 inside \(\operatorname{Pic}^0(V)\), showing that the extremal holomorphic torsion is invisible to integral Chern classes and to the Brauer group.
