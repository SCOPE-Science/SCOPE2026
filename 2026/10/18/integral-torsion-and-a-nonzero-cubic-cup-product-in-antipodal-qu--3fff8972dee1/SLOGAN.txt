The geometric antipodal quotients of the scale-two Hamming Vietoris–Rips complexes on the four- and five-cubes have integral first-homology torsion \(\mathbb Z/2\), free third-homology ranks \(5\) and \(25\), and a nonzero cube of their degree-one mod-two cohomology class. Their cohomology rings agree with those of \(\mathbb{RP}^3\vee\bigvee^4S^3\) and \(\mathbb{RP}^3\vee\bigvee^{24}S^3\), respectively, without asserting corresponding homotopy equivalences.
