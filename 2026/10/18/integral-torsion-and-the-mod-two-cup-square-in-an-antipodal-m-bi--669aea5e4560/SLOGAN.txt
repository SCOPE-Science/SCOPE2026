The free antipodal quotient of the twelve-vertex Möbius-ladder independence complex has \(\pi_1=\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\), \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z\), and no higher integral homology. Its degree-one mod-two class has **nonzero square**, so the complete cohomology ring matches \(\mathbb{RP}^2\vee S^2\) without asserting that homotopy type.
