For the clique complex of the seventeen-vertex Paley graph, the integral second homology is a cyclic translation-augmentation lattice of rank sixteen with an explicit unimodular orbit basis. Its full rational affine symmetry is the irreducible augmentation of the natural seventeen-point permutation representation, with character values \(16,-1,0\) on the identity, nontrivial translations and nontranslation elements, respectively.
