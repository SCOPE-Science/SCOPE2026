The ordinary open-neighborhood complexes of the three-subset Johnson graphs \(J(6,3)\) and \(J(7,3)\) have homotopy types \(M(\mathbb Z/2,2)\vee\bigvee^{141}S^3\) and \(M(\mathbb Z/2,2)\vee\bigvee^{771}S^3\), respectively. Complete acyclic discrete-Morse reductions and exact integer attaching degrees prove that \(n=6\) is the first torsion case in this natural rank-three sequence (starting at \(n=4\)).
