In the undamped five-stage stochastic Runge–Kutta–Chebyshev method, two strictly interior component-curvature resonances turn each sampled update into a reflection, giving \(\operatorname{Var}(X_n)=4n\) while the corresponding full-gradient update contracts by \(61/64\). Moving the constant step off resonance restores a stationary law, but its variance diverges as \(100/(h-25)^2\).
