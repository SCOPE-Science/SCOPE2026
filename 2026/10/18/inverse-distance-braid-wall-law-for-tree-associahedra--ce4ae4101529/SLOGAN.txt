For the canonical graph associahedron of any labelled tree, exactly \((n-1)!/\operatorname{dist}_T(u,v)\) braid-wall edges swapping a specified pair of labels survive the normal-fan coarsening, regardless of the positive simplex weights. These pairwise counts reconstruct the entire tree metric; their sum is \((n-1)!\) times the Harary index, making the surviving-wall fraction vanish along paths while approaching one half along stars.
