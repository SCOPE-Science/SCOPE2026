Every negative tense expansion of a finite Gödel chain is determined by two nonincreasing interval-boundary sequences on its dual chain. Forgetting all but one negative modality gives exactly \(1+\binom{2n-2}{n-1}\) possible reducts for a chain of \(n+1\) elements, and each nonzero reduct has between two and \(\binom{2n-2}{n-1}\) full completions, with both bounds sharp for \(n\geq2\).
