Along the first-generation critical-curve pencil \((a^3b^3)^m a^2\) of an area-preserving piecewise-linear planar map, every fixed \(0<a<1\) has a sharp parabolic limit \(b_*(a)\). Its legal curve coordinates obey \(b_*(a)-b_m(a)=\pi^2/[d(a)(m+\kappa(a))^2]+O_a(m^{-4})\), with explicit positive coefficients and a nontrivial phase shift.
