Every rank of the strong-Bruhat Hasse diagram on \(\mathfrak S_n\) has an explicit, span-refined edge census: \(E_n(q)=[n]_q!\sum_{d=1}^{n-1}(n-d)dq^{d-1}/([d]_q[d+1]_q)\). Its Mallows interpretation reveals that every \(n\ge4\) has a nonuniform optimal bias \(0<q<1\), while fixed \(q\ne1\) gives linear expected upward degree and the uniform law gives order \(n\log n\).
