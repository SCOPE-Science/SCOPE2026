The unique exceptional 22-vertex Hirzebruch Batyrev mirror polytope has integral lattice automorphism group exactly \(C_2\); its involution exchanges all 20 nodal faces in ten pairs, while on the polar it yields twelve nodal pairs and two fixed nodal faces. The eight-dimensional space of polar node-diagonal relations splits into \(5\) invariant and \(3\) anti-invariant dimensions, and the two coloop nodes are exchanged.
