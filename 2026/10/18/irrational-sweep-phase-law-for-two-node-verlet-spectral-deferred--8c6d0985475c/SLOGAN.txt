For two-node Gauss–Legendre velocity-Verlet spectral deferred correction on an undamped oscillator, fixed-sweep small-step stability is governed by an exact determinant coefficient \((-1)^kqH^kc\) whose phase is irrational. Exactly half of all positive sweep counts are eventually locally stable and half locally unstable, so the observed low-count odd/even pattern does not persist as a universal parity rule.
