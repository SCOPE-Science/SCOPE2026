For every complete bipartite graph \(K_{m,n}\) with \(m,n\ge3\), the second rational homology of its unordered two-point configuration space is exactly \((S^{(m-2,2)}\boxtimes S^{(n-2,1,1)})\oplus(S^{(m-2,1,1)}\boxtimes S^{(n-2,2)})\), with the convention that \(S^{(r-2,2)}=0\) when the relevant side size is \(r=3\). For every balanced \(K_{r,r}\) with \(r\ge4\), the whole homology is irreducible under the full side-permuting and side-exchanging automorphism group, with zero character on every side-exchange symmetry.
