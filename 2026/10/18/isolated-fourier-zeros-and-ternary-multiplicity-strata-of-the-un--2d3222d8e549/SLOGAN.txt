For the uniform eight-digit Sierpiński carpet measure, the real Fourier-zero set consists exactly of rescaled analytic mask ovals and an isolated shifted lattice \(3(\mathbb Z+\tfrac12)^2\), with no intersections between the two strata. Isolated zeros have unbounded even Taylor orders with exact geometric frequency distribution \(\Pr(\operatorname{ord}=2r)=8/9^r\) and vanishing local phase index.
