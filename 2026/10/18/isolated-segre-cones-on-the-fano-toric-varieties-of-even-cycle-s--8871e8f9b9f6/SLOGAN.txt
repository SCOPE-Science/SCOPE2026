For the face-fan toric Fano variety of the symmetric-edge polytope of an even cycle \(C_{2r}\), the reduced singular locus consists of \(\binom{2r}{r}\) isolated fixed points, and every point has an affine neighborhood equal to the rank-one \(r\times r\) determinantal cone. Each point has exact Hilbert–Samuel multiplicity \(\binom{2r-2}{r-1}\); the known odd-cycle smoothness criterion is not part of the originality claim.
