Positive weighted zero-sum hyperplanes of real finite cubes admit a complete isometry classification: below the sharp dominance threshold, their abstract norm recovers the entire normalized weight multiset, and every isometry is an ambient global-sign coordinate permutation. At and beyond dominance, all weights collapse to the single \(\ell_\infty^{n-1}\) isometry type; the two regimes have distinct facet counts and symmetry groups.
