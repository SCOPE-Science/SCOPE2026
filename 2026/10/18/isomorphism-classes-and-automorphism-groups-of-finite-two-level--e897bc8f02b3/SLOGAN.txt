Finite two-level reflexive-transitive bi-intuitionistic modal frames are classified up to isomorphism by pairs of preorders carrying automorphism-orbits of permissible order ideals; their automorphism groups split into the two marked-preorder stabilizers. A four-term exact formula counts their isomorphism classes for every pair of level sizes, giving \(38\), \(135\), and \(452\) classes at sizes \((2,2)\), \((2,3)\), and \((3,3)\), respectively.
