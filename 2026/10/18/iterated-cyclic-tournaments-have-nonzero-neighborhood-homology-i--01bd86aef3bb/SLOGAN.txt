For the recursively defined regular tournaments \(T_1=\overrightarrow C_3\) and \(T_{k+1}=\overrightarrow C_3[T_k]\), the out-neighborhood complex is a torsion-free wedge of spheres with nonzero integral homology in every degree \(1,\ldots,k-1\) for \(k\ge3\), including exactly \(7\cdot3^{k-2}\) top-dimensional spheres. The structural mechanism is \(\mathcal N^+(\overrightarrow C_3[U])\simeq S^1\vee\bigvee^3\Sigma\mathcal N^+(U)\) for every tournament \(U\) of order at least two.
