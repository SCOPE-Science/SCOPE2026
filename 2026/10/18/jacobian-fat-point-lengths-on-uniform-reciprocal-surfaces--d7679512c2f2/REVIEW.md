Evaluation at the individual roots proves that the spanning forms are independent. The Fitting ideal is generated by maximal-size minors of the Jacobian of the standard Hankel quadrics: homogeneity gives the upper inclusion, a triangular minor gives a nonzero generator, and irreducibility of the indicated symmetric power in characteristic zero forces equality with the expected maximal-ideal power. The resulting Hilbert-function length calculation is exact. Symbolic checks for three degrees support but do not replace those arguments.
