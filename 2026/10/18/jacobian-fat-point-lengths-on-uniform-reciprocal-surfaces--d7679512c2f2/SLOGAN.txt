For generic rank-three reciprocal surfaces in characteristic zero, the intrinsic Jacobian singular scheme is a union of \(n\) fat coordinate points, each analytically defined on a degree-\((n-2)\) Veronese cone by \(\mathfrak m^{n-3}\). Their exact total length is \(n(n-3)((n-2)(n-4)+2)/2\); the reduced singular-point support was previously known.
