For the two-layer Boolean pyramid \(\Delta(n,\{0,k\})\), the John ellipsoid has exact transverse squared semiaxis \(\min(k,n-k)^2/[n(n+1)]\), axial squared semiaxis \(k^2/[n(n+1)^2]\), and a sharp tangent-facet transition at \(2k=n\). It becomes a Euclidean ball precisely at \(n=m^2-1\), \(k=m(m-1)\), giving an infinite family of nonsimplex round-John polytopes.
