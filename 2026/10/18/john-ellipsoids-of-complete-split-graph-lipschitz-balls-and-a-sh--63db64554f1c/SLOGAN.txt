For every complete split graph \(K_p\vee\overline K_q\) with \(p\ge3\), \(q\ge2\), the unique John ellipsoid of its real Lipschitz-dual unit ball has an explicit three-eigenspace shape and exact Euclidean volume. The \(\binom p2\) clique edges are ellipsoid-invisible precisely when \(q\ge p+1\), but their supporting facets become strictly nontangent only when \(q\ge p+2\).
