For every odd prime and every finite Heisenberg group \(H_n(p)\), an explicit Gaussian-inversion formula counts all abelian-image endomorphisms jointly by their exact fixed-point subgroup size and image size. It gives the number of fixed-point-free such maps, including \(p^5-2p^3\) when \(n=1\), without asserting uniqueness of associated Yang--Baxter constructions.
