For a uniform random chord diagram, the total shorter-arc span and the number of crossings have a nondegenerate bivariate Gaussian limit on the \(n^{3/2}\) scale, with limiting correlation \(\sqrt{15}/4\). An asymptotic linear predictor using span explains exactly \(15/16\) of crossing-count fluctuation variance; the remaining residual variance is \(1/720\) after the same normalization.
