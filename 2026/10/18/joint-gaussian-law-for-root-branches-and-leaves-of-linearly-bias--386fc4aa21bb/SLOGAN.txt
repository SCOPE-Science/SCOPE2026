When the root weight of a Hoppe recursive tree grows proportionally to its number of vertices, root-branch and leaf counts have a nondegenerate **joint Gaussian limit** with an explicit, strictly positive limiting covariance \(c/[2(c+1)^2]\). The limiting covariance matrix differs from the fixed-root-weight regime and quantifies the dependence between the tree's first-generation branching and its terminal leaves.
