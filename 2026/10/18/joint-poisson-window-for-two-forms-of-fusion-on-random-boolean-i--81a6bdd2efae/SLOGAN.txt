On a random Boolean information frame, the ordered witnesses for fusion of two independently valued atoms and the distinct-pair witnesses for self-fusion have independent Poisson limits with respective means \(t^2\) and \(t^2/2\) at the sharp density window \(p=t3^{-k/2}\). Exact bivariate moments and an exponential total-variation bound explain why the shared atomic valuation does not prevent asymptotic independence.
