Commuting affine retractions over \(\mathbb F_q^n\) have one affine-conjugacy orbit for each of the \(2^k\)-component joint-eigenspace dimension vectors, with an exact factor \(q^{n-d_{[k]}}\) refining the linear orbit count. Their complete product-image-rank distribution admits a closed sum and generating product, and its balanced dimension vectors converge in total variation to a tilted discrete Gaussian.
