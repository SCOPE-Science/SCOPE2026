For a complete bipartite metric with \(k\ge2\) edges incident to one vertex jointly lengthened, the anchored Lipschitz-dual ball has an exact finite-binomial-sum volume law. Deleting those edges produces strictly superadditive volume increments, and the volume becomes exactly \(C^{p+q-k-1}\), but not \(C^{p+q-k}\), at the redundancy threshold \(h=3\).
