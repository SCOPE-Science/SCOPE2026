For every finite Coxeter monoid, the complete three-support distribution of commuting parabolic idempotent pairs is an explicit connected-component polynomial of its unlabeled Coxeter graph. In type \(A_m\) the full trivariate sequence has a rational generating function, giving \(1,4,14,48,164,560,\ldots\) total commuting ordered pairs; the underlying commutation criterion was previously known.
