For the auxiliary Fibonacci-bimodal Markov map, a finite-ceiling rational generating function gives the joint first-return duration and maximum-cell-index law from the fifth cell, for every \(0<\lambda<1\). Its Catalan time marginal and exact depth tail yield distinct \(\varepsilon^{-2}\) and \(\varepsilon^{-1}\) crossover scales near \(\lambda=1/2\); earlier general branching-process results already explain portions of the separate marginals.
