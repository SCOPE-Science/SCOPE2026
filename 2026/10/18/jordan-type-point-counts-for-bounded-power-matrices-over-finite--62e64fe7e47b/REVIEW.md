# Scientific review

**Correctness: PASS.** The exact matrix-power expansion over the dual-number ring splits the problem into nilpotent residue matrices and the kernel of a linear operator. A characteristic-free rank computation for every pair of Jordan blocks establishes the exponent-dependent kernel dimension. The standard nilpotent class-size calculation yields every coefficient. Direct brute-force checks of all dual coefficients over prime fields in six cases and independent block linearization tests across six exponents and four primes corroborate the proof, without being used as an infinite proof.

**Originality: PASS within the inspected comparisons.** The cited 2021 decomposition paper treats square-zero summands and existence of lifts but supplies no arbitrary-exponent, mixed-Jordan-type count. The 2023 dual-complex Jordan-form paper does not count finite-field solutions. A one-block nilpotency-index profile is not the complete mixed-block Jordan-type distribution. Exponent-two point counts and related square-zero scheme properties are not claimed here. The main remaining risk is an equivalent Hall-polynomial or jet-scheme enumeration appearing without the same terminology.

**Value: PASS.** The finite-point spectrum of bounded-power matrix schemes over length-two rings is a natural arithmetic invariant. The formula gives the entire Jordan-type distribution, not just a numerical example, uniformly in all dimensions, exponents at least three, field sizes, and field characteristics. In particular the block interaction rank is exact and characteristic-free; it allows arithmetic investigation of singular strata and nilpotent module deformations.

Closest sources: Danchev–García–Gómez Lozano (2021), Qi–Cui (2023), and the earlier square-zero scheme paper of Knutson–Zinn-Justin (2005). Finite computations cannot substitute for the supplied general proof. Possible unindexed equivalent Hall-algebra literature remains a residual risk.
