The commuting coordinate-swap and inversion symmetries of the Borges–Korchmáros–Speziali plane-curve pencil have a Klein-four quotient with an explicit Chebyshev equation and exact genus \((q-1)^2/4\) for every odd prime power \(q\). In characteristic three its elliptic quotient has \(j(\lambda)=-\lambda^6/((\lambda-1)^4(\lambda+1))\), a separable degree-six modular map branched only over \(0\) and \(\infty\).
