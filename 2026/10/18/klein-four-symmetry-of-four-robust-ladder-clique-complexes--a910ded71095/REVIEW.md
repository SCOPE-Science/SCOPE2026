# Review of the four-robust ladder symmetry finding

## Correctness — PASS

The ordinary rank and concentration follow from Chandrakar–Hazra–Rout–Singh, arXiv:2408.07646, Theorem 1.1, together with combinatorial Alexander duality. The remaining proof is an independent signed Lefschetz calculation. Every fixed simplex under an involution decomposes into two-element orbits and fixed singleton vertices. The sharp face-size condition from König's theorem reduces the complete infinite-family enumeration to induced edge and matching tests on at most three orbits, which the six cases in the proof classify explicitly. The results imply the rational group character and quotient ranks by semisimplicity and finite-group transfer. Exhaustive finite calculations agree for 99 traces and five complete simplicial homology stages. The integral equivariant module is not claimed.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result upgrades a known infinite-family ordinary homology formula to the complete rational Klein-four symmetry representation, revealing the exact parity contribution of the central ladder square and sharp differences between two geometrically natural quotient actions. The rationally acyclic full quotient is a structural consequence not readable from the original wedge rank. This is a motivated invariant of a recently studied topological construction rather than an arbitrary finite statistic.
