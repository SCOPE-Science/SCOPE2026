For every two-row ladder with at least four columns, the complete rational row-and-column reflection character of its four-robust clique complex is \((\binom{n-1}{3},-\binom{n-1}{3},-f_n,f_n)\), where \(f_n=n/2-1\) for even \(n\) and zero otherwise. Row exchange negates all fifth-homology classes, so the quotient by both reflections is rationally acyclic, while the two other reflection quotients have explicitly different ranks.
