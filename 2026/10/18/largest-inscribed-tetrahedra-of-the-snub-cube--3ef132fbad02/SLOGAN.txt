For the snub cube with vertices at the parity-selected permutations of \((\pm1,\pm\tau^{-1},\pm\tau)\), the maximum contained tetrahedron volume is exactly \(\frac23(\tau^2+\tau+2)\), where \(\tau^3=\tau^2+\tau+1\).
