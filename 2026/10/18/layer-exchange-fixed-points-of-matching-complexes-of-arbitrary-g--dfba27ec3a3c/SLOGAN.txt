For every finite graph \(G\), the geometric layer-exchange fixed locus of the ordinary matching complex of its prism \(G\square K_2\) is PL homeomorphic to the matching complex of the fully whiskered graph \(W(G)\). It is contractible if \(G\) has no perfect matching, and otherwise is a wedge of exactly \(p(G)\) spheres of dimension \(|V(G)|/2-1\), where \(p(G)\) counts perfect matchings of \(G\).
