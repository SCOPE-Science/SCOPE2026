On any fixed finite Alexandrov rooted tree with \(k\) leaves, the exact eventual-period spectrum for one-atom truth sets under repeated next is \(\{t\ge1:\sum_{q\mid t}q^{v_q(t)}\le k\}\) (with the sum zero for \(t=1\)). Its sharp maximum is Landau's permutation-order function \(g(k)\), independent of tree height and branching.
