For a uniform labeled tree embedded on a convex polygon, the expected number of edge crossings decreases strictly with the number of leaves, with an exact Stirling-number regression and a negative mixed covariance. The leaf count explains asymptotically \(1/[2(e-2)]\approx69.6\%\) of the variation in the shape-induced mean by linear prediction, even though tree shape accounts for only \(5/(2n^2)\) of total crossing variance.
