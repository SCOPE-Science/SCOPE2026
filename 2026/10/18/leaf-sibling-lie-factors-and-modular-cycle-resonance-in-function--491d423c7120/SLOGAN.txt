For an unweighted evolution algebra \(e_v^2=e_{f(v)}\), every derivation Lie algebra splits into leaf-sibling matrix factors and, outside characteristic two, a central direction for each cycle resonant with powers of two. The factors are skew-symmetric in odd or zero characteristic and symmetric-commutator factors in characteristic two, yielding exact dimension formulas and detecting attached-tree information invisible to idempotent schemes.
