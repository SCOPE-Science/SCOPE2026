The excluded area of any two rotated regular polygons is an exact folded cosine whose angular period is governed by the least common multiple of their side counts. For equal-area polygons approaching circles, the orientation-dependent range scales as \(\pi^2 A_0/\operatorname{lcm}(n,m)^2\), so equal-order and consecutive-order mixtures exhibit different quadratic versus quartic decay rates; the complete random-orientation law is explicit.
