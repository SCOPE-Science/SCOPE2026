For affine \(L^2\)-projection under an arbitrarily asymmetric Dirichlet law on a simplex, the exact sharp \(L^1\) and \(L^\infty\) norms depend only on total concentration and the smallest shape parameter, and only vertices with that smallest parameter maximize amplification. Equal shape parameters uniquely minimize worst-case amplification at fixed total concentration, while fixed-proportion high-concentration growth has an explicit Gaussian constant.
