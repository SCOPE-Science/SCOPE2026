For the rate-one directed cycle Markov semigroup on \(n\) sites, the sharp time-weighted \(\ell^1\) and \(\ell^\infty\) derivative norm grows as \(0.1491464853520236\ldots\,n\), with every maximizing time asymptotic to \(0.0505220542889816\ldots\,n^2\). Both constants are uniquely determined by a positive-odd-integer theta series; the limiting endpoint coefficient is strictly larger than the Hilbert-space coefficient \(1/(\pi e)\).
