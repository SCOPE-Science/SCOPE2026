Uniform finite words of the multiplicative golden-mean system converge on every fixed set of coordinates to a product of boundary-biased golden-mean Markov chains, but their typical global frequencies of ones converge to distinct constants \(0.3665416620\ldots\) and \(2/(4+\sqrt5)=0.3207149131\ldots\). Their growing-prefix total-variation distance tends to one exponentially, while the uniform-word count of ones obeys a Gaussian central limit theorem with an explicit positive variance series.
