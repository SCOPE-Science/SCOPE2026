For the normal-fan toric variety of symmetric doubly stochastic matrices, the affine divisor class group at a torus-fixed point is \(\mathbb Z^t\oplus(\mathbb Z/2)^c\), where \(t\) counts transposition blocks and \(c\) counts odd-cycle blocks of its support. Its local canonical Cartier index is two exactly when the matrix size is odd and an odd-cycle block occurs; an explicit bivariate generating function enumerates every such local type.
