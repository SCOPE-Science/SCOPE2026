# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The claim is established for every connected complete multipartite graph of order at least three. The necessary and sufficient locating-code reduction to a vertex-cover condition is proved outside stars; stars are handled separately. The quotient graph with loops translates coalition pairs into two-vertex covers. A pair of disjoint constraint edges bounds every cover-participating block index by four; clique components of size three force at most three. The two-edge matching construction reaches four, and a twin-pair construction reaches three in all remaining cases. Exhaustive checks for 86 part-size profiles (orders three to nine) and 1,024 five-index simple quotient graphs passed. Finite cases alone do not prove the unrestricted theorem.

## Originality — PASS, with residual bibliographic risk
The February 2026 introducing paper defines the invariant and proves a general size-dependent upper bound, a small-order extremal classification and results for paths and cycles. The available portions do not state the uniform multipartite theorem or its matching-constraint equality classification. Search comparisons included exact phrasings, vertex-cover equivalents, twin-class aliases, direct small-order tables, and general domination bounds. The closest published record on complete multipartite self/solid locating codes concerns different requirements and does not imply a coalition partition count. The full 23-page introducing manuscript was inspected, including the general bound, paths/cycles, small-order equality classification, complexity treatment and references; it does not contain the claimed all-multipartite formula. Definitive worldwide bibliographic priority remains unverified. The few small order examples falling under its general characterization are expressly credited and not claimed individually as new.

## Value — PASS
The recent coalition invariant previously had a general upper bound depending on the graph order and locating-domination number. This theorem gives a uniform sharp constant of four on a natural unlimited-order and unlimited-part family, a complete structural boundary for attaining four, and explicit extremal partitions. Its reduction to two-vertex covers of a block-projection constraint graph explains the structural obstruction; this is more than an isolated small count or routine renaming.
