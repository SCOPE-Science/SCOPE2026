For every prime \(p\), all Loewy layers of the endomorphism ring of \(C_{p^a}\oplus C_{p^b}\) have explicit orders, with a sharp three-case maximum nilpotent-element index: \(2b\) for equal exponents, \(2b-1\) for adjacent exponents, and \(b\) when their gap is at least two. The full radical-power filtration is given by exact cornerwise divisibility formulas.
