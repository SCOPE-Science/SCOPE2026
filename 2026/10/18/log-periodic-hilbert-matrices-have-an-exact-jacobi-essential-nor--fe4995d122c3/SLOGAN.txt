The essential norm of a log-periodically row-modulated Hilbert matrix is exactly the norm of an explicit compact bilateral Jacobi operator, with exponentially certified finite truncations. Its small-amplitude inflation has the sharp frequency-dependent coefficient \(\frac{1+3\operatorname{sech}^2(\pi\omega)}{4(1-\operatorname{sech}^2(\pi\omega))}\), distinguishing fixed log-scale oscillations from slowly varying weights.
