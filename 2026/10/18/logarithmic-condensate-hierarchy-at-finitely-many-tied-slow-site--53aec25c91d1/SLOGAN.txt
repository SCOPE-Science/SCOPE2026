At the critical \(q(1+1/k)\) hopping law, finitely many identical slow sites do not share the condensate extensively: one holds almost all the excess mass, while the other sites occupy independent uniformly distributed powers of the system size. The probability of two slow sites each holding a fixed fraction \(\varepsilon\) of the excess is asymptotic to \((m-1)\log((1-\varepsilon)/\varepsilon)/\log L\), despite Gaussian ordinary-site fluctuations.
