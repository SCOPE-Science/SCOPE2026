Adding chords from vertex \(n\) to the powers-of-two vertices \(2,4,8,\ldots\) below \(n-1\) makes every integer from \(1\) to \(n(n+1)/2\) the sum of a connected vertex set. Thus \(\mathrm{mEC}(n)\le\lfloor\log_2(n-2)\rfloor\) for all \(n\ge8\), strengthening the published linear bound to a logarithmic one.
