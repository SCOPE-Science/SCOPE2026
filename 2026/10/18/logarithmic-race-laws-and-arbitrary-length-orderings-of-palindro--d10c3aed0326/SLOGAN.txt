For any bases whose reciprocal logarithms are rationally independent, the palindrome-counting races have explicit strictly positive logarithmic densities for every ordering. The explicit arbitrarily long families \(3\cdot2,3\cdot2^2,\ldots,3\cdot2^k\) satisfy this condition and hence realize all \(k!\) orderings infinitely often; for example \(\delta_{\log}\{n:A_2(n)>A_3(n)\}=\log2/(2\log3)\).
