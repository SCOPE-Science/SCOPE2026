For balanced symmetric Pólya–Friedman urns with Friedman probability \(p_N\to0\), the precise crossover occurs when \(p_N\log N\) stays finite: the color proportions at logarithmic time \(N^s\) converge jointly to \(u+e^{-kb\lambda s/c}(B-u)\) for one shared Dirichlet vector \(B\). If \(p_N\log N\to\infty\), all strictly positive logarithmic times instead converge to uniform proportions.
