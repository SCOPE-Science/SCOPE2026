For every finite simple graph without isolated vertices, the newly defined \(\Gamma\)-cubical homology recovers its ordinary graph homology in degrees \(0,1,2\); in particular, \(H_2^{\Gamma}(G;\mathbb Z)=0\) integrally even when triangles and squares occur. The key is that every singular \(\Gamma\)-square lives on one graph edge and is bounded by an explicit cone chain.
