For Poisson births at low rate \(\lambda\) and any bounded positive mean-one lifetime law \(Q\), extinction duration and total population-time satisfy \(\operatorname{Cov}(T_\lambda,S_\lambda)=\operatorname{Var}(Q)+\lambda(\tfrac12+2\operatorname{Var}(Q))+O(\lambda^2)\). Thus the previously observed insensitivity of the two means breaks at their covariance, with a strictly positive linear response determined solely by lifetime variance.
