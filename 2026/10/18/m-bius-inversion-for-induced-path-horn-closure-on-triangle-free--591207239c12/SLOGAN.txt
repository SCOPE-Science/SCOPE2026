For the induced-three-vertex-path Horn closure on the line graph of any triangle-free graph, every closed-set interval has Möbius number \(\mu(A,B)=(-1)^{|R|}I_{G[W]}(-1)\), where \(R\) is the newly active vertex set and \(W\) consists of new vertices with no old active neighbor. Thus all intervals have Möbius numbers in \(\{-1,0,1\}\) for forests, while disjoint six-cycles exhibit unbounded powers of two.
