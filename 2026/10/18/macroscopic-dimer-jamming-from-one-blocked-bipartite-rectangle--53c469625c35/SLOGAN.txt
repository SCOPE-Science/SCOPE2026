Uniformly random dimer deposition on a balanced complete bipartite graph with one macroscopic blocked rectangle leaves a positive limiting vacancy fraction, even when a perfect matching exists. For blocked proportions \(\alpha,\beta\in(0,1)\), the fraction is exactly \(\alpha\exp(-(1-\beta)\log(\beta/\alpha)/(\beta-\alpha))\) off the diagonal, with continuous diagonal value \(\alpha\exp(-(1-\alpha)/\alpha)\).
