In a one-expert electorate with exchangeable beta-binomial novice signals, the optimal number of ballots delegated to the expert is determined by an exact single-crossing test and grows proportionally with electorate size: \(r_k/k\to1-((1-q)/q)^{1/(a-b)}\). At novice accuracy \(3/5\), correlation \(1/6\) and expert accuracy \(7/10\), the optimal delegated fraction approaches \(2/7\), whereas with independent novice signals the optimal number is bounded by one, apart from the trivial dictator-equivalent three-voter tie.
