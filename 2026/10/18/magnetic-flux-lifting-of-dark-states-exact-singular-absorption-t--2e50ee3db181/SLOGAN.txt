An arbitrarily weak magnetic flux converts exact dark states of a quantum ring with one absorbing site into metastable modes whose detection times diverge as \(\Phi^{-2}\). The coefficient is exactly \(\gamma d(n-d)/8\) for odd rings and \(\gamma r(m-r)/4\) for even rings with \(n=2m\) and \(r=d\bmod m\), revealing a parity-dependent antipodal exception and a universal slow-time survival profile.
