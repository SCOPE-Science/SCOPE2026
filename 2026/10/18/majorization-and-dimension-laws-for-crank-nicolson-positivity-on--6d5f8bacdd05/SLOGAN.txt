For the completed Crank–Nicolson heat step on a Boolean cube with a fixed total coordinate conductance, the entrywise-positivity cutoff is strictly increased by every genuine concentration of the directional rates; equal rates uniquely give the smallest cutoff. Its dimension-normalized isotropic value decreases from \(1+\sqrt5\) to \(2\), with sharp correction \(1/d+5/(4d^2)+O(d^{-3})\), while concentrating the rate onto a single coordinate makes the positivity cutoff infinite.
