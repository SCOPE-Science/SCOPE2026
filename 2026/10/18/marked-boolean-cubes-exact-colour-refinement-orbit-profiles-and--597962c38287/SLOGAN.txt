For every nonempty set of individually marked vertices of a Boolean cube, one-dimensional colour refinement computes exactly the pointwise-stabilizer orbits within \(d+1\) rounds, with explicitly calculable class sizes. It becomes discrete exactly when the marked vertices assign different binary signatures to all coordinates, so the sharp landmark number is \(1+\lceil\log_2 d\rceil\) (the latter numerical threshold was previously known as the determining number).
