In a uniform recursive tree, forcing any prescribed set of earlier vertices to be leaves leaves the depth of the newest vertex distributed exactly as in the smaller tree. This pruning invariance yields the complete Eulerian–Stirling joint law of terminal depth and leaf count and the strict negative-covariance identity \(\operatorname{Cov}(H_n,L_n)=-(n-2)/(2(n-1))\) for \(n\ge3\).
