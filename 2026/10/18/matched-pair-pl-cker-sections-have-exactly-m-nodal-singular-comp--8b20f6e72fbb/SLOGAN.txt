In the Grassmannian \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,2m)\), imposing the \(m\) Plücker equations that kill disjoint coordinate pairs gives a normal complete intersection whose reduced singular locus has **exactly \(m\)** irreducible components, each a smaller matched-pair section. Every point with \(z\) vanished pairs has \(z\) independent ordinary threefold nodes transverse to a smooth factor; the singular-support degree is \(\frac{m}{2m-3}\binom{4m-8}{2m-4}\).
