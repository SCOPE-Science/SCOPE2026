For every prime \(p\ge5\), two complex three-tap signals with the same prescribed support have sharp minimum short-time Fourier support \(5p-4\) when the taps form an arithmetic progression and \(7p-2\) otherwise. The four optimal zeros in the progression case satisfy exactly the resonance law \(\alpha\beta=\gamma\delta\), yielding \(p\binom p2\) distinct optimal zero patterns per progression.
