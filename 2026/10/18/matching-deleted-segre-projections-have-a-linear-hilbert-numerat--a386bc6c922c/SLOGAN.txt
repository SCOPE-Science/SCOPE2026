For a rank-one \(a\times b\) matrix with \(a,b\ge3\), deleting any \(k\) entries in distinct rows and columns subtracts exactly \(kz\) from the Segre Hilbert-series numerator without changing its denominator. The projected variety is projectively normal, with degree \(\binom{a+b-2}{a-1}-k\), and its coordinate ring is Gorenstein precisely for square matrices with no deletions or for the exceptional square size \(a=b=3\).
