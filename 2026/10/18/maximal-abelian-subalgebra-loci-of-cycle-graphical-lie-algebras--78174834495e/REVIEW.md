# Review: maximum abelian-subalgebra loci of cycle graphical Lie algebras

## Correctness
PASS. The support-of-coordinate-functionals lemma independently proves the maximum dimension, then forces precisely the two alternating even-cycle planes or the \(n\) projective-line families for an odd cycle. Intersections are verified by maximum independent-set incidence; distinct Plücker lines meet transversely at rational nodes. The point count subtracts exactly \(n\) double-counted nodes. Characteristic two and inseparable ground fields cause no exceptions. The direct finite-field RREF verifier agrees in all nine recorded cases. Only reduced locus structure is asserted, not the unreduced scheme.

## Originality
PASS under the available comparisons, subject to residual literature risk. The foundational graph-to-Lie-algebra construction is due to Mainkar (arxiv:1310.3414v1), and the dimension upper bound is already covered by Aldi and collaborators (arxiv:2504.19836v1). Rossmann (arxiv:2107.05564v1) and the cycle graphical-group character-degree theorem calculate single-element rank or character data; neither entails the incidence scheme of maximum commuting linear spaces. The distinct Gaudin moduli problem (arxiv:1004.3253v1) is not the same Lie algebra. No equivalent all-length reduced component and point-count theorem was found in the checked literature. A classification under another algebraic or Grassmannian name remains possible.

## Value
PASS. Equality geometry is a natural refinement of known abelian-subalgebra dimension bounds. The odd-cycle locus is a complete connected nodal genus-one polygon, while the even-cycle locus is two reduced points; the exact \(\mathbb F_q\) point count sharply distinguishes the two. Such a complete structural classification of an infinite family goes beyond a routine dimension estimate or isolated computation.

## Scope and limitations
Only the reduced geometric locus and exact finite-field rational points for maximum-dimensional abelian subalgebras are determined; nonreduced scheme structure, other subalgebra dimensions, and other graphs are not addressed. All primary sources were checked to the stated extent, and priority outside this corpus remains uncertain. Independent mathematical audit has not been performed.
