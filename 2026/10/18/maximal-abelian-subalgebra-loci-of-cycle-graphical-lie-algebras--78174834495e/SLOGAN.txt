For the two-step graphical Lie algebra of an \(n\)-cycle, the reduced variety of maximum-dimensional abelian Lie subalgebras consists of two points when \(n\) is even, but is a nodal cycle of \(n\) projective lines when \(n\) is odd. Its exact \(\mathbb F_q\)-point count is respectively \(2\) or \(nq\), in every characteristic.
