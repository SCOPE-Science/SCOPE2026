Every maximal scattered common subsequence of the increasing and decreasing \(q\)-symbol permutation blocks, each repeated \(m\) times, is already longest, with length \(2m-1\). The ternary pair has exactly \(\binom{2m+2}{m+1}-2\binom{2m}{m}+\binom{2m-2}{m-1}\) such subsequences, yielding a closed exponential-size benchmark for enumeration algorithms.
