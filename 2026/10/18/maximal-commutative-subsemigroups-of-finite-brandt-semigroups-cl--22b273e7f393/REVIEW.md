# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof verifies both directions of the structural classification using explicit Brandt multiplication, identifies the only possible off-diagonal source and target sets, proves the diagonal fibers are maximal abelian groups, and shows maximality under addition of any missing element. The order polynomial follows by uniquely counting labeled partitions of indices into unused/source/target. Directly enumerated maximal commuting subsets and product-closure checks over five group examples agree with the classification and polynomial. Finite checks support but do not replace the proof.

## Originality — PASS (literature checked)
Kumar--Dalal--Pandey (arXiv:2010.05000v1), Lemma 3.10 and Theorem 3.14, already establish a source--target obstruction and the maximum clique size for the trivial group; those are credited. Margolis--Rhodes--Silva (arXiv:1705.04956v1) classify aspects of subsemigroup lattices and associated complexes, but not all maximal commutative subsemigroups. Paulista (arXiv:2412.06560v1), Corollary 3.5, treats completely simple Rees matrix semigroups without zero, not Brandt completely zero-simple inverse semigroups. The group-general inclusion-maximal classification and size generating series are not implied by these inspected results. Residual risk: equivalent unindexed literature under other terminology.

## Value — PASS
The classification supplies a natural complete invariant for a canonical, arbitrary-group family of inverse semigroups. The order series incorporates the internal subgroup-size spectrum and distinguishes maximum-size cliques from the full collection of inclusion-maximal commutative subsemigroups. This captures, for example, multiple maximal abelian subgroup orders in nonabelian groups and yields an explicit exponential counting law for fixed group.
