For every finite group \(G\) and \(n\ge2\), all inclusion-maximal commutative subsemigroups of the Brandt semigroup \(B_n(G)\) consist either of maximal abelian groups in all diagonal fibers or of a complete zero-product source--target block with maximal abelian groups on all unused diagonal fibers. Their full order generating series depends only on the maximal-abelian-subgroup size polynomial \(H_G(z)\), and their total count is \((a+2)^n-2(a+1)^n+2a^n\), where \(a\) is the number of maximal abelian subgroups of \(G\).
