Every inclusion-maximal dissociation set of a rectangular rook graph is a disjoint edge-and-two-star packing that either covers both bipartite sides or contains no isolated edges and covers one entire side. A closed bivariate exponential generating function counts every such set; on boards with \(n\ge2m\), its support contains only the even sizes \(2\lceil m/2\rceil,2\lceil m/2\rceil+2,\ldots,2m\), with exact labelled coefficients.
