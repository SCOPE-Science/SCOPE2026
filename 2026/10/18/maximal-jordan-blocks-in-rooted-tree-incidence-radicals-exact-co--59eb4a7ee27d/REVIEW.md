# Review: maximal Jordan blocks in tree incidence radicals

## Correctness

**PASS.** The proof counts the rank of the highest possible power of a strictly incidence-supported nilpotent matrix. Its unique saturated-chain identity is valid over every finite field and ensures that the maximum-rank entry has no additive cancellation; rank is always zero or one. The exact probability uses independence only of distinct Hasse-edge coordinates, and the number of other coordinates is incorporated in the radical cardinality. The concave iteration and reciprocal-difference arguments establish the fixed-point alternatives and the critical constant. Direct finite matrix-power tests support the result but do not replace the proof.

## Originality

**PASS within examined coverage.** Gargate–Gargate (arXiv:1907.06805) give finite incidence-algebra involution counts. Fuchs–Kirillov (arXiv:2204.00066) give unrestricted triangular Jordan-type counts, and Van Peski (arXiv:2407.19578) gives random full-triangular rank results. None of the inspected scope statements determines the conditional maximal-power event for the tree ancestry zero-pattern. The classical bond-percolation survival recurrence is expressly excluded from originality as an isolated probabilistic result; the claimed synthesis is its algebraic maximal-block interpretation and exact finite-field full-radical census. Priority in older poset-algebra literature remains a concrete risk; no independent historical originality certificate is claimed.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The highest Jordan block is a natural structural invariant of radical elements. The theorem completely counts its presence over all rooted-tree posets and finite fields, gives a linear-time exact recurrence, proves its maximal multiplicity is at most one, and locates a sharp binary-field critical threshold in a growing-tree family. This is not a classification of all lower Jordan blocks.
