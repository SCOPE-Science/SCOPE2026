In a rooted-tree incidence algebra over \(\mathbb F_q\), the radical's natural incidence matrix has at most one maximal Jordan block, and its exact occurrence probability is the survival probability of nonzero Hasse-edge paths. For full binary trees, this probability is asymptotic to \(4/h\) over \(\mathbb F_2\), but approaches \(q(q-2)/(q-1)^2>0\) for every \(q\ge3\), with exact integer counts at every finite height.
