# Mathematical review: maximal mutual-annihilation cliques and all clique counts

## Correctness — PASS
The dyadic product formula proves that rank-one square-zero commutation implies two-sided zero products, including the scalar-multiple boundary. The image span of any clique determines an enclosing complete family, and the double-annihilator argument shows that each family is inclusion-maximal with a unique image span. Projective scalar factoring gives its exact size and the Grassmannian count. Every finite clique has unique image and covector spans; the two subspace Möbius inversions select both spans exactly and count subsets (not ordered tuples). The empty clique is handled separately. Exhaustive literal-product tests across six prime-field cases independently agree with all coefficients. The proof works for arbitrary prime powers without relying on extrapolation.

## Value — PASS
The theorem gives a complete natural graph invariant for a nontrivial algebraically defined graph at **all** finite-field sizes and all matrix dimensions. Maximal cliques are canonically parameterized by an entire Grassmannian, and their cardinality stratification has a sharp middle-dimension extremum. The clique polynomial resolves not just the maximum or triangles but all face numbers of the flag complex, by a nontrivial double Möbius inversion. These are structurally motivated invariants for commuting and zero-product problems, not an arbitrary numerical slice.
