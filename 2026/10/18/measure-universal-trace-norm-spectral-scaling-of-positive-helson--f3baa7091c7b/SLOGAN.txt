Every finite positive Helson moment measure scaled toward the Dirichlet boundary \(\tfrac12\), under the single condition \(\int(2a)^{-1}d\nu(a)<\infty\), has a **complete trace-norm spectral limit** given by the Cauchy kernel \(1/(a+b)\) on \(L^2(\nu)\), with an explicit universal \(O(\sqrt\varepsilon)\) error. The Helson matrices nevertheless converge strongly to zero on \(\ell^2\) while their nonzero norms survive, and their traces have the universal Euler correction \(\gamma_{\mathrm E}\varepsilon\nu((0,\infty))\).
