For every odd prime \(p\), all four-frequency Fourier interpolation minors of a centered four-point aperture in \(\mathbb Z_2\times\mathbb F_p\) have rank three or four: the singular sets are exactly parity-homogeneous quartets and balanced quartets whose two frequency pairs have equal sums. Each aperture has precisely \(2\binom p4+p(p-1)^2/4\) singular frequency partners, of which exactly \(p(p-1)^2/4\) have no proportional rows or columns.
