For \(n\ge4\), the minimal forts of the triangular graph \(L(K_n)\) are exactly spanning star forests and nonspanning cycles, giving a complete enumerator. The fractional fort-cover optimum is \(\binom n2/3\) for \(n\ge5\); Steiner triple systems attain the matching disjoint-fort packing bound at all admissible orders \(n\ge7\).
