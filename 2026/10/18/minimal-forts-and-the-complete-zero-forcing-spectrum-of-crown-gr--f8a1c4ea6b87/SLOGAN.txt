On the crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M_n\) for \(n\ge3\), every minimal fort is either a triple in one bipartition class or two complete deleted-matching pairs; this gives the entire zero forcing polynomial and the exact count of minimum sets. The fort packing number is \(\lfloor2n/3\rfloor\) while fractional zero forcing is \(2n/3\), with an integral-to-fractional zero-forcing ratio tending to three.
