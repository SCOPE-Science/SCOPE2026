For cyclic Gabor systems using half the modulation frequencies and \(k\) consecutive translations, no constant-amplitude window can be tight when \(k\) is odd. When \(k\) is even, the least phase-shift alphabet has order \(2\), \(3\), or \(4\): the ternary case occurs exactly when the half-length is odd and \(3\mid\gcd(M,k/2)\), while binary phases suffice when \(M\) is even.
