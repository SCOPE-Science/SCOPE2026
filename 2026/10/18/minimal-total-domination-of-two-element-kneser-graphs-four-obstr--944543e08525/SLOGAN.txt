Every inclusion-minimal total dominating set of \(K(n,2)\) is a three-edge matching, a triangle plus an isolated edge, a five-cycle, or a four-clique in the underlying complete graph, giving \(P_n(z)=15\binom n6z^3+10\binom n5z^4+12\binom n5z^5+\binom n4z^6\). Its minimal-total-domination coefficients first lose log-concavity at \(n=9\) and first lose unimodality at \(n=10\), with both failures persisting thereafter.
