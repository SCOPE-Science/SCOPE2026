The smallest energy-sensitive Kawasaki–Ising ring has an exact two-temperature stationary law: every strict bath contrast dissipates heat, but positive spin alignment at the heated bond appears only above the sharp threshold \(p>q(1+q)/(1-q)\), possible only for \(q<\sqrt2-1\). Its infinite-hot-bath entropy production has a unique optimum strictly inside that alignment region, illustrating a finite-size distinction between irreversibility and local ordering.
