On the rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) with \(1\le m\le n\), the minimum Italian dominating weight is \(\min\{n,2m\}\), and every minimizer is either a surjective one-per-column placement of \(1\)'s or an independent weight-two configuration in each row. Their exact multiplicities exhibit a sharp structural transition at \(n=2m\), where both families coexist with an explicit intersection correction.
