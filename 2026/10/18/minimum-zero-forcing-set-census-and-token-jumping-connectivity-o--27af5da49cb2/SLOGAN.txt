For powers \(C_n^k\) of cycles, minimum ordinary zero-forcing sets admit a complete three-regime classification, with exactly \(n\), \(nk\), or \(n(2k-1)\) optima according to whether \(n\le2k+1\), \(n=2k+2\), or \(n\ge2k+3\). Their one-vertex-exchange reconfiguration graph is connected for every \(n\ge3\) and \(k\ge1\).
