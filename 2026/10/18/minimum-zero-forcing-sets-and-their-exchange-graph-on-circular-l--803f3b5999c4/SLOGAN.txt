For every circular ladder \(C_n\square K_2\) with \(n\ge6\), all minimum ordinary zero-forcing sets fall into five explicit local pattern families totalling \(13n\). Their single-vertex replacement graph is connected, has \(48n\) edges, and has exactly \(4n\), \(8n\), and \(n\) vertices of degrees \(5\), \(8\), and \(12\), respectively.
