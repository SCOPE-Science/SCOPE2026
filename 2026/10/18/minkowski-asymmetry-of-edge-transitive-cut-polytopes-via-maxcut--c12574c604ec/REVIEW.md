## Correctness — PASS

The proof checks the full-dimensionality identity, group averaging for a feasible center, the exact reflection-to-diagonal equivalence, and the matching MaxCut upper and automorphism-orbit lower bounds. It uses the tight MaxCut supporting inequality after *each* switch and orthogonality of distinct edge parity characters to force uniqueness; this rules out nonuniform minimizing centers. The equal-part MaxCut calculation uses coordinatewise affine dependence and not an unsupported symmetry assumption. The exact-rational verifier checks 174 edge-transitive graph instances and 60 balanced multipartite pairs. Computation is supporting evidence, not proof in infinite dimension.

Nikolaev's 2024 study establishes combinatorial one-skeleton distances and clique estimates, not Minkowski asymmetry. The 2015 Deza–Dutour Sikirić facet census and the original Barahona–Mahjoub framework contain switching and symmetry facts but not the explicit edge-transitive MaxCut ratio or uniqueness argument. The earlier minor-free odd-girth formula does not cover \(K_r\) for \(r\ge5\), where the exact asymmetry is unbounded with \(r\). The checked published-finding corpus entries concern cut-cone metric distortions or asymmetries of different bodies. A global theorem hidden in a broad cut-polytope monograph or under other coefficient-of-symmetry terminology remains a genuine residual priority risk; no independent bibliographic certification is claimed.

## Value — PASS

The classification identifies the full affine reflection geometry of an infinite cut-polytope class by the single natural graph invariant MaxCut and proves center uniqueness. The balanced multipartite corollary gives an exact parity-sensitive law invariant under arbitrary equal vertex blowup. The claim does not require a paper-sized application to merit value: it is a complete natural affine-invariant classification and structurally explains how graph symmetry converts optimization bounds into geometric optimality.
