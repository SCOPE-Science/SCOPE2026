For any finite simple edge-transitive graph, the cut polytope has unique Minkowski center \(\tfrac12\mathbf1_E\), and its exact asymmetry is \(|E|/(2\operatorname{MaxCut}(G)-|E|)\). Balanced complete \(r\)-partite graphs have asymmetry \(r-1\) for even \(r\) and \(r\) for odd \(r\), independently of the size of every part.
