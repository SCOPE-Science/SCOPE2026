The exact endpoint norm of a cumulative ANOVA projector on mixed diffuse and fair-binary inputs is a finite sum of singular-face total variations. For fixed interaction order \(k\), the sharp transition occurs at \(m\asymp\sqrt n\), with a shifted-Hermite profile \(F_k(\lambda)\) connecting Boolean growth \(n^{k/2}\) to diffuse growth \(2^km^k/k!\).
