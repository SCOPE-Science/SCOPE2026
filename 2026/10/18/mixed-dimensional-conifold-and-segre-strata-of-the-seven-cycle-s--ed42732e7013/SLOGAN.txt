The normal-fan toric sevenfold of \(\operatorname{STAB}(C_7)\) has a non-equidimensional reduced singular locus: seven fourfolds with generic conifold transversals and seven \(\mathbb P^3\) components with generic rank-one \(2\times3\) determinantal transversals. An exact facet-and-lattice census finds \(322\) singular torus orbits and no singular simplicial cones.
