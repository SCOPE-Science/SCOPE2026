For every \(n\ge3\), the first mixed-dimensional Euclidean Rips stage of the unrescaled type-\(B_n\) roots, at \(\sqrt2\le r<\sqrt3\), is exactly \(\left(\bigvee^{8\binom n3}S^2\right)\vee S^{n-1}\). A crosspolytope-skeleton and homotopy-pushout decomposition proves this integral homotopy type uniformly; the preceding stage is a wedge of \(2n(n-2)+1\) circles.
