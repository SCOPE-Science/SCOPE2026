The multipath complex of the fully bidirected five-vertex theta graph \(\overleftrightarrow{K}_{2,3}\) has integral homotopy type \(S^1\vee\bigvee^{6}S^2\), established by an exhaustive acyclic Morse matching with vanishing integral attaching degrees. Its complete rational \(S_2\times S_3\times C_2\) homology character is determined, distinguishing hub interchange, branch permutations and global direction reversal.
