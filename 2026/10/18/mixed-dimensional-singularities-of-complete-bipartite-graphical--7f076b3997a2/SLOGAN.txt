For the normal-fan toric variety of the complete-bipartite graphical zonotope \(K_{2,m}\), every \(m\ge3\) produces a non-pure singular locus: exactly \(2^{m-1}\binom m2\) components \((\mathbb P^1)^{m-2}\) with generic transverse conifold type, alongside \(2^m\) isolated singularities with rank-one \(2\times m\) Segre-cone charts. The singular support also equals the non-\(\mathbb Q\)-factorial locus.
