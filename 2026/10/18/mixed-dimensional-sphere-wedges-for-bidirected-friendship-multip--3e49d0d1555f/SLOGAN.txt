For every \(n\ge2\), the directed linear-forest (multipath) complex of \(n\) bidirected triangles sharing a single vertex has integral homotopy type \(S^{n-1}\vee\bigvee^{2n}S^n\). A uniform discrete-Morse matching and explicit independent top cycles prove the mixed-dimensional sphere wedge; the single-triangle boundary case is two disjoint circles.
