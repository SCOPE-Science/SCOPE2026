The ordinary independence complex of the twenty-four-vertex seven-hexagon coronene graph has reduced integral homology \(\widetilde H_5\cong\mathbb Z\) and \(\widetilde H_7\cong\mathbb Z^2\), with no other reduced homology. Its full hexagonal-dihedral rational homology action is a reflection sign line in degree five and an inflated two-dimensional triangle-reflection representation in degree seven, giving a complete rational homology formula for every subgroup quotient.
