For the mixed-length full-Fock symbol \(L_1+L_{22}+L_1^*+L_{22}^*\), letter-length finite-section eigenvalue laws converge in total variation to an explicit pure-point mixture of finite Fibonacci-type tree spectra. The limiting mass at zero is exactly \((d-1)(d^2-d-1)/(d^3-1)\), hence \(1/7\) for two letters.
