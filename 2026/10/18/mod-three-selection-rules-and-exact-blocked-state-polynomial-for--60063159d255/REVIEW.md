# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact local spin-flip energy is an odd sum of three interaction variables, so every blocked configuration is a cyclic string of defects separated by at least three sites. The missing finite-ring ingredient is a direct rank computation over the binary field: the periodic spin-to-interaction map is bijective except at lengths divisible by three, where its image is selected by equal residue parities and each allowed word has four preimages. A finite-state transfer and character projection give the asserted full polynomial. The cubic characteristic polynomials of the two transfer products give the entropy and sharp subleading term. A standalone direct-Hamiltonian checker passed the recorded finite comparisons; that experiment is not substituted for the proof.

## Originality — PASS, with bibliographic limitations

Kisker, Rieger, and Schreckenberg, arXiv:cond-mat/9408060 (1994), already establish the local defect-blocking rule and leading exponential rate. Those are expressly prior art. The additional result is the exact length-dependent image projection and the resulting **physical spin** polynomial, finite excitation rules, and plastic-constant subleading contribution. De Smedt, Godrèche, and Luck, arXiv:cond-mat/0202465 (2002), treat a priori blocked-state ensembles of different kinetically constrained spin Hamiltonians. Searches of the accessible mathematical-results records did not locate an equivalent or broader three-spin-ring theorem. A 1989 random-bond Ising metastability study was accessible only at abstract level; unindexed transfer-matrix or periodic-code analogues remain possible.

## Value — PASS

A priori blocked-state counts are central to quantitative studies of slow freezing and metastability. A complete finite-ring count is useful precisely because a formal isolated-defect census can differ from the number of realizable periodic spins. The result identifies a sharp arithmetic selection rule for the first excited band and a genuinely exponential, though thermodynamically subleading, size-dependent correction. It is a structural all-size finite-volume classification, not simply a new decimal estimate.

## Scope and limitations

The statement is restricted to classical periodic consecutive three-spin ferromagnets and strict one-spin blocking. It says nothing about kinetic basin probabilities, finite-temperature aging, or an independent external validation of novelty. Some bibliographic uncertainty remains.
