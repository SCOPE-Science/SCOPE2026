A periodic three-spin Ising ring has an exact, length-class-dependent polynomial counting all zero-temperature one-flip blocked configurations: when the length is divisible by three, periodic-spin compatibility removes every isolated interaction defect and creates a subleading growth term governed by the plastic constant. Consequently, the lowest physical excitation jumps from \(2J\) to \(4J\) on every sufficiently long multiple-of-three ring, even though the leading blocked-state entropy is unchanged.
