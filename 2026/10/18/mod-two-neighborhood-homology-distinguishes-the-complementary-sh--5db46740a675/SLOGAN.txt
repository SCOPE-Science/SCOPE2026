The complementary open-neighborhood complexes of the cospectral Shrikhande and four-by-four rook graphs have different complete mod-two homology: \(\widetilde H_4(\mathcal N(\overline S);\mathbb F_2)\cong\mathbb F_2^{11}\), while \(\widetilde H_2(\mathcal N(\overline R);\mathbb F_2)\cong\mathbb F_2^2\) and \(\widetilde H_4(\mathcal N(\overline R);\mathbb F_2)\cong\mathbb F_2\), with every other reduced group zero. Exact exhaustive binary boundary calculations establish both classifications.
