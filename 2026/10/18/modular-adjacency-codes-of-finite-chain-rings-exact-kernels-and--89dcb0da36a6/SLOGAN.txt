For every finite commutative chain ring of nilpotency length \(m\ge3\) and residue-field order \(q=p^f\), its loopless zero-divisor adjacency row code over \(\mathbb F_p\) has exact rank \(q^{\lfloor m/2\rfloor}\), minimum distance \(1\), dual distance \(2\), and Euclidean hull dimension \(1\). An explicit valuation-layer parametrization describes the entire dual code, and every nonzero socle coordinate supports a weight-one codeword, including in the previously studied length-four case.
