For the dihedral quandle of odd prime order \(p\) over a field of characteristic \(p\), all nonzero idempotents in its quandle algebra form a Takasaki quandle of dimension \(1+\lfloor(p-2)/\operatorname{lcm}(2,\operatorname{ord}_p(2))\rfloor\). Over \(\mathbb F_q\) this gives exactly \(1+q^{\kappa}\) idempotents and a first prime-field nontrivial-idempotent jump at \(p=17\).
