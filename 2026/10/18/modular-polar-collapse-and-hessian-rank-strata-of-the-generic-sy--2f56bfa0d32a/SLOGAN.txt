For the generic symmetric determinant, characteristic two collapses the polar image to a diagonal projective space and, in even matrix size, creates an additional linear base component of invertible alternating matrices outside the determinantal hypersurface. A complete characteristic-dependent classification of all Hessian matrix ranks—including an odd-characteristic Hessian-vanishing but Cremona phenomenon—follows from an integral Hessian identity and rank-stratum normal forms.
