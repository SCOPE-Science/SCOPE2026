A common-noise Itô diffusion reproduces **all first and second joint moments at every time** of a simultaneously multiplicatively reset affine system, at arbitrary reset rates and strengths, with an explicit full-rank stationary Cauchy covariance. Yet under complete Poisson resetting in dimension at least two, the stationary laws are mutually singular—total-variation distance exactly \(1\)—although their means and covariance matrices coincide.
