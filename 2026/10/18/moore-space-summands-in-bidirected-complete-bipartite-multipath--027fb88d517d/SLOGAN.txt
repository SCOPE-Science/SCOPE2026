The multipath complexes of the bidirected complete bipartite graphs \(K_{2,n}\) have explicit integral homotopy types for \(n=3,4,5,6\); the middle two contain a mod-two Moore space, whereas the endpoints are wedges of spheres. The torsion examples are unstable dynamical modules, giving counterexamples to the published torsion-implies-stability conjecture for multipath complexes.
