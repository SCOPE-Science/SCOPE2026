For every \(n\ge5\), the projective hypersurface defined by the next-to-banana elementary symmetric polynomial \(e_{n-2}\) has an explicit polynomial class in the Grothendieck ring, obtained by turning its full-coordinate stratum into a split quadric under coordinate inversion. Over the complex numbers, its Euler characteristic is \(2^{n-1}-n\) for even \(n\), and \(-2^{n-1}+3n\) for odd \(n\).
