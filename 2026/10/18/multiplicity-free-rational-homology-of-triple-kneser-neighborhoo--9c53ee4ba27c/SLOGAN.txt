For every \(n\ge7\), the top rational homology of the ordinary neighborhood complex of \(\mathrm{KG}(n,3)\) is a multiplicity-free sum of exactly three irreducible \(\mathfrak S_n\)-modules: \(S^{(5,1^{n-5})}\oplus S^{(4,2,1^{n-6})}\oplus S^{(3,3,1^{n-6})}\). The character of every permutation is an explicit sign-twisted quartic in its numbers of fixed points and two-cycles, refining the previously known nonequivariant sphere-wedge classification.
