For an almost-abelian Lie algebra whose defining operator has at least two distinct nonlinear irreducible factors, the exact subalgebra-permutability count depends only on factor degrees; exterior–exterior permutable pairs factor as \(\prod_i(1+3q^{d_i})\). The commutativity probability tends to \(1/4\) when the ideal dimension is four and to \(1\) in every dimension at least five.
