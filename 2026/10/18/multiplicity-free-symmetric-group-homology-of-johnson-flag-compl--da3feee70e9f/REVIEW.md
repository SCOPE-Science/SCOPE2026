# Scientific assessment: symmetric-group homology of Johnson flag complexes

**Correctness — PASS.** For every \(n\ge2k\ge8\), the two Johnson maximal-clique types account for all faces with at least three vertices exactly once. The elementary reductions and the simply connected hypersimplex two-skeleton establish homology concentration in degree two. The equivariant reduced Euler chain identity is sign-sensitive and follows from a complete accounting of all star/top simplex contributions and the orientation of Johnson edges. Its Frobenius characteristic is reduced by a fully displayed Jacobi–Trudi telescoping identity to distinct Schur functions. Exact finite computations independently check all conjugacy-class characters of \(S_8\), core boundary ranks, and Pieri expansions; these tests supplement, rather than replace, the unbounded proof.

**Originality — PASS with residual risk.** The original hypersimplex graph paper supplies the geometric object; Shuldiner–Oldford supplies the clique classification. The closest existing Johnson scale-one result proves only the nonequivariant sphere-wedge homotopy type, so it fixes the rank but not the homology character. A second closest result computes the character only for the constant-weight-three layer \(k=3\), expressly excluded here. A 2026 paper on full Boolean-cube Rips homology studies a different simplicial complex (all weight layers together), whose homology representation does not formally restrict to the induced one-layer complex. No inspected primary result or indexed database record was found to determine all the displayed \(k\ge4\) irreducible constituents. A differently indexed equivalent theorem remains possible.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** This uniform all-parameter result reveals a multiplicity-free family of \(k-1\) irreducible representations in a canonical topological complex. It distinguishes symmetries invisible to the known Betti number and supplies a complete description of the \(S_n\) and \(A_n\) invariant subspaces; the self-complementary \(J(8,4)\) case already has three distinct large constituents. It is a genuine structure theorem, not an arbitrary parameter increment, and does not claim general persistence or integral equivariant data.

**Closest literature and limitations.** Rispoli, arXiv:0811.2981; Shuldiner–Oldford, arXiv:2208.12710; the two published Johnson-scale-one and weight-three equivariant records cited in `RESULT.md`; Galetto–Montaño–Wellner, arXiv:2606.20784. The full Boolean-cube paper's introductory theorem and scope were inspected, not its complete 41 pages. Less-indexed equivalent material may remain. This is not a claim of external scientific certification.
