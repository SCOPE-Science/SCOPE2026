The degree-two rational homology of the scale-one Johnson flag complex \(\operatorname{Cl}(J(n,k))\) is the multiplicity-free \(S_n\)-representation \(\bigoplus_{j=1}^{k-1}S^{(n-j-2,j,1,1)}\) for every \(n\ge2k\ge8\). This determines the complete symmetry action beyond previously known sphere counts, with no nonzero class invariant under \(S_n\) or \(A_n\).
