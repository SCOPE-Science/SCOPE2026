A binary threshold observable of a Chebyshev map has an exact alternating, piecewise-quadratic covariance profile across threshold locations at every lag, with zeros precisely on the corresponding inverse-branch grid. Remarkably, zero lag-one covariance forces the *entire* threshold process to be i.i.d. Bernoulli, including \(q-1\) distinct, non-generating factors when the map has \(q\ge3\) branches.
