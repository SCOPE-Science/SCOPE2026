Deleting a non-spanning matching from a complete bipartite graph admits an exact five-parameter classification and full cardinality enumeration of mutually visible vertex sets. For \(a,b\ge3\) and \(1\le t<\min\{a,b\}\), the maximum size remains \(a+b-2\), but its exact multiplicity is \((a-t)(b-t)+\mathbf1_{\{t=1\}}(1+2\mathbf1_{\{a=3\}}+2\mathbf1_{\{b=3\}})\).
