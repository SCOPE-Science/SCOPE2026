In a weighted bipartite conformist–rebel game, every Nash equilibrium—not just the fully mixed ones—is a point of a product of two explicit matrix-nullspace polytopes, of dimension \(c+r-2\operatorname{rank}(W)\). With independent atomless positive edge weights, that dimension is the number of vertices left unmatched by a maximum matching; a perfect matching makes uniform mixing uniquely optimal, while pure equilibria almost surely disappear.
