For a finite Boolean algebra with \(m\) equally long \(d\)-cycles of atoms and a named cyclic automorphism, complete quantifier-free \(k\)-types correspond exactly to nonempty sets of at most \(m\) necklaces on \(2^k\) colors. Their count determines \((d,m)\) from the entire arity spectrum except for the unique collision between two fixed atoms and one transposed pair; automorphism invariance coincides with quantifier-free definability exactly when \(m\leq2\).
