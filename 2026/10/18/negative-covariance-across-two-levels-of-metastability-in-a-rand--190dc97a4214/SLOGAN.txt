For an iid continuously disordered ferromagnetic Ising ring, the logarithms of the numbers of strictly one-spin- and two-spin-flip-stable states are *negatively* correlated, with exact covariance \(-n(\log2)^2/315\) for every \(n\ge11\), even though every two-spin-stable state is one-spin-stable. The entire two-level fluctuation vector has a universal nondegenerate Gaussian limit with an explicit rational covariance matrix.
