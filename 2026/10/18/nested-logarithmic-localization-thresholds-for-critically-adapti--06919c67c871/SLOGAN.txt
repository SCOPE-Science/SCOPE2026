At the critical inverse-square resetting strength \(r(x)\sim2D/|x|^2\), the first logarithmic correction changes whether a one-dimensional Brownian reset process has any stationary probability: the threshold coefficient is exactly \(3\). At that boundary, a second correction of order \(1/[|x|^2\log|x|\log\log|x|]\) produces a second sharp threshold, again with coefficient \(3\), and determines logarithmic stationary-tail exponents when localization occurs.
