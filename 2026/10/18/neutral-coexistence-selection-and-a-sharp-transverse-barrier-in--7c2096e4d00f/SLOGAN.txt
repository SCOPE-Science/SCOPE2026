At equal rates, every strictly positive orbit of the lottery–Ricker competition map converges to a single point on the neutral equilibrium segment. For \(r>2\), non-eventually-landing trajectories have a sharp lower selection threshold \(x_\infty/r\geq(r-2)/(r-1)\), although one-step preimages can land on every interior equilibrium, even a transversely repelling one.
