For every \(n,m\ge1\), the probability that a product of \(m\) independent uniform matrices in \(M_n(\mathbb F_q)\) is nilpotent is exactly \(1-(1-q^{-n})^m\)—the same as the zero-product probability in \(\mathbb F_{q^n}\). The formula multiplies across simple factors, giving the complete law for all finite semisimple rings.
