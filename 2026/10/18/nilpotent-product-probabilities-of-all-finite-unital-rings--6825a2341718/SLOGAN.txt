For every nonzero finite associative unital ring, the probability that two uniform elements have a nilpotent product is an explicit product determined solely by the finite matrix-ring factors of its semisimple quotient. In particular, \(M_n(\mathbb F_q)\) has exactly the same probability, \((2q^n-1)/q^{2n}\), as the field \(\mathbb F_{q^n}\); the maximum \(3/4\) is attained by noncommutative local rings as well as commutative ones.
