# Scientific review

## Correctness (PASS)
The object is the cubic bipartite incidence graph of the nine points and nine nonvertical lines over \(\mathbb F_3\). A selected paired-dominating set has equal point and line counts. Degree gives the lower bound \(\gamma_{\rm pr}\ge6\), and nine explicit six-vertex core constructions achieve it. Exhaustive enumeration with independent bit-mask matching and coordinate-bijection matching finds exactly these nine optimum sets. The eight-vertex finite classification is certified both by scanning all \(126^2\) candidate balanced selections and by reducing the four-point side to four orbits under 108 affine automorphisms; the full nine-core/24-extension construction agrees exactly with the scan. Direct versus crossed matching routes justify the four allowed extra lines for each extra point. The small graph is \(K_{6,6}-C_{12}\); its line graph has 24 vertices, degree 6, 72 edges and diameter 3. Pairwise core invariance excludes intercomponent exchanges. Each embedded verifier executed successfully and its included output matches the replayed result. The proof is exhaustive for this fixed finite graph and does not extrapolate.

## Originality (PASS, bounded-search judgement)
The 1998 foundational definition does not assert this particular reconfiguration. Mynhardt–Teshima (2017), inspected as full arXiv HTML, treat minimum-cardinality paired domination and token *sliding* in a universal realization framework; neither the fixed Pappus graph nor its eight-vertex token-*jumping* graph is classified. The 2023 dissertation's contents, introductory definition, chapter-four scope, and searchable document were examined: it treats paired domination and minimum \(\gamma\)-paired dominating graphs on listed standard families, not this Pappus eight-vertex graph. Related mathematical-record findings about a different twelve-vertex cubic graph concern optimum scalar paired-domination values, not a covering statement. Explicit searches for the Pappus graph with paired domination, jump reconfiguration, and the relevant line-graph parameters returned no covering result. This is not a proof of worldwide publication priority; an unindexed equivalent result remains possible. The publisher full text of the 1998 origin article was not accessible, and no negative whole-paper assertion is based on its abstract.

## Value (PASS)
The Pappus graph is the smallest nontrivial biaffine incidence graph beyond the eight-cycle, and paired domination adds a genuine matching constraint to ordinary guarding. The result isolates an exact first-augmentation phenomenon: all optimum placements are immobile, yet adding precisely two selected vertices creates large mobile regions, which remain separated by a uniquely recoverable six-vertex geometric core. Identification with nine copies of the line graph of a dense bipartite graph missing a Hamilton cycle gives a structural description and exact reachability/diameter, not just a computed count. The fact is a complete, natural finite classification; it requires no asserted downstream theorem to be meaningful.
