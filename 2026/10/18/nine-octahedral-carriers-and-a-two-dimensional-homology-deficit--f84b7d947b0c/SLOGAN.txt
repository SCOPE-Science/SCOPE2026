The independence complex of the eighteen-vertex generalized Petersen graph \(\mathrm{GP}(9,3)\) is a wedge of eleven four-spheres, but its exactly nine smallest induced homology spheres account for only nine of the eleven rational generators. Under the full dihedral symmetry, these nine spheres form the permutation representation, leaving a genuinely different two-dimensional representation factoring through the triangle group \(D_3\).
