# Review: nine three-spheres and signed cube symmetry

## Correctness — PASS
The result concerns the twelve edges of the three-dimensional cube, with edge subsets forbidden precisely when containing any one of its 44 three-edge matchings. Enumeration of all 4096 subsets gives 601 augmented faces. The specified deterministic elementary face matching has 295 nonempty matched pairs, exactly one critical vertex, and nine critical 3-cells; its entire 600-face directed Hasse diagram is acyclic. Ordinary discrete Morse theory therefore supplies a CW complex with precisely one zero-cell and nine 3-cells, forcing the integral homotopy equivalence to a ninefold wedge of three-spheres, without unverified attaching maps. A separate exact mod-two boundary computation agrees. Every one of the 48 signed coordinate cube symmetries has a directly computed simplicial Lefschetz trace agreeing with the proposed nine-dimensional rational character. Since the rational representation category of this finite group is semisimple, equality of characters gives the complete module isomorphism. All assertions have reproducible finite evidence in `verify.py` and `verification_output.json`.

## Originality — PASS, subject to residual discovery risk
Linusson–Shareshian–Welker prove the nine-sphere wedge for the *complete* bipartite graph \(K_{4,4}\), not for the matching-deleted cube subgraph, and do not give the cube's full signed-coordinate automorphism module. Shan–Wang prove top-degree homology injection under bipartite edge addition, which makes nine an upper bound on the cube's degree-three rational rank, but does not establish equality, integrality, complete homotopy type, or the character. Holmsen–Lee give general vanishing, not the exact rank or equivariant character. Closely related published records describe the cube's ordinary matching, cut, and discrete-Morse complexes, all non-equivalent constructions. Searches of source titles, alternative objects, parameters, implications, and database records revealed no prior exact statement. The full available texts of the two most relevant research sources were materially examined for precise hypotheses and conclusions. No failure of retrieval was taken as evidence of originality. An unindexed prior example or an undiscovered equivalent formulation remains possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The three-dimensional cube is the smallest noncycle hypercube and a natural highly symmetric, matching-deleted \(4\)-by-\(4\) bipartite graph. It gives a structurally meaningful equality case in a new edge-addition comparison: deleting a perfect matching from \(K_{4,4}\) preserves the whole nonequivariant non-matching homotopy type. The complete rational action of all 48 automorphisms is stricter information than a Betti number and is not implied by prior general Leray bounds or the complete-bipartite homotopy theorem. Both parts form one exact graph-complex classification, not a mere benchmark routine variation.
