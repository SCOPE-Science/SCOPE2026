The three-non-matching complex of the eight-vertex cube is integrally homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^9S^3\), despite the cube being a matching-deleted proper subgraph of \(K_{4,4}\). Its full signed-cube symmetry on rational third homology is \(P\oplus(\epsilon\otimes U)\oplus(\epsilon\otimes\bigwedge^2U)\), refining the nine-dimensional Betti number into three natural representations.
