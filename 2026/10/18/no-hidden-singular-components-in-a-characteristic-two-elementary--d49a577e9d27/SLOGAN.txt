For the characteristic-two quartic \(V(e_4)\) with \(n\ge11\) and \(n\equiv3\pmod8\), every singular point lies on one of the previously identified three-block partition planes of block sizes \(1\pmod8\): there are no hidden singular curves or points beyond these components. The complete reduced singular support is a pure union of \(N_n\) planes; at \(n=11\) its 55 planes have the triangular graph \(T(11)\) as their line-intersection graph.
