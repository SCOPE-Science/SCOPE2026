In a two-habitat linear stochastic population with symmetric dispersal and arbitrarily correlated environmental forcing, each patch's stationary variance is monotone or has a single minimum—never a maximum—and both patches cannot have minima. For independent forcing, the exact variance-minimum threshold at habitat 1 is \(r/q>(a^2+4ab+5b^2)/(a^2+4ab+b^2)\); a concrete example has a strictly nonmonotone local variance even though total-abundance variance is constant.
