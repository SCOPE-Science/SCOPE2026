For every pair of distinct primes \(p,q\), the arithmetic subderivative \(D_{\{p,q\}}\) has no nontrivial positive periodic cycle: every positive periodic point is already fixed. The complete periodic set consists of \(p^p m\) and \(q^q m\) with \(\gcd(m,pq)=1\); the fixed-point descriptions were previously known, while their exhaustion of all periodic orbits for every prime pair is the new theorem.
