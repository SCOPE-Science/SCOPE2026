For every \(N\ge5\), the diagonal-hyperplane seventh-power hypersurface \(\sum_i x_i=\sum_i x_i^7=0\) is normal and has only ordinary double points, counted exactly by a Franel-number binomial transform divided by six. All singular points are defined over \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})\), and the numbers of rational versus quadratic closed points are explicit.
