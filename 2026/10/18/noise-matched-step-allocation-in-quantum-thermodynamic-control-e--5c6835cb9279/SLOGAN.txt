For a one-parameter step-equilibrated thermodynamic protocol with local variance \(\phi\), joint optimization of the quadratic noisy-control cost gives the sharp speed law \(\dot x=N\sqrt{\phi(x)}\), rather than constant thermodynamic-metric speed. At constant noise strength the optimal parameter ramp is linear for every positive metric; a thermal-qubit example shows an asymptotic 11.07% metric-speed penalty, and globally optimal integer grids obey the same equal-increment law as noise vanishes.
