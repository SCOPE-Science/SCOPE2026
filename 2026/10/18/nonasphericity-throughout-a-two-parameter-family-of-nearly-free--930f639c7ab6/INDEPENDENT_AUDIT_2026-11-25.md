# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Deconing the line x=0 identifies the projective complement with the affine grid-and-diagonal complement. Along the given deformation, horizontal heights are distinct; s≠0 makes the diagonal transverse to both parallel classes; and excluding each reciprocal vertical coordinate from the extra u_j's leaves exactly the specified shared-index triples. The three directions stay distinct at infinity, so the full projective lattice, not just the finite intersections, is constant. The ordered configuration space is path-connected (one can move into temporary distinct positions and then to the target, avoiding finitely many forbidden points at each move), and affords a piecewise smooth lattice isotopy. In the terminal real arrangement, b=b_q is the unique smallest horizontal height, 0<b<1/2. The open triangle y<1, z<b, y+z>1 has 1-b<y<1 and 0<z<b, so no other line cuts it. At its three vertices the only incident lines are its two sides, including when p=q=2. The simple-triangle obstruction applies, and Randell invariance transfers nonasphericity back.
