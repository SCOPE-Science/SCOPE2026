# Scientific assessment of the complete-bipartite temporal residual

## Correctness — PASS
The proposition quantifies the asymptotic, time-averaged Kolmogorov-consistency defect for one site-localized input on a complete bipartite graph under strictly positive Laplacian-energy dephasing. The proof first reconstructs the energy pinching reduction that yields the established spectral formula \(R=\tfrac12\|(F-F^2)e_v\|_1\), with distinct-eigenvalue spectral projectors. It then resolves all four invariant subspaces of the bipartite Laplacian, including the collision at \(m=n\), calculates the exact three-block vector \((F-F^2)e_v\), determines signs for \(m\le2\) and \(m\ge3\), and differentiates the resulting macroscopic profile to prove the unique integer optimum. Every size and boundary case is covered analytically. An exact-rational projector verifier confirms 70 finite pairs, and an independently assembled eigendecomposition checks 361 more pairs; finite checks corroborate rather than establish the general statement. The result requires \(\gamma>0\), a fixed localized initial position, and the stated limit order.

## Originality — PASS, with explicit uncertainty
The strong overlapping prior result is the universal spectral reduction of Luppi, Benedetti and Smirne [1], especially Appendix B, which **covers the general form of the limiting invariant**, and their nondegenerate three-vertex path example. Those components are credited and excluded from the novelty claim. The exact closed expressions for all \(K_{m,n}\), the sharp size-two versus size-three persistence boundary, and the unique limiting optimizer \(m=5\) were not identified in that paper's complete treatment of complete/cycle graph examples or its appendix. Baptista, Coutinho and Marques [2] treat adjacency-matrix average mixing and uniformity, not this two-time Laplacian invariant. A 2026 Laplacian average-mixing paper [3] concerns the trace rather than the nonlinear column difference, but its complete manuscript could not be examined: it remains a priority risk rather than being characterized as not covering. Relevant publication searches and related record searches revealed no directly equivalent statement; search absence is not proof of priority. The present result is a motivated exact family-wide classification derived using established spectral ideas, not a claim to the general spectral identity.

## Value — PASS
The primary motivating work [1] reports a vanishing \(O(N^{-1})\) energy-dephased temporal residual for its complete-graph and cycle examples. The present all-size law reveals that an expanding highly connected opposite part can instead leave an order-one temporal consistency defect, provided the initial vertex lies in a fixed part of size at least three. The sharp threshold at three and the unique 5-vertex maximizing part connect a concrete graph eigenspace structure to long-time temporal correlations, while the explicit finite-size correction distinguishes genuine limiting persistence from numerically long transients. This is an exact natural-family benchmark, not a general operational robustness theorem.
