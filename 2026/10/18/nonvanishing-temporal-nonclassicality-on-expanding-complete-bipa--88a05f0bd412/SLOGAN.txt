Energy-dephased Laplacian quantum walks on \(K_{m,n}\) retain a nonzero two-time Kolmogorov-consistency defect as \(n\to\infty\) exactly for fixed initial-part sizes \(m\ge3\); the surviving value is \(2(m-1)(m-2)/m^3\). Among fixed integer part sizes, \(m=5\) uniquely maximizes this residue at \(24/125\).
