For a finite meet-semilattice with a top and any finite group, all reflexive compatible relations on their direct product are exactly normal-subgroup labelings of meet-closed supports, with inclusion and congruences determined by the corresponding orders. When the group is nontrivial simple and the semilattice is a chain, the count is independent of the group, begins \(2,17,337,12152,696076\), and gives a strict quantitative refinement of the Clifford DSC bound.
