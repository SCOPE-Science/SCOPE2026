## Correctness
The full-spark coefficient hypothesis ensures that the four quadrics and the fixed complete-intersection curve are smooth. The fixed curve is a genus-17 complete intersection of four quadrics in projective five-space with normal bundle \(\mathcal O_C(1)^2\). The involution has two negative normal eigenvalues, giving transverse \(A_1\) quotient singularities. Blowup and double-cover intersection identities produce the cubic form. The quotient Euler formula, invariant degree-two cohomology, adjunction, and Riemann–Roch establish the Hodge and Chern numbers. A separate exact-arithmetic script checks the numerical derivations; infinite assertions depend on the written proof rather than finite tests. Fundamental-group triviality uses the simply connected blowup and its branched involution quotient.

## Originality
The 2025 public generalized-Fermat quotient research direction precedes the present construction. Hidalgo–Hughes–Leyton-Álvarez establish the generalized-Fermat action and Calabi–Yau geometry; the accessible full text of Billi–Palomino–Sarti explicitly assumes fixed-point-free automorphisms in its classification and reserves nonfree actions for later investigation. Older Humbert–Edge literature already supplies the genus-17 curve, and Hodge numbers \((2,58)\) are already realized in other Calabi–Yau families. The submitted object is not the genus, the already known Hodge pair in isolation, or general blowup theory; it is the particular nonfree crepant quotient with its full cubic and second-Chern pairings. ## Value
The exact cubic intersection polynomial and second-Chern linear form on the rational Picard rank-two quotient-resolution family are natural threefold birational and deformation invariants. They refine an otherwise non-distinguishing Hodge pair and supply a reproducible example in the nonfree generalized-Fermat quotient regime. The computation has a geometric object-level motivation and is not a table row selected solely for its numerical appearance.
