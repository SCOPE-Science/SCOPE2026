The fixed-curve quotient of a smooth diagonal four-quadric Calabi–Yau threefold by a two-coordinate sign involution admits a simply connected crepant resolution with Hodge numbers \((2,58)\). Its hyperplane and exceptional divisors have cubic intersection coefficients \((8,0,-32,-128)\) and second-Chern pairings \((56,64)\), providing a concrete polarized numerical package beyond the already known occurrence of the Hodge pair.
