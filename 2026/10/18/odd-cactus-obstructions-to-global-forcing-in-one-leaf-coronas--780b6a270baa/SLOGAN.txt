For any graph, the maximal-matching global forcing sets of its one-leaf corona are exactly the edge observations whose unobserved core has no even cycle and whose components each hide at most one pendant edge. The resulting minimum is the number of core edges minus the largest odd-cactus cycle rank; the classical corona bound is sharp precisely for bipartite cores, and whiskered paths have \(F_{2n}\) minimum forcing sets on \(n\) core vertices.
