For cycle sums over fields of odd characteristic, a divisor retraction reduces every finite-support division problem to a product of square-zero local algebras \(K\oplus K^a\). Over \(\mathbb F_q\), the precise number of divisible ordered pairs at cutoff \(m=p^a u\) is \(\bigl((q-1)q^{2a+1}+q(q^a-1)+1\bigr)^{\tau(u)}\), with an explicit componentwise solvability test.
