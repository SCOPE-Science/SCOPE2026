For \(n=2^kp^e\) with \(k\ge1\), \(e\ge2\), and odd prime \(p\), a sum of distinct **odd** divisors equals \(\sigma(n)-2n\) precisely when \(p=2^{k+1}-1\) is a Mersenne prime, and the deleted set is uniquely \(\{p,p^2,\ldots,p^{e-1}\}\). Thus each Mersenne prime supports exact odd-only near-perfect omissions of every cardinality from one upward, although the unrestricted omission problem is not classified.
