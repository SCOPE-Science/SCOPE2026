For every \(m\ge2\), a square Cartesian grid of \(m^2\) distinct complex sampling points gives complete linear intensity tomography on the first \(m\) Gaussian-Hermite modes precisely when \(m\) is odd, regardless of node spacings. Every even order has an explicit nonzero Hermitian blind mode, whereas every odd order also guarantees phase retrieval for polynomial signals of degree at most \(m-1\).
