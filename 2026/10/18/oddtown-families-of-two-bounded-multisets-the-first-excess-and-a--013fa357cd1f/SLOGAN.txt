Allowing multiplicity two changes the Oddtown extremal problem: its first five maxima are \(1,2,3,6,7\), so the ordinary \(n\)-element bound first fails on four coordinates. A ten-word six-coordinate construction amplifies on disjoint supports, yielding \(O_2(n)\ge10\lfloor n/6\rfloor+(n\bmod6)\) and asymptotic lower density at least \(5/3\).
