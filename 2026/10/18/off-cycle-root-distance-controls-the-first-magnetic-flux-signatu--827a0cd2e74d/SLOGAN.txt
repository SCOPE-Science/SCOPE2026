For a walker rooted \(d\ge1\) edges away from the unique \(m\)-cycle of a positively weighted unicyclic network, the first generic magnetic-flux effect on the return probability occurs exactly at time order \(m+2d\) for even \(m\), but at order \(2(m+d)\) for odd \(m\). The leading coefficients depend only on the products of cycle and root-path couplings, not on any branches elsewhere in the graph.
