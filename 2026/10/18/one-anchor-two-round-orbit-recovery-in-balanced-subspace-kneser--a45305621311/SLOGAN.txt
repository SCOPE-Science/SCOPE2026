In the balanced subspace-disjointness graph \(qK_{2k:k}\), marking just one \(k\)-subspace makes two rounds of ordinary colour refinement recover **all** \(k+1\) automorphism-orbit classes, given by intersection dimension with the mark. The bound is sharp for \(k\ge3\): one round sees only three classes, while strictly increasing common-neighbour counts separate every intersection dimension in the second.
