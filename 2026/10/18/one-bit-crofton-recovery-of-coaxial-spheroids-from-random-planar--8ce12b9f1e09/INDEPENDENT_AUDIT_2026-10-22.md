# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For two disjoint congruent coaxial spheroids with a,b>0 and d>2a, rotationally averaged plane normals have x=|u_z| uniform [0,1]. Their offset intervals have halfwidth h_b=sqrt(b²+(a²-b²)x²), separation dx, overlap length (2h_b-dx)_+, total union width 4h_b-overlap. Thus P(two disk components)=I/(4J-I) with exact integrals I,J, including the prolate artanh, spherical, and oblate arctan branches. Independently integrated numerical quadratures (a=1,d=3,b=.1,.5,1,1.5,10) with errors ≤1.5e-13 and checked strict numerical increase over 300 log spaced b. The analytic proof is the monotone likelihood-ratio order in x as beta=b² increases together with the decreasing-in-x and increasing-in-beta overlap fraction phi_beta; it gives strict 0→1 and inverse uniqueness. The small/large b rates follow the closed integrals; no inference of monotonicity from samples.
