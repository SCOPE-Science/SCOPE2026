# Scientific review: weighted repulsive four-cycle

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof exhausts the sine-flux equations, derives the four new solutions only for \(t>1\), and proves all stationary points Morse for \(t\ne1\), with complete index counts. The sharp weighted Laplacian and descent inequality establish convergence to the finite critical set. Because the analytic map has Jacobian determinant not identically zero, all iterated preimages of the null local stable manifolds are null even beyond the globally invertible range. The antiphase eigenvalues give the sharp linear stability interval. The 1,852 finite diagnostics supplement rather than replace the proof.

**Originality — PASS subject to bibliographic risk.** Algebraic studies of four-cycle zero-injection power-flow equations already give the complex bound of twelve and a signed-weight realization with twelve real equilibria; neither gives the sharp fully-positive one-edge threshold or Morse-index breakdown. The weighted discrete Huygens source establishes descent generally; the later equal-weight ring paper analyzes a different degenerate parameter. Earlier equivalent positive-weight power-flow classifications remain a risk, and the 2018 continuous-time ring paper was not obtained in full.

**Value — PASS.** The result completely resolves a natural one-edge splitting of three unweighted critical circles, explains which side reaches the maximum real-root count, and proves a.e. convergence on a step interval sharper than the familiar invertibility bound. It is not a generic root-table re-evaluation or an arbitrary narrow numerical slice.

**Limitations.** Excludes \(t=1\) and the endpoint \(h=2/\Lambda_t\). In the descent interval the critical list is the full fixed-point list; additional winding fixed points outside that interval are not classified. No claim of global basin attraction, heteroclinic transversality, or independent certification.
