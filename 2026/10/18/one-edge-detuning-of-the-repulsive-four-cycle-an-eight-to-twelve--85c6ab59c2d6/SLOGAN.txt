For the four-cycle with positive repulsive edge weights \((t,1,1,1)\), the critical-point count jumps sharply from eight to twelve as \(t\) crosses one; the complete Morse-index counts are \((1,3,3,1)\) and \((1,5,5,1)\). For every \(t\ne1\) and \(0<h<2/[t+2+\sqrt{(t-1)^2+1}]\), the alternating antiphase state attracts almost every discrete orbit, with the step threshold sharp for local stability.
