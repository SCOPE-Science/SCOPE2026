Deleting one sample from a two-layer finite-abelian Fourier strip forces every exponential basis to have squared condition number at least \(2n\), although the intact strip has uniformly bounded conditioning; this loss is sharp for every even vertical order and all optimizers are antipodal. The same family admits a complete basis criterion, an exact product formula and classification for every determinant-maximizing frequency set, and an explicit cubic spectral law in odd vertical order.
