For a bounded nonnormal perturbation \(A+itB\) with strictly positive real part, the one-sided defect \(\|(|A+itB|-A)_+\|\) can be exactly quadratic in \(t\), while reversing the sign of \(t\) yields a linear defect. Every finite nilpotent-shift section of the same construction instead has linear defects in both directions, each with leading coefficient \(1/2\).
