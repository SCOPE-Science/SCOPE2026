Every reflective numerical semigroup becomes Arf after at most one Arf-closure step; in the nontrivial case the step fills precisely the \(\lfloor g/a\rfloor\) gaps \(g+a,g+2a,\ldots,g+\lfloor g/a\rfloor a\). Consequently a reflective semigroup is already Arf exactly when it is ordinary or has multiplicity two.
