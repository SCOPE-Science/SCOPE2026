A single adsorption hub has opposite signed order-one effects on final occupancy in wheels and alternating-spoke gears: respectively \(-\lambda(1-e^{-2})/2\) and \(+\lambda e^{-2}\) relative to an otherwise identical long cycle. Yet both hubs increase count variance by order \(m\), with exact coefficients \(\lambda(1-e^{-2})^2/2\) and \(\lambda e^{-4}\) per cycle pair.
