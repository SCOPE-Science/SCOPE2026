For the Carleman Hankel operator with the canonical rank-one exponential perturbation, the unique exterior eigenvalue emerges above \(\pi\) by \(2\gamma^2/\pi\) for positive coupling, but below zero by the exponentially small scale \(2\pi\exp[-\pi^2/(2|\gamma|)]\) for negative coupling. An exact scalar spectral equation explains both scales and identifies two exact nonzero coupling landmarks.
