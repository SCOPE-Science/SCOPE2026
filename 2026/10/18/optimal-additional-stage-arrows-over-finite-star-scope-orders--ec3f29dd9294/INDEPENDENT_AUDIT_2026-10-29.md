# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For every finite rooted star with k>=1 labelled leaves, any extending stage preorder is determined by an arbitrary leaf preorder T and a root-merger flag (possible precisely when T has a least SCC U). If no leaf reaches the root, every nonsingleton leaf SCC needs at least |C| additional internal arcs and every quotient cover needs one; disjoint lower bounds are attained by cycles and cover edges. If U is merged with root, every member u of U needs an additional outgoing root-component arc, met by u->r; fixed root->leaf arcs supply all covers leaving the root class and other SCC cycles/covers give the rest. Mantel triangle-free bound on Hasse cover graph gives f_k(q)=floor(q^2/4)+k-q+min(q,k-q), whose sharp maximum is 1,2,3,5 for k=1..4 and floor(k^2/4) for k>=5; witnesses are complete bipartite height-two orders or two 2-cycles with one cover at k=4. With an upward-closed valuation of p equal to all leaves or all but one leaf, Box_{>=!}p at a leaf detects any changed root edge or leaf comparison under the actual BML one-world semantics. Independently enumerated all 2^(k^2) leaf-origin presentations at k=1..4, yielding respectively 2,7,45,500 distinct preorders, unique modal signatures, and maximal minimum costs 1,2,3,5, each with exact agreement of direct minimization and cost formula. Checked the discrete extremal upper-bound function for every k=2..200. Finite enumeration is corroboration; the unrestricted result rests on the argument.

A finite BML stage structure must extend fixed scope transitions, which are not resources to be re-added.
