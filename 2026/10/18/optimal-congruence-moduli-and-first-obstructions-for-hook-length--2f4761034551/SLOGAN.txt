For the Nekrasov--Okounkov hook-length series and the \(t\)-core partition series, the greatest integer dividing every coefficient difference is exactly \(p^{a+1}\) when \(t=p^a\), and exactly \(1\) otherwise. The stronger prime-power congruence first fails modulo \(p^{a+2}\) at degree \(p^{a-1}\), giving the optimal modulus and the earliest sharpness witness.
