For a functional, inverse-functional, acyclic directed graph with finitely many path components, the exact minimum number of extra edges forced by any linear-order successor completion is \(\max\{0,f-z-1\}\), where \(f\) counts finite paths and \(z\) counts double rays. Infinitely many components always permit completion with no extra edges; the finite-component formula shows precisely why one-way rays cannot serve as substitutes for double-ray separators.
