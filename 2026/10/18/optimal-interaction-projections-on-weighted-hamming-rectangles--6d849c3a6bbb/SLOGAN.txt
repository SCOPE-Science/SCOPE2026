The zero-row- and zero-column-marginal subspace of the real Lipschitz-free space of a weighted Hamming rectangle has minimum projection norm \(2\min\{\alpha,\beta\}\max\{(n-1)/(n\alpha),(m-1)/(m\beta)\}\). It is contractively complemented for all weighted \(2\times2\) rectangles, for the specified sharp weight ratios when exactly one alphabet is binary, and never when both alphabets have size at least three.
