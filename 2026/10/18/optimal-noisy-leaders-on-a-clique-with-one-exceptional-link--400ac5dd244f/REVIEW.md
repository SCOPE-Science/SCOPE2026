# Scientific assessment

**Correctness: PASS.** For every integer graph order at least three, every feasible fixed leader count, positive finite gain, and positive exceptional conductance, two rank-one inverse identities give the exact trace cost for each endpoint orbit. Algebraic strict inequalities establish the weak and reinforced regimes, and a single affine sign expression identifies all reinforced-link ties. The example threshold is exactly \(27/22\). The standalone rational checker independently reproduced 495 parameter instances and 7470 leader subsets; the universal proof is analytical, not extrapolated from these tests.

**Originality: PASS, with residual risk.** Lin, Fardad, and Jovanović establish the stochastic selection objective and generic algorithms; Fitch and Leonard's finite-gain explicit cases concentrate on one and two leaders and on cycles and paths; Patterson, Yi, and Zhang supply a general resistance representation and selected graph-specific solutions, but no exact optimizer law for the perturbed clique at arbitrary finite gain and leader cardinality. The exact three-orbit comparison and link-strength transition are not supplied by those broader formulas. Searches for weighted-clique, missing-link, defective-edge, joint-centrality, and rank-one formulations did not reveal an equivalent prior statement. The possibility of unindexed or differently phrased coverage remains.

**Value: PASS.** A single faulty or reinforced link is a minimal, naturally motivated departure from a fully symmetric communication network. The classification gives a complete robust sensor/leader placement law with all parameters and boundary ties: weakening reverses the optimal location upon adding a second leader, and reinforcement causes a genuine, exactly located change in optimal endpoint occupancy. This is more than evaluation of one numerical instance or of a generic inversion identity.

Scientific scope is limited to one altered edge, homogeneous finite gains, independent equal-intensity disturbances, and exactly specified leader counts. Independent validation has not been performed.
