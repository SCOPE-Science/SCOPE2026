Every four-vertex minimum paired dominating set of \(\mathrm{KG}(n,2)\), for \(n\ge6\), is a four-edge ground family containing a three-edge matching; there are exactly \(180\binom n6+315\binom n7+105\binom n8\) such sets. Their one-vertex-swap reconfiguration graph is connected for every \(n\ge6\).
