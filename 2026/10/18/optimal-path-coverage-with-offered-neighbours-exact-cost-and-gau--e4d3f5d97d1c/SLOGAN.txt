For a path with \(n+1\) vertices, a controller choosing among \(c\ge2\) independent random-neighbour offers minimizes cover time by visiting the nearer endpoint first and then the farther endpoint, giving exact all-start formulas and a worst-start expectation \(3n/[2(1-2^{1-c})]+O_c(1)\), versus order \(n^2\) for one offer. The optimal cover time has a Gaussian \(\sqrt n\)-scale limit; with two offers and central start, \((T-3n)/\sqrt n\Rightarrow N(0,9)\).
