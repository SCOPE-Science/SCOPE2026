For \(m,n\ge4\), every minimum Roman labeling of \(K_m\times K_n\) is a three-defender matching, a three-defender rectangle elbow, a two-defender cross-corner pattern, or one of two sharp four-wide boundary patterns. Their exact number is \(6\binom m3\binom n3+6\binom m2\binom n2+\mathbf1_{\{n=4\}}m\binom n2+\mathbf1_{\{m=4\}}n\binom m2\), refining the previously known minimum weight of \(6\).
