For a deterministic low-density seed bank with any number of depth classes, the growth-optimal allocation of newborn seeds is pure except at explicitly characterized ties: the optimal growth factor is the upper envelope of one affine function per depth. As disturbance frequency varies, the optimal depth switches at most \(n-1\) times, and this bound is attainable for every \(n\ge2\) even when seed survival decreases and germination increases strictly across depth classes.
