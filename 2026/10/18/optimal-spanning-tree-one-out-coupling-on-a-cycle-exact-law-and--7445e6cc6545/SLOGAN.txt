Every rotation-invariant random edge set on a cycle admits an **optimal uniform-spanning-tree coupling** whose edge symmetric difference equals the absolute difference between its missing-edge count and one, pathwise. For biased independent one-out orientations, the resulting exact transport cost is \(np(1-p)-1+2(p^n+(1-p)^n)\), with a Gaussian fixed-bias regime and a Poisson rare-bias regime.
