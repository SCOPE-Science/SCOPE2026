# Verification record
The closed-form mean arises from the continuum backward generator with absorbing outgoing-characteristic conditions. The moment inequalities prove strictness and sharpness for all nonnegative integrable profiles, not only the numerical examples.

To replay the independent finite-dimensional check, use `python verification.py` with NumPy and SciPy. The script constructs the actual Markov generator for a lattice velocity-jump approximation, numerically solves its linear mean-hitting-time equations for grid sizes 40, 80, 160, 320, 640 and 1280, and compares each solution against separately integrated continuum formulas for a uniform profile, an interior layer, and two endpoint layers. It checks the exact sharp bounds at every gridpoint and decreasing generator approximation errors. This is a numerical cross-check; it does not prove the continuum result or establish bibliographic uniqueness.
