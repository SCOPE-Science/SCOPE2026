For each circular-arc function plane \(E_\theta=\operatorname{span}\{1,z\}\), the unique optimally conditioned symmetric two-evaluation design samples at angles \(\pm2\arctan(\sin(\theta/2))\) and has sharp inverse completely bounded norm \(\sqrt{1+\sin^2(\theta/2)}\). The result is optimal among symmetric node pairs, not a claim about arbitrary two-point placements.
