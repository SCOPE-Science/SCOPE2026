The Shrikhande and \(4\times4\) rook graphs have identical strongly regular parameters and the same threshold-three minimum seed size \(4\), yet have respectively \(720\) and \(864\) optimal contagious sets and different token-jumping diameters \(5\) and \(4\). An exact six-orbit versus two-orbit classification and both token-jumping and token-sliding exchange metrics show how activation dynamics distinguish these cospectral graphs.
