For the dihedral quandle \(R_p\) over any characteristic-\(p\) field with odd prime \(p\), the idempotent scheme is a reduced point together with affine space of dimension \(1+\lfloor(p-2)/\operatorname{lcm}(2,\operatorname{ord}_p(2))\rfloor\); its nonzero idempotents themselves form a Takasaki quandle. The first dimension jump occurs at \(p=17\), giving exactly \(290\) idempotents over \(\mathbb F_{17}\).
