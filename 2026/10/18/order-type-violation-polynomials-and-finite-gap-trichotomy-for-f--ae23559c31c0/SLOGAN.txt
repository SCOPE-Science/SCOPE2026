For every \(k\)-variable equation on finite Gödel chains, the exact number of violating assignments is a nonnegative binomial-basis polynomial \(\sum_{d=0}^{k}c_d\binom{n-2}{d}\), with coefficients determined in the single chain \(C_{k+2}\). Such an equation is either universally valid, asymptotically almost surely valid without a finite gap, or has an explicit uniform gap at least \((k+2)^{-k}\) on every finite product of Gödel chains; its limiting violation probability is \(c_k/k!\).
