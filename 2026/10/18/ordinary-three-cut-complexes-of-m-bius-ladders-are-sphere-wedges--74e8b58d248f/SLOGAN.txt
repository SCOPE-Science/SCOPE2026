For every Möbius ladder \(M_{2n}\) with \(n\ge4\), its ordinary three-cut complex is exactly a wedge of \(2n^2-8n+1\) spheres of dimension \(2n-4\), integrally and up to homotopy. A uniform elementary collapse of the Alexander dual shows that this natural cyclic twist has the same sphere-count formula as the distinct circular ladder, including the initial Wagner-graph case \(S^4\).
