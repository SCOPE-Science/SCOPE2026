# Scientific review: ordinary three-cut complexes of Möbius ladders

**Correctness — PASS.** A complete structural proof classifies the dual's faces: a complete graph as one-skeleton, exactly \(6n\) induced-path triangles and \(n\) four-cycle tetrahedra, and no larger faces. Explicit elementary collapses leave a Möbius strip plus exactly \(2n(n-4)\) chords, thus a bouquet of \(2n(n-4)+1\) circles. Integral Alexander duality and a verified connectivity range imply the claimed bouquet of \((2n-4)\)-spheres. Standalone exact small-case computations verify critical combinatorics and Morse attachments. There is no infinite inference from samples.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** The complete, relevant 2024 cut-complex paper determines other graph families. Its conditional Theorem 6.1 cannot imply the present homotopy type, because the requisite codimension-one skeleton is absent. The 2026 Möbius-ladder paper treats independence and perfect matching complexes instead and expressly lists cut complexes as a further problem. Published analogous computations of total cut complexes concern a different functor, and cycle-power theorems concern a different graph family. No equivalent infinite-family ordinary three-cut formula was identified. Unindexed or unpublished equivalent work remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** This is a complete, natural, parameter-uniform answer for the first nontrivial ordinary cut parameter on a classical infinite family of symmetric cubic graphs. It directly addresses an identified research direction, determines exact integral homotopy types, and exposes a geometric Möbius-band mechanism. It is not a routine numerical increment, special-case extraction, or a claim about all graph cut complexes.

**Boundaries.** The theorem excludes \(n<4\), higher cut parameters, the total cut complex, and equivariant refinements. The verifier supports but does not substitute for the proof. Independent external validation remains outstanding.
