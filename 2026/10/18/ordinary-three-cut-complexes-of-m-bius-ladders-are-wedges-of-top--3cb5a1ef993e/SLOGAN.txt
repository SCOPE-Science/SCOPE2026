For every Möbius ladder with \(2n\ge8\) vertices, the ordinary three-cut complex is homotopy equivalent to a wedge of exactly \(2n(n-4)+1\) spheres of dimension \(2n-4\). The proof collapses its Alexander dual to a Möbius strip with \(2n(n-4)\) extra circles and then applies integral Alexander duality and connectivity.
