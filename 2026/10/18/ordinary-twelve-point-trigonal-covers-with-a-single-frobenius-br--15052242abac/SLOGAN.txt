For each \(n\ge5\) coprime to \(6\), the cyclic trigonal curves \(y^3-y=(x^3-x)/Q(x)\), with \(Q\) monic irreducible of degree \(n\) over \(\mathbf F_3\), are ordinary genus-\(2n-2\) curves attaining the twelve-point gonality bound and having one Frobenius-transitive branch orbit and exactly the deck automorphisms over \(\mathbf F_3\). They represent precisely \(\frac1{6n}\sum_{d\mid n}\mu(d)3^{n/d}\) distinct arithmetic isomorphism classes.
