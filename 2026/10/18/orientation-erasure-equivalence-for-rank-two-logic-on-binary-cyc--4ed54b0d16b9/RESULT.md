# Orientation-erasure equivalence for rank-two logic on binary cycles

## Finding
Fix a finite alphabet \(\Sigma\) and consider all finite, coherently oriented directed cycles of length \(n\geq3\), with every vertex carrying exactly one of the named unary colours \(P_a\) for \(a\in\Sigma\). Write \(R\) for the oriented successor relation and \(E(x,y)\) for the symmetric reduct \(R(x,y)\lor R(y,x)\).

**Theorem.** If \(|\Sigma|\leq2\), then two such oriented, coloured cycles satisfy the same first-order sentences of quantifier rank at most two in the signature \((R,(P_a)_{a\in\Sigma})\) **if and only if** their undirected reducts satisfy the same sentences of rank at most two in \((E,(P_a)_{a\in\Sigma})\). The statement holds for unequal cycle lengths and arbitrary placements of the colours, including monochromatic cycles. Equivalently, on this class, every rank-two directed sentence admits a rank-two undirected sentence with exactly the same truth set.

The bound on alphabet size is sharp. With three colours, the oriented coloured triangles \(0\to1\to2\to0\) and \(0\to2\to1\to0\) have identical colour-preserving undirected reducts, whereas the rank-two directed sentence \(\exists x\,\exists y\,(P_0(x)\land P_1(y)\land R(x,y))\) distinguishes them. Consequently the forgetful reduct ceases to preserve rank-two equivalence as soon as at least three colours are available.

## Assumptions and scope
All cycles are finite, nonempty, loopless, simple and consistently oriented, with exactly one incoming and one outgoing arc at each vertex and length at least three. Colours are **named**, mutually exclusive and exhaustive. The logical fragment is unrestricted first-order logic without counting quantifiers but with *quantifier rank* at most two; there is no bound on the number of Boolean connectives or on the number of variable names. The result does **not** assert equivalence at higher quantifier ranks, for arbitrary digraphs, or for directed paths.

For a cyclic word \(w=(w_0,\ldots,w_{n-1})\), subscripts are read modulo \(n\). At position \(i\), put \(a=w_{i-1}\), \(b=w_i\), \(c=w_{i+1}\), and let \(F_i\subseteq\Sigma\) be the set of colours occurring at vertices distinct from \(i-1,i,i+1\). Define the **directed pointed profile** as \((b,a,c,F_i)\) and the **undirected pointed profile** as \((b,\{a,c\},F_i)\). Here braces denote a set, not a multiset.

## Proof
**1. Rank-two sentences are determined by realized rank-one pointed types.** For a finite relational signature with unary colours and one binary relation, a quantifier-free formula in two variables depends only on their atomic two-point diagram (equality, both directed relation orientations, and colours). A formula of quantifier rank at most one with one free variable is a Boolean combination of tests \(\exists y\,\theta(x,y)\) for quantifier-free \(\theta\), together with quantifier-free tests. This remains valid for universal quantification by negation. Finally, every closed formula of rank at most two is a Boolean combination of tests \(\exists x\,\psi(x)\) where \(\psi\) has rank at most one. Thus equality of the *sets of realized pointed rank-one types* is necessary and sufficient for equality of rank-two sentence theories. For necessity, a differing pointed type is singled out by the conjunction of its finitely many atomic two-point existence and nonexistence tests, nested under \(\exists x\).

**2. Local profiles describe those pointed types exactly.** In a directed cycle with \(n\ge3\), relative to a chosen vertex \(x\), a second vertex \(y\) is exactly one of: \(x\) itself; its unique predecessor; its unique successor; or a non-neighbour. Each of the two immediate neighbours has one colour, and each non-neighbour colour either occurs or does not occur. Hence the directed rank-one pointed type is exactly \((b,a,c,F_i)\). For the undirected reduct, predecessor and successor are symmetric and its pointed type is exactly \((b,\{a,c\},F_i)\). This also covers \(n=3\), for which \(F_i=\varnothing\).

**3. Binary cyclic reversal lemma.** In any circular binary word, occurrence of the consecutive triple \(001\) is equivalent to occurrence of \(100\): both occur precisely when a cyclic maximal run of zeros has length at least two and some one is present. Similarly, \(011\) occurs if and only if \(110\) occurs, witnessed by a maximal run of ones of length at least two. The only binary triples whose outside letters differ are these two reversal pairs. Therefore every cyclic length-three factor \(abc\) occurs if and only if its reversal \(cba\) occurs.

For occurrences of reversed triples \(abc\) and \(cba\) in a fixed word, the colour multiplicities among the three distinguished positions are identical. Subtracting these multiplicities from the total multiplicity of each colour shows that their non-neighbour colour sets \(F_i\) are identical. This subtraction is a proof device, not a counting operation of the logical language.

**4. Recovering the directed rank-one type set.** Given an undirected profile \((b,N,F)\) in a binary cycle: if \(N\) has one colour, both predecessor and successor have that colour, yielding exactly one directed profile. If \(N=\{0,1\}\), the reversal lemma ensures that both possible predecessor/successor orders occur and, by the preceding paragraph, both occurrences have the same \(F\). The directed profile set is therefore recovered deterministically from the undirected profile set, for **each cycle separately**. The reverse implication is immediate by forgetting orientation in every directed profile. Step 1 now proves the equivalence of rank-two sentence theories for every pair of binary cycles.

More strongly, because there are only finitely many possible undirected pointed rank-one profiles, each realizable set of them has a rank-two characteristic sentence: assert the existence of a vertex realizing every listed profile and the nonexistence of a vertex realizing each omitted profile. A finite disjunction of such characteristic sentences translates any given directed rank-two sentence on the class to an undirected rank-two sentence. Its quantifier rank remains two, irrespective of Boolean formula length.

**5. Optimality of two colours.** On the vertex set \(\{0,1,2\}\), give each vertex its own named colour, and orient the triangle either \(0\to1\to2\to0\) or \(0\to2\to1\to0\). The undirected reducts coincide exactly. The displayed rank-two sentence is true only of the first directed triangle. For every larger alphabet these three labels remain available, giving the same counterexample.

## Verification
Run `python3 verify.py` and `python3 verify_game.py` in the same directory. The first program exhaustively checks 32,760 binary cyclic words of lengths three through fourteen, confirming that each undirected pointed-profile set determines exactly one directed pointed-profile set and checking reversal of all realized triples. It also checks the ternary witness. The second implementation independently builds rank-one types from atomic two-point diagrams and checks all 1,016 binary cyclic words of lengths three through nine, including agreement with the first implementation. Both programs use only the Python standard library.

These finite checks corroborate the definitions and the small witness; **they do not establish the universal result**, which follows from steps 1–5 above. The code deliberately does not use numerical coincidence between theory counts as a substitute for a proof.

## Relationship to prior work
Brown and Hoshino (2007) characterize Ehrenfeucht–Fraïssé equivalence for **uncoloured, undirected** paths and cycles; their cycle theorem compares lengths, not the rank-two equivalence of directed versus undirected *coloured* cycles. Weis and Immerman (2009) provide quantifier-depth and variable structure theorems for **linearly ordered words** with or without successor; their ranker setting does not state this cyclic orientation-reduct property. An earlier finite classification of directed two-colour cycle theories concerns their total number, whereas the present theorem determines the exact loss—or, in the binary case, lack of loss—under an orientation-forgetting change of signature, with a sharp ternary obstruction. General two-round EF-game methodology is established and not claimed as new.

## Limitations
No claim is made that orientation is definable pointwise, that a directed edge predicate has a quantifier-free undirected definition, or that the analogous preservation holds at rank three. It is *the space of rank-two sentence truth sets on the specified class* that is unchanged by forgetting edge direction. The priority comparison is limited to identified accessible sources and targeted research searches; an earlier specialized treatment of this cyclic coloured-reduct phenomenon may exist.
