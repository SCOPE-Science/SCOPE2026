On a simple coherently oriented n-cycle (n≥3), the pointed type records the center color, predecessor color, successor color, and the set of colors present among non-neighbors. The undirected pointed type forgets the predecessor/successor order. In any binary circular word, each length-three factor abc and its reversal cba have equal occurrence support: the only non-palindromic cases are 001↔100 and 011↔110, paired at ends of maximal monochromatic runs. Reversed triples remove the same color multiset from global counts, so the far-neighbor support is equal.
