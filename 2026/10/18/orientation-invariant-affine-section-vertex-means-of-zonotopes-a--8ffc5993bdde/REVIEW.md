# Scientific review

## Correctness
The weighted section mean follows by integrating indicator functions for edge intersections over projected offset intervals. The chamber-edge correspondence accounts for all zonotope edges, and the derivative-kink argument at each distinct generator orthogonality hyperplane proves the necessity of constant zone multiplicity without hidden generic-position assumptions. Exact rational edge enumeration checked three structures over twenty-one orientations; the script does not replace the proof. The displayed balanced and unbalanced examples and the general-position formula have been checked independently of the integral identity.

## Originality
Swan's full four-page text establishes the orientation-free expectation for a hypercube and all parallelotopes, which are covered cases here. Shephard's 1974 full paper provides the traditional zones/face-combinatorics, while Holtz–Ron gives the arrangement correspondence. Neither inspected full text states the **if-and-only-if condition for orientation independence of an affine section-vertex mean of a general zonotope**, nor the nongeneric balanced six-generator example. The zonotope-zone enumeration and the integration method are classical ingredients and explicitly not asserted to be new. Unindexed prior formulations remain possible.

## Value
The classification decides a natural conditional stereological invariance property of an arbitrary zonotope solely from combinatorial zone multiplicities, with a constructive class of nongeneric positive examples and a contrasting false universal claim. It specifies exactly when the parallelotope orientation-free phenomenon survives dependent generators and when it fails. It is an exact structural characterization rather than a minor numerical example. Further higher-codimension laws are outside the claim.
