For a finite graph, the norm of its edge-variation lattice element in \(\operatorname{FBL}^{(p)}[\ell_1^n]\) is a sharp orientation-moment optimization, and for every \(p>1\) the maximum possible norm occurs exactly on bipartite graphs. For \(r\) triangles sharing a hub, the exact norm is \(6r(2/3)^{1-1/p}\) for every \(1\le p\le\infty\).
