# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** For every vertex the condition \(d_vh_v=0\) leaves three disjoint types with exact multiplicities. On every arrow, multiplication reduces to a single homogeneous equation for one-sided zero products and a rank-at-most-one two-equation system for mutual annihilation; their exact ranks prove the claimed transfer matrices. The product and bilateral counts were checked by direct independent algebra-element enumeration at \(q=2,3\) and short orientations. The all-parameter proof rests on these rank calculations and finite matrix identities, not on extrapolation from the enumerations.

**Originality — PASS, subject to ordinary bibliographic risk.** Shumyatsky–Vannacci's complete 2023 paper develops the finite-ring zero-product probability and ideal bounds, but its full text does not give an orientation-sensitive quiver transfer or an orientation-independent mutual-annihilation graph sum. Green–Schroll's quiver algebra study addresses quotient structure rather than counting zero products. Separate results enumerating commuting pairs or commutators do not imply the two zero-product counts, because commuting and annihilating are different equations. The close 2022 article of Dolžan was accessible only as an abstract; whether it contains an equivalent specialized formula was not completely resolved, and the literature search does not certify novelty.

**Value — PASS.** The theorem computes the natural finite-ring zero-product probability exactly for every oriented cycle and a stronger two-sided annihilation count for every loopless simple oriented graph. The difference between orientation-sensitive one-sided multiplication and orientation-independent mutual annihilation is structural rather than a numerical sample, and the strict four-cycle gap holds uniformly over all finite fields. The exact transfer description permits arbitrary orientation words and yields uniform recurrences for coherent cycles.
