In finite radical-square-zero cycle algebras, one-sided zero-product counts depend on arrow orientations, but the number of mutually annihilating ordered pairs does not. Exact transfer matrices and a general graph formula establish the contrast over every finite field; on the four-cycle the coherent and alternating orientations have \(5152\) and \(6928\) one-sided zero-product pairs over \(\mathbb F_2\), but \(2176\) mutual-annihilation pairs each.
