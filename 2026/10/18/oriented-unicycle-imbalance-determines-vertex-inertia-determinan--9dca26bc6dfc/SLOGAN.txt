For a connected oriented graph, the intrinsic vertex-covariance determinant of its directed edge polytope is exactly \(n/m^n\) times the sum of squared orientation imbalances of all spanning unicycles. For tournaments this identity gives a complete covariance spectrum and sharp extrema: transitive orientations minimize the determinant, while regular or near-regular orientations maximize it, with maximum condition number \(3n/(n-2)\).
