Nested Euclidean balls have strictly increasing surface area weighted by \(e^{\lambda\|x\|_2^2}\) for every \(\lambda\ge0\), even when neither ball contains the origin. The logarithmic increase has the sharp universal lower bound \((d-1)(R-r)/\max\{R,\|a\|_2,\|b\|_2\}\), and the same bound survives positive exponential mixtures.
