# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The square space is exactly the two-dimensional span of the part-sum vectors; preservation of its zero cross-product forces a diagonal or anti-diagonal restriction. Mixed-product and norm identities then force block diagonal matrices in the first case and equal part sizes in the second. The finite-field constants follow from nondegenerate orthogonal and alternating hyperplane forms. Full-table finite enumeration corroborates six small cases, but does not constitute the general proof.

**Originality — PASS (self-review; residual bibliographic uncertainty).** The closest inspected 2025 full text establishes diagonal and graph-induced subgroup actions, but not the full orthogonal/symplectic classification and finite-field orders for complete bipartite graph evolution algebras. The 2017 graph/random-walk paper and the 2010 graph-evolution paper discuss adjacent notions, not this complete classification. Classical orthogonal and symplectic group order formulas are prior knowledge, explicitly invoked rather than credited as new. The possibility of an earlier equivalent bilinear-form classification under different terminology remains.

**Value — PASS.** Graph-induced symmetries can miss most algebra automorphisms because the square space has rank two. The theorem identifies the entire hidden continuous or finite classical-group symmetry, produces exact finite-field counts, and explains the characteristic-two symplectic transition for a natural graph family. Concrete examples demonstrate why standard graph symmetries are insufficient.

**Limitations.** Characteristic dividing a part size is excluded. An independent mathematical audit has not been performed. The finite checker is a consistency test, not a proof for all fields.
