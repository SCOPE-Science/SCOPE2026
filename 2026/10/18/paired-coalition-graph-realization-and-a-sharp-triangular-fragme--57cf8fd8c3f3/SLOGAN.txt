Every paired-coalition graph of \(K_{m,n}\) is a disjoint union of bicliques indexed by distinct nonzero vertex-block imbalances, with an exact integer feasibility criterion. The maximum number of its components is \(\lfloor(\sqrt{8\min\{m,n\}+1}-1)/2\rfloor\), and a bivariate exponential generating formula counts all feasible partitions by both components and blocks.
