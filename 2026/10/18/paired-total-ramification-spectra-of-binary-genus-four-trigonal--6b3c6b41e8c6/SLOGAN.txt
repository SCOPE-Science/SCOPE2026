For every smooth genus-four curve of bidegree \((3,3)\) over \(\mathbf F_2\) on the split quadric, the two trigonal maps together have at most three rational totally ramified fibres, and exactly six unordered two-map ramification profiles occur. Their complete \(16020\)-equation and \(252\)-isomorphism-class distributions distinguish curves even when the Weil polynomial agrees.
