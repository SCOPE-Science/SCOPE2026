Individual and pairwise plant coextinction probabilities, even together with pollinator degrees, do not determine one-to-one service capacity under random pollinator loss. The smallest connected counterexample has nine species: two four-plant/five-pollinator networks have identical first- and second-order loss laws, yet their expected maximum-matching capacities differ by \(q(1-q)^2(3-3q+q^2)>0\) for every \(0<q<1\).
