A base with \(r\) endpoint-containing strongly pseudoperfect representations produces exactly \(r^{\lceil k/2\rceil}\) palindromic-shell representations of its \(k\)-th power. Since the odd base \(45045\) has \(695\) such representations, its powers have at least \(695^{\lceil k/2\rceil}\) distinct reciprocal-closed divisor-sum representations.
