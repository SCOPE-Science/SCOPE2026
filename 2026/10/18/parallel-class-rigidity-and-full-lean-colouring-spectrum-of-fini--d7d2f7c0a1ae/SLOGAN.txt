For the line hypergraph of any finite affine plane of order \(q\ge3\), every non-injective lean point-colouring collapses exactly a nonempty selection of parallel lines, giving \(\operatorname{Lean}=q\) and precisely \(q(q-2)\) missing colour counts. The entire named-palette counting polynomial is \( (t)_{q^2}+(q+1)\sum_{k=1}^{q}\binom qk(t)_{q^2-k(q-1)}\).
