After conditioning the BCZ horocycle roof on a rare large excursion, the length of the ensuing index-two itinerary, divided by the excursion threshold, converges to a Pareto law with lower endpoint \(1/2\). Before that boundary, the rescaled orbit has the explicit random diagonal profile \((1-tV,1-tV)\), where \(V\) has density \(2v\) on \((0,1)\).
