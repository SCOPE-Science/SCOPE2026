The counts of permutations whose first two powers avoid \(132\) have eventually opposite adjacent-coefficient curvature on even and odd indices. Their Turán determinants satisfy \(a_n^2-a_{n-1}a_{n+1}\sim (-1)^n (3P_+P_-/(2\pi))4^n/n^2\), where \(P_\pm>0\) are explicit Catalan-composition constants; the alternation arises from the usually overlooked singularity at \(-1/2\).
