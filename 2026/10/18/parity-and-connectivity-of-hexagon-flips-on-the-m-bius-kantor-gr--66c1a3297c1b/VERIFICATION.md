# Reproducible finite checks

The graph is the generalized Petersen graph \(GP(8,3)\) with all vertices and edges explicitly generated by `verify_cycle_masks.py`. The enumerator always matches the least currently unmatched vertex to one of its currently unmatched neighbors, so it visits each of the graph's perfect matchings exactly once. Every simple six-cycle is generated by an exhaustive depth-first walk without repeated vertices and deduplicated as an edge mask. The resulting matching/cycle intersection test yields each permitted flip; all rows are checked against `hexagon_flip_certificate.json`.

Execute `python3 verify_cycle_masks.py hexagon_flip_certificate.json` and `python3 verify_bipartite_permutations.py hexagon_flip_certificate.json`. The second program instead enumerates the 8-by-8 admissible matching permutations and detects a flip precisely when two permutations differ in three positions, then compares all reconstructed transitions to the certificate. Execute `python3 verify_circulant.py` for an independent circulant-biadjacency check using integer determinant, permanent-subset recurrence, and complete permutation enumeration. No nonstandard dependencies are needed.

Observed checks: 33 matchings, 24 simple six-cycles, 96 undirected permitted flips, bipartite-sign classes of sizes 30 and 3, components of sizes 30+1+1+1, nontrivial degree distribution 24 times degree six and six times degree eight, longest nontrivial shortest-path distance exactly four, and an edge-disjoint 3-matching partition of the graph. The circulant matrix has permanent 33 and determinant -27. The certificate contains the ordered edges, perfect-matching masks, six-cycle masks, all adjacency rows, parity signs and component lists.

These are complete finite checks for this labeled graph, not statistical samples; they do not establish analogous assertions for other graphs. No independent audit or external certification was performed.
