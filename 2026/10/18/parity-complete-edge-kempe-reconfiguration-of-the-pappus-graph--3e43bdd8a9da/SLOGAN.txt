The edge-Kempe reconfiguration graph of the Pappus graph, after identifying permutations of the three edge colors, consists of two ten-vertex windmills, each made of three copies of \(K_4\) sharing one vertex. Two colorings are reachable from one another if and only if the products of their three point-to-line matching-permutation signs agree; every reachable pair is connected by at most two single-cycle switches.
