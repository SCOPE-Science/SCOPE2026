For a metric on \(2k\) points whose only short distances join disjoint pairs, the largest Euclidean ball in its real Lipschitz-free unit ball has an exact parity-dependent radius. When \(k\) is odd, shortening the pairs through \(t=(2k-2)/(2k-1)\) abruptly changes the optimal center set from affine dimension \(k\) to a unique center, although the facet count does not change.
