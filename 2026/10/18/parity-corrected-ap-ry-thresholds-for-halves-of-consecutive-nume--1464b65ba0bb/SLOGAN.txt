For every odd \(a\ge5\) and \(3\le k\le a-2\), the half-quotient of \(\langle a,a+1,\ldots,a+k\rangle\) has an explicit parity-corrected Apéry set and a complete three-branch Frobenius formula. Its Frobenius number equals half the original gap maximum rounded down except in a sharply characterized terminal-block case, where it drops by \((a-1)/2\).
