For a walker initialized at one vertex of a uniform magnetic cycle, position-readout flux information turns on at order \((Jt)^n\) for even lengths and \((Jt)^{n+1}\) for odd lengths, with explicit farthest-vertex coefficients. All position scores are exactly blind at integer half-turn fluxes on even cycles and odd quarter-turn fluxes on odd cycles, although the pure-state quantum Fisher information in the fixed uniform-phase encoding is \(8J^2t^2/n^2\).
