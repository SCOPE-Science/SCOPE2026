For rectangular rook graphs whose side lengths have the same parity, the previously known maximum odd-induced-subgraph size has a new complete equality-case classification: when the shorter side \(m<n\) is even, the maximizers are complements of row-to-column assignments with even fibres, counted by \(m![x^m](\cosh x)^n\), whereas for odd \(m<n\) only full-column deletions occur. Nontrivial square boards have two disjoint transposed families; in opposite-parity rectangles, the full board is uniquely optimal, and the number of maximizers grows polynomially in the long side at fixed even width but only linearly at fixed odd width of matching parity.
