For a flux-threaded quantum cycle with arbitrary site-dependent pure dephasing, every Liouvillian spectral moment below order \(N\) is flux-blind when \(N\) is even, while every moment below order \(2N\) is flux-blind when \(N\) is odd. At the first sensitive order the magnetic-flux response has an exact, nonzero, dephasing-rate-independent coefficient.
