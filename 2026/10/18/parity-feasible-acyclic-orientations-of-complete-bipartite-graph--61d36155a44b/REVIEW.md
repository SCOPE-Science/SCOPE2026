# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The unrestricted existence classification follows by induction after deleting a prescribed-even source and toggling exactly the opposite part's bits. The exceptional-source branch chooses a source from the other part; both residual parity feasibility and availability of a zero are proved. Stars are the base case. The two-row fibre count classifies all acyclic columns and counts the choices of equal and mixed column types without overlaps. The 21-family exhaustive checks (2,390 feasible signatures) independently agree, but are not the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS (conditional on accessible literature)
The 2026 motivating study gives necessary parity/source/sink conditions and results for Cartesian grid/cycle products, without the biclique classification. The 2025 complete-multipartite counting paper and the earlier poly-Bernoulli study enumerate orientations without their full prescribed-vertex-parity fibres. Exact literature/published-finding corpus searches uncovered no dominating theorem; a statement hidden under a different encoding remains a risk. The present contribution is the all-biclique necessary-and-sufficient condition plus the explicit two-row fibre refinement, not the total orientation formula or simple source obstruction.

## Value — PASS
The general existence question is open on unrestricted graphs. Its complete constructive resolution on a fundamental dense two-part family supplies a sharp counterpoint to products of paths and cycles; the exact \(K_{2,b}\) parity-fibre law quantifies a nonuniform splitting of the known total orientation count and identifies the sole forbidden signature. Both are natural unbounded families, with parameters prescribed independently at labelled vertices.
