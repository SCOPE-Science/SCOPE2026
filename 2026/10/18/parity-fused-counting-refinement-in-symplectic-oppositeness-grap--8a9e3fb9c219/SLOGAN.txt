For the graph of pairwise transverse Lagrangians in a \(2n\)-dimensional symplectic space, marking one vertex creates a sharp rank-parity dichotomy: three rounds of counting refinement recover all \(n+1\) intersection strata when \(n\ge4\) is even, but odd rank permanently fuses consecutive strata into exactly \((n+3)/2\) classes by two rounds (already after one in rank three). A symmetric-matrix nullity identity proves the classification over every finite field.
