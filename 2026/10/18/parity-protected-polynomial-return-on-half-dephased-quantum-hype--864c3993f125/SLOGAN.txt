Dephasing every even-parity site of a quantum hypercube completely erases initial-state memory in odd dimensions, but preserves a decoherence-free block of dimension \(\tfrac12\binom d{d/2}\) in even dimensions. For an initially localized unmonitored vertex, the exact stationary return probability decays as \(2/(\pi d)\) along even dimensions, versus \(2^{-d}\) in odd dimensions.
