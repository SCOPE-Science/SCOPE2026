For finite centered Cauchy mixtures, the unshifted Möbius trapezoidal rule has exactly one mass-preserving scale with odd node count, but no mass-preserving scale with even node count unless all component widths coincide. The even-node mass is strictly convex in log scale, and for two equally weighted widths the uniquely optimal scale is their geometric mean with exact positive bias \(2r^n/(1-r^n)\).
