For a uniformly random equitable two-edge-colouring of \(K_n\), the probability of needing exactly one wild edge is \(4n/(e2^n)\) times \(1+(1+6\varepsilon_n)/(3n^2)+O(n^{-3})\), where \(\varepsilon_n\) records the parity of \(\binom n2\). Needing two or more wild edges has probability \(O(n^2 4^{-n})\), so the same parity-sensitive expansion holds for the expected wild number.
