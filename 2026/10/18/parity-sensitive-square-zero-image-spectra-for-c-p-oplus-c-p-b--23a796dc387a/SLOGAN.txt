For every prime \(p\) and exponent \(b\geq1\), all square-zero endomorphisms of \(C_p\oplus C_{p^b}\) have cyclic image, and their complete distribution by image order is explicit. The total count is \(p^2\) when \(b=1\), \((2p-1)p^{b/2}\) for even \(b\geq2\), and \(p^{(b+3)/2}\) for odd \(b\geq3\), with an extra maximal-image stratum exactly at odd exponents.
