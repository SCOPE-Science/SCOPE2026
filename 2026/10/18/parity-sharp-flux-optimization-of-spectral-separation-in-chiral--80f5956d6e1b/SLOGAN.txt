For a magnetic quantum walk on any finite cycle, the total flux maximizing the **smallest pairwise energy separation** is exactly \(\pi/2\) for odd cycles but \(n\arctan(\tan(\pi/n)/3)\) for even cycles, tending to \(\pi/3\). The sharp separation is of order \(n^{-2}\), with a leading coefficient \(4/3\) larger for even cycles; this optimizes a rigorous worst-pair Cesàro-dephasing bound, not necessarily the actual mixing time.
