For every finite irreducible reset-chain Markov generator with strictly positive reset probabilities, every even-degree diagonal Padé exponential is entrywise positive at all positive step sizes, whereas odd degrees eventually create negative transition entries except on a balanced two-state chain. One binary scaling-and-squaring step always restores a strictly positive stochastic matrix, and the three-stage Gauss–Legendre case has an exact sharp cubic positivity boundary for uniform reset chains.
