For the top-Laplacian polytope of a simplex boundary, parity changes an orthogonal free sum of two regular simplices into an anisotropic simplex: all facets touch the exact inball in both regimes, but in odd size the minimum enclosing sphere touches only the larger alternating-sign vertex class. Exact Euclidean radii, centers and John–Löwner principal semiaxes are explicit for every size.
