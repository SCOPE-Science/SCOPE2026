# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The exact kinetic flip condition has been reconstructed from its neighborhood-dependent rate definition. Two facilitators among one site's three neighbors permit a flip, hence full arrest equals the open-packing condition. The alternating-rail permutation identifies every conflict edge and all connected components, and the cyclic-gap calculation proves all coefficients without relying on computation. Perron–Frobenius proves the all-size entropy law. Direct finite enumeration, independent cycle-square checks, symbolic transfer-matrix checks, and an independent conflict-edge construction corroborate the analytic derivation.

## Originality — PASS (with bibliographic risk)
The 2006 kinetic-model paper treats kinetic relaxation on square and cubic lattices, not the finite-prism complete blocked-polynomial formula. The 2023 prism-packing theorem establishes only a *maximum open-packing cardinality* for bipartite base graphs, not the distribution of all open packings; it does not apply to an odd cycle base. Multiple phrasing, parameter, equivalence and full-claim searches in a scientific-results index returned no equivalent statement. The independently known cycle-square independence coefficients are used explicitly as background. A possible unidentified open-packing-polynomial paper remains a residual bibliographic risk; absence of a search hit is not taken as uniqueness proof.

## Value — PASS
An exact all-size frozen-microstate polynomial on a periodic two-leg glassy-dynamics model yields a complete finite-volume physical census, an analytically computable iid fully frozen probability, its entropy, and a nontrivial parity-frustrated extremal occupancy. The invariant is natural to the underlying kinetic arrest problem, and not a deliberately chosen numerical slice. The theorem does not assert a new general relaxation bound or classify non-frozen communicating classes.
