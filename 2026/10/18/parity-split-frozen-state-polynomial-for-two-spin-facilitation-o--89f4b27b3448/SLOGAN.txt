On a cyclic two-leg ladder, the fully frozen states of the two-spin-facilitated Fredrickson–Andersen model have exact generating polynomial \(F_{2n}(z)\) for odd rung count and \(F_n(z)^2\) for even rung count, where \(F_m(z)=\sum_k\frac{m}{m-2k}\binom{m-2k}{k}z^k\). This parity split gives exact activity-resolved microstate counts, sharp maximal facilitator numbers, and a common thermodynamic frozen-state entropy \(\log\rho(z)\) with \(\rho^3-\rho^2-z=0\).
