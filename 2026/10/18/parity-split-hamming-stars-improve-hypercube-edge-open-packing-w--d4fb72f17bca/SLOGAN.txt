A parity-split Hamming-code construction gives \(\rho_e^{\mathrm{o}}(Q_d)\ge(2^k-1)2^{d-k-1}\) for every \(k\ge2\) and \(d\ge2^k-1\), improving the previously recorded nine-cube lower bound to \(224\). An exhaustive five-cube certificate proves \(\rho_e^{\mathrm{o}}(Q_5)=12\) and classifies all \(80\) maximum edge-open packings as one family of four induced three-edge stars.
