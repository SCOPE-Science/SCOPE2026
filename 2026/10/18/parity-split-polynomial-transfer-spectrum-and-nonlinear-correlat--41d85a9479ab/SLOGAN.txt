The four-branch even-symmetric chaotic map has an explicit parity-split transfer spectrum on every polynomial space: all centered odd polynomials vanish after one transfer, while the nonzero even-degree eigenvalues are \(2[d^{2j+1}+(1/2-d)^{2j+1}]\). Its coordinate is uncorrelated at every positive lag, yet its centered quadratic observable has exact normalized autocorrelation \([3(d-1/4)^2+1/16]^n\), a quantitative nonlinear dependence that also lifts to the map's skewed conjugate.
