# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The claimed restricted Lie isomorphism is given by an explicit coefficient bijection together with a monomial-basis intertwiner. The proof verifies the intertwining separately on even and odd monomials, thereby obtaining an operator similarity plus a zero block; this proves preservation of commutators, squaring, every power rank, and all nonzero Jordan blocks. The square-zero criterion is exactly \(eg=g^2=0\) in the length-\(s\) coefficient ring. A valuation partition counts every coefficient pair once; both polynomial simplification and total-point count are checked symbolically. The included independent-style finite-field matrix test additionally checks exhaustive cases but is not invoked as a proof for the unrestricted family.

## Originality — PASS, with residual uncertainty

The material inspected in Premet's preprint develops regular derivations and restricted nilpotent geometry explicitly in characteristic greater than two, with different truncation exponents and without finite-field operator-rank multiplicities. The closely worded searches for a parity isomorphism, stable natural representations, and square-zero rank polynomials did not locate the same implication. Previously reported counts for quadratic-jet derivations treat a different algebra, with two generators and cubic augmentation truncation, so they do not imply the one-variable parity isomorphism or these coefficients. This is a justified provisional originality assessment, not a proof that no equivalent theorem exists. In particular older modular Witt or derivation-ring classifications remain a priority risk.

## Value — PASS

The isomorphism identifies a characteristic-two structural parity phenomenon between derivation algebras of nonisomorphic underlying local rings. The exact polynomial resolves every square-zero operator-rank fibre uniformly in both truncation lengths and all finite fields of characteristic two; its geometric-to-flat coefficient transition is a nontrivial arithmetic refinement of mere feasibility. The invariant is natural for restricted derivation algebras and their canonical modules. The result is not a count of a hand-picked exceptional matrix or a restatement of the known characteristic-odd regularity theorem.

## Scope and external verification

The enumeration does not concern general derivations without the operator-square-zero constraint or their automorphism orbits. No infinite theorem has been deduced solely from finite computation. Search incompleteness and potential alternative formulations remain residual originality risks. The supplied verifier is reproducible with Python's standard library; its scope is stated exactly.
