In characteristic \(2\), the restricted derivation Lie algebras of the consecutive truncated polynomial rings \(K[t]/(t^{2s})\) and \(K[t]/(t^{2s+1})\) are explicitly isomorphic, and their natural representations differ only by one trivial summand. Over \(\mathbb F_q\), their square-zero derivations have the same complete rank polynomial \(1+(q^2-1)\sum_{r=1}^{\lfloor s/2\rfloor}q^{2r-2}T^r+(q-1)q^{s-1}\sum_{r=\lfloor s/2\rfloor+1}^{s}T^r\).
