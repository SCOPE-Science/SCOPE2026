For any Rees zero-matrix semigroup over a group, the natural partial-inner monoid is a Brandt semigroup over \(G/Z(G)\) with index set its nonzero idempotents, plus an external identity, apart from two explicit boundary cases. Consequently, two nonisomorphic completely zero-simple semigroups can have identical partial-inner monoids while disagreeing on whether complete mappings exist.
