Every minimum dominated coloring of a finite projective-plane incidence graph is built from one pencil of lines and one dual pencil of points, giving exactly \((q^2+q+1)^2(q+1)^2\) unlabeled optima. Single-vertex recoloring freezes both pencil centers: the optimal-coloring graph is a disjoint union of \((q^2+q+1)^2\) copies of \(K_{q+1}\square K_{q+1}\).
