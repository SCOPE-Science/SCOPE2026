The Euclidean 24-cell has perfect discrete-Morse matchings with critical vectors \((1,0,23)\) and \((1,0,0,1)\) at its first two nontrivial Vietoris–Rips scales. Explicit certificates with 96 and 791 elementary face pairs prove the corresponding integral homotopy types \(\bigvee^{23}S^2\) and \(S^3\), refining previously computed homology ranks.
