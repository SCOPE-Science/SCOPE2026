Deleting the last edge of a matching from \(K_{n,n}\), for \(n\ge4\), makes its minimum perfect-edge-dominating set jump from \(n\) to \(n(n-1)\) edges. More generally, all perfect-edge-dominating sets of \(K_{m,n}\) minus any matching, with \(3\le m\le n\), are counted by an explicit three-term polynomial, with a single six-cycle exception.
