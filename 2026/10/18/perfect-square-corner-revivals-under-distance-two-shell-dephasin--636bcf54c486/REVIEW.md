For nonsquare dimensions the origin return tends to \(1/b_d\) (order \(d^{-3}\)); along squares a protected nonzero-frequency pair creates order \(d^{-1}\) revival peaks despite strictly positive noise. The complete fixed-dimension asymptotic law also describes arbitrary initial quantum states, so the result is not a normalization check or an isolated small example.
