Dephasing every Hamming-weight-two vertex of an unweighted quantum hypercube destroys all persistent return oscillations from the zero corner except when the dimension is a perfect square. In square dimension \(d=s^2\), two exactly dark radial energies \(\pm s\) retain an observable \(4a_d^2\cos^2(st)\) return term, with \(a_d=2^{-d}\binom{d}{(d-s)/2}\), at arbitrary positive dephasing rates.
