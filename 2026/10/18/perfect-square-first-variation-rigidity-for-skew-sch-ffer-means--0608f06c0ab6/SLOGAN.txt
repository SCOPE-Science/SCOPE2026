For real \(L^4\), the first variation of a skew Schäffer power mean is an exact perfect square above its optimum, and its entire zero set consists of rigid two-band rotations with algebraic fourth-power weights. The resulting quantitative defect classifies every asymptotically minimizing pair uniformly across fixed outer exponents \(1\le t<4\); the numerical slope alone is already implied by matched-exponent results.
