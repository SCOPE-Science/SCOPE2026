For integral generalized parking-function polytopes with all upper subset inequalities facet-defining, two distinct upper cardinality orbits can touch the Euclidean inball simultaneously only when both sizes are perfect squares. Conversely every nonempty selection of perfect-square cardinalities (and every singleton cardinality) is attainable, giving the exact maximum contact count \(n+\sum_{1\le j\le\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor}\binom{n}{j^2}\).
