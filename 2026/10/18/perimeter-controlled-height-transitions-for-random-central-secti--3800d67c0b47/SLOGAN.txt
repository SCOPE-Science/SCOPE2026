For random central sections of a right prism with a centered cyclic \(m\)-gon base, the expected section-vertex count has a unique finite height maximizer exactly when the base perimeter exceeds \(m\) times its circumradius. Cyclic pentagons exhibit both regimes, while all centered cyclic polygons with at least six vertices have only an unattained tall-prism supremum; a regular pentagon has the sharp optimum \(h/R=(3+\sqrt5)/4\).
