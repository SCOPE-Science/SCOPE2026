At the critical logarithmically weighted Hilbert-integral endpoint, a periodic perturbation on the \(\log\log\)-scale has an exact essential norm given by the top eigenvalue of a positive periodic Green-kernel operator. For fixed frequency \(\omega>0\), its essential norm increases from \(2\) by precisely \((3/2+1/(2\omega^2))\varepsilon^2+O_\omega(\varepsilon^4)\), exposing a frequency-sensitive amplification invisible in the constant-weight endpoint.
