Every complex Carleson orbit frame with Stolz spectrum can be sampled at **any** prescribed average time spacing after an arbitrarily small, spectrally chosen periodic jitter, even if the unjittered powers collide. An exact Vandermonde fiber criterion and alias-multiplicity formula decide the minimum repair period, and the sharp small-jitter lower-frame loss is \(\varepsilon^{2(m_r-1)}\), where \(m_r\) is the largest power-collision multiplicity.
