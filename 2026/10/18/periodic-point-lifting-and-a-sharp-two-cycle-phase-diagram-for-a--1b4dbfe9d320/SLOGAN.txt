In the nonlinear three-dimensional polynomial modular map of Zhang–Dong–Zhang–Ding, integer expanding fiber gains lift every scalar base \(n\)-periodic point to exactly \((a^n-1)(c^n-1)\) points, yielding a rational dynamical zeta function when the base gain is also integral. With a nonintegral base gain between one and two, an exact golden-ratio transition governs the existence of fixed-point-free primitive two-cycles; at gains \((a,c,e)=(2,3,17/10)\) and fixed forcing \(r=1/5\), exactly \(24\) distinct two-cycles exist for arbitrary polynomial couplings.
