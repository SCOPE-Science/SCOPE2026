For a one-dimensional run-and-tumble particle with a positive periodic direction-reversal rate, the long-time diffusivity depends only on the spatial mean rate, but the fourth displacement cumulant acquires an exact nonnegative correction proportional to the squared norm of the centered primitive of the rate landscape. A sharp Fourier bound identifies first-harmonic landscapes as extremal, and a sinusoidal landscape exhibits an explicit negative-to-positive fourth-cumulant crossover.
