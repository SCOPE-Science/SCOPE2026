For a reflected–absorbed Brownian searcher with weak Ornstein–Uhlenbeck propulsion, the effect of resetting to a fixed outward rather than inward velocity on the onset of beneficial resetting reverses as propulsion persistence changes. The exact threshold susceptibility tends to \(4/125\) at long persistence but is asymptotic to \(-1/\eta\) at rapid relaxation; at least one intervening sign reversal is rigorously guaranteed, without claiming its uniqueness.
