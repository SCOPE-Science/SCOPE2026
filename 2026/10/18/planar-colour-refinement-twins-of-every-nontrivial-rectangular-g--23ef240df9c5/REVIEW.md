# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The all-size construction is explicit for three exhaustive parameter cases. The half-turn/reflection automorphisms establish the colour equalities needed for an inductive vertexwise proof of exact refinement agreement. Boundary detours prove connectivity. The exterior-arc drawings, plus Kuratowski's degree obstruction in the exceptional strip, prove planarity. Direct length-three-path counting gives exactly two fewer four-cycles, except for the three-by-three grid, which gains two. The four-cycle is the only two-regular simple graph of order four. Independent finite consistency calculations reproduced all conclusions in 152 parameter cases but are not treated as the proof of the infinite claim.

## Originality — PASS, with priority risk

Arvind–Köbler–Rattan–Verbitsky (arXiv:1502.01255, Lemma 6 and Theorem 9) and Kiefer–Schweitzer–Selman (arXiv:1503.08792, Theorem 17) already classify unrestricted colour-refinement identification; the unrestricted nonidentification of grid graphs is not claimed as independently new. What remains is the uniform **single-switch** witness that stays **connected and planar** and modifies the four-cycle count by **exactly two**, together with the sharp exceptional grid. Neither inspected text states this planar-preserving quantitative construction. Broad search cannot rule out specialist earlier examples or derivations.

## Value — PASS

Rectangular grids are canonical connected planar graph families, and \(C^2\) identification restricted even to connected planar alternatives is a natural strengthening of unrestricted nonamenability. A uniform minimal-edge witness and a signed, exact four-cycle discrepancy exhibit a concrete boundary between what one-dimensional refinement detects and which planar cycle information it omits. The four-cycle exception and the nonuniform \((3,3)\) sign supply sharp structural boundaries beyond an isolated counterexample.
