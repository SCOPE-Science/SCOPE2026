Every rectangular Cartesian grid \(P_m\mathbin\square P_n\) with \(2\le m\le n\), other than the four-cycle, has a connected planar, nonisomorphic graph obtained by one edge-switch that is indistinguishable by two-variable counting logic. The switch changes the number of four-cycles by exactly two, whereas the four-cycle itself is logically identified even among arbitrary finite simple graphs.
