For any fixed run length \(r\ge2\), the missing runs of \(r\) consecutive part sizes in a uniform random partition form an inhomogeneous Poisson process after scaling their starting indices by \(N^{r/(2(r+1))}\), with intensity \((\pi/\sqrt6)^r x^r\,dx\). In particular, the first such run obeys a Weibull law of shape \(r+1\), and all successive run-order distributions are explicit.
