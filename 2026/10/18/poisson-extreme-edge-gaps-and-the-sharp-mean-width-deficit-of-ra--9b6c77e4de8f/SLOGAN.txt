For fixed \(0<p<1\), deleting graph edges independently from a complete graph produces a nondegenerate Poisson/Bernoulli extreme-pair law for the Gaussian support loss of its symmetric edge polytope. The expected Euclidean mean-width deficit is asymptotic to \(\sqrt2\,c(p)/\sqrt{n\log n}\), where \(c(p)\sim\log(1/p)\) as \(p\downarrow0\) and \(c(p)\sim(1-p)/2\) as \(p\uparrow1\).
