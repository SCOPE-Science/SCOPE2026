At the classical random-subset comparability window, a bounded lattice of positive propositional truth sets is, with high probability, a product of exactly \(Z\) three-element chains and \(n-2Z\) two-element chains, where \(Z\) has the previously known Poisson limit of mean \(t^2\). Consequently its retained fraction of all Boolean truth sets converges in distribution to \((3/4)^Z\), with limiting mean \(e^{-t^2/4}\); the new point is the lattice-valued expressivity law, not the older comparable-pair Poisson theorem.
