Finite sums of distinct Fock dilation compositions admit one bounded-symbol Toeplitz representation exactly when every nonzero dilation parameter lies in the closed disk \(\Re\lambda\ge|\lambda|^2\) and no zero parameter occurs; forbidden pieces cannot cancel. For the polynomial calculus of \(C_{re^{i\theta}}\), the admissible exponents have an explicit angular density, and the Toeplitz-polynomial space is an operator algebra precisely for angular rotations of order at most four.
