On derivative-based Hardy–Sobolev spaces, the affine operator \(F(z)\mapsto F(az+ib)\) has an essential spectral annulus when \(b=0\), but every \(b>0\) collapses it to a universal disk. Its powers obey a sharp damping transition, and the normalized transient amplification grows like \(b^{-n}\) as \(b\downarrow0\), despite strong convergence of the operators.
