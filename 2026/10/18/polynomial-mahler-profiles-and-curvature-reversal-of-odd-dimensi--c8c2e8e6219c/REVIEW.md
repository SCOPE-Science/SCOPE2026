Direct differentiation gives both endpoint second derivatives, and elementary factorial induction proves the strict signs; the IVT supplies at least one curvature reversal. Exact-rational examples check both equivalent integral representations. The claim does not assert a unique inflection.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The capsule is a standard, geometrically canonical path from the Euclidean ball toward a long cylinder, and its Mahler product is a natural affine-invariant extremal quantity. The explicit finite-degree profile turns qualitative shadow-system inequalities into an exact benchmark. The universal curvature reversal starting in dimension five establishes a genuine shape property that cannot be read off from mere maximization or monotonicity, and warns against concavity-based interpolation bounds in this canonical normalization.
