For every fixed cycle length \(\ell\ge3\), the pinned-coordinate volume of its Boolean quadric polytope is an exact even polynomial, and a flower of \(k\) such cycles has a sharp volume ratio \(\rho_\ell^k\sqrt{b_{Q,\ell}/b_{P,\ell}}(1+O_\ell(k^{-1}))\) against its edgewise relaxation. The common pinned coordinate obeys a Gaussian fluctuation law, with strictly broader fluctuations for the exact polytope than for the relaxation.
