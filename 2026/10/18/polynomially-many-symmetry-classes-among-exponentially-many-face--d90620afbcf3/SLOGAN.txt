For the shortest-path metric on two poles joined by one two-edge path and \(m\ge2\) three-edge paths, the facets of the real transportation ball split into exactly \(\frac12[\binom{m+5}{5}+H_m+3\binom{m+2}{2}+\lfloor m/2\rfloor+1]\) orbits under graph automorphisms and sign reversal, where \(H_m=\sum_{j=0}^{\lfloor m/2\rfloor}(j+1)(m-2j+1)\). Every orbit size is given by a multinomial count times \(2\) or \(4\); hence the symmetry-class count grows as \(m^5/240\) despite exponentially many facets, whose aggregate count was previously known.
