For every nonzero nonnegative \(f\) on the unit cube, the positive-real resolvent of the tensor Volterra operator has exact exponential type \(d\bigl(\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{f(t)>0}\prod_j(1-t_j)\bigr)^{1/d}\). In two dimensions, equal coordinate-marginal supports can have different local resolvent types, so the invariant detects genuine joint support geometry.
