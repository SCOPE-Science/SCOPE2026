For every characteristic-zero rank-one rectangular determinantal cone, the intrinsic differential Fitting singular ideal is exactly the \((a-1)(b-1)\)-st power of the vertex ideal. Consequently the full hypersimplex toric singular scheme has an explicit fat-point length at each coordinate vertex, giving degree \(\binom nr\sum_{d=0}^{(r-1)(n-r-1)-1}\binom{r+d-1}{d}\binom{n-r+d-1}{d}\).
