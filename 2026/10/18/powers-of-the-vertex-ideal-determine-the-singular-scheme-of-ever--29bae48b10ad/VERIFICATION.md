# Verification
The exact proof uses the conormal presentation for Kähler differentials, one explicit \(c\times c\) Jacobian subdeterminant, and characteristic-zero irreducibility of a product-group homogeneous coordinate representation. No finite computation implies the all-size result.

Run the accompanying Python 3 source with `python3 hypersimplex_fitting_check.py`. SymPy is required for symbolic pivot minors. The script checks coefficient spaces of \(2\times m\) determinants for \(m=2,3,4,5\) over \(\mathbf F_{101}\) and \(\mathbf F_{103}\); it also checks the distinguished Jacobian pivot minors for five rectangular shapes up to \(4\times4\). It prints `VERIFY_OK` when successful. It does not verify irreducibility of all symmetric-power representations (a characteristic-zero theorem) or exhaustive coverage of the mathematical literature.

The explicit chart proof applies to the toric semigroup generated by \(r\times(n-r)\) exchange roots. Quotienting the rank-one semigroup ring by the \(c\)-th power of its irrelevant maximal ideal gives Hilbert function \(\binom{a+d-1}{d}\binom{b+d-1}{d}\) in degrees \(0\le d<c\), yielding exact singular-scheme lengths. No independent validation has been performed.
