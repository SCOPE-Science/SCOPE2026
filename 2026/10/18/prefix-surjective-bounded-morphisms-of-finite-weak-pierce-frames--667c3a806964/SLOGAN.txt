On finite weak-Pierce Kripke frames whose levels all have size at least two, every bounded morphism consists of levelwise-surjective prefix maps followed by either a constant tail or one arbitrary last-level map. This gives exact formulas for all bounded morphisms, image-rank polynomials, idempotents and automorphisms, including a \(22\)-element endomorphism monoid with \(7\) idempotents on the three-level two-point frame.
