For the two-by-two matrix anticommutator \((X,Y)\mapsto XY+YX\) over every finite field, all prescribed-output fiber cardinalities admit explicit formulas in the field size and the target's rational kernel and nonzero-eigenline counts. In characteristic two, all nonzero traceless targets have one common fiber size, while odd characteristic exhibits five nonscalar fiber types; the zero-fiber count is already related to established anticommuting-matrix enumeration.
