For the reduced denominator of \(\sum_{j=1}^{n}1/\gcd(n,j)\), the proportion divisible by a fixed prime \(\ell\) is zero for \(\ell=3\) or \(\ell\equiv1\pmod6\), exactly one-half for \(\ell=2\), and a strictly positive explicit Euler product for \(\ell\equiv5\pmod6\). The dichotomy is governed by roots of the sixth cyclotomic polynomial, and the density at five is approximately \(0.1992415\).
