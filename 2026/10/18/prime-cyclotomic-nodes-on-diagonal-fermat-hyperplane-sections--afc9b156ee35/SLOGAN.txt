For every odd prime \(p\), the trace-zero hyperplane section of the degree-\(p+1\) Fermat hypersurface is smooth unless \(p\) divides its number of coordinates; in the divisible case all singularities are nodes and the complete singular scheme splits into copies of \(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Q(\zeta_p)\). Both the geometric node count and its symmetric-group and Galois orbit structure have closed formulas.
