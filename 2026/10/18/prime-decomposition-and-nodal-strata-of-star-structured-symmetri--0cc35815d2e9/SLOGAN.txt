For symmetric arrowhead determinant hypersurfaces with \(m\ge3\) leaves in characteristic different from two, the radical corank-two ideal has exactly \(m+\binom m3\) minimal primes: \(m\) determinantal complete intersections and \(\binom m3\) coordinate linear spaces. Every component is generically transversely nodal, and the reduced singular locus has degree \(\binom{m+2}{3}\).
