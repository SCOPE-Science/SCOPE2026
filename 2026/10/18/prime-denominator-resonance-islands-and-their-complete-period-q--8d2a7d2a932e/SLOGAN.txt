For every odd prime \(q\ge5\), the entire period-dividing-\(q\) locus of the symmetric two-half-plane rotation is either empty or exactly two regular \(q\)-gons, depending on rotation orientation. A cyclotomic rigidity argument excludes all mixed-sign itineraries and gives the exact total area \(\frac q2\cot^2(\pi p/q)\tan(\pi/q)\), uniquely maximized at \(p=q-1\).
