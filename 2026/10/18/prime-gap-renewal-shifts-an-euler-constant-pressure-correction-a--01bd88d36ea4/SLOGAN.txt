For the symbolic shift whose permitted runs of zeros have prime lengths, the marker-penalized pressure has the sharp low-activity denominator \(t+\log t-\gamma\), and the maximal entropy at marker density \(\rho\) loses \(\rho\log\log(1/\rho)\) relative to unrestricted binary data. Under equilibrium conditioning, the suitably rescaled marker positions converge to a unit-rate Poisson process, with an explicit first logarithmic correction to their Palm gap distribution.
