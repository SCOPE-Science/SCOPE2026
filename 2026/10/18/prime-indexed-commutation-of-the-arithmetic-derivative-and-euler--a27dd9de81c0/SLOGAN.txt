For \(a,b\ge2\), the arithmetic derivative commutes with Euler's totient at \(2^a3^b\) exactly when \(a=2u\) and \(3u+b\) is prime. The number of such exponent pairs with \(a+b\le X\) is asymptotic to \(X^2/(4\log X)\), and each fixed \(b\ge2\) supports infinitely many pairs precisely when \(3\nmid b\).
