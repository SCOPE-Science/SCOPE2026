For the type-\(A_n\) Lipman toric variety, each distinct prime dividing \(n+1\) contributes exactly one singular component, with an explicit generic transverse cyclic quotient; its singular locus is isolated exactly when \(n+1\) is prime. The torus-fixed minimal log discrepancy is \(n/2\), and the variety is terminal exactly when \(n\ge4\).
