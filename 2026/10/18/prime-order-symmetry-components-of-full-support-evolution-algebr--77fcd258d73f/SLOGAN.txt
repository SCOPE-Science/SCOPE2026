For every dimension \(n\ge4\), the geometric locus of full-support perfect evolution algebras with nontrivial ordinary automorphisms has irreducible components indexed exactly by prime-order permutation subgroups, with codimension \(r(p-1)(2n-pr-1)\) for cycle type \(p^r1^{n-pr}\). In a uniformly random such algebra over \(\mathbb F_q\), the probability of extra symmetry is \(\binom n2q^{-(2n-3)}+O_n(q^{-(2n-2)})\) as \(q\to\infty\), with transpositions providing all leading contributions.
