Every power of \(2\) has polynomial divisors of all possible degrees over every prime field. For odd prime \(r\), \(X^{r^a}-1\) has this property exactly for \(p=r\) or \(p\equiv\pm1\pmod r\) when \(a=1\), and exactly for \(p=r\), \(p\equiv1\pmod r\), or \(p\equiv-1\pmod{r^2}\) when \(a\ge2\). Each excluded odd-prime-power case first fails at degree \(2\) or \(r+1\), and the qualifying characteristics for \(a\ge2\) have natural density \((r+1)/(r(r-1))\).
