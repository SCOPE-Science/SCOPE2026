For every cobalancing-square window length \(k\), the prime-power divisibility of its global gcd is determined at every prime \(p\ge5\), \(p\ne7\), by the order of \(3+2\sqrt2\) modulo \(p\). The prime five is exceptional: its valuation is \(1+\nu_5(k)\) exactly at lengths divisible by six, while for \(p\ge11\) the valuation is \(\nu_p(k)\) exactly when that order divides \(k\).
