The deficit \(2^n-D(n)\) for Pongsriiam's relatively-prime-subset divisor sum is asymptotic, uniformly in \(n\), to \(2^{\lfloor n/r(n)\rfloor}\), where \(r(n)\) is its least nondivisor. Every such \(r(n)\) is a prime power, and the normalized logarithmic deficit has an exact atomic limiting law with weight \((1-1/p)/\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,p^a-1)\) at \(1/p^a\); the same structure gives exponential separation between the two deficits considered in the original question.
