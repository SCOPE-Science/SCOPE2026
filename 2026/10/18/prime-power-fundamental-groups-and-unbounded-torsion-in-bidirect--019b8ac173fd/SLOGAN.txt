Every dihedral-subgroup quotient of the bidirected polygon multipath complex on \(n\ge6\) vertices is simply connected except its full cyclic quotient, whose fundamental group is \(\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z\) exactly when \(n\) is a power of the prime \(p\). For every prime \(p\ge7\), that cyclic quotient also has \(\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z\)-homology in every odd degree through \((p-5)/2\), producing torsion in arbitrarily many degrees absent from the simply connected covering complex.
