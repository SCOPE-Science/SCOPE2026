# Finite verification and proof limits

Run `python verify_orbit_quotients.py` with Python 3; no third-party packages are required. `verification_output.txt` records the full output. The program constructs every directed arc of the bidirected polygon for \(6\le n\le60\), checks rotation/reflection arrow orbits for the directed multipath property, and enumerates all rotational and reflection-containing subgroups of the dihedral group. For each subgroup it computes the subgroup generated by geometric-fixed-point elements and checks the predicted quotient index. The final section independently forms signed cellular quotient boundaries for the free sevenfold cyclic action at dimensions zero to two, verifies their composition is exactly zero, and calculates first homology over the fields of characteristics \(2,3,5,7,11\). The characteristic-seven result is one-dimensional and the others vanish.

The infinite claim is not inferred from the finite enumeration. Its proof is the exact barycentric-support classification of fixed rotations, the explicit two-arrow witnesses for reflections, the cited simple-connectivity theorem, Armstrong's orbit-space theorem, elementary normal-subgroup arithmetic, and the homology Cartan–Leray vanishing-line argument. The checker cannot establish the premise of Armstrong’s theorem or detect unindexed prior literature; these are separately reasoned and cited. No independent audit was performed.
