The cyclic geometric quotient of the bounded-independence sphere \(B_d(C_{2d})\) has fundamental group and integral first homology \(\mathbb Z/p\) at odd-prime-power \(d=p^a\), \(\mathbb Z/4\) at two-power \(d\), and zero otherwise, for every \(d\ge3\). Point-isotropy rotations, rather than rational homology characters, govern this sharp prime-power torsion classification.
