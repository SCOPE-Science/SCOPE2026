Unindexed equivalent treatments and unstated consequences of repunit theory remain residual novelty risks.

The independent density estimate measures the sparsity of the admissible digit lengths, which is not supplied by sparse magnitude alone. This is not a trivial substitution in the referenced Novák theorem: the base-ten repunit valuations at the prime \(3\), the initial-prime obstruction, and the quadratic character of \(10\) control different arithmetic. The result does not assert optimal asymptotics or solve the finite-seed conjecture.
