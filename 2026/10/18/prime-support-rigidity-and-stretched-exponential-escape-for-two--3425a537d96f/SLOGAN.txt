For each of the two quadratic divide-or-choose maps with odd branch \(n(n\pm1)/2\), every escaping orbit gains a new, disjoint prime factor at each odd return. Even when every intervening division by two is counted, its growth satisfies \(\liminf_{t\to\infty}\log x_t/\sqrt{t\log t}\ge\sqrt{(\log2)/2}\).
