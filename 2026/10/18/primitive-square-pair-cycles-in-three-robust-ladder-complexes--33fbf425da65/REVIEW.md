# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** A complete proof supplies explicit three-cycles from exterior products of square boundaries. Each pair has a uniquely detecting four-vertex cell with coefficient a unit and zero coefficients for the other pairs. The published all-grid sphere-wedge theorem gives exactly the number of top integral homology generators, so the coefficient map is an isomorphism over the integers. The two involutions act on each source boundary by a minus sign and on every square pair without a sign. A standalone verifier checks all integer cycle equations and action identities for nine ladder sizes, plus independent mod-two top boundary ranks.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** The 2026 original paper determines homotopy type and abstract rank, not an integral cycle basis. Publicly indexed related statements determine rational symmetry representations or virtual rational characters (and, in one case, the Alexander-dual top homology) but not a primitive integral lattice presentation. Rational characters do not determine integral permutation versus split trivial/sign lattices. Full text of those publicly indexed related records was not accessible, and an implicit equivalent lemma remains a risk.

**Value — PASS.** The canonical elementary square-pair cycles give a natural geometric integer basis, with a unit-coefficient test that rules out lattice-index ambiguity. This supplies an integral representation and exact free coinvariants for every ladder length, answering a structurally motivated question left undecided by abstract wedge decompositions and rational character calculations. The proof does not claim higher-dimensional-grid extensions or quotient homology.
