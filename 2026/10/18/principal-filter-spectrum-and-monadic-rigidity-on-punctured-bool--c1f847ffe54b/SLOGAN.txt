On the distributive nearlattice of nonempty subsets of an \(n\)-set, exactly \(2^{n^2}-(2^n-1)^n+n!\) of the \(2^{n^2}\) filter-valued quasi-modal operators admit an ordinary unary necessity representation; the proportion tends to zero like \(n/2^n\). Among the \(B_n\) monadic quasi-modal operators, only identity necessity is unary representable.
