For an arbitrary graph with \(k_i\ge1\) independent private leaves at each core vertex, every locating-dominating set is determined locally and its complete counting polynomial is \(z^{\sum_i k_i}\prod_i(k_i+1+z)\), independently of the core edges. Exactly \(2^{|\{i:k_i=1\}|}\) ordered dominating/locating-dominating bipartitions exist, and two locating-dominating parts are possible precisely when all bundles have one leaf.
