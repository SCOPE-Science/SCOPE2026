# Review of the product-rank distribution theorem

## Correctness — PASS
For every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\) admitting a nonzero nonidentity twist, every matrix \(A\), and every ordered pair of idempotents \(X,Y\) in its twisted code, the proof first derives \(AX=XA=AY=YA=0\) with no characteristic exception. The full two-sided annihilator algebra is identified with a sandwich matrix algebra having identity block size \(t\) and radical defect \(s\). Each rank-\(r\) idempotent lifts a rank-\(r\) projection \(P\) in exactly \(q^{2sr}\) ways. Matrix factorization proves \(\operatorname{rank}(XY)=\operatorname{rank}(PQ)\) for arbitrary, potentially noncommuting factors. A Grassmann intersection count yields the exact number of rank-\(u\) projections \(Q\) producing each product rank \(v\), including boundary cases. Multiplying the independently counted choices gives the displayed trivariate coefficient. The proof remains valid for \(s=0\), \(t=0\), and characteristic two extension fields.

The standalone checker exhaustively enumerated every ambient matrix for 24 small field-and-twist tests, directly testing idempotence, twisted commutation with \(A\), and ranks of every ordered product. A separate check exhaustively enumerated all \(19683\) matrices of the \((t,s,q)=(2,1,3)\) sandwich algebra and all \(36100\) idempotent pairs. Every triple-rank coefficient agreed with the theorem. Finite tests are corroboration, not a substitute for the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS within inspected coverage
Muchlis et al. (arXiv:2110.01826v1, 2021) give bases/dimensions of twisted centralizer codes; Alahmadi et al. (arXiv:1608.04079, 2016) supply the twist-product law, whose elementary idempotent-annihilation consequence is acknowledged as prior. Dolinka and East (arXiv:1503.03139, 2015; primary sandwich semigroup source) structurally characterize regular elements, Green relations and idempotent-generated subsemigroups, including products of idempotents; the inspected full text does not present an all-parameter trivariate distribution of factor ranks and product ranks. Yu, Luo and Li (arXiv:2407.00892, 2024) investigate Munn idempotent generation and zero-product determination, rather than this rank-refined product census.

The closest previously recorded mathematical statement is a complete **commuting-only** joint-rank polynomial for idempotents of rectangular sandwich semigroups. That result counts just pairs satisfying \(XY=YX\), whereas the present theorem counts all ordered pairs and their product ranks, including genuinely noncommuting pairs and products that are not idempotent. Neither the commuting-only polynomial nor Green-class cardinalities determine these noncommuting fibers. The Gaussian intersection count itself is a known standard ingredient, as is the ordinary \(s=0\) framework; novelty is assessed only for its complete sandwich/twisted-code rank-triplet multiplicities. Equivalent enumerators in older matrix-factorization or finite semigroup literature remain a substantive originality risk. Missing matches in search results do not prove uniqueness.

## Value — PASS
The rank of the product of two idempotent operators records how far the image of one projection meets the kernel of the other; this is central to the multiplication geometry of the idempotent-generated matrix semigroup, not an arbitrary invariant. The result classifies *every* attainable triple of ranks and counts every fiber over all finite fields and all sandwich defects. The \(q^{2s(r+u)}\) factor shows how the radical kernel-image intersection changes actual matrix-pair counts, while the Gaussian factor determines the entire variation of product rank at fixed factor ranks. For \(q=3\), \((t,s)=(2,1)\), rank-one pairs divide into exactly 2916 with zero product and 8748 with rank-one product; commuting-only statistics do not give that partition. Although the derivation relies on classical Grassmannian enumeration, the complete parameter-free answer to this naturally motivated multiplication question is nontrivial and reproducible.

## Limits and risks
There is no claim for zero/identity twists, products of arbitrary codewords, or specified product matrices' factorization multiplicities. Structural results and possible exact earlier formulas under alternative terminology are acknowledged. A self-review is not an independent mathematical audit.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
