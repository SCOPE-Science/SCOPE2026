Among regular plant–pollinator networks with \(q^2+q+1\) species on each side and degree \(q+1\), finite-projective-plane incidence webs simultaneously minimize the variance of plant coextinctions for every random pollinator-removal count. They are exactly the networks that prohibit two plants from going extinct together after only \(2q\) pollinator losses, even though all competing regular webs have the same mean extinction curve.
