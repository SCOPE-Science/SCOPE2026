# Projective rigidity and non-real descent of Pokora's nearly-free degree-nine arrangement

## Finding
The nearly-free complex plane arrangement exhibited in Piotr Pokora, *On the Numerical Terao Conjecture*, Theorem 4.1, has projective automorphism group exactly \(C_2\), generated by the involution
\[
\tau:[x:y:z]\longmapsto[x:y:2x+y-z].
\]
Its complex conjugate is **not** projectively equivalent to it. Consequently, its projective equivalence class has absolute field of moduli exactly \(\mathbb Q(i)\), and it has no defining equation over \(\mathbb R\) after any complex projective coordinate change. This statement concerns the specified embedded curve and does not exclude another real nearly-free arrangement with the same weak combinatorics.

## Assumptions and scope
Work in \(\mathbb P^2_{\mathbb C}\) and choose \(i\) with \(i^2=-1\). Put
\[
L=xyz(x-y)(2x-z)(y-z)(2x+y-z),
\qquad Q=z^2-(2x+y)z+(1-i)xy.
\]
Let \(C=V(LQ)\) be the reduced degree-nine plane curve. The cited theorem establishes that \(C\) has seven line components and one smooth conic, with weak combinatorics \((7,1;8A_1+D_4+4X_9)\), and is nearly free with exponents \((3,6)\). All projectivities below are elements of \(\operatorname{PGL}_3(\mathbb C)\), acting on the embedded curve. The field-of-moduli assertion is relative to \(\overline{\mathbb Q}/\mathbb Q\).

## Proof
**(1) Distinguishing the line subsystem.** A projectivity preserving \(C\) permutes its irreducible components and preserves degree; therefore it preserves the seven-line arrangement \(V(L)\). Pairwise intersections of the seven lines have precisely five multiplicity-three points and six multiplicity-two points. The five triple points are
\[
P_0=[0:0:1],\quad P_1=[0:1:0],\quad P_2=[0:1:1],\quad
P_3=[1:0:0],\quad P_4=[1:0:2].
\]
Among these five points, the only collinear triples are \((P_0,P_1,P_2)\) and \((P_0,P_3,P_4)\). Hence every projectivity preserving the lines fixes \(P_0\), and preserves or interchanges the two lines \(x=0\) and \(y=0\). The third line of the arrangement through \(P_0\), namely \(x-y=0\), must be fixed.

**(2) Exhausting line-arrangement projectivities.** A projectivity preserving \(x=0\), \(y=0\), and \(x-y=0\) separately has (after one common scalar normalization) the form
\[
[x:y:z]\mapsto[x:y:c x+d y+e z],\qquad e\ne0.
\]
The pair \(\{P_1,P_2\}\) imposes either \((d,e)=(0,1)\) or \((d,e)=(1,-1)\); the pair \(\{P_3,P_4\}\) imposes either \((c,e)=(0,1)\) or \((c,e)=(2,-1)\). The only mutually compatible possibilities are the identity and \(\tau\).

If \(x=0\) and \(y=0\) are interchanged, the transformation has instead the form
\[
[x:y:z]\mapsto[y:x:c x+d y+e z].
\]
The images of \(P_1,P_2\) demand \(e=2\) or \(e=-2\), whereas the images of \(P_3,P_4\) demand \(e=1/2\) or \(e=-1/2\); hence there is no such transformation. Thus the line subsystem has full projective stabilizer \(\{1,\tau\}\). Conversely, direct substitution verifies that \(\tau\) permutes all seven lines, so both possibilities genuinely occur.

**(3) The conic and full stabilizer.** Write \(s=2x+y\), so that \(Q=z(z-s)+(1-i)xy\). The involution replaces \(z\) by \(s-z\); consequently \(z(z-s)\) is unchanged, and so is \(Q\). Therefore
\[
\operatorname{Stab}_{\operatorname{PGL}_3(\mathbb C)}(C)
=\{1,\tau\}\cong C_2.
\]

**(4) No real projective form.** Complex conjugation fixes the seven lines and replaces \(Q\) by
\[
\overline Q=z^2-(2x+y)z+(1+i)xy.
\]
Any projectivity carrying \(C\) to \(\overline C\) would preserve the common seven-line subsystem and thus be either \(1\) or \(\tau\). Each fixes \(Q\) and also fixes \(\overline Q\) separately. But \(Q\) and \(\overline Q\) are not proportional: their \(z^2\)-coefficients both equal one, while their \(xy\)-coefficients differ. Thus no such projectivity exists.

A curve projectively equivalent to a curve defined over \(\mathbb R\) would be projectively equivalent to its complex conjugate, contrary to this calculation. Since this curve is defined over \(\mathbb Q(i)\) and its nontrivial conjugate over \(\mathbb Q\) is not projectively equivalent, its absolute projective field of moduli is exactly \(\mathbb Q(i)\).

Gaussian-integer polynomial arithmetic verifies the conic identities, inequivalence of the two conjugate conics, and \(\tau^2=1\). ## Relationship to prior work
Pokora's 2026 Theorem 4.1 supplies the arrangement, its nearly-free status and weak combinatorics. Its Problem 4.3 asks whether a Numerical Terao counterexample can be constructed over \(\mathbb R\). The projective stabilizer and obstruction to real descent asserted here do not appear in its inspected Section 4. Pokora's earlier classification already established a **free** real seven-line/one-conic arrangement with the same weak combinatorics. The result here restricts only the **specific nearly-free** curve in the 2026 construction; it does not settle the real counterexample problem.

## Limitations
No claim is made about the existence of other real free/nearly-free pairs with matching weak combinatorics, or about arbitrary nonprojective transformations, abstract automorphisms of components, or changes of the embedding. The impossibility is for real *projective models* of the specified complex embedded arrangement. Unindexed descriptions of this particular symmetry and descent obstruction remain an originality risk; no independence or external validation is asserted.
