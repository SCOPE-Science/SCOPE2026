Sun's normalized binomial convolution satisfies \(s_n\equiv8\pmod{16}\) precisely at powers of two and \(s_n\equiv0\pmod{16}\) otherwise, proving the parity criterion recorded in OEIS A189286. An exact binary-carry valuation of each summand and reflection pairing yield the stronger bound \(\nu_2(s_n)\ge s_2(3n)+2\) away from powers of two.
