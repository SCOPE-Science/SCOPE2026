# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For a>=3 and m=F_a, the cited Zeckendorf Apéry theorem gives w(x)=x+β(x)m for residues 0<=x<m, with β the number of nonconsecutive Fibonacci summands F_2..F_{a-1}. Maximality in the semigroup order is equivalent to no generator addition yielding a strictly higher Apéry representative; non-wrapping jumps require β(x+F_j)≤β(x), and wrapping jumps cannot raise the required count by the cited coin-reduction lemma. A non-dominating independent set admits insertion of an isolated index and therefore is not maximal. For a dominating set with 2 present, Fibonacci identities 2F_j=F_{j+1}+F_{j-2} (j>=4), 2F_3=F_4+F_2 and 2F_2=F_3 reduce doubled occupied digits without increasing minimal coin count. Dominating sets beginning at 3 fail unless they are exactly the alternating 3,5,...,a-1 set for even a (boundary x=F_a-1). The standard Apéry PF=w-m identity converts all maximal words to the explicit spectrum. The first-2-containing independent dominating binary words correspond to compositions into 2/3 blocks and satisfy Padovan count P_{a-2}; the even exceptional word adds 1. Independent Dijkstra residual shortest-path method, separately enumerated independent dominating words, confirmed full PF values and types a=3..14. None of these finite checks certifies a universal theorem in lieu of proof.

Resultary and web searches with type/PF/Padovan aliases found related Fibonacci-triple semigroups, not this exact result; best-of-knowledge only.
