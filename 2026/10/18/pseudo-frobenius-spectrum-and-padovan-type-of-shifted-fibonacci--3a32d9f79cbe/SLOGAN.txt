The pseudo-Frobenius numbers of the shifted-Fibonacci semigroup \(S(a)=\langle F_a+F_j:j\ge0\rangle\) are indexed by dominating nonconsecutive Fibonacci digit sets containing index \(2\), plus one alternating exceptional set for even \(a\). Its type is exactly \(P_{a-2}+\mathbf1_{2\mid a}\), where \(P_1=P_2=P_3=1\) and \(P_n=P_{n-2}+P_{n-3}\).
