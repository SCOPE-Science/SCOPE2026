For postselected Laplacian quantum stochastic walks on stars, any fixed nonzero imperfect-detection efficiency suppresses the hub's stationary weight as \(m^{-2}\), while total site-basis coherence decays as \(m^{-1}\) despite its persistence on finite networks. Detection efficiency scaled as \(\eta=\xi/m\) produces a different sharp hub-weight crossover, \(m\rho_{00}\to(1+\xi)^{-1}\).
