For two-sorted modal frames with \(n\) points of each sort and independent incidence probability \(c/\sqrt n\), the number of stable window-modality propositions is \(n^2F(c)+O_{L^1,c}(n^{3/2})\), where \(F(c)=1-e^{-c^2}(1+c^2+c^4/2)+(c^4/4)e^{-2c^2}\). The coefficient comes from a sharp decomposition into two-sided concepts with a side of two and maximal \(2\)-by-\(2\) rectangles; the result is an \(L^1\) law, not an almost-sure assertion.
