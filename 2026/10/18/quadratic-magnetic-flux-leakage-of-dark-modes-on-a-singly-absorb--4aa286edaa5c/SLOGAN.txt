For a finite quantum ring with one absorbing site, detuning magnetic flux from zero or half a flux quantum gives every protected dark eigenvalue the exact leading imaginary shift \(-i[2J^2\sin^2(q)/(\gamma N)]\delta^2\). The quadratic real-energy shift vanishes identically, the next possible correction is fourth order, and the no-jump probability decay rate is twice the magnitude of that imaginary shift.
