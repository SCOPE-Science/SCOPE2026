The incidence matrix between maximal commuting positive-root sets of the strictly upper-triangular type-A Lie algebra and individual root vectors has only \(1+(n-2)+\binom{n-2}{2}\) independent rows, despite having \(2^{n-2}\) rows, and every nonzero Smith factor is \(1\). Its complete integral kernel consists of Boolean finite differences of order at least three; rationally, exactly the higher Walsh modes are invisible to all single-root measurements, with the first failure of recovery at \(n=5\).
