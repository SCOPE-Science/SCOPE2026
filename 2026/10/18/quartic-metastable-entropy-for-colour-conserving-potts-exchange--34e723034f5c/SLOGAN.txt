With energy-neutral exchanges suppressed, blocked colourings of the \(q\)-state ferromagnetic Potts ring have exact exponential count \(B_{N,q}\sim\lambda_q^N\), where a five-pattern transfer quotient gives a universal quartic equation for \(\lambda_q\). The familiar binary tribonacci exponent changes to a distinct three-colour quartic exponent, and the formula applies to every integer \(q\ge2\).
