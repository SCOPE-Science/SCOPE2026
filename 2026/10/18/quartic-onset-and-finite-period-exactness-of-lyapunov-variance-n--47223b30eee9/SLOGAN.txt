A two-harmonic perturbation of the doubling map is tangent to a smooth conjugacy, so its periodic Lyapunov-exponent variance is suppressed to exactly \(36\pi^4\varepsilon^4/n+O_n(|\varepsilon|^5)\) for every fixed period \(n\ge5\). The same coefficient \(36\pi^4\) governs the infinite-period central-limit variance, giving a concrete, sharp quartic onset of nonrigid fluctuations.
