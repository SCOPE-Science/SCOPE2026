For every complex Fermat hypersurface of degree \(d\ge4\) and ambient dimension at least two, its unlabelled **non-Galois** quasi-Galois centers alone recover the missing Galois coordinate vertices: the only lines containing four or more such centers are the coordinate edges. Their full ambient projective stabilizer is exactly \((\mu_d^r/\mu_d)\rtimes S_r\), and the Fermat hypersurface is the unique smooth invariant degree-\(d\) hypersurface.
