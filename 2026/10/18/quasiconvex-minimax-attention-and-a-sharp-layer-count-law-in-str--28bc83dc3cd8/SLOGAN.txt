For strategic consensus across network layers sharing one stationary distribution, the largest manipulation of *any* bounded private-opinion profile can be globally minimized by quasiconvex, second-order-cone feasibility. Perfect immunity requires a convex-hull cancellation, and a sharp family of distinct networks needs all \(n\) layers: with only \(m\), its exact best worst-case distortion is \(\delta(1-m/n)/(n+\delta+m)\).
