**Correctness — PASS.** The lift's three affine full branches were independently reconstructed from the exact functions in the motivating paper. The integer branch cocycle follows directly; the boundary exception is countable. Finite-cylinder exponential estimates give the upper dimension bound. Explicit sign-dependent digit product measures, a strong law for bounded independent variables, and superexponential alternation of endpoint-optimal distributions yield the lower bound even for every historic interval. A Frostman argument makes the cylinder-local estimate Euclidean. The proof includes limiting endpoint tilt distributions rather than extrapolating finite-tilt formulas. The exact-rational verifier independently checks 15,891 word itineraries and pressure normalization but does not substitute for the infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS with residual priority risk.** Li–Lloyd–Romaña prove the rotation interval, historical cluster-interval realizability, and full random entropy, but do not determine Hausdorff dimensions of the corresponding sets. Barreira–Li–Valls establish residuality for prescribed average accumulation intervals in autonomous symbolic dynamics, not this nonstationary fixed-environment dimension function. Liu–Liu consider a uniquely ergodic base and Anosov fibers, conditions inapplicable to the Bernoulli degree-one expanding circle model. General conditional multifractal/saturation techniques may imply extensions, but no inspected result asserts the explicit quenched all-interval formula for these maps. A stronger unpublished or unlocated specialization remains a priority risk.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The full regular and historic spectrum answers a natural quantitative question left after existence of every rotation and historic interval was established. The extremal-level dimensions are positive and the entire historic interval has an explicit positive dimension, unlike a mere nonemptiness statement. The result is a self-contained exact classification for a geometrically natural random expanding system, not a generic relabeling or arbitrary finite slice.

**Scope and limits.** The dimension formula is specific to the uniform three-branch model and frequency-regular environments; it does not cover general random degree-one maps, arbitrary distortions, or packing/measure refinements. The earlier paper's coding and rotation range are treated as known. No independent mathematical audit or publication-priority certification has been performed.
