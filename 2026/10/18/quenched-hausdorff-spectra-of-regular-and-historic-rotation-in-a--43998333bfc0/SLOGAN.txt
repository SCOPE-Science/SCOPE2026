In the three-branch Bernoulli random circle example, the initial points realizing a prescribed rotation-average cluster interval \([a,b]\) have Hausdorff dimension exactly \(\min\{D_p(a),D_p(b)\}\), where \(D_p\) is an explicit Legendre-transform spectrum. Even trajectories accumulating on the entire rotation interval \([p-1,p]\) form a set of strictly positive Hausdorff dimension \(\min\{p,1-p\}\log2/\log3\).
