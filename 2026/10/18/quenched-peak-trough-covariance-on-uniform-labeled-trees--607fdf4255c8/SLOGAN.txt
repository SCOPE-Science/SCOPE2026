For a uniformly random labeled tree carrying i.i.d. continuous vertex marks, the average *conditional-on-the-tree* covariance of the number of local maxima and the number of local minima grows as \(cn\) with a certified negative constant \(-0.03934225<c<-0.03934110\). On a path the analogous covariance is positive for every \(n\ge5\), revealing a geometry-dependent sign reversal.
