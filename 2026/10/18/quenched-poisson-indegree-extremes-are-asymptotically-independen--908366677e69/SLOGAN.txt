Revealing the entire labeled birthday-length orbit of a uniform random mapping changes the law of its extreme-indegree vertex count by at most \(2\mathbb E[T]\Pr\{\operatorname{Bin}(n,1/n)\ge k-1\}+\Pr\{\operatorname{Bin}(n,1/n)\ge k-2\}\) in total variation. At Poisson-critical indegree thresholds this is \(O(k/\sqrt n)\), so the number of extreme indegrees is asymptotically Poisson and independent of the limiting orbit-tail and orbit-cycle lengths.
