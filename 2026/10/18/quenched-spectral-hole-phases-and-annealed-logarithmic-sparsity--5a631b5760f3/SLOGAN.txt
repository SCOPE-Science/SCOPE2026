In a compound-Poisson grey spherical field with logarithmic frequency-dependent rates, the number of exactly absent harmonic degrees has a random almost-sure growth exponent \((1-cT_\beta)_+\), while its expectation grows instead on the sharply different scale \(L/\log L\). The complete spectral-hole phase diagram includes a known Dickman boundary law and a regime where the weighted hole count is almost surely finite but has infinite conditional mean.
