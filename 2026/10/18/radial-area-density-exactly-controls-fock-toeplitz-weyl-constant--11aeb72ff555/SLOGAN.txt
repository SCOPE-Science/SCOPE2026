For a positive power-decaying radial Toeplitz symbol on any uniformly Hessian-bounded radial Fock space in one complex variable, an exact Weyl coefficient exists **if and only if** the normalized radial slope \(\Phi'(r)/r\) converges, and the coefficient equals that limit. Without convergence, the complete cluster interval of normalized singular values is determined by the lower and upper radial slopes, with a uniform \(O(n^{-1/2})\) saddle-tracking error.
