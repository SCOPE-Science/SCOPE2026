There are real radial \(L^1\) symbols on the Bergman disk with arbitrarily small \(L^1\) norm whose Toeplitz operators are positive, bounded, and noncompact, yet whose Berezin transforms tend to zero at the boundary. An explicit inverse-Laplace family gives \(\|T_f\|_{\mathrm{ess}}\ge1\) while \(\|f\|_1\) is arbitrarily small, closing the radial-symbol gap left by previously known nonradial counterexamples.
