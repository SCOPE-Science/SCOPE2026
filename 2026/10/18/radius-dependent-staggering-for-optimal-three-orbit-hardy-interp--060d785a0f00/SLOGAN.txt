For three regular \(m\)-gons sampling analytic polynomials, two on radius \(r\) and one on radius \(s>r\), the unique determinant-maximizing relative angular stagger has an explicit radius-dependent radical formula rather than the usual equally spaced angles. At each fixed radius contrast, its squared interpolation determinant beats equal staggering by \((4/3)^m(1+o(1))\).
