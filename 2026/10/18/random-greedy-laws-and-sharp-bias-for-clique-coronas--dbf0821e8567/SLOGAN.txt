The random-greedy maximal-independent-set law on any clique corona \(H\odot K_s\) is exactly a Bernoulli-thinned greedy law on its core, whereas uniform maximal-set sampling induces an independent-set Gibbs law. The two laws agree exactly when the core is edgeless, and for every fixed core their total-variation bias is \(|E(H)|/s^2+O_H(s^{-3})\).
