For a regular Reuleaux odd-\(n\)-gon of width \(w\), let \(R=w/(2\cos(\pi/(2n)))\) and \(\alpha=\pi/(2n)\); the convex hull of \(m\) independent uniformly rotated copies has an exact expected-perimeter formula, with deficit from the circumdisk asymptotic to \(8\pi R\alpha^2/m^2\). Its worst-direction Hausdorff deficit is of order \((\log m)^2/m^2\) and obeys a sharply normalized Gumbel law determined by the largest rotation-phase gap.
