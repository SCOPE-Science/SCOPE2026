For exact post-step polar spectral weight decay on an isotropic strongly convex matrix quadratic, generic positive singular values converge to a common nonzero level rather than vanish. Crossing \(\eta a=1\) changes the post-step attractor from a two-cycle to a fixed rank-flat matrix, while the pre-step two-cycle grows without bound relative to it as \(\eta a\uparrow2\); exact proximal shrinkage instead terminates at zero.
