For every matrix size \(n\ge2\), the first-order lift locus of \(A^2=0\) over dual numbers has exactly \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor+1\) geometric irreducible components, all of dimension \(n^2\), one for each reduction rank. A uniform Gaussian-binomial formula counts every square-zero matrix over \(\mathbb F_q[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)\), in every characteristic.
