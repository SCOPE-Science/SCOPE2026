For any single forbidden scattered subsequence \(P\), finitely many words have a common \(P\)-avoiding supersequence exactly when each word avoids \(P\); the minimum achievable greedy pattern-prefix rank is the maximum input rank. An inclusion-minimal avoiding common supersequence can be built in linear time for two inputs, and in \(O(kN+|P|)\) time for \(k\) inputs with delimiter-inclusive length \(N=\sum_i(|W_i|+1)\).
