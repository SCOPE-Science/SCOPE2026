# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** A rank-one linear map is \(R(X)=\operatorname{tr}(VX)U\). Substitution into the original weight-zero identity gives \(U^2=\lambda U\) and \(VU=aV,\ UV=bV\), with \(a+b=\lambda\) and \(a(a-\lambda)=b(b-\lambda)=0\); the two resulting normal forms are necessary and sufficient over any characteristic. The normal-form redundancy, geometric enumeration, and rank-one operator square-zero claim are proved explicitly. The original polynomial and predicted counts were exhaustively compared in three prime-field cases by the packaged checker, and the proof does not depend on those tests.

## Originality
**PASS, with residual bibliographic risk.** The full relevant Theorem 1.1 and introductory discussion of Gubarev, arXiv:2609.05647, concern operators of maximal minimal-polynomial degree in characteristic zero. The full relevant §5.1 Theorem 6 of Benito–Gubarev–Pozhidaev, arXiv:1801.07037, covers \(M_2\) over algebraically closed fields, including already-known rank-one equivalence types but not an arbitrary-dimension finite-field count. Focused mathematical-record searches for rank-one, decomposable tensor, finite-field, normal-form and enumeration equivalents found no result implying the new uniform claim. The possibility of an older unindexed rank-one classification or equivalent associative Yang–Baxter statement remains; no priority guarantee is made.

## Value
**PASS.** The maximal-degree matrix-operator classification motivates a complementary, naturally defined smallest-nonzero-rank stratum. This theorem fully classifies that stratum for all sizes and field characteristics, reveals an exact two-part geometric decomposition by square-zero versus idempotent image lines, and counts both strata over every finite field. The two counts are an exact invariant unavailable from a single small-dimension orbit classification.

## Closest literature and limitations
Gubarev (2026), arXiv:2609.05647, treats the maximal-index, characteristic-zero case, rather than operator rank one. Benito–Gubarev–Pozhidaev (2018), arXiv:1801.07037, gives the dimension-two characteristic-not-two equivalence classes. Exhaustive checks are limited to the packaged three small cases; no higher-rank operator result is claimed. Equivalent formulations via associative Yang–Baxter tensors remain a literature risk.
