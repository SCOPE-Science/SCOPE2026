For every split-semisimple selfadjoint target over an odd finite field, the complete rank distribution of its ordered decompositions into two square-zero selfadjoint operators factors into orthogonal-isometry torsors and isotropic-subspace counts on the zero eigenspace. The only nonzero-eigenvalue obstructions are mismatched opposite eigenspace dimensions and an explicit determinant square-class condition; every permitted summand rank occurs.
