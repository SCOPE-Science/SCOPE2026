For every \(n\ge3\) in characteristic different from two, the projective rank-at-most-two locus of symmetric zero-diagonal \(n\times n\) matrices is a reduced union of \(2^{n-1}-1\) Segre varieties, with degree \(\frac12\binom{2n-2}{n-1}\). Its entire singular support consists of \(n(2^{n-2}-1)\) Segre components of degree summing to \(\frac n2\binom{2n-4}{n-2}\), and every local germ is a product of transverse double crossings times a smooth factor.
