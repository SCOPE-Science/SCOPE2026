## correctness

PASS

A simple d-polytope with d+2 facets is combinatorially Delta_p × Delta_q, p,q>=1, p+q=d, via the standard dual Gale 1D class theorem. Proper nonempty product faces correspond to nonempty A,B subsets of each factor vertex set. No proper common containing face exactly when A∪C=I, B∪D=J. Complementary rank dimension bounds force equality |A|+|B|+|C|+|D|=d+2 and therefore disjoint partitions A,C and B,D, with |A|+|B|=k+1. This yields coefficient [z^(k+1)] G_(p+1)G_(q+1). Identity G_(j+1)=(1+z)G_j+z+z^j and binomial difference gives positive coefficients at every rank for transfer (p,q)->(p+1,q-1) when q>=p+2; endpoints prism and balanced product, total 4(2^p-1)(2^q-1). Independently enumerated every ordered face pair for all products d=2..6 (55 rank/shape checks) and exact-integer strict balance inequalities d=3..19, all agreeing with analytic proof.
