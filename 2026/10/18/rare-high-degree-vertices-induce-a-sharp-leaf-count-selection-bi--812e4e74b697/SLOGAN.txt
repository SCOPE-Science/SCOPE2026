At the critical maximum-degree window of a uniform labeled tree, conditioning on the presence of a rare high-degree vertex shifts the expected number of leaves by a positive quantity proportional to the critical degree threshold. The sharp covariance law is \(\operatorname{Cov}(L_n,\mathbf1_{\{\Delta_n\ge k_n+1\}})/k_n\to\lambda e^{-\lambda-1}\).
