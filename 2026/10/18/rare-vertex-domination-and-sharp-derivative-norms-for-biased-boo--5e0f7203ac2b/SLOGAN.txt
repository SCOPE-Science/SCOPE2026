Every biased Bernoulli product noise semigroup has a single rare input vertex that simultaneously maximizes the exact \(L^1\) and \(L^\infty\) norms of all its parameter and time derivatives, by a parameter-independent stochastic channel. For identical biases, its derivative norms obey explicit binomial formulas, fixed-time Hermite limits, and a sharp Poisson boundary-layer transition from square-root to linear growth.
