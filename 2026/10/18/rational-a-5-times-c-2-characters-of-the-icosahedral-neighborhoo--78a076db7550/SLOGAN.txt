The icosahedral graph’s open-neighborhood complex has a \(23\)-dimensional second rational homology representation with complete \(A_5\times C_2\) decomposition \((V_6\oplus V_5)\boxtimes\mathbf1\oplus(2\mathbf1\oplus2V_5)\boxtimes\varepsilon\). Its quotient by rotations has two rational two-cycles, whereas its quotient by all symmetries is rationally acyclic.
