# Scientific review: rational automorphism characters of \(\mathcal M(K_{2,n})\)

## Correctness
**PASS.** The domain is all integers \(n\ge2\) for the ordinary acyclic-incidence-matching complex of the graph \(K_{2,n}\), with the explicitly specified natural \(C_2\times S_n\)-action. The Jojić homotopy theorem supplies the degree-\(n\) concentration, and signed simplicial Lefschetz gives each character as an exact whole-orbit face sum. The proof separates the no-swap and swap cases, checks each possible admissible outgoing-arrow pattern and directed-four-cycle exclusion, tracks orientation and Euler-sign conventions, and derives both character formulas. The augmentation, exterior-square, and unordered-pair characters add to exactly those formulas. Finite exact checks test all conjugacy classes through \(n=6\) and compute independent mod-two boundary ranks through \(n=5\), without substituting finite data for the general proof.

## Originality
**PASS, qualified.** Jojić (2012), Corollary 3.1, covers this *same* directed-forest complex (after reversing arcs) and already proves the nonequivariant sphere-wedge classification. A later publicly posted exact-family result gives that same known count. Lin–Scoville (2019) computes complex automorphism groups; Scoville (2026) develops other Morse-complex structural topology. None supplies these natural action traces or the rational module decomposition. Statement-level checks examined cyclic, directed-tree, rooted-forest, permutation-module, equivariant-homology and exact-family aliases. Unindexed equivalent work remains a priority risk. The claim makes no novelty assertion for the homotopy type.

## Value
**PASS.** Rational representation type is not implied by the homotopy type or the abstract existence of graph symmetries. The formula specifies every group element, detects its fixed and two-cycle counts and the parity-dependent left interchange, and identifies canonical permutation-derived summands for the entire naturally parameterized family. It is a motivated equivariant refinement rather than an arbitrary numerical table. Scope is rational, not integral or equivariant homotopy.

## Limitations and references
The precise full-automorphism action for the four-cycle case and integral representation lattices are not treated. Relevant primary texts are Jojić (2012), DOI:10.2298/PIM1206043J; Lin–Scoville, arXiv:1904.10907; and Scoville, arXiv:2604.18172v1. The topological calculation from the 2012 paper is prior work used as an input.
