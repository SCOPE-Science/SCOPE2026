For every \(n\ge2\), the natural \(C_2\times S_n\)-action on the top rational homology of the Morse complex of \(K_{2,n}\) has exact character \(\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)(f(\sigma)^2-1)\) without a left swap and \((-1)^n\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)(f(\sigma)-2t(\sigma)-1)\) with a left swap. This determines its complete \((n^2-1)\)-dimensional rational representation, refining the known nonequivariant sphere-wedge theorem.
