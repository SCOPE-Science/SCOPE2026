Among the 32 geometrically smooth pointless plane-quartic classes over \(\mathbf F_7\), the rational-bitangent counts are exactly \(0,1,2,4,6,8\), with frequencies \(4,16,7,2,2,1\), and the bound eight has a unique extremizer.
