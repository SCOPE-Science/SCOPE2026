For every smooth split cubic threefold \(F(u,v)+G(x,y,z)=0\), quotienting by the order-three scaling of \((x,y,z)\) gives a rational \(\mathbb Q\)-Fano threefold of anticanonical volume \(8\) with precisely an elliptic \(\frac13(1,1)\) singular curve and three isolated \(\frac13(1,1,1)\) points. The quotient has rational middle Betti number \(4\) and weight-three Hodge numbers \(h^{2,1}=h^{1,2}=2\).
