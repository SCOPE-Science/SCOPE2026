For every even alphabet size \(q\), locally irreducible reverse-complement length-two words have a universal rational generating function with cubic denominator \(1-(q-1)z-(q-2)z^2-(q-1)z^3\). In the four-symbol DNA case their exponential growth rate is \(\log_4(3.74734654030721\ldots)=0.95293469938397\ldots\), despite the known zero capacity of codes correcting arbitrarily many such duplications.
