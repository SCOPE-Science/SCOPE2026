Among \(4,099,680\) geometrically smooth ternary plane-quartic equations, the joint numbers of rational ordinary flexes and rational hyperflexes fall into exactly seventeen strata. The sharp maximum of seven rational flex points occurs for exactly \(936\) equations in one projective orbit, represented by \(X^3Y+2X^3Z+XZ^3+Y^4=0\), whose seven rational points are all flexes.
