# Review of rational singularity types in the plane-filling pencil

## Correctness — PASS
For every odd prime power, differentiation at rational points yields the exact parametrization \(P_t=[t:1:t^3]\), with \(t^7+kt^3-1=0\). The explicit Hessian determinant \(4(4t^7-1)/t^2\) proves the node criterion and the discriminant \(4(1-4t^7)/t^2\) proves the rational branch-splitting criterion. When the Hessian degenerates, the unique tangent direction has a nonzero cubic term, verified separately for the exceptional field \(\mathbf F_3\); this establishes ordinary cusps, not merely a double tangent. Group theory gives the exact number of rational singularities of the member \(k=0\), and the distinct cusp-fiber count. The all-field proof is algebraic, supported by exact symbolic identities and finite-field checks, not extrapolated from finite enumeration.

## Originality — PASS
The closest primary text is Asgarli–Ghioca, *Plane-filling curves of small degree over finite fields* (arXiv:2307.03072v1): their equation (1) is this same pencil and their Theorem 1.2 states only the rational smoothness criterion. The relevant sections were read and compared by implication. That criterion supplies the candidate singular points but does not determine ordinary-node versus ordinary-cusp type, tangent splitting, or the exceptional cusp-bearing members. The general pencil paper *Smoothness in pencils of hypersurfaces over finite fields* (arXiv:2203.07169v1) addresses existence of pencils avoiding the discriminant, not local types of this pencil. Search comparisons of published mathematical research records found nearby work on other Hessians and singular families, but no equivalent claim for these objects. The remaining risk is an unindexed or differently phrased local computation in unrelated literature; search absence is not a mathematical proof of novelty.

## Value — PASS
The source studies geometric smoothness of this explicit low-degree plane-filling pencil, so a classification of its singular side is directly motivated. The result resolves the *complete arithmetic and geometric local type* of every rational singularity for all odd finite fields, including split/nonsplit branches, an exact cusp-parameter locus, and an infinite characteristic-three family with seven simultaneous rational cusps. These facts distinguish singular members that the known root-existence criterion treats identically and are useful local input for normalization, rational-point behavior, and further study of the source's open geometric smoothness conjecture. It does **not** claim that the algebraic-closure singular locus has been classified.

## Evidence and limitations
The exact symbolic and pointwise finite-field verifications are reproducible using `verify.py` and described in VERIFICATION.md. The primary source is available as the eight-page open preprint at https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03072. A second relevant primary full text is available at https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.07169. No conclusion about nonrational singularities, global irreducibility, or the general smoothness conjecture is made. Bibliographic completeness and independent validation remain unestablished.
