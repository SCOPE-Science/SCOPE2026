For the Asgarli–Ghioca degree-\(q+3\) plane-filling pencil over any odd finite field, every rational singularity is either a node or an ordinary cusp, with the exact transition \(4t^7=1\) and an explicit arithmetic criterion for splitting the nodal tangents. The zero-parameter member has seven rational cusps over \(\mathbf F_{3^{6m}}\), while outside characteristic three it has \(\gcd(7,q-1)\) rational nodes.
