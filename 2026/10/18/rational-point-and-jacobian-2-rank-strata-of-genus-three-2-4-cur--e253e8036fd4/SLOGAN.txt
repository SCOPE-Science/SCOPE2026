Smooth genus-three \((2,4)\) curves on the split quadric over \(\mathbf F_2\) have an exact \(17\)-stratum joint spectrum of rational-point counts and Jacobian \(2\)-ranks: \(9{,}216\) equations in \(294\) ruling-preserving orbits. Exactly \(1{,}083\) equations attain six rational points, in \(37\) orbits, and only Jacobian \(2\)-ranks \(1\) and \(3\) occur.
