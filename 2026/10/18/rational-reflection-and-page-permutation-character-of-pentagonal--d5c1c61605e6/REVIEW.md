# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The graph edges, action, and ordinary matching-complex convention are explicit. An augmented acyclic Morse matching toggles the first middle edge of a page missing both blockers and then pairs all but \(n\) residual \(n\)-faces. Its all-parameter acyclicity follows from monotonic blocker patterns and the finite residual Hasse shape; hence the only reduced rational homology is the claimed rank-\(n\) degree. An independent signed-orbit calculation evaluates the reduced Lefschetz character for each pure page permutation and each reflection-composed permutation, with all group elements quantified. The representation is identified from its character over \(\mathbb Q\). The supplied code checks complete Hasse acyclicity through seven pages and all group elements through five pages. Risk: the code does not prove the infinite family; the combinatorial proof does.

## Originality — PASS
The closest general polygonal-tiling paper (Bayer–Jelić Milutinović–Vega, arXiv:2211.12559, Propositions 10–11) makes the nonequivariant sphere-wedge reduction accessible, and that result is expressly credited rather than counted as new. Its Theorem 19 concerns polygonal *lines*, not three or more pentagons sharing one edge, and provides no complete page-permutation/reflection homology character. Donovan–Scoville (arXiv:2207.13780, §5) study vertex-glued Dutch windmills with different graph topology, and not the edge-book group action. Bate–Everitt–Ford–Ramos (arXiv:2312.13750, §§1–3) treat the matching complex of the complete hypergraph and broad fibre-closed asymptotics, but no exact edge-book formula. Targeted published-result comparisons returned related complexes rather than a covering theorem; unsuccessful searches are not proof of absolute novelty. Residual risk: an equivalent unindexed computation may exist.

## Value — PASS
The natural symmetric family has increasing top-homology dimension and two geometrically distinguished kinds of automorphisms. Its known homotopy rank cannot determine an \(S_n\times C_2\) character: different rational representations of the same dimension exist. The result identifies the *entire* group action for every page number, including the alternating twist, the reflection parity, and the hook Specht summand. This directly refines a meaningful topological family rather than recording a routine numerical instance.

## Limits and closest literature
The representation is rational, not an integral module classification or an equivariant homotopy equivalence. The prior nonequivariant wedge is acknowledged; the action is the new information. References: arXiv:2207.13780, arXiv:2211.12559, arXiv:2312.13750.
