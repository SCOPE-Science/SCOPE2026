The perfect-matching-generated complexes of \(P_3\square P_n\), for \(n=4,6,8\), have top rational reflection characters \((1,-1,1,-1)\), \((1,-1,-1,1)\), and \((2,0,0,2)\), respectively, in the group order \((1,h,v,hv)\). At width \(8\), the space is \(S^3\vee S^3\), but the two homology generators carry distinct Klein-four signs: one trivial and one negative under both reflections.
