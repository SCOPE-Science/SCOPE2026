For equally spaced affine branch fixed points and uniformly random ordered cuts, the number \(K_n\) of attracting periodic orbits has explicit exact factorial moments and satisfies \(K_n/\sqrt n\Rightarrow\mathrm{Rayleigh}(1)\). The maximum possible \(n\) attractors occur with probability exactly \((n-1)!/(n+1)^{n-1}\), despite typical multiplicity of order \(\sqrt n\).
