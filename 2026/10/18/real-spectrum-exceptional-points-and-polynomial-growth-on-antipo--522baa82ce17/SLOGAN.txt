In a homogeneous gain–loss ring with opposite defects, the spectrum stays entirely real up to gain–loss strength \(2\), as previously known, but exact Jordan exceptional points already appear at \(2\sin(k\pi/m)\) on a \(2m\)-site ring. The earliest point scales as \(2\pi/m\) and causes linear-in-time evolution growth despite real energies; the terminal point produces quadratic growth for even \(m\).
