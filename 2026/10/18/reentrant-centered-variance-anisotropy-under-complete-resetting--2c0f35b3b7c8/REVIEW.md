# Review: exact covariance-ellipse crossover under full resetting

## Correctness
**PASS.** The regenerative stationary law exists for positive Poisson resetting. Angular Fourier modes and mixed generator moments produce the stated rational difference of centered variances. An independent excursion-age integral reproduces the raw second-moment difference. For each \(\beta>0\), exact signs of the quartic at \(0\), \(\sqrt\beta\), and infinity give two positive roots, while Descartes' rule bounds their total multiplicity by two; consequently both are simple and exhaust all positive roots. Scaled polynomials with simple roots give all four limiting laws. The executable exact-arithmetic verification corroborates the calculation; its finite grid is not used as an infinite proof.

## Originality
**PASS, with explicit derivative-priority risk.** Shee (arXiv:2409.06920v1, revised v2) introduced the full-reset harmonic active-Brownian model, calculated its stationary mean, longitudinal second moments and variances, and classified **kurtosis**, not the transverse-vs-longitudinal centered variance ordering. Those established formulas make the algebraic inputs recoverable, and the mean optimum is known; the new, all-parameter exactly-two-crossing theorem, strict location of the known mean optimum between crossings, and boundary asymptotics were not stated in either inspected version. The closest other inspected model (arXiv:2405.06769v1) uses orientation-only resets. The unconfined earlier model arXiv:2008.03294 was inspected at abstract level only. An earlier implicit or explicit equivalent classification remains possible. Searches cannot prove novelty; independent priority audit is not yet performed.

## Scientific value
**PASS.** Uncentered longitudinal moments misleadingly suggest permanent longitudinal extension, whereas centered covariance predicts an entire open interval of transverse-dominant fluctuations for every positive confinement strength. The two changes of covariance eigenvalue ordering are an experimentally interpretable, strictly second-order phenomenon distinct from kurtosis classification. The mean-displacement-optimal reset rate invariably falls between them, a non-obvious relationship between two observables in a physical control problem. The result is scoped to full resetting, strictly positive confinement and propulsion, and isotropic Brownian noise.

## Limits and references
The primary published moment identities are prior art; the work establishes a new proposed qualitative implication, not an independently discovered new covariance formula. Relevant source: A. Shee, arXiv:2409.06920v1/v2, equations 9, 60 and 63. Related resetting protocols: arXiv:2405.06769v1 and arXiv:2008.03294. Potentially stronger implicit implications are a residual priority risk.
