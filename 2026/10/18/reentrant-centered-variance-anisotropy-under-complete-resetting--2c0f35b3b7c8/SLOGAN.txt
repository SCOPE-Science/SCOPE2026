For a harmonically confined active Brownian particle undergoing joint position-and-orientation resetting, the centered covariance ellipse reverses its major axis **exactly twice** as resetting accelerates, although the longitudinal uncentered second moment is always larger. For every trap strength \(\beta>0\), the reset rate \(\rho=\sqrt\beta\) maximizing mean longitudinal displacement lies strictly in the transverse-dominant covariance phase; both sharp transition rates and their limiting scalings are explicit.
