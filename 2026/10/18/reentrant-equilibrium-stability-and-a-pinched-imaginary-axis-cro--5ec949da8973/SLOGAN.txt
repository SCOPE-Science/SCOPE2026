For Dong's quadratic hidden-attractor flow, an exact factored Routh determinant gives a stability diagram independent of \(b\). When \(a>c\) and \(a\ne2c\), both equilibria are exponentially stable in a bounded interval of negative feedback, with an imaginary pair at each endpoint. At \(a=2c\) the interval pinches shut and the imaginary pair touches the stability boundary quadratically without a stable side. No nonlinear Hopf conclusion is asserted.
