Two equally pinned noisy leaders on a unit path exhibit an exact ten-vertex gain-reentrant placement transition: as feedback gain rises, optimal separation changes from \(5\) to \(6\) to \(7\) and back to \(6\). This reversal is not a small-graph artifact: on every \(P_{5m}\) with \(m\ge2\), the globally optimal separation strictly decreases from \(3m+1\) at gain \(1\) to \(3m\) at gain \(2\).
