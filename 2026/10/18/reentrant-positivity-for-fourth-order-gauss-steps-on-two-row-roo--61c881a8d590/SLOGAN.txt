For fourth-order two-stage Gauss–Legendre diffusion steps on the \(2n\)-vertex rook graph \(K_2\square K_n\), entrywise positivity holds for all steps at \(n=2,3\), for one initial interval at \(4\le n\le17\), but on **two disjoint positive-length intervals** for every \(n\ge18\). Two explicit quartics determine all interval endpoints, making \(36\) vertices the sharp onset of this reversible-network positivity reentrance.
