For every even Möbius ladder, the two mirror-reflection classes have explicitly determined actions on the two-dimensional reduced rational homology of its perfect-matching complex: vertex-axis reflections have trace zero, while edge-axis reflections have trace \(0\) for \(4\mid n\) and \(2(-1)^k\) for \(n=4k+2\). As a result, the edge-reflection quotient has reduced rational Betti rank \(1,2,0\) when \(n\equiv0,2,6\pmod8\), respectively, refining the previously known nonequivariant sphere-wedge and rotational classifications.
