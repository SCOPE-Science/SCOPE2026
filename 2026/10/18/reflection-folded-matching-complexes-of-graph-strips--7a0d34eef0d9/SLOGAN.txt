Reflection of any graph strip with at least four layers folds its geometric matching-complex fixed points exactly onto either a join of a half-strip and a central-layer matching complex (odd height) or the matching complex of a half-strip whiskered along its boundary (even height). Consequently every odd-height rectangular grid with width \(n\equiv2\pmod3\) has a contractible layer-reflection fixed-point space, while other widths reduce by a precise suspension shift to the half-height grid.
