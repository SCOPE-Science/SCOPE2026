For every polynomial degree bound \(d\), the magnitudes of a polynomial at any \(2d+1\) distinct circle points and of its shifted Euler derivative \(zP'-\alpha P\) at any other \(2d-1\) distinct circle points determine the polynomial up to phase exactly when \(2\operatorname{Re}\alpha\notin\{1,\ldots,2d-1\}\). At each excluded half-integer wall, every ambiguity is precisely a shifted reciprocal-conjugate pair, and genuine two-point fibers occur.
