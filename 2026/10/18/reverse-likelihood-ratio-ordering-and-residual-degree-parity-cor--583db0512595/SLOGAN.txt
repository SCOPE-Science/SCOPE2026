In a uniform labeled tree, conditioning on a larger even-distance color class makes the root degree smaller in reverse likelihood-ratio order; this forces nonpositive covariance for every pair of increasing scores. The normalized parity-class fluctuation and root degree become asymptotically independent Gaussian and shifted-Poisson variables, even while their unscaled covariance tends to \(-1/4\) and their correlation is asymptotic to \(-1/\sqrt n\).
