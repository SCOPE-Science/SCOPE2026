In exact arithmetic, depth-one two-residual two-grid acceleration with scalar Richardson presmoothing is precisely restarted GMRES(2), regardless of the positive smoothing weight. The coarse normal matrix nonetheless has a unique best-conditioned weight, \(\omega_\star=\sqrt{2}\|Ar\|_2/\|A^2r\|_2\), and its condition number diverges quadratically as that weight tends to zero or infinity.
