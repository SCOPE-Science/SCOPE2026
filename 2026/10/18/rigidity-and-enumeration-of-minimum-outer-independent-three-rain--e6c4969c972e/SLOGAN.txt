For Möbius ladders of order \(2m\), minimum outer-independent three-rainbow assignments are completely rigid outside \(m\equiv1\pmod6\): there are exactly \(12\) labeled optimum functions when \(m\equiv3\pmod6\), and exactly \(12m\) in the other four treated residue classes. The previously known optimum *weights* are not new; the result counts and classifies every optimum assignment in those classes.
