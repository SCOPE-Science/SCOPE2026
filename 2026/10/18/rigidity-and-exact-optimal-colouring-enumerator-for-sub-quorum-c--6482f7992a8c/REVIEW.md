# Scientific review: sub-quorum colourings of clique prisms

**Correctness (C): PASS.** The proof starts from the exact coloured closed-neighbourhood inequality in each of the two complete layers. The row-colour lemma proves that three colours within one layer force exactly three same-coloured matching partners, leaving no room for a fourth global colour. In a four-colour assignment, two disjoint two-colour palettes then force equal positive colour multiplicities and prohibit every doubly coloured matched pair. The converse is checked directly. Orders one through three are treated individually. The multinomial enumerator and exponential generating function count precisely the four-colour optimizers, with the divide-by-four factor justified by within-layer colour swaps. Direct neighbourhood enumeration agrees for all graphs through \(n=5\), but the infinite result rests on the proof.

**Originality (O): PASS subject to stated access risk.** The defining 2026 paper gives the sub-quorum notion, a general degree bound, and exact results for paths, cycles, complete multipartite graphs, ordinary path ladders, selected joins/coronas, grids and cubes. Its bound evaluates here to \(\lfloor2n^2/(2n-1)\rfloor\), which exceeds four for \(n\ge5\). Its path-ladder result concerns \(P_2\square P_n\), not the clique prism \(K_2\square K_n\). Existing published mathematical records on clique-prism topology concern other invariants. Targeted term, alias, parameter, table and implication searches did not identify a covering four-colour classification or optimal-map enumerator. The numerical small cases need not be new in view of the initiating paper's degree bound. The newest source revision's full text and possible unindexed equivalences remain a bibliographic risk; searches alone cannot establish priority.

**Value (V): PASS.** The leading paper leaves the behaviour of the invariant on many graph families unresolved. This finding gives a uniform sharp four-colour ceiling on an infinite dense family whose order and degree both grow, whereas the applicable general upper bound increases with size. It also classifies every optimizer, characterizes equality and supplies a nontrivial exact named-palette polynomial and Bessel-series exponential generating function. The optimal support is not forced to be a dissociation set: both clique layers may contain repeated colours in large balanced classes. These structural and enumerative conclusions go beyond a small-case calculation or relabelling of known extremal numbers.

**Closest literature and limits.** H. Ma, R. Sahbi and W. Zhang, *Sub-quorum colorings of graphs*, arXiv:2609.25128 (first posted 2026-09-20); S. M. Hedetniemi et al., *Quorum colorings of graphs* (2013); published mathematical record *Open-neighborhood complexes of clique prisms* (topological, not colouring). The accessible full online article and the latest revision abstract were compared. The newest complete revision was inaccessible. No claim is made for arbitrary rook graphs, all nonoptimal colourings, publication priority or external certification.
