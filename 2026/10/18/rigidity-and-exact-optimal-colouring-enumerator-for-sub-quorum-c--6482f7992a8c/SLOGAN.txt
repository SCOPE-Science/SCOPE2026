For the clique prism \(K_2\square K_n\), the sub-quorum colouring number stabilizes at exactly four for every \(n\ge4\). Every optimal four-colouring consists of disjoint balanced two-colour supports in the two clique layers, giving the complete named-palette enumerator \(F_n(k)=\frac{(k)_4}{4}\sum_{a,b\ge1,\,2a+2b\le n}\frac{n!}{(a!)^2(b!)^2(n-2a-2b)!}\).
