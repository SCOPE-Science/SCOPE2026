## Correctness
The theorem follows from the exact Frobenius-additive line-section classification, a sharp affine-incidence capacity argument, and affine-plane rigidity/switching. Exhaustive enumeration in the smallest admitted quadratic example finds exactly 40 optimal covers; an extension-degree-three switch is verified explicitly. The general proof does not depend on finite experiments.

## Value
The integer covering number alone is a simple specialization of an additive-map construction. The additional **complete classification and exact number of every minimizer** in the quadratic extension case, contrasted with constructed nonparallel minimizers in all higher extension degrees, resolves a natural structural refinement of a newly emphasized arithmetic-geometric covering problem. The exact count is a natural invariant of this geometrically integral curve family, not an arbitrary small instance.
