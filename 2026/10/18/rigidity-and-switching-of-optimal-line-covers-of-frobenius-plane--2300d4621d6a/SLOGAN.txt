For \(C_t:YZ^{t-1}=X^t\) over \(\mathbf F_{t^h}\) with prime-power \(t\ge3\), the minimum line-cover size is \(t^{h-1}+1\). At \(h=2\), every minimizer is a parallel class of maximal secants plus one arbitrary line through the point at infinity, giving exactly \((t+1)(t^2+1)\) covers; for \(h\ge3\), nonparallel minimizers exist.
