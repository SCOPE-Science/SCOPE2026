Run `python3 check.py` from the directory containing the stand-alone files. The checker uses no nonstandard packages. It constructs the finite fields from specified irreducible polynomials; tests all 91 projective lines in the order-nine plane and every four-line subset (the lower bound establishes four is minimum); asserts the 40 expected covers; constructs and checks a nonparallel switched parallel-class partition in the order-27 example. All computations are finite supporting checks; the full-parameter theorem is proved in RESULT.md by additive Frobenius kernels and affine incidence counting.
