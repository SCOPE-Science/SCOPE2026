For every \(n\ge5\), the graph \(\mathrm{GP}(n,2)\) has no dominating induced matching unless \(5\mid n\), when it has exactly five; their explicit cyclic edge classes partition all edges. Consequently, whenever a strong five-edge coloring exists, it is unique up to permuting the five colors, yielding exactly \(120\) color-labelled strong five-edge colorings.
