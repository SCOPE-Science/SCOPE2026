For an arbitrary head graph \(H\), the complete Roman-domination weight polynomial of either a thin or a thick spider depends only on \(|V(H)|\) and the polynomial \(R_H(z)\); explicit algebraic formulas give every coefficient. These transformations, with exact union and join rules, provide a fully specified weight-polynomial recursion for all \(P_4\)-sparse graphs, strengthening known minimum-weight formulas without claiming priority for general bounded-clique-width counting algorithms.
