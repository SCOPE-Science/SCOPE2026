For the reflexive row-or-column incidence of an \(m\)-by-\(n\) rook board, every formal concept, the exact lattice automorphism group, and the optimal Horn inference widths admit uniform formulas. On boards with both sides at least three, iterative implications need at most three premises while single-pass implications sometimes need four; on two-row boards the entire lattice forgets the column pairing and its symmetry group enlarges to a wreath product.
