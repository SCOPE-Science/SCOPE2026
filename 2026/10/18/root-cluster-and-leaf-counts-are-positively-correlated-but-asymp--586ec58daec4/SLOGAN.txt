In a uniformly grown recursive tree, Bernoulli pruning creates a root component whose size is strictly positively correlated with the number of leaves in the unpruned tree, with exact covariance \(\frac{r(1-r)(n-2)}{2(1+r)(n+r-1)}\frac{\Gamma(n+r)}{\Gamma(1+r)\Gamma(n)}\). For fixed \(0<r<1\), that covariance grows like \(n^r\), but the correlation decays universally like \(n^{-1/2}\).
