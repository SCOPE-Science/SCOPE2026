For the differential Hilbert operator between power-weighted Bergman Hilbert spaces, compactness yields every quasi-Schatten class \(\mathcal S_p\), \(p>0\), with explicit rank-\(O(\log^2(1/\varepsilon))\) approximants. Its Hilbert–Schmidt norm has exact critical residue \((\alpha+2)(\alpha+3)B(\alpha+2,-\alpha)\) as the target weight approaches the noncompact boundary.
