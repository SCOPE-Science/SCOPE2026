The recorded literature and subject-matter searches found no dominating implication. A missing specialized source or unpublished derivation remains a publication-priority risk; negative searches alone do not prove uniqueness.

## Value — PASS
The precise orbit partition and stabilization depth form a natural, all-rank invariant in a classical family of cubic graphs. Equality of refinement depth with root eccentricity is sharp and nontrivial: the final obstacle is an explicit antipodal colour collision rather than only unreached distance shells. The induction exhibits a constant-complexity wavefront on an arbitrarily long graph, giving a usable example of exact counting-logic depth.

## Limitations
The result is not a statement about isomorphism testing among arbitrary graphs, alternate roots or arbitrary generalized Petersen parameters. The finite tests do not certify the infinite claim independently of the proof; publication priority is not independently established.
