For the shortest-path convex hull on the \(q\)-ary Hamming graph, every nontrivial prime Horn premise is a minimal coordinate cover with a private witness for each premise point. An exact inclusion-exclusion formula counts such rules by premise size, and reveals a \(|V|^{\Theta(\log |V|)}\)-size minimum one-pass unit basis even though an ordinary multipass unit basis has only \(O(|V|^3)\) rules.
