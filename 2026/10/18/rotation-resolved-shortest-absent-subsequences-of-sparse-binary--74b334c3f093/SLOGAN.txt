For a cyclic binary word whose gaps between successive ones are one or two zeros, the complete rotation-by-rotation histogram of shortest absent scattered-subsequence counts is determined solely by the lengths of consecutive short-gap runs. For the finite Fibonacci word \(W_m\) with \(m\ge3\), its \(F_{m+2}\) distinct rotations have multiplicities \(1,2,3\) in exact counts \(F_{m-1},2F_{m-1},2F_{m-2}\), respectively.
