For the dual incidence order of every finite graph \(G\), the number of distinct lower–upper rough approximation pairs is exactly \(2^{k(G)}T_G(3,2)\), equivalently a two-state zero-field Ising partition function. This gives deletion–contraction and an exact complexity divide: \(\#\mathrm P\)-complete counting on arbitrary graphs, but polynomial-time computation on planar graphs by established Tutte/Ising algorithms.
