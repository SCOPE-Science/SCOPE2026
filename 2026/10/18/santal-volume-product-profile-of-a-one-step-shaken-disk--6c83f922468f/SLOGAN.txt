For the canonical one-step \(\lambda\)-Steiner deformation of the unit disk, the Santaló volume product is an exact, strictly decreasing one-variable profile determined by elementary integrals and a unique scalar minimizer. Its sharp initial deficit is \((32/3-\pi^2)\delta^2+O(\delta^4)\), with \(\delta=1-2\lambda\), and the shaking endpoint has product approximately \(8.716345507152886\). The Santaló center initially moves at the centroid's speed but has a strictly negative cubic displacement relative to it.
