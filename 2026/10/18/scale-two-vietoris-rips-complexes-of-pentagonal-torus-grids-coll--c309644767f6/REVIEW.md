**PASS.** The vertex metric and inclusive Rips convention are explicit. The proof exhausts all facets by considering one, two, or three consecutive columns and the radius-one row-neighborhood intersections in a five-cycle. It gives six elementary free pairs for each of the \(5n\) distance-two-column facets and two for each of the \(5n\) square tetrahedra. The proof then identifies the surviving torus triangulation, shows that each full column simplex meets it along one essential meridional five-cycle, and computes the homotopy type by replacing those contractible attachments with disks. The integer homotopy conclusion follows from a CW cell cancellation, not from extrapolated finite-field data. A direct-distance exhaustive checker verifies all face sets, each freeness condition, and the final complex for \(7\le n\le14\), with mod-two boundary ranks for \(7\le n\le10\).

## Value

**PASS.** The five-cycle factor is the first pentagonal-period boundary outside the square-grid torus theorem. It simultaneously creates an essential surviving longitudinal circle and \(n\) independent degree-two classes, unlike the larger square case where both torus loops survive, the length-four factor where the new classes are degree three, and the clique-fiber cylinder where no positive-degree two-classes survive. The explicit, parameter-uniform collapse explains the topological mechanism and the precise dependence on \(n\), rather than supplying another isolated Betti table. The result is a naturally motivated exact homotopy classification of a previously unaddressed rectangular family.
