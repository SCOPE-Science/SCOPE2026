For every \(n\ge7\), the inclusive scale-two graph-metric Vietoris–Rips complex of \(C_5\square C_n\) simplicially collapses in \(40n\) elementary pairs to a torus with \(n\) parallel meridians filled, and hence has homotopy type \(S^1\vee\bigvee^nS^2\). This uniform pentagonal-width phase differs from the known four-cycle-width sphere-wedge result and the large square-torus regime.
