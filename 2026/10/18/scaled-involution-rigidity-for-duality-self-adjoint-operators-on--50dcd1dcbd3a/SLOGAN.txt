On every real or complex non-Hilbert \(\ell_p(I)\) with \(1<p<\infty\) and at least two coordinates, a bounded operator obeys \(T'J_p=J_pT\) exactly when it is zero or a positive scalar multiple of a surjective isometric involution given by conjugate-paired coordinate weights. Consequently, nonzero such operators have \(T^2=\|T\|^2I\), and none is compact when \(I\) is infinite.
