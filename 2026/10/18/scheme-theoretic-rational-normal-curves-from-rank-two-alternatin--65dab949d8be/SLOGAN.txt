For every field and every \(n\ge4\), the entire rank-at-most-two Pfaffian scheme of an alternating Toeplitz \(n\)-by-\(n\) matrix is a smooth rational normal curve of degree \(n-2\), not merely set-theoretically but as a prime quadratic ideal. Exactly \(\binom{n-2}{2}\) selected Pfaffians minimally generate it, determining its Hilbert series and full linear free resolution.
