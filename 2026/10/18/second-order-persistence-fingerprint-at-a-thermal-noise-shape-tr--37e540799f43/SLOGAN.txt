In the harmonically trapped thermal run-and-tumble model, a previously known two-peak transition has the exact postcritical law \(z_\pm^2=3\delta+K(\ell)\delta^2+O_\ell(\delta^3)\), with an explicit persistence-dependent coefficient \(K(\ell)\). That first model-specific correction changes sign between rare and frequent turning, whereas the leading coefficient is the generic Gaussian-smoothing value.
