# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof fixes a finite world set and a specified S5 partition, translates the nested modal-probabilistic identity into a minimum over subjective measures versus a sum of masses of fully included information cells, and proves both directions. Full-cell events force identical target-block masses; complements of one selected point in every positive block force all joint selector distributions; the selector condition constructs a minimizing row for every event. The cardinal obstruction follows from distinct selectors. A separate complete eight-world example attains its first nontrivial two-target-block bound. The exact finite checker tests all partitions on up to three worlds with half-integer probability vectors and all event truth sets; it is evidence only for those finite instances.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

The 2026 motivating paper defines the relevant epistemic and probabilistic operators and treats examples, expressivity and complexity; its inspected definitions, examples and main results do not state the all-event classwise equivalence. Classical lower-envelope and belief-function representations are expressly credited: general selection-core existence is not claimed new. Targeted published-record, literature, and previous-result comparisons did not locate a theorem imposing exactly the selector-saturated finite S5 kernel together with the sharp source-class budget. A stronger or equivalent result in older imprecise-probability/epistemic-probability literature remains a substantive residual risk, and there is no independent originality certification.

## Value — PASS

Equality between knowing a numerical probability and assigning probability to an event's being known is a natural structural question in a probabilistic-epistemic language allowing both nestings. The answer is restrictive and complete, identifying a product-size combinatorial resource requirement rather than merely producing a countermodel. The result explains when deterministic beliefs and permutations are forced by epistemic support and provides a constructive multi-target sharp example.

## Scope and independence

The theorem holds for finite S5 information partitions with arbitrary full-power-set world-dependent probability measures and Boolean event valuations. The result is not an assertion about continuous-world measurable models, independence of observations, or all fuzzy-modal formulas. The reviewer is not independent.
