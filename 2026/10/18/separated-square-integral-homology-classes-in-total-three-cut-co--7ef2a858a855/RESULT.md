# Separated-square integral homology classes in total three-cut complexes of clique–path strips

## Finding

Let \(m\ge3\) and \(n\ge4\) be integers, let \(G=K_m\square P_n\), and let \(\Delta^t_3(G)\) denote the total three-cut complex, generated by the complements of independent vertex triples in \(G\). Then

\[
\widetilde H_{mn-6}(\Delta^t_3(K_m\square P_n);\mathbb Z)
\supseteq_{\mathrm{split}}
\mathbb Z^{(m-1)^2\binom{n-2}{2}}.
\]

The notation \(\supseteq_{\mathrm{split}}\) means an explicitly identified direct summand, not an equality. Consequently **the pure \((mn-4)\)-dimensional total three-cut complex is not shellable** for any \(m\ge3,n\ge4\).

## Assumptions and scope

The graph has vertices \((a,i)\), with \(0\le a<m\) and \(0\le i<n\). Two distinct vertices are adjacent precisely when they share a column (any different rows) or lie in the same row in consecutive columns. An independent triple is a three-element set with no adjacent pair. All chain groups and pairings below have integer coefficients; oriented simplicial chains use increasing vertex order. No statement about the exact homology rank, absence of additional summands or homotopy type is asserted.

## Proof

**1. Alexander dual.** Put \(N=mn\) and \(D=\Delta^t_3(G)\). Since the facets of \(D\) are the complements of independent triples, its Alexander dual, taken inside the full simplex on the \(N\) graph vertices, is

\[
L=D^*=\{S\subseteq V(G):\alpha(G[S])\le2\}.
\]

Thus \(L\) is the three-robust clique complex. Integral combinatorial Alexander duality yields

\[
\widetilde H_{N-6}(D;\mathbb Z)\cong\widetilde H^3(L;\mathbb Z).
\]

**2. Separated four-cycles.** Fix row \(0\). For every horizontal gap \(i\in\{0,\ldots,n-2\}\) and every row \(a\in\{1,\ldots,m-1\}\), let \(z_{i,a}\) be the oriented simplicial one-cycle supported on the four-cycle

\[
(0,i)\to(a,i)\to(a,i+1)\to(0,i+1)\to(0,i).
\]

Every edge of this cycle belongs to \(L\). Choose gaps \(0\le i<j\le n-2\) with \(j\ge i+2\), and rows \(1\le a,b<m\). The cycles have disjoint vertex supports, and their simplicial join

\[
Z_{i,j;a,b}=z_{i,a}*z_{j,b}\in C_3(L;\mathbb Z)
\]

is a three-cycle. Indeed, each of its sixteen signed tetrahedra is the union of one graph edge from each square, so no three of its vertices are pairwise nonadjacent. The chain belongs to \(L\), and the join boundary identity, together with \(\partial z_{i,a}=\partial z_{j,b}=0\), gives \(\partial Z_{i,j;a,b}=0\).

**3. Maximal tetrahedra.** Define the distinguished tetrahedron

\[
F_{i,j;a,b}=\{(a,i),(a,i+1),(b,j),(b,j+1)\}.
\]

This tetrahedron is a maximal simplex in \(L\): no fifth vertex may be adjoined while keeping independence number at most two. To see this, first suppose \(a\ne b\), or \(a=b\) and \(j\ge i+3\). The induced graph on \(F\) is a disjoint union of two horizontal edges. No external vertex is adjacent to both endpoints of either horizontal edge in \(K_m\square P_n\). From each edge choose an endpoint not adjacent to the proposed fifth vertex. The chosen pair is itself nonadjacent, producing an independent triple.

The remaining case is \(a=b\) and \(j=i+2\). Here \(F\) induces a four-vertex path occupying columns \(i,i+1,i+2,i+3\) of one row. Any external vertex in a different row is adjacent to at most one of these four vertices, so at least three nonneighbors include a nonadjacent pair. Any external vertex in the same row lies to the left or right of this four-column interval; respectively choose the two vertices in columns \(i+1,i+3\), or \(i,i+2\). In both cases they are nonadjacent and also nonadjacent to the external vertex. Thus no five-simplex contains \(F\) (indeed, no four-simplex contains it).

Because the tetrahedron \(F\) is maximal, the cochain \(\phi_{i,j;a,b}\in C^3(L;\mathbb Z)\), which evaluates as \(1\) on its increasing oriented simplex and \(0\) on all others, is a **cocycle**.

**4. Unimodular evaluation.** The tetrahedron \(F_{i,j;a,b}\) appears with coefficient \(\pm1\) in \(Z_{i,j;a,b}\): it uses the unique nonbase-row horizontal edge of each four-cycle. It does not occur in any other \(Z_{k,\ell;c,d}\) from the indicated gap pairs and rows, since these two nonbase-row horizontal edges determine the two separated gaps and their row labels. After reversing the orientation of each \(Z\), if necessary, the evaluation matrix is exactly the identity:

\[
\langle\phi_{i,j;a,b},Z_{k,\ell;c,d}\rangle
=\delta_{ik}\delta_{j\ell}\delta_{ac}\delta_{bd}.
\]

A cocycle paired with a cycle depends only on their (co)homology classes. The identity pairing shows that the \(\phi\)-classes give a split free submodule of \(H^3(L;\mathbb Z)\), with explicit retraction by evaluation on the \(Z\)-cycles. There are \(\binom{n-2}{2}\) unordered gap pairs at distance at least two and \((m-1)^2\) choices of rows. Alexander duality proves the displayed split summand.

**5. Shellability.** The complex \(D\) is pure of dimension \(N-4\): all facets are complements of independent triples. The established rank is positive for every \(m\ge3,n\ge4\) and lies in degree \(N-6\), strictly below the top dimension. A pure shellable complex has no reduced homology below its top dimension. Therefore \(D\) is not shellable. \(\square\)

## Verification

The accompanying dependency-free script `verify.py` constructs the robust-clique dual and checks the signed join cycles, all maximal tetrahedra, and the integral diagonal evaluation for seven parameter pairs: \((3,4),(3,5),(3,6),(4,4),(4,5),(5,4),(5,6)\). It also computes full characteristic-two third homology for the first six pairs using complete simplex enumeration and Gaussian elimination. All finite checks succeeded; actual output is included as `verification_output.txt`. These computations are consistency checks, not substitutes for the all-parameter proof above.

For example, the theorem gives a split \(\mathbb Z^4\) in degree six when \(m=3,n=4\), while the complete characteristic-two homology in the dual has dimension twelve, consistent with the distinction between a lower bound and a full classification.

## Relationship to prior work

Chauhan, Shukla and Vinayak, *Total 2-cut complexes of powers of cycle graphs and Cartesian products of certain graphs* (2025; arXiv:2512.04486), **Conjecture 4.3**, predicts the stronger full homology formula for total three-cut complexes of \(K_m\square P_n\): the only nonzero reduced group should have rank \((m-1)^2\binom{n-1}{2}\) in degree \(mn-6\). Their Theorem 1.4 proves a result only for the *total two-cut complex*; their total three-cut statement is presented as a conjecture supported by finite computations. The present theorem proves a proper all-parameter lower bound, including integral splitting and non-shellability, without asserting the conjectured exact value.

Filakovský, *The Topology of k-Robust Clique Complexes in Grid-like Graphs* (2026; arXiv:2602.11365), proves a full three-robust clique classification for ordinary rectangular **path-by-path grids** \(P_m\square P_n\). Although the Alexander-dual terminology agrees, the theorem there does not cover the present **clique-by-path strips**, which contain triangles for \(m\ge3\). Earlier studies of total cut complexes treat different parameters, graph families or the two-cut construction. See references below.

## Limitations

No equality of ranks is proved, no torsion outside the displayed summand is excluded, and no sphere-wedge homotopy type is claimed. The result does not address \(m=2\) or \(n\le3\). Full broad-literature exhaustiveness cannot be guaranteed; equivalently phrased, unindexed or future independently discovered results are possible. The correctness argument is a complete symbolic proof plus independently reproducible finite checks, not external certification.
