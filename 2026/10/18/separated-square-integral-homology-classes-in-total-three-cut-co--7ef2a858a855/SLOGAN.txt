For every \(m\ge3\) and \(n\ge4\), the total three-cut complex of \(K_m\square P_n\) has an explicitly split integral homology summand \(\mathbb Z^{(m-1)^2\binom{n-2}{2}}\) in degree \(mn-6\), and is consequently non-shellable. This proves a substantive infinite-family lower bound toward the published conjectured exact rank, without claiming the complete homology or homotopy type.
