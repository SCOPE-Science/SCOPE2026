The six-element crown's projective Hibi toric sixfold has a non-pure reduced singular locus: six three-dimensional quadric cones and a separate projective line. Generic transverse slices are rank-one \(2\times2\) and \(3\times3\) determinantal cones, with multiplicities \(2\) and \(6\), respectively, and its \(96\) singular torus orbits admit an exact census.
