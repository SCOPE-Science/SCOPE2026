## Mathematical value: PASS

The exact finite sharp threshold answers when outbreak-source rankings can be chosen consistently for **all loss thresholds** instead of just for expected case counts. The smallest witness shows that a seed with larger expected spread may be less likely to cause a strict-majority epidemic, uniformly in transmissibility. That is a substantive decision boundary between average-burden and large-outbreak-risk prioritization. The exact seven-node example is not an arbitrary simulation or a routine monotonicity check; the minimality certificate protects its interpretation.
