The normal projective toric sixfold associated with the Fano matroid base polytope has exactly \(70\) irreducible singular components: \(21\) three-dimensional tetrahedral orbit closures, \(21\) two-dimensional quadrilateral orbit closures, and \(28\) two-dimensional triangular orbit closures. Every component has generic transverse toric class group \(\mathbb Z\), and the singular locus equals the non-\(\mathbb Q\)-factorial locus.
