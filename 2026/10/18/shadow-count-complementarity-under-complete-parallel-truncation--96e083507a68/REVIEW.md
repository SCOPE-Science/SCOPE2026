The truncated tetrahedron has four hexagonal original facets H_i with normals n_i and four triangular cut facets T_i with normals -n_i; six H_iH_j edges and twelve T_iH_j edges (i!=j). Generic silhouette edges correspond to incident facets whose viewing-normal signs differ. If k original facet signs are positive, tetrahedron shadow has k(4-k) vertices; truncated shadow has k(4-k)+2[binom(k,2)+binom(4-k,2)]=12-k(4-k), for k=1,2,3. Exact pointwise equality follows. Independently projected 1000 irregular-tetrahedron directions with unequal admissible cuts: observed pair counts (3,9):412 and (4,8):588. The solid-angle probability follows from Feldman–Klain Theorem 1, not independent novelty.
