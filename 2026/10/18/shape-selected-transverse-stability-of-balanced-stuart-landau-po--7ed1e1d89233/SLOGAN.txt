For equal-amplitude rotating zero-mean Stuart–Landau polygons with dissipative and reactive all-to-all coupling, the complete transverse Floquet spectrum depends on shape only through the second angular moment, and a sharp quartic inequality decides attraction. Four oscillators at identical coefficients can have an unstable square but an attracting skew antipodal-pair polygon, with the exact transition at \(\cos^2\phi=13/18\).
