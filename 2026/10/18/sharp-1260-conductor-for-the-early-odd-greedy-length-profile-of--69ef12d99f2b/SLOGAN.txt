For primes \(p>3\), the exact odd-greedy stopping categories of \(2/p\) through seven terms have least possible congruence modulus \(1260\). The prime densities for exactly two, four, and six terms, and for no termination by seven terms, are respectively \(1/2\), \(1/8\), \(13/48\), and \(5/48\); eventual termination in the last category is not asserted.
