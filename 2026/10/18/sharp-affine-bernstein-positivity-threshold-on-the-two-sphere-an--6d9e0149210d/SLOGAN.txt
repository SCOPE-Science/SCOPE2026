Ordinary affine Bernstein approximation preserves every continuous zonal positive-definite kernel on the two-sphere exactly at degrees one, two and three. A single explicit Gegenbauer coefficient formula gives a positive-definite counterexample at every degree \(n\ge d+2\) on every sphere \(\mathbb S^d\), sharpening the previously known isolated fourth-degree counterexample on \(\mathbb S^2\).
