For uniform volume, the sharp affine \(L^2\)-projector endpoint norm on the \(d\)-dimensional cross-polytope grows as \(d/2\), but on its polar cube it grows as \(\sqrt{6d/\pi}\). Exact finite formulas show that the norms agree only in dimensions one and two and that the cross-polytope is strictly less stable in every dimension at least three; its amplification dominates by a factor asymptotic to \(\sqrt{\pi d/24}\).
