The area-normalized covariance of regular P_n is (1+2 cos²(pi/n))/12 times I, yielding kernel 1+kappa x·y. Convexity of the Lebesgue function reduces the endpoint norm to a vertex, and splitting the negative cap gives the signed shoelace integral.
