The uniform-area affine \(L^2\) projector on every regular inscribed polygon has an exact sharp endpoint norm computable from one clipped polygon cap. As the number \(n\) of sides increases, its endpoint norm exceeds the unit-disk value by \(5\pi\sqrt{15}/(12n^2)+O(n^{-4})\), a sharp polygonal stability loss with a rigorously controlled fourth-order remainder.
