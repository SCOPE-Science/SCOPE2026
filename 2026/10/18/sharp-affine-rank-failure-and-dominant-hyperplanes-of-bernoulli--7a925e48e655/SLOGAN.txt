A Bernoulli-sampled Boolean polytope loses affine dimension with sharp probability \(n(n+1)(1-p)^{2^{n-1}}(1+O_p(e^{-c_p2^n}))\). Conditioned on this rare event, its affine hull is overwhelmingly one of \(n(n+1)\) explicit half-cube hyperplanes, with two-coordinate relations accounting for asymptotically the fraction \((n-1)/(n+1)\).
