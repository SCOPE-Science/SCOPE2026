Among the nonnegative integer affine weights \(f_c(k)=1+c(k-1)\) in the generalized Möbius recurrence, the fixed-function condition \(h_c(n)=-1\) detects precisely the primes for all \(n\ge2\) exactly when \(c\ge3\). A Dirichlet-convolution congruence excludes repeated prime factors, while a strict squarefree generating-function inequality excludes every other composite.
