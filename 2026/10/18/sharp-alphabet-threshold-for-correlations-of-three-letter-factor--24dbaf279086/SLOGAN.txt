For two distinct nonloop length-three motifs sharing a middle letter in a uniformly random order-two de Bruijn cycle, every covariance is nonnegative exactly from alphabet size \(q=5\) onward, and every covariance is strictly positive from \(q=6\) onward. The complete four-case law isolates a unique crossed-motif independence class at \(q=5\), with exact negative cases for smaller alphabets.
