For nuclei \(j_S\) and \(j_T\) on the upset algebra of any finite poset of height \(h\), the alternating product reaches \(j_{S\cap T}\) after at most \(\lfloor h/2\rfloor+1\) repetitions, and this is the sharp uniform bound. A concrete interval-bridge condition characterizes when the product is a nucleus in a single step.
