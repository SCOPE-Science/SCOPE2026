Among all Poisson-turning laws with a fixed persistence and effective chirality, the smallest possible stationary angular-jump entropy production is exactly \(2\alpha R\operatorname{artanh}R\), where \(R=|\Omega|/\sqrt{D_\theta(2\alpha-D_\theta)}\). For nonzero chirality, exactly one asymmetric pair of opposite turning angles attains the optimum; smooth turning densities approach it arbitrarily closely.
