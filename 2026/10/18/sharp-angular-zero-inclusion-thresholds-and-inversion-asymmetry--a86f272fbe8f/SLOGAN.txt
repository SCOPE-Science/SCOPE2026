For every invertible upper-triangular operator on complex \(\ell_p^2\), zero membership in its spatial numerical range and in that of its inverse has sharp explicit angle-dependent thresholds whose ratio is \((|a|/|d|)^{(p-2)/p}\), independent of the diagonal angle away from antipodal phases. Consequently the full complex \(\ell_p^2\) space has simultaneous zero inclusion for all invertible operators exactly when \(p=2\).
