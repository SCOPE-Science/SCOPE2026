For random compositions of integer beta transformations, the centered first-\(1/q\) cell has a rational covariance generating function and an exact prime-valuation criterion for when all later covariances vanish. When randomly switching doubling and tripling, the first-sixth-cell correlation instead has sharp exponential rate \(\max\{p/2,(1-p)/3\}\), uniquely optimized at \(p=2/5\) with rate \(1/5\), faster than either deterministic choice.
