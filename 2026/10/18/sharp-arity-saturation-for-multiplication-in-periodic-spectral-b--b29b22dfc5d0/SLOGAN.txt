In every dimension and for every \(m\ge2\), multiplication of \(m\) periodic spectral Barron functions with Fourier weight \((1+|k|^2)^{s/2}\) has exactly the same sharp multilinear norm \((5/4)^{s/2}\), attained by two or three aligned unit-frequency characters and constant remaining factors. This arity saturation is specific to lattice frequencies: the analogous Euclidean weighted Fourier-\(L^1\) product norms grow at least like \((m/e)^{s/2}\) for \(s>0\).
