For the quadratic harmonic-Bergman Toeplitz family \(T_{z^2+bz}\), the norm of the nonorthogonal Riesz projection onto its isolated eigenvalue has an exact logarithmic formula. The same spectral outlier becomes logarithmically ill-conditioned at \(|b|=7/4\) but quadratically ill-conditioned at \(|b|=7/3\), with respective sharp coefficients \(1/7\) and \(7/36\).
