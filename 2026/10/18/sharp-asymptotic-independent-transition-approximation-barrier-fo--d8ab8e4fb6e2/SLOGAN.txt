For a fixed nonuniform categorical source, no model with one independent Bernoulli variable per adjacent transition can approximate the run-change count arbitrarily well, even as the sequence length grows: its smallest possible asymptotic Kolmogorov error is the explicit positive constant \(\Phi(u/\tau)-\Phi(u/\sigma)\). The ordinary binomial proxy attains this optimum, while uniform categorical sources permit an exact independent-transition representation.
