Among complete-bipartite unit-edge metric spaces with a fixed number of vertices, the Lipschitz-free Mahler product is strictly increased by balancing two parts once both have at least two vertices, and its unique maximizing bipartition is the most balanced one for \(N\ge6\). The minimum equals \(4^{N-1}/(N-1)!\) exactly when the smaller part has at most two vertices; the exact product is \(4^{N-1}p!q!H_{p,q}/((N-1)!)^2\), where \(H_{p,q}=\sum_k\binom{p-1}k\binom{q-1}k\binom{2k}k/4^k\).
