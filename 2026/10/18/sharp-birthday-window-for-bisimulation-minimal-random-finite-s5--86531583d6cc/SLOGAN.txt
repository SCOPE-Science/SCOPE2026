A uniformly random finite one-agent \(\mathsf{S5}\) model, with independently uniform atomic colors, becomes bisimulation-minimal at the sharp palette scale \(q\asymp n\log n\). At \(q/(n\log n)\to\theta\), within-cluster color collisions converge to \(\operatorname{Poisson}(1/(2\theta))\) and minimality has limiting probability \(e^{-1/(2\theta)}\); an exact finite generating polynomial is also available but uses a classical simple-hypergraph counting identity.
