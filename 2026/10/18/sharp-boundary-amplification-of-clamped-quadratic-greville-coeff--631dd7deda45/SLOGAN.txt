For clamped uniform quadratic B-splines, Greville-sample coefficient recovery has exact sharp \(\ell^\infty\) sensitivity \(2+(\sqrt2-1)(1+q^{n-1})/(1-q^n)\), where \(q=3-2\sqrt2\). The extreme coefficients are always adjacent to an endpoint, and their sharp stability constant decreases from \(5/2\) to \(1+\sqrt2\), exceeding the pure-cardinal bulk limit \(2\) by exactly \(\sqrt2-1\).
