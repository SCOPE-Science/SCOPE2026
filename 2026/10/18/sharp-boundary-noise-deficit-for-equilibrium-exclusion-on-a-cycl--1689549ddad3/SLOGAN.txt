For equilibrium symmetric exclusion on a finite cycle, the two bonds that directly change a contiguous block's occupancy are individually the most important sources of integrated-occupation noise, yet together they contribute exactly \(6m(n-m)/[n(m(n-m)+2)]\) of its long-time variance. This fraction is independent of particle density, vanishes uniformly as \(6/n\), and falls below one half for every block as soon as \(n\ge12\); all formulas follow from a full interacting-particle Poisson corrector.
