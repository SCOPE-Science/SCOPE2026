For Jacobi-weighted Bernstein–Durrmeyer smoothing, the exact bounded-input-to-Lipschitz seminorm is \(2n\max\{V_{n,a,b}(0),V_{n,a,b}(n-1)\}\), with a unique maximizing endpoint whenever \(a\ne b\). In the classical unweighted case the constant is \(2n(n/(n+1))^n\sim2n/e\); no interior evaluation point maximizes for \(n\ge2\).
