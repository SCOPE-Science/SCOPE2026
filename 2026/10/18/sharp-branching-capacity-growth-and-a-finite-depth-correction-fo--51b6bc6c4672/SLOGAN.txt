For the unweighted ancestor-summation operator on a complete rooted \(b\)-ary tree of height \(N\), the sharp \(\ell^p\) norm has an explicit branching-dependent limit after division by \(b^{(N+1)/p}\), for every fixed \(1<p<\infty\). At \(p=2\), the first relative finite-depth correction is precisely \(-\big(N(b-1)-1\big)b^{-(N+1)}\), with a controlled smaller remainder.
