The unnormalized heat flow on a diameter-three tree consisting of two \(m\)-leaf stars joined at their hubs has sharp time-weighted bounded-input-to-edge-Lipschitz constant asymptotic to \(e^{-1/2}\sqrt m\). For sufficiently many leaves the bridge edge is the only maximizing edge, and every optimal time is asymptotic to \(m/4\); an explicit two-exponential formula holds for the bridge at every time.
