On every complex Hilbert-space double-star weighted shift with \(p,q\ge1\) pendant edges, the dual numerical-radius norm of the all-pendant sum plus \(\beta\) times the bridge weight is exactly \(2(\sqrt p+\sqrt q)\) until \(\beta=(pq)^{1/4}\), and exactly \(2\sqrt{p+q+\beta^2+pq/\beta^2}\) afterward. Thus the bridge becomes active only beyond a sharp, branching-dependent threshold.
