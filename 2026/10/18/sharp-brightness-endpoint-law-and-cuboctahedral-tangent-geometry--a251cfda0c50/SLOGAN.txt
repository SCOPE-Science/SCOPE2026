The four-dimensional regular cross-polytope has an exact Haar hyperplane-projection-volume upper-tail cap law, while its lower tail is \(\frac{320\sqrt2}{\pi^2}t^3+O(t^4)\) above the minimum in the stated normalization. The sharp lower constant comes from a cuboctahedral tangent body of volume \(160/3\), rather than from the previously known extremal projection volumes.
