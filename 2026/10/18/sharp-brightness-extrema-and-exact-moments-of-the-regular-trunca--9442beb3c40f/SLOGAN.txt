For the unit-edge regular truncated octahedron, the area of an orthogonal planar shadow lies sharply between \(4\sqrt2\) and \(\sqrt{51}\): the minimum occurs along face diagonals, while the maximum occurs only along signed permutations of \((7,1,1)/\sqrt{51}\). Its complete directional area formula has two linear chambers, giving exact first and second orientation moments and a variance of \(0.0933124491448\ldots\).
