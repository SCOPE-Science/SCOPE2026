# Scientific assessment — sharp broadcast-dose frontier

**Correctness (PASS).** The proof reduces the stated homogeneous, uniformly driven, row-stochastic network system exactly to a scalar saturation ODE by uniqueness; it does not extrapolate from simulation. It separates the zero-input basins at the threshold \(q=a/k\), then establishes the sharp lower bound for *all* nonnegative finite-duration broadcast profiles using the pointwise calibration \(u\ge\Phi'(s)\dot s\). The explicitly constructed saturated-speed input attains the boundary dose but not success, and an arbitrarily small subsequent positive pulse gives actual success. Constant-pulse traversal time follows by separation of variables; strict convexity of its dose functional proves uniqueness of its minimizing amplitude. Symbolic identities, integrals, and example numerics were checked with the included reproducibility program. No infinite-time theorem depends on finite numerical enumeration.

**Originality (PASS, qualified).** Belaustegui et al., arXiv:2603.29214v1/v2 (2026), establish the clipped quadratic opinion-network dynamics, autonomous cascade structure, and qualitative square-wave signal effects. Sections IV–VII, including the revised 2026-09 full HTML article, were inspected and contain no all-input minimum-dose law, uniquely optimized rectangular amplitude, or comparison between their costs. Related earlier opinion-cascade works arXiv:2103.14764 and arXiv:2103.12223 use distinct sigmoid/attention models and focus on bifurcation sensitivity and input placement rather than minimum total broadcast dose. Gastaldi et al. arXiv:1805.10116 concerns a different two-timescale synaptic stimulation model; qualitative stimulus optimality is prior art, not claimed new. Search results in published mathematical research records and accessible prior finding claims gave no statement implying the combination. Remaining risk: a broadly formulated scalar minimum-fuel optimal-control result could yield the pointwise principle as a special case, and model-specific equivalents may exist under different terminology. This is not an independent priority certification.

**Mathematical value (PASS).** The motivating 2026 paper singles out temporal signal structure as a core modeling advantage and explicitly defers its formal study. The theorem supplies the exact cost frontier across arbitrarily many directed networks, the sharp nonattainment boundary, the earliest feasible triggering time, and a uniquely characterized optimal simple pulse. It quantifies a nonzero cost for restricting an intervention from optimally shaped to constant intensity. This is an application-specific structural control result, not a routine parameter substitution or claim that pulse optimization is itself new.

**Limitations and comparison.** The accepted claim is for one homogeneous broadcast control from the zero state, rather than optimal choice of influenced nodes, heterogeneous control, general sigmoid dynamics, or uncertain agent responses. Independent assessment of historical originality and scientific validity remains to be performed. Numerical checks corroborate, but do not prove, the general result.
