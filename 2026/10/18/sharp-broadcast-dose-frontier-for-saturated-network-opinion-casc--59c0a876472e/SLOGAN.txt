A uniform broadcast can tip any homogeneous row-stochastic nonlinear-opinion network from zero to full activation only with dose strictly greater than the exact, topology-independent value \(D_*=(1+k-a)q+kq^2/2+(k-a)\log(1-q)\), where \(a=1-\mu-\gamma\) and \(q=a/k\). Rectangular pulses have a unique least-cost amplitude, but even their optimal critical cost is strictly above the sharp time-shaped broadcast infimum.
