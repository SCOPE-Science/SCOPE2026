When individual susceptibility is capped and its mean and variance are known, the heterogeneous SIR fluid limit has an exact, fully attainable interval of cumulative infection pressure and eventual epidemic size, with the maximal outbreak realized by a two-point law supported at \(L\) and \(m-v/(L-m)\). The optimal two envelopes separate first at order \(t^4\), with pressure-width coefficient \(\tau^4s_0i_0^3vL[1-v/(m(L-m))]/24\); the opposing endpoint is the previously known coefficient-of-variation extremizer.
