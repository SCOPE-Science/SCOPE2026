The characteristic-two Serre-type maximal curves \(y^4+y=x^{(2^n+1)/3}\), for every odd \(n\ge3\), have Cartier nilpotency index exactly \(n\), attaining the general maximal-curve upper bound. An antipodal modular indexing of regular differentials explains the vanishing, while explicit chains show \(\mathcal C^{n-1}\ne0\) and give at least two longest blocks for \(n\ge5\).
