For any fixed richness \(s\ge3\), Shannon/Hill-one and inverse-Simpson/Hill-two evenness agree on all strict rankings until the census contains seven individuals beyond one per species; six excess individuals already permit a Simpson tie with unequal Shannon evenness. At seven excess individuals, the unique first strict disagreement, up to relabeling, is \((2,2,6)\) versus \((1,4,5)\) with matching singleton padding, and its exact arithmetic certification works for all richness levels.
