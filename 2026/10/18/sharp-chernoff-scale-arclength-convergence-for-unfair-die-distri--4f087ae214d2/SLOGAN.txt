For every strictly positive, nonuniform \(q\)-sided die, the deficit to limiting arclength \(2\), and the arclength created by each extra roll, both have sharp order \(\rho^n/\sqrt n\), where \(\rho\) is the Chernoff affinity against the fair die. The leading constants are explicit beta-function expressions in the nonarithmetic case and strictly positive, phase-dependent lattice sums in the arithmetic case.
