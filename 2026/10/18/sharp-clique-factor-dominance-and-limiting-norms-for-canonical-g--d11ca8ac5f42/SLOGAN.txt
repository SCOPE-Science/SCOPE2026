For every connected finite graph \(H\), adding a clique Cartesian factor \(K_m\) forces the canonical cycle projector to exceed the cut projector by at least \((m-4)/(m+2)\) for \(m\ge4\), sharply attained only when \(H=K_2\). As the clique grows, their endpoint norms approach \(3+\|Q_H\|\) and \(2+\|Q_H\|\), respectively, with no symmetry assumption on \(H\).
