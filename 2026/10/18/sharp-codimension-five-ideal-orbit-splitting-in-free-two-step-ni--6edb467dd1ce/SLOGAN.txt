For the free two-step Lie algebra \(\mathbb F_q^d\oplus\bigwedge^2\mathbb F_q^d\), the first automorphism-orbit split within a fixed pair of natural ideal defects occurs at codimension five: a four-dimensional alternating form has rank two or four. Every codimension-five ideal lies in one of exactly four orbits, with explicit Gaussian-polynomial orbit sizes valid for all finite fields.
