## Correctness — PASS
The proof fixes the row-versus-column Gram normalization and translates every full-size minor through an orthogonal complement. The complementary rows have the same norm because the original rows have the same norm. Their doubled-angle phases sum to zero. A zero-containing circular triangle has an arc of at least \(2\pi/3\), forcing a squared chord of at least three. The equality geometry restricts all phases to three cubic roots and balance then requires equal counts. A constructive completion proves attainment; compactness proves strictness in every remaining finite size. Exact-rational enumeration of the advertised extremizers for \(m=6,9,12\) corroborates the constants. Its finite scope is expressly limited.

Fodor--Naszódi--Zarnócz prove the general-weight Cauchy--Binet-strength guarantee and, by their dimension-two label reduction, already imply the single \(n=2\) numerical coefficient. Ivanov's inspected results discuss tight frames, exterior powers and maximization of sums of minors for projected zonotopes. The closest indexed published results concern total two-volume for five Parseval vectors, Grassmannian duals with one excess vector, or coherence, none of which covers this maximum-full-minor statement. The exact improvement factor and equality multiplicities were compared with the stronger-coverage possibilities. An equivalent unindexed tight-frame extremal result remains a meaningful residual risk.

## Value — PASS
The equal-weight restriction is natural: it is exactly the unit-norm tight-frame/equal-leverage subclass of the motivating decompositions, not an arbitrary coordinate slice. In a sharp finite-codimension regime, the guaranteed squared volume rises by a factor \(3(m-1)/(2m)\) over the standard lower bound (from \(5/4\) at the first sharp case toward \(3/2\)), and the full equality set is determined with a divisibility obstruction. These are quantitative and structural improvements relevant to determinant selection and redundant frame design. The problem remains open here outside the stipulated class.
