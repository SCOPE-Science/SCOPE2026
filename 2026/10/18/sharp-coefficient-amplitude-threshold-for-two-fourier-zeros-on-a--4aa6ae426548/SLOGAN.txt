Two exact Fourier zeros of a three-atom signal on a prime parabola require a sharp coefficient-magnitude ratio of at least \(\sin(\pi\lceil p/3\rceil/p)/\sin(\pi\lfloor p/3\rfloor/p)\). Near-equilateral root triples achieve equality on every support; exactly \(3p^2\) projective coefficient rays attain the threshold for primes \(p\ge7\), and \(6p^2\) do so at \(p=5\).
