The dissipative kicked rotor admits arbitrarily many distinct attracting period-one winding modes as damping weakens. Their sharp simultaneous capacity is an explicit integer staircase in \(W=((1+b)/(\pi(1-b)))^2\), with strict and interlaced thresholds \(W>q^2-1\) for \(2q\) sinks and \(W>q^2\) for \(2q+1\) sinks.
