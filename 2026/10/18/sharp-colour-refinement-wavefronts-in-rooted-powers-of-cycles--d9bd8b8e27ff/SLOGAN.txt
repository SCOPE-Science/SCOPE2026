For every \(k\ge2\), marking one vertex in the \(k\)-th power of a cycle of length \(n\ge4k\) makes counting colour refinement recover exactly the reflection-distance orbits. The complete round-by-round wavefront and optimal stabilization time are explicit; at the antipodal boundary \(n=4k\) a unique final collision forces three rounds, while at \(n=4k+1\) just two suffice.
