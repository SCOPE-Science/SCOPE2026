For the three-dimensional Hénon map, the fixed-point saddle-node is also the exact boundary of all bounded recurrence: above it every orbit escapes to infinity at least linearly, while at equality every bounded forward orbit converges to the sole fixed point. An exact affine-height increment gives a quantitative finite-residence bound and the stationary law \(\mathbb E[(X-c)^2]=\alpha_{\mathrm{SN}}-\alpha\).
