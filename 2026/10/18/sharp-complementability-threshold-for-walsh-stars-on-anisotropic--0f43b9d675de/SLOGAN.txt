For the Walsh star spectral subspace on a Hamming cube with one heavy coordinate of weight \(w\), the exact relative Lipschitz projection constant is \(\max\{1,\mathbb E[|S_m|\min\{|S_m|,w\}]/w\}\), where \(S_m\) is a sum of \(m\) independent random signs. For every \(m\ge3\), the subspace becomes contractively complemented precisely at the sharp threshold \(w=m\).
