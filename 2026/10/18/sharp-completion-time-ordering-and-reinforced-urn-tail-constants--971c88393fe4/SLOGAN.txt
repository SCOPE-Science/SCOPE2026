For every balanced \(q\)-color urn and every feasible equal copy target, coupon-completion times are strictly stochastically ordered: sampling without replacement is fastest, independent replacement is intermediate, and unit Pólya reinforcement is slowest. The reinforced completion tail has the exact decay \(\Pr(T_+>k)\sim q\Gamma(qa)\big/\Gamma((q-1)a)\sum_{j=0}^{r-1}(a)^{\overline j}/j!\; k^{-a}\), giving the sharp moment cutoff \(s<a\).
