For symmetric Dirichlet simplex weights, the sharp affine least-squares projector norm has an exact incomplete-beta formula and a uniform Gamma-coupling error at most \(\sqrt{na+1}/n\) after normalization by the number of barycentric coordinates. Its high-dimensional amplification remains bounded exactly when total Dirichlet concentration \(na\) remains bounded; if \(na\to c\), the norm tends to \(1+2c\), whereas divergent concentration gives a sharp Gamma absolute-deviation law.
