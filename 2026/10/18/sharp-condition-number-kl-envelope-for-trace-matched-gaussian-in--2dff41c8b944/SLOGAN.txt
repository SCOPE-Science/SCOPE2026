For trace-matched isotropic approximation of a \(k\)-dimensional inverse covariance at precision condition number \(K\), the exact maximum directed Gaussian KL divergence is \(\frac{k}{2}\max_{1\le m<k}\Phi_K(m/k)\), and every extremizer has only the two endpoint eigenvalues. The sharp large-condition-number loss grows like \(K/8\) per coordinate in high dimension, rather than the quadratic order of a previously available upper bound, and is attained inside a nontrivially coupled Lanczos inverse construction.
