For a full cyclic Gabor projector system generated by two taps, the exact best noise condition number on its entire observable matrix band is \(\sqrt{5-4\sin^2(\pi/(2L))}\) when their step has odd order \(L\ge3\), and \(\sqrt5\) for even step order. The optimal tap-power ratio is uniquely determined up to inversion, and the best separation on a fixed cycle is governed exactly by its smallest odd prime divisor (or the power-of-two regime).
