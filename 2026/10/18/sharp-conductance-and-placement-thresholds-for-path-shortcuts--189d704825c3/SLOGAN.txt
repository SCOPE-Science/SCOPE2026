For a unit-weight path, one shortcut of conductance \(w\) reaches the absolute one-link spectral-gap ceiling \(2-2\cos(2\pi/n)\) exactly when it is reflection-centered and meets an explicit sine threshold. The smallest such conductance is \(\sin(2\pi/n)/\cos((2\pi/n)\operatorname{dist}(n/4,2\mathbb Z+1))\), asymptotic to \(2\pi/n\); for a unit shortcut, there are precisely \(\lfloor n/4\rfloor\) optimal placements.
