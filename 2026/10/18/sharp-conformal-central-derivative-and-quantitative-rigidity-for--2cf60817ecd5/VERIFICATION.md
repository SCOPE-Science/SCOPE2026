# Verification and scope

The result is established by a direct analytic argument: Poisson first Fourier coefficients, ellipse support duality, strict convex minimization, an explicit boundary map, and the Radó–Kneser–Choquet theorem. The support-function deficit identity gives an explicit nonzero trace-stability coefficient. The near-round and eccentricity limits are integral/implicit-function consequences, not experiments.

Run `python3 checker.py` with Python 3 standard library to perform supplementary finite numerical checks of first harmonics, the ellipse boundary equation, support-function inequality, round-disk limit and near-round expansion. These checks do not validate infinitely many ellipses, independently audit the proof, or establish novelty.

The recent same-topic paper on general target geometry was available only at abstract level, so historical-priority uncertainty remains even though the inspected earlier full paper does not contain the ellipse classification.
