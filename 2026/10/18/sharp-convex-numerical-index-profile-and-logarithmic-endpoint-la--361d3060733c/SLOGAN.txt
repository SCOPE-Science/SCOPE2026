For the real two-dimensional \(\ell_p\) numerical index, the duality-invariant profile in \(\alpha=|1-2/p|\) is strictly convex and satisfies globally optimal linear bounds, with an explicit cubic correction at the Hilbert point. Its approach to the \(\ell_1\) and \(\ell_\infty\) endpoints has a sharp logarithmic deficit and a matching escape law for the extremizing coordinate ratio.
