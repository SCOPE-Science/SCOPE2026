The canonical octahedrally symmetric quartic spherical harmonic gives a convex support function \(1+\varepsilon(\sum_{i=1}^3u_i^4-3/5)\) exactly for \(-10/39\le\varepsilon\le5/18\). The two sharp endpoints develop different curvature-radius rank losses—twelve face-diagonal rank-one normals versus six axial rank-zero normals—and the enclosed volume is an explicit cubic polynomial in \(\varepsilon\).
