# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The projection formula follows from an explicit orthogonal cone–negative-dual decomposition. Its smooth Jacobian has symmetric blocks; induced coordinate sum and maximum matrix norms agree. Exact row sums, Cauchy–Schwarz equality, scalar strict concavity and a rational tangent upper bound prove the piecewise global formula and its sharp dimension-five crossover. The global bound is obtained by integrating derivatives and the lower bound by directional derivatives. The packaged standard-library checker replays 130,484 finite assertion groups; it does not certify the infinite family.

## Originality — PASS
Wang and Xiu (2011) provide the projection and derivative (their eqs. (10)–(11)); Kong, Tunçel and Xiu (2009) give a general generalized Jacobian. These establish differential input, not the optimized coordinatewise norm or its sharp dimension threshold. Their relevant full-text mathematical statements were inspected. An original 2008 coderivative article by Outrata and Sun was accessible only by abstract; potential unlocated equivalence remains a real residual risk. The exact derivative theorem does not, by a mere renaming of parameters, state the new optimal constant.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The common sharp Lipschitz modulus is a natural stability invariant for Lorentz-cone projection under coordinate errors. Its unique low-dimensional extremizer and exact cutoff from spatial to axial worst-case sensitivity yield a complete structural classification in arbitrary finite dimension, not a small numerical improvement.

## Limits
Real, unweighted coordinate norms only; Euclidean projection onto the standard Lorentz cone; no complete finite-pair extremizer classification. Bibliographic coverage is not exhaustive and the third historical source has not been inspected in full.
