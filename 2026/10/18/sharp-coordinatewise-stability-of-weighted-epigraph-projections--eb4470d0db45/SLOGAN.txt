The Euclidean projector onto a weighted maximum-norm epigraph has an exact sharp coordinatewise Lipschitz constant computable from sorted-prefix active sets; it is nonexpansive only at unit weights. Over all positive weights in dimension \(n+1\), the maximum is \((1+\sqrt{n+1})/2\), attained by exactly \(n+1\) explicit and geometrically distinct designs, whereas the unit-weight polar \(\ell^1\)-epigraph projector has constant \(2n/(n+1)\).
