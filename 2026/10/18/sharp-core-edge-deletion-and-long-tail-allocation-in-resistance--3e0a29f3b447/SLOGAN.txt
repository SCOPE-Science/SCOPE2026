On a clique with a pendant path, the unique conductance allocation minimizing total effective resistance deletes all edges between nonroot clique vertices exactly when their price reaches \(2(m+\ell)(m-1)/[(\ell+1)+(m+\ell)(m-2)]\). The entire optimum has explicit square-root weights, and for a long pendant path the optimally funded clique receives only a \(\Theta(\ell^{-3/2})\) fraction of the budget.
