For \(\mathrm{PL}(\mathrm{NE})\), every failed entailment with \(m\) antecedent nonemptiness occurrences already has a counterteam of at most \(m+1\) valuations, and this bound is sharp for every \(m\). Semantic entailment is therefore \(\mathrm{coNP}\)-complete, settling a complexity question explicitly raised in the 2025 nonemptiness-logic literature.
