# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The finite structures are bijective with side-preserving isomorphism types of simple regular bipartite incidence graphs. For fixed cardinality, direct induction over \(C^2\) formulas gives uniform truth using the exact atomic-pair count vector \((1,k-1,k-1,n-2k+1)\). For the first two available orders, complete incidence and matching complements are unique. For the third, the complement is 2-regular, classified by its even-cycle decomposition, with type count \(p(k+2)-p(k+1)\). For \(k\ge3\) all graphs at that threshold are connected by degree comparison. The Python checks provide independent finite corroboration and do not replace these infinite arguments.

A literature search cannot prove global publication novelty.
