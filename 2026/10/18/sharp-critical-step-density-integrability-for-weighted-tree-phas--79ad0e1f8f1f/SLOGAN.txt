At the exact step where a positive-weight tree phase map first loses its diffeomorphic Jacobian, it remains a homeomorphism, but pushing uniform phases forward creates a density in \(L^q\) exactly for \(q<1+d/2\). The endpoint divergence is logarithmic, with an explicit coefficient determined by the Perron eigenvector and the nonleading spectral gaps of the tree's weighted edge Laplacian.
