For a Euclidean-motion-invariant random affine hyperplane section of the \(n\)-cube, the vertex-count variance has sharp asymptotic \(\operatorname{Var}(V_n)\sim 4^n\sqrt n/(2\sqrt2\,\pi)\). Although its known mean is \(2^{n-1}\), the actual count divided by that mean converges to zero in probability.
