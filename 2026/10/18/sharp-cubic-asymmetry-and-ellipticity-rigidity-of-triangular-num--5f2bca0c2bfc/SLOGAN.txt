For nondegenerate upper-triangular numerical ranges on complex \(\ell_p^2\), the exact Hausdorff central-symmetry defect under a small diagonal split \(L\) is \(\frac{|\alpha\beta(\beta-\alpha)|}{6\alpha^{2\alpha}\beta^{2\beta}}\frac{L^3}{B^2}+O_p(L^5/B^4)\), where \(B\) is the off-diagonal magnitude, \(\alpha=(p-1)/p\), and \(\beta=1/p\). These always-positively-curved numerical ranges are centrally symmetric (and hence ellipses) precisely at the Hilbert exponent \(p=2\), apart from the disk and segment degeneracies.
