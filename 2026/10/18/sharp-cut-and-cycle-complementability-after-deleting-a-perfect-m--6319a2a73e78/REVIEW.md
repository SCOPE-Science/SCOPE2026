# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For crown graph Cr_n=K_{n,n} minus the diagonal matching with n≥3, the oriented edge space consists of n(n-1) coordinates a_ij (i≠j). The vertex-incidence cut space is constants plus two centered standard S_n modules; any S_n-equivariant map from this cut space into its Euclidean orthogonal complement is zero by stabilizer coefficient constraints; adjoints give the reverse cross-map. Consequently the Euclidean orthogonal cut/cycle projections are the unique equivariant projections, and averaging shows they minimize operator norms for every coordinate ℓp. Vertex Laplacian has nonzero eigenvalues n, n-2, 2(n-1), leading to a five-orbit signed column formula; summing absolute coefficients yields cut norm 3-4/n and cycle norm 2(2n²-6n+3)/(n(n-1)). Symmetry transfers ℓ1 columns to ℓ∞ rows. Directly built exact rational 2n×n(n-1) oriented incidences at n=3,4,5,6,7 and computed Moore-Penrose projectors: P²=P, ranks 2n-1, and both endpoint norms match every exact formula. Comparisons with intact K_nn give positive deletion gaps 2/n² and 2(3n-2)/(n²(n-1)), with crown crossover at n=5 versus intact n=4. The all-n proof relies on symmetry/eigen decomposition; finite tests are not exhaustive.

## originality

PASS

Dilworth–Kutzarova–Ostrovskii 2023 covers invariant-projection representation methods and Hamming/discrete-torus examples, including the n=4 cube special case; it does not state the general crown-family constants or deletion gaps. Published crown graph edge metric bases, domination and matching-deleted facet enumerations concern other invariants. The intact complete bipartite values are a comparison baseline, not a derivation of the crown values; deleting matching changes projection norms and threshold. Original component is the exact infinite family, not the already known 6-cycle and 3-cube.

Complementability of graph cut/cycle spaces is a concrete finite-dimensional Banach-space invariant. Deleting a canonical perfect matching from a dense edge-transitive graph is a natural test; exact unrestricted endpoint minima and the sharp switch of the dominant projector exhibit a nontrivial structural sensitivity of a standard family.
