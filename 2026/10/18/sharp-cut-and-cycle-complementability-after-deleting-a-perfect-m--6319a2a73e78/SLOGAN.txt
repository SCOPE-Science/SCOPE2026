Deleting a perfect matching from \(K_{n,n}\) yields exact real cut and cycle projection constants \(3-4/n\) and \(2(2n^2-6n+3)/(n(n-1))\) in the canonical edge \(\ell_1\) and \(\ell_\infty\) presentations. For the resulting crown graphs, the more expensive projection switches from cuts to cycles at \(n=5\), one dimension later than for the intact complete bipartite graphs; the orthogonal projections are optimal in every real edge \(\ell_p\) norm.
