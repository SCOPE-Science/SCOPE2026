For every triangular Johnson graph \(J(n,2)\) with \(n\ge5\), the cut and cycle spaces have exact real endpoint projection constants \((n-2)(3n-1)/(n(n-1))\) and \(2(2n-5)/(n-1)\), respectively. Their orthogonal projections are globally norm-minimizing in every edge \(\ell_p\), while the endpoint constants rise to distinct limits \(3\) and \(4\).
