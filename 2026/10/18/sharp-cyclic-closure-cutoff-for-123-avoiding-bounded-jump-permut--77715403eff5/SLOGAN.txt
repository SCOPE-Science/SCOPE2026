A classical \(123\)-avoiding Hamiltonian path with jumps at most \(m\) closes into a Hamiltonian cycle on \([n]\) for some ordering exactly when \(m\ge2\) and \(3\le n\le3m+1\). At the sharp cutoff \(n=3m+1\), every such ordering has the same four anchored entries: \((\pi_1,\pi_2,\pi_{n-1},\pi_n)=(2m+1,3m+1,1,m+1)\).
