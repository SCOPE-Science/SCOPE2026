For integer recurrences \(a_n=b^n+u_{n\bmod h}\) with periodic integer correction, the exact least digit budget for an infinite right-appending walk is the smaller largest circular gap among residues where \(u\) equals \(0\) or \(-1\). Below this sharp threshold, **every** walk has a uniform finite length bound independent of the starting term; if neither correction value occurs, even unbounded digit blocks cannot sustain an infinite walk.
