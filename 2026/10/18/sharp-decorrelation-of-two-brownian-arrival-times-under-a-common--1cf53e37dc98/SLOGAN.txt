Two independent Brownian searchers sharing one Poisson reset clock have strictly positively correlated individual arrival times at every finite reset rate. At high reset rate their Pearson correlation nevertheless obeys the sharp law \(\operatorname{Corr}(T_1,T_2)\sim2^{1/4}(\pi z)^{-1/2}e^{-(\sqrt2-1)z}\), where \(z=d\sqrt{r/D}\), quantifying how synchronized search histories decouple even under continued global resetting.
