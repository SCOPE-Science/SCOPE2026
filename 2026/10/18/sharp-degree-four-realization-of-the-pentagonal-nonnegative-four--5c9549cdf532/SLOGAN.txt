For real even, pointwise nonnegative and positive-definite Fourier polynomials normalized at zero and vanishing at the opposite pentagon chord, degrees at most three allow adjacent correlation at most \((3-\sqrt5)/2\) and necessarily give completely positive five-point Gram matrices. A degree-four polynomial realizes the sharp correlation \((\sqrt5-1)/2\) and the classical non-completely-positive pentagon matrix; a degree-preserving perturbation makes the kernel strictly positive and the five-point Gram strictly positive definite while retaining non-complete positivity.
