For mean-zero trigonometric polynomials of Fourier degree at most two, Poisson smoothing decreases every \(L^p\) norm at least by the radius factor; at degree three this already fails for \(L^4\) at **every radius**. The first three-mode failure has the exact algebraic small-radius amplification \(1.02744398345\ldots\), with a unique extremizing coefficient pattern up to its natural symmetries.
