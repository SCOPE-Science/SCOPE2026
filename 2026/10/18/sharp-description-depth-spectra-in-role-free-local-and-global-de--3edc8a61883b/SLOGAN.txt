With \(k\) unary predicates and no relations, local definite descriptions collapse at nesting depth two and have exactly \(2^k(1+3^{2^k-1})\) pointed theories, while global descriptions collapse at depth one with exactly \(2^k(1+2\cdot3^{2^k-1}-2^{2^k-1})\). Every theory has a representative of size at most \(\max\{2,2^{k+1}-1\}\), and both bounds are sharp.
