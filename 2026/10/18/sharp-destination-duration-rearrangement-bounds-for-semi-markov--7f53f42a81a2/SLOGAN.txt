For a finite absorbing semi-Markov network with fixed transition probabilities and fixed holding-time distributions at each state, the absorption-time mean is invariant under destination–duration dependence, but its variance has an exact sharp quantile-rearrangement interval. Sorting longer holds toward destinations with larger remaining mean absorption time maximizes the variance simultaneously from every starting state; reversing this sorting minimizes it, and a three-state example has variance ranging from \(1/12\) to \(13/12\) with the same marginals and mean.
