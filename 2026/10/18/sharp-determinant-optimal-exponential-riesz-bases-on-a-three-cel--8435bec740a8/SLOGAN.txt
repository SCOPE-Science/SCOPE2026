For three integer frequency cosets sampling the multittile \([0,2)\cup[3,4)\), the squared determinant of the exact analysis matrix has the sharp maximum \((9748+1360\sqrt{10})/729\). All optimal phase sets are rotations of \(\{-t_*,0,t_*\}\) with \(t_*=\arccos((\sqrt{10}-2)/6)\), and none of these three-coset systems can be tight.
