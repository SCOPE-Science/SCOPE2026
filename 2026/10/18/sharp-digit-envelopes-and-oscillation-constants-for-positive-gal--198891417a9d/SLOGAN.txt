Positive integer Galileo sequences with \(S_1=1\) and \(S_{2n}=kS_n\) have exact simultaneous pointwise lower and upper partial-sum envelopes given by binary-to-base-\(k\) digits and their dyadic reflection. Their best universal normalized growth constants are \(1/(k-1)\) and \(k-1/(k-1)\), both attained in the limit by explicit extremal sequences.
