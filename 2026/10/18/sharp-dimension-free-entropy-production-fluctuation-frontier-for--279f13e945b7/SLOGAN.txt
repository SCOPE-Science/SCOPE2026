For every positive mean log-likelihood ratio \(K\) between nondegenerate Gaussian endpoints, the complete attainable relative entropy variance interval is exactly \([(1-u_-(K))^2/2,(u_+(K)-1)^2/2]\), where \(u_\pm-1-\log u_\pm=2K\). The sharp extrema concentrate covariance mismatch in one mode and require coincident means; in the exact-score long-time thermodynamic limit this gives a dimension-free, sharp fluctuation envelope at fixed mean entropy production.
