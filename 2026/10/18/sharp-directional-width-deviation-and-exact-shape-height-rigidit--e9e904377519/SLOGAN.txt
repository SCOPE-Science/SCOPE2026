Every three-dimensional right prism has directional-width coefficient of variation at least \(\sqrt{16/[3(\pi^2-4\pi+8)]-1}\), with equality exactly for constant-width planar bases at one universal height-to-width ratio. An exact sum-of-squares identity separates base-shape and height defects, and the sharp polygonal prisms inherit the Reinhardt-base classification when the side count is not a power of two.
