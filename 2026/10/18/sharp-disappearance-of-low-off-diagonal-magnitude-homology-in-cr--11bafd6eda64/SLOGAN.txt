For the crown graph \(\mathrm{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}\setminus M_n\), the integral off-diagonal magnitude homology groups \(\operatorname{MH}_{2,3}\) and \(\operatorname{MH}_{3,4}\) are respectively \(\mathbb Z^6\) and \(\mathbb Z^{12}\) at \(n=3\), but both vanish for every \(n\geq4\). Thus isometric six-cycle subgraphs retain these classes internally even though their maps to the ambient crown vanish.
