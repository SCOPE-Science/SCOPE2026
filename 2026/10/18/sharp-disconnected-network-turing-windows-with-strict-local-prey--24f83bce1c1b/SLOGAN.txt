In a finite-patch strong-Allee predator–prey model, every simple nonuniform Turing threshold produces positive patterned equilibria whose mean prey and predator densities both decrease quadratically, with an exact coefficient determined by the Laplacian eigenvector and the mobility ratio. On path networks the homogeneous equilibrium can alternate between stability and instability through arbitrarily many disjoint diffusion windows, with a sharp predator mobility-ratio threshold of \(9\).
