For every positive integer shape, the Yule–Simon law has a sharp discrete power-concavity threshold: its largest admissible parameter is \(\alpha=-a_\rho\), where \(a_\rho\) is determined by the first three masses through \(((\rho+3)/2)^{a_\rho}+(\rho+2)^{-a_\rho}=2\). This exact lattice threshold strictly improves the earlier sufficient continuous-curvature parameter, and all later triples satisfy the inequality strictly at the boundary.
