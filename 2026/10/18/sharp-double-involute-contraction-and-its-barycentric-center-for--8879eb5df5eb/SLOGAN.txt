For every strictly convex six-sided centrally symmetric Minkowski norm, the Craizer–Martini central equidistant contracts exactly by \(\rho=2xyz/((x+y)(y+z)(z+x))\) per double involute, with a fixed explicitly weighted barycentric center. Its sharp universal bound is \(\rho\le1/4\), attained exactly by affine-regular hexagons; every strictly positive smaller rate also occurs.
