For the radial retraction of the real \(\ell_p\) unit ball, the exact weighted coordinate maximum-norm and sum-norm Lipschitz constants are governed by distinct scalar optimization laws with exponents \(p\) and \(p'=p/(p-1)\). Uniform coordinate weights uniquely minimize each constant; for every non-Hilbert exponent, the relative size of the two sensitivities reverses between dimension two and sufficiently large dimensions.
