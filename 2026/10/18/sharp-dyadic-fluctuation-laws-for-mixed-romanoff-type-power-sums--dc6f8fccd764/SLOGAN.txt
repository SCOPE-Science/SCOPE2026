For every integer \(r\ge2\), the mixed Romanoff set \(\{2^a+2^{b^r}:a,b\ge1\}\) has exact dyadic count \((N-1)\lfloor(N-1)^{1/r}\rfloor-\binom{\lfloor(N-1)^{1/r}\rfloor}{2}\) at most \(2^N\). Its normalized second-order counting error has a uniform limit law on \([-3/2,-1/2]\) for \(r=2\) and on \([-1,0]\) for \(r\ge3\), with effective Kolmogorov error \(O_r(K^{-1/r})\) over the first \(K\) cutoffs.
