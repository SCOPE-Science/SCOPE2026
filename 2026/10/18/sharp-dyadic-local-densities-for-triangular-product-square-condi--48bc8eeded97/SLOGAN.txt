The triangular-number map preserves Haar-uniformity over \(\mathbb Z_2\), allowing an exact formula for the probability that \(T(a)T(b)+n\) is a dyadic square for every integer \(n\). This local density always lies sharply between \(5/48\) and \(1/3\), with equality precisely at \(n\equiv2\pmod4\) and \(n\equiv1\pmod8\), respectively.
