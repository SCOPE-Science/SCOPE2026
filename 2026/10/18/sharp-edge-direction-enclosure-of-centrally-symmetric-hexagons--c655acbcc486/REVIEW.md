# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The representation of every genuine centrally symmetric hexagon as a three-generator planar zonotope follows directly from paired opposite edges. Projecting onto the basis of two generators gives the exact minimum coordinate bounding parallelogram. The excess for a pair is exactly the product of all three pair determinants divided by the square of the chosen determinant. Sorting the determinant magnitudes proves the optimum and rigidity without genericity assumptions beyond genuine six vertices. Affine-regular sharpness is verified by a complete enumeration of all 715 possible three- or four-support-line chord polygons for a fixed integral affine-regular model, using exact rational polygon clipping. The latter certificate was executed from the packaged file. The result makes no all-polygon assertion.

## Originality — PASS (residual historical risk)
Vígh (arXiv:2610.08168v1) defines chord quadrilaterals and gives a general \(\sqrt2\) chord bound; he verifies the pentagon case and computes the affine-regular hexagon ratio but does not prove an all-centrally-symmetric-hexagon sharp chord bound. Petty (1955), as described in Vígh and Fodor–Grundbacher (arXiv:2506.05185), gives \(4/3\) for **unrestricted** circumscribed parallelograms of centrally symmetric bodies, without any stated direction restriction. Neither inspected paper supplies the exact three-generator edge-direction optimizer law. Earlier equivalent results could exist; Petty's complete original text was not available and this risk is not treated as disproven by absence of a search hit. public mathematical-result index searches for chord hexagons, Petty-style parallelogram enclosures, and zonotope determinant equivalents returned nearby but non-covering records.

## Value — PASS
The 2026 chord conjecture imposes a discrete geometric direction restriction not addressed by the classical unrestricted theorem. The theorem settles the sharp bound and equality class for the entire natural family of genuine centrally symmetric hexagons (also all non-parallelogram one-fold planar translative tiles) and obtains an exact affine-invariant two-parameter area profile and complete optimizer-direction selection. This is a complete classification for a motivated geometric restriction, not a pointwise numeric improvement or arbitrarily chosen single parameter.
