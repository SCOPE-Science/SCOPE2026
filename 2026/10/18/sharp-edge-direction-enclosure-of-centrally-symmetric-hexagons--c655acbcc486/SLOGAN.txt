Every centrally symmetric hexagon has a circumscribed chord quadrilateral of at most \(4/3\) its area, sharply and with equality only for affine-regular hexagons. The least edge-parallel enclosing parallelogram has exact area \(S+\alpha\beta/\gamma\), where \(S\) is hexagon area and \(\alpha\le\beta\le\gamma\) are its three pairwise generator determinant magnitudes.
