**Correctness — PASS.** The full feasible set is symmetric, nonnegative stochastic kernels supported on one-edge-deleted balanced bicliques, with free holding loops. Group averaging justifies the orbit reduction. The proof diagonalizes the resulting operator into two scalar eigenspaces and a two-by-two odd block. The small-size column-norm certificate proves the branch through size seven. For every size at least eight, three positive convex-combination weights annihilate the gradient of the active spectral bounds, proving a global lower bound and uniqueness in the invariant family. Feasibility, boundary inequalities and the exact deletion penalty are explicitly reconstructed in `RESULT.md`. Finite matrix and symbolic checks are independent corroboration, not universal certification.

**Originality — PASS, with residual literature risk.** The foundational papers by Boyd and collaborators already establish the FMMC semidefinite framework, symmetry reduction and the intact complete-bipartite optimum. The known fastest-path solution subsumes the four-vertex case. The reviewed contribution is the complete two-orbit optimum for one deleted cross-edge for every larger balanced biclique, including the sharp seven/eight switch and quadratic deletion penalty. The inspected primary sections do not state or mathematically imply this explicit classification without the new analysis. A general SDP formulation permits computations but does not settle the symbolic all-size formulas. Source-index coverage cannot establish uniqueness against all publications; unindexed equivalents remain a risk.

**Value — PASS.** A single lost link in an otherwise complete bipartite network is a natural robustness test of optimal reversible mixing. The theorem determines exact globally best transition probabilities and a nontrivial switch in the active eigenvalue constraints, showing that the repair cost decays like the reciprocal square of the part size. These are quantitative, reusable network-design facts, not an arbitrary small-instance computation.
