Deleting one edge from a balanced complete bipartite network produces an exact change in its fastest symmetric Markov mixing rate: the optimal eigenvalue modulus equals \(1/\sqrt m\) through \(m=7\), then follows an explicit square-root formula for \(m\ge8\). A unique symmetry-invariant optimal allocation undergoes a sharp eight-part transition, while the large-network penalty for the missing edge is asymptotic to \(4/(9m^2)\).
