For two unit-length cyclic histogram grids displaced by \(0<t<1\), the exact \(L^1/L^\infty\) commutator norm is \(2\max\{t,1-t\}^2\) with three or more bins, and \(2\max\{t,1-t\}|2t-1|\) with two bins. It tends to the maximal value \(2\) as the displacement vanishes, despite convergence to zero in every \(L^p\) operator norm with \(1<p<\infty\).
