For every equal-order finite generalized quadrangle of order \((q,q)\), the canonical real cut and cycle projections on its incidence graph have exact endpoint norms \(1+6q^4/((q+1)^2(q^2+1))\) and \(8q^4/((q+1)^2(q^2+1))\). The larger norm switches from cut to cycle at \(q=3\), while the sharp uniform upper bounds \(7\) and \(8\) are approached along prime-power orders without requiring edge transitivity.
