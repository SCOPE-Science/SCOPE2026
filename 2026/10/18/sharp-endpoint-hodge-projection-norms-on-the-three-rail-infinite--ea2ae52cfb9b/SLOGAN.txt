For the infinite three-rail square-lattice strip, the canonical orthogonal cut and cycle-flow projectors have sharp \(\ell_1\) norms \(7/3+1/\sqrt5-1/\sqrt{21}\) and \(2+2/\sqrt{21}\), respectively. Their worst-case single-edge inputs have different orientations: an outer horizontal edge for the cut projector, a vertical edge for the cycle projector.
