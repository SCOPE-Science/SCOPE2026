For sums of finite-product functions depending on at most \(d\) coordinates, the canonical interaction projection is least-norm in every real \(\ell_p\), and its exact \(\ell_1\)/\(\ell_\infty\) constants reduce to explicit signed elementary-symmetric sums. For a fixed alphabet of size \(q\) and fixed interaction order \(d\ge2\), those sharp endpoint constants grow like \(r^{d/2}(q-1)^{d/2}\mathbb E|\operatorname{He}_d(Z)|/d!\).
