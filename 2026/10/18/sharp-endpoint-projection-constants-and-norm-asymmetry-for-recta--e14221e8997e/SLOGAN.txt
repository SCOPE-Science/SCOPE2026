For the unequal-axis rook graphs \(K_m\square K_n\), \(3\le m<n\), all four real endpoint projection constants of the cut and cycle spaces in the edge \(\ell_1\) and \(\ell_\infty\) norms have explicit sharp formulas. The cut constants satisfy \(\lambda_\infty(B)>\lambda_1(B)\), while the cycle constants satisfy \(\lambda_1(Z)>\lambda_\infty(Z)\), and the optimizing equivariant projections are nonorthogonal.
