Among all two-term Müntz spaces \(\operatorname{span}\{1,t^\alpha\}\) with \(\alpha>0\), the ordinary affine space uniquely minimizes the \(L^1\)- and \(L^\infty\)-norms of its \(L^2\) orthogonal projector, with sharp value \(5/3\). The worst evaluation endpoint switches at \(\alpha=1\), while the projector norm and Christoffel peak exhibit different singular asymptotic orders.
