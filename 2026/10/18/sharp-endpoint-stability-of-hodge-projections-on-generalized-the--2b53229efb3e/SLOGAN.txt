On any simple \(r\)-arm generalized-theta network, the Hilbert-orthogonal edge-gradient projector has its sharp \(\ell^\infty\) norm on a shortest arm, with a closed formula depending only on the path lengths through their reciprocal sum. Its bounded-input amplification can approach \(r\), whereas equal-length arms keep it below \(3\); the complementary circulation projector has a parallel sharp law approaching \(r-1\).
