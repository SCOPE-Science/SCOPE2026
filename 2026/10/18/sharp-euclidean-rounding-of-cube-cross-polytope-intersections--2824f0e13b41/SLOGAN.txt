The cube–cross-polytope intersection \(K_{d,t}=[-1,1]^d\cap tB_1^d\) is uniquely closest to Euclidean space at cutoff \(t=\sqrt d\) in every dimension \(d\ge2\). Its sharp Banach–Mazur distance is \(\sqrt{\lfloor\sqrt d\rfloor+(\sqrt d-\lfloor\sqrt d\rfloor)^2}\), attained only by spherical distance ellipsoids, and has order \(d^{1/4}\).
