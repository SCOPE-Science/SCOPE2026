The minimum Euclidean width of the standard \(n\)-vertex cyclohedron is exactly \(\sqrt n(A_n+\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor)/\sqrt{\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor}\) for \(n=3\) and \(n\ge5\), with \(A_n=(n-1)(n-2)/2\); at \(n=4\) it is instead \(4\sqrt3\). Its only optimal normal directions are centered indicators of singleton cyclic blocks at \(n=3,4\) and balanced contiguous blocks thereafter, a sharp cut-balance transition.
