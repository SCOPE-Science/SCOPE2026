For every even number of colors, odd-part overpartition tuples obey a sharp internal \(2\)-adic congruence at every sufficiently deep dyadic level: the exponent is \(3i-4\) for \(\nu_2(m)=1\), and \(3i+2\nu_2(m)-5\) otherwise, with equality exactly at odd squares. A stable Chebyshev-polynomial invariant proves the previously conjectural threefold valuation growth for all even colors.
