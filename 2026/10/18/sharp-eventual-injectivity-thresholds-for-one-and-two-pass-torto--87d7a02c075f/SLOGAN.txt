For the period-doubling infinite word, the tortoise map is injective on all length-\(n\) factors exactly from \(n=10\) onward, while its twofold iterate is injective from \(n=18\) onward; collisions at lengths \(9\) and \(17\) make the eventual thresholds sharp. A finite substitution-closed factor certificate and a general prefix-lifting argument prove both infinite statements.
