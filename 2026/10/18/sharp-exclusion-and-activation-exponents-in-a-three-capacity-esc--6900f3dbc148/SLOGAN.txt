For three equally spaced ecological capacity scores, maximizing order-\(q\) Hill diversity at a fixed escort mean excludes an extreme species precisely below a sharp capacity threshold when \(0<q<1\), whereas all three species remain in the benchmark at interior budgets for \(q\ge1\). The newly admitted species turns on with a rigorously determined power law whose exponent changes formula at \(q=1/2\), revealing a second, order-dependent regularity transition.
