For a row-normalized independent-uniform random transition matrix on \(n\) states, excluding one prescribed deterministic map from the support of every grand coupling is possible with probability \(1-q_n\), where \(q_n\sim e^{n+1/2}/(n^n[n(n-1)]!)\). The exact representability criterion is \(\sum_i p_{i,f(i)}\le n-1\), giving a sharp high-dimensional answer to a one-map instance of the coupling-support problem.
