For original FBi-PG on a rank-deficient two-dimensional quadratic, the exact universal bilevel-selection boundary is \(\gamma=2\): every initialization and allowed offset converges to the bilevel minimizer for \(0<\gamma\le2\), including the critical endpoint, whereas every \(\gamma>2\) admits an allowed offset with a strictly nonoptimal limit. The distinction comes from the \(\lambda_kt_k\asymp1/k\) critical dissipation, not merely summability of the bare Tikhonov weights.
