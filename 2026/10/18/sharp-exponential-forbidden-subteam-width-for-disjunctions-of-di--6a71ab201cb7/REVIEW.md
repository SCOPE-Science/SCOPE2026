# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The claim is explicitly about the smallest **at-most-size** failing-subteam witness bound. Lax splitting equals one chosen constant per coordinate. The finite-character induction covers infinite teams, and the diagonal dual tensor pairing over rational numbers proves that a finite inclusion-minimal obstruction has at most \(2^n\) rows. The complete Boolean cube proves sharpness. The diagonal with \(n+1\) distinct coordinate values gives the smaller extremal size. All quantitative claims apply to every integer \(n\ge1\), including the empty-team boundary. Reproducible finite tests checked 788 enumerated teams and 30,790 dual tensor pairings; they support, but do not establish, the general theorem.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk
The relevant full conference version of Fröhlich–Kolaitis–Meier (2026, first preprint 2025-08-22) gives the two-disjunct case with width 4, not arbitrary distinct-coordinate disjunct counts. The full May 2026 Barlag et al. preprint proves a qualitative coherence/rewritability connection; its repeated-same-variable example and its trivial binary-propositional \(2^n\) size bound do not imply an alphabet-independent optimal bound for general relations. Targeted research-record and literature searches under several equivalent combinatorial formulations found no dominating result. Nonetheless an older transversal or database formulation is possible, and the complete 2010 thesis was unavailable. Priority is provisional, not independently verified.

## Value — PASS
The theorem extends the exact two-constancy-atom benchmark to **every** arity, determines an exponential sharp cutoff, distinguishes small \(n+1\)-row failures from maximal \(2^n\)-row failures, and gives a structural linear-algebraic proof usable even for infinite relations over arbitrarily large alphabets. It supplies a precise quantitative obstruction theory for a natural family of team-semantic integrity constraints, beyond mere first-order definability or a bounded sample.

## Principal scientific limits
The theorem does not cover general functional dependence atoms, does not establish a classification of all minimal obstruction shapes or all attainable intermediate sizes, and may have an earlier equivalent formulation in literature not inspected in full. The 2010 doctoral thesis was not accessible for complete comparison. There is no independent audit.
