For normalized interpolation on \(N\) equally spaced points of a Bargmann–Fock circle of squared radius \(\rho N\), the worst squared interpolation norm grows as \(\Theta_\rho(\sqrt N e^{NM(\rho)})\), with an explicit positive identric-mean rate. The worst Fourier residue undergoes a sharp transition at \(\rho=e^{-1}\), and its limiting location is determined by the fractional part of the unique identric-mean crossing.
