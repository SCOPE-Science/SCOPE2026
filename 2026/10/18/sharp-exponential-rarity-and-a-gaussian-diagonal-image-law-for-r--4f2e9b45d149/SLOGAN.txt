For a uniform random binary operation on \(n\) labels, regularity in the magma monoid has sharp probability \(\Pr(R_n)\sim1.25697010057\,e^{-0.17189797338n}\). Among regular operations the number of distinct diagonal values obeys a Gaussian limit centered at \(0.68001562579n\), with variance asymptotic to \(0.09395019n\); the prior exact finite enumeration is credited to Baiduk–Kozerenko.
