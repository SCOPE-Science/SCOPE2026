# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof explicitly centers a four-level test vector and computes squared norm \(V=9pq/(p+q)-4\). Every budgeted spoke/bridge has squared test-vector difference \(1\) and each extra intra-module edge has difference \(0\), proving the upper bound. Equality forces zero extra rates and fixes all remaining conductances by the eigenvector equation, without assuming symmetry of competitors. A four-orbit quotient and the transverse eigenspaces yield the exact eigenvalue list and the size-ratio iff criterion; compactness gives strictness otherwise. An exact-rational verification program passed \(529\) parameter pairs; universal validity is derived analytically.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

Sun–Boyd–Xiao–Diaconis supply the general budgeted spectral-gap convex optimization model. The explicit barbell spectra of Boyd–Diaconis–Parrilo–Xiao concern a discrete-time stochastic objective. Jafarizadeh (2020) also treats a bridged clique family, but under a reversible discrete-time SLEM objective with stationary-distribution normalization. Ghosh–Boyd–Saberi solve resistance minimization on the same barbell and retain nonzero internal conductances. Fiedler and Richter–Rocha provide general tree and double-broom results, which may subsume the restricted double-star values but do not by themselves prove optimal deletion of arbitrary additional intra-module edges. Published mathematical records located by targeted claim and alias searches did not identify the full supergraph sparsification theorem or factor-two iff cutoff. Machine-accessible full mathematical text for some tree sources was incomplete; unindexed equivalent coverage remains a risk.

## Value — PASS

The work derives an exact globally unique resource allocation across a broad natural family of connected networks, including arbitrarily dense cliques on either side of a bottleneck. It proves that unused dense edges are not merely optional choices of an invariant optimizer but are forced to receive zero budget, and identifies exactly when the proposed sharp dual certificate succeeds. The exterior imbalanced optimization remains open within this result.
