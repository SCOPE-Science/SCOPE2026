For two hub-centered networks joined by a single bridge and given a fixed total conductance budget, the sharp fastest-continuous-time-mixing bound is attained exactly when the vertex counts differ by at most a factor of two. In that range the unique optimizer deletes every optional nonhub-to-nonhub edge, concentrates conductance on a weighted double star, and has exact Laplacian gap \(B/(9pq/(p+q)-4)\).
