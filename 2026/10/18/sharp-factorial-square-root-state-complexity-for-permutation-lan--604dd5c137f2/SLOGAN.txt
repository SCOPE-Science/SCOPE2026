For every minimal \(n\)-state permutation DFA, the square-root language \(\{w:ww\in L\}\) has at most \(n!\) deterministic states, and a two-letter symmetric-group witness attains all \(n!\) for every \(n\ge3\). The bound collapses to one state for \(n\le2\), and no unary witness attains the factorial bound when \(n\ge3\).
