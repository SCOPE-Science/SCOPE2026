For a fixed real two-column basis with true condition number \(\kappa>\tau>1\), a Gaussian sketch with \(s\) rows falsely reports condition number at most \(\tau\) with sharp probability \(p_s\sim\mathcal C(\kappa,\tau)s^{-1/2}e^{-s\mathcal I(\kappa,\tau)}\), where \(\mathcal I=\log((\kappa^2+\tau^2)/(2\kappa\tau))\) and the multiplier is explicit. An exact finite-sample beta integral accompanies the rate; the guarantee applies to a fixed basis independent of the sketch, not to an entire adaptive restarting procedure.
