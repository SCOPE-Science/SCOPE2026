# Scientific review: cyclic golden-mean all-window covers

## Correctness (C): PASS
The exact graph, degree formulas, balancing duplicate arcs, first-one potential, and tight path-cost inequality establish a general theorem for every \(n\ge2\). The potential lower bound applies to every cyclic covering word, including all repeated-window choices. The explicit Eulerian augmentation attains equality. A dependency-free check confirms complete window coverage, exact multiplicities, dual values, and the constructed cycles through \(n=17\); shorter legal cycles were exhaustively excluded through \(n=5\). The finite replay is supporting evidence rather than a replacement for the infinite proof.

## Originality (O): PASS within inspected sources
Moreno and Matamala's forbidden-substring universal cycles require each demanded word to be periodically admissible and optimize lexicographic order. The present theorem demands **all linearly admissible** windows; the word \(101\) at order three distinguishes these domains. Contemporary bounded-weight universal cycles are about weight and decoding, RLL constructions optimize distinct-window *maximum* lengths, and Hamming-radius covering cycles use a different coverage relation. The inspected texts do not imply the exact Fibonacci route-inspection cost. Searches by the equivalent postman, constrained-superstring, golden-mean, and Fibonacci names did not identify a covering formula. This is a bounded literature conclusion, not proof that no older formulation exists.

## Value (V): PASS
A whole infinite family of constrained binary cyclic cover problems receives an exact optimum and an optimal repeated-window multiset. The sharp graph potential is a usable structural certificate and provides a benchmark for constrained universal-window generation, beyond isolated experimental counts.

## Closest prior work and limits
Relevant inspected primary sources are arXiv:0711.1695 (restricted de Bruijn cycles), arXiv:2603.11934 (bounded-weight decoding), arXiv:2403.01454 (maximum distinct-window RLL cycles), and arXiv:2404.13674 (Hamming covering). Unindexed postman special cases and the unexamined full text of arXiv:math/0310385 remain literature risks. The theorem does not classify all optimum cycles, other forbidden constraints, or efficient ranking algorithms.
