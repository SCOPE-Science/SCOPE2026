For all \(n\ge2\), the shortest cyclic binary word avoiding adjacent ones yet containing every admissible length-\(n\) window has exactly \(F_{n+2}+F_n-1\) positions. An optimal tour repeats precisely the \(F_n-1\) admissible windows of form \(0u0\) whose middle contains a one, and a sharp potential bound proves no shorter tour exists.
