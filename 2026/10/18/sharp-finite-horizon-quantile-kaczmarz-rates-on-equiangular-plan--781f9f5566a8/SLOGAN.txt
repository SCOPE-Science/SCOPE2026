On equispaced planar tight-frame equations, tie-inclusive quantile Kaczmarz has the exact worst k-step squared-error factor Q_{m,r}B_{m,r}^{k-1}. At nonzero post-projection error, even rank r<m admits r+1 rows; at zero error all rows are admitted and the iterate stays at the solution. Fixed rank r≥2 has a contraction gap of order m^-2 despite perfect frame conditioning; rank one stalls.
