For the real degree-two recursive TensorSketch, separable rank-one trace estimation has exact variance \(2q(a+b)+2Uab\), where \(q=(2m-1)/m^2\) and \(U=2q^2+(m+\gcd(2,m)-2)(m-1)^2/m^4\). Flat tensor factors uniquely maximize this variance, and the leading large-width worst-case constant approaches \(10/m\) as factor dimension grows.
