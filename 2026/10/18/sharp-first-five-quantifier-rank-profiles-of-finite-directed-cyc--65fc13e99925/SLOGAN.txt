For finite one-component directed successor cycles, the first five first-order quantifier-rank cutoffs are exactly \(2,4,7,11,19\): at rank \(q\leq5\), distinct lengths below the corresponding cutoff have distinct theories, while every two lengths at or above it have the same theory. The rank-five boundary \(19\) is sharp despite the elementary uniform long-cycle game bound of \(32\).
