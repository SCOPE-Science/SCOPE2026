For order-\(4n\) dicyclic groups, the total discrepancy \(\sum_{A}(|AA|-|AA^{-1}|)\) is strictly negative for \(2\le n\le29\) and strictly positive for every \(n\ge30\). An exact Lucas-number formula and a rigorous large-parameter bound certify this sharp first-moment transition without asserting product-versus-quotient dominance of subset counts.
