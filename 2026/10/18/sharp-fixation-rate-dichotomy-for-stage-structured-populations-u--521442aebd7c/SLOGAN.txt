In a stage-structured population repeatedly exposed to genotype-dependent treatment, residual susceptibility has a sharp exponential Perron-root decay rate when heterozygotes are selected against. When resistance is completely dominant, the decay instead obeys the universal law \(k[1-y_i(k\tau^+)]\to1/\sum_j s_jw_jv_j\), with reproductive-value weights determining each stage’s impact.
