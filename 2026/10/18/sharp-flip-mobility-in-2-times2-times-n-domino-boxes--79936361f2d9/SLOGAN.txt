The minimum and maximum numbers of elementary flips available from a domino tiling of a fixed \(2\times2\times n\) box are exactly \(\lceil2n/3\rceil\) and \(3n-2\). At lengths \(n=3k\), exactly \(4\cdot3^{k-1}\) least-mobile tilings exist, with a complete description by alternating three-layer tubes; a fixed sixteen-state transfer also enumerates every possible local flip degree.
