For recursive trees with attachment-weight fractions \(p_n=cn^{-\beta}\), \(1/2<\beta<1\), total path-length variance changes from a superquadratic power to \(3c^3n^2\log n\) at the sharp threshold \(\beta=2/3\), then becomes asymptotically quadratic. Above that threshold, centered path length divided by \(n\) converges almost surely and in \(L^2\) to a nondegenerate limit; at or below it, the same normalization cannot converge in \(L^2\).
