Compared records on central odd-polygon halfplane cuts, square partial-plank area, octagonal **symmetry-axis only** comparisons, and hexagonal enclosing-parallelogram problems have different domains or objects and do not imply this exact unrestricted single-normal profile. Searches for regular-hexagon strip maxima and their equivalent one-dimensional projection concentration formulation did not identify a stronger covering theorem. Historical-priority searches cannot eliminate unindexed older exact hexagon-strip calculations; independent priority review is outstanding.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A natural planar covering problem recently reduced to one plank still has a genuine direction/placement optimization. This proof computes the global optimum over **all normals** of the regular hexagon, identifies two distinct oblique optimization windows, and proves three non-artificial sharp transition widths, in a way not supplied by the general reduction or by symmetry-axis comparisons. Its explicit profile is a benchmark for shape sensitivity in plank concentration problems, not a parameter substitution or merely a finite numerical experiment.
