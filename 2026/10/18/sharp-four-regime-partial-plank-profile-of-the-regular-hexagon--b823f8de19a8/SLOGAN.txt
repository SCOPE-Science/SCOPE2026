For a regular unit-circumradius hexagon, the largest area covered by any finite family of Euclidean planks of total width \(0<W<\sqrt3\) has a sharp four-regime formula attained by one centered plank. Its uniquely optimal normal direction changes from edge-normal to oblique to vertex-normal and back to oblique, with exact transitions at \(W=\sqrt3/2\), \(W=1\), and \(W=3/2\); the full formulas and the oblique direction equation are proved in `RESULT.md`.
