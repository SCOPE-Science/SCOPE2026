A sharp four-dimensional simplex-volume transition occurs when the long and short roots of type \(F_4\) have equal length: the unrestricted optimum for \(K_t=\operatorname{conv}(L\cup tS)\) is \(\max\{1/2,t^4/8\}\). Exactly 48 simplices maximize on either side of \(t=\sqrt2\), while at the transition 96 pure-root simplices tie and all mixed-root simplices have a strict volume gap.
