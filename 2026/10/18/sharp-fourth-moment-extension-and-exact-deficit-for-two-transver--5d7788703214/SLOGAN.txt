For a union of the two coordinate axes over any finite abelian group \(A\) of order \(N\ge4\), the sharp normalized-source fourth-power Fourier-extension constant is exactly \(N^3/(2N-1)^2\); every nonzero extremizer is a single character concentrated on one entire axis. An exact eight-term nonnegative convolution-deficit identity proves both sharpness and rigidity and identifies order four as the threshold beyond the exceptional three-point group.
