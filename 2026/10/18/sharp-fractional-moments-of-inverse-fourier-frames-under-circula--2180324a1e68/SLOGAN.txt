For an \(N\)-point circular \(\beta\)-repulsive Fourier sampling design, the \(p\)-th moment of inverse-Gram amplification exists exactly when \(2p<\beta+1\), independently of \(N\) and of bounded positive changes to the sampling density. Thus unitary repulsion gives finite mean but infinite variance for squared Gaussian reconstruction error, even though the classical determinantal identity supplies its exact mean.
