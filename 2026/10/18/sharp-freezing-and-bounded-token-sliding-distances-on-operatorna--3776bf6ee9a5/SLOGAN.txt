For every \(n\ge5\), token sliding on independent sets of \(\operatorname{KG}(n,2)\) is connected with up to three tokens but becomes entirely frozen at four, with all higher occupancies isolated. The three-token graph admits an exact Johnson-incidence distance formula and has diameter at most eight, while the two-token graph always has diameter four and both levels have exactly \(\frac{n(n-1)(n-2)(n-3)}2\) edges.
