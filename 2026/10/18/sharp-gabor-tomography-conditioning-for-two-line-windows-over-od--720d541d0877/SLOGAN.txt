For the cross-shaped window \(g_c=\mathbf1_{x=0}+c\mathbf1_{y=0}\) on \(\mathbb F_q^2\) with odd characteristic \(p\), the complete Gabor projector system has optimally small condition number \((q-1)/\sin(\pi/(2p))\), attained uniquely by opposite line weights \(c=-1\). This sparse optimizer is informationally complete, and its weakest Weyl eigenspace has an explicit multiplicity, including a characteristic-three boundary exception.
