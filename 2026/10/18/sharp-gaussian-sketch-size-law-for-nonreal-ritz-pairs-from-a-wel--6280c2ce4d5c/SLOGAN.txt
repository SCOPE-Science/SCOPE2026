For two-step real-Gaussian sketched Arnoldi on the positive-definite matrix \(\operatorname{diag}(1,2,3)\), nonreal Ritz pairs occur with strictly positive probability at every finite sketch size. The probability obeys the sharp law \(P_s\sim\sqrt{3/(2\pi s)}\,3^{-s/2}\), even though ordinary Ritz values are real.
