For real \(\mathcal L(\ell_1^n,\ell_\infty^m)\), a norm-one rank-one operator with \(r\) saturated row factors and \(c\) saturated column factors needs at most \((m-r+1)(n-c+1)\) rank-one extreme contractions, and this count is generically necessary on every saturation stratum. The global worst case is exactly \(mn\): multiplicatively many extreme terms can be forced even though the operator has rank one.
