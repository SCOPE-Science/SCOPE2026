# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS
The Artin–Schreier genus formula and splitting rules apply to the specified polynomial one-pole family. Seven explicit degree-six Frobenius orbits over \(\mathbf F_2\) have trace moments summing to zero through exponent twenty; any curve with no degree-six place would require seven odd trace values, contradicting that identity. For the degree-twenty-one witness the eleven necessary trace values are checked individually, as are \(N_1=5\), \(N_2=5\), \(N_3=17\), \(N_6=17\). The standalone exact arithmetic checker reproduces every certificate. The lower bound is universal within the stated family, not a finite sample.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
The 2023 work of Aubry, Herbaut and Monaldi investigates universal closed-place thresholds and gives exact results through genus three, but not this restricted-family genus-ten sharpness theorem. Xarles's 2020 census covers genus four and cannot imply the explicit genus-ten witness or the all-genus-below-ten trace-dual obstruction. Focused searches for the example equation, equivalent degree-six/degree-three inertness, low-conductor binary Artin–Schreier curves and Frobenius trace-code formulations located no decisive covering theorem. Older results stated in trace-code terminology remain a bibliographic risk.

## Value — PASS
The exact smallest genus is a naturally motivated endpoint for degree-six place avoidance among one-pole double covers. The seven-orbit parity witness gives a concise obstruction stronger than checking a finite list of genus-four models, while the very sparse genus-ten witness attains the threshold. The method points toward analogous sharp conductor thresholds for different degrees and base fields without claiming them here.

## References and limitations
Aubry, Herbaut and Monaldi, arXiv:2310.11218 (2023); Xarles, arXiv:2007.07822 (2020). The result is not extended to arbitrary genus-ten covers or multiple-pole Artin–Schreier models. Mathematical priority remains subject to external examination.
