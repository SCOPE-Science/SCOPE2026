# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The proof independently specifies the full positive-parameter vector field, all real initial conditions, the quotient-cylinder lift, and the exact stability threshold. The auxiliary \((y,z)\)-subsystem is uniformly bounded by a stable linear convolution with bounded sinusoidal forcing, proving global forward existence. For \(b>cd\), the explicit \(V_h\) has positive-definite \((y,z)\) quadratic part and strict dissipation. At \(b=cd\), the coercive sum-of-squares energy has an exact nonpositive derivative and its zero-derivative set has no non-equilibrium complete invariant trajectory; compactness, LaSalle, connected omega limits, and lift continuity prove convergence without assuming hyperbolicity. The energy barrier gives a strict invariant strip. For \(b<cd\), a complete Routh sign analysis establishes hyperbolic saddles and the lower-dimensional union of their stable manifolds. Symbolic reproduction and finite ODE checks succeeded; neither replaces the infinite-time proof.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence, residual priority risk retained

Li–Zeng (2023), 12-page primary open-access PDF, supplies the same smooth sine jerk equations, equilibrium lattice, local spectral calculations, numerical bifurcation plots and multistability. Its results do not give the parameter-uniform all-orbit if-and-only-if convergence criterion, critical sum-of-squares energy, or trapping barrier. Ma et al. (2014) concern sine-forced jerk circuits with mixed Heaviside control, but only their abstract and bibliographic record were accessible: its full text remains a genuine coverage risk. Ding–Feng (2020) use a different special-form, nonsmooth sine feedback. Published generic third-order feedback weighted-slope identities give a **covered necessary condition** for compact recurrence; they do not imply global convergence of all forward orbits or the explicit closed critical barrier. published-finding corpus semantic queries using the source name, model equations, parameter threshold, alias phrasing and Lyapunov equivalences found no closer same-model theorem. Search absence is not proof of uniqueness; older unindexed control/jerk-system treatments remain a priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The source's sine-jerk model is explicitly motivated by multiscroll chaotic and multistable regimes; a sharp global convergence boundary is mathematically consequential because it separates a closed region where the phase flow is gradient-like from a region where all equilibrium stable sets are negligible in Lebesgue measure. The critical endpoint cannot be inferred from strict Hurwitz stability: it contains equilibria with imaginary eigenvalues. A constructive energy barrier further identifies nontrivial trapping subbasins. These are structural global results for the entire positive-parameter family, rather than a narrow numerical slice or a routine reparameterization.

## Limits and inspection risks

The proof makes no chaos-existence claim below threshold, gives no full basin census, and does not claim independent audit. Priority risk remains for Ma et al. (2014) full text and unindexed equivalent passivity arguments; these are reported rather than treated as disproved antecedents.
