For the sinusoidal jerk flow \(\dot x=y,\ \dot y=az,\ \dot z=-y-bz+c\sin(dx)\), every initial state converges to a lattice equilibrium exactly when \(b\ge cd\), including the nonhyperbolic boundary. At \(b=cd\), an explicit sum-of-squares energy both proves convergence and certifies trapped attraction basins below the barrier \(E=2c^2\); below that parameter threshold almost every state fails to converge to any equilibrium.
