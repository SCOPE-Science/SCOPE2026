For the smooth coupled Gaussian-cubic map, every real-plane orbit is bounded precisely for \(-\tfrac12\le b<\tfrac12\); escape opens outside that interval. At the excluded endpoint \(b=\tfrac12\), every escaping orbit obeys the universal ultraslow clock \(|v_n|^2=\log n+2\log\log n+\log(2a)+o(1)\), whereas outside both endpoints unbounded orbits escape exponentially.
