Belief adaptation controls the complete inverse geometry of a risk-sensitive two-state learning map: it is one-to-one exactly when \(a(1-ckp_1/(1-k))\le4\), with a sharp cubic critical inverse at equality. Above the fold threshold, the extended map has a precisely described three-preimage strip of width \(\frac13(B-4)^{3/2}+O((B-4)^{5/2})\), while strong adaptation restores smooth injectivity at every learning intensity.
