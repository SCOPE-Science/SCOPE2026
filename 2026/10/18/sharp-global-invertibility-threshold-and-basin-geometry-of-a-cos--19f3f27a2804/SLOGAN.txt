For the unit-circle normal-return map generated by the strictly convex outer curve \(R(\theta)=3+\varepsilon\cos(2\theta)\), global invertibility is lost at exactly \(\varepsilon=(\sqrt{97}-7)/8\), while the two thickness maxima remain attracting beyond this threshold. Up to the critical homeomorphism endpoint, the complete dynamics consists of two attracting and two repelling fixed points with explicit semicircular basins.
