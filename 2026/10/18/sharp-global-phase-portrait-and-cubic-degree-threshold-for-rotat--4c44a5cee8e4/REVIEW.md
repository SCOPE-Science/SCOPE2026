## Mathematical value — PASS
The motivating gap is a concrete global-stability question for planar rotation-equivariant embeddings: when a unique hyperbolic sink cannot be extrapolated to the full plane. The classification answers it for the **whole minimal nonlinear polynomial degree** rather than at an arbitrary parameter value. The strict determinant boundary identifies precisely which members are genuine global smooth diffeomorphisms, and the unique invariant-circle formula completely resolves their basins, escape and recurrent dynamics. The degree-three lower bound and uniform explicit realization of every prescribed finite rotation period give a sharp polynomial-complexity result. Existing general facts about invariant radii or local normal forms alone do not contain these global and optimality assertions. The finding does not claim a new general theory of arbitrary equivariant maps.
