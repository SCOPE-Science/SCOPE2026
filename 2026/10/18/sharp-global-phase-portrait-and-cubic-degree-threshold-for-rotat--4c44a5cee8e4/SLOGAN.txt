Every rotationally equivariant cubic polynomial smooth plane diffeomorphism with a hyperbolically attracting origin is either linear or has exactly one repelling invariant circle separating the origin's basin from escape, with a sharp algebraic criterion for invertibility. Cubic degree is optimal for realizing any prescribed least period \(q\ge2\) on that circle while retaining exactly \(SO(2)\) as the linear symmetry group and the origin as the unique fixed point.
