For any Cartesian product of \(m\ge2\) full-dimensional convex bodies, the normalized Godbersen mixed-volume coefficient has a sharp finite-dimensional upper bound equal to a multivariate hypergeometric collision probability, with equality precisely for simplex factors. When each factor occupies a positive fraction of dimension and the index is proportional to dimension, this optimal bound decays exactly like \(n^{-(m-1)/2}\), with constant \([4\pi\beta(1-\beta)]^{-(m-1)/2}/\sqrt{\prod_i\alpha_i}\).
