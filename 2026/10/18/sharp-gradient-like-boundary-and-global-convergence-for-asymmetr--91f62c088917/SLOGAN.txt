For the asymmetric two-dimensional coupled sine map, every real orbit converges to one of four or six fixed points throughout the closed region \(a>0\), \(b>0\), \(0\le c<1\), and \(a+(2-c)b\le2\), including its neutral-flip boundary. A hidden weighted-gradient potential rises strictly outside fixed points, and its global monotonicity fails sharply when this inequality is reversed.
