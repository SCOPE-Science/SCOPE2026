# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The normalization identity \(RA-B=h(h-1)^2a\) is exact. The all-residue monotonicity calculation has only two cases (no carry and carry) and covers both possible quotient ranges at and above the stated threshold. Every semigroup element in a fixed residue arises from a nonnegative residue coin index. This proves the full Apéry set, and the two maximal corners yield the complete pseudo-Frobenius set after an explicit incomparability test. The exact sign of their difference gives the Frobenius branches; summing normal forms gives genus. The boundary counterexample is an exact congruent, strictly smaller representation. Standalone Dijkstra verification checks 640 stable parameter pairs, eight boundary failures and independent invariants. Those tests support, rather than replace, the all-parameter proof.

**Originality — PASS within inspected evidence.** Liu–Xin–Ye–Yin arXiv:2306.03459 motivates a general base \(h\) but concentrates its stated theorems on binary base and does not give the sharp, all-\(h\) three-generator Apéry stabilization and type dichotomy. Liu–Xin arXiv:2303.07149 provides a general residue-minimization method and explicit results for other slope patterns; it does not state this threshold or this parameterwise symmetry criterion. Morales–Dung arXiv:2601.07467 supplies broader algorithms for related three-generator semigroups; the inspected portion gives no explicit theorem with these uniform formulas. Relevant existing work on three-term affine repunits uses the distinct third generator \(h^2a+(h+1)d\), so its statements do not imply the present \(h^2a+(h^2-h+1)\) formulas by simply matching parameters. Mathematical-record and historical-claim overlap checks did not identify the stated normal form or sharp onset. A hidden consequence of a broad algorithm or unindexed prior computation remains a concrete residual risk; no independent priority is claimed.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The generating family is the natural first length-three specialization of an explicitly raised general-base Frobenius direction. The uniform sharp cutoff, complete Apéry set and divisibility criterion for symmetry are structural, not a table or a single numeric slice, and give exact genus and Frobenius data throughout an unbounded two-parameter region. The boundary witness explains where the greedy carry argument first breaks.

The scope does not include values below the cutoff, other nonunit shifts or longer generator strings. The separate algorithmic literature may permit a less direct route to these formulas; this is a limitation on the originality assessment, not on the direct proof.
