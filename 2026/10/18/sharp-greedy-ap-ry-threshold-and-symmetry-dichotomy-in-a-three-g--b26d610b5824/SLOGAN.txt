For the infinite family \(\langle a,ha+1,h^2a+h^2-h+1\rangle\) with \(h\ge4\), the greedy Apéry normal form begins at the exact sharp uniform threshold \(a=(h-3)(h^2-h+1)+h+1\). Above this threshold the semigroup is symmetric exactly when \(h^2-h+1\mid a\), and its full pseudo-Frobenius set, Frobenius number and genus have explicit formulas.
