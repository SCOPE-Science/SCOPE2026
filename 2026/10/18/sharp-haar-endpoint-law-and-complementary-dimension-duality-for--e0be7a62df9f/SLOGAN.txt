For Haar-random \(k\)-dimensional shadows of the regular \(n\)-cross-polytope, the probability of being within relative volume \(\varepsilon\) of maximal is asymptotic to an explicit constant times \(\varepsilon^{k(n-k)/2}\), with a sharp second-order coefficient that vanishes whenever \(k=2\) or \(n-k=2\). The entire near-maximal probability law has an exact independent-beta-product representation and is identical in complementary dimensions for all sufficiently small \(\varepsilon\).
