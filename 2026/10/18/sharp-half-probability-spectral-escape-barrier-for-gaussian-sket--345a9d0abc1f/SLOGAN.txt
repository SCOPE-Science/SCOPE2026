For every fixed Gaussian sketch size, a one-dimensional sketched Ritz value of a symmetric matrix can lie outside the true eigenvalue interval with probability arbitrarily close to \(1/2\), even though the input approaches an exact eigenvector. The exact sharp fixed-Rayleigh-value risk is a two-Student-tail expression, attained by mixtures of the extreme eigenspaces; its balanced value decays exponentially in sketch dimension.
