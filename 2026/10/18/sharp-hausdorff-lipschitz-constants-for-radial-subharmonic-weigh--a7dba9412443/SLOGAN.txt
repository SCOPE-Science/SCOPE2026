For every nonnegative nondecreasing radial-subharmonic weight, the radius-\(R\) ball maximizes the outer-parallel weighted-perimeter increment among origin-containing convex bodies inside that ball; a positive Laplacian gives a strict geometric deficit. The resulting global Hausdorff Lipschitz constant is exactly \(n\omega_n((n-1)R^{n-2}\phi(R)+R^{n-1}\phi'(R))\), including the sharp power-weight coefficient \(n\omega_n(n-1+p)R^{n-2+p}\) for all \(p>0\).
