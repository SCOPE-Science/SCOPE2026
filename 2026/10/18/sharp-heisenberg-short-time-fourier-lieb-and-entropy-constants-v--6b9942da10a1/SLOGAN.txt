For the Heisenberg-group short-time Fourier transform, the sharp Lieb \(L^p\)-moment constant is \((2/p)^{2n+1}\) for every \(p>2\), although no nonzero pair attains it. An explicit anisotropic Gaussian family has an exact Gamma-integral deficit of order \(\epsilon^2\) and proves that the matching Shannon entropy bound \(2n+1\) is also sharp.
