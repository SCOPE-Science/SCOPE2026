With two types and independent continuous first-arrival clocks, the probability that the eventual winning collector was never behind is exactly \(\frac12+\operatorname{Var}(F_2(X))+\operatorname{Var}(F_1(Y))\), and its sharp attainable range is \([1/2,3/4]\). For exponential clocks, the maximum instead equals \(2/3\), attained only by equal type rates.
