The real Lipschitz-free space of a weighted \(m\)-page book has squared Euclidean Banach–Mazur distance \(1+m(2-t)/(2+t)\) below the sharp hub-edge threshold \(t=2/(m+\sqrt{m^2-m+1})\), and \(m+t/(4-t)\) above it. Its **unique most Hilbertian metric** occurs at that threshold, with the optimal hub edge asymptotic to \(1/m\).
