In the formal context whose objects are all cuts of \(K_n\) and whose attributes are edges crossed by those cuts, repeated Horn inference needs premises of size only \(3\), while a single-pass direct basis needs the sharp width \(2\lfloor(n-2)/2\rfloor+1\). Odd paths give a ternary complete basis, and long odd cycles with an external vertex force the growing direct lower bound.
