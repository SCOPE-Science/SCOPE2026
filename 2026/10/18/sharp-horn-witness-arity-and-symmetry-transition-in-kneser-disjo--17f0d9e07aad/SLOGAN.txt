In the uniform Kneser disjointness context, the sharp number of premises needed to witness any Horn consequence is \(n-2k+2\) for \(k\ge2\). Its concept-lattice automorphism group jumps from \(\mathfrak S_n\) above the matching threshold to the much larger \(\mathfrak S_{\binom{2k}k}\) precisely at \(n=2k\).
