For every product \(\Delta_{m-1}\times\Delta_{n-1}\), the sharp vertex maximum over all affine hyperplane sections is \(\sum_{i=1}^{\min(m,n)}(\lfloor(m+n+1)/2\rfloor-i)(\lceil(m+n+1)/2\rceil-i)\). Exactly the positive cells of a universal antidiagonal weight pattern must occur in a maximizing generic section, with only the zero-weight cells free; in particular, square products attain \(q(q^2-1)/3\) section vertices, asymptotically just two-thirds of the unrestricted skeleton maximum cut.
