Frobenius-nearest positive-semidefinite projection of real symmetric \(2\times2\) matrices has sharp global Lipschitz constants \(3/2\), \(3/(2\sqrt2)\), and \(3/2\) in the independent-entry \(\ell_1\), \(\ell_2\), and \(\ell_\infty\) norms. The \(\ell_1\) bound is attained by an explicit rank-one-ray pair, while the other two constants arise as sharp boundary-derivative limits.
