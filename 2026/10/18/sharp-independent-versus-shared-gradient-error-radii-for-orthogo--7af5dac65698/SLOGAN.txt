For two orthogonal planar objective gradients of lengths \(a,b\), the minimum-norm convex-combination direction remains a true common descent under arbitrary per-objective errors of norm less than \(\min(a,b)/2\), and this radius is sharp. If both gradients instead share exactly the same error vector, the sharp radius increases to \(ab/\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\), making error coupling—not merely marginal precision—decisive for robust Pareto descent.
