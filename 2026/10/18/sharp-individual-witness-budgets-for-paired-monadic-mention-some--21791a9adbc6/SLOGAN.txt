Two opposed monadic mention-some questions admit exactly the ordered support pairs encoded by nonempty Boolean-lattice convex sets; their least common-domain witness cost is the number of distinct minimal and maximal boundary sets. The maximum cost on \(n\) worlds is the sum of the two largest binomial levels, and on four worlds the \(3937\) pairs split by least cost \(1,\ldots,10\) as \(16,120,450,901,1110,849,366,103,20,2\).
