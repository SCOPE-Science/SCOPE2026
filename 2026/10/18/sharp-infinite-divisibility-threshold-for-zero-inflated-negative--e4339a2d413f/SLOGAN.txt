For a zero-inflated negative-binomial count of positive integer shape \(m\), infinite divisibility holds exactly when \((1-q)((1-w)/w)^{1/m}\ge 2\cos(\pi/m)\). Shapes one and two remain infinitely divisible at every interior inflation weight, while every larger integer shape has a sharp phase boundary and explicit compound-Poisson jump intensities on the admissible side.
