For every nondegenerate tetrahedron \(\Delta\) and \(s>0\), the largest tetrahedron in \(\Delta+s(-\Delta)\) has volume exactly \((1+s)(1+s^2)\operatorname{Vol}_3(\Delta)\), with precisely six vertex-supported maximizers indexed by directed four-cycles. Its sharp occupancy ratio \((1+s^2)/(1+8s+s^2)\) is uniquely minimized at the symmetric cuboctahedral case \(s=1\), whose isolated value is already known.
