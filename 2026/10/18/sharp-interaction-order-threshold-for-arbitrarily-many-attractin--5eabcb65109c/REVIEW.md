# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The scalar vector field factors into prevalence times a degree-\(M\) polynomial, and alternating inward crossings bound the number of attracting interior roots by \(\lceil M/2\rceil\) even for nonhyperbolic equilibria. The constructive quotient \(Q(y)=[1-R(y)/R(1)]/(1-y)\) is a polynomial; the elementary-symmetric coefficient bound \(\sum_{s=1}^M e_s<1\) proves **every** prescribed infection rate is strictly positive whenever the positive ordered roots have sum at most \(1/2\). The exactly factored field gives the complete list of hyperbolic equilibria and stability signs. The standard-library exact-rational certificate independently replayed all coefficients and slope signs for \(M=1,\ldots,18\), including the explicit six-body case. The proof, not the finite test, covers all positive integers.

## Originality

PASS within the accessible primary literature and claim-comparison records. The 2023 founding study states the arbitrary-positive-endemic-state possibility as a direction and only solves bifurcations up to four-body interactions; the 2024/2025 follow-up classifies low orders and a two-population extension. The 2019 simplicial contagion and 2021/2023 community-hypergraph works have different, weaker, or nonhomogeneous conclusions. The 2026 infection–activity feedback study proves a polynomial-degree bound in a distinct coupled-state system and displays three endemic attractors but does not construct prescribed roots subject to **positive infection coefficients at every simplicial order** in the single-population SIS flow. An exact-title, equation, arbitrary-order, alias, positive-parameter, and implication-oriented comparison found no established source asserting the sharp formula and inverse realization together. This is not a claim of exhaustive universal literature coverage; the remaining risk is an unindexed theorem or a more general inverse-design result that specializes to this model.

## Value

PASS. The motivating 2023 article explicitly asks whether raising simplicial order produces arbitrarily many distinct endemic levels. Existence of many roots in an unconstrained polynomial is insufficient: biologically admissible coefficients must all be strictly positive. The proof supplies a constructive admissible inverse realization, an exact sharp maximum of stable endemic levels at fixed maximum interaction order, and a minimal six-body threshold for three coexisting endemic attractors. The construction holds on the homogeneous complete structure and on an open positive-rate parameter set, distinguishing a structural mechanism from community-induced multistability and from nongeneric zero-rate examples.

## Closest literature and limitations

The closest direct source is Kiss et al. (2023), arXiv:2309.12752v1, especially manuscript pp.9–14; the more recent Kiss–Bick–Simon paper (arXiv:2410.15330v1; journal 2025), §§1–4, is a crucial stronger-comparison source but treats lower orders and two populations. Ferraz de Arruda et al., arXiv:2112.04273v1, treats a different community-dependent mechanism; Alexandersen et al., arXiv:2608.26528v1, adds an activity state. The result concerns only deterministic homogeneous scalar SIS equilibria, not exact finite-population stochastic metastability or the unproved general hydrodynamic limit.
