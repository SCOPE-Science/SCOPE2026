On weighted harmonic Bergman spaces, the coordinate Toeplitz resolvent grows inside the unit circle at the sharp rate \(2^{-(\alpha+2)}(1-r)^{-(\alpha+2)}\), but outside it has exactly the normal-operator rate \((r-1)^{-1}\). An exact rank-one analytic-to-anti-analytic block causes this asymmetry and yields the inner pseudospectral penetration \(\tfrac12\varepsilon^{1/(\alpha+2)}\).
