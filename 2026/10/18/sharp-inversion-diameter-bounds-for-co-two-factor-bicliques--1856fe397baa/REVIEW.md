# Scientific review: inversion diameters of co-two-factor bicliques

**Correctness — PASS.** The inversion difference vector on a bipartite graph factors into binary coordinatewise products exactly when that many vertex-subset inversions suffice; both implications are written in the proof. For every deleted spanning 2-factor, the non-neighbour-pair multigraph on the left vertices is loopless 2-regular, so a proper three-colouring gives pairwise independent nonzero two-bit columns of a rank-two parity check. The kernel generator matrix has invertible restriction to each present-neighbour set; the proof verifies invertibility by the absence of a nonzero kernel word supported on the two excluded rows. Thus every specified column of edge differences is solved by one square binary system. For Hamilton-cycle deletion, the explicit fixed edge differences force a unit lower-triangular \((n-2)\)-minor of every completion, certifying a matching lower bound. Both proofs hold for all integers \(n\ge3\), regardless of connectedness of the remaining graph. The included finite BFS, factorization and rank checks passed; they are supporting checks, not a substitute for proof.

**Originality — PASS, subject to residual bibliographic risk.** The closest mathematical literature is Havet–Hörsch–Rambaud (2024), establishing the inversion-to-binary-rank correspondence and complete multipartite graph values; Bo–Li–Lian–Yan (2026), proving general edge-count/bipartite degree upper bounds and exact diameters for *unmodified* complete bipartite graphs; and Arana et al. (2026), providing broader homomorphism and degree bounds. Those checked statements do not imply the sharp \(n-2\) bound on every co-two-factor (a degree-sensitive but strictly larger general bound does not establish the sharp one), and complete-biclique exact results do not cover cycle deletions. Targeted published mathematical-record and public mathematical-record searches for inversion diameter, cyclic deletions, co-2-factor completions, low-rank completion and aliases did not find a directly covering theorem. Similar results in unindexed work or implicit in differently named matrix-completion research remain possible; absence from search results is not uniqueness proof.

**Value — PASS.** The theorem certifies a sharp degree bound for a structured family with arbitrarily large maximum degree, together with exact values on the natural Hamilton-cycle-deletion subfamily. The two-check parity-code method constructs an optimal-length-or-better sequence for *every* orientation difference in polynomial time. This addresses a motivated sharpness gap left by general inversion-diameter degree estimates rather than a routine numerical example.

**Closest literature and limitations.** [1] arXiv:2405.04119, especially the algebraic characterization and complete multipartite discussion; [2] arXiv:2606.17974v1, especially the bipartite upper bound and exact complete-biclique discussion; [3] arXiv:2602.24171v1, general degree bounds. The theorem does not claim exact diameters for non-Hamiltonian deleted 2-factors; it does not settle arbitrary-graph degree conjectures. Independent scientific validation and full bibliographic priority determination remain outstanding.
