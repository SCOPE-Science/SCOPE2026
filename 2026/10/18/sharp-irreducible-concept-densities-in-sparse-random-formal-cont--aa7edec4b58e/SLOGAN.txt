For a sparse Bernoulli formal context with \(n\) objects, \(\lfloor\alpha n\rfloor\) attributes and incidence probability \(c/n\), both counts of join- and meet-irreducible concepts have explicit deterministic densities with \(L^1\) error \(O(n^{-1/2})\). A universal four-cycle error bound reduces these lattice invariants to degree-local statistics, and duplicate singleton rows cause a strictly positive gap between individually indispensable input rows and the irreducible object representation.
