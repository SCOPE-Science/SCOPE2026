## Correctness: PASS

The unique maximal-volume inscribed ellipsoid inherits the two reflection symmetries and exchange symmetry of the hull; this forces the center to the origin and semiaxes to \((a,b,b)\) without restricting the set of competitors. The exact support function of each generating disk gives a scalar minimax problem, with the transition at \(s=t^2+2t\). Both necessary and sufficient support constraints are derived, not merely numerically checked. Their two-parameter determinant maximization has a unique optimum and a threshold at \(t=1\). The derivative of the resulting volume gives its unique minimum at \(t=2\). The delivered finite checker reproduced exact identities and sampled support tests, and no infinite proof depends on the samples.

## Value: PASS

This is a natural complete extremal-ellipsoid problem on a geometrically motivated continuum of convex bodies, not a substitution into a known tabulation. The reduction is exact for arbitrary ellipsoids, the active contact regime bifurcates at \(t=1\), and the John volume has a surprising unique global minimum at the disk-contact separation \(t=2\). Both geometric thresholds and the complete formula are usable benchmarks for semialgebraic convex-body ellipsoid optimization. Its value does not depend on a claimed theorem about general disk configurations.
