For the \(a\times b\) partial-permutation polytope with \(2\le a\le b\), the product-of-row-simplices John ellipsoid survives all genuine column-cap constraints exactly when \(b\ge a+\lceil\sqrt a\rceil-1\). This yields its exact ellipsoid and volume in the whole survival regime, with a sharp column-facet contact transition; below the threshold every column-cap facet necessarily touches the different true John ellipsoid.
