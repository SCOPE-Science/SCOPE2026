For two balanced binary conditional expectations, the commutator has the exact all-exponent sharp norm \(2s((1+s)/2)^{1-\theta_p}((1-s)/2)^{\theta_p}\), with \(s\) the absolute event correlation. As the events become identical, every interior \(L^p\) commutator vanishes at a sharp power rate while both endpoint norms approach the maximal value \(2\); the optimal correlation at every interior exponent is explicit.
