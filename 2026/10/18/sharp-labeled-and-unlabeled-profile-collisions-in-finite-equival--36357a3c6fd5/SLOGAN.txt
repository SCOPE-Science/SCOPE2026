The complete counts of parameter-free quantifier-free definable relations in a finite equivalence structure are a Stirling transform of its labeled induced-substructure profile, but they do not reconstruct the structure. Among nondegenerate equivalence relations, the first complete all-arity collision is the unique five-point pair with class sizes \((3,2)\) and \((2,1,1,1)\); the corresponding unlabeled age profile already has a collision on four points.
