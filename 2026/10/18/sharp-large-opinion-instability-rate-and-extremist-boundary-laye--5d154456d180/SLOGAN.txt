In the incremental-voter mean-field model, a symmetry-breaking perturbation grows at the exact rate \(\lambda_k=k^{-1}-2^{-1/2}k^{-3/2}+o(k^{-3/2})\) as the number of available opinion states grows. Its unstable eigenvector is concentrated near the two extreme opinions in boundary layers of width \(\sqrt{k}\), with universal profile \(e^{-\sqrt2 x}\).
