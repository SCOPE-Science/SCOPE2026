The integer-lattice covering radius of the planar simplex-reflection mixture \(S_\lambda=(1-\lambda)T+\lambda(-T)\) is exactly \(2/(1+\min\{\lambda,1-\lambda\})\), and its optimal-covering density decreases sharply to \(4/3\) at the symmetric difference body. Exactly there, the deepest-hole locus changes from one periodic point to two.
