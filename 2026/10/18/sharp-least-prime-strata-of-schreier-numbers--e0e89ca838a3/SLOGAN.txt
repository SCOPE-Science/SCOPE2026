For every fixed least prime divisor \(p\), Schreier numbers have an explicit sharp asymptotic count of order \(X(\log\log X)^{\lfloor\log_2(p+1)\rfloor-1}/\log X\), with a leading coefficient that detects whether a \(p^2\)-layer is also allowed. In particular, Schreier numbers with least prime \(2\) number asymptotically \(3X/(4\log X)\), while the least-prime-\(3\) stratum grows as \(X\log\log X/(3\log X)\).
