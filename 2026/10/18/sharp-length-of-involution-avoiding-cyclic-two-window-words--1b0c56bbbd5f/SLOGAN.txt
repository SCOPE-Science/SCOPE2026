For any alphabet involution with \(f\) fixed symbols and \(t\ge1\) swapped pairs, the longest cyclic word using distinct two-windows and omitting self-invariant windows and never using both windows in any involution pair has length \(\frac{(2t+f)^2-f^2}{2}\), except that exactly one window-orbit must be omitted when \(f=0\) and \(t\) is odd. A parity obstruction proves the deficiency, and explicit Eulerian edge selections attain every bound.
