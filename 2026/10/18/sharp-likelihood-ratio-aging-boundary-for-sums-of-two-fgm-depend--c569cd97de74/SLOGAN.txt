For two common-rate exponential lifetimes coupled by an FGM survival copula, the sum is ILR (its density is log-concave) exactly for \(-1\le\theta\le\theta_c\), where the sharp positive threshold has an explicit one-dimensional variational formula and \(\theta_c\approx0.8602582731\). The model remains IFR throughout \([-1,1]\), so likelihood-ratio aging fails under sufficiently strong positive dependence despite preserved increasing hazard rate.
