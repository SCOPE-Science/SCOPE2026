# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

All quantifiers, distinctness hypotheses, group type, and characteristic are fixed. A common maximal torus of the split even orthogonal group has distinct one-dimensional standard weights forming hyperbolic pairs. The matching-graph necessity follows. Conversely, each nonorthogonal edge gives a nondegenerate hyperbolic plane, pairwise orthogonal across edges; isolated independent isotropic vectors extend through Witt's theorem to a hyperbolic basis of the remaining split complement. A graph vertex of degree at least two gives a triple obstruction; otherwise any dependency among isolated vectors has an inclusion-minimal circuit of cardinality at most \(m+1\), because independent totally isotropic sets have size at most \(m\). The \(m+1\)-point circuit in a maximal isotropic space is sharp. The projective-space statement follows directly from the compatibility-and-reconstruction proposition for toric ray parabolics and the standard fan of \(\mathbb P^d\). An exact rational checker was executed, but the universal conclusion is justified by the proof, not by finite trials.

The originating 2026 paper leaves the full type-\(D_m\) Helly number between \(m+1\) and \(m+2\) and bounds line-parabolic Levi-type families by \(m+2\) using its general Proposition 5.9. It already supplies the overall \(m+1\) lower bound through \(\mathrm{GL}_m\), so neither that lower bound nor the generic apartment definition is claimed anew. The new restricted line-parabolic upper bound, classification of *all* minimal incompatibility mechanisms, and the exact restricted toric splitting threshold are not stated there. Pankov's apartment characterization treats other geometric representation and extension questions. Older Cooperstein–Kasikova–Shult and Cooperstein partial-frame results plausibly cover elements of frame extension, and their uninspected full texts leave an explicit overlap risk, but accessible abstracts did not establish the claimed restricted Helly and toric threshold. Searches of the scientific literature and relevant indexed mathematical claims did not reveal a dominating statement; search absence alone was not treated as proof of novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result sharpens the source's general \(m+2\) local-to-global bound by one in a natural maximal-parabolic orbit, explains every possible obstruction by an elementary polar-incidence/circuit dichotomy, and identifies exactly the dimensions of projective spaces where a geometrically specified class of toric principal bundles is forced to split. These are natural structural and geometric questions directly motivated by the unresolved type-\(D_m\) boundary and the new bundle splitting criterion, rather than arbitrary parameter evaluations. It does not claim to settle the unrestricted type-\(D_m\) case or to supersede prior general polar-frame theory.
