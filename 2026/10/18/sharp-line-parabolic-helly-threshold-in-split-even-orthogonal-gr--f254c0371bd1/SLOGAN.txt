Isotropic-point parabolics in split \(\mathrm{SO}_{2m}\) share a maximal torus exactly when their nonorthogonality graph is a matching and its isolated lines are independent; every minimal obstruction has at most \(m+1\) points, and this bound is sharp. Consequently, toric principal \(\mathrm{SO}_{2m}\)-bundles on \(\mathbb P^d\) with only isotropic-line ray parabolics always split equivariantly exactly for \(d\ge m+1\) (within \(d\ge2\)).
