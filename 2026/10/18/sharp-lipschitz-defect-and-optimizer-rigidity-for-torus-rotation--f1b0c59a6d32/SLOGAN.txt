For a translation on \(C(\mathbb T^d)\), the smallest torsion-intertwining defect under a Lipschitz budget \(L\) is exactly the nearest-character distance \(\min_{\|k\|_2\le L}|e^{i\langle k,\beta\rangle}-\lambda|\); when the translation is minimal and the defect is below \(2\), all optimizers are unimodular multiples of characters. In particular, irrational circle rotations admit arbitrarily accurate but no exact continuous torsion anticommuters, with a sharp finite-budget Diophantine profile.
