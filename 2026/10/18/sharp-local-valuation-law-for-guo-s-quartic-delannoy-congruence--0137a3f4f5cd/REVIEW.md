# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The proposed identity is proved for every odd prime and every nonzero integer in the two exceptional residue neighborhoods. The coefficientwise \(p\)-divisibility follows from \(D_{p-1-k}\equiv D_k\pmod p\) and pairing. Three exact alternating polynomial sums provide \(h_1=4p^3\), \(p^3\mid h_2,h_3\), while all remaining coefficients have at least one \(p\). For every \(h\ge1\), all higher-degree terms have valuation at least \(h+4\), strictly above the leading \(h+3\). The involution \(x\mapsto-1-x\) gives the second branch. The explicit exceptional values at \(p=3,5\) and the cubic sum's denominator nine were checked separately. Exact verification code reproduces the polynomial certificates and finite cases.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

Guo (arXiv:1412.7724v1) proves only the universal \(p^2\) congruence and conjectures the universal \(p^3\) one. Neither provides the sharp local formula \(3+\nu_p(x(x+1))\) or the nonzero leading coefficient \(4p^3x\). The 2017 coefficient-divisibility results for Schmidt polynomials concern divisibility by the length, not this exact defect; the 2025 generalized/linear Delannoy sums have different weights and claims. Accessible primary literature and public research records did not identify a statement implying this valuation law, but unindexed or uninspected source coverage cannot be excluded. This is a best-knowledge priority assessment, not proof of novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The natural singular residues \(x=0,-1\) are distinguished by \(x(x+1)\), which appears directly in Guo's conjectural comparison sum. The theorem gives a **sharp complete depth law** for every odd prime and every positive prime-adic distance to either residue, rather than an arbitrarily fixed small prime, a finite table, or a routine single substitution. It demonstrates a systematic extra congruence order, its exact failure at the next order and an explicit local coefficient, while acknowledging that Guo's full conjecture remains unresolved.
