For \(X\sim\operatorname{Bin}(n,p)\) with \(0<p<1\), the quantized law \(\lfloor2X/3\rfloor\) is log-concave only for precisely determined probability intervals when \(n\le5\); for every \(n\ge6\), it fails log-concavity regardless of the success probability. The complete sharp classification includes algebraic boundaries involving \(\sqrt{33}\) and \(\sqrt{41}\).
