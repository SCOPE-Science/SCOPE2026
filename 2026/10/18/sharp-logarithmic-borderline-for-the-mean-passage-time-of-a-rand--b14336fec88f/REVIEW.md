# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The reflection-principle identity gives the exact absorbed-walk survival at operational time and bounds survival from every fixed starting site by a multiple of the one-site survival. Monotone physical survival reduces mean finiteness to sampled survival, whose positive spectral kernel sums to an exact resolvent. Local Fourier analysis gives the integrability criterion; the tail Laplace identity and Karamata/Potter bounds give the critical logarithmic threshold. The explicit example law is monotone and realizable. The companion finite consistency checks passed, but finite tests are not offered as proof of the infinite-time claim.

Independent priority verification has not been performed.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result resolves a sharp marginal question raised by the existing pure-power transition. It provides a universal exact integral test and distinguishes finite from infinite mean at the same heavy-tail power index; the logarithmic threshold \(\kappa=1\) is an actual distributional separation. No uniformity for changing starting distance, changing redraw periods, correlated clocks or biased walks is claimed.
