For a symmetric walk whose total jump rate is independently refreshed at fixed intervals, the mean time to hit zero is finite exactly when \(\int_0^1 ds/[\sqrt{s}(1-\mathbb Ee^{-sR})]\) converges, regardless of starting distance or refresh period. At a critical rate tail \(\Pr(R>u)\sim c u^{-1/2}(\log u)^\kappa\), finiteness holds precisely for \(\kappa>1\): a logarithmic factor overturns the infinite-mean verdict of the pure-power borderline.
