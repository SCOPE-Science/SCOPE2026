For the complement of every \((0,x,1,k+1)\)-hiccup sequence with \(k\ge2\) and \(x\ge3\), an exact stage-by-stage extremal formula gives the sharp logarithmic discrepancy slopes \(x-k-1\) and \(x-2\). The missing integers have density \((k-1)/k\), yet their deterministic fluctuations attain both distinct logarithmic envelopes.
