In the Brownian search-fed \(G/M/c\) queue, the critical Poisson resetting rate has the sharp high-server law \(r_{\mathrm{eq}}\sim(c^2\mu^2L^2/D)[\log(c\mu L^2/D)]^2\). An exact one-crossing argument shows why this squared logarithm—not a fixed power larger than two—governs stabilization as server count increases.
