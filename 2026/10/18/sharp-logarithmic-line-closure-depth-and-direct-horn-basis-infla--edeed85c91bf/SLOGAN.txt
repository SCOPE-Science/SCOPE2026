In \(\operatorname{PG}(d-1,q)\), the canonical Horn basis has exactly \(\binom{(q^d-1)/(q-1)}{2}\) pair premises and reaches closure in a sharp worst-case \(\lceil\log_2 d\rceil\) synchronous rounds. The smallest one-pass basis instead has \(\sum_{r=2}^d\frac1{r!}\prod_{j=0}^{r-1}\bigl((q^d-q^j)/(q-1)\bigr)\) grouped rules, exhibiting a superpolynomial size-versus-depth separation.
