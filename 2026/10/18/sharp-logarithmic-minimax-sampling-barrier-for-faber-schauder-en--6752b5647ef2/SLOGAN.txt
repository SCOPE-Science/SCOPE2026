In the Faber–Schauder energy space \(\mathcal E^p([0,1])\), with \(1<p<\infty\), even fully adaptive deterministic point sampling has sharp worst-case uniform recovery error \(\mathfrak R_n(p)\sim\tfrac12(\log_2n)^{-1/p}\). The ordinary dyadic piecewise-linear interpolant reaches the same exact leading constant; one empty dyadic cell defeats every competing sampling strategy.
