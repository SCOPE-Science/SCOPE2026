For the canonical right shift on every first Schreier space \(S_p\), \(1\le p<\infty\), the sharp power-growth law is \(\|R^n\|^p=\log_2 n+O(1)\), with matching explicit finite-power harmonic and dyadic bounds. Every finitely supported vector has an eventually constant orbit norm, although a residual set of vectors has unbounded orbits.
