Every nonempty equality–constancy type of \(\mathsf{FOT}^{-}\) with \(r\) distinct nonconstant columns on an \(m\)-element domain has an exactly smallest fresh realization of \(\lceil\log_m(m+r)\rceil\) rows for \(r>0\) (one row if \(r=0\)). A realization drawn from the original team always uses at most \(r+1\) rows, and both budgets are sharp.
