For unit-norm monomial sensors on a weighted Bergman space, every frame assembled from forward multiplication-shift orbits has asymptotic condition ratio \(\kappa_\infty>1\) and requires at least \((\alpha+1)/\log\kappa_\infty\) generators per unit of logarithmic degree. Geometrically spaced sensors attain this sharp frontier for every \(\kappa_\infty>1\); on ordinary Bergman space, degrees \(2^j-1\) give exact global frame bounds \(1\) and \(2\).
