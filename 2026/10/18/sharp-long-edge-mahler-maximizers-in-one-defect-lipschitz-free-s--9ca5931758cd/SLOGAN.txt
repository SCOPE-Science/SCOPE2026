Lengthening just one distance in a \((d+1)\)-point equilateral metric produces an exactly solvable Mahler-product deformation: every \(d\ge3\) has a unique strict interior optimum, unlike \(d=2\). The optimal exceptional distance converges to \(3/2\), while the relative Mahler gain is asymptotic to \(2/[d(d+1)]\).
