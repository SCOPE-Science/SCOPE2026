# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The absolute-value reduction is exact for every branch. Three consecutive applications of the increasing outer map to its largest possible starting input have value \(\rho<1\), which forbids four consecutive outer visits. All four-step products are thus at least \(ts^3\). The displayed absolute four-cycle is obtained by solving the affine itinerary fixed point and has strict branch inequalities. A single inner visit flips the sign, giving a genuine primitive signed period-eight cycle. For invariant measures, the four-window inequality gives inner frequency at least \(1/4\); equality forces four-periodic symbolic itineraries. Affine expansion \(ts^3>1\) then leaves only the displayed orbit and its uniform measure. The exact rational script checks nonstandard arithmetic and representative branch boundaries; the proof, not the finite test, establishes the infinite quantifiers.

## Originality — PASS, qualified by source-access risk

The 2023 open article was inspected in its model, theorems, Lyapunov-exponent discussion, figures and conclusions. It supplies the exact map and its chosen parameters, not the sharp all-invariant-measures extremum or period-eight minimizing orbit. The originating 2021 work's full text remained unavailable; its accessible abstract describes absence of fixed points, positive Lyapunov examples and Devaney chaos. Its possible additional results, and unpublished or unindexed material, remain priority risks. General eventual-expansion and ergodic-optimization results concern other maps or abstract sufficient principles, not a directly located computation of this model's unique minimizing measure. Dedicated searches for symbolic conjugacies, source parameters, invariant measures, periodic minimizers, and the specific multiplier returned no covering assertion.

## Value — PASS

The motivating papers study a family of chaotic maps with locally contracting branches, and use numerical Lyapunov plots to evaluate its dynamics. The theorem gives a rigorously positive Lyapunov floor for *every* boundary-avoiding orbit in a nontrivial open region, proves its optimal constant, and completely classifies equality using a unique primitive period-eight orbit. This supplies a natural sharp invariant-measure extremum and tests the extent of dynamical expansion more precisely than numerical typical-orbit estimates. It does not assert cryptographic security or a complete parameter phase diagram.
