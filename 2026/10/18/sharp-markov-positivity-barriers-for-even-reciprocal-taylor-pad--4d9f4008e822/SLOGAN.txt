For every even \(m\ge4\), the reciprocal-Taylor \([0/m]\) Padé exponential step preserves Markov-state positivity at sufficiently small steps for all generators with at most \(m+2\) states, and a pure-birth generator with \(m+3\) states proves that this boundary is sharp. In the first fourth-order obstruction, a seven-state pure-birth chain has the exact positive-step preservation set \([\tau,\infty)\), where \(\tau\approx0.326062780129\) is the unique positive root of an explicit degree-18 polynomial: shrinking a timestep below the threshold destroys positivity.
