The largest simplex in every Euclidean unit-ball cap of height \(0<h\le1+1/n\) has one apex vertex and a centered regular \((n-1)\)-simplex on the cap's base, with its exact volume obtained from a sharp encompassing ellipsoid. The optimum saturates at the full-ball regular-simplex volume exactly when \(h=1+1/n\); all equality cases in every dimension \(n\ge2\) are classified.
