Disjoint even-parity codeword polytopes have identical vertex counts and identical Löwner balls at fixed dimension and number of checks, yet their Euclidean mean width is uniquely maximized by balanced check lengths and minimized by the most unequal admissible lengths. Their directional-width loss also has distinct odd/even parity fluctuation laws and an absolute mean-width visibility threshold at \(k\asymp N^{3/4}\).
