For the Hilbert-cube shift with metric \(d(x,y)=\sum_{j\in\mathbb Z}2^{-|j|}|x_j-y_j|\), every stationary full-support hard-core Markov process with a mandatory gap of \(m\ge1\) zeros between ones has the sharp measure-expansivity radius \(\min\{3/2,1+2/(2^{m+1}-1)\}\), independently of its nondegenerate transition probability. The critical closed Bowen ball of a constant ambient center has full measure, while every smaller radius has measure-zero Bowen balls around every center.
