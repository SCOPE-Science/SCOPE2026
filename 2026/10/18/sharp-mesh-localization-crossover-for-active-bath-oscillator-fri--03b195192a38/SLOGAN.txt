For an active-particle bath coupled to a cyclic oscillator chain, the weak-coupling friction's modewise sign threshold crosses from \(p^{1/4}\sqrt n\) to \(\sqrt p\) when the number of sites becomes comparable to the square root of the von Mises localization parameter. The crossover has an exact theta-series profile and exposes a singular point-interaction limit of the reduced-friction coefficients, without asserting that the full microscopic dynamics obeys that nonuniform limit.
