For symmetric positive-semidefinite control spectra contained in \([\mu,L]\), the unique scalar feedback shift minimizing the worst-case Euclidean reduced-Hessian condition number is \(\delta_* = \alpha^{-1/2}\cot((\arctan(\sqrt\alpha\,\mu)+\arctan(\sqrt\alpha\,L))/2)\), with sharp factor \(\sec^2((\arctan(\sqrt\alpha\,L)-\arctan(\sqrt\alpha\,\mu))/2)<2\). A three-eigenvalue matrix attains the bound, whereas matrices supported only on the two interval endpoints become perfectly conditioned at that shift.
