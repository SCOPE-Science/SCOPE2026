In the mean-field incremental voter model, every nontrivial trajectory approaching extreme consensus loses its penultimate opinion with the sharp rate \(e^{-(1-1/(2k))t}\), while all more distant opinions obey an explicit polynomial-times-\(e^{-t}\) cascade when \(k\geq2\). The three-opinion case is resonant: the far-opposite opinion instead decays as \((C^2/2)t e^{-t}\), with \(C\) the positive penultimate-opinion amplitude.
