For complete powerset information states equipped only with an empty-state and singleton-state marker, binary-union formulas of depth \(d\) distinguish cardinalities exactly up to \(2^d\); every larger finite or infinite cardinality has the same depth-\(d\) theory. There are exactly \(2^d+2\) pointed theories, and the least depth distinguishing sizes \(1\le n<m\) is \(\lceil\log_2 n\rceil\).
