For Gaussian generalized Nyström identity-matrix inputs, the squared leave-right-out diagnostic has an exact chi-square-ratio law: its mean is infinite without left oversampling, while its variance becomes finite only with at least three extra left-sketch columns. In the fully square case, the leave-pair-out and leave-twins-out squared diagnostics are still heavier-tailed, with finite moments exactly below order \(1/2\), despite exact full-sketch reconstruction.
