For strongly negative Dirichlet weights, the smallest possible optimal-approximant zero modulus has the sharp asymptotic \(2(2/3)^{s/2}\) with three explicit, strictly ordered exponential corrections as \(s\to\infty\). When only first-degree optimal approximants are considered, limiting the generator to degree two has a sharp additional gap of scale \((20/27)^s\) relative to the unrestricted radius; the extremal weighted coefficients concentrate on their first two entries.
