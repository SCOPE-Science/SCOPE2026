For two-probe Gaussian XTrace on the nearly rank-one spectrum \(\operatorname{diag}(1,\varepsilon,\ldots,\varepsilon)\), the sharp trace-estimation variance is \(c_n\varepsilon\), while the published residual-normalization variant has exactly \((n-2)/(n+1)\) of that leading variance. Rare almost-orthogonal sketches, not typical samples, determine both constants; in dimension two normalization makes the estimate identically exact.
