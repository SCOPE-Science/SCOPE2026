For every real cube \(\ell_\infty^m\) between half and all of the \(2^{n-1}\) projective sign patterns, the sharp unrestricted \(n\)-term von Neumann–Jordan constant is an explicit square-root function of \(n\) and \(m\). A sharp missing-sign-pair construction also gives the exact optimum for every prescribed collection of input lengths, including \(2+\sqrt{19}/4\) for four inputs in the five-dimensional cube.
