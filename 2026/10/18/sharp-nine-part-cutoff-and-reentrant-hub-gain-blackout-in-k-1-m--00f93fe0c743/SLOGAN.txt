For variance-optimal continuous feedback on \(K_{1,m,m}\), the universal hub is always pinned when \(2\le m\le8\), but for every \(m\ge9\) it is uniquely optimal to leave the hub unpinned on one closed, nonempty intermediate budget interval. The two switching budgets scale respectively as \((4/3)m\) and \(2m^2\), even though the globally optimal allocation is unique for every budget.
