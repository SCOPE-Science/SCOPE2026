For logistic threshold networks, the product of feedback strength and the Dobrushin row-overlap coefficient has an exact, sharp consensus threshold of \(4\): below it differences decay exponentially, and at equality the sharp asymptotic envelope is \(\sqrt{3/(2t)}\). The nonlinear bound holds under arbitrary common time-dependent forcing, is saturated at every time by a symmetric two-node example with constant forcing, and has a persistent unequal-activity counterexample above the threshold.
