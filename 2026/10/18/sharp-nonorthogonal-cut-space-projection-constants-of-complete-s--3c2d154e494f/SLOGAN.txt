For the cut subspace of the real edge \(\ell_1\) space of \(K_m\vee\overline K_n\), all \(m,n\ge2\), the minimum projection norm is an explicit rational function attained by a one-parameter symmetry-invariant correction of the orthogonal projector. Orthogonal projection is optimal only for \(m=2\); for every \(m\ge3\) it is strictly worse, with the gap approaching \(3/14\) along \(m=n\to\infty\).
