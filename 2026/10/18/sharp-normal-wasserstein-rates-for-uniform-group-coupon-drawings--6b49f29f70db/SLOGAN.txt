For independent uniformly drawn coupon packets, the finite-horizon number of unseen coupons is always Poisson–binomial, even for varying deterministic packet sizes. Consequently, in the logarithmic sampling regime \(0<\alpha<1\), the standardized unseen count has the sharp normal Wasserstein-one error \(\Theta(n^{-(1-\alpha)/2})\), resolving the lower-\(\alpha\) rate question of Betken–Thäle.
