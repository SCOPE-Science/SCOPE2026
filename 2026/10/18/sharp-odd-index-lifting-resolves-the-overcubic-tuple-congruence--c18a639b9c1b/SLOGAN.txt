Maity--Saikia's overcubic tuple Conjecture 1.5 holds with **sharp** powers of two: for \(M=2^ik+2^{i-1}-1\), the counts at \(8n+5\) and \(8n+7\) vanish modulo \(2^{i+2}\) and \(2^{i+3}\), respectively, for all \(i\ge3,k,n\ge0\). A stronger odd-index congruence reduces the entire infinite family to the known triple case by exploiting a parity cancellation in the quadratic binomial correction.
