For a two-point binomial success-probability mixture, the sharp log-odds gap ensuring ultra-log-concavity for **every** mixing weight is \(2\operatorname{arsinh}(1/\sqrt{n-1})\); ordinary log-concavity survives up to the larger sharp gap \(2\operatorname{arsinh}(\sqrt{(n+1)/\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor})\). At each fixed weight, failures of either curvature condition occupy at most one consecutive index band.
