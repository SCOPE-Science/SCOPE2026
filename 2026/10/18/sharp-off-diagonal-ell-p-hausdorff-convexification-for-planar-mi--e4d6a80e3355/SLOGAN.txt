For every \(p>2\), the exact worst \(\ell_p\) Hausdorff defect of the Minkowski sum of two planar compact sets with prescribed defects \(a,b\) is \(\bigl(((a+b)^p+|a-b|^p)/2\bigr)^{1/p}\), attained by pairs of two-point sets even when \(a\ne b\). This sharp off-diagonal profile strengthens the known constant-factor inequality by combining planar parallelogram reduction with Hanner's inequality.
