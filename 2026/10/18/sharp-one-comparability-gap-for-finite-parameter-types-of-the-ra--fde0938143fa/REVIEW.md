# Review of the sharp one-comparability type gap

**Correctness (PASS).** The one-point extension condition (lower ideal, upper filter, cross-comparability) is exact. The proof identifies one unique three-member restriction fiber, establishes that an induced non-antichain base also has a one-member fiber, and telescopes the resulting doubling inequality from the two-element chain. Independent counts confirm the expected labeled poset totals and extremizing families. The universal assertion is proved symbolically, not extrapolated from finite enumeration.

**Originality (PASS, best knowledge).** A previously published one-type counting and complexity theorem already establishes the ideal/filter representation and the antichain count. Both are expressly excluded from the novelty claim. Direct inspection of its full proof found no sharp bound conditioned on a comparable pair or the doubling lemma. The 2008 homogeneous-structure source supplies context, while Pretzel (1980) considers a related insertion-position construction but its available abstract centers on order dimension. Broad equivalence searches identified no prior sharp numerical threshold. Risk remains that Pretzel's uninspected full text or an unindexed earlier extremal result could cover some or all of the inequality. Novelty is not independently established.

**Value (PASS).** The natural model-theoretic invariant is the number of nonalgebraic finite-parameter orbits of the universal homogeneous poset. The result converts the exact maximum into a quantitative stability theorem: introducing any comparability causes a sharp multiplicative loss; a structural restriction-fiber lemma explains the phenomenon across all finite posets, and different extremal geometries show sharpness beyond a single special construction. It provides a useful boundary for counting problems without claiming that the count is efficient to compute generally.

**Scope.** Finite induced parameter posets, complete nonalgebraic one-types, standard strict-order language; no extremizer uniqueness assertion. The verification script is corroborative; the induction is the proof.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
