In the countable homogeneous random poset, \(n\) incomparable parameters allow \(2^{n+1}-1\) nonalgebraic one-types, but one comparable pair already forces at most \(3\cdot2^{n-1}\). This sharp one-comparability gap follows from a doubling lemma for one-point extensions and is attained by both a single chain with isolated points and height-two stars.
