For all finite abelian groups of order \(N\ge6\), changing just one phase of a flat Gabor window attains the largest possible phase-only measurement rank \(N^2-N+1\). The sharp best visible-subspace condition number within this one-defect family is \(N/[4\sin(\pi/(2E))]\) for odd exponent \(E\), or \(N/[2\sin(\pi/E)]\) for even \(E\), with exactly determined optimal phases.
