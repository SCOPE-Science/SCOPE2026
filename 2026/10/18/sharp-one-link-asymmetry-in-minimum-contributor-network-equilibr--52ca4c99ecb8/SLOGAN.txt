In the binary best-shot network public-good game, adding one link changes the **minimum pure-equilibrium contributor count** by at least \(-1\) and at most \(\lfloor(n-4)/2\rfloor\) on any connected \(n\)-agent graph with \(n\ge4\), and both extremes are attained. One added link to a tree achieves the arbitrarily growing upper gap, first at six agents.
