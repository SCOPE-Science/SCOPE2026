Among binary partition values \(b(2n)\), the failures of representation by \(x^2+y^2+2z^2\) have density \(1/6\), but their discrepancy is sharply one-sided: \(6T(X)-X\le7\) for every integer cutoff, with equality at \(X=17\). The negative fluctuations have the exact sharp logarithmic coefficient \(\liminf (T(X)-X/6)/\log_2 X=-1/4\), witnessed by an explicit base-four digit family.
