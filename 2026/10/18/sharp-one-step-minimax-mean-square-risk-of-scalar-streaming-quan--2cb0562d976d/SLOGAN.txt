The scalar streaming quantile Kaczmarz rule under jointly adversarial Bernoulli corruption has an exact binomial-tail minimax squared-risk factor, giving a sharp one-step contraction feasibility boundary of \(\beta<1/4\). At fixed admissible quantile and growing relative corruption amplitude \(B\), its minimum sufficient calibration batch has a logarithmic leading term and a negative \(\log\log B\) correction; these conclusions are specific to the scalar and state-relative-bounded setting.
