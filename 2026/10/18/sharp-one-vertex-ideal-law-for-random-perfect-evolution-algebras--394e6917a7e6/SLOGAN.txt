For a uniformly sampled labelled perfect evolution algebra of dimension \(n\) over a fixed field \(\mathbb F_q\), failure of simplicity has sharp probability \(2n(1-q^{-1})q^{1-n}(1+o(1))\). Conditional on failure, a one-dimensional ideal or a codimension-one ideal is almost always responsible, with asymptotically equal chances.
