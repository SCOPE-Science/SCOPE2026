For a periodic screened-Poisson quadratic, uniform sampling of consecutive \(q\)-coordinate blocks exactly maximizes the worst-case expected one-step energy reduction among **all fixed interval-sampling distributions**, with a closed hyperbolic-function formula valid in every dimension. As the coupling approaches the singular limit, the sharp efficiency transition is \(1-2\tanh(s/2)/s\), where \(s\) is block length divided by the correlation length.
