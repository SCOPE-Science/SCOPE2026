For every coordinate-permutation group with \(r\) orbits, the sharp norm of a maximum of \(k\) positive unit vectors in the fixed sublattice of \(\operatorname{FBL}^{(p)}[\ell_1^n]\) is exactly \(\min\{k,r\}^{1/p}\). This yields a complete orbit-count law for the failure of the AM property, with a sharp plateau once \(k\geq r\).
