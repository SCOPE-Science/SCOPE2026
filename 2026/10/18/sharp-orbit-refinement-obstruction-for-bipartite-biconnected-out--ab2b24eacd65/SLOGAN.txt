The first failure of uniform-start counting colour refinement to recover automorphism orbits in a biconnected bipartite outerplanar graph occurs at twelve vertices, uniquely in a twelve-cycle with two opposite length-three chords. An infinite matched-arc family on \(4\ell\) vertices, for odd \(\ell=2h+1\ge3\), has exactly \(h+1\) stable colours but \(2h+1\) automorphism orbits, giving an unbounded orbit-colour deficit.
