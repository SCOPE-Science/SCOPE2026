# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. For every tree, all directed neighbor choices are uniquely indexed by one mutual edge. This yields the probability law and exact Radon--Nikodym derivative. Spanning-tree exchanges establish the iff cycle-monochromatic characterization. A finite weighted-Laplacian determinant identity proves nonpositive covariance of distinct tree edges; Cauchy--Schwarz and the minimum spanning-tree weight yield the bound. A triangle-block product decomposition, exact rational mean and variance, and an i.i.d. central limit theorem with uniform integrability prove that its order is sharp. Exhaustive independent rational enumeration on six small graphs confirms the finite formulas.

## Originality

PASS, qualified. The nearest motivating article, Mészáros--Peled (arXiv:2610.11554v1, Section 3.1), constructs a coupling of *unconditioned* one-out graphs with uniform spanning trees using a root-excluded one-out function, not the all-vertices-choose map conditioned to have tree support. The weighted conditioning distribution, exact tree-weight rigidity and sharp total-variation rate are not stated there. Searches also compared random functional digraphs, two-cycles, conditioned connectivity, weighted spanning-tree laws and conductance size bias. No direct covering theorem was identified. The logical difference is in the conditional law and metric, not in superficial notation. Prior general random mapping enumeration or determinantal measure results may contain a formulation implying parts of the finding; independent priority audit remains necessary.

## Value

PASS. The theorem answers a natural robustness question raised by comparison of product-like one-out processes with weighted spanning trees: conditioned on the rare event of connectivity as a tree, does the resulting law approximate the canonical weighted tree measure in a strong metric? It gives an explicit exact density, an iff criterion for *exact* equality, a graph-independent quantitative total-variation approximation and an extremal family proving the convergence rate cannot be improved in order. This is a full structural statement for arbitrary finite connected weighted graphs, not a restatement of the unweighted coupling theorem or one isolated finite example.

## Limits

No result for directed transition kernels, infinite graphs, zero conductances or general multigraphs. Existing weighted spanning-tree covariance identities and Gaussian limit theory are credited. Comparison against all historical random-mapping sources is not claimed exhaustive.
