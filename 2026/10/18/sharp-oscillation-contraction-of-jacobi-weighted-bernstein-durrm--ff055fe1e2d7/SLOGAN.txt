For every Jacobi-weighted Bernstein–Durrmeyer Markov kernel, the exact worst-case oscillation contraction is attained between its two interval endpoints and equals the total-variation distance of two explicit beta laws. Its one-step contraction defect is asymptotic to \(2^{2-a-b}n^{(a+b)/2-1}2^{-n}/\sqrt{\Gamma(a)\Gamma(b)}\), exponentially smaller than the known \(L^2\) spectral gap.
