The fast-\(x\) planar Oregonator has a sharp parameter-uniform oscillation cutoff: if \(q\ge q_*\), where \(10q_*^3+6q_*^2+12q_*-1=0\), all positive-parameter flows converge to their sole equilibrium. For each \(0<q<q_*\), some positive auxiliary parameters produce a hyperbolic source and at least one periodic orbit.
