For an ideal Gaussian HMC sampler whose two harmonic frequencies are in the integer ratio \(1:m\), the best worst-direction expected squared synchronous-coupling factor over **all iid integration-time laws** is exactly \(0\) for odd \(m\), and \(\sin^2(\pi/[2(m+1)])\) for even \(m\). Every optimizer is supported on explicitly classified phase classes, so randomizing the duration cannot beat a suitable deterministic duration for this precise metric.
