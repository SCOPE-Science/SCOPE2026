## Correctness — PASS
The proof fixes the complex unilateral Hilbert-space shift convention, gauges complex weights to nonnegative magnitudes, and uses a normalized two-level trial vector on the entire interval. This gives the claimed universal bound for arbitrary bounded weights and arbitrary starting positions. For odd intervals, disjoint two-vertex blocks attain equality. For even intervals, the displayed positive Jacobi eigenvector satisfies all interior and endpoint equations exactly; positive-eigenvector Perron comparison proves its top eigenvalue is one. Equality forces the same eigen-equations, whose left-to-right recursion proves uniqueness among interval-supported extremizers. The package's checker verifies exact algebraic identities and numerical matrix tests; the infinite quantifiers rest on the analytical proof.
