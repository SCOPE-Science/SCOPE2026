For any finite family of Euclidean planks of total width at most \(W<1\), the area covered in the unit square is at most \(\sqrt2 W-W^2/2\) below \(W=1/\sqrt2\), and at most \((1+W^2)/2\) above it; both bounds are attained by a single central plank. The optimal plank normal turns continuously away from the diagonal after the sharp \(3/4\)-area transition, and every near-optimal family obeys a quantitative weighted angular-dispersion bound.
