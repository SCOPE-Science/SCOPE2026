# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The primary map and the Möbius rotation formula are given by Mañosa–Pantazi, Proposition 3. Its multiplier simplifies to the angle defect \(r(h)=\pi^{-1}\arctan(\epsilon\sqrt{-4-2h})\). The exact inverse energy is established algebraically. A reduced-fraction determinant bound gives \(s-r\ge 1/[P(P-1)]\); strict convexity of the energy transform and \(r\ge1/P\) make the edge pair the unique minimizer. Möbius inversion, an absolutely convergent \(\zeta(2)\) identity, and a Jacobian change of variables prove the count and limiting density. The finite verifier is supplementary.

## Originality

**PASS under examined evidence; independent priority determination remains open.** Mañosa–Pantazi establish elliptic conic rotation conjugacy and the qualitative density and existence of unbounded periods, but do not state a period-capped count, a sharp minimum energy gap, or this energy-density law. Llorens–Mañosa give general genus-zero rotation tools but do not quantify this particular pencil's period-capped spectrum. Petrera–Suris address geometric conic-pencil representations; only the abstract and bibliographic description were accessible here, so incidental equivalent statements remain a priority risk. The proof uses standard Farey/Möbius tools on a nontrivial explicit dynamical parametrization. A mathematically equivalent derivation not located by the inspected searches remains possible.

## Value

**PASS.** The invariant-conic resonance set is dense and of energy measure zero, so finite-period resolution is the relevant quantitative question when comparing discrete resonances with a continuum phase portrait. The global closest-pair exponent \(P^{-3}\), its exact constant and unique location close to the parabolic degeneration, and the explicit square-root energy concentration provide a sharp cutoff-level description. This is not presented as a general theorem about Farey fractions, and neither mere rational density nor the inversion formula alone is offered as the contribution.

## Scientific limits

The minimum is in conserved-energy coordinates, and phase-space geometric separation is not treated. Estimates fix \(0<\epsilon<1\). No perturbation stability or numerical observability theorem is established. The 2019 conic-pencil article was not materially inspected beyond its available abstract, and a specialized earlier implicit derivation could exist.
