For the elliptic invariant conics of a concrete Kahan map, the closest distinct resonance energies among prime periods at most \(P\) occur uniquely at periods \(P\) and \(P-1\), with sharp separation \(\Delta_P\sim\pi^2/(\epsilon^2P^3)\). The conic count grows quadratically in \(P\), and their energy distribution has an explicit integrable square-root pile-up at the parabolic boundary.
