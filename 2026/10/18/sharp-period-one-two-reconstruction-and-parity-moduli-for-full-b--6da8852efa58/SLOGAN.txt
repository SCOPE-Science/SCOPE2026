In continuous full-branch affine zigzag interval maps, unlabelled fixed-point locations determine the entire map exactly when the number of branches is even; for odd branch count, the fixed-point fiber is an open one-parameter family. In every case the smallest exact-period-two point obeys \(q=p_2b_1/(1-p_2+b_1)\), providing a single additional coordinate that restores unique reconstruction at the sharp period-two threshold.
