For the unnormalized hat-spline Gabor system with integer modulations and any \(k\) surviving translation cosets from an \(m\)-point period, every mask has exact upper bound \(k\) and an explicit cyclic-cut variance formula for its optimal lower bound. The worst lower bound is exactly \(k(k^2-1)/(3m^2)\), attained only by cyclic consecutive cosets, so any two retained cosets always suffice for stable reconstruction.
