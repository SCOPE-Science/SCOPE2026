For two Fourier modes on the circle, the loss from optimal interval concentration is bounded below by the exact sharp nonlinear function \(\frac{2\sin a}{\pi}(1-\cos(m/2))\) of mass exchanged relative to the Fourier-phase-centred arc. Even among fractional observation masks, every equality case is a characteristic function made of a central arc and two exterior caps, and the exchanged mass must be strictly less than \(2\pi/3\).
