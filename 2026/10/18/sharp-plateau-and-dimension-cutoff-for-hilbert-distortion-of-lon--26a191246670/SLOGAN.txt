For every real even cycle of order at least six with all but one edge of length one, the sharp Euclidean Banach--Mazur distance has an explicit two-branch formula when the last edge has length \(1<t\le2\). A flat distortion interval exists exactly for cycle orders \(6,8,10,12,14,16\); from order \(18\) onward the distortion strictly decreases throughout this entire interval.
