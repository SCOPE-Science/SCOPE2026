For a uniform multiset permutation with one symbol repeated \(\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor\) times and all other symbols distinct, the total-variation error in Poisson approximation of the fixed-point count is asymptotic to \(5e^{-2}/(3\sqrt n)\). A uniform Poisson–Charlier correction gives the signed approximation error for every repeated multiplicity proportional to \(\sqrt n\).
