# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The exponential spacing representation is exact for independent uniform circular points. The directional-missing indicators are exact spacing comparisons outside an explicitly exponentially improbable maximal-gap event. Their one-point probabilities and all locally dependent pair configurations are handled analytically. The dependency-graph Poisson theorem supplies total-variation convergence; a second-moment estimate supplies the lower regime, and coverage implies (and is implied by) universal exact greedy success on ordinary circular samples. The supplied verification script independently checks finite cases and algebraic inequalities. The sharp probability limits do not rely on finite experiments.

## Originality — PASS, with literature limitations
Full-text inspection of Bhamidi, Dhara, Schroeder and Stegehuis, arXiv:2610.12312v1 (October 8, 2026), shows only logarithmic-pool sufficiency for approximate navigation in its general-dimensional setting; it does not identify this one-dimensional exact threshold or Poisson failure count. Arratia–Goldstein–Gordon (1989) proves a generic Poisson-approximation technique, not the graph-specific event equivalence. The abstract of Glaz–Naus–Roos–Wallenstein (1994) shows Poisson approximations for ordered m-spacings, but no directional-neighbor or routing result. Its full article was not materially available for comparison; an indirect implication is a residual risk. Recorded semantic research-record queries found no statement subsuming the combined theorem. A negative search is not a proof of novelty.

## Value — PASS
The motivating theorem leaves logarithmic constants unspecified and guarantees approximate rather than exact routing. A sharp threshold at base two, a finite-size Poisson critical window, and an exact equivalence between local directional faults and global navigation failure identify the smallest sampling budget for a natural one-dimensional model and quantify the risk of failure. This is a substantive stochastic-geometric improvement rather than a minor numerical refinement.

## Limits
No claim is made about nonuniform points, nontrivial hierarchies, other dimensions, or finite-n exact Poisson distributions. The classical ordered-spacing literature warrants further specialist comparison, and independence has not been tested externally.
