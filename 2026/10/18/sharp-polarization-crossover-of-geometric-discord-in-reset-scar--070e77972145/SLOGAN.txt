A Poisson-reset many-body scar state is globally separable but its distinct-site pairs remain quantum-discordant at every finite, nonzero reset rate. In the sparse-reset limit its squared Hilbert--Schmidt geometric discord has a sharp polarization optimum \((2-\sqrt3)/3\), where the best measurement axis switches from transverse to longitudinal; the result is independent of the size of the chain and site separation.
