The exact smallest degree of a holomorphic polynomial whose power-weighted radial squared modulus equals \(1+2\varepsilon\cos(k\theta+\phi)\) is \(\max\{k,\lceil(k/\sqrt{1-4\varepsilon^2}+k-\beta-1)/2\rceil\}\) for \(\beta>-1\) and \(0<\varepsilon<1/2\). All sharp-amplitude extremizers are uniquely determined up to global phase, and the degree diverges like \(k/(4\sqrt{1/2-\varepsilon})\) near the positive-cone boundary.
