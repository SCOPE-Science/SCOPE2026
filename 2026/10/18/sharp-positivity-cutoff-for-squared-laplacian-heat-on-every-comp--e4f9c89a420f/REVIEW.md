The exact orthogonal Laplacian decomposition supplies all entries of the bi-heat matrix. The endpoint norm is the maximum absolute row sum, and within-part off-diagonal entries are the only possible negative entries. A single-crossing argument in time and a separate one-crossing argument in the normalized part parameter establish the unique global positivity cutoff and strict largest-part ordering. Explicit limiting root equations establish the two timescales. The full proof and a separately reconstructed finite test suite are present; finite tests are not substituted for the universal argument.

The published three-spectral-level barrier gives a general no-gap principle for up to three eigenvalues but not the exact formula or the arbitrary-multipartite family with many levels. A related complete-multipartite ordinary-heat derivative result concerns a different operator and has no implication for positivity of the squared-Laplacian exponential. Targeted mathematical-record and literature searches found no covering formula. Historical priority remains provisional because alternate terminology might conceal an older equivalent computation.

## Value — PASS
Entrywise positivity precisely means preservation of every input range for this mass-preserving smoother. The sharp recovery time is a natural stability metric, and the theorem shows it depends only on the largest independent part even though the full Laplacian spectrum depends on other sizes. The structural contrast between equal short-time norm growth and a linearly diverging ratio of positivity-recovery times makes the result more than a standard diagonalization exercise. No general graph bound is claimed.
