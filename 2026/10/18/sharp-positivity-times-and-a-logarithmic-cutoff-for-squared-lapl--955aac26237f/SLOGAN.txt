On a Boolean cube, squared-Laplacian heat is positive exactly at time zero and after one sharp, strictly dimension-increasing cutoff, located at the antipodal kernel's final zero crossing. The cutoff is \(\frac14\log d-\frac{1}{8d^2}+\frac{1}{8d^3}+O(d^{-4})\), so worst-case bounded-data stability returns only on a logarithmic time scale.
