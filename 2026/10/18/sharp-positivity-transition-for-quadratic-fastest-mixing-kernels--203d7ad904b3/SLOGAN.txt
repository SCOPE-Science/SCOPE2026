For nonnegative quadratic Laplacian filters on \(K_{p,q}\), the unique fastest-mixing kernel changes sharply at \((p-2)q=p^2\): beyond that boundary the optimal holding probability on the higher-degree part is exactly zero, and a new closed-form spectral rate replaces the unconstrained polynomial optimum. Unlike any product of two individually stochastic one-hop steps, the optimal two-hop kernel retains a uniformly bounded contraction factor when one part grows while the other stays fixed.
