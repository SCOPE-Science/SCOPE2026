For every reflexive exponent \(1<p<\infty\) and \(0<\alpha<1/p\), the positive lazy Cesàro-bounded shift \(T=(1-\delta)I+\delta W_\alpha\) has the sharp opposing rates \(\|T^n\|\asymp n^{\alpha/2}\) and \(\|T^{n+1}-T^n\|\asymp n^{(\alpha-1)/2}\). Thus its powers diverge even while consecutive powers coalesce in operator norm, with a spectrum tangent to the unit circle only at \(1\).
