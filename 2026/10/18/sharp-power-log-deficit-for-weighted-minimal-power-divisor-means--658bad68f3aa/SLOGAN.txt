For every fixed integer \(r\ge2\), the mean of the weighted divisor function of the least \(r\)-th-power multiple has an explicit, strictly positive deficit of order \(x^{1/(r+1)}/\sqrt{\log x}\), with coefficient \(-\zeta(1/(r+1))A_r\). This replaces the previously known inverse-logarithmic error by a sharp signed secondary term.
