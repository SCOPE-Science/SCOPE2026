For every \(1\le p<\infty\) and every Cesàro-primitive weight \(0\le\sigma\le1\), the power substitution \(f(x)\mapsto f(x^\alpha)\) is bounded exactly when \(0<\alpha\le1\), with the same sharp norm \(\alpha^{-1-1/p}\) for the entire family. The sharp factor is approached by intervals concentrating at the right endpoint, is never attained for \(0<\alpha<1\), and reverses to unbounded amplification from the left endpoint for \(\alpha>1\).
