For a regular \(2k\)-gon, the weak powered-Dowker inequality has an exact all-exponent dichotomy: every nonnegative exponent works except at \(k=4,5,7\), where the threshold is exactly \(1\). Consequently every other regular even-gonal normed plane obeys the log-honeycomb inequality for normal convex unit-area tilings, strengthening the previously known ordinary-perimeter conclusion.
