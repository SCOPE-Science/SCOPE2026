The maximum number of ordered \(n\)-cycles sharing one complete left-right orbit signature is at most \(\prod_{i=1}^{n-1}\gcd(n,i)\), and this bound is sharp exactly when \(n\) is a prime power. For \(n=p^k\) the exact maximum is \(p^{(p^k-1)/(p-1)-k}\), realized by digit-reversal orbit orders and independent signature-preserving suffix translations.
