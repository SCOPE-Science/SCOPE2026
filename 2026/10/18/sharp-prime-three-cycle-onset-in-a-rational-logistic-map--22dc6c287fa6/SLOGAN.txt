For \(K_\mu(x)=\mu x(1-x)/(1+x)\) on \([0,1]\), the first prime-three orbit occurs at the exact algebraic boundary \(\mu_*\approx5.64136371874529\): there are no such points below it, one neutral three-cycle at it, and exactly two distinct three-cycles above it up to \(3+2\sqrt2\). The boundary is the unique root greater than \(1\) of \(\mu^4-4\mu^3-10\mu^2+4\mu+1=0\).
