The exact minimum projection norm onto the coordinate-sum-zero hyperplane of every real finite quadratic Lorentz space \(N_t(x)^2=t\|x\|_2^2+(1-t)\|x\|_\infty^2\) has two explicit branches meeting at the dimension-dependent threshold \(t=1/(n-1)\). An extremizing vector changes from \((1,-1,\ldots,-1)\) to \(((n-1)t,-1,\ldots,-1)\), while the optimal projection remains coordinate centering.
