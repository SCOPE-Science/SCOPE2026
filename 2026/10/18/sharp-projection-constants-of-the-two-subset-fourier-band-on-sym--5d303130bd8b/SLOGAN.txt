For the full Fourier coefficient band of the permutation action on unordered pairs, the exact complex projection constant on \(S_n\) is a three-term derangement-without-short-cycles formula, with sharp limit \(\lambda(E_n)/\sqrt{\dim E_n}\to3e^{-3/2}\). Adding the two lower isotypes to the \((n-2,2)\) block strictly raises its projection constant for every \(n\ge4\), by an amount asymptotic to \(ne^{-3/2}\).
