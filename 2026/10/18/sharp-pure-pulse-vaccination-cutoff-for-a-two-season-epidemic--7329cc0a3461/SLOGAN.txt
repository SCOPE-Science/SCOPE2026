In a two-season epidemic with waning vaccine protection, concentrating an entire period's vaccination budget exactly at the start of the high-transmission season is uniquely optimal among all periodic measure-valued schedules precisely up to the explicit threshold \(B_* = \min\{\log(\beta_H/\alpha),\log(\alpha/\beta_L)\}\). Above that cutoff, splitting off an arbitrarily small second pulse gives a strict improvement; for the published school-term example the sharp transition is \(B_*\approx0.390979957597\).
