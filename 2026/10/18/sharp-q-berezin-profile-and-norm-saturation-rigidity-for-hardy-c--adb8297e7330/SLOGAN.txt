Every constant-symbol composition operator \(C_cf=f(c)\) on Hardy \(H^2\) has exact \(q\)-Berezin number \(2q/[1-|c|\sqrt{1-q^2}+q\sqrt{1-|c|^2}]\), with an explicit unique normalized-kernel extremizer when \(c\ne0\). The \(q\)-Berezin number reaches the operator norm at precisely \(q=\sqrt{1-|c|^2}\), and its normalized deficit is an exact squared chordal separation of two unit parameter vectors.
