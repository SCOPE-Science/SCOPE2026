# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The result distinguishes distinct scattered subsequences from index embeddings and contiguous factors. The four disjoint strata by one-count exhaust all palindromes because the source has exactly three ones. For the two-one stratum, the feasible parameter pairs form three explicit integer rectangles; their union is counted without multiplicity, separately for the two outer-gap orders. The three-one stratum is an independent rectangular product. This proves the exact identity even with zero-length gaps. The leading homogeneous form is bounded by an exhaustive four-region analytic case split (outer imbalance relative to the first middle gap, and order of the middle gaps). Its strict equality characterization gives asymptotic rigidity. The quadratic certificate in the final region was verified symbolically, and the standalone verifier independently checked 5,985 exhaustive gap quadruples and 300 larger random instances.

The numerical checks are supporting evidence, not proof of the infinite formulas. A close reading of the analytic cases and boundary equalities found no missing parameter regime. The public result asserts no exact finite global extremal formula.

## Originality — PASS on inspected evidence

The closest inspected primary work is Chowdhury–Hasan–Iqbal–Rahman, arXiv:1110.5296v1 (2011), concerning a *longest common* palindromic subsequence of two strings, rather than the number of distinct palindromic subsequences of a weight-constrained single string. A general dynamic-programming formulation of the arbitrary-word counting problem is established background, and is not claimed as new. That algorithm does not state the explicit four-gap identity, sharp \(3/16\) extremal coefficient, or limiting four-gap rigidity. Published research about distinct palindromic *factors* of circular/linear words involves contiguity, not the present scattered object. Targeted comparisons of scientific statements yielded no stronger matching result.

Search completeness is not a proof of novelty. A previously unindexed formula, an implicit specialization in a broader enumeration article, or equivalent sparse-palindrome optimization remains a residual risk. No independent literature certification has been performed.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The exact formula resolves a natural infinite four-parameter fixed-composition enumeration, avoiding an exponential subsequence census. The sharp asymptotic extremal coefficient \(3/16\) is established by a nontrivial homogeneous optimization, and the unique continuous optimizer forces any exact maximizing configurations to become equispaced. This links palindrome-subsequence diversity to placement geometry and supplies a natural baseline for studying higher marked weights. The four-gap setting is mathematically motivated as the first source class with a three-one palindrome layer; the finding is not merely an arbitrary finite lookup. The main value is the structural classification plus rigorously sharp limit, not a general alphabet theorem.
