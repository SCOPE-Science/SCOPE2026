For the real scalar–Hilbert one-sum \(\mathbb R\oplus_1H\) with \(\dim H\ge3\), the uniform Steinhaus modulus has the sharp quadratic law \(U_X(d;a)\sim d\,a^2/(4-2d)\) at every fixed separation \(0<d<2\). This determines the precise curvature-controlled separation rate despite the norm's flat unit-sphere faces.
