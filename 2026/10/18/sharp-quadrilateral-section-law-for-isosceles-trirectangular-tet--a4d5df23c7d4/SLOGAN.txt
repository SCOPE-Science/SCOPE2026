For \(T_t=\operatorname{conv}\{0,e_1,e_2,te_3\}\), a motion-invariant random affine-plane section is quadrilateral with a completely explicit arctangent probability that increases strictly to its unique maximum \(0.25328442627564\ldots\) at \(t=1\), then decreases strictly to zero. The full one-parameter probability profile has an exact two-to-one inverse interval, so the section polygon-type frequency alone does not identify the shape.
