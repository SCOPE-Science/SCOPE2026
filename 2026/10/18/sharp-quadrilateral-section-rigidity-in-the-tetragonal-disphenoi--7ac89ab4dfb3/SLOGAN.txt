For invariant-plane sections of every tetragonal disphenoid \(T_t\), the quadrilateral probability has an exact elementary dihedral-angle formula and is uniquely minimized at the regular tetrahedron \(t=1\), with value \(2\pi/\arccos(-1/3)-3\). The probability decreases strictly for \(0<t<1\), increases strictly for \(t>1\), and the result includes sharp degeneration limits and quadratic stability.
