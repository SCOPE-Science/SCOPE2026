For undirected finite cycles, the number of parameter-free quantifier-free \(k\)-types grows strictly with cycle length up to exactly \(2k\) vertices and then stabilizes; any two distinct cycle lengths \(n<m\) first have different expressivity counts in arity \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor+1\). The exact counts combine labeled linear forests with Stirling partitions, while the fraction of automorphism-invariant \(k\)-ary relations that are quantifier-free definable decays as \(2^{-(1/2+o(1))n^{k-1}}\) for fixed \(k\ge2\).
