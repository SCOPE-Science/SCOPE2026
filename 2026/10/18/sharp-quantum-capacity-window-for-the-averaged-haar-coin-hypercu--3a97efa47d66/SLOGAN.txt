For the Haar-coin-averaged hypercube vertex channel, one logical qubit survives every number of steps, while the excess unassisted quantum capacity has a sharp window at \(t=(d/4)\ln d+cd\) with limiting profile \(e^{-4c}/(2\ln2)\). The result identifies the exact dimension-dependent time needed to reduce the available quantum transmission rate to within any fixed amount of the protected qubit.
