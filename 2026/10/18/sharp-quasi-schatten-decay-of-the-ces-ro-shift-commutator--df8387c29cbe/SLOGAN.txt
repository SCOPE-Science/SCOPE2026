For the classical discrete Cesàro operator \(C\) and the unilateral forward shift \(S\), the singular values of \(CS-SC\) satisfy \(s_m(CS-SC)\asymp m^{-2}\). Consequently this natural Toeplitz-generator commutator belongs to \(\mathcal S_p\) exactly for \(p>1/2\), attaining the sharp weak quasi-Schatten endpoint.
