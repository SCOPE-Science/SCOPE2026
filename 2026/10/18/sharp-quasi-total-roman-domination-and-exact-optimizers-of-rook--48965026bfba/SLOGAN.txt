For all rectangular rook graphs \(K_m\square K_n\) with \(2\le m\le n\), the quasi-total Roman domination number is \(2m\), and all optimal labelings admit an exact associated-Stirling enumeration. Every optimizer is a supported one-per-row (or, on square boards, one-per-column) placement of label \(2\), apart from fully classified additional optima on the \(2\times2\) and \(3\times3\) boards.
