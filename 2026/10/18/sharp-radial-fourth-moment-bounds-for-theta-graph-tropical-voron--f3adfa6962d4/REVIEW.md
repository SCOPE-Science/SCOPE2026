## Originality: PASS, subject to bibliographic risk
The directly relevant de Jong–Shokrieh paper proves the Voronoi realization and **already computes the second moment**, but its inspected Sections 7–8 do not state the fourth moment or either sharp constant. The Bach–Beck–Rehberg paper gives the matroid-dual cographic zonotope and Ehrhart information without these metric moments. Searches for theta-graph, genus-two Voronoi, lattice-quantizer fourth moments, normalized hexagon radial moments, and the two constants found no statement that implies the new theorem. Generic polygon quadrature is a method of proof, not coverage of the sharp parameterized inequality. An unindexed classical planar quantization formula could still cover the result.

The theorem yields a complete all-parameter rational classification and *sharp* dimensionless inequalities, with a rigid equilateral equality class and a distinct degenerate limiting extremizer. The polynomial certificate exhibits global extremality over three independently varying lengths; it is not a mere single example or an automatic restatement of the known second moment.
