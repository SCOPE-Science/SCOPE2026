For every nontrivial Cartesian product of simplices, radial mean bodies satisfy the globally sharp containments \(c_{D,p}(K-K)\subset R_pK\subset c_{\delta,p}(K-K)\) for all real \(p>-1\), where \(D\) is total dimension and \(\delta\) the smallest simplex-block dimension. Extremal directions are completely classified, and for two equal high-dimensional simplex factors the optimal radial distortion undergoes an explicit transition at \(p\asymp D\).
