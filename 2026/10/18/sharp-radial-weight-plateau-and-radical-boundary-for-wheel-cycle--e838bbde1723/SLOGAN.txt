For every weighted wheel with at least six rim vertices, unit rim lengths and spoke length \(1/2\le t\le(1+\sqrt{17})/4\), the smallest real weighted-\(\ell_1\) projection norm onto its full cycle space is exactly \((1+2t)^2/(1+2t+2t^2)\), independent of rim size. The radical boundary is sharp: this formula fails strictly for every larger spoke length below \(3/2\), and six is the smallest uniform saturation size on the nondegenerate interval.
