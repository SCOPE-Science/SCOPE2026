Weighted Minkowski sums of Euclidean balls in coordinate hyperplanes have exact Euclidean in- and circumradii; their outer-sphere contact set has precisely \(2^k\) points, where the active count \(k\) follows a sharp sorted-weight threshold rule. In three dimensions the contact count jumps from four to eight at a Pythagorean equality, while in every dimension equal weights uniquely optimize both radii and their ratio at fixed mean width.
