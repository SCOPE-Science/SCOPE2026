# Review of Sharp random-shadow variance and constant-width rigidity of right prisms

For every positive-area compact planar convex base, the projected prism is the Minkowski sum of a projected base and projected vertical segment. The resulting exact brightness identity, Haar beta moments, strictly convex height quadratic, and width-variance identity prove the entire claim, including rigidity and a full equality-case distribution. `verify.py` successfully checked numerical/symbolic examples, including an independent cuboid comparison; finite quadrature was not used as proof.
