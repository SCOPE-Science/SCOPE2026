Among right prisms of arbitrary planar convex base, the smallest variance of a uniformly random planar shadow area, normalized by squared base area, is a universal sharp constant \(C_*\approx0.0125877019\); equality holds precisely at the unique optimal height above a constant-width base. The exact excess above \(C_*\), after optimizing height, depends strictly monotonically on just the normalized variance of the base's directional widths, giving an equality classification and quantitative stability law. At the optimal height, all constant-width bases moreover share the identical full base-area-normalized random-shadow distribution.
