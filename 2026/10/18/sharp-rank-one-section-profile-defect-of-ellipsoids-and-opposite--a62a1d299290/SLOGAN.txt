A three-dimensional ellipsoid's parallel section-area function has an exactly computable best rank-one relative \(L^2\) error: three angular moments determine it, and it vanishes precisely for balls. Its sharp near-ball coefficient is \(\|B-(\operatorname{tr}B)I/3\|_F^2/24\), yet equally eccentric prolate and oblate ellipsoids behave radically differently—the former retain a positive limiting error while the latter approach zero like \(1/(45\log L)\).
