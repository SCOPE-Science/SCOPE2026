For a full-dimensional Bernoulli Boolean polytope at any fixed vertex-retention probability \(p\in(0,1)\), the chance of nontrivial Minkowski decomposability is asymptotic to \(n[p^2+(1-p)^2]^{2^{n-1}}\), with exponentially small relative error. Almost every rare decomposition consists of one coordinate segment and an indecomposable complementary factor.
