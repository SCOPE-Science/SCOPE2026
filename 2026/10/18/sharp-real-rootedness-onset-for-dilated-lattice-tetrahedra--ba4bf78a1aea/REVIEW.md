# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The open-box symmetry and coefficient-count identities are proved explicitly from lattice fundamental domains, Ehrhart reciprocity, and inclusion–exclusion. The critical gap is \(G_m=1+\frac m2(G_2-1)+\frac H3(m^3-4m)\), where \(G_2=2E+4h_2-11\ge1\). Combined with nonnegative Ehrhart coefficients, this proves all onset conditions and root multiplicities, including the unimodular sharp example. The standalone exact enumerator corroborated 28 tests; computations are not substituted for an infinite proof.

## Originality
PASS with priority risk. BKN (2023, Proposition 4.5, full manuscript page 16) gives a general local coefficient identity, but does not state the explicit strictly positive gamma-gap for dilated tetrahedra or the sharp 2/3/4-onset classification. Nill (2026, Theorem 1.2, full manuscript pages 1–4) proves a qualitative sufficiently-large-width real-rootedness theorem without this finite exact dilation threshold. Direct implication comparisons distinguished box polynomials from ordinary Ehrhart numerators; targeted published-result and historical-claim searches returned no equivalent theorem. The proof uses standard ingredients, so the possibility of earlier unstated or unindexed specialization remains.

## Mathematical value
PASS. This is a natural complete sharp classification for the first dilations of every lattice tetrahedron, strengthening qualitative eventual real-rootedness to a dimension-three sharp universal cutoff. The edge-incidence identity \(G_2=2E+4h_2-11\) supplies a structural inequality, not merely a numerical census, and the unimodular example proves the cutoff cannot be improved. No claim is made for arbitrary three-dimensional polytopes.
