For every finite abelian group \(G\) and prescribed nonempty support \(S\), the largest linear tomography rank of a full Gabor projector orbit generated by a real window is exactly \(|G|\,|S-S|-(|G|/2)\,|((S-S)\cap G[2])\setminus\{0\}|\). Explicit positive integer windows attain the bound, and generically it is attained; nonzero two-torsion is the sole additional obstruction beyond missing support differences.
