For the diagonal-deleted multipartite graphs \(T^*(r,a)\), the general-position polynomial is unimodal at \(a=3\) exactly for \(1\le r\le10\) or \(r\ge18\), and at \(a=4\) exactly for \(1\le r\le11\) or \(r\ge40\). A direct structural classification proves the full coefficient formula and both sharp nonunimodality windows.
