# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The section nesting is exact: moving the component height upward removes equal nonnegative multiples of the base and its reflection, so the earlier Minkowski section contains the later one by addition of the difference body. The resulting two-section formula gives a dimension-raising coefficient identity via finite integration. The Alexandrov--Fenchel log-concavity of base mixed volumes gives the lower bounds, and equality in Minkowski's first inequality characterizes centered symmetry for base dimension at least two. Both endpoint ratios are covered by the divided-difference polynomial \(\Psi_k\). No finite computation is taken as a proof of the all-dimensional statement. The standard-library checker passes 1,050 exact coefficient identities and 36 exact polygon-section integrals; independently constructed three-dimensional convex hulls also agree in 36 cases.

## Originality — PASS with bibliographic limitations
Full recoverable proofs were examined for the published simplex-truncation Godbersen profile and the published Santaló/volume-product profile of homothetic frusta. The former is an explicit special case recovered by substituting simplex base coefficients into the new recursion, not a competing discovery. The latter concerns the same frustum family but only polar volumes, Santaló centers, and product monotonicity. The present result instead supplies the entire reflected Minkowski polynomial for arbitrary bases, together with the sharp coefficientwise centrally symmetric minimum and rigidity. Searches also covered reflected prisms, cones, Cayley sums, pyramids, mixed-volume recursions, and the higher-order Godbersen primary work. Relevant primary arXiv full texts were inaccessible in the available session and could contain unindexed related statements. Historical priority remains a residual risk; a failed search is not a proof of absolute novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The theorem transfers every opposite-body mixed-volume coefficient from dimension \(n-1\) to dimension \(n\) through a two-term recurrence for the full class of homothetic frusta. It determines the sharp lower envelope of *each* coefficient with one simultaneous equality class and gives exact, nontrivial difference-body ratios, including the dimension-dependent pyramid constant \(2^{n+1}-2\). This extends the published simplex-only computation to nonsmooth, asymmetric and nonpolyhedral bases. It is a structural geometry result rather than an arbitrary numerical slice. The upper Godbersen extremal inequality and higher-order extensions are not claimed.
