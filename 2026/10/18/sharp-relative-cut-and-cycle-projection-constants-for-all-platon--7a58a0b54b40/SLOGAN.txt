For each Platonic-solid graph, the canonical orthogonal cut and cycle projections minimize their \(\ell_1\) and \(\ell_\infty\) edge-space norms among **all** linear projections. The dodecahedron has exact minima \(38/15\) for cuts and \(34/15\) for cycles, while its icosahedral dual exchanges them; explicit automorphism-character and rational-current certificates establish the full five-solid classification.
