For finite equal proximal stepsizes, scalar four-operator splitting has an exact open relaxation window and two different sharp curvature thresholds: \(17\) for individually tuned relaxation and \((5+\sqrt{57})/2\) for a common relaxation across all smaller concave curvatures. A globally strongly convex smooth problem with strictly positive weak-concavity modulus has a linearly unstable minimizer for every relaxation when the proximal stepsize is too large.
