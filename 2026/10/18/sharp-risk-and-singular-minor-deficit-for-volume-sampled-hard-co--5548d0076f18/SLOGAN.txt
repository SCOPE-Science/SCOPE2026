For inconsistent least squares, imposing \(k<n\) exact row equations selected by the hat-projector volume law increases the expected full-data squared residual by at most the sharp factor \(\frac{n+1}{n-k+1}\). The exact shortfall from this worst-case frontier is a nonnegative sum of singular minors of a rank-one-augmented projector, revealing precisely how dependent row sets lower the risk.
