# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The clipped-opinion map is uniformly outward-pointing at every community corner because each vertex has at least one excess same-sign neighbor. This makes its local derivative zero. The infection/vaccination Jacobian is triangular after separating positive- and negative-opinion nodes, and its nontrivial infection block is exactly \((1-\alpha)\operatorname{Id}+\lambda A_{MM}\). Symmetry and a three-satellite reduction give the induced negative-run Perron eigenvalue in closed form. The end-of-word decomposition establishes the finite-word count for arbitrary community number. The strict parameter witness is proved by rational inequalities. The boundary \(\lambda r_L=\alpha\) is not adjudicated.

## Originality

PASS, narrowly scoped to the combined explicit model and topology. Roy et al. (arXiv:2603.24403, Sections Model Description/Methodology/Epidemic Threshold) analyze full anti-vaccine threshold and simulations, not the exact family of partially vaccinated pinned corners. Benjamini--Zakharov--Zhukovskii (2026, Section 6.2) already give exponentially many stable majority partitions, and Delmas--Dronnier--Zitt (2025 and related work) already give broad spectral vaccination principles; neither ingredient is claimed new in isolation. The claimed difference is the derived exact negative-run spectral sequence, associated attracting-corner count, and same-coverage opposite-stability witness for this specific coupled map. A different two-layer SAIS/opinion model by Zhai et al. (2026) reports multiple equilibria; only its abstract was accessible and direct subsumption cannot be excluded absolutely. An unindexed specialized calculation or general reduction remains a risk. Searches and material-inspection extent are recorded in AUDIT.json.

## Value

PASS. The result addresses a concrete uncertainty about whether the aggregate vaccination fraction captures epidemic control in a coupled social/biological network. It shows that equilibrium arrangements with exactly the same vaccination total can have opposite local epidemic stability, quantifies the sharp dependence on anti-vaccine cluster length, and counts all stable polarized corners in a natural infinite graph family. The conclusion is an exact deterministic mathematical boundary rather than an empirical intervention recommendation.

## Scientific limits

Only the stated chain-of-cliques family, the specified pinned equilibria and strict spectral regimes are classified. Finite checks support but cannot replace the proof. The linearized spectral comparison at equality is inconclusive. Stochastic finite-population models, general graph families and nonlinear endemic attractors are outside scope.
