For a Möbius ladder with individually chosen conductances and a fixed total budget, determinant-optimal network design deletes every antipodal rung exactly when its relative unit price satisfies \(t\ge n^2/(2n-1)\); below this boundary the unique optimum gives all rungs positive weight. At prices \(t_n/n\to c\in(0,1/2)\), the optimal rung-to-rim ratio is asymptotic to \(2z_c^2/n^2\), where \(\tanh z_c=2cz_c\), and the weighted spanning-tree advantage over a pure cycle tends to \(e^{-2cz_c^2}\cosh^2z_c>1\).
