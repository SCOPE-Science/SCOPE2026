The expected Jaccard similarity of two replicate species inventories can fall as both inventories grow: the first possible finite census is six individuals, uniquely with abundances \((1,5)\). In the entire singleton-plus-dominant family, independent sampling gives \(1-m/N+(m/N)^2\), while disjoint sampling gives \(1-m/N\) for sample sizes \(2\le m\le N/2\), an exact protocol-dependent gap of \((m/N)^2\).
