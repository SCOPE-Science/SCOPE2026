For uniformly chosen invertible structure matrices of perfect evolution algebras over a fixed finite field, nontrivial diagonal automorphisms occur asymptotically only through isolated shortest permitted cycles; their exact rarity is \(\sim \binom{n}{r}(r-1)!(q-1)^r|\mathrm{GL}_{n-r}(q)|/|\mathrm{GL}_n(q)|\). The least symmetry-support length depends on the multiplicative orders of two modulo odd prime divisors of \(q-1\), and a finite squaring-orbit formula gives the exact expected diagonal-group order in every dimension.
