## correctness

The frozen claim concerns the exact scalar recursion with stationary standard Gaussian AR(1) design, positive observation-noise variance and independent centered noise. Conditioning on the design eliminates noise cross terms and gives the stated positive finite-horizon sum. For a=|1-sx²|, the Mehler expansion makes the transfer operator the trace-norm convergent sum of rho^j times the rank-one operators generated by a h_j: the Hermite recurrence gives ||a h_j||=O(j+1). Its positive kernel gives a simple Perron root with positive endpoint overlaps in both c_k and d_k. Thus the homogeneous term decays and the causal noise series converges in L² below one, with linear/exponential divergence of the forced second moment on/above one. For nonexistence, expand a hypothetical L² causal stationary iterate k steps back: its residual is orthogonal to all more recent noise contributions conditional on the full design, giving the same divergent lower bound. For uniqueness below one, the k-step product tends to zero in probability since its expected square is c_k; stationary L² residual differences are tight, so their products tend to zero without a design/iterate independence assumption. The Rayleigh comparison for increasing rho is strict: for the lower-rho eigenvector v, av lies in L² by the eigenvalue equation and convergence of the expansion after one extra factor of a (||a² h_j||=O((j+1)²)). Equality would force av constant by the Hermite coefficients and hence v proportional to 1/a, impossible at the real zeros. Compactly supported Rayleigh tests and strong convergence of the Markov operator show divergence as rho tends to one. For joint analyticity in s and rho use the unitarily equivalent signed multiplier 1-sx², whose Hermite expansion is locally analytic in trace norm. Reflection commutes with the operator; on the even sector B(-rho)=B(rho), and the simple branch through rho=0 is even. The frozen proof's reference to the entire spectrum being even is too broad (odd-sector eigenvalues change sign); only the leading branch is needed. The first perturbation kills the even rank-one eigenvector; the h_2 overlap yields 32/9 at s=2/3, and the step derivative is 2, proving the stated -16/9 coefficient. The theorem is unchanged by these proof clarifications. Independent finite Hermite projection (no uploaded script executed) agrees across two orders; at rho=.04 it gives .663823770660.
