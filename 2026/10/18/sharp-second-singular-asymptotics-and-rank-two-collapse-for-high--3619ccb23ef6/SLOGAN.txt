For large real order \(\alpha\), the normalized Volterra fractional integral \(T_\alpha=\Gamma(\alpha+1)I^\alpha\) has a sharp second singular value \(s_2(T_\alpha)\sim1/(8\alpha)\), and its total squared singular mass beyond rank two is \(O(\alpha^{-4})\). This rank-two collapse yields the second-order norm law \(\|T_\alpha\|=1/2+1/(8\alpha)+1/(32\alpha^2)+O(\alpha^{-3})\).
