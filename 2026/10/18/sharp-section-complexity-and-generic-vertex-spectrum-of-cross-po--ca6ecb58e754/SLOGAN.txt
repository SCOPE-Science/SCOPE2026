For every \(d\ge2\), the cross-polytope prism \(C_d\times[-1,1]\) has a sharp hyperplane-section vertex maximum of \(2d(d-1)+2\). Every generic section vertex count belongs to an explicit two-parameter integer spectrum, every spectrum value is realized, and maximizing generic sections have exactly \(d+1\) positive vertices on one layer and \(d-1\) on the other.
