For every cyclic four-polytope with \(n\ge6\) vertices, an affine hyperplane section has at most \(\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor+2n-12\) polygonal facets, and this bound is sharp. For \(n\ge7\), all generic maximizing sections are characterized by balanced vertex signs and exactly one singleton run of each sign, which also fixes their triangular and quadrilateral face counts.
