For a uniformly sampled query in a depth-\(N\), \(d\)-dimensional uniform-cut tree, the chance of a tagged cell with fixed aspect ratio at most \(\beta>1\) is asymptotic to \(\sqrt d(d/\pi)^{(d-1)/2}(\log\beta)^{d-1}N^{-(d-1)/2}\). Despite this rare geometric event, the centered logarithmic volume still has a standard Gaussian limit after conditioning.
