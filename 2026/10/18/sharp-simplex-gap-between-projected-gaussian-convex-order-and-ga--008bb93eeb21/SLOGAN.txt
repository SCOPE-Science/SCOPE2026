For the equal-weight rank-one Gaussian mixture supported on the \(d+1\) directions of a regular simplex, all one-dimensional convex-order tests pass up to the exact isotropic Gaussian scale \(\sqrt{2/[d(d+1)]}\), but the optimal jointly Gaussian Jensen-block certificate stops sharply at \(1/d\). The gap tends to a factor \(\sqrt2\), and the endpoint Gaussian component coupling is unique at the level of correlations; this is a certificate obstruction, not a full multivariate convex-order counterexample.
