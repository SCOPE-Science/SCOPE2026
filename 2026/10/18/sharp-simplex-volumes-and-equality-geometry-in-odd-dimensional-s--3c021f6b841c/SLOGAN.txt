In every odd Euclidean dimension, the largest simplex inside a unit ball truncated by a symmetric slab has an exact two-branch volume law, saturating at slab half-width \(1/\sqrt d\). Before saturation its unique equality geometry, up to orthogonal motion, comprises two orthogonal regular simplices on the opposing boundary rims, and its fraction of the truncated ball's volume decreases strictly with slab width.
