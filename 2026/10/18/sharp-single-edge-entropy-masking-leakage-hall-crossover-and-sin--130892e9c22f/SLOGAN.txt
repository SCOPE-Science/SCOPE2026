A single softly penalized transport edge has a sharp dimension-free worst-case mass error \(\sqrt t/(1+\sqrt t)\), where \(t=e^{-M/\varepsilon}\), and that same balanced Hall-boundary instance has the slowest local full-sweep Sinkhorn rate \(((1-\sqrt t)/(1+\sqrt t))^2\). Exact Hall slack restores the faster \(t\)-scale leakage law, revealing a sharp tradeoff between approximating a forbidden edge and conditioning the scaling iteration.
