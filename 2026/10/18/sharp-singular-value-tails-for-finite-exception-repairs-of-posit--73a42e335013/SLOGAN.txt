For a positive compact perturbation of \(cI\), the entire singular-value spectrum of the smallest positive \(\beta(H)\) repair that may retain \(m\) eigenvalues below \(c\) is exactly the negative spectral tail \((\kappa_{m+n})_{n\ge1}\), even against noncommuting competitors. Thus a Schatten-\(p\) repair exists exactly when the negative part lies in \(\mathcal S_p\), and the optimal finite-exception clipping realizes every rank and Schatten error simultaneously.
