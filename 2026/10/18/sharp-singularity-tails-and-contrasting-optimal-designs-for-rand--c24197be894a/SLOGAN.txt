In centered-rectangle reconstruction from \(m\) independent random support directions, the exact small-information tail is \(\Pr\{\lambda_{\min}(X^{\mathsf T}X)\le\varepsilon^2\}\sim\sqrt m\,\omega_{m-1}(\int f^m)\varepsilon^{m-1}\), uniquely minimized by uniform folded angles; unconstrained Gaussian least squares has finite unconditional squared error exactly for \(m\ge4\). In contrast, the unique angular law maximizing the expected Gram determinant samples only the two coordinate axes and has an unavoidable rank-deficiency probability \(2^{1-m}\).
