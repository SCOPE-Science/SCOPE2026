For the symmetric three-dimensional thread–sheet Lozi map, its known strict quadrant-changing two-cycle is a sink precisely when \(a^2(1+4b^2)<1\) and an index-two saddle when the inequality reverses (with \(0<b<1\) and \(a<2b-1\)). At equality, a two-dimensional center plane contains an **exact** nested family of locally invariant ellipses rather than a single bifurcating invariant circle.
