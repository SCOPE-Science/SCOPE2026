On the six-root \(A_2\) Fourier hexagon, every unimodular polynomial has sixth moment in the sharp interval \([546,2040]\); the minimum has exactly two character-phase orbits, both involving primitive sixth roots, while the maximum has one. A \(q\)-phase alphabet attains the unrestricted minimum exactly when \(6\mid q\), and the same theorem holds on every nonaliasing rectangular finite torus.
