The sharp \(L^2\)-to-\(L^6\) norm of three consecutive Fourier characters on a compact Abelian group has five exact torsion regimes: orders \(3\), \(4\), \(5\), \(6\), and \(\ge7\) (including infinite order). All extremizing coefficient triples are classified, and the fifth- and sixth-order exceptional constants exhibit genuine Fourier-aliasing thresholds.
