# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The exact reduced Markov generators, stationary measures, centered heat-flux Poisson solutions, and temperature derivatives reconstruct the two rational heat-capacity formulas. Their derivative factorizations give the sharp thresholds, uniqueness above threshold, and strict monotonicity otherwise. Independent symbolic full-state reconstruction and ten numerical relaxation-response checks corroborate the formulas. No limiting particle-number or thermodynamic claim is inferred from finite computations.

**Originality — PASS, with material residual risk.** Published active-calorimetry work establishes the concept of calorimetric shape-transition peaks; general finite-state quasipotential/Drazin techniques are also known. The assessed contribution is the exact two-versus-three-site threshold separation and full tumbling-rate classification. Search comparisons with the accessible prior findings and primary literature disclosed no equivalent explicit statement or dominating quantitative bound. The closest 2026 lattice study was accessible as an abstract and secondary scientific discussion but not as complete primary text. Its appendices could already contain the small-size thresholds, so originality remains provisional. The general finite-state method is not claimed as new.

**Value — PASS.** The size at which a driven confined run-and-tumble system first develops a response peak is a natural finite-system precursor to calorimetric shape-transition diagnostics. The exact intermediate interval shows that a peak is not a universal consequence of drive or confinement and supplies a testable control criterion, with no claim about larger systems.

**Closest literature and limits.** Santra (2026), arXiv:2608.17903, directly motivates the physical observable and exhibits general peaks; Dolai–Maes–Netočný (2023) and Khodabandehlou–Maes (2023) establish the calorimetry/quasipotential framework. Scope is restricted to the two and three-site unit-prefactor nearest-neighbor model, not a general lattice or arbitrary kinetic rate convention.
