For a reflecting, single-particle run-and-tumble chain, an interior tumbling-rate maximum of excess-heat capacity appears only above a sharp drive threshold: \(q_{3,c}=2.443045\ldots\) on three sites versus \(q_{2,c}=3.146264\ldots\) on two sites, where \(q=e^{f/(2T)}\). Thus an entire drive window has a unique calorimetric peak on three sites but no peak on two, with exact rational capacity formulas and an algebraic uniqueness proof.
