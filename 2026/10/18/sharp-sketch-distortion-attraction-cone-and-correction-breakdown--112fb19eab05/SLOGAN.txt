For the exactly solved, one-vector-restarted sketched Jacobi–Davidson correction on a real two-eigenvalue problem, a squared-norm embedding distortion of \(\epsilon\) permits uniform convergence to the intended eigenvector precisely inside the sharp open slope cone \(|t|<\sqrt{(1-\epsilon)/(1+\epsilon)}\). The exact worst-case next-step slope has an attained closed form, while an admissible diagonal sketch makes the boundary correction inconsistent and sends exterior starts to the other eigenvector.
