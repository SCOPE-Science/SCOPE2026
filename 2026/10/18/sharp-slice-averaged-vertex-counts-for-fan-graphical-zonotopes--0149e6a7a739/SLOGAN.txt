For the \(n\)-dimensional graphical zonotope of a fan graph, the number of vertices in a uniform-offset hyperplane section, averaged along any fixed normal, ranges sharply from \(4(n+1)3^{n-3}/n\) to \(2\cdot3^{n-2}\), with every extremizing normal classified. A universal degree-weighted formula for cones over trees explains the range, and the ratio of maximum to minimum approaches \(3/2\).
