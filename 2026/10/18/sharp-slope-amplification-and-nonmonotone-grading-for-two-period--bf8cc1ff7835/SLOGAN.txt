For linear-spline orthogonal projection on a bi-infinite grid with alternating knot gaps \(1,r\), the exact sharp bounded-input-to-Lipschitz norm is \(\max\{F(r),F(1/r)/r\}\), with \(F\) given by an explicit decaying-Green-kernel formula. Its sharp gradient amplification first rises above the uniform-grid value under slight grading, yet at spacing ratio \(2\) falls below it.
