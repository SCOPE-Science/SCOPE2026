For four normalized Gaussian Gabor atoms at the corners of a rectangle of small fixed phase-space area \(h\), the largest Gram determinant is asymptotic to \(5.1456237927599208\ldots h^4\); every optimal rectangle becomes strongly anisotropic, with its longer side approaching a nonzero universal length. A square of the same area achieves only \(\pi^6h^6/3+O(h^7)\), showing a sharp and counterintuitive loss of finite-atom volume under symmetric sampling.
