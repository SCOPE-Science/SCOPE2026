For every nondegenerate three-line centered Brownian last-passage functional, the lower tail has a sharp power law \(\mathbb P(F\le\varepsilon)=\Theta(\varepsilon^{2+2\pi/\arccos(-\rho)})\), where \(\rho\) is the correlation between successive Brownian differences. A positive-area terminal confinement event matches the earlier cone-based upper estimate and proves sharpness throughout the full correlation interval.
