PASS. The analytic proof first verifies the full Cartesian Jacobian cubic, shows the two auxiliary Routh–Hurwitz coefficients stay positive over the entire noncompact half-plane for small dissipation, obtains the exact discriminating coefficient, proves uniform coercivity from three strictly positive homogeneous forms, and invokes analytic minimizer and scalar implicit functions. The symbolic verifier independently reconstructs all polynomial identities and exact radicals. Boundary claims are pointwise and include the non-Hurwitz equality case.

## Originality

PASS under the inspected primary text and scoped overlap searches. The original 2026 paper proves Hurwitz at one parameter pair and nearby open parameters but does not derive the small-dissipation sharp shear ratio, the positive first correction, or the unique imaginary-spectrum circle. Other recovered claims concerning the same vector field determine basins, orbit escape and a Dulac parameter range, not the Jacobian Hurwitz boundary. The source's explicit coefficients and periodic-circle results are prior work. A different implicit derivation in earlier Hurwitz-polynomial literature remains possible.

## Mathematical value

PASS. Sharp shear scaling quantifies precisely how the periodic-orbit counterexample satisfies the stringent pointwise Hurwitz condition as radial dissipation tends to zero. The nonzero correction identifies a nontrivial curved rather than purely linear stability boundary; the loss occurs on a geometrically distinguished circle. This addresses a mathematically motivated parameter-range question left open by the construction rather than a numerical substitution.
