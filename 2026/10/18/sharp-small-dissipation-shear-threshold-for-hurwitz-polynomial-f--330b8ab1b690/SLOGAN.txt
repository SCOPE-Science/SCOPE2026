For the three-dimensional Hurwitz polynomial flow with a saddle cycle and an attracting cycle, the sharp small-dissipation angular shear threshold is \(e/\mu=\frac{5000-400\sqrt{62}}{21}+428.3966137550\ldots\mu+O(\mu^2)\). Exactly at the threshold the Jacobian loses strict Hurwitz stability on one rotational circle, while both periodic orbits persist.
