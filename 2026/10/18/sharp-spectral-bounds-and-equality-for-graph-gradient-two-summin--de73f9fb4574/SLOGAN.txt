For every finite connected simple graph, the absolutely two-summing norm of its real vertex-to-edge gradient is at least the Laplacian square-root trace divided by the square root of the edge count, with equality **exactly when** the edge half-resistances \(b_e^\top(L^\dagger)^{1/2}b_e\) are constant. This gives exact spectral norm formulas for all edge-transitive and all connected strongly regular graphs, including non-edge-transitive Chang graphs.
