# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The exact two-stage update is reconstructed from the stated tableau, including the stage-weight perturbation and the source's near-consistent quadratic root. The total-energy increment is calculated exactly as \(M_1-2cM_1+c^2M_2\), with discriminant equivalent to \(M_2\le M_1\). Modal amplification factors share the same branch parameter. If the highest active squared frequency does not exceed one, the weighted-average identity and uniform strict amplification ordering force exponential lower-frequency depletion while guaranteeing feasibility at every step. If it exceeds one, every feasible step boosts the highest-mode energy by a fixed factor greater than one; conservation of finite total energy forces a failed real-root request after finitely many steps. An explicit rational two-mode witness has a feasible first step and strictly infeasible second step. The packaged `verify.py` independently reconstructs source quadratic coefficients and corroborates the algebra; its finite tests are not substituted for quantified analytic proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
Najafian and Vermeire (arXiv:2401.14508v1) already introduced the RF-RK scheme, algebraic energy correction, local solvability, and qualitative energy transfer in Fourier-mode plots. Ketcheson (arXiv:1905.09847v1) previously developed a different relaxation RK modification and observed modal energy redistribution. This result does not claim those principles as new. The exact all-step modal concentration criterion, explicit uniform exponential energy-ratio bound, general finite-step infeasibility criterion, and exact feasible-then-infeasible oscillator pair are stronger oscillator-specific statements not found in the inspected source arguments. Targeted published-record and scholarly searches did not identify an equivalent dominating theorem. An equivalent older theorem phrased using a spectral filter or energy-orthogonal projection may exist; failed searches do not prove uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The finding provides a strong long-horizon obstruction for an invariant-preserving integrator: exact preservation of total quadratic energy is compatible with systematic transfer to the fastest active oscillator, followed by unavoidable loss of any real correction above a sharp modal boundary. Both a robust all-step regime and a finite-failure regime are characterized, including an explicit rational diagnostic. The result explains a motivated concern identified in the introducing paper rather than merely redoing its quadratic equation.
