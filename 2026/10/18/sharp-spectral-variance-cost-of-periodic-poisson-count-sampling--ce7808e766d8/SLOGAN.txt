For a sliding-window Poisson sampler with the *same* Poisson target at every time, every nonconstant periodic intensity raises the physical-time asymptotic variance of the quadratic factorial-count estimator by an explicit, strictly positive Fourier penalty. Constant intensity uniquely minimizes that variance, while arbitrarily concentrated pulses approach a sharp upper bound; by contrast, the linear count-average variance is insensitive to the modulation.
