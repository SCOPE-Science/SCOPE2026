The complete spherical covering radius of two regular \(n\)-gonal latitude rings with arbitrary relative twist is the arccosine of the smaller of their polar-facet and lateral-facet distances. Every twist has a unique optimal latitude; optimizing twist too forces half-step staggering and \(h^2=\cos(\pi/n)/(1+\cos(\pi/n))\), and for \(n\ge4\) the covering-optimal antiprism is not edge-uniform.
