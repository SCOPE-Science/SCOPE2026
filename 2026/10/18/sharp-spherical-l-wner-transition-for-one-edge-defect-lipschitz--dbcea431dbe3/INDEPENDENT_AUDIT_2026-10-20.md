# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For G=K_(p+2) minus one edge, p>=2, the centered potential ball has every vertex of the I(i,j), II(j) classes: oriented-incidence total unimodularity gives integral differences and the connected tight-edge characterization exhausts vertices. Projection squared norms are (i(p-i)/p,j(2-j)/2,(2i-pj)^2/(2p(p+2))) and (0,2j(2-j),2p(j-1)^2/(p+2)). Invariant log-determinant maximization gives exact positive KKT multipliers and feasible inverse squared semiaxes for p=2..5: (3/2,1/2,1), (1,1/2,5/8), (3/4,1/2,7/12), (13/21,1/2,1/2). Independently recomputed all 42 small-p orbit inequalities and positive multipliers using exact Fraction arithmetic. For p>=6, containing the equilateral dual ball forces at least its optimal sphere radius squared floor((p+2)^2/4)/(p+2), while all missing-edge type-II vertices have squared norm <=2. The radius is >=2 iff p>=6; equality for extra II(1) at p6 and strict extra-vertex interior for p>=7. Independently checked the bound for p=6..199. Finite checks corroborate the exhaustive symbolic all-p argument, not replace it. Centering and canonical Euclidean metric, not an inscribed ellipsoid or nonorthogonal anchored chart, are essential.
