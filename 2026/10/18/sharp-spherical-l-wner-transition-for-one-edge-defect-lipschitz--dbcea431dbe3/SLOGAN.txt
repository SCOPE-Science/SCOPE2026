For the Lipschitz dual unit ball of a unit-edge complete graph with one edge removed, the exact least-volume containing ellipsoid is anisotropic in the first four graph orders and becomes a Euclidean sphere precisely when the remaining clique has at least six vertices. At the threshold, the new vertices are still tangent; from the next order onward the enlarged ball and the equilateral ball share a containing sphere with all new vertices strictly inside.
