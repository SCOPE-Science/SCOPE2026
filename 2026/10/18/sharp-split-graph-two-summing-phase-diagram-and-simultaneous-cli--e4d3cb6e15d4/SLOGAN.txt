The real free two-convex lattice norm of the edge differences of a complete bipartite graph stays unchanged when an arbitrarily large clique-edge family is inserted into one part, exactly when each added amplitude is at most an explicit threshold \(\tau_{a,b}\). Beyond the threshold the full uniform-clique deformation has a closed radical norm formula, and for fixed clique size the sharp screened amplitude grows like \((b/a)^{1/4}/\sqrt2\) as the opposite part grows.
