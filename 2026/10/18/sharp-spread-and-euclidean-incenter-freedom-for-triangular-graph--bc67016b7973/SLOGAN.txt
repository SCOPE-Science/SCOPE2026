The sharp Euclidean inradius of the transportation ball over the triangular Johnson graph \(J(n,2)\) is \(\bigl((n-2)\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor/n\bigr)^{-1/2}\), and every contacting facet is indexed by a balanced bipartition of the underlying \(n\) points. Despite this exact contact classification, the set of centers of largest inscribed balls has affine dimension \(n(n-3)/2\).
