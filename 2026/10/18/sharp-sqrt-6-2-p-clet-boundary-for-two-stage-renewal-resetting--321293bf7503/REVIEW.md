# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS
The stated assumptions identify both the absorbing diffusion and the independent, two-stage renewal clock. The unreset Laplace transform, general renewal identity, and Erlang-two survival reduce the mean time to an explicit rational-exponential quotient. The complete derivative-series identity is verified symbolically; its first four coefficients have ordered positive roots, while the displayed polynomial formula proves strict positivity for every coefficient index at least seven. Dividing at the last negative coefficient gives a strictly increasing quotient and one global minimum; all boundary limits and the critical linear scaling are derived from the same exact expression. Independent inverse-Gaussian quadrature and high-precision optimizer comparisons agree with the formula. Computational samples are corroboration, not replacements for the sign proof.

## Originality — PASS (self-review)
Ray, Mondal and Reuveni's 2018 full treatment establishes the exponential-clock Péclet-one transition, but its reset clock lacks the two-stage waiting law. Pal and Reuveni's 2016 full treatment supplies general renewal machinery without the Erlang-two finite-rate global threshold. Linn and Lawley's 2023 full treatment includes gamma-two clocks for frequent resetting and driftless search, but not the target-directed drift optimizer with the exact square-root-six critical boundary. Evaluating a known reduction alone would not confer priority; the claimed contribution is the proof of the distinctive all-rate threshold, unique minimizer and critical scaling. The source texts and appendix material were compared with these implications, and no decisive coverage was found. General stochastic-restart literature could contain an unlocated equivalent specialization; this is a real residual priority risk, not a novelty guarantee.

## Scientific value — PASS
A two-exponential-stage renewal clock is the minimal nonmemoryless phase-type alternative to Poisson resetting and is experimentally interpretable as a two-step triggering mechanism. The sharp classification shows a regime of positive Péclet numbers between one and \(\sqrt6/2\) where two-stage restarting can improve transport even though the Poisson protocol cannot. The theorem excludes additional local optima for every admissible drift and establishes a parameter-sensitive linear onset. The result does not require broader-clock universality or a proposed application beyond this motivated comparison.
