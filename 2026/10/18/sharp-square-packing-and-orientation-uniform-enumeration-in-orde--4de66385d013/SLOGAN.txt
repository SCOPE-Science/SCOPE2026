In any order-three de Bruijn cycle over \(q\ge2\) symbols, at most \(\binom q2\) length-four factors are squares \(abab\), and each unordered pair of distinct symbols contributes at most one of \(abab\) and \(baba\). Every selection of one orientation per pair is realized, and all \(2^{\binom q2}\) maximal orientation classes have exactly the same explicit BEST/matrix-tree count.
