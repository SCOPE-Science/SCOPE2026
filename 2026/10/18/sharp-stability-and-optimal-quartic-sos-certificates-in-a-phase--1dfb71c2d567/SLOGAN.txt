For the angular-locked fourfold homogeneous cubic flow, global attraction begins at the sharp threshold \(a>\sqrt{\gamma^2-\omega^2}\), and throughout that full region a single explicit quartic has positive-definite SOS representations for both itself and its negative time derivative. In the nontrivial stabilization wedge \(\sqrt{\gamma^2-\omega^2}<a\le\gamma\), no globally positive-definite strict polynomial Lyapunov function of degree less than four exists.
