For degree-one orthogonal spline projection on the real line, a perfectly alternating two-length mesh has sharp \(L^\infty\) norm \(2-(a-b)^2/(3a^2+10ab+3b^2)\), strictly **below** the uniform-mesh value \(2\) whenever \(a\ne b\). The norm decreases monotonically toward the unattained extreme-contrast value \(5/3\), reversing the strict increase caused by a single isolated mesh defect.
