# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** The new claim fixes the graph, Euclidean metric, law of directional scores and every normalization. An omitted extreme pair forces a support loss at least equal to the smaller endpoint rank gap; a missing matching has exactly the next-best two surviving edges. Sorting labels are independent of spacings, which yields the exact universal Bernoulli-mixture lower law. Any incident pair of omitted edges supplies a positive-density strict witness. The Gaussian intrinsic-width identity uses the mean of the \(\chi_{n-1}\) radius. The normal endpoint integral and independent Poisson extreme spacings, with explicit hazard-rate uniform integrability, establish the exact and asymptotic constants. Exact finite cases and a seeded independent uniform-score benchmark were replayed successfully, but are not treated as universal proofs.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** D'Alì et al., arXiv:2201.09835, anchor the signed graph-root-polytope family and primary MSC; their main statements concern \(h^*\)- and \(\gamma\)-vectors. D'Alì--Delucchi--Michałek, arXiv:1910.05193, establish graph-root/adjacency/Kantorovich--Rubinstein equivalences and face/volume methods, not a width deletion extremum. Braun--Bruegge--Kahle, arXiv:2204.07239, study fixed-edge-count random graph facet statistics, while Ohsugi--Tsuchiya, arXiv:2008.08621, study matching enumerators. Kabluchko--Litvak--Zaporozhets, arXiv:1511.08479, supply the general Gaussian mean-width principle but not the forbidden-edge stochastic minimizer. Relevant indexed results on symmetric-edge inballs under sparse deletion and graphical-zonotope width variance address non-dominating invariants. Equivalent results under other terminology remain a risk; some indexed comparisons were summary-only.

**Value — PASS.** At a fixed edge-deletion budget, all choices have the same probability of removing the maximally exposed root but not the same support loss. The theorem solves the complete extremal choice problem for a natural metric invariant, upgrades it to a distribution-free stochastic statement, and quantifies the sharp asymptotic geometric cost. This is not a renamed facet count or an arbitrary selected parameter case.

**Limits.** The stated deletion budget is at most half the graph order; general weighted/affine root realizations and larger deletion budgets are not covered. Standard Gaussian extreme-value convergence and its required uniform integrability are argued analytically, while finite tests alone establish no infinite assertion. An independent literature audit and mathematical audit have not been performed.
