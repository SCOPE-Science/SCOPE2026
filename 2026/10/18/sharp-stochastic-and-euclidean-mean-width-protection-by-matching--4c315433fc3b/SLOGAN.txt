For a symmetric edge polytope of \(K_n\) with \(k\le\lfloor n/2\rfloor\) deleted edges, disjoint deletions uniquely maximize Euclidean mean width: the exact loss is \(4k\eta_n/[n(n-1)a_{n-1}]\), where \(\eta_n\) is the expected smaller of the two outer Gaussian order-statistic gaps. More strongly, matching deletions minimize the entire support-loss distribution for every continuous i.i.d. score law, with a sharp Gaussian width deficit asymptotic to \(2k/[n^{5/2}\sqrt{2\log n}]\).
