# Scientific review: balanced two-omission collection times

## Correctness — PASS
The claim quantifies over all integers \(n\ge4\), all balanced probability vectors on omitted pairs and every integer sampling horizon \(k\ge1\), with the package at first completion counted. Inclusion-exclusion gives the exact survival law \(\Pr(T_p>k)=n(2/n)^k-\sum p_{ij}^k\), since three distinct coupons cannot all be omitted in any single draw. Jensen's strict inequality uniquely identifies the uniform law as the slow-tail maximizer. Rescaling by \(2/n\) gives a fractional perfect matching, whose extreme points are the disjoint union of weight-one edges and weight-one-half odd cycles: the proof derives this from incidence-column independence and explicitly rules out even cycles. Strict convexity of the power sum and parity then identify the matching or matching-plus-triangle fastest law, including all equality cases for every \(k\ge2\). Geometric summation proves the mean bounds. The included checker passed 384 exact rational assertions, with independent direct enumeration at small sizes; these are checks and not the universal proof.

## Originality — PASS on inspected evidence
The closest established theorems and their implications were examined. Betken–Thäle (arXiv:2202.07485, first released 2022-02-15, later DOI 10.1017/jpr.2023.6) prove uniform-draw missing-count asymptotics, not finite exact optimality under prescribed single-coupon inclusion marginals. Berend–Sher (arXiv:2509.17201, later DOI 10.1080/15326349.2026.2671340) settle Schilling's unrestricted **mean** nonoptimality conjecture but do not determine the sharp **balanced** two-sided stochastic bounds or odd-size matching-plus-triangle equality class. Fang–Chang's 2014 half-package stochastic result overlaps only at the known \(n=4\) edge case. Falgas-Ravry–Larsson–Markström (arXiv:1601.04455) obtain general balanced coverage and concentration results, without this finite exact phase. Schilling's 2021 original mean paper was available through its abstract only; its full-text coverage remains a named risk. Targeted semantic-result searches found no equivalent or stronger claim, but absence from a database is not proof of novelty. A differently indexed classical extremal result remains possible.

## Value — PASS
The fairness constraint holds each coupon's observation probability fixed; the theorem resolves precisely how higher-order dependence among package omissions changes collection-time risk. It gives a natural **complete** sharp stochastic-order interval, not just an expectation comparison, for all admissible balanced designs, with exact all-time tails, sharp means, and parity-sensitive optimizer structures. The odd \(n\) extremizer is a nontrivial fractional-matching phenomenon (one triangle plus a matching), and both endpoints uniquely determine the optimal designs. The \(n=4\) half-package special case is not counted as a new contribution on its own. The theorem does not extrapolate to unbalanced package laws or other package sizes.
