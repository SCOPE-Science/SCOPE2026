For iid packages omitting two of \(n\) coupons, fixing every coupon's omission probability at \(2/n\) yields a complete sharp stochastic-order interval for the collection time: the uniform pair law is always slowest, while a perfect matching (even \(n\)) or a matching plus one triangle (odd \(n\)) is fastest. Both bounds hold at every sampling horizon, with exact tails, means, and unique optimizer classes up to label permutations.
