In the two-flux three-arm photonic lattice, a left boundary traps a nonzero surface pair exactly when \(c<v\) and \(u>c^2/v\); a finite \(N\)-cell chain detaches below the bulk squared band exactly when \(v>c(1+1/N)\). For equal hoppings, the critical flux is \(\arccos(1-3/[2(N+1)])\), and the emerging half-line surface energy separates from the bulk by \(J\phi^3/(6\sqrt2)+O(\phi^5)\).
