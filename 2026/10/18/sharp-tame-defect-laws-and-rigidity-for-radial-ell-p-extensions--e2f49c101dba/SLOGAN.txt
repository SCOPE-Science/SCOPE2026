The weak-null distortion of every normalized finite-dimensional radial \(\ell_p\) extension reduces exactly to a scalar variational formula; tameness forces the extension to be an isometric \(p\)-sum. For every nonzero finite-dimensional \(F\), the optimal multiplicative tame defect of \(F\oplus_q\ell_p\) is exactly \(2^{|1/p-1/q|}\), including \(q=\infty\).
