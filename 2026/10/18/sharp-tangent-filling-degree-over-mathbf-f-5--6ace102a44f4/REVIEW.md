## Value — PASS

The result exactly resolves the motivating minimum-degree question for the first odd prime field excluded by the general construction. The explicit smooth quartic proves the sharp bound in a field where the previously proposed degree-four construction fails; the exhaustive obstruction shows that degree three is impossible even without irreducibility or smoothness. This is a natural extremal existence-and-exclusion result, not an arbitrary parameter slice.
