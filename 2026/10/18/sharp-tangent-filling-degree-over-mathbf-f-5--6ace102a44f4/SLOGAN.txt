Over \(\mathbf F_5\), the minimum degree of an irreducible tangent-filling plane curve is \(4\): no degree-\(1\), degree-\(2\), or degree-\(3\) plane equation works, even if singular or reducible. The smooth genus-three quartic \(X^4+X^3Y+Y^3Z-Z^4=0\) attains the sharp bound with eleven rational tangent lines covering all thirty-one projective points.
