On a known tree with at most nine vertices, the expected size of an independently transmitted outbreak as a function of transmissibility uniquely determines the source up to symmetry; ten is the sharp first failure, with exactly two ambiguous source-orbit pairs in two different trees. For every tree through ten vertices, adding just the outbreak-size variance at transmission probability \(1/2\) resolves both exceptions, whose variance differences have magnitudes \(21/128\) and \(1/16\).
