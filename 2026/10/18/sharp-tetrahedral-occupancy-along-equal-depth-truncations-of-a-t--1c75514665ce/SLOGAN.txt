For every equal-depth vertex truncation of a tetrahedron, the exact largest contained tetrahedron occupies the fraction \(\frac{(1-t)(1-3t+3t^2)}{1-4t^3}\) of the remaining body. This sharp affine-invariant fraction has a unique, unexpectedly interior minimum at \(t\approx0.432030293635\), below both the Archimedean truncated-tetrahedron and rectified-octahedron values.
