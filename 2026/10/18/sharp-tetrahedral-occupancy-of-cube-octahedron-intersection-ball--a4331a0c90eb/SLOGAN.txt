For the three-dimensional intersection of the cube \([-1,1]^3\) and the \(\ell_1\)-ball of radius \(c\), the unrestricted maximal contained-tetrahedron volume has three exact polynomial branches, changing at \(c=3/2\) and \(c=2\). The tetrahedral occupancy ratio is globally at least \(1/6\), with equality uniquely at the regular truncated-octahedron member \(c=3/2\).
