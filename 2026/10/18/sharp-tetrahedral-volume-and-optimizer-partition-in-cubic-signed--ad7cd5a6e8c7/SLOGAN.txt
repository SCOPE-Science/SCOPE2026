Every polytope obtained as the signed-permutation orbit hull of \((1,1+q,1+2q)\), for \(q>0\), has exactly twelve largest contained tetrahedra: vertex-disjoint disphenoids that partition its 48 vertices, with common volume \(\frac83(1+q)(1+2q+2q^2)\). Their sharp occupied-volume fraction reaches a unique minimum at \(q\approx2.03113493\), and at the regular truncated cuboctahedron \(q=\sqrt2\) it equals \((1+14\sqrt2)/138\).
