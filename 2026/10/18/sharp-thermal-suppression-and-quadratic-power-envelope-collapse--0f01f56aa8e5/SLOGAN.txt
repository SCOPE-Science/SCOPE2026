In a one-dimensional run-and-tumble active bath, independent thermal diffusion abolishes every positive-power Fourier mode of a weak moving probe precisely at diffusivity \(D_c=v^2/(4\alpha)\), and below this threshold positive power is achievable even with a nonnegative localized interaction whenever \(0<|V|<\sqrt{v^2-4\alpha D}\). The optimally extractable leading power per unit interaction \(L^2\) norm vanishes as \(\frac{4\alpha^2}{9v^2}(1-D/D_c)^2\) after normalization by mobility and density.
