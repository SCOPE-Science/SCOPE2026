For three consecutive characters, the sharp \(L^2\)-to-\(L^4\) fourth-moment quotient jumps from \(3\) at character order \(3\), to \(7/3\) at order \(4\), and to \(15/7\) at every larger or infinite order. All extremizing coefficient triples are classified, and an exact nonnegative deficit identity explains their rigidity.
