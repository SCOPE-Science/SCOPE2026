# Scientific assessment: sharp three-person consensus windows

## Correctness — PASS

The first-then-neighbor activity rule yields exact unordered dyad probabilities \(q_{ij}\). The initially distant endpoints never interact before a bridge does. Once a bridge succeeds, the two coincident opinions either isolate the third agent irreversibly, or leave all three dyads permanently receptive. In the latter case, conservation of the arithmetic mean and a strictly contracting quadratic disagreement functional prove almost-sure consensus. The two strict success intervals are nonempty exactly above \(2/3\) and overlap exactly above \(3/4\); equality cases are handled explicitly. The attached independent exact-rational checker passed 23,670 comparisons. Finite testing is not used as the proof of infinite-time convergence.

## Originality — PASS, subject to residual priority risk

The motivating full-text paper of Li–Porter (arXiv:2206.09490, sections II, IV–V) provides the model and qualitative Monte Carlo fragmentation observations, not this exact conditional three-agent terminal lottery or two sharp confidence windows; indeed the discussion motivates activity/opinion alignment as a future direction. Lanchier–Li (arXiv:2005.13141) gives probability bounds in a differently sampled multivariate Deffuant process, not the stated exact law. Chen–Su–Mei–Bullo (arXiv:1901.02092) handles heterogeneous confidence thresholds rather than heterogeneous sampling weights. The Bernardo–Altafini–Proskurnikov–Vasca survey concerns mostly synchronous updates. Scientific-query searches and relevant existing mathematical findings did not identify stronger coverage. Standard dyad-conditioning and average contraction are credited as classical tools. Equivalence in less indexed finite-particle literature remains a nontrivial risk and a failed search is not proof of novelty.

## Value — PASS

This is a complete classification of the smallest nontrivial intermediate-confidence motif: two naturally distinguished cutoffs, every terminal outcome and its probability, plus a sharp sign reversal of the same activity intervention under mirrored initial social alignments. It identifies an exact mechanism behind model-dependent fragmentation, unlike a mere recomputation of a known mean or a randomly chosen finite table. Its scope is deliberately finite: no general network-size theorem is implied.

## Scientific limitations and closest literature

A general compromise fraction changes the conclusion; the theorem requires pairwise midpoint replacement. The strict contact boundary is part of the model, and initial endpoints at distance one are essential to the normalized presentation. The principal original article was inspected in its full accessible arXiv HTML representation, including model definition and conclusions, rather than inferred from snippets.
