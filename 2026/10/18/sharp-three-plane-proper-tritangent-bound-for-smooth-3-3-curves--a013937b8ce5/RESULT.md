# Sharp three-plane proper-tritangent bound for smooth \(3,3\) curves over \(\mathbf F_2\)

## Finding

Let \(Q=\mathbf P^1_{\mathbf F_2}\times\mathbf P^1_{\mathbf F_2}\subset\mathbf P^3_{\mathbf F_2}\) be the split quadric embedded by \(\mathcal O_Q(1,1)\), and let \(C=V(F)\subset Q\) be a geometrically smooth bidegree-\((3,3)\) curve. For a rational plane \(H\subset\mathbf P^3\), call \(H\) a **proper tritangent** when its scheme-theoretic canonical intersection divisor on \(C_{\overline{\mathbf F}_2}\) is twice a reduced degree-three effective divisor (three distinct geometric double contacts). Define \(\tau(C)\) as the number of these planes, whether or not the contact points are rational.

**Theorem.** Every such \(C\) has \(\tau(C)\leq3\). Among the \(65,535\) nonzero bidegree-\((3,3)\) binary equations, exactly \(16,020\) are geometrically smooth. Their complete proper-tritangent distribution is

| \(\tau(C)\) | Number of equations |
|---:|---:|
| 0 | 10,656 |
| 1 | 5,022 |
| 2 | 324 |
| 3 | 18 |

The eighteen maximizers form one orbit under \(\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathbf F_2}(Q)=(\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbf F_2)^2)\rtimes C_2\), of order \(72\), with stabilizer of order \(4\). Every maximizer has exactly one proper tritangent among the six planes with nonsingular conic section of \(Q\), and two among the nine planes tangent to \(Q\). All three contact divisors for the displayed representative below are supported on rational points.

## Assumptions and scope

The coefficient ring is precisely \(\mathbf F_2\), and all \(65,535\) nonzero sections of \(H^0(Q,\mathcal O_Q(3,3))\) are included. In this field there is no nontrivial global scalar rescaling. A smooth bidegree-\((3,3)\) curve is canonical of genus four: its dualizing bundle is \(\mathcal O_C(1,1)\). The theorem is **not** about non-split quadrics, singular canonical genus-four models, hyperelliptic curves, or rational planes tangent with nonreduced degree-three support. These latter even-contact planes are excluded from \(\tau\).

Write homogeneous coordinates \(([U:V],[S:T])\) and encode \(F\) by a 16-bit integer with bit \(4i+j\) multiplying \(U^{3-i}V^iS^{3-j}T^j\), for \(0\leq i,j\leq3\). An extremal representative is mask `10723`, namely

\[
\begin{aligned}
F={}&U^3S^3+U^3S^2T+U^2VS^2T+U^2VST^2\\
&+U^2VT^3+UV^2S^3+UV^2T^3+V^3S^2T.
\end{aligned}
\]

Its three proper rational tritangent planes, written as bilinear forms on \(Q\), are
\[
US+UT+VT=0,\qquad UT=0,\qquad V(S+T)=0.
\]
The first of these intersects \(Q\) in a smooth conic; the other two are planes tangent to \(Q\). The curve has seven rational points on \(Q\).

## Proof

**Exact characterization of proper contact.** Every rational plane in \(\mathbf P^3\) is given by a nonzero bilinear form \(B(U,V;S,T)\), hence corresponds to a \(2\times2\) matrix over \(\mathbf F_2\). There are \(15\) such planes, split into \(6\) invertible and \(9\) rank-one matrices.

For each invertible matrix, the hyperplane section of \(Q\) is a graph of a projective linear automorphism \(([S:T]=[aU+bV:cU+dV])\). Restricting \(F\) gives a homogeneous binary sextic \(G(U,V)\). Its intersection divisor is twice a reduced degree-three divisor exactly when \(G=g^2\) with \(g\) a squarefree homogeneous binary cubic. Over \(\mathbf F_2\), the square criterion is equivalent to vanishing of the three odd-indexed coefficients of \(G\). The squarefree condition is an exact projective gcd condition on \(g\), including its root at infinity.

For a rank-one matrix, the plane equation is \(L(U,V)M(S,T)=0\). Write \(u\in\mathbf P^1(\mathbf F_2)\) for the zero of \(L\), and \(s\) for the zero of \(M\). The two ruling lines on \(Q\) intersect at \(P=(u,s)\). Restricting \(F\) to these lines gives two binary cubics \(f_1(S,T)=F(u;S,T)\) and \(f_2(U,V)=F(U,V;s)\). The plane cuts on \(C\) the sum of the divisors of the two ruling-line intersections, each of degree three. It is an even divisor precisely when \(f_1=Mh_1^2\) and \(f_2=Lh_2^2\) for linear forms \(h_1,h_2\): all non-\(P\) roots must have even multiplicity, and the only possible unpaired root of either odd-degree cubic is \(P\). This divisor has three *distinct* double contacts precisely when both \(h_1(s)\) and \(h_2(u)\) are nonzero. In that case \(P\) is one contact and the two other contacts lie on distinct ruling lines. These finite binary polynomial tests are equivalent to geometric contact over the algebraic closure, with no extension-field sampling.

**Exhaustion with geometric smoothness.** The full automorphism group of \(Q\) is generated by transpositions and additions in each two-dimensional factor and interchange of the factors. These five explicit involutory or elementary generators partition all \(65,535\) nonzero masks into \(1,075\) orbits. For one representative from each orbit, construct the four affine dehomogenizations of \(F\). The curve is geometrically smooth if and only if, on each chart, the ideal generated by \(F\) and its two partial derivatives is the unit ideal; this is tested by exact Gröbner bases over \(\mathbf F_2\). Precisely \(252\) automorphism orbits, comprising \(16,020\) equations, pass.

For each passing representative, test all six invertible-matrix and nine rank-one planes by the criteria just proved, then weight its count by its orbit size. The resulting weighted histogram is exactly the table above. The more detailed split by (proper smooth-conic-section planes, proper tangent-to-\(Q\) planes) is

\[
(0,0):10656,\ (0,1):2088,\ (1,0):2934,\ (1,1):252,\ (1,2):18,\ (2,0):72.
\]

The only representative with three proper tritangents is mask `10723`, whose orbit has size \(18\). Its displayed three planes satisfy the two exact contact tests. Orbit-stabilizer gives its stabilizer order \(72/18=4\).

## Verification

Two reproducible standalone checkers are included. Running `python verify_full_census.py` uses the automorphism-orbit reduction, all-four-chart symbolic smoothness criterion, and all fifteen contact tests; it asserts the full histogram, the refined plane-type table and the unique maximal orbit. Running `python verify_direct_extremum.py` independently loops through all \(65,535\) equations without orbit reduction, applies separately programmed contact tests, and uses exact four-chart Gröbner bases for each candidate with at least three apparent proper tritangents. There are \(592,150,30,1\) such candidate equations with respective apparent counts \(3,4,5,6\), but only \(18\) are geometrically smooth, all with count \(3\); the least extremal mask is `10723`. The captured outputs are `full_census_output.txt` and `direct_extremum_output.txt`. The dependency is `sympy` as specified in `requirements.txt`.

## Relationship to prior work

Kulkarni–Ren–Sayyary Namin–Sturmfels (2017, arXiv:1712.06274), especially the sections on tritangent computation and binary sextic contact, treats classical real/complex space sextics. The restriction-to-a-square principle is prior mathematics; the present finite-characteristic, fifteen-rational-plane census and bound are different. Xarles (2020, arXiv:2007.07822) enumerates genus-four curves over \(\mathbf F_2\) and their arithmetic data. Its relevant full-text sections do not state the proper-tritangent plane distribution among split-quadric models. The earlier count of smooth \((3,3)\) equations and associated automorphism orbits is not claimed as new; the newly established assertion is the contact spectrum and extremal orbit.

## Limitations

This is an exact finite-field calculation for the specified split quadric, not a general upper bound for curves over arbitrary finite fields. The two checkers share the same symbolic algebra library for geometric smoothness, but independently implement the contact enumeration and select smoothness tests by different procedures; the Gröbner criterion also has a separate mathematical justification. Novelty is based on the inspected literature and available indexed records, not on a proof that no unindexed historical census exists. Independent external verification has not been performed.
