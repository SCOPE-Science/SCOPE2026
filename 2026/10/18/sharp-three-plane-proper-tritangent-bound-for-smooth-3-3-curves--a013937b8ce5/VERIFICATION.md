# Verification of the binary genus-four tritangent spectrum

Run `python verify_full_census.py` and `python verify_direct_extremum.py` with `sympy` installed according to `requirements.txt`. Both scripts completed with their final PASS markers, and their captured outputs accompany this record.

The full census enumerates \(65,535\) coefficient masks, explicitly constructs \(1,075\) automorphism orbits, tests \(1,075\) representatives for geometric smoothness on four affine charts, and applies all \(15\) plane tests to each smooth representative with orbit weights. The independent extremal search performs a direct unweighted equation scan, independently computes the fifteen contact predicates, and tests each equation admitting at least three candidate planes for geometric smoothness with Gröbner bases. This confirms the complete extremal count (and the absence of more than three) without using the first script's group calculations.

Proof-level checks: a rational plane section of the split quadric has matrix rank one or two; rank two gives the binary sextic square-of-squarefree-cubic criterion, while rank one gives two degree-three ruling-line restrictions with the exact square-linear factorization and nonvanishing at the meeting point. The ideal of a singular point in a smooth chart is generated by the affine equation and its two derivatives; the unit-ideal test over \(\mathbf F_2\) excludes points over the full algebraic closure.

Limits: these are ordinary deterministic finite scripts, not independent third-party certifications. The two checkers depend on the same symbolic Gröbner implementation; their contact-enumeration approaches and coefficient scans are distinct. No claim is made about fields other than \(\mathbf F_2\).
