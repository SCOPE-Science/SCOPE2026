On the two-hub network \(K_{2,m}\), optimizing the worst endpoint effective resistance after any one edge deletion admits an exact globally optimal conductance allocation with two sharp exceptional-route price thresholds. Across the entire interval \(2(m-1)/(m^2-3)\le t\le(m-1)^2/2\), equal conductances remain optimal despite unequal prices; outside it, the unique symmetric optimum reallocates according to explicit square-root laws.
