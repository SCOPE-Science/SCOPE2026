# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
For all odd integers \(n\ge3\), all widths \(w>0\), and all three real rotations, Fourier support calculus gives the exact two-copy area kernel and the three-copy deficit identity. A constrained critical-point argument reduces possible three-gap minima to an equally spaced interior point and boundary candidates. The strict boundary comparison follows from a coefficientwise inequality on convergent Fourier sums. Curvature averaging identifies the maximizing body and the exact area expression; a monotone elementary function establishes the order trend and the Taylor expansion gives the asymptotic. The standalone regression code passed 42 Fourier instances, 30,000 sample triples, 9 independent boundary constructions, and six asymptotic/hierarchy checks. These finite computations do not establish universal quantifiers, which are proved mathematically.

## Originality — PASS (available-evidence priority assessment)
Resnikoff supplies the Fourier framework and classical constant-width/Reuleaux geometry, not three-summand global phase optimization. The published exact two-copy rotation profile concerns two summands of a Reuleaux triangle; its full result was inspected and does not imply the unique three-summand maximizer for all odd orders. Falco–Myroshnychenko address minimum-area bodies within symmetry-constrained classes, not this fixed-summand Minkowski area maximum. Related random rotations of polygon intersections and regular-polygon unions optimize different operations. Primary full-text and indexed-research comparisons found no covering theorem. Older unindexed works may contain equivalent statements; such priority risk remains.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Three-rotation Minkowski averaging is the first genuinely multi-angle extension of the classical two-body mixed-area profile. Exact global phase rigidity, an explicit optimal body with triple symmetry order, and the sharp area deficit across every odd order resolve a natural nontrivial family rather than a special numerical example. The strict optimum includes all rotations and widths, not a restricted parameter grid.

## Scientific limitations
No universal result for unequal weights, arbitrary bodies of constant width or four-plus summands. The finite checker is a regression tool, not an exhaustive certificate. Historical priority cannot be established from indexed search alone. Independent scientific audit has not been performed.
