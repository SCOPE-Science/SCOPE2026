Among all Minkowski means of three arbitrarily rotated copies of a regular Reuleaux odd-\(n\)-gon, the unique area-maximizing rotation pattern is equal spacing modulo \(2\pi/n\). The resulting body is exactly a disk–Reuleaux \(3n\)-gon Minkowski blend, and its disk-area deficit is one ninth of that of the regular Reuleaux \(3n\)-gon.
