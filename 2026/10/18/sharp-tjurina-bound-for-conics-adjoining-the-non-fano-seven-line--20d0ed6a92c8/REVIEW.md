# Scientific review: sharp non-Fano conic-extension Tjurina bound

## Correctness
**PASS.** The proof first reconstructs the unique non-Fano combinatorial type from seven lines with six triples, normalizes four high lines and the three perfect-matching low lines, then exhausts every possible number of original double points lying on a new conic. A dual-conic determinant argument excludes the only case \(h=2,t=4\), while discriminants of the conics through all three old doubles exclude \(h=3,t\ge3\). Thus \(\tau=41+h+t\le46\) for the full complex geometric class. A separate explicit smooth conic has \(h=2,t=3\); exact arithmetic confirms every contact and other singularity. The proof is characteristic-zero and does not assert anything about other line configurations.

## Originality
**PASS, with bibliographic residual risk.** The nearest primary source, Dimca–Pokora (2022), Proposition 5.6, proves nonfreeness of any seven-line/one-conic arrangement with the allowed singularities and identifies the non-Fano extremal line configuration under a *free* hypothesis. Its proof excludes the stronger condition of all four high lines tangent and the conic through all three old doubles, but does not prove the sharp \(\tau\le46\) statement or exhibit equality, and it does not settle the \(\tau=47\) near-free boundary. Gałecka (2022), Section 5, leaves degree-nine near-freeness open generally. Bannai–Guerville-Ballé–Shirane (2024) supply two seven-line/one-conic Zariski pairs with only three or five original line triples, whereas the present object has six. Lampa-Baczyńska–Wojcik (2024) address a six-line/one-conic near-free class. Published related records on non-Fano quartics, degree-eight near-free arrangements, and seven-line/one-conic arrangements admitting ordinary quadruple points have different hypotheses and do not imply this bound. Targeted claim/alias/parameter searches returned no covering result; search absence is not proof of novelty.

## Value
**PASS.** The object is the uniquely realizable seven-line combinatorial type with six ordinary triple points, precisely the borderline type arising in the known free classification. The previously unresolved degree-nine near-freeness issue motivates the invariant \(\tau\) and its exact cutoff \(47\). The sharp upper bound \(46\), attained by an explicit complex smooth conic, gives a complete extremal fact for this natural stratum and a substantive near-free obstruction. It is not a routine numerical substitution, and it does not claim the much harder full degree-nine classification.
