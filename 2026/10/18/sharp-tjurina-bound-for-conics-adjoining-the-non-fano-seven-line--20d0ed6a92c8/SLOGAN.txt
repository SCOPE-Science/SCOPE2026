For every smooth conic adjoining a non-Fano seven-line arrangement with only nodes, tacnodes, and ordinary triple points, the total Tjurina number is at most \(46\); a conic over \(\mathbb Q(i)\) attains equality with singularity basket \((5,3,8)\). This sharp bound excludes nearly free degree-nine arrangements in the six-triple-point line stratum, without addressing all degree-nine arrangements.
