When the rightward parking probability approaches \(1/2\) at rate \(n^{-1/2}\), the total-variation distance of the last preferred location from uniform has a sharp curved crossover: \(n\,\mathrm{TV}\to(1+\sqrt{1+32t^2/\pi})/2\). For any fixed noncentral probability, \(\sqrt n\,\mathrm{TV}\to2\sqrt{2/\pi}|p-1/2|\), making both known rate regimes quantitatively exact.
