In the stable two-population nonreciprocal flow, the **exact** worst-case transient gain and its unique peak time are computable in closed form; a damped exceptional-point approach produces a nonconstant universal crossover \(kG\to 2e^{-s\arctan(1/s)}/\sqrt{1+s^2}\), where \(s\) is damping divided by oscillation frequency. These results are for the linearized dynamics, not finite-amplitude nonlinear responses.
