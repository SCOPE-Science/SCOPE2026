For planes chosen by Euclidean-motion-invariant measure and conditioned to hit a rectangular box, the chance of a triangular section is strictly increased by balancing unequal side lengths; only the cube maximizes it. The sharp cube value is \(2-4\sqrt2\arctan\sqrt2/\pi\), and the exact fixed-sum quadratic stability coefficient is \(\arctan\sqrt2/(\pi\sqrt2)-1/(3\pi)\).
