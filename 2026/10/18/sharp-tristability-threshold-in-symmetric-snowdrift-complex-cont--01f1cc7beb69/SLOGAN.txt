In the symmetric Snowdrift game with payoff-biased complex contagion, conformity with \(1<\gamma<2\) produces exactly three long-run attractors—two pure states and one stable coexistence state—precisely when \(2(\gamma-1)<\alpha c<2\). The theorem proves the complete basin partition and the sharp critical equality, with an exact five-equilibrium example at \(0,1/5,1/2,4/5,1\).
