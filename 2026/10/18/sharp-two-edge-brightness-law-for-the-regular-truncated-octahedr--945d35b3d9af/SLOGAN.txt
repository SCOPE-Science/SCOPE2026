For the Archimedean truncated octahedron, a uniformly random planar shadow has a precisely uniform top brightness tail starting at \(10\sqrt2\), with a complete two-branch elementary law down to brightness \(14\). Its first cap-overlap correction has exponent \(3/2\), whereas the opposite minimum-brightness tail has exponent \(2\) and exact coefficient \(7\sqrt2/(64\pi)\).
