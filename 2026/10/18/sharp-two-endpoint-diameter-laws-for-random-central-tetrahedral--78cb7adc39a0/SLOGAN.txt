The diameter of a uniformly oriented central section of a regular tetrahedron has sharp and unequal endpoint laws: its lower tail grows as \(6\varepsilon/\pi\), while its upper tail grows as \(3\varepsilon^2/(\pi\sqrt2)\), in the specified edge-length normalization. The rare minimizing planes have square-shaped local direction fluctuations, whereas the maximizing edge planes have an anisotropic rectangular limit law.
