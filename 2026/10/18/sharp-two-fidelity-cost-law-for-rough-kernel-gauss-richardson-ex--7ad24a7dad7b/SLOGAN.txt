For the first-order Gauss–Richardson extrapolation model with exponential error kernel and reciprocal-fidelity simulation cost, optimally placed two simulations reduce fixed-scale posterior variance from the single-simulation order \(C^{-2}\) to the sharp order \(12\sqrt3/(\ell C^3)\). Their unique asymptotically optimal coarse-to-fine fidelity ratio is \(2+\sqrt3\), despite the rough covariance kernel.
