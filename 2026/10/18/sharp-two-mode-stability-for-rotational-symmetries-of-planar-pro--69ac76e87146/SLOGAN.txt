The second and third great-circle Fourier modes give a sharp quantitative rigidity theorem for three-dimensional convex support functions: their joint mean-square measurement is equivalent to distance from balls modulo translation in the negative half-order Sobolev norm, with best constants \(1/(2\pi^2)\) and \(35/(128\pi)\). Degree-three harmonics attain the upper constant, while arbitrarily high harmonics show that the lower constant and the half-derivative loss are both sharp.
