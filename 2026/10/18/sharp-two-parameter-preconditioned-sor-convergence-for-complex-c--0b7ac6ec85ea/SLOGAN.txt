For block-preconditioned SOR whose real generalized spectrum consists of one nonreal conjugate pair, the exact globally optimal spectral contraction is \(R_*=(1-\sqrt{1-q^2})/(1+\sqrt{1-q^2})\), where \(q\) is determined by the Cayley distance of the eigenpair from \(i\); a unique finite real preconditioner and relaxation attain the rate whenever the pair has nonzero real part. The tuning can restore convergence even where every unpreconditioned real SOR relaxation diverges.
