For two-periodic, reciprocal-step primal-first PDHG on a scalar bilinear saddle, every frozen update is Schur stable when the step product lies in \((0,1)\), yet the periodic trajectory can diverge. There is an exact universal jump-ratio threshold \(q_*=2.55249898195048\ldots\), above which a fully classified interval of otherwise admissible step products produces an unstable two-step monodromy.
