Double-star trees have an exact two-phase minimum-distortion profile under a prescribed nonnegative weighted Lin--Lu--Yau curvature floor: the less-populated center's leaf edges stay at unit weight, while the opposite leaf edges begin increasing at an explicit threshold \(k_*=4/(2b+1+\sqrt{1+4a(b+1)})\) for \(a<b\). The optimal central-edge weight—and hence the distortion—is continuous but has a sharp upward derivative jump at that interior threshold.
