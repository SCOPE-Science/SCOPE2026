For complex affine polynomials on any proper circular arc \(K_\theta\), the smallest norm of a two-sample interpolation projector is exactly \(\sqrt{1+\sin^2(\theta/2)}\). The unique optimal nodes lie at angles \(\pm2\arctan(\sin(\theta/2))\), strictly inside the arc, while using its endpoints has norm \(\sec(\theta/2)\) and can be arbitrarily ill-conditioned.
