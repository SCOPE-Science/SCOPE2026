For the two-convex free Banach-lattice supremum of edge differences on \(K_{a,a,b}\), the exact norm is a one-parameter weighted-Laplacian square-root trace with a unique maximizing weight. Edges between the two equal vertex classes become entirely norm-redundant precisely at the sharp integer threshold \(b\ge2a+2\), although those edges remain pointwise active and the corresponding Löwner-ellipsoid cutoff is one integer lower.
