For every rectangular cycle-product network, the unique globally resistance-optimal conductance allocation is constant within each of the two edge orientations, with an exact scalar spectral equation determining their ratio. At fixed short circumference, the optimal budget fraction spent on transverse edges decays sharply as \(m^{-2/3}\) with an explicit constant, and the optimized trace of the Laplacian pseudoinverse approaches one-half the cost of uniform conductances.
