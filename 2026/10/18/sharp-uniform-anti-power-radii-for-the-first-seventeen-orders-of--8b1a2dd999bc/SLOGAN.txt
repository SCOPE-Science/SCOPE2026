For the regular paperfolding word, the sharp worst-position block-length radius for order-\(k\) anti-powers equals \(2,5,6,7,9,15\) on \(k=2\), \(3\), \(4\), \(5\!\le k\!\le8\), \(9\!\le k\!\le16\), and \(17\), respectively. In particular, the exact radius jumps from \(9\) at order \(16\) to \(15\) at order \(17\); finite exhaustive certificates become infinite-position proofs using the established exact paperfolding factor complexity.
