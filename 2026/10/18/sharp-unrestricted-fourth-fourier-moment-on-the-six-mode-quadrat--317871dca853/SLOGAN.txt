The six-term analytic quadratic on the bidisk has sharp unrestricted fourth-moment amplification \(\mathcal K=2.949235612050376\ldots\), strictly greater than its flat-amplitude value \(17/6\). Every extremizer has equal corner magnitudes, equal midpoint magnitudes in the unique ratio \(\rho=1.402255788644281\ldots\), and affine-character phases; the same classification holds on every nonaliasing finite rectangular torus.
