For every crown graph \(K_{n,n}\) minus a perfect matching with \(n\ge5\), the lower and upper edge-domination numbers are respectively \(n-1\) and \(2n-4\). Exactly \(nD_{n-1}\) deranged matchings are minimum, while exactly \(n(n-1)\) two-star forests are maximum inclusion-minimal edge-dominating sets, giving an unbounded gap of \(n-3\).
