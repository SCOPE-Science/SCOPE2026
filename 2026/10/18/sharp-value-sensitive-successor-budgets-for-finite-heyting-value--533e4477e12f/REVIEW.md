# Review of sharp value-sensitive successor budgets

## Correctness

PASS. A finite distributive algebra is exactly the lattice of down-sets of its join-irreducible poset. The proof explicitly shows why retaining one witness for each minimal missing generator in each coordinate preserves all simultaneous meets. A disjoint-leaf construction supplies an indispensable witness for every boundary element of every chosen exact output value, so pointwise and global bounds are tight. The fixed-instance characterization has both implications of the hitting-set equivalence. Membership and the star-frame reduction prove NP-completeness. A checked finite program provides independent exact arithmetic on four small posets, but the proofs, not enumeration, establish generality.

## Originality

PASS under best-knowledge comparison, with historical-priority caveats. Das–Ray–Mali (arXiv:2608.05550v1, Sections 5.1–5.2, inspected primary full text) establish the less precise height-based bound, not the boundary-count minimum, sharp simultaneous construction or optimal-pruning complexity. Birkhoff representation and lattice breadth are credited as classical mathematics. Gurfinkel–Chechik (2003) is different De Morgan-algebra CTL counterexample literature, inspected at the accessible abstract/bibliographic level only; lack of full-text comparison is expressly a residual risk. Targeted published-research-record queries and source searches showed no equivalent claim. This is not proof that no unindexed formulation exists.

## Value

PASS. Exact modal truth-value extraction is a defined current research problem. The result improves a concrete 2026 branching guarantee from the potentially large lattice-height quantity to the sharp join-irreducible width and further to an output-specific antichain boundary sum. For chain-valued logics, this can reduce the per-box bound from the number of strict lattice levels to just one; for unrestricted finite instances, it supplies an exact hypergraph reduction and a meaningful tractability obstruction. It is an interpretable structural and algorithmic boundary rather than a routine recomputation of a known table.

## Closest literature and limitations

The 2026 source's Theorem 5 and Lemma 7 already provide height-sensitive tree certificates. The exact locally optimal hypergraph instance need not yield a globally minimum modal model. Classical lattice-breadth facts are excluded from novelty claims, and unindexed older formulations cannot be ruled out. The finite computations are cross-checks only.
