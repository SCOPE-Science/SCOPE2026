On a finite intuitionistic fork, a root's entire \(n\)-variable theory is determined exactly by its atomic color and the set of distinct colors at its leaves, giving an explicit binomial-sum census. Forks with \(k\) and more leaves become indistinguishable to the \(n\)-variable logic exactly when \(k\geq2^n\); below this cutoff, finite minterm formulas separate every successive branching level.
