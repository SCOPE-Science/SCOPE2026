For particle-only resetting against an independently switching gate on a reflecting interval, weak resetting improves mean absorption precisely when the scaled restart location lies below one unique threshold \(a_*\), and every positive switching-rate pair obeys \(1-1/\sqrt3<a_*<1-1/\sqrt5\). Unlike protocols that also restart the gate, gating here always shrinks the weak-resetting improvement region relative to an ungated absorber.
