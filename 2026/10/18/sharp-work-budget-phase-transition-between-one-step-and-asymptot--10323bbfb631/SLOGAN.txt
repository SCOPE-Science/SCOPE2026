At equal expected update work, the exact one-step and long-run mean-square-optimal sampling laws for two-row randomized Kaczmarz differ whenever the row coherence lies strictly between \(1-2/w\) and \(\sqrt{1-2/w}\), for a full-batch work cost \(w>2\). The former prefers full-batch-or-skip updates while the latter prefers equally weighted singleton-or-skip updates; this sharp reversal occurs even with consistent equations and exact arithmetic.
