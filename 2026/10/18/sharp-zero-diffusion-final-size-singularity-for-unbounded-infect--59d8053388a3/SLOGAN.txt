In the unrestricted infectivity-diffusion SIR equation, any \(D>0\) produces a full eventual attack rate for every nonzero compactly supported initial infectivity distribution, while \(D=0\) or a fixed hard infectivity cap leaves a strictly positive susceptible fraction. The exact identity \(\operatorname{Var}_t(\lambda)=\operatorname{Var}_{\rho_0^{A(t)}}(\lambda)+Dt\) explains the zero-diffusion singularity; the unlimited trait tail is a mathematical idealization, not a biological prediction.
