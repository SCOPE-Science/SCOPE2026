For every \(n\geq3\), the shortest legal cyclic binary word covering every length-\(n\) factor avoiding \(010\) has exactly \(E_n+C_n\) positions, where \(\sum E_nz^n=(1+z^2)/(1-2z+z^2-z^3)\) and \(\sum_{n\geq3}C_nz^n=z^3(2-z)/(1-z)^2+z^5(1+z)/((1-z)^2((1-z)^2-z^3))\). An explicit shortest-path matching balances the restricted overlap graph and produces optimal Eulerian covering cycles.
