The ordinary Möbius–Kantor matching complex has exactly \(912\) induced octahedral four-spheres on the minimum possible \(10\) vertices, and every fundamental class is primitive in \(\widetilde H_4\cong\mathbb Z^8\). Their ten graph-symmetry orbits have ranks \(8,7,7,7,4,8,8,8,7,7\), and each of four full-rank orbits separately generates the entire integral homology group.
