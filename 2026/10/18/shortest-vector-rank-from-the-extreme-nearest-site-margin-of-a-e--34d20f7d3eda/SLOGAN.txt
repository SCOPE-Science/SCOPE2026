For a uniform point in the Voronoi cell of any full-rank Euclidean lattice, the probability that the squared first-versus-second nearest-site gap is within \(\varepsilon\) of its maximum is \(C\varepsilon^r+O(\varepsilon^{r+1})\), where \(r\) is the dimension spanned by shortest lattice vectors and \(C>0\) has an explicit convex-volume formula. The endpoint distribution therefore detects well-roundedness, and its tail is an exact polynomial near the endpoint.
