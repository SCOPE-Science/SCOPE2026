The fourth integral homology of the open neighborhood complex of \(T_7=L(K_7)\) is \(\mathbb Z^7\), while its rational seven-point symmetry is the **sign-twisted** natural permutation module \(\operatorname{sgn}\otimes\mathbb Q^{[7]}\). Its character is \(\chi(g)=\operatorname{sgn}(g)|\operatorname{Fix}(g)|\), a distinction invisible to previously computed mod-two Betti numbers.
