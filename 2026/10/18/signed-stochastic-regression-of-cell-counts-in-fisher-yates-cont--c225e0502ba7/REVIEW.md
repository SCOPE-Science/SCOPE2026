# Self-review

## Correctness (PASS)
The nested-subset hypergeometric representation supplies an explicit finite-sample coupling for each of the two cell geometries. The order of conditional tails follows for all admissible margins, not merely asymptotically, and the symmetrization identity yields association signs for arbitrary nondecreasing transforms. Exact exhaustive finite enumeration verified 48,818 comparisons; the all-size claim relies on the coupling proof. Boundaries of the hypergeometric supports are handled by restricting to attainable conditioning values.

## Originality (PASS, qualified)
Diaconis–Simper (arXiv:2102.04576) already provides the Fisher–Yates distribution and asymptotic cell-covariance geometry. Paguyo (arXiv:2112.07728) points to zero-cell positions as an interesting question; Simper (arXiv:2208.10700) studies the same table law through a Markov chain. None of their inspected relevant statements asserts this full finite-sample conditional stochastic order. Stronger classical multivariate hypergeometric or negative-dependence results might implicitly imply the theorem; independent priority determination remains open. The ordinary cell-covariance identities are not claimed as individually new.

## Value (PASS)
The classification controls all pairwise zero-position correlations and all increasing cell statistics for arbitrary positive margins, providing more information than familiar count covariances. Its two-stage finite-population mechanism explains why shared-row/column cell pairs repel while disjoint pairs attract. It is a natural, complete pairwise dependence theorem motivated by the spatial distribution of zeros.

## Limitations
The theorem neither asserts global multivariate association nor covers the uniform-on-tables measure. Covariance inequalities may be equalities for degenerate transformed variables, and exhaustive small cases alone do not establish the general result.
