For the crown graph obtained by deleting a perfect matching from \(K_{n,n}\), the complete signed total Roman weight enumerator has an explicit nine-type multinomial formula, and its minimum weight is exactly \(6\) for every \(n\ge3\). The number of minimum functions is given by closed parity-sensitive binomial sums, beginning \(10,84,900,3280,44100\) for \(n=3,4,5,6,7\).
