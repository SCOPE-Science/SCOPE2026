For every unweighted complete multipartite graph with at least three equally sized parts of size at least two, the Euclidean cycle and cut projections simultaneously minimize operator norm in every real \(\ell_q\) edge geometry. Their exact \(\ell_1\) and \(\ell_\infty\) constants have two-parameter rational formulas, and the cycle constant switches from increasing to decreasing behavior as the number of parts changes.
