Deleting one Chebyshev–Lobatto interpolation sample changes logarithmic Lebesgue growth to sharp linear growth, with asymptotic coefficient \(2\) for an endpoint loss, \(1\) for a loss whose index recedes from both endpoints, and an explicit strictly larger-than-one boundary-layer coefficient \(\rho_j\) for a fixed interior distance \(j\). The classification is uniform over every erased-node index, and the boundary coefficients satisfy \(\rho_j=1+3/(8\pi^2j^2)+O(j^{-4})\).
