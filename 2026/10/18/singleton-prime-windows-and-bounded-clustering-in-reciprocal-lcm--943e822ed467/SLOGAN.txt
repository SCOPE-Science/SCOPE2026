For every fixed \(\kappa>5\), the normalized reciprocal LCM endpoints have an explicit nonempty interval of parameters selecting any sufficiently large prime to exponent one; simultaneous exponent-one primes lie within asymptotic distance \((\kappa-5)/6\). Consequently singleton-prime support is uniformly bounded, and for \(5<\kappa<17\) infinitely many late endpoints have exactly one singleton prime while no late endpoint has more than one.
