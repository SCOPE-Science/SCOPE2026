For every integer box section \(\sum_i x_i=k\), the normal-fan toric variety has only isolated singularities: they occur exactly at box-corner subsets with cap sum \(k\) and cardinality between \(2\) and \(n-2\), and each full affine chart is a rank-one determinantal cone. A single subset-sum polynomial gives the entire singularity basket, including multiplicities and the distinction between Gorenstein and non-\(\mathbb Q\)-Gorenstein points; nonuniform caps can create both types in one projective variety.
