# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The claim is a complete reduced-singular-locus classification for all complex \(\operatorname{sGr}(2,n)\), \(n\ge5\), with specified generic quotient types and Gorenstein parity. The ring-theoretic premise is the published prime ideal of the hollow symmetric rank-three matrix scheme. The extra needed normality argument is reconstructed from the product of normal quadratic cones and exact invariants for the linearly reductive orthogonal group. The Plücker-relation calculation identifies every generic square fiber with its column-sign orbit, so the normal quotient is the original projective variety, not merely its normalization. The complete involution fixed-locus partition, minus-eigenspace dimensions, and absence of pseudoreflections establish the set-theoretic singular support and quotient types. The Reid–Tai and determinant criteria yield canonical, nonterminal, and Gorenstein-parity conclusions. Exact finite checks in `verify.py` test fibers, tangent dimensions, and four representative Jacobian ranks; they are not offered as an infinite proof. Remaining limits are component-intersection schemes and positive characteristic.

## Originality — PASS, with residual bibliography risk
Kulesza–Taskar's 2012 projection determinantal processes already give the squared minors. Al Ahmadieh–Vinzant 2021 give the general squared-Grassmannian definition and hyperdeterminantal relations; Devriendt–Friedman–Reinke–Sturmfels 2024/2025 give the hollow symmetric determinantal defining ideal and degree. The published record "Eleven singular surfaces on the smallest squared Grassmannian" directly covers \(n=4\), so it is excluded from the new claim. Targeted equivalence, general-rank, quotient, and singular-component searches did not locate a covering claim for all \(n\ge5\). The new classifies zero-row and bipartition Segre components, generic different transverse quotient dimensions, and parity-dependent Gorensteinness. An unindexed equivalent treatment or implications from deeper determinant-quotient literature remain possible; missing hits alone do not prove uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The projected rank-two Grassmannian is a classical family with direct projection-DPP significance. A uniform singular-component classification as \(n\) varies distinguishes a codimension-two branch from growing-codimension mixed branches, gives a closed formula for every component and its dimensions, and identifies canonical/nonterminal and Gorenstein behavior. These geometric consequences are not merely recalculations of the published ideal or degree. The result may inform resolution, duality, and likelihood-geometry studies, although no such downstream theorem is claimed.
