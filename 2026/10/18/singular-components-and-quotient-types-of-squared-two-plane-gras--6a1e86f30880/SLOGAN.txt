For every \(n\ge5\), the coordinatewise-squared Plücker image of \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,n)\) has exactly \(n+2^{n-1}-1\) irreducible singular components: \(n\) zero-row components and one Segre component for every nontrivial bipartition of the coordinates. Its generic transverse types are \(\mathbb A^2/\{\pm1\}\) and \(\mathbb A^{n-2}/\{\pm1\}\), and it is canonical but nonterminal, Gorenstein exactly for even \(n\).
