The projective toric variety of the rank-\(r\) matroid whose only missing basis is one \(r\)-subset, for \(r,s\ge3\), has exactly \(r+s+\binom{r+s}{r}-1-rs\) reduced singular components: \(r\) copies of \(\mathbb P^{s-1}\), \(s\) copies of \(\mathbb P^{r-1}\), and the remaining isolated fixed points. The positive-dimensional components meet in a complete bipartite pattern; generic transverse germs and isolated affine charts are explicit rank-one determinantal varieties.
