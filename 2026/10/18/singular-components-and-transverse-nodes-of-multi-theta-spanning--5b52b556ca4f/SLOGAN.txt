For the spanning-tree hypersurface of \(K_{2,m}\), \(m\ge4\), the reduced singular locus has precisely \(m+\binom m3\) irreducible codimension-three components: \(m\) integral degree-\(m\) hypersurface sections and \(\binom m3\) linear spaces. Its degree is \(m^2+\binom m3\), and every component is generically transverse to an ordinary quadratic double point.
