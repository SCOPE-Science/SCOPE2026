# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

At rank N-2, Pfaffian differential represents the decomposable two-vector spanning wedge^2(kernel A). Restriction to allowed entries vanishes iff this bivector lies in the span of the k disjoint forbidden coordinate bivectors; Pluecker on two disjoint pair edges forces only one coefficient, so a forbidden 2-coordinate kernel pair and two zero rows result. At rank<=N-4 all Pfaffian derivatives vanish. Parameter A=B J_r B^T with r=N-4 has k independent irreducible isotropy quadrics b_(2i-1) J b_(2i)=0 on disjoint row pairs, giving an integral rank locus of expected codimension six in ambient. A generic 4-dimensional kernel avoids any forbidden coordinate pair; restriction to wedge^2 kernel is surjective, and Pfaffian Schur reduces to a 6-parameter nondegenerate quadratic. At a zero-row-pair component, Pfaffian Schur reduces to u B^(-1) v^T, a nondegenerate form in 2(N-2) variables. R1/S2 proves normality. Independent GF101 checks compare explicit Pfaffian-minor gradients with predicted strata at N=6 k=1,3 and N=8 k=2, including forced zero-row-pair witnesses. A separate explicit rank-2 N=6 k=3 matrix built from three proportional forbidden pairs has Hessian rank exactly six over GF101. These checks support but do not prove the all-N statement.

## originality

PASS

Classical Pfaffian corank stratification covers rank<=N-4 singularities of a full generic Pfaffian; prescribed zero matching creates k additional exceptional row-pair components and distinct generic quadratic ranks. Primary De Negri–Gorla full-text ladder Pfaffian papers are about square-subladder ideals, normality, liaison, multiplicity and regularity, rather than this complement-of-a-matching hypersurface Jacobian decomposition. Goryunov–Mond abstract studies deformations of general matrix singularities, not an explicit all matching pattern theorem. Published Resultary matching search yielded only different matrix/cycle singular examples.
