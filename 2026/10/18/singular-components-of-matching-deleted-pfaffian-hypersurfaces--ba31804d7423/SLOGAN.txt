For alternating \(2m\)-by-\(2m\) matrices with \(k\) disjoint forbidden off-diagonal entries (\(m\ge3\), \(1\le k\le m\)), the Pfaffian hypersurface has exactly \(k+1\) reduced singular components: one rank-defect locus and \(k\) linear zero-row-pair loci. Their generic transverse singularities are nondegenerate quadratic cones of ranks \(6\) and \(4m-4\); the hypersurface remains normal and Gorenstein.
