In the translated Boole family \(T_\beta(z)=z+\beta-1/z\), the finite orbit-height limit satisfies the explicit bound \(|\beta b_\infty(\beta)-\pi|\le\beta^3/3+2\beta^2\log(4\pi/\beta)\) for \(0<\beta\le1/4\). This determines the sharp \(\beta^{-3}n^{-2}\) singular dual quasi-mixing normalization as positive drift vanishes, alongside the known \(n^{-1/2}\) law at exactly zero drift.
