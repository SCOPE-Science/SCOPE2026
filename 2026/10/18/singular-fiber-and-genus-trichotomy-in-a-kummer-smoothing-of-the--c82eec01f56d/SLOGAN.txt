The canonical \((xyz)^m\)-perturbation of the degree-\(3m\) Fermat–Ceva line arrangement has exactly two nonzero nodal parameters, determined by \(\lambda^2+27=0\). Every such fiber is integral with precisely \(m^2\) new nodes and normalization genus \(2m^2-3m+1\), whereas every other nonzero finite fiber has only the three fixed ordinary \(m\)-fold vertices and genus \(3m^2-3m+1\).
