For the projective rank-one model retaining exactly the off-diagonal entries of an \(n\times n\) table, the complete reduced singular locus has exactly \(2n+\binom n2\) coordinate linear components: \(2n\) copies of \(\mathbb P^{n-2}\) and \(\binom n2\) additional lines. Their generic transverse germs are rank-one determinantal cones of sizes \(2\times(n-1)\) and \(2\times(2n-4)\), respectively, giving a sharp transition from nine equidimensional lines at \(n=3\) to mixed-dimensional singular strata for \(n\ge4\).
