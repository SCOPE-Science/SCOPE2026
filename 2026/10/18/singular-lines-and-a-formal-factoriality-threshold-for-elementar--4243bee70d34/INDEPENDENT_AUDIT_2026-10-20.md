# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Differentiation gives g_i=e3(hat i)= -H(x_i), where H(T)=T^3-e1T^2+e2T-e3. If all derivatives vanish, every coordinate is among <=3 roots of H. Newton sums 1,2,3 for the coordinate multiset and roots of H agree. Vandermonde in the distinct nonzero roots forces their coordinate multiplicities to equal root multiplicities, so at most three coordinates are nonzero; for n>=6 a coordinate zero implies H(0)=0, reducing bound to two. Conversely two-support points are singular. As the singular locus consists of lines, projective hypersurface cannot be reducible or nonreduced; by R1/S2 it is integral normal. Near a general coordinate line [a:b:0:...], quadratic e4=ab e2(z)+higher and Hessian determinant (-1)^(n-3)(n-3)(ab)^(n-2) nonzero. The n=6 split 4-variable completed quadric has class group Z, while for n>=7 local CI rings are regular through height 3 and Samuel–Grothendieck parafactorial/factoriality applies to all localizations; no claim that n=6 algebraic local rings are nonfactorial. Independent exact SymPy Hessian at a=2,b=3 produced -3888 for n6 and 31104 for n7 and checked noncritical points on all 3/4-support signed bounded integer vectors (5120 and 11200). These finite checks are corroboration only.

## originality

PASS

The object is the fixed-degree fourth elementary symmetric projective hypersurface, not the degree-(n-1) banana graph polynomial or generic weighted symmetric Reed–Solomon hypersurface. Aluffi–Marcolli full preprint studies banana-graph motive/Cremona classes; Cafure–Matera–Privitelli full original analyzes singularity *bounds* for other symmetric-polynomial families and proves Vandermonde-type restrictions, but not this e4 exact coordinate-line classification nor n=7 local-factoriality transition. Samuel–Grothendieck factoriality is explicitly classical and applied after the specific singular codimension computation. Published Resultary searches found different quartic singular curves but not this e4 family.
