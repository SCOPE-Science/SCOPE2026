For every characteristic-zero elementary-symmetric quartic \(V(e_4)\subset\mathbb P^{n-1}\) with \(n\ge6\), the reduced singular locus is exactly the \(\binom n2\) coordinate lines. Their generic transverse nodes have nonfactorial completed local rings at \(n=6\), whereas for every \(n\ge7\) the whole hypersurface is locally factorial.
