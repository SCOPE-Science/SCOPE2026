For every \(n\ge4\), the normal toric variety of the \(n\)-cycle cut polytope has singular locus equal to the union of all \(2^{n-2}\binom n2\) invariant rational curves; no higher-dimensional orbit is singular. The generic transverse germ along each curve is the rank-one \(2\times(n-2)\) determinantal cone, of multiplicity \(n-2\), with exactly \(\binom n2\) such curves meeting at each torus-fixed point.
