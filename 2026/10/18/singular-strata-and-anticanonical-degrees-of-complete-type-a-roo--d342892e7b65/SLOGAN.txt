For the Gorenstein toric Fano variety of the full type-A root polytope on \(n\geq4\) indices, the reduced singular locus has exactly \(6\binom n4\) irreducible components, each isomorphic to \((\mathbb P^1)^{n-4}\) and generically transverse to an ordinary threefold node. Every component has anticanonical degree \((n-4)!\), giving total degree \(n!/4\) in every characteristic.
