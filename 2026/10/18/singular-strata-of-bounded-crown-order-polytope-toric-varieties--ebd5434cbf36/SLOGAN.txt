For the projective toric variety of the bounded crown with \(n\ge4\) matched middle pairs, the reduced singular locus has precisely \(2n+\binom n2\binom{n-2}{2}+\binom n3\) components in three families, with generic transverse singularities given by cones over \((\mathbb P^1)^{n-1}\), \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\), and \(\mathbb P^2\times\mathbb P^2\). All components share a common \(\mathbb P^1\), and exactly one of the \(2^{n+1}+n+1\) torus-fixed points is smooth.
