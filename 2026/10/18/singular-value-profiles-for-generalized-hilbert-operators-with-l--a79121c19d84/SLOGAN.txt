For generalized Hilbert operators on \(H^2\) with Hadamard-lacunary symbols, every approximation number is uniformly comparable to the corresponding decreasing rearrangement of \(\sqrt{n_j}|b_j|\). The pointwise profile extends the known Schatten membership theory to every quasi-Schatten exponent \(0<p<1\), and realizes arbitrary prescribed compact singular-decay rates up to constants.
