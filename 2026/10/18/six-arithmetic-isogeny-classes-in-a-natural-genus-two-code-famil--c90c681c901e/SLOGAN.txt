The fifteen admissible genus-two curves \(y^2=x^6+ax^3+1\) over \(\mathbb F_{19}\) in the Beshaj--Shaska code family have Jacobians in exactly six arithmetic isogeny classes, completely classified by explicit elliptic-quotient traces. In particular, their geometrically nonisomorphic examples with \(a=6\) and \(a=8\) share \(L(T)=(1-2T+19T^2)^2\), and even distinct hull profiles can belong to the same Jacobian isogeny class.
