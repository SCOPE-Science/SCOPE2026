For every \(M\geq3\), the reduced singular locus of the three-vector complex orthogonal-frame complete intersection has exactly six irreducible components: three linear spaces and three isotropic rank-one Segre cones, all of dimension \(M\), with total degree \(6M-3\). Generically along each component, the transverse analytic singularity is an ordinary quadratic node in \(2M-2\) variables.
