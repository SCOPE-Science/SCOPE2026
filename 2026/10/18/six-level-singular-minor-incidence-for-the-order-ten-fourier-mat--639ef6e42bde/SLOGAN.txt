The fourth-order Fourier minors at cyclic order ten have exactly \(5000\) singular row/column pairs out of \(44100\); each four-row harmonic frame has precisely \(0\), \(10\), \(20\), \(30\), \(110\), or \(130\) singular maximal minors. An exhaustive exact cyclotomic certificate determines the six corresponding row multiplicities, refining the previously known full-spark/non-full-spark dichotomy.
