The six disjoint-pentagon product tori in the unordered two-particle configuration space of the Petersen graph generate its entire integral second homology, with exactly one relation: their suitably oriented sum vanishes. Every five are an integral basis, and the natural \(S_5\)-lattice is the nonsplit six-point permutation quotient \(\mathbb Z^6/\mathbb Z(1,1,1,1,1,1)\).
