For the normal projective toric fivefold of the rank-three six-element matroid with one missing basis, the reduced singular locus is exactly six projective planes arranged in a \(3\times3\) intersection grid together with ten isolated points. Its generic planar transverse singularities are threefold conifolds, while the ten isolated affine germs are rank-one \(3\times3\) determinantal cones.
