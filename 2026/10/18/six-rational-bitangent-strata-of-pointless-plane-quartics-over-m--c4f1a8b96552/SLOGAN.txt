Among all 31 smooth pointless plane-quartic isomorphism classes over \(\mathbf F_5\), the numbers of rational bitangent lines fall into exactly six strata, and the sharp maximum is 16. The unique maximizer is the Fermat quartic \(X^4+Y^4+Z^4=0\), whose rational bitangents are precisely the 16 lines \(X+bY+cZ=0\) with nonzero \(b,c\).
