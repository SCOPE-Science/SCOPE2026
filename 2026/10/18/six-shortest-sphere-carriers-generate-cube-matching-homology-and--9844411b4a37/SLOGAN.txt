Exactly six eight-vertex induced subcomplexes of the cube's ordinary matching complex carry its shortest nonzero second-homology classes, each collapses to a two-sphere, and four of their classes form an integral basis of \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z^4\). The full \(S_4\times C_2\) symmetry splits rational second homology into a three-dimensional antipodally even Specht module and a one-dimensional antipodally odd trivial \(S_4\)-module.
