On circular single-species-per-site transects, five sites or fewer are too small for two landscapes to share their complete mean species–area curve yet have different window-richness laws; six sites are sharp. For every larger size, two landscapes can even share every species-specific detection curve while their richness variances at half-transect sampling differ by the sharp within-family amount \(2(\lfloor N/2\rfloor-2)/N\), approaching one.
