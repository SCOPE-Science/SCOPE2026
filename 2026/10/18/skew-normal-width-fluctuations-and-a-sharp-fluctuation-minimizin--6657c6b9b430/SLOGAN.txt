The random Euclidean width of a high-dimensional isocanted cube has an exact centered skew-normal limit, with nonzero third moment \(a^3\sqrt{2/\pi}(4/\pi-1)\) for every positive cant. With its bounding coordinate width fixed, the limiting width variance is uniquely smallest at cant ratio \((\pi-3)/(2\pi-5)\), not at the ordinary cube.
