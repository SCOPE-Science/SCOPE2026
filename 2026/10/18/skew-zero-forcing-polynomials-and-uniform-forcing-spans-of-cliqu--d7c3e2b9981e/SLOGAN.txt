For clique bouquets \(K_1\vee\bigsqcup_i K_{n_i}\) with at least two blades of size at least two, all skew-zero-forcing sets are counted by \((1+z)\prod_i Q_i(z)-\prod_i E_i(z)\), where \(Q_i(z)=\sum_{j=0}^2\binom{n_i}{j}z^{n_i-j}\) and \(E_i(z)=\binom{n_i}{2}z^{n_i-2}\). Their inclusion-minimal forcing sets all have size \(N-2r\), while the skew-zero-forcing span is \(2r-2\) whenever any blade has size at least three (and is zero for bouquets of triangles).
