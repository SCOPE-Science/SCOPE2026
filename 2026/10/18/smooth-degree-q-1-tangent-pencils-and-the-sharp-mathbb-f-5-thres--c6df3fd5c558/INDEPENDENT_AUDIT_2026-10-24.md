# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For every prime power q=p^e with p>3, the partials of X^(q-1)+X^(q-2)Y+Y^(q-2)Z-Z^(q-1) are the stated q-mod-p formulas. Any common zero has X ne 0, after scaling X=1 forces Y=-1/2, Z=1/8 and F_Z=6 ne 0; X=0 case also has no common zero. Therefore the plane curve is geometrically smooth and integral, degree q-1 and genus (q-2)(q-3)/2. The q+1 points Q_infty,Q_0,Q_z are distinct and their gradients give exactly the pencil of all q+1 rational lines through external P=[1:1:0], so every rational projective point is on a rational tangent. For q=5 independent C++ code regenerated all 2,441,406 normalized nonzero ternary homogeneous cubic coefficient vectors, all 31 projective points and their smooth-rational-point tangents, finding max coverage 29, no covering cubic (186000 cubics attaining 29); line/conic incidence bounds exclude degrees <3. An independently implemented F5 quartic point/gradient calculation counted 11 curve points whose tangent union is all 31 rational points. The finite cubic census is exhaustive only over F5, not a proof of minimum q-1 for other q.

## originality

PASS

Asgarli–Ghioca define tangent filling and construct degree-(q-1) examples for sufficiently large q, but their displayed construction is different and singular and their explicit main minimum-degree question is not settled for F5. The submitted smooth all-p>3 pencil plus exact complete cubic obstruction sharpens existence and solves the first indicated field. No equivalent all-F5 classification was found in relevant Resultary records; residual risk of earlier reparameterized examples remains.

A smooth extremal quartic together with an exhaustive proof excluding every cubic at q=5 is substantive, and the all-characteristic>3 smooth tangent-pencil family extends the scope without extrapolating the small-field census.
