# Scientific review: smooth tangent pencils and the sharp small-field threshold

**Correctness — PASS.** The gradient computation is an algebraically closed-field proof of smoothness for every prime power of characteristic above three; Bézout gives geometric integrality. The exhibited \(q+1\) rational smooth points yield each line in the complete pencil, so filling holds. For \(\mathbb F_5\), an exact exhaustive enumeration of all \(2{,}441{,}406\) projective cubic forms computes the union of all tangent lines at smooth rational points and finds a maximum of \(29<31\). The full self-contained C++ checker was compiled and executed. Standard elementary arguments exclude lines and conics. Assumptions, projective normalization, completeness and boundary characteristic conditions are explicit.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** The motivating 2023 paper of Asgarli–Ghioca introduces tangent-filling and treats a different singular degree-\(q-1\) family for \(q\ge11\), but its inspected full manuscript does not supply a smooth all-characteristics-above-three tangent-pencil family or the exact \(q=5\) minimal-degree obstruction. Source, equivalent statement, parameter-shift, broad-coverage, and exact-census searches of primary literature, public research records, found no dominating result. Lack of a matching database entry is not a proof of absolute priority. An unindexed finite-geometry classification or equivalent change of projective coordinates remains a risk.

**Value — PASS.** The infinite smooth construction gives a stronger geometric regularity property than the motivating singular model and an especially transparent tangent-line geometry. The exact quartic minimum for the smallest odd characteristic above three makes a natural quantitative question sharp, supported by a complete obstruction for lower degrees rather than an isolated example.

**Closest prior literature and limitations.** Asgarli and Ghioca, arXiv:2302.13420v1 and DOI:10.1017/S0004972723000382, give the incidence definition, a different large-field singular construction and the general minimum-degree question; arXiv:2307.03072 studies plane filling, not tangent filling. Exact minima for fields other than \(\mathbb F_5\) are not established. The exhaustive cubic computation has ordinary finite-code reproducibility, but independent auditing has not occurred.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
