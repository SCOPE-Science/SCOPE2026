# Scientific review: smooth Fano graph and complete Pfaffian multidegrees

## Correctness — PASS

For a general alternating subsystem of vector dimension \(n+1\), the point–form universal incidence over a fixed hyperplane is a smooth projective bundle over the hyperplane. A general linear cut has the expected dimension and is smooth and irreducible by Bertini, and projects birationally onto the fixed hyperplane. It is therefore the actual closed graph, not merely an extraneous incidence correspondence. Its zero-section bundle has rank \(n\) and Chern polynomial \((1+h)^{n+1}/(1+2h)\). Intersections produce the stated full multidegree formula; adjunction gives \(K_\Gamma=\mathcal O_\Gamma(-1,-1)\), and therefore ampleness of \(-K_\Gamma\). The independent arithmetic recurrences both reproduce the first four explicit sequences and endpoint consistency in 49 odd dimensions. No finite computation substitutes for the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with identifiable residual risk

Theorem 14 and formula (4) of Bolognesi–Ciliberto–Verra, arXiv:2610.11925v1, establish the parametrization and give degrees of the map and its focal base scheme. Those facts imply the source/image endpoint degrees, and the second multidegree can be recovered from the base degree. The claimed new statement is the smooth Fano *closed graph* and the full intersection polynomial, including previously uncomputed intermediate degrees. The originating source does not explicitly contain this theorem. Han’s 2010 genus-nine Fano fourfold congruence (relevant Section 4 inspected) has a different incidence construction. The earlier same-author index-one Fano manuscript arXiv:2609.21503v1, whose full text could not be read, is a genuine residual risk; available abstract identifies a distinct section type. Targeted semantic and exact mathematical-literature searches found no matching claim; the closest named multidegree result concerns an unrelated Enriques Cremona map. Unindexed or undiscovered stronger results cannot be excluded.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The family consists of natural Pfaffian hypersurfaces highlighted by the originating rationality theorem. Its complete projective degree vector records all generic linear-section inverse-image degrees and supplies intersection-theoretic information unavailable from birationality and the first/last degrees alone. The Fano property of the smooth graph is a structural statement that applies uniformly even where the target becomes singular. This is a motivated enumerative and birational invariant for an infinite family, not a coordinate renaming, one-off numerical sample, or an unmotivated slice.

## Limitations and closest comparison

Only complex, Zariski-general systems and hyperplanes are covered. The result does not classify arbitrary Pfaffian models, degenerate data, singular target structures, or other birational resolutions. The originating paper provides a strong starting theorem; the graph smoothness and its Chow class are proved afresh. The proof invokes standard Bertini, Euler-sequence and adjunction results, which are not claimed as new. Arithmetic checks are corroborative. Independent checking remains outstanding.
