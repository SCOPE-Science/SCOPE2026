For every odd \(n\ge5\), the closed graph of the general Palatini–Pfaffian parametrization is a smooth Fano \((n-1)\)-fold with \(K_\Gamma=\mathcal O_\Gamma(-1,-1)\). Its entire projective multidegree sequence is the degree-\((n-1)\) truncation of \((1+t)^{n+1}/(1+2t)\), beginning with \((1,4,7,6,3)\) for \(n=5\).
