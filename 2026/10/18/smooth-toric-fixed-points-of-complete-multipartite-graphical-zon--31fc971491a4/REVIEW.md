# Review of complete-multipartite smooth fixed-point series

**Correctness — PASS.** Canonical properly colored blocks represent all acyclic orientations of the complete multipartite graph. Consecutive blocks contribute the entire Hasse cover graph, whose tree condition is equivalent to singleton internal blocks. For a tree, signed root normals form an integral basis, so the corresponding maximal normal cone is smooth in any characteristic; the converse is nonsimplicial. A proper-color transfer matrix gives the exact multivariate generating series, and two independent finite enumeration methods reproduce the stated counts.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** The general smoothness/tree-poset criterion is known from Postnikov–Reiner–Williams, and the earlier two-part complete-bipartite smooth fixed-point enumeration already exists, so neither is claimed as new. The present result enumerates **every number of color classes at least three and every multipartite size tuple** by a single explicit multivariate transfer-matrix series. Inspected primary sources and targeted indexed searches did not disclose this full smoothness-refined enumeration. A previously published general multipartite simplicial-chamber count could dominate the claim and remains a qualified risk.

**Value — PASS.** Complete multipartite graphs form a basic class with globally singular graphical-zonotope fans in many parameters. The series refines ordinary acyclic-orientation and face counts to actual toric regularity across all multipartite parameters, extending the known two-part result to proper-color walks with arbitrarily many classes. It gives testable exact geometric point counts (for example the \(288\) smooth fixed points of \(K_{2,2,2}\)) without any exhaustive high-dimensional fan construction.

**Limitations.** The result concerns torus-fixed points only; it does not classify the full singular support. Experiments cover small tuples, while the theorem follows from the symbolic argument. Independence has not been established by an external reviewer.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
