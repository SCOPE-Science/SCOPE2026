For every complete multipartite graphical-zonotope toric variety with at least three nonempty vertex classes, a fixed point is smooth exactly when all nonterminal blocks in its canonical properly colored order are singletons. Its full multivariate smooth-fixed-point census is an explicit rational matrix series in the nonempty-block exponentials, including \(288\) smooth fixed points out of \(426\) for \(K_{2,2,2}\).
