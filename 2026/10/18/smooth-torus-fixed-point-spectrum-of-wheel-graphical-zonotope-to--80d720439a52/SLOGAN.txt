The wheel graphical-zonotope toric variety has exactly \(4m-4+((3+\sqrt5)/2)^m+((3-\sqrt5)/2)^m+2\cos(m\pi/3)\) smooth torus-fixed points, out of \(3^m-3\), for every rim length \(m\ge3\). A finite binomial formula also counts smooth fixed points by the number of spokes directed into the hub; the smooth proportion decays exponentially.
