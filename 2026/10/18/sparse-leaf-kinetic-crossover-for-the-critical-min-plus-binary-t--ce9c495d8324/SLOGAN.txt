For a critical random sum/min binary tree whose leaves are positive with vanishing probability \(r_N\), the **conditional law of a positive root** undergoes a complete transition at the clock \(Nr_N\): it remains \(1\), converges in total variation to a nonlinear convolution-minus-maximum kinetic law, or escapes every fixed finite set. The finite-clock approximation has an explicit total-variation error bound, and its probability of value \(1\) is exactly \(2/(2+t)\).
