For a sparse Bernoulli formal context with \(n\) objects, \(n\) attributes and incidence probability \(c/n\), the number of concepts is asymptotic in probability to \(n[2(1-(1+c)e^{-c})+ce^{-2c}]\). After subtracting the two boundary concepts, degree-at-least-two vertices and isolated edges, the remaining integer-valued correction converges to a marked compound-Poisson law generated by four-cycles.
