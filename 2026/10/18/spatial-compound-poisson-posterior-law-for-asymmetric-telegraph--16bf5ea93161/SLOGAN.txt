For asymmetric two-speed telegraph motion killed at an independent exponential time, conditioning on the terminal position produces an exact spatial convolution semigroup: moving the observed terminal position farther along either ray adds a deterministic travel delay and an independent compound-Poisson time with an explicit Bessel-function jump measure. The resulting posterior termination time is strictly stochastically increasing with distance along each ray, and its conditional mean and variance are exactly affine in that distance.
