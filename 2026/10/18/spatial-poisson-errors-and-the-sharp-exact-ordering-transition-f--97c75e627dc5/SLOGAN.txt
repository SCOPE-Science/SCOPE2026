When independent exponential clocks have consecutive rate ratios of order \(n^{-1}\), their entire random ranking differs from the intended order only by spatially Poisson, disjoint adjacent swaps with probability tending to one. For geometrically decreasing rates, the exact-ranking probability has a sharp transition at ratio \(1/n\), with the limiting Gumbel profile \(\exp(-e^{-x})\).
