For one-dimensional Page–Rényi dimer parking, the positions and centered times of the last successful arrivals form an asymptotic Poisson field with intensity \(e^{-2}\,du\,e^{-x}dx\), so the final few cars appear at asymptotically independent uniform locations. The count of arrivals later than \(\log n+x\) admits an \(O(\log n/n)\) Poisson total-variation bound; the scalar Gumbel limit itself was already known.
