An associated-Laguerre finite-rank Hankel family has a closed bulk spectral gap when \(\tau\ne1\), yet for \(\tau>1\) a single singular value becomes exponentially small inside that gap; at \(\tau=1\), gap closure is algebraic and the entire signed spectrum has an elementary sine formula. The smallest nonzero singular value is asymptotically \((1-\tau)/2\) below the transition, exactly \(\sin(\pi/(4m+2))\) at it, and asymptotically \((\tau^2-1)/(2\tau^{m+1})\) above it.
