For independently resampled real Gaussian tensor probes of a fixed separable matrix residual, the exact missed-error probability decays as \(\tau^{-\ell}(\log\tau)^\ell\) at matrix rank one but as \(C\tau^{-\ell}\) at rank at least two. The coefficient \(C\) is an explicit singular-value integral, uniquely minimized at equal nonzero singular values, and the inverse error ratio has finite \(p\)-th moment precisely for \(p<\ell\).
