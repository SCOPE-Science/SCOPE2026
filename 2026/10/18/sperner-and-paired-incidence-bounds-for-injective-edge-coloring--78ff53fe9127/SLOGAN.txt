For the one-subdivision \(S(G)\) of any graph with chromatic number \(q\ge2\), an explicit middle-layer incidence coloring gives \(\lceil\log_2q\rceil\le\chi'_{\mathrm{inj}}(S(G))\le\log_2q+\frac12\log_2\log_2q+O(1)\), proving the universal sharp leading logarithmic coefficient. A paired-incidence construction separately colors \(S(K_n)\) with \(t\) colors whenever \(n\le2\binom{t-1}{\lfloor(t-1)/2\rfloor}\), including \(S(K_{12})\) with five colors.
