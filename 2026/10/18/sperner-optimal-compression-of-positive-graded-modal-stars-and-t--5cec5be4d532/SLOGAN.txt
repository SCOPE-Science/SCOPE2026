Positive graded modal logic on finite rooted stars has a sharp successor-compression bound of \(k\binom{m}{\lfloor m/2\rfloor}\) for \(m\) propositional letters and counting thresholds at most \(k\). With two letters the exact pointed-theory count is \(4\left(\binom{k+3}{4}+\binom{k+2}{3}+\binom{k+3}{3}\right)\); the numerical sequence itself was previously known in another context.
