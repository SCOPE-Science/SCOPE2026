For every connected regular graph, the Minkowski sum of unit disks supported on its edge-coordinate planes has **both** John and Löwner ellipsoids equal to explicit concentric Euclidean balls, even without vertex-transitivity. The outer-sphere contact set consists of \(2^n\) isolated points for nonbipartite graphs but is an embedded \(K_{2^{n/2},2^{n/2}}\) for bipartite graphs.
