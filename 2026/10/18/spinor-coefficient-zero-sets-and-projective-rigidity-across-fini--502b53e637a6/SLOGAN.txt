Every rank-one coefficient of the defining spin representation of a finite nonabelian subgroup of \(\mathrm{SU}(2)\) vanishes exactly on nothing, on one antipodal pair, or on a coset of a maximal noncentral cyclic subgroup. Every such coset is realized by exactly two projective rank-one coefficients; this yields a complete support-size and zero-set incidence census across the dicyclic and exceptional binary polyhedral groups.
