At the critical boundary \(n=4p\), every ordinary three-cut complex of the powered cycle \(C_{4p}^{p}\) has a split \(\mathbb Z^{p-1}\) summand in next-to-top integral homology and therefore fails shellability. An octagonal sphere and an explicit integral block-map degree matrix prove the obstruction uniformly, without asserting the conjectured exact homotopy type.
