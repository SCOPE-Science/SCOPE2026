Coordinate-square flips are connected for perfect matchings of \(Q_2\) and \(Q_3\), but on \(Q_4\) their 272-state graph consists of a 264-state component and eight immobile matchings. An explicit cross-disjoint quartet of frozen four-cube matchings yields at least \(2^{2^{d-4}}\) frozen matchings in every dimension \(d\ge4\).
