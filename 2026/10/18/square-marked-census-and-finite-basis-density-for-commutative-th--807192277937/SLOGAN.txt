The complete square-count polynomial for commutative three-nilpotent semigroups admits exact labelled and isomorphism-class formulas. Among uniformly sampled labelled multiplication tables in this class, the proportion whose full power semiring is not finitely based is at most \(\max\{2^{-(n-2)/2},(n/2)^{-\sqrt n}\}\) for \(n\ge4\), and hence vanishes faster than any inverse power of \(n\).
