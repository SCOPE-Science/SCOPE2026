# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** For both independently defined continuous-time dynamics, spin-flip noise makes the finite chain irreducible and spin reversal gives zero mean. The stationary pair-generator equations close exactly on the three symmetry-orbit pair types; solving them gives the stated positive rational denominators and magnetization variances. The finite-size formulae imply the asymptotic constants by explicit dominant-term comparisons with fixed positive \(\eta\). An independent exact-rational lumped generator/stationarity implementation checks 54 finite cases including both singleton and nonsingleton sides, but finite enumeration is only a corroboration.

**Originality — PASS within the inspected source set; residual risk remains.** Sood–Antal–Redner (arXiv:0712.4288) discusses voter, invasion and link clocks and complete bipartite examples, but not stationary positive-noise variances. Ramadas (arXiv:1403.1991) addresses node-clock noisy-voter temporal mixing, not paired clock statistics. Carro–Toral–San Miguel (arXiv:1602.06935) supplies exact general node-clock pair-moment equations, which are an important methodological precursor, then studies heterogeneous networks using annealed approximation: the current node-clock formulas are thus an evaluated application of existing moment machinery, **not** a new general closure. Pickering–Lim (arXiv:1408.2130) and Ben-Ari et al. (arXiv:2004.10187) address zero-noise voter or its conditional quasi-stationarity, and the broader 2018/2021 noisy-voter sources do not directly state both exact clock formulas or their square-root comparison. Checked literature and relevant research-record searches did not produce a dominating statement. The residual risk is an equivalent general coalescing-random-walk or degree-weighted covariance formula from which this specific crossover may follow by specialization; a negative search does not rule that out.

**Value — PASS.** Per-node versus per-edge clocks are two standard, physically distinct microscopic ways to allocate interaction activity on heterogeneously connected networks. Their expected copying workloads differ; holding independent flip noise fixed is an intentional experimentally meaningful comparison, not a hidden rate normalization. The sharp intermediate scale \(m\asymp\sqrt n\), the unique leading-order reversal and the different fixed-side and balanced-side limits provide a complete structural description of a stationary fluctuation inversion, rather than an arbitrary finite enumeration or a restatement of the general covariance equation. It can benchmark heterogeneous updating and approximation schemes.

**Scientific limitations.** The equality of limiting variances at critical \(c_*\) leaves next-order ordering undetermined. The conclusion concerns normalized instantaneous stationary magnetization variance, not long-time integrated fluctuations or dynamic mixing. Clock activity is not matched between protocols. The originality evidence is bounded to material actually inspected; historical equivalences remain possible.
