For the positive-noise two-state voter model on \(K_{m,n}\), comparing unit per-vertex copying clocks with unit per-directed-edge copying clocks produces a sharp stationary magnetization-noise reversal at \(m\sim[8(1+\eta)]^{-1/2}\sqrt n\). The variance ratio tends to \(8(1+\eta)c^2\) when \(m/\sqrt n\to c>0\), although both versions use the same graph and independent spin-flip noise.
