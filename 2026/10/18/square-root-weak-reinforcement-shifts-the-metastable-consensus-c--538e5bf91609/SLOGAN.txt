In the conclave voting process with vanishing power reinforcement \(\alpha_n=c/\sqrt n\), the full-consensus time divided by \(2^{n-1}e^{-c^2/2}\) converges to a mean-one exponential law, and the same scale is its expected value. The process almost always reaches a final two-candidate contest long before consensus, while a Gaussian fluctuation of its binary tallies supplies the nontrivial factor \(e^{-c^2/2}\).
