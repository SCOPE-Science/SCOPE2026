# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The central correspondence is bijective: for the standard nondegenerate dot product, a symmetric zero-diagonal square-zero matrix has a totally isotropic image, and its associated alternating form factors nonsingularly through the dual of that image. The converse construction recovers a unique matrix and enforces each equation. Nondegenerate alternating forms exist exactly in even dimensions. The isotropic-image census follows from the characteristic-two hyperplane, whose restricted form is symplectic in odd dimension and has a canonical one-dimensional radical in even dimension. The two graph/lift cases supply the exact summands. Orbit–stabilizer establishes the fiber factor. Independent small-case matrix computations reproduced all tested rank coefficients, including extension fields.

## Originality — PASS, with historical uncertainty
Buckhiester (1974) counts rank-prescribed rectangular Gram-matrix solutions, not the simultaneously alternating, self-adjoint and square-zero endomorphism slice. Brouwer–Gow–Sheekey (2014), Section 4.4, derives numbers for symmetric nilpotent matrices by Jordan shape, but zero diagonal is not imposed; for characteristic two this omission matters. Their explicit reference to Lusztig (1976) concerns all nilpotent skew-symmetric matrices of a fixed rank, not the joint requirement that the square vanish. Xue (2008) classifies nilpotent orbits, not this exact rank census. Fulman–Goldstein (2015) studies the rank distribution of unconstrained alternating matrices. No inspected source proves the stated joint-condition rank formula. Lusztig's full text was unavailable; an equivalent derivation remains a specific bibliographic risk, and absolute novelty is not claimed.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The theorem identifies a complete natural arithmetic rank invariant of the square-zero locus inside the alternating-matrix space, including sharp parity restrictions and a geometrically meaningful two-stratum decomposition that is invisible in ordinary symmetric-nilpotent counts. The image/form bijection is structural and works uniformly over all characteristic-two fields; its nontrivial finite-field enumeration is applicable to incidences in symplectic polar spaces and nilpotent self-adjoint endomorphism geometry. The result is not an arbitrary numerical slice or an assumption-free corollary of a single table.

## Limits and remaining comparisons
The criterion uses a nonalternating ambient bilinear form and the square-zero exponent, neither of which can be silently dropped. An earlier explicit rank-and-exponent enumeration for orthogonal Lie algebras could subsume this count, and the complete Lusztig (1976) paper was not available. No claim of independent audit or external certification is made.
