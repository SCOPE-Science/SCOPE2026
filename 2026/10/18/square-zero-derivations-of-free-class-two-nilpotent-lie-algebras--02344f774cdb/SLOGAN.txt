For the free two-step nilpotent Lie algebra on \(n\ge3\) generators over any finite field, the complete count of square-zero derivations is a closed Gaussian-binomial sum stratified by rank on the abelianization, with additional positive ranks possible only in characteristic two. The zero-rank stratum has asymptotic proportion \(1/2\) for \(n=3\), but proportion tending to one for every fixed \(n\ge4\).
