# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The Leibniz relation uniquely parameterizes derivations by \(f=D(t)\). The proof separately derives the conditions \(f^2=ff'=0\) in odd characteristic and \(uv=v^2=0\) over \(\mathbb F_q[s]/(s^m)\) in characteristic two. Valuation counts and explicit image bases yield every rank coefficient. The odd–even correspondence is checked at the algebra-truncation boundary. The irreducible-component assertion is restricted to the reduced locus. The independently implemented matrix checker exhaustively verifies the predicted coefficients in \(27\) finite field/length cases, including \(\mathbb F_4\); finite enumeration is not claimed as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS within searched coverage

The object is the **ordinary Leibniz derivation**, not an arbitrary square-zero module endomorphism. Banks (arXiv:2212.03794v1, Definition 2.1 and sections 3–5) studies the latter and numerical Betti phenomena. Premet (arXiv:1405.2426v3, introduction and sections 3–4) establishes regularity and invariant-theoretic results in odd characteristic, not finite-field rank coefficients for the operator-square-zero locus. Chen (arXiv:1910.00954v1, abstract and Cartan-type nilpotent-variety material) addresses substantially broader nilpotent conditions primarily in odd characteristic. Their propositions do not logically yield the stated paired characteristic-two rank polynomials or component decomposition. Separate title, alias, equivalence, stronger-coverage, and finite-table searches found no coincident result. This is not a uniqueness certification; modular restricted Lie theory and \(\alpha_2\)-actions could contain an overlooked equivalent formulation.

These are structural statements rather than arbitrary isolated counts.
