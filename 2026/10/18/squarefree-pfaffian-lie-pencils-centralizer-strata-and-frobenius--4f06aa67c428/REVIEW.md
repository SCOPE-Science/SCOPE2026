# Review: Squarefree Pfaffian Lie pencils

## Correctness
**PASS.** The bracket has nondegenerate first alternating form; the two adjoint coefficient rows are dependent for a noncentral vector precisely when it is an eigenvector of the semisimple self-adjoint operator \(S\). The squarefree hypothesis gives \(m\) distinct two-dimensional eigenblocks. Explicit maximal minors then generate the squarefree monomial cross-block ideal, which is radical with exactly \(m\) minimal primes. The singular locus and Hilbert series follow from this explicit ideal. Frobenius root orbits produce the exact extension-field counts, zeta factors, rank enumeration and commuting probability. The companion-matrix finite-field program checks eight field/polynomial pairs, including extension fields and characteristic two; it is not used as an infinite proof.

## Originality
**PASS, with residual literature risk.** Lauret and Oscari, arXiv:1209.3060 (2012), develop Pfaffian invariants, real nonsingular two-step Lie algebra classification and matrix-pencil structure. Mackey, Mackey, Mehl and Mehrmann (2012) develop skew-symmetric matrix-polynomial Smith forms and paired elementary divisors. These background structural results do not state the reduced determinantal rank-one centralizer scheme, singular locus, extension-field point count, zeta function or reconstruction of factor-degree multiplicities proved here. Related studies of adjoint-rank distributions for maximal-class filiform Lie algebras address a different Lie family. Targeted semantic searches and primary full-text inspection found no decisive covering statement. Coverage by additional finite-group or Lie-pencil literature remains possible; no search establishes mathematical uniqueness.

## Value
**PASS.** Centre-two nilpotent Lie algebras built from regular alternating pencils are a natural and well-motivated family. The theorem completely characterizes their adjoint rank-drop geometry, including scheme-theoretic reducedness and singularities, and gives all extension-field counts. Most importantly, a centralizer-stratum zeta function encodes the finite-field factorization-degree pattern of the defining Pfaffian. This converts an arithmetic factorization invariant into a geometric Lie-algebra invariant with a proof uniform across field characteristics and dimensions.

## Closest literature and limits
Lauret–Oscari (2012), arXiv:1209.3060, Sections 1–3; Mackey–Mackey–Mehl–Mehrmann (2012), Sections 4–6. The theorem does not cover repeated factors or arbitrary singular pencils; finite test cases supplement rather than replace the proof. A specialized equivalent result outside the inspected sources may remain undiscovered.
