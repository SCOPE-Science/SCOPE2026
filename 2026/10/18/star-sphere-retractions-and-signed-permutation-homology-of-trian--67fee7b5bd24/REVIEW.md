# Scientific assessment: triangular-graph star-sphere retractions

## Correctness — PASS
For every integer \(n\ge7\), the spoke sets are boundaries of simplices because every proper spoke subset has an omitted-spoke neighbor and the complete spoke set has none. Each map \(r_i\) is checked on all graph neighborhoods, which are the maximal generating simplices of the open neighborhood complex. A neighborhood centered at a spoke avoids that spoke after applying \(r_i\); one centered at a nonspoke uses at most five of the \(n-1\ge6\) target spokes. Hence these are genuine simplicial retractions to the sphere boundaries. The image of any different spoke sphere under \(r_i\) uses at most four vertices and has zero top-degree map. The resulting integral left inverse establishes the full quantified split injection and excludes torsion-only or rational-only interpretations of the classes. The permutation action follows from deleting one row and column in the sign of the inducing permutation.

At \(n=7\), the independent exhaustive face enumerator produces 18,494 nonempty simplices and gives binary ranks \(2534\) and \(2646\), leaving \(7\) homology dimensions in degree four. Separate pivot conventions return identical ranks. The integral retractions give at least seven rational dimensions and universal coefficients give at most seven, proving the complete rational representation. The finite calculation is not used to prove the general retraction construction.

## Originality — PASS
The closest identifiable publicly posted research record, *Mixed-dimensional mod-two neighborhood homology first appears at* \(T_7\) (published-finding corpus/2026, 2026-10-11, identifier `5026f3f1e8ec`), explicitly establishes the seven-dimensional mod-two group but says nothing about the integral star-cycle splitting, the signed \(S_7\) action, or any larger \(n\). Merely reproducing its Betti number would be covered. The present construction supplies an integral, explicit, infinite-family structural retraction theorem, and uses the finite number only to prove rational exhaustiveness at \(n=7\).

Matsushita's *Fundamental groups of neighborhood complexes* (arXiv:1210.2803v1, 2012) develops fundamental groups and graph homomorphism obstructions, not this higher-dimensional signed integral summand. Matsushita–Wakatsuki's *Dominance complexes, neighborhood complexes and combinatorial Alexander duals* (arXiv:2312.02639) includes a general large-clique argument showing **nonvanishing** of mod-two homology in a suitable degree; applying it to the \(n\)-vertex stars does not establish the linear independence of all \(n\) integral classes, their simultaneous split injections, or their representation. Fröberg's 2026 paper computes Stanley–Reisner Betti data for paths, stars, complete or multipartite graphs, and two-row grids, not the line graphs of complete graphs. Targeted public-text and semantic-index searches found no implication-equivalent or stronger triangular-graph theorem.

Residual scientific uncertainty remains about unpublished, unindexed, or differently named spectral-sequence calculations. Noncoverage in search is not a uniqueness proof. The claim does not pretend to be an independent novelty certificate.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The large stars of \(T_n=L(K_n)\) are its canonical maximal cliques; a general clique argument makes their associated neighborhood spheres plausible, but higher-dimensional simplices could have killed their classes or made them dependent. Explicit retractions prove that **all \(n\)** survive as primitive integral cycles for every \(n\ge7\). The built-in \(S_n\)-action is not an accidental numerical refinement: it is the canonical sign-twisted permutation lattice, and at the first mixed-degree example it determines the entire rational fourth-homology module. The theorem thus gives a motivated and reusable structural constraint on an infinite Johnson-graph family, sharper than an isolated Betti table.
