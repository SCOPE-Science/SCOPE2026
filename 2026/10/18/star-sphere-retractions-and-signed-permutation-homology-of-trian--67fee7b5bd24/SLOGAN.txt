For every \(n\ge7\), the ordinary neighborhood complex of the triangular graph \(L(K_n)\) contains \(n\) canonical, primitively independent \((n-3)\)-sphere classes forming a sign-twisted permutation lattice, split as an abelian group by explicit simplicial retractions. At \(n=7\), these classes constitute all rational fourth homology, with \(S_7\)-representation \(S^{(1^7)}\oplus S^{(2,1^5)}\).
