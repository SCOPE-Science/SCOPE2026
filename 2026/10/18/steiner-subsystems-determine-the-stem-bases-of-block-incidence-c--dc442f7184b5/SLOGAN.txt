For the block–point incidence context of any Steiner triple system, the Duquenne–Guigues canonical premises are exactly all point pairs and the minimal proper nontrivial Steiner subsystems. In the binary projective family on \(2^d-1\) points, this gives exactly \(\binom{2^d-1}{2}+\begin{bmatrix}d\\3\end{bmatrix}_2\) implications, producing cubic growth in the point count from a quadratic-size incidence structure.
