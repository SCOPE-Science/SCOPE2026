In the compact parameter region for the differential Hilbert map between weighted Bergman Hilbert spaces, explicit dyadic Gauss–Legendre approximants of rank at most \(m^2\) attain operator-norm error \(O(2^{-hm})\) for \(h=\frac14\min\{\beta-\alpha-2,-\alpha\}>0\). Consequently its singular values decay at least stretched-exponentially and the operator belongs to every Schatten class \(\mathcal S_p\) with \(p>0\), including the quasi-Schatten range.
