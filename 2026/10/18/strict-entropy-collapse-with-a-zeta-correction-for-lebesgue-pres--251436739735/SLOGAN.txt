In the exact Lebesgue-preserving symmetrized intermittent maps of Shinkai and Aizawa, Kolmogorov–Sinai entropy decreases strictly with the power exponent \(B\), from \(\log 2\) at \(B=1\) to zero. The decay is quadratic near \(B=1\), while for large \(B\) it has the exact expansion \(h(B)=(2\log2+\pi^2/12)/B-(2\log2+\pi^2/12+3\zeta(3)/4)/B^2+O(B^{-3})\).
