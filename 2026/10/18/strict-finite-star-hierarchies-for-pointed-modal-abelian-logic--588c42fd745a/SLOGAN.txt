In pointed real-valued modal Abelian logic, the valid formulas of nonreflexive rooted stars form a strictly descending hierarchy with every added leaf. Exactly \(m+1\) occurrences of \(\Box\) are needed to distinguish a star with \(m\) leaves from one with \(m+1\), and a one-variable depth-one formula achieves this even using countervaluations confined to \([0,1]\).
