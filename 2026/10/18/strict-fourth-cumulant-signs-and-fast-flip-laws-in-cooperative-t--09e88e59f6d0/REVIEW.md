# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The fourth joint cumulant of symmetric stationary telegraph noise is \(-2e^{-2\gamma[(t_3-t_1)+(t_4-t_2)]}\) for ordered times. Independent linear response yields a fourfold convolution with an exact factor \(-48\); positivity of the response follows from the Metzler matrix semigroup, and its exponential stability licenses the integral and its derivatives. Strict positivity of the integral kernel establishes the claimed sign and complete monotonicity for every reachable nonnegative probe. Scaling the three ordered gaps establishes the coefficient \(-3\int h^4\). The covariance comparison uses only identical two-point forcing correlations. Twenty-six exact symbolic/rational checks, including independent stationary-generator moments and the signed-probe sign reversal, succeeded using the included program. No finite experiment substitutes for the proof.

## Originality — PASS, with a specific priority risk
Sarkar, Santra and Basu (arXiv:2210.09035; first posted 2022-10-17) derive the active-forcing covariance equivalence for an inertial chain and discuss non-Gaussian fluctuations at boundaries, but their inspected manuscript does not state a uniform finite-network negative fourth-cumulant theorem, its complete monotonicity, the sharp fast-switch coefficient, or a sign-changing-observable boundary. An inertial Green function may change sign, so it does not itself establish the positive-semigroup conclusions. Singh and Kundu (arXiv:2012.13910) study two-point fluctuations, while Di Bello et al. (arXiv:2309.13424) study Poisson-shot noise rather than dichotomous active forcing. A potentially important older paper, Pawula, Phys. Rev. A 35, 3102 (1987), describes rather general output-moment formulas for filtered dichotomous noise; only its abstract was accessible, and it may imply some ingredients. That unresolved general-theorem priority possibility is acknowledged; the checked accessible material and published-result comparisons yielded no decisive equivalence to the stated sign-plus-asymptotics theorem. Independent priority review remains necessary.

## Scientific value — PASS
A whole connected overdamped harmonic network inherits a non-Gaussian active-force signature at every site even when the complete time-lagged covariance matrix is indistinguishable from a Gaussian active model. A strict sign theorem, complete switching-rate ordering for the fourth cumulant, and a sharp inverse-cubic decay quantify precisely how the measurable signal disappears as activity decorrelates. The signed-probe counterexample isolates the cooperative positivity hypothesis and rules out an unmotivated universal claim. These are structural restrictions on physically natural finite networks, not a finite small-parameter census or merely a renamed single-particle formula. The restriction to stable Metzler drift, nonnegative probes, finite dimension, and independent symmetric telegraph drives is essential.
