# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The full carry-distribution generating function includes both the random low bits and the geometric tail after the shift. The two-state matrices yield an exact rational difference, which is truncated through the total number of ones. A finite triangular convolution inequality proves strict positivity for every parameter triple in range, and negative coefficient indices give saturation. The natural density is justified by finite-residue approximation with a geometric tail. Exact arithmetic checks cover 972 increment identities and 45 independent residue enumerations. The computations do not replace the universal proof.

## Originality

**PASS, subject to residual priority risk.** The archived 2015 paper sets up the underlying digit-sum correlation problem. The especially close 2024/2025 Sobolewski–Spiegelhofer paper contains a general correlation decomposition, a detailed formula for two runs, and conjectures on changing block lengths. These statements cover calculation of individual biases and some lower bounds, but the inspected sections do not prove strict monotonicity under every enlargement of the intervening zero run, nor the exact optimal finite plateau. The global positivity result from 2026 does not imply these comparisons. Accessible literature and mathematical research records show no equivalent theorem. Its status as an unindexed or implicit algebraic consequence of the earlier general formulas remains the main novelty risk.

## Value

**PASS.** The number of intervening zeros is a natural structural parameter within the two-block case singled out for detailed study in prior literature. The exact positive increments show not merely an asymptotic decorrelation, but complete stabilization after the sharp, interpretable threshold equal to the total Hamming weight. This is a useful finite-correlation boundary, and provides a definite two-run instance of a broader proposed monotonicity under zero-block changes.
