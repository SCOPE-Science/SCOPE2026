For every unweighted Boolean hypercube and any two positive, even unequal, leader feedback gains, noisy-consensus coherence is strictly better at each successive Hamming separation: antipodal placements are exactly optimal. The entire ranking is gain-independent, and the one-bit-from-antipodal penalty has an exact weak-gain limit of \(2\alpha\beta/[d(\alpha+\beta)^2]\) for relative gains \(\alpha,\beta\).
