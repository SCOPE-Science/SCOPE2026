Removing a growing concentric disk strictly reduces the sharp endpoint norm of affine least-squares projection on a planar annulus. At the intrinsic radius \(r_*=2-\sqrt3\), the norm has a sharp \((r-r_*)_+^{5/2}\) boundary-contact term and is \(C^2\) but not \(C^3\), despite an elementary closed formula on either side.
