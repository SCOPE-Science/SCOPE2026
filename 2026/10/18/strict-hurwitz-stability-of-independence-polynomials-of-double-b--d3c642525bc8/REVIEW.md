# Scientific review of double-broom independence-polynomial stability

**Correctness — PASS.** A four-part proof (hub-status independent-set partition, path ratio half-plane induction, uniform leaf-factor inequalities, and a normalized strict positive-real certificate) proves a nonvanishing statement on the entire closed right half-plane for every integer parameter in the claimed domain. The boundary case of adjacent hubs is separate. Exact independent-set enumeration and exact rational Hurwitz determinants independently corroborate 96 bounded parameter triples; neither finite check is used as an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS with residual bibliographic risk.** Brown and Cameron's 2018 paper treats stars, bounded independence number and certain graph operations; these are not all-parametric stability results for double-brooms. Zhang and Tu's September 2026 spider theorem concerns one branching vertex, whereas these trees have two. Targeted literature, research-record and equivalence searches yielded no stronger covering assertion, but an inaccessible 2026 full manuscript and unindexed equivalent results remain risks. The full 2018 article was materially inspected; the 2026 article was inspected at title/abstract/metadata level only. Search absence is not a uniqueness certificate.

**Value — PASS.** A natural open classification problem asks which trees have stable independence polynomials. This result gives a new two-branch infinite family, unbounded in hub degrees, hub separation and independence number. The explicit uniform positive-real certificate adds structural information beyond a finite enumeration or a reparameterization of a spider.

**Limitations.** Not a result about all caterpillars or all trees. The uniform margin is not claimed sharp; the full 2026 source could not be read; independent external audit is outstanding.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
