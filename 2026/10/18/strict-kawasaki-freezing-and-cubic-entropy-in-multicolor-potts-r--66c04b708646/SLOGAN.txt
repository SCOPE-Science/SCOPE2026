Strict zero-temperature Kawasaki freezing in a cyclic \(q\)-color Potts ferromagnet is classified by long domains and isolated singleton domains flanked by three distinct colors. Its labeled finite-size counts have a cubic-polynomial generating function, and its frozen-state entropy is \(\log\alpha_q\), where \(\alpha_q^3-\alpha_q^2-(q-1)\alpha_q-(q-1)(q-2)=0\); for three colors the singleton density is positive, unlike in the Ising case.
