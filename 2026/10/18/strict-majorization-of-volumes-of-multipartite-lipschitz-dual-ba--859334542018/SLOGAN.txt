For complete multipartite metrics with cross-part distance one and shared within-part distance \(1<t\le2\), the anchored volume of the real Lipschitz dual ball is exactly \(N+\sum_i n_i(n_i-1)\int_1^t r^{n_i-2}(2-r)^{N-n_i}\,dr\), and it is strictly Schur-convex in the part sizes. Balanced parts uniquely minimize this volume, while a single large part maximizes it; at \(t=2\), the corresponding growth laws range from linear or \(N^{3/2}\) to exponential at fixed part count.
