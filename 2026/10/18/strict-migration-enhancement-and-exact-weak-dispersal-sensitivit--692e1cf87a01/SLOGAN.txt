In the cyclic three-patch population model with symmetric migration only between the currently unfavorable patches, the principal growth exponent increases strictly with migration intensity at every finite period. Its exact initial sensitivity is \(\bigl(e^{(a-b)T/9}-1\bigr)^2/\bigl(3e^{(a-b)T/9}\bigr)\), converting a previously numerical monotonicity observation into an exact statement.
