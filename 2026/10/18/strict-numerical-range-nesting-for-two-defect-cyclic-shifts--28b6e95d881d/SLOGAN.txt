For cyclic weighted shifts with exactly two nonunit positive weights, their numerical ranges form a **strictly nested chain** as the weights move apart: the nesting direction is governed exactly by the sign of \((a^2-1)(b^2-1)\). An explicit Chebyshev determinant-difference identity proves this for every dimension and every support direction, including the reversal when one weight exceeds \(1\) and the other is below \(1\).
