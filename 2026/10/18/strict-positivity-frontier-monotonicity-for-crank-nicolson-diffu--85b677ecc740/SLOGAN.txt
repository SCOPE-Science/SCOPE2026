For Crank–Nicolson heat diffusion on any unweighted rectangular discrete torus \(C_m\square C_n\) with \(m,n\ge3\), the exact positivity timestep becomes strictly smaller when either period increases by one, and the largest threshold is \((1+\sqrt{57})/14\). Every finite threshold exceeds an explicit two-dimensional infinite-lattice elliptic-integral constant \(H_\infty\in(0.588197,0.588198)\), with a proven exponential error bound \(H_{m,n}-H_\infty\le(25/2)z_\infty^{\min(m,n)}\).
