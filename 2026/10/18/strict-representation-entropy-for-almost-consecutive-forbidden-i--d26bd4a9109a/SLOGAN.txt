For the \(S=[k]\setminus\{k-c\}\) greedy decomposition system in its established recurrence range, the average number of legal representations below the \(n\)-th term grows at the exact exponential base \(\rho_{k,c}/\lambda_k>1\), where the two roots satisfy \((\rho-1)(\rho^k-\rho^c)=1\) and \((\lambda-1)\lambda^k=1\). For every fixed \(c\), the logarithm of that growth base is asymptotic to \(W(k)/k^2\) as \(k\to\infty\).
