For the intersection of the \(n\) perpendicular unit Euclidean cylinders in \(\mathbb R^n\), the volume divided by that of the unit ball decreases *strictly* with dimension. Its excess has sharp expansion \(\pi/[2(n+1)(n+2)]+7\pi^2/[4(n+1)(n+2)(n+3)(n+4)]+O(n^{-6})\), even though its maximal radial excess is of order \(1/n\).
