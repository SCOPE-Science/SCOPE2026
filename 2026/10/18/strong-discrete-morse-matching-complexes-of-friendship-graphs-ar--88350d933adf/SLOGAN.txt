For the one-dimensional friendship graph of \(n\ge2\) triangles meeting at one hub, its full strong discrete-Morse matching complex is a wedge of \(3^n+2n\) spheres of dimension \(n-1\). An explicit decomposition into a join of four-point complexes and \(2n\) cones gives both the homotopy classification and the exact face polynomial \((1+4t)^n+2nt(1+2t)^{n-1}\).
