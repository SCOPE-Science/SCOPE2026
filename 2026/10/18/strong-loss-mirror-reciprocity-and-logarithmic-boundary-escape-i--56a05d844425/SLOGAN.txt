In a finite non-Hermitian quantum walk with arbitrary graded losses, the strong-absorption escape probabilities satisfy an exact inverse-loss mirror rule: \(w_j\pi_j=w_{N+1-j}\pi_{N+1-j}\). For a linear gradient and end-site injection, the far-left escape probability is asymptotically \(C/\log N\), while the right endpoint receives exactly \(1/N\) of that probability in the strong-loss limit.
