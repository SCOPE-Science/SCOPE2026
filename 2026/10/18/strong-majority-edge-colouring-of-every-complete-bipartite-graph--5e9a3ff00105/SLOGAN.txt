For every complete bipartite graph \(K_{a,b}\) with \(2\le a\le b\), the strong-majority edge index is two exactly for \(a=2\) with even \(b\), four exactly for \(K_{3,4}\) and the previously known \(K_{4,5}\), and three otherwise. A row–column rigidity argument classifies all optimal two-colourings, while a three-colour modular construction and two sharp small obstructions complete the classification.
