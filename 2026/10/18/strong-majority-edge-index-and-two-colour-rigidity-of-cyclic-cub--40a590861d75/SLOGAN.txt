For circular ladders \(C_n\square K_2\), two strong-majority edge colours suffice exactly when \(n\equiv0\pmod4\); for Möbius ladders on \(2n\) vertices they suffice exactly when \(n\equiv2\pmod4\), and otherwise three suffice. Each permitted two-colour case has exactly eight indexed assignments with named colours, imposed by a rigid period-four strip pattern.
