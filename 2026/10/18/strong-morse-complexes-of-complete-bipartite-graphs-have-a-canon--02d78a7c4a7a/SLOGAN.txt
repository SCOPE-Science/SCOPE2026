For every complete bipartite graph \(K_{m,n}\) with \(m,n\geq2\), the full complex of strong discrete-Morse matchings is a wedge of \(2mn-m-n+1\) circles, even though its simplicial dimension can grow without bound. Its first integral homology, with the complete automorphism action, is exactly the cycle lattice of the graph obtained by doubling every edge with opposite orientations; for a fixed bipartition it splits into a backtracking permutation lattice and the usual bipartite cycle lattice.
