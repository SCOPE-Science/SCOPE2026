The canonical cut and cycle projections of a connected strongly regular graph have exact \(\ell_1\)-endpoint norms controlled by a local exclusive-neighbor edge count, not just its three Laplacian eigenvalues. The cospectral rook and Shrikhande graphs have cut norms \(9/4\) and \(19/8\), respectively, and cycle norms \(21/8\) and \(11/4\).
