For random formal contexts with independent incidence probability \(1-c/n\), fixed \(0<c<1\), the number of concepts satisfies \(\log C_n/n\to F(c)>0\) in \(L^1\), where \(F(c)\) has an absolutely convergent tree-component series and an effective local recursion. Its low-density law is \(F(c)=c\log2-c^2\log2+c^3(\log3-\tfrac23\log2)+O(c^4)\): nearly complete incidence tables typically still have exponentially many concepts.
