For every finite (even nonabelian) group, the past–future excess entropy of the iid group-difference factor \(Y_i=X_i^{-1}X_{i+k}\) is exactly \(k(\mathsf H(\mu)-\log_2|K_\mu|)\), where \(K_\mu\) is the law's left-translation stabilizer. The entire discontinuity spectrum at a law is indexed by subgroups of \(K_\mu\), including quantized intermediate jumps at nonnormal symmetries.
