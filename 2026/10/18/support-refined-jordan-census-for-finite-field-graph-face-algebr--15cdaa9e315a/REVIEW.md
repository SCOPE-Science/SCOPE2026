# Scientific review: support-refined graph face algebra Jordan census

## Correctness — PASS

The radical cube is zero, so multiplication Jordan types are determined by the ranks of the operator and its square. With nonempty linear support, the degree-two part of multiplication is the weighted unsigned graph-incidence map. Its kernel has one free coordinate for every outside vertex with no neighbor in the support, plus one per connected support component in characteristic two or bipartite support component in odd characteristic. The image of the unit adds one independent dimension. The explicit product formula for the square and the independent coefficient count finish the proof. All rank and Jordan multiplicity formulas were re-derived from these arguments and checked against a direct matrix implementation. The empty support, disconnected graphs, odd cycles, and characteristic-two cases are handled explicitly.

## Originality — PASS within examined coverage

Dao and Nair, arXiv:2112.09434, develop the same underlying face-ring incidence map and establish a full-support characteristic-zero rank fact for weak Lefschetz purposes. This earlier fact is not claimed as new. Nguyen and Tran, arXiv:2310.14368, investigate paths/cycles and Lefschetz properties. Altafi, Iarrobino, and Macias Marques, arXiv:2307.00957, survey Jordan-type theory. Migliore, Nagel, and Schenck, DOI:10.7146/math.scand.a-116681, discuss quadratic monomial quotients and WLP. The inspected texts and mathematical search records did not supply the **all-graph, all-radical-element, all-characteristic, support-size-refined joint rank distribution** stated here. The support-by-support and quadratic-coefficient census, degree-sequence extraction, and same-Hilbert-function distinction are more than the previously stated full-support incidence-rank lemma. Search noncoverage is not proof of historical novelty; earlier equivalent presentations remain a genuine risk.

## Value — PASS

Weak Lefschetz/Jordan-type questions give a natural mathematical reason to count ranks of multiplication operators in this canonical finite-dimensional algebra family. Exact joint ranks give every Jordan partition and frequency, and the support refinement provably recovers the graph's degree multiset. The paw/four-cycle example shows the unrestricted rank spectrum discriminates equal-Hilbert-function algebras. Distinguishing characteristic-two and odd-cycle behavior demonstrates a structural rather than arbitrary finite-field statistic. The result is an exact infinite-family invariant, not a sample computation or a rename of the full-support rank lemma.

## Sources, limits, and independence

The closest known source for the incidence method is Dao–Nair (2021), with Nguyen–Tran (2023) relevant for paths/cycles. Exact all-element, support-refined census priority remains uncertain, especially in older incidence/monomial literature. The proof does not address degree-three-or-higher face algebras; no subexponential general subset-evaluation bound is asserted. The executable checker corroborates selected finite cases and does not replace the proof.
