For the zero-diagonal chiral quantum walk on the cone over the one-subdivision of any connected cubic graph \(G\), swift apex departure and exact equal-probability arrival at the first speed-limit time are possible precisely when \(G\) is bipartite. In an apex-fixed gauge, all swift phase designs are parametrized by proper three-edge-colourings of \(G\) and a global circle phase, modulo the three cyclic colour rotations.
