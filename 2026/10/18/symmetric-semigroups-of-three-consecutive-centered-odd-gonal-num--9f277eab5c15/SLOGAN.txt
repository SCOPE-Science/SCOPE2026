For every odd polygonal side count \(m\ge3\), the semigroup generated by three consecutive centered \(m\)-gonal numbers is symmetric exactly when \(2n+1\mid3m+8\), apart from the three extra pairs \((m,n)=(3,2),(5,2),(7,2)\). Its symmetric-case Frobenius number and the exact number of symmetric starting indices follow from explicit formulas.
