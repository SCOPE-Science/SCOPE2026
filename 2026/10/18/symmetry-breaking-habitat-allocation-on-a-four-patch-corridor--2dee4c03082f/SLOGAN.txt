For a four-patch corridor with symmetric area-elastic colonization weights \((a_i a_j)^{3/8}\) and fixed total habitat area, every globally best allocation breaks the corridor's reflection symmetry: a certified asymmetric allocation outperforms **all** symmetric splits. Two- and three-patch corridors cannot exhibit this effect for the same model and exponent; this is a linear-invasion threshold, not a claim about stochastic extinction.
