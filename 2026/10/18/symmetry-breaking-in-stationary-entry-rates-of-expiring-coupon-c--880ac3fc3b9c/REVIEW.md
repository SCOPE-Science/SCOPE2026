## Mathematical value — PASS

The source emphasizes stationary entrance intensity as the correct object for clustered coverage events. The result shows that uniform sampling, while uniquely maximizing stationary coverage mass, is a strict local *minimum* of the entry intensity once the memory window reaches four draws. This is a qualitative design reversal at an exact sharp threshold, not a merely numerical improvement. The optimal two-type bias and its large-window constant are explicit, and for every fixed number of types a sparse policy improves entrance rate from exponential to reciprocal-window order. This distinction is useful when frequent new complete-coverage episodes, rather than long residence in coverage, is the intended criterion. General multi-type global optimization remains open.
