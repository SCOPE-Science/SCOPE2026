In a deterministic-expiration coupon collector with two types, maximizing the stationary frequency of entering complete coverage requires equal sampling only for windows of length \(2\) or \(3\); for every length \(M\ge4\), the optimum splits into two strictly biased laws. Their rare-type probability is asymptotic to \(1/M\), creating an entrance rate of order \(1/M\) instead of the exponentially small balanced rate, while balanced sampling still uniquely maximizes the time spent fully covered.
