# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof explicitly characterizes globally onto bounded morphisms by surjections of each source equivalence block, exactly quotients source relabelings by positive fibre compositions, and then quotients target relabelings through an explicit group action. The counting formula follows from fixed-multiset cycle factors, inclusion–exclusion on target block cycles, and Burnside. Rational generating-function denominators give the eventual quasi-polynomial, and the kernel is exactly the group contribution at the highest degree. All finiteness, source/target nonemptiness, global surjectivity and fixed-support asymptotic conditions are stated. Direct program checks and full-group-action checks support the theorem.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

The most relevant source, De Berardinis–Ghilardi (2025), establishes the categorical finite-family/surjection background. Goranko–Otto provides standard modal p-morphisms; Figallo–Pascual–Ziliani handles an augmented Kripke class. The exact double-symmetry orbit enumeration, cycle-coverage coefficient formula, and quasi-polynomial leading coefficient were not found in these inspected claims or targeted research-index searches. They are nevertheless built from classical composition counting and Burnside/Pólya techniques, and specialized prior formulas may exist. Independent priority checking remains outstanding.

## Value — PASS

The theorem solves a natural classification and counting problem for morphism diagrams in a complete infinite class of finite modal frames. It removes both kinds of irrelevant relabelings, computes a genuine moduli invariant, shows fixed-target tractability and gives a precise asymptotic growth law. Its value is the uniform orbit classification, not merely code correctness or a small finite census.

## Literature and limitations

The full scope is a single unvalued equivalence accessibility relation, globally onto bounded morphisms, and isomorphism of diagrams by independent automorphisms. The quasi-polynomial theorem holds when a fixed finite set of source block sizes has all its multiplicities grow. No assertion is made for multi-agent frames or target sizes varying in the algorithmic complexity statement. Priority uncertainty is strongest for generic Pólya reductions of surjection families.
