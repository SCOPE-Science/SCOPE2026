The normal-fan toric fivefold of the pentagon's stable-set polytope has precisely ten singular components, all projective planes in two five-element symmetry orbits, with generic transverse conifold equation \(uv-wz=0\). Five torus-invariant lines belong to three planes each, and the singular locus has exactly forty torus orbits and coincides with the non-\(\mathbb Q\)-factorial locus.
