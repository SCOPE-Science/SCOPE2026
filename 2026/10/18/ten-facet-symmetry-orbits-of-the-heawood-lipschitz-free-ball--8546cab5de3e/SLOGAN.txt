The real Lipschitz-free unit ball of the Heawood graph has exactly \(660\) facets, classified into ten orbits of sizes \(168,112,112,84,56,42,28,28,28,2\) under graph automorphisms and sign reversal. A Fano-plane monochromatic-line count proves the total analytically, and exhaustive exact finite enumeration establishes the ten-orbit refinement.
