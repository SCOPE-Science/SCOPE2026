# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The unordered Abrams cubical model is valid with no subdivision for two particles on K6: each essential-vertex-to-essential-vertex path has at least one edge and circuits have >=3 edges. It has C0=15, C1=60 (oriented edge, distinct free vertex), C2=45 unordered pairs of disjoint oriented edges and no C3. The boundary formula d2(e,f)=(f,b)-(f,a)-(e,d)+(e,c) is exact. For each of ten 3+3 partitions the product of directed triangle cycles is a 2-cycle; the sign of ordered six-block coordinates normalizes them, giving the coefficientwise ten-term sum zero. A 9x9 torus-column minor has determinant 1, hence the first nine classes generate a saturated lattice in Z^45. A 36x36 boundary minor has determinant 2, so rank(d2)>=36, while the nine tori force rank<=36: kernel is primitive rank 9 and equals the nine torus classes. Every nine are basis by the single diagonal relation, not just rational spanning. The original source exact verifier exhausts all 720 vertex-permutation actions on signed 2-cubes; independent regeneration reproduced C0,C1,C2=(15,60,45) and both minors (1,2). Rational Specht character follows, but integral quotient is a strictly stronger invariant than character. Does not extend to n>6 or ordered particles.
