# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The collision-free two-particle cubical model of \(K_6\) is valid without edge subdivision. Integer cellular boundary signs, all ten disjoint-triangle product cycles and their diagonal relation are checked from complete graph data; an explicit \(9\times9\) torus minor has determinant \(+1\) and a \(36\times36\) boundary minor has determinant \(+2\). Together with the ten-cycle relation, these prove the kernel has rank nine and is generated integrally by any nine tori. The complete signed action of all \(720\) vertex permutations is checked at chain level. No finite computation is extrapolated to an infinite family.

## Originality — PASS, with residual bibliographic risk
The closest inspected primary source, Ramos–Yun (arXiv:2606.13813v1, §3.2), already proves the rational irreducible representation for unordered two-particle complete-graph configurations and identifies disjoint-triangle toric generators of the corresponding ordered rational module. These are explicitly excluded from the new claim. It does not establish the unique integral ten-term relation, the index-one property of every set of nine torus generators, or the sign-twisted **integral** permutation quotient. Ko–Park (arXiv:1101.2648v1) studies graph-braid first homology rather than this integral second-homology presentation. Related graph-configuration and torus-lattice findings on other graphs do not imply integral saturation in this complex. Equivalent unlocated literature remains a risk; publication priority is not independently certified.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The all-ten-generator relation and index-one basis property resolve the natural geometric integral presentation for the first complete graph with paired disjoint triangle tori. They distinguish a saturated integral lattice from the previously known rational representation. The exact action of every graph automorphism is a further structurally useful integral invariant. The claim is limited to this natural finite configuration space, with no unsupported general-family extrapolation.

## Limitations
The proof makes no claim about integral \(S_n\)-modules for larger \(n\), first-homology torsion, or exhaustive bibliographic priority. Its given determinants are reproducible by a standalone finite exact verifier. Further comparison with less-indexed literature remains appropriate.
