Exactly ten \(\mathbf F_3\)-isomorphism classes of geometrically smooth plane quartics have six rational points forming a nontrivial blocking set, the smallest possible such point set. Their projective equation orbits contain nine sets of \(5,616\) equations and one of \(1,404\), for exactly \(51,948\) smooth extremal-blocking quartic equations over \(\mathbf F_3\).
