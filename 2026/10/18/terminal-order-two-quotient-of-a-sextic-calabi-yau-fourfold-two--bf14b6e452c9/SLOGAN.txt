For every smooth plane sextic, the associated sextic Calabi–Yau fourfold modulo the block swap has exactly two genus-\(10\) singular curves of transverse type \(\frac12(1,1,1)\), and their blowup is smooth with discrepancy \(\frac12\). The quotient and resolution have rational Betti vectors \((1,0,1,0,1283,0,1,0,1)\) and \((1,0,3,40,1287,40,3,0,1)\), respectively.
