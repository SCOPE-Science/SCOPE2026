The complete length-two substring-composition observations of ternary words have an explicit rational generating function and exactly \(n^5/80+O(n^4)\) distinct profiles at length \(n\). For every fixed alphabet size \(q\), a connected Euler-trail characterization gives the sharp leading profile count \(\frac{\binom{q+1}{2}}{2^{q-1}(\binom{q+1}{2}-1)!}n^{\binom{q+1}{2}-1}\).
