# Reproducibility and verification limits

Execute `python verify_k3_sections.py` from a directory containing this script. The script has no external dependencies and reconstructs the matrix \(Q\) from the original geometric height matrix \(G\) and the published integral-basis replacement \(T\), without relying on any saved computation output.

The certificate checks positivity and rational \(LDL^{\!T}\) reconstruction of \(Q\), and uses the proved completion-of-squares bounds to enumerate all integral vectors with \(z^{\!T}Qz\le12\). It checks the exhaustive norm histogram \((1,204,576,432)\) at \((0,8,10,12)\), the two type-IV component signatures and their multiplicities, all \(20706\) unordered pairs of minimal sections, and every weighted row sum. Finally it verifies \((RQ-120I)(RQ-144I)=0\), \(\operatorname{tr}(RQ)=1632\), and the exact orthogonality needed to derive the stated full spectrum. It independently counts \(E_2\) and \(E_5\) points over \(\mathbf F_{23}\).

The finite enumeration is exhaustive within its rigorously proved lattice bound, not a sampling or a computation extending to arbitrary height. The complete geometric Mordell--Weil basis, type-IV fibre structure, and specialized height pairing are taken from cited published literature. Their mathematical proofs are not regenerated by this script. No independent external audit has been performed.
