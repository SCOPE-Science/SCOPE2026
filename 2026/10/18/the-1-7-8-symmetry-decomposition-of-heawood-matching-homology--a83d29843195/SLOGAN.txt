The rational top homology of the Heawood graph matching complex splits, under its full 336-element automorphism group, into irreducible representations of dimensions \(1\), \(7\), and \(8\). The \(1\oplus7\) summand is the permutation representation on the eight edge one-factorizations, and the full-symmetry quotient has one-dimensional reduced rational homology in degree four.
