For every Möbius ladder \(M_n\) with \(n\ge3\), the entire 1-limited dominating-set distribution has an explicit logarithmic-derivative generating function. Its minimum cardinality is \(n\) precisely when \(4\mid n\) and \(n+1\) otherwise; the numbers of minimizers are respectively \(4\), \(2n\) for \(n\equiv2\pmod4\), and \(3n\) for odd \(n\).
