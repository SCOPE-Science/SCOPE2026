# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For conic pencils over GF(4), characteristic-two polar gradient shows a ternary conic smooth iff its unique critical point is off the conic. Source exact full census enumerates 1365 projective conic forms, 1008 smooth, all 93093 projective 2-planes, exactly 20160 pencils whose five GF4-rational members are smooth. Frame of one rational plus three Frobenius-conjugate base points has stabilizer order 3 in PGL3(F4) of order 60480, showing one orbit. I independently implemented GF4 form/line arithmetic and verified all 1365 and 1008 single-conic cases, and for the canonical smooth pencil found 15 lines skew to ≥1 member, precisely 108 unordered three-line full witnesses, and no two-line witness. The general two-line obstruction follows because two lines meet in a point P and a rational pencil member passes through P (or P is a base point). All other pencils are projectively equivalent by the proved orbit and full source finite census.

## originality

PASS

Asgarli–Ghioca establish existence of smooth rational conic pencils and four-base-point construction generally; Asgarli–Ghioca–Yip supply nonblocking and efficient witness existence, often in odd-characteristic conditions. Neither establishes every GF4 pencil's 108 triple incidence. One rational pencil can exist without proving classification of all 20160; a prior three-line existence theorem does not force 108 unordered triples per pencil. Known two-line impossibility is acknowledged rather than presented as new. Priority is best-of-knowledge; accessible primary material and finite indexed Resultary results cannot establish exhaustive noncoverage.

PASS

Every-pencil uniform 108-optimal-triple theorem and precise one-orbit enumeration in PG(2,4) is a motivated complete incidence classification for efficient line witnesses to nonblocking smooth conic pencils; exact small-field counts are natural, not arbitrary slices.
