# Review of the rank-one niltriangular commuting-tuple result

## Correctness
PASS. All rank-at-most-one minors and the three nonzero commutator coordinates were independently reconstructed. The nonzero middle-coordinate stratum is dense in a product of irreducible \(2\times2\) determinantal cones, and its complement decomposes into coordinate components and proper projective-line incidence images. The generic noncontainment argument proves that the entire listed cover consists of components, not just subvarieties. The finite-field point count uses disjoint strata, with a unique line in every mixed nonzero configuration. Direct finite enumeration verifies eleven cases, including characteristic two and the extension field \(\mathbb F_4\).

## Originality
PASS within the inspected source scope. The closest published theorem is Basili's explicit two-component classification of the **unrestricted** \(4\times4\) niltriangular commuting-pair variety (arXiv:0803.0722, Example A). Intersecting that two-component variety with separate per-matrix rank-one determinantal loci does not imply the present five-component classification, and it gives neither higher-tuple components nor an exact finite-field count. The papers of Wong–Ma–Zhou and Ou–Zhong analyze graph automorphisms on rank-one upper-triangular matrices; their target invariant differs, and their identified support parametrizations do not imply the component count. Earlier full-rank-constrained matrix results in other families do not imply this fixed-flag intersection. Residual risk: an equivalent flag-constrained determinantal classification might be present under terminology not located by the searches, and parts of the 2014 paper and the 2016/2017 Basili version were not fully accessible.

## Value
PASS. This is a complete classification and point-count hierarchy for the first strictly upper-triangular size in which three row/column support components and a genuine mixed orthogonality mechanism coexist. It gives a sharp contrast with the known two-component unrestricted commuting-pair variety: after a natural rank-one restriction, there are five components for pairs and exponentially many in tuple length, with a precise non-equidimensionality boundary. The component classification and all-arity arithmetic invariant are motivated by a natural determinantal cut of a studied commuting variety; no general theorem for larger matrices is asserted.

## Limitations
Only reduced varieties, rank at most one, strict triangular shape in size four, and labeled ordered tuples are treated. The standalone finite-field program does not substitute for the proof over all fields. Literature-priority review is not independent.
