For the reduced variety of ordered commuting \(t\)-tuples of rank-at-most-one strictly upper-triangular \(4\times4\) matrices, there are exactly \(2^t+1\) geometric irreducible components: three of dimension \(3t\) and \(2^t-2\) of dimension \(2t+1\). An exact closed formula counts every such ordered tuple over every finite field, and the varieties are non-equidimensional precisely when \(t\ge2\).
