For the quartic \(e_4\) in nine variables over an algebraically closed field of characteristic three, the entire reduced projective singular locus consists of exactly \(351\) lines: \(36\) coordinate lines and \(315\) balanced lines indexed by a singleton and an unordered four/four partition of the remaining coordinates. Their union has degree \(351\), and the ambient quartic sevenfold is geometrically integral and normal; the nonreduced Jacobian scheme is not classified.
