The normal-fan toric sixfold of the seven-element non-Fano matroid has exactly 58 reduced singular components: 18 copies of \(\mathbb P^3\), 24 copies of \(\mathbb P^2\), 15 copies of \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\), and one isolated fixed point. Their generic transverse models are rank-one determinantal cones of sizes \(2\times2\), \(2\times3\), and \(3\times4\), with the last of multiplicity ten.
