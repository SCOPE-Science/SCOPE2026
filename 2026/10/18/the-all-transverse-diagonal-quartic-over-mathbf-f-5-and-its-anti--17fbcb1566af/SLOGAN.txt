Among the 64 diagonal degree-two del Pezzo models \(w^2=ax^4+by^4+cz^4\) over \(\mathbf F_5\), every one of the 31 rational anticanonical curves is geometrically smooth exactly when \((a,b,c)\) is a permutation of \((\lambda,\lambda,2\lambda)\). These two quadratic-twist classes have distinct complete genus-one section point-count spectra, even though neither branch quartic has a rational point.
