The terminal quotient of the cubic CM elliptic fourfold in Ajit’s non-vanishing counterexample has a Cartier Néron–Severi pullback lattice of index \(3^{10}\), cut out by reduction of Hermitian matrices modulo \(1-\zeta\). Its nef cone is the full positive-semidefinite Hermitian cone, with infinitely many extremal rays, so the fourfold is not a Mori dream space.
