For every geometrically smooth minimal bidegree-\((4,4)\) curve filling \(\mathbf P^1\times\mathbf P^1\) over \(\mathbf F_3\), the genus-nine Jacobian has \(3\)-rank either \(8\) or \(9\), and \(a\)-number at most \(1\). Exactly \(48\) of the \(264\) embedded curves are ordinary, and they constitute one orbit under the ambient rational automorphism group.
