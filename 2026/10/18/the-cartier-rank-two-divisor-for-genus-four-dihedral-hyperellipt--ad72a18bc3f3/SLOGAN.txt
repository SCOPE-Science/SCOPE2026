For the two-parameter dihedral genus-four hyperelliptic normal form in characteristic \(7\), the entire smooth-family nonordinary locus is the explicit Cartier determinant curve \(P(s,t)=0\), with \(a\)-number exactly \(2\) there and \(0\) elsewhere. Among the \(31\) smooth \(\mathbf F_7\)-parameter pairs, exactly two have \(a=2\), and both have \(7\)-rank zero.
