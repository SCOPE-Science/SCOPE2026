# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The smooth toric hexagon is covered by six symmetric affine-plane charts. The relative Hasse 2-jet matrix is written explicitly in the proof. Exhaustive *integer* computation of all its maximal proper minors gives the integral ideal \((2u^2,uv,2v^2)\) on every chart. Taking fibers produces \((u,v)^2\) outside characteristic two and \((uv)\) in characteristic two; the global schemes and exact ranks follow by toric gluing. The standalone integer minor checker ran successfully, while the global six-chart argument was verified mathematically rather than inferred from a finite sample. The six line components in characteristic two meet cyclically in six nodes and have Hilbert polynomial \(6d\).

Perkinson also supplies general toric Fitting-minor machinery. The present new assertion is the exact integral 5-minor ideal and its characteristic-two degeneration to the reduced boundary, including the rank-four stratum. Comparisons of equivalent formulations, broader general theorems, exact tables, and statement-to-statement implications found no explicit equivalent calculation in the examined primary material. ## Mathematical value — PASS
A classical Laplace-defective smooth toric surface changes from having six isolated length-three infinitesimal flex loci to possessing an entire reduced inflection divisor after reduction modulo two. The universal integral Fitting ideal captures the arithmetic cause and measures nonflatness, and the resulting degree/genus and rank strata are natural geometric invariants. This is a structural positive-characteristic boundary phenomenon rather than a routine restatement of the known characteristic-zero rank table.
