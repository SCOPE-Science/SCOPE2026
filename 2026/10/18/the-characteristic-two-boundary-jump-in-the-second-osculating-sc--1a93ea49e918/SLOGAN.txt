For the smooth degree-six Togliatti del Pezzo projection in \(\mathbb P^5\), the second Hasse-jet rank-drop ideal has universal vertex-chart equation \((2u^2,uv,2v^2)\). Its fibers are six length-three fat points outside characteristic two, but in characteristic two they jump to the reduced six-line toric boundary cycle, with rank three at vertices and rank four along the open boundary.
