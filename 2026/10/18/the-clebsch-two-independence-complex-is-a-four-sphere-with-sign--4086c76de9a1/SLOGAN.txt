The two-independence complex of the 16-vertex degree-five Clebsch graph has exact integral homotopy type \(S^4\), despite being a seven-dimensional nonpure simplicial complex. Its full automorphism group \((\mathbb Z/2)^4\rtimes S_5\) acts on the integral generator of \(\widetilde H_4\cong\mathbb Z\) by the sign of the \(S_5\) permutation, with translations acting trivially.
