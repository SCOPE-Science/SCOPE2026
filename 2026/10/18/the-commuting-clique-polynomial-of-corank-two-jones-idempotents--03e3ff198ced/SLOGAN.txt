Among the rank-\(n-2\) idempotents of the Jones diagram monoid, distinct diagrams commute precisely when their elementary-generator supports are separated by at least one index. Their full commuting-clique polynomial satisfies \(P_m=P_{m-1}+zP_{m-2}+2zP_{m-3}\), and its extremal cliques and ordered commuting-pair count have exact formulas for every \(n\ge2\).
