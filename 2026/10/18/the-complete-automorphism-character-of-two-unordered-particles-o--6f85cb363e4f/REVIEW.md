# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The cubical chain model is specified in full, including signs, subdivision criterion and every finite index range. The executed standalone checker verifies the boundary composition, ranks \(152,270\) over \(\mathbb F_3\), the rank \(162\) of the augmented first-cycle map, all \(216\) automorphism traces, and the exact 13-class multiplication algebra. The rank inequalities imply rational Betti numbers \(1,10,27\); the natural equivariant cycle map provides the full first-homology character; the Lefschetz identity then gives the second character. Six exact class-algebra homomorphisms and orthogonality identities prove irreducibility and all multiplicities. The second integral homology is free because it is the kernel of a morphism of free chain groups with no dimension-three chains.

## Originality — PASS
The closest general treatments are Farber–Hanbury (arXiv:1005.2300) and Ko–Park (arXiv:1101.2648). They provide substantial graph-configuration frameworks but no explicit full Pappus 216-element two-particle character. Other indexed studies address different graphs, the three-particle Pappus case, signed Borel–Moore theories, Pappus independence homology or Pappus matching homology. Searches for alternative titles and equivalent exact module decompositions found no coverage; this search absence is not a claim of exhaustive world-literature novelty. No newer paper is used as the archival anchor. Remaining risks are unindexed treatments and unpublished computations.

## Value — PASS
The Pappus graph is a highly symmetric incidence graph. Its 216-element action makes full equivariant classification a well-defined natural invariant. The five pairwise distinct rational irreducibles, the nontrivial one-dimensional constituent, the conjugate degree-six pair, and zero invariant subspaces refine numerical configuration-space homology in ways not obtainable from Betti numbers or generic graph-braid abelianization. The finite classification is complete, structurally motivated and independently reproducible, without claiming a general-family theorem.

## Limits
The claimed rational module classification is exact for this graph only. Integral equivariant extensions and the full braid group are outside scope. Residual bibliographic uncertainty is acknowledged.
