For the genus-four hyperelliptic family \(y^2=x^{10}+x^7+ax^4+bx\) over \(\overline{\mathbb F}_{17}\), the complete Cartier \(a\)-number stratification has generic value \(1\), value \(2\) on an explicit coefficient divisor, value \(3\) at exactly \((a,b)=(4,12),(10,7)\), and value \(4\) at the known superspecial point \((13,12)\). Exact characteristic-\(17\) elimination establishes the classification over the algebraic closure, rather than only for tested field points.
