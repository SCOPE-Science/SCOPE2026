For the circular binary word \(0\,1^a0\,1^b0\,1^c\) with \(1\le a<b<c\), every minimal absent contiguous factor is explicitly classified; there are exactly \(c+4\), independent of the smaller gaps. The full antidictionary has respectively \(1,1,c,2\) members with zero, one, two, and three zero symbols.
