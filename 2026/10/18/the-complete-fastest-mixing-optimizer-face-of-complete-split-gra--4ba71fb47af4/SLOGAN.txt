For a fastest-mixing symmetric walk on the complete split graph \(K_k\vee\overline{K_m}\), every cross-edge transition is forced to be uniform, but the full set of optimal clique-internal transitions is an explicit Laplacian spectral band. At the sharp thresholds \(m=k\) and \(m=2k\), optimal clique edges change from necessary to optional to impossible, and the globally optimal kernel becomes unique exactly when \(m\ge2k\); the optimal mixing-rate formula itself follows from prior clique-lift results.
