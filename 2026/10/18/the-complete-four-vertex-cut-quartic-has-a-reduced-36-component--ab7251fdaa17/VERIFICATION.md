# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Differentiating the exact binomial K4 cut equation F=product(x1..x4)-product(y1..y4) gives eight squarefree degree-three monomials, independent of characteristic. Each four-variable squarefree Veronese ideal is the intersection of six coordinate ideals generated by two variables, so the disjoint-block sum is radical with precisely 36 minimal coordinate height-four primes, giving 36 reduced P3 components and no embedded primes. Hilbert–Burch for each height-two ideal gives 0→R(-4)^3→R(-3)^4→R→R/L→0. Their tensor resolution is minimal exact, yielding Betti positions (0,0):1,(1,3):8,(2,4):6,(2,6):16,(3,7):24,(4,8):9 and h(t)=(1+2t+3t²)^2. The coordinate ring has dimension=depth=4, degree36, CM type9. h=(1,4,10,12,9) gives Euler characteristic -8, arithmetic genus9. At generic points of each P3, four transverse zero coordinates put F into uv-st=0, including small characteristics. Independent symbolic recomputation of the generators, Betti numerator, 36 minimal prime count, h vector and genus corroborated these deductions. No affine/projective saturation ambiguity: the ideal is radical and has no irrelevant associated prime.

## originality

PASS

PASS with substantive caveat: Sturmfels–Sullivant explicitly published the defining K4 quartic, and the present derivative + squarefree Veronese resolution uses classical tools. The particular complete Jacobian scheme, 36 P3 components, full Betti/h-vector, CM type and arithmetic genus were not reported in the relevant primary cut-variety work examined. Generic binomial monomial-Jacobian literature could make this a special case, which is a real originality risk.

## value

PASS

K4 is the first complete-graph quartic cut hypersurface, a canonical small benchmark. Complete scheme-theoretic singularity resolution (radicality, irreducible components, full homological invariants and transverse type) is a coherent exact natural invariant package not equivalent to checking nonsmoothness or repeating the defining equation. Although elementary from identified monomial ideals, it is an independently useful classification of this standard toric object.
