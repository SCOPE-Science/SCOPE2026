# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact face-sign/switching-class bijection reduces the \(2^{11}\) classes to even vertex subsets of the dual icosahedron. The minimum negative-edge count equals the minimum dual \(T\)-join size, proved by the shortest-path pairing argument. Two distinct exhaustive programs check every one of 2048 classes and agree pointwise. The 120-element full graph automorphism action and its 46 orbits are checked from explicit permutations. The unique weight-six class has exactly 125 minimum supports by the independently implemented perfect-matching recursion.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risks

Solé–Zaslavsky (1994) already provides the coding and duality framework and access to the maximum frustration six. Zaslavsky (2012) classifies signings of the Petersen graph, which is not the dodecahedron. Bowlin (2012) treats bipartite signed graphs. Sehrawat–Bhattacharjya (2019) obtains bounds for generalized Petersen graphs without the full distribution, 46 symmetry types, or unique maximal-class minimizer count. The exact present claim was not recovered in targeted research-record and prior-claim comparisons. The full indexing of historical computational tables and unindexed literature was not established; publication priority remains subject to external confirmation.

## Value — PASS

A signed graph's worst possible frustration alone does not tell how typical the obstruction is or which classes achieve it. This complete 2048-class distribution quantifies imbalance across all dodecahedral signings, while its 46-class symmetry quotient supplies a canonical classification and the 125-minimizer phenomenon gives a natural dual matching interpretation. It is a motivated finite classification of a standard symmetric graph, not an arbitrary restricted parameter choice.
