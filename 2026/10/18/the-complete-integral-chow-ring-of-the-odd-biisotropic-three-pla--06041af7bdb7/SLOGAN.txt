The smooth odd biisotropic three-plane sixfold \(\operatorname{BIGr}(3,7)\) has a complete integral Chow and even-cohomology ring presentation \(\mathbb Z[c_1,c_2,c_3]/((h_5,h_6,h_7):(c_1c_2-c_3)^2)\). Unimodular Schubert intersection pairings prove that ambient classes generate the integral ring, answering the multiplicative-structure question for this first nontrivial three-plane case.
