The induced-forest filtration of the icosahedral graph has integral homotopy types \(S^2\vee\bigvee^6 S^1\), \(\bigvee^{20}S^3\), and \(\bigvee^{40}S^4\) at degrees zero, one, and every degree at least two. Its complete persistent homology consists of bars in degrees one through four, with no torsion.
