For every \(2\le m<n\), the **simultaneous**, not merely target-only, source-and-color permutation character on the top rational homology of \(\operatorname{Hom}(K_m,K_n)\) is determined by cycle lengths and divisibility. Under diagonal relabeling of three sources and three corresponding target colors, the quotient has top rational Betti number \(\bigl(2^n-3+7(-1)^{n-3}\bigr)/6\), a sharp contrast with the previously known rationally acyclic full color quotient.
