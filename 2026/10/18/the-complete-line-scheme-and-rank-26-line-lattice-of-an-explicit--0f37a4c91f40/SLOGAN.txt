For the explicit sextic branch surface \(x_1^6+x_1x_2^5+x_3^6-x_4^6=0\), the entire line Hilbert scheme consists of exactly \(36\) reduced geometric points, of which exactly \(4\) are rational, and the corresponding divisor classes span a rank-\(26\) subspace. More generally, the analogous degree-\(d\) split surfaces for every \(d\ge4\) have exactly \(d^2\) geometric lines with an explicit finite-étale parameter scheme and rank \((d-1)^2+1\) line-class span.
