For the Petersen graph, the degree-two induced-forest complex simplicially collapses onto the degree-one complex in exactly 180 elementary steps; their common homotopy type is \(S^3\), while the unrestricted induced-forest complex is \(\bigvee^6S^5\). The resulting integral forest-filtration barcode has four \(H_2\) intervals \([0,1)\), one \(H_3\) interval \([1,3)\), and six \(H_5\) intervals \([3,\infty)\).
