The matching complex of the truncated-tetrahedral graph has an exactly determined rational \(S_4\)-homology character \((22,0,2,-2,-2)\) in degree three, splitting as \(S^{(1,1,1,1)}\oplus3S^{(3,1)}\oplus2S^{(2,1,1)}\oplus3S^{(2,2)}\). Its homology has a unique alternating-group-invariant line but no invariant class under all graph automorphisms.
