For a uniform interior point of any Euclidean parallelogram, the number of distinct boundary normals has an exact three-atom law on \(\{4,6,8\}\). At fixed acute-angle cosine \(c>0\), the eight-normal event disappears exactly when the side ratio reaches \(1/c\), where the six-normal probability uniquely peaks at \(1-c^2\); rhombi uniquely maximize the mean at \(8-4c\).
