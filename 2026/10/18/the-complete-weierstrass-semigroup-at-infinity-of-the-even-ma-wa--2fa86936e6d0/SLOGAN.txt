For the new even-exponent maximal curves of Ma–Wang, the entire Weierstrass semigroup at the unique point at infinity is an explicitly generated arithmetic progression \(\langle m,m+b,\ldots,q^n,q^n+1\rangle\), with \(b=q(q-1)\) and \(m=q^2(q^{n-1}+1)/(q+1)\). Its conductor and minimal embedding dimension are determined uniformly, and the semigroup is never symmetric.
