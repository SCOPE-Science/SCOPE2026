# The complete wild-ramification and QRT spectrum of binary biquadratic genus-one curves

## Finding
Let \(k=\mathbf F_2\), and let \(C\subset\mathbf P^1_k\times\mathbf P^1_k\) range over all geometrically smooth divisors of bidegree \((2,2)\) defined by nonzero bihomogeneous binary forms. For each \(C\), let \(N=\#C(k)\), let \(b_x,b_y\) be the numbers of rational branch fibres of its two degree-two ruling maps, and let \(m\) be the exact order of the automorphism obtained by composing the two ruling involutions. The pair of branch counts is sorted into nondecreasing order.

Exactly \(144\) out of \(511\) nonzero coefficient forms define geometrically smooth curves. Under the full ambient automorphism group \((\operatorname{PGL}_2(k)\times\operatorname{PGL}_2(k))\rtimes C_2\), which has order \(72\), they form precisely the following six orbits. Every row is a single orbit.

| \(N\) | \((b_x,b_y)\) | Global QRT order \(m\) | Forms | Orbit representative mask |
| ---: | :---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2 | (0,2) | 2 | 18 | 121 |
| 3 | (1,1) | 3 | 36 | 98 |
| 4 | (0,0) | 2 | 9 | 186 |
| 4 | (0,2) | 4 | 36 | 122 |
| 4 | (2,2) | 2 | 9 | 113 |
| 5 | (1,1) | 5 | 36 | 99 |
| **Total** | | | **144** | **6 orbits** |

The order-five locus is exactly one ambient orbit of \(36\) forms. Rational point count alone does not determine the QRT order: the three rows with \(N=4\) have orders two or four. The \((1,1)\) projection types feature one geometrically ramified rational fibre in each ruling, a phenomenon of wild characteristic-two branching.

## Assumptions and scope
Fix homogeneous coordinates \(([X_0:X_1],[Y_0:Y_1])\). Number the nine bihomogeneous monomials by \(k=3i+j\), for \(0\leq i,j\leq2\), with the monomial at this position equal to
\[
X_0^{2-i}X_1^iY_0^{2-j}Y_1^j.
\]
A coefficient mask \(M\in\{1,\ldots,511\}\) is the form whose coefficient at position \(k\) is the \(k\)-th binary digit of \(M\). A scalar does not create a further form because \(k^\times\) is trivial. Every smooth divisor of bidegree \((2,2)\) is geometrically integral and has genus one by adjunction. Its degree-two projections are separable, and each has a canonical deck involution.

The branch count \(b_x\) (respectively \(b_y\)) is the number of points of \(\mathbf P^1(k)\) whose geometric fibre under the first (respectively second) projection consists of one point of multiplicity two. A non-ramified fibre has either zero or two rational points. In characteristic two, a degree-two cover of an elliptic curve over the projective line can have wild ramification; no tame-four-branch-point assumption is used.

The order \(m\) is the order of the **global automorphism** \(\iota_y\circ\iota_x\) of the smooth complete curve, where \(\iota_x\) fixes the first ruling coordinate and \(\iota_y\) fixes the second. Reversing the composition gives the inverse and the same order. The classification is of embedded equations under the full rational automorphism group of the split quadric, **not** a claim of six abstract elliptic-curve isomorphism classes.

## Proof
**Geometric smoothness.** There are \(2^9-1=511\) nonzero coefficient vectors. For every mask, on the four standard affine charts, substitute either \(X_0=1\) or \(X_1=1\) and either \(Y_0=1\) or \(Y_1=1\). Form the ideal of the resulting polynomial \(f\) and its two partial derivatives. In each chart the curve has no geometric singularity exactly when this ideal is the unit ideal over \(\mathbf F_2\). The stand-alone exact Gröbner computation in `check_qrt_sympy.py` implements this criterion for every mask and finds exactly \(144\) smooth forms. It uses computation in the polynomial ring over \(\mathbf F_2\), not the absence of rational singularities.

**Orbit exhaustiveness.** The group \(\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbf F_2)\) is generated by the homogeneous-coordinate swap and shear. Apply those two substitutions independently in each ruling and add the factor interchange. They generate the complete \(72\)-element ambient group. Acting on every smooth coefficient vector produces six disjoint orbits of sizes \(18,36,9,36,9,36\), exhausting all \(144\) vectors. The full orbit representatives and explicit finite generating substitutions are printed in the stand-alone checker `qrt_f2_verify_independent.py`. In particular no unexamined projective-equivalence classes remain.

**Rational branches.** For each of the nine points of \(\mathbf P^1(\mathbf F_2)^2\), substitute its homogeneous coordinates exactly. In a smooth degree-two fibre defined over \(\mathbf F_2\), the number of rational geometric points is \(0,1,\) or \(2\); the middle possibility forces a double point of the fibre and hence branch. Counting singleton rational fibres gives the unordered branch pair in the table. For context in characteristic two, writing the equation as \(A(X)Y_0^2+B(X)Y_0Y_1+C(X)Y_1^2=0\), the branch locus of the first projection is the zero scheme of \(B(X)\). A double root gives one wild geometric branch fibre, whereas two separable roots yield two geometric branch fibres. Thus branch counts \(1,2,0\) correspond, respectively, to one rational wild branch, two rational branches, or a conjugate quadratic pair in this finite family.

**Global translation order.** Choose any \(O\in C(\mathbf F_2)\), which exists for all six rows. For an elliptic curve with origin \(O\), every divisor class of degree two determines the involution \(P\mapsto Q-P\), where \(Q\) is its class minus \(2O\). Therefore the two ruling deck involutions are of this form, and their composite is translation by a rational point of \(C(\mathbf F_2)\). Its order as an automorphism equals its order as a permutation of \(C(\mathbf F_2)\), since applying powers to \(O\) detects the identity. This argument holds in characteristic two and does not mistake finite rational-point iteration for a proof of an arbitrary map's global periodicity.

The checker constructs the two involutions by exchanging the two rational points over each coordinate (fixing singleton ramified fibres), computes their composite, and determines its exact permutation order. Every one of the six group orbits has the profile in the table. The composition globally has the asserted exact order by the translation argument.

**Independent finite-field cross-check.** A second, polynomial-arithmetic-independent program `qrt_f2_verify_independent.py` evaluates the defining polynomial and all four homogeneous derivatives at every \(\mathbf P^1(\mathbf F_{16})^2\) point using an explicit field \(\mathbf F_2[t]/(t^4+t+1)\). All \(367\) singular forms detected by Gröbner bases have a singularity already over \(\mathbf F_2\) (\(318\) forms) or \(\mathbf F_4\) (\(49\) forms). The same program independently reconstructs all six automorphism orbits and all rational dynamic profiles. The Gröbner criterion supplies the geometric-smoothness proof; the extension-field sweep is a supplementary independent cross-check, not a claim that a bounded point search generally decides smoothness.

## Relationship to prior work
Joshi and Kassotakis, *Re-factorising a QRT Map* (2019), describe QRT maps as compositions of the two biquadratic ruling involutions and recall earlier globally periodic examples, including period five. Neither the construction of a QRT map nor the existence of order-five examples is claimed as new. Asgarli and Ghioca, *Smoothness in Pencils of Hypersurfaces over Finite Fields* (2022), address smoothness across linear families, a related but different finite-field question. Standard genus-one theory interprets marked degree-two linear systems as involutions.

The present claim concerns the entire binary coefficient space, its six rational ambient orbits, characteristic-two wild branch splitting, and the exact joint distribution with global translation orders. No inspected primary source tabulates or logically gives this specific complete spectrum. Earlier finite-field analogues outside characteristic two do not determine wild branch behaviour or these coefficients.

## Limitations
Only smooth bidegree-\((2,2)\) *embedded* curves over \(\mathbf F_2\) are classified, not curves over all finite fields or all abstract elliptic curves. The count of six is an ambient-orbit count. Global QRT order is inferred from its action on rational points only after proving the translation structure. A previously unindexed complete binary biquadratic classification remains a possible literature-priority risk.
