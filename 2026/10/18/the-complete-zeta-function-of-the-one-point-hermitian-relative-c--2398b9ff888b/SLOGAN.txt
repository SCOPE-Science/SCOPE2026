The unique Hermitian-relative plane-curve class with just one \(\mathbb F_{s^2}\)-point has zeta numerator \(\bigl(1-(-sT)^p\bigr)^{s^2/p}/(1+sT)^s\) for every \(s=p^r\). Its Frobenius eigenvalues are \(-s\psi(a)\) for \(a\in\mathbb F_s^\times\), each with multiplicity \(s\), giving exact point counts over every finite extension.
