The joint height–Smith-content zeta function for rank-one \(m\times n\) polynomial matrices over \(\mathbb F_q[T]\) is an explicit rational function in two variables. At fixed matrix dimensions, the content degree of a uniformly chosen rank-one matrix of height degree \(D\) converges to a geometric law with ratio \(q^{1-\max\{m,n\}}\), while equal dimensions produce a sharp linear enhancement in the total height count.
