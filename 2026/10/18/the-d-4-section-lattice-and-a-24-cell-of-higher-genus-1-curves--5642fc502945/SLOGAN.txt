For every prescribed genus \(g\ge2\), the Li--Liu elliptic surface contains a rank-four inherited Mordell--Weil lattice \(2D_4^*\), yielding 24 distinct genus-\(g\) \((-1)\)-sections disjoint from the zero section. Their pairwise intersection pattern is uniform: each meets the other 23 with intersection numbers \(0,1,2,3\) in multiplicities \(8,6,8,1\).
