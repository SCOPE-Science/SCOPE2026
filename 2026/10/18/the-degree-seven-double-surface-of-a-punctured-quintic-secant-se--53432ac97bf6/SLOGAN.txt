The secant hypersurface of the rational monomial quintic \([1:t:t^3:t^4:t^5]\subset\mathbb P^4\) is an integral sextic whose entire reduced singular locus is one irreducible rational surface of degree \(7\). This surface is an affine-plane graph completed by two intersecting boundary lines, and the general transverse singularity is an ordinary two-branch node, making the sextic nonnormal.
