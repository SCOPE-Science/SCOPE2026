# Scientific review — density-seven prefix-normal generating function

## Correctness — PASS

The proof starts from the established equivalent gap criterion and reduces all length-seven density constraints to four nontrivial linear inequalities after nonnegative changes of variables. A six-cone partition covers all admissible triples with explicit inverse parameters. Summing the remaining two variables gives an exact series; integer-polynomial denominator clearing in the delivered verifier confirms the stated rational expression. Independently, direct factor-based enumeration and the gap method agree for every length from 7 through 25 and reproduce the generating-function coefficients. The infinite result follows algebraically; no finite check is presented as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS

The 2014 source states fixed-density generating functions only for densities zero through six (Section 4, Lemma 3), though it provides the gap criterion used here. The 2016 expanded source proves general fixed-density rationality, again gives explicit formulas only through density six (Section 4.2, Proposition 16), and says attempts at concrete formulas beyond six were unsuccessful. The present explicit numerator and denominator for seven are not stated there and do not follow as a numerical identity from merely knowing rationality. Semantic published-record checks and existing pertinent comparison records did not identify a covering result. Bibliographic incompleteness remains possible, and this is not an independent priority certification.

## Value — PASS

Density seven is the first fixed number of ones absent from the explicit source formulas, not a freely chosen numerical slice. The result determines exact counts at every length via a finite rational expression and resolves the documented difficulty of computing the next density. The six-cone decomposition reveals a nontrivial structured lattice-point count, rather than simple substitution in an earlier case. No general-density formula is claimed.
