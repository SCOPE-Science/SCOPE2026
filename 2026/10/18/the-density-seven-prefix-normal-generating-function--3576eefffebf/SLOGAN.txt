The exact length-generating function for binary prefix-normal words with seven ones is \(F_7(z)=z^7P(z)/[(1-z)(1-z^3)(1-z^4)^2(1-z^6)^3]\), where the explicitly determined palindromic degree-18 polynomial \(P\) is given in the proof. This supplies the first density beyond the previously displayed formulas through six.
