# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The classification uses the full Luo--Meng linearity theorem for irreducible totally invariant varieties, then a direct elementary proof: invariance under the coordinate root-of-unity deck group forces each invariant linear subspace to be coordinate; the inverse-image equality is equivalent to a block of zeros of the mixing matrix. Directed reachability, condensation and independent linear conditions yield the incidence lattice and coefficient-locus decomposition. Explicit triangular matrices realize every finite distributive lattice. The finite verifier gives independent exact sanity checks but is not the infinite proof.

## Originality
PASS with residual risk. Luo--Meng, arXiv:2610.10103v1, proves the general linearity theorem, not the specialized support-graph classification or parameter-space component formula. Chang--Luo, arXiv:2607.27738v1, already proves the sharp numerical binomial bound and shows equality for pure coordinate power maps; this known result is not claimed as novel. No equivalent statement was found in the searched mathematical records. The complete Chang--Luo text was not accessible, so a more specialized overlap cannot be conclusively excluded. No database search proves uniqueness.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The result provides a constructive answer for an entire natural family of mixed power maps: it recovers every irreducible invariant variety, their inclusions and their exact parameter-space prevalence from a graph, and realizes every finite distributive incidence lattice. This is more informative than a general linearity theorem or the existing maximal numerical count.

## Limits
The classification concerns complex characteristic-zero maps, single-step inverse-image invariance and irreducible closed varieties. Reducible totally invariant or totally periodic sets are not classified. Results are not independently audited.
