For the dodecahedral graph \(D=GP(10,2)\), the two-independence complex has integral homotopy type \(\operatorname{Ind}_2(D)\simeq S^6\), despite possessing eleven-dimensional facets. Its canonical order-ten rotation acts by \(-1\) on the sole nonzero reduced integral homology group \(\widetilde H_6\cong\mathbb Z\), so its cyclic character is \(\chi(\rho^t)=(-1)^t\).
