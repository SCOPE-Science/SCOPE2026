# Review: singular geometry of the second-hypersimplex dual

## Correctness — PASS
The dual equality follows from the tangent condition \(Ax=0\) on the dense torus and the irreducible Gram parametrization of hollow matrices of rank at most \(n-1\). The complete reduced Jacobian support is derived from \(2\operatorname{adj}(A)_{ij}\); at corank one the adjugate has rank one and its off-diagonal entries vanish exactly on zero-row loci. For \(n\ge5\), Gram parametrization by irreducible isotropic cones proves the corank-two rank locus is irreducible of ambient codimension three. Dimension and incomparability arguments prove that the listed \(n+1\) components are exhaustive. Exact rational rank-three normal-map witnesses for sizes five and six, with hyperbolic block induction, justify the generic \(A_1\) transverse type on the determinantal component. The Schur complement gives a nondegenerate quadratic transverse equation along each zero-row component, and Serre's criterion proves normality. The independent code checks finite witness identities but is not treated as an all-dimensional proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual access risk
Bourel--Dickenstein--Rittatore's toric-duality framework supplies the notion of duality, not the singular-locus classification. Devriendt--Friedman--Reinke--Sturmfels classify rank-at-most-three hollow symmetric matrices as a squared Grassmannian; this overlaps a **component** when \(n=5\), but it does not imply the singular locus and generic local types of the rank-at-most-\(n-1\) hollow determinant for every \(n\ge5\). The published four-by-four hollow quartic analysis has eleven singular surfaces, a genuinely different exceptional case excluded from the claim. Searches using the dual discriminant, the hypersimplex, hollow symmetric determinants, corank, zero rows, and equivalent rank terminology did not identify a statement that covers the asserted all-parameter \(n+1\) decomposition. Oldekop's 2026 paper concerns maximum-likelihood Euler stratifications and principal-discriminant factors, rather than explicitly the singularities of this hypersurface, but its full text could not be accessed, leaving residual overlap risk. Search failure alone is not a novelty certificate.

## Value — PASS
The second hypersimplex is a standard toric variety, and its discriminant is a natural geometric object. The result distinguishes two geometrically different singular mechanisms (corank two and a coordinate-supported kernel), proves an exact all-dimensional component count and dimensions, and establishes two transverse quadratic models and normality. The non-equidimensional singular locus for all \(n\ge5\) contrasts with the exceptional eleven-component quartic in dimension four. These are structural geometric invariants, not a formal relabeling or arbitrary small computation.
