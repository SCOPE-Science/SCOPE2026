For every \(n\ge5\), the projective dual of the second-hypersimplex toric variety is a degree-\(n\) hollow symmetric determinant hypersurface whose reduced singular locus has exactly \(n+1\) irreducible components: one corank-two determinantal component and \(n\) zero-row linear components. The generic transverse singularities are ordinary quadratic cones of dimensions two and \(n-2\), respectively, and the hypersurface is normal.
