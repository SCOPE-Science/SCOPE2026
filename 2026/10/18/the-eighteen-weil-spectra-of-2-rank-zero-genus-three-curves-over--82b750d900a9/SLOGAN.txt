The 32 arithmetic isomorphism classes of genus-three hyperelliptic curves of \(2\)-rank zero over \(\mathbf F_4\) realize exactly 18 Weil polynomials, with class multiplicities six singletons, ten doubles and two triples. Every class has the same Newton slopes \(1/3\) and \(2/3\), each repeated three times, so a single Newton stratum contains 18 distinct Jacobian isogeny types.
