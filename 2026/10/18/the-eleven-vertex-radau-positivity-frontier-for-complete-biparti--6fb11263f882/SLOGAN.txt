For third-order, two-stage Radau IIA diffusion on any unweighted complete bipartite graph \(K_{p,q}\), nonnegative completed steps persist for every timestep exactly when \(p+q\leq11\). Every larger graph loses positivity at the explicit step \(h=8/\max(p,q)\), but regains it at sufficiently large steps, so the twelve-vertex frontier is independent of the partition sizes.
