For the classical Perazzo cubic, the Jacobian singular scheme is not merely its reduced plane: it has an embedded smooth conic carrying a square-zero theta-characteristic line bundle \(\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1}(-1)\). Its exact coordinate-ring Hilbert series is \(\frac{1+2t-2t^2}{(1-t)^3}\), and the conic is precisely the pinch-degeneration locus of the transverse normal crossings.
