Every orientation of a rectangle inside a regular pentagon has an explicit sharp area envelope \(5R^2/[4\cos(\pi/5-\theta)\sin(2\pi/5-\theta)]\) on a fundamental \(18^\circ\) orientation interval. The unrestricted maximum is \(R^2(5-\sqrt5)\sin(\pi/5)\), attained by exactly five rectangles, each supported on one pentagon edge.
