The Euclidean four-dimensional double cone has a sharp Fourier-zero-free transverse strip \(s<\pi\), with its first boundary zeros at axial frequencies \(\pm3\pi\). Its nearest real Fourier-zero distance has the explicit expansion \(\kappa^2=\pi^2\alpha^2+9\pi^2-36\pi^2/\alpha^2+O(\alpha^{-4})\), evaluating a previously variational leading constant and its first correction.
