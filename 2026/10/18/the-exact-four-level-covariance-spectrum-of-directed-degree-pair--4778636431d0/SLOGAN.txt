For every independent-arc directed Erdős–Rényi graph, the Pearson-standardized covariance of its full in-/outdegree histogram has exactly two zero modes and only three positive eigenvalue levels: \(n\), \(n^2/(n-1)\), and \(2n\), with exact finite-size multiplicities independent of edge density. The associated universal factor-two upper variance bound is sharp for the centered total degree, while the total in-minus-out degree is deterministic.
