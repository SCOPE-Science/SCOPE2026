For the seven-edge banana graph hypersurface in characteristic zero, the excess of its Jacobian singular scheme over the reduced singular support has exact Hilbert series \(\frac{14t^5+21t^6+21t^7+14t^8}{(1-t)^3}\). The excess is generically square-zero of length two along 35 coordinate projective planes, yielding the Hilbert-polynomial correction \(35d^2-350d+903\).
