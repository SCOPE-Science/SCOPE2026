# Scientific review: exact John ellipsoids of circular frusta

## Correctness — PASS

The proof invokes the standard unique-inscribed-ellipsoid theorem, then uses horizontal orthogonal invariance to reduce the unrestricted problem to a coaxial ellipsoid with two semiaxes. The exact support calculation gives necessary and sufficient containment for the full continuous boundary. The volume derivative \(1-(d+1)(1-t)b\) determines the unique optimizer in both regimes and at their junction; direct integration computes the occupied-volume fraction. A weighted-mean pairing argument proves strict monotonicity for every integer \(d\ge2\). The exact arithmetic checker reproduces the junction identities, rational competitors, sampled boundary inclusion and strict rational-grid monotonicity. Finite checks do not replace analytic quantifiers. All stated endpoints are included.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk

The closest full-text prior work, Güler--Gürtuna (arXiv:0709.0707; later published as DOI 10.1080/10556788.2011.626037), specifically addresses the same truncated second-order cone class but supplies its minimum-volume *covering* ellipsoid; its revised §1 explicitly says the maximum-volume *inscribed* ellipsoid for that class is not solved. A covering ellipsoid does not solve this inscribed problem through a trivial parameter substitution; polars would have to be treated, and no corresponding exact formula is asserted in the source. Brandenberg--König (arXiv:1310.4368) provides general John-inequality context without this explicit two-branch volume formula. Horwitz (arXiv:0907.1647) treats planar inscribed ellipses and universal quadrilateral area inequalities, not this all-dimensional frustum profile. Searches of relevant indexed records found no dominating inscribed-frustum theorem; the closest thematic indexed result treats a different half-cone. The remaining risk is a differently named or unindexed exact classification; searches cannot rule that out.

## Value — PASS

Circular frusta continuously interpolate between two fundamental convex bodies, cones and cylinders. The previously untreated inscribed member of the natural enclosing-versus-inscribed pair receives a complete all-dimensional formula, an exact dimensional contact threshold, a rigid equality classification and a strict occupied-volume law. These supply reusable, non-discrete, affine-invariant benchmarks for convex-body ellipsoid approximations; they are not a mere single-parameter table lookup or an arbitrary special slice. The initial cones and final cylinders are consistent with classical cases, but those endpoints do not imply the sharp intermediate transition.

## Limitations and context

The general uniqueness theorem is cited rather than reproved. The result concerns affinely circular/ellipsoidal homothetic frusta, not arbitrary convex cones or general homothetic section bodies. Historical priority remains uncertain; no independent audit has yet been performed.
