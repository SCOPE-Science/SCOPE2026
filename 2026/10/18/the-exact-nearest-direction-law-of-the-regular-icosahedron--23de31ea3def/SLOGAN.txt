For the twelve vertices of a regular icosahedron, the full nearest-direction spherical-distance distribution is elementary and has a sharp \(3/2\)-power loss at first cap overlap and a quadratic uncovered tail at complete coverage. The same Voronoi geometry gives an exact mean squared nearest-chord loss of \(2-(15/\pi)\arccos(\sqrt5/3)\sin(\tfrac12\arccos(1/\sqrt5))\).
