A closed-form three-layer real \(\operatorname{SUNTF}(2,5,3)\) resolves the smallest open case left by the 2026 stratified-frame construction. Together with an elementary two-layer system and a one-layer rank obstruction, it proves that a real \(\operatorname{SUNTF}(2,5,R)\) exists exactly for integers \(R\geq2\).
