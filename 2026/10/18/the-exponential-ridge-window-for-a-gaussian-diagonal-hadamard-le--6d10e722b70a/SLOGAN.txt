For a Gaussian-diagonal Hadamard least-squares sketch, rare row-sign collisions make the unregularized squared error nonintegrable at every finite with-replacement sample count; without replacement, all positive moments first become finite after more than half the Hadamard rows are sampled. Ridge regularization restores finite risk, with an exact \(4^{-m}\) regularization threshold and a sharp \(\sqrt\pi\) critical-window constant on this explicit regression instance.
