For every odd finite field, a symmetric \(2\times2\) matrix lies in the Jordan-multiplication images of exactly \(0\), \(1\), \(2\), or \(q-\chi(-1)\) primitive idempotents, with a complete closed-form incidence distribution. Its incidence-zero nonscalar matrices give an exact census of a substantial finite-field obstruction to representing matrices by left-nested Jordan words in idempotents.
