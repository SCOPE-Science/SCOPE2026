# Review: geometric fixed topology of partition-graph clique complexes

## Correctness — PASS

The all-size orbit model follows from the barycentric-coordinate description of fixed points of a simplicial involution, with the flag-complex condition imposed both inside and between orbits. The transposed-corner characterization follows from diagram intersection and the two asymmetric added cells, and is independently checked against the full edge definition for every size in the tested range. The finite exact certificate enumerates all integer partitions and orbit-complex simplices through size 22, verifies every elementary free-face collapse, and identifies the residual graphs; a graph cycle rank gives the full **integral** homotopy type. Independent mod-two chain calculations corroborate the finite certificates but are not used to promote mod-two data to homotopy. The character formula follows from simplicial Lefschetz and the established ordinary wedge-of-two-spheres theorem (`arXiv:2603.14370`). No conclusion about topological stabilization beyond 22 is drawn.

## Originality — PASS, subject to literature residual risk

The full published primary text of `arXiv:2603.14370` treats the nonequivariant topology, not conjugation-fixed geometric realizations. Sections 2–5 and the results of `arXiv:2603.22546` study the self-conjugate **vertices**, their edgeless axis, and axial spines, but do not identify the geometric fixed-space homotopy, which involves barycenters of conjugate edge pairs. The full text of `arXiv:2603.26656` supplies ordinary clique-count/Euler data, not fixed-point traces. `arXiv:2606.20773` motivates equivariant modeling. Targeted published-literature searches for conjugation-fixed partition-graph clique homology and the fixed-point orbit complex, together with mathematical-result searches for aliases, did not identify a prior dominating classification. The published automorphism, ordinary wedge theorem, and edgeless axis are explicitly excluded from the novelty claim. Search incompleteness and possible unindexed equivariant results are genuine residual risks.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Conjugation is the canonical internal symmetry of the partition graph, and its geometric fixed-point set is mathematically different from the known fixed-vertex axis. The all-size orbit model turns a large partition clique complex into an exact smaller flag model. The fully exhaustive **sharp** first loop at size \(n=18\), and the first negative ambient homology trace at size \(n=22\), are natural qualitative thresholds in the symmetry topology and cannot be inferred from ordinary Betti numbers or axis counts. The exact finite collapses establish integral homotopy rather than merely numerical Euler data. This is a meaningful partial response to the source literature's equivariant-topology direction, without claiming a paper-wide solution for all sizes.
