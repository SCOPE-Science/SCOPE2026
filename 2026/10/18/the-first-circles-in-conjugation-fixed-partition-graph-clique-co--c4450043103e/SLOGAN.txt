Conjugation-fixed points of the partition-graph clique complex are canonically modeled by a small flag complex on compatible conjugation orbits. Exhaustive, replayable simplicial collapses show that integral first homology first appears at size \(n=18\), while the first negative conjugation trace on ambient two-dimensional rational homology is \(-1\) at \(n=22\); both are sharp throughout \(1\leq n\leq22\).
