For every fixed \(d\ge1\), equal-product collisions among two ordered triples of monic degree-\(d\) polynomials over \(\mathbb F_q[T]\) have two explicit leading terms as \(q\to\infty\). The \(q^{3d-1}\) coefficient is negative and equals minus nine times a natural \(3\times3\) contingency-matrix count.
