For nearly complete random formal contexts with \(n\) objects and \(\lfloor\alpha n\rfloor\) attributes, distributivity of the concept lattice first fails at missing-incidence probability \(q\asymp n^{-6/5}\). In the window \(q=t n^{-6/5}\), the probability of distributivity tends to \(e^{-\alpha^3t^5}\), because isolated six-vertex missing-edge paths introduce precisely the first nondistributive pentagon factors.
