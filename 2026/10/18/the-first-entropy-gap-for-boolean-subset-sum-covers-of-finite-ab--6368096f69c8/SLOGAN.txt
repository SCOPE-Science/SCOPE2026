The elementary abelian group \((\mathbb Z/5\mathbb Z)^2\) is the first finite abelian group whose shortest Boolean subset-sum cover exceeds \(\lceil\log_2|G|\rceil\): it needs six vectors, not five. Five vectors cover at most \(24\) of its \(25\) points, with exactly \(1920\) extremal five-element sets in six linear symmetry classes.
