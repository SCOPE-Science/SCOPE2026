# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The input graph and bounded-degree-two faces are defined without implicit choices. All \(531{,}745\) finite faces across the five graphs were enumerated from graph incidence masks and checked against the forbidden-three-incidence criterion. The resulting complete matchings contain \(265{,}865\) pairs and their directed Hasse graphs, with \(4{,}259{,}728\) incidences overall, are each acyclic by exhaustive topological sorting. The exact signed Morse matrices are recomputed from the matching rather than read from logs. All nonzero Smith factors are units, and the remaining single-dimension CW models establish integral wedges. The baseline \(n=3\) is a known special case and is not presented as newly discovered.

## Originality
**PASS (qualified).** Full text of Vega's arXiv:1909.10406 was inspected: it treats wheel graphs and caterpillars but no Möbius ladders. Singh's arXiv:2006.13632 contains the \(M_2(K_{3,3})\) baseline but its complete-graph and complete-bipartite statements do not cover the other four cubic ladders. Abstract-level published results on ordinary matching, perfect-matching, independence, cut and Morse complexes for Möbius ladders have different face definitions and cannot imply the degree-two complex's homotopy types. The recent same-graph paper arXiv:2608.30601 had only abstract-level access, so undetected equivalent work is a residual risk.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** A natural bounded-degree relaxation of matchings on canonical cubic graphs exhibits a nonmonotone sequence of homotopy types. The first five cases establish a concrete three-sphere exceptional stage and a return to a single sphere, providing finite structural boundary data for a family whose other matching-related complexes display periodic patterns. This is exact integral homotopy information rather than a face-count or rational-Betti exercise. The cutoff captures the first departure and recovery; no unsupported extrapolation is made.

## Limitations
The contribution is finite and nonequivariant. No all-parameter periodic formula or map between the complexes is determined. Relevant 2026 same-graph work was only read at abstract level. Independent mathematical audit is not available.
