## Mathematical value — PASS

At the first market size after the classical three-agent cases, PS produces positive expected Borda-welfare improvement even though it loses in a larger number of profiles than it wins. This exact disagreement between *frequency of profile-wise benefit* and *ex ante welfare magnitude* matters when comparing market-design mechanisms under a natural ordinal rank score; it is not inferred from ordinary ordinal-efficiency comparisons. Both ranking signs persist in arbitrary larger square markets. The conclusion is only for equally spaced rank utility and, for averaged signs, impartial culture.
