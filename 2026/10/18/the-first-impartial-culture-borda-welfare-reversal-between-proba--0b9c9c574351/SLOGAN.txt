In strict one-to-one random assignment under independent uniform preferences, probabilistic serial has lower mean total Borda welfare than random priority for three agents but higher mean for four, with an exact four-agent improvement of \(1283/62208\). At four agents it wins on \(87{,}408\) profiles and loses on \(115{,}200\), showing that favorable average welfare and more frequent profile-by-profile wins are different properties; both comparison signs remain possible at every larger market size.
