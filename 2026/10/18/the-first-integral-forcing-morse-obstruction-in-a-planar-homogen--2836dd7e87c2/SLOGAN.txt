The least nonnegative integral forcing in a natural planar homogeneous cubic family that destroys every \(C^2\) Morse Lyapunov function is \(b=2\), although an explicit strict quartic polynomial Lyapunov function still proves global asymptotic stability. Two exact positive-definite Gram matrices certify the quartic, while a three-direction Hessian argument proves the impossibility of any nondegenerate quadratic leading term.
