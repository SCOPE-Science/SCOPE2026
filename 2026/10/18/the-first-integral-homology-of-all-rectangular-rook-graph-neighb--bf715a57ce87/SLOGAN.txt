For every rectangular rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) with \(m,n\ge3\), the open-neighborhood complex has first integral homology \(\mathbb Z^2\) when \(m=n=3\), \(\mathbb Z\) when exactly one dimension is three, and \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\) when both dimensions are at least four. Thus the family exhibits a sharp, universal transition from free homology to genuine order-two torsion.
