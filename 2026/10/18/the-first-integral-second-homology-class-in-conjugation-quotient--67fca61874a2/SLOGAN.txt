For the group clique complex of \(S_n\), its geometric conjugation quotient has vanishing integral second homology for every \(1\le n\le14\), but \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z\) at \(n=15\). A four-triangle tetrahedral shell indexed by the primes \(2,3,5,7\) is the primitive generator; exact partition-chain elimination proves that it is the first and only second-homology class at that rank.
