# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

A face is exactly a set with no selected triple that spans at least two edges: degree ≥2 gives such a triple and conversely. Each row therefore contributes at most two cells. I independently enumerated faces as tuples excluding these triples, reapplied the published pivot orders, and topologically sorted the full directed Hasse graph; the cell and matching counts agree for all six widths, including one critical vertex in every case. For n≤5 all critical positive-dimensional cells are in one dimension, giving the claimed wedges by the discrete Morse CW theorem. At n=6 I independently reduced the original oriented simplicial boundaries over Q, not the Morse differential: ranks of ∂4 and ∂5 are 443 and 90, giving b4=540−443−90=7. In the Morse CW model the only possible nonzero boundary is the integral map Z^7→Z^113. The full b4=7 forces it to be zero over Q and therefore over Z. The 3-skeleton is a wedge of 3-spheres and is 2-connected; Hurewicz identifies π3 with integral H3, so the zero degree vector of every 4-cell makes its attaching map null-homotopic. Thus this proves the integral homotopy claim, not just agreement of field Betti numbers.
