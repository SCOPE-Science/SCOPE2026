Among the two-independence complexes of three-row rook graphs, the first six integral homotopy types are completely determined: the three-by-six board is the first to have homology in two degrees, with type \(\bigvee^{113}S^3\vee\bigvee^{7}S^4\). An explicit exact acyclic matching and zero integral Morse differential prove this wedge decomposition; nothing is asserted for larger boards.
