Gaussian shocks do not make finite stochastic opinion compromise Gaussian: the centered stationary distribution is Gaussian exactly for two agents, and non-Gaussian for every larger fully interacting neutral-utility population. Its exact variance has a \(((N-1)/N)^2\) finite-size factor, and the first non-Gaussian case \(N=3\) has excess kurtosis \(3t/(16-12t)\), where \(t=2\mu(1-\mu)\).
