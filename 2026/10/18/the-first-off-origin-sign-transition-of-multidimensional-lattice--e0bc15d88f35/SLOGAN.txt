On the integer lattice \(\mathbb Z^d\), \(d\ge2\), the discrete heat-kernel time derivative has exactly one negative spatial coefficient until a unique dimension-dependent threshold \(\tau_d\); this is the maximal interval on which both endpoint operator norms have an exact one-site Bessel formula. The threshold decreases strictly with dimension and satisfies \(\tau_d=1/(2d)+1/(4d^2)+1/(8d^3)+O(d^{-4})\).
