The first nonzero \(p\)-adic correction between Nekrasov--Okounkov hook coefficients and \(p^a\)-core partition counts is determined at **every index**, not just the first sharp index: after dividing by \(p^{a+1}\), its complete reduction modulo \(p\) is an explicit product of the partition generating series and a divisor-sum Lambert series. The formula works uniformly for all primes, including \(p=2\).
