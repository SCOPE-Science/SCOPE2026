# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The local collision is exhaustively characterized by the bounded divisibility of \(2^d-1\) for \(1\le d\le20\). A fixed-shift simultaneous squarefree density lemma is proved using Siegel–Walfisz and eighteenth moments. The all-failure formula follows by exact inclusion–exclusion, and the correction is strictly positive by a probability coupling. The attached `verify.py` independently checks the finite residue calculation and an exact truncated product-model identity. Infinite prime equidistribution relies on the standard Siegel–Walfisz theorem, not on numerical experiments.

## Originality
PASS with residual risk. The arXiv:2610.07214 primary text gives densities through sixteen admissible exponents and explicitly identifies but leaves aside the first width-twenty collision. Relevant published-finding corpus queries, mathematical literature searches and archived claim comparisons found no exact eighteen-shift factor or strictly positive correction; generic multi-shift sieves may imply it implicitly.

## Value
PASS. The finding quantifies the first point where the published disjoint-residue prime-square model fails, proves an exact signed correction, and obtains a positive-density class of primes failing eighteen eligible representations. It resolves a natural arithmetic phase boundary rather than simply extending a finite cutoff.

## Limitations
No error term or evaluated decimal for the very small density. The result neither settles the unrestricted squarefree-plus-power-of-two conjecture nor supersedes general sieve theory.
