Among primes \(p\equiv7\pmod{36}\), the exact positive density for which all eighteen admissible remainders \(p-2^a\) with \(0\le a\le20\) fail squarefreeness has a convergent Euler-product formula. A strictly positive correction appears at this first collision window because \(2^{20}\equiv1\pmod{25}\), defeating the disjoint-residue extrapolation valid for shorter windows.
