Under the maximal-entropy measure, affine-branch digits of the shrinking random beta transformation are reversible on every word of length at most four, but not five: \(p(00010)-p(01000)=-\lambda_n^{-(n+3)}/(2\mu_n)\). The entire five-symbol time-reversal total variation is \(4\lambda_n^{-(n+3)}/\mu_n\), asymptotic to \(2^{-n}/6\).
