For bidirected cycles with four, five, and six graph vertices, the multipath complexes are, respectively, \(\bigvee^3 S^1\vee\bigvee^2 S^2\), \(S^2\vee\bigvee^2 S^3\), and \(S^2\vee\bigvee^2 S^4\). Exhaustive discrete-Morse certificates and a vanishing integral cup square resolve the unstable attaching-map obstruction in the six-vertex case.
