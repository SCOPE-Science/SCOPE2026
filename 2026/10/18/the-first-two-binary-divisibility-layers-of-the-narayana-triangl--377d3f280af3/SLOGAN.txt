Among all entries in Narayana rows \(1\le n<2^L\), exactly \(3^{L-1}-3\) have two-adic valuation one for every \(L\ge2\); the odd-entry count is \((5\cdot3^{L-1}-3)/2\). An exact binary-submask decomposition explains both counts and shows that valuation-one entries approach \(2/7\) of those not divisible by four.
