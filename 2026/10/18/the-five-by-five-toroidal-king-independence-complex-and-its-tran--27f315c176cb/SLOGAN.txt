The independence complex of the five-by-five toroidal king graph is integrally homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{141} S^3\). Its full translation action has rational third-homology character \(141\) at the identity, \(-4\) in eight special directions and \(1\) otherwise, yielding quotient ranks \(25\), \(29\), or \(5\) for the relevant translation subgroups.
