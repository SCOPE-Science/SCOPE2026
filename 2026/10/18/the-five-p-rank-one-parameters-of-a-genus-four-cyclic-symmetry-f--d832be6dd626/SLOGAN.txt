For the smooth genus-four family \(y^2=x^{10}+x^7+ax^4+bx\) in characteristic seven, the Jacobian \(p\)-rank is always positive, the \(a\)-number is at most two, and the complete \((p\text{-rank},a\text{-number})\) stratification is explicit. Exactly five smooth geometric parameter pairs have \(p\)-rank one: two over \(\mathbf F_7\) and three conjugate over \(\mathbf F_{7^3}\).
