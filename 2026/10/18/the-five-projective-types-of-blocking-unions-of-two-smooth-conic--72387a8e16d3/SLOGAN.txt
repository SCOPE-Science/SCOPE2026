Over \(\mathbf F_3\), exactly 1,638 unordered pairs of distinct smooth plane conics have a nontrivially blocking rational union, split into two projective orbits of sizes 702 and 936. Their 234 six-point unions and 234 complementary seven-point unions admit exactly three and four conic-pair decompositions, respectively; allowing trivial blocking unions gives exactly five projective pair types.
