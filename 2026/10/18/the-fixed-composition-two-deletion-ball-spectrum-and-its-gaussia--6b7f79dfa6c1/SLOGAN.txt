The complete distribution of the number of distinct exact two-deletion descendants is determined for every binary word length and every fixed Hamming weight by an explicit alternating-run coefficient formula. Its exact conditional mean is rational in the length and weight, and at every interior limiting density its centered fluctuations have a Gaussian limit on the \(n^{3/2}\) scale with variance constant \(16\alpha^4(1-\alpha)^4\).
