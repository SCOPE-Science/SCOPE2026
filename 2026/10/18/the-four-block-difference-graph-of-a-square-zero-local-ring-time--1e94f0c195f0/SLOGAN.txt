For a finite square-zero local ring \(A\) and a finite field \(F\), the weak-minus-ordinary zero-divisor difference graph of \(A\times F\) is exactly a four-block clique/independent blow-up of \(P_4\). Its abstract graph recovers the residue-field order, maximal-ideal size and \(|F|\), but not the local ring: \(\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z\times\mathbb F_2\) and \(\mathbb F_2[t]/(t^2)\times\mathbb F_2\) give the same graph.
