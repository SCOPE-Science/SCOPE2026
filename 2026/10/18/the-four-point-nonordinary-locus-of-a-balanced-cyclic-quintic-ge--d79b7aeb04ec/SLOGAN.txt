For the genus-four normalization of \(y^5=x(x-1)^2(x-t)^3\) in characteristic \(11\), the nonordinary locus consists of exactly four geometric parameters, cut out by \((t^2-5t-4)(t^2+4t-3)=0\). Each exceptional Jacobian has \(11\)-rank two and \(a\)-number two, while every other smooth member is ordinary; all four exceptional parameters are defined over \(\mathbf F_{121}\) but not \(\mathbf F_{11}\).
