A cubic phase can leave a Gaussian signal's entire radar ambiguity function zero-free while transforming its integrated moments: the exact \(L^p\) moment has a sharp strong-chirp transition at \(p=4\). Below that exponent it has a power law, at the threshold it has a logarithmic enhancement \(\asymp (\log c)/c\), and above it has a universal \(c^{-1}\) decay with explicit constants.
