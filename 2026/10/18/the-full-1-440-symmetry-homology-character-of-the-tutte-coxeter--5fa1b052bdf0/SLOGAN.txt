The natural nine-dimensional top homology of the Tutte–Coxeter independence complex carries the irreducible \(S_6\)-module \(S^{(2,2,1,1)}\). Its complete \(1440\)-element automorphism character is determined, including four distinct bipartition-swapping vertex-cycle patterns and a \(4+5\) eigenspace splitting for an incidence-duality involution.
