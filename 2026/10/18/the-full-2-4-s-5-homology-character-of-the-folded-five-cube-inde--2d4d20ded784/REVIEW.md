Its standalone exact verifier reproduces all 393 faces, mod-two boundary ranks `(15,65,95,16)`, rational homology dimension nine, and the proposed character at all 1,920 automorphisms by two independent Lefschetz implementations. Rank inequalities make the mod-two boundary ranks exact over the rationals. Limits: no integral equivariant decomposition is inferred from rational data.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** Barmak's triangle-free suspension theorem, the known folded-cube independence polynomial and existing published rank-nine wedge computations imply neither the full character nor the two irreducible constituents. The closest general folded-cube translation theorem applies to the open-neighborhood complex, which has a different dimension and homology rank. Exact named-graph and alias searches did not surface the proposed automorphism character. The inaccessible full text of a 2026 strongly-regular clique-homology paper and unindexed equivalents remain possible overlap risks; an absence claim is not deduced from abstract-only access.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** For a canonical 16-vertex, vertex-transitive, triangle-free graph, the natural homology representation has nontrivial structure invisible to its known wedge-of-nine-spheres homotopy type: a four-dimensional sign-twisted standard quotient, a five-dimensional monomial constituent induced by a nontrivial orbit of translation characters, and contrasting four-dimensional translation invariants versus no full-group invariants. The complete group-character formula makes this a structural topological symmetry determination rather than a disconnected face-count recomputation. It does not claim a broader infinite-family theorem or integral lattice classification.
