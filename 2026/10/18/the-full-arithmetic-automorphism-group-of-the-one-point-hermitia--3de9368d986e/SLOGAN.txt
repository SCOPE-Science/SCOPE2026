For the unique-rational-point Hermitian-relative curve \(X^{s+1}+\eta Y^{s+1}+Y^sZ+YZ^s=0\) over \(\mathbb F_{s^2}\), \(s\ge3\), every rational automorphism belongs to an explicit unitary Heisenberg semidirect product of order \(s^3(s+1)\). The full stabilizer is given by norm-one and trace equations, not just by a subset of projective symmetries.
