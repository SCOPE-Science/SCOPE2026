The \(57\)-dimensional third homology of the cuboctahedral matching complex has a fully explicit rational \(S_4\times C_2\)-character, with central antipodal eigenspaces of dimensions \(34\) and \(23\). Exactly one rational homology line is fixed by every automorphism; this symmetry classification is not determined by the previously known nonequivariant sphere-wedge homotopy type.
