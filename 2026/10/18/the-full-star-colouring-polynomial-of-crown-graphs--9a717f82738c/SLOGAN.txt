All star-colour partitions of the crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M_n\) admit an exact Stirling-number enumerator: the coefficient for \(n+j\) colours is \(2\left\{\!\begin{matrix}n+1\\j+1\end{matrix}\!\right\}-\binom nj+\binom n2\binom{n-2}j\) for \(0\le j\le n\). Beyond the previously known star chromatic number \(n\), there are exactly \(1+\binom n2\) optimal partitions, arising from matched pairs or a single two-index exchange.
