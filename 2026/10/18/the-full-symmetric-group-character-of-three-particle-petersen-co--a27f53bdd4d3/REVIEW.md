# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The reduced Świątkowski model computes configuration-space homology functorially. Exact modular ranks in two characteristics are converted to rational equalities using the explicit augmentation upper bound, the injective top boundary, and a six-dimensional first-homology lower bound supplied by parked-particle pentagons. All 120 signed chain supertraces were directly reconstructed independently of the cycle-index expression. Character inner products are exact and integral. These steps prove the specific rational representation without treating modular or finite exploration alone as a substitute for the missing characteristic-zero reasoning.

## Originality — PASS
Ko–Park treat first homology, not this second-homology character; An–Drummond-Cole–Knudsen give the general computational model rather than the evaluated Petersen representation. Maciążek–Sawicki's Section 5 provides product-cycle generators for classes including subcubic graphs but does not supply the corresponding symmetric-group character; its displayed tables do not give this three-particle Petersen instance. The closest existing equivariant Petersen graph configuration calculation concerns **two** particles, so it does not imply this result, while published equivariant three-particle calculations for other graphs have different underlying representations. Searching alternative terminology, direct character tuples, Specht decompositions, and broader parameter families revealed no covering statement. An unindexed identical calculation remains a residual risk; search absence by itself is not proof of absolute novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The Petersen graph is the minimal canonical cubic symmetric nonplanar graph and has full automorphism group \(S_5\). Passing from dimensions of homology groups to their complete automorphism representation distinguishes four irreducible constituents and proves vanishing of invariant two-cycles, information that ordinary Betti numbers and first-homology descriptions cannot reveal. The graph-configuration setting supplies a natural motivation for exactly three particles and the full symmetry action, rather than an arbitrary numerical slice.

## Boundaries
Only three unordered particles, one graph, and rational homology are classified. The proof neither determines integral torsion nor produces an equivariant homotopy splitting. Unindexed literature remains an originality risk; an independent mathematical audit has not been performed.
