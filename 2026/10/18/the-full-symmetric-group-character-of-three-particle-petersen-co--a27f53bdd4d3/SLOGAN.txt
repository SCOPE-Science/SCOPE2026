For three unordered particles on the Petersen graph, the full \(S_5\)-action on \(H_2(-;\mathbb Q)\) has character \((25,-3,1,-2,0,1,0)\) and decomposes as \(S^{(3,2)}\oplus S^{(3,1,1)}\oplus2S^{(2,2,1)}\oplus S^{(2,1,1,1)}\). In particular, there are no fully symmetry-invariant rational degree-two classes.
