The eleven first-homology generators of the Petersen graph's open-neighborhood complex form two nonisomorphic irreducible rational \(S_5\)-modules, \(S^{(3,1,1)}\oplus S^{(2,2,1)}\), of dimensions six and five. Their complete character is \((11,-1,-1,-1,-1,1,1)\), and no generator survives averaging over all graph automorphisms.
