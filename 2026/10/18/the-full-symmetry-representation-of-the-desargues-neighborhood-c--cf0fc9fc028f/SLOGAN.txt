The Desargues graph's open neighborhood complex has two components, each with the homotopy type of eleven circles, but its full rational homology symmetry is finer: \(\widetilde H_1\cong(S^{(3,1,1)}\oplus S^{(2,2,1)})\boxtimes(\mathbf1\oplus\varepsilon)\) under \(S_5\times C_2\). The orbit space by all graph automorphisms is exactly a closed triangle, not merely rationally acyclic.
