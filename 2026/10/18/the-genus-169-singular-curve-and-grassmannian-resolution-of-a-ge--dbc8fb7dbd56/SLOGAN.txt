The singular locus of the general rational Pfaffian quartic sixfold in \(\mathbb P^7\) is a smooth curve of degree \(84\), genus \(169\), and canonical bundle \(\mathcal O(4)\), with an ordinary quadratic transverse singularity at every point. Blowing up the curve resolves the hypersurface with discrepancy \(3\) and a relative Klein-quadric exceptional divisor whose two maximal-plane rulings remain globally separate.
