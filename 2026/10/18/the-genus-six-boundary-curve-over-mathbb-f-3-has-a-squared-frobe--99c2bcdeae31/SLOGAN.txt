The ordinary genus-six curve \(y^3-y=x+1/(x^3-x)\) over \(\mathbb F_3\), arising from an exceptional automorphism-group boundary family, has the complete Weil numerator \(L(T)=((1-T+3T^2)(1+T-2T^2+3T^3+9T^4))^2\). Its Jacobian is \(\mathbb F_3\)-isogenous to the square of an elliptic curve times a simple abelian surface, and its rational point count is \(3^n+1\) for every \(3\nmid n\).\n
