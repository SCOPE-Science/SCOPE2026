The full certificate constructs the labeled Fano-plane incidence graph and every ordinary four-cut facet. Independently of the algorithm, the connected induced four-vertex sets are precisely 14 claws and 84 three-edge paths. All five-vertex induced graphs are forests, yielding a complete eight-skeleton. The executable check enumerates 15,816 simplices, constructs all 108,416 Hasse covers, proves the entire oriented matching graph acyclic, and leaves exactly 188 critical dimension-nine faces. This establishes the integral wedge homotopy type by discrete Morse theory, not by extrapolation from samples. An independent direct mod-two boundary calculation confirms the homology in all dimensions.

## Value — PASS

The Heawood graph is the canonical cubic, girth-six Fano incidence graph. Its ordinary four-cut complex is a natural exact topological invariant, and the complete 188-sphere homotopy classification provides more than Euler characteristic, a mod-two rank, or a yes/no topological test. It is a finite but complete classification, supported by a compact, transparent, exhaustively checkable acyclic matching. No broader graph-family theorem, shellability or symmetry-character claim is made.
