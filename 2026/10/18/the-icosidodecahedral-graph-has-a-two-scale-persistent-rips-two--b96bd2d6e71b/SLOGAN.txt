The thirty-vertex icosidodecahedral graph has graph-metric Vietoris–Rips types \(\bigvee^{29}S^0\), \(\bigvee^{11}S^1\), \(S^2\), \(S^2\), \(S^{14}\), then a point at successive integer radii. Its degree-two sphere survives integrally from radius \(2\) to radius \(3\), yielding a single all-field persistence interval \([2,4)\).
