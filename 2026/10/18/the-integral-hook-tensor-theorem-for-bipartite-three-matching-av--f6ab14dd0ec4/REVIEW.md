# Scientific assessment: integral three-matching-avoidance representation

## Correctness — PASS

The domain is the simplicial complex of all subgraphs of a complete bipartite graph with no three-matching. The published Linusson--Shareshian--Welker theorem supplies the exact free rank and vanishing outside degree three. A fully specified 16-term octahedral cycle in the \(3\times3\) base is proved primitive by a six-supported integral cocycle pairing to one; both its cycle boundary and the cocycle's coboundary are checked from the signed incidence definitions. Lefschetz computations establish the separate row and column sign characters. Vertex-amalgamation maps preserve the matching-avoidance property, and their composites with triple embeddings are either zero in homology or signed degree-one maps. They yield a split surjection of equal-rank free groups, proving an integral basis. The coordinate calculation identifies precisely the tensor of augmentation exterior squares and its entire relation module, for arbitrary \(r,s\ge3\). The supplied verifier confirms the finite seed and selected families of checks; it is not substituted for the uniform argument.
