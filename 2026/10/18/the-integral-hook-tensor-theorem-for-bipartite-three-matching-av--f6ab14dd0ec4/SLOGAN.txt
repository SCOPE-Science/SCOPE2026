For every \(r,s\ge3\), the integral third homology of the bipartite three-matching-avoidance complex is exactly \(\bigwedge^2U_r\otimes\bigwedge^2U_s\) as a \(\mathbb Z[S_r\times S_s]\)-module, where \(U_m\) is the coordinate-sum-zero lattice. Explicit sixteen-tetrahedron spheres in three-by-three subgraphs give an integral basis and all alternating four-term relations, settling the \(k=3\) case of the classical hook-tensor prediction.
