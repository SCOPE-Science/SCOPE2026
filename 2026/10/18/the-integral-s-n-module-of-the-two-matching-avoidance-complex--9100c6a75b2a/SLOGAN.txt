For every \(n\ge4\), the two-matching-avoidance complex of the complete graph has natural integral second-homology lattice \(\bigwedge^3\ker(\mathbb Z^n\to\mathbb Z)\), with a basis of four-label triangle combinations. This determines the full symmetric-group action—not merely the previously known Betti number—and answers the \(k=2\) case of the bounded-matching-complex representation question.
