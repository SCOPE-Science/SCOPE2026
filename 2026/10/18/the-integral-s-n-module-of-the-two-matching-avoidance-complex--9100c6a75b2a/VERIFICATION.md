# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

A nonstar pairwise-intersecting edge family must be the three edges of a triangle: once {i,j},{i,k},{j,k} occur, no fourth edge meets all three. Thus the pair with the union B of stars has relative chains only in dimension two, freely generated by the oriented triangles. The edge-vertex permutation on a triangle is the same sign as the permutation of its labels (via opposite edges), giving Λ³V integrally. In each star simplex the barycenter arms form a tree; different stars meet only at a common edge-vertex, already in the arms. Attaching a contractible star along its contractible tree preserves homology, and the graph of arms is the equivariantly subdivided K_n. The boundary of [ij,jk,ik], rewritten through the three star centers, follows i→j→k→i, so the pair connecting map is exactly augmentation contraction, with no coefficient or index discrepancy. Writing V=Ze₁⊕U shows directly that the graph cycles are Λ²U, that contraction onto them is surjective, and that its kernel is Λ³U. The pair sequence gives homology concentration and the integral basis, even at n=4 (the sign representation). The determinant product gives the exterior character and the nontrivial hook identification. After barycentric subdivision, simplex stabilizers fix simplices pointwise; rational quotient cellular chains are coinvariants. Averaging makes coinvariants exact and identifies them with invariants, so the absence of trivial summands in positive-degree homology yields the quotient conclusion.
