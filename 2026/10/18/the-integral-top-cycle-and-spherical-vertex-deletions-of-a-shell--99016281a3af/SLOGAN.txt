For the historically minimal shellable, non-vertex-decomposable circulant \(C_{16}(1,4,8)\), every vertex deletion collapses to \(S^2\), while every vertex link has homotopy type \(S^2\vee S^2\). Its unique primitive integral three-cycle uses all 80 facets, with 16 forced double coefficients and sharp \(\ell_1\)-norm 96, so no proper induced subcomplex carries third homology.
