# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The graph is specified unambiguously by disjoint non-line triples in the Fano plane. Exhaustive enumeration constructs 196,009 augmented independent faces and a unit-coefficient acyclic Morse matching with 97,999 pairs. The exact 8-by-2 and 2-by-1 Morse boundary matrices have rank one each, proving reduced integral sixth homology is free of rank seven and all other groups vanish. Exact orbit-polynomial Lefschetz traces for all 336 graph automorphisms are independently checked against direct signed-face traces on one representative of each element order. Character orthogonality proves absolute irreducibility for the full group and the 168-element Fano subgroup. The certificate's limits are finite and explicit; no infinite-family or integral equivariant classification is claimed.

No accessible statement covering this topological identification was found. Full texts of Coxeter (1983) and the 2026 same-object note were inaccessible; no claim of exhaustive bibliographic uniqueness is made.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The Coxeter graph is a canonical 28-vertex cubic distance-transitive graph with a distinguished Fano-plane realization and a full automorphism group twice as large as its 168 Fano collineations. The identification of its natural topological homology with one of two seven-dimensional irreducibles of the full group is a complete nontrivial symmetry invariant. In particular, neither the ordinary Betti number seven nor the seven-sphere homotopy description determines whether all 336 symmetries act irreducibly or whether any homology class is fixed; this calculation answers both naturally motivated questions. No arbitrary parameter slice or tiny numerical improvement is involved.
