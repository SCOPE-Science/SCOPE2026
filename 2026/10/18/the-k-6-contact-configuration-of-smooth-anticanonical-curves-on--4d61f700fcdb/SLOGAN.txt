On the split quintic del Pezzo surface over \(\mathbf F_2\), precisely six of the \(63\) anticanonical curves are geometrically smooth; their rational-point incidence is the complete graph \(K_6\), and every pair meets only at its unique rational point, with full multiplicity five. They form one sharply three-transitive \(S_5\)-orbit and contain no all-smooth rational pencil.
