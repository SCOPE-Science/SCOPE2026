For every \(q\ge2\), swapping the two graph sheets and interchanging the equal complete-graph coordinates give a four-element action on \(\widetilde H_3(\operatorname{Ind}(K_2\times K_q\times K_q);\mathbb Q)\) with exact character \((B_q,(q-1)^2,-(q-1)^2,1-q)\), where \(B_q=(q-1)^2(q^2-2)/2\). Its full symmetry quotient has third reduced rational Betti rank \(((q-1)^2(q^2-2)-2(q-1))/8\); the underlying nonequivariant sphere count was previously known.
