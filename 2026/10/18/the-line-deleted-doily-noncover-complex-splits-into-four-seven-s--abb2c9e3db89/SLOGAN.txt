Removing one line from the fifteen-line Cremona–Richmond (doily) incidence structure produces a noncover complex homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^4S^7\); its inclusion into the intact complex is a split wedge inclusion with five additional seven-spheres. The remaining line-stabilizer acts on the four-dimensional rational homology as an internal-pair-flip-sign line plus an irreducible three-dimensional representation, while the intact nine-dimensional homology rank was already implicit in earlier no-perfect-matching theory.
