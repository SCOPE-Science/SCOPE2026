For every level-three Picard modular compactification in the Memarinasorkhabi--Yeung family, the embedded rational modular curve has normal bundle \(\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^1}(-1)^{\oplus(n-1)}\). Consequently these interior-meeting curves are infinitesimally rigid and give reduced isolated points of their Hilbert schemes in all dimensions \(n\ge2\).
