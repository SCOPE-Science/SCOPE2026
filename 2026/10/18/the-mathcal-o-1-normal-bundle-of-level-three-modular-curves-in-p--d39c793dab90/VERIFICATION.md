# Verification scope

The proof is symbolic and uniform: (1) direct division of \([a\tau+b:c\tau+d:\boldsymbol\xi]\) by \(c\tau+d\) yields transverse factor \((c\tau+d)^{-1}I_{n-1}\); (2) at infinity the period-three cusp generator has \(c=0,d=1\) and preserves each transverse coordinate; the four cusp extensions are transported by block-diagonal modular matrices, using the originating paper's explicit toroidal chart; (3) the canonical intersection \(K\cdot C=n-3\) gives normal degree \(1-n\); (4) splitting into \(n-1\) identical lines forces degree minus one for each summand; (5) the standard cohomology of \(\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^1}(-1)\) vanishes in degrees zero and one.

The geometric construction, embedding, toroidal charts, and canonical intersection number are taken from the specified primary paper, arXiv:2610.12071v1, Lemma 2.1, Lemma 3.1, Proposition 3.3 and Proposition 4.1. No numerical experiment purports to establish the infinite family and no proof of those external theorems is claimed here. The Hilbert-scheme conclusion uses the standard identification of tangent and obstruction spaces for a smooth embedded curve.
