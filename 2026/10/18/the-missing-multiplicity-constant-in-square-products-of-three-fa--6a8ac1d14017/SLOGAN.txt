Counting the same squarefree factorial part twice changes the leading constant for square products of three distinct factorials: the triple count is \((C_0+K)\sqrt X+O_\varepsilon(X^{2/5+\varepsilon})\), where \(K=\sum_{t\ge2}s((t^2)!)^{-1/2}>0\). This gives a precise multiplicity correction to the previously conjectured coefficient \(C_0\), and a positive limiting share of endpoints with two representations.
