# Scientific review of the nine-column matching-complex claim

## Correctness
PASS. The object is exactly the 27-vertex, 42-edge Cartesian strip. The tree's pair/split rule partitions all independent subsets; its monotone first-neighbor branch ordering precludes gradient cycles. Reconstructed critical cells number 182 in degree eight and 19 in degree nine. The signed integral Morse boundary has an explicit determinant-one 19-row minor, rather than merely a rank computed over a finite field. Attaching maps to a wedge of eight-spheres are classified by their integral degrees, and the unit minor permits 19 integral cell cancellations. The independent transfer count verifies the reduced Euler characteristic.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The nine-column case occupies the precisely motivated boundary between earlier finite sphere calculations through eight columns and documented higher-dimensional homology at ten columns. The result supplies a complete integral homotopy certificate rather than a field Betti number, and establishes that the single-degree sphere-wedge behavior persists through this last unresolved width. It is deliberately not presented as a general recurrence or a theorem for larger grids.
