The smooth one-point Hermitian-relative quartic \(X^4+\eta Y^4+Y^3Z+YZ^3=0\) over \(\mathbb F_9\), with \(\eta^2=-1\), has exact Hasse–Weil numerator \((1-3T+9T^2)^3\). Its Jacobian has \(343\) rational points and is \(\mathbb F_9\)-isogenous to three copies of the elliptic curve \(v^2=u^3-u+1\) after base change from \(\mathbb F_3\).
