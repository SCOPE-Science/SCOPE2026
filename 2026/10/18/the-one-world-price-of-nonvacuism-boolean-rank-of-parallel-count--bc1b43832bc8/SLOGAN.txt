Under SCP, the minimum number of anti-logical worlds realizing a prescribed finite table of parallel counterpossibles is the Boolean rank of its false-conditional matrix. SCP1 nonvacuity adds exactly one world if any row of conditionals is entirely true, and the sharp worst-case budgets for \(r\) antecedents and \(s\) consequents are \(\min(r,s)\) and \(\min(r,s+1)\).
