The ordinary four-cut complex of the Petersen graph is homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{26}S^5\). Its rational \(S_5\)-action on top homology is \(\operatorname{Sp}^{(5)}\oplus2\operatorname{Sp}^{(4,1)}\oplus\operatorname{Sp}^{(3,2)}\oplus2\operatorname{Sp}^{(3,1,1)}\), as certified by an exhaustive discrete-Morse matching and signed simplicial traces.
