The natural five-letter symmetry of the Petersen two-matching complex acts on its four-dimensional rational seventh homology as the standard representation of \(S_5\), with trace equal to the number of fixed original letters minus one. Every subgroup quotient has rational reduced homology of rank \(c(H)-1\) in degree seven and no other reduced rational homology, where \(c(H)\) is its number of orbits on the five letters.
