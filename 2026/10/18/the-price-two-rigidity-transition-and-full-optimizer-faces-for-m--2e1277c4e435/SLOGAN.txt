For a complete network with a prescribed perfect matching, the exact fastest-diffusion design changes at matching-edge price \(t=2\): cheaper matching edges force one uniquely uniform design, while dearer matching edges are all deleted. Above the transition, nonuniform global optima form a space of dimension \(m(m-3)/2\) for \(m\ge4\), and at the critical price the full optimal face has dimension \(m(m-1)/2\).
