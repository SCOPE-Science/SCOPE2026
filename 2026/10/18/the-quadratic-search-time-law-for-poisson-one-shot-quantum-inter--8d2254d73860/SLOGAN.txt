For a coherent nearest-neighbor quantum walker interrogated at one lattice site after independent exponential waiting times and re-prepared at the origin after every failed test, the globally optimal mean detection time at distance \(n\) is asymptotic to \(3.26221174933594\ldots\,n^2/|h|\), with optimal interrogation rate asymptotic to \(2|h|\alpha_*/n\), where \(2K_0(\alpha_*)=\alpha_*K_1(\alpha_*)\). The quadratic search-time law is sharp within this one-shot Poisson protocol, despite free ballistic quantum propagation.
