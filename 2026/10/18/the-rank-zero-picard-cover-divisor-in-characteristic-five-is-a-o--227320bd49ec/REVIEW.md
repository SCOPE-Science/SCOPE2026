# Scientific review

## Correctness

The universal Cartier coefficients follow directly from \(y^3=f(x)\) and the Cartier coefficient rule in characteristic five. Because \(\Delta=(a^2+c)Q+3b^2(ac+b^2)\), smoothness rules out \(Q=b=0\), so the Cartier rank is exactly two; its stable rank is two for \(Q\ne0\) and zero for \(Q=0\). The weighted quotient of the rank-zero divisor is explicitly computed on the \(a\ne0\) chart and at the unique \(a=0\) point, establishing the torus. A standalone exact verifier checks the polynomial identities and all rational coefficient triples, not merely a log of assertions.

## Originality

The prior Hasse–Witt methods and the \(a\)-number-one result are acknowledged as prior art. The claimed addition is the characteristic-five **global smooth rank-zero divisor as an explicitly identified torus of marked cyclic covers**, with its two singular boundary points and disjointness from the bielliptic locus. The bielliptic Prym study is strictly narrower; generic \(L\)-polynomial algorithms and the levelled Picard modular-surface supersingular component description do not supply this unlevelled coarse parameter identification. Searches of research records and relevant primary literature found no stated equivalent. An unindexed equivalent computation remains possible.

## Value

The theorem completely identifies the rank-zero stratum in a natural two-dimensional genus-three Picard Hurwitz space rather than tabulating an arbitrary small-parameter sample. Its torus uniformization determines irreducibility, geometric boundary and coarse rational parameter counts, and explains why the previously studied bielliptic subfamily cannot meet this stratum. These are useful for arithmetic questions about the Picard-moduli stratification; no unproved Newton-polygon or abstract-isomorphism assertion is included.

## Limitations

The result classifies deck-marked cyclic-cover presentations, not unrestricted abstract isomorphism classes or arithmetic twists. It does not determine the full Newton polygon at rank-zero points. General published Cartier methods imply much of the coefficient calculation; the new geometric identification is the part assessed for originality and value. Independent audit remains outstanding.
