For smooth genus-three cyclic triple covers \(y^3=x^4+a x^2+b x+c\) in characteristic five, the full Jacobian \(5\)-rank-zero locus is the single irreducible divisor \(a^2c+ab^2+c^2=0\), whose coarse space of marked covers is \(\mathbf G_m\). This divisor is disjoint from the bielliptic locus, and all remaining smooth covers have \(5\)-rank two.
