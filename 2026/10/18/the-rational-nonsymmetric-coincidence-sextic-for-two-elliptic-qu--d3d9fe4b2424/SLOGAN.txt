For the smooth genus-three curves \(y^2=(x^4+ax^2+1)(x^4+bx^2+1)\), two distinguished degree-two elliptic quotients coincide up to complex isomorphism on exactly two components: the known symmetry line \(a+b=0\) and an explicitly determined irreducible rational sextic in \((a+b,ab)\). The second component contains smooth nonsymmetric curves with Jacobian isogenous to a product \(E_0\times E^2\), and its full modular equality locus has an explicit rational parametrization.
