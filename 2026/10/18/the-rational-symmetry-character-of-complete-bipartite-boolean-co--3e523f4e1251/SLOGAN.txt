For the Boolean cell complex of every complete bipartite graph \(K_{m,n}\), the full side-preserving rational top-homology character at \((\sigma,\tau)\) is \(\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)\operatorname{sgn}(\tau)\beta(K_{c(\sigma),c(\tau)})\). Its subspace fixed by every side-preserving vertex permutation has dimension one exactly when the two parts have equal size, and dimension zero otherwise.
