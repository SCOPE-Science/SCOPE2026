For the quadratic-residue Paley graph on \(17\) vertices, the nonpure second-independence complex has exact integral homotopy type \(\operatorname{Ind}_2(P_{17})\simeq\bigvee^{18}S^3\). Its translation action on rational third homology is \(\mathbb Q\oplus\mathbb Q[C_{17}]\), a trivial representation plus a regular representation.
