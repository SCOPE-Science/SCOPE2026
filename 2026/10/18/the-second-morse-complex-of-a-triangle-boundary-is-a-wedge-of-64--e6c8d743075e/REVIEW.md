# Review

## Correctness — PASS
The incidence-model equivalence is valid for graphs: selected vertex–edge pairs with distinct vertices and edges are acyclic if and only if the induced functional orientation contains no directed cycle. Two independent enumerations recover the same \(1728\) augmented masks and face vector \((1,18,126,428,705,450)\). The specified staged matching has \(832\) pairs; a complete cover-graph topological sort checks acyclicity across \(6624\) incidences. Removing the augmented empty-face pair gives one critical vertex and \(64\) critical four-cells. By discrete Morse theory this CW type is exactly \(\bigvee^{64}S^4\). The equivalence with the second Morse iterate uses the published simplicial identification of the first iterate as the prism, and is not inferred from homology. The independent mod-two boundary calculation yields ranks \((17,109,319,386)\), consistent with, but not replacing, the homotopy proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
The most relevant published mathematical record analyzes precisely the same triangular-prism Morse complex and establishes only its mod-two homology, explicitly disclaiming integral homology and homotopy. The new full homotopy equivalence does not follow from those mod-two ranks or the published lower connectivity bound. Scoville's 2026 paper identifies the first Morse iterate and does not classify the second. Chari–Joswig's rooted-forest construction is an enumeration model, not this particular homotopy result. Jojić's directed-forest shellability theorems cover graphs with complete sources or graphs essentially underlying trees, neither of which includes the bidirected prism. Searches for prism aliases, Cayley directed-forest complexes, exact sphere multiplicities, and iterates revealed no stronger covering result. Kennedy Courtney's Cayley-graph directed-forest thesis is plausibly relevant, but its full text was unavailable; an equivalent unpublished or unindexed theorem also cannot be excluded.

## Value — PASS
The graph \(P=C_3\square K_2\) arises canonically by applying the discrete Morse-complex functor to the boundary of a triangle, making the second iterate mathematically natural. Its homotopy type was not settled by the existing exact homology benchmark: integral torsion and unstable attaching maps are invisible to mod-two Betti ranks. The explicit complete acyclic matching supplies an exact integral wedge classification and a reusable finite test case for investigations of Morse-complex iteration. No extrapolation to arbitrary prisms or iterates is claimed.
