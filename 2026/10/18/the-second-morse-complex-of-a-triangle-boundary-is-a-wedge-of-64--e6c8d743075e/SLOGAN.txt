The second discrete Morse complex of a triangular boundary is homotopy equivalent to a wedge of \(64\) four-spheres: \(\mathcal M(\mathcal M(\partial\Delta^2))\simeq\bigvee^{64}S^4\). An explicit complete acyclic matching on the triangular-prism Morse complex upgrades previously known mod-two homology to an integral homotopy classification.
