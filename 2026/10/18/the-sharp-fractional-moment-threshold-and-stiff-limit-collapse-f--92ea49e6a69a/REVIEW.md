## Value — PASS
A probability-one stability guarantee for a numerical integrator does not control even very low-order expected errors as stiffness increases. This theorem quantifies exactly which error moments are stable for each scalar stiffness and determines their collapse at the mathematically natural stiff limit with a nontrivial explicit constant. The complete critical-exponent classification is useful in reliability analysis and method comparison, not an arbitrary finite test or a restatement of the already-known second-moment bound.
