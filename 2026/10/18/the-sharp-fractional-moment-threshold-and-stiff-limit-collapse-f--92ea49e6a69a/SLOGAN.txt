The randomized implicit RK2 method is almost surely stable for every negative scalar test parameter but, for each stiffness \(a>2\), its absolute moments have a unique sharp critical order \(p_*(a)\) separating exponential decay from exponential growth. This moment threshold collapses as \(p_*(a)\sim12\log(a)/(\pi^2a)\), so no fixed positive moment remains stable under arbitrarily strong scalar damping.
