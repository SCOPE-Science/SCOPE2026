For contractive scalar multipliers of the Drury–Arveson space satisfying \(f(0)=0\), the universal absolute monomial Bohr set is exactly the \(\ell^2\) ball of radius \(1/\sqrt2\), in every finite or countably infinite dimension. Explicit degree-two Blaschke multipliers touch every boundary point with rigid coefficient magnitudes, and the optimal radial majorant crosses level one with the sharp universal slope \(2\sqrt2\).
