In the independent-gap packet-reversal overhand shuffle, the exact permutation-parity eigenvalue has generating function \((1+(1-p)z)/(1-pz+(1-p)z^2)\). Its even-versus-odd bias is positive for every deck size exactly when \(p\ge2(\sqrt2-1)\), and oscillates in sign across infinitely many deck sizes below that sharp threshold; repeated-pass parity probabilities follow by taking powers of the eigenvalue.
