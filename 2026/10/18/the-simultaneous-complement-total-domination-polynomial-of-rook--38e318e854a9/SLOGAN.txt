For every rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) with \(m,n\ge2\), an explicit inclusion–exclusion formula counts all sets that simultaneously dominate the rook graph and totally dominate its complement. For \(2\le m\le n\), the minimum size jumps from \(4\) at \(m=2\) to \(m\) at \(m\ge3\), with complete structural classifications and exact numbers of minimizers.
