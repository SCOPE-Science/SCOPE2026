For the ladder \(P_2\square P_n\), every maximum-cardinality minimal dominating set is a prefix of just six periodic column patterns, of which two are admissible only when \(n\) is even. Thus there are exactly two optimizers for \(n=1\), four for odd \(n\ge3\), and six for even \(n\ge2\), even though the total number of minimal dominating sets grows rapidly.
