## correctness

The exact real ell1 norming-functional criterion gives x⊥B y iff |sum_{i in supp(x)}sign(x_i)y_i| ≤ sum_{i outside supp(x)}|y_i|, valid also at zeros. For a mutual clique containing an axis, reverse orthogonality forces others into its orthogonal two-coordinate plane, where at most two lines coexist (clique <=3). For a given two-coordinate support, a mutual pair forces both vectors to have equal absolute nonzero coordinates with opposite relative signs; at most the two diagonal projective lines. Three full-support clique vectors cannot exist because sign-functional equations force one of three nonzero coordinates to vanish; with any full-support line, there are ≤2 full-support and ≤1 for each of three pair supports, total ≤5. Therefore max 6, and equality forces exactly the unique six diagonals across three pair supports. Independent exact evaluation of all 15 pairs of those six candidates confirmed bidirectional inequality. The finite projective-coordinate-grid CHECK.py was examined (49 lines in dimension three), but the all-real infinite claim rests on the symbolic support proof, not grid exhaustion.
