The mutual Birkhoff–James orthogonality graph on real projective lines in \(\ell_1^3\) has clique number exactly six. Its sole maximum clique consists of the six directions \([e_i+e_j]\) and \([e_i-e_j]\) with \(1\le i<j\le3\); the result holds for all real vectors, not only a finite coordinate grid.
