The six distinct \(I_2\)-fiber parameters of the rational elliptic surfaces studied by Ciliberto and collaborators lie on precisely the classical degree-\(15\) Hermite hypersurface of binary sextics; an explicit product of fifteen matching determinants gives its equation. Their associated genus-two curves are exactly bielliptic curves on Humbert \(H_4\), and the elliptic-surface and genus-two moduli are birational via a generically unique reconstruction.
