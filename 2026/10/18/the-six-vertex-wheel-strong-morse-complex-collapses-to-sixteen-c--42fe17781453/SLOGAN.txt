The full strong-Morse matching complex of the six-vertex wheel \(K_1*C_5\) collapses through twenty free-face pairs to a graph of rank sixteen, so it is \(\bigvee^{16}S^1\). Its first rational homology is \(4\varepsilon\oplus3V\) under the dihedral group \(D_5\), and hence has no globally invariant classes.
