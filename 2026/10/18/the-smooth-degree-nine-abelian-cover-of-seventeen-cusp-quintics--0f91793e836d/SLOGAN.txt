For the 2026 double-Barth–Rams family of 17-cusp quintic surfaces, the two known 12-cusp divisibility relations force a third such relation and an exact ternary \([17,2,12]\) cusp code. Their smooth loci have abelianization \((\mathbb Z/3)^2\), and the resulting smooth degree-nine quasi-étale canonical covers have \(K^2=45\), \(p_g=10\), and \(q=0\).
