# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** The leading coefficient is derived from the probability of a rare monotone extinction excursion, then a renewal identity, an exact ordering-chamber graph-cut integral, and independent edge-coordinate/Laplace-density reductions for trees and unicyclic graphs. The proof accounts for upward-reinfection excursions in the asymptotic remainder. Exact arithmetic covers every labeled tree through six vertices (1,441) and every connected labeled unicyclic graph through five (238), plus independently solved finite-state CTMC equations for six selected examples at two rates. Finite tests alone are not proof.

**Originality (PASS, with bibliographic limits).** McVinish–Pollett's discrete-time many-patch limit does not imply the continuous-time, fixed-graph, high-colonization theorem. Holme–Tupikina's fully inspected 2018 study includes all 12,110 connected graphs through eight vertices and explores leading SIS prefactors, but does not give the exact cut-integral or unrestricted tree/unicyclic formulas. Van de Bovenkamp–Van Mieghem (2015) report exact complete/star formulas, which do not state the claimed all-tree law; only its publisher abstract, not its full text, could be inspected. Published results from other size- and rate-scaling regimes were also compared. An unnoticed equivalence remains possible, especially in inaccessible text.

**Mathematical value (PASS).** The strong-colonization graph invariant has an exact probabilistic and integral interpretation. Universal behavior over *all* tree shapes, paired with a strict cycle-length gain in unicyclic networks, answers a natural network-design comparison and goes beyond a routine finite graph enumeration. The finding is a theorem in a restricted stochastic occupancy model, not a conservation management recommendation.

**Closest literature:** R. McVinish and P. K. Pollett (2014), DOI 10.1239/jap/1402578626; R. van de Bovenkamp and P. Van Mieghem (2015), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevE.92.032806; P. Holme and L. Tupikina (2018), DOI 10.1088/1367-2630/aaf016.

**Limitations and risk:** Neither finite-\(\lambda\) topology ordering, heterogeneous rates, nor joint graph-size/colonization limits are proved. Related full texts unavailable for inspection could affect priority. The analytical result is not independently validated.
