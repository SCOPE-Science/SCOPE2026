In a homogeneous finite habitat network with colonization rate \(\lambda\), the leading mean time to total extinction is \(\lambda^{n-1}/Z(G)\), where \(Z(G)\) is an exact graph-cut integral. Every tree on \(n\) patches has the same leading lifetime \((\lambda/2)^{n-1}\), while for a unicyclic network the entire leading improvement depends only on its cycle length, not on where its attached trees branch.
