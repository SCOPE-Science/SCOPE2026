Every minimum feedback vertex set of the degree-five Clebsch graph is one of exactly eighty seven-vertex sets obtained by deleting a single point from a coordinate hyperplane of its even-parity five-bit realization. Their one- and two-exchange graphs are \(10K_8\), but allowing three simultaneous replacements makes the optimum reconfiguration graph connected, 23-regular, and of diameter three; the same configurations are exactly the smallest four-neighbor contagious sets.
