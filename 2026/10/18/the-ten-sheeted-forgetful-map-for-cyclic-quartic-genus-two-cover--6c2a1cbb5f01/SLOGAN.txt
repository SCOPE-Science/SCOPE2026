For the characteristic-two genus-two Artin–Schreier–Witt covers \((F-1)(Y_0,Y_1)=(x,\lambda x^3)\), the parameter \(\lambda\ne0\) uniquely identifies the unlabelled cyclic-quartic marking, while forgetting the subgroup identifies exactly the parameters with equal \(J(\lambda)=(\lambda^2+\lambda+1)^5/\lambda^6\). This separable degree-ten forgetful map has ten marked classes over every nonzero finite invariant and only two over zero.
