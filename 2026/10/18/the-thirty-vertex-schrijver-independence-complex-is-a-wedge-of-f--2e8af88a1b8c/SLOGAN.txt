The independence complex of the thirty-vertex Schrijver graph \(SG_{3,3}\), encoding intersecting families of cyclically stable triples, has the integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^4 S^3\). An exhaustive acyclic discrete-Morse matching reduces its 15,291 nonempty faces to one vertex and four three-cells, resolving this natural finite case beyond the previously classified two-subset family.
