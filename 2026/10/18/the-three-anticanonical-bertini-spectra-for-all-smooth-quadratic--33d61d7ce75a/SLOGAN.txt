Among all all-smooth pencils of binary quadrics in \(\mathbf P^4\), the anticanonical hyperplane Bertini profile has exactly three possibilities: 12, 15, or 9 geometrically smooth genus-one sections out of 31 rational hyperplanes. The two profiles with half-discriminant factorization \((2,3)\) have disjoint point-count ranges for their smooth sections, despite each base surface having seven rational points.
