A motion-invariant random plane cutting a regular cuboctahedron produces only quadrilaterals, hexagons, and octagons, with exact probabilities \(0.1930593371\ldots\), \(0.3253457827\ldots\), and \(0.4815948802\ldots\). This complete three-atom law follows from a surprising direction-by-direction parity rigidity: every generic affine-plane section has an even number of sides, a property shared by all affine cuboctahedra.
