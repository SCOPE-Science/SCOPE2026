The three-clique-free complexes of two cospectral strongly regular graphs have different integral homotopy dimensions: \(\operatorname{CF}_3(\mathrm{Sh})\simeq\bigvee^9S^6\) whereas \(\operatorname{CF}_3(K_4\square K_4)\simeq\bigvee^{15}S^5\). Both graphs have sixteen vertices, forty-eight edges and thirty-two triangles, so those standard counts cannot determine this clique-free topology.
