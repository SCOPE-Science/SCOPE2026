# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The transported multiplication is checked against the basis operation. The associative-group-ring calculation of the associator is exact and is used to establish necessity and sufficiency for all three nucleus conditions. The middle-nucleus inverse image follows from the full image of the group-ring endomorphism. The right-nucleus dichotomy carefully distinguishes the supports of the coset sums even when the coefficient characteristic divides the group order; the full-nucleus dimension then follows by rank-nullity. Every field characteristic and the degenerate subgroup cases are included. Direct, unrelated associator-matrix construction checks all 346 cases in the attached verifier; these finite examples are supplementary, not a substitute for the proof.

## Originality — PASS (historical equivalence risk disclosed)
Zhang's arXiv:2610.08204v1, Proposition 2.1, contains the transported multiplication but no classification of the nuclei; the same paper's main theorems concern augmentation filtrations, commutators, idempotents, and Peirce spectra. Sangare and Ta's arXiv:2608.01550v2, Proposition 4.7, already treats the left nucleus of odd dihedral quandle algebras. Connected-quandle and Latin-quandle nuclei are also known special cases, and are not asserted as new. The assessed contribution is the simultaneous formula for nonconnected finite Alexander quandles and its sharp right-nucleus/characteristic dichotomy. Relevant published-result comparisons and searches for subgroup-orbit, left/middle/right nucleus, and twisted group-ring equivalent formulations did not reveal this stronger statement. An earlier equivalent twisted-algebra theorem remains a residual historical risk; absence from an index is not proof of novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The classification supplies all three natural associativity invariants and their intersection in a family where Latin-quandle simplifications fail. It exposes a sharp invariant jump under change of the coefficient field: the four-element dihedral quandle has trivial full nucleus outside characteristic two but a two-dimensional full nucleus in characteristic two. Unipotent examples yield arbitrarily large such jumps, and the formula gives immediate tests for them without brute-force associator systems. This is a natural structural extension of recent group-ring methods, not merely another small-table census.

## Boundaries
Finite abelian underlying groups and coefficient fields only; no statement of priority certainty, independent verification, or generalization to nonabelian groups. The connected and Latin specializations were previously known.
