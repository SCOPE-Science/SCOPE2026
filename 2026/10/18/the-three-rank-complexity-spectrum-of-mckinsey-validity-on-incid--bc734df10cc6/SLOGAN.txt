On three-layer incidence Kripke frames, the McKinsey axiom turns into the complement of hypergraph two-colourability. Uniform hyperedge sizes \(1\), \(2\), and \(3\) give respectively first-order-decidable, \(\mathsf L\)-complete, and \(\mathsf{coNP}\)-complete finite-frame validity problems, with an explicit first-order reduction establishing the sharp rank-two lower bound.
