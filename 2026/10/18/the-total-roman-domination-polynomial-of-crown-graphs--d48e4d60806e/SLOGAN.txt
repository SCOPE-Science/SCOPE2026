For the crown graph \(\mathrm{Cr}_n=K_2\times K_n\), an explicit seven-term formula enumerates every total Roman dominating labeling by its weight, for all \(n\ge2\). The minimum-weight counts are \(1\) at \(n=2\), \(31\) at \(n=3\), and \(n^2(n-1)\) for \(n\ge4\); the full polynomial, rather than the previously known minimum value, is the result.
