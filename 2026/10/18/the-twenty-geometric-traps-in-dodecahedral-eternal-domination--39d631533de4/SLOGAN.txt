In the dodecahedral graph, seven guards suffice for all-guards-move eternal domination, but only \(760\) of the \(780\) dominating seven-guard placements are indefinitely defensible. The other twenty are exactly the distance-shell sets \(S_1(x)\cup S_4(x)\cup S_5(x)\); every defensible placement has at least \(26\) distinct defensible replies to every attack.
