# The twenty-one smallest induced three-sphere carriers of Heawood independence homology

## Finding
Let \(H\) be the fourteen-vertex Heawood graph (the point–line incidence graph of the Fano plane), and let \(K=\operatorname{Ind}(H)\) be its independence complex. Among all vertex subsets \(W\), the least cardinality for which the inclusion
\[
\widetilde H_3(K[W];\mathbb Q)\longrightarrow\widetilde H_3(K;\mathbb Q)
\]
is nonzero is **ten**. Exactly **twenty-one** ten-vertex subsets attain this minimum. Each is the complement of two distinct Fano points and the **two lines incident with neither point**. Every associated induced subcomplex has integral homotopy type \(S^3\); its generator survives in ambient rational and mod-two homology. The twenty-one images span \(\widetilde H_3(K;\mathbb Q)\cong\mathbb Q^6\), and the corresponding mod-two images span the six-dimensional ambient mod-two group.

Under the canonical incidence-preserving automorphism group \(\operatorname{GL}_3(\mathbb F_2)\), these twenty-one induced sphere carriers constitute a single orbit indexed by unordered pairs of Fano points. Equivalently, Hochster's formula gives the squarefree graded Betti number \(\beta_{6,10}(\mathbb Q[K])=21\) for the Stanley–Reisner ring, with all twenty-one multidegrees carrying one-dimensional third reduced homology.

## Assumptions and scope
Represent both points and line covectors by the seven nonzero binary triples, identified with integers \(1,\ldots,7\). Point \(p\) and line \(\ell\) are adjacent exactly when the mod-two scalar product \(p\cdot\ell\) is zero. Label point vertices \(0,\ldots,6\) (vectors one through seven) and line vertices \(7,\ldots,13\) (covectors one through seven). An induced subcomplex \(K[W]\) consists of independent sets entirely contained in \(W\). All statements on maps refer to the actual inclusions \(K[W]\hookrightarrow K\), not merely abstract isomorphisms of the source homology groups. Integral homotopy claims use ordinary finite simplicial complexes; vector-space spanning claims use the indicated fields.

## Proof
**The ambient complex.** Enumerating all independent vertex sets gives numbers \((1,14,70,154,147,56,14,2)\) by cardinality. On the 457 nonempty simplices, use the deterministic vertex-toggle matching: for each pivot in increasing label order, pair every still-unmatched face not containing that pivot with its still-unmatched enlarged face. The exhaustive matched Hasse graph, oriented upwards on matched incidences and downwards otherwise, is acyclic. It has 225 matched pairs, one critical vertex and six critical three-simplices. Forman's elementary discrete-Morse theorem therefore implies \(K\simeq\bigvee^{6}S^3\), including \(\widetilde H_3(K;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^6\). This ambient homotopy type was previously established; it is reconstructed here to verify the inclusion targets.

**The sharp ten-vertex threshold.** Enumerate all \(\binom{14}{9}=2002\) nine-vertex subsets. For each \(W\), form the reduced simplicial chain boundary matrices over \(\mathbb F_2\). Every induced subcomplex has \(\widetilde H_3(K[W];\mathbb F_2)=0\). If \(|W|<9\), include \(W\) in some nine-vertex subset, so the map to ambient rational third homology factors through a zero rational group. Indeed, for any integral boundary matrix its rank over \(\mathbb Q\) is at least its rank modulo two, so vanishing mod-two third Betti number implies vanishing rational third Betti number. This proves the lower bound for a nonzero rational inclusion map.

For the \(\binom{14}{10}=1001\) ten-vertex subsets, the same exhaustive matrices give third mod-two Betti number zero for 980 subsets and one for exactly 21. These 21 are identified *independently of the homology calculation*: their complements are precisely \(\{p,q,\ell,\ell'\}\), where \(p\ne q\) are Fano points and \(\ell,\ell'\) are the two lines containing neither. There are exactly two such lines for each of the 21 unordered point pairs, and the computed list of complements agrees with this incidence classification.

**Integral sphere representatives.** On each of the twenty-one induced complexes, apply the same increasing-pivot face matching, now restricted to its ten retained vertices. Each resulting oriented Hasse graph is acyclic and has exactly 69 pairs, one critical vertex, and one critical three-simplex (140 nonempty simplices total). Therefore \(K[W]\simeq S^3\) integrally for each listed \(W\).

**Survival and generation.** In each such \(W\), Gaussian elimination on the mod-two matrix \(\partial_3\) produces a nonzero three-cycle. The exhaustive verifier checks that it is not in \(\operatorname{im}\partial_4\) locally **or** in the full ambient \(\operatorname{im}\partial_4\). This proves that its class survives in \(\widetilde H_3(K;\mathbb F_2)\). Since the source and ambient degree-three integral homology are free, a nonzero mod-two image forces a nonzero rational inclusion map. The 21 explicit mod-two cycles, adjoined to a basis of the ambient four-boundary image (rank 44), increase the total rank by exactly six. Hence they generate the entire ambient six-dimensional mod-two homology. The corresponding integer inclusion maps therefore have images spanning the six-dimensional rational homology as well. No assertion of a saturated integral generating lattice is made.

**Symmetry and Betti corollary.** The incidence-preserving Fano collineation group is transitive on unordered pairs of distinct Fano points, since any two different nonzero binary vectors are independent. This proves the single-orbit statement. Hochster's formula applied to the just-established rational induced homology data gives \(\beta_{6,10}(\mathbb Q[K])=21\).

## Verification
The accompanying standalone, standard-library Python program `verify.py` rebuilds the graph and all independent sets. It recomputes the complete 2002-subset and 1001-subset third-homology census over \(\mathbb F_2\); independently identifies the 21 complements by point–line incidence; constructs and verifies 22 acyclic Morse matchings (ambient and one for each carrier); and produces 21 explicit mod-two cycles whose images survive and span the six-dimensional ambient homology quotient. It does not rely on precomputed tables or downloaded certificates. Running `python3 verify.py` returns `VERIFY_OK 2002_NINE_ZERO 21_TEN_SPHERES 21_NONZERO_MAPS SPAN_6`.

## Relationship to prior work
Barmak's star-cluster methods provide general reductions for independence complexes. The published calculation *The Heawood independence complex is a wedge of six three-spheres* determines the ambient homotopy type, and *The exceptional rational homology representation of the Heawood independence complex* determines its action under Fano collineations. Neither statement specifies the smallest induced carriers or the images of their inclusion maps. Tsukuda's theorem relating independence complexes of incidence graphs to Alexander duality is a useful general reduction, but does not enumerate or classify these twenty-one inclusion-essential subcomplexes. Hochster's formula relates the new classification to the multigraded Betti number; it does not supply the specific value without the carrier computation.

## Limitations
The theorem is specific to the Heawood graph and the stated degree-three homology. The rank calculation over \(\mathbb F_2\) and the Morse certificates are exhaustive for fourteen vertices, not for larger finite projective planes. No claim is made that the 21 embedded integral sphere classes form a saturated basis of the full integral homology lattice. A differently indexed exact induced-homology classification could exist; the literature comparison is limited to the named sources and searches in the accompanying review.
