# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The theorem is a complete arithmetic statement for a fixed integral Klein arrangement, not an inference from selected examples. The packaged exact-arithmetic script exhausts 1330 line triples, 210 line pairs, and 49 projective sections. All nonzero triple determinants have norms that are powers of 2, proving stability at every odd prime ideal. The two mod-8 Hensel reductions determine all section specializations because the minimum coordinate valuation is at most 2 in all cases; equality of alternative cross-product representatives is explicitly verified. No claim is made about scheme-theoretic lengths.

**Originality — PASS, with residual literature risk.** Bauer et al. describe the 49 Klein points and characteristic-seven phenomena. Innamorati lists the explicit Klein lines and generic incidences; its characteristic-two result concerns a different Ceva arrangement. Neither inspected relevant full-text portion contains the computed bad-reduction support or the two 2-adic 49-section collision tables. Targeted searches of related works and published-result records found no equivalent. The full text of a related Klein lines-and-conics work and unindexed older sources could still overlap.

**Value — PASS.** Bad reduction of the actual 49 blowup centers is a natural arithmetic-geometric boundary question. The result exhausts *all* primes for incidence stability and gives the precise, asymmetric 49-to-seven center-collision patterns at the only bad rational prime. This is a motivated complete classification, not an arbitrary slice of computational data.

The classifications apply to the named integral coordinate model. They do not establish flat-closure local lengths, special-fiber blowup regularity, or an intrinsic reduction statement after nonintegral changes of projective coordinates. Independent validation is unavailable.
