For rook graphs \(R_{m,n}=K_m\square K_n\), every proper coloring is conflict-free when \(m\ne n\), but the square graph \(R_{n,n}\) for \(n\ge2\) needs exactly \(n+2\) colors rather than its chromatic number \(n\). The two-color gap is explained by a forced coincidence of row and column missing-color sets with \(n+1\) colors, and closed by a cyclic construction with distinct omitted-pair gaps.
