The abelian two-plane variety of the strictly upper-triangular \(4\times4\) Lie algebra has exactly two five-dimensional irreducible components, meeting in an irreducible fourfold, with \(2q^5+3q^4+2q^3+2q^2+q+1\) points over \(\mathbb F_q\). Its restricted elementary-subalgebra variety changes from two components in characteristic at least five to four components (one fivefold and three fourfolds) in characteristic three.
