The Petersen graph's ordinary matching complex carries two different five-dimensional rational \(S_5\)-representations: its degree-two homology is the sign twist of the natural five-point permutation module, whereas its degree-three homology is the irreducible Specht module \(S^{(3,2)}\). Exact oriented-chain and cyclic-invariant calculations determine both characters, refining the known nonequivariant homology ranks.
