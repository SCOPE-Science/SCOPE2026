# The unique smooth space-filling quartic section of the symplectic cubic over \\(\\mathbf F_2\\)

## Finding
Let \\(k=\\mathbf F_2\\), and fix the smooth cubic surface \\(S\\subset\\mathbf P^3_k\\) defined by \\(G=x^2y+xy^2+z^2w+zw^2\\). For a quartic \\(F\\) vanishing at every point in \\(\\mathbf P^3(k)\\), consider the scheme \\(C_F=V(G,F)\\). Quartics are counted modulo \\(G\\) times linear forms, so different classes give different quartic sections of \\(S\\).

Among exactly \\(2^{16}=65,536\\) such classes, precisely \\(288\\) have \\(C_F\\) smooth at all fifteen rational points. Under the rational symplectic automorphism group \\(\\operatorname{Sp}_4(k)\\) of \\(S\\), they form two orbits of \\(144\\) classes each. Exactly one of these two orbits consists of geometrically smooth curves. Thus there are precisely **144 geometrically smooth quartic sections through all fifteen points**, forming **one automorphism orbit**; every such section has a stabilizer of order \\(5\\). These curves are geometrically integral, space filling, of degree \\(12\\) and genus \\(19\\).

One smooth representative is \\(F=0\\), where
\\[
\\begin{aligned}
F={}& yz^2w+y^2w^2+y^2zw+y^2z^2+y^3w+y^3z\\\\
 &+xw^3+xz^3+xyw^2+xyz^2+xy^2w+xy^2z+x^2z^2+x^3w.
\\end{aligned}
\\]

## Assumptions and scope
All counts are over \\(k=\\mathbf F_2\\) with the particular cubic \\(G\\) fixed. Geometric smoothness means smoothness over an algebraic closure, not merely nonsingularity at rational points. No classification of complete intersections on other cubic surfaces is asserted. The symplectic group acts via projective linear maps preserving \\(G\\).

## Proof
There are \\(35\\) ternary? No: **quaternary** homogeneous monomials of degree \\(4\\). Evaluation at the fifteen rational points of \\(\\mathbf P^3(k)\\) has rank \\(15\\) by exact binary Gaussian elimination. Its kernel \\(W\\) has dimension \\(20\\). The four quartics \\(Gx,Gy,Gz,Gw\\) are independent and span \\(G H^0(\\mathbf P^3,\\mathcal O(1))\\), so \\(W/GH^0(\\mathcal O(1))\\) has dimension \\(16\\).

For every rational point \\(P\\), the gradient \\(dG(P)\\) is nonzero. On a class \\(F\\), the intersection is nonsingular at \\(P\\) exactly when \\(dF(P)\\notin\\{0,dG(P)\\}\\). Adding \\(G L\\) changes \\(dF(P)\\) by \\(L(P)dG(P)\\), hence does not affect this test. Enumerating all \\(65,536\\) classes by Gray code and checking this rank criterion simultaneously at all fifteen rational points yields exactly \\(288\\) classes.

The cubic \\(G\\) is the polynomial \\(B(v,v^{[2]})\\) attached to the alternating form \\(B(u,v)=u_xv_y+u_yv_x+u_zv_w+u_wv_z\\). Each rational tangent plane contains exactly three rational lines on \\(S\\), and there are \\(15\\) such isotropic lines in all. For every rationally nonsingular quartic class the tangent line of \\(C_F\\) at a rational point is one of these fifteen lines. The finite enumeration returns fifteen distinct tangent lines in each of the \\(288\\) cases, giving \\(288\\) distinct point-to-line perfect matchings.

The symplectic transvections \\(T_v(u)=u+B(u,v)v\\), for the fifteen nonzero vectors \\(v\\), generate \\(\\operatorname{Sp}_4(k)\\), of order \\(720\\). Their explicit permutations of rational points and isotropic lines partition these \\(288\\) tangent matchings into exactly two orbits, of size \\(144\\) each. Since tangent matching is invariant under adding \\(GL\\) and each matching appears exactly once in the enumeration, these are precisely the two quartic-section orbits.

Geometric smoothness is checked on the two orbit representatives by the Jacobian criterion. On each affine chart \\(x_i=1\\), compute over \\(k\\) the Gröbner basis of the ideal generated by \\(G,F\\) and the six \\(2\\)-by-\\(2\\) minors of their gradient matrix. For the displayed smooth representative the basis equals \\(\\{1\\}\\) on all four charts, ruling out every geometric singularity. For the representative of the other orbit none of the four ideals is the unit ideal, proving geometric singularities exist over the algebraic closure. The projective group action preserves geometric smoothness. Therefore exactly the claimed orbit of \\(144\\) classes is geometrically smooth.

The scheme is a smooth complete intersection of positive degrees in projective three-space, hence connected and geometrically integral. Bézout gives degree \\(3\\cdot4=12\\); adjunction gives genus \\(1+3\\cdot4(3+4-4)/2=19\\). Its rational locus contains all \\(15\\) ambient rational points, and hence equals that whole point set. Orbit–stabilizer gives \\(720/144=5\\).

## Verification
The attached scripts `verify_tangent_census.py`, `verify_group_orbits.py` and `verify_geometric_smoothness.py` respectively implement complete binary linear-algebra enumeration, the symplectic group action on the tangent matchings, and exact characteristic-two Gröbner basis tests. The first returns a twenty-dimensional quartic interpolation kernel, a sixteen-dimensional quotient, \\(288\\) rationally nonsingular classes and \\(288\\) distinct matchings. The second returns two orbits of \\(144\\). The last returns a unit ideal on all four charts for the smooth representative, and non-unit ideals for the singular representative. These are finite exhaustive and symbolic checks, not extrapolations from numerical sampling.

## Relationship to prior work
Campbell–Dedvukaj–McCormick–Moon–Morales construct minimal-degree space-filling complete intersections, including examples over \\(\\mathbf F_2\\), and establish the minimum complete-intersection degree. Their result supplies the natural family but does not enumerate all quartic sections on the fixed cubic, separate rational and geometric smoothness, or calculate their symplectic-group orbits. Vikulova classifies the most symmetric cubic surfaces in characteristic two and supplies context for the highly symmetric fixed surface; this theorem concerns a different moduli problem, namely the quartic sections on that surface. Searches of relevant mathematical research records did not reveal a dominating classification.

## Limitations
The result concerns the fixed model \\(S\\), not every smooth cubic surface, all possible quartic complete intersections, or all space-filling curves. The mathematical counting and smoothness statements are exhaustive for the specified quotient, but a previously unindexed equivalent published enumeration cannot be categorically excluded. Symbolic Gröbner verification requires SymPy; the other enumerations use standard Python.
