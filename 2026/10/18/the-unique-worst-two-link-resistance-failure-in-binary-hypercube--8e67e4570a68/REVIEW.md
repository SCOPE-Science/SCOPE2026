# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The rank-two pseudoinverse identity, positive-definite deletion correction, Walsh correlation formulas and two-orientation exhaustive classification are derived in the manuscript. The parameter comparison uses a strictly increasing weighted expectation and a positive dimension-five product difference; dimensions three and four are handled by exact integration. All dimensions are covered analytically. The reproducible exact-rational checker evaluates 7,503 identities, including complete finite all-pair tests and independent deleted-graph inverses. These finite tests support but do not substitute for the general proof.

## Originality — PASS, residual bibliographic risk

The closest intact-hypercube Kirchhoff source is Liu et al. (2013), DOI 10.1155/2013/543189, whose main results give unreduced graph indices. The 1992 Hamburger–Pippert–Weakley publisher abstract concerns removal leverage for sums of shortest-path distances, a different invariant, and is not a full-text check. A known spectral-gap edge-failure classification concerns the smallest positive eigenvalue rather than the Kirchhoff reciprocal-spectrum sum, and selects different pair geometry. A 2014 folded-hypercube paper concerns a distinct intact network. No covering statement was identified in the checked mathematical literature and abstract database; the 1992 full text and unindexed specialized work remain residual risks.

## Value — PASS

The claim solves a natural exact worst-two-link failure problem for all binary hypercubes, enumerates every worst pair, quantifies the maximal total-resistance deterioration, and reveals that resistance degradation and spectral-gap degradation can favor different outage shapes.

## Scientific limitations

Only unit-conductance binary cubes of dimension at least three and exactly two distinct lost links are included; no generalization to weighted or directed networks is claimed. No independent mathematical audit has been performed.
