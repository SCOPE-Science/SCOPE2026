Among all solid right circular cones, one and only one height-to-base-radius ratio minimizes the squared coefficient of variation of their random directional widths: \(t_*=1.7090130666\ldots\), with the sharp value \(0.00168696608\ldots\). Unlike the cone of least worst-direction width ratio, whose parameter is \(\sqrt3\), this optimally mean-square-isotropic cone is strictly shorter and broader.
