Among all convex floating bodies of any planar parallelogram, the one at cap-area fraction \(\alpha=3/2-\sqrt2\) is uniquely closest to a Euclidean disk in Banach--Mazur distance, with exact distortion \(2(\sqrt2-1)/\sqrt{4\sqrt2-5}\). The complete distance profile has distinct sharp transitions at \(\alpha=3/2-\sqrt2\) and \(\alpha=1/8\), caused respectively by outer and inner circular-contact changes.
