For reciprocal sums of base-\(b\) palindromes, the previously bounded third-order error has the *exact* leading term \((\log b)/b^3\), with coefficient \(1\). More precisely, after multiplication by \(b^3\), the difference between this error and \(2Y_b-H_b\) remains bounded in absolute value by \(4\) for every integer base \(b\ge2\), where \(Y_b=\sum_{m=b}^{b^2-1}1/m\) and \(H_b=\sum_{a=1}^{b-1}1/a\).
