The domain is every nondegenerate Euclidean triangle with two independent arclength-uniform perimeter points and every real homothety scale \(s\in[0,1]\). The affine reference-triangle reduction preserves conditional uniform side parameters, and the positive-definite inellipse shape matrix gives the incidence inequality \((3uv-u-v)^2\le s^2(u^2+v^2-uv)\). The half-square parameterization and its exact roots establish the two integral regions. The displayed radical primitive differentiates to the integrand, and evaluation yields the asserted closed form. Endpoint factorization, small-scale Taylor expansion, logarithmic endpoint cancellation and side-mixture formula were separately checked. The attached Python checker reproduces symbolic identities, direct one-dimensional quadratures and physical segment/ellipse trials; experiments supplement rather than prove the theorem.

Searches of equivalent, broader and parameterized formulations uncovered no such complete profile. This is a best-knowledge originality assessment, not proof that a prior unindexed calculation cannot exist.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The event is a natural endpoint-sampled chord hitting a canonical inscribed ellipse, where the sampling law depends on Euclidean side lengths while the ellipse is affine-natural. The result extracts an exact universal conditional law and a complete global shape classification, together with distinct origin and tangency asymptotics including an explicit logarithmic cusp. A full continuum of ellipse scales and all triangle shapes are covered, not a single numerical evaluation or an arbitrary slice. It supplies a reproducible benchmark for interpreting random-chord sampling conventions in convex geometry.
