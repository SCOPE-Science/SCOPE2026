For every Euclidean triangle, a chord joining two independent arclength-uniform boundary points hits the centroid-scaled Steiner inellipse with probability \(Q(s)(1-\sum_{i=1}^3p_i^2)\), where \(Q\) is a universal explicit elementary function. This yields sharp triangle-shape extremizers, \(Q(1)=2/3+2\log(2)/9\), and a logarithmic cusp as the scaled ellipse reaches the three sides.
