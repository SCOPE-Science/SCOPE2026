The centered \(d\)-dimensional White Whale zonotope has Euclidean inradius \(2^{d-2}/\sqrt2\) and exactly \(d(d-1)\) tangent facets, whose normals are coordinate differences and whose shapes recursively embed the White Whale one dimension lower. Despite this quadratic contact shell, every \(d\ge2\) has a nontrivial one-dimensional segment of optimal inball centers; its Euclidean circumradius is \(2^{d-2}\sqrt d\).
