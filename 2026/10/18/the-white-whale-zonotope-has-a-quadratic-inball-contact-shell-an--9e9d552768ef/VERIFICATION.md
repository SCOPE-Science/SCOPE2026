# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Writing each binary bit as (1+ε_i)/2 gives precisely h(u)=2^(d−2) E|m+X| with m=Σu_i and X=Σu_i ε_i. Reflection of X gives E|m+X|=E max(|m|,|X|). Theorem 7 of De–Diakonikolas–Servedio provides the sharp first-moment inequality and exact two-coordinate equal-magnitude equality condition. Equal signs fail to achieve equality for the shifted expectation because X=0 on a positive-probability event and m≠0; unequal signs have m=0. Thus the only unit equality normals are (e_i−e_j)/√2 for ordered i≠j. The centered inscribed radius is the minimum support and is also globally optimal: the paired opposite support constraints would bound any translated ball by the same radius. Each minimizing normal annihilates binary generators with b_i=b_j, spanning the entire hyperplane (via e_i+e_j and remaining basis vectors). The supporting face is exactly the zonotope of these free summands, translated by the fixed endpoints of the other summands, and deleting the duplicated coordinate gives the smaller White Whale with the stated √2 stretch. Its tangent point is in the facet interior since a second supporting facet there would also be tangent to the smooth ball at the same point and therefore have the same normal. The two opposing ball constraints force c_i=c_j for all optimal centers. Every other facet has strictly positive slack at zero, and there are finitely many, so a nonzero diagonal interval remains feasible; symmetry, convexity and compactness make this the claimed segment. Every coordinate is bounded by 2^(d−2), and all-plus and all-minus choices attain the antipodal cube corners. Any mixed-sign corner would force the segment generated by e_i+e_j to its two different endpoints, which is impossible. The two antipodes alone force the unique enclosing ball of radius 2^(d−2)√d. This fully checks d≥2, including the embedded one-dimensional facet model when d=2. The frozen checker was inspected, not run; its cofactor code uses rounded floating-point determinants followed by exact integer support comparisons, and its sampled stresses are not an all-dimensional argument.
