At the first non-thin valuation strata of subalgebras of \(k[t]/(t^{14})\), the *non-thin* algebras are abstractly monomial, while the *thin* algebras are intrinsically nonmonomial in every characteristic. The two seven-dimensional types have smooth automorphism varieties \(\mathbb G_m^2\times\mathbb A^9\) and \(\mathbb G_m^2\times\mathbb A^{11}\), respectively.
