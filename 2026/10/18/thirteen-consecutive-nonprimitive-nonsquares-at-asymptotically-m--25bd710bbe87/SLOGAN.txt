The thirteen consecutive integers \(46256,\ldots,46268\) become nonprimitive nonsquares in every odd-degree extension of \(\mathbb F_{65537}\). Along prime extension degrees the primitive-element density approaches its absolute ceiling \(1/2\), showing that for every block length \(3\le k\le13\) this is the sharp, unattained supremum even when \(k\) consecutive nonprimitive nonsquares are required.
