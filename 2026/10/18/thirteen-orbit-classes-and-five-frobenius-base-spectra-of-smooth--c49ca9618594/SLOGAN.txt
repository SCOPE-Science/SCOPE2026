Over \(\mathbf F_2\), exactly 804 pencils of bidegree-\((2,2)\) genus-one divisors on \(\mathbf P^1\times\mathbf P^1\) have all three rational members geometrically smooth, forming 13 projective-coordinate orbits; none extends to an all-smooth net. Every base is eight distinct geometric points and its Frobenius cycle partition is exactly one of \((8)\), \((2,3,3)\), \((1,7)\), \((1,2,5)\) or \((1,1,6)\).
