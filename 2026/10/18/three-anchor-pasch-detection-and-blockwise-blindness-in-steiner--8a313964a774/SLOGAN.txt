Individually marking any number of points lying on one block of a finite Steiner \(2\)-design leaves its incidence graph's \(C^2\) theory determined by the design parameters alone. Three noncollinear marks are the sharp threshold for detecting a Pasch configuration: a rank-four sentence separates explicit projective and Bose Steiner triple systems on fifteen points, while two marks, or three collinear marks, cannot distinguish them.
