# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The recurrence and Pell identities produce exact window-sum expressions. A unimodular step-two recurrence collapses the infinite gcd to a two-argument integer gcd; prime-adic analysis at two and three reduces these gcds to the stated common divisor. The transition at even \(k\) and the extra factor of three at odd multiples of three are checked explicitly. Direct exact-arithmetic verification covered 240 window lengths and 7,620 checks. The finite checks are supplementary to the infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS within inspected material.** The closest primary source, Mbirika–Schrader–Spilker (2023), §6.1, Question 30, offers empirical relationships between these families but asks for closed forms. Those empirical relationships are explicitly credited here. The present proof identifies all three families as \(\gcd(k,B_k)\) times elementary local factors; that arithmetic identification is absent from the inspected text. The related 2022 generalized-Fibonacci-square theorem covers a different recurrence and does not imply the results. Targeted literature, indexed-result and sequence-table searches found no stronger published formula; absence of retrieval is not a uniqueness certificate.

**Value — PASS.** The paper's open problem singles out exactly these four families. This theorem gives a unified exact solution for three of them, proving the observations and strengthening them to a divisibility theorem. The surviving dependence on \(\gcd(k,B_k)\) and the unhandled ordinary cobalancing family are genuine limitations rather than erased parts of the problem.

**Residual risk.** Other literature could contain these equalities under different sequence conventions; any complete classification of the term-index gcd would imply these formulas. An independent audit has not been performed.
