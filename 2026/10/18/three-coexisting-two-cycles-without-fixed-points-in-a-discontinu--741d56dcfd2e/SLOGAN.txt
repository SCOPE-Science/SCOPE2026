In the Lawnik–Moysis–Volos two-branch power map, three is the sharp maximum number of primitive two-cycles whenever the power exponent satisfies \(d\ne1\). The explicit fixed-point-free parameters \((a,b,c,d)=(1/20,1/10,21/200,1/2)\) yield precisely three hyperbolic two-cycles—two repellers and one attractor—throughout a nonempty open parameter region.
