On \(K_{n,n,n}\), the three-colour antiferromagnetic Potts model has exactly six single-site-blocked configurations whenever \(\gcd(n,6)=1\). At sizes congruent to \(2\) or \(4\) modulo \(6\) their number grows with entropy per spin \(\log2\), while sizes divisible by \(3\) have entropy \(\log3\): a three-level arithmetic metastability effect absent from ordinary equilibrium entropy reasoning.
