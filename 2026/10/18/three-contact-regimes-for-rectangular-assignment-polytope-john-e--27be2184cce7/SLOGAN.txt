For every rectangular \(m\)-by-\(n\) fixed-cardinality assignment polytope with \(2\le m<n\) and \(1\le k<m\), the unique John ellipsoid has an exact rational three-eigenspace spectrum found by a scalar capped water-filling equation. The two unequal sides produce three sharply separated contact patterns—coordinate facets only, coordinate plus row caps, or all coordinate, row, and column facets—with explicit threshold inequalities and volume.
