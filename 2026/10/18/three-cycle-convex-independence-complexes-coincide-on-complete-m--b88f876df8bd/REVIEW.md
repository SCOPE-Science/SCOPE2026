# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The cycle-addition rule is equivalent to two neighbours in one induced connected component. Explicit hull tables for stars, cyclic complete bipartite graphs, and multipartite graphs with three or more parts classify all admissible sets for all three definitions; every counting coefficient follows from pure subsets and the permitted mixed sizes. Collapsing each contractible pure-part simplex gives a one-dimensional connected CW model when at least three parts are present, and a simply connected two-dimensional CW model in the cyclic bipartite case. Euler characteristic and standard integral homotopy theory give the stated sphere multiplicities. A standalone predicate-based enumeration and mod-two homology checker passed all 58 unordered multipartite types through eight vertices, but is supplementary.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
The September 2026 Nair–Anand–Chandran–Nascimento–Anil paper studies cycle-convex Helly/Radon/rank complexity and near-extremal cases, not an equality of independence **families** or their topological realizations on the complete multipartite class. The April 2026 Carathéodory paper explicitly gives values on complete multipartite graphs, including three for cyclic bipartite graphs, but uses a different notion. Published dissociation-polynomial work already implies the \(r\ge3\) numerical face polynomial for a different set family; that formula alone is therefore not asserted new. Comparisons used statement implications, related aliases and relevant published-finding corpus records, and found no source covering the three-family equivalence and wedge homotopy result. The inaccessible complete text of an IWOCA 2026 paper on cycle-convex rank/general position and other unindexed work remain bibliographic risks.

## Value — PASS
This resolves three a priori different hereditary independence systems on an infinite natural graph family and provides their full generating polynomials, rather than merely numerical rank parameters. It distinguishes a bipartite class with arbitrary-size two-sphere homology from multipartite graphs with circle homology and explicitly describes the boundary at stars. The topology and exceptional mixed triples are genuine additional information beyond familiar Carathéodory ranks or dissociation counts.

## Scientific limits
No inference about unrelated graph classes or general equality of these three independence notions is made. The proof, not the bounded mod-two checks, supports the integral infinite-family conclusion. A fully decisive global priority search and independent validation remain outstanding.
