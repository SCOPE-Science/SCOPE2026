The projective edge toric variety of every complete tripartite graph has at most three singular components: pairwise disjoint Segre varieties, one for each omitted part of size at least two. Along each component the transverse singularity is exactly a rank-one \(2\times w\) determinantal cone, with multiplicity \(w\); this gives the complete non-\(\mathbb Q\)-factorial locus and the sharp Gorenstein cutoff.
