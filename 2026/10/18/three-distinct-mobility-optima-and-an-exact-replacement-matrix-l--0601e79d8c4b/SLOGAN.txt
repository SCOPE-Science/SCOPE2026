On every regular territorial-mobility network, the complete replacement matrix is an exact quadratic polynomial in the graph adjacency matrix, so triangle counts change individual interaction weights even while all vertex temperatures remain identical. On triangle-free networks of degree \(d\ge2\), total interaction temperature peaks at fidelity \(1\), raw neighboring encounters at \(d\), and the actual multiplayer-weighted neighboring replacement strength at a unique, strictly larger fidelity \(h_d^*>d\).
