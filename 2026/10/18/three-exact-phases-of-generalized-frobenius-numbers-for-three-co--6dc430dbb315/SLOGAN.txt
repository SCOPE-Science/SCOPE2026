For the three consecutive denominations \(a,a+1,a+2\), the generalized \(p\)-Frobenius number has a closed three-phase formula for every \(a\ge3\) and \(0\le p\le2a\). Its two new phases extend the previously known bound \(p\le\lfloor a/2\rfloor\) to linear-size parameters, with a final alternating parity pattern.
