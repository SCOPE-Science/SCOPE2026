# Scientific review: three Frobenius base spectra on a split degree-seven del Pezzo surface

## Correctness — PASS

The eight-dimensional anticanonical space over \(\mathbf F_2\) has 255 nonzero sections. An exact rank-15 Macaulay smoothness condition identifies 60 smooth sections and 104 smooth-member pencils. A separate chart Gröbner computation agrees. The eight-element full surface automorphism group partitions the pencil set into thirteen size-eight orbits. For every representative the supplied elimination certificates give a graph ideal defined by one squarefree degree-seven univariate polynomial. Together with the two imposed plane points, Bézout length nine certifies a reduced intersection and a reduced residual base scheme on the blowup. Independent extension-field counts and projective point evaluations reproduce the closed-degree strata and the five point-count spectra. The differential-evaluation obstruction rules out all-smooth nets. These are exact algebraic and exhaustive finite calculations, not finite extrapolation.

## Originality — PASS, with qualified priority

The already recorded ambient smooth plane-cubic pencil census has 5544 pencils in 37 projective classes, and its rational-basepoint statistics imply the restricted count 104 and supply representative equations. That numerical statement and the existence of the pencils are *not* claimed as novel. The current claim is the new complete scheme-theoretic reducedness, residual Frobenius degree partition and its equivalence with the three elliptic-member point counts. Relevant Asgarli–Ghioca smooth-pencil work (arXiv:2203.07169), Asgarli–Ghioca–Reichstein linear-family work (arXiv:2207.00918), and Blache et al. finite-field anticanonical work (arXiv:1903.09397) were inspected at the identified theorem/section level. None states or logically determines the added scheme and point-count classification without additional computation. Full priority against unindexed tables is not proven; derivability from older explicit representative equations remains an acknowledged risk.

## Value — PASS

The intersection scheme and its Frobenius orbits provide natural arithmetic geometry of a complete anticanonical pencil. A geometric closed point of degree seven versus a degree-two/degree-five split or a rational point plus degree-six orbit are genuinely different base-scheme structures. The exact correspondence with smooth member point counts is useful for recognizing these structures over \(\mathbf F_2\) without extending fields. Both reducedness and the no-net bound address finite-field Bertini behavior; mere re-enumeration of pencils is excluded from the stated advance.

## Limitations and references

The conclusion is solely for the split degree-seven del Pezzo blowup over \(\mathbf F_2\), with all *rational* pencil members smooth. It makes no claim about all geometric pencil members, nonsplit surfaces, arbitrary finite fields, or isomorphism classes of abstract genus-one curves.
