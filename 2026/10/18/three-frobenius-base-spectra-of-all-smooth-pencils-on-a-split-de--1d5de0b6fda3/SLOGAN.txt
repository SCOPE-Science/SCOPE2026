On the split degree-seven del Pezzo surface over \(\mathbf F_2\), every rationally all-smooth anticanonical pencil has seven distinct geometric base points, and their Frobenius degrees are exactly \((7)\), \((2,5)\), or \((1,6)\), occurring for \(32\), \(24\), and \(48\) pencils. The point counts of the three smooth genus-one members determine which partition occurs; the complete classification has thirteen automorphism orbits.
