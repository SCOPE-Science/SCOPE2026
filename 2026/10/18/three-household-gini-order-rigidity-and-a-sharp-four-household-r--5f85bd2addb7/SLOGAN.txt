For two equally weighted positive income profiles with equal totals and a Lorenz/majorization ordering, every common increasing concave income transformation preserves their relative-Gini ordering when there are at most three households. Four households are the sharp failure threshold: the profiles \((1,1,1,3)\) and \((1,1,2,2)\) reverse their Gini ranking after a common top-code, or even after a smooth strictly increasing strictly concave transformation.
