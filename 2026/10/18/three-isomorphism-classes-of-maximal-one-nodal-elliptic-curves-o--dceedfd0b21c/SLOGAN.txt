The \(270\) ways to pinch a degree-two point on the maximal elliptic curve \(y^2=x^3+1\) over \(\mathbb F_{25}\) yield exactly three, not \(270\), isomorphism classes of maximal one-nodal arithmetic-genus-two curves. Their automorphism groups are \(C_2\times C_2\), \(C_2\), and \(C_2\), detected by three orbits of nonzero four-torsion differences.
