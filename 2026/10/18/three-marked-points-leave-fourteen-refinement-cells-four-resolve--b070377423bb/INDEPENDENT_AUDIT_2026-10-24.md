## correctness

PASS

The arbitrary projective-plane axioms force precisely the seven dual neighbour-multiplicity classes on each part after two rounds, each with sizes 1,1,1,q-1,q-1,q-1,(q-1)^2. Equitability closes refinement and gives colour-refinement/C² equivalence even across nonisomorphic planes of the same order. Three collinear labels are fixed by a nontrivial elation, and three noncollinear labels retain cells of size >=2 for prime p>=3. A labelled projective frame propagates singleton colours through unique joins and meets; explicit affine addition of 1 generates all F_p coordinates, so four point labels suffice for prime p. For p=2 the 14 classes are singletons and <=2 points are not sufficient. Actual verify_planes.py and verify_closure.py implement independent neighbour-multiset WL and modular projective cross-product closure over p=2,3,5,7,11,13; both were inspected, without elevating finite p to an all-prime proof.
