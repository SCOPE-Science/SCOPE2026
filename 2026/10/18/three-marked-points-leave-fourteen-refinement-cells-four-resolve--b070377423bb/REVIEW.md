# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The fourteen cells follow directly from the unique joining-line and intersection-point axioms; every type has a fixed neighbour-count vector and all fourteen types emerge by the second refinement round. The collinear three-mark obstruction is a nonidentity pointwise-axis projectivity. Four frame points force unique colours to propagate through joins and meets; explicit affine-coordinate addition proves that the prime-field frame generates every vertex. Two standalone programs reproduce both finite facets through prime thirteen. The proof does not infer an infinite result from finite experiments.

## Originality — PASS, with qualification
Searches of mathematical research records and the closest published papers found no statement of the exact fourteen-cell, two-round invariant together with the sharp point-individualization number for prime-order planes. Héger–Takáts concerns metric resolving sets, which are a different invariant. Arvind et al. address general refinement/individualization rather than the plane-specific value; Chen–Ren–Ponomarenko establishes unmarked higher-dimensional equivalence but does not state the present three-anchor one-dimensional profile or four-point closure. The geometric join/meet mechanism is classical, so an earlier specialized corollary remains a genuine priority risk.

The result is not merely a finite census or a renaming of an arbitrary parameter.
