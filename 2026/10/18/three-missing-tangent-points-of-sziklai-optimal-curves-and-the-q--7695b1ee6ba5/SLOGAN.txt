Every Sziklai-optimal degree-\(q-1\) curve over \(\mathbf F_q\), for \(q\ge5\), has the same rational tangent-incidence profile and misses exactly three rational points with all its tangents, so it is never tangent-filling. The unique optimal quartic over \(\mathbf F_5\) has arithmetic bitangent Frobenius type \(2^2 4^6\), with none of its \(28\) geometric bitangents rational over \(\mathbf F_5\).
