# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The matching complex is exactly the union of the path-edge base and cones on all apex-edge links. The path-independence suspension recurrence gives the complete links, with a homotopy-equivalent first attachment in the residue-one case and nullhomotopic attachments in the other cases. Low-dimensional endpoints are checked directly. These arguments determine the **integral homotopy type**, not just rational ranks. The standalone finite verifier agrees for all \(2\le n\le15\) and provides independent face-count/Euler checks; finite enumeration is corroboration, not the universal proof.

**Originality — PASS with residual risk.** The fully inspected arXiv:2411.04601v2 proves only the qualitative outerplanar wedge-or-contractible property; it does not calculate fan multiplicities. The fully inspected arXiv:2211.12559v2 determines a different zigzag-triangular-line graph family (including small graph coincidences), not the unbounded-degree fan family. Searches on the fan matching complex and graph-join aliases found no equivalent universal formula; unlike matching complexes, independence complexes of fan graphs themselves are a different construction. An unindexed derivation of this elementary-looking specialization remains possible.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Fans are the canonical all-diagonals-at-one-vertex maximal outerplanar triangulations. Their matching-complex face counts grow rapidly, while the exact number of sphere summands grows linearly and the unique homological degree follows a three-step pattern. The formula provides a natural explicit benchmark and an integral refinement of a recent general qualitative theorem, including the non-obvious cancellation in the residue-one class. It is a complete natural parameter-family classification rather than an arbitrary finite slice.

The proof does not establish a formula for all maximal outerplanar graphs or any equivariant strengthening. Primary-text overlap cannot exclude an unpublished or unindexed earlier computation.
