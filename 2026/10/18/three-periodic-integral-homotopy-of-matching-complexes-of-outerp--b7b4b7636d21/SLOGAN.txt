For every fan \(F_n=K_1\vee P_n\), the ordinary matching complex is a single-dimensional **integral sphere wedge** with exact three-periodic formula: \(\bigvee^{k+1}S^{k-1}\) for \(n=3k\), \(\bigvee^kS^k\) for \(n=3k+1\), and \(\bigvee^{2k+2}S^k\) for \(n=3k+2\). The result turns the known qualitative outerplanar wedge theorem into a complete infinite-family homotopy classification.
