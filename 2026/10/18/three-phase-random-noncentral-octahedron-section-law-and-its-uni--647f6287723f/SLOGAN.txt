A uniformly oriented plane at prescribed offset from a regular octahedron has an exact three-phase law for empty, quadrilateral and hexagonal sections. The quadrilateral probability is uniquely maximized at offset \(t=\sqrt{(5+2\sqrt2)/17}\) times the vertex radius, attaining \(0.9066856793\ldots\), and the proof isolates the genuinely new section-count information from previously known width probabilities.
