For every fixed prime \(p\ge5\), three-by-three cyclic Fourier minors of orders \(p^m\) have a complete rank-one/two/three classification determined only by four \(p\)-adic difference valuations. As \(m\to\infty\), a random minor is singular with sharp probability \(6/(p^{m+1})+o(p^{-m})\), and asymptotically almost every singular minor has rank two.
