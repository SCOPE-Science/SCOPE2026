A geometrically smooth plane quartic over \(\mathbf F_2\) has at most three rational hyperflexes, and every noncollinear rational triple is realized by exactly one such quartic. The \(28\) extremal quartics form one \(\operatorname{PGL}_3(\mathbf F_2)\)-orbit with stabilizer \(S_3\), and the hyperflexes are precisely their rational points.
