For the first pivot of an \(n\)-by-\(n\) Gaussian unitary matrix, every fixed planar \(\ell^q\) comparison norm has a sharp no-swap asymptotic, with a three-regime transition at \(q=2\). Below two, the exponent is \(2^{2/q-2}\), while above two every norm—including \(\ell^\infty\)—has the identical leading law \(\pi^{1/4}n^{-1/2}(\log n)^{-1/4}/2\).
