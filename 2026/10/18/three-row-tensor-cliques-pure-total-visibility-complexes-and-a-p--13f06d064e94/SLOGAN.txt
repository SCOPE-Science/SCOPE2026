For the three-row tensor product \(K_3\times K_n\), total-mutual-visibility sets are exactly complements with at least two vertices in each row and no duplicate two-element row supports. This class has a complete polynomial enumerator, a pure maximal-set complex with exactly \(\binom n2(\binom n2-1)(\binom n2-2)\) facets of size \(3n-6\), and a cubic Poisson limiting law in the \(1/n\) omission window.
