Balanced complete-bipartite graph associahedra exhibit three sharply different vertex geometries: under one Gaussian direction the terminal-leaf count tends to a geometric law, under uniform vertex selection it tends to a positive Poisson law, but conditional on a two-direction collision its deficit from the maximum tends to Poisson of mean one. Their rare collision probability is asymptotic to \(2e(m!)^3/((2m)!)^2\), even though ordinary Gaussian samples have only a bounded number of terminal leaves.
