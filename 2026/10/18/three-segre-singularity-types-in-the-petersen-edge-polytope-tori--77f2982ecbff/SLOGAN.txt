For the normal-fan projective toric ninefold of the Petersen **graph edge polytope**, the reduced singular locus has exactly 115 irreducible components: 60 with generic transverse rank-one \(2\times3\) cones, 45 with \(2\times4\) cones, and 10 with \(3\times3\) cones. Their six natural \(S_5\)-orbits have sizes \(10,20,30,15,30,10\); every simplicial normal cone is smooth, and every torus-fixed point is singular.
