At the exact intersection of the two mean-field social-contagion thresholds, denier density always decays like \(1/t\), but the infected density obeys three sharp regimes: \(t^{-2}\), \(t^{-2}/\log t\), or \(t^{-(1+\delta/\mu)}\), according as \(\delta<\mu\), \(\delta=\mu\), or \(\delta>\mu\). The universal leading coefficients are explicit in the first two regimes, and the third has a strictly positive initial-condition-dependent amplitude.
