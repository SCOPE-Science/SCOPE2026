Exactly three positive complete Hamming shells form an isometric sampling design for every quadratic Walsh polynomial precisely when their coordinate sums \(a<b<c\) obey \(ab+ac+bc=-(3n-2)\) and \(abc=-n(a+b+c)\), with automatically positive uniquely specified weights. An explicit integer discriminant test classifies every design; the first two inequivalent designs occur at dimension \(65\), and a Pell recurrence produces infinitely many such dimensions.
