For the normal-fan toric variety of \(\operatorname{STAB}(K_{p,q})\), with \(p,q\ge2\), the reduced singular locus has exactly \(p+q+\binom p2\binom q2\) components in three natural face families. Their generic transverse singularities are Segre cones over \((\mathbb P^1)^q\), over \((\mathbb P^1)^p\), and ordinary conifolds, with respective multiplicities \(q!\), \(p!\), and two.
