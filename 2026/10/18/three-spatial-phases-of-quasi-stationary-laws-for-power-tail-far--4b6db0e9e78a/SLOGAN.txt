For power-tail \(\alpha\)-Farey maps with holes shrinking onto the indifferent fixed point, canonical quasi-stationary states have three distinct spatial limits: a finite stationary law for \(\theta>1\), a uniform **logarithmic** depth distribution at \(\theta=1\), and an explicit nontrivial hole-scale density for \(0<\theta<1\). The critical logarithmic convergence is quantified by \(O(1/\log n)\).
