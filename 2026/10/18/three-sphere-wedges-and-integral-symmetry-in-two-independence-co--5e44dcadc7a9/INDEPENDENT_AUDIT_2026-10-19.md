## correctness

Reviewed full face and cellular proof. A family F of graph vertices (edges of K_n) inducing maximum disjointness degree ≤1 either is pairwise intersecting, hence lies in a star or triangle, or contains two disjoint edges and therefore every further edge meets both, so all lie in a four-point, six-edge simplex. The n star simplices form a good cover with nerve complete graph; adjoining triangular simplices for triples gives the two-skeleton of Δ^{n−1}. Every four-set simplex intersects this two-core in an octahedral S² with eight facets. Its relative oriented two-cycle has signed triangle facets (1,-1,1,-1), the usual tetrahedron boundary, and all pairwise four-set overlaps lie in the two-core. Simply connected two-core and π₂≈H₂ prove the attaching maps agree with the three-skeleton of simplex. Thus integral H3=ker ∂3 of rank binom(n−1,4), an equivariant lattice ker(∧⁴Z^n→∧³Z^n)=∧⁴ ker(sum) with natural S_n action; higher/other homology vanishes. Independent exact graph face computations and mod2 chain ranks for n=5,6 yielded Betti_3=1,5 and no other Betti, confirming finite instances. This is an infinite structural proof, not extrapolation.
