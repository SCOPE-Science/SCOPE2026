For every \(n\ge5\), the two-independence complex of the two-subset Kneser graph is homotopy equivalent to the three-skeleton of a simplex, hence to \(\bigvee^{\binom{n-1}{4}}S^3\). Its natural \(\mathfrak S_n\)-action on integral third homology is exactly \(\bigwedge^4\ker(\mathbb Z^n\xrightarrow{\sum}\mathbb Z)\); the \(n=5\) Petersen special case was already known.
