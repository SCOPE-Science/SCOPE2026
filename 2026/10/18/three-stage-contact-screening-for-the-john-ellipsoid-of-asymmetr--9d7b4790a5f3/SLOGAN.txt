The real Lipschitz ball of an asymmetric \(m\)-page book metric has a unique, explicitly evaluated John ellipsoid whose contacts pass sharply through short-spoke-plus-hub, all-edge, and two-spoke-only phases. The enclosing metric body keeps expanding after the second threshold, while its John ellipsoid stays fixed; the logarithmic volume is exactly \(C^1\) but not \(C^2\) at either transition, and the all-edge phase has width asymptotic to \(A^2/(m\sqrt{B^2-A^2})\).
