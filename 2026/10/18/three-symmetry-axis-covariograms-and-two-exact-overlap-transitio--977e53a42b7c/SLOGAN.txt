For the regular rhombic dodecahedron, the overlap volumes under translation along its three crystallographic symmetry axes have exact piecewise-polynomial formulas. At fixed Euclidean displacement, the best of these three axes switches sharply from threefold to twofold at \(3\sqrt2-2\sqrt3\), then from twofold to fourfold at the unique root \(\beta\in(1,\sqrt2)\) of \(2\beta^3-9\beta^2+(24-9\sqrt2)\beta-4=0\); no claim is made about all other directions.
