For every dimension \(m\ge4\), standard CountSketch–QR on an isometric mean-zero subspace has its conditioning outcome confined to \(1\), \(\sqrt m\), or rank failure (all three occur when the sketch has at least \(m\) rows), with closed-form probabilities. At the quadratic sketch-width scale the three probabilities have explicit Poisson-limit masses; linear sketch widths fail with probability tending to one despite a perfectly conditioned original matrix.
