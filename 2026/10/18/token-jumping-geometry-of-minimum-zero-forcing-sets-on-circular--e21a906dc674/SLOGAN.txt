For every \(n\ge6\), the token-jumping graph of minimum zero-forcing sets of the circular ladder \(C_n\square K_2\) is connected with exactly \(13n\) vertices and \(48n\) edges. Its degrees are \(12\), \(8\), and \(5\) with respective multiplicities \(n\), \(8n\), and \(4n\), determined by a five-orbit adjacency quotient.
