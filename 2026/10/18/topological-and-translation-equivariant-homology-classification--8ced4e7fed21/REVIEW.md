**Correctness: PASS.** The precise finite-field graph is fixed, its full face vector is verified exhaustively, and explicit elementary free-face reductions yield a two-complex. The main nonstandard step is not an abelian rank shortcut: an exact \(126\)-generator fundamental-group presentation is eliminated by valid noncommutative Tietze substitutions. A simply connected two-complex of Euler characteristic \(65\) is homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{64}S^2\) by Hurewicz and the homological Whitehead theorem. The translation Lefschetz values and Fourier character multiplicities follow from invariant simplices and exact strongly regular parameters. The standalone finite checker was executed from the scientific package with all assertions satisfied.

**Originality: PASS, qualified.** The 2026 Cioabă–Mim theorem determines the *field homology* of all Latin-square-graph clique complexes, including this \(64\)-dimensional second homology. That part of the result is covered, and is not claimed to be original. Inspection of the primary paper's relevant sections shows no van Kampen argument, fundamental-group computation, or homotopy classification. The explicit simple-connectivity certificate and resulting integral wedge decomposition are therefore a non-implied strengthening. Search did not identify an equivalent published Paley-\(25\) homotopy classification; unindexed material remains possible.

**Value: PASS.** The square-order Paley graph on \(25\) vertices is a canonical, symmetric first three-direction affine example. Its known vanishing first homology leaves open the possibility of a nontrivial perfect fundamental group. Resolving that obstruction with an explicit nonabelian presentation calculation converts a general homology-only result into a precise homotopy type and a full natural equivariant top-homology module. This is a motivated structural finite example, not a claim of a new homology formula or a general Latin-square theorem.
