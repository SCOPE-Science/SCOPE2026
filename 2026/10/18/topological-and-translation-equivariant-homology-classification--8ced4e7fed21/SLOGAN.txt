For the \(25\)-vertex Paley graph, the ordinary clique complex is \(\bigvee^{64}S^2\), and its additive-translation action on second homology has character \((64,4,-1)\) on the identity, nonzero square shifts, and nonsquare shifts. The resulting additive-character multiplicities are \(4\) on the trivial character and respectively \(3\) and \(2\) on the two classes of twelve nontrivial characters; the ordinary Betti number was already known.
