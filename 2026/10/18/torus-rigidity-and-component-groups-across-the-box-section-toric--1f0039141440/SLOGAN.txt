The face-fan toric varieties of the sum-zero box sections \(-A\le d_i\le B\) have no Demazure roots for any coprime \(B\le A\le nB\), so their connected automorphism group is always \((\mathbb C^*)^n\). Their full component group is \(\mathfrak S_{n+1}\), doubled by coordinate inversion exactly at \((A,B)=(1,1)\).
