The complete total-domination polynomial of the middle graph \(M(K_{2,n})\), for every \(n\ge3\), is \(z^n(1+z)^n[((2+z)^n-2)(1+z)^2-2n]\). It determines all feasible-set counts, including \(2^n-2n-2\) minimum sets for \(n\ge4\), and yields an exact Bernoulli total-domination threshold with limit \(e^{-\lambda^2}\).
