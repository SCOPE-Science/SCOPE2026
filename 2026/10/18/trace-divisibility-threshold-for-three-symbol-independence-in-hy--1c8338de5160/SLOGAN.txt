For the trace-\(t\) hyperbolic toral automorphism, quantizing one coordinate into \(q\) equal bins yields pairwise independent observations, but three consecutive labels are independent exactly when \(q=2\) or \(q\mid t\). The complete three-label law is explicit, and its conditional information defect at \(q\ge t+1\) equals \(\log(q/t)-(\log 2)/t\); binary labels are in fact independent at every triple of distinct times.
