For every finite abelian group \(A\) of order at least five, the complete translation character on second rational homology of its group-Latin-square clique complex is given by three line directions and a precise involution/intercalate correction. Every subgroup quotient has an explicit rational Betti number; for the full translation quotient this is \(|A|-|A[2]|\), so it is rationally acyclic exactly for elementary abelian \(2\)-groups.
