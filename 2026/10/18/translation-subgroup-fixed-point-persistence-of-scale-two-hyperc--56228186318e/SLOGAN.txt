For the scale-two Vietoris–Rips complex of every binary cube, every nontrivial coordinate-translation subgroup has a fixed space that is either empty, a discrete set of exactly \(2^{n-2}\) points, or the graph \(Q_{n-1}\) with \(1+(n-3)2^{n-2}\) independent loops. The classification determines all translation-subgroup fixed-point persistence through scale two, including the inclusions from scale one.
