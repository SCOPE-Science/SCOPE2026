For all integers \(n\ge2\) and \(1\le r<n\), the reduced rational Lefschetz number of transposition on the bounded-degree chessboard complex \(M_r(K_{n,n})\) is the signed count of labeled simple \(r\)-regular graphs on \(n\) vertices. This uniform identity gives homology lower bounds and explicitly determines the factor-swap orientation on the sphere \(M_{n-1}(K_{n,n})\).
