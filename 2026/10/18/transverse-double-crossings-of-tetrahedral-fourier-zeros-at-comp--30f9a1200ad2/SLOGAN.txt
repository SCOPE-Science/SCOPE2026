At every reciprocal node \(2\pi(m_1,m_2,m_3)\) of a tetrahedral indicator's Fourier transform with \(0,m_1,m_2,m_3\) pairwise distinct, the real zero set has exactly two analytic branches crossing transversely. Their tangent lines are the null directions of an explicit universal saddle quadratic form obtained from four Vandermonde weights.
