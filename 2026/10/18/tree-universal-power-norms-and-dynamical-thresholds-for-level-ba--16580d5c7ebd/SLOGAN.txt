For the canonical level-balanced backward shift on the Cesàro space of *any* locally finite rooted tree, every power has exact norm \(|\gamma|^n[\zeta(p)/\zeta(p,n+1)]^{1/p}\), and precisely the aligned vectors on the matching level attain it. Its universal critical growth \(n^{1-1/p}\) accompanies a sharp transition from norm-decaying powers to mixing without frequent hypercyclicity, then to chaotic frequent hypercyclicity.
