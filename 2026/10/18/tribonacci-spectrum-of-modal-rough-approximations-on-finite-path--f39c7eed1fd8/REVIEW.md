The full 2015 Chiaselotti–Ciucci–Gentile–Infusino path/cycle paper defines its similarity relation as the complement of the irreflexive graph adjacency, and studies preclusive closure, associated open/closed sets and orthogonality graphs. Published mathematical-record searches encountered a Tribonacci dissociation polynomial for fan graphs, but its counted objects and initial coefficients differ and it supplies no implication to the rough-image count. Searches are not a proof of global uniqueness; equivalent finite-state image-language results remain a residual risk.
