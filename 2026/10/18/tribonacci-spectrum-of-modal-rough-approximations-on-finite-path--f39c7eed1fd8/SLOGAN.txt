On the reflexive path frame, the joint image of modal necessity and possibility on all Boolean valuations has a Tribonacci counting law: its generating function is \((1+z+z^3+z^4)/(1-z-z^2-z^3)\). The number of distinct rough-set profiles grows as \(1.108072623\ldots\,(1.839286755\ldots)^n\), strictly slower than the \(2^n\) underlying valuations.
