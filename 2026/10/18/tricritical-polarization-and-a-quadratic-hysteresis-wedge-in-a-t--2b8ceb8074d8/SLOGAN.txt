A three-agent sigmoidal-confidence system with opposing fixed zealots has a unique codimension-two polarization point at \(\gamma=\sqrt3/2\), where the cubic drift disappears, polarization emerges with fourth-root scaling, and critical relaxation follows \(t^{-1/4}\). On the subcritical side, a locally bistable five-equilibrium regime has an exact leading hysteresis width \(10(\gamma-\sqrt3/2)^2/9\) in its linearization parameter.
