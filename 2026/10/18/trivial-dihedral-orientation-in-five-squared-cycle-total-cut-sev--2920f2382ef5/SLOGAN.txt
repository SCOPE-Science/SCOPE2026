For five consecutive squared-cycle total cut complexes \(\Delta_k^t(C_{3k+3}^2)\), \(4\le k\le8\), every dihedral symmetry acts by \(+1\) on their integral seventh-homology generator. Thus each dihedral orbit space retains a rational degree-seven class, an equivariant refinement not determined by the previously known seven-sphere homotopy types.
