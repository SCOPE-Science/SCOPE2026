On the accidental rank-four singular surface of Hikami's five-puncture determinantal quintic, every transverse germ is an \(A_1\) surface node and its integral vanishing-cycle local system is globally constant. The triviality is controlled by the exact square \(\det H=-16(s^2-4)^2(t^2-4)^2\); the quintic is consequently rationally smooth along this open stratum.
