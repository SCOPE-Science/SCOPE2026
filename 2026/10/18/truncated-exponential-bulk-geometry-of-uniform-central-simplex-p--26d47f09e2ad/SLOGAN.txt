For a uniform-volume point in the fixed-ratio central polytope of an \((m-1)\)-simplex, its scaled barycentric coordinates converge empirically to an explicit truncated exponential law. Its squared Euclidean distance from the simplex centroid concentrates at the sharp scale \(m^{-1}\), with limiting coefficient \(1-c(\log c)^2/(c-1)^2\); at edge-trisection ratio \(c=2\) this coefficient is \(1-2(\log 2)^2\).
