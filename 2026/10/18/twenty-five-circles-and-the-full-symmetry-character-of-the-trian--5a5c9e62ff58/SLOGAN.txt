The full strong discrete-Morse matching complex of the unfilled triangular-prism graph is homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{25}S^1\). Under its full \(S_3\times C_2\) graph symmetry, first rational homology is \(2\mathbb Q[S_3\times C_2]\oplus\varepsilon\), with \(\varepsilon\) the inflated sign representation, so its invariant subspace is two-dimensional.
