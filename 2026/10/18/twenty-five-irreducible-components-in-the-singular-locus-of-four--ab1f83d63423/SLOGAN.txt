For every \(M\ge5\), the reduced singular locus of the four-vector complex orthogonality complete intersection has exactly \(25\) irreducible components, all of dimension \(2M-1\). They consist of six two-zero loci, four isotropic-plane loci, twelve mixed anisotropic/isotropic-line loci, and three perpendicular-isotropic-pair loci.
