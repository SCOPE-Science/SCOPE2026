For point–block incidence posets with a new top, pseudocomplemented sections exist exactly at incidence degree at most two, and their unsharp implication is nonsingleton precisely on ordered twin-point pairs with the same two blocks. On degree-at-most-two multigraph incidence posets, each double-edge component contributes exactly two nonsingleton implication entries; uniform labelled examples have a limiting \(\operatorname{Poisson}(1/2)\) number of these components, so globally singleton implication has limiting probability \(e^{-1/2}\).
