# Assessment of modular twisted metacyclic Cartan matrices

## Correctness
PASS. The algebra presentation follows from inflation because the coefficient-unit group has order coprime to the normal cyclic subgroup. The ideal generated by \(x-1\) is two-sided and nilpotent and has separable cyclic quotient, proving it is exactly the Jacobson radical. The Frobenius formula for the roots of \(Y^m-a\) determines each simple factor degree. Left multiplication by \(y\) on successive projective layers multiplies the root by powers of \(r\), yielding a complete cycle of simple types and the exact Cartan entries. The proof includes endpoints \(k=0,p\), fields \(\mathbb F_{p^f}\) for all \(f\ge1\), and nonsplit twists. The included standalone finite-prime-field checker covers twelve cases, with complete basis associativity checks up to dimension twenty.

## Originality
PASS relative to the searched literature and available records. The closest direct 2026 source (Bhowmick–de la Cruz–Martínez-Moro, arXiv:2603.22185v1) computes cocycle classes but treats its Wedderburn decomposition under \(\operatorname{char}F\ne p\). Wallace (1970) already supplies a general twisted-group-ring radical theorem, so merely identifying the radical is *covered background* and not the claimed new contribution. Margolis–Schnabel (2019/2020) supply twisted group-ring isomorphism and central-cover techniques rather than explicit nonsplit modular Cartan data. General cyclic-defect/Brauer-star theory covers related split untwisted structures but does not itself state the finite-field twist-order and Galois-orbit formula. Targeted database searches returned no statement covering the claimed classification. Residual risk: other modular projective-representation sources may contain the exact specialization; absence from search is not a proof of novelty.

## Value
PASS. The classification is a natural missing characteristic regime in an actively studied family of twisted group algebras. It gives all simple types, projective Loewy series, and Cartan matrices for *every* scalar cohomology class over *every* finite field of characteristic \(p\). Twist classes can change the simple count from \(m\) to \(1\) while leaving the Cartan determinant invariant at \(p\); the complete divisor spectrum is attained. The explicit finite-field descent is useful for modular projective representations and ring-invariant comparisons, beyond the known general radical statement.

## Closest literature and limitations
Full text inspected: Bhowmick–de la Cruz–Martínez-Moro (2026), especially Theorem 2.3 and Sections 4–5; Margolis–Schnabel (2019), introduction and central-cover theorem; Wallace (1970), Theorems 2.6 and 3.4. Saunders (2024) was considered at the abstract level only as cyclic-defect context. The proof does not determine arbitrary indecomposable modules or all algebra isomorphism types. Independent verification remains outstanding.
