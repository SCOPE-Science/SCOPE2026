For every modular scalar twist of the faithful Frobenius group \(C_p\rtimes C_m\), the cocycle parameter controls how many simple modules survive over the finite coefficient field: if \(h=m/\operatorname{ord}(a^{(q-1)/m})\), the twisted algebra has \(h\) simple types and Cartan matrix \(I_h+\frac{p-1}{h}\mathbf1_h\mathbf1_h^{\mathsf T}\). All projective covers are uniserial of length \(p\), and every divisor \(h\mid m\) occurs.
