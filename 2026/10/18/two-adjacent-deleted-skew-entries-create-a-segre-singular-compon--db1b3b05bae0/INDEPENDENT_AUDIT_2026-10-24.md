For even n>=6 with only a12=a13=0, the allowed partials of the full Pfaffian are complementary Pfaffians. At rank n-2 the full gradient is the decomposable bivector of ker(A), so allowed partials vanish iff that 2-plane contains e1 and another vector in span(e2,e3), which is equivalent to the displayed linear equations and rank-one minors defining Z_n. At rank <=n-4 all Pfaffian partials vanish, giving the reduced union Y_n U Z_n. Z_n is a prime projective cone over Segre P1xP^{n-4}, with free vertex, codimension 2n-6 and degree n-3. Gr(4,n) kernel incidence and proper projection make Y_n irreducible of ambient codimension 6 for n>=8; at n=6 the Plucker intersection splits into Gr(2,5) degree 5 and a Schubert component degree 9. Separate Z_6 degree 3 gives three six-dimensional components, total degree 17; n>=8 two components have distinct dimensions. The Gauss primitive expansion and Serre R1,S2 establish integral normality, and Schur complements give generic transverse quadratic rank 6 and 2n-6. Independently enumerated all 8,191 nonzero F2 points of the 13-coordinate six-vertex slice, calculating each Pfaffian gradient directly from matchings and matrix rank independently, obtaining zero union discrepancies and 351 singular points (rank2:267, rank4:84). This F2 identity check is not a characteristic-zero or arbitrary-size proof.

## originality

PASS

The full Pfaffian corank singular locus and ladder Pfaffian ideal primeness are classical; the novel claimed invariant is the change caused by two adjacent deleted entries, including exceptional Segre locus and sharp n=6 rank-locus split. De Negri–Gorla treats ideals generated by Pfaffians supported in ladders, not the different operation of specializing two entries of a single full Pfaffian; neither that theorem nor general alternating-rank identities states these component dimensions and degrees.
