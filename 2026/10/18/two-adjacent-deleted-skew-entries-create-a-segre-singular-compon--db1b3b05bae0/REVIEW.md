# Review of the adjacent-deletion Pfaffian singular-component theorem

**Correctness — PASS.** For a rank-corank-two alternating matrix the full Pfaffian gradient represents the decomposable kernel bivector. Requiring every *allowed* derivative to vanish restricts that bivector to the two missing coordinates; it yields the exact two-row proportionality ideal defining the exceptional component. The independent incidence argument proves rank-locus irreducibility for matrix size at least eight, while a Plücker case distinction proves its two-component splitting in size six. Component dimensions, the Segre degree and the two Schubert degrees are consistent with their integral ideal descriptions. At generic points, Schur-complement calculations identify nondegenerate transverse quadrics. `verify.py` independently checks the entire six-vertex binary projective coordinate space and several exact Hessian ranks, but does not replace the all-size proof. The theorem is restricted to characteristic zero.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** De Negri–Gorla's ladder Pfaffian theory treats prime/Cohen–Macaulay ladder ideals and liaison, not Jacobian singular components of the full Pfaffian specialized by two adjacent zeros. Kodiyalam–Lam–Swan provide general Pfaffian identities and alternating-rank algebra, but not the geometric decomposition. The existing missing-edge singular classification with pairwise disjoint forbidden edges does not imply the nonmatching adjacent-edge case: its index patterns forbid a shared endpoint and do not supply the exceptional rank-one two-row condition or the exceptional six-vertex rank-locus split. Classical Schubert varieties already explain the six-vertex *individual* rank-stratum degrees, which are not independently claimed. An equivalent conclusion derivable from unindexed sparse-Pfaffian or general degeneracy theory remains possible. Targeted literature and mathematical-record queries returned no directly covering result, but failure to find one does not prove uniqueness.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The full reduced singular-support classification treats the smallest obstruction to extending disjoint-zero Pfaffian geometry to a nonmatching zero pattern. It proves a structural change at the six-vertex boundary: three equal-dimensional components of degrees 5, 9 and 3 become two unequal-dimensional components in larger even sizes. The exceptional component has a natural Segre-cone model and a uniform degree and transverse quadratic-rank formula. These are geometric invariants relevant to singular strata, resolutions and intersection theory; the result is not a numerical instance or a mere restatement of the rank criterion.

**Closest literature and limits.** The comparison included De Negri–Gorla (arXiv:0809.3417v1), Kodiyalam–Lam–Swan's Pfaffian-ideal/alternating-rank paper, conventional Grassmann/Schubert geometry, and the directly adjacent disjoint-edge classification. Only reduced singular support and generic transverse types are classified: nongeneric intersection germs and the nonreduced Jacobian scheme are not resolved. No claim of independent mathematical validation is made.
