For the Pfaffian hypersurface of a generic alternating \(n\)-by-\(n\) matrix with the adjacent entries \(a_{12}=a_{13}=0\), the reduced singular locus gains a degree-\(n-3\) Segre-cone component absent from ordinary corank stratification. At \(n=6\) its rank-deficient component further splits into two Schubert pieces, giving three six-dimensional singular components of degrees \(5,9,3\), whereas every even \(n\ge8\) has two components of unequal dimensions.
