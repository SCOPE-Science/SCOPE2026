# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The result is established by exact simplicial enumeration and integer lattice certificates, not numerical fitting. The \(104\) carriers are all induced tetrahedral boundaries, all are nontrivial modulo two after comparison with every ambient three-boundary, and the complete affine orbit partition is checked. Mod-two rank bounds combined with boundary-composition upper bounds prove the characteristic-zero ranks. A unimodular minor in the edge-cycle lattice and separate unimodular minors for both size-\(39\) orbit generator sets prove integral freeness and integral generation. The self-contained verifier reconstructs all columns, proves the rank facts, and computes the determinants without invoking numerical solvers.

## Originality
PASS within the searched public literature; residual priority uncertainty remains. Two highly relevant earlier computational papers on exactly the same complex establish the ambient wedge-of-\(25\)-spheres homotopy type, which is explicitly not presented as a new result. Their complete inspected claims and proofs do not give the \(104\) minimum induced carriers, the \(26/39/39\) orbit decomposition, or either orbitwise unimodular generator certificate. Matsushita's general fundamental-group theorem and Shukla's low-degree and four-regular circulant results do not imply them. The consecutive-interval circular-arc nerve theory has a different facet family. Equivalent independently derived finite computations may be unindexed.

## Mathematical value
PASS. Minimal homology support and generation are natural local-to-global invariants distinct from ambient Betti numbers. This computation identifies all shortest induced representatives, distinguishes three symmetry orbits, and proves the surprisingly strong fact that each of two distinct orbits independently provides an *integral* generating set for twenty-five global homology directions. The rank-deficient remaining orbit shows this is not an automatic consequence of transitivity or of the existing wedge decomposition. The theorem is restricted to the standard canonical thirteen-vertex graph.
