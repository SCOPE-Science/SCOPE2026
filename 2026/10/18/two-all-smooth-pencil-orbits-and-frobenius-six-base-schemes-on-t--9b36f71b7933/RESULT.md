# Two all-smooth pencil orbits and Frobenius-six base schemes on the split degree-six del Pezzo surface over \(\mathbf F_2\)

## Finding
Let \(S\) be the blow-up of \(\mathbf P^2_{\mathbf F_2}\) at its three coordinate points. Among the \(127\) nonzero anticanonical sections, precisely \(23\) define geometrically smooth genus-one curves. Exactly \(12\) rational anticanonical pencils have **all three** rational members geometrically smooth. Under the full rational automorphism group \(\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathbf F_2}(S)\cong D_{12}\) the pencils comprise exactly two orbits of size \(6\), each with stabilizer of size \(2\). Their three-member rational-point multisets are, respectively, \((3,5,5)\) and \((4,4,5)\).

For **every** such pencil the complete scheme-theoretic base locus on \(S\) is geometrically reduced of length \(6\), with its six geometric points forming **one Frobenius orbit of degree six**. Consequently none of these pencils has a rational base point. There is no anticanonical projective linear system of dimension at least two over \(\mathbf F_2\) whose every rational member is geometrically smooth; the projective dimension \(1\) attained by these pencils is sharp.

## Assumptions and scope
The ground field is exactly \(\mathbf F_2\); smoothness always means geometric smoothness. The blow-up centers are \(P_1=[1:0:0]\), \(P_2=[0:1:0]\), \(P_3=[0:0:1]\). With \(H\) the pullback of a line and \(E_i\) the exceptional divisors, \(-K_S=3H-E_1-E_2-E_3\), \((-K_S)^2=6\), and the anticanonical section space has the ordered basis
\[
(M_0,\ldots,M_6)=(X^2Y,X^2Z,XY^2,XYZ,XZ^2,Y^2Z,YZ^2).
\]
The binary mask \(m\in\{1,\ldots,127\}\) denotes \(F_m=\sum_{j=0}^6 ((m\mathbin{\mathrm{div}}2^j)\bmod2)M_j\); the mask convention is only for the reproducibility code and is not a geometric restriction. A pencil is a two-dimensional \(\mathbf F_2\)-subspace of this seven-dimensional space, with three nonzero forms \(F,G,F+G\). The rational-point count of a smooth member means its number of \(\mathbf F_2\)-points on \(S\), equivalently on its smooth plane-cubic model.

## Proof
**1. Complete smooth-section test.** For a homogeneous cubic \(F\) in characteristic \(2\), Euler's identity gives \(F=XF_X+YF_Y+ZF_Z\), so a singular point is exactly a common nonzero zero of its three quadratic partial derivatives. In \(\mathbf F_2[X,Y,Z]\), these three quadrics have no projective common zero over the algebraic closure if and only if they form a homogeneous regular sequence. Their quotient then has Hilbert series \((1+t)^3\); equivalently, multiplication by the three partials from the three copies of degree-two forms onto all degree-four forms is surjective. The codomain has dimension \(15\). The explicit binary rank calculation gives precisely \(23\) masks:
\[
49,51,53,55,59,61,62,70,71,79,86,87,93,94,102,103,107,110,113,115,117,118,121.
\]
Independently, exact affine-chart Jacobian Gröbner tests give precisely the same list. All these cubics are smooth at the three blown-up centers, and their strict transforms are smooth genus-one anticanonical curves.

**2. Pencil and automorphism classification.** A triple \(\{u,v,u\oplus v\}\) represents an all-smooth pencil exactly when all its masks lie in the above list (\(\oplus\) denotes coefficientwise addition). Enumerating unordered pairs and normalizing the resulting triples gives precisely \(12\) pencils; their complete list appears in `artifacts/expected.json`. The rational automorphism group of the split degree-six del Pezzo surface has order \(12\): the torus of the toric surface has only its identity as an \(\mathbf F_2\)-point, and its hexagonal fan symmetries are generated by permutations of the coordinates and the quadratic Cremona involution. On the mask basis the latter sends \(M_j\) to \(M_{6-j}\). The displayed group action preserves all \(12\) pencils and splits them into exactly two six-element orbits. Representatives are \(\{49,71,118\}\) and \(\{51,93,110\}\); each stabilizer has order \(2\).

The representative generators in homogeneous coordinates are
\[
\begin{aligned}
F_{49}&=X^2Y+XZ^2+Y^2Z,\\
F_{71}&=X^2Y+X^2Z+XY^2+YZ^2,\\
F_{51}&=X^2Y+X^2Z+XZ^2+Y^2Z,\\
F_{93}&=X^2Y+XY^2+XYZ+XZ^2+YZ^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
Counting the seven rational points of \(\mathbf P^2(\mathbf F_2)\) on each representative and noting that blowing up a rational smooth point replaces it by one rational tangent point gives the multisets \((3,5,5)\) and \((4,4,5)\). Thus the two pencil orbits are arithmetically distinct.

**3. Complete geometric base schemes.** On the chart \(Z=1\), the first representative's two generator equations have Gröbner elimination relations
\[
x+y^6+y^4+y^2=0,\qquad y(y^6+y^5+y^4+y+1)=0.
\]
The second representative analogously has
\[
x+y^5+y^3+y^2+y=0,\qquad y(y^6+y+1)=0.
\]
These relations follow by exact polynomial reduction modulo \(2\) and are separately reproduced by resultant elimination. The factor \(y=0\) corresponds to a blown-up coordinate center, not a point of the proper base on this affine complement. Set
\[
g_A(T)=T^6+T^5+T^4+T+1,\qquad g_B(T)=T^6+T+1.
\]
Both are irreducible over \(\mathbf F_2\), as certified by \(T^{64}\equiv T\pmod g\) and \(\gcd(g,T^4-T)=\gcd(g,T^8-T)=1\). For a root \(t\) of \(g_A\), \((X,Y,Z)=(t^6+t^4+t^2,t,1)\) is a common zero of \(F_{49},F_{71}\); for a root of \(g_B\), \((X,Y,Z)=(t^5+t^3+t^2+t,t,1)\) is a common zero of \(F_{51},F_{93}\). Direct substitution modulo each irreducible sextic verifies these equations. Neither coordinate is zero modulo the sextic. In each case, all six distinct conjugate geometric points lie off the exceptional divisors. As the intersection product of two distinct anticanonical curves equals \((-K_S)^2=6\), these points exhaust the scheme-theoretic base locus and each has local intersection multiplicity one. Transport by the full automorphism group proves this property for all \(12\) pencils.

**4. Maximality.** The surface has rational points. If a three-dimensional vector subspace \(W\) of anticanonical sections had every nonzero \(\mathbf F_2\)-section geometrically smooth, evaluation at any rational point \(p\in S(\mathbf F_2)\) would have a kernel of dimension at least two. That kernel would define an all-smooth rational pencil with \(p\) as rational base point, contradicting step 3. Every higher-dimensional subspace contains a three-dimensional one. Thus no all-smooth anticanonical net or larger rational system exists.

## Verification
`artifacts/verify.py` (Python 3 standard library) exhausts the \(127\) sections, computes the \(15\)-dimensional regular-sequence rank test, separately checks all singular geometric cubic points in finite extensions of degrees at most three, enumerates all \(12\) pencils, constructs all \(12\) automorphisms, and verifies both irreducible sextic base certificates. It also verifies directly the absence of rational common base points, including on exceptional curves. `artifacts/verify_symbolic.py` (SymPy) independently runs projective-chart Jacobian Gröbner tests on all \(127\) sections and exact Gröbner and resultant elimination on representatives. Both scripts were executed against the embedded reference JSON outputs; the results match byte-for-byte. The finite enumeration is exhaustive because each cubic is uniquely one of the \(127\) binary masks, and each pencil is uniquely one normalized triple.

## Relationship to prior work
Asgarli–Ghioca (2022) studies pencils whose rational members are smooth, providing the finite-field Bertini motivation but not this anticanonical pencil classification. Asgarli–Ghioca–Reichstein (2022) constructs smooth rational linear families of unrestricted plane hypersurfaces; those examples do not impose the three blow-up-center base conditions. Blache and collaborators (2019) describe degree-six del Pezzo anticanonical constructions and the split automorphism group, but do not calculate the present two pencil orbits or their sextic Frobenius base schemes. The general plane-cubic pencil census supplies broader ambient counts and orbit data; this result does not claim those counts as new. Its further degree-six residual Frobenius and scheme-theoretic classification is a different invariant not entailed by the known coarse rational-base orbits. The comparison with all-smooth pencil obstructions on a split degree-five del Pezzo surface also makes this degree-six existence boundary geometrically meaningful.

## Limitations
Only the split degree-six surface over \(\mathbf F_2\) and **rational members** of its anticanonical systems are classified; no assertion is made that every geometric member of any pencil is smooth. The finite computations have independently checked pathways but are not a formal proof-assistant certification. A previous but unindexed classification of geometric base schemes could affect historical priority. The classification is not extended to other ground fields or other forms of degree-six del Pezzo surfaces. No independent external audit has been conducted.
