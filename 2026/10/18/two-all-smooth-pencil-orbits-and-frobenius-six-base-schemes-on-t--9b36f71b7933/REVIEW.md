# Scientific review

**Correctness: PASS.** The seven-dimensional anticanonical space and its 127 nonzero sections are explicitly specified. Exact surjectivity of the degree-four Jacobian multiplication map establishes geometric smoothness for precisely 23 forms; separately, affine-chart Gröbner ideals reproduce these forms. The 12 smooth pencils are obtained from every admissible pair. The full rational automorphism group has 12 elements, and the computed action yields two six-element orbits. In each orbit, a representative pencil has six conjugate geometric base points certified by explicit irreducible sextics and substitutions; the anticanonical self-intersection 6 proves there is no hidden point or multiplicity. Evaluation at a rational point proves the nonexistence of an all-smooth net. The precise certificates and reproduction outputs are embedded.

**Originality: PASS, with residual risk.** Blache et al. (arXiv:1903.09397), especially its description of the split degree-six surface and automorphisms, does not give the present all-smooth anticanonical pencil and geometric base schemes. Asgarli–Ghioca (arXiv:2203.07169) supplies the all-smooth-pencil motivation but not this small-field surface classification. Asgarli–Ghioca–Reichstein (arXiv:2207.00918) constructs unrestricted smooth plane-cubic nets, which do not obey the imposed three blow-up-center conditions. A broader finite-field plane-cubic pencil census does give ambient counts and rational-base orbit invariants; those facts are not claimed here as new. The claimed advance is the **intrinsic two-orbit anticanonical classification with two specific genus-one point spectra and, above all, the complete reduced degree-six Frobenius base scheme** and sharp maximality for this surface. Prior unpublished or unindexed complete base-scheme tables remain a priority risk.

**Value: PASS.** The scheme-theoretic fields of definition of anticanonical pencil base points and the simultaneous smoothness of every rational member are natural arithmetic-geometric invariants; in particular, geometric intersection degree six is not determined by a coarse number of plane rational base points. Two arithmetically different pencils nevertheless have the same irreducible Frobenius-six base pattern. The existence of all-smooth pencils here contrasts naturally with the obstruction on the split degree-five del Pezzo surface. This is a complete small-field Bertini and intersection classification, not merely a few more finite instances of a table.

**Limits.** The results concern only this split surface over \(\mathbf F_2\); other fields and nonsplit twists have not been classified. The verification is repeatable exact computation plus mathematical reduction, not independent outside certification. A hidden earlier complete computation remains possible.
