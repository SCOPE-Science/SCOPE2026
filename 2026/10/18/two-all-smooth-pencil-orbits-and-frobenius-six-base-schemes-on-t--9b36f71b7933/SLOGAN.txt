On the split degree-six del Pezzo surface over \(\mathbf F_2\), exactly 12 anticanonical pencils have all three rational members smooth, forming two six-element automorphism orbits with rational-point spectra \((3,5,5)\) and \((4,4,5)\). Every such pencil has six reduced geometric base points forming one Frobenius orbit, and no all-smooth anticanonical net exists.
