For every generically projected standard cross-polytope, the normalized monotone-path polytope has a unique origin-centered minimum enclosing sphere of radius \(\frac12\sqrt{1+(a_{n-1}/a_n)^2}\). Exactly two antipodal coherent one-intermediate paths touch it; every longer coherent path has a strict explicit clearance, so the Euclidean outer geometry depends only on the two largest projection heights.
