For a uniformly random planar silhouette of a frustum with two directly homothetic convex \(n\)-gon bases, the number of vertices is almost surely either \(n\) or \(n+2\), with the \(n\)-vertex probability exactly equal to a normalized solid angle. That one probability strictly identifies the effective aspect \(h/(1-t)\) for a known base, and has an elementary arctangent formula for regular polygon bases.
