# Review: all-smooth plane-filling quintic pencils over \(\mathbf F_3\)

## Correctness — PASS
The theorem has an explicitly specified finite parameter space and counts **distinct two-spaces**, not generator pairs. Direct enumeration finds \(2016\) qualifying pencils; a structurally different RREF code scans all \(896260\) two-spaces and reproduces the total. Two PGL orbits of individual quintics are independently shown geometrically smooth using full algebraic-closure Jacobian ideals on three charts. The full projective group and its generators are verified, so the two pencil orbits really exhaust all \(2016\) pencils. The base-scheme descriptions follow from actual chartwise Gröbner bases, irreducibility certificates, transverse rational contacts and Bézout length \(25\). Higher-dimensional exclusion follows directly from the two-dimensional cotangent space at a rational base point. No statement about an untested extension field is used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with source-access risks
The closest papers are Homma–Kim (arXiv:0903.1918), Duran Cunha (arXiv:1805.07345) and Homma (arXiv:1906.00164) on individual Tallini curves, and Asgarli–Ghioca (arXiv:2203.07169) on smooth rational pencil members. The new simultaneous-pencil count and its reduced/nonreduced base-scheme orbit dichotomy do not follow from their inspected results. The two individual smooth-curve orbits and standard matrix parametrization are background, not claimed as new. Published research-record and web query comparisons are recorded in AUDIT.json. The full text of the original 2009 and 2019 classification papers was not available in this run, and earlier unindexed computations remain possible. Lack of a search match is not used as proof of priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The chosen invariant is the complete finite geometry of the all-smooth pencil locus inside the minimal plane-filling linear system. Its sharply constrained \(13\) rational base points and two fundamentally different residual schemes give information about incidence, tangency and Frobenius not present in counts or automorphisms of individual curves. The projective linear dimension bound supplies a natural maximality result. No generalization to arbitrary fields is claimed.
