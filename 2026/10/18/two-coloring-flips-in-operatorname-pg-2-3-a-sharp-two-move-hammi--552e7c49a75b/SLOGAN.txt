The single-point flip graph of proper labeled two-colorings of the thirteen-point projective plane has a sharp Hamming detour of at most two moves: \(d(\sigma,\tau)\in\{h(\sigma,\tau),h(\sigma,\tau)+2\}\). Exactly \(112\) destinations per coloring require the additional two moves, giving \(26{,}208\) exceptional unordered pairs among \(468\) proper colorings.
