Resetting only one coordinate of a harmonic Brownian gyrator gives two inequivalent signed-area transport rates: continuous-path rotation reverses only when \(T_y<T_x\), whereas the full path including reset chords reverses only when \(T_x<T_y<2T_x\). At equal bath temperatures the rates have opposite signs for every positive resetting rate, and in the rapid-reset limit their leading contributions cancel exactly.
