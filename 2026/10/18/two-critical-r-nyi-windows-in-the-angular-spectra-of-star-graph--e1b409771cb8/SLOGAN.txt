The Gaussian normal-cone law of the star graphical zonotope has a Rényi critical window of width \(1/n\), while adding just the connected three-vertex simplices changes the entire refined star fan to a narrower window of width \(1/(n\log n)\). Exact factorial angular spectra yield both crossover functions and show that the independent-direction vertex-collision probability is asymptotically halved by this first refinement.
