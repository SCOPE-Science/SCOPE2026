# Scientific review

## Correctness: PASS
For every nondegenerate parallelogram the Crofton conditional direction density is the geometric width divided by \(2(a+b)\). Direct integration of \(|\sin\theta|e^{2ik\theta}\) yields the explicit Fourier coefficients, and independent pair sampling squares their moduli. The algebraic inequalities \(0<q\le s\le1\) give a necessary region, while the inverse construction \(s=1-(1-B)/(4(1-A))\), \(\eta=(1-A)/s\) proves sufficiency and uniqueness including square, rhombus and rectangle boundaries. The two shapes with \(T_1=1/81\) certify one-harmonic failure. The packaged checker confirms 20 quadratures, 1,500 inverse reconstructions and six sampling experiments; it is corroboration rather than proof.

## Originality: PASS relative to inspected evidence
Primary published sources on body-indicator covariograms [Bianchi–Gardner–Kiderlen 2010], direction-unlabeled projection-length distributions [Kim–Yaskin–Zvavitch 2015], and a different two-chord angle model [Barahona Torres et al. 2019] use mathematically different data. Three publicly available nearby research results concerning parallelogram width statistics, tangential polygon width laws, and fourth chord-length moments were inspected in full; none supplies an angular-correlation inverse theorem for *independently Crofton-hit* lines or the exact feasible parabola. The two-point Fourier magnitude inversion is classical in spirit and may exist under alternative terminology; this remains the main historical-priority risk.

## Mathematical value: PASS
The result gives a complete, explicit, and minimally two-observable inverse classification for a natural stereological sampling model. The exact feasible-data region supplies a realizability test, with geometric meaning to each boundary and a constructive recovery formula. A single harmonic fails even among natural rectangle/rhombus examples; this is not just a forward computation or a special tabulated probability. Potential use is shape inference from unlabeled line-orientation pairs without chord lengths or absolute direction calibration.

## Limits
Exact population expectations, an invariant-line sampling convention and a nondegenerate parallelogram prior are essential. The theorem does not assert stability under noise, reconstruct nonparallelogram convex bodies, or establish independent verification or exhaustive historical novelty.
