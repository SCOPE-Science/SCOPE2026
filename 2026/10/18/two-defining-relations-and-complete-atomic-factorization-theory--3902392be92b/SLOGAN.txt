The multiplicative monoid of Jordan–Pólya numbers with no prime factor exceeding \(13\) has eight atoms but only two essential relations, \((14!)(5!)=(15!)(2!)^3\) and \((7!)(13!)=(15!)(2!)^2(3!)\). This complete-intersection presentation forces every atomic length set to be an unbroken progression of step \(2\), and its exact catenary degree is \(4\).
