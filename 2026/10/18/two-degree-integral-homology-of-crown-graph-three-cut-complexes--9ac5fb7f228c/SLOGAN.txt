For the crown graph \(K_{n,n}\) with a perfect matching deleted, the ordinary three-cut complex has torsion-free integral reduced homology in exactly two degrees: ranks \(B_n=(n^4-10n^3+31n^2-30n+4)/4\) in degree \(2n-6\) and \(n\) in degree \(2n-4\), for every \(n\ge5\). This supplies an infinite natural family of pure, nonshellable cut complexes, via an Alexander dual that is a two-row chessboard complex with \(n\) additional matched-pair edges.
