In symmetric Dirichlet random formal contexts, the first object count making every attribute subset a formal intent has an infinite mean whenever the total precision does not exceed \(m+1\). Under the simplex-uniform precision \(m+1\), the time on scale \(m^2\log m\) has an inverse-exponential limit, whereas at fixed positive total precision its logarithm divided by \(m\) has an exponential limit.
