Higashitani's three-dimensional very ample nonnormal polytopes have arbitrarily many degree-two semigroup holes, but for every parameter \(h\geq2\) their associated normal projective toric threefold has exactly two disjoint singular projective lines. Both lines have generic transverse equation \(UV=W^4\); the only non-\(\mathbb Q\)-factorial points are their four torus-fixed endpoints.
