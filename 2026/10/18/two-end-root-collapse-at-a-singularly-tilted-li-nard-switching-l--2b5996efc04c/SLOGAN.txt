An obliquely switched polynomial Liénard oscillator has the exact first-order average \(M_s(r)=rF(r^2)+sG(s^2r^2)\). When the coefficients are fixed and the switching angle tends to zero, its simple positive roots consist of the inherited smooth roots, at most one root of radius proportional to \(s\), and at most one escaping root with the explicit exponent \((2q+1)/[2(q-p)-1]\).
