For the finite constant-rate pure-birth chain absorbed at its last state, the sharp time-weighted \(\ell^\infty\) derivative norm is \(\sqrt{2n/\pi}(1+o(1))\), uniquely attained at time \(n\), whereas the sharp \(\ell^1\) derivative norm is \(n-\sqrt{n\log n}(1+o(1))\), uniquely attained eventually at a time shifted left by \(\sqrt{n\log n}\). Both exact time-dependent norms are elementary stopped-Poisson expressions, but their optimized orders diverge even though every generator has spectrum \(\{-1,0\}\).
