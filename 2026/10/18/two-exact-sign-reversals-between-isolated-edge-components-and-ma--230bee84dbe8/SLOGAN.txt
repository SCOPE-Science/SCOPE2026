In \(G(n,p)\), the covariance between the number of isolated two-vertex components and the number of triangle-free edges changes sign exactly twice, positive–negative–positive, for every \(n\ge3\); the sharp exception is \(n=2\), where the covariance stays positive. The two thresholds occur at distinct probability scales, with \(np_{n,-}\to1/2\) and \(\sqrt n\,p_{n,+}\to\sqrt2\).
