# Scientific review

**Claim:** Two transposed exponentially \(\psi\)-mixing copula-generating Markov kernels have a reversible, uniformly geometrically ergodic equal-weight average for which every positive-lag \(\psi\)-coefficient is infinite.

## Correctness (C): PASS
Lebesgue invariance and exact time reversal follow from mass transport between three intervals; each interval has measure \(1/3\). At lag two the starting within-cell coordinate is erased by an unconditional uniform draw, so the explicit three-state powers yield the exact full-state density at all later lags. The rank-one recurrence proves \(\psi_n=2^{2-n}\) for all \(n\ge2\), without inferring an infinite statement from finite checks. The alternating all-deterministic word in the mixture has nonzero weight \(2^{-n}\) at every lag, producing a joint/product ratio diverging as the source interval shrinks. The same mixture contains the all-forward word with sufficient uniformly positive density at lag three, giving exact Doeblin constant \(3/32\). The proof checks every state, integer lag and positive-measure event specified in the claim; it distinguishes \(\psi\)-mixing from total-variation mixing. Exact-rational code checks finite matrix identities and branch weights only.

## Originality (O): PASS on inspected evidence
The closest paper, Longla–Mous-Abou–Ngongo [1], states the unrestricted convex-closure question explicitly in its 2022 Section 5. Its Theorem 2.2.2 proves convex closure only with the extra one-step condition \(\psi_1<1\), inapplicable to the two component singular copulas here. Its negative example has a non-\(\psi\)-mixing constituent, while both of the present constituents are exponentially \(\psi\)-mixing. The 2024 paper [2] proves positive closure under bounded-density hypotheses unavailable to this reversible mixture. Earlier lower-\(\psi\) statements [3] concern a weaker coefficient. The 2021 full-text [4] makes an unrestricted closure assertion in Theorem 2.3.3, but its proof assumes that eventual \(\psi\)-mixing implies a finite coefficient at lag one; the 2022 reference [1] explicitly withdraws that general proof. The present construction supplies a counterexample rather than relying on that assertion. Published-record semantic searches and accessible previous claims identified related copula/mixing topics but no equivalent statement or stronger covering theorem. This supports novelty relative to inspected evidence, not a proof of global priority.

## Value (V): PASS
This is a precise negative resolution of a clearly formulated published research question about preservation of a strong mixing property under convex combinations. The example is structural rather than merely computational: each constituent has an exact exponential coefficient profile, their midpoint is a natural time-reversal symmetrization, and it remains uniformly geometrically ergodic despite failing \(\psi\)-mixing at every lag. The example isolates a persistent low-mass singular-branch mechanism that separates total-variation ergodicity from rare-event-normalized dependence.
