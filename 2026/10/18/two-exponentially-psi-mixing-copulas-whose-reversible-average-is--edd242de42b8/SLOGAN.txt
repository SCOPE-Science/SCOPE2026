There exist two transposed copulas, each generating an exponentially \(\psi\)-mixing stationary Markov chain, whose equal-weight average generates a reversible, uniformly geometrically ergodic chain with \(\psi_n=\infty\) at every positive lag. The obstruction is an arbitrarily small but persistent alternating deterministic branch, showing that rapid total-variation mixing does not ensure convex closure of \(\psi\)-mixing.
