# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The anticanonical linear system is the six-dimensional space of plane cubics through the four frame points. Groebner/Jacobian tests for all 364 projective sections give 132 geometrically smooth members. Independent finite-extension singular tests through degree three give the same exact list. Full span enumeration identifies exactly 240 all-smooth projective pencils and no all-smooth net. The explicitly generated full order-120 automorphism group acts in two free 120-element orbits; two representative residual intersection schemes have irreducible degree-five eliminants and transverse fixed base points. The complete proof and independently executable finite certificates are in RESULT.md, src/, and data/.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
The closest relevant publication is Asgarli–Ghioca, *Smoothness in Pencils of Hypersurfaces over Finite Fields* (arXiv:2203.07169v1, 2022-03-14), whose complete preprint treats existence and hypersurface examples, not this exact classification of anticanonical pencils on a quintic del Pezzo surface over F3. Their prior Bertini-type hyperplane-section article (arXiv:2004.03479) addresses a different question. Candelas et al. (arXiv:1206.4961) give the quintic del Pezzo's symmetry and genus-six construction but no finite-field genus-one pencil census. Actual Published record and earlier mathematical claim searches found no dominating statement; a prior unindexed equivalent classification remains a residual risk. Novelty is not certified independently.

## Value — PASS
This is a natural complete small-field classification of the finite-field Bertini phenomenon on a classical del Pezzo surface, not an artificial coefficient slice. The exact two free symmetry orbits, invariant fiber-point spectra, globally Frobenius-transitive degree-five residual base schemes, and obstruction to all-smooth nets distinguish arithmetic-geometric structures beyond a mere list of equations. A result over F2 for the same surface admits no analogous pencils and does not imply this classification.

## Limitations
Only four rational members per pencil are required smooth, not every member over field extensions. Results concern a fixed split surface over F3 rather than all degree-five del Pezzo forms or all finite fields. Program replay is not independent expert validation. An unindexed prior enumeration could affect priority.
