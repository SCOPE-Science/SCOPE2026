Over \(\overline{\mathbf F}_7\), exactly two geometric isomorphism classes of smooth genus-three Picard curves have Jacobian \(7\)-rank zero: \(y^3=x^4+x\) and \(y^3=x^4+x+\eta\), where \(\eta^3=2\). Both have \(a\)-number \(2\), and the second lies beyond the previously studied classical CM model.
