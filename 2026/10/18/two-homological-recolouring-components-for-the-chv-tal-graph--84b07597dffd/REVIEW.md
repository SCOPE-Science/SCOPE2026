# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The claim is restricted to the explicitly labeled twelve-vertex Chvátal graph, four **named** palette colors and exactly one-vertex moves. The twenty-term integral 2-chain is checked face-by-face and edge-boundary-by-edge-boundary; for each coloring its image is checked against all four coefficients of the oriented boundary of a tetrahedron. Contiguity of the two neighborhood-complex coloring maps after one move proves equality of induced homology degrees without assuming a finite experiment proves an infinite statement. Direct substitution gives opposite degrees for an initial coloring and a transposition. Complete backtracking (every possible color at each of twelve vertices) gives 18024 states, and BFS from the two opposite-degree seeds visits disjoint 9012-state classes whose union is the entire state space. A different independent-set-partition enumeration and union-find connectivity checker confirms 751 partitions, 18024 labeled states, 64320 edges and two 9012-state components. Both actual included scripts were run successfully. The tests establish the exact finite claim; source code and the explicit 2-cycle, not logs alone, constitute the proof.

## Originality — PASS, with bounded uncertainty

The original Chvátal construction proves graph order, triangle-freeness and four-chromaticity but not coloring reconfiguration. Cereceda–van den Heuvel–Johnson (2005) introduces precisely the one-site coloring graph and general connectedness criteria but the complete inspected report has no Chvátal-specific table. The 2015 Bonamy–Bousquet–Feghali–Johnson theorem already guarantees **Kempe** connectivity here, which is a different, coarser operation; we explicitly exclude it. Published same-graph topology computations give the source complex's sphere homotopy type and orientation character, but no maps generated by all proper four-colorings or exact two-component recoloring classification. Both semantic mathematical-record search and targeted general literature searches were carried out; negative search alone is not used as a proof of novelty. A 2022 related thesis was located but inaccessible as full text and possible unindexed duplication remains.

## Value — PASS

A natural and classical extremal four-chromatic graph supplies an exact benchmark for zero-temperature coloring Glauber connectivity at the **minimum** palette, where the question is structurally delicate. This result does more than count finite objects: it gives a verifiable topological *reason* single-site moves cannot cross between two exact globally connected regions, and distinguishes this failure of local reconfiguration from universally available Kempe equivalence on connected noncomplete four-regular graphs. The use of a concrete signed two-cycle makes the obstruction independently useful for other graph-homomorphism reconfiguration tests; no extrapolation to a family is asserted.
