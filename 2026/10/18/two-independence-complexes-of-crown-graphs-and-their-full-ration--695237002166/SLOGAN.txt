The two-independence complex of every crown graph \(K_{n,n}\) with a perfect matching deleted, for \(n\geq3\), is a wedge of exactly \(\binom{n-1}{2}\) two-spheres. Its rational second-homology action of \(S_n\times C_2\) is the exterior square of the standard permutation quotient, twisted by the sign of exchanging the two parts.
