# Scientific review

**Correctness (C): PASS.** Direct partial-fraction summation supplies a normalized probability-generating function and positive compound-Poisson jump intensities. A self-contained integral proof establishes that Bernoulli-thinning self-decomposability is equivalent to nonnegativity of the full defect sequence; strict convexity reduces that infinite family to the two neighboring integers around the unique real minimizer. The parameterized limiting construction rigorously proves arbitrarily late failures. Exact-rational verification reproduces \(375\) cases and \(4\) delayed families, but the universal claim uses the written proof, not the experiments. No disputed boundary \(q=t\), \(r=t\), or \(w\in\{0,1\}\) is included in the formula's stated domain.

**Originality (O): PASS on investigated evidence.** Pakes (2022) already establishes compound-Poisson divisibility of geometric mixtures and the *general* monotone-canonical-sequence characterization, neither of which is claimed as new. Bouzar (2021) concerns quasi-geometric, rather than ordinary thinning, infinite divisibility. Nastić et al. (2016) consider dependent counting in a model with two-geometric innovations, not a two-index classification of the ordinary independent-thinning property. A published 2025 article by Waza and Ahmad has a strongly related title but only its publisher abstract was accessible, and its abstract highlights distribution construction, estimation, and reliability properties without addressing the present classification. The exact two-index reduction, contiguous-failure block, and arbitrarily delayed violations were not found in the inspected literature or targeted mathematical records. The possibility of an unindexed earlier equivalent result remains.

**Value (V): PASS.** The theorem turns an infinite collection of positivity constraints into a complete constant-number-of-tests decision rule across the natural three-parameter family. Proving that failures can occur arbitrarily late shows why arbitrary finite truncations are mathematically unsafe and makes the derived two-index rule substantive rather than cosmetic. These properties matter for determining when a positive geometric-mixture counting law admits thinning-stationary innovations; no wider model-class theorem is asserted.

The 1979 original primary research paper was located by DOI and metadata, but the full text was inaccessible through the inspected link. The 2025 Waza–Ahmad paper was abstract-only; comparison with its complete text remains a limitation. Neither source is represented as fully read. The general 2022 geometric-mixture and self-decomposability treatment was read in substantive sections and explicitly credited for its stronger known ingredients.
