For both substitution towers \(h^k(0)\) and \(h^k(1)\) of the ternary fixed point \(h(0)=01, h(1)=21, h(2)=0\), every cyclic rotation extended by two wraparound letters is a factor for all \(k\ge3\), although the square of the prescribed tower word is never a factor. This gives infinitely many explicit Lie-complexity witnesses with a stronger two-letter local cyclic-realizability property.
