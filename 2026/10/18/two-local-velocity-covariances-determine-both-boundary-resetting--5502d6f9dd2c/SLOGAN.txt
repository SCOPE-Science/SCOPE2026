In a thermodynamic-limit harmonic chain driven by two equally diffusive resetting walls, just the bulk velocity variance and nearest-neighbor covariance determine both positive resetting rates uniquely up to interchange. Their normalized values \(S,T\) are physically admissible exactly on the sharp wedge \(\sqrt{1+S^2}-1\le T<(\sqrt{1+4S^2}-1)/2\), with the lower edge characterizing equal rates.
