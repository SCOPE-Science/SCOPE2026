## Correctness — PASS
The structure is specified by three **named** reflexive equivalence relations on cells. An explicit line-intersection induction makes every point of the generated subgroup square singleton-coloured; a fully enumerated quotient-neighbour analysis proves the proposed partition equitable in each relation, and line incidences separate all its parts. The full autotopism group is derived from the row/column/symbol functional equation, not guessed from a subgroup. Congruence arguments give the exact generator-or-four-element orbit-recovery dichotomy. Two independent partition-level tests and brute-force small-order permutation tests corroborate the algebra. The finite computations are not substituted for induction or the congruence proof.

Published work on Latin square autotopisms, unmarked unordered-pair orbits, and equitable partitions of *merged* Latin-square graphs was inspected. No inspected source supplies the claimed exact cyclic two-marker theorem. Equivalence hidden in specialized work on coherent configurations remains possible; no independent priority certification is asserted.

## Value — PASS
The result answers a natural local-versus-global symmetry question for an infinite family of 3-net incidence structures. It provides all stable classes and sizes, and a complete arithmetic classification of when these counting classes do or do not match true rooted automorphism orbits. The unique order-four exception and infinite families of failures materially distinguish cyclic groups from the binary-vector setting. Its mathematical purpose is structural classification, not just enumeration or renaming.
