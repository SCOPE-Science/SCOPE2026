For every nonbinary Hamming graph \(H(d,q)\) with \(q\ge3\), individually marking any two distinct vertices makes ordinary colour refinement recover exactly their pointwise-stabilizer orbits. If the marks have Hamming distance \(b\), the resulting number of stable classes is \((d-b+1)\binom{b+2}{2}\), with explicit sizes, and at most \(d+1\) rounds suffice.
