The first two three-color cycle products beyond the published single-dimension families have exact homotopy types \(\operatorname{Ind}(C_7\times K_3)\simeq S^3\vee3S^4\) and \(\operatorname{Ind}(C_8\times K_3)\simeq S^4\vee3S^5\). They are torsion-free and abstractly suspension-related, giving two explicit affirmative cases of a published torsion-freeness question.
