Every full strong discrete-Morse matching complex of a complete multipartite **graph** with at least three nonempty parts splits into disjoint two-part complexes and simplicially collapses to graphs. With \(s\) singleton parts, its component count is \(\binom r2+\binom s2\) and its integral first Betti number is \(\sum_{i<j,\,n_i,n_j\ge2}(2n_in_j-n_i-n_j+1)\), with a canonical automorphism-equivariant cycle-lattice description.
