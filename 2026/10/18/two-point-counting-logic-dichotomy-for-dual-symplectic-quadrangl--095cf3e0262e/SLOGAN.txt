For every odd prime power \(q\), two individually marked points make the point-line incidence graphs of the dual symplectic generalized quadrangles \(W(q)\) and \(Q(4,q)\) distinguishable by two-variable counting logic exactly when their pairs are noncollinear; four colour-refinement rounds suffice. More strongly, in any finite thick generalized quadrangle, any individually marked subset of a single line gives the same parameter-determined stable quotient, even when the entire line is marked.
