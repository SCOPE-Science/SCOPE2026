In Mallows permutations with \(q_n\to0\), the probability of being a product of disjoint adjacent swaps undergoes a sharp transition at \(nq_n^2\): it converges to \(e^{-2c}\) if \(nq_n^2\to c\). Two different local obstructions converge to independent spatial Poisson processes, and the exact finite-size matching probability is a path-matching polynomial divided by the Mallows normalizer.
