For two-agent epistemic frames formed from independent uniform label pairs, commutativity of knowledge first disappears at \(n\asymp k^{2/3}\) with limit \(e^{-t^3}\), then reappears at \(n=2k^2\log k+sk^2\) with limit \(\exp(-e^{-s})\). An exact component-count recurrence computes its finite probability for every label alphabet and world count.
