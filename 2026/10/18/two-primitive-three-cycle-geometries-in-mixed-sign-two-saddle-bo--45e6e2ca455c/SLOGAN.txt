In the mixed-sign two-saddle border-collision normal form, every strict primitive period-three orbit has itinerary \(LLR\) or \(LRR\), with at most one of each. Every admissible \(LRR\)-cycle is a saddle, while an explicit open parameter region has a coexisting attracting \(LLR\)-cycle and an exactly located \(-1\)-multiplier transition.
