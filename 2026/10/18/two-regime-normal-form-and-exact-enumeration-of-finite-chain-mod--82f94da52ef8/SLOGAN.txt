All modal Kleene necessity operations satisfying the displayed meet, positivity and consistency axioms on an arbitrary finite involutive chain have a two-regime monotone/constant-prefix normal form; the resulting exact counts are \(1,2,5,13,40,115,\ldots\). They constitute asymptotically a fraction \(1/\sqrt{\pi n}\) of the chain's order-preserving self-maps, and exactly counted top-preserving operations form asymptotically half of them.
