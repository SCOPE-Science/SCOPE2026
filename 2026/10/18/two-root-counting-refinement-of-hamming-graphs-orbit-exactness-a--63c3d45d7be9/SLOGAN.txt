In the Hamming graph \(H(d,q)\), individualizing two vertices forces ordinary counting colour refinement to recover **exactly** every orbit of their pointwise stabilizer, with an explicit all-parameter class and size formula. The simpler ordered two-distance partition is already exact precisely for \(q=2\), adjacent marked vertices, or antipodal marked vertices; otherwise it fails equitability, and one further counting-refinement round repairs it.
