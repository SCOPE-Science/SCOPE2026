For every integer \(n\ge4\), the triangular graph \(L(K_n)\) has induced-three-vertex-path Horn closure that stabilizes after at most two parallel rounds, with a precise cherry obstruction to finishing in one. Its closure lattice has an exact strictly log-concave rank polynomial and Möbius number \((-1)^n\), while the lattice automorphism group changes sharply from \(\mathfrak S_2\wr\mathfrak S_3\) at \(n=4\) to \(\mathfrak S_n\) thereafter.
