In the strict-inclusion lattice of all subspaces of a finite vector space, individually marking any nested flag makes two rounds of counting colour refinement recover **exactly** every flag-stabilizer orbit, with a field-size-independent number \(\prod_i(d_i+1)\) of classes. Their exact sizes are Gaussian products, and a marked two-plane in \(\mathbb F_2^4\) shows that the two-round bound is sharp.
