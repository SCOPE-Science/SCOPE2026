# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof derives the full line-graph neighbour-degree moment formula by separately counting edges incident to both endpoints and subtracting the twice-counted original edge. Two recovered symmetric functions determine the unordered pair of part sizes. A transitive subgroup establishes that these types cannot split under refinement. The size formula, phase rule, and unique order-eight obstruction follow from this result. Three executable programs check direct refinement, symbolic integer moments, and selected full automorphism groups; no finite search substitutes for the proof.

PASS, subject to literature-priority risk. Comparison with related accessible historical findings on two-part rook graphs, complete-graph Johnson cases, and tree line graphs shows none implies this arbitrary-part-size theorem. The full Key–Seneviratne paper was not accessible during the comparison; an equivalent derivation in specialized literature remains a residual risk.

## Value
PASS. This is a natural complete classification, not a fixed-size calculation: a single two-step invariant recovers all symmetry classes for arbitrarily many arbitrarily unbalanced parts. It also classifies exactly when zero, one, or two rounds suffice, gives sizes of every class, proves the unique smallest extra-round obstruction, and supplies an unbounded sharp family. These facts locate a concrete boundary where the first degree statistic fails and a second neighbourhood moment repairs it.
