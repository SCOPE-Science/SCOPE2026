For the real Lipschitz dual ball of the graph formed by at least three parallel unit-edge paths of length three, the exact anchored volume is a moment of an explicit two-branch density, giving a complete rational formula and strictly increasing growth ratios converging to \(3\). Its large-arm volume has a Gaussian beta-function main term and a strictly positive, sharp secondary contribution of order \(2^m/m^2\), caused by the junction of the two cross-sectional regimes.
