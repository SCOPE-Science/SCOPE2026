**PASS.** First-arrival fragmentation proves the path recurrence and the two-seed cycle formula. Uniform edge-rank conditioning and rotational symmetry prove the full-law single-bond invariance. The Taylor/Beta integral computes the signed response, strict bounds, and factorial asymptotic; all limits and exceptional small path lengths are specified. The finite tests are corroboration, not an infinite proof.

The 1997 impurity manuscript was materially read; its random-impurity ensembles are distinct from position-fixed mandatory first arrivals. The inspected literature did not establish an implication-equivalent result; absence from search results would not certify novelty.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The first controlled bond has exactly zero effect on the terminal-count law, while the second causes a parity-sensitive signed response, making this a minimal cooperative adsorption mechanism. The decay scale is factorial rather than the exponential screening one might expect from a local process. This provides a concrete benchmark for prepatterned one-dimensional jamming and separates externally imposed pair perturbations from unconditioned two-point correlations.

## Limitations
The result concerns first-arrival, no-retry irreversible dimers on a homogeneous cycle, and only conditional two-seed expectations, not unforced multibond rate derivatives. Full bibliographic exhaustiveness and independent scientific validation are not claimed.
