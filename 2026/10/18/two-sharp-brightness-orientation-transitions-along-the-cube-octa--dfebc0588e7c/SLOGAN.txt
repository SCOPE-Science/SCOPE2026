The sharp minimum and maximum orthographic shadow areas of \(K_c=[-1,1]^3\cap\{\|x\|_1\le c\}\) have complete exact formulas for all \(1\le c\le3\). Maximizing viewing directions jump from 24 tilted axes to eight body diagonals at \(c=(7+\sqrt7)/6\), while minimizing directions switch from twelve face diagonals to six coordinate axes at \(c=(4-\sqrt2+\sqrt{10-6\sqrt2})/2\); both transitions occur without a change of the polytope's combinatorial type.
