# Scientific review: two covariance sign boundaries in symmetric Pólya sampling

**Correctness — PASS.** The Pólya sequence probability is constant over count vectors after accounting for multinomial reorderings. Uniform weak-composition counting gives the exact three probabilities \(u\), \(v_r\), and \(w_r\); their ratio yields the local covariance formula. The diagonal events \(\{X_i=0,X_i=r\}\) are disjoint for \(r\ge1\), and summing the off-diagonal events yields the aggregate formula. Positivity of the denominators for \(d\ge3\) proves all sign and equality assertions. An independent exact rational enumeration of 330 parameter triples, plus direct Pólya transition recursions, confirmed the formulas, but is not the general proof.

**Originality — PASS on the inspected literature, with historical-priority risk.** The underlying distribution and empty-category moments are classical, and are expressly not claimed. Shuldiner–Oldford's 2021 Section 4.4 inspects the same uniform weak-composition probability measure but gives empty-urn marginal moments, not the cross-frequency covariance thresholds. The closely related published independent-ball occupancy sign law concerns a different sampling measure, a different occupied-count variable, and forbids equality; it cannot imply the reinforced model's infinitely many exact zeros. Related composition and indicator-precision studies address different statistics. A previously published mixed-factorial-moment identity may imply the presented formulas, so independent priority assessment remains necessary.

**Value — PASS.** Empty-color counts and fixed-frequency counts are standard occupancy diagnostics. The two exact sign changes reveal a structural difference between the dependence of two individual color events and the dependence of global frequency-class counts. The resulting opposite-sign example and infinite exact-uncorrelated family are natural finite-sample phenomena not determined by empty-count moments alone. The value claim is a complete qualitative classification, not a claim that elementary probability identities require new analytical machinery.

**Scientific limits.** Only the unit-symmetric Dirichlet-multinomial/Pólya case and positive frequencies are covered. Covariance zero does not establish independence. The full historical literature is not exhaustively searchable; the nearest accessible mathematical statements were compared, and no independent audit is reported.
