In symmetric unit-reinforcement Pólya sampling, the covariance between an empty color and a different color observed exactly \(r\) times changes sign at \((d-1)r=n\), while the covariance between the total numbers of empty and \(r\)-observed colors changes sign at \(d(r-1)=2(n-1)\). The sharp thresholds differ: a positive local covariance can coexist with negative or zero aggregate covariance, and exact aggregate-zero cases occur infinitely often.
