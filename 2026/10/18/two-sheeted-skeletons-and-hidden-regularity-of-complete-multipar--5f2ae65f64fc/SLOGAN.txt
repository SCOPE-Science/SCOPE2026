For every complete multipartite shortest-path metric with at least three parts, the graph of the real Lipschitz-free unit ball is a signed double cover of a clique blow-up of a triangular graph. Every complete tripartite instance has a regular skeleton of degree \(M-1\), where \(M\) is the number of metric graph edges, regardless of imbalance; with four or more parts, regularity occurs exactly when all parts have equal size.
