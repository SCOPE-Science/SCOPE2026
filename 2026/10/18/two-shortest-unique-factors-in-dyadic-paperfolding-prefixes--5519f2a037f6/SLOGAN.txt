For every dyadic regular-paperfolding prefix of length \(2^n\) with \(n\ge6\), the shortest uniquely occurring factor has length \(2^{n-4}+1\). Exactly two factors attain this length, starting at one-based positions \(11\cdot2^{n-4}\) and \(12\cdot2^{n-4}\); the proof uses a nine-symbol synchronization certificate and two-adic occurrence classes.
