## Originality: PASS

The 2010 Padé positivity paper and its 2012 expansion establish general sufficient criteria and counterexamples, not the explicit two-sided matching-conductance interval. The unweighted complete multipartite special case \(a=b\) is acknowledged rather than claimed. A previously established matched-edge controllability classification concerns a different invariant and does not imply positivity of the rational diffusion map. No statement equivalent to the two-sided cutoff, its exactly-one-root horizon, or the sharp crown-graph dimension boundary was identified in the searched literature and claim database. This is best-knowledge originality, not a claim of exhaustive uniqueness; unindexed matrix-function results remain a residual risk.

## Value: PASS

A-stability does not guarantee positivity. This exact classification explains two qualitatively distinct ways positivity can fail under a natural single-orbit edge perturbation, offers a complete timestep prescription outside the safe window, and identifies the dimension-six transition for crown graphs and a nontrivial limiting upper weight ratio of \(5/4\). These go beyond a routine statement of stability or a single counterexample.
