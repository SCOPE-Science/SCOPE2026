In two linearly coupled quadratic maps, a small mismatch between the individual drives shifts the non-diagonal period-two orbit's unit-circle spectral threshold by \(\bigl[(b+1)(3b+1)(3b+2)/(b-1)^2\bigr]\delta^2+O_b(\delta^4)\). The shift changes sign exactly at coupling \(b=-2/3\) and \(b=-1/3\), distinguishing three local heterogeneity-response regimes.
