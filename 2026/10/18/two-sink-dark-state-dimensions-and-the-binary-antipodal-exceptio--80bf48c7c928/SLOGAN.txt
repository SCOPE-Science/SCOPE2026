For two irreversible local traps on the coherent \(q\)-ary Hamming graph, the bright-space dimension is exactly \(2n+1\) for \(q\ge3\), but for binary hypercubes it is \(2n\) except that antipodal traps collapse to the single-trap dimension \(n+1\). All eventual capture probabilities, including those from individual starting vertices, follow from explicit Krawtchouk projector formulas and do not depend on positive trap strengths.
