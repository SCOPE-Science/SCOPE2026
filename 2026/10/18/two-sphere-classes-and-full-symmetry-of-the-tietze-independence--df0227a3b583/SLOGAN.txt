Tietze's twelve-vertex cubic graph has independence complex homotopy equivalent to \(S^2\vee S^2\), with a complete 87-pair acyclic Morse reduction. Its full \(S_2\times S_3\) action on rational second homology is \(\operatorname{sgn}_{S_3}\boxtimes(\mathbf1\oplus\operatorname{sgn}_{S_2})\), so neither sphere class is invariant under every symmetry.
