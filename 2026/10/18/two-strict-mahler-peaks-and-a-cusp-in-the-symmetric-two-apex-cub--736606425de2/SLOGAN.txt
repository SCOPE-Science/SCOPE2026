For the symmetric cube with two antipodal pairs of adjacent corner apices, the exact three-dimensional Mahler product has **two strict local maxima**, at \(t=\sqrt5-1\) and \(t=4/\sqrt3\), separated by a cusp minimum at \(t=2\). The first peak is globally maximal, while the cube value \(32/3\) is strictly exceeded at every finite positive parameter and approached again as \(t\to\infty\).
