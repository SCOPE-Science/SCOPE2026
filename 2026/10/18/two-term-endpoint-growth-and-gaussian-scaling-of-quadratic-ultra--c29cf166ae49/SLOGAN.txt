For fixed-degree quadratic ultraspherical projection, concentrating the weight at the origin makes the sharp endpoint operator norm grow as \(4\phi(1)a+7\phi(1)+4Q-1+o(1)\), with the endpoints uniquely extremal for all sufficiently large \(a\). A separate Gaussian Hermite-kernel profile survives at evaluation points of order \(a^{-1/2}\).
