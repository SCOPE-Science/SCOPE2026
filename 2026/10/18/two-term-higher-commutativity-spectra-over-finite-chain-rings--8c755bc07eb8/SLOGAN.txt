For the three-by-three Heisenberg group over any finite commutative chain ring of length \(m\ge2\) and residue size \(q\), every higher commuting-tuple count is \(A_m(q)q^{2mr}-(A_m(q)-1)q^{(2m-1)r}\), where \(A_m(q)=1+q+\cdots+q^m\). This exact two-term spectrum also gives \(A_m(q)\) maximal-order abelian subgroups, independently of the chain ring's characteristic or isomorphism type.
