Resetting only a Brownian probe while leaving its viscoelastic bath untouched produces a strictly positive two-exponential stationary autocovariance. Even though fast resetting makes the probe's one-time variance match isolated resetting Brownian motion at leading order, the integrated time correlation remains larger by the exact asymptotic factor \(1+\gamma/2\), and fixed positive-lag correlations retain an explicit bath-memory tail.
