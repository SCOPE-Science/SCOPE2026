Among binary length-\(n\) words with exactly three ones, every complete \(3\)-binomial scattered-subsequence profile has at most two realizations; all ambiguous pairs are given by an explicit interchange of successive zero gaps. Their number has generating function \(x^7/((1-x)^2(1-x^2)(1-x^4))\), and asymptotically one quarter of weight-three words have exactly one indistinguishable partner.
