The total four-cut complex of the sixteen-vertex Clebsch graph is five-connected, with \(\widetilde H_6\cong\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\) and \(\widetilde H_8\cong\mathbb Z^{45}\). Its Alexander dual is four-connected and also has two-torsion, with \(\widetilde H_5\cong\mathbb Z^{45}\) and \(\widetilde H_6\cong\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\); no full wedge decomposition is asserted.
