For \(M/M/1\) busy-period surveillance, the duration's arrival-rate Fisher information tends to \(2/\mu^2\) in light traffic but grows as \(1/[2\mu(\mu-\lambda)]\) near critical load. Consequently the two previously defined covert square-root constants differ by an unbounded factor \((2\lambda/\mu)^{-1/2}\) in light traffic yet become asymptotically equal in heavy traffic.
