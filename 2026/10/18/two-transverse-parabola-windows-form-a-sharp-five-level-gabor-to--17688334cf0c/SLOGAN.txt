Two explicit, transposed parabola-indicator windows, each supported on only \(q\) of the \(q^2\) points of \(\mathbb F_q^2\), provide complete complex-matrix Gabor tomography for every odd prime power \(q\) with exactly five normal-operator eigenvalues \(1,2,q,2q,2q^2\). The sharp condition number is \(\sqrt2q\), and no single window supported on at most \(q\) points can achieve full linear matrix tomography.
