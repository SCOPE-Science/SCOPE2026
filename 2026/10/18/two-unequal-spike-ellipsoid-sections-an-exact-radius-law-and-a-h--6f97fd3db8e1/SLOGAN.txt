The Euclidean radius of a Haar-random central hyperplane section of an ellipsoid with two unequal exceptional axes has an exact two-by-two rank-one-matrix law, giving a finite-dimensional Dirichlet CDF and a nondegenerate Gaussian critical limit. When the longest axis grows faster than \(\sqrt N\) but the second remains of order \(\sqrt N\), the limiting normalized radius has an exact \(t^{-1}\) tail and finite positive moments precisely below order one; its infinite mean forces the expected random-to-optimal radius ratio to diverge.
