Sampling one uniform point on each side of any parallelogram gives a universal affine-coordinate pointwise containment depth, with a uniquely certain center. At an independent area-uniform point, the lower-depth probability is asymptotic to \(4\varepsilon/\log(1/\varepsilon)\), whereas the near-certain upper tail is asymptotic to \(\pi\varepsilon/4\), revealing two different boundary-layer scales.
