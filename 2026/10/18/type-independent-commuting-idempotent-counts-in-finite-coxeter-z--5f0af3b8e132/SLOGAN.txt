Every irreducible finite Coxeter zero-Hecke monoid has the same number of ordered commuting idempotent pairs at fixed rank \(n\): it is \(a_n\), where \(a_0=1\), \(a_1=4\), and \(a_n=4a_{n-1}-2a_{n-2}\). A connected-union criterion for parabolic-longest idempotents and a shape-invariant weighted partition function on subcubic trees explain the coincidence across types \(A\) through \(H\), including branching and exceptional diagrams.
