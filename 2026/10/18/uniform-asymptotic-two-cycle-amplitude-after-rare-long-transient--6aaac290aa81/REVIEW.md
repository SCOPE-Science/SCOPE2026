## Mathematical value — PASS

When an attracting family contains a continuum of distinct period-two orbits, a qualitative attraction theorem leaves open **which** orbit an ensemble selects. The exact conditional fraction of direct capture and the surprising cancellation of a linearly tilted signed-phase law into a uniform unordered amplitude resolve a motivated statistical-selection question. This is not a mere restatement of the already-known trapping strip. The result concerns a distinguished rare-transient subensemble; all other basin-selection laws remain open here.

## Limitations

Only the exactly critical \(\alpha=3/4\) parameter and densities continuous and positive at the origin are covered. Direct-capture conditioning excludes subsequent recrossings. Limit laws are weak convergence statements, not global phase-distribution formulas.
