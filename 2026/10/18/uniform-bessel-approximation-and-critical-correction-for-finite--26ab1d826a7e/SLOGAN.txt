For every finite Euler binomial summability matrix, its total squared singular-value mass is within \(1/2\) of an explicit modified-Bessel expression, uniformly over the dimension and smoothing parameter. In the critical window \(r(1-r)=\lambda/N\), the finite-size correction has the explicit Bessel profile \(\lambda e^{-2\lambda}[I_0(2\lambda)-I_1(2\lambda)]\) with a uniform inverse-dimension remainder on bounded \(\lambda\)-ranges.
