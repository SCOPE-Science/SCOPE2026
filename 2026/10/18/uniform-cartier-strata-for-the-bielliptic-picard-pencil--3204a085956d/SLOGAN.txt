For the smooth bielliptic Picard pencil \(y^3=x^4+2tx^2+1\) in every prime characteristic \(p\ge5\), one explicitly given degree-\(m\) Hasse polynomial, \(m=\lfloor(p-1)/6\rfloor\), has exactly \(m\) simple smooth-parameter roots and completely determines both Jacobian \(p\)-rank and \(a\)-number. At its roots the invariants jump from \((3,0)\) to \((1,2)\) if \(p\equiv1\pmod6\), and from \((2,1)\) to \((0,3)\) if \(p\equiv5\pmod6\).
