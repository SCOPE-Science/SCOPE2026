The isotropic Plücker quadric \(\sum p_I^2=0\) has a singular locus of exact codimension three for every \(2\leq k\leq n-2\), sharply improving the earlier dimension-dependent bound. Its singular points are exactly planes with radical dimension at least two, with transverse \(A_1\) type on the dense radical-two stratum and a single two-component exception at \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,4)\).
