For any finite abelian group \(G\), every prescribed colored ordered partition has an explicit full joint block-count polynomial for its pairwise-commuting \(r\)-factorizations, depending only on \(|G|\), \(r\), and the number of output blocks. The gap-by-gap factorization simultaneously gives a uniform all-arity exponential generating function and is independent of the color-group isomorphism type.
