The periodized long-range Zeno jump generator has an exact finite-size critical crossover: when \(p_N=3+z/\log N\), its spectral gap is asymptotic to \(4\pi^2\kappa(1-e^{-z})\log N/(zN^2)\), interpreted continuously at \(z=0\). Its precisely marginal inverse gap scales as \(N^2/\log N\), with a computable next constant.
