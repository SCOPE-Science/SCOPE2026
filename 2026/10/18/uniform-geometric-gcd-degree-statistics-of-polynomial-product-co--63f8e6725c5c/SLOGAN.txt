For bounded-degree monic polynomial collisions \(f_1f_2=f_3f_4\) over \(\mathbb F_q[T]\), the two gcd degrees have an exact joint count depending only on their difference and sum. Their first gcd degree becomes uniformly distributed across the degree range, independently of a bilateral-geometric degree imbalance, with total-variation error at most \(3q/((q^2-1)(d+1))\).
