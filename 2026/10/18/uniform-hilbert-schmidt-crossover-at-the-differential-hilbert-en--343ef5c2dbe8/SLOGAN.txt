At the noncompact boundary of the differential Hilbert operator between weighted Bergman spaces, its finite-section squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm grows with the exact coefficient \(\Gamma(\alpha+4)\Gamma(-\alpha)\). Moving a distance \(\delta\) into the compact regime produces a universal crossover governed by \((1-e^{-\delta\log N})/(\delta\log N)\), and the full squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm has an exact simple pole as \(\delta\downarrow0\).
