The full strong discrete Morse matching complex of every cycle with at least six edges has completely determined, torsion-free integral reduced homology, with at most two nonzero degrees prescribed by the cycle length modulo three and four. The previously uncomputed 22-cycle has \(\widetilde H_{10}\cong\mathbb Z\) and \(\widetilde H_{13}\cong\mathbb Z^2\), and no other reduced homology.
