PASS. Each newly cloned vertex attaches a cone on its original link; the old cone makes the attaching map nullhomotopic. Induction with commuting links gives the full wedge decomposition indexed by nonempty independent subsets. Deleting closed neighborhoods decomposes each link into gap paths; known path types determine precisely which link factors survive. Formula (3) correctly accounts for the cyclic root multiplicity \(n/s\), and the two exact binomial identities prove the multiplicities of (4) and (5). The base cycle calculation supplies the remaining spheres. This proves integral homotopy, not just the observed finite Betti numbers. Exact independent face and boundary enumerations for four cases agree.

Exact formula, graph-product aliases, base/dual conventions, stronger coverage and accessible prior recorded claims were checked. An unindexed derivation of this cyclic specialization remains the main residual risk, and a database search by itself never proves novelty.

## Value
PASS. The proof resolves a natural cyclic case left outside forest-based lexicographic-product calculations, for an infinite two-parameter family, and provides every integral sphere dimension and multiplicity in closed form. Multiple homology degrees grow without bound in number as \(n\) increases. The cyclic-gap counting device gives structural information rather than an isolated low-parameter evaluation.

## Limits
The theorem assumes \(n\ge7\), \(m\ge2\), uniform complete fibers. No general graph-fiber or equivariant theorem is asserted. Finite checks are corroboration only, and no independent mathematical validation has occurred.
