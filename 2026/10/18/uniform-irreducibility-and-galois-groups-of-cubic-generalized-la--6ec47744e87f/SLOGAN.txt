Every cubic generalized Laguerre polynomial \(L_3^{(a)}\) with integral \(a\geq0\) is irreducible over \(\mathbb Q\), without a parameter cutoff. Combining this uniform result with the previously known cubic discriminant formula, its Galois group is cyclic of order three exactly when \(a=3k^2-2\) for an integer \(k\geq1\), and otherwise is \(S_3\).
