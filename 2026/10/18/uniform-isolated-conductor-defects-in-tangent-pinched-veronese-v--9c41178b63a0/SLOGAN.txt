Deleting all first-order coordinate tangent monomials from a degree-\(d\) Veronese embedding (\(d\ge5\)) produces exactly \(n\) isolated nonnormal points on a variety normalized by \(\mathbb P^{n-1}\). Each point has the universal local ring \(k+(z_1,\ldots,z_{n-1})^2\), and the resulting normalization defect has total length \(n(n-1)\).
