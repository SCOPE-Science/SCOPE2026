For every integer \(k\ge2\), the primitive-multiplier Frobenius number of any coprime \(a,b\) with \(\min(a,b)>\prod_{i<k}p_i\) exceeds \(kab-(a+b)\prod_{i<k}p_i\). Consequently its ratio to \(ab\) grows uniformly at least as fast as \((1-o(1))\log\min(a,b)/\log\log\min(a,b)\), and it exceeds \(4(ab-a-b)\) whenever both generators are at least \(413\).
