For every real \(L_4\) of dimension at least two, **every** skew Schäffer power mean, including the maximum mean, has the same sharp quadratic onset \(1-\tau/(2\sqrt2)+9\tau^2/16+O(\tau^3)\), uniformly over the power exponent. An exact square identity characterizes and quantitatively stabilizes the extremizing pairs of the antisymmetric duality-map variation.
