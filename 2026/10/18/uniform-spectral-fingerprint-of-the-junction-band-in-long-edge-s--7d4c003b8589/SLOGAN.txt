For every finite connected \(d\)-regular parent graph with \(d\ge3\), uniformly long inserted edge chains create a junction band whose entire rescaled adjacency spectrum converges, uniformly in graph size, to \(c_d\) times the parent's spectrum, where \(c_d=(d-2)^2/[2(d-1)^{3/2}]\). Its exact leading bandwidth is \(c_d(d-\mu_{\min})(d-1)^{-\ell/2}\), attaining the degree-controlled maximum precisely for bipartite parents.
