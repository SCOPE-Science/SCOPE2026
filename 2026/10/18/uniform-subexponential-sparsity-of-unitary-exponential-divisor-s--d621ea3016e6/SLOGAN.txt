For every positive real \(c\), the integers with \(\sigma^*(n)/\sigma^{(e)}(n)=c\) number at most \(X\exp[-(1/\sqrt2+o(1))\sqrt{\log X\log\log X}]\) through \(X\), uniformly in \(c\). In particular, equal unitary and exponential divisor sums have a quantitatively zero-density solution set, though their infinitude remains open.
