For every \(n\ge7\), the complete coordinate-permutation character of top rational homology of the ordinary Kneser neighborhood complex \(\mathcal N(\mathrm{KG}(n,2))\) is \(\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)(f_1(\sigma)^2-3f_1(\sigma)+1)\). It is the direct sum of exactly two rational Specht modules \(S^{(2,2,1^{n-4})}\oplus S^{(3,1^{n-3})}\), strengthening the previously known nonequivariant classification and extending beyond the separately known six-point calculation.
