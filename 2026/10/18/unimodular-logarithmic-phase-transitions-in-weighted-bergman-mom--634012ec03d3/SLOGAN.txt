A unit-modulus power-logarithmic boundary chirp has a sharp weighted Bergman Toeplitz compactness threshold: noncompact for \(0<\tau\le1\), but compact and outside every finite Schatten class for \(1<\tau<2\). In the compact regime the eigenvalues satisfy \(\log|\lambda_n|\sim-(\pi\tau/2)(\log n)^{\tau-1}\), while the essential spectrum collapses from a circle to \(\{0\}\).
