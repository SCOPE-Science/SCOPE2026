# Review of exact path-actuator volume rigidity

**Correctness — PASS.** The exact claim concerns the unit-weight Laplacian and its full-state coordinate-input Kalman matrices. A normalized discrete cosine eigenbasis gives the location-dependent product of squared modal amplitudes. Multiplication by an odd unit modulo \(2n\) permutes the cosine magnitudes; any common odd factor instead forces a spectral zero. The endpoint triangular Kalman determinant fixes the Vandermonde normalization exactly, yielding integer determinants of absolute value zero or one. Orthogonal modal factorization then establishes every finite-horizon volume claim; positive-definiteness follows from independent exponential or monomial features. A Cauchy determinant proves the infinite-horizon stable-damping formula. Exact integer and rational calculations supplement, but do not replace, these arguments. The important restrictions are unit edge weights, one actuator, finite positive horizons, and positive damping for continuous infinite horizon.

**Originality — PASS with historical risk.** Parlangeli–Notarstefano (arXiv:1109.3556v1, Theorem IV.4) already characterize which locations are controllable, and Fitch–Leonard (ECC 2016, Theorem 2) already establish reduced-space modal reachable-volume dependence. Dilip (IEEE Control Systems Letters 2019, Theorem 3) already provides a full-state generic determinant comparison; **its Example 1 explicitly gives the four-way tie for a six-vertex path**, so that special case is not original. The evaluated contribution is the exact **all-size** unimodular Kalman identity, full-horizon determinant equality, and closed common determinants for the entire unweighted path family. A related prior discrete adjacency-path family has a different operator and a different arithmetic pattern, not a graph-Laplacian result. The published general spectral formulas reduce the mathematical distance to this theorem and leave some priority uncertainty; no independently checked comprehensive literature exclusion is claimed.

**Value — PASS.** A designer choosing one site to maximize full-state reachable-volume need not compare numerically ill-conditioned Gramians on a path: exactly \(\varphi(2n)\) explicit arithmetic locations tie globally at *every* finite horizon and damping. The much stronger integer-lattice statement that every full-rank Kalman matrix is unimodular supplies an exact structural explanation, not a casewise numerical table. The unimodular integer-lattice consequence and determinant-optimality distinguish this statement from mere controllability and from trace or worst-case-energy optimization.
