On every unweighted path, a single-node Laplacian Kalman matrix has determinant exactly zero or one in absolute value: it is unimodular precisely at locations satisfying \(\gcd(2j-1,n)=1\). Consequently, all \(\varphi(2n)\) controllable locations have identical positive D-optimal Gramian volume at every finite horizon, even though their other controllability metrics need not agree.
