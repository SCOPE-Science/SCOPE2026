For every finite connected nonbipartite undirected network with at least one nonneutral social-pressure bias, two supercritical boundary Perron multipliers guarantee one—and only one—interior Pólya-urn opinion equilibrium. That equilibrium attracts every interior expected trajectory and, using the established stochastic convergence and boundary-repulsion theorems, the stochastic declaration frequencies almost surely; a reciprocal-bias bipartite network instead supports a continuum of equilibria.
