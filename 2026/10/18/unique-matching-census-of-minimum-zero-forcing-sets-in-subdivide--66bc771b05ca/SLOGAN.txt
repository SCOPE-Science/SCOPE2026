In wheels with every rim edge subdivided once and one leaf on each original rim vertex, every minimum zero-forcing set is controlled by a unique bipartite perfect matching. The zero-forcing number is \(m+1\), and the exact counts of minimum sets have generating function \(\sum_{m\ge3}N_m t^m=t^3(24-92t+101t^2-42t^3+6t^4)/((1-t)(1-3t+t^2)^2)\).
