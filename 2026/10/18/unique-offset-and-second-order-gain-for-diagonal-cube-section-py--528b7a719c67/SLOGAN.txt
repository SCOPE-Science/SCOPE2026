Among all pyramids whose bases are main-diagonal hyperplane sections of the unit \(n\)-cube, the optimizing section is uniquely displaced by \(t_n=1/6+2/(45n)+O(n^{-2})\) from the center in coordinate-sum units. Its volume exceeds the optimal central pyramid volume by the sharp relative factor \(1+1/(6n)+7/(120n^2)+O(n^{-3})\), providing an explicit lower benchmark for the unrestricted cube-pyramid problem.
