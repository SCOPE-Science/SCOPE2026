Among mixed spiders with \(k\) two-edge arms, the unique direct-leaf count minimizing random-greedy maximal-independent-set bias is \(2^k-k\) for every \(k\ge4\), with exceptional optima \(2\) at \(k=1\) and \(6\) at \(k=3\) (and the expected boundary values for \(k=0,2\)). The optimal bias has the sharp asymptotic \(\sqrt{1/(2\pi)}\,\sqrt{k}/4^k\), refining the previously known order bound without asserting optimality among all trees.
