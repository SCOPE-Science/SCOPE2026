In an asymmetric periodic local-capture Brownian ratchet, the magnitude of the drift has exactly one optimal Poisson reset rate, decreasing strictly with a fixed refractory delay and increasing strictly with geometric asymmetry. The optimal rate diverges with exponents \(2\) and \(2/3\) near perfect asymmetry without and with a positive fixed delay, respectively; the underlying instantaneous-current identity is established prior work.
