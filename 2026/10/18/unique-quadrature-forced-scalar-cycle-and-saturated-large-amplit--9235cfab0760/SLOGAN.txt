In the quadrature-forced first-harmonic scalar equation \(x'=a\cos(t)|x|+\sin(t)\), every nonzero coefficient produces exactly one periodic state, with a complete global attraction-or-escape classification. As \(a\to+\infty\), its multiplier tends to the strictly positive universal constant \(e^{-4c}\), where \(c\tanh c=1\), instead of vanishing.
