For every \(n\ge2\), the variance of uncovered circle length under \(n\) independent equal-length random arcs has a unique maximizing arc length \(s_n\); at that length the uncovered-gap count and total vacancy have zero covariance, with a strict negative-to-positive sign switch. Its sharp optimal-density law is \(ns_n=c_*-c_*(2c_*-1)/(2n)+O(n^{-2})\), where \(c_*=1.79328213290076\ldots\) solves \(e^{c_*}=1+c_*+c_*^2\).
