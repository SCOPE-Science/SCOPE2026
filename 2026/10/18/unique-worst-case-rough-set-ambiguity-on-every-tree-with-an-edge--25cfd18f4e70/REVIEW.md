# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The argument defines one symmetric reflexive neighborhood relation and verifies both approximation operators explicitly. Mixed vertices are precisely cut-edge endpoints. Unique paths give the exact two-to-one cut map for a tree. Outside the completely mixed observation, at least one monochromatic vertex fixes the global color orientation, making the degree-shifted edge-cover map injective. Connectedness and nontrivial order ensure the positive shift and strict uniqueness. The rooted two-state edge-cover recurrence is derived with a disjoint choice of selected and absent child edges. The standalone checker directly enumerates every labeled tree with 2 through 7 vertices and 600 reproducible larger examples, including all realized fibers and shifted polynomial coefficients. The general proof is independent of these finite tests.

## Originality — PASS with explicit residual risk

The closest broad 2023 reflexive-relation rough-set study concerns algebraic structure, not sizes of inverse fibers of the paired operators. Existing 2013 research on edge-cover polynomial extremal properties, and a distinct 2013 equivalence between graph edge covers and covering-based rough-set reducts, are credited and excluded from the novelty claim. The exact polynomial injection comparing *each* noncentral reflexive-neighborhood rough observation to tree edge covers, and the unique central worst-case inverse image and min-entropy, were not found in the comparisons completed. A differently worded theorem involving weak 2-colorings or rough-set ambiguity may nonetheless be prior coverage. This is a self-review, not independent publication-priority verification.

## Value — PASS

The inverse-fiber maximum is a natural quantitative measure of information erased by a rough observation. The theorem computes it for **all** finite trees, not merely paths, and proves a stronger coefficientwise comparison for every alternative observation. It connects the observation's worst-case entropy to an established graph invariant computable exactly in linear arithmetic time, and identifies a unique extremizer. The generality and information-theoretic interpretation motivate the precise invariant beyond a small finite computation.

## Closest literature and limitations

The relevant comparisons are Järvinen–Radeleczki (2023; arXiv:2304.05641), Järvinen–Radeleczki (2013; arXiv:1303.6332), Csikvári (2013; doi:10.1002/jgt.21693), and *Equivalent Characterizations of Some Graph Problems by Covering-Based Rough Sets* (2013; doi:10.1155/2013/519173). Published edge-cover counts and extremality are not asserted as new. The proof requires tree acyclicity; the rooted recurrence counts arithmetic operations, not bits. Equivalent unnamed earlier coverage is a residual risk.
