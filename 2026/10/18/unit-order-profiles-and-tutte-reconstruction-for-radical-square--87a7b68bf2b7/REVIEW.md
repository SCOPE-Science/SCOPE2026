# Review: exact unit orders of square-zero quiver algebras

## Correctness — PASS
The multiplication in the arrow ideal is square-zero. The arrow coefficient of each power is verified by a one-radical-term expansion; equal and unequal diagonal roots give different coefficient constraints. Explicit unipotent conjugation proves the class fibers, and the characteristic-\(p\) exponent argument proves the separate \(pd\)-power count. Möbius inversion gives exact element orders. Infinite-field-size interpolation and the standard Fortuin–Kasteleyn change of variables prove the recovery statement. A fresh standalone direct enumeration matched the formulas in 49 configurations over four finite fields. These tests supplement the proof and cannot certify its unrestricted scope.

## Originality — PASS within checked coverage
The algebra setup is standard in Iovanov–Sistko (2017); Sokal's Potts/Tutte transform is established mathematics; and Qureshi–Reis (2021) studies functional graphs of power maps for other finite-group classes. A prior quiver-unit commuting enumeration addresses a different graph-polynomial evaluation, while prior incidence-algebra idempotent results recover Tutte polynomials only for comparability graphs. Neither recorded statement directly yields the complete quiver unit-order and conjugacy distribution in this result. There remains priority risk from earlier unindexed ring-unit or semidirect-product enumerations; a search miss is not proof of historical uniqueness.

## Value — PASS
Element-order profiles and conjugacy distributions are intrinsic finite-group invariants. Their full enumeration for radical-square-zero quiver algebras explains a characteristic-\(p\) dichotomy and supplies a precise way to reconstruct the loopless graph Tutte polynomial from the family of order profiles, including quivers with noncomparability underlying graphs. The conclusion is limited to Tutte data and total loop count, not an orientation or isomorphism reconstruction.

## Scientific limits and closest literature
See Iovanov–Sistko, arXiv:1705.00762, §4.1; Qureshi–Reis, arXiv:2107.00584, §§3–4; and Sokal, arXiv:math/0503607, §§1–2.3. All three have relevant distinct scopes. The exact results are proved only for algebras with \(J^2=0\). No independent audit has been performed.
