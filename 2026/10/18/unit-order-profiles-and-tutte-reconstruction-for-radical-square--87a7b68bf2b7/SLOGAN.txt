For every finite quiver, the full element-order distribution of the unit group of \(\mathbb F_qQ/J^2\) is given by a vertex-coloring polynomial and two Möbius inversions, with a single conjugacy class for each fixed prime-to-characteristic diagonal labeling. Across finite-field sizes, these order distributions recover the Tutte polynomial of the underlying undirected nonloop multigraph and the total number of loop arrows, but not the orientation.
