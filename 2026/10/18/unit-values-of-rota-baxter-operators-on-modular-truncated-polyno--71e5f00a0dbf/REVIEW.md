# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** For any characteristic-\(p\) field, the iterated Rota–Baxter identity gives \(P(1)^p=0\) without dividing by factorials. Conversely, a target \(a\) of valuation \(r\ge\lceil N/p\rceil\) has nilpotence length \(L=\lceil N/r\rceil\le p\). The leading-term triangular basis \(\{t^ia^j:i+rj<N,\ 0\le i<r\}\) proves that \(k[a]\) is a direct \(k[a]\)-module summand. Truncated integration on \(k[a]\), composed with the module-linear projection, is verified to satisfy the weight-zero identity and maps the unit to \(a\). The triangular inverse depends regularly on \(a\) wherever its leading coefficient is nonzero. Finite tests separately verify the constructed operators and enumerate all operators in five small cases; infinite coverage comes from the proof.

**Originality — PASS with literature risk.** The positive-characteristic factorial obstruction and general truncated integration are not claimed as original. The source comparison distinguishes the new all-depth, arbitrary-positive-characteristic *surjectivity and regular-stratum-section* theorem from prior classifications on dimensions two and three, characteristic-zero polynomial rings, general Rota–Baxter characteristics, and characteristic-zero square-zero truncations. Searches for equivalences, unit evaluation loci, Frobenius-nilpotent images, and stronger operator classifications found no explicit covering theorem in the inspected texts or queried mathematical research records. No search can establish exhaustive novelty; an unlocated specialized treatment could subsume the claim.

**Value — PASS.** Evaluation at the algebraic unit is a natural invariant of an operator variety. The theorem supplies a complete geometric/arithmetic range over an unbounded truncation family and every positive characteristic, establishes that the known \(p\)-power necessary obstruction is sharp, and provides explicit algebraic sections over all nonzero valuation strata. This is more informative than rederiving the classical necessary condition or enumerating isolated low-dimensional operators; it may support stratified studies of operator families even though their full moduli spaces remain unclassified.

**Closest literature and limits.** Guo–Yu's divided-power setting is the strongest necessary-condition antecedent; Guo–Rosenkranz–Zheng's polynomial classifications use characteristic zero. Farooq's similarly titled work assumes characteristic zero and radical square zero. Coverage by other unlocated sources is a residual risk. The statement is set-theoretic with regular sections over reduced strata, not a theorem about scheme-theoretic image or total operator counts.
