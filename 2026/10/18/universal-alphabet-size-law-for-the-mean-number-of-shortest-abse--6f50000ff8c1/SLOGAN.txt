For every fixed alphabet of size \(q\ge3\), the sum of shortest absent scattered-subsequence counts over all words of length \(n\) has an explicit rational generating function, obtained by counting one greedy target match in each complete arch. In particular, the ternary generating function is \(3(1-z)^2/(1-4z+5z^2-14z^3+20z^4-4z^5)\), and the expected number grows like \(1.692088487\ldots(1.079489353\ldots)^n\).
