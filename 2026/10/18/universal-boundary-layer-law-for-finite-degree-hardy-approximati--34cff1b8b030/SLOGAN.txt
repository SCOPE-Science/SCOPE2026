For every fixed polynomial degree and every real \(p>2\), approximation of the reciprocal of a Blaschke factor with zero tending to the unit circle has the universal limiting error ratio \(C_0(p)/2>1\) relative to unrestricted analytic approximation. The optimal polynomial develops an exact \( (1-a)^{1/(p-1)}\) boundary layer, with a fully explicit degree-one profile and sharp next-order error.
