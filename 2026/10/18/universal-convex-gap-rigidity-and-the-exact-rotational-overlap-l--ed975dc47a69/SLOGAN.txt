For two concentric regular polygons of different orders tangent to one circle, the rotational intersection-area profile obeys an exact \(\gcd(n,m)\)-controlled convex gap law: coincident supporting normals uniquely maximize area, while the halfway phase uniquely minimizes it. The uniform-rotation mean has a logarithmic closed form independent of \(\gcd(n,m)\), but the sharp rotational oscillation is asymptotic to \(\pi^3r^2\gcd(n,m)^2/(4nm^3)\) for fixed smaller order.
