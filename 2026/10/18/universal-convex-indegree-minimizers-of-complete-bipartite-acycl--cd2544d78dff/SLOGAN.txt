Every strictly convex indegree cost has exactly the same acyclic minimizers on a complete bipartite graph: its topological part-word consists of balanced `AB`/`BA` pairs followed by any excess vertices in the larger part. Both the universal minimum indegree multiset and the exact number of labelled minimizing orientations are explicit, the latter equalling \(m!(m+t)!(3/2)^{m-1}(1/t!+1/(t+1)!)\) for \(m=\min\{a,b\}\), \(t=|a-b|\).
