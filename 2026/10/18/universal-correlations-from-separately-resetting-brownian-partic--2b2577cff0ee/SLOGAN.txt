Brownian particles that reset separately can nevertheless develop a strictly positive stationary correlation in their squared displacements when they share a fluctuating resetting intensity. For a near-zero rate density proportional to \(\lambda^{\alpha}\) with \(-1<\alpha<1\), the slow-environment limit is the universal \((1-2^{-\alpha})/(3\alpha)\), interpreted as \(\log(2)/3\) at \(\alpha=0\).
