For independent calendar-time velocity reversals satisfying \(k p_k\to c>0\), the entire ballistic-scale persistent-walk path converges to an integrated stationary telegraph signal in logarithmic time; its beta-shaped marginal is known from continuous random-flight theory, while the discrete universality and full path limit are established here. The limiting two-time displacement covariance has a sharp aging change at \(c=1/2\), and the critical Binder cumulant is exactly \(2/(2c+3)\).
