In a globally regenerated one-dimensional iid random environment, every genuinely random mean-zero site law produces a **cubic local maximum** of diffusivity at complete refresh: the deficit is \(8\operatorname{Var}(\omega_0)^2(1-p)^3+o((1-p)^3)\). Uniform over site laws, near-zero drift reverses that sign at the sharp scale \(\delta^2=(2/3)\operatorname{Var}(\omega_0)(1-p)\), with an explicit fourth-order error bound.
