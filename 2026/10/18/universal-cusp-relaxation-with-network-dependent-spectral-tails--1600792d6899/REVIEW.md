# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** Scalar comparison is valid for all nonnegative row-stochastic matrices, regardless of connectivity, and proves a unique globally attracting logistic cusp equilibrium. Exact cubic/quintic coefficients yield the inverse-square asymptotic \(\frac83t-\frac85\log t\). For irreducible matrices, the transverse spectrum has negative real parts, and the nonlinear forcing in the stationary weighted mean is quadratic in the exponentially decaying transverse error; this secures one finite shared asymptotic constant. The homogeneous variational matrices commute, permitting exact integration and its spectral power-law correction. Reproducibility checks are packaged and replayable. The reciprocal-square law is restricted to strictly one-sided nonstationary initial data.

## Originality

**PASS, with a material literature caveat.** The full text of Boudourides (2026), including the equilibrium results and appendices, supplies the source model, global convergence strictly below cusp feedback, and the equilibrium Jacobian. It does not claim equality-case global attraction, reciprocal-square logarithmic relaxation, irreducible constant matching, or the exact time-dependent spectral tail. The scalar Kaye source provides the activation mechanism, and prior results on monotone dynamics, Perron theory, and mode decompositions contribute established techniques. Published-result searches and available prior claims revealed no directly implying theorem for this combined assertion. Kaye's full PDF was not inspected, and specialized network critical-dynamics literature could contain a broader theorem; no absolute uniqueness claim is made.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** This result closes the natural boundary of a published strict-contraction guarantee and quantifies critical slowing precisely at the physically motivated cusp. It distinguishes graph-independent bulk activation aging from topology-sensitive transient directions and shows when all nodes share a single asymptotic phase constant. The connected/reducible contrast cannot be inferred from equilibrium bifurcation diagrams alone. This is a mathematical result for a deterministic normalized model, not a claim about field data.
