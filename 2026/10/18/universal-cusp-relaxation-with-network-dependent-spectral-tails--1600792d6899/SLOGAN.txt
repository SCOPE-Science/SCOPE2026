At the natural logistic cusp of a normalized threshold-response network, every activation converges to one half, and one-sided starts obey the topology-independent law \(1/(x_i(t)-1/2)^2=(8/3)t-(8/5)\log t+O(1)\). Strongly connected networks share even the finite asymptotic constant across nodes, while infinitesimal heterogeneous relaxation carries a separate graph-dependent exponential and power-law spectral signature.
