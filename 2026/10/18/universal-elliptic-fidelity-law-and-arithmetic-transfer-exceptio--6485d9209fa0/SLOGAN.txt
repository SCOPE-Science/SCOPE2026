At almost every static staggered magnetic flux, transfer fidelity between neighboring central sites of a rhombic quantum chain has one universal long-time distribution, an explicit elliptic-integral law independent of flux and hopping strength. High-fidelity observation times occupy \(\varepsilon/\pi+O(\varepsilon^2)\) of the time axis at tolerance \(\varepsilon\), whereas dense rational flux resonances divide sharply into exact-transfer and persistently obstructed classes.
