Even though every fixed-size minimum of iid Poisson observations is ultra-log-concave, any independent random sample size with positive probabilities of both one and two observations destroys this property at all sufficiently large indices. The relative ultra-log-concavity defect is asymptotic to \((w_2/w_1)e^{-\lambda}\lambda^{k-1}/(k-1)!\), independently of all sample-size probabilities above two.
