Random-edge dimer jamming on any \(K_N\) with at most two forbidden edges incident to each vertex has failure probability \(3|E(H_N)|/(N^2-1)\) to relative error \(O(N^{-1/3})\), uniformly over the placement of the forbidden edges. A deleted cycle and a deleted path therefore share the asymptotic law \(NP_N\to3\).
