The four-dimensional split local algebras \(A_\lambda=k\langle x,y\rangle/(x^2,y^2,yx-\lambda xy)\) with \(\lambda\ne1\) all have the same algebraic unit group \(\mathbb G_m\times U_3\) and identical schemes of commuting tuples of every length, although their associative multiplication retains \(\lceil q/2\rceil\) distinct isomorphism types over \(\mathbb F_q\). This nonreconstruction phenomenon is dimension-sharp among noncommutative split local associative algebras.
