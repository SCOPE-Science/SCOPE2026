For every fixed Hermite analysis window and every fixed positive annulus area, sending the annulus to infinity produces a sharp, universal Hermite–Gaussian envelope for its maximal time-frequency concentration and identifies every asymptotically optimal Hermite signal degree. For the first excited Hermite window, the Poisson skewness resolves two equal leading peaks: only the lower-frequency band eventually maximizes, with an explicit \(c^{-1/2}\) correction.
