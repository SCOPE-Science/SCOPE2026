For stationary \(N\)-continued-fraction digits with exceedance thresholds proportional to \(N\), the covariance at every fixed lag \(k\) has an explicit nonzero limit after multiplication by \(N^k\): \((-1)^k u(1-u)v(1-v)/2^k\). Each extra lag contributes a universal asymptotic factor \(-1/(2N)\), refining earlier qualitative alternating-dependence and contraction results.
