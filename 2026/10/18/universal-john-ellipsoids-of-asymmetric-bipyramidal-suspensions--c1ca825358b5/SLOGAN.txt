For a double pyramid over **any** centrally symmetric convex base, the unique John ellipsoid has the same explicit axial scaling and height shift as for a Euclidean-ball base, with its horizontal section equal to a scaled John ellipsoid of the original base. At fixed base and total height, its volume fraction decreases strictly with the imbalance of the two apex heights, with a sharp universal symmetric-to-cone factor \(\frac{d+1}{2\sqrt d}(\frac{d+1}{d})^{(d-1)/2}\).
