For every fixed positive root weight in a Hoppe recursive tree, the covariance between the leaf count and total root-to-vertex path length is strictly negative for every size at least three, with universal leading behavior \(-n/4\). Its correlation vanishes at the sharp \(n^{-1/2}\) rate, with an explicit root-weight-dependent constant; the uniform-weight special case is already implicitly covered by an earlier joint generating function.
