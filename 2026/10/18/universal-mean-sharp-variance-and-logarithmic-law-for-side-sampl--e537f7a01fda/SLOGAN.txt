Choosing one independent uniform point from each side of any strictly convex quadrilateral gives normalized inscribed area of mean \(1/2\) and sharp variance \(1/144+(7/72)((p-1/2)^2+(q-1/2)^2)\), where \(p,q\) are its diagonal-intersection fractions. Parallelograms uniquely minimize that variance and have a universal exact area density with a logarithmic singularity at half the original area.
