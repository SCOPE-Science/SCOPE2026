# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The exact semantic clauses for Gödel implication, box and diamond are stated. Finite positive support bounds the box minimum, and the opposite direction assigns positive atomic values tending to zero on countably many successors. For diamond, finite positive superlevel sets contain every possible near-maximum candidate, whereas an infinite positive superlevel set supports an atomic sequence tending strictly upward to an unattained supremum. All boundary cases, including empty positive support and a zero diamond supremum, are covered. Finite exhaustive calculations corroborate rather than replace the proof.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
The 2026 witnessed-model paper (arXiv:2606.31906), read in full at the semantics and relevant results, defines the notion and gives an infinite failure but not the universal pair of frame criteria. Bílková–Frittella–Kozhemiachenko (2022), Theorem 2, supplies related finite-branching and definability results in a different richer logic. The box boundary is therefore closely anticipated, and its finite-branching principle is explicitly credited. The differentiating assertion is the sharp c0-style row criterion for unconditional diamond attainment and its separation from box witnessing on an infinite fuzzy frame. Targeted mathematical research-record and broader literature searches did not identify an exact prior statement. It remains possible that a general theorem on maximum attainment for fuzzy possibility distributions subsumes the diamond criterion.

## Value — PASS
Witnessed semantics is the defining restriction in the motivating 2026 modal system. The classification determines precisely which frames enforce that restriction independently of truth-value assignments, detects non-witnessability using one atom, and identifies a distinct, genuinely infinite-branching class on which diamond alone is universally witnessed. The modality asymmetry is a structural semantic boundary and not merely a numerical example. No axiomatization or complexity improvement is claimed.
