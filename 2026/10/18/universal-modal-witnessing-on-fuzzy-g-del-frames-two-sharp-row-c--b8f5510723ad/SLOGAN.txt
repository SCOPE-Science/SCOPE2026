For standard fuzzy Gödel–Kripke frames, every valuation witnesses every \(\Box\)-infimum at a world exactly when its positive-weight successor set is finite, whereas every \(\Diamond\)-supremum is always witnessed exactly when every positive accessibility superlevel set is finite. Thus accessibility weights \(1/n\) on infinitely many successors provide a sharp \(\Diamond\)-only witnessing frame; a single propositional atom detects failure of either criterion.
