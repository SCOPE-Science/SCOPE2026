Near slope \(-1\), the number of primitive two-cycles of a floor-affine map has an exact two-tower distribution over its natural translation-parameter circle. After scaling by the slope distance \(\varepsilon\), the multiplicity tends to \(\operatorname{Uniform}[0,1/2]\), while a uniformly chosen cycle at a uniformly sampled nonempty phase has a rescaled gap with universal density \(-\log g\) on \(0<g<1\).
