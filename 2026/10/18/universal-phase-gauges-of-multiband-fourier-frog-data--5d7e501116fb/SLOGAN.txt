For separated integer Fourier bands, every diagonal phase symmetry that preserves all frequency-resolved optical gating intensities for every input is exactly an affine frequency phase on each band, with band offsets constrained by overlaps of pairwise sum intervals. For two bands \([0,a]\) and \([b,b+c]\), an extra invisible phase circle survives precisely when \(b>\max(2a,a+c)\); the first phase-sensitive zero-delay rows and their exact number are determined by the same sharp arithmetic threshold.
