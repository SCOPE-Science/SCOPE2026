Every pair of distinct Hermite eigenvalues of a fixed-width Gaussian annular localization operator crosses exactly once as the annulus moves outward, and no eigenvalue has multiplicity greater than two. The number of crossed index pairs by inner area \(R\) has the universal, width-independent asymptotic \(N_w(R)\sim C R^2\), with \(C=\frac{e^2}{2}\int_0^1t^{2t/(1-t)}\,dt\approx1.171770243\).
