# Correctness, originality, and mathematical value review

**Correctness — PASS.** For an arbitrary body of constant width with odd cyclic symmetry, the support Fourier frequencies are exactly odd multiples of the symmetry order. Rotated midpoint averaging multiplies each nonnegative Fourier area-deficit weight by a cosine square. Single-harmonic strictly convex test bodies prove that comparing just the first and third admissible modes is a necessary obstruction; their equality angles form exactly the stated three-point set. In the regular Reuleaux subclass, the explicitly reconstructed two-branch support is continuously differentiable, has curvature-radius density equal to the width or zero on alternating normal blocks, and gives the exact mixed area by integration. Differentiation proves the strict area monotonicity; complementary angles cancel the sine contribution; Taylor expansion proves the stated uniform limit. The standalone checker succeeds on multiple odd orders, widths, rotation angles, strict-convexity tests, and asymptotic samples. No finite experiments are substituted for proofs.

**Originality — PASS, with historical priority uncertainty.** The recent symmetry-constrained Blaschke–Lebesgue work classifies the canonical minimizers and gives their individual areas but not the full angular mixed-area formula or the universal-angle classification. Bogosel's analysis addresses mixing with the point reflection, corresponding to the known endpoint only. A published exact rotational profile covers the Reuleaux triangle; this case is explicitly credited and excluded from the new all-odd-order extension. Random reflection averaging and two-harmonic area minimization pose different mathematical questions. Sun and Sun's fixed-rotation paper is directly related at the operator level and proves dynamical classification and rates; its abstract states no one-step constant-width area-deficit classification, but the inaccessible full article leaves a material, explicitly reported risk. No negative search result is represented as proof of worldwide uniqueness.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The work identifies all and only the orientation offsets at which a natural geometric area defect contracts by a body-independent factor across an infinite nonsmooth convex class. It also gives the complete exact angular mixed-area profile of the geometrically distinguished minimizing Reuleaux odd-gons for every odd order beyond the previously treated triangle, with strict monotonicity, a complementary-area conservation law, and a sharp uniform rescaled limit. A one-frequency convex counterexample shows that the monotone profile is not a routine consequence of cyclic symmetry; the exact all-order geometry is an additional structural result. These are natural invariants of deterministic rounding, not arbitrary numerical slices or mere renaming.
