Among all planar constant-width bodies with odd \(n\)-fold rotational symmetry, Minkowski averaging with a quarter-period rotation \(\pi/(2n)\) halves the disk-area deficit exactly, and only three angles in a symmetry half-period have any body-independent contraction factor. For regular Reuleaux odd-gons the full rotational area profile is elementary, strictly increases to the disk, and has a universal sharp cubic angular shape as \(n\to\infty\), extending the previously known triangular case to all odd orders \(n\ge5\).
