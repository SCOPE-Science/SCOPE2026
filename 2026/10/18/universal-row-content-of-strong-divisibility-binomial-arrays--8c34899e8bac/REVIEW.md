# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The statement covers all positive integer strong divisibility sequences, all row indices \(n\ge2\), and all interior columns. For any prime, the sets on which \(p^e\) divides a sequence term are precisely the multiples of a rank \(r_e\), with ranks forming a divisibility chain. This yields the exact floor-carry expansion of every generalized-binomial valuation, including integrality. Each level first appearing at index \(n\) contributes one to every interior entry. The explicit choice of the largest proper rank dividing \(n\), or \(1\) when none exists, eliminates all other levels. Exactly those first-appearance levels also measure the increase in the prefix lcm. The last displayed Möbius equality is a previously proved factorization, cited to Nowicki (arXiv:1310.2416v1, Theorem 2.1). The packaged exact-integer verifier checked ordinary, Gaussian, Fibonacci and non-recurrence rank-chain examples. No finite test is used to infer the universal result.

## Originality — PASS, with explicitly bounded priority risk

Nowicki's fully inspected 2013 manuscript identifies the lcm increment as a divisor-product/Möbius factor, not as the gcd of an interior generalized-binomial row. Bousla and Farhi (2020, doi:10.5802/crmath.64, inspected relevant pages) establish a full-row lcm formula and a gcd formula after **column-dependent lcm weights** are inserted; neither states the unweighted content identity, and their weighted identity cannot be divided by a single common factor. Their factorization Proposition 6 supplies related valuation tools, but the present simultaneous zero-carry column for each prime requires an additional rank-chain argument. The Ando–Sato 2000 Fibonacci Quarterly paper's inspected statements treat GCD/LCM symmetry of local triangular configurations, not an entire row's unweighted gcd. Nagpal and Snowden (2016), Section 2, identify admissible primitive factors and example Fibonacci/Gaussian arrays, not the exact row-content invariant in the inspected passages. OEIS A061446 gives the known primitive Fibonacci factor sequence and therefore does not by itself establish novelty or negate the distinct universal row-content relationship. Targeted research-record and literature searches found no exact or stronger coverage; a failed search alone is not a novelty proof. The Ando–Sato chapters (1991, 1999) were relevant but inaccessible in full, leaving a material risk of unlocated implicit or explicit antecedents. No comprehensive priority certification is claimed.

## Value — PASS

The claim gives a natural complete row statistic (the content of the nontrivial row) across **every** positive-integer strong divisibility sequence, and an explicit column realizing the relevant minimum valuation prime by prime. This connects row divisibility in generalized binomial arrays to new-prime-power appearances in underlying sequences; it explains at once the prime-power rigidity of ordinary binomial rows, the primitive Fibonacci factors, and cyclotomic factors of numerical Gaussian rows. The universal structural identity and witnesses are more substantive than simply reprinting a known sequence, and remain useful without a stronger downstream theorem. The result does not assert that all primewise minimizing columns can be chosen equal.
