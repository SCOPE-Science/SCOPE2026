For every positive-integer strong divisibility sequence, the gcd of the non-endpoint generalized binomial coefficients in row \(n\) equals the precise lcm increment \(\operatorname{lcm}(U_1,\ldots,U_n)/\operatorname{lcm}(U_1,\ldots,U_{n-1})\). A prime-adic rank-chain construction identifies an interior column attaining the minimum valuation for each prime, unifying ordinary prime-power row gcds, Fibonacci primitive factors and cyclotomic Gaussian-row factors.
