In every finite local Artinian Gorenstein \(\mathbb F_q\)-algebra of length \(s\), prescribing a rank-\(r\) square-zero matrix as the action of a socle generator on dimension \(sr\) forces the representation to be free. The exact number of such presentations, \(q^{(s-2)r^2}|\operatorname{GL}_{(s-2)r}(\mathbb F_q)|\), depends only on \(q,s,r\), not on the algebra's multiplication.
