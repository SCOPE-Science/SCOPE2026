Under a fixed-sample permutation null, all non-antipodal circular autocorrelation lags have the same exact variance and pairwise correlation \(-2/(n-3)\), independently of the observed values. Yet for generic continuous data, the count of possible exact permutation outcomes at lag \(d\) depends sharply on \(\gcd(n,d)\): for length nine, lags one and three have respectively 20,160 and 280 equally likely outcomes despite their identical variance.
