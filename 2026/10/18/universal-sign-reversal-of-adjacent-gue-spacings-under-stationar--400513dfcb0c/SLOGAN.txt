Three ordered eigenvalues of a reset-free \(3\times3\) GUE Brownian matrix have negatively correlated consecutive gaps at every fixed positive age, but every stationary renewal-reset protocol with finite second waiting-time moment reverses their covariance to a strictly positive value. The exact lower bound \(\operatorname{Cov}(G_1,G_2)\ge [(108\sqrt3-81-32\pi)/(8\pi)]\,\mathbb EA\) is sharp and is attained precisely by deterministic reset intervals.
