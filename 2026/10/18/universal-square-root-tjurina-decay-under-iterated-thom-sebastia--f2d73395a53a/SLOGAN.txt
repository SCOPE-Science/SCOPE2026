For every isolated complex plane-curve germ, the Tjurina numbers of its repeated separated sums have an exact central-binomial formula determined solely by the original Milnor and Tjurina numbers. For nonquasihomogeneous germs, the normalized Tjurina numbers decay sharply like \(\sqrt{\mu/(\pi(\mu-\tau)r)}\); for quasihomogeneous germs the ratio stays one.
