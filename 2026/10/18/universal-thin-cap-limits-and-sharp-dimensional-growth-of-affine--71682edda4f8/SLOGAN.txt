For affine \(L^2\) least-squares projection, every shrinking cap at a positively curved exposed point of a convex body has the same sharp limiting \(L^1/L^\infty\) norm, given by an explicit one-dimensional maximization on a canonical paraboloid cap. In dimension \(d\) this universal norm grows as \(d/e+O(\sqrt d)\), with sharp leading coefficient \(1/e\).
