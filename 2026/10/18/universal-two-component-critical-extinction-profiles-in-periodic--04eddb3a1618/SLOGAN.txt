At the exact zero-invasion boundary of a periodically forced cooperative two-species Lotka–Volterra system, all positive trajectories share a universal pair of positive periodic \(t^{-1}\) profiles: the vanishing species decays at that rate and the resident species approaches its periodic state from above. The period-averaged extinction amplitude is exactly \(1/(d_1-\sigma\tau/d_2)\), independent of the seasonal forcing waveform.
