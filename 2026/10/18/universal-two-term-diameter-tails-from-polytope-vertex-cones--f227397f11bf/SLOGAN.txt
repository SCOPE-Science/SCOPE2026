For any full-dimensional Euclidean polytope, the probability that two independent uniform points are within \(\varepsilon\) of its diameter has a two-term expansion whose \(\varepsilon^{2d}\) and \(\varepsilon^{2d+1}\) coefficients are explicit finite sums of low-order moments of diametral vertex cones. The second coefficient detects transverse cone geometry, and neither uniqueness of the longest chord nor simplicial cones are required.
