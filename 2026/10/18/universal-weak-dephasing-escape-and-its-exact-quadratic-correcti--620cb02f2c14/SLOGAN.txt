An odd quantum path with a perfectly central sink has a reflection-protected dark sector, but arbitrarily weak independent site dephasing makes every state eventually absorb. For any fixed half-length \(L\), hopping \(g\neq0\), and capture rate \(\kappa>0\), the exact slow escape exponent is \(\gamma/2-[L/(4\kappa)+\kappa(L+1)(L+2)/(192g^2)]\gamma^2+O(\gamma^3)\), with a universal leading slope and an explicitly length-dependent quadratic correction.
