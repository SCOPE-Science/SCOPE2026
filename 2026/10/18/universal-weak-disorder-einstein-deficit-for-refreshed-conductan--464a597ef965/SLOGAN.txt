For a constant-speed walk on independently refreshed weak random conductances, the variance rate exceeds the linear-response mobility by \(\frac12\sigma^2\sqrt{\mu/(\mu+2)}\,\varepsilon^2+O_\mu(\varepsilon^4)\) at every fixed refresh rate \(\mu>0\). Thus arbitrarily weak symmetric edge disorder produces a quantitatively universal Einstein-relation deficit, not merely an isolated counterexample.
