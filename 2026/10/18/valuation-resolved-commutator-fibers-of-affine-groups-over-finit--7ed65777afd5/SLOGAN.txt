For every finite commutative chain ring \(R\), the exact size of each fiber of the group commutator on \(\operatorname{AGL}_1(R)\) is an explicit sum indexed by the valuation of its translation target. Every translation occurs when the residue-field size exceeds two, whereas exactly the translations in the maximal ideal occur when the residue field has two elements; all nonzero fiber sizes increase strictly by valuation.
