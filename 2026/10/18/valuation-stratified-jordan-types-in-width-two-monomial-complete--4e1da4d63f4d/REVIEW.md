# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The claimed map is represented on \(k[y]/(y^m)\oplus xk[y]/(y^m)\) by a triangular \(2\times2\) matrix. Binomial expansion yields its powers, and the two determinantal divisors over the discrete valuation ring give the stated Smith exponents. Computing truncated diagonal ranks recovers every power rank and hence every Jordan multiplicity. Valuation-layer cardinalities independently prove the finite-field counts. Explicit exhaustive modular computations over four prime fields and up to exponent seven agree, including characteristic-divisibility boundaries; finite tests are not the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

Primary full-text checks of Cook II, arXiv:1111.4979v1, and Lundqvist--Nicklasson, arXiv:1604.06820v1, establish Lefschetz-existence and linear-form results, not the complete all-element finite-field partition census. Iarrobino--Macias Marques--McDaniel (arXiv:1802.07383, Example 3.37) records the three linear-direction partitions for the case \(m=3\), without counting all radical elements; interpreting their poset sentence as an all-radical classification would require resolution, as \(xy\) yields \((2,2,1,1)\). Altafi--Iarrobino--Khatami (arXiv:1810.00716, Example 1.2) studies the same (2,3) example only for linear forms in characteristic zero/large characteristic, without all-radical finite-field counts. The 2023 Jordan-type survey explores broader Jordan phenomena without an identified covering formula for all \(f(y)+xg(y)\) on \(k[x,y]/(x^2,y^m)\). Related exact censuses for the symmetric exponent-three complete intersection and for homogeneous quadratics in equal-exponent intersections address different families. The Smith normal-form calculation and the generic linear-form specialization are classical; the assessed claim is the precise simultaneous valuation stratification with finite-field multiplicities. An as-yet-unlocated equivalent special calculation is a genuine remaining risk.

## Value — PASS

Jordan type records more than Lefschetz existence: it measures the full nilpotent action of every radical element on the regular module. The uniform formulas solve a natural infinite-width family with mixed exponents and all coefficient characteristics, give exact arithmetic strata with no matrix-by-matrix enumeration, and isolate prime-divisibility jumps in both intermediate Jordan partitions and maximal index. This provides explicit test cases for modular Lefschetz degeneration and representation-theoretic nilpotent invariants, without claiming a downstream theorem.

## Limitations

No statement is made about other exponent pairs, automorphism orbits, geometric closures of strata, or any independent validation. The finite-field partition counts are complete explicit bounded sums, not individually factored polynomial tables. A specialized earlier theorem using an equivalent Smith or tensor-module formulation could change priority.
