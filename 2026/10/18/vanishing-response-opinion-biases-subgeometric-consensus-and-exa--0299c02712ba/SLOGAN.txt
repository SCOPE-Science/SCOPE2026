On every finite strongly connected influence graph, a receptive response vanishing faster than disagreement guarantees consensus but forces the disagreement diameter to decay more slowly than every geometric sequence. For the 2024 paper's two-agent flat-bias example, the sharp law is \(d_t^{-1}=\log t+2\log\log t+\log2+o(1)\), making the time to reach a tiny tolerance exponentially large in its reciprocal; analytic biases instead give geometric or polynomial rates.
