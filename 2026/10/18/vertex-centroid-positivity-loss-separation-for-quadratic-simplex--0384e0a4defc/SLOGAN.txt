For the local quadratic \(L^2\) projector on any \(d\)-simplex, the sharp evaluation norm grows as \(\gamma(d+1)^2\) at a vertex but only as \(\sqrt{(d+1)/(2\pi)}\) at the barycenter, with explicit positive \(\gamma\). Exact Dirichlet kernels turn this gap into a sharp contrast in pointwise positivity loss; no formula for the global projection norm is claimed.
