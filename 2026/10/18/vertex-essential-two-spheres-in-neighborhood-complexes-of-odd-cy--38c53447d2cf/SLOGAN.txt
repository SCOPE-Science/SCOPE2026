For every odd \(n\ge5\), the open neighborhood complex of the Mycielskian of \(C_n\) is homotopy equivalent to \(S^2\), yet deleting any of its \(2n+1\) vertices makes the induced complex contractible. Thus its nonzero integral second-homology class requires all \(2n+1\) vertices, while rotations preserve that class and reflections negate it.
