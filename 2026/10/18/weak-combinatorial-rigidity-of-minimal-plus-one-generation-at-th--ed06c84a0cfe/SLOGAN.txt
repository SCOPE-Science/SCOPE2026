For any arrangement of \(k=2,3,4\) smooth complex conics with exactly two nodes and \(k(k-1)-1\) tacnodes, the entire minimal Jacobian syzygy-degree pattern is forced to be \((k,k,k+1)\). Thus the near-maximal tacnodal weak combinatorics determines minimal plus-one generation for every realization, ruling out weak Ziegler pairs in those three strata.
