For composition by \(z^d\) on the coefficient-weighted analytic Hilbert scale \(\mathcal H_s\), the self-commutator is a scalar projection plus a compact defect whose two signed weak-trace residues are equal and explicit. At every nonzero \(s\), the defect lies exactly at the non-trace-class \(1/j\) threshold, while its canonical degree-cutoff signed trace converges to \(-d^s\,s(1-1/d)\log d\).
