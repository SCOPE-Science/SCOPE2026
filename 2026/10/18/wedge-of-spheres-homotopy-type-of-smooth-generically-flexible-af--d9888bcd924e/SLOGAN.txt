For Nikitina's smooth generically flexible but non-flexible complex affine quotients, the invariant divisor is \(\mathbb A^1\times(\mathbb G_m)^n\), its normal bundle is trivial, and its complement is \(\mathbb A^{n+2}\). The entire classical homotopy type is \(\bigvee_{r=0}^{n}\bigvee^{\binom nr}S^{r+2}\), so the integral cohomology is torsion-free and all positive-degree cup products vanish.
