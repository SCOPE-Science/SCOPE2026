Canonical cosmological polytopes of graphs with \(\sum_v\binom{\deg(v)}2=o(m^{3/2}(\log m)^{1/4})\) have an intrinsic random-direction width converging, after explicit Gaussian extreme-value normalization, to the sum of two independent Gumbels. The criterion holds for both bounded-degree sparse graphs and all dense simple graph families; bounded-degree graphs also have an exact leading width-variance constant \(\pi^2/(2\log(3m))\) after intrinsic-dimension scaling.
