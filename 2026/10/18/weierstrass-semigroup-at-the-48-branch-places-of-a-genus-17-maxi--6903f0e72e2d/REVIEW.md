# Scientific review: complete gap sequence at the 48 branch places

**Correctness — PASS.** The multiquadratic cover has twelve simple tame branch values and genus \(17\). Its six genus-two character quotients and one genus-five character quotient supply all \(17\) independent regular differentials. At \(x=-1\), the even and odd local-expansion matrices have respective pivot columns \((0,1,2,3,4,6,7,8,9,12,13)\) and \((0,1,2,3,6,7)\), proved by exact arithmetic in characteristic \(11\). These give the complete canonical order sequence and the claimed semigroup; the source's transitivity result carries it to all 48 points. The matrix certificate and an executable checker are provided.

The originating curve is proved not to be a Hermitian subfield. Targeted record, exact-generator, alias, and broader-formula searches revealed no coverage.
