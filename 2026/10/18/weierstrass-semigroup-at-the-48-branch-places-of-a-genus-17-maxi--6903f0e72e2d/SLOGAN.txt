For every one of the 48 ramified rational places on the genus-\(17\) maximal function field of Beelen–Montanucci–Niemann over \(\mathbb F_{121}\), the complete Weierstrass semigroup is \(\langle 10,11,12,18,26\rangle\). This semigroup has conductor \(28\), obtained by an exact character-differential expansion over \(\mathbb F_{121}\).
