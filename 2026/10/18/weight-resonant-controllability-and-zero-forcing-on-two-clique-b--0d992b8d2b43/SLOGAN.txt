For two positively weighted cliques joined by a single bridge, every independently actuated vertex set is controllable exactly when all but at most one nonbridge vertex is actuated on each side, with one extra actuator required precisely at the conductance resonance \(ap=bq\). At that resonance the controllable sets coincide exactly with zero-forcing sets; away from it, exactly \((p-1)(q-1)\) smaller placements become controllable regardless of the positive bridge weight.
