For zonotopes obtained by projecting a positively weighted cube onto the sum-zero hyperplane, the complete optimal-inball center locus is a clipped root-difference polytope whose affine dimension is exactly one less than the number of components in the graph of minimum weight-pairs. The centered inball touches precisely those pair facets, while the outermost vertices are selected by optimal sign partitions: the inner geometry depends on the two lightest weights, but the exact outer-radius threshold encodes the PARTITION problem.
