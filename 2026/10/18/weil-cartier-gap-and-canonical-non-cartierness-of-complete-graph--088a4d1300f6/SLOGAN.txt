The normal-fan toric variety of the perfect-matching polytope of \(K_{2m}\) has Picard group \(\mathbb Z\) but free Weil class group of rank \(4^{m-1}\), and every fixed affine chart has free class group of rank \(m(2^{m-1}-1)\). For \(m\ge3\), every fixed chart is non-\(\mathbb Q\)-Gorenstein, despite having only one global Cartier-divisor direction.
