The projective scheme cut out by all \(2\)-minors of an \(n\)-by-\(n\) generic circulant matrix is the finite flat group scheme \(\mu_n\) over \(\mathbb Z\). In characteristic \(p\mid n\), its \(n\) complex rank-one points coalesce into exactly \(n/p^{v_p(n)}\) curvilinear fat points of equal length \(p^{v_p(n)}\), although its Hilbert function and linear Betti table remain unchanged.
