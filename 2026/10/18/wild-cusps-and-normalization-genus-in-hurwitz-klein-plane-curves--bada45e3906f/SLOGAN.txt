For the plane Hurwitz–Klein equation \(x^ny+y^nz+z^nx=0\), the exceptional prime characteristics dividing \(m=n^2-n+1\) create exactly \(r=m/p^{v_p(m)}\) cusps, each formally \(U^2=V^{p^{v_p(m)}}\). The normalization loses genus from \((m-1)/2\) to \((r-1)/2\) and is an explicit tame three-point Kummer cover.
