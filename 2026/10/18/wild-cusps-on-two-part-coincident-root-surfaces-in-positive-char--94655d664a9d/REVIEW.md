# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The coefficients of the monic binary form recover the weighted center coordinate because the total degree is prime to the characteristic. The exact Frobenius factorization forces every exponent into the semigroup generated by \(2\) and \(p^e\), while its quadratic and first odd coefficients are units. Consequently the entire image coordinate ring equals \(k[v,w^2,w^{p^e}]\), and the hypersurface presentation, normalization, conductor and Jacobian computation follow rigorously. Uniqueness of the two unequal-multiplicity factors and projective changes of coordinates globalize the local result. The packaged coefficient checker corroborates, but does not replace, the symbolic argument.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
The closest primary full texts are Chipalkatti (2001), which explicitly works over the complex numbers and determines a much smaller conductor quotient in characteristic zero, and Kurmann (2011), which explicitly assumes an algebraically closed characteristic-zero base field. Their theorems identify the singular support and normalization mechanism but do not imply that when \(p\mid(b-a)\), the transverse exponent jumps to \(p^{v_p(b-a)}\) or that the Jacobian scheme has that same growing transverse length. The documented published singular-support software description also does not provide this local characteristic-dependent classification. Searches for modular coincident-root singularities and equivalent two-part formulations revealed no stronger covering statement. Undiscovered or unindexed positive-characteristic literature remains a risk, and no uniqueness certification is claimed.

## Value — PASS
This classifies a naturally occurring bad-reduction phenomenon in an infinite family of classical projective surfaces. In characteristic zero the transverse cusp has exponent three; positive characteristic can produce exponents \(5,25,125,\ldots\), unbounded normalization defects, and Frobenius-thickened singular schemes. All these precise invariants govern the severity of a geometric degeneration and are not an arbitrary finite calculation or a rephrasing of singular support.

## Scope and integrity
The hypotheses require odd characteristic, nonzero multiplicities modulo the characteristic, and a positive \(p\)-adic valuation of their difference. Characteristic two and the other arithmetic regimes are not classified. The symbolic proof covers infinitely many parameters. Originality remains subject to independent literature review.
