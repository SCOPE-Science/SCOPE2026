When the unequal root multiplicities \(a,b\) are congruent modulo an odd characteristic \(p\) but not divisible by \(p\), the two-root coincident-root surface acquires an exact transverse cusp \(y^2=x^{p^{v_p(b-a)}}\) along the pure-power curve. Its normalization defect and Jacobian thickness grow without bound with the \(p\)-adic divisibility of \(b-a\).
