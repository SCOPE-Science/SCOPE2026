At every prime \(p\ge5\), sums of two \(p\)th powers have explicit target-by-target prime-power representation densities controlled by a finite Teichmüller sumset and one singular recursion. Their normalized four-variable collision energy grows as \(p^{(p-4)n/p}\), and the limiting \(p\)-adic fibre density lies in \(L^s\) exactly for \(0<s<p/(p-2)\).
