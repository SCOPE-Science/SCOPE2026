Deleting a matching of size \(t\) from \(K_{a,b}\), with \(a,b\ge3\), yields an exact five-term zero-forcing polynomial from a complete classification of permissible initial white sets. Its zero-forcing number drops in three sharp stages, from \(a+b-2\) at \(t=0\) to \(a+b-3\) at \(t=1\), and then to \(a+b-4\) for every \(t\ge2\).
