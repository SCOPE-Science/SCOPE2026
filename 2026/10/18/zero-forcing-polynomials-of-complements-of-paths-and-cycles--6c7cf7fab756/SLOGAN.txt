The full ordinary zero-forcing polynomials of \(\overline{P_n}\) and \(\overline{C_n}\), for every \(n\ge5\), are determined by an exact classification of their minimum forcing sets. Closing the underlying path into a cycle changes only the lowest nonzero coefficient, by \(2n-10+2\mathbf1_{\{n=6\}}\), while the zero-forcing number remains \(n-3\).
