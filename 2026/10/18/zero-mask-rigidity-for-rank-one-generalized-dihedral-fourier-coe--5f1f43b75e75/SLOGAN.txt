In every two-dimensional generalized-dihedral Fourier block, the zero set of a nonzero rank-one coefficient is one of exactly \(L^2+2L+3\) masks, determined by cosets of the squared-character kernel. A mask with a proper nonempty zero fiber in each group half determines precisely two projective coefficient functions; the complete support-size spectrum is \(\{N,2N-2N/L,2N-N/L,2N\}\).
