Alternating between two different Pareto-duration step resets in infinite symmetric exclusion admits a unique choice of residence-time scale with zero mean within-cycle current, while its right and left tails decay with distinct exact powers \(2(\alpha_+-1)\) and \(2(\alpha_--1)\). For \(3/2<\alpha_\pm<2\), the mean is finite but the stationary current variance diverges, despite exact mean cancellation.
