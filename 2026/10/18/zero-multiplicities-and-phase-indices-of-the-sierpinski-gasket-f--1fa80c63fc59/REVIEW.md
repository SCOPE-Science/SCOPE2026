# Scientific assessment

## Correctness
PASS. The product formula follows from the independent equal-weight three-digit convolution. The only mask zeros are the two primitive-cube-root configurations, whose dyadic dilation sets are nested; the uniform summability of nonzero tail factors rules out additional zeros. At each zero, exactly the initial finitely many factors vanish. Their real-linear Taylor jets alternate orientation, giving the claimed exact degree, positive-definite leading magnitude, and phase index. Exact scaling of lattice cosets and disk-cell estimates prove the multiplicity densities. All quantifiers, Euclidean-coordinate normalization, the Fourier sign, and the meaning of the real Taylor order are fixed in the proof. The standalone finite checker ran successfully; it is corroboration rather than a replacement for the infinite argument.

## Originality
PASS, qualified by literature-access risk. The 2006 affine-IFS theory already provides the underlying invariant measure and Fourier construction. Chen–Ai's 2025 equations (1.3), (2.1), and (2.2) already cover the planar mask cosets and the union-of-dilated-zero-sets principle: those are specifically excluded from the originality claim. The analyzed claims do not state the fine order sequence, alternating local phase orientation, bounded vortex charge despite arbitrarily high order, or the order-and-charge disk census. Other nearby identified work on Sierpinski spectrality addresses Fourier orthogonal families. Search failures alone were not treated as proofs of novelty. Deng–Lau (2015) was accessible at abstract level only, so historical equivalence remains a risk.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The motivating object is the canonical singular probability measure on the planar Sierpinski gasket and the natural local divisor of its complex Fourier transform. Fourier zero *locations* alone, important in spectrality, fail to distinguish transverse and higher-order annihilation or the local topological phase. The complete all-orders classification shows arbitrarily large Taylor multiplicities coupled to uniformly bounded phase indices; exact scaling gives a geometric statistical law and an exact finite-radius charge cancellation. These describe a natural invariant over infinitely many modes, not an arbitrary computational slice or table reproduction.

## Sources and residual limits
The most relevant full texts inspected were Dutkay–Jorgensen (arXiv:math/0604547) and Chen–Ai (arXiv:2508.20809); both were compared at the level of implications, not titles alone. The potentially relevant Deng–Lau Journal of Functional Analysis article (DOI:10.1016/j.jfa.2015.06.013) remained inaccessible in full. An equivalent older presentation of jets or phase indices could exist. Only the equal-weight scalar dyadic planar case is proved. None of the computations independently proves the universal statement.
