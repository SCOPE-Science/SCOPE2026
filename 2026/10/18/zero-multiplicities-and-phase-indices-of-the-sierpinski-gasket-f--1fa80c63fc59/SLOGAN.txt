The standard dyadic Sierpinski-gasket Fourier transform has isolated zeros of every Taylor order, but its local phase winding is always only \(-1\), \(0\), or \(+1\). Zero orders have the exact asymptotic geometric law \(\mathbb P(r=k)=3/4^k\), while the phase-index proportions are exactly \(2/5,1/5,2/5\) asymptotically and the two nonzero index counts agree in every centered disk.
