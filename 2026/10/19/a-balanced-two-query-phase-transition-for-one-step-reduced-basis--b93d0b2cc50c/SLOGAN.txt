For a balanced pair of right-hand sides sharing one Type-I reduced-basis preconditioner, the exact best one-step PCG parameter switches at the spectral spacing \(t=3\): below the threshold, any finite regularization worsens mean squared energy error; above it, a unique finite regularization strictly improves that error. At that finite optimum, the condition number is unchanged from the original matrix, revealing a sharp short-horizon/conditioning separation.
