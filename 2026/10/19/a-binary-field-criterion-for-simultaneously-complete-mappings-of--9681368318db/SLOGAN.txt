A finite nonzero commutative unital ring admits one permutation that completes both its additive and multiplicative tables exactly when it is a product of finite binary fields of order at least four. The successful permutations have explicit coordinatewise scalar constructions; nilpotent rings, odd-field factors, and two-element field factors are all excluded. On \(\mathbb F_2\times\mathbb F_4\), both operations separately admit complete mappings, but no single permutation completes both.
