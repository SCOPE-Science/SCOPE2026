The entire binary-subsequence-universality distribution of Dyck words is a Catalan substitution: its bivariate series \(H\) satisfies \((1-t)(H-1)=tu(1-tu)H^2\). At each fixed positive index \(k\), the number of Dyck words of semilength \(n\) is exactly a polynomial of degree \(k-1\), with leading coefficient \(C_k/(k-1)!\).
