# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The endomorphism functor is a product of two independent power-zero schemes. The coefficient of \(x^{p-1}y\) forces \(ab=0\) when p is invertible; the coefficient-of-\(x^{p-1}\) unit argument eliminates remaining pure-y terms, proving the three linear-space support exactly. The differential \(pu^{p-1}v\) gives full tangent space on \(\mathfrak m^2\) and rank p-1 on a dense open of xA/yA. Inclusion–exclusion yields all finite-field counts. A standalone exhaustive verifier checks four finite prime-field cases; infinite claims follow from the proof.

## Value

PASS. The affine scheme parametrizing all augmentation-preserving substitutions of the generators is a natural moduli object of a classical Artin complete intersection. The characteristic transition changes both its reduced geometry and its infinitesimal structure, with a third component generically thickened for every \(p\ge3\). The complete component and field-point classifications are reusable geometric and enumerative invariants for an infinite family, not arbitrary small slices.
