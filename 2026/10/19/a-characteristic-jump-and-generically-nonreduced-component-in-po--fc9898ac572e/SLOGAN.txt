For \(k[x,y]/(x^p,y^p)\), the scheme of augmentation-preserving endomorphisms jumps from affine space in characteristic \(p\) to the square of a three-component power-zero scheme in other characteristics. For \(p\ge3\), one component is generically nonreduced despite the two principal-ideal components being generically smooth, and the finite-field point counts are exact.
