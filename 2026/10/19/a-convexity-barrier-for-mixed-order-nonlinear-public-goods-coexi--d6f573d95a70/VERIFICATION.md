# Verification

The geometric marginal benefit of a focal cooperator in an interaction with \(K\) other cooperators is \(b_\ell\delta_\ell^K/\ell\). Binomial averaging gives the polynomial formula for the total advantage. Every second-derivative summand has nonnegative coefficient and a positive base on the closed unit interval. If all strictly convex summands vanish, the function is affine and the constant degeneracy is explicitly excluded from isolated-root counting.

Run `python3 verify.py` from the package directory. It requires Python 3's standard library only. The program uses rational arithmetic to reconstruct the explicit three-order example and certify the strict sample inequalities and derivative formula. The universal theorem is proved by the analytical differentiation, not inferred from numerical tests. The decimal roots are for orientation, not certificates.

The originality comparison included material examination of the 2026 two-order source and full accessible texts of Peña (2012) and Hauert–McAvoy (2024). Those comparisons do not replace independent priority assessment.
