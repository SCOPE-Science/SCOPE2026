For a weighted two-hub tree with at least two leaves per hub and independent hub onsite potentials, infinite-time quantum average mixing loses exactly one central rank when—and only when—the onsite potentials agree and the two aggregate squared leaf couplings agree: \(c=d\) and \(pa^2=qb^2\). Each leaf group of size two supplies one additional null direction, while every other case with at least three leaves per group is positive definite; the central resonance persists at every positive inter-hub hopping strength.
