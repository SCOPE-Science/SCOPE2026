A Poisson-reset Brownian comb has independent stationary horizontal and vertical Laplace coordinates and exactly diagonal, exponentially decaying second-order correlations, yet its squared-coordinate temporal coupling has an irreversible sign: \(\operatorname{Cov}(X_t^2,Y_0^2)<0\) while \(\operatorname{Cov}(Y_t^2,X_0^2)=0\) for every \(t>0\). The negative correlation has an explicit complementary-error-function law and a sharp exponential long-time constant.
