For geometric-sum generators \(1+z+\cdots+z^m\), the globally optimal linear inverse in \(H^4\) reduces exactly to a strictly convex quartic; its zero lies beyond \(1\) by \(2c/m+O(m^{-2})\) and its fourth-power error equals \(3/8-\kappa/m+O(m^{-2})\), where \(c\) is the positive root of an explicit cubic. Reciprocal symmetry forces a one-real-parameter optimizer in every \(H^p\), \(1<p<\infty\).
