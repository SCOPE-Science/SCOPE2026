For two equal-width Gaussian localization rings separated by twice the Poisson standard deviation, the maximizing Hermite index has a critical displacement \(n_a=a-2^{1/3}a^{1/3}+o(a^{1/3})\), despite the rings being symmetric about \(a\). Below the critical separation the index stays within \(O(1)\) of \(a\); above it the lower of two Gaussian-limit peaks wins by an explicit skewness correction.
