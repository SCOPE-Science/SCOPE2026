In a uniform labeled tree, the expected total number of leaves conditioned on the distance between two marked vertices rises before falling, with its maximum at a distance of order \(n^{1/3}\), far below the typical \(n^{1/2}\) scale. Despite that initial increase, the global leaf-count/distance covariance is strictly negative for every \(n\ge4\) and is asymptotic to \(-\sqrt{\pi/2}\sqrt n/(2e)\).
