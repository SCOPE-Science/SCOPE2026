The high-index turning points of concentric spherical-shell Fourier zeros obey an explicit dimension-dependent phase law: their critical-gap positions have density \(\rho_d(u)=\pi\sin(\pi u)|\cos(\pi u)|^{(3-d)/(d-1)}/(d-1)\), with an integrable midpoint cusp for dimensions at least four. The sharp two-parity phase limit also determines the first index-scale correction to every interior critical radius.
