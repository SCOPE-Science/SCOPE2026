On a finite circle, continuum car parking completes in finite physical time almost surely, yet its expected last-deposition time is finite exactly when the circumference is at most three rod lengths. For circumferences between three and four rod lengths, the terminal-time tail has the sharp constant \(\lim_{t\to\infty}t\Pr(T_L>t)=2/(L-2)\), revealing a singular finite-size jamming transition.
