For round-to-nearest binary16 CountSketch and four-layer SparseStack on the perfectly incoherent all-ones vector, each signed bucket is exactly a reflected walk confined to \(\pm2048/\sqrt\zeta\). Therefore the FP16 sketch has an absolute squared-norm ceiling \(m2048^2/\zeta\), although the corresponding exact-arithmetic sketch preserves norm with variance \(2N(N-1)/m\); its FP16 output converges to an explicit independent uniform finite-state law as input height grows.
