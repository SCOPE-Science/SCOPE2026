The coupled full-logistic map has the same mean transverse Lyapunov exponent \(\log2\) for all \(-2<\varepsilon<2\), but its finite-time transverse fluctuation variance depends sharply on coupling: it remains bounded at \(\varepsilon=0,1\) and grows as \(n\pi^2/3\) at \(\varepsilon=-1\). An absolutely convergent dyadic Fourier series gives the exact variance rate at every coupling in the range.
