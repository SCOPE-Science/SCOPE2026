For the nonisomorphic four-dimensional solvable Lie algebras \(L_\lambda\) with \(\operatorname{ad}_e\) eigenvalue multiplicities \(2+1\), the subalgebra commutativity degree is independent of \(\lambda\ne0,1\): it equals \(1-\frac{q^2(q-1)(q+1)^2(3q+1)}{(2q^3+5q^2+4q+5)^2}\). This exact invariant tends to \(1/4\) as the finite-field order grows.
