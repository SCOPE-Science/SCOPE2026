On a periodic cosine clock chain, adding a fifth spin direction **reduces** the exponential count of states stable against strictly energy-decreasing single-site recolourings: the four-state entropy is \(2\log\varphi\), whereas the five-state entropy is \(\log(1+\sqrt2)\). Exact finite-ring formulas reveal the decoupled-Ising and winding-constrained origins of this reversal.
