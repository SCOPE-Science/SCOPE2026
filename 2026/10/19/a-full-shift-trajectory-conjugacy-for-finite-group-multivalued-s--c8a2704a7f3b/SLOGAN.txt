For a finite group acting freely on a finite alphabet, the complete trajectory shift of the multivalued map \(F(x)=\{h\sigma x:h\in H\}\) is *exactly* a full shift on \(A\times H\). Thus the multivalued binary complement-shift has trajectory entropy \(\log 4\) and exactly \(4^n\) trajectories fixed by the \(n\)-step time shift.
