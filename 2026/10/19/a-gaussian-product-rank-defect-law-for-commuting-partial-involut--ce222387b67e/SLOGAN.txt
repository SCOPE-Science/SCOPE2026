A uniformly selected commuting ordered pair of partial involutions on \(n\) labeled points has a product whose undefined-point count is asymptotically Gaussian: \((n-\operatorname{rank}(ab)-2\sqrt n-3n^{1/4})/(2n^{1/4})\) converges to a standard normal. Exact cycle-refined generating functions and the number of factorizations of every prescribed partial involution make the limit law explicit.
