# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
For the given irreducible scalar action, the kernel of an endomorphism restricted to the elementary-abelian Frobenius kernel is either zero or the whole kernel: in the injective case, idempotence forces the identity; in the zero case, a quotient-to-complement description and the Chinese remainder theorem give precisely the stated retractions. Direct composition gives the left-regular-band law and the image-order and commuting-pair formulas. The standalone checker independently enumerates group homomorphisms and verifies the specified band invariants in eleven finite-field cases; computation is supplementary to the general argument.

## Originality — PASS within checked coverage
The 2021 Frobenius-group study addresses group-element order classes. Chebolu–Lockridge 2024 addresses realizability of group endomorphisms as ring-unit endomorphisms, although its retract criterion is relevant prior groundwork. A published result on the pseudo-algebra of scalar Frobenius groups studies character codegrees rather than this endomorphism semigroup. Published work on Frobenius-group Engel digraphs and subgroup-commutativity degrees also concerns different objects. Actual published-literature, indexed-record, alias and stronger-coverage searches did not identify an implication covering the explicit band multiplication and simultaneous commuting-pair census. An unindexed or historically phrased semigroup result remains possible; novelty is not certified by a failed search.

## Value — PASS
The object is the full set of group retractions of an infinite, structurally natural family, not an arbitrary finite statistic. The fact that these retractions are closed under composition and form a left-regular band is a substantive closure phenomenon, absent for general group endomorphism semigroups. The complete image-order spectrum and commuting-pair formula distinguish the arithmetic and support structure of the family and provide canonical semigroup invariants.

## Limits
The irreducible-scalar hypothesis is necessary for the argument; no assertion is made about reducible or nonscalar Frobenius actions. Finite checks establish examples only. Literature coverage cannot rule out unindexed equivalences.
