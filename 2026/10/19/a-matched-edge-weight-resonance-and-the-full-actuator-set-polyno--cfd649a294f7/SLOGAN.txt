For a fixed weighted \(K_{n,n}\) with all nonmatching edges of conductance \(a>0\) and each matched edge of conductance \(b>0\), the minimum number of independently actuated vertices jumps from \(n-1\) to \(2n-2\) **only at** \(b=a\), despite unchanged topology. Every successful actuator set is characterized exactly by occupied index pairs away from resonance and by per-side occupancy at resonance; their full counting and random-reliability polynomials follow.
