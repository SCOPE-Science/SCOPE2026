For square-subgroup Fourier compression over \(\mathbb F_{p^2}\), every character of exact order \(12\) has a monomial compressed transform precisely when \(p\equiv13,17,19,23\pmod{24}\); otherwise every matrix entry is nonzero. The unexpected classes \(17,19\) arise from pure Jacobi sums even though neither subfield-triviality nor Frobenius swapping explains the cancellation.
