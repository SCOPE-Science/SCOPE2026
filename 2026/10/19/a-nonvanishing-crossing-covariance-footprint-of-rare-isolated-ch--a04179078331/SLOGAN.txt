In a uniform random chord diagram, the nonsimple isolated-chord count vanishes in probability, with mean \(1/(2n)+O(n^{-2})\), yet its covariance with the total number of crossings tends to \(-1/3\). This rare-event effect produces the sharp total-isolate covariance \(-n/3-1/6+O(n^{-1})\), with an exact negative formula for every \(n\ge2\).
