Random greedy adsorption on complete bipartite graphs has a path-length parity dichotomy: fixed even-length paths leave only bounded waste at balance, while odd-length paths with \(k\ge5\) vertices leave vacancies on the anomalous scale \(N^{(k-2)/(2(k-1))}\). The complete critical crossover is a fractional power of a shifted Gaussian, and an extensive initial imbalance produces the exact jamming fraction \(|\delta|^{k/(k-1)}\).
