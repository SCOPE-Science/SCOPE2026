For the doubling map with absorbing hole \([3/4,5/6]\), Lebesgue survival has an explicit exact formula and decays as \(((2+\sqrt5)/30)n((1+\sqrt5)/4)^n\), with a polynomial prefactor invisible to the escape rate. Conditioned on long survival, the first transition between its two equal-entropy symbolic components occurs at an asymptotically uniform fraction of the observation window.
