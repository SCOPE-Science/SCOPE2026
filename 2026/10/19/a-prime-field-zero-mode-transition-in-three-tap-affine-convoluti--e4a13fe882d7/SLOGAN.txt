For three-tap convolution by the identity, a nonzero translation and a primitive affine dilation on \(\operatorname{AGL}(1,q)\), the exact determinant vanishes if and only if \(q\) is an odd prime; otherwise it is an explicit positive integer. When it vanishes the nullity is \(q-1\), producing an infinite family of singular three-tap filters on groups whose orders need not be divisible by three.
