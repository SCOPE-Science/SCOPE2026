Equal-priority irreversible dimer adsorption on complete multipartite graphs has an exact product-form thermodynamic jamming law: a uniquely largest vertex class retains a positive fraction given by its initial fraction times the products of its relative gaps from every other class. On perfectly matchable \(K_{8m,7m,5m}\), the fraction of unmatched vertices converges to \(3/160\), while tying the largest fractions eliminates macroscopic jamming.
