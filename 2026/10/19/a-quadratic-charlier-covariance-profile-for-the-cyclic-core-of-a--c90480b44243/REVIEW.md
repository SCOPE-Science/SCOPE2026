# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The exact conditioning-on-cyclic-points law for all indegree classes follows from the rooted-forest Prüfer construction, including the distinguished last root. Its associated cyclic-count distribution has an explicit enumeration and sub-Gaussian scale tail. A uniform fixed-degree second-order binomial expansion, together with Rayleigh moment convergence, produces the claimed quadratic Charlier coefficient. Exact enumeration and rational comparisons were replayed with the packaged verifier (`CHECKS_OK 207`). Finite tests support, but do not replace, the analytic limit proof. No uniformity in growing indegrees is asserted.

## Originality — PASS with residual uncertainty
A detailed comparison was made with Hansen–Jaworski's exchangeable-indegree random mapping framework and their conditional-on-cyclic-points forest treatment. Those sources supply relevant broad ingredients, but the inspected treatments did not explicitly identify the leading complete indegree-histogram covariance as the quadratic Charlier mode, with its integer-degree sign partition. The empty-box marginal was previously treated separately, so it is not asserted as an individually novel corollary. More general degree-sequence identities might implicitly subsume the asymptotic result, and this possibility is an explicit priority risk.

## Value — PASS
The cycle core and indegree histogram are canonical global and local summaries of a functional graph. The complete leading covariance profile resolves which degree classes carry the dependence while meeting *both* exact conservation constraints (number of vertices and total indegree). The Charlier structure gives a conceptual second-order explanation beyond an isolated numeric moment identity. Neither an unrelated slice nor a routine restatement of a single-class occupancy formula is advanced as the contribution.

## Limits and sources
The original 2006 preprint was read through its accessible full-text sections on exchangeable indegrees and cyclic points. The 2010 conditional cyclic-point article was consulted at its introductory and relevant rooted-forest pages, but not every page was fully inspected. An equivalent covariance coefficient buried within a broad generating-function identity cannot be ruled out, and independent audit remains outstanding.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
