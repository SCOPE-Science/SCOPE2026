For the six-dimensional Takiff algebra of \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\) in characteristic different from \(2\), the Grassmannian scheme of abelian two-planes is a normal fourfold whose only singularities form a \(\mathbb P^2\) of transverse quadratic cones. Its open part is \(T\mathbb P^2\), its projective resolution is \(\mathbb P(\mathcal O\oplus T\mathbb P^2)\), and it has exactly \((q^2+q+1)(q^2+1)\) points over every odd finite field \(\mathbb F_q\).
