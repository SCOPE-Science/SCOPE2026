For the Gauss map with a hole \((1/k,1]\), finite-time Lebesgue survivor mass is uniformly \(k^{-1}\big(\zeta(2,k)-2\zeta(3,k)^2/\zeta(2,k)\big)^{n-1}(1+O_C(k^{-2}))\) through \(n\le Ck^2\). Relative to the independent-branch baseline, the sharp quadratic-time scaling limit is \(e^{-\tau/2}\) when \(n/k^2\to\tau\).
