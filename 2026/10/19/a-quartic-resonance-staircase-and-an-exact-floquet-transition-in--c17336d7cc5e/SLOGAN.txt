The biquadratic nontwist map has infinitely many action-preserving primitive two-cycles for every positive sine-kick amplitude, with an exact quartic action-cutoff counting staircase. Their two-step Floquet trace is \(2-(2\pi bW'(y))^2\), so only finitely many of these cycles are elliptic while an infinite hyperbolic ladder has spectral radius growing like \(m^{3/2}\).
