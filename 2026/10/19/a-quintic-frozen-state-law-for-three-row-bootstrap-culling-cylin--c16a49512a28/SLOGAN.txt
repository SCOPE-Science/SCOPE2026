Threshold-three bootstrap culling on every three-row periodic cylinder has an exact quintic fixed-state generating law, with stable-configuration entropy \(\log(2.607490996204133\ldots)/3\) per site. In the uniform ensemble of fixed states, the occupied-site fraction tends to \(0.82148891766942\ldots\), with Gaussian fluctuations, and about \(42.06\%\) of columns are partially occupied.
