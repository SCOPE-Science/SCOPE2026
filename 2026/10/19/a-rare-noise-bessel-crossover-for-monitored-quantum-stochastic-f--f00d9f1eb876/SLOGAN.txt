In a monitored one-dimensional quantum-stochastic walk at the exchange-coin endpoint, any positive classical-step probability changes a deterministic two-step return into an almost-sure return with infinite mean. As classical noise \(p\downarrow0\), the rare trajectories surviving the second step have an exact \(p^{-1}\)-scale conditional return law with survival \(e^{-t}(I_0(t)+I_1(t))\), a sharp Bessel crossover obtained from the previously known first-return generating function.
