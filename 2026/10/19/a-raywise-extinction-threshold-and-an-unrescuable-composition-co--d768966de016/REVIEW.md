# Scientific review: open-hypercycle ray thresholds

## Correctness
PASS. The source ODE is decomposed *exactly* by a positive time change into an autonomous simplex replicator and a forced scalar biomass equation. The logarithmic-product Lyapunov identity drives every persistent composition to the uniform state. Scalar comparison, openness of finite-time crossing, the sign pattern of the limiting vector field, and a positive lower-root characteristic exponent yield the unique critical mass on each rescuable ray. The explicit unrescuable composition cone follows from a global maximum of \(Se^{-\gamma S}\) and a short-time differential inequality. The proof treats all positive initial masses; the finite checker provides independent finite sanity checks only.

## Originality
PASS under the available primary-literature comparison, subject to a material historical-access risk. Bratus–Drozhzhin–Novozhilov (2025), arXiv:2511.06509v1, derives the original open cyclic model, the two symmetric equilibria and local stability; it does not present a proved full three-species ray classification or a composition-only extinction region. The normalized simplex entropy mechanism alone cannot decide the open-system biomass fate. Pavlovich–Bratus (2012), DOI 10.3103/S0278641912030077, is an especially close predecessor on open cyclic replication; only indexed excerpts and an abstract could be inspected, not full theorem statements. No accessible comparison established the specific quantitative conclusion; this incomplete comparison remains a nontrivial publication risk. Related classical closed-replicator dynamics and dominated-species exclusion address distinct conclusions.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The 2025 motivation poses qualitative basin separation without a proof, and the analytic reduction closes this gap for the natural smallest cyclic interacting community. The stronger counterintuitive statement—an open region of positive *composition ratios* that cannot be rescued at any finite population size—distinguishes population magnitude from diversity balance. The guaranteed explicit cone and raywise trichotomy are structural information, not a numerically fitted basin portrait. The bound is not claimed optimal, nor are results asserted for larger cycles.

## Limits of the review
A prior full-text historical article could not be inspected, and mathematical novelty is not externally certified. Numerical checks do not establish infinite-dimensional or all-parameter conclusions. No independent validation has been performed.
