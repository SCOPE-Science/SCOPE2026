For diagonal planar contractions with disk-bounded disturbances, the unique invariant convex attractor always has a \(C^2\) boundary, but \(C^3\) smoothness holds exactly when \(b<a^3\) or \(b>a^{3/2}\), with \(0<b<a<1\). A reentrant non-\(C^3\) phase appears between these sharp eigenvalue thresholds, confined to two vertical-normal points.
