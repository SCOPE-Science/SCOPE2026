On every positively weighted odd unicyclic phase network with at least five cycle vertices, the antiphase spanning-tree configuration is the unique global minimum exactly up to the inverse-resistance threshold \(w_c=(\sum_j a_j^{-1})^{-1}\). At this sharp boundary, a nonexceptional nearby discrete trajectory decays as \(n^{-1/2}\) with an explicit coefficient involving the full tree's Green matrix, so hanging trees change relaxation speed without changing the symmetry-breaking threshold.
