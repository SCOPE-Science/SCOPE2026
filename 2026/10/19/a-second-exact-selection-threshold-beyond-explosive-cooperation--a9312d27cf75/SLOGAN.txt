In the nonlinear higher-order cooperation payoff, deterministic cooperative bistability begins at \(C_{\mathrm{fold}}\) but stationary cooperation under an explicitly defined rare-mutation Fermi chain begins only at the larger \(C_{\mathrm{sel}}\). For symmetric payoffs the thresholds differ by a sharp factor of \(4/3\); even at equal potential heights, exploration of order \(N^{-2}\) selects defection.
