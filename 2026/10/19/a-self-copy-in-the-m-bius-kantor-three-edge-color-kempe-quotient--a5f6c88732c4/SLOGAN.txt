For the Möbius–Kantor graph \(M=\operatorname{GP}(8,3)\), the single-cycle Kempe-switch graph on three-edge-colorings modulo color renaming is exactly \(K_{1,3}\sqcup M\): one component is a star and the other is a copy of the original graph. With colors named, the two components have orders \(24\) and \(96\), and diameters \(3\) and \(4\).
