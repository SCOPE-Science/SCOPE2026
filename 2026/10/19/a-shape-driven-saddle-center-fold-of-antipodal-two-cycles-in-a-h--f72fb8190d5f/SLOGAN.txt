A hyperbolic double-well lattice map changes the global count of centrally symmetric primitive two-cycles exactly at \(\mu^2=3/2\): beyond this shape threshold, two such orbits coexist in a nonempty parameter wedge and are created at a nondegenerate fold. The coexistence wedge opens quadratically, while the degenerate onset at the threshold follows an exact fourth-root radius law; no claim is made about nonsymmetric cycles.
