For the fourth-order Gauss–Legendre heat step on every connected symmetric weighted graph with Laplacian spectrum \(\{0,a,b\}\), all-step positivity holds exactly when the largest conductance is at most \((a+b)/n\); otherwise an explicit quadratic root gives the unique sharp positivity horizon. For every connected noncomplete strongly regular graph, this reduces to the simple all-step criterion \(2k-\lambda+\mu\ge v\), distinguishing, for example, Petersen and Paley graph diffusion.
