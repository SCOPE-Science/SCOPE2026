For a fixed three-face tetrahedral Hodge preconditioner, inheriting graded face weights \((a,1,a^{-1})\) gives condition number \(2+a+a^{-1}\), while adding the omitted face's unit weight to each retained face keeps the asymptotic condition number at \(3+2\sqrt2\). This constant is the sharp asymptotic infimum among all positive reweightings of those fixed faces, yielding an unbounded quantitative advantage without changing the subcomplex.
