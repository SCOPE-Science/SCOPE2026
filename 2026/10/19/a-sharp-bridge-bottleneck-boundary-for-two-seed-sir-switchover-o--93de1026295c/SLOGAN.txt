For SIR epidemics started from two seeds on a double-star contact network, centralized and uniform seeding exchange expected-outbreak dominance only when each hub has exactly two private leaves, at the sharp transmission probability \(p_*=(\sqrt{265}-5)/12\). With three or more leaves per hub, central seeding dominates for every \(0<p<1\), and its advantage remains a positive fraction of the population as the number of leaves increases.
