For the reset-age-induced hydrodynamic density of an expanding Thomas–Fermi condensate, the covariance between center density and density at radius \(zx_0\) stays positive for every reset rate exactly when \(0\le z\le1/\sqrt3\); beyond that sharp radius, it changes from positive at slow resetting to negative at fast resetting. The critical radius has an anomalous \(r^{-6}\) high-rate decay, versus the \(r^{-4}\) decay in the interior and exponentially suppressed negative covariance outside the initial support.
