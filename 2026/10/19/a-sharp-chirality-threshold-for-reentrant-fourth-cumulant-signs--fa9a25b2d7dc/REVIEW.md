# Mathematical review: chirality-reversal fourth-cumulant threshold

## Correctness — PASS

The stationary moment hierarchy is finite and triangular in total degree, and its two-state blocks have the strictly positive determinant \((p+q+m^2)(p+q+m^2+2r)+m^2u^2\) for every nonzero polynomial degree. Its order-two and order-four solutions give the displayed factorization. Independent ordered-time Fourier-semigroup integrations reproduce both moments exactly at several nontrivial rational parameter choices. The polynomial \(P\) is strictly convex in the reversal rate; the critical-speed function is well-defined, has a unique minimizer, and therefore produces precisely the zero/tangency/two-simple-crossing alternatives. All quantifiers are restricted to \(\kappa=D_R>0\), \(v_0>0\), \(\omega,\gamma>0\), and \(D\ge0\). The thermal-noise invariance follows from independence and additivity of isotropic planar fourth cumulants. The companion exact checker passes 34 assertions; the continuous sign theorem rests on the analytic argument, not those finite checks.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

The closest first-source manuscript, Das–Basu arXiv:2301.11194v1, solves the *unconfined* chirality-reversing swimmer and explicitly raises confinement as a future question. Santra–Basu–Sabhapandit arXiv:2107.12640v1 confines a swimmer but flips its **propulsive direction**, not the **sign of angular drift**; its stationary kurtosis does not cover the present generator. Pattanayak and collaborators, arXiv:2509.03448v1 and arXiv:2603.26967v1, study trapped constant-chirality motion and provide fourth moments, but do not have a telegraph reversal-rate parameter. The static limit at zero reversals is consistent with those works, whereas two distinct *positive reversal-rate* sign changes are not implied by merely specializing a fixed-chirality formula. Public mathematical research search and accessible existing-claim comparisons did not identify a stronger equivalent statement. Broad Markov-modulated cumulant machinery may imply the formulas after nontrivial work, so novelty is a qualified self-review, not a claim of certified priority.

## Scientific value — PASS

Reentrant signs of the **stationary** fourth cumulant furnish a measurable distinction between three fluctuation regimes produced solely by changing the chirality-switching timescale. The result identifies a *sharp* onset in angular speed, proves both rates and no further hidden transitions, and predicts a bounded lower threshold and linearly expanding upper threshold at strong chirality. Isotropic thermal noise has no effect on the sign boundary. The matched rotational/trap timescale is physically meaningful; the theorem does not assert a complete phase diagram for all diffusivity ratios or infer spatial-density modality from a cumulant sign.
