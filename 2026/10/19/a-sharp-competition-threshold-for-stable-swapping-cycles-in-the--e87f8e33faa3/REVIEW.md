# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The precise map, positivity, parameter quantifiers, cycle definition, and strict linear asymptotic stability notion are explicit. Two logarithmic identities determine the entire exchange-swapped orbit family and prove existence/uniqueness. Coordinate-swap symmetry reduces the second iterate to the square of an exact real matrix. Three factored Schur inequalities, strict monotonicity of two scalar functions and one comparison identity prove the sharp cutoff, including \(k=0\), \(k=k_*\), and limits of the admissible interval. Exact-rational identities and numerical checks in `verify.py` support, but do not replace, the all-parameter argument. No dependence on approximate bifurcation plots is needed.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** Ryashko–Otman–Bashkirtseva (2025), DOI `10.3390/math13050857`, already present this **identical Ricker model**, the antiphase two-cycle and a numerical transition (around \(R=8.927\) at \(k=0.03\)); these are not claimed new. Goldar et al. (2026), DOI `10.1155/ddns/7255117`, already give general 2-cycle Jacobian/Jury stability criteria. Neither inspected source determines the exact stability interval of the symmetric swapping class for all cross-couplings, nor the sharp upper cross-coupling \(k_*\). The analytical classifications rather than the map or generic Schur test are the assessed extension. Closest other published Ricker work treats synchronous diagonal cycles rather than swapped ones. Equivalence to an older specialized calculation remains a priority risk, not resolved by negative search results.

**Value — PASS.** Antiphase alternating-dominance cycles are an explicitly studied, natural invariant family of the two-population competition model. A sharp competition intensity above which these cycles can exist but never be linearly attracting separates existence from persistence of stable temporal niche partitioning. The full growth-rate stability window gives a motivated parameter classification and predicts a precise disappearance of the observed stable antiphase mode. It is not an arbitrary numerically chosen slice and does not claim a global bifurcation classification.

**Limitations and residual risk.** Only a local two-step Floquet statement for exact swapping cycles is proved. Equality boundaries may require nonlinear analysis. Other cycles may remain stable at large cross-coupling. Historical publications containing an equivalent special-case derivation cannot be excluded. Independent verification is not yet performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
