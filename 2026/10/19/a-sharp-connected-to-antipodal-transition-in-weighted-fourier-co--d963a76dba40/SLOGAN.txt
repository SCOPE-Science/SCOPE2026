A three-site Fourier concentration window undergoes an exact spectral-mask geometry transition: as its positive middle-site gain decreases, the optimal fixed-measure frequency mask switches sharply from one connected arc to two equal antipodal arcs. For Fourier weights \(p,q>0\), the transition occurs at \(q\sin^2(\pi\alpha)=p\); both geometries, and no others, tie at equality.
