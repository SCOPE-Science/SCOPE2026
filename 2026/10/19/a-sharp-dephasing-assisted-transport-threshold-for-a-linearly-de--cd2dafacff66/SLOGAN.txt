A linearly detuned three-site quantum chain has dephasing-assisted faster trapping exactly when the squared detuning exceeds the unique positive root of a specified cubic: below it the noise-free chain is optimal, above it there is exactly one strictly positive global optimum. For large detuning, the optimal dephasing rate satisfies \(g_*=|\delta|-k/3+O(|\delta|^{-1})\), excluding twin optimum peaks in this entire model.
