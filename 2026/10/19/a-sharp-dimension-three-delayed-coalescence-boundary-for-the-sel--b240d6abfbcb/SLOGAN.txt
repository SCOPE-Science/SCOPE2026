For the normalized self-power map \(T_n(p)_i=p_i^{p_i}/\sum_j p_j^{p_j}\), all dimensions \(n\ge4\) forbid genuinely new collisions after the first iteration. In three coordinates, an explicit fold *inside the first-step image* instead creates an open interval of nonuniform states with distinct trajectories first merging exactly at step two, despite global convergence to uniformity.
