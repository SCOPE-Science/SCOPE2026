In the positive monotone regime of a published planar rational map, every fixed positive starting height has exactly one critical initial velocity separating exponential capture by a line of equilibria from ballistic escape with a universal logarithmic drift. The critical orbit escapes only at square-root speed, and at the natural endpoint parameter its separating curve is exactly \(cxy=1\) with the correction \(y_n^2=2n+\frac12\log n+C+o(1)\).
