Symmetric one-step exploration in the incremental opinion model creates exactly one noise-driven polarization threshold, \(\varepsilon_c(k)=(2k-1)/3\), for every odd number \(2k+1\) of ordered opinions. Below it two locally stable tilted equilibrium laws coexist with an unstable uniform law, whereas at and above it uniformity attracts every trajectory; the polarized branches satisfy an exact second-moment law and turn on with a square-root exponent.
