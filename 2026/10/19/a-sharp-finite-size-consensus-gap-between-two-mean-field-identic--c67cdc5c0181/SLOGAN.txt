Unanimous two-neighbor and three-neighbor nonlinear voter imitation have the same deterministic infinite-population equation, but at finite population size their exact fixation probabilities are binomial and hypergeometric cumulative laws. In the majority critical window, the largest consensus-prediction gap converges to \(\Phi(\sqrt{2\log 2})-\Phi(\sqrt{\log 2})=0.08303203749\ldots\), at an initial majority advantage asymptotic to \(\tfrac12\sqrt{N\log2}\).
