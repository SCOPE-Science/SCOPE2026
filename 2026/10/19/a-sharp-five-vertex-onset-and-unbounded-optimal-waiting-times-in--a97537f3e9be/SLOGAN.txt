The neutral fixation probability of a growing network need not improve monotonically with the number of updates between arrivals: a five-vertex four-cycle with a leaf has a unique optimum at \(g=2\), and no smaller connected growing network can show this reversal. A natural family of final trees has the exact optimizer \(g=\lceil\log_2(2t+1)\rceil\), proving that the best finite waiting time can grow without bound.
