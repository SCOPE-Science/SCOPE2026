For a separated two-state triangular random-matrix cocycle, initial vectors approaching its exceptional equator at rate \(\rho^{tn}\) have a sharp crossover at \(t=1\): after exact normalization, the limiting behavior is deterministic above the threshold and Cantor-distributed at or below it. Both nontrivial normalized pathwise cluster sets have Hausdorff dimension \(\log2/\log(1/\rho)\), and the growth exponent changes slope exactly at the Lyapunov-gap scale.
