For a homogeneous two-dimensional border-collision map with one zero branch determinant and negative traces, the origin is globally exponentially stable exactly when \(ab-1<d<ab+1/a\). The two boundary surfaces support continua of distinct primitive two- and three-cycles, while the central surface \(d=ab\) forces every point to reach zero in exactly at most five steps—even when both branch matrices have spectral radii greater than one.
