A bilinear logit-learning game coupled to environmental feedback has an exact local Hopf-criticality law, \(16s(1-s)\ell_1=(r-2)\omega^2-(r+2)\), which determines whether an emergent small cycle attracts or repels. It proves timescale-robust attracting Hopf cycles for the original 2023 example but also exhibits a rational-payoff feedback game whose Hopf criticality reverses at a sharp environmental timescale threshold.
