For the binary lift of Kang–Jäger's regular Toeplitz word recursion with every \(q_k\ge4\), unique ergodicity holds exactly when \(\sum_k1/q_k=\infty\); otherwise there are exactly two ergodic measures. The entire invariant-measure simplex and its explicit tower-base marginals are governed by \(\prod_{j\ge k}(1-2/q_j)\).
