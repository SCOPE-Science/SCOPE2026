A fixed nonspectral measure on three parallel unit segments has exponential Riesz bases with optimal conditioning arbitrarily close to one, although no orthogonal exponential family is complete. Within natural three-coset frequency bases, the best condition-number excess at vertical bandwidth \(R\) is of sharp order \(R^{-1}\), with an explicit Pell subsequence whose leading coefficient is \(4\pi/(3\sqrt3)\).
