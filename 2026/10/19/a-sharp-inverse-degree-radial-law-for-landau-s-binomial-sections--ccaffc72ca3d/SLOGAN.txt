The nearest zero of Landau's central-binomial polynomial \(P_n(z)=\sum_{k=0}^n\binom{2k}{k}z^k/4^k\) lies at modulus \(1+c_*/n+O(n^{-2})\), where \(c_*>0\) is the smallest real part of a zero of \(\int_0^1t^{-1/2}e^{wt}\,dt\). This supplies the sharp inverse-degree law sought in Technau's 2026 Open Problem 5.7.
