For a harmonically trapped run-and-tumble particle with independent, fair-spin Poisson resetting, minimizing stationary positional kurtosis over **every centered reset law** singles out a symmetric two-point law with finite optimal radius precisely when \((q-1)(q+g)<3\), where \(q\) is reset rate over trap relaxation and \(g\) is twice the tumbling rate over trap relaxation. At and above this sharp boundary, no finite reset law attains the kurtosis infimum: the optimizing radius diverges like the inverse square root of the distance to threshold.
