On a unit-conductance path, a fixed-strength extra edge should join the endpoints for at most eight vertices, but from nine vertices onward there is an exact conductance threshold \(W_n=12(n-2)/[(n-3)(n-1)(n-8)]\) at which the endpoint chord first ties the next-inward chord and then loses optimality. At any fixed positive shortcut strength, an optimal long-path chord spans asymptotically three quarters of the vertices and leaves one-eighth-length tails on each side.
