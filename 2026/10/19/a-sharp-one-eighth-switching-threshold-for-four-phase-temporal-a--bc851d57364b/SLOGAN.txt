A symmetrically Markov-switched pair of complementary full-branch skew tent maps has an exact rational generating function for its directional third-order moment, although the switching environment is itself reversible. Its moment first changes sign at the sharp switching-persistence threshold \(p=1/8\); below this threshold, an explicitly determined interval of turning-point separations produces a permanent four-lag sign pattern, while the equality surface gives infinitely many exact zeros.
