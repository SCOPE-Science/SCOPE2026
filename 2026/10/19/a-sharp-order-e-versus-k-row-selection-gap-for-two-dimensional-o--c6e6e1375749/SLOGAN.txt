Among all orthonormal-column \(N\)-by-two frames, selecting two rows to maximize the smallest singular value can produce a condition number \(\sqrt{N-3}\) times that of a condition-optimal selection; the worst possible multiplicative loss is nevertheless at most \(\sqrt{2(N-1)}\). Thus E-optimal and K-optimal deterministic row sketches can differ by the sharp order \(\Theta(\sqrt N)\), even for a perfectly conditioned original basis.
