In a stationary two-state Markov chain, conditioning the occupation count on an even number \(2s_n\) of total runs versus the adjacent odd count \(2s_n+1\), with \(s_n/n\to\rho\in(0,1/2)\), produces a sharp \(n^{-1/2}\) total-variation gap with an explicit positive constant. The two conditional distributions coincide *exactly* at every finite size only on the symmetric-chain and iid-chain parameter curves; the finite joint laws themselves are classical.
