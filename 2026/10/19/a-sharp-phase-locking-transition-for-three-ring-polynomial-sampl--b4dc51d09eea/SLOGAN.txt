For three prescribed concentric regular sampling \(n\)-gons at geometrically spaced radii, the determinant-optimal angles on \(\mathcal P_{3n-1}\) change at \(nh=\log(2+\sqrt3)\): the outer polygons go from reflected staggered positions to alignment opposite the middle polygon. For orthogonal monomials, the Gram-determinant formula and the maximizing rotations hold for every choice of positive monomial norms.
