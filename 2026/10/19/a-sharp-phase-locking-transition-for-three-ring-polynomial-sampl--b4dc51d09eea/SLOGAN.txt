For three prescribed concentric regular sampling polygons in any rotationally invariant finite polynomial Hilbert space, the determinant-optimal relative angles undergo a universal transition at the logarithmic radial gap \(nh=\log(2+\sqrt3)\): the outer rings move from reflected staggered positions to alignment opposite the middle ring. The sharp Gram-determinant formula and the entire set of maximizing rotations are explicit, independently of all positive monomial weights.
