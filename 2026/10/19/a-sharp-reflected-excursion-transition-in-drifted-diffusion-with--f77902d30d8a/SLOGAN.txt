For drifted Brownian motion that reverses and contracts its position at Poisson times, the stationary left tail changes mechanism at the sharp threshold \(b_c=\lambda_+/\lambda_-\): its density passes from \(e^{-\lambda_- t}\) to the critical \(t e^{-\lambda_-t}\) and then to \(e^{-(\lambda_+/b)t}\). The right-tail decay rate remains \(\lambda_+\), and all tail prefactors are explicit and strictly positive.
