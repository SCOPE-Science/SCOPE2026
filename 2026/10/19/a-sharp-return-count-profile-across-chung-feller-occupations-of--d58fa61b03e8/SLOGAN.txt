For uniformly sampled symmetric nearest-neighbor bridges of at least four steps, expected completed returns increase strictly from either extreme of Chung–Feller positive occupation toward the one or two central levels; the two-step bridge has a flat profile. Although occupation and return count have zero covariance, the covariance of returns with squared centered occupation is exactly \(-n(n-1)4^n/[48\binom{2n}{n}]\), negative for every \(n\ge2\).
