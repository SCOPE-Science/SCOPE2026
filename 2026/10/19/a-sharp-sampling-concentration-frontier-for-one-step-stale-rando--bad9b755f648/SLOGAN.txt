For fixed independent coordinate sampling on a positive diagonal system, one-step-stale randomized Gauss–Seidel converges in mean square for every initial pair **exactly** when the relaxation satisfies \(\beta<4/(1+\sqrt{1+8p_{\max}})\), with conservation at equality and an exponentially growing mode above it. A positive quadratic Lyapunov identity proves the sharp criterion; for uniform sampling its threshold is \(2-4/n+O(n^{-2})\), substantially beyond existing general sufficient bounds.
