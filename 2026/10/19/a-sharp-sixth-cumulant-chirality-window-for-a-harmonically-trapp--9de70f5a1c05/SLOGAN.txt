For a harmonically confined circle swimmer with constant angular torque, the stationary sixth Cartesian cumulant is negative only in one finite window of squared chirality, and that window disappears at the unique trap-to-rotational-diffusion threshold \(b_*=0.4475510940\ldots\). This sharp sixth-order phase boundary differs from previously known fourth-cumulant transitions and is unchanged by independent translational Brownian noise.
