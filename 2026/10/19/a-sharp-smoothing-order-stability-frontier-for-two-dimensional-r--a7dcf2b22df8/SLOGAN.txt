For normal-equations residual-minimal two-grid correction on a two-mode positive-definite system, smoothing before correction is always a Euclidean contraction, while reversing the order can amplify error even with a contractive Richardson smoother. The sharp amplification frontier is the root \(\kappa_*\approx2.875129794\) of \(\kappa^3-\kappa^2-4\kappa-4=0\), and the worst post-smoothed one-step norm grows like \(\kappa/2\) despite an asymptotic spectral radius below one.
