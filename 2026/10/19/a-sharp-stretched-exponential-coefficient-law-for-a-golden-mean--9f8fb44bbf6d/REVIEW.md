# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The action and zeta normalization specify every subgroup and coefficient. The reflection counts follow by direct cycle reflection geometry, while cyclic fixed-point counts are the transfer-matrix traces. These prove the exact rational-exponential form. Factorization gives only two dominant real singularities; comparison of their pole coefficients selects the positive saddle. The circle-saddle calculation reconstructs the Gaussian scale, the analytic constant \(B\), the radial constant \(A/2\), and the coefficient \(1/(2\sqrt{\pi p})\). Exact finite enumeration and a separate logarithmic coefficient implementation verify low degrees; finite tests are not the proof of the universal asymptotic. Remaining technical risk is standard uniform saddle remainder control, addressed by central-arc expansion and exponential tail bounds.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

The 2003 general flip-zeta decomposition and 2014 matrix treatment cover the form of the invariant, not a coefficient asymptotic with this constant. The 2015 group-action formulation and 2021 dihedral prime-orbit theorem concern, respectively, more general group zeta definitions and a mathematically different orbit-counting function. No equivalent coefficient asymptotic was identified in the inspected primary texts or targeted mathematical research comparisons. General analytic-combinatorics saddle methods are pre-existing. A special-case derivation could exist in unindexed work; no claim of globally certified priority is made.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The coefficients of a dynamical zeta function encode an entire finite-index subgroup-fixed-point hierarchy. Determining their growth precisely is a natural orbit-complexity question, not an arbitrary low-index number. The calculation isolates a stretched exponential between the dominant exponential factor and its power-law prefactor, with an explicit constant and a nontrivial competition of equal-modulus singularities. It sharpens what radius/entropy or finite-index fixed-point growth alone disclose.
