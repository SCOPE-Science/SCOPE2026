For the natural coordinate flip of the golden-mean shift, the infinite-dihedral Lind zeta has a sharp coefficient law \(c_n\sim C\varphi^{n/2}n^{-1/2}\exp(2\sqrt{An})\), with explicitly evaluated \(A=1.045631229\ldots\) and \(C=0.089886319\ldots\). The stretched-exponential correction is controlled by a positive singularity that strictly dominates a competing negative singularity on the same circle of convergence.
