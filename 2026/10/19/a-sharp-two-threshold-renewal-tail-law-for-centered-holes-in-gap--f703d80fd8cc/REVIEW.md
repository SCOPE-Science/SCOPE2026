# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The claim quantifies over any infinite \(S\subseteq\mathbb N_0\) and integer \(a,b>\min S\). The equivalence between a forbidden \(0^{a}1 0^{b}\) and an adjacent gap pair \((s,t)\) with \(s\ge a\), \(t\ge b\) is exact. The weighted two-state matrix has the stated entries. Expansion of its determinant gives \((1-F)(1+B_{\max})+B_aB_b\), and its Perron root crosses one uniquely. A coefficientwise sandwich between anchored-gap counts and all binary words proves equality of their exponential growth, without assuming that forbidden boundary padding is admissible. Uniform convergence of generating-function tails and first derivatives proves the sharp two-threshold limit without a bound on the cutoff ratio. Direct finite-state and exact-rational checks agree with the formulas but are not used as infinite proofs.

## Originality — PASS, with qualifications

The June 2025 motivating paper explicitly omits words that both start and finish with zero from its general \(S\)-gap perturbation theorem. The claimed product-tail asymptotic addresses a two-threshold centered subfamily, with no soficity or relative-threshold restriction. Dillon's earlier general \(\mathcal S\)-graph theorem is a genuine stronger *framework*: the exact fixed-cutoff root is a specialization under a length-colored gap recoding, rather than a standalone priority claim. However, Dillon's theorem is a stationary graph-entropy identity and does not state or supply the joint moving-cutoff asymptotic, coefficient or lacunary staircase. Haydarov et al. obtain qualitative strict entropy gaps and Ramirez–Somers study a distinct gap-shift generalization. Focused literature and mathematical-record searches found no directly covering asymptotic; the broad possibility of an equivalent deduction from general renewal perturbation theory remains a priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The two-threshold product law quantifies a canonical rare forbidden word class expressly excluded by a recent entropy-perturbation result. Its leading coefficient is uniform over arbitrary relative cutoff growth for fixed infinite gap set. The cancellation to a *product* rather than a single tail explains exceptionally small and stepwise entropy losses for nonsofic lacunary gap shifts. The exact fixed-cutoff equation is supporting structure, not the sole claimed contribution.
