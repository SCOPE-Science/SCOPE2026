For every infinite binary gap shift, deleting the centered word \(0^{a}1 0^{b}\) decreases topological entropy by \(B_a(r)B_b(r)/(rF'(r))\,(1+o(1))\) as both thresholds grow, even at arbitrarily unequal rates. An exact two-state renewal equation covers every fixed threshold; for power-of-two gap sets the symmetric losses form exact plateaus separated by super-exponentially sharp drops.
