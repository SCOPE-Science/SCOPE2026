In the constant-response friend-of-friend opinion model, the exact critical responsiveness is \(c_*=N/[2(N+1)]\): below it every tie dies exponentially and initial disagreement survives, at it every tie dies like \(1/t\) yet each original component reaches consensus, and above it ties persist and consensus is exponential. The classification is exact for every finite symmetric initial network, with no connections created between originally disconnected components.
