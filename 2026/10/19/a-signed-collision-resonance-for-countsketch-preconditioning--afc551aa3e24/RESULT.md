# A signed-collision resonance for CountSketch preconditioning

## Finding
For the smallest redundant planar unit-norm tight frame, signed hash collisions cause an exact discontinuity in CountSketch rank failure and a sharp inverse-distance blow-up of the *full-rank conditional* spectral condition number of a sketch-derived QR preconditioner. The obstruction persists at every fixed sketch size, including the six-bucket choice corresponding to an oversampling factor of three for two unknowns.

Let \(r_1=(1,0)\), \(r_2=(-1/2,\sqrt3/2)\), and \(r_3=(-1/2,-\sqrt3/2)\). For an arbitrary fixed direction \(v\in\mathbb R^2\) with \(\det(v,r_j)\ne0\) for \(j=1,2,3\), perturb only the third informative row: \(A_\varepsilon=(r_1;r_2;r_3+\varepsilon v;0;\ldots;0)\). Here row concatenation is used. With \(m>s\ge2\) and independent standard CountSketch \(S\in\mathbb R^{s\times m}\), define \(R_\varepsilon\) on the event \(\operatorname{rank}(SA_\varepsilon)=2\) by a thin QR factorization \(SA_\varepsilon=QR_\varepsilon\).

The sharp formulas are
\[\begin{aligned}
\Pr\{\operatorname{rank}(SA_0)<2\}&=\frac{3s-1}{2s^2},\\
\Pr\{\operatorname{rank}(SA_\varepsilon)<2\}&=\frac1{s^2}\quad(0<|\varepsilon|<\varepsilon_0(v)),\\
\lim_{\varepsilon\to0,\ \varepsilon\ne0}|\varepsilon|\,\mathbb E[\kappa_2(A_\varepsilon R_\varepsilon^{-1})\mid\operatorname{rank}(SA_\varepsilon)=2]&=\frac1{s+1}\sum_{j=1}^3\frac1{|\det(v,r_j)|}.
\end{aligned}\]
The same limit holds for the conditional spectral condition number of \(SA_\varepsilon\). At \(v=(1,1)\) and \(s=6\), the respective failure probabilities are \(17/72\) and \(1/36\); the pole coefficient equals \((1+2\sqrt3)/7\).

## Assumptions and scope
Every hash \(h(i)\in\{1,\dots,s\}\) is independent and uniform, and every sign \(\sigma_i\in\{-1,1\}\) is independent and uniform, including independence from the hashes. The \(i\)-th input row contributes \(\sigma_i A_{i,:}\) to bucket \(h(i)\). Zero padding ensures the numerical least-squares setting \(m>s\) without changing the distribution of the three informative rows. All matrices are real and all statements use exact arithmetic. Spectral condition number is \(\kappa_2(M)=\sigma_{\max}(M)/\sigma_{\min}(M)\) for full-column-rank matrices; rank loss is reported separately and not put into the conditional expectation. The preconditioner is the unsafeguarded QR factor \(R_\varepsilon^{-1}\), not a truncated or regularized SVD.

## Proof
**Input tightness.** The three vectors satisfy \(r_1+r_2+r_3=0\), \(\|r_j\|_2=1\) and \(\sum_j r_j^{\mathsf T}r_j=(3/2)I_2\). Hence \(A_0\) has condition number one and, by continuity, \(\kappa_2(A_\varepsilon)\to1\).

**Exact rank events.** Zero rows have no effect. If all three informative hashes coincide, the sketch has at most one nonzero row; this event has probability \(s^{-2}\). If all three hashes are different, their rows span two dimensions for all sufficiently small perturbations, as do the input rows. In the remaining cases exactly one pair \(\{i,j\}\) shares a bucket, with distinct remaining index \(k\). The probability of each such bucket pattern is \((s-1)/s^2\). If the pair signs agree, the two nonzero sketch rows, up to sign, are \(r_i+r_j+\varepsilon v\,\mathbf1_{3\in\{i,j\}}\) and \(r_k+\varepsilon v\,\mathbf1_{k=3}\). Their signed determinant is exactly \(\varepsilon\det(v,r_k)\) up to sign: indeed their signed sum before hashing equals \(r_1+r_2+r_3+\varepsilon v=\varepsilon v\). It is therefore zero at \(\varepsilon=0\), and nonzero for all \(\varepsilon\ne0\) by the direction hypothesis. If the colliding signs disagree, the determinant at \(\varepsilon=0\) has absolute value \(\sqrt3\), because \(\det(r_i-r_j,r_k)=\pm\sqrt3\); it remains nonzero sufficiently near zero. These are all hash and sign classes. The agreement probability is \(1/2\), so rank loss at zero is \(s^{-2}+3(s-1)/(2s^2)=(3s-1)/(2s^2)\). For every sufficiently small nonzero perturbation, only the all-hashes-equal event loses rank, with probability \(s^{-2}\).

**Singular-value pole.** In a pair-collision class with equal signs and remaining row \(k\), the two nonzero sketched rows approach \(\pm r_k\) and \(\pm r_k\). Their limiting squared Frobenius norm is \(2\), and their determinant has absolute value \(|\varepsilon|\,|\det(v,r_k)|\). For a real rank-two matrix with only two nonzero rows, \(\kappa_2(M)=\sigma_{\max}(M)^2/|\det(M_{\mathrm{nonzero}})|\). Consequently \(|\varepsilon|\kappa_2(SA_\varepsilon)\to2/|\det(v,r_k)|\) on each such class. For all-distinct bucket patterns and opposite-sign pair collisions, the limit is zero because their condition numbers remain bounded. All-hashes-equal cases are excluded by conditioning on rank two. Every rank-two event has probability \(1-s^{-2}\), while each of the three equal-sign pair-collision classes has probability \((s-1)/(2s^2)\). There are finitely many outcomes for each fixed \(s\); summing the limits yields
\[\frac{1}{1-s^{-2}}\sum_{k=1}^3\frac{s-1}{2s^2}\frac{2}{|\det(v,r_k)|}=\frac1{s+1}\sum_{k=1}^3\frac1{|\det(v,r_k)|}.\]

**Preconditioned pole.** On a rank-two outcome the Gram matrix of the sketch satisfies \((SA_\varepsilon)^{\mathsf T}(SA_\varepsilon)=R_\varepsilon^{\mathsf T}R_\varepsilon\), so \(\kappa_2(SA_\varepsilon)=\kappa_2(R_\varepsilon)\). Input tightness gives the Loewner comparison \((3/2)(1-o(1))I_2\preceq A_\varepsilon^{\mathsf T}A_\varepsilon\preceq(3/2)(1+o(1))I_2\). Congruence by \(R_\varepsilon^{-1}\) bounds the ratio of the condition numbers \(\kappa_2(A_\varepsilon R_\varepsilon^{-1})/\kappa_2(SA_\varepsilon)\) between factors tending to one uniformly over all rank-two hash outcomes. Thus the same conditional limit holds for the actual right-preconditioned matrix. Finally \(|\det((1,1),r_1)|=1\), \(|\det((1,1),r_2)|=(\sqrt3+1)/2\), and \(|\det((1,1),r_3)|=(\sqrt3-1)/2\), whose reciprocal sum is \(1+2\sqrt3\). This completes the proof.

It checks the predicted asymptotic coefficient numerically, including direct right-preconditioner computation on a representative collision. Finite enumeration and numerical limits are reproducibility checks; the above algebraic proof establishes the infinite parameter-range assertions.

## Relationship to prior work
Chen and Tan [1, §2.2, Algorithms 1–2, Theorems 3.1–3.2, §4.2] define this CountSketch and analyze conditional embedding guarantees for QR/SVD-preconditioned LSQR. Their sufficient sketch-dimension hypothesis is substantially stronger than a fixed oversampling factor; the present result does not refute it. Their reported experiments at oversampling three motivate examining a minimal tight-frame boundary and the preconditioner's *conditional* conditioning. Malik and Becker [2, Remark 1–2] already identify qualitative rank-deficiency and semi-coherence hazards for CountSketch in interpolative decomposition; Li and Liu [3, §1] prove general CountSketch embedding lower bounds using collisions. None of those inspected statements gives the three-row tight-frame rank jump or its exact conditioned QR-preconditioner pole coefficient. The difference from standard coordinate-collision examples is that the input remains asymptotically perfectly conditioned and all three informative row leverages equal \(2/3\), yet signed sums create near-singular sketches. General embedding lower bounds and this exact finite-frame analysis address different quantifiers.

## Limitations
The claim concerns an informative three-row core with optional zero padding, fixed sketch dimension, independently sampled signs/hashes and an unregularized QR factor. Its formal QR preconditioner is undefined on rank-loss outcomes. Truncated SVD, resampling, higher per-column sketch sparsity and finite precision can change the outcome. The conditional expectation diverges only in the limit as the input approaches the stated exact row dependence; no universal failure probability is claimed for arbitrary matrices or high-probability subspace embeddings. Establishing complete bibliographic priority, especially against less searchable hash-embedding examples, requires independent review.
