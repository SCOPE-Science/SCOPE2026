Under asynchronous strict local-majority updates with ties retaining opinions, one added social tie can first create absorbing polarization on five vertices; exactly two edge-marked transitions do so, with exact polarization probabilities \(7/48\) and \(31/192\). Connecting two leaves of an \(m\)-leaf star produces polarization with probability approaching \(1/4\) as \(m\to\infty\), although the original star always reaches consensus.
