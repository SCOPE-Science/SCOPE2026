For a quantum walk on an \(n\)-vertex ring with one edge weighted \(g\ge0\), every nonunit link \(g\ne1\) forces the infinite-time average-mixing matrix to have rank exactly \(\lceil n/2\rceil\) and kernel exactly the reflection-antisymmetric vectors. The exact moment determinant \(\det W=(-1)^{\lceil n/2\rceil-1}(g^2-1)^{\lceil n/2\rceil-1}\) explains the sharp odd-ring rank collapse at a perfect link and the nullspace switch for even rings.
