For size-\(j\) components in a fixed-sample random geometric graph on the circle, the thermodynamic covariance density with the total number of components changes sign exactly at \(t=\log((j+1)/2)\) for every \(j\ge2\), yielding a strictly size-ordered hierarchy; isolated vertices remain positively correlated at every positive scale. The result also gives an exact, radius-independent conditional law for the expected size-\(j\) component count given the number of separating gaps.
