The Thomas cyclic sine flow has nonzero synchronous hyperbolic sinks in infinitely many explicitly delimited, pairwise-disjoint damping intervals, with exactly two such sinks whenever one interval is entered and none otherwise. The total length of these intervals below damping \(\varepsilon\) is sharply \(\frac{9}{8\pi}\varepsilon^2+O(\varepsilon^3)\), quantifying how an infinite hierarchy of stable equilibria becomes sparse at weak damping.
