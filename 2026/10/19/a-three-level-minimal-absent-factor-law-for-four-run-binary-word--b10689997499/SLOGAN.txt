For the linear binary four-run word \(0^a1^b0^c1^d\), the complete set of minimal absent contiguous factors has an explicit ten-family classification and cardinality \(b+c+2^{\mathbf{1}_{a>c}+\mathbf{1}_{d>b}}\). The excess over the two middle-run lengths can be \(1\), \(2\), or \(4\), but never \(3\).
