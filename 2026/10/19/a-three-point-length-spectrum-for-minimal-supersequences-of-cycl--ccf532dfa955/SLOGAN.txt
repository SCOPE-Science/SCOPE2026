For the two permutations \(0\,1\cdots(n-1)\) and \(1\,2\cdots(n-1)\,0\), every minimal common supersequence at \(n\ge3\) has length \(n+1\), \(2n-1\), or \(2n\); both shorter lengths have exactly one realizing word, and the full length always occurs. This gives an arbitrarily long interval of impossible intermediate minimal-supersequence lengths.
