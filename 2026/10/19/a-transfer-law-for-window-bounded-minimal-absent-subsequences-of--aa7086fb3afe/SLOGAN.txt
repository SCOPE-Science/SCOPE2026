For periodic streams formed by a cyclic permutation of \(q\) symbols, all minimal absent subsequences from length-\(p\) windows admit a local cyclic-gap classification and an exact rational length enumerator. For the alternating binary stream, the full bivariate generating function is \(2t^2z(2+z+tz^2)/(1-tz^2-t^2z^3)\), so its minimal-obstruction count follows a Padovan recurrence and grows at the plastic constant.
