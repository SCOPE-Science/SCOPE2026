For normalized \(\alpha\)-spirallike functions, the sharp radius of exponential starlikeness undergoes an exact two-contact-to-one-contact transition at \(\lvert\alpha\rvert=\arctan((\sin1-\cos1)/(\sin1+\cos1-1))\). Above this angle the radius is explicitly \(\sin(1/2)/\cos(\lvert\alpha\rvert-1/2)\), and the sharp radius is \(C^1\) but not \(C^2\) at the transition.
