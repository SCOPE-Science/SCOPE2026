In a natural three-node conservative Winfree phase network, a global Hamiltonian coordinate change exposes an invariant two-torus with precisely two centers, three saddles and one maximum for every \(0<\varepsilon<1\). Its regular energy levels undergo an exact two-to-one transition in the number of periodic trajectories, at the explicit saddle energy \(-\log((1-\varepsilon)(1+\varepsilon)^2)\).
