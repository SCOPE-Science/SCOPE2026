For the least-squares polynomial inverse to \(1-z\) in Hardy \(H^2\), every degree has an exact \(45\)-degree outward zero-free cone based at the boundary singularity \(1\). Under magnification by the degree, all nearby zeros converge to the nonzero solutions of \(e^w=1+w\), with an explicit second-order Lambert-\(W\) correction.
