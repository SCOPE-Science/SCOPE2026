The published two-stage Gaussian RBF-RK stage coefficients and local-error-cancelling shape parameter are specialized exactly to \(\dot z=\omega Jz\) with \(J^2=-I\). The resulting map is \(aI+bJ\), whose squared energy factor and determinant coincide with \(Q((h\omega)^2)\). All signs, normalizations, frequency quantifiers, and zero-state cases are given explicitly in RESULT.md. Strict convexity of the exponential polynomial proves the unique positive energy/symplectic boundary; the derivative argument proves the unique global damping minimum. The long-time law follows from a uniform local Taylor expansion for each fixed frequency, not from a simulation. The reproduced standalone program passed 1,537 checks, including exact-rational sign enclosures and 750 direct-stage comparisons. Scientific limitation: the method's optimized shape is defined by continuous extension at component zeros; no approximation of the shape is modeled.

The 2024 preprint and 2025 journal publication already derive the RBF-RK2 stability function and draw its stability region; those are *prior work*, including qualitative harmonic stability. The reviewed contribution is the analytically sharp and unique *positive symplectic step*, the *global energy-damping minimizer*, the *incompatibility across unequal frequencies*, and the exact \(h^{-3}\) damping profile, rather than the stability function itself. The journal article's Duffing example reports bounded nonconserved energy and proposes future generally symplectic variants, but does not supply these quantified harmonic statements. The 2008 EFRK invariant-preservation paper provides conditions for a different exponentially fitted method class; its general results and distinct symplectic constructions do not directly yield the Gaussian formula's root or long-time coefficient. Targeted mathematical-record queries, older relevant Runge–Kutta literature, and the accessible previous claims showed no exact covering result. The nearest previous two-frequency symplectic-step claim applies to globally projected classical RK4 and is not a statement about the unprojected Gaussian RBF-RK2 map. Residual risk: the published stability plot qualitatively predicts the special point, and a calculation in unindexed literature may already provide its exact form. No assertion of independent priority is made.
