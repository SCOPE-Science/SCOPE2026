# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The joint telegraph/oscillator process has a unique stable stationary causal realization for positive damping. Generator equations for mixed monomials yield the exact second and fourth moments. A strictly positive denominator reduces the fourth-cumulant sign to the explicitly displayed polynomial. Its strict convexity in the switching rate and strict increase with damping establish a unique global critical damping and exactly two rate crossings below it. The embedded checker reproduces symbolic all-parameter generator solutions and independent rational examples. Numerical values of the critical pair are approximate; the qualitative theorem is analytic.

**Originality — PASS with documented residual risk.** Pawula's 1987 article already establishes general oscillator output-moment machinery for dichotomous noise and is not displaced by this finding. Its full text was unavailable; the abstract alone cannot rule out the present classification. Smith–Farago's openly inspectable 2022 article treats this very oscillator, but studies stationary large deviations in the short-correlation limit and does not establish the all-rate fourth-cumulant sign phase portrait. The distinguishing claim is the **unique positive damping threshold** and complete negative–positive–negative sequence as switching varies; the elementary possibility of calculating moments is known prior art. An equivalent older derivation remains possible and merits independent checking.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** An exact phase classification for the sign of stationary kurtosis is a natural, nontrivial property of this canonical filtered telegraph process. It is not determined by the known second moment or by the conventional boundary between overdamped and underdamped motion. The result gives a sharp testable condition for a super-Gaussian stationary position distribution and rigorously identifies its disappearance.

**Closest literature and limitations.** Pawula (1987), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevA.35.3102, full text unavailable; Pawula (1987), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevA.36.4996, abstract only; Smith and Farago (2022), arXiv:2208.06848, openly inspected full text. The conclusion applies only to stationary instantaneous fourth cumulants of a stable linear oscillator under symmetric rate-constant telegraph forcing. There is no independent scientific audit and no conclusion for nonlinear forcing, additional white noise, or the zero-damping endpoint.
