For the periodic one-dimensional box-kernel nonlocal Laplacian, the sharp worst-horizon condition number of an inverse-Poisson-plus-mass preconditioner has a unique optimal mass coefficient \(b_*=0.174777992381\ldots\), yielding \(\mathcal K(b_*)=1.700200564562\ldots\). The kernel-mass coefficient \(b=1/2\) instead has robust condition number exceeding \(4.01355\), a demonstrable factor-of-\(2.36\) gap in this precise continuum model.
