In a finite nonlinear public-goods game, the fixation probability of one cooperator can lie above neutral drift only at intermediate selection strengths: a sharp endpoint-payoff criterion guarantees exactly one positive neutrality crossing and exactly one fixation maximum. For fixed interaction-group size, the critical strength scales as \(\tau_*/N\), where \(\tau_*\) is the unique positive solution of an explicit one-dimensional integral equation; cooperation can remain favored over defection even after it becomes worse than neutrality.
