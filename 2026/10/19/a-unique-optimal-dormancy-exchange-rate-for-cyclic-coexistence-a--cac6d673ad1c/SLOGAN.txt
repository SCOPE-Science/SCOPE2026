In an active–dormant rock–paper–scissors population with matched reactivation and intrinsic oscillation frequencies, the local return to equal coexistence is fastest at a unique, finite exchange rate \(a_*=0.602354933981\ldots\), characterized by a strictly monotone quintic. Both vanishing and excessively strong exchange slow the exponential relaxation; this is a deterministic local rate result, not a stochastic fixation theorem.
