For the first Hermite-window short-time Fourier transform, every disk of area one captures at most \(1-2/e\) of a unit signal's energy, whereas an explicit equal-area annulus captures \((1-e^{-1})e^{-1/(e-1)}\) from a Gaussian input. Thus a disk-maximization principle valid for Gaussian analysis windows fails already at the first excited Hermite window; the annulus is uniquely optimal within the fixed-input concentric-annulus family.
