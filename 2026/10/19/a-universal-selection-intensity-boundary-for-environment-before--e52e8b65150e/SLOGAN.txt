In a three-player nonlinear public-goods game, drawing a common synergy/discounting environment before applying Fermi imitation creates a unique selection-intensity threshold above which defection wins at every interior population frequency, even when averaging payoffs first would favor cooperation. The critical synergy probability approaches one-half with a universal \(C/s\) correction, where \(C=\max_{z>0}z^2/(2\sinh(2z))\approx0.1380816117\).
