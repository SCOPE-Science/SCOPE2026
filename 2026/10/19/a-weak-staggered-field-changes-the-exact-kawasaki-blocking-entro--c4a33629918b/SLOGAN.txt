On an even ferromagnetic Ising ring, any nonzero staggered field weaker than the coupling selects a distinct set of zero-temperature Kawasaki-blocked configurations with an exact rational magnetization generating series. The absorbing-state entropy jumps from \(\log((1+\sqrt5)/2)\) at zero field to \(\tfrac12\log\rho\), where \(\rho^5-2\rho^4-3\rho^3-1=0\), even as the field strength tends to zero.
