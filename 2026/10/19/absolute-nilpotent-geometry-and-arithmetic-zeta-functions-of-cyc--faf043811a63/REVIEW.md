# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof starts from the exact natural-basis product and obtains the cyclic recurrence \(x_{i+2}^2=-x_i^2\). Odd-length and twice-odd-length cycles force zero in odd characteristic. In characteristic two, passing to the reduced locus converts this recurrence into a linear periodicity condition. For lengths divisible by four in odd characteristic, the two alternating parity blocks independently decompose into explicit square-root lines; their products are the stated irreducible planes. The field counts correctly distinguish whether \(-1\) is a square. The rational zeta functions follow by summing these counts over extensions. Thirty-eight small field/length cases, including \(\mathbb F_4\) and \(\mathbb F_9\), and separate formal-logarithm checks provide finite computational corroboration, not infinite-field proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
Elduque and Labra (arXiv:1312.4685v1, 2013) develop the evolution-algebra graph dictionary and algebra-wide nilpotency and automorphism theory, not this zero-square point distribution. Khudoyberdiyev, Omirov, and Qaralleh (*Few Remarks on Evolution Algebras*, Proposition 4.1) establish a general complex-field singular-matrix criterion for existence of absolute nilpotents. That prior criterion covers the mere geometric existence/nonexistence distinction and is **not** claimed as new here. Neither inspected statement implies the explicit component multiplicities, arithmetic splitting over finite fields, rational point counts or zeta factors for simple undirected cycles. Related indexed records on cycle graphical groups concern different algebraic objects and variable antisymmetric edge matrices, not the fixed adjacency-square equations. A closely related 2026 Proposition 2 on S-graphicable algebras asserts that zero-square elements lie in the annihilator; it cannot be used as covering prior work because the explicit four-cycle example \(e_1+\mathrm i e_3\) over \(\mathbb C\) disproves that universal assertion, as direct squaring shows. The classification does not rely on this conflicting statement. Targeted searches located no decisive covering theorem. An unlocated general theorem on finite-field graph-evolution zero fibers remains possible; originality is not an independent literature certification.

## Value — PASS
The zero-square fiber is an intrinsic absolute-nilpotent invariant of the graph evolution algebra, and the family of all simple cycles is a standard testing ground. The theorem supplies a complete geometric and arithmetic classification, including a natural boundary where geometrically numerous linear components have no nonzero rational points and a zeta-function signature of quadratic splitting. The exact formulas are not only a special case of complex-field existence theory: the finite-field extension arithmetic is the primary motivated gap. No downstream theorem or general-graph classification is claimed.

## Scope and remaining limitations
The theorem is set-theoretic on reduced varieties, not a statement about radicality of the original quadratic scheme. It concerns simple undirected cycles rather than directed permutation algebras or arbitrary weighted graphs. All assertions of originality are relative to inspected sources; a specialized unindexed classification could dominate the result. No independent validation has been performed.
