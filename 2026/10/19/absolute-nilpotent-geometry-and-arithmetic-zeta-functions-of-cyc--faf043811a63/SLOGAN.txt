For the evolution algebra of every undirected cycle \(C_n\), the absolute-nilpotent locus has an explicit geometric decomposition and a complete finite-field zeta function. When \(4\mid n\), it has \(2^{n-2}\) geometric plane components in odd characteristic, yet just one rational point over every \(\mathbb F_q\) with \(q\equiv3\pmod4\); over fields with \(q\equiv1\pmod4\) its point count is \(\bigl(1+2^{n/2-1}(q-1)\bigr)^2\).
