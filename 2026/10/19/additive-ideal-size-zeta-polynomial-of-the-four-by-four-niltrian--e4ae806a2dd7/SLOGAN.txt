For the strictly upper triangular \(4\)-by-\(4\) ring over every finite field \(\mathbf F_{p^m}\), the complete size polynomial of additive two-sided ideals is an explicit Gaussian-binomial expression, including ideals that are not \(\mathbf F_{p^m}\)-linear. Already over \(\mathbf F_4\) there are \(3011\) additive ideals, only \(59\) of them scalar-linear.
