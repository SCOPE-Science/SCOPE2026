For uniform random \(q\)-ary words, the numbers of distinct outputs under single deletion bursts of different fixed lengths are uncorrelated, yet their first three-length mixed cumulant is nonzero exactly when one length equals the sum of the other two. For distinct \(a<b<c<n\), that moment is \(-2(n-c)(q-1)/q^3\) if \(a+b=c\), and zero otherwise.
