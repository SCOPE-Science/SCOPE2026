# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
Every claim follows from a four-case Fourier-mask analysis, the irreducible cyclotomic polynomial of order \(p^2\), the sharp \(p\)-element cap for each frequency residue block, and injectivity of the parametrization. The proof checks primes \(p\ge5\), all functions \(h\), normalization, necessity and sufficiency of orthogonality, and exact affine enumeration. Exact-integer tests corroborate the mask formula and all small spectra; extrapolation is not used for the theorem.

## Originality — PASS (best knowledge)
The closest inspected primary papers treat distinct-prime product groups (Fallon–Kiss–Somlai 2021) and a qualitative Fuglede classification in the repeated-prime group (Zhou 2024), not this exact all-graph spectrum census. Related public findings, whose complete statements and proofs were read, classify polynomial graphs of degree two or three rather than every digit-lift graph, and their conclusions do not imply the displayed formula for arbitrary \(h\). Searches used graph, Frobenius, finite-ring, mask-zero, and lift-parameter equivalences. A hidden general spectral-transversal theorem or an unindexed equivalent count remains a residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result identifies an infinite naturally motivated family of \(p^p\) distinct spectral sets sharing the *entire* Fourier-zero geometry, not merely existence of one Fourier basis. Its unique parametrization yields exact exponential-spectrum entropy and the affine/nonaffine census for the canonical Frobenius example and all its digit perturbations. These invariants distinguish the sharply organized space of bases beyond general tile-spectral equivalence.
