For every prime \(p\ge5\), all \(p^p\) residue-digit lifts of the diagonal graph in \((\mathbb Z/p^2\mathbb Z)^2\) have exactly the same Fourier-zero set and precisely \(p^{p^2+p}\) common orthogonal exponential spectra. An explicit complete parametrization distinguishes the \(p^4-p^3\) affine spectra from a vast nonlinear family, including for the Frobenius graph \(x\mapsto x^p\).
