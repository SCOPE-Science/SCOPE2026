For every prime \(p\equiv2\pmod3\) with \(p\ge5\), the quartic graph over \(\mathbb Z/p^2\mathbb Z\) has exactly \(p^{p+1}\) exponential spectra, all explicitly parametrized by an arbitrary function on \(\mathbb F_p\). Exactly \(p^3\) spectra are affine frequency lines; all remaining ones are nonlinear.
