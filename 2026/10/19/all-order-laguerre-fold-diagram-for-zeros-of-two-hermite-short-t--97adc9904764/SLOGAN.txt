For the signal \(h_n\) analyzed with the window \(h_0+t h_n\), the short-time Fourier transform has exactly \(2n+n\sum_{j=1}^{n-1}(2\mathbf1_{\{t>\tau_j\}}+\mathbf1_{\{t=\tau_j\}})\) isolated real zeros, with explicit Laguerre-ratio fold thresholds \(\tau_j\). Every positive integer order exhibits a complete transition from \(2n\) to \(2n^2\) zeros, and each fold creates oppositely indexed pairs without changing the total signed index.
