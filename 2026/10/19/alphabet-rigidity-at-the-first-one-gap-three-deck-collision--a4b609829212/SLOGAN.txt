At the first nondegenerate one-gap three-deck collision length, every ambiguous word pair over any finite alphabet uses only two symbols: the entire length-thirteen reconstruction graph consists of exactly \(4\binom q2\) disjoint edges. Consequently the exact maximum zero-error code size is \(q^{13}-4\binom q2\), with \(2^{4\binom q2}\) maximum labeled codes.
