# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The Gaussian quadratic-form moment generating function reduces exactly to \((1-2s_2t^2-8dt^3)^{-1/2}\) for real traceless symmetric \(3\times3\) matrices. Coefficient extraction gives the full even-moment polynomial; the real-root cubic discriminant gives \(0\le d^2/s_2^3\le1/54\). The strictly positive degree-one coefficient in every polynomial for integer \(m\ge3\) proves strict shape ordering, and discriminant equality gives both complete extremizer spectra. All normalizations were recomputed, including \(\|Y\|_2^2=2s_2/15\). A separate exact-rational checker independently evaluates sphere monomials and confirms 368 tests, while the analytic proof establishes the infinite statement.

## Originality — PASS, best knowledge

Actual published-finding corpus semantic searches for spherical-harmonic sixth moments and traceless-quadratic determinant shape invariants found the close September 2026 sharp-degree-two \(L^4\) theorem (`SCOPE-sharp-l4-degree-two-spherical-harmonics--d4106097159f`). Its full proof covers the shape-invariant **fourth moment only** and does not imply ordering of sixth and all higher even moments. The original large-sieve paper arXiv:1909.01670 and the asymmetry paper arXiv:2209.00212 were checked in their primary papers, including theorems and relevant text. Neither has the claimed determinant-ordered all-even moment interval. Search did not find a covering higher-moment formula. There remains a real residual risk that older Gaussian-quadratic-form or invariant-theory literature states an equivalent formula under another name, and no global priority certificate is claimed.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The previously established equality of fourth-moment quotients across **every real quadratic spherical harmonic** on \(\mathbb S^2\) leaves an exact natural question: which real harmonic shapes first become distinguishable by higher integrability? The answer is a strict sixth-order transition and one invariant ordering **simultaneously for every higher even moment**, with explicit sharp extremizers and two rational sixth-order constants. It concerns a canonical full eigenspace, not an artificial parameter slice; it does not assert any novel extension of the known fourth-moment case.

## Limits

Only real-valued degree-two harmonics on the probability-normalized two-sphere and even integer moments are considered. Finite checks support, but do not certify, the all-order proof. An equivalent older source could narrow the originality claim. Complex-valued sharp \(L^6\) extremality and asymptotic high-degree problems remain untreated.
