For real degree-two spherical harmonics on \(\mathbb S^2\), every normalized even moment is an explicit positive-coefficient polynomial in the single shape invariant \(\det(A)^2/\operatorname{tr}(A^2)^3\). The fourth moment is shape-blind, but all even moments from order six onward strictly interpolate between sharp sectoral and zonal extremes, with the exact sixth-moment interval \([5625/1001,6625/1001]\).
