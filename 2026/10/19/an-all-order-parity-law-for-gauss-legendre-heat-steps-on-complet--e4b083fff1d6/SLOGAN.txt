For every even number of Gauss–Legendre Runge–Kutta stages, the completed heat step on every unweighted complete multipartite graph is strictly positive for every positive timestep; odd stage counts eventually produce negative diagonals whenever the graph has at least three vertices. The all-stage mechanism is the strict decrease of \((1-R_s(-x))/x\), and even stages already fail unconditionally on the four-vertex path.
