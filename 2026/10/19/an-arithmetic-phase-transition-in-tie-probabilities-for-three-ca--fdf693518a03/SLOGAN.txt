For three candidates and two seats under independent fair approval, the exact chance of a tied winning Thiele committee decays as \(n^{-1/2}\) for every fixed rational second marginal, with an explicit denominator-dependent constant, but as \(n^{-1}\) for every fixed irrational second marginal. In particular the exact leading constants distinguish Chamberlin--Courant, proportional approval, and ordinary approval voting.
