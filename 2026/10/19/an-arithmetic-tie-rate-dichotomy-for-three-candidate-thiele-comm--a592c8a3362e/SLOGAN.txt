With three candidates and two Thiele committee seats under independent fair approval, exact winner ties decay as \(n^{-1/2}\) for each fixed rational second-seat marginal weight, but as \(n^{-1}\) for every fixed irrational one. The irrational coefficient is universally \(3/\pi\); the rational coefficient is \(\frac32\sqrt{2/[\pi(p^2+q^2)]}\) for a reduced weight \(p/q\).
