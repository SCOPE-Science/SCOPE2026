For the two-stage Gauss–Legendre approximation of heat flow on the eight-vertex path, entrywise positivity holds exactly at timestep zero and on an isolated nonzero interval \([\alpha,\beta]\), with \(\alpha\approx0.317755\) and \(\beta\approx4.286368\). Eight is also the sharp path size at which arbitrarily small positive steps begin to violate the discrete maximum principle, and every longer unit-rate path has that small-step obstruction.
