For an infinite sequence of preperiodic symmetric holes converging to the discontinuity of the negative-doubling map, the survivor entropy is exactly \(\log\lambda_m\), where \(\lambda_m^{m+1}=2\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}\lambda_m^j\). The entropy deficit from the full map equals the shrinking hole's length to first order, with relative error \(O(m2^{-m})\).
