# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The root rank is uniformly distributed and conditional subtrees are independent. The exact leaf and path-length mean recurrences imply a root-split covariance source of \(-4(H_{n-1}-1)/(3n)\). Induction cancels its harmonic contribution against the accumulated subtree covariance and yields the claimed rational finite-size identity, including the boundary \(n=2\). Marginal variances establish the stated sharp correlation scale. Exact-rational moments through \(n=80\) and exhaustive permutation enumeration through \(n=8\) independently corroborate the proof; neither finite exercise proves all sample sizes.

**Originality — PASS with recorded risk.** Chern–Fuchs–Hwang–Neininger, arXiv:1501.05135, Section 5.2, particularly Theorems 5.4 and 5.6, explicitly treats the **same** binary-search-tree leaf/path-length pair and proves linear-order covariance and asymptotic independence. It leaves the dimension-one covariance coefficient as a general constant and does not display the finite-sample formula or the leading correlation constant. Devroye (1991) supplies the leaf local-counter description and a marginal leaf central limit theorem, not the joint identity. A classic path-length analysis supplies separate marginals. Closest indexed related results concern squared root imbalance versus path length, or different random-tree models. Thus the claim is a strict quantitative refinement of earlier joint asymptotics, **not** a first discovery that the pair is dependent or asymptotically independent. The full text of Holmgren–Janson's 2014 fringe-tree study could not be inspected, and an equivalent classic computation could remain implicit in earlier literature.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Leaves encode local tree shape; total internal path length is exactly the number of comparisons for standard random BST insertion/Hoare-Quicksort recursion. Their dependence cannot be understood quantitatively from separate expectations, variances or merely a decorrelation theorem. The exact covariance determines both sign and all finite-size corrections, identifies the dimension-one linear coefficient \(-1/9\), and gives a precise nonzero prefactor for the disappearing correlation at the natural \(n^{-1/2}\) rate. The proof isolates a negative root-split mechanism and a rational harmonic cancellation.

**Scope and risk.** Uniform insertion permutations only; \(n=2\) has deterministic leaf count and undefined correlation. Finite tests are corroboration, not proof. Some relevant older literature may contain implicit equivalent formulas; comparison evidence is not exhaustive.
