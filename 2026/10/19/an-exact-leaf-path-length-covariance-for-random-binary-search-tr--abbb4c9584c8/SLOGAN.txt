In a random binary search tree, the leaf count and total internal path length have exact covariance \(\operatorname{Cov}(L_n,P_n)=-[n(n+1)-6]/(9n)\), strictly negative for \(n\ge3\). Their correlation decays as \(-\sqrt{5/[18(7-2\pi^2/3)]}\,n^{-1/2}\), supplying the explicit coefficient and finite-size corrections beyond earlier qualitative asymptotic decorrelation.
