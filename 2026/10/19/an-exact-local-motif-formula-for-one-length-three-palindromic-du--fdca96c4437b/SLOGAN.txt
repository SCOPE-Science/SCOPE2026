A single length-three palindromic duplication has an exact per-word descendant count: the \(n-2\) possible sites lose one distinct output for each occurrence of just four local patterns, \(aaaa\), \(abaaba\), \(baaaaba\), and \(bbaabbab\) (with \(a\ne b\)). This four-motif rule also yields the exact mean sphere size on uniformly random words over every finite alphabet.
