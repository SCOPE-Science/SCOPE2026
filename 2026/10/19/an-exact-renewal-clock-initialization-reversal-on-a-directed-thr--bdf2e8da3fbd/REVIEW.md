# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** A renewal-epoch martingale yields the complete three-opinion fixation vector for any strictly positive unit-mean interarrival distribution; a uniform positive conditional consensus opportunity proves almost-sure finite absorption. Fubini's equilibrium residual transform and its derivative are applied with justified integrability. The Erlang-2 vector is independently reproduced by exact generator-harmonic computations and trajectory sampling in the packaged program. Finite checks do not replace the all-law proof.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** The two closest complete primary publications are Chu–Porter (2023; first public preprint 2022-08-26), which studies renewal copying and a two-agent first-event mechanism but does not give a directed three-cycle all-law winner vector or an equilibrium two-winner equality, and Takaguchi–Masuda (2011), which already uses equilibrium renewal initialization for a different *edge-triggered* voter rule. Heterogeneous Poisson voter research is a memoryless special setting. Searches for directed-cycle fixation, renewal phase initialization, and equivalent Laplace-transform laws did not reveal a covering statement. A standard renewal transform is not itself original; derivability from broad prior frameworks and unindexed treatments remains a residual priority risk.

**Value — PASS.** A directed three-cycle is the smallest natural copying network where the first event does not itself decide the outcome. The formulas quantify full three-way selection, not merely mean consensus time, for an infinite class of heavy-tailed or atomic clocks without extra moments. The equality of two distinct winners after stationary initialization and strict benchmark reversal at exactly equal mean activation rates delimit a concrete modeling choice with substantive consequences. No larger-network theorem or empirical validation is asserted.
