For the rank-one QR-normalized Gaussian generalized Nyström sketch of \(\operatorname{diag}(a,b)\), the unique Frobenius-risk-optimal positive ridge is \(\lambda_*=ab/(a^2-ab+b^2)\), independent of the number of left Gaussian test directions. This ridge cures the infinite expected squared error at one or two test directions, but on a scalar identity matrix it worsens the fidelity of the original trace.
