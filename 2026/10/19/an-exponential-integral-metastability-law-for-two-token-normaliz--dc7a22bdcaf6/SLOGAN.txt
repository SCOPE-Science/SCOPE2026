Two-token Euclidean-normalized softmax attention has a sharp deterministic escape clock: from fixed non-antipodal correlation its consensus hitting time grows like \(e^{\beta(1-c_0)}/[\alpha\beta(1-c_0^2)]\). In the critical antipodal window \(c_0=-1+z/\beta\), the normalized escape time instead converges to \(E_1(z)/(2\alpha)\), a universal exponential-integral profile on an angular scale \(\beta^{-1/2}\).
