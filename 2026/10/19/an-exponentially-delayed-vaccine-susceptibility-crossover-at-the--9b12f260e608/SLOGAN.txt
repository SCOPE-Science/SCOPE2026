At the critical reinfection threshold of a leaky-vaccination epidemic, infectious prevalence universally decays like \(1/t\), but the constant offset in cumulative reinfections jumps when vaccinee susceptibility exceeds recovered-host susceptibility. The transition has an exact profile \(\log(1-ve^{-z})-1\) on the exponentially delayed time scale \(t=\exp(z\phi/(\theta-\phi))\).
