A Brownian particle reset at a randomly refreshing exponential rate has an exact stationary density given by a positive mixture of exponentials with one isolated slow decay mode. In the slow-refresh limit, this mode controls the ultimate spatial tail at rate \(\delta\sim\mu\) but carries mass only \(A\sim(L/\mu)^2e^{-\gamma-L/\mu}\), while the mean-square-displacement rate behaves instead as \(L/\log(L/\mu)\).
