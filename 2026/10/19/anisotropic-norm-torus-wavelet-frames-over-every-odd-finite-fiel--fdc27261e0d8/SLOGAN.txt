For every odd prime power \(q\), an explicit spectral graph in \(\mathbb F_q^2\) meets each anisotropic norm level exactly once, giving a norm-one cyclic dilation tiling and a single-generator Parseval wavelet frame on the mean-zero subspace. Its \(q(q+1)\) atoms form \(q+1\) orthogonal regular simplices, with exact Gram spectrum \(1^{[q^2-1]}\) and \(0^{[q+1]}\); the construction works in particular when \(q\equiv1\pmod4\).
