Among all \(2048\) binary order-five de Bruijn cycles, the number of whole-period Abelian-square cuts is always even and takes exactly the values \(2,4,\ldots,22\), with sharp extrema realized by \(300\) and \(8\) cycles. The complete distribution, symmetry-orbit counts, mean and variance follow from two exhaustive Eulerian-circuit enumerations.
