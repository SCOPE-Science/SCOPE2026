# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The all-size result follows by exact Fourier diagonalization, the two-site cancellation congruence, an involution-orbit count with its fixed-point correction, and a finite-dimensional dissipative spectral decomposition. The formula includes odd and even rings, the all-simple generic-flux spectrum, positive unequal sink rates, and the exact long-time trace claim. The standalone checker independently evaluates the two-sink spectral nullspaces in 5,307 small instances, enumerates the congruence counts at larger sizes, and checks eight representative finite-time evolutions. These computations are consistency checks, not replacements for the proof. Risk: the model is restricted to ideal homogeneous hopping and strictly positive sink rates.

## Originality — PASS, subject to bibliographic limitations
The strongest direct baseline is Parlangeli and Notarstefano's arXiv:1109.3556, §IV.B (read in full), whose ordinary-cycle two-observer theorem covers the zero-flux part. The novel claim is the **complete magnetic phase classification** and especially its exact half-flux parity-controlled switching. The full physical text of Wang et al. (DOI:10.1088/1674-1056/20/2/020303) was compared: flux-driven dark interference is known there for three- and four-quantum-dot transport, but their statements do not give the all-size two-loss formula. Kassal and Aspuru-Guzik (arXiv:1201.5202) study a different, single-trap scenario; Razzoli et al. (arXiv:2011.13794) provide a general trapping-subspace method. Liu and Ji (DOI:10.1002/rnc.3870) treat unweighted path/cycle controllability. Neither targeted published-finding corpus comparisons nor primary-text searches found the precise magnetic two-loss enumeration. Risk: unseen signed-cycle observability literature could contain an equivalent half-flux formula; the full text of Liu and Ji was not available in the consulted material.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result answers a physically motivated all-size quantum-transport question with a sharp tuning law, not an arbitrary finite table: two permanently lossy sites may jointly fail to absorb a walker, and half a flux quantum can change the exactly persistent fraction in opposite directions determined by the sink-separation arithmetic. For even rings with odd reduced circumference the dimension increases by one; for even reduced circumference every dark mode vanishes. The loss-rate-independent asymptotic survival is a direct benchmark for coherent quantum pumping and sensor/sink placement. The exact zero-flux classification is explicitly acknowledged as known; the value lies in the flux-dependent distinction. Finite-time crossovers and robustness to disorder remain open.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
