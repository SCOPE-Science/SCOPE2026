# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The finite Gabor Fourier transform diagonalizes the projector Gram matrix with precise factor \(N\). The new prime-square part uses an exact stationary-phase sum and the rigorous fact that two roots of unity of odd order cannot cancel. The characteristic-prime cubic case is covered by a collision argument using a quadratic conic count. All shift/frequency congruence classes are disjoint and exhausted. Exhaustive exact cyclotomic tests at four primes, together with direct Gram eigenvalue comparisons, match the claimed normalizations and ranks. The finite checker does not substitute for the all-prime proof.

**Originality — PASS (best knowledge).** Bojarovska–Flinth's finite Gabor injectivity criterion and Alaifari–Wellershoff's ambiguity identities are established prior work. Their inspected relevant full-text passages do not give the all-cell quartic prime-square classification or rank formula. Published results for cubic chirps and for graph-indicator windows use different generators; they do not imply this specific quartic phase-window theorem. The close later paper arXiv:2609.09614 could only be inspected at abstract level, and an older waveform-design equivalent remains a bibliographic risk. Search failure is not offered as proof of absolute novelty.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** This is a motivated complete classification of linear Gabor measurement directions at the first prime-power ramification, with exact operator-kernel dimension. The asymptotic rank fraction changes from \(1\) to \(1/2\), and a sharp prime-mod-3 correction survives. It supplies information useful for choosing phase-coded sensing windows, without claiming nonlinear phase-retrieval failure or noise robustness.

**Scientific limitations.** Only cyclic orders \(p\) and \(p^2\) for primes \(p\ge5\) are handled. The full positive-eigenvalue multiplicity distribution is not simplified beyond the explicit ambiguity-sum spectrum. Independent validation has not been performed.
