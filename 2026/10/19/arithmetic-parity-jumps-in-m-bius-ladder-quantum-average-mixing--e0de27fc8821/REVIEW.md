# Scientific review: Arithmetic parity jumps in Möbius-ladder quantum average mixing

## Correctness — PASS
The statement fixes \(n\ge3\), the unweighted cubic Möbius ladder, and Cesàro averaging. The spectral projector identity reduces average mixing to a circulant, nonnegative Fourier counting problem. Same-parity equal energies yield only even modes. For opposite parity, an algebraic-integer norm argument exhausts all rational-angle cosine differences equal to one; its hypotheses and conjugate bounds hold at every ladder size. The remaining two configurations yield precisely the three- and two-mode exceptions. The exact finite checker independently builds cyclotomic equal-energy classes and verifies 118 orders; it is corroboration rather than an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with limits
The foundational average-mixing literature [1,2,4] establishes the projector decomposition and general computation, not the explicit Möbius-ladder congruence classification. The rank-focused paper [4] provides a commutant formulation and cubic-graph rank tables, but no Möbius family characterization. The Möbius-device paper [3] studies the same physical topology, its Hamiltonian spectral effects, transmission and decoherence, not this long-time average-mixing Fourier rank. The adjacency spectra of ordinary prisms and cycles do not imply the opposite-parity collision classification of \(S+S^{-1}+S^n\). A stronger, unlocated theorem on spectra of cubic circulants or twisted ladders might cover the result. The literature search and full-text comparisons do not establish exhaustive priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The twisted-ladder lattice is a physically motivated hopping geometry [3], not an arbitrary parameter slice. Its generic rank defect depends on the topology of antipodal bonds, while rare size congruences activate precisely classified Fourier modes. The exact all-size dichotomy gives a reproducible boundary for long-time quantum transport and requires a nontrivial cyclotomic rigidity lemma. It is a structural classification rather than a small finite table.

## Scope and follow-up
No arbitrary rung weights, magnetic fields, site potentials or finite observation-time claims. Numerical and exact finite checks are not treated as the proof. Stronger unlocated circulant theorems, and physical conventions involving additional onsite terms, remain comparison risks.
