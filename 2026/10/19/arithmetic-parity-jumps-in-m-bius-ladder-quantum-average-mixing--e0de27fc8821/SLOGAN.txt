The average-mixing rank on a unit-coupling Möbius ladder with \(2n\) vertices is exactly \(n\), except for a jump of three when \(n\equiv3\pmod6\) or two when \(n\equiv2\pmod4\). An algebraic-integer norm obstruction classifies every extra Fourier mode, so the entire nullspace is explicit for all \(n\ge3\).
