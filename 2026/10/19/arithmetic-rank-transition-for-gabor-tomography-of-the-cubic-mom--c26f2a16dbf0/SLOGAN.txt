For the normalized indicator of the cubic moment curve in \(\mathbb F_p^3\), the full Gabor-projector operator rank is exactly \(p^3[p(p-1)+1]-Z_p\), with \(Z_p=p-1\) or \(p^2-1\) according as \(p\equiv1\) or \(2\pmod3\). Every lost Weyl direction is classified by missing curve differences or by a prime-field cubic permutation polynomial.
