For the one-sided Poisson shift on \(\ell^2(\mathbb Z)\), exactly \(q\) temporal samples on the sublattice \(q\mathbb Z\) are always injective, but stable except at the explicit arithmetic times \(\pi m/\sin(\pi d/q)\). The sharp frame bound is asymptotic to \(t^{2(q-1)}/\binom{2q-2}{q-1}\) near zero and vanishes quadratically at every positive resonance, with an exact coefficient when \(q=2\).
