At every positive temperature, the zero-field susceptibility of a ferromagnetic Ising ring with a prescribed number of two bond strengths is minimized exactly by a balanced (mechanical/Christoffel) cyclic arrangement, even though its zero-field partition function is independent of bond order. For equal numbers of strong and weak bonds, macroscopic segregation increases the limiting susceptibility per spin by precisely \((x-y)^2/[(1-x)(1-y)(1-xy)]>0\), with \(x,y\) the respective hyperbolic tangents of the bond energies.
