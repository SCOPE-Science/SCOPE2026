For every generalized quaternion group of order \(4\cdot2^k\) with \(k\ge2\), the nullity of any four-term convolution having two taps in each cyclic coset is determined by two differences, their parity and dyadic gcds. Exactly half asymptotically have nullity one, the average nullity is \(k+\tfrac12+o(1)\), and the maximal nullity \(3\cdot2^k\) occurs exactly on the order-four subgroup cosets.
