# Verification

The reproducible standalone checker `verify_balanced.py` requires Python 3, NumPy and SymPy. Run `python verify_balanced.py` from the extracted package directory. The recorded standard output in `verify_output.txt` is regenerated by this exact script.

The checker evaluates 80 independent real-matrix versus product-formula determinants over degrees one through five and randomly perturbed distinct nodes; performs 28 rank comparisons between trigonometric sampling and an explicitly generated Hermitian measurement matrix, including resonant cases; and performs five exact symbolic checks in degree two using rational coordinates and an algebraic rotation. The maximum observed relative determinant error is under \(8.0\times10^{-13}\). The special four-plus-four degree-two examples have exact ranks seven and eight and determinant squares zero and 256, and an exact non-phase-equivalent ambiguity when angular products coincide.

The general determinant, optimality, image dimension, and uniqueness statements are proved algebraically in `RESULT.md`. Finite numerical experiments do not prove them. Numerical rank uses a declared tolerance and is not a certification of arbitrary exact configurations; that role is supplied by the proof. No independent validation has been conducted.
