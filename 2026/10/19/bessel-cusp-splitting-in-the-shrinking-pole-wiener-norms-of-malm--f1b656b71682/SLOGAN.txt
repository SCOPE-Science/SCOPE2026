For the Malmquist–Walsh rational family with shrinking pole parameter \(\lambda=c/n\), the Wiener norm converges uniformly on bounded \(c\)-ranges to the Bessel absolute-sum profile \(\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z}|J_k(2c)|\). Every positive integral-order Bessel zero produces a cusp, and a cusp from order \(m\ge1\) splits into two signed-Fourier-mode zero crossings separated by exactly \(m c_{m,r}/n+O(n^{-2})\), with a complete first-order norm boundary-layer formula.
