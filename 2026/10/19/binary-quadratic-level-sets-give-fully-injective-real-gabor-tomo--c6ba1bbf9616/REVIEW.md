Leopardi's 2015 work treats quadratic bent functions and real monomial Clifford representations but not the claimed lifting spectrum. Two particularly close published mathematical results were inspected in full: the odd-characteristic hyperbolic Gabor theorem excludes characteristic two, while the binary-quadric Walsh sampling theorem concerns only a fixed first-degree character band, not full real intensity lifting. Explicit scientific database searches for aliases, symmetric projector span, bent-support Gabor measurements, and projective designs returned no stronger or equivalent claim. The absence of a match is **not** presented as a uniqueness proof; priority risk remains, including possible general group-covariant moment identities hidden in other terminology.
