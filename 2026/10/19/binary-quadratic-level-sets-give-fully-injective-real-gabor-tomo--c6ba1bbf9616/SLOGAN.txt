Indicators of nondegenerate binary quadratic level sets yield real Gabor projectors spanning every symmetric matrix in dimensions \(N=2^{2m}\) for \(m\ge2\), with exact three-band lifting spectrum and sharp condition number \(2^m+\rho\). Precisely at \(N=16\) with six-point support, the entire \(256\)-vector orbit becomes a real projective \(2\)-design; no other member of this quadratic-level family does.
