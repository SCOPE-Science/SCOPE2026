# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The one-dimensional classification follows from explicit equations for square-zero and idempotent elements. For every plane, the displayed quadratic form is zero on that plane if and only if the plane is totally isotropic for its polarization; ranks two and three of this form give an exhaustive plane classification over arbitrary fields of characteristic different from two. The Frattini and Boolean conclusions follow from explicit intersections. Finite-field exhaustive tests over 22 parameter configurations independently reproduce the classification, but the unbounded field statement rests on the symbolic proof.

**Originality — PASS, with bibliographic risk.** The full text of Hu–Wen's motivating paper gives the idempotent structure and the characteristic square ideal but not this lattice. The 2025 Frattini paper develops general definitions, criteria and selected classification cases but not the exact Hu–Wen family. The earlier published automorphism result at \(\lambda=4\) does not imply the Boolean lattice or Frattini calculation. The closest unresolved risk is an unadvertised case-by-case plane census implicit in older three-dimensional evolution-algebra classification tables.

**Value — PASS.** The result identifies all ordinary subalgebras in a recently important non-solvable family, including those without a natural evolution basis. The sharp Boolean/dually-atomistic and Frattini transitions connect recent idempotent-free counterexamples to the established Frattini theory. These are natural invariants motivated before calculation, rather than arbitrary parameter slices. The restrictions to nonzero parameters and odd characteristic are explicit.
