For a three-dimensional positive-definite Lanczos inverse-covariance model with one trace-matched bulk direction, the two directed Gaussian KL losses are exactly \(\frac12\log(1+\delta)\) and \(\delta-\frac12\log(1+\delta)\), where \(\delta=c^2(ab-c^2)/(b^2+c^2)^2\); remarkably, neither depends on the strength of the resolved-to-complement coupling. Their sharp bounds depend only on the *uncorrected* complement condition number, even along families whose coupling-corrected Schur condition number diverges.
