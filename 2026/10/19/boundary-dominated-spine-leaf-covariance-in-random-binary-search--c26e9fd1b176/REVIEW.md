# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The exact rankwise covariance formulas follow by expressing extreme-key spine membership as prefix-minimum events among independent priorities and internal leaves as adjacent-rank local maxima. Relative-order independence leaves only three possible nonzero interactions per interior leaf; their conditional integrals give a strictly negative rational expression. The two endpoints are computed separately and telescope to the stated positive aggregate covariance. The conditional regression formulas follow by extracting the first two coefficients from an exact root-split generating-function recurrence. Exhaustive permutation comparisons through eight keys and rational tests through 1000 agree. The finite checks corroborate rather than replace the proof.

## Originality — PASS (self-review)
The 2014 Holmgren–Janson text supplies the priority construction, leaf means and fringe-function limit theory. The 2005 Drmota–Hwang text addresses covariance across *depth profiles*, rather than rank-indexed leaf indicators jointly with minimum-key spine length. Martínez–Roura (1998) gives the uniform root split and random-tree model. The present rankwise boundary–bulk covariance classification and nonmonotone conditional regression are not directly implied by the inspected statements. A possible residual risk is an older record/permutation-pattern identity equivalent to the displayed local formula; independent priority assessment has not been performed.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The dependence of search-path depth on fringe leaf abundance is a natural joint shape statistic, not an arbitrary parameter slice. The theorem resolves the complete finite-size sign profile at *every key rank*, explains the globally positive sign by a single boundary contribution even though all other rankwise covariances are negative, and disproves the tempting inference that a positive global covariance entails monotone conditional regression. Its bounded \(1/12\) limit contrasts with the logarithmic path and linear leaf-count scales.
