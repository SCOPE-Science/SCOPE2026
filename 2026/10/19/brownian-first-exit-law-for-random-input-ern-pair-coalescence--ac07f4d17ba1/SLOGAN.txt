For any macroscopic pair of states of a Černý automaton, the random-input coalescence time divided by \(n^3\) converges to a Brownian exit time, with diffusion coefficient \(p^2(1-p)\) when the cycle letter has probability \(p\). This gives the complete limiting law and shows that the unique bias \(p=2/3\) simultaneously minimizes all limiting coalescence-time quantiles.
