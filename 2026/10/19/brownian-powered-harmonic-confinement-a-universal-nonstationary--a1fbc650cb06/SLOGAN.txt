A particle confined by a harmonic stiffness proportional to \(|B_t|^p\) has a universal long-time thermal-position law: \(t^{p/4}X_t\) converges to a Gaussian divided by \(|G|^{p/2}\), with exact tail index \(2/p\). Brownian-squared stiffness yields a Cauchy limit even though all finite-time moments exist, and self-normalizing by the observed stiffness environment recovers a Gaussian limit.
