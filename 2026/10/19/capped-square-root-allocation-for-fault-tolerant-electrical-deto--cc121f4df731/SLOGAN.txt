On any bridgeless cactus with arbitrarily priced conductances, minimizing the worst post-failure resistance between the endpoints of a deleted edge has a unique closed-form optimum: every cycle obeys a capped square-root price law and receives budget proportional to its squared local threshold factor. A cycle with one exceptional edge price \(t\) retains equal optimal conductances throughout the sharp interval \(0<t\le(n-1)/(n-2)\), despite the unequal prices.
