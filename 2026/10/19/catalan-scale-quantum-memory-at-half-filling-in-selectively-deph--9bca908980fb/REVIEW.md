# Scientific review: selectively dephased Johnson walks

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof identifies the exact monitored-to-unmonitored inclusion matrix. Its rank follows from the Boolean-lattice up/down commutator, valid at the square central case and both non-square sides. The identity \(B^*B=kI+A(J(n-1,k))\) makes every dark vector an energy-\(-k\) eigenvector, so all dark density-matrix coherences are stationary. The Hilbert–Schmidt dissipation identity forces every stationary operator to commute with the individual monitored projectors; connectivity of the monitored Johnson subgraph reduces the bright block to a scalar. Nonzero imaginary eigenvalues are excluded by the monitored diagonal and cross-block equations. These steps prove a true ordinary limit for every initial state and every strictly positive set of rates. Independent matrix constructions in `verify_johnson.py` passed 15 small cases and 14 additional Catalan binomial identities. Tests do not replace the infinite-family proof.

**Originality — PASS within the inspected literature, with residual risk.** The general Lindblad invariant-subspace and stationary-state machinery is established by Baumgartner and Narnhofer (arXiv:0806.3164); Johnson eigenspaces and their multiplicities are established by Filmus (arXiv:1406.0142); coherent Johnson quantum search is treated by Wong (arXiv:1601.04212). Those building blocks are explicitly prior. The inspected manuscripts do not give the monitored-coordinate Lindblad fixed algebra, exact return law, or adjacent half-filling exponential/polynomial jump. Search terms covering monitored slices, incidence kernels, spectral equivalents and dark states produced no decisive stronger coverage. General background plus known harmonic dimensions makes the derivation approachable in hindsight; novelty is asserted only for the combined dynamical statement, not for incidence rank or Lindblad commutant principles. Unindexed or differently named equivalent results remain possible, and independent bibliographic review is outstanding.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** In this naturally highly connected, non-bipartite quantum-walk family, single-coordinate selective site monitoring turns the top Johnson harmonic representation into a fully coherent memory space, even though the protected Hamiltonian energy is \(-k\), not zero. The exact Catalan multiplicity at the central threshold and the sharp change from exponentially small to polynomially small long-time return under an adjacent family-size change quantify a spectral memory transition that is not visible merely from knowing the Johnson adjacency spectrum or generic dephasing principles. The theorem supplies complete parameter and initialization formulas rather than an arbitrary isolated instance.

**Closest literature and limits.** Wong's 2016 Johnson search Hamiltonian has a marked-vertex oracle, not site dephasing of an entire Johnson face. Filmus's 2014 work establishes the harmonic rank and eigenspaces without open-system time evolution. Baumgartner and Narnhofer establish the general finite-dimensional stationary-state framework, but do not calculate this Johnson-family phase boundary. A broader equivalent specialization hidden in the literature remains a risk. Finite-time relaxation uniform in graph size, unequal hopping weights, collective dephasing, and robustness to Hamiltonian disorder are not proved.
