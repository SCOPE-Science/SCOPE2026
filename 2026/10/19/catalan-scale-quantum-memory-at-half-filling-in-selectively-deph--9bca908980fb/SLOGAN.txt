For a Johnson-graph quantum walk dephased on every subset containing one distinguished element, the protected subspace is absent at \(n=2k\) but has Catalan dimension \(C_k\) at \(n=2k+1\). This single-step half-filling change turns long-time local return from exponential decay into an exact \(k^{-2}\)-order law, while preserving arbitrary coherences in the dark sector.
