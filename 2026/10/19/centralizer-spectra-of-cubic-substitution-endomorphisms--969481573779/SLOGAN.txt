For every finite field, the unital coefficient-field-linear endomorphism monoid of \(\mathbb F_q[t]/(t^4)\) has an explicit complete centralizer-size spectrum and commuting-pair polynomial. Its center has exactly \(8\), \(6\), or \(2\) elements as \(q=2\), \(q=3\), or \(q\ge4\), respectively; the entire length-four monoid is commutative only at \(q=2\).
