The rank-one weight-zero Rota–Baxter operators on the eight-dimensional three-generator exterior algebra admit an exact finite-field count determined by the lowest exterior degree of their image. Their locus has dimension \(9\) in odd characteristic but \(10\) in characteristic two, with \(2689\) such operators over \(\mathbb F_2\) and \(58658\) over \(\mathbb F_3\).
