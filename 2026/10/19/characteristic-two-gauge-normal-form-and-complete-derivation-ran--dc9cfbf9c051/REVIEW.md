# Scientific review: characteristic-two filiform derivation ranks

## Correctness (C): PASS

The theorem specifies an alternating Lie bracket in every characteristic-two field, precisely \(n\ge4\), and **all** derivations. Applying the derivation equation to \([e_2,e_3]=0\) forbids the otherwise possible \(e_1\)-component of \(D(e_2)\), and induction then supplies the complete block form. For the \(a=0\) block the rank is controlled by the valuation of \(w\) and whether \(v\) is in its image. For \(a\ne0\), the formal recursively solvable gauge equation is proved and diagonalizes the operator into independent even and odd blocks after conjugation. The respective valuation counts \(W_\ell(j)\) are exact at every cutoff, and summing gives the total cardinality \(q^{2n-1}\). Exhaustive finite examples, a separately enumerated binary derivation-identity test, and 960 gauge identity tests corroborate these logical steps; they do not substitute for the general proof.

## Originality (O): PASS, with named risks

The classic model bracket and characteristic-zero derivation normal forms are antecedents, not new contributions. The contribution assessed is the full characteristic-two finite-field **rank multiplicity distribution of every derivation**, obtained through a parity-separated gauge transform, not ranks of inner derivations or existence of a derivation basis. published-finding corpus's closest adjoint-rank-spectrum item concerns \(\operatorname{ad}_x\) as the algebra element varies and therefore does not imply counts for all derivations; its related characteristic-two Leibniz item concerns a different non-Lie algebra. Full relevant primary material from Bahturin–Goze–Remm (2013), Tsartsaflis (2017), and Sheng–Liu–Liu (2024) was compared for statement-level implication. The 1996 Goze–Khakimdjanov monograph was not inspected in full; a disguised truncated-polynomial gauge or modular derivation-rank theorem could predate this result. No database search is regarded as a proof of uniqueness. Detailed query and source comparisons are recorded in AUDIT.json.

## Value (V): PASS

Rank-stratified derivation counts are a natural canonical invariant for a central maximal-class nilpotent Lie algebra family. The complete infinite-parameter polynomial answers all ranks; the characteristic-two splitting has genuine structural content; the sharp \(\mathbb F_2\) nonexistence versus larger-even-field positive invertible fraction is dimension independent. This is stronger than an isolated finite rank or routine restatement of the well-known derivation normal form.

## Verification boundaries

Only finite fields of characteristic two and \(n\ge4\) are covered. The formula concerns derivations, not automorphism orbits, odd characteristic, or scheme structure. The finite program is a reproducibility check, not an infinite proof. Literature uniqueness and independent mathematical validation are unproved.
