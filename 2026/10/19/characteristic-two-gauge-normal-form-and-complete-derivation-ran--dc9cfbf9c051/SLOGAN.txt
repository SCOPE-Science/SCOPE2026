Every derivation of the model filiform Lie algebra over a characteristic-two finite field has an explicitly enumerable rank, obtained by a nonlinear gauge transformation that splits the truncated-polynomial operator into even and odd resonance blocks. In particular, for every dimension \(n\ge4\), the proportion of invertible derivations is exactly \((q-1)(q-2)/q^2\), so it vanishes over \(\mathbb F_2\) and is dimension-independent over larger even fields.
