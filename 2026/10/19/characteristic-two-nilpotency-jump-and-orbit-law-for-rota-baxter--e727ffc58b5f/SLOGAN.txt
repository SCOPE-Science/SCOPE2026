In characteristic two, every weight-zero Rota–Baxter operator on the square-zero local algebra \(K\oplus K^n\) is a square-zero endomorphism with an arbitrary marked vector, so its nilpotency index can jump from two to three. Up to algebra automorphisms there are exactly \(2n+1\) orbits when \(n\) is even and \(2n\) when it is odd, and over every fixed finite field of characteristic two almost all such operators have nilpotency index three, with an explicit sharp exponentially small exception probability.
