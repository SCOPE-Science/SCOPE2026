Over every characteristic-two field, all weight-one Rota–Baxter operators on the nilpotent chain evolution algebra \(e_i^2=e_{i+1}\) form explicit Frobenius-geometric support families determined by a single column. Over \(\mathbb F_q\), the exact total is \(q^2+2(n-2)q(q-1)\), and every operator rank has a closed formula.
