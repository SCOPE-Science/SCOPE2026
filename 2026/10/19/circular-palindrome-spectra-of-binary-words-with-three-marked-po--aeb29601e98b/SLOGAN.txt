For every binary circular word with exactly three ones, its number of distinct palindromic factors of length at most one circuit is determined exactly by the three intervening zero gaps. At all lengths \(n\ge14\), the sharp restricted maximum is \(2n-2-\lceil n/3\rceil\); precisely one, two, or one dihedral classes attain it according as \(n\equiv0,1,2\pmod3\).
