Every finite binary period-doubling word of order \(n\ge3\) has minimum circular-attractor size \(2\) and at least five explicit distinct optimum pairs, transported by a cardinality-preserving morphism rule. The five pairs are proved to be the complete set of optima for \(3\le n\le13\), including the length-\(8192\) word; uniqueness beyond this finite range is not asserted.
