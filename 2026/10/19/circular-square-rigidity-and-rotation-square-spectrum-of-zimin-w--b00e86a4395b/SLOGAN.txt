Every finite Zimin word \(Z_n\) has exactly \(n-1\) circular square occurrences, one of each form \(Z_kZ_k\) with \(1\le k<n\), and no cyclic factor of exponent greater than two. Among its distinct rotations, exactly one is squarefree, the numbers containing exactly \(j\) squares are \(2^{j+1}\) for \(1\le j\le n-2\), and exactly two rotations contain all \(n-1\) squares.
