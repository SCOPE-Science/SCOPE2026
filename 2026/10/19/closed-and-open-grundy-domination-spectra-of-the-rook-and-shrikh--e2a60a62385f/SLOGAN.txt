The two nonisomorphic strongly regular graphs with parameters \((16,6,2,2)\), namely the rook and Shrikhande graphs, have **different complete closed-neighborhood Grundy domination sequence polynomials** but **identical complete open-neighborhood polynomials**. Their ordinary Grundy domination numbers are \(6\) and \(7\), whereas both Grundy total domination numbers equal \(6\).
