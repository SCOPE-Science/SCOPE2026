# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The zero edges form one r-cycle; the one-loop fixes the state phase for any word having a marker. Thus the only discrepancy in the trace is replacing r lifts for r|n (zero otherwise) of the zero word by one. The identity and the two nontrivial cycle-cover terms give det(I-tM)=1-t-t^r; the trace correction gives the displayed ordinary zeta. A nonzero reflected word has a nonempty marker list in a fixed half. Interior distances contribute z/(1-z^r) and sequences of them give C. At a vertex end either the site is a marker or the nearest mirrored markers are separated by 2h with 2h=1 mod r; at an edge end the distance is 2h+1 and 2h=0 mod r. In particular even r forbids a zero at a fixed vertex of any nonzero word. Odd r admits h=(r+1)/2: the odd example has gaps 1 and r+1, the even example two gaps r+1. The half spans are m for odd and vertex–vertex types and m-1 between the extreme representative sites for edge–edge; this accounts for the extra z in E, while the vertex–vertex constant term is deleted. The zero word must be restored once at each actual period. The purely rotational subgroup of period n has index 2n; there are n subgroups of index n generated by that rotation and one reflection, with one conjugate type at odd n and equally many of each at even n. This gives the stated Lind normalization. At r=2, put z=t² and D=(1-z)(1-z-z²); the reflection exponent numerator over D is t(2-z-2z²)+z(2-z-z²), agreeing with the published expression. The independent replay enumerated all binary words for r=2..8 through n=15, checking both reflection parities and ordinary counts against the rational coefficients; using i↦k−i instead of i↦−k−i does not change counts, since these are conjugate by a rotation.
