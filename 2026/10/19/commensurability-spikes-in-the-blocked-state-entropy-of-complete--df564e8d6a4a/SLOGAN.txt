On complete bipartite antiferromagnetic Potts graphs, allowing only strictly downhill single-site recolourings produces high-energy blocked states precisely when both part sizes are divisible by the palette size; allowing two-site recolourings eliminates all of them. With fixed \(q\ge3\) colours and equally sized parts, the blocked-state entropy per spin alternates between \(\log q\) on divisible sizes and \(\frac12\log\lfloor q^2/4\rfloor\) otherwise, so its full-size limit does not exist.
