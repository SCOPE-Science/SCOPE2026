# Review of the commuting-idempotent scheme classification

**Correctness — PASS.** The construction splits each idempotent diagonal into scheme-theoretic zero/one strata without any assumption on characteristic. Mutually commuting idempotents give orthogonal spectral projectors; a column-by-column polynomial conjugator and the triangular factorization of the unipotent incidence group produce an explicit polynomial affine slice with one coordinate for each comparable pair having unequal diagonal colors. These arguments work functorially over every commutative base algebra, not solely over finite fields. The graph-coloring to Fortuin–Kasteleyn identity follows by a direct edge expansion; interpolation across \(2^r\) and all prime powers establishes the Tutte equivalence in the connected case. Four-vertex star/path orientations and the three-vertex classification verify sharpness in the asserted class. The accompanying checker exhaustively reproduces the individual diagonal fibers of commuting idempotent pairs over two fields for several posets, including one non-tree example.

**Originality — PASS, with literature qualifications.** Primary comparisons include Brion's 2013 idempotent conjugacy/smoothness theorem, the 2022 incidence-monoid idempotent-variety classification of Bilen Can–Casimiro–Malheiro, Lemma 8 on orthogonal idempotents in Santulo–Souza–Yasumura (2019), and Sokal's classical 2005 Potts/Tutte identity. Their established ingredients are not claimed as new; none of the inspected statements gives the all-arity *scheme-theoretic* affine normal form, its exact converse as a Tutte invariant, and the minimal height-one incidence-algebra nonreconstruction threshold. Closely relevant 2024 work by Kolegov on idempotent generators is only accessible in abstract form and has a distinct object; that limited access and possible stronger unlocated treatments are residual risks. No inference of novelty is drawn merely from failed search.

**Value — PASS.** The result is a complete structure theorem for a natural moduli scheme in arbitrary characteristic, with a precise graph-polynomial bridge and a minimal obstruction to associative-algebra reconstruction. The Tutte equivalence clarifies exactly what the complete family of unmarked schemes remembers. The star/path witnesses show that increasing arity does not recover orientation even when all schemes are known.

Limitations: the isomorphisms are generally noncanonical; the reconstruction threshold is restricted to connected height-one posets; related idempotent literature may contain an unstated or stronger implication. Finite-field checks are evidence of implementation consistency rather than proofs of universal statements.
