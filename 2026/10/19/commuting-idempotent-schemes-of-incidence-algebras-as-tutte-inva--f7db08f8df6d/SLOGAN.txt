Every commuting-idempotent tuple scheme of a finite incidence algebra is a disjoint union of affine spaces indexed by \(\{0,1\}^r\)-colorings of its comparability graph; the dimensions count bichromatic edges, and all finite-field counts specialize its Tutte polynomial. Distinct four-point height-one incidence algebras of star and path type consequently have identical tuple schemes in every arity and characteristic.
