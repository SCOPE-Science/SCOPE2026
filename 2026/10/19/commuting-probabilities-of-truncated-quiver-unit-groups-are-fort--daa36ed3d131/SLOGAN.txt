For the unit group of every finite radical-square-zero quiver algebra over \(\mathbb F_q\), the complete commuting-pair count is \(q^{|E|}Z_H((q-1)^2,q-1)\), so the commuting probability and conjugacy-class number depend only on the undirected multigraph. Directed and transitively oriented triangles furnish nonisomorphic groups over every prime field \(\mathbb F_p\) with \(p\ge5\), yet have equal values of both invariants.
