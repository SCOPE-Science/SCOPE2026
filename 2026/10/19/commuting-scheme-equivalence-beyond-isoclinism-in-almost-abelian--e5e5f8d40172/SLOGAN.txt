For every field, the commuting schemes of all tuple lengths in an almost-abelian Lie algebra depend only on the rank of its defining endomorphism; their two geometric components and exact finite-field joint-centralizer rank polynomials are explicit. In particular, two five-dimensional nilpotent Lie algebras of different nilpotency class, not even isoclinic, have linearly isomorphic commuting schemes at every arity.
