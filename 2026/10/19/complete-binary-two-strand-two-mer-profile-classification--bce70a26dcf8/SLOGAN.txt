For a pooled length-two substring spectrum from two unlabeled binary strands of equal length \(n\ge2\), the exact number of reconstructible observation profiles—and hence the optimal zero-error code size—is \(5n^2-8n+6\) for even \(n\) and \(5n^2-8n+4\) for odd \(n\). A complete flow-imbalance classification explains the parity correction and the exceptional all-constant profiles.
