For prime cyclic groups of order \(p\ge7\), every pair of exactly three-sparse complex signals attaining the known sharp cross-STFT support bound \(5p-4\) is characterized: their supports are progressions with a common step, and their coefficients lie in an explicit family parameterized by three sampled Fourier-root choices and one free nonzero complex number. For normalized consecutive supports the minimizers form exactly \(p^2(p-1)/2\) connected copies of \(\mathbb C^\times\); the numerical lower bound itself is prior work.
