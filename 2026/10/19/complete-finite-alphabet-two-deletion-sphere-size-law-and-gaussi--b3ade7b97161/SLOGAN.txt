For every alphabet size \(q\ge2\) and word length \(n\ge2\), a two-state polynomial transfer gives the exact distribution of the number of distinct descendants after two deletions. A nonbinary return-pattern correction yields an explicit variance and, for fixed \(q\), a Gaussian limit on the \(n^{3/2}\) scale; the previously known binary variance is recovered when \(q=2\).
