The proof partitions ordered pairs by the two scalar cross-contractions, constructs all rank-one and rank-two outputs, counts each oriented fiber and the zero fiber, and independently counts every similarity class. Exact matrix enumeration over the four fields of sizes \(2,3,4,5\) verifies all coefficients and normalizations. The algebraic proof is uniform in the finite-field order.
