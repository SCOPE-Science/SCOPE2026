For every finite field, the fixed-space dimension distribution of all automorphisms of the free class-two Lie algebra on three generators is given by a finite seven-degree polynomial obtained from explicit simultaneous eigenvalue tables in \(\mathrm{GL}_3(\mathbb F_q)\). The fixed-point-free coefficient is exactly \(q^{12}(q^6-3q^5+q^4+4q^3+q^2-3q-8-\epsilon_q)\), with \(\epsilon_q\) recording the odd-characteristic scalar \(-I\) exception.
