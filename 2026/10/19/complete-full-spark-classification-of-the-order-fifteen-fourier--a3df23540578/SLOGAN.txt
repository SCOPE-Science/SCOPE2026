For the order-\(15\) discrete Fourier matrix, every nonempty proper selection of rows is full spark exactly when its residues are balanced modulo \(3\) and \(5\) and neither it nor its complement belongs to five explicit exceptional affine orbits. The classification gives \(2310\) such frames and exposes a five-row failure of divisor balance that vanishes at six rows and reappears at seven.
