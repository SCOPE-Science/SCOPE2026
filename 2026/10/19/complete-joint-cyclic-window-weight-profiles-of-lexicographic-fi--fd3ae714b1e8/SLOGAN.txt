In the natural binary-index ordering, \(\mathrm{RM}(1,m)\) has only \(2^{m-1}+2\) distinct complete cyclic \(b\)-window weight profiles across all window lengths, although it has \(2^{m+1}\) codewords. Aside from two singleton and one doubleton profiles, every profile occurs exactly four times, governed by a two-scale dyadic zero-run classification.
