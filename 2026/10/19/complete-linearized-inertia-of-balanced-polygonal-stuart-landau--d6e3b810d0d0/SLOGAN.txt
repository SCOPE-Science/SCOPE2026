Every noncollinear balanced polygon in an all-active, purely dissipative mean-field Stuart–Landau network has exactly two unstable directions, \(M\) stable directions and \(M-2\) geometric neutral directions, for every \(M\ge3\). Collinear polygons instead obey an exact one-parameter inertia trichotomy, including a sharp change in unstable dimension at \(\tau=1\).
