# Scientific assessment of the count

**Correctness.** Complete mappings fix zero. The two local permutation conditions force a five-bit parametrization of all labeled complete mappings of \(B_2(C_2)\), giving exactly 32. A direct enumeration of the possible product bijections also gives 32.

**Relationship to prior work.** The even-index existence criterion and the displayed construction are credited to the theory of Araújo, Bentz, Cameron, Hendrey, and Kinyon (arXiv:2608.25092v1), with the coordinate rule written as an explicit specialization of their Theorem 7.5. Proposition 6.5 of the same work counts a different, five-element Brandt semigroup over the trivial group. The cited construction does not enumerate all labeled complete mappings of the nine-element semigroup. The count alone is the distinct claim; another unindexed enumeration could overlap.

**Value and scope.** The count is the full Cayley-table transversal count at the first admissible Brandt index over \(C_2\), whose group table has no transversal. The five free bits describe all labeled maps in this case. No count for other even indices or for automorphism classes is asserted.
