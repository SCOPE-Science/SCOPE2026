In the group algebra of \(C_2^5\) over every even-characteristic finite field, the regular-module multiplication ranks of all augmentation elements are exactly \(0,1,2,4,6,8,10,12,16\), with explicit multiplicities. The first genuinely mixed quadratic–cubic rank jump is from \(8\) to \(10\) and occurs precisely when the bivector has rank two and its wedge with the cubic term is nonzero.
