The 16 binary order-four de Bruijn cycles have an exact cut-attractor spectrum: among their 256 linear unfoldings, 192 need four marks and 64 need five. Every cycle has 8, 12, or 16 four-mark cuts, with cycle multiplicities 4, 8, and 4, respectively; the unique possible four-mark set is \(\{4,8,12,16\}\).
