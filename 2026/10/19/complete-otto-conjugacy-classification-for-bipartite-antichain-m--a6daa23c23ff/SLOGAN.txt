The Otto-conjugacy classes of a bipartite antichain incidence monoid are exactly products of one collapsed class and projective-point classes, independently in every maximal-vertex column. This resolves the Otto part of a published classification question: over finite fields the idempotents form precisely one whole class only over \(\mathbf F_2\), and over \(\mathbf C\) their class also contains nonidempotent units.
