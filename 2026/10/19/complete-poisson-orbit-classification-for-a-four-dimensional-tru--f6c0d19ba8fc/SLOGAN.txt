For the four-dimensional algebra \(\mathbb F_q[x,y]/(x^2,y^2)\), ordinary Poisson brackets form exactly \(3q\) isomorphism classes when \(q\) is even, represented by \(\{x,y\}=zxy\), \(x+zxy\), and \(1+zxy\) for every \(z\in\mathbb F_q\). When \(q\) is odd only brackets \(\{x,y\}=zxy\) occur, with \(z\) identified with \(-z\), giving \((q+1)/2\) classes.
