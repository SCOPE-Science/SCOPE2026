The three-dimensional parabolic map \(F(x,y,z)=(ay^2,by(1-z),c(1-x))\) has exactly zero, one or three primitive two-cycles, according as \(b(c-1)\le1\), \(1<b(c-1)\le2\), or \(b(c-1)>2\). Its symmetric cycle is exponentially attracting exactly for \(1<b(c-1)<(3+\sqrt5)/4\), whereas both additional cycles always have an expanding complex Floquet pair.
