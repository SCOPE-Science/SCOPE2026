The complete Jordan partition of left multiplication by every positive-degree element in \(\bigwedge(\mathbb F_q^4)\) admits an exact six- or seven-stratum enumeration, with explicit multiplicities summing to \(q^{15}\). Odd characteristic permits three-step nilpotents and distinguishes \(\ell\wedge\omega\) from the Pfaffian rank of a two-form; in characteristic two every such multiplier squares to zero.
