For Ostrovski's resonant polygonally foliated square maps, normalized area on the outer annulus has an exact piecewise-quadratic rotation-number distribution; the reciprocal rotation number has a sharp \(1/u\) tail, so its mean is infinite and precisely its moments below order one are finite. The unnormalized tail coefficient grows as \(4K^2-\frac{32}{3}K+\frac{128}{45}+O(K^{-1})\) along the resonant sequence.
