For every graphical group over a finite field of characteristic two, the complete prescribed-square fiber distribution is governed by multiplicative edge-label consistency, bipartite components, and a residual graph's independence polynomial. The exact number of distinct squares is \(\sum_{W}(q-1)^{|W|-b(W)}\), over induced vertex sets with no isolated vertices, where \(b(W)\) counts bipartite components; the exact number of elements of order dividing two is \(q^{|E|}I_\Gamma(q-1)\).
