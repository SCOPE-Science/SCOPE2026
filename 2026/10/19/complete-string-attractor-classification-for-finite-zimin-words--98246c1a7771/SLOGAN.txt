For every finite Zimin word \(Z_n\), a position set is a string attractor exactly when it includes at least one occurrence of **every** letter; no further factor constraints are needed. Hence the complete attractor-size polynomial is \(\prod_{j=1}^{n}((1+t)^{2^{n-j}}-1)\), and the number of smallest attractors is \(2^{n(n-1)/2}\).
