For the stationary integer-degree Chebyshev map, every three-observation ordinal probability at every integer lag is an explicit rational function of \(q=b^m\), with separate even and odd formulas. The time-reversal total variation never vanishes at finite lag and is asymptotic to \(2b^{-2m}\) for even degree versus \((2/3)b^{-2m}\) for odd degree.\n
