# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The Fourier transform gives exactly the coordinate product \(E_\chi E_\psi=\delta_{\chi,\psi}E_{\chi^2}\). The associated square map is a permutation, so vanishing of products of distinct natural-basis elements forces every algebra isomorphism to be monomial. The resulting permutation-conjugacy classification is reconstructed from all fixed-point counts, and the odd-prime lifting-the-exponent identity translates those counts exactly into the truncated exponent profiles. Both directions of the square-Wieferich boundary are proved explicitly. The automorphism order follows by counting the permutation centralizers and all compatible root-of-unity scalars. The standalone certificate verifies 150 directly enumerated small groups, 193 same-order pairs, seven Fourier algebra identities, and two exhaustive monomial automorphism cases. These checks are supplemental; no finite test is used as an infinite proof.

## Value — PASS

This is an exact reconstruction theorem and a sharp boundary for the entire family of finite connected medial commutative midpoint quandles. It explains both algebraic rigidity and explicit failures, not merely isolated calculations. The obstruction at Wieferich-square orders is rare and mathematically motivated, and the full automorphism group follows uniformly. For example \(C_{1093^2}\) and \(C_{1093}^2\) have nonisomorphic underlying quandles but isomorphic complex algebras, with a fully specified 3,282-cycle witness.
