For finite odd-order abelian groups, two complex midpoint quandle algebras are isomorphic exactly when multiplication by two has the same cycle structure on their underlying groups. This invariant determines every group of a fixed odd order precisely when that order has no squared Wieferich-prime divisor, and it gives explicit algebra-isomorphic but quandle-nonisomorphic examples starting with \(C_{1093^2}\) and \(C_{1093}^2\).
