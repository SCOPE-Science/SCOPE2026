# Scientific review: composition-flat binary three-universality

**Correctness — PASS.** The statement covers every nonnegative length and every admissible Hamming weight, with the boundary case separated explicitly. Three-complete-arch equivalence follows from a greedy subsequence-matching argument, so no unproved infinite-pattern assertion is hidden. Unique arch factorization yields \(R(1+A+A^2)\), and coefficient extraction gives exactly two one-arch words and \(3(n-2)-2\) two-arch words in the interior. A direct independent checker enumerates all \(131071\) binary words through length sixteen and tests each of the eight subsequence patterns; all \(153\) length/composition pairs agree. Computation is corroborative only.

**Originality — PASS, with bounded residual risk.** Adamson's arXiv:2304.04583 gives a length-only counting algorithm; the full published result titled *Closed-form law for binary subsequence universality* gives the complete length/universality-index bivariate distribution and therefore its length-only three-universality counts. Both are materially compared, and neither statement specifies the count in **every fixed Hamming-weight layer**. The two-variable generating function and its cancellation exhibit a uniform deficiency across all nonboundary compositions that cannot be recovered from a length-only generating function by specialization. The general DFA counting theorem in arXiv:2311.10658 provides an algorithm capable of evaluating constrained counts, but not this all-parameter closed identity. The more recent arXiv:2610.06717 concerns existence/universality and approximate single-pattern occurrence within fixed binary Parikh-matrix fibers; its abstract does not state the identity, but its full text was unavailable and remains a bibliographic risk. No comprehensive independent novelty determination is claimed.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Fixed-composition sampling is a natural constrained-word model; the exact probability of containing all length-three scattered patterns is not determined by the length-only law. The absolute number of failures remaining constant while the number of ones ranges from three to length minus three is a genuine structural feature, not a single tiny finite improvement or arbitrary table entry. The formula handles infinitely many lengths and all compositions, and gives a sharp exact boundary where the probability becomes zero. It does not address target length four or larger alphabets.

**Limitations.** Existing arch theory is used as context rather than claimed as a discovery. Asymptotic distributions, algorithmic optimality, and independent verification are not asserted. A related 2026 publication was accessible at abstract level only.
