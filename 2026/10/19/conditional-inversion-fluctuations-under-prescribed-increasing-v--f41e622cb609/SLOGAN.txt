For a uniform permutation conditioned on prescribed adjacent increasing consecutive-value runs, the full inversion law is a weighted inversion law on the forced blocks, with an explicit general variance formula. A single long forced run has identical conditional mean at all positions but a quadratic variance profile: at macroscopic length, endpoint fluctuations have a non-Gaussian uniform limit on the \(n^2\) scale while centrally placed runs have vanishing fluctuations on that scale.
