The Kaplan–Yorke stationary fiber has exact conditional third centered moment \(\frac{3\alpha^5}{4}(\frac1{1-\alpha^3}+\cos(2\pi x))\): it is strictly positive for positive contraction but changes sign on a sharp fraction of fibers for negative contraction. Even though conditional means and variances cannot distinguish \(\alpha\) from \(-\alpha\), global vertical variance together with the third moment recovers the signed parameter.
