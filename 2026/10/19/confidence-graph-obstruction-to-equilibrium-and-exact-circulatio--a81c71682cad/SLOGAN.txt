Uniform independent opinion noise does not make finite bounded-confidence copying reversible: every confidence graph with both permitted and forbidden opinion pairs forces a stationary cycle affinity \(\log(1+m(1-p)/p)\) on every admissible social graph. For one-neighbor imitation on complete social contacts, only empty and complete confidence graphs are reversible; in the original smallest three-opinion system the count-chain entropy production is exactly \(4p(1-p)\log((3-2p)/p)/27\), uniquely maximized at \(p\approx0.291985\).
