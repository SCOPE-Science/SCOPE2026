The class-three algebra group \(1+J/J^4\) of the oriented four-cycle has conjugacy classes of exactly the sizes \(1,q^2,q^4,q^6\), with an explicit uniform class-size polynomial over every finite field. This even-exponent phenomenon is unique among fourth-truncated oriented cycle algebra groups: three vertices permit a class of size \(q\), and every cycle with at least five vertices permits a class of size \(q^3\).
