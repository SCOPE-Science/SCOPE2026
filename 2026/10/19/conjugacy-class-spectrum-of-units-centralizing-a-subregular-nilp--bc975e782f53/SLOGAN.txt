For the invertible centralizer of every nilpotent Jordan matrix of partition \((m,1)\) over \(\mathbb F_q\), all internal conjugacy classes have size \(1\), \(q(q-1)\), or \(q^2\), with exact multiplicities for every \(m\ge2\). Its commuting probability is exactly \((q^2+1)/(q^3(q-1))\), independent of the Jordan-block length.
