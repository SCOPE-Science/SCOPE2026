The unit group of a height-one unicyclic incidence algebra depends, as an algebraic group, only on its number of edges \(m\) and unique cycle length \(c\), and over \(\mathbb F_q\) it has exactly \(q^m+(q-2)q^{m-c+1}\) conjugacy classes. Thus nonisomorphic incidence algebras can have identical unit groups, while for \(q>2\) the unit group's order and class number recover both graph parameters.
