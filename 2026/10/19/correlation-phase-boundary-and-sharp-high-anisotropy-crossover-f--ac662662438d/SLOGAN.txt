For one-probe Gaussian range finding on a real \(2\times2\) diagonal matrix, the exact squared Frobenius error has a sharp covariance-correlation phase boundary: correlation helps, hurts, or is neutral according to which singular direction carries greater sketch variance after scaling. With equal probe marginals and singular-value ratio \(\kappa\), the error remains bounded as \(\kappa\to\infty\) exactly when \(\kappa\sqrt{1-\rho^2}=O(1)\), with critical limiting value \(2+\tau\).
