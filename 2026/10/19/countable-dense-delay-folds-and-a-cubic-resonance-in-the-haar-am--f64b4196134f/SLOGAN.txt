For the canonical Haar wavelet, the delay parameters at which two real Doppler ambiguity zeros merge form a countable dense subset of \((0,2/3)\), yet the joint ambiguity nodal curves remain nonsingular. At the sharp boundary \(\tau=2/3\), infinitely many nonzero cubic Doppler zeros coexist with one quadratic zero at zero frequency; every Doppler zero at larger overlapping delays is simple.
