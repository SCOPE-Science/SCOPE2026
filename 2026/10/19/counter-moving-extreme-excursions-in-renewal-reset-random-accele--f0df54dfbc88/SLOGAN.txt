Under heavy-tailed renewal resets of a randomly accelerated particle, the probability of moving toward the reset point conditional on arbitrarily large positive displacement stays nonzero: it is exactly \((2-\sqrt3)/4\) when the equilibrium renewal-age tail exponent is \(3/2\). The complete conditional reversal curve decreases from \(1/6\) to zero with the tail exponent, while joint displacement–velocity extremes remain asymptotically dependent.
