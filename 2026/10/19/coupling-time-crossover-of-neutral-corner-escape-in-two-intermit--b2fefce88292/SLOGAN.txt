For two diffusively coupled Pomeau–Manneville maps, the rare neutral-corner survival constant has a sharp crossover when \(n\varepsilon_n\to\rho\): it equals the survival area of an explicit coupled blow-up ODE. Its coefficient increases from \(a_p^2\) without coupling toward \(2a_p^2\) under faster coupling, so every fixed positive coupling doubles the leading area constant while leaving the power-law exponent unchanged.
