# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
For symmetric nonnegative weighted graphs, equation (3) is an exact Lyapunov dissipation identity. At and below the positive generalized eigenvalue threshold, equality is possible only at consensus because vanishing repulsion-edge differences together with \(H_*y=0\) imply \(L_Ay=0\). This proves global convergence at the nonhyperbolic boundary independently of numerical trajectories. In the simple-kernel case the Taylor cubic projection is an edge fourth-power sum, strictly positive by attraction-graph connectivity. A one-dimensional center manifold and its strong-stable foliation yield the sharp \(t^{-1/2}\) law and exceptional exponential set. For the three-agent path, the transformed system exactly splits into \(\dot p=-p\) and \(\dot q=-(q-\tanh q)/3\); the inverse-square expansion yields the \(-2/5\) logarithm. Exact algebra and an independent numerical convergence check are supplied in CHECK.py. Risks: standard center-manifold foliation assumptions require a simple neutral mode; that assumption is explicit.

## Originality — PASS
The closest primary paper, Boyce et al. (arXiv:2512.05725; https://doi.org/10.1007/s00332-026-10333-0), already supplies the model, the strict-spectrum criterion, the identical-graph equality case, and the path threshold \(1/2\). Those are acknowledged as existing results. Its full text, including theorem, section 3.2 and Appendix C, does not supply the global semidefinite-boundary theorem for arbitrary different graphs or the claimed vector asymptotics and logarithmic correction. General odd-edge nonlinear consensus papers (arXiv:2008.12022 and arXiv:2002.08408) address equilibrium structure, linear stability and other particular systems; they do not state the present tanh-model critical time laws. Signed-Laplacian threshold theory (arXiv:1408.2187) is prior mathematics, not a novelty claim. Relevant indexed literature searches found no stronger implication. Residual risk: unpublished or unindexed general nonlinear-gradient literature could contain a formulation subsuming the method; novelty remains subject to outside expert review.

## Value — PASS
A zero eigenvalue is precisely where the existing strict spectral theorem ceases to decide the behavior of mixed attraction and repulsion topologies. Closing the boundary establishes stability without an artificial spectral gap, while the exact \(t^{-1/2}\) coefficient predicts the timescale of persistent disagreement, and the exceptional exponential set distinguishes trajectories invisible to the linear criterion. The first logarithmic correction in the source's natural three-agent path topology gives a reproducible, nontrivial quantitative refinement. This is a motivated critical-transition result for a specified opinion model, not a claim about observed social behavior.
