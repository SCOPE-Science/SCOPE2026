At the exact spectral boundary of a two-topology nonlinear opinion model, every initial condition still converges to consensus, but a simple critical mode generically slows convergence to the sharp \(t^{-1/2}\) law with a computable edge-energy coefficient. For a three-agent path with repulsion between the endpoints, the inverse-square disagreement has the further universal correction \(-\frac25\log t\), while one codimension-one set converges exponentially.
