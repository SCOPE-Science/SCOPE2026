## Mathematical value (PASS)

The motivating phase diagram marks where linear relaxation vanishes, but it does not quantify how finite populations fluctuate on those boundaries. The theorem determines the natural fluctuation magnitude, number of rounds and non-Gaussian limiting law for the actual reshuffled population; its \(1/6+2a^2\) variance records nontrivial dependence caused by grouping without replacement. Exact duality answers the corresponding alternating instability without assuming a second independent approximation. These are meaningful stochastic predictions for a recently published social-interaction model, not an arbitrary isolated parameter value. Limits: no finite-chain invariant convergence, off-critical crossover or real-world inference.
