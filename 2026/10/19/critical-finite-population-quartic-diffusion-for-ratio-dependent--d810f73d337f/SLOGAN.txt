For uniformly reshuffled finite triplets with ratio-dependent contrarian decisions, the two deterministic critical boundaries have a sharp \(N^{-1/4}\) fluctuation scale and \(N^{1/2}\) time scale, converging to a quartic diffusion with variance \(1/6+2a^2\) at the non-flipping boundary. The critical flipping boundary is exactly conjugate, at every population size, to the non-flipping boundary after alternating opinion complementation.
