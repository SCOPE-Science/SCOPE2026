In an exactly solvable rational Chebyshev hierarchy, the natural invariant measures approach an endpoint atom with an exact Cauchy-square boundary layer but a sharp stationary-moment transition at exponent \(q=1/2\). The critical logarithm has a universal, locally uniform crossover function \((1-e^{-s})/s\), separating core-dominated from rare-bulk-dominated observables for every fixed degree \(N\ge2\).
