For the generalized Bacciotti oscillator \(\dot x=y^3-x^5\), \(\dot y=-x+my^9\), the previously uncomputed threshold \(m=1/3\) is repelling: its first nonzero local return displacement is a strictly positive beta-function constant times \(\rho^{22}\). Just below threshold, an unstable limit cycle is born with exact amplitude exponent \(1/14\) and a physical period diverging with the reciprocal exponent.
