For the split local algebra \(k\oplus V\) with \(V^2=0\), weight-zero Rota–Baxter operators in characteristic two can have nilpotency index three, unlike the characteristic-zero square-zero classification. Every operator is determined by a square-zero map and one vector, with exact finite-field counts and only \(2n\) or \(2n+1\) conjugacy classes when \(\dim V=n\), according to the parity of \(n\).
