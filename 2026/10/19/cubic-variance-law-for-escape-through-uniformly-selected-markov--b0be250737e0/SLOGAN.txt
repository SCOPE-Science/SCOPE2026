For a uniformly chosen length-\(n\) Markov hole in \(x\mapsto bx\bmod1\), the escape-rate variance has the sharp rare-orbit law \(\operatorname{Var}(\Gamma_n)\sim C_b b^{-3n}\), with \(C_b=\sum_{p\ge1}a_b(p)b^{-2p}\) determined by primitive symbolic periods. The typical escape rate is asymptotic to the hole size, but a sparse family of short-period holes controls its much larger ensemble fluctuations.
