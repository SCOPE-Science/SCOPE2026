In a natural planar contraction switching across \(y=x^p\), every positive-height orbit switches infinitely often but only \(\log T/\log(p/(p-1))+O(1)\) times by time \(T\). The power \(p\) fixes the universal return-time growth factor \(p/(p-1)\); at the classical parabolic boundary \(p=2\), the switching times eventually satisfy an exact affine doubling recurrence.
