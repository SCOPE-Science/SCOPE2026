For degree-\(2n-1\) Gaussian Fock polynomials, the D-optimal normalized value-and-derivative sampling design on one circle consists of a regular \(n\)-gon at a uniquely determined radius, with an exact two-by-two Fourier-block singular spectrum. Relative to the optimal design using \(2n\) distinct normalized value samples, the determinant of the jet design is asymptotically \(e4^{-n}\) as large.
