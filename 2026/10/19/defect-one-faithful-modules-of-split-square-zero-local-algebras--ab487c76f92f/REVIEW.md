# Review of the defect-one faithful-module classification

**Correctness (C): PASS.** The dimension bound follows from the injective action of the radical on the top into the socle and the sharp quadratic product bound. The three possible profiles, frame-orbit sizes and hyperplane rank counts follow from explicit linear algebra; the critical pointwise-stabilizer lemma is established using an invertible matrix in every hyperplane and a sharp dimension bound for nonscalar matrix centralizers. The block commutation equations independently yield the stated endomorphism rings. Reproducible prime-field computations supply checks but are not treated as a general proof.

**Originality (O): PASS under checked-source best knowledge, with residual risk.** Classical Schur–Jacobson theory already implies the minimum faithful degree, and it is expressly excluded as an original standalone contribution. Bergman's arXiv:1310.5365 analyzes minimal faithful Artinian modules and bounds on top and socle; Bleher–Chinburg–Huisgen-Zimmermann's arXiv:1407.3045 analyzes radical-square-zero representation varieties. The inspected material does not establish fixed-source-algebra counts by projective hyperplane-normal rank, nor the particular commutant-dimension dichotomy. Semantic searches for equivalent presentations, numerical small-field counts and stronger classifications found no decisive covering claim. A specialized matrix-space orbit classification could give an alternative published implication, so that residual risk remains open and human verification is warranted.

**Value (V): PASS.** The radical size \(s^2-1\) is the first below the classical maximal square-zero block capacity on \(2s\) dimensions. The result classifies the entire smallest faithful-module family, rather than a chosen subclass, and records exactly how module endomorphism algebras jump between the three naturally forced profiles. Exact counts over every finite field retain the action of the fixed algebra and supply natural representation-theoretic invariants. The novelty is not asserted to lie in the old degree bound or standard Gaussian matrix counting alone.

The proof applies uniformly across characteristics and to all positive integer \(s\ge2\); it does not classify arbitrary higher-dimensional modules. The independent status remains unperformed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
