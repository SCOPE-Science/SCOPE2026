At each column matching incoming rim colors force matching outgoing rim colors; unequal incoming colors force the outgoing pair to be reversed. The identical-rim family is counted by the trace of the nth power of the 3-color transition matrix with zero diagonal and unit off-diagonal entries (eigenvalues 2,-1,-1). For an omitted color occurring r>0 times in identical rims, deletion leaves r corresponding pairs of paths: spokes join their matching endpoints and not their interior vertices, yielding r bichromatic components. An omitted color absent from the rim instead gives two intact rims. In the opposite-rim family a color pair using the spoke color is one spanning cycle: alternating rim steps with spokes advances one column per rim step in the same direction and returns only after n such steps. Excluding the spoke color gives the two rims. Exactly three cycles contain the anchor, and different remaining cycles yield different neighbors because their changed edge sets distinguish them. This proves the full multiset and the handshake formulas. At n=3 there is one isolated vertex; at n=4 the merged degree-one multiplicity is three, with the other vertex of degree three.
