For every circular ladder \(C_n\square K_2\), the vertex-fixed three-edge-Kempe graph is \((n-3)\)-regular when \(n\) is odd; when \(n\) is even, it has exactly two exceptional colorings of degrees \(n-1\) and \(1\), with all other degrees \(n-3\). The theorem determines every vertex and edge count by a rim-color recurrence and identifies both exceptional coloring geometries.
