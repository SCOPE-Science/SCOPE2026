For positive three-digit planar dyadic Sierpiński measures, the centered Fourier-zero density is generically \(2/3\), but drops to \(\frac23(1-4^{-h})\) at a countable dense family of odd-torsion mask resonances, where \(h\) is the first signed doubling period. At the triangle-inequality boundary the density becomes \(1/3\), and beyond it there are no real Fourier zeros; resonances have zeros of arbitrarily high Taylor order.
