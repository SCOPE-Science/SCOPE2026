For every \(n\ge2\), \(s\ge2\), and field of characteristic zero or greater than \(n+1\), the reduced commuting-\(s\)-tuple variety of derivations of \(k[t]/(t^{n+1})\) has exactly \(\lceil n/2\rceil\) explicit rank-one determinantal components. Their dimensions and finite-field point counts are exact, and these varieties become non-equidimensional exactly when \(n\ge3\) and \(s\ge3\).
