When trimers are deposited on an alternating one-dimensional lattice, turning on an arbitrarily weak arrival rate on the previously inactive parity changes the exact terminal vacancy density discontinuously, from \(1/4+3e^{-2}/4\) to the one-sided limit \(1/4-3e^{-2}/4\). A closed three-variable empty-interval system proves the noncommutation of weak-flux and infinite-time limits.
