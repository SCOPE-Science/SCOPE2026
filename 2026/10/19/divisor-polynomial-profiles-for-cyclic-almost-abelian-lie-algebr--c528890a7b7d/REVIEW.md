# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact statement is restricted to \(m\ge4\), monic \(f\ne t^m\) over every finite field, with the Lie bracket specified. The proof partitions subalgebras by whether they project onto the one-dimensional quotient; stable subspaces correspond bijectively to monic divisors. For ideals, the additional necessary and sufficient condition is containment of the image of multiplication by \(t\). A pair with a nonzero exterior coordinate generates one exterior line plus the cyclic module of its determinant-like vector \(av-bu\), allowing an exact fiber count. Unit classes modulo divisors determine every possible dimension, and their total is \(q^m\). The Gaussian and Euler-totient formulas follow without characteristic restrictions. Reproducible independent brute-force enumerations of eleven small prime-field configurations agree in all three distributions. These experiments are checks; the proof is universal. The nilpotent controls are known prior cases, not included in the new theorem's novelty scope.

## Originality — PASS, subject to specific residual bibliographic risk

The closest exact Lie zeta results are Lee (arXiv:2010.02268, Theorems 3.1 and 3.5) for nilpotent maximal-class algebras, isomorphic to the explicitly **excluded** action \(f=t^m\), and Lee's arXiv:2602.07254 for all solvable algebras of dimension at most four. The present claim covers all remaining cyclic adjoint polynomials, unbounded dimensions, repeated factors, and both invertible and mixed actions; none of the inspected formulations implies the complete profile of all generating-pair dimensions. Brent--Gao--Lauder (2003) give the underlying classical Krylov module-generation probability; it is credited, and the new assertion is not novelty of that probability alone. Jezernik--Zozaya (arXiv:2606.31747) study random generation of simple special-linear Lie algebras, not this solvable family. Closest semantically retrieved public claims concern lower-dimensional plane counts, affine Lie free-algebra growth, and filiform adjoint ranks, none with these objects and invariants. A potentially equivalent unpublished or unindexed classification is a residual risk; unsuccessful search does not prove absolute novelty.

## Value — PASS

This is a natural complete algebraic classification of two Lie-subobject zeta polynomials and the full dimension spectrum of two-generated Lie subalgebras in an infinite, unbounded-dimensional family motivated by recent finite-field zeta and random-generation research. It works uniformly for irreducible, repeated-root, reducible and mixed nilpotent/non-nilpotent cyclic actions. The structural gap for irreducible actions and the exceptional additional ideals for a singular action are exact and distinctive consequences, not arbitrary slices or a small numerical improvement. The proof is elementary module theory, and no separate downstream theorem is asserted or needed for this natural invariant classification.

## Boundaries and independent review

The proof does not treat noncyclic modules. The finite tests do not establish the theorem alone, nor do they check all extension fields. An equivalent known theorem under module-theoretic or Lie-zeta terminology remains possible. The single-model findings have not been independently verified.
