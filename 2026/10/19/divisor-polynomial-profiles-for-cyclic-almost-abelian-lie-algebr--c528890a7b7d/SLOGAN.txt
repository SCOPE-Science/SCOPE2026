For every nonnilpotent cyclic almost-abelian Lie algebra \(\mathbb F_qh\ltimes\mathbb F_q[t]/(f)\) of dimension at least five, monic divisors of \(f\) determine all subalgebra and ideal codimension counts and the full ordered-pair Lie-generation dimension distribution. An irreducible action forces a sharp generation gap: only dimensions \(0,1,2\) and the full dimension occur.
