For degree-at-most-three spherical harmonics sampled at the twenty dodecahedral vertices, every erasure-pattern rank is determined by the bipartite components of a five-vertex graph arising from the Petersen antipodal quotient. Exactly \(2960\) four-vertex erasures permit full reconstruction, with the sole distinct-antipodal-pair obstructions being four-cycles and a triangle plus disjoint edge of \(K_5\).
