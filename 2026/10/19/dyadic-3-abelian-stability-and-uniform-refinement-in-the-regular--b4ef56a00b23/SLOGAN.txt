At every dyadic length \(2^k\) with \(k\ge5\), the regular paperfolding word has exactly 36 distinct \(3\)-Abelian factor classes, and each of its twelve \(2\)-Abelian classes splits into exactly three finer classes. The classification follows from an exact 24-state reduction of length-three factor statistics; the preceding dyadic length sixteen has 32 classes.
