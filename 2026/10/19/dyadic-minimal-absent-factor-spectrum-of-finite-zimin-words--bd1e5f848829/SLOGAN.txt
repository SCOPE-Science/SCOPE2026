Finite Zimin words have a sparse, entirely dyadic minimal-absent-factor length spectrum: the only lengths are \(2^j+1\), with exactly \((k-j-1)^2+1\) factors at each such length. The full antidictionary over the word's exact \(k\)-symbol alphabet consists of \(k+k(k-1)(2k-1)/6\) explicitly classified words.
