Every three-by-three Fourier minor on \(H\times\mathbb F_p\) is nonsingular when \(H\) is a finite abelian \(2\)-group, \(p\) is odd, and both the row and column triples have distinct prime-factor coordinates. The obstruction to cancellation survives arbitrary dyadic phase twisting, even though the complete composite-group Fourier matrix has many singular minors.
