Eight arbitrary intensity measurements—five at distinct points of one circle, and three at distinct points of a different concentric circle—recover every complex quadratic up to phase. The Hermitian lifting has exactly the nonsingular one-dimensional kernel \(\mathbb R\operatorname{diag}(R^2,-1-R^2,1)\), and eight is optimal among all complex-linear intensity measurements in dimension three. The exact best rank-two kernel-angle gap is \(\min\{1,R^4\}/[2(R^4+R^2+1)]\).
