For nonnegative functions on the Boolean cube supported at exactly six sites of a three-dimensional subgroup and with nonnegative Walsh transform, precisely twenty local Fourier-support masks are possible, not all \(255\) nonempty masks. An exact eight-vertex convex polytope controls them, and their cardinalities are \(3,4,5,6,7,8\), scaled by \(2^{d-3}\) in ambient dimension \(d\).
