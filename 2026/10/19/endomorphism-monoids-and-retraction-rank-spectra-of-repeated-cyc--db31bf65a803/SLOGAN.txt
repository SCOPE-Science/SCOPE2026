The full endomorphism monoid of \(r\) identical cyclic evolution algebras of length \(\ell\) has \((1+r\ell\gcd(q-1,2^\ell-1))^r\) elements. Its composition-idempotent maps have exactly \(\binom rk(1+k\ell\gcd(q-1,2^\ell-1))^{r-k}\) retractions of rank \(k\ell\), yielding every possible retract type at once.
