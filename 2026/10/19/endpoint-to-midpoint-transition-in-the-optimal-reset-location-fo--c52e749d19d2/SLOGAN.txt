For a reflecting Brownian interval with Poisson resetting back to the chosen starting point, the global mean-cover-time optimum jumps directly from the two endpoints to the midpoint at the exact dimensionless reset rate \(\rho_c=[\operatorname{arcosh}((1+\sqrt{17})/4)]^2\). A distinct lower spinodal \(\rho_s=[\operatorname{arcosh}(\sqrt{1+1/\sqrt5})]^2\) creates a metastable midpoint before the globally optimal location switches; no off-center interior location ever minimizes.
