For every fixed Taylor degree, quadruple-angle recovery of cosine and sinc admits inputs with arbitrarily small seed error that yield an exactly energy-preserving \(-I_2\) oscillator map while the true map tends to \(I_2\), giving asymptotically maximal operator error \(2\). At degree one, all \(2^s\) distinct positive zero-energy-defect inputs after \(s\) restorations can be enumerated explicitly, and none gives the correct phase.
