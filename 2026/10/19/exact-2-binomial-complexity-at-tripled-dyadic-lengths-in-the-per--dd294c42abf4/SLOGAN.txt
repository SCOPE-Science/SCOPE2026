For the classical period-doubling word, 2-binomial complexity has the exact non-dyadic law \(b_{\mathbf p}^{(2)}(3\cdot2^k)=2^{k+1}+k+3\) for every \(k\ge0\). Its three Hamming-weight layers contain exactly \(2^k\), \(k+3\), and \(2^k\) classes, with the middle layer governed by an explicit arithmetic progression of weighted one-position sums.
