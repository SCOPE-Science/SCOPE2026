For any primitive binary Christoffel word, the number of Abelian-border lengths in rotation \(s\) is exactly \(2\min(j,n-1-j)\), where \(j\equiv sb\pmod n\) and \(b\) counts ones. Across all rotations the count spectrum is independent of the slope: each even count occurs twice, except the maximal count when the word length is odd, which occurs once.
