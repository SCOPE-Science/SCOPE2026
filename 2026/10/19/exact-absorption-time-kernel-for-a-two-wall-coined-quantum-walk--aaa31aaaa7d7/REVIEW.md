# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The finite-dimensional loss operator is stable for every admitted nontrivial coin. An explicit all-position quadratic-form kernel satisfies the Lyapunov equation in three algebraically exhaustive gap regimes, yielding the lifetime formula. The symbolic checker verifies these parameter-general polynomial identities and 27 exact finite-strip boundary certificates; simulation supplies independent numerical consistency checks. The proof does not extrapolate from finite enumerations.

**Originality — PASS, with a residual risk.** Ammara et al. analyze exit probabilities, Bach et al. analyze probabilities and long-time tails, and Konno et al. provide generic first-hit recurrences rather than this closed first-moment kernel. Yamasaki et al. consider different absorbing geometry and Mondal et al. a single absorber on an infinite line. The indexed searches and available historical claims showed no matching formula, but an implicit evaluation of a known generating function remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** The exact finite-state absorption-time kernel quantifies monitored-transport duration rather than eventual loss and reveals both the arbitrary-position qubit dependence and the exact initial-coin independence at the symmetric midpoint. The Hadamard case furnishes a sharp quantum-versus-classical first-moment benchmark in the same interval.

**Limitations.** Homogeneous real one-parameter coin; symmetric perfect sinks measured after each step; finite interval, localized pure start; no claim for alternative measurement schemes or noisy coins.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
