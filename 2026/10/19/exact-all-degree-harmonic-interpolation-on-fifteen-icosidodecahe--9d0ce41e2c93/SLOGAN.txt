On the fifteen antipodal lines of the icosidodecahedral root system \(H_3\), individual-degree spherical-harmonic interpolation first becomes complete at degree \(7\), loses exactly the mean at degrees \(8\) and \(14\), and is complete at every degree \(\ell\ge15\). An exact quadratic-field and modular certificate plus a uniform Legendre bound proves the entire degree-by-degree rank classification.
