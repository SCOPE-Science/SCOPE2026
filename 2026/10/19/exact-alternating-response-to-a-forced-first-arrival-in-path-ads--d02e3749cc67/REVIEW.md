# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The unconditional path mean follows from the first-arrival recursion and generating function. The new strict sign proof uses the positive integral remainder \(u_m\), its factorial bound and strict ratio estimates, covering odd and even endpoint distances and the exceptional sizes \(1\), \(2\) and \(4\). Exact fractions were independently checked against direct enumeration of vertex orderings for \(n\le8\), followed by 5,050 finite sign/bound tests through \(n=100\). Finite tests support but do not replace the all-size proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The closest reference is Krivelevich–Mészáros–Michaeli–Shikhelman, arXiv:1907.07216, Section 8: equation (10) already conditions on the first vertex, equation (11) already gives exact expected occupancy, and equation (13) supplies signed second differences. These components are fully credited. The accepted claim is only the all-site global priority-response sign classification and strict factorial decay, requiring further inequalities beyond the recorded formulas. Searches for equivalent priority/inhomogeneous/first-vertex results did not find the same theorem. The 1994 inhomogeneous adsorption and earlier general-graph work remain full-text access risks. An unindexed equivalent inequality statement is possible.

## Value — PASS
A single early proposal can increase or decrease eventual saturation depending solely on parity of its boundary distance. Exceptional small systems and a quantitative factorial boundary effect are determined sharply in kind for all finite paths. This structural intervention law is more informative than recomputing individual expected occupancy values.

## Scope and limitations
This result treats one guaranteed first site on a finite path, not finite-rate perturbations, other graphs, or multiple simultaneously preferred sites. The factorial constant is not optimized. Published exact formulas already determine the unconditioned expected occupancy and numerical first-conditioned means. Independent scientific audit has not been performed.
