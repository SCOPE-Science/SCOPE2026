In random sequential adsorption on a finite path, forcing one vertex to arrive first changes expected final coverage with an exact alternating sign determined by its distance to the nearer endpoint. The response is zero for every site only on paths of lengths \(1\), \(2\), and \(4\); away from these exceptions it is strictly signed and bounded in magnitude by \(3\cdot2^d/(d+1)!\) for interior boundary distance \(d\ge1\).
