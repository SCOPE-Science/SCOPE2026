# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The word decomposition by the first and last occurrences of \(a\) is unique. Scattered \(ab^ra\) avoidance is equivalent to having fewer than \(r\) intervening \(b\)'s; the contributing \(ab\) pairs equal \(Ae+\sum i x_i\). Summing all internal weak compositions gives a shifted Gaussian polynomial, and choosing the number of trailing \(b\)'s fixes the leading block. This reconstructs the full coefficient identity. Reversal proves symmetry, the two endpoint regimes are disjoint exhaustive cases, and the two-\(a\) formula gives a rigorous parity/valley test for unimodality. A separate direct enumerator agrees for 264 finite parameter cases. No infinite claim relies on finite checking.

## Originality — PASS within inspected literature, with residual risk

Fazekas–Huang–Mercaş, arXiv:2610.06717 (2026), Sections 2, 4, 5, give arithmetic existence/universality results and an additive approximation for any fixed pattern, but not the stated uniform exact \(ab^ra\) avoidance coefficients or the parity-driven unimodality boundary. Richomme, arXiv:2510.07159 (2025), Section 4, supplies the well-known partition bijection and canonical extremal representatives; those are explicitly background, and the singleton \(r=2\) consequence is not claimed as novel. Atanasiu (2007) gives older equivalence-class representations and rewrite relations, not the identified bounded-gap coefficient distribution. Search comparisons found no identical theorem; differently indexed bounded-spread partition enumeration or historical coverage remains a meaningful risk.

## Value — PASS

The result gives exact conditional pattern probabilities across an infinite, arithmetically indexed family where the motivating 2026 paper established only an approximation scheme in general. It simultaneously identifies a striking transition in restricted Gaussian-coefficient geometry: flat counts at the two-interior-symbol exclusion threshold, ordinary Gaussian counts once the pattern is too long, and an infinite parity-classified failure of unimodality between these regimes. It supplies a reusable coefficient formula instead of an isolated numerical case.

## Scope and limitations

The result is specific to scattered two-ended run patterns over two symbols; coefficients can be computed by finite polynomial arithmetic but no bitlength-polynomial algorithm is asserted. The Gaussian partition model and the singleton boundary are established background. The nearest published sources have been inspected in relevant full HTML sections. Additional older papers on canonical Parikh representatives and possible bounded-spread partition formulas remain a residual prior-art risk.
