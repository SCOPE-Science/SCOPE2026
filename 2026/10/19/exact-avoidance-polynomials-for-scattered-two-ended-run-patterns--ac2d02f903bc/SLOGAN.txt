For binary Parikh-matrix classes, every scattered pattern \(ab^ra\) has an exact avoidance generating polynomial obtained from bounded-spread Gaussian partitions; this gives the exact probability of pattern occurrence for arbitrary fixed letter and pair counts. In the two-\(a\) family with \(2\le r\le B\), these polynomials are unimodal precisely for even \(r\); every odd \(r\ge3\) has non-unimodal examples at every \(B\ge r\).
