For the canonical quadratic mutually unbiased bases over \(\mathbb F_{p^r}\) in odd characteristic, the exact counts of all normalized three-vector Bargmann phases follow a trace–quadratic-character formula: the number of realized phases jumps sharply from \(p+1\) to \(2p\) at extension degree \(r=2\). The census determines every three-vector Gram spectrum and identifies exactly \(36\) dependent unordered distinct-basis triples in dimension \(3\).
