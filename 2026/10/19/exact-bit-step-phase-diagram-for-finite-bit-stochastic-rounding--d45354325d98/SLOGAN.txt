Truncating stochastic rounding with only \(r\) random bits can trap exact scalar quadratic gradient descent at any of \(\max\{0,\lceil (2^r\eta)^{-1}-\tau\rceil\}\) nonstationary grid points. Its adjacent-state stationary mean obeys an exact bit–stepsize staircase, with sharp escape threshold \(2^r\eta\tau\ge1\), whereas ideal stochastic rounding remains unbiased in stationarity.
