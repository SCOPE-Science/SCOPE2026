A positive hard triadic Lotka--Volterra interaction can be hidden **exactly for all time** along a continuum of nonconstant periodic trajectories: a purely pairwise model produces the identical full three-species abundance data on \(U=(b/d)VW\). Yet the higher-order model attracts perturbations back to this surface at the explicit transverse rate \(b\alpha\delta/(\kappa\gamma)\), while the pairwise model is transversely neutral, giving a precise experimental way to separate the mechanisms.
