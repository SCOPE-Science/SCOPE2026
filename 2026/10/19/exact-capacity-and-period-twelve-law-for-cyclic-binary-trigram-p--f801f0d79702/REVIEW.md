# Scientific review: exact capacity and period-twelve law for cyclic binary trigram profiles

## Correctness: PASS

The proof models a cyclic word as an Euler tour in the directed order-two de Bruijn graph. The three independent balance equations identify five nonnegative parameters. The argument separately proves connectivity for every positive-support pattern, including the exceptional disjoint-loop profiles that balanced equations alone would admit incorrectly. Every admissible support family is counted once, leading to the exact rational generating function. Its noncancelled primitive third- and fourth-root poles prove the least quasipolynomial period twelve; the fifth-order pole at one supplies the leading coefficient. The optimal code size equals the number of observation classes by a one-representative argument. Direct exhaustive word checks, an independently coded Euler feasibility test, and parametric-versus-series checks support (but do not replace) these all-length proofs.

## Originality: PASS, with residual risk

The closest general source is Kiah, Puleo, and Milenkovic's *Codes for DNA Sequence Profiles* (arXiv:1502.00517) and its earlier public version (arXiv:1410.8837), especially its de Bruijn-profile representation and Ehrhart treatment (Section 4, Theorem 4.3 and Section 5). These yield asymptotic/quasipolynomial structure rather than this exact cyclic binary trigram numerator or connectivity classification. Jacquet, Knessl, and Szpankowski's *Counting Markov Types* (2010) treats first-order cyclic Markov type matrices; the binary example counts bigram matrices, not the four-vertex order-two graph. Suzuki and Myers' *Accurate k-mer Classification Using Read Profiles* (2022) studies an algorithmic classification problem on observed read-count sequences rather than the number of cyclic realizable source profiles. Related exact two-mer reconstruction enumerations involve different observation maps. An exact count for noncyclic binary ordered-trigram profiles similarly does not imply this closed-cycle capacity: nonclosed paths admit different balances and supports, so neither their total number nor their asymptotics fixes the cyclic class count. The relevant statements and implications were compared; an exact equivalent elsewhere in poorly indexed literature remains possible. No independent verification of novelty has been performed.

## Mathematical value: PASS

The fully ordered cyclic three-mer multiplicity vector is a natural noiseless observation model in Markov type enumeration, DNA spectrum reconstruction, and coding. This is the smallest binary cyclic case with nontrivial four-vertex Euler connectivity. The classification distinguishes realizable spectra from merely balanced integer flows, and the exact generating function resolves a complete, infinite, finite-length reconstruction-capacity function rather than providing only an asymptotic growth order. Its sharp period twelve is caused by genuine cycle-length arithmetic, not a chosen arbitrary parameter slice. Higher alphabets, longer reads, noisy observations, and profile-class sizes remain open in this package.
