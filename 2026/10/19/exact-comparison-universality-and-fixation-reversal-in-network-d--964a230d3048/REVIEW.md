**Mathematical value — PASS.** The result identifies the entire topology class on which even extreme opposing behavioral susceptibilities have no effect on the transition law, a structural nonidentifiability absent from analysis restricted to a few sample networks. It also gives the exact critical inversion for the donor game on cliques, at finite (not infinitesimal) selection, and a sharp path witness showing fragility of bipartite invisibility. The value is the universal model classification plus a rigorous finite-population consequence, not the known Moran-type formula on its own.
