For donation-game death–birth updating, social comparison is exactly invisible at every selection strength on a complete bipartite graph when susceptibility is constant within each part; apart from zero comparison, only complete graphs admit any other universal scalar payoff reduction. On a clique of \(n\) vertices, uniform comparison rescales selection by \(1-\lambda/(n-1)\), producing exact neutrality at \(\lambda=n-1\) and reversal above it.
