Deleting \(a^k b^k\) from the alternating word \((ab)^m\) (with \(k\ge2\) and \(m\ge2k-1\)) produces exactly \(\binom{m-1}{2k-2}+\binom m{2k}\) distinct residual words. Every residual has a unique decomposition into intact \(ab\) pairs and either \(k\) or \(k-1\) isolated \(b\)'s followed by the same number of isolated \(a\)'s.
