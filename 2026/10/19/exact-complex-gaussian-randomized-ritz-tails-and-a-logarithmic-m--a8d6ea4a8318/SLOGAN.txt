For a simple diagonal Hermitian eigenproblem, the square complex-Gaussian randomized Rayleigh–Ritz pencil has an exact normalized squared-angle law \(qV/(U+qV)\) with independent unit exponentials, while its unrefined target Ritz value has infinite second moment. As the trial-space angle \(\theta\) vanishes, the mean squared eigenvector angle is asymptotic to \(2(b/a)^2\theta^2\log(1/\theta)\), a sharp logarithmic penalty relative to deterministic Rayleigh–Ritz on the same subspace.
