On a natural expanding Markov slice of a 2023 tunable-mean tent map, the invariant density and every positional autocovariance have exact rational expressions. The stationary mean rises monotonically, but the Gaussian time-average variance \(\sigma_b^2=\frac{b(1-b)(b^2-b+1)}{12(1+b)^3}\) has a unique interior maximum; a separate parameter causes a triple correlation-pole resonance.
