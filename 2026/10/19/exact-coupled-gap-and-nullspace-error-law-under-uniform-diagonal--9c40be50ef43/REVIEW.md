**Value — PASS.** Randomized nullspace solvers simultaneously need separated perturbed eigenvalues for feasible Krylov convergence and small perturbation-induced nullspace error. The two quantities arise from the same random diagonal, so combining their marginal bounds as independent events would be unsound. This exact minimal graph provides a reproducible joint-risk benchmark and a nontrivial all-parameter negative-association theorem, while revealing a conditional U-shaped response that would be invisible to marginal estimates. The restricted three-column setting is intentional and does not establish general-matrix behavior.
