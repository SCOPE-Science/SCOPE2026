# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The result is for the compact, orientation-preserving velocity-reset telegraph process with positive reset and flip rates and smooth odd increasing drag. The stationary transport equations yield two currents; exact elimination produces the one-sided slope and positive critical curvature without any guessed shape of the stationary density. Independently, histories with no tumble since the most recent reset have an explicit positive density that diverges at the terminal speeds precisely under the stated sufficient inequality. This establishes the two side minima by continuity when the central cusp condition also holds. The reset-free comparison follows from the zero-current equation. Five independently implemented finite-volume checks corroborate the local identities and edge growth. The argument does not establish uniqueness of outer or inner extrema.

**Originality — PASS, with stated uncertainty.** The closest work, Howlader–Mondal–Das, arXiv:2606.00560 (2026), treats the same model, already derives its forward equations and reports qualitative resetting-induced cusps and secondary peaks numerically. Those observations are prior work, not novel contributions. Its analyzed statements and figures do not provide the exact local critical line, the critical positive quadratic derivative, the universal terminal-speed divergence condition, or the immediate reversal of local type for arbitrarily small reset rate. Olsen–Löwen, arXiv:2401.12685 (2024), studies partial velocity resets chiefly through position transport coefficients; reset-free nonlinear-drag density formulas predate the present result. Targeted mathematical-record and literature comparisons found no equivalent full claim. A broader piecewise-deterministic Markov-process theorem could implicitly subsume parts; available full-text access to some older papers was limited, and novelty is not an independently established fact.

**Value — PASS.** The observable of interest, the stationary velocity-density morphology in the actual nonlinear-drag run-and-tumble model, is investigated in recent physical literature but primarily through numerical plots with no exact threshold. The calculation supplies an explicit transition surface independent of higher drag nonlinearities, a robust regime with a central cusp and terminal concentration, and a singular weak-reset effect that can be missed by finite-resolution simulation. These exact boundaries constrain interpretation of mode changes in shear-thickening and shear-thinning media. The contribution is a classification/boundary theorem, not a claim to solve the full stationary density.

**Limitations and closest literature.** The full edge density may have additional contributions beyond the no-tumble lower bound. No exact count of interior modes or complete edge normalization is asserted. The predecessor's stationary PDE is sufficiently general that a previously unnoticed implicit implication is possible; broader telegraph/PDMP theory may contain closely related identities. The 2024 partial-reset paper's abstract was accessible but its complete manuscript was not materially inspected here. The 2026 primary full-text exposition and its reported reset-free equations were materially examined.
