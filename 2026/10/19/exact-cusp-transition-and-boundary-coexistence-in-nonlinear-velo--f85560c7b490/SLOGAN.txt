For orientation-preserving Poisson velocity resets of a nonlinear-drag run-and-tumble particle, the stationary origin switches exactly from a local minimum to a cusp maximum at \(r+2\lambda=g'(0)\), with a positive critical quadratic curvature. The separate condition \(r+\lambda<g'(v_*)\) forces divergent endpoint peaks, so a central cusp and two edge singularities coexist; even arbitrarily weak resetting can reverse the reset-free central minimum.
