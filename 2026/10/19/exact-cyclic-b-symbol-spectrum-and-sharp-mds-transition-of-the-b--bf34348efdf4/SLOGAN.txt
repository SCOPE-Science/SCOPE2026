The binary primitive \([31,16,7]\) BCH code has an exact cyclic \(b\)-symbol weight distribution for every width \(1\le b\le31\), with all coefficients supplied and reproduced by two distinct exhaustive algorithms. It is \(b\)-symbol MDS exactly for \(5\le b\le16\); for \(9\le b\le15\), every non-full-weight multiplicity follows a geometric run-length formula.
