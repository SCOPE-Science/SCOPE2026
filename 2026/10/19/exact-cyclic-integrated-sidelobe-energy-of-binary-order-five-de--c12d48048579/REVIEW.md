# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Local bijections at every four-bit graph vertex exhaust the \(2^{16}\) possible edge-successor assignments; a single 32-edge permutation cycle is equivalent to an Eulerian traversal and therefore a de Bruijn cycle. Independent depth-first enumeration yields the identical set of 2048 rooted cycles. Direct periodic autocorrelation and an independently programmed support-intersection formula agree at all 32 lags for every cycle. The full energy and peak-sidelobe joint census, moments, and extremal symmetry orbits are checked from the embedded package. Independent published aggregate RMS values (0.146, 0.123, 0.078) are recovered by rounding the corresponding means obtained from our exact energy counts.

**Originality — PASS against inspected evidence, with residual historical risk.** Spinsante–Andrenacci–Gambi (2011, DOI 10.1186/1687-1499-2011-4, full text including Table 3 inspected) already report the counts of 12 low-peak and 784 peak-at-most-eight sequences and aggregate RMS statistics, but not the exact 47-level energy distribution or extremal orbit classification. Fujisaki–Nabeshima (2011, DOI 10.1587/nolta.2.400, full PDF and its Tables I–II inspected) cover minima and maxima at individual correlation delays, not the sum of squared periodic sidelobes. Song–Babu–Palomar (2014, arXiv:1501.02252, definition and optimization sections inspected) formalize periodic integrated sidelobe objectives on unconstrained unimodular codes, not the de Bruijn-constrained discrete spectrum. Targeted mathematical-record searches and own-claim comparisons yielded no equivalent or dominating result. Unknown or unindexed datasets remain a priority risk; no assertion of independent historical novelty certification is made.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Periodic integrated sidelobe energy is an established aggregate signal-quality objective; the cited DS-CDMA study explicitly motivates correlation-based filtering of de Bruijn sequences. A complete distribution over a natural finite population supplies sharp feasible range, moments, extremal symmetry structure, and a reproducible benchmark. Peak-only threshold selection leaves energy unresolved: even within peak-eight cycles it ranges from \(224\) to \(992\). This goes beyond computing another formulation of the previously tabulated peak thresholds.

The result is finite at order five. It does not claim performance guarantees for aperiodic signaling, unconstrained length-32 codes, or cross-correlation code families.
