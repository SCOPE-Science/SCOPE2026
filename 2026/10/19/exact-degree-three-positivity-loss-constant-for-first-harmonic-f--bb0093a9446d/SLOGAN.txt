The least uniform norm of a real Fourier operator reproducing harmonics \(0,\pm1\) and outputting only harmonics through \(\pm3\) is the exact constant \(\Lambda_{1,3}\approx1.16139544326947\), determined by a unique five-equation three-zero system. Its unique even convolution optimizer has three simple sign changes, and its positivity loss is strictly smaller than the degree-two optimum \((4/\pi-1)/2\).
