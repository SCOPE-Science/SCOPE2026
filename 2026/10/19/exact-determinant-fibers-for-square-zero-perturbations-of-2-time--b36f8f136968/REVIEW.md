# Mathematical review: determinant-fiber distribution

## Correctness — PASS
The square-zero rank-one parametrization partitions nonzero perturbations by their unique common image/kernel line. The determinant lemma yields an exact affine functional on each such line; the distinction between invariant and noninvariant lines proves every determinant fiber. Characteristic-two diagonalizability follows from the separable/nonseparable trace dichotomy, and odd-characteristic cases follow from the quadratic discriminant and the scalar exception. The proof independently covers every prime power and every two-dimensional similarity type. Exhaustive standalone checks across fields of orders 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, and 8 reproduce all fibers and counts. The finite checks are not interpreted as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with stated limitations
The 2026 characteristic-two article (arXiv:2604.15286v1) and 2025 odd-characteristic article (arXiv:2507.05762v1) prove existence, not the exact number of square-zero decompositions or the fixed-determinant fiber distribution. The older two-by-two existence theorem is quoted in the latter full-text source. The closest indexed additive torsion-unit/nilpotent and binary three-dimensional results address different counting objects; multiplicative idempotent–nilpotent fibers address a different operation. No inspected statement logically supplies these determinant fibers or their four odd-characteristic cases. Complete text of the 2022 predecessor and potentially unindexed equivalent treatments remain unexamined, so this is a literature-bounded, not absolute, novelty judgment.

## Value — PASS
The qualitative two-by-two existence problem is settled in the literature; the new theorem gives the full naturally defined conditional determinant distribution of all square-zero perturbations, hence the exact count of all decompositions for each matrix similarity type over every finite field. Its uniform characteristic-two multiplicity and four-way odd-characteristic stratification quantify the full solution set rather than select a witness. The invariant-line mechanism makes this structural, not an ad hoc small-field census.
