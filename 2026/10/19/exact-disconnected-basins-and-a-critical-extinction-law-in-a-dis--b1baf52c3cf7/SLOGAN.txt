A density-dependent discontinuity creates two exact attraction basins even when both logistic branches are nonchaotic: the upper basin is \((a,b)\), while the lower basin is disconnected. The lower attractor changes to extinction at \(\varepsilon=1-1/\mu\), with critical decay \(n x_n\to1\), and the upper-basin fraction approaches \(2/\mu-1>0\) as the dispersal jump vanishes.
