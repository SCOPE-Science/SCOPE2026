The cosine piecewise map has an exact three-parameter fixed-point phase diagram bounded by two dual saddle-node curves: every breakpoint \(b\in(0,1)\) admits four fixed points with two distinct attracting fixed points, whereas the opposite parameter inequalities give no fixed points. The left fold threshold and the right fold threshold's deficit from one vanish quadratically in their respective narrow-branch limits, with the explicit coefficient \(2/\pi^2\).
