For integer-ordered binary first-order Reed–Muller codes, every non-full nonzero cyclic \(b\)-symbol weight at a dyadic-plus-one width \(b=2^s+1\) occurs exactly four times: \[W_{m,s}(Z)=1+4\sum_{r=1}^{2^{m-s-1}-1}Z^{2^{m-1}+2^sr}+\bigl(2^{m+1}-2^{m-s+1}+3\bigr)Z^{2^m}.\] The same exact enumerator applies to reflected-Gray coordinate order.
