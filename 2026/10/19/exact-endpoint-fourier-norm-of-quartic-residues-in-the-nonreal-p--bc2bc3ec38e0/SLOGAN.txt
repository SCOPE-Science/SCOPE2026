For every prime \(p\equiv5\pmod8\), the sharp endpoint norm of the Fourier projection onto the nonzero fourth-power residues is an explicit two-radical expression in the uniquely signed coordinate \(a\equiv1\pmod4\) of \(p=a^2+4b^2\). All complex maximizers are the phase-matched translates of the nonvanishing quartic-period convolution kernel.
