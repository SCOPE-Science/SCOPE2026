For every labelled alphabet of size \(q\ge3\) and span \(n\ge2\), the exact number of cyclic words containing each length-\(n\) word of Hamming weight one or two precisely once is \((q-2)!((q-1)!)^{(n-1)(q-1)}q^{(n-1)(q-2)+\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor}\), counting only cyclic rotations as equivalent. A color-symmetric directed-tree determinant closes the nonbinary enumeration beyond the previously known binary case.
