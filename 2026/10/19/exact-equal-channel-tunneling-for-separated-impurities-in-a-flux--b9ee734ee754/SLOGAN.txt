Two separated equal impurity pairs in a flux-threaded diamond chain generate upper and lower weak-coupling doublets whose first-order tunneling amplitudes are exactly equal: \(t_\pm=\varepsilon(1-r^2)r^{d-1}/4+O(\varepsilon^2)\), with \(r=e^{-\operatorname{arcosh}(\sec(\phi/2))}\). An exact four-eigenvalue flat-band calculation explains the previously observed near-equality and predicts the quarter-flux distance-eight versus distance-six coupling ratio \(3-2\sqrt2\).
