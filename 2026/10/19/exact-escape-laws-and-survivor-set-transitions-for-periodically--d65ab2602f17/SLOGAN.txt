For the doubling map with alternately removed left and right endpoint holes of common width \(h\ge1/4\), the exact finite-time survivor probability has a linear-in-time prefactor precisely for \(1/4\le h<1/3\), but is a pure \(2^{-n}\) law for \(1/3\le h<2/3\) and vanishes after two observations for \(h\ge2/3\). The infinite survivor simultaneously changes from a countably infinite backward orbit to a singleton and then the empty set.
