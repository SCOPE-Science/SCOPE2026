For every squarefree cyclic group of order \(N=p r_1\cdots r_t\) with least prime \(p\), all orthogonal \(p\)-point Fourier spectral pairs obey a coordinatewise singleton criterion. Every fixed spectral spatial set has exactly \(Q\prod_{r\in S(A)}r^{p-1}\) literal spectra, yielding precisely \(2^t\) multiplicity levels and \(Q\prod_{r\mid Q}(2r^p-r)\) ordered pairs, where \(Q=N/p\).
