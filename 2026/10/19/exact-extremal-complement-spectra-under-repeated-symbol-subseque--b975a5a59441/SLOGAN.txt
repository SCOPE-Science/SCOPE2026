Deleting \(k\) copies of one designated letter from a length-\(n\) word gives the most distinct complements when, for some number of runs, that letter occupies balanced runs separated by single other letters; an explicit one-parameter coefficient formula determines the exact maximum for every \(n\ge k\ge1\). For three deletions this has a closed form, with the exceptional odd lengths \(3,5,7\) preceding a simple binomial law.
