# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
Symmetry averaging is justified by convexity of \(\|P-J/n\|\). The quadratic Fourier symbol has its endpoint minimum at modes 1 and \(n/2\), and its discrete maximum at mode \(\lceil n/4\rceil\). The explicit positive three-mode affine combination supplies a global lower bound met by a feasible nonnegative kernel. Every nonstandard inequality and normalization is written in RESULT.md. Finite numerical LP and matrix-spectral checks corroborate but do not substitute for the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, residual risk
The 2004 general formulation (Boyd, Diaconis, Xiao) and 2007 symmetry report (Boyd, Diaconis, Parrilo, Xiao) establish the SDP and ordinary-cycle techniques, not the all-even squared-cycle formula or mod-four holding transition. The separately available odd squared-cycle calculation does not entail the antipodal active-frequency contact or the two distinct holding asymptotics. Targeted published-record and web searches for alternate formulations did not reveal a covering theorem; hidden/unindexed circulant literature remains possible. Search failure is not treated as mathematical proof of priority.

## Value — PASS
An infinite network family receives an exact globally optimal sparse Markov kernel, a transparent dual certificate, and a genuinely parity-dependent probability-allocation law. For graph diffusion/network mixing, the mod-four discontinuity in the leading holding rate distinguishes network sizes that share the same leading spectral gap. This closes the even-order structural case left by the odd-order computation, rather than merely substituting a numerical parameter or giving a finite instance.

## Scope and risk
Only symmetric stochastic kernels on the square-of-cycle support are covered; unrestricted uniqueness is not proved. The original and published articles were inspected where relevant, though independent literature audit has not been undertaken.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
