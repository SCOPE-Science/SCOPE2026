For every even squared cycle \(C_n^2\), the globally fastest symmetric random walk has explicit unique orbit weights, three alternating Fourier contacts, and a strictly positive holding probability. Its holding-to-nearest-jump ratio is exactly 1 when \(4\mid n\), but approaches 1/2 when \(n\equiv2\pmod4\), even though both subsequences have the same sharp \(8\pi^2/n^2\) spectral-gap law.
