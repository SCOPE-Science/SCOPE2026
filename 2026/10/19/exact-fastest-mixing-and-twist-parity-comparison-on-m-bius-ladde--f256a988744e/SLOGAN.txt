The globally fastest symmetric stochastic walk on every Möbius ladder has an explicit unique rotation-invariant edge allocation, with a positive antipodal probability that vanishes quadratically as the ladder grows. Twisting a Cartesian prism preserves its optimal spectral convergence factor at odd half-lengths but strictly improves it at even half-lengths; the even advantage is asymptotic to \(\pi^4/m^4\), while the ladder's optimal spectral gap is asymptotically four times that of the plain cycle on the same vertices.
