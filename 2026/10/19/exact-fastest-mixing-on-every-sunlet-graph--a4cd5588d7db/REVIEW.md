# Scientific review: fastest-mixing sunlet chains

## Correctness — PASS
The stated global optimum follows from convex averaging under the dihedral automorphisms, which cannot increase the spectral-norm objective. The resulting two-edge-orbit chain has explicit \(2\times2\) Fourier blocks; the positive first-harmonic eigenvalue supplies a lower bound for every admissible chain. Maximizing the rim-edge probability for fixed leaf-edge probability reduces that bound to a strictly convex scalar function with the displayed unique critical point. Its exact value is attained because all remaining Fourier eigenvalues are proved to have moduli at most the same bound. The inequalities use only \(0<x_n^2\le3/2\). The argument covers all integers \(n\ge3\), not only the computational tests. A standalone program checks the symbolic stationarity and spectral bounds and samples explicit matrices. **Correctness risk:** none identified within the stated reversible, uniform-stationary, SLEM domain; the script is supplementary, not the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS with residual risk
Boyd–Diaconis–Xiao (2004, DOI `10.1137/S0036144503423264`) supply the general semidefinite formulation, which does not imply the new exact sunlet optimizer without solving its family-specific spectral problem. Boyd–Diaconis–Parrilo–Xiao (arXiv:`0709.0955`, §3.1.1 and §5.3.3 in the preprint; §5.4.3 in the journal version) explicitly solve cycles and *wheels*, not sunlets: a wheel has one hub adjacent to every cycle vertex, whereas a sunlet has distinct leaves, producing a different family of Fourier blocks and a different optimum. Olesker-Taylor–Zanetti (arXiv:`2111.05816`) bound relaxation orders geometrically, not exact SLEM and weights on this family. Targeted searches for the sunlet/cycle-corona/circular-comb aliases found no covering exact formula in the accessible material. The closest indexed spectral studies concern different graph families and objectives. **Originality risks:** unindexed technical reports, unpublished notes or equivalent formulations could still have the exact answer; a generic numerical SDP solver can recover individual instances but not the displayed all-size formula directly.

## Value — PASS
A sunlet is a canonical regular ring of sites each burdened with one one-way peripheral state. The result gives the exact globally optimal spatially local symmetric mixing law at every graph size, proves the rim holding probability vanishes, and identifies the nontrivial \(1/n\) pendant exchange scale. Its leading optimal gap is half the bare cycle's, giving a precise quantitative cost of doubling the state space by pendant attachment. This is a natural complete extremal classification, not a small numerical improvement or a relabeling of a published wheel formula.

## Limits
Only the symmetric, uniform-stationary, fixed-time-homogeneous SLEM optimization is treated; possible additional non-dihedrally-invariant optimal matrices are not classified. No assertion concerns exact cutoff-dependent mixing times. Prior-literature coverage is bounded by accessible indexed and full-text sources; independent external audit is not part of this review.
