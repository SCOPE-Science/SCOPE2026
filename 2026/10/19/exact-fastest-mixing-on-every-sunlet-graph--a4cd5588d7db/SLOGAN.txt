For every sunlet graph \(C_n\odot K_1\), the globally fastest symmetric stochastic walk has explicit rim and leaf transition probabilities, with zero holding at rim vertices and a unique dihedrally symmetric optimizer. Its optimal absolute spectral gap is exactly \(x_n^2/(x_n^2+2x_n+2)\), where \(x_n=\sqrt2\sin(\pi/n)\), asymptotically half the optimal gap of the bare cycle.
