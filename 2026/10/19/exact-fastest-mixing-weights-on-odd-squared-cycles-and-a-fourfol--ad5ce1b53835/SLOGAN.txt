Every odd squared cycle \(C_n^2\), \(n\ge7\), has an explicit globally fastest symmetric stochastic walk: its unique circulant optimizer balances three Fourier contacts, uses strictly positive probabilities for holding and both jump lengths, and assigns only \(O(n^{-2})\) mass to length-one jumps. Those vanishingly rare moves nevertheless quadruple the sharp asymptotic spectral gap over the fastest walk using length-two jumps alone.
