# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The Fourier equation follows by direct evaluation of the algebra product. Nonzero support must be a union of the \(\langle-1,2\rangle\) frequency orbits. Pairing opposite equations forces inversion on each support orbit, and doubling forces power-by-two behavior. Distinguishing whether negation is a power of doubling proves the two claimed root exponents. The Frobenius cycles are exactly the cosets of \(\langle-1,2\rangle\) under multiplication by \(q\), giving the prescribed gcd count. Fourier inversion is valid precisely under the stated coprimality hypothesis. A standalone checker compared the formula against direct full algebra enumeration in fourteen instances. These tests do not replace the symbolic proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
The closest primary literature establishes the Fourier multiplication equation, integral idempotent rigidity, and Peirce spectra, but not the stated uniform finite-field divisor-product count. The previously indexed three-element quandle case is explicitly excluded from the novel contribution. Searches for alternative names (Takasaki/core/dihedral quandle ring), finite-field counts, Frobenius orbit parameterizations, and stronger results did not identify an implication covering all odd orders. An equivalent older finite-field character-orbit theorem remains possible; this review does not certify priority independently.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The exact idempotent count is a natural full invariant of a nonassociative algebra; the result classifies it for an infinite family over all coprime-characteristic coefficient fields. The count depends substantially on arithmetic Frobenius action rather than merely dimension, and the explicit examples show nontrivial jumps. The result is not a reformulation of integral uniqueness or a computation of a single small instance.

## Limitations
No extension to even orders or characteristic dividing the order is proved. The finite checker covers fourteen instances only. Full independent audit and historical priority certification have not been performed.
