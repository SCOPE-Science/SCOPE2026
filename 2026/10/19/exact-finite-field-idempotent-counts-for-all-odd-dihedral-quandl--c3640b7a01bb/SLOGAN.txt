For every odd \(n\ge3\) and finite field \(\mathbb F_q\) with \(\gcd(n,q)=1\), all idempotents of the dihedral quandle algebra \(\mathbb F_q[R_n]\) are classified by multiplicative character orbits and Frobenius descent, yielding an explicit exact divisor-product count. For example, the algebras over \(\mathbb F_2\) of orders \(11\) and \(15\) have exactly \(68\) and \(768\) idempotents, respectively.
