For noisy pairwise unanimity voting on every finite complete graph, sampling influencers with replacement produces a stationary opinion count smaller in convex order than sampling distinct influencers; the variance gap is strict for every \(N\geq4\) and \(0<p<1\). The direction reverses at influence-group size \(q=3\) already for \(N=4\), so repetitions need not universally increase or decrease polarization.
