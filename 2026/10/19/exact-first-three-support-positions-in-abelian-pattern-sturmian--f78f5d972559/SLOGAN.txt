For \(0\le a<b<c\), an Abelian pattern Sturmian word can have its first three sparse-letter occurrences exactly at \(a,b,c\) if and only if \(a<c-b-\gcd(b-a,c-b)\). The exact sum-incidence cycle rank of those three positions is \(\max\{0,a+1-(c-b)+\gcd(b-a,c-b)\}\), and every admissible triple has an explicit infinite forest-preserving continuation.
