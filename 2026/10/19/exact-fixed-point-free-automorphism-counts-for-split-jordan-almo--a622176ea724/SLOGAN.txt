For a centerless almost-abelian Lie algebra over \(\mathbb F_q\) with invertible split-Jordan defining operator, the exact number and proportion of fixed-point-free automorphisms are products of finite centralizer orders and linear-derangement factors, summed over nonidentity scalar symmetries of the Jordan partitions. Such automorphisms exist exactly when the complete Jordan data admit a nontrivial multiplicative scalar symmetry.
