# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The proof starts from the unrestricted symmetric stochastic problem, justifies group averaging via convexity, and derives all nontrivial eigenvalues on a complete basis. The resulting scalar minimax expression has exactly four parameter regimes and a complete solution set within the invariant subclass. Positivity and stochasticity are checked at every formula endpoint. Exact-rational matrix/eigenvalue and independently enumerated linear-program tests supplement the proof, not replace it.

## Originality
**PASS under the stated literature comparison, with residual risk.** Boyd, Diaconis and Xiao (2004) give the overarching semidefinite model and solve complete bipartite graphs. Boyd, Diaconis, Parrilo and Xiao (2007), Section 5.3.2, give a general multipartite block reduction; their printed result presents the reduced program rather than the solved four-branch tripartite optimum and the middle-regime continuum of invariant optimizers. Jafarizadeh (2010) studies different multipartite interconnection geometries. The general spectral reduction is acknowledged as known. A fully covering calculation in specialized or unindexed literature remains possible.

## Value
**PASS.** Exact fastest mixing for a natural unequal-size three-class network family is a meaningful network design problem. The result identifies when one edge-orbit may be deleted, when zero holding probabilities become forced, and when optimal allocation becomes genuinely nonunique. The answer is an all-size arithmetic phase diagram, not a numerical instance or a relabeling of an already solved family.

## Limits
Only symmetric chains and uniform stationary law are considered. Full characterization of unrestricted (non-invariant) minimizers is not claimed; the global optimal value is unrestricted. The finite verification program does not certify the infinite family or completeness of literature indexing.
