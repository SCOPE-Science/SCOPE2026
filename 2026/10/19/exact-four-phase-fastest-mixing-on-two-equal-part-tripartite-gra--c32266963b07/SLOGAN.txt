For complete tripartite graphs with two equal-sized classes and a third arbitrary class, the globally minimum second-largest eigenvalue modulus of a symmetric Markov chain follows an exact four-phase formula with sharp size-ratio transitions at \(2p/q=1/2,2,4\). In the intermediate phase, the set of automorphism-invariant optimal edge allocations is an entire line segment, whereas the other phases have a unique invariant optimizer.
