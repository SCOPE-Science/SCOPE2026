# Scientific review: multicolor Potts ladder frozen states

**Correctness — PASS.** With three distinct neighbors, strict increase under every recoloring is equivalent to having two or three like-colored neighbors. At a mismatched rung both horizontal neighbors must agree with the central sites, and this forces both rows to be constant with different colors; if every rung matches, the allowed cyclic color words have no singleton runs. This proves the complete classification. The directed-pair transfer has nonzero spectral factors \(x^2-x-(q-1)u\) and \(x^2-x+u\), with multiplicities one and \(q-1\), which proves both polynomials. Its simple dominant root controls the logarithmic entropy and gives positive analytic Gaussian wall variance \(\lambda_q(\lambda_q-1)/(2\lambda_q-1)^3\). An independent exhaustive program checked all relevant individual recoloring energy differences, the geometry, the wall polynomial and the energy polynomial on 2,123,582 small labeled configurations, plus 40 direct transfer-trace identities. The theorem's infinite range is supported by the proof, not the finite tests.

**Originality — PASS, with residual bibliographic risk.** The main physical source, Olejarz–Krapivsky–Redner (2013), analyzes long-time two-dimensional Potts coarsening, pinned interfaces, and complex static states; its inspected theory neither asserts the two-leg rigidity classification nor derives the all-length microcanonical frozen-energy polynomial. Derrida–Zeitak (1996) treat coarsening-domain distributions in a single chain, not uniform enumeration of two-leg local minima. Biggs–Shrock (2000) give antiferromagnetic proper-coloring strip partition functions, not ferromagnetic strict local-minimum counts. Existing binary Ising ladder work covers a lower-color case but does not imply the \(q\ge3\) all-color formula or its wall central limit theorem. Targeted literature and mathematical-results searches found no exact broader theorem with the same hypotheses and conclusions. This negative search is not a uniqueness certificate; older narrow-strip enumerations not located remain a genuine risk.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The physically established complexity of multicolor two-dimensional coarsening makes its confinement to a minimal three-regular cylinder a motivated boundary question. The result is a complete all-color classification rather than an arbitrary small-instance census. It reveals a rigidity dichotomy absent in unconstrained two-dimensional mosaics, and supplies an exact finite-volume metastable energy density of states, sharp entropy, macroscopic wall density, and nondegenerate Gaussian fluctuations. These distinguish uniform frozen-state statistics from dynamical reachability and give explicit benchmarks for thin-strip kinetic simulation. Elementary local majority reasoning alone does not provide the microcanonical polynomial and asymptotic fluctuation law.

**Closest sources and limits.** See arXiv:1305.1038 (2013; two-dimensional dynamics), arXiv:cond-mat/9606042 (1996; one-dimensional coarsening), and arXiv:cond-mat/0001407 (2000; antiferromagnetic strip proper colorings). The model does not include zero-cost blinking or dynamic basin probabilities. Full bibliographic uniqueness remains unproved.
