Every strictly frozen \(q\)-color ferromagnetic Potts ladder, for \(q\ge3\), is either a pair of differently colored uniform rows or a shared cyclic row whose monochromatic domains all have length at least two. This rigidity gives the full energy-generating polynomial and a Gaussian horizontal-wall law with entropy per spin \(\frac12\log[(1+\sqrt{4q-3})/2]\).
