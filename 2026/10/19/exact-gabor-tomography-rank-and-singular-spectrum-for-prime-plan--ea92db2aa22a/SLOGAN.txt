For the indicator of any split or nonsplit quadratic conic in the prime-field plane, the complete Gabor intensity lifting has exact complex rank \(p^2(1+(p-\chi(d))^2/2)\); its kernel is exactly the Weyl span of translations outside the conic difference set. All singular values and their multiplicities are explicit, combining elementary cosine levels from two-point intersections with a Kloosterman-valued zero-shift band.
