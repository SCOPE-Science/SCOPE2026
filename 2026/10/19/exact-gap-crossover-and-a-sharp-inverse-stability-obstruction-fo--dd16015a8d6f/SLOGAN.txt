For two separated intervals with fixed centers, fixed gap and a small opposite imbalance \(\pm\eta\) in their lengths, the squared \(L^2\) distance of their Fourier intensities is exactly \(|\eta|^3\Phi(g/|\eta|)\), with an explicit three-regime crossover. At any fixed positive gap, the spectral discrepancy scales as \(|\eta|^{3/2}\) while the optimal translation/reflection-invariant geometric discrepancy is \(2|\eta|\), ruling out every local inverse Hölder exponent above \(2/3\) for squared-magnitude \(L^2\) data.
