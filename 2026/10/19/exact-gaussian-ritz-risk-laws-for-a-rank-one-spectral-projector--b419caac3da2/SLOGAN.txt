For square complex-Gaussian randomized Rayleigh--Ritz on any trial subspace of a rank-one-complement projector, the unrefined Ritz value has an exact complex-Cauchy law and no second moment, while Rayleigh-quotient refinement has an exact beta-transformed law. The refined value's expected error is amplified by a sharp logarithm relative to ordinary Rayleigh--Ritz, and its high-dimensional consistency threshold is \((m-1)\sin^2\theta\to0\).
