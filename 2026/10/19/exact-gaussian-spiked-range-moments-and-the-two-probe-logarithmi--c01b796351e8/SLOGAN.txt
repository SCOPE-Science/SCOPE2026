In a one-spike diagonal matrix, the squared Frobenius loss of a Gaussian range finder has an exact beta-transformed chi-square law whose moment behavior changes sharply when the moment order reaches half the number of probes. The first extra probe creates a logarithmic boundary, while the two-pass row-aware left subspace attains the optimal leading rank-size loss with two or more probes, and restores quadratic mean loss even with one.
