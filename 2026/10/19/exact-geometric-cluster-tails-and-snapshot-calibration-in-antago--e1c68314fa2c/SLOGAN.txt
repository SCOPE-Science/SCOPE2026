For one-shot, two-species antagonistic adsorption on the infinite line, the probability of any consecutive A block of length \(m\geq2\) is exactly \(p^m t^{m-2}J_A(t)^2\), and the lengths of maximal A clusters conditional on length at least two have a geometric law with continuation probability \(pt\). The analogous B continuation probability is \((1-p)t\), so the two cluster tails identify both deposition progress and species-proposal bias from an infinite spatial snapshot.
