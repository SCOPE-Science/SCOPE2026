The stationary coordinate of the Pelikan random map has an exact Green–Kubo asymptotic variance \(\Gamma(p)=(-12p^4+50p^3-43p^2+14p-1)/[(3p-1)^3(5p-1)]\) for \(1/2<p\leq1\). This variance decreases strictly from the singular limit \(4\) to \(1/4\), even though the stationary measures collapse to the absorbing atom at criticality, where its variance is \(0\).
