For the circle map \(z\mapsto(\alpha+e^{i\theta}z)/|\alpha+e^{i\theta}z|\), the exact half-turn two-cycle locking half-width is \(\arcsin((1-\sqrt{1-\alpha^2})/2)\). The interior has one attracting and one repelling two-cycle, while each endpoint has one neutral two-cycle; the two orbit branches merge at a sharp square-root rate.
