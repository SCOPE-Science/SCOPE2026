A single-site sink in a dynamically percolated Grover walk on a hypercube has an exact Hamming-shell-dependent absorption probability expressible through the electrical resistance of the cube with that vertex deleted. For a single initial edge at fixed depth \(k\), the positive sink-deletion correction decays as \(k((k-1)!)^2/(2d^{2k})\) in dimension \(d\), giving a sharp radial-shielding law beyond the previously analyzed three-cube.
