Every privileged binary word with exactly three ones belongs to an explicit palindromic or nonpalindromic zero-gap family. Their separate generating functions are \(z^3/(1-z^2)^2\) and \(2z^8/((1-z^2)(1-z^3)(1-z^4))\), so nonpalindromes asymptotically dominate this first nonpalindromic sparse stratum.
