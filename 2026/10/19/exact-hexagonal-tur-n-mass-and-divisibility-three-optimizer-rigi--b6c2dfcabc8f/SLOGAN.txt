For the seven-point triangular-lattice neighborhood on \((\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)^2\), the sharp positive-definite Turán mass is an explicit nearest-third-root trigonometric expression, equal to \(3\) exactly when \(3\mid n\) and strictly larger otherwise. Its optimizer is unique away from three-divisibility, but at multiples of three the optimizer set has real dimension four.
