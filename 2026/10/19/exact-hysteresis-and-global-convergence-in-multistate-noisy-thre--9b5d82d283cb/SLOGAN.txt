The multistate noisy three-majority mean-field map has two exact noise thresholds, \(1/(k+1)\) and \(k/(5k-4)\), enclosing a genuine hysteresis interval where a uniform opinion distribution and \(k\) competing ordered distributions are all locally stable. A strict potential-increase inequality proves that every deterministic frequency trajectory converges to a fixed point, without claiming the same finite-population stochastic behavior.
