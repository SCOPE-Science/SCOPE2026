For two-dimensional perfect evolution algebras over every finite field, having no nonzero idempotents is equivalent to irreducibility of an explicit cubic; exactly one third of perfect marked tables qualify. Their exact isomorphism census is \(q(q+1)/6\) or \((q(q+1)+4)/6\), with one exceptional order-three automorphism class precisely when \(q\equiv1\pmod3\).
