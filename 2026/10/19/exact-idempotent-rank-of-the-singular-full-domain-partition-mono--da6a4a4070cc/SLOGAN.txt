The singular ideal of the full-domain partition monoid has exact idempotent rank \(n(n-1)\) for every \(n\ge3\): all \(\binom{n}{2}\) elementary equivalence projections, plus the \(\binom{n}{2}\) defect-one transformations from any strongly connected tournament, form a minimum set. The exceptional case \(n=2\) has idempotent rank \(3\).
