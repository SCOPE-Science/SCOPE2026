For every \(n\ge9\), the identifying-code number of the complementary prism of \(C_n\) is exactly \(7\lfloor n/9\rfloor+a_{n\bmod9}\), where the nine offsets are \((0,1,1,3,3,4,5,6,6)\). This replaces a previously known \(7n/9+O(1)\) estimate with a complete periodic formula, proved by a finite-state shortest-path certificate and explicit constructions.
