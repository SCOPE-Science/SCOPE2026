For every binary Sturmian word of irrational \(1\)-frequency \(\alpha\) and every integer \(k\ge2\), the exact \(k\)-pass tortoise image complexity at length \(n\ge1\) is \(\max\{2,n+1-\lfloor k/\alpha\rfloor\}\). The final length at which it agrees with Abelian complexity is precisely \(\lfloor k/\alpha\rfloor+1\), giving a Beatty-structured hierarchy independent of the Sturmian intercept.
