For ordered \(k\)-tuples of matrices over \(\mathbb F_q\) with every pairwise product zero, a Gaussian-binomial and subspace-Möbius formula gives the exact joint distribution of their total image dimension and common-kernel codimension. Equivalently, it counts labeled modules of the local radical-square-zero \(k\)-loop algebra by both radical and socle sizes, in every dimension and over every finite field.
