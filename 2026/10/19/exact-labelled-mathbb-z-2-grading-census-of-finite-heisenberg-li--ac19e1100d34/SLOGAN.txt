For every finite Heisenberg Lie algebra \(H_n(\mathbb F_q)\), all labelled \(\mathbb Z/2\)-gradings split into \(n+1\) even-center symplectic strata and one odd-center Lagrangian stratum, each with an exact cardinality. The odd-center stratum comprises a proportion \(1-q^{-n}+\mathcal{O}_n(q^{-n-1})\) as \(q\) grows, and in characteristic two none of the nontrivial gradings comes from an inner adjoint idempotent.
