**Correctness — PASS.** The proof uses the exact fixed-point identities \(p_{2j}=0\) and \(p_{2j+1}=1-p_j\) to exclude \(11\) and \(0000\), rules out all odd fully represented conjugacy classes beyond length three, and establishes an explicit bijection \([u]\mapsto[\delta(u)]\) for each even length at least four. Both injectivity and surjectivity are proved, including why any suitable substituted factor must start at an even absolute index. The three base counts determine all positive lengths. Reproducible finite verification through length 200 corroborates but is not used to justify infinite quantifiers.

Dedicated search for period-doubling Lie complexity and equivalent dyadic support found no covering formula. A formulation hidden in unpublished notes or unindexed work remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** Lie complexity measures fully realizable cyclic conjugacy classes rather than usual factor complexity. The result gives a complete infinite-length answer for a canonical non-Sturmian automatic word, identifies a structural desubstitution bijection, and shows that its nonzero values are supported at only two geometric progressions (zero natural density). This extends the landscape of exact examples for the invariant introduced in [1] and distinguishes it from the previously studied cyclic-complexity count.
