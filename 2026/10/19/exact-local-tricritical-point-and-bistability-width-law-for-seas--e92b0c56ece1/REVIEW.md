# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The model is specified by a normalized lifespan size bias and an analytic one-dimensional Poisson cavity map. Exact symbolic expansion yields the zero-state threshold, the sole quadratic-coefficient cancellation, and the negative cubic coefficient. Two separate implicit-function arguments yield the local branch and the saddle-node; the signs of \(G'(q)-1=q h_q\) distinguish the two positive branches. The included verification script reproduces the formal identities and evaluates finite parameter witnesses. The results are expressly local; iteration stability is not biological physical-time stability.

## Originality

PASS, subject to residual risk. The entire mathematical analysis and conclusions of Clegg and Gross's 2026 arXiv preprint were compared with the particular sharp tricritical coordinates and bistability-width coefficient. The paper provides the basic cavity equations and qualitative changes of regime; the present quantified codimension-two expansion is more specific. Hypergraph core tricritical methods and cross-feeding network bistability are acknowledged as established distinct settings. Focused research-record and literature searches did not reveal this exact specialization. A generic or unindexed percolation analysis could subsume these coefficients, and the work makes no stronger uniqueness claim across all literature.

## Value

PASS. The mean active lifetime where the onset changes orientation is a natural target parameter for interpreting seasonal temporal bottlenecks. The square-root amplitude gives the critical scaling of viable diversity, and the small quadratic width explains why a bistable regime close to this lifetime can be difficult to resolve numerically. The result supplies reproducible local benchmark quantities in the model identified by recent ecological-network research, not merely a numerical parameter sweep.
