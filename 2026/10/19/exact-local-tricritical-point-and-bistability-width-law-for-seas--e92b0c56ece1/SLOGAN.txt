In the shifted-Poisson seasonal plant–pollinator viability model, the unique local tricritical point occurs at mean active lifetime \(\tau_*=1.420049608669704\ldots\) and mean degree \(z_*=1.470159219746206\ldots\). At the point, viable cavity states emerge with square-root scaling; immediately above it, an attracting high-viability cavity state coexists with the attracting zero state in a parameter window of width \(1.711455938024846\ldots(\tau-\tau_*)^2+O((\tau-\tau_*)^3)\), a local rather than global statement.
