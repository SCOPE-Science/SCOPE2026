For independent Gaussian one-direction exact Newton sketches on any positive-definite planar quadratic, the exact long-run expected-energy factor is \((3+q^2+\sqrt{1+14q^2+q^4})/8\), with \(q=(\sqrt\kappa-1)/(\sqrt\kappa+1)\). It is strictly smaller for every \(\kappa>1\) than the sharp one-step factor \(\sqrt\kappa/(1+\sqrt\kappa)\), distinguishing long-run decay from the uniform-in-time bound.
