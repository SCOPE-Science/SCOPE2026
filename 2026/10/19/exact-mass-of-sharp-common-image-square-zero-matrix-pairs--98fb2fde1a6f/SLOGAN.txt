When two square-zero operators on a \(4r\)-dimensional space have products with the same image of the maximally possible rank \(r\), the number of labeled ordered pairs over \(\mathbb F_q\) is exactly \(q^{-3r^2}\lvert\mathrm{GL}_{4r}(q)\rvert\). The proof reduces the equality locus to invertible similarity parameters and shows that their centralizer denominators sum to one.
