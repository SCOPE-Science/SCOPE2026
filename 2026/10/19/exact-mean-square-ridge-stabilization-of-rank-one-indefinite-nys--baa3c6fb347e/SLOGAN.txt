For a normalized Gaussian one-column Nyström sketch of the balanced \(2\times2\) indefinite matrix with eigenvalues \(\pm\lambda\), the scalar-core ridge inverse has uniquely optimal parameter \(\tau=\lambda/\sqrt2\) and expected squared Frobenius error \(8\sqrt3\lambda^2/9\). This is strictly better in expectation than the best hard core cutoff, whose exact error is \((1+2/\pi)\lambda^2\), while the unregularized inverse has infinite mean-square error.
