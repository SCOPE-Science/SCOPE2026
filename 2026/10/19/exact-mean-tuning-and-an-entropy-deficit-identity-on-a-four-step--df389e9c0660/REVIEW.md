# Review of the exact skew-tent Markov-curve finding

**Correctness (PASS).** All real parameters \(s>1\) are addressed by a direct four-point critical itinerary, a primitive three-cell expanding Markov partition, an exactly normalized Frobenius--Perron fixed density, exact mean differentiation, and a probability-vector KL proof of the unique entropy maximum. Endpoint conventions and expansion are explicit. The 190 checked rational instances and nine entropy comparisons supplement rather than replace the infinite analytical proof.

**Originality (PASS, residual risk noted).** Moysis et al. [1, 2] define the relevant map and seek theoretical mean tuning after numerical experiments. The exact 4-step critical orbit condition, all-parameter mean calibration and KL formula were not found in the complete relevant 2023 article or the mathematical records examined. The 2020 work [3] already establishes general skew-tent invariant-density methods and entropy flexibility; those general theorems are *not* claimed as discoveries. Bruin--Keszthelyi [4] addresses critical-orbit density and matching in a distinct zero-intercept family. The earlier conference version [1] was accessible only as a dated abstract; it remains a priority risk. A different two-cell endpoint-tent construction has a shorter critical orbit; it does not establish this three-cell formula.

**Value (PASS).** The result analytically calibrates every possible target mean in an explicit interval for a source-motivated family while maintaining the same symbolic topological complexity. The KL identity quantifies entropy loss away from a unique canonical parameter and exposes a physical-entropy collapse invisible to topological entropy. It is a motivated exact family-level invariant, not a single arbitrary parameter evaluation.

**Limitations.** It is restricted to the natural 4-step critical orbit curve, real exact arithmetic, and the physical acip; it does not control all parameters or finite-precision statistics. The general entropy-flexibility phenomenon was known, and the earlier conference full text was unavailable. An equivalent older closed form remains possible.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
