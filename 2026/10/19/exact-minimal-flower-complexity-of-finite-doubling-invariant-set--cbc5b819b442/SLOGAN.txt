For every finite invariant set of the circle doubling map, the minimum number of petals of a containing flower is exactly the number of switches in the ordered predecessor-branch word, including a necessary seam correction. In particular the full set of points fixed by the \(n\)-th iterate requires exactly \(2^n-1\) petals, and a single primitive cycle requires one fewer petal than the number of runs in its Burrows--Wheeler transform.
