For quadratic group-conformity updates on complete uniform hypergraphs, the probability that a fixed handful of dissenters converts the entire population is exactly computable and has a sharp \(N^{-1/2}e^{-NI_d}\) rare-event law. Its true exponent \(I_d\) strictly exceeds the Gaussian diffusion exponent for every fixed nonlinear group size, revealing a boundary-layer failure invisible near balanced opinions.
