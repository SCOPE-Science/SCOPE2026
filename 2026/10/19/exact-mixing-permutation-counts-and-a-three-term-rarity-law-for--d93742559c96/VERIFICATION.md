# Reproducibility and verification

Run `python3 verify.py` with Python 3 standard library. Expected result:

`VERIFY_OK 5166 full fine-graph comparisons; 70 exact recurrence levels; N=3,5,7 counts (4, 80, 3552)`

For every permutation of \(N\) cells with \(N\in\{3,5,7\}\), the code constructs the entire \(2N\)-state fine Markov graph directly from the images of fine cells, tests strong connectivity by two traversals, and measures the directed period using the gcd of edge-distance differences. Separately it checks transitivity of the group generated by the associated permutation \(U\) and reflection \(H\); these agree exhaustively and reproduce the exact recurrence counts. The exact recurrence is evaluated through \(k=70\) with integer arithmetic; an additional rational spot check tests the three-term asymptotic approximation.

These computations verify the definitions and many critical finite cases. They cannot establish all \(N\), nor the infinite asymptotic. The universal facts require the parity, fixed-point-orbit and ratio-bound arguments in `RESULT.md`; those arguments are supplied in full. This review is not independent external validation.
