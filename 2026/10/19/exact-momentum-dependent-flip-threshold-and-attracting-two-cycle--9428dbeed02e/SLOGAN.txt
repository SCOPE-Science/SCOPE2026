For the smooth-sign scalar Muesterov iteration on a strongly convex quadratic, momentum \(0\le\beta<1\) produces the sharp stability boundary \(a_c(\beta)=2(1+\beta)/(1+2\beta)\). Beyond it there is exactly one primitive two-cycle, with explicit square-root amplitude, and that cycle is locally exponentially attracting at every larger step; at zero momentum a complete global convergence-versus-oscillation dichotomy also holds.
