For the map \(T_b(x)=bx\bmod1\), strictly increasing orbit segments of length \(m+1\) have exact Lebesgue probability \(\binom{m+b-1}{b-2}/((b-1)b^m)\). Conditionally on this rare event, their empirical measures converge to a Dirichlet-uniform random mixture of the map's \(b-1\) interior fixed-point measures, and their orbit averages converge to a rescaled sum of \(b-2\) independent uniform variables.
