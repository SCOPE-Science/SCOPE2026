The complete normalized Gram spectrum of all single-coordinate multiplicative Fourier characters restricted to the finite-field hypersurface \(\sum_i a_i x_i=b\), with every \(x_i\ne0\), is explicit for every field order and dimension. Moving the level \(b\) away from zero removes all rank defects except the one at \((q,d)=(3,3)\), yielding exact linear-sampling ranks and stability constants.
