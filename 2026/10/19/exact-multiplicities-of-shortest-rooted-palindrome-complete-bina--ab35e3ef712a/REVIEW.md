# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Each cyclic word rooted at a prescribed all-zero length-\(k\) window corresponds bijectively to an augmented de Bruijn-graph path beginning with the zero-loop and ending at the zero vertex with all required palindrome-edge bits set. A complete BFS yields exact shortest distances and counts the shortest paths without multiplicity ambiguity. An independent compulsory-edge Held–Karp dynamic program reproduces the shortest lengths and its optimal tour counts agree with the BFS counts. Both program sources are embedded, compiled, and executed, checking the computed values \(R_7=6272\), \(R_8=768\) and all earlier orders. Computational limits: only \(2\le k\le8\); no general expression is asserted.

**Originality — PASS on examined evidence, with explicit risk.** The nearest exact database values are OEIS A330022 and A390882, describing shortest **linear** superstring lengths and multiplicities. The length data match but are acknowledged prior art, and the linear multiplicities at orders seven and eight are \(960\) and \(176\), not the rooted cyclic multiplicities \(6272\) and \(768\). None of the inspected literature provides this rooted cycle count. Targeted mathematical-record searches for cyclic palindrome-covering counts and aliases returned no matching theorem. Residual risk: specialized cyclic-cover enumeration could exist outside the indexed and inspected sources.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Rooted optimal-cycle multiplicity is an intrinsic count of minimum-length periodic pattern-test designs, distinct from counting shortest linear strings. It measures design freedom at a fixed optimum rather than just rechecking already known shortest linear lengths. The marked drop from \(6272\) to \(768\) even though both orders require sixteen palindromes gives a concrete structural distinction between odd and even windows. The finite cutoff is the complete first regime with at most sixteen compulsory patterns; the next order has thirty-two. No large-order theorem or practical experimental validation is claimed.

Closest sources: Anisiu–Anisiu–Kása (2010), Section 2; OEIS A330022 and A390882; Blondin Massé et al. (2008); Orenstein et al. (2013). Main residual risk is equivalent indexed or unpublished cyclic enumeration. The public conclusion is narrower than a general minimal-superstring theorem.
