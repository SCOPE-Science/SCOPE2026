# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** At each uniformly chosen leaf, the cherry indicator and selected depth give an exact four-state conditional moment system. The auxiliary expectation of the sum of depths of cherry-member leaves is derived with its three-leaf initial condition, followed by an exact covariance recurrence and telescoping. The boundary at two leaves is explicitly handled. An independent exact enumeration of weighted growth histories through twelve leaves and rational recurrences through three hundred leaves matches every asserted finite formula (1232 comparisons); the proof is analytic and does not rely on finite enumeration.

**Originality — PASS, subject to residual risk.** Ford (2005) treats the Sackin–Colless pair and separately counts cherries, Cardona et al. (2012) treat Sackin/cophenetic moments, and Wu–Choi (2015) treat cherry/pitchfork joint distributions. The relevant accessible primary texts were examined, including introductions and claim-bearing sections. Their published formulas do not directly give the cherry–Sackin covariance or its cherry-member-depth auxiliary moment. Indexed searches of synonymous claims and relevant mathematical findings likewise uncovered no covering statement. This is a best-knowledge assessment, not a guarantee that an older joint enumeration cannot imply the formula; the complete McKenzie–Steel article was not read.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Cherry number and Sackin depth are two standard but different Yule null-model diagnostics: local sister-pair abundance and global shape depth. An exact mixed moment is needed for calibrating joint diagnostics rather than combining marginal error bars as independent, and the strictly negative sign at all nondegenerate sample sizes is an interpretable structural dependence. The cancellation of harmonic growth through the new cherry-member-depth identity is non-obvious, yields a closed finite-size law, and fixes its linear asymptotic scale.
