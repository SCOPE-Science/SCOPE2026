For Yule–Harding–Kingman binary trees, the covariance of cherry count with the Sackin leaf-depth sum is exactly \(-\frac{(n-3)(n+2)}{9(n-1)}\) for every \(n\ge3\), hence strictly negative from four leaves onward. An exact cherry-member depth moment makes all harmonic terms cancel, leaving a simple finite-size covariance.
