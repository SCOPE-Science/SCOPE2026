In the neutral complete-population game–strategy imitation model, the entire joint distribution of the two eventual surviving attributes is exactly a mixture of their independent initial frequencies and their initial paired-label distribution. The paired component has the sharp coefficient \(\theta=p_sp_g/[N(p_s+p_g-p_sp_g)+p_sp_g]\le1/(N+1)\), so neutral shared-model imitation alone cannot sustain order-one game–strategy association from a fixed initial contingency table as population size grows.
