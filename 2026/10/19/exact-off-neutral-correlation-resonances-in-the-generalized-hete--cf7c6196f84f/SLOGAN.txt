The generalized two-dimensional heterochaos baker map has an exact algebraic generating function for its Lebesgue-stationary central-coordinate autocovariances. Two off-neutral pole–branch collisions at \(a=1/10\) and \(a=2/5\) change the correlation prefactor, while the integrated covariance has a unique cusp maximum at the familiar neutral parameter \(a=1/4\).
