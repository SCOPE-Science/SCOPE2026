The classical Heawood and Pappus incidence graphs have domination-game starting values \((7,6)\) and \((8,7)\), respectively, and both require an adjacent second move for Staller to prolong a Dominator-start game optimally. In the Staller-start game, every Dominator reply is optimal on Heawood, but exactly the two distance-four replies are optimal on Pappus; all legal two-ply scores are exhaustively classified.
