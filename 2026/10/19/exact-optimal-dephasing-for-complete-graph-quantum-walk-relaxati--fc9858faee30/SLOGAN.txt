For a complete-graph quantum walk with uniform site dephasing, the unique noise strength maximizing the full Lindbladian relaxation gap is \(\gamma_*=J(n-1)\sqrt{n/(n-2)}\), for every \(n\ge3\). The maximal gap is exactly \(J\sqrt{n/(n-2)}\), proved using a three-dimensional spectral reduction and a sharp real-root discriminant bound.
