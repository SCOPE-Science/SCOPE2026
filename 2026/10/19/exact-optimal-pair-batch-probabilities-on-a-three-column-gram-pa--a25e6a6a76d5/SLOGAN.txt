For a three-column least-squares design whose Gram correlations form a path, the exact rate-optimal probability law for two-coordinate randomized block minimization is explicit, strictly positive, and unique even with unequal adjacent correlations. A balanced residual direction certifies the sharp worst-case one-step contraction \(\Delta/Z\), where \(\Delta=1-u^2-v^2\) and \(Z=3-u^2-v^2-2u\sqrt{1-v^2}-2v\sqrt{1-u^2}\).
