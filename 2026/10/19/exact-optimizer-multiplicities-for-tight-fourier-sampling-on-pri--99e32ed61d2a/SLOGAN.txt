For an arbitrary spatial set on the cyclic group \(\mathbb Z/p^k\mathbb Z\), every smallest unweighted tight Fourier sampling frame is a prescribed \(p\)-ary digit tree, and its exact number of realizations is \(p^{\sum_{j\notin J}p^{|J\cap\{0,\dots,j-1\}|}}\), where \(J\) is determined by the valuations of spatial differences. Every optimizer has the same translation stabilizer, yielding an exact orbit count as well.
