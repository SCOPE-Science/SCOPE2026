# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The pair automaton is finite and fully specified; reverse multi-source BFS from all diagonal pairs computes the exact minimum word length needed to merge every pair, as proved by path reversal and BFS shortest-path minimality. The main standalone checker exhausts every state pair for each of the \(1000\) parameter values, confirms all pairs merge, and detects the exact claimed diameter and its unique maximizing pair. It processes \(1{,}510{,}524{,}000\) off-diagonal pair vertices in aggregate. Independent explicit reversed-adjacency BFS for \(k\le40\) and independent forward BFS from the farthest pair for ten widely separated parameters, including \(k=1000\), agree. The finite program is an exact exhaustive certificate for the specified range, **not** a general proof for arbitrary \(k\); rerunning the checker requires a standard C++17 environment.

**Value — PASS.** Pairwise merging diameter is a natural measure of the hardest two-state synchronization subproblem. The additional unique-extremizer classification identifies where the worst-case difficulty occurs and may guide any future general proof. The result makes no claim to settle Conjecture 8.2 for all integers.
