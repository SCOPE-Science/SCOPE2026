For the first \(1000\) Dżyga–Szykuła automata \(A_k\), the exact worst-case cost of merging an arbitrary state pair obeys the conjectured parity-dependent quadratic formula, and **one consecutive state pair** uniquely realizes the maximum for each \(k\). A full reverse-pair-graph census proves these finite statements, extending the published proved diameter range from \(k\le16\) to \(k\le1000\); no conclusion for larger \(k\) is asserted.
