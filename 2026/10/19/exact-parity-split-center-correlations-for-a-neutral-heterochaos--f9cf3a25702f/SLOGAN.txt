The neutral three-dimensional heterochaos baker map has an **exact algebraic generating function** for correlations of its central coordinate, and the sharp decay is \(\sqrt{2/\pi}(1+(-1)^n/81)n^{-3/2}+O(n^{-5/2})\). The distinct even- and odd-lag amplitudes refine the previously known optimal \(n^{-3/2}\) mixing rate and arise from the two square-root endpoints of an absorbing-walk Green function.
