Exponential-timestep Euler on \(\dot u=-a u\) is exponentially stable almost surely up to the **sharp** mean-step threshold \(ah=2.6845103508207\ldots\), even though its second moment becomes unstable at \(ah=1\) and its signed mean at \(ah=2\). The threshold is the reciprocal of the unique positive zero of \(\operatorname{Ei}\), and the same scalar model has an exact continuum of absolute-moment stability boundaries.
