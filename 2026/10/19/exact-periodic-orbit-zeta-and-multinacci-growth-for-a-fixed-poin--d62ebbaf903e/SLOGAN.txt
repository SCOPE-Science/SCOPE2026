For every integer central gain \(k\ge2\), a fixed-point-free four-branch interval map has the exact Artin–Mazur zeta \(\zeta(t)=(1-t^{k+1})/[1-(t+\cdots+t^k)^2]\). Its periodic-point growth is controlled by the multinacci root; the numerator records one boundary orbit that has two distinct symbolic representations.
