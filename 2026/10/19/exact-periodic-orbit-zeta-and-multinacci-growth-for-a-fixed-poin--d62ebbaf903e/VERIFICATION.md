# Verification evidence and limits

Run `python verify.py` with Python 3 standard library. The checker enumerates exact rational branches for integer gains \(k=2,3,4,5\) and periods \(1\le n\le10\); it passed 96 checks, including the exact periodic point correction. It does not establish the infinite theorem, which depends on the written graph coding, unique inverse-cycle representatives, the classification of endpoint germs, the renewal determinant and Perron–Frobenius. No claim of independent proof review is made.
