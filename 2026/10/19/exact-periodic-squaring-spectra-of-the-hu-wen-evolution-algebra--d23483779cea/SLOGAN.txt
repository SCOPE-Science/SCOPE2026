For the Hu–Wen evolution algebras over every odd finite field, the nonlinear squaring map has an explicit fixed-iterate count for every period, hence a complete cycle distribution. At the idempotent-free parameter \(\lambda=2\), every nonzero periodic orbit has length divisible by the field characteristic, despite a periodic locus of size \(1+q\,\operatorname{oddpart}(q-1)\).
