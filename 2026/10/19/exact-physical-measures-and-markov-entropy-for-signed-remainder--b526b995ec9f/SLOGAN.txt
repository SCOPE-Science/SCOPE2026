For every integer \(m\ge2\), signed-remainder chaotification of the contracting linear map has two opposite, mixing physical phases whose exact stationary distributions come from a primitive \((4m-4)\)-state Markov chain with entropy \(\log m\). Every nonzero real orbit enters its sign's compact invariant core, yet neither physical distribution is uniform; at \(m=2\), the four stationary cell masses are exactly \((1/6,1/3,1/3,1/6)\).
