Identifying \(M\geq2\) infinite tight-binding wires at two sites \(L\geq2\) lattice steps apart creates exactly \((M-1)(L-1)\) compact embedded eigenstates, with known energies and multiplicities, and either one or two simple symmetric pairs of exterior-band bound states. The second bound pair is absent only for \((M,L)=(2,2)\), where it is replaced by a non-square-summable band-edge resonance; the full classification requires no on-site potential.
