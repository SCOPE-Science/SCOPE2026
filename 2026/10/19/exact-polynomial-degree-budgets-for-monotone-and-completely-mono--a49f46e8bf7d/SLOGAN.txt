For exact-endpoint-slope polynomial dense output of two-state exponential decay, monotone conservative positivity needs only degree \(2\sqrt z+O(1)\) at normalized stiffness \(z\), with exact sharp degree-by-degree thresholds from Legendre and Jacobi kernel bounds. Requiring complete monotonicity instead needs the exact degree \(\lceil z(1-e^{-z})/[1-(1+z)e^{-z}]\rceil=z+O(1)\).
