For fourth-order Gauss–Legendre diffusion on a star with one exceptional spoke of conductance \(x\) and \(k\ge2\) ordinary unit spokes, every timestep preserves nonnegative states exactly when \(1/2\le x\le1+1/k\). Outside this sharp heterogeneity window the complete positivity set is an explicitly computable finite interval, and sufficiently weak exceptional spokes exhibit a second, later sign loss between ordinary leaves.
