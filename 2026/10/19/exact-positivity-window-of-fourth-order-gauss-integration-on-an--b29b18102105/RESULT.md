# Exact positivity window of fourth-order Gauss integration on an eight-state directed ring

## Finding

Let \(P\) be the \(8\times8\) cyclic permutation matrix with \(P_{i,i+1}=1\) for \(i=0,\ldots,6\), and \(P_{7,0}=1\). Define the continuous-time Markov generator \(Q=P-I\). Its rate-one directed ring is irreducible, conserves mass, and has the uniform stationary distribution. For timestep \(h\ge0\), define the two-stage fourth-order Gauss--Legendre endpoint map
\[
T(h)=(12I+6hQ+h^2Q^2)(12I-6hQ+h^2Q^2)^{-1}.
\]
There are two *distinct, uniquely specified algebraic constants* \(0<\ell<u\), given by the positive roots of
\[
F_-(h)=7h^6+84h^5+420h^4+1008h^3+1008h^2-432
\]
and
\[
\begin{aligned}
F_+(h)={}&5h^{12}+96h^{11}+792h^{10}+3672h^9+9288h^8\\
&-111024h^6-528768h^5-1446336h^4-2612736h^3\\
&-3110400h^2-2239488h-746496.
\end{aligned}
\]
Their numerical values are \(\ell\approx0.512251544372714\) and \(u\approx3.52719746626943\). Then
\[
\boxed{T(h)\text{ is entrywise nonnegative}\iff h=0\ \text{or}\ \ell\le h\le u.}
\]
Every \(T(h)\) is real, has unit row and column sums, and exists for all \(h\ge0\). Thus it is doubly stochastic precisely on this positivity set. In particular a *smaller* positive step can destroy positivity, which returns on a bounded intermediate interval before failing permanently at larger steps.

The first forbidden interval is witnessed by the coefficient of \(P^7\), whose leading term is \(-h^7/1728\). The later forbidden ray is witnessed by the coefficient of \(P\). The underlying continuous-time semigroup \(e^{tQ}\) is entrywise nonnegative for all \(t\ge0\). For a common transition rate \(\lambda>0\), the physical steps are those satisfying \(\lambda h=0\) or \(\ell\le\lambda h\le u\).

## Assumptions and scope

All statements concern exact real arithmetic, exactly eight states, the homogeneous one-way ring, and the linear end-step map. We use the row-generator convention \(Q\mathbf1=0\); the result is unchanged by transposing the orientation. Entrywise nonnegativity refers to every one of the 64 entries. No inference about positivity of intermediate Runge--Kutta stages is made.

## Proof

The scalar Padé rational function is \(r(z)=(12+6z+z^2)/(12-6z+z^2)\). Its denominator vanishes only at \(3\pm i\sqrt3\), in the open right half-plane. Every eigenvalue of \(Q=P-I\) has nonpositive real part, so \(T(h)=r(hQ)\) exists for all \(h\ge0\). Because \(Q\mathbf1=0\), \(\mathbf1^TQ=0\), and \(r(0)=1\), all row and column sums equal one.

Since \(P^8=I\), polynomial inversion modulo \(x^8-1\) gives unique rational coefficients \(c_k(h)\) such that \(T(h)=\sum_{k=0}^7 c_k(h)P^k\). Every matrix entry is one of these eight coefficients. For \(h>0\), write their common positive denominator as
\[
\begin{aligned}
W(h)={}&4(h^2+3h+3)(h^4+6h^3+18h^2+36h+36)\\
&\cdot(h^8+12h^7+84h^6+432h^5+1800h^4+5184h^3\\
&\qquad+9504h^2+10368h+5184).
\end{aligned}
\]
Direct reduction of \((12+6h(x-1)+h^2(x-1)^2)/(12-6h(x-1)+h^2(x-1)^2)\) modulo \(x^8-1\) yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
Wc_1&=-3hF_+(h),\\
Wc_2&=-9h^2(h+2)F_2(h),\\
Wc_3&=-3h^3F_3(h),\\
Wc_7&=3h^7F_-(h),
\end{aligned}
\]
where
\[
\begin{aligned}
F_2(h)={}&h^{10}+10h^9-516h^7-4248h^6-19728h^5-60912h^4\\
&-128736h^3-181440h^2-155520h-62208,\\
F_3(h)={}&h^{10}-12h^9-396h^8-3888h^7-22176h^6-84672h^5\\
&-225072h^4-414720h^3-508032h^2-373248h-124416.
\end{aligned}
\]
The other four numerators \(Wc_0\), \(Wc_4\), \(Wc_5\), and \(Wc_6\) are nonzero polynomials with nonnegative coefficients, hence strictly positive at \(h>0\). Their complete expansions are independently generated by the accompanying exact symbolic checker, using polynomial inversion rather than a precomputed table.

Each of \(F_-\), \(F_+\), \(F_2\), and \(F_3\) has exactly one positive real zero: its descending nonzero coefficient sequence changes sign just once, and the constant and leading terms have opposite signs (Descartes' rule plus continuity). Moreover, exact substitution gives
\[
F_-(0)<0<F_-(1),\qquad F_+(3)<0<F_+(4),
\]
and \(F_2(4)<0\), \(F_3(4)<0\). Thus \(0<\ell<1<3<u<4\), while the positive roots of \(F_2\) and \(F_3\) both exceed \(4\).

Consequently, for \(\ell\le h\le u\) the coefficients \(c_1,c_2,c_3,c_7\) are nonnegative, as are the remaining four coefficients. For \(0<h<\ell\), \(c_7<0\); for \(h>u\), \(c_1<0\). Finally \(T(0)=I\). This proves the exact set, including both boundary steps. The expansion of \(c_7\) at zero is \(-h^7/1728+O(h^8)\), matching the first negative coefficient of the rational exponential approximation.

## Verification

The standalone \(\texttt{artifacts/verify.py}\) uses exact SymPy polynomial arithmetic to reconstruct all eight rational coefficients by inversion modulo \(x^8-1\), check the four sign-controlling factorizations, certify positivity of the other four numerator polynomials, establish each one-root sign argument, and evaluate six independent rational matrix inverses. It also checks stochastic row/column sums, the small-step leading coefficient, and the algebraic normalization. Run using `python artifacts/verify.py` with Python 3 and SymPy installed. The printed decimals are informational; the proof uses only exact inequalities and uniqueness of roots.

## Relationship to prior work

Zappavigna, Colaneri, Kirkland and Shorten (2012) proved that diagonal Padé methods can violate positivity at arbitrarily small steps, using an eight-dimensional nilpotent shift and shifted Metzler matrices. Their construction is not a conservative, irreducible uniform ring and does not state the present two algebraic thresholds. Lóczi and Ketcheson (2013 preprint; 2014 journal) analyzed radii of absolute monotonicity for rational Runge--Kutta stability functions, a general obstruction that does not determine this graph-specific all-step positivity set. The published *Eight-state barrier for Markov positivity of the [2/2] Padé map* establishes that seven-state-or-smaller CTMC generators are eventually positive at sufficiently small steps and gives an exact window for an eight-state *absorbing pure-birth chain*. Our finding shows that the sharp eight-state behavior also occurs in an irreducible, doubly balanced cyclic chain, with **different** exact switching polynomials and endpoints. It neither claims the global dimension barrier nor the Padé negativity mechanism as new.

## Limitations

This is one canonical finite irreducible Markov generator, not a classification over all eight-state chains, all directed cycles, or nonuniform rate assignments. The exact algebraic thresholds apply to the mathematical endpoint map; floating-point implementations can introduce rounding errors near either boundary. Earlier general Padé nonpositivity results and the eight-state dimension threshold are not new contributions. A mathematically equivalent unpublished or unindexed computation of this particular uniform ring remains possible.
