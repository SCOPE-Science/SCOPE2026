The two-stage Gauss--Legendre method on the irreducible uniform eight-state directed Markov ring is doubly stochastic exactly for \(h=0\) and one bounded interval \(0.5122515\ldots\le h\le3.5271974\ldots\). Thus making the timestep smaller can **create** a negative transition probability, which later disappears before returning at large steps; both switching boundaries are exact unique polynomial roots.
