For symmetric Markov chains on \(K_{n,n}\) with one perfect matching priced differently, the entire minimum-cost-versus-mixing frontier has two exact thresholds: the price \(2(n-1)/(n-2)\) and the mixing rate \(n/(3n-4)\). Above the price threshold the optimum deletes every matched edge when slower mixing is allowed, but retains matched edges for faster mixing; at the price threshold a complete continuum of invariant optimal allocations appears.
