Repeating the distinctly labeled Zimin word produces a rich periodic word whose privileged and palindromic factor sets coincide. At every positive length \(\ell\), their common complexity is exactly one for even \(\ell\) and \(\max\{1,n-\lfloor\log_2\ell\rfloor\}\) for odd \(\ell\); a single circular period contains exactly \(3\cdot2^{n-1}-2\) distinct nonempty factors of either kind when lengths are limited to that period.
