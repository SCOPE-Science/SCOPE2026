For a waning-immunity epidemic with two constant transmission seasons, vaccination concentrated exactly at the start of the high season is uniquely optimal among all fixed-mass periodic measures if and only if the per-period mass does not exceed an explicit sharp threshold. In the published half-year example that threshold is exactly \(\log\!\frac{7(1-e^{-1})}{7-6e^{-1/2}-e^{-1}}=0.390979957597\ldots\), resolving the numerically observed transition near \(0.4\); a general inequality identifies whether reallocation first becomes favorable just before or just after the seasonal jump.
