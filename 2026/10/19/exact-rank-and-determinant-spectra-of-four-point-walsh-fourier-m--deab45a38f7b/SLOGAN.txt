For the unnormalized Walsh Fourier matrix of \(\mathbb F_2^d\), every \(4\times4\) minor has absolute determinant \(0\), \(8\), or \(16\), and all three multiplicities are explicit polynomials in \(N=2^d\). A complete affine-plane versus affine-independent classification simultaneously determines the number of minors of each real rank \(1,2,3,4\) for every \(d\ge3\).
