Every three-by-three Fourier minor of a cyclic prime-power group has an exact rank census determined by the minimum and maximum \(p\)-adic valuations of row differences. The complete rank-one multiplicity has a closed formula, and its probability among all minors is \((1-p^{-2})k/N^2+O_p(N^{-2})\), with \(N=p^k\).
