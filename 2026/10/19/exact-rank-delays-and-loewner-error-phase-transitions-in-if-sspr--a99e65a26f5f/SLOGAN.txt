On controllable integrator chains with \(d\ge2\), positivity-preserving IF-SSPRK2 and IF-SSPRK3 require exactly \(d-1\) and \(\lceil(d-1)/2\rceil\) steps to recover a full-rank finite-time Gramian, despite the exact Gramian being positive definite immediately. Their signed covariance errors undergo sharp all-step Loewner transitions: the second-order method becomes indefinite in dimension three and the third-order method in dimension four.
