The ranks of successive products of independent uniform idempotent self-maps of an \(n\)-point set form an explicitly enumerable finite Markov chain, whose integer transfer matrix counts products of every length. For fixed \(n\ge2\), the sharp synchronization tail is \(\Theta_n(\lambda_n^m)\), where \(\lambda_n\) is the exact probability that a random idempotent separates two prescribed points; ordered factor-rank product laws first become directionally asymmetric on four points.
