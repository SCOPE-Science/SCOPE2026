# Scientific review: exact binary three-length-limited composition profiles

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof derives a sufficient and necessary signature comprising the total weight, two run counts, two singleton-run counts, and a two-endpoint correction. Both directions are proved from the observed adjacent-pair and length-three composition counts, including the mixed triple counts. The separate feasibility analysis of the endpoint correction is checked for balanced and imbalanced run numbers. Summing existence-generating functions gives the rational generating function as a formal identity for all positive lengths; it is not an inference from fitted finite data. Literal enumeration through length 16, independently constructed run-feasibility sums through length 20, and a state-based finite automaton over observable counts through length 38 agree with the rational coefficients. The finite checks do not substitute for the infinite proof. Residual risk: an unnoticed algebraic simplification error or missing boundary case is possible despite independent checks.

**Originality — PASS on searched literature, with residual risk.** Ye and Elishco, *Reconstruction of a Single String from a Part of its Composition Multiset* (arXiv:2208.14963, Section VIII and conclusion), formulate the limited-composition model but do not give the exact limit-three fixed-blocklength count. Their Lyndon-word construction and rate bounds are not a determination of the full set of indistinguishability classes. Acharya et al., *String Reconstruction from Substring Compositions* (arXiv:1403.2439), use all substring lengths, which gives a strictly finer observation and does not determine the number of limit-three classes. The closest publicly indexed exact limit-two result has quadratic counts; the present limit-three recurrence requires additional singleton-run and endpoint information and has quartic growth, so neither implication nor reparameterization covers it. Ordered adjacent-pair spectrum findings are also insufficient. Searches for the formula, first terms, three-gram Parikh aliases, fragment composition reconstruction, stronger observation models, and publicly indexed mathematical findings did not identify covering work. Residual risk: a differently named finite-alphabet profile enumeration or unindexed earlier manuscript could be equivalent. Search failure itself is not treated as proof of novelty.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The fixed-length maximum reconstruction code size is an exact natural invariant of the length-limited composition channel introduced by Ye and Elishco. The next nontrivial limit beyond the previously solved binary limit-two case changes the growth degree from quadratic to quartic; exact coefficients and periodic fluctuations are fully identified for every blocklength. The proof isolates the new endpoint effect of three-symbol windows. This supplies a sharp capacity benchmark and a reusable method for studying further fixed observation lengths. The value is a natural complete infinite-family answer, not an arbitrary isolated small calculation. Scope is binary and noiseless; no claim about efficient encoding or higher limits is made.

(2014 preprint, arXiv:1403.2439), definition of full composition multiset; *Exact binary \(r=2\) length-limited composition reconstruction* (2026 public scientific record).
