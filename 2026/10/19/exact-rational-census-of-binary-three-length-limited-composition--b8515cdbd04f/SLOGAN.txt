The exact number of distinct binary substring-composition multisets when only fragment lengths at most three are measured has a rational generating function with denominator \((1-z)^5(1+z)(1+z^2)\). The largest exact reconstruction code under this observation has \(R_3(n)=n^4/32+O(n^3)\) codewords, improving the quadratic limit-two profile to a sharp quartic law.
