# Review of the exact binary trigram-multispectrum generating function

**Correctness — PASS.** The directed Euler-trail criterion is necessary and sufficient for profile realizability. The explicit imbalance parameterization, automatic connectedness of nonloop support, careful exclusion of disconnected loops, and rational summation provide a complete proof for all word lengths. Exact symbolic simplification supplies the displayed rational generating function, and the pole at one gives the stated leading coefficient. Independently enumerated source words and integer flows agree with all checked coefficients. Finite checks do not constitute the infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS, with explicit residual uncertainty.** The *sequence of initial values itself is known*: OEIS A132298 already records the first eighteen values, following Varshney and Goyal (2007). The scope of the present claim is deliberately narrower than claiming a new sequence: the inspected 2007 paper and table contain no exact infinite rational generating function or leading constant. The nearest general rationality literature (Cassaigne et al., 2017) analyzes finer 3-abelian equivalence, and OEIS A289657 is a different sequence, with 60 rather than 52 at length six. published-finding corpus searches located a formula for length-two read multispectra but none proving the exact length-three rational function. The 2017 journal full text was not accessible; a prior unindexed derivation remains a residual novelty risk. Search absence is not a uniqueness proof.

**Value — PASS.** The exact formula upgrades a previously finite Markov-type sequence to a provable all-length law and gives the exact leading asymptotic constant, which determines the sharp fourth-degree scaling of the observation-alphabet size in a canonical lossy-order, exact-read reconstruction problem. This is an infinite classification and explicit summation, not a routine new row of a published table. The proof supplies usable structural boundary cases at disconnected loops.

**Limitations.** Binary alphabet and complete contiguous length-three read histograms only; not a formula for word counts in each class, noisy reads, or general alphabets. The known table is acknowledged, and the 2017 inaccessible full-text issue remains documented. All checks are internal, with no independent validation.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
