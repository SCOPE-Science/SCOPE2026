For a fixed-speed, athermal run-and-tumble particle in a harmonic trap, exact finite-time stationary-to-stationary control using only positive stiffness and tumbling rates is possible precisely when the stationary edge exponent \(\beta=\alpha/(2\kappa)-1\) is unchanged and the support expands by less than \(vT\). The obstruction follows from a strictly positive no-tumble contribution that preserves the edge-tail power, while a smooth time-change construction achieves every allowed transfer.
