# Exact reset-age two-point correlation law and logarithmic edge crossover in a Glauber chain

## Finding
Consider the symmetric, zero-field Glauber Ising chain on the infinite integer lattice. For a fixed integer separation \(n\ge1\), the correlation averaged over spin trajectories and the reset preparation depends on the time since the most recent global reset. Sampling this time from its stationary exponential distribution therefore generates a nontrivial **random two-point correlator** \(Y_n\).

All statements below hold for \(n\in\{1,2,\ldots\}\), \(0<\gamma<1\), and resetting rate \(\rho>0\). Define
\[
q=\frac{1-\sqrt{1-\gamma^2}}{\gamma},\qquad
C_n(t)=n\int_0^t \frac{e^{-2u}I_n(2\gamma u)}{u}\,du,
\]
where \(I_n\) is the modified Bessel function. Then \(C_n\) is strictly increasing from \(0\) to \(q^n\). With its inverse \(t_n:(0,q^n)\to(0,\infty)\), the entire stationary reset-age law is
\[
\mathbb P(Y_n\le y)=1-e^{-\rho t_n(y)},\qquad
f_n(y)=\frac{\rho e^{-\rho t_n(y)}}{C_n'(t_n(y))},\quad 0<y<q^n.
\]
The density is zero outside that interval and carries no boundary atoms. In particular, as \(y\downarrow0\),
\[
f_n(y)\sim\frac{\rho}{n}\left(\frac{n!}{\gamma^n}\right)^{1/n}y^{1/n-1}.
\]
For the upper edge write \(\delta=q^n-y\), \(\lambda=2(1-\gamma)\), and \(a=\rho/\lambda\). Then as \(\delta\downarrow0\),
\[
\boxed{\displaystyle
f_n(q^n-\delta)\sim a\left(\frac{\sqrt{4\pi\gamma/\lambda}}{n}\right)^a\delta^{a-1}[\log(1/\delta)]^{3a/2}.}
\]
Thus the density diverges at its positive upper edge for \(\rho<\lambda\); at the exact threshold \(\rho=\lambda\) it **still diverges logarithmically** as \([\log(1/\delta)]^{3/2}\); and for \(\rho>\lambda\) it tends to zero. This is a singularity classification for a density, not a claim of a thermodynamic phase transition.

The mean alone has the simple geometric dependence
\[
\mathbb E[Y_n]=\left(\frac{2\gamma}{2+\rho+\sqrt{(2+\rho)^2-4\gamma^2}}\right)^n.
\]

## Assumptions and scope
Spins \(\sigma_i\in\{-1,1\}\) indexed by \(i\in\mathbb Z\) flip at rate
\[
w_i(\sigma)=\frac12\left[1-\frac\gamma2\sigma_i(\sigma_{i-1}+\sigma_{i+1})\right].
\]
Time is normalized so each isolated spin flips at rate \(1/2\). At the start of each run the chain is prepared in the infinite independent symmetric Bernoulli spin measure (equivalently, for the quantity treated here, a single such initial preparation can be fixed and annealed over). Independent Poisson global reset events occur at rate \(\rho\). Let \(A\) be the stationary age since the most recent event; \(A\) is exponential with rate \(\rho\). Define \(C_n(t)=\mathbb E[\sigma_0(t)\sigma_n(t)]\) under a fresh initial measure and reset-free Glauber dynamics, and \(Y_n=C_n(A)\). The expectation in \(C_n\) averages thermal spin trajectories and the preparation **before** sampling \(A\); it must not be confused with the single-shot product \(\sigma_0(A)\sigma_n(A)\), which takes only the values \(\pm1\).

## Proof
**Correlation closure and positivity.** Differentiating the product under the spin-flip generator gives, for \(n\ge1\),
\[
\dot C_n=-2C_n+\gamma(C_{n-1}+C_{n+1}),\qquad C_0(t)=1,\qquad C_n(0)=0.
\]
The half-line Dirichlet heat kernel is
\[
K_{n,m}(t)=e^{-2t}\big[I_{n-m}(2\gamma t)-I_{n+m}(2\gamma t)\big],\quad n,m\ge1.
\]
Duhamel's formula for the boundary injection \(\gamma\) at \(n=1\), followed by the Bessel recurrence
\[
I_{n-1}(z)-I_{n+1}(z)=\frac{2n}{z}I_n(z),
\]
yields
\[
C_n'(t)=n\,\frac{e^{-2t}I_n(2\gamma t)}{t}>0,\qquad
C_n(t)=\int_0^t C_n'(u)\,du.
\]
This nonnegative bounded solution is the unique solution of the half-line system generated by the bounded Dirichlet operator on bounded sequences. The stationary recurrence \(0=-2c_n+\gamma(c_{n-1}+c_{n+1})\), with \(c_0=1\) and decay as \(n\to\infty\), has the bounded solution \(c_n=q^n\), where \(q\) is the root in \((0,1)\) of \(\gamma(q+q^{-1})=2\). Since \(0<\gamma<1\), the transient term tends to zero, so \(C_n(t)\uparrow q^n\).

**Full distribution.** The invariant age law of the independent Poisson reset clock is \(\mathbb P(A\le t)=1-e^{-\rho t}\). Strict monotonicity makes the transformation \(A\mapsto C_n(A)\) invertible and proves the CDF and density by a change of variables. There is no mass at either endpoint because \(\mathbb P(A=0)=\mathbb P(A=\infty)=0\).

**Lower edge.** The Bessel series \(I_n(z)=(z/2)^n/n!+O(z^{n+2})\) gives \(C_n(t)=\gamma^nt^n/n!+O(t^{n+1})\). Solving for \(t\) as \(y\downarrow0\) and using \(f_n(y)=\rho e^{-\rho t}/C_n'(t)\) gives the displayed lower-edge law, including the finite value \(\rho/\gamma\) when \(n=1\).

**Upper edge and its exact equality case.** At each fixed \(n\), the Bessel large-argument formula gives
\[
C_n'(t)\sim\frac{n}{\sqrt{4\pi\gamma}}\,e^{-\lambda t}t^{-3/2},\qquad
q^n-C_n(t)\sim\frac{n}{\lambda\sqrt{4\pi\gamma}}e^{-\lambda t}t^{-3/2}.
\]
Let \(\delta=q^n-C_n(t)\) and \(A_n=n/(\lambda\sqrt{4\pi\gamma})\). Thus
\[
 e^{-\rho t}\sim (\delta/A_n)^a t^{3a/2},\quad
 \frac{\rho}{C_n'(t)}\sim\frac{a}{\delta},\quad
 t\sim\lambda^{-1}\log(1/\delta).
\]
Substitution gives the stated asymptotic constant and logarithmic factor. At \(a=1\), the power of \(\delta\) cancels but the positive \([\log(1/\delta)]^{3/2}\) persists, proving the strict classification including equality.

**Stationary mean.** Fubini gives \(\mathbb E[Y_n]=\int_0^\infty e^{-\rho t}C_n'(t)\,dt\), the Laplace transform of the boundary forcing. Its discrete recurrence is \((2+\rho)m_n=\gamma(m_{n-1}+m_{n+1})\), \(m_0=1\), with the solution \(m_n=u_\rho^n\), where \(u_\rho=2\gamma/[2+\rho+\sqrt{(2+\rho)^2-4\gamma^2}]\). This proves the final formula.

## Verification
Run `python verification.py` with NumPy and SciPy. The program performs 108 checks: Bessel quadratures for limiting equilibrium correlations, Laplace-transform mean comparisons, short-time scaling, rescaled long-time tail asymptotics, and comparisons with independently assembled 256-state Glauber generators on an eight-spin periodic ring. All 108 assertions passed. The largest raw discrepancy, \(0.005612\) to the displayed precision, comes from comparison of the exact infinite-chain law against finite-ring approximations; the finite-ring test is deliberately tolerance-limited and is not an equality check. The theorem's infinite-volume and asymptotic conclusions depend on the proof, not these finite computations. Numerical integration is not interval-certified.

## Relationship to prior work
Magoni, Majumdar, and Schehr (2020) provide the Ising-resetting framework, exponential renewal-age mixture, and magnetization-law threshold \(\rho=1-\gamma\), and explicitly propose understanding the distribution of two-point correlations as further work. The general reset-age mixing principle is prior art and is not claimed here. Mayo, Fan, Chern, and del Campo (2021) derive modified-Bessel two-point correlators for cooling schedules without the global-resetting age distribution. Magoni, Carollo, Perfetto, and Lesanovsky (2022) analyze two-point quantum correlations in a **noninteracting coherent** spin model with reset protocols; its dynamics are not the interacting Glauber generator. The present finding combines the classical Glauber boundary evolution with reset-age change of variables, extracting the entire distance-dependent two-point law and a different sharp upper-edge threshold with a logarithmic equality singularity. Neither a novel resetting identity nor a novel Bessel identity is claimed.

## Limitations
The result is for the infinite homogeneous one-dimensional chain, unbiased product initial state, memoryless globally synchronized resets, \(0<\gamma<1\), fixed finite \(n\ge1\), and \(\rho>0\). It is not a law for fluctuating single-configuration spin products; finite chains, biased initial magnetization, field-driven dynamics, non-Poisson resetting, zero-temperature \(\gamma=1\), and joint separation/time scaling require separate arguments. The upper-edge claim is a local density singularity, not a statement about bulk thermodynamic phases. An independently verified exhaustive priority claim, particularly relative to unpublished supplements and general renewal-correlation formulations, is not supplied.
