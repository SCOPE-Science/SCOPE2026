For the complete binary prefix code obtained by replacing the single length-\(m\) word \(1^m\) with its two children, the literal decoder has exactly \(2^m\) states and its shortest reset word has length exactly \(m^2\), for every \(m\ge2\). The explicit word \((0\,1^m)^{m-1}0\) attains the bound; a modular parsing argument proves that no shorter synchronizing word exists.
