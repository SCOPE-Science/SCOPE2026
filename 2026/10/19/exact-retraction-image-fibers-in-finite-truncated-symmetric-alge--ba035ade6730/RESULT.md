# Exact retraction-image fibers in finite truncated symmetric algebras

## Finding
Let \(q\) be a prime power, \(n\geq1\), \(d\geq2\), and
\[
A=A_{n,d}(q)=\mathbb F_q[X_1,\ldots,X_n]/(X_1,\ldots,X_n)^d,
\qquad \mathfrak m=(\overline X_1,\ldots,\overline X_n).
\]
A retraction is a unital \(\mathbb F_q\)-algebra endomorphism \(e:A\to A\) with \(e^2=e\). Its *tangent rank* is the rank \(r\) of the induced map on \(\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2\). Put
\[
H_d(a)=\sum_{j=2}^{d-1}\binom{a+j-1}{j}\quad(a\geq1),\qquad H_d(0)=0,
\]
where empty sums are zero. For every integer \(0\leq r\leq n\), **the number of distinct image subalgebras** \(e(A)\) with tangent rank \(r\) is exactly
\[
\boxed{I_{n,d,r}(q)={n\brack r}_q\,q^{\,r(H_d(n)-H_d(r))}.}
\]
**Every one of these image subalgebras occurs as the image of exactly**
\[
\boxed{F_{n,d,r}(q)=q^{(n-r)(r+H_d(r))}}
\]
retractions. In particular, the number of retractions of tangent rank \(r\) is
\[
\boxed{T_{n,d,r}(q)={n\brack r}_q\,
q^{\,r(H_d(n)-H_d(r))+(n-r)(r+H_d(r))}.}
\]
The symbol \({n\brack r}_q=\prod_{i=0}^{r-1}(q^{n-i}-1)/(q^{r-i}-1)\) denotes a Gaussian binomial coefficient. Every image is isomorphic, as a \(\mathbb F_q\)-algebra, to \(A_{r,d}(q)\); its cardinality is \(q^{\binom{r+d-1}{d-1}}\). Exactly one retraction has tangent rank zero, and exactly one has tangent rank \(n\). Summing \(T_{n,d,r}(q)\) over \(r\) gives the complete idempotent-endomorphism count.

## Assumptions and scope
All endomorphisms fix \(\mathbb F_q\) pointwise and preserve the unit. The quotient ideal is the **entire power of the maximal ideal**, not the ideal generated by separate powers of individual variables. There is no restriction on the characteristic of \(\mathbb F_q\). The image is counted as a literal subalgebra of the fixed labeled algebra \(A\), not only up to abstract isomorphism. The fiber count concerns retractions with one specified image subalgebra, not an arbitrary map having the same tangent rank. A zero-variable algebra means \(A_{0,d}(q)=\mathbb F_q\), and the conventions above include \(r=0\) and \(r=n\).

## Proof
**1. Endomorphisms and coordinates.** Every algebra endomorphism sends \(\mathfrak m\) into \(\mathfrak m\), because elements of \(\mathfrak m\) are nilpotent and elements outside it are units. Conversely, arbitrary elements \(f_i\in\mathfrak m\) uniquely specify an algebra endomorphism through \(X_i\mapsto f_i\): every product of \(d\) such elements vanishes. Such a substitution is an automorphism exactly when its linear map on \(\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2\) is invertible. Indeed, the induced substitution on each homogeneous quotient \(\mathfrak m^j/\mathfrak m^{j+1}\) is the \(j\)-th symmetric power of the tangent map; an invertible tangent map therefore makes the filtered endomorphism bijective.

**2. Retractions are conjugate to tangent projections.** If \(e^2=e\), the induced tangent map is a projection. Split the tangent space as its image \(U\) and kernel \(W\), with dimensions \(r\) and \(s=n-r\). Choose linear-coordinate lifts \(u_1,\ldots,u_r,w_1,\ldots,w_s\). Write \(P\) for the standard algebra projection fixing all \(u_i\) and killing all \(w_j\). Define a substitution \(h\) by
\[
h(u_i)=e(u_i),\qquad h(w_j)=w_j-e(w_j).
\]
Because its tangent map is the identity, \(h\) is an automorphism by Step 1. Since \(e^2=e\), we have \(eh=hP\), whence \(e=hPh^{-1}\). Thus its image is a coordinate copy \(h(\mathbb F_q[u_1,\ldots,u_r]/(u)^d)\), and its vector-space dimension is \(\binom{r+d-1}{d-1}\).

**3. Count images with one fixed tangent \(r\)-plane.** Fix \(U\) and one complement \(W\). Let \(G_1\) be the automorphisms of \(A\) whose tangent map is the identity. Each of the \(n\) generators can acquire any nonlinear tail in \(\mathfrak m^2\), a space of dimension \(H_d(n)\), independently. Hence \(|G_1|=q^{nH_d(n)}\). The group \(G_1\) acts transitively on the image subalgebras whose tangent space is \(U\), by Step 2. The stabilizer of the standard image \(B_0=\mathbb F_q[u_1,\ldots,u_r]/(u)^d\) consists precisely of substitutions with the nonlinear tails of the \(u_i\) lying in \(B_0\cap\mathfrak m^2\) (\(H_d(r)\) choices of coefficients each) and arbitrary nonlinear tails for the \(w_j\) (\(H_d(n)\) coefficients each). This stabilizer has order \(q^{rH_d(r)+sH_d(n)}\). The orbit therefore has cardinality \(q^{r(H_d(n)-H_d(r))}\). There are \({n\brack r}_q\) possible tangent \(r\)-planes, all disjoint as classes of image subalgebras; multiplication gives \(I_{n,d,r}(q)\).

**4. Count retractions onto one image.** Conjugate a given image \(B\) to \(B_0\). Any retraction onto \(B_0\) fixes \(B_0\) pointwise. It is thus uniquely determined by freely choosing the images \(e(w_j)\) in the maximal ideal \(B_0\cap\mathfrak m\); conversely, any such choices define a retraction, because substitutions with outputs in \(\mathfrak m\) respect the \(d\)-th power relation and the resulting map fixes \(B_0\). The dimension of \(B_0\cap\mathfrak m\) is
\[
\sum_{j=1}^{d-1}\binom{r+j-1}{j}=r+H_d(r).
\]
Therefore precisely \(q^{s(r+H_d(r))}\) retractions have image \(B_0\). Conjugation shows the same number for every image of the same tangent rank. Multiplying Steps 3 and 4 proves all formulas. The boundary cases \(r=0\) and \(r=n\) follow directly from the same equations. \(\square\)

## Verification
A standalone exhaustive program, `check_retractions.py`, enumerates all algebra endomorphisms by arbitrary maximal-ideal substitutions and independently tests \(e^2=e\) using truncated polynomial multiplication. For every idempotent, it computes the tangent rank and canonical row-reduced basis of the image subalgebra, then independently checks the three statistics: retraction count, number of distinct images, and the common size of each image fiber. Tests completed for \((n,d,q)=(2,3,2),(2,3,3),(2,4,2),(3,2,2),(3,2,3)\), and a separate direct \(2\)-by-\(2\) matrix enumeration over \(\mathbb F_4\) at \((n,d)=(2,2)\). In all six cases, all three predicted statistics matched exact enumeration.

For illustration, at \((n,d,q)=(2,3,2)\), there are \(12\) one-dimensional-tangent image subalgebras, each admitting \(4\) retractions, for \(48\) rank-one retractions and \(50\) total retractions. At \((2,3,3)\) there are \(36\) such images, each with \(9\) retractions, for \(326\) total. At \((2,4,2)\) there are \(96\) such images, each with \(8\) retractions, for \(770\) total. Finite tests corroborate but do not replace Steps 1–4, which prove the theorem for every prime power, dimension, and truncation length.

## Relationship to prior work
Chakraborty, Dasgupta, Dutta and Gupta, *Some results on retracts of polynomial rings*, arXiv:1910.11023v1 (24 October 2019), Theorem 6.7, prove a power-series coordinate normal form for retractions. That qualitative coordinate-linearization theorem is **prior art** and is not asserted here as new. The result above is the explicit complete finite-field enumeration of **embedded image subalgebras and prescribed-image fiber sizes** in the finite quotient by a maximal-ideal power, with the nonlinear coefficient stabilizer counts carried out for all truncation orders. The cited paper's inspected theorem and proof do not give these numerical fiber formulas.

Chang, Shu and Yao, *Inverse limits of automorphisms of truncated polynomials and applications related to Jacobian conjecture*, arXiv:2401.11072 (20 January 2024), study automorphism criteria and inverse limits, mainly for coordinatewise prime-power truncations. Their inspected statements do not count retractions onto specified images in the quotient by \(\mathfrak m^d\). Published descriptions of a distinct truncated coordinate-cross algebra impose the further relations annihilating products of distinct variables, so their automorphism orders do not enumerate the unrestricted symmetric quotients here.

## Limitations
The finding counts \(\mathbb F_q\)-algebra maps; unrestricted ring endomorphisms that act nontrivially on the coefficient field are outside its scope. No claim is made that coordinate linearization itself is new, or that the enumeration is the only possible expression for the count. It does not classify arbitrary subalgebras, only those that occur as retraction images. Older finite-jet invariant literature or unindexed tables might contain equivalent exact fiber formulas; mathematical priority is not independently certified.
