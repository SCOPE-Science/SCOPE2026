# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof shows that displacement translations force every homomorphism of a connected finite Alexander quandle to be affine over the prime field, and that a retraction is exactly a commuting linear projection plus one translation in its kernel. Invariant factors describe all ordered stable splittings. The finite-module endomorphism-ring count, reduction modulo the Jacobson radical, and orbit–stabilizer yield the explicit multiplicity formula; complement graphs yield the per-image factor. Nine independent exhaustive commuting-idempotent matrix cases, three complete fixed-image fiber cases, and two exhaustive tiny quandle-map cases pass, including nonsemisimple irreducible-primary powers. The proof, not finite enumeration, supplies the general quantifiers.

**Originality — PASS within inspected literature, with residual risk.** The major relevant 2010 quandle-homology source already recognizes retracts as split module epimorphisms, and an earlier calculation supplies the semisimple retraction-count formulas. Both are excluded as original content. The explicit arbitrary nonsemisimple elementary-divisor retraction-image and fiber multiplicities, including mixed Jordan lengths and irreducible primary powers, were not established in the inspected full texts of those works or the affine-subquandle and homomorphic-image papers. Quantitative orbit counting is additional to the structural retraction criterion, but older finite-module enumeration may supply an equivalent result under different terminology. A negative search is not proof of absolute novelty.

**Value — PASS.** Retraction images and idempotent endomorphisms are canonical invariants of finite quandles. A uniform exact count across all nonsemisimple primary types is stronger than isolated small examples: even for connected quandles of the same cardinality, a two-dimensional scalar operator has forty-six retractions while a single length-two Jordan block has ten. The theorem computes all image multiplicities and the exact criterion for having no proper nontrivial retract, supporting comparisons of affine quandles and their idempotent endomorphism monoids. It is limited to the stated prime-field connected class, not the general classification of all quandles.

Closest sources: Jedlička et al., arXiv:1708.02362; Niebrzydowski–Przytycki, arXiv:0907.4732, §5; Jedlička–Stanovský, *Homomorphic images of affine quandles* (2021); Taniguchi, arXiv:2302.11138. The nonsemisimple formula should be compared further against older endomorphism-ring idempotent enumerations before asserting unconditional priority.
