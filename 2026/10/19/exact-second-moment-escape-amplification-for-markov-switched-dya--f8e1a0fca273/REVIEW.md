# Scientific review of Markov-switched dyadic-hole survival moments

**Correctness — PASS.** The binary-shift cylinder representation gives two independently derived finite-state transfer matrices. The exact Perron-gap identity follows by polynomial reduction modulo the first-moment quadratic, with strict signs on the full open parameter interval; primitivity gives asymptotic positive coefficients. An independently enumerating exact-rational verifier agrees at 49 horizon–parameter pairs. The finite tests alone are not used to infer the universal theorem.

**Originality — PASS, subject to literature limitations.** The closest prior work develops iid random-hole transfer operators (Bahsoun–Schmeling–Vaienti), deterministic moving holes (Livorati–Georgiou–Dettmann–Leonel), and random-versus-noisy ensemble fluctuations (Bódai–Altmann–Endler). Those results do not themselves state the evaluated Markov-persistence two-hole first-plus-second-moment matrices or the strict relative variance exponent for all interior persistence probabilities. An independently selected fair-hole mean is an acknowledged existing limiting specialization, not the asserted novelty. An unindexed broader finite-state transfer theorem might subsume the example; full historical priority remains uncertain.

**Value — PASS.** Environment-conditioned survivor mass is the natural experiment-wide statistic when a moving hole is shared by many initial points. Exact first and second moments distinguish annealed mean decay from environmental reproducibility, show exponential relative fluctuations throughout an open interval, and identify a singular collapse of variance at deterministic switching endpoints even as mean survival remains exponentially decaying. The polynomial identity makes this a complete parameter-wise classification, not a one-parameter numerical illustration.

**Scope.** Symmetric two-state Markov chain, fixed complementary dyadic holes, Lebesgue initial distribution, all integer horizons. No almost-sure escape, arbitrary hole geometry, or absolute novelty certification is claimed.
