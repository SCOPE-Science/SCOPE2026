# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The exact positive/negative branch maps give two mutually exhaustive cases: after a crossing, the image is inside a small leakage interval and must remain on its new side for the next \(n\) transitions; conversely, an onto holding branch and an onto switching-plus-recovery branch realize all and only the hard-core switch blocks. Finite-word counts follow an exact renewal recurrence, and standard Perron-root analysis gives the stated entropy. The small-leak entropy limit uses the independently established critical-orbit equation. The included diagnostic confirms 12,276 finite cylinders; infinite claims rely on the written proof, not the computation.

**Originality — PASS, subject to priority risk.** Horan's 2019 preprint, §4.6, explicitly specifies the same Markov parameter sequence and full transition matrix, and his 2020 thesis, Chapter 5, establishes detailed spectral information. The new statement is not those prior construction/spectral results: it identifies the complete coarse **sign** language, proves every allowed cylinder has positive length, and evaluates its entropy-collapse law. Horan's 2020 quotient is based on absolute value, not sign itinerary; the 2024 study computes random invariant-density and Oseledets limits, not this observation's topological language. It remains possible that the full prior Markov graph has elsewhere been projected and simplified to an equivalent entropy formula.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Sign occupation is a canonical observable for a metastable system with two competing halves. The theorem gives the exact deterministic minimal return time, complete admissibility rather than a mere delay bound, and the scale of informational loss in the small-leak limit; these facts distinguish macroscopic switching complexity from persistent local expansion. It does not estimate physical switching rates, compute metric entropies or extend to randomly varying leakage.
