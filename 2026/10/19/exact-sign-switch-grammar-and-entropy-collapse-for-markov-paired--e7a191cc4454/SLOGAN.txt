For the known Markov sequence of symmetric paired tent maps, the exact binary sign-itinerary factor forbids switches closer than \(n+1\) transitions, with every permitted word realized on a positive-length cylinder. Its entropy is \(\log\rho_n\), where \(\rho_n^n(\rho_n-1)=1\), and collapses as \(W(n)/n\) even while branch expansion approaches a factor of two.
