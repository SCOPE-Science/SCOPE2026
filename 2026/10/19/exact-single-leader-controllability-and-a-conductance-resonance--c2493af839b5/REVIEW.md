# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The PBH reduction, two-arc recurrence, complex-trigonometric elimination and separate eigenvalue-4 calculation jointly cover every finite graph and positive real chord conductance in the claim. The sine eigenvalues are distinct and the exceptional vector has eigenvalue 4, so dimensions add. Reproducible finite arithmetic checks cover 2,645 rational configurations, but do not substitute for the proof.

**Originality — PASS, with documented uncertainty.** Compared the full relevant arXiv text of Parlangeli–Notarstefano (2011; ordinary paths/cycles and multiple input nodes) and Liu–Lu (2014; Laplacian spectral structure of theta graphs). These do not state the exact one-input weighted-chord rank law or the isolated resonance. The abstract-level general work of Aguilar–Gharesifard (2015) and the later cycle-derived network analysis of Liu–Ji (2017/2018) were also considered. The latter's complete text was inaccessible; its possible overlap remains a real risk, and no claim is made about statements in unread sections. No exact indexed or previously accessible claim was found, but absence in searches is not a novelty proof.

**Value — PASS.** The formula is a complete, parameter-independent arithmetic classification plus a sharp one-weight controllability failure on a canonical network family. It explains which cycle harmonics survive a chord incident to a single actuated node and how changing only conductance can create a new unreachable mode exactly at the reciprocal of the original-cycle endpoint effective resistance.

**Limitations.** No control-energy quantitative estimate is claimed. All-zero/nonpositive chord weights are excluded. The novelty review has bounded coverage and is not an independent certification.
