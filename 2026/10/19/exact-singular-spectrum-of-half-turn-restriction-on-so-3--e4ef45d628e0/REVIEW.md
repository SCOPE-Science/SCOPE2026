# Scientific review: half-turn restriction on \(SO(3)\)

## Correctness — PASS
The domain is the complete Peter–Weyl band with \(0\le\ell<L\), the target is probability-normalized \(\mathbb{RP}^2\), and the operator is continuous point restriction, not averaging over fibers. Peter–Weyl orthonormality gives the restricted kernel \(\sum_{\ell<L}(2\ell+1)U_{2\ell}(u\cdot v)\). The Chebyshev derivative identity converts it into \(C_{2L-2}^{(2)}\). Funk–Hecke diagonalizes this zonal kernel on even spherical harmonics. An integration-by-parts identity and a *positive* cosine expansion of each odd Legendre polynomial prove all consecutive eigenvalue gaps are positive. Leading-coefficient orthogonality proves the last eigenvalue positive and gives its rational closed form. Rank, nullity, sharp condition number, and asymptotic follow, with exact rational corroboration for all \(1\le L\le12\) in `checker.py`. The last step does not assume experimental tests imply a universal theorem.

## Originality — PASS, subject to independent audit
Bernstein–Pesenson, arXiv:1210.2652 (especially Sections 2, 5 and 8), reconstructs from *averaged* rotations over \(SO(2)\) fibers with data on \(S^2\times S^2\). McEwen et al., arXiv:1508.03101, samples all of \(SO(3)\) on a three-angle grid for full Wigner reconstruction. The later compact-Lie-group restriction literature studies \(L^p\) estimates, not the exact spatial trace spectrum of the half-turn conjugacy surface. These operators and conclusions do not imply the rank \(2L^2-L\), strict eigenvalue ordering, closed minimal eigenvalue, or its sharp \(L^{1/4}\) condition-number growth. Related permutation-group conjugacy-class sampling is over finite, not rotation, groups. Searches for Wigner matrix coefficient sampling, half-turn conjugacy class, reproducing kernel spectral trace, and equivalent spherical harmonic descriptions did not yield an identical result in the inspected corpus. Residual risk remains that a general compact symmetric-space polynomial trace theorem might imply parts of the result without using this phrasing; this is not a claim of exhaustive novelty certification.

## Value — PASS
The half-turn conjugacy class is a canonical two-dimensional measurement surface in a three-dimensional compact group. Precisely which degrees of freedom survive this severe measurement reduction, the exact inversion cost, and whether different output harmonics have comparable sensitivity are mathematical questions with natural sampling significance. The result gives the *entire* sharp singular spectrum, strict ordering, an exact rank/ambiguity law, a minimum-norm reconstruction, and a nonobvious, mild \(L^{1/4}\) conditioning scale. These are structural and quantitative statements, not isolated arithmetic examples or routine numerical improvements.

## Scope and residual risk
No recovery outside the visible quotient, finite sampling construction, stability under arbitrary noise, or independently checked novelty is asserted. Formulae depend on Haar and axis probability measures, explicitly fixed in the proof. Some possible older representation-theoretic treatments of conjugacy-class trace were not individually located; they are a residual bibliographic risk rather than negative evidence.
