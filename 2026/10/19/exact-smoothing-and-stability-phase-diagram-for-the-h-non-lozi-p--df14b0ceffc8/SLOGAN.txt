For the quadratic smoothing of the dissipative Hénon–Lozi map, a prime two-cycle exists uniquely up to an explicit flip threshold, remains exactly unchanged through an initial smoothing interval, moves only quadratically when it first enters the smoothing strip, and then vanishes at a universal square-root rate. An exact stability phase diagram shows when smoothing converts the initial saddle cycle into an attracting cycle before its disappearance.
