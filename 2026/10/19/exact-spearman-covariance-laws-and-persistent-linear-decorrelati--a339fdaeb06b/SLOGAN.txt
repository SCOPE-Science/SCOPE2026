For full-branch piecewise-linear maps with arbitrary branch orientations, permuting equal output cells makes the stationary lag-one coordinate covariance an exact affine function of Spearman rank correlation, yielding closed-form shuffle moments and Gaussian ensemble fluctuations. Precisely balanced orientation vectors have a stronger rigidity: the initial coordinate is uncorrelated with every bounded observable of every future state under every output-cell permutation; this does not mean the states are independent.
