# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The six labeled families are distinct by cross-edge patterns and the two within-part clique flags; neighborhood toggles close the list and reach all members from the biclique. For instance at U_i an X leaf does nothing, the X center removes all Y clique edges and connects its neighbors across the parts (giving C_X), and a Y vertex toggles the Y clique among its neighbors and the i-to-other-Y spokes (giving W_ij). At W_ij only the centers act; complementing at i makes the X clique and cross spokes into j, giving V_j. The generators are involutions, so the uniform vertex-choice operator is symmetric even with repeated and null moves. On part-constant functions the Laplacian is a six-cycle quotient in the order B,C_X,U,W,V,C_Y, with diagonal N,N,n+1,2,m+1,N and adjacent off-diagonal products n^2,m,n,m,n,m^2. Exact expansion as 18 signed matching terms and two full-cycle terms gives t(t-N-1)p(t); independent coefficient substitution at t=1-h has positive constant 8 in u=m-2,v=n-2,h and no negative coefficients. The quotient is real-symmetrizable by family sizes. A zero-sum X contrast has Laplacian block [[n+1,-n],[-1,2]] with eigenvalues 1,n+2; the Y analogue gives 1,m+2. Doubly zero-sum W tensors give 2. Dimensions exhaust the labeled orbit and give a simple zero eigenvalue and a first positive eigenvalue 1, including m=n=2. Scaling by N yields the claimed one-sided gap.
