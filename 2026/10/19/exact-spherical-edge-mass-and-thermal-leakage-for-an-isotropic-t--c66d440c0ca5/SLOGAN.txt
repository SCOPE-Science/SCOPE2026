For a harmonically confined isotropic run-and-tumble particle in every dimension \(d\ge2\), the athermal probability of lying within distance \(\varepsilon\) of its turning sphere has the sharp form \(C_{d,\alpha}\varepsilon^\alpha\), with an explicit digamma constant. Weak thermal noise creates an exactly normalized Gaussian boundary layer: the probability of crossing the turning sphere is asymptotic to an explicit constant times \(D^{\alpha/2}\).
