# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
For a broken directed cycle the logistic recursion yields a unique Hurwitz-stable limiting support; the intact-cycle roots-of-unity Jacobian and full boundary invasion analysis give the parity and spectral threshold. The full probabilistic census and richness mean follow by exact finite counting. The accompanying finite exhaustive spectral checker is supplementary, not an infinite proof. Critical gains are excluded.

## Originality — PASS, with acknowledged residual risk
Compared full accessible papers by Nassor et al. (arXiv:2609.17232v1), Çelik et al. (arXiv:2609.09286v1), Hong and Pego (arXiv:2102.00611), and Tarabolo et al. (arXiv:2512.22563v1). Known secant criteria and logistic recursion are ingredients rather than claimed inventions. No inspected source states the complete Bernoulli-diluted directed-cycle multiplicity/richness law. The Chen–Shih 2026 cyclic-feedback source was compared only at accessible bibliographic/abstract level; a more general unknown exact theorem remains a risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The exact topology-dependent probability law gives a motivated finite-population benchmark for system-centered equilibrium-count statistics, explaining how random removal of just one interaction changes stable community multiplicity. It also yields a sharp rare-deletion asymptotic and an explicit mean-survivor statistic. The setting is a uniform one-way ecological cycle; this is not a universal random-network theorem.

## Limits
The strict noncritical gain assumptions are essential to Hurwitz counting. No claims about alternative attractors, general random graphs, or independent external certification are made.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
