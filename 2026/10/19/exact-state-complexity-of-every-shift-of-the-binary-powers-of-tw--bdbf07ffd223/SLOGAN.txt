For the binary powers-of-two indicator shifted by any positive integer \(c\), the exact minimal complete MSD-first automaton size is \(s+2\) for power-of-two \(c\), and \(s+r+h+3\) otherwise, where \(s\) is its bit length, \(r\) counts the leading ones of \(2^s-c\), and \(h\) measures an explicit padded-prefix overlap. In every nonpower dyadic band, the sharp range is \(s+4\) through \(2s+2\), strengthening a prior logarithmic state-complexity bound to an exact bitwise law.
