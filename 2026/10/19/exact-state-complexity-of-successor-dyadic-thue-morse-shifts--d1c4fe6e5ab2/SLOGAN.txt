For each integer \(k\ge1\), the smallest binary MSD-first DFAO computing the shifted Thue–Morse sequence \(t(n+2^k+1)\) has exactly \(2F_{k+4}\) states, the same number as the shift \(t(n+2^{k+1})\). An exact residual-fingerprint partition separates even and odd prefixes and yields a Fibonacci recurrence.
