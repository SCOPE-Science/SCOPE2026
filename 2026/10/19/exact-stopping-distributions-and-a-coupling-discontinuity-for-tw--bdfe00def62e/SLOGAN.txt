For iid two-coordinate randomized CGLS, the exact finite-solve time has a rational probability-generating function and is minimized by uniform sampling. Any nonzero normal-matrix coupling raises the mean by exactly two iterations relative to the diagonal case: \(1+1/[p(1-p)]\) versus \(1/[p(1-p)]-1\); ordinary randomized coordinate descent never attains exact finite termination for generic coupled starts.
