A set of positions attracts every factor of the Zimin word \(Z_n\) if and only if it contains at least one occurrence of each of the \(n\) letters. Consequently the exact size enumerator of all attractors is \(\prod_{k=1}^{n}((1+t)^{2^{n-k}}-1)\), and there are \(2^{n(n-1)/2}\) smallest attractors.
