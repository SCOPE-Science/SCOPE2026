In a three-spin XX chain with strictly positive excitation and relaxation noise acting only on the center, every initial state converges to a two-sector thermal mixture determined solely by its conserved end-singlet weight. The sharp stationary end-spin concurrence is \(C_\infty=[w-3(1-w)q/(1+q+q^2)]_+\), so even a separable single-end excitation generates persistent entanglement at every finite positive temperature with \(0<q<1\).
