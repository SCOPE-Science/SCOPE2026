For the four-symbol one-deletion confusability graph, the exact number of unordered triangles is \(\bigl[4^n(405n^3+81n^2+482n-2344)+1920n^2+2176n+2560\bigr]/288\) for every \(n\ge1\). In particular, it is asymptotic to \((45/32)4^n n^3\); a finite-state recurrence with an exact Cayley–Hamilton certificate proves the result at all lengths.
