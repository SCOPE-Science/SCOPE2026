For two-agent adaptive opinion dynamics with reciprocal logistic trust, an exact conserved potential divides consensus from permanent disagreement by the sharp initial-trust threshold \(w_*(d_0)=\tanh F(d_0)\). Exactly at the boundary, the gap approaches a positive critical separation at rate \(t^{-1}\) while trust disappears at rate \(t^{-2}\), even though the interaction kernel remains strictly positive.
