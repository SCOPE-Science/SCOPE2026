Two sequential exact subspace-Newton line minimizations on any real planar positive-definite quadratic reduce expected energy by exactly the factor \(\bigl((\kappa-1)/(\kappa+1)\bigr)^2\) when the second uniform direction is coupled orthogonally to the first rather than drawn independently. At condition number \(\kappa=4\), the sharp worst-case two-step contractions are \(11/75\) versus \(11/27\); at \(\kappa=1\), orthogonal pairing terminates exactly.
