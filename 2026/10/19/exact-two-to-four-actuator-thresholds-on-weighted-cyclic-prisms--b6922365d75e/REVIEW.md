## Correctness — PASS
The finite-dimensional symmetric Laplacian admits a complete separation into symmetric/antisymmetric layer parities and real cycle harmonics. Cross-parity eigenvalue coincidences occur exactly at differences of cycle cosines; their geometric eigenspaces have dimension two, three, or four as classified. PBH supplies an unavoidable dimensional lower bound. Evaluating spatial sine/cosine harmonics on consecutive positions, supplemented by the opposite layer, supplies exact nonzero minors for the proposed sharp actuator sets, including the simple-to-simple coincidence. The gcd condition and parity exception for every two-site placement follow from the first harmonic with a vanishing sine. A standalone SymPy script executed 18 exact rational Kalman checks and one exact radical identity; these are supplementary, not the infinite proof.
