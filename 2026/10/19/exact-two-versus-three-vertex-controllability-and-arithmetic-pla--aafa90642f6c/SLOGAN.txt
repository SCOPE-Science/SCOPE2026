The minimum number of independently actuated vertices in a uniformly weighted wheel is exactly two except at the finite set of spoke/rim resonance ratios \(a/b=m/(2-2\cos(2\pi j/m))\) with \(1\le j<m/2\), where it is three. Every optimal two-vertex placement obeys an explicit totient-counted coprimality rule, and at prime rim orders every three-vertex choice is optimal at resonance.
