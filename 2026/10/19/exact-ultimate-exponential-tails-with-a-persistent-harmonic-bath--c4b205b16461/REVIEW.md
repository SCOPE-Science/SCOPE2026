# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** The free excursion uses an explicitly diagonalizable rank-one drift semigroup and its covariance integral, verified independently in six numerical parameter regimes. The retained bath's reset-time chain is a strict random affine contraction, giving a Gaussian-scale-mixture invariant law. A dominated-convergence exponential Lyapunov bound supplies every bath exponential moment needed below the common diffusion abscissa. The stationary Poisson-age integral then proves both convergence and divergence of the tracer moment-generating function at the exact threshold, including equality. A restricted-age Gaussian large-deviation lower event matches the Chernoff upper bound. The result is for fixed strictly positive parameters only; no numerical experiment is promoted to a proof.

**Originality — PASS with residual risks.** The complete primary text of Santra–Das [1] treats exact moments and Gaussian *strong-memory* asymptotics, not a fixed-finite-memory logarithmic far-tail threshold. The closest alternative is Goswami [2], which develops a last-reset renewal approximation and a strong-coupling exponential limiting profile. It assumes a newly sampled bath initial distribution at each last-renewal step, unlike the invariant retained-bath reset state; hence it does not prove the true finite-coupling tail. In fact its finite-parameter second-moment approximation does not generally match the exact stationary generator moment, while the present proof handles that state explicitly. Tal-Friedman et al. [3] solve proportional resets of one coordinate, not full resetting of one member of a coupled two-coordinate diffusion. Targeted research-record and primary-literature searches found no implication-identical theorem. There remains risk from broader unindexed Markov-renewal/affine perpetuity literature, and from the strong-coupling limit suggesting the exponent.

**Value — PASS.** In a natural physical model reported to cross from exponential to Gaussian-looking profiles with increasing memory, deciding the mathematically well-defined *ultimate* tail for every finite relaxation time is a structural question. The exact abscissa quantifies the rare long reset-free excursion mechanism and makes explicit the noncommuting-limit issue; this is neither a cosmetic parameter substitution nor a trivial finite table. No density prefactor or crossover-scale theorem is claimed.
