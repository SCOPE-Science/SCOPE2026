When only a Brownian tracer is Poisson-reset while its harmonic bath keeps its memory, the **ultimate stationary tails** are exponential on a logarithmic scale at every fixed finite bath relaxation time: the exact exponent is \(\sqrt{r(a+b)^2/(b^2D_x+a^2D_y)}\). For the dimensionless viscoelastic bath this becomes \(\sqrt{r(1+\gamma)}\), distinguishing the fixed-memory far-tail limit from an earlier Gaussian strong-memory approximation.
