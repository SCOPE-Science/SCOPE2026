For a two-dimensional positive-definite operator, Gaussian sketching loses positivity in some sketch-dependent direction with exact probability \(I_z((k-1)/2,1/2)\), while the worst fixed direction has probability \(\tfrac12 I_z(k/2,1/2)\), where \(z=((\kappa-1)/(\kappa+1))^2\). The sharp criterion for uniform positivity risk to vanish as conditioning deteriorates is \(k/\kappa\to\infty\).
