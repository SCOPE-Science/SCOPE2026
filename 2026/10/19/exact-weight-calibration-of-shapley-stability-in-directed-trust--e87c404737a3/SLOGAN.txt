In a strongly connected directed trust network where every recipient has a common incoming trust weight, the Shapley allocation equals the unique core either automatically on indegree-one cycles, never when indegree one and larger indegree coexist, or at exactly one projective weight vector determined by a reverse-walk stationary distribution. For uniform reciprocal networks, fairness and stability coincide exactly on componentwise-regular graphs; every irregular component produces an explicit Shapley-blocking coalition.
