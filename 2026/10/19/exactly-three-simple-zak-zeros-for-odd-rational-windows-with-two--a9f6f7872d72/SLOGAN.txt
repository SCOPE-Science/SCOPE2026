For every two-pair symmetric imaginary-pole rational window \(g_{a,b}(t)=t/((t^2+a^2)(t^2+b^2))\), with arbitrary \(a,b>0\) including coinciding poles, the unit Zak transform has precisely the three parity-forced zeros \((0,0)\), \((0,\tfrac12)\), and \((\tfrac12,0)\). All three are nondegenerate, and no hidden Zak zeros occur for any pole spacing.
