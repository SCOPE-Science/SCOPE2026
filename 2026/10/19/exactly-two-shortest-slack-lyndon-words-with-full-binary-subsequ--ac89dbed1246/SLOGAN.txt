For every \(k\ge1\), exactly two length-\(2k+1\) binary Lyndon words contain **all** length-\(k\) binary subsequences: \(001(01)^{k-1}\) and \((01)^k1\). For \(k\ge2\), the extra symbol above the unrestricted minimum length \(2k\) is both necessary and sufficient for Lyndon universality, and the two words are the entire first-slack class.
