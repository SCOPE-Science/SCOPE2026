For a uniformly random word containing two copies of each of \(q\) labels, the exact mean number of distinct palindromic scattered subsequences is \(\sum_{m=1}^q\binom qm 2m/(2m-1)!!\). It grows as \(\frac12 e^{-1/4}\sqrt{2q}\,e^{\sqrt{2q}}\), revealing stretched-exponential average complexity in a family whose maximum is exponential.
