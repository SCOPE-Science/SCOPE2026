# Exponential component decomposition of the adjacent-swap derangement graph

## Finding
For every integer \(n\ge2\), the adjacent-position-swap graph on derangements of \([n]\) has exactly \(2^{n-2}\) connected components. These are indexed by the subset of values \(i\in\{2,\ldots,n-1\}\) that lie **to the left of position \(i\)** in the one-line word. Every such subset occurs. Each component has a constructible unique designated normal form and diameter at most \(n(n-1)\). When \(n\ge3\), exactly two states are isolated, namely the two one-step cyclic shifts. Connectivity inside a component does not require a globally shortest sequence of adjacent swaps; a six-letter example has restricted distance \(7\), versus unrestricted distance \(5\).

## Assumptions and scope
A derangement \(\pi\) is a bijection \(\pi:[n]\to[n]\) with \(\pi(j)\ne j\) for every \(1\le j\le n\). The graph \(\mathcal D_n\) is the induced subgraph of the full adjacent-position-transposition graph on \(S_n\), retaining precisely derangements. Its undirected edges swap \(\pi(j)\) and \(\pi(j+1)\) for some \(1\le j<n\); swaps that create even one fixed point are disallowed. The symbol \(p_\pi(i)\) denotes the position of value \(i\); hence \(p_\pi(i)=\pi^{-1}(i)\). Define \(L(\pi)=\{i\in\{2,\ldots,n-1\}:p_\pi(i)<i\}\). By derangement, value \(1\) must occur right of position \(1\), and value \(n\) must occur left of position \(n\). For \(L\subseteq\{2,\ldots,n-1\}\), define \(N_L\) as the word consisting first of \(L\cup\{n\}\), in ascending order, then \(\{1,\ldots,n-1\}\setminus L\), in ascending order. There is no claim that all paths confined to a component are shortest in the full symmetric group.

## Proof
**Invariant.** Under a legal adjacent swap, a value moves one position left or right. Its relative side of its *own* forbidden position cannot change without landing at that position at some step. Therefore, for every value \(i\), the truth value of \(p_\pi(i)<i\) is constant along paths. In particular \(L(\pi)\) is invariant.

**All patterns exist.** Fix \(L\subseteq\{2,\ldots,n-1\}\). Write its augmented ascending list as \(\ell_1<\cdots<\ell_k\), where \(\ell_k=n\), and its complementary ascending list as \(r_1<\cdots<r_{n-k}\), where \(r_1=1\). Each \(\ell_j\ge j+1\), so \(\ell_j\) placed at position \(j\) lies strictly left of its own index. Also \(r_j<n\), and of the \(r_j\) integers in \([r_j]\), exactly \(j\) are right-side values. Because \(n\) is a left-side value beyond \(r_j\), the total number \(k\) of left-side values strictly exceeds \(r_j-j\). Thus \(r_j<k+j\), so \(r_j\) at position \(k+j\) is strictly right of its index. Consequently \(N_L\) is a derangement and has pattern \(L\).

**Canonical connectivity algorithm.** Start from any derangement with pattern \(L\), and mark an initially empty fixed prefix. First process all left-side values in increasing numerical order. At each step let \(i\) be the smallest unprocessed left-side value, currently at position \(q\), and let \(k\) be the next prefix position. Move \(i\) to position \(k\) by swaps with its immediate left neighbor. Because \(q<i\), all intermediate positions for \(i\) remain strictly smaller than \(i\). Every displaced right-side value moves one position *right*, which keeps it right of its own index; every displaced left-side value \(j\) satisfies \(j>i\), and its displaced position is at most \(q<i<j\). Hence every intermediate permutation remains a derangement with the same pattern. Repeat until all left-side values occupy the initial \(k\) positions in increasing order.

All remaining values are right-side values. Let \(r\) be the smallest one, and \(k+1\) the next position to fill. Necessarily \(r<k+1\): otherwise no remaining right-side value could occupy position \(k+1\) in the current valid arrangement. Bubble \(r\) left to that position. It stays to the right of position \(r\), while every displaced right-side value only moves right. Continue in ascending order. The resulting word is exactly \(N_L\). Thus every vertex of a fixed pattern can reach the same \(N_L\), and the invariant proves that there are precisely \(2^{n-2}\) components. Each bubble moves a value at most \(n-1\) positions and never swaps an already fixed prefix; at most \(\binom n2\) moves suffice to reach \(N_L\). Connecting two states through it gives diameter at most \(n(n-1)\).

**Isolated states.** Consider positions \(j,j+1\). A swap creates a fixed point precisely when \(\pi(j)=j+1\) or \(\pi(j+1)=j\). Hence in an isolated word each of the \(n-1\) adjacent position boundaries must satisfy at least one of these two conditions. Label the former condition type R and the latter type L. Type R at boundary \(j\) and type L at boundary \(j+1\) would place value \(j+1\) in two different positions; type L at boundary \(j\) and type R at boundary \(j+1\) would require two different values at position \(j+1\). Thus any selection of blocker types across all boundaries is uniform. All R gives \((2,3,\ldots,n,1)\), and all L gives \((n,1,2,\ldots,n-1)\). Both have no legal adjacent swap, and for \(n\ge3\) they are distinct. At \(n=2\), they coincide in the unique derangement.

**Non-isometry witness.** For \(n=6\), let \(a=(3,1,2,5,6,4)\) and \(b=(3,6,2,5,1,4)\). Both have \(L=\{3,5\}\). Their unrestricted adjacent-transposition distance is the Kendall inversion number \(5\). Exact breadth-first search within the derangement-induced graph returns minimum distance \(7\), witnessed by
\[
\begin{gathered}
(3,1,2,5,6,4),\ (3,1,5,2,6,4),\ (3,5,1,2,6,4),\\
(3,5,2,1,6,4),\ (3,5,2,6,1,4),\ (3,5,6,2,1,4),\\
(3,6,5,2,1,4),\ (3,6,2,5,1,4).
\end{gathered}
\]
The complete breadth-first search and every displayed intermediate configuration are independently replayable using `verify_components.py`. This last finite assertion is a verified computation, not an additional infinite theorem.

## Verification
`verify_components.py` reconstructs the graph and enumerates **all** derangements for \(2\le n\le9\), checking their connected components, signature invariant, edges, isolated words, and the six-letter shortest path. The component counts are \(1,2,4,8,16,32,64,128\); the derangement counts are \(1,2,9,44,265,1854,14833,133496\). Independently, `verify_normalform.py` constructs an explicit legal bubble-to-normal-form path from **every** derangement for \(2\le n\le8\), and checks precisely the two frozen words. Both programs are dependency-free Python 3 source and emit `VERIFY_OK` when all assertions pass. The proof, rather than any finite enumeration, establishes the claim for all \(n\ge2\).

## Relationship to prior work
The 2004 work of Baril and Vajnovszki and of Korsh and LaFollette supplies unrestricted Gray-code algorithms for derangements by transpositions and/or three-cycles. Such moves are more permissive than a **single adjacent-position swap that must preserve the derangement condition at each step**. Baril, Mansour and Petrossian (2014) classify permutations by *excedance boards*, recording positions and values above the diagonal; this is finer than the sign of each inverse-position comparison and does not determine local-swap reachability. Ehrenborg and Steingrímsson (2000) count prescribed excedance sets but not the components of this induced adjacent-swap graph. Chitturi and Krishnaveni (arXiv 2016) study permutation adjacencies in the sense of *successions*, not connectedness of derangements under local swaps. Mütze's Gray-code survey contrasts full permutation adjacent-swap Gray codes with derangement Gray codes using more general operations. No examined statement implies the exponential component decomposition or the two frozen states; equivalence beyond the consulted texts remains a possible literature risk.

## Limitations
The theorem concerns adjacent swaps of **positions**, with every intermediate permutation fixed-point-free. It does not describe swaps of arbitrarily distant positions, multi-entry rotations, or all shortest-path distances inside components. The bounded-distance witness relies on finite exhaustive breadth-first search; a general formula for internal component diameters is not claimed. A relevant 2013 thesis on adjacent interchanges with fixed multiset restrictions was located bibliographically, but its PDF could not be retrieved for comparison. Unindexed or inaccessible equivalent results may exist. No independent external scientific audit has been completed.
