In a stationary Brownian gas with simultaneous Poisson resetting, empirically width-normalized extreme particles have a non-max-stable two-time Hüsler–Reiss age-mixture limit at lag \(s/(r\log N)\). Its ordered-limit upper-tail dependence is the universal stretched exponential \(\chi(s)=e^{-\sqrt{s}}\), even though each one-time edge has a standard Gumbel law.
