For the classical Wilkinson matrix, uniformly breaking all exact partial-pivot ties gives \(\Pr(G_n=2^{n-1})=2/n!\), even though the conventional first-tie rule realizes exponential growth deterministically. The entire growth distribution is explicit, and \(\mathbb E G_n=2+2/n+O(n^{-2})\): the worst-case construction has factorially rare catastrophic pivot histories under this precise tie policy.
