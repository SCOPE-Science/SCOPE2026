# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The claim follows from the exact binary-digit path identity \(S_n=uM^n\mathbf1\); the four trace/determinant cases exhaust the twelve ordered pair assignments. Cayley--Hamilton proves the all-horizon rational generating functions. At fair selection either the right constant-vector eigenidentity or a rank-one quadratic matrix identity gives exact geometric survival for all \(n\). For biased selection, exact resultants and a sample-point root comparison, together with positivity and continuity, prove strict ordering globally in the open parameter interval. The accompanying standalone symbolic script checks all critical identities and independently enumerates bounded horizons. The enumeration is corroboration, not an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS (provisional bibliographic comparison)

The closest 2012/2014 primary papers establish random-hole escape theory and a transfer-operator representation but do not state or imply the all-pairs four-class fixed-length cylinder classification without new explicit analysis. The closest exact geometric example in Georgiou--Dettmann--Altmann concerns uniform random sampling from *all four* holes and therefore has constant spatial hazard, unlike the two-hole switching regime here. Previously reported deterministic periodic switching is a different model. Searches for binary random Markov holes, two-cylinder annealed escape, and equivalent finite-state transfer classifications found no equivalent result. More remote automata and weighted forbidden-word literature may contain a repackaging; this remains a residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The natural first nontrivial random-hole model with two overlapping-digit cylinders admits a *complete* finite-horizon and asymptotic classification. Equal probabilities induce exact geometric memorylessness despite nonuniform local hazards; an arbitrarily small bias splits this universality into four distinct asymptotic escape rates with a strict global order. This isolates a measurable failure of naive spatial-hazard reasoning, not merely a parameter replacement in the general transfer theorem.

## Scientific limits

This is an annealed statement with an initially uniform base point and iid fresh holes. Quenched rates, longer word lengths, and extension beyond the binary doubling map are not established. Independent source coverage is not exhaustive. General transfer-operator machinery is credited to prior authors.
