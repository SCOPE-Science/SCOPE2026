A uniformly positive but arbitrarily weak longitudinal field creates exponentially many strictly absorbing states in the weakly frustrated one-dimensional Ising–Glauber ring, even though zero field leaves at most six. The complete field-dependent classification yields a transfer-matrix growth constant \(\lambda_*\) satisfying \(\lambda_*^4-\lambda_*^3-1=0\), and sharp disappearance of the exponential absorbing family at \(h=2K\).
