On a fifteen-person tree network, the uniquely best source for independent message propagation can lose first place and then regain it as link reliability increases, even though no tree with at most fourteen people permits this strict global reentry. The two exact switching points are the roots in \((0,1)\) of \(p^4+p^3+p^2-3p+1\), approximately \(0.4369111272\) and \(0.7197399637\).
