Fifth-degree spherical harmonics interpolate arbitrary values at the normalized triangle centroids of a regular simplex with \(m\ge7\) vertices, except when \(m=27\): then precisely the mean-zero data are attainable on all \(2925\) centroids. Replacing any one centroid sample by any original simplex vertex restores complete interpolation, and the four exact Gegenbauer–Johnson eigenvalues reveal the isolated resonance.
