For ten dyadic lengths from \(16\) through \(8192\), Rudin–Shapiro factor necklaces have an exact three-level spectrum: \(4n-12\) singleton classes, two two-factor classes, and four complete length-\(n\) rotation classes. The finite census yields \(c_r(n)=4n-6\) at each tested length; no infinite extrapolation is asserted.
