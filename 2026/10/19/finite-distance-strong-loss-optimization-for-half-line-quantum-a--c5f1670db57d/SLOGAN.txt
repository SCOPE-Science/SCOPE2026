For a half-line quantum walk with one Lindblad boundary sink, the maximum eventual absorption probability occurs at a *stronger-than-matched* loss rate for every finite starting distance beyond the boundary. At large distance \(s\), the unique optimal loss ratio is \(1+3/(4s^3)+O(s^{-4})\), and the gain over matching is \(3/(16\pi s^6)+O(s^{-7})\).
