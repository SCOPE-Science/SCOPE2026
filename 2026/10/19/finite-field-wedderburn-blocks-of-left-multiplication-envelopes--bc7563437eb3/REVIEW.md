# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The identity \(L_{e_x}U^{-1}=T_{\varphi x}\), for \(U=L_{e_0}\), recovers every group translation. The explicit Fourier projectors are well-defined because the coefficient characteristic does not divide the abelian group's order. Projectors and powers of \(U\) give every matrix unit within, and none between, the character orbits of \(\beta\). Frobenius descent and the finite-division-ring theorem yield full matrix rings over finite fields; the same split-block argument gives the commutant and exact dimensions. The standalone direct-rank checker agrees in nineteen cyclic and five rank-two configurations. The proof, rather than those samples, handles all stated parameters.

## Originality — PASS, qualified by literature risk

The 2024 Kaur–Singh Theorem 8.10 already covers multiplicity freeness over \(\mathbb C\) of the right regular action for affine connected quandles; using the parameter swap \(\varphi\leftrightarrow1-\varphi\), its complex conclusion is an antecedent to the left-action question here. No novelty is claimed for that complex multiplicity-freeness. The finite-field statement adds exact extension-field matrix blocks and semisimplicity even in characteristics dividing the affine acting group's order, where complex representation results do not imply the conclusion by Maschke. Zhang (2026) addresses different Alexander-algebra invariants, the 2022 dihedral representation work treats the complex right action, and 2021 Lie transformation results do not identify this finite-field associative envelope. Searches of relevant research records and accessible earlier findings did not identify a covering finite-field theorem. Risk remains that modular affine-group representation literature contains an equivalent block decomposition under different terminology.

## Value — PASS

An intrinsic associative operator algebra of every finite Latin Alexander quandle receives a complete Wedderburn decomposition, center and commutant description, and easily calculated dimensions. Modular semisimplicity (despite characteristic dividing the group of generated permutations) and extension-field block fusion provide meaningful field-sensitive structure, not a change of notation or isolated numerical examples. The result supplies a reproducible classification usable for nonassociative representation and quandle-algebra questions.

## Boundaries

The characteristic dividing the abelian group's order and non-Latin cases remain outside the theorem. Some complex behavior was known; possible equivalence to general modular affine permutation algebra results is the main bibliographic uncertainty. No independent audit has been conducted.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
