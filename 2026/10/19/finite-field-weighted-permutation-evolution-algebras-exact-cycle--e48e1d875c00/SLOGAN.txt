Weighted-permutation evolution algebras over \(\mathbf F_q\) are classified by squaring-orbits of scalar labels on directed cycles; their isomorphism-class generating function, automorphism orders, and element-idempotent counts all have explicit cycle formulas. A nonzero-idempotent-free algebra of this form exists precisely when \(q-1\) has an odd prime factor, with a sharp minimal dimension given by the least order of \(2\) modulo such a prime.
