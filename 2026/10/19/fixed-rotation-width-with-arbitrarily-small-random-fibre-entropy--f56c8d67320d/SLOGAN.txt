An iid pair of real-analytic degree-one circle maps can have any fixed positive random rotation-set diameter \(\Delta\) while its positive fibre topological entropy tends to zero: choose an identity map and the rare sine lift \(x\mapsto x+K\sin(2\pi x)\), with active probability \(\Delta/(2K)\). Its entropy is exactly the active probability times the deterministic entropy, bounded between \(\frac{\Delta}{2K}\log(K+1)\) and \(\frac{\Delta}{2K}\log(1+2\pi K)\); hence rotation width alone cannot furnish a positive uniform random-entropy lower bound.
