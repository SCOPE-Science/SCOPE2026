Nilpotent \(2\times2\) matrices over every finite commutative chain ring of length \(m\ge3\) have either index at most \(m+1\) or index exactly \(2m\); the latter occur with proportion \((q^2-1)(q-1)/q^3\) among all nilpotent matrices. The forbidden interval is sharp and can fail over non-chain local rings: \(k[x,y]/(x^2,y^2)\) admits an index-five witness in odd characteristic.
