For any height-one forest poset with \(m\) Hasse edges and \(c\) components, its incidence algebra's commutator Lie reduct is \(k^{\oplus c}\oplus\mathfrak b_k^{\oplus m}\), irrespective of forest shape or orientation. Over every \(\mathbb F_q\), the entire common-centralizer codimension distribution of ordered \(s\)-tuples is \(q^{cs}(1+(q^s-1)(q+1)t+(q^s-1)(q^s-q)t^2)^m\), so even all tuple lengths fail to distinguish explicit associative algebras that are neither isomorphic nor opposite-isomorphic.
