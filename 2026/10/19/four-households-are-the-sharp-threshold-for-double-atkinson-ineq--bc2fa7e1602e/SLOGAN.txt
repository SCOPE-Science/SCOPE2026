Four equally weighted households are the minimum population size for two strict reversals of the same pair's Atkinson inequality ranking as aversion increases. For \(X=(4,9,9,22)\) and \(Y=(5,5,16,18)\), the ranking follows \(X>Y\), then \(X<Y\), then \(X>Y\), with changes near \(\varepsilon=1.42112\) and \(\varepsilon=2.14055\).
