For a \(k\)-fold expanding map with one forbidden length-two cylinder in each of three recurring time slots, all \(k^6\) schedules fall into exactly four strictly ordered escape-rate classes determined by the number of cyclic digit matches. Period three is the earliest switching period at which permuting the *same* holes changes the asymptotic escape rate: binary schedules can have a defective unit transfer eigenvalue or a Perron multiplier \(2+\sqrt3\).
