For every finite two-run binary power \((0^a1^b)^m\) with \(a,b,m\ge2\), exactly \(2m(m-1)\) distinct minimum string attractors exist, and all are characterized by two explicit families of boundary pairs. Remarkably, any two positions attract every factor precisely when they hit just the four factors \(0\), \(1\), \(01\), and \(10\).
