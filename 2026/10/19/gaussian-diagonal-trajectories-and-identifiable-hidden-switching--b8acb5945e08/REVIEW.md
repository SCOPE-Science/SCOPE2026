# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The stationary stochastic convolutions exist uniquely by exponential contraction. Conditional on the complete two-state bath trajectory, the two coordinate processes are independent Gaussian processes with complementary random variances. Their summed projected covariance is nonrandom exactly for the two diagonal directions, proving *process-level* Gaussianity; all other directions have strictly positive stationary fourth cumulant. The beta law for the filtered telegraph state follows from the zero-flux stationary two-velocity transport equations, with parameter \(\nu=\gamma/(2\kappa)\). Exact generator calculations independently reproduce the fourth, sixth and eighth cumulants. The crossed-square Gaussian identity produces strict joint dependence, and the two-dimensional linear filter-moment system proves the lag formula and exact rate-recovery identity, including the repeated-root case. No finite calculation is used as an infinite-domain proof. Risks are standard stationary-boundary justifications for the PDMP and sign-relabeling nonidentifiability, both dealt with in the proof.

## Originality

**PASS as a specific combined classification, with material residual priority risk.** The switching diffusion is a special case of the multiple Markov-modulated OU models in Huang et al. (arXiv:1412.7952, Section 6); conditional normality and all-moment recurrences are not claimed. Chechkin et al. (arXiv:1611.06202) and Alexandre et al. (arXiv:2207.01880) provide general fluctuating-diffusivity and cumulant tools. Ślęzak et al. (arXiv:1710.02222) already recognize non-Gaussian joint dependence hidden behind Gaussian marginals in other Langevin mixtures. The specific sharp classification of every Gaussian projected *process* in complementary switching, exact fourth-lag matrix signs, and point-identification of switching rate from its curvature were not located as claims in the inspected source sections or searches. Since several features are straightforward to derive from broader general models, independent mathematical-priority review is necessary and an unlocated equivalent specialization remains possible. Absence of matching search results is not proof of novelty.

## Scientific value

**PASS.** The motivating inverse question is whether a switching thermal environment can be detectable when covariance measurements and even two complete scalar Gaussian process laws are indistinguishable from equilibrium OU. The answer provides a sharp yes, with a signed, nonzero, time-resolved fourth-order dependence and an exact inverse formula for the environmental flip rate. The result delineates a natural structural nonidentifiability boundary at zero noise contrast and shows what statistical information must be retained to distinguish the models. This is more than a moment recomputation: it classifies all Gaussian observation directions and the full two-coordinate hidden dependence, while identifying an unknown physical rate that no second-order observation can recover. It makes no claim about finite-sample estimation precision or entropy production.
