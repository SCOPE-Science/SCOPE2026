In a two-dimensional harmonic diffusion with complementary telegraph-switched noise intensities, exactly the two diagonal projections are complete Gaussian Ornstein–Uhlenbeck processes, yet together they are non-Gaussian and have strictly correlated squared amplitudes. All observed second-order covariances match a Gaussian equilibrium model, but a fourth-order lag function recovers the hidden switching rate exactly: \(\gamma=-A''(0^+)/[4\kappa A(0)]\).
