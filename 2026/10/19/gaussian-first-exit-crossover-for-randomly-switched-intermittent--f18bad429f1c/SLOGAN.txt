For independent switching between two intermittent interval maps, a deep excursion from the neutral fixed point has a sharp escape-time crossover at exponent ratio \(\beta/\alpha=3/2\): below it the slower map produces a deterministic correction of order \(Z^{2-\beta/\alpha}\), at equality it shifts a Gaussian limit, and above it the Gaussian is centered. The Gaussian variance is exactly \((1-p)/(\alpha p^2)\) for every fixed supercritical ratio.
