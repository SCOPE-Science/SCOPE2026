When only position is reset, an unbounded Brownian-square diffusion medium retains enough memory to prevent stationarity: the position divided by \(\sqrt t\) converges quantitatively to a Gaussian times an independent Laplace variable. Its logarithmic center, \(2/3\)-stretched-exponential tails and kurtosis \(18\) distinguish it sharply from the same-mean deterministic diffusivity's Laplace limit of kurtosis \(6\).
