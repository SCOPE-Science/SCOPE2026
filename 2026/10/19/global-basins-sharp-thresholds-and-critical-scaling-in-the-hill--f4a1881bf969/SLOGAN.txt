For the positive Hill-feedback map \(T(x,y)=(y,b/(1+x^m))\), the complete global basin structure changes at an explicit feedback threshold: a fixed-point attractor gives way to an attracting four-cycle separated by the exact coordinate-line stable set of a saddle four-cycle. At criticality convergence has a universal \(n^{-1/2}\) coefficient, and the born four-cycle has an explicit square-root parameter amplitude.
