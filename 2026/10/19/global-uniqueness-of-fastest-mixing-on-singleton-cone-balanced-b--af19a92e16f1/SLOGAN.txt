On the balanced complete bipartite graph augmented by one universal hub, the fastest symmetric stochastic mixing kernel is globally unique for every \(m\ge2\), even without assuming that competing edge probabilities respect any graph symmetry. All hub edges must have weight \(1/(2m)\), all bipartite edges weight \((2m-1)/(3m^2)\), and the exact optimal SLEM is \(1/3-1/(6m)\).
