# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Brownian killed-resolvent integration yields the one-trial success probabilities and exit-time expectation. Geometric restart gives exact passage probability and duration; reflected two-type renewal cycles have zero reward mean, probabilities \(p(1-p)\) for each signed \(L\) displacement, and i.i.d. finite-moment cycle data. Hence the centered renewal variance theorem gives the claimed diffusivity, including the alternating-step covariance. Hyperbolic identities reduce optimization to a strictly convex scalar function and an exact golden-ratio endpoint comparison. The standalone checker independently integrates the resolvent and passes 2177 assertions. Proof, not sampling, establishes infinite-parameter conclusions.

**Originality — PASS, subject to priority risk.** The exact model and symmetric lattice result belong to Alston and Bertrand; those are not claimed as new. Their general first-passage formulas are inputs rather than novel techniques, and their irregular-site and globally optimized bounds motivate the question. The checked primary text does not formulate or classify the fixed-period two-site centered dispersion phase transition. In particular, a two-site cycle has cancelling mean steps and a nonzero adjacent-step covariance, not captured by simply summing local uncentered step squares. Uniform-period biased-capture models are not equivalent to alternating physical site spacings. An unpublished derivation or implicit use of more general Markov-renewal theory remains possible; independent priority assessment has not been performed.

**Value — PASS.** Two alternating gaps are the minimal nonuniform periodic reset-site architecture, and diffusivity maximization at fixed cell length and reset intensity is a natural physical design question. The theorem gives a complete optimizer/supremum classification with an exact golden-ratio switching value and reveals the significance of anti-persistent neighbor passages. It does not claim superiority over the globally rate-optimized homogeneous lattice or over all possible arrays.

**Scientific limitations.** Only instantaneous Poisson resetting, strictly positive alternating gaps, free Brownian motion, and one-dimensional long-time centered diffusivity are covered. The cluster is a nonattained geometric limit. Known first-passage and renewal machinery is cited rather than reinvented.
