For homogeneous quadratic forms in \(\mathbb F_{3^f}[x_1,\ldots,x_n]/(x_i^3)\), the polar rank alone determines every *graded* Jordan-chain starting degree of multiplication, yielding \(n+1\) distinct explicit quotient Hilbert series. Their exact frequencies over every finite field of characteristic three follow from Gaussian coefficients and orthogonal-group orders.
