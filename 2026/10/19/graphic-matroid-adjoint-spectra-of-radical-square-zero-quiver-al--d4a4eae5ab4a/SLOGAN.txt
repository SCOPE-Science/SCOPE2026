For every loopless finite quiver, the distribution of ranks of commutators with individual elements in its radical-square-zero algebra is controlled by the underlying graphic matroid, with an explicit flats formula and a closed cycle-correction formula for every unicyclic graph over every finite field. Reorienting arrows preserves all pairwise-commuting tuple schemes at every arity, even though the cyclic and transitive orientations of a triangle give nonisomorphic commutator Lie algebras in odd characteristic.
