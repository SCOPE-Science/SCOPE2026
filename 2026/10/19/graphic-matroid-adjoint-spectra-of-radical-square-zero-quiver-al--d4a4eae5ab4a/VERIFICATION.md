## Reproducible algebraic checks

For the commuting schemes, write the commutator component for each arrow and pair of arguments as in `RESULT.md`; reversing the third arrow multiplies its generators by a unit and leaves all other generators fixed. For the Lie comparison, compute the quotient action on the three basis arrows. The first two weight functionals are independent and the full relation line is generated by `(1,1,1)` or `(1,1,-1)`, respectively. A permutation and a common nonzero scalar do not relate those lines in odd characteristic. These are proof-based checks over the given field, not calculations at selected primes.

The rank-distribution checker in the historical source package concerns the original rank formulas, outside the narrowed finding. It does not check ideal equality or Lie nonisomorphism and is not required to replay this proof.
