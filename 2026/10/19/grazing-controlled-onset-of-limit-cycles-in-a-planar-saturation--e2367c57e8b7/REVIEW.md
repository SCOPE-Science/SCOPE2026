# Scientific review: grazing-controlled saturation-oscillator onset

## Correctness — PASS

The precise claim concerns the local cycle at fixed positive coupling \(b\) and sufficiently small positive detuning \(a\). The exact polar equations imply an angularly monotone flow in the shrinking annulus. Four saturation caps contribute total radial defect \((16\sqrt2/3)\eta^{3/2}\), whereas the interior instability contributes \(2\pi a\) per revolution. The moving-radius error is \(O_b(a)\) over one turn; the cap-integral error is \(O_b(a^{4/3})\), sufficient to prove the radius expansion. The divergence integral establishes a strictly contracting Poincaré map and the \(a^{1/3}\) Floquet law. The reciprocal angular-speed integral yields the physical-period coefficient. The included finite tests are consistent with, but are not substitutes for, the analytical estimates.

## Originality — PASS, subject to historical-priority risk

Ma et al. (arXiv:1306.6880v1, equation (2), §§III–V) present precisely this clipped-feedback family and identify the qualitative periodic-orbit transition. Their inspected equations and discussion do not state the four-cap selected-radius coefficient, the leading transverse multiplier coefficient, or the physical-time correction. **Important earlier art:** Freire, Ponce and Ros (1999; see bibliographic abstract, DOI:10.1142/S0218127499000638), and their 2005 SISO saturation Theorem 1 (full author-provided text, DOI:10.3182/20050703-6-CZ-1902.00763) derive the same \(2/3\) amplitude and \(1/3\) Floquet *exponents* and a first-order period law for a single-input clipped output. Those exponents and the general mechanism are therefore **not claimed as original**. Their theorems restrict the nonlinearity to one scalar saturation, giving three zones with two parallel switching lines, while the present model clips both coordinate channels independently and has four distinct angular caps. Their coefficients and assumptions do not give this particular two-channel result by substitution. Simpson (arXiv:1905.01329, Table 1 and §10.2) has a \(2/3\)-exponent for a *different* hysteretic two-fold event. Targeted published literature and mathematical-result searches found no direct implication covering the full, parameter-specific two-channel coefficient triple. **The 1999 origin article was not obtained in full**, so possible broader coverage there remains a genuine unresolved historical-priority risk; the review is an informed provisional comparison, not certification of priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The qualitative limit-cycle transition in the source paper leaves its near-critical amplitude, physical frequency, and relaxation time undetermined. The selected cycle appears at the *edge of a period annulus*, so smooth Hopf scaling at the origin is inapplicable. The *already known* fractional exponents \(2/3\) and \(1/3\) acquire a new concrete four-cap realization; the coupling-independent leading amplitude constant, independently determined leading Floquet coefficient, and explicit period response quantify a two-channel geometry not covered by the SISO theorem. These model-specific constants and the fixed-\(b\) uniform proof are the valuable content, not the exponents in isolation.
