At the boundary of a disk of neutral periodic motions, a planar clipped-feedback oscillator selects an attracting cycle whose radial excess is \(C_*a^{2/3}\), with \(C_*=(3\pi/(8\sqrt2))^{2/3}\). Its physical period is \((2\pi/b)(1+a)+O_b(a^{4/3})\), while its transverse Floquet multiplier approaches one with the distinct rate \(1-\mu_a\sim(8\sqrt{2C_*}/b)a^{1/3}\).
