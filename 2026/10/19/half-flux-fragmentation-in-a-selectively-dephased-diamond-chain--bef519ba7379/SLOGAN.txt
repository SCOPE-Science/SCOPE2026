Independent dephasing at every backbone site of a periodic diamond chain leaves \(n^2+1\) stationary operators at generic magnetic flux but \(n^2+n\) at half flux, where independent Aharonov--Bohm cages retain their populations. A backbone-localized walker returns with probability \(1/(2n)\) generically but \(1/2\) at half flux; for a bridge-localized walker the half-flux return is exactly \(5/16\).
