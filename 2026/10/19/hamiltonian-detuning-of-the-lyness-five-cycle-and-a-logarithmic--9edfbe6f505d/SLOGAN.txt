For the positive Lyness map, perturbing the global five-cycle at \(a=1\) advances each noncritical invariant oval by an exact first-order Hamiltonian time: \(\partial_a\rho_a(r)|_{a=1}=1/(5P(r))>0\). This parameter response has an explicit harmonic-center limit and decays sharply as \(1/(25\log r)\) on high-energy ovals.
