# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The basis-size lemma handles every possible generating word of length at most two and reduces rank preservation to the independent induced-edge inequalities. The standard unicyclic graph criterion and the already established factor-basis theorem then give the exact irreducible structure. Labelled cyclic decomposition proves the enumeration; the rooted-tree singularity supplies the asymptotic. The direct factor-pair exhaustive checks for two and three generators independently corroborate the reduction, with larger graph and coefficient checks.

**Originality — PASS, with bibliographic limits.** Bell–Foster–Reidenbach, arXiv:2603.15177v3, Theorem 1, already gives a general test for irreducibility by nontrivial factor bases. Its Sections 3–5 and Appendix A, together with the October proceedings manuscript, were inspected. The stated Hamiltonian characterization, unrestricted alphabet-size counting generating function and exact exponential rarity do not appear there. Targeted full-text and terminology searches and comparison with nearby indexed mathematical results found no covering result. The known general criterion entails the yes/no cases, but not the new explicit enumerative theorem without an additional graph/forest derivation. Equivalent classical combinatorial enumeration under other terminology remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** The first nontrivial uniform-length class is mathematically natural, and this result gives both a sharp factorization geometry and an exact size distribution for all alphabet sizes; the exponential rarity quantifies typical structure in a way not available from the qualitative factor-basis criterion alone. The two-cycle exception, orientational multiplicities, and labelled EGF are structural rather than arbitrary finite slices.

Closest prior: Bell–Foster–Reidenbach 2026, general factor-basis theorem and incidence-matrix tests. The result is restricted to 2-uniform labelled word endomorphisms; a full uniqueness claim is not made. Formal external proof certification and independent auditing remain unperformed.
