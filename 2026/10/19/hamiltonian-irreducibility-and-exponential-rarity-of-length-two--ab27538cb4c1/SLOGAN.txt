A rank-preserving endomorphism sending each generator to exactly two letters is irreducible precisely when its two-letter image graph is a spanning cycle (with the opposite-word two-cycle at order two). For \(n\ge3\) there are exactly \(2^{n-1}n!(n-1)!\) such endomorphisms, an asymptotic fraction \(2^{-3/4}e^{1/8}\Gamma(1/4)n^{-1/4}e^{-n}\) of all rank-preserving length-two endomorphisms.
