In a finite heterogeneous Brownian gas that resets collectively whenever any particle reaches its own positive threshold, every joint far-left excursion has the same exact power \(R^{-(N-2)}\). Conditional two-particle extreme dependence is \((D_j/(D_i+D_j))^{N/2-1}\), independently of all reset thresholds, and the stationary moment cutoff is precisely order \(N-2\).
