# Research review: hexagon-switch graph of Pappus three-edge-colourings

## Correctness — PASS
The statement concerns one fixed finite graph, with a fully explicit point-line incidence convention, three named edge colours, and switches restricted to alternating six-cycles. The local permutation-sign obstruction is proved directly, with eighteen sign changes on a full Hamiltonian switch or six sign changes on a hexagon. Every colouring is enumerated either from perfect matchings and complementary even cycles or independently from point-local colour permutations with incremental line constraints. Both exact procedures obtain the same canonical list of \(120\) states and the same \(216\) local-transition pairs. Checking the complete adjacency set gives two parity-homogeneous components of size \(60\). Removing degree-nine hubs and inspecting the remaining cycles proves the graph isomorphism to two copies of \(T(K_{3,3})\). All-pairs breadth-first search proves the component radii and diameters. Source programs and exact state/transition data are embedded and reproduce their recorded outputs.

## Originality — PASS, with limitations
McDonald–Mohar–Scheide treat four-colour equivalence, not three-colour local switching. Belcastro–Haas investigate unrestricted edge-Kempe classes and general colouring reconfiguration graphs, not this exact Pappus graph six-cycle transition graph. Goedgebeur–Östergård's 2021 enumeration covers unrestricted three-edge Kempe classes of bipartite cubic graphs at this order and may already include the Pappus unrestricted class count. Consequently, the novelty asserted is **not** the number of unrestricted classes or the number of matchings. It is the exact two-copy \(T(K_{3,3})\) structure, local-versus-unrestricted adjacency/metric comparison, and the completeness of parity as a restricted-reachability invariant. An earlier Pappus *three-edge-colouring quotient* computation has already identified the unrestricted graph on \(20\) colour-name orbits as \(2(K_1\vee(3K_3))\), with \(36\) orbit edges and a matching-sign invariant; no novelty is claimed for that structure or obstruction. The new graph is the nontrivial **six-sheeted named-colour covering** of this quotient under hexagon-only moves, isomorphic to two copies of \(T(K_{3,3})\), with proved degree spectrum and exact diameter \(6\). A quotient does not determine which lifts of each transition are adjacent or whether its cover stays connected. The Pappus *single-perfect-matching* switch graph also does not determine simultaneous compatibility of three matchings. The possibility of an equivalent unindexed work or a more detailed supplementary graph-level data file is an explicit remaining risk; no independent publication-priority certification is claimed.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The operation is the shortest possible nontrivial edge-Kempe switch in a cubic graph of girth six, and the Pappus graph is a canonical symmetrical finite configuration. The theorem completely determines whether two feasible three-edge-colourings communicate using only local moves and gives the exact structure and worst-case number of those moves. It also proves that restricting allowed Kempe cycles changes diameters from \(4\) to \(6\) without splitting further classes, while quotienting out colour names shortens the diameter to \(2\). The alternating six-cycle thickening of \(K_{3,3}\) explains the entire degree and component profile. These are coherent structural reconfiguration facts, not a parameter-renaming exercise or a restatement of earlier matching counts.
