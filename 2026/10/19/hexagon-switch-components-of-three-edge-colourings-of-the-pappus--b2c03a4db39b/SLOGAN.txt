The hexagon-switch graph of proper three-edge-colourings of the Pappus graph is exactly two copies of an alternating six-cycle thickening of \(K_{3,3}\), each with \(60\) states and diameter \(6\). A canonical local colour-permutation sign completely characterizes reachability, and allowing switches on longer alternating cycles reduces the component diameter to \(4\) without merging components.
