Poissonian Maxwellian refreshes of an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck velocity preserve its exact Gaussian one-time law and can be covariance-matched by an ordinary Gaussian process, yet leave a strictly positive fourth cumulant in every positive-time displacement. The long-time absolute fourth-cumulant rate and standardized excess-kurtosis coefficient have distinct unique optimal refresh rates, \((\sqrt{10}-2)\gamma/3\) and \(\sqrt2\,\gamma\), while a fourth-order velocity-lag observable identifies the hidden refresh rate.
