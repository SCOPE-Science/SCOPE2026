For every fixed Hill order \(n\ge3\), sufficiently strong environmental transmission creates at least two endemic equilibria even when the invasion reproduction number is below one and the disease-free state is exponentially stable. Their infected levels split into a small branch of order \(\alpha^{-1/(n-1)}\) and a large branch converging to \(\Lambda/K\), exposing a distinction between local transcritical bifurcation and global subthreshold persistence of equilibria.
