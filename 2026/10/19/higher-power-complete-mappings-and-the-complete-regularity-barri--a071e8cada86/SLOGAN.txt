For every even integer \(k\ge2\), a finite semigroup admits a \(k\)-complete mapping **exactly when** it is completely regular, with an explicit power-map witness. For all \(k\ge2\), finite inverse semigroups must be Clifford, and when their maximal subgroups are abelian, odd \(k\) works exactly when no component has a nontrivial cyclic Sylow \(2\)-subgroup.
