# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Both Green-class parametrizations are bijective: rank-two maps have a single kernel cut and two increasing image values, while rank-codimension-one maps have a single doubled adjacent kernel block and one omitted image value. The proof spells out the exact two-sided transversal conditions and gives inverse-branch formulas on disjoint cases, fixing idempotents. Independent finite replay is supportive rather than a substitute for these quantified arguments.

**Originality — PASS (self-review).** The October 2026 primary article proves existence of a permutation inverse matching, not an involution. The 2024 integer-ratio argument implies an involution in the even-chain rank-two case, but not uniformly for odd-chain rank two or for rank-codimension-one. Earlier orientation-preserving monoid results concern a larger class and its canonical involution is not closed on chain order-preserving transformations. This result is a strict all-chain, idempotent-fixing strengthening in the two stated extremal classes, not a solution for intermediate ranks. Finite-chain results do not imply a theorem for arbitrary chain length. Equivalent independently developed constructions remain a residual bibliographic risk.

**Value — PASS.** The open structural distinction between permutation and involution matchings motivates explicit rank-wise progress. Uniform formulas, fixing all idempotents, locate the unresolved obstruction entirely among the interior ranks of the Green stratification. The property cannot simply be inferred from the known permutation matchings or from divisibility arguments in the nondivisible cases.

**Limitations.** The larger orientation-preserving monoid has known involution matchings, but restriction is not automatic. The results do not establish a matching for a full general \(\mathcal O_n\) at arbitrary size, and existing finite computations are not an infinite proof.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
