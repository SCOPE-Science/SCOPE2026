# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The quotient-coordinate-ring equations are \(X_w=\sum_{f(v)=w}X_v^2\). Peeling incoming trees sets their variables literally to zero in the quotient ring, leaving exactly one monic polynomial \(T^{2^L}-T\) per cycle. Root multiplicities and cyclic multiplicative-group counts then yield the scheme and arithmetic assertions. Divisor and multiple Möbius inversion recover cycle multiplicities with a known vertex bound. The two explicit endofunctions have identical cycle multisets but different ranks of the structural matrix, so their algebras are nonisomorphic. The standalone checker tests hundreds of graph-field pairs by direct multiplication and checks polynomial separability; infinite statements rest on the displayed proofs.

## Originality — PASS, subject to stated priority risks

The closest precise source is Khudoyberdiyev–Omirov–Qaralleh, arXiv:1307.0993v1, whose cycle decomposition of permutation evolution algebras is antecedent and not claimed as new. Elduque–Labra, arXiv:1312.4685v1, covers graph structure, nilpotence and automorphisms, not the asserted all-field idempotent schemes or finite-cutoff inversion. Narkuziyev, arXiv:1803.11415v1, studies two-permutation structure and idempotents in special real cases but does not appear to state the inverse arithmetic law. Later general treatments of idempotent schemes cover separability phenomena but not the particular reconstruction/indistinguishability boundary. An equivalent formula in finite monomial polynomial dynamical systems or a Galois-theoretic restatement of older results remains a genuine priority risk, and failed search alone is not considered proof of uniqueness.

## Value — PASS

The result answers a natural identifiability question for evolution-algebra idempotent loci: exactly which functional-digraph data are recovered by all finite characteristic-two extension-field idempotent counts? The theorem recovers the full recurrent-cycle length profile using only the first \(n\) counts, determines all scheme-theoretic multiplicities, and proves a concrete structural obstruction to reconstructing incoming trees. This is more than a one-parameter enumeration and clarifies the information retained by a natural algebraic invariant.

## Scope and scientific limitations

The normal form is for unweighted, single-outgoing-arrow evolution algebras. No claim is made about weights, multiple successors, optimality of the observation cutoff or full graph reconstruction. Full historical priority cannot be certified from available published comparisons. Finite checks do not certify an infinite theorem.
