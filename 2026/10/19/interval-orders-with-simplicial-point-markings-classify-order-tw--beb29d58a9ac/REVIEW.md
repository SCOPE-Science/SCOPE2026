# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The spectrum recovers interval comparabilities from absent reversed pairs, and recovers singleton marks from absent repeated pairs. The necessity of simplicial independent markings follows from intervals reduced to points. The converse uses a strict-overlap interval representation and one-dimensional Helly's theorem to contract each admissible interval to a point without altering any comparison. The endpoint word attains the sharp length \(2q-|S|\); the independent-marking polynomial follows from the true-twin clique decomposition of simplicial vertices. Two separate finite enumerations agree through five labeled letters. The universal quantifier is supported by the geometric proof, not these finite enumerations.

## Originality
**PASS, subject to literature-coverage risk.** Simon's level-two index counts, labeled interval-order counts, and algorithms for Simon-class representatives are established prior results. The 2013 index paper's complete text, including its numeric appendix, does not express the classes through interval orders with independently markable simplicial points. The 2023 piecewise-complexity paper addresses minimality indices and algorithms for individual words; its full text does not derive this general structural bijection or length enumerator. The 2023 binary alpha-beta-factorization paper addresses a related but differently scoped factorization of binary classes; the full abstract and bibliographic record were available, but its complete text was not obtainable in this review. The concrete noncoverage of the first two texts plus differences in the third supports originality with a stated residual risk; the index sequence alone is not claimed as new.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** At degree two, deciding which abstract pair spectra and repetition statuses can coexist is a natural realizability question, distinct from merely tabulating the finite index. The simplicial-point characterization gives a graph-theoretic answer for every alphabet size and a complete, exact minimum-length distribution. The proof connects word subsequences, interval-order geometry, Helly intersections, and simplicial true twins. The contribution is structural classification, not a small numerical improvement.

## Limitations
The theorem is restricted to full-support level-two spectra and provides a finite combinatorial sum rather than an asymptotic formula or closed-form sequence. The complete-text limitation on the binary factorization paper and possible unindexed equivalents remain originality risks. Independent verification has not been performed.
