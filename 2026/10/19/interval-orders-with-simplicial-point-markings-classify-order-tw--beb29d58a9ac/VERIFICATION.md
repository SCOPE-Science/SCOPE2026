# Verification

To reproduce the finite experiments, run `python check.py` with Python 3. The file uses only the standard library. It computes all labeled interval orders from words with two occurrences of each letter, then enumerates all independent subsets of simplicial vertices of their incomparability graphs. Independently, a breadth-first search through all attainable subsequence signatures of orders at most two determines every distinct class's minimum representative length. Histograms match exactly for alphabet sizes one through five.

For five symbols, the shortest-length histogram at lengths five through ten is \(120,1800,8400,15720,12385,3451\), and the full-support total is \(41876\). The equality tests and literal output are generated by the program. The general classification rests on the interval-representation and Helly arguments in RESULT.md, not on finite testing.
