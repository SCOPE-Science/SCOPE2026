**Correctness — PASS.** Every automorphism is uniquely a quadratic shear followed by an invertible linear substitution; this is proved from the degree filtration. Squaring reduces to \(L^2=I\) and a linear kernel equation. The odd-characteristic eigenspace count is reconstructed term by term; the even-characteristic decomposition of the cubic tensor module into free and trivial \(C_2\)-modules yields the precise kernel and conjugacy quotient. The fixed-linear-part orbit claim is distinct from full-group conjugacy. The included actual verification program exhaustively checks all quadratic maps for \(n=2\) and prime fields \(q=2,3\), and all square-one linear parts for \(n=3\) and \(q=2,3\). These finite checks corroborate but are not substituted for the general proof.

The 2013 Dangovski–Drensky–Findik text has a different invariant-theory problem. Drensky–Yu's 2007 survey concerns generator-fixing automorphisms (only bibliographic abstract examined). The published fixed-tensor dimension-two record covers an adjacent rank-two tensor calculation but no dimension-at-least-three count or quadratic conjugacy; the truncated coordinate-cross automorphism record concerns a different quotient algebra. General modular \(C_2\)-representation theory is an explicit component, not an originality claim. No search proves novelty, and unindexed general classification is a residual risk.

This is not merely a renamed group-cohomology vanishing observation: the exact tensor-module decomposition and resulting class/fiber formulas are determined for the algebra family.
