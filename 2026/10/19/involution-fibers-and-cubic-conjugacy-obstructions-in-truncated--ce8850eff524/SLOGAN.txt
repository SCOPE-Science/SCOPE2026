In truncated free associative algebras \(k\oplus V\oplus(V\otimes V)\) of generator rank at least three, the number of square-one automorphisms has explicit Gaussian-binomial formulas in every finite characteristic. Quadratic conjugacy rigidifies each linear involution fiber in odd characteristic, but in characteristic two leaves exactly \(q^{(n-2r)^3}\) distinct orbits over a linear involution of rank \(r\).
