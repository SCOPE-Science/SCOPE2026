Cao and Schwartz (1999/2000) introduced the rank-one precursor, which is acknowledged; their information bounds and numerical experiments do not cover the batch theorem or this rate. Bittanti–Bolzern–Campi (1990) investigate another directional forgetting convergence problem, not the same normalized information-map identity. Searches of mathematical research publications with synonym, angular, geometric and recurrence queries did not identify a covering result. No unsuccessful search is interpreted as a uniqueness proof. **Value — PASS.** The global limit identifies the exact matrix selected by a broad, nonperiodic class of uniformly exciting equal-strength batches, improving a mere upper/lower condition-number bound into a limiting conditioning statement. The sharp local angular rate exposes a nontrivial price of such conditioning: nearly collinear directions converge at an inverse-square angular time scale even when the eventual condition number is one. The theorem therefore distinguishes steady conditioning from equilibration of information, relevant to normalized system-identification experiments and the design of excitation schedules.
