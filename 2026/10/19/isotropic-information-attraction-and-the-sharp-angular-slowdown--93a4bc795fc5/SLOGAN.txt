For equal-strength vector regressors, the SIFt-RLS information matrix globally approaches \(\rho^2 I/(1-\lambda)\) under uniformly spanning row subspaces, with an exact decreasing Stein divergence. On two alternating nearly parallel directions, the sharp two-step local contraction gap is only \(2(1-\lambda)\theta^2/(1+\lambda)+o(\theta^2)\): asymptotically perfect conditioning can coexist with arbitrarily slow information equilibration.
