For weak endomorphisms of every finite directed path, every idempotent is an interval clamp, and the rank distribution of its entire centralizer has an explicit binomial formula. Summing these local distributions yields the rational bivariate generating function \(\frac{xuz(1-x-xuz)^2}{(1-x)^2(1-xuz)(1-x(1+z))^2(1-xu(1+z))^2}\) for commuting pairs classified by both image ranks.
