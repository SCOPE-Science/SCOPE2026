For \(\mathbb F_q\)-linear maps with image in a fixed \(r\)-space of codimension \(c\), the regular nilpotents form one stabilizer-conjugacy orbit per Jordan partition \(\lambda\vdash r\), with exact multiplicity \(N_\lambda(q)q^{c(r-\ell(\lambda))}\). Their proportion among nilpotents decays as \(q^{-c}\prod_{i=2}^r(1-q^{-i})\), and only nonregular nilpotents can attain index \(r+1\) when \(c>0\).
