# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The normalized simplex integral and Hermite–Genocchi identity yield precisely the derivative of the reciprocal knot polynomial, with the stated normalization. The derivative numerator has degree \(nm\) and has no cancellations at any knot. The positive even-degree complete homogeneous polynomial identity excludes even-order real zeros; the next even derivative gives strict monotonicity and exactly one simple root in each gap for odd order. Conjugation and degree then give the upper-half-plane count with full analytic multiplicities. The proof covers every admissible knot tuple, \(n\), and \(m\), including \(m=0\) and \(m=1\); four knot tuples and seven orders were checked by exact symbolic computation.

## Originality — PASS, with priority qualification
Baxter–Brummelhuis's Hermite–Genocchi formula and Hunter's even homogeneous polynomial positivity, as documented by Böttcher–Garcia–Omar–O'Neill, are established components and receive explicit attribution. Langley's reciprocal-derivative results address a different infinitary setting, and Mallat–Waldspurger's Cauchy-wavelet phase-retrieval results do not give this finite spline-family zero census. The new claim is the single complete knot-independent all-orders upper-zero and real-root parity classification, not any of these inputs. Searches and comparisons found no equivalent statement, but an unindexed rational-function treatment may exist, and independent publication priority has not been established.

## Mathematical value — PASS
For arbitrarily nonuniform compactly supported B-splines and all higher Cauchy-kernel orders, the theorem completely determines the number of analytic zeros and their real-boundary parity behavior without solving high-degree equations. This exact global invariant quantifies the finite upper-half-plane zero/Blaschke complexity of a natural analytic transform family. Its parameter-uniform nature and complete classification supply more than a numerical census or a one-off derivative identity. No phase-retrieval stability consequence is asserted.

## Boundaries
Nonreal root simplicity and locations are not classified. Distinct knots are required, and unrelated wavelets are not covered. The 28 exact test cases supplement, not replace, the proof. Source inspection cannot exclude every older unpublished or unindexed equivalent.
