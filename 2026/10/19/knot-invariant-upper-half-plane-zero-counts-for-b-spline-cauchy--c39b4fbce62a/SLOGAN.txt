For every collection of \(n+1\) strictly ordered real knots, the probability-normalized B-spline Cauchy transform of kernel order \(n+m+1\) has exactly \(n\lfloor m/2\rfloor\) upper-half-plane zeros counted with multiplicity, independent of knot spacing. Its rational continuation has no real zeros when \(m\) is even and exactly one simple real zero in each knot gap when \(m\) is odd.
