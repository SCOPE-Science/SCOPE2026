For normalized row systems with pairwise inner product \(-\alpha/(n-1)\), the exact asymptotic error multiplier of a cyclic Kaczmarz sweep converges as the row count grows to \(-\alpha/W_{-1}(-\alpha e^{-\alpha})\). A sharp finite-size correction and the uniform near-simplex expansion \(1-r_n(\alpha)\sim2(1-\alpha)\) quantify this signed-correlation regime beyond existing positive-equicorrelation analyses.
