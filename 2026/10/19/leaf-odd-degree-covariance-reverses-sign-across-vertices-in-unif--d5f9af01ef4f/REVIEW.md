For a uniform labeled tree, Prüfer multiplicities are independent uniform categorical draws conditional on their total sample size. The exact parity-transform identities give diagonal and off-diagonal leaf/odd-degree indicator covariances. The strict global sign is established by a derivative inequality valid for all integers with at least four vertices; the zero cases are evaluated separately. Full second-moment formulas give the correlation limit. The accompanying verifier enumerated every Prüfer word at orders two through eight and passed 489 exact and asymptotic checks. The proof, not the experiment, establishes the universal claims.

The classic Prüfer/multinomial identities make the derivation accessible; they are not claimed as new. ## Mathematical value — PASS
Leaf count and the number of odd-degree vertices are canonical tree-shape statistics. Their dependence matters for random graph degree-profile fluctuations and for separating local competition from global behavior. The result determines a full all-size sign classification, exhibits opposite dependence signs at the vertex-pair and total-count levels, and shows a substantial correlation that persists asymptotically. This is more informative than tabulating a few instances or repeating a known marginal moment.
