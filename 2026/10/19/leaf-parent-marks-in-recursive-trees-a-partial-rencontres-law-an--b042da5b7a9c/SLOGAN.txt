In a uniform recursive tree, any compatible selection of \(r\) specified parent-to-final-leaf edges has probability \(1/(n-1)_{\underline r}\), so each vertex's entire direct-leaf-child indicator field has exactly the law of a subset of uniform-permutation fixed points. Different parents' leaf-child counts have explicit negative covariance yet converge jointly to independent birth-time-dependent Poisson laws; this gives a complete finite-size age-resolved protected-vertex formula.
