For the equilateral farthest-orthogonal-projection map on three complete lines, the two attracting three-cycles have exactly computable global basins separated by a discrete, exponentially spaced set of midpoint preimages. Along an oriented supporting ray the fraction attracted to either cycle has no limit: its sharp lower and upper accumulation values are \(1/3\) and \(2/3\), with an explicit log-periodic phase profile.
