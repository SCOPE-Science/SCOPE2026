For positive-definite systems with one positive and one negative rank-one-correctable spectral defect, forward and reverse log-determinant Bregman preconditioners switch at different logarithmic-mean thresholds. Even when each uniquely minimizes its divergence, its spectral condition number can exceed the globally best rank-one preconditioner by the sharp factor \(\frac{(K-1)^2}{K(\log K)^2}\), which is unbounded as \(K\to\infty\).
