For persistent walkers reversing with probability asymptotic to \(c/(n\log n)\), the distance to the ballistic edge grows like a *random power* of time: \(\Pr(N-|X_N|\le N^z)\to z^c\) for \(0<z<1\). The exponent comes from an exact scale-invariant Poisson hierarchy of the most recent reversals, refining the previously known ballistic-ray limit.
