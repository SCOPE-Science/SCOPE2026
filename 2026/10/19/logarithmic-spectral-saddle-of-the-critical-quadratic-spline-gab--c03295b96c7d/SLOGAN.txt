For the critical unit-lattice Gabor operator generated by the centered quadratic cardinal B-spline, the complete spectral measure has an explicit one-dimensional integral formula and a unique interior logarithmic density-of-states singularity at \(s=1/4\), with sharp coefficient \(1/\pi\). Its lower spectral density tends to \(1/2\), while the upper spectral density diverges like \(1/(\pi\sqrt{1-s})\).
