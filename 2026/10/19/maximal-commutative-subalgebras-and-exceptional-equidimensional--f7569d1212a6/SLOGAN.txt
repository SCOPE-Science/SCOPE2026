Although a rectangular band has no commuting distinct elements, its finite-field semigroup algebra has exactly two pure maximal commutative subalgebras plus a projective family of mixed ones, all classified explicitly. Across every tuple arity, its reduced commuting variety has one to three geometric components, with equidimensionality confined to four exceptional parameter patterns (and the two-by-two one-component case), and its point counts have a uniform closed formula.
