On the unweighted Boolean hypercube of every odd dimension \(n=2r+1\), exactly \(\binom{n}{r}\) independently actuated vertices are necessary and sufficient for full Laplacian controllability, attained by driving every vertex of either middle Hamming-weight layer. The proof identifies nonzero harmonic Fourier-restriction coefficients on each Laplacian eigenspace, whereas in even dimensions no single complete Hamming-weight layer yields controllability.
