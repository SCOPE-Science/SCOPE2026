Two cospectral sixteen-vertex strongly regular graphs have opposite minimum-landmark mobility: the rook graph's metric bases form sixteen copies of \(K_3\square K_3\) under one-vertex exchange, whereas all forty-eight Shrikhande bases are frozen. Nevertheless, allowing two replacements at once connects the bases of both graphs with diameter three; equivalently, exactly two spare landmark slots suffice for resolution-preserving add/delete reconfiguration.
