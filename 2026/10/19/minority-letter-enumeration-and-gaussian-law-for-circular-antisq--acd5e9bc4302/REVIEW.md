# Scientific review — circular antisquare-avoiding binary words

## Correctness — PASS

The periodic-language reduction is supported by Baranwal et al., Theorem 11 and Proposition 18. The cyclic first-difference mapping is exactly two-to-one for every even-parity difference sequence. The allowed block grammar gives a strict minority and the isolated-short-gap classification. Marked cyclic composition counting gives an integer coefficient for each length and minority count; a log-determinant identity yields the stated rational series. Root-sum and singularity arguments then give the precise oscillatory asymptotic and Gaussian law. The packaged independent exhaustive check covers lengths through fifteen, the coefficient recurrence through nineteen and rational root/variance bounds. These finite checks support but do not replace the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with stated uncertainty

The closest primary work is Baranwal et al., *Antisquares and Critical Exponents*, arXiv:2209.09223, Theorem 11, Proposition 18 and Section 7. Its Pansiot run grammar is an explicit prior premise, and its Theorem 19 establishes a exponential-order estimate for linear words. It does not explicitly enumerate circular periodic words by length and minority multiplicity or establish their Gaussian minority fluctuations. The earlier paper arXiv:1904.09157 addresses a different simultaneous avoidance problem; arXiv:2209.09598 addresses avoiding factor/complement coexistence. Targeted published-finding corpus searches for cyclic good words, periodic antisquare avoidance, circular bivariate generating functions and minority distributions identified no equivalent theorem. An unindexed exact earlier refinement remains possible. This is not an independence or priority certification.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The exact bivariate enumeration and minority Gaussian law answer a natural constrained-language question in full for every length and composition. In addition to growth-rate information, they quantify feasible letter balance and periodic-orbit composition, with a sharp cutoff and the nontrivial twelve maximizers at one-third minority density. These are natural reconstruction and constrained-code parameters. They are not arbitrary single-length tables.

## Limits

Labeled periodic words only; not rotation-orbits or arbitrary finite linear words. The crucial minimal-antisquare classification and exceptional periodic obstruction are imported, explicitly cited results. Experimental verification is bounded; theorem proofs are supplied separately.
