Circular binary words containing no antisquare of order at least two have a unique minority letter whose occurrence gaps are \(2\) or at least \(4\), with no adjacent \(2\)-gaps. Their exact length/minority-count generating function is \(2z(1+4uz^3+6u^2z^5)/(1-z-uz^4-u^2z^6)\), and the minority proportion has asymptotically Gaussian fluctuations about \(0.1942540040\).
