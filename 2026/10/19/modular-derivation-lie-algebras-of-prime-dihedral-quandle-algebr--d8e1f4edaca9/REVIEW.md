# Scientific review: modular dihedral-quandle derivations

## Correctness

**PASS.** The group-algebra formula is checked on every original quandle basis product; the finite logarithm and exponential are legitimate in characteristic \(p\) because the truncation degree is strictly less than \(p\). The induced monomial product is homogeneous. Projecting Leibniz identities to each degree shift and testing the products with \(1\) eliminates all but two explicit families. The remaining recursion identifies every coefficient and rules out unexamined boundary shifts. Direct substitution verifies sufficiency. Differentiation of the explicit operators proves the bracket table and the asserted derived subalgebra. Separate original-basis matrix calculations check the dimensions for twelve primes and every proposed bracket for thirteen primes. The proof, not finite enumeration, establishes the statement over all odd primes and all characteristic-\(p\) fields.

## Originality

**PASS within the checked literature.** Elhamdadi et al. (2021, arXiv:2106.08289) derive a general linear system and a complete characteristic-zero classification, with modular tables through quandle size six, but not the uniform equal-prime-characteristic solution or its bracket law. Aader (2023, thesis hdl:10062/91201) repeats the characteristic-zero dihedral material and develops a different group-conjugation quandle family. Kaur–Singh (2023, arXiv:2303.06948) address module decompositions over \(\mathbb R\) or \(\mathbb C\), not characteristic \(p\) derivations. Previous work on modular idempotents shares the truncated logarithmic coordinates and arithmetic resonance parameter, but does not imply the complete derivation algebra: the Leibniz constraints and commutators are independent calculations. Searches of published mathematical records and targeted paper searches found no same-claim or stronger covering theorem. Residual risk: an unindexed modular derivation calculation or a nonstandard alias might contain an equivalent solution. The public record search is not a proof of universal uniqueness.

## Value

**PASS.** This solves the natural positive-characteristic gap in a paper devoted specifically to dihedral-quandle derivations: an exact basis and Lie bracket law, uniformly over all odd primes and coefficient fields of matching characteristic. The dimension shows a nontrivial arithmetic resonance, with the first jump at prime \(17\); the derived algebra and abelian ideal provide structural information beyond dimension counts. It distinguishes the modular family from the characteristic-zero vanishing theorem and permits direct computation of symmetry algebras in large examples without solving quadratic-size linear systems.
