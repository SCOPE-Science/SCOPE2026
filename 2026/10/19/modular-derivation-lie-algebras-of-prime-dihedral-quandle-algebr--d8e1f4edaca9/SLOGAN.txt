For every odd prime \(p\), the ordinary derivations of the dihedral quandle algebra over any characteristic-\(p\) field form an explicit \(2(p-1)/\operatorname{lcm}(2,\operatorname{ord}_p(2))\)-dimensional Lie algebra of truncated differential and multiplication operators. Its complete brackets explain the modular dimension jump from two to four at \(p=17\) and yield a canonical abelian ideal and one-dimensional abelianization.
