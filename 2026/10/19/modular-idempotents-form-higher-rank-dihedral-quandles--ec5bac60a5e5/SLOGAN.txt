For every odd prime \(p\) and every field \(K\) of characteristic \(p\), the nonzero idempotents of the dihedral quandle algebra \(K[R_p]\) themselves form a core quandle of vector-space dimension \((p-1)/|\langle-1,2\rangle|\). Over \(\mathbb F_q\) there are exactly \(1+q^{(p-1)/|\langle-1,2\rangle|}\) idempotents including zero, with higher-rank families appearing at \(p=17\) and for infinitely many primes.
