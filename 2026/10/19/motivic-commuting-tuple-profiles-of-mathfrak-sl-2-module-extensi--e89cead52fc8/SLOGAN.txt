For \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\ltimes\bigoplus_j\operatorname{Sym}^{m_j}(k^2)\) in characteristic zero or sufficiently large positive characteristic, the Grothendieck classes of every ordered commuting-tuple variety depend only on the module dimension, the number of irreducible summands, and the number with even highest weight. Two nonisomorphic, perfect, centerless ten-dimensional Lie algebras consequently have identical commuting-tuple point counts and zeta functions in every arity.
