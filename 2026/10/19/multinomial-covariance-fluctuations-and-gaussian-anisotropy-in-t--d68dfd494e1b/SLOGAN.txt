In every fixed dimension \(M\ge3\), the twisted baker map has correlated finite-time coordinate expansions whose Gaussian covariance nevertheless matches independent uniform coordinate sampling exactly. Its Euclidean Jacobian condition number has a nondegenerate \(\sqrt n\)-scale Gaussian-range law, and its mean logarithmic anisotropy has an explicit sharp constant (\(\sqrt{3/\pi}\log2\) in dimension three).
