At the exact rung-compensation point of the decorated Ising ladder, every four distinct apex spins have a strictly negative connected correlation equal to minus a crossed product of two apex pair correlations. The full zero-field apex fourth-cumulant density is an explicit negative rational function of \(\tanh(J/T)\) and \(\tanh(2G/T)\), with sharp strong-leg growth \(-3\tanh^4(2G/T)e^{6J/T}/8\).
