Over any finite commutative chain ring of length \(L\ge2\), the full all-arity pairwise-commuting count for an arrow-square-zero quiver algebra is an explicit sum over \(L\) nested graph-edge thresholds. The count depends only on the graphic matroid, vertex count, residue-field order, and ring length, so nonisomorphic rings such as \(\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z\) and \(\mathbb F_2[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)\) have identical commuting-tuple counts for every quiver and arity.
