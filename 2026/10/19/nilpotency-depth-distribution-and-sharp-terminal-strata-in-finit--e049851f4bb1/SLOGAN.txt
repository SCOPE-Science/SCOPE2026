For a finite-field Munn algebra with sandwich-matrix rank \(r\), every nilpotency-index count is an explicit Jordan-partition and Gaussian-rank sum, with exactly \(q^{mn-r}\) nilpotent elements in total. The maximal index is \(r\), \(r+1\), or \(r+2\) according to the two rectangular excess dimensions, and its stratum has a closed product formula.
