For every nilpotent matrix over \(\mathbb F_q\), the full nilpotency-index distribution of all its lifts to \(\mathbb F_q[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)\) is determined explicitly by ordered pairs of its Jordan-block sizes. If its largest Jordan block has multiplicity \(s\), the chance that a uniform lift has the maximal possible index is exactly \(1-q^{-s^2}\).
