# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

For increasing partial injections, nilpotency is equivalent to having no fixed point. The exact gap characterization from Sareeto--Koppitz partitions allowed successive graph pairs into three disjoint types and gives a reversible terminal-pair recurrence. The cumulative parity-index polynomials are related by finite inclusion--exclusion. Omitted-position monotonicity proves the constant-displacement theorem through defect four and the explicit defect-five example establishes sharpness. The attached checker directly enumerates the defining fence relations and agrees coefficientwise for sizes through nine; high-rank formulas and sharp examples are checked further through size twenty. Finite testing supports, but does not replace, the general proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual bibliographic risk

The full texts of Sareeto--Koppitz on generating ranks (arXiv:2208.03202), maximal ideal subsemigroups (arXiv:2403.06957), and presentations (DOI:10.15826/umj.2023.2.015) were compared at statement level. Their results supply related structure but not the nilpotent rank polynomial together with the defect-five threshold. Laradji--Umar (2015, DOI:10.1080/10236198.2015.1005080) treat a broader class of order-preserving or reversing subpermutations, whose total counts do not directly imply a simultaneous fence/parity-filtered enumeration. Their full theorem statements were inaccessible; a more general transfer enumeration remains a documented risk. Literature searches do not certify absolute priority.

## Value — PASS

Nilpotent rank strata of a natural finite inverse monoid are meaningful invariants. The complete polynomial-time recurrence for all sizes and the sharp transition between translation-only and nonuniform high-rank elements provide structural, rather than merely computational, information and pinpoint a natural boundary.
