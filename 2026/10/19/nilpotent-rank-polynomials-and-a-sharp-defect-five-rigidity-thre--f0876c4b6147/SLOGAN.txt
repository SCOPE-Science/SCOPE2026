The nilpotent rank polynomial of the order-, fence-, and parity-preserving partial-injection monoid has an explicit two-index polynomial-time recurrence. Every positive-rank nilpotent with domain complement at most four points has a single translation displacement, whereas nonuniform displacement first appears at defect five; the top nonzero rank coefficients are \(2\), \(2(n-2)\), and \((n-2)(n-3)+2\).
