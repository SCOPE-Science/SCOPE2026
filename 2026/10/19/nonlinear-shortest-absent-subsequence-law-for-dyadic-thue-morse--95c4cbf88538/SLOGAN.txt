The number of shortest absent scattered subsequences of the length-\(2^m\) Thue–Morse word obeys an exact nonlinear recurrence \(a_{j+2}=a_{j+1}(a_j^2+2)\), yielding counts \(3,5,12,74,2666,\ldots\). Its exponential growth per binary arch has the explicit constant \(\rho\approx1.5686647891503443\), strictly below the full binary rate \(2\).
