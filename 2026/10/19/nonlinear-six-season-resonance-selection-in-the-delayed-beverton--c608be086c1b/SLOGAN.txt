Vanishing six-season environmental forcing of order \(\lambda^{-1}\) produces a finite, nonconstant attracting response in a delayed Beverton--Holt map as its growth factor \(\lambda\to\infty\). The selected phase amplitude is the unique real solution of \(\tfrac23[\sinh q+\sinh(q/2)]=\eta\), giving exact nonlinear saturation of a sixth-root resonance.
