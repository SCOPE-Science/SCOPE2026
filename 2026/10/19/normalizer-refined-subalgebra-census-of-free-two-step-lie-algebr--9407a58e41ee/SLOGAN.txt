For the free class-two nilpotent Lie algebra on any number of generators over \(\mathbb F_q\), the normalizer-index distribution of every subalgebra with one-dimensional generator projection has an exact Gaussian-intersection formula. In the first six-dimensional case, a complete bivariate polynomial counts **all** subalgebras simultaneously by dimension and normalizer codimension, refining both the known ideal census and the ordinary subalgebra count.
