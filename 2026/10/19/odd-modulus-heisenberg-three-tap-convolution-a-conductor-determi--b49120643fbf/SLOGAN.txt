For every odd modulus, the weighted three-tap convolution \(aI+bL_X+cL_Y\) on the finite Heisenberg group has an exact conductor-factorized determinant and an equally explicit complex-kernel formula. With all three weights equal to one, invertibility holds exactly when \(3\nmid n\); otherwise the kernel has dimension \(2\sum_{m\mid n/3}m\varphi(m)\), including composite moduli.
