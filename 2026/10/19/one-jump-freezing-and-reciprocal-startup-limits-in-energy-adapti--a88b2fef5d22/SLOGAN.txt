For smooth energy-adaptive preconditioned descent, if the infinite-base-step first landing point is nonstationary, then every sufficiently large finite base step makes the *entire* trajectory converge to a nearby nonstationary point while the auxiliary energy decays to zero; the limit has an explicit first-order correction. On the strongly convex scalar quadratic, this limit approaches \(-2c/x_0\), with a rigorous explicit trapping range for a normalized instance.
