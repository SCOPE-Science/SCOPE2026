On an interval avoiding collisions the ordered arcs can be followed continuously, and all L digit values are constant on each moving arc. At a cut only one ceiling argument changes and the ceiling convention takes its left-hand limiting word. Intercept density gives precisely these words for the irrational rotation; for reduced rational denominator Q>L the cuts lie on the Q-grid and each arc's right endpoint realizes its word as a shift of the rational mechanical word. The cost is at most theta^(q-1) and the gain from digit deficit is greater than t/(2q^2), which wins for every fixed theta<1 and t>0 along unbounded lower convergents. The digit potential has sup norm and Lipschitz seminorm at most one, giving the stated arbitrarily small perturbation.
