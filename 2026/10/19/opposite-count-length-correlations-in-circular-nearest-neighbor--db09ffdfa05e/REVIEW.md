# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

Independent exponential gaps represent uniform circular spacings, with the normalizing gamma sum independent of the gap proportions. All local expectations, two-step joint integrals, variance sums, and mixed covariance contributions are explicitly evaluated in the proof. Exact ordered-exponential chamber calculations reproduce the first and second moments for sizes five through eight, independently from the local-integral derivation. The analytic argument covers every integer size at least five; finite enumeration alone is not treated as a proof.

## Originality — PASS with documented residual risk

Wade's 2006 public preprint (Theorems 2 and 3, including reciprocal-pair edge-weight correction) gives law-of-large-numbers limits, not the claimed finite-n covariance with a reciprocal-pair count or the finite-n reciprocal-length variance. Bahadır–Ceyhan's 2016 public preprint (Sections 2–5) gives unweighted counts and variances on an interval, not weighted circular mixed moments. Enns–Ehlers–Misi's 1999 study of neighbor-defined clusters is relevant but its complete text was unavailable. An existing isolated-vertex radius covariance in separate research implies the negative directed-length comparison, so that comparison is expressly not treated as new. Targeted mathematical-index searches for the weighted mixed statistic found no equivalent published mathematical statement among returned records, which is evidence of comparison rather than proof of global uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Reciprocal pairs are the two-cycles/components of a directed nearest-neighbor network. Exact weighting measures how much *distance* those components account for. The count is positively associated with the aggregate reciprocal-edge length yet negatively associated with overall directed edge length: two natural, nontrivially linked geometric functionals have opposite dependence signs. Both normalized correlations stay bounded away from zero, so this is a persistent structural distinction, not merely a tiny finite-sample correction. The new exact reciprocal-length variance and mixed covariance quantify the effect.

## Risks and restrictions

The geometry is the geodesic circle, not the line, with at least five independent uniform points. The inaccessible 1999 primary paper and possible implicit multivariate formulas in other literature remain originality risks. Independent assessment has not occurred.
