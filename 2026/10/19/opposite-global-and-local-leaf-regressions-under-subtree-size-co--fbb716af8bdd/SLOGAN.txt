In a uniform recursive tree, the expected number of leaves *within* a specified descendant subtree is nondecreasing in its size, while the expected number of leaves in the *entire* tree is nonincreasing, and strictly decreasing for every subtree root labeled \(k\ge3\) with nontrivial size support. The exact global size–leaf covariance is \(-\frac{n-k}{2(n-1)}\), opposite in sign to the within-subtree covariance whenever at least three subtree sizes are possible.
