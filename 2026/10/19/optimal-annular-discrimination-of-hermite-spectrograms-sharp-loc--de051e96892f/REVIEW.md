# Scientific review

**Correctness (C): PASS.** The STFT normalization gives the Gamma probability densities exactly, and the physical-area change of variables is \(dA=dt\) on a complete radial shell. The positive signed difference is strictly log-concave by the explicit second logarithmic derivative for every pair \(0\le n<m\), including \(n=0\). The equal-height equation has one solution for each positive area, and the superlevel/bathtub comparison proves uniqueness against arbitrary measurable masks. The stability proof quantifies two distinct boundary collars and both equal exchanged masses: its exact limiting coefficient is \(pq/[4(p+q)]\), with matching collar constructions. Stirling and the exact adjacent-degree Gamma ratio justify the two separate asymptotic regimes; only the Gaussian limits are used to pass from local root convergence to integral convergence. The included direct quadrature, finite difference and Gamma-tail tests are supplementary, not proofs of universal claims. Scientific limitations are stated in RESULT.md.

**Originality (O): PASS on available literature, with residual priority uncertainty.** Full HTML text of arXiv:2607.21590v1, full publisher text of Knutsen (2020), and the original Nicola–Tilli open-access article were inspected for the objects, signs, extremal region, stability and high-degree limits. Knutsen's §3.1 is the most closely relevant: it already uses prescribed rings and compares adjacent individual Hermite eigenvalues, but its Proposition 3.1 addresses the norm of a fixed ring, not the global optimization of a signed two-level diagonal contrast over all masks or sharp symmetric-difference stability. Ramos and Nicola–Tilli address the largest eigenvalue under varying signals, not signed fixed-level contrast; the existence of the classical bathtub argument is explicitly accounted for rather than treated as new. The 2023 stability preprint's abstract and available statements were inspected, but its detailed full text was not obtained; that access limit leaves a residual appendix-level risk, although its objective is different. Seven actual semantic database queries for Hermite two-level annulus, signed Gamma gap, related phase-space discrimination and high-degree crossover did not return a covering or dominating theorem. An unindexed equivalent calculation remains possible; search absence alone is not a novelty proof.

**Value (V): PASS.** The functional is a canonical measurable-design contrast for two fixed Hermite spectrograms; a priori, the optimizing support need not be connected, radially symmetric or bounded. The exact all-pair optimizer, uniqueness, sharp quantitative infinitesimal stability and nontrivial two-scale limit provide reproducible design benchmarks and delimit the level-separation scale at which finite-area masks retain nonvanishing discriminating contrast. The theorem goes beyond restating either individual eigenvalue formula or the generic rearrangement principle, while making no claim to global first-eigenvalue optimization or general signal discrimination. A possible application to physical mask design is interpretive, not asserted as independently verified engineering utility.
