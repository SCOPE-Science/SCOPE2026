For any two Hermite levels, the phase-space mask of prescribed area that maximizes their Gaussian spectrogram contrast is uniquely a centered annulus; the complete sharp infinitesimal symmetric-difference stability coefficient is \(g'(a)(-g'(b))/[4(g'(a)-g'(b))]\). In high degree the optimal contrast exhibits distinct Gaussian limits for fixed level gaps and gaps of order \(\sqrt n\).
