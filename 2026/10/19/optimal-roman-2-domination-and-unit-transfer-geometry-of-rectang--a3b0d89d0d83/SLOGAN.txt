The unrestricted unit-transfer graph of minimum Roman \(\{2\}\)-dominating functions on \(K_m\square K_n\), \(2\le m\le n\), is connected exactly when \(n>m\). For \(n>2m\) it is an \(m\)-fold Cartesian product of multiset-pair graphs, with an exact degree distribution and radius and diameter \(2m\).
