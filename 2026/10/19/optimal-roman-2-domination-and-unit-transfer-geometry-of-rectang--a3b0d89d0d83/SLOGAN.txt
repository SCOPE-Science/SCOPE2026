For the rectangular rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\), \(2\le m\le n\), minimum Roman \(\{2\}\)-dominating functions switch sharply at \(n=2m\) from onto column-to-row maps to independent row-weight-two configurations. Their single-unit-transfer graph is connected exactly when \(n>m\); for \(n>2m\) it is an \(m\)-fold Cartesian product of multiset-pair graphs, with exact degree spectrum and diameter \(2m\).
