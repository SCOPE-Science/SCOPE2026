The minimum two-neighbour percolating seed sets on \(Q_4\) and \(Q_5\) are mutually reachable by both token jumping and adjacent token sliding, and their jump graphs both have diameter four. Sliding is substantially harder: its diameter changes from six to eleven, while \(Q_5\) develops a unique 960-state central symmetry orbit and a nontrivial radius-nine/diameter-eleven metric.
