The Möbius–Kantor graph has exactly \(684\) minimum paired-dominating sets: a single connected token-jumping space of diameter \(8\), but no legal token slides at all. Moving adjacent guard pairs instead leaves \(54\) configurations frozen—precisely those inducing \(4K_2\)—and requiring three partnerships to remain intact splits the other \(630\) configurations into components of sizes \(432,168,30\).
