For every fixed alphabet of size \(q\ge3\), the repeated permutation word \((a_1\cdots a_q)^k\) has the exact length-refined scattered-palindrome generating function \(\sum_{k\ge0}F_{q,k}(t)z^k=qzt(1+zt)/((1-z)(1-zt^2(q-1+z)))\). Despite having only \(q\) different contiguous palindromes, it contains exponentially many distinct scattered palindromes as \(k\) increases, and its longest ones have length \(2k-1\).
