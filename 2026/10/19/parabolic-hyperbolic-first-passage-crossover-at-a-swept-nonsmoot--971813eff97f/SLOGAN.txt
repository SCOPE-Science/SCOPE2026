A slowly swept scalar nonsmooth fold has a sharp parabolic–hyperbolic first-passage crossover: if the repelling slope excess is \(\delta\sim\kappa\sqrt{\varepsilon}\), the threshold time is \(T_\kappa(L)/\sqrt{\varepsilon}\), with \(\int_0^{T_\kappa(L)}s e^{\kappa(T_\kappa(L)-s)}ds=L\). On the near-neutral hyperbolic side, the exact logarithmic correction is \(N\log(1+\delta)-\log(L\delta^2/\varepsilon)\to0\).
