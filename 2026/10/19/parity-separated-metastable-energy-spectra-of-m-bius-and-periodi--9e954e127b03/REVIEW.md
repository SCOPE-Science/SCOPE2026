# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The cubic-graph spin-flip inequality is exact: stability means that aligned edges form a matching. A fully specified 16-state transfer matrix counts blocked configurations and their aligned edges, with a row-swap closure distinguishing Möbius from periodic boundaries. Its two invariant-sector characteristic polynomials are checked by exact symbolic computation and yield the closed forms for all graph sizes. The parity and thermodynamic consequences follow by coefficient cancellation and strict Perron-root dominance. Exhaustive independent graph enumeration for all sizes from three through nine rungs checks the formulas. Limits: there is no assertion about dynamical occupation probabilities.

## Originality — PASS, with limitations
The strongest directly matching full-text source is Kalinin–Berloff (2022), which studies the same antiferromagnetic Möbius ladder but gives ground-state information and algorithmic hardness rather than the entire single-flip-blocked energy polynomial. Its ground-state degeneracy at even rung counts is known, and is not presented as new. Moreover, the even-rung periodic antiferromagnetic baseline is gauge-equivalent to a known ferromagnetic circular-ladder blocked-state enumeration, so it is not independently new. Neither antecedent covers the Möbius closure or energy-support comparison. Spirin–Krapivsky–Redner (2001) study freezing in ferromagnetic hypercubic lattices. Masui–Southern–Jacobs (1989) provide a broad recursive method for disordered strips; the full text was inaccessible, preventing an absolute exclusion of equivalent earlier coverage. Searches using the Möbius-ladder, locally maximal-cut, blocked-state and transfer-polynomial formulations revealed no decisive equivalent or dominating theorem, but failed searches are not proofs of novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The complete energy-resolved enumeration is a natural metastability invariant for a benchmark frustrated Ising topology, not an arbitrary parameter slice. It reveals an unusually strong boundary signature: the two uniform blocked-state energy distributions become mutually singular even though their entropies and leading energy moments agree. The all-size exact formula provides a benchmark beyond the known ground-state degeneracy for energy-landscape studies. No downstream physical-quench theorem is inferred.

## Remaining uncertainty
One older spin-glass strip paper was only available as an abstract. Independent scholarly assessment and experimental validation are absent. The result concerns the uniform blocked-state ensemble rather than the nonequilibrium distribution generated by a specific quench.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
