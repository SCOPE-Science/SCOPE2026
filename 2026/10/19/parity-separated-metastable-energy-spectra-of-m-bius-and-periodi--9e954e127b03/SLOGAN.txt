Antiferromagnetic Möbius and ordinary periodic Ising ladders have exact, different energy-resolved single-spin-flip metastability polynomials; their uniformly sampled blocked-state energies occupy complementary congruence classes modulo four, apart from at most two exceptional configurations. Thus their finite-energy laws become mutually singular even though their metastable entropies and typical aligned-bond densities converge to the same values.
