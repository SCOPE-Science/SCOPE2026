For Altmann--Deiml fixed-sweep second-order poroelasticity splitting, a scalar weak-flow mode has the exact parity-dependent stability frontier \(3\kappa^K<(2+\kappa)^{K-1}\) for odd \(K\), but only \(\kappa^K<(2+\kappa)^{K-1}\) for even \(K\), away from equality. In particular, two inner sweeps remain stable for all sufficiently small positive flow at couplings \(\kappa<2\), beyond the published sufficient ceiling \(\kappa<1\).
