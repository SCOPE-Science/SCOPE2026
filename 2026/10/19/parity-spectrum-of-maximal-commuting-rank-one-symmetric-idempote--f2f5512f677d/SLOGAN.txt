Commuting rank-one symmetric idempotents over a characteristic-two finite field form finite orthonormal-frame complexes whose inclusion-maximal cliques have exactly the sizes \(n,n-2,n-4,\ldots\) down to \(1\) or \(2\) according to parity. The complete clique polynomial and the number of maximal cliques at every size follow from explicit symplectic and nonalternating-isometry group orders; in odd dimension there is a unique isolated projection.
