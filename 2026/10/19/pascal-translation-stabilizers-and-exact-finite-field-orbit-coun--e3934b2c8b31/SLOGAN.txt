The canonical extremal weight-zero Rota–Baxter operator on \(M_n(K)\) has exactly the Pascal translation group \((K,+)\) as its inner stabilizer, in every characteristic. Over \(\mathbb F_q\), its two disjoint conjugacy and transpose-dual orbits contain exactly \(2|\mathrm{GL}_n(q)|/[q(q-1)]\) distinct operators; this is not a classification of all positive-characteristic extremal operators.
