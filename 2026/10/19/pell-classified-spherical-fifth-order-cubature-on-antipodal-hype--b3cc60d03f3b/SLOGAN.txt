The equal-weight antipodal orbit of centered \(k\)-subsets of \(n\) coordinates integrates every spherical polynomial through degree five exactly if and only if \(n(n+1)=6k(n-k)\). Every solution with \(n\ge4\) belongs to the Pell family \((n-1)+(n-2k)\sqrt3=(2+\sqrt3)^j\) for \(j\ge2\), beginning with the previously known 56-point design and extending to infinitely many higher-dimensional examples.
