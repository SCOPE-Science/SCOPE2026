For a degree-\(\ell\) spin-two field, a single equatorial ring of \(2\ell+1\) equally spaced samples loses the zonal mode at every odd degree and loses two more Fourier modes precisely at an infinite Pell sequence beginning \(\ell=8,49,288\). The complete sampling spectrum and image are explicit, and exactly as many sparse normal-derivative samples as the nullity always repair the lost information.
