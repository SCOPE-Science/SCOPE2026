Every strongly connected \(N\)-vertex \(0\)-\(1\) Markov graph with the minimum nontrivial edge budget \(N+1\) lies in the continuous-orbit-equivalence class of the full two-shift; its exact dynamical zeta spectrum is classified by two cycle lengths. The numbers of distinct zeta profiles at every dynamical period satisfy an explicit totient formula, and the mixing subfamily has exactly \(\frac12\sum_{k=1}^{N}\varphi(k)\) profiles.
