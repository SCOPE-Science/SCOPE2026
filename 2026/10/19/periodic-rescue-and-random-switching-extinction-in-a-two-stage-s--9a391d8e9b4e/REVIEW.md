# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. Each two-stage demographic product has an exact diagonal two-step form. Ergodic frequencies on both parities give the typical multiplier for every aperiodic Markov chain with \(0<q<1\); deterministic alternating products give the distinct endpoint value. The four-dimensional expected-state recursion is exact, and the explicitly factored characteristic polynomial identifies a Perron root crossing one at \(q=7/13\). The proof covers each boundary with the stated qualifications. The standalone symbolic and rational verifier succeeds, but the analytic ergodic and Perron arguments—not finite checks—prove the infinite-time claims.

## Originality

PASS, subject to named residual risks. Sanz (2019) analyzes two-stage stochastic population growth and develops general bounds; no covering exact Markov-persistence-independent growth law and rational mean/typical phase split was located in the examined full text. Chitour–Mazanti–Sigalotti (2018) supplies qualitative deterministic/probabilistic product-growth gap theory, which does not by itself determine this specific two-stage seasonal law or its exact expected-growth transition. Monmarché–Schreiber–Strickler (2024) addresses continuous-time positive flows. Indexed mathematical results and available historical claims were searched using season switching, antidiagonal matrices, semelparous stages, aliases, and exact thresholds. A general product-cocycle result could implicitly include some ingredients; inaccessible specialized Leslie literature remains a residual risk. Search noncoverage alone is not a proof of novelty.

## Value

PASS. A strictly positive pair of initial stage abundances experiences opposite persistence outcomes under two declining fixed environments, precise periodic alternation, and arbitrarily rarely disrupted alternation. The discontinuous edge law and the nontrivial open interval in which expected abundance grows while typical abundance vanishes are relevant to interpreting stochastic growth versus mean abundance for synchronized semelparous cohorts. They are quantitative structural findings, not an arbitrary matrix entry or a small table extension.

## Scope and limits

This is a density-independent mean-field projection model with strict semelparity and symmetric environmental transitions, not a theorem for primitive matrices, demographic shot noise, or general nonlinear ecological dynamics. Independent audit has not been performed.
