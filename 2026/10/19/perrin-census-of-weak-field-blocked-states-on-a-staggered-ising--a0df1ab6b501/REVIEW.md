# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The statement applies only to even periodic sizes \(n\ge4\), couplings \(J>0\), and strictly intermediate staggered fields \(0<h<2J\). The exact spin-flip energy \(\Delta_iH=2t_i(h-J(t_{i-1}+t_{i+1}))\) gives both forbidden patterns without any statistical approximation. Marking a defect and enumerating all length-two and length-three return gaps gives the stated fixed-\(k\) formula, including its range and endpoint values. A three-state automaton yields the Perrin recurrence by Cayley–Hamilton; the eigenvalue comparison establishes the thermodynamic exponent. Exact finite exhaustive enumeration, a separate transfer-matrix method and symbolic characteristic polynomials agree with the proof. The all-size theorem does not rest on finite experiments.

## Originality — PASS with residual bibliographic risk

The claim distinguishes weak-field one-flip *local minima* from the critical-field equilibrium ground states. The critical Lucas-number count of Castorene et al. is acknowledged as prior art, and is excluded from the new contribution. The constrained kinetic systems of De Smedt et al. have different local admissibility conditions; Yi and Baek use temporal, spatially homogeneous driving with a different tie-breaking dynamic. The relevant 1989 recursive study of spin glasses and random ferromagnets had an accessible abstract but not an accessible full paper. It could contain a more general method or an unlocated special-case formula; this is a genuine priority risk, not proof of absence. Database comparisons found no direct claim or logically dominating explicit all-size formula among retrieved findings.

## Value — PASS

The object is a standard kinetic Ising ring with a minimal spatially staggered perturbation. The exact blocked landscape—not merely equilibrium configurations—is a physically motivated quantity. The theorem gives a full finite-size, magnetization-resolved count and a nontrivial plastic-constant complexity throughout an open field interval. It quantifies the exponential abundance of local traps and separates weak-field metastability from the already-solved critical ground-state degeneracy. No dynamical trapping probabilities are inferred.

## Limits and literature

The accessible full papers by De Smedt et al. (2002), Yi and Baek (2015), and Castorene et al. (2025/2026) were checked against precise model assumptions and conclusions. The Masui–Southern–Jacobs 1989 abstract is a plausible overlapping source whose complete text could not be inspected. Searches are not an exhaustive novelty certificate.
