For every even staggered Ising ring and every weak field \(0<h<2J\), exactly \(\frac nk\binom{k}{n-2k}\) spin configurations are stable against all energy-lowering single-spin flips with \(k\) field-opposing spins. Their total is the Perrin number \(P_n\), with configurational entropy \(\log\rho\) for \(\rho^3=\rho+1\); this is distinct from the previously known Lucas degeneracy at the critical field.
