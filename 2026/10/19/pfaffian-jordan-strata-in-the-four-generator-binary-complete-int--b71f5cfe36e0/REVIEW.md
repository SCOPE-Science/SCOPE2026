# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The coefficient formula follows from mutually exclusive leading-degree cases. For a nonzero linear component, a local-algebra automorphism takes the element to a generator and gives multiplication rank eight. For nonzero quadratic leading part, the degree filtration gives rank \(2+r\), where \(r\) is the rank of a four-dimensional alternating form. Its Pfaffian determines rank two or four and the Gaussian count gives exact multiplicities. The cubic, quartic, and zero cases finish the partition. Counts sum to \(q^{15}\). The checker independently exhausts \(\mathbb F_2\) and the entire quadratic slice over \(\mathbb F_4\). No finite sample is substituted for the general proof.

## Originality

PASS within accessible comparisons. The closest primary work is Iarrobino--Macias Marques--McDaniel, arXiv:1802.07383, which provides general Jordan-type loci and treats nonhomogeneous elements, but does not give this specific four-generator characteristic-two Pfaffian rank distribution or exact rational-point census. Costa--Gondim, arXiv:1811.02072, treats characteristic-zero mixed-Hessian formulae and linear forms; Korhonen, arXiv:2101.01365, treats induced operators on symmetric and exterior squares; and Altafi--Iarrobino--Khatami, arXiv:1810.00716, treats two-variable linear-form types under different characteristic assumptions. Equivalent older calculations in the theory of exterior-algebra multiplication or modular regular representations remain a priority risk. Searching and comparison do not establish absolute novelty.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The regular representation of \((C_2)^4\) over a field of characteristic two provides a canonical Artinian complete intersection. Its radical elements all square to zero, so nilpotency index alone does not distinguish them; the full Jordan-type distribution does. Four generators are the first square-zero complete-intersection dimension in which a nonzero quadratic leading part splits by alternating-form rank, and the Pfaffian hypersurface explains this transition geometrically. The result gives both a uniform exact field-dependent census and a natural locus stratification; it is not a named parameter without mathematical motivation.

## Scientific limitations and residual risks

The claim is only for the four-generator regular module in characteristic two. Finite checks cover \(\mathbb F_2\), all quadratic elements of \(\mathbb F_4\), and a finite sample of its nonhomogeneous elements. The exact formula may exist in older literature using different terminology; no independent priority or proof assessment has occurred.
