The eigenline analysis resolves all finite characteristics. The closest published classification, de Seguins Pazzis's arXiv:2107.04273 (Theorem 1.2), reports the Wang–Wu/Botha existence criterion for unrestricted square-zero summands; it does not count additive fibers constrained to two nonzero rank-one summands. Hua's arXiv:2102.07290 concerns nilpotent tuple orbits under simultaneous conjugation, and Danchev–García–Gómez Lozano's arXiv:2102.10460 concerns potent-plus-square-zero sums. Searches under minimal-nilpotent orbit convolution, rank-one square-zero sums, characteristic-two scalar targets, and rank-two prescribed target fibers found no equivalent formula in inspected coverage. A prior two-transvection **product** class distribution has the same rank-one count as one case here but lacks the prescribed **additive** rank-two spectral multiplicities. Search noncoverage is not proof of priority: an older orbit-algebra formulation remains a specific risk.

## Value — PASS
The theorem computes a natural complete additive orbit-convolution invariant, rather than an arbitrary matrix slice. It gives every prescribed-target fiber throughout an infinite family, detects a sharp characteristic-two scalar hole, and distinguishes split and nonsplit two-dimensional spectra. Such fibers directly give the two-step sum distribution for a uniform random minimal nilpotent matrix; existing qualitative existence classifications do not determine these probabilities.
