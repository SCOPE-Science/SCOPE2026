In a uniform length-\(2q\) word in which each of \(q\) letters appears twice, the total number of contiguous Abelian-square occurrences converges to a Poisson distribution of mean one, at total-variation rate \(O(q^{-1})\). Longer Abelian squares occur with probability \(O(q^{-1})\), so the probability of avoiding every Abelian square tends to \(e^{-1}\).
