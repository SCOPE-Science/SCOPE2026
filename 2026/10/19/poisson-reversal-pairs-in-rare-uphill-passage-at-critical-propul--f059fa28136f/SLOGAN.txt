For a thermal run-and-tumble particle moving uphill against a force tuned within \(O(\sqrt D)\) of its propulsion speed, the total number of direction reversals **conditional on the rare uphill hit** converges to twice a Poisson random variable. In the same limit the first-passage time multiplied by \(\sqrt{rD}\) converges to an explicit deterministic constant, revealing a nontrivial reversal-pair mechanism behind successful escape.
