On the four-dimensional algebra \(K[x,y]/(x^2,y^2)\), all Poisson brackets are classified for every field; over \(\mathbb F_q\), there are exactly \(3q\) isomorphism types in characteristic two but \((q+1)/2\) in odd characteristic. In characteristic two a full family has a three-dimensional simple derived Lie algebra, except at a distinguished Heisenberg parameter.
