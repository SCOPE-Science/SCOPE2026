In finite-temperature diagonal completion, every nonzero skew-complex perturbation \(I_d+b_0J\) in even width generates a full-rank escaping orbit with \(\|W(t)\|_F\sim\sqrt d\,(2ct/N)^{N/2}\), completion loss asymptotic to a positive constant times \(t^{-2N}\), and a fixed normalized limit \(\operatorname{sgn}(b_0)J/\sqrt d\). This is an exact nongeneric family for every integer depth \(N\ge2\), with \(c=(N-1)(d-1)/(2\beta)\).
