# Scientific review — polynomial retract images of finite split square-zero algebras

## Correctness — PASS

The claim is over every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\) and every integer \(d\ge1\), with polynomial coefficients in the full split square-zero ring. The derivative evaluation identity follows exactly from \(V^2=0\). Chinese-remainder interpolation at all residue points prescribes the base and derivative functions independently, and Lagrange interpolation prescribes the nilpotent translations; this establishes that no extra polynomial-function constraint was overlooked. Composing the resulting affine fiber maps yields three necessary-and-sufficient idempotence equations. On a fixed residue fiber, the scalar idempotent is precisely zero or one, giving the singleton/full dichotomy. Unoccupied base fibers contribute exactly one mapping choice per target singleton and \(q^{d+1}\) per target full fiber. This proves the per-image, rank, and sharp-gap formulas for all parameters. All coefficient-polynomial cases at \((q,d)=(2,1),(2,2),(2,3),(3,1)\), and all affine representations for \((3,2)\), were checked by direct finite composition; the theorem is proved independently of the finite census.

## Originality — PASS within examined coverage

Al-Ezeh–Al-Maktry–Frisch, arXiv:1910.00238v1, and Al-Maktry, arXiv:2002.01304v1, derive and count the polynomial-function normal forms; this is an explicitly acknowledged predecessor, not a purported new lemma. Al-Maktry, arXiv:2010.00342v1, focuses on invertible polynomial functions and their groups. The inspected full texts do not give a classification of nonempty images of composition-idempotent functions, exact numbers onto each image, or the rank polynomial. Aichinger's paper on near-ring idempotents and polynomial functions on direct products of \(\Omega\)-groups, DOI:10.1017/S0013091599001418, was only accessible at abstract level: it is a residual overlap risk, not evidence for a covering formula. Searches for polynomial retractions, finite dual numbers, composition idempotents and rank refinements, including published research records, found no displayed equivalent. The search does not establish absolute novelty.

## Value — PASS

The theorem solves a complete naturally defined structural enumeration problem rather than a selected small case: it classifies every polynomial-retract image, every image fiber of the map from retractions to images, all possible image cardinalities, and the maximal proper retraction rank, uniformly in both the field order and radical dimension. The full/singleton fiber structure makes the image classification conceptually useful for composition-monoid and polynomial-function investigations. Its genuinely new target invariant is not implied by a bare count of polynomial functions or permutations.

## Limitations and sources

Primary: arXiv:2002.01304v1 (2020-02-03), primary MSC 13B25, with full text examined around the normal form and function enumeration. Comparators: arXiv:1910.00238v1 (full text); arXiv:2010.00342v1 (full text); Aichinger 2001 DOI:10.1017/S0013091599001418 (bibliographic abstract only). The conclusion is limited to split square-zero algebras, not general local rings or multivariate polynomial maps. Finite checks cannot replace the parameter-uniform proof. Earlier results on near-ring retracts may bear on originality.
