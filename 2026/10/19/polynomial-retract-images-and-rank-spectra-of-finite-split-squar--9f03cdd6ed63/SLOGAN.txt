For every split square-zero algebra \(A=\mathbb F_q\oplus\mathbb F_q^d\), the images of unary polynomial retractions are exactly the nonempty unions of residue fibers that are individually empty, singletons, or full fibers. Each such image with \(k\) occupied and \(s\) full fibers has exactly \((k-s+s q^{d+1})^{q-k}\) polynomial retractions onto it, giving a complete rank census and a sharp proper-rank gap of \(q^d-1\).
