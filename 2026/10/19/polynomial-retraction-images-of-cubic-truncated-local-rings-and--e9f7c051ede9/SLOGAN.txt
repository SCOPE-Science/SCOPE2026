Polynomial retractions of \(\mathbb F_q[t]/(t^3)\) have exactly the nonempty images formed by independently taking each residue fiber as empty, a singleton, or full; a specified image with \(k\) occupied fibers and \(s\) full ones occurs with multiplicity \((k-s+sH_q)^{q-k}\), where \(H_q=q^5\) except \(H_2=16\). This gives the complete image-rank polynomial and exhibits a genuine binary polynomial-jet exception beyond the square-zero case.
