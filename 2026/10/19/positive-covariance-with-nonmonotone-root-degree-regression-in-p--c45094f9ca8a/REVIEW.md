# Scientific assessment

**Correctness.** The two root-degree masses agree by the one-step degree recurrence and the common initialization at size 3. Tracking the leaf count restricted to those two degree classes yields an exact affine recurrence for their conditional mean difference. Its complementary difference telescopes to \(1/(2n-5)\). The maximum-degree star has a greater leaf count, so the strict drop is indeed part of a nonmonotone regression.

**Originality.** The earlier Resultary article *Oldest-hub degree has positive square-root covariance with leaf abundance*, equations (3)–(6), already gives the covariance for this seed and kernel. The finding is limited to the two conditional leaf means and their comparison. The earlier covariance statement and marginal moments do not determine these conditional means. The abstract of Mahmoud–Smythe–Szymański (1993) concerns related branch leaf counts; its full text was not inspected, so absolute priority is not certified.

**Value.** The exact drop concerns the two equally probable lowest root degrees at every size. It shows that the positive covariance does not correspond to monotone mean dependence on the distinguished root degree; the calculated drop even tends to one leaf as size increases.
