In the edge-seeded Barabási–Albert tree, root degree and total leaf count have strictly positive covariance for every \(n\ge4\), growing asymptotically like \(2\sqrt{n}/(3\sqrt\pi)\), even though the expected leaf count *decreases* when conditioning root degree from 1 to 2. The two lowest root degrees are equally probable, and the overall correlation decays as \(\sqrt{2/(\pi-2)}\,n^{-1/2}\).
