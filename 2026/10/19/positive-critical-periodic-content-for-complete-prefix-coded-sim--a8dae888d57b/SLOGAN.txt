For every strongly separated similarity system whose contraction ratios come from lengths of a complete finite \(q\)-ary prefix code, the critical periodic content is strictly positive, with \(\mathfrak P^s\ge(\delta/r)^s/L\) and \(s=\log q/\log(1/r)\). In particular, a concrete inhomogeneous planar three-map system of ratios \(1/2,1/4,1/4\) satisfies \(\mathfrak P^1\ge1\); the constructed periodic-orbit measures converge to normalized Hausdorff measure.
