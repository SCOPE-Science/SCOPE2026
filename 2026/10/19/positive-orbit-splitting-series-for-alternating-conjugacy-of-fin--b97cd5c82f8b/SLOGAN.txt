For every \(n\ge2\), an \(S_n\)-conjugacy class of endofunctions splits under \(A_n\) exactly when its rooted-tree, primitive-cycle, and repeated-component symmetries are all even. A positive recursively defined Euler product counts the splitting classes, beginning \(1,4,9,20,45,111,276,666\) for \(n=2,\ldots,9\), and explains the integer coefficients of a separate signed Burnside sum.
