Five classical cubic bipartite symmetric graphs have connected, self-centered minimum power-domination token-jumping graphs, but token sliding freezes every minimum configuration in the Heawood and Möbius–Kantor graphs while remaining connected for Pappus, Desargues and Tutte–Coxeter. The Tutte–Coxeter sliding graph has radius \(11\) and diameter \(13\), versus token-jumping diameter \(4\).
