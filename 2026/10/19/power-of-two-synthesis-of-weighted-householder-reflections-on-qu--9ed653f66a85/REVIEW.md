# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Direct normalization gives hub couplings -v_i/√2 for L>=2 and -v_i for L=1. The inter-layer couplings are scalar matrices, -I/2 in the interior and -I/√2 at the terminal edge. Splitting the arm coordinates into v and its orthogonal complement therefore gives a radial block and k-1 identical angular blocks. On the doubled path, unit even and odd pair vectors give these same blocks: the central coupling in the even sector adds by √2 and in the odd sector cancels. This works for L=1 as well (even coupling -1, odd block [1]). Taking the maximum of the two sector errors proves the norm equality independent of multiplicity. For i!=j the terminal kernel is v_i v_j(a-b), whereas the doubled-path end-to-end kernel is (a-b)/2 with the same terminal return amplitudes a,b. Every path eigenvector has nonzero endpoint weight by tridiagonal recurrence. A convex combination of signed unit phases can approach modulus one only if the individual phases coalesce; the term for eigenvalue zero has positive fixed phase, so every phase tends precisely to its reversal sign. This proves the endpoint-to-full-operator implication including exact equality, not just equality up to phase. The thesis theorem and published path classification support both iff claims. Independently, for M=2L the path eigenvalues are 1-cos(jπ/M), with reversal parity (-1)^j. For M a power of two, independence of 1 and cos(jπ/M), 1<=j<M/2, follows from the power basis of the primitive 2M-th root. Pair j with M-j in any integral eigenvalue relation: coefficients on each pair are equal and their parity contribution is even; the central index is even for M>=4 (M=2 is direct). The torus character criterion gives approximation. For an odd prime p dividing M, the two sets of angles generated by adding all multiples of 2π/p to 0 and to π/M each have cosine sum zero; folding each set back to j in [0,M] preserves parity, yields equal eigenvalue sums and opposite parity, and obstructs approximation. At M>=4 an exact time would have t an integer multiple of π by the eigenvalue 1, contradicting the irrational eigenvalue 1-cos(π/M). At L=1 exact recurrence also gives arbitrarily large positive realizing times, and when there is no exact hit the justifying sequence may be taken unbounded by compactness on bounded time intervals; time reversal permits positive times. The upper probability bound is the finite-time quantity referred to by attainment; attainment of a possibly smaller supremum is not decided. The phrase ‘approximate only’ in the limitations should be read as ‘if realizable, only approximately’ for L>=2. VERIFY.py and VERIFICATION.md were inspected, not executed; numerical examples are not an all-L check.

## originality

PASS

The retained contribution is the weight-independent all-time norm and amplitude reduction for weighted equal-length arms, with the full Householder target. The exact path-transfer arithmetic is known and is not credited as new.

## value

PASS

The exact two-sector identity reduces a coherent operation on every arm and depth, for any positive cross-arm strength profile, to a single path problem. Its norm equality controls all input states and the same reduction yields sharp weighted channel maxima, not only a simulation or a restatement of endpoint transfer. The cancellation of within-arm weight scales is useful in choosing a normalized quantum-walk multiport; no robustness or runtime claim is inferred.
