For finite equal-length quantum spiders with arbitrary positive arm strengths, normalized-Laplacian evolution approximates the conductance-weighted Householder reflection on every depth simultaneously if and only if arm length is a power of two. The operator-norm gate error is exactly that of path reversal on \(P_{2L+1}\) at every time, and the sharp cross-terminal transfer supremum is \(4w_iw_j/(\sum_kw_k)^2\); exact gate realization occurs only at arm length one.
