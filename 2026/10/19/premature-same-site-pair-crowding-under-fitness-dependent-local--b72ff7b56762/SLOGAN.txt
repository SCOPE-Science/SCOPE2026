In a fitness-dependent locally reset zero-range process, the stationary density of ordered particle pairs occupying the same site becomes singular already when the reset rate vanishes faster than the square root of the distance from maximal fitness, although the mean density stays finite until a stronger vanishing regime. The critical exponent is exactly \(\nu=1/2\), with a \(4C^{-2}\log(1/\zeta)\) threshold law and explicit power-law amplitudes above it.
