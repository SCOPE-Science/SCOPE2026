# Assessment of the two-scale superlacunary renewal-clock result

## Correctness — PASS
The pressure normalization follows directly from inducing on markers and applying the relative-entropy inequality. Uniqueness treats zero-marker ergodic states separately. The quantitative superlacunary bounds control both the unweighted pressure sum and the length-weighted tail required for size bias. The exact geometric-renewal Laplace transform has only one surviving microscopic contribution, from the immediately preceding allowed gap length; stationary Palm conditioning yields the random lattice phase. The factorial first-order correction follows by a verified factorial-series estimate. Numerical checks inspect independent finite instances, not the general theorem.

## Originality — PASS (provisional historical assessment)
Earlier general \(S\)-gap theorems establish unweighted entropy, intrinsic ergodicity, and symbolic topology. They do not state the singular penalty window or combined stationary two-scale clock law. Nearby geometric-gap pressure results assume bounded ratios, and the previously reported prime-gap renewal limit concerns a different gap-density regime with Poisson, not lattice, behavior. Relevant searches found no directly encompassing superlacunary crystallization theorem; a more general renewal theorem could imply it, and the search is not a uniqueness guarantee. The claim is scoped to the new combined limit rather than the classical pressure equation or size-bias principle.

## Value — PASS
This identifies a non-Poisson scaling mechanism for intermittent marker processes: a vanishing fraction of enormous return intervals accounts for almost all physical time. It quantifies both the macroscopic periodic clock and the nontrivial independent geometric jitter one scale below. The universal divergence-of-gap-ratios hypothesis explains the phenomenon beyond a single handpicked sequence; factorial gaps provide an explicit sharp correction. The result is a structural boundary case for equilibrium renewal dynamics, not just an arithmetic substitution into an entropy formula.

## Closest literature and limitations
Climenhaga–Thompson (2010) gives intrinsic ergodicity for \(S\)-gap shifts and explicitly uses even-factorial gaps to refute almost specification; Baker–Ghenciu (2014) gives the classical entropy relation; Ramirez–Somers (2025) addresses a broader family of unweighted entropy formulas. Published fixed-ratio geometric-gap results are structurally distinct. Excluded regimes: boundary layers in the window parameter, whole unfiltered marker point processes, and gap sequences without divergent successive ratios. A separate independent priority check remains appropriate.
