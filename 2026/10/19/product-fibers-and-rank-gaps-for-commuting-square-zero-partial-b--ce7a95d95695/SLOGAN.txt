For commuting square-zero partial bijections on an \(n\)-set, every nonzero product arrow forces an isolated four-vertex diamond, so the sharp product-rank ceiling is \(\lfloor n/4\rfloor\). Every prescribed square-zero product of rank \(r\) has exactly \((n-2r)!Z_{n-4r}/(n-4r)!\) ordered factorizations, where \(\sum_{m\ge0}Z_m z^m/m!=\exp(z+2z^2/(1-z))/(1-z^2)\).
