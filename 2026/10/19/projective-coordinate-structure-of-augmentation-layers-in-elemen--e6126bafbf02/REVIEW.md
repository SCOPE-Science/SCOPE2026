# Scientific review: elementary-abelian core-quandle augmentation algebra

## Correctness — PASS
The claim has a defined integral setting, all positive degrees, odd prime \(p\), and positive rank \(d\). Zhang's augmentation transport identifies the quandle powers with the ordinary \(I\)-adic powers; it also identifies the nonassociative transported product. The binomial relation \((1+t)^p=1\) yields the homogeneous projective-point relations after proving \(pt\in I^p\). Cyclotomic character evaluations on every nonzero dual vector identify the graded polynomial map with evaluation at projective rational points. The explicit normal monomials, together with stratified finite-field evaluation, prove injectivity; no guess about dimensions or unproved completeness assumption is needed. The induced action of \(-1\) and \(2\) on the associated graded yields the precise \((-1)^r2^s\) twist. The normal monomial basis proves the Hilbert function and sharp stabilization. Exact Smith-form and evaluation-rank calculations independently test specified finite configurations. Remaining correctness risks are ordinary human-proof errors; the finite tests cannot establish the infinite statement.

## Originality — PASS, with a specified bibliographic risk
The complete relevant portions of Zhang, *Group-ring methods for Alexander quandle rings* (arXiv:2610.08204, Sections 2 and 3), were inspected. Zhang proves group-ring transport and cyclic augmentation quotients but not the multivariate projective coordinate algebra, Hilbert series, or degree-twisted graded product claimed here. The additive-powers presentations of Tang (2001), Bak–Tang (2004), and quotient-basis theorem of Chang–Tang (2011) are serious related sources, so the current result does not claim that the numerical additive ranks were previously unknown. Their accessible abstracts and available citations describe additive presentations, not the precise canonical projective-coordinate identification plus quandle product. Their full texts were not accessible in this review; an equivalent implicit graded multiplication statement in those works is an explicit residual risk. Chang (2021) gives a general stabilization result without the present sharp degree or geometric algebra model. Searches for the theorem under projective coordinates, augmentation powers, normal bases, Alexander-core rings, and finite-projective evaluation recovered no stronger covering statement. Absence from an index alone is not proof of novelty; this review finds no identified dominating implication from the material actually accessible.

## Value — PASS
The quotient rank of a fixed small group is not the objective. The result provides the full infinite sequence of augmentation layers for every odd-prime elementary abelian core quandle, including every degree and every rank, as a recognizable projective-point coordinate algebra. It reconstructs exact multiplication rather than simply listing finite abelian groups and supplies a sharp stabilization threshold. This connects a natural filtration in quandle ring theory to finite projective geometry and reduced polynomial-function bases. The connection gives explicit homogeneous coordinates for classes and direct computational access to the nonassociative graded product. Its value is structural despite incomplete bibliographic certainty.
