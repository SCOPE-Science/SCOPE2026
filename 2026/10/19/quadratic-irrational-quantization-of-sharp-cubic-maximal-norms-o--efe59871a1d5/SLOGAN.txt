The sharp uniform cubic maximal norm of complete graphs has an irrational two-level occupancy, so no finite graph attains it. Its exact finite-size deficit is \(\left(1-\sqrt7/14\right)\|n\alpha\|_{\mathbb Z}^2/n^2\) to relative error \(O(n^{-1})\), producing genuine \(n^{-2}\) versus \(n^{-4}\) subsequence oscillations.
