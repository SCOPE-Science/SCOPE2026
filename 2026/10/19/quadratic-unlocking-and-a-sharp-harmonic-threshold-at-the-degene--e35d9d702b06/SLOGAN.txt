At the marginal half-turn resonance of a smooth two-harmonic circle family, the rotation number unlocks quadratically, with exact coefficient \(\frac{\pi|b|}{2}\sqrt{16a^2-b^2}\), precisely for \(0<|b|<4a\). At \(b=0\) and \(|b|\ge4a\), the family instead remains exactly locked at rotation number \(1/2\) near zero, revealing a sharp two-sided non-\(C^2\) response boundary.
