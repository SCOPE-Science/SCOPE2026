The previously known symmetric fastest-mixing weights on every wheel \(K_1\vee C_m\), \(m\ge4\), are in fact the **only** globally optimal weights even when each edge is allowed an independent transition probability. An explicit global spectral-to-edge stability estimate forces all spoke and rim weights to approach those unique values at root-mean-square rate \(O_m(\sqrt\varepsilon)\) whenever the SLEM exceeds its minimum by at most \(\varepsilon\).
