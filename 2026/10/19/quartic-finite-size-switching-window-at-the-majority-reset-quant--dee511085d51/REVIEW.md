# Scientific review: quartic finite-size switching window

**Correctness (PASS).** The exact binomial majority law is inherited from the published microscopic quantum-reset protocol, and the periodic exponential waiting-time average is derived directly. Subcritical Stirling and a geometric-tail argument yield the exact Bernoulli entropy exponent and prefactor; Gaussian Laplace analysis supplies the additional \(N^{-1/2}\) factor. At the critical tangency, the correct rescaled phase is \(N^{-1/4}\), and uniform binomial Chernoff domination permits passage to the exact Gaussian-CDF/Gamma profile. Above threshold, bounded convergence yields the explicit positive interval weight. The triangular-array binomial CLT with dominated convergence yields the entire \(a_N-1/2=\xi N^{-1/2}\) window. The standalone verifier passed 23 finite-size numerical and independent-integral assertions. No numerical test is substituted for the asymptotic proof.

**Originality (PASS in self-review, subject to priority risk).** Magoni, Carollo, Perfetto and Lesanovsky, arXiv:2202.12655 (2022), already supply Protocol II, the finite-ensemble binomial majority formula (Supplementary S4), the renewal integral (S3), and the qualitative finite-size slowdown. Their Gaussian-tail approximation (S10) does not provide the correct fixed-subcritical large-deviation exponent in general or the uniform quartic tangency analysis. The new claim is specifically the evaluated sharp three-regime event-switch asymptotics and full critical-window function, not discovery of the model or its exact finite-size formula. Public mathematical-record searches for this model, the quartic scaling, its Gamma constant, and equivalent transfer-kernel language did not identify a matching evaluated statement. Some related work on quantum-reset renewal processes and generic binomial scaling offers antecedent tools, not the same critical-window theorem. The most important residual risk is that another specialization of the source's exact finite-size formulas may already evaluate the asymptotic window; mathematical priority has not been independently assessed.

**Value (PASS).** The cited model's discontinuous infinite-population transition conceals a noncommuting finite-size metastability limit. The theorem fixes the physically meaningful reset-label flip observable, the entropy-correct exponential lifetime away from criticality, the sharply different power-law critical regime, and a universal window connecting them. This classification motivates when a finite spin ensemble may switch reset labels within a realistic number of reset opportunities. No general downstream theorem is asserted or needed for this precise finite-size result.

**Scope and limitations.** Only odd spin counts, noninteracting identical Rabi spins, strict-majority conditional all-up/all-down resetting, Poisson waiting times, and event-indexed switching are covered. Continuous physical-time relaxation, finite-sample inference, and general many-body dissipation remain unproved. The original finite-size majority formula is known, and independent priority verification is outstanding.
