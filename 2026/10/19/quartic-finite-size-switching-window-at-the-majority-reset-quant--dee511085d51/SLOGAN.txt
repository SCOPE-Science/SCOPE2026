In the Poissonian majority-reset protocol for noninteracting driven spins, the event-wise reset-label switch probability crosses from an entropy-controlled \(N^{-1}e^{-NI}\) law to an exact \(N^{-1/4}\) critical law, and then to a positive supercritical limit. The critical window is governed by the explicit non-Gaussian profile \(\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\Phi(2\xi-u^2)\,du\), quantifying finite-size metastability near the known quantum-reset transition.
