# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The two classes give an exact count chain with transition probabilities \((X-1)/(S-2)\) and \((Y-1)/(S-2)\). The gamma-ratio integrating factor is an exact martingale; its predictable quadratic variation and terminal barrier are derived from explicit state-dependent conditional moments. The proof controls both positive-density absorption and the exceptional cutoff at three remaining vertices. In the critical regime it proves the martingale Gaussian limit and bounds post-cutoff fluctuations before applying the continuous-mapping theorem. Independent exact-rational physical-path enumeration agrees with the count recurrence in nine finite graph instances; 99 drift/variance tests and 125 gamma identities pass. The finite checks do not certify the infinite theorems, which depend on the analytical argument.
