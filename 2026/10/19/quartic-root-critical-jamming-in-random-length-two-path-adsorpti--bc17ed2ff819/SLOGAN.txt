Uniform random adsorption of three-vertex paths on \(K_{a_N,b_N}\) leaves a macroscopic fraction \(|\delta|^{3/2}\) unmatched when the relative class imbalance tends to \(\delta\ne0\), even in graphs admitting perfect path packings. Within the \(\sqrt N\) imbalance window, the terminal vacancy count instead has a \(N^{1/4}\)-scale \(|Z+\lambda|^{3/2}\) limit, with \(Z\) standard normal.
