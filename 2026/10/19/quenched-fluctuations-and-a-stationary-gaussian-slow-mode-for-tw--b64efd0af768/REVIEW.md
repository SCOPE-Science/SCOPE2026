# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Positive bounded i.i.d. transition rates produce a unique stationary pullback invariant vector. The centered recurrence yields an exact finite-leakage second moment even when the two rates at a given time are correlated. Conditioning yields all lag covariances; a stationary coupling with constant-drift innovations has squared error of order \(\varepsilon^2\), and a bounded triangular-array central limit theorem proves the finite-dimensional stationary Ornstein–Uhlenbeck limit. Exact rational checks for distinct finite environment laws provide independent computational diagnostics, not an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS, with qualified historical priority.** González-Tokman and Peters introduced the random two-state model and an exact pullback representation in arXiv:2406.09672, Remark 5.3. That prior representation is fully acknowledged; it determines the random state pathwise and permits, but does not evaluate, the displayed covariance or Gaussian scaling limit. Their arXiv:2505.22996 considers jump and observable diffusion laws of particle dynamics, not the stationary random invariant vector. Broad random-affine/stochastic-approximation diffusion theories remain a possible source of equivalent generalized coverage. The displayed exact model-specific formulas and rigidity were not found in the inspected primary sources or semantic record search.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The sharp \(\sqrt\varepsilon\) spread and \(1/\varepsilon\) memory scale refine qualitative averaging in the smallest genuinely random metastable representation. The cancellation criterion distinguishes random environments with identical average rates but different quenched statistical behavior.

**Limits.** i.i.d. positive rates only; fixed driving distribution; finite-dimensional Gaussian limit only. This is a theorem for the induced Markov cocycle, not for interval-map random invariant densities. Further historical verification is appropriate.
