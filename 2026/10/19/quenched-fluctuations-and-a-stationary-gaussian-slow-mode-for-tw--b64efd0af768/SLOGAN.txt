In a two-state metastable Markov cocycle driven by i.i.d. random leakage rates, the quenched invariant state's variance and lag covariance have exact all-parameter formulas. Its centered weights have a stationary Ornstein–Uhlenbeck finite-dimensional limit at amplitude scale \(\sqrt\varepsilon\) and time scale \(1/\varepsilon\), and fluctuations vanish exactly when every environment has the same instantaneous equilibrium ratio.
