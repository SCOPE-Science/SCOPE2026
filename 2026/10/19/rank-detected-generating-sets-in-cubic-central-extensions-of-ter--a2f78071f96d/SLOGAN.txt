For every cubic central extension of a ternary midpoint quandle with nonzero alternating three-form, a tuple generates the entire nonmedial quandle exactly when its projected points affinely span the underlying vector space. Consequently the probability that \(k\) uniformly sampled elements generate a \(3^{d+1}\)-element member is exactly \(\prod_{j=0}^{d-1}(1-3^{j-k+1})\) for \(k\ge d+1\), independent of the cubic deformation.
