For an Ewens–Pitman partition, the mixed covariance between any weighted within-block edge count and each block-size frequency depends on the edge weights only through their sum, so the entire pair-by-size cross-covariance matrix has rank one. As block size increases, the covariance with any positive-total-weight graph score changes sign exactly once, at an explicit threshold that reduces to \((2n+\theta)/(\theta+2)\) for the Ewens law.
