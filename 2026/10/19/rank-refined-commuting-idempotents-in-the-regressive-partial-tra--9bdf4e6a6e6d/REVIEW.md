# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. Every idempotent has a largest-prior-fixed-point normal form on each defined nonfixed coordinate. The four local definedness cases yield exact commutation conditions. Explicit recurrences for the no-common prefix and post-common block are solved algebraically; the reset argument counts product rank without omission or overcount. Independent exhaustive finite enumerations through size seven and twelve-state recurrence checks through size sixteen match. The general result rests on the recurrence proof, not a finite inference.

## Originality
PASS, with limitations. Zhao–You–Xu–Hu treat this exact partial transformation semigroup, but the accessible statement establishes idempotent rank and generation, not commuting-pair product-rank enumeration. Fernandes's full article addresses partial injective order-preserving actions and semigroup pseudovarieties, not this rank-refined count. Total Catalan idempotent fibers and symmetric-inverse-monoid partial involutions have different hypotheses. Relevant mathematical-record searches did not reveal an equivalent formula; this is not conclusive uniqueness. The uninspected full text of Zhao et al. is a specific residual risk.

## Value
PASS. The full bivariate rational generating series, all fixed-rank layers and the closed zero-product formula quantify the commuting-idempotent structure of a natural idempotent-generated partial transformation semigroup. The renewal decomposition makes the calculation uniform in chain length and distinguishes partial-domain behavior from previously considered total or injective families. The result is a natural exact invariant, not a chosen small table.

## Scope and limits
The theorem gives aggregate product-rank fibers, not an individual-product formula. Verification by exhaustive finite enumeration is supporting evidence only. No independent external audit has been performed.
