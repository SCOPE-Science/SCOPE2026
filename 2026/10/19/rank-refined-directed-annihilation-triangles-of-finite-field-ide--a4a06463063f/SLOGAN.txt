Ordered directed zero-product triangles of idempotent finite-field matrices admit a complete Gaussian rank-triple formula: positive ranks \(r,s,t\) occur exactly when all three pairwise sums are at most the ambient dimension. The maximum total rank is \(\lfloor3n/2\rfloor\), and when \(n=2m\), every equal-rank extremal triangle has full-rank reverse products and exact multiplicity \({2m\brack m}_q q^{m^2}|\mathrm{GL}_m(\mathbb F_q)|\).
