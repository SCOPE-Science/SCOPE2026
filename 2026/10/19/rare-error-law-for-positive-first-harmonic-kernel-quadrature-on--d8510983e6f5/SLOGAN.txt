For an i.i.d. uniform angular pool, the best positive-weight quadrature rule for a two-dimensional centrally symmetric feature curve has a rare-error probability of exactly \(N/2^{N-1}\), while the conditional error is of order \(N^{-1}\) with an explicit geometric exponential-mixture limit. For anisotropic first-harmonic weights \(a,b>0\), the sharp unconditional expected squared worst-case error is \(\pi^2ab/(N2^N)(1+o(1))\), revealing a geometric-mean anisotropy prefactor.
