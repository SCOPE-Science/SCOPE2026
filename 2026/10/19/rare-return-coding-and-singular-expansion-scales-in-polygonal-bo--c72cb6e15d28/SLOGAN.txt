The Glendinning–Wong polygonal border-collision attractors have a four-excursion Markov coding with exact symbolic zeta \(1/(1-2z^{n+1}-2z^{n+2})\). Its entropy decays as \((\log4)/n\), while the two branch area factors approach their singular limits at radically different rates, \(D_R-1\asymp n^{-3}\) and \(|D_L|\asymp n^2\).
