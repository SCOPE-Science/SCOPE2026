For a uniform random permutation, a record at position \(i\) is negatively correlated with every earlier value-threshold indicator, positively correlated with the indicator at its own position, and independent of later values. Consequently total records and total excedances have an exact reciprocal-binomial covariance that is negative for \(2\le n\le4\), positive for every \(n\ge5\), and increases to \(1/2\).
