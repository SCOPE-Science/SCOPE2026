The regular elements of every finite partition-preserving transformation monoid admit an exact integer-counting formula, which specializes for uniform blocks to a finite-exponential generating function. For each fixed block size \(n\geq2\), their proportion decreases at a rigorously determined exponential rate \(\rho_n^m\), with \(0<\rho_n<1\), as the number of blocks \(m\) increases.
