For a cubic amplitude-dependent planar rotation, nonlinear RK325 has a unique nonstationary radius where generic sixth-power energy drift cancels through eighth power, leaving a positive tenth-power defect. Starting at that radius, the rescaled error over \(n\asymp h^{-8}\) steps converges to an explicit Riccati law up to its finite scaled-time pole, which is not a claim of discrete blow-up.
