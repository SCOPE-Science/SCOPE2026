# Self-review

## Correctness

PASS. The stationary stochastic convolution exists since the deterministic propagator is an exponentially contracting rotation. Conditional Gaussianity gives the exact product stationary law, and sign coupling plus contraction gives uniqueness. The stationary probability currents and jump detailed balance yield the specified *fixed-label* path-relative-entropy rate. The explicit conjugacy \(L^*=TLT\) gives all-order position reversal symmetry when switching is balanced; a nonzero oriented stochastic area proves the converse. The telegraph two-state characteristic ODE yields the covariance and non-Markov contradiction. Conditional Wick calculations show a positive off-axis squared-moment covariance when ordinary off-axis covariance is zero. The autonomous squared-radius generator proves the full radial-law equivalence. The standalone checker corroborates these calculations but does not substitute for the proof.

## Originality

PASS for the precise combined multiplicative-vortex theorem, subject to independent priority assessment. The fixed-chirality Brownian-vortex model and its entropy formula were established by Di Terlizzi, Baiesi and Ritort (arXiv:2403.10442, Section 5). Cerasoli et al. (arXiv:2201.04903) consider nonequilibrium gyrators with spectral diagnostics, not stochastic torque sign changes. Critically, Knight, Kaveh and Pruessner (arXiv:2507.22199) already give a reversible observed path and positive hidden entropy for **additive symmetric telegraph propulsion**: the general hidden-irreversibility idea is therefore covered and is not claimed. The present theorem treats a distinct multiplicative planar solenoidal drift with a sharp necessary-and-sufficient switching boundary, non-Gaussian reversible two-time measurements, a complete equilibrium radial alias, and an exact rate constant; these statements are not shown or implied by the additive model's restricted dynamics. Broader structural symmetry theorems may nevertheless imply portions, and full priority examination remains a risk.

## Scientific value

PASS. The theorem supplies an exact boundary between observable path irreversibility and strict invisibility for a nonconservative stochastically switched vortex, rather than relying on zero mean current. Its contrast between all-order path reversibility, non-Markov temporal memory, non-Gaussian two-time statistics, and an exactly equilibrium radial path is physically informative for fluctuation-based inference. The fixed-label entropy rate remains nonzero even at high switching rates, while the visible law approaches equilibrium; with a calibrated model, time-symmetric covariance curvature can nevertheless recover the torque magnitude. No claim about arbitrary nonlinear settings or physical parity of the switching variable is made.

## Scope and residual risks

The proof uses finite strictly positive rates, isotropic thermal noise, time-even hidden labels for joint path reversal and harmonic radial confinement. Results about hidden-entropy principles in other active particle models are expressly prior art. A possible general operator-conjugacy result and a fuller search across stochastic torque literature could reduce the priority scope. No independent audit or empirical implementation has been carried out.
