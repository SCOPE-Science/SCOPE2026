A harmonic Brownian vortex with randomly reversing chirality has a stationary planar trajectory that is exactly time reversible at every order **if and only if** its two switching rates agree, although the fully observed sign-and-position process has positive fixed-label reversal entropy rate \(2\omega^2/k\). Its radius has precisely the equilibrium Ornstein–Uhlenbeck path law, while its planar trajectory is non-Markov and has a strictly non-Gaussian two-time statistic.
