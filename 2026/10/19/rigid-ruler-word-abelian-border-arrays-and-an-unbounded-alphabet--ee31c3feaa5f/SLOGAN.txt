The length-\(n\) prefix of the ruler word \(r_i=\nu_2(i)\) is uniquely recoverable, up to letter renaming, from its longest Abelian-border array \(\pi(i)=i-2^{\nu_2(i)}\), even when competing words may use arbitrary alphabets. This explicit infinite family forces at least \(1+\lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor\) distinct symbols, so no fixed alphabet realizes every valid Abelian-border array.
