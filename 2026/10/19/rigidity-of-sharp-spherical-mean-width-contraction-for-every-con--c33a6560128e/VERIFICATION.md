# Verification and limits

The universal argument is analytic. Its critical checks are: strict concavity of the cap moment function, equality propagation through the rearrangement and Stieltjes integral, nonvanishing of every nonconstant spherical-harmonic cap multiplier at some radius charged by a nonatomic nonzero Stieltjes measure, uniqueness of spherical-cap bathtub selectors, and local-to-global rigidity of maps pulling back equal-radius caps to equal-radius caps. The support-function and rearrangement inequalities are established in Zhong–Kalaj, arXiv:2609.09316.

Run `python3 verify_examples.py` to compare eighteen circular-cap Fourier multipliers with quadrature and the predicted hemisphere-step equality for a nonorthogonal map. These checks are finite numerical corroboration and **not** proofs of the universal theorem. The general result does not cover discontinuous kernels.

Independent scientific validation has not been performed.
