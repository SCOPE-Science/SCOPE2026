For odd primes \(p\), the \(p^4\) natural inner Heisenberg skew braces have precisely \(p^2\) liftable isomorphism types and \(p\) nonliftable types under Rota–Baxter realization, with exact lift multiplicities determined by trace and determinant. Among the \(p^6+p^4\) Rota–Baxter maps, exactly \(p^6-3p^5+4p^3\) have both the map and its dual bijective, a proportion tending to one.
