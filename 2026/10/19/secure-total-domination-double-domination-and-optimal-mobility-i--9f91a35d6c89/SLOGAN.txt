For the \(13\)- and \(17\)-vertex Paley graphs, the full secure-total domination polynomial equals the closed-neighborhood double-domination polynomial, and both minimum numbers equal \(5\). Yet optimal secure-total configurations move freely in a connected sliding graph at order \(13\), while at order \(17\) even unrestricted one-token jumps leave exactly \(68\) isolated pairs of configurations.
