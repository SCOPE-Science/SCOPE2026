The length-three tetrahedral longest-edge-bisection rotation from Adiprasito--Kalmanovich--Solomon remains admissible on every ellipse \(Q(x,y)=4x^2+6xy+4y^2=q\) exactly through the sharp level \(q=63/32\), not merely on the paper's sample level \(1/16\). Beyond this threshold, every irrational formal orbit eventually violates the prescribed longest-edge choice, and for \(63/32<q\le2\) the density of illegal blocks is exactly \(\pi^{-1}\arccos\sqrt{(63/32)/q}\), with a square-root onset.
