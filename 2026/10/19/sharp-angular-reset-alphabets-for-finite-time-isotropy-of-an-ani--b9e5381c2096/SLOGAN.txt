For an anisotropic Brownian colloid with random absolute-orientation resetting, exact isotropy of every spatial moment through order \(2m\) at all positive times requires and is implied by the first \(m\) angular reset harmonics vanishing; the unique smallest reset alphabet consists of \(m+1\) equally weighted regular unoriented axes. Three axes erase all directional differences through fourth order while preserving a strictly positive isotropic fourth cumulant and an explicit sixth-order directional signature.
