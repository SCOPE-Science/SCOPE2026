In a finite half-filled exclusion ring whose entire configuration resets at independent Pareto times, the stationary first density Fourier mode has time-integrated variance growing as \(T^{3-\alpha}\) for \(1<\alpha<2\), as \(T\log T\) at \(\alpha=2\), and linearly for \(\alpha>2\). The exact leading coefficients show that renewal-clock memory alone defeats ordinary time-average self-averaging, even though the underlying finite exclusion process has a positive spectral gap.
