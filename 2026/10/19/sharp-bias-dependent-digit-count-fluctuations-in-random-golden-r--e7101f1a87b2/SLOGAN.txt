For stationary biased random golden-ratio expansions, the digit-count asymptotic variance is exactly \(V(q)=\frac{pr}{(1+p)^3}+\frac{r^2(p^2+5p+2)}{p(1+p)^3}q(1-q)\), where \(p=\beta^{-2}\) and \(r=\beta^{-1}\). Its unique maximum at fair switch choices is over five times the limiting deterministic-choice variance, even though the times between switch visits obey the same geometric renewal law at every bias.
