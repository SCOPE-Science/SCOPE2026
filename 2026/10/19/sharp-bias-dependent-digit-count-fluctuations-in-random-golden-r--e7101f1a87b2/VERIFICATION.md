# Reproducible mathematical verification

Run `python3 verify.py` in the extracted directory. It needs only Python 3's standard library. The checker computes in the exact quadratic number field generated by \(p=(3-\sqrt5)/2\), verifying the stationary Markov distribution and every displayed mean/variance identity at five rational switching biases. It tests exact excursion probabilities for lengths \(2\) through \(14\). An independent spectral route computes the dominant root of a tilted transition matrix for five values of the tilt at each bias, confirms the equivalent renewal equation, and numerically compares first and second derivatives to the evaluated mean and variance. Finally it checks positivity and strict maximization at the fair switching probability.

These checks support the algebra and the dynamical interpretation. They do not certify originality, prove an infinite-time central limit theorem by finite experiment, or constitute an independent audit. The analytical proof in `RESULT.md` supplies the necessary infinite-step argument.
