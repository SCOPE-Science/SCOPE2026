# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact identity \(\dot Y=\beta(a-u)Y-\beta b\) for \(Y=N^\beta\) is reconstructed directly, with \(a,b,\beta>0\), measurable \(0\le u\le U\) and \(N_0>N_c\). The integrating factor yields a precise expression for all positive trajectories. Front-loaded cumulative effort pointwise dominates every other admissible cumulative schedule. At fixed total effort the difference of final powered populations is a strictly positive weighted integral except when schedules coincide almost everywhere. The maximal-control autonomous solution reaches the threshold exactly when \(U>a-b/N_0^\beta\), and its time and budget formulas follow algebraically including \(U=a\). Comparison at every time excludes threshold access under any cheaper schedule, even with later interventions. The decreasing minimum-effort frontier and its singular/impulse limits follow from an exact integral. The argument is valid until reaching the reference-domain boundary; the target is strictly above that boundary.

The packaged standalone finite checks passed. Their rational identities, sample policies and quadrature do not establish the infinite schedule theorem; the proof does.

## Originality — PASS, with specified residual risks

Crawley and Tanaka (arXiv:2610.12436v1) state the uncontrolled strong-Allee threshold and propose population pacing but do not formulate or prove fixed-budget proportional-loss intervention. Cid, Hilker and Liz (2014), DOI 10.1016/j.mbs.2013.12.003, address discrete within-season impulse harvesting in an Allee population; Grey, Lenhart, Hilker and Franco (2021), DOI 10.1111/nrm.12321, optimize yield and seasonal harvest timing in different discrete maps. Their full accessible texts were compared materially, including Cid's Proposition 4.1 and Grey's Sections 2–4. Lu, Liu and Li (2023), DOI 10.3934/math.20231481, consider profit-optimal control in a two-species system; accessible information was narrower and remains an access limitation. None of the checked statements proves the exact all-schedules ordering together with the controlled collaboration-law cap and budget frontier. Scalar integrating factors and bang-bang principles are established mathematical tools, not asserted as new techniques. A possibly unindexed general control theorem remains an originality risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The source's natural unanswered containment question receives an exact necessary-and-sufficient intervention-cap threshold, optimal immediate schedule under every finite integrated effort budget, explicit critical cost, and sharp logarithmic blowup near the required cap. It distinguishes integrated dose from peak delivery capacity and is not a routine numerical parameter slice.

## Scientific limitations

The controlled loss intensity is a specified mathematical extension of the original model, not a measured policy effect. Conclusions require the continuous deterministic population law, positive per-capita parameters, and states in the published power-law probability's calibration range. Extinction at low population sizes, stochastic persistence, model calibration, monetary intervention costs and congestion are not proved. Independent verification has not been conducted.
