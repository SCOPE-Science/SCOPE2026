For two cliques of orders \(p>2q\) joined by one bridge, maximizing weighted-Laplacian algebraic connectivity under a unit-cost conductance budget has a sharp structural transition at \(p(p-1)=2(2q-1)^2\). At or below that threshold the unique optimum discards all internal nonhub chords and has exact gap \(B/(p+4q-4)\); above it every optimum must invest positive conductance in a nonhub chord of the larger clique and achieves strictly more than that benchmark.
