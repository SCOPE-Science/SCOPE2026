For a competitive Lotka–Volterra graph consisting of a universal hub and arbitrary disjoint peripheral cliques, the hub survives exactly below the unique threshold \(\tau\sum_j q_j/[1+\tau(q_j-1)]=1\); every clique merger strictly raises that threshold. At equality the hub has the universal critical law \(t h(t)\to1/(1-\tau)\), while all peripheral abundances approach explicit positive limits with matching inverse-time corrections.
