For a Hermitian positive-definite input of spectral ratio \(\kappa\), a joint trial/image squared-norm distortion \(\varepsilon\) below the known right-half-plane threshold bounds every ordinary sketched Ritz argument by the sharp angle \(\arcsin(\varepsilon(\kappa-1)/(2\sqrt\kappa\sqrt{1-\varepsilon^2}))\), attained even by a complex \(2\times3\) sketch.
