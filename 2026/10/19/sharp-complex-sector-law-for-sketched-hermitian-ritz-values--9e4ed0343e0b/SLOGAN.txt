For a Hermitian positive-definite matrix of condition ratio \(\kappa\), every ordinary sketched Ritz value lies in the sharp sector \(|\arg\lambda|\le\arcsin(\varepsilon(\kappa-1)/(2\sqrt\kappa\sqrt{1-\varepsilon^2}))\) whenever the joint trial/image space is embedded with squared-norm distortion \(\varepsilon<2\sqrt\kappa/(\kappa+1)\). Two-mode complex sketches attain the angle, and at the exact distortion threshold a real sketch can produce zero or negative Ritz values.
