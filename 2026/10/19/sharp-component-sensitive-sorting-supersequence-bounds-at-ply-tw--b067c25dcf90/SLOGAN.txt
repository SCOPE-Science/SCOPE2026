Any family of \(n\) distinctly labelled intervals with ply at most two and \(c\) overlap components admits a sorting-supersequence of length at most \(2n-c\), and this universal worst-case bound is sharp for every \(1\le c\le n\). More generally, every width-two poset \(P\) has a constructible length-\(n+i(P)\) word containing all its linear extensions as subsequences, where \(i(P)\) counts incomparable pairs.
