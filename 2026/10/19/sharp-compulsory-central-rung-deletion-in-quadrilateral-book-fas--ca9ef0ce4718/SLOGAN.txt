On the quadrilateral-book graph \(K_{1,m}\square K_2\), the fastest symmetric Markov chain must use the common edge for two pages but must delete it for every \(m\ge3\), regardless of whether edge weights are assumed symmetric under graph automorphisms. The exact optimal contraction is \(2-\sqrt2\) for two pages and \(1-1/m\) thereafter, with an explicit interval of optimal peripheral-rung probabilities.
