For \(n\) separated positive rectangular pulses, the first-order analytic Cauchy wavelet has the maximal \(n-1\) upper-half-plane zeros exactly on an explicit nonempty open convex cone of amplitudes. For two pulses of lengths \(\ell_1,\ell_2\), gap \(g\), and amplitude ratio \(r\), the sharp transition is \(g(g+\ell_1+\ell_2)=\ell_1\ell_2\sinh^2(\tfrac12\log r)\), where a pair of nonreal conjugate zeros coalesces into a double real zero.
