For the first diagonal-proportional randomly pivoted Cholesky step, the trace gain is always a conditional expectation of a size-biased eigenvalue: its distribution lies sharply between the deterministic spectral mean and the full size-biased spectral law in convex order. Both endpoints are attained by real orthogonal changes of basis with identical spectra, so the one-step trace-error variance ranges exactly from zero to \(\sum_j\lambda_j^3/\sum_j\lambda_j-(\sum_j\lambda_j^2/\sum_j\lambda_j)^2\) despite identical expected errors.
