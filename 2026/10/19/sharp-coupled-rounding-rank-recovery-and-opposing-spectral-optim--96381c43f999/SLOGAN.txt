In independent-row binary stochastic rounding of an all-equal rank-one two-column matrix, allowing dependent rounding within each row makes \(p=2/3\) with equally likely nonzero row patterns the unique design maximizing the exact full-rank probability \(1-3^{1-n}\). The different choice \(p=1/2\) with perfectly anticorrelated columns uniquely maximizes the asymptotic smallest singular value, exhibiting a sharp input-phase rank-recovery versus conditioning tradeoff (for fixed \(p\), both criteria favor maximal anticorrelation).
