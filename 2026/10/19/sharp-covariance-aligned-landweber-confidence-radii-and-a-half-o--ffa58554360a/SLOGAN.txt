For source-ball Landweber reconstructions with exact Gaussian covariance, the smallest uniformly valid covariance-aligned confidence ellipsoid has a noncentral chi-square radius determined by a sharp spectral bias-to-noise ratio. As a normal-matrix eigenvalue tends to zero, its radius has a sharp smoothness transition at \(
u=1/2\): above the threshold it stays bounded even though the conventional normwise bias-inflated radius diverges.
