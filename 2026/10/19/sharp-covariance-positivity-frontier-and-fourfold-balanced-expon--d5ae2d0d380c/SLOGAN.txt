For stable symmetric differential Lyapunov equations with constant positive-semidefinite forcing, symmetrized METD1 can lose covariance positivity at a sharp correlation-dependent step, whereas a square-root-balanced forcing always preserves positivity. The balanced update is second order on this model, improves every finite-step Frobenius error relative to symmetrization, and divides its leading error by exactly four whenever forcing and drift do not commute.
