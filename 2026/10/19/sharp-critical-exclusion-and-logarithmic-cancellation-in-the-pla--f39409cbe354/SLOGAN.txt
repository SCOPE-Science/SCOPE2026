At the transcritical competitive-exclusion boundary of the planar Ricker map, every positive trajectory has a sharp reciprocal extinction law \(1/y_n=(1-\alpha\beta)n+[(1-\alpha\beta)/2-\alpha\beta/r]\log n+C+o(1)\). The universal logarithmic correction changes sign and vanishes on the explicit surface \(r=2\alpha\beta/(1-\alpha\beta)\), where the reciprocal population has a finite affine offset.
