For the unit-coupling, zero-detuning six-site chiral triangle-with-pendants router at flux magnitude \(\pi/2\), transfer from any individual internal vertex to any individual pendant vertex has the same exact probability supremum \((23+8\sqrt7)/63\approx0.701048\). The ceiling is approached arbitrarily closely but is never attained at finite time, sharply contrasting with the network's known near-perfect same-layer routing.
