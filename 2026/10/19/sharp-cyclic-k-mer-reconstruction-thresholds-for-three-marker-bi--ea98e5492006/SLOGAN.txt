For a binary circular word with exactly three ones and sorted zero gaps \(a\le b\le c\), the smallest multiplicity-preserving oriented read length that uniquely identifies the cycle is exactly \(a+b+3\) when the gaps are all distinct; repeated gaps have the sharp shorter thresholds \(a+1\), \(a+2\), or \(b+1\) according to their equalities. The proof combines a reversal obstruction with exact gap-multiplicity reconstruction, uniformly at every length.
