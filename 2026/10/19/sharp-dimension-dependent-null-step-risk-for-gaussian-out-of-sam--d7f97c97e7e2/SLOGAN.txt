For Gaussian noninterpolating gradients, projected out-of-sample hypergradient scaling learns a Student-ratio stepsize whose distribution is unchanged by minibatch size. Even with the validation null step, its exact two-update risk at the population optimum has three sharp small-learning-rate regimes: \(\eta\) in dimension one, \(\eta^2\log(1/\eta)\) in dimension two, and \(\eta^2\) from dimension three onward.
