For a normalized pure state supported on an additive Sidon set of size \(m\) over an odd-prime vector space, the discrete Wigner absolute mass has a sharp bound \(1+(m-1)/(p\sin(\pi/(2p)))\), with exact phase-quantized extremizers and a nonnegative amplitude-and-phase deficit. Uniform states on quadratic Sidon graphs attain this bound across unbounded dimensions and reach a fixed positive fraction of the universal \(\sqrt N\) limit.
