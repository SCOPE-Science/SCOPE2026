For instantaneous Poisson capture of a freely diffusing particle by a periodic lattice, effective diffusivity grows strictly with the asymmetry of the capture basins at fixed rate and lattice spacing. Its exact sharp range runs from \(D z^2\cosh(z/2)/(4\sinh^2(z/2))\) for nearest-site capture to \(D z^2\coth^2(z/2)/4\) for maximally directed capture, where \(z=L\sqrt{r/D}\).
