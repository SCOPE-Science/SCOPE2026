For an active Brownian swimmer with randomized absolute-orientation resets, every attainable diffusion tensor at fixed mean velocity lies in an explicit disk, and every point in that disk is realized by at most four reset angles. Exactly isotropic centered fluctuations can coexist with nonzero directed motion at every observation time, up to a sharp drift threshold whose uniquely optimal reset protocol switches from symmetric angles to antipodal angles as the reset-to-rotation rate crosses \(1/2\).
