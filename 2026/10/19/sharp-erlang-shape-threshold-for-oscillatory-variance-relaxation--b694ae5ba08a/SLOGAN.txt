For harmonic Ornstein–Uhlenbeck diffusion restarted at integer-shape Erlang renewal times, stationary variance is crossed infinitely often exactly when the Erlang shape is at least four, or it is three with stage rate \(r<4\kappa\). The otherwise surprising ringing comes from a dominant noncancelled conjugate renewal-pole pair; at or above the shape-three threshold, the variance instead approaches stationarity from below.
