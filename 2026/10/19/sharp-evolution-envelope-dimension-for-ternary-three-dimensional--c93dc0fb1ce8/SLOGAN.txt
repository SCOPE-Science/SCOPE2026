Among all \(19683\) three-dimensional unital commutative algebra tables over \(\mathbb F_3\), exactly \(288\) attain the maximal evolution-envelope dimension \(6\); these are precisely the commutative division algebras, and under unital isomorphism they form precisely two classes of \(144\) tables, one associative and one nonassociative. A projective-conic incidence criterion and a complete exact enumeration prove the classification.
