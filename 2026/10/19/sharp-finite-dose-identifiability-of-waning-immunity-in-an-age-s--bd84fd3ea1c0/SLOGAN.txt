In an age-structured SIRVS model, any \(2k-1\) exact endemic measurements at distinct subcritical vaccination rates uniquely recover a recovered-immunity law with at most \(k\) exponential components, and one fewer measurement cannot suffice. Without a finite-mixture assumption, every finite vaccination panel leaves distinct smooth immunity laws indistinguishable even with a known mean, whereas an exact response curve on any open dose interval identifies the full law.
