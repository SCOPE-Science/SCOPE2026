For every inner disk function, the complete degree-\(n\) optimal-polynomial approximation objective in \(H^{2m}\) is determined by exactly the first \((m-1)n+1\) Taylor coefficients, and a finite Blaschke product of degree at most \((m-1)n+1\) reproduces it identically. Both universal cutoffs are sharp, giving a finite model for an otherwise infinite-dimensional nonlinear Hardy approximation problem.
