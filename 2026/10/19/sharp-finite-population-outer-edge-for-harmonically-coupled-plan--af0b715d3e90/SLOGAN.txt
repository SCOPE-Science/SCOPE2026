For any fixed finite planar population of harmonically coupled isotropic run-and-tumble particles, the stationary distance of a particle from the centroid has an exact hard upper edge \(L_*\), and its near-edge probability decays as \(C_{N,\alpha}\varepsilon^{N\alpha+(N-1)/2}\). The additional angular-alignment exponent shows precisely why a large-population Gaussian closure cannot represent the true finite-population outer tail.
