For the three-twisted rotating wave on a directed finite-range Kuramoto ring, the sharp large-degree network-size transition is centered at \(\kappa(m+1/2)\), with \(\kappa=2\pi/\arccos((1+\sqrt{13})/6)\), and has a calculable negative next correction. Uniform control of every Fourier mode shows that this finite-size boundary, not just a continuum estimate, governs orbital exponential attraction.
