For every stationary planar harmonic Brownian gyrator with two distinct relaxation rates, the deterministic force-torque covariance has a single exact exponential clock, yet detecting its nonzero mean over a finite window faces a sharp bath-independent signal-to-noise ceiling. The associated unit-SNR observation-time threshold exists uniquely and diverges as a relaxation mode softens, even in the classical two-temperature model where the normalized torque memory time stays fixed.
