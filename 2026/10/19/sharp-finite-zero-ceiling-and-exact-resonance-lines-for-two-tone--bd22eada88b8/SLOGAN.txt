For every Hermite-window order \(n\), two equal tones have at most \(4n+1\) spectrogram zeros per zero-pattern period outside an explicit finite set of separations, and the bound is attained by \(4n+1\) simple zeros at all sufficiently wide gaps. Exactly when the gap equals a positive difference of two Hermite roots, entire horizontal-in-time zero lines appear at their midpoints; these are all the nonisolated zero components.
