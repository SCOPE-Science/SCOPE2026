The first one-step fitness delay in a three-person Moran game has an exact two-curve fixation phase diagram: it helps exactly when \((x-y)(xy-2y-2)<0\), where \(x,y\) are the historical mutant-to-resident fitness ratios. In one fixed positive-payoff game, increasing selection strength reverses the delay's effect at the sharp threshold \(w_*=(2+\sqrt{97})/31\), even while the ordering of relative fitness across states stays unchanged.
