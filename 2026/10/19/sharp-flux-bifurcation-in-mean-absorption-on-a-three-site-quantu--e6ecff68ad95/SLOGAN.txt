For a chiral three-site quantum ring with one continuously absorbing vertex, the mean absorption time is exactly \(3/\Gamma+[2J\sin\phi]^{-1}+\Gamma/[4J^2\sin^2\phi]\) away from dark fluxes. The globally best flux changes sharply at \(\Gamma=J\): two nonquadrature optima with \(\sin\phi=-\Gamma/J\) coalesce into the unique quadrature optimum \(\phi=3\pi/2\).
