Four Gaussian-weighted intensities at the origin and an equilateral triangle determine every affine Bargmann–Fock signal up to phase. Among all triangle radii, the Hermitian tomography condition number is uniquely minimized at \(r=\sqrt{u_*}\), where \((u_*-1)e^{2u_*}=3(u_*^2-u_*+1)\); the optimum has three equal least singular values and sharp condition number \(e^{u_*}/\sqrt3\).
