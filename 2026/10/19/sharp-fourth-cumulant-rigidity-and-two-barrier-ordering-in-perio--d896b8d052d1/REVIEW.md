# Scientific assessment

The exact fourth-order identity is established by reducing the equilibrium unicyclic characteristic relation to the Kirchhoff index and subtracting its homogeneous reference at fixed total resistance. Summing the parallel-arc resistance expansion cancels the term linear in the harmonic-coordinate corrector and gives its centered square exactly. The proof holds at arbitrary profile contrast for finite positive symmetric rates and unwrapped displacement. The accompanying finite rational replay checks the normalization and the identity by full-generator perturbation rather than the characteristic relation.

The contribution is the exact variance identity, not new universal bounds on the fourth-to-second ratio, a new determinant method, or a new two-defect spacing law. For common total resistance, this directly measures which profiles have the same or different fourth cumulant and explains why the homogeneous value has a quadratic, positive excess. The accessible unicyclic cumulant literature provides spectral formulas and bounds; historical fourth-order transport formulas that could give an equivalent variance representation remain a priority risk where full text was unavailable.
