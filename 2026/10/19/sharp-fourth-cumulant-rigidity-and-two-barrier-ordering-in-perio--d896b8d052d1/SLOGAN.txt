For symmetric nearest-neighbor hopping with any period \(n\ge3\), long-time diffusion depends only on total edge resistance, while the fourth-to-second displacement cumulant ratio equals \(1+12nE/R^2\), where \(E\) is the exact cyclic resistance-primitive energy, and lies sharply in \([1,n^2)\). For two identical slow bonds, the fourth cumulant strictly decreases with circular separation: adjacent bottlenecks maximize it and maximally separated bottlenecks minimize it.
