The six square-free quadratic Fourier characters on the four-torus have sharp complex-coefficient fourth-moment ratio \(\|P\|_4^4/\|P\|_2^4=5/2\), attained exactly by the character-phase coefficient family \(a_{ij}=c u_i u_j\). An exact nonnegative three-matching identity quantifies both amplitude imbalance and phase misalignment, and persists on every nonaliasing finite torus.
