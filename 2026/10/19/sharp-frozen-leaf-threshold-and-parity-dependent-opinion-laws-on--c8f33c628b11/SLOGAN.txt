On a star with \(k\) leaves, pure neighborhood Deffuant–Weisbuch dynamics admit completely unreceptive leaves exactly for \(\varepsilon\le\lfloor k/2\rfloor/k\), yet the hub can visit every subinterval of \([0,1]\) infinitely often almost surely. At half-compromise, this same frozen-leaf construction has a uniform limiting occupation law for even \(k\), but a singular full-support law of binary-entropy dimension for odd \(k\).
