Gaussian coefficient noise gives a sharp one-step Kaczmarz moment frontier: if rows are selected with probabilities proportional to their \(2s\)-th powers of observed length, the \(q\)-th update moment is finite exactly for \(q<\min\{md,d+2s\}\). Classical squared-row-norm sampling can restore finite variance in low dimensions where uniform sampling cannot, but no finite amount of Gaussian measurement averaging changes the threshold.
