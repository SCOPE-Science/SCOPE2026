For commuting Gaussian covariances, exact semi-unbalanced barycenter hybrid iteration is globally contractive with the sharp dimension-independent factor \(q=\max_j\sum_i 2w_i/(2+\tau/a_{ij}^2)<1\), without a bounded-covariance-iterate hypothesis. A single covariance standard deviation tending to infinity has a finite effect for every positive clean weight, with an exact inverse-square correction and a \((1-w)^{-1/2}\) ceiling divergence as that weight vanishes.
