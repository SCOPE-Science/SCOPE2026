The sharp second Taylor-Hankel determinant for normalized spirallike-convex maps has the universal grazing-angle law \(M(\beta)\sim (11/96)\cos^2\beta\) as \(\beta\to\pm\pi/2\). A sharp \(33/16\) Herglotz three-moment inequality yields a quantitative error bound and an explicit asymptotically extremizing two-point measure.
