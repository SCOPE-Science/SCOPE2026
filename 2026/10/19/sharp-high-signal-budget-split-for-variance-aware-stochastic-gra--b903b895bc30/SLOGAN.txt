For ideal variance-aware stochastic gradient steps with nondegenerate Gaussian additive noise on a quadratic, dividing a fixed gradient budget into successive blocks strictly improves expected squared error over pooling it at the start. In the high-signal limit, the optimal two-block allocation has a sharp dimension-one risk ratio \(0.6804153100\ldots\), a \(31.958\ldots\%\) benefit; the advantage decreases with dimension.
