For a scalar discrete-time plant whose unknown constant drift fills an interval of half-width \(t>0\), the unique worst-case spectral-rate optimum is \(t\) with static feedback and exactly \(t/2\) with any finite memory length at least two. One delayed-state tap is enough, and Schwarz-lemma rigidity proves that every extra tap is useless for this interval minimax objective; the corresponding strict stabilizable-width threshold doubles from \(2\) to \(4\).
