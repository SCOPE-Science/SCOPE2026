The regular zero-mean polygon in a globally coupled Stuart–Landau population has a sharp Kerr-dependent stability window: \((1-x)(y-2h)^2>(2-x)^2(x+h^2)\) with \(0\le x<1\). Nonlinear frequency shear shifts the center of the forbidden reactive-coupling interval to \(2h\), breaking the positive/negative reactive-coupling symmetry while preserving an exact, population-size-independent transverse spectral classification.
