# Review of the tent-map forward-core degeneration theorem

## Correctness — PASS

The branchwise derivative matrices preserve the cone \(|u|\le|v|\) and yield a contraction of short axes by \([\delta/(1-2\delta)]^n\), uniformly over all \(2^n\) itineraries. The associated coverings prove the explicit upper box bound. The Hausdorff lower bound is supplied by an exact two-cycle. The reverse bound uses a uniform contraction on cyclic orbit coordinates, preservation of tent branch labels, and the length-\(m\) tent periodic net. All parameter restrictions and the approximation constants are stated. Exact arithmetic replay covered 246 nonzero periodic points and the indicated finite cone checks. The proof, not the computation, handles all iterates and parameters.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge assessment)

The original Góra--Boyarsky--Li full text, arXiv:1607.01658, establishes singular SRB measures and cone estimates for the same parameter range but does not identify a quantitative Hausdorff limit for the full forward core or the displayed model-specific Minkowski estimate. The predecessor arXiv:1604.06991 concerns \(\alpha\le3/4\); its entire text was not available in this review. Prior claims about special lower-memory tent parameters concern different dynamics. Related generic dimension and stable-foliation principles are acknowledged; the model-specific sharp rate requires the periodic shadowing argument. A more general unindexed result may subsume parts of this statement and remains a priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

A physical measure supported on a thin planar attractor need not quantify how its full dynamical core approaches the one-dimensional zero-memory limit. The theorem proves the optimal *order* of this collapse, gives an explicit all-parameter fractal upper bound, and shows collapse of the dimension envelope to one. Its two independent proof mechanisms provide geometric information beyond the motivating existence theorem.

## Limits

The forward core is not identified with the entire physical SRB support. No exact fixed-parameter fractal dimension or two-sided dimension correction is claimed. Absolute historical priority is not certified.
