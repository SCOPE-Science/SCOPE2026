The best affine recovery of Spearman footrule from all pairwise ranking disagreements has exact weights proportional to the positive cut sums \(\sum_{t=a}^{b-1}t(n-t)\). It explains at least \(58/65\) of the uniform-null footrule variance at every size and asymptotically exactly \(51/56\), leaving a quantifiable nonlinear remainder.
