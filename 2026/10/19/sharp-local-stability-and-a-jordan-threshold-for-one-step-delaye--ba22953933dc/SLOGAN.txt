For a one-step stale-gradient polar eigensolver on a symmetric matrix, the largest locally attracting stepsize has a sharp three-regime boundary and is uniquely maximized when one quarter of the gradient is delayed, doubling the all-fresh ceiling from \(2/L\) to \(4/L\). Exactly at that optimum boundary, the extreme transverse mode becomes a Jordan block with amplification \(k+\sqrt{k^2+1}\) after \(k\) linearized steps.
