For compact diagonal spectral gaps in geometric progression, the worst-case decay on the natural fractional-power source ball has an exact \(n^{-\alpha}\) rate whose normalized constant never settles: its full cluster interval is \([L_b^\alpha e^{-L_b},\alpha^\alpha e^{-\alpha}]\), where \(L_b=\alpha\log b/(b-1)\). Two logarithmically incommensurate spectral ladders produce a quasiperiodic envelope with exact cluster interval \([\max\{L_{b_1}^\alpha e^{-L_{b_1}},L_{b_2}^\alpha e^{-L_{b_2}}\},\alpha^\alpha e^{-\alpha}]\), even though every individual orbit converges and convergence on the full unit ball is nonuniform.
