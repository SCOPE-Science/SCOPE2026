For the centered triadic Cantor-gap expanding map, the invariant conformal mass of an \(\varepsilon\)-neighborhood of its singular Cantor set has exact sharp oscillating normalized limits \(2^\delta\) and \(3\,2^\delta/2\), where \(\delta=\log(3/2)/\log3\). On every fixed geometric phase, first visits are exactly geometric and the \(\varepsilon^\delta\)-scaled exponential rate varies with phase, preventing a single global entrance law.
