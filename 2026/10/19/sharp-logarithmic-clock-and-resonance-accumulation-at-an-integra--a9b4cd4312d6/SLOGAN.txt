On the integrable Hopf–Langford critical locus, the radial period diverges as \((\gamma d)^{-1}\log(C_\gamma/(-E))\) with an exact positive constant \(C_\gamma=2^{1+2\gamma}(\gamma d)^2((\gamma+1)/\gamma)^\gamma\). Consequently, closed trajectories with \(n\) angular turns have exponentially small separatrix energies with this same sharp prefactor.
