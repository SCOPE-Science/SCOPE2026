For a quadratic objective, a two-point antithetic Gaussian Hamilton–Jacobi proximal estimate has strict conditional mean-square descent for every nonzero input exactly when \(\kappa t\le2/3\), and fresh draws converge to the minimizer almost surely throughout this sharp range. Reusing one Gaussian draw can instead create a locally attracting nonzero two-cycle with exact probability \(\operatorname{erfc}(1/\sqrt{\kappa t})\), even when fresh sampling converges.
