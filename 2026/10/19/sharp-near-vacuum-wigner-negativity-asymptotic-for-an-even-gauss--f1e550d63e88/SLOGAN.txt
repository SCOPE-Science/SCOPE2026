For the normalized even superposition of two unit-width Gaussians at separation \(2a\), the integrated negative Wigner mass has the sharp near-coincidence law \(\mathcal N(a)\sim 2^{7/4}e^{-1/2}\pi^{-3}a^{9/2}\exp[-\pi^2/(4a^2)+\pi/(\sqrt2a)]\). The law resolves a remote oscillatory fringe and a shrinking transverse Gaussian boundary layer, proving positivity for every \(a>0\) but decay faster than every algebraic power as \(a\downarrow0\).
