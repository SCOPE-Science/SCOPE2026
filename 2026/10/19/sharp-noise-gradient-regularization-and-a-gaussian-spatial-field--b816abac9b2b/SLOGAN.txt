In a one-way stochastic Gaussian chain with independent bath strengths \(D_j=Dj^{-\beta}\), the upstream variance diverges by a power for \(0\leq\beta<1/2\), grows logarithmically at \(\beta=1/2\), and remains bounded for \(\beta>1/2\), while the finite-chain drift spectrum stays uniformly stable. For \(0\leq\beta<1/2\), the critical damping window also produces an explicit \(\sqrt N\)-scale Gaussian spatial field with bath-tunable local roughness.
