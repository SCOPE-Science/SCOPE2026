In uniformly random \(q\)-ary de Bruijn cycles with \(q\ge3\), every nonconstant next-order word occurs with probability between \((q-2)/(q(q-1))\) and \(1/(q-1)\), uniformly in the order. Only two-symbol alternating triples attain the global lower endpoint, and only constant runs with one different boundary symbol attain the upper endpoint.
