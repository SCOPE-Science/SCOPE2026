In the classical Holling-II predator–prey oscillator, the physical-time period of each emerging stable cycle increases relative to the actual linearized period by \(C(h)r^2+o(r^2)\), where \(r\) is the prey first-harmonic amplitude. The exact coefficient has one sharp global minimum, \(C(h_*)=1.223281644705308\ldots\) at \(h_*=0.372462737190824\ldots\).
