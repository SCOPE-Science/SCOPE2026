Two-sided sketching leaks a normal residual into the fixed-rank tangent space by an exactly computable operator norm; with squared-norm distortions \(\varepsilon_L,\varepsilon_R<1\), its sharp relative leakage is \(\sqrt{[(1-\varepsilon_L^2)(1-\varepsilon_R^2)]^{-1}-1}\). The bound is attained already by a rank-one \(2\times2\) matrix, giving an exact per-step embedding-accuracy budget for oblique low-rank dynamics.
