For the distance-calibrated local linear-minimization step on positive-definite quadratics, objective descent has the exact condition-number threshold \(7.306674433\ldots\), even though distance contracts for all condition numbers. Above this threshold, objective values can rise by a factor approaching five; for an ill-conditioned quadratic with one stiff direction and a growing intermediate-dimensional ambient space, this amplification is typical under a uniform-sphere initialization.
