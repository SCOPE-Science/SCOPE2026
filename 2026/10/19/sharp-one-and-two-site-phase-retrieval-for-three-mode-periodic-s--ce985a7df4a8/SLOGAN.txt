Three-mode free Schrödinger waves on the circle need only one spatial intensity sensor unless their three squared wave numbers form an arithmetic progression, when precisely two well-spaced sensors become necessary and sufficient. At resonance, a single sensor admits fourfold phase ambiguity, while the sharp two-sensor demixing condition number is minimized exactly at \((k_2-2k_1+k_0)h\equiv\pi\pmod{2\pi}\).
