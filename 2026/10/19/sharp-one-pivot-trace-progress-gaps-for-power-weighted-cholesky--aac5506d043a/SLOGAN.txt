For one-pivot positive-semidefinite Cholesky, the canonical diagonal-proportional rule can lose a sharp factor \((1+\sqrt r)/2\) in expected trace progress to uniform selection at rank \(r\), and the first reversal already occurs in a three-by-three matrix. Every fixed superlinear diagonal power loses even this rank-dependent protection: uniform sampling can be arbitrarily faster in expected first-step trace decrease on rank-two matrices.
