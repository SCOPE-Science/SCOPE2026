As a greedy \(\beta\)-transformation approaches any integer base \(b\ge2\) from below, its canonical invariant probability has a sharp \((b-\beta)\log(1/(b-\beta))\) response whose normalized signed limit is \((dx-\delta_1)/(b\log b)\). Its distance to Lebesgue probability in total variation has the same scale with exact coefficient \(1/(b\log b)\).
