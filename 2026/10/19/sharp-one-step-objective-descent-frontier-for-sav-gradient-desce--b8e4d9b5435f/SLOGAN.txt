For the unrelaxed SAV descent step on the scalar double-well objective with shift \(C=1/4\), the sharp all-initialization original-energy descent limit is \(h_*=0.974932804015453\ldots\), strictly below the local stability limit \(h=1\). Above it the original objective can increase despite decreasing auxiliary energy, and suitable first steps from arbitrarily near a minimizer make the original objective diverge while auxiliary energy tends to zero.
