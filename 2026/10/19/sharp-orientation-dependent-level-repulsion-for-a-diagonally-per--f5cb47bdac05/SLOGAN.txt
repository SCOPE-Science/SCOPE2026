In the smallest diagonally perturbed two-dimensional null space, the eigenvalue-gap distribution has an exact, orientation-dependent local polynomial: a quadratic repulsion term with a cubic correction for fully oblique kernels, versus linear repulsion when the kernel contains a coordinate direction. This sharp geometric change moves the inverse-gap moment cutoff from order \(2\) to order \(1\), and it governs the first-order splitting of the two zero eigenvalues in the diagonal-perturbed Lanczos model.
