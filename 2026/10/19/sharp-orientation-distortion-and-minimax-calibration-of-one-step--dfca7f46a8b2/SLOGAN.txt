On matrices with perfectly orthonormal columns, one-step pivoted Hybrid LSLU can silently replace a fixed Tikhonov parameter by an orientation-dependent parameter ranging sharply from \(\mu/\sqrt n\) to \(\mu\sqrt m\) in the zero-noise limit. Even the best orientation-independent scalar calibration leaves the exact worst-case normalized gap \((\sqrt{mn}-1)/(2(\sqrt{mn}+1))\) relative to ordinary parameter-one Tikhonov regularization.
