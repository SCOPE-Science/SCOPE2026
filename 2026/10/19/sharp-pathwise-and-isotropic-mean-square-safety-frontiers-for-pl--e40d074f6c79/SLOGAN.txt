For planar unweighted residual-history PLSS, exact two-step termination coexists with a sharp first-step safety gap: arbitrary-direction Euclidean residual nonexpansion holds through \(\kappa=1+\sqrt2\), but isotropic mean-square nonexpansion holds through \(\kappa=2+\sqrt3\). Above the first threshold the exact probability of a growing first residual is an elementary arctangent function of the condition number, approaching one half at high conditioning.
