# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Exact cut-edge resistance decomposition and a grounded-clique inverse give the three-variable objective \(K=A/c+C/a+D/(a+mb)\). Strict convexity of \(\operatorname{tr}(L+J)^{-1}\) yields a unique unrestricted optimizer and permits averaging under the graph's automorphisms. For low price a sharp three-term Cauchy--Schwarz bound is attained with \(b>0\); for high price the boundary Cauchy--Schwarz optimum satisfies the complete nonnegativity KKT inequalities. The two branches coincide at the threshold. A standalone exact-arithmetic checker independently reconstructs the small Laplacians and tests feasibility and stationarity; it is not substituted for the infinite-family proof.

**Originality — PASS, with qualification.** Ghosh--Boyd--Saberi (MTNS 2006 Section V, SIAM Review 2008 Section 5) proves the uniform-price \(t=1\) barbell optimum and the general graph convex framework. That is a direct predecessor and is not claimed as new. The assessed result is instead the global variable-price \(t>0\) two-phase classification, the exact deletion threshold, and the price-insensitive optimal regime, which are not stated by those inspected originals. Johnson--Chertkov (2010) discusses line costs and network structure but not this explicit all-parameter theorem in its inspected full text. Relevant mathematical research records and other source formulations were searched; failure to find coverage cannot establish bibliographic uniqueness.

**Value — PASS.** When redundant within-community links incur greater per-unit resource cost, whether and at what precise price to abandon them is a natural network-design question. The threshold \(4(m+1)/(2m+1)\) is an exact all-size sparsification boundary; above it the resource-optimal network is a pair of stars joined by a bridge even though the permitted graph contains two cliques. This gives a concrete cost-versus-robustness decision rule and a nontrivial support transition, not just a restatement of the unpriced optimum.

**Limitations and risks.** The graph family is balanced and edge prices are orbit-constant. No explicit unequal-clique or heterogeneous-spoke theorem is claimed. General convex network design formulations already encompass the optimization problem algorithmically; a specialized published analytic extension, if present, could cover this statement. An October 2026 manuscript discussing cost convexity was available as an abstract only; its exact graph-level coverage was not verified by full-text inspection. Date-resolved archival evidence is the 2006 Kyoto TuP12.2 meeting appearance, while earlier manuscript-day evidence could not be resolved.
