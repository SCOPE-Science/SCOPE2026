On two equally sized cliques joined by one bridge, optimizing all nonnegative edge conductances for total effective resistance under a linear budget produces a sharp peripheral-link deletion threshold \(t_c=4(m+1)/(2m+1)\), where \(m\) is the number of nonhub vertices in each clique. Below this price every peripheral chord is used; at and above it all those chords disappear and both the globally unique allocation and its optimal resistance become independent of their price.
