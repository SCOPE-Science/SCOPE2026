For a single fermionic mode and an arbitrary bath, overwriting the bath covariance while retaining a fraction \(\eta\) of system–bath coherence preserves all valid Gaussian one-particle covariances exactly when \(\eta^2\le\min\{\inf\sigma(B),1-\sup\sigma(B)\}\). Thermal band edges turn this into an exponentially shrinking low-temperature coherence-retention ceiling, and a one-mode local quantum-channel implementation has a distinct, stricter sharp threshold.
