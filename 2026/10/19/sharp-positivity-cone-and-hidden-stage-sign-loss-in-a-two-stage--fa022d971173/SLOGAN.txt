A natural unshifted two-stage Chebyshev two-step integrator is absolutely stable for scalar decay throughout \(0<z<4\), but with exact positive starting data its reported iterates stay positive exactly through the sharper threshold \(z_*=3.480888475257457945\ldots\). Its very first internal stage already turns negative when \(z>\log3\), and beyond the endpoint threshold the first negative iterate is delayed logarithmically as the threshold is approached.
