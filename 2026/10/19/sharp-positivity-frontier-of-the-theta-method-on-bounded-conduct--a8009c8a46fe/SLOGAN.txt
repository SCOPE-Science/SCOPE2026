For every connected weighted \(n\)-vertex graph with edge conductances at most \(w\), the theta-method diffusion update remains entrywise nonnegative uniformly through the sharp step \(1/[w(n-1-n\theta)]\) whenever \(\theta<(n-1)/n\), and is unconditionally nonnegative above that theta threshold. Exactly the graphs with a fully saturated universal vertex attain the finite worst-case boundary, even when all other edges are arbitrary.
