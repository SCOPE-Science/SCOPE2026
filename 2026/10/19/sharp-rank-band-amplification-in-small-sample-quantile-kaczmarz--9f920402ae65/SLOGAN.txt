The lower sample median can become an almost-surely corrupted projection even when the fraction of bad equations vanishes: its sharp adversarial selection probability follows the Gaussian crossover \(2\Phi(\theta)-1\) at corruption fraction of order \(k^{-1/2}\). An explicit full-rank, unit-row Kaczmarz family has first-step expected squared error rising from \(3/4\) to \(9/4\), with a sharp crossover through the starting error \(1\).
