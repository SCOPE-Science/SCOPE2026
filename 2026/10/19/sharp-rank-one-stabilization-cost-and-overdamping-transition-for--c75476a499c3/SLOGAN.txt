For a real unstable planar rotation–dilation mode, the exact minimum Frobenius cost of an arbitrary rank-one correction jumps from infeasibility at zero rotation to \((\alpha^2+b^2)/b\) when \(0<b<\alpha\), and becomes \(2\alpha\) when \(b\geq\alpha\), where \(\alpha\) is growth plus the target spectral margin. Below the transition every optimal correction is oblique and yields a defective boundary matrix, while symmetric rank-one damping works only at or above the transition and loses stability again when overdamped.
