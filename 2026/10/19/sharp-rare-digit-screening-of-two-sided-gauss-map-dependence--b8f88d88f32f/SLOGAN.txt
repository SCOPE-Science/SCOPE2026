Conditioned on an exceptionally large digit of the stationary Gauss continued-fraction process, the digits immediately before and after it become asymptotically independent: their exact residual total-variation dependence is \(1/(32N^2)\), while their mutual information is asymptotic to \(\tfrac12(\pi^2/12-3/4)^2N^{-4}\). The corresponding continuous backward and forward tails have the same sharp total-variation coefficient but a different sharp mutual-information coefficient, \(1/(288N^4)\).
