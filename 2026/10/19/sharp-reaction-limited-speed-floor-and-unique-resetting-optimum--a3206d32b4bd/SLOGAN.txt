For one-dimensional inward drift toward a partially reactive point target, Poisson resetting has exactly one optimal rate at every finite drift speed, and mean absorption time always exceeds the sharp reaction-limited floor \(eL/k\). At strong inward drift the optimal reset rate is \(U/L-k/(eL)+O(U^{-1})\), while the optimal mean time approaches \(eL/k\); for any positive finite reactivity the optimal rate initially falls with drift strength but ultimately grows, forcing at least one turnover.
