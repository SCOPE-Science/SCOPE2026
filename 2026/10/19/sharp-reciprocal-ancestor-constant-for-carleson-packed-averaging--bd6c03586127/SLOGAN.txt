For a \(\Lambda\)-Carleson nested family, the sharp reciprocal-ancestor kernel constant is \(\lfloor\Lambda\rfloor+\Lambda(\Lambda-1)/\lfloor\Lambda\rfloor\) for \(\Lambda>1\), with value \(1\) at \(\Lambda=1\). Finite atomic chains attain this constant in the limit, improving the previously stated quadratic \(\Lambda^2\) bound to exact linear-order growth.
