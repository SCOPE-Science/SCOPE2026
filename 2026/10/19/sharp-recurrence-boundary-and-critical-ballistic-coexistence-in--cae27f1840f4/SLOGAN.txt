For the unit-feedback three-dimensional sine map, compact recurrent dynamics exist exactly when \(|\alpha|\le|\beta+\gamma-\delta|\); above this boundary every trajectory escapes ballistically. At equality all bounded trajectories converge to equilibrium, yet stationary and ballistic orbits coexist, and attracting center-manifold trajectories obey the exact critical relaxation law \(n(z_n-\zeta)\to-2/\alpha\).
