Regular elements of the binary-operation magma monoid form an exponentially thin fraction \(\sim 1.256970100571444\,(0.842065081839284)^n\) of all operations on an \(n\)-element set. Conditioned on regularity, the number of labels missing from the diagonal image has a Gaussian limit centered at \(0.319984374206401n\), with variance asymptotic to \(0.093950190003772n\).
