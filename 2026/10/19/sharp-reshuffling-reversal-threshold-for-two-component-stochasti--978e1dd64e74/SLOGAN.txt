On a two-component strongly convex quadratic, random reshuffling can have a strictly worse sharp one-epoch expected squared-error factor than independent sampling, even though every stochastic extragradient step is nonexpansive. The exact reversal starts at the unique effective-stepsize root \(k_*=1.6454871463\ldots\) of an explicit sextic, and already occurs at condition number \(2\) with a rational gap \(5/729\).
