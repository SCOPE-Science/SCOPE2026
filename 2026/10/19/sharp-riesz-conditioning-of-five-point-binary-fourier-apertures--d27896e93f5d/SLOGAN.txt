Every affinely independent five-point aperture in \(\mathbb F_2^d\), for \(d\ge4\), has sharp exponential-basis Riesz ratio \(9/4\); no affinely dependent five-point aperture attains it. All optimal five-frequency selections are precisely the lifts of translates of one dual five-point simplex, with exactly \(16\cdot2^{5(d-4)}\) partners per aperture.
