A binary word contains every length-\(k\) palindrome as a subsequence with exactly \(2\lceil k/2\rceil\) runs, and no word with fewer runs can do so. The explicit shortest witnesses \((0011)^m\) and \((0011)^m01\) also show that an \(r\)-run word supports palindromic-subsequence universality of order at most—and sometimes exactly—\(2\lfloor r/2\rfloor\).
