For every fixed number \(k\ge4\) of ones, all but \(\Theta_k(n^{k-1})\) of the \(\binom nk\) binary length-\(n\) words have the maximum possible \(2k\) runs in their cyclic Burrows–Wheeler output. Pairwise-separated positive cyclic zero gaps guarantee this maximal fragmentation, while two equal largest gaps give a matching-order family of exceptions.
