On every dyadic cyclic group \(\mathbb Z_{2^m}\), \(m\ge3\), a complex signal supported at exactly four sites has Fourier support either at the subgroup-extremal size \(2^{m-2}\) or at least \(3\cdot2^{m-3}\). The second threshold is sharp for every group order, giving a universal 50% jump above the uncertainty minimum for all nonextremal four-sparse signals.
