# Verification evidence and limits

The proof is fully specified in `RESULT.md`. Lemma 1 is a direct parity argument for all nonzero two-/three-spike complex signals, Lemma 2 is a Parseval–Cauchy equality analysis, and the induction classifies all antipodal-pair patterns and the no-pair projected-coset obstruction. The ratio of two putative quotient characters cannot have a fourth power of negative one at a generator, which is the critical rigidity step. The explicit polynomial gives the sharp bound for each integer group order in scope.

The standalone checker `verify.py` was executed in its actual package source location with NumPy. It produced `VERIFY_OK 22732 finite sampled comparisons`: all four-element supports on \(\mathbb Z_8\) with four nonzero coefficients independently selected from \(\{1,-1,i,-i\}\); pseudorandom complex-phase examples for larger dyadic orders; and explicit equality and second-level witnesses through \(N=256\). This is neither exhaustive over complex coefficients nor a substitute for the analytic induction. No external or independent verification was conducted.
