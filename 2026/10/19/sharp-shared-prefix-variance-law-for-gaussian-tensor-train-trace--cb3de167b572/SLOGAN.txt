Reusing the first \(k\) Gaussian tensor-train cores across \(L\) trace probes changes the sharp worst-case relative variance to \((3(1+2/r)^{d-1}-1)/L+(1-1/L)((1+2/r)^k-1)\), attained by separable rank-one targets. If any prefix core remains shared at finite bond rank, arbitrarily many independently resampled suffixes retain a strictly positive error floor; proportional core sharing and bond rank produce the exact limiting floor \(e^{2\theta/\alpha}-1\).
