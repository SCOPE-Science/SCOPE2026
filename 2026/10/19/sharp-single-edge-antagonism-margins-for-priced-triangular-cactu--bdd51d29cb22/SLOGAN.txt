A single-edge antagonism margin on any priced triangular cactus has the exact optimal value \(B/(\sum_i k(t_i)+\sum_j c_j)\), where the explicit triangle coefficient \(k\) changes branch at prices \(2/5\) and \(4\). Every optimum makes the two equal-price spokes of each triangle equally conductive; its backup edge is more conductive below \(2/5\), equal-conductive for \(2/5\leq t<4\), optional at \(t=4\), and entirely omitted above \(4\).
