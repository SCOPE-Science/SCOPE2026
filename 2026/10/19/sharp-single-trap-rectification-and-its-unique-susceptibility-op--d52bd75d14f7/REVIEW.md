# Scientific assessment

## Correctness (PASS)

The claim follows from the explicitly enumerated irreducible 16-state exclusion generator with physical rates one half of the symbolic rates. The product detailed-balance measure at equal reservoirs is exactly verified; the two normalized stationary Taylor equations are solved in rational functions of the defect rate. Symbolic identities match both polynomial response coefficients exactly. Positivity of the polynomial coefficients proves the sign; a transformed derivative with precisely one Descartes sign change and opposite endpoint derivative signs establishes existence and uniqueness of the physical optimum. Six separate exact finite-bias stationary computations corroborate the local expansion. The theorem does not extrapolate finite checks to an infinite-volume system.

## Originality (PASS, with risks)

The primary 2026 boundary-driven exclusion paper establishes the model, spatial reflection restriction and approximate/numerical rectification, but its inspected treatment does not state the all-parameter exact single-defect polynomial sign criterion or its unique optimal quadratic response. The related full-text 2025 preprint concerns periodic external-field driving, not reservoir-driving. Earlier finite-sample analysis of the same open model concerns a fixed, different four-site rate profile and zeros as reservoir density varies; it does not imply a unique optimum as one defect speed varies. Claim-directed research-record searches returned superficially related nonlinear-response and fluctuation studies, not the present finite open model. General exact Markov-perturbation methods are longstanding; a more encompassing exact disordered exclusion solution may imply this specialization, so novelty is not certified independently.

## Value (PASS)

The source literature asks when microscopic asymmetry yields nonlinear current rectification and notes the difficulty of sufficiency. A trap at the first nontrivial interior position supplies the minimal physical model in which broken mirror symmetry is provably sufficient for a strictly signed quadratic response. Its uniquely optimal slowdown depth supplies an interpretable, nontrivial design invariant rather than a single computational data point.
