On a uniformly coupled quantum star with independent site-projector dephasing, the *full* Lindbladian relaxation gap is globally optimized at \(\gamma_*=J(m-1)/\sqrt{m-2}\) for every \(m\ge3\) leaves, with sharp maximum \(J/\sqrt{m-2}\); the two-leaf star has the separate optimum \((\gamma_*,g_*)=(2\sqrt2J,\sqrt2J)\). The complete operator-space characteristic polynomial is explicit, and the best achievable relaxation rate decreases as the star grows even though the optimal dephasing strength increases.
