For fourth-order Gauss–Legendre time stepping on the unit undirected cycle \(C_n\), every step is positivity-preserving exactly when \(n\le5\); the first large-step loss occurs at \(n=6\), whereas arbitrarily small steps first fail at \(n=14\). The exact six-cycle step threshold and a limiting \(1-1/\sqrt3\) fraction of negative large-step transition entries quantify both obstructions.
