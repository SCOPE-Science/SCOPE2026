On every one-leaf corona of a connected vertex-transitive core, the unique budget-optimal rooted-forest grounding puts **all** gains on leaves until a single threshold determined exactly by the core Laplacian spectrum, then uses both leaf and core gains. The critical budget increases strictly under transitivity-preserving edge additions and is at most \(m(m-2)/2\), with equality only for a complete core on \(m\) vertices.
