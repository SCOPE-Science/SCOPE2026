In ideal spectral-deflation log-determinant estimation, one Gaussian probe is optimal at every finite budget for every log-spectrum satisfying \(a_{j+1}\leq q a_j\) exactly when the universal guarantee has \(q\leq2^{-1/(2c)}\), where \(c\) is the fixed per-probe cost; the threshold is sharp. Geometric log-spectra require only a budget-independent optimal number of probes, whereas polynomial log-spectra asymptotically allocate a positive fraction of the budget to probes.
