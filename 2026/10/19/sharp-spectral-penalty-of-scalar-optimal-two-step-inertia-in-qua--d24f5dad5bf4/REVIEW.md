# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The entire theorem reduces to the explicitly derived cubic modal characteristic polynomial. Evaluation at one and the product over its three roots yield a sharp all-real-parameter scalar optimum with a unique triple-root achiever. With that achiever fixed, an elementary strictly monotone parametrization of the unique real root turns the complete two-complex-root spectral radius into a one-variable maximization with its unique optimizer \(u=2/3\). The transfer to the actual two-dimensional proximal matrix is exact, not approximate. Rational inequalities establish compatibility with all strict inequalities in the relevant published convergence condition. SymPy identities and direct updates were replayed, and the analytic proof—not sampling—covers every mode.

**Originality — PASS (best-knowledge, with specified residual risks).** The 2024 two-step splitting preprint's actual algorithm, sufficient parameter regime, main convergence analysis and numerical examples were read, and its statement did not cover triple-root scalar optimality or the sharp faster-mode reversal. The 2023 numerical double-inertial FBF paper's available abstract and verified primary MSC were compared, but its complete subscription text was unavailable; no unsupported full-text negative assertion was made. Targeted semantic searches found related two-stage Richardson, Kaczmarz, fixed-step accelerated-gradient and inertial splitting results, but their implications do not yield the cubic companion spectral envelope \(2/\sqrt3\) at \(8q/9\). Accessible historical claims do not cover that envelope. Older multistep acceleration literature might contain an equivalent formulation.

**Value — PASS.** Choosing two inertia parameters to achieve the best possible contraction rate at an apparently worst eigenmode is a natural spectral-tuning practice. This result precisely locates an interior spectral mode that becomes the true bottleneck, with an exact nontrivial \(15.47\%\) spectral-radius penalty. The two-dimensional strongly convex example proves the phenomenon is not an artifact of unstable or inadmissible tuning: the parameters satisfy the source's own strict sufficient convergence inequalities. The result is a useful modal benchmark and a warning against extrapolating scalar-optimal momentum tuning to a mixed-curvature quadratic. No general nonlinear acceleration guarantee is claimed.

Iyiola–Jolaoso–Shehu (2024), arXiv:2410.01099v1, directly supplies the reproduced three-operator iteration and sufficient parameter regime but does not report the present exact rate reversal. Prior spectral polynomial and critical-damping facts are not claimed as new. The theorem concerns fixed parameters, self-adjoint nonnegative contractions, exact arithmetic, and lifted-state asymptotic spectral radii; the critical-root mode need not admit a prefactor-one geometric bound.
