For a scalar proximal contraction with factor \(q\), uniquely rate-optimal two-step inertia triples its characteristic root at \(t=1-(1-q)^{1/3}\). The same parameters incur a sharp universal interior-mode penalty \(2/\sqrt3\): among all faster modes in \([0,q]\), the mode \(8q/9\) converges slowest, including in a strongly convex two-dimensional quadratic example satisfying published strict convergence conditions.
