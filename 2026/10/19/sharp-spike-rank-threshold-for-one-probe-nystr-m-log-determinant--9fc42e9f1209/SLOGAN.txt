For a two-level positive-definite spectrum with \(r\) diverging eigenvalues, a Gaussian Nyström sketch of width \(\ell\) leaves exactly \((r-\ell)_+\) uncontrollable spike directions: the one-Gaussian-probe log-determinant mean-square error equals \(2(r-\ell)_+\log^2(1+t)+O_{r,s,\ell}(1)\). Thus \(\ell=r\) already guarantees contrast-uniform second moments without any extra oversampling, and every smaller sketch fails.
