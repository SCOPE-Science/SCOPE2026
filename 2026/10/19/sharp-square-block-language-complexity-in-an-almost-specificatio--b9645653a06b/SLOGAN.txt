In a classical almost-specification family, the normalized word-complexity deficit has an explicit stretched-exponential law with a genuine, nonconstant square-block phase: for the \(p=2,q=4\) example, its \(k4^{-k}\)-scaled subsequential limits fill \([864/25,3456/25]\). Two variants with different published weak-specification properties nevertheless have exactly the same word count at every length.
