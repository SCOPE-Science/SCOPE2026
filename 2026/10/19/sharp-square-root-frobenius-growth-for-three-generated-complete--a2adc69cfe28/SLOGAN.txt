The least Frobenius number among embedding-dimension-three complete-intersection numerical semigroups of multiplicity \(m\) is asymptotic to \(2m^{3/2}\), and the least genus is asymptotic to \(m^{3/2}\). An explicit gluing with a coprime multiplier in a short arithmetic window attains the sharp leading constant for every sufficiently large multiplicity, without a primality assumption.
