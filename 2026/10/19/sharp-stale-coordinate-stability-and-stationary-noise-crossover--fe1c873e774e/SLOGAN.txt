For a two-variable randomized Gauss–Seidel iteration that reads its own coordinate now but its neighbor one step late, the exact mean-square stability threshold is \(\beta<4/(1+\sqrt{1+4r^2})\). Surprisingly, at matched update-noise variance, stale reads reduce the limiting coordinate variance under underrelaxation \(0<\beta<1\), increase it under overrelaxation, and leave it unchanged at \(\beta=1\).
