At fixed stationary position variance, randomizing the squared strength of Poisson partial resets can only make the Brownian operational clock more variable in convex order: a fixed contraction is the least variable protocol, and random full-or-null resets are the most variable. This gives sharp bounds \(6/(1+m)\le K\le6\) for stationary kurtosis, together with opposite monotonicity of central density and mean absolute displacement.
