For two three-tap signals on \(\mathbb Z_p\) whose supports are not translates, the exact smallest short-time Fourier support is \(p(9-E)-E+\varepsilon\), where \(E\) counts coincident pair differences and \(\varepsilon\) detects a reflected nonprogression triangle. For every prime \(p\ge7\), the sharp unmatched-support minimum is \(6p-3\), while reflected nonprogression supports necessarily lose one simultaneous Fourier cancellation.
