For every four-point cap in \(\mathbb F_3^d\), the best exponential-basis Riesz ratio is exactly \((7+3\sqrt5)/2\) when the affine span is a plane, and exactly \(7/3\) when its four points are affinely independent. The numbers of optimal four-frequency bases are respectively \(36\cdot3^{4(d-2)}\) and \(54\cdot3^{4(d-3)}\).
