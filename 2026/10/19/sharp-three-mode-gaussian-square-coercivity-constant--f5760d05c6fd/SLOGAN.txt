The sharp Gaussian centered-square coercivity constant on \(\operatorname{span}\{1,z,z^2\}\) is \(C_2=1.5633577120464347\ldots\), attained exactly by scaled and rotated copies of one destructive-interference quadratic. It strictly exceeds the affine constant \(\sqrt2\) and supplies a rigorous lower bound for the unrestricted Fock-space constant, without determining that unrestricted constant.
