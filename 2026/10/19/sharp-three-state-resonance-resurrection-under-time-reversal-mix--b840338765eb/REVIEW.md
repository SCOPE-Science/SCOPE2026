# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. A nontrivial doubly stochastic square root of the uniform averaging projection has a rank-one nilpotent centered component on its two-dimensional mean-zero space. Averaging it with its stationary transpose and squaring produces a scalar multiple of the identity on that space. This yields exact all-time matrix powers, correlations, and eigenvalues, including the boundary weights. The extremal singular-value bound uses the fact that among three equally spaced sine phases, one is at most \(-1/2\); entrywise nonnegativity then forces \(\sigma\le\sqrt3-1\). The radical witness saturates this inequality. The proof explicitly checks expansion, Lebesgue invariance, and Markov coding. A standalone exact-arithmetic checker reproduces 345 finite assertions; it does not stand in for the universal proof.

PASS within the examined literature, with material residual priority risk. Jain–Phalempin (arXiv:2608.05649v1) already establishes general formulas for annealed transfer spectra and a two-map example of nonaveraged resonances. The present claim is not that general phenomenon, but the optimal three-state time-reversal and exact-two-step classification with a sharp universal \(\sqrt3-1\) singular-value threshold and uniform even-lag correlation law. Higham–Lin (2011) treats stochastic matrix roots but does not present the sharp time-reversal resonance extremum. The Bezakova–Stefankovic colloquium abstract indicates an older broad convex-mixture slowdown phenomenon, so precedent for the qualitative mechanism is acknowledged; its underlying full study was not inspected. No directly dominating statement appeared in the checked full texts or relevant indexed mathematical records. An unindexed or implicit equivalent optimization remains possible; priority is not independently verified.

## Mathematical value

PASS. This is a natural sharp quantitative boundary for a counterintuitive mixing phenomenon: random switching induces correlations at every even time although each constituent loses all centered correlations after two steps. The value \(\sqrt3-1\) is the exact universal optimum in the **minimal possible state dimension**, not a single tuned numerical example. The observable-sector realization makes the phenomenon concrete for one-dimensional expanding dynamics. Neither the result nor the scope asserts a universal statement in higher dimensions or for arbitrary fine-scale interval observables.
