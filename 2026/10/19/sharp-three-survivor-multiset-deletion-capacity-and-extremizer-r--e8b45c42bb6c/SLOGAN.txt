For every alphabet of size \(q\ge5\), the maximum multiset code correcting all but three deletions has exactly \(q+1\) codewords from length \(q+2\) through \(2q\), then exactly \(q\); one length shorter, \(q+2\) words are possible. The terminal \(q+1\)-capacity code is unique at length \(2q\) and has exactly \(q^2\) labeled realizations at length \(2q-1\).
