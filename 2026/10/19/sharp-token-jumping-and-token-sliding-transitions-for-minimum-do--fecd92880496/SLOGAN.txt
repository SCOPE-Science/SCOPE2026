The known minimum dominating triples of the Kneser pair graph \(\operatorname{KG}(n,2)\) split into frozen triangles and three-edge matchings. For the matching family, token jumping becomes connected at \(n=7\), whereas token sliding is frozen through \(n=7\), consists of \(105\) copies of \(K_4\) at \(n=8\), and becomes connected precisely at \(n=9\).
