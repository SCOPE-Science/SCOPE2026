For the three-consecutive-character Parseval frame on \(\mathbb Z_N\), \(N\ge6\), deleting any three coefficients leaves a squared lower bound at least \(1-[3+(b_N+\sqrt{b_N^2+8a_N^2})/2]/N\), where \(a_N=1+2\cos(2\pi/N)\), \(b_N=1+2\cos(4\pi/N)\). Equality occurs exactly for the \(N\) cyclic consecutive three-erasure masks; every other mask is strictly better.
