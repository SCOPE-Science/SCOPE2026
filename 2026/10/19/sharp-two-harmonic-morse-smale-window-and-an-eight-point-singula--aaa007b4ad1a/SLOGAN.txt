For a smooth two-harmonic Huygens update with interaction \(g_c(s)=\sin s+c\sin^3s\), diagonal dominance gives a **sharp** Morse–Smale interval \(0<h<1/[\lambda_{\max}(K)q(c)]\), valid for every \(c\geq0\). On the three-edge star, the first singular Jacobian moves from the antipodal sink to eight distinct nonfixed configurations precisely when \(c\) crosses \(1/6\), despite the sink remaining hyperbolic on the latter side.
