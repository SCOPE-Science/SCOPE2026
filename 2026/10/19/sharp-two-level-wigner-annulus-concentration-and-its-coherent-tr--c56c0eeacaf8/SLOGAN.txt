Every concentric annulus has an exact, uniquely determined optimal population for squared Wigner concentration among superpositions of the first two Hermite states. Strictly coherent optimizers occupy a nonempty parameter region, and in the thin-shell limit the population changes from vacuum to superposition at \(4\pi r^2=1\), then to the first excited state at \(4\pi r^2=2+\sqrt2\).
