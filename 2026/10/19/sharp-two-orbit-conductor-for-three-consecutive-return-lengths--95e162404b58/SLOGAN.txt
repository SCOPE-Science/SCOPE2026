For the binary \(S\)-gap shift with return lengths \(\{a,a+1,a+2\}\), the last period having fewer than two primitive orbits is exactly \(6\) for \(a=2\), \((a+1)(a+2)/2\) for odd \(a\ge3\), and \(a(a+2)/2+1\) for even \(a\ge4\). At the threshold there is exactly one orbit, and every larger period carries at least two.
