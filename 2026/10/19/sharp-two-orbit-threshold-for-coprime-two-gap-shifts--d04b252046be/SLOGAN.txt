For every mixing two-gap binary shift with coprime return lengths \(2\le a<b\), the last length carrying fewer than two distinct primitive periodic orbits is exactly \(\max\{(a+1)b,2(a+b)\}\), and there is exactly one orbit at that length. This sharp multiplicity threshold refines the previously known existence-only period spectrum; the zeta-function and primitive-necklace formulas themselves are established tools.
