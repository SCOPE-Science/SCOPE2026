For a positive definite matrix with one distinguished hub coordinate and an equicorrelated clique of \(m\ge3\) other coordinates, the best positive diagonal spectral preconditioner has an explicit two-branch formula with a sharp eigenvalue-multiplicity transition. Jacobi scaling is globally optimal precisely for equal hub–clique and within-clique couplings; in a six-dimensional example, the exact condition number falls from \((3+\sqrt6)/(3-\sqrt6)\) to \(9\).
