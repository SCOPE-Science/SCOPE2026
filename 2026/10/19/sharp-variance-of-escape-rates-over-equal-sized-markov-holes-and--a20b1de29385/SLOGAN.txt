For every fixed integer \(q\ge2\), the normalized escape rates of uniformly chosen length-\(n\) Markov holes in \(x\mapsto qx\pmod1\) have variance asymptotic to \(K_q q^{-n}\), where \(K_q=\sum_{p\ge1}a_q(p)q^{-2p}\) is an explicit primitive-orbit series. Under the variance-weighted distribution of holes, the least self-overlap period converges to the probability law \(a_q(p)q^{-2p}/K_q\): rare short-period holes account for all limiting fluctuations.
