For \(n\ge3\), the largest number of binary length-\(n\) words with exactly the same multiset of adjacent pairs equals \(\frac{n-1}{h+1}\binom{\lfloor(n-3)/2\rfloor}{h}\binom{\lceil(n-3)/2\rceil}{h}\), with \(h=\lfloor(n-3)/4\rfloor\). Every maximizing spectrum is classified, and the worst-case ambiguity is asymptotic to \(2^{n+1}/(\pi n)\).
