For every nonnegative Dirichlet exponent, the zero of any linear optimal polynomial approximant to a symmetric multiaffine bidisk generator lies outside the sharp radius \(R_\alpha=\sqrt{3^\alpha(3^\alpha/2^\alpha+2^{\alpha+1})/(3^\alpha+4^\alpha)}>1\). The complete extremizing family remains zero-free on the closed unit bidisk for positive exponents, sharply separating bidegree-one stability from known higher-degree counterexamples.
