On every finite positive unit-weight Huygens star, at \(\alpha=\sqrt{\pi^2+2}\) along the diagonal, two distinct prime-two cycles simultaneously undergo supercritical flips, each creating an attracting prime-four orbit in the **full** phase torus. Their exact cubic coefficients are \(\frac12(2\pm\sqrt2)(5\pi^2+2)\), and their local splitting amplitudes have limiting ratio \(\sqrt2-1\).
