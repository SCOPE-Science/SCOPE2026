# Scientific assessment

**Correctness: PASS.** The exact positive-semidefinite compression criterion reduces the kernel to simultaneous bright and dark spectral conditions. The bright quotient has simple nonzero spectrum for every nonzero potential and an explicitly nonzero squared-eigenvector-moment determinant; the two independent dark-cell projectors leave exactly the size-two contrasts. The unmarked case is established separately. An independent exact-rational commutant calculation agrees in 100 parameter profiles, including both potential signs and zero, but those finite cases are supporting checks rather than a proof of generality.

**Originality: PASS, with residual risk.** Foundational exact average-mixing theory [1], the general commutant interpretation [2], the marked balanced bipartite quantum-search diagonalization [3], and unmarked alternative-sampling uniformity [4] were compared at the level of mathematical implications. In particular, [3] supplies spectral data for a balanced special case, not the full arbitrary part-size Cesàro rank classification. A stronger unlocated perturbation theorem or an unpublished result could still subsume the theorem; this bounded search does not prove absolute priority.

**Value: PASS.** Local vertex detuning is a natural model of quantum search and transport disorder. The theorem distinguishes three sharp regimes—rank increase, decrease, and invariance—across all part sizes and all nonzero potential magnitudes, and identifies each surviving dark mode exactly. Such a classification is a structural result about long-time quantum dynamics rather than a finite numerical census or routine reparameterization.

**Scientific limitations:** Only a single potential on a connected unit-weight complete bipartite graph with both parts of size at least two is treated. No instantaneous-mixing, multiple-defect, nonuniform-coupling, or spectral-gap claim is made. The calculation is analytically complete under those hypotheses but has not received an independent audit.
