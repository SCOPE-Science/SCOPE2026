Marking one vertex of \(K_{a,b}\), with \(a,b\ge2\), raises the long-time quantum average-mixing rank by one if the marked part has size two, lowers it by one if that part has size three, and leaves it unchanged otherwise. The exact nullspace consists only of contrasts within two-vertex unmarked cells (plus the corresponding whole-part contrasts without a mark); this holds for every nonzero potential, regardless of magnitude or sign.
