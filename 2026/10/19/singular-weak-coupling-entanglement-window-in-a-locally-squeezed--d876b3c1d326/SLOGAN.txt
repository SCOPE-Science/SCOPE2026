For a pair of harmonic modes with identical phase-locked local squeezed baths, the exact steady-entanglement-optimal coupling is \(J_*=(\gamma/2)\sqrt{1-\tanh(2r)}\). In the joint limit \(r\to\infty\), \(J=\gamma z e^{-2r}\), entanglement survives precisely inside a sharp finite window in \(z\) when the bath occupation satisfies \(2\bar n+1<\sqrt2\); the global logarithmic-negativity supremum is \([1/2-\log_2(2\bar n+1)]_+\).
