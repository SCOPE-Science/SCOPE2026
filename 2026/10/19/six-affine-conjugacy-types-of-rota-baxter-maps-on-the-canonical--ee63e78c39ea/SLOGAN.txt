For the canonical Lie affgebra on the three-dimensional Heisenberg Lie algebra over any field, all affine weight-minus-one Rota–Baxter operators fall into exactly six full affine-conjugacy types. Over \(\mathbb F_q\), their class sizes are \(q\), \(q^3-q\), \(q^3(q+1)\), \(q^2(q+1)\), \((q-1)q^2(q+1)\), and \(1\), for a total of \(2q^4+3q^3+1\).
