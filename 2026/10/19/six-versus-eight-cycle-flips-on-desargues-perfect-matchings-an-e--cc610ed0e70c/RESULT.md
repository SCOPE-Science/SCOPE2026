# Six- versus eight-cycle flips on Desargues perfect matchings: an exact connectivity threshold

## Finding

Let \(D\) be the Desargues graph, identified either with the generalized Petersen graph \(GP(10,3)\) or with the inclusion graph between the ten two-element subsets and ten three-element subsets of \(\{0,1,2,3,4\}\). Let \(\mathcal M(D)\) be its perfect matchings. For \(\ell\in\{6,8\}\), define \(R_\ell(D)\) on \(\mathcal M(D)\) by joining two matchings if their symmetric difference is the edge set of exactly one simple alternating cycle of length \(\ell\). Set \(R_{\le8}(D)=R_6(D)\cup R_8(D)\), with the same vertex set.

There are exactly \(60\) perfect matchings. The shortest-cycle reconfiguration graph \(R_6(D)\) has \(120\) edges and consists of six isolated matchings and one connected component on \(54\) matchings. The connected component is bipartite and biregular: one part has \(30\) vertices of degree \(4\), the other has \(24\) vertices of degree \(5\). Every vertex in this component has eccentricity \(6\), so its radius and diameter are both \(6\).

The octagon-only graph \(R_8(D)\) has \(60\) edges and is isomorphic to \(S(2K_6)\sqcup24K_1\). Here \(2K_6\) is the multigraph with two parallel edges between each pair of its six vertices, and \(S\) subdivides each edge once. Its six degree-ten hubs are exactly the matchings isolated in \(R_6(D)\); its thirty degree-two vertices are exactly the degree-four vertices of \(R_6(D)\). The remaining twenty-four vertices are isolated in \(R_8(D)\).

Adding the octagon flips makes the union graph **connected on all \(60\) states**, with \(180\) edges, radius \(4\), diameter \(4\), and eccentricity exactly \(4\) at every state. Therefore \(8\) is the minimum upper bound on permitted alternating-cycle length that permits reconfiguration between every pair of perfect matchings of \(D\); the bound \(6\) does not suffice.

The natural \(S_5\)-action on the inclusion graph has exactly three matching orbits, of sizes \(6\), \(30\), and \(24\), agreeing with the three \(R_6(D)\)-degree classes. With any fixed ordering of the two bipartition classes, the six frozen matchings have one permutation sign, and the other fifty-four have the opposite sign. Six-cycle flips preserve this sign, while eight-cycle flips reverse it.

## Assumptions and scope

The vertex set of the inclusion graph consists of \(T=\binom{[5]}2\) and \(U=\binom{[5]}3\), where \([5]=\{0,1,2,3,4\}\). A \(T\)-vertex \(t\) is adjacent to a \(U\)-vertex \(u\) precisely when \(t\subset u\). A perfect matching consists of ten edges covering all twenty vertices. Cycles are **simple cycles of the original twenty-vertex graph**, not cycles of the matching-reconfiguration graph; switching all alternating edges around a cycle must leave a perfect matching. We use all simple six- and eight-cycles, with no choice of embedding or facial restriction.

The statements are finite exact results about this specific graph and specified moves. Distances between states in disconnected components are not assigned finite values; the eccentricity-six statement is restricted to the fifty-four-state component of \(R_6(D)\).

## Proof

**1. Exhaustive state and cycle model.** Index the ten members of \(T\) and \(U\) separately in increasing lexicographic order. A perfect matching is a bijection \(p:T\to U\) satisfying \(t\subset p(t)\) for each \(t\in T\). The search in `verify_incidence.py` branches, for each successive \(t\), over **every** currently unused \(u\) containing it. This produces every feasible matching exactly once and terminates after ten levels. It produces \(60\) matchings; an independent subset-bitmask permanent calculation returns the same \(60\). The alternative presentation `verify_gp.py` defines vertices \(0,\ldots,9\) outside and \(10,\ldots,19\) inside, with edges \(i(i+1)\), \(i(10+i)\), and \((10+i)(10+i+3)\) using indices modulo ten where appropriate. Independent recursive matching enumeration gives \(60\) states. The explicit twenty-entry isomorphism in that program proves that the two graph models are identical.

**2. Exact move relation.** Distinct members of \(T\) have at most one common three-subset neighbour, so the incidence graph has no four-cycles. Thus two different matching bijections that disagree on exactly three members of \(T\) differ by a single alternating six-cycle. If they disagree on exactly four, the symmetric difference has eight edges and cannot decompose into two four-cycles, so it is a single alternating eight-cycle. Conversely, a flip of length \(2k\) changes exactly \(k\) matched pairs. This justifies the exhaustive pairwise tests in `verify_incidence.py`. Independently, `verify_gp.py` enumerates **every** simple six-cycle and eight-cycle of the underlying graph by vertex-simple closed walks, obtaining exactly \(20\) and \(30\), respectively. It toggles each alternating cycle against every matching and obtains precisely \(120\) undirected six-flip edges and \(60\) undirected eight-flip edges. Every state and transition is recorded in `state_transition_certificate.json`; an explicit graph-model isomorphism matches the two transition lists edge by edge.

**3. Six-cycle classification.** Counting legal transitions in the complete certified adjacency sets gives degree multiplicities \(0^6,4^{30},5^{24}\). Every six-flip edge joins a degree-four vertex to a degree-five vertex. Breadth-first exploration exhausts all sixty states and produces one connected component of size fifty-four and six singletons. Breadth-first search rooted at **each** active vertex gives exactly two distance profiles: \((1,4,12,16,16,4,1)\) at each of the thirty degree-four vertices and \((1,5,10,20,12,5,1)\) at each of the twenty-four degree-five vertices. Both profiles end at distance six. Hence the large component is biregular and self-centred with radius and diameter six.

**4. Octagon multigraph and the sharp threshold.** The eight-flip degrees are \(10^6,2^{30},0^{24}\), with their degree-ten vertices exactly the preceding six isolated states. Every degree-two octagon vertex meets two distinct hubs; no octagon edge has endpoints outside those two degree classes. Moreover, each pair of hubs has exactly two common degree-two neighbours. This is precisely the one-subdivision of the doubled complete multigraph \(2K_6\), together with twenty-four isolated states. The union of the two move sets has \(180\) edges and degrees \(10^6,6^{30},5^{24}\). Exhaustive breadth-first search from **every** state verifies connectedness and the following distance profiles, for the respective degree classes: \((1,10,25,20,4)\), \((1,6,21,24,8)\), and \((1,5,15,25,14)\). Every profile ends at distance four. There are no even cycles shorter than six in \(D\); six-only moves fail to connect the states, whereas six- and eight-cycle moves connect them all, proving the claimed minimal flip-length cutoff of eight.

**5. Symmetry and parity.** Every permutation of the five ground symbols acts simultaneously on \(T\) and \(U\), preserving incidence, perfect matchings, and cycle lengths. Exhausting all \(120\) such permutations on the full state set gives three matching orbits of sizes \(6\), \(30\), and \(24\). A six-flip replaces a bijection on three domain elements by a three-cycle, so its permutation sign is unchanged; an eight-flip induces a four-cycle and reverses sign. Exhaustive sign computation shows that the six frozen states have one sign and the other fifty-four have the opposite sign. This parity obstruction and the exact eight-flip subdivision give a structural explanation of the reachability transition.

## Verification

All computations are integer and finite. Run `python3 verify_gp.py` and `python3 verify_incidence.py` from the package directory. Both use only Python's standard library and read the included `state_transition_certificate.json`. They independently reconstruct the graph through its generalized-Petersen and middle-level subset representations. The scripts assert the full set of matchings, every labelled edge of both move graphs, component sizes, orbit sizes, degree splits and all rootwise distance profiles. `verify_gp.py` independently enumerates actual cycles before switching; `verify_incidence.py` proves that its permutation-difference tests correspond to single cycles and checks an independent permanent. The stored outputs `verify_gp.out` and `verify_incidence.out` end in `VERIFY_OK`. The certificate stores all sixty matchings and both lists of permitted state transitions, not just numerical digests.

## Relationship to prior work

Dejter's 2009 public preprint studies shortest six-cycles and their oriented arc-incidence constructions for the Desargues and Pappus graphs. It supplies the natural configuration-graph context and counts the twenty shortest cycles, but the inspected text does not define adjacency between **perfect matchings** by alternating six- or eight-cycle flips. Ito, Kakimura, Kamiyama, Kobayashi, and Okamoto (2019) treat alternating-cycle reconfiguration with cycles of **unrestricted** lengths, under which reachability of any two perfect matchings follows directly from decomposing their symmetric difference. That unrestricted observation does not imply the exact shortest permissible cycle length, the six frozen states, the octagon subdivision of \(2K_6\), or the exact distance profiles when only six- and eight-cycles are allowed. Bošković's 2026 treatment focuses on decorated-graph families and associated cycle-length bounds, not this exact graph classification. Known presentations, girth and cycle counts for the Desargues graph and general perfect-matching enumeration are not claimed as new.

## Limitations

This is a computer-assisted exhaustive finite theorem; the proofs of its exact component and distance counts rely on independently specified, fully enumerable finite state spaces and scripts, not a symbolic formula for larger middle-level graphs. The optional group-theoretic explanation uses all permutations of five symbols but does not claim a universal generalization. Equivalence with unpublished or unindexed results remains possible. A recent matching-polynomial table about the Desargues–Levi graph was located but its complete publisher text was temporarily inaccessible; no assertion about the absence of matching-reconfiguration results in that uninspected text is made. Independent external mathematical audit has not yet occurred.
