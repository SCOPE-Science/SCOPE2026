For the Desargues graph, six-cycle flips on its sixty perfect matchings leave six states frozen and connect the other fifty-four in a self-centred, biregular component of diameter six. Octagon-only flips form a subdivided doubled \(K_6\) on thirty-six states; allowing both hexagons and octagons makes all sixty states connected with diameter four, proving that eight is the sharp permissible-cycle-length threshold.
