For the symbolic system with exactly \(m^2\) zeros between successive markers, the marker-activity pressure extends smoothly but not analytically to zero activity: its entire Taylor series is an analytic Jacobi surrogate, while the true pressure exceeds it by exactly \(4P_{\rm an}\exp(-\pi^2/P_{\rm an})(1+o(1))\). The unique equilibrium has marker frequency asymptotic to \((\pi/2)z^2\), making the invisible exponential term an explicit arithmetic signature in a mixing dynamical system.
