A Singer difference-set window is the unique (up to support choice and coefficient phases) best-conditioned full Gabor intensity-measurement window among all windows with the minimum feasible coordinate sparsity \(k\): its ambiguity margin is exactly \(1/k\) and its full condition number is \(k\). Its weakest spectral direction lies at least \(7/8\) of the universal SIC benchmark and approaches that benchmark along infinitely many composite cyclic dimensions.
