For the exact Gaussian state-dependent Newton update on positive-definite quadratics, the critical noise exponent \(\alpha=1/2\) has almost-sure convergence precisely when \(\mathbb E\log(Z^{\mathsf T}AZ/2)<0\). In two dimensions, concentrating the Hessian spectrum at fixed trace can switch almost-sure divergence to convergence even while the expected objective grows at exactly the same exponential rate.
