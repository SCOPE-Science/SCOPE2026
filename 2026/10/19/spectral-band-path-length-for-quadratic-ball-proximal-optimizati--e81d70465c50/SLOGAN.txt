For every positive-definite quadratic, exact Euclidean ball-proximal iterations have total travel at most \(\|x^0\|_2\min\{\sqrt d,2\sqrt{1+\lfloor\log_2\kappa\rfloor}\}\), irrespective of the chosen positive radii. A geometric-spectrum family proves that the square-root spectral-band dependence is necessary in order, turning the length bound into a dimension-independent sharp-order finite-call guarantee for constant radii.
