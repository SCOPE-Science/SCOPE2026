For a positive-definite function supported in a Cartesian product of three-point cyclic neighborhoods, the sharp Turán mass has a **unique** complex maximizer exactly when every cyclic side has odd order; then that maximizer must factor into the canonical one-dimensional functions, and its complete Fourier zero mask is forced by two contact hyperplanes per coordinate. Any even side of order at least four produces a continuum of distinct maximizing functions, although the known scalar tensor-product optimum remains unchanged.
