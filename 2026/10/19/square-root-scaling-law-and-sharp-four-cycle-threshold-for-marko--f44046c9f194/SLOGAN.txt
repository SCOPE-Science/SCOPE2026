For Crank–Nicolson scaling and squaring on a directed Markov cycle, two states need no squaring and three states need at most one, but every fixed squaring count eventually produces a negative transition probability in four or more states. The minimum positivity-restoring count grows as \(\tfrac12\log_2 h+O(1)\), and on four states its exact critical scaled limit solves \(e^q=1+2\sin q\).
