For square-zero matrices of every size \(d\ge3\) over any finite commutative length-two chain ring, the count with residue rank \(a\) is exactly \(|\mathrm{GL}_d(\mathbb F_q)|/[P_a(q)P_{d-2a}(q)]\), independent of the ring's characteristic. At fixed \(q\), the residue rank of a uniformly chosen square-zero matrix approaches the uniform distribution on all admissible ranks in total variation at rate \(O_q(d^{-1})\).
