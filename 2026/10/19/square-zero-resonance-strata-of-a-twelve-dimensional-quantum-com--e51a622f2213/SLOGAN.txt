For \(k\langle x,y\rangle/(x^3,y^4,yx-\lambda xy)\), the reduced square-zero variety has exactly five geometric regimes: generically three six-dimensional components, resonance-induced seven-dimensional components, and a single eight-dimensional affine space in characteristic two at \(\lambda=1\). Its complete Grothendieck class is an explicit polynomial in \(\mathbb L\) in every regime, giving every finite-field square-zero count.
