For every Cantor-like substitution \(1\mapsto1\,0^r1\), \(0\mapsto0^{r+2}\) with integer \(r\ge1\), each full morphic block of generation \(k\ge2\) is exactly \(2^k\)-universal for binary scattered subsequences. Its entire shortest-absent set has exactly nine members when \(r=1\), but exactly four members for every \(r\ge2\); both sets have explicit boundary-word descriptions and their cardinalities stay fixed as \(k\) grows.
