The weighted Bergman moments of the bounded unimodular boundary chirp \(\exp(i/(1-|z|^2))\) decay with the sharp complex factor \(n^{\alpha/2+1/4}\exp[-(1-i)\sqrt{2n}]\), including an explicit nonzero leading constant. Consequently, its radial Toeplitz compression is in every Schatten class despite the symbol having no boundary limit.
