For the exact single-step gain–loss dynamics associated with four-person majority interactions, increasing either independence or unanimity-triggered anticonformity noise strictly decreases **every** nontrivial stationary polarization tail, at every population size \(N\ge6\). The proof gives an all-parameter monotone likelihood-ratio order and identifies six agents as the sharp threshold for irreducibility of the anticonformist gain–loss chain; the claim does not extend to simultaneous four-spin updates.
