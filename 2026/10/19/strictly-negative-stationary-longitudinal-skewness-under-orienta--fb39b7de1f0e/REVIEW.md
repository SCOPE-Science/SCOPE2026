# Review of stationary longitudinal third cumulant under orientational resetting

## Correctness
The stationary angular harmonics equal \(r/(r+n^2)\); generator identities for six coupled moments give a triangular rational system. Its solution yields the explicit third central moment. Every numerator coefficient and denominator factor required for the negative sign is positive. Translational thermal noise cancels in the third cumulant, as expected from independent Gaussian addition. Exact rational checks over 300 parameter combinations agree. The proof requires \(r,\beta,v>0\), \(D\ge0\), deterministic orientation resetting, and a harmonic trap.

## Originality
The 2024 harmonic resetting work of Shee (arXiv:2409.06920v1) resets both position and orientation, explicitly identifying orientation-only trapping as a separate future protocol. The 2024 work of Baouche et al. (arXiv:2405.06769v1) treats the free orientation-reset model and documents negative intermediate-time displacement skewness; neither a free finite-time statistic nor a complete-reset stationary statistic is the trapped orientation-only stationary cumulant derived here. Earlier trapped active-particle work without resetting and free orientation-only protocols were also compared. General angular moment-recursion literature creates a genuine residual priority risk for the exact formula, and absence from indexed results does not prove priority.

## Value
The sign of third-order stationary fluctuations measures a persistent departure from Gaussian symmetry in a protocol physically different from full resetting. The result resolves a natural explicitly identified model extension with one universal inequality and a closed formula valid for all positive physical control parameters. It additionally distinguishes invariance of unstandardized third cumulants under thermal noise from changing standardized skewness, and distinguishes covariance isotropy from distributional symmetry. It does not establish a full density or robust inference under noisy data.
