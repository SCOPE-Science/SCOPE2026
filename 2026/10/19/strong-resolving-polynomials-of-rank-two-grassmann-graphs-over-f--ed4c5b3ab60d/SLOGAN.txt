For every rank-two finite Grassmann graph \(G_q(d,2)\), \(d\ge4\), the complement of a strong resolving set is exactly a pairwise-intersecting family of projective lines; a closed polynomial counts all such sets by size. Its inclusion-minimal strong resolving sets are precisely the complements of point pencils and projective-plane tops, with an unbounded size gap for \(d\ge5\).
