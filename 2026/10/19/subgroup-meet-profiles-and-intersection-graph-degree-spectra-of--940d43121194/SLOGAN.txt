For scalar Frobenius groups \((C_p)^n\rtimes C_m\) with \(n\ge2\) and \(m\mid p-1\), the orders of intersections of *every ordered subgroup pair* have an exact Gaussian–affine profile. It yields the full degree polynomial of the proper-subgroup intersection graph, resolving how both subspace dimension and complement divisor arithmetic determine local graph structure.
