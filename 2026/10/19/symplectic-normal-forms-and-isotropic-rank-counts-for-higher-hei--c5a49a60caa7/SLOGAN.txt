For every odd prime and every rank \(m\ge2\), all group Rota–Baxter maps of the extraspecial Heisenberg group \(H_{2m+1}(\mathbb F_p)\) have one unique symplectic-matrix normal form, and the extreme central characters \(c=0,-1\) have exact rank-stratified counts via isotropic subspaces and surjections. The higher-rank theorem extends the known rank-one classification without assuming invertibility or counting maps only up to equivalence.
