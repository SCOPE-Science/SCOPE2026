The four vertices of a regular tetrahedron independently interpolate all eight trace-free spherical-harmonic Hessian measurements at every exact degree \(\ell\ge4\), with an exact three-branch Gram spectrum of multiplicities \(2,3,3\). At degree \(\ell=3\), the unique invisible harmonic is the cubic \(xyz\); lower-degree ranks are \(0,0,5\).
