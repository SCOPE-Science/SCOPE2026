For the three-dimensional nonassociative algebra \(i^2=j^2=-1\), \(ij=0\), \(ji=t(i+j)\) over any field with \(t\ne0\), the elements having both a nonzero left and a nonzero right annihilator form a union of at most nine explicit lines. Their exact finite-field count has a quadratic-character formula, while in characteristic two it is always \(4q-3\).
