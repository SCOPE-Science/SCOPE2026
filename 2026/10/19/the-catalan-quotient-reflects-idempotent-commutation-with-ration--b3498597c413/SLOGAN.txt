The natural quotient from the type-\(A_n\) 0-Hecke monoid to the Catalan monoid reflects commutation between idempotents, and therefore preserves every pairwise-commuting idempotent configuration. All fixed-arity commuting-tuple enumerators are rational; for ordered triples the series is \((1-2z^2+2z^3)/(1-8z+16z^2-12z^3+2z^4)\).
