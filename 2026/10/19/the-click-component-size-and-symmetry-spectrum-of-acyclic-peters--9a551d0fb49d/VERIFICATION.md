# Executable finite verification

Run `python3 verify_click_petersen.py` in the directory containing the two packaged verification artifacts. The program enumerates exactly 32768 orientations of the 15 edges of \(\operatorname{KG}(5,2)\). Independently it checks acyclicity using Kahn elimination and DFS, evaluates the chromatic polynomial at \(-1\) by all edge-subsets, computes legal source/sink flips, builds the complete 704-component graph, verifies the six fundamental-cycle-signature partition, constructs all 120 automorphism actions, and performs complete breadth-first searches from every one of the 16680 acyclic orientations.

The deterministic output in `verification_output.txt` is the exact record generated by the standalone script. The last line is `VERIFY_OK` and requires all assertions to succeed. The graph is fixed and finite: the certificate does not establish a result for other graphs. Running the program is not a substitute for independent mathematical review.
