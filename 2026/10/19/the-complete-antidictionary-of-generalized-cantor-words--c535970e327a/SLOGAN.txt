Every generalized Cantor fixed point \(\mu_q(1)=1\,0^{q-2}1\), \(\mu_q(0)=0^q\), for \(q\ge3\), has an exactly describable minimal-forbidden-factor language: forbidden zero gaps plus two recursive families built around its substitution blocks. Its forbidden-factor count at length \(n\) is one apart from explicit lacunary exceptions, and its cumulative count is \(n+\log_q n+O_q(1)\).
