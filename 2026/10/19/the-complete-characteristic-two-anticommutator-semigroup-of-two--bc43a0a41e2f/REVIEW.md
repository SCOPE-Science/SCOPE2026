# Scientific review: characteristic-two two-by-two anticommutator semigroup

## Correctness — PASS
The calculation begins with the actual unnormalized product \(AX+XA\), not a formally specialized odd-characteristic Jordan operator. Every parameter and coordinate is uniquely specified, and the composition identity is checked directly. A triple-product reduction proves that arbitrarily long positive words collapse to length two. Both the necessary constraints and explicit preimages for each type of two-factor normal form are supplied, including the degenerate cases where the scalar multiplier vanishes. Alternating-form nondegeneracy establishes the required preimages over even the smallest characteristic-two field. The exceptional one-factor elements are excluded from the two-factor set by simultaneous nonvanishing of both vector parameters. Disjoint counting and image-space analysis give the stated census and ranks. Direct independent matrix computations over three finite fields corroborate each count; only the symbolic argument establishes the universal theorem.

## Originality — PASS, with residual historical risk
The primary motivating preprint, Gogić–Kazalicki–Tomašević, arXiv:2604.17664v1 (April 19, 2026), establishes the full endomorphism semigroup over characteristic different from two. Its characteristic-two observation is strictly weaker: all products kill scalar matrices and have traceless image, but there remain many maps respecting both properties that are absent from the semigroup. The new result gives the **entire** generated semigroup, its rank distribution, exact count over every binary finite field and the sharp word-length collapse. Primary text was inspected at the main theorem, relevant proof sections, the characteristic boundary, finite-field corollary, conclusion and two-by-two appendix.

A related published prescribed-commutator-graph result over two-by-two matrix rings concerns the number of inputs solving a commutator equation; it is not a classification of compositions of the associated commutator operators. Searches using the aliases inner-derivation composition semigroup and unnormalized Jordan multiplication found no dominating statement. An indexed summary, rather than the full text, was available for the commutator-graph record. An older result formulated under alternate Lie-semigroup terminology cannot be excluded, and independent priority verification has not occurred.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Recent complete-generation results make the excluded characteristic-two boundary a natural structural question rather than an arbitrary finite enumeration. The theorem explains the size of the resulting failure of generation and identifies precisely which low-rank transformations remain attainable. The unusually small bound of two generators for every nonempty product is a uniform structural fact, not a small-field observation. An exact polynomial formula and rank spectrum hold over all binary finite fields. The larger-dimensional analog and variants permitting operator addition are outside the claim.
