For any characteristic-two field, every nonempty product of two-by-two symmetrized matrix-multiplication operators admits explicit and unique normal forms and can be shortened to at most two factors. Over \(\mathbb F_q\), the resulting semigroup has exactly \((q-1)q^4+2q^3+q^2-q-1\) elements, with only ranks \(0\), \(1\), and \(2\).
