For every uniform binary morphism \(0\mapsto0^{q-1}1\), \(1\mapsto0^q\) with \(q\ge3\), all minimal absent factors form an explicit palindromic family obtained by repeatedly applying \(u\mapsto\mu_q(u)0^{q-1}\) to finitely many zero-run seeds. Exactly \(2q-2\) forbidden words occur at each substitution scale, with no other forbidden factors.
