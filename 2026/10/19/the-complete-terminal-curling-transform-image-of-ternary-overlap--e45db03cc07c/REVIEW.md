# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The finite transition recurrence preserves exactly the words for which both the source and its direct-prefix curling transform are overlap-free: all new overlaps necessarily end at the newly appended position, and an overlap-free word's next curling number is \(2\) exactly when it ends in a square. The Python symmetry-reduced search and an independently implemented exhaustive C++ search with no symmetry quotient reproduce the same two output strings, the fiber sizes \(4032\) and \(2016\), the \(2016\) fully three-way extendable parents, and the empty length-\(85\) level. All code, input-free execution instructions and actual outputs accompany the proof. Finite enumeration is complete by induction rather than treated as asymptotic evidence.

**Originality — PASS relative to examined evidence.** Caveney–Dong–Shallit, arXiv:2608.15670v1, establish the exact terminal length and the number \(6048\), but do not state the two-value image, common length-\(83\) transform prefix, fiber multiplicities, or terminal three-way branching. Their article text in Sections 2 and 4 and Appendix B was inspected. The older curling-number study (Chaffin–Linderman–Sloane–Wilks, 2013) addresses a different unconstrained sequence-statistics problem. Searches for equivalent image/fiber statements, stronger covering results, tables containing these output profiles and matching mathematical records identified no coverage. A residual risk is that an unindexed record or an unpublished analysis of the original enumerator could already contain these identities.

**Value — PASS.** The transformed image of a natural extremal family is itself a natural object: the known source count \(6048\) conceals an essentially deterministic \(83\)-position transform trace. Identifying its complete image, exact preimage sizes and the unusual three-way freedom at the maximal boundary is a substantive classification of how square-ending events are constrained. The result does not rely on an arbitrary new statistic, a tiny threshold increment, or a merely reproduced count.

**Limits.** The classification is exact for the 84-letter terminal family only. It does not classify all shorter-length transform fibers or assert a general recurrence. All source comparisons are evidence of no covering theorem in the material examined, not proof that unindexed equivalents cannot exist.
