In the two-dimensional Gaussian residual-tracking recursion, the exact almost-sure convergence boundary is \(b=0.937087371920279\ldots\), even though the expected squared error contracts only for \(b<1/2\). The unique fastest almost-sure step is \(b=0.324408517687780\ldots\), not the mean-square-optimal step \(b=1/4\); neither threshold applies automatically to the full noisy algorithm.
