For any two-character cyclic Fourier space, the sharp concentration over all noninterval \(k\)-site observation masks is \((k+R_{N,k})/N\), where \(R_{N,k}^{2}=[\sin(k\pi/N)/\sin(\pi/N)]^{2}-4[\sin^{2}(k\pi/N)-\sin^{2}(\pi/N)]\). Exactly the one-gap arcs attain this runner-up value, giving a strict, explicit spectral gap beneath the previously known interval optimum.
