At the smallest system where independent and neighbor-gated on–off clocks can differ—three sites and two particles—the original zero-range process has strictly more same-site clustering for every positive activation rate. The exact excess is \((c+2)/[(3c+4)(3c^2+7c+3)]\), with a \(1/6\) slow-clock limit and an asymptotic \(1/(9c^2)\) fast-clock decay.
