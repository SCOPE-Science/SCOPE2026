The whole-plane Gingerbreadman map has no primitive odd cycles of periods \(3\), \(7\), or \(9\), but has exactly two at period \(5\) and two at period \(11\); the period-eleven cycles are explicit rational hyperbolic saddles with traces \(\pm5\). Exact integer-only branch elimination checks all \(2^{11}\) possible sign itineraries, including the switching boundary.
