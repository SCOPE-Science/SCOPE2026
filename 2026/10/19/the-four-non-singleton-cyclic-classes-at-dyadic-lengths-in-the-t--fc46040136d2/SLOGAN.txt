For every dyadic length \(N=2^k\) with \(k\ge2\), the Thue–Morse factor language has exactly four cyclic-rotation classes containing more than one distinct factor: one containing all \(N\) rotations of \(\mu^k(0)\), and three containing exactly two factors each. Every other class is a singleton, giving precisely \(2N-8\) singleton classes and refining the previously known aggregate cyclic complexity.
