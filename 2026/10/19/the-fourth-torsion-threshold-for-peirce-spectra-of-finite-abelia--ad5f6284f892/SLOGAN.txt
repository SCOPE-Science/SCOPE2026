For every finite abelian group \(G\), the right Peirce spectrum of its complex core-quandle algebra is exactly \(\{1\}\) when \(2G=0\), \(\{0,1,-1\}\) when there is nontrivial odd torsion but no fourth-order torsion, and all of \(\mathbb C\) as soon as \(G\) has an element of order four. The full complex spectrum arises from an explicit one-parameter family of nonzero idempotents.
