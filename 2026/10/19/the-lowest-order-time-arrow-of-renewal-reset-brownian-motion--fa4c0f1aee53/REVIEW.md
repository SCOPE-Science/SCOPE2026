# Scientific review: time-oriented moments under renewal resetting

**Correctness — PASS.** Under the equilibrium renewal joint age/residual law and independent Gaussian excursions, every trajectory containing a reset between the two observation instants contributes zero to the mixed odd-power contrast. On a no-reset interval, the Gaussian moment calculation gives precisely \(12D^2At\). Integration over the equilibrium age yields \(6D^2t\mathbb E[(T-t)_+^2]/\mathbb ET\), including atomic renewal durations. Ordinary differentiation of the squared positive-part transform proves the right-second-derivative inversion. The recovery of diffusivity uses its exact initial slope and moments of the already recovered duration law; the positivity condition and minimal polynomial degree are checked separately. The hierarchy follows from a finite binomial/Gaussian moment expansion. The standalone exact checker independently reconstructs all coefficients for small integer orders, but is not used as a substitute for the universal argument.

**Scientific value — PASS.** The result identifies the first two-time polynomial statistic that can distinguish forward from backward trajectories when all one-time odd moments vanish. It quantifies this irreversibility at every observable lag, converts it into a squared residual-duration transform, reconstructs the complete hidden reset-duration law and diffusivity from one oriented observable, and extends positivity across a natural mixed-moment hierarchy. This is a motivated non-equilibrium measurement/inverse problem, not a special-parameter calculation. The known covariance carries related inferential information, so no extra information over complete second-order data is claimed. Finite-sample stability is unproved.
