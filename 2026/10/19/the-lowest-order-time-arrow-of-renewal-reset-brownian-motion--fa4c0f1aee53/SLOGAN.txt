For stationary Brownian motion with arbitrary independent complete renewal resetting, the lowest-order directional two-time moment is the fourth-order contrast \(\Delta(t)=6D^2t\mathbb E[(T-t)_+^2]/\mathbb ET\), strictly positive exactly below the maximum reset interval. Its normalized second lag derivative recovers the entire renewal survival function; with its short-lag amplitude it also determines the unknown diffusivity. Higher even-order contrasts have explicit positive renewal-moment formulas.
