The 120 undirected Hamiltonian paths of the Petersen graph form one simply transitive \(\mathfrak S_5\)-orbit, and elementary Pósa rotations at either endpoint give precisely the Cayley graph with generators \((1\,2\,3\,4)\), \((1\,4\,3\,2)\), \((1\,4)\), and \((0\,1)(3\,4)\). This connected four-regular graph has diameter six and exact distance-sphere profile \((1,4,11,25,39,29,11)\) about each of its 120 vertices.
