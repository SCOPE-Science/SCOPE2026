**Correctness — PASS.** The characteristic radical argument identifies ordinary automorphisms as \(\mathrm{GL}_n(q)\). The two-point condition forces homogeneity and projective-point injectivity; conversely the dependent/independent pair split supplies witnesses by basis extension. Finite projective-point permutation and independent scalings give the wreath group and factorial count. The sharp rigidity exceptions follow by fixed-space size bounds. The standalone checker corroborates three small cases without replacing proof.

Earlier 2024 filiform associative two-local results also concern different multiplication types. An inspected Jordan-algebra paper imposes symmetry restrictions on the witnessing matrices and does not entail the unrestricted projective permutation count. Targeted published-finding corpus and external searches found no matching theorem. However, a general older equivalent description of pairwise-interpolable linear group actions could dominate this claim; search failure does not establish priority. One close filiform source was inspected only to abstract level.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The theorem computes every nonlinear two-local automorphism of an infinite natural local algebra family, including the complete group structure, exact counts for every finite field and dimension, and necessary and sufficient global rigidity cases. It specifies a meaningful obstruction rather than merely producing one exceptional map.

The theorem does not address infinite base fields, nonsplit local algebras, higher radical powers, or ring automorphisms that do not fix the coefficient field.
