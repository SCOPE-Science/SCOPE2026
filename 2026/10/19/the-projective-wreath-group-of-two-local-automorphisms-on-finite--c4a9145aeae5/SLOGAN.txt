For the split local algebra \(A=\mathbb F_q\oplus V\) with \(V^2=0\), the entire group of nonlinear two-local automorphisms is a projective-line permutation/scaling wreath product, of exact order \((q-1)^N N!\), where \(N=(q^{\dim V}-1)/(q-1)\). Every two-local automorphism is global only when \(\dim V=1\) or \((q,\dim V)=(2,2)\).
