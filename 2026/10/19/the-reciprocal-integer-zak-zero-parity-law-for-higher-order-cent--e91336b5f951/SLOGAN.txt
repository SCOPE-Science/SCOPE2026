For every centered cardinal B-spline of order \(n\ge3\) at Zak scale \(1/m\), the zero set consists of the \(m-1\) horizontal lines \(\nu=j/m\), together with just one isolated half-period zero when \(m\) is odd; no other zeros occur. An exact dilation–Zak factorization yields the parity law and order-\(n\) vanishing along the lines.
