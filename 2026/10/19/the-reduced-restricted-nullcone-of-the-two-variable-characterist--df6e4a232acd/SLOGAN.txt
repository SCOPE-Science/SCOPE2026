The reduced restricted square-zero locus of \(\operatorname{Der}_k(k[x,y]/(x^2,y^2))\) in characteristic two is a geometrically irreducible rational fourfold. Over every \(\mathbb F_q\) of characteristic two it has exactly \(q^4+q^3-q\) points, with linear-operator ranks \(0,1,2\) occurring respectively \(1\), \(q^3+q^2-q-1\), and \(q^4-q^2\) times.
