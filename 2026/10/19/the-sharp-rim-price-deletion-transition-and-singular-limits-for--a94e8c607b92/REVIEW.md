# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

For simple wheels with rim size \(n\ge3\), positive prices and budget, the strict-convexity proof applies to every independently variable edge, so orbit-constant weights follow from uniqueness instead of being imposed. The exact wheel spectrum reduces the objective to a strictly convex scalar reciprocal sum. The first derivative at zero gives the sharp deletion condition, and the second derivative gives the nonzero critical slope. Compactness and a divergence estimate justify minimizer convergence under the Riemann-sum limit; a separate fixed-order asymptotic gives the square-root cheap-rim law. A standalone independent matrix checker passed 187 finite tests. The infinite assertions do not rely on finite sampling.

## Originality — PASS with acknowledged residual risk

The 2006/2008 work of Ghosh, Boyd, and Saberi already proves the general convex framework and treats wheels explicitly when spoke and rim conductance prices are equal. This overlap is explicitly excluded from the novelty claim. Its wheel section does not cover the free rim-price threshold, the closed critical derivative, or the two singular large-order pricing limits. Published mathematical-result searches did not identify an equivalent statement. Recent broadly related cost-allocation preprints could not be fully inspected, so exact priority is not certified.

## Value — PASS

Different unit construction costs are intrinsic to a wheel's radial and circumferential links. The theorem answers the substantive design question of exactly when a network with available redundant ring edges should discard all of them, for every order and budget, not merely how to numerically optimize weights. The finite-to-infinite threshold shift and the noncommuting order-of-limits phenomenon show that extrapolating small wheels or cheap-rim asymptotics gives qualitatively misleading design rules. This is a sharp structural classification motivated by a cited optimization problem, not a restatement of its equal-price instance.

## Limits

The result is restricted to simple wheels and two uniform price classes. One self-review has been completed; no independent audit is claimed. Earlier general convexity and equal-price wheel results remain prior work. Broader unindexed sources could contain an equivalent price-transition theorem.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
