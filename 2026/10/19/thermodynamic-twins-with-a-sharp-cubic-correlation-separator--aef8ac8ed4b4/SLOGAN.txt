The explicit nonconjugate interval maps from Fuhrmann--Gröger--Passeggi have identical invariant measures, entropy for every stationary measure, and pressure for every continuous potential; their length-\(n\) orbit distributions differ by the exact sharp Wasserstein amount \((1-3^{-\lfloor n/2floor})/(4n)\) in the worst case. Nevertheless, a common stationary distribution separates the maps through cubic lag-one autocorrelation by \(-1/138240\), whereas all quadratic observables give zero difference.
