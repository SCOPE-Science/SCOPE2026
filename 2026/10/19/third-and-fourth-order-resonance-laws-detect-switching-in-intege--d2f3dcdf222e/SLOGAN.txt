For every prescribed sequence of integer expanding circle maps, the third and fourth moments of a finite cosine orbit sum obey exact resonance laws involving the numbers of doubling steps, tripling steps and consecutive doubling pairs. When switches are restricted to multipliers \(2\) and \(3\), the third moment and fourth cumulant together recover the doubling count and its adjacent-pair count, with a sharp gap-free spectrum at every length and fixed doubling count.
