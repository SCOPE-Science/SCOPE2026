For the cyclic Latin square of order \(9\), two transversals can be connected by swaps changing exactly three selected cells at a time if and only if their column-permutation and symbol-permutation signs agree. The \(2025\) transversals split into four such components of sizes \(486,486,486,567\), whereas at order \(7\) the same move leaves \(21\) transversals isolated.
