For the nilradical of a three-block parabolic of sizes \((r,1,s)\), with \(r,s\ge2\), the reduced variety of commuting \(t\)-tuples has exactly three geometrically irreducible components for every \(t\ge2\), and is equidimensional only at \((r,s,t)=(2,2,3)\). A closed formula counts every labeled commuting tuple over every finite field.
