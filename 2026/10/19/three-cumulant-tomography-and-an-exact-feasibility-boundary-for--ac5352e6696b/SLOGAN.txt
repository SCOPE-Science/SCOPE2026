For a thermally diffusing run-and-tumble particle in a harmonic trap, its stationary variance, fourth cumulant and sixth cumulant uniquely recover propulsion scale, tumble-to-trap ratio and bath variance; explicit inequalities characterize exactly which triples are physically attainable. The variance and fourth cumulant alone leave infinitely many parameter choices, making the sixth the first additional even cumulant that resolves the ambiguity.
