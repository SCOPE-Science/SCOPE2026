For every finite commutative principal ideal ring, the annihilator-inclusion graph has an exact multiplicative Wiener polynomial: apart from a disconnected two-field boundary, its distance-three pairs are exactly opposite Boolean valuation corners, numbering \((2^{r-1}-1)|R^\times|\). In particular, the graph for \((\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z)^2\) has diameter three, not the previously reported four.
