For every unit-weight Huygens star with at least two leaves, the invariant diagonal has exactly one attracting two-cycle when \(2<c<\pi\), then exactly two attracting opposite-phase two-cycles when \(\pi<c<\sqrt{\pi^2+2}\); the symmetric predecessor becomes repelling. All diagonal period-two states in this range and all their full-torus Floquet multipliers are classified explicitly.
