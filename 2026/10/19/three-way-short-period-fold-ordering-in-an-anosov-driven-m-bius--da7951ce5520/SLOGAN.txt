For the Chigarev–Kazakov–Pikovsky cat-driven Möbius map at coupling \(\varepsilon=2/5\), the first fold among all base-period-one and base-period-two fibers switches exactly twice as the forcing phase varies: from one primitive two-cycle, to the other, to the fixed base orbit. Both switch phases have unique analytic definitions and explicit linear weak-forcing asymptotics; the statement does not rank higher-period folds.
