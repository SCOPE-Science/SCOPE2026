In the Cantor word, length-\(3^k\) factors with a fixed positive number \(j\) of ones form exactly one cyclic class when \(j\) is a power of two, and exactly two otherwise; the zero factor contributes one more. Thus its cyclic complexity is exactly \(c_C(3^k)=2^{k+1}-k\) for every \(k\ge0\), with the discrepancy determined by palindromic prefixes of the dyadic valuation gap sequence.
