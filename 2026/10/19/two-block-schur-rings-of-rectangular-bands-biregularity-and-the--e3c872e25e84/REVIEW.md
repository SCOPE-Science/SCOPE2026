# Review

## Correctness — PASS
The new equivalence follows from an exact product coefficient calculation and a row/column degree dichotomy. Automorphicity is tested using the complete side-preserving automorphism group. The lower-size rigidity proof separately handles a short side of size at most three and the only remaining smaller rectangles. The displayed \(4\times6\) matrix proves failure; a classification of all loopless 3-regular multigraphs on four vertices gives \(968\) total two-block partitions, \(428\) automorphic and \(540\) non-automorphic. The included finite checker supports the construction and census without substituting experiments for the arbitrary-size proof.

## Originality — PASS, with bibliographic limitations
The closest primary paper is Marrow–Misseldine, arXiv:2601.09940, which introduces semigroup Schur rings, discusses automorphic constructions and rectangular-band direct products, and enumerates semigroups only up to order seven. It does not provide this two-block classification or its threshold. A related classification for two-row rectangular bands guarantees automorphicity only in that smaller family and does not cover the displayed \(4\times6\) obstruction. Targeted statement, graph-equivalence, stronger-coverage and exact-table searches did not reveal a covering theorem. Unindexed equivalents remain possible; search failure is not proof of uniqueness.

## Value — PASS
A natural rigidity question for the new semigroup Schur-ring framework has an exact threshold within the two-block rectangular-band setting. Biregularity classifies the infinite family, while the sharp first obstruction shows that non-automorphic partitions occur already at \(24\) elements and comprise \(540\) out of \(968\) two-block partitions there. This materially clarifies the limits of automorphic constructions.

## Limitations
The theorem does not classify partitions with three or more blocks or claim an absolute first non-automorphic Schur ring among all semigroups. The arithmetic census is exact, but no independent audit or exhaustive literature certification has occurred.
