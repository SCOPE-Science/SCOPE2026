For rectangular bands, a partition into two Schur blocks is exactly a row cut, a column cut, or an edge/nonedge partition of a biregular bipartite graph. The first non-automorphic two-block examples occur at \(24\) semigroup elements: the \(4\times6\) band has \(968\) such partitions, of which exactly \(540\) are non-automorphic.
