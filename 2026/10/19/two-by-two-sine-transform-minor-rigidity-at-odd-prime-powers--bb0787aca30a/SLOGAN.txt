For every odd prime power \(p^k\) with \(p\ge5\), every singular two-by-two minor of the inversion-odd cyclic sine transform arises from one of three explicit modular row, column, or zero-product obstructions; no additional cyclotomic cancellations occur. At every odd prime square, the exact number of singular minors is \((p-1)^2(p-3)(8p^2-7p-3)/64\), including zero at order nine despite a zero matrix entry.
