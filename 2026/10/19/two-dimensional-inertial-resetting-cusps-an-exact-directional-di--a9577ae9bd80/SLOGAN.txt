Two-dimensional inertial Brownian particles restarted with zero velocity have an exact \(s^{-4/3}\) stationary spatial cusp whose first anisotropic correction is a propulsion-driven \(s^{-1}\) dipole. Restarting with Maxwellian velocity instead produces an isotropic \(s^{-1}\) cusp; mixing the two protocols gives an exact cubic mixture crossover and a small-disk directional-bias law.
