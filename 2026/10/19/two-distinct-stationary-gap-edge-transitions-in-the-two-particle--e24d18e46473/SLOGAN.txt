Two logarithmically repelling run-and-tumble particles confined by a harmonic trap have distinct small-gap and large-gap stationary boundary exponents, \(\theta_a-1=(\gamma/\lambda)(1-v/h)-1\) and \(\theta_b-1=(\gamma/\lambda)(1+v/h)-1\), with \(h=\sqrt{v^2+2\lambda g}\). Their two sharp tumbling-rate transitions straddle the confining rate, and the sum of the edge exponents is independent of the repulsive coupling.
