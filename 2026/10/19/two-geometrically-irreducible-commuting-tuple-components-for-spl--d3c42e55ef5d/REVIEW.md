# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. Zorn multiplication and the nondegenerate trace-zero norm give an exact centralizer dichotomy. At a null element, the commutator reduces to a nondegenerate alternating form on a two-dimensional quotient; hence commuting tuples span at most two imaginary dimensions. The null quadric and its projective-line fibers establish irreducibility and component dimensions. Unique projective directions or planes give disjoint finite-field counting strata, including the all-arity formula. Exact exhaustive finite checks corroborate the algebra in odd prime fields of sizes three and five; these are not substituted for the proof.

## Originality
PASS, subject to explicit residual risk. The closest general primary works are Grishkov–Merlini Giuliani–Zavarnitsine's finite-field classification and counts of **unital subalgebras**, and Knarr–Stroppel's all-field subalgebra classification and local centralizer results. The latter full text was inspected, and the component theorem for the global variety of all ordered commuting tuples and its all-arity span-refined count was not located there. The 2010 work could not be inspected in full, and it may implicitly make some finite-field counting steps available. Exact-result searches did not find an equivalent or dominating theorem; lack of a match is not a uniqueness certificate. Classical split Cayley hexagon incidence and local centralizer structure are acknowledged rather than claimed.

## Value
PASS. The theorem determines the previously unassembled component structure and arithmetic of a canonical exceptional composition algebra across all tuple lengths. The non-equidimensional two-component phenomenon distinguishes degenerations of proportional nonisotropic tuples from null commuting planes; the geometric split-Cayley-hexagon interpretation accounts for the unexpectedly simple two-term count. No general theorem outside this canonical algebra is asserted.

## Limits
Characteristic two excluded, and all component statements describe reduced supports only. Specialized equivalent literature remains possible, particularly the earlier subalgebra enumeration paper. Independent validation has not occurred.
