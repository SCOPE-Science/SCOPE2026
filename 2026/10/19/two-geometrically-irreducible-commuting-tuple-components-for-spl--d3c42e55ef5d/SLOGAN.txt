For odd finite fields, ordered commuting tuples in the split octonions have a reduced parameter space with exactly two irreducible components, of dimensions \(2s+6\) and \(3s+5\) for every \(s\ge2\). Their exact number is \([6]_q q^{3s}-q[5]_q q^{2s}\), explained by the null directions and commuting planes of the split Cayley hexagon.
