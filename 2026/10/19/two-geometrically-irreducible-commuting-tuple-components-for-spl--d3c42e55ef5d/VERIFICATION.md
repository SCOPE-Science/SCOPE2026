# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Scalar directions split off in odd characteristic. For a nonisotropic pure element v, subtract the v-component from a commuting w so that the remainder is orthogonal to v; the anticommutator then vanishes, so commutation implies the product vanishes, and alternativity and v²=−N(v) force the remainder to be zero. The rational null case is covered by the explicit nilpotent centralizer and its conjugacy over the base field in Knarr–Stroppel §3, including characteristic three. In the Zorn model for a null direction, the pure kernel is a null three-space and its bracket induces a nondegenerate alternating pairing on the two-dimensional quotient by v with values in kv. Pairwise commuting vectors therefore span at most two pure dimensions. On the five-dimensional null quadric this constant-rank kernel modulo the tautological line is a rank-two bundle, whose projectivization is the flag of incident null lines and commuting planes. The flag is also a projective-line bundle over the closed plane locus, giving geometric irreducibility and dimension five of that locus. The rank-one determinantal locus and the proper image of the tautological tuple bundle are irreducible, neither contains the other, and rank two determines a unique plane. They have dimensions 2s+6 and 3s+5 including scalars; their intersection is the null-direction rank-one cone with scalar coordinates, dimension 2s+5. Over the finite field the affine null equation has q⁶ solutions including zero: for each nonzero upper vector and each diagonal entry it has q² lower vectors, and for zero upper vector it has q³ choices with zero diagonal. Dividing by q−1 after removing zero gives [6]_q projective points; double counting the flag gives equally many planes. Counting the unique rank-one direction or rank-two plane and its spanning column matrices gives the polynomial. The modular script checks ranks and counts over small fields and is not a proof of the geometry.
