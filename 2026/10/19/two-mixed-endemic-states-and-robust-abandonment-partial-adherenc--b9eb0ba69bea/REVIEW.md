# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The endemic-reduction polynomial has degree at most two for the full parameter range, and it cannot vanish identically. The rational example has two simple roots certified in rational intervals inside the endemic domain. The boundary state is a stable endemic equilibrium. The mixed-state Jacobian identities and strict Routh–Hurwitz margins certify one mixed saddle and one mixed sink; these margins and nondegenerate roots persist under sufficiently small perturbations. The accompanying SymPy checker independently recomputes algebraic certificates. The proof does not infer global dynamics from finite sampling.

**Originality — PASS, subject to residual literature risk.** Full text, model equations, figures and supplementary equilibrium/stability sections of Kumata–Ito–Yamamichi (2026) were read. That paper supplies the basic feedback reduction and Jacobian and describes other types of bistability, but does not establish the combined sharp two-mixed-state maximum and stable abandonment–stable partial-adherence construction. Saad-Roy–Traulsen (2023) provides the earlier adherence SIRS setting, without this protection/conformity mechanism. Relevant indexed research findings concern distinct SIR noncompliance thresholds, media-sensitive multiple equilibria, or general feedback systems, none logically subsuming this parameter-certified sharp construction. The possibility of unindexed equivalent results, and the abstract-only assessment of the Martin–Castella–Hamelin (2026) mask model, are retained as risks.

**Value — PASS.** This resolves a natural equilibrium-multiplicity question for a recently introduced coupled behavioral–epidemic mechanism and proves a qualitatively distinct stable-endemic coexistence pattern. The example shows that reducing intervention adherence can be either a trap at zero or self-sustaining at a positive fraction without changing epidemiological parameters; the intervening saddle establishes local separation. The contribution is a sharp finite algebraic classification plus a nontrivial three-dimensional stability certificate, not simply a new name for known bistability. No real-world predictive power or global basin theorem is claimed.
