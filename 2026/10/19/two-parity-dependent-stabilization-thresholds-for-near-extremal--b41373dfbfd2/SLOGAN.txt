The one-swap graph of sum-free sets of size \(\lceil n/2\rceil-1\) has exactly two components for odd \(n\ge15\) and even \(n\ge20\), with fully explicit clique-and-attachment structures and edge counts. One component contains all near-maximum all-odd sets, and no sum-free sequence of fixed-cardinality swaps can cross to the other; the parity thresholds are sharp.
