The Gaussian limit of a nearly classical interpolating quantum walk conceals two rare-displacement scales: at \(|x|\asymp\sqrt{r/q}\) its density gains a universal \(I_0(qx^2/r)\) factor, while at \(|x|\asymp\sqrt r/q\) a second universal suppression \(e^{-q^2x^2/r}\) emerges. Explicit multiplicative bounds and a uniform far-tail formula quantify both crossovers for the exact fixed-parameter weak-limit law.
