# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The stationary generator identities give each distinct squared-position joint moment exactly. Substitution produces the stated third connected cumulant. For all N at least ten, explicit positivity certificates prove the global comparisons with all batch sizes four through N minus two. Exact endpoint identities handle sizes N minus one and N; a quadratic with its upper root between 73 and 74 decides the competition between batches of sizes two and three. Direct rational comparison covers the seven smaller populations. The separate `verify.py` checks polynomial identities and rational examples, and does not substitute finite enumeration for the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS

The closest source is de Mauro, Majumdar and Schehr (arXiv:2601.20077): it introduces exactly this batch-reset process and a general stationary Fourier recursion and analyzes pair connected correlations. Its general recursion already provides a route to the third-moment calculation. The new claimed result is specifically the complete *global maximizing batch-size classification* for the third connected correlation; the source optimizes a different, pairwise objective with a different transition. Full openly available source material, including supplements, and the related overview arXiv:2603.03162 were compared; targeted published-finding corpus source, claim, alias and threshold searches found no equivalent theorem or larger result clearly implying it. Exact search queries and the nearest records are in AUDIT.json. These checks do not establish publication-level uniqueness; hidden implicit treatment or unindexed literature remains a risk.

## Value — PASS

The connected third cumulant captures irreducible three-particle position-square fluctuations beyond the pairwise covariance studied in existing theory. Two distinct sharp thresholds at populations ten and seventy-four create a complete natural physical phase diagram for optimizing the next-order observable over the entire integer batch-size range, a substantive distinction from the source's single pair-correlation threshold.

## Limits

Only independent Brownian particles, a uniform fixed-size batch, common Poisson reset clock, and the unnormalized stationary connected third cumulant of three distinct squared coordinates are covered. The result does not claim new general moment closure, nonuniform-batch theory, higher-cumulant thresholds, or independent experimental validation.
