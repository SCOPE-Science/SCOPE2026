In a Brownian gas with random fixed-size batch resetting, the stationary connected correlation of three distinct squared displacements has a unique optimal batch size: \(m=N-1\) for \(3\le N\le9\), \(m=3\) for \(10\le N\le73\), and \(m=2\) for \(N\ge74\). These two exact population-size transitions are distinct from the previously known pair-correlation transition.
