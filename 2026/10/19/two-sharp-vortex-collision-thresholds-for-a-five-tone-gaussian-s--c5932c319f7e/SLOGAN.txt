Five equally spaced, equal-amplitude tones have exactly four Gaussian short-time Fourier zeros per time period, with precisely two sharp collision gaps \(\Delta_\mathrm{A}=\sqrt{\log2/(2\pi)}\) and \(\Delta_\mathrm{B}=\sqrt{\log(1+\sqrt5)/(2\pi)}\). The four zeros move from the central-frequency axis through an off-axis rectangle to the half-time line; at either transition two pairs merge into exactly two double vortices.
