For each fixed shifted-Legendre cutoff \(d\ge2\), centered compressions of chaotic logistic iterates have exactly two leading eigenvalues of order \(4^{-n}\) and exactly \(\min(2,d-1)\) secondary eigenvalues of order \(64^{-n}\); the rest are \(o(64^{-n})\). Endpoint values generate the leading rank-two profile, while endpoint derivatives and a Schur-complement correction of \(21/16\) determine the second scale.
