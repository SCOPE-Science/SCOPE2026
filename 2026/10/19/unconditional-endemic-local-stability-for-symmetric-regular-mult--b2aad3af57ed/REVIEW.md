# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The positive homogeneous equilibrium is obtained by explicit substitution. Orthogonal diagonalization of the common symmetric contact matrix reduces every spatial perturbation to a three-dimensional Jacobian block. The exact characteristic polynomial has three positive coefficients; a direct positive expression for its Routh–Hurwitz determinant at the homogeneous mode, together with a strictly positive derivative across the transverse-mode parameter, proves stability for all network eigenvalues. Symbolic determinant identities were executed and independently evaluated examples include vaccination exceeding both recovery rates. A finite numerical test is not treated as an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS, with priority risk.** The inspected general multi-group SAIRS paper establishes a sufficient local endemic-stability criterion requiring additional parameter bounds. The older one-community work establishes the spatially uniform special case. Neither statement implies stability of every nonuniform symmetric-network eigenmode with arbitrarily high vaccination. Searches for graph spectral reduction, proportional contact matrices, aliases and broader coverage, including published research records, did not identify a claim establishing this joint unrestricted conclusion. A broad network epidemic theorem not captured by the inspected sources could still imply it.

**Value — PASS.** Stability against network-heterogeneous perturbations is epidemiologically and mathematically distinct from homogeneous one-group stability. The network spectral argument eliminates the motivating paper's strong vaccination-vs-recovery assumptions for a natural, infinite class of regular undirected contact networks, giving a motivated, parameter-uniform result rather than a numerical slice.

**Limitations.** No global convergence, directed-network theorem, non-proportional asymptomatic/symptomatic contact layers, or heterogeneous-rate conclusion is established. Independent scientific validation has not occurred.
