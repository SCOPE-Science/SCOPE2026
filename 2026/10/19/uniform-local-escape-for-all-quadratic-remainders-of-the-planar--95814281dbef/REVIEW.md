# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The two branch matrices cancel the determinant-dependent terms when the coordinates are summed. The exact identity for \(\Phi=x+y-\lambda y^2\), the three-variable quadratic remainder bound, and the explicit small-ball choice give a strictly positive \(\mu/2\) drift. The bounded range of \(\Phi\) on the ball forces exit within \(\lfloor4K/\mu\rfloor+1\) iterates. This is an analytic proof for all permitted coefficients, not a finite experiment. The standalone exact rational checker independently exercises four determinant-sign patterns and 5,968 sample states.

## Originality

**PASS, with priority uncertainty.** The closest direct precedent is D. J. W. Simpson, *Nonsmooth folds as tipping points*, §§3–4 and Appendix A, Proposition 6 (arXiv:2406.04587). That paper proves divergence of the homogeneous truncation and a single quadratic nonlinear example with the restrictions \(\delta_L>0\), \(\delta_R<0\); it explicitly leaves the general higher-order problem open. The present statement covers *every* compatible quadratic-order remainder in *both* coordinates, with arbitrary real determinant parameters and a uniform quantitative exit bound. It is not a restatement of the cited special quadratic formula. The general conjecture remains open, and possible unpublished extensions are an acknowledged risk.

The related 2024 Simpson–Glendinning paper studies persistence of chaotic attractors through some border collisions; its abstract does not establish this virtual-fixed-point, arbitrary-remainder local escape statement. Literature and mathematical-record searches found no claim dominating the present theorem; absence of a database hit is not a proof of absolute priority.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** This is a mathematically motivated partial resolution of the specifically posed higher-order nonsmooth-fold conjecture, covering an infinite-dimensional class of \(C^2\) perturbations instead of a single worked polynomial and removing both determinant sign conditions. It produces a uniform local no-invariant-set conclusion and an explicit parameter-dependent exit-time upper bound. The correction isolates the transverse quadratic obstruction that prevents the source's linear drift from persisting under nonlinear terms.

## Limits

The theorem requires the two-dimensional companion-matrix structure and a uniform quadratic remainder bound. It does not settle the source's unrestricted piecewise-\(C^1\), arbitrary-matrix conjecture, establish a globally divergent orbit, or determine a sharp exit-time asymptotic. Independent priority and proof review remains outstanding.
