In the planar companion-form nonsmooth fold, every orbit started sufficiently close to the collision escapes that neighborhood when the switching parameter is positive, uniformly for all two real determinant parameters and all quadratically small compatible perturbations. A quadratic Lyapunov correction proves a finite \(O(\mu^{-1})\) upper bound on the first exit time, extending a published single-perturbation example without resolving the unrestricted nonlinear conjecture.
