For every Cartesian product of complete graphs with arbitrary alphabet sizes, the symmetric fastest-mixing Markov chain is unique even when every edge probability can be chosen independently: its coordinate-edge weights are \(t/q_i\), where \(t=\min\{2/(d+1),1/\sum_i(1-1/q_i)\}\). The sharp holding transition is \(\sum_i1/q_i>(d-1)/2\), and adding an arbitrary alphabet to \(d-1\) binary coordinates leaves the optimal SLEM fixed at \((d-1)/(d+1)\).
