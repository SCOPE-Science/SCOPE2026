For \(\mathbb F_q[\varepsilon;\sigma]/(\varepsilon^2)\), the entire spectrum of unit-conjugacy class sizes depends only on the order of the fixed field of \(\sigma\), even though the ring isomorphism type remembers \(\sigma\) itself. Over \(\mathbb F_{32}\), two Frobenius twists yield rings that are neither isomorphic nor anti-isomorphic yet have identical conjugacy spectra, commuting probabilities, and zero-divisor graphs.
