For a ferromagnetic Ising chain with iid continuous positive bonds, configurations stable against every simultaneous flip of at most \(r\) spins are exactly arbitrary domain-wall subsets of radius-\(r\) coupling minima. Their almost-sure entropy density is \(\log2/(2r+1)\), with a universal explicit Gaussian fluctuation variance and a two-point metastability count when the cooperative budget is proportional to chain length.
