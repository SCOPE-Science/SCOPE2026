At zero invasion growth in a periodically pulsed competitive flow with aligned interactions, every positive trajectory converges to resident-only persistence while the rare species decays as an explicit phase-dependent multiple of \(1/n\). The dimensionless per-cycle competitive load satisfies the universal limit \(n\int_{nT}^{(n+1)T}g(t)y(t)\,dt\to1\), and its cumulative load is \(\log n\) plus an explicit constant.
