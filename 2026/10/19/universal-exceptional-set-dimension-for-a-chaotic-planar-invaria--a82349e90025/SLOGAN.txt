For the chaotic planar invariant graphs of \(F_b(x,y)=(|x|-y-1,x-|y|+b)\) throughout \(2\le b\le4\), the set that never reaches a constant graph edge has the universal Hausdorff dimension \(\log(1+\sqrt2)/\log4\). Three exact first-return contractions have ratios \(1/4,1/4,1/16\), proving that plateau preimages occupy full graph arclength even though the map has positive topological entropy.
