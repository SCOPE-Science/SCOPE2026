For the first rank-one Krylov projection of a stable symmetric Lyapunov equation, both pseudo-minimal-residual and exact minimal-residual extraction strictly reduce Frobenius residuals while strictly increasing true Frobenius solution error compared with Galerkin, unless the forcing vector is already an eigenvector. A two-eigenvalue family makes the pseudo-minimal-residual solution error arbitrarily large relative to Galerkin even though the residual improves by a fixed factor.
