For critical polynomial sampling at two regular concentric \(n\)-gons, half-step angular staggering is the unique simultaneous optimizer of determinant, least eigenvalue, and condition number for every positive radial monomial weighting. In the finite Hardy space, the optimal squared condition number still grows like \(2r^{-2n+2}\) at fixed interior radius, but has an explicit finite limit when \(r=e^{-c/n}\).
