For a fixed positive sum of two centered Gaussians of distinct widths, the self-ambiguity function has \(\frac{(b-a)^2}{a+b}T^2+O(1)\) positive real Doppler zeros at delay \(T\to\infty\), independent of the positive mixing weight. All such large-delay zeros lie in a narrow widening wedge \(aT+O(T^{-1})<\nu<bT+O(T^{-1})\), with two simple zeros in each interior negative interference lobe.
