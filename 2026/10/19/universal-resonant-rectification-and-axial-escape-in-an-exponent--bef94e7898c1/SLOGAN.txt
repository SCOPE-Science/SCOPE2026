In a precisely bounded weak-forcing regime of the natural-exponential Duffing lift, every base orbit synchronizes with one anti-periodic response, while the added coordinate has an initial-condition-independent ballistic velocity whose sign is exactly that of the exponential-tilt parameter (up to the axial coupling sign). At exact linear resonance its drift is \(\alpha\beta\varepsilon^2/2+\alpha\beta^3\varepsilon^4/16+O(\varepsilon^6)\), and the cubic Duffing stiffness cancels from the entire fourth-order term.
