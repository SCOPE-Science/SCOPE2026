For every fixed \(0<\nu\le1\), the scaled exponential starlikeness and convexity radii of all six Jackson/Hahn–Exton \(q\)-Bessel normalizations have universal explicit limits with strictly positive first corrections: order \(q^2\) for Jackson, but \(q\) for Hahn–Exton. The exact interfamily radius ratio is \(4^{1/m}q^{-\nu/m}(1-\beta q+O(q^2))\), with an explicit positive coefficient \(\beta\) for each normalization and radius type.
