For every \(n\ge3\), all invariant probabilities avoiding the switching-ray orbit of \(T_n(x,y)=(2\cos(\pi/n)|x|-y,x)\) induce the same complete binary sign process: a uniform cyclic block of length \(n\) shifted by \(n-1\) in \(\mathbb Z/n^2\mathbb Z\). Its adjacent signs are independent, its signs two steps apart cannot both be negative, and its entire lag-covariance sequence follows a closed circular-overlap formula.
