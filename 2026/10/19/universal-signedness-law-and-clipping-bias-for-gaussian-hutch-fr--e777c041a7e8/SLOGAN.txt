For the nonadaptive Gaussian Hutch# squared-Frobenius estimator, every fixed sketch budget has a universal high-effective-rank limit \(2-\chi^2_{r^2}/r^2\): negative estimates persist with probability \(\Pr\{\chi^2_{r^2}>2r^2\}>0\), and clipping them upward creates an exact positive bias. Rank-one inputs follow a different, sharply quantified negative-tail law with exponent \((1-\log2)r\), rather than the flat-spectrum exponent \((1-\log2)r^2/2\).
