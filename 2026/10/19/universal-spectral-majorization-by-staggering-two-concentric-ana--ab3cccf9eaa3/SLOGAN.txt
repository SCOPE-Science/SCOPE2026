For two concentric regular sampling rings in every integer-order Hardy–Bergman analytic kernel \(K_\alpha(z,w)=(1-z\overline w)^{-\alpha}\), a half-grid rotation minimizes the entire Gram spectrum in majorization order, while alignment maximizes it. The same offset uniquely optimizes every strictly convex spectral trace, determinant, and spectral condition number simultaneously; the proof combines finite Fourier blocks with the known negative-root structure of Veronese numerator polynomials.
