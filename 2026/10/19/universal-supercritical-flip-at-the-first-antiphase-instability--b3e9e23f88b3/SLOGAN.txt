At the sharp first antiphase instability of every finite positively weighted tree in the discrete repulsive-sine network, a locally attracting two-cycle is born supercritically. Its edge-phase displacement is an explicit positive Perron eigenvector times \(\sqrt{\delta/\kappa}\), where \(\kappa=\sum_e a_ev_e^4/3\), with critical two-step Floquet multiplier \(1-4\delta+O(\delta^2)\).
