For any full-support categorical law, overlapping adjacent-pair counts have an exactly distribution-free weighted covariance spectrum: \(0,1/m,1,2-1/m\), with explicit multiplicities and sharp variance extremizers. Closing an independent string into a cycle creates exactly \(q-1\) new covariance-null directions, and the full known-probability Pearson statistic has limit \(\chi^2_{(q-1)^2}+2\chi^2_{q-1}\).
