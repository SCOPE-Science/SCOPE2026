An arbitrarily small positive crystal field creates exponentially many strictly absorbing configurations in a one-dimensional spin-one Blume–Capel ring: isolated vacancies separate alternating polarized domains, each of length at least two. The exact vacancy-graded polynomial yields entropy \(\log\lambda\), where \(\lambda^3-\lambda^2-1=0\), for \(0<D<J\), but zero absorbing-state entropy at \(D=0\) or \(D\ge J\).
