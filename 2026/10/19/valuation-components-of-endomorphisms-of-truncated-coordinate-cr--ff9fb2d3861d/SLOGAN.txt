The reduced augmentation-preserving endomorphism variety of the \(r\)-branch algebra \(k[x_1,\ldots,x_r]/(x_ix_j\ (i\ne j),x_i^{d+1})\) is an explicit union of \(c_{r,d}^{\,r}\) affine-linear components, becoming non-equidimensional exactly when \(r,d\geq3\). Only \(r!\) components meet the automorphisms, and every finite-field endomorphism count has the exact form \(T_{r,d}(q)^r\) with a closed valuation-sum formula.
