# Scientific assessment: square-zero matrices over chain rings

**Correctness — PASS.** Primitive square-zero matrices over any length-\(k\) finite commutative chain ring form one \(\operatorname{GL}_2\)-conjugacy orbit: a vector outside the residual kernel produces the unimodular basis \((Bv,v)\). The centralizer consists of invertible upper-Toeplitz matrices and gives \((q^2-1)q^{2k-2}\) primitive elements. For valuation \(c<n/2\), exact quotient reduction with \(q^{4c}\) independent lifts proves the claimed constant lower-layer count; upper layers telescope. Algebraic parity simplification proves the total and rational series. Separate brute-force multiplication tests covered nine prime-power integer rings and two equal-characteristic degree-four rings. The proof does not infer arbitrary length from experiments.

**Originality — PASS (bounded bibliographic risk).** The inspected full texts of Danchev–García–Gómez Lozano, Theorem 2.6, and Cheraghpour–Ghosseiri, Theorem 3.7/Corollary 3.8, respectively establish decomposability and unrefined nilpotent counts, neither of which implies the valuation-stratified square-zero enumeration. Carnevale–Shechter–Voll counts trace-zero matrices by elementary-divisor type, but nilpotence of index two is a different constraint over nonreduced rings. No dominating result was identified by direct wording, finite-chain-ring and valuation aliases, and prior claims. The 1969 Ramirez–Bollman full text could not be inspected; it remains a material risk, particularly for the \(\mathbb Z/p^n\mathbb Z\) specialization. Novelty is conditional on this bounded search, not an assertion of global uniqueness.

**Value — PASS.** Square-zero matrices are a natural nilpotency-index stratum directly used in matrix decomposition studies. Unlike a field-only enumeration, the result gives a universal exact count for arbitrary chain rings and a valuation equidistribution law for every valuation below half the length; the upper-stratum transition and rational generating function explain how counts grow under nilpotent thickening. These distinguish the invariant from a routine restatement of the known total nilpotent count.

**Limits.** Dimension two and commutative chain rings only. The old nilpotent-matrix literature may have an equivalent formula in an uninspected source, and no independent scientific review was performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
