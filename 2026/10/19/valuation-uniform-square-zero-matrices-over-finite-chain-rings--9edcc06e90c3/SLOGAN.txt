For every finite commutative chain ring of length \(n\) and residue-field order \(q\), the nonzero \(2\times2\) square-zero matrices have exactly \((q^2-1)q^{2n-2}\) representatives at **each** entrywise valuation \(c<n/2\), independent of the ring's isomorphism type. Their total number is \((\lfloor n/2\rfloor+1)q^{2n}-\lfloor n/2\rfloor q^{2n-2}\), with explicit counts at all remaining valuations.
