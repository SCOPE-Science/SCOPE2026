Even with an identity Hessian, an exact \(A^{\mathsf T}A\)-preconditioned proximal direction, and a full Hessian-orthogonal two-dimensional trial plane, two independent exact proximal line solves followed by an exact ray refinement can recover only \(\Theta(N^{-2})\) of the best joint-model decrease. Thus these three scalar solves alone have no positive uniform fraction-of-optimal-decrease guarantee for coupled nonsmooth regularizers.
