# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The normal form follows from the vertex-corner decomposition of the free acyclic path algebra: arrows in the two adjacent corners carry projections \(S,T\), bypass arrows carry \(D\) and a shear \(B\), and length-two paths carry \(S\otimes T\). Requiring composition-idempotence yields the exact equation \(BD+(S\otimes T)B=B\). Splitting domain and codomain by their projections proves the exponent for allowed shears, and standard finite-field counts of projection kernels give the Gaussian factors. The resulting rank is \(3+a+b+d+ab\). The integer polynomial and rank gaps follow without characteristic restrictions. Six exhaustive small finite-field cases, including \(\mathbb F_4\), verified the calculation. A finite checker alone would not prove the unrestricted theorem.

## Originality — PASS within inspected coverage
Iusenko–MacQuarrie (arXiv:1704.06152, Sections 4.4 and 7.1) already discuss the bypass shear and describe quiver morphism structures, but not composition-idempotent rank counts. Iovanov–Sistko (arXiv:1705.00762, Theorem 4.1 and Proposition 4.2) classify maximal subalgebras, not retract images or their multiplicities. Gargate–Gargate (arXiv:2009.04243) enumerate potent algebra **elements**, not potent endomorphisms. Published-record searches for vertex-fixed quiver retractions, rank-weighted idempotents, and the tensor-bypass equation and searches of mathematical research records did not reveal an identical or stronger covering theorem. These searches cannot establish absolute novelty. A formulation through more general filtered- or parabolic-endomorphism theory remains a risk.

## Value — PASS
The object is the natural vertex-anchored endomorphism monoid of a finite quiver path algebra, and image dimension is a natural invariant of split-image subalgebras. The result treats every finite-field order and all parallel-arrow multiplicities in this three-vertex bypass family. Crucially, length-two path products contribute \(ab\) to image dimension and \(q^{d(rs-ab)+(t-d)ab}\) to multiplicity, so neither naive product of independent arrow projections nor the radical-square-zero quotient recovers the answer. The missing rank ten at \((2,2,1)\), for every field, is a sharp structural obstruction, not an arbitrary small enumeration.

## Boundaries and residual risks
The vertex-idempotent condition is essential; no all-endomorphisms classification is claimed. The completeness assertion is mathematical within this family, not across other quivers. Direct equivalence with a more general theorem on filtered idempotent endomorphisms has not been ruled out in every publication. Independent audit has not been performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
