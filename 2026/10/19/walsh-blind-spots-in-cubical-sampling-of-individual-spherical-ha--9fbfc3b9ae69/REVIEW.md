# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The claimed domain is exactly dimensions \(d\ge6\) and degrees \(0\le\ell\le d+2\). Coordinate parity gives the upper image bound; explicit planar harmonics construct every internal Walsh character, a three-coordinate degree-six zonal harmonic supplies missing parity-even constant witnesses, and harmonic trace identities exclude precisely two exceptional characters. The proof covers the indicated infinite parameter family. An exact rational checker additionally passed 505 Gegenbauer–Walsh eigenvalue tests in dimensions 6 through 10. These finite checks are independent corroboration, not an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with unresolved older-literature risk
The most relevant inspected full text is Roy and Suda, arXiv:1104.4692v1, Sections 1–3, concerning complex spherical designs and harmonic kernel methods; Bezubik–Strasburger's harmonic decomposition slides provide another foundational comparison. The 1977 Delsarte–Goethals–Seidel and 1980 Lloyd papers were identified as serious precursors, but their full texts were not available, so their exact degree-wise cubical sampling implications remain a residual risk. No retrieved material directly states the complete two-exception image classification; searching is not proof of historical uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Cubical vertex sampling of spherical harmonics is an intrinsic harmonic-interpolation and design-theory question. The theorem answers which Boolean parity frequencies are observable at every individual harmonic degree through \(d+2\) and reveals a sharp one-dimensional blind spot immediately after parity saturation. This has mathematical value independent of any conditioning claim.

## Scope and limits
The result concerns rank/images, not singular values, noise stability or higher harmonic degrees. It has not been independently audited.
