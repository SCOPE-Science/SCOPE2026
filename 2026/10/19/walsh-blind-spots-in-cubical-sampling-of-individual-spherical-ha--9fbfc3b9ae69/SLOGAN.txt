On the vertices of the Euclidean sign cube, each individual spherical-harmonic degree up to \(d+2\) realizes exactly the Walsh levels permitted by parity and degree, except that degree \(2\) misses constants and degree \(d+2\) unexpectedly misses the top Walsh character. In dimensions \(d\ge6\), three consecutive harmonic degrees attain an entire parity half of all cubical data, followed by a one-dimensional rank loss.
