# Review of Witt-insensitive ideal annihilation in short Gorenstein local rings

## Correctness
**PASS.** A finite Artinian Gorenstein local ring has one-dimensional socle. With radical cube zero but square nonzero, the socle equals the radical square. The proof establishes that all nonzero proper ideals are inverse images of residue-field subspaces of the radical modulo its square; it does not assume a field of coefficients inside the ring. Their multiplication is controlled by a symmetric nondegenerate bilinear pairing, whose orthogonality-incidence counts are Gaussian coefficients independent of its isometry class. Nonzero products of tuples have at most two nonunit ideals, yielding the stated probability exactly. Boundary cases, ordered counts, and the treatment of zero/unit ideals are explicit. Twelve finite-field ring models and a directly enumerated characteristic-four example test the proof's critical claims. The unrestricted theorem rests on the argument, not finite experiments.

## Originality
**PASS, subject to bibliographic uncertainty.** The closest motivating source, Chakraborty–Patra (arXiv:2608.14618v1, §§4 and 10), establishes the invariant and finite-chain/principal-ideal formulas while asking about nonchain universality. Its chain formulas do not imply the nonprincipal Gorenstein enumeration. Aliniaeifard–Behboodi (arXiv:1102.4835v1, Lemma 2.8) analyzes graphs of the same local rings but proves a particular complete-bipartite-subgraph obstruction rather than dimension-resolved counts. Essa–Mohammad (2024, doi:10.26713/cma.v15i1.2378, pp. 253–263) considers distinct-ideal minimal vanishing hyperedges, not ordered tuples with repeats; the full paper was read for its definitions and principal conclusions. The abstract of Curtis–Diesl–Rieck (2018) describes graph classification but does not give the displayed formula. Existing results about multiplication forms and graph isomorphism are acknowledged; the new assertion is the exact complete Gaussian incidence and arbitrary-tuple probability. Equivalent uncatalogued graph counts remain possible, and the Curtis–Diesl–Rieck full text was unavailable for review.

## Value
**PASS.** The motivating 2026 work explicitly asks for universality beyond chain rings. This theorem gives an infinite, nonprincipal Gorenstein family in every finite residue-field order and arbitrary embedding dimension, including rings without coefficient fields. It resolves the *entire* ordered pair incidence by dimensions, extends to all tuple lengths, and reveals an information-loss phenomenon: inequivalent multiplication forms can have identical ideal zero-product probabilities despite different square-zero-ideal counts. These are motivated ring-theoretic invariants rather than an arbitrary isolated computation.

## Scope and residual risks
The formulas require a one-dimensional socle and radical of nilpotency length exactly three; no claims about other lengths or non-Gorenstein rings are inferred. Finite computations check selected small fields and tuples up to length four only. A matching older orthogonality-graph enumeration or inaccessible full text could restrict bibliographic originality. Publication-year discrepancies in metadata for the motivating arXiv record do not affect the mathematical result.
