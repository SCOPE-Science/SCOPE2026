For every finite commutative Gorenstein local ring with \(\mathfrak m^3=0\ne\mathfrak m^2\), the probability that any \(s\ge2\) uniformly chosen ideals have zero product has an exact Gaussian-binomial formula depending only on residue-field order and embedding dimension. The entire dimension-refined zero-product incidence is equally universal, even though inequivalent multiplication forms have different numbers of square-zero ideals.
