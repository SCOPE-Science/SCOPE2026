The self-ambiguity function of the convolution of three causal exponentials with decay rates \(a,a+d,a+2d\) is nonzero at every real delay and frequency for all \(a,d>0\). At each nonzero delay, its meromorphic complex-Doppler continuation has exactly two zeros that undergo a unique double-root transition when \(d|\tau|=\log(7+4\sqrt3)\).
