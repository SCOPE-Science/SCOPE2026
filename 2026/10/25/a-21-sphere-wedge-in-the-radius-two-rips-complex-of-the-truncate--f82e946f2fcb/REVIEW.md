# Scientific review: radius-two Rips complex of the truncated icosahedron

## Correctness
**PASS.** The construction fixes a particular 60-vertex cubic planar graph. Exhaustive independent clique enumeration gives the integral face vector (60, 270, 340, 120, 12), and the reproducible checker verifies 120 genuine elementary collapses onto dimension two. Every new distance-two edge is homotopic to a path of two old edges; pentagonal and hexagonal facial loops explicitly fill, proving simple connectivity. Euler characteristic 22, Hurewicz, and homological Whitehead give precisely 21 two-sphere summands. There is no appeal to finite experiments to prove an infinite-family assertion.

## Originality
**PASS subject to the identified residual risk.** The full text of Saleh–Titz Mite–Witzel (arXiv:2302.14388v1) addresses Platonic vertex sets and identifies Archimedean solids as a possible extension, but does not classify the truncated icosahedron. Searches for fullerene, soccer-ball, buckyball, C60, graph square, Rips radius two, and sphere-wedge equivalents did not identify prior exact coverage. Adamaszek’s high-girth collapse requires at least girth seven or a centered-clique condition, both violated by the pentagonal Rips cliques here. The closest indexed graph-girth theorem assumes girth at least six and pairwise edge-disjoint six-cycles, both inapplicable here. Search absence is not a proof of priority; unindexed and privately circulated solutions remain possible.

## Value
**PASS.** This is a complete integral homotopy classification of a canonical 60-vertex Archimedean solid at the first radius when pentagonal and hexagonal facial relations arise. It shows the complex is simply connected with 21 genuine two-sphere summands, substantially stronger than a Betti-number or Euler-characteristic count. The phenomenon and the constructive high-dimensional collapse offer a motivated benchmark for Rips complexes of mixed-face polyhedral graphs.

## Scientific limitations
Only radius two in the unweighted graph metric is classified; higher scales, natural symmetry representations, and more general fullerenes remain open in this result. The collapse is computer-enumerated with source supplied; the planar facial filling and homotopy deductions are explicit. Independent audit has not been performed.
