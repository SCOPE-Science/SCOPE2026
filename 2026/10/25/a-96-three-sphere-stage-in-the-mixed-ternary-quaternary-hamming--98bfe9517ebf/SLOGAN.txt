The mixed \((3,3,4)\) Hamming box has Vietoris–Rips homotopy stages consisting of \(36\) points, \(\bigvee^{40}S^1\), \(\bigvee^{96}S^3\), and a point at scales \(0,1,2,3\), respectively. An exhaustive acyclic Morse matching proves the conjecturally predicted but previously unestablished \(\operatorname{Ind}(K_3\times K_3\times K_4)\simeq\bigvee^{96}S^3\) case.
