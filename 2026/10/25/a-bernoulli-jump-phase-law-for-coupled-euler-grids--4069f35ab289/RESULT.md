# A Bernoulli jump-phase law for coupled Euler grids

## Finding
For a scalar linear stochastic equation with additive compound-Poisson jumps, coupled Euler terminal errors on meshes \(1/n\) and \(1/(2n)\) have an explicit **joint stable limit**. The fine/coarse difference is conditionally a weighted sum of **independent fair Bernoulli marks**, not a sum of independent single-grid uniform marks. Thus the asymptotic conditional variance of the normalized level difference is exactly
\[
\frac{a^2}{16}\sum_{j=1}^{N_1}H_j^2e^{2a(1-T_j)}.
\]

## Assumptions and scope
Work on \([0,1]\). Let \(N\) be a Poisson process with fixed rate \(\lambda>0\), arrival times \(T_j\), and independent identically distributed real finite marks \(H_j\), independent of \(N\). No moment assumption is needed for convergence in law: there are almost surely finitely many finite jumps. Set \(J_t=\sum_{j=1}^{N_t}H_j\). Fix finite \(a,x_0\in\mathbb R\). The exact solution is
\[
X_t=x_0e^{at}+\sum_{j:T_j\le t}H_je^{a(t-T_j)}.
\]
For positive integer \(m\), use the *non-jump-adapted fixed-grid* additive-noise Euler recursion
\[
\widehat X^{(m)}_0=x_0,\qquad
\widehat X^{(m)}_{k+1}=(1+a/m)\widehat X^{(m)}_k+(J_{(k+1)/m}-J_{k/m}),\quad 0\le k<m.
\]
Its increments contain all jumps in each grid cell without modifying the grid. Put \(Q_m=m(X_1-\widehat X^{(m)}_m)\), and define \(\{u\}=u-\lfloor u\rfloor\). Every appearance of stable convergence below is relative to the full original sigma-field generated by the Poisson times and marks.

## Proof
**Exact expansion.** With probability one no jump occurs at a grid boundary at any positive integer grid resolution. Iteration gives
\[
\widehat X^{(m)}_m=x_0(1+a/m)^m+
\sum_{j=1}^{N_1}H_j(1+a/m)^{m-1-\lfloor mT_j\rfloor}.
\]
Writing \(u_{j,m}=\{mT_j\}\), Taylor's formula for the logarithm, uniformly over \(0\le T_j\le1\) and \(0\le u_{j,m}<1\), yields
\[
Q_m=\frac{a^2x_0e^a}{2}+\sum_{j=1}^{N_1}H_je^{a(1-T_j)}
\left[a(1-u_{j,m})+\frac{a^2}{2}(1-T_j)\right]+o(1).
\]
For each fixed sample path, the remainder is bounded by a random finite constant times \(m^{-1}\); no expectation of that bound is assumed.

**Shared phase limit.** Conditional on \(N_1=r\), the ordered arrival-time vector has a density on the simplex \(0<t_1<\cdots<t_r<1\), and jump marks are independent of the times. For every bounded measurable function \(F\) of those times and jump marks and every continuous periodic \(g\) on the \(r\)-torus, a Fourier approximation followed by the Riemann–Lebesgue lemma proves
\[
\mathbb E[F\,g(\{nT_1\},\ldots,\{nT_r\})]
\longrightarrow\mathbb E[F]\int_{[0,1]^r}g(u)\,du.
\]
Approximate general bounded measurable original-field multipliers by functions of finitely many Poisson coordinates and truncate the Poisson count. This is stable convergence of the jump phases to independent uniform marks \(U_j\), independent of the original process. The second-grid phases obey the *exact identity* \(\{2nT_j\}=\{2\{nT_j\}\}\). Joint continuous mapping applies because the map \(u\mapsto\{2u\}\) has only finitely many discontinuities of zero uniform mass. The expansion at \(m=n\) and \(m=2n\) therefore gives jointly
\[
(Q_n,Q_{2n})\Longrightarrow_{\rm stable}
\bigl(E(U),E(\{2U\})\bigr),
\]
where
\[
E(V)=\frac{a^2x_0e^a}{2}+\sum_{j=1}^{N_1}H_je^{a(1-T_j)}
\left[a(1-V_j)+\frac{a^2}{2}(1-T_j)\right].
\]

**Dyadic cancellation.** Since \(Q_n=n(X_1-\widehat X^{(n)}_n)\) and \(Q_{2n}=2n(X_1-\widehat X^{(2n)}_{2n})\),
\[
n(\widehat X^{(2n)}_{2n}-\widehat X^{(n)}_n)=Q_n-\frac12 Q_{2n}.
\]
The exact phase identity
\[
(1-u)-\tfrac12(1-\{2u\})=\tfrac12\mathbf1_{\{u<1/2\}},\qquad u\ne1/2,
\]
shows that the stable limit is
\[
D=\frac{a^2x_0e^a}{4}+\sum_{j=1}^{N_1}H_je^{a(1-T_j)}
\left[\frac{a^2}{4}(1-T_j)+\frac a2 B_j\right],
\quad B_j=\mathbf1_{\{U_j<1/2\}}.
\]
Given the entire original jump process, the \(B_j\) are independent Bernoulli \((1/2)\). Their variance \(1/4\) yields
\[
\operatorname{Var}(D\mid J)=\frac{a^2}{16}\sum_{j=1}^{N_1}H_j^2 e^{2a(1-T_j)},
\]
with conditional mean \(a^2x_0e^a/4+\sum_jH_je^{a(1-T_j)}[a^2(1-T_j)/4+a/4]\).

## Verification
The standalone `verify.py` exercises exact grid recurrences against the closed-form solution for multiple jump times, signs and drift coefficients and checks the first-order asymptotic formula, the dyadic phase identity and the Bernoulli conditional-variance coefficient. Run `python verify.py` using Python 3 standard library. These finite tests are diagnostic only: the density/Fourier argument is the proof of stable convergence.

## Relationship to prior work
M. Hirokane, *Asymptotic Error Distributions for Euler–Maruyama Schemes with Additive Semimartingale Noise*, arXiv:2610.20225v1 (first public 2026-10-14), proves a **single-grid** stable limit, including shared uniform marks for simultaneous vector-coordinate jumps. Its Introduction explicitly distinguishes a coupled two-discretization-level theorem as outside that result, and Section 3.3 treats uniform marks shared across coordinates, not the dyadically dependent phases across mesh levels. The present exact scalar compound-Poisson specialization is about **two nested fixed grids** and the emergent Bernoulli difference mark. Ben Alaya and Kebaier's 2015 multilevel Euler central limit theorem treats coupled levels in a broader numerical context; this self-contained jump-phase formula is not claimed to imply a general MLMC estimator CLT.

## Limitations
Only fixed-rate compound-Poisson additive noise, scalar linear drift, one terminal time, dyadic nested grids and stable distributional convergence are proved. No rate of convergence, unconditional finite-mesh variance convergence without moment hypotheses, nonlinear drift theorem, jump-adapted-grid theorem or MLMC estimator asymptotic is asserted. Previously published general multilevel stable convergence theorems may imply portions of this formula; independent priority review has not occurred.
