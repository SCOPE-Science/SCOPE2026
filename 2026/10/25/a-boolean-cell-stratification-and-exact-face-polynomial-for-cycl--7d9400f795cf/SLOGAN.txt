For every regular cyclic tournament \(R_{2m+1}\), \(m\ge2\), the directed-triangle Hom complex has a unique Boolean anchor description of all its regular cells, with exact face polynomial \(\bigl(2m+1\bigr)\binom{m+1}{2}(1+t)^{m-1}\). This makes its CW structure pure and determines every quotient cell count under target rotation; it does not assert a new homotopy type.
