The mean number of distinct nonempty scattered Dyck-word subsequences of an iid binary source has the exact generating function \((C(g_0(z)g_1(z))-1)/(1-z)\), with an explicit Catalan square-root asymptotic. At fair source probabilities the mean is asymptotic to \(3\sqrt{3/\pi}(3/2)^n n^{-3/2}\), and the exponential base is uniquely maximized by the fair source.
