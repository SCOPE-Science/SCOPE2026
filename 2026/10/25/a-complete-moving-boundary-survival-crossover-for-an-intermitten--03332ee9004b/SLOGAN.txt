Conditioning an intermittent LSV orbit to remain below a horizon-dependent boundary \(h_n\to0\) produces a complete conditional-path crossover governed by the single dimensionless quantity \(n h_n^\alpha\). The survivor mass is asymptotic to \(K_\alpha(h_n^{-\alpha}+\alpha2^\alpha n)^{-(1-\alpha)/\alpha}\), and the entire appropriately rescaled surviving path converges to an explicit beta-mixture of deterministic escape profiles, with a sharp transition at \(h_n\asymp n^{-1/\alpha}\).
