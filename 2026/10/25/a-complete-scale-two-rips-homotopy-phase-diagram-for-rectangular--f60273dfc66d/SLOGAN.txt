For every rectangular torus grid \(C_m\square C_n\) with \(m,n\ge5\), the entire integral scale-two Vietoris–Rips homotopy type is controlled by how many factors have circumference five or six: the result is a torus, a circle wedged with two-spheres, or only a wedge of two-spheres. The previously untreated one-short-factor families have exactly \(n\) or \(2n\) two-sphere summands, and the mixed \((5,6)\) torus gives fifteen two-spheres.
