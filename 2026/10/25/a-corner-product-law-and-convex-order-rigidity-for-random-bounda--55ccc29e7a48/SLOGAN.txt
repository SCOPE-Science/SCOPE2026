For every fixed convex polygon, uniformly sampled boundary points produce a perimeter-normalized missing-area limit \(\sum_j\tfrac12\sin\theta_j\,E_jF_j\), with an explicit all-order mean and a sharp stretched-exponential upper tail. Equal-sine angle patterns are exactly the equality cases for minimal normalized convex-order fluctuations, whereas equiangular polygons are exactly the maximizers of the leading absolute mean at fixed perimeter and number of corners.
