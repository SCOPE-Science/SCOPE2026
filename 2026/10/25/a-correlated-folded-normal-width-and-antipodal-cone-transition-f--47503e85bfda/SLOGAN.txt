For chain polytopes of complete two-level posets, a part-size imbalance of order \(\sqrt n\) controls a joint transition in random directional width and antipodal exposed-vertex classes. The limiting width is an independent Gaussian plus two negatively correlated folded Gaussians, while the balanced probability that opposite supporting vertices belong to the same level is exactly \(\tfrac12-\pi^{-1}\arcsin(1/(\pi-1))\), not one half.
