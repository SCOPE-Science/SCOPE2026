For a uniform point in a regular \(n\)-gon, the excess \(p\)-th moment of distance to the nearest vertex has three distinct convergence rates, with a logarithmic threshold at \(p=1\). In the critical window \(p=1+\lambda/\log(n/\pi)\), the normalized excess converges uniformly on bounded \(\lambda\)-ranges to \((1-e^{-\lambda})/(3\lambda)\).
