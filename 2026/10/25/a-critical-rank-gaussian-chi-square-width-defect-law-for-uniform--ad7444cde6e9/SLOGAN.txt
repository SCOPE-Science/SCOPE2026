At rank \(k=n/2+c\sqrt n+o(\sqrt n)\), a uniform-matroid independence polytope has the same leading random width as its enclosing cube, yet their width deficit has a non-Gaussian positive-part-square limit at scale \(n^{-1/2}\). At the balanced rank the limit is a scaled \(\chi_1^2\); increasing the rank through the critical window creates an explicit atom at zero while the ordinary cube-width Gaussian fluctuation stays asymptotically independent.
