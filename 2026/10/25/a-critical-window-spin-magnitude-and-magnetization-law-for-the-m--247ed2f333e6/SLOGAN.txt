In the \(n^{-1/2}\) inverse-temperature window about the critical point of the mean-field spin-half Heisenberg ferromagnet, the total-spin magnitude divided by \(n^{3/4}\) has the exact non-Gaussian density proportional to \(y^2e^{ty^2-4y^4/3}\); the measured magnetization has an explicit uniform-mixture law, becoming an \(\operatorname{erfc}\) profile exactly at criticality. The same crossover yields explicit partition and susceptibility scaling functions.
