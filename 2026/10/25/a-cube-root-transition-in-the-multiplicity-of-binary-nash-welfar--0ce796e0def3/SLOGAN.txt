For two agents with independent binary approvals of \(m\) goods, the probability that more than one cleaned Nash-welfare-optimal allocation exists has a sharp transition when the approval probability is of order \(m^{-1/3}\). At \(p_m=\lambda m^{-1/3}\), the limiting probability is \(\operatorname{erf}(\lambda^{3/2}/2)\), with an explicit nondegenerate law for \(m^{-1/3}\log K_m\), where \(K_m\) counts optimal allocations of labeled goods before tie-breaking.
