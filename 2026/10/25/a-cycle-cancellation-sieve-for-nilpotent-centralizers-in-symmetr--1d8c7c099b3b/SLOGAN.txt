For every nilpotent partial bijection, a signed cycle-cancellation sieve gives the full rank distribution of nilpotent elements in its symmetric-inverse-monoid centralizer by deleting at most one chain of each distinct length. When the fixed map has \(c\) chains of equal vertex-length \(L\), the nonnilpotent centralizers of rank \(r\) are exactly \(c\) times the unrestricted centralizers of \(c-1\) such chains of rank \(r-L\).
