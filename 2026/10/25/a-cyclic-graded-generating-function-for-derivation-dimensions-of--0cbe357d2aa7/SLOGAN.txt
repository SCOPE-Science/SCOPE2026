For every odd finite-field characteristic, the complete distribution of derivation-space dimensions across marked perfect evolution algebras has the formal generating function \(G_q(x)F_{q,r}(x^r)^{(u-1)/r}\), where \(r\) is the multiplicative order of \(2\) modulo the characteristic. Nonzero derivations first occur in dimension \(r\), and the maximal possible derivation dimension at size \(n\) is exactly \(\lfloor n/r\rfloor\).
