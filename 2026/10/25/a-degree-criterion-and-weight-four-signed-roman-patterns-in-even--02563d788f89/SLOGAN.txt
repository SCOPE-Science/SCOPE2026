For every even \(n\ge12\), the Kneser graph \(K_{n,2}\) has an explicit signed Roman dominating function of weight \(4\), improving the previously published even-order upper bound of \(5\). A degree-based necessary-and-sufficient test for a natural bipartition family proves that weight \(4\) is optimal within that family, and gives an exact count of a large constructive subfamily; unrestricted optimality is not claimed.
