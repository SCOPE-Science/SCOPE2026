In the standard nested Loday triple \(\mathrm{Perm}_n\subseteq\mathrm{Asso}_n\subseteq\mathrm{Para}_n\), the uniform-vertex mean squared centered radii are exactly \(n(n^2-1)/12\), \(n(n-1)(n+1)(2n+1)/60\), and \((n-1)^3/2\), respectively. Surprisingly, although the convex sets remain nested, the associahedron's vertex inertia first exceeds the enclosing parallelepiped's at dimension \(n-1=10\) (that is, \(n=11\)).
