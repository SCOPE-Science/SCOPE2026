# Review

## Correctness

PASS. The top stratum is \(F_k(M)/\mathfrak{S}_k\), and the lower-cardinality stratum has dimension at most \(n(k-1)\). For \(n\ge2\), the relative top group determines top homology. A regular-value configuration has preimages obtained by independently choosing one point from each fiber, giving the mod-two product and, in even oriented dimension, the signed integer product. The proof states the dimension and orientation restrictions explicitly.

## Value

PASS. The result is a coefficient-sensitive structural law for induced maps on finite-subset spaces: it identifies when an integral degree is available, gives the mod-two degree in all dimensions \(n\ge2\), and explains the parity obstruction in the unordered configuration stratum. It supplies a reusable top-degree invariant without claiming more about lower homology or homotopy type.

The theorem here is limited to closed connected smooth manifolds of equal dimension at least two. The integral statement requires even dimension and orientability; the mod-two statement does not.
