For an isosceles triangle, all three termwise bounds \(R_j+R_k\ge2r_i+r_j+r_k\) hold at every point exactly when the apex angle is at least \(120^\circ\), or when the triangle is equilateral. This yields the cyclic inequality in Liu's 2015 Conjecture 1 for those two shape regimes, but not a resolution of the general conjecture.
