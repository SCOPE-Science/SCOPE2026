The arrow patterns \((12;3\to3)\) and \((23;1\to1)\) are arrow-Wilf-equivalent for every permutation length. Their shared formal generating function is \(D(z)+\frac{z}{1-z-z^2}D(\frac{z}{1-z-z^2})\), with \(D\) the derangement series; the equality follows from a factorial-moment transform rather than a conjectured bijection.
