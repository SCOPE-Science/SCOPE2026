# Review

## Correctness

The claim is proved by exact distance enumeration. At length \(4\sqrt2\), the source has exactly four degree-two chains and no degree-three chains. In the extension, the same four cycles are all boundaries, with explicit certificates \(\partial(A,O,\bar A,A)=-(A,\bar A,A)\). The standalone checker reproduces the chain counts and boundary identities.

## Originality

The closest literature is Bi, Feng, Li, and Wu, *Persistent magnitude homology on finite metric spaces* (arXiv:2601.03685v1). It sets up persistence under isometric inclusions and includes a degree-one perturbation example, but does not state this degree-two square-center inclusion. The claim is limited to the exact five-point metric extension; no general priority claim is made. An unindexed equivalent example remains a residual risk.

## Value

This example demonstrates an exact non-injective map in the persistence setting: adjoining one center point kills a rank-four magnitude-homology group without changing the metric on the original points. It is a compact benchmark for understanding finite deaths in degree two.
