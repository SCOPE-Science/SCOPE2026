For equispaced tangents to any smooth strictly convex oval, randomizing the normal-angle phase gives an exact Fourier formula for mean circumscribed area, whose fourth-order error includes a negative curvature-gradient energy. Smooth ovals with identical perimeter and identical curvature-radius histograms can therefore have opposite fourth-order correction signs, with an explicit threshold between Fourier modes \(m=5\) and \(m=6\).
