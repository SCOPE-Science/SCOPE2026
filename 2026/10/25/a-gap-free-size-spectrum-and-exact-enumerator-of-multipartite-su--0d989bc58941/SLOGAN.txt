For finite complete bidirected multipartite relations, subset-maximal teams satisfying \(\operatorname{dep}(x;y)\) and \(x\subseteq y\) are exactly cross-part partial permutations leaving unused vertices in at most one part. Their complete labelled repair-size polynomial has an explicit rook-inclusion-exclusion formula, and every integer between the sharp endpoints \(\max\{n-a_1,2a_2\}\) and \(\min\{n,2(n-a_1)\}\) occurs, where \(a_1\ge a_2\) are the two largest part sizes.
