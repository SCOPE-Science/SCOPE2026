For an exponentially weighted stationary sum of independent squared Gaussian gradients, every inverse moment is finite at each fixed positive memory parameter, but the exact small-memory divergence changes exponent at every half-integer moment order. At the critical orders \(p=k/2\), the sharp growth is \(\beta^{-k(k-1)/4}\log(1/\beta)\); a finite bias-corrected startup has a reciprocal moment precisely when its number of observations exceeds \(2p\).
