Complete-bipartite Laplacian simplices become asymptotically equilateral while their vertex normal cones retain a sharp logistic bias: an imbalance of order \(n/\log n\) changes the limiting chance that a random supporting vertex belongs to either color class. Their centered random Euclidean widths converge jointly with the exposed-color indicators to a shifted sum of two independent Gumbels and two independent logistic-parameter Bernoulli variables.
