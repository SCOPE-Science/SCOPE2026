The equalizing reset rate for a diffusion-fed finite-server queue grows as \(r_e(c)\sim(c\mu L)^2D^{-1}[\log(c\mu L^2/D)]^2\), rather than a fixed superquadratic power of the server count. The normalized square-root rate has explicit \(2\log\log\) and next-order corrections, uniformly across all positive crossover solutions at large server counts.
