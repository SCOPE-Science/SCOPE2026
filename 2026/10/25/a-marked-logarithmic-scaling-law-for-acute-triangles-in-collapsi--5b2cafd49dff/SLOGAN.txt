For three interior-uniform points forming a rare acute triangle in a rectangle of height \(h\downarrow0\), the normalized logarithm of the first horizontal gap converges to \(\mathrm{Unif}(-1,1)\), independent of the middle point's vertical height, whose exact limiting density is \(\frac52(t^4+(1-t)^4)\). The full four-variable marked limit has an explicit piecewise polynomial density, explaining the classical acute-event probability scale \(h^2\log(1/h)\) without claiming the previously known scalar rectangle probability as new.
