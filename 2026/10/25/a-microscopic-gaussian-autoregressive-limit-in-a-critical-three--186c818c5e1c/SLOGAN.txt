In a critical three-allele Poisson clone–mutant branching model conditioned by its Q-process, finite consecutive-generation allele-imbalance fluctuations become a stationary Gaussian autoregression after division by the square root of the current population. The limiting lag-\(h\) correlation is exactly \((-1/2)^h\), independent of mutation probability, and the Gaussian process is independent of the Gamma-distributed population scale.
