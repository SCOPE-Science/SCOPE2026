# Review of a mixed-dimensional triangular-graph neighborhood wedge

## Correctness
PASS. The certificate independently reconstructs the 21-vertex ordinary open-neighborhood complex from the line graph of the seven-vertex complete graph. All 18,494 faces and 9,120 distinct covering pairs are accounted for, the full reversed-pair Hasse graph is acyclic, and there are exactly one critical zero-cell, 246 critical three-cells, and seven critical four-cells. The seven integral Morse boundaries are recomputed by exact signed path arithmetic and are all zero. Hurewicz on the 2-connected three-skeleton turns zero attaching-degree vectors into nullhomotopic four-cell attachments, giving the claimed wedge. The executable and complete compressed pairing list are supplied, not merely a verification log.

## Originality
PASS under inspected evidence, with residual bibliographic risk. A directly relevant published published-finding corpus calculation of the same object has only mod-two homology in degrees three and four; its RESULT.md explicitly states that integral torsion, attaching maps and homotopy type are not determined. Neither Kahle's general neighborhood theory, Matsushita's fundamental-group results, nor Fröberg's neighborhood Stanley–Reisner families implies the exact integral wedge statement. Multiple searches used the aliases \(T_7\), \(J(7,2)\), \(L(K_7)\), and the ranks 246 and seven. Unindexed homotopy computations remain possible.

## Value
PASS. The triangular graph is a canonical Johnson/line graph, and the earlier known mod-two mixed-degree profile leaves open whether integral torsion or nontrivial four-cell attaching maps occur. This exact homotopy classification settles both questions at the first observed mixed-dimensional member of that family; it supplies a reusable perfect discrete-Morse benchmark, not merely a restatement of the mod-two ranks. No infinite-family claim is made.

## Limits
The statement concerns only the ordinary open-neighborhood complex of \(T_7\). An equivariant decomposition or general \(T_n\) theorem is not established. Same-object literature comparison was performed against the complete published RESULT.md; the claimed additional homotopy information is separate from its mod-two result. The artifact requires its compressed matching list for reproducibility.
