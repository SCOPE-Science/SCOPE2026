The ordinary open-neighborhood complex of the triangular graph \(T_7=L(K_7)\) has exact integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{246}S^3\vee\bigvee^7S^4\). A complete acyclic discrete-Morse matching and a zero integral attaching-degree certificate strengthen previously known mod-two Betti numbers to a torsion-free mixed-dimensional wedge decomposition.
