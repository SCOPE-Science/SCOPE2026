The directed-forest complex of the twelve-vertex ternary length-three Kautz digraph has integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{8}S^{9}\vee\bigvee^{12}S^{10}\). Its \(4,608\) top-dimensional facets coexist with \(132\) unextendible facets one dimension lower, and an explicit acyclic discrete-Morse matching proves the mixed sphere-wedge decomposition.
