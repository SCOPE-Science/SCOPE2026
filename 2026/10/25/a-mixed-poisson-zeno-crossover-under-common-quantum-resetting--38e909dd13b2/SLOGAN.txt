For noninteracting Rabi spins with a shared Poisson reset clock, the sharp collective Zeno scale is \(\gamma_N\asymp\Omega\sqrt N\): the stationary number of flipped spins converges to a mixed Poisson law with random mean \(E^2/c^2\), rather than the ordinary Poisson law produced by independent clocks. Common resetting thereby creates persistent count overdispersion and a stretched-exponential tail despite identical single-spin marginals.
