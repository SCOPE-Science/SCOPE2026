For a four-oscillator cycle with alternating intrinsic frequencies \((a,-a,a,-a)\), phase locking appears exactly at \(K=a\): for every \(K>a\) the entire equilibrium set modulo common phase is one sink, one source, and a normally hyperbolic saddle circle. The circle has sharp positive transverse exponent range \([2\sqrt{a(K-a)},\sqrt{2(K^2-a^2)}]\).
