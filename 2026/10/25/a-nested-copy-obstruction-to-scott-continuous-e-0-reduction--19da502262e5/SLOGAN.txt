If copies \(A_0\subseteq B_0\subseteq A_1\) satisfy \(A_0\cong A_1\not\cong B_0\), every Scott-continuous map into \(\mathcal P(\mathbb N)\) that respects isomorphism modulo \(E_0\) collapses the middle copy into the endpoint's \(E_0\)-class. Thus this nested-copy pattern forbids a Scott-continuous reduction of isomorphism to \(E_0\).
