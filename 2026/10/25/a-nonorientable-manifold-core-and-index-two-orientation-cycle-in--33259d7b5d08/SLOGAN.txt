Deleting one canonical sixteen-tetrahedron translation orbit from the independence complex of the exceptional graph \(C_{16}(1,4,8)\) leaves a closed nonorientable three-manifold with \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z/2\). The full shellable complex has a primitive top cycle with coefficient magnitudes one on its sixty-four core tetrahedra and two on the deleted orbit, realizing an index-two relative homology map.
