# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The theorem is proved over integral cellular chains for every pair of cycle lengths at least five. The explicit unique-center free-face collapse leaves two oriented square disks on every grid face, with common signed perimeter. The torus face-boundary kernel is generated by its primitive fundamental cycle, yielding the stated integral basis and exact sequence. Direct signed coordinate-symmetry computations give the nontrivial reflection matrix; an invariant integral lift would require half an integer coefficient, whereas twice the quotient generator lifts. The standalone checker confirms 1,337 local elementary free-face collapses and 1,528 signed disk-action comparisons on six independent toroidal sizes. Finite checks do not establish the infinite theorem; the chain argument does.

## Originality — PASS, subject to residual risk
The relevant 2025 closed-neighborhood preprint, the existing complete square-completion homotopy decomposition for the same toroidal graphs, and another published triangle-free closed-neighborhood collapse were compared through their mathematical claims. They determine the underlying nonequivariant homotopy type and homology ranks but not the integral reflection module, canonical quotient-to-orientation sequence, or its exact nonsplit order two. A different total two-cut complex has a related-looking but non-implying winding obstruction. Searches under neighborhood nerve, graph Čech, square-completion, dihedral reflection, lattice extension and equivariant homology produced no directly covering indexed result. Equivalence in unindexed literature remains possible; searches do not constitute a uniqueness proof.

## Value — PASS
The reflection-symmetric toroidal family has a natural geometric orientation quotient. Its integral extension is not split although the rational action splits, distinguishing genuine order-two arithmetic information that nonequivariant sphere counts, Betti numbers and ordinary rational character computations cannot detect. The result is an all-size structural classification, rather than an isolated example or routine numerical calculation.

## Limitations
The theorem applies to cycle circumferences at least five and the specified coordinate dihedral subgroup; no additional coordinate-switch symmetry is asserted. The nonequivariant wedge description belongs to prior work. Exact standalone script checks use six finite grid sizes. The literature comparison is not exhaustive of all unpublished work.
