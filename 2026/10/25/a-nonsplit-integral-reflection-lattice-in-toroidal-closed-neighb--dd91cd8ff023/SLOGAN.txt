For the closed-neighborhood complex of every toroidal grid \(C_m\square C_n\) with \(m,n\ge5\), the natural integral second-homology lattice is a nonsplit extension of the torus orientation character by the permutation lattice on square faces. The obstruction has exact order two: although a rationally invariant orientation lift exists, every integral lift of the primitive torus class fails reflection equivariance.
