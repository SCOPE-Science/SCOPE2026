In the quotient of the four-by-five chessboard complex by cyclic column shifts, the integral homology is \(\mathbb Z,\mathbb Z/5,\mathbb Z^4,\mathbb Z\) in degrees zero through three. Its canonical mod-five degree-one class has a nonzero cup product with its reduced integral Bockstein, obstructing the wedge decomposition suggested by those homology groups alone.
