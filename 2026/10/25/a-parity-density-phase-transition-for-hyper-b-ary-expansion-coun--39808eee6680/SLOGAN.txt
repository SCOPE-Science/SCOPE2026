For every integer base \(b\ge2\), the number of integers below \(b^L\) having an odd number of hyper-\(b\)-ary expansions satisfies the exact recurrence \(A_b(L)=(b-1)A_b(L-1)+2A_b(L-2)-1\). The odd-value set has density \(2/3\) for \(b=2\), but for every \(b\ge3\) its counting function grows as \(\Theta_b(X^{\log_b((b-1+\sqrt{(b-1)^2+8})/2)})\), with exponent strictly below one.
