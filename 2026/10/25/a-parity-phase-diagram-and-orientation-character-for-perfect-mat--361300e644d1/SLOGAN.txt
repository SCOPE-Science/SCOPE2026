For every multi-theta graph with at least four internally disjoint arms of length at least two, the perfect-matching complex is a sphere \(S^{r-2}\) when all \(r\) arms have odd length, contractible when exactly two are even, and void otherwise. In the spherical case equal-length arm permutations act on the unique integral top-homology class by their sign, while exchange of the two terminals acts trivially.
