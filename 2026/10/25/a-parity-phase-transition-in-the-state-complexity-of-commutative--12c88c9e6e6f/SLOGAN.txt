For the identity-word language of the dihedral group of order \(2n\), its commutative closure has minimal complete-DFA size \(4n-1\) when \(n\ge3\) is odd, but only \(n+4\) when \(n\ge4\) is even (with four states for \(n=2\)). This gives an infinite family in which commutative closure alternately increases and decreases state complexity according to the parity of the group parameter.
