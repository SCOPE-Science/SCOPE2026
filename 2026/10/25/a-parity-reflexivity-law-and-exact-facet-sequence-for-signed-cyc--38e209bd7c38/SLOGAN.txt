The signed-cycle root polytope \(Q_n=\operatorname{conv}\{\pm e_i,\ \pm(e_i-e_{i+1})\ (i<n),\ \pm(e_n+e_1)\}\) is reflexive exactly in even dimensions; in odd dimensions its polar has precisely two fractional vertices. Its number of facets is \(s_n-(-1)^n\), where \(s_0=3,s_1=1,s_2=5\) and \(s_n=s_{n-1}+2s_{n-2}+2s_{n-3}\), giving exactly \(136\) facets for the six-dimensional Möbius-strip cohomology example.
