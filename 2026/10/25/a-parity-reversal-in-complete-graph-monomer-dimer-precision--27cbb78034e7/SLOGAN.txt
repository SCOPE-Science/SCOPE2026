For monomer–dimer matchings of \(K_n\), inverse-covariance entries between disjoint edges behave as \(-2t^2\) at low activity but as \(6t/(n-1)\) for odd \(n\) and \(-2t/(n-2)\) for even \(n\) at high activity. Thus all odd graph orders at least five exhibit a sign reversal in full-order linear partial correlation, even though no claim about uniqueness or nonlinear conditional dependence is made.
