The projected cross-polytope \(\Omega_n=\operatorname{conv}\{\pm(e_i-\mathbf1/n)\}\) has an exact parity-dependent covariance and a sharp isotropic-constant reversal: it exceeds the regular cross-polytope's isotropic constant only for \(n=4,6\), and falls below it in every other dimension \(n\ge3\). The squared-constant ratio approaches one from below with first-order coefficient \(\log(\pi/2)-1/2\).
