In an externally forced evolutionary public-goods game, every interior periodic cooperative state has exactly the payoff-weighted cooperation fraction \(\theta/(1+\theta)\). For pairwise interactions the law sharpens to \(\operatorname{Var}(x)=\bar x[\theta/(1+\theta)-\bar x]\), so every nonconstant seasonal oscillation strictly lowers mean cooperation; explicit locally stable seasonal orbits exist.
