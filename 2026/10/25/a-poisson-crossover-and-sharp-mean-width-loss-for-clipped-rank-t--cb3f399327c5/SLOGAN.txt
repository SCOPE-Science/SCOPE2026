For rank-two Schubert matroid base polytopes with one forbidden parallel class, the normalized random directional width has a non-Gumbel Poisson-process crossover as the class-to-complement ratio tends to a finite constant \(c\). The exact second-order Euclidean mean-width deficit relative to the full hypersimplex is \(2[\log(1+c)-c/(1+c)]\) in the natural \(a_q/\sqrt d\) scale.
