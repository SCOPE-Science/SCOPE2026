**Correctness — PASS.** The six-cone transform identity, rational derivative numerator, sharp hexagonal invariant identity, positive boundary factor, and negative quadratic discriminant yield a complete analytic proof of strict hexagonal positive definiteness. The exact negative square Fourier density at \(\eta^2=10\) excludes square positive definiteness. Symbolic replay succeeded.

The counterexample has positive hexagonal spectral density and negative square spectral density, so it is not a corollary of the sufficient representation; the closest \(\ell_1\)-radial results apply to the square class. Searches found no exact statement with the same implication. The full 1997 and 2004 sources were not accessible for complete-text comparison, and unindexed equivalents remain possible.

**Limitations.** The proof does not characterize the full hexagonal radial cone. Independent audit and validation remain outstanding.
