The explicit profile \(\phi(t)=e^{-t}(1+13t/20+t^2/6)\) is strictly positive definite under the regular-hexagonal norm \(\max\{|u|,|v|,|u+v|\}\), but fails positive definiteness under the square norm \(\max\{|u|,|v|\}\). This separates the hexagonal radial positive cone from the classical square-radial representation class and answers the necessity question left open by Xu (2015).
