# Review

## Correctness

**PASS.** For the specified pushout, the normal-bundle formula gives \(\mathcal T_X^1\cong\mathcal O_E(-2)\), so its support is the curve \(E\) while its global sections vanish. The tangent-sheaf exact sequence, the relative tangent calculation on \(\mathbb F_1\), and the surjective restriction of vector fields imply \(H^1(X,T_X)=0\). The local-to-global Ext sequence then forces the global first-order deformation group to vanish.

## Originality

**PASS.** The claim is a concrete counterexample to the projective implication in Lemma 4.2 of arXiv:2512.13203v1, not a restatement of its positive-dimensional-support criterion. The closest published-finding corpus records concern unrelated singular strata or Hirzebruch-surface geometry; no matching claim was returned by the recorded semantic searches. Tziolas's normal-crossing formula is supporting literature and does not itself state this global-vanishing example.

## Value

**PASS.** The example isolates a useful distinction between a nonzero local deformation sheaf and a globalizable first-order deformation. It prevents a twisted nonvanishing argument from being treated as nonvanishing of the untwisted global Ext group without additional hypotheses.
