In a uniform random recursive tree, the covariance between the child count of vertex \(i\) and the depth of a later-born vertex \(j\) decreases by exactly \(1/j^2\) whenever the target birth label advances from \(j\) to \(j+1\), independently of \(i\). For every nonroot source the sign reverses at a unique quadratic birth horizon \(J_i=2i^2-2i/3+O(1)\), although the individual cross-covariances are small.
