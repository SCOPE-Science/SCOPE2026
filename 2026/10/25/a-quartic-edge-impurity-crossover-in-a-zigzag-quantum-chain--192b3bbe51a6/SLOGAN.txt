At the point where the upper edge of a zigzag tight-binding chain changes from one quartic maximum to two quadratic maxima, the bound state of a weak repulsive impurity obeys a universal \(\varepsilon^{4/3}\) law. The complete detuning crossover is the unique positive solution of \(t^2(t-4s)=1/4\), with distinct sharp limits on the unsplit and split-band sides.
