For motion-invariant random affine hyperplanes hitting a regular simplex with \(m\) vertices, the section vertex count has a random polynomial exponent: \(\log V_m/\log m\Rightarrow2-U^2\), with \(U\) uniform on \([-1,1]\). Quadratically complex sections occur with probability asymptotic to an explicit Gaussian-quantile constant divided by \(\sqrt{2\log m}\), while the exact expected number of vertices is \(\binom m2/(\sqrt\pi\,\mathbb E\max_{i\le m}Z_i)\).
