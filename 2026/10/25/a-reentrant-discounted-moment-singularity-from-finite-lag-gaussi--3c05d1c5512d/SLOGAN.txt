An exact finite-lag, uncentered-increment surrogate of a Gaussian Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process has Student tails and the correct invariant variance, yet may lack a fourth moment. For discounted quartic evaluation, a sharp universal discount-to-damping ratio \(0.553698915832467\ldots\) separates an intermediate **closed band of divergent sampling lags** from the regime where every lag gives a finite value; at smaller discounts, both shorter and longer lags recover finiteness.
