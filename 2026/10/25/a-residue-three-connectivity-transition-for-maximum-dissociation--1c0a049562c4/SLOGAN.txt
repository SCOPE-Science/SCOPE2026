For maximum dissociation sets of the triangular graph \(T(n)=L(K_n)\), a single selected-vertex replacement connects all optimal sets unless \(3\mid n\). At multiples \(n=3k\), the exchange graph splits into exactly \(n!/(6^k k!)\) disjoint copies of \(K_3^{\square k}\); a spare singleton or selected edge enables full mobility in the other residue classes.
