# Reproducibility and limits

Execute both standalone programs in the same directory using Python 3 with its standard library:

```text
python3 check_exhaustive.py
python3 check_structural.py
```

`check_exhaustive.py` tests every feasible edge subset generated by the defining maximum-induced-degree-one condition in \(T(n)\), independently of any structural partition formula. `check_structural.py` enumerates candidate maximum sets by vertex blocks and independently reconstructs the exchange graph. For each \(3\le n\le9\), both outputs report the maximum cardinality, number of optimal configurations, component-size multiplicities, and vertex degrees. Both completed with pass markers; outputs are preserved in `exhaustive_stdout.txt` and `structural_stdout.txt`. Their agreement verifies these finite cases, not all orders.

The general proof is a direct occupancy argument plus explicit label-transposition moves, with a separately proved triple-partition invariant for multiples of three. The scripts do not supply a proof for unbounded \(n\), do not test all published literature, and do not constitute an independent audit. The stated independence status is not performed.
