For two-position exactly GC-balanced probabilistic composite DNA, the allowed capacity at resolution \(k\) and error tolerance \(\varepsilon<1/2\) scales uniformly as \(\log_2 k+2\log_2(1+k\varepsilon)+O(1)\). This yields the sharp resolution exponent \(3-2\min\{\alpha,1\}\) when \(\varepsilon=c k^{-\alpha}\), plus an exact first mixed-symbol threshold at \(\varepsilon=1/k\) with an asymptotic one-bit capacity jump.
