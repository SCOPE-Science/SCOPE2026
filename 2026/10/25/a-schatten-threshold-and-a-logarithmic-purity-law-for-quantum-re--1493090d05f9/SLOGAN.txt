Uniform-history quantum resetting has a sharp topology boundary: every density matrix dephases in Schatten norms \(\mathcal S_p\) for \(p>1\), but trace-norm convergence occurs exactly for initial states entirely in the Hamiltonian's pure-point spectral subspace. A continuous flat energy band loses purity at the sharp rate \(r\sqrt{\pi/(2\log(rt))}\), while compact-resolvent systems can exhibit arbitrarily slow trace-norm dephasing.
