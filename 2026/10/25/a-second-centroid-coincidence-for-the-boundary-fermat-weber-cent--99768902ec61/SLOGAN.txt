The arclength-uniform boundary Fermat–Weber center of a non-equilateral isosceles triangle can coincide exactly with its area centroid: this occurs for at least one height \(h\in(1/10,1/8)\), in addition to the equilateral height \(\sqrt3\). Its normalized altitude approaches \(1/2\) at both the thin and tall extremes but falls below the centroid level \(1/3\) for intermediate shapes, giving two genuine reversals in geometric-center order.
