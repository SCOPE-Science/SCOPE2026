For a uniformly random rooted ordered tree with \(n\) edges and exactly \(k\) leaves, the depth of its leftmost leaf has the law of the second-smallest element of a uniform \(k\)-subset of \(\{1,\ldots,n\}\), minus one (using a sentinel when \(k=1\)). This yields a single monotone coupling across all leaf counts and a Beta-to-Gamma-to-discrete depth scaling crossover.
