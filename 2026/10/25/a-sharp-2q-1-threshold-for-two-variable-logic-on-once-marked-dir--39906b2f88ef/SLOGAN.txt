A single marked vertex makes finite directed cycles distinguishable by two-variable first-order logic up to the **sharp** length \(2q\) at quantifier rank \(q\geq2\); all cycle lengths at least \(2q+1\) become indistinguishable. Exactly \(2q+1\) rank-\(q\) sentence theories occur, proved by explicit threshold formulas and a two-pebble game.
