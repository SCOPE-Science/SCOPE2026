# Scientific review

**Correctness: PASS.** The Rayleigh witness with values \(\pm1\) at the two hubs and \(\pm(1+\sqrt2)\) at their leaves gives a universal upper bound on the Laplacian spectral gap without any symmetry-of-weights assumption. The explicitly constructed chain attains it, its full spectrum has no worse negative mode, and equality in the Rayleigh quotient plus hub saturation forces every individual edge probability, establishing uniqueness. Exact symbolic identities and independently computed full-matrix spectra provide supplementary reproducible checks, not an infinite proof.

**Originality: PASS, with residual specialized-literature risk.** Boyd–Diaconis–Xiao (2004) gives a general SDP formulation, and the 2007 Boyd–Diaconis–Parrilo–Xiao preprint treats two bridged cliques, including the same quotient algebra when *internal* edges are artificially forced to zero, but its presented optimizer keeps positive internal clique edges. Thus the unrestricted bridged-clique optimum does not imply the optimum over this different tree graph. The known path theorem covers only \(k=1\). The continuous-time budget problem and one-hub two-star fusion address different constraints and graphs. Targeted mathematical database and primary-literature searches found no dominating exact double-star solution; a not-yet-identified specialized treatment remains a genuine risk.

**Value: PASS.** Fastest mixing with uniform equilibrium is a natural control and stochastic-optimization problem. The result gives a nontrivial exact global optimizer, establishes uniqueness even without imposing automorphism symmetry, produces a simple sharp spectral dual witness, and quantifies a linear-in-size improvement in spectral gap over two standard heuristics. The asymptotic disadvantage is practically interpretable for bridge-limited networks; it is not a mere restatement of an SDP or a small fixed-size computation.

**Scope and limitations.** Only balanced double stars and symmetric nonnegative stochastic matrices are covered. The proof does not treat general trees, nonreversible processes, or arbitrary signed consensus weights. Historical literature comparison is not proof of global uniqueness in the literature. An earlier month-dated 2003 technical report is known, but its public-release day was not verified; the precise supporting date is the 2004 journal's electronic publication date.
