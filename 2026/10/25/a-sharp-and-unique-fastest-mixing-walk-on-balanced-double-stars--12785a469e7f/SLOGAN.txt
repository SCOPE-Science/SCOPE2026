For every balanced double star with \(k\) leaves on each side, the unique fastest symmetric nonnegative stochastic walk has spectral contraction \(1-2/[1+k(3+2\sqrt2)]\), with every hub self-loop zero. Its spectral gap is of order \(1/k\), whereas both maximum-degree and Metropolis weighting have gaps of order \(1/k^2\).
