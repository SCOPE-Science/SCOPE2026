For finite Alexandrov spaces formed from the rank-one and rank-\(k\) levels of a Boolean lattice, pairwise-intersection neighborhoods give a contractible clique complex, whereas requiring intersections of \(k+1\) neighborhoods creates \(\binom{n-1}{k}\) spheres of dimension \(k-1\). This threshold is sharp, and the spheres' integral homology is the natural \(\bigwedge^k\) augmentation representation of \(S_n\).
