For stationary reflected Brownian diffusion on an interval, every uniformly bounded nonnegative time-dependent surface reactivity causes eventual reaction almost surely exactly when its total time integral diverges; the time-weighted boundary-local-time variance has the sharp universal bound \(\operatorname{Var}(H_t)\le(2/3)\int_0^t\kappa^2\). In contrast, a smooth sequence of increasingly narrow and tall activation pulses can have infinite total reactivity yet leave a strictly positive never-reaction probability, with finite accumulated hazard almost surely but infinite mean.
