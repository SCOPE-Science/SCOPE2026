For arclength-uniform random triangles on the convex threefold oval \(r(\theta)=1+\eta\cos(3\theta)\), an arbitrarily small fixed positive deformation changes the near-maximal-area probability from linear in the deficit to order \(\varepsilon^{3/2}\). When deformation and deficit vanish together, their ratio determines an exact arccosine crossover profile with a sharp nonanalytic rotational-phase cutoff.
