# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The backward stationary marked-Poisson construction yields conditionally iid particle positions, and exponential thinning gives their exact marginal fourth moments. Conditioning produces the geometric-weight exponential perpetuity and its exact variance. For the vanishing-mark regime, the strong law and geometric Riemann sums bound conditional fourth moments and yield a conditional Lyapunov central limit theorem. An independent weighted-exponential triangular-array CLT handles the common environment, and conditional characteristic functions establish asymptotic independence. The stated finite-system generator and characteristic identities were checked using exact rational arithmetic; these finite checks are not substituted for the asymptotic proof.

## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge assessment)
Primary full-text examination of arXiv:1102.2704, arXiv:2205.10273, arXiv:2211.00563, arXiv:2401.03501, and arXiv:2509.06658 separates independent per-particle resetting, total simultaneous resetting, and other interaction-dependent resetting from the Bernoulli-thinned common-clock mechanism. The 2025 paper explicitly describes partial-subset resetting as a prospective extension but considers resetting exactly \(m\) particles, whereas this theorem independently includes each particle. Searches in research records and general literature did not identify an equivalent stationary squared-radius formula and the \(Nq\) crossover. More general hidden results on marked Poisson common shocks or renewal-mixture particle systems remain a plausible priority risk; negative searches do not prove uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The squared radius is a natural collective observable of a resetting Brownian gas. Unlike a routine perturbative coefficient, the result identifies the entire fixed-participation non-self-averaging limit and a sharp universal Gaussian crossover between per-particle and shared-environment fluctuations. Its exact finite-size covariance law measures the strength of collective effects, while the limiting perpetuity exposes the stochastic mechanism. This addresses a motivated intermediate model between previously studied independent-reset and all-particle-reset endpoints, without claiming to settle the distinct fixed-cardinality subset problem.

## Scope and residual limitations
Identical independent Brownian particles, one common Poisson clock, independently Bernoulli-marked subsets, fixed positive \(r,D\), stationary observations. Neither non-Poisson clocks nor fixed-cardinality subsets are established. Full historical priority has not been independently established.
