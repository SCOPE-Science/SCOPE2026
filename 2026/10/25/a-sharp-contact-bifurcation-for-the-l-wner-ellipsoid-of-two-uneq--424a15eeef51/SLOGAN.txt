The minimum-volume enclosing ellipsoid of the convex hull of two unequal Euclidean balls has a sharp separation threshold \(L_c=d(1-q^2)/((d-1)q)\): before it the smaller ball is screened except for one tip, while beyond it both spherical boundaries contribute entire contact latitudes. Explicit formulas and one unique monotone scalar root determine every semiaxis and the global contact certificate in every dimension.
