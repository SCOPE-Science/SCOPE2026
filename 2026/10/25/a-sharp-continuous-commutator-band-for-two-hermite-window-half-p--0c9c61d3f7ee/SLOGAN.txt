For the first two Hermite windows, commutators of distinct horizontal half-plane concentrators have a complete, purely absolutely continuous imaginary spectral band. Their sharp norm is \(d e^{-d^2/2}/(\sqrt2\pi)\) at threshold separation \(d\), attaining the universal pairwise maximum \(1/(\pi\sqrt{2e})\) exactly at \(d=1\), although no nonzero signal attains an individual operator norm.
