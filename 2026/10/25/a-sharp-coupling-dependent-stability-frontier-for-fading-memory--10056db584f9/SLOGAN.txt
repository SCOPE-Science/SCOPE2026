For two-coordinate CoCD on a strongly convex coupled quadratic, the exact fading-memory stability boundary is \(\alpha\lambda<4/[1+\gamma+\sqrt{(1+\gamma)^2-8c^2\gamma(1-\gamma)}]\). Strong coupling can make partial gradient memory tolerate a larger step than ordinary cyclic coordinate descent, with a sharp overall supremum of \(3/\lambda\), whereas permanent memory cuts the allowable normalized step to \(1\).
