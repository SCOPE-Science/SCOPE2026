For polygons inscribed by independent uniform circle points, worst-case Hausdorff error and perimeter loss have independent Gumbel and Gaussian fluctuations at their natural scales, yet their covariance has the sharp positive asymptotic \(\operatorname{Cov}(H_n,D_n)\sim \pi^5(\log n)^4/(3n^5)\). The logarithmic coupling is determined by the exceptional largest circular spacing and is not visible in either marginal limit.
