For probabilistic forward/backward parking, the last preference conditioned on universal success has a sharp \(p=1/2\) total-variation window: when \(\sqrt n(p-1/2)\to c\), its distance from uniform multiplied by \(n\) converges to \((1+\sqrt{1+32c^2/\pi})/2\). Outside that window, \(\sqrt n\|Q_{n,p}-U_n\|_{\mathrm{TV}}/|2p-1|\to\sqrt{2/\pi}\); the crossover is caused by two endpoint boundary layers and a signed bulk profile.
