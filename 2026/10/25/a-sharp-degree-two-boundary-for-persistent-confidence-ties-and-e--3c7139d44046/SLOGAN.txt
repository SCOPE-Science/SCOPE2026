In asynchronous self-inclusive bounded-confidence averaging, every existing trust edge survives every future update for all opinion states exactly on graphs of maximum degree at most two; one degree-three vertex can break this property immediately. On an initially fully receptive cycle \(C_n\), the optimal uniform one-step energy contraction factor is \(1-16\sin^2(\pi/n)/(9n)\), yielding a geometric tail for approximate consensus.
