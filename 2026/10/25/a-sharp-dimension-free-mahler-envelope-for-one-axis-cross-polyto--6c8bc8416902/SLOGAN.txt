For the one-axis Minkowski extrusion \(B_1^d+[-h,h]e_1\), the normalized Mahler product has an exact formula and one unique peak in every dimension \(d\ge2\). All finite-dimensional values lie strictly below the *sharp* universal constant \((1+\alpha)^2/(1+\alpha+\alpha^2)=1.298425607525639\ldots\), where \(e^\alpha=1+\alpha+\alpha^2\); the peaks increase to this constant as dimension grows.
