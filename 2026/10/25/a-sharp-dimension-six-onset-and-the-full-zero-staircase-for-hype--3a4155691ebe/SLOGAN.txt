For the complex unit ball weighted by \(|z_1|^2(1-\|z\|^2)^\alpha\), its holomorphic Bergman kernel is zero-free exactly when \(d+\alpha\le5\), giving a sharp dimension-six onset when \(\alpha=0\). All zero fibres are explicit conjugate pairs \(q=1-e^{2\pi i j/(d+\alpha+1)}\) indexed by \(0<6|j|<d+\alpha+1\), with each pair entering at an exact weight threshold.
