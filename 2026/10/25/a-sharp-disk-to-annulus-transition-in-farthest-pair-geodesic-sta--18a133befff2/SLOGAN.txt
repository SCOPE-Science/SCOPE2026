For two uniform-area points in a planar concentric annulus with the obstacle-avoiding intrinsic metric, the near-diameter distance tail is cubic for every positive hole, whereas the disk has exponent \(5/2\). At the sharp hole scale \(r=a\sqrt{R\varepsilon}\), a closed crossover coefficient and a conditional three-coordinate law interpolate between the regimes, and the complete distance distribution recovers both radii.
