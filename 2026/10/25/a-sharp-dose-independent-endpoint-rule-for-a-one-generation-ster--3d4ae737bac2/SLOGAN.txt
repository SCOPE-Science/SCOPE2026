In a Beverton–Holt pest model with one generation of sterile-male mating suppression, the release that minimizes terminal wild adults is always in the first or last generation, never strictly between. For \(R>1\), the switch occurs exactly at initial abundance \((R-1)/(1+R^{n/2})\), independent of sterile dose; for \(R\le1\), last is always best.
