For the edge-on Lambert sphere with new phase at periastron, two off-full reflected-light peaks emerge at \(e\approx0.324898009\), but overtake the full-phase peak only at a unique, strictly larger eccentricity \(e\approx0.325836263\). The intermediate regime has three local maxima on the full orbit, and its exact fold-and-crossover classification follows from a one-dimensional derivative factorization and a strictly increasing peak-height ratio.
