For fifth-column Stirling differences \(S(2^n(1+2^q),5)-S(2^n,5)\), the exact \(2\)-adic valuation grows as \(n+q-1\), spikes once to \(2^n-1\), and then settles permanently at \(2^n-2\). The sharp transition identifies where the unrestricted \(k=5\) specialization of a published valuation formula ceases to hold.
