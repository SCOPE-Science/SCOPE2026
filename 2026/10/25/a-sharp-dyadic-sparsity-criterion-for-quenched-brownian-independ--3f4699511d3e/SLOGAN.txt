In a dyadic-sparse randomized bootstrap walk, only \(O(\log n)\) fresh Bernoulli coefficients per time step suffice for a quenched two-dimensional Brownian limit. Its two coordinates become asymptotically independent exactly when the number of active binary digits tends to infinity in natural density; otherwise their possible Brownian correlation is the Cesàro limit of \(\prod_j(1-p_{n,j})\).
