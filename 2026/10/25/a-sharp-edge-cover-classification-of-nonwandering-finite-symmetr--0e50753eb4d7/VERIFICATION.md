# Verification

The proof in RESULT.md supplies the universal all-\(n\) argument. The bundled script `verify.py` independently implements the finite transition graph, its maximum-matching edge-cover formula and a brute-force comparison with edge-cover enumeration for all subsets of a representative eight-vertex sample through size four. It also checks the sharp level-four threshold for a fixed point plus two islands and the level-two classification of the source paper. These finite checks only test the graph arithmetic, not all dynamical configurations; the passage from dynamics to the graph is proved analytically.
