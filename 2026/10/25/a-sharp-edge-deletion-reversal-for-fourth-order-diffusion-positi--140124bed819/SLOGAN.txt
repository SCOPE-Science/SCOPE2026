Deleting a perfect matching from a complete bipartite graph makes classical fourth-order Runge--Kutta diffusion lose entrywise positivity exactly at its forward-Euler step limit \(1/(n-1)\), even though the method remains spectrally stable beyond that limit. For \(n\ge4\), the deletion strictly lowers the absolute safe RK4 step relative to the intact graph, despite reducing every vertex degree; for \(n=3\), the inequality reverses.
