The normalized spherical normal-cone angle of an endpoint vertex of the canonical path symmetric edge polytope exceeds the uniform-vertex benchmark by a sharp positive correction of order \(n^{-4/3}(\log n)^{-1/3}\). The exact leading coefficient is \(\sqrt3\,\Gamma(4/3)/(4\pi^{1/3})\), obtained by closing the path into a cycle and resolving a rare adjacent-Gaussian extreme event.
