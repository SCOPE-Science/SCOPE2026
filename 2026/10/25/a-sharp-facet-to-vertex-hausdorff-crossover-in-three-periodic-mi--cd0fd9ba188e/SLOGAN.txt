Three-periodic facet lengths on equally spaced polygon normals yield a sharp, explicit transition from facet-controlled to vertex-controlled Hausdorff error at \(t_N=1-3\pi^2/(5N^2)+O(N^{-4})\). Despite a transport discrepancy of order \(N^{-1}\), the resulting Steiner-centered Minkowski polygons approximate the unit disk to order \(N^{-2}\), uniformly in the entire parameter interval.
