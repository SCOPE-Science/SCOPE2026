For a regular geodesically convex spherical odd-\(n\)-gon, the points farthest from a specified vertex undergo a sharp curvature-driven transition: two opposite vertices become an entire opposite edge at \(\rho=\arctan(1/\sqrt{\cos(\pi/n)})\), then collapse to its unique geodesic midpoint. In the even-\(n\) case the opposite vertex alone remains farthest at distance \(2\rho\).
