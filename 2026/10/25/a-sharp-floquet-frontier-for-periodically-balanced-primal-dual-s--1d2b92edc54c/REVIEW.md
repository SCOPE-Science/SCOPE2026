## Originality (PASS, best-knowledge assessment)

Variable-step PDHG nonconvergence under individually subcritical products was already explicitly noted by Goldstein et al. (2013, arXiv:1305.0546) and Goldstein–Li–Yuan (2015, NeurIPS); it is *not* the contribution. The original adaptive SPDHG work (Chambolle et al., arXiv:2301.02511, Algorithm 2.1 and Theorem 2.1) supplies the exact update and the quasi-increase assumption, but gives no necessary-and-sufficient stability analysis for permanent two-periodic reciprocal steps. Related published research on scalar PDHG covers fixed, not alternating, step pairs; the 2012 Bonettini–Ruggiero variable-step image-restoration framework uses different conditions. The mathematical additions are the exact sharp period-two Floquet frontier, its all-singular-value and uniform-contrast consequences, and a certified strongly convex–concave divergent instance. Equivalent formulations in general switched nonexpansive-operator or older two-periodic splitting literature are the residual originality risk. No independent originality search or expert audit is claimed.

## Value (PASS)

The exact transition at a dual-step contrast near \(1.74007\), while every primal–dual product is strictly below the classical bound, quantifies why temporal regularity is a substantive convergence requirement rather than a technical proof artifact. The sharp spectrum-uniform threshold and the finding that the most vulnerable normalized singular mode lies at \(2/\sqrt5\), below the endpoint, are concrete stability design data. The explicit strongly convex–concave failure prevents overinterpreting strong convexity as an automatic replacement for step-sequence control. This is a structural boundary, not a routine numerical benchmark or a claim that an already safeguarded practical update diverges.
