Two-periodic reciprocal primal–dual steps can turn a convergent dual-extrapolated PDHG method into a divergent one even though their product is strictly below the usual spectral bound at every step. The exact scalar frontier is \(t+t^{-1}<(4+5r^2-2r)/(3r)\), and the sharp uniform contrast threshold over all normalized singular modes is \((4\sqrt5-2)/3\); an exact strongly convex–concave example also diverges.
