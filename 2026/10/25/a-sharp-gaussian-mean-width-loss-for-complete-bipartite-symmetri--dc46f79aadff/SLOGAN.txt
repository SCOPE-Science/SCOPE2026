The complete and complete-bipartite symmetric edge polytopes have the same circumradius and leading Euclidean mean width, but their directional-width loss has a nonnegative four-Gumbel limit with a nontrivial zero atom. At fixed bipartition proportion \(\alpha\in(0,1)\), its sharp scaled mean is \(-\log(\alpha(1-\alpha))-1\), while the exact finite-dimensional probability of a strict loss is \([p(p-1)+q(q-1)]/[n(n-1)]\).
