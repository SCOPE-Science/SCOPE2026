For the squared-Hamming Gaussian kernel \(q^{h(x,y)^2}\) on a binary cube, strong damping \(0<q\le1/16\) forces a sharp positive-definiteness cutoff within one integer of \(q^{-1}\): every dimension through \(\lfloor q^{-1}\rfloor\) works, every dimension two steps higher fails, and the sole borderline is decided by one explicit alternating binomial sum. The first obstruction is uniquely the parity Walsh character, and the critical parameter in dimension \(d\) equals \(1/d+1/(2d^3)+O(d^{-4})\).
