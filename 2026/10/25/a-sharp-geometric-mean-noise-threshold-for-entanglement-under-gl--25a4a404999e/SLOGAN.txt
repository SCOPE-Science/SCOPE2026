For two \(XX\)-coupled qubits with independent local bit-flip rates \(a,b>0\) and full-state Poisson resets to \(|00\rangle\), stationary entanglement is possible precisely when \(J^2>ab\); in that regime it appears at one and only one positive reset-rate threshold. The exact separability polynomial is quartic, and strong resetting leaves concurrence \(C(r)\sim2(J-\sqrt{ab})/r\) rather than causing a second finite-rate disentanglement transition.
