For balanced complete bipartite graphs with \(k\) independent edges deleted, the total perfect Roman domination number is \(4\) until the last matching edge is removed, when it jumps to \(6\). Every minimum function is classified, giving exactly \((n-k)^2\) optimizers before the jump and \(n^2(n-1)+13\mathbf1_{\{n=3\}}\) afterward, for all \(n\ge3\).
