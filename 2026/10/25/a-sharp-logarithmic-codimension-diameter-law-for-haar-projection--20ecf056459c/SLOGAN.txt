A Haar projection that discards \(c\log m\) dimensions of a regular \((m-1)\)-simplex loses \((s_c/\sqrt2+o_{\mathbb P}(1))c\log(m)/m\) of its diameter, where \(s_c-1-\log s_c=4/c\). The coefficient comes from a growing-dimensional Gaussian nearest-pair transition, proved despite dependence between pairs sharing a vertex.
