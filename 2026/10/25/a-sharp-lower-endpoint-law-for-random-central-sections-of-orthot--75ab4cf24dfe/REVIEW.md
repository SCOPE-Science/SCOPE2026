# Review

## Correctness

**PASS.** For a centered orthotope, the section is centrally symmetric, so its diameter is twice its maximum Euclidean radius. Every generic section vertex lies on a box edge. The strict gap \(a_1^2-a_2^2>0\) isolates the minimizing orientation and forces the small-tail event into the cone where only first-coordinate edges cross. In projective normal coordinates the event is exactly a weighted \(\ell_1\)-ball; its volume and the spherical Jacobian give the stated exponent and coefficient. The proof includes the error estimate \(1+O_B(\varepsilon)\).

## Value

**PASS.** This gives a natural local stochastic invariant for random central sections: both the exponent and exact leading constant of the probability of being near the minimum diameter. The coefficient's independence from the longest half-length is a structural conclusion, while the shorter axes determine the first-order tail. This is more informative than a vertex-count law or a typical-diameter bound.
