For the one-step-per-delay randomized Runge–Kutta discretization of delayed negative feedback, the exact mean-square stability cutoff is the unique root \(\rho\in(1,6/5)\) of \(z^7-z^5-4z^4-4z^3+12z^2-24z+24=0\). The mean still converges until a strictly larger midpoint cutoff, so at \(z=7/6\) every nonzero deterministic starting triple has an exponentially decaying mean and exponentially growing mean-square error.
