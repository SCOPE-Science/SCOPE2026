For the vertices of every Euclidean \(d\)-cross-polytope, one midpoint-averaging step halves the Hausdorff convexification gap exactly, from \(1\) to \(1/2\). Every farthest point is either a half-axis point or, beginning in dimension four, a signed quarter-sum of four coordinate vectors; there are precisely \(2d+16\binom d4\) such points.
