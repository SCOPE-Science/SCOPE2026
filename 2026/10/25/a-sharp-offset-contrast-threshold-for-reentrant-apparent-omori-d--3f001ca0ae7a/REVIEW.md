## Value — PASS

Heterogeneity of Omori onset/regularization scales is a seismologically motivated issue, and different physical and observational mechanisms can affect apparent slope. The sharp, amplitude-uniform cutoff provides a clean mechanism discriminator **within this precise model**: below a roughly 34-fold offset contrast, superposition alone cannot create an increasing–decreasing–increasing local exponent; above it, that pattern can arise despite identical constituent exponents. This is a complete structural boundary for the natural minimal two-component model, not a superficial fit or an arbitrary numerical slice. It does not establish empirical detectability or a hazard recommendation.
