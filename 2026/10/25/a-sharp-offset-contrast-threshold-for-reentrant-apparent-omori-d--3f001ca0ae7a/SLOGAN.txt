A sum of two coeval inverse-time Omori intensities can have an apparent decay exponent that rises, falls, then rises, although each constituent has fixed exponent one. This reversal is possible for some positive mixture weights **exactly when** the characteristic-time ratio exceeds \(17+12\sqrt2\); at or below this sharp threshold every positive mixture has a strictly increasing apparent exponent, and no two-component mixture can exhibit more than two turning points.
