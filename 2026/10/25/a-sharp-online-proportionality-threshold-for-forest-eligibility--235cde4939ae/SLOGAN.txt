When unit goods are eligible for the two endpoints of a growing forest edge, the exact irrevocable minimax proportionality shortfall after \(t\) arrivals is \(\lfloor\log_2(t+1)\rfloor\) in signed integer units. Eligibility-aware Prop1 can therefore be maintained at every prefix on seven or fewer agents, but an adaptive adversary forces its first unavoidable forest failure with eight agents and seven goods.
