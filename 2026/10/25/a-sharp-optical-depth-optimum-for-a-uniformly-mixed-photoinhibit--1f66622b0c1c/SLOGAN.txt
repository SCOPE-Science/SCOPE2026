With Steele photoinhibition and Beer attenuation in a fixed, uniformly mixed water column, attenuation maximizes instantaneous depth-mean photosynthesis uniquely at zero optical depth when surface light is at or below the physiological optimum, and at a unique positive depth otherwise. The optimal optical depth rises strictly with surface light, beginning as \(\frac32(q-1)\) above threshold and growing as \(\log q+\log\log q+o(1)\) under extreme illumination.
