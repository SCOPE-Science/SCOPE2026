# Review of the exponential tree-kernel classification

## Correctness
PASS. The exact resolvent identity for the bounded adjacency operator reduces the subcritical regime to the two endpoints of an affine spectral factor. The radial Hankel-type positivity matrix gives an exact critical-index formula and, above critical decay, a sequence of negative two-by-two determinants for every nonzero first-order coefficient. Explicit positive Gram factorizations settle both critical endpoints; convexity settles the intervening interval, and the tree-metric Hilbert embedding settles the pure exponential. All quantifiers, the three regime boundaries, and the parameter normalization were checked. The symbolic verifier reproduced key identities and exact finite checks, while the infinite claims rest on analytic arguments.

## Originality
PASS under bounded search. The 2009 Haagerup–Steenstrup–Szwarc article classifies radial Schur multipliers, a weaker property. The 2013 Haagerup–Knudby article gives the general positive spherical representation on free groups. Vergara's 2019 Theorem 1.2 supplies a general criterion applying to the present functions, but its worked examples do not evaluate the explicit polynomial-times-exponential positivity cone or identify this critical collapse. No indexed published-finding corpus record or the inspected primary literature gave an equivalent or stronger exact three-regime classification. The published general criterion is explicitly cited as a prerequisite, and unindexed or differently described equivalences remain a real priority risk.

## Mathematical value
PASS. A first-order perturbation of the canonical exponential tree covariance is an intrinsic and natural radial family. Its complete parameter region is not governed by a single uniform inequality: sharp two-sided bounds turn into a one-sided boundary interval and finally into rigidity exactly at the radial square-summability threshold. The result supplies explicit usable positivity tests and identifies their precise spectral mechanisms; it is not a mere finite sample, normalization check, or arbitrary parameter slice.

## Limitations and priority risks
The theorem is restricted to homogeneous trees of finite branching degree, positive bases below one, and one first-order distance factor. The general tree criteria precede this work; independent originality verification across all literature has not occurred. The checker does not certify infinite positivity by enumeration. No independent audit or validation has been performed.
