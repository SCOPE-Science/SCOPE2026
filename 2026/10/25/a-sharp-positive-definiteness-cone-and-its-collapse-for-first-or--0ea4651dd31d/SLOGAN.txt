For every finite regular-tree branching parameter \(q\ge2\), the exact positive-definiteness region of the first-order exponential radial kernel \(r^{d(x,y)}(1+b\,d(x,y))\) is an explicit two-sided interval below \(r=q^{-1/2}\), the interval \([0,(q-1)/(q+1)]\) at that threshold, and only \(b=0\) above it. The collapse comes from a change from adjacency-spectrum endpoint obstructions to asymptotically negative principal minors of the radial positivity matrix.
