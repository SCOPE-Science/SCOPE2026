For the unweighted graph of an \(r\)-fold pyramid over a convex \(n\)-gon, all node resistance curvatures are strictly positive exactly when \(2r\ge n(n-3)-2\). Polygon-vertex curvature is always positive, while apex curvature changes sign across consecutive integer values of \(r\); hence the sharp number of pyramiding steps grows quadratically with the base polygon size.
