For every dimension \(n\ge2\), the weighted surface area \(\int_{\partial B_r(c)}|x|^p\,d\mathcal H^{n-1}\) increases strictly with radius for all fixed centers exactly when \(p\ge2-n\). Below that sharp threshold, every off-origin center produces a contact-radius local maximum with an exact \(|r-|c||^{n-1+p}\) cusp whose leading constant is independent of the offset.
