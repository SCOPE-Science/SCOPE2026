For normalized two-row Monge polytopes, the column-vertex simplex captures the leading mean width, but two exceptional singleton-row vertices leave a sharp positive deficit: \(w_H(\mathcal M_{2,n})-w_H(Q_n)\sim\sqrt2\pi^{1/4}/[n(\log n)^{3/4}]\). Gaussian directional support follows a Gumbel law, while the exact second-order geometric width contribution is governed by a rare-exposure Laplace integral.
