With two fixed-precision scalar Kalman sensors that must each be used once, the stronger sensor is optimally used last at both small and large dynamics gain but first on an exact intermediate interval when process noise is sufficiently low. The stronger-first window exists precisely below the sharp noise-to-prior threshold \(\nu<1/(\sqrt{1+pr}+\sqrt{1+ps})^2\), with two explicit crossover gains and a square-root collapse at threshold.
