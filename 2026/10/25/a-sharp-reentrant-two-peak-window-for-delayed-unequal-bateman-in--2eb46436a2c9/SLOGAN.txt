For two unequal delayed Bateman absorption inputs, the concentration has two strict peaks exactly when the delay \(d\) exceeds the isolated peak time \(t_0\) and the second-input fraction \(m\) exceeds an explicit decaying-slope threshold \(Q(d)\). When \(0<m<m_*\), with sharp critical ratio \(m_*=(\kappa/\lambda)^{-(\kappa+\lambda)/(\kappa-\lambda)}\) for distinct rates (and \(m_*=e^{-2}\) at coincident rates), increasing the delay produces a surprising two-peak, one-peak, two-peak sequence.
