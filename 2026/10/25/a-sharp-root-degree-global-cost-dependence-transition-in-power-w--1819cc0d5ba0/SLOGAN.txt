In deterministic power-weight recursive trees, normalized root-degree fluctuations become Gaussian and asymptotically independent of the centered total-path-length limit exactly when the weight exponent satisfies \(\alpha\le0\); for \(\alpha>0\), the root degree stabilizes and retains strictly negative limiting covariance with global path cost. With linear weights, that covariance is \(-(7-2\pi^2/3)\) and the optimal linear-regression slope is exactly \(-1/2\).
