# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The forbidden cliques are exactly the central clique and the attached cliques. The peripheral-only complex is a join of local suspensions of simplex-boundary joins. Reinstating the central obstruction gives a Mayer--Vietoris decomposition with spherical intersection. In the saturated case an explicit integral join cycle maps by a unit connecting homomorphism, proving integral acyclicity; simple connectivity and Whitehead upgrade it to contractibility. In the unsaturated case inclusion of the intersection is an integral homology equivalence between simply connected finite complexes, hence a homotopy equivalence. The small one-dimensional boundary case is handled directly. Independent finite mod-two calculations support but do not replace these proofs.

**Originality — PASS (best-knowledge, with bibliographic risk).** Ghosh--Selvaraja arXiv:2602.09623v1, in particular Theorems 3.3, 4.2, 4.8 and 4.9, supplies shellability, clique-attachment criteria, and purity for a different construction, not this exact sphere/contractible dichotomy or its dimensions. Kim arXiv:2408.14321v2 provides an at-most-one Betti number statement for acyclic hypergraphs and a general suspension reduction, without the stated saturation criterion or dimensions. Related cover-ideal results also do not imply an integral homotopy type. Published and indexed comparisons showed no equivalent statement; indexing is not a completeness guarantee.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** This class of block graphs has unbounded central clique size and arbitrarily many, nonuniform pendant blocks. The complete classification resolves both integral homotopy type and the exact sharp saturation boundary, sharpening a modern qualitative shellability theory for a natural family. The theorem is not just a numerical recomputation or a renamed old construction: the degree-one Mayer--Vietoris connecting map is the substantive mechanism behind the abrupt change from a sphere to a contractible complex.

**Limitations and closest sources.** Scope is the specified two-level clique bouquet. Arbitrary block graphs and more general clique-whiskerings remain outside the theorem. The main comparisons are Ghosh--Selvaraja (2026), Kim (2024, later-version full text), and Muta--Terai (2025, abstract). Unindexed prior exact formulas remain a risk; no external independent validation was performed.
