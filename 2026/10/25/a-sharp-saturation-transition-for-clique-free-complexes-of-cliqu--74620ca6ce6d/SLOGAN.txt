For a central \(K_t\) with any number of pendant \(K_t\) blocks attached separately at its vertices, the \(t\)-clique-free complex is exactly one sphere of dimension \((q+1)(t-2)\) whenever some central vertex has no attached block. Attaching a block at the last uncovered central vertex makes the complex contractible, for every \(t\ge3\) and arbitrary other attachment multiplicities.
