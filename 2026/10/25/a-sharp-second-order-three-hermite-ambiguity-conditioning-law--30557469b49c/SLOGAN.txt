For three independently weighted Hermite spectrogram channels, the sharp squared ambiguity-inversion floor on an expanding disk obeys \(M_3(R)=F_R-CF_R^2+o(F_R^2)\), where \(F_R=e^{-R}(1-2R+R^2/2)^2\) and \(C=5.984283368174\ldots\). Every optimal allocation assigns the Gaussian and first-Hermite windows asymptotically the strictly positive precision shares \(0.929064805486\ldots F_R\) and \(5.055218562688\ldots F_R\), with the remaining precision assigned to the second Hermite window.
