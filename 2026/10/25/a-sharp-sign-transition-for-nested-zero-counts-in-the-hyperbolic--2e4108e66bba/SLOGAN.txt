For the hyperbolic Gaussian analytic function of intensity \(2\), the boundary-scaled covariance of zero counts in nested disks changes sign exactly at the algebraic distance-ratio threshold \(v^4+2v-1=0\), becoming strictly negative for more disparate boundary gaps. An explicit absolutely convergent covariance series also shows negative nested-disk correlations for every intensity \(L>1\) at sufficiently separated boundary gaps.
