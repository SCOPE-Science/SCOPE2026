Restricting the determinant \(x\) to two values restores a sharp six-tuple forbidden-obstruction bound for the otherwise incoherent dependence-logic disjunction \(\operatorname{dep}(x,y)\lor\operatorname{dep}(y,z)\). Every inclusion-minimal failure has size four or six, with exact labelled counts \(2\binom{b}{2}\binom{c}{2}^{2}\) and \((4\binom{b}{2}+44\binom{b}{3})\binom{c}{2}^{3}\) for alphabets of sizes \(b,c\) on the remaining coordinates.
