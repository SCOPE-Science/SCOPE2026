For the Möbius ladder \(M_{2n}\), the inclusive graph-vertex scale-two Rips complex is contractible for \(n=3,4\), is \(S^2\) for \(n=5\), and for every \(n\ge6\) collapses in exactly \(6n\) elementary steps onto an embedded Möbius band. Its Hamiltonian rim represents twice a primitive integral first-homology class, while the \(n\) square boundaries generate precisely the kernel from graph homology.
