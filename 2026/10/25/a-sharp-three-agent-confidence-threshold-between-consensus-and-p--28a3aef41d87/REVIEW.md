# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof reconstructs the exact two oriented confidence indicators from the directed neighborhood definition in Krishnagopal–Porter, with no symmetry shortcut. An initially blocked leaf pair stays blocked because leaf-gating depends only on the leaf gap. The resulting independent affine recursion gives a unique contracting invariant law, a strong-law Cesàro mean and an almost-surely nonconvergent center. Above the strict threshold, mutual compromise preserves opinion order, freezes the gate open, conserves the total, and contracts the centered quadratic energy with the stated expectation bound. Exact boundary cases are addressed; finite rational checks supplement but do not establish the probability-one claims.

**Originality — PASS, with residual bibliographic risk.** The model itself is taken from Krishnagopal and Porter (arXiv:2402.05368); the manuscript's full mathematical setup and conclusions were inspected. Acemoğlu, Como, Fagnani and Ozdaglar (arXiv:1009.2653, especially Sec. 2.1) already establish the two-exogenously-stubborn-agent fluctuations, invariant random beliefs and binary-expansion uniform law. These classical ingredients are explicitly excluded from the novelty claim. Zhang and coauthors (arXiv:2107.04948) likewise prove fluctuations for a different random-clique update. The remaining claimed contribution is the NDW-specific endogenous sharp split between this fixed-leaf process and an all-receptive consensus process. The searched mathematical records did not exhibit a covering three-node classification, but failed searches are not proof of worldwide novelty. A mathematically equivalent state-dependent model might exist elsewhere.

**Value — PASS.** A three-agent path is the smallest network displaying unequal transitive neighborhood gates. The exact necessary-and-sufficient leaf-gap threshold identifies a nontrivial mechanism by which confidence determines whether there is conservation and consensus or persistent directed influence with stationary fluctuations. The strict equality case, all-parameter quantitative contraction, and precise stationary law make a natural, reproducible classification, not an arbitrary isolated computation.

**Scientific limits.** Only fixed pure-neighborhood, uniformly and independently edge-sampled, ordered three-agent paths are covered. General topologies, mixed influence, rewiring, and all other initial orders are unresolved. The lack of independent validation and remaining bibliographic equivalence risk do not constitute independent certification.
