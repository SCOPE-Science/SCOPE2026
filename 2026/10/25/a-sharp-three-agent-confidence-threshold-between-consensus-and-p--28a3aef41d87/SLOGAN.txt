On the three-agent path in the pure neighborhood Deffuant–Weisbuch model, confidence \(\epsilon\) crosses a sharp threshold at one-half of the initial leaf-opinion gap: at or below it the leaves freeze and the center fluctuates indefinitely, whereas above it all agents converge almost surely to their common mean. The frozen phase has an explicit Bernoulli-convolution stationary law, uniform at compromise strength \(\rho=1/2\); this classical stochastic mechanism is generated endogenously by the model's directed confidence gate.
