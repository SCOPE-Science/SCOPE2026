Knowing both letter counts and just the scattered-subword counts for \(ab\) and \(aab\) determines all binary words through length six, but yields exactly two ambiguous pairs at length seven. Even with exactly three \(b\)'s, explicit observations at length \(2\lceil7^{r/2}\rceil+4\) have exactly \(\lfloor r/2\rfloor+1\) preimages, so ambiguity is unbounded; with at most two \(b\)'s reconstruction is always unique.
