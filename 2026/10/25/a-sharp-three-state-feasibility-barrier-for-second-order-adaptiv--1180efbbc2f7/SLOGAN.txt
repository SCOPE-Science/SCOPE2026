For the SSPRK(3,3) stages with adaptive, formally second-order endpoint weights, every two-state continuous-time Markov chain admits positivity-preserving weights at every step, but the equal-rate three-state pure-birth chain does so exactly through \(\lambda h\le4\). At the boundary the sole feasible weights yield the identity step, and beyond it no real weights satisfying the second-order constraints can preserve endpoint positivity.
