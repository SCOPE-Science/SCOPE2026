The sharp universal real Banach-valued Lipschitz extension constant from the leaves of a symmetric double-star tree to both hubs has an exact rational formula, with optimal positive interpolation and a support transition at hub separation \(2m/(m-2)\) for \(m>2\). At and beyond this threshold the constant is exactly \(2-2/m\), whereas the two-leaves-per-hub case approaches one without ever losing cross-cluster support.
