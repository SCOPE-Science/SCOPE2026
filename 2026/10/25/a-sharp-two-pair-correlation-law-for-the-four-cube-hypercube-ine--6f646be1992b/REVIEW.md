**Correctness — PASS.** The finite four-cube identity is verified independently by complete exact integer determinant enumeration over all 4,368 five-vertex subsets and coefficient grouping; its factored polynomial agrees with the displayed exact ratio. Every coordinate marginal is unbiased, giving the requisite facet normalization. Exact differentiation proves the unique sharp maximum and strict monotonicity on all positive parameters. No finite weight sample is used as an infinite-domain proof.

Therefore the previously claimed role as a confirmation of an *open* conjecture is obsolete. Prior exact-table duplication remains a qualified residual risk; absence of an identical search result does not certify priority.

The derivative profile and matching-pair determinant identity distinguish it from the universal qualitative inequality and the distinct Hamming-weight symmetry cone. This exact structural invariant is potentially useful for studying stability and strength of the now-established inequality.
