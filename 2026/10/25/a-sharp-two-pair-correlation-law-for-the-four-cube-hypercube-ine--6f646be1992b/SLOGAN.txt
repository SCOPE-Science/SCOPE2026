On the four-dimensional Hypercube polynomial, the natural family of two independent unbiased bit pairs with arbitrary within-pair correlations \(\rho,\sigma\) has the exact normalized ratio \((1-\rho^2)(1-\sigma^2)[105+15(\rho^2+\sigma^2)-7\rho^2\sigma^2]/128\). Uniform weights uniquely maximize the ratio at \(105/128\). This is a sharp quantitative correlation law complementing, not reproving, the already established unrestricted Hypercube inequality.
