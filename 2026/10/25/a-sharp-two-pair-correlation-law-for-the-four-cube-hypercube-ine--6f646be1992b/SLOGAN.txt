On the first open four-cube Hypercube inequality, two independent unbiased bit pairs with arbitrary within-pair correlations \(\rho,\sigma\) have an exact normalized simplex-volume ratio \((1-\rho^2)(1-\sigma^2)[105+15(\rho^2+\sigma^2)-7\rho^2\sigma^2]/128\). The uniform weights uniquely maximize this ratio at \(105/128\), proving a sharp \(23/128\) relative gap for this natural correlated family without resolving the unrestricted conjecture.
