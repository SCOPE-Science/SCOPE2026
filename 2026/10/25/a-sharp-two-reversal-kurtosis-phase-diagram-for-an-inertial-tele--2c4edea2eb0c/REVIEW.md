# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** For all strictly positive damping, stiffness, amplitude, and telegraph flip rates, the stable oscillator has a unique stationary law with bounded moments. Generator identities give the exact second and fourth moments. The fourth cumulant factors into an explicitly signed fifth-degree polynomial divided by a strictly positive denominator. Strict convexity in the flip rate and a uniform positive damping derivative prove the unique cusp and exhaustive zero counts; two exact rational values bracket its damping location. The standalone checker passed 683 assertions, including 672 exact stationarity residuals. Finite tests are not used to infer global uniqueness.

**Originality — PASS (self-review).** Dutta et al. (arXiv:2406.06120) already analyze the identical inertial run-and-tumble model and derive a recursion sufficient in principle to compute arbitrary moments. The assessed difference is the factored fourth-cumulant sign polynomial and globally sharp double-reversal damping phase diagram, not generic moment computation. Sun et al. (arXiv:2409.00578) use a different first-order run/stop model. Pawula's 1987 broader dichotomous-filter work is plausible prior coverage; only its abstract was accessible, so exact priority remains at risk. No claim of certified global uniqueness or publication priority is made.

**Value — PASS.** The discovered boundary sharply separates an underdamped range where negative, positive, then negative displacement fourth cumulants occur as switching accelerates from a larger region where they cannot occur. This resolves a specific open-ended issue beyond position variance and density-shape descriptions: the sign of the leading non-Gaussian stationary statistic, with an exact reproducible phase diagram and a physical sign-changing-filter mechanism.

**Limitations.** These are stationary single-particle moment results, not density modality, thermodynamic phase transition, finite-sample inference, or interacting transport claims. The quantitative threshold digits are approximate. The earliest motivating public source is arXiv:2406.06120 (2024-06-10); the general recursion and oscillator model are prior. Full text of the relevant 1987 filter work could not be inspected. The work is not independently audited.
