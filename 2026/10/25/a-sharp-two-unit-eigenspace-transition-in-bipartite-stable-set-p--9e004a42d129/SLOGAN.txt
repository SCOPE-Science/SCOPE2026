For the complete-bipartite stable-set polytope, the smallest uniform-volume covariance eigenspace is longitudinal exactly when \(|p-q|\le1\), but transverse exactly when \(|p-q|\ge2\), with no integer tie. Balanced instances have largest covariance eigenvalue exactly \(1/12\); along every growth path the affine-invariant isotropic constant approaches \(1/\sqrt{12}\).
