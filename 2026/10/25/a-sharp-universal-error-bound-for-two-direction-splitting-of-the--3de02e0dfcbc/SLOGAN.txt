For two symmetric dissipative tensor factors, direction splitting of the first exponential-integral matrix function always underestimates the exact operator in Loewner order, and its **sharp** worst-case spectral-norm error is \(0.06613989397537867\ldots\), attained exactly when both factor spectra include the same unique dimensionless stiffness. The corresponding relative underestimation can approach \(100\%\) as both factors become very stiff.
