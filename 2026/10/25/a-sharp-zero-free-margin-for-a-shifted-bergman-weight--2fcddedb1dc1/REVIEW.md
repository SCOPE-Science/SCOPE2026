# Review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The weighted-kernel decomposition reduces the problem to the displayed polynomial \(P_r\). The numerator/denominator comparison proves that \(P_r\) is zero-free on the closed bidisk for every \(0\le r<1\). The torus reduction and the two curvature cases give the exact minimum \((1-r)^2\), with equality at \(z=v=1\).

## Originality

**PASS, with bounded residual risk.** The inspected primary source gives the general one-zero decomposition, and a later source gives a finite-Blaschke-factor representation. Neither inspected text states the sharp global minimum of this canonical numerator. Targeted literature searches found related radial-weight and jet-kernel work but no exact match in the inspected sources. This is a claim about the additional sharp extremal inequality, not a claim to have invented the kernel decomposition.

## Value

**PASS.** The Lu Qi-Keng property is qualitative. The exact numerator margin is equivalently the sharp sup-norm radius for preserving its zero-free property under additive perturbations. This gives an exact robustness threshold that degenerates quadratically as the weight's interior zero approaches the boundary.
