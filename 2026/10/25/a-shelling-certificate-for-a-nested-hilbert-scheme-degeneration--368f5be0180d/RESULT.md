# A shelling certificate for a nested Hilbert-scheme degeneration

## Finding

For \(n=4\), let \(V=[5]\times[4]\) be the 20 vertices indexed by variables \(w_{i,j}\). Let \(K_4\) be the square-free monomial ideal generated by the supports of \(x_2,\ldots,x_5,y_2,\ldots,y_5,z_3,\ldots,z_5\) in Proposition 7.3 of Ramkumar–Sammartano, and let \(\Delta_4\) be its Stanley–Reisner complex. Then \(\Delta_4\) is shellable. It has exactly 50 facets, each containing 16 vertices, so its dimension is 15. The included order of the 50 facets is a shelling order. Therefore \(k[\Delta_4]\) is Cohen–Macaulay over every field \(k\).

## Assumptions and scope

The vertex set is \(V=[5]\times[4]\). A face is a subset of \(V\) containing none of the 11 generator supports. A complementary face (a c-face) is a set meeting every generator support; a c-facet is a minimal such set. The paper identifies this complex as the initial complex used in its local analysis of nested Hilbert schemes, with matrix parameter \(n=4\), corresponding to the compressed-pair case \(m=\binom{5}{2}=10\). This finding concerns the finite simplicial complex and its Stanley–Reisner ring only. It does not assert characteristic-independent rational singularities or Cohen–Macaulayness of the original nested Hilbert scheme.

## Proof

For each of the 11 square-free monomial generators, form its support as a subset of \(V\) using the formulas in Proposition 7.3. A set \(C\subseteq V\) is a c-face exactly when it intersects every support. It is a c-facet exactly when it is a c-face and deleting any one of its vertices destroys that property. The included standard-library Python program enumerates all \(2^{20}=1,048,576\) subsets of \(V\), so this step is exhaustive, not a partial search. It finds exactly 50 minimal c-faces, all of cardinality 4. Taking complements gives exactly 50 facets of \(\Delta_4\), all of cardinality 16.

The certificate lists the omitted 4-vertex set for each facet in the proposed order. For every current facet \(F_j\) and every earlier facet \(F_i\), the verifier checks that there is an earlier facet \(F_k\) such that \(|F_j\setminus F_k|=1\) and \(F_i\cap F_j\subseteq F_k\cap F_j\). This is the pure shelling criterion. All ordered pairs pass. Hence \(\Delta_4\) is pure and shellable of dimension 15. The standard shellability theorem for pure simplicial complexes then gives that its Stanley–Reisner ring is Cohen–Macaulay over any field.

## Verification

Run `python nested_hilbert_n4_shelling.py` in an environment with Python 3. The program has no third-party dependencies. It independently reconstructs the 11 supports, exhaustively enumerates all subsets, checks minimality of every cover, constructs the shelling order deterministically, and verifies the shelling criterion for every earlier/current facet pair. The accompanying `nested_hilbert_n4_shelling.expected.txt` records the output of the included program. The finite computation is complete for this 20-vertex complex; it is not evidence for any \(n\ne4\).

## Relationship to prior work

Ramkumar and Sammartano define the same complex in *Rational singularities of nested Hilbert schemes*, Proposition 7.3 and Section 7. They prove that it is Cohen–Macaulay in characteristic zero (Corollary 7.12), then explicitly ask whether it is Cohen–Macaulay in all characteristics and whether it is shellable or vertex-decomposable (Question 7.13; also Question 9.7). The finite shelling certificate here resolves the shellability question for the parameter \(n=4\), and thus proves the characteristic-independent Cohen–Macaulay property of this one complex. It does not settle either question for general \(n\).

## Limitations

Only the case \(n=4\) is established. The result does not prove shellability or vertex-decomposability for all \(n\), does not determine the topology of \(\Delta_n\) for other parameters, and does not establish \(F\)-rationality in positive characteristic for \(\operatorname{Hilb}^{(m,2)}(\mathbb A^2)\). The shelling certificate is a finite combinatorial proof; an independent mathematical audit has not been performed.
