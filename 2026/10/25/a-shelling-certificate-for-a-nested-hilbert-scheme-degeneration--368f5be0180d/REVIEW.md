The 11 supports used for n=4 agree with the x_h, y_h and z_h products of Proposition 7.3. A complement is a face exactly when the omitted set hits every support; maximality corresponds to cover minimality. The independent replay `/workspace/branches/f082b7c1-d2c5-4bfc-8b30-e5ab3104cb79/independent_n4_check.py` exhausts all 1,048,576 subsets and tests that every vertex of a minimal cover is the unique hit of some support. It finds 50 covers, all of size four. The 50 omitted sets in the frozen `verification/nested_hilbert_n4_shelling.expected.txt` give exactly their complements without repetition. In the given order, every one of the 1,225 earlier/current pairs has a witnessing earlier facet whose intersection with the current one has codimension one and contains the pair's intersection. This is precisely the pure shelling criterion: the intersection with the earlier union is generated by codimension-one faces. Dimension is 16−1=15, and pure shellability implies that this Stanley–Reisner ring is Cohen–Macaulay over every field. Neither the finite enumeration nor the criterion establishes any result for other n or characteristic-independent singularity statements about the original scheme.
