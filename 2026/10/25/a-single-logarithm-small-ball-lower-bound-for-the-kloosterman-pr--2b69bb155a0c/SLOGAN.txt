Dyadically graded, conditionally centered Fourier coefficients improve the uniform Kloosterman-process small-ball lower bound from an \(\varepsilon^{-2}\log^2(2/\varepsilon)\) exponent to \(\varepsilon^{-2}\log(2/\varepsilon)\), for the same closed Hölder-\(1/2\) target class with interior Sato--Tate Fourier coefficients.
