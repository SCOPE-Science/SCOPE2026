For the Tutte–Coxeter eight-cage, the 360 disjoint-octagon pairs with two bridge edges whose endpoints lie three cycle steps apart on both octagons generate \(H_2(\operatorname{UConf}_2(|T|);\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^{90}\) integrally. A deterministic selection of 90 of their product tori has a signed square-coordinate minor of determinant \(-1\); none of the other three individual six-letter symmetry orbits generates integrally.
