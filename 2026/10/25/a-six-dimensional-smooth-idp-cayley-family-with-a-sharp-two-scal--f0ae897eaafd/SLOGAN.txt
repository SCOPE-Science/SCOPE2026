A positive four-box Cayley construction produces infinitely many smooth six-dimensional lattice polytopes with regular unimodular flag triangulations and non-log-concave \(h^*\)-polynomials. Its exact two-parameter log-concavity test has a single negative interval with asymptotic endpoints \(b\sim8.63359556a\) and \(b\sim a^3/69\); on the integer ray \(b=36a\) failure begins precisely at \(a=100\).
