For two noise-free leaders on \(K_{n,n}\) with exactly one missing link, the unique optimal pair for \(3\le n\le5\) comprises the missing link's endpoints, while for every \(n\ge6\) the only optimal pairs are two healthy vertices on the same side. The switch is governed exactly by the sign of \(n^3-6n^2+5n-1\), and all other pair types are strictly dominated.
