In characteristic \(5\), the exact nonordinary locus of a natural genus-four Jacobian-square pencil is a six-punctured rational curve, with four rational and two quadratic punctures. Over \(\mathbb F_{5^m}\) it has \(5^m-4-2\mathbf1_{2\mid m}\) smooth coordinate-marked points, and its unique \(\mathbb F_5\)-point has supersingular, nonsuperspecial Jacobian.
