For six-tip rooted phylogenetic trees, a simple shared-catastrophe extinction model strictly ranks all six tree shapes by excess expected evolutionary-diversity loss for every interior extinction probability. Two trees that tie at the minimum Sackin balance index nevertheless differ by exactly \(\theta q^2(1-q)^2\) in excess loss, and replacing the worse six-tip fringe shape by the better one preserves every species' leaf depth in arbitrarily large trees.
