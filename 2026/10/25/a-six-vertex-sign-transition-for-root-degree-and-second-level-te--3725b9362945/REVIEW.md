## Originality
PASS on inspected evidence, with a substantial residual historical-risk caveat. The 2002 full level-size covariance theorem concerns all vertices at a depth and does not directly identify terminal vertices. The 2022 protected-node article's accessible abstract concerns protected/nonprotected profiles and asymptotic correlations, not the specific exact root-degree versus second-level-terminal-count covariance. A published root-degree versus all-leaves identity is insufficient to recover the depth-two component. The text of the 1982 degrees-by-stratum paper was not accessible, so implicit prior coverage cannot be excluded.

## Value
PASS. The statistic couples the first two generations with a terminal condition, giving a sharp finite sign boundary and logarithmically diverging covariance, in contrast to the bounded covariance for root degree versus all terminal leaves. This establishes cancellation across terminal generations and distinguishes depth-specific interactions from global marginals.

## Limitations
The result is for uniform recursive trees and depth-two terminal leaves. No general depth profile theorem or independence conclusion is claimed. Literature access and historical-priority uncertainties remain.
