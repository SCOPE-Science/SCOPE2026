In a uniform random recursive tree, root outdegree and the number of childless vertices exactly two edges from the root have covariance \(H_{n-2}-H_{n-2}^{(2)}-(n-2)^2/(n-1)^2\). This is negative for \(3\le n\le5\), positive for every \(n\ge6\), and grows logarithmically despite the bounded covariance with the total leaf count.
