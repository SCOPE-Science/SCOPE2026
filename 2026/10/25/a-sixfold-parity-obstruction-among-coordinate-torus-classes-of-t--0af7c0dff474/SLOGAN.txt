In the ordinary two-point configuration space of the four-cube graph, the 132 tori formed by disjoint coordinate square faces integrally generate \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z^{88}\). The 96 nonparallel-face tori already span rationally, but their integral subgroup has quotient exactly \((\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^6\); adjoining either parallel-face orbit eliminates this parity obstruction.
