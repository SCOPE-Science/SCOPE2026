The Paley graph on \(25\) vertices has a flag complex homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{64}S^2\), with simple connectivity proved by explicit integral fundamental-group eliminations rather than inferred from known Betti numbers. Its full \(600\)-element semiaffine symmetry has rational second-homology character histogram \(\{(-16,15),(-1,72),(0,450),(4,62),(64,1)\}\) and no invariant vectors, refining a previously known restriction to translations.
