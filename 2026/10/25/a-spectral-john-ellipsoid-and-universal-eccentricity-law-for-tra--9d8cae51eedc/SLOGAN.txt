For every connected vertex- and edge-transitive bipartite graph of degree at least three, the John ellipsoid of its stable-set polytope has a signless-Laplacian precision matrix, a uniquely determined scalar spectral parameter, and tangency with every facet. Its squared-semiaxis condition number is strictly between \(1+\sqrt2\) and \(3\), converging universally to \(1+\sqrt2\) along any sequence with growing degree.
