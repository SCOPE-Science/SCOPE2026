For noise-free coherence minimization on \(K_{m,m+1}\), every optimal set with \(k\le m\) places all its leaders in exactly one bipartition part: the smaller part if \(k^2<2m+1\), the larger if \(k^2>2m+1\), and either entire-part choice (never a mixed set) at equality. Above that range, all vertices of the larger part must be leaders, so the full budget-dependent optimum and its unavoidable non-nesting have exact formulas.
