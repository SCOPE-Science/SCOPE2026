In characteristic \(2\), the intrinsic Jacobian singular scheme of the cubic determinant of a generic symmetric \(3\)-by-\(3\) matrix is not the reduced Veronese surface: it is a degree-eight complete intersection of three quadrics, a square-zero doubling with nilradical \(\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^2}(-3)\). Its homogeneous-coordinate Hilbert series is \(\frac{(1+t)^3}{(1-t)^3}\), even though the ambient cubic is integral and normal.
