In the shift whose consecutive markers are separated by perfect-square numbers of zeros, the exact sparse-marker entropy is exponentially close to an explicit cubic-root expression. Its entropy per marker begins with \(\frac12\log(1/t)+\frac12\log(\pi/2)+\frac12-\sqrt{t/(2\pi)}\), and each positive marker density below \(1/2\) has a unique entropy-maximizing renewal measure.
