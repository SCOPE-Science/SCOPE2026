The Euclidean mean width of \(\mathcal P(m,k)\), with \(k/m\to\alpha>0\), grows like \(2m^{3/2}H(\alpha)\) for an explicit Gaussian-tail function \(H\) that is twice but not three times differentiable at \(\alpha=1/2\). The same threshold is the sharp normal-angle saturation boundary: a random exposed vertex has \(\min\{k,\operatorname{Bin}(m,1/2)\}\) nonzero entries.
