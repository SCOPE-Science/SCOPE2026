For a uniformly viewed regular tetrahedron, the probabilities of nearly minimum-area triangular and quadrilateral shadows have the same leading coefficient, but their cubic coefficients differ. Conditioned on an area surplus below \(\varepsilon\), the triangular-shadow probability is \(1/2+(\sqrt2/24)\varepsilon+O(\varepsilon^2)\), giving a sharp first-order silhouette bias at the brightness minimum.
