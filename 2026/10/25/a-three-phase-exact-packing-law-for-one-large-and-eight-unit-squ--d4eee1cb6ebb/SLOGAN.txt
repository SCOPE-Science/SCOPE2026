For nine axis-parallel squares consisting of one side-\(t\) square and eight unit squares, the exact optimal container side is \(t+2\) for \(2\le t\le3\), stays exactly \(5\) for \(3\le t\le4\), and becomes \(t+1\) for \(t\ge4\). A two-strip crossing obstruction certifies the sharp plateau, while explicit strip packings attain every phase.
