For uniform-matroid independence polytopes, Euclidean uniform-volume covariance has a sharp three-regime rank transition: a collapsing sum-direction eigenvalue below half rank, a truncated-Gaussian critical profile, and asymptotic cube isotropy above half rank. At exact half rank the transverse eigenvalue is identically \(1/12\) in every even dimension, while the longitudinal eigenvalue has an explicit nontrivial deficit.
