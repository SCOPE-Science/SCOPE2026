# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The simplicial vertices are the 4n directed arcs of bidirected K_{2,n}; subsets are admitted precisely when indegrees and outdegrees are ≤1 and no directed cycle occurs. Each face has ≤4 arcs. Independent enumeration gives all augmented face vectors for n=1..4; exact integral unit-incidence Schur cancellations give surviving reduced chain ranks for n=4 of eight degree-two generators and one degree-three generator with boundary coefficients −2,2,2. Its Smith divisor 2 yields Htilde_2=Z^7⊕Z/2 with no other n4 homology; previous n=1..3 groups match the claimed free homology. The arc-overlap graph of oriented four-cycles is connected, so Definition 4.2(b) of Caputi et al forces every nonempty dynamical region to contain all arcs; the connected whole graph meets the axioms and is the unique minimal module, and every vertex has positive in/out degree. Thus it is an unstable module. Independent standard-library integer cancellation reproduced face vectors (1,16,80,144,72), pivot sequence (71,65,15,1) and residual divisor two. This is an exhaustive finite computation because the chain and all faces were enumerated.

Caputi–Collari–Di Trani–Smith state the conjecture that a multipath complex of a torsion-bearing dynamical module should be stable and calculate other orientations including alternating bipartite graphs; their complete-bipartite orientation is not bidirected K2,4. The new explicit integral 2-torsion in an unstable single module directly contradicts that conjecture, not merely a nearby special-case bound. Published bidirected polygon computations and directed-tree matching complexes are different digraph families. No prior exact bidirected K2,4 integral calculation was located in the queried literature or Resultary.
