The bidirected complete bipartite digraph \(\overleftrightarrow{K}_{2,4}\) is a single **unstable** dynamical module, yet its multipath complex has \(\widetilde H_2\cong\mathbb Z^7\oplus\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\). This gives a concrete integral-torsion counterexample to the proposed necessity of stable dynamical modules, and the two-sided biclique family is torsion-free at sizes \(n=1,2,3\).
