The Lovász open-neighborhood complex of \(K_3\square C_n\) simplicially collapses, for every \(n\ge5\), onto an explicit triangulated torus in exactly \(6n\) elementary moves. Its noncirculant subfamily \(3\mid n\), \(n\ge6\), supplies a uniform torus classification at the exceptional three-color boundary of the clique-cycle product family.
