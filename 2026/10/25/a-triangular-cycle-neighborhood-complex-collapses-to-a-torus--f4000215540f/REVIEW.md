Each graph vertex (a,b) has unique open-neighborhood tetrahedron F_ab={(a,b+1),(a,b-1),(a+1,b),(a-1,b)} for n>=5. The two vertical other-fiber vertices share only (a,b) as common neighbor, giving each triangle {u,v,p} a unique tetrahedral coface. After its deletion the edge {u,v} has exactly one surviving triangle {u,v,m}, producing a second free-face pair. Enumerating residual faces gives 6n triangles, 9n edges and 3n vertices. A parity-even infinite triangular tiling with edge directions (2,0),(1,1),(1,-1) quotiented by the rank-two translation lattice Λ∩(nZ×3Z) is identified with the residual simplicial complex. Since no fundamental triangle is self-identified for n>=5, the quotient is an orientable torus, establishing the nonzero H1 cup product. Independent reconstruction of all tetrahedra, collapse pairs, links and orientability for 17 distinct n=5..21 corroborates; the periodic planar covering is the all-n argument.
