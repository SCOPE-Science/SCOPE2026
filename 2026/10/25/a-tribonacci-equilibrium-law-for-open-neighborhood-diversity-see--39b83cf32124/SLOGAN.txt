On every fully occupied two-color cycle with a strict preference for one neighbor of each type, **all** mutually profitable swaps are adjacent and remove a four-vertex alternating pattern; hence every trajectory reaches a stable coloring in at most \(\lfloor(n-2)/2\rfloor\) moves. The entire equilibrium-by-color polynomial comes from an explicit eight-state transfer matrix, and the total number of equilibrium colorings is \(U_n+1+2\cos(n\pi/2)\), with \(U_n\) the tribonacci-Lucas sequence.
