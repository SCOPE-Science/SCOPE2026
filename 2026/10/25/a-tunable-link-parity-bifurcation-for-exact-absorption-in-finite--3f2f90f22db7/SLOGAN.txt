A tunable joining bond converts the known even-length obstruction to band-center perfect absorption into a sharp bifurcation: below a critical coupling there is no matching loss, at threshold there is one, and above it there are exactly two. The critical coupling approaches the homogeneous value with a logarithmic finite-length correction, and at threshold the reflection intensity has the universal quartic coefficient \((2n+1)^2/16\) in the natural loss coordinate.
