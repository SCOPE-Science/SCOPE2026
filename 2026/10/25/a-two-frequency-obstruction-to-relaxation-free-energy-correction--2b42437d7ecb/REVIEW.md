# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The exact SSPRK(3,3) stage equations and the endpoint-weight perturbation uniquely give the cubic multiplier displayed in RESULT.md. Evaluating the multiplier at both imaginary frequencies yields exact rational quadratic defects. The orthogonal-block energy is a convex combination, whose discriminant is a rational polynomial with the claimed two roots. Its strictly positive leading coefficient makes the sign test necessary and sufficient, including both boundary fractions. At the rational fraction \(w=2/3\), a completed-square identity yields the positive uniform squared-energy gap \(223/2908\). `verify.py` reproduces critical identities with fractions and also tests the original four-dimensional stages. Numerical cases are not used as infinite proofs. The result has no hidden assumption of admissible positivity of altered RK weights.

**Originality — PASS (best-knowledge).** The initial paper [1] already introduces RF-RK, its scalar discriminant, the RF-SSPRK(3,3) perturbation vector, and the fact that correction may fail for large steps. The assessed distinct contribution is the *exact classification of energy fractions on a four-dimensional orthogonal direct sum where both separate modes are correctable but the coupled step has no real correction*. The complete phase interval and rational gap certificate do not follow from an earlier two-stage SSPRK claim, which concerns a different correction direction and does not exhibit individually feasible modes becoming jointly infeasible. The related quasi-orthogonal method [2] uses a different correction family. Bencomo and Chan [3] study variable-time-step relaxation and adjoints. Primary full-text sections concerning feasibility, linear spectra, and examples were read for [1], and the projection construction and introduction were examined for [2]; the relevant motivation, abstract, and method definition were inspected for [3]. Targeted searches of primary literature and mathematical publications showed no explicit covering statement. Residual priority risk: a previously published general non-closure theorem or independently rediscovered two-frequency RF-SSPRK(3,3) calculation could entail the result; neither complete indexing nor independent assessment is claimed.

**Value — PASS.** Solvability of the scalar energy correction is a design constraint for fixed-step methods on multimode skew dynamics. The theorem gives a structural limitation that is not visible by checking each mode in isolation: *individual feasibility is not closed under a direct sum*. The complete energy-fraction obstruction interval, its double-root endpoints, and the strictly positive exact gap for a rational witness quantify when a global correction cannot exist. The construction is at the smallest natural dimension accommodating two independent nonzero planar oscillator frequencies. It motivates multimode safeguard diagnostics or method changes; it is not a theorem about all energy-preserving schemes.

Scientific limitations: exact step size one, exact arithmetic, fixed RF-SSPRK(3,3) endpoint-weight direction, and exactly two specified frequencies. The result is compatible with small-step solvability and does not rule out other correction techniques. Independent priority verification is still needed.
