For the fixed-step relaxation-free SSPRK(3,3) correction, two individually energy-correctable oscillator modes need not remain correctable when excited together. An explicit four-dimensional two-frequency system has real energy-preserving correction exactly at mode-energy fractions \(w\in[0,w_-]\cup[w_+,1]\), with a nonempty open interval in which every real scalar correction strictly raises energy.
