For every \(r\ge2\) and \(m\ge2\), the maximum-independent-set exchange graphs of the cycle powers \(C_{(r+1)m+s}^r\) have three sharply different regimes for \(s=0,1,2\): isolated configurations, a single cycle, and a connected four-regular graph with exactly \((r+1)m+2\) triangles. In particular, this classifies the optimal exchange connectivity of all antiprism graphs and distinguishes the triangles that arise exactly when two surplus spaces can be pooled.
