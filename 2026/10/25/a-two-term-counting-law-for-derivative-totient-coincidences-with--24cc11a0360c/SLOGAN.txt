For each fixed odd prime \(p\), numbers with exactly two prime factors \(p<q\) satisfying \(D(n)=\varphi(n)\) have an explicit two-term counting law of order \(\log X/(\log\log X)^2\), with a harmonic leading constant and a computable \(\log\log\log X\) correction. The pre-existing existence construction is not claimed as new; the result quantifies its full fixed-prime distribution.
