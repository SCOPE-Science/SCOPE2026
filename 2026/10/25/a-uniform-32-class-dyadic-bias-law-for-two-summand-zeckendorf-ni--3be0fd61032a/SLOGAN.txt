For each fixed dyadic modulus \(2^a\) with \(a\ge5\), two-summand Zeckendorf–Niven integers have a universal table of 16 positive coefficient types on the even residues modulo 32, repeated with the appropriate scaling at every higher power of two. Their largest and smallest positive frequencies differ by the sharp factor \(7/3\), although their previously known aggregate frequency remains \(5/9\).
