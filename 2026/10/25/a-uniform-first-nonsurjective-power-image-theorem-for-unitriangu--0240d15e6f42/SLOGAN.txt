For every prime \(p\), the first nonsurjective \(p\)-th power map on \(UT_{p+3}(K)\) admits a single explicit six-coordinate membership test over all characteristic-\(p\) fields. Over \(\mathbb F_q\), its image contains exactly \(q^6-q^5+q^4+q^3-q^2\) elements, generalizing the already known five-by-five characteristic-two case.
