For the smooth bielliptic Picard pencil \(y^3=x^4+2t x^2+1\) in every prime characteristic \(p\ge5\), one explicit degree-\(\lfloor(p-1)/6\rfloor\) Hasse polynomial has distinct roots and locates every exceptional Jacobian Newton polygon. Its four arithmetic types have geometric \(p\)-ranks \(3,1,2,0\) according to \(p\bmod6\) and the vanishing of this polynomial; the rank-zero members are superspecial.
