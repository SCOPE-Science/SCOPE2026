For the projection widths of every regular planar polygon, angular autocovariance decreases strictly from positive to negative values within each half symmetry sector and crosses zero exactly once. At large side count, its normalized profile converges uniformly to \(1-\tfrac{15}{8}q^2(2-q)^2\), with a universal zero at \(q=1-\sqrt{1-\sqrt{8/15}}\).
