For every even \(n\ge6\), a randomly oriented central plane through a regular \(n\)-gonal prism produces an \((n+2)\)-gon with an exact arcsine probability, uniquely maximized at half-height \(\sqrt{3\cos^2(\pi/n)-1}\) when the base circumradius is one. The optimal probability decays like \(2\pi^2/(3\sqrt3\,n^2)\), whereas the edge-uniform prism has probability only asymptotic to \(\pi^4/n^4\).
