For the one-step lecture-hall simplices with parameters \((a,\ldots,a,a+1)\), the Ehrhart coefficient \([t^{n-4}]L_{n,a}(t)\) has an exact positive-prefactor quartic expression with a unique positive root for every \(n\ge5\). Its first negative integer parameter grows as \(\beta(n)\sim(15/8)^{1/3}n^{4/3}\), upgrading the known eventual-negativity result to a sharp all-dimensional sign threshold for this coefficient.
