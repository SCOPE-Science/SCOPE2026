For the normalized rank-two Nyström preconditioner on \(A=\operatorname{diag}(k,1,1)\), every nontrivial spike \(k>1\) and positive regularization admit both Gaussian sketches that worsen the original spectral condition number and sketches that improve it. A unique positive cubic root gives the exact sketch-angle crossover, and a three-state family attains an asymptotic deterioration factor of \(32/27\).
