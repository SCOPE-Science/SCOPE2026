**Correctness — PASS.** The stationary generator yields an exact triangular recurrence for the mixed position-orientation moments; direct symbolic elimination gives the displayed third centered moment with a strictly positive denominator. The coefficient inequalities exclude every positive-to-negative reversal in the quartic sequence, so Descartes' rule and endpoint signs prove a unique simple positive crossing. Symmetry resolves the degenerate equal-rate case. The included exact symbolic checker independently confirms the polynomial, endpoint value and auxiliary identity. The proof does not mistake numerical roots for an infinite-domain certificate.

**Originality — PASS, with residual priority uncertainty.** Asymmetric orientation and preserving orientation during resetting materially differ from previously inspected symmetric and unconfined run-and-tumble models. The general reset-moment recurrence can be derived from known generator methods, but the all-parameter sign-reversal classification is not an immediately stated consequence of the accessible cited results. Published database searches and the available prior claim ledger were checked for equivalent quartic-root, skewness, telegraph and reset formulations. Full texts of certain relevant papers could not be inspected; their possible stronger coverage remains a priority risk, not an assertion that they are known not to cover the theorem.

**Value — PASS.** A sharp universal stationary-observable transition with one and only one reset threshold is a motivated phase classification, not an arbitrary parameter slice. It supplies a new qualitative response signature separating fast, position-only resetting from the unreset, orientationally biased harmonic trap. It also isolates an experimentally meaningful distinction between resetting the position alone and refreshing the internal motor orientation.

**Scope and closest literature.** Wang et al. (2014) establish harmonic confinement of active swimmers without this asymmetric reset classification.
