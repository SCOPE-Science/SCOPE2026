The same normalized function \(g(z)=\operatorname{arctanh}z\) attains the exact classical Bloch operator norm of every positive Hausdorff matrix whose reciprocal first moment is finite. For a single atom at \(0<t<1\), the norm strictly exceeds its spectral radius \(1/t\), and the sharp norm-to-radius ratio tends to \(C_*\approx1.1176848412\) as \(t\downarrow0\).
