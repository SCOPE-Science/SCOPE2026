A chord drawn between two independent perimeter-uniform points of any Euclidean rectangle has an exact logarithmic law for the smallest concentric homothetic rectangle it meets; a single atom identifies the rectangle's aspect ratio. Remarkably, every aspect ratio has the same hit probability \(0.4381056198\ldots\) at one and only one nontrivial inner scale \(0.5604879233\ldots\), and the ordering of the probability curves reverses there.
