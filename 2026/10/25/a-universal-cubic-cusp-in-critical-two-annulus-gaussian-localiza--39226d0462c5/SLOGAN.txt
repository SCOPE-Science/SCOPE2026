For two Gaussian localization annuli approaching their mode-splitting threshold at separation \(\delta_R=1+\kappa R^{-1/3}\), the Hermite spectrum develops a universal quartic cusp, with a sharp one-to-two-peak transition at \(\kappa=1/2\). An asymmetric first-order correction selects the inward peak as the unique global optimizer and gives the exact \(R^{-2/3}\) relative norm correction.
