# Verification and limits
The proof is analytic: Feynman–Kac survival (under the stated exogenous risk-path model), weighted-time sampling, two conditional Jensen inequalities, and the unique positive root of a convex final-size equation. Each step is displayed in RESULT.md. The finite numerical example uses the three-dimensional two-risk-class SIR ODE; `verify.py` compares two integration step sizes and independently solves the benchmark final-size roots.

Finite tests do not establish the universal theorem. The theorem does not assume a finite-state Markov process, and the numerical code does not sample arbitrary paths. Publication priority is provisional; no external independent verification has been undertaken.
