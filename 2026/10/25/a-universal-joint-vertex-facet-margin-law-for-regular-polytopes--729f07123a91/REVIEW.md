# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The geometric definitions distinguish facet supporting planes from finite facets. On each nondegenerate characteristic simplex the squared-distance vertex gap and supporting-plane facet gap are affine; endpoint comparisons prove that their first and second optimizers are fixed. The values at the characteristic vertices prove that these normalized gaps are different Dirichlet barycentric coordinates. Their exact joint density and all corollaries follow by Dirichlet integration. Numerical checking on the five three-dimensional Platonic solids is supplemental, not a replacement for the dimensional proof.

## Originality
**PASS (bibliographic risk acknowledged).** The closest accessible documented prior finding gives only the nearest-vertex marginal \(U\sim\operatorname{Beta}(1,d)\), not the facet-side observable or joint dependence. The new result does **not** claim that beta marginal as new; it adds a simultaneous primal–dual law with support restriction, correlation, and asymptotic independence. Bäsel's regular-polygon point-distance calculations and Kabluchko–Litvak–Zaporozhets' regular-polytope projection-width formulas use distinct stochastic observables and do not imply this law. Search incompleteness and possibly unindexed equivalent flag-cell statements remain risks.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** Joint robustness of both vertex and facet decisions is a natural geometric descriptor. The universal exclusion region \(U+V\le1\), exact negative dependence, and large-dimensional independent-exponential limit are not recoverable from knowledge of either marginal alone. The result holds for all regular-polytope types, not a hand-picked low-dimensional example.

## Limitations
No claim of novelty for the already documented vertex marginal or of extension to semiregular polytopes, arbitrary sampling laws or distances to finite facet sets. No independent scientific verification was performed.
