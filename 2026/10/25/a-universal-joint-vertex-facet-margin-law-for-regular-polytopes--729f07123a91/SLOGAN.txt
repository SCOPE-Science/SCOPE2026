For a uniform point in any regular Euclidean \(d\)-polytope, the normalized gap between its two nearest vertices and the normalized gap between its two nearest facet supporting planes are the first two coordinates of \(\operatorname{Dirichlet}(1,\ldots,1)\). Both are \(\operatorname{Beta}(1,d)\) marginally, but jointly they satisfy \(U+V\le1\) and have the universal correlation \(-1/d\).
