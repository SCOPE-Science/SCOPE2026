For irreversible dimer adsorption on a wheel with rim-edge rate one and any positive spoke rate, the hub survives only on even rims; its exact probability is a hypergeometric ratio coefficient. For \(n=2m+2\) rim vertices, the survival probability has the sharp law \(P_n=2\rho^{-m-1}+O(R^{-m})\), whose amplitude is universally \(2\) even as the rate-dependent decay constant \(\rho\) changes.
