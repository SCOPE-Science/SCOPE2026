The scalar incoherent feedforward model \(\dot x=-x+u\), \(\dot y=u-xy\) has the universal finite-horizon high-dose law \(C(u,T)\sim\frac12\log u\), independent of its nonnegative initial state. An exact initial-load threshold forces \(C(u,T)\) to decrease at small input but eventually diverge, giving a rigorous nonmonotone cumulative response outside the pre-adapted regime.
