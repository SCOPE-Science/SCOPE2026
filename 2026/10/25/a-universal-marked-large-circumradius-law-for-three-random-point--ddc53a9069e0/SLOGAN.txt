For any planar convex body, triangles from three area-uniform points with circumradius above \(T\) have probability asymptotic to \(Q_6(K)/(30A(K)^3T)\), where \(Q_6\) is the sixth chord-power integral. Conditioned on this rare event, the chord line is sixth-power biased, while four normalized point gaps obey the universal mixture \(\tfrac12\operatorname{Dirichlet}(1,3,2,1)+\tfrac12\operatorname{Dirichlet}(1,2,3,1)\), independently of a uniform transverse mark.
