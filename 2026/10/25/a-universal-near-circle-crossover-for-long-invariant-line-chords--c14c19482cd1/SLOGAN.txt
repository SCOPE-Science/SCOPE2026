A disk-to-oval perturbation of the same size as a long-chord deficit has an exact universal crossover \(G_f(x)=\pi^{-1}\int_0^\pi\sqrt{(1-x[M-f(\theta)-f(\theta+\pi)])_+}\,d\theta\). Only the even part of the support deformation matters at leading order; isolated nondegenerate longest-width directions give a sharp inverse-square-root far-crossover law.
