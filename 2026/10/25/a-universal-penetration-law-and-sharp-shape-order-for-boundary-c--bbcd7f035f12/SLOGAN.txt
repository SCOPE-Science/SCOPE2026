For two independent perimeter-uniform boundary points of any Euclidean triangle, the smallest centroidal homothetic core hit by their chord has an exact logarithmic law whose entire shape dependence is \(\sum_i(\ell_i/P)^2\). Its conditional law given distinct sides is universal; the equilateral triangle uniquely maximizes penetration probabilities at every interior scale, while nonsimilar triangles can share the same law.
