For a finite abelian group, two allowed Latin sum levels with nongenerating gap split into \(g^2\) orientable surfaces of genus \((h-1)(h-2)/2\), wedged with exactly \((g-1)(3q-g-1)\) circles; here \(h\) is the gap order and \(g=q/h\). This subgroup-index transition produces connected singular complexes with identical face counts but different integral homotopy types.
