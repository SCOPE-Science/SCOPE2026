Two independent positive instantial neighbourhood modalities agree on all complementary zero-instance scopes with probability \(p_n=4^{-n+o(n)}\) when the empty set is forbidden as a neighbourhood, with an exact weighted-antichain formula for every finite \(n\). Permitting the empty neighbourhood changes the agreement probability to exactly \((1+p_n)/4\), and hence to a limiting \(1/4\).
