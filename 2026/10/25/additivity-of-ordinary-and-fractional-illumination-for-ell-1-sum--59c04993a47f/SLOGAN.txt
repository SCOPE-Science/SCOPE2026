Both ordinary and fractional illumination are exactly additive under finite \(\ell_1\)-sums of real normed balls, because directions illuminating pure-block boundary points belong to disjoint dominant-coordinate cones. Thus the complex \(\ell_1\) unit ball in \(\mathbb C^r\) has ordinary illumination number \(3r\) and fractional illumination number \(2r\), unlike the exponential behavior of the Cartesian polydisc.
