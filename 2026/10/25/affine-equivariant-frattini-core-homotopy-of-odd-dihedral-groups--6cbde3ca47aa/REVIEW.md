## correctness

PASS

Reconstructed maximal subgroups R=<a>, M_{p,t}=<a^p,a^t s>, and Φ=<a^rad(n)> for odd n. Nerve of maximal subgroup simplices has face criterion: sets missing a whole prime block yield cone A over a fixed R apex, while those meeting all blocks are admissible only without R with exactly one vertex per prime block. Chinese remainder gives q complete (k−1) simplices σc attached along boundaries in A. The invariant cone A contracts Aut-equivariantly onto its apex and inclusion is an equivariant cofibration, so its quotient is an equivariant wedge. Each automorphism preserves prime labels, sends c to uc+v mod q, and has degree +1 on each sphere. Fixed sets and Z[Z/q] follow; solving (u−1)c≡−v mod q gives trace gcd(u−1,q) if it divides v, else 0; affine translations are transitive and quotient yields S^(k−1). Explicit k=1 S0 case is handled with q singleton points plus a fixed apex. Independent reconstruction of all maximal-subgroup intersection faces for n=15 matched the criterion, 92 nerve faces and reduced Euler −15; independent finite structural check, not proof for all n.
