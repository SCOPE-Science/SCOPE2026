For every odd dihedral group, deleting the Frattini cone vertices leaves a complex equivariantly homotopy equivalent to \(\operatorname{rad}(n)\) spheres of dimension \(\omega(n)-1\), permuted without orientation signs by the full affine automorphism group. Every subgroup fixed locus, the integral top-homology character, and the full-automorphism quotient follow explicitly from this sphere-permutation model.
