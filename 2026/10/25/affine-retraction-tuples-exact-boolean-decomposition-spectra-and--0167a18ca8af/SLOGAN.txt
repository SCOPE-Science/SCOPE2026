Compatible affine retractions over \(\mathbb F_q^n\) are classified by \(2^k\) simultaneous Boolean eigenspaces, with exact translation-fibre counts and one affine-conjugacy class per weak composition. At fixed arity \(k\), their joint dimensions obey residue-dependent discrete-Gaussian laws, and the probability that independent retractions commute is asymptotic to a positive periodic constant times \(q^{-(k/2-1+2^{-k})(n^2+n)}\).
