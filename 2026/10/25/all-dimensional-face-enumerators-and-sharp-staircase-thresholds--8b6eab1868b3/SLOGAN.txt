Every-dimensional face counts for the integer-partition transfer clique complexes have a single closed partition-product series: for cliques of size \(r\ge3\), it is \(P(q)\bigl(q^{1+\binom r2}+q^{\binom{r+1}2-1}\bigr)/\prod_{j=1}^r(1-q^j)\). This yields an exact unique staircase simplex at each new dimension threshold \(1+d(d+1)/2\), establishing both conjectured first-occurrence laws for all dimensions.
