Every group Rota–Baxter operator on the standard finite Heisenberg group of order \(p^{2n+1}\), for odd \(p\) and \(n\ge2\), is uniquely governed by a shifted symplectic-similitude equation. Exactly two scalar strata have arbitrary isotropic-image endomorphisms; the others are symplectic-similitude cosets, giving a closed all-operator count and an exact rank distribution.
