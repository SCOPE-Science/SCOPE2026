# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof covers every irrational \(\alpha\in(0,1)\), every integer \(k\ge2\), and every \(n\ge1\). The zero-position shift \(z'_i=\max\{i,z_i-k\}\) establishes monotonicity of transformed factors in phase order, preventing disjoint image fibers from being double-counted. The only merged boundaries are the swap boundaries with \(j\alpha<k\); the last-letter boundary is excluded by conserved content. The independent zero-position and repeated-operation implementations agree on 114,832 exact-rational parameter cases. Computational checks support but do not replace the general proof.

## Originality
PASS within examined sources. Campbell--Rampersad (arXiv:2604.25811, Sections 3--7) defines the invariant and poses Sturmian extensions without calculating its iterated two-parameter profile. Defant--Kravitz (arXiv:1809.09158) studies the underlying finite-word operator but not Sturmian factor-image counts. A previously known one-iteration rational-mechanical calculation covers a boundary case only, not arbitrary \(k\ge2\). Targeted mathematical-record searches identified related Sturmian work, including cyclic lifts, but not a theorem about these iterated images. Unindexed equivalent results remain possible.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The exact all-length, all-iterate formula resolves an explicit natural family raised by the motivating literature. It identifies the precise phase transition from Abelian complexity two to linear growth, computes the Abelian-agreement cutoff as \(\lfloor k/\alpha\rfloor+1\), and yields recovery of \(\alpha\) from the complete sequence of cutoffs. The zero-position order lemma supplies a reusable structural explanation rather than an isolated numerical census.

Scientific limits: binary Sturmian words only; irrational slopes; no claim of independent validation or of exhaustive historical priority.
