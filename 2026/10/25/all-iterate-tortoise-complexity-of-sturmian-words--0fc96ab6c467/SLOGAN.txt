For every irrational Sturmian slope \(\alpha\) and every iteration count \(k\ge2\), the number of distinct \(k\)-fold tortoise images of length-\(n\) factors is exactly \(\max\{2,n+1-\lfloor k/\alpha\rfloor\}\). This solves the entire two-parameter profile, pinpoints its Abelian-agreement cutoff, and lets the collection of profiles recover the slope.
