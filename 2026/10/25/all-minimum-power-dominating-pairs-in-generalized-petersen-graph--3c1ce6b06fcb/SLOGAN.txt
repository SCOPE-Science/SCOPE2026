In \(GP(n,2)\), for every \(n\ge9\), exactly \(6n\) two-vertex sensor sets power dominate: outer pairs separated by three, consecutive inner pairs, and outer–inner pairs at offsets \(\pm3,\pm4\). Their single-sensor exchange graph is connected and \(10\)-regular, while for \(n\ge13\) precisely one seventh of graph-distance-three pairs fail, contradicting an unconditional distance-three sufficiency assertion in the literature.
