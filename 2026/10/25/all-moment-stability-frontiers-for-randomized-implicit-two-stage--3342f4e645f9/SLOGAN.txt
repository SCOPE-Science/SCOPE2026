For two existing uniform-randomized implicit Runge–Kutta schemes, every positive-order moment of scalar dissipative decay is optimized by exactly the same dimensionless step \(\sqrt2\), but its sharp stability cutoff depends strictly on moment order. The cutoffs range from \(\sim12\log(1/p)/(\pi^2p)\) as \(p\downarrow0\) to \(2+\log(4p)/p+o(1/p)\) as \(p\to\infty\), quantifying how rare amplification events separate moment stability from almost-sure stability.
