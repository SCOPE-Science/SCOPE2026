For independent arclength-uniform points on an equilateral triangle, the centroid-avoidance probability of the sample hull has an exact finite sum in \(\mathbb Q+\mathbb Q\log2\) for every sample size. Its sharp large-sample form is \(3\sqrt{\pi m/5}\,(5/9)^m\), including the square-root prefactor absent from the halfplane-depth exponent alone.
