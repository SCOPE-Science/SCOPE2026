In a uniform recursive tree, birth position controls whether a vertex's outdegree is positively or negatively correlated with the total number of rooted leaves. Nonroot covariance signs form ordered blocks without a positive reversal (the positive block can be empty for small trees); the asymptotic boundary lies at the unique interior solution \(\alpha=0.284668137\ldots\) of \((1-\alpha)/2+\alpha\log\alpha=0\).
