The Dirichlet convergence threshold for labeled sum systems admits an all-orders generalized-power expansion around \(\log_2(m-1)\), indexed by additive combinations of \(\log_2(n/2)\) for integers \(n\ge3\). Its first resonant exponent \(\log_2 3\) combines a genuine sixth-integer term with interactions between the third and fourth terms, producing an exact coefficient \(-\log3/(\log2)^2\).
