For fixed edge density \(p>0\), the second-order spherical width of an Erdős–Rényi symmetric edge polytope has an almost-sure graph-quenched limit: a standard Gumbel shifted by the logarithm of an explicit geometrically weighted reciprocal-Poisson-arrival sum. Its sharp upper tail has coefficient \(p\), while the exact directional probability of matching the complete-graph width is simply the graph's edge density.
