For a natural approval election with \(k+1\) voters and \(kr+1\) candidates, every size-\(k\) committee is Pareto efficient, yet the exact fraction of ordinary-core committees satisfying fractional core+ vanishes as \(r\to\infty\) for any fixed \(k\ge3\). The complete ordinary-core and core+ classifications give the respective counts \(\binom{kr}{k-1}+r^k+k(k-1)\binom r2 r^{k-2}\) and \(\binom{kr}{k-1}\).
