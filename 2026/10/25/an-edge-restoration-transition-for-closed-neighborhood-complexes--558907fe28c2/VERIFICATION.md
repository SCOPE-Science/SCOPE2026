# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For a bipartite G=A union B, split N[G] into hemispheres generated by closed neighborhoods centered on A and B. Their intersection is exactly the 1-dimensional graph G: any mixed edge belongs to both precisely when an original edge is present, and no same-side 1-simplex is common. In each hemisphere, cones over each closed-neighborhood apex can be collapsed relative to its nonempty opposite-side base, leaving the opposite-side simplex if that side has a universal vertex. Thus if each side has such an opposite universal vertex, both hemispheres are contractible while their intersection is G, giving the homotopy pushout Sigma G and integral H2=H1(G). For crown j=0 the two sides collapse to boundaries of (n-1)-simplices rather than full simplices, yielding the already-known two S^{n-2} together with n^2-3n+1 S² (when n=4 these spheres lie in degree 2). Restoring the first diagonal gives both universal vertices, and each further insertion adds one graph cycle. Naturality of the Mayer–Vietoris sequences gives primitive split-injective H2 maps for j>=1 and rank-one cokernels; first-stage H2 image rank b=n^2-3n+1, kernel rank two only at n=4 and cokernel rank one, whereas n>=5 no H2 kernel and two higher classes disappear. I separately reconstructed finite complexes and mod-2 Betti numbers for every stage n=4,5,6 (18 cases). The integral homotopy and maps rest on explicit collapse and MV proof, not mod-2 data.

## originality

PASS

The bare j=0 crown endpoint (two high spheres and lower S² wedge) is already known in open-neighborhood two-row rook/clique-prism results and is NOT claimed new. Matsushita (2025) defines the closed-neighborhood functor and proves general independence-complex identities and fundamental-group connections. Its first two chapters and main theorem were read in full PDF; Theorem 1.2 concerns the complement graph and a suspended independence complex, not this universal-pair suspension of G or natural integral maps along matching restoration. Nearby published SCOPE open-neighborhood/high-girth results have different functors or conditions (dense restored crowns contain K2,3). The new statement is a complete graph-family suspension/persistence transition under a natural edge-restoration filtration.
