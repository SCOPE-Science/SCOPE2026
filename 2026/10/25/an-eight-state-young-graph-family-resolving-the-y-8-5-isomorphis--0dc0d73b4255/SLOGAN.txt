For every \(n\ge3\), the full Young graph \(Y(n^2-1,n^2-n-1)\) is isomorphic to \(Y(8,5)\), preserving initial, even-pivot and odd-pivot structure; this proves Kendrick's published Conjecture 3. The known reverse-multiple counting series for \(Y(8,5)\) consequently holds uniformly over this infinite quadratic family.
