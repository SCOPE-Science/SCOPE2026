# Verification

Run `python3 verify.py` with Python 3 (standard library only). The checker validates the polygon, its primitive edge list and normalized area 7; checks the degree-one equations vanish under the displayed elimination; obtains the degree-two vector polynomials exactly as in the proof; and verifies that every monomial in degrees 3 through 20 belongs to the monomial ideal generated by the square of the first parameter and its product with the second.

The full all-degree statement **does not** follow from checking degrees through 20. It follows from the symbolic quotient identity in `RESULT.md`, valid for every degree at least 2. Miniversality of the complete local base rests on Altmann's isolated toric Gorenstein versality theorem; the checker does not independently prove that theorem. The calculation does not describe the deformation ring of a global compactification. Independent validation has not been performed.
