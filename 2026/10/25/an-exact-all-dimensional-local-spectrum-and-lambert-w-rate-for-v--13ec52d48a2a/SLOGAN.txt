For exact-line-search away-step cyclic descent on an isotropic simplex, the full local sweep has an explicit all-dimensional characteristic polynomial with a simple zero eigenvalue and \(n-2\) simple nonzero eigenvalues. The sharp local contraction factor approaches \(1/|W_1(-e^{-1})|=0.1238303227\ldots\) as the dimension grows, and the nonzero spectrum changes from real at dimension three to a conjugate pair at dimension four.
