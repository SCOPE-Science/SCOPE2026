In a homogeneous independent-pollinator extinction model, every compatible ecological network reproducing all proper-subset plant coextinction probabilities belongs to one explicit consecutive-integer interval of pollinator censuses, with incidence multiplicities given by a parity-alternating Möbius formula. Knowing the census selects a unique network, but observations of order at most \(m-2\) fail to identify a network in general even when the census and extinction probability are known.
