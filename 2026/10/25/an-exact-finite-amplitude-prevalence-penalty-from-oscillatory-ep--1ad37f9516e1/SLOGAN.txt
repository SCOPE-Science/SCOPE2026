For the Frieswijk–Zino–Ye–Rizzo–Cao behavioral epidemic flow, every nonconstant compact interior stationary state has negative protection–infection covariance and an exact finite-amplitude variance shell. In the established oscillatory regime, the mean infection prevalence strictly exceeds the interior endemic-equilibrium prevalence by \((D-\sqrt{D^2-4\operatorname{Var}(x)})/\zeta\), even though the instantaneous prevalence oscillates around that equilibrium.
