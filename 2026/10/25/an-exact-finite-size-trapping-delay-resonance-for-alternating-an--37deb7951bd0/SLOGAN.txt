On a clockwise directed cycle with two antipodal traps that alternate activation at rate \(g\), the unconditional mean absorption time is exactly \(M_k(g)=(k-1)/2+k(1+2g)^{k-1}/[1+(1+2g)^k]\). For every \(k\ge3\), switching creates a unique **maximum** trapping delay at \(g=((k-1)^{1/k}-1)/2\); asymptotically the worst mean is \(3/2\) times the static-trap mean.
