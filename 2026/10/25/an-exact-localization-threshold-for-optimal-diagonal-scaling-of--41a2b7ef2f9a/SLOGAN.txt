For \(I+tuu^{\mathsf T}\) with one exceptional spike coordinate and \(n-1\) equal coordinates, arbitrary positive diagonal scaling improves spectral conditioning exactly when the exceptional coordinate carries more than half of \(\|u\|_2^2\); the globally optimal scaling and condition number admit closed formulas. In an explicit five-dimensional example, Jacobi diagonal equilibration worsens conditioning even though the true optimal diagonal scaling improves it.
