For \(k\) uniformly sampled Gödel truth levels on an \(n\)-world complete frame, the probability that \(\Box(p\to r)\) equals \(\Box p\to\Box r\) has an exact finite sum and converges to \(1/(1+e^\lambda)\) when \(n/k\to\lambda\). The result identifies a sharp changing-alphabet transition between almost-sure strictness and a limiting half-probability of equality, together with noncommuting large-world and fine-truth-scale limits.
