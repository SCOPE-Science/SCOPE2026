# Verification scope

The exact order criterion was derived directly from the Gödel residuum and the two minima. No stochastic independence assumption is used for that deterministic step. Probabilities then use explicitly independent atom values and independence across worlds. The geometric limit uses convergence of tails and a geometric summation; endpoint cases use elementary mass and union bounds. Fixed-chain asymptotics compare the two genuine exponential mechanisms rather than inferring an infinite claim from finite examples.

Run `python3 checker.py` using Python's standard library. The checker exhausts 20 uniform configurations and 9 nonuniform configurations, representing 486,250 finite valuation pairs, and tests several crossover approximations. The output terminates with `ALL_CHECKS_OK`. This finite computation is corroboration, not a proof of the general theorem.
