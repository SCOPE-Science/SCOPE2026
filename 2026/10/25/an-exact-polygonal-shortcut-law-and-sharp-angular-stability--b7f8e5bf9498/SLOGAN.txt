For two uniformly sampled points on the perimeter of any convex polygon, the short-radius excess of Euclidean proximity over boundary-arc proximity is exactly quadratic, with coefficient \(\sum_i(eta_i/\sineta_i-1)\). This intrinsic-to-extrinsic shortcut coefficient obeys a sharp, equiangular-equality angle-variance bound with optimal universal constant \(1/6\).
