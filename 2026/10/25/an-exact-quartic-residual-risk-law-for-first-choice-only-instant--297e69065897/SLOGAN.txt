For three-candidate instant runoff under the flat Dirichlet prior on all fifteen partial rankings, even infinitely many first-choice-only observations leave the plurality runner-up with exact posterior winning probability \(\tfrac12(1-\delta)^3(1+\delta)\) when \(0<\delta=(a-b)/c<1\). The irreducible uncertainty vanishes cubically near the first-choice majority boundary, rather than linearly as in the otherwise analogous six-complete-ranking prior model.
