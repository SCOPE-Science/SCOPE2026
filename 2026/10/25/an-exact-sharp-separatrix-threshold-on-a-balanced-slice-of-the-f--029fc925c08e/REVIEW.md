# Scientific review — An exact sharp separatrix threshold on a balanced slice of the four-oscillator Sakaguchi ring

## Correctness — PASS
The three-coordinate reduction follows from the four-site sine equations. Two independent first integrals are checked by differentiation, and the envelopes have exact unique maxima. The trichotomy follows from invariant-branch monotonicity, convergence to an explicit stationary saddle at equality, and regular compact level-set gluing above threshold. The explicit quadrature counts all four branches. `checks.py` gives numerical checks of the reduction and examples without substituting finite tests for proof.

## Originality — PASS under accessible evidence
The strongest primary source, Burylko–Mielke–Wolfrum–Yanchuk (2018), treats a **gauge-equivalent** four-site skew-neighbor system and already establishes the qualitative periodic/dissipative coexistence and heteroclinic surface. Those conclusions are not new. The detailed source's section 5.2 lacks the explicit nonzero-lag exponential integrals and the sharp \(\rho_c\) separating outcomes on the initial slice. The 2026 motivating paper addresses basin complexity for the symmetric equation and the Mihara–Medrano-T paper supplies local spectral theory, not this initial-value boundary. The residual priority risk is an alternative exact separatrix calculation in other coordinates.

## Value — PASS
A natural one-parameter balanced family cuts across the known but qualitatively described separatrix. The analytic boundary \(\rho_c(\alpha)\), limiting saddle, and period quadrature quantify this cut for every \(0<\alpha<\pi/2\), supplying a benchmark for basin geometry rather than rediscovering qualitative neutral orbits.
