For the symmetric four-oscillator Sakaguchi ring, a balanced initial-phase family crosses from synchronization to nonconstant relative-periodic motion at the exact amplitude \(\rho_c=(\cos\alpha\,e^{\alpha\tan\alpha})^{-1/2}\), with the equality trajectory asymptotic to an explicitly located saddle. The known qualitative periodic/dissipative coexistence is sharpened to an analytic initial-value separatrix and exact period quadrature.
