For the unit in-in zonotope of \(K_{p,q}\), \(2\le p\le q\), the Euclidean inradius is exactly \(\min\{\sqrt{p+q},p+1\}\). At \(p+q=(p+1)^2\), the tangent facets switch from a single opposite whole-graph pair to opposite minimum-degree singleton pairs, and the affine dimension of all optimal ball centers passes through three explicitly determined regimes.
