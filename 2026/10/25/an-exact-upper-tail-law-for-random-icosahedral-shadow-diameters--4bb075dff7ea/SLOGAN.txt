The diameter of a uniformly oriented planar shadow of the regular icosahedron has an exact elementary upper-tail distribution through the first triple-overlap of its six vertex-equator bands. The sharp threshold is \(t_*=\sqrt{(1-2/\sqrt5)/3}\), and the same formula gives a four-term near-maximum expansion whose leading terms are \(6t-15\sqrt5\,t^2/\pi\).
