The Petersen graphic-matroid forest complex has an explicit characteristic-two top-homology basis consisting of two full 120-element \(S_5\)-orbits. Consequently \(\widetilde H_8(\mathcal F(P);\mathbb F_2)\cong\mathbb F_2[S_5]^{\oplus2}\), a genuinely modular free-module refinement of the known rational character.
