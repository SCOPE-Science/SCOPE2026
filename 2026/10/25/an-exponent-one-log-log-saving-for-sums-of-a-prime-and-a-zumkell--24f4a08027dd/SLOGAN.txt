The exceptional set of integers that are not a prime plus a Zumkeller number satisfies \(\alpha(X)\ll X/\log\log X\), improving the previously published exponent approximately \(0.238\) to \(1\). Consecutive-prime Zumkeller blocks have \(\varphi(z)/z\to1/2\), giving a sharp per-seed failure-density factor \(1/2\) in this sieve without proving a global lower bound.
