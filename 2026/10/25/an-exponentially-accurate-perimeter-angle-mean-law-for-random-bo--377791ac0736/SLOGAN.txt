For independent arclength-uniform boundary samples of any fixed convex polygon, the expected missing hull area is \(C_K/((N+1)(N+2))\) up to exponentially small error, where \(C_K=(P^2/2)\sum_i\sin\alpha_i\). Its \(N^2\)-scaled area loss converges, with all moments, to the non-Gaussian sum \((P^2/2)\sum_i\sin\alpha_i E_iF_i\) of independent products of unit exponentials: perimeter and vertex angles, not individual edge lengths, determine the complete limit law.
