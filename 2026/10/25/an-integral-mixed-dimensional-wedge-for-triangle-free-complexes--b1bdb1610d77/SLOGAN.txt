When two complete graphs overlap in exactly one edge, their triangle-free vertex-subset complex has integral homotopy type \(S^1\vee\bigvee^{2(a-1)(b-1)}S^2\vee\bigvee^{\binom{a-1}{2}\binom{b-1}{2}}S^3\) for all \(a,b\ge2\). The persistent circle and simultaneous higher-dimensional sphere wedges expose topology beyond diamond-free shellability criteria.
