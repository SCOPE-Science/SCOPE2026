# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The pair sequence for the star-union subcomplex is an actual integral exact sequence. Since the stars and their one-point pairwise intersections form a cover whose nerve is the complete graph, the degree-one cycle lattice is naturally \(\bigwedge^2V_n\). Exceptional triangle attachments give the contraction \(\bigwedge^3\mathbb Z^n\to\bigwedge^2V_n\). The kernel is exactly \(\bigwedge^3V_n\) over the integers. The oriented octahedron boundary projects to \(\partial_4\), and the augmented simplex chain complex is split exact, giving all generators and relations, without relying on finite-field ranks. An elementary link argument gives the six-vertex lower bound. Direct signed-complex checks for five small sizes agree.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
The nearest same-object result, Barmak (2010), Theorem 4.11, determines the homotopy wedge and the combinatorial star/triangle decomposition, not the natural integral symmetric-group module, its character or the all-orbit primitive generator-relation presentation. Braun (2009) treats the stable Kneser graph, which is a different simplicial complex. Feng–Wang (2024) and Feng (2026) focus on three-subset and larger-subset families. Relevant published mathematical records on the \(KG(6,2)\) *neighborhood* complex and on stable Kneser independence-complex dihedral actions involve different functors or different vertex families; their conclusions do not imply the stated integral \(S_n\)-isomorphism. An unindexed equivalent representation-theoretic derivation of Barmak's construction remains a risk. Source full texts were inspected where available.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The previously known count of two-sphere summands does not identify how all coordinate permutations act or whether geometrically natural cycles generate homology integrally. The result supplies a uniform minimal geometric basis and a complete integral relations presentation with a closed character formula, which can be used directly in equivariant calculations for this canonical family. This is not a claim of a new homotopy type.

## Limits
No complete classification of every possible six-vertex carrier is asserted. The result is at the level of integral homology representations, not equivariant homotopy type. Published search coverage is incomplete and independent audit is outstanding.
