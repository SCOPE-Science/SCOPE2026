For every \(n\ge4\), the integral second homology of the independence complex of the two-subset Kneser graph is the natural \(S_n\)-lattice \(\bigwedge^3\ker(\mathbb Z^n\to\mathbb Z)\), not merely a free group of known rank. Six-vertex octahedral spheres from four ground labels form a primitive integral generating orbit, with every relation generated by alternating five-label relations; six vertices are the sharp support minimum.
