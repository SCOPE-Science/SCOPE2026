In a two-player asymmetric lottery contest, exact alternating best responses have a positive four-move cycle for every cost ratio \(4/25<c<3-2\sqrt2\), even though the previously reported reset cycles occurred at or below \(4/25\). The Nash equilibrium loses local stability at the sharp ratio \(3-2\sqrt2\), and an exact-rational certificate gives a genuinely attracting interior cycle at \(c=1/6\).
