For Haar-random central plane sections of the regular octahedron, section area has support \([3\sqrt3/4,2]\), a logarithmically divergent density at \(\sqrt2\) with coefficient \(\sqrt6/\pi\), a linear lower-endpoint probability, and a quadratic upper-endpoint probability. The singularity comes from twelve square-section saddle orientations, whereas the endpoints reflect regular-hexagonal and axial square sections.
