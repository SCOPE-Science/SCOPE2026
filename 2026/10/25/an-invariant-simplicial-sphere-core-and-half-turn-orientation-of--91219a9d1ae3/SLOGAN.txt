For every \(n\ge2\), the perfect-matching complex of the \(1\times2\times n\) honeycomb simplicially collapses onto an explicit **half-turn-invariant** boundary \((n-1)\)-sphere. The half-turn has integral orientation degree \((-1)^{1+\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor}\), which is periodic modulo four and determines the quotient's reduced rational homology.
