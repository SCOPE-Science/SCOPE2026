In a lossy two-dot Kitaev chain at equal pairing and hopping, far detuning one dot squeezes the exact zero-real-energy exceptional-point corridor of the other to half-width \(4\gamma t^2/R^2-8\gamma t^2(2\gamma^2+t^2)/R^4+O(R^{-6})\). The two corridor edges are genuine second-order exceptional points, quantifying the inverse-square loss of Majorana spectral pinning under extreme remote-dot detuning.
