The symbolic determinant identity and the exact rescaling polynomial identity are checked by the included `verify.py` script. The proof uses the real-analytic implicit function theorem, compact root separation, and a nonzero Schur off-diagonal term; numerical tests alone do not establish the infinite-range statement.
