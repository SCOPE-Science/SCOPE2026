# Scientific review: octahedral recursive self-volume

## Correctness — PASS
The starting convention is the recursive central-section self-volume of Wolansky (2026). Every facet of the coordinate octahedron has Euclidean area \(\sqrt3/2\), and its orthogonal central section is an affine-regular hexagon of area \(3\sqrt3/4\) and self-area \(3\). The eight facet contributions give \(\Omega_3=16/3\). An independent boundary-product decomposition establishes multiplicativity, yielding the exact odd-dimensional ratio \(8/9\). The included exact-arithmetic checker verifies the key finite geometry. The unbounded family follows from the proof, not from a finite test.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
The principal source, Wolansky, arXiv:2604.01950 (2026), explicitly proposes the contradicted odd-dimensional minimum in Section 4.4, while giving the recursive definition and product theorem. Its full text, including the examples, polygon section and conjecture, was examined. It does not assert the octahedral value. Ghandehari–Martini (2019) concerns planar self-circumference. Searches for octahedral self-volume, the numerical value \(16/3\), equivalent central-section formulations and cross-polytope counterexamples, together with comparisons against a published mathematical findings index, uncovered no statement implying the new counterexample. Possible unindexed 3D calculations remain a risk; absence of a search hit is not a proof of global novelty.

## Value — PASS
This is not merely the recomputation of a classical volume. The exact octahedral section calculation contradicts a specific recently posed sharp extremal claim in dimension three and, by structural multiplicativity, in every odd dimension greater than one. It redirects the candidate extremizers for a natural affine-invariant geometry problem. The true minimum remains unknown.

## Scope and limitations
No universal lower bound, even-dimensional extremal claim, or uniqueness assertion is made. The 2019 paper was compared through its publicly available abstract; full text was not used for a whole-document negative assertion. The main 2026 paper was inspected directly as a full PDF, including Definition 4.1, Theorem 4.2, and the conjecture. No independent audit or human certification has occurred.
