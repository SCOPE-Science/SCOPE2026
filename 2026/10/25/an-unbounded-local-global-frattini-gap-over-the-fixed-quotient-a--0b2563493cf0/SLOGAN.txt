For the diagonal action of \(A_5\) on \(t\) copies of its six-point Sylow-\(5\) permutation module over \(\mathbb F_2\), the global Frattini subgroup has dimension \(5t\) but the local Frattini subgroup has dimension only \(t\). Their quotient therefore has order \(2^{4t}\), giving an unbounded gap with a fixed simple quotient and an elementary abelian kernel.
