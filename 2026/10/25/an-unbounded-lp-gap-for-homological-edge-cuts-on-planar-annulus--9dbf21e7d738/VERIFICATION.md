The included verification.py constructs the explicit annulus triangulation and checks the cocycle equations, ring pairings, and support count for integer parameters from two through ten. These finite checks audit the cochain formula only; the all-parameter cut lower bound and unbounded ratio follow from the analytic arguments in RESULT.md. The scaled cochain has sum-of-absolute-values norm below two, and no claim is made that this feasible cochain is LP-optimal. Independent audit and independent validation have not been performed.
