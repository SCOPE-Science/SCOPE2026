The \(24\)-vertex four-letter permutohedron has exactly \(169\) perfect matchings, with anti-forcing polynomial \(4z^4+16z^5+44z^6+48z^7+39z^8+16z^9+2z^{10}\). Its matchings form \(17\) orbits under symbol relabeling and position reversal; even the complementary two-factor cycle partition does not determine the anti-forcing number.
