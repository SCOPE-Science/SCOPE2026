An odd cycle's fractional stable-set relaxation exceeds its integral stable-set polytope by a single simplex of exactly \(1/(2n!)\) volume, with exact relative volume \(1/(n|E_{n-1}|)\). Conditional on landing in that vanishingly rare cap, the squared Euclidean distance from the all-half vertex converges to an antiperiodic Brownian quadratic functional whose Laplace transform is \(\operatorname{sech}(\sqrt{s/2})\).
