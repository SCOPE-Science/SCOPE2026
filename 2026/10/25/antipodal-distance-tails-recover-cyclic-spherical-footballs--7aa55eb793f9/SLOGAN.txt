On the round cyclic spherical football \(S^2/C_q\), the probability that two uniform points are within \(\varepsilon\) of maximal intrinsic separation has a fourth-order tail whose explicit coefficient \(\frac1{32}[\csc^2(\pi/q)-(q/\pi)\cot(\pi/q)]\) strictly distinguishes all cone orders \(q\ge2\). The ordinary sphere has a quadratic tail instead; with the diameter, these two-point distance statistics recover the quotient's radius and cyclic order.
