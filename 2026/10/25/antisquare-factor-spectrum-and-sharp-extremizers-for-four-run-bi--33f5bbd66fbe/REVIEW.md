# Scientific review: four-run antisquare factors

## Correctness

PASS. The known run-parity obstruction eliminates antisquares with four or five runs in the host. The surviving two- and three-run forms split into four disjoint factor types, indexed without multiplicity; their cardinalities produce the exact formula. Two charge inequalities prove the \(\(n-1\)\) ceiling, and solving the equality conditions yields two explicit exhaustive families. The simplex/independent-exponential argument gives the mean coefficient \(\(7/16\)\). An independent exhaustive enumerator checked 4096 parameter quadruples, and a separate finite search checked every composition at lengths 4–80. These are finite checks and do not prove the infinite claims.

## Originality

PASS under accessible-public-literature comparison, with residual unindexed-literature risk. Baranwal et al., arXiv:2209.09223, Lemma 14 and Section 6 already establish the shape restriction for antisquares; that is acknowledged and not presented as novel. Their stated focus is minimal antisquares, repetition thresholds, and avoidance rather than the exact count and sharp extremizers for all four-run hosts. Ng et al., arXiv:1904.09157, studies simultaneous pattern avoidance and finite limits under small allowed counts. The four-parameter enumeration, equality classification, and mean are not consequences given in the inspected statements. This literature comparison does not establish global uniqueness.

## Mathematical value

PASS. Four runs are the smallest alternating-run family with two interior centers admitting opposite types of three-run antisquare; the two compete with repeated boundary types. An exact host-dependent factor spectrum, all fixed-length maximizers, and the mean density answer three canonical structural and extremal questions for this natural first competing-center case. The result is not a length-specific census or an arbitrary parameter slice: it holds uniformly for every positive run-length quadruple.

## Scope and limitations

The result concerns contiguous factors of exactly four-run binary words only. The \(O(1)\) expectation error is not given as an exact finite-\(n\) formula. The existing run-shape lemma is acknowledged prior work. The paper search remains nonexhaustive and independent replication has not occurred.
