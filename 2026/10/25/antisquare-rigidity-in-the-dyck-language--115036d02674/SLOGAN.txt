Among all Dyck words of any fixed positive semilength, exactly one avoids every contiguous binary antisquare of order at least two: `01` at semilength one and \(\mathtt{0}^{n-1}\mathtt{10}\mathtt{1}^{n-1}\) at each semilength \(n\ge2\). Thus a language with Catalan-many words has only one word per size after antisquare avoidance.
