For uniformly randomized implicit stages, antithetic pairing \(V=1-U\) is the **only** coupling of two uniform marginals that makes every negative-real scalar mode samplewise contractive for all step sizes; it also contracts every dissipative nonnormal matrix. Its exact squared-contraction gap decays as \((8\log s-8+o(1))/s\), showing the price of this unconditional stability in the stiff limit.
