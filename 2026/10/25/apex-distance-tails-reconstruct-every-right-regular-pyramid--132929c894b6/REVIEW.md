## Correctness
PASS. The central distance identity follows by matching a globally 1-Lipschitz distance candidate across faces, and by constructing for every base point an actual straight unfolded path through a valid edge segment. The strict unique maximum uses raywise convexity and \(L>a\). The two upper-tail coefficients follow from a uniform radial-root expansion and exact angular integrals; inversion and the squared moment are algebraic consequences. Independent high-order numerical quadrature and path-crossing checks corroborate the specific identities. Boundary conditions \(n\ge3\), \(a>0\), \(h>0\), and uniform *total surface area* are essential.
