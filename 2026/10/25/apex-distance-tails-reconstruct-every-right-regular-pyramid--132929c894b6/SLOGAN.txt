For a uniformly sampled point on a right regular pyramid's complete surface, the geodesic distance from its apex has an upper tail \(\varepsilon^2/(aC)+\tan^2(\pi/n)\varepsilon^3/(3aC^2)+O(\varepsilon^4)\), where \(a\) is the base apothem and \(C=a+\sqrt{a^2+h^2}\) is the largest possible distance. The endpoint and these two coefficients uniquely recover the polygon side count, base size, and height; the exact second moment is \(L(L+a)/2+a^2\sec^2(\pi/n)/6\).
