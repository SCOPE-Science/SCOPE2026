For every \(B\ge18\), the strictly increasing sequence \((1,\ldots,B,Q,2Q,3Q,\ldots)\), where \(Q=2+\sum_{j=1}^{B}j^2\), satisfies the entire printed finite certificate of the generalized anti-Waring Theorem 6 with \((k,n,r,b,\widehat N,x)=(2,1,1,B,129,4)\), yet misses every integer \(hQ-1\). Thus a published completeness implication for arbitrary sequences fails even after arbitrarily long exact square-sum verification; the ordinary positive-integer version is not affected.
