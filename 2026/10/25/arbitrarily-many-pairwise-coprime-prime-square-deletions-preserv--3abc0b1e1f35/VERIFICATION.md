The infinite theorem is proved by a Dirichlet--CRT prime induction, Hensel lifts at two auxiliary split prime squares per pair, and Jacobi's two-square formula. These are mathematical arguments, not finite certificates. The standalone `verify.py` checks all integer two-square sums up to the maximal pair sum for the explicit prime set \(\{5,37,89\}\) using exact integer arithmetic. It also checks that each deleted-root pair sum has an alternative unordered square decomposition. A successful finite test does not establish the infinite theorem or independent validation.
