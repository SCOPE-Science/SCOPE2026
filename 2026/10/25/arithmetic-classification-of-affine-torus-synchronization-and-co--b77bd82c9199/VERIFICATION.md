# Verification and limits

The written proof checks the nonstandard transition from image-coset equality to synchronized local rules using a path-lifting function for both torus bundles, with the second lifted path reversed to meet its endpoint. It checks the difference-map cohomology rank and the Smith-normal-form connected-component calculation, including the zero-rank case. The equality of image subtori is decided using rational column spaces; affine translation membership is decided by the entire saturated integer annihilator.

An independent arithmetic calculation gave Smith factors \(1\) for \([-2\ \ 3]\), \(2\) for \([-2\ \ 4]\), and \(1,1\) for the two orthogonal coordinate inclusions. These checks illustrate but do not prove the all-matrix statement; the proof itself uses Smith normal form and LS cup length.

Prior general results imply the difference-map category reduction and the bundle inequalities; the new contribution is the exact combined family classification and fibre profile. This review is not an independent audit.
