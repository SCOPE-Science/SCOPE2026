Two positive Lindblad sinks at vertices \(0\) and \(a\) of a homogeneous magnetic \(N\)-cycle leave an exactly classified dark subspace and vertex-resolved survival probability, determined solely by \(\gcd(N,a)\), the cycle parity and whether the flux is \(0\), \(\pi\), or generic. Complete absorption for every input and every flux is guaranteed exactly when \(\gcd(N,a)=1\), while half-flux can entirely suppress dark transport for an even reduced cycle.
