For an even path with only its central edge reweighted, single-vertex Laplacian observability follows an exact finite trigonometric resonance law that is periodic in the half-length modulo \(2j-1\) for a sensor at vertex \(j\). At the second vertex, half-lengths divisible by three are always unobservable, those congruent to one lose observability exactly at central conductance \(1/2\), and those congruent to two remain observable for every positive central conductance.
