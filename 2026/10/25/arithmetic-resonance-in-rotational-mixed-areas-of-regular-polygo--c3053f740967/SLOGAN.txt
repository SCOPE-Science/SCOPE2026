For two regular polygons with \(m,n\ge3\) sides, their Minkowski-sum area under relative rotation is exactly \(A_0+B\cos d(\phi)\), where \(d\) is distance to an arithmetic phase lattice of period \(2\pi/\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)\). Its minima occur precisely when \(\gcd(m,n)\) outward side normals coincide, producing a sharp change from \(m+n\) to \(m+n-\gcd(m,n)\) sides; the full angular area distribution also follows explicitly.
