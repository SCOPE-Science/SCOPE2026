For every fixed finite field, the number of isomorphism classes of all \(n\)-dimensional evolution algebras is asymptotic to \(q^{n^2}/(n!(q-1)^n)\), including a positive-density singular population. Each fixed square-space corank \(r\) has the explicit asymptotic proportion \(q^{-r^2}P_\infty(q)/P_r(q)^2\), with an exponentially small error uniform across all coranks in the normalized absolute count.
