# Scientific review: Balanced quadrilaterals span every signed complete-bipartite cycle space

## Correctness — PASS

After gauge normalization, signed incidence cycles correspond bijectively to interior arrays whose negative-position entries sum to zero. Positive entries are killed by balanced corner squares. Negative entries are linked by balanced same-row/column squares, or by a balanced four-negative/positive-corner square after positive elimination; any two negative entries therefore differ by a generated vector. This proves exact kernel equality and the dimension dichotomy for every m,n>=2. The regular signed-cover two-path relations realize each balanced square as a boundary. Exact finite checks: 606 signing/rank cases and 94 independently assembled cover-path cases; finite checks corroborate rather than replace the proof.

The all-signings balanced-square spanning theorem and signed first GLMY vanishing were compared against precise aliases and implications, accessible own ledgers, published-finding corpus, Chaplin and Kempton–Münch–Yau primary full texts; no covering claim was found. The 2026 signed GLMY source was compared via accessible abstract only, with its uninspected complete body an explicit residual risk.

Closest sources: Bai–Li–Yau, arXiv:2609.25557 (only abstract accessible, full-text comparison unresolved); Chaplin, DOI 10.1007/s41468-022-00108-3 (full text); Kempton–Münch–Yau, arXiv:1710.01264 (full text). An integral signed coinvariant formula restricted to girth five or greater does not cover the present four-cycle family.

## Value — PASS

This is an all-parameter structural spanning theorem for a natural signed graph family, with an exact dimension loss under arbitrary frustration. It establishes that real degree-one signed GLMY homology is blind to all choices of edge signing on dense triangle-free bicliques, even though the unbalanced signed double cover may retain ordinary cycles. The generative connectivity lemma is not a single numerical invariant or a merely negative normalization check.

## Limitations

Only real deck-anti-invariant degree one for complete bipartite signed graphs is addressed. The originality assessment is provisional given the inaccessible 2026 full text and the possibility of broader gain-graph spanning results.
