A Poisson-reset Ising pair violates a Bell inequality for every coupling ratio \(J/\Omega\ge2\) at the explicit intermediate reset rate \(r/\Omega=\Omega/(2J)\), even though either extreme of resetting is Bell-local at fixed finite coupling. In the strong-coupling window \((J/\Omega)(r/\Omega)=s\), its maximal CHSH value converges to \(2\sqrt{1+[2s/(1+4s^2)]^2}\), uniquely maximized at \(\sqrt5\) when \(s=1/2\).
