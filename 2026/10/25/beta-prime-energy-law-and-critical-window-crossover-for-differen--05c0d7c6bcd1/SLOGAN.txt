At the compactness boundary of the differential Hilbert operator between weighted Bergman spaces, the squared matrix-entry energy of large polynomial compressions splits into an exponentially tilted logarithmic degree scale and an independent beta-prime column-to-row ratio. This yields the sharp signed critical-window law \(\|T_{N,\lambda}\|_{\mathcal S_2}^2=\Gamma(\alpha+4)\Gamma(-\alpha)\,\Phi(\lambda)\log N+O(1)\) for every fixed \(-1<\alpha<0\).
