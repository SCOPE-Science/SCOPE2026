For every positive i.i.d. letter distribution on a fixed ordered alphabet, the mean number of distinct Lyndon scattered subsequences has an exact multivariate-Witt generating function and grows as \(r^{-n}/((1-r)n)\), where \(\sum_a p_a r/(1-(1-p_a)r)=1\). The exponential rate is strictly Schur-concave in the source probabilities and is uniquely maximized by the uniform source at \(2-1/q\); the previously published exact uniform finite-length mean is not claimed as new.
