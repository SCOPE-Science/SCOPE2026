For every binary de Bruijn order \(k\ge2\), adding exactly two cyclic windows while retaining all \(k\)-mers allows only four surplus multisets: three built from constant words and one from the alternating pair. Their exact rotation-class count is \(2^{2^{k-1}-k}\bigl(14/3+(-1)^k/(3\cdot2^{k-2})\bigr)\), with initial totals \(5,9,76,9472\).
