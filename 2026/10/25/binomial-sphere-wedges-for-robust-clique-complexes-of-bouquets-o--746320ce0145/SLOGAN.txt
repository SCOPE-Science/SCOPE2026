For every bouquet of \(m\) four-cycles meeting at one central vertex and every \(k\ge4\), its robust clique complex is \(\bigvee^{\binom m{k-1}}S^{2k-3}\), with zero spheres interpreted as contractibility. The explicit ball-and-boundary decomposition gives an integral homotopy classification outside the usual edge- and corner-glued square-sequence class.
