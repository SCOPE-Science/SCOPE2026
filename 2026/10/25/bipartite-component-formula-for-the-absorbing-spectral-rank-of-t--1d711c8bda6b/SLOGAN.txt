For arbitrary positive absorbing losses on any nonempty subset of the triangular quantum graph \(J(n,2)\), the eventual bright-space rank is exactly \(n+|F|+1-b(F)-b(\overline F)-\varepsilon(F)\), where the terms count bipartite components of the trap graph and its complement plus a balanced-coloring correction. Four traps arranged as a quadrilateral monitor precisely one fewer coherent mode than the same four traps arranged as a triangle with a pendant edge, even though both configurations have four trap sites.
