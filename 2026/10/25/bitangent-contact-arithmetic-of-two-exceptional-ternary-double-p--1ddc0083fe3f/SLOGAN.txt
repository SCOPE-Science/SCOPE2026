For the two exceptional degree-two del Pezzo double planes over \(\mathbb F_3\), the first branch quartic has one hyperflex and 27 ordinary bitangents, with 55 contact points in Frobenius cycles \(1^1 2^9 6^6\); the second has 28 hyperflexes, with contact-point cycles \(1^4 4^6\). Explicit equations for every bitangent explain the different least contact-splitting fields \(\mathbb F_{3^6}\) and \(\mathbb F_{3^4}\).
