For every finite length-two commutative chain ring, square-zero matrix lifts induce **uniformly distributed arbitrary endomorphisms** on the reduced homology \(\ker A/\operatorname{im}A\), with exactly \(q^{2r(n-r)}\) lifts for each secondary map. Its rank gives the torsion part of the image, yielding an explicit complete joint rank/Smith-shape count independent of whether the ring has mixed or equal characteristic.
