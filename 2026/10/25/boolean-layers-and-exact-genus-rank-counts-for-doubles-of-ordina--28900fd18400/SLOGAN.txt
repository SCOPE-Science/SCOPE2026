The complete fiber of numerical semigroups \(S\) with \(S/2=\{0\}\cup[m,\infty)\) splits into Boolean layers of dimension \(m-1\), indexed by the least odd member. Its bivariate genus–relative-rank series is exactly \(z^{g_0}w(z+w)^{m-1}/(1-z)\), so the number at genus \(g\) stabilizes sharply at \(2^{m-1}\).
