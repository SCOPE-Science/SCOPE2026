A commutator of two finite-exponential harmonic Toeplitz operators on the Gaussian Fock space extends boundedly exactly when every noncancelled mixed frequency lies on the anti-diagonal \(a+b=0\). Each such bounded commutator has essential norm equal to its operator norm, and two surviving distinct frequencies have the exact norm \(\sum_a e^{|a|^2/2}|q_{a,-a}|\).
