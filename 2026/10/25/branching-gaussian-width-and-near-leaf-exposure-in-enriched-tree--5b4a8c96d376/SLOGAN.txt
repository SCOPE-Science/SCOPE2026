Enriched chain polytopes of complete rooted \(b\)-ary trees have a normalized Gaussian mean-width constant \(c_b\) that exceeds the leaf-cube value \(\sqrt{2/\pi}\), decreases strictly with branching, and is computable with a superexponential remainder bound. A Gaussian-exposed supporting antichain is confined to the last \(O(\log\log n)\) tree levels with probability tending to one, despite the persistent mean-width gain from internal nodes.
