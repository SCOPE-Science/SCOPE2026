For a volume-uniform point of the two-row generalized Pitman–Stanley polytope, reversing its second-row coordinates and scaling them by \(\sqrt m\) gives a profile converging to \(\sqrt2\) times Brownian excursion. The squared Euclidean distance to the second-row-zero face converges to \(2\int_0^1e(t)^2\,dt\), with Laplace transform \( (2\sqrt{s}/\sinh(2\sqrt{s}))^{3/2}\), mean \(1\), and variance \(4/15\).
