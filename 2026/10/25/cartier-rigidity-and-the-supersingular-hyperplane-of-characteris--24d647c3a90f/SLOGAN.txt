Every smooth monic characteristic-two Picard quartic \(y^3=x^4+d x^3+a x^2+b x+c\) has Jacobian \(a\)-number exactly \(1\); its supersingular locus is precisely the separable hyperplane \(d=0\), and all other members have \(2\)-rank \(2\). Over \(\mathbb F_q\), the two loci contain exactly \(q^2(q-1)\) and \(q^2(q-1)^2\) smooth coordinate-marked quartics, respectively.
