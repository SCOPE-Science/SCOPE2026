Surjective bounded morphisms from a rectangular reflexive Kripke grid of fixed width \(a\) onto the three-world chain have a positive binomial expansion in the other side length, whose top coefficient is the Catalan number \(\frac1{a+1}\binom{2a}{a}\). Consequently the bounded-morphism proportion among surjective monotone maps tends to one at fixed width; for width two the exact count is \(\frac{(b-1)(b^3+5b^2+10b-12)}{12}\), with exactly \(b(b^2-1)/3\) surjective monotone maps failing the back condition.
