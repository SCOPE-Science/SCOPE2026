In the monoid of nondecreasing contractions of a finite chain, every idempotent's centralizer has an explicit formula in its two end gaps and interval length; balanced intervals minimize and end-anchored intervals maximize that size at fixed length. The normalized binary logarithm of the centralizer of a uniformly random idempotent converges to a nondegenerate law with CDF \((3t-2)^2\) on \([2/3,1]\) and mean \(8/9\).
