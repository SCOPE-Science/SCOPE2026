# Review

## Correctness

The geometric automorphism charts are obtained from the images of the two generators and the relations \(X^2=0\), \(Y^m=0\). The identity tangent space is computed directly from first-order substitutions, yielding \(2xu=0\) and \(my^{m-1}v=0\). The boundary case \(m=2\) in odd characteristic is handled separately.

## Originality

The closest inspected primary sources on zero-dimensional monomial algebras explicitly assume characteristic zero. Searches for the exact family and the dimension/tangent-excess table returned adjacent topics but no matching result. This supports, but does not prove, originality; the search is not exhaustive.

## Value

The result gives a complete characteristic-dependent smoothness criterion and exact tangent excess for the natural family \(k[x,y]/(x^2,y^m)\). The characteristic-two parity split is a concrete structural invariant, not just an example of nonsmoothness.

## Closest literature and limitations

Díaz–Liendo–Manzano-Flores–Regeta, arXiv:2408.02197, treats ordinary automorphism groups of zero-dimensional monomial algebras in characteristic zero. Díaz–Lucchini Arteche, arXiv:2409.15081, likewise assumes characteristic zero. The present result does not provide a full coordinate-ring presentation and has not received independent verification.
