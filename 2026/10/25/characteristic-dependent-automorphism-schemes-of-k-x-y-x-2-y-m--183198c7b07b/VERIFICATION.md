The geometric automorphism calculation reduces to the coefficient equations \(f^2+2xaf=0\) and \(mxb g^{m-1}=0\). Coefficient counts give the four scheme dimensions; derivations give the tangent dimensions. The case \(m=2\) was checked separately in odd characteristic and agrees with the general dimension formula. The stated smoothness criterion follows by comparing tangent and local dimensions at the identity of the group scheme.
