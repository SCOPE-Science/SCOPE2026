The characteristic-three reduction of the three-quadric Wiman genus-five model has exact zeta numerator \((1+3T^2)^3(1-2T^2+9T^4)\). Its Jacobian splits by a prime-to-three isogeny into three supersingular elliptic factors and an ordinary simple abelian surface; the resulting \(3\)-rank is \(2\) and the \(a\)-number is \(3\).
