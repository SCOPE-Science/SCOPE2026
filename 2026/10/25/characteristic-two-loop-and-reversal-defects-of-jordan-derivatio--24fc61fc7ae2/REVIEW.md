# Scientific review: square-Jordan defect of radical-square-zero quiver algebras

## Correctness — PASS

The proof directly checks the square-only identity and its polarized consequence on the complete vertex-idempotent/arrow basis. The idempotent values give a unique inner normalization. A non-loop arrow can map to its own or reversed hom-space, and the reversed part is exactly the Leibniz defect. A loop maps only to its own loop space in odd characteristic. In characteristic \(2\), loop images may have loop outputs at any vertex and diagonal outputs constant along every non-loop connected component. Ordinary derivations among these allow only local loops and the single scalar-derivative choice for each non-loop-isolated vertex possessing exactly one loop. Thus the computed dimensions are explicit vector-space differences, not inferred from finite enumeration. Sixteen separate coefficient-system tests over \(\mathbb F_2\), \(\mathbb F_3\), and \(\mathbb F_5\) all agree. The finite checks are illustrative and not infinite proofs.

## Originality — PASS with residual priority risk

Li–van Wyk–Wei (arXiv:1202.2527v1, Proposition 3.2) already completely parameterize Jordan maps on two-block generalized matrix rings without a characteristic restriction, and their Theorem 3.11 gives derivation-plus-antiderivation decompositions for **2-torsion-free** zero-pairing instances. The general 2012 parameterization can in principle be applied recursively to the quiver setting, so the novelty claim is restricted to the fully evaluated closed graph-theoretic dimension and characteristic-two loop correction, not the existence of a parameterization. The arrow-reversal mechanism in two-block, odd-characteristic cases is therefore not a new discovery; it is fully credited. Li–Wei (arXiv:1203.4925v1) treats rigidity of selected acyclic path algebras, not the general radical-square-zero defect. Crucially, the new positive characteristic-two contribution \(\sum_{u\ne v}\ell_u\ell_v+Lc-s\), including central component identities, is not implied by the torsion-free theorem. Generalized triangular characteristic-independent theorems cover some no-opposite/no-loop subfamilies, not arbitrary cyclic multi-loop quivers. Searches of accessible mathematical literature found no matching all-quiver quotient-dimension formula; absence of a search match does not prove novelty. A major residual risk is that the closed formula might be regarded as a routine corollary of the 2012 general parameterization; no absolute-priority claim is made. Unknown published or unpublished tabulations remain additional risks.

## Value — PASS

The invariant is an exact full classification across all finite directed multigraphs and fields, not a hand-chosen finite slice. It locates the **complete** failure of the Jordan-to-Leibniz implication in orientation reversals and two characteristic-two loop mechanisms; the single-loop dual-number exception is sharp. The formula determines an exact finite-field map-count ratio and an elementary graph criterion, extending the motivated 2012 antiderivation question into torsion-characteristic quiver settings. Small finite calculations alone are not the basis of its value.
