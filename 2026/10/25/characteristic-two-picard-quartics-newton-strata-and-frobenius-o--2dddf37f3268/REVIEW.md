# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The smoothness criterion \(\Delta=c^2+abc+a^2d\ne0\) follows from the branch polynomial derivative. Riemann--Hurwitz gives genus three, and the three regular differentials yield the exact Cartier arrows \(0\), \(\omega_2\), and \(\sqrt c\,\omega_0+\sqrt a\,\omega_1\). They prove the \(a\)-number and both stable ranks. The additivity of \(x^4+b x^2+c x\), the explicit quotient invariant \(z=x^2+t x\), and the nonzero coefficient \(c/t\) prove the elliptic isogeny and supersingularity. The Frobenius-orbit determinant argument proves the formula over every finite field. The standalone checker verifies all \(\mathbb F_2\) smooth models, both \(\mathbb F_2\)/\(\mathbb F_4\) smoothness censuses and each of four supersingular \(\mathbb F_2\) orbit factors. Finite checks are corroborative, not proofs of the infinite-field assertions.

## Originality — PASS, with a defined duplication risk

Nart--Ritzenthaler, arXiv:math/0312366, already cover the broad Newton strata for all nonhyperelliptic genus-three curves in characteristic two. Nart--Ritzenthaler, arXiv:0905.0546, already prove the general three-elliptic-quotient decomposition in the presence of a rational Klein-four group, and exhibit supersingular normal families. Neither abstract classification nor the general isogeny principle is new. Direct inspection did not locate the combined coefficient-specific criterion, exact three-type Frobenius splitting census, and explicit orbit-induced zeta formula when the three nonzero translations are permuted by Frobenius. The strongest remaining risk is a coordinate equivalence or a published descent corollary in the same authors' broader literature. Other inspected results do not settle that comparison by matching their statements to the complete coefficientwise formula.

## Value — PASS

The result describes an intrinsically motivated Picard stratum, gives explicit equations for elliptic quotients, and computes an entire family of genus-three zeta functions through at most three small elliptic point counts. The nonrational-translation cases and exact three-way counts add arithmetic information not supplied by simply counting geometrically split elliptic factors. Both the smooth irreducible coefficient divisor and its exact complete Newton-polygon transition are natural, not arbitrary small parameter slices.
