For smooth characteristic-two Picard quartics \(y^3=x^4+a x^3+b x^2+c x+d\), the supersingular coefficient divisor is \(a=0,c\ne0\); otherwise the \(2\)-rank is two, and the \(a\)-number is always one. On the supersingular divisor, a complete three-type Frobenius orbit census and a zeta-factorization formula give every finite-field \(L\)-polynomial from elliptic quotient point counts, including the case of no rational involutions.
