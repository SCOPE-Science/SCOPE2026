# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** Substitution in the weight-zero Rota–Baxter identity forces the scalar coefficient and scalar functional to vanish and gives precisely \(T^2=0\), \(2Tu=0\). Bilinearity gives sufficiency. Flag counting proves the exact rank distribution, and explicit centralizer shears establish four nonempty-orbit types, their sizes, and the parity-dependent total. Direct exhaustive tests on 328,464 linear maps and finite binary automorphism orbits confirm small cases; the proof handles all dimensions and all fields in scope.

## Originality
**PASS within compared coverage.** Gubarev (arXiv:1805.00723, Theorem 5.5) establishes characteristic-zero nilpotency, not the modular class census; Gubarev (arXiv:2006.02654, Theorem 2) proves a general nilpotency statement in positive characteristic, not these rank and conjugacy profiles. Farooq (arXiv:2605.15670, Theorem 3.4) treats exactly the split square-zero algebra but **only characteristic zero**, obtaining \(Tu=0\). Its full text was inspected. The distinguishing modular possibilities \(Tu\ne0\), the corresponding sharp nilpotency index, and the exact \(\mathrm{GL}(V)\)-orbit distribution are not implications of those characteristic-zero statements. Related public results on a four-dimensional exterior algebra treat a different multiplication. The remaining risk is uncatalogued equivalent modular literature; no uniqueness certification is claimed.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** Rota–Baxter index and algebra-automorphism orbits are intrinsic invariants. The characteristic-two boundary changes the maximal nilpotency index from two to three already at radical dimension two, while the exact rank-stratified counting and complete conjugacy classification resolve the moduli problem for every dimension over finite even fields, not just an isolated numerical case.

## Limitations
No assertion concerns nonzero-weight operators or radical powers higher than two. Finite checks do not prove the infinite family. Full historical exhaustiveness has not been established. The published characteristic-zero theorem is explicitly credited, and independent validation remains outstanding.
