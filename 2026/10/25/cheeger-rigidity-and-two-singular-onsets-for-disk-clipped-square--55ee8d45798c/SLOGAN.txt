For the disk-clipped square \(K_R=[-1,1]^2\cap B_R\), the ordinary Cheeger constant and set become exactly independent of \(R\) at \(R_*=\sqrt2-(\sqrt2-1)2/(2+\sqrt\pi)\). Before that sharp plateau the Cheeger constant follows a unique circular-arc equation, with distinct \(3/2\)-order and quadratic endpoint singularities.
