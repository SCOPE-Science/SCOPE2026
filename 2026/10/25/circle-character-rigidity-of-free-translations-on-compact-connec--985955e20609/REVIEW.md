# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Nullhomotopy of the circle eigenfunction lifts to the real line; connectedness makes each translation increment constant; compactness forces the resulting real character to vanish, contradicting the nontrivial circle character. Character separation gives the translation theorem; the join/no-nullhomotopy equivalence is explicit in the primary paper. The p-adic embedding is a coordinatewise continuous injective map from a compact group into a Hausdorff product and is therefore closed. No computational or independence claim is involved.

**Originality — PASS, subject to bounded-literature risk.** The full recent source [1] proves a group-level existential characterization and exhibits a free p-adic action admitting a join map. It does not imply existence or nonexistence for a specified connected torus-translation action. The older source [2, Sections 1 and 3] develops the general conjecture and reductions but does not state the character-lift translation theorem. Semantic database searches found related finite-circle mapping space results, which concern different objects and do not imply the claim. Classical character separation and join/nullhomotopy equivalence are appropriately credited. An unindexed analogous character criterion remains a residual overlap risk.

**Value — PASS.** The recent counterexample makes the action-specific boundary urgent: the very same torsion-free profinite group has an allowable join map on one connected compact product and a forbidden map on another. The broad structural theorem for all compact connected abelian translation targets explains this separation by a transparent topological invariant, not a parameter substitution or a finite instance.

**Relevant limitations.** Requires the circle-valued eigenfunction and connectedness; not universal for arbitrary free actions. Prior literature search is bounded. The only imported standard results are covering homotopy, Pontryagin character separation, and a cited join correspondence.
