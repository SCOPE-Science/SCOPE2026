Binary circles avoiding every mesosome within one turn are exactly the constant circles, two-run circles with a singleton run or two odd runs, and even alternating circles. Their necklace count is \(4\) at every odd circumference \(n\geq3\) and \(n/2+3\) at even circumference \(n\geq4\), exhibiting a sharp parity collapse from the richer linear avoidance family.
