# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
For each farthest vertex the Euclidean radial functional uniquely exposes it. The strict exposed-vertex inequality bounds the scaled tangent section, and the inward ray establishes positive relative interior. The proof establishes angular localization proportional to the radial deficit; an active-facet expansion on a bounded gnomonic chart reduces spherical cap volume to a finite polyhedral tangent-section volume with an explicitly justified \(O_K(\varepsilon^d)\) error. The spherical Jacobian and antipodal-count normalization agree with direct rectangle and octahedron tests, and exact Gram determinants reproduce the orthotope and cross-polytope formulas. The scope is all fixed, full-dimensional origin-symmetric convex polytopes; no smooth or non-symmetric generalization is asserted.

## Originality — PASS, with residual uncertainty
The cited September 2026 primary manuscript studies random radii of convex-body sections but gives bounds, not the stated exact polyhedral vertex-cone tail. The published parity-resolved central-chord result is restricted to regular planar polygons. The published cross-polytope hyperplane result samples sections of a different dimension and observable. The prior orthotope exact law includes the dimension-three box special case (which is not claimed as new), while the present theorem covers arbitrary polyhedral face structures and every fixed dimension. Classical offset-randomized chord laws use a different distribution. Search, full-text comparisons, and past-claim deduplication reveal no general result implying the statement. This does not establish exhaustive mathematical priority: unindexed older integral geometry remains an originality risk.

## Value — PASS
The exact exponent and leading coefficient form a general structural law for the extreme probability of random central chords, with coefficient computable by finite linear inequalities from the circumvertex normal fans. It gives a meaningful all-dimensional distinction between vertex-controlled polyhedral tails and smooth-body endpoint behavior, extends beyond symmetry-regular special families, and provides sharp constants for all orthotopes and all cross-polytopes. It is an exact motivated geometric-probability limit, not a mere numerical instance or routine single-parameter substitution.

## Limits
The error constant is body-dependent; full distributions, non-symmetric chords and smooth-body regimes are not determined. The checks are deterministic finite diagnostics, not proofs of all-dimension claims. Literature indexing is incomplete.
