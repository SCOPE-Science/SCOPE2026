# Scientific review: color-permutation characters of cycle–clique products

## Correctness — PASS
The theorem's group action is specified on the actual simplicial vertex set, and the augmented simplicial-chain sign is \(-(-1)^s\) for a setwise invariant face having \(s\) orbits. Adjacent nonempty row subsets in the categorical product must equal a common single fixed color; hence all other nonempty invariant row choices have the exact signed aggregate weight \(f-1\). The resulting cyclic transfer has a sum-zero color-eigenspace of multiplicity \(f-1\) with eigenvalue \(-1\), and a remaining cubic characteristic polynomial \(t^3-(f-1)\). The zero-fixed-color boundary is treated separately, avoiding an invalid negative-dimensional eigenspace. This proves the equivariant Euler identity without asymptotics or experiments. Only the last upgrade to a single-degree character relies on the independently published all-parameter homotopy classification of \(\operatorname{Ind}(C_{3r}\times K_n)\). Every sign and boundary case has been checked against the finite script and direct oriented-simplex traces.

## Originality — PASS on available evidence
The relevant primary full text (Antolín Camarena–Carnero Bravo, arXiv:2307.12401v2, Section 3 and Theorem 22) determines a nonequivariant wedge-of-spheres type. It yields only the **identity** character value; an isomorphic nonequivariant vector space can carry many inequivalent \(S_n\)-actions. The earlier Goyal–Shukla–Singh source (arXiv:1905.06926v1, Section 3) studies categorical products of complete graphs, not the canonical color action on cycle–clique products. Comparisons with published character calculations for generalized Mycielskians, short categorical products and other graph complexes do not imply the statement for **all** \(r,n\). The theorem adds a closed character value on every nonidentity conjugacy class, and also a uniform virtual character outside the pure-degree range. The rank specialization itself is not claimed as new. Searches cannot exclude an equivalent unindexed result; no independent originality audit has been performed.

## Value — PASS
This is a systematic symmetry refinement of an established infinite-family homotopy classification: the calculation extracts every rational \(S_n\)-homology character value from a canonical action that the nonequivariant theorem does not constrain. It distinguishes permutation actions, exhibits fixed-point-count rigidity independent of nontrivial color cycle lengths, and supplies an exact representation-ring identity involving tensor powers of the augmentation representation. The non-divisible-by-three formula provides a uniform graded Euler-character constraint in the range where the homotopy type and possible integral torsion are not completely resolved. The result does not claim to resolve those remaining homotopy or torsion questions.

## Closest literature and limitations
Theorem 22 of arXiv:2307.12401 is the crucial input for homology concentration; a prior calculation of \(\operatorname{Ind}(C_5\times K_n)\) gives the augmentation character in that short-cycle case but not the divisible-by-three all-parameter theorem. A separately published \(S_n\)-character result for generalized Mycielskians concerns a different graph construction and does not dominate this result. Primary text was read for arXiv:2307.12401 and arXiv:1905.06926; some neighboring published findings were accessible only as abstract/summary records. Risks are unindexed equivalents and unknown full-text comparisons to those neighboring abstracts. The theorem is rational-equivariant, not integral-equivariant; for \(3\nmid m\), only a virtual graded character is established.
