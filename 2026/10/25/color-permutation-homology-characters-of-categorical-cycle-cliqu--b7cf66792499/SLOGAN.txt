Color relabelings of \(\operatorname{Ind}(C_{3r}\times K_n)\) act on its unique reduced rational homology group with character \(3(f-1)^r+(-1)^r(f-1)\), where \(f\) is the number of fixed colors. The signed-face transfer calculation also determines the full equivariant Euler character at every other cycle length, without asserting a torsion classification there.
