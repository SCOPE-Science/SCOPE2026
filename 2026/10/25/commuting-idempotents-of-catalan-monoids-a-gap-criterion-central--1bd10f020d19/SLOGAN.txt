In the classical Catalan monoid, two predecessor-projection idempotents commute exactly when no gap between consecutive common fixed points contains both kinds of exclusive fixed points. Ordered commuting idempotent pairs have exact count \(\big((2+\sqrt2)^n-(2-\sqrt2)^n\big)/(2\sqrt2)\), with an explicit transfer formula for each idempotent's commuting-idempotent centralizer and exponentially decaying pair density.
