For the four-vertex path quiver with \(a\) parallel arrows on the first edge and \(c\) on the last, the radical commuting-pair count over every \(\mathbb F_q\) has an explicit uniform formula involving only ranks of \(a\times4\) matrices. It also gives an exact conjugacy-class count for the four-block unipotent group of block sizes \((a,1,1,c)\), beyond previously established polynomiality.
