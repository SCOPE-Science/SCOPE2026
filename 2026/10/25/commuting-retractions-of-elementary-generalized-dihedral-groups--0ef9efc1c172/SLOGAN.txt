For odd primes \(p\) and dimensions \(d\ge2\), the commuting graph of all retractions of \(\mathbb F_p^d\rtimes C_2\) has exactly \(p^d|\operatorname{GL}_d(\mathbb F_p)|/(d!(p-1)^d)\) maximal cliques, each of size \(2^d+1\), and every clique is the full Boolean family of affine coordinate projections together with the trivial group endomorphism. Explicit Gaussian-binomial formulas also give the complete rank multiplicities and every vertex degree.
