For every four-run binary word \(0^a1^b0^c1^d\), the complete length spectrum of contiguous factors that occur together with their bitwise complements has an explicit three-part formula. At length \(n\ge6\), its sharp maximum is \(2t(n-2t+1)\), where \(t=\lceil n/4\rceil\), attained exactly when the two interior runs both have length \(t\) and the outer runs are at most \(t\) with the prescribed total length.
