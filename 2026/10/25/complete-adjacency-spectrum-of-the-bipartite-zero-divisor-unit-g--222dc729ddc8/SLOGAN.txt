For every finite reduced commutative nonfield, the complete adjacency spectrum of the bipartite zero-divisor–unit graph factors explicitly over coordinate subsets, with all eigenvalue multiplicities determined by the sizes of the finite-field unit groups. Fourier characters identify its nonzero singular values and show that the unit-side incidence matrix has rank \(\prod_i(q_i-1)-\prod_i(q_i-2)\), resolving the spectral question posed for this graph in arXiv:2511.07854v1.
