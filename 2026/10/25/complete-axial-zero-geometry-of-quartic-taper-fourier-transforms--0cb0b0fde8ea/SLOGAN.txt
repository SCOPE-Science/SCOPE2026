For every positive quartic-taper parameter \(t\), all complex zeros of the Fourier transform of \(1-tx^4\) on \([-1,1]\) are real or purely imaginary, with exactly one real zero per higher \(\pi\)-interval. A unique imaginary pair exists precisely for \(1<t<5\), merging at the origin when \(t=5\); the proof is an explicit positive-residue partial-fraction representation.
