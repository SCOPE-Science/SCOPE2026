For two labeled, collision-free particles on the \(m\)-branch banana graph, \(m\ge3\), the full rational first-homology representation of branch permutations, pole reversal and label exchange is the signed ordered-pair permutation module modulo its one-dimensional total-sum line. Unordered first homology is the pole-sign twist of \(\bigwedge^2\mathbb Q^m\), while all higher integral homology vanishes.
