For Haar-uniform central planes, a regular rhombic triacontahedron has exactly three generic section types: decagons with probability \(0.1226017195\ldots\), dodecagons with probability \(0.5252352041\ldots\), and tetradecagons with probability \(0.3521630763\ldots\). A complete four-triangle partition of its fundamental icosahedral spherical chamber gives exact elementary formulas for all three probabilities.
