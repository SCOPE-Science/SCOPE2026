For the first positive constant shift of the reciprocal quadratic divisor problem, every positive coprime solution with \(a<b\) is exactly \(a=(d^2-d+2)/2\), \(b=(d^2+d+2)/2\), with \(d\) odd or a multiple of \(4\). In contrast to the constant-one case, this yields infinitely many integer two-cycles and the explicit quadratic family containing every prime two-cycle.
