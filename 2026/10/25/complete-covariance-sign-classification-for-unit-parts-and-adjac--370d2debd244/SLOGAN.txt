In a uniformly random ordered composition of \(n\) into \(k\) positive parts, the covariance between the number of unit parts and adjacent equalities is negative only for two or three parts at even \(n\), but positive for all \(k\ge5\) once \(n\ge k+2\). Exactly four parts give a sharp parity boundary: positive covariance at even \(n\ge6\) and zero covariance otherwise.
