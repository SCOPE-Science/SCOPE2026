For the open Lovász neighborhood complex of the ternary three-cube, the entire 56-dimensional rational second-homology character under the 1296-element symmetry group is determined on all 22 conjugacy classes. Restricting to ternary translations yields \(2\mathbb Q[(\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z)^3]\oplus2\mathbb Q\); the full symmetry quotient is rationally acyclic.
