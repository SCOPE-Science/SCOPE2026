## Value — PASS
The theorem gives the entire rational \(D_n\)-representation for a natural two-parameter family previously classified only nonequivariantly. It identifies rotation order effects, the two reflection conjugacy types, and a period-\(r+2\) finite-state mechanism. Even rank-one cases can have distinct actions: at \((r,n)=(2,6)\), the generating rotation and one reflection have trace \(-1\), whereas the other reflection has trace \(+1\). At \((2,8)\), the rank-three action has rotation traces \((3,1,-1,1,3,1,-1,1)\). These are genuine equivariant invariants not encoded in sphere ranks and are useful for rational homology of finite symmetry quotients.
