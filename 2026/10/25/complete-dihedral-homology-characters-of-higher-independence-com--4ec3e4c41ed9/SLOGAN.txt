For \(r\ge2\), the rational dihedral homology character of every higher-independence complex \(\operatorname{Ind}_r(C_n)\) is determined exactly: rotation traces depend only on \(\gcd(n,a)\), while reflection traces are coefficients of a short rational generating function. This refines the known nonequivariant sphere-wedge classification and distinguishes the two geometric reflection types when \(n\) is even.
