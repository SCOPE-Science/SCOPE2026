# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The theorem fixes \(m,r,R,F,a,b\) precisely. The key characteristic-two identity \(P(X+1)+P(X)=P'(X)\) follows termwise from the seven-term construction. The two-to-one derivative property follows independently by computing the kernel of the additive Frobenius difference and then its trace-zero image. Taylor expansions mod \(4\) prove two distinct invariances of the lifted difference, first on each residue class and then under translation by \(a\); the induced output values are distinguished by their residues. These steps force exactly \(q/2\) outputs of multiplicity \(2q\), for every coefficient lift. Finite checks over a concrete ring are compatible with, but are not substituted for, the proof.

## Originality

**PASS, with residual search risk.** Fukui (arXiv:2610.21178v1, Theorem 6.2 and the paragraph following it) expressly leaves exceptional unit directions to an upper bound and lists only sample tables for \(m=5\). The present all-parameter, all-coefficient-lift, exact characteristic-four row is stronger than that bound and is not implied by the published determination of other rows. Rønjom–Sandrib's Proposition 4(1) is an upper bound only. Fukui (arXiv:2610.21169v1, Section VIII) proves a global maximum rather than this row distribution. Relevant mathematical-result records and accessible prior claims did not expose an equivalent or stronger result. No claim of comprehensive uniqueness or priority is made.

## Value

**PASS.** A conspicuously excluded residue class in a recent complete-ring lifting analysis has a rigid distribution at the first non-field level. The classification establishes which output differences are realized, their exact multiplicities and the invariance under arbitrary coefficient lifts—information not contained in a differential-uniformity bound. The distinction is directly motivated by the recently published special-direction problem and is meaningful for exact differential probabilities.

## Closest sources and limitations

The primary motivating source is arXiv:2610.21178v1, especially Theorem 6.2; see also Rønjom–Sandrib, DOI:10.1007/s12095-026-00895-x, Proposition 4, and arXiv:2610.21169v1, Section VIII. The theorem does not extend to higher characteristic levels without a new argument; outside its specified sparse family the special difference/derivative identity need not hold. Independent external mathematical assessment has not been performed, and unidentified prior coverage is still a risk.
