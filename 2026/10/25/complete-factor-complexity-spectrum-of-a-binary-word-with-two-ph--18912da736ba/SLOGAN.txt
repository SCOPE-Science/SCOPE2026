For the binary word \(W=(01)^a0(01)^b0(01)^c\) with positive \(a,b,c\), every distinct-contiguous-factor count, at every length, is given exactly by four diagonal lattice-rectangle counts plus an alternating-background term. The complete number of distinct nonempty factors is \(2T-1+AB+BC-\min(A,B)\min(B,C)+AC\), where \(A=2a+1\), \(B=2b+1\), \(C=2c\) and \(T=\max(A,B,C)\).
