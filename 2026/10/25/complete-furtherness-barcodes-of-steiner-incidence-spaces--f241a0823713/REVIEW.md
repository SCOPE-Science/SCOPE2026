# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. For a point root the intermediate incidence graph is a tree because every other point determines exactly one block through that root. For a block root every point is joined to a block intersecting the root, so the intermediate graph is connected; its cycle rank is exactly \((k-1)(v-k)\). Graph inclusions inject on degree-one homology, so the computed cycle-rank increases give genuine persistent bars over every field and integrally. Direct finite checks at 100 roots in four designs agree. Stong's core theorem yields the finite-space nonhomotopy conclusion, since no incidence element is a beat point and nonisomorphic designs produce nonisomorphic ordered spaces.

## Originality
PASS subject to priority uncertainty. The closest motivating primary work, Badra–Singh arXiv:2610.16386v1, constructs the furtherness filtration and studies degree-zero classes, but its statements do not provide the root-dependent degree-one barcode formulas for Steiner incidence spaces. The previous furtherness paper arXiv:2603.18580v1 gives the metric formula, not persistence. Other known results for Steiner incidence graphs analyze Lovász neighborhood or graph-metric Vietoris–Rips constructions, not this filtration. Existing tabulations enumerate the 80 designs but do not supply these barcodes. The first-homology computation relies on a design-specific counting argument in addition to standard graph topology. A universal proof of priority is not claimed.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The theorem gives a complete, field-independent persistent-homology profile for an infinite and naturally motivated class of finite spaces, exhibits a two-stage homological birth phenomenon invisible in the original degree-zero framework, and establishes a concrete separation limit: 80 distinct finite-space homotopy types share the same complete rooted homological barcode collection. The negative classification is structural rather than an arbitrary finite numerical example.

## Closest literature and limitations
The cited Badra–Singh work is the source of the precise filtration. The 2023 Steiner-system classification and Stong's finite-core theorem supply external input for the 80-space consequence. Closely related persistent Rips and neighborhood-complex calculations apply to other complexes. The result does not extend without additional hypotheses to general designs with multiple blocks per pair. Some related records were accessible only as summaries, leaving residual bibliographic priority uncertainty.
