For every nontrivial Steiner \(2\)-design, the complete degree-zero and degree-one furtherness barcodes of its finite incidence space admit closed formulas depending only on the design parameters: point roots acquire all cycles at time \(k+1\), while block roots acquire \((k-1)(v-k)\) cycles already at time \(k\). As a consequence, all 80 nonisomorphic Steiner triple systems on 15 points yield pairwise nonhomotopy-equivalent finite spaces with indistinguishable complete rooted homological persistence profiles.
