On the complete divisor lattice of every positive integer, the generalized LCM-to-GCD spectrum factors into explicit prime-subset pairs, yielding a closed determinant polynomial for \(G_n+tL_n\). In particular, \(G_n+L_n\) is singular precisely when \(n\) has an odd number of distinct prime factors and every prime factor occurs to at least the second power, with exact nullity and inertia given by the exponent vector.
