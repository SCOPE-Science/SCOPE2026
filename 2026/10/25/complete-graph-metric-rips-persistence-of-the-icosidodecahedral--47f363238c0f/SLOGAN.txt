The graph-metric Vietoris–Rips filtration of the icosidodecahedron has homotopy stages \(\bigvee^{29}S^0\), \(\bigvee^{11}S^1\), \(S^2\), \(S^2\), \(S^{14}\), and a point at integer thresholds zero through five. The inclusion between the two \(S^2\) stages is itself a homotopy equivalence, giving one persistent integral degree-two class on \([2,4)\).
