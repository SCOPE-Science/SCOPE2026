Every higher Frobenius–Schur indicator of every simple module of the Drinfeld double of a finite abelian-kernel, cyclic-complement Frobenius group has an explicit formula using only character orders, \(\gcd(k,m)\), and kernel torsion counts. The complete dimension-tagged indicator spectrum recovers the abelian kernel and complement order, yet cannot distinguish different fixed-point-free actions—even for two nonisomorphic groups of order \(147\).
