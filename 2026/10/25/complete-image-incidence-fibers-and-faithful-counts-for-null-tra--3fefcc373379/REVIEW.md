# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof first forces each generator to fix the zero point by associativity, then uses every zero composite to prove that every generator annihilates the *union* of all generator images. The converse holds because each image is contained in that annihilated union together with zero. The cell multinomial partitions exactly the labeled nonzero carrier; adjoining one dummy input labels the zero fiber and yields \(a_i!\left\{\begin{smallmatrix}r+1\\a_i+1\end{smallmatrix}\right\}\) surjections per generator, independently. Strict positivity of these factors gives the claimed necessary-and-sufficient inequality even when some \(a_i\) vanish. Partition-lattice Möbius inversion correctly enforces injectivity of all \(m+1\) semigroup elements, not only distinct generator image sets. Exhaustive independent checks cover every incidence profile in ten small parameter cases and directly check the faithful inversion. The calculations are finite diagnostics; the proof is all-parameter.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

The full texts of Cameron–Gadouleau–Mitchell–Péresse (2015), Cameron–East–FitzGerald–Mitchell–Pebody–Quinn-Gregson (2021/2023), and Cain–Malheiro–Paulista (2023) were compared at their relevant statements. The 2015 article studies chains and maximum null families in principal factors. The 2021 article's Lemmas 4.2–4.3 classify null subsemigroups by annihilated image sets and count each full ambient family; Section 4.2 counts maximal null subsemigroups. Those results are explicitly credited as structural prior art, rather than relabeled as new. They do not state or immediately evaluate the full labeled generator-image Venn-vector fibers, nor the exact faithful embedding totals. The 2023 article gives maximal-size theorems. Targeted research-record queries for null semigroup homomorphism counts, faithful transformation representations, Stirling image-incidence spectra, and annihilating transformation tuples revealed no direct coverage. Related literature may implicitly contain an equivalent occupancy formula; lack of a matching hit is not priority proof.

## Value — PASS

A null semigroup is the basic nilpotency-index-two case of a semigroup representation. The incidence profile of all generator images is an intrinsic, natural statistic of a labeled action, and the complete multivariate distribution determines every joint intersection statistic and its feasibility boundary. The exact count of **injective** representations refines existing minimum-degree/existence results to exact multiplicities and can support representation-growth and random-action calculations. The result handles arbitrary generator number and carrier size, not a small arbitrary numerical slice. It does not claim a broader semigroup classification or downstream theorem.

## Literature and limits

The nearest structural statement is Lemma 4.2 and the cardinality Lemma 4.3 of arXiv:2110.09701; the exact new counting problem requires specification of all generator images, their incidence cells, independent surjectivity, and a partition-lattice inversion. Papers arXiv:1501.06394 and arXiv:2310.08481 address different extremal problems. Residual risks are equivalence hidden in earlier enumerations, different conventions for the zero action, and the absence of independent expert validation. Higher-rank zero maps, unpointed automorphism orbits, and non-null semigroups are outside the theorem.
