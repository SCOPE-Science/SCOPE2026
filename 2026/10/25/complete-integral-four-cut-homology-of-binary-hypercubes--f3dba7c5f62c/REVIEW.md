# Scientific review: binary hypercube four-cut homology

## Correctness — PASS
The exact input is the ordinary four-cut complex of \(Q_d\) for \(d\geq4\). The proof excludes five-faces in its Alexander dual via the impossible induced \(K_{2,3}\), classifies connected four-sets as squares or three-direction sets, and splits the tetrahedral-support complex relative to cubical square fillings. An exact integral local Smith computation gives a rank-35, unit-diagonal three-boundary and rank-21 two-boundary on the three-cube. A long exact sequence and cellular cubical exactness give the global integral homology; Alexander duality transfers it to the original cut complex. The supplied checker reproduces the base matrix and independently confirms dimensions three through six. Finite checks are not treated as proof for all dimensions.

## Originality — PASS against inspected evidence
The closest indexed statement is the known ordinary four-cut result for the three-cube; this is precisely the boundary case and not an implication to all larger cubes. The published shellability heredity result already provides a nonshellability corollary, so that alone is not counted as new. Other inspected theorems apply to total three-cuts of the four-cube, four-cuts of cubic paths, or restricted grid families. None establishes the two integral homology groups for every dimension. Relevant repository full texts could not all be inspected, and independent bibliographic verification remains prudent.

## Value — PASS
This yields exact torsion-free integral homology in two successive degrees for every higher binary cube, including the first case with ranks 25 and 200, and explains the ranks through local three-cube topology and cubical attaching relations. It goes substantially beyond an isolated calculation or inherited nonshellability, but makes no unsupported homotopy classification of the original cut complex.

## Scientific limitations
The local Smith calculation is finite and supplied, while the infinite-family theorem depends on the proved relative decomposition. No independent check of the entire proof has yet occurred. A yet-unlocated equivalent publication remains possible.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
