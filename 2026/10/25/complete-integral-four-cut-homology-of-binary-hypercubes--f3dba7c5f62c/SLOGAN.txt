For every binary hypercube \(Q_d\) with \(d\geq4\), the ordinary four-cut complex has exactly two nonzero reduced integral homology groups, in degrees \(2^d-6\) and \(2^d-5\), with explicit torsion-free ranks determined by the numbers of coordinate squares and three-cubes. The local three-cube calculation and the relative cubical decomposition explain the lower-degree classes and give complete formulas across the infinite family.
