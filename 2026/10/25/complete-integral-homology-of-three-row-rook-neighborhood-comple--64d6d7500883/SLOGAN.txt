For every \(n\ge5\), the open neighborhood complex of the three-row rook graph has one integral one-cycle, \(3n^2-9n+3\) independent two-cycles, and three classes in degree \(n-2\), with no torsion or other reduced homology. A three-row Mayer–Vietoris decomposition isolates the crown-graph contributions and proves the complete infinite-family classification.
