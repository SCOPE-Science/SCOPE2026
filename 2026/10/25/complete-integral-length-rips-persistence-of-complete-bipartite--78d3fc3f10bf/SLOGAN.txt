The symmetrized \(\ell^1\)-Rips filtration of every complete bipartite shortest-path metric has an explicit integral sphere-wedge homotopy type at **every** scale, with all reduced persistence bars of length one and binomially determined multiplicities. Despite the graph metric having diameter two, arbitrarily high-dimensional spheres arise at larger length scales; these results concern unordered complexes, not blurred-magnitude simplicial sets.
