For the rank-span filtration of every proper Boolean lattice, the complete reduced integral persistent homology has exactly \(b_{n,k}\) one-step bars in degree \(k<n-2\), with \(b_{n,0}=2^n-3\) and \(b_{n,k}=\binom nk(2^{n-k}-2)-b_{n,k-1}\). A unique final sphere class lives forever; the sphere-wedge existence is previously known, while the uniform multiplicities and complete barcode are determined here.
