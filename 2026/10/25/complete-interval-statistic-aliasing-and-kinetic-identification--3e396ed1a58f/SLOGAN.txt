In a one-dimensional coalescing-particle gas reset to fresh Bernoulli configurations, every stationary contiguous-void statistic is invariant under exchanging the reset-length root and the Bernoulli vacancy probability; a sharp twofold inverse ambiguity survives arbitrarily long interval measurements. The stationary density and one initial occupation-autocorrelation slope uniquely recover both the reset rate and Bernoulli density, with an exact realizability region.
