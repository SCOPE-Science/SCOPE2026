A cube's complete vertex-centered intrinsic surface-distance distribution has an explicit two-area formula, with transitions at \(1\) and \(\sqrt2\), a unique farthest point at distance \(\sqrt5\), and exact even moments \(\mathbb E[R^2]=3/2\) and \(\mathbb E[R^4]=61/18\). Its distance formula is proved by an exhaustive 80-face-walk unfolding comparison, and its farthest-distance tail is quadratic with coefficient \(5/12\) in the radius deficit.
