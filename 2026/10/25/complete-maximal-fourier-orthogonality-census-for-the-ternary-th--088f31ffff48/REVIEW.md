# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The full 216-frequency domain and counting normalization are specified. The exact product of the three geometric character sums vanishes precisely when at least one frequency difference is congruent to 2 or 4 modulo 6. This is exactly the adjacency predicate constructed by the bundled integer-only graph generator. A standard exhaustive pivoted Bron--Kerbosch recursion with its explicit \(R,P,X\) invariant visits every maximal clique once. All 248,264 cliques, the five size counts, and sample maximality witnesses were checked without floating-point tests or premature search termination. The separate NetworkX implementation independently gives the same histogram. This is a finite theorem only.

## Originality — PASS, best knowledge

Kolountzakis--Lev--Matolcsi, *Maximality and completeness of orthogonal exponentials on the cube* (arXiv:2410.12387), address the motivating phenomenon in continuous cubes and distinct-prime-square cyclic examples, but not this exact finite census. Agora--Grepstad--Kolountzakis (arXiv:1602.03165) classify *complete* finite-cube spectra through tilings; this does not count maximal incomplete systems. Dutour Sikirić--Itoh (arXiv:0805.2493) study a related but inequivalent cube-packing graph. The full 2011 Łysakowska--Przesławski structural classification was not accessible; its relevance remains a bibliographic risk. No independent claim of absolute priority is made.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The finite ternary cube has a natural 27-dimensional character-sampling space. Determining the exact sizes and multiplicities of **unextendible** mutually orthogonal exponential families quantifies the gap between maximality and basis completeness beyond a construction of one example. The sharp cardinality gap, nontrivial minimum 12, and 93,312 incompletes motivate the particular invariant. This is not a mere normalization check or a claim about general dimensions.

## Limitations

Only full-grid discrete frequencies in \((\mathbb Z/6\mathbb Z)^3\) are considered; no conclusion about off-grid or continuous cube frequencies is implied. Priority search cannot rule out an older unpublished or differently named exact census. The 2011 paper is a specifically unresolved full-text comparison.
