For the ternary digit cube \(\{0,1,2\}^3\) inside \((\mathbb Z/6\mathbb Z)^3\), maximal orthogonal character families have *exactly* the sizes 12, 13, 14, 15, and 27, with 248,264 labeled families in total. Incompleteness is possible at precisely the four smaller sizes; no maximal family has between 16 and 26 members.
