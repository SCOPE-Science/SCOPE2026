For every balanced Johnson graph \(J(2k,k)\) with \(k\ge4\), the rational \(S_{2k}\times C_2\) action on its unique nonzero reduced homology has a closed cycle-index character, including all coordinate permutations followed by complementation. Pure complementation always has trace \(-1\) and splits the degree-two homology into eigenspaces of dimensions \((B_k-1)/2\) and \((B_k+1)/2\).
