# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The diagonal-plus-arrow multiplication is exact because the arrow ideal has square zero. The geometric-sum coefficient has only the two possibilities identified by equal or unequal prescribed diagonal powers; these yield independent, exhaustive arrow equations for every target, every characteristic, every exponent, and loops/multiple arrows. The resulting root fibers, image sizes, and identity fibers follow without asymptotics or field-size assumptions. The direct-multiplication verifier checks every target in 126 small finite configurations, including characteristic two and four-element fields. This is corroboration, not the general proof.

## Originality — PASS
Primary-literature comparison: Słowik's 2012 paper classifies involutions and counts *total* identity fibers in complete triangular groups; the full text could not be retrieved, which is recorded as priority risk. Gargate--Gargate's complete, accessible 2020 manuscript gives total potent-element counts and diagonal classification for full triangular and selected incidence algebras, under characteristic restrictions. The exact statements in its Theorems 1.1--1.2 and Section 5 do not supply a full prescribed-target \(k\)-th-root fiber classification for arbitrary radical-square-zero quiver unit groups, particularly when the characteristic divides the exponent. Therefore special identity-fiber cases are recognized as earlier coverage, but the joint all-target/all-characteristic theorem is not directly implied by those cited prior statements. The accessible published records located by additional targeted searches concerned class sizes, character degrees, or different groups. A complete priority census is not claimed.

## Value — PASS
For any finite group, the complete power-map fibers refine element-order data, identify the image of every exponent map, and quantify root multiplicities for prescribed targets. This theorem determines the whole natural invariant uniformly for a broad representation-theoretic algebra family, including loops and parallel arrows, and demonstrates a sharp structural transition at exponents divisible by the characteristic. The graph-coloring interpretation yields computable exact formulas and forest/cycle evaluations. These features are not merely rephrasings of an isolated known involution count.
