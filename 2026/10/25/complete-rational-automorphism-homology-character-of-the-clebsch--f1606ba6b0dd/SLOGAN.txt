The rational third homology of the Clebsch independence complex is exactly the nine-dimensional \(1920\)-symmetry module \(\operatorname{sgn}\otimes(P_0\oplus M)\), where \(P_0\) is the standard four-dimensional coordinate-permutation representation and \(M\) is the five-dimensional module assembled from the five distinguished translation characters. In particular its translation-invariant subspace has dimension four and its full-automorphism-invariant subspace is zero; this refines, rather than replaces, the known nine-sphere homotopy classification.
