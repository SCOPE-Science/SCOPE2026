The natural \(S_6\)-action on the two nonzero reduced rational homology groups of the six-vertex graph domination complex \(D_{6,3}\) has an explicit, fully determined Specht-module decomposition. In particular, its \(24\)-dimensional fifth homology is \(S^{(5,1)}\oplus S^{(4,2)}\oplus S^{(4,1,1)}\), and neither nonzero homology group has invariant vectors.
