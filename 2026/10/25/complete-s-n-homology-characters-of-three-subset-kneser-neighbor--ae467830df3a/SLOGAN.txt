For every \(n\ge6\), the full rational \(S_n\)-homology character of the neighborhood complex of the three-subset Kneser graph is \(\operatorname{sgn}(g)Q(a(g),b(g))\), where \(Q\) is an explicit quartic in the fixed-point and two-cycle counts. It has neither trivial nor sign constituents, so both full-symmetric and alternating-group quotients are rationally acyclic; the known sphere-wedge ranks alone do not imply this.
