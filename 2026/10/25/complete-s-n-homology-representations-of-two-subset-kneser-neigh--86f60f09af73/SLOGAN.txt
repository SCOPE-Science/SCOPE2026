For every \(n\ge4\), the complete rational top-homology representation of the open-neighborhood complex of \(\operatorname{KG}(n,2)\) is \(\operatorname{sgn}_n\otimes(\bigwedge^2U_n\oplus S^{(n-2,2)})\). A permutation fixing \(f\) ground elements has character \(\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)(f^2-3f+1)\); thus the entire action is controlled by parity and the fixed-point count.
