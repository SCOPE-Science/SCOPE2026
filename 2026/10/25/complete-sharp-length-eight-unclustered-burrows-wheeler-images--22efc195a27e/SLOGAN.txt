Every sharp four-run primitive necklace of length eight with completely unclustered Burrows–Wheeler image is represented by one of exactly six ordered output patterns. For any alphabet with \(q\ge3\) symbols, there are precisely \(3\binom q3+3\binom q4\) such necklaces, and their allowed positive Parikh profiles are exactly the ordered permutations of \((4,2,2)\) and \((2,2,2,2)\).
