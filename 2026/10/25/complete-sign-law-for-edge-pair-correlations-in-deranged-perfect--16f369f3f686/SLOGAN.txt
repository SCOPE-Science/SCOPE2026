In a uniformly random deranged perfect matching, two disjoint allowed edges are eventually negatively correlated exactly when they cross the same two original forbidden pairs; all other disjoint-edge types are positively correlated. The crossing covariance has an exceptional positive sign at four original pairs and decays as \(-1/(16n^4)\), while the other disjoint-edge covariances are of positive order \(n^{-3}\).
