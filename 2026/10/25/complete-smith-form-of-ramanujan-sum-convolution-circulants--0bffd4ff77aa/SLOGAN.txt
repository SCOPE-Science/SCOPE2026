The full integer circulant generated by Ramanujan sums has a completely explicit Smith normal form at every modulus: for \(r=\varphi(n)\), its nonzero factors are \(d_j=\prod_{p^a\parallel n}p^{a-1+\mathbf{1}_{\{j>r/(p-1)\}}}\) for \(1\le j\le r\). Consequently its finite cokernel is cyclic exactly for \(n\in\{1,2,3,6\}\), a property not determined by its Fourier eigenvalues alone.
