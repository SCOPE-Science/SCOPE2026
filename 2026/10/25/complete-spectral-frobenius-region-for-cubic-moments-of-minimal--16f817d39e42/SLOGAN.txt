Among centered isotropic \(d\)-dimensional laws with exactly \(d+1\) atoms, fixing the worst directional third moment fixes the smallest atom mass and leaves a **complete sharp interval** of possible squared third-tensor Hilbert–Schmidt norms. The two ends are attained by one-small-mass versus all-but-one-small-mass profiles, while the regular simplex is the unique simultaneous minimizer of both norms.
