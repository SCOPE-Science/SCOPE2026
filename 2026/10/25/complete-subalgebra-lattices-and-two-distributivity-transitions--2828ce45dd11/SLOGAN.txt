For Hu–Wen's non-solvable evolution algebras \(E_\lambda\) over fields of characteristic not two, the full subalgebra lattice has exactly six, eight, or seven elements, according as \(\lambda=2\), \(\lambda=4\), or neither. It is distributive precisely at \(\lambda=2\) and \(\lambda=4\), with a Boolean three-atom lattice at \(\lambda=4\).
