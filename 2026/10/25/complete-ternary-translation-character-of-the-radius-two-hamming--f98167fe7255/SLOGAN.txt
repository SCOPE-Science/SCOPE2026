For the radius-two Vietoris--Rips complex of the 27-point ternary Hamming cube, the 46-dimensional third rational homology has translation traces \(46,4,-2,1\) at Hamming weights \(0,1,2,3\), respectively. Each ternary Fourier character occurs twice except the eight characters of full coordinate support, which occur once; consequently exactly two independent third-homology classes survive the translation quotient over \(\mathbb Q\).
