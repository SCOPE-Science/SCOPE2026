For every \(p,q,r\ge3\), the second rational homology of two unordered points on the complete tripartite graph has an explicit, uniform \(S_p\times S_q\times S_r\) decomposition into rational Specht tensor products, with 34 distinct constituents once all parts have at least four vertices. Its full character depends only on fixed vertices and two-cycles in each part, and its invariant subspace under independent part relabelings is zero.
