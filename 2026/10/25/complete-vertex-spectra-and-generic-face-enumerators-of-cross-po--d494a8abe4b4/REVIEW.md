# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

A cut section vertex is exactly a vertex lying in the plane or one crossing intersection from an original edge. In C_n all distinct vertex pairs except antipodes are edges. With nonnegative offset b>0, r original vertices above, s on plane and 2n-r-s below, the crossing count is r(2n-r-s)−r plus s: v=s+r(2n-r-s−1). For b=0, r positive, r negative and 2(n-r) on the plane, crossing count r²−r, so w=r(r−1)+2(n−r). Explicit normals with coordinates 2,1,0 or 1,0 realize every indicated pair. Algebra bounds non-supporting sections below by 2n−2 and above by n(n−1). For vertex-avoiding cuts, every crossed signed-simplex j-face contributes one (j−2)-face and inclusion-exclusion on sign choices gives the polynomial. Generic r=1..n counts r(2n-r−1), last two coincide, the remainder strictly increase, giving exactly (n−2)² omissions in the integer range. Independently enumerated crossed edges and signed faces for every normal-sign type n=3..8; all exact identities agree. Geometric combinatorics, not the finite checks, proves general n.

## originality

PASS

General section face-poset correspondence is known; dimension-three octahedral special case is known. Complete all-dimensional two-parameter vertex spectrum, max/gap characterization and generic full f-polynomial are not stated in the inspected primary works. Qualify against a possible older cross-polytope specialization.

## value

PASS

The section-vertex spectrum and its sharp gaps are natural full classification questions for a central regular polytope family. A uniform all-dimensional answer, including degenerate support/central sections, complements the existing octahedral and general slice literature. The independent generic full-face polynomial is mathematically useful for enumerating all combinatorial face ranks, rather than a single unmotivated numeric slice.
