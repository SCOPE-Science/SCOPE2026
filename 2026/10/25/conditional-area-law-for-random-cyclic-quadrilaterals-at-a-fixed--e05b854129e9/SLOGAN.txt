For four independent uniform points on a circle, conditioning on the acute angle between the two diagonals turns the quadrilateral area into the difference of two independent truncated-arcsine cosine variables, yielding its complete conditional distribution and explicit conditional mean and variance. The conditional area support is exactly \([0,2\sin t]\), and its probability of lying within \(\varepsilon\) of the maximum has a sharp linear coefficient at every fixed diagonal angle \(t\).
