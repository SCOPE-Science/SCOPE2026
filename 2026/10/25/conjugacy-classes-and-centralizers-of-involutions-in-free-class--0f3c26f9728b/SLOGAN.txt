For every odd finite field and every generator rank at least three, the involutions in the automorphism group of the free class-two nilpotent Lie algebra split into exactly one conjugacy class per possible \(+1\)-eigenspace dimension on the abelianization, with explicit Gaussian class-size and centralizer formulas. With fixed field order and growing generator rank, almost every order-dividing-two automorphism induces \(-I\) on the abelianization and fixes the commutator ideal, with an explicit exponentially small error bound.
