For the three-level diamond poset with \(b\ge2\) incomparable middle points, the unit group of its finite-field incidence algebra has exactly \(q(q-2)(q+1)^b+(2q)^b+(q-1)^{b+1}\) conjugacy classes. The extra term \((q-1)^{b+1}\), beyond the height-two square-zero comparator, detects the first nonabelian Heisenberg radical layer.
