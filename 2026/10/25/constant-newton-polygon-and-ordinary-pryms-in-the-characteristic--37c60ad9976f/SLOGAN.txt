Every smooth genus-three bielliptic Picard curve \(Y^3Z=X^4+aX^2Z^2+bZ^4\) in characteristic \(5\) has \(5\)-rank exactly \(2\), \(a\)-number \(1\), and Newton slopes \(0,0,\tfrac12,\tfrac12,1,1\). Its elliptic quotient is supersingular but its Prym abelian surface is ordinary, uniformly across the entire smooth one-parameter family.
