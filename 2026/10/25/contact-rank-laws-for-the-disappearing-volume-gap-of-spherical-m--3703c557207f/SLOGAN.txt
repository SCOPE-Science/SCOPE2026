For every origin-symmetric polytope, the volume missing from the sum of its dilate with a unit sphere vanishes at the exact exponent \((d+s)/2\), where \(s\) is the span dimension of its farthest vertices; a polar-body formula gives the sharp coefficient. For planar rhombi this produces an exact two-disk-lens threshold and a symmetry-driven jump from exponent \(3/2\) to exponent \(2\).
