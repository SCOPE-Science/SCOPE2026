For the Hamming cube, the geometric fixed-point space of any single-coordinate flip on its inclusive Vietoris–Rips complex is canonically the Vietoris–Rips complex of a cube one dimension smaller at a scale one unit lower. At scale four this identifies every fixed locus for \(n\ge6\) with a mixed wedge of four- and seven-spheres, using Feng's previously established sphere multiplicities.
