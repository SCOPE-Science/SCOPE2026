For every \(q\ge3\), the geometric fixed-point space of the first-two-coordinate swap on the Hamming-scale-two Rips complex of \([q]^3\) is \(\bigvee^{(q-1)^2(q^2-2)/2}S^2\). A uniform orbit-simplex decomposition explains these two-spheres as the surviving classes after explicit triangles kill the loops of a complete bipartite graph.
