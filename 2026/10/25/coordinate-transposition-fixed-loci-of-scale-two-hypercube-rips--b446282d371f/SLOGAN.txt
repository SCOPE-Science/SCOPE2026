A coordinate transposition of the binary Hamming cube creates a geometric fixed locus in the inclusive scale-two Vietoris--Rips complex with integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^{(n-3)2^{n-2}+1}S^2\vee\bigvee^{2c_{n-2}}S^3\), where \(c_m\) is the known scale-two hypercube three-sphere rank. Thus the fixed space exhibits exponentially many two-spheres and, for \(n\ge5\), mixed-dimensional sphere wedges even though the ambient scale-two complex has homology only in degree three.
