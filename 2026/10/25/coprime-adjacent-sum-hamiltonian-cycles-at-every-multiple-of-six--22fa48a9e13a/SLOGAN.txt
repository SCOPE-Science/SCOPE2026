Sun's adjacent-sum coprimality conjecture holds for every positive multiple of six: an explicit cyclic permutation of \(0,1,\ldots,n\) begins at \(0\), ends at \(n\), and has each neighboring sum coprime to both \(n-1\) and \(n+1\). A reflection lift of a cyclic two-or-three-step permutation proves the entire infinite family.
