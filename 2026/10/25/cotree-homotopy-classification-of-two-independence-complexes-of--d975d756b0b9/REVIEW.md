# Scientific review

## Correctness (PASS)
The face condition under disjoint union gives an exact simplicial join. Under graph join, any vertex set intersecting both parts with at least three members is connected on at least three members, leaving precisely the cross edges as mixed faces. Both two-independence complexes have complete one-skeleta, so attaching \(m(G_1)m(G_2)\) cross edges to two connected spaces creates exactly \(m(G_1)m(G_2)-1\) new independent loops. The usual join–smash equivalence computes sphere dimensions and multiplicities in the union case. A one-vertex base and cotree induction prove the homotopy statement for all finite cographs. Recomputed 240 random cotrees and one mixed-dimensional example agree with the direct mod-two chain computation; these are only finite consistency checks.

## Originality (PASS under available evidence)
The closest primary literature comprises [1] the 2026 cograph finite-generation theorem, which does not compute the full homotopy type or Betti polynomial; [2] the 2020 higher independence classification of complete multipartite graphs, which addresses a strict subclass; and [3] the 2025 Alexander-dual shellability and gap-free criteria on cographs, which do not state or imply the full integral homotopy formula for every cograph. Chordal-graph higher-independence classifications do not subsume cographs: the four-cycle is a cograph but not chordal. published-finding corpus and literature searches for the same object, shifted indexing, cotree recursions, direct homotopy, and stronger coverage did not locate an equivalent statement. Undiscovered prior work remains a residual risk.

## Value (PASS)
The result computes the complete integral homotopy type of a natural higher-independence construction for every graph in a major hereditary class, from its cotree without enumerating simplices. It gives a uniform torsion-free obstruction, exact graded Betti multiplicities, and the first explicit two-independence cotree mechanism; an example yields independent first- and third-degree homology, which the known complete multipartite equidimensional formula cannot capture. The two-dimensional face bound is structural, not an arbitrary cutoff.
