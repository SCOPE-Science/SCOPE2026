Every finite cograph has a two-independence complex homotopy equivalent to an explicitly computable wedge of spheres, with all integral Betti ranks given by an exact union/join cotree recursion. The complex for \((K_3\sqcup K_3)\vee K_3\) is \(S^3\vee\bigvee^{18}S^1\), demonstrating mixed-dimensional homology within the class.
