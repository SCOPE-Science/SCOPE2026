For a polynomially weighted countable-branch expanding interval map, forbidding consecutive returns to the same branch lowers Hausdorff dimension by a universal \(\zeta(2)(q-1)^3\) to leading order, regardless of how simultaneously vanishing geometric gaps are scaled. The complete dimension deficit splits asymptotically into leakage \((q-1)\eta\) and symbolic collision \((q-1)\zeta(2q)/\zeta(q)^2\).
