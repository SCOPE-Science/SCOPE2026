In a homogeneous local-Poisson-reset growth system with finite stationary variance, absolute cross-site covariance increases, stays exactly constant, or decreases under stronger redistribution according as \(m<r/2\), \(m=r/2\), or \(m>r/2\), even though Pearson correlation always increases. At the critical ratio \(m=r/2\), the covariance is exactly \(x_0^2/(N-1)\) for every positive redistribution rate, and the finite-system variance threshold has a sharp \(N^{-1/2}\) crossover.
