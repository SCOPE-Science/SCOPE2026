# Scientific review: Critical scaling of the finite-cell covariance lower-bound coefficient

**Correctness — PASS for the corrected final statement.** For fixed inverse temperature, the cell covariance Taylor calculation has leading term \(\beta h^3 E'^2\operatorname{sech}^2(\beta E)/12\), with summed uniform-in-cell \(O_{\beta}(M^{-4})\) remainder. The low-temperature coefficient is an independent, subsequent endpoint asymptotic, with \(K_z>0\) given by a convergent integral. In the original formulation, a suggested scaling of cell count as a **necessary** condition on the exact functional at fixed tolerance was not justified by either iterated limit. In fact, for the admissible \(E(k)=\pi-k\), at each fixed \(M\), \(S_M(\beta)\to\log 2/(2M)\) while \(C_E(\beta)\sim\pi\beta/12\), disproving such a uniform necessity. The corrected final statement retains the valid coefficient law, distinguishes a formal leading-term criterion from a joint-limit theorem, and supplies the exact boundary-layer counterexample. These changes are substantive and not merely a disclaimer.
