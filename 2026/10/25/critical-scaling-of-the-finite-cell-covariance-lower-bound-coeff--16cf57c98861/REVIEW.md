# Review: Critical scaling of the finite-cell covariance lower-bound coefficient

**Correctness — PASS.** The claim defines the functional, the regularity and endpoint hypotheses, the fixed-\(\beta\) limit, and the separate low-temperature limit. A cellwise Taylor expansion gives the coefficient \(\pi\beta^2/12\int E'^2\operatorname{sech}^2(\beta E)\); endpoint rescaling gives the constant \(K_z\). The included numerical checks corroborate but do not replace the proof.

**Originality — PASS.** The closest primary source is Shirai's arXiv:2510.13555v1, whose Eq. (42) gives the Ising-model covariance lower bound, whose main text reports only the \(M^{-2}\) order, and whose Appendix C treats the Ising critical exponent \(z=1\). The exact coefficient and the conditional classification for arbitrary endpoint order \(z\ge1\) are not stated in the inspected material. published-finding corpus searches returned related entropy and thermal-spin results, but not this functional or the \(K_z\) law. The novelty claim is limited to the stated asymptotic refinement and generalization, not to midpoint quadrature as a technique.

**Value — PASS.** The coefficient quantifies the low-temperature growth of the finite-cell lower-bound term and gives a necessary resolution scale for reducing that term below a target tolerance. The exponent \(1/z\) distinguishes linear critical dispersion from higher-order critical endpoints and provides a concrete comparison target for future integrable or multicritical thermal-preparation models.

**Closest literature and limits.** The primary source is arXiv:2510.13555v1 (first public date 15 October 2025), especially Eq. (42), the discussion of \(S_M\sim M^{-2}\), Appendix B's derivation of Eq. (42), and Appendix C's \(z=1\) critical behavior. published-finding corpus comparisons included “Sharp \(N^{-1}\) entropy decay for a dephasing mean-field Ising witness,” “Corrected magnetic-field phase diagram for the anisotropic XY–DM thermal concurrence,” “Two-scale low-temperature crossover in field-induced entanglement of a three-qubit XXX ring,” “Optimal-alphabet multicritical transitions from Gaussian quadrature,” and “Exact infinite-temperature Hessian of Gibbs mutual information”; these address different quantities or models.

**Residual risks.** Midpoint-covariance expansions and endpoint Laplace asymptotics are classical tools, so the contribution is the physically motivated coefficient/exponent classification for this specific lower-bound functional. Broader literature on analogous functionals for other critical models may exist beyond the searches recorded here. The theorem does not establish saturation of the physical bound for finite cell count or automatically transfer the bound to arbitrary Hamiltonians.
