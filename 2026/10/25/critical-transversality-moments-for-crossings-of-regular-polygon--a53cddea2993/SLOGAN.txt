A uniformly selected crossing pair of diagonals of a regular \(n\)-gon has an exact quadratic angular mass function. Its reciprocal-sine transversality moments have a sharp phase transition: bounded limits below order \(2\), growth \((12/\pi^2)\log n\) at order \(2\), and explicit zeta-constant times \(n^{q-2}\) above order \(2\).
