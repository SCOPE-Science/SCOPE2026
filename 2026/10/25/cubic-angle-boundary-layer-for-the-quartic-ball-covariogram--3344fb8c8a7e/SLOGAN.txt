For the planar unit quartic ball, the overlap-area correction changes from fifth order along a coordinate axis to third order along a fixed oblique direction. At angles \(\theta=\lambda t^3\) there is an explicit strictly increasing cubic-root integral crossover \(t^5F(\lambda)\), with \(F(0)=1/160\) and \(F(\lambda)\sim|\lambda|^{2/3}/4\).
