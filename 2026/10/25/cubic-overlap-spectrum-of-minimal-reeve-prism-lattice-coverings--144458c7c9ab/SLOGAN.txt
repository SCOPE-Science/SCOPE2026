For the eight-vertex minimal lattice coverings formed by Reeve tetrahedra thickened by a unit vertical segment, the entire covering-multiplicity distribution has an exact cubic-tail formula, with a parity-sensitive variance and a universal triangular limiting law. Although these lattice coverings remain inclusion-minimal, the proportion of each translate that is covered by no other translate is exactly \(4(3m-2)/(m^2(m+6))\), decaying as \(12/m^2\).
