For the canonical Euclidean Laplacian simplex of an \(n\)-vertex path, each endpoint has spherical normal-cone angle asymptotic to \(8\sqrt3\pi(\log n)/n^3\), whereas every interior vertex has angle asymptotic to \(1/n\), uniformly. A star Laplacian simplex instead concentrates asymptotically half the entire angular mass at its hub, despite the lattice equivalence of all tree Laplacian simplices.
