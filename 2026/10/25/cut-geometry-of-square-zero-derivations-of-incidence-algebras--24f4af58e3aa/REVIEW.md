# Scientific review: square-zero incidence-algebra derivations

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof explicitly derives innerness from primitive idempotents and the least element, establishes uniqueness modulo scalars, extracts the necessary matrix-unit products without a cancellation assumption, and constructs the down-set of support sources. The component, intersection, singular-locus and finite-field formulas follow from elementary coordinate cut geometry. The characteristic-two boundary is independently counted by a complete finite-variable case split. The standalone program computes actual double commutators in 15 complete finite examples, including nonchains and a genuine extension field; those experiments supplement, rather than substitute for, the proof.

**Originality — PASS (best-knowledge, not guaranteed priority).** Full-text comparisons with Xiao's derivation classification, Panyushev's abelian-Borel-ideal classification and Hu's strong-adjoint-nilpotence analysis separate prior statements from the present second-iterate-zero locus. In particular, the type-A rectangular abelian ideals and the all-derivations innerness theorem are already known and are credited; neither by itself asserts that every element with squared adjoint zero is supported on a single cut. A paper whose title mentions “square-zero derivations” was checked for the different condition it actually studies (constraints at square-zero input matrices). Searches also used the alias “sandwich elements” or “absolute zero divisors”. Earlier general treatments of structural matrix algebras and potential indirect corollaries remain residual risks.

**Value — PASS.** The problem asks for a complete description of differential structures \(D\circ D=0\) on a natural combinatorial algebra. The cut decomposition identifies its reduced algebraic moduli, gives precise irreducible components and singularities in the chain case, yields an all-dimensions finite-field census, and locates the first sharp characteristic-two failure. These are natural structural invariants rather than arbitrary small enumerations.

**Limits.** The theorem assumes a least element and characteristic different from \(2\). Scheme-theoretic nonreduced structure, exact large characteristic-two counts, and absolute literature priority are not established. The checked primary papers are not an exhaustive survey of every published or unpublished classification.
