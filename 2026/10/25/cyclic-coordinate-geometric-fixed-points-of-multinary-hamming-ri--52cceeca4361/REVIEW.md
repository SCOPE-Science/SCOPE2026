Direct Hamming-distance case analysis establishes that every maximal face belongs to one of the \(q\) disjoint simplices, aside from the cross-block edges. Relevant primary preprints on binary hypercube Rips complexes and on categorical complete-graph products likewise do not imply the geometric fixed-point classification. An earlier binary-alphabet cyclic-rotation result covers \(q=2\), which is explicitly excluded from the new claim. The relation between ambient homology and fixed-point topology is not an implication. Unindexed equivalent results remain possible.

The arbitrary-alphabet theorem determines a natural geometric fixed-point space and its complete integral alphabet-permutation module. ## Limitations

The graph is obtained by homotopy quotienting contractible simplex blocks; the full fixed simplicial complex need not be one-dimensional. The proof and finite checker have not received independent validation. The literature search does not establish absolute uniqueness.
