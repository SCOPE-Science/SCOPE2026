For every alphabet size \(q\ge3\), cyclic rotation of the three coordinates fixes a scale-two Hamming Vietoris–Rips space homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{(q-1)^2}S^1\). Its first integral homology is the natural cycle lattice of the complete bidirected alphabet multigraph, with \(S_q\)-character \(\sigma\mapsto(f(\sigma)-1)^2\).
