For every \(r\ge1\) and \(n\ge2\), the entire rotation character on the top rational homology of \(\operatorname{Ind}(C_{3r}\times K_n)\) is determined by a three-periodic transfer-matrix formula. The representation has a positive description using cyclically permuted \((n-1)\)-color words, and its invariant rank is a necklace count with a parity correction.
