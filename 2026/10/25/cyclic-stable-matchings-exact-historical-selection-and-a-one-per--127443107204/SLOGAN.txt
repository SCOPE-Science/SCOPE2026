In a cyclic strict marriage market of size \(n\ge3\), exactly two of its \(n\) stable matchings are reachable by any repeated entry/exit history restricted to terminal agents, so the reachable fraction is \(2/n\). One temporary historical man is both necessary and sufficient to reach the first otherwise-unreachable shift, and at \(n=3\) that same one-person augmentation restores all three stable outcomes.
