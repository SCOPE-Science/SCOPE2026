# Cyclic-subgroup fixed persistence of dodecahedral Rips complexes

## Finding
Let \(D\) be the dodecahedral graph, modeled as the generalized Petersen graph \(GP(10,2)\), and let \(K_r=\operatorname{VR}_{\le r}(D)\) be the inclusive Rips complex in graph distance, for real \(r\ge0\). For **every nontrivial cyclic subgroup** \(H\le\operatorname{Aut}(D)\), the geometric fixed space \(|K_r|^H\) has the homotopy type in the following exhaustive table. The types are constant on \(j\le r<j+1\) for integer \(j\) with \(0\le j<5\), and at \(r\ge5\) all are contractible.

Write \(P_a\) for a disjoint union of \(a\) contractible components, \(W_3=\bigvee^3 S^1\), and \(*\) for a point. An empty entry means an empty space. The count is the number of actual cyclic subgroups in this conjugacy class, not the number of group elements.

| Subgroup class | Count | \(r=0\) | \(r=1\) | \(r=2\) | \(r=3\) | \(r=4\) | \(r\ge5\) |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| rotation \(C_2\) | 15 | empty | \(P_2\) | \(P_2\) | \(S^1\) | \(S^3\) | \(*\) |
| central inversion \(C_2\) | 1 | empty | empty | empty | empty | empty | \(*\) |
| reflection \(C_2\) | 15 | \(P_4\) | \(P_4\) | \(S^1\) | \(S^1\) | \(S^5\) | \(*\) |
| rotation \(C_3\) | 10 | \(P_2\) | \(P_2\) | \(P_2\) | \(W_3\) | \(S^3\) | \(*\) |

Moreover, **all reduced integral homology persistence maps** are determined. Each table entry below lists the complete nonzero interval summands \(\mathbb Z_{[a,b)}\) in the indicated homological degree; multiplicities are written explicitly. Here the interval is supported at every real threshold \(r\) with \(a\le r<b\), with identity maps within the interval.

Thus involutions of the *same order* have sharply different geometric fixed persistence: a half-turn has a three-sphere stage, a reflection has a five-sphere stage, and central inversion has no fixed point until the filtration becomes contractible. These data are not determined by the ordinary homology representation of \(K_r\).

## Assumptions and scope
There are twenty vertices \(u_i,v_i\) indexed by \(i\in\mathbb Z/10\). The graph has edges \(u_iu_{i+1}\), \(u_iv_i\), and \(v_iv_{i+2}\), with indices modulo ten. Every edge has unit length and distance means the graph shortest-path distance (not an uncalibrated Euclidean threshold). A nonempty set of vertices spans a simplex of \(K_r\) precisely when all pairwise distances are at most \(r\). The full graph automorphism group is \(A_5\times C_2\), with the second factor generated by antipodal inversion. The seven subgroup classes shown exhaust the nontrivial *cyclic* subgroups; no classification of noncyclic-subgroup fixed sets is asserted. The table concerns geometric realization fixed points, which can lie at barycenters even without individually fixed original vertices. Homotopy equivalences in the table need not be PL homeomorphisms.

## Proof
**1. Exhaustive subgroup types.** The dodecahedral automorphism group is \(A_5\times C_2\). Within \(A_5\) there are fifteen order-two subgroups, ten order-three subgroups and six order-five subgroups. Multiplication by the central inversion yields the one central \(C_2\), fifteen reflection \(C_2\)'s (products of inversion with a half-turn), ten cyclic \(C_6\)'s and six cyclic \(C_{10}\)'s. The total is \(15+1+15+10+6+10+6=63\), arranged in seven conjugacy classes. This count and the action on the twenty graph vertices are independently certified by the exact standard-library enumerator described below.

**2. The orbit-simplex lemma.** For a finite group \(H\) acting simplicially on a finite complex \(K\), assign a coordinate \(t_v\ge0\) to each original vertex with \(\sum_v t_v=1\). A point is fixed by \(H\) if and only if \(t_{hv}=t_v\) for every \(h\in H\); hence its support is a union of vertex orbits. Such an invariant support is allowed precisely when its union is a simplex of \(K\). Mapping an admissible orbit vertex \(O\) to its uniform barycenter \(|O|^{-1}\sum_{v\in O}v\) gives a simplicial PL homeomorphism from the admissible orbit complex to \(|K|^H\). For the Rips complex, the orbit complex is flag: retain an orbit \(O\) if \(\max_{x,y\in O}d(x,y)\le r\), and join two retained orbit vertices \(O,O'\) if \(\max_{x\in O,y\in O'}d(x,y)\le r\). This reduction is *natural across the filtration*, with no assumption of a free action.

**3. Exact homotopy classification.** Apply the orbit-simplex lemma to the seven conjugacy-class representatives. Exhaustive shortest-path and orbit-distance comparisons produce the following complete critical face vectors (counts by increasing simplex dimension), with elementary collapse certificates for the nonspherical cases:

- Rotation \(C_2\) at \(r=3\): \((6,8,2)\), three valid elementary face-pair collapses, and a five-cycle. At \(r=4\): \((8,24,32,16)\), the flag complex of the complete four-partite graph with two vertices per part, namely \((S^0)^{*4}\cong S^3\).
- Reflection \(C_2\) at \(r=2\): \((10,18,8)\), eleven elementary collapses to a seven-cycle. At \(r=3\): \((10,30,30,10)\), thirty-three elementary collapses to a seven-cycle. At \(r=4\): \((12,60,160,240,192,64)\), the flag complex \((S^0)^{*6}\cong S^5\).
- Rotation \(C_3\) at \(r=3\): \((8,14,4)\), five elementary collapses to a connected graph with seven vertices and nine edges, so a wedge of three circles. At \(r=4\): \((8,24,32,16)\), the cross-polytope boundary \(S^3\).
- Rotation \(C_5\) at \(r=4\): \((4,4)\), the four-cycle \(S^1\).

For the smaller scales, the orbit complexes are empty, isolated vertices, or forests retracting onto exactly the recorded number of points. For all classes, at \(r\ge5\) every orbit is admissible and all orbit vertices span a simplex, since the graph diameter is five. Central inversion, and the order-six and order-ten cyclic subgroups, have no admissible orbit at any \(r<5\). The standalone verifier reconstructs the graph from its thirty edges, enumerates the entire automorphism group without external software, independently checks conjugacy classes, and tests the full classification on **all 63 actual cyclic subgroups at all six critical scales**, not just on sampled representatives. All collapse pairs are checked for freeness and maintain a simplicial subcomplex; the spherical identifications use the explicitly verified perfect matching of missing graph edges. Since every stage collapses to a graph or is a join of zero-spheres, the homology is free abelian, and there is no hidden torsion.

**4. Persistent maps.** Orbit barycenters identify the fixed filtration stagewise by inclusions of the same orbit-vertex families. The number and attachment of components give each \(\widetilde H_0\) interval. Every positive-degree group except reflection \(C_2\) in degree one appears at a single integer stage before disappearing in a higher-dimensional or contractible stage, so its interval is immediate from the table. For the reflection case, the inclusion from \(r=2\) to \(r=3\) is an integral isomorphism on \(H_1\): in the orbit ordering of the verifier, an oriented seven-edge generator of the collapsed \(r=2\) complex has vertex loop \((0,1,3,4,5,6,8)\); upon inclusion and the verified elementary-collapse chain maps at \(r=3\), it becomes the oriented seven-edge primitive cycle \((0,2,3,4,5,6,7)\), up to sign. The supplied verifier implements the exact integral signed chain substitution and confirms coefficient \(\pm1\), not merely nonzero reduction modulo a prime. Therefore the \(H_1\) interval is \([2,4)\). These checks give the stated complete reduced integral persistence decomposition.

## Relationship to prior work
Saleh, Titz Mite and Witzel, *Vietoris–Rips complexes of Platonic solids* (arXiv:2302.14388v1), determine the **ordinary** homotopy types of the dodecahedral Rips complexes and explain their symmetric exceptional tetrahedra at scale three. Their Section 4 and Remark 4.5 discuss the induced representation on ambient third homology, not the geometric fixed spaces for every cyclic subgroup and not the induced fixed-space persistence maps. A published integral-lattice refinement of the ambient scale-three representation (identifier `2026/10/11/SCOPE-integral-icosahedral-homology-lattice-of-the-dodecahedral-scale--88d743acf01d`) also does not determine these fixed spaces; its full `RESULT.md` explicitly excludes persistence over other scales and equivariant homotopy types. Adams, Lagoda, Moy, Sadovek and De Saha (arXiv:2408.17331v1) study equivariant cohomology of **continuous-circle metric thickenings**, a different metric space and invariant. The earlier cyclic-polygon fixed-filtration classification concerns the cycle graph, not its dodecahedral counterpart. No equivalence or reduction from those results gives the seven-class dodecahedral geometric-fixed persistence table. Published priority searches found no identical theorem, though negative searches alone cannot establish absolute uniqueness.

## Limitations
The classification is restricted to the graph metric on one canonical twenty-vertex solid and to nontrivial cyclic subgroups. The space-level table gives ordinary homotopy types of geometric fixed spaces, not an equivariant homotopy equivalence of ambient complexes or a PL homeomorphism to each listed sphere. The interval result is integral but does not compute Borel equivariant cohomology, restriction maps between different subgroups, or fixed spaces of noncyclic subgroups. Priority risk from unindexed equivariant polyhedral calculations remains.
