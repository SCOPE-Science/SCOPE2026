# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The claim is a finite, exhaustive classification of geometric fixed spaces under all 63 nontrivial cyclic subgroups of the 120-element dodecahedral automorphism group, with complete reduced integral persistence maps. The orbit-simplex lemma is proved directly from invariant barycentric coordinates. The separate standard-library checker generates every automorphism by exhaustive distance-preserving root-star extensions, partitions all cyclic subgroups into seven verified conjugacy classes, and enumerates all orbit complexes at every critical scale. Each nonspherical core follows by individually verified elementary collapses; spherical cores are exact joins of zero-spheres, so no higher torsion can hide. A signed integral cellular collapse map verifies the only nontrivial continuation \(H_1(r=2)\to H_1(r=3)\) is multiplication by \(\pm1\). The omitted real thresholds are constant between successive integer graph distances, and the last stage is the full simplex. No finite sample is being extrapolated to an infinite claim.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The closest directly overlapping primary full text is Saleh–Titz Mite–Witzel, arXiv:2302.14388v1. Its theorem and Section 4 address ordinary homotopy types and the ambient scale-three homology action, not geometric fixed spaces and their persistence for all cyclic subgroups. The closest public integral-homology research result for the same scale and graph was read in full (identifier `2026/10/11/SCOPE-integral-icosahedral-homology-lattice-of-the-dodecahedral-scale--88d743acf01d`); it explicitly excludes persistence over other scales and equivariant homotopy types. The continuous-circle Borel-cohomology result arXiv:2408.17331v1 has a different space and invariant. Both title-level and implication-level searches were performed, including subgroup aliases and fixed-filtration formulations. Exact equivalence not located. Residual risk is an unindexed or implicit geometric-fixed computation; non-detection is not a proof of universal novelty.

## Value — PASS
The seven-class table is a complete natural equivariant persistence invariant of a canonical Platonic metric graph. It sharply separates three inequivalent order-two actions: inversion stays fixed-point-free, half-turn rotations develop three-dimensional homology, and reflections develop fifth-dimensional homology, all before the same final contractible stage. The integral persistence map between the two reflection-fixed circle stages is nontrivial and has degree one, a feature not inferable from stagewise Betti numbers alone. This produces a reproducible finite benchmark for distinguishing geometric fixed persistence from ambient representation characters, not a renaming of the previously known nine-sphere result.
