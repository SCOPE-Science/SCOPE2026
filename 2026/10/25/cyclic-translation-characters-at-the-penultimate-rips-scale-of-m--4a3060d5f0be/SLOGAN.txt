For odd \(m\) and even \(2b\), penultimate-scale Rips homology of \(C_m\square C_{2b}\) is concentrated in one dimension, and the even-coordinate translation has an explicit integral action: a negative cyclic permutation on \((\mathbb Z^2)^{\otimes b}\) if \(3\mid m\), otherwise multiplication by \((-1)^b\). Its complete character is \((-1)^k2^{\gcd(b,k)}\) or \((-1)^{bk}\), respectively.
