The \(84\) perfect matchings of the Coxeter graph form \(42\) isolated pairs under eight-cycle switches, but ten-cycle switches connect all of them with diameter \(6\) and distance layers \((1,4,12,25,28,13,1)\). Every matching has one farthest partner, characterized exactly by a symmetric difference of two disjoint alternating cycles of lengths \(8\) and \(16\).
