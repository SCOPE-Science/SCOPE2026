For every finite hypergraph with no unsupported vertices, the first integral \(\Gamma\)-cubical homology is naturally the ordinary first simplicial homology of its hyperedge-generated closure, including under filtrations. In particular, a ten-triple triangulation of \(\mathbb{RP}^2\) gives genuine \(H_1^{\Gamma}\cong\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\) torsion.
