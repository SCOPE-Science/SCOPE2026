# Determinantal obstruction and four orbit types of smooth trilinear elliptic intersections over \(\mathbf F_2\)

## Finding
Let \(T=(\mathbf P^1_{\mathbf F_2})^3\), and let \(W\) range over the \(10795\) two-dimensional subspaces of \(H^0(T,\mathcal O_T(1,1,1))\). Exactly \(1512\) of the common zero schemes \(C_W\) are geometrically smooth curves. Their orbit sizes under the full polarized automorphism group \(G=(\mathrm{PGL}_2(\mathbf F_2)^3)\rtimes S_3\) are \(216,648,324,324\). Their respective numbers of rational points are \(3,5,4,4\). The two four-point orbits are distinct (indeed the full group computation separates them).

Under projection onto the first two factors, the smooth biquadratic images \(B\subset \mathbf P^1\times\mathbf P^1\) number exactly \(144\), by rational-point count:

| \(N=\#B(\mathbf F_2)\) | Smooth biquadratics | Number of trilinear pencil lifts per fixed biquadratic |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2 | 18 | 0 |
| 3 | 36 | 6 |
| 4 | 54 | 12 |
| 5 | 36 | 18 |

Consequently, exactly the eighteen two-point smooth biquadratics fail to admit a determinant presentation by a \(2\times2\) matrix of bilinear forms. The remaining 126 admit such a presentation. These claims concern embedded equations over \(\mathbf F_2\), not moduli classes of abstract curves.

## Assumptions and scope
Everything is over \(k=\mathbf F_2\); geometric smoothness means smoothness after extension to an algebraic closure. Each homogeneous trilinear form has eight coefficients. Its coefficient mask is \(\sum a_{ijk}2^{4i+2j+k}\). Each biquadratic has nine coefficients and mask \(\sum b_{ij}2^{3i+j}\). Subspaces are counted without ordered bases. Rational points mean \(k\)-rational points.

## Proof
1. **Determinantal projection and smoothness.** Write two generators \(F=F_0 z_0+F_1 z_1\), \(H=H_0 z_0+H_1 z_1\), where the coefficients are bilinear in the first two coordinate pairs. Their resultant \(B=F_0H_1+F_1H_0\) is of bidegree \((2,2)\). If \(B\) is geometrically smooth, the matrix has rank exactly one at each point of \(B\), since a rank-zero point would also annihilate all first derivatives of \(B\). Hence the unique kernel line determines the third coordinate, and projection induces an isomorphism \(C_W\simeq B\). Conversely, a smooth complete intersection of two ample divisors of type \((1,1,1)\) is connected and therefore integral. A rank-zero matrix point would create an entire vertical \(\mathbf P^1\) component, impossible for such a smooth integral curve. Thus projection is an isomorphism onto its image and \(B\) is smooth. Adjunction gives \(K_{C_W}\cong\mathcal O_{C_W}\), so the genus is one.

2. **Geometric classification of degree-two bundles.** A smooth curve \(B\) of bidegree \((2,2)\) is genus one. Over \(\mathbf F_2\), Hasse's bound gives at least one rational point. Its degree-two Picard torsor therefore has \(N=\#B(\mathbf F_2)\) rational points. The two projection bundles \(L_x,L_y\) are distinct; otherwise their maps differ by a target automorphism and their joint map could not embed a smooth bidegree-\((2,2)\) curve. Given any rational degree-two line bundle \(L_z\) distinct from both, the complete sections of these three line bundles define an embedding \(B\hookrightarrow (\mathbf P^1)^3\). The kernel of the trilinear restriction map has dimension two, by the basepoint-free pencil trick (or Bhargava–Ho, Lemma 6.8): the two trilinear equations form its complete intersection. There are \(N-2\) possible \(L_z\), and exactly \(\lvert\mathrm{PGL}_2(\mathbf F_2)\rvert=6\) choices of third-axis coordinates for each; distinct choices yield distinct embedded pencils, and every smooth pencil arises this way. Hence exactly \(6(N-2)\) pencils have a prescribed first-two-factor determinant \(B\).

3. **Finite exhaustive arithmetic census.** Enumerate all \(511\) nonzero biquadratics. On each of the four affine charts of \((\mathbf P^1)^2\), the ideal generated by the dehomogenized polynomial and its two partial derivatives is the unit ideal exactly when no geometric singular point exists on that chart. The executable certificate `enumerate.py` uses exact characteristic-two Gröbner bases, obtaining \(144\) geometrically smooth biquadratics, distributed by \(N=2,3,4,5\) as \(18,36,54,36\). Thus the formula from Step 2 gives \(36\cdot6+54\cdot12+36\cdot18=1512\). Independently, enumerate the \(10795\) reduced finite-field pencil representatives as triples \((F,H,F+H)\) with \(F,H\ne0\), testing each determinant's geometric smoothness. This reproduces the exact same collection of \(1512\) pencils and their rational-point distribution \(216,648,648\) for point counts \(3,4,5\).

4. **Complete symmetry-orbit census.** The full polarized automorphism group has \(6^3\cdot6=1296\) elements. Apply every element to each of the \(1512\) pencils, canonicalizing the unordered triple of nonzero members. The four resulting orbits and canonical representative coefficient triples are:

| Representative masks | Orbit size | Rational curve points |
|---|---:|---:|
| (22,129,151) | 216 | 3 |
| (22,131,149) | 648 | 5 |
| (22,139,157) | 324 | 4 |
| (24,107,115) | 324 | 4 |

Each orbit is closed under all the specified transformations; the four are disjoint and their sizes sum to 1512. The program `direct_jacobian.py` additionally computes the projective Jacobian condition on all eight affine charts directly for each representative, independently of the determinant test.

## Verification
Run `python enumerate.py`, `python direct_jacobian.py`, and `python biquadratic_orbits.py` with Python 3 and SymPy. `enumerate.py` enumerates all biquadratics and all trilinear pencils, and exhausts all group elements; `direct_jacobian.py` tests the four curve representatives by complete-intersection Jacobian ideals on eight charts; `biquadratic_orbits.py` independently classifies the smooth \((2,2)\) sections under the 72-element polarized automorphism group of the two-dimensional split quadric. Their expected summaries are recorded in `counts.txt`. Equality between the pencil census and the bundle formula is also checked for every one of the 144 smooth biquadratics in `counts.txt`.

## Relationship to prior work
Bhargava–Ho, *Coregular spaces and genus one curves* (2013), §6.2 and Lemma 6.8, already establish the general correspondence between genus-one curves with degree-two line bundles and multilinear models; this correspondence is **not claimed as new**. Bremner–Stavrou (2011), §4, classify small binary four-tensors under larger linear group actions, but their reported tensor-rank/orbit catalogue does not give this geometric smoothness and fixed-projection obstruction census. Tran (2019) treats invariants of genus-one models and determinantal representations over a different arithmetic regime. The contribution here is the fixed-field exact obstruction distribution, the four polarized projective orbit types, and a complete computational certificate tied to geometric smoothness.

## Limitations
The 1512 objects are equation pencils, not abstract isomorphism classes of elliptic curves. The group orbits are polarized ambient-coordinate orbits, not equivalence under arbitrary birational transformations. The exact arithmetic census is independently reproducible from the included programs but has not been independently audited. Earlier unpublished enumerations or interpretation of existing four-tensor orbit tables could overlap part of the claim; publication priority remains provisional.
