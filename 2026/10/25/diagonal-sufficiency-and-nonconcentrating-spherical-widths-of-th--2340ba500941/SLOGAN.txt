The White Whale zonotope has exponentially many Boolean-subset generators, but the leading random-direction Euclidean width, divided by \(2^d\), is asymptotically determined by a single diagonal projection. Its non-Gaussian limit is \(\frac12\mathbb E_G|Z+G|\), where \(Z,G\) are independent standard normals, with limiting variance \(1/12+(\sqrt3-2)/(2\pi)>0\); a sharp \(1/(8d)\) correction determines the spherical mean.
