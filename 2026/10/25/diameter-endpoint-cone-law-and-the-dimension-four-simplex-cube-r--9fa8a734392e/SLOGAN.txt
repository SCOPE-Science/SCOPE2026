For two uniform random interior points of any full-dimensional convex polytope in dimension \(d\ge3\), the probability of falling within \(\varepsilon\) of its diameter is asymptotic to an explicit sum of diametral-vertex tangent-cone volumes times \(\varepsilon^{2d}\). Among equal-diameter regular simplices and cubes the leading probabilities reverse their ordering precisely at dimension four: simplex first in dimension three, cube first from dimension four onward.
