## Value — PASS
The seven-cycle is the first cycle with two distinct distance types among independent pairs and independent triples, producing meaningful competing degree-two summands. The exact reflection trace distinguishes the trivial from reflection-sign isotypic multiplicities, which ordinary wedge decompositions and Betti numbers cannot distinguish. The result gives an infinite family parameterized by clique size and supplies the dimensions of symmetry-invariant homology, useful for equivariant comparisons and rational quotient homology. It is not proposed as a rebranding of nonequivariant ranks.
