For every \(q\ge2\), the complete rational \(D_7\)-homology character of \(\operatorname{Ind}(C_7[K_q])\) has degree-two traces \(7q(q-1)^2\), \(0\), and \(-(q-1)(q-2)\) at the identity, nontrivial rotations, and reflections; its degree-one class transforms by reflection sign. The result refines previously known nonequivariant polyhedral-join decompositions by determining the actual symmetry representation.
