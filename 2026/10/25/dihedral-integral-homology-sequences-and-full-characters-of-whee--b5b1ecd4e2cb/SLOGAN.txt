For every wheel \(W_{n+1}\) with \(n\ge3\), the ordinary matching complex has an explicit complete rational dihedral homology character; integrally, its top module is either the rim-cycle module, its tensor product with the spoke augmentation lattice, or a specified extension by an induced reflection-sign module. These representations refine the known sphere-wedge homotopy types without claiming an integral split in the extension case.
