# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The first statement relies explicitly on the published component-and-cover classification of Fujii–Iwamasa–Kimura–Nozaki–Suzuki (arXiv:2408.04802v1). The covering-coordinate identity \(p_f(a)(v)=f(v)+2a_v\pmod k\) and the positive diagonal deck generator yield a direct, all-parameters conjugation calculation under source and target automorphisms. The cycle-source component enumeration is established by the published winding/component classification of Čukić–Kozlov (arXiv:math/0408015). Exact rational transfer and an orientation-reversing reflection on winding-zero components yield the quotient Betti numbers. Finite combinatorial and cellular tests independently reproduce both signs and all small parameter cases tested. None of the finite computations is represented as a proof of the infinite family.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

The point-or-circle classification and the winding classification are prior results, not new claims. The new assertions are the natural **full integral** source-and-target automorphism representation on circle fundamental classes and its **all-parameter rational target-dihedral quotient Betti formula**. The closely related 2024 papers arXiv:2408.04802, arXiv:2412.19039 and arXiv:2412.19144 discuss universal coverings, but the inspected relevant theorems and arguments do not state these target-dihedral module/quotient consequences. Other published mathematical-result records concerning cycle Hom, dihedral representations of different complexes, and circle-coloring components likewise do not imply the exact formulas. Residual risk: the orientation computation is short once the covering is available, and an unindexed equivalent equivariant observation may exist.

## Value — PASS

An abstract classification as points and circles does not identify their natural orientation characters or the topology of quotients by color symmetries. The first theorem determines the integral symmetry action uniformly for every finite connected domain. The second turns this into a complete, exact, infinite two-parameter rational homology classification of a canonical and commonly considered symmetry quotient; it separates positive winding circles from the seemingly similar zero-winding circles, whose quotient rational first homology vanishes. These invariants are useful for symmetry-reduced graph coloring spaces rather than just a retelling of the Betti counts of the original spaces.

## Closest literature and limits

The proof depends on the covering and circle-component theorems cited in the result, while the signs, full module and quotient formulas are proved here. The result excludes four-color cycle targets, does not classify integral quotient torsion or simultaneous domain/target quotients, and has not undergone independent validation. Equivalent unindexed formulations cannot be completely ruled out.
