Three independent uniform-angle points on an ellipse form an acute Euclidean triangle with exact probability \(\frac14-\frac{3}{2\pi^2}\operatorname{Li}_2(((a^2-b^2)/(a^2+b^2))^2)\). The circle uniquely maximizes this probability, while a flattening ellipse exhibits a sharp thickness-squared logarithmic rarity law; the same numerical probability was previously known for a different circular point-containment experiment.
