For the coupled multitype inverse-Gaussian subordinator, a collection of coordinates can jump simultaneously exactly when a vertex of the directed excitation graph reaches every member; an exact nonnegative recursion computes each mixed cumulant. A six-type example has all three pairwise covariances of coordinates \(4,5,6\) equal to \(t/25>0\), but their joint third cumulant is zero and no jump affects all three at once.
