For a Lebesgue-uniform point in the intrinsic polar of the symmetric edge polytope of \(K_{p,q}\), the two within-part ranges and their residual oscillation slack, all divided by two, have the exact law \(\operatorname{Dirichlet}(p-1,q-1,2)\). At fixed total order, the slack distribution is independent of the bipartition; in proportional high-dimensional growth it has a universal gamma limit independent of the anticorrelated Gaussian fluctuations of the two ranges.
