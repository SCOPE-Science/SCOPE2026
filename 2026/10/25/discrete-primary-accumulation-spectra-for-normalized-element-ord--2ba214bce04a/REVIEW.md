## Originality — PASS (self-review)

The fixed-prime **accumulation set plus joint-moment rigidity** is the assessed claim. Dey–Mondal (arXiv:2208.05161, Lemma 2.3 and Theorem 1.4) supply an exact cyclic formula and recursive enumeration of finite abelian \(p\)-groups, which are expressly not new and form the essential prior foundation; their inspected results do not state a uniform infinite-sequence accumulation classification. Lazorec–Tărnăuceanu (arXiv:1911.10557) show global density allowing changing primes, and recognize the one-component cyclic limit; this does not imply the complete fixed-prime set across all abelian types. Chatterjee–Das–Poddar (arXiv:2610.11804) study global normalized power moments and density, but the inspected results do not state the fixed-prime joint rigidity. Amiri (2026) studies exact sums for finite groups of equal order, not this asymptotic limit spectrum. Actual mathematical-record searches and relevant historical claims were compared for fixed-prime, exponent-defect and limiting-spectrum equivalents. Residual risk: a previous paper or unstated corollary may contain an equivalent asymptotic formulation, since general exact enumerations could be adapted to obtain the limit with further argument.
