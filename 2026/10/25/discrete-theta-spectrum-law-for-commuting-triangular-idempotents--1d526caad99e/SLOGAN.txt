For a fixed finite field and a fixed number of commuting upper-triangular idempotents, the joint eigenspace multiplicities exhibit a residue-dependent discrete-theta limit with fluctuations of order one. The probability that they generate fewer than all \(2^k\) split factors has the sharp quadratic exponential rate \(-\log(q)/(2\cdot 2^k(2^k-1))\); the underlying color-count formula is acknowledged as closely related to known enumeration of triangular potent matrices.
