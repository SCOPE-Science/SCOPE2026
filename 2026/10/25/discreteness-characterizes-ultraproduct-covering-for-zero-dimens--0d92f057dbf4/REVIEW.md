# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The two directions were reconstructed from Definition 4.2. The discrete case is pointwise \(\mathbf{Set}\) and uses the ultrafilter finite-partition principle. In the nondiscrete case, clopen separation proves that every ultrafilter-large set of neighbourhoods has singleton intersection; nonisolation prevents a common open neighbourhood. Stalks commute with filtered colimits, but not generally with infinite products, and the proof explicitly uses the uniform neighbourhood required by equality of product-sheaf germs. This yields a genuine stalkwise failure of epimorphy, not merely a discrepancy of global sections.

## Originality
PASS within the inspected literature, with a substantial historical qualification. Kamsma--Tendas Definition 4.2 supplies the property and Example 9.9 covers Cantor space via varying subterminal sheaves. Kanalas' 2026 slides give still broader earlier obstructions: a real-line example and a two-point constant-sheaf/global-sections obstruction for Stone spaces from any infinite Boolean algebra. Neither inspected text states the iff criterion for **all** zero-dimensional Hausdorff spaces or its local witness at every nonisolated point, including arbitrary noncompact spaces. The new result extends rather than rediscovers those cases; previous generalities in sheaf-topos or locale terminology could reduce priority.

## Value
PASS. This supplies a complete, natural boundary for a categorical condition needed in the positive-logic version of the enriched Łos theorem, separating infinite discrete bases (positive) from all nondiscrete zero-dimensional Hausdorff bases (negative). The witness is uniform and minimal in the input objects: two copies of the terminal sheaf suffice, even when \(X\) has no countable local base. In particular, the precise transition is topological discreteness, not finiteness, compactness or cardinality. The result makes no claim that the wider enriched compactness hypotheses hold in every such topos.

## Sources and residual risk
Kamsma--Tendas, *The Compactness Theorem for Enriched Logic*, arXiv:2610.23278v1, Definition 4.2, Theorem 8.2, Theorem 9.7 and Example 9.9, were examined in primary full text. Kanalas, *Realizing types in topos-valued models* (2026), the full slides with Examples 1 and 2, was inspected by PDF page. Published records on Boolean-power orbits and first-order Boolean-algebra rank bounds concern other invariants, not the full comparison map. The nearest known constructions may already imply the compact Stone part; an unlocated general localic analogue is the principal originality risk. No independent audit was performed.
