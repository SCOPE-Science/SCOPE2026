# Review of the intrinsic-distance half-twist theorem

**Correctness — PASS.** The covering-strip metric gives exactly two candidate shortest deck-image classes, with transverse differences and sums. Uniform horizontal separation and the diamond law of the transverse sum/difference give the exact integral. A positive-measure contact configuration proves the strict range. Its \(\varepsilon^{3/2}\) leading term comes from a genuinely shrinking set of alternate-parity paths. The two local coefficients determine the geometric parameters. The accompanying code was run against independent direct minimization across multiple covering images and a numerical integral; numerical sampling is corroboration only, not an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS under accessible-literature comparisons.** Starostin and van der Heijden (2007) work on elastic Möbius-band equilibrium shapes; Sabitov (2007) studies flat Möbius immersions; Zhuang and Pan (2011) give ambient Euclidean random-distance laws in rhombi. None of those accessible statements implies the present two-surface stochastic law or the new onset exponent. An existing flat-torus pair-distance inverse statement concerns a different class, not a general theorem covering nonorientable strips. Full original texts of two historical Möbius works were not both retrievable; they remain a bibliographic risk, without any discovered stronger covering statement. Absence of a search hit is not proof of novelty.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** A complete, parameter-uniform and sharp topology-sensitive probability comparison is obtained for two canonical flat surfaces sharing local geometry, area and total boundary length. The exact \(3/2\) onset and inverse classification make the result stronger than a routine mean-distance computation. No claims for arbitrary curved ribbons or all compact surfaces are made.

**Closest literature and limitations.** DOI 10.1038/nmat1929 (2007-07-15), DOI 10.1070/IM2007v071n05ABEH002376 (2007), arXiv:1106.1257 (2011-06-07), Tangente's geodesic unfolding exposition (2016-04-25). Source-specific full-text availability limitations are recorded in the structured review; potential equivalent unindexed work remains unresolved. Mathematical scope is rectangular flat quotients and area-uniform two-point intrinsic distance only.
