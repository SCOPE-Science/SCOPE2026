For every circumference \(C>0\) and width \(H>0\), a flat Möbius band and a flat cylinder have identical random-pair intrinsic-distance distributions up to \(C/2\), but the Möbius distribution is strictly larger at every threshold between \(C/2\) and the cylinder diameter. Their CDF difference first appears as \(\frac{8}{3H\sqrt C}(r-C/2)^{3/2}\), and the complete distance law recovers both dimensions and distinguishes the half-twist.
