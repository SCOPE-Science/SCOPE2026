The four Burek–Cynk nonresolvable complete-intersection quotient examples have affine Weil class groups \((\mathbb Z/2)^2\), \((\mathbb Z/2)^2\), \((\mathbb Z/3)^2\), and \(\mathbb Z/2\), respectively. The 288-element example admits a quasi-étale double cover onto the 576-element example, with vanishing class pullback and deck involution acting by inversion on its three-torsion divisor classes.
