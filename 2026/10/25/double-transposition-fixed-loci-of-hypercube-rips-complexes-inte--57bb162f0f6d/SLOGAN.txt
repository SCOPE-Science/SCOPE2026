The simultaneous double-coordinate transposition has geometric scale-two hypercube Rips fixed loci \(S^1\), \(S^1\vee\bigvee^4S^2\), and \(S^1\vee\bigvee^{20}S^2\) in dimensions four, five, and six; in dimension seven the fixed locus has \(\pi_1\cong\mathbb Z\) and integral reduced Betti ranks \((1,68,4)\) in degrees one through three. Equivariant coordinate retractions preserve these nontrivial homology groups as split summands in all higher cube dimensions.
