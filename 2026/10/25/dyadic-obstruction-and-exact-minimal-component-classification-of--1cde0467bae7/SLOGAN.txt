For the one-letter \(\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z\)-extension of the Thue–Morse shift, the exact number of strictly ergodic minimal components is \(2^{\nu_2(q)}\); each component is conjugate to the Thue–Morse system times translation by \(2^{-1}\) modulo the odd part of \(q\). Despite nonminimality when \(q\) is even, Thue–Morse prefix sums are equidistributed modulo every \(q\).
