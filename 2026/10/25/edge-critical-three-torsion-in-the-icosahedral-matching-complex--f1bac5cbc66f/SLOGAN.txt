For every edge \(e=\{u,v\}\) of the icosahedral graph \(I\), deleting that edge changes its ordinary matching complex from a space with integral three-torsion into \(\mathcal M(I-e)\simeq\bigvee^{68}S^3\). The deletion-link connecting map is an injection \(\mathbb Z^6\to\mathbb Z^{68}\) whose image has saturation index exactly three, detecting the torsion created when the edge is restored.
