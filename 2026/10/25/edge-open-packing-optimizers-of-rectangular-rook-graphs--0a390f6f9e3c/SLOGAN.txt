For every rectangular rook graph \(K_p\square K_q\), \(2\le p\le q\), the maximum edge-open-packing size equals \(p\), except that an odd square board loses exactly one edge. Every maximizing packing is a disjoint assembly of horizontal segments, vertical segments, and two-edge bends, yielding closed factorial formulas for their exact labeled counts and a sharp parity obstruction.
