For the path edge-corona \(H_n=P_n\diamond K_1\), edge-resolving sets are exactly the covers of an explicit path-with-terminal-triangles auxiliary graph, with interior path vertices free. This gives a complete cardinality polynomial and \(\operatorname{edim}(H_n)=\lceil n/2\rceil+1\) for \(n\ge3\), correcting a published constant-value claim.
