The ordinary matching complex of the Tietze graph is a wedge of sixteen three-spheres, and exactly 63 induced eight-vertex octahedral spheres are its shortest possible nonzero third-homology carriers. Eight pairs exchanged by the central graph involution already form an integral basis, so its third homology is eight copies of the regular integral \(C_2\)-module and its even-plus-odd eigensublattice has quotient \((\mathbb Z/2)^8\).
