For the inclusive scale-four Vietoris–Rips complex on the seven-by-seven torus grid, every one of its \(392\) natural coordinate-dihedral symmetries has geometric fixed-point space of type \(S^3\), \(S^2\), \(S^1\), \(S^0\), or empty, with a complete explicit classification. The natural integral top-homology character is the product of the two coordinate-reflection signs; coordinate exchange introduces no additional sign.
