On every clique--path product \(K_m\square P_n\) with \(m\ge3\) and \(n\ge4\), the canonical cut projection reaches its endpoint norm on a central path edge, while the cycle projection switches its maximizing edge direction sharply at \(m=4\). For triangular clique strips, the larger of the two norms reverses with the parity of the path length; for all wider strips the cycle norm is strictly larger.
