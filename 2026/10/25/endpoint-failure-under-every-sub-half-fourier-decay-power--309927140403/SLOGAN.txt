For every \(0<\rho<1/2\), there is a compactly supported absolutely continuous probability measure \(\mu_\rho\) on \(\mathbb R\) with \(|\widehat{\mu_\rho}(\xi)|\lesssim_\rho(1+|\xi|)^{-\rho}\), yet its dyadic lacunary maximal operator is unbounded from \(H^1(\mathbb R)\) to \(L^{1,\infty}(\mathbb R)\).
