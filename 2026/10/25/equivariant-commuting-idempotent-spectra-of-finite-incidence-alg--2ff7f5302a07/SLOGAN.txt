Every ordered family of commuting idempotents in a finite poset incidence algebra has an equivariant counting law: automorphism-fixed families are weighted colorings of the vertex-orbit graph with \(2^t\) colors and one \(q\)-factor per bichromatic strict-pair orbit. The same colorings index the irreducible components of the reduced commuting-idempotent variety, with dimensions given by their bichromatic-orbit counts. For crowns, simultaneous rotations admit an exact totient-weighted cycle-transfer formula over every finite field.
