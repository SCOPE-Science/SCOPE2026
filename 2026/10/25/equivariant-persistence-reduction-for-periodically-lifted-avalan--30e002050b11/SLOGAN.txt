Repeating any nonnegative grain pattern around a consistently oriented cycle produces an avalanche filtration that equivariantly deformation-retracts, at every time, onto the shorter pattern's filtration; all integral persistent homology is therefore independent of the repetition factor. When the repeated binary pattern has exactly one zero per period of length \(k\), its final avalanche complex is \(S^{k-2}\), including the period-dividing case of a previously proposed sphere law.
