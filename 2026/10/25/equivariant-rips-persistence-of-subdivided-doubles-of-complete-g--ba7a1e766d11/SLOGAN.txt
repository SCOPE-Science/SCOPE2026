For every subdivided double of a complete graph, the rational graph-metric Rips barcode has explicit symmetry modules: a signed twin-flip graph-cycle representation at scale one and the exterior cube of the vertex-permutation representation at scale two. In particular, all \(3840\) automorphisms of the twenty-vertex Folkman graph have determined characters, with rational homology dimensions \(21\) and \(10\) at successive scales.
