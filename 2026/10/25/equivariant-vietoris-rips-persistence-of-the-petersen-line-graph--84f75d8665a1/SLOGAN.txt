The shortest-path Rips filtration of the fifteen-vertex Petersen line graph jumps from six circles at radius one to thirty-two four-spheres at radius two, then becomes contractible at radius three. The complete rational \(S_5\)-homology character is \((6,0,-2,0,0,0,1)\) in degree one and \((32,0,8,2,0,0,2)\) in degree four, where precisely two independent degree-four classes are invariant under all symmetries.
