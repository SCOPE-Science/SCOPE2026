# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The exact stopped-excursion renewal law follows from two verified killed-diffusion Laplace transforms and the derivative representation of Erlang survival. Large-barrier asymptotics use a finite-order derivative expansion with a uniquely dominant highest power of the barrier, giving exact constants and two different exponents. The geometric normalization, size-biased conditional law, and Gumbel centering are derived from that exact renewal law. Nine independently computed eigenfunction-series exit probabilities and high-precision sharp-tail/limit tests pass. The infinite limits rely on the analytic argument, not on finite computations.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risks.** The closest primary sources concern Poisson reset clocks (Guo–Yan–Chen 2023/2024), fixed observation windows (Singh–Pal), or generalized-clock first-passage time rather than maximum/count joint laws (Linn–Lawley). Existing renewal and extreme-value principles provide parts of the proof, but inspected results do not imply the multistage-specific polynomial tail coefficient and exact \((\ell-1)\log\log k\) conditional centering without the new model-specific calculation. Searches of mathematical research records and accessible earlier claims revealed only different extreme-value objects or generic reset renewal phenomena. An unindexed renewal-extremes calculation or an inaccessible recent splitting-probability paper might overlap, and this search cannot prove uniqueness.

**Value — PASS.** Staged resetting is the natural simplest non-Poisson clock family. The theorem gives an exact joint law for two simultaneously relevant search observables (spatial overreach and completed restart cost) and a genuinely different count-conditioned hazard scale depending on whether any reset has occurred. The order-dependent second-order Gumbel correction distinguishes multi-stage clocks that have the same leading spatial decay, making a quantitative extreme-risk and restart-budget benchmark beyond routine unconditioned mean calculations.

**Limits.** Independent identical Erlang clocks and fixed-location one-dimensional Brownian drift/diffusion only; noninteger shape, correlated clocks, and other underlying motions are unproved. All comparisons pertain to the exact stated model and specified conditionings.
