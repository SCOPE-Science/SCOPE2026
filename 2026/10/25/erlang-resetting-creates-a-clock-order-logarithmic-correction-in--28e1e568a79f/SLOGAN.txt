For Brownian search with Erlang renewal resetting, a single completed reset changes the pre-absorption spatial-maximum decay exponent from \(\kappa\) to \(\alpha\), and rare large excursions select a size-biased geometric reset count. Conditioning on \(k\) completed resets yields a Gumbel law with the clock-specific centering correction \((\ell-1)\log\log k\), for every integer Erlang order \(\ell\ge2\).
