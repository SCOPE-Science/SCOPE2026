The \((3,5)\)-geodesic chromatic number of the circular ladder \(C_n\square K_2\) is two for \(n=3,4\), three for \(n=5,6\), five only for \(n=9\), and four otherwise. Explicit finite colorings and a four-column pumping argument establish the infinite four-color plateau, while a finite isometric ladder obstruction proves the eventual lower bound.
