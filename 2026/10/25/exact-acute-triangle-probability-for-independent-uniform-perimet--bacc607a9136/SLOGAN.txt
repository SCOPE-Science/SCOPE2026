Three independent arclength-uniform points on an equilateral triangle's whole perimeter form an acute triangle with exact probability \(\frac{17}{3}\log(4/3)-\frac43=0.2968650772\ldots\), with collinear triples counted as nonacute. The mixed-edge component has the distinct exact probability \(4\log(4/3)-\frac56\); the one-per-side component was already known.
