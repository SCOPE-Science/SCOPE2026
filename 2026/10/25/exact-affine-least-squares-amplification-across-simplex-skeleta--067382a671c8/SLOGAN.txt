For uniformly face-balanced skeletal measures on a simplex, the sharp affine least-squares \(L^1\) and \(L^\infty\) projection norm is \(2B_{n,s}(1-\nu_{n,s})^s-1\), with \(B_{n,s}=(n-1)(s+1)/(2n-s-1)\) and \(\nu_{n,s}=(s-1)/((n-1)(s+1))\). The complete intermediate-skeleton profile has the nontrivial high-dimensional limit \(\|P_{n,s}\|/n\to2\alpha e^{-\alpha}/(2-\alpha)\) when \(s/n\to\alpha\in(0,1]\).
