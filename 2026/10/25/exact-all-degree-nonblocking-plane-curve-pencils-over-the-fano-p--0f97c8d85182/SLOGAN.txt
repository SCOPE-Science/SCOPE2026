For every degree \(d\ge3\), exactly \(287\,2^{2(\binom{d+2}{2}-7)}\) pencils of plane curves over \(\mathbf F_2\) have all three rational members nonblocking; their common rational base loci have size zero or one only. In degree three these 18,368 pencils split into 112 rational projective orbits, and 784 have all three members geometrically smooth.
