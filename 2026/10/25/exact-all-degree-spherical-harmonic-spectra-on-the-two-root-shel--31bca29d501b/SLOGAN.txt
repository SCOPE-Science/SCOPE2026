For every dimension \(n\ge3\) and every exact spherical-harmonic degree, the complete evaluation spectrum on all normalized short and long roots of type \(B_n\) reduces to scalar levels and explicit \(2\times2\) matrices. In particular, for every \(n\ge5\), the parity-compatible sampling rank drops from \(n^2\) in degree five to \(n^2-1\) in degree six, with no finite-dimension exceptions.
