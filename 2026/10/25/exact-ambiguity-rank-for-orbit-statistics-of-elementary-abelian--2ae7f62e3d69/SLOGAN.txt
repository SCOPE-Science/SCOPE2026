For \(G=(C_p)^r\), the joint statistics consisting of every subgroup's orbit count and every global orbit-size count have exactly \(\sum_{j=2}^{r}(\begin{bmatrix}r\\j\end{bmatrix}_p-1)\) independent rational ambiguities. A uniform collection of explicit integral relations between transitive \(G\)-sets spans the entire ambiguity space over \(\mathbb Q\).
