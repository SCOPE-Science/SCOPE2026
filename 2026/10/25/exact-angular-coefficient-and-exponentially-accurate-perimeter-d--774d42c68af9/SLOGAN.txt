For independent uniform-arclength boundary samples from a fixed convex polygon, the expected **perimeter** lost by their convex hull is \(L\sum_i h(\beta_i)/(N+1)\), up to an explicit exponentially small error, with \(h(\beta)=1-\cos^2(\beta/2)\operatorname{artanh}(\sin(\beta/2))/\sin(\beta/2)\). Thus the first-order perimeter shortfall depends only on total perimeter and exterior angles, in sharp contrast with the inverse-square area shortfall.
