For the Gaussian time-frequency localization operator, the rank-\(N\) Ky Fan eigenvalue sum has an exact Fourier-diagonal second variation under every smooth area-preserving angular deformation of a disk. The translation mode vanishes, while the elliptic mode has the strictly positive sharp coefficient \(\frac{e^{-s}s^{N-1}}{4(N-1)!}\frac{N+2s+1}{(N+s)(N+s+1)}\) for every \(N\ge1\) and \(s>0\).
