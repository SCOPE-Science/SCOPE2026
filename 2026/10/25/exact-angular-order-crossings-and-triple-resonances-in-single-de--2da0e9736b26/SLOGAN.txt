For a single fixed spherical-harmonic degree \(\ell\), consecutive angular-order cap-concentration levels cross exactly \(\ell-m-1\) times, at explicit positive Legendre-derivative zeros. Every consecutive triple resonance comes from a nodal zero of its middle order; there are \(\lfloor(\ell-1)^2/4\rfloor\) indexed triples and no four consecutive levels can coincide away from the hemisphere.
