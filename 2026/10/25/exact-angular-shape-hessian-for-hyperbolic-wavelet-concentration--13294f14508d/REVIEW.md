# Scientific review

## Correctness (PASS)
The normalized weighted Bergman monomials and incomplete-beta disk eigenvalues yield an isolated simple principal eigenvalue. The moving-domain operator is twice differentiable in trace norm; its Rayleigh–Schrödinger expansion gives the full shape Hessian including the eigenfunction-response term. The strict ordering follows from the explicit finite-binomial derivative identity, not from a numerical trend. Boundary cases \(a>0\), \(H>0\), and the zero first-harmonic curvature are checked. Finite-section matrix experiments reproduce the exact coefficient in a mixed-mode test; they are not presented as infinite-dimensional proof.

## Originality (PASS, with residual priority risk)
The 2022 Ramos–Tilli theorem proves global disk optimality but not the local shape Hessian. The full 2024 Gómez–Kalaj–Melentijević–Ramos text was compared at its Section 4.1: it differentiates a different nested optimization over analytic functions and maximizing level sets, obtaining functional coefficients \(W_k\), whereas the present formula differentiates the top eigenvalue at a perturbed fixed domain and contains inverse radial spectral gaps. A previously indexed Gaussian analogue uses a different weight and Euclidean constraint and does not cover the hyperbolic all-parameter result. No equivalent or strictly covering formulation was located, but historical priority cannot be absolutely guaranteed by searchable records.

## Value (PASS)
The exact Hessian determines local robustness of optimally concentrated analytic wavelets to geometrical mask errors. It identifies all infinitesimal degeneracies, the uniquely sharp first nontrivial angular mode, and a closed-form curvature for every positive Bergman weight and hyperbolic area. These are quantitative spectral facts not given by qualitative disk rigidity or by global estimates with nonoptimal coefficients.

## Limits
Only smooth radial perturbations with fixed positive parameters and the largest localization eigenvalue are treated. No independent validation or universal literature-priority certificate is asserted.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
