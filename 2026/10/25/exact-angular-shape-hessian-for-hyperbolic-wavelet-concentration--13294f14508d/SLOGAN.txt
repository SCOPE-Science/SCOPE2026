For equal-hyperbolic-area perturbations of a Cauchy-wavelet concentration disk, the optimized top Bergman localization eigenvalue has an exact angular-Fourier shape Hessian. The only null modes are infinitesimal hyperbolic translations, and the sharp transverse quadratic gap occurs at the second harmonic, with coefficient \(a(a+1)(1+H)^{-a-2}(2-u)/(2+(a+1)u)\).
