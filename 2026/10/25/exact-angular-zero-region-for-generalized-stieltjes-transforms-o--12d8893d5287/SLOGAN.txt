A positive mixture of \((z+t)^{-\lambda}\) over \(t\in[a,b]\) can vanish exactly when the segment \([-b,-a]\) subtends an angle at least \(\pi/\lambda\) at \(z\). Thus no zeros are possible for \(\lambda\le1\), while every admissible point for \(\lambda>1\) is realized by a two-atom positive measure.
