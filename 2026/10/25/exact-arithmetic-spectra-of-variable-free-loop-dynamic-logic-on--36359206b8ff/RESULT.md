# Exact arithmetic spectra of variable-free loop dynamic logic on directed cycles

## Finding

Let \(C_n\) be the directed cycle with \(n\ge 1\) vertices, a single directed successor program \(a\), its converse \(a^{-1}\), and no propositional-letter valuations. In full propositional dynamic logic with program union, composition, iteration, formula tests, Boolean negation, modal operators, and the exact-return operator \(\operatorname{loop}\), the family of all length spectra
\[
T_\varphi=\{n\ge1:C_n,0\models\varphi\}
\]
is **exactly** the Boolean algebra of subsets of the positive integers generated by singletons \(\{m\}\) and divisibility predicates \(D_d=\{n:d\mid n\}\), for positive integers \(m,d\).

Equivalently, for each formula \(\varphi\), effective integers \(N\ge0\) and \(M\ge1\) and a subset \(G\subseteq\{d:d\mid M\}\) exist such that
\[
 n>N\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\bigl(n\in T_\varphi\iff\gcd(n,M)\in G\bigr).
\]
Conversely, every such eventually greatest-common-divisor-invariant set is definable. The family is effectively closed under the full syntax, including nested arbitrary formula tests and negated loop assertions.

Every spectrum is ultimately periodic, but the converse fails: for example, the congruence class \(n\equiv1\pmod3\) is not a spectrum. Every infinite spectrum has strictly positive rational natural density; specifically the above presentation gives density
\[
\frac1M\sum_{d\in G}\operatorname{EulerPhi}(M/d),
\]
where \(\operatorname{EulerPhi}\) is the arithmetic Euler totient function. Thus infinite sets of natural density zero, such as square numbers and prime numbers, are not spectra.

## Assumptions and scope

A directed cycle \(C_n\) has vertices \(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z\), and the atomic program \(a\) maps \(x\) to \(x+1\). Its converse \(a^{-1}\) maps \(x\) to \(x-1\). For \(n=1\), both are self-loops. The syntax includes Boolean constants \(\top,\bot\), complement and conjunction of formulas; programs comprise \(a\), \(a^{-1}\), composition, union, Kleene star, and tests \(\theta?\) of any formula. Formula modalities \(\langle\pi\rangle\theta\) and \([\pi]\theta\) are standard; \(\operatorname{loop}(\pi)\) means the binary relation interpreted by \(\pi\) contains \((x,x)\). No program intersection, complement, global counting, named worlds, additional atomic programs, or propositional letters are assumed. The result concerns formula validity/truth **on the fixed unlabelled cycles**, not satisfiability over arbitrary relational models or formulas with freely chosen valuations.

Rotations are automorphisms, so every formula is either true everywhere or false everywhere on each \(C_n\), including formulas appearing inside programs as tests. The cycle truth condition is therefore unambiguous without choosing a distinguished world.

## Proof

**1. Arithmetic reduction of a test-free program.** After replacing every test by either the empty relation or the identity, a program denotes a regular language \(L\subseteq\{a,a^{-1}\}^*\) (the identity is the empty word, and the empty relation is the empty language). For a word \(w\), define its signed displacement
\[
\delta(w)=|w|_a-|w|_{a^{-1}}\in\mathbb Z.
\]
The action of \(w\) on \(C_n\) moves each vertex by \(\delta(w)\) modulo \(n\). Hence
\[
C_n\models\operatorname{loop}(L)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \exists w\in L\ \bigl(n\mid\delta(w)\bigr).
\]
The Parikh image of a regular language is effectively semilinear; applying the signed linear map yields an effective finite union of integer linear sets
\[
\delta(L)=\bigcup_{i=1}^t \bigl(b_i+\mathbb Nv_{i1}+\cdots+\mathbb Nv_{is_i}\bigr),
\]
where parameters may be negative or zero, and the generator set may be empty.

For every positive \(n\), the additive submonoid of \(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z\) generated by finitely many integers is the subgroup they generate: each negative residue of a generator is a nonnegative multiple of that generator because its order is finite. Therefore an integer linear component contains a multiple of \(n\) exactly when
\[
 \gcd(n,v_{i1},\ldots,v_{is_i})\mid b_i.
\]
For an empty generator set this means \(n\mid b_i\). Put \(d_i=\gcd(|v_{i1}|,\ldots,|v_{is_i}|)\). If \(d_i=0\), then \(n\mid b_i\): it is universal when \(b_i=0\) and otherwise a finite set of divisors of \(|b_i|\). If \(d_i>0\), then \(\gcd(n,d_i)\mid b_i\) is an effectively computable Boolean combination of prime-power divisibility predicates. In detail, for each prime \(p\mid d_i\) with \(v_p(b_i)<v_p(d_i)\), its condition is \(p^{v_p(b_i)+1}\nmid n\); when \(b_i=0\) no restrictions arise. Here for nonzero \(b_i\) the \(p\)-adic valuation is the usual nonnegative integer. Thus every loop test of a test-free program belongs to the asserted Boolean algebra.

**2. Nested tests and formulas.** Proceed simultaneously by structural induction on formulas and programs. Every subformula \(\theta\) has a globally constant truth value on any particular \(C_n\). Partition possible \(n\) by the finitely many truth values of all test subformulas of the program at hand. Each piece is in the same Boolean algebra by induction. On that piece, replace \(\theta?\) by identity if its subformula is true and by the empty relation otherwise. A fixed test-free regular language then describes the program. Its loop condition belongs to the algebra by Step 1; the disjunction across pieces does as well. Also, the property that a test-free program has **some** successful execution is constant in \(n\), because every path word can be traversed on every cycle. Thus the modal formula \(\langle\pi\rangle\theta\) equals the conjunction of the (uniform) truth of \(\theta\) and nonemptiness of the applicable test-free language, in each piece. Boxes follow by duality, and Boolean connectives preserve the algebra. This proves the upper inclusion, with an effective procedure. In particular, contrary to a tempting shortcut, merely discarding tests without splitting into their truth profiles would not be valid; the partition is essential.

**3. Expressing generators.** For each \(m\ge1\), the formula
\[
E_m=\operatorname{loop}(a^m)\land\bigwedge_{1\le j<m}\neg\operatorname{loop}(a^j)
\]
holds on \(C_n\) exactly when \(n=m\): the first conjunct requires \(n\mid m\), and the remainder excludes every proper divisor \(n<m\). Powers denote explicit finite compositions.

For a prime \(p\) and \(e\ge1\), let
\[
P_{p,e}=\neg\operatorname{loop}\bigl((a^{p^e})^*;a^{p^{e-1}}\bigr).
\]
The corresponding path displacements are \(p^{e-1}+tp^e\) with \(t\ge0\). A return exists exactly when \(\gcd(n,p^e)\mid p^{e-1}\), equivalently \(p^e\nmid n\). Hence \(P_{p,e}\) defines \(p^e\mid n\). Every \(d\mid n\) is a finite conjunction of the prime-power predicates, and Boolean combinations of these and \(E_m\) realize the full Boolean algebra.

**4. Equivalence of the arithmetic formulations and density.** A finite Boolean combination of singleton tests and \(d\mid n\) has, beyond the largest exceptional singleton, truth determined by \(\gcd(n,M)\), where \(M\) is the least common multiple of its finitely many divisibility parameters (take \(M=1\) if there are none). Conversely each condition \(\gcd(n,M)=d\), for \(d\mid M\), is a Boolean combination of predicates \(p^e\mid n\) comparing the \(p\)-adic exponents of \(n\) to those in \(d,M\); arbitrary finite exceptions are supplied by singletons. The residue classes modulo \(M\) having gcd \(d\) with \(M\) number \(\operatorname{EulerPhi}(M/d)\). Thus the density is the displayed rational number, positive whenever the spectrum is infinite.

For any \(M\), integers coprime to \(M\) occur in arbitrarily large numbers in both residues \(1\) and \(2\) modulo \(3\): if \(3\mid M\) use the Chinese remainder theorem on its prime-power factors and choose a coprime residue \(1\) or \(2\) modulo the \(3\)-power; if \(3\nmid M\), apply the Chinese remainder theorem to \(M\) and \(3\). Hence the predicate \(n\equiv1\pmod3\) is not eventually determined by \(\gcd(n,M)\), and therefore cannot be defined. This also shows explicitly why ultimate periodicity alone is insufficient.

## Verification

The companion standalone Python program `verify.py` checks, by finite residue-closure enumeration, the equivalence between nonnegative-semigroup reachability modulo \(n\) and the stated gcd criterion, including negative and zero displacements and empty generator sets. It also evaluates the prime-power and exact-cycle formulas over many small cycles. Execute using `python3 verify.py` (standard library only). Its observed terminal signal is `CYCLE_SPECTRA_ARITHMETIC_OK 18054 1600 4900 1584`. The mathematical argument, not finite extrapolation, establishes the result for every formula and every positive cycle length.

## Relationship to prior work

Bednarczyk and Swoboda study the finite-satisfiability problem of loop-enhanced propositional dynamic logic on arbitrary frames and obtain an ExpTime-complete result for a restricted negation-normal-form fragment, also with converse. Their methods address model existence and foldability, not the present fixed-frame cycle-length expressiveness classification. The classical PDL exposition in the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, §5.3, gives the semantics and classical expressiveness separations of looping and cyclic operators; it does not state this complete arithmetic spectrum theorem. Gargov and Passy study determinism and looping in combinatory PDL; their accessible discussion concerns global decidability and expressive features, without an identified exact cycle-length spectrum result. The semilinearity of regular-language Parikh images is classical and is used as a lemma, not claimed as new.

## Limitations

The formula must have no propositional letters, no state names and no additional program relations or program-intersection operator. In particular, cyclic words with arbitrary colour labels, several independently interpreted programs, and unrestricted finite-frame satisfiability are outside the claim. The effective construction via semilinearity is not presented as a tight computational-complexity bound. The historical priority of this exact restricted-spectrum theorem is qualified: an older specialized result, especially one on loop logic over permutation cycles, could subsume it. No independent scientific audit has been performed.
