The definition specifies a single unlabeled finite directed cycle, converse successor, full regular-program syntax with arbitrary formula tests, and the exact-return operator. The proof proceeds by a simultaneous induction on formulas and programs. The nonstandard critical step is the equivalence between a nonnegative signed linear set meeting \(n\mathbb Z\) and the divisibility condition on \(\gcd(n,v_1,\ldots,v_s)\); this is valid because finite cyclic additive semigroups are subgroups. Empty or zero generator sets and \(n=1\) are explicitly covered. Prime-power division witnesses and singleton-cycle witnesses prove the converse expressiveness direction. Finite residue checks replay the modular algebra; the full universal result rests on the written proof.
