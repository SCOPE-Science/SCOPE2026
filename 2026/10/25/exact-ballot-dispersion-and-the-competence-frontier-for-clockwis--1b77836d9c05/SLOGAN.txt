On a one-way delegation circle with independent direct-voting probability \(q\), the exact mean sum of squared guru weights is \(n[(2-q)/q-(2n-1+2/q)(1-q)^n]\), giving asymptotic vote-noise inflation \((2-q)/q\). When caster and noncaster information advantages are \(c_D/\sqrt n\) and \(c_A/\sqrt n\), delegation beats direct majority in limiting truth-tracking accuracy exactly when \(c_D(\sqrt{q/(2-q)}-q)>(1-q)c_A\).
