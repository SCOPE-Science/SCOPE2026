Among length-\(n\) binary necklaces with precisely three ones, sentinel-free circular BWT has exactly \(2\), \(4\) or \(6\) linear runs: the counts are \(1\), \(n-4+\mathbf1_{3\mid n}\), and the remaining necklaces, respectively, for every \(n\ge4\). Almost every such necklace has the maximum six runs as \(n\) increases.
