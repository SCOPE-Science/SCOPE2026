For the numerical semigroup generated by \(R_n+aR_{i-1}\), where \(\gcd(a,R_n)=1\), the exact ordinary catenary degree is \(a+b+1\), independent of the number of generators. The critical element \((a+b+1)R_n\) has exactly two factorizations, and \(g_2+bg_n=(a+b+1)g_1\) is its unique shortest generator trade.
