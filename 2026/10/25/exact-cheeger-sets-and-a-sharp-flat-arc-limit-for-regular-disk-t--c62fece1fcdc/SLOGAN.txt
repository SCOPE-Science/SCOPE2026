For every regular radius-\(R\) disk triangle of fixed minimum width \(w\), the Cheeger set has exactly six alternating circular arcs, with its radius determined by a unique explicit inner-parallel-area equation. The Cheeger constant approaches its equilateral-triangle limit \((3+\sqrt{\pi\sqrt3})/w\) with a sharp positive order-\(1/R\) deficit of coefficient \((H^2+6H-9)/(6H)\), where \(H=3+\sqrt{\pi\sqrt3}\).
