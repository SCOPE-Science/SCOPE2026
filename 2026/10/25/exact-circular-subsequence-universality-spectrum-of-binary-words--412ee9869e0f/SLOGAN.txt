For binary words, circular scattered-subsequence universality equals the maximum matching size of the cyclic graph of unequal adjacent letters. Its entire fixed-length distribution has the exact spectrum \(N_{n,k}=2^k\frac{n}{n-k}\binom{n-k}{k}-2\mathbf1_{n=2k}\) for \(1\le k\le\lfloor n/2\rfloor\), with two zero-index words, and its mean is \(n/3+O(n2^{-n})\).
