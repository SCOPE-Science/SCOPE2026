For homogeneous finite-field products \(R=(\mathbb F_q)^t\) with \(q\ge3\) and \(t\ge5\), the weak zero-divisor difference graph has an exact parity-dependent clique polynomial obtained by selecting all supports above half size, one vertex from each coatom class, and, in even dimension, one side of every complementary middle pair. Every maximum clique has this form, giving exactly \((q-1)^{t(t-1)}\) maximum cliques for odd \(t\), and \(2^{\binom{t}{t/2}/2}(q-1)^{t(t-1)}\) for even \(t\).
