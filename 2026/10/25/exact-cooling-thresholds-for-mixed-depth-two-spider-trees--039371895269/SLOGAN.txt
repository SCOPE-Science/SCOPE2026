The cooling number of a spider with \(a\) one-edge arms and \(b\) two-edge arms is exactly \(3\), \(4\), or \(5\), with five rounds precisely when \(b\ge4\) or \(b=3,a\ge2\). Within this mixed family the two minimal five-round configurations are \(T_{1,4}\) and \(T_{2,3}\); the latter marks a new sharp mixed-arm threshold beyond the previously studied equal-length case.
