For the symmetric scalar specialization of a recent fully decoupled thermo-poroelastic scheme, the exact stability criterion is \(hb>4d^2-2-2c\), so excessively strong coupling creates an anti-CFL regime in which decreasing the step size causes instability. The exact all-step and first-order-convergence boundary is \(2d^2\leq1+c\), strictly improving the sufficient parameter bound reported for this specialization.
