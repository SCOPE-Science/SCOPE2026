For one uniformly chosen direction, the widths of two congruent \(d\)-cubes have an exact covariance determined by the squared entries of their relative rotation. A uniform quantitative inequality makes Hadamard rotations optimally anticorrelated whenever they exist, reducing the variance of a paired mean-width estimator; non-Hadamard dimensions retain the exact covariance formula but need not attain the Hadamard lower bound.
