Four equal spherical caps centered at the vertices of a regular tetrahedron have an exact five-state coverage-multiplicity law for every angular radius \(0\le r\le\pi\). Pair overlaps emerge with exponent \(3/2\), while the last uncovered gaps and first triple overlaps exchange the identical sharp coefficient \(3\sqrt3/\pi\) at \(r=\arccos(1/3)\).
