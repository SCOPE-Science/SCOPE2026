For the quadratic Dickson map \(x\mapsto x^2-2\) modulo \(5^k\), every periodic vertex has one depth-one predecessor, but depth-two branches occur only above the fixed point \(2\) and one of two cycles at each even five-adic valuation level. Their numbers are exactly \(5^{\lfloor k/2\rfloor}\) at the fixed point and \(2\cdot5^{t/2}\) per vertex on the selected level-\(t\) cycle, giving the complete rooted functional graph for every \(k\ge1\).
