The two one-sided gradients on a regular facet are computed from the two nearest Euclidean lifts. The facet integral is evaluated analytically and summed once per opposite facet pair. The rectangular specialization uses the exact side lengths of the two Voronoi facets. The fixed-area extremum follows from the derivative sign on \(r>1\) and the symmetry \(h(r)=h(1/r)\). Vertices of the cut locus have zero one-dimensional Hausdorff measure. No independent audit or external validation is claimed.
