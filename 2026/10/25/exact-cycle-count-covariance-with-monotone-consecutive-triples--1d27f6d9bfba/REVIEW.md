# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Given three injective prescribed arrows of a uniform permutation, their nonclosed directed paths contract, and the expected cycle count is H_{n-3} plus the number of prescribed closed cycles. For each increasing triple the closed-cycle average is 3/(n-2); for each decreasing triple the fixed-point/two-cycle average is 3(n^2-3n+4)/(n(n-1)(n-2)). Subtract H_n, multiply by probability 1/6, sum n-2 positions. Thus Cov(C,U)=(3n-2)/(6n(n-1)) and Cov(C,V)=(10-3n)/(6n(n-1)), local covariances dividing by n-2, for every n>=3. Independent exhaustive rational n=3..7 checked all locations; not an infinite proof.

## originality

PASS

Prior related statistics treat individual inversion comparisons, distinct consecutive patterns, or other Stirling/Euler/Mahonian combinations, not the joint cycle count with an overlapping two-comparison event. The local conditional closed-cycle calculation is not implied by single inversion covariances.

## value

PASS

The number of cycles and length-three consecutive monotonicity are standard, independently motivated permutation statistics. Their exact cross-covariance exposes a sign asymmetry (decreasing triple turns negative at n>=4), positional invariance, and precise 1/n decay, all beyond known first moments or pairwise inversion covariances.
