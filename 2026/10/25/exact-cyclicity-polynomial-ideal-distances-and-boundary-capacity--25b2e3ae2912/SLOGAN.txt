For normalized monomial-circle zero sets on every nonball complex ellipsoid \(\Omega_p\), \(p>1\), the polynomial \(1-\lambda z_1^a z_2^b\) is cyclic in the exact Poletsky–Stessin Dirichlet-type space precisely when \(\beta\le3/2\). Its **unrestricted** degree-constrained polynomial-ideal error is an explicit inverse Gamma-weight sum, whose limit is exactly the reproducing-kernel capacity of its boundary zero circles, with sharp power and logarithmic convergence laws.
