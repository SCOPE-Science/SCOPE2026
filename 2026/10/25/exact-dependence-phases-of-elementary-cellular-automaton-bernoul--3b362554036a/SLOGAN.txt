The one-step output of any elementary binary cellular automaton from biased independent input is automatically two-dependent, but it is one-dependent for exactly 32 of the 256 rules at every interior bias and for 24 additional rules only at the fair bias \(p=1/2\). In contrast, independence of outputs two sites apart has seven exceptional bias values—including quadratic irrationals—and can conceal measurable dependence between separated output blocks.
