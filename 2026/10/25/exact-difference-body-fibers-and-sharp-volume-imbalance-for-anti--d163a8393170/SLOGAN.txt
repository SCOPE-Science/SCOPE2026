Every \(d\)-cube with two opposite vertex cuts of depths \(a,b\in[0,1]\) has exactly the same difference body as every other such cube with the same total depth \(a+b\), in all dimensions \(d\ge3\). Among these identical difference-body fibers, balanced cuts uniquely minimize the difference-body volume ratio at \(2^d\), and maximally unbalanced cuts uniquely maximize it.
