For three-stage, second-order RKL2 on a finite one-dimensional Dirichlet grid, the exact positivity cutoff inside the bulk stability interval is \(\frac{15-\sqrt{30}}{13}\) for two interior nodes and \(\frac{15-\sqrt{15}}{14}\) for three or more. Linear stability alone therefore does not guarantee a nonnegative update at a Dirichlet boundary.
