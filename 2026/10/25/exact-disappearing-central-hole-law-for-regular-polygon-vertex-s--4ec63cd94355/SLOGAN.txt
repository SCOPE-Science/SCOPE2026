For every odd regular \(n\)-gon, the part not covered by vertex translates of \(\lambda\) times the polygon, for \(1\le\lambda\le\sec(\pi/n)\), is exactly the interior of a reversed homothetic regular polygon. Its missing area is **exactly** \(n\tan(\pi/n)\) times the square of the remaining Hausdorff deficit, revealing a sharp quadratic disappearance that the previously known convexification threshold does not quantify.
