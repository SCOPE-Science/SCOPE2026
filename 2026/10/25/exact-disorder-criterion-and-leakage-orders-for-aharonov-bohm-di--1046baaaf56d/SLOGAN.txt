In a complex-weighted diamond chain, complete confinement of every hub state to its nearest two arm pairs holds exactly when both arms of every diamond have equal on-site energy and the two complex hub-to-hub path amplitudes cancel. This criterion permits arbitrary bounded cell-dependent disorder satisfying those local relations, while generic interference-breaking and arm-detuning perturbations first cause adjacent-hub transition probabilities of orders \(t^4\) and \(t^6\), respectively.
