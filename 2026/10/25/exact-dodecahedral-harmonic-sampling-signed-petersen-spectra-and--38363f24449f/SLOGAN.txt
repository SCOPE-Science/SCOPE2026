The 20 vertices of a regular dodecahedron have a complete three-sector exact-degree spherical-harmonic sampling spectrum governed by a signed Petersen matrix; their antipodally compatible sample space is fully attained at every degree at least \(15\), with isolated lower-degree losses. Across all degrees, the least strictly positive normalized Gram eigenvalue is exactly \(6188/1594323\), attained only at degree \(13\).
