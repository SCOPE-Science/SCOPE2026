In two separate depleted-prey Holling-II assays, a predator with weaker dilute-prey attack but shorter handling time consumes more by duration \(T\) precisely when initial prey exceeds \(N_c(T/q)/(1-e^{-T/q})\); otherwise the faster searcher consumes more. The threshold is sharp at every positive duration, and for initial prey above \(N_c\) the cumulative ranking reverses exactly once.
