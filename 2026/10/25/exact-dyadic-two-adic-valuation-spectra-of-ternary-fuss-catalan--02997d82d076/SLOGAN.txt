For ternary Fuss-Catalan numbers \(C_3(n)=\binom{3n}{n}/(2n+1)\), the entire two-adic valuation histogram on every interval \(0\le n<2^L\) has one explicit rational bivariate generating function. Its maximum valuation is exactly \(L\) with Fibonacci multiplicity, while a uniformly selected valuation satisfies a Gaussian limit with variance \(11L/12+O(1)\).
