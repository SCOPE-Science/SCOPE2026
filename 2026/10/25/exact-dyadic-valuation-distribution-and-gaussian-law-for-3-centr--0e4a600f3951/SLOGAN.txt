The complete \(2\)-adic valuation distribution of the \(3\)-central binomial coefficients over \(0\le n<4^L\) has an explicit two-state transfer matrix and rational bivariate generating function. The valuations have exact mean \(L-2/3+2/(3\cdot4^L)\), variance \(4L/3-14/9+2/4^L-4/(9\cdot16^L)\), and a Gaussian fluctuation law after scaling by \(\sqrt{4L/3}\).
