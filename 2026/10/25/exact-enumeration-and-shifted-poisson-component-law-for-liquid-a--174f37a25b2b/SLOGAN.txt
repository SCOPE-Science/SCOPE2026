For labeled transitive-delegation approval ballots, the exact exponential generating function is \(\exp((e^z-1)e^{T(z)})\) with \(T=ze^T\). A uniformly chosen *distinct* liquid profile has asymptotically \(1+\operatorname{Poisson}(e(e^{1/e}-1))\) connected components, whereas a uniformly chosen delegation function has vanishing probability of being connected.
