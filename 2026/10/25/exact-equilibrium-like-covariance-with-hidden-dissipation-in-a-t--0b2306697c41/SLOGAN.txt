At the unique finite switching rate \(\kappa=\sqrt{ab}\), a Brownian particle in two unequal positive harmonic traps has exactly the stationary covariance \(C(t)=(D/\kappa)e^{-\kappa t}\) of an equilibrium Ornstein–Uhlenbeck particle, yet positive and arbitrarily large joint entropy production. On that entire curve, the non-Gaussian excess kurtosis \(K\) determines dissipation exactly by \(\Sigma=3\kappa K/(9-K)\).
