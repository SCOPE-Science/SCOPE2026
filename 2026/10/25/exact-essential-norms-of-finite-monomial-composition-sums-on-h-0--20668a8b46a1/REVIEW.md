# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The associated weight is exactly \(v_\alpha\); the published boundary formula gives \(\|C_{z^m}\|_e=m^{-\alpha}\). The upper bound follows from subadditivity. For the lower bound, the explicit peaks are in \(H^0_{v_\alpha}\), are weakly null after finite summation at distinct boundary points, have norm tending to one, and force the full sum of boundary contributions against every compact perturbation.

## Originality
PASS on the inspected evidence. The 2022 survey covers single weighted composition operators, and the inspected 1999 theorem covers a single composition operator. The 2009 paper is a close two-term antecedent, while the claim is limited to \(N\ge3\) distinct monomial symbols. Targeted published-finding corpus searches returned no exact N-term match. The unread full text of the 2009 paper remains an explicit prior-art risk; no claim of global novelty is made from a failed search alone.

## Value
PASS. Exact essential-norm additivity for this finite family is stronger than the general triangle-inequality upper bound and yields a usable formula for compact-perturbation questions. The separated-boundary condition explains why no essential cancellation occurs for distinct powers.

## Closest literature and limitations
Bonet's 2022 survey supplies the exact single-operator boundary formula; Bonet–Domański–Lindström (1999) provide foundational essential-norm estimates; Wolf (2009) treats differences of weighted composition operators, with full-text scope unverified here. The theorem is only for \(H^0_{v_\alpha}(\mathbb D)\), \(v_\alpha(z)=(1-|z|)^\alpha\), \(\alpha>0\), finite \(N\ge3\), and distinct monomial symbols.
