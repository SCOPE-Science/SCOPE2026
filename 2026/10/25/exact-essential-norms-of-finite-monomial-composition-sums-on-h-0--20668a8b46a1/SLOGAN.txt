For distinct monomial symbols, the essential norm of \(\sum_{j=1}^N a_j C_{z^{m_j}}\) on \(H^0_{v_\alpha}\), where \(v_\alpha(z)=(1-|z|)^\alpha\), is exactly \(\sum_{j=1}^N |a_j|m_j^{-\alpha}\) for finite \(N\ge3\). Separated boundary peaks force exact additivity rather than only the triangle-inequality bound.
