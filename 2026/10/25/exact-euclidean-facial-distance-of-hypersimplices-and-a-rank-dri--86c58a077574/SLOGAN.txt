Every nontrivial hypersimplex \(\Delta_{k,n}\) has exact Euclidean facial distance \(\Phi=\sqrt{n/H(n,\min(k,n-k))}\), where the sharp denominator is piecewise quadratic and changes regime at \(n=4\min(k,n-k)\). The minimizing faces are edges at and above the balanced-rank threshold, but become higher-dimensional in the sparse-rank regime, with an exact parity-dependent tie classification.
