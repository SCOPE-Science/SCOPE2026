The composition operator permutes Fourier indices; weighted Hilbert-space normalization gives singular weights \(\theta^{|k|_1+|A^n k|_1}\), with no missing factor of two. Exact-integer enumeration for \(0\le n\le12\), plus candidate identities through time \(120\), corroborates the proof without substituting for an infinite argument. The selected Fourier characters attain the bound.
