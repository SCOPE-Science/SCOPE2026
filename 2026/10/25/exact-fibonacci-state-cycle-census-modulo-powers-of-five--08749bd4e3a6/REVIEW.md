# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof handles every \(k\ge1\), every state and every potential period. The exact identity \(B^2=5(B+I)\), the explicit \(C_1\) and its determinant, and the modulo-\(25\) induction prove that all lifted matrices have Smith valuations \((j,j+1)\) at the prime five. The resulting fixed-point counts separate into exact-period classes because \(3\) has order four modulo five. The zero state and the modulus-one boundary are treated explicitly. The standalone finite checker provides separate numerical corroboration for 406900 states, not a substitute for proof.

## Originality — PASS, subject to priority caveat

OEIS A015134 explicitly calls the all-exponent powers-of-five total a conjecture (Stephen Bartell, 20 August 2023). The author-available 2006 primary paper by Coleman et al. supplies a generic cycle class equation and the exceptional four-cycle, not a formula for the cycle count at every five-power nor a valuation description of its full spectrum. The full five-power histogram implies the proposed total, unlike the class equation alone. A related archived Fibonacci state-cycle result for modulus \(3\cdot2^k\) operates in a different local arithmetic setting and does not imply the \(5\)-adic conclusion. The remaining priority risk is an unlocated theorem that supplies equivalent Smith valuations or directly settles the listed conjecture.

## Value — PASS

The problem is a separately formulated arithmetic conjecture about the natural finite-state Fibonacci recurrence. The result settles that infinite-family prediction and strengthens it to a complete and uniform orbit-length distribution, with a structural explanation for ramification at five. This is not a finite table, a routine change of variables or a fixed-index computation.

## Limits of review

No independent mathematical audit or exhaustive priority determination has been performed. The exact arithmetic claims are proved for powers of five, not for general moduli. Relevant full-text results and their particular implications are compared without asserting that a search establishes uniqueness.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
