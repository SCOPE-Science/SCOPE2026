For the reciprocal-sum recursion with square parameter \(h^2\), an exact rational generating polynomial gives the density of every possible set of indices where \((2/h)a_n+b_n\) fails to be an integer square. The formula works for every positive \(h\), using finite-field first-zero hitting depths and an idempotent Chinese-remainder product that correctly accounts for simultaneous obstructions at the same index.
