# Review: exact finite-field idempotent count for odd dihedral quandle algebras

## Correctness — PASS

The hypotheses are explicit: odd \(n\ge3\), a finite coefficient field of order \(q\) coprime to \(n\), and zero included. Fourier inversion is valid because \(n\) is invertible; the nonassociative product gives exactly \(y_r=y_{2r}y_{-r}\). The nonzero-orbit equations imply both \(y_{2r}=y_r^2\) and \(y_{-r}=y_r^{-1}\). Their consistency is equivalent to a root-of-unity condition of order \(N_t\), whether or not \(-1\) belongs to the subgroup generated by \(2\) modulo the possibly composite modulus \(t\). Galois descent imposes one additional congruence per coset-cycle, yielding precisely \(g_t\) nonzero choices. The proof checks existence of those roots in the splitting field and thereby avoids overcounting. The finite examples were verified by direct coefficient enumeration, independently of the Fourier proof. Limits: the case of coefficient characteristic dividing \(n\) is not addressed.

## Originality — PASS against inspected sources

The closest antecedent is Elhamdadi–Ta–Virgin, arXiv:2609.03799 (2026), whose Theorem 3.2 and Corollary 3.3/Proposition 3.4 supply the complex Fourier multiplication identity. That Fourier reformulation is acknowledged, not presented as new. The new claim solves the orbit equations explicitly for **every odd composite or prime order** and enforces the extra finite-field Frobenius condition to obtain a numerical product for each \(q\). The 2020 Bardakov–Passi–Singh treatment establishes special \(R_3\) cases, including the already-known binary eight-idempotent case. Elhamdadi–Nunez–Singh (2022) considers quandle-ring knot invariants and small instances, and Narayanan–Panja (2022) concerns integral coefficients. None of the inspected accessible material states this all-odd-order finite-field divisor product. This finding does not assert novelty for the \(R_3\) case itself or for the Fourier identity. An equivalent formula under other terminology remains a genuine priority risk.

## Value — PASS

The number of idempotents of a quandle algebra is a natural algebraic invariant: each quandle basis element is idempotent and earlier work uses the full idempotent set for algebraic and knot-coloring constructions. A general count is not merely a new value for a selected small modulus. It distinguishes finite coefficient fields for the **same** quandle; for example, \(R_{11}\) has \(68\) idempotents over \(\mathbb F_2\) and \(24\) over \(\mathbb F_3\). The formula handles prime and composite orders uniformly, resolves the subtle case in which \(-1\notin\langle 2\rangle\) despite even doubling order, and provides a usable parameterization as well as cardinalities. The characteristic-coprimality boundary is mathematically necessary for this Fourier method. No speculative downstream theorem is needed for the value claim.

Material from arXiv:2608.01550 was available only in abstract-level form; this remains a specific access/priority risk, although its stated scope is automorphism groups and integral-domain idempotents. The separate previous characteristic-three \(R_3\) classification concerns a regime excluded by this theorem. Computational checks only cover finite examples and do not certify the infinite formula.
