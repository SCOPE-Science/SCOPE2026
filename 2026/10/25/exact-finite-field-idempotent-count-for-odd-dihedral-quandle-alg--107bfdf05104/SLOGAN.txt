All idempotents of every odd dihedral quandle algebra over a finite field of characteristic coprime to the quandle order admit a Fourier-orbit parametrization, giving an explicit divisor product of root-of-unity counts. The arithmetic matters: \(\mathbb F_2[R_{11}]\) has \(68\) idempotents, whereas \(\mathbb F_3[R_{11}]\) has \(24\).
