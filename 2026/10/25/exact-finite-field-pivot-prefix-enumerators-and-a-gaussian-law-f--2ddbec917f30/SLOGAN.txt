For every finite field, the count of invertible matrices with exactly \(b\) nonsingular leading principal corners is the coefficient \(q^{n^2}[z^n]F_q(z)^b\) of an explicit first-return series. For a uniformly chosen invertible matrix, that number of corners has asymptotically Gaussian fluctuations with an exact positive variance defined by a convergent finite-field Euler-product series.
