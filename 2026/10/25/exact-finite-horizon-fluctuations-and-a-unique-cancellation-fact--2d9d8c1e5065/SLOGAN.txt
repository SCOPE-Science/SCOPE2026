For stationary Brownian diffusion with Poissonian contraction factor \(q\), the quadratic loss accumulated at contraction jumps has a mean independent of \(q\), but its exact finite-time variance has a memory correction proportional to \(1-5q^2\). At the unique factor \(q=1/\sqrt5\), that variance becomes linear for every observation duration, while a long-time Gaussian limit coexists with infinite positive exponential moments at every finite positive duration.
