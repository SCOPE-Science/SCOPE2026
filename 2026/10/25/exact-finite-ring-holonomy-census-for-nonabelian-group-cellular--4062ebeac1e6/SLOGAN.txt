For every surjective one-dimensional cellular automaton over a power of a nonabelian finite simple group, a cycle-holonomy formula gives **all** temporal fixed-point counts on every periodic spatial ring, hence every primitive temporal orbit count. Finite-ring conjugacy to the untwisted coordinate permutation holds exactly when all finite-volume holonomies vanish—even though the corresponding infinite system is a full shift whenever every component has nonzero spatial drift.
