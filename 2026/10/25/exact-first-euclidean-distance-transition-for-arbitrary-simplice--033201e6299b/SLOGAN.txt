For every full-dimensional Euclidean simplex of dimension at least three, the two-point distance CDF is an exact polynomial until the inradius of the difference body, after which its first whole branch is an explicit sum of congruent spherical-cap integrals. The first correction has sharp exponent \((3d+1)/2\); for arbitrary tetrahedra it is an exact quintic times a linear factor, and its coefficient records the multiplicity of the shortest dual-face separations.
