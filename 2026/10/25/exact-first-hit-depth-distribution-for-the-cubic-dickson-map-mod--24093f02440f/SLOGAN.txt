For the cubic Dickson map \(x\mapsto x^3-3x\) modulo \(3^k\), every residue reaches exactly one of \(0,2,-2\), and a closed valuation formula gives its precise first-hit time. The complete depth distribution has an explicit finite-sum polynomial, with branch heights \(k-1\) versus \(\lfloor k/2\rfloor\) for \(k\ge2\).
