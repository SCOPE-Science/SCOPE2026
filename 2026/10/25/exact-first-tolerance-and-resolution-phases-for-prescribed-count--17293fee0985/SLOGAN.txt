For every fixed sliding-window length \(w\ge3\) and required exact GC count, the first positive tolerance \(1/k\) has a completely classified composite-DNA code space: mixed sites are separated by at least \(w\), giving an explicit hard-core polynomial and capacity root. The full resolution–tolerance crossover has exponent \(3-2\min\{\alpha,1\}\), independent of the window length and prescribed count, while zero tolerance has only one-third of the normalized high-resolution capacity.
