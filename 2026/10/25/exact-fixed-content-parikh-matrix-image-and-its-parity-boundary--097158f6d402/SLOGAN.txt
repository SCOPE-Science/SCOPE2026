For ternary words with exactly two smallest symbols, two largest symbols, and an arbitrary number \(m\) of middle symbols, the number of distinct Parikh matrices is exactly \(\frac{(m+1)(m+2)(2m+1)}2\). The complete image has a parity-restricted slice precisely when \(\binom{w}{ab}+\binom{w}{bc}=2m\), with interval-valued slices on both sides.
