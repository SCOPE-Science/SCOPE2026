For every wheel \(K_1\vee C_n\) with \(n\ge5\), all partial proper four-colourings that force a complete colouring are classified and counted by an explicit all-degree polynomial. The minimum seed size is \(\lceil n/2\rceil\), with exactly \(4I_{n,\lfloor n/2\rfloor}(2^{\lceil n/2\rceil}-3-(-1)^{\lceil n/2\rceil})\) labelled minimum assignments.
