The normalized associated-Legendre coefficient gives the stated central-binomial weight; the weights satisfy \(\gamma_{\ell,k}\sim2/(\pi\ell)\). The mode-two and mode-five terms detect exactly all degrees outside \(\{0,1,3\}\), yielding the claimed \(H^{-1/2}\) equivalence. Single-harmonic tests prove optimality of the exponent.

Its harmonic lemma supplies a qualitative zero-set argument but does not state the exact rotation-averaged energy formula, the central-binomial weights, or the sharp Sobolev stability scale. Searches for the exact averaged identity and for a quantitative threefold projection-symmetry stability result returned no matching statement. The qualitative classification itself is explicitly not claimed as new.

**Value — PASS.** The identity provides an exact, translation-invariant diagnostic for imperfect projection data and specifies precisely how noise in two Fourier modes controls the higher-harmonic defect. The sharp exponent identifies the strongest standard Sobolev scale available without extra regularity.

Closest literature: Myroshnychenko, Ryabogin, and Saroglou, arXiv:2609.27192. Limitation: the estimate is not a Hausdorff or uniform stability theorem.
