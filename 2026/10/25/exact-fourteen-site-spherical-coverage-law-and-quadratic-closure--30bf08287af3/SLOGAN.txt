For the fourteen equally radialized cube-and-octahedron directions, the Haar-uniform uncovered sphere fraction has an exact one-dimensional formula. Just below the previously known covering radius, the uncovered fraction is \(\frac{6}{\pi q^3(1-r)}\,\delta^2+o(\delta^2)\), where \(r=\sqrt3-1\), \(q=(1+r^2)^{-1/2}\), and \(\delta\) is the angular shortfall in radians.
