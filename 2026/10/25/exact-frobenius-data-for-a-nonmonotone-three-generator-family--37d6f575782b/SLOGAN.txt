The three-generator family \(\langle a,ha+d,h^2a+(h^2-h+1)d\rangle\), for \(h\geq3\), positive coprime offset \(d\), and \(a\geq(h-1)(h^2-h+1)\), has an explicit Apéry set, Frobenius number, genus, and complete pseudo-Frobenius set. Its type is one exactly when \(h^2-h+1\) divides \(a\), and otherwise two; the maximizing Frobenius branch undergoes an exact offset-dependent transition.
