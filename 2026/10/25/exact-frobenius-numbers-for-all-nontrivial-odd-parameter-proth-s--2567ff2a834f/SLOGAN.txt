For every standard Proth numerical semigroup with odd \(m\ge3\), the exact Frobenius number is \(M^2+(h+c-1)M+h+c+t-2\), where \(M=m2^n\), \(h=s_2(m)\), and \(c,t\) come from the tiny greedy Mersenne-coin expansion of \(h\). The formula includes all previously untreated cases in which several initial greedy coefficients vanish and identifies the unique maximal Apéry residue.
