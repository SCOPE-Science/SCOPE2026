The completed two-stage fourth-order Gauss–Legendre diffusion step on every connected noncomplete strongly regular graph is entrywise nonnegative for all nonnegative timesteps exactly when \(v-2k+\lambda-\mu\le0\); otherwise its unique sharp positivity limit is \((3+\sqrt{9+12(v-2k+\lambda-\mu)/\mu})/(v-2k+\lambda-\mu)\). Spectral stability holds at every step, even when edge transitions become negative beyond that limit.
