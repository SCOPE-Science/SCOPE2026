# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Direct Gaussian integration gives the positive Gram kernel. Below the known coherent-state threshold, a Gaussian generating basis completely diagonalizes it, and independent trace evaluations agree. At the threshold, exact Bargmann conjugation to a positive Gaussian multiplier proves boundedness and continuous spectrum; above it imaginary coherent states witness unboundedness. The verifier checks the critical coefficient identities and spectral sum, not the infinite-dimensional completeness proof.

**Originality — PASS, with risk.** The numerical threshold is NOT claimed new: Proposition 14 of Dong–Wang–Zhu (2026) already provides it for normalized coherent-state norms. Their Corollary 16 treats only the unitary boundary. Dong–Zhu (2025) gives the beta-zero canonical singular spectrum, reproduced here as a consistency check. Neither inspected theorem states the strict-regime operator-level sufficiency, full Gaussian-symbol singular values, or the critical multiplication model. A broader oscillator-semigroup formulation could overlap.

**Value — PASS.** The finding converts a model-specific kernel-testing boundary into a complete, quantitatively spectral compact/continuous/unbounded trichotomy and obtains every singular value on a natural radial family. It is a motivated infinite-dimensional Fock-operator invariant.

**Limitations.** Real strict parameters, one variable, Hilbert norm, radial Gaussian symbols; no conclusion for arbitrary symbols or complex parameters. Independent verification has not been performed.
