The explicit 81-element multiplication, its affine projection, and the determinant formula for the four-variable medial defect determine generation exactly. The key structural steps are proved: projected subquandles are affine subspaces; an affinely independent quadruple produces two points in one central fiber; three points in that fiber propagate to all fibers. Full-row-rank matrices count all ordered projected affine spanning tuples, and three lifts per point give the exact formula. The 2006 free-four-generator theorem makes the automorphism group order equal the number of generating quadruples. The bundled finite checker independently exhausts all \(81^3\) distributivity instances and \(27^4\) determinant instances. Its finite computation does not substitute for the unbounded integer-tuple counting argument.

## Value — PASS

The complete ordered generating-tuple distribution is a natural invariant of the minimal nonmedial commutative kei and yields its exact probability of random generation at every tuple length. Its affine-rank criterion recognizes generating tuples without computing closure of the 81-element multiplication. The same theorem gives a closed automorphism-group order for the free four-generator object, answering a concrete structural counting question adjacent to current quandle automorphism research. The principal value is not the already-known fact that four generators suffice.
