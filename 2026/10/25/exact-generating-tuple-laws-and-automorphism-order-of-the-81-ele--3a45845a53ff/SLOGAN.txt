For the classical 81-element nonmedial commutative kei \(D(1)\), an ordered tuple generates exactly when its projection to \(\mathbb F_3^3\) affinely spans that space. This yields \(G_k=3^{k+3}(3^{k-1}-1)(3^{k-1}-3)(3^{k-1}-9)\) generating ordered \(k\)-tuples for \(k\ge4\), and \(|\operatorname{Aut}(D(1))|=24\,564\,384\), using its previously known free four-generator description.
