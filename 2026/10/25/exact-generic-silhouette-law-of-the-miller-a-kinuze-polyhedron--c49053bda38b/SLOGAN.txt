The Miller–Aškinuze solid has a complete generic orthographic-shadow vertex law supported on exactly 10, 11, and 12, with an explicit three-piece spherical-angle formula and positive probability of 11-gons. Its uniform rhombicuboctahedral lookalike has only even-sided generic shadows, despite both solids having the identical expected silhouette count \(6+24\arctan(1/\sqrt2)/\pi\).
