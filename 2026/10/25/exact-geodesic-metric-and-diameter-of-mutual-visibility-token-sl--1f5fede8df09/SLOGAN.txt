For every connected mutual-visibility token-slide layer of \(K_{m,n}\) with \(m,n\ge3\), the distance between configurations is exactly the larger of their two partwise symmetric-difference sizes; every such layer is isometric in the unrestricted token graph. For \(0\le k\le m+n-3\), its diameter is exactly \(\min\{2k,2(m+n-k-1),\max(m,n)\}\), and the connected maximum layer has diameter three.
