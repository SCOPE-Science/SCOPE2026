For \(m\) uniform spacings, the normalized Gini and Greenwood statistics have exact correlation \(\tfrac12\sqrt{3(m+3)/(m+2)}\), which remains above \(\sqrt3/2\). Their positive dependence gives a closed finite-sample variance for the previously proposed ranked-spacing discrepancy and its sharp asymptotic null variance \(37/12\), instead of the value obtained by incorrectly treating the two statistics as independent.
