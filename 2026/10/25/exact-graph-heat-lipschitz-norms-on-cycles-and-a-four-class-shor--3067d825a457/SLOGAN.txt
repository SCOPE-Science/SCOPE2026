The exact Lipschitz norm of heat flow on every unweighted cycle with at least five vertices is the absolute deviation of its radial heat kernel from a time-independent median, attained by one fixed potential at every time. Its initial contraction defect has exact order \(t^{\lfloor(n+2)/4\rfloor}\) with coefficient \((4\lfloor(n+2)/4\rfloor+2-n)/\lfloor(n+2)/4\rfloor!\), exposing an arbitrarily high-order onset of strict contraction.
