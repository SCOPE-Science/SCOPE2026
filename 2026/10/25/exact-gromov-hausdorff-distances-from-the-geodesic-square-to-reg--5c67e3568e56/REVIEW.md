# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The claim is an exact identity for every integer \(k\ge1\), with an explicit metric normalization and no hidden genericity assumptions. For any correspondence, four fibers cover \(4k+2\) vertices and one has at least \(k+1\); a proved circular packing lemma forces a pair at distance at least \(k\) edge steps. The explicit surjective four-block correspondence has maximum distortion exactly \(k\) steps. The two estimates match in all cases, including cyclic wrap-around and \(k=1\). Tests with many finite values are supplemental, not the basis of the infinite theorem.

**Originality — PASS (best-knowledge comparison, not a global priority certificate).** Talipov's primary paper (arXiv:2210.09971v1, Theorems 3.1–3.3) gives formulas only for divisibility and source sizes two or three. Lim, Mémoli and Smith (arXiv:2105.00611v6, Appendix C) explicitly pose the general regular-polygon distance question and give results for consecutive cardinalities and for polygons versus the circle. The computed square-to-\(4k+2\) distances follow from neither. Exact-value searches and related indexed sources were checked; chord-metric hyperbolicity is a different invariant. A non-indexed publication could nevertheless preempt the result; not every later finite-metric source was exhaustively read.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Gromov–Hausdorff distances are natural metric invariants, and the value for the first non-simplex source polygon versus arbitrary even cardinality had a clearly delimited published gap. A sharp cyclic packing bound for arbitrary correspondences produces the exact complementary congruence class, completing even-cardinality targets for the geodesic square in combination with Talipov's theorem. The statement is not a general formula for all pairs of polygons, odd targets, or Euclidean chord metrics.

**References and remaining limitations.** Talipov, arXiv:2210.09971v1, https://arxiv.org/html/2210.09971v1; Lim–Mémoli–Smith, arXiv:2105.00611v6, https://arxiv.org/html/2105.00611v6. Absolute publication priority and independent proof validation are not claimed.
