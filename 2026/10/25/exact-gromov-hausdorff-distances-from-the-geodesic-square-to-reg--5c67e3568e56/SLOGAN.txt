For vertices of the regular \(4k+2\)-gon with inherited circle-geodesic distance, the exact Gromov–Hausdorff distance to the regular geodesic square is \(\pi k/(4k+2)\) for every integer \(k\ge1\). A pigeonhole packing obstruction matches the distortion of an explicit cyclic four-block correspondence, completing the even-cardinality square-to-polygon distances alongside the previously known divisible cases.
