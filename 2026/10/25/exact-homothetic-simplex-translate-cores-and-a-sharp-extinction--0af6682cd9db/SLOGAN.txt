For every simplex, intersections of homothetic-simplex translates that capture a fixed fraction \(\beta\) of its volume are exactly concentric simplices, with a universal nonemptiness threshold \(\beta=(d/(d+1))^d\). Testing bodies smaller than \(d/(d+1)\) times the simplex have no nonempty high-mass translate core at any admissible overlap level, while larger tests admit a completely explicit sharp shrinkage profile.
