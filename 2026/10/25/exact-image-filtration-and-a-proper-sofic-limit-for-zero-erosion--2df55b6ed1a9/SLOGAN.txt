For the binary zero-erosion cellular automaton with unique active neighborhood \(0^r1\), the exact \(n\)-step image forbids precisely \(0^r1^j0\) for \(1\le j\le n\), for every \(r\ge2\). These finite-time images are strictly descending shifts of finite type, yet their intersection is a proper sofic non-SFT limit set, with an exact backward-depth formula.
