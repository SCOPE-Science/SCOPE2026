For the ordinary matching complex of the fan graph \(F_n=K_1\vee P_n\), the complete integral homotopy type is a wedge of spheres whose dimension and multiplicity depend only on \(n\bmod3\): respectively \((q-1,q+1)\), \((q,q)\), and \((q,2q+2)\) for \(n=3q,3q+1,3q+2\). A hub-spoke cone decomposition proves the formula for every \(n\ge3\).
