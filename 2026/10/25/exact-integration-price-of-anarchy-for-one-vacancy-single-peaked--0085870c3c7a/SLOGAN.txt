For every one-vacancy cycle at the half peak, the exact integration price of anarchy in the single-peaked jump Schelling game is \(\min\{r+b,3b\}/(b+\lceil b/2\rceil+1)\) for \(r\ge b\ge1\) and \(r+b\ge3\); the two-agent triangle has price \(1\). The worst equilibrium is obtained by blue runs of length at most two separated by single red vertices, and the ratio approaches \(2\) for sufficiently imbalanced populations.
