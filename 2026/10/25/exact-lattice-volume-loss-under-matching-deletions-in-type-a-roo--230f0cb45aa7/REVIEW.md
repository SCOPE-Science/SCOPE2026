# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof gives an exact lattice basis, primitive-height cut planes, an explicit crosspolytopal cap, and an exact determinant. Disjointness of two removed caps follows from the complete-root support function, and cutting at these planes deletes precisely the intended root vertices. The full-type-A normalized volume follows by orthant integration. The resulting additive formula holds for every admissible parameter, not just those tested by the accompanying exact checker.

## Originality — PASS, subject to historical-priority risk

Higashitani–Mori (arXiv:2608.02445) characterizes Minkowski decomposable symmetric-edge polytopes; this is acknowledged prior art used for a qualitative corollary. D'Alì–Delucchi–Michałek (2022, doi:10.37236/10387) develops normalized volumes and general triangulation tools for other graph families; its inspected primary full text does not state an exact matching-deletion volume formula. The closest retrieved research records about edge deletions describe inball contacts rather than normalized volume. Equivalence and stronger-coverage searches did not identify an implication proving the exact additive formula, but this does not establish global priority against uninspected literature.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The formula quantifies how every matching edge deletion affects lattice volume, a directly motivated invariant in graph-derived lattice polytopes. The exact additive loss and its exponentially small ratio contrast with the immediate Minkowski indecomposability transition for all sufficiently large complete graphs. This is an infinite all-parameter classification, not a small finite data point.

## Limits

Not generalized to adjacent deleted edges, weighted roots, or full Ehrhart series. Computational checks supplement the exact proof and cannot certify originality. Independent validation has not been performed.
