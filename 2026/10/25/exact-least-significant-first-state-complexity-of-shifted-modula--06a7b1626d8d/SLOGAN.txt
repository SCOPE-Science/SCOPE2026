For integers \(b,m\ge2\), shifting the base-adic valuation-residue word by \(c\ge0\) costs exactly \(2m+\min\{\ell\ge0:b^\ell\ge c+1\}\) states in a minimal least-significant-digit-first automaton. Its entire state graph is a transient chain feeding a residue cycle, together with \(m\) constant states.
