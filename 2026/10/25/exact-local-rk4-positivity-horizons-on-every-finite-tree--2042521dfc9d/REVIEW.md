# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The distance-by-distance proof covers all finite simple unweighted trees and every nonnegative timestep. Exact entry formulas for distances one through four, a scalar sum-of-squares certificate on the diagonal, a negative discriminant at distance two and a strictly decreasing edge cubic jointly establish the necessary-and-sufficient horizon. The exact-matrix test ran on 1,696 trees and checked 50,631 unordered vertex-pair coefficients; these finite checks corroborate but do not replace the general proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
Searches of mathematical research records under tree heat kernels, classical RK4, Taylor transition polynomials, edge roots and distance-three thresholds identified no equivalent all-tree statement. Three primary full texts, including the 2001 and 2003 SSP linear-operator foundations and later positivity-preserving Laplacian integration work, were inspected. Earlier results for stars, paths, subdivided regular graphs and third-order RK on arbitrary trees are noncovering restrictions or different schemes. Specialized unindexed publications may still contain an equivalent theorem.

## Value — PASS
The formula classifies positivity for every finite tree using degree and neighbor-degree data in linear time, with a proof that only adjacent and distance-three transitions can fail and that no later positivity interval exists. The five-path and subdivided-star witnesses show both mechanisms are substantive.

## Limits
Finite simple unweighted trees; classical RK4 endpoint linear heat flow only, not nonlinear problems or internal-stage SSP. Sources inspected do not establish worldwide novelty. No independent mathematical audit has been performed.
