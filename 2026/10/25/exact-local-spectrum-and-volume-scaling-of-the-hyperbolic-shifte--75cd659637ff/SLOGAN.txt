For the volume-preserving shifted Gauss flow in \(\mathbb H^{n+1}\), every linearized shape mode of degree \(\ell\ge2\) decays at the exact rate \(\frac{\alpha}{n}(\coth R-1)^{\alpha-1}\operatorname{csch}^2R[\ell(\ell+n-1)-n]\). The sharp gap decreases with the sphere radius and is asymptotic to a constant times \(\operatorname{Vol}(B_R)^{-2\alpha/n}\) for large balls.
