For the two-type local swap Schelling game on a fully occupied cycle, the exact worst-equilibrium welfare is \(n-2r_*\), where \(r_*\) is the smaller color count or \(\lfloor n/3\rfloor\), whichever is least, minus one precisely in a sharp balanced divisibility-by-three exception. The resulting exact local price of anarchy closes the previously non-tight fixed-composition cycle bound.
