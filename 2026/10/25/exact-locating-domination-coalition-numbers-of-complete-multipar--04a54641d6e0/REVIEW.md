# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

LD sets are exactly the dominating sets omitting at most one per original multipartite part and at most one across singleton original parts. A coalition graph with >=4 nonempty non-LD blocks either has disjoint edges or a star, but a star center with >=3 partners is itself LD: disjoint partner coverage forces every omitted part count <=1 and omitted singletons <=1. Two disjoint LD unions force each original part to have size <=2 and s<=2. A fifth block leads to an LD partner contradiction. Constructed 3- and exceptional 4-block partitions establish the full equality characterization for N>=3.
