A one-line Haar-sketched Euler step for planar rotation has exactly i.i.d. complex circular multipliers: its logarithmic radius and phase have explicit equal variances \(\tfrac12\operatorname{Li}_2(t^2/(1+t^2))\), yet its radius grows exponentially almost surely despite every step preserving area. Correcting the mean projection bias by a factor of two yields the sharp fixed-time mean-square error \(\mathbb E|z_{\lfloor T/h\rfloor}-z(T_h)|^2\sim\omega^2T|z_0|^2 h\), exposing radial and phase diffusion in a conservative problem.
