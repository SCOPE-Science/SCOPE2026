For coherent, midpoint-separated trains of rectangular pulses, the frequency-truncated Wigner negative volume grows as \([2/\pi^2+8\sum_{j<k}|a_ja_k|/(\pi^3\sum_j|a_j|^2)]\log R+O(1)\), independently of pulse locations and relative phases. The sharp coefficient for \(N\) pulses is \(2/\pi^2+4(N-1)/\pi^3\), attained exactly when all amplitudes have equal magnitude.
