For every binary four-run word \(0^a1^b0^c1^d\), the exact minimum deletion cost of retaining a Lyndon scattered subsequence is \(\min\{b,\max(0,c-a+\mathbf{1}_{b\ge d})\}\). Its entire fixed-length distribution has a rational bivariate generating function, and the normalized optimal length converges to a law with mass \(1/2\) at one and density \(3(2x-1)^2\) on \((1/2,1)\).
