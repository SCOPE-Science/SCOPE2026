On a directed de Bruijn shift graph, independently naming any set of words lets two-sided counting refinement recover exactly which previously named alphabet letter occurs in each coordinate—and nothing about equality among the remaining letters. The stable colouring has exactly \((t+\mathbf 1_{\{q>t\}})^k\) classes, and for word length at least two it matches marked automorphism orbits precisely when the named words form a determining set.
