On the incidence graph of every nontrivial symmetric block design, the sharp maximum-coordinate amplification of the gradient and circulation Hodge projections has an exact formula depending only on \((v,k,\lambda)\). A universal crossover occurs at block size four: gradients are less stable below it, while circulations are less stable from it onward; projective-plane examples approach the respective limits \(5\) and \(6\).
