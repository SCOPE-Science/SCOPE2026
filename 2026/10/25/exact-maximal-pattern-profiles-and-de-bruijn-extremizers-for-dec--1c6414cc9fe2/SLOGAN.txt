A periodically decorated irrational Sturmian word has an exact maximal-pattern profile \(p_y^*(k)=2k+C_{c,k}\), with the correction determined solely by distinct finite signatures of its decorative residues. Among period-\(m\) decorations over a disjoint \(q\)-letter alphabet, the sharp excess is \(\min\{q^k,m-s_q(m)\}\), attained by an explicit de Bruijn necklace.
