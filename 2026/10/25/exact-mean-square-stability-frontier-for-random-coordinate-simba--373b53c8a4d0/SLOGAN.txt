For random-coordinate, rank-one Simba momentum near a strongly convex quadratic minimizer, the exact local mean-square stability ceiling is \(\eta\mu/\sqrt m<2(1-\beta^2)/(1+\beta-2\beta\ell/n)\). Random coordinate omissions strictly shrink the stability window for any positive momentum, and make its ceiling collapse as momentum approaches one even while full-coordinate stability stays finite.
