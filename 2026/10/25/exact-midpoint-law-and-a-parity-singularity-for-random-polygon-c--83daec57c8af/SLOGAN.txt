For two independent arclength-uniform points on the boundary of any convex polygon, their chord midpoint has a complete explicit law: a piecewise-constant planar density plus one-dimensional singular components exactly on polygon edges and the midpoint lines of parallel edge pairs. The total singular probability for a regular \(n\)-gon is exactly \(1/n\) for odd \(n\) and \(2/n\) for even \(n\); for centrally symmetric \(n\)-gons it is at least \(2/n\), with equality precisely for equilateral polygons.
