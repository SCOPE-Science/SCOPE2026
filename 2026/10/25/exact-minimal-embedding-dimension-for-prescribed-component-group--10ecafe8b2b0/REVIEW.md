Thus pi_0 Aut embeds into S_r, yielding n>=mu(Gamma). Conversely for faithful Gamma<=S_n put v=(1,..,n), s=n(n+1)/2, N=s+1 and I=(x_i^N, x^(sigma v), sigma in Gamma). ## originality

PASS

Díaz–Lucchini Arteche–Manzano-Flores (2026) establish arbitrary finite groups can occur as component groups and a solvable split identity part using Frucht graphs, but do not state optimal embedding dimension mu(Gamma) or give this orbit-monomial minimal-dimension construction. Their strong structural theorem is explicitly used for the lower bound; group-theoretic minimal permutation degree itself is known, not claimed newly computed. A different monomial-aut group reconstruction paper discusses recovery of algebras from connected groups, not dimension minimization. Qualified priority remains for independent minimal-variable constructions.
