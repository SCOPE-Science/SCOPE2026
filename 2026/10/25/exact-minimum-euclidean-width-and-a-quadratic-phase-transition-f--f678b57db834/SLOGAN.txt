For every non-simplex hypersimplex \(\Delta_{n,k}\), intrinsic Euclidean minimum width is exactly \(\min\{\sqrt{n/(n-1)},\,\min(k,n-k)\sqrt{n/(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\lceil n/2\rceil)}\}\). The only shortest-width directions switch sharply from a singleton coordinate partition to balanced partitions at \(n=4\min(k,n-k)^2-1\), where both coexist.
