The minimum solid angle of a coordinate-right simplex occurs exactly at the endpoint of a longest orthogonal leg, with an exact integral formula for every vertex angle. In a one-long-leg degeneration the known universal fullness-to-solid-angle bound is asymptotically sharp, and its first nonzero relative deficit has the explicit positive coefficient \(\frac n2\max_j b_j^2-\frac{1}{n+1}\sum_jb_j^2\).
