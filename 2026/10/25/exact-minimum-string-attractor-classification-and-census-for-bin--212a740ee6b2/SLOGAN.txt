For every binary word with exactly four maximal constant runs, the minimum string-attractor size is exactly two or three, with two explicit and disjoint parameter regimes characterizing when three is necessary. The length generating function for four-run words requiring three positions is \(2(z^6+z^8)/((1-z)^2(1-z^2)^2)\), so asymptotically half of all four-run words require three positions.
