For three independent arclength-uniform points on a circle, the minimum width of their inscribed triangle has an exact one-dimensional distribution, with mean \(5/\pi^2\) and second moment \(1-47/(8\pi^2)\) on the unit circle. Its near-zero tail is \(\frac{3t}{\pi^2}(\log(8/t)+1)+o(t)\), whereas near the equilateral maximum \(3/2\) its survival probability is asymptotic to \(12\varepsilon^2/\pi^2\).
