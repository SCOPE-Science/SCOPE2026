Brandenberg–von Dichter–González Merino, arXiv:2006.07259, provides a general three-contact criterion and golden-ratio symmetrization results, but not the evaluated quadrilateral LP. Related mathematical records with exact *other* quadrilateral invariants (Makai product and centroid-chord asymmetry) and Minkowski asymmetry of special hexagons do not logically establish the present optimizing containment value. A preceding general vertex-convexification lower bound is strictly below this asymmetry when the two ratios differ, and thus cannot imply it. Classical or unindexed quadrilateral treatments remain a priority risk; searchable-source absence alone is not a novelty certificate.
