Every nondegenerate convex quadrilateral has a unique point-inversion Minkowski center and exact asymmetry \(s(Q)=1+(1-pq)/(1+\min\{p,q\})\), where \(p,q\) are its two shorter-to-longer diagonal-intersection ratios. This affine classification realizes exactly the range \([1,2)\), with equality \(s=1\) only for parallelograms.
