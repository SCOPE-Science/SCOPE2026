Ternary Pascal-row integers are always congruent to \(0\) or \(1\) modulo \(4\): residue \(1\) occurs exactly for base-three indices using only \(0\) and \(2\) with an even number of \(2\)s. There are exactly \(2^{k-1}\) such indices below \(3^k\), and an exact signed-binomial-residue identity explains the congruence.
