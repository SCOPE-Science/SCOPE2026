For nondegenerate rank-one evolution algebras over \(\mathbb F_2\), all natural-basis graphs are exactly complete-out digraphs whose target-set sizes form either the singleton \(\{n\}\), all positive odd sizes below \(n\), or all positive even sizes below \(n\). A family of such graphs is simultaneously realizable within this class precisely when every target set has size \(n\), or all target-set sizes are below \(n\) and have the same parity.
