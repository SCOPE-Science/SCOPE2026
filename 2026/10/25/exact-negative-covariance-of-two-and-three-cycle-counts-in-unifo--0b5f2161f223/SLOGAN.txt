In an edge-labelled uniform attachment multigraph, the numbers of parallel-edge pairs and triangles have strictly negative covariance for every \(n\ge3\), exactly \(-m^2(m-1)\sum_{q=2}^{n-1}(q-1)/q^3\). For fixed \(m\), this tends to the nonzero negative constant \(-m^2(m-1)(\zeta(2)-\zeta(3))\), exposing finite second-order dependence beneath the known asymptotically independent cycle-count laws.
