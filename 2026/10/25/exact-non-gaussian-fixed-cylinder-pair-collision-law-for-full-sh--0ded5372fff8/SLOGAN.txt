At any fixed symbolic resolution, the \(q\)-ary Bernoulli shift has an explicitly weighted, non-Gaussian chi-square limit for orbit pair-collisions, with a completely determined overlapping-word covariance spectrum. Its collision-count variance is asymptotic to \(\frac{(q+1)q^k-(q+1)-2k(q-1)}{2(q-1)q^{2k}}N^2\) for all fixed integers \(q\ge2\), \(k\ge1\).
