For planar zonogons formed by \(n\) independent, uniformly oriented unit segments, the isoperimetric deficit from the perimeter-matched disk, divided by \(n\), converges to an explicit non-Gaussian sum of independent exponentials. An exact nonnegative Fourier certificate yields its finite-sample moments, elementary Laplace transform, and sharp upper tail \(\Pr(T>t)\sim16e^{-3\pi t/2}/(3\pi)\).
