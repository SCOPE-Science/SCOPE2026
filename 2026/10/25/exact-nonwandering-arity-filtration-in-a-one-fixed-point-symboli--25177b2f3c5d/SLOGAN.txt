For the countable symbolic homeomorphism with exactly one nonwandering fixed point constructed by Đorić, every finite configuration has an explicit sharp symmetric-product order at which it becomes nonwandering. The order is \(2\max(a,b)\) for balanced-or-unbalanced collections containing both kinds of step points without the fixed point, and \(2c+2\max(a,b)+\mathbf1_{\{c=0,a=b\}}\) when the fixed point is present, where \(c\) counts isolated finite-block points.
