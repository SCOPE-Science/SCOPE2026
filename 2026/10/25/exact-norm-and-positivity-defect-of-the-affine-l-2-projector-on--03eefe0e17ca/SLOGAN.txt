For uniform surface measure on the boundary of the \(\ell_1\) unit ball, the affine \(L^2\) projector has exact \(L^\infty\) norm \(1+\frac{(d-1)(d+2)}{2d}(1-\frac{2}{d(d+1)})^{d-1}\) for \(d\geq2\), attained at coordinate vertices. Its exact worst negative excursion on inputs between zero and one is half the norm's excess over one; in dimension one the projector is the identity.
