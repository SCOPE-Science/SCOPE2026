The proof supplies full quantifiers and equality cases, an exact hyperbolic-sine identity, and a complete extended-limit argument. It does not state the general obtuse-triangle probability, fixed-angle optimizer/stability, or nonuniform right-angle transition. The closest retrieved published abstract on regular odd polygons covers the equilateral triangle, not the arbitrary obtuse family. A more obscure equivalent pedal-chamber area formula cannot be excluded by these bounded searches.
