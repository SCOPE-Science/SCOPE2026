On a rank-one inverse problem with genuinely independent row and coordinate sampling, doubly stochastic coordinate descent drives the data residual to zero but retains an exactly computable, strictly positive expected squared distance from the minimum-norm solution for every admissible deterministic stepsize schedule. In the two-row, two-coordinate example, that distance converges to \(17/126\) while the squared residual contracts as \(5(65/128)^k\).
