For the three binary codebooks newly published with parameters \((22,84,9)\), \((24,196,9)\), and \((25,65,11)\), the exact distributions of how many codewords lie within one-error-beyond-unique-decoding radius are determined. The maximum lists are four, four, and three respectively; a majority-center lemma reduces the exhaustive proof to edge, triangle, and four-clique counts.
