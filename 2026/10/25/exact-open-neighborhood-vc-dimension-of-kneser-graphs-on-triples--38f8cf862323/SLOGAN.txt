The open-neighborhood VC dimension of the rank-three Kneser graph \(\operatorname{KG}(n,3)\) rises through \(0,1,2,3,4,5\) and stabilizes at exactly \(6\) from \(n=13\) onward. An exhaustive reduction to 9,177 seven-edge multigraph profiles proves the sharp universal bound, with explicit witnesses for every transition.
