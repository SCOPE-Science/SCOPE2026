Fourier integration gives the stated all-time formula, including the \(e^{-5\pi^2t}\) mixed correction; Gaussian unfolding gives the short-time exponent and prefactor. The standalone finite check reproduces the integral and spectral values. The proof, not the numeric samples, establishes the claims.

Targeted published-finding corpus queries and 103 accessible prior claims showed no exact matching assertion. The 1960 total-positivity original was available as bibliographic metadata only; an unindexed two-dimensional evaluation is not excluded.
