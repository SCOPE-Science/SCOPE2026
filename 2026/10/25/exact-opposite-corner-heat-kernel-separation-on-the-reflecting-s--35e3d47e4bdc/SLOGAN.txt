For Neumann heat flow on the unit square, the exact total-variation distance between laws started at opposite corners is attained by the diagonal triangular test \(u+v<1\). Its short-time deficit has the sharp geometric law \(1-D(t)\sim8\sqrt{2/\pi}\sqrt t\,e^{-1/(8t)}\), while its long-time profile begins \(16\pi^{-2}e^{-\pi^2t}-32(3\pi^2)^{-1}e^{-5\pi^2t}\); this is a fixed-pair distance, not a global contraction coefficient.\n
