For the weighted orbit square-function renorming of \(L^1\), the exact upper distortion detects whether an invertible measure-preserving action has uniformly bounded finite orbit lengths: it is the maximum of explicit aggregated-weight constants in that case and the full sum \(\sum_k\sqrt{a_k}\) otherwise. The full-sum value is never attained by a nonzero function, and asymptotic extremizers are characterized exactly by vanishing pairwise overlap of shifted weighted magnitudes.
