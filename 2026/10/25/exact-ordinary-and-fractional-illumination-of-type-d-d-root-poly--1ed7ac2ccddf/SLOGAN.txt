For each integer \(d\ge4\), the type-\(D_d\) root polytope has exactly \(2d\) ordinary illuminating directions and fractional illumination value \(2d\); an explicit \(2d\)-direction design partitions all of its vertices into equally sized illuminated classes. In particular, the regular \(24\)-cell has ordinary and fractional illumination numbers both equal to \(8\).
