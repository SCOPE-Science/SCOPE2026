The area of the Cartesian bounding box of every uniformly rotated regular \(n\)-gon has an exact three-branch cosine distribution, with sharply distinct high-order variances in the ratios \(256:16:1\) according to \(n\bmod4\). After normalizing by its attainable area range, its orientation loss converges to the \(\operatorname{Beta}(1/2,1)\) law with an explicit sharp Kolmogorov correction.
