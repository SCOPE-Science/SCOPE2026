# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
All 4095 nonzero bihomogeneous forms are checked for geometric smoothness on all four charts by exact Gröbner bases. The 1116 smooth equations and every point-count/2-rank cell agree in a direct scan and a projective-orbit census. Two algebraically different 2-rank procedures agree, using the separable degree-two branch-support formula and the mod-2 zeta numerator derived from F4 points. The genus and coordinate conventions are explicit.

## Originality — PASS with publication-priority qualification
Zobernig (2021) counts coarse-moduli p-rank strata; Cardona, Nart and Pujolàs (2002) classify genus-two curves and their ramification types. Neither inspected full source tabulates the exact joint frequencies for coordinate-fixed embedded (2,3) models. A previously recorded smooth-pencils result in this linear system does not imply the cross-frequency table as stated. Unindexed tables and indirect derivations remain a genuine priority risk.

## Value — PASS
The natural genus-two family on the split quadric has a canonical separable double projection. Its equation-weighted joint point/2-rank stratification gives precise arithmetic information beyond the underlying coarse-moduli p-rank counts. The finding is a complete natural finite classification, not a fabricated general theorem.

## Limitations
The counts are not isomorphism-class counts; the theorem is field- and embedding-specific, and publication novelty is not externally certified. All verification scripts use exact finite computations.
