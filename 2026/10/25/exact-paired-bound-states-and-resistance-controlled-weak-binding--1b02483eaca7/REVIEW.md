# Review of the neutral lattice-defect spectral result

## Correctness

PASS. The two-site resolvent determinant and its strictly decreasing positive factors prove exactly one simple sub-band eigenvalue for every positive coupling. A checkerboard-reflection unitary produces exactly one equally displaced super-band partner. The square-lattice Watson integral and an explicit \(O(\kappa|\log\kappa|)\) difference bound produce the separation-dependent prefactor, with nearest-neighbor normalization \(R_{e_1}=1/4\). The supplied checker is auxiliary; existence and asymptotics are analytic.

## Originality

PASS, with a stated priority risk. Simon (1976) already derives inverse-square-coupling essential singularities for zero-integral **continuum** potentials, and McCutcheon (2004) supplies the square-lattice free Green function. Their statements, the 2002 continuum bound-state counts of Chadan–Khuri–Martin–Wu, and the inspected free-lattice handbook do not state the signed two-site all-coupling spectral pairing and exact effective-resistance prefactor. Relevant mathematical-record comparisons concern distinct threshold and impurity models. General finite-rank determinant methods may provide implicit coverage in uninspected work; independent literature assessment is not claimed.

## Mathematical value

PASS. A net-zero onsite perturbation binds on both sides of a finite band for arbitrarily small coupling. The structural symmetry, uniqueness, and explicit resistance-controlled exponentially small binding gap link impurity geometry to measurable localization energies, extending beyond a single arbitrarily selected lattice configuration.

## Limitations

The statement is about exterior eigenvalues only, on the infinite nearest-neighbor square lattice with balanced, purely onsite defects. It does not decide embedded eigenvalues, multiple-defect generalizations, other lattice dimensions, or a separation-dependent uniform scaling limit.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
