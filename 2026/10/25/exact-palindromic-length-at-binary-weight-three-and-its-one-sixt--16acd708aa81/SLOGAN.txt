Every binary word with exactly three ones has palindromic length one, two, three, or four according to an explicit four-zero-gap formula; the four-piece case occurs exactly when the two inner gaps differ and both outer gaps exceed them. Among length-\(n\) words with exactly three ones, the proportion requiring four palindromes tends to \(1/6\), with exact generating function \(2z^8/((1-z)^2(1-z^3)(1-z^4))\).
