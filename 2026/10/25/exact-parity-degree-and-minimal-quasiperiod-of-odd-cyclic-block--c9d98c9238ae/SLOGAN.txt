For odd cyclic block polytopes, the apparent period-two bound is always sharp: even- and odd-dilation Ehrhart polynomials differ in exact degree \(m(a-1)\), with leading coefficient \(1/(2^{ma}((a-1)!)^m)\). The first parity-sensitive coefficient sits precisely \(m\) degrees below the volume term, and a local cone-parity identity gives the entire discrepancy polynomial.
