On a clique with one private leaf per vertex, synchronous tie-retaining binary majority reaches a fixed point or two-cycle in at most three steps, and the entire periodic-orbit and transient-depth census has explicit binomial formulas. Even clique orders have a recurrent-state fraction asymptotic to \(2/(\pi m)\), but odd orders have the exponentially smaller fraction \(2^{-m}\sqrt{2m/\pi}\).
