For synchronous binary neighbour-XOR dynamics on any finite rooted tree with an even number of children at every nonleaf, the parity of every walk is determined exactly by vertex depths and lowest common ancestor depth. If the maximum depth is \(h\), then \(A^{2h}\) is a nonzero rank-one outer product and \(A^{2h+1}=0\); exactly half of all initial states survive to the penultimate time.
