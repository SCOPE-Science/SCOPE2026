Every complex two-by-two unimodular Schur symbol has exact fourth-power Schatten-four multiplier norm \((9-c+\sqrt{(9-c)^2-64})/8\), where \(c\) is the cosine of its cross phase; by the known two-by-two amplification theorem the same constant governs its completely bounded norm. Every nonfactorable symbol has precisely equal-entry-modulus norming matrices with one uniquely determined cross phase, and its norm grows linearly in the absolute phase near zero.
