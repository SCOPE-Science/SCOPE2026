For commuting unitary fine and coarse Parareal propagators, one or two corrections are energy-expansive whenever their phases differ, but three and four corrections restore exact completed-block nonexpansivity precisely below sharp mismatch thresholds. The squared operator-norm mismatch ceilings are \(3/[(N-1)(N-2)]\) and \(8/[(N-1)(N-3)]\), respectively, for \(N\) windows.
