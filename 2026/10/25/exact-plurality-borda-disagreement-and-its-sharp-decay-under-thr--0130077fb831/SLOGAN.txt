For three candidates under independent uniform single-peaked Walsh votes, the probability that plurality and Borda choose different unique winners is exactly a finite multinomial sum and decays as \(2(1+\sqrt2)(2\pi\sigma^2n)^{-1/2}((1+2\sqrt2)/4)^n\), where \(\sigma^2=2\sqrt2/(1+2\sqrt2)\). Such a mismatch can only put an extreme plurality winner against the middle Borda winner, and first occurs with four voters.
