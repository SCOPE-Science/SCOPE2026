A Haar-random central plane cuts the regular truncated octahedron into a hexagon, octagon, or decagon with respective exact probabilities \(0.4984506899177037\ldots\), \(0.3161088555801904\ldots\), and \(0.1854404545021058\ldots\), each given by elementary spherical-triangle angles. The same law independently reproduces the sharp mean side count \(36\arccos(4/5)/\pi\).
