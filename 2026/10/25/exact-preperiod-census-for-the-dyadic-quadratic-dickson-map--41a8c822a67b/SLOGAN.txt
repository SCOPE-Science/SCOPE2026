The monic quadratic Dickson map \(x\mapsto x^2-2\) modulo every power of two has precisely two attracting fixed points, and its complete functional graph admits explicit least-arrival-time formulas and exact depth histograms for both parity basins. The odd basin has \(2^t\) points at every depth \(2\le t\le k-2\), while the even basin is governed by a two-step valuation growth law.
