For the full tent map, **all** points of any exact prime period \(p\) simultaneously achieve star discrepancy, bottleneck transport distance and interval covering radius \(2/(2^p+1)\) relative to Lebesgue measure. Thus their values determine an arbitrary \(L\)-Lipschitz observable with sharp minimax sup-norm error \(2L/(2^p+1)\), while composite periods need not have the same discrepancy law.
