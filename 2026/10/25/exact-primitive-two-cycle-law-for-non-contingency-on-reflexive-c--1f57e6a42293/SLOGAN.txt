On the reflexive three-neighbor cyclic Kripke frame of length \(n\), iterated non-contingency has exactly \(C_n\) primitive valuation two-cycles, with generating function \(\sum_{n\ge3}C_nz^n=(2z^4+5z^5+3z^6)/(1-z^4-2z^5-z^6)\). Two-cycles exist for every \(n\ge4\) except \(n=7\), and their counts grow as \(\rho^n/2\) for the plastic constant \(\rho\).
