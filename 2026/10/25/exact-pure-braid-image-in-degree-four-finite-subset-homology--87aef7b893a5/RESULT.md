# Exact pure-braid image in degree-four finite-subset homology

## Finding

For every integer \(n\ge2\), let \(D_n\) be a disk with \(n\) punctures and \(P_n\) its pure braid group. On the integral top homology \(H_4(\exp_4 D_n;\mathbb Z)\), the image of \(P_n\) is trivial for \(n\le3\) and is isomorphic to \(\mathbb Z^{\binom n2-1}\) for every \(n\ge4\). For \(n\ge4\), the correction operators \(N_{ij}=\rho(A_{ij})-I\) have exactly one integral linear relation, \(\sum_{i<j}N_{ij}=0\), where \(A_{ij}\) are the standard pure-braid generators.

## Assumptions and scope

The finite-subset space \(\exp_k X\) consists of the nonempty subsets of \(X\) of cardinality at most \(k\). The punctured disk \(D_n\) is homotopy equivalent to the bouquet \(\Gamma_n\) of \(n\) circles, and \(\exp_k\) is a homotopy functor. We use Tuffley's cellular chain-ring model for \(\exp_4\Gamma_n\), with the standard Artin-generator convention. The claim is an exact integral image calculation in degree four only; it does not assert exact images in other degrees.

## Proof

Work first in the rational chain ring \(R_n=\widetilde{\mathcal C}_*(\Gamma_n)\otimes\mathbb Q\). Put \(a_i=\widetilde\sigma_i^1\) and \(b_i=\widetilde\sigma_i^2\). The ring is generated by the degree-one exterior generators \(a_i\) and the degree-two commuting generators \(b_i\). Tuffley's local Artin-generator formula gives \(a_i\mapsto a_{i+1}\), \(a_{i+1}\mapsto a_i\), \(b_i\mapsto b_{i+1}\), and \(b_{i+1}\mapsto b_i-2a_i a_{i+1}\). Consequently, the standard pure generator \(A_{ij}\) acts by the translation
\[
 b_i\mapsto b_i-2a_i a_j,\qquad b_j\mapsto b_j+2a_i a_j,
\]
fixing all \(a_r\) and all other \(b_r\).

In degree four, work over \(\mathbb Q\) and write \(\delta=\tfrac12\partial\) for the rescaled cellular boundary. Then \(\delta(b_i)=a_i\) and \(\delta(a_i)=0\), extended as a graded derivation; rescaling by a nonzero scalar does not change the cycle kernel. The top homology is the kernel of this boundary in degree four, since there are no chains of degree five in \(\exp_4\Gamma_n\). A basis of this kernel consists of the following cycles, with indices increasing:
\[
\begin{aligned}
 u_{ij}&=a_i a_j b_i, & v_{ij}&=a_i a_j b_j,\\
 x_{ijk}&=a_i a_j b_k+a_i a_k b_j, &
 y_{ijk}&=a_i a_j b_k-a_j a_k b_i,\\
 z_{ijkl}&=a_i a_j a_k a_l.
\end{aligned}
\]
There are two pair-supported cycles for each pair, two independent cycles for each triple (the three triple-supported monomials have a one-dimensional boundary image), and one fourfold exterior cycle for each four-set. Pure-braid corrections kill the pair-supported and fourfold exterior cycles. On the two-dimensional cycle space for a triple \(\{i,j,k\}\), with an outside index \(l\), the coefficient of \(a_i a_j a_k a_l\) under a linear combination \(N_c=\sum_{p<q}c_{pq}N_{pq}\) vanishes on both cycle generators exactly when
\[
 c_{il}=c_{jl}=c_{kl}.
\]

If \(n\ge4\), this condition for every triple and every outside index forces all edge coefficients \(c_{pq}\) to be equal: for a fixed \(l\), any two edges incident to \(l\) occur together in such a condition by choosing a third vertex. Conversely, the all-ones coefficient vector kills every cycle. Thus the integral kernel of the map from the free abelian group on the \(A_{ij}\)-operators is exactly \(\mathbb Z(1,\ldots,1)\). Each correction maps triple cycles into the fourfold exterior subspace and kills that subspace, so \(N_{ij}N_{pq}=0\) on \(H_4\); products of the operators therefore add their correction terms. The image is
\[
 \mathbb Z^{\binom n2}/\mathbb Z(1,\ldots,1)\cong\mathbb Z^{\binom n2-1}.
\]

For \(n\le3\), there is no outside vertex \(l\) and no fourfold exterior class. Every potential correction on a triple-supported cycle repeats an exterior generator and vanishes. Hence the pure-braid image is trivial. The integral conclusions follow from the integral cellular chain model and the explicitly integral correction operators; the rational chain-ring coordinates are used only to simplify the calculation.

## Verification

The accompanying standard-library script `verify_h4.py` constructs the exact correction matrix on the two cycle generators for each triple and computes its rank over \(\mathbb Q\) by rational Gaussian elimination. For \(2\le n\le8\), the computed ranks are \(0,0,5,9,14,20,27\), agreeing with the formula \(0\) for \(n\le3\) and \(\binom n2-1\) for \(n\ge4\). It also checks the all-ones relation. These finite checks are supplementary; the proof of the all-\(n\) statement is the cycle-space and coefficient-equality argument above.

## Relationship to prior work

Tuffley gives the chain-ring model and proves a general nilpotency bound for the pure-braid action on top homology. A separate exact-image calculation determines the image in degree three. Neither result gives the degree-four integral image or its threshold at four punctures. The present calculation uses the published chain-ring formulas but adds a degree-four cycle-space classification and determines the complete relation lattice of the correction operators.

## Limitations

No exact-image formula is claimed for \(H_k(\exp_k D_n;\mathbb Z)\) when \(k\ne4\). The finite checker covers \(n\le8\) only and is not a proof for arbitrary \(n\). The literature search found the degree-three exact-image result but no degree-four result; an unindexed or later source could still contain the same calculation. No independent audit has been performed.
