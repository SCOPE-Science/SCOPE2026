# Review summary

## Correctness

**PASS.** The degree-four chain group splits by exterior degree. Its cycles are the pair-supported classes \(a_i a_j b_i\) and \(a_i a_j b_j\), two independent cycles for each triple, and the fourfold exterior classes. The pure-generator corrections kill the pair-supported and fourfold exterior classes. On each triple cycle space, the correction vanishes exactly when the three coefficients incident to any outside vertex agree. For \(n\ge4\), these conditions force the coefficient vector to be constant; the constant vector is indeed the unique relation. The correction operators kill their own image, so the integral image is the free quotient by that diagonal relation. For \(n\le3\), all corrections vanish.

## Originality

**PASS, with residual bibliographic risk.** Tuffley's paper supplies the chain-ring model and a general nilpotency bound, but not the exact degree-four image. The closest exact-image calculation is for degree three only. The new claim determines the degree-four image for every puncture count and the sharp threshold at four punctures. Search failure is not treated as proof of novelty; an unindexed later calculation remains a risk.

## Value

**PASS.** This is a natural next-degree exact invariant after the known degree-three calculation. It identifies when degree-four top homology first detects nontrivial pure-braid motion, and it gives the complete integral relation lattice rather than only a nilpotency bound or a finite sample.

## Closest literature and limits
