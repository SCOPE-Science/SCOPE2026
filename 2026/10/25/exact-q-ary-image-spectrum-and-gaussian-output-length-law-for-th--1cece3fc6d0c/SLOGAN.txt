For deterministic threshold-one contextual deletions over \(q\ge2\) symbols, an output \(y\) from length \(n\) is attainable exactly when \(|y|+r(y)-1\le n\), yielding a closed rational generating function for every output-length frequency and an exact zero-error capacity \(\log_q((1+\sqrt{4q-3})/2)\). Uniformly selected distinct outputs also have an explicit, nondegenerate Gaussian length law.
