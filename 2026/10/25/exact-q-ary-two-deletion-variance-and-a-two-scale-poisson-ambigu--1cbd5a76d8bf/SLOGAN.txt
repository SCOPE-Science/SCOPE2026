For uniformly random \(q\)-ary words, the exact variance of the two-deletion sphere size has an all-alphabet closed form; its binary specialization agrees with the known binary variance. At \(n/q\to\theta>0\), the volume deficit exhibits a genuinely two-scale limit governed by two independent \(\operatorname{Poisson}(\theta)\) variables, and the fraction of full-volume centers tends to \(e^{-2\theta}\).
