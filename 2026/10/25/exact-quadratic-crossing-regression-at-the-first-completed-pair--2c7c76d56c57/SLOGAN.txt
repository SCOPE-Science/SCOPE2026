In a uniform random chord diagram, the expected number of crossings conditioned on the first completed pair occurring at position \(t\) is exactly \(n(n-3)/6+(t-1)(t+2)/12\), and therefore strictly increases with \(t\). The resulting positive waiting-time/crossing covariance grows as \(\sqrt\pi\,n^{3/2}/6\), even while the correlation vanishes.
