For unordered multisets over four symbols, the exact largest code correcting all but three symbols is \(S_4(n,n-3)=6\) at \(n=5\), \(5\) for \(6\le n\le8\), and \(4\) for every \(n\ge9\). A heavy-coordinate packing lemma and the exceptional triangle-free light-vector compatibility pattern make these bounds sharp for every length.
