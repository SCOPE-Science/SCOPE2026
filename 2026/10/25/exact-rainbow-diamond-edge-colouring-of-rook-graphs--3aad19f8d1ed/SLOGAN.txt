Every rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) with \(2\le m\le n\) and \(n\ge4\) has D-chromatic index exactly \(\binom n2\), attaining the unavoidable palette size of one complete row despite intersections with all column cliques. The only smaller cases have indices \(2\), \(3\), and \(5\) for \((m,n)=(2,2),(2,3),(3,3)\), respectively.
