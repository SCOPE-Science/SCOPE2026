The exact rainbow pushability of the \(d\)-dimensional cross-polytope is \(\pi_r=\min\{1,2^{d-r}\}\) for all \(d\ge2\) and \(r\ge1\). For \(r\ge d\), every optimizer is individually antipodally symmetric and assigns a dedicated axis-exclusive random vertex law to each of the \(d\) coordinate axes, and these conditions are sufficient.
