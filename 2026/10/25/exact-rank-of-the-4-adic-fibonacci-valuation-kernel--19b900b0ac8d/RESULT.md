# Exact rank of the 4-adic Fibonacci valuation kernel

## Finding

Let \(F_0=0,F_1=1,F_{m+2}=F_{m+1}+F_m\), and for \(n\ge0\) set \(s(n)=\nu_4(F_{n+1})\), where \(\nu_4(t)=\max\{e\ge0:4^e\mid t\}\) for positive integers \(t\). The \(\mathbb Z\)-module generated by the 4-kernel \(\{(s(4^e n+i))_{n\ge0}:e\ge0,\ 0\le i<4^e\}\) has rank exactly \(5\).

## Assumptions and scope

All sequence indices are nonnegative integers. The 4-kernel of \(s\) is the family of sequences \(s_{e,i}(n)=s(4^e n+i)\), with \(e\ge0\) and \(0\le i<4^e\). Its generated module is taken over \(\mathbb Z\); rank means the dimension of its rational span. No formula for the rank at general composite bases or for general Lucas sequences is claimed.

## Proof

For positive \(m\), the standard 2-adic Fibonacci formula is
\[
\nu_2(F_m)=\begin{cases}
\nu_2(m)+2,&6\mid m,\
1,&m\equiv3\pmod6,\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]
Since \(\nu_4(F_m)=\lfloor\nu_2(F_m)/2\rfloor\), put \(h(t)=\lfloor(t+2)/2\rfloor\) for integers \(t\ge0\). Then
\[
s(n)=\begin{cases}h(\nu_2(n+1)),&6\mid n+1,\0,&6\nmid n+1.\end{cases}
\]
For \(j\in\{0,1,2\}\), let \(\delta_j(n)=1\) if \(n\equiv j\pmod3\), and \(0\) otherwise, and set
\[
g(n)=\delta_2(n)h(\nu_2(n+1)).
\]

We show that the full kernel module is generated by \(s,g,\delta_0,\delta_1,\delta_2\). Fix \(e\ge1\), \(0\le i<4^e\), and write \(B=4^e\). If \(i+1<B\) is odd, then \(Bn+i+1\) is odd and \(s(Bn+i)=0\). If \(i+1<B\) is even, then \(\nu_2(Bn+i+1)=\nu_2(i+1)\), because \(\nu_2(i+1)<2e\). Also \(Bn+i+1\equiv n+i+1\pmod3\), since \(4^e\equiv1\pmod3\). Hence
\[
s(4^e n+i)=h(\nu_2(i+1))\,\delta_{(-i-1)\bmod3}(n).
\]
The remaining offset is \(i=B-1\). In that case \(Bn+i+1=B(n+1)\), so
\[
s(4^e n+B-1)=\delta_2(n)h(2e+\nu_2(n+1))=g(n)+e\delta_2(n),
\]
using \(h(t+2e)=h(t)+e\). The case \(e=0\) contributes just \(s\). Thus every kernel sequence lies in the stated five-generator span.

Conversely, the generators belong to the kernel module. Directly from the formula for \(s\),
\[
s(16n+1)=\delta_1(n),\qquad s(16n+5)=\delta_0(n),\qquad s(64n+9)=\delta_2(n),
\]
and \(s(4n+3)=g(n)+\delta_2(n)\). Thus \(g\) and all three indicators are in the module, so the five sequences generate it.

They are linearly independent. Suppose \(a s+b g+c_0\delta_0+c_1\delta_1+c_2\delta_2=0\) for integers \(a,b,c_0,c_1,c_2\). Evaluating at \(n=0\) and \(n=1\) gives \(c_0=c_1=0\). Evaluating at \(n=2\), \(n=5\), and \(n=11\) gives respectively
\[
b+c_2=0,\qquad a+b+c_2=0,\qquad 2b+c_2=0.
\]
These imply \(a=b=c_2=0\). Therefore the five generators are independent, and the kernel module has rank exactly \(5\). \(\square\)

## Verification

The accompanying standard-library script checks the stated 2-adic Fibonacci formula against exact Fibonacci integers through index 10,000; checks the kernel decomposition for every offset at depths \(1\) through \(6\) and indices \(0\) through \(149\); and checks the generator identities and independence witnesses. These are finite consistency checks only. The all-depth kernel containment and independence are established by the symbolic proof above.

## Relationship to prior work

Murru and Sanna prove that \(\nu_k(F_{n+1})\) is \(k\)-regular for composite \(k\) under their hypotheses, and determine ranks in the prime-base case. Their concluding remarks explicitly ask for rank results when \(k\) is composite, singling out prime powers as a natural easier case. The result here determines the exact rank for the first nontrivial prime-power base \(k=4\). Medina and Rowland provide the 2-adic valuation formula used in the proof, but do not determine this composite-base kernel rank. Sobolewski later classifies regularity for broad valuation families, including prime-power bases, but the inspected results classify regularity rather than this exact module rank.

## Limitations

This is one exact rank calculation, for \(\nu_4(F_{n+1})\), not a general formula for \(\nu_{p^a}(F_{n+1})\) or for arbitrary Lucas sequences. The finite script is corroborative, not an infinite certificate. An unindexed or differently phrased proof of this special rank may exist; independent audit has not been performed.
