For every nonzero rational \(\alpha=\pm a/b\) in lowest terms, the ordered pair-correlation profile of \(\{\alpha(n+\log n)\}\) exists at every fixed scale and equals \(2s/[a(e^{1/a}-1)]+2\sum_{h\ge1}(1-ah/s)_+\) for \(s>0\). The exact law depends on the numerator but not the denominator, and it is non-Poissonian despite the almost-everywhere Poissonian theorem.
