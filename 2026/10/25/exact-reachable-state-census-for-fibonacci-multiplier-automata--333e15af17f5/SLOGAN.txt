The standard bounded-difference Zeckendorf automaton for the two-track relation \(Y=mX+c\), with \(m\ge1\) and \(0\le c<m\), has exactly \(6m^2+6m-4\) reachable non-sink states before DFA minimization. An irrational-rotation partition gives the complete current-difference state profile and improves the ambient \(36m^2\) construction.
