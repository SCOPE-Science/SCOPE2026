For every empty lattice tetrahedron of normalized volume \(m\), the prism obtained by multiplying by an integer segment of length \(k\) has an entirely real-rooted Ehrhart numerator exactly in an explicit, sharp two-parameter window. Small volumes exhibit finite, sometimes vanishing windows, whereas every volume \(m\ge9\) acquires real-rootedness permanently at precisely \(k\ge\lceil m/2\rceil\), even among width-one examples.
