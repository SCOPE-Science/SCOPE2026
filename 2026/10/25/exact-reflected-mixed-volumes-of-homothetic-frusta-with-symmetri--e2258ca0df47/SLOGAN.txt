Every homothetic frustum over a centrally symmetric convex base has the same normalized reflected mixed volumes, independently of the base’s shape: \(R_{d,k}(a)=1+(1-a^k)(1-a^{d-k})/(1-a^d)\) for \(0\le a<1\), with continuous value \(1\) at \(a=1\). At each interior index the ratio strictly decreases from \(2\) for pyramids to \(1\) for prisms, while the index profile peaks in the middle.
