The ternary substitution fixed point \(0\mapsto01\), \(1\mapsto21\), \(2\mapsto0\) has no factor of length eight or more whose reversal also occurs, and seven is sharp. Its exact reflection complexity is \(1\) at length zero, \(3,3,6,7,10,12,15\) at lengths one through seven, and \(2n+1\) at every length \(n\geq8\).
