# Exact Reidemeister-number distribution for automorphisms of the odd Heisenberg group

## Finding
For every odd prime \(p\), let \(H_p\) be the nonabelian group of order \(p^3\) and exponent \(p\). For an automorphism \(\varphi\), let \(R(\varphi)\) denote the number of \(\varphi\)-twisted conjugacy classes. The **complete multiplicity distribution** is
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
R(\varphi)&\#\{\varphi\in\operatorname{Aut}(H_p):R(\varphi)=r\}\\\hline
1&p^3(p+1)(p-2)^2\\
p&p^3(2p^2-2p-3)\\
2p-1&p^2(p^2-1)\\
p^2+p-1&p^2.
\end{array}
\]
In the table, the row label \(r\) is the value of \(R(\varphi)\) in that row. These four values are distinct and exhaust the Reidemeister spectrum. In particular, for \(p=3\) the four multiplicities are respectively \(108,243,72,9\), summing to \(432=|\operatorname{Aut}(H_3)|\).

The \(R=1\) stratum has a previously established equivalent fixed-point-free count for the three-dimensional Heisenberg Lie algebra. The contribution assessed here is the **complete four-stratum group-automorphism distribution**, not that first stratum in isolation.

## Assumptions and scope
Let \(p\ge3\) be prime, \(V=\mathbb F_p^2\), and \(\omega((x,y),(u,v))=xv-yu\). Write the group in coordinates
\[
H_p=V\times\mathbb F_p,\qquad
(a,z)(b,w)=(a+b,z+w+\tfrac12\omega(a,b)).
\]
The inverse is \((a,z)^{-1}=(-a,-z)\). The scalar \(\tfrac12\) is valid only for odd \(p\). The group is isomorphic to the upper-unitriangular \(3\times3\) group over \(\mathbb F_p\). Twisted conjugacy means the equivalence relation generated by \(x\sim_\varphi g x\varphi(g)^{-1}\). Counts are over all group automorphisms, not just inner automorphisms, and each automorphism is counted once.

## Proof
**Automorphisms.** The commutator satisfies \([(a,z),(b,w)]=(0,\omega(a,b))\), so the center and commutator subgroup are both \(Z=\{0\}\times\mathbb F_p\). An automorphism induces some \(A\in\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb F_p)\) on \(H_p/Z\cong V\), and acts on \(Z\) by its determinant \(d=\det A\), as forced by the commutator identity. Writing its action on \((a,0)\) as \((Aa,\ell(a))\), compatibility with the displayed multiplication gives \(\ell(a+b)=\ell(a)+\ell(b)\). Thus \(\ell\in V^*\), and every automorphism is uniquely
\[
\varphi_{A,\ell}(a,z)=(Aa,d z+\ell(a)),\qquad A\in\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb F_p),\quad \ell\in V^*.
\]
Conversely this formula preserves multiplication (because \(\omega(Aa,Ab)=d\,\omega(a,b)\)) and is bijective. There are exactly \(p^2\) automorphisms over each \(A\), so
\[
|\operatorname{Aut}(H_p)|=p^2|\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb F_p)|=p^3(p-1)^2(p+1).
\]

**Twisted-class count.** A noncentral conjugacy class consists of all \((a,z)\) with its fixed nonzero quotient coordinate \(a\): conjugating by \((b,w)\) shifts \(z\) by \(\omega(b,a)\), and the shift takes every field value as \(b\) varies. The \(p\) central elements each form singleton classes. For a finite group the number of twisted conjugacy classes of an automorphism equals the number of ordinary conjugacy classes fixed by the induced automorphism; this follows, for example, by Burnside averaging the action \(g:x\mapsto gx\varphi(g)^{-1}\). Therefore if \(k=\dim\ker(A-I)\), exactly \(p^k-1\) noncentral classes and \(p\) or \(1\) central classes are fixed according as \(d=1\) or \(d\ne1\). Hence
\[
R(\varphi_{A,\ell})=p^k+(p-1)\mathbf1_{\{d=1\}}.
\]
This proves, in particular, that \(R\) is independent of the central shift \(\ell\).

**Matrix enumeration.** The determinant-one fiber \(\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb F_p)\) has \(p(p^2-1)\) elements. Among them, only \(I\) has \(k=2\). The nonidentity unipotent matrices have \(k=1\); their number is \(p^2-1\), as may be seen by choosing the common one-dimensional image/kernel of \(A-I\) (\(p+1\) choices) and a nonzero map from the one-dimensional quotient to that line (\(p-1\) choices). The remaining \(p^3-p^2-p\) determinant-one matrices have \(k=0\).

For each determinant \(d\ne1\), a matrix with a fixed nonzero vector has distinct eigenvalues \(1,d\); it is conjugate to \(\operatorname{diag}(1,d)\) and its centralizer has order \((p-1)^2\). Thus the number with \(k=1\) and \(d\ne1\) is \(p(p+1)(p-2)\). Subtraction from \(|\operatorname{GL}_2|-|\operatorname{SL}_2|\) leaves \(p(p+1)(p-2)^2\) matrices with \(k=0,d\ne1\).

Collecting the matrix counts, there are \(p(p+1)(p-2)^2\) quotient matrices with \(R=1\), \(2p^3-2p^2-3p\) with \(R=p\), \(p^2-1\) with \(R=2p-1\), and one with \(R=p^2+p-1\). Multiply all four numbers by the \(p^2\) central shifts. This is exactly the displayed distribution. Their sum agrees with \(|\operatorname{Aut}(H_p)|\), and all four counts are positive for every \(p\ge3\), proving exhaustiveness.

## Verification
The standalone `check_reidemeister.py` uses only Python's standard library. For \(p=3,5,7,11\), it enumerates all invertible \(2\times2\) matrices, computes \(\dim\ker(A-I)\) and \(\det A\), and checks the complete four-stratum histogram and total automorphism count. For \(p=3\), it additionally enumerates all \(432\) group automorphisms and independently computes **actual twisted orbits** on all \(27\) group elements by the defining action; every orbit count agrees. The packaged checker outputs `VERIFY_OK`. The parameter-uniform proof above does not depend on these finite checks.

## Relationship to prior work
Senden and Tertooy (2026) give general character and conjugacy-class formulas for bi-twisted conjugacy, with an extraspecial-group example involving a different endomorphism-pair statistic. Their fixed-conjugacy-class equality underlies the reduction used here; they do not tabulate the full automorphism distribution of \(H_p\). Kumar and Pradhan (2019) describe endomorphisms, automorphisms, and **orbits of elements under the whole automorphism group** for extraspecial groups; the latter is not the family of twisted-conjugacy orbits attached to each single automorphism. Tertooy (2024) studies extreme Reidemeister spectra rather than this prime-uniform histogram.

An earlier published mathematical result, *Exact fixed-point-free automorphism count for the three-dimensional Heisenberg Lie algebra* (2026; public record `17f0de4cb789`), gives \(p^3(p+1)(p-2)^2\) fixed-point-free automorphisms. The odd-characteristic class-two correspondence identifies that count with the \(R=1\) group stratum. Accordingly the first row is not asserted to be new. The three remaining multiplicities and their joint complete distribution are the result here.

## Limitations
The theorem covers primes \(p\ge3\) and the exponent-\(p\) extraspecial group of order \(p^3\) only. It does not assert the same distribution for \(p=2\), the exponent-\(p^2\) group, higher-dimensional extraspecial groups, endomorphisms, or general bi-twisted pairs. The result gives a complete histogram, not representatives of individual twisted-conjugacy classes. An older characterization of all finite Heisenberg twisted-conjugacy spectra could potentially imply these exact counts; the accessible targeted full-text comparisons did not establish such prior coverage.
