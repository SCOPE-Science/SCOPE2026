For every odd prime \(p\), the Reidemeister numbers of all automorphisms of the order-\(p^3\), exponent-\(p\) Heisenberg group take exactly the four values \(1,p,2p-1,p^2+p-1\), with explicit polynomial multiplicities. The complete distribution refines the previously known fixed-point-free count for the corresponding Heisenberg Lie algebra.
