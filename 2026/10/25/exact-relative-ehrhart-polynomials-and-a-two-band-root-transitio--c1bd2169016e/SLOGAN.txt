For the Reeve tetrahedron of integer height \(m\), the exact number of lattice translates of a fixed vertical integer segment inside every dilation has a closed two-parameter formula. The eventual relative Ehrhart cubic has three simple real roots in two explicit and sharp residue bands, and its first nonempty dilation is exactly \(2\lceil L/m\rceil\); in particular, a unit segment always yields the fully real-rooted cubic \(m(t-1)t(t+1)/6\).
