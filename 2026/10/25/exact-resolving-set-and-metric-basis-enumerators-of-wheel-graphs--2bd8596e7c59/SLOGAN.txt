For every wheel \(W_m=K_1\vee C_m\) with \(m\ge7\), a closed binomial sum counts its resolving sets at every cardinality, and the hub is an entirely optional landmark. Extracting the first nonzero coefficient gives five explicit congruence-class formulas for the number of metric bases, sharpening the known minimum-cardinality result without claiming the previously established gap characterization as new.
