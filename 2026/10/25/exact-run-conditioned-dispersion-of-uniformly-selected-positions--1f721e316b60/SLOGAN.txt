For a uniformly chosen \(k\)-subset of \(\{1,\ldots,N\}\), the expected sample variance of selected positions conditioned on exactly \(r\) consecutive runs is an explicit, strictly increasing affine function of \((r-1)/(r+1)\). Its unconditional covariance with run count is exactly \((N-k)(N+k+1)/(6N)\), positive whenever \(k<N\).
