For odd integers \(n=3^a5^b7^c11^d13^e\) with \(e\ge1\), the second Schemmel-totient stopping time is the weighted exponent sum \(a+b+c+2d+2e\) plus one or two explicitly determined start-up delays. This complete branching-chain formula proves Defant's logarithmic lower-bound conjecture strictly on the entire stated family.
