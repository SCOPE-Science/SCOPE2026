The 127 three-spaces on which the Welch map \(x\mapsto x^{11}\) over \(\mathbb F_{128}\) has zero sum form a single scalar orbit. Their intersection graph is 42-regular and has exactly seven common-neighbor pair types: two adjacent types (15, 21) and five nonadjacent types (8, 10, 12, 14, 16), with an exhaustive exact certificate.
