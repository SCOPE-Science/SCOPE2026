For an isometric finite-group action, the orbit-saturated multivalued map has exactly the ordinary and periodic shadowing moduli of its quotient, and periodic-point counts are weighted by symmetry-fibre sizes. Freeness transfers positive expansivity back from the quotient, whereas a branched two-sheet full-shift example retains both shadowing properties but loses positive expansivity and has point-count zeta \((1-z)/(1-2z)^2\).
