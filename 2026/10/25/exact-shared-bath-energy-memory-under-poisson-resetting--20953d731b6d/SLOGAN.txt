In a Brownian particle whose coordinates share one fluctuating squared-Brownian diffusivity and a common Poisson reset, the entire stationary covariance matrix of squared coordinates has the explicit lag form \(16\Lambda^4e^{-rs}[28I_d+(13+6rs)J_d]/r^4\). The long-time variance coefficient of the normalized time-averaged total squared displacement is \(2(19+28/d)/r\), so it remains \(38/r\) as the dimension tends to infinity, unlike the vanishing variance under independent baths and resets.
