For every fixed odd shift \(h\), the primes whose two neighbors \(p-h\) and \(p+h\) have squarefree odd parts possess an exact Euler-product density, determined solely by the odd prime divisors of \(h\). Divisibility of the shift by three creates a sharp universal density gap: all such shifts have density greater than \(3/4\), while shifts prime to three have density below \(2/3\).
