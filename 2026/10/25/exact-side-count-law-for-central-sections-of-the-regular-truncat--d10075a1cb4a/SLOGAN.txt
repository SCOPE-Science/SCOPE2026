For a uniformly oriented plane through the center of the regular truncated tetrahedron, the section has exactly four, five or six sides with respective exact probabilities \(0.215936085312640\ldots\), \(0.520247465238935\ldots\), and \(0.263816449448423\ldots\). Pentagons are the strict majority, and a finite spherical-chamber classification gives closed inverse-cosine formulas for the entire distribution.
