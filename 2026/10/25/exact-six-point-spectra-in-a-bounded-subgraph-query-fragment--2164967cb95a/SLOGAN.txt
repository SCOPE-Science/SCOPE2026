For Boolean combinations of subgraph-occurrence tests on at most four vertices, the largest possible spectrum of \(G(n,n^{-\alpha})\) has exactly six critical exponents: \(\{2/3,4/5,1,4/3,3/2,2\}\). A parity combination of \(K_4\), \(K_4-e\), \(K_3\), \(P_4\), \(P_3\), and \(K_2\) attains all six, with explicit nontrivial Poisson limits.
