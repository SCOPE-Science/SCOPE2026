On the split quadric over \(\mathbf F_2\), exactly \(44\,070\) pencils of bidegree-\((2,3)\) curves have all three rational members geometrically smooth of genus two. They form \(1\,242\) polarized-projective orbits, and their rational base loci contain precisely \(0,1,2\), or \(3\) points with respective frequencies \(6\,066,16\,884,17\,424,3\,696\).
