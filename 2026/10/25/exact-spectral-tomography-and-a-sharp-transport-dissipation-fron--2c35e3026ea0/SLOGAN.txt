For a stationary two-particle nonreciprocal chase-and-run diffusion on a circle, the exact all-time covariance of unwrapped displacements reveals every Fourier power of the interaction and determines the entropy-production rate. At any fixed positive entropy budget, a fundamental sinusoidal interaction uniquely maximizes both the covariance at every positive time and the long-time diffusion enhancement, up to phase and an additive constant.
