For two equal cold electron beams moving in opposite directions through a stationary third cold electron population, the spectrum has an exact density threshold \(b/a=1/4\): below it, low-wavenumber growth is nonoscillatory before becoming oscillatory, while at or above it every exponentially growing mode is oscillatory. The two transition wavenumber squares separate by \(4\sqrt6\sqrt{b/a}+O(b/a)\) in normalized units when the stationary population is dilute.
