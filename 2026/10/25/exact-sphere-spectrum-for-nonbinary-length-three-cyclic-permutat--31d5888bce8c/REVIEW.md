# Scientific review: three-cycle burst sphere spectrum

**Correctness — PASS.** The output-collision lemma shows that each nonconstant length-three window yields two changed outputs, and the only inter-window duplicate is an adjacent swap shared by neighboring windows with the explicit four-letter overlap condition. This proves the formula at every word length, independent of computation. The complete defect distribution follows from an exhaustive three-state transition classification; evaluating its zero-defect sector yields the sharp nonbinary extremal census. A standalone independent exhaustive program compares direct output enumeration, the defect identity, transfer coefficients, means and extremal recurrences on 218584 words over 27 parameter combinations.

**Originality — PASS, with residual uncertainty.** Chen–Milenkovic (arXiv:2610.15701v1, Sections III–V) introduce the cyclic channel and provide binary coding bounds/decoders, not the nonbinary sphere spectrum. A prior exact length-three *unrestricted* permutation sphere distribution is a distinct channel; a three-letter all-distinct input has six outputs under all permutations but only three under cyclic rotations. published-finding corpus comparisons and targeted published-literature searches found no equivalent cyclic statement, but unindexed prior work cannot be ruled out. Binary length-three equivalence to unrestricted permutation is known, not claimed as new.

**Value — PASS.** The result determines every nonbinary source-word output-ball-size population, the exact mean, and all maximizers through a rational enumerator. This is a natural finite-length ambiguity profile for the explicitly motivated DNA-related cyclic rotation channel and addresses information absent from redundancy inequalities. It makes no unsupported decoder or rate claim.

**Closest literature and limits.** The motivating full text is Yuan-Pon Chen and Olgica Milenkovic, *Correcting Burst Errors with Applications to DNA-Based Data Storage*, arXiv:2610.15701v1 (2026-10-11), https://arxiv.org/html/2610.15701v1. An existing unrestricted length-three sphere theorem is recorded under the opaque identifier 3371874f-7756-4cd8-898e-7f326673fdfb. Scope excludes repeated bursts, bursts with length varying up to three, and code-optimality results. Possible unindexed equivalent treatments remain a novelty risk.
