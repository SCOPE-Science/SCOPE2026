Under one exactly length-three cyclic substring permutation, the distinct-output sphere size of every word is \(2n-3-2H-K\), where \(H\) counts constant triples and \(K\) counts a precise four-letter overlap pattern. A three-state matrix gives the complete sphere-size distribution for every alphabet and length; for \(q\ge3\) the sharp maximum is \(2n-3\), with an explicit rational generating function counting all maximizers.
