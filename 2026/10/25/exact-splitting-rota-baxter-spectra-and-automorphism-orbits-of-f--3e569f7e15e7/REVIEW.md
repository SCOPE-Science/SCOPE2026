# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. Ordered exact factorizations map injectively to splitting operators because kernel and image recover both factors. The full subgroup list divides into cyclic and dihedral types. Direct intersection and size tests give exactly the unitary-divisor families and the parity-constrained dihedral/dihedral family. The automorphism orbit proof uses the explicit affine action on reflection indices and a compatible Chinese remainder system. The parity limit follows algebraically from the exact counts. The packaged verifier independently reproduces finite counts, operator identities and orbit numbers. Its finite checks support, but do not replace, the general proof.

## Originality

PASS against the inspected literature, with residual priority risk. Galt–Gubarev (arXiv:2404.14078, §§2,6) already establish the splitting construction and completeness for odd rotation orders. Ochoa Arango–Umbarila Martín (arXiv:2409.10428, Definition 1.2, Lemma 1.8 and Theorem 1.9) already classify abstract exact-factorization types up to exchange and count them. Neither inspected result states the embedded ordered-factorization generating polynomial, the finer image-cardinality spectrum, or the ordinary automorphism orbit sizes and multiplicities. The individual small-order cases at \(n=3\) and \(n=4\) are not claimed as novel numerical discoveries; the known eighteen splitting operators on \(D_8\) are acknowledged. The finite-group combinatorial formulas are derived explicitly rather than asserted from an absence of search hits. A stronger uninspected exact embedded-factorization census remains a plausible risk.

## Value

PASS. The whole image-size distribution and the orbit sizes provide invariants of a natural operator moduli problem that are lost when counting factorization types up to group isomorphism and swapping factors. The explicit parity-sensitive dihedral/dihedral term also implies a limiting structural law for random splitting operators, which the coarse exact-factorization number does not provide. The result applies uniformly at every rotation order \(n\ge3\), rather than adding one more low-order table entry.

## Limits and closest work

The even-order formula counts only splitting operators, not all Rota–Baxter operators. No claim of novel splitting construction, odd-order completeness, or small-order \(D_8\) classification is made. The proof is self-reviewed; independent external audit and exhaustive publication-priority checks have not been performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
