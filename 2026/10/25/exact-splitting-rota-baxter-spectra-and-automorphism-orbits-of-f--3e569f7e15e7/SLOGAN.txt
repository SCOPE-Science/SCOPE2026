For every \(n\ge3\), the image-size polynomial and ordinary automorphism-orbit spectrum of splitting group Rota–Baxter operators on \(D_{2n}\) are explicit in unitary divisors of \(n\); in odd rotation order these formulas cover every Rota–Baxter operator. At even rotation order, an exact dihedral/dihedral term produces a parity-driven dominance phenomenon as the number of distinct odd prime factors grows.
