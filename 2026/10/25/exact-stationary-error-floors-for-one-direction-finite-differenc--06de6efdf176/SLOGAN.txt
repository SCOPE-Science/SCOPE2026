For the one-direction forward-difference Newton method on any positive diagonal planar quadratic, uniform-circle sketches have exact stationary expected error \(h^2(a+b+\sqrt{ab})/12\), whereas uniformly signed coordinate sketches have \(h^2(a+b)/8\). The latter exceeds the former by exactly \(h^2(\sqrt a-\sqrt b)^2/24\), although coordinates have the faster worst-direction mean-square coupling rate when the curvature is anisotropic.
