For every complete bipartite graph with both parts of size at least two, the strong-majority edge index is \(2\) exactly for \(K_{2,2r}\), is \(4\) exactly for \(K_{3,4}\) and \(K_{4,5}\), and is \(3\) otherwise (up to exchanging parts). The two four-colour exceptions admit exhaustive finite obstruction certificates, while explicit periodic colourings cover the infinite families.
