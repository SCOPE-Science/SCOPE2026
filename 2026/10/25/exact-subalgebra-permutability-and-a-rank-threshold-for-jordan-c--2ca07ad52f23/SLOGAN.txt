For Jordan-chain Lie algebras \([h,e_i]=\lambda e_i+e_{i-1}\) over any finite field, a uniform Gaussian-binomial sum exactly counts permutable ordered pairs of subalgebras independently of \(\lambda\). As the field grows, their subalgebra-commutativity degree tends to \(0\) for Jordan lengths \(2,3\), to \(1/4\) for length \(4\), and to \(1\) for every length at least \(5\).
