For a point-supported derivative of order \(m\) in \(\mathbb R^n\), a positive compactly supported \(\phi\)-kernel constant near the origin gives a pointwise-zero transform with exact local-\(L^p\) threshold \(p\le n/(n+m)\). This quantifies a subunit-integrability obstruction for the general distributional \(\phi\)-transform, without changing the known support theorem for \(p\ge1\).
