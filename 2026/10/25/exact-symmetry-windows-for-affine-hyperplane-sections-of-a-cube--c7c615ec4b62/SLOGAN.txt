A hyperplane section of a cube is centrally symmetric precisely when it passes through the cube center or is a parallelotope. For normals with at least three nonzero coefficients, all noncentral symmetric cuts lie in the sharp offset window \([W-m,m]\), where \(W\) is the sum and \(m\) the maximum of the absolute normal coefficients; the window has positive length exactly when one coefficient exceeds the sum of the others.
