The exact distribution of \(\nu_3(C_n)\) over every ternary block \(0\leq n<3^L\) has the rational generating function \(\sum_{L\geq0}z^L\sum_{n<3^L}u^{\nu_3(C_n)}=(1+(1-2u)z)/(1-2(1+u)z+3uz^2)\). It yields explicit mean and variance at each block length and a Gaussian limit with centering \(L/2\) and scaling \(\sqrt{L/2}\).
