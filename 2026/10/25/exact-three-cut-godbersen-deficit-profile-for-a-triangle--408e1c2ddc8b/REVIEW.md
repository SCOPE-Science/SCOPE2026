# Review: Exact three-cut Godbersen deficit profile for a triangle

## Correctness

**PASS.** The removed corner areas give \(2\operatorname{area}(K)=1-q\). The planar mixed-area facet formula applied to the six listed edges gives \(V_2(K,-K)=1-s+ab+ac+bc\); substituting \(ab+ac+bc=(s^2-q)/2\) yields the stated deficit. At fixed \(q\), the deficit is a concave function of \(s\), with derivative \((1-s)/(1-q)\). The endpoint comparison is strictly positive throughout \(0<q<1/2\), and the stationary level \(s=1\) is feasible exactly from the transition range onward. The proof states the full regime and equality cases.

## Value

**PASS.** The family is a natural deformation of the simplex extremizer under the exact constraint of fixed removed area. The result supplies a complete extremizer classification and a nontrivial transition: balanced cuts maximize the deficit up to \(q=1/3\), after which the maximum is a constant \(1/2\) on the entire level \(a+b+c=1\). It is a family-specific benchmark for stability questions, not a general stability theorem.
