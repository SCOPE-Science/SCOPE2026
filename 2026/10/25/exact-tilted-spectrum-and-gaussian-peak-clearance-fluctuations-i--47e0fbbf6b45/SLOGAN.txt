The canonical zigzag-fence order polytope has an exactly solvable exponential tilt by the sum of its local peak-facet clearances, with principal eigenvalue determined by \(k\cot k=t/2\) or its hyperbolic continuation. Its normalized clearance obeys a Gaussian limit with variance \(24/\pi^4-2/\pi^2\) per peak and a full explicit Legendre-transform large-deviation principle; the first-order mean is compatible with previously known one-coordinate sampling asymptotics.
