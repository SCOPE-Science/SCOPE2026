For every reduced rational slope \(p/q\), the one-step tortoise-image complexity of the periodic Christoffel word is exactly \(n+1-\min\{n-1,\lfloor(q-1)/p\rfloor\}\) below length \(q\), then \(\min\{q,n-\lfloor(q-1)/p\rfloor\}\) thereafter. It reaches full injectivity at the sharp length \(q+\lfloor(q-1)/p\rfloor\), revealing a periodic boundary effect absent from irrational Sturmian codings.
