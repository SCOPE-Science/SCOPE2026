For equal-rate two-species antagonistic adsorption on the infinite line with repeated Poisson attempts, the density of sites already permanently blocked at time \(t\) is exactly \(\tfrac12(1-e^{-t})^2\exp[-(1-e^{-t})]\). This proves the previously numerically conjectured jammed occupied fraction \(1-1/(2e)\), and yields its exact exponentially decaying late-time blocking deficit.
