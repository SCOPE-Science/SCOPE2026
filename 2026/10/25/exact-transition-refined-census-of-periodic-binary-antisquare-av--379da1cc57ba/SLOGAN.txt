Periodically good binary words, whose two-sided repetition avoids every antisquare except `01` and `10`, have the complete transition-refined census \(\sum_{n,t}c_{n,t}z^nu^t=-2z\partial_z\log(1-z-u^2z^4-u^4z^6)\). The formula yields exact counts for every circumference and change count, primitive-necklace counts, and the asymptotic \(g_n=2\psi^n+O(1)\) for \(\psi^3=\psi^2+1\).
