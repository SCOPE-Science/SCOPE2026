For two agents who identically value each indivisible item at \(+1\) or \(-1\), the exact number of weighted-EF1 allocations has an explicit two-binomial-layer formula, and all allocations are Pareto optimal. Under uniform random assignment, a fixed imbalance between goods and chores makes fairness exponentially rare when entitlements are unequal, whereas balanced signs give a universal central-binomial fraction and a Gaussian crossover.
