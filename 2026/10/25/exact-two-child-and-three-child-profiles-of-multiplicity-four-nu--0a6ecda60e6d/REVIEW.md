# Scientific assessment: exact offspring degrees at multiplicity four

## Correctness — PASS

The proof begins with the necessary and sufficient Kunz inequalities, derives the individual minimality and effectiveness tests, and enumerates all six families in which any two of the three nonmultiplicity Apéry generators can simultaneously be effective. The three-way intersection consists of precisely one Kunz triple in each genus; correcting the three pair counts for this intersection gives the closed rational two-child series. The exceptional ordinary genus-three semigroup contributes a fourth effective generator. An independent direct-decomposition checker verified the degree counts, pair families, and coefficients for all genera through 90. These computations support, but do not replace, the infinite symbolic counting proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The relevant 2026 study *Internal numerical semigroups* (arXiv:2608.19984v1) develops internal/leaf algorithms and aggregate counts, not an exact child-degree partition at multiplicity four. The fully inspected multiplicity-four section of García-Sánchez, Marín-Aragón and Robles-Pérez (arXiv:1803.06879v1) enumerates all multiplicity-four semigroups, while its child-degree classification is for multiplicity three. The existing genus-preserving multiplicity-four leaf census settles zero-child counts but does not count the two- or three-child internal strata. O'Dorney's 2024 paper establishes unrestricted-genus asymptotics, not these exact fixed-multiplicity formulas. Targeted exact-statement, alias and parameter searches found no equivalent result. Residual risk: an unindexed or differently phrased low-multiplicity child-degree table may contain an equivalent formula; mathematical priority has not been independently established.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The number of children is the intrinsic local branching invariant of the numerical-semigroup tree. The result completely determines the higher branching strata at multiplicity four: exact counts of two-child semigroups at every genus, unique three-child semigroups in every genus after the root, a four-child exception, and an exact rational generating function. This complements known aggregate and leaf counts, reveals a simple period-five quasipolynomial, and makes fixed-multiplicity branching quantitatively explicit rather than giving isolated small tables.

## Limits

The theorem uses the full tree, not the tree retaining fixed multiplicity. It does not cover higher multiplicities. Testing through genus 90 is finite; the infinite statements rest on the six-family proof. Bibliographic uniqueness and independent mathematical audit remain open.
