At multiplicity \(4\), the number of genus-\(g\) numerical semigroups with exactly two children has generating function \(z^5(1+z+z^3)/((1-z)(1-z^5))\), hence grows as \(3g/5+O(1)\). There is exactly one semigroup with three children at each genus \(g\ge4\), and the ordinary genus-\(3\) semigroup is the sole four-child case.
