The number of two-colour closed-neighbourhood conflict-free assignments of \(\operatorname{GP}(n,k)\) equals \(2^{\deg\gcd_{\mathbb F_2[t]}(t^n+1,h_k(t))}\), with an explicit polynomial \(h_k\). In the Petersen family \(\operatorname{GP}(n,2)\), the answer jumps from \(2\) or \(4\) to \(32\) or \(64\) exactly when \(5\mid n\).
