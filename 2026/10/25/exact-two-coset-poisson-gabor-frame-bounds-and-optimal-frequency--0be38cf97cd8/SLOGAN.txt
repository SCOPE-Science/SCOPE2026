For the Poisson window \(g_w(t)=1/(t^2+w^2)\), the union of integer time shifts and two frequency cosets \(\mathbb Z\cup(\mathbb Z+\delta)\) has explicit sharp Gabor-frame bounds for every \(0<\delta<1\). The balanced shift \(\delta=1/2\) is uniquely best conditioned, while the optimal lower bound collapses quadratically as the cosets coalesce.
