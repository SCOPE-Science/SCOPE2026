For every \(d\ge3\), the volume-deficit Hessian at an inscribed regular \(d\)-simplex has exactly two positive levels: \(V_*\) with multiplicity \(d\) and \(2dV_* /(d+1)\) with multiplicity \((d+1)(d-2)/2\); rotations account for the entire kernel. In dimension two only the first level is present, and in every dimension the spectrum gives sharp quadratic stability in intrinsic distance to the regular-simplex orbit.
