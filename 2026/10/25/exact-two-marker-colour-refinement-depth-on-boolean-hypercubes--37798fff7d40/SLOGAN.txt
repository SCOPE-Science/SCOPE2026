With two independently marked vertices of the binary Hamming cube, every colour-refinement round is exactly the partition by the two distances truncated at that round's visibility radius. The sharp number of rounds is \(d-1\) for adjacent markers, \(d-2\) for distinct nonadjacent nonantipodal markers, and \(\lfloor(d-1)/2\rfloor\) for antipodal markers, with precisely \((t+1)(d-t+1)\) terminal orbit classes.
