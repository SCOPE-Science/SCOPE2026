# Scientific review

## Correctness (PASS)
The two scalar reduced coefficients are obtained by exact inversion of the positive-definite two-by-two Galerkin Gram matrix. Strict Cauchy–Schwarz gives the positive determinant cofactors; affine dependence makes their signs exactly those of the interpolation barycentric factors, including extrapolation. The three-dimensional witness has positive diagonal stiffness for the entire stated domain and positive exact solutions; an exact rational computation exhibits a negative Galerkin component. Dimension-one and dimension-two minimality follows from scalar positivity in a one-dimensional reduced span, or exactness when the snapshots span the entire ambient space. The standalone verifier reproduces 9,250 rational coefficient and Galerkin-orthogonality identities plus the exact witness. Main risk: higher-dimensional analogue with more than two snapshots is not covered and must not be inferred.

## Originality (PASS; residual priority uncertainty)
The September 2026 primary source defines affine symmetric-coercive snapshot Galerkin ROMs, but its Theorem 5.1 and conclusions provide approximation errors rather than the exact sign of each two-snapshot Galerkin coefficient. A 2011 comprehensive reduced-basis review discusses Galerkin projection, stability and error estimates but does not state the exact two-snapshot cone criterion. A 2002 paper in precisely the same symmetric-coercive setting has only been verified at abstract level; its full comparison was not available and remains a risk. Separate semantic searches of public mathematical records returned positivity criteria for other algorithms (MINRES, conjugate gradients, two-step Lanczos) and did not identify equivalent coverage for this snapshot ROM. The search outcome is not a proof of uniqueness; the full 2002 source and any uncatalogued textbooks remain priority risks. The sharp three-dimensional extrapolation witness and exact if-and-only-if cone law constitute the proposed distinct claim.

## Mathematical value (PASS)
Reduced Galerkin surrogates can violate physically important sign constraints even when the full-order family is uniformly coercive and positive. The result gives a precise, checkable interpolation domain in which positivity is automatic from just two positive snapshot vectors, without imposing nonnegative reduced coordinates, and a sharp minimal dimension at which extending the same guarantee beyond the training interval can fail. This clarifies when surrogate positivity follows from the model's affine structure rather than from an approximation-error estimate; it does not claim universal positivity after extrapolation.

## Sources and limits
Primary: Hannukainen–Toresen, arXiv:2609.34865v1, sections 2–5. Comparative review: Quarteroni–Rozza–Manzoni, *Certified Reduced Basis Approximation for Parametrized PDEs and Applications* (2011). Additional priority risk: Maday–Patera–Turinici (2002), available abstract and references only. The result concerns two independent snapshots, fixed forcing, affine SPD matrices. Numerical testing supports algebra but is not the infinite-dimensional proof.
