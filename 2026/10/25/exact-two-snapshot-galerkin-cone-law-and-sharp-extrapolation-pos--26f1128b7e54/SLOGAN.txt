For any affine symmetric positive-definite system with fixed forcing, the two-snapshot Galerkin approximation stays in the positive cone of the snapshots exactly between their sample parameters: its two coefficients have the respective signs of \(t-y\) and \(y-s\). This dimension-free interpolation guarantee is sharp for physical positivity: a three-dimensional diagonal positive system has strictly positive exact solutions everywhere but a negative reduced coordinate under extrapolation, and no smaller dimension can do so.
