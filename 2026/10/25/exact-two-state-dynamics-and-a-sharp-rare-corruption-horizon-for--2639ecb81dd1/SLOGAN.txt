For the sampled-quantile equality update in a two-level scalar Kaczmarz problem, odd-sample rank controls the sign of temporal correlation, while the median produces independent iterates with an exact hypergeometric corruption probability. Their first corrupted-iterate horizon has a sharp \(s^{-1/2}e^{-\kappa_\beta s}\) rare-event prefactor and an explicit finite-population zero-risk cutoff; this result does not extend to generic matrices or the different threshold-accepted algorithm.
