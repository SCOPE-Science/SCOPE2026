# Scientific review

## Correctness
The stationary telegraph resolvent and its velocity-conditioned density were independently reconstructed. Poisson marking converts the cross-time current covariance to the joint occupation intensity of one anchored particle. Splitting by occurrence of a reset gives an exact renewal identity, and integrating the explicit free telegraph resolvent gives the stated Laplace transform. The initial slope follows from actual crossing and reset hazards; the plateau follows from independent future renewal intervals conditional on fixed anchors. Cancellation of the putative leading reset branch pole leaves the claimed positive square-root branch coefficient, yielding the sharp long-time correction. Direct regenerative particle simulations agree with independent Laplace inversion within statistical error. No finite simulation is used as proof of an all-time theorem.

## Originality
The 2023 Di Bello et al. paper determines one-time annealed and quenched current statistics in exactly this resetting RTP gas but does not evaluate its stationary two-time current covariance or spectral transform. The 2023 Jose et al. paper analyzes current statistics without stochastic resetting. Previously reported Brownian-reset-gas autocovariance is not the present two-velocity finite-speed law and cannot determine either its ballistic derivative or separate reversal-rate dependence. Searches for both the telegraph covariance transform and RTP two-time current laws found no covering statement. General particle-marking and renewal principles remain established tools, and an unnoticed exact specialization is a residual priority risk.

## Scientific value
Two-time current fluctuations characterize memory and spectral relaxation, which cannot be recovered from a single-time stationary current distribution. The transform describes the full lags rather than a selected numerical case, while the finite initial slope and the exact exponentially weighted square-root tail provide experimentally distinguishable finite-speed and relaxation signatures. The nonzero plateau identifies disorder-induced failure of decay in annealed current covariance despite independent particle mixing.

## Scope and uncertainties
The anchors must be Poisson, the particle dynamics independent, velocity resampled fairly after each reset, and the displacement process stationary. The tail is a fixed-parameter limit for positive reversal rate. Priority has not undergone an independent review.
