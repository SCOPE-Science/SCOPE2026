For a stationary Poisson gas of independently resetting run-and-tumble particles, the complete two-time crossing-current covariance has an explicit Laplace transform, a finite ballistic initial slope, and a nonzero long-lag plateau. Its approach to that plateau is sharply proportional to \(e^{-rt}t^{-1/2}\), with an exact coefficient depending on both resetting and velocity-reversal rates.
