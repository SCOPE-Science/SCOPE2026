For a uniform sample, the covariance of each rank-specific spacing with the unbiased sample variance has an exact concave quadratic profile: endpoint gaps have negative covariance with dispersion, while sufficiently central gaps have positive covariance. Exact zero covariances occur at infinitely many and only Pell-indexed sample sizes, beginning with 6, 25, and 96, and the asymptotic sign boundary lies at \(\tfrac12(1\pm1/\sqrt3)\).
