For every nondegenerate convex quadrilateral, the exact dilation threshold at which its four vertex translates fill its dilated convex hull is \(1+(1-pq)/(1+\max\{p,q\})\), where \(p,q\) are the two shorter-to-longer diagonal-segment ratios. This gives a complete affine classification, with threshold \(1\) exactly for parallelograms and every value below \(2\) attainable.
