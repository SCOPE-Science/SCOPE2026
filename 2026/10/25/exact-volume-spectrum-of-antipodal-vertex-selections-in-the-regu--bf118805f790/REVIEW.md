# Review

## Correctness

PASS. The coordinate model fixes the scale, and exactly one of six antipodal pairs is selected, giving \(64\) cases. The verification artifact uses exact arithmetic in \(\mathbb Z[\varphi]\), exhaustively finds supporting facets, and sums exact signed determinant contributions. It asserts the four volume levels and multiplicities; no floating-point calculation is used in the proof path.

## Originality

PASS on a best-knowledge basis. The closest primary literature studies general antipodal polytopes and a different five-pair three-dimensional counterexample, while the related asymmetric-polygon paper is planar. No checked source states this exact six-pair regular-icosahedron spectrum. Search absence is not proof of global novelty; the residual risks in the structured review remain.

## Value

PASS. The full exact classification of a canonical, highly symmetric three-dimensional antipodal point set is a useful finite benchmark for extremal polytope volume, motivated by the documented gap between planar and higher-dimensional antipodal-polytope behavior. It is not presented as a general theorem.
