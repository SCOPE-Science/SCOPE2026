For the standard edge-length-\(2\) regular icosahedron, the \(64\) convex hulls formed by choosing one vertex from each antipodal pair have exactly four volumes, with multiplicities \(12,20,20,12\). The exact minimum and maximum are \((4+2\varphi)/3\) and \((6+8\varphi)/3\), where \(\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\).
