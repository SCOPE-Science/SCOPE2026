For fixed \(k\ge4\), weak limits of exchangeable \(k\)-wise independent Bernoulli sums with marginal \(\lambda/n\) are exactly the nonnegative-integer laws whose factorial moments equal \(\lambda^j\) below order \(k\) and are at most \(\lambda^k\) at order \(k\). Every such law is attainable; any deficit at order \(k\) can escape to counts of order \(n\).
