For every \(2\le a\le b\), the attainable wild numbers of all surjective red/blue edge-colourings of \(K_{a,b}\) are exactly the integers from \(\max\{0,2(a+b-1)-ab\}\) to \(a+b-2\). The minimum is \(2\) on \(K_{2,b}\), \(1\) on \(K_{3,3}\), and \(0\) on every remaining graph in the family; there are no gaps.
