For the 28 bitangents of the smooth Fermat quartic \(X^4+Y^4+Z^4=0\), triple concurrence occurs over algebraically closed fields of characteristic \(p\ge5\) **only** at \(p=5\) and \(p=17\), producing respectively 16 and 48 triple points. Every such characteristic has exactly 15 quadruple points, and an exact cyclotomic determinant-norm sieve explains why no other exceptional primes can occur.
