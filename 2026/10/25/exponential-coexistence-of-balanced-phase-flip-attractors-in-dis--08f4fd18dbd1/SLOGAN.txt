An even-leaf discrete Huygens star possesses \(\binom{m}{m/2}/2\) distinct balanced phase-flip two-cycles that all become locally attracting in the exact high-step window \(h_m<h<\pi\). Their number grows exponentially with the leaf count, their common stability window has width \(4/[\pi(m+1)]+O(m^{-2})\), and every one persists under sufficiently small independent positive edge-weight perturbations.
