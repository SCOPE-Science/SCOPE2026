For vertex–facet incidence spaces of \(m\)-cross-polytopes, the closure-meeting and inseparability graph clique complexes are spheres of dimensions \(m-1\) and \(2^{m-1}-1\), respectively. Their genuine joint-intersection nerves remain homotopy equivalent to \(S^{m-1}\): graph flag completion can break the duality by an exponential gap in dimension.
