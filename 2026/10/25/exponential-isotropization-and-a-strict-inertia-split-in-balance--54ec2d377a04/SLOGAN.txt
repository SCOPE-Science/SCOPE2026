Balanced complete multipartite rank-two matroid base polytopes are never exactly isotropic in their Euclidean volume covariance: within-class directions have strictly larger variance than between-class directions, with an explicit positive beta-function gap. For a fixed number \(r\ge3\) of parallel classes, the relative variance gap decays at the sharp exponential rate \(C_r\sqrt m\,\rho_r^m\), where \(\rho_r=r^r/[2^r(r-1)^{r-1}]<1\).
