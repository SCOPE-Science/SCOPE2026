For partial proper \(q\)-colorings of a cycle, the entire color-relabeling quotient has an exact reduced Euler characteristic given by a fixed-point and transfer-matrix formula. Its Euler characteristic stays bounded as the cycle grows for \(q\le4\), but for every \(q\ge5\) the quotient's total rational Betti number grows at least exponentially with base \(\frac{q-2+\sqrt{q(q-4)}}2\).
