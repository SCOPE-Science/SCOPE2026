# Verification and scope

The general upper estimate is proved by splitting the representing \(L^2\) domain, bounding the restricted high-degree composition factors by a Hilbert–Schmidt monomial tail, and approximating by rank at most \(2N\). Geometric decay of the tail is an analytic consequence of \(r<1\). The necessity of no common contact for nonconstant linear fractions invokes an inspected published compactness criterion for the identical weighted space, and the published Hardy classification. These are the actual proof dependencies; the program is not an infinite-dimensional certificate.

Run `python verify.py` with Python 3.9 or newer. Its exact rational arithmetic checks the unweighted Bergman moment formula, the affine maps' pointwise separation identity and contact geometry, and the finite coefficients of the cross-product; floating-point tail tests probe the predicted exponential envelope at several finite cutoffs. The checks do not certify the universal operator-norm bound, publication priority, or any independent audit.

**Scientific limitations:** the singular-value exponent is only bounded above; general symbols with \(\delta=1\) are outside the classification. Equivalent earlier formulations may exist; independent scientific validation has not been performed.
