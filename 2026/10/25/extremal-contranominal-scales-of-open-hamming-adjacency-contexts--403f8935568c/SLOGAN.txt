For the open, distance-one incidence context of a \(q\)-ary Hamming graph, the largest contranominal scale has dimension \(q\) in dimensions at most two and \(\max\{q,4\}\) in higher dimensions. All maxima of dimension at least four are rigid full coordinate lines, two-parallel-pairs configurations at \(q=4\), or binary-face parity tetrahedra; their exact counts exhibit an unbounded Boolean-suborder dimension despite a uniform open-neighborhood VC-dimension bound of three.
