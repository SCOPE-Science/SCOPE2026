For each integer \(k\ge3\), a planar convex body of constant \(k\)-order width \(c\) is Minkowski-extreme modulo translations exactly when its curvature-radius density takes only the values \(0\) and \(c\) almost everywhere. Consequently every least-area body in this class exists and must have this two-valued form, reducing a generalized Blaschke–Lebesgue problem to a constrained class of curvature selections.
