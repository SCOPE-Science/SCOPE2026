There are exactly \(F_n\) nonisomorphic \(n\)-element prelinear quasi-Nelson chains, and the mismatch between their canonical consistency and undeterminedness operators has a complete Fibonacci-refined count. Under uniform sampling of chain isomorphism classes, this mismatch converges to a geometric law with mass \(\varphi^{-(j+2)}\) at each \(j\ge0\), so asymptotically \(\varphi^{-1}\) of the chains have nondual recovery operators.
