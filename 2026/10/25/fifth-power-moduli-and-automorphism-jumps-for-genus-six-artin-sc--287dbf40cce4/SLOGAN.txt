The genus-six characteristic-two Artin–Schreier fibre-product covers are isomorphic as unlabelled Galois covers exactly when \(\lambda^5=\mu^5\). Their cover-automorphism groups have orders \(960\), \(16\), or \(4\), according as \(\lambda=0\), \(\lambda^5=1\), or neither, revealing an exceptional symmetry class within one Ekedahl–Oort stratum.
