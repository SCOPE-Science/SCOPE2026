## Correctness — PASS
The Paley arc set is defined explicitly and all oriented simplices are uniquely specified. The standalone certificate enumerates face counts \((11,55,110,55)\), checks the integral boundary identity, classifies the complete set of 462 induced five-vertex subcomplexes, and proves every exceptional induced complex is a triangular-bipyramid sphere. Exact matrix elimination with tracked unimodular changes identifies the integral cycle lattice. The two exact Smith computations yield diagonal lists \(1^{54},2\) and \(1^{65}\), which prove the homology type and integral generation rather than rational generation alone. Proposition 3.5 in Govc supplies torsion-freeness for remaining five-vertex induced tournament complexes. The certificate was replayed successfully from the packaged script.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
Govc's Proposition 3.4 gives the whole complex's homotopy type, but not a count of minimum induced sphere supports, their affine action, or their collective inclusion-image lattice. The 2013 Paley tournament reference concerns orientation and domination rather than directed-flag integral generators. Searches for the same object, induced two-sphere supports, affine orbit, and Smith-lattice generation against research records returned related but different tournament sizes or unrelated eleven-vertex triangulations. A conceptually equivalent computation in an unindexed dataset cannot be ruled out. The result does not claim uniqueness of the abstract homology computation.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The canonical eleven-point Paley tournament is the unique doubly regular tournament at the first size whose directed flag complex carries torsion in this family. The finding identifies all minimum-vertex carriers of its integral two-homology and a full affine symmetry orbit, and proves these elementary local sphere cycles collectively account even for the global two-torsion. Neither wedge/Moore classification nor mere homology dimensions gives this generator-level description. This is a motivated inclusion-level topology calculation rather than a convenient arbitrary slice.

## Scientific limitations
Only this eleven-point tournament and induced supports of size at most five are classified; no general Paley-family theorem or external independent verification is claimed. Bibliographic incompleteness remains a risk.
