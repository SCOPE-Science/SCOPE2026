For every planar disk of radius \(0<r<R\), the entire finite-level \(R\)-ball illumination family is an explicitly parameterized family of concentric disks, saturating exactly at level \(\pi(R^2-r^2)\) and radius \(2R-r\). Its radius grows with the known small-level exponent \(2/3\), but approaches saturation **quadratically** in the remaining level deficit.
