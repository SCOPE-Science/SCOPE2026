In the classical genus-three hyperelliptic \(D_6\) pencil \(y^2=x^7-tx^4+x\), characteristic \(31\) is the first prime at least \(5\) where a smooth member with nonzero marked parameter is superspecial. Its superspecial parameters are exactly \(0,12,-12\) over \(\overline{\mathbf F}_{31}\), determined by an exact pair of Cartier-coefficient polynomial gcds.
