For Haar-random nearby central sections of a regular dodecahedron, the infinitesimal polygon-type transition graph is exactly \(6\leftrightarrow8\leftrightarrow10\): six-to-ten changes require at least second-order probability, while the two one-way transition fluxes are \(30h/\pi^2\) and \(15p/\pi^2\) per radian, with explicit spherical lengths \(h\) and \(p\).
