For binary non-overlapping codes with exactly four ones per word, the primitive-necklace packing bound is tight at every length from \(5\) to \(11\), but first fails at length \(12\): the exact optimum is \(39\) codewords versus \(40\) primitive necklaces. An exhaustive \(39\)-clique partition of all \(128\) eligible words proves the strict upper bound, and a matching codebook attains it.
