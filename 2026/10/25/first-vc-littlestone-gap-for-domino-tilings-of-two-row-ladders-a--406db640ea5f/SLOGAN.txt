The \(2\times5\) ladder has eight domino tilings, VC dimension \(2\), and Littlestone dimension \(3\), the first strict static–adaptive shattering gap among two-row ladders. Connecting copies by bridges that no perfect matching can use yields connected planar bipartite subcubic graphs with exact dimensions \(2s\) and \(3s\), so the gap is unbounded.
