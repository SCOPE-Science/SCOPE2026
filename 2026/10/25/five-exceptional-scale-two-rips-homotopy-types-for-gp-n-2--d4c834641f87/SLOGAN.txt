The five finite exceptions immediately below the stable generalized Petersen circle range have exact scale-two graph-metric Vietoris–Rips types \(S^2,S^1,S^2,S^1,\bigvee^3S^2\), in the order \(n=8,9,10,11,12\). Exhaustive acyclic Morse certificates upgrade the boundary case \(GP(12,2)\) from previously known mod-two homology to a simply connected, torsion-free wedge of three integral two-spheres.
