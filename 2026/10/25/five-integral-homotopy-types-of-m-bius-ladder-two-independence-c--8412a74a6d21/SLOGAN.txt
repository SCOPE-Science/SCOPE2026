For the five smallest nondegenerate Möbius ladders beyond \(K_{3,3}\), the two-independence complexes have exact integral homotopy types \(S^2,\ \bigvee^9 S^3,\ \bigvee^6 S^3,\ S^4,\ \bigvee^{31}S^5\), respectively. Exhaustive acyclic face-poset matchings, not mod-two Betti numbers alone, certify these finite classifications.
