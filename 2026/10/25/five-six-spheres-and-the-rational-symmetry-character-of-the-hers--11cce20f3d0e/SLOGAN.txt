The simplicial complex of edge sets contained in a Hamiltonian spanning path of the eleven-vertex Herschel graph has integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^5 S^6\), despite having pure dimension nine. Its five-dimensional rational top homology realizes \((\operatorname{sgn}\oplus\operatorname{Std})\boxtimes\mathbf 1\oplus\operatorname{Std}\boxtimes\varepsilon\) under \(\operatorname{Aut}(H)\cong S_3\times C_2\), and has no nonzero full-group invariant.
