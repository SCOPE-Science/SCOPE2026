The simplicial complex of all triangle-free graphs on five labeled vertices has integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^5S^3\vee S^4\); its natural \(\mathfrak S_5\)-action is \(\operatorname{sgn}\otimes\mathbb Q^5\) in third homology and trivial in fourth homology. The fifth graph vertex is the first point where twelve pentagons separate triangle-free graphs from the already studied bipartite graph complex.
