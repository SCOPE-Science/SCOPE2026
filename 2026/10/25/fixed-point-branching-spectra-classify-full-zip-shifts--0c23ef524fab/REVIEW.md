# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For τ:A→B onto, the full zip shift on B^{Z<0}×A^{Z≥0} has precisely |τ^-1(y_{-1})| predecessors of y; the only unconstrained predecessor coordinate is x0. Every fixed point p^a consists of constant future letter a and constant past τ(a), giving c_d=d n_d where n_d counts transition fibres of cardinality d. Any homeomorphism conjugacy preserves fixed points and preimage cardinalities, hence each n_d. Conversely equal unordered fibre sizes yield bijections α:A→A′, β:B→B′ with τ′α=βτ; coordinatewise relabeling on two halves is an actual conjugacy. Thus conjugacy classes are indexed by partitions of N=|A| (or partitions with exactly M parts if |B|=M), and realizable polynomials have d|c_d and Σc_d=N. Periodic points have N^k possible length-k future words, ζ=(1−Nz)^−1; a two-sided A-shift factor onto Xτ and a one-sided A-shift factor from Xτ sandwich entropy at log N. Count formulas include all boundary cases N≥2 and positive fibre sizes.

## originality

PASS

The 2025 primary zip-shift paper gives the exact model and predecessor machinery, and related work covers N^k periodic counts; these do not directly assert the complete criterion under arbitrary topological conjugacies or the partition enumeration for unequal fibre sizes. The proof supplies the missing necessity from intrinsic preimage degrees at fixed points and sufficiency by relabeling. Novelty is qualified, particularly against equivalent classification of one-step noninvertible symbolic endomorphisms.
