For every full one-block finite-alphabet zip shift, the polynomial counting fixed points by their numbers of immediate preimages is a complete invariant of topological conjugacy: its coefficients recover the unordered fibre sizes of the transition map. There are exactly \(p(N)\) conjugacy classes with \(N\) future symbols, although all have entropy \(\log N\) and zeta function \(1/(1-Nz)\).
