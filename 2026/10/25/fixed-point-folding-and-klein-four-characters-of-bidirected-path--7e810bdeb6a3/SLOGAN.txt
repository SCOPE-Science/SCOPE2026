For the multipath complex of every bidirected path, its natural Klein-four symmetries have fixed-point spaces that fold exactly to smaller bidirected-path complexes (or to the empty set). The resulting complete integral symmetry character is eight-periodic, and the full symmetry quotient has nonzero reduced rational homology precisely when the path length is congruent to \(0\) or \(7\) modulo \(8\).
