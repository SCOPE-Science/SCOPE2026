For a uniform permutation \(\Pi\) and any fixed \(\eta\), the covariance between their commutator's fixed-point count and the fixed-point count of \(\Pi\) is exactly \(f(n-f)/[(n-1)(n-2)]\), where \(f\) is the number of fixed points of \(\eta\). When \(\eta\) is a derangement, every individual commutation-agreement indicator is independent of every individual fixed-point indicator, yet the two aggregate counts remain dependent.
