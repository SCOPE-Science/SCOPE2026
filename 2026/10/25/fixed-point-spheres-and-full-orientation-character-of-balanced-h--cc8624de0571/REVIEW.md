# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Transpose orbits are n diagonal singleton edges plus n(n-1)/2 pairs, equivalently loops and undirected edges with n forbidden incident stars. The fixed orbit complex D_n has n minimal nonfaces T_i. Alexander dual nerve of complements of the T_i has all proper intersections nonempty and total intersection empty because each T_i contains unique loop, hence dual sphere S^{n-2}, giving integral homology of D_n in dimension C(n,2)-1. D_n is simply connected for n>=4 because all triples are faces; n=3 missing triangle boundaries fill via a different loop. Hurewicz/Whitehead upgrades homology to homotopy sphere, n=2 directly S0. For ambient X_n, natural Alexander dual of row-column forbidden stars and its 2-factor boundary join yields full group action: multiplying nerve degree by ambient-edge permutation sign gives chi((sigma,tau)s^e)=sgn(sigma)^(n+1) sgn(tau)^(n+1) (-1)^{e(n-1)(n+2)/2}. Rational transfer then gives quotient top iff n=1 mod4. Independent mod2 fixed complexes n=2,3,4 gave sole Betti dimensions 0,2,5 and exact signed Lefschetz for five representative elements each n, 15 trace checks.

## originality

PASS

Singh proves the underlying X_n sphere; Reiner–Roberts Theorem 3.3 treats side-preserving S_n×S_n homology. Neither of these results determines the geometric fixed space for swapping the bipartition nor the sign of its integral top orientation, which is required for full G representation/quotient. Published equivariant balanced bipartite Morse results concern oriented vertex-edge incidence forests, a different simplicial complex and drastically different homology rank.
