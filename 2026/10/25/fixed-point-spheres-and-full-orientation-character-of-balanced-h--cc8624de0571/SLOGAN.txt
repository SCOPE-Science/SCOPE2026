For every \(n\ge2\), the bipartition-interchange fixed-point space of the \((n-1)\)-matching complex of \(K_{n,n}\) has homotopy type \(S^{\binom n2-1}\), although the whole complex is a sphere of dimension \((n-1)^2-1\). The complete integral orientation character of the full automorphism group yields nonzero reduced rational homology for its full quotient exactly when \(n\equiv1\pmod4\).
