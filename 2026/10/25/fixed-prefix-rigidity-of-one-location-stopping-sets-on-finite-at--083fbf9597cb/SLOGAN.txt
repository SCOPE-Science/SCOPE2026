On a finite Poisson space with positive atomic intensities, every stopping set revealing at most one occupied location is a fixed ordered-prefix search, stopped at the first nonempty site; no observation-dependent reordering is possible. The exact number of such rules on \(n\) labeled atoms is \(\sum_{r=0}^n n!/(n-r)!\), and every rule is realized pointwise by an atomic continuous-time decision tree.
