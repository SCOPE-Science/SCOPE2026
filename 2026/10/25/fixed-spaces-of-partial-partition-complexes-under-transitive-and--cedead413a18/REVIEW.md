# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

For a transitive action, support invariance forces an invariant full partition with one block orbit; the unique fixed point inside each such simplex is its barycenter, and imprimitivity blocks correspond to intermediate subgroups. For a Young action with every factor of size at least two, all admissible vertex-orbits are exactly macroblock unions or singleton-orbits inside one macroblock; disjoint support is the precise compatibility condition. Fixed barycentric coordinates give the stated PL orbit complex. Sequentially duplicating the singleton vertices yields split homology sequences because each attaching link already lies in a cone; Gottstein's torsion-free integral homology theorem supplies the closed Betti formula, with the empty-complex augmented convention checked at the boundary. The two standalone checkers independently exhaust finite examples, including integral-rank predictions cross-checked over the two-element field. The finite tests corroborate rather than prove the universal statements.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

Gottstein's complete arXiv HTML (arXiv:2311.11190, current revision, sections 1–7) establishes the complex, its ordinary homology and its full automorphism group, but does not state geometric fixed-space classifications or the Young-factor orbit model. The homology-rank theorem is acknowledged as the input for the explicit new fixed-space Betti formula. Arone and Brantner (arXiv:1801.01491) study Young fixed points of the distinct proper partition-**poset order complex**, not this complex of disjoint subsets. Its abstract and publication description were examined; complete full text was unavailable, so unindexed equivalent formulations remain a documented risk. Published-result searches yielded related but not covering homology characters for different combinatorial complexes.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The combined result determines full geometric fixed spaces across two natural, infinite and complementary classes of permutation actions on the same simplicial family. It distinguishes primitive from imprimitive transitive symmetry through fixed-space components, recovers subgroup lattices in regular actions with nonabelian conjugation symmetry, and explains the onset of positive-dimensional Young fixed spaces. The exact torsion-free integral Betti formula is independent of individual Young factor sizes, so it gives an entire family of nontrivial topological invariants not available from the previously published nonequivariant Betti numbers.

## Scope and limitations

Arbitrary intransitive subgroups outside the full Young class are not classified. The integral rank formula does not assert a canonical module splitting under all external symmetries. The subgroup-system correspondence and link-inclusion splitting are known tools; the new claim is their specific fixed-space geometric identification and uniform combination. Full-text access to the older partition-poset paper was limited; it has a different underlying complex, but residual priority risk persists.
