# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. Haar-preserving unimodular coordinates yield the five explicit integer linear forms. Odd moments vanish by inversion; pair moments vanish by surjective torus homomorphisms; all four-moments except the identified one vanish by mod-two half-translation symmetry. The remaining four-point expectation equals exactly \(1/21\) by exhaustive rational polygon clipping with 156 positive-area regions. A separate 168-region computation reproduces every five-word probability and all four-block marginals. The included standalone verifier completed these tests successfully; the clipping enumeration covers every integer strip intersecting each polygon, and the proof justifies why the finite rational computation is exhaustive.

## Originality

PASS on inspected evidence, with residual historical-priority risk. Cafaro et al. (arXiv:1507.07262v1) analyze dependence under finite partitions of a tent map; this does not specialize to the hyperbolic toral automorphism here. Hu and Rosenhaus (arXiv:2204.13655v1), Section 3, derive Fourier expressions for cat-map two-point correlations and the Fibonacci recurrence, but the inspected relevant content neither evaluates the five-sign half-strip law nor states the isolated fourth-order rational moment. The older Crawford–Cary treatment focuses on two-point decay; its full text was not accessible for direct comparison. A general Fourier formalism would permit setting up the integral but does not supply its evaluated rational value or its unique five-block Walsh profile without additional analysis. No claim of exhaustive historical priority is made.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The classical hyperbolic cat automorphism is a canonical strongly chaotic system, yet its simplest half-strip bit observation exactly mimics four consecutive independent fair bits. The next observation reveals a single explicit, nonzero interaction, and the exact conditional mutual information gives a quantitative boundary between misleading short-block tests and genuine predictive dependence. This is a natural finite-block invariant of a named system and a standard binary partition, rather than an arbitrary parameter slice.

## Boundaries

The result does not classify arbitrary observation partitions, nonconsecutive indices, infinite Markov order, or longer block distributions. Formal derivability from general Fourier expansions and unindexed prior computations remain priority risks. The exact finite computation has a transparent geometric partition justification, but independent external checking has not occurred.
