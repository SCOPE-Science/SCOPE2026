Binary half-strip observations of Arnold's cat map look like independent fair signs in every four-consecutive-symbol window, but the complete five-symbol law is \(\mathbb P(Z_0=\varepsilon_0,\ldots,Z_4=\varepsilon_4)=\frac1{32}(1+\varepsilon_0\varepsilon_1\varepsilon_3\varepsilon_4/21)\). The first visible dependence is a sharp exact fourth-order interaction of strength \(1/21\), despite all four-block marginals being Bernoulli.
