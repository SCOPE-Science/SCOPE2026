# Review of the four-cuspidal characteristic-three sextic

## Correctness — PASS
The explicit coordinate-square quotient maps the degree-six defining polynomial to the displayed cubic. At the unique cubic singularity the exact local equation has quadratic tangent cone and a nonvanishing transverse cubic coefficient, giving an ordinary unibranch cusp. A line-component argument proves cubic integrality, the discriminant hypothesis gives nine simple axis intersections, and independent square classes give a geometrically connected tame degree-four Kummer cover. Riemann--Hurwitz and plane genus both give geometric genus \(6\). The finite-field checker examines all admissible parameters and rational projective points over \(\mathbf F_3\) and \(\mathbf F_9\) but is not offered as a proof over all fields.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
Kawakita (2017) and Iezzi--Kawakita--Timpanella (arXiv:2306.01928) describe the underlying family in different characteristic assumptions. Giulietti--Kawakita--Lia--Montanucci (arXiv:1805.06317, Remark 3.1) treat a special Wiman sextic that becomes reducible in characteristic three; that does not dominate the present nonsymmetric open-set claim. Published mathematical-record and literature searches for the same family, characteristic-three cusp basket, square-map cubic quotient and nine-point Kummer branch locus located no identical or stronger result. Unindexed computations and special-case cross-reductions remain a risk; a missing match does not constitute a proof of historical uniqueness. The exact day of the older 2017 source's public appearance remains unverified.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The finding resolves a naturally excluded characteristic for an established arithmetic family: despite degeneration to four cusps, the geometric normalization retains genus six, has an explicit tractable Galois cover, and preserves finite-field point counts. The branch and singularity description is structural, not a routine restatement of the cited positive-characteristic results. The excluded parameter boundary remains open.
