For every four-dimensional convex polytope, the stellahedral-transform gamma polynomial is coefficientwise at least \(1+26t+43t^2\), and either sharp coefficient singles out the four-simplex. The two exact gaps decompose into nonnegative deficits in vertices, facets, vertex degrees, and edge/two-face incidences, providing a quantitative four-dimensional case of the published simplex-minimality conjecture.
