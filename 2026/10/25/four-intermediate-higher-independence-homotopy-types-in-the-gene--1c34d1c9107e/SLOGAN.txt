The two-independence complexes of \(\operatorname{GP}(n,2)\) for \(n=6,7,8,9\) have exact homotopy types \(\bigvee^3 S^3\vee S^4\), \(S^5\), \(\bigvee^7 S^5\), and \(\bigvee^4 S^5\), respectively. Complete acyclic Morse matchings and an integral zero-attachment computation certify the exceptional mixed-dimensional first case.
