For the cubic Möbius ladder on \(2n\) vertices, \(n\ge3\), the minimum 1-limited dominating-set size is \(n\) exactly when \(4\mid n\), and \(n+1\) otherwise. Every minimum set is classified by an explicit twisted-rung word, yielding exactly \(4\), \(2n\), or \(3n\) labelled minima as \(n\) is respectively divisible by four, congruent to two modulo four, or odd.
