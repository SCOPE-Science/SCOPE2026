Every four-robust clique complex of a graph formed from at most four successively edge- or corner-glued squares is contractible or a wedge of \(5\)-spheres. The \(29\) four-square graph types have exact wedge multiplicities \(0,1,2,3,4\), and the \(3\times3\) grid gives \(\bigvee^4S^5\); this completes the first finite corner-gluing threshold of an explicit conjecture, not the infinite conjecture.
