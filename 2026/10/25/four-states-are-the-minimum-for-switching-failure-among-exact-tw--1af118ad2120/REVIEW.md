# Scientific review

## Correctness (C)

**PASS.** Row-stochasticity and the square-to-uniform identity imply column-stochasticity. Any deterministic row would force a uniform row at its target, contradicted by column balance. In dimension at most three this forces every two row supports to overlap, giving Dobrushin coefficient strictly smaller than one; compactness makes the bound uniform over all allowable kernels. The four-state bit-shift/transpose pair has square equal to the uniform kernel and an alternating idempotent preserving one original bit. Exact algebra and a standalone rational checker establish the positive perturbation and complete switching profile. The argument covers all finite times, all real allowable kernels in small dimensions, and every stated parameter endpoint; finite tests do not replace the general proof.

## Originality (O)

**PASS on examined evidence; a residual priority risk remains.** The 2021 Shimizu–Shiraga dynamic-walk bounds assume reversible kernels and therefore do not cover the nonreversible counterexample. The 2017 Chevalier–Gusev–Hendrickx–Jungers article studies the broader existence of synchronizing products and SIA indices but does not give the all-time switched failure under individual exact-two-step mixing, the four-state minimality, or the exact positive-family slowdown. Classical nilpotent centered operators, and published three-state doubly stochastic matrices with nontrivial Jordan blocks, are acknowledged as prior knowledge. The sharp stochastic minimum-size boundary was not found in the inspected published material or the specific prior-record searches. The broader theory might contain an unlocated equivalent corollary; historical novelty has not been independently established.

## Value (V)

**PASS.** Static two-step perfect mixing is a particularly strong and natural hypothesis; the theorem shows exactly how severely it fails to control mixing under switching, with the first dimension of failure proved sharp. Every positive perturbation remains a perfect static two-step mixer, while its switching time scale diverges as the perturbation vanishes. This isolates an essential gap between fixed-kernel spectral behavior and time-inhomogeneous convergence, complementing the reversibility restrictions of existing dynamic-walk theory.
