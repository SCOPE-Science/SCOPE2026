Four states are the smallest state space where switching can destroy convergence even though each fixed Markov kernel mixes exactly in two steps: a forward bit shift and its transpose preserve one bit when alternated. For every \(0<\varepsilon<1\), strictly positive perturbations still mix individually at the second step, yet their alternating total-variation error is precisely \((1-\varepsilon)^t/2\), which can decay arbitrarily slowly.
