Every genuine convex quadrilateral has no multiplicity discount: its \(m\)-fold illumination number is \(3m\) unless it is a parallelogram, when it is \(4m\); its fractional illumination number is respectively \(3\) or \(4\). An angular-star obstruction forces all simultaneously illuminable vertex pairs of a non-parallelogram quadrilateral to share one vertex, and it classifies the incidence structure of every optimal cover.
