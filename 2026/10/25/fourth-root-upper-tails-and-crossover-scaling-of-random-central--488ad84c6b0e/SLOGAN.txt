At the previously known shape-optimization transition for central sections of a right circular cylinder, the probability of landing within \(\varepsilon\) of the maximal normalized area decays as an explicit constant times \(\varepsilon^{1/4}\), rather than the generic square-root order. A sharp two-sided crossover function describes the entire window in which the squared half-height/radius aspect differs from \(3\) by order \(\sqrt\varepsilon\).
