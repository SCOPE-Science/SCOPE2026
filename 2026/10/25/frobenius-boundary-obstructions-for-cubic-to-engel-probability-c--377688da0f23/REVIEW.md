# Review of the Frobenius comparison obstruction

## Correctness — PASS
The finite group is explicitly specified, the fixed-point-free hypothesis makes each \(1-s\) bijective, the nested commutator test splits by complement component, and the cube count follows from the absence of kernel \(3\)-torsion and the two complement elements of order \(3\). Euler's theorem constructs infinitely many fields with the required cyclic complement; the limit and monotone-function implication are exact. All nine finite enumeration cases agreed with the formulas. This is a self-review, not independent validation.

## Originality — PASS
The motivating 2026 primary paper, Proposition 1, establishes a positive nondecreasing comparison, not the necessary restrictions \(f(2/m)\le1/m\). The previously published Engel-digraph structure of abelian-kernel cyclic Frobenius groups gives related 2-Engel information, so the standalone 2-Engel count is not presented as novel. Neither these known structure results nor the motivating comparison theorem alone states the coupled cube/Engel limiting obstructions. Searches for equivalent Frobenius and power-word formulations identified no covering statement; the risk of an unindexed equivalent asymptotic comparison remains.

## Value — PASS
The result supplies an infinite, arithmetically indexed family of exact boundary constraints on *every* comparison function of the type whose existence was recently proved. For instance it rules out \(f(2/3)>1/3\), rather than merely computing a parameterized probability without consequence. It gives concrete extremal test families for attempts at quantitative improvement. Its claim is deliberately limited to upper obstructions; optimal attainable lower bounds remain unknown.

## Limitations
Independent verification has not been performed. The groupwise formulas are restricted to cyclic-complement, abelian-kernel Frobenius groups. Full novelty cannot be guaranteed against unindexed publications.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
