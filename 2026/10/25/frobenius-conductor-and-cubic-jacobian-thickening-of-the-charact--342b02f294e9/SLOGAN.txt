For the universal monic cubic discriminant in characteristic \(3\), normalization separates a square conductor \((a,b)^2\) from a cubic intrinsic Jacobian ideal \((a,b)^3\). The reduced conductor is a purely inseparable degree-three Frobenius cover, and the normalization quotient is exactly \(k[a,c]/(a)\oplus k[a,c]/(a^2)\) as a \(k[a,c]\)-module.
