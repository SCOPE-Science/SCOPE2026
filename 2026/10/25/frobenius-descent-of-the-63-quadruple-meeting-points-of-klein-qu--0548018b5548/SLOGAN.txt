For the characteristic-three Klein quartic \(X^3Y+Y^3Z+Z^3X=0\) over \(\mathbb F_3\), the 63 quadruple concurrence points of its known 28 bitangents have Frobenius cycle type \(3^3 6^9\): none is defined over \(\mathbb F_9\), exactly nine are defined over \(\mathbb F_{27}\), and all are defined over \(\mathbb F_{729}\). The previously known bitangent parametrization is used to certify the complete incidence computation.
