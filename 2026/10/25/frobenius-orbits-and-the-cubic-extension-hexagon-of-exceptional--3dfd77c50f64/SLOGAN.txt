On the explicit unirational degree-one del Pezzo surface over \(\mathbb F_5\) of Cheltsov–Loginov–Orlov–Prokhorov, Frobenius partitions all \(240\) exceptional curves into two orbits of length \(3\) and twenty-six of length \(9\). The six curves appearing over \(\mathbb F_{125}\) have a six-cycle as their disjointness graph.
