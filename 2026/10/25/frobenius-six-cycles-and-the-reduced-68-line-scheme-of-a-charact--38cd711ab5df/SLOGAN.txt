The characteristic-two Rams–Schütt–Veniani quartic with 68 geometric lines has a reduced, finite étale line scheme whose Frobenius cycle type over \(\mathbb F_2\) is \(1^4 2^2 3^2 6^9\). Consequently, every line is defined over \(\mathbb F_{64}\), exactly four are defined over \(\mathbb F_2\), and the line-scheme zeta function is \(\bigl((1-T)^4(1-T^2)^2(1-T^3)^2(1-T^6)^9\bigr)^{-1}\).
