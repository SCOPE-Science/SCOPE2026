In characteristic greater than three, the squarefree trace-cubic stratum of three-dimensional unital commutative algebras over \(\mathbb F_q\) has exactly \(q^3+q^2+q\) isomorphism classes. Their full automorphism groups are trivial, \(C_2\), \(C_3\), or \(S_3\), with respective class counts \(q^2(q-1)\), \(2q^2-q\), \(q\), and \(q\).
