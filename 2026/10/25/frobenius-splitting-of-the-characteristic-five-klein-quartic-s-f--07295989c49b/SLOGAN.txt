The characteristic-five Klein quartic has a reduced 24-point flex scheme with Frobenius cycles \(1^3 3^1 6^3\); its eight canonically associated residual-tangent triangles have cycles \(1^2 6^1\). Thus three flex points but two triangles are rational over \(\mathbb F_5\), and both schemes split completely over \(\mathbb F_{5^6}\).
