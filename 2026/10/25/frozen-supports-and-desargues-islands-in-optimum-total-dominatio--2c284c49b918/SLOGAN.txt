The optimum total-domination exchange graph of every rectangular rook graph decomposes into fixed-row or fixed-column support components modeled by surjections whose fibers have size at least two. These components become mobile precisely when they have a spare selected square; the five-column case consists of isolated states and copies of the Desargues graph, giving \(\mathcal X_{5,5}\cong10K_1\sqcup20\operatorname{Des}\).
