The two nonzero rational homology representations of the Möbius–Kantor graph independence complex are a bipartition-sign line in degree four and a bipartition-and-permutation-sign twist of the four-body-diagonal cube permutation module in degree three. This complete character for all \(96\) automorphisms determines rationally acyclic full-symmetry quotients and one-dimensional homology in two natural index-two quotients.
