For two labeled particles on the \(n\)-arm star, \(n\ge4\), the full rational arm-permutation-by-particle-exchange homology is exactly \(\bigwedge^2 V_n\) with exchange sign \(+1\), plus \(S^{(n-2,2)}\) with exchange sign \(-1\). A canonical equivariant retraction onto the crown graph also gives the complete integral cycle lattice; the three-arm case has only the positive sign-of-arms summand.
