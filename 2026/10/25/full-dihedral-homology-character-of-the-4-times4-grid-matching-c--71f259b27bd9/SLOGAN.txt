The ordinary matching complex of the Cartesian \(4\times4\) square grid has full dihedral rational homology modules \(\widetilde H_4\cong A_1\oplus2A_2\oplus B_1\oplus E\) and \(\widetilde H_5\cong E\). Consequently, its dihedral quotient has precisely one nonzero reduced rational homology class, in degree four, although its nonequivariant sphere-wedge homotopy type was already known.
