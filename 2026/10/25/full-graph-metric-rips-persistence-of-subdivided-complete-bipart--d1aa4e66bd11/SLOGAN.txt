For all \(m,n\ge2\), the graph-metric Rips filtration of the once-subdivided complete bipartite graph has \((m-1)(n-1)\) persistent circles through radius two, then exactly \(((m-1)(n-1))^2\) three-spheres at radius three, and becomes contractible at radius four. The radius-three complex is *exactly* the join of the biclique and rook-graph clique complexes.
