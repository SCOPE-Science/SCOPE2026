The genus-three unordered three-particle surface of the four-path theta graph has first rational homology \((S^{(3,1)}\oplus S^{(2,1,1)})\boxtimes\varepsilon\) under four-path permutations and hub exchange. Its orientation-preserving \(A_4\)-quotient is a sphere with four cone points of orders \(2,2,3,3\); the ordinary genus-three homotopy type is previously known.
