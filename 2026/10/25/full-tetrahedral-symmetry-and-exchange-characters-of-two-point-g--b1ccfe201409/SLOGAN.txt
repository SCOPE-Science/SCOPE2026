For the truncated-tetrahedral graph, the twenty-dimensional rational second homology of the ordered two-particle configuration space splits under particle exchange as \(\mathbf1\oplus2V\oplus(V\otimes\operatorname{sgn})\) in the even sector and \(2\mathbf1\oplus2V\oplus W\) in the odd sector, where \(V\) and \(W\) are the standard three-dimensional and two-dimensional irreducible representations of \(S_4\). Exactly one second-homology class is fixed by all tetrahedral relabelings and particle exchange.
