For the graph-metric Vietoris–Rips filtration of the regular icosahedron, the actual inclusion from the two-sphere stage to the five-sphere stage is equivariantly homotopic to the linear inclusion \(S(U)\hookrightarrow S(U\oplus U^\sigma)\) of conjugate three-dimensional icosahedral representations. For every subgroup, the nonempty fixed spheres change from \(S^{d-1}\) to \(S^{2d-1}\), so their reduced integral persistent homology always dies at that step.
