Independently deleting vertices of a regular polygon gives an exact finite-size expected hull area and a Gaussian area deficit on the sharp \(n^{-5/2}\) fluctuation scale. For fixed retention probability \(p\in(0,1)\), the limiting variance is \(16\pi^6(1-p)(2-p)[1+5(1-p)+(1-p)^2]/p^5\), obtained from a cubic geometric-gap renewal reward.
