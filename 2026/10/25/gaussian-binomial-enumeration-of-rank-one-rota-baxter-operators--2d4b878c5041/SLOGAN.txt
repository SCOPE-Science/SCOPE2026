For every finite field, the non-square-zero-image rank-one weight-zero Rota–Baxter operators on upper-triangular \(n\times n\) matrices are counted by a Gaussian-binomial sum. For \(3\times3\) triangular matrices, every rank-one operator is counted exactly: \(3q^5+4q^4+4q^3-8q^2-2q-1\), including characteristic two.
