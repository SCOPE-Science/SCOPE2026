For a uniform labeled associative quasitrivial semigroup, the number of noncommuting pairs is Gaussian on its usual \(\sqrt n\) fluctuation scale about \(0.4044012818\,n\), with an exact weighted-block counting polynomial. Yet noncommutation among at least \(\delta n^2\) distinct pairs has probability \(\exp[-(\sqrt{2\delta}+o(1))n\log n]\) for every fixed \(0<\delta<1/2\).
