At axial heights \(z=U/\sqrt\mu\), the saddle-cycle basin boundary of a recent polynomial Hurwitz-family construction converges to the noncylindrical Gaussian graph \(r=\exp(200U^2/21)\), rather than the unit cylinder. Its first correction is explicitly odd in \(U\), of order \(\sqrt\mu\), and quantifies the asymmetry between high positive and negative initial heights.
