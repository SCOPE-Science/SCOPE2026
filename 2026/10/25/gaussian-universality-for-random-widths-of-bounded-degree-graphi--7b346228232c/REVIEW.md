# Scientific review: Gaussian universality for random widths of bounded-degree graphical zonotopes

## Correctness — PASS
The theorem explicitly fixes the Euclidean generator scaling, connectedness, a uniform graph-degree bound, and the convergence of two normalized local counts. The exact width equals a Gaussian absolute-edge-difference sum divided by the centered Gaussian radius. Pairwise covariance is computed separately for disjoint and incident edges, with the incident-edge coefficient \(\kappa=1/3+(2\sqrt3-4)/\pi\); its covariance with the centered Gaussian squared norm is \(\mu m_n\). A bounded dependency-graph cumulant argument gives the joint Gaussian limit. Expanding the radial denominator, rather than replacing it by \(\sqrt n\) prematurely, produces the essential correction \(-2\alpha^2/\pi\) to the limiting variance. The variance is strictly positive using the degree-sum bound \(\tau\ge 2\alpha^2-\alpha\). All proof steps are reproduced in `RESULT.md`; the standalone program verifies 160 finite geometric identities and three variance diagnostics. No claim of infinite proof by simulation is made.

## Originality — PASS, with bibliographic uncertainty
Padrol–Pilaud–Poullot (arXiv:2111.12422) provide deformation-cone equations and facets of graphical zonotopes, not a distribution for their Haar-directional width. Pešović–Stojadinović (arXiv:2209.12053) study interpolating graph polytopes, enumerators, and normal fans rather than the Euclidean asymptotic distribution. Poullot (arXiv:2404.02669) studies the rays and faces of deformation cones. The closest indexed width result, *Sharp width-variance extremizers among tree graphical zonotopes*, proves exact variances and tree extremizers; exact first and second moments do not imply a central limit theorem. Other indexed results treat individual graph families or finite third moments. The current result supplies a **general bounded-degree graph-sequence law** with a two-count Gaussian variance, not merely a restatement of a special-case path, cycle or broom result. Searches for a graph Gaussian-total-variation formulation also did not identify a covering theorem. Such a treatment outside the indexed mathematical literature remains a residual risk; originality is not certified by a failed search.

## Value — PASS
Graphical zonotopes give canonical Euclidean realizations of graphical hyperplane arrangements, but graph-local combinatorial data alone do not generally determine the full spherical geometry. The theorem proves that across every bounded-degree connected graph sequence satisfying the stated two density limits, the entire centered random-width distribution Gaussianizes and is governed by just the edge and wedge densities—even though exact finite higher width moments detect further motifs. This separates a broad universality class from growing-hub phenomena, which can yield non-Gaussian limits. The resulting complete limiting classification is a motivated geometric fact, not a parameter substitution or a routine next finite moment.
