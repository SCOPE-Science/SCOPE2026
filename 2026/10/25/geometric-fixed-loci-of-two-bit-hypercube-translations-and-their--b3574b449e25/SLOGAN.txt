At Hamming scale two, simultaneously flipping two coordinates of the binary \(n\)-cube has a geometric Vietoris–Rips fixed-point space that is **exactly the graph of an \((n-1)\)-cube**, with \((n-3)2^{n-2}+1\) independent circles, all killed at scale three. The full residual-translation character of these circles has weight-dependent multiplicities, distinguishing geometric fixed-point topology from the known ambient symmetry representation.
