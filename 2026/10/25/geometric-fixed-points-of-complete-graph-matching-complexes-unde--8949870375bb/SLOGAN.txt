For every involution of the complete graph with \(m\) swapped vertex pairs and \(r\) fixed vertices, its ordinary matching complex has a contractible geometric fixed-point space when \(m\) is odd; when \(m=2k\), the fixed space is a wedge of \((2k)!/k!\) suspended copies of the matching complex on the \(r\) fixed vertices (with explicit \(r\le2\) cases). This yields, for instance, a natural fixed-point space with \((\mathbb Z/3)^2\) torsion in its second homology.
