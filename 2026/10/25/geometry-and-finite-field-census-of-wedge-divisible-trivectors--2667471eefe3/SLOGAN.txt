For every \(n\ge6\), the locus of trivectors possessing a linear exterior divisor is a rational irreducible variety of dimension \(\binom n2\) whose singular locus is exactly the cone of decomposable trivectors. Its divisor-rank strata have explicit finite-field cardinalities for every prime power, yielding a complete distribution of wedge-map ranks and, in six variables, the homology dimensions of cubic exterior multiplication.
