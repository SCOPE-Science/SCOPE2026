For symmetric holes in the negative-doubling circle map, every parameter \(3/7\le a\le4/9\) has the same positive entropy \(\log\varphi\), yet **every individual periodic point** remains unchanged until the endpoint. At \(a=4/9\), precisely two primitive three-cycles enter and multiply the Artin–Mazur zeta function by \((1-z^3)^{-2}\), even though the entropy stays constant.
