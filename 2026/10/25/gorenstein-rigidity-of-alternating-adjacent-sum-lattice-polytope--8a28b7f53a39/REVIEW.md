# Review of the alternating adjacent-sum Gorenstein classification

## Correctness
**PASS.** Every coordinate lower bound is a facet, hence the unique interior lattice point of a reflexive dilation must have all coordinates one. Every active primitive adjacent-sum facet of capacity \(b\) forces \(qb=3\). For \(s\ge2,d\ge3\), explicit relative-interior witnesses prove that edges one and two are facets of distinct capacities. For \(s=1\) and odd \(d\ge3\), the first and last edges are facets of capacities one and two. Both arrangements contradict a single positive integer index. In dimension two, the only possible index pairs are checked constructively, and in even dimensions for \(s=1\) the polytope decomposes into unit triangles. Exact lattice-point transfer checks independently confirm all 72 parameter pairs with \(2\le d\le10\) and \(1\le s\le8\). They are not used as the infinite proof.

## Originality
**PASS under inspected available evidence.** Jiang--Wen--Zhong (2026, arXiv:2607.14887v1) explicitly pose their Question Q1 to classify the even-dimensional Gorenstein cases for \(s\ge2\). Their published claims stop at isolated non-Gorenstein dimensions, so they do not imply the universal exclusion obtained here. The general reflexive-facet criterion is established background, not claimed new. Miyazaki (2020) and Hamano--Hibi--Ohsugi (2016) address ordinary or fractional stable-set polytopes with uniform capacities, not this alternating higher-capacity family. Actual mathematical and indexed-literature searches found no matching complete classification. Historical priority is still provisional; external indexing cannot establish uniqueness.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The result provides the full necessary-and-sufficient classification of a natural all-dimensional family posed explicitly as an open problem in July 2026. The obstruction is uniform and geometric, bypassing casewise \(h^*\)-computations. The additional odd-dimensional conclusion sharpens the scope. The contribution is a new classification, not merely rederivation of already tabulated small examples; the short proof does not reduce the value of resolving a stated research question.

## Closest literature and residual risks
The most directly relevant source is the 2026 primary paper, particularly Q1 and Sections 3.5–3.6. Uniform-edge stable-set Gorenstein theorems are not equivalent to the nonuniform lattice family. Possible unindexed equivalent weighted-facet classifications remain an uncertainty, as does independent mathematical review. No broader cyclic or general-period result is claimed.
