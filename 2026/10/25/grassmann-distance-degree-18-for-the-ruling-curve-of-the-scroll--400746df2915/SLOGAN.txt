The curve of ruling lines on the first asymmetric rational normal scroll \(S(2,3)\subset\mathbb P^6\) has Grassmann distance degree exactly \(18\): a generic rank-two projection-matrix data point produces \(18\) distinct complex critical rulings outside the cut locus. An explicit rank-two idempotent witness proves that the constrained-data count attains the previously known unrestricted ED degree.
