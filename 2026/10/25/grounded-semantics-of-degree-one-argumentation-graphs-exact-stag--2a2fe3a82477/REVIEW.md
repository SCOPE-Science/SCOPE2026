# Scientific review of the corrected effective theorem

## Correctness — PASS for the corrected claim
The structural proof by indegree/outdegree-one component decomposition and even-distance induction remains valid. The former effective hypothesis was genuinely insufficient: a partial-computable predecessor does not permit deciding whether a vertex is unattacked; a halting-set construction of isolated vertices and directed edges gives grounding ordinal one with noncomputable grounded set. The corrected statement expressly requires total computable optional-neighbor maps with a terminating no-neighbor indicator. Under that precise assumption, source detection is decidable, the finite-stage upper bound gives a decider, the extension is uniformly c.e., and the W-star path gadget has computable total optional-neighbor maps and realizes every c.e. degree at omega.

## Originality — PASS, best of knowledge
The nearby Andrews–San Mauro work on computable grounded semantics and tree-rank representations, and their broad finitary argumentation complexity table, are acknowledged. Their statements do not directly provide the joint degree-one structural/stage classification with total optional-neighbor presentations. The basic path parity computation and generic c.e. coding are established methods, not asserted as standalone discoveries; an earlier specialization may cover parts of the corrected result.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A complete structural and ordinal normal form of the simplest nonbranching abstract-argumentation class is useful. The exact distinction between optional-neighbor computability and ordinary partial neighbor computability is substantive: otherwise even stage-one grounded semantics can encode the halting complement. With the corrected effective assumption, the sharp transition between finite computable grounded extensions and omega-stage arbitrary c.e. degrees is a meaningful natural classification.

## Limits
Only countable frameworks of both indegree and outdegree at most one are covered; the degree theorem requires total optional-neighbor functions, not arbitrary partial recursive functions. Historical priority is best-of-knowledge, and no external attestation or formal verification is asserted.
