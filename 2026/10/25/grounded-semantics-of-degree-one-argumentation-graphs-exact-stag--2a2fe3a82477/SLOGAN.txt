For countable argumentation graphs of indegree and outdegree at most one, grounded vertices are exactly those at even distance from source vertices and stabilization occurs by stage omega. If both missing and present neighbors are computably decidable by total optional-neighbor maps, finite grounding stages have computable grounded sets while at omega every c.e. Turing degree occurs. Ordinary partial-computable neighbor maps alone do not suffice: even a stage-one grounded set can encode the complement of the halting problem.
