# Reproducibility and mathematical verification boundaries

For a degree-one source-rooted path, apply the defense operator successively; at stage t precisely its even-distance vertices at distances at most 2t−2 have entered. A source-free component stays empty. With total computable optional-neighbor maps, detecting an unattacked vertex is a terminating test; finite ordinal t therefore permits a bounded 2t-predecessor membership test. The W-star construction's every edge is determined by a finite-stage c.e. approximation, so the resulting optional-neighbor maps are total and computable. Odd-index coding forces arbitrarily late stages.

Ordinary partial-computable predecessor maps do not meet that assumption. In the halting counterexample, the predicate that a designated vertex has no attacker is co-c.e.-complete although its attacker, if present, is found by a partial algorithm. The resulting grounded extension is noncomputable at stage one. No finite simulation can certify this infinitary noncomputability claim; it follows by the usual halting problem reduction.
