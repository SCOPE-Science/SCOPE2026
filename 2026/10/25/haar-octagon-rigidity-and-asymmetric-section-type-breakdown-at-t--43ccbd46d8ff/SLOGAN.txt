Every generic central section of the regular cuboctahedron is an octagon, but small truncation changes destroy that certainty with asymmetric exact first-order rates: \(6/\pi\) toward the octahedron and \(6\sqrt2/\pi\) toward the cube. On the cube side, the quadrilateral probability is an explicit spherical-cone arctangent, emerging only quadratically from the cuboctahedron.
