Descending the natural height filtration of the integer-partition transfer clique complex creates primitive two-dimensional homology in exact, independently surviving batches indexed by the cycles of explicit bipartite upper-link graphs. The total sphere rank counts pairs of distinct part sizes \(b+2\leq a<\max(\lambda)\) inside a partition \(\lambda\), giving a positive partition-theoretic explanation of the known bouquet multiplicity rather than merely another Euler sum.
