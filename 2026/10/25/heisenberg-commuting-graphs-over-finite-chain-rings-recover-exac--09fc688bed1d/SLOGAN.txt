The full commuting graph of a Heisenberg group \(\operatorname{UT}_3(R)\) over a finite commutative chain ring determines exactly the residue-field order and the ring length, but not the ring itself. For every prime \(p\) and length \(m\ge2\), the groups over \(\mathbb Z/p^m\mathbb Z\) and \(\mathbb F_p[t]/(t^m)\) have isomorphic commuting graphs yet are not isoclinic; isolated class-two counterexamples were known previously, while the stated result is a uniform classification.
