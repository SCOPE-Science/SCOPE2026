For every fixed total degree \(m\ge2\), the sharp uniform norm of the \(L^2\) Legendre polynomial projector on the \(d\)-cube is asymptotic to \(\frac{3^{m/2}}{m!}\mathbb E|H_m(Z)|\,d^{m/2}\). More strongly, every evaluation point has a uniform Hermite-profile asymptotic controlled by its mean squared coordinates, and near-maximizing points are precisely those approaching the cube's corners in average squared deficit.
