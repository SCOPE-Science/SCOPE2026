# Reproduction and limits

Execute `python3 verify.py` in this directory. The script requires only the Python standard library and uses exact rational arithmetic throughout. `verification_output.txt` contains output generated by executing that exact packaged script.

The checker evaluates stationary lag-zero, lag-one and lag-two population autocovariances, the contemporaneous cross covariance, the quadratic pole reconstruction, innovation variance reconstruction and cross-innovation reconstruction. It includes rational examples with distinct environmental colors, repeated demographic/environmental poles, and the full-filter interchange invariance when the colors agree.

The full-domain uniqueness, impossibility and local insufficiency assertions are proved symbolically in `RESULT.md`; the finite grid checks are reproducibility aids, not infinite certificates. No independent review or field-data test has been performed. The model assumes exact stationary moments, known time units, linear dynamics and no measurement error. Near coinciding environmental poles, the inverse may be ill-conditioned even though it is unique.
