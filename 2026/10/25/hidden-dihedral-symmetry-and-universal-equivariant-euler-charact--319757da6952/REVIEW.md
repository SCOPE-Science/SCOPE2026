# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The claimed object is precisely the multipath complex of the four arc types over every leaf. Its face condition is reconstructed from the indegree/outdegree restriction and the exhaustive exclusion of directed 2- and 4-cycles, and every face uses at most four arcs. The type-permutation group preserving the two allowed same-leaf pairs has order eight, with explicitly verified dihedral generators. The augmented Lefschetz sign is \((-1)\) per selected orbit, fixing normalization and boundary cases. Because any invariant face uses whole leaf cycles, every coefficient of the cycle-index expression is determined by one of twelve weight-at-most-four templates. The standalone integer enumeration evaluates all coefficients and reproduces each of the five polynomials, making the infinite extension exact rather than empirical. Direct face/cycle comparison (9,686 subsets), all eight action checks (9,432 transformed faces), and 220 full-character comparisons provide cross-checks. No graded homology concentration or integral torsion theorem is asserted.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

The January 2026 full text of Caputi–Collari–Smith studies bidirected paths and polygons, not bidirected complete bipartite graphs or their four-type symmetry. Caputi–Collari–Di Trani–Smith's Theorem 3.7 (2022) treats one-way complete bipartite orientations, not the directed 2/4-cycle exclusion of this family. The earlier finite \(K_{2,6}\) result provides a nonequivariant homotopy type, without type-dihedral characters. Closest indexed work on bidirected double stars and on an ordinary \(K_{2,n}\) Morse complex concerns different face conditions. Searches also used the aliases "cycle-free chessboard", "bidirected complete bipartite", "equivariant Lefschetz" and "arc-type symmetry". None of the recovered statements implies these five universal class polynomials. A missed equivalent calculation in nonindexed work remains possible; database noncoverage does not certify uniqueness.

## Value — PASS

A natural, infinite family of directed graph complexes has a simplicial symmetry beyond its evident graph symmetries. Computing its full alternating \(S_n\times D_8\) rational homology character answers an equivariant topological question inaccessible from ordinary Betti numbers; the quarter-turn is controlled solely by four-cycles, while the remaining classes exhibit different dependence on one- and two-cycles. The exact formulas apply uniformly, including parameters with unknown graded homology. They are more informative than a single Euler number or a routine small-instance enumeration and isolate a transferable bounded-support orbit method.
