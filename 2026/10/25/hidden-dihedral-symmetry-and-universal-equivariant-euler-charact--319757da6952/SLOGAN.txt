The multipath complexes of bidirected \(K_{2,n}\) carry a hidden eight-element dihedral symmetry acting on their four arc types, commuting with permutations of leaves. Their entire alternating reduced rational-homology character under \(S_n\times D_8\) is determined by five universal polynomials using only the numbers of 1-, 2-, and 4-cycles of the leaf permutation.
