For every odd \(n\ge3\), strong inversion bias in the finite Mallows model makes fixed-point count positively correlated with inversion count, reversing the strictly negative correlation at uniform bias; even sizes retain negative covariance at sufficiently strong bias. The positive phase is rigorously guaranteed for \(q\ge100n^2\), disproving unrestricted fixed-point/inversion monotonicity, and the first odd size \(n=3\) changes sign exactly at \(q=1+\sqrt3\).
