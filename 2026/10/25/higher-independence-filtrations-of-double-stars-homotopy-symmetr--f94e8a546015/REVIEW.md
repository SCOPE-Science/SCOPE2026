# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The statement quantifies over every integer \(a,b\ge1\) and \(r\ge1\). The four-piece simplicial decomposition follows by classifying faces according to their two center vertices. Each of the two single-center attaching subcomplexes contains a full simplex factor on a nonempty opposite leaf set and is therefore contractible. Two gluing steps give a contractible core; the remaining two-center faces attach along a suspension of the uniform leaf skeleton. This proves the integral homotopy formula including \(r=1\), \(r=2\), and \(r\ge a+b+2\). A natural relative chain isomorphism, not an asserted equivariant contraction, gives the full integral automorphism module, including the center-swap sign. The finite checker independently confirmed 54 parameter cases and 892 signed Lefschetz comparisons. There is no extrapolation from those cases: the proof covers all parameters.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority uncertainty
The closest motivating source is Sawant, *On the Vertex Decomposability of \(r\)-Independence Complexes of Trees* (arXiv:2605.25150, May 24, 2026). Its full-text main theorem proves vertex decomposability for all trees, which implies a sphere-wedge type but not the numerical Betti formula. Deshpande and Singh, *Higher Independence Complexes of Graphs and Their Homotopy Types* (arXiv:2001.05448), obtain exact results for perfect rooted trees and paths and explicitly pose the general sphere-multiplicity problem; the path \(P_4\) is a covered boundary instance of the present family, not a novel example. Abdelmalek et al. (arXiv:2106.10863) establish caterpillar vertex decomposability. Ghosh and Selvaraja (arXiv:2505.06614) establish shellability for bounded-diameter trees. None of these inspected results states the all-parameter double-star binomial formula, its integral center-exchange character, or the threshold-by-threshold barcode. Searches of published mathematical research records found a multipath complex on bidirected double stars, which concerns a different ground set and different face predicate. A previously recorded theorem for trees with many length-two arms treats a different tree family and has a different homotopy threshold. Absence from searches cannot establish publication priority; independent bibliographic review remains necessary.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The 2020 multiplicity question remains substantive even after all-tree vertex decomposability was proved in 2026. The two-center double star is the smallest tree class allowing two independently varying groups of pendant branches. The formula unexpectedly loses the two branch counts separately, retaining only their sum; it identifies all thresholds and the complete integral symmetry module, including the nontrivial center-exchange twist. It also determines the entire field-valued higher-independence persistence module with exactly one-step bars. Those explicit invariants cannot be recovered from qualitative shellability or an assertion that some sphere wedge exists. The conclusion is confined to double stars and makes no broader-tree claim.

A complete priority search is not mathematically decidable from a finite collection of sources; some relevant literature may have escaped retrieval. This result gives an integral homology representation, not an equivariant splitting of spaces. Independent review has not been carried out.
