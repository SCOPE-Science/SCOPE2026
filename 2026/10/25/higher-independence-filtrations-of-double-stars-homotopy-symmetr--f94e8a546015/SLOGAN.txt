For a double-star tree with \(N\) leaves, its \(r\)-independence complex is contractible at \(r=1\) and \(r\ge N+2\), and otherwise is a wedge of \(\binom{N-1}{r-2}\) spheres of dimension \(r-1\), independently of the distribution of leaves between centers. The integral automorphism module is \(\bigwedge^{r-2}U_N\) twisted by the center-swap sign, and every nontrivial positive-degree persistent-homology bar lasts one threshold step.
