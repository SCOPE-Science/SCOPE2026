The two strongly regular graphs with parameters \((16,6,2,2)\) have two-independence complexes homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{57}S^3\) (Shrikhande) and \(\bigvee^{105}S^3\) (four-by-four rook). A common explicit acyclic matching proves both integral homotopy types, and their sphere-count order reverses the already known ordinary-independence comparison \(23>15\).
