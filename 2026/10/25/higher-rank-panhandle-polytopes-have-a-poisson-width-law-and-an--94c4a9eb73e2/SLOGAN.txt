For every fixed rank \(r\ge3\), panhandle matroid polytopes have an explicit higher-order Poisson limit for random-direction width and lose precisely \(2a_p\mathcal D_r(\rho)/\sqrt{p+q-1}\) in mean width relative to the corresponding hypersimplex, to leading order. The strictly positive loss has a quadratic onset in the ratio of the two coordinate blocks.
