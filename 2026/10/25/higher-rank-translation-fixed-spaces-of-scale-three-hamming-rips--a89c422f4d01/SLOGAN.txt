For every translation subgroup of rank at least two acting on the inclusive scale-three Vietoris--Rips complex of the binary cube, the geometric fixed space is either empty, a cube graph, a disjoint union of four-cycles, or a discrete set; the precise case follows from the subgroup's weight spectrum. The sole admissible rank-two exception to the cube-graph pattern has weight multiset \(\{2,3,3\}\) and produces \(2^{n-4}\) circular components.
