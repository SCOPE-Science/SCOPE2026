The two twelve-vertex Archimedean graphs have sharply different two-independence topology: the truncated tetrahedron has homotopy type \(\bigvee^3 S^3\vee\bigvee^3 S^4\), while the cuboctahedron has \(\bigvee^{32}S^3\). Exhaustive acyclic matchings and an exact zero integral Morse boundary establish the sphere wedges despite both complexes being nonpure.
