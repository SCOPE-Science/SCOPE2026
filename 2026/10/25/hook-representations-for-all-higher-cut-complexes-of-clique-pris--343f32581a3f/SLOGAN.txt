For every higher cut parameter \(n\ge k\ge4\), the ordinary \(k\)-cut complex of the clique prism \(K_n\square K_2\) has a single irreducible rational homology representation: the hook \(S^{(n-k+1,1^{k-1})}\) of column permutations. Exchanging the two layers acts on that homology by \((-1)^{n+1}\), revealing a parity effect invisible to the previously known sphere-wedge homotopy type.
