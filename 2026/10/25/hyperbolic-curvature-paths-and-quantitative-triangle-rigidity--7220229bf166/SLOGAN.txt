The centroid of an outer Euclidean parallel body traces a hyperbolic arc whenever its area, perimeter, and Steiner curvature centroids are noncollinear; its signed curvature never changes sign. For triangles this happens **exactly when the triangle is scalene**, with an explicit positive angle-gap lower bound for nonflatness; isosceles triangles instead have monotone straight paths (constant only in the equilateral case).
