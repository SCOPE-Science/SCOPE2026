Every nonzero relative rotation of the regular bases of an \(n\)-gonal prism has ordinary illumination number \(2\lceil n/\lfloor n/2\rfloor\rceil\) and fractional illumination number \(2n/\lfloor n/2\rfloor\), independent of rotation angle and height. For even \(n\), the unrotated prism has strictly larger invariants, so an arbitrarily small twist produces a sharp illumination discontinuity.
