Every equal-height right regular \(n\)-gonal bipyramid has illumination numbers \(I=2+\lceil n/\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor\rceil\) and \(I^*=2+n/\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor\), with a rigid cyclic fractional optimizer except for one parity class. Equal pole heights matter: shifting the lower pole of the regular pentagonal bipyramid from height \(-1\) to \(-3\) changes \((I,I^*)\) from \((5,9/2)\) to \((4,7/2)\).
