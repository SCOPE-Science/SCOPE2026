# Scientific review: independent double-cone character-degree recurrence

**Correctness — PASS.** An unrestricted two-row alternating block extension reduces, by congruence, to a rank-two radical coupling and an alternating two-by-two symplectic correction. The ranks zero, one and two of the coupling are exhaustively counted; the orthogonal-row count is derived from nondegeneracy, with the zero-dimensional case treated separately. The classical twisted-group-algebra argument yields the odd-characteristic character count. Direct finite-field enumeration independently agrees in eight complete configurations. Bounds: the finite examples do not themselves prove the formula for all graphs, and no characteristic-two character result is claimed.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** Full-text comparison with Rossmann (2022), Sections 1.6 and 5, separates this character-degree transition from that paper's different conjugacy-class join formula. A published single-universal-vertex character recurrence (record 732adc755a75) is the closest item found. Its abstract concerns coning and the true-twin graphs \(K_t\vee K_{a,b}\); the present operation adds two nonadjacent vertices, so it is not obtained by applying the single-cone operation twice. Its full text was inaccessible through the available reader, leaving a specific possibility of an implicit two-vertex corollary. Mainkar's earlier graph–Lie correspondence is background, not a character enumeration.

**Value — PASS.** This is an exact, field-uniform structural operator on a natural representation-theoretic invariant, not a single small numerical count. The third rank-increment branch and its symplectic orthogonality correction are absent in the single-vertex operation. Substitution of the rectangular-matrix rank distribution gives the previously unaccounted complete tripartite family \(K_{2,a,b}\) for all positive part sizes and odd finite fields, a concrete answer on a family in the setting of Rossmann's character-counting question.

**Closest literature and limits.** Mainkar (arXiv:1310.3414v1) supplies the graph-Lie construction. Rossmann (DOI:10.1112/blms.12665), particularly Section 1.6 and Section 5, supplies the character-degree question and a different conjugacy counting theorem. Record 732adc755a75 supplies a single-cone character result; only its title and abstract were available. Risks: the accessible comparison is not a definitive priority determination; the group-theoretic statement excludes characteristic two; the recurrence is specifically for an independent pair of universal new vertices.
