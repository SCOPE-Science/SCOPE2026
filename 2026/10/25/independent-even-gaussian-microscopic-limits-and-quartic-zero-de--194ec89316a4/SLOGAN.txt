For circular-Gaussian palindromic Kac polynomials, the logarithmic \(1/n\)-windows around \(1\) and \(-1\) converge to independent copies of an even cosine-transform Gaussian entire function. Reciprocal symmetry produces an exact quartic microscopic zero depletion, \(\mathbb E N(B(0,r))=\frac{2}{45}r^4+O(r^6)\), although every finite polynomial has exactly half its zeros inside the unit circle.
