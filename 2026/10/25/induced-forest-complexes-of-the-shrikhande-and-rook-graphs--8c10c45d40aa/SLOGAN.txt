The induced-forest complexes of the Shrikhande and \(4\times4\) rook graphs have respective integral homotopy types \(\bigvee^{141}S^5\vee\bigvee^{36}S^6\) and \(\bigvee^{33}S^5\vee\bigvee^{72}S^6\). Thus identical strongly regular parameters and identical feedback-vertex-set numbers do not determine this natural two-degree topological invariant.
