The simplicial complex of induced forests in the icosahedral graph is integrally homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{40}S^4\). Its fourth rational homology splits under the full \(A_5\times C_2\) symmetry into antipodally even \(V_4\oplus V_5\oplus V_6\) and antipodally odd \(2V_4\oplus V_5\oplus2V_6\), with no rotationally invariant vector.
