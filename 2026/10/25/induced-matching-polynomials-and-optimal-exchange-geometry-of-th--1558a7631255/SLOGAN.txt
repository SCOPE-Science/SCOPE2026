The cospectral Shrikhande and four-by-four rook graphs have induced-matching polynomials \(1+48z+192z^2+32z^3\) and \(1+48z+216z^2\), respectively. All \(32\) maximum induced matchings of the Shrikhande graph are exchange-isolated, whereas the rook graph's \(216\) maxima form a connected, \(16\)-regular exchange graph of diameter \(3\).
