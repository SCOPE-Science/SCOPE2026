The induced-matching polynomial of every rectangular rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) has an explicit two-index factorial formula and maximum degree \(\min\{m,n,\lfloor(m+n)/3\rfloor\}\). For the two-row family, every minimum strong total cover is classified and counted, with exceptional counts eight and forty-five at widths two and three and \(\binom n2\binom{n-2}{2}\) thereafter.
