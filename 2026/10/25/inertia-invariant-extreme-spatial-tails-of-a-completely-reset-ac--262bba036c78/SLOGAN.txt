For a completely reset inertial active Brownian particle with nonzero propulsion and thermal noise, both stationary coordinate tails have a sharp exponential rate \(k_*\) determined by a rotational Mathieu eigenvalue and *independent of inertia*. At strong resetting, the far-tail length is of order \(r^{-1/2}\), whereas the stationary standard deviation is of order \(r^{-3/2}\).
