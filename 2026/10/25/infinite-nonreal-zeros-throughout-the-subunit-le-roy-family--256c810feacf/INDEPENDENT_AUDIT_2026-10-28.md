# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For 0<p<1 set tau=1-p. The compensated Gamma Levy exponent makes log E, for E unit-exponential, infinitely divisible; its tau-power determines a real Y with E exp(sY)=Gamma(1+s)^tau for s>-1. Thus X=exp(Y)>0 has E X^n=(n!)^tau and all positive exponential moments, so E exp(zX)=F_p(z). Its E X^{-q}=Gamma(1-q)^tau diverges as q tends to 1 from below; restriction to X<=1 plus monotone convergence proves E X^{-1}=infinity. Tonelli gives integral_0^infty F_p(-x)dx=infinity while dominated convergence yields F_p(-x)->0. The coefficient order is 1/p finite. If F_p had finitely many zeros, Hadamard gives F_p=P exp(Q); positivity on the negative ray forces Q(-x) real modulo a constant phase. A nonconstant real polynomial Q(-x) either grows, contradicting F_p(-x)->0, or decays, contradicting nonintegrability; a constant Q makes F_p a polynomial, also impossible. Therefore infinitely many zeros, none on either real half-axis; conjugation yields infinitely many off-real pairs. The separate Rosas p>1 theorem and F_1=exp finish the positive-p classification.
