For a fixed target sampled once at exponentially distributed distance, Brownian search with Poisson resetting has a finite \(m\)th passage-time moment exactly when the target-distance decay rate exceeds \(m\sqrt{r/D}\). The unique mean-optimal resetting rate has infinite variance, while resetting four times more slowly uniquely minimizes finite coefficient of variation at \(\sqrt7\), with only a \(4/3\) increase in mean search time.
