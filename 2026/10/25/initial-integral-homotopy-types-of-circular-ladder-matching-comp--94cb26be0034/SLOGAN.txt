The ordinary matching complexes of the circular prisms \(C_n\square K_2\) have exact integral sphere-wedge homotopy types for every \(3\le n\le8\), with new four-case classifications at \(n=5,6,7,8\). The five- and eight-rung cases have mixed-dimensional sphere wedges, proved by complete acyclic discrete-Morse matchings and signed attaching-degree matrices rather than by homology ranks alone.
