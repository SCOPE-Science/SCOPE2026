For every \(n\ge5\), the ordinary three-cut complex of the crown graph has torsion-free reduced integral homology of ranks \(1+n(n-2)(n-3)(n-5)/4\) and \(n\) in exactly two degrees, and is a pure nonshellable complex. The complete rational \(S_n\times C_2\) characters on both homology groups are explicit functions of the numbers of \(1\)-, \(2\)-, and \(4\)-cycles in a label permutation.
