For ordinary \(k\)-cut complexes of graphs with girth at least \(k+2\), \(k\ge4\), the complete integral graph-automorphism homology lattice is the orientation-twisted dual of an explicit augmentation-exterior-power quotient by connected \(k\)-set boundaries. The quotient is torsion-free and determines the action over every coefficient field, whereas earlier nonequivariant sphere counts and rational virtual characters do not specify this integral lattice.
