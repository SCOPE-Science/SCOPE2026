For every \(n\ge5\), the integral degree-two homology of the open-neighborhood complex of \(K_n\square K_2\) is the row-and-column-zero matrix lattice supported off the diagonal, with layer exchange acting by matrix transposition. Its rational symmetry types are exactly \(S^{(n-2,2)}\) and \(S^{(n-2,1,1)}\), and the two high-dimensional sphere classes carry the sign-twisted permutation action.
