The ordinary matching complex of \(m\) parallel four-edge paths between two poles has an explicit integral \(S_m\times C_2\) top-homology module: the augmentation lattice \(U_m\), twisted by the arm-permutation sign and by pole-reversal sign to the power \(m\). Its nonequivariant sphere-wedge homotopy type is already implied by published path-contraction results; the contribution is the full integral symmetry structure.
