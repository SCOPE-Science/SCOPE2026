For the canonical integral Lie rings with positive diagonal weights arising from the classified almost-abelian family, all higher integral Lie cohomology is finite torsion: the summands in degree \(k\) have orders equal to the positive sums of the weights on \((k-1)\)-element subsets. The primes at which reduction changes cohomology are exactly the divisors of these subset sums, and already three-dimensional examples carry arbitrarily large coprime torsion orders in consecutive degrees.
