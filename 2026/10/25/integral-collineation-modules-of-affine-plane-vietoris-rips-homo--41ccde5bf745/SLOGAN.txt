For every finite affine plane of order \(q\ge3\), the scale-two incidence-graph Rips homology carries two natural **integral** collineation modules: the incidence-cycle lattice in degree two and an orientation-twisted tensor of parallel-class augmentation lattices in degree \(q\). On a Desarguesian affine plane, translation quotienting kills degree-two rational homology and retains degree-\(q\) rational homology exactly when \(q\ge4\).
