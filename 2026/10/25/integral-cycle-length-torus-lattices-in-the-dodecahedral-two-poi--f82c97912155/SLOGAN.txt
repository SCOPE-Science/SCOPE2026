For unordered two-point configurations on the dodecahedral graph, the exact integral quotient by product tori of each pair of simple-cycle lengths is classified. The 36 disjoint-pentagon tori form an integral basis, while the 75 octagon–octagon tori span rationally but generate a sublattice of index \(4096\), with quotient \((\mathbb Z/2)^{12}\).
