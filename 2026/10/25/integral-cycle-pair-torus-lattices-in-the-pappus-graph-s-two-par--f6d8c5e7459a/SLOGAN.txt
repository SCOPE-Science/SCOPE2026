The 45 product tori from disjoint hexagons in the Pappus graph form an integral generating family for \(H_2(\operatorname{UConf}_2(|P|);\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^{27}\). Its two automorphism orbits of shortest-cycle tori have ranks \(17\) and \(27\), but the full-rank orbit misses an index-\(32\) sublattice quotient \((\mathbb Z/2)^5\); other length classes exhibit a genuine \(\mathbb Z/4\) obstruction.
