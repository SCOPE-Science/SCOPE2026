For \(n\ge7\), the \(n\) integral two-sphere homology generators of \(\operatorname{VR}_{\le2}(C_5\square C_n)\) form the **edge-permutation lattice of the column cycle, twisted by both dihedral reflection signs**. This determines every \(D_5\times D_n\) homology trace and makes the full symmetry-invariant positive-degree rational homology vanish.
