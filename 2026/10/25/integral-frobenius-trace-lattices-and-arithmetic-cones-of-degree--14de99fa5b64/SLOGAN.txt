For every smooth degree-five del Pezzo surface over a finite field, the Frobenius-orbit traces of its ten exceptional curves span the arithmetic effective cone, but fail to generate the full integral Picard lattice precisely for the \((3,1,1)\) and \((4,1)\) Frobenius types, with indices \(3\) and \(2\). A uniform seven-row classification also determines every arithmetic Picard discriminant group and the number of Mori and nef extremal rays.
