On the Fermat quartic K3 surface over \(\mathbb F_5\), twenty-four Frobenius traces of conjugate lines generate the entire arithmetic Picard lattice integrally, even though the surface has no rational points and no rational lines. This rank-eight lattice has discriminant \(-4096\) and discriminant group \((\mathbb Z/2)^6\oplus(\mathbb Z/8)^2\).
