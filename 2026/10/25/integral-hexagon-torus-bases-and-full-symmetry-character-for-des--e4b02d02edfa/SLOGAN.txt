For two unordered particles on the Desargues incidence graph, thirty-five disjoint-hexagon product tori from a single sixty-element symmetry orbit form an integral basis of \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z^{35}\), while the other ten-element orbit spans a primitive rank-ten sublattice. The full \(S_5\times C_2\) rational character is explicitly determined, with central-complement trace \(-5\) and no invariant direction.
