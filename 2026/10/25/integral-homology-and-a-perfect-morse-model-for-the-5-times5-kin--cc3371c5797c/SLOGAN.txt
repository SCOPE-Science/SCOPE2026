The nonattacking-king complex of the nonperiodic \(5\times5\) board is 2-connected, with torsion-free reduced integral homology of ranks \(45\), \(32\) and \(2\) in degrees \(3\), \(4\) and \(5\), respectively. An exhaustive acyclic matching yields a perfect 80-cell CW model with zero integer cellular differential; it does not determine the five-cell attaching maps.
