For every hypercube \(Q_d\) with \(d\ge3\), the ordinary three-cut complex has torsion-free integral homology in exactly two adjacent dimensions, with explicit ranks \(2^{d-2}(4-2d+\binom d2)-1\) and \(2^{d-1}(2^d-1-d-\binom d2)\). Its Alexander dual is a wedge of circles and two-spheres, proving nonshellability throughout the family.
