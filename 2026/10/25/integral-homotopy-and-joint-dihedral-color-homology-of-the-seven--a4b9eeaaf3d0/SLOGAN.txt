The independence complex of the categorical graph product \(C_7\times K_3\) is integrally \(S^3\vee3S^4\); its complete rational \(D_7\times S_3\)-homology action is \(\varepsilon\boxtimes\operatorname{sgn}\) in degree three and \(\mathbf1\boxtimes(\operatorname{sgn}\oplus V_{\mathrm{std}})\) in degree four. An exhaustive integral discrete-Morse certificate and signed action calculation prove the homotopy and character statements for this finite graph.
