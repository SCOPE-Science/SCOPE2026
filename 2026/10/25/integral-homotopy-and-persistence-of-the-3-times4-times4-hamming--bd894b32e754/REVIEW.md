# Scientific review: \(3\times4\times4\) Hamming-box filtration

**Correctness: PASS.** Directly reconstructed the 48-vertex categorical product, its 432 edges, the complete 243270-face augmented independence complex, and a deterministic matching of 121536 disjoint codimension-one pairs. Checked every one of 1843680 directed Hasse incidences; the topological sort removes all 243270 faces. The resulting ordinary Morse model contains one zero-cell and 198 three-cells, proving the *integral homotopy* type, not just a field-valued Betti calculation. Separately established the radius-one nerve and its cycle rank 57 by the contractible coordinate-line cover. The only radius values are zero, one, two and three, determining the full persistence filtration.

**Originality: PASS, with residual risk.** The closest primary full texts are Goyal--Shukla--Singh (2019), §3 (two factors and a three-factor conjecture), and Antolín Camarena--Carnero Bravo (2023), introduction, §4, Proposition 25 and Conjecture 26 (three factors with at least one binary coordinate; general three-factor result conjectural). Neither proves the case with alphabets \((3,4,4)\). The previously reported \((3,3,3)\) and \((3,3,4)\) cases also do not imply this 48-point homotopy type by any stated parameter-stability result. Searches for exact numerical/symbolic statements, Hamming-box aliases, the broader triple-product conjecture, and prior table coverage revealed no equivalent or stronger covering result. This does not exclude an unindexed proof, and the published conjecture itself predicts the wedge dimension.

**Value: PASS.** The first two-quaternary-coordinate instance tests the conjectured three-sphere homotopy concentration outside both the proved binary-factor regime and the one-quaternary-coordinate cases. It gives a complete *integral homotopy and persistence* classification rather than only a face count, Euler characteristic, or rational Betti rank. The exact 198-sphere multiplicity is not a corollary of the cited general results and supplies a reproducible benchmark for a structurally new mixed Hamming box. The result is finite, not a solution to the full family.
