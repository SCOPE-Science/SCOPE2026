The 48-point \(3\times4\times4\) Hamming box has a complete integral Vietoris--Rips filtration consisting successively of 48 points, \(\bigvee^{57}S^1\), \(\bigvee^{198}S^3\), and a point. An exhaustive acyclic discrete-Morse matching proves \(\operatorname{Ind}(K_3\times K_4\times K_4)\simeq\bigvee^{198}S^3\), a finite two-quaternary-coordinate case of a published three-factor sphere-wedge conjecture.
