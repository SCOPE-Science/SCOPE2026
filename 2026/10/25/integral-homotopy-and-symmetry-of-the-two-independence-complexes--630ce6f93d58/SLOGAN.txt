For every tree of diameter at most four, the two-independence complex has an explicit integral sphere-wedge homotopy type determined solely by its depth-two branch sizes; at exact diameter four, the noncontractible cases are \(\bigvee^{\prod_i(b_i-1)}S^{2k-1}\). Their top integral homology is the tensor product of the leaf-set augmentation lattices, with an explicit full automorphism character.
