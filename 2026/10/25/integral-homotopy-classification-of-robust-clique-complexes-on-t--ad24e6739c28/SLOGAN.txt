All robust clique complexes of the \(4\times4\) square grid have been classified up to **integral homotopy**: for \(2\le k\le8\), they are wedges of \(9,36,84,122,102,29,1\) spheres respectively, always of dimension \(2k-3\), and they are contractible for \(k\ge9\). The previously unknown homotopy decompositions at \(k=4,5,6,7\) are certified by exhaustive acyclic discrete-Morse matchings; their numerical Betti ranks were already known.
