For every odd integer \(n\ge3\), the full clique complex of the cyclic Latin-square graph on \((\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)^2\) is homotopy equivalent to a wedge of exactly \((n-1)^3\) two-spheres. This upgrades the known Betti-number identity to an integral homotopy classification by proving simple connectivity and a two-dimensional CW model.
