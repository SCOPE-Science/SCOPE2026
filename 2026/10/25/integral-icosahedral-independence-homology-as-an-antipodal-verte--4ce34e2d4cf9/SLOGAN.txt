The ordinary independence complex of the icosahedral graph is a two-sphere with six antipodal edges attached, hence has homotopy type \(S^2\vee\bigvee^6 S^1\). Its integral first homology is canonically the anti-invariant lattice of the twelve vertices under antipodal inversion, yielding the rational icosahedral rotation character \((6,-2,0,1,1)\); top homology is the inversion-sign line.
