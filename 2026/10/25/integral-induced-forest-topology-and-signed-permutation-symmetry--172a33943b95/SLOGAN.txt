The induced-forest complex of the sixteen-vertex, five-regular Clebsch graph is integrally homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{15}S^5\). Its full \((\mathbb Z/2)^4\rtimes\mathfrak S_5\)-action on rational fifth homology is the sign twist of the sixteen-point augmentation representation, splitting into irreducible summands of dimensions five and ten.
