For the integral deck-quotient refinement of signed GLMY graph homology, every connected unbalanced signing has genuine degree-zero \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\) torsion even though its double cover has no anti-invariant integral degree-zero homology. With no triangles or four-cycles, degree-one homology is free of rank \(|E|-|V|+1\) for balanced signings and one less otherwise, via an exact integral incidence reduction.
