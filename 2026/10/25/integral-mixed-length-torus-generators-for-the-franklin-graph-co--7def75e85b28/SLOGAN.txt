For the nonplanar Franklin graph, \(H_2(\operatorname{UConf}_2(|F|);\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^9\) has an explicit integral basis made of six square–hexagon and three hexagon–hexagon product tori. All square–square torus classes are already in the square–hexagon lattice, but at least three hexagon–hexagon classes are essential; the complete length-type lattice quotients are torsion-free.
