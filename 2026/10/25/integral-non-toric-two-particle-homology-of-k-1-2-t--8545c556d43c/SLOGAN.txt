For the nonplanar three-part graph \(K_{1,2,t}\), \(t\ge4\), the complete integral top homology of two unordered particles is the sign-twisted kernel of the unsigned complete-graph incidence map, naturally under \(S_2\times S_t\). Its rank is \(t(t-3)/2\), with explicit cellular cycles despite the impossibility of two disjoint graph cycles; the ordinary Betti rank and the general existence of non-toric classes have prior precedents.
