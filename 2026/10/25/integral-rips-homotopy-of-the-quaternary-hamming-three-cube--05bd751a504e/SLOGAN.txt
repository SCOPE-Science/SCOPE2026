The sixty-four-point quaternary Hamming three-cube has a scale-two Vietoris--Rips complex homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{405}S^3\), proving the previously conjectured \((4,4,4)\) complete-graph-product case. Its integral homotopy types pass from \(64\) points through \(81\) circles and then \(405\) three-spheres before becoming contractible.
