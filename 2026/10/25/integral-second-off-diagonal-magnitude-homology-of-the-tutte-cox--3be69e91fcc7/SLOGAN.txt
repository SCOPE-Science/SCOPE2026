For the Tutte–Coxeter graph, the integral magnitude group \(\operatorname{MH}_{3,5}(-;\mathbb Z)\) is torsion-free of rank \(1440\), with one copy of \(\mathbb Z^4\) for each ordered distance-three pair and no contribution from other endpoints. Exact signed Smith reductions strengthen an existing characteristic-two rank calculation to an all-primes integral classification.
