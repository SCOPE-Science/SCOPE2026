For two unordered particles on the Nauru graph, \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z^{54}\), with an explicit integral basis of 54 product tori built from disjoint eight-cycles. Six–eight-cycle tori span rationally but have integral index four, while disjoint hexagon tori generate a primitive rank-30 sublattice; all short-cycle-pair symmetry orbits are classified.
