For every \(n\ge3\), the top **integral** homology of the loopless higher-matching complex \(M_{n-2}(K_n)\) is the standard \(S_n\) augmentation lattice, twisted by sign exactly when \(n\) is even; all other reduced homology vanishes. Its complete rational character is \(\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)^{n-1}(\#\operatorname{Fix}(\sigma)-1)\), refining the known sphere count.
