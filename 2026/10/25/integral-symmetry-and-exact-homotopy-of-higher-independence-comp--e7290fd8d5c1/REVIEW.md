# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS

The direct partition of faces by whether both central vertices are present gives two contractible subcomplexes with a suspended truncated-simplex intersection. Exact augmented integer chains establish \(\widetilde H_{r-1}\cong\varepsilon\otimes\bigwedge^{r-2}E\), including the orientation sign when the centers are exchanged. The truncated simplex has the stated sphere-wedge homotopy type, and rational invariant theory establishes the full automorphism quotient ranks. The formal \(r=2\) empty-face case and contractible \(r\ge a+b+2\) case are separately checked. Forty-five exhaustive finite tests confirm mod-two homology ranks and signed traces, but the general claim relies on the proof.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

Deshpande–Singh (arXiv:2001.05448) treats other exact graph families and covers some special limiting examples; Deshpande–Shukla–Singh (arXiv:2001.06775) covers general chordal sphere-wedge existence. Abdelmalek et al. (arXiv:2106.10863) prove caterpillar vertex decomposability, while Sawant (arXiv:2605.25150) gives a stronger all-tree structural theorem. The explicit double-star homology lattice and center-swap sign are not an implication of these inspected statements. Actual searches for double-star/bistar, alternative graph terminology and equivariant exterior powers returned closest records on *different* multipath and discrete-Morse complexes; see the structured audit for comparison. Unindexed equivalent research remains a risk.

## Value — PASS

The result gives a complete natural two-branch family, a binomial sphere count determined only by total leaves, integral automorphism modules, and a balanced-versus-unbalanced quotient invariant distinguishing symmetry geometry. These are not a renaming of a generic shellability theorem.

## Scope and limitations

This is ordinary homotopy type plus integral homology-module information for \(a,b\ge1\), \(r\ge2\). It does not describe equivariant homotopy type or integral orbit-space torsion; no independent validation has been performed.
