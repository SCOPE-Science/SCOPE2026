For every double-star tree, the higher independence complex has a closed-form integral sphere-wedge homotopy type and a canonical automorphism representation \(\widetilde H_{r-1}\cong\varepsilon\otimes\bigwedge^{r-2}\ker(\mathbb Z[L]\to\mathbb Z)\). Exchanging equal-sized leaf branches kills rational quotient homology except for one degree-two class at \(r=3\).
