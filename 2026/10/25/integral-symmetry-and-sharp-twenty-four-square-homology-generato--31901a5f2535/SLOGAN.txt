For two unordered particles on \(K_{3,n}\), \(n\ge4\), the integral second-homology module is exactly \(\operatorname{sgn}_{S_3}\boxtimes\ker(\mathbb Z^{\binom n2}\to\mathbb Z^n)\), with the unsigned incidence map on right-vertex pairs. Every nonzero two-cycle in the canonical cubical model uses at least 24 squares, and all \(3\binom n4\) primitive minimum-support classes generate the entire integral group.
