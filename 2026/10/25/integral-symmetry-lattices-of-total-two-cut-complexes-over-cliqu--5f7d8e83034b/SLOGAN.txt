For every clique–tree product \(K_m\square T\), the total two-cut complex has integral top homology equal to a signed tree-edge permutation lattice tensored with the symmetric-group weight lattice \(\mathbb Z^m/\mathbb Z(1,\ldots,1)\). The natural equivariant lattice extension is nonsplit of exact order \(m\), although the homology itself is free abelian and the extension splits rationally.
