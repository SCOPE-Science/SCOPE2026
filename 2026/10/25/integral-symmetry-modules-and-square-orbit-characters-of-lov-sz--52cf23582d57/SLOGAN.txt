For every connected graph whose distinct four-cycles never share an edge, the open-neighborhood complex has a canonical automorphism-equivariant model obtained by filling both lifts of each square in the bipartite double cover. Its full rational first-homology character is \(c_\gamma+2e_\gamma-2v_\gamma-\sum_{\gamma Q=Q}\epsilon_\gamma(Q)\), with an explicit integral signed-square-module presentation.
