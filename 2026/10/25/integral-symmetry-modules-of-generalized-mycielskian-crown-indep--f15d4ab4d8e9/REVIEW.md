# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The graph, group, and integral augmentation module are explicitly defined. The independence complex of a crown graph is a two-simplex-union-and-bridges model with cycle lattice \(U_n\), and the part swap acts by a negative sign. The Kronecker double cover is two crown copies; the join interchange sign is positive in degree three. Entire-layer equivariant folds give three apex-deletion/link patterns, and the equivariant Mayer–Vietoris sequence yields the stated integral modules in all degrees. The formula for the part-exchange trace follows from the tensor-flip identity. The six small-case mod-two homology checks and 180 signed orbit enumerations agree with the claimed formulas. The proof, not these finite experiments, supports the infinite parameter range.

**Originality — PASS (best-knowledge assessment).** Carnero Bravo, *Independence complexes of generalized Mycielskian graphs* (arXiv:2601.13432, full HTML text inspected, particularly Theorem 5, Proposition 10, Corollary 8), already proves the nonequivariant homotopy and Betti ranks of this crown family, but does not provide its joint integral \(S_n\times C_2\) homology action. Goyal–Shukla–Singh, *Homotopy Type of Independence Complexes of Certain Families of Graphs* (arXiv:1905.06926, full HTML text inspected) proves underlying complete-graph-product results and complete-graph Mycielskians, not these actions. A related research record gives symmetric-group homology modules for generalized Mycielskians of complete graphs, a different base family with no crown part-exchange action. Direct published-finding corpus and literature queries for crown/Mycielskian/representation, and related aliases, found no implication of the claimed signed tensor-pair action. An equivalent uninspected equivariant classification remains possible; a negative search is not a uniqueness proof.

The induced representation and all joint character values are determined, not just the identity trace or isolated examples. This answers a motivated symmetry-refinement question rather than merely reevaluating known Betti numbers.

**Scientific boundaries.** No claim is made about genuinely equivariant homotopy equivalence, topology of fixed-point spaces, integral quotient homology, or higher iterates. The verification script is finite evidence, not an exhaustive proof. The closest source already subsumes every nonequivariant rank/dimension computation.
