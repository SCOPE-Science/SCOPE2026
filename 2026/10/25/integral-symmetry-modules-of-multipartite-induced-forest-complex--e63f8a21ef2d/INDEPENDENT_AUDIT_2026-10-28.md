# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The package first proves an induced subgraph is a forest iff its chosen vertices lie in one part or exactly two parts with at least one singleton; three parts force triangles, and two parts each with >=2 force a square. The cover by two-part Y_ij has contractible intersections. In Y_ij the union of two contractible joins has intersection the complete bipartite graph K_(|A_i|,|A_j|); integral Mayer–Vietoris identifies its H2 with the primitive four-cycle lattice U_i⊗U_j. Four oriented vertices give a tetrahedral boundary cycle; exchanging the two 2-vertex blocks is an even orientation permutation, establishing unsigned flip. The cover spectral sequence has q=0 nerve homology H1(K_r)=∧²W and q=2 only p=0, with no possible differentials or extensions. The action of permuted cover labels and orientation signs is tracked; trace of ∧² W gives ((c1-1)(c1-2))/2-c2 and trace of tensor flip gives the stated h_ij-1 term. Singleton parts and r=2 fit the same proof. An independent full automorphism census of K_(2,2,2) checked all 48 actions via signed Lefschetz counts, using independently generated forest faces; source script also handles 11 finite examples but does not prove the general integral statement.
