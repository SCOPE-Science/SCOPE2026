The ordinary three-cut complex of the ternary Hamming three-cube \(H(3,3)\) has exactly \(\widetilde H_{22}=\mathbb Z^{53}\) and \(\widetilde H_{23}=\mathbb Z^{108}\), with no other reduced integral homology or torsion. Its nonzero next-to-top homology makes this natural, pure complex nonshellable.
