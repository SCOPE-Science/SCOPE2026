The bidirected \(K_{2,5}\) multipath complex has integral homology \(\widetilde H_2\cong\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\) and \(\widetilde H_3\cong\mathbb Z^{31}\), and its full rational \(S_5\times C_2\) homology representation splits into explicit 16-dimensional hub-symmetric and 15-dimensional hub-antisymmetric Specht sums. In particular, no nonzero rational third-homology class is fixed by all leaf permutations.
