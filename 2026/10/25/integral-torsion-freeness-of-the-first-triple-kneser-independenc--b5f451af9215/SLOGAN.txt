The first unresolved triple-Kneser independence complex, \(\operatorname{Ind}(\mathrm{KG}(3,1))\), is \(5\)-connected and has torsion-free reduced integral homology \(\mathbb Z^{29}\) in degree \(6\) and \(\mathbb Z^7\) in degree \(9\), with no other reduced homology. An exact acyclic Morse matching on a six-point transversal subcomplex, followed by unimodular integer boundary reductions, settles this first case of the published integral torsion question without asserting a sphere-wedge homotopy type.
