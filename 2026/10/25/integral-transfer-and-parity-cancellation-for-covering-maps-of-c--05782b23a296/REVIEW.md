# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The explicit cubical universal cover of Fujii--Iwamasa--Kimura--Nozaki--Suzuki gives deck period \(t/\gcd(t,2)\); the vertex-evaluation circle therefore has degree \(d(t)\), establishing the exact integer coefficient from the commutative evaluation diagram. Repetition of unit steps determines target winding and parity; component-wise Smith reduction gives the full kernel/cokernel. The independent finite checker verifies examples spanning both parity branches. The all-parameter theorem rests on the written proof, not finite enumeration.

## Originality
**PASS, with residual risk.** Čukić--Kozlov classify components and Fujii et al. compute the universal cover and circle-or-point topology, but neither inspected paper states an integral covering-induced first-homology morphism nor computes its Smith cokernel. Dochtermann establishes functoriality only, without these coefficients. The naturality argument is short but the parity cancellation and the full integral cokernel classification are distinct mathematical claims not implied by mere component counts or target-automorphism characters. Equivalent unindexed functorial calculations remain possible.

## Value
**PASS.** First-homology maps under graph coverings are structural functorial invariants, and their torsion cokernels distinguish coverings that act identically on component homotopy types. The contrast between a primitive two-sheeted odd-target transfer and the index-two even-target transfer is a motivated, nontrivial obstruction to naive geometric-degree transfer; the Smith classification works for every source/target winding and both independent covering degrees.

## Scope and risk
The result applies to canonical orientation-preserving cyclic coverings with both target lengths at least five, including source components that are circles only. It does not assert new nonequivariant homotopy classifications, a general graph-map theorem, or independent validation. Published full-text comparisons are limited to the three sources below.
