For every folded hypercube \(FQ_n\) with \(n\ge5\), the ordinary three-cut complex has a canonical \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\) torsion summand in degree \(2^{n-1}-5\), with all remaining integral homology given by explicit formulas. The source of torsion is the projective two-skeleton revealed by elementary collapses of its combinatorial Alexander dual.
