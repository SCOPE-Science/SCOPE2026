For every \(n\ge4\), the full integral vertex-permutation-and-particle-exchange homology of the two-point deleted product of the \((n-3)\)-skeleton of an \((n-1)\)-simplex is an orientation-twisted \(2n\)-point permutation lattice modulo its diagonal. This determines all symmetry characters and a parity transition in the rational homology of the particle-unlabeled quotient, beyond the previously known sphere-wedge count.
