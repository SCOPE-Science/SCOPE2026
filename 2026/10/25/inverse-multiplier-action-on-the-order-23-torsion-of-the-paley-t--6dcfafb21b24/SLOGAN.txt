The order-\(23\) torsion class in degree-three homology of the Paley tournament directed-flag complex transforms under every quadratic-residue affine symmetry \(u\mapsto au+b\) by the inverse multiplier \(a^{-1}\). In particular, scaling by \(2\) acts with order \(11\), a symmetry invariant absent from the known nonequivariant homotopy classification.
