# Review

## Correctness — PASS
The explicit two-qubit physical channel yields a three-dimensional recursion with characteristic polynomial and three distinct real modes for every fixed \(0<\lambda<1\) sufficiently near a perfect exchange. The root derivatives give the exact modal magnitude splitting, the coefficients converge to \(1/2,1/2,0\), and a parity-separated uniform sign argument establishes the count of positive increments. Independently simulated full density matrices match the three-variable recursion in 1,432 finite assertions. Those tests corroborate, and do not replace, the analytic asymptotic proof.

## Originality — PASS (best knowledge)
Bhoi--Shaji (arXiv:2606.14163v1) contains the repeated finite-environment setting and finite-window non-Markovianity regimes, but not this infinite-horizon fixed-input revival-count asymptotic. Ciccarello--Giovannetti (arXiv:1305.0225v1) has a different general renewal collision realization and no such near-exchange parity-mode count. Megier et al. (*Entropy* **23**, 905 (2021)) study revival counts of distinct jump/renewal channels. General backflow criteria from Breuer--Laine--Piilo are not claimed as new. A more general unlocated spectral theorem could subsume this specialization; priority is not independently certified.

## Value — PASS
An exact number of discrete information-return events captures a distinct aspect of quantum memory from the summed BLP backflow. The singular \(\cos^{-2}\theta\) horizon is a substantial, naturally motivated boundary, invisible to a fixed-length numerical phase diagram. A full coefficient, not only the divergence exponent, is obtained. The result is for one prescribed pair and fixed reservoir retention only.
