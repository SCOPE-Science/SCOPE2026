# Review of involution-quotient branch passports

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof gives all seven abstract involution classes, determines their fixed-point counts using the already established two-short-orbit action, applies the weakly ramified Riemann–Hurwitz formula, and distinguishes hyperelliptic and bielliptic quotients using the residual deck involution and Castelnuovo–Severi. The minimal-genus Artin–Schreier equation is justified by the independent biquadratic square, the exact branch locus, étale local comparison, and residue transfer. The packaged finite-group checker gives corroboration at five small powers of two. This check alone does not prove the all-power theorem.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** Full-text inspection of Tafazolian, arXiv:2609.14830v1, identified the original cover, its automorphism group, central quotients, and short orbits as *preexisting inputs*. Sections 4 and 6 do not supply the odd/even reflection quotient passport or the stated residue-normalized rational Artin–Schreier equation; Section 7 gives equations for the original curve, not this intermediate quotient. The proof compares implications rather than matching titles. Semantic search of published mathematical finding records and targeted literature did not identify the same equation. The full 2015 Giulietti–Korchmáros article could not be read; implicit earlier treatment remains possible, but no decisive prior coverage has been identified.

**Value — PASS.** The result classifies every involution quotient geometrically across a natural nontrivial infinite family and reveals both hyperelliptic and nonhyperelliptic quotients of the same genus. Beyond the numerical genera, it gives an intrinsic equation with \(q/2\) exact branch positions and nonzero principal-part coefficients, permitting further study of the quotient's moduli, isomorphisms, and arithmetic.

**Limits.** No claim about arbitrary subgroup quotients, \(q=4\), descent to finite ground fields, or independent certification is made. The external historical-priority risk is recorded separately.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
