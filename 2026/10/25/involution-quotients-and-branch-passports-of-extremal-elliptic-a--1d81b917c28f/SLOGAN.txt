For the ordinary genus-\(q+1\) elliptic Artin–Schreier curves with \(q=2^h\ge8\), all seven involution classes admit a complete geometric quotient census: the hyperelliptic quotient genera are \(q/2-1\), \(q/2\), and \(q/2+1\), while the two free central quotients are nonhyperelliptic and bielliptic. The minimal-genus quotient is explicitly a rational Artin–Schreier cover with \(q/2\) simple poles whose coefficients are intrinsic residues transferred through the elliptic double quotient.
