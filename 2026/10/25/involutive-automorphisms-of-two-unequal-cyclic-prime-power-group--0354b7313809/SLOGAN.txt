For unequal cyclic prime-power summands \(C_{p^a}\oplus C_{p^b}\), the number of automorphisms squaring to the identity is \(2+2p^{2a}\) for every odd prime, but exhibits a sharp three-regime exponent-gap transition at the prime two. Its binary count stabilizes once the larger cyclic exponent exceeds the smaller by at least three, with explicit low-exponent correction terms.
