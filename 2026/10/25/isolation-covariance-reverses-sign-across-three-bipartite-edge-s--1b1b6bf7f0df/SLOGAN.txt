In a random bipartite graph, the cross-class covariance of isolated-vertex counts is strictly negative throughout its nondegenerate range if the exact edge count is fixed, zero if endpoints are sampled with replacement, and positive under independent edges. In the balanced sparse regime, fixed-edge covariance tends to \(-c^2e^{-2c}/2\) while matched independent-edge covariance is asymptotic to \(cn e^{-2c}\).
