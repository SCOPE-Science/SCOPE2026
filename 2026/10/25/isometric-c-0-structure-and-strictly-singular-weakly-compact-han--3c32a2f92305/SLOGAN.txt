For every infinite set \(S\subseteq\mathbb N\), the Boolean derivative space of the matched-block language \(\{0^n1^n:n\in S\}\) is linearly isometric to \(c_0\), and its language Hankel map is weakly compact and strictly singular despite being noncompact. Its exact compact-distance \(\tfrac12\) is the previously known general nonregular-language bound, here realized by this entire class.
