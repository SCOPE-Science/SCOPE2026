For every finite positively weighted path, the sharp one-input energy amplification caused by increasing sorting is the maximum ratio of a prefix-edge weight to the minimum same-cardinality cut; surprisingly, this constant is identical for every nonzero convex edge-energy density. One centered weak defect remains harmless in even order, but two centered weak defects in odd order yield unbounded amplification as their common weight vanishes, with exact contractivity windows determined by the thresholds \(1/2\) and \(2\).
