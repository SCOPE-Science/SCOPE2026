For every clique bouquet \(K_1\vee(K_{m_1}\sqcup\cdots\sqcup K_{m_r})\) with \(r\ge2\) and \(m_i\ge2\), conditioning on the central label gives the complete Italian domination polynomial \(x^2\prod_i A_{m_i}+x\prod_i(A_{m_i}-1)+\prod_i(A_{m_i}-1-m_i x)\), where \(A_m=(1+x+x^2)^m\). This counts every feasible weight, including all friendship graphs and heterogeneous generalized windmills.
