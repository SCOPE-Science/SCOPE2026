For ordered pairs of particles separated by a positive clearance on an equilateral \(m\)-arm metric star, the complete integral \(S_m\times C_2\)-equivariant persistence has one sharp transition at clearance \(1\). In its connected regime, rational first homology splits into the particle-exchange-even Specht module \(S^{(m-2,1,1)}\) and the exchange-odd Specht module \(S^{(m-2,2)}\) (the latter vanishes at \(m=3\)).
