For the canonical cube–crosspolytope polar signohedron in dimension \(d\), a random Euclidean supporting vertex uses asymptotically \(2\log d\) nonzero coordinates despite the polytope having \(3^d-1\) vertices. After explicit Gaussian-tail centering, the directional width and support rank have a joint normal limit with covariance matrix \(\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}\).
