# Scientific review: Joint Poisson phase diagram for global predicate supervenience

## Correctness — PASS

For fixed r and a, the proof reconstructs global supervenience as pairwise equality of complete predicate extensions. Mixed factorial moments: disjoint labelled pairs give (m)_(2K)/(2^K q^(aK)) -> (c²/2)^K; any connected component of ≥3 world vertices is o(1) by spanning-tree q^(-a(v-1)), and repeated differently marked edges on one pair cost an extra q^-1. Markov excludes all collisions on >a predicates; a pairwise-independence variance calculation excludes every nontrivial implication from <a predicates. The proof handles all A,B simultaneously and proves the limiting count of size-a minimal determining families. Exhaustive finite tests exercise the mapping and moment identities but are not used as an infinite proof.

The two-vertex repeated-edge bound and the \(v\ge3\) connected-component spanning-tree bound cover all non-disjoint factorial-moment terms, including cycles and differently marked pairs. For \(H\subseteq[r]\) with \(|H|<a\), the pairwise-independent variance argument holds even when two compared world-pairs share one world, because the conditional event probability is independent of the shared world's value. The resulting theorem is simultaneous over finitely many subsets, not merely pointwise in one dependence statement.

## Value — PASS

A natural structural question in finite-world supervenience is which families of predicate extensions determine all others, and how these dependencies co-occur. The theorem gives a complete nondegenerate law for that entire logical dependence matrix at each natural birthday threshold, including independently varying critical families and a binomial minimal-key count. This goes beyond known one-implication marginal estimates and supplies an interpretable random-model phase diagram.

## Limitations

Only independent equiprobable predicate extensions in complete S5 clusters with fixed \(r,a,c\) are covered. No stronger claim of model-theoretic generality, independent originality certification, or a new marginal birthday approximation is made.
