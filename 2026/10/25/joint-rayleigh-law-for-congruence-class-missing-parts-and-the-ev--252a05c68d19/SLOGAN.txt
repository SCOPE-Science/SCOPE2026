For every fixed modulus, the least absent parts in the individual congruence classes of a uniformly random ordinary partition converge jointly, after division by \(n^{1/4}\), to independent identically distributed Rayleigh variables. The first missing part among any fixed set of residue classes consequently chooses its class asymptotically uniformly; in particular the Andrews–El Bachraoui even-companion partition counts satisfy \(a_e(n)=b_e(n)\sim p(n)/3\) and \(t_e(n)\sim2p(n)/3\).
