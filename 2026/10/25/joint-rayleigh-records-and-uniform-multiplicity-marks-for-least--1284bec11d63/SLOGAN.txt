The entire hierarchy of fixed least multiplicity gaps in a random partition converges to nested minima of independent Rayleigh variables, rather than independent gaps. At the first part deficient in \(s\) copies, its actual multiplicity is asymptotically uniform on \(\{0,\ldots,s-1\}\) and independent of the gap location; consequently \(\Pr(M_r=M_s)\to r/s\) for \(1\le r<s\).
