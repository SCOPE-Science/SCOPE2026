The matching complexes of the categorical products \(P_\ell\times P_3\) have a complete, explicit rational Klein-four homology character under reversal of either path; the character alternates with \(\ell\) and exhibits exponentially growing reflection traces. Consequently the full two-reflection quotient has an exact rational top Betti number for every \(\ell\ge2\), refining previously known sphere-wedge homotopy types.
