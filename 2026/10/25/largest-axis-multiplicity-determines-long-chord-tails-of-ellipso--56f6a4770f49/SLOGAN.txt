For an ellipsoid in \(d\) dimensions, the near-diameter tail of an invariantly sampled random affine-line chord has exponent \((2d-k-1)/2\), where \(k\) is the multiplicity of its longest semiaxis, and an explicit positive coefficient. This separates triaxial and prolate, oblate, and spherical bodies by their long-chord decay while preserving the required intersection-conditioned line measure.
