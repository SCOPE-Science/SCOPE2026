For delayed fourth-order extrapolation of Strang splitting, every two-state Markov problem has the exact identity \(M_{h,p}=M_{h,1}^{p}\) and is positivity-safe for all step sizes and delays. Three states are minimal for failure: a directed equal-rate pure-birth split loses positivity exactly beyond \(h=4\log(4+\sqrt{15})/\lambda\), independently of the delay length.
