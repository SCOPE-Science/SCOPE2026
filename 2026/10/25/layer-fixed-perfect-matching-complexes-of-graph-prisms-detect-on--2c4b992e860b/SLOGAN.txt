For every finite graph \(H\), the fixed-point space of the layer interchange on the perfect-matching complex of \(H\square K_2\) is exactly the matching complex of the fully whiskered graph \(W(H)\). Its integral homotopy type is a wedge of one \(S^{|V(H)|/2-1}\) per perfect matching of \(H\), or is contractible when no such matching exists; this determines the reduced Lefschetz trace.
