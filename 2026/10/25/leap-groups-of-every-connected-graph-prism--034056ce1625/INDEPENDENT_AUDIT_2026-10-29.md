For each base edge uv, a directed square 4-cycle hop with vertical hops outside, composed with the global vertical hop, gives a single diagonal transposition ((u,0),(v,1)); reversing square gives the complementary diagonal transposition. These are exactly the edge transpositions in the bipartite double cover D(G). For connected G, D is connected iff G has an odd cycle, giving all S_{2n}. If G is bipartite, D has two connected size-n components and generates Sym(X)×Sym(Y); every hop on bipartite prism swaps the bipartition classes and the vertical hop supplies their exchange, yielding wreath S_n wr C2. The n=1 case is K2 and is included. Independent direct hop enumeration for K1,K2,P3,K3 gave group orders 2,8,72,720; general result derives from the transposition argument, not small examples.
