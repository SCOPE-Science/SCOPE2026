## Correctness

Solving the three exact commutator equations gives the centralizer basis in each disjoint coordinate regime. The internal Lie brackets distinguish the two five-dimensional types by the coefficient \(-2be\), proving the sharp characteristic-two collapse. The six explicit counts add to \(q^6\). The separate exact checker constructs centralizer nullspaces and both lower-central terms over \(\mathbb F_2,\mathbb F_3,\mathbb F_4,\mathbb F_5\), corroborating every claimed multiplicity. These finite tests do not establish the general theorem; the displayed bases and brackets do.

Soffer (arXiv:1411.5389v1) supplies upper bounds for group conjugacy-class numbers, not isomorphism of centralizer Lie subalgebras. Bhunia (arXiv:1901.07869v1) studies group-centralizer conjugacy (z-classes), not this abstract Lie-isomorphism census. Targeted semantic searches did not locate an equivalent statement. Absolute priority remains uncertain.

## Value

The characteristic-two collapse is structural: a five-dimensional class-three Lie centralizer cannot occur in even characteristic while its exact frequency over odd characteristic is \(q(q-1)^2\). The two five-dimensional types have identical \(\operatorname{ad}\)-rank and nilpotency indices, demonstrating that standard centralizer sizes and adjoint rank profiles cannot recover the full answer.
