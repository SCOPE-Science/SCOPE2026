# Review

## Correctness

The explicit family \(\mathcal F_n\) has size \(2n+2\). The incomparable-pair cases exhaust the possible pairs in the family and rule out an induced copy of \(P\). For each missing set \(S\), choosing \(i,j\in S\) distinct and \(k\notin S\) constructs the required five sets. The lower bound follows from the published theorem for posets with legs, whose hypothesis is satisfied by \(P\).

## Originality

The closest literature is Ivan and Jaffe’s linear-upper-bound theorem for complete layered posets with no adjacent singleton layers, together with their conjecture covering all layer patterns having a non-singleton level. The target pattern \((2,1,1,1)\) is outside the theorem’s hypotheses. Freschi et al. provide a lower bound for posets with legs but not the upper construction. A recent classification of all posets with at most four elements does not cover this five-element poset. Targeted searches did not find the same claim; absolute priority is not asserted.

## Value

The result establishes linear induced-saturation growth for a specific five-element layered poset with three consecutive singleton levels, giving a concrete instance of the broader conjectured class. It does not determine the exact saturation number or optimal leading constant.
