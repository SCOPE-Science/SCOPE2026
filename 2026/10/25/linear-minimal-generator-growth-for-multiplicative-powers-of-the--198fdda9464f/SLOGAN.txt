The ideal-multiplication powers of the smallest three-generated ordinary numerical semigroup, \(H=\langle3,4,5\rangle\), have exactly \(2k+1\) minimal additive generators at every exponent \(k\ge1\). Their complete normal form consists of the \(k+1\) products in \(3,4\) and the \(k\) products with a single factor of \(5\); the identity \(25=9+16\) and a sharp divisibility argument prove both generation and minimality.
