For every fixed number \(n\ge3\) of independent points sampled on a smooth positively curved convex curve, the probability that their convex hull contains a point at inward depth \(\delta\) is asymptotic to \(2n(n-1)f(z)^2\delta\log(L/\delta)/\kappa(z)\). Under uniform arclength sampling this local rare-event law determines the boundary curvature \(\kappa(z)\).
