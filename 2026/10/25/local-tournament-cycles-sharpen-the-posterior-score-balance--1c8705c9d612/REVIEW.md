# Review of Local tournament cycles sharpen the posterior score balance

## Correctness
PASS. The exact conditional fair-crossing-edge binomial law is independently derived, the likelihood ratio increases with score balance, and the strictness boundary follows from the strictly increasing number of crossing pairs. Full directional-reversal symmetry yields the fixed conditional score mean; the variance formula and covariance moment identity follow without simulation. The independent brute-force orientation verifier checked every tournament for sizes 3 through 7, with 681 exact assertion groups. Finite tests supplement rather than prove the infinite statement.

## Originality
PASS, provisional. Liu's 2012 complete Section 4 treats connectivity of cyclic triangles in prime deterministic tournaments; Sanchez and Kolesnik's 2020 full article concerns tournament score/mean-score polytope feasibility. Neither gives the exact posterior distribution of rooted cyclic triangle counts. Andersson's 1998 paper on asymptotic global random-tournament motif counts and Nowicki's 1989 triad-count statistics could not be read in full; a hidden historical implication cannot be excluded. The general binomial monotone-likelihood-ratio principle is not claimed as new. Targeted database/published-record comparisons found no dominating statement, but absence of a hit is not a proof of priority.

## Value
PASS. Conditioning on more directed cycles sharpens the entire score-balance posterior in likelihood-ratio order, including an exact size-parity plateau cutoff, even though the signed score has unchanged conditional mean and zero covariance with cycle count. The complete finite-sample conditional law and variance contraction are more informative than isolated mixed-moment calculations. The result concerns an intrinsic tournament statistic, not an arbitrary numerical slice.

## Limitations
Uniform independent fair orientations only. Single fixed vertex, local cycles only. The analytically established theorem does not order a full tournament score vector. Older inaccessible works and equivalent parameterizations remain a publication-priority risk. Independent mathematical audit has not occurred.
