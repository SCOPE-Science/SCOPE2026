A local positive site dephaser and a local irreversible absorber exclude precisely the same Hamiltonian dark directions from infinite-time survival on any finite connected positive-weight quantum network, although their finite-time dynamics differ. On a cycle with a sink at zero and a single dephaser at \(s\), survival of an excitation at \(a\) is exactly \(\gcd(n,2s)/(2n)\) when \(\gcd(n,2s)
mid2a\), and zero otherwise.
