In the crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M_n\), locating-total domination is exactly the rule “at least two selected vertices on each side, and at most one matching index entirely unselected.” This yields the full two-variable set enumerator, and every inclusion-minimal set is minimum, with exactly \(n(2^{n-1}-2n)\) minimum sets for \(n\ge5\) and explicit small exceptions.
