# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The exact all-dimensional support-function identity separates all connected bichromatic subsets from their monochromatic counterparts. Gaussian projection provides the intrinsic spherical law. Extreme-order Poisson convergence, a geometrically weighted uniform tail bound, explicit suppression of the smaller-class term and radial control yield the distributional assertions; gamma-log covariance identities give both moments. A standalone checker verifies 520 exhaustive-support comparisons and independent moment diagnostics. The numerical check is not used as proof of asymptotic claims.

## Originality

**PASS, qualified.** The closest generalized-permutahedra paper studies Minkowski linear functionals and Ehrhart polynomials, and the closest graph-associahedron work treats combinatorial diameters. Existing related biclique inradius, stellohedron asymmetry and interval-associahedron Poisson-width findings do not imply this logarithmic class-size crossover. Full texts of the two scientifically closest references were materially inspected. The algebraic-structures reference was available at abstract level only; other less-indexed Gaussian-functional treatments remain a bibliographic risk, and the finding does not claim absolute priority.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The exact normalization reveals a nontrivial structural phase transition for a natural family of high-dimensional generalized permutahedra. The threshold \(p\asymp\log_2\log n\), full limiting non-Gaussian law and explicit moments provide more than a routine finite-dimensional invariant and separate near-complete bipartitions from shrinking-but-diverging sides.

## Scientific limitations

The theorem depends on the canonical unweighted Minkowski-sum realization, isotropic intrinsic Euclidean widths and \(p\to\infty\). There is no claim for fixed \(p\), arbitrary affine realizations or independence from all unpublished literature. The mathematical demonstration remains subject to external independent checking.
