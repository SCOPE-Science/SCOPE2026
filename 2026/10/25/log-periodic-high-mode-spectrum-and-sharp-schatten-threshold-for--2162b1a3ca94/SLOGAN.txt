For a critical infinite union of hyperbolic Cantor annuli, the weighted Bergman localization operator is compact but its monomial eigenvalues have a **genuinely nonconstant log-periodic** leading law \(\lambda_k=k^{\delta-1}(P_\alpha(k)+o(1))\), where \(\delta=\log 2/\log 3\). Its exact Schatten threshold is \(p>1/(1-\delta)\), with failure at the strong endpoint.
