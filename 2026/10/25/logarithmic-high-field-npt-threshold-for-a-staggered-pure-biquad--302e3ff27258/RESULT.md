# Logarithmic high-field NPT threshold for a staggered pure-biquadratic spin-1 dimer

## Finding

Let \(J>0\) and \(h>0\), set \(k_B=1\), and consider two spin-1 qutrits with Hamiltonian \(H=-J(\mathbf S_1\cdot\mathbf S_2)^2+h(S_{1z}-S_{2z})\). Define \(T_{\mathrm{NPT}}(J,h)\) as the supremum of temperatures \(T>0\) for which the Gibbs state \(e^{-H/T}/\operatorname{Tr}(e^{-H/T})\) has a partial transpose that is not positive semidefinite. As \(h/J\to\infty\), \(T_{\mathrm{NPT}}(J,h)=\frac{2h}{\log(4h/J)+o(1)}\); equivalently, \(h/T_{\mathrm{NPT}}(J,h)=\frac12\log(4h/J)+o(1)\). The first loss of positivity is controlled by the partial-transpose block generated by the coherence between \(|1,-1\rangle\) and \(|-1,1\rangle\), rather than the two other off-diagonal blocks.

## Assumptions and scope

The spins are dimensionless spin-1 operators; \(J>0\) and \(h>0\) have energy units, and Boltzmann's constant is set to one. The observable is the NPT threshold, not a claim that every PPT state is separable. The limit is \(h/J\to\infty\). Adding the scalar \(J I\) to the Hamiltonian does not change its Gibbs state.

## Proof

Write \(P_0\) for the projector onto the two-spin singlet. For two spin-1 particles, \((\mathbf S_1\cdot\mathbf S_2)^2=I+3P_0\), since \(\mathbf S_1\cdot\mathbf S_2\) has eigenvalues \(-2,-1,1\) in total-spin sectors \(0,1,2\). Thus, after the irrelevant scalar shift \(+J I\), the Hamiltonian is \(H'=hD-3JP_0\), where \(D=S_{1z}-S_{2z}\).

In the total-magnetization-zero basis \(a=|1,-1\rangle\), \(b=|0,0\rangle\), \(c=|-1,1\rangle\), the dimensionless block \(H'/h\) is

\[
A_\epsilon=\begin{pmatrix}2-\epsilon&\epsilon&-\epsilon\\\epsilon&-\epsilon&\epsilon\\-\epsilon&\epsilon&-2-\epsilon\end{pmatrix},\qquad \epsilon=J/h.
\]

Put \(x=h/T\) and \(G=\exp(-xA_\epsilon)\), with entries \(g_{ij}\) in the order \((a,b,c)\). The other six product states are not mixed by \(P_0\): two have Gibbs weight \(1\), two have weight \(e^{-x}\), and two have weight \(e^x\). The partial transpose decomposes into positive one-dimensional blocks and three two-dimensional blocks with diagonal entries respectively \((1,1)\), \((e^{-x},e^{-x})\), and \((e^x,e^x)\), and off-diagonal entries respectively \(g_{ac},g_{ab},g_{bc}\). Therefore the Gibbs state is NPT exactly when at least one of

\[
|g_{ac}|>1,\qquad |g_{ab}|>e^{-x},\qquad |g_{bc}|>e^x
\]

holds.

For small \(\epsilon\), ordinary nondegenerate perturbation theory for \(A_\epsilon\) gives its lowest eigenvalue and a normalized eigenvector (with positive \(c\)-component)

\[
\lambda_-(\epsilon)=-2-\epsilon+O(\epsilon^2),\qquad
v_-(\epsilon)=\bigl(\epsilon/4+O(\epsilon^2),\,-\epsilon/2+O(\epsilon^2),\,1+O(\epsilon^2)\bigr).
\]

In the transition window \(x=\tfrac12\log(4/\epsilon)+O(1)\), one has \(\epsilon x\to0\), so the lowest-eigenvector contribution dominates \(g_{ac}\) and

\[
g_{ac}=\frac{\epsilon}{4}e^{2x}(1+o(1)).
\]

The other two entries satisfy \(g_{ab}=O(\epsilon)\) and \(g_{bc}=O(1)\) uniformly in that window. Since \(e^{-x}\asymp\sqrt{\epsilon}\) and \(e^x\asymp\epsilon^{-1/2}\), their NPT inequalities are false there for sufficiently small \(\epsilon\). For any fixed \(\delta>0\), the same estimates show that all three blocks are positive for \(x\le\tfrac12\log(4/\epsilon)-\delta\), whereas the first block is NPT for \(x\ge\tfrac12\log(4/\epsilon)+\delta\) near the crossing. Hence the first NPT boundary satisfies

\[
x_\mathrm{NPT}=\tfrac12\log(4/\epsilon)+o(1).
\]

Substituting \(x=h/T\) and \(\epsilon=J/h\) yields the stated temperature asymptotic.

## Relationship to prior work

The spin-1 biquadratic interaction is established in the spin-chain literature. Published spin-1 dimer studies also examine thermal negativity under inhomogeneous fields or under uniform fields with exchange and single-ion anisotropies. The closest inspected inhomogeneous-field model is bilinear XXZ exchange with a Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya term; the inspected uniform-field dimer uses bilinear XXZ exchange and single-ion anisotropy. Neither has the Hamiltonian \(-J(\mathbf S_1\cdot\mathbf S_2)^2+h(S_{1z}-S_{2z})\) or gives the coefficient \(2\) and logarithmic constant \(4\) in this NPT threshold.

## Limitations

The result is an asymptotic as \(h/J\to\infty\); it does not provide a closed form for the threshold at finite field. It concerns NPT, so it does not rule out PPT entanglement above the threshold. The full text of the 1989 chain paper and the 2005 nonlinear-coupling paper was not available in the bounded public search; a related but unindexed derivation for this exact staggered-field Hamiltonian remains a residual novelty risk.
