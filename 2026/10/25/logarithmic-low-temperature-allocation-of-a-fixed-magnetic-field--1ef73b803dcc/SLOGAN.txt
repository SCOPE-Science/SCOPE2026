For a two-spin XX Gibbs state with a fixed quadratic budget of longitudinal magnetic fields, the low-temperature concurrence-maximizing allocation changes sharply at budget \(R=J\). Above and at that threshold the optimal uniform component lies \(\log(2\beta s)/(2\beta)+o(\beta^{-1})\) below the product–entangled spectral crossing \(s=\sqrt{(J^2+R^2)/2}\), producing an exact leading logarithmic loss of the maximum concurrence; below threshold the optimal field is exactly uniform for all sufficiently low temperatures.
