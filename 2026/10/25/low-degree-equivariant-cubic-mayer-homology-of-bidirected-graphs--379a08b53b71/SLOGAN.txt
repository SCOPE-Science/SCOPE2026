For every connected graph of girth at least five, bidirecting its edges makes cubic Mayer path homology explicitly computable through degree three: its Betti numbers depend only on vertices, edges, and bipartiteness. The complete automorphism characters follow from natural vertex, arc, and even-walk permutation modules, and the \(q=2\) second homology has exact rank \(2|E|-2|V|+c\), where \(c\) counts even-walk classes.
