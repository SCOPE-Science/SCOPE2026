# Scientific review: low-energy Möbius-ladder spin-flip geometry

**Correctness: PASS.** The result is an infinite-family statement proved by the folded-chord decomposition, not by finite interpolation. For even width, counting the uniform chords and the two cycle interfaces classifies every configuration with at most three defects. Each three-defect state has precisely two ground neighbors; the ground-state pair-flip graph is a single \(2n\)-cycle, and each pair-flip has two distinct legal orders. For odd width, the bipartite reference spin configuration converts defects to the edge boundary of the flipped set. A complete classification of edge boundaries up to four yields the two rooted incidence components and the exact number of isolated blocks. An explicit interleaved spin-flip sequence attains defect threshold five; the low-energy disconnection proves the lower bound. Direct exhaustive checks of all states in eight consecutive graph sizes agree. Critical scope: binary unweighted antiferromagnetic edges, labeled spins, exactly one spin flipped per move.

**Originality: PASS, qualified.** Greeni and Rajasingh give a width-five linear arrangement of the same graph; this already supplies the five-defect upper-bound path and must not be claimed as new. Kalinin and Berloff give the even-width optimum defect count and the \(2n\) ground-state degeneracy; those numerical statements are likewise prior. Kaul and West analyze general improving local cut flips. The additional claim here is a **complete exact classification of the labeled low-energy one-flip transition graphs**, including \(n(n-3)\) isolated states at odd width and an even-width necklace with \(2n\) doubled two-step ground-state connections. The relevant primary full texts and accessible mathematical records did not state or imply these whole-graph classifications. The exact odd-width threshold by itself is elementary once cutwidth five is known; the novelty judgment does not rest on that threshold alone. An equivalent study under Ising metastability or cut-boundary reconfiguration language could still exist.

**Value: PASS.** This specifies which physically distinguishable low-energy spin states are reachable by single-site dynamics, not only their minimum energy. Odd width has two ground-state basins that remain disconnected even after every state through four defects is included, as well as a growing family of completely isolated excited states. Even width has a connected one-excitation sublevel space with a regular, explicit necklace of four-cycles. The sharp parity change and exact sublevel graph types are natural dynamical invariants for a canonical sparse cubic Ising benchmark. The statements are full infinite-family classifications, not unmotivated small-graph arithmetic.
