At odd width, the antiferromagnetic Möbius ladder has two ground states separated by a sharp five-defect single-spin barrier, with its entire four-defect sublevel graph decomposing into two rooted incidence components and explicitly counted isolated states. At even width, the three-defect sublevel graph is a necklace of \(2n\) four-cycles formed by doubling each subdivided edge of the \(2n\)-cycle of ground states.
