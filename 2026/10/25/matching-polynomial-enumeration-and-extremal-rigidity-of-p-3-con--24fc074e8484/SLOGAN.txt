Every \(P_3\)-convex vertex set in a once-subdivided graph is encoded by forced internal edges, a matching among unselected original vertices, and independently chosen cross-edges. For once-subdivided complete graphs this yields the exact counting series \(\exp(t+t^2/2+t e^t)\) and exactly \(n(n-1)\) largest proper convex sets when \(n\ge3\).
