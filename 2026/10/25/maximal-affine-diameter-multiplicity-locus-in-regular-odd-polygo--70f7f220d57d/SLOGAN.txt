For every regular odd \(n\)-gon with \(n\ge5\), almost every interior point lies on an odd number of affine diameters, and every odd multiplicity from \(1\) to \(n\) occurs on positive area. The maximal-multiplicity locus is a central regular \(n\)-gon occupying the exact area fraction \(\sin^2(\pi/(2n))/\cos^2(\pi/n)\sim\pi^2/(4n^2)\).
