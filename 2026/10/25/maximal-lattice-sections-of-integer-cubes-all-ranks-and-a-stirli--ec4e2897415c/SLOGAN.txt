Every affine \(\ell\)-flat meeting the integral cube \([0,k]^d\) in the maximum \((k+1)^\ell\) lattice points is a signed coordinate-diagonal flat, obtained by grouping varying coordinates into \(\ell\) blocks and fixing the rest. Their exact number is \(\frac{1}{2^\ell\ell!}\sum_{j=0}^{\ell}(-1)^{\ell-j}\binom\ell j(k+1+2j)^d\), a polynomial in \(k\) valid from the first positive dilation for every rank and dimension.
