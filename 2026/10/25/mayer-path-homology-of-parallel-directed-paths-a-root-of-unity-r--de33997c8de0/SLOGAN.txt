For any multiarm directed theta with at least three internally disjoint source-to-sink paths, all complex Mayer path-homology dimensions admit exact formulas for every nilpotency order \(N\ge3\) and every \(1\le q<N\). Two independent transitions control the answer: path lengths agreeing modulo \(\operatorname{ord}(-\xi)\) and the number of parallel two-edge arms; every homology degree above two vanishes.
