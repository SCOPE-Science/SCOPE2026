For every alternating binary word \((01)^m\), the number of minimal absent scattered subsequences of each possible length is encoded exactly by the bivariate rational series \(\frac{uz^2(3+(z^2-2z)u+2z^3u^2)}{(1-zu)^2-z^4u^3}\). A run-based characterization explains the formula and shows exponential growth of their total number with base approximately \(1.754877666\).
