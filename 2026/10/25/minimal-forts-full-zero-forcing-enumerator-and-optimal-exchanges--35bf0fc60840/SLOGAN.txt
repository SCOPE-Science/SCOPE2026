For every \(n\ge4\), the one-step Mycielskian of \(K_n\) has exactly five types of minimal forts and an explicit five-term zero-forcing polynomial, giving \(n(n-1)(n-2)(n+3)/4\) minimum sets. Their one-vertex-exchange graph is connected, has exactly three degree strata, and has \(n(n-1)(n-2)(2n^2+3n-19)/4\) edges.
