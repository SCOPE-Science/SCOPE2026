For finite Cartesian grid box closure, the Duquenne–Guigues pseudo-intents are exactly the nonclosed point pairs, giving a globally minimum basis of \(\binom{N}{2}-\sum_i(q_i-1)\prod_{j\ne i}q_j\) multi-conclusion Horn rules. This canonical minimum basis takes exactly \(\lceil\log_2 d\rceil\) simultaneous rounds in the worst case when \(d\ge2\), independent of factor lengths.
