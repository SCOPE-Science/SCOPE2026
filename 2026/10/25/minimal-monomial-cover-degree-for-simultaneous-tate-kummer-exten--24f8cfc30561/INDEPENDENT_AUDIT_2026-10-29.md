# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Verified the exact Kummer pullback exponent matrix A M over F_p; independence of coordinate classes gives vanishing iff Abar Mbar=0. Its kernel has dimension r-rho, so modulo p the integer lattice image of M has rank <=r-rho, and Smith normal form forces at least rho p-divisible invariant factors, hence degree divisible by p^rho. A unimodular lift of an adapted F_p basis (using SL_r(Z)->SL_r(F_p) for r>=2) followed by diag(p I_rho,I) realizes determinant exactly p^rho; the r=1, rho=0 and rho=r cases are straightforward. The statement is restricted to finite etale monomial covers on a coordinate torus where p is invertible and the coordinate Kummer classes remain independent; arbitrary covers not addressed. Independent bounded determinant searches for p=3,5 and four 2x2 matrices reproduce p^rho including dependent and independent rows. Finite search does not prove the lattice theorem.

Despite being an elementary Smith normal form application, the invariant is a precise natural optimization problem arising when several Tate-parameter Kummer classes are killed simultaneously by monomial finite etale covers. The formula distinguishes duplicate/dependent degenerations from genuinely independent directions and specifies minimal covering cost p^rho, which a researcher choosing simultaneous covers can use directly.
