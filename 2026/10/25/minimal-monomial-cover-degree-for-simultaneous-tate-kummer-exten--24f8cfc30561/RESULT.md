# Minimal monomial-cover degree for simultaneous Tate Kummer extensions

## Finding

Let \(p\) be an odd prime, let \(r,s\geq 1\), and let \(U\) be a base with independent coordinate Kummer classes \(\kappa_j=[T_j]\in H^1_{\mathrm{\acute et}}(U,\mu_p)\) for \(1\leq j\leq r\). Let \(A=(a_{ij})\in\mathbb Z^{s\times r}\), and define \(\alpha_i=\sum_{j=1}^r a_{ij}\kappa_j\). In the Tate-curve setting, these are the Kummer classes of parameters \(q_i=p^p\prod_{j=1}^r T_j^{a_{ij}}\) on a domain where all parameters are topologically nilpotent.

Consider finite étale monomial covers of the coordinate torus given by an integer matrix \(M\in\mathbb Z^{r\times r}\) with \(\det M\ne0\), via \(T_i=\prod_{j=1}^r S_j^{M_{ij}}\). If \(\bar A\) denotes reduction modulo \(p\), then all pulled-back classes vanish exactly when

\[
\bar A\bar M=0.
\]

Writing \(\rho=\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_p}(\bar A)\), the least possible degree \(|\det M|\) of such a cover is exactly

\[
\boxed{p^{\rho}}.
\]

Thus the minimum depends on the number of independent Kummer directions, not on the number of Tate degenerations.

## Assumptions and scope

The coordinate classes \(\kappa_1,\ldots,\kappa_r\) are assumed linearly independent over \(\mathbb F_p\). This holds for the standard algebraic torus: a monomial in the coordinate units can be a \(p\)-th power only when every coordinate exponent is divisible by \(p\), as seen from its orders along the coordinate boundary divisors. The same classes describe the Kummer covers on the standard unit-torus domain used for Tate parameters.

The constant \(p^p\) is a \(p\)-th power, so it contributes zero to the Kummer class. Only finite étale monomial covers are optimized over; no minimality statement is made for arbitrary finite étale covers. For the Tate-curve interpretation, the parameters must be topologically nilpotent on the chosen domain.

## Proof

Let \(\bar A\) and \(\bar M\) be the reductions modulo \(p\), and set \(\rho=\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_p}(\bar A)\).

First, Kummer functoriality gives

\[
g_M^*\kappa_i=\sum_{j=1}^r M_{ij}\kappa'_j,
\]

where \(\kappa'_j=[S_j]\). Hence the pulled-back class associated to row \(i\) is \(\sum_j(AM)_{ij}\kappa'_j\). Independence of the \(\kappa'_j\) implies that all these classes vanish if and only if \(\bar A\bar M=0\).

For the lower bound, this equation implies \(\operatorname{im}(\bar M)\subseteq\ker(\bar A)\), so \(\operatorname{rank}(\bar M)\leq r-\rho\). By Smith normal form, the rank of an integer matrix modulo \(p\) equals the number of its invariant factors not divisible by \(p\). Therefore at least \(\rho\) invariant factors of \(M\) are divisible by \(p\), and \(p^\rho\mid\det M\). Every admissible cover consequently has degree at least \(p^\rho\).

For attainment, choose a unimodular basis change \(V\in\operatorname{GL}_r(\mathbb Z)\) whose reduction has its last \(r-\rho\) columns spanning \(\ker(\bar A)\). Such a basis change exists by lifting a determinant-one basis change over \(\mathbb F_p\); the cases \(r=1\), \(\rho=0\), and \(\rho=r\) are immediate. Then \(\bar A\bar V=[B\;0]\) for some matrix \(B\), and the integer matrix

\[
M=V\,\operatorname{diag}(pI_\rho,I_{r-\rho})
\]

satisfies \(\bar A\bar M=0\) and \(|\det M|=p^\rho\). This attains the lower bound and proves the formula.

For example, \(A=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&0\end{pmatrix}\) has rank one modulo \(p\), so a degree-\(p\) cover kills both classes. In contrast, \(A=I_2\) has rank two and requires degree \(p^2\) among monomial covers.

## Verification

The proof uses only the Kummer-class pullback formula, linear independence of the coordinate classes, and Smith normal form. The lower bound applies to every nonsingular integer exponent matrix, while the displayed construction attains it. The edge cases \(\rho=0\) and \(\rho=r\) give respectively the identity cover and a degree-\(p^r\) cover. No numerical search or finite experiment is used as a substitute for the proof.

## Relationship to prior work

Pentland's *A prismatic Riemann-Hilbert correspondence* gives a one-coordinate Tate-curve example with parameter \(q=p^pT\), identifies its Kummer class with \([T]\), and exhibits the corresponding nonsplit constructible-sheaf extension (Example 5.35). The result here isolates the simultaneous multi-coordinate Kummer-class problem and determines the exact minimum degree of a monomial cover that kills all classes at once. The Kummer sequence and Smith normal form are standard ingredients; the claim is the resulting sharp optimization formula, not a new Kummer theory or a general statement about all covers.

## Limitations

The conclusion is restricted to classes generated by independent coordinate Kummer classes and to finite étale monomial covers. It does not classify arbitrary finite étale covers, non-monomial Kummer classes, or splitting of unrelated constructible sheaves. The formula is an elementary exact invariant; broader treatments of the same optimization outside the sources searched remain a residual possibility.
