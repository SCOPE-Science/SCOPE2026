For every odd regular bipyramid, the probability of a four-vertex planar shadow has a closed inverse-sine formula and decays as \(\pi h/(4n)\) for fixed height. All counts from four through \(n+2\) occur, and for the equilateral pentagonal bipyramid the four-, five-, six-, and seven-vertex probabilities are approximately 11.74%, 60.69%, 18.77%, and 8.80%, with the seven-vertex atom known previously.
