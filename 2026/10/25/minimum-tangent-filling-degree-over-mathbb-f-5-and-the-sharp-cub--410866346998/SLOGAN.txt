The smallest degree of an irreducible tangent-filling plane curve over \(\mathbb F_5\) is exactly four: the smooth quartic \(X^4+X^3Y+Y^3Z-Z^4=0\) has six rational tangents covering the plane. An exhaustive census of all \(2{,}441{,}406\) projective cubic equations proves that even their smooth rational tangents cover at most \(29\) of the \(31\) projective points, and a smooth cubic attains this bound.
