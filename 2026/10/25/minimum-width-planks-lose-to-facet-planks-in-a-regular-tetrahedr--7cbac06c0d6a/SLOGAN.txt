For a regular tetrahedron of minimum width \(2\), every minimum-width plank loses in captured volume to a facet-parallel plank throughout the exact interval \(0<W<8\sqrt3-12\). The respective optimized volume fractions are \(3W/4-W^3/16\) and \(1-(1-\sqrt3W/4)^3\); their reversal beyond the threshold concerns these two orientation classes, not all possible plank normals.
