# Mathematical review

## Correctness

PASS. A direct forbidden-triple argument classifies the Alexander dual as coordinate-line simplices, rectangle tetrahedra, and additional long-distance edges. Rectangles attach to the line subcomplex along four-edge cycles. Its two-cell skeleton is the two-skeleton of a product of simplices and is simply connected. The relative integral homology sequence has an integer-free kernel, establishing the two-sphere multiplicity; adding isolated higher-distance edges supplies the circle multiplicity. Integral combinatorial Alexander duality gives both reduced homology degrees of the original cut complex. The packaged checker independently constructs the finite models and verifies their full mod-2, mod-3 and mod-5 Betti vectors in seven examples. The infinite and torsion-free statements are supported by the proof, not by finite sampling.

## Originality

PASS against examined evidence. The 2023 foundational paper treats complete multipartite graphs and clique prisms; its 2024 sequel treats path grids. A published rook-graph result covers exactly two complete-graph factors. Other previously recorded outcomes concern binary hypercubes or one ternary three-cube. These are subsumed as special boundary cases but do not entail the mixed-alphabet all-factor formula or its dual homotopy classification. A paper on total two-cut complexes uses a different defining condition. No equivalent or stronger same-object statement was found in the targeted literature and indexed-record searches. An unindexed Cartesian-product or 2-connected-triple theorem remains a scientific priority risk.

## Value

PASS. This is an exact, dimension-uniform classification for a canonical multi-parameter family of graph products. It determines integral groups without torsion ambiguity, identifies all lower-dimensional obstructions to shellability, and exposes a geometric source for both ranks: surviving rectangle two-cycles and long-distance edges. The result genuinely covers nonbinary three-coordinate and higher mixed-alphabet graph products not determined by the earlier two-factor, binary-only, or isolated small examples.

## Scope and limitations

No equivariant integral module structure or homotopy type of the original three-cut complex is established. Independently unindexed work might imply some or all of the theorem. The finite checker is supplementary to, not a replacement for, the general proof. No external independent audit has occurred.
