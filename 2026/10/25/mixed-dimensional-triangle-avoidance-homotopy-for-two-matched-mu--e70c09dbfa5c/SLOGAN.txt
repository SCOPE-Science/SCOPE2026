The triangle-free vertex-subset complex of a complete multipartite graph with two independently perfect-matched parts has integral homotopy type \(S^{k+l-1}\vee\bigvee^{s+1}S^k\vee\bigvee^{s+1}S^l\vee\bigvee^{\binom{s+1}{2}}S^1\), for every \(k,l\ge2\) and \(s\ge0\) singleton parts. The top join sphere records the interaction between two triangle-generating matched parts, beyond classifications that require a triangle-free residual graph.
