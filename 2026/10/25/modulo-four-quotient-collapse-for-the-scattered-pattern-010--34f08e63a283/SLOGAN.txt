For binary words counted by occurrences of the scattered pattern \(010\) modulo \(m\), the minimal DFA has \(m^3\) states for odd \(m\), \(m^3/2\) for even \(m\) with even target or \(m\equiv2\pmod4\), and only \(m^3/8\) when \(4\mid m\) and the target residue is odd. The fourfold collapse comes from an exact classification of indistinguishable modular subsequence-count triples.
