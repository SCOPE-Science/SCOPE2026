In a cycle \(C_n\), a probe set detects every edge failure exactly when no successive cyclic gap reaches half the circumference; this gives an exact generating function for every monitoring edge-geodetic set. Inclusion-minimal configurations are all triples for odd cycles, but every even cycle of length at least six additionally has exactly \(\binom{n/2}{2}\) irredundant four-probe configurations made from antipodal pairs.
