The categorical products \(K_p\times K_q\), for all \(p,q\ge3\), are strongly 2-monophonic, and their monophonic-position sets of size at least three are exactly cliques, yielding the full enumerator \(1+pqz+\binom{pq}{2}z^2+\sum_{k=3}^{\min(p,q)}\binom pk\binom qk k!z^k\). The inclusion-maximal sets are precisely nonadjacent pairs and largest cliques, so the lower and upper maximal sizes are \(2\) and \(\min(p,q)\), with an unbounded gap.
