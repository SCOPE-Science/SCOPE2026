On an \(n\)-vertex path with homogeneous independent transmission probability \(p\), the expected-reach-maximizing **two-seed distance** increases monotonically through every integer from \(\lceil(n+1)/3\rceil\) to \(n-1\), with exact strictly ordered phase transitions. Four vertices are the sharp minimum for a change of optimal distance as \(p\) varies, with threshold \(p=1/2\).
