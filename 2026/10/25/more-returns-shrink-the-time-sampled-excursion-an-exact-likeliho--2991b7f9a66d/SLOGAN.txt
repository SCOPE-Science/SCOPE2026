In a fair random-walk bridge, conditioning on more zero-return excursions makes the excursion seen from a uniformly sampled time cell strictly smaller in likelihood-ratio order. Consequently every nontrivial excursion-length power sum and the variance of the positive-excursion semiduration decrease strictly with the return count; when the return count is \(\rho n\), the variance is asymptotic to \(n(\rho^2-2\rho+2)/(4\rho^2)\).
