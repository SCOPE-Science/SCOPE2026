Five is the first number of binary constant runs for which the number of minimum palindromic factorizations is unbounded: for \(0^a1^b0^c1^d0^e\), the multiplicity is \(c+2\) exactly when \(b\ne d\) and \(c<\min(a,e)\), and never exceeds two otherwise. Uniform five-run compositions have a sharp two-component scaling limit: an atom of weight \(2/3\) at zero and density \(4(1-3t)^3\) on \((0,1/3)\), with expected multiplicity \(n/45+O(1)\).
