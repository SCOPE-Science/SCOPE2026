For every \(m\ge4\) and \(n\ge2\), the rational reduced homology of \(\operatorname{Ind}(P_m[K_n])\) is a **multiplicity-free** representation of the full independent color-permutation group \((S_n)^m\), with one explicit outer tensor product for each admissible independent set of path positions. Its restriction to diagonal \(S_n\) has exact binomial multiplicities \(\binom{p+1}{3(d+1)-m}\binom{d+1}{p}\) on tensor powers of the standard color-augmentation module.
