The natural 2304-element coordinate symmetry acts multiplicity-freely on the 63-dimensional third rational homology of \(\operatorname{Ind}(K_2\times K_4\times K_4)\), with exactly eight irreducible constituents. Its coordinate-permutation invariant line is negated by exchanging the two four-point factors, and the independent layer flip has 36 positive and 27 negative eigenvalues.
