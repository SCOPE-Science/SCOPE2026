# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The statement is for simple loopless degree-at-most-two graph edge sets on exactly six labeled vertices. Complete enumeration finds 1,858 faces including the empty face. An explicit fully checked acyclic discrete-Morse matching leaves a vertex, fourteen three-cells and fifty four-cells; exact integral cancellation and a unimodular maximal minor establish the integral sphere-wedge decomposition. Two independently programmed fixed-simplex trace methods agree for all eleven conjugacy types of \(\mathfrak S_6\); Jacobi–Trudi character arithmetic yields exactly five multiplicity-one constituents. Direct mod-two and mod-three simplicial ranks independently match the predicted homology. Reproducible certificate and executable checks are embedded.

## Originality — PASS, qualified by source access
The central 36-dimensional integral Betti number is **not original**: Jonsson's *Simplicial Complexes of Graphs*, Table 12.2, tabulates this exact rank. The new claim is the complete rational \(\mathfrak S_6\)-module with its five irreducible constituents and full trace vector. Wachs's section 7 theorem gives representations for a loop-*admitting* family; the six-label loopless chain complex differs and is not immediately a specialization. Focused mathematical literature and indexed result comparisons yielded no statement that implies this representation. The decisive residual risk is the uninspected full text of Jonsson's directly relevant chapter; only its indexed table and scope were verified, not a full-document negative. Existing general formulas may indirectly imply the character with additional work. This is self-review, not an exhaustive prior-art guarantee.

The value claim is limited to this natural canonical object, not a downstream theorem or a conjectured infinite sequence.
