The scale-one Vietoris–Rips complex of every Johnson graph \(J(n,k)\) with \(n\ge6\) and \(3\le k\le n-3\) has a multiplicity-free rational \(S_n\)-action on second homology: \(\widetilde H_2\cong\bigoplus_{j=1}^{\min(k,n-k)-1}S^{(n-j-2,j,1,1)}\). This determines the complete symmetry representation beyond the previously known sphere count, and yields no full-symmetry-invariant second-homology class.
