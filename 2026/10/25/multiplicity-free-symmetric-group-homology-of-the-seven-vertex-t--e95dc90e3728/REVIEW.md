**Correctness — PASS.** Every one of the 133,501 faces, 917,280 cover incidences, 66,695 matching pairs, and 111 critical cells is handled by exact algorithms. Full directed-Hasse topological sorting proves matching acyclicity. Integral Smith computations from two independent Morse matchings reproduce the previously known nonequivariant homology. Exact chain lifts and projections compute rational traces for all fifteen conjugacy classes, cross-checked against direct signed Lefschetz sums over the original complex. Independent Murnaghan–Nakayama character inner products yield the stated complete Specht decomposition. The scientific assertion is only for the seven-vertex complex over the rationals.

The statement newly evaluated here is their complete equivariant degree-by-degree representation. No accessible primary statement or indexed equivalent covering result was located; separately, the closely related published graph-complex comparisons concern different parameters or face conditions. The full relevant chapter of Jonsson's book could not be inspected, so an unindexed representation computation there is an explicit material residual risk, not a declaration of noncoverage.

**Value — PASS.** The canonical first small case with a nontrivial bounded-covering parameter exhibits two distinct nontrivial homology degrees and integral 2-torsion. The symmetric-group action refines its known Betti table to a sign-twisted seven-point module, a multiplicity-free 55-dimensional Specht sum, and one invariant top class; none follows merely from the homology ranks. This is a meaningful exact equivariant classification, even without a general-size theorem.
