For the seven-vertex triangle-free graph complex, rational fifth, seventh, and eighth homology are multiplicity-free \(\mathfrak S_7\)-modules with respective Specht constituents \(S^{(2,1,1,1,1,1)}\oplus S^{(1^7)}\), \(S^{(4,2,1)}\oplus S^{(4,1,1,1)}\), and \(S^{(7)}\). This is an equivariant refinement of previously tabulated nonequivariant integral homology, not a new calculation of its Betti ranks.
