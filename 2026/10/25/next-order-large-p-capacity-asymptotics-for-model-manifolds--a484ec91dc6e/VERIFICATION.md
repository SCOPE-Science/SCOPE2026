The exact radial capacity identity was checked against the full text of arXiv:2604.14600v1, including the radial-ball capacity formula used in the rank asymptotic, and against arXiv:2502.13651v1 for the hyperbolic annulus special case. The asymptotic derivation is analytic: the model integral is evaluated by the upper incomplete gamma function; the relative \(O(r^{-1})\) sphere-area error contributes \(O(\varepsilon^2\log(1/\varepsilon))\) after multiplication by \(h\varepsilon\); the gamma expansion is then carried through the reciprocal and dimension normalization. No numerical experiment is used to establish the theorem.

The claim is limited to complete rotationally symmetric manifolds with the stated precise sphere-area asymptotic, fixed \(R>0\), and \(p\to\infty\). It does not assert existence of the asymptotic expansion for arbitrary manifolds. Independent audit and independent validation have not been performed.
