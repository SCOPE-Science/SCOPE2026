The nilpotency index of an order-preserving partial isometry is the longest consecutive run of occupied translation steps plus one, giving an exact generating polynomial jointly refined by rank and least zero power. For a uniformly chosen nilpotent, the index fluctuates on a bounded lattice scale around \(\log_2 n\), with \(\Pr(\nu\le k_n)\to\exp(-\theta/2)\) whenever \(n2^{-k_n}\to\theta\in(0,\infty)\).
