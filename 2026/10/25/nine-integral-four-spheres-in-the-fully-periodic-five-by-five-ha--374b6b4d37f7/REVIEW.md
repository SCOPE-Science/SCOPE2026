## originality

PASS

Jonsson 2006 analyzes signed Euler characteristics of the exact doubly periodic grid and rhombus tilings, which do not determine integral homotopy. Bousquet-Mélou–Linusson–Nevo give open/cylindrical grid homotopy types, geometrically distinct from full toroidal periodicity. A 2010 Jonsson specialist article treats periodic cross-polytope cycles; only abstract was accessible after lawful OA search and institutional Oxford reported VPN disconnected, so it remains a material historical risk. A related published 4x4 torus is not this 5x5 calculation. Best-of-knowledge O PASS acknowledges no whole-document noncoverage of Jonsson 2010.

The 25-vertex 5x5 toroidal hard-square complex is a canonical small non-bipartite fully periodic grid where signed partition functions are known but integral homotopy can carry attaching/torsion information that Euler counts miss. The complete explicit discrete-Morse certificate gives its full homotopy type (nine 4-spheres), not a mere recomputation of a known table. This exact natural finite benchmark is useful in periodic lattice topology despite no general m,n theorem.
