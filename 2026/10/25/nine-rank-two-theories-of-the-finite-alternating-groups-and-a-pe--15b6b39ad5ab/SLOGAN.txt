In the ternary-relational multiplication language, finite alternating groups have exactly nine quantifier-rank-two theories, with a sharp least-representative bound of 239500800 elements within the family. Unlike symmetric groups, their rank-two theories oscillate forever: for all sufficiently large degrees, the unique distinction is whether the degree is congruent to \(4\) or \(5\) modulo \(8\), detected by an involution without an alternating square root.
