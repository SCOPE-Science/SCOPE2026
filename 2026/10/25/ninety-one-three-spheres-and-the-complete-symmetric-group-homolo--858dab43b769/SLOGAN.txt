The Lovász open-neighborhood complex of the six-label triangular graph is integrally homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{91}S^3\), and its full rational \(S_6\)-homology action splits explicitly into eight Specht-module types. Although its 91-dimensional mod-two homology was known, the homotopy classification, complete symmetry character, and rational acyclicity of the full permutation quotient give stronger information.
