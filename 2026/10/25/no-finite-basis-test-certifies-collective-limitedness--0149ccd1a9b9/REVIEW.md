# Review: No finite basis test certifies collective limitedness

## Correctness
The proof constructs, for each finite prescribed family of bases, unit vectors with uniformly vanishing coordinates on those bases. The resulting rank-one operators have norm one. The tested image unions are relatively compact, while the full unit-ball union contains the nonlimited sequence of coordinate vectors in \(c_0\). The weak-star-null coordinate functionals give an explicit witness.

## Originality
The closest inspected paper gives the universal orthonormal-sequence criterion for collective limitedness and notes that a single basis does not suffice for a single operator. The new statement strengthens this to every finite list for a family of rank-one operators. Targeted literature and accessible own-ledger searches found no exact match. This is a best-knowledge novelty assessment, not a global priority guarantee.

## Value
The result gives a sharp obstruction to finite predetermined basis tests for collective limitedness, even when each tested union is relatively compact. It clarifies why the universal quantifier in the known criterion cannot be replaced by a finite list.

## Closest literature and limitations
S. Gorokhova, *On compact (limited) operators between Hilbert and Banach spaces*, arXiv:2403.06262, Theorem 1 and Theorems 2–3. The claim concerns finite lists of bases and does not classify infinite test families. A broader literature-priority risk remains.
