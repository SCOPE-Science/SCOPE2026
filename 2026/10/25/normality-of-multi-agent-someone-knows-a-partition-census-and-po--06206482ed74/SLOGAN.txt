For three or more S5 agents, someone-knows is a normal modality exactly when each distributed-knowledge cell is already an information cell of some agent; a Stirling–Bell inclusion-exclusion formula counts all such labelled frames. Among independent uniform agent partitions, the transition to normality occurs at \(r\asymp n\log n/W(n)\), with limiting probability \(\exp(-e^{-c})\) in the critical window \(rB_{n-1}/B_n-\log n\to c\).
