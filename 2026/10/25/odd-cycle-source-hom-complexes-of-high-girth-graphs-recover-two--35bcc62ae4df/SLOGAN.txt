For odd \(g\ge5\), the complex \(\operatorname{Hom}(C_{g+2},H)\) of every finite simple graph \(H\) of girth at least \(g\) is a disjoint union of exactly \(2c_g(H)\) circles and \(2(g+2)c_{g+2}(H)\) points. Each circle is a canonical orientation of a shortest \(g\)-cycle, and each point is a frozen injective traversal of a \((g+2)\)-cycle.\n
