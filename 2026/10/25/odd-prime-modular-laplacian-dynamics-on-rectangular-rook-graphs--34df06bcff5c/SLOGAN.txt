For synchronous graph-Laplacian iteration over every odd prime field on rectangular rook graphs \(K_m\square K_n\), explicit residue-dependent formulas give every recurrent-state count, primitive-period census and exact transient-depth distribution. In particular, when the prime divides both dimensions, all configurations become zero within three steps, and the bound is sharp, and the sharp fraction requiring three steps is \(1-1/p\), independent of graph size.
