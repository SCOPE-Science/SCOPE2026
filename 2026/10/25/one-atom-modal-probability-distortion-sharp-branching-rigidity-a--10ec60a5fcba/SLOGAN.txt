For a finite uniform-probability Kripke frame, allowing possibility to change every proposition's probability by at most one atom doubles the maximum number of accessibility pairs from \(n\) to \(2n\). From five worlds onward every maximally branching frame has one universal predecessor and an almost-surjective residual map, with exactly \(n!(\binom n2+\binom n3+3\binom n4)\) labelled realizations; four worlds admit precisely 144 additional triangle configurations.
