# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Every open neighborhood generator centered at (v,i) is (R_v\{(v,i)}) union {(w,i):w~v}. If >=2 off-fiber vertices occur in one face, the C4-free condition makes its center unique. Descending-dimensional matching with a fixed in-fiber pivot removes all such mixed faces by genuine elementary collapses. The residual consists of edge blocks indexed by E(H), with row-boundary spheres S^{m-2}; for an edge the two half-blocks meet along the connected crown graph K_{m,m} minus matching, β1=m²-3m+1. Each half-block retracts relative to its row sphere, and attaching maps of the crown graph to simply connected S^{m-2} are null for m>=4; homotopy pushout splits off β1 two-spheres per base edge. Gluing over H leaves first Betti rank e-n+1 and one high-dimensional sphere per base vertex. This is integral homotopy, not just F2 Betti counts. Independent face/chain enumeration over an edge, triangle, four-vertex path and C5 for m=4,5 agrees in every case.

## originality

PASS

Published open-neighborhood formula for single base edge (clique prism) and full text for base C_n,n>=5 are exact special cases, not the all-H result. A similar published theorem over arbitrary high-girth H concerns a different closed-neighborhood metric Čech nerve and cannot imply the open-neighborhood wedge via matching parameters. Shukla bounded-degree and circulant classifications do not subsume arbitrary branching H and unbounded m. Best-of-knowledge PASS, allowing possible unpublished graph-of-spaces generalization.
