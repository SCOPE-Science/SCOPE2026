For every connected four-cycle-free graph \(H\) with \(n\) vertices and \(e\) edges and each \(m\ge4\), the open-neighborhood complex of \(H\square K_m\) is the wedge of \(e-n+1\) circles, \(n\) spheres of dimension \(m-2\), and \(e(m^2-3m+1)\) two-spheres. The formula applies uniformly to branching trees, triangles, and bases with several independent cycles.
