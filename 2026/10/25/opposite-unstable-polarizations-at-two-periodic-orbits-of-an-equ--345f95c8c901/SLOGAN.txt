For three equal oblate ellipsoids centered at an equilateral triangle, the planar and vertical unstable Lyapunov exponents reverse their ordering along a triangular three-bounce orbit at the universal flattening \(\beta=2/\sqrt3\). A simultaneous two-bounce orbit has the opposite ordering for every \(1<\beta<2/\sqrt3\), ruling out uniform dominance of either canonical unstable direction on an open family of open billiards.
