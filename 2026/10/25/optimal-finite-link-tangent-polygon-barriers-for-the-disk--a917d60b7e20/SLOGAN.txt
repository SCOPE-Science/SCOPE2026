For every finite number \(k\ge2\) of links, the shortest chain obtained by deleting one side of a polygon circumscribed about a disk has a unique, generally nonregular arrangement of tangent lines with two endpoint angles and one common interior angle. Its exact excess above the optimal connected-disk-barrier length \(\pi+2\) is asymptotic to \(\pi^3/[12(k-1)^2]\); no optimality outside this tangential-polygon class is claimed.
