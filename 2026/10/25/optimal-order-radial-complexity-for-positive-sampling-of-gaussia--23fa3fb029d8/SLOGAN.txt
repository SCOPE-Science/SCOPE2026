Uniformly conditioned positive point sampling of degree-\(N\) Gaussian Fock polynomials requires at least a constant multiple of \(\sqrt{N}\) distinct radii, regardless of how many angular samples are used. An explicit set of \(\lceil\sqrt N\rceil+1\) radial levels with root-of-unity angular samples attains this optimal order and has degree-independent frame bounds \(1/5\) and \(8\).
