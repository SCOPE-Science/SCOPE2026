# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** Multiaffine vertex reduction, the strict dominance of the crossed four-point chord determinant, and strict concavity of the symmetric three-factor sine function reduce the continuous three-dimensional problem to balanced positive integer gap words. The counting factor \(2n\binom4s/4\) correctly distinguishes lower/upper choices. The extremal determinant's nonzero linear gradient forces points to polygon edges or vertices; multi-affinity gives an exact necessary-and-sufficient all-corner equality test. Independent upper and lower dimension arguments prove the \(0/1/2\) residue pattern. A reproducible standalone finite enumeration corroborates these nonstandard steps, but does not replace the proof. Check the fixed-height, nondegenerate, \(n\ge4\) hypotheses.

## Originality
**PASS for the equality classification, not the numerical maximum.** Rote (arXiv:1905.11203v2, Lemmas 1–3) combined with the published exact minimum enclosing parallelogram area of a regular polygon implies the volume *value*. That coverage was explicitly identified and excluded from the originality claim. Closest other prior records classify even regular-polygon rectangles and general inner quadrilaterals. The precise new contribution is the equality locus for three-dimensional regular prisms, all vertex-orbit counts and the mod-four optimizer-family dimension transition. Results may still occur in specialized older prism literature; searches are evidence, not a guarantee of priority.

## Value
**PASS.** Extremizer nonuniqueness is a natural quantitative invariant in polytope volume optimization: an identical maximal volume hides a topological/combinatorial transition from isolated maxima to one- or two-parameter families. The exact counts, gap orbits and constructive finite criterion classify all optimizers rather than merely the scalar optimum. This is more than a routine numerical corollary of the planar formula, but does not claim an arbitrary-body theorem or algorithmic breakthrough.

## Sources and limitations
Rote's full mathematical proof and its equality lemmas were inspected, as were the two closest regular-polygon published reports. Hoehner–Ledford (arXiv:2608.04392v1) supplies a recent neighboring extremal-volume motivation, not the numerical result. The main residual originality uncertainty is an unindexed historical special case. The checker is finite; independent review has not been performed.
