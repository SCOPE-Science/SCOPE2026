The exact maximal-volume tetrahedra in every regular polygonal prism have balanced cyclic gap patterns and a complete finite face classification. Their vertex-supported counts are \(n/2\), \(3n\), or \(2n\) according to \(n\bmod4\), while the largest dimensions of continuous optimizer families are \(0\), \(2\), or \(1\), respectively; the numerical volume formula itself is already implied by earlier planar theory.
