For every product subgroup of row and column permutations on the near-full matching complex of \(K_{n,n}\), the geometric fixed-point space is empty if either part acts transitively and otherwise has the homotopy type \(S^{(s-1)(t-1)-1}\), where \(s,t\) count the orbits. The result strengthens a square-only nonequivariant sphere classification to a uniform classification of subgroup fixed spaces.
