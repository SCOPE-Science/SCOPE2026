The complex of all nonattacking-queen placements on a toroidal \(7\times7\) board has \(\widetilde H_2\cong\mathbb Z/9\) and \(\widetilde H_3\cong\mathbb Z^{120}\), with every other reduced integral homology group zero. An explicit coordinate change identifies this complex with the clique complex of the \(49\)-vertex Paley graph.
