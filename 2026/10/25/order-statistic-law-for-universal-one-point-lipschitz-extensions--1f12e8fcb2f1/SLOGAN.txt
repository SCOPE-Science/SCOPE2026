For an arbitrary unit-equilateral finite metric space and one added point at compatible distances \(r_i\), the sharp universal real-Banach Lipschitz extension factor is \(\max\{1,\max_{2\le k\le n}(k-1)/\sum_{j=1}^{k}r_{(j)}\}\), with distances ordered increasingly. An optimal linear extension has nonnegative weights, and for factors greater than one its unique positive weight vector is \(\alpha_i=(1-\Lambda r_i)_+\), producing exact support transitions as the point moves.
