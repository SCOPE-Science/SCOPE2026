# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The exact graph GP(10,2), 36 perfect matchings and every extendible subset give 30,503 nonempty faces. An independently implemented toggle matching yields 15,231 pairs, one 0-cell, ten 2-cells and thirty 3-cells; topological sorting all 163,650 directed Hasse incidences proves acyclicity. An independent signed Morse boundary calculation gives a 10x30 integral matrix of mod-2 rank 9 and exact Smith factors (1 nine times,2 once), computed using Sympy Smith form independently of the package determinant checks. With no 1-cells, the 2-skeleton is a wedge of ten S^2 and pi_2 equals H_2; integral row/column moves of the three-cell degree matrix leave one degree-two attachment and twenty trivially attached 3-cells. Thus the complete homotopy type is M(Z/2,2) wedge 20 S^3. The finite certificate enumerates the entire explicitly fixed graph, not other dodecahedral complexes.
