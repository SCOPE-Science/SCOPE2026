The proof exhaustively classifies the combinatorial Alexander dual's faces and its sixty free-face tetrahedral collapses; a subcomplex triangulates a torus and surjects on the dual's fundamental group, whose two generators die on tetrahedral squares. Euler characteristic, integral Hurewicz and Whitehead then establish a wedge of eleven two-spheres, and integral Alexander duality places eleven free generators in original cut-homology degree eleven.
