For the radical of a branching diamond incidence algebra, square-preserving automorphisms form a hyperbolic orthogonal-similitude group with free central shears, while genuine algebra automorphisms preserve its two distinguished isotropic summands. Over every finite field the exact index is \(2q^{r(r-1)/2}\prod_{i=1}^{r-1}(q^i+1)\), and the Jordan-derivation space exceeds the ordinary derivation space by precisely \(r(r-1)\) dimensions.
