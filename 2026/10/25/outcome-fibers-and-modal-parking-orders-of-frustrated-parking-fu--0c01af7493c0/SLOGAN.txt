For frustrated parking with \(n\) cars, the number of preference words producing a prescribed arrival-order parking permutation \(\pi\) is exactly \(\prod_{i\in R(\pi)}(\pi_i-n+i)\), where \(R(\pi)\) is the set of right-to-left maxima. The largest fiber has size \(\max_{0\le k<n}(k+1)^{n-k}\), and its outcomes are precisely the permutations of the first \(k\) small spots followed by the remaining spots in decreasing order, for maximizing \(k\).
