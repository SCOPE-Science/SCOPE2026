At the tight coverage orders \(n=3k+1\), every minimum paired-dominating set of the triangular graph \(L(K_n)\) is a packing of \(k\) two-edge stars and one unused label. Its fixed-size token-slide graph is a connected, \(4k\)-regular quotient of token swapping on a bouquet of squares, with \((3k+1)!/(2^k k!)\) vertices; its first three diameters are exactly \(3,6,10\).
