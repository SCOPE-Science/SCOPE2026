# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** The probability measure is explicitly specified. The transition matrix of the uniformly proper ternary word has spectral decomposition \(P^t=J/3+(-1/2)^t(I-J/3)\). Every legal interior factor becomes abundant with exponentially high probability; effective Hanf locality therefore reduces first-order satisfaction to finitely many independent-limit boundary pairs. The palette action on pairs of length at least two is free, so every anonymous boundary orbit has exactly six members. Named and anonymous spectrum inclusions and attainability both follow. Proper cycles have no boundary contribution, hence a zero–one law. Exhaustive finite checks independently support enumerative identities but are not treated as infinite proofs.

**Originality (PASS; historical priority qualified).** Recent work on regular languages and small permutation classes supplies the motivating logical limit-law framework. Classical locally threshold testable characterizations already identify the syntactic boundary/interior decomposition. Earlier colour-forgetting zero–one laws consider markedly different probability spaces. No inspected source directly supplies the strict arithmetic difference between the two ternary spectra; a stronger unlocated transfer theorem remains a residual risk. Details and the actual comparison searches appear in `AUDIT.json`.

**Value (PASS).** The theorem gives complete, effectively computable probability spectra in two natural observational languages and a sharp non-definability boundary: a probability of \(1/9\) is available with named colours but impossible without them. An explicit finite-group orbit mechanism explains why removing names erases a factor of three from the allowed denominators. Exponential convergence and a cycle zero–one contrast sharpen the classification.

**Limitations.** Ordinary first-order successor languages only; anonymous means keeping equality of colour, not forgetting all colour information. The result does not classify palettes of size at least four or richer logics, and independent scientific validation has not been performed.
