## Value — PASS
The Paley tournament is a canonical arithmetically defined doubly regular tournament. Its directed neighborhood is a natural and topologically meaningful invariant, distinct from its directed flag complex. An integral sphere-wedge classification plus the entire 55-element affine character explains which two-dimensional classes are preserved by symmetry and calculates quotient homology; these facts cannot be extracted from Betti numbers or generic directed-neighborhood existence theorems alone. The exact natural finite invariant has meaningful uses as a reproducible benchmark for tournament homotopy and equivariant constructions.
