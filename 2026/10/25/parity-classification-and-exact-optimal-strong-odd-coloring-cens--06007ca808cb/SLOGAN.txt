For the complete bipartite graph with every edge subdivided once, the strong odd chromatic number is four exactly when both bipartition sizes are even, and otherwise three; optimal three-colorings are enumerated by an explicit parity formula. For the even-width multitheta subfamily \(S(K_{2,q})\), the full number of optimal named strong odd four-colorings is \(12\cdot4^q+6\cdot3^q-18\cdot2^q-6\).
