# Parity-controlled avalanche spheres on two-jump directed cycles

## Finding
For every integer \(n\ge5\), let \(D_n\) be the simple digraph on \(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z\) with exactly the arcs \(i\to i+1\) and \(i\to i+2\) from each vertex. Use parallel sandpile firing, in which every vertex holding at least two grains fires once in a round, loses two grains, and sends one grain to each of its two out-neighbors. For \(q\in\{0,1\}\), start from the configuration \(c^{(0)}_0=q\) and \(c^{(0)}_i=2\) for all \(i\ne0\). Let \(\mathcal A_n(q)\) be the simplicial avalanche complex generated by all sets of simultaneously firing vertices, and let \(\mathcal A_n^t(q)\) use only rounds \(0,\ldots,t\). Put \(k=\lfloor n/2\rfloor\).

**Theorem (complete homotopy and persistence classification).** The avalanche complex satisfies
\[
|\mathcal A_n(q)|\simeq
\begin{cases}
S^{k-1},&n\text{ odd, for both }q=0,1,\\
S^{k-1},&n\text{ even and }q=1,\\
\mathrm{pt},&n\text{ even and }q=0.
\end{cases}
\]
For every coefficient field \(\mathbb F\), the unreduced degree-zero persistence barcode of \(\{\mathcal A_n^t(q)\}_{t\ge0}\) is exactly one essential interval \([0,\infty)\). All higher-degree barcodes are empty except in the sphere cases, where the sole positive-degree interval is \([k,\infty)\) in degree \(k-1\), with multiplicity one. The integral reduced homology is accordingly \(\mathbb Z\) in degree \(k-1\) in the sphere cases and zero otherwise, with no torsion.

When \(n\) is even, changing **one initial grain** at vertex \(0\), from \(0\) to \(1\), changes a contractible avalanche complex into a sphere of unbounded dimension \(n/2-1\), and introduces one persistent class born at round \(n/2\). Neither configuration has a round in which all vertices fire. For odd \(n\), the same one-grain change preserves the sphere homotopy type and the entire persistence barcode.

## Assumptions and scope
The graph is sinkless, directed and strongly connected. All indices are taken modulo \(n\); since \(n\ge5\), the two outgoing neighbors are distinct and the graph is simple. Parallel firing is synchronous, and the firing threshold is **at least** the outdegree, not strictly greater. A firing set generates its full simplex, including all subsets. Empty firing sets contribute nothing. At any time the avalanche complex is the union of the simplices from all firing sets through that time. Homotopy equivalence refers to geometric realizations. Integral assertions follow from the explicit nerve homotopy types, not from computations over a field.

## Proof
**1. Defect recurrence.** Write \(d_i^{(t)}=2-c_i^{(t)}\), and let \(h_i^{(t)}\) be \(1\) if vertex \(i\) does **not** fire at time \(t\) and \(0\) otherwise. Because every vertex has two incoming and two outgoing arcs, the update obeys
\[
d_i^{(t+1)}=d_i^{(t)}-2h_i^{(t)}+h_{i-1}^{(t)}+h_{i-2}^{(t)},
\qquad h_i^{(t)}=\mathbf1_{\{d_i^{(t)}>0\}}.
\]
Let \(e_j\) denote the vector with one unit at coordinate \(j\), with subscripts read cyclically. In both configurations the first excluded (nonfiring) set is \(B_0=\{0\}\). The first defect vectors are
\[
\begin{aligned}
q=0:&\quad d^{(0)}=2e_0,\qquad d^{(1)}=e_1+e_2;\\
q=1:&\quad d^{(0)}=e_0,\qquad d^{(1)}=-e_0+e_1+e_2.
\end{aligned}
\]
The recurrence gives, for \(2\le t\le k\) when \(n=2k+1\), and for \(2\le t\le k-1\) when \(n=2k\), respectively,
\[
\begin{aligned}
q=0:&\quad d^{(t)}=-e_1+2e_{2t-1}+e_{2t},\\
q=1:&\quad d^{(t)}=-e_0-e_1+2e_{2t-1}+e_{2t}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Here all displayed coordinate positions are distinct for the indicated ranges. To prove these formulas, insert the expressions at \(t=1\) into the recurrence to obtain \(t=2\); from a formula at \(t\ge2\), the positive coordinates are precisely \(2t-1\) and \(2t\). Subtracting twice their indicator and adding its two right shifts cancels the old two positive coordinates and places \(2\) at \(2t+1\) and \(1\) at \(2t+2\); the negative fixed coordinates stay unchanged until cyclic wraparound. This is induction for all indicated \(t\).

Thus, for \(1\le t<k\), exactly the vertices
\[
B_t=\{2t-1,2t\}
\]
fail to fire. In particular, every other vertex fires, even when its grain count exceeds the threshold. The only remaining case is the last round of distinct firing sets.

**2. Wraparound, periods and exhaustive firing sets.** If \(n=2k+1\), the same expression at \(t=k\) gives \(B_k=\{n-2,n-1\}\). One more application of the recurrence returns the original defect vector \(d^{(0)}\), for either \(q\). Explicitly, the increments from the two positive positions at \(n-2,n-1\) place two units at \(0\) and one at \(1\), cancelling the fixed negative defect coordinates as required.

If \(n=2k\), applying the recurrence once to the formula at \(t=k-1\) gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
q=0:&\quad d^{(k)}=e_0-e_1+2e_{n-1},\quad B_k=\{0,n-1\};\\
q=1:&\quad d^{(k)}=-e_1+2e_{n-1},\quad B_k=\{n-1\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The next recurrence step returns \(d^{(1)}=e_1+e_2\) for \(q=0\), and returns \(d^{(0)}=e_0\) for \(q=1\). Since the dynamics is deterministic, these first \(k+1\) recorded firing sets therefore generate **all** firing sets, including those occurring on the periodic tail. No unexamined later firing set can change the complex or its persistence. In the even \(q=0\) case, only round zero is transient; the next \(k\) states form a periodic orbit. Otherwise the orbit starts at round zero.

**3. Nerve and homotopy.** Put \(\sigma_t=(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)\setminus B_t\) for \(0\le t\le k\). The avalanche complex is the union of the full simplices \(2^{\sigma_t}\). Every nonempty intersection of these full simplices is itself a simplex. The finite simplicial nerve lemma therefore identifies the homotopy type of the union with the nerve of the cover. A subset \(J\subseteq\{0,\ldots,k\}\) is a simplex in the nerve precisely when
\[
\bigcap_{t\in J}\sigma_t\ne\varnothing
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\bigcup_{t\in J}B_t\ne\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z.
\]
If \(n=2k+1\), the blocks \(B_0,B_1,\ldots,B_k\) partition all \(n\) vertices: a singleton followed by \(k\) consecutive pairs. If \(n=2k\) and \(q=1\), the same holds with a singleton at each end and \(k-1\) intervening pairs. In either case **every proper subfamily** has a nonempty complement, while the full family covers every vertex. Hence the nerve is the boundary of a \(k\)-simplex and the avalanche complex has homotopy type \(S^{k-1}\).

If \(n=2k\) and \(q=0\), the last excluded set is \(B_k=\{0,n-1\}\). Its firing simplex \(2^{\sigma_k}\) lies inside the initial simplex \(2^{\sigma_0}\), since \(B_0=\{0\}\subset B_k\). It can be omitted without changing the union. Every remaining firing simplex \(2^{\sigma_t}\), for \(0\le t<k\), contains vertex \(n-1\). Their union is therefore a **simplicial cone** and is contractible. This also proves that no all-vertex firing set occurs: each \(B_t\) is nonempty.

**4. Persistent homology.** For each \(t<k\), the union \(B_0\cup\cdots\cup B_t\) omits at least one vertex of \(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z\). Therefore every one of the first \(t+1\) firing simplices contains a common vertex, making \(\mathcal A_n^t(q)\) a cone. All these complexes are nonempty and connected. At \(t=k\), the two sphere cases acquire \(S^{k-1}\), and no later stage changes the simplicial complex. Consequently exactly one positive-dimensional homology class is born at time \(k\) and survives forever; in the even \(q=0\) case every stage is contractible. Since \(k\ge2\), no new reduced degree-zero class appears, and the unreduced degree-zero module has only the essential class born at time zero. This proves the full barcodes over every field as well as the integral homology claim.

## Verification
The standalone standard-library program `verify.py` is included. Run `python3 verify.py`. It independently simulates all parallel sandpile rounds until exact state repetition for both configurations for every \(5\le n\le81\), reproduces the orbit from the defect recurrence, checks the omitted-block formulas and cone/partition criteria, tests translated defect positions, and directly computes \(\mathbb F_2\) simplicial homology at every filtration stage for \(5\le n\le12\) without using the nerve reduction. The recorded output is `VERIFY_OK orbit_cases=476 direct_mod2_stages=80`. These are finite consistency checks; the infinite, integral and homotopy assertions follow from the proof above.

## Relationship to prior work
Riihimäki and Smith, *Avalanche homology of digraphs via sandpile dynamics*, arXiv:2606.26786v1, introduce avalanche complexes and their temporal persistence filtrations, and analyze directed paths and the **one-outgoing-arc** directed cycle. Their results for those graphs do not give the firing orbit or homotopy type of the **two-outgoing-arc circulant** treated here. Their discussion highlights the effect of a one-grain perturbation; the present even/odd classification supplies a distinct mechanism in which no stage becomes a full simplex.

The published result *Sharp arithmetic transitions in complementary residue avalanche complexes* treats binary configurations on ordinary single-step directed cycles, where firing sets translate unchanged. That hypothesis fails for the present two-step digraph: its firing supports are driven by a nonbinary, nonlinear recurrence, and they change between a singleton and disjoint pairs. Neither result covers or implies the theorem above. Existing parallel chip-firing work on directed graphs investigates firing periods, but periodicity alone does not determine the simplicial nerve or its persistent homology.

## Limitations
Only the two specified near-threshold initial configurations are classified; the result does not claim arbitrary initial grain vectors, arbitrary two-step circulant edge offsets, or robustness under graph modifications. The persistence filtration is by **parallel firing rounds**, not by the number of distinct nonempty simplices. The exact combinatorial formula is particular to the inclusive threshold of two and the jumps \(+1,+2\). The literature searches and accessible full texts do not establish global priority or exclude an unindexed equivalent theorem. Independent scientific validation has not been performed.
