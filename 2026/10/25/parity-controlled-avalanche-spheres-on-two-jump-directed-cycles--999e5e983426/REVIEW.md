# Scientific review: two-jump avalanche parity theorem

## Correctness
**PASS.** The defect update is an exact algebraic rewriting of simultaneous firing on a two-in/two-out directed circulant. The proof reconstructs every state through its first repetition and includes separate cyclic-wrap calculations for both parities and both values of the exceptional initial grain count. It establishes the precise finite generating simplices. The simplicial nerve lemma yields sphere homotopy types when the exclusion blocks partition the vertex set; a literal common-apex cone handles the even zero-grain case. Early stages are cones and the final stage is constant, so the persistence intervals and integral homology follow without a field-specific assumption. The included checker independently verifies direct simplicial homology in small dimensions, but finite tests are not used as the general proof.

## Originality
**PASS, within checked literature.** The originating arXiv:2606.26786v1 develops avalanche complexes for directed paths and single-step cycles; it does not determine the nonlinear two-jump firing orbits. The closest public mathematical record, `SCOPE-sharp-arithmetic-transitions-in-complementary-residue-avalanche--0cd469567fa0`, concerns cyclic translates of binary supports on a one-outregular digraph, not the two-outregular circulant or the even/odd one-grain transition proved here. The inspected 2024 directed chip-firing literature discusses period constraints rather than exact avalanche nerves. The exact pair-block orbit, its homotopy and complete persistence are not implications of those results. Some priority risk from unindexed literature remains.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The motivating preprint explicitly requests new graph families and sharper persistent-avalanche analysis. This is a uniform infinite-family result on the canonical first extension of the directed cycle: adjoining the next-nearest forward edge. It identifies an unbounded-dimensional, sharp parity-controlled topological change under a one-grain perturbation, with exact birth time. The effect is not explained by the trivial presence of an all-vertex firing set, because no such set occurs in either configuration. This makes the theorem a structural model and a reproducible benchmark, not an arbitrary small numerical increment.
