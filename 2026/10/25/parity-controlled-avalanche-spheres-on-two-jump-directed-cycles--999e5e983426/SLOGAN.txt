For the directed two-jump cycle with two grains at every vertex except one, replacing zero by one grain creates an essential \((n/2-1)\)-dimensional avalanche-homology class when \(n\) is even, while the zero-grain case remains contractible. When \(n\) is odd, both configurations have a single essential sphere class in degree \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor-1\); all positive-degree persistence classes, when present, are born precisely at round \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\).
