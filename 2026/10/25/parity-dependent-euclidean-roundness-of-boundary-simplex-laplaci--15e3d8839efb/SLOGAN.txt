The canonical top-Laplacian polytope of a simplex boundary has a sharp parity gap in Euclidean roundness: in even order its inradius tends to \(\sqrt2\), whereas in odd order it is asymptotic to \(n^{-1/2}\), although its edge lengths become nearly equal. Exact in- and enclosing-ball centers, radii and contact sets reveal that only a strict majority of odd-order vertices support the minimum enclosing sphere.
