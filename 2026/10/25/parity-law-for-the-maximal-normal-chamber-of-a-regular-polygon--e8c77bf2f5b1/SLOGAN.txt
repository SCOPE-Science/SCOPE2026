For every regular Euclidean \(n\)-gon with \(n\ge5\), the maximal-normal chamber closes to a regular \(n\)-gon when \(n\) is even and a regular \(2n\)-gon when \(n\) is odd. Its area fraction is \(\tan^2(\pi/n)\) in the even case and \(2\tan(\pi/n)\tan(\pi/(2n))\) in the odd case, with a parity split in the \(n^{-4}\) term.
