The ideal product of \(\langle2,3\rangle\) with the ordinary numerical semigroup \(\{0\}\cup[m,\infty)\) has a complete parity-and-residue normal form for every \(m\ge3\). This gives exact formulas for its minimal generators, entire pseudo-Frobenius set, genus and Frobenius number, exhibiting a period-six pattern in an emerging ideal-multiplication setting.
