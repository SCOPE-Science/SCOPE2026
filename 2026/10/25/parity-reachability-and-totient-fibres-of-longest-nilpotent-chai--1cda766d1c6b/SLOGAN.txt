For longest nilpotent chains in the monoid of injective cyclic-order-preserving partial maps on \([n]\), every distinct ordered terminal/initial pair occurs when \(n\) is odd, while even \(n\) permits exactly the pairs with odd circular separation. Each neighboring pair supports exactly \(\varphi(n-1)\) such maps, giving \(2n\varphi(n-1)\) in total.
