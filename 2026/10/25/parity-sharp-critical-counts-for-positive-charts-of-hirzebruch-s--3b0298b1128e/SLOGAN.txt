For the explicit positive Cox charts of every Hirzebruch surface \(\mathbb F_m\), the chart is always \(\mathbb A^2\), but its very affine locus has \(H_2\)-rank \(m+4\) for even \(m\) and \(m+3\) for odd \(m\). The exact number of generic complex logarithmic critical points is \(m+1\) or \(m\), respectively, giving the paired sequence \(1,1,3,3,5,5,\ldots\).
