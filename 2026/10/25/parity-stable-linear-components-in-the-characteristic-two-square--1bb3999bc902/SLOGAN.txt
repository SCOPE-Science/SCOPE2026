For every characteristic-two field, the reduced square-zero derivation varieties of \(k[t]/(t^{2m})\) and \(k[t]/(t^{2m+1})\) are isomorphic unions of exactly \(\lfloor m/2\rfloor+1\) explicit linear components. Over \(\mathbb F_q\) each has \(q^m+\lfloor m/2\rfloor(q-1)q^{m-1}\) points, with a completely determined acyclic stratum in even dimension.
