For every characteristic-two field, the reduced rank-\(r\) varieties of square-zero derivations of \(k[t]/(t^{2m})\) and \(k[t]/(t^{2m+1})\) are isomorphic: below the half-rank threshold each is \(\mathbb A^{2r-2}\times(\mathbb A^2\setminus\{0\})\), and above it each is \(\mathbb G_m\times\mathbb A^{m-1}\). Over \(\mathbb F_q\), this gives exactly \((q^2-1)q^{2r-2}\) or \((q-1)q^{m-1}\) differentials of rank \(r\), respectively.
