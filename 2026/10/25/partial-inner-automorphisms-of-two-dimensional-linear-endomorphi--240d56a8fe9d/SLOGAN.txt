For the full two-dimensional linear endomorphism monoid over any field, every partial inner automorphism is either a full projective-linear conjugation, the zero-only map, or a trace-preserving linear isomorphism between rank-one matrix lines. This gives an exact order \(q(q^2-1)+q^2(q+1)^2+(q+1)^2(q-1)+1\) over \(\mathbb F_q\), solving the first nontrivial dimension of the recent linear-monoid problem.
