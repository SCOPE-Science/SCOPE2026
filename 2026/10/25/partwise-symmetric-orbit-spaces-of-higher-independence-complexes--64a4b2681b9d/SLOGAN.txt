For every complete multipartite graph with \(s\) nonempty parts, the geometric quotient of its \(r\)-independence complex by independent permutations within the parts is homotopy equivalent to the \((r-1)\)-skeleton of the \((s-1)\)-simplex. An explicit sorted-coordinate simplicial model strongly collapses to that skeleton, so the quotient is \(\bigvee^{\binom{s-1}{r}}S^{r-1}\) when \(1\le r<s\) and contractible otherwise, independently of the part sizes.
