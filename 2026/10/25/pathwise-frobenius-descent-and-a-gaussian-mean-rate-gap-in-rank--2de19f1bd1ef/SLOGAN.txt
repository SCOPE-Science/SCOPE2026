For rank-one power-Nyström approximation of any nonnegative operator-monotone matrix function vanishing at zero, the squared Frobenius error decreases for every fixed sketch, yet an exact two-eigenvalue Gaussian formula shows that mean excess error can decay at only half the fixed-sketch exponential rate. A continuous nondecreasing scalar function outside the reciprocal-convex class reverses this descent in an explicit two-dimensional example.
