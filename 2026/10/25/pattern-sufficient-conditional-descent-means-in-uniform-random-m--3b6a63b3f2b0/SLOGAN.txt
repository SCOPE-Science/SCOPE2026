For a uniform matching, the mean number of descents after conditioning on any uniformly marked submatching depends only on the number of descents in its endpoint-rank pattern: \(\mathbb E[D(\pi)\mid\tau]=n+(k/n)(D(\tau)-k)\). The resulting exact covariances with crossing, nesting, and alignment counts are respectively \(-(n-1)/3\), \((n-1)/3\), and zero.
