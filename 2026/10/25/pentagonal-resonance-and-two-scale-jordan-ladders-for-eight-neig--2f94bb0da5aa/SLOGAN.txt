For eight-neighbour XOR dynamics on every \(5\cdot2^a\) by \(5\cdot2^b\) periodic square grid, exactly \(16\cdot2^{a+b}\) binary dimensions are recurrent; the remaining dimensions form two explicit scales of nilpotent Jordan ladders. Every configuration becomes periodic by the sharp time \(2^{\max(a,b)}\), and the fraction requiring the final update is exactly \(1-2^{-8\cdot2^{\min(a,b)}}\) except on the \(5\times5\) base, where it is \(1-2^{-9}\).
