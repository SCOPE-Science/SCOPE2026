The ordinary matching complexes of all five rectangular grids \(P_3\square P_n\) with \(3\le n\le7\) admit explicit **perfect acyclic face-poset matchings** with one critical vertex and, respectively, \(5,9,16,31,55\) critical \((n-1)\)-simplices. These complete constructive certificates realize the corresponding sphere-wedge homotopy types, whose numerical values were previously asserted in a subsequently retracted paper.
