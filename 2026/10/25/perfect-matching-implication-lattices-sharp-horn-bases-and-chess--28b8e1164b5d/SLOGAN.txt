The positive Horn closure defined by all permutation matrices has a cardinality-optimal implication basis of \(n^2(n-1)+nn!\) rules for \(n\ge3\), computes every consequence in one round, and has an exact Laguerre-polynomial interval Möbius formula. Every proper interval beneath inconsistency is self-similar, and its order complex has the simple-homotopy type of a square chessboard complex; these are new connections among a natural bijection theory, logical consequence, and matching topology, while known general tools are credited.
