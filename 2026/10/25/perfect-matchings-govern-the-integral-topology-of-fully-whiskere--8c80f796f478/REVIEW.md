# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
An all-graph discrete Morse matching pairs faces differing by the pendant edge of the least uncovered original vertex. Its directed face-poset graph is acyclic because original-edge subsets cannot increase, and each fiber has a nonremovable pivot along a directed ascent. The critical faces are precisely the original perfect matchings; a distinguished singleton is the ordinary critical zero-cell. The explicit joins of zero-cycles pair by an integral identity matrix with the cocycles extracting maximal perfect-base-edge simplices, proving primitiveness. Endpoint choices differ by boundaries, and permutations of reduced zero-cycle factors give the orientation signs. Verified finite computations: 190 acyclic matchings, 162 complete mod-two homology calculations and 54 signed-Lefschetz actions.

Singh (arXiv:2101.05534) proves vertex decomposability for the specific fully whiskered matching complexes but not this explicit rank, primitive basis, or automorphism action. The 2-matching complex of Vega (arXiv:1909.10406) is not the ordinary matching complex. Search cannot exclude an unindexed equivalent theorem, and specializing prior general machinery may duplicate some non-equivariant parts.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The count of perfect matchings, a central graph invariant, equals the exact multiplicity of topological sphere summands for all fully whiskered finite graphs; the theorem upgrades this to explicit primitive integral cycles and a complete group action. It applies to nonouterplanar and disconnected base graphs and gives information absent from ranks alone.
