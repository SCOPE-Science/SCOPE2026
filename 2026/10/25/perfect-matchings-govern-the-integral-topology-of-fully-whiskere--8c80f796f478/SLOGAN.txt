For any finite graph \(G\), the ordinary matching complex of its fully whiskered graph is contractible if \(G\) has no perfect matching; otherwise, with \(|V(G)|=2m\), it is a wedge of one \(S^{m-1}\) for each perfect matching. These spheres have explicit primitive integral cycles, and graph automorphisms act by a signed permutation of perfect matchings determined by the ordering of their base edges.
