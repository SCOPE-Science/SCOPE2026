In the asymmetric simple inclusion process, all separated sites have strictly negative stationary occupancy covariance, represented exactly by a two-particle death-chain collision probability. With periodically varying gate rates, the normalized covariance between sites \(L\) and \(L+s\sqrt L\) converges to \(-\operatorname{erfc}(s\bar a/(2\sqrt{\bar b}))\), so genuine rate heterogeneity broadens the macroscopic anticorrelation layer through the ratio of inverse-rate moments.
