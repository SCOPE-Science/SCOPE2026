For every \(n\ge19\), the inclusive radius-two Rips complex of \(GP(n,3)\) is \(S^1\vee\bigvee^n S^2\): its \(n\) hexagons kill a primitive rank-\(n\) sublattice of first homology and contribute an integral basis of octahedral two-spheres. The natural dihedral group permutes those spheres without orientation signs, while reflections reverse the surviving circle.
