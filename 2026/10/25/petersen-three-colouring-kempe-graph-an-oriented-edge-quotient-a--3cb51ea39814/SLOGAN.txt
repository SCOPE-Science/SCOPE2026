The proper vertex three-colourings of the Petersen graph, up to global palette renaming, have a Kempe-switch graph isomorphic to the line graph of the once-subdivided \(K_5\). The quotient has diameter \(3\), while the entire \(120\)-state labelled Kempe graph is vertex-transitive with exact diameter \(4\) and distance shells \((1,6,25,61,27)\).
