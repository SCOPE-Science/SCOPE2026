The coordinatewise conjunction of two equal-slope Sturmian shifts has a completely explicit ergodic-measure simplex: for \(\alpha<1/2\), an entire interval of phase separations collapses to the zero measure, while for \(\alpha>1/2\) every separation remains distinct despite a flat symbol-frequency plateau. Exactly at antipodal separation in the long-window regime, the ergodic process loses all odd rotation eigenvalues and retains only \(e^{2\pi i 2k\alpha}\).
