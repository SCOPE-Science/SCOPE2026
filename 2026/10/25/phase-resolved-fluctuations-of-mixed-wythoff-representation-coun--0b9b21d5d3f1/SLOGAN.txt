Mixed lower-plus-upper Wythoff representation counts obey the uniform phase law \(r(n)=nF(\{n\varphi^{-2}\})+O(\log(2n))\), with an explicit three-piece continuous profile \(F\). Sampling \(r(n)/n\) over integers gives an effective limiting mixture: an atom of mass \(\sqrt5-2\) at \(\varphi^{-2}\) and density \(2\) on \((0,\varphi^{-2})\), with Wasserstein error \(O(\log^2(2X)/X)\).
