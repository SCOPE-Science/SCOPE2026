For the four rectangular grids with at least three rows and columns and at most sixteen vertices, fourth robust clique homology in degree five is the planar-orientation-sign twist of the rational permutation module on unordered triples of elementary squares. In particular the sixteen-vertex square has \(D_4\)-character \((84,4,0,-10,-10)\), so its reflections act with negative trace despite the known 84-sphere nonequivariant homotopy type.
