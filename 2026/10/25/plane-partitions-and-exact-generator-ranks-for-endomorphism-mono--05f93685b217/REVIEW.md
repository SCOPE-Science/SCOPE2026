# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The actual RESULT.md proves a bijection between permissible (t,e), t^3=t, e^2=e, te=et=e, and e-fiber multisets of t-fixed components and two-cycle components. A nondistinguished fixed component contributes A(z), an unordered two-cycle leaf pair contributes B(z), and the outer multiset gives r3=[z^n](M(A M(A+B))-M(A M(A))). The rank-two essentiality iff each e-fiber has at most one nondistinguished t-fixed component follows from constructing/undoing a two-cycle square root. This yields the MacMahon product minus partition product and, via Gould–Grau–Johnson Proposition 4.2, m(n)=3+r2+r3 for n>=5. Independently implemented exact coefficient transforms reproduce m(5..12)=35,86,207,503,1206,2880,6808,15996 and (r3,r2) for n3..6 as (1,3),(4,8),(15,17),(46,37). No finite count is substituted for the general bijection.

## originality

PASS

The published endomorphism-monoid theorem supplies the reduction to orbit counts but explicitly leaves their generating functions as an enumeration problem. This work gives complete exact expressions for all n>=5 and a nontrivial essentiality criterion; classical plane partitions only identify the resulting product, not its interpretation. Best-of-knowledge originality, not exhaustive priority.
