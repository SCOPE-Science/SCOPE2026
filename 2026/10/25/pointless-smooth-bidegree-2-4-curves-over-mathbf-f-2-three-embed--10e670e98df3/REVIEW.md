The small exceptional set and its two different branch-point patterns make an exact classification useful beyond the bare existence theorem, without claiming a new general existence criterion.

**Limits.** Restricted to \(\mathbf F_2\) and to factorwise embedded equivalence, not abstract curve isomorphism. Exact computer-assisted enumeration rather than symbolic closed-form classification for arbitrary fields.
