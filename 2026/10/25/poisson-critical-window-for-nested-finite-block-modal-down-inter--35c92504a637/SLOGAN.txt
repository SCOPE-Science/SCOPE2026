Fresh balanced-block down-interpretations have an exact finite Markov kernel, and their nested modal information survives on the scale \(t\asymp\log_b\log n\). When \(np^{b^t}\to\lambda>0\), the number of surviving blocks converges to \(\operatorname{Poisson}(\lambda/b)\), so the chance of a nonbottom modal value tends to \(1-e^{-\lambda/b}\).
