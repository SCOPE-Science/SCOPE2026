At the sparse threshold \(p_n=c n^{-1-1/m}\), violations of \(\Box^{m+k}p\to\Box^m p\) are isolated \(m\)-arc directed paths whose count tends to \(\mathrm{Poisson}(c^m)\). The minimum edge-deletion repair count and the number of optimal repairs converge jointly to \((Z,m^Z)\), giving a sharp local-to-global modal validity transition.
