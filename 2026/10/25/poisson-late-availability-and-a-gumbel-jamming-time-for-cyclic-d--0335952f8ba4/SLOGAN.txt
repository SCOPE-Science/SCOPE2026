On an initially empty cycle with unit-rate irreversible dimer arrivals, the still-available edges at time \(\log n+x\) form an asymptotically spatial Poisson process of intensity \(e^{-2-x}\), with an \(O(\log n/n)\) approximation error for the total count. Consequently, the last successful deposition time, centered as \(T_n-\log n+2\), converges to the standard Gumbel law.
