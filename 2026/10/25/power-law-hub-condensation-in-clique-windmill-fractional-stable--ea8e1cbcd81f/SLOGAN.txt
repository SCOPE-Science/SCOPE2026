For fixed clique size \(r\ge2\), the hub coordinate of a uniform point in the fractional stable-set polytope of a windmill with \(m\) cliques lives on scale \(m^{-1/r}\), versus \(m^{-1}\) for the integral stable-set polytope. After removing the independent-block volume factor, the relaxation volume has a sharp asymptotic \(m^{1-1/r}\) amplification; for friendship graphs the volume ratio itself is asymptotic to \(\sqrt{\pi m/2}\).
