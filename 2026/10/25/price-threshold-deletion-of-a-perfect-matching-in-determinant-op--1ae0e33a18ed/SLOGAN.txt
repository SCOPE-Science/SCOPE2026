For determinant-optimal conductance allocation on \(K_{n,n}\) with unit-priced nonmatching edges and matching edges priced at \(\tau\), the unique global optimizer deletes the entire distinguished perfect matching exactly when \(\tau\ge n(2n-3)/[(n-2)(2n-1)]\), for every integer \(n\ge3\). Below the threshold, all optimal conductances are positive and have explicit closed forms; the threshold is exactly the ratio of missing-edge to present-edge effective resistances in the crown graph.
