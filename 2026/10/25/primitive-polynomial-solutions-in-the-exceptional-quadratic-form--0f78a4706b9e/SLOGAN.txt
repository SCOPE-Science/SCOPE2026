For every \(n\in\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\}\) and every \(c\in\mathbb Z\), the exceptional family \(F_c=(x+y)^2+cyz\) has an explicit integer-polynomial solution to \(t^2=nxyzF_c\) with \(\gcd_{\mathbb Z[u,v]}(x,y,z)=1\). This supplies the polynomial-coprimality case left open for that family in Conjecture 2.4(a) of Gawron–Ulas.
