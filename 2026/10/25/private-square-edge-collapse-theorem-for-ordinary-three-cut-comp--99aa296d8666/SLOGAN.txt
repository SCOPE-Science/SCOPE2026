For triangle-free graphs with no three common neighbors and a private edge in every four-cycle, the ordinary three-cut complex is a wedge of exactly \(\binom{N-1}{2}-\sum_v\binom{d(v)}2+q\) spheres of dimension \(N-4\). This extends the edge-disjoint-square result to overlapping squares, including all Möbius ladders on at least eight vertices.
