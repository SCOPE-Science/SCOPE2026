For Kollár's characteristic-two rational plane curve \(Z^2Y^q=X^{q+2}+XZ^{q+1}\), \(q=2^n\ge4\), every geometric projective automorphism is diagonal: \((X,Y,Z)\mapsto(u^qX,uY,Z)\) with \(u^{q+1}=1\). Hence the full projective stabilizer of the embedding is cyclic of order \(q+1\), even though its affine normalization is a line.
