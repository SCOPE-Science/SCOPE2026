The exact weight-three binary three-deck generating function conjectured by Chrisnata and collaborators is proved for every length: \(\sum_{n\ge3}D_{3,3}(n)z^n=z^3/(1-z)^4-z^7/((1-z)^2(1-z^2)(1-z^4))\). Every ambiguous three-deck has exactly two words, related by an explicit swap-and-transfer involution of their four zero gaps, and the asymptotic uniquely reconstructible fraction is \(3/4\).
