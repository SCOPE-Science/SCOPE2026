In characteristic \(7\), the symmetric genus-five hyperelliptic family \(y^2=x^{12}+ax^6+1\) has exactly four Jacobian rank strata \(2,3,4,5\), and its genus-two quotient and three-dimensional Prym exchange superspeciality at disjoint parameters. At \(a=0\) the Prym is superspecial while the quotient is ordinary; at \(a^2=-1\) the quotient is superspecial while the Prym is ordinary.
