Deleting any single block of \(k\geq3\) consecutive generators from a Sally numerical semigroup of multiplicity \(e>2k\) gives an explicit two-branch pseudo-Frobenius formula. As the deletion position varies, the Cohen--Macaulay type and last Betti number run through every integer from \(1\) to \(e-k-1\), each exactly once; only the symmetric position is almost symmetric.
