For holomorphic homogeneous degree-\(d\) polynomials on the complex unit sphere, concentration in the union of two equal coordinate caps is maximized exactly by the complex linear span of the two pure powers \(z_1^d,z_2^d\). The next spectral level consists of the one-mixed-power monomials, and a strictly positive explicit beta-integral gives the optimal stability gap for every degree, dimension, and cap threshold.
