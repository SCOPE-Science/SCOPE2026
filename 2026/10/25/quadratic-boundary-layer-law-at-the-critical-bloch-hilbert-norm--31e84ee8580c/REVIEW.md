# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** A reconstructed proof uses the exact radial norm formula, uniformly controlled endpoint expansions, explicit beta-function coefficients, and a global localization argument. The limiting profile has a unique maximum and determines both claimed coefficients. The numerical script checks representative parameters but does not establish the infinite limit.

**Originality — PASS, with priority risk.** The closest primary source is Cui–Lin–Lu–Ye (arXiv:2609.27812v3), which obtains an exact one-variable norm representation and fixed-parameter boundary behavior. Its asymptotic is not uniform as the threshold parameter is approached. The present result computes the missing linear endpoint term uniformly and derives sharp joint-scaling and global-optimality results. Hu–Ye (arXiv:2410.16598) give earlier two-sided estimates, and Ye–Zheng (arXiv:2510.23314) study logarithmically weighted codomains. Unindexed equivalent analyses remain possible.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** A natural operator-norm phase boundary has a quantified quadratic detachment of optimizers and a sharp quadratic excess, beyond the known statement of strict inequality. This describes the structure and strength of the recently identified transition, rather than a cosmetic numerical refinement.

**Limitations.** The result is one-sided near the critical exponent; there is no uniqueness assertion for each fixed parameter and no independent validation.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
