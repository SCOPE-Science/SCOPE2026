For the area-normalized outer parallel body of the regular dodecagon, the nearest Fourier zero moves **strictly inward** after an arbitrarily small amount of rounding. A single facet-normal zero provides the certified quadratic bound \(\kappa(K_t)\le\kappa(P)-\eta t^2+O(t^3)\), where \(0.00014158574849<\eta<0.00014158574850\).
