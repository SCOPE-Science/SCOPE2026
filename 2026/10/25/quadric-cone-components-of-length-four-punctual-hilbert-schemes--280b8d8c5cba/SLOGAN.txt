For every reduced complex plane-curve triple point with \(r\) distinct tangent directions, the reduced length-four punctual Hilbert scheme has one \(\mathbb P^2\) component and exactly \(r\) projective quadric-cone components, attached along the tangent-direction boundary lines. Its motivic class is \(1+\mathbb L+(r+1)\mathbb L^2\), and ordinary triple points have a triangle of three attaching lines.
