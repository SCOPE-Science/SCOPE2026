For Haar-random central plane sections of the side-two cube, the probability that the area is within \(\delta\) of the maximum \(4\sqrt2\) is \(3\sqrt2(4+\pi)\delta^2/(32\pi)+o(\delta^2)\). Among these near-maximal sections, the limiting quadrilateral and hexagon proportions are respectively \(2/(4+\pi)\) and \((2+\pi)/(4+\pi)\).
