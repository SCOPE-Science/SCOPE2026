For trinomials \(1+e^{it}+\rho e^{i\phi}e^{i(k+2)t}\) with \(k\ge2\) and \(2k<p<2k+2\), an explicit positive amplitude threshold guarantees strict increase of the \(p\)-moment from aligned to opposite third-mode phase, with a quantitative phase gap. The exact first-order sign contrast is a gamma quotient and decays as a power of the degree at fixed fractional exponent.
