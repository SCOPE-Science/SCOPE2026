# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** The argument classifies normal kernels on both sides of the rotation subgroup. It proves that the embedding relation among generalized-dihedral quotient types is exactly the finite-abelian kernel embedding relation, including the delicate elementary \(2\)-group case. It then derives the partition lattice and Gaussian-binomial multiplicities by disjoint exhaustive cases. The exact finite checker confirms thirteen representative kernels, but generality rests on the proof.

**Originality — PASS, subject to literature risk.** Halder and Ballal (arXiv:2610.13076, Theorem 2.3) already classified the ordinary cyclic-kernel dihedral case; this is credited, not counted as new. Their abelian-group theorem provides a foundational component, but the noncyclic generalized-dihedral normal-quotient classification and exact elementary quotient multiplicities are not stated in the inspected relevant full text. Searches for quotient classes, embeddings, quotient-self-duality, 2-primary fibers and generalized-dihedral aliases yielded no equivalent or stronger coverage. Earlier subgroup-type theory could implicitly contain this conclusion; an independent literature audit remains necessary.

**Value — PASS.** A full quotient-type lattice for a nonabelian family, arbitrary even-primary behavior, and exact multiplicity profiles of normal kernels are natural structural invariants. The results clarify why quotient-class counts differ from subgroup counts, and extend a recent motivating cyclic theorem to every finite abelian rotation kernel.

**Scientific boundaries.** The result is restricted to inversion semidirect products. Small finite computations are corroboration, not an infinite proof. Novelty remains vulnerable to broadly formulated classical normal-subgroup results.
