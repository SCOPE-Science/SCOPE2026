For every positive radial weight and every polyanalytic order, centered-disk concentration has strictly decreasing nonnegative angular-block eigenvalues and admits only radial maximizers. In the unweighted bianalytic Bergman space its complete spectrum is explicit, with a simple first and second eigenvalue and a sharp stability gap asymptotic to \(\tfrac34(1-R)^3\) at squared radius \(R\uparrow1\).
