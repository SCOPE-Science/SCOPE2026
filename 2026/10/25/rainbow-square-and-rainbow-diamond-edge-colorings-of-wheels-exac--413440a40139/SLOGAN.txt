For every wheel with \(n\ge5\) rim vertices, rainbow-four-cycle and rainbow-diamond proper edge colorings coincide and need exactly \(n\) colors; all optimal assignments have a cyclic offset-word classification counted by \(n!\operatorname{tr}(T_{n-4}^{\,n})\) with named colors. The four-rim wheel is the sole exception to equality of the two indices, requiring eight versus six colors.
