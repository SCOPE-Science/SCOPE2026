Best-rank truncated projected exponential Euler is positive-semidefinite-safe at rank one, but rank two already admits a negative covariance eigenvalue for a strictly stable Lyapunov flow with rank-one positive forcing. The exact rank-two step boundary is \(h_*=(2a)^{-1}\log(1+2at_*)\), with \(t_*=(e+\sqrt{4de+5e^2})/2\), and positivity-constrained rank repair can have arbitrarily larger Frobenius residual than unrestricted truncation.
