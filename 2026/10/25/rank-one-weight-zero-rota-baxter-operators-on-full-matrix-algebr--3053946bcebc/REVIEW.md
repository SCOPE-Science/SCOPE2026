# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

A nonzero image line gives \(R(X)=f(X)B\). The Rota–Baxter equation forces \(B^2=tB\) and the left/right eigenfunctional equations with eigenvalues summing to \(t\). The polynomial relation for multiplication by \(B\) forces the square-zero or complementary-idempotent alternatives, in every characteristic. Counts come from nested image/kernel spaces for square-zero matrices, complementary spaces for idempotents, and exact functional dimensions. `check.py` directly verifies low-dimensional cases. Boundary cases \(n=2\), characteristic two, and normalization of image lines were checked.

## Originality — PASS, bounded comparison

Tang–Zhang–Sun's 2014 classification and its explicit reproduction in Benito–Gubarev–Pozhidaev (2018, Theorem 5.2) already cover the \(2\times2\) algebraically closed orbit types; this is not claimed as new. Gubarev's general AYBE correspondence and matrix-algebra nilpotency bounds (arXiv:1805.00723, Theorem 3.4, Section 5.4) do not give the all-\(n\), all-\(q\) rank-one census. The new all-dimensional image/functional dichotomy and Gaussian-binomial formula are not statements or immediate numerical consequences of those cited results. A bounded search found no matching stronger count. The inaccessible original Tang full text and possible unindexed literature remain priority risks.

## Value — PASS

Operator rank one is the first nonzero stratum of the quadratic Rota–Baxter scheme of an important central simple algebra. The uniform closed formula, image-rank refinement, and the proved change of dominant image geometry at \(n=3\) provide a natural baseline for geometric and arithmetic analysis of higher-rank strata. The exact result is not a single-field table or an arbitrary parameter slice.

## Limitations

The theorem does not classify rank greater than one, algebra-automorphism orbits, or the entire Rota–Baxter variety. This is a self-review, not a claim of external certification or absolute publication priority.
