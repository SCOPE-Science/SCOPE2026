For a symmetric inverse monoid sandwiched by any rank-\(r\) partial bijection, exactly a fraction \(1-rk/n^2\) of the rank-\(k\) elements are nilpotent. Uniform random elements exhibit a sharp square-root boundary: a rank deficiency asymptotic to \(\tau\sqrt n\) makes the nilpotence probability asymptotic to \((1+\tau)/\sqrt n\).
