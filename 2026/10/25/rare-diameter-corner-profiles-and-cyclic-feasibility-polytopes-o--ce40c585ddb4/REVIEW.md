# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The starting joint corner-variable convergence and scalar diameter representation are explicitly cited from Lao, Theorem 5.3. The new conditional law follows by disjoint pairing into independent gamma sums, exact beta-gamma splitting, a coordinate permutation into a cyclic polytope, concentration of the gamma excesses, and dominated convergence away from null hyperplanes. The scalar upper-tail prefactor follows from the exact gamma survival function; the cubic small-tail coefficient follows by local inclusion-exclusion with a triangle-free incidence graph. The standalone verifier confirms algebraic incidences, cyclic-constraint equivalences, and finite numerical diagnostics, without substituting sampling for proof.

## Originality
PASS subject to further bibliographic review. The strongest close prior result, Lao's full implicit scalar law, implies that *some* endpoint asymptotics are in principle computable, but it neither identifies nor states the joint conditional mark distribution, cyclic polytope, independence phenomenon, or computed coefficients. The latter are explicit sharpenings rather than a new scalar weak-limit theorem. Koroleva–Nikitin addresses different U-max observables. published-finding corpus claim searches for the original title, diameter aliases, Poisson corner limits, cyclic polytope and gamma sums found no dominating marked conditional result. The book's appendix D.2 evaluates the equilateral triangle special case, not fixed odd polygon orders at least five. Historical coverage beyond inspected sources remains a risk.

## Mathematical value
PASS. For a natural longstanding geometric extreme-value model whose scalar limiting law is available only as a complicated dependent exponential minimum, a rare-event conditional law describes the *spatial profile of the extremal absence*, not merely a scalar probability. The exact cyclic feasible set explains the multiplicative tail constant and dependency structure. The small-deficit correction isolates overlap effects of competing near-diametral pairs. This is a substantive geometric mechanism, not an arbitrary parameter substitution.

## Limitations
Fixed odd polygon order, an iterated limit rather than uniform rates, no elementary evaluation of the positive polytope volume, and remaining historical-priority uncertainty. The date of initial online release for the cited 2010 book could not be verified to the day. Independent reproduction and mathematical review have not been performed.
