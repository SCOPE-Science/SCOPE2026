Deleting the matching-diagonal vertices from a product of two simplices loses exponentially little volume but retains a Hausdorff gap tending to \(1/\sqrt2\). Its random directional-width loss occurs with exact probability \(2/n-1/[n(n-1)]\), and the rare positive loss has a sharp conditional exponential limit after scaling by \(\Phi^{-1}(1-1/n)\sqrt{2n}\).
