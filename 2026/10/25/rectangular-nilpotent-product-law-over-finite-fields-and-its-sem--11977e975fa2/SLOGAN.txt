Two independent uniform matrices of sizes \(m\times n\) and \(n\times m\) over \(\mathbb F_q\) have nilpotent product with exact probability \(q^{-m}+q^{-n}-q^{-(m+n)}\). Consequently, the full matrix ring \(M_d(\mathbb F_q)\) has the same nilpotent-product probability as the field \(\mathbb F_{q^d}\), and every finite semisimple ring has an explicit product formula.
