Three-stage RKL2 has a three-state pure-birth Markov generator whose endpoint-positivity step sizes form the two disjoint intervals \([0,(5-\sqrt5)/2]\cup[(5+\sqrt5)/2,15/4]\), despite scalar stability throughout both and the gap. Two stages on any finite Markov chain, or three stages on at most two states, cannot exhibit this loss-and-recovery phenomenon.
