# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The facet identification is direct from the two row choices and reduces the complex to the Alexander dual of a hypergraph whose edges are all consecutive length-\(t\) windows. The augmented simplicial trace and complement-sign identity are explicitly derived, including the empty-face contribution. Reflection-fixed independent sets are palindromic words, and an exact block decomposition yields a rational generating function whose coefficients prove the Lefschetz formula for every \(L,t\ge1\). The 2025–26 source's Theorem 3.6 supplies the known integral rank-one sphere/contractible dichotomy; multiplication by the computed sign then follows integrally. The standalone script independently checks column facets, mod-two homology, signed chain traces, forbidden-run palindrome sums and many integer substitutions. A finite test is not treated as the all-parameter proof.

## Originality — PASS, with documented risk
The closest exact primary text is Navarra–Qureshi–Veer, arXiv:2511.13477, especially Proposition 3.1 and Theorem 3.6. Its accessible full text classifies homotopy types and does not compute the involution, Lefschetz series, or quotient homology. General Alexander duality gives an isomorphism with an orientation twist but does not give the sign of the concrete palindrome-counting involution. Relevant indexed claims about reversal of Morse complexes and hexagonal-chain independence complexes concern different simplicial complexes, with no parameter equivalence or dominating theorem established. Originality is best-knowledge rather than a proof of global uniqueness: an unindexed symmetry calculation for path-hypergraph independence complexes could cover this claim.

## Value — PASS
The recently introduced complexes have alternating sphere/contractible topologies, but those spaces' ordinary Betti numbers do not decide whether their canonical diagram reflection preserves or reverses orientation. The computed two-parameter integral character resolves this missing equivariant datum, yields an exact rational Lefschetz series, and determines the complete reduced rational homology of reflection quotients. The distinction is substantive even at equal nonzero sphere dimension: the quotient can retain or lose its rational top class. The scope is a natural infinite two-parameter family rather than an arbitrary finite slice.

## Scope and limitations
Only the rectangular two-row Young shape and the canonical reversal are classified. Fixed sets' complete homotopy types and arbitrary-row representations remain undetermined. Earlier nonequivariant homotopy ranks are not presented as new. Indexing and accessible full-text examination cannot rule out every unindexed equivalent result.
