For every nilpotent \(A\) over \(\mathbb F_q\), a uniformly chosen nilpotent matrix commuting with \(A\) is regular nilpotent exactly with a computable Euler-product probability, positive precisely when the Jordan partition of \(A\) is almost rectangular. In the nonzero case the unique polynomial expressing \(A\) in that regular nilpotent is exactly uniform over all polynomials of the necessary valuation, independently of the Jordan block scale.
