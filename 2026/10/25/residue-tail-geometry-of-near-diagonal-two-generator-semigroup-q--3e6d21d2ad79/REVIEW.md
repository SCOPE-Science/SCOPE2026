# Review of the residue-tail theorem

## Correctness

PASS. The representation equation modulo \(a-1\) forces a positive index \(t\) and gives an explicit finite arithmetic progression for each such index. The inequalities \(u_{t+k}\le at+k\) and \(u_{t+k}\ge u_t+k\) prove that every congruence class becomes a single uninterrupted infinite tail. Coprimality separates the classes. The gap formulas and the maximal-embedding-dimension description then follow by explicit parity and decomposability arguments. A standalone exact-integer verifier checked 2,721 coprime parameter pairs and 44 embedding-dimension examples. Its finite scope is explicitly distinguished from the proof.

## Originality

PASS under inspected-source comparisons, with residual priority risk. Liu (arXiv:2607.23076) studies the general Frobenius question and fixed-quotient specializations; Cabanillas (arXiv:1904.08240) supplies a general algorithm for all two-generated quotients; Adeniran et al. (arXiv:1809.09360) already give general genus formulas; Liu (arXiv:2604.26415) treats quotient divisors of an original generator. No inspected result explicitly states the full two-parameter arithmetic-tail decomposition and the derived unbounded maximal-embedding-dimension family. General algorithms imply computability but do not eliminate the need for the proved uniform structural formulas. Possible equivalent older specializations remain a publication-priority risk.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The formula completely classifies an unbounded two-parameter family of natural numerical-semigroup quotients, rather than a sporadic parameter value. It exhibits a direct gap geometry and Hilbert series with \(k\) terms independent of the size of \(a\), and identifies an infinite family where quotienting a two-generated semigroup yields arbitrarily large maximal embedding dimension. Its full membership result is substantially more informative than recomputing a known single genus value.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
