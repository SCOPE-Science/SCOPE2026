For every coprime pair \((a,k)\), the quotient \(\langle a,a+k\rangle/(a-1)\) is exactly a disjoint union of \(k\) arithmetic-progression tails whose starting points are explicit floor expressions; hence its full gap set, Hilbert series, genus and Frobenius number are explicit. When \(k=2\), these quotients form an infinite family of maximal-embedding-dimension numerical semigroups with explicitly classified minimal generators and unbounded embedding dimension.
