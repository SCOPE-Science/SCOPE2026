For every Johnson graph \(J(n,k)\), every vertex set of size \(\binom nk-2\binom{n-2}{k-1}+1\) resolves the graph, with exactly two exceptions: the sharp thresholds are \(5\) for \(J(4,2)\) and \(37\) for \(J(8,4)\). A coefficientwise binomial inequality proves that adjacent pairs are the worst landmark ambiguity except at those two antipodal anomalies.
