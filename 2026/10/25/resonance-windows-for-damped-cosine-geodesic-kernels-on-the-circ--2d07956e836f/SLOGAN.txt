For the damped geodesic cosine \(e^{-a\theta}\cos(\omega\theta)\) on the circle, four explicit scalar inequalities decide positive definiteness for every finite configuration, and admissibility always implies strict definiteness on distinct sites. At high frequency, admissible modulation exists in narrow resonant windows about each integer, with sharp asymptotic half-width \(a(e^{\pi a}-1)/(\pi k)\).
