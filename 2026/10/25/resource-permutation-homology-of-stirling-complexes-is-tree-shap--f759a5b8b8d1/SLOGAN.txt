The permutation character on the unique nonzero reduced rational homology of a Stirling complex is \(\chi(\sigma)=\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)\sum_{a=1}^{m-1}(-1)^{m+a+1}\binom{m}{a+1}a^{c(\sigma)}\), independent of the shape of the underlying tree. Forgetting the resource labels by quotienting under \(S_n\) annihilates every reduced rational homology group.
