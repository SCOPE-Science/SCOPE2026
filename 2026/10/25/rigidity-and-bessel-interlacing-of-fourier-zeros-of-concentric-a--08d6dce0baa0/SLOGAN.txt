For every concentric annulus in \(\mathbb R^d\) with \(d\ge2\), the Fourier amplitude has exactly one simple radial zero between each pair of successive zeros of \(J_{d/2-1}\), with no other nonzero real zeros. These nodal spheres move analytically from ball-zero radii to sphere-zero radii as the inner radius grows; one-dimensional shells instead exhibit infinitely many double zeros at an exact arithmetic set of ratios.
