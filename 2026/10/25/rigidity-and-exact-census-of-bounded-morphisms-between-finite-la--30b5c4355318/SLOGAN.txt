For finite weak orders whose target has at least two points in every level, every surjective Kripke bounded morphism is necessarily a levelwise surjection of equal-height orders. Their full labelled count is \(\prod_i q_i!\left\{\!\begin{smallmatrix}p_i\\q_i\end{smallmatrix}\!\right\}\) at equal heights and zero otherwise; one singleton target level is enough to break the height rigidity.
