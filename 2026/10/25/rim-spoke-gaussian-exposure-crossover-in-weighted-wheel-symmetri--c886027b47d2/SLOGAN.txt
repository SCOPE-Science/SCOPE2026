The Euclidean random width of a weighted wheel symmetric edge polytope has a sharp rim-to-spoke transition at spoke weight \(\sqrt2\): fixed subcritical weights exhibit Gumbel fluctuations, while supercritical weights exhibit half-normal fluctuations. Inside the \(1/\sqrt{\log m}\) critical window, the directional-width correction converges to \((c+\sqrt2|Z|)_+\), and the limiting angular fraction exposing a spoke is explicit.
