For antipodal point sets on \(S^3\) whose pairwise angles have a common even root-of-unity period \(L\), the degree-by-degree harmonic Gram matrices obey an exact period-plus-ramp law and the point count is at most \(2L\). At equality, degree \(L-2\) has exactly one compatible blind mode, degree \(L-1\) is an exactly tight interpolation frame, and every later degree remains surjective; infinitely many quaternionic configurations attain the bound.
