With one marked vertex, ordinary counting colour refinement in every connected Kneser graph \(\mathrm{KG}(n,k)\) recovers all \(k+1\) intersection-size strata. For \(k\ge3\), precisely two rounds suffice exactly when \(n\ge3k-2\), while the sparse odd graphs \(\mathrm{KG}(2k+1,k)\) need exactly \(k-1\) rounds.
