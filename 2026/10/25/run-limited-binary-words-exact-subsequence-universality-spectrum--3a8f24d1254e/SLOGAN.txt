For every homopolymer cap \(R\), the complete distribution of binary scattered-subsequence universality indices has rational generating function \(1+2zA_R(z)(1+uz)/(1-uz^2(A_R(z)+A_{R-1}(z)))\). The sharp forcing threshold is length \((R+1)k\): every admissible word is \(k\)-universal, while exactly two admissible words one symbol shorter are not.
