For the complete prism \(K_n\square K_2\), secure connected dominating sets under single-neighbor replacement admit an exact three-case classification and a closed all-cardinality polynomial. Its inclusion-minimal configurations have precisely the sizes \(4\) and \(n+1\), with respective counts \(\binom n2(2n-3)\) and \(2n\), giving an unbounded \(n-3\) gap in an \(n\)-regular connected graph family.
