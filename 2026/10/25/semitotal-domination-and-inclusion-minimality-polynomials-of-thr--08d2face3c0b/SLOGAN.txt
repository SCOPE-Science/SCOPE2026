For \(K_n\square P_3\), \(n\ge3\), all semitotal dominating sets admit an exact three-layer generating polynomial and all inclusion-minimal ones are counted by \(28u^3+12u^4\) at \(n=3\), or \(n^3u^3+(2^n-2n-1)u^n+2nu^{n+1}\) for \(n\ge4\). The minimum semitotal domination number is always \(3\), while the upper semitotal domination number is \(n+1\), yielding an unbounded gap \(n-2\).
