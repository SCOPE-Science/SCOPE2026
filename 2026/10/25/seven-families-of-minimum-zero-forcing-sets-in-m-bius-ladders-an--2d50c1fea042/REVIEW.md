PASS. The first-force argument both excludes smaller sets and reduces every possible minimum set to finitely many local fourth-vertex placements. Explicit propagation and dihedral symmetry give seven complete rotation classes. The seven-class overlap table is established by a bounded affine-congruence calculation, not extrapolation from a finite test range; the class table proves degree counts, edge count, and connectivity for all \(n\ge6\). The separate six-rung integer walk-trace check proves the stated finite nonisomorphism witness. Two runnable verification implementations reproduce the finite evidence.

## Mathematical value

PASS. Minimum-cardinality zero forcing is already known on Möbius ladders, but does not tell how many optimal control placements exist or whether they can be changed without adding controls. The seven-family census and connected exchange structure answer both questions for a natural infinite family. The comparison with the untwisted ladder shows robust local counts despite a detectably distinct global reconfiguration graph. This is more than repeating the known value four or extending a small finite table.

## Scope and limitations

The main theorem is for all integer \(n\ge6\). The spectral comparison concerns only \(n=6\). The work does not prove exchange diameters or mixing properties for the family. Mathematical-record searches cannot prove absolute novelty. The supporting verification programs are ordinary reproducibility checks, not external independent review.
