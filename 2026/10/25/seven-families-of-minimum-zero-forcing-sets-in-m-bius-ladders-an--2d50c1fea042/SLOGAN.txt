Every Möbius ladder \(M_{2n}\) with \(n\ge6\) has exactly \(13n\) minimum four-vertex zero-forcing sets, in seven rotation classes; their single-vertex exchange graph is connected with \(48n\) edges and degree enumerator \(4nt^5+8nt^8+nt^{12}\). Despite matching the circular ladder's counts at \(n=6\), the two exchange graphs are nonisomorphic, detected by distinct sixth adjacency traces.
