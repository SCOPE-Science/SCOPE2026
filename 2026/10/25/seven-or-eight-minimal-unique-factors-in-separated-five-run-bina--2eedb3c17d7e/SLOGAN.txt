For separated five-run binary words \(0^a1^b0^c1^d0^e\), all minimal unique contiguous factors belong to an explicit list of seven mandatory patterns and one conditional pattern. Among uniform positive run-length compositions of length \(n\), their count converges to seven with probability \(2/3\) and eight with probability \(1/3\), with error \(O(n^{-1})\); the two probabilities are exact after conditioning on separation at every \(n\ge13\).
