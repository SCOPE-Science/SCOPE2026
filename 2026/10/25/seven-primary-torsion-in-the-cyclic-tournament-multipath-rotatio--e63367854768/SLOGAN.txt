The free sevenfold rotation quotient of the seven-vertex regular cyclic tournament's multipath complex has \(H_3\cong\mathbb Z\oplus(\mathbb Z/7\mathbb Z)^2\) and \(H_1\cong\mathbb Z/7\mathbb Z\), while the covering complex has torsion-free integral homology. Exact cellular Smith reductions also determine its entire rational rotation-homology representation.
