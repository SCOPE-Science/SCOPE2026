The finite dicyclic groups \(\operatorname{Dic}_n\), \(n\ge2\), have exactly seven theories at first-order quantifier rank two in the ternary relational multiplication language. Indices \(2,3,4\) are isolated, while all larger indices are distinguished precisely by divisibility by \(3\) and by \(4\); every group has a rank-two equivalent representative of order at most \(48\), and the bound is sharp.
