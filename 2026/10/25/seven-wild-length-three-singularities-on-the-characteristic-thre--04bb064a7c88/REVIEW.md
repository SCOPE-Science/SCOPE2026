# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The six projective partials reduce in characteristic three to adjacent-coordinate recurrence relations. Every singular point lies on the affine torus, and successive elimination gives \(k[t,t^{-1}]/(t^{21}-1)\) exactly; the cubic equation is in the eliminated partial ideal. The polynomial factors as \((t^7-1)^3\), proving exactly seven supports and local lengths three. The Hessian at a representative point has rank four and kernel \((2,1,0,2,1)\), with nonzero cubic restriction, so characteristic-not-two formal quadratic splitting proves the complete local normal form. The cyclic diagonal symmetry and Frobenius exponents yield the remaining assertions. The accompanying exact checker reproduces key identities, but finite samples are not used for the universal proof.

## Originality — PASS

The 2010 González-Aguilera–Liendo paper defines the Klein cubic over the complex numbers and studies smooth cubic hypersurfaces and automorphisms. The 2025 He–Li–Wang–Zheng paper identifies the cyclic six-variable equation as a smooth complex Klein cubic and locates a different seven-\(A_2\) singular member at parameters \((1,1)\); the Klein equation corresponds to parameters \((0,0)\). Those results do not imply the scheme of the coefficientwise reduction in characteristic three. Targeted literature and published-record queries, and a comparison of recovered prior mathematical claims, identified no covering result. Residual risk: a specialized characteristic-three singularity calculation might already be published without the searched terminology, and not every historical source was available.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This is a complete natural bad-reduction classification of a classical cubic fourfold, not a selected numerical example. It detects a nonreduced singular scheme of total length twenty-one, gives the formal singularity type and all arithmetic Frobenius orbits, and explains the failure of smoothness in a distinguished residue characteristic. The infinitesimal structure is not recoverable from a point count, and the result gives a precise input for further study of local and global degeneration.
