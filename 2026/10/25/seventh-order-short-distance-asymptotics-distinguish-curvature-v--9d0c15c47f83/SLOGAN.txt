For two independent area-uniform points in a smooth strictly convex oval, the seventh-order small-distance coefficient is exactly \(A^{-2}(\frac1{2240}\int\kappa^4ds-\frac1{2520}\int\kappa_s^2ds)\). Unlike the positive fifth-order term, it changes sign across explicit real-analytic ovals with identical perimeters and identical distributions of curvature radius.
