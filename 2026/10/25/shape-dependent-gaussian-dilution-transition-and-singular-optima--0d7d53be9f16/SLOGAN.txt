For a Gaussian perturbation of a compound-Poisson process with Gamma jumps, the variance-exact right sub-gamma scale has an exact shape-dependent dilution threshold: it stays at the Gamma scale for shape at most one, while larger shapes switch between local-cumulant and interior control. Arbitrarily dilute Gamma jumps leave a nonzero limiting optimal scale, with a sharp, shape-dependent \(\rho^{1/(\alpha-1)}\) boundary correction; Erlang-two jumps admit a fully explicit square-root formula.
