Among genus-\(17\) Humbert–Edge curves in characteristic \(5\), the Jacobian has \(5\)-rank at least \(13\) and \(a\)-number at most \(4\), and both bounds are sharp. At most four of the fifteen elliptic double quotients can be supersingular; equality has a unique six-branch-point projective configuration, where the remaining genus-two quotient is ordinary.
