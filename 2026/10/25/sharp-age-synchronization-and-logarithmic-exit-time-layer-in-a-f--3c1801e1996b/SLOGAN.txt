In the stationary infinite-swarm farthest-reset model for anomalous Brownian motion, the normalized particle age becomes uniform as the diffusion exponent \(H\) diverges, with an exact total-variation error \(0.1606639484570\ldots/H+o(H^{-1})\). The transition layer at the maximal typical age has a universal, non-Gaussian profile given by a centered logarithm of Brownian interval-exit time.
