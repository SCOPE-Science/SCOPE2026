For the alternating-frequency Kuramoto four-cycle, strengthening one existing edge strictly raises the stable-locking threshold at every positive relative weight, with the complete threshold given by a unique-root algebraic formula. The threshold increases from \(1/2\) to \(0.568126105332\ldots\) even as the strengthened edge becomes infinitely strong, and its exact initial slope is \((\sqrt2-1)/8\).
