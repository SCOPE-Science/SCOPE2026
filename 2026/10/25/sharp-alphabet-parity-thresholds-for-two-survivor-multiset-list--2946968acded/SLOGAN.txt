For \(q\ge4\), multiset deletion codes with exactly two surviving symbols attain the universal capacity bound \(\lfloor qL/2\rfloor+q2^{q-2}\) first at \(L=(q+3)2^{q-3}\) for even alphabets and one allowance earlier for odd alphabets, with explicit constructions. At the even-parity boundary, every optimal codebook is classified and the number of labeled optima is exactly \(\binom{n-3}{2^{q-3}}^{\binom q2}\).
