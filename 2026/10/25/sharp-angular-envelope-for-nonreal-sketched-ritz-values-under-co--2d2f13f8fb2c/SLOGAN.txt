For two-vector sketched Rayleigh–Ritz with one unobserved coordinate, the largest possible imaginary part of a sketched Ritz value of a symmetric positive-definite matrix is exactly \(\frac{L-m}{2}\sqrt{(\gamma^{-1}-1)/2}\), where \(\gamma\) is the smaller singular value of the sampled orthonormal trial basis. Every nonzero observation angle admits equality, so an arbitrarily accurate coordinate sketch need not preserve real Ritz values.
