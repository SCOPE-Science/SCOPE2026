For \(3\times3\) upper-bidiagonal systems with eigenvalues all equal to one, two-step GMRES (and the matching first two-grid acceleration cycle) has a sharp, ratio-dependent stagnation frontier \(uv\ge\min_{t>0}(t^2+1)(t+v/u)^2/[t(t^2+(v/u)t+(v/u)^2)]\). Unequal couplings can cause complete stagnation at a coupling product where the balanced Jordan case always makes progress, even though ordinary Richardson solves the same systems exactly in three steps.
