At the critical radial–angular spherical Fourier extension exponent, every fixed \(L^2\) datum has an exact logarithmic growth coefficient governed only by its norm and antipodal correlation, with a universal \(1/\log R\) exponent-crossover law. Purely even or odd data are the exact sharp maximizers, and a global, optimally constant parity-defect inequality quantifies the loss for mixtures.
