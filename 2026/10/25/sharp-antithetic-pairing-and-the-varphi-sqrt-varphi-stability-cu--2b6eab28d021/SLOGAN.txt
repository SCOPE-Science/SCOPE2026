For a two-component bilinear saddle, antithetic pairing of the two stochastic extragradient oracle calls can converge in mean square at step sizes where both independent sampling and identical-sample reuse diverge in mean square. This strict three-way separation is possible exactly when the larger-to-smaller component ratio lies between \(1\) and \(\varphi+\sqrt\varphi\), where \(\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\).
