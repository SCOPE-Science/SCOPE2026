Every regular Euclidean polygon with an odd number \(n\ge3\) of sides has an explicit direction-by-direction area-halving chord length, with minimum \(2R\cos(\pi/(2n))\sqrt{\cos(\pi/n)}\) and maximum \(R(1+\cos(\pi/n))\). The equality orientations are exactly the dihedral symmetry classes, and the extremal gap decays at the sharp rate \(R\pi^2/(4n^2)\).
