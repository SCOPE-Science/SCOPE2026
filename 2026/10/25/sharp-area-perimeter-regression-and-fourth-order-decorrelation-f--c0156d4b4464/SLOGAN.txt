For a polygon generated by \(n\) independent uniform points on a circle, the population-optimal linear prediction of area from perimeter has regression slope \(2-24\pi^2/n^2+O(n^{-3})\) and irreducible linear residual variance \(9504\pi^{10}n^{-9}(1+o(1))\). The area–perimeter correlation approaches one at the sharp fourth-order rate \(1-\rho_n\sim297\pi^4/(10n^4)\), while the leading perimeter and optimally projected area fluctuations have independent Gaussian limits.
