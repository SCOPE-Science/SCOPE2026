For any nonuniform one-dimensional consistent-mass Dirichlet \(P_1\) heat mesh with at least three interior nodes, backward Euler preserves all nonnegative nodal data exactly at \(\tau=0\) and at steps \(\tau\ge\max_{2\le j\le n}h_j^2/6\); below that positive threshold an explicit two-node-distance entry is negative. Every adjacent-node update coefficient remains strictly positive for every positive step, and the two boundary element lengths play no role in the sharp cutoff.
