For every planar triangle, the area of its intersection body divided by the triangle area has the exact barycentric profile \(2+4\sum_{t\in\{a,b,c\}}t(1-t)\log((1-t)/t)\). This strictly Schur-concave ratio is maximized uniquely at the centroid, where it equals \(2+(8/3)\log2\), and approaches \(2\) at every boundary approach.
