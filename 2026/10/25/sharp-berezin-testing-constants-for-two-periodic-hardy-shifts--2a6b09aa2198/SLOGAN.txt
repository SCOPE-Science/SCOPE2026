Every nonzero two-periodic complex unilateral shift on Hardy space has Berezin number at least \((3-2\sqrt2)\) times its operator norm and at least \((\sqrt2-1)/2\) times its numerical radius. Both sharp bounds have exactly the same extremizers: the odd-indexed weight is nonzero and the even-indexed weight equals \(-(4\sqrt2-5)\) times it, where the entire closed Berezin disk is attained.
