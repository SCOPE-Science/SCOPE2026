For the robust-clique complexes of the \(3\times5\) and \(3\times6\) rectangular grids, every parameter has an exact integral sphere-wedge homotopy type or is contractible; the first nine higher-parameter cases beyond the general \(k\le3\) theorem have explicit full-face acyclic Morse certificates. The expected binomial sphere count is exact through \(k=4\) but first breaks at \(k=5\), giving \(67\) instead of \(70\) and \(206\) instead of \(210\).
