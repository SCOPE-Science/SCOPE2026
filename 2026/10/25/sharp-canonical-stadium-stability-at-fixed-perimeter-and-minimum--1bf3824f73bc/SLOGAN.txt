For each planar convex body, subtract the canonically oriented stadium with the same perimeter, minimum width, and Steiner point: the stadium area surplus is exactly the squared Fourier-energy distance, and it controls squared Hausdorff distance with optimal coefficient \(3/(2\pi)\). This constant is sharp at every noncircular perimeter–width pair and drops to \(1/(2\pi)\) exactly on the constant-width boundary.
