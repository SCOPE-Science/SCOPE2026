The all-right spherical triangle has exact centroidal hemisphere fractions ranging from \(\frac4\pi\arctan\frac{3-\sqrt5}{2}\) to its complement, with unique optimal normal orbits \((2,-1,-1)\) and \((1,1,-2)\). For the positive spherical orthant in dimension \(d\), the centroidal cut normal \((1,\ldots,1,-(d-1))\) captures a strictly increasing fraction, tending to \(\operatorname{erf}(1/\sqrt\pi)\).
