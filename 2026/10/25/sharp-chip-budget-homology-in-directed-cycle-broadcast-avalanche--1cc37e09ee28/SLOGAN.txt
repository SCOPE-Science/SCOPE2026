A single broadcaster with exactly \(k\) chips feeding directed cycles of lengths \(gm_i\), with pairwise coprime quotients \(m_i\), produces an avalanche complex that is exactly an isolated point plus \(g\) joins of discrete sets; its top integral homology is \(\mathbb Z^{g\prod_i(m_i-1)}\) in degree \(k-1\). This realizes, for every \(k\ge2\), the sharp homological dimension permitted by the \(k\)-chip budget.
