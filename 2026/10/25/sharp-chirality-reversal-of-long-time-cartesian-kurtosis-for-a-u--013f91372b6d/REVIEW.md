# Scientific review

**Correctness: PASS.** Exact Fourier perturbation of the isolated simple principal eigenvalue gives the full second- and fourth-cumulant growth coefficients. The recurrence bounds which Fourier modes contribute at order four. The nonnegative-root classification follows from an explicit quadratic and strict positivity of the denominator. The standalone symbolic/eigenvalue checker passed and the theorem states its fixed-parameter, asymptotic and threshold limitations.

**Originality: PASS (self-review, priority risk).** The primary paper of Santra, Basu and Sabhapandit (arXiv:2211.07337), inspected in its complete HTML exposition including equations and conclusion, treats no deterministic angular drift and records the \(\omega=0\) baseline. Mallikarjun and Pal (arXiv:2209.05912) consider four cardinal headings, not continuously rotating, uniformly refreshed headings. Broader active-particle expansion work (arXiv:2202.12117) provides methods but the available inspected abstract does not assert the exact one-crossing threshold. Related 2026 rotational-diffusion/inertial cABP work concerns different generators. Search failure is not proof of priority; an equivalent unrecognized spectral computation remains possible.

**Value: PASS.** The motivating literature explicitly studies non-Gaussian corrections beyond long-time Gaussian diffusion. The exact chirality threshold shows that deterministic heading rotation can reverse the *sign* of the first non-Gaussian temporal correction, a qualitative transport change that the diffusivity alone cannot indicate. Its explicit parameter-independent Brownian-noise robustness and unique-threshold proof provide a physically interpretable benchmark, not merely a parameter substitution.

The result excludes interactions, finite-time signs at the critical rotation, and nonuniform reset angles.
