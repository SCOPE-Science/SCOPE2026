For a freely moving two-dimensional run-and-tumble particle whose heading rotates at angular speed \(\omega\) between complete Poisson reorientations of rate \(\gamma\), the long-time Cartesian fourth-cumulant growth changes sign exactly once, at \(|\omega|/\gamma=\sqrt{(5+2\sqrt7)/3}\). Independent translational Brownian noise changes the variance but not this sharp negative-to-positive non-Gaussianity threshold.
