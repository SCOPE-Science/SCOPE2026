Every length-\(n\) binary word with exactly two ones has exactly \(n+\max(A,B)(\min(A,B)+1)\) distinct nonempty closed factors, where \(A=\min(a,b)\), \(B=\min(b,c)\) are the truncated zero-gaps in \(0^a1\,0^b1\,0^c\). The sharp maximum is \(n^2/9+O(n)\) with explicitly classified optimizers, whereas zero or one marked occurrence permits only \(n\) closed factors: two marked positions already suffice for quadratic closed-factor complexity.
