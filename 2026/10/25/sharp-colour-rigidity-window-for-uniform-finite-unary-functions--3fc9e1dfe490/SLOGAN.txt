A uniformly random unary function on \(n\) labelled points, independently coloured with \(q_n\sim\alpha n\) named colours, has no nonidentity colour-preserving automorphism with limiting probability \(\exp(-e^{-2}/(2\alpha))\). The entire critical obstruction is asymptotically a Poisson number of same-coloured leaf siblings, with mean \(e^{-2}/(2\alpha)\).
