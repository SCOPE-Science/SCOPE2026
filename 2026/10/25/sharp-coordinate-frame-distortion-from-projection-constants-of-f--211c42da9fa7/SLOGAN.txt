For every real or complex hyperplane of a finite-dimensional supremum-norm space, the best coordinate-deletion distortion \(\Delta\) is bounded sharply by \(\lambda/(2-\lambda)\), where \(\lambda\) is its absolute projection constant. Equality is completely classified by norm-one complementability or equal nonzero annihilator-coefficient moduli; the sharpness concerns coordinate deletion, not arbitrary Banach--Mazur isomorphisms.
