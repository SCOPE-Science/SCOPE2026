# Review of sharp causal Gabor frame bounds

## Correctness — PASS
The critical Zak frame multiplier is normalized by the unitary \(\sqrt a\)-factor. The explicit Laplace-resolvent representation is justified by absolute convergence under \(\int s^{-1}d\mu<\infty\). The elementary real-part inequality \(\operatorname{Re}(1-re^{-i\theta})^{-1}\ge(1+r)^{-1}\) bounds the multiplier away from zero. Both extremal bounds are attained as essential boundary limits, proving **optimality** rather than only admissibility. The gamma-mixture, zeta/eta evaluations, and Fejér-kernel obstruction for \(1/2<p\le1\) separately cover each parameter range. The finite checker confirms numerical consistency but is not substituted for the proof.

## Originality — PASS, with specified priority risk
Belov–Kulikov (arXiv:2505.14207v2, sections 1–4) treats stably decreasing causal windows and related frame sets; its hypotheses enforce exponential decrease, and it does not state the present optimal sample-sum formula or non-Bessel power-tail threshold. Existing nonoptimal estimates for convex integrable causal windows establish frame existence for \(p>1\) but do not imply **sharp constants**, or unboundedness for \(1/2<p\le1\). Published results on sharp Gabor constants for Gaussian, spline, or certain explicitly solvable windows do not logically cover arbitrary positive Laplace mixtures. The 1996 paper by Janssen on frame-bound calculations is materially relevant; only its description was accessible, not the full text, and its exact families have not been exhaustively excluded. Complete historical priority is therefore not certified.

## Value — PASS
The output determines two natural **exact spectral invariants** (the optimal analysis bounds) across an infinite-dimensional class of causal windows using one structural positivity mechanism, and supplies a complete sharp Bessel/frame transition and exact special-function condition number for the canonical polynomial-tail continuum. The change from coarse estimates to the attained sharp constants is quantitatively substantial: for \(g_2\), the lower constant is \((1-\pi^2/12)^2\), while the simple convex-window lower estimate is only \(1/64\). The explicit blow-up of the optimal condition number as \(p\downarrow1\) quantifies the stability boundary. This is more than a renaming, a finite table, or a standard frame-existence deduction.

## Limits and evidence
The theorem is confined to one-sided positive Laplace mixtures on critical rectangular lattices, with the power-tail non-Bessel direction proved specifically for that family. External validation is still required, and the inaccessible full text of Janssen (1996) leaves residual priority uncertainty. The full accessible article of Belov–Kulikov and bibliographic records for Janssen and Gaussian comparisons were examined.
