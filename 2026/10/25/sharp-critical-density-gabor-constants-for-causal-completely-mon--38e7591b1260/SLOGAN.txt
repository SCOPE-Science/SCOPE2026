Every positive integrable Laplace-mixture window supported on a half-line has explicitly computable **optimal** critical-density Gabor frame bounds, with the lower bound determined by an alternating sample sum and the upper by an ordinary sample sum. For the canonical power tail \(g_p(t)=(1+t)^{-p}\mathbf1_{t\ge0}\), the exact frame range is \(p>1\) with bounds \((1-\eta(p))^2\) and \(\zeta(p)^2\), whereas \(1/2<p\le1\) is not even Bessel.
