For equiangular discrete constant-width polygons with ten or twelve sides, the sharp Wigner-caustic-to-polygon area suprema are respectively \((\sqrt5-2)/4\) and \((\sqrt3-1)/8\), and every limiting extremal deformation is classified. The increase between these two side counts exhibits a nonmonotone discrete asymmetry transition: all twelve decagonal parameter vertices maximize, whereas only four of twenty-eight dodecagonal vertices do.
