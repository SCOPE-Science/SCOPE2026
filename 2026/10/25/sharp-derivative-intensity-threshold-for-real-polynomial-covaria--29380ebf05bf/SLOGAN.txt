For real polynomial covariance matrices of degree \(N\), derivative-intensity measurements determine every positive semidefinite covariance exactly when the highest measured derivative order reaches \(\lfloor N/2\rfloor\); below that threshold, distinct strictly positive definite matrices remain indistinguishable. The entire nullspace consists of symmetric bivariate coefficient polynomials divisible by \((X-Y)^{2K+2}\).
