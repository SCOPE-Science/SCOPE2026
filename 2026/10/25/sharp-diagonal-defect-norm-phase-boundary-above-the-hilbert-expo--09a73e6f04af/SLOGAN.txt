On finite \(\ell_p\)-sums with \(p>2\), an anisotropic positive diagonal strict contraction has a norm-valued Pythagorean defect precisely when \((\max d_j)^p\le(\min d_j)^{p-2}\); any nonzero diagonal with a zero multiplier fails. The exact relative curvature constant is \((\max d_j)^p/(\min d_j)^{p-2}\), giving genuinely unequal non-Hilbert dilation examples in the single-operator sense.
