For strictly positive radial quadratic-Gaussian windows, the real self-ambiguity zero set changes from exactly two bounded ovals in one dimension to noncompact asymptotically orthogonal sheets in every higher dimension. The exact sharp Doppler null radius has a dimension-and-parameter phase transition at \(d+4c=6\), while the one-dimensional frequency fold changes at \(c=(\sqrt3-1)/4\).
