For a biased nearest-neighbor walk that independently resets to its running record even when already at that record, resetting boosts the asymptotic exploration speed exactly when the right-step probability satisfies \(p<3/4\). The unique optimal reset rate is \(r_* = 1-[2\sqrt{1-p}]^{-1}\) below the threshold and zero above it; the speed benefit vanishes quadratically at the threshold.
