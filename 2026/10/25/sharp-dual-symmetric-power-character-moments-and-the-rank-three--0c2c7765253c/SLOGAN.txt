For the normalized fourth moment of the central packet \(a\chi_{\mathrm{Sym}^p}+b\overline{\chi_{\mathrm{Sym}^p}}+c\) on \(SU(N)\), the sharp optimizer excludes the trivial character in every rank \(N\ge4\), whereas in rank three it necessarily mixes all three modes. The exact rank-three gain is algebraic, positive for every \(p\ge1\), asymptotic to \(2/(3p)\), and its projective maximizing phase classes jump from three to one when \(3\mid p\).
