For multiplicative half-Cauchy shrinkage with fixed \(0<\zeta<1\), the expected number of factors above a small threshold equals \(\log(1/\varepsilon)/|\log\zeta|\) plus the exact constant \(1/2+\pi^2/[8\log^2\zeta]\), with an exponentially small error. The full count, including post-crossing rebounds, has Gaussian fluctuations with variance coefficient \(\pi^2/[4|\log\zeta|^3]\).
