For the standard lattice join of any two full-dimensional lattice polygons, every ordinary Ehrhart coefficient is strictly positive below normalized volume \(168\); this cutoff is sharp and its first failure has a uniquely determined pair of polygon Ehrhart-data types. The empty triangles of normalized areas \(12\) and \(14\) have join linear coefficient \(-1/15\), and an exact two-inequality criterion identifies every possible linear or quadratic sign failure in this family.
