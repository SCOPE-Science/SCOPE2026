# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof uses the explicit nonlinear gluing map, the nontrivial Serre-dual cocycle, an exact coefficient formula, formal-function extension with \(H^{1}(C,\mathcal O_C(qp))=0\) for \(q\ge3\), and a product cancellation whose order-six coefficient is \(-z\). The pole semigroup \(\langle2,5\rangle\) gives six associated-graded generators; five chosen formal functions supply all six initial classes and hence the upper bound five. The known graded-cotangent computation supplies the matching lower bound. The standalone script checks the stated elementary identities, not the infinite lifting theorem.

**Originality — PASS with residual priority risk.** Park--Shin arXiv:2610.21093v1, Proposition 8.6, proves only \(\operatorname{edim}\ge5\) for the relevant genus-two family. Its general lifting lemma alone does not supply the explicit product cancellation. The earlier Grauert affine-torsor construction is acknowledged. Fujiki's 1996 Section 5 discusses orbit smoothness and extension obstructions, not this exact invariant. Historic priority is not independently certified because Grauert's original 1962 pages were not obtained in full.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The exact embedding dimension places this natural non-\(\mathbb Q\)-Gorenstein zero-Wahl-defect family sharply at the ambient-dimension-five threshold immediately beyond the dimension-four hypothesis of Wahl's codimension-two conjecture. The result adds a structural bound absent from the recent source. No classification for higher genus, negative defect, or defining equations is claimed.
