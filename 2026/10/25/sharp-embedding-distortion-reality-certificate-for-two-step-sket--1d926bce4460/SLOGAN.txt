For two-step sketched Lanczos on any real symmetric matrix, squared-norm embedding distortion \(\varepsilon\) guarantees real Ritz values whenever \(\varepsilon\le a/\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\), with \(a,b\) the consecutive Lanczos couplings. The sharp universal imaginary-part envelope is \(\sqrt{[a(b\varepsilon/\sqrt{1-\varepsilon^2}-a)]_+}\), attained by positive-definite three-dimensional examples that become defective at the exact reality threshold.
