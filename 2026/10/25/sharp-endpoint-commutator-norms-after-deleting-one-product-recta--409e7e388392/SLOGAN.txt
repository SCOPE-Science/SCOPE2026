Deleting one rectangular block from an otherwise independent product law creates a rank-two commutator of the two coordinate conditional expectations with exact endpoint norm \(2\max\{pq,p(1-q),(1-p)q\}\). Its sharp \(L^1\)/\(L^\infty\) amplification approaches two even along distributions where its Hilbert norm \(\sqrt{pq(1-pq)}\) vanishes.
