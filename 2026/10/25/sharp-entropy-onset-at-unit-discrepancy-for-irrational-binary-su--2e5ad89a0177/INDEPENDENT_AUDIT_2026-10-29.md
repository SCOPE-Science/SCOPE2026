# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Define two-sided heights T_{k+1}-T_k=x_k-alpha and T_0=0. All-window discrepancy ≤B is exactly diameter(T_k)≤B. Since T_k≡-k alpha modulo 1 and alpha irrational, dense height residues require diameter≥1, proving emptiness below one. At B=1 each n-block sum is floor(n alpha) or ceil(n alpha), giving aperiodic one-balanced binary sequences; the Morse-Hedlund Sturmian classification and mechanical-word inclusion yield exactly the minimal slope-alpha Sturmian shift with complexity n+1. For any 0<epsilon<min(B-1,alpha,1-alpha), rotation phases r_i in (1-alpha-epsilon,1-alpha) identify separated 01 pairs. Swapping any subset to 10 changes only T_{i+1} by +1 and keeps heights in (theta-1,theta+epsilon), so all interval constraints hold. Weyl equidistribution gives epsilon n+o(n) disjoint eligible sites in length n, hence entropy≥epsilon log2; taking the supremum yields min(B-1,alpha,1-alpha)log2. Uniform irrational frequency excludes periodic points and the proper zero-entropy Sturmian subsystem excludes minimality for B>1. Independently checked 48 finite-window flip cases across two irrational slopes; no finite experiment proves the universal statement.

## originality

PASS

The Sturmian endpoint follows classical Morse-Hedlund, and exponential numbers of finite C-balanced words for integer C>1 are attributed to Lipatov 1982. These do not by themselves imply an extendible two-sided fixed-irrational-alpha corridor with arbitrarily small real excess B-1 and uniformly positive exponential language complexity. Full accessible Berthé–Cecchi Bernales and Bédaride–Berthé–Julien mathematics concern dendric/factor balance and higher-dimensional billiard words, respectively. García-Ramos–Pavlov–Reyes treat hereditary upper bounded-density shifts; their positive entropy theorem does not apply to the simultaneous lower and upper all-window constraint. The new fixed-slope quantitative threshold survives these comparisons. Lipatov 1982 original full text unavailable and is retained as priority risk.

## value

PASS

The uniform all-window binary discrepancy corridor is a canonical quantitative enlargement of Sturmian systems. Exactly at the minimal feasible width 1 it is minimal zero-entropy, while every arbitrarily small positive widening carries positive entropy at every irrational slope without introducing periodic points. The density of independent digit swaps yields a nontrivial explicit lower bound and a sharp universal phase boundary, which aggregate integer C-balanced counts do not mechanically provide.
