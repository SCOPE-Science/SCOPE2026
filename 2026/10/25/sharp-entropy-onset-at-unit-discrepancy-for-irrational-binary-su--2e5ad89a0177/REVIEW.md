For the positive case, it checks every exchanged pair, the exact one-height effect of each exchange, disjointness, global height containment, Weyl's frequency estimate, and the word-injection entropy count. Uniform frequencies exclude periodic points and the embedded Sturmian subsystem proves nonminimality. An executable finite checker passed six examples; it is supplementary.

**Originality — PASS under examined evidence, with priority uncertainty.** The original results in Bédaride--Berthé--Julien (2025; arXiv:2502.18211) concern balance of hypercubic billiard factor frequencies, not the topological entropy of this entire binary corridor. Berthé--Cecchi Bernales (2018; arXiv:1810.07453) recounts the known exponential number of integer-\(C\)-balanced finite words from Lipatov (1982); such aggregate counts do not by themselves give the fixed-irrational-slope and arbitrarily-small-real-excess theorem. The one-balanced Sturmian endpoint is explicitly classical. No direct semantic match or implying mathematical statement surfaced in the examined literature/finding searches. Lipatov's original paper was not fully available for comparison and is the main historical risk.

**Value — PASS.** At the sharp smallest feasible width, the shift is minimal with zero entropy; any positive relaxation permits an explicit density of independent digits and positive entropy while all configurations remain uniformly irrational-frequency and aperiodic. The exact transition and quantitative lower bound answer a motivated structural question that cannot be inferred merely from aperiodicity or correctness of an example.

**Limitations.** The positive entropy itself is not determined exactly, and no result is claimed for rational slopes or larger alphabets. Priority is provisional until further scholarly comparison.
