For every irrational binary frequency \(\alpha\), the uniform all-window discrepancy corridor has a sharp threshold at width \(1\): below it the shift is empty, at it the system is the zero-entropy minimal Sturmian shift, and above it the shift is nonminimal and has no periodic points but positive entropy. The quantitative bound \(h_{\mathrm{top}}\ge\min\{B-1,\alpha,1-\alpha\}\log2\) follows from a positive-density family of independent adjacent digit exchanges.
