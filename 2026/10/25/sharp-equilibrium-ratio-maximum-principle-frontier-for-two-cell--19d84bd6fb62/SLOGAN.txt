For the original two-stage modified Patankar–Runge–Kutta method on a two-cell conservative diffusion, the equilibrium-weighted upper maximum is preserved for every positive time step and strictly positive input exactly when \(\max\{\pi_1,\pi_2\}\le(\sqrt5-1)/2\). Beyond this sharp equilibrium-imbalance boundary, the method remains positive, conservative and equilibrium-preserving yet can amplify the largest equilibrium-relative density.
