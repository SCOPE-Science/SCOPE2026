For every nonsquare rectangle and every finite \(p>-1\), the radial \(p\)-mean body's centered Euclidean incircle touches in exactly four oblique directions. For \(p>2\), its circumcircle exhibits a sharp aspect-ratio transition from four oblique contacts to two axial contacts, with exactly six contacts at coexistence; at \(p=4\) the threshold is \(\sqrt{(963+459\sqrt{17})/2048}\).
