For the canonical unit in-in zonotope of a tree, the exact Euclidean inradius is \(\min\{\sqrt n,2\}\); for a cycle it is \(\min\{\sqrt n,2\sqrt2\}\). The sharp changes at tree order four and cycle order eight also determine every tangency facet and the affine dimensions of all largest-ball centers, including a parity-dependent alternating center segment for even cycles.
