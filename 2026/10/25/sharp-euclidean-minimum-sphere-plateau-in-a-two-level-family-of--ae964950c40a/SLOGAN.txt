For the equal-upper-transfer continuous marked-poset family of a complete two-level poset, the unique Euclidean minimum sphere has \(R^2=q/4+\bigl(p-qt(1-t)\bigr)_+^2/(4p)\). When \(q>4p\), its radius develops an exact constant plateau and its contacting-vertex count drops from \(2^q+1\) to \(2^q\), even though the family keeps the same combinatorial type for every \(0<t<1\).
