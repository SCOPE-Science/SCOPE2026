For a perturbed implicit midpoint method with true SPD generator \(A\) and a rank-one overdamped stage generator \(A+vv^\mathsf{T}\), Euclidean one-step contraction holds exactly through the sharp step ceiling \(h_*=4/[\sqrt{(v^\mathsf{T}Av)(v^\mathsf{T}A^{-1}v)}-\|v\|_2^2]\), unless \(v\) is an eigenvector, when every step is safe. Beyond that ceiling the step can amplify Euclidean norm, despite being spectrally stable and a strict contraction in the \(A^{-1}\)-weighted norm for every positive step.
