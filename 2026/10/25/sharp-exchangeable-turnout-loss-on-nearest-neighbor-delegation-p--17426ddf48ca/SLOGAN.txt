When turnout is exchangeable and default delegates may be only immediate path neighbors, the least expected lost voting weight is exactly \(2a_2+\sum_{s=3}^{n}a_s\), achieved by a reciprocal delegation at an endpoint and all other arrows pointing toward it. For independent abstention probability \(0<q<1\), only the two endpoint arrangements attain the minimum, giving an exact, nonvanishing absolute reliability penalty compared with unrestricted cyclic delegation.
