# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The Fraïssé one-point extension property supplies exactly \(m+1\) independent insertion slots in every coordinate. The subgroup preserving the individually named base corresponds to permutations of coordinate ranking lists up to reversal, and its normalized action on slots is a product of multiset actions. Counting those orbits proves the formula; discrete strict concavity of \(inom{m+r}{r}\) proves both extremizers and uniqueness of occupancy patterns. The singleton-base exception is treated separately. The direct signed-action checker corroborates \(987\) finite cases but is not substituted for the unrestricted proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
Cameron (2002) supplies the multiorder Fraïssé foundation; Linman–Pinsker (arXiv:1405.4297) analyzes the signed symmetries of the two-order random permutation; Braunfeld–Simon (arXiv:1807.07110) classifies homogeneous finite-dimensional permutation structures. No inspected statement or relevant indexed record covers this arbitrary-dimensional **finite-base** stabilizer type spectrum with its sharp maximum, minimum, equality configurations and factorial symmetry-breaking threshold. A previous two-order unmarked orbit-profile result does not imply the marked-base formulas. Implicit elementary prior derivations cannot be ruled out.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The theorem determines exactly how incomplete naming leaves hidden order-coordinate symmetries and how that affects the model-theoretic one-type invariant. The sharp extremizers give the full range of type-count extremes at every dimension and base size, and the factorial threshold states precisely how much naming suffices to destroy the residual finite coordinate group. These are motivated classification/boundary questions, not just an isolated computed instance.

## Limitations and relationship to literature
This is a single natural intermediate overgroup, not a classification of all reducts. Only nonalgebraic one-point types are counted; the finite checker covers small dimensions only. Full earlier texts were inspected in their directly relevant sections, and unindexed overlapping statements remain possible.
