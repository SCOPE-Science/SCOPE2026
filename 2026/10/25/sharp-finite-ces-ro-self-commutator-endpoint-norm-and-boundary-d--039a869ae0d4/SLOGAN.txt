The induced \(\ell^\infty\) norm of the self-commutator of the finite \(n\)-by-\(n\) Cesàro averaging matrix has an exact signed-step extremizer and equals \(2-2\sqrt{2/n}+2/(3n)+O(n^{-3/2})\). This converges to twice the endpoint norm of the infinite Cesàro self-commutator's kernel because finite truncation introduces a rank-one boundary correction.
