For a randomly accelerated particle whose velocity alone undergoes Poisson resetting, stationary-velocity initialization makes the finite-horizon displacement tail exactly \(\Pr(X_T>x)\sim\frac12e^{-2rT/3}e^{-x\sqrt{r/D}/T}\). An extreme displacement then reveals, with probability tending to one, that no velocity reset occurred during the observation window—even though the displacement variance grows only linearly at long times.
