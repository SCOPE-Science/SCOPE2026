# Reproducibility

Run `python verify.py` from the directory containing the unpacked files. All checks are exact integer calculations. The certificate closes the nine-state invariant under all 10 five-agent edges; in six agents it enumerates all 15 possible greedy pair transitions at every depth through seven; the eight-line adversarial trace is checked. These tests do not replace an independent review and do not claim uniqueness in the literature.
