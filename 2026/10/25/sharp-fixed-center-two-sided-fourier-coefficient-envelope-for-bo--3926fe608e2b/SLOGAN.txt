For bounded complex harmonic disk maps with prescribed center modulus \(c\), the exact sharp two-sided Fourier coefficient bound at every positive index is \(\inf_{\lambda\ge0}\{\int\sqrt{\lambda^2+4\cos^2t}\,dm-c\lambda\}\), attained precisely by unimodular imaginary-cosine boundary traces. The sharp envelope falls from \(4/\pi\) at zero center to zero at unit center, with a logarithmically weakened quadratic defect near zero and square-root decay near one.
