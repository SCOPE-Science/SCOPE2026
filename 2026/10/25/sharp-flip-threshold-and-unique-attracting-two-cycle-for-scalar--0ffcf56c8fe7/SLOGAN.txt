On a scalar quadratic, Soft Muon changes from exponential to algebraic attraction at the exact momentum-dependent ratio \(\alpha a/\varepsilon=2(1+\beta)/(1-\beta)\), with critical \(n^{-1/2}\) relaxation along its center manifold; the origin becomes unstable above that ratio. Exactly one locally attracting nonconstant period-two orbit exists above the threshold and none at or below it.
