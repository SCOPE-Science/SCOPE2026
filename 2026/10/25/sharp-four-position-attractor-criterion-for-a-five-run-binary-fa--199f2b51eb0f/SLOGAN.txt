For the five-run binary family \(0\,1^b0^c1\,0^e\), four string-attractor positions are necessary and sufficient exactly when \(b\ge4\), \(c\ge3\), and \(e>c\); the necessity is witnessed by four disjoint uniquely occurring substrings. Among all members of length \(n\), the fraction needing four positions tends to \(1/2\), and their exact generating function is \(z^{13}/((1-z)^2(1-z^2))\).
