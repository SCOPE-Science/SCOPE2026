For a random walk whose signs persist with probability \(q\), the positive-occupation count is magnitude-distribution-free for up to three steps, but at four steps its entire law depends on the magnitudes through just \(\Pr(\eta_1>\eta_2+\eta_3)\). This single parameter ranges sharply over \([0,1/3)\), and four-step universality survives exactly at \(q=1/2\) or \(q=1\).
