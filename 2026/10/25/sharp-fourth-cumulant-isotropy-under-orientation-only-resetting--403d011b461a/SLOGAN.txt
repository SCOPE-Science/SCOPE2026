For a passive anisotropic Brownian particle whose *orientation only* undergoes Poisson resetting, the displacement's fourth cumulant has an exact finite-time directional law and a unique anisotropy-reversal rate \(r=2q\), where \(q\) is rotational diffusivity. At this rate the fourth cumulant is identical in every direction even though the displacement variance remains anisotropic; the law is not Gaussian.
