# Scientific review

## Correctness: PASS

The coordinate-time circular and radial frequencies were reconstructed from the displayed metric, with an independent check from the second variation of the geodesic effective potential. Positivity of energy, angular momentum and the static metric coefficient is established on the complete inverse fiber. The proof excludes all impossible parameter branches, shows necessity and sufficiency of the strict frequency-ordering condition, and establishes global injectivity in the mass-radius ratio through an explicit strictly monotone parameterization. Two endpoint configurations are limits only, never positive-parameter members. Symbolic checks and 4,700 seeded numerical comparisons are replayable through the supplied program. Numerical experiments do not establish the infinite theorem.

## Originality: PASS, with residual risk

The classical Kottler effective potential and the forward epicyclic-frequency expressions were already known and are not claimed as original. Kunst–Perlick–Lämmerzahl (arXiv:1505.05728v1, full text inspected) investigate isofrequency pairing of bound orbits, with background parameters normally fixed, and discuss changing the background; that gives broader qualitative nonidentifiability, but not the exact sharp frequency-only image and full monotone inverse fiber proved here. Boshkayev–Luongo–Muccino (arXiv:2212.10186v1, full HTML inspected, Eq. 13) explicitly write the forward frequency formulas and perform data fitting; their formulas can be specialized and algebraically manipulated to obtain the present statements, but the complete strict feasibility classification, sharp three-parameter bounds, and one-observable uniqueness are not stated there. The highly relevant Stuchlík–Hledík article (Phys. Rev. D 60, 044006, 1999) was checked at publisher-abstract level only, not whole text. Its inaccessible material remains a priority risk; an equivalent earlier inverse statement cannot be categorically ruled out. The substantive contribution assessed is the complete physical inverse classification rather than a new forward law or a general isofrequency claim.

## Mathematical value: PASS

The two measured geodesic frequencies constitute a natural inverse problem in relativistic orbital dynamics. The classification identifies exactly which frequency pairs have physical positive-mass, positive-cosmological-constant explanations, proves that those data alone leave a continuously adjustable background, and gives simultaneous sharp ceilings on the mass, orbital radius and cosmological constant. The strict monotone mass–cosmological-constant tradeoff proves that *any one* extra independently measured scalar among those three restores uniqueness, and precisely characterizes the data values for which recovery is possible. This is a complete parameter-identification result, not a mere symbolic rewriting of one forward formula, although the algebraic derivation is short once that formula is available. The ideal coordinate-time model and the absence of noise or astronomical clock calibration limit its practical reach.
