For strictly stable circular geodesics in positive-mass, positive-\(\Lambda\) Kottler space, the entire attainable orbital/radial-frequency plane is \(0<\kappa<\Omega\), and each frequency pair determines an explicit one-parameter family of distinct backgrounds with sharp mass, radius and cosmological-constant ceilings. Throughout that family mass increases while \(\Lambda\) decreases, and knowing any one additional parameter—mass, radius, or \(\Lambda\)—restores global uniqueness.
