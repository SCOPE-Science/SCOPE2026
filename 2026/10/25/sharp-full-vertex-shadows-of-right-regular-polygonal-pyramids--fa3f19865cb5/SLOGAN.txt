For every right regular \(n\)-gonal pyramid with \(n\ge5\), the probability that a random orthographic shadow retains **all** \(n+1\) vertices is an explicit one-triangle solid angle and has exactly one optimal apex height. As \(n\to\infty\), that height approaches \(1/\sqrt2\) times the base circumradius and the maximal probability is asymptotic to \(2\pi^2/(3\sqrt3\,n^2)\).
