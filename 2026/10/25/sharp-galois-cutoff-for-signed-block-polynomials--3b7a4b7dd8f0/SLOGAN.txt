The signed-block polynomial \(\sum_{j=k}^{n-1}X^j-\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}X^j\) has full symmetric Galois group whenever \(n\ge8\) and \(\gcd(n,k)=1\), sharpening the recently proved \(n>8\) range to its exact threshold. Across all \(n\ge2\), the only exceptions are \((n,k)=(7,2),(7,5)\), whose Galois group is \(S_3\times S_3\).
