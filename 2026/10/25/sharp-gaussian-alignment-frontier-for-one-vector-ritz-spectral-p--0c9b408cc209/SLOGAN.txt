For a one-product Gaussian Hessian sketch and a rank-one Rayleigh-quotient spectral preconditioner, the exact chance of actually improving a one-spike system's condition number has a sharp covariance alignment scale \(\tau\asymp\kappa^{-3/2}\). A partly aligned sketch can instead inflate conditioning to order \(\kappa^2\), and in three or more dimensions it generically adds an exact-arithmetic CG step.
