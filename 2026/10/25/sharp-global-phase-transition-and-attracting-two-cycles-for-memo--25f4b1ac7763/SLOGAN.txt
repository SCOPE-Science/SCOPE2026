Memoryless embedded-circle Riemannian RMSProp converges globally exactly up to the normalized step \(\alpha=2\varepsilon\), with critical error \(|\theta_n|\sim\varepsilon/(2n)\) on nonterminating trajectories. Above that threshold a unique symmetric locally attracting two-cycle appears with linear amplitude onset \((\alpha-2\varepsilon)/2\) and tends to angular amplitude \(\pi/4\) at large steps.
