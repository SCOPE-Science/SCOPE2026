For every even \(m\ge4\), the optimum paired-dominating sets of the square rook graph \(K_m\square K_m\) are precisely two even-occupancy orientations, counted by \(2m![z^m](\cosh z)^m\). All single-vertex exchanges are frozen, double exchanges connect each orientation, and the sharp batch size connecting the entire optimum space is \(m/2\).
