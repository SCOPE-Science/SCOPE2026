For the nonstationary circle covariance \(P_r(x-y)[1+\varepsilon\cos(k(x+y))]\), positive semidefiniteness holds exactly for \(|\varepsilon|\le2r^k/(1+r^{2k})\) for every integer \(k\ge1\). At either sharp boundary, the only finite-set singularities come from one explicitly oriented regular \(2k\)-gon with alternating coefficients; all other distinct-site configurations remain strictly positive definite.
