For the classical Hilbert matrix from logarithmically weighted \(A^2\) to ordinary \(A^2\), the squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm has the exact critical blow-up \(\|\mathcal H\|_{\mathcal S_2}^2=(\alpha-2)^{-1}+O(1)\) as \(\alpha\downarrow2\). In particular its supercritical compactness strengthens to Hilbert–Schmidt membership for every \(\alpha>2\).
