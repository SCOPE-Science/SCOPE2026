For \(m\) harmonics driven by one common actuator over a full period, no partition with \(2m\) or fewer hold intervals can reach every target, while \(2m+1\) equally spaced holds suffice. At this minimal budget, the worst-target minimum-energy overhead is explicitly \(\operatorname{sinc}^{-2}(m\pi/(2m+1))<\pi^2/4\), with the constant sharp as \(m\to\infty\).
