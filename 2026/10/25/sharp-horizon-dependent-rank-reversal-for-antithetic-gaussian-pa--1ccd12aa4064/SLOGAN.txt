For equal-sign reflected Gaussian particle pairs in a one-dimensional heat solver, the variance-optimal matching switches from rank order to reverse rank at a sharp diffusion-horizon-dependent separation scale \(T_r=\sigma\sqrt{2r(r+1)\log(1+1/r)}\). For distant translated particle blocks, nearest-rank pairing can capture an exponentially smaller fraction of the available variance reduction than reverse pairing, even though it minimizes total matching distance.
