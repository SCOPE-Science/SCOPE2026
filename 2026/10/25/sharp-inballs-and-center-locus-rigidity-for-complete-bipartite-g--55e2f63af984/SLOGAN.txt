The centered graphical zonotope of every \(K_{p,q}\) has a sharp Euclidean inradius controlled by minimum-degree singleton cuts, except for the four-cycle, whose insphere is controlled by mixed cuts. The full maximum-ball center locus has dimension \(p-1\) for \(2\le p<q\), a unique center for \(p=q\ge3\), and an explicit exceptional segment for \(K_{2,2}\); the entire two-row center interval is given in closed form.
