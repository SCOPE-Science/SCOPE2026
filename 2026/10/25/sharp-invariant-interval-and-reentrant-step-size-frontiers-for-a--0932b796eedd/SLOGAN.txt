The homogeneous Allen–Cahn specialization of a doubly stabilized Crank–Nicolson method preserves \([-1,1]\) exactly when an explicit cubic in the step size is nonnegative. Its sharp parameter diagram has an unconditional zero-second-stabilization regime at \(s\ge4+2\sqrt2\), and a reentrant regime in which small and large steps are safe but intermediate steps violate the bound.
