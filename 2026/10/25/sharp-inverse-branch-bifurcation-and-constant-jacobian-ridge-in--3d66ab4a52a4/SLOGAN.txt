The risk-sensitive two-variable multiplicative-weights map has an exact global inverse-branch transition at \(a(1-ckp_1/(1-k))=4\): below it the map is one-to-one, whereas above it an explicit caustic band admits three distinct physical preimages and opens with width proportional to the \(3/2\) power of the threshold excess. At the unique memory rate \(k=1/(1+cp_1)\), the same map has constant Jacobian \(1-k\) in log-odds coordinates, fixing the Lyapunov-exponent sum of every compact interior invariant state.
