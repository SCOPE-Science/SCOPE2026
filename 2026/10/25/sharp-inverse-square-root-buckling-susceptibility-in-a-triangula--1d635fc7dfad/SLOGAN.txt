The pressure-softened two-dimensional triangular active crystal has an exact inverse-square-root divergence of nearest-neighbor displacement variance, with coefficient \(2\sqrt{2/3}\,(\Gamma^2/2+\nu^2/\mu)/U''(\ell)\). The critical spectral weight is distributed equally over the two shear-mode lines transverse to the measured bond, while the third softened line is canceled by the bond observable.
