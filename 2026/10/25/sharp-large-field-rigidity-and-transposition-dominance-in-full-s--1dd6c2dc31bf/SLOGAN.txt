Among fixed-dimensional full-support perfect evolution algebras over growing finite fields, the probability of nontrivial symmetry is \(\binom n2(q-1)^{3-2n}(1+O_n(q^{-1}))\) for every fixed \(n\ge5\). Given any symmetry, with probability tending to one the entire automorphism group is generated by one basis-line transposition, even though the support graph has every possible edge.
