In the smoothness-one Chebyshev-periodized Korobov lattice rule, optimal cubature weights are positive on every even \(N\)-point grid exactly up to an explicit \(\gamma_N=N+\pi^2/3+O(N^{-1})\). Beyond it, nonnegative polynomial data can yield negative quadrature, even while the rule's computed integral of the positive constant stays positive until order \(N^4\).
