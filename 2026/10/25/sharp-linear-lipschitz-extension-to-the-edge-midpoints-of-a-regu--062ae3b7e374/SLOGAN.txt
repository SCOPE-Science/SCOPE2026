For the vertices of a unit regular Euclidean simplex, simultaneous real-linear Lipschitz extension to **every** edge midpoint has exact optimal norm \(8-4\sqrt3\) in the triangular case and \(2\sqrt2(n-1)/(n\sqrt2+n-2)\) with \(n\ge4\) vertices. A strictly positive symmetric averaging rule attains these bounds, which increase to \(4-2\sqrt2\), and improves on ordinary arithmetic midpoint interpolation in every dimension.
