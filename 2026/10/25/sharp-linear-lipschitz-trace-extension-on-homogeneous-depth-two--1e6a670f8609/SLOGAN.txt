For the homogeneous unit-edge depth-two tree with \(m\ge2\) branches and \(k\ge2\) leaves per branch, the least real-linear Lipschitz extension norm from leaves to all vertices is exactly \(4(2mk-k-1)/(m(3k+1))\), attained by a positive permutation-symmetric rule. It first exceeds \(2\) when \((m-2)(k-1)>4\) and tends sharply to the unattained supremum \(8/3\).
