# Review

## Correctness

The perimeter integral is smooth near equal unit semiaxes. Its first derivatives are both \(\pi\), so the three measured length errors linearize to \(\pi(\epsilon_i+\epsilon_j)\). The inverse of the pair-sum map has exact \(\ell_\infty\)-operator norm \(3/2\). The direction \((3/2,-1/2,-1/2)\) attains that norm, proving sharpness of \(3/(2\pi)\).
