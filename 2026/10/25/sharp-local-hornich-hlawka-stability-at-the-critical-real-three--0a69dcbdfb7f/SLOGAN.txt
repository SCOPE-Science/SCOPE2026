At the unresolved critical exponent \(p_*=\log3/\log(3/2)\), the standard real three-coordinate Hornich--Hlawka equality witness has exactly one flat mode (scaling) and a sharp positive transverse quadratic deficit with coefficient \((p_*-1)(1-\sqrt6/3)/2\). Its nearby normalized minimum remains strictly positive as \(p\uparrow p_*\), vanishing linearly at rate \(6\log3/p_*^2\); no global endpoint inequality is claimed.
