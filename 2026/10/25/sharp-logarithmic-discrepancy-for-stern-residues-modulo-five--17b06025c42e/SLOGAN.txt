The signed discrepancy between Stern-sequence residues \(1\) and \(4\) modulo five has exact dyadic extremal recurrences \(M_{r+12}=M_r+4\) and \(m_{r+12}=m_r-4\) for every \(r\geq8\). Its sharp oscillations are logarithmic, with normalized limit superior \(1/3\) and limit inferior \(-1/3\), in contrast to the bounded modulus-three discrepancy.
