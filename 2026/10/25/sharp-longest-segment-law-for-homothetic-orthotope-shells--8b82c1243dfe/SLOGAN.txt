The maximum Euclidean length of a straight segment lying entirely in a centered homothetic orthotope shell is exactly \(\sqrt{\sum_i s_i^2-(1-r^2)\min_i s_i^2}\), where \(r=a/(1-a)\) and \(a\) is the relative boundary thickness. Every maximizer traverses all but one coordinate side completely, leaving precisely the shortest side only fractionally traversed; this extends a known planar square-annulus benchmark to every dimension and unequal side lengths.
