Accessible research-record searches and earlier published findings showed no equivalent claim. Possible toric-scroll reformulations remain a historical-priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result supplies a natural complete two-parameter classification of magic versus ordinary Ehrhart positivity inside the smooth three-dimensional Cayley box family. It pinpoints a strict intermediate phase, infinitely many exact magic-boundary integer pairs, and sharp minimal normalized volumes 97 and 141 for the two distinct failures. This resolves more than a one-off sign example without claiming a general theorem for all smooth polytopes.

## Scientific limitations
The construction has two rectangles whose side lengths are interchanged; it does not classify general Cayley sums. The prior exact Cayley formula is expressly acknowledged. Priority remains provisional under differing terminology. Finite checks corroborate but do not prove the quantified theorem.
