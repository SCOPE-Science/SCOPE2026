For the smooth eight-vertex Cayley polytope between the rectangles with swapped side lengths \(a,b\), magic positivity holds exactly when \((a-b)^2\) is at most \(6(a+b-2)\), while Ehrhart positivity has the distinct strict bound \(6(a+b+1)\). The first normalized volumes at which these two positivity properties fail are respectively \(97\) and \(141\), and every magic-boundary integer pair is classified.
