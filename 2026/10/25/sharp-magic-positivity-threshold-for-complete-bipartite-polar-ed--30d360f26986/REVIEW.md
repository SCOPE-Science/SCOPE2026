# Review of the exact magic-positivity classification

## Correctness

**PASS.** Graph-potential coordinates give \(2n+1-|t|\) independent integer choices on each of the \(m\) vertices for every \(-2n\le t\le2n\). The binomial/Faulhaber coefficient formula and the magic-basis transform are algebraic identities. An exact finite check covers all parameters \(m\le19\), while a single negative ordinary coefficient proves failure at every larger parameter. The stored checker independently interpolates the same polynomial from counts and checks the boundary conventions. The infinite step does not rely on finite extrapolation.

## Originality

**PASS under inspected sources, with residual priority risk.** Konoike already reports the sign table through \(m=11\), and Liu–Tsuchiya already derive the analogous Bernoulli pattern for star-poset order polytopes; these are explicitly acknowledged. The new scope is the full infinite classification of **magic positivity together with its distinct ordinary positivity threshold for reflexive complete-bipartite polar edge polytopes**, including the previously untabulated intermediate range and a uniform infinite-tail proof. The 2026 Ohsugi–Tsuchiya positivity theorem treats distinct classes and cycle polars rather than the present family. Equivalent formulas or a complete classification under an alternative presentation remain possible but were not identified in the inspected primary sources.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The object is a canonical reflexive lattice-polytope family singled out by existing finite magic-positivity data. The result resolves its natural all-parameter threshold question exactly, proves the sharp eleven-parameter gap between magic and ordinary Ehrhart positivity, and locates the structural Bernoulli obstruction for all large dimensions. The finite table alone is not presented as the whole contribution.

## Boundaries

This does not classify arbitrary complete-bipartite graphs or prove anything about real-rootedness in the nonmagic cases. Historical priority is not an independent literature certification; independent audit has not occurred.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
