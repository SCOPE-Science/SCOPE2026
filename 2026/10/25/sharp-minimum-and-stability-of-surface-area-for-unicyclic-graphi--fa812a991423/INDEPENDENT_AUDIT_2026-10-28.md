## correctness

Every independent (n-2)-subset of root incidence vectors corresponds to a two-component spanning forest with Gram determinant s(n-s). For unicyclic connected n-vertex G with c-cycle, selecting n-2 edges by removing either a bridge and cycle edge (c such sets per bridge), or two cycle edges (interval components A_{i,k}); deleting two bridges leaves a cycle and contributes zero. Therefore S(G)=2c sum_bridge sqrt(s_e(n-s_e))+sum_{i,k}sqrt(A_{i,k}(n-A_{i,k})). Each bridge s_e between 1 and n-c has sqrt(s(n-s))>=sqrt(n-1), equality only s=1, forcing all off-cycle vertices direct leaves. The strictly concave sum of cyclic interval terms reaches its minimum at a vertex of the simplex a_i>=1,sum=n, i.e. all leaves at one cycle vertex, proving the global bound and rigidity. Inside direct-leaf family, Jensen gives sharp balanced upper bound when c|n. Because f_double_prime<=-2/n, strong concavity yields the exact quadratic stability deficit. Independent all-integer direct-leaf allocations for (n,c)=(6,3),(8,4),(9,3) confirm extrema and deficit; infinite proof is symbolic.
