# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact identity follows by classifying independent rank-\(n-2\) subsets of \(n\) edge vectors and evaluating forest Gram determinants via the tree Laplacian matrix-tree identity. Two-edge deletions are either one bridge plus one cycle edge, or two cycle edges; two bridges retain the cycle and cannot contribute. All factors of two are controlled by the Steiner surface-area normalization. The bridge inequality is sharp at subtree size one, and strict concavity on the affine allocation simplex forces cycle weight into a single root for the global minimum. Jensen and the globally valid curvature \(f''\le-2/n\) give the restricted maximum and explicit stability. Independent polytope-hull calculations, exhaustive small-graph checks, and exact symbolic identities stress-test the normalization; finite tests do not establish generality, which follows analytically.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Joós–Lángi and Brazitikos–McIntyre supply the general intrinsic-volume generator formula, whose unicyclic two-forest expansion is prior-implied and is not claimed as original. Padrol–Pilaud–Poullot treat graphical zonotopes via deformation and face combinatorics. The nearest identified tree graphical-zonotope results concern acyclic trees or width variance, while the nearest cycle result concerns one-skeleton Laplacians or all-rank **pure cycle** volume, rather than fixed-length unicyclic attachments. None of the actually inspected full text states the global sharp minimum, leaf-family maximization or quantitative stability for fixed \(n,c\). Residual risk is an equivalent graph-cut or forest-moment extremal theorem in less indexed literature. Absence in searches alone is not evidence of uniqueness.

## Value — PASS

The equal-volume, same-graphic-matroid infinite family isolates Euclidean surface-area variation from combinatorics and volume. Sharp tree-attachment minimization, a rigid concentration-versus-balance reversal among leaf-only competitors, and an explicit stability penalty describe the geometric effect of where tree mass is placed along a cycle. Unlike arbitrary finite enumeration, the theorem is uniform in all cycle lengths and numbers of leaves and locates natural equality classes.

## Limitations and closest literature

The maximal-area classification is only among directly attached leaves and exact when \(c\mid n\). It is not a global unicyclic maximum. The generator-sum formula, forest Gram determinant, and concavity principles are standard tools. Closest inspected sources: arXiv:2111.12422 and arXiv:2206.03204; arXiv:2102.05900 is explicitly credited as the earlier general formula. Independent verification remains outstanding. An unexpected broader extremal covering from graph cut energies is a bibliographic risk.
