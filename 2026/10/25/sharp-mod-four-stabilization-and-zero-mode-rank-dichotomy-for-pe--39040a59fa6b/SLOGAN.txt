For every even-length binary Rule 90 ring followed by **any** fixed permutation of its sites, the one-step periodic core exists for all wirings precisely when the ring length is \(2\pmod4\), where it contains exactly \(2^{N-2}\) states and every other state enters in one step. At lengths divisible by four, an even-to-even wiring parity instead forces exactly one or two nontrivial zero-eigenvalue Jordan chains, giving a sharp formula for the second image size.
