On an exactly rank-one positive-semidefinite rotational matrix flow, a one-column Gaussian Nyström compression of a Runge–Kutta endpoint has an infinite-mean one-step error exactly when the endpoint stability polynomial leaves the imaginary-axis unit disk. The error's sharp inverse-linear tail has coefficient \(2\sqrt{-\det Z}/\pi\), with explicit RK3/RK4 step thresholds and a period-four small-step law across Taylor orders; independent left/right sketches remain infinite-mean at every nonsingular endpoint.
