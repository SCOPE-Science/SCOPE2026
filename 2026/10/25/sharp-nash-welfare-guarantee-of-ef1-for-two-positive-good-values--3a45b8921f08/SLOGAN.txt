For identical additive goods with values \(a\) and \(2a\), every EF1 allocation preserves at least \((3/4)^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor/n}\) of optimal Nash social welfare when there are at least \(n\) goods and \(n\ge2\) agents. This factor is attained for every agent count by a construction with \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\) low-utility and high-utility pairs of agents.
