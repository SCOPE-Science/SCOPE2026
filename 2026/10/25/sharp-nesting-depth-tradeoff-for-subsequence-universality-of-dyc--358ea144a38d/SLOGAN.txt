For binary Dyck words of nesting height at most \(h\ge2\), the largest possible semilength at fixed scattered-subsequence universality index \(k\) is exactly \(h(k+1)/2\) for odd \(k\), and \(hk/2+1\) for even \(k\). Repeated full-height mountains, with at most one final short mountain, attain every boundary, giving an exact nesting-depth versus universality tradeoff.
