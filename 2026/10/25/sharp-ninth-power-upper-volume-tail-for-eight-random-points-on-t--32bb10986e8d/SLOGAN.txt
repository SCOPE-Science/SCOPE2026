Eight independent uniform points on the unit three-sphere generate a four-polytope whose volume lies within \(\varepsilon\) of the sharp maximum \(2/3\) with probability asymptotic to \((2916/\pi^3)\varepsilon^9\). The ninth power is the half-codimension of the \(105\) labelled maximizing rotation orbits, and its exact coefficient comes from their transverse volume Hessian.
