For every integer slant order \(r\ge2\) and distinct nonnegative monomial degrees, the corresponding Bergman slant Toeplitz commutator has norm equal to its essential norm: exactly \(r\sqrt2\) when the degree difference is not divisible by \(r^2\), and exactly \(2r\) otherwise. The essential spectrum of its positive adjoint square changes from a singleton to an interval, and the commutator never attains its norm.
