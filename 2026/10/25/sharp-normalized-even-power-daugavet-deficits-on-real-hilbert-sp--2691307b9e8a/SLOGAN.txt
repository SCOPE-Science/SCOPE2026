On real Hilbert spaces, norm-one rank-one even powers have a sharp normalized Daugavet deficit: for squares its maximum is \(1-2/\sqrt5\) with exact quadratic overlap stability. For growing even degree \(m\), the worst deficit decays as \(W(1/(2e))/m\), and all optimizing overlaps approach one at the precise rate \((1+W(1/(2e)))/m\).
