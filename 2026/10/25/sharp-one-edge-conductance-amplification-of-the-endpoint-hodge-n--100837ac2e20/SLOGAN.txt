On the dense bipartite network \(K_{r,r}\), tuning just one edge conductance makes the canonical cut and cycle projections grow from uniformly bounded endpoint norms to a sharp common maximum \(\frac12(1+\sqrt{1+9(r-1)^2/(2r-1)})\sim\frac3{2\sqrt2}\sqrt r\). For every \(r\ge29\), the two maxima occur at distinct explicitly determined conductances, related by an exact product identity.
