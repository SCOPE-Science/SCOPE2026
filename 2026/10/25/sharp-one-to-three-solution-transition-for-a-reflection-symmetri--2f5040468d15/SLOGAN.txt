For the five equally spaced pentagonal normals and cone volumes \((t,t,1,1,t)\), the complete fiber has one convex pentagon for \(0<t\le t_*\) and exactly three for \(t>t_*\), where \(t_*=3.085259816987\ldots\) is an explicitly defined algebraic threshold. The extra two polygons form a reflection pair and admit an exact cubic-parametrized classification.
