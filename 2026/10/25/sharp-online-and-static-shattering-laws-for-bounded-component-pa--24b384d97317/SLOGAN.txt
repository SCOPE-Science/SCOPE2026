For finite strict partial orders with comparability components bounded by \(b\), static shattering is exactly the class of transitively reduced acyclic query digraphs with components of order at most \(b\), yielding an exact VC-dimension formula for every \(b\). For \(b\le4\) the Littlestone dimension also has a closed formula: static and online shattering agree for \(b\le2\), but at \(b=3\) the gap is exactly \(\lfloor n/3\rfloor\), already unbounded with three-vertex components.
