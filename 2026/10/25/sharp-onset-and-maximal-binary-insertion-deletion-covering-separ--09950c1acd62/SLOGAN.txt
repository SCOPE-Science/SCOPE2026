For every radius \(R\geq1\), binary insertion covering first fails to imply deletion covering at the sharp blocklength \(n=R+4\). At that boundary the only inclusion-maximal separating codes are the two families avoiding scattered \(0110\) or \(1001\), each of size \(\sum_{j=0}^{3}\binom{R+4}{j}\); the first family's existence was previously known, while the threshold and exhaustive maximality are established here.
