Binary synchronous majority on two cliques connected by one edge stabilizes in exactly \(2\), \(4\), or \(5\) updates in the worst case, according as the clique sizes are both odd, both even, or of opposite parity. The critical five-update states have the exact binomial count \(\binom{a-1}{(a-1)/2}\binom{b}{b/2}\) for odd \(a\) and even \(b\), and primitive two-cycles exist only when both cliques are even.
