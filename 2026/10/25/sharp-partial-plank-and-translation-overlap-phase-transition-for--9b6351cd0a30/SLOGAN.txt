For the square of side two, the largest area covered by any finite family of planks of total width \(W<2\) is exactly \(2\sqrt2W-W^2/2\) up to \(W=\sqrt2\), then \(2+W^2/2\); one centered plank always suffices. The unique optimal normal orbit switches from diagonal to a continuously rotating oblique direction at \(W=\sqrt2\), equivalently describing the sharp **minimum** fixed-distance square covariogram and its optimizers.
