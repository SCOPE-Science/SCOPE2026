The exact two-step mass variance of scalar cubic stochastic Schrödinger splitting has a sharp frequency-phase envelope: even optimally rotated real Gaussian kicks retain strictly greater variance than equal-power isotropic complex kicks whenever the nonlinear phase strength is positive. With zero linear phase, the variance has a unique minimum at \(\beta=\tfrac14\tan(2\pi/5)\), despite the expected mass being independent of that phase.
