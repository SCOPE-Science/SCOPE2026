The normalized damped oscillatory radial kernel \(e^{-\alpha r}(\cos(\omega r)+\beta\sin(\omega r))\) is positive definite on \(\mathbb R^d\) exactly when \(\phi=\arctan(\omega/\alpha)\le\pi/(d+1)\) and \(-\cot(d\phi)\le\beta\le\cot\phi\). This sharp phase–frequency cone extends the familiar damped-cosine slice and permits an oscillating kernel in three dimensions at a frequency where the pure cosine is inadmissible.
