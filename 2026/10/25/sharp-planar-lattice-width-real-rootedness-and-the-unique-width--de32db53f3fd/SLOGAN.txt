Every planar lattice polygon of width at least four has two distinct negative real Ehrhart \(h^*\)-roots, and four is the sharp integer cutoff. Already at width three the only obstruction, up to affine-unimodular equivalence, is the primitive triangle \(\operatorname{conv}\{(0,0),(1,0),(3,7)\}\), whose Ehrhart numerator is \(1+3t+3t^2\); no width-three polygon has a double root.
