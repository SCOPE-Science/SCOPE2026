# Review of sharp regular-pentagon midpoint extension constant

## Correctness — PASS

The exact domain is the five vertices of a side-one Euclidean regular pentagon and the target adds precisely the five Euclidean edge midpoints. Every bounded linear extension preserves constants. Averaging an arbitrary operator over the dihedral group gives a three-orbit midpoint row, without imposing positivity. Three explicit distance-function inequalities and the identity \(2H_0+2H_1+H_2=2(\varphi+1)\) prove the universal lower bound. A positive three-orbit row attains all vertex-to-midpoint bounds; two explicit couplings give **strict** bounds for both midpoint-pair orbits. The sign-critical inequalities admit certified rational interval proofs in the accompanying standalone checker. The proof is not an extrapolation from finite sampling.
