All five edge midpoints of a regular Euclidean pentagon admit one positive linear Lipschitz extension with the **sharp unrestricted norm** \(2(\varphi+1)/(1+2u+w)=1.0397164477425\ldots\), where \(\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\), \(u=\sqrt{2\varphi+3}/2\), and \(w=\sqrt{4\varphi+3}/2\). A three-orbit distance-function certificate forces this value; two explicit transportation couplings show that simultaneously adding every midpoint costs no more than adding one.
