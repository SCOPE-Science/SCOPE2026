For every subquadratic cosine-brightness power \(0<p<2\), the sharp worst-relative-error positive two-view scheme uses equally weighted perpendicular directions, with error \(\tfrac12(c_p-\tfrac12)\) and quantitative weight/angle rigidity. The exponent-one specialization was previously established by an all-nodes Cauchy quadrature theorem; the new range is \(p\ne1\).
