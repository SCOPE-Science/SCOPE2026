For every \(0<p<1\), the sharp first-Taylor-coefficient bound for unit \(H^p\) functions with a prescribed interior zero \(a\) is an explicit strictly increasing function \(\mathcal C_{2/p}(|a|)\), determined by a one-variable quadratic; every extremizer has exactly the prescribed Blaschke factor and a completely specified linear outer root. Equivalently, fixing the modulus of the inner factor at zero gives an exact continuous coefficient-loss profile, including both endpoint asymptotics.
