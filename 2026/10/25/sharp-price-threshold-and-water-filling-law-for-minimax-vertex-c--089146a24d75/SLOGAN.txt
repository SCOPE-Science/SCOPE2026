For noisy consensus on a star with arbitrary positive edge prices, the unique conductance allocation minimizing the worst vertex's steady-state disagreement variance has an exact clipped-square-root resistance profile, with its active set determined by one scalar threshold. Equal conductances remain optimal precisely when every price is at least the total price divided by \(m^2+m-1\), and even under extreme price heterogeneity their worst-vertex variance is at most \((m^2+m-1)/m^2\) times optimal.
