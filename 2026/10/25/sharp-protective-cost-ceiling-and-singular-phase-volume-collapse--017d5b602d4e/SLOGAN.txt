In a quorum-protection public-goods game, the maximum risk-weighted pivotal benefit gives an exact protective-cost threshold above which protection disappears under replicator dynamics whenever ordinary cooperation is initially present. Just below that threshold, the population-composition area favoring protection scales as \(\varepsilon^{3/2}\) at every fixed positive risk-survival parameter, but as \(\varepsilon^{1/2+1/b}\) at the complete-risk endpoint, where \(b\) is the number of nonprotective partners on the pivotal event.
