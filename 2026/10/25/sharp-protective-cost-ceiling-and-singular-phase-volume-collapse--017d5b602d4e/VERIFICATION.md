# Verification and boundaries

The self-contained `verify.py` evaluates the focal-player pivotality event by exact rational multinomial summation and independently evaluates the simplified closed polynomial. All finite rational comparisons check nonnegativity and the claimed global upper bound against the exact rational candidate maximum. It also integrates one-dimensional slices of the strict-advantage region using bisection and composite Simpson quadrature and compares small-parameter areas with the theorem's constants.

The general uniqueness of the maximum is established by strictly decreasing dependence on the ordinary-cooperator fraction and the unique maximum of a binomial-type boundary product. The equality case of extinction requires the compactness/interval argument in RESULT.md rather than a strict uniform gap. Both area limit exponents are proved by local Taylor expansions, uniqueness of the maximum, and scaled domain convergence. The complete-risk endpoint is a limiting model extension; asymptotics for fixed positive survival parameter are not uniform down to zero.

These program checks validate instances only. The result has not been independently audited or certified, and historical novelty is not guaranteed.
