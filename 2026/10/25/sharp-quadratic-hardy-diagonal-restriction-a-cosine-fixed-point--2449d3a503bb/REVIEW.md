## correctness

PASS

The bidisk homogeneous quadratic F=az^2+bzw+cw^2 has H^p norm equal to the circle L^p norm of a degree-two analytic polynomial P, and its diagonal A^p norm is (p+1)^(-1/p)|P(1)| under normalized area. Complex conjugation and reciprocal reversal preserve the constraint P(1)=1; convex averaging reduces the full complex problem to Q_lambda with real lambda, not a presumed symmetric subfamily. Strict convexity for p>1 and the continuous sign-change distribution at p=1 put a unique optimum in (1/2,1). The L1 trigonometric integral reduces the stationary equation to cos(tau)=tau, giving K1=pi/(4 tau). At p=2 Phi2=1-2lambda+3lambda^2/2, optimum lambda=2/3, K2=1. Fourier Abel regularization of log|1+2 cos t| yields the exact derivative coefficient 7/12-(log 3)/2. Independently recomputed tau=0.7390851332151606, K1=1.0626626461566304 and p=1.99 near-endpoint slope consistent. These checks corroborate only their finite/numerical scope; the analytic reductions establish the quantifiers.

## originality

PASS

The degree-two homogeneous block is equivalent by a unimodular factor to sharp Lp point evaluation for trigonometric frequencies {-1,0,1}; that equivalence and the general Nikolskii extremal framework are prior mathematics. Brevig et al. establish the unrestricted contractivity at p>=2 and a p<2 linear obstruction; their explicitly stated Corollary 4.1 treats point evaluation p>2, not the submitted evaluated p=1 fixed point or sharp derivative at p=2. No inspected Resultary record supplies the same evaluated degree-one L1 extremal and slope. Earlier Geronimus/Ziegler-type explicit degree-one constants could nevertheless imply them, so priority is qualified rather than certified.
