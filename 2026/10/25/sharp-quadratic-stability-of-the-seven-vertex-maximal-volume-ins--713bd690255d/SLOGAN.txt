The seven-vertex maximum-volume polytope on \(S^3\)—an orthogonal free sum of a regular triangle and two diameters—has an exactly computable \(21\)-dimensional constrained volume-Hessian spectrum, with six and only six rotational zero modes. Its best local quadratic volume-deficit coefficient, measured by squared total Euclidean vertex displacement modulo rotations, is \(\sqrt3/16\).
