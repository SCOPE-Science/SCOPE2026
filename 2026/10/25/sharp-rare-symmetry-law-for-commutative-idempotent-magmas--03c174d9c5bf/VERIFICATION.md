# Mathematical verification

The standalone standard-library Python program `verify_ci_magma_symmetry.py` enumerates every labeled commutative-idempotent operation table for orders \(3\) and \(4\) and tests each possible permutation as an automorphism. It obtains \(9\) nonrigid tables of \(27\) at order \(3\), and \(400\) of \(4096\) at order \(4\). Full automorphism groups generated by exactly one transposition occur in \(6\) and \(336\) cases. Independent orbit partitioning gives \(7\) and \(192\) isomorphism classes, of which \(4\) and \(38\) are nonrigid. The checker additionally verifies permutation fixed-table orbit counts and the support-deficit inequality for every permutation of degree at most \(7\); it evaluates the leading-term convergence at larger orders without enumerating their tables.

Run `python verify_ci_magma_symmetry.py`; its generated text is in `verify_ci_magma_symmetry.out` and ends in `ALL_CI_MAGMA_SYMMETRY_CHECKS_PASSED`. The argument for every \(n\ge3\), the explicit error for \(n\ge20\), and the conditional asymptotic follow analytically from orbit counting, the support-deficit bound, and a union bound; the exhaustive finite checks cannot replace that proof. Independent mathematical validation has not been performed.
