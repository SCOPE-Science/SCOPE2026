Each positive integer profile of this form is Eulerian on a connected three-vertex directed graph, hence has an actual cyclic realization. ## Mathematical value — PASS
The strict-rank observation is natural for frequency-order DNA storage, while excluding homopolymers is a standard synthesis constraint. Beyond the previously deducible feasible-message count, the result gives a sharp per-rank realization-length classification and the optimal worst-case length for representing every permitted message. The sharp length \(27\) and detailed distribution distinguish this classification from generic polynomial upper bounds, simple feasibility examples and profile-count formulas. The scope is deliberately limited to the first nonbinary three-symbol case and to cyclic read-length-two rankings.
