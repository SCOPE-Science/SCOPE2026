The 60 feasible strict orders of six positive, symbol-changing frequencies in circular homopolymer-free ternary words have an exact, sharp minimum-length spectrum: \(21,22,26,27\) with multiplicities \(24,12,12,12\). In particular, twelve rankings need at least \(27\) cyclic positions, and all sixty have realizations by that length; the count sixty itself is already implied by prior unrestricted rank results.
