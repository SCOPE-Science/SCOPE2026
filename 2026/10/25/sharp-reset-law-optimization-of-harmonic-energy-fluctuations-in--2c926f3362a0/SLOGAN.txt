For a logarithmic-harmonic diffusion with Poisson resetting, the unique reset law minimizing fluctuations of the **harmonic energy contribution** \(\beta X^2/2\) is deterministic exactly when \(\alpha>D\) and \(r<\beta(\alpha-D)/D\). Above this sharp rate threshold, the optimal site escapes to the excluded origin; an exact completed-square identity penalizes both resetting away from the optimum and randomness in reset sites.
