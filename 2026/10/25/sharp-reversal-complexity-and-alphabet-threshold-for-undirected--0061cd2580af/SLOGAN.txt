Reversal of every complete deterministic undirected automaton is again recognizable by a complete deterministic undirected automaton: with \(n\) states and \(m\) final states its exact reverse complexity is the orbit size of the final set, at most \(\binom{n}{m}\). The sharp central-binomial worst case needs exactly three involutive letters for every \(n\ge4\), but two letters at \(n=3\) and one at \(n=2\).
