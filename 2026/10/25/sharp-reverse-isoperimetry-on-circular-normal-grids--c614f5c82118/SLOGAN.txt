Fix the perimeter and restrict a convex polygon's facet normals to an equally spaced grid: an odd number \(N\) of directions forces an exact positive area minimum, attained only by the \(N\) isosceles triangles with angles \(\pi/N,\pi/N,\pi-2\pi/N\). Even grids admit polygons of arbitrarily small positive area; the regular \(N\)-gon is the unique maximal-area shape, and every intermediate area occurs.
