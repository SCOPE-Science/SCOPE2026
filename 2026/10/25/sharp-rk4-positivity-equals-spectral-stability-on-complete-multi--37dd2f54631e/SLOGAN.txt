For every unweighted complete multipartite diffusion graph with \(N\) vertices, the classical fourth-order Runge–Kutta endpoint is nonnegative if and only if it is spectrally contractive, exactly when \(h\le\rho/N\) for the universal constant \(\rho=2.78529356\ldots\). At the sharp boundary all transitions between distinct vertex parts vanish simultaneously while every diagonal and within-part transition remains positive; beyond it every cross-part transition turns negative.
