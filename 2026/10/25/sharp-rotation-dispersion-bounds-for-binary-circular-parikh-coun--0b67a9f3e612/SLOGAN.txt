For binary circular words with \(a\) zeros and \(b\) ones, the rotation-to-rotation variance in scattered \(01\) counts lies sharply between \(\gcd(a,b)^2(((a+b)/\gcd(a,b))^2-1)/12\) and \(ab(ab+2)/12\). Exactly the circularly balanced (Christoffel-power) words minimize dispersion, whereas single-block words maximize it, even though every circular word has the same mean \(ab/2\).
