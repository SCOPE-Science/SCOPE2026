For every \(n\ge3\), the second-largest convex-hull area of an edge-connected array of \(n\) unit-area regular hexagons lies exactly \(1/6\) below the maximum when \(n\equiv0,1\pmod3\), but exactly \(1/3\) below it when \(n\equiv2\pmod3\). The residue-dependent forbidden gap is sharp and has explicit three-run polyhex witnesses.
