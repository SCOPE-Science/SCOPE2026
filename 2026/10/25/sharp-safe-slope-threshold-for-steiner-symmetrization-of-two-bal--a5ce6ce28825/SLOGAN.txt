The spherical-cap curvature matrix \(\sqrt{1+\|p\|^2}(I+pp^{\mathsf T})\) is Loewner convex exactly up to the sharp slope radius \(\sqrt{2+\sqrt6}\). Consequently every smooth mixed fiber of a Steiner-symmetrized two-unit-ball lens retains all principal curvatures at least one when both active cap slopes obey that bound, and arbitrarily close counterexamples occur beyond it.
