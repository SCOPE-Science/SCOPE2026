Near a Euclidean ball in every dimension \(d\ge3\), the best common sampling law for random-polytope approximations of any collection of intrinsic volumes has a sharp quadratic worst-error penalty determined only by the two extreme intrinsic-volume indices and the variance of \((\Delta_S+d-1)f\). Its exact spherical-harmonic spectrum has only dilation and translation zero modes, and degree-two perturbations attain the optimal stability constant.
