For the unary residue language \(L_{p,r}=\{a^n:n\equiv r\pmod p\}\), the exact minimum number of states in a loop-free bipartite-cactus NFA is \(\operatorname{lcm}(p,2)\). Compared with the unrestricted epsilon-free NFA minimum \(p\), the graph constraint doubles the state count exactly for odd \(p\) and costs nothing for even \(p\).
