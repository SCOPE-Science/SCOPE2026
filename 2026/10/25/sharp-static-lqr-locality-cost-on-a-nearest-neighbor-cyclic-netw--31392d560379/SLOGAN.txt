For a homogeneous discrete-time cyclic LQR with nearest-neighbor state-cost coupling, the best asymptotic excess cost among all stable radius-\(r\) static feedback matrices is \(\Theta(r^{-3}4^{-r})\). An exact Riccati performance-difference lower bound and a square-root singularity of the centralized feedback Fourier symbol jointly determine the sharp exponential base and cubic correction.
