For every nonparallelogram convex quadrilateral, the area of the four-side-tangent inellipse at a uniformly chosen center on its diagonal-midpoint segment, normalized by the largest such area, is stochastically bounded above by the universal trapezoid law \(\Pr(B>u)=\sqrt{1-u^2}\). Equality at even one nontrivial threshold characterizes trapezoids; all other quadrilaterals satisfy strict inequality at every threshold.
