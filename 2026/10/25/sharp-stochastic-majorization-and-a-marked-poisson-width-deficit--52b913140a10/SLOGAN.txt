For complete multipartite symmetric edge polytopes with at least three parts, the Gaussian and spherical width deficits relative to the complete root polytope increase stochastically as part sizes become more unequal, giving unique extremal part profiles. For fixed positive proportions \(\alpha_c\), the rescaled deficit has survival \(\sum_c\alpha_c^2/[\alpha_c+(1-\alpha_c)e^t]^2\) and mean \(-1-\sum_c\log(1-\alpha_c)\).
