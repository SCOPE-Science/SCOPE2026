For a finite one-spike diagonal kernel, residual-diagonal pivoted Cholesky and fixed-size determinantal volume sampling have an exact, target-dependent quadrature-risk crossover: the sign is determined by \(Lg_0^2-\sum_{i=1}^m g_i^2/m\). On the dominant-coordinate target, the volume-sampling expected squared error stays positive while the pivoted-Cholesky error vanishes, with a sharp ratio growing as \(L^{k-1}\); neither sampler dominates for every target.
