The exact stationary variance is obtained from the complete absolutely summable AR(1) covariance double series, and the analytic positive second derivative proves the global optimizer and all boundary cases. The two-mode sign reversal follows from the exact midpoint multipliers and input gains. The finite-horizon order bound uses a uniform Toeplitz covariance estimate for correlations bounded away from ±1. The packaged standard-library verifier passed 1,080 supporting checks; numerical evidence is not used as a universal proof.

**Originality — PASS, with priority uncertainty.** Altmann–Moradi's source construction is Gaussian and independent in time and their midpoint equation separately shows the residual injection. Conrad et al. analyze independent probabilistic solver perturbations. Classical stationary linear-system spectra give the basic covariance tool, not the optimized constrained-midpoint color criterion and injection-side reversal as inspected. The closest recovered mathematical records concern different covariance targets or different numerical methods. Specialized work in linear filtering or colored stochastic integration could contain an equivalent, and two older plausible papers were inspected at abstract level only.

**Value — PASS.** The theorem settles a natural numerical-design question under fixed per-step marginal uncertainties. Its dimension-general convex law, exact sharp spectral balance condition, and concrete positive-versus-negative optimal temporal coloring for two fully specified injection choices provide an informative boundary rather than a generic covariance calculation. The second-order accuracy estimate shows that nondegenerate fixed coloring does not automatically sacrifice deterministic midpoint convergence order. This is not an assertion that minimizing numerical uncertainty yields statistically calibrated uncertainty estimates.

**Limitations.** Real finite-dimensional homogeneous, symmetric, kernel-invariant dynamics; stationary Gaussian AR(1) errors diagonal in the dynamic eigenbasis; fixed steps; root-mean-square finite-horizon order uniform only away from singular correlation. The two injection choices do not have equal output marginals. Published priority has not been independently certified.
