For linear constrained midpoint integration with stationary AR(1) numerical perturbations of fixed one-step marginals, the long-time mean-square spread is a strictly convex function of the temporal correlation, with a sharp spectral condition for a unique interior optimum. On a two-mode constrained example, placing isotropic noise after the midpoint solve makes positive correlation optimal, while placing isotropic noise in the midpoint equation makes negative correlation optimal; both variants retain second-order finite-horizon root-mean-square accuracy for each fixed nondegenerate correlation.
