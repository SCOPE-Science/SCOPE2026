For an equilateral complete-bipartite wire network discretized with consistent linear finite-element mass, the \(\theta\)-method has an exact positivity phase diagram: no positive step when \(\theta<1/3\), one isolated step when \(\theta=1/3\), a bounded interval when \(1/3<\theta<1-1/(2m)\), and a half-line above that, where \(m\) is the larger part size. The lower threshold is always \(t/\ell^2=1/(6\theta)\) in the positive-interval regimes, and an explicit quadratic root supplies the sharp upper threshold.
