# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The Gaussian hereditary dynamics reduce exactly to the nonzero translation balance previously established in the primary source. Strict monotonicity proves unique nonzero speed above gain threshold. Monotone convergence proves the if-and-only-if third-age-moment criterion without presupposing finite higher moments. The polynomial-tail laws have independent dominated-convergence, logarithmic-cutoff, and beta-moment proofs, with all constants and parameter boundaries explicit. The numerical checker only samples finite scales.

## Originality

**PASS under inspected sources, with historical-priority risk.** Vrabel's arXiv:2610.24152v1, Theorem 13, already gives the universal moving-speed balance, and Proposition 16 handles a Gaussian with exponential memory; these are not claimed as new. Theorem 15 assumes finite fifth age moment. The present result identifies the sharp *third* age-moment criterion and proves its full algebraic-memory anomalous-onset regimes including the critical logarithm. The related full-text papers arXiv:2505.21711v1 and arXiv:2512.09597v1 study different stochastic/chemotactic effects. A general historical Tauberian or active-memory theorem might imply a specialization; independent priority review remains appropriate.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The finite-versus-infinite third-moment boundary is a structural property of the Gaussian memory model, not a chosen finite numerical cutoff. The explicit exponents and sharp coefficients distinguish heavy-tail behavior at and below the marginal fourth-power tail. No paper-sized theorem, global attracting dynamics, or nonlinear stability claim is implied.

## Limits

The classification is restricted to *complete uniform translations* and fixed nonnegative age weights with a Gaussian spatial imprint. It neither classifies other trajectories nor proves orbital stability of any translation. Exact-tail coefficients require the stated normalized power weight. Finite quadratures are checks, not infinite proofs.
