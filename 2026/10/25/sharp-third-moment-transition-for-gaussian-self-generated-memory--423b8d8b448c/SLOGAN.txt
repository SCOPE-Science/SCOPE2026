For Gaussian self-generated hereditary motion, the third moment of the age kernel is the sharp dividing line: a finite moment yields a classical square-root translating-speed onset, while an infinite moment suppresses it. Normalized power-law memories exhibit exact fractional-power speeds for \(2<p<4\), a logarithmic critical law at \(p=4\), and an explicit square-root coefficient for \(p>4\).
