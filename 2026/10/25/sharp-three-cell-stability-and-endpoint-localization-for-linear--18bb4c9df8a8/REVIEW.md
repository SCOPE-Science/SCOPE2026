The proof uses a precisely specified continuous one-dimensional hat basis, its exact positive-definite Gram matrix and an alternating-sign inverse justified by an irreducible diagonally dominant Stieltjes matrix. Convexity of the absolute kernel gives knot localization. A signed-affine integral identity gives the exact four row norms. The displayed factorizations prove (i) domination of each interior knot by the adjacent endpoint, (ii) shorter-end selection, (iii) uniform strict \(5/3\) and \(8/3\) bounds. Exact limiting sequences prove sharpness, while sign-valued inputs attain the sharp norm at each fixed partition. The standalone checker reconstructs every critical identity and both limits; finite numerical comparisons are supplementary.
