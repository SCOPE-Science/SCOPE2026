For a fixed-orientation unit-circumradius equilateral triangle and a diamond-oriented square of circumradius \(r\), the sharp maximum translative intersection area has three exact phases, with thresholds \(3(\sqrt3-1)/4\) and \((3+\sqrt3)/4\) and a unique intermediate optimizer. At equal circumradii, aligning the two centroids loses the exact area \(21/8-3\sqrt3/2\) compared with the optimal translation.
