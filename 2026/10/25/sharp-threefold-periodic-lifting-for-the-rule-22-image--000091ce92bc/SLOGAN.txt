Every spatially \(n\)-periodic output of elementary cellular automaton Rule 22 has a preimage with spatial period equal to one of \(n\), \(2n\), or \(3n\), and every nonconstant output admits a preimage with period dividing \(2n\). The only obstruction needing a threefold multiple is the all-one output when \(3\nmid n\); a fully closed 2375-relation certificate proves this uniform classification.
