For every integer base \(b\ge2\) and translation \(c\ge-1\), the characteristic word of \(\{b^j+c:j\ge0\}\) has minimal Abelian maximal pattern complexity exactly when \(c<b(b-2)\). At and above the sharp cutoff, a concrete \(2(b+1)\)-cycle within the first three sum-incidence matchings obstructs the required forest property.
