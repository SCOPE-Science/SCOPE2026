For Fang Hong’s symmetric two-cut hyperbolic balls, the fixed-area sphere is locally stable exactly through surface area \(2\pi\), and its total-mean-curvature deficit changes sign at fourth order beyond that threshold. The emerging optimal truncation has squared angle proportional to excess area and improves total mean curvature by exactly \(25\sqrt3/(1458\pi^2)\) times the cube of that excess, to leading order; every area between \(2\pi\) and the sphere/double-disk crossover consequently admits a counterexample below both benchmarks.
