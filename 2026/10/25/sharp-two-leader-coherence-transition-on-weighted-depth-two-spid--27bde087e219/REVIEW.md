# Scientific review: two-leader coherence on weighted depth-two spiders

## Correctness — PASS

The effective-resistance diagonal Green-function identity follows from series/parallel reduction after shorting two vertices to ground. Every unordered leader pair belongs to precisely one of six branch-permutation orbits. The six exact formulas are reconstructed from this identity and independently checked through direct rational grounded-Laplacian inversion for 950 concrete cases. Subtracting candidates yields strict positive rational expressions for all \(k\ge4\), and at \(k=3\) a single sign change at \(t=1\). The proof covers all real \(t>0\) and integer \(k\ge3\); finite checks are auxiliary, not exhaustive proof.

## Originality — PASS, with explicit residual priority risk

The inspected primary material by Fitch and Leonard (arXiv:1407.1569v1) develops multi-leader joint centrality and gives path/cycle optimizations, not the exhaustive two-leader continuous-weight classification of a depth-two spider. The general framework of Patterson and Bamieh and the algorithmic relaxation of Lin, Fardad and Jovanović do not themselves imply this classified optimum without the new six-orbit comparison. Accessible mathematical-record searches for weighted spiders, grounded Laplacian coherence, and two leaders did not return an equivalent theorem, but search absence does not establish priority. Sun, Li and Zheng's 2021 article on symmetric and asymmetric trees is close in subject; only its abstract was accessible, so its full-text overlap remains a material risk. This is not represented as disproved coverage.

## Value — PASS

The balanced depth-two spider is a basic branching network directly beyond paths and stars, and minimizing stationary follower variance under two permanently controlled vertices is a standard design objective. The theorem classifies every optimizer in an infinite family across a continuous resistance parameter, identifies a sharp three-arm transition and a distinct regime where four or more arms force the same structural design regardless of relative conductance. This changes optimal leader location and is more than an isolated finite computation.

## Boundaries and closest literature

The exact result applies only to identical outer resistance \(t>0\), unit inner resistance, and exactly two noiseless leaders. It does not settle longer or irregular spiders, alternative covariance assumptions, or the distinct grounded spectral-radius objective. Primary context: Patterson–Bamieh (2010); Fitch–Leonard arXiv:1407.1569v1 (2014); Lin–Fardad–Jovanović arXiv:1302.0450; Sun–Li–Zheng DOI 10.1088/1742-5468/ac0f66 (2021, abstract-only inspection).
