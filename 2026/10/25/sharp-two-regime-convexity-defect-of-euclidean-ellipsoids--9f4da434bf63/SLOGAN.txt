The convexity-defect function of any Euclidean ellipsoid boundary has an exact initial branch determined only by its longest and shortest semiaxes: \(h_E(t)=a(1-\sqrt{1-t^2/b^2})\) precisely through \(t_*=(b^2/a)\sqrt{2-b^2/a^2}\). For every nonround ellipsoid this affine-circle bound becomes strictly nonsharp immediately afterward, while the exact defect equals the shortest semiaxis \(b\) from scale \(b\) onward.
