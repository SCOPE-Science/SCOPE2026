# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The row/column edge translation gives a sharp size bound from three-vertex path packing, and equality forces \(k\) stars of each orientation. A case-by-case classification of two simultaneous replacements gives \(4k^2+8\binom{k}{2}\) choices; the fiber argument and the nonbipartite Johnson tensor product prove connectivity for all \(k\ge1\). Separate exact-cover and center-assignment programs reproduce the \(k=1,2\) counts and degrees. Computation is corroborative only.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** The packing equivalence is explicit in the Orlovich et al. source, and earlier enumerative rook-graph work already implies the count; those are not claimed as novel. Bonsma et al.'s claw-free independent-set reconfiguration results do not apply to selected sets inducing a matching. The prior triangular-graph dissociation reconfiguration result concerns \(L(K_n)\) rather than \(L(K_{n,n})\) and does not imply the paired-center mobility theorem. The searched literature and published mathematical-record hits do not identify a covering two-replacement connectivity/degree theorem. Unindexed reconfiguration or exact-cover results could nevertheless overlap.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The exact sharp movement budget separates totally frozen optimum solutions from a connected, explicit regular reconfiguration graph over an infinite symmetric graph family. The proof supplies a tangible paired-center mechanism, not merely optimum counts or finite-case tabulations. This is relevant to understanding the topology of optimal solution spaces when an optimizer may replace two selected vertices simultaneously.

**Limits.** Only square rook graphs whose order is divisible by three are covered. The old induced-matching count is a supporting specialization, not the new contribution. Broader sizes and shortest two-move distances are not determined. Some potential synonyms in unpublished literature may remain inaccessible.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
