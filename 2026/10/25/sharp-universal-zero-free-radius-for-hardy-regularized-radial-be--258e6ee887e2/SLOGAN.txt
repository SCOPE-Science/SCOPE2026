For any normalized radial disk probability measure, adding a Hardy boundary norm of strength \(\kappa>0\) guarantees a zero-free reproducing kernel exactly throughout the universal product disk \(|z\overline w|<\min\{\kappa,1\}\), with an optimal quantitative modulus bound. The cutoff is sharp: when \(\kappa<1\), smooth radial weights can create genuine negative-product zeros approaching \(-\kappa\) from outside at inverse-weight-exponent speed, although the separate Hardy and weighted Bergman kernels are zero-free.
