# Reproducibility and limits

The standalone `checker.py` runs with Python 3's standard library and uses exact rational arithmetic. Its output in `checker_output.txt` was generated by executing that source. For the smooth normalized weight with exponent \(3\) and the boundary strength \(\kappa=1/6\), it checks rational truncations at \(t=-9/10\) and \(t=-24/25\), together with rigorous tail bounds. These prove opposite signs and hence an interior real zero by continuity. Exact center-mass algebra is also tested.

The universal all-measure inequality is proved analytically from positivity and telescoping of coefficient increments. The sharp smooth-weight assertion follows by locally uniform convergence and Rouché's theorem; it is not an assertion established by finitely many experiments. No independent mathematical audit has been performed.
