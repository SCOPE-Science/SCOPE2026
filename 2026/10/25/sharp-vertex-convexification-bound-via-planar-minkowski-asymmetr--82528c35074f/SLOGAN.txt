For every planar convex polygon \(P\), the minimum \(\lambda\) such that its vertices plus \(\lambda P\) fill \((1+\lambda)P\) lies between \(1\) and its Minkowski asymmetry \(s(P)\). Both bounds are sharp: every regular odd \(n\)-gon has exact threshold \(\sec(\pi/n)\), while every regular even \(n\)-gon has threshold \(1\).
