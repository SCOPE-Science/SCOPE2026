Every six-point spherical \(2\)-design in three dimensions is the union of two orthonormal triples, and its prescribed-equal-area Minkowski polyhedron belongs to a single sharp volume family. Among all such polyhedra of surface area \(4\pi\), the triangular bipyramid uniquely minimizes volume at \(8\pi^{3/2}/(9\sqrt3)\), while the cube uniquely maximizes it at \((2\pi/3)^{3/2}\).
