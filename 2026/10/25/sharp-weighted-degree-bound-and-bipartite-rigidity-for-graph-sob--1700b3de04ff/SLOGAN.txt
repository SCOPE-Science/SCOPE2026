On every finite weighted graph, the two-point graph-Sobolev amplification of positive-part truncation is at most \((1+\gamma\Delta)/\sqrt{1+2\gamma\Delta}\), and for positive maximum weighted degree equality occurs exactly when a component is weighted-regular and bipartite. An exact cut-Schur spectral formula computes the graph-specific modulus, which exceeds one whenever the graph has an edge even though truncation decreases every signal's individual energy.
