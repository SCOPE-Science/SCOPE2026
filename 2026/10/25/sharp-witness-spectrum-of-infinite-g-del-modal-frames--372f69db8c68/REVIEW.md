# Review of sharp witness spectrum of infinite Gödel modal frames

**Correctness: PASS.** The proof tests each modal operator against an arbitrary scalar input. Necessity for the box is an explicit nonattained infimum approaching the requested cutoff; sufficiency reduces all nontrivial residual terms to finitely many strict-superlevel entries unless the zero minimum is already attained. For diamond, finite positive superlevels force attainment of every positive supremum, and an infinite superlevel admits an explicit nonattained positive supremum. Finite palettes are treated separately, without an invalid nesting inference. All threshold intervals are realized by explicit countable rows. Exact rational enumeration and prefix identities from `checker.py` passed; they are not substituted for the infinitary proofs.

**Originality: PASS within inspected evidence.** Ferrari–Fiorentini–Giardini–Rodriguez (arXiv:2606.31906, sections 1–2 and conclusion) formulate witnessed Gödel modal models and prove finite-model and calculus results, not the universal-valuation condition for a fixed infinite frame. Vidal–Rodriguez (arXiv:2605.15810, sections 0.2, 0.4, 0.5) show modal-logical completeness for diamond-witnessed and partially witnessed classes; this existential choice of models does not imply a rowwise equivalence under all valuations. Bílková–Frittella–Kozhemiachenko (IJCAR 2022, theorem 2 and discussion) characterize frame definability of finite branching in different bi-Gödel systems but do not give the strict-superlevel cutoff theorem. The closest mathematical-record hit, “Ordinal regrading classes of finite Gödel Kripke frames,” concerns finite labelled frames and cannot cover infinite nonattainment. Searches for equivalent frame-uniform formulations, strict superlevel thresholds, and finite-palette analogues found no decisive covering result; non-discovery alone is not treated as uniqueness evidence.

**Value: PASS.** Witnessed versus unwitnessed extrema are a central semantic distinction in recent fuzzy modal logic. This is a natural complete frame classification under the genuinely stronger quantifier order “every valuation on this fixed frame,” plus a sharp continuum spectrum of positive-degree cutoffs. In particular, infinite positive branching can guarantee every possibility witness without guaranteeing necessity witnesses, and the full cutoff interval is realized by explicit frames. The distinction is mathematically structural and applies at every modal depth in the unrestricted cases; it is not a mere finite table or normalization.

**Limitations and priority risks.** The proof does not assert any new validity theorem or finite-model property. The 2012–2022 fuzzy relational/description-logic literature contains closely related sup–min and witnessed-model notions; a theorem stated solely in functional-analytic or fuzzy-relation terms could anticipate individual scalar equivalences. The accessible 2022 frame-definability result supplies part of the conceptual background but not the full new conjunction and cutoff realization. An independent literature and proof audit remains necessary.
