For smooth symmetric blends of two equispaced degree-\((d-1)\) Lagrange extrapolants, the smallest possible worst-case data-noise gain is exactly \(\Lambda_d(\lfloor C/2\rfloor)\), which grows as \(C^{d-1}/(d-1)!\) at fixed degree. This bound is attained by a smooth strictly increasing transition when the continuation length \(C\) is even, but is only an unattained infimum when \(C\) is odd.
