For the real harmonic Bergman space on the unit ball with weight \(1-|x|^2\), every dimension \(d\ge3\) has a sharp product zero-free radius determined by one cubic; above it, precisely one angular kernel zero occurs at each fixed product of radii. The first zero is antipodal, the nodal angle is analytic, and the critical product radius increases strictly with dimension, approaching one at rate \(\sqrt{12/d}\).
