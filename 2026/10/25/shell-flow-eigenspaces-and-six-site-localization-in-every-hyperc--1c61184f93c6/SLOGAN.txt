In the position-phased Grover hypercube walk, every dimension \(d\ge3\)—including odd dimensions—has an exact six-site eigenstate with stationary probability \(1/6\) at each occupied vertex. Explicit adjacent-Hamming-shell flow eigenspaces occupy a fraction \(1-4/d+(2d+4)/(d2^d)\) of the entire position-coin space; these are non-fixed eigenstates and do not settle the separate eigenvalue-one conjecture.
