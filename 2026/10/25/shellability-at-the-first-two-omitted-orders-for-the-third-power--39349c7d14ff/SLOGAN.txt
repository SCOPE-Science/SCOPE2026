Explicit finite shellings close the two-order gap in the published third-power-cycle three-cut shellability theorem: \(\Delta_3(C_{13}^3)\) and \(\Delta_3(C_{14}^3)\) are shellable, with one and eight top-dimensional sphere summands. Combined with the known theorem for larger orders, \(\Delta_3(C_n^3)\) is shellable for every \(n\ge13\), confirming the positive branch of the conjectured threshold when the cycle power is three.
