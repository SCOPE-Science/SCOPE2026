# Shortest crosspolytopal carriers and integral octahedral symmetry in the truncated-octahedral independence complex

## Finding
Let \(T\) be the 24-vertex truncated-octahedral (three-dimensional permutahedral) graph, with vertices the coordinate permutations of \((0,\pm1,\pm2)\) and edges joining vertices at squared Euclidean distance \(2\). Let \(K=\operatorname{Ind}(T)\), its ordinary independence simplicial complex. Its previously determined homotopy type is \(K\simeq\bigvee^4 S^5\), so \(\widetilde H_5(K;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^4\).

A vertex-induced subcomplex maps nontrivially to \(\widetilde H_5(K;\mathbb Z)\) only if its support has at least 12 vertices. Exactly 56 twelve-vertex supports attain this bound; each induces six disjoint graph edges, hence a crosspolytope boundary \((S^0)^{*6}\cong S^5\). Every one of the 56 oriented fundamental cycles maps to a nonzero **primitive** integral homology class.

The complete graph automorphism group \(O_h\cong S_4\times C_2\) (order 48) has four orbits on these 56 supports, of cardinalities \(8,12,12,24\). The **unique eight-element orbit generates the entire integral fifth-homology lattice**: four of its oriented sphere classes are an integral basis with determinant \(+1\) in explicit Morse coordinates. Each of the other three orbits generates a primitive rank-three proper sublattice. Thus being shortest and primitive individually does not imply that any single symmetry orbit spans all homology, while one distinguished orbit does.

If \(\varepsilon\) denotes the nontrivial character of the central inversion factor and \(\mathrm{Std}_3\) the rational three-dimensional standard \(S_4\)-representation, the complete rational symmetry module is
\[
\widetilde H_5(K;\mathbb Q)\cong_{\mathbb Q[S_4\times C_2]}
\bigl(\operatorname{sgn}_{S_4}\oplus\mathrm{Std}_3\bigr)\boxtimes\varepsilon.
\]
In particular central inversion acts as minus the identity on the **integral** homology, and no nonzero rational homology class is invariant under all rotations. This character formula does not by itself assert a direct-sum splitting of the integral lattice into rational irreducible constituents.

## Assumptions and scope
Vertices are indexed \(0,\ldots,23\) in lexicographic order on the explicitly given signed coordinate triples. Every graph edge is an unordered pair of indices, sorted lexicographically. A face of \(K\) is a set of pairwise nonadjacent vertices of \(T\), represented in verification by the sum of its vertex bitmasks. A minimal *carrier* means a vertex subset \(W\) of smallest possible cardinality for which inclusion \(K[W]\to K\) has nonzero image in reduced integral fifth homology. An *induced matching of size six* means exactly six pairwise disjoint edges and no additional graph edge between any of their twelve endpoints.

For six independently disjoint graph edges \(\{a_i,b_i\}\), their twelve vertices induce \(6K_2\), and their fundamental sphere cycle is the join
\[
\left([a_1]-[b_1]\right)\ast\cdots\ast\left([a_6]-[b_6]\right),
\]
with the usual signed orientation of simplicial chains. The entire result is for this finite graph, ordinary independence complex, integral homology and the specified full geometric automorphism group. It does not classify higher-dimensional permutahedra.

## Proof
**A sharp flag-complex support lemma.** For any flag simplicial complex having a nonzero reduced degree-\(d\) homology class over a field, a vertex-minimal induced support has at least \(2d+2\) vertices; equality forces the boundary of a \((d+1)\)-crosspolytope. One proves this by induction on \(d\). For a vertex-minimal support \(W\), deleting any vertex \(v\) kills degree-\(d\) homology. The deletion–star exact sequence therefore forces nonzero reduced degree-\((d-1)\) homology in the link of \(v\), requiring at least \(2d\) link vertices. There is also a non-neighbor \(w\) of \(v\), since otherwise the flag complex is a cone, yielding \(|W|\ge2d+2\). In the equality case the link is inductively a \((d-1)\)-crosspolytope sphere. The only remaining vertex \(w\) must be adjacent in the flag one-skeleton to every vertex in that link: otherwise attaching its star cannot kill the generator of the top homology of the link, contradicting the existence of the original degree-\(d\) class. Thus the whole support is the suspension of that link. Base case \(d=0\) is two isolated vertices. Here \(K\) is flag; its missing edges are precisely the original graph edges. Equality at \(d=5\) thus means an induced matching of six graph edges. This proves sharpness and completeness once all such matchings are enumerated and their images checked.

**Exact topology and oriented integral coordinates.** `verification/verify.py` constructs the graph independently of any saved graph data and enumerates all 37,075 nonempty independent faces. Greedy vertex-toggle pairing, with vertices considered in ascending order and an unmatched face paired with its unmatched one-vertex extension when possible, leaves one critical vertex and four critical five-faces. The program orients all 219,216 nonempty face-cover incidences upward for matched pairs and downward otherwise; an exhaustive topological sort visits all 37,075 faces. This supplies an acyclic discrete-Morse matching, hence an integral homotopy equivalence \(K\simeq\bigvee^4S^5\).

The four critical degree-five faces have integer bitmask indices \(1575960,2149378,2441252,8603650\), in ascending order. For a closed simplicial five-chain, eliminate all coefficients at matched lower-five faces using the signed boundaries of their paired six-faces. There are 5,083 such pairs; the dependency graph is acyclic, so elimination is integral and unitriangular. The coefficients remaining on the four critical five-faces are the integral homology coordinates. The remaining coefficients at upper-five faces are forced by the boundary equations and are not mistaken for additional homology directions. Acyclic Morse cancellation proves that this coordinate map is an integral isomorphism on homology, not merely a rational projection.

**Complete minimum-carrier census and lattice certificates.** Exhaustive enumeration finds exactly 56 induced six-edge matchings, therefore exactly 56 twelve-vertex crosspolytope supports. Every oriented fundamental cycle has 64 signed simplicial five-faces and zero boundary. All 56 have coordinate tuples of greatest common divisor one. Signed coordinate permutations of \(\mathbb R^3\) give 48 graph automorphisms, and a separate graph-isomorphism enumeration confirms they form the full graph automorphism group. They partition the 56 supports into orbit sizes \(8,12,12,24\). The 4-by-8 matrix of coordinate columns for the unique eight-element orbit has Smith diagonal \((1,1,1,1)\). More explicitly, when matchings are indexed by the verifier's lexicographic edge-recursion, the sphere indices \((0,1,6,28)\) lie in that orbit and their 4-by-4 coordinate determinant is \(+1\). Every other orbit matrix has rank three and Smith diagonal \((1,1,1)\). The former therefore generates all of \(\mathbb Z^4\), while each latter orbit generates a primitive rank-three sublattice.

**All 48 automorphism characters.** For each signed coordinate permutation \(g\), the verifier computes its reduced simplicial Lefschetz number as the signed alternating sum of the fixed oriented faces, including the augmentation contribution. Since the only positive reduced homology is in degree five, its negative is exactly the degree-five trace. The trace multiset is \(4\) once, \(-4\) once, \(2\) six times, \(-2\) six times, \(1\) eight times, \(-1\) eight times, and \(0\) eighteen times. Under the identification of orientation-preserving octahedral symmetries with \(S_4\) acting on the four unoriented cube body diagonals, the trace is \(\operatorname{sgn}(\pi)+\#\operatorname{Fix}(\pi)-1\). Central inversion multiplies the trace by \(-1\), giving the displayed character. Independently, all 48 automorphisms are applied directly to the four chosen integral geometric cycles, giving unimodular integer action matrices with exactly these traces; the central inversion matrix is \(-I_4\).

## Verification
From the package root install `verification/requirements.txt` and execute `python verification/verify.py`. The script uses only elementary exact integers, SymPy for integer Smith forms and matrices, and NetworkX for an independently generated adjacent-transposition Cayley model and the full 48-element graph automorphism count. It explicitly checks the complete Hasse acyclicity, boundary-elimination dependency DAG, minimum-carrier enumeration, all 56 integral homology classes, primitive Smith diagonals, a determinant-one four-class basis, all automorphism orbits, signed Lefschetz values and direct integral action matrices. Its success sentinel is `VERIFY_OK_TRUNCATED_OCTAHEDRAL_H5_OCTAHEDRAL_ACTION`. No floating-point tolerance, finite-field inference of integral saturation, or previously generated certificate file is needed.

## Relationship to prior work
The graph is the three-dimensional permutahedron and the adjacent-transposition Cayley graph of \(S_4\), as described in *The Graphicahedron* (arXiv:0910.3908, especially Lemma 3.3). Barmak's *Star clusters in independence complexes of graphs* (arXiv:1007.0418) develops general independence-complex and suspension methods. Neither source asserts the present 56 shortest integral carriers, their orbit lattices or the full octahedral character.

A previously published specific computation, *Vertex-uniform independence-complex splitting for the truncated octahedron* (public research record dated 2026-10-11), already proves the four-five-sphere homotopy type and identifies all vertex deletion and closed-neighborhood deletion homotopy types. **Those previously known statements are not offered as new.** Neither their numerical ranks nor the vertex-local cofibration splitting determine the induced 12-vertex carrier count, the index-one orbit generating property, the distinct primitive rank-three orbit lattices, or the full group action. Comparisons were also made with results for truncated-cube and other Archimedean graphs, which concern different simplicial complexes. The new content is an explicit integral geometric generator classification and its symmetry refinement, not the nonequivariant Betti calculation.

## Limitations
The result is a finite exact computation with a general sharp flag-support lemma, not a theorem for all permutahedra. The homotopy type and vertex-local splitting were already known. The representation formula is over \(\mathbb Q\); no claim is made that its summands split as integral modules. Full coverage of inaccessible or unindexed literature is not guaranteed. No general-family theorem or classification of the underlying integral representation into irreducible lattices is asserted.
