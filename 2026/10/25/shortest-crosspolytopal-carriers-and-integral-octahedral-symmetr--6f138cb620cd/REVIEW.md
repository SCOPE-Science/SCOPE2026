# Scientific review: truncated-octahedral shortest carriers and symmetry

**Correctness — PASS.** The 24-vertex coordinate graph is independently isomorphic to the adjacent-transposition \(S_4\) Cayley graph, and has exactly 48 automorphisms. Complete independent-face enumeration, an explicitly oriented and exhaustively topologically sorted Morse matching (37,075 vertices in its Hasse digraph), and a unitriangular chain reduction give \(\widetilde H_5\cong\mathbb Z^4\). A sharp flag-complex support argument limits 12-vertex carriers to induced six-edge matchings. All 56 are enumerated and have primitive degree-five coordinates; exact Smith computations show the eight-element orbit spans integrally and the three other orbits are primitive rank-three lattices. All 48 signed Lefschetz traces agree with direct integral action matrices and the stated rational character.

**Originality — PASS, with residual bibliographic risk.** A published result for the exact same 24-vertex permutahedral graph already established \(\operatorname{Ind}(T)\simeq\bigvee^4S^5\), uniform vertex deletion, and closed-neighborhood cofiber splitting. Those are background only. Its inspected full RESULT does not enumerate all 56 minimal carriers, distinguish their four automorphism orbits by integral Smith forms, provide the integral generating eight-orbit, or determine the full rational octahedral character. *The Graphicahedron* identifies the permutahedral Cayley graph, while Barmak proves general independence-complex suspension and star-cluster statements; neither directly implies the asserted geometric integral generators. Searches included the signed-coordinate permutahedron model, equivalent adjacent-transposition language, octahedral symmetry, induced six-edge matchings, all parameters and relevant result records. A specific unindexed prior computation may remain unknown.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The smallest possible nonzero fifth-homology carriers form a natural extremal geometric family in an Archimedean graph. The main result distinguishes a single saturated eight-sphere symmetry orbit from three proper primitive rank-three orbits; this is stronger than homotopy type, Betti number, local vertex splitting or the rational character alone. Explicit certificates make the integral phenomenon reusable and testable. The character also identifies the rotation-invariant obstruction and integral antipodal sign.

**Limits.** The result is specific to the truncated octahedron; its rational constituent decomposition is not asserted to split integrally, and exact finite calculations do not prove a higher-dimensional family statement. Independent external scientific validation has not been performed.
