The truncated octahedron has exactly 56 minimum-support crosspolytopal five-spheres in its independence complex; all are primitive integral homology carriers, but a unique eight-element symmetry orbit already generates the full rank-four lattice. Its octahedral rational homology representation is \(\bigl(\operatorname{sgn}_{S_4}\oplus\mathrm{Std}_3\bigr)\boxtimes\varepsilon\), with central inversion acting as minus the identity integrally.
