For the unique cubic girth-seven cage, the 152 tori made from disjoint shortest heptagons form a primitive generating family for \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z^{54}\). Its seven symmetry orbits exhibit a sharp integral threshold: five orbits are necessary and sufficient for generation, although three designated orbits already span rationally with integral quotient \((\mathbb Z/2)^2\oplus\mathbb Z/4\).
