In the Petersen graph's ordinary matching complex, all 110 shortest nonzero second-homology carriers are six-vertex octahedral spheres split into three \(S_5\)-orbits: one generates an index-two lattice, while either other orbit generates the entire integral homology. The two nonzero rational homology modules are \(\operatorname{sgn}\otimes\mathbb Q^{[5]}\) in degree two and \(S^{(3,2)}\) in degree three.
