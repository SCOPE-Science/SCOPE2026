The complete rational-canonical similarity distribution of \([E,F]\) is explicit for every ordered pair of rank-one idempotents over \(\mathbb F_q\), with a sharp distinction between zero, nilpotent Jordan blocks of lengths two and three, and cyclic quadratic blocks. At fixed field order, the probability of a length-three nilpotent block tends to \((q-1)/q^2\), even though both factors are idempotent.
