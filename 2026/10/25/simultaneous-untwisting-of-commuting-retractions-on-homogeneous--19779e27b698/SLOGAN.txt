Every finite family of commuting idempotent group-labelled transformations can be simultaneously untwisted by a single diagonal conjugation, even when the labeling group is nonabelian. For a fixed base family on \(n\) positions whose product has rank \(c\), exactly \(|W|^{n-c}\) compatible lifts exist, giving a uniform transfer principle for higher retraction tuples and semilattice subsemigroups.
