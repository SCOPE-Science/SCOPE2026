Normal-basis smooth hypersurface systems carry a Singer action with exactly \(\gcd(d,(q^{n+1}-1)/(q-1))\) rational-member orbits; when this gcd is one, their rational-point sets realize the entire projective point--hyperplane incidence design. In the first plane-quartic case over \(\mathbb F_3\), all thirteen rational members are \(\mathbb F_3\)-isomorphic Klein quartics, form a \(2\)-\((13,4,1)\) design, and share zeta numerator \(1+27T^6\).
