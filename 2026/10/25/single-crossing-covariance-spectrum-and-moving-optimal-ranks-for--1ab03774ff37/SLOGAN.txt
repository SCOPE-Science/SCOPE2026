For nonoscillatory Landweber inversion with a single noisy observation, every pair of distinct covariance eigenmodes swaps variance order exactly once as the iteration count increases. The best rank-\(r\) covariance eigenspace is always a consecutive spectral window that moves only toward smaller singular values, while a fixed leading-normal-operator rank-\(r\) window can retain an arbitrarily small fraction of the optimal covariance trace.
