The diagonal order-six quotient of the Fermat sextic Calabi–Yau fourfold has exactly two smooth genus-four singular curves and nine isolated cyclic quotient singularities; the curves share the explicit canonical model \(U^2+V^2+W^2=0,\ T^3=UVW\). Its coarse Euler characteristic is \(435\), whereas the commuting-pair orbifold Euler invariant is \(441\).
