For the exterior algebra on six generators over every odd finite field, element-centralizers have exactly six possible dimensions \(48,57,58,60,63,64\), with complete multiplicities determined by trivector wedge-annihilators. The exact probability that two uniformly random algebra elements commute is \(\bigl(q^{20}+q^{17}+q^{16}-q^{11}-q^{10}+q-1\bigr)/q^{33}\).
