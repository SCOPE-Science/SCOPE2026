The Petersen graph's vertex-induced forest complex has integral homotopy type \(\bigvee^6 S^5\), despite being nonpure with maximal faces in two dimensions. Its rational fifth homology realizes the irreducible six-dimensional \(\mathfrak S_5\)-module \(\bigwedge^2 V\cong S^{(3,1,1)}\) under the graph's full symmetry group.
