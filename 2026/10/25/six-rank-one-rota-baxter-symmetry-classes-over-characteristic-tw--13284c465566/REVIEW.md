# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact claim is restricted to \(q=2^e\), \(e\ge2\), for weight-zero rank-one linear operators on \(\Lambda_{\mathbb F_q}(\mathbb F_q^2)\). The proof derives the rank-one criterion from the Rota–Baxter equation, using square-zero multiplication by radical elements to force annihilation of both the left and right image ideals. The images divide into the canonical lines inside \(J^2\) and outside \(J^2\). Projective normalization gives the stated polynomial without scalar overcounting. Automorphism conjugation acts transitively within each of the six invariant strata; the argument works also in characteristic two. The normal-form representatives and orbit sizes exhaust the count. Both standalone scripts were executed on their actual paths and succeeded, including complete enumeration over \(\mathbb F_4\). The scripts check finite fields only; the uniform theorem is established by the algebraic proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The closest 2026 real-coefficient paper is Shakoor–Noor-ul-Ain (arXiv:2609.07377); its full accessible HTML was read, including Theorem 4.1 and its real matrix families. It does not give a characteristic-two extension-field classification or finite-field orbit-count formulas. Hua–Liu (2013) is an especially relevant earlier classification, but its available abstract explicitly restricts to algebraically closed fields of characteristic different from two; its full text was not retrieved. A published classification of all operators over \(\mathbb F_2\) already includes the six rank-one orbit sizes \(1,3,3,6,6,6\), so that boundary case is recognized as covered, not claimed anew. Searches for finite-field normal forms, rank-one counting polynomials, and compatible automorphism actions found no stronger result for \(\mathbb F_{2^e}\) with \(e\ge2\). The residual risk is an earlier characteristic-two extension-field calculation outside the inspected sources; absence from search is not proof of uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This is a complete classification of the first nonzero rank stratum of Rota–Baxter endomorphisms on a natural four-dimensional algebra over every characteristic-two extension field, not merely a fixed-size experiment. It identifies the invariant geometric features, gives all conjugacy normal forms, a uniform six-class theorem, and exact orbit-size and total-count polynomials. Characteristic two materially changes exterior multiplication from the odd-characteristic setting, while extending a single-field census to every extension degree requires the rank-one and automorphism arguments. No claim is made about higher ranks or other weights.
