For every characteristic-two extension field \(\mathbb F_q\) with \(q=2^e\) and \(e\ge2\), the two-generator exterior algebra has exactly \(q^4+2q^3-q^2-q-1\) nonzero rank-one weight-zero Rota–Baxter operators, grouped into six full algebra-automorphism conjugacy classes. The six orbit sizes and normal forms are uniform across all extension degrees.
