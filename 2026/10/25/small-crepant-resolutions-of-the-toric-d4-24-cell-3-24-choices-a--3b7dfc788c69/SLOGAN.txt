The toric fourfold of the \(D_4\) root 24-cell has exactly \(3^{24}\) smooth small torus-equivariant resolutions over the fixed base, obtained by choosing an octahedral diagonal independently at each of 24 singularities. They are crepant, have 24 disjoint \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\) exceptional fibers and Betti vector \((1,20,54,20,1)\); at least one is projective.
