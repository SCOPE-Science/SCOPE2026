For every odd prime power \(q\), the coordinate-marked smooth Ciani quartics form a set of exactly \((q-1)^2(q^3-4q^2+6q-5)\) members. Each admits precisely \(q(q^2-1)\) diagonal bidegree-\((2,2)\) conic-bundle branch presentations up to scalar, forming one free \(\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbb F_q)\)-orbit.
