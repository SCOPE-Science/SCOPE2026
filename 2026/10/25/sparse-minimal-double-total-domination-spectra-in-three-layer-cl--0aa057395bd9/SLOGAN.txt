For every \(n\geq6\), the inclusion-minimal double total dominating sets of \(K_n\square P_3\) have an exact six-term counting polynomial and only the possible sizes \(\{6,7,8,9,n+2,2n\}\) (coincident sizes combined). The minimum size is six, while the unique largest deletion-minimal solution is the union of the two outer clique layers, with size \(2n\).
