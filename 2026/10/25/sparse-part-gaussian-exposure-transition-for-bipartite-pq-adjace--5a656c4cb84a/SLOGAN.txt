The PQ adjacency polytope of a sparse complete bipartite graph has a sharp random-direction exposure transition: diagonal vertices dominate below the exponent \(3-2\sqrt2\), while cross-part vertices dominate above it. At a logarithmically corrected critical part size, intrinsic spherical widths converge, after explicit second-order normalization, to the sum of two independent maxima built from five Gumbel variables, and the diagonal exposure probability has an explicit integral law.
