The exponential growth rates of \(A^{-n}TB^n\) and \(A^nTB^{-n}\) are exactly the largest spectral-coupling ratios \(b/a\) and \(a/b\), independent of which Schatten quasi-norm \(\mathcal S_p\), \(0<p<\infty\), is used. The two-sided rate is one precisely when \(AT=TB\), even for continuous spectra and operators on different Hilbert spaces.
