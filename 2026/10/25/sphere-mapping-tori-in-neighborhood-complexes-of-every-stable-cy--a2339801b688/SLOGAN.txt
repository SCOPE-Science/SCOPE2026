For every cycle power \(C_n^{(k)}\) with \(k\ge2\) and \(n\ge4k+1\), the open-neighborhood complex is homotopy equivalent to the mapping torus of a sphere map of degree \((-1)^{kn}\). Thus all higher odd powers on odd cycles exhibit integral order-two torsion, while the even-parity cases have product-sphere-bundle homotopy type.
