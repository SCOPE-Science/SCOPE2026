In a staggered pair of regular \(n\)-gonal latitude rings on the sphere, optimal packing and optimal covering coincide only for the octahedron: for every \(n\ge4\), their unique optimal heights differ, and using the packing optimum asymptotically wastes \(\pi/4\) of covering radius. At the unique covering-optimal height, the farthest-site set undergoes an exact \(2\to 2n+2\to 2n\) contact-count transition.
