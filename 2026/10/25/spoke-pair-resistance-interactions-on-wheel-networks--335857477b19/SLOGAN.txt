On a unit-conductance wheel with \(n\) rim vertices, deleting two neighboring spokes always maximizes Kirchhoff-index damage among spoke pairs. The damage interaction relative to two isolated spoke failures changes from positive to negative with separation precisely when \(n\ge6\), and the first negative separation grows like \((\log n+\log\log n)/\log((3+\sqrt5)/2)\) up to a bounded error.
