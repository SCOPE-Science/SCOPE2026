# Review: two-rainbow independent domination on rook graphs

## Correctness — PASS

The colored sets are separately nonattacking rook placements. Their missed rows and columns form a rectangle forced into the opposite color, hence of size at most one. This proves the rectangle-versus-square classification. The corresponding injection and derangement counts reconstruct every displayed coefficient without reliance on an unverified lemma. Direct definition-based enumeration checks small cases, including both boundary derangements.

## Originality — PASS (bounded comparison)

The original 2017 introduction of two-rainbow independent domination gives the precise definition and product equivalence but does not supply this census. The 2021 article establishes general inequalities and the 2023 Cartesian-product article treats cycle factors. Related complete-multipartite rainbow-domination records describe a different invariant. Equivalent phrasing as maximal independent sets of \(K_2\square K_p\square K_q\) was explicitly searched and considered. The remaining risk is a previously unindexed equivalent formula or broader Hamming-graph theorem.

## Value — PASS

The result computes a complete polynomial rather than a single minimum, with a sharp structural transition from single-weight rigidity on nonsquare boards to exactly three possible weights on square boards. Explicit injection and derangement counts identify a natural enumeration gap left by previous minimum-parameter results.

## Scientific limitations

Only two color classes, two complete-graph factors, and labeled functions are covered. Small-board checks corroborate but do not prove the infinite theorem. No independent validation or exhaustive publication-priority certification is asserted.
