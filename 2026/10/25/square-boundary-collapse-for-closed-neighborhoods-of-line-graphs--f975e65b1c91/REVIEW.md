For each G-edge e=uv, closed neighborhood in line graph is S_u∪S_v; clique complex Y is union of star simplices because triangle-free. Any face outside Y contains two disjoint G-edges a,b. Every center must join an endpoint of a to an endpoint of b; triangle-free implies at most two such connectors; two only if the face is an opposite-edge pair of a unique four-cycle. Retain all tetrahedral boundaries B_C on E(C). ## originality

PASS

Previously published girth>=5 theorem gives K=N[L(G)] collapses to Y=Cl(L(G)), but explicitly excludes four-cycles. The new exact relative collapse handles every finite connected triangle-free G, including arbitrarily overlapping four-cycles, and adds each tetrahedral boundary B_C; its homotopy splitting is Q(G) plus one S2 per four-cycle, even for K2,3. The known Y≃G and general graph Čech neighborhood formulas are acknowledged. A separate published square-completion theorem concerns the Čech nerve of G itself under additional no-K2,3 restriction, not L(G) with triangles, so does not imply this result directly. Best-of-knowledge novelty PASS, with residual equivalent nerve-model risk.
