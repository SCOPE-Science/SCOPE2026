For every finite connected triangle-free graph \(G\), the scale-two closed-neighborhood complex of its line graph collapses to the line-graph clique complex plus the tetrahedral boundary on each original four-cycle. Its homotopy type is the square-completion of \(G\) with one additional \(S^2\) wedged on for every four-cycle, even when squares overlap or \(G\) contains \(K_{2,3}\).
