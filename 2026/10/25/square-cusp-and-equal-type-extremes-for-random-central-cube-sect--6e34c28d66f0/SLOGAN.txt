A Haar-random central section of the cube with perimeter within \(\varepsilon\) of its maximum occurs with probability \(\frac{3\sqrt2}{8\pi}\varepsilon^2+O(\varepsilon^3)\). Conditioned on this rare event, two normalized orientation defects become independent uniform variables on \([0,1]\), and exactly half of the sections are asymptotically hexagons while half are quadrilaterals.
