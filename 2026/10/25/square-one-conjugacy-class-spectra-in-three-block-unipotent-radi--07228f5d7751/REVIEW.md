# Review

## Correctness
PASS. Direct multiplication gives the exact characteristic-two square-one condition \(XY=0\); linear-image decomposition gives the class exponent \(as+cr-rs\), and Gaussian coefficient counts enumerate all rank-constrained \((X,Y)\). The bound for maximal exponent is attained at the explicit rank pairs, including the equal outer dimension case. The independently constructed literal-conjugacy verifier matches all eight exhaustive configurations over \(\mathbb F_2\) and \(\mathbb F_4\). Its finite tests are not a general proof; the unrestricted proof is given in the result.

## Originality
PASS, with a serious residual priority risk. Diaconis–Thiem (arXiv:math/0610161v1, Section 5, Example 3 and Corollary 5.1) already establish the exact same three-block group and affine orbit framework. Those premises and the known rank-one Heisenberg special case are excluded from the asserted new contribution. Goodwin–Röhrle (arXiv:0901.0667v1) establishes polynomiality of the total class number for this broader group family, not the square-one conditional class-size enumerator. Soffer (arXiv:1411.5389v1) provides upper bounds rather than this exact conditional rank census. The contribution assessed here is the complete explicit class-size polynomial restricted to square-one elements with the sharp block-threshold and extremal-class counts. Those conclusions are not stated in the materially inspected sections, but may be derivable from existing orbit results through additional rank combinatorics; this priority risk is significant and needs independent verification.

## Value
PASS. In characteristic two, separating order-two from order-four conjugacy classes within a natural family of finite nilpotent groups is a structural question beyond counting all classes. The exact size-resolved polynomial describes all possible centralizer indices on the order-two locus, and the sharp middle-block threshold identifies a genuine change in which class sizes are possible. The answer is uniform over infinitely many block dimensions and even finite fields, not an arbitrary finite slice or an unsupported claim of major generality.

## Evidence and limitations
General conjugacy-orbit machinery is established previously. Exhaustive experiments concern only eight finite configurations. Publication priority and independent validation are unconfirmed; the theorem is not about odd characteristic, ambient conjugacy or order-four class-size distributions.
