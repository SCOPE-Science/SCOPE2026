For every three-block unipotent radical over an even finite field, the conjugacy classes of elements satisfying \(g^2=1\) admit an exact Gaussian-binomial rank-pair enumerator by class size. The largest such class jumps to size \(q^{ac}\) precisely when the middle block is at least as large as the smaller outer block; otherwise its sharp size is \(q^{b\max(a,c)}\).
