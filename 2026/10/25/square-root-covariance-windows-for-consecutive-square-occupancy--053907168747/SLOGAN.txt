For independently and uniformly allocated balls, exact-load counts at every consecutive-square pair \(a^2,(a+1)^2\) have a critical positive-covariance window centered at \(a(a+1)m\) of asymptotic half-width \(\sqrt{a(a+1)m}\). Their covariance has the universal rescaled profile \(e^{-2\lambda}\lambda^{r+s}(1-z^2/\lambda)/(r!s!)\), revealing order-one dependence invisible in the leading fixed-density limit.
