For every finite commutative local ring with square-zero maximal ideal, the probability that \(k\) uniformly chosen ideals have zero product is exactly \(1-[1+k(L-2)]/L^k\), where \(L\) is the number of ideals. A nonfield has the same probability as a field exactly when \(k=2\) and its radical is two-dimensional over \(\mathbb F_2\), giving eight-element counterexamples to a recent field-characterization conjecture.
