For spiders with at least three arms, all of length at least three, the stable-range higher-independence complex has homotopy type \(\bigvee^{\binom{b-1}{r-N-1}}S^{r-1}\) whenever \(0\le r-N-1\le b-1\), and is otherwise contractible. Its exact integral arm-permutation homology module is \(\det(\mathbb Z^J)\otimes\bigwedge^{r-N-1}U_Z\), revealing a parity-dependent symmetry twist invisible to sphere counts.
