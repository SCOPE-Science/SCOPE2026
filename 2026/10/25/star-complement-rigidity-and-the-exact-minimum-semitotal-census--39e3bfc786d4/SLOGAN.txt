For \(p,q\ge2\), every minimum semitotal dominating set of the once-subdivided biclique \(\operatorname{S}(K_{p,q})\) is an independent complement-of-a-star configuration except at \((p,q)=(2,2)\), yielding the exact trivariate optimizer census and \(p(2^q-q-2)+q(2^p-p-2)\) minima outside that corner. The eight-cycle corner has exactly 30 minima, only two independent; the minimum-cardinality formula itself was already covered by a general subdivision theorem.
