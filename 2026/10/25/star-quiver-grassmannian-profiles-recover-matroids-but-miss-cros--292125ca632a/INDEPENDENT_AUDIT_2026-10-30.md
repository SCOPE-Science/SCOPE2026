# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For each leaf subset S, the subrepresentation scheme is the functor of d-subspaces containing L_S; the quotient W/L_S identifies it with Gr(d-h(S),r-h(S)) including empty cases. Minimal nonempty d recovers h(S), and the q-count transform t^h G_{r-h} has unit triangular diagonal, recovering A_{s,h} and hence the Tutte polynomial. Fixing three labeled projective points forces any endomorphism to be scalar, and a fourth lambda-line distinguishes all bricks. Independently tested all 48 indexed cases for each lambda=2,3,4 over F5, but the all-fields theorem rests on scheme functor and projective rigidity.
