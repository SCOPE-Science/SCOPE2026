For an inward star representation with one-dimensional leaves, every indexed quiver Grassmannian is either empty or an ordinary Grassmannian determined by the rank function of the arrow-vector matroid; over finite fields, the coarse point-count profile is equivalent to the matroid's Tutte polynomial. Yet a cross-ratio family of pairwise nonisomorphic bricks has identical indexed abstract Grassmannian schemes, so these geometric invariants do not recover its representation parameter.
