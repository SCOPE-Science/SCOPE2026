Under the stylic-to-Catalan quotient, each one-step transformation supported on \(b\) consecutive runs has exactly \(B_b\) preimages, with exactly \(\left\{\begin{smallmatrix}b\\h\end{smallmatrix}\right\}\) preimages represented by \(N\)-tableaux with \(h\) rows. A canonical bijection sends these preimages to set partitions of the run starts, so even idempotent one-step transformations can have large Bell-number fibers.
