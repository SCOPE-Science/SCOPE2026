# Mathematical review: stretch-invariant angular susceptibility

## Correctness — PASS

For every fixed positive triple of orthogonal axis scales, an exact enumeration of the 24 rhombic-dodecahedral edges gives six section edges except in one of the six strict coordinate-dominance cones, where it gives eight. The spherical solid-angle identity evaluates the normalized combined area of these cones as the displayed formula for the octagon probability. Their geodesic boundary arcs have cyclic lengths \(\delta_a,\delta_b,\delta_c\), and the rigorously derived identities \(\cot\delta_a=bc/(a\sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2})\) imply \(\delta_a+\delta_b+\delta_c=\pi\). Therefore the total shared boundary length is exactly \(8\pi\), independent of the scales. The tubular crossing calculation with normalized spherical area and mean absolute tangent-direction cosine \(2/\pi\) gives \(4t/\pi+O(t^2)\) for changing section type, and reversibility yields equal directed rates. The sphere partition is finite; junction contributions are \(O(t^2)\). An independently generated edge list and numerical spherical integration checked all identities for multiple unequal parameter triples. Limits as any axis collapses, and uniformity across degenerating families, are **not** claimed.

## Originality — PASS on the inspected record, with a stated residual risk

Hausel, Makai and Szűcs (1999) treat rhombic-dodecahedral covering properties, not random central-section side-count or perturbation response. Matteo (2014) gives combinatorial two-orbit polytope and honeycomb classification, not Haar central plane incidence statistics. Full mathematical reports for published rectangular-box and icosahedron central-section laws were examined and concern different polytopes with different normal-sphere partitions. The rectangular-box report already uses the triangle-inequality chamber, but its four/six section types and solid-angle calculation do not imply an octagon probability for this different polytope, still less the unexpectedly invariant interface length \(8\pi\). The standard solid-angle and local crossing formulas are acknowledged tools, not new assertions. Search records did not identify an implication-dominating version of this precise three-parameter invariant. This is best-knowledge originality rather than a claim to have comprehensively settled historical priority, especially in potentially unindexed integral geometry.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The result identifies an exactly measurable shape perturbation response, invariant under **all** positive orthogonal axis stretches of the rhombic dodecahedron, even though the individual hexagon and octagon proportions vary continuously and approach an endpoint in anisotropic limits. Its geometric explanation is a non-obvious spherical boundary-length identity, not a substitution into a standard probability calculation. The exact side-count distribution and the distinct invariant linear response make an informative stereological pair of statistics and distinguish frequency from sensitivity. The rigidity of the protected quantity is a natural phenomenon for a classical space-filling solid, rather than a numerical isolated special case.
