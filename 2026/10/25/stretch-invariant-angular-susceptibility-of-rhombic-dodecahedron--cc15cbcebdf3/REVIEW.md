## correctness

PASS

For all fixed a,b,c>0, direct incidence of 24 edges with a generic central plane gives 6 sides unless one of a|u_1|,b|u_2|,c|u_3| exceeds the sum of the other two, in which case it gives 8. Six signed-axis spherical chambers split into equal spherical triangles and yield the arctangent p_8 formula. Each cone has four great-circle boundary arcs with lengths delta_i obeying cot(delta_a)cot(delta_b)+...=1 and delta_a+delta_b+delta_c=pi, hence total interface length 8pi even after anisotropic stretches. For a uniformly random geodesic step of angle t, the spherical first-crossing probability is t L/(2pi^2)+O_{a,b,c}(t^2)=4t/pi+O(t^2); spherical reversibility gives exactly equal directed transitions each 2t/pi+O(t^2). Direct independent 24-edge classifications matched for 24,000 random normals at each of four anisotropic scales; perimeter was 8pi to floating error. The finite checks are not used to infer the all-scale formula.
