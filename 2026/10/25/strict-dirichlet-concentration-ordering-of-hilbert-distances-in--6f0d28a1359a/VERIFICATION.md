The proof identifies simplex Hilbert distance as the range of independent logarithmic gamma ratios, factors their characteristic functions into independent Bernoulli–Laplace terms, and applies an even log-concave translated-convex-set probability bound with an analyticity argument for strictness. Numerical reproducibility: run `python check.py` after installing NumPy and SciPy. `check.log` records the check outcomes. The comparison assumes the full logarithmic cross-ratio convention; half-cross-ratio distances and thresholds must both be halved.
