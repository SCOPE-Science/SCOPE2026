Along the nested stable-set polytopes of complete split graphs, the affine-invariant isotropic constant increases strictly at every step from the cube to the simplex. The exact determinant arises from a beta-slice covariance shear, and when the clique occupies an asymptotic fraction \(\alpha\) of the coordinates the constant tends to \(e^{-\alpha}/12^{(1-\alpha)/2}\).
