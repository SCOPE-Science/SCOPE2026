The canonical signohedron \(\Sigma_d=\{x:\|x\|_1+\|x\|_\infty\le1\}\) has exact volume \(2^d/(d+1)!\) and a harmonic-number formula for its uniform-volume covariance. Its affine-invariant isotropic constant is strictly below that of the \(d\)-cross-polytope for every \(d\ge2\), with the sharp relative deficit \(1-L(\Sigma_d)/L(\Diamond_d)\sim(\log d)^2/(4d)\).
