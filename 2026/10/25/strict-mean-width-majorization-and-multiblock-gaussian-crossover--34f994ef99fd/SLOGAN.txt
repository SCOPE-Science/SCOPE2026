For complete multipartite stable-set polytopes with any fixed \(r\ge3\) vertex classes, Euclidean mean width strictly increases when the class-size profile becomes more unequal; the balanced partition uniquely minimizes it and one large class with singletons uniquely maximizes it. The near-balanced correction is an explicit \(r\)-Gaussian maximum, and the ratio of the extreme widths converges to \(r\).
