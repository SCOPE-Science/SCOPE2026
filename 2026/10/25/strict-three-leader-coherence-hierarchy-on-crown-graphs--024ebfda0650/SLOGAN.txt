For the unweighted crown graph \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M_n\) with \(n\ge4\), the noise-free three-leader coherence cost has a strict three-class ranking: three leaders on the same bipartition side always beat a mixed triple containing a deleted-matching pair, which always beats a mixed triple containing no such pair. Exactly \(2\binom{n}{3}\) leader triples are optimal, with explicit rational costs and positive gap formulas for every class.
