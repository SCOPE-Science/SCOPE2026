For the complete prism \(K_n\square K_2\), a vertex set is strong geodetic exactly when it meets every matching column and either fills at least one column or uses at least two vertices in each layer. This yields every cardinality count, the exact inclusion-minimal polynomial, and a sharp minimum-set token-jumping transition: six isolated states at \(n=4\), but an isometric connected middle-layer Boolean cube of diameter \(n\) for every \(n\ge5\).
