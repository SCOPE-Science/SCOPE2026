For every \(n\ge3\), the full strong discrete Morse matching complex of \(K_{2,n}\) has maximal simplices of dimension \(n-1\), yet it is homotopy equivalent to exactly \(3n-1\) circles. Its integral first homology is the sum-zero lattice of three right-vertex permutation modules, so the rational character at a permutation \(\sigma\) is \(3\#\operatorname{Fix}(\sigma)-1\).
