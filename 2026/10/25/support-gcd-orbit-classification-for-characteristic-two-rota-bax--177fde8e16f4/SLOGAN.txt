The weight-zero Rota–Baxter operators on nilpotent evolution chains over characteristic-two finite fields have an exact automorphism-conjugacy census: for every terminal coefficient and nonzero-coordinate support, gcd formulas determine both the number and sizes of orbits. An arithmetic divisor sum involving \(\operatorname{ord}_d(2)\) counts all classes; the totals are \(3\cdot2^{n-2}\) over \(\mathbb F_2\) and \(q+1\) in dimension two over \(\mathbb F_q\).
