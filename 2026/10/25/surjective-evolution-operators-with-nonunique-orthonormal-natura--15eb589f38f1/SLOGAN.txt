For each \(m\ge2\), a Hilbert evolution algebra has dense algebra square and a surjective bounded evolution operator with \((m-1)\)-dimensional kernel, yet its orthonormal natural bases have the complete positive-dimensional moduli \(O(m)/B_m\). Injectivity on \(\ell^1\), rather than surjectivity or density alone, guarantees orthonormal natural-basis rigidity.
