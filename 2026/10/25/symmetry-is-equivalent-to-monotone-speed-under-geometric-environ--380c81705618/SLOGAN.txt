For mean-balanced i.i.d. nearest-neighbor random environments with geometric complete refresh, the effective velocity is monotone in the refresh probability if and only if the site law is reflection-symmetric; otherwise both endpoint speeds vanish while the interior speed is nonzero. Its first nonzero full-refresh term detects the first odd centered site moment \(m_{2r+1}\) at the sharp order \((1-p)^{4r}\), with coefficient \(2m_{2r+1}\mathbb E[(Y_1+Y_2)^{2r}]\).
