The ten disjoint-triangle product tori in the ordinary unordered two-point configuration space of \(K_6\) generate \(H_2\cong\mathbb Z^9\) with exactly one alternating relation, so any nine are an integral basis. Under all vertex permutations, this primitive relation transforms by the sign character, giving an integral \(S_6\)-module quotient of a rank-ten signed torus permutation lattice.
