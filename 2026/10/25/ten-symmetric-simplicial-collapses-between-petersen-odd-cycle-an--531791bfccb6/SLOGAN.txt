For the Petersen graph, the complex of induced bipartite vertex sets simplicially collapses to the induced-forest complex through exactly ten symmetry-permuted free-face pairs. Their common homotopy type is \(\bigvee^{6}S^{5}\), and the induced \(\mathfrak S_5\)-action on fifth rational homology is the irreducible \(\bigwedge^{2}V\).
