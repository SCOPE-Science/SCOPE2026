The natural tetrahedral \(S_4\)-action on the truncated-tetrahedron graph-metric Rips filtration has rational degree-one module \(\varepsilon\otimes V\) at scale one and degree-two module \(\mathbf1\oplus\varepsilon\oplus V\) at scale two, where \(V\) is the standard three-dimensional representation and \(\varepsilon\) is sign. The sign summand makes the rotational quotient carry two rational two-dimensional homology classes while the full-symmetry quotient carries only one.
