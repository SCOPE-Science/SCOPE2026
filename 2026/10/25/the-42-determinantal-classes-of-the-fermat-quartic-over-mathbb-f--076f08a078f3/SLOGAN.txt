For the smooth Fermat quartic \(X^4+Y^4+Z^4=0\) over \(\mathbb F_5\), there are exactly \(42\) inequivalent linear determinantal representations: \(16\) admit a symmetric representative and \(26\) do not. Its Jacobian has group \(\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z\oplus(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^4\); this explains the arithmetic theta count underlying the classification.
