In characteristic three, genus-five Artin–Schreier covers have only three possible Jacobian a-numbers: \(1\), \(2\), and \(3\), with nonempty strata of dimensions \(4\), \(3\), and \(2\). In the two-pole normal form \(y^3-y=x^4+a x^2+b x+c/x\) with \(c
e0\), the a-number jumps from \(2\) to \(3\) exactly on the reduced coefficient divisor \(a=0\).
