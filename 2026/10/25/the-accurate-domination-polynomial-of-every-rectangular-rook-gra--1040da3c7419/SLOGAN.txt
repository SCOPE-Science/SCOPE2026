Every accurately dominating set of the rectangular rook graph \(K_p\square K_q\) is governed by a sharp half-order dichotomy: below or at half the vertices it must contain a full row and a full column, while above half ordinary domination suffices. This yields an explicit accurate-domination polynomial, \(\gamma_a=p+q-1\), and all minimum-set counts, including exceptional two-row and \(3\)-by-\(3\) boards.
