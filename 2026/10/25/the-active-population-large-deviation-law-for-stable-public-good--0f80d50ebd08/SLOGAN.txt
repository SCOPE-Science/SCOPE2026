For \(\delta\in(1/\varphi,1)\), stable equilibria on a path have an exact active-population count and an explicit exponential rate profile. Under uniform selection among stable equilibria, the active fraction concentrates near \(\alpha_*=0.5569404629\ldots\); for \(\alpha\in[1/2,3/5]\), its rate is \(I(\alpha)=\log\lambda-(2-3\alpha)H((2\alpha-1)/(2-3\alpha))\), with \(\lambda^5=\lambda^3+1\).
