For every real \(b>0\), the generalized Hilbert operator on \(H^2\) has Fredholm essential spectrum exactly the completed complex beta-function curve \(\{B(\tfrac12+i\tau,b+\tfrac12-i\tau):\tau\in\mathbb R\}\cup\{0\}\). Unlike the classical Hilbert matrix, this curve contains nonreal points for every positive deformation parameter.
