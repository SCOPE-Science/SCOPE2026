For the genus-three dihedral pencil \(y^2=x^8+a x^4+1\) in characteristic \(11\), the Jacobian has \(p\)-rank \(1\) at \(a^2=5\), \(2\) at \(a=0\) or \(a^2\in\{3,-1\}\), and \(3\) otherwise on smooth fibers. The Hasse divisor on the parameter line has exactly two double points and five simple points, and no fiber has \(p\)-rank zero.
