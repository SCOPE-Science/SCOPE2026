In characteristic five, the two Hasse parabolas of the normalized bielliptic genus-two family meet in one smooth superspecial parameter and three singular triple-root boundary points. For every \(q=5^n\), its smooth marked coefficient pairs have Jacobian \(5\)-rank counts \(1\), \(2q-6-2(-1)^n\), and \(q^2-3q+6+2(-1)^n\) in ranks zero, one, and two, respectively.
