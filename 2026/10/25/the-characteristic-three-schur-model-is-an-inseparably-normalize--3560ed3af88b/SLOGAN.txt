The literal Schur quartic equation in characteristic \(3\) becomes a nonnormal ruled quartic whose normalization is \(\mathbb F_2\), with a purely inseparable degree-three map over its singular directrix. It has exactly \(q+3\) lines and \((q+1)^2\) points over every \(\mathbb F_q\) of characteristic \(3\), unlike the smooth characteristic-three good reduction model.
