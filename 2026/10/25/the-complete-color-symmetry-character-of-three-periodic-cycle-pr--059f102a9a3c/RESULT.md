# The complete color-symmetry character of three-periodic cycle-product homology

## Finding

Let \(\ell\ge1\), \(n\ge2\), and let \(X_{\ell,n}=\operatorname{Ind}(C_{3\ell}\times K_n)\), where the product is categorical and the second factor consists of \(n\) colors. The symmetric group \(S_n\) acts by permuting colors. By the established nonequivariant homotopy classification, the only nonzero reduced rational homology is in degree \(2\ell-1\). For every \(\sigma\in S_n\), write \(f(\sigma)=|\{c\in[n]:\sigma(c)=c\}|\). Its entire character is

\[
\operatorname{tr}\!\left(\sigma\mid\widetilde H_{2\ell-1}(X_{\ell,n};\mathbb Q)\right)
=3\bigl(f(\sigma)-1\bigr)^\ell+(-1)^\ell\bigl(f(\sigma)-1\bigr).
\]

Equivalently, for the rational standard augmentation representation \(V_n=\ker(\mathbb Q^n\xrightarrow{\mathrm{sum}}\mathbb Q)\), the following identity holds in the rational representation ring:

\[
[\widetilde H_{2\ell-1}(X_{\ell,n};\mathbb Q)]
=3[V_n^{\otimes\ell}]+(-1)^\ell[V_n].
\]

The difference for odd \(\ell\) denotes a virtual-module identity, not a formal direct-sum decomposition. It is represented by an actual module: \(V_n^{\otimes\ell}\) contains \(V_n\), using invariant pairings when \(\ell\) is odd. In particular, the character depends only on the number of individually fixed colors, even on derangements with distinct cycle structures.

A structural statement underlying the character calculation holds for any nonempty finite simple graph \(G\). If \(\sigma\in S_n\) fixes \(f\ge1\) colors, then the geometric fixed-point space \(|\operatorname{Ind}(G\times K_n)|^{\langle\sigma\rangle}\) simplicially collapses, in its orbit-complex model, onto \(\operatorname{Ind}(G\times K_f)\). If \(\sigma\) has no fixed color and has \(t\) disjoint color cycles, the fixed space is naturally homeomorphic to \(|\operatorname{Ind}(G[\overline K_t])|\), where \(G[\overline K_t]\) replaces every vertex of \(G\) by \(t\) pairwise nonadjacent clones, joining two clone groups completely exactly when the base vertices are adjacent.

## Assumptions and scope

All graphs are finite and loopless. For graphs \(G,H\), the categorical product \(G\times H\) has adjacent vertices \((u,a),(v,b)\) exactly when \(uv\in E(G)\) and \(ab\in E(H)\). The complex \(\operatorname{Ind}(G)\) consists of all independent vertex subsets, including the empty face for chain conventions. Geometric fixed points refer to the fixed points in the geometric realization under the cyclic subgroup generated by \(\sigma\); they are not the subcomplex on individually fixed vertices. The character formula is for rational homology and for the color \(S_n\)-action, not for simultaneous rotations or reflections of the cycle.

## Proof

**1. Orbit-complex model.** Write the orbits of \(\sigma\) on colors as \(O_1,\dots,O_s\). Each orbit of vertices under its action on \(G\times K_n\) has the form \(\{v\}\times O_i\). This is an independent set because \(G\) has no loops. A fixed point of a simplex has constant barycentric coordinate on each color orbit. Thus the geometric fixed space is naturally the realization of the simplicial complex of compatible unions of such orbits. This is the independence complex of a quotient graph \(H\) with vertices \((v,O_i)\). If \(vw\in E(G)\), two such vertices are adjacent in \(H\) exactly when some colors in their respective orbits differ. No two vertices over the same base vertex are adjacent.

**2. Collapse with fixed colors.** Suppose \(\{c\}\) is a singleton orbit. For any nonsingleton color orbit \(O\) and any base vertex \(v\),
\[
N_H((v,\{c\}))\subseteq N_H((v,O)).
\]
Indeed, any neighbor over \(w\) of \((v,\{c\})\) has \(vw\in E(G)\) and its color orbit contains a color distinct from \(c\). Since \(|O|\ge2\), some color of \(O\) differs from some color in that neighbor orbit, giving adjacency to \((v,O)\). Moreover, \((v,O)\) and \((v,\{c\})\) are nonadjacent. The standard independence-complex folding collapse therefore deletes \((v,O)\): every independent face containing it can be extended by \((v,\{c\})\). Repeating over all nonsingleton color orbits leaves the quotient graph induced by singleton colors, precisely \(G\times K_f\). This proves the asserted simplicial-collapse equivalence; it works with isolated vertices too.

**3. No fixed colors.** If every orbit contains at least two colors, the adjacency condition across each edge of \(G\) holds for every pair of orbit labels, including equal labels. Consequently \(H=G[\overline K_t]\) exactly, proving the second fixed-space identification.

**4. Reduced Lefschetz calculation.** For a finite simplicial automorphism, barycentric subdivision identifies the reduced Lefschetz number with the reduced Euler characteristic of its geometric fixed-point space. Since the published homotopy theorem concentrates reduced homology of \(X_{\ell,n}\) in the odd degree \(2\ell-1\),
\[
\operatorname{tr}(\sigma\mid\widetilde H_{2\ell-1})=-\widetilde\chi\bigl(|X_{\ell,n}|^{\langle\sigma\rangle}\bigr).
\]
For \(f\ge2\), the collapse and the published homotopy count give this trace as
\[
B_\ell(f)=3(f-1)^\ell+(-1)^\ell(f-1).
\]
For \(f=1\), the remaining categorical product with \(K_1\) has no edges, so its independence complex is a simplex; the trace is zero, agreeing with \(B_\ell(1)\).

For \(f=0\), let \(I_Y(z)=\sum_{A\in\operatorname{Ind}(Y)}z^{|A|}\) be the independence polynomial (including the empty set). In \(C_{3\ell}[\overline K_t]\), an occupied base vertex can carry any nonempty subset of its \(t\) clones. At \(z=-1\) its total weight is \(\sum_{j=1}^t\binom tj(-1)^j=-1\). Hence
\[
I_{C_{3\ell}[\overline K_t]}(-1)=I_{C_{3\ell}}(-1).
\]
The transfer matrix \(T=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}\) for binary occupation of a cycle satisfies \(I_{C_m}(-1)=\operatorname{tr}(T^m)\). Its eigenvalues are \(e^{\pm i\pi/3}\), so \(I_{C_{3\ell}}(-1)=2(-1)^\ell\). Since \(-\widetilde\chi(\operatorname{Ind}(Y))=I_Y(-1)\), the derangement trace is \(2(-1)^\ell=B_\ell(0)\). The character formula therefore holds for every permutation.

**5. Representation identity.** The permutation representation \(\mathbb Q^n\) has character \(f(\sigma)\). Its augmentation subrepresentation \(V_n\) therefore has character \(f(\sigma)-1\). Characters multiply under tensor powers; the formula above is precisely the character of \(3[V_n^{\otimes\ell}]+(-1)^\ell[V_n]\), which identifies the rational representation class. For odd \(\ell\), the \(S_n\)-invariant positive-definite inner product on \(V_n\) supplies an invariant nonzero tensor in \(V_n^{\otimes(\ell-1)}\), embedding \(V_n\) in \(V_n^{\otimes\ell}\) and showing that the virtual expression has nonnegative multiplicities.

## Verification

Run `python verify_color_character.py` with Python 3 and the standard library. The program constructs the actual graph of color orbits and exactly computes its independence polynomial at \(-1\) by a vertex-deletion recurrence. Independently, for small graphs it enumerates invariant original independent faces and computes their signed simplicial traces using the orientation of the induced vertex permutation. Across 192 test actions with cycle lengths \(3,6,9\) and two through five colors, the independently obtained values equal the stated character polynomial. The direct signed-face checks cover 11,504 invariant independent faces in total. The finite checks are corroboration; the all-parameter proof is the orbit-collapse and Lefschetz argument above.

## Relationship to prior work

Antolín Camarena and Carnero Bravo established the nonequivariant homotopy types of \(\operatorname{Ind}(C_{3\ell}\times K_n)\) for every \(\ell\ge1\) and \(n\ge2\). Their sphere count is \(B_\ell(n)\), the present character at the identity. That rank does not determine the action of other permutations. Goyal, Shukla and Singh had studied products of complete graphs and independently motivated the later cycle-product classification; their results also do not determine the color representation here. The present contribution is the fixed-orbit reduction for graph products and the resulting complete \(S_n\)-character. It concerns the color action, distinct from the cyclic action on the cycle coordinate.

## Limitations

The result is a rational character classification, not a canonical integral lattice decomposition or the combined \(S_n\times D_{3\ell}\) character. It does not determine the homotopy type of the clone-blowup fixed space for arbitrary base graphs beyond the explicit orbit-graph identification. The novelty comparison cannot exclude unindexed equivalent formulations or results not accessible to bibliographic searches. The pre-existing nonequivariant homotopy theorem is used as an input, not reproved.
