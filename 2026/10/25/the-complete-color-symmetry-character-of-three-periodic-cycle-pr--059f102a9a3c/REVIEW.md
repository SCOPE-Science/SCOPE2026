# Scientific review of the color-symmetry character

## Correctness

**PASS.** The fixed-point orbit complex is constructed explicitly, and the two cases of color-cycle decomposition are proved. With an individually fixed color, each nonsingleton orbit vertex is dominated by the vertex of that fixed color above the same base vertex, producing a genuine simplicial collapse. Without fixed colors, all orbit labels are joined across any base edge, giving the exact clone-blowup graph. The reduced Lefschetz sign is negative because the published homology degree is odd. The known sphere rank, the transfer-matrix trace for the fixed-color-free case, and the permutation-augmentation character together give the formula for all \(\ell\ge1,n\ge2\). Independent integer computations corroborate 192 small actions; the proof does not infer the universal result from those experiments.

## Originality

**PASS, with residual bibliographic uncertainty.** The nearest primary theorem (Antolín Camarena–Carnero Bravo, arXiv:2307.12401, Theorem 22) determines the nonequivariant homotopy type, not the action of color permutations. Goyal–Shukla–Singh (arXiv:1905.06926, Proposition 3.2) concerns complete-graph categorical products and also does not classify the present representation. Related results about cycle-coordinate rotations do not imply the commuting color \(S_n\)-character. Comparisons addressed the orbit-fixed formulation, standard augmentation tensor powers, stronger equivariant coverage, and potentially identical rank formulas. A hidden equivalent calculation remains possible.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The complete rational representation is determined uniformly for two unbounded parameters, by a character polynomial in a single intrinsic permutation statistic. Distinct nonidentity permutations can have different traces despite the same known Betti number, and derangements of different cycle types have the same explicit trace. The underlying fixed-space collapse also holds for every finite simple base graph, making the result a structural equivariant-topological reduction rather than a numerical tabulation.

## Scope and risks

The color action is treated, not the full product action with cycle symmetries; the identity is in the rational representation ring and does not identify an integral equivariant lattice. The 2023 classification is a necessary published input. Unindexed equivalent literature is the remaining originality risk.
