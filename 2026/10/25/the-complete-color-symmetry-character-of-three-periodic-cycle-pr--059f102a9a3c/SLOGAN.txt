For \(X=\operatorname{Ind}(C_{3\ell}\times K_n)\), the complete color-permutation character on its unique reduced rational homology group is \(\chi(\sigma)=3(f(\sigma)-1)^\ell+(-1)^\ell(f(\sigma)-1)\), where \(f(\sigma)\) counts fixed colors. The formula follows from a geometric fixed-point folding theorem for arbitrary graph products, and refines the known nonequivariant sphere count.
