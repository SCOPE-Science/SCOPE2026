For the octahedral graph \(K_{2,2,2}\), the full signed-permutation group \(C_2\wr S_3\) acts on the \(675\)-dimensional fourth rational homology of its ordinary discrete Morse complex with an explicitly determined ten-class character and all ten irreducible multiplicities. The full-symmetry invariant subspace has dimension \(11\), while central antipodal inversion has eigenspaces of dimensions \(325\) and \(350\); these symmetry distinctions are not determined by the previously known homotopy type alone.
