Consecutive ones of the Baum–Sweet sequence have an exact Fibonacci-counted dyadic-prefix gap histogram, with possible differences precisely \(1,2,3,6,9,18,33,66,\ldots\). In the limit over dyadic prefixes, the mass at each distinct higher-scale gap \(G_m=2^{m-2}+1+\mathbf1_{2\mid m}\) is \(\varphi^{-m}\) for \(m\ge4\); the gap of length three has mass \(\varphi^{-1}\), and the resulting return-gap law has infinite mean.
