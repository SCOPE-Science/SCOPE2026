For every prime-field multiplicative subgroup of order \(13\), the shortest vanishing sum of subgroup elements has length \(3\) precisely for primes \(53,2731\), length \(4\) precisely for \(79,131,157,1327,2003,7229,398581\), and length at least \(5\) otherwise. An exact census of \(21\) affine-orbit cyclotomic norms proves the unrestricted classification.
