Exact integer determinants calculate the cyclotomic norms. Exhaustive affine orbit enumeration covers every one of the \(286+1001\) admissible multisets. All sixteen verified norms and their complete prime factorizations imply the stated prime sets. The one- and two-term exclusions follow directly from odd subgroup order. The bundled checker was run successfully, with a separate sixteen-case SymPy polynomial-resultant cross-check and seven prime checks; the infinitude of primes is handled by the resultant equivalence, not numerical extrapolation.
