For equally arclength-spaced polygons inscribed in a smooth strictly convex curve, the rescaled small-chord boundary-shortcut probability has a second-order term involving both \(\int\kappa^4\) and the negative gradient energy \(-\int(\kappa')^2\). Two ovals with identical arclength curvature histograms have opposite signs of this correction, so it detects oscillation information that every curvature moment misses.
