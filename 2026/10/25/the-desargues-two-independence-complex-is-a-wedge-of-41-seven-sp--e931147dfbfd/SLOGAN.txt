The two-independence (three-path-free) complex of the twenty-vertex Desargues graph has integral homotopy type \(|\operatorname{Ind}_2(\operatorname{GP}(10,3))|\simeq\bigvee^{41}S^7\). A complete acyclic discrete-Morse matching and an integral unimodular attaching-map computation establish the exact wedge decomposition.
