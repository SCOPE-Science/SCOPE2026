For three-point subsets of every dihedral group, the product--quotient size discrepancy can only be \(-1\), \(0\), \(1\), or \(3\), with an exact formula for every value and rotation/reflection type. As the group grows, the product-dominant, balanced, and quotient-dominant proportions tend to \(3/4\), \(1/8\), and \(1/8\).
