For ordinary FirstFit on a uniformly randomly ordered labelled crown graph, the entire number-of-colours distribution is explicit: exactly two colours are used with probability \(1-1/n\), and every higher-colour tail is a factorial ratio governed by a factor-three linear-extension recurrence. In particular, the expected number is \(2+1/n+O(n^{-2})\), despite a worst-case count of \(n\) colours.
