For two uniformly sampled points on any convex polyhedral surface, the short **ambient Euclidean** pair-distance distribution is exactly quartic in radius, with a strictly positive cubic coefficient \(\frac{4}{3A^2}\sum_e L_e((\pi-\alpha_e)/\sin\alpha_e-1)\). This coefficient detects the edge-weighted bending of the surface and sharply dominates \(\frac{2}{9A^2}\sum_e L_e(\pi-\alpha_e)^2\).
