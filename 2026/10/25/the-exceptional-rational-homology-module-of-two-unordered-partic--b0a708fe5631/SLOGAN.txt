For two distinct unlabeled points on the Heawood incidence graph, the Fano collineation group acts on rational second homology as the direct sum of the exceptional rational six-dimensional module and the eight-dimensional Steinberg module. The degree-two character is \((14,-2,-1,2,0)\) on elements of orders \(1,2,3,4,7\), respectively, and has no invariant line.
