The least mean needed for a homogeneous binomial count to make its \(r\)-th point mass exceed its zero mass has a complete optimizer phase diagram: finite trial counts \(n= r\) through \(r=4\), the unique sequence \(7,10,15,25,51,294\) for \(r=5,\ldots,10\), then an unattained Poisson-limit infimum \((r!)^{1/r}\) for every \(r\ge11\). The transition follows from strict concavity of the geometric mean of consecutive linear factors.
