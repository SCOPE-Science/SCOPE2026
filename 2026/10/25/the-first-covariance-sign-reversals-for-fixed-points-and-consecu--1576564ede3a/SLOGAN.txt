Fixed-point weighting of a uniform random permutation first exhibits opposite finite-size responses among consecutive rank-pattern counts at length four. Exactly three four-letter patterns reverse the sign of their fixed-point covariance as the permutation size grows: \(2143\) at sizes \(4,5,6\), \(4231\) at \(5,6,7\), and the delayed reversal of \(3142\) between \(17\) and \(18\).
