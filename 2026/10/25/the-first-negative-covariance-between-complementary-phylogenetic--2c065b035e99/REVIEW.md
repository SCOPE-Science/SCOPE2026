# Review of the complementary phylogenetic-diversity covariance threshold

## Correctness
PASS. Every asserted universal quantifier ranges over the finite family of unrooted binary unit-edge trees with four through thirteen leaves and nontrivial sample sizes; the fourteen-leaf uniqueness assertion is only for a six-versus-eight split. Edge-split counting gives an exact integer formula for diversity and cross moments. Every unlabelled shape is generated by edge subdivision from the three-leaf tree, and exact AHU-style center encodings identify isomorphism. The supplied deterministic program exhausts the stated domain and independently verifies the caterpillar by direct subtree pruning. The sums \(3003,47619,55627,882033\) imply the stated negative covariance exactly. No statement about arbitrary tree size follows from the finite computation.

## Originality
PASS subject to residual literature risk. Hardy–Senterre (2007) concerns diversity partitioning by mean divergence; Nipperess–Matsen (2013) establishes marginal rarefaction means and variances, not the cross covariance of two complementary samples or the fourteen-leaf first failure of positivity. The compared published literature and database records did not establish a stronger implication containing this finite sign theorem. The exact published rarefaction framework is acknowledged, not rebranded as new. A less discoverable result on covariance of random complementary subtrees could overlap the claim, so independent bibliographic confirmation remains desirable.

## Mathematical value
PASS. A pair of disjoint reserves drawn by uniform leaf partition need not have negatively correlated retained evolutionary branch lengths merely because the reserves compete for species; remarkably the covariance is strictly positive on every small unit-length binary topology through thirteen taxa and first turns negative at fourteen. The sharp boundary and unique fourteen-leaf exception describe a natural finite structural phenomenon, not an arbitrary fitted numeric example. The result is an exact benchmark for complementary-reserve sampling and motivates careful distinction between independent and complementary sampling.

## Limitations and close literature
The outcome is specific to the unrooted unit-length model and has no claimed robustness for continuous edge weights, larger trees, nonuniform partitions, or inferred phylogenies. Checked closest sources: Hardy and Senterre, Journal of Ecology (2007), DOI 10.1111/j.1365-2745.2007.01222.x; Nipperess and Matsen, Methods in Ecology and Evolution (2013), DOI 10.1111/2041-210X.12042. The finite census and independent witness check are reproducible by running the included verifier.
