# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For the ordinary nonperiodic knight-attack graph on n×n chessboards, N1 and N2 have no edges and N3 has an isolated central vertex, hence Ind(Nn) is contractible for n<=3. On n4, an independently constructed 24-edge graph yields exactly 1364 nonempty independent faces with f=(16,96,276,412,340,170,48,6). Reimplementing the explicit 16-pivot face matching yields 679 disjoint matched cover pairs and exactly six critical faces (one vertex, five 3-simplices). Reversing precisely those paired incidences in all 5772 directed Hasse covers yields an acyclic graph by full topological sorting, proving integrally Ind(N4) homotopy equivalent to a wedge of five S3 by discrete Morse theory. Separate signed fixed-face Lefschetz traces of all eight D4 board symmetries give H3 character (5,-1,1,-1,3,-1,3,-1), which decomposes over Q as 1+2η+ρ for η(r)=-1,η(s)=1. This is not a mod-two-only conclusion; no equivariant Morse matching is asserted.

## originality

PASS

The same nonperiodic knight graphs are analyzed by Cibu et al. (2024), but their principal results are gonality rather than independence-complex topology or the D4 module; the much older square-lattice independence-complex theorem uses nearest-neighbor adjacency, not knight moves. Related published four-cube graph independence calculations concern a 32-edge periodic 4×4 knight graph rather than this 24-edge ordinary board, and therefore cannot imply the five-sphere and character statement. Priority remains qualified against unindexed chess-graph topology calculations.

## value

PASS

The first noncontractible ordinary square-board knight independence complex is a canonical small graph-topology transition, and its complete integral homotopy type plus rational dihedral character convey meaningful geometric symmetry information beyond nonattacking-placement counts and graph gonality. The natural comparison with periodic board conditions is a substantive reason for this exact finite result.
