The nonattacking-knight complex on the ordinary \(4\times4\) board is \(\bigvee^{5}S^3\), making four the first noncontractible square-board size. Its natural dihedral action on rational third homology splits as \(\mathbf1\oplus2\eta\oplus\rho\), with \(\eta(r)=-1,\eta(s)=1\) and \(\rho\) the geometric square representation.
