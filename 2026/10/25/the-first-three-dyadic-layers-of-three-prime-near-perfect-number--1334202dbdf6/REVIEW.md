# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** The exact surplus \(\Delta=(2^{a+1}-1)(p+q+1)-pq\) yields a strict bound on \(p\). If \(p<H\), a required prime factor \(q\) must divide \(H(p+1)\); if \(p>H\), positivity bounds \(q\). The single equality \(p=H\) is impossible on \(2\)-adic valuations. Consequently the source program checks all finite candidates, not a guessed search range. It finds exactly the five known values, and directly confirms each divisor sum. Finite verification does not substitute for the proof of the bounds.

**Originality — PASS with publication-priority risk.** Shevelev's initial constructions and Ren–Chen's proved classification apply to two distinct prime factors, whereas this is a complete theorem for three prime factors and the first three dyadic layers. Ren–Chen explicitly lists **all five examples**: none is claimed new. Their examples alone do not prove that no others exist with \(2^apq\), \(a\le3\). Comparable investigated near-perfect and deficient equations are not implications of this statement. A further equivalent result not captured by inspected materials may exist.

**Value — PASS.** A natural dyadic filtration of the three-prime, squarefree-odd-part case of the established finiteness conjecture has a sharp first nonempty layer at \(a=3\). The exact bound-backed classification is mathematically distinct from listing examples or an arbitrary finite numerical cutoff. Other exponents and repeated odd prime factors remain outside scope.

**Closest literature:** Shevelev, arXiv:1011.6160v3, §§1–3; Ren–Chen, Bull. Aust. Math. Soc. 88 (2013), Theorem 1.2, page 521, and Conjecture 1.3; OEIS A181595. The latter two already list the examples. Independent publication-priority and mathematical audit remain open.
