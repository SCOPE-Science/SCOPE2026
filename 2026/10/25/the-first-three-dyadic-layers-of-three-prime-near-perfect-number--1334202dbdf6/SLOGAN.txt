Among near-perfect integers \(2^apq\) with distinct odd primes \(p<q\), there are no solutions for \(a=1,2\), while \(a=3\) has exactly the five previously known examples \(5624,9112,11096,13736,17816\). The new statement is their completeness in this infinite family, proved using strict prime bounds and an exhaustive finite divisibility certificate.
