# Scientific assessment

## Correctness
PASS. The face construction enumerates all complements of independent triples, with no sampling. Reproducible pivot-order matchings leave exactly one zero-cell and respectively one five-cell, one six-cell, or two eight-cells. A complete directed-Hasse-graph topological sort verifies acyclicity on 31,070, 84,910, and 205,800 covering relations. Forman's theorem then proves the integral sphere-wedge types because no intermediate-dimensional attaching cells remain. An independent simplicial homology computation over the two-element field corroborates the ranks. Actual programs, complete parameter certificate, and outputs are bundled.

## Originality
PASS at the accessible literature and mathematical-record coverage level, with residual risk. Carnero Bravo, arXiv:2602.21427, Theorem 43(c) begins for the third-power total three-cut case at 16 vertices; Proposition 44 deals with 12 vertices; Question 49 specifically asks for the interval 13 through 15. Bayer et al., arXiv:2209.13503, cover other graph families and squared cycles, while Chauhan–Shukla–Vinayak, arXiv:2512.04486, address total two-cut complexes. The ordinary three-cut complex is not the same object as the total three-cut complex. Searches for the exact three cases, aliases, and equivalent Alexander-dual formulations found no dominating statement. This is not a uniqueness or priority guarantee; unindexed overlap is possible.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The result resolves the **entire first open finite interval** of a precise, published homotopy-classification question in a canonical graph-power family. The transition from one five-sphere, to one six-sphere, to two eight-spheres is not obtained by substituting parameters into existing theorems. The two-sphere-summand endpoint is an informative boundary for any general answer to Question 49, and a fully checkable integral homotopy result is substantially stronger than a numerical Betti table.

## Scope and remaining limitations
The proof covers exactly three finite graph orders, not the general open parameter question. Unindexed prior results and unpublished work are possible. No independent scientific audit has been performed.
