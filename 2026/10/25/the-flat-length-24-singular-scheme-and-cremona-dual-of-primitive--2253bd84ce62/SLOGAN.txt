For the primitive five-monomial Heisenberg quartic spread in characteristic two with nonzero end coefficients, the relative singular scheme is finite flat of length \(24\), while the ADE basket jumps exactly as \((12-4r)A_1+2rA_3\) when \(r\) pairing coefficients vanish. Every member has a separable birational Gauss map given by the cubic Cremona involution and an irreducible dual of degree \(12\).
