For three unordered collision-free particles on a star with \(m\ge3\) arms, the full \(\mathfrak S_m\)-character on rational first homology is \(1-c_1+\binom{c_1}{2}+2\binom{c_1}{3}-c_2-c_3\), with \(c_j\) counting the length-\(j\) cycles of an arm permutation. The known first Betti rank is refined by the conclusion that no nonzero rational first-homology class is invariant under all arm permutations.
