The full \(E\rtimes S_5\)-action on the nine-dimensional third rational homology of the Clebsch independence complex is \((\mathrm{sgn}\otimes\mathrm{Std})\oplus W\), where \(W\) is the five-dimensional signed coordinate module. Its trace at \((a,\sigma)\) is \(\mathrm{sgn}(\sigma)(f(\sigma)-1+\sum_{i:\sigma(i)=i}(-1)^{a_i})\), giving a four-dimensional translation-invariant subspace but no full-group invariant classes.
