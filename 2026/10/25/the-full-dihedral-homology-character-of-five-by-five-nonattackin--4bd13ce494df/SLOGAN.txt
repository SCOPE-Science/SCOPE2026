For nonattacking kings on the \(5\times5\) board, the complete graded \(D_4\)-action on reduced homology has character vectors \((45,-1,5,3,-3)\), \((32,0,0,4,0)\), and \((2,0,-2,0,0)\) in degrees three through five. In particular, top homology is the faithful two-dimensional dihedral representation, a distinction invisible in the previously known nonequivariant sphere-wedge classification.
