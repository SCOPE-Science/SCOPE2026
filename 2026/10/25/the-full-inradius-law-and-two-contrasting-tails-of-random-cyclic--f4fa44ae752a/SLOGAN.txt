For a triangle spanned by three independent uniform points on the unit circle, the exact incircle-radius distribution reduces to one inverse-cosine integral. Its rare thin-circle tail is \(\Pr(R\le t)\sim(6/\pi^2)t\log(1/t)\), while near the equilateral maximum \(\Pr(R>1/2-\varepsilon)\sim 8\varepsilon/(\pi\sqrt3)\).
