# Supported scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem is restricted to the rational graded \(\mathfrak S_7\)-module for the 35-vertex complex of pairwise-intersecting three-subsets of seven points. Feng's published rational homology theorem [1] proves concentration in degrees six and nine, with ranks 29 and 7. Feng–Wang's published independence proof [2] gives seven degree-nine embedded sphere classes; their stabilizer sign action supplies \(\chi_9\). Every invariant simplex is a union of compatible orbits of the triple action; the signed Hopf trace is exactly the negative independence polynomial at \(-1\) of the finite orbit graph. A self-contained exact-integer checker exhausts all fifteen conjugacy classes and all irreducible character inner products. The proof does not extrapolate beyond seven points and makes no integral-homology assertion.

## Originality — PASS, with residual uncertainty

The inspected full-text sources [1] and [2] provide ranks, independent top spheres and a nontrivial Fano-plane class, but do not compute either complete symmetric-group character or the degree-six irreducible decomposition. Searches under the equivalent Rips/intersecting-family nomenclature and comparisons with related stable Kneser and neighborhood-complex results did not reveal a stronger covering theorem. The implication of [2] plus [1] that seven top sphere classes form a basis is explicitly treated as known, not new. Equivalent unindexed computations cannot be ruled out.

## Value — PASS

This is the first nonzero degree-six case of the studied triple Kneser family, where the published rank 29 lacks a described basis. The exact decomposition \(29=1+14+14\) into a unique invariant line and two inequivalent sign-partner irreducibles reveals significant symmetry information absent from the Betti count. It constrains possible combinatorial degree-six bases and explains why all odd permutations have trace one. A finite but complete natural representation classification is useful independently of an infinite-family extension.

## Limits and independent verification

The rational Betti theorem and sphere independence are published prerequisites, and the 15-class signed enumeration is furnished for replay. Neither integral torsion nor a wedge decomposition is addressed. Independent scientific audit has not been performed.
