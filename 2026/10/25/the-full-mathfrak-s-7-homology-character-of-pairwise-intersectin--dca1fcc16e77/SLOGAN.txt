For the complex of pairwise-intersecting triples of seven points, the 29-dimensional rational sixth homology is the \(\mathfrak S_7\)-module \(S^{(7)}\oplus S^{(4,3)}\oplus S^{(2,2,2,1)}\), while its seven-dimensional rational ninth homology is \(S^{(1,1,1,1,1,1,1)}\oplus S^{(2,1,1,1,1,1)}\). The paired 14-dimensional constituents explain why every odd permutation has degree-six trace \(1\).
