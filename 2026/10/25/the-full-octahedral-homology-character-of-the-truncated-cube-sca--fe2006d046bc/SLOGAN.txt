The graph-metric scale-three Rips complex of the truncated cube has a complete octahedral rational homology representation: degree two is the inversion-sign line, while degree three is \((S^{(4)}\oplus S^{(2,2)})\boxtimes\mathbf1\oplus S^{(2,1,1)}\boxtimes\varepsilon\). Thus its six third-homology dimensions split as three inversion-even and three inversion-odd directions, a distinction absent from the previously known sphere-wedge homotopy type.
