The points never landing on a distinguished six-cycle of the continuous map \(F(x,y)=(|x|-y-1,\ x-|y|-13/16)\) form a planar set of exact Hausdorff dimension \(1+s\), where \(16^{-s}+8^{-s}=1\). Their intersection with the invariant polygonal graph has dimension only \(s\); the additional dimension comes from rank-one planar preimage fibers.
