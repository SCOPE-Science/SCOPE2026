For each of two natural 58-line quartic K3 surfaces over \(\mathbb F_3\), the complete Hasse–Weil zeta function is \(1/[(1-t)(1-9t)(1-3t)^6(1-3^8t^8)^2]\). A Frobenius-equivariant rank-22 intersection lattice of their lines gives geometric Picard rank \(22\), arithmetic Picard rank \(8\), and exact point counts over every finite extension.
