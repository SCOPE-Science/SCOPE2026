# Reproducible arithmetic checks

It reconstructs the degree-eight field, the two separable additive root spaces, all 58 geometrically embedded lines for each surface, every Plücker intersection identity and all 357 graph edges, the exact 58-by-58 rational Gram rank 22, and the fixed orbit-sum ranks (8,10,14,22). It independently enumerates the first two point counts, (28,136), over degrees 1 and 2. An additional separate, exactly executed degree-four point-counter returned 7048 in both cases.

The decisive infinite argument is not the point counter: for a smooth quartic K3 the 22-dimensional rational H² is generated by the 22-dimensional line lattice; the finite-order Frobenius isometry is therefore completely determined by its ranks of invariant subspaces, yielding the stated characteristic polynomial and all extension-field counts. The program checks the finite algebraic certificate, not a formal mechanized proof of étale cohomology, the published line total of Veniani, or the comparison with every possible literature source.
