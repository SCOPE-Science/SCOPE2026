# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The graph is identified by a reproducible combinatorial certificate: eleven vertices, eighteen edges, a sphere rotation system with nine quadrilateral faces, three-connectivity after every vertex-pair deletion, and odd-order bipartiteness. The ordinary matching complex has \(751\) nonempty faces with \(f\)-vector \((18,111,280,270,72)\). The complete acyclic Morse matching has \(367\) pairs and critical cells in dimensions \(0,2,3\) with multiplicities \(1,2,14\); every Hasse incidence is inspected. Exact integral cancellation gives a rank-two Morse boundary with a unimodular two-by-two minor, and the absence of one-cells promotes the attaching-map degrees to the complete homotopy classification \(\bigvee^{12}S^3\). The packaged verifier reproduces the signed arithmetic and independent mod-two ranks. This is a finite proof, not an extrapolation from experiments.

## Originality — PASS

The closest inspected full primary text is Bayer–Jelić Milutinović–Vega (arXiv:2411.04601v1), which establishes the sphere-wedge property for outerplanar graphs and discusses the nonouterplanar planar gap. Herschel is three-connected and nonouterplanar, and no exact Herschel matching-complex value is stated there. Engström (arXiv:math/0512420) gives general connectivity bounds in the available abstract; Linusson–Shareshian–Welker (arXiv:math/0410345) studies a different bounded-matching-number graph complex. Published record comparisons distinguish the present independence complex of the *line graph* from a Herschel independence-complex calculation, and distinguish the Goldner–Harary and Franklin matching-complex calculations from this graph. Actual alias, equivalent-object, exact-value and broader-coverage searches gave no covering claim. Residual risk includes unindexed tables and older full texts not inspected; failed search is not proof of absolute novelty.

## Value — PASS

The Herschel graph is an extremal polyhedral graph, not an arbitrary chosen small graph. Its matching complex supplies a complete homotopy type for a canonical three-connected planar graph outside the outerplanar theorem, with exact degree-three multiplicity and no torsion. Its pure sphere-wedge type contrasts with the mixed-dimensional matching homotopy of the other canonical eleven-vertex extremal non-Hamiltonian polyhedron, Goldner–Harary. This is a motivated exact classification even without an infinite-family theorem.

**Limitations:** One graph only. Exact finite certificates support the mathematical claim; uninspected bibliographic records cannot be used to establish universal novelty. Independent validation has not been undertaken.
