# Verification procedure

Run `python3 scripts/verify.py` from the package root with any standard Python 3 version supporting integer `bit_count` (Python 3.8 can adapt that one call). No third-party packages or network access are required.

The program independently checks all \(2^{12}\) candidate vertex subsets using two face predicates; their complete agreement matters because a bounded sample would not establish the simplicial complex. It constructs all \(324\) faces including the empty simplex and all \(984\) codimension-one incidences. It tests each explicit matching pair and topologically sorts the entire reversed-matching Hasse graph, giving a rigorous finite acyclicity certificate. Critical cells consist of one vertex and twenty three-cells. Discrete Morse theory therefore gives the integral wedge homotopy equivalence, not just ranks over a field.

As an additional independent homological check, bitset Gaussian elimination of the three mod-two simplicial boundaries gives ranks \(11,55,85\). This finite-field check alone would not rule out odd torsion; the integral homotopy conclusion instead follows from the acyclic matching with critical cells only in dimensions zero and three.

The verifier checks 120 graph-preserving permutations generated by two rotations and a central antipodal involution, including their classwise signed Lefschetz traces. The icosahedral vertex stabilizer upper bound establishes that this generated group is all of \(\operatorname{Aut}(I)\). The group representation result is only over \(\mathbb Q\). The verification is a self-review computation and independent validation has not been performed.

The actual recorded stdout is embedded in `verification_output.txt` and finishes with `VERIFY_OK`.
