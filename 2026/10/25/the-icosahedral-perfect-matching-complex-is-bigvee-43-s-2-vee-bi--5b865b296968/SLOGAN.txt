The complex generated by all 125 perfect matchings of the icosahedral graph is homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{43}S^2\vee\bigvee^{19}S^3\). Although every two-edge matching extends to a perfect matching, 140 three-edge matchings do not, and the five-dimensional complex has torsion-free reduced integral homology concentrated in dimensions two and three.
