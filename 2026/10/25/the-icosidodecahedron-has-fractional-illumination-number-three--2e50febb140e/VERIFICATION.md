# Verification of the icosidodecahedral fractional illumination theorem

It verifies that the twelve standard icosahedron vertices give thirty edges and twenty triangular faces; the twelve pentagonal and twenty triangular supporting planes of their midpoint hull touch five or three points respectively. The sixty induced boundary segments each occur on exactly two of these thirty-two faces. It verifies six antipodal pairs, reconstructs the signed conference matrix, tests \(\,A^2=5I_6\), exhausts all sixty-four antipodal transversals, and confirms the distribution of edge counts.
