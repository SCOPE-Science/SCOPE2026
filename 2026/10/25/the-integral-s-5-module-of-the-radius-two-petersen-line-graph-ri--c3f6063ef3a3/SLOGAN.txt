At inclusive Rips radius two, the fifteen-vertex Petersen line graph has a five-partite join with top integral homology \(\mathbb Z^{32}\), carrying an explicit signed \(S_5\)-tensor action. Its rational top character is \((32,0,8,2,0,0,2)\) on the seven ordered cycle types, and its integral \(S_5\)-coinvariants are \(\mathbb Z^2\), without torsion.
