In characteristic two, the five-coordinate Clebsch cubic has a complete marked Eckardt-incidence split: its \(45\) Eckardt points form \(S_5\)-orbits of sizes \(5,10,30\), and its \(27\) lines form orbits of sizes \(15,12\), with line-by-point incidences \((1,2,2)\) and \((0,0,5)\). The \(15\) rational points and lines form \(W(3,2)\), with a distinguished five-point ovoid, while the \(30\) non-rational Eckardt points are exactly the edges of the remaining twelve-line double-six intersection graph.
