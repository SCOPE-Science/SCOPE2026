Every odd binary integer of length at least seven with exactly two zeroes has first Hamming-weight-reducing multiplier equal to \(3\), \(5\), \(7\), or \(9\), or has no such multiplier; the precise answer is determined by its three runs of ones. The five classes have exact counts \(\binom{L-4}{2}\), \(2L-11\), \(1\), \(2\), and \(1\) at length \(L\).
