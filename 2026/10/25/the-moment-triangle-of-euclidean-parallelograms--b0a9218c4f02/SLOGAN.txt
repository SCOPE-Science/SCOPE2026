For two independent area-uniform points of any nondegenerate Euclidean parallelogram, the even distance moments \(M_2,M_4,M_6\) reconstruct both side lengths and the angle, whereas \(M_2,M_4\) alone admit a continuum of noncongruent shapes. The normalized pair \((M_4/M_2^2,M_6/M_2^3)\) ranges over an exact sharp triangle with its degenerate side removed.
