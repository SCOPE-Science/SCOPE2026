# The multiplicity-free wreath-product homology character of the ternary Hamming three-cube

## Finding

Let \(X=\{0,1,2\}^3\) with Hamming distance, and let \(K=\operatorname{VR}_{\le2}(X)\), with simplices the finite nonempty subsets of diameter at most two. The group \(G=S_3\wr S_3=(S_3)^3\rtimes S_3\) has order \(1296\); the three base symmetric groups relabel the values in each coordinate, and the upper symmetric group permutes coordinates. The natural rational \(G\)-module \(\widetilde H_3(K;\mathbb Q)\) is **multiplicity-free with seven irreducible constituents**, of dimensions \(8,12,6,1,12,6,1\). All other reduced rational homology groups vanish.

To name the constituents, write \(\mathbf1\), \(\mathrm{sgn}\), \(W\) for the trivial, sign and two-dimensional standard irreducible representations of the base \(S_3\). A rational irreducible of \(S_3\wr S_3\) is labeled by three partitions \((\lambda_{\mathbf1},\lambda_{\mathrm{sgn}},\lambda_W)\) with total size three, by the standard induced Specht-tensor construction. Empty partitions are written \(\varnothing\). Every constituent occurs once:

| Irreducible label | Dimension | Multiplicity |
|---|---:|---:|
| \((\varnothing,\varnothing,(1,1,1))\) | 8 | 1 |
| \((\varnothing,(1),(1,1))\) | 12 | 1 |
| \((\varnothing,(1,1),(1))\) | 6 | 1 |
| \((\varnothing,(1,1,1),\varnothing)\) | 1 | 1 |
| \(((1),\varnothing,(1,1))\) | 12 | 1 |
| \(((1,1),\varnothing,(1))\) | 6 | 1 |
| \(((1,1,1),\varnothing,\varnothing)\) | 1 | 1 |

For a conjugacy class in \(G\), each cycle of the upper coordinate permutation contributes a pair consisting of its length and the conjugacy class of the **product of base symbol permutations** around that cycle. Write `I`, `T`, `C` for identity, transposition and three-cycle in \(S_3\). The multiset of these pairs is a complete conjugacy-class label. The entire character of \(\widetilde H_3(K;\mathbb Q)\) is:

| Cycle-product class | Class size | Homology trace |
|---|---:|---:|
| `(1C,1C,1C)` | 8 | 1 |
| `(1C,1C,1I)` | 12 | -2 |
| `(1C,1C,1T)` | 36 | 0 |
| `(1C,1I,1I)` | 6 | 4 |
| `(1C,1I,1T)` | 36 | 0 |
| `(1C,1T,1T)` | 54 | 0 |
| `(1C,2C)` | 72 | 1 |
| `(1C,2I)` | 36 | -2 |
| `(1C,2T)` | 108 | 0 |
| `(1I,1I,1I)` | 1 | 46 |
| `(1I,1I,1T)` | 9 | 0 |
| `(1I,1T,1T)` | 27 | 6 |
| `(1I,2C)` | 36 | -2 |
| `(1I,2I)` | 18 | -14 |
| `(1I,2T)` | 54 | 0 |
| `(1T,1T,1T)` | 27 | 0 |
| `(1T,2C)` | 108 | 0 |

In particular, \(\widetilde H_3(K;\mathbb Q)^G=0\). Under the normal subgroup \((S_3)^3\), however, the fixed subspace has dimension one; upper coordinate permutations act on that line by their sign. The other one-dimensional constituent is the product of the three base-sign characters twisted by the sign of the upper coordinate permutation.

## Assumptions and scope

Vertices are exactly the 27 triples with entries in \(\{0,1,2\}\), lexicographically identified with integers \(0\) through \(26\). The metric is the discrete Hamming metric, not Euclidean distance, and the Rips threshold uses weak inequality. The only coefficients claimed for the representation are rational. The action is by the full graph/metric automorphism group generated by independent symbol permutations and coordinate permutations. The product graph \(K_3\times K_3\times K_3\) means categorical graph product; its independence complex is exactly \(K\), because two triples are forbidden together precisely when they differ in all three coordinates.

## Proof

**Rational homology concentration.** Enumerate every face by increasing-vertex clique recursion: starting from the empty set, adjoin a vertex only from the current common-neighbor candidate mask. Every possible clique appears once. The exact size-vector for nonempty faces, from vertices through 9-element simplices, is

\[
(27,243,945,1728,1620,945,351,81,9).
\]

Build the full augmented simplicial boundary maps over \(\mathbb F_2\) by deleting each vertex once from each oriented face. For the maps from face-size \(2,3,\ldots,9\) to the immediately lower face-size, exhaustive bitset Gaussian elimination gives the exact ranks

\[
(26,217,728,954,666,279,72,9).
\]

Hence the mod-two Betti numbers are one connected component and reduced rank \(46\) in degree three, with zero mod-two reduced Betti numbers in all other degrees. Any integral boundary matrix has rank over \(\mathbb Q\) at least its rank over \(\mathbb F_2\), since an odd nonzero minor remains nonzero rationally. Consequently the rational reduced homology vanishes off degree three. The alternating face count (Euler characteristic) then forces \(\dim_\mathbb Q\widetilde H_3(K;\mathbb Q)=46\). This proof concerns this finite complex and is independent of accepting earlier claims about its integral homotopy type.

**Homology character from fixed simplices.** Fix \(g\in G\) and list the cycles of its permutation on the 27 vertices. A face is setwise fixed exactly when it is a union of entire vertex cycles. A chosen cycle is allowed only if its vertices form a clique, and any two chosen cycles must have all cross edges present. For each allowable selection of \(c\) vertex cycles, the induced permutation of its oriented face has sign \((-1)^{|\sigma|-c}\). Its nonaugmented Lefschetz sign is therefore \((-1)^{c-1}\); the empty face gives the reduced correction. Since reduced rational homology occurs only in degree three, multiplying its reduced Lefschetz number by minus one yields the character formula

\[
\chi(g)=\sum_{\substack{S\text{ an allowable collection}\\\text{of vertex cycles of }g}}(-1)^{|S|},
\]

**including the empty collection**. The `verify.py` program reconstructs every vertex orbit, tests allowed unions using the original adjacency matrix, and evaluates this finite exact sum for each of the \(1296\) group elements. As a logically separate check it computes, for a representative of every conjugacy class, the signed oriented simplicial Lefschetz trace directly from **all** \(5949\) nonempty faces, plus the reduced empty-face term. The two independent methods agree on all 22 conjugacy classes. The resulting class trace/size data are exactly the displayed table. Both the group enumeration and the class-size sum are exhaustive.

**Irreducible decomposition.** For each of the three rational base irreducibles \(\mathbf1,\mathrm{sgn},W\), choose partitions with total size three and induce the corresponding wreath-product Specht-tensor character. For an element with cycles of lengths \(\ell\) and cycle products \(p\), the induced character is the sum over ways to color the coordinate cycles by the three base types: each color receives cycles whose total lengths equal the corresponding partition size; its summand is the Specht character of the colored cycle lengths times the product of the base characters evaluated at the cycle products. This formula is the standard character formula for wreath-product induction and is derived directly by tracing the induced tensor basis over invariant colorings; no precomputed character table is loaded. The program constructs all 22 irreducible characters using \(S_3\) character values \(\mathbf1=(1,1,1)\), \(\mathrm{sgn}=(1,-1,1)\), and \(W=(2,0,-1)\) on the identity, transposition and three-cycle classes, respectively.

For every triple label it computes the exact character inner product

\[
\frac1{1296}\sum_{\mathcal C}|\mathcal C|\,\chi(\mathcal C)\chi_{\lambda}(\mathcal C).
\]

Exactly the seven labels listed in the finding have value one, and all the other 15 have value zero. Their dimensions add to \(46\), and the class table has character squared norm seven, independently confirming multiplicity-freeness. The trivial label \(((3),\varnothing,\varnothing)\) is absent, giving no globally invariant vector. The only base-invariant constituent is \(((1,1,1),\varnothing,\varnothing)\), which is the coordinate-permutation sign line.

## Verification

Run `python3 verify.py` (Python standard library only) from any directory. The program derives vertices, faces, all rational-homology prerequisites, group elements, conjugacy labels, fixed-orbit traces, independent signed simplex traces, induced wreath characters, and exact inner products. A successful run prints `TERNARY_HAMMING_WREATH_CHARACTER_VERIFIED 5949 22 1296 7`, followed by the 22-row table and seven constituents. Exact integer arithmetic is used throughout. All \(1296\) group elements are checked for constancy on each computed class. The verification is exhaustive for this 27-vertex complex, not a finite sample offered as an infinite-family proof.

## Relationship to prior work

Antolín Camarena and Carnero Bravo [1] study independence complexes of categorical products, prove results with a two-vertex complete-graph factor, and formulate the three-factor case. Previously published exact calculations [3,4] identify the **same** ternary-cube Rips complex as a wedge of 46 three-spheres and give its ordinary persistence barcode. Those facts establish its Betti number and homotopy type, but do **not** specify the full \(S_3\wr S_3\) action, determine any of the 22 character values, or force the seven irreducible multiplicities. Goyal, Shukla and Singh [2] compute other product-graph independence complexes, not this complete equivariant module. Galetto, Montaño and Wellner [5] determine representations for **binary** hypercubes, so their stronger equivariant results apply to a different alphabet and do not imply the ternary claim. Nearby published symmetry calculations for graph complexes similarly address different complexes or symmetry groups. No exact existing ternary-cube character statement was identified, although an unindexed equivalent result remains possible.

## Limitations

This is one exact finite natural complex, not a uniform formula for all ternary cube dimensions or alphabet sizes. The decomposition is over \(\mathbb Q\); no integral equivariant lattice or equivariant homotopy wedge decomposition is claimed. Exhaustive deterministic computation is a legitimate proof for this finite case, but requires ordinary trust in the supplied code execution and integer arithmetic. The finite check does not itself settle a general categorical-product conjecture. An independently performed mathematical audit has not occurred, and an unindexed equivalent publication could limit originality.
