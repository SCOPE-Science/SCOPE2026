The 46-dimensional third rational homology of the distance-two ternary Hamming three-cube is multiplicity-free under its full 1,296-element symmetry group \(S_3\wr S_3\): it splits into exactly seven irreducibles of dimensions \(8,12,6,1,12,6,1\). Its complete character has 22 conjugacy-class values and no invariant vector under the full group, information not determined by the previously known wedge of 46 three-spheres.
