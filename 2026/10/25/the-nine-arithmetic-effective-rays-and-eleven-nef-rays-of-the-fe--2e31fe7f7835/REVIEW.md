**Correctness — PASS.** On the smooth Hermitian Fermat cubic, explicit equations produce every one of its twenty-seven lines. Exact \(\mathbb F_4\) intersection calculations give three rational lines and twelve conjugate pairs, equally split between meeting and skew. The geometric Mori cone is generated by these lines, meeting-pair traces are decomposable, and disjoint exceptional pairs are contractible faces. An explicit unimodular seven-class geometric basis and a unit determinant minor prove that the three rational lines and one skew-pair trace generate the entire integral arithmetic Picard lattice. Exhaustive integer supporting-triple enumeration proves nine primal and eleven dual rays; Gram determinant \(-8\) and Smith factors \(1,2,2,2\) were reconstructed.

The delimited claim is the concrete invariant cone and minimal primitive line-trace basis, not those known facts. The relevant complex Fermat sixer record is about a different invariant.
