For the Fano matroid, deleting the seven collinear triangles from the complete two-skeleton produces a canonical integral exact sequence \(0\to H_2\to\bigwedge^3W\to T\to0\) of \(GL_3(\mathbb F_2)\)-modules. Its exact equivariant splitting exponent is four, although it splits rationally; the thirteen-dimensional top homology has complex irreducible decomposition \(7\oplus3\oplus\overline3\).
