At the double-conic parameter of the symmetric Ciani quartic, the quadratic base change \(q=s^2\) has smooth octahedral hyperelliptic genus-three limit \(W^2=U^8+14U^4V^4+V^8\). The nontrivial deck transformation specializes to the hyperelliptic involution, so tame inertia acts by \(-1\) and the minimal ramification index for good reduction is exactly \(2\).
