# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** Derivations are parametrized by a \(2\times2\) block and a free central row; conjugation removes the latter uniquely for invertible blocks. A resultant isolates the only exceptional prime, and explicit semisimple similarity classes give exact orbit sizes. Direct exhaustive matrix checks across five prime fields corroborate the count; the proof, not these finite checks, covers extension fields.

**Originality (PASS; bounded evidence).** The 2011 work of Burde--Moens proves existence and displays concrete period-six Heisenberg derivations. Their 2020 study identifies a characteristic-seven resultant phenomenon for polynomially constrained derivations. Neither inspected article reports the stated finite-field conjugacy class count, the orbit-size partition, or the order-three scalar census. Related archived finite-field Heisenberg automorphism counts involve a different action and different operators. Absolute priority is not established by the bounded source search.

**Value (PASS).** Period six is singled out structurally for nonabelian Lie algebras by the older literature. The smallest nonabelian example now has a complete arithmetically varying conjugacy census; the result distinguishes rational split and nonsplit spectra and quantifies the characteristic-seven degeneracy. This provides an exact base case for finite-field periodic-derivation classification beyond existence, not merely a sample calculation.

**Closest literature and limitations.** Burde--Moens, arXiv:1108.3548v1 (2011), and arXiv:2009.05434 (2020) contain the strongest directly relevant theory inspected. The latter predicts the exceptional prime through a resultant but does not give orbit multiplicities. Characteristics two and three, higher Heisenberg dimensions and periods other than divisors of six are not covered. Literature searches cannot prove unique priority.
