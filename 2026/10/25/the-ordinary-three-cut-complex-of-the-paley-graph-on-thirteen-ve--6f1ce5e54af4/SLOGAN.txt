The ordinary three-cut complex of the thirteen-vertex Paley graph has reduced integral homology \(\mathbb Z^2\) in degree \(7\) and \(\mathbb Z\) in degree \(8\), with all other reduced groups zero. Its combinatorial Alexander dual is exactly \(S^2\vee S^3\vee S^3\), and the original pure nine-dimensional complex is nonshellable.
