Ordinary synchronous chip-firing on an open path has exactly the Pell number \(P_{n-1}\) of primitive two-cycle orbits, while the complete count at each conserved chip mass is encoded by \(\sum_{n\ge2}C_n(z)u^{n-2}=z/(1-z(1+z)u-z^3u^2)\). Sampling an orbit uniformly produces an exactly symmetric mass distribution with mean \((3n-4)/2\) and asymptotic variance \((\sqrt2/8)n\).
