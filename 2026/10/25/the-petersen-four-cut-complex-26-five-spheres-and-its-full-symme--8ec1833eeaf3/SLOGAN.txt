The ordinary four-cut complex of the Petersen graph is integrally a wedge of 26 five-spheres. Its rational top homology under the full label-permutation group \(S_5\) is \(S^{(5)}\oplus2S^{(4,1)}\oplus S^{(3,2)}\oplus2S^{(3,1,1)}\), so the full symmetry quotient retains exactly one reduced rational homology class.
