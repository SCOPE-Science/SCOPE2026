# Review

## Correctness

PASS. The lower-bound set has \(p+2\) elements and misses two elements in one residue class. For the upper bound, the Chinese remainder decomposition reduces the claim to three fibers in \(\mathbb F_p\); all fiber-size cases are handled with the Cauchy–Davenport and Dias da Silva–Hamidoune bounds. The inequalities are explicit and valid for every prime \(p\ge7\) with \(p\equiv1\pmod3\).

## Originality

PASS, subject to the stated literature risk. Bajnok’s 2014 preprint states the exact critical-number formula as a conjecture and lists prime/even orders plus finite verification as known cases. The present proof establishes the infinite family \(n=3p\), \(p\equiv1\pmod3\), which is odd composite for all admissible \(p\). The 2026 general odd-order result gives a weaker density bound for this family, not the exact value.

## Value

PASS. This settles an infinite subfamily of a published exact-value conjecture, including a matching obstruction one element below the threshold. The result is sharper than the general density bounds for \(p\ge13\), and its proof gives an explicit residue-fiber mechanism rather than a finite census.
