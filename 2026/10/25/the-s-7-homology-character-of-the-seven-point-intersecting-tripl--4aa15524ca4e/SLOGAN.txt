For the 35-vertex complex of intersecting triples on seven points, the natural \(S_7\)-representation on sixth rational homology is exactly \(\mathbf 1\oplus S^{(4,3)}\oplus S^{(2,2,2,1)}\): a unique invariant line and two conjugate 14-dimensional irreducibles. Ninth rational homology is the oriented-six-set module \(\bigwedge^6\mathbb Q^7\); all other reduced rational homology vanishes.
