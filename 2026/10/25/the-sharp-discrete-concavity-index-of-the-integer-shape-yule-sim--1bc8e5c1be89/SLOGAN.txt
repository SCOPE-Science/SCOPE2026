For every positive integer Yule–Simon shape \(r\), the exact maximal discrete concavity parameter is \(-a_r\), where \(a_r>0\) is the unique positive root of \(((r+3)/2)^{a_r}+(r+2)^{-a_r}=2\). Its sharp large-shape law is \(a_r\sim(\log2)/(\log r)^2\), strictly improving the previously published continuous-derivative sufficient index.
