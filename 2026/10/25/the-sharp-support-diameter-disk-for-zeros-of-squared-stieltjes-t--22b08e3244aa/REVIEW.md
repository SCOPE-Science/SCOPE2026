# Review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof reduces zero-freeness outside the disk to the angular width of the family \((z+t)^{-2}\). Outside the disk that width is strictly less than \(\pi\), so a rotation makes every integrand have positive real part. The converse constructs a positive two-atom measure by selecting two kernel values with opposite phases and balancing their magnitudes. The exact checks at \(a=0\), \(b=1\) agree with the claimed boundary and interior locations.
