The analytic proof was reconstructed from the definitions. The geometric identity
\[
(x+a)(x+b)+y^2=\left|z+\frac{a+b}{2}\right|^2-\frac{(b-a)^2}{4}
\]
connects the angle between \(z+a\) and \(z+b\) to the disk boundary. Outside the disk the phase width of the order-two kernels is strictly less than \(\pi\), which excludes cancellation by a nonzero positive measure. Inside the disk the intermediate value theorem gives two kernel values with opposite phases, and the explicit positive masses balance their magnitudes.

Exact checks: for \(\mu=\delta_0+\delta_1\), the numerator of \(z^{-2}+(z+1)^{-2}\) is \(2z^2+2z+1\), with roots \((-1\pm i)/2\). For \(z=-1/2+i/4\), the measure \(4\delta_0+\delta_{5/8}\) gives zero exactly. These checks corroborate the construction but do not replace the proof for all measures.

Limits: no claim is made for other kernel powers, signed measures, or unbounded support. The Love–Byrne source was inspected only at abstract level. Independent audit and independent validation have not been performed.
