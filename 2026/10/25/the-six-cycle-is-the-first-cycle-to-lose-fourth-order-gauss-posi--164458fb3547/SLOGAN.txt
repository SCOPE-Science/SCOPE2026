For the fourth-order Gauss–Legendre heat step on unit cycles, unconditional entrywise positivity holds exactly for \(C_3,C_4,C_5\); every longer cycle eventually has a negative nearest-neighbor entry. The first failure, on \(C_6\), occurs at the unique positive root of \(5h^4-12h^3-102h^2-144h-72=0\), after which only its nearest-neighbor entries are negative.
