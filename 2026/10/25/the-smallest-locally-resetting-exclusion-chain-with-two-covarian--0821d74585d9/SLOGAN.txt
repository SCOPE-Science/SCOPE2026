Three sites are the smallest open locally resetting exclusion chain that supports two reversals of an equal-time nearest-neighbour correlation: for injection \(1\) and extraction \(1/10\), the covariance between sites \(2\) and \(3\) is negative, then positive, then negative as the resetting rate increases. Its two thresholds are the exact positive roots of a specified quartic, and the phenomenon persists under small changes in the boundary rates.
