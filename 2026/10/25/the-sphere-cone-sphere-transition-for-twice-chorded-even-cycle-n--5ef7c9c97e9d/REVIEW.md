# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The theorem quantifies over every integer \(t\ge2\). Its proof enumerates exactly the perfect matchings in the three graphs, identifies the unused cone edge at the intermediate stage, and reduces the final complex by joins and a two-cone homotopy pushout with sphere intersection. The \(t=2\) boundary is handled separately. All homotopy equivalences are integral; twelve finite mod-two homology computations and eighteen exact face enumerations independently check small instances but are not the general proof.

## Originality — PASS (bibliographic risk remains)
Shan–Wang (2026), Theorems 2.1 and 2.3, proves injection only for edge additions *preserving bipartiteness*, while Lemma 2.2 already establishes the original cycle sphere. Neither chord in this theorem preserves bipartiteness. Linusson–Shareshian–Welker gives full-complete-graph results (so the \(t=2\) endpoint is known); Holmsen–Lee gives nonmatching homological vanishing ranges rather than an exact sparse two-chorded homotopy type. Searches of analogous forms and adjacent work found no dominating all-size three-stage result. This is not a guarantee of absolute priority.

## Value — PASS
The exact sphere-to-cone-to-higher-sphere transition sharply tests a theorem whose bipartiteness condition is essential. Both the vanishing of the original degree-\(2t-3\) integral class after the first chord and the new degree-\(2t-2\) class after the second chord are valid for arbitrarily large graph order. The transition and explicit topological proof convey substantially more than a field-homology counterexample at a small graph.
