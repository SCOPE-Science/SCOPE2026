For every \(t\ge2\), the non-matching complex of the \(2t\)-cycle is \(S^{2t-3}\); adding the chord \(\{0,2\}\) makes it contractible, but then adding \(\{1,3\}\) makes it \(S^{2t-2}\). This provides an integral all-size sphere–cone–sphere transition showing why bipartiteness is essential to degree-\(2t-3\) homology persistence under edge insertions.
