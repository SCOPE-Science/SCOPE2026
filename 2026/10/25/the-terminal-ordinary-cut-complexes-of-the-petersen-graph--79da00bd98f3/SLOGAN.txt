The last three nonvoid ordinary cut complexes of the Petersen graph have homotopy types \(S^3\vee\bigvee^5 S^4\), \(\bigvee^{11}S^3\), and \(\bigvee^{11}S^1\) at cut parameters \(5,6,7\), respectively. The first is a pure four-dimensional complex with mixed-dimensional homology, certified by an integral discrete-Morse boundary with four unit Smith factors.
