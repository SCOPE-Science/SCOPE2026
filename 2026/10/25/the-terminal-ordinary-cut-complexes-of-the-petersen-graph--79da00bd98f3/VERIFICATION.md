The bundled `verify_petersen.py` uses Python 3 standard library only. It was executed on the packaged source path before delivery. Direct enumeration found respectively 120, 65 and 10 facets for the three levels and 496, 211 and 51 total faces including the empty simplex. The matching Hasse digraphs were all exhaustively topologically sorted. For the mixed-dimensional stage, the signed Morse boundary was explicitly reconstructed; a displayed four-by-four minor is a unit and a row dependence bounds the rank by four. A separate mod-two chain reduction returned reduced Betti ranks \(1,5\) in degrees \(3,4\) for \(k=5\), and rank \(11\) in degree \(3\) or \(1\) for \(k=6,7\). No disconnected induced sets of size \(8\) or more were found.

The finite exhaustive check plus the discrete-Morse theorem supplies the homotopy argument for this graph. It does not constitute a theorem for all Kneser graphs, and no independent external validation has been performed.
