Every finite magma monoid has a three-rank description of its Green \(\mathcal J\)-order: the diagonal-image count, free-image orbit count, and total image-orbit count give the exact three inequalities for two-sided divisibility. On an \(n\)-element underlying set it has exactly \(n(n+1)(3n^2-5n+8)/12\) principal two-sided ideals, and the regular classes are exactly those with no fixed-image defect.
