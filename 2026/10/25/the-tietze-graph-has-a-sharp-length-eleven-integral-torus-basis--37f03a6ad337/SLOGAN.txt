For the Tietze graph, disjoint-simple-cycle product tori generate \(H_2(\operatorname{UConf}_2(|G|);\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^9\) integrally if and only if the permitted total cycle length reaches at least \(11\). The ten triangle–hexagon and pentagon–hexagon tori span an index-two sublattice, and any triangle–pentagon torus removes that parity obstruction.
