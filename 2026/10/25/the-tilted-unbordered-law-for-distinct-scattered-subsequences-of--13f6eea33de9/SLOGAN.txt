Among distinct scattered subsequences of any fixed finite-alphabet independent source, a strictly positive asymptotic fraction in the ratio of expected counts are unbordered; this fraction is the classical unbordered-word density of an explicit, source-dependent tilted distribution. For binary letter bias \(p\), the tilt is proportional to \((\sqrt p,\sqrt{1-p})\), and the restricted expectation has the same exponential rate as the unrestricted one.
