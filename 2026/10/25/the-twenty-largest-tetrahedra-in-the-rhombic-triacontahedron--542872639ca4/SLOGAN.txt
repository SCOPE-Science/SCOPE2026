The largest tetrahedron in the rhombic triacontahedron has volume \((5+2\sqrt5)/3\) and occupies exactly \((5+2\sqrt5)/60\) of the solid. Exactly twenty maximizing tetrahedra exist: each pairs a large equilateral triangle of three icosahedral vertices with its opposite dodecahedral apex; none comes from the famous regular-tetrahedron ten-compound.
