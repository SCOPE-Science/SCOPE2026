In a symmetrically reinforced \(q\)-color Pólya urn, two different colors being drawn exactly once are negatively correlated outside a single critical window and positively correlated within it; for \(q\ge5\) the covariance reverses sign twice as the sample grows. The positive window has endpoints \(q\pm\sqrt q-3/4+O(q^{-1/2})\), and its sharply scaled covariance is \((1-s^2)/32\) at sample size \(q+\lfloor s\sqrt q\rfloor\).
