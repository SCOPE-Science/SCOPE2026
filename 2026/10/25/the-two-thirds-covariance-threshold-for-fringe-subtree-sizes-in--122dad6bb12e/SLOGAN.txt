In a uniform random binary search tree, squared root-split imbalance has a negative covariance with the count of fringe subtrees of size \(k<(2n-1)/3\), but a positive covariance for \(k>(2n-1)/3\). The exact all-size spectrum, \(4k(3k+1-2n)/(3n)\), resolves the known positive imbalance–path-length covariance into competing small- and large-subtree contributions.
