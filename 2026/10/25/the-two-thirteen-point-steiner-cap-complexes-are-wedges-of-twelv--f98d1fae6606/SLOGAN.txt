The two order-thirteen Steiner triple systems have distinct cap-complex homotopy types: \(\bigvee^{12}S^4\) for the cyclic design and \(\bigvee^{17}S^4\) for its Pasch-switched companion. Exact acyclic matchings and unimodular integral Morse boundaries prove both classifications; the cyclic translation group acts on fourth rational homology as its twelve-dimensional augmentation representation.
