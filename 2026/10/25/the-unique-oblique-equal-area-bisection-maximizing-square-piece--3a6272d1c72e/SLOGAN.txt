The sharp minimum inradius-to-circumradius ratio over two equal-area convex pieces of a square is \(0.465023362935585\ldots\), and it is attained only by an oblique center cut of slope \(t_*=0.288678389721968\ldots\) up to square symmetries. The unique optimum is specified exactly by \(4(1-t_*)=(t_*+\sqrt{1+t_*^2})(1+t_*)^3\), rather than by a horizontal or diagonal bisection.
