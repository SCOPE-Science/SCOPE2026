The natural densities of axial lattice triangles with exactly zero, one, or two relative-interior lattice points are explicit combinations of two convergent coprimality Euler products. A complete pairwise-GCD classification identifies the first three strata, and nontrivial prime-adic corrections give exact densities \(3Q-15P/8\), \(3583P/17280\), and \(151P/2100\), respectively.
