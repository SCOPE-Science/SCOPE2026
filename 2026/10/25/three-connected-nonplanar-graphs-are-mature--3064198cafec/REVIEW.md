## Correctness

PASS. The proof reduces to the three basic Barnette–Grünbaum operations. In each case, deleting the endpoints of the newly added edge leaves a connected graph: respectively by 3-connectivity, by 2-connectivity after deleting one vertex and then an edge, and by 3-edge-connectivity after deleting two edges. The prescribed-subdivision construction theorem and the edge-addition criterion apply at every step.

## Originality

PASS, with a clearly bounded scope. Farber–Hanbury explicitly leave the maturity classification as a conjecture and state the relevant base cases, subdivision invariance, and edge-addition theorem. Schmidt’s prescribed-subdivision theorem supplies the construction sequence. The explicit operation-by-operation compatibility argument yields the 3-connected nonplanar case, which was not stated as a theorem in the inspected sources. This is a synthesis of existing ingredients, not a claim that those ingredients are new.

## Value

PASS. The result resolves a natural structural case of a published classification conjecture for graph configuration spaces and converts it into a reusable criterion for every finite simple 3-connected nonplanar graph. It does not claim the full conjecture.
