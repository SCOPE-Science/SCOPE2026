Translation by the three-bit all-one vector has free two-point vertex orbits of diameter 3, so geometric fixed set is empty below scale 3. At scale 3 orbit labels (four complement-pair colors,cube vertex u) are compatible exactly when colors differ and d_H(u,v)≤1; this was independently checked for m=0..4. For each cube edge the four opposite color pairs yield the 4-dimensional crosspolytope boundary S3, and intersections are the tetrahedral facets at cube vertices. Contracting disjoint tetrahedra and a spanning tree of the cube graph yields e copies of S3 and e-v+1 circles (e=m2^(m-1),v=2^m); m=0 is a tetrahedron. Independent face vectors for m=0,1,2,3 agree with exact block formula.
