# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The orbit-barycenter homeomorphism is valid for a free involution acting simplicially. The largest cross-orbit distance is computed exactly as outside Hamming distance plus the maximum of two complementary three-bit distances. The resulting orbit graph has edges only within a base-cube fiber or across one cube edge and between different colors. Because a cube graph has no triangles, every clique lies in one cross-polytope edge block. Collapsing the disjoint contractible vertex tetrahedra gives a graph of genuine three-spheres; a spanning tree produces the claimed integral wedge. The finite verifier checks the combinatorics and mod-two boundaries for the first five nontrivial dimensions, but the proof is the basis for the all-parameter claim.

The complete text of the closest 2026 primary works could not be retrieved, so priority of possible unpublished intermediate lemmas remains unresolved at a nondecisive level; their stated results do not cover the new conclusion.

## Mathematical value
PASS. Three-coordinate translation is the first new simultaneous-bit-flip threshold after the two-bit case; at threshold three the fixed locus makes a structural jump from a one-dimensional fixed graph model to a uniform family of cross-polytope three-spheres supported by every edge of the remaining cube. The formula determines integral fundamental-group rank and top-dimensional free homology simultaneously, in every ambient dimension, with exponentially growing multiplicities. These are geometrically natural invariants of the new fixed action, not arbitrary finite-table slices.
