Flipping three coordinates of a binary hypercube produces, at the first possible fixed-point Rips scale, a simplicial space assembled from three-sphere cross-polytope boundaries along tetrahedra. In dimension \(n\ge4\), its integral homotopy type is exactly \(\bigvee^{m2^{m-1}-2^m+1}S^1\vee\bigvee^{m2^{m-1}}S^3\), where \(m=n-3\); in dimension three it is contractible.
