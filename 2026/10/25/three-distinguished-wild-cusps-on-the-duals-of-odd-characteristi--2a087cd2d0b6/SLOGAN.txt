For every odd prime power \(q\), the three distinguished tangent images on the projective dual of the Pellikaan curve are unibranch cusps with value semigroup \(\langle q,(q+1)^2+1\rangle\) and delta invariant \((q-1)(q+1)^2/2\). In the original finite-field Tallini model, the three cusps form one Frobenius orbit of length three.
