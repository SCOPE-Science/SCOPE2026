The zero-run-even set \(A\) (OEIS A060142, including zero), a zero-density binary automatic set, satisfies the exact additive identities \(A+A=A+2A=\mathbb N_0\) and \(A+4A=\mathbb N_0\setminus\{n:n\equiv2\pmod4\}\). Its dyadic counting function is exactly \(F_{k+2}\) at \(2^k\), so it is a sparse additive basis of optimal order two.
