Exactly three genus-two curves over \(\mathbf F_5\), up to \(\mathbf F_5\)-isomorphism, attain the twelve-rational-point upper bound, and their six Weierstrass points have precisely the Frobenius partitions \((2,4)\), \((3,3)\) and \((6)\), one each. Their Weil polynomials have distinct middle coefficients 18, 19 and 17, so their respective \(\mathbf F_{25}\)-point counts are 26, 28 and 24.
