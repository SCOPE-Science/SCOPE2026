# Scientific review: three ordinary Möbius-ladder matching-complex homotopy types

**Correctness — PASS.** Graph edges and matchings are explicitly defined for precisely three finite graph orders. The standalone verifier enumerates the entire complexes by two methods, checks acyclicity of the complete reversed Hasse graphs, and computes full signed Morse differentials. Their integral unit-pivot ranks and zero residual matrices justify all cell cancellations. The proof reduces the CW complexes to wedges using the fact that attaching maps in the relevant lowest adjacent dimensions are determined by degrees. Independent full-chain mod-two ranks agree. Limit: this is a finite, computation-assisted proof, not an infinite-family theorem.

**Originality — PASS with residual bibliographic uncertainty.** The September 2026 full-text motivating paper explicitly names ordinary matching complexes as a future question while classifying different complexes. Index results for the eight- and ten-vertex Möbius ladders concern smaller orders, not these three. The full accessible Bayer–Jelić Milutinović–Vega polygonal-string and Matsushita open-grid works do not cover twisted-closure Möbius ladders. Published records of outerplanar results do not apply to nonplanar ladders. Query results do not amount to a proof of global novelty; unindexed work and unavailable underlying full records remain risks.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** This is a natural three-consecutive-order exact finite layer of an explicitly posed topology problem for a highly symmetric graph family. It distinguishes the ordinary and perfect matching complexes, determines three complete integral homotopy types, and reveals a mixed-dimensional example at sixteen graph vertices. Exact homotopy information is stronger than counting matchings, rational ranks or an unsupported sphere-wedge guess; the certificate supplies the actual attaching-degree calculations. The work does not extrapolate beyond the three specified orders.
