The ordinary matching complexes of the twelve-, fourteen-, and sixteen-vertex Möbius ladders have exact integral homotopy types \(\bigvee^{10}S^3\), \(\bigvee^{16}S^4\), and \(\bigvee^2S^4\vee\bigvee^4S^5\), respectively. Exhaustive discrete-Morse certificates with integral unit-pivot reductions establish these three cases of an explicitly proposed family problem, without claiming an infinite-family formula.
