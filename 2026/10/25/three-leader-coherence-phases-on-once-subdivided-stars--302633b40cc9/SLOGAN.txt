For three noise-free leaders on an unweighted star with every edge subdivided once, coherence-optimal placements change sharply with the number \(k\) of arms: all leaves for \(k=3\), one intermediate and two leaves on distinct other arms for \(k=4\), and the hub plus two leaves on distinct arms for every \(k\ge5\). The exact minimum inverse-grounded-Laplacian trace is respectively \(8/3\), \(27/4\), and \(3k-5\), with all optimizers classified.
