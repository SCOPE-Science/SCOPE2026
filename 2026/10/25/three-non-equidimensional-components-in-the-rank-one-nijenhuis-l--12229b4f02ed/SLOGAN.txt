The reduced rank-one Nijenhuis locus of the six-dimensional class-three niltriangular Lie algebra \(\mathfrak n_4\) has exactly three irreducible components of affine dimensions \(6,7,8\), uniformly over every field. Over \(\mathbb F_q\) its nonzero operators have the exact three-stratum census \(T(q)=\frac{(q^3-1)(q^6-1)}{q-1}+\frac{q^3(q^2-1)(q^3-1)}{q-1}+q^5(q-1)\), with precisely \((q^3-1)q^5\) satisfying \(N^2\ne0\).
