For three indistinguishable particles separated by at least \(r\) on an \(m\)-arm equilateral metric star, every nonempty positive-separation configuration space is a graph up to homotopy. Its circle rank changes exactly at \(r=L/2\) and \(r=L\), after which the space splits into \(\binom m3\) contractible components until the packing limit \(r=2L\).
