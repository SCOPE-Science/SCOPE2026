The complex of no-three-collinear subsets of the thirteen-point projective plane \(\operatorname{PG}(2,3)\) has homotopy type \(\bigvee^{66}S^3\vee\bigvee^3 M(\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z,2)\). Its three independent order-three torsion summands are certified by an explicit acyclic Morse matching and an integral boundary matrix with Smith factors \(1,3,3,3\).
