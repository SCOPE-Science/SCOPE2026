For synchronous edge-neighbor XOR on every complete multipartite graph with even-sized parts, the line-graph operator dies in three steps and has exactly \(c=r-1-\mathbf1_{\{r\text{ even}\}}\) Jordan blocks of length three. The sharp last-step survival fraction for \(r\ge3\) is \(1-2^{-c}\), depending only on the number of parts, not their sizes.
