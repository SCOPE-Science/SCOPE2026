# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem is about invariant entropy conditioned on the single-marker density of the bilateral prime-permitted zero-gap shift, not the global topological entropy and not actual consecutive-prime differences. Kac and Abramov reduce the variational problem to entropy per induced prime-return label, accounting explicitly for the all-zero atom; Gibbs inequality fixes the unique maximizer. The quantitative calculation uses the classical effective prime number theorem for two Laplace–Stieltjes sums, exact first two logarithmic moments of gamma densities, and inversion of the strictly decreasing return-length mean. Re-expansion in the observed variable \(Q=\log(1/t)\) gives the displayed constant \(\gamma^2/2+\pi^2/6-1/2\), including the otherwise easily missed \(-1/2\) term. A standalone finite numerical check of the exact truncated-prime Gibbs identity is supplementary; no finite computation is treated as the PNT proof.

## Originality — PASS under inspected evidence

The closest classical source, Baker–Ghenciu (arXiv:1411.7167v1), establishes topological and combinatorial facts about \(S\)-gap shifts, not fixed-marker-frequency prime asymptotics. Ramirez–Somers (arXiv:2501.08438v1) explicitly singles out prime-permitted gaps, but computes global topological entropy of generalized gap shifts, a distinct invariant. Haydarov–Kadyrov–Makhmudov–Mamayusupov (arXiv:2606.17031v1) supplies renewal-potential thermodynamic methods, not the arithmetic sparse-density expansion. The published *Log-periodic second-order entropy law for geometric gap shifts* (record 166969f68799) supplies essentially the same general renewal variational method but specializes to powers, whose counting law and leading rare-marker entropy are qualitatively different; it neither states nor implies the prime-specific Euler/\(\pi^2\) coefficients. Exact bibliographic and semantic searches for the prime-return formula found no directly covering source. Priority relative to unindexed arithmetic-renewal calculations remains a genuine limitation.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The permitted-gap set is natural and explicitly motivated in the literature. Its density of allowed return lengths differs from the much-studied geometric case and produces a measurable \(t\log\log(1/t)\) entropy penalty beyond unconstrained sparse Bernoulli markers. The explicit next two arithmetic constants quantify how quickly this canonical arithmetic symbolic system approaches the sparse limit. This is an exact asymptotic invariant and not an arbitrary finite-table calculation, renaming, or numerical extrapolation. The result is specialized and does not claim a broad new thermodynamic formalism.

## Scope and remaining risk

The theorem assumes classical quantitative PNT estimates, and the originality assessment is restricted to inspected accessible primary materials and retrieved research records. A previously published equivalent Tauberian expansion may exist outside that coverage. Its correctness and priority have not been independently audited.
