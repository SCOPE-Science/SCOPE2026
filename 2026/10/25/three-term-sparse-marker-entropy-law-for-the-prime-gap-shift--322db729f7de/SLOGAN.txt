For the binary shift whose consecutive 1s are separated by a prime number of zeros, the maximum entropy at rare marker density \(t\) is \(t[Q-\log Q+1+\gamma/Q+(\gamma^2/2+\pi^2/6-1/2)/Q^2+O(Q^{-3})]\), where \(Q=\log(1/t)\). The coefficients quantify an explicit arithmetic sparsity penalty; this concerns prime-valued permitted gaps, not actual consecutive-prime differences.
