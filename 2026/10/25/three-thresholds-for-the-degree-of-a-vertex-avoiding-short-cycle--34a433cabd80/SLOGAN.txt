In an Erdős–Rényi graph conditioned so one fixed vertex belongs to no triangle or four-cycle, the vertex's degree exhibits sharp transitions at \(\alpha=1/2\) and \(\alpha=2/3\) when \(p_n=c n^{-\alpha}\), while the conditioning-event probability changes again at \(\alpha=3/4\). An exact finite-size degree law and a critical large-deviation principle identify the mechanisms behind these three boundaries.
