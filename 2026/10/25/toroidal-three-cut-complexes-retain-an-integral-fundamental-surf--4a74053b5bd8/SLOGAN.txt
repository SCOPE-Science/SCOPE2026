For every toroidal product grid \(C_m\square C_n\) with \(m,n\ge5\), the Alexander dual of its ordinary three-cut complex is a two-torus wedged with \(mn(mn-13)/2\) circles. The original pure cut complex has exactly two nonzero, torsion-free reduced homology groups, including one fundamental-surface class below top dimension, and is nonshellable.
