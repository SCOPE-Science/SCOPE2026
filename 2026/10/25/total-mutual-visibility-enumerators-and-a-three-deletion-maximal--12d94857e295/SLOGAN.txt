Deleting a proper matching of size \(t\) from \(K_{p,q}\) yields a completely factorized total mutual-visibility polynomial, and its inclusion-maximal sets are determined by a one-unmatched-vertex versus three-matched-vertices omission rule in each part. The third deleted edge creates three distinct maximal-set sizes, lowering the least size by four while leaving the greatest size \(p+q-2\) unchanged.
