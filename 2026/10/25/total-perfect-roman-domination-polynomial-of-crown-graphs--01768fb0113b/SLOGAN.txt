Deleting a perfect matching from \(K_{n,n}\) raises the total perfect Roman domination minimum from \(4\) to \(6\) for every \(n\ge3\). The entire total-perfect-Roman weight polynomial of the resulting crown graph has an explicit finite formula, with exactly \(31\) minimum assignments at \(n=3\) and \(n^2(n-1)\) for every \(n\ge4\).
