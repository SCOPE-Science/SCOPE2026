For rank-one idempotent two-sided averaging operators on full matrix algebras, the matrix \(P(I_n)\) determines smooth components with an exact Gaussian-binomial census, and conjugation orbits reduce to trace-one matrix similarity classes with a correction exactly when the characteristic divides their size. At fixed field order, the corank of \(P(I_n)\) has a complete limiting distribution normalized by the first Rogers–Ramanujan product.
