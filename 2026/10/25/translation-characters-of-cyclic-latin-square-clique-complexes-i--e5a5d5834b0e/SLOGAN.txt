For every odd order \(n\ge3\), the complete translation representation on degree-two homology of the cyclic Latin-square graph clique complex has character multiplicities \(n-3+\mathbf1_{r=0}+\mathbf1_{s=0}+\mathbf1_{r=s}-\mathbf1_{(r,s)=(0,0)}\). This equivariant decomposition distinguishes three special Fourier directions even though the ordinary homology rank \((n-1)^3\) is already known.
