The 16 translations of the Shrikhande graph act on \(\widetilde H_2(\operatorname{Ind}(S);\mathbb Q)\) as \(\mathbb Q[(\mathbb Z/4)^2]\oplus\mathbf1\oplus V_{1,1}\oplus V_{1,2}\oplus V_{2,1}\). Consequently exactly two independent homology classes are translation-invariant, refining the known nonequivariant wedge of 23 two-spheres.
