# Scientific assessment

## Correctness
PASS. The Alexander dual has a complete one-skeleton, \(6N\) path triangles and \(N\) square tetrahedra. A three-stage free-face collapse leaves a triangulated two-torus with \(N(N-13)/2\) extra one-cells. Its top generator is fixed by translations. The reduced Hopf trace calculation, together with the sign twist in equivariant Alexander duality, determines the two characters exactly. Character averaging yields the stated parity formulas. Independently constructed twisted orbit chain complexes have the predicted exact rational ranks for five sample parameter pairs.

## Originality
PASS against inspected sources. The primary foundational and sequel papers do not state this toroidal two-cycle translation character or quotient parity phenomenon. The closest accessible earlier toroidal-grid cut calculation determines nonequivariant ranks, which cannot imply representations of a nontrivial symmetry action. Search results about planar square-grid symmetries, one-cycle powers, and total two-cut toroidal grids do not cover this exact ordinary three-cut product-of-cycles problem. Equivalent unindexed work remains possible.

## Value
PASS. The complete translation character determines rational homology for every intermediate translation quotient, and the full quotient loses its one-dimensional higher class precisely in the mixed-parity cases. This is a motivated representation-theoretic refinement of a natural toroidal graph-complex family, not merely a restatement of its ordinary Betti numbers.

## Risks and limits
The theorem concerns rational representations only. The finite checker has independently verified multiple parameter and parity regimes but does not replace the structural proof. The checked literature is not exhaustive, and integral torsion in quotients is not addressed.
