For the scale-one Johnson clique complex \(K(n,k)\), the geometric fixed space of a ground-set transposition is a wedge of exactly \(\binom{n-2}{k-1}-1\) circles and \(b(n-2,k)+b(n-2,k-2)\) two-spheres, for every \(n\ge6\) and \(3\le k\le n-3\). In particular, a natural involution of a simply connected sphere wedge has a fixed space with a large free fundamental group.
