For every finite tree and every subgroup of tree automorphisms, the rational top-homology character of its Stirling complex takes the same value \((-1)^{n-m+1}\) at **every nonidentity automorphism**. This gives the complete Specht decomposition for the star's leaf-permutation action asked for in Kozlov's Open Question 1, together with rational homology and fundamental groups of the finite symmetry quotients.
