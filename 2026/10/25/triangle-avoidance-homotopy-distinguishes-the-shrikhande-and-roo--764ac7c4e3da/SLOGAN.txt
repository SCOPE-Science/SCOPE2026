Although the Shrikhande and four-by-four rook graphs have identical strongly regular parameters \((16,6,2,2)\) and exactly 32 triangles each, their induced-triangle-avoiding vertex complexes have homotopy types \(\bigvee^9S^6\) and \(\bigvee^{15}S^5\), respectively. The rook case was already known as a two-matching chessboard complex; the new integral homotopy classification is the Shrikhande case and its dimension-separating comparison.
