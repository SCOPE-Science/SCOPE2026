# Scientific review: triangle-free graph joins

## Correctness — PASS
The exact admissible-face classification splits the triangle-free vertex-set complex into pairwise joins of factor independence complexes, together with full factor simplices. The incidence diagram is cofibrant because its latching inclusions are simplicial cofibrations; all incidence inclusions are nullhomotopic inside a join using any vertex of the opposite factor. The resulting graph-of-spaces splitting counts the incidence graph's cycle rank, the repeated suspensions, and all pairwise joins. The subsequent simplex attachments are homotopy cofibers of nullhomotopic inclusions. All steps hold integrally and without assuming independence complexes are connected. The cycle specialization uses an explicitly acknowledged classical recurrence. Ten complete mod-two face/homology checks matched predictions; finite tests are supplementary.

## Originality — PASS with residual uncertainty
The closest motivating primary paper, Ghosh–Selvaraja (arXiv:2602.09623v1), proves a disjoint-union identity for clique-free complexes (Proposition 3.2) and qualitative shellability results for certain chordal graphs. Its graph operation and conclusions do not entail an integral graph-join homotopy classification for arbitrary triangle-free factors. Adamaszek (arXiv:1106.6250) establishes independence-complex splittings and reports Kozlov's cycle recurrence, but does not compute the triangle-avoidance complex of graph joins. Targeted bibliographic comparisons and semantic mathematical record searches did not identify an equivalent or stronger theorem. A previously recorded matched-multipartite example is a proper specialization (two matching factors and isolated singleton factors), but does not establish this arbitrary-factor graph-of-spaces theorem. The general graph-of-spaces method itself is standard and is not claimed novel; an unidentified equivalent application remains a bibliographic risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result is a natural universal topology calculation for a dense graph operation on arbitrary triangle-free factors, not a finite table, arbitrary slice, or new notation for a known theorem. It gives a full all-size homotopy decomposition, detects a universal degree-one contribution that persists independently of factor sizes, and generates mixed-dimensional integral sphere wedges with independently variable dimensions and multiplicities for joined cycles. These are materially stronger than shellability or Euler characteristic alone.

## Boundaries
No claim of equivariance, factor graphs with triangles, or larger forbidden cliques. The finite checks are not infinite proofs. An independent mathematical audit has not been performed.
