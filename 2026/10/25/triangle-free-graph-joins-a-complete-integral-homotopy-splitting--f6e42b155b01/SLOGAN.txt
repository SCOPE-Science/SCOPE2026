For the graph join of any \(r\ge2\) nonempty triangle-free graphs, the triangle-avoidance complex splits integrally into a universal wedge of \(\binom{r-1}{2}\) circles, \(r-1\) suspensions of each factor's independence complex, and the pairwise joins of those independence complexes. Joining three five-cycles yields \(S^1\vee\bigvee^6S^2\vee\bigvee^3S^3\), an explicit mixed-dimensional infinite-family phenomenon.
