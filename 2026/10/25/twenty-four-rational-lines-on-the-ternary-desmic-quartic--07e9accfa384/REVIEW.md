# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The four F3-valued pencil parameters a+b+c=0 have three coefficient-zero tetrahedral members and one [1:1:1] member with A+B+C=2F. The formula F=sum x_i^4-(sum x_i²)^2 gives partial derivatives x_i(x_i²-S), and the singularity criterion over the algebraic closure leaves only the four coordinate and eight full-sign points with nonsingular local quadratic terms. For an F3 projective point, a coordinate is nonzero precisely when its square is 1; F=binomial(r,2) mod 3, so the permitted nonzero counts r=1,3,4 yield 4+16+8=28 rational points. An independent canonical RREF enumeration of all (4 choose 2)_3=130 two-dimensional subspaces tests all five coefficients of F(su+tv) and finds exactly 24 rational lines, 16 having three singular points and eight having none; exact incidence is degree 4 at each of 12 nodes and degree 3 at each of 16 smooth points. The eight extra lines are exactly the basis pairs (1,0,a,b),(0,1,c,d) with a,b,c,d in {±1}, ac+bd=0. The finite complete enumeration establishes all F3-defined lines only, not geometric lines over all field extensions.

## originality

PASS

The 12 desmic nodes and 16 Reye lines are a classical configuration, explicitly present in Dolgachev–Kondō and not novel. The characteristic-three special F3 rational pencil member, its 8 additional rational lines, exhaustive 24-line classification and exact rational-point incidence are the residual new result. Other literature containing 24-line configurations concerns distinct cubic-line complexes or degree-six K3 surfaces, not this quartic and field.
