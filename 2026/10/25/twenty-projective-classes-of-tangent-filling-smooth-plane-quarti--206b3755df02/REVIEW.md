# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The projective-incidence lower bound is elementary and the equality example has a direct algebraic-closure smoothness proof. Two separately implemented exact enumerators recover 2,782 rationally nonsingular tangent-filling candidates, 2,656 geometrically smooth equations, 20 projective orbits, and the same seven-row joint distribution. The first computes all 168 projective transformations and classifies all 32,767 nonzero quartics; the second uses only three explicit generators and tests each candidate orbit. Geometric smoothness is decided by unit Gröbner ideals on every projective affine chart, not by bounded finite-extension sampling. The finite proof rests on recomputable code included in the package.

## Originality
PASS, with publication-priority risk. Asgarli–Ghioca (arXiv:2302.13420) supplies the named tangent-filling problem but no inspected binary-quartic census. Nart–Ritzenthaler (arXiv:math/0312366) gives the general smooth characteristic-two quartic classification, which supplies the underlying objects, but its inspected mathematical statements do not give the tangent-incidence partition. Earlier arithmetic quartic results concerning rational Gauss bijections, rational theta lines, flexes, or automorphism profiles do not imply the full 20-class tangent-filling subset; their overlapping 24 Gauss-bijective equations are acknowledged as previously known. A previously unindexed invariant computation based on the Nart–Ritzenthaler normal forms remains a real originality risk; unsuccessful searching is not taken as proof of priority.

## Value
PASS. Tangent-filling is a motivated dual-plane blocking condition introduced in current finite-field arithmetic geometry, and binary smooth quartics form the first nonhyperelliptic genus-three canonical family over the smallest field. The new classification gives its complete rational-tangent incidence locus, a sharp minimum of three rational curve points with four distinct equality classes, and 616 examples where tangent filling differs from point blocking. The invariant is not an arbitrary coefficient slice or a restatement of the known 78-class curve census.

## Literature and limits
The literature comparison treats published smooth-quartic models as prior coverage of the underlying moduli, not as a proof that the new tangent-line statistic was previously enumerated. The theorem applies only over \(\mathbf F_2\); the exact enumeration is independently replayable but has no independent external audit. Supporting sources: arXiv:2302.13420 and arXiv:math/0312366.
