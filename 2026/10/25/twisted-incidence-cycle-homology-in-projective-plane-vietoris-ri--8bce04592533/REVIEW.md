# Scientific review: equivariant incidence-cycle homology

**Correctness — PASS.** The underlying scale-two simplices admit an exact cover by two contractible subcomplexes intersecting in the incidence graph; this is a proof for every finite projective plane, not an extrapolation. The chain-level Mayer–Vietoris sequence, with its signed inclusion into the ordered pair of cover summands, establishes the integral connecting isomorphism and its change of sign under point–line exchange. The elementary graph-chain Lefschetz formula then gives all symmetry traces. The Fano specialization was verified by a complete enumeration of the \(336\)-element group, all \(331\) Rips faces, exact signed fixed-simplex traces, and mod-two simplicial boundary ranks. Character squared norm \(1\) proves absolute irreducibility. A separate script recomputes the 3461-simplex Heawood matching complex, all six mod-two boundary ranks, eight Sylow-seven subgroups, and all 336 residual-character equalities for the cross-complex comparison. Both verification programs are included and executable.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** The primary 2021 graph-power persistence study gives general homological bounds but no plane-incidence symmetry module. The most directly relevant published plane-incidence Rips theorem already proves \(\operatorname{VR}_{\le2}(\Gamma)\simeq\Sigma\Gamma\) **nonequivariantly**; its original sphere count is not claimed as new. Its explicit \(U\cup V\) cover does not itself specify the orientation sign on the equivariant Mayer–Vietoris connecting isomorphism. The published Heawood matching-complex classification determines an eight-dimensional module \(W\) for a different simplicial complex, but not the Rips homology action or the sign-twist formula. The new contribution is the uniform integral \(G\)-module identification, the full Fano trace character and the cross-complex identification with \(W\). Remaining risk: an equivalent result may be indexed in the specialist literature under incidence-geometry or polarity terminology.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The phenomenon is not merely another Betti number: consecutive persistent stages each have \(q^3\) generators but dualities act with opposite trace, and the integral lattice has a canonical geometric realization as a twisted graph cycle lattice. For the canonical Fano configuration, the entire \(336\)-element character is absolutely irreducible, the polarity traces distinguish adjacent degrees, and a previously known irreducible matching-homology constituent gains a second topological realization. These conclusions motivate further investigation of equivariant persistence and the comparison of two unrelated graph-complex constructions.
