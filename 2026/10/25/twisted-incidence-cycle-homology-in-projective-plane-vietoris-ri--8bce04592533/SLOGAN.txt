For every finite projective plane, the scale-two Vietoris–Rips integral second-homology module of its incidence graph is the **point–line-duality sign twist** of the graph's cycle lattice, although the preceding first-homology group has the untwisted action. In the Fano plane this yields an absolutely irreducible eight-dimensional representation of all \(336\) automorphisms, with polarity traces changing from \(-2\) to \(+2\); this representation also realizes the known eight-dimensional constituent of the distinct Heawood matching complex.
