Random Minkowski symmetrization about only two fixed mirror lines has an exact Fourier-series expected area deficit: rational angle ratios retain a positive resonance defect, while irrational ratios tend to a disk without a uniform exponential rate. For a natural real-analytic strictly convex body and bounded-type irrational angles, the expected deficit has sharp stretched-exponential order \(\exp[-\Theta(t^{1/3})]\).
