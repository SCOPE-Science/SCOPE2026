Independently resetting the Brownian diffusivity and the particle positions at rates \(a\) and \(b\) gives an exact two-exponential squared-position covariance. The cross-coordinate/own-coordinate covariance ratio increases with lag and tends to one precisely for \(a\le b\); for \(a>b\) it stays strictly below one asymptotically, even though both covariances themselves vanish.
