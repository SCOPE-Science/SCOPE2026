At Hamming scale three, flipping two coordinates fixes a space homotopy equivalent to the two-skeleton of a smaller cube plus one independent three-sphere for every square of that cube. For \(n\ge4\), its integral Betti numbers are exactly \(\beta_2=2^{n-2}-(n-2)2^{n-3}+2^{n-4}\binom{n-2}{2}-1\) and \(\beta_3=2^{n-4}\binom{n-2}{2}\).
