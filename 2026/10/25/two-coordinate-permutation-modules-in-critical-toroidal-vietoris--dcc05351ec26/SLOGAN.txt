At the critical one-third scale, the rational second homology of every square toroidal grid \((\mathbb Z/3k\mathbb Z)^2\), \(k\ge2\), becomes the sum of two coordinate-coset permutation modules with one trivial line removed. Four axial order-three translations have exceptional trace \(3k^2-1\), while every other nonidentity translation has trace \(-1\); the known sphere-wedge count alone does not determine this symmetry information.
