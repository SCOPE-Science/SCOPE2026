For any bouquet of at least two complete graph blocks meeting in one vertex, its \(t\)-clique-avoidance complex is contractible below a sharp block-size threshold and otherwise splits integrally into two explicitly counted sphere wedges, in dimensions \(r(t-1)-1\) and \(r(t-2)\). Its natural within-block permutation actions on those homology groups are tensor products of exterior powers of integral augmentation lattices.
