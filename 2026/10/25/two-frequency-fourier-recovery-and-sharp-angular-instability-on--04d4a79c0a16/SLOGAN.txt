Two distinct low-frequency spherical Fourier traces uniquely recover independent angular densities on two concentric source spheres, whereas a single trace never does. The exact smallest high-degree singular value is asymptotic to \(\kappa_\ell(au)^\ell(b^2-a^2)(v^2-u^2)/(4(\ell+d/2))\), exposing a sharp factorial instability despite uniqueness.
