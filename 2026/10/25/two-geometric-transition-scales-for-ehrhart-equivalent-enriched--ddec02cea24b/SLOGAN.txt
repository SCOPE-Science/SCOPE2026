For two comparable antichains, the Ehrhart-equivalent enriched order and enriched chain polytopes have a nonnegative **pointwise Euclidean width gap** equal to \((|\sum_jv_j|-|\|v\|_1-\|u\|_1|)_+\), with a sharp Gaussian critical window at equal block sizes. The enriched order member is tangential with dimension-independent golden-ratio inradius, yet its minimum enclosing sphere changes contact orbit at the different threshold \(p=4q\).
