The strongly regular Shrikhande graph and four-by-four rook graph have the same parameters \((16,6,2,2)\), but their two-independence complexes are integrally homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{57}S^3\) and \(\bigvee^{105}S^3\), respectively. Complete acyclic discrete-Morse matchings determine both homotopy types, not just their mod-two homology.
