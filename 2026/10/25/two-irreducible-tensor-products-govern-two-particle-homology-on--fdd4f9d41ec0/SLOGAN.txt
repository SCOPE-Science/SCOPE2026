For every \(m,n\ge4\), the complete independent-vertex-permutation representation on rational second homology of two unordered collision-free particles on \(K_{m,n}\) is exactly \(S_m^{(m-2,2)}\boxtimes S_n^{(n-2,1,1)}\oplus S_m^{(m-2,1,1)}\boxtimes S_n^{(n-2,2)}\). The formula identifies two multiplicity-one irreducible symmetry channels, not merely the known total Betti number.
