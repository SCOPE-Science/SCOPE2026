On a weighted clique windmill, two noiseless leaders switch from hub-plus-peripheral to different-petal peripherals at \(b=3\) for exactly three petals, and at \(b=(2s-3)/(2s-1)\) for two petals; with four or more petals, a hub leader is always optimal. Exact grounded-inverse trace formulas prove the classification for every real internal conductance \(b>0\), with same-petal pairs strictly suboptimal.
