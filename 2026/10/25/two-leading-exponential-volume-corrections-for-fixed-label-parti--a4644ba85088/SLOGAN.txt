For every fixed label bound \(n\ge5\), the normalized volume of the partial permutohedron has its first two exponential deficits determined exactly: \(v(m,n)=S^m-mA^m-\binom m2(m-B)B^{m-1}+O_n(m^{2n-3}C^m)\), where \(S=\binom{n+1}2\), \(A=\binom n2\), \(B=\binom{n-1}2\), and \(C=\binom{n-2}2\). The cancellation of overlapping coordinate caps with pair-facet truncations pushes the remainder all the way to the next conjectured exponential base.
