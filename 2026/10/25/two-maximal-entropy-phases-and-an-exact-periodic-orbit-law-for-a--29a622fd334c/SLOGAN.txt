The binary shift avoiding all antisquares except `01` and `10` has exactly two complementary ergodic measures of maximal entropy, with distinct symbol-one frequencies \(\delta=1/[\psi^2(3\psi-2)]\) and \(1-\delta\). Its exact periodic-orbit zeta function is \(1/[(1+z^3)^2(1-z-z^3)^2]\), where \(\psi^3=\psi^2+1\).
