The twenty-vertex Folkman graph has a vertex-ball nerve filtration with simultaneous integral classes in degrees one and two at radius one, and in degrees three and four at radius two. Its exact reduced barcode has multiplicities 19 in degree zero, 6 and 15 in degrees one and two, and 5 and 15 in degrees three and four; all homology is torsion-free, and the complex becomes a full simplex at radius three. This classifies the vertex-ball nerve, not the intervening odd stages of the graph-geometric Čech filtration.
