For Gaussian Fock monomials of degree \(N\), the optimally chosen radius of an \(m=o(\sqrt N)\) regular-polygon value-and-first-derivative kernel orbit captures asymptotically \(2m/\sqrt{2\pi eN}\) of the squared norm. Unlike value-only sampling, near-optimal first-jet radii separate into two shells with squared radius \(N\pm\sqrt N\).
