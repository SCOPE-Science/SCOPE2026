Every Haar-random central plane section of a right square antiprism is almost surely a hexagon or an octagon, regardless of positive aspect ratio, and the exact octagon probability increases strictly from zero to one with height. For the equal-edge solid it equals \((8/\pi)\arccos((2\sqrt2-1)/7)-3=0.3270440971384682\ldots\), with quadratic thin-limit and inverse-height tall-limit laws.
