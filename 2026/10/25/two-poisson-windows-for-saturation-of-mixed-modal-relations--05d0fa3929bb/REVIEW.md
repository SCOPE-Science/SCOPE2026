# Review of two mixed-modal saturation windows

## Correctness — PASS
Relational composition inclusion is exactly the universal-valuation meaning of the interaction axiom. Every sampled relation contains the diagonal, making the minimal admissible third relation reflexive. The proof establishes exact off-diagonal absence probabilities, uniform \(h\)-fold joint-absence estimates, two Poisson factorial-moment limits, and intermediate-regime concentration. The independent checker exhaustively verifies the elementary finite correspondence for all reflexive triples through three worlds and checks the exact rational first moments. A finite computation is not used as an asymptotic proof.
