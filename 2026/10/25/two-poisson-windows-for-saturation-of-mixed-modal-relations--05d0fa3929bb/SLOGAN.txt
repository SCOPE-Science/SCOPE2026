Two independent random reflexive modalities have a first Poisson window at \(np_n^2=\log n+t\), where the number of third modalities compatible with the mixed axiom converges in law to \(2^{\operatorname{Poisson}(e^{-2t})}\). Their two ordered compositions do not both become universally accessible until a second, asymptotically independent Poisson window at \(np_n^2=2\log n+t\).
