For Hermite/Fock ladders with photon-number gap \(r>2\lfloor(d-1)/2\rfloor\), two exact homodyne quadrature distributions recover every \(d\)-level density matrix unless a phase-gap resonance occurs. The dimension of the blind space is precisely \(\sum_{k=1}^{d-1}(d-k)\mathbf1_{\{rk\Delta\in\pi\mathbb Z\}}\), yielding an explicit denominator law at rational phase differences.
