For every plane-curve singularity of multiplicity at least three and every rank \(d\), the fixed-minimal-generator punctual Quot locus of length \(d+2\) is a Grassmannian stratum together with a rank-\(2d-1\) vector bundle over \(\mathbf P^{d-1}\times\mathbf P^1\). Its motivic class and the second normalized Quot-series coefficient are explicit and independent of the higher singularity equation.
