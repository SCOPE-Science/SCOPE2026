For the 32-vertex Dyck graph, the 168 two-point product tori involving a six-cycle span all 104 rational second-homology directions but miss the full integral lattice by exactly \((\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^2\). The missing two integral generators can be supplied by octagon–octagon tori from either one of the graph's two symmetry orbits, linking its classical hexagonal and octagonal regular-map geometries.
