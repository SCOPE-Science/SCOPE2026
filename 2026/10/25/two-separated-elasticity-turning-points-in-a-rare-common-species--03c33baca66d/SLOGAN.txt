In a two-species independent-Poisson species–area model, a sufficiently rare second species forces **exactly two** turning points of the local log–log richness exponent: a valley at area \(\log(1/\varepsilon)+\log\log(1/\varepsilon)+o(1)\), and a secondary peak at area asymptotic to \(0.768039047\ldots/\varepsilon\). The secondary peak exponent approaches the universal \(0.2319609529\ldots\), sharply quantifying a scale-dependent shape phenomenon known previously in qualitative form.
