Every uniform-density right regular \(n\)-gonal bipyramid has exactly three nondegenerate gravitational-equilibrium signatures as its two apex heights vary: \((2n,3n,n+2)\), then \((n,2n,n+2)\), then \((n,n,2)\). Two sharp simultaneous \(n\)-fold equilibrium collisions occur at the dimensionless asymmetries \(\tau=\cos^2(\pi/n)\) and \(\tau=1\), and the intervening phase has width \(\sin^2(\pi/n)\). The polyhedral mechanical complexity therefore jumps sharply from \(0\) to \(2n\) to \(4n\) without changing the face vector.
