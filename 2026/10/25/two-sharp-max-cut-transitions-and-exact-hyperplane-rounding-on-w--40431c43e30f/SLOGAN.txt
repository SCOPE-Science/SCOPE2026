On a weighted Möbius ladder, the Max-Cut SDP loses exactness at antipodal-edge weight \(1-\cos(\pi/n)\), whereas the optimal integer cut changes at \(2/n\) for every even \(n\ge4\). Above the second threshold, random-hyperplane rounding of the unique rank-two SDP optimum always returns an optimal cut despite a strictly positive SDP integrality gap; the gap ratio is maximized exactly at \(2/n\).
