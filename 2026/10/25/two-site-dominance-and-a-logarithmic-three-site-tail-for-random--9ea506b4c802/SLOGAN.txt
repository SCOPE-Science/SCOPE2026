For three uniform random points in a disk, a nearly maximal minimum enclosing circle is overwhelmingly supported by a diametral pair: the two-site event has probability asymptotic to \(128\sqrt2\,\varepsilon^{5/2}/(5\pi)\), while a three-site support has the strictly smaller logarithmic tail \(16\varepsilon^3\log(1/\varepsilon)/\pi^2\). The conditional chance of a three-site support therefore decays like \(5\sqrt\varepsilon\log(1/\varepsilon)/(8\sqrt2\pi)\).
