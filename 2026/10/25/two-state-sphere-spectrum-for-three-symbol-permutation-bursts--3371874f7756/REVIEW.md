# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The decomposition into changed-position supports is injective and exhaustive: a nontrivial three-position permutation either swaps one unequal pair at separation one or two, or induces one of two three-cycles on three distinct letters. The weighted transition states retain exactly the equality information needed for the next score. This proves the population identity for every alphabet size and length in scope. The finite covariance identities and extremal leading coefficients follow by explicit conditional enumeration and the characteristic-polynomial recurrence, respectively. The limit uses a bounded two-dependent central limit theorem with strictly positive computed variance. The standalone verifier independently reconstructs all permutation outputs at 337,673 center words and agrees with the population, moment and extremal formulas; computation supports, but does not replace, the general proofs.

## Originality

PASS within inspected sources, with residual risk. The October 2026 primary paper gives a general **binary** pointwise volume formula and binary three-position permutation error classification; those results are explicitly credited and excluded from the novel contribution. A related binary length-four output-sphere distribution addresses a different window size. No inspected primary result or mathematical record supplies the finite-alphabet two-state generating law, nonbinary three-cycle contributions, exact all-alphabet variance and full extremal population counts. A different indexing or reduction could nevertheless cover some of these statements; full independence or priority is not certified.

## Value

PASS. Sphere-size variability matters for sphere packing, probabilistic center selection, and finite-length modeling of the weight-preserving permutation-burst channel. The exact polynomial simultaneously gives every finite-length ball-size count, while the moment and Gaussian laws describe typical ambiguity; the sharp extrema quantify worst-case and best-case center populations. The four-symbol DNA alphabet is within scope. The two-state reduction is a structural compression of a length-three local-permutation process, not merely a single small-parameter enumeration. No gain in actual coding redundancy or decoding performance is asserted.

## Limitations

The claim treats a single exactly-three-position non-wrapping permutation, not multiple bursts or variable-length edits. The primary paper's binary pointwise enumeration is prior knowledge. The CLT is classical given the proved local decomposition. Indexed searches do not prove novelty and external validation is outstanding.
