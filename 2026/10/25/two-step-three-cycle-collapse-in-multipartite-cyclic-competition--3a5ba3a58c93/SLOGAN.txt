For synchronous three-color cyclic competition on every complete multipartite graph with at least three nonempty parts, all states enter a fixed point or a primitive three-cycle within two steps, and the complete labelled recurrent-state census is an explicit seven-support inclusion-exclusion sum. Adding a third part eliminates the period-four cycles possible in bipartite networks, with a sharp two-step transient bound whenever any part has at least two vertices.
