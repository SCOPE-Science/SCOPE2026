For a Brownian search with a hidden fixed target at either \(-L\) or \(+L\), optimizing over every possible reset-position law on \([-a,a]\) forces all mass onto the two endpoints and yields a unique, explicit optimal bias with two sharp prior thresholds. With equally likely targets, equal endpoint resetting simultaneously minimizes mean and worst-target first-passage time and maximizes the completion-time Laplace transform at every positive discount.
