For any full-dimensional convex polytope in dimension \(d\ge2\), the probability that two independent uniform points are within \(\varepsilon\) of the Euclidean diameter has explicit, positive \(\varepsilon^{2d}\) and \(\varepsilon^{2d+1}\) coefficients computed from its diametral vertex cones. Among fixed-volume rectangular boxes, cubes uniquely minimize the leading coefficient; the next coefficient quantifies the transverse correction invisible to projection-only extreme-distance asymptotics.
