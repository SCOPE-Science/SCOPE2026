The logarithmic Mahler measure of a degree-\(n-1\) independent complex-Gaussian polynomial has sharp variance \(C/n+A/n^3+o(n^{-3})\), with explicit positive sinc–dilogarithm constants \(C\approx0.3311986\) and \(A\approx0.039306\). The entire \(n^{-2}\) term vanishes because the first Gaussian logarithmic-chaos component contributes exactly \(1/(4n)\).
