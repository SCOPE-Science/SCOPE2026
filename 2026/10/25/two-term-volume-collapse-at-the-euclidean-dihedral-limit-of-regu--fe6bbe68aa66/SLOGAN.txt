At the Euclidean dihedral limit, every regular hyperbolic \(d\)-simplex loses volume as an explicit constant times \((\arccos(1/d)-\alpha)^{d/2}\), with a sharp first correction. In dimension four the exact collapse is \(10\sqrt5\,t^2(1+13\sqrt{15}\,t/15+O(t^2))\), quantifying the simplex branch on the low-angle side of the known hyperbolic four-polytope minimizer switch.
