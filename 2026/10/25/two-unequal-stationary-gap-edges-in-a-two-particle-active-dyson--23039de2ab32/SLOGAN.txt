The stationary separation of two logarithmically repelling run-and-tumble particles in a harmonic trap has unequal power-law edges with exact exponents \(\alpha_a=2\gamma a/[\lambda(a+b)]\) and \(\alpha_b=2\gamma b/[\lambda(a+b)]\). Two distinct flip-rate thresholds create a regime where the small-gap density diverges but the large-gap density vanishes, a behavior impossible to infer from single-particle edge exponents alone.
