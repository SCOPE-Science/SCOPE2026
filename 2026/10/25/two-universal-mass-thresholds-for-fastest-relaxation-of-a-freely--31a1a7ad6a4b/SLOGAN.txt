A free spring-linked two-mass oscillator with one grounded viscous damper has a unique rate-optimal friction coefficient for every mass ratio. Two universal thresholds, \(m_1/m_2=1/8\) and \(m_1/m_2=1/4\), separate defective double-root optimization, equal-real-part optimization, and a slow-complex-pair optimum; at the first threshold the nonzero spectrum has a defective triple root.
