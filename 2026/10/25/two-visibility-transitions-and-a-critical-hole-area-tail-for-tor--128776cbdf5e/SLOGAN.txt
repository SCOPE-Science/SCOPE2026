For a solid circular torus viewed from a Haar-random direction, the shadow has three exact regimes: an embedded annulus, an annulus with overlapping normal offsets, and a simply connected region, with probabilities \(1-\sqrt{s}\), \(\sqrt{s}-s\), and \(s\) for tube-to-major-radius ratio \(s\). The collapsing hole obeys a sharp \(3/2\)-power area law, producing a \(2/3\)-power onset in its random-area distribution.
