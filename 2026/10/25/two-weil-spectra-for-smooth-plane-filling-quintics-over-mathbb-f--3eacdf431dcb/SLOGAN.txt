Smooth plane-filling quintics over \(\mathbb F_3\) have exactly two possible zeta numerators: the cube \((1+3T+5T^2+9T^3+9T^4)^3\) and an irreducible degree-twelve Weil polynomial. Their genus-six Jacobians are, respectively, isogenous to a cube of a simple abelian surface and simple over \(\mathbb F_3\).
