# Review

## Correctness — PASS
The canonical divisor/class-group criterion reduces Cartierness of \(mK\) to an integer cocircuit-weight equation. For \(U_{r,n}\), comparing all cocircuits of size \(n-r+1\) forces a constant weight, and the least positive multiple is exactly \((n-r+1)/\gcd(n-r+1,2)\) for \(r\ge2\). The rank-one case is handled separately.

## Originality — PASS
The closest literature gives a general Gorenstein/Q-Gorenstein criterion and the canonical orbit-divisor framework. The inspected sources do not state the minimal Cartier-index formula for the full uniform family or the unboundedness consequence. The general criterion is prior work; the contribution is the exact index computation.

## Value — PASS
The index measures how far a \(\mathbb Q\)-Gorenstein variety is from having Cartier canonical divisor. The formula identifies the first Cartier pluricanonical multiple across a natural family and proves that these indices are unbounded already in rank two.

## Closest literature and limitations
The principal source is Porcher’s cocircuit-weight criterion for matroid Schubert varieties; Crowley–Proudfoot provide the canonical divisor framework. This result does not classify singularity types or indices for arbitrary matroids. The literature comparison was bounded, and no independent audit has been performed.
