Starting with one supporter of each peripheral opinion and \(n-d\) hub supporters in a star-compatible voter model, every fully coexisting terminal split is equally likely, with probability \((n-d)!(2d-1)!/(n+d-1)!\). Full peripheral coexistence occurs with probability asymptotic to \((2d-1)!n^{-d}/(d-1)!\); conditional terminal proportions converge to the uniform Dirichlet simplex law.
