For cyclic width-\(r\) incidence posets with \(n\ge2r+1\), the complete bivariate polynomial of two-sided one-point types has a uniform closed form and totals exactly \(nr2^{r-1}\). All nonalgebraic one-types grow asymptotically as \(2\rho_r^n\), where \(\rho_r^{r-1}(\rho_r-2)^2=1\), and the growth base decreases strictly to \(2\) as the incidence width increases.
