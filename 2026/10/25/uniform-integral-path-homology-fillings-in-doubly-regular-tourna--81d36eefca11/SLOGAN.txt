Every doubly regular tournament of order \(4t+3\) with \(t\ge1\) has vanishing first **integral regular path homology**, and every integral one-cycle admits a transitive-triangle filling with \(\ell_1\)-size at most three times the cycle size. Exactly \(t\) universal source-or-sink apexes fill each directed triangle, whereas the directed three-cycle has nonzero first path homology.
