For every finite forest, edge collisions in a uniformly random equivalence relation factor into independent Bernoulli variables after conditioning on Stam's box count. Uniformly over all forest sizes and shapes, their number has a moving-parameter Poisson approximation, and the probability that \(r\) independent equivalence relations avoid every forest edge is \(\exp(-r|E(F)|W(n)/n)(1+o(1))\), even for spanning trees where this is only \((W(n)/n)^r(1+o(1))\).
