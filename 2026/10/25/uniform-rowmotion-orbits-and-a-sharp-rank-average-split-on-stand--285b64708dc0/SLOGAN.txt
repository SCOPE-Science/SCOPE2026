For every \(n\ge3\), order-ideal rowmotion on the dimension-\(n\) standard example has exactly one three-cycle, \(n\) five-cycles, and \(2^n-2n-2\) two-cycles; its permutation order is only 15 or 30. The ideal-cardinality orbit average is uniformly \(n\) at \(n=3\), but for every \(n\ge4\) equal-length two-cycles split into means \(n/2\) and \(3n/2\).
