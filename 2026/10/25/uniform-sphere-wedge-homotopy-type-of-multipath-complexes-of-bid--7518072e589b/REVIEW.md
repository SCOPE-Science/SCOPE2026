# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
A face is a cyclic word over \(\{+, -,0\}\) with adjacent nonzero signs equal and not all one sign. Mixed-sign faces have at least two zero positions, so all top-dimensional simplices are pure-sign faces missing exactly one arc. For each sign their integral top boundary kernel is generated by the embedded boundary sphere; the two kernels are disjointly supported, furnishing the full top-homology basis as actual homotopy maps. Theorem 2.8 of [1] establishes simple connectivity for all \(n\ge6\); the lower vanishing degree for \(n\equiv0\pmod4\) is promoted to connectivity by Hurewicz. Theorem 2.10 gives the single lower free homology group. Hurewicz realizes its generators, and a wedge of those maps and the two orientation sphere inclusions induces an integral homology isomorphism between simply connected CW complexes, hence a homotopy equivalence by Whitehead. Finite enumeration and exhaustive acyclic matching corroborate parameters \(6\le n\le13\). This is an infinite proof conditional on published theorems, not a finite-data extrapolation.

## Originality — PASS, with residual bibliographic risk
The motivating January 2026 paper [1] explicitly says that its homology computation does not establish a sphere wedge and leaves the homotopy question open. The new geometric generator lemma bridges that exact gap. An earlier calculation for this same family covers only \(n\in\{3,4,5\}\), intentionally excluded here. Other nearest indexed records concern rational dihedral homology characters, quotient fundamental groups and bidirected double stars, rather than the full nonequivariant homotopy for all \(n\ge6\). Searches for aliases, same-family theorems and stronger coverage found no dominating result. Unindexed papers and later revisions remain a residual risk; no claim of exhaustive literature coverage is made.

## Value — PASS
This settles the explicitly posed infinite-family homotopy classification for every \(n\ge6\), rather than supplying a routine Betti computation or isolated small case. The top-cycle geometric realization rules out nontrivial attaching-map ambiguity left by the previously published homology and connectivity results; these invariants alone do not imply the stated wedge decomposition. The result also provides a transparent structural explanation for the two top-dimensional spheres across all parameters.
