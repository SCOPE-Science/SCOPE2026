# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The proof gives a measure-preserving partition of the translated zonotope into its central cube and exactly one swept positive facet for each coordinate. The Jacobians are one, so the mixture is uniform by construction. Direct integration within the pieces yields the two covariance eigenvalues and positive cross-coordinate covariance. The determinant identity and logarithmic limit follow by substitution and elementary asymptotics. A coupling for fixed-coordinate marginals has explicitly bounded failure probability; the empirical-mean conclusion uses the ordinary independent-uniform central limit theorem. Exact rational polygon integrations and independent rejection sampling corroborate, but do not replace, these arguments. Risks: careful distinction between sampling uniformly in the body and sampling uniformly in its generator parameter cube; the proof consistently uses the former.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

The 2020 founding paper supplies the defining family and its combinatorics; the 2024 volume paper supplies the zonotope representation and scalar volume determinant. The 2025 lower-Lebesgue-volume paper concerns face measures and incidence rather than the interior second-moment tensor, at least as described in its accessible abstract. Previous records concerning circumradii, ellipsoid contacts and spherical widths operate on vertices, enclosing ellipsoids or directions, not the probability law of a uniform interior point. No covering statement was found in targeted title, covariance, conditional-range and indexed mathematical-record searches. The 2025 full text was not available during comparison, and an equivalent conditional-sampling formula under another name remains a specific residual risk.

## Value — PASS

The result determines a natural affine-invariant statistic (the isotropic constant) and all Euclidean covariance eigenvalues for a nontrivial, arbitrarily dimensional zonotope family. The unexpected persistent pairwise dependence, uniform latent common shift, and universal \(-\log d\) correction explain how a body may approach the cube isotropic scale while exhibiting a covariance eigenvalue growing linearly with dimension. These features are not simply a restatement of the already-known volume determinant.

## Boundaries

Fixed real parameters \(0<a<\ell\) are necessary for the limiting statements; changing cants are not covered. Finite checker samples do not establish infinite-limit results. All source attributions and the remaining novelty risk appear in the main result and structured review.
