Uniform points in an isocanted cube admit a disjoint-cube-and-swept-facets mixture, giving the complete covariance spectrum and the sharp universal isotropic correction \(d\log(12L(K_d)^2)+\log d\to2\). Their fixed coordinate marginals approach a shared-uniform-shift law, with an explicit total-variation error of order \(1/d\) for fixed positive cant.
