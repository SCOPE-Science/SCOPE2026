For diagonal-base pyramids in the unit \(n\)-cube, the unique optimal section is displaced from the central section by \(1/6+2/(45n)+O(n^{-2})\), and its volume exceeds the central benchmark by the sharp factor \(1+1/(6n)+7/(120n^2)+O(n^{-3})\). This optimizes the diagonal family, not all cube pyramids.
