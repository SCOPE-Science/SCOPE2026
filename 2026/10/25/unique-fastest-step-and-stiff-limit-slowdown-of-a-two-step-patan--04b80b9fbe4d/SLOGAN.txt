The MPE-embedded two-step Patankar multistep method is positive and conservative at every step size, but its two-state equilibrium relaxation is uniquely fastest at \(h=0.536566\ldots\) and slows to a contraction factor \(1-1/(2h)+O(h^{-2})\) for very large steps. Its exact local spectral factor is always worse than the backward-Euler factor on the same exchange mode.
