Under stationary Gaussian exponential dephasing, exactly two ideal control inversions have a unique globally optimal placement: symmetric pulses whose middle duration \(b\) obeys \(T/\tau=2b+2\log\cosh(b/2)\). Their first normalized pulse time drifts strictly from \(1/4\) toward \(1/3\), making conventional quarter–three-quarter timing strictly suboptimal at every positive finite observation-to-correlation ratio.
