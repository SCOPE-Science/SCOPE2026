For a Poisson-reset Brownian particle in linear shear, the age of a rare large-longitudinal-displacement excursion and its transverse endpoint exhibit correlated Gaussian fluctuations with **universal correlation magnitude** \(1/\sqrt3\). The conditioned path follows cubic and parabolic normalized profiles; the cubic bridge shape and marginal tail have known random-acceleration antecedents, while the joint age–endpoint law supplies the proposed contribution.
