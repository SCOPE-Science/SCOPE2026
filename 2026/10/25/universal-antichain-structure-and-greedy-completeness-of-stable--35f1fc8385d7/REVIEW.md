# Review of the antichain realization theorem

## Correctness

PASS. SUB proves downward closure of individual rationality. Given an IR matching, IRP upgrades an accepted singleton to retention of both the old holding and the new edge, so CY singleton blocks coincide with IR one-edge extensions; SUB reduces every set block to such a singleton. This proves stable = maximal IR. A contract accepted by SMA produces exactly an IR extension, and the hereditary greedy algorithm reaches every facet by processing that facet first. The star construction has worker accept-all choice and firm choice equal to the menu on the prescribed hereditary family and empty otherwise; both SUB and IRP follow casewise. Exact finite tests over all 194 nonempty hereditary families on up to four contracts and all 67 SUB–IRP firm choices on three contracts supplement but do not replace the proof.

## Originality

PASS under presently inspected sources and available records, subject to independent priority review. The September 2026 source proves existence and permanence for SMA but does not give the all-orders image, the maximal-IR characterization, or arbitrary-antichain universality. The March version discusses structural questions under a different axiom. Bando–Hirai–Imamura (2025) define maximal choice sets, give a three-choice-set example, and study arbitrary hereditary general upper-bounds constraints and a priority-prefix greedy selection in many-to-one markets. This is the closest feasibility/greedy antecedent, but the priority-prefix choice can violate IRP and they do not state the uniform SUB–IRP structural identification with full SMA order-surjectivity. Closest general-published similarity concerns classical stability and fairness, not arbitrary families of feasible contract sets. A prior disguised realization or general greedy-choice result is a residual priority risk.

## Value

PASS. The primary motivating paper explicitly leaves the structure of the CY-stable family open. This theorem gives the exact structural restriction in the specified SUB–IRP domain, proves that its constructive algorithm generates every stable outcome by order variation, and shows by a universal star construction that no stronger finite inclusion-shape restriction is possible. Unlike a lone small counterexample, arbitrary antichain realization settles an entire natural classification problem, while giving a concrete boundary for possible uniform lattice claims.
