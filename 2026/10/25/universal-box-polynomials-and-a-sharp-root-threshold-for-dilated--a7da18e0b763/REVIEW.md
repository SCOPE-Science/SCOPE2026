# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
An empty three-dimensional lattice simplex has Ehrhart numerator \(1+(m-1)z^2\) from lattice-point emptiness, reciprocity, and the normalized volume sum. Exact Ehrhart inversion after dilation, plus Boolean-face inclusion-exclusion and unimodularity of each proper face, gives the stated universal box polynomial. Its two gamma coefficients are strictly positive for all integer \(m\ge2\), \(r\ge2\), so the quadratic factor has positive discriminant and strictly negative distinct roots. The ordinary numerator at the borderline dilation factors explicitly; Jochemko's theorem handles higher dilations. The extracted exact checker passed 124 parameter cases and 124 independently counted open-box cases. The infinite claims follow from the proof rather than the enumeration.

## Originality — PASS under inspected evidence
Nill (2026, arXiv:2608.03635v1), Theorems 1.1–1.2, addresses unspecified large-width thresholds. Jochemko (arXiv:1602.09139), Theorem 1.1 and Corollary 4.4, establishes the ordinary numerator's root properties at dilation exceeding degree, but does not assert the integer-volume borderline or describe the local box polynomial. Bajo et al. (arXiv:2309.01186v4), Sections 1–2 and Theorem 1.1, examine one-row HNF local box formulas and asymptotics without the universal dilated-empty-tetrahedron statement. Liu–Tao–Xin (arXiv:2605.23544v2) include the classical Reeve polynomial. Explicit full-statement comparisons and mathematical-index searches did not identify a covering theorem. An older equivalent HNF formula not recovered remains a priority risk.

## Value — PASS
This is a natural complete classification of an intrinsically defined finite lattice enumeration invariant for every positive integer dilation of every empty lattice tetrahedron, not a single ad hoc computation. Shape-independence, strictly positive gamma coefficients, and a sharp universal transition at dilation two make the emerging large-width real-rootedness direction exact in a canonical dimension-three family. The coincidence of the ordinary and local strict-root thresholds is not granted by the general theorems cited above. No claim is made for arbitrary nonempty simplices.

## Evidence and limits
The proof is symbolic and all-parameter; the checker supplies finite independent lattice counting, not an infinite certificate. The literature comparison is best-knowledge rather than a guarantee of world-first priority. Scientific correctness has been checked in this self-review only.
