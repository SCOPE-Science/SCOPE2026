For every fixed \(n\ge1\), a sufficiently weak coherent vacuum–\(n\)-Fock superposition has exactly \(n\) separated Wigner-negative islands, each converging after translation to a disk of radius \(1/\sqrt2\); their total phase-space area tends to \(n\pi/2\). Their centers escape to infinity while their integrated negative Wigner mass is exponentially small, with exact logarithmic rate \(-(n!/2^n)^{1/n}\) on the amplitude scale \(a^{2/n}\).
