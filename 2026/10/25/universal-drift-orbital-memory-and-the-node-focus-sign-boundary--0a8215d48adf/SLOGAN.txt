For any stationary two-dimensional Ornstein–Uhlenbeck flow, the centered drift-only orbital activity has an exact single-exponential autocorrelation with rate \(\operatorname{tr}B\), even when the drift is irreversible or nonnormal. Its instantaneous sign distribution is two-sided for a stable node with distinct real eigenvalues, one-sided for a focus, and one-sided rank-one at the repeated-eigenvalue boundary except for scalar drift; noisy increment-based angular momentum is a different observable.
