Every nontrivial symmetric \(2\)-\((v,k,\lambda)\) design with \(2\le\lambda\le k-2\) has exactly computable canonical cut and cycle \(\ell_1\)/\(\ell_\infty\) projection norms depending only on the design parameters, without any edge-transitivity hypothesis. The cycle projection always has strictly larger endpoint norm, and the proof is an explicit seven-current-class calculation.
