The first nontrivial prefix–suffix Parikh-deck ambiguity occurs at length six for every alphabet of size \(q\ge2\): exactly \(\binom q2^2\) decks have four source words, indexed by ordered pairs of distinct-symbol pairs. All other length-six decks have one or two sources, giving precisely \((q^6+q^3)/2-\binom q2^2\) distinguishable decks.
