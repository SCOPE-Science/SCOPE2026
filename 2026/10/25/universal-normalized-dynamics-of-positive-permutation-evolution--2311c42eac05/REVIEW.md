# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. On each permutation cycle, the weighted-root formula is the unique positive solution of the conjugating gauge recurrence. Substitution gives an exact diagonal conjugacy; commuting permutation and normalized powers yields closed iterates. Taking ratios to the maximum proves superexponential phased convergence. Periodic points must be uniform on their support because a common multiple of any period and the order of the permutation eliminates the permutation, leaving positive powers only. Subset invariance, Möbius inversion, and a strict-maximum perturbation give the periodic-orbit census and the stability dichotomy. The packaged checker corroborates permutation and subset identities for all permutations through six vertices; it is not used as a proof for general dimension.

## Originality
PASS, subject to the stated bibliographic risk. The most relevant source, the 2026 evolution-algebra survey, poses precisely the normalized-dynamics question but does not give the gauge, complete basins, periodic-point formulas or stability trichotomy for permutation graphs. An expander-evolution paper's operator is linear; the closest permutation QSO is polynomial with nonzero cross terms; the mosquito-evolution paper uses a different map. Relevant published research-record searches on normalized square maps, weighted permutation cycles, positive projective monomial maps, and orbit counting did not identify an equivalent assertion, and earlier accessible claims on finite-field algebraic squaring and weighted-cycle automorphism groups do not imply the real normalized-dynamics result. Search absence is not a proof of novelty; broader unindexed power-map literature remains the main residual risk.

## Value
PASS. The 2026 survey explicitly asks for structural graph conditions governing convergence and periodic-orbit stability. The theorem gives a complete solution for every positive degree-one directed graph (arbitrary disjoint unions of oriented cycles), not a single isolated example: weight-independent topological dynamics, explicit phase basins and superexponential rates, a sharp convergence criterion, all periodic-point and primitive orbit counts, and the exact stable-cycle classification. The strongly connected subfamily consists of simple perfect evolution algebras and admits a natural necklace interpretation.

## Limits and comparison
The result concerns positive weighted permutation matrices and normalized nonnegative real dynamics only. It does not extend to general evolution matrices or unnormalized dynamics. The literature comparison includes the relevant material of the 2026 survey, Giacomelli's linear-operator results, Jamilov's permutation-based polynomial QSO and Rozikov–Velasco's mosquito operator. Equivalent specialized projective-power dynamics not indexed under these terms remains possible.
