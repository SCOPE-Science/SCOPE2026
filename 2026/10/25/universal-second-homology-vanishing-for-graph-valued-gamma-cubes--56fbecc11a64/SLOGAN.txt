For every finite simple graph without isolated vertices, normalized \(\Gamma\)-cubical homology vanishes integrally in degree two: \(H_2^{\Gamma}(G;\mathbb Z)=0\). Each singular \(\Gamma\)-square is supported on one edge, and a cubical prism contracts each edge-supported two-cycle; the result is specific to this new hypergraph theory and does not hold for conventional graph cubical homology.
