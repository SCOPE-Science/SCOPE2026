For polynomially weighted radial dilations on power Fock spaces, the full near-boundary singular-value density is the two-branch rearrangement of \(Cx^{k/m}e^{-x}\); only the polynomial's leading coefficient survives the scaling limit. This gives sharp norm and every Schatten \(\mathcal S_p\) blow-up constant, together with the optimal approximation-rank curve.
