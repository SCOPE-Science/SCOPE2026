# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The full quadratic Lindblad generator leads to an explicitly stated real drift and diffusion matrix. Both invariant drift blocks have strictly Hurwitz characteristic polynomials when \(0<\Omega<\gamma/2\) and \(g>0\); substitution into the Lyapunov equation gives a unique coupling-independent diagonal fixed covariance. Its Gaussian block structure implies literal tensor-product factorization, not merely absence of detectable entanglement. An explicit covariance Taylor expansion, followed by local Gaussian symplectic normalization and the Simon criterion, proves the universal short-time negative-PPT-eigenvalue coefficient. The limiting partial-transpose spectral gap proves a finite interval after which entanglement cannot reappear. Numerical Fock evolution, covariance reconstruction, and matrix identities are independent crosschecks but are not used as infinite-dimensional proofs.

## Originality — PASS, subject to specific residual risk
Downing and Ukhtary (2023), arXiv:2311.02424, develop the *same* physical oscillator model and compute stationary single-holder energy, ergotropy, and quadrature moments. Their Figure 5 negativity analysis fixes nonzero drive detuning; their main article does not supply the exactly resonant short-time birth coefficient, universally separable product fixed state, and permanent-death theorem as a single classification. The current result does not claim their Hamiltonian, second-moment method, or battery energy formulas. Simon's Gaussian PPT criterion and Ge–Tasgin–Zubairy's passive beam-splitter results do not provide open-system irreversible late-time decoupling for this device. A 2024 pulsed-charging model instead changes the driving protocol. Isar (2011) derives generic covariance tools but works with two *uncoupled* modes and finds no entanglement birth from separable Gaussian initial states in that regime. A specific residual priority risk remains: the original paper's supplementary moment equations, not fully accessible during this comparison, may contain equivalent results or a stronger covariance representation. No noncoverage assertion is made for that material, and an independent literature audit is still needed.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Two strongly coupled modes can be transiently quantum entangled yet have *exactly no stationary intermode correlations* for all positive coupling strengths at resonance. The sharp \(g\Omega t^2\) entanglement birth law and spectral-gap proof of permanent eventual separability locate two qualitatively distinct regimes without numerical parameter tuning. This bears directly on when entanglement can contribute to or diagnose charging in a naturally motivated two-mode device; energy remaining in the holder does not imply residual charger–holder entanglement. The full phase-diagram or arbitrary-noise extension is not asserted.
