# Review of weight-three translation fixed spaces

## Correctness
**PASS.** The claim uses a precise inclusive Rips threshold, actual geometric fixed points, and every integer \(n\ge4\). Barycentric orbit identification is a PL homeomorphism. The Hamming-distance case split yields a union of cross-polytope-boundary three-spheres indexed by the edges of a smaller cube. The intersections are either single full tetrahedra or two disjoint tetrahedra; a spanning-tree order and homotopy-pushout calculation give exactly \(e\) three-spheres and \(e-v+1\) circles. The finite checker confirms the entire orbit clique structure and mod-two homology for \(n=4,5,6,7\). The all-parameter proof is independent of these finite checks.

## Originality
**PASS, with residual literature risk.** Feng's hypercube scale-three theorem concerns the nonequivariant homotopy type. Adams and Virk's related results concern Betti bounds and cross-polytopal generators. Galetto, Montaño and Wellner already calculate all ambient rational homology representations at this scale; this significant closest result is not claimed again. Their full paper concerns homological representations, not the homotopy types or fundamental groups of geometric fixed loci. Targeted mathematically equivalent wording, geometric fixed sets, and implication searches in published mathematical records found no directly covering result. Unindexed equivalent work remains possible.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The free fundamental group of a geometric fixed set cannot be inferred from ambient rational homology characters. The formula yields explicit mixed-dimensional sphere wedges for an infinite natural symmetry conjugacy class and a striking geometric difference from the simply connected original complexes. The value is the full homotopy type, not a small numerical refinement.

## Scope and limitations
The theorem is restricted to the weight-three translation involution at scale three. It neither classifies all subgroup fixed points nor proposes a complete equivariant type or new ambient characters. The numerical checker supplements, but does not replace, the proof. An independent mathematical audit has not been carried out.
