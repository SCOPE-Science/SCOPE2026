# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For n>=4, an order-two translation flipping exactly three coordinates has no fixed original vertices. Any geometric fixed point is barycentrically supported in complete translation orbits, giving a PL homeomorphism from the orbit complex. Orbit vertices are four antipodal colors over each of 2^(n-3) residual cube vertices; a pair across distinct cube sites is compatible at Hamming threshold three iff the residual sites are adjacent and the colors differ. The orbit complex is flag and decomposes into S³=(S⁰)^{*4} crosspolytope boundaries, one per edge of the residual m-cube, with pairwise intersections contractible tetrahedral facets. Adding spanning-tree edge spheres wedges one S³ per edge; non-tree edges attach along two disjoint tetrahedra, adding one S³ and one S¹ by homotopy pushout. Thus |K_n|^<t> ~= wedge^(e-v+1) S¹ vee wedge^e S³, e=m2^(m-1), v=2^m. Independently recomputed the orbit graphs and maximal cliques for m=1..4, obtaining 16,60,176,464 tetrahedral facets and circle/S³ ranks (0,1),(1,4),(5,12),(17,32). Parameter-uniform proof does not follow from finite experiments.

## originality

PASS

Feng and Galetto–Montaño–Wellner determine ambient hypercube Vietoris–Rips homotopy and homology representation at scale three. Their all-dimensional hyperoctahedral homology decomposition is a genuinely stronger statement about homology of K_n, but does not imply the topological fixed-point space under a specific subgroup: fixed-point homotopy is finer than invariant homology. A recent October 2026 Cambridge paper on cohomological properties and connectivity of the same ambient complexes does not discuss these geometric fixed spaces in its inspected statements. No equivalent orbit-crosspolytope gluing classification located.

## value

PASS

Computing geometric fixed-point spaces is a natural genuine equivariant-homotopy invariant, not an invariant-subspace calculation. The theorem supplies complete all-n homotopy types including free nontrivial π1 for larger residual cubes, a phenomenon invisible from the simply connected ambient wedge results.
