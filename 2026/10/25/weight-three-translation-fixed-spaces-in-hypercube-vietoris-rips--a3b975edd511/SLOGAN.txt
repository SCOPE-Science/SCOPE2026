A weight-three translation on the scale-three Vietoris–Rips complex of the Boolean \(n\)-cube has a geometric fixed space homotopy equivalent to \(\bigvee^{e-v+1}S^1\vee\bigvee^eS^3\), where \(v=2^{n-3}\) and \(e=(n-3)2^{n-4}\). Thus symmetry-fixed spaces develop explicitly computable free fundamental groups even though the ambient complexes are simply connected.
