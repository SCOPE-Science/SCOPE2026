For two agents, the “someone knows” disjunction \(K_1\varphi\lor K_2\varphi\) is a normal \(\mathsf{S5}\) modality precisely when their information partitions are locally nested, equivalently every component of their join is a single block of one agent. The number of such ordered pairs of partitions on \(n\) labelled worlds has exponential generating function \(\exp(2\exp(e^z-1)-e^z-1)\), with initial counts \(1,4,19,111,774,6229\).
