# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For n>=7, every radius-two clique outside a consecutive-column slab contains a unique distance-two endpoint pair B_(j,a) in columns j-1,j+1 with equal C5 coordinate. Its remaining vertices form an arbitrary subset T of the three compatible middle-column vertices a-1,a,a+1. Uniqueness follows from column-span and n>=7, so the 5n disjoint eight-face families admit four codimension-one free-face pairs each, removing exactly 40n faces in 20n collapses. Each remaining two-column slab S_j is the independence complex of the forbidden cross-column C10 (not a matching): Ind(C10) ~= S^2 by the standard path/cycle independence-complex recurrence. Adjacent slab intersections are full five-vertex simplices and there are no nonadjacent or triple intersections. The homotopy pushout of this cyclic cover is S^1 wedge n copies of S^2. Inclusion-exclusion and removed faces give the full nonempty face vector (5n,30n,55n,35n,6n). Independent clique enumeration and exhaustive immediate-coface tests for n=7,8,9,10,11 confirmed all 20n collapses and remaining slabs; finite tests only corroborate the combinatorial uniform proof, not replace it.

## originality

PASS

Best-of-knowledge: the nearest published result establishes an analogous cyclic collapse for width four, not width five; the local forbidden graph changes from four independent edges to a ten-cycle, shifting the wedge spheres from dimension 3 to 2. Adams et al. compute the square C5xC5 case, which is explicitly excluded, not the long rectangular C5xCn family. Known individual-cycle independence-complex formulas supply a non-new local input but not the global cyclic gluing and exact 20n collapse.

## value

PASS

A long thin rectangular torus is a natural periodic graph metric space. Its entire radius-two Rips homotopy type and exact collapse exhibit nontrivial pi_1, exactly n integral 2-sphere classes and a change of sphere dimension from width four. This is a complete infinite-family classification with a constructive finite local geometric mechanism, not a table recomputation or a mere symbol substitution.
