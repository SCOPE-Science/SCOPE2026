For every \(n\ge7\), the radius-two graph-metric Vietoris--Rips complex of the rectangular torus \(C_5\square C_n\) collapses in \(20n\) explicit steps to a cyclic necklace of \(n\) two-spheres, and has integral homotopy type \(S^1\vee\bigvee^nS^2\). The sphere dimension differs from the previously known width-four rectangular family.
