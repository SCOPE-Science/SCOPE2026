For the integral Klein plane curve \(x^m y+y^m z+z^m x=0\), bad reduction occurs exactly when the characteristic divides \(N=m^2-m+1\). Writing \(N=p^a h\), there are exactly \(h\) wild cusps of completed type \(X^2+Y^{p^a}=0\), and the normalization genus collapses to \((h-1)/2\) while the intrinsic singular-scheme length stays \(N\).
