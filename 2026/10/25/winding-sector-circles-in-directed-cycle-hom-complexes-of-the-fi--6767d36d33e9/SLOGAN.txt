For every \(k\ge4\) with \(5\nmid k\), the directed Hom complex from the oriented \(k\)-cycle to the five-vertex cyclic tournament is a disjoint union of circles indexed by integers \(w\) with \(k<5w<2k\). Each circle is a connected five-sheeted cover of a particle-configuration circle, and target rotations act through its nontrivial deck transformations.
