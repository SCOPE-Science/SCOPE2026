Stationary asymmetric telegraph noise never produces an exact finite-time zero of the qubit's dephasing amplitude, while sufficiently slow symmetric noise produces infinitely many. Any nonzero jump-rate asymmetry leaves only finitely many coherence-recovery intervals, with their count diverging as \(N(\varepsilon)\sim[(v^2-a^2)/(2\pi v)]\operatorname{arcosh}(v/a)/|\varepsilon|\) when \(v>a\) and \(\varepsilon\to0\).
