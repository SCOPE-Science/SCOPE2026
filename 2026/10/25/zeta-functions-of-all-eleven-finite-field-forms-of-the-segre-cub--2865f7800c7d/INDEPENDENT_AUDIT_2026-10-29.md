For characteristic p>=5, the Segre cubic has ten nodes indexed by unordered 3+3 partitions N and 15 planes indexed by perfect matchings M. Blowing up ten nodes yields smooth Y isomorphic to compactified M_{0,6}. Keel’s Picard presentation has 25 boundary generators and 9 independent relations; the 15 plane classes plus the invariant sum of exceptional classes give the full 16-dimensional rational Picard representation with trace 1+fixed(M). Hard Lefschetz and Poincaré duality produce equal characters on H2 and H4 and point count Y=1+q^{3s}+(1+fixed(M^{sigma^s}))(q^s+q^{2s}). Replacing each exceptional quadric by its node subtracts fixed(N) q^{2s}+fixed(R) q^s, where R indexes the two individual tangent-quadric rulings per node and accounts for non-split quadrics. This yields the exact all-s extension-count formula and the zeta Euler-product factors. Fresh independent combinatorial enumeration of 15 matchings, 10 nodes and 20 rulings reconstructed all 11 conjugacy profiles; separately direct F5^d trace-equation enumeration for degrees d<=6 verified all 11 twisted F5 point counts (256,146,176,226,121,131,211,186,136,156,151), not just the untwisted case. Finite q=5 checks corroborate the cited geometry; the all-prime-power statement rests on the blowup/Picard trace argument.
