For every prime \(p>1{,}344{,}800\), the reciprocal reduced-Farey-product valuation alternates sign at \(mp-1\) and \(mp\) for every \(3\le m\le819\), yielding at least 1634 sign changes by \(820p\). The exact prime-scale coefficient stays between \(0\) and \(\varphi(m)\) through \(m=819\) but first becomes negative at \(m=820\).
