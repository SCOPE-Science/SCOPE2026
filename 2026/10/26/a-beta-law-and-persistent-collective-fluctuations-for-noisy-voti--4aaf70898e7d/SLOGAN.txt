For the noisy voter model on a growing star with fixed spontaneous-flip rate \(a>0\), the stationary leaf magnetization converges to a rescaled \(\operatorname{Beta}(\nu,\nu)\) law, \(\nu=((1+2a)^2-1)/(2(1+2a)^2)\), while the hub sign is conditionally biased by the magnetization. The leaf mean has a strictly positive limiting variance even though the noise rate remains fixed.
