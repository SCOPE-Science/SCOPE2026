For an explicit smooth family of Fano fourfolds of bidegree \((2,1)\), the full automorphism group is \(S_4\) away from the special fiber and \(\operatorname{GL}_2\) on it. The special stabilizer orbit has an irreducible four-dimensional binary-cubic normal representation \(\operatorname{Sym}^3(V^*)\otimes\det V\).
