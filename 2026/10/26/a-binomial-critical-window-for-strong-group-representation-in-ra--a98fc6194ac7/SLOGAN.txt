Under independent Bernoulli approvals, one-cohesive Strong and Semi-Strong Justified Representation have the same optimal committee score with an explicit exponentially small error: \(\min\{k,R\}/R\) for the number \(R\) of cohesive candidates, with vacuous score one if \(R=0\). Their feasibility exhibits an exact binomial critical-window limit at \(p=1/k\), despite their general worst-case separation and computational hardness.
