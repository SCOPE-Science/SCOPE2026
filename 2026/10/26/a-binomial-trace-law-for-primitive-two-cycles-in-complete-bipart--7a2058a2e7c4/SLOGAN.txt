Primitive two-cycle orbits of standard parallel chip firing on \(K_{a,b}\) have the closed count \(\frac{a^b b^a}{2}\sum_{k=0}^{\min(a,b)}(a)_{\underline{k}}(b)_{\underline{k}}/(a^k b^k)\), obtained from the trace of a two-stage binomial transition. When both shore sizes grow, the count is asymptotic to \(a^b b^a\sqrt{\pi ab/[8(a+b)]}\), without a fixed-ratio assumption.
