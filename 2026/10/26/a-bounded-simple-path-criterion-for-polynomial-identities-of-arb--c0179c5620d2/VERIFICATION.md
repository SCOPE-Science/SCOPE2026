The proof was checked symbolically in four parts: (1) two cycles sharing a vertex yield a free subalgebra; (2) unbounded vertex-simple paths contain all finite line-quiver path algebras and admit the displayed matrix-unit separator; (3) under cycle-disjointness, each path support decomposes into at most \(L+1\) strongly connected components, each of size at most \(L+1\); and (4) the Swan-quiver Eulerian-path cancellation yields the standard identity. No computation is needed for the universal claim. The identity bound is sufficient but not claimed optimal. The original Swan article was not directly accessible; the exact theorem statement was read in Section 4 of the inspected 2025 paper.
