# A canonical square-zero Jacobian doubling on the corank-two symmetric determinant stratum in characteristic two

## Finding

For every integer \(n\ge4\) over an algebraically closed field \(k\) of characteristic \(2\), consider the universal symmetric-matrix determinant hypersurface \(X_n\) in \(\mathbb P(\operatorname{Sym}^2 k^n)\). Let \(Y_n\) be its intrinsic hypersurface Jacobian scheme, \(Z_n\) the reduced corank-at-least-two locus, and \(U_n\) the smooth stratum of matrices of rank exactly \(n-2\). Then \(|Y_n|=Z_n\), while \(Y_n|_{U_n}\) is a canonical square-zero double of \(U_n\). Its nilpotent line is
\[
\mathcal I\ \cong\ \det K\otimes\mathcal O_{U_n}(-1),\qquad \mathcal I^2=0,
\]
where \(K\) is the universal rank-two kernel of the symmetric bilinear form. Every geometric generic point of \(Z_n\) has scheme-theoretic length \(2\) in \(Y_n\), hence \([Y_n]_{\mathrm{top}}=2[Z_n]\). On the smooth rank-\(n-1\) locus, the Gauss map factors as the kernel-line map followed by characteristic-two Frobenius, and the projective dual of \(X_n\) is the diagonal-coordinate linear space \(\mathbb P^{n-1}\).

## Assumptions and scope

The field \(k\) is algebraically closed of characteristic \(2\), and \(n\ge4\). The ambient space has independent homogeneous coordinates \(m_{ii}\) and \(m_{ij}=m_{ji}\) for \(i<j\). Here \(X_n=V(\det M)\); the intrinsic Jacobian scheme \(Y_n\) is defined in \(X_n\) by the images of all first derivatives of \(\det M\). The reduced determinantal locus is \(Z_n=\{\operatorname{rank}M\le n-2\}_{\mathrm{red}}\), of pure codimension \(3\) in the ambient projective space, and \(U_n=\{\operatorname{rank}M=n-2\}\) is its smooth dense stratum. The bundle \(K\) is the rank-two kernel of the universal morphism \(\mathcal O_{U_n}^{n}\to (\mathcal O_{U_n}^{n})^*\otimes\mathcal O_{U_n}(1)\). All line bundles \(\mathcal O(\pm1)\) are restrictions of the ambient projective bundles. Assertions about square-zero structure and the kernel line concern \(U_n\), not the deeper corank strata.

## Proof

**(1) Gradient and reduced support.** In characteristic \(2\), terms of the determinant of a symmetric matrix indexed by a permutation cancel in inverse pairs unless the permutation is an involution. Thus every off-diagonal variable occurs with an even exponent and all off-diagonal partial derivatives vanish identically. Differentiating the diagonal entry \(m_{ii}\) gives its principal \((n-1)\)-minor \(C_{ii}\). Hence the Jacobian ideal on \(X_n\) is generated by the diagonal entries of the adjugate. If \(\operatorname{rank}M\le n-2\), the entire adjugate vanishes. If \(\operatorname{rank}M=n-1\), its adjugate has rank one and is symmetric, therefore is \(\lambda vv^{\mathsf T}\) for a nonzero kernel vector \(v\) and some \(\lambda\ne0\). At least one entry \(\lambda v_i^2\) is nonzero. On \(X_n\), these two cases exhaust the points, proving \(|Y_n|=Z_n\). Note carefully that arbitrary full-rank matrices need not have a nonzero diagonal adjugate in characteristic \(2\); they are outside \(X_n\) and are not relevant to this support assertion.

**(2) Exact local Jacobian structure.** At a rank-\(n-2\) point, a constant congruence change of basis and localization give an invertible symmetric block \(A\) of size \(n-2\). Write the remaining blocks as \(B\) and \(C\), and set \(S=C-B^{\mathsf T}A^{-1}B\). The transformation from \(C\) to \(S\) is an invertible local coordinate change. Write
\[
S=\begin{pmatrix}u&w\\w&v\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The exact Schur identity is
\[
\det M=(\det A)(uv+w^2).
\]
As \(\det A\) is a unit, the hypersurface Jacobian ideal in this localized coordinate ring is precisely \((u,v,w^2)\): the derivatives with respect to \(u,v,w\) give \(v,u,0\), and the original defining equation contributes \(w^2\) modulo \((u,v)\). The reduced rank stratum is cut out by \((u,v,w)\). Thus the nilradical is locally generated by the class of \(w\), with \(w^2=0\), and has rank one over \(\mathcal O_{U_n}\). This calculation proves the multiplicity-two statement and the cycle formula without assuming any global property along the deeper rank locus.

**(3) Gluing the nilpotent line.** The first-order transverse symmetric form is a section of \(\operatorname{Sym}^2K^*\otimes\mathcal O_{U_n}(1)\). For a kernel-frame transition \(G=\begin{psmallmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{psmallmatrix}\), the matrix of this form changes by \(S\mapsto G^{\mathsf T}SG\). Its off-diagonal coefficient transforms modulo \((u,v)\) as
\[
w\longmapsto ab\,u+cd\,v+(ad+bc)w\equiv(\det G)w\pmod{(u,v)}.
\]
In characteristic \(2\), \(ad+bc=\det G\). Changing a local trivialization of \(\mathcal O(1)\) by a factor \(\lambda\) rescales this coefficient by \(\lambda^{-1}\). Therefore the transition factors of the nilpotent generator are those of \(\det K\otimes\mathcal O(-1)\), giving the canonical line-bundle identification. Consequently there is an exact sequence of sheaves of abelian groups
\[
0\to \det K\otimes\mathcal O_{U_n}(-1)\to\mathcal O_{Y_n|_{U_n}}\to\mathcal O_{U_n}\to0
\]
with square-zero kernel. This is a scheme-theoretic statement, not merely a tangent-space or set-theoretic calculation.

**(4) The Gauss image.** At a rank-\(n-1\) matrix the gradient coordinates are the diagonal principal cofactors \(\lambda(v_1^2,\ldots,v_n^2)\), with every off-diagonal gradient coordinate zero. Hence the Gauss map factors through the kernel-line map \(M\mapsto[v]\in\mathbb P^{n-1}\) and the coordinatewise Frobenius \([v_i]\mapsto[v_i^2]\). The kernel map is surjective: for every line in \(k^n\), a rank-\(n-1\) symmetric form with precisely that radical is obtained by choosing a nondegenerate form on a complementary hyperplane. Since \(k\) is algebraically closed, Frobenius is surjective on geometric points, proving that the projective dual image equals the full diagonal-coordinate \(\mathbb P^{n-1}\). The Frobenius factor also shows that the Gauss differential vanishes.

## Verification

The included standalone file `verify.py` uses exact binary linear algebra, not floating-point arithmetic, to enumerate all symmetric matrices over \(\mathbb F_2\) of sizes \(4\) and \(5\). On the determinant-zero locus it checks that the diagonal-adjugate gradient vanishes exactly at rank at most \(n-2\), and that at corank one it equals the square of a kernel vector (which over \(\mathbb F_2\) equals that vector). It also tests the off-diagonal congruence transition for all six invertible \(2\times2\) binary matrices. Run `python3 verify.py`. The general proof is symbolic and applies to all \(n\ge4\) over every algebraically closed characteristic-two field; these finite checks are corroborative rather than substitutes for that proof.

## Relationship to prior work

Ilic and Landsberg investigate symmetric degeneracy loci, constant-rank spaces and dual varieties over the complex numbers. Grenet, Monteil and Thomassé give the characteristic-two involution expansion of symmetric determinants (their Proposition 2.1), which supplies the determinant ingredient in Step (1). Ishitsuka and Ito consider symmetric determinantal representations of smooth plane curves in characteristic two. The present statement concerns instead the **intrinsic scheme** of the universal determinant hypersurface and computes its rank-two-kernel nilpotent line on every exact-corank-two stratum, simultaneously in all matrix sizes \(n\ge4\). The elementary characteristic-two Gauss collapse is included as a structural complement, not asserted as an independently novel discovery.

## Limitations

Nothing here claims that the whole Jacobian scheme is globally a square-zero thickening of \(Z_n\) across corank at least three; embedded components or more complicated higher-rank-degeneracy structure are not determined. The formula for the nilpotent line is on the smooth rank-exactly-\(n-2\) stratum only. Previously unidentified discussions of universal symmetroids in modular characteristic could contain equivalent claims; no independent peer review is implied. The finite-field checker does not certify infinite-family assertions.
