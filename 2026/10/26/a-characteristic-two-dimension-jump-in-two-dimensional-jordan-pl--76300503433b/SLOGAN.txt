In matrix size \(2\), the reduced representation variety of the Jordan plane \(XY-YX=Y^2\) has two components of dimension \(4\) in odd characteristic, but components of dimensions \(4\) and \(5\) in characteristic two. Its exact finite-field point count jumps from \(2q^4-q^2\) for odd \(q\) to \(q^5+q^4-q^3\) for even \(q\).
