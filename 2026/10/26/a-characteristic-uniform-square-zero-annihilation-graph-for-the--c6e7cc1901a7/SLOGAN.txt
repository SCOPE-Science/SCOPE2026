For every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\), the annihilation graph of nonzero square-zero elements of \(\mathbb F_q[x,y]/(x^3,y^3)\) is exactly \(K_{q^3-1}\vee\bigl((q+1)K_{(q-1)q^3}\bigr)\). The same graph arises in characteristic two and odd characteristic despite different square-zero equations; in characteristic three it describes the canonical square-zero subgraph of the modular group algebra of \(C_3\times C_3\).
