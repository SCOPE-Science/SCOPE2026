For unweighted anti-Monge least squares, the first three nontrivial square-grid sharp coordinatewise Lipschitz constants are \(3/2\), \(17/9\), and \(9/4\). The \(4\times4\) optimum occurs uniquely on a five-plaquette checkerboard rather than the completely pooled additive face, whose norm is only \(17/8\); the resulting \(9/4\) instability persists as a lower bound on every larger square grid.
