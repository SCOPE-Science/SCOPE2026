# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The equation and noncritical parameter conditions give smoothness and exactly \(m\) simple quadric degenerations over the punctured coordinate line. The regular quadric deformation-retracts explicitly to \(S^{n-2}\). Local degenerate pieces are star-shaped after the change of base coordinate; the homotopy-pushout gluing and mapping-torus quotient give the claimed bouquet, rather than only cohomological agreement. A direct Euler-characteristic computation checks the parity and rank. The proof does not assume a globally proper projection.

**Originality — PASS subject to stated residual risks.** Takeuchi's new paper proves cohomological restrictions and proposes the stronger homotopy statement as Conjectures 3.7 and 3.9, rather than as a theorem. The inspected Danielewski-Fieseler results concern a different affine-line-fibration classification and do not imply this exact punctured quadric-bundle homotopy theorem. Semantic searches across homotopy, quadratic fibrations and Danielewski aliases did not identify an equivalent claim; this is not proof of first priority. General classical Lefschetz/Danielewski results could contain an implicit formulation.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** This proves the full integral homotopy statement for a natural infinite-dimensional and infinite-degree explicit family of rational functions, including exact sphere multiplicities and \(\pi_1\). It is a motivated partial case of an explicit current conjectural phenomenon, not a numerical re-tabulation.

**Closest literature and limits.** Kiyoshi Takeuchi, arXiv:2610.20025v1 (Conjectures 3.7/3.9), and Adrien Dubouloz, arXiv:math/0401225. Only generic levels of the specified complex rational maps are covered. Earlier implicit classical coverage remains a residual risk.
