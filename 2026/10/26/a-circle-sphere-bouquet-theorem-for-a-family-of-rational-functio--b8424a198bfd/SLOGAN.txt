For the rational map \(f(x,y)=p(x)+(y_1^2+\cdots+y_{n-1}^2)/x\) on \(\mathbb C^*\times\mathbb C^{n-1}\), with \(p(0)=0\), \(p'(0)\ne0\), and \(\deg p=m\ge2\), every generic complex fiber has homotopy type \(S^1\vee\bigvee^m S^{n-1}\). The proof uses \(m\) quadratic vanishing-cycle thimbles and shows that the exact sphere count is independent of the regular-fiber monodromy around the puncture.
