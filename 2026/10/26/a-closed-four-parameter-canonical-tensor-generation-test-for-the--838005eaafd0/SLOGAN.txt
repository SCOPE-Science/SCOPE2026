Canonical tensor-generation on the unrestricted four-integer Fujino–Sato toric threefold family has a closed ten-test arithmetic characterization: four strict feasibility predicates and six nonvanishing wall tests. It yields a finite admissible parameter band for each fixed triple and an infinite pairwise torically nonisomorphic nonprojective family \(Z''_{13}(0,m,m,0)\) for every integer \(m\ge2\).
