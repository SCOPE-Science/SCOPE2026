For every integer geometric spacing base \(b\ge2\), the all-pairs distance shift has the same complete automorphism group \(\mathbb Z\times(\mathbb Z^{(\mathbb N)}\rtimes\operatorname{Sym}_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathbb N))\), although the shifts for different bases are nonconjugate. Their exact word complexities grow as \(n\log n\) with base-dependent coefficients and nonconstant digital oscillations; every invariant probability is concentrated at zero.
