# Scientific review of the extremality criterion

## Correctness — PASS
The characteristic assumption \(p>2\) makes the quadratic polar form nondegenerate modulo its radical. The affine point-count trace criterion, genus calculation, rank-two equivalence, and additive-composition identities have been checked coefficient by coefficient. The finite-field norm count is exact for arbitrary odd primes; exhaustive independent arithmetic for \(p=3\) and \(p=5\) returns the predicted 240 and 3120 pairs. The finite calculations are corroborative only; the algebraic proof handles the infinite prime family. The theorem concerns the union of maximal and minimal cases.

## Originality — PASS, subject to stated residual risk
The nearest motivating work, Almeida Filho–Tafazolian–Vieira (arXiv:2610.17476v1, §10, Question 10.4), poses a threshold-realization question and illustrates selected small cases; it does not state the complete full-coefficient \(\mathbb F_{p^4}\) criterion or its exact count for every odd prime. Ito–Tatematsu–Tsushima (arXiv:2412.00902, especially Proposition 4.12) provides a general maximality/splitting framework but not this computed closed-form norm equation and enumeration. Bouw et al. (arXiv:1410.7031) provide earlier general additive-polynomial machinery; Anbar–Meidl address specialized coefficient settings, including coefficients in a prime field. Statement-level comparisons and focused mathematical-record searches identified no dominating statement. A less searchable existing calculation remains possible.

## Value — PASS
Question 10.4 makes the existence threshold a concrete mathematical target. The theorem answers the full \(h=1\) infinite-prime subcase with an explicit necessary-and-sufficient criterion and exact count rather than isolated examples; the nontrivial arithmetic norm condition also describes all eligible two-parameter models over the smallest predicted extension. This is directly useful for constructing extremal curves and distinguishing the feasible coefficient locus. The coefficient count is not a count of isomorphism classes or separate signs.

## Scope of review
The asserted theorem, computation, and open limits are confined to nonzero coefficients in odd characteristic. The review makes no claim of exhaustive global literature coverage or independent validation.
