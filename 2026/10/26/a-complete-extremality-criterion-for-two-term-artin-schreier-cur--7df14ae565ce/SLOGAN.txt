For every odd prime \(p\), the two-term Artin–Schreier curves \(y^p-y=ax^{p+1}+bx^2\) over \(\mathbb F_{p^4}\), with \(ab\ne0\), attain a Hasse–Weil extremum exactly when \(t^{p+1}=N^{-1}-N^{-p}\), where \(N=a^{p^2+1}\) and \(t=2b^p/a^{p+1}\). Precisely \(p(p^4-1)\) ordered coefficient pairs satisfy this, proving threshold sharpness for \(h=1\) in every odd characteristic.
