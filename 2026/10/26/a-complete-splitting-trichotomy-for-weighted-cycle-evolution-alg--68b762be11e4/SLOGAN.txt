Over \(\mathbb F_4\), weighted directed-cycle perfect evolution algebras have one isomorphism type in each odd dimension and exactly two in each even dimension. The unbalanced algebra in dimension \(n=2h\) has cyclic automorphism group \(C_{3h}\), with a nonsplit diagonal extension exactly when \(3\mid h\); consequently every dimension divisible by six supplies a new single-cycle nonsplit example.
