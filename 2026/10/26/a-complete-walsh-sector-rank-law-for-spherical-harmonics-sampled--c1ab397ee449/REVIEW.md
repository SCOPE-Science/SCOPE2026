# Self-review

**Claim under review.** For full sign vertices of the embedded \(d\)-cube, \(d\ge3\), the exact-degree spherical-harmonic evaluation range is the sum of degree- and parity-permitted Walsh sectors, except for the constant sector at harmonic degree \(2\) and the full-product sector at harmonic degree \(d+2\). The rank formula and sharp eventual threshold \(d+3\) follow.

## Correctness — PASS

The source of every sector is the weighted homogeneous moment polynomial \(M_{\ell,S}\). Its Fischer harmonic projection is nonzero precisely when its weighted evaluation functional is nonzero. Fischer decomposition and irreducibility of the complex quadratic form in dimensions at least three reduce the issue to the nonvanishing of explicitly defined power-series coefficients on a null hyperplane. All mixed sectors have a two-coordinate complex-null witness. The all-cosh and all-hyperbolic-sinc extreme sectors have two-coordinate witnesses at even indices and certified nonzero three-coordinate transverse derivatives at odd indices at least three; their first odd indices are exactly the two claimed exceptions. The proof therefore covers all integer dimensions and degrees. The separate exact-rational checker confirmed 1825 corresponding Gram sector eigenvalues and verified the critical derivative formulas in finite cases. The check is supplementary, not an extrapolation to infinity.

## Originality — PASS, with priority risks

The 2013 foundational harmonic-index-design manuscript (arXiv:1308.5101) and the 2015 extension (arXiv:1507.05373) provide Gegenbauer addition formulas, spherical-design tests, and Fisher-type inequalities. Those are substantially different from an all-degree **range** characterization for Euclidean spherical harmonics restricted to every vertex of an embedded hypercube. In the portions examined and principal theorem statements, neither gives the two exceptional Walsh sectors or the eventual threshold. The closest discrete-Hamming literature instead concerns Walsh/graph harmonics, not solutions of the continuous Euclidean Laplace equation. Claim-level overlap comparisons found no dominating statement among the identified sources or historical research claims. This is a best-knowledge assessment, not a proof of publication priority.

The principal residual risk is an older treatment of harmonic interpolation on reflection-group orbits, Boolean cubature, or association schemes that may encode the same positive-eigenvalue pattern without matching title terminology. The full text of a related 2014 article about harmonic distributions on equitable Hamming partitions remained inaccessible; only bibliographic data and its abstract could be assessed. It does not, on accessible evidence, resolve the Euclidean exact-degree claim.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The full-cube vertex set is a canonical sampling configuration, and the dimension-independent rank classification is an exact interpolation invariant, not a recomputation of one degree or a numerical example. The isolated rank loss at degree \(d+2\), after parity-surjectivity in earlier degrees, is a substantive nonmonotonicity. The exact eventual threshold \(d+3\) and the identity of every missing Walsh direction provide data needed to design or diagnose exact-degree spherical-harmonic interpolation on this configuration. The theorem does not claim optimal conditioning, stability under perturbations, arbitrary vertex weights, or a classification for truncated/cumulative harmonic spaces.
