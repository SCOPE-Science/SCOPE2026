Minimum identifying codes of \(K(8,2)\) and \(K(9,2)\) both have six vertices, yet their exchange graphs jump from one connected graph on \(141120\) vertices to \(280\) disconnected copies of the ternary Hamming graph \(K_3\square K_3\square K_3\). At nine ground points, the optimal codes are precisely the edge sets of spanning unions of three three-vertex paths.
