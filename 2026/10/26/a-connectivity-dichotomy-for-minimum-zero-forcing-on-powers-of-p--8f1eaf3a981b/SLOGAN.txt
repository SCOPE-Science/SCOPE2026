For every \(k\ge2\), the minimum-zero-forcing exchange graph of \(P_n^k\) changes sharply from a prism of cliques to a one-bridge graph and then to two disconnected cliques. In contrast, for \(n\ge2k+4\) the exchange graph of \(C_n^k\) is connected, has \(n(2k-1)\) configurations, and exactly \(n(k+1)(2k-1)\) exchanges.
