For Malitsky–Mishchenko's adaptive gradient descent-2 on a scalar strongly convex quadratic, a whole open interval of initial stepsizes produces exact alternating steps forever, despite geometric convergence of the iterates to the minimizer. All positive nonconstant two-cycles form a one-parameter continuum with a sharp ratio cutoff \(r_*\) defined by \(r_*^3-2r_*/3-1=0\), and interior cycles absorb transverse stepsize perturbations in finite time.
