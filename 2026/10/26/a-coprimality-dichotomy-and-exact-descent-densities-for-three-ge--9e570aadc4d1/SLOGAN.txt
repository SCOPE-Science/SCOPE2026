For primitive triples of positive coin denominations, the three-variable denumerant eventually increases at every step exactly when the denominations are pairwise coprime; a noncoprime pair forces positive-density increases and decreases unless a denomination equals \(1\). With exactly one noncoprime pair of gcd \(d\), strict descents have exact density \((d-u)/d\), where \(u\) is the inverse of the third denomination modulo \(d\).
