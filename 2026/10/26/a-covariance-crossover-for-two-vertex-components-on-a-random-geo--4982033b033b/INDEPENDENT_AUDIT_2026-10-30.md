PASS

The source model is the classical equal-length random-arc covering of a circle. Huillet (2003), Theorem 7, studies physical lengths of connected arc clusters, not the number of sampled vertices in clusters of size two jointly with total cluster count. The fixed-binomial Dirichlet-gap inclusion–exclusion identities are elementary prior methods, not claimed as novel. The exact mixed covariance and fixed-length-corrected sign crossover are the surviving contribution.
