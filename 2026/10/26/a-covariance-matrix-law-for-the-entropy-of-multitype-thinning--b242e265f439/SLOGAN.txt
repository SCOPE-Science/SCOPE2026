For independently thinned multitype counts, the exact leading entropy deficit from independent Poisson laws is one quarter the squared Frobenius norm of the normalized second factorial-cumulant matrix. The leading total correlation is \(\frac1{2n^2}\sum_{i<j}\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j)^2/(\lambda_i\lambda_j)\), and a two-type example retains this dependence even as both marginal Poisson-fit entropies shrink as \(O(n^{-4})\).
