For the positivity-preserving GeCo2 discretization of symmetric two-state exchange, the exact flip boundary is **globally attracting** but only at the sharp rate \(n|x_n|\to1/[2(1+e^{-z_c})]\). For every larger normalized step there exists a locally attracting primitive two-cycle; its amplitude appears linearly at threshold and approaches \(1/3\) at very large steps.
