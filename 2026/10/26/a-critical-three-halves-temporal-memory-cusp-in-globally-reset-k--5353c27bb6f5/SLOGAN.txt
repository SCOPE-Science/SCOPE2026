For globally Poisson-reset Lorentzian Kuramoto coherence, the stationary two-time covariance is a positive pure exponential off criticality but acquires a universal \(t^{-3/2}\) factor at the coupling \(K=2\sigma\). The exponent is shifted from the uncentered critical \(t^{-1/2}\) relaxation by exact covariance centering, and its positive coefficient is explicit.
