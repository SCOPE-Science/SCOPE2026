On the critical branchpoint–pole boundary of the open Pelikan random map, survival from the top dyadic interval is exactly asymptotic to \(4\sqrt{2/\pi}(1-\varepsilon)/[\varepsilon(2-\varepsilon)]\,\lambda^n/\sqrt n\). The surviving distribution converges in total variation to an explicit geometric-convolution quasi-stationary law, even though the return kernel is critical and non-positive-recurrent.
