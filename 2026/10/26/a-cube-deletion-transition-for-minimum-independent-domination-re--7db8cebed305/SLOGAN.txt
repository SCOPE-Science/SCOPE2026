For a once-subdivided star with \(s\ge3\) arms, the token-slide reconfiguration graph of minimum independent dominating sets is exactly \(Q_s\) with its zero vertex deleted. Adding one short arm turns this into \(Q_s\) with a pendant vertex, whereas adding a second short arm collapses the reconfiguration graph to one vertex; the first two graphs are Hamiltonian traceable and have opposite median-graph behavior.
