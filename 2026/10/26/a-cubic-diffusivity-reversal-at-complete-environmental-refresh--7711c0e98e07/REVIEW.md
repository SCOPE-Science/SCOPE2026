## Originality — PASS, with stated residual risk
Martin–Nagel (arXiv:2610.11919v1) already derive the general regenerative variance series and the second derivative at complete refresh, including a positive curvature for nonzero mean drift; these are not claimed new. Their displayed result does not evaluate the universal third derivative or classify the zero-drift reversal, its root-refresh crossover, or the globally exact fair deterministic-arrow example. Their general series *can* be specialized to obtain these conclusions after independent four-step moment evaluation or geometric two-cycle summation: this close implication is acknowledged rather than hidden. Avena–den Hollander (arXiv:1610.00641v1) and Avena–Chino–da Costa–den Hollander (arXiv:1803.03295v1) use deterministic cooling schedules, not this independent geometric-refresh regime. Targeted indexed-mathematics and literature searches found no direct prior statement of these explicit coefficients or the rational fair-arrow law. As an originality risk, an older calculation may already have performed this specialization without easily searchable terminology.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is not a generic Taylor expansion of an unrelated statistic: the second derivative established in the motivating paper vanishes exactly at zero mean bias, leaving open the qualitative direction of environmental-refresh effects. The third-order coefficient unexpectedly has the opposite sign and depends only on the squared site-bias variance. The derived \(\sqrt{1-p}\) weak-drift boundary distinguishes two transport regimes and is uniform over arbitrary iid site laws. The exactly solvable fair-arrow model confirms the adverse effect *globally*, not merely infinitesimally. The result clarifies when environmental regeneration enhances versus reduces annealed diffusivity, without asserting a universal global inequality.
