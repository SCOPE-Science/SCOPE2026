In an iid site environment refreshed geometrically, nonzero mean drift makes full refresh a local diffusivity minimum, but exactly zero drift reverses it into a cubic-order maximum: the universal difference is \(3d^2w(1-p)^2-\tfrac12w^2(1-p)^3+O((1-p)^4)\). For independently fair deterministic site arrows the entire curve is explicit, \(V(p)=p(3-p)^2/((2-p)(1+p)^2)\), and lies strictly below the full-refresh diffusivity whenever \(0<p<1\).
