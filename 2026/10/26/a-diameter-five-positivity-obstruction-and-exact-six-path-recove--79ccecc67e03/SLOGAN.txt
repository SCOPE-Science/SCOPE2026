For the two-stage third-order Radau IIA heat step, every positive-weight graph of diameter at least five loses entrywise positivity at arbitrarily small positive timesteps, although positivity eventually returns. Unweighted paths are positive at every timestep exactly through five vertices; on the six-vertex path the only negative entries are at opposite endpoints, and positivity recovers exactly at the unique positive root of \(h^5+35h^4+168h^3+216h^2+60h-12\).
