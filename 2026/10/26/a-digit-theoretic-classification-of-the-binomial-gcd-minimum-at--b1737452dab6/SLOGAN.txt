For every prime \(p\ge7\), the Erdős–Szekeres minimum \(f(6p)\) is exactly two when \(p\) has no digit two in base three, exactly three when that fails but its binary digits have no adjacent ones, and six otherwise. The exceptional primes are \(O(X^{\log_2((1+\sqrt5)/2)})\) up to \(X\), so the value six holds for relative density one of primes.
