For a rank-one small eigenvalue and one real spherical probe, the biased one-node Gaussian estimator of normalized inverse trace has **strictly smaller mean-square error than an unbiased spectral-endpoint interpolant in every dimension at least four**, at every positive condition number. The interpolant always wins in dimension two, and dimension three has a unique, exactly characterized reversal at eigenvalue ratio \(\eta\approx0.0503804097941\).
