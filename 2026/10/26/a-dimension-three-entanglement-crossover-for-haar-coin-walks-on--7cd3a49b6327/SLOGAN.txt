In a hypercube quantum walk with fresh Haar-random coins, the exact mean coin purity changes from \(2/(d+1)\) after one step to \((d+5)/(d+1)^2\) after two. Thus the second step reduces mean coin–position linear entanglement in dimension two, leaves it unchanged in dimension three, and increases it in every dimension at least four—even though the ensemble-averaged coin is maximally mixed at both times.
