For every higher-cardinality balanced rendezvous game on a binary hypercube with \(t\ge2\) and \(d>2t\), the quantum success supremum is strictly below the no-signalling benchmark by at least \(\binom{2t}{t}(d-2t)/(512(d-t)\binom{d}{t}^{4})\). A Kneser-graph spectral estimate and an incompatible Hamming layer make the separation uniform over all finite local Hilbert-space dimensions.
