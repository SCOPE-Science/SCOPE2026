An improved explicit quantum–no-signalling gap for balanced hypercube rendezvous: for all integers t≥2 and d>2t, the relative deficit is at least (d−2t)/(512(d−t)·binom(d,t)^3), improving the earlier dimension-uniform bound proportional to binom(d,t)^−4. The qualitative strict separation was already known; the contribution is the stronger Kneser-spectral stability estimate.
