For independent rank-\(2m\) idempotents in \(M_{4m}(\mathbb F_q)\), conditioning on commutation leaves a nondegenerate discrete-theta fluctuation in product rank: \(\Pr(\operatorname{rank}(EF)=m+j\mid EF=FE)\to q^{-4j^2}/\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z}q^{-4k^2}\). The commutation probability itself is asymptotic to \(q^{-4m^2}(\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z}q^{-4k^2})/\prod_{h\ge1}(1-q^{-h})\), at every fixed prime-power field order.
