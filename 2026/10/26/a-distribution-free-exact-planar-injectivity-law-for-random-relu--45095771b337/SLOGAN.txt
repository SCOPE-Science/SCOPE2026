For every \(m\ge4\), an independent centrally symmetric planar zero-bias ReLU layer with noncollinear rows is globally injective with the exact distribution-free probability \(1-m(m-2)/2^{m-1}\). Conditionally on any deterministic set of distinct unoriented row lines, exactly \(2^m-2m(m-2)\) of the \(2^m\) equiprobable independent orientation assignments are injective, giving the first positive probability at width five.
