For the Grossi–Moretti Lagrangian surface, the Albanese cycle has self-intersection \(14\). Its pullback to the degree-\(9\) abelian cover has square \(126\), incompatible with the \(144\)-divisibility of every integral divisor-product square there. Thus the cycle is not one product of two integral divisor classes; this does not rule out the rational span of such products.
