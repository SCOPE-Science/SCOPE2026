For \(y^3=x^5+ax+b\) in characteristic \(7\), the smooth two-parameter genus-four family has a **doubled** Hasse divisor along \(a=0\): its Jacobians are ordinary away from that line and have \(7\)-rank zero on it. The exceptional geometric curve has Newton slopes \((1/4)^4,(3/4)^4\), and the two marked strata have exactly \(q-1\) and \((q-1)^2\) points over every \(\mathbb F_{7^n}\).
