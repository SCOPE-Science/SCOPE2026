For a uniform permutation, the covariance of any record indicator with any consecutive-pattern indicator vanishes unless their positions overlap, and the nonzero kernel has an exact beta-moment formula. For every fixed pattern length \(k\), total-record/pattern-count covariance converges to a finite constant determined solely by the pattern’s proper prefix-record values, with sharp extremizers and a size-five sign reversal for pattern \(132\).
