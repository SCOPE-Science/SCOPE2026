For the smooth characteristic-two Picard quartic \(Y^3Z+X^4+aX^3Z+bX^2Z^2+cXZ^3+dZ^4=0\), the scheme of lines with square quartic intersection is exactly \(\operatorname{Spec}k[v]/(v^7+(a/c^3)v)\). It is finite flat of length seven: four geometric points with multiplicities \((1,2,2,2)\) when \(a
e0\), degenerating to one curvilinear length-seven point when \(a=0\).
