If the susceptible-sensitivity \(G=\partial_S(\beta S-\gamma)\) vanishes only \(q\) times along a transversal time-optimal SIR vaccination trajectory, then every optimal policy has at most \(q+1\) switches. For two published nonlinear transmission benchmarks violating global \(G>0\), this proves that every unrestricted optimum has the form \(1\), \(0\to1\), or \(1\to0\to1\), without assuming that the numerically reported two-switch policy is globally optimal.
