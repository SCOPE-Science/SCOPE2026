For binomial van der Geer–van der Vlugt covers \(y^p-y=ax^{p^h+1}+bx^2\), the point-count discrepancy over \(\mathbb F_{p^{2n}}\) is at most \((p-1)p^{n+\gcd(h,2n)}\) when \(n\ge h\). When \(ab\ne0\), maximality or minimality forces \(n>h\) and \(h\mid2n\), creating a coefficient-uniform arithmetic gap beyond the general Cartier bound.
