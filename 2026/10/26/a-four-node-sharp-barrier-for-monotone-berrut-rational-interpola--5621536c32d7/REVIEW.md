# Self-review

The proof verifies denominator positivity, alternating-tail inequalities and attainability, then maximizes an explicit symmetric one-variable rational envelope. Descartes' sign rule and an exact polynomial resultant identify a unique algebraic constant. The two four-node witnesses attain both bounds. Exact-rational sampling across \(1\le n\le30\) and symbolic calculations passed; these are not substitutes for the proof.

**Originality — PASS, subject to specified access limitations.** The closest fully examined primary paper by Bos, De Marchi and Hormann derives \(O(\log n)\) unrestricted Lebesgue growth for the same interpolation operator. This does not imply a sharp dimension-independent restriction for monotone data. Other known sharp monotonicity defects involve different interpolation weights or polynomial schemes. The original Berrut 1988 source and an article on comonotone rational interpolation were not available in full, so an equivalent previously stated theorem remains a genuine residual risk.

**Value — PASS.** A sharp range-safety margin for all grid sizes is a natural invariant of an approximation operator applied to bounded monotone data. The finite four-node extremizer governs arbitrarily large grids, in contrast to increasing unrestricted Lebesgue norms; the result is not merely an isolated numerical constant.

**Scientific limitations:** Equispaced nodes, original alternating weights and nondecreasing real observations only. It does not guarantee monotonic interpolants, cover altered weights, or establish independent novelty certification.
