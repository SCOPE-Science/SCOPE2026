For Berrut's original alternating-weight rational interpolant at every equispaced grid size, bounded nondecreasing data in \([0,1]\) stay in the **sharp universal range** \([-\delta_*,1+\delta_*]\), where \(\delta_*=0.255032152490845\ldots\) is the unique positive root of \(12z^4+24z^3+32z^2-6z-1=0\). The largest overshoot and undershoot are both attained already by four-node step data, even though the unrestricted Lebesgue constant grows with grid size.
