For affine Walsh least squares on the even-parity sign cube, the sharp endpoint projection norm differs from the unrestricted-cube norm by the exact relative factor \(1+(-1)^{(n-1)/2}/n\) in odd dimensions and \(1+(-1)^{n/2+1}/(n^2-1)\) in even dimensions. The parity check creates a four-phase nonmonotonicity: infinitely many adjacent dimensions decrease in amplification and infinitely many adjacent dimensions tie.
