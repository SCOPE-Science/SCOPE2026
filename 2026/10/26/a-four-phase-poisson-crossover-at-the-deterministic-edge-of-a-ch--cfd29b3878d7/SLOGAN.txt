At the deterministic edge of a four-direction chiral lattice walk, the rare-randomization window \(n(1-p_n)\to\lambda\) has four residue-class formulas (three distinct non-Gaussian position laws with a uniform initial direction) indexed by \(n\bmod4\), each with an explicit three-exponential characteristic function. A conserved-orbit center becomes a compound-Poisson lattice walk, while the physical mean-square displacement tends exactly to \(\lambda+1-e^{-\lambda}\cos(\pi r/2)\).
