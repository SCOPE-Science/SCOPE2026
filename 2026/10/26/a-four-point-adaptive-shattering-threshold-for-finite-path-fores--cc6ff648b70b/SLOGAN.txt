For known path-forest component supports of size at most five, binary strict-betweenness queries have VC dimension \(m_3+2m_4+4m_5\) and Littlestone dimension \(m_3+3m_4+5m_5\), where \(m_j\) counts components of size \(j\). Four vertices are the sharp first component size with an online-versus-static gap, and every four- or five-vertex component contributes exactly one to that gap.
