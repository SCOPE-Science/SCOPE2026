In a graph formed by superimposing random triangles, adjacent-edge indicators are always positively correlated, but their full-order linear partial correlation reverses asymptotic sign as the number of layers per vertex crosses \(4/3\). For \(m/n\to\alpha>0\), the scaled adjacent and disjoint partial correlations tend to \((3\alpha-4)/(6\alpha)\) and \(-2/(3\alpha)\), respectively.
