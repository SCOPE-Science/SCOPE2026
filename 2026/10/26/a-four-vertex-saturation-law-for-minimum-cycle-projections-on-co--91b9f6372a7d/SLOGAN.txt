For the real edge-\(\ell_1\) cycle space of the complete split graph \(K_r\vee\overline{K_s}\), the globally minimum projection norm has an exact two-branch formula and stabilizes at \(3(r-1)/r\) as soon as \(s\ge4\), despite continued growth of the graph. Finite-group averaging reduces unrestricted minimization to one parameter, whose explicit optimizer can be nonorthogonal; for \(s>4\) the canonical orthogonal projection is strictly suboptimal.
