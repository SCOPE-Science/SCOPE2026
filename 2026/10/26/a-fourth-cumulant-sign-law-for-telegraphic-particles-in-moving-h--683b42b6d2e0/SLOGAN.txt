Monotonically dragging a harmonic trap holding a stationary telegraph-driven particle always produces a strictly negative fourth cumulant of experimenter work, whereas a sufficiently stiff short cyclic reversal can make that cumulant positive. At the established minimum-mean-work dragging protocol the leading long-duration excess kurtosis is \(-6\omega^4/[\gamma^3(D+\omega^2/\gamma)^2T]\), independent of trap stiffness and transported distance, and absent for covariance-matched Gaussian propulsion.
