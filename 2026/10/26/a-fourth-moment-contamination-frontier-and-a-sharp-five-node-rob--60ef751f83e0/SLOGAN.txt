For a vanishing high-variance Gaussian contaminant, the global positive three-node degree-five rule follows a sharp \(\varepsilon L^4\) transition and can collapse to \(\delta_0\) while the target measure converges in total variation to a standard normal. Positive degree-five quadrature with the correct uncontaminated three-node weak limit then requires **at least five nodes**, and componentwise Gauss-Hermite matching attains that bound.
