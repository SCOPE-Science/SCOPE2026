The positive self-commutator of the real-order Cesàro Hausdorff matrix on \(\ell_2\) is trace class for every \(s>0\), with exact trace \(s^2(\psi(2s)+\gamma)/(2s-1)\) (continuously interpreted as \(\pi^2/24\) at \(s=1/2\)). The same profile governs finite-section commutator flux for every fixed real shift \(\alpha>-1\), without a positivity assertion for shifted operators.
