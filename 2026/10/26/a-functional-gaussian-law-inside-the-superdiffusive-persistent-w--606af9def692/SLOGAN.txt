In a power-law persistent random walk with independent flip probabilities \(p_k=c k^{-\alpha}\), \(0<\alpha<1\), the entire superdiffusively rescaled trajectory converges to Brownian motion at deterministic time \(t^{1+\alpha}/[c(1+\alpha)]\). Thus macroscopic Gaussian independent increments emerge despite strongly aging microscopic velocity correlations; prior work established the second-moment exponent but not this functional law.
