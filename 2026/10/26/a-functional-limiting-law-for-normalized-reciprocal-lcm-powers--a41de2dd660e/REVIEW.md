# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The power-mean identity proves positivity and all parameter endpoints. Uniform first-moment control of prime tails proves convergence in the uniform path topology, and CRT handles fixed-prime valuations. The moment-product, support and Sperner anti-atom arguments are checked step-by-step in `RESULT.md`; exact local identities were independently replayed. The proof makes no inference from a finite sample to an infinite law.

## Originality
PASS, best-knowledge with residual risk. Molnar, arXiv:2610.12816, covers pointwise monotonicity and analytic order bounds, not the joint functional limit. Fan–Kobayashi–Molnar, arXiv:2511.02106, studies a distinct unnormalized family. Davenport's classical endpoint law and general Erdős–Wintner scalar distribution are credited. The claimed distinction is the complete parameter-indexed law, non-atomic full-support marginals and explicit mean. A more general unindexed functional limit result might subsume some aspects.

## Mathematical value
PASS. Typical-value behavior and a continuous probability law fill a structurally motivated gap in the recently introduced normalized reciprocal LCM-power family, where only averages and extremes were treated. The path, endpoint coupling and atomlessness mechanism are substantive even though no general density or effective rate is asserted.
