For every finite local associative algebra with residue field \(\mathbb F_{q^m}\) and square-zero radical, the radical profile generated by random elements (conditioned on surjecting to the residue field) is a product of independent matrix-rank laws: \(r\) rows for the untwisted component and \(r-1\) for each nontrivial Galois twist. This gives an exact all-parameter full-generation probability and a sharp minimum-generator threshold.
