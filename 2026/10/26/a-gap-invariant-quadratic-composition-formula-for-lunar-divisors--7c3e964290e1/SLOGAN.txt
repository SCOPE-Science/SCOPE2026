For every block gap \(h\ge3\), the base-two lunar divisor count of the repeated \(11\)-blocks \(3\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}2^{hj}\) is independent of \(h\) and equals \(\sum_{b=1}^n(c_b(n-b)+c_b(n-b)^2)\), where \(c_b\) counts bounded-part compositions. The quadratic term records two distinct residue rows sharing a single lunar cofactor; the restriction \(h\ge3\) is sharp.
