In independently biased ordered tournaments, the directed-triangle count has an exact linear–quadratic variance decomposition and a sharp Gaussian crossover when \(\sqrt n(p_n-1/2)\to c\), with limiting variance \(1/32+c^2/12\) after scaling by \(n^{3/2}\). The variance changes from order \(n^3\) at fair orientation to order \(n^4\) at fixed nonzero bias.
