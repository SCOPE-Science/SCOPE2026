For every genus \(g\ge3\), the leaf numerical semigroups of multiplicity \(4\) inject explicitly into the internal semigroups of the same genus, proving a full fixed-multiplicity case of the internal-versus-leaf conjecture. Their exact four-family census satisfies \(L_4(g)=13g/30+O(1)\), and the leaf proportion is \(26/(5g)+O(g^{-2})\).
