The five extended-Dynkin Fano quiver-moduli series have globally explicit smooth fibers after ordering distinct stable factors: four become products \(U_k\times(\mathbb P^1)^k\), while the Kronecker framing \(\widetilde A_1(b)\) retains a nontrivial projective-bundle twist in every dimension. The obstruction is the non-two-divisible hyperplane class in \(\operatorname{Pic}(F_k(\mathbb P^1))\), while all five have full symmetric-group monodromy on their fiber rulings.
