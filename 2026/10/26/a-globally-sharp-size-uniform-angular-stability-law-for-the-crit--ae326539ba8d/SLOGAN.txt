For any \(N\ge2\), the critical weighted-Bergman energy of \(N\) boundary Cauchy poles exceeds its regular-polygon minimum by at least \((2\log2-1)N\) times the centered ordered angular-square discrepancy, even when poles collide. The coefficient is universally sharp by the two-pole antipodal limit, and an exact nonnegative integral remainder quantifies the excess above it.
