A Brownian particle with reflective, contracting position resets followed by exponential motionless rests has an exact three-regime stationary autocovariance: no sign reversal, exactly one sign reversal, or infinitely many damped sign reversals. The one-reversal regime terminates at the golden-ratio contraction magnitude \((\sqrt5-1)/2\), and its first-zero lag diverges logarithmically at the no-reversal boundary.
