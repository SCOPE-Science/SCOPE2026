For Brownian particles sharing a two-state switching harmonic trap, the stationary *cross-particle* squared-position covariance changes its slowest decay mechanism exactly when the switching rate equals the harmonic mean \(2ab/(a+b)\) of the trap relaxation rates. At that spectral collision, a genuine \(t e^{-2rt}\) term appears; the coefficients and crossover hold for every finite particle count at least two.
