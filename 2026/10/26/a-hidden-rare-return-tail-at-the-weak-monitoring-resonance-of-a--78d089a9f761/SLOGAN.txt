A two-site quantum walker monitored with vanishing click strength has an exceptional-point family of first-detection laws when its hopping angle shrinks proportionally to the detector strength. At the resonance endpoint, an asymptotically vanishing \(4\kappa^2\) fraction of trajectories survives for order \(1/(\varepsilon\kappa^2)\) steps, so the limiting detection time is exponential even though the exact scaled mean remains two and the variance diverges.
