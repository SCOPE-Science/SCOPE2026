For the Gaussian droplet-survival kernel \(\phi_b(t)=\exp(-\pi t^2/(4b^2))\), every normalized positive exponential mixture has uniform log-error at least \(\pi T^2/(24b^2)\) on \([0,T]\), regardless of the number of terms. Positive exponential compartments therefore cannot maintain arbitrarily small relative fidelity over arbitrarily long horizons.
