For hyperplane-kernel rank-nine bundles on \(\mathbb P^5\) defined by a bivector, rank six gives uniform splitting, while rank four produces exactly a Lagrangian quadric \(Q^3\) of jumping lines with splitting \(\mathcal O(1)^6\oplus\mathcal O^2\oplus\mathcal O(-1)\). The fourteen-column hyperplane in arXiv:2610.20208v1 lies in the rank-six uniform stratum, and its rank-four limit has precisely this jumping quadric.
