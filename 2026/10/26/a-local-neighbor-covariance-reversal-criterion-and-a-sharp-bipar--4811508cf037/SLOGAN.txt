For a finite symmetric exclusion network reset to a fixed particle configuration, the high-reset covariance of two initially occupied sites is governed by their empty-neighbor motif \(d_xd_y-2|A_x\cap A_y|\), while the weak-reset covariance is strictly negative. On \(K_{2,m}\), resetting the two distinguished vertices produces exactly one covariance sign reversal if and only if \(m\ge3\), at rate \(4/(m-2)\).
