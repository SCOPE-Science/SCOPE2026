A directed two-population epidemic whose source and receiver recovery rates nearly coincide has extinction time \(\log n+\log\log n\) plus a shifted Gumbel fluctuation. Detuning receiver recovery by \(\theta/\log n\) changes the Gumbel intensity through the exact crossover factor \((1-e^{-\theta})/\theta\), while the receiver count at extinction scale converges to a Poisson law.
