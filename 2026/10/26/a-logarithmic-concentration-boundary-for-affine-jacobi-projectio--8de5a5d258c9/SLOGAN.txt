For symmetric Dirichlet-weighted affine least-squares projection on an \((n-1)\)-simplex, the exact endpoint norms are \(1+2C_{n,\alpha}\), where \(C_{n,\alpha}\) is one explicit beta-tail expression, and only vertices maximize amplification. Keeping total concentration \(\lambda=n\alpha\) fixed produces the sharp limit \(1+2\lambda\) with a nontrivial \((\log n)/n\) deficit and an explicit digamma correction, in contrast to linear growth at fixed coordinate concentration.
