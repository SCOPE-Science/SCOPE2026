For two independently seeded orbits of the integer expanding circle map, the normalized sharp-window cross-pair count has an exact finite-sample covariance decomposition and \(N\operatorname{Var}(Q_N)=2s(b+1)/(b-1)-8s^2\log_b(N)/N+O_{b,s}(N^{-1})\). The logarithmic correction distinguishes deterministic orbit reuse from independent sampling even though both models have Poissonian first-order pair statistics.
