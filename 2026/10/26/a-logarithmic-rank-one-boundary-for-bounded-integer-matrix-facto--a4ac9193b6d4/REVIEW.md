# Review of the rank-one matrix-factorization asymptotic

**Correctness — PASS.** The factorization space is the union of two explicit primitive-vector families. Inclusion–exclusion gives the exact formula; a uniform Bézout fiber estimate and \(8\varphi(h)\) primitive-vector shells yield the coefficient \(384/\pi^2\) and the \(O(T^2)\) remainder. Exact independent finite enumeration agrees through height six. Finite checking is not an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** The 2026 paper of Browning et al. studies the identical matrix divisor function; its asymptotics concern nonsingular and zero targets, while its singular rank-one result is only a lower bound. Afifurrahman's earlier theorem gives a general nonzero-singular upper bound. No equivalent sharp rank-one asymptotic was found in the inspected primary material or targeted searches. An unindexed equivalent remains possible.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The theorem settles a natural missing rank class in dimension two and identifies a genuinely different logarithmic scale, with an explicit coefficient and a power-sized remainder. It is not merely a finite table or a repackaging of the existing lower bounds.

**Limitations.** The height is entrywise, the target is fixed \(E_{11}\), and nothing is asserted for arbitrary singular targets or higher dimensions. The standalone enumeration corroborates the proof. Independent mathematical and bibliographic validation remain outstanding.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
