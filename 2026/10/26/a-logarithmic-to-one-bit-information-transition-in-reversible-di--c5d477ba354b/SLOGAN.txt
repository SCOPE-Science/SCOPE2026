For reversible dimer adsorption on an even periodic ring, the stationary mutual information between its alternating sublattices is the entropy of their shared particle count without hopping, but only the entropy of a shared parity bit under any positive symmetric hopping rate. At fixed nonzero density this changes \(\tfrac12\log m+O(1)\) information into \(\log2+o(1)\) on two \(m\)-site sublattices, despite every proper-site marginal being independent and fair at equal reaction rates with hopping.
