At an exponentially distant target \(x=\exp(b/\alpha)\), Brownian first passage under independently refreshed Mittag–Leffler diffusivity has a geometric integer-block limit as \(\alpha\downarrow0\), with mean \(e^{2b}\). Jointly over logarithmic distances, those hitting blocks form an increasing independent-increment process whose jump sizes \(d\ge1\) have Poisson intensities \(u^{d-1}\,du\) in the coordinate \(u=(1+e^{-2b})^{-1}\).
