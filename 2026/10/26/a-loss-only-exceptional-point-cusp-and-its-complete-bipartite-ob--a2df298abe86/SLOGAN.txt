A passive trimer with loss only at an endpoint has no exceptional points until the near-end hopping exceeds the far-end hopping by a factor of \(2\sqrt2\); at equality, a single third-order exceptional point appears, and above it two second-order points bound an all-imaginary spectral window. Uniform complete-bipartite networks cannot reach this trimer threshold through loss at a non-universal vertex: only a lossy singleton-class hub exhibits an exceptional point.
