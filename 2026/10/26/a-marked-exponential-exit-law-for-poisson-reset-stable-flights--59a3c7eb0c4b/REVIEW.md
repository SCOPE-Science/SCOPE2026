The exact regenerative Laplace identity transfers the marked rare-event law to the exit time and location. The assumptions fix \(0<\alpha<2\), \(\sigma,r>0\), \(\Lambda\in(-1,1)\); the one-jump proof controls all excursion paths and not merely simulated paths. The verification code checks closed-form constants and identities, not the limiting theorem.
