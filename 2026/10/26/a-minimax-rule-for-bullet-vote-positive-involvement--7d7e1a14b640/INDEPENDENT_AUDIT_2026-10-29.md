# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For finite X with |X|>=2, there is at most one strict Condorcet loser so argmin of maximal incoming pairwise margin over nonlosers is nonempty; any Condorcet winner has strictly negative worst defeat score and all others positive. Under a bullet ballot for selected x, every incoming margin to x decreases by 1, all competing worst scores weakly increase, old Condorcet losers stay excluded and x remains eligible, so x becomes unique winner. Score orders depend only on ordinal majority-margin ordering, giving invariance and one-voter resolvability. Independent skew-matrix tests on 3,000 random matrices for each k=2..8 corroborated the universal inequalities; source Q3-to-Q2 transition witnesses failure of full participation. Proof is elementary but complete.
