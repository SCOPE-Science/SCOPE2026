For mixed prime powers \(n=q^tp^e\) with distinct primes \(q\) and \(p\ge5\), the prime-base binomial congruence is equivalent to a base-\(q\) digit-sum bound and two explicit \(p\)-adic conditions on a binomial coefficient compressed to exponent \(\lfloor(e-1)/3\rfloor\). The first three \(p\)-power layers use a single fixed binomial integer, producing verified squared and cubed examples.
