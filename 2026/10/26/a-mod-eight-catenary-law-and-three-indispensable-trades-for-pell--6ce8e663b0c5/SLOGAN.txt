For every odd-index Pell triple \(\langle P_i(u),P_{i+2}(u),P_{i+4}(u)\rangle\) with \(u\ge1\) and \(i\ge5\), three explicit indispensable factorization trades give a minimal presentation. The exact catenary degree is \(b-\lfloor a/(u^2+2)\rfloor+u^2+1-r\), with a sharp dichotomy determined by \(i\pmod8\), where \(r\) is the positive residue of \(a\) modulo \(u^2+2\).
