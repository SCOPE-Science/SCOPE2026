For every even cyclic prism \(C_m\square K_2\) with \(m\ge4\), the minimum signed double Roman weight is \(m\), but the number of minimizing four-label functions is \(2\) when \(m\equiv2\pmod4\) and \(14+4L_{m/4}\) when \(4\mid m\), where \(L_0=2\), \(L_1=7\), and \(L_{k+2}=7L_{k+1}-L_k\). Thus optimum-labeling multiplicity alternates between complete rigidity and exponential growth.
