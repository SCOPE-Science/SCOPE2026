On each labeled cycle \(C_n\) with \(n\ge6\), the number of maximum general three-position sets obeys an exact mod-four law: \(6,\ n(n+3)/4,\ 2,\ n(n+1)/4\) in residue classes \(0,1,2,3\), respectively. For odd cycles the number of rotational classes is \(\lfloor(n+3)/4\rfloor\), revealing many optimal sets beyond the maximally even constructions that attain the previously known maximum size.
