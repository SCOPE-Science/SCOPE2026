For polynomial covers of degree \(d+4\) with one \(d\)-fold branch and two paired simple-ramification branch values, the geometric monodromy is exactly \(A_{d+4}\) when \(d\) is odd. When \(d\) is even, its two topological classes have sharply different groups: \(C_2\wr S_{(d+4)/2}\) for the quadratic-composition class and \(S_{d+4}\) for the other.
