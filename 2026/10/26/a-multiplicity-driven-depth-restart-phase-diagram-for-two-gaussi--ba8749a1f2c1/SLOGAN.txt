With exactly two matrix–vector products, deepening one Gaussian eigenvector probe versus selecting the better of two independent probes has an exact angular-success crossover controlled by spectral contrast and trailing-eigenspace dimension. In two dimensions a nonempty reentrant window appears for \(\kappa_*<\kappa<2\), where \(\kappa_*\approx1.93910347\), while in three dimensions the comparison has just one tolerance boundary at \(\varepsilon=\sqrt{1-2/\kappa^2}\) when \(\kappa>\sqrt2\).
