Randomly placing about \(n\log n\) sensors on the rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) has a sharp reliability crossover when the excess \(m-n\) is of order \(n/\log n\). The exact finite power-domination polynomial and matching Bernoulli/fixed-budget Poisson limits show how two independent near-square occupancy channels collapse into one asymmetric channel.
