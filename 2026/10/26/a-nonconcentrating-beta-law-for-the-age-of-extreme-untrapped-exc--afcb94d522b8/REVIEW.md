# Scientific review — conditional extreme-excursion age law

## Correctness — PASS
The stationary stochastic convolution exists because the finite-state environment has a strictly positive long-run confinement occupation. The excess conditional variance follows a piecewise deterministic linear evolution; its stationary zero-flux equations give explicitly normalized gamma mode densities. A constant-hazard entry-time argument then splits the off-state variance into independent gamma variables with a common rate. The beta-gamma ratio is exactly independent of the total variance and hence of the Gaussian positional observation. A strictly convex one-dimensional rate function has a unique positive minimizer, so conditional total variance scales linearly with the observed radius; this produces the stated Beta age profile. An exact off/on density ratio proves the separate inverse-distance mode posterior. Finite quadrature assertions corroborate the identities; the limiting conclusions rely on the proof. No finite-size sampling or independent validation is claimed.

None of the relevant inspected analyses states the *current off-episode backward age conditional on an increasingly extreme fixed-dimensional spatial observation*, or a nonconcentrating Beta law for that age, or the exact inverse-distance trap-state posterior. Static mixtures are expressly treated as prior work, not claimed as new. The novelty assessment is best-knowledge only; general gamma-filtering or rare-event results could subsume this specialization, and an independent scholarly priority review is needed.

## Scientific value — PASS
For a distant observed particle, the question of *how long the current trap-off episode has lasted* is a motivated microscopic history statistic. A naive single-optimal-age picture fails: even after total variance concentrates at a unique large-deviation saddle, the fraction generated in the current excursion retains a nondegenerate Beta law. This identifies inherited versus new fluctuation contributions, in any finite spatial dimension, and supplies a quantitative prediction for history-conditioned optical-tweezer observations. Its value is the natural and exact conditional path statistic, not a repackaging of existing stationary spatial densities.
