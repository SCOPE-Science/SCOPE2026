For a Brownian particle in a randomly switched harmonic trap, the current free-excursion age conditioned on an extremely distant stationary position has a nonconcentrating limit: \(2\sqrt{aD}\,T/\|X\|\Rightarrow\operatorname{Beta}(1,b/(2k))\). The exact beta-gamma excursion decomposition explains why the far-field particle almost surely sees the trap off, with residual on-state probability asymptotic to \(\sqrt{aD}/(k\|X\|)\).
