When independent disease samples must be tested in two-person Dorfman pools, variance-minimizing retest workloads always admit a noncrossing pairing after sorting individual risks—even when those risks straddle the usual \(1/2\) regime. This gives an exact \(O(m^3)\) interval algorithm for \(2m\) specimens; six distinct risks already yield a unique optimum that is neither adjacent nor opposite-extreme pairing.
