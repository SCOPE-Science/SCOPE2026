A concrete minimal Sturmian shift has a positive continuous roof whose suspension is not topologically mixing, although the roof is not cohomologous to a constant. The circle-factor return sums converge to their mean at the explicit rate \(\|S_{Q_k}\rho-2Q_k\|_\infty<3/Q_k\), giving a quantitative counterexample to a 2025 minimal-shift suspension question.
