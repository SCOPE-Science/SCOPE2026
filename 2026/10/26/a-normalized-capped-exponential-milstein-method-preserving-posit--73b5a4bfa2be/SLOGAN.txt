For the nonlinear positive martingale \(dX_t=X_t^2/(1+X_t)\,dB_t\), capping the Brownian-square exponent and exactly normalizing its Gaussian mean yields a scheme that is strictly positive, an exact discrete-time martingale, and finite in every moment for every step size. Its coupled-grid mean-square error is \(O(h^2)\), giving strong order one without a positivity or coarse-step moment restriction.
