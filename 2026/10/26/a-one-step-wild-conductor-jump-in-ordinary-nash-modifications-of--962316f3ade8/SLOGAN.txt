For odd \(m\ge3\), the first ordinary Nash blowup of \(xy=z^m\) has generic exceptional semigroup \(\langle2,m\rangle\) in characteristic dividing \(m\) but \(\langle2,m-2\rangle\) otherwise. Its generic normalization defect increases by exactly one, and its conductor exponent by two, although its normalized toric blowup is unchanged.
