# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
Clebsch–Gordan fusion and character orthogonality give every term of the exact sixth-moment polynomial, including the exceptional \(n=3,5\) coefficients and the pure-character cubic \(S_n\). Nonnegative invariant multiplicities reduce complex phases to the aligned real polynomial, with equality phases checked separately. Its Bernstein derivative is positive for even \(n\ge6\); the two smaller even weights have a single positive critical root. Descartes’ rule and endpoint signs give exactly one positive critical root for each odd \(n\ge3\), and expansion provides the asymptotic gain. The standalone checker independently recomputes tensor-fusion multiplicities and finite Chebyshev moments and records 4,724 passing tests. These finite checks supplement an all-weight analytic proof.

## Originality — PASS (best knowledge)
Crampé–Poulain d’Andecy–Vinet (arXiv:1905.06346) develops the standard \(\mathrm{SU}(2)\) tensor couplings used as a starting point; Grow–Myers (arXiv:2005.11245) supplies Haar and character formulas for a different Fourier-convergence problem. A published fourth-moment high-weight parity classification of central character packets does not imply sixth-moment values or optimizer equality cases. Related sharp sixth moments for abelian character supports do not apply to the nonabelian \(\mathrm{SU}(2)\) fusion ring. No matching exact all-weight two-character sixth-moment classification emerged from targeted record, alias, and literature searches. Some records were available only as abstracts, and unindexed equivalent formulations remain possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result is a complete sharp constant and equality classification for a natural two-dimensional central Fourier packet in every irreducible weight. It proves a persistent parity effect in extremizer support, identifies two exceptional even weights, and quantifies the smaller character’s residual contribution at all odd high weights. The classification is structural rather than a single exact integration exercise.

## Limits
Only two ordinary trace characters, probability-normalized Haar on \(\mathrm{SU}(2)\), and sixth moments are treated. Finite checker tests do not replace the proof, bibliographic searches do not certify novelty, and no independent validation has been carried out.
