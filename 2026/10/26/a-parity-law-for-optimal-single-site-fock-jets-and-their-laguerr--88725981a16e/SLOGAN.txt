For every fixed derivative order \(k\ge2\), optimal one-point Gaussian Fock approximation of a high-degree monomial exhibits a strict parity law: the squared sampling radius centers at \(N\) for even \(k\), but shifts by \(\pm\rho_{k+1}\sqrt N\) for odd \(k\), with \(\rho_{k+1}\) the first positive Hermite zero. At each finite degree, every possible optimal radius belongs to an explicit set of \(k+1\) Laguerre zeros.
