For the ordinary circular prism \(C_n\square K_2\), strong odd coloring jumps from two colors at even circumference to five at every odd circumference \(n\ge5\), with the triangular prism alone requiring six. All four-palette colorings have periods \(2\), \(4\) or \(6\), yielding an exact formula for their count and a fixed \(260\)-state trace formula for every odd optimal-coloring count.
