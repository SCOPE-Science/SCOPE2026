In competitive irreversible monomer–dimer adsorption on a complete graph, a weak monomer rate produces a Poisson count of voluntary monomers that is *rounded upward*, not conditioned, to satisfy the final matching parity, with a uniform total-variation error at most \(5\varepsilon\). At the natural rate \(\varepsilon=c/\log n\), even and odd system sizes have different explicit non-Poisson limits, pinpointing a logarithmic finite-size crossover.
