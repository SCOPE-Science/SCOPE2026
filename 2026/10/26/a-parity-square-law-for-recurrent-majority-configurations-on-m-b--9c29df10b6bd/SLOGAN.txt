For synchronous binary majority dynamics on the \(2n\)-vertex Möbius ladder, the recurrent-state count is exactly the square of the fixed-point count when \(n\) is odd. When \(n\) is even it is instead \(L_n^2+2\) for \(6\mid n\) and \(L_n^2-1\) otherwise, where \(L_n\) is the Lucas sequence; an exact swap-twisted transfer matrix proves the parity law.
