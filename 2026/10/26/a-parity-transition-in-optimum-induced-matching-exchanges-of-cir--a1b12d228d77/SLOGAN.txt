For every circular ladder \(C_n\square K_2\) with \(n\ge4\), the maximum induced matchings have size \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\), but their one-edge-exchange geometry changes abruptly with parity: all optima are isolated at even orders, while the \(n^2\) optima at odd orders form a connected graph with degrees four, five and six. An explicit cyclic matching parametrization gives every exchange edge and the exact edge count \(2n(n+1)\) for odd \(n\).
