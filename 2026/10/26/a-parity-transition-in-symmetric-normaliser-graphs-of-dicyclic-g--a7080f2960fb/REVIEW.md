# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The group-law calculation gives necessary and sufficient mutual-normalisation congruences, and resolving them separately for odd and even parameters determines every edge. A direct invariant-subspace decomposition then gives the full Laplacian spectrum and the matrix-tree theorem gives exact spanning-tree counts. Exact computational cross-checks for small groups are supplementary, not a proof of the general statement.

## Originality
PASS with residual risk. Surbhi--Venkataraman (2026, arXiv:2601.19569v1) define the symmetric normaliser graph and establish broad graph-equality criteria, but their fully inspected text does not describe the all-parameter dicyclic graph or its spectra. Surbhi--Venkataraman (2024, arXiv:2406.00710v1) treat power and enhanced-power graphs rather than mutual cyclic normalisation. The odd-parameter graph decomposition is already covered by the combination of their 2024 enhanced-power formula and 2026 graph-equality criterion, and is credited as known. The even-parameter case supplies a different cross-coset adjacency and is not their commuting graph. Searches of published mathematical records did not yield the claimed decomposition. The risk is an equivalent implicit result in general cyclic-subgroup normalisation literature.

## Value
PASS. This completely determines a recently introduced group graph on an infinite, standard family rather than merely tabulating small cases. The parity transition changes universal vertices, reflection-clique sizes and independence numbers; exact Laplacian spectra and spanning-tree complexity quantify the structural difference. Consequences are bundled with the single structural result.

## Limitations
All results are for \(Q_{4m}\) with \(m\ge3\), plus the explicitly stated exceptional \(Q_8\). They do not characterize arbitrary generalized dicyclic groups. The originality assessment is based on inspected accessible literature and is not independent publication-priority certification.
