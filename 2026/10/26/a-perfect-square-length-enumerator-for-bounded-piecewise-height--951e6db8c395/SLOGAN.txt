For binary words with exactly four alternating constant runs, the complete length generating polynomial of words whose Simon piecewise height is at most \(H\) is the square \(\bigl(zS_{H-2}(z)+S_{H-3}(z)T_{H-2}(z)\bigr)^2\) for every integer \(H\ge3\). Consequently, there are exactly \((H^2-5H+7)^2\) such words beginning with zero, summed over all lengths; the square arises from independent constraints on the two opposite-run pairs.
