The self-generated-resource branching Brownian motion admits a dominating Poisson resource-ancestry forest: every corpse can fund at most one independent Poisson(\(\theta\)) family in the potential coupling. This gives explicit exponential extinction-time tails when \(\theta<1\) and a universal \(\(O(t^{-1/2})\)\) upper tail at the critical recycling threshold \(\theta=1\), for arbitrary bounded symmetric interaction kernels.
