Two-colour continuum adsorption with random available-colour selection and fixed-priority selection have different total particle densities already at cubic order in time. The priority density exceeds the random-choice density by \(r^3\sigma^2t^3/12+O(t^4)\) for rods on the line and by \(\sqrt3\pi r^3\sigma^4t^3/16+O(t^4)\) for disks in the plane, so these colouring rules cannot be interchanged in an exact density calculation.
