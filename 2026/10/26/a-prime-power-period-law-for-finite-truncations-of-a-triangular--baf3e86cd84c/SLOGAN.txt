For the finite-coordinate, finite-ring quotients of a triangular linear cellular automaton, the exact global temporal period is \(d\,p^{\lceil\log_p(m+p^a)\rceil}\) when the ring length is \(p^a d\), with \(p\nmid d\). The extra prime-power resonance yields finite cyclic dynamics at every cutoff but an infinite-order coefficientwise limit.
