For every prime \(p\ge5\) and integer \(m\ge1\), the central trinomial coefficient at the digit boundary satisfies \(T_{pm-1}\equiv(p/3)3^{m(p-1)}T_{m-1}\pmod{p^2}\), where \((p/3)\in\{-1,1\}\) is the quadratic character modulo \(3\). Iteration gives \(T_{p^r-1}\equiv(p/3)^r3^{p^r-1}\pmod{p^2}\) for every \(r\ge1\).
