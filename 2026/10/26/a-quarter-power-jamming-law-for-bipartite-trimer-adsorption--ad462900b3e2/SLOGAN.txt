For random trimer adsorption using one or two sites from each of two equal complete pools, the number of uncovered sites at jamming scales as \((2n)^{1/4}\) and converges after normalization to \(|Z|^{3/2}\), where \(Z\) is standard normal. This quarter-power and non-Gaussian law follows from an exact two-colour martingale and is not the fixed-size vacancy law of unpartitioned complete substrates.
