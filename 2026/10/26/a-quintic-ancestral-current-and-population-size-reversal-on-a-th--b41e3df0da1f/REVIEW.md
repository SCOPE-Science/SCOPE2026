# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The ten-state population generator follows directly from motion plus deaths with a donor at a distinct site. The eighteen-state founder equation includes deaths in which copying happens at the same site; it is anchored by exchangeability when all three particles share a site. The package contains an exact rational-function solve and an identity check that the factored formula is algebraically equal to the current defined from stationary probabilities and fixation probabilities. A separate absorbing two-colour Dirichlet solve agrees with this calculation at two positive contrasts and reproduces the previously published heterogeneous-killing example. Positive denominator factors and numerator factors settle the global sign, while division by the fifth power proves the coefficient. Irreducibility, almost-sure finite-label coalescence, and the fixed-family range \(-1<\varepsilon<1\) justify the inversions and quantifiers. No finite sample is substituted for an infinite theorem.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk

The closest primary full text, Lin and Lu arXiv:2610.12589v1, states the current's definition and proves the cubic response only for populations of four or more. It specifically flags cancellation at three particles but only calculates a three-particle nonzero current at the unrelated vector \((1,2,4)\). Neither the general algebraic nonvanishing result nor that single example implies the current's sign along the entire symmetric one-parameter family, its exact quintic order, or its opposition to the published cubic for four particles. The same authors' later Brownian paper studies a continuous-space Euclidean current for a two-harmonic profile, rather than this discrete port current. Cloez and Thai's earlier complete-graph invariant formulas assume constant killing and do not produce heterogeneous ancestral-port currents. Searches of published research records for aliases, coefficients, current orientation, rational formula and parameters did not return a covering finding. These searches cannot establish absolute novelty; historical finite-state exact-current calculations remain a nonzero priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The motivating paper explicitly singles out the three-particle cancellation of its cubic coefficient, leaving an identifiable gap in the smallest population where irreversible ancestry can occur. The exact fifth-order law resolves whether this natural symmetric perturbation produces zero or nonzero three-particle circulation and determines the global direction for all admissible positive killing rates. The sign reverses when population size increases from three to four near neutral killing, demonstrating a qualitative population-size effect missed by generic nonvanishing and by the already known four-particle cubic result. This is structural rather than a routine coefficient change.
