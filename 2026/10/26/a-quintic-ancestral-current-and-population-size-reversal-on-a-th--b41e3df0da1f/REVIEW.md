# Scientific assessment of the all-contrast claim

The stationary count law and founder fixation equations include each motion and copying event, including copying at a shared site. The two normalized systems are nonsingular when the killing rates are positive. Their exact elimination gives the stated rational current, and straightforward factor bounds fix the direction across the entire admissible interval.

The prior October 18 finding derives the fifth-order onset and the local comparison with larger populations for exactly this family. That result is not presented as new. Neither the local expansion nor Lin and Lu's generic nonvanishing theorem and off-family example rule out a zero at a larger contrast on this line. The exact formula provides a certificate that the local direction does not reverse at any admissible contrast. No broader family of rates, graph, or population size is classified.
