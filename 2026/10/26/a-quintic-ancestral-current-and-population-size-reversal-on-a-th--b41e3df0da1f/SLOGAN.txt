For the three-site Fleming–Viot model with three particles and killing rates \(k=(1-\varepsilon,1,1+\varepsilon)\), the exact ancestral current has the sign of \(\varepsilon\) throughout \(-1<\varepsilon<1\) and starts at positive order \(\varepsilon^5\) with coefficient \(89/56010528\). The known cubic current for every fixed population \(N\ge4\) instead has negative sign near equal killing, so increasing the population from three to four reverses ancestral circulation.
