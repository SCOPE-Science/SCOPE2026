For an isotropic run-and-tumble particle in a fixed harmonic trap, increasing spatial dimension does not make its stationary squared radius deterministic: the radius converges to the nondegenerate Simpson concentration \(S=\sum_jP_j^2\) of the persistent-run stick-breaking weights. Joint conditional Gaussian fluctuations show that the radial correction scales as \(d^{-1/2}\), while a fixed Cartesian coordinate multiplied by \(\sqrt d\) tends to a non-Gaussian Gaussian-variance mixture.
