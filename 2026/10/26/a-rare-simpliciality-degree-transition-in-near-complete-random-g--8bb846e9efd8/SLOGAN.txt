In an almost-complete Erdős–Rényi graph with missing-edge probability \(c\log n/n\), conditioning one vertex to have a clique neighborhood creates a sharp, localized degree transition: its normalized degree tends to \(1\) for \(c\le1\), and to \(1/c\) for \(c>1\), while all other degrees remain asymptotically full. An explicit conditional degree law gives a full large-deviation principle and a twice-differentiability break in the rare-event exponent at \(c=1\).
