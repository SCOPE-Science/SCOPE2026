The quadratic field extension underlying the Sendra–Arranz--Sendra--Sendra degree-two parametrization of a smooth cubic threefold has a rational, normal anticanonical \((2,3)\) complete-intersection model branched along two elliptic cubic cones meeting in a tripled line. Its singular line has generic transverse type \(A_5\), jumping at precisely two points to simple elliptic \(\widetilde E_7\) with \(j=0\).
