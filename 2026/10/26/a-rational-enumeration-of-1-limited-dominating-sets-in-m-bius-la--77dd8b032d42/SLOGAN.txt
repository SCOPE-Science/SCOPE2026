Every cardinality of 1-limited dominating sets in the cubic Möbius ladder \(M_{2n}\) has an exact rational generating function obtained from a seven-state-versus-three-state twisted transfer. Its minimum-set multiplicity is \(4\) when \(n\equiv0\pmod4\), \(2n\) when \(n\equiv2\pmod4\), and \(3n\) for odd \(n\); the previously known minimum-size formula is recovered rather than claimed as new.
