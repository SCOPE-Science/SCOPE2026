For every assignment of incident tangent lines to the seven rational points of the Fano plane, there is a unique locally smooth plane-filling quintic over \(\mathbf F_2\); it is geometrically smooth exactly when its rational tangent-map fibers have sizes \((2,1,1,1,1,1)\), \((2,2,1,1,1)\), or \((2,2,2,1)\) with the three doubled lines nonconcurrent in the last case. Exactly \(1512\) of the \(2187\) assignments pass, grouped into ten projective orbits, and three concurrent rational bitangents are an exact obstruction.
