For a finite metric star with \(m\ge2\) equal unit arms and one exceptional arm of length \(t\), the critical Hilbert-snowflake exponent is the unique root of an explicit scalar equation, invariant under \(t\leftrightarrow1/t\), and uniquely minimized at the equilateral star. As the exceptional arm collapses or diverges, the exponent approaches the threshold of the remaining star with a power-law defect for \(m\ge3\) but a Lambert-\(W\) logarithmic defect for \(m=2\).
