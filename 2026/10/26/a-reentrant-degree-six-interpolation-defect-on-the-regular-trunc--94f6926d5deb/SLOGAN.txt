On the twelve vertices of the regular truncated tetrahedron, exact-degree spherical-harmonic sampling has ranks \(1,3,5,7,9,11,9\) in degrees \(0\) through \(6\), then rank \(12\) at every degree \(\ell\ge7\). The degree-six three-dimensional interpolation defect disappears under every sufficiently small nonzero deformation of the regular truncation ratio, exhibiting an isolated geometric resonance.
