The third-order, two-stage Radau IIA heat step on \(K_q\square K_q\) is nonnegative for all steps when \(q\le3\), but for every \(q\ge4\) its positivity set consists of two disjoint intervals \([0,a_q/q]\cup[b_q/q,\infty)\), with both endpoints explicitly determined by a quartic. At \(q=4\), the diagonal becomes \(-4/323\) at step \(h=1\), while the method remains spectrally nonexpansive for every step and eventually recovers positivity.
