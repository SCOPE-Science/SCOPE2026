For two unequal instantaneous storms routed through three identical linear reservoirs, a second strict runoff peak occurs exactly when the storm separation \(d>2\) and the second-to-first volume ratio exceeds \(e^{-d}(d+\sqrt{d^2-4})^2/4\). The critical ratio is nonmonotone in separation, so a fixed ratio in \((e^{-2},(3+2\sqrt2)e^{-2\sqrt2})\) exhibits two peaks, then one, then two as separation increases beyond the single-storm modal delay.
