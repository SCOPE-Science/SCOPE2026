For a uniform random recursive tree on \(n\ge2\) labels, root outdegree and leaf count have exactly the same **joint law** as newest-vertex depth and the complementary nonleaf count: \((R_n,L_n)\stackrel{\mathrm d}=(D_n,n-L_n)\). The reflection reverses the sign of their leaf covariances and already fails in a root-weighted Hoppe tree of size three unless the root weight is one.
