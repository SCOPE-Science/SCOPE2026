In the expanding Thomas–Fermi condensate with shared Poisson resetting, stationary mass fractions in a central core and an initially empty outer shell are positively correlated for weak resetting but negatively correlated for strong resetting, for **every** core–shell geometry strictly separated by the initial edge. The high-rate anticorrelation has a sharp \(e^{-rt_c}/r^2\) law, with a coefficient independent of the shell's outer radius.
