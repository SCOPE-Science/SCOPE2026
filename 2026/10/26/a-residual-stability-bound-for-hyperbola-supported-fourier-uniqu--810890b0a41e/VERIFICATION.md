# Verification

The piecewise-linear sampling lemma was derived from the interval Poincare–Wirtinger inequality. The bounds on the interpolant and its derivative were recomputed from the gap assumptions. The normalized Fourier transform is unitary, its derivative identity was checked, and the reciprocal-change-of-variables identities are \(\\|t\\varphi\\|_2=\\|\\psi\\|_2\) and \(\\|\\varphi\\|_2=\\|t\\psi\\|_2\). Applying the lemma first to the reciprocal transform proves the needed regularity; applying it to the ordinary transform and combining the inequalities gives the two bounds.

No numerical experiment is used as proof. The estimate is not claimed to be sharp and is not established at the critical or supercritical boundary. Independent audit and independent validation remain unperformed.
