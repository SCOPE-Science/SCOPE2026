For four symmetric positive atoms on two concentric pairs, the first-order Cauchy-wavelet transform has exactly three upper-half-plane zeros counted with multiplicity; the three-imaginary-zero region ends at the exact support ratio \(r=3-2\sqrt2\). Its two discriminant boundaries merge into a nondegenerate order-three zero, and their parameter gap closes with the sharp \(3/2\)-power cusp law.
