Every trivially perfect graph on at most \(15\) vertices has a log-concave domination polynomial, but exactly one isomorphism class fails at order \(16\): \(K_1\vee(3K_5)\). A complete rooted-forest census proves the sharp cutoff and uniqueness, while the family \(K_1\vee(3K_m)\) fails log-concavity for every \(m\ge5\).
