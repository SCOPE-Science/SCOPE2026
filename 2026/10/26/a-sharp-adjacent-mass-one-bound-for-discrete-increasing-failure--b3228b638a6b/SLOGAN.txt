When an integrable integer-valued IFR law has mean at least an integer \(m\) and its mass at \(m+1\) is at least its positive mass at \(m\), the probability of exceeding \(m\) is at least the sharp constant \(a^2=0.324717957\ldots\), where \(a^3=(1-a)^2\). The unique equality law has a single left atom and a two-rate geometric right tail, rather than the familiar three-point extremizer.
