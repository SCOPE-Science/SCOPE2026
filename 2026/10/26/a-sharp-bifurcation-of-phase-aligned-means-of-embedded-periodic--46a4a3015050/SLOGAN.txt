For two regular embedded limit-cycle shapes \(\gamma_\pm(t)=(1\pm\varepsilon\cos2t)e^{it}\), the phase-shift Fréchet mean is uniquely the circle up to \(\varepsilon^2=2/5\), then splits into exactly two distinct means with an explicit square-root phase law. Higher harmonics show that this nonuniqueness occurs for pairs of attracting periodic orbits arbitrarily close to a common circle in the uniform time-parametrized metric.
