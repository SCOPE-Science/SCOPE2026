For proportional positive Prisoner's-dilemma payoffs in a coupled growing-population game, a single explicit carrying capacity \(K_c=N_0\Phi(x_0)/\Phi(1/(1+\theta))\) separates eventual full resource preservation from complete depletion. The resource reaches its unique peak precisely when the population reaches \(K_c\), independently of resource feedback speed; at the critical capacity it converges to an interior level.
