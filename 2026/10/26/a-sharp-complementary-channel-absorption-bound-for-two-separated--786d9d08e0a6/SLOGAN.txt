Two separated positive point absorbers exhibit an exact coherent-perfect-absorption transition at total normalized loss \(L=1\); beyond this threshold the only perfect-absorber designs require quarter-wave spacing and an unequal strength split. Among all such designs, the fraction of incident power absorbed even for the least favorable coherent illumination is at most \(1/2\), attained uniquely at \(L=2\) up to swapping the absorbers and half-wavelength phase shifts.
