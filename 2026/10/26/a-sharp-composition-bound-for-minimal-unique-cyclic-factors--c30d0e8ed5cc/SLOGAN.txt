For every cyclic word of length \(n\ge2\), the number of minimal unique cyclic factors beginning with a letter occurring \(m\) times is at most \(\min\{m,n-m\}\). Thus binary words with \(k\) ones have at most \(2\min\{k,n-k\}\) such factors, and binary de Bruijn cycles attain equality at arbitrarily large lengths.
