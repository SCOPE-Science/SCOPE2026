In the replicator opinion–leadership model, two opposed communities with equal cross-camp exposure \(q\) and homogeneous interior initial leadership have a sharp conviction threshold \(\rho=4q^2\): above it all become leaders, below it both leadership and polarization disappear, and at equality a continuum of partial-leadership equilibria remains. Below the threshold opinion decays with the universal amplitude \(\lim_{t\to\infty}\sqrt t\,s(t)=1/\sqrt{2(4q^2-\rho)}\), despite arbitrary within-camp wiring.
