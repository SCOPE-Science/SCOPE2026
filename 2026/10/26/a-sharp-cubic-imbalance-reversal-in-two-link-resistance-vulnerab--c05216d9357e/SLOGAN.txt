For the complete bipartite network \(K_{p,q}\) with \(p\ge q\ge3\), the most damaging two-link failures share a degree-\(q\) endpoint, but the order of disjoint failures and failures sharing a degree-\(p\) endpoint reverses sharply at the integer threshold \(p=q(q-1)^2\). All three two-link motifs have strictly positive Kirchhoff-index interaction, even across that reversal.
