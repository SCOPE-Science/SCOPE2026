At the complete-mixing-optimal common gradient-tracking step \(\alpha=1/\bar h\), two agents have a sharp curvature–mixing convergence boundary: arbitrary curvature mismatch is harmless for \(\lambda\ge-1/3\), but stronger negative mixing requires an exact radical bound on mismatch and eventually defeats this step even with equal curvatures. The boundary follows from a fully initialized three-state Schur analysis, not numerical sampling.
