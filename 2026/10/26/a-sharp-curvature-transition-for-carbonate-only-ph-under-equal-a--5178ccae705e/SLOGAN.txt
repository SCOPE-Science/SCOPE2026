In the ideal carbonate-only system with fixed carbonate alkalinity, equilibrium pH as a function of dissolved inorganic carbon is globally strictly convex exactly when the ratio of dissociation constants is at least \(1/112\); below this sharp threshold it has exactly two inflection points. This produces both signs of pH averaging bias under equal-alkalinity mixing, with a concavity window opening at order \(\sqrt{1/112-K_2/K_1}\).
