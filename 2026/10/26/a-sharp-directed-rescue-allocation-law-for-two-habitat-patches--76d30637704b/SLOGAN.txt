With a fixed total two-way rescue-colonization rate in a two-patch metapopulation, the unique allocation maximizing mean survival time is \(u_* = \operatorname{proj}_{[0,C]}[(C+\alpha e_1-\beta e_2)/2]\). Equal two-way effort is optimal from full initial occupancy even with unequal extinction risks; when only one patch is initially occupied, the policy switches sharply from one-way to split effort as the budget increases.
