In a uniform graph with a fixed number of edges, the covariance between degree-one vertices and triangles changes sign at the exact integer boundary \(m=n+1\) for every \(n\ge5\), whereas it remains strictly negative in the independent-edge graph at every nontrivial edge probability. In the sparse regime with \(m/n\to c>0\), the fixed-edge covariance tends to \(8c^3(c-1)e^{-2c}\).
