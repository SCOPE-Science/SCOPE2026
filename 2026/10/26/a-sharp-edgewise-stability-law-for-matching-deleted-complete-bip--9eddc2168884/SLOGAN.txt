Deleting \(t<n\) disjoint edges from \(K_{n,n}\) lowers the exact maximum-coordinate norm of its least-squares gradient-flow projector by \(2t/[n^2(n(n-2)+t)]\), but deleting the final matching edge produces an exceptionally large stability improvement. The final drop is exactly \((n-2)(n+1)\) times the preceding one, and every sharp norm is attained by a sign-valued edge perturbation.
