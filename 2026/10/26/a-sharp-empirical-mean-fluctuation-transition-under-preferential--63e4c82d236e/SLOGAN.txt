Uniform relocation to a randomly selected past position leaves every one-time harmonic-trap position marginal exactly Gibbs--Gaussian, yet the variance of the trajectory mean crosses sharply from \(T^{-1}\) through \((\log T)/T\) to \(T^{-2\mu/(r+\mu)}\) when the relocation rate passes the trap relaxation rate. Exact Kummer-function formulas determine the full finite-time variance and all three asymptotic constants.
