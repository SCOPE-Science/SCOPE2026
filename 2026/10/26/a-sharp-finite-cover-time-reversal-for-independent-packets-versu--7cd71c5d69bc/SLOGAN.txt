For \(n>r\ge1\), covering all labels by successively replacing one coupon in an \(r\)-packet has exactly the ordinary \((n-r)\)-coupon collection-time law, regardless of any nonanticipating adaptive eviction policy. Independent fresh \(r\)-packets finish strictly sooner in expectation exactly when \(2\le r\le n-2\); at both boundary families, adaptive replacement finishes strictly sooner.
