For the dicyclic group \(\operatorname{Dic}_n\) of order \(4n\), the mean over all subsets of \(|AA|-|AA^{-1}|\) is strictly negative for \(2\le n\le29\) but strictly positive for every \(n\ge30\). An explicit cycle-count formula and a uniform tail bound prove that the first sign reversal occurs sharply at order \(120\); this does not assert majority product dominance.
