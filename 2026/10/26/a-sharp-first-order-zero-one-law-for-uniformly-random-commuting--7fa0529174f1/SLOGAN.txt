For a uniformly chosen pair of commuting involutions, every first-order sentence has limiting probability zero or one, determined at quantifier rank \(q\ge1\) by an **optimal** \(11q\)-point five-orbit model. The error decays at the sharp scale \(e^{-n^{1/4}}\), and the probability of no common fixed point is exactly zero for odd sizes but asymptotic to \(2e^{-r_n}\) for even sizes, where \(r_n+3r_n^2+r_n^4=n\).
