For the primitive two-generator Frobenius problem, every fixed \(a\ge3\) admits coprime partners \(b\to\infty\) with \(g(a,b)/b\to\infty\) along an explicit subsequence. The published linear formulas for \(a\in\{1,2\}\) make \(a=3\) the sharp boundary for uniform linear growth in the varying generator.
