For positive mixtures of two gamma densities with the same real shape \(m\) but different rates, the sharp relative Fourier noncancellation margin is \(\cos(\min\{m(2\arctan\sqrt{b/a}-\pi/2),\pi\}/2)\). Every mixture is Fourier-zero-free for \(m\le2\); for \(m>2\), real zeros—and failure of Wiener translate cyclicity—occur only at explicitly enumerated mixing weights once \(b/a\ge\tan^2(\pi/4+\pi/(2m))\).
