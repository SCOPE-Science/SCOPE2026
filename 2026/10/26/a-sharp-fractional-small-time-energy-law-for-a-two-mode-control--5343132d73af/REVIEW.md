## Correctness

PASS. The endpoint map is represented by the stated square-integrable Mittag-Leffler kernel. Its leading two independent powers have an explicit Gram determinant; the trace/determinant quotient gives the two eigenvalue asymptotics and the sharp worst-target energy exponent. The claim is restricted to fixed 1/2<alpha<1 and distinct fixed rates.

## Originality

PASS on best available evidence, not as an absolute priority claim. The full-text fractional controllability source establishes Gramian nonsingularity, not the displayed small-time constants. The classical 2026 Gramian expansion covers the integer-order hierarchy but not the fixed fractional-order exponents. Targeted bibliographic and semantic comparisons found related control-Gramian and observation-horizon work, but no equivalent fractional two-mode asymptotic. The 2021 motivating paper's full text remained inaccessible; this is recorded as a residual risk.

## Value

PASS. The result gives an exact, natural finite-dimensional benchmark for the short-horizon energy cost of fractional control. It identifies the hardest target direction and the rate-separation dependence, which are directly relevant to horizon selection and conditioning; it is not a routine numerical recomputation.
