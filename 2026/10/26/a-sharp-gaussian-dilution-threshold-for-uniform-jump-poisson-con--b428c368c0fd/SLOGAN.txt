For centered compound-Poisson sums with independent uniform \([1,2]\) jumps and Gaussian noise, the exact optimal variance-preserving right sub-gamma scale switches from third-cumulant control to a uniquely interior-controlled optimizer at \(\sigma^2/\lambda=257/372\). A log-concavity principle proves uniqueness for a wider class of positive jump distributions and yields an exact quadratic crossover law for the uniform example.
