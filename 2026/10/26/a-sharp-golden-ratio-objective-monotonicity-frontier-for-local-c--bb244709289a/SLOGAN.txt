For the isotropic quadratic \(f(x)=\|x\|_2^2/2\), local-curvature descent 3 with admissible fixed matrix \(C\preceq I\) and \(\lambda_{\max}(C)=1\) has an exact worst one-step objective amplification, attained already in the two extreme eigendirections. Its objective is nonincreasing for every initial vector precisely when \(\operatorname{cond}(C)\le(11+5\sqrt5)/2\); beyond that sharp frontier, objective overshoot grows without bound with the condition number.
