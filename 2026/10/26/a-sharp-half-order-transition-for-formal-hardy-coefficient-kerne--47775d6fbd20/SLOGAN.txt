For fractional positive-real symbols \(\phi_\alpha(z)=\sum_jw_j(1-\lambda_jz)^{-\alpha}\) with infinitely many distinct phases, the ordinary Hardy coefficient transform is injective below \(\alpha=1/2\) but has a dense nullspace and full graph closure at and above that sharp threshold. Every symbol in this family has an atomless Herglotz measure, so the pathology is not confined to atomic boundary data.
