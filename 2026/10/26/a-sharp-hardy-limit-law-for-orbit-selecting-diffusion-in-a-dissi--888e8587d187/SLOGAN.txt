The orbit-label diffusion gap of a dissipative time crystal increases strictly with dissipation relative to oscillation, yet its critical-drive normalization decreases to the sharp Hardy constant \(1/16\). Its weak-dissipation expansion is \(\mu(q)=1/2+3q^2/10+O(q^4)\), while \(\mu(q)/(1+q^2)\to1/16\) as \(q\to\infty\); these are statements about the reduced diffusion, not a uniform finite-spin Liouvillian limit.
