For odd populations in the frictionless opportunity-transfer game, the average-welfare loss of a maximal matching lies between \(\frac{q(p-\frac34p^2)}{n}\) and \(\frac{1-q(1-p)}{n}\). The rate is therefore exactly \(\Theta(n^{-1})\) for fixed admissible \(p,q>0\), although the globally optimal odd-population graph is not classified here.
