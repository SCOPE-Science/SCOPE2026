The on/off harmonic trap has an infinitely divisible stationary position law at every positive switching rate, with an explicit universal-rate \(\log2\) compound-Poisson factor. It admits a scalar Lévy-driven Ornstein–Uhlenbeck realization of its stationary marginal exactly for \(\alpha\ge2(\sqrt2-1)\), with a constructive jump law above the threshold and a sharp obstruction below it.
