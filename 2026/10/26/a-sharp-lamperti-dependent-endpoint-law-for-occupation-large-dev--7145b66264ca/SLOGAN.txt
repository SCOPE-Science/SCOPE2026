Poisson resetting of a continuum sluggish random walk produces a full occupation-fraction large-deviation principle with a computable rate for every sluggishness exponent \(\mu=1/(\alpha+2)\). Its rare-event boundary has the sharp law \(I(1-\delta)=r-r\mu^{-\mu}(1-\mu)^{\mu-1}\delta^\mu+o(\delta^\mu)\), revealing a continuously variable endpoint singularity that is invisible in the previously known Gaussian limit.
