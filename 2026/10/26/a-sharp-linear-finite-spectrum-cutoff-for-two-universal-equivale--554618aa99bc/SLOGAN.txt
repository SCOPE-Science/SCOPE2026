Over finite equivalence relations, two-universal Bernays–Schönfinkel sentences have a sharp **linear** finite-spectrum cutoff: if a sentence with \(k\) existential witnesses has a model of size \(2k+2\), then it has models of all sizes from \(k+2\) upward. The optimal finite-spectrum maximum is \(2k+1\), contrasting with the exponential \(k+2^k\) cutoff for unrestricted simple graphs.
