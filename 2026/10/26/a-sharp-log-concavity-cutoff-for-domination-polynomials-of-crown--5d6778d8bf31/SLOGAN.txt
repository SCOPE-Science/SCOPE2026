For the crown graphs \(\operatorname{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M_n\), the ordinary domination polynomial has log-concave coefficients exactly for \(2\le n\le12\), while its total-domination counterpart is log-concave at every order. From \(n=13\) onward the ordinary coefficients fail the degree-three log-concavity test, even though their nonzero coefficient sequences remain strictly unimodal with mode \(n\).
