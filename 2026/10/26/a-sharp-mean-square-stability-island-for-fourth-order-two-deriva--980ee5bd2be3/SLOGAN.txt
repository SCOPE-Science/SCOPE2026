For fourth-order two-derivative Runge–Kutta integration of a planar oscillator with one independent, symmetric relative curvature perturbation shared by both evaluations in each step, nonzero noise creates a mean-square stability band strictly separated from zero step length. This band vanishes at the exact threshold \(\epsilon_c=0.488912721036588\ldots\), above which every positive step amplifies mean-square energy.
