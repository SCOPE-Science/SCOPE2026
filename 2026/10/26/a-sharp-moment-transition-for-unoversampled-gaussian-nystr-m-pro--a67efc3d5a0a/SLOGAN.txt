For an almost rank-one matrix with singular values \(1\) and \(h\), one-vector Gaussian generalized Nyström has exact squared-error expectation \(\frac{k-1}{k-2}h\) for every left-sketch width \(k\ge3\), instead of the optimal \(h^2\). The full moment spectrum switches from \(h^q\) to \(h\log(1/h)\) to \(h\) at exponent one, exposing a rare-sketch accuracy barrier even for one exact step of a smooth constant-forcing matrix ODE.
