The effective matrix is derived with the stated equal-capacity, undirected, quasi-stationary hypotheses. Its positive Perron eigenvalue is simple. The general-graph perturbation proof obtains the leading limiting eigenvalue and the first correction from the exact identity \(d^Tv=an\mu\). On stars, radial reduction yields the quadratic \(a(a+(m+1)f)\mu^2-2mf\mu-m=0\), whose unique positive root is used in an exact sign-preserving crossing comparison. The resulting polynomial \(-\delta f^2+Bf+4am\) gives both directions and the equality boundary, while the rational 21-patch witness is verified by substituting \(6/25\). The separately replayed, exact-rational checker verifies 1,302 star parameter configurations and the star graph-energy identity across 20 sizes. Finite tests are corroborative, not the universal proof. ## Mathematical value — PASS

Exploration is normally associated with improving habitat reach, but lethal transit introduces a real ecological tradeoff. The graph-energy coefficient identifies a structural diagnostic measuring degree heterogeneity through effective resistance; the star theorem converts the asymptotic signal into an exact, sharp mortality threshold for all finite exploration rates. The explicit example changes the deterministic viability verdict between finite and very large exploration. This is a motivated threshold and boundary for the same metapopulation observable as the current source literature, not an arbitrary graph table or parameter slice. The result does not claim a closed optimal exploration rate or a stochastic extinction-time theorem.
