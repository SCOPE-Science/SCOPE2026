In the fast-explorer ecological network model, a star-shaped landscape admits strictly better metapopulation capacity at finite exploration than at unlimited exploration exactly when explorer mortality satisfies \(1+g>4m(m+1)/(m-1)^2\), where \(m\) is its number of leaves. A graph-Laplacian degree-energy formula explains the high-dispersal reversal, and an exact 21-patch example switches between persistence and extinction as exploration becomes too large.
