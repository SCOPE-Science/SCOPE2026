For descendant-cone hypotheses on any finite rooted tree of height \(h\ge1\), the Littlestone dimension lies in the two-point set \(\{\lfloor\log_2(h+1)\rfloor,1+\lfloor\log_2(h+1)\rfloor\}\), and both values are attained at every height. Complete binary trees attain the larger value from height two onward, although every such cone family has VC dimension at most two.
