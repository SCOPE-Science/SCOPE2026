When a positive semidefinite matrix has a one-coordinate leading eigenvector and tail spectrum at most \(q<1\), top-\(m\) truncated power iteration retains that eigencoordinate for every possible tie-breaking rule exactly above the sharp initial squared-overlap threshold \(q^2(m-1)/(m^2+q^2(m-1))\). Below it, an explicit rank-one tail causes permanent loss of the leading direction in one update.
