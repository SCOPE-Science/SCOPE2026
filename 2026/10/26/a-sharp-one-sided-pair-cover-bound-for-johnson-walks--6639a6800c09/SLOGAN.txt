For the Johnson walk collecting all unordered pairs inside successive random \(r\)-sets, the expected cover time has the conjectured sharp leading upper constant \(\limsup_{n\to\infty}\mathbb E T_{n,r}/[\binom n2\log\binom n2/(r-1)]\le1\) for every fixed \(r\ge3\). An exact two-state killed-chain eigenvalue also determines the limiting mean number of unseen pairs at the critical time scale.
