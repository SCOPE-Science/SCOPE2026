# Reproducibility and verification

The result is analytical. The killed generator on injective labeled configurations is the exclusion generator minus the diagonal of the collision hazard. When that hazard is constant, a scalar semigroup factorization proves the exact path-law equality. Necessity is proved by Perron eigenfunctions on labeled communicating components and the connectivity of the unlabeled token graph; the graph classification follows from comparing sums over all equally sized vertex subsets.

Run `python3 verify.py` using Python 3 and the standard library only. The current program passed 117816 exact classification assertions, including exhaustive unweighted connected graphs on three through six vertices, independently sampled directed rates on complete graphs, weighted sparse cycles, and four-site labeled-generator checks. Exact rational tests do not establish results at arbitrary dimension. No external or independent scientific audit has been performed.

The one-time survival criterion requires equality of survival probabilities across **all** distinct initial placements at the same strictly positive horizon. Connectivity and positive rates in both orientations of every undirected edge are assumptions, not conclusions.
