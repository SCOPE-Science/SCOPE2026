On the real affine-regular hexagonal plane, the quadratic skew Schäffer constant has an exact two-branch profile separated by the unique root \(\rho\in(0,1)\) of \(1-2\rho+\rho^2-\rho^3=0\). At \(\tau=1\) it equals \(4/\sqrt{10}\), and the optimizer leaves a transformed-norm facet kink at \(\rho\).
