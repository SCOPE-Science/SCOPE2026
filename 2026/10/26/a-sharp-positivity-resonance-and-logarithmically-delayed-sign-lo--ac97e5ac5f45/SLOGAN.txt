For a genuine second-order damped two-step Chebyshev method, the scalar exponential stays endpoint-positive throughout its stable step interval only down to the sharp threshold \(z_*=-3.76208003388795\ldots\), even though absolute stability extends to \(-9/2\). The boundary step reproduces the scalar exponential exactly, while steps just beyond it hide an eventual negative solution for a logarithmically diverging number of iterations.
