For two-level quantized gradient descent, truncating an independent stochastic-rounding probability to \(r\) random bits produces exactly \(\min\{1,(2^r\eta)^{-1}\}\) worst-target stationary total-variation distortion relative to ideal stochastic rounding. Uniform stationary fidelity as the learning rate \(\eta\) vanishes holds if and only if \(2^r\eta\to\infty\); otherwise the finite-bit rule can lock at the wrong grid point.
