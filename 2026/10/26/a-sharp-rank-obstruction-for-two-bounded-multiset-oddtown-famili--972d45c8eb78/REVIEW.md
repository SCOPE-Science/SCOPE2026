# Scientific review: doubly bounded multiset Oddtown

## Correctness — PASS
The binary layer representation satisfies the minimum-intersection identity coordinate by coordinate. The row Gram matrix is an identity over the binary field, giving a rank bound. The new constraint that second-layer indicators are nested inside first-layer indicators forces a contradiction at full rank and, in even ground-set order, also at codimension one. The complement-projection argument accounts explicitly for characteristic two and for both parity cases. Certificates of sizes six and ten were checked pair by pair. Exhaustive enumeration of all maximal cliques of the finite compatibility graph proves the remaining exact small orders; an independent maximal-clique implementation corroborated these counts. No finite test is extrapolated to prove the unrestricted rank bound.

## Value — PASS
The result identifies exactly where the classical linear bound first fails under the smallest nontrivial multiplicity extension. It proves a structural obstruction for all even ground-set sizes, gives explicit sharp families in two distinct nontrivial orders, and builds families growing at least five-thirds as fast as the ground-set order in the limit. These are mathematically motivated consequences of the Eventown/Oddtown parity duality, not arbitrarily selected evaluations. The exact maximum beyond six elements is still unknown.
