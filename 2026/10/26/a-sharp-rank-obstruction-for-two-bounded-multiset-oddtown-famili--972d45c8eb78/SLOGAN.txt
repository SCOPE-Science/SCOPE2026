For two-bounded multisets with odd self-intersections and even pairwise intersections, the maximum family has size at most \(2n-2\) when the ground set has even size \(n\), and this is sharp at \(n=2,4,6\). The first six maxima are \((1,2,3,6,7,10)\), yielding disjoint-block families of asymptotic size at least \((5/3)n-O(1)\), in contrast to the ordinary Oddtown bound \(n\).
