Every rank-one associative Nijenhuis operator on \(M_n(k)\) is weight-zero Rota–Baxter when \(n\ge3\), but exactly the \(2\times2\) case admits a separate scalar-idempotent family. Over \(\mathbb F_q\), that exceptional family contains exactly \(q^2(q^2-1)\) operators, and the full nonzero rank-one Nijenhuis census is \((q^2-1)(q+1)^2\).
