In a well-mixed public-goods model, synergistic groups may become exponentially rare as group size grows while the basin of full cooperation retains positive width. The sharp rarity exponent is \(\log(1+\delta)\); at its critical value, cooperation's basin contracts on a \(1/n\) scale, and at the exact critical amplitude it contracts on the finer \((1-\delta)^n/n\) scale.
