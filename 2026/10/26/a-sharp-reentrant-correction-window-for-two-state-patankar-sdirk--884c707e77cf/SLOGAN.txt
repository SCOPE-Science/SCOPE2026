For a two-species reversible exchange, the final-stage Patankar correction of L-stable SDIRK21 tends, as its denominator floor \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\), exactly to the simplex-clipped uncorrected predictor—not necessarily the true flow. From a pure-species start, the predictor becomes negative precisely inside a finite step-size window when the equilibrium fraction is below \(3-2\sqrt2\), so the positive, mass-conserving corrected step converges to a simplex face a fixed distance from the exact positive state.
