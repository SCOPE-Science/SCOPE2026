## Value — PASS

The motivating question is not another local-stability check: which conservative dispersal intensity **maximizes the worst linear return rate** following small shocks, without solving equilibrium continuation at every intensity? The RMS ceiling provides a sharp ecology-specific limit depending only on habitat growth heterogeneity. Its graph eigenvector/equitable-partition equality characterization gives a constructive, checkable structural criterion; a complete-graph network with exactly two growth levels always reaches the limit, whereas with three or more it cannot, even with unlimited dispersal. The explicit three-patch example shows a genuine finite optimum that beats both absent and infinite mixing. These are motivated structural classifications, not parameter substitutions or numerical gains without interpretation.

## Limitations and priority risk

Positive rates, finite undirected connected graphs, one globally scaled symmetric dispersal operator and identical quadratic self-limitation are essential. The exponent is local at the positive equilibrium; it does not rank stochastic extinction risk, transient far-field recovery or biomass.
