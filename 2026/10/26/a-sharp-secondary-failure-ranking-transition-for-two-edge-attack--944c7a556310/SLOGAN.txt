For a crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M\) with \(n\ge4\), exactly the incident two-edge failures cause the maximum Kirchhoff-index loss. Among disjoint failures, the second-worst pattern switches sharply at \(n=12\): independent-index pairs lead for \(4\le n\le11\), but reciprocal-index pairs lead for all \(n\ge12\).
