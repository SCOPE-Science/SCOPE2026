# Scientific review

**Correctness (C): PASS.** Conditional on four specified edges of a labelled square, the remaining pairing is exactly uniform. A complete partition of parallel-edge pairs into one-forced/one-residual and two-residual cases gives the stated rational covariance. The proof explicitly handles \(L=4\), degree-one degeneracy, strict sign and fixed-degree asymptotics. Exact rational full enumeration for seven small models agrees with the claimed formulas; it is an additional finite consistency check, not a replacement for the proof.

**Originality (O): PASS, with qualified priority.** The most relevant inspected papers concern simple biregular graph short cycles or marginal multiple-edge counts in the pairing model. Neither model/result directly implies this finite mixed covariance or its sharp degree-two sign switch. Searches for two-/four-cycle mixed moments and alternate parameterizations identified no covering published theorem. A more general unindexed exact factorial-moment result remains a priority risk; absence of a search result is not proof of uniqueness.

**Value (V): PASS.** Both observables are natural short-cycle statistics for the bipartite configuration model. The result settles their entire finite-size covariance sign for all allowed degrees, including one exceptional smallest model, and identifies a novel difference in leading covariance decay orders. This supplements marginal and limiting Poisson laws with exact finite dependence information.

**Closest literature and limitations.** Dumitriu and Zhu, arXiv:2008.11760, study simple biregular graphs, forbidding multiple edges; Angel, van der Hofstad and Holmgren, arXiv:1603.07172, consider multiple-edge approximations without the mixed covariance; McKay, Wormald and Wysocka, DOI:10.37236/1819, cover short-cycle laws for simple graphs. The theorem does not apply directly to uniform simple graphs, unlabelled-uniform multigraphs, or four-cycles in the collapsed simple support. No independent audit has been performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
