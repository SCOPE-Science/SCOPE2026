In the uniform bipartite pairing multigraph, the covariance between parallel-edge pairs and edge-distinguished four-cycles is strictly positive only at degree two with at least three vertices per side, and strictly negative for every other nondegenerate degree pair (including the two-vertex degree-two exception). An exact finite-size formula reveals a sharp transition from \(L^{-2}\) positive dependence to \(L^{-1}\) negative dependence as the degrees leave two.
