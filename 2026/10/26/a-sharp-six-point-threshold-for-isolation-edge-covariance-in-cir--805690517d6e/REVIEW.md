# Scientific review

## Correctness

The proof reduces covariance to the difference between the expected edge count conditional on one specified vertex being isolated and the unconditional mean. Conditional isolation places all remaining points independently inside a single permitted interval, so their edge probability is exactly the two-point triangular-area formula. The resulting exact covariance has a quadratic sign polynomial for radii below one third and a linear sign factor above. Discriminant, endpoint, and monotonicity arguments prove the sharp six-point threshold and the two simple roots for every larger size. Rational arithmetic and independently seeded simulations support the derivation; finite tests are not an infinite proof.

## Originality

The closest inspected work concerns isolated-count limit distributions and connectivity for soft geometric graphs, or large deviations of the total edge count for Poisson geometric graphs. Their stated models and theorems neither coincide with nor directly imply the fixed-size hard-circle mixed covariance sign classification. Relevant prior mixed-moment or Palm-calculus literature could implicitly yield parts of the formula; full historical priority is not established. Accessible mathematical records did not identify the complete two-reversal theorem.

## Value

Isolation and edges are standard competing notions of sparse-network structure. Positive covariance is counterintuitive without accounting for vertex clustering: conditioning on an isolate confines all other points to one interval, raising their mutual edge propensity. The exact size-six threshold and two universal radius transitions quantify this mechanism with a complete infinite-family classification, not an arbitrary finite example.

## Scientific limitations

The result is specific to iid uniform points on the one-dimensional torus and a hard connection rule. It does not cover soft graphs, nonuniform intensities, or higher dimensions. Search-based evidence cannot guarantee novelty, and no independent review has been performed.
