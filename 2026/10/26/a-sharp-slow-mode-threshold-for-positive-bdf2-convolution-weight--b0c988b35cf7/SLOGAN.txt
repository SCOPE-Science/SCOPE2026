For the positive memory kernel \(k(t)=\int_a^b e^{-st}\,ds\) with \(0\le a<b\), every uncorrected BDF2 convolution-quadrature coefficient is strictly positive exactly when \(ha\le1/2\). Beyond this threshold, infinitely many coefficients of each sign occur, regardless of how large the upper relaxation rate \(b\) is.
