For every \(n\ge2\) and \(m\ge2\), the automorphism group scheme of \(k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]/(x_1^m,\ldots,x_n^m)\) is smooth exactly when \(m\) is invertible in \(k\); otherwise it acquires \(n\) independent infinitesimal translation directions and has tangent dimension \(nm^n\). When \(m\) is a power of the characteristic, its reduced automorphism group and its finite-field point count also have explicit uniform formulas.
