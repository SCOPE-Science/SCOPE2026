For exponential Euler with noncommuting symmetric positive-definite generator \(K\) and reference operator \(H\), stability for every positive step is equivalent to \(K\preceq2H\), with a common decreasing quadratic energy. Nevertheless, Euclidean one-step amplification is possible at **every** step size when \(H\) is nonscalar, and its sharp unrestricted amplification supremum is \(\sqrt{\kappa_2(H)}\).
