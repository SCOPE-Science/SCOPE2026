The proof was checked analytically from the stated definitions. The sequence of coordinate functionals has norm one, each \(R_n\) is rank one with norm one, and an orthonormal sequence is weakly null. For a compact \(S\), this gives \(\|Se_n\|\to0\). The Cauchy--Schwarz maximization yields the upper bound \(\sqrt{1+\|S\|^2}\), and \(S_0=f_0\otimes z\) gives equality \(\sqrt2\) for every index. Thus the supremum and its sharpness are established without numerical computation.
