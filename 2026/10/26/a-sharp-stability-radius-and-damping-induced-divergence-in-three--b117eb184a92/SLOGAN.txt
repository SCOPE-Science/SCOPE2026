Exactly three Picard evaluations of an implicit saddle-point proximal step have the sharp uniform normal-plane contraction radius \(\gamma L<\rho_*=0.940521095740431\ldots\), even though the inner fixed-point map contracts for every \(\gamma L<1\). A strongly convex–strongly concave quadratic with \(\gamma L=19/20\) expands every nonzero outer iterate, while both pure-gradient and purely bilinear endpoints are stable at the same step.
