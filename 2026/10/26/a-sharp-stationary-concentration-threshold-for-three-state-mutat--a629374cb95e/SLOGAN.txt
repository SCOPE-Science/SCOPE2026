For no-self-transition mutation kernels on three alleles with fixed stationary law \(\pi\), the best nonstationary spectral radius equals \(1/2\) exactly when \(\max_i\pi_i\le4/9\). Above that sharp concentration threshold it becomes \((1+\sqrt{9-4/\max_i\pi_i})/2\); the complete circulation parametrization also identifies exactly when complex spectra and defective optimizers occur.
