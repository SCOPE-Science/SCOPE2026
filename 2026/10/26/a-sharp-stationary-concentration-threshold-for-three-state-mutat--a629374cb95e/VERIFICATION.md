# Verification and scope

The core proof is symbolic and finite-dimensional. For the defined zero-diagonal kernels, it solves all row/column flow equations, computes the characteristic polynomial and sharp circulatory discriminant, and minimizes over the *entire closed feasible current interval*. The equality conditions and the nonuniform repeated-root Jordan structure are explicitly proved. No numerical experiment is used to justify a universal quantifier.

The accompanying `verify.py` uses only Python standard-library rational arithmetic. To reproduce the finite checks, run `python3 verify.py` next to these documents. It verifies the flow construction, stationarity, exact trace and determinant, boundary identity and formulas for a finite grid of stationary triples and circulations, reporting **93,708 checks passed**. Finite sampling does not establish exhaustive correctness over all real stationary distributions.

The novelty comparison examined the primary source defining the same no-holding mutation matrices, reversible fastest-mixing work, related stochastic-root abstracts, direct historical claims and targeted semantic mathematical records. Prior coverage could remain in uninspected older literature; the available Guerry 2022 full text was inaccessible and requires further human-enabled access.
