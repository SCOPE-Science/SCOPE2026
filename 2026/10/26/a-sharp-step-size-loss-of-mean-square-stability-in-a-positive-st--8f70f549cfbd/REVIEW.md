# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
For the published noise-first, heat-second update on two interior nodes, the heat matrix and independent lognormal noise moments determine the entire expected squared-norm dynamics through a closed positive \(2\times2\) recursion. Its Perron eigenvalue gives the exact cutoff. Factoring \(\det(I-K)\) yields \(m_*=(2+y+y^2)/(y(1+y+2y^2))\), while a strictly negative exact second derivative proves the noise-threshold function decreases from \(3\) to \(2\). The almost-sure result follows from an independent conditional \(\ell^1\) estimate and Borel–Cantelli; the semidiscrete Itô comparison follows from the two-site moment generator. The exact doubling-step witness was checked without numerical approximation. The standalone script passed 1,526 algebraic/moment assertions. Those assertions do not constitute the universal proof; the identities and inequalities in the result do.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk
The original 2023 preprint and its 2024 published full text were inspected through the scheme definition, principal results and related analysis. The source establishes finite-horizon positivity, CFL-type moment estimates and convergence, not a fixed-grid infinite-time phase diagram. Lang–Petersson–Thalhammer (2017), inspected in open full text, provide a general necessary/sufficient spectral-radius framework for mean-square stability of discretizations, including different rational and Euler-type schemes; applying an abstract criterion still requires the nontrivial exact threshold and strict monotonicity proved here. Chen–Dang–Hong (2022), inspected in the author-hosted published full text, treat periodic boundary conditions and a \(\theta\)-scheme, so their weak-intermittency statements do not imply the Dirichlet exact Lie–Trotter cutoff. Yang–Yang–Zhang (2022) study a different split-step backward-Euler scheme with finite-dimensional noise; only the accessible abstract was inspected, and institutional full-text retrieval was unavailable. Yang–Hu (2025) analyze correlated-noise implicit/spectral discretizations; accessible abstract only. Searches for the exact two-node lognormal Lie–Trotter threshold and aliases in mathematical records identified no covering statement, but neither unsuccessful searches nor different-method comparisons certify absolute novelty. The general almost-sure/mean-square dichotomy is established background and is not claimed as original. The reviewed claim is the sharp, strictly step-dependent \(3\)-to-\(2\) frontier for the particular published update.

## Value — PASS
The authors' original finite-horizon CFL and positivity analysis naturally raises the distinct question of long-time stability at fixed mesh. The exact two-node calculation exhibits a practically relevant false inference: positivity preservation and even almost-sure exponential decay do not prevent mean-square blow-up when the step is increased. Unlike an arbitrary isolated numerical example, the complete classification supplies a sharp cutoff for all noise amplitudes, proves its strict dependence on the step, matches the small-step semidiscrete stability boundary and gives an exact witness of the induced mismatch. No extension to finer grids or general coefficients is asserted.
