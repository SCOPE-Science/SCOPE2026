On the two-interior-node Dirichlet grid, a published positivity-preserving stochastic heat Lie–Trotter splitter is almost-surely exponentially decaying for every noise intensity and every step, yet its exact mean-square stability threshold \(\zeta=\mathcal F(\tau/h^2)\) falls strictly from \(3\) to \(2\) as the step grows. Thus at fixed noise intensity \(2<\zeta<3\), increasing the step alone creates exponential second-moment growth while preserving positivity and pathwise decay.
