For identical opponent-coordinating payoffs on a complete network, mixed imitation and best-response updates share the strict switch potential \(\Phi(k)=|k-h|\), so every fair sequential activation schedule converges after at most \(n-1\) actual switches, and one best responder suffices to force consensus. The bound is sharp, while an all-imitator population can additionally retain exactly classified, payoff-tied mixed equilibria.
