At fixed positive throughput and exact shear-dissipation budget in a no-slip planar channel, the Taylor–Aris dispersion-maximizing velocity profile undergoes a sharp reflection-symmetry-breaking transition when the budget exceeds its Poiseuille minimum by \(3.085047\ldots\%\). Below the threshold the optimizer is uniquely symmetric; above it precisely two reflected optimizers appear with an exact square-root asymmetry amplitude and a linear optimal-dispersion law.
