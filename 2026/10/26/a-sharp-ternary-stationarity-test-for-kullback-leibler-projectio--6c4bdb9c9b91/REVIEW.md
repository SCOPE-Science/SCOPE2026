# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

The curve through each positive reference is differentiable by the inverse-function and implicit-function theorems, since \(f''(1)>0\). Its tangent is the weighted centered statistic. Differentiating divergence in the second slot gives the \(h(u)=f(u)-uf'(u)\) identity. The explicit asymmetric perturbation keeps normalization and mean exactly fixed; its derivative yields an identity for any \(x>1\), \(0<y<1\). Differentiating this identity forces \(h'\) constant, and integration yields precisely positive multiples of KL plus an affine generator. KL converse and two-point minimality are separately established. Exact-arithmetic checks independently confirm representative identities and a non-KL counterexample. The proof relies on neither finite enumeration nor inaccessible numerical certificates.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Bec–Le Corre–Garivier (arXiv:2610.21872v1, Sections 2.3–4 and Remark 18) prove a broad characterization under a stronger hypothesis that quantifies over every finite support, with a five-point configuration for their single-statistic argument. Their displayed three-point counterexamples apply only to particular non-KL generators. The fixed-three-atom and first-order-stationarity implication is neither stated nor a direct logical consequence of their universal-support theorem. Jiao et al. (arXiv:1404.6810v2, Sections III-C–D) require stronger structural Bregman/sufficiency hypotheses. Targeted searches for ternary information projection rigidity and generalized exponential-family local stationarity, together with review of the accessible prior research claims, identified no equivalent characterization. Residual risk: an older equivalent statement in alternate statistical-geometric language may exist.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This isolates the smallest possible alphabet needed to diagnose KL projection geometry under only one moment constraint. It replaces an all-finite-support global projection requirement with the substantially weaker local first-derivative test, while retaining a sharp if-and-only-if characterization. The result is directly motivated by the 2026 characterization's finite-configuration proof and by its ternary examples; the minimal ternary threshold is not a mere cosmetic numerical improvement.

## Scope and outstanding risks

The theorem deliberately excludes the nontrivial degenerate-curvature case \(f''(1)=0\); the motivating work's broader theorem handles it with additional configurations. The exact minimal witness should not be extrapolated to other moment families. All novelty judgments are provisional pending independent assessment.
