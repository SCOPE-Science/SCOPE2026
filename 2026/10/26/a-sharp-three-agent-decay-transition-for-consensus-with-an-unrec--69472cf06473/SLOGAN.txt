On a three-agent adaptive-confidence HK triangle whose initial effective network is a path, the confidence-decay factor has an exact threshold \(\delta=1/2\): below or at it, all opinions converge together while the missing dyad remains unreceptive and its confidence tends to zero; above it, that dyad activates at a computable time and exact consensus follows one step later. Three agents are minimal for this persistent disagreement-in-receptiveness despite agreement-in-opinion.
