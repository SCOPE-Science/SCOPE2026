The three-stage PISV projective integrator has three distinct negative-real-axis stability bands exactly when its inner-to-outer step ratio satisfies \(0<\lambda<1/9\), but a single gap-free band when \(\lambda\ge1/9\). The two instability gaps, all band endpoints, and two neutral modes at the critical ratio are given by exact formulas; this is a scalar spectral statement, not a nonlinear stability guarantee.
