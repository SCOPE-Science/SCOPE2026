Exact block-Jacobi scaling cannot worsen the condition number of a rank-one SPD spike split into two blocks, but it can do so with three: for block energies \((s,s,t)\), the deterioration regions are exactly \(0<t<s\) and \(s<t<s(4s+3)/(s+1)\). Across this family, the worsening factor is strictly less than \(3/2\), and the bound is sharp.
