For proper \(q\)-colourings of the \(n\)-vertex path tested by binary vertex-colour questions, online shattering is \(n\) for every \(q\ge3\), but ordinary VC dimension equals \(n-\lfloor n/4\rfloor\) **only** when \(q=3\) (and equals \(n\) when \(q\ge4\)). Thus three colours, unlike two or four, give an unbounded VC–Littlestone gap on connected paths.
