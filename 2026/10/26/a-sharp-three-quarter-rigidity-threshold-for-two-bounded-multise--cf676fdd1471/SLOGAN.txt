In every two-bounded multiset Eventown family on \(n\ge2\) labelled elements, allowing even one occurrence of multiplicity \(1\) forces its size down to at most \(3\cdot2^{n-2}\), exactly three-quarters of the unrestricted maximum. This sharp threshold is attained by \(\{(0,0),(1,1),(2,2)\}\times\{0,2\}^{n-2}\).
