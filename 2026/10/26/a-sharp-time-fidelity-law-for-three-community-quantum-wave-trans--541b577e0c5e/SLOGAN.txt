For a three-community quantum walk with equal internal masses and equal weak-link multiplicities, the reduced endpoint wave has the sharp long-time fidelity law \(\liminf_{\tau\to\infty}\tau^2(1-P(\tau))=(M/k)\pi^4/144\). A Pell sequence with optimized observation times attains this bound; no faster-than-quadratic fidelity improvement is possible for the reduced wave.
