In a two-season Ricker stock–recruitment model, moving one proportional harvest from before the first season to before the second reverses its terminal-abundance ranking twice as the initial stock varies, if and only if the cross-season crowding pressure exceeds the exact threshold \(\rho>s^{-1/(1-s)}\). The reversal is possible even with locally stable unharvested recruitment, and its boundary is independent of the final season's uncrowded recruitment multiplier.
