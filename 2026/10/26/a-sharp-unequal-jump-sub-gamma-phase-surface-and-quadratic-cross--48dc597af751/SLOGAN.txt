For a centered variance-one compound Poisson law with unequal positive and negative jump sizes, the exact right sub-gamma scale changes from an interior optimum to local cumulant control on an explicit two-parameter phase curve; Gaussian smoothing appears as a nontrivial endpoint with critical positive-jump variance fraction \(3/4\). The excess scale emerges quadratically across the curve, while the equal-jump endpoint of the phase boundary is previously known.
