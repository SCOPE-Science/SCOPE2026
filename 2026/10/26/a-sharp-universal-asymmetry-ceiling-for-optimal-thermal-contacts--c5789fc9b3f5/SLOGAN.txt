For an unpinned two-mass Langevin thermal conductor with fixed total damping, a unique split maximizes steady heat current, depending only on the mass ratio and not on the spring, budget, or bath temperatures. The maximizing damping fraction never differs from \(1/2\) by more than the sharp constant \(\sqrt7/28\), attained precisely at reciprocal mass ratios \((16\pm5\sqrt7)/9\).
