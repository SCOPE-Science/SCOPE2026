For the published two-stage damped Chebyshev two-step method, a vanishing damping parameter \(\varepsilon\) creates internal evaluation times of order \(h/\varepsilon\) and a local error coefficient of order \(1/\varepsilon\) on the smooth equation \(y'=t^2\). With exact startup, global convergence as \(h\to0\) and \(\varepsilon\to0\) holds **if and only if** \(\varepsilon/h\to\infty\); at \(\varepsilon\asymp h\), the numerical solution has a nonzero limiting bias.
