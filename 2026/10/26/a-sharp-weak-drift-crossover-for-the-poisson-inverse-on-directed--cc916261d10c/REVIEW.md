**Correctness — PASS.** The finite Green formula is obtained from a closed first-difference recurrence and checked against the full mean-zero Poisson equation. The exact endpoint norm follows from cyclic row permutations and sign extremizers. Fourier domination proves the weak-drift scaling, and an absolute-sum error estimate proves the stronger-drift scaling; the results are not inferred from finite enumeration. The symmetric-cycle calculation in Beveridge establishes that the zero-bias Green kernel is prior, not new. Relevant searches used group-inverse, deviation-matrix, Green, Poisson inverse and weak-drift terminology. **Mathematical value — PASS.** The exact sharp bound solves a motivated inverse-stability problem for a nonreversible Markov operator and identifies the natural critical drift scale where the norm changes order. The sign structure and absolute-Green integral supply an informative sharp interpolation not obtained from qualitative walk convergence.

**Limitations.** The result is only for spatially homogeneous nearest-neighbor cyclic walks. General Green representations, the symmetric-cycle kernel and standard advection–diffusion heuristics are acknowledged prior art. This is not an independent mathematical or bibliographic audit.
