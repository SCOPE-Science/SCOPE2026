For the biased nearest-neighbor directed cycle, the sharp mean-zero Poisson inverse norm crosses from \((\sqrt3/27)n^2\) at zero bias to \(n/(4\varepsilon)\) when \(n\varepsilon\to\infty\). At critical bias \(n\varepsilon\to\beta\), its exact normalized limit is the absolute integral of an explicit periodic advection–diffusion Green kernel, whose negative set is one interval.
