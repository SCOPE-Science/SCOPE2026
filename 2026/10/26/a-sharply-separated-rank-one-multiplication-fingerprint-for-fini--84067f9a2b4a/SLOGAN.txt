A single mixed rank-one product count \(B_n(a)=n!\sum_z |a^{-1}(z)|^n\) recovers the entire kernel partition of a finite sandwich transformation, and hence distinguishes all full-transformation variant-isomorphism classes via Tsyaputa's criterion. The different values are separated by at least the sharp gap \(n!(2^n-2)\) for \(n\ge2\), with explicit greedy recovery.
