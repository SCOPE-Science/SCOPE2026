For \(P_s(z)=cz^2+s bz+a\) with independent circular Gaussian coefficients, the number of zeros in the unit disk changes exactly once as \(s\) increases, at the random threshold \(T=\bigl||c|^2-|a|^2\bigr|/|b\overline c-a\overline b|\). Its exact law is \(\mathbb P(T\le s)=s/\sqrt{s^2+2}\), independent of an equiprobable choice between zero and two initial roots; the threshold has finite mean but infinite variance.
