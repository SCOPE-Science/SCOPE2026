For distinct short-cycle counts in Ewens permutations conditioned to have no fixed points, the covariance has the universal leading term \(\theta^2(1-\theta)/n^2\) whenever \(\theta\ne1\), irrespective of which two cycle lengths are counted. Exactly at \(\theta=1\), every finite covariance instead alternates in sign with \(n-a-b\) and decays at the factorial scale \(e/[ab(n-a-b+1)!]\).
