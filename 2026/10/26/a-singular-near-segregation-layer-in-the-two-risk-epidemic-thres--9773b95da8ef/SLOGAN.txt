For the two-risk network epidemic criterion, almost-perfect assortativity creates a singular window \(1-h\asymp\delta\) in which the response to a small risk contrast \(\delta\) follows an explicit square-root law. Perfect segregation amplifies the first-order threshold shift by a factor \(1/p\) relative to any fixed imperfect segregation, while the published trace-only low-transmission approximation can underestimate the exact degree-asymptotic threshold by a sharp factor of two.
