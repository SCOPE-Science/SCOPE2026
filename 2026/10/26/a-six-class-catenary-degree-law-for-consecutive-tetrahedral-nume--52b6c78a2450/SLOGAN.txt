The ordinary catenary degree of the semigroup generated by four consecutive tetrahedral numbers has an exact three-branch law: it equals \(n+5\) for \(n\equiv3,5\pmod6\), \(n+4\) for \(n\equiv0\pmod6\), and \(n+3\) for \(n\equiv1,2,4\pmod6\), for every \(n\ge4\). The key is that one of the known minimal-presentation trades is necessarily a bottleneck in each congruence class.
