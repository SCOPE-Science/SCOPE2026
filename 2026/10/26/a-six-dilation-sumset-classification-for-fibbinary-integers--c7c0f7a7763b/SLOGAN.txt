For \(\mathcal F\) consisting of nonnegative binary integers without adjacent ones, the first six dilated sumsets \(\mathcal F+d\mathcal F\) are classified exactly. The sixth has precisely the logarithmically sparse nonrepresentable integers \(\{(4^k-7)/3:k\ge2\}\), while the fourth has a full residue-class obstruction and the fifth misses only \(3\).
