# Mathematical assessment

## Correctness: PASS

For the specified irreducible directed sixteen-cycle, the invariant phase distribution is uniform and the Palm distribution is precisely intensity weighted. A Duhamel expansion produces a leading Fourier-mode integral and a nonasymptotic absolute remainder bound. At the specified time, elementary inequalities give the strictly negative survival difference, with a strict positive derivative at time zero. The proof verifies all rates are positive, the generator is conservative, and the approximation remainder cannot mask the sign. Arbitrary-precision matrix exponentials are an additional reproducibility check, not a replacement for this argument.

## Originality: PASS (bounded search and residual priority risk)

The 2019/2022 Asanjarani–Nazarathy full manuscript defines exactly these initial laws, proves special cases, and explicitly conjectures the universal inequality. A four-state example in that manuscript invalidates a hazard-rate claim, not the stochastic order at issue. Targeted literature and relevant public-result searches did not reveal this sixteen-state crossing construction or a stronger result implying it. Of particular concern, an earlier 2018 preprint by the same authors **claims in its abstract** a general proof and associated stochastic order, whereas the later full paper treats the order as open. That earlier full text was not accessible; the contradiction is recorded, and historical priority is not certified. The proof here is independent of that literature conflict.

## Value: PASS

The order is a substantive qualitative premise for comparisons of stationary point processes. An explicit, rigorously certified counterexample changes its general validity and exposes why finite-dimensional random searches at short horizons can miss a very small oscillatory crossing. The use of a single Fourier mode supplies a transparent nonreversible mechanism and an exact lower-bound certificate.
