A sixteen-phase directed-cycle Markov-modulated Poisson process with strictly positive sinusoidal rates has crossing time-stationary and event-stationary waiting-time survivals, disproving the universal stochastic order conjectured by Asanjarani and Nazarathy. The reversal at \(u=3\pi/[2\sin(\pi/8)]\) is certified by explicit Fourier analysis and an analytic perturbation-error bound, not numerical subtraction.
