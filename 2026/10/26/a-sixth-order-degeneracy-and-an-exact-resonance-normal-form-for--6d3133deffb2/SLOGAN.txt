At the sharp Chang–Marshall Dirichlet exponential extremizer, the \(z^2\) mode is quadratically flat, its unique real \(z^3\) correction cancels the fourth-order deficit, and the optimal \(z^4\) correction leaves an exact sixth-order logarithmic deficit \(x^6/24+O(x^8)\). Hence no Dirichlet-distance stability inequality with positive constant and exponent less than six can hold near the extremizer orbit; any putative exponent-six constant is at most \(e/192\).
