The proof was checked in two cases. If \(\lambda\ge\kappa\), the function equal to \(\lambda\) at one state and \(\lambda^+\) elsewhere belongs to the size cut and witnesses both strict inequalities. If \(\lambda<\kappa\), a nonconstant cardinal in the domain has all coordinates at least \(\kappa\), but a candidate intermediate is pointwise at most \(\lambda^+\le\kappa\); the equality case forces the candidate to be constant. The pointwise transfer lemma establishes ZFC for the filtered domain, and GCH supplies \(2^\lambda=\lambda^+\). No finite computation is used to support the infinite claim.

Limitations: the result assumes a transitive set model of \(\mathrm{ZFC}+\mathrm{GCH}\) and covers one specific size cut. Independent audit and independent validation have not been performed.
