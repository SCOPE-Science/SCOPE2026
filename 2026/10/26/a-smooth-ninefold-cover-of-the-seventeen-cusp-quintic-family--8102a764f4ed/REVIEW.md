# Mathematical review: a smooth ninefold cover of the seventeen-cusp quintic family

## Correctness — PASS
The source hypotheses specify a normal complex quintic with exactly seventeen \(A_2\) cusps and two independent weight-twelve cusp-code vectors meeting in seven cusps. Tan’s weight restrictions and the Griesmer lower bound force code dimension exactly two; counting nonzero incidences yields weights \(12,12,12,15\) and column blocks of sizes \(5,5,5,2\). The localization sequence, vanishing of \(H^1(\widetilde S,\mathbb F_3)\), and local \(A_2\) uniformization establish the smooth connected ninefold cover. Euler, Noether and character-invariance arguments give the stated Hodge and Chern invariants. The checked Python enumeration validates the finite code model only, not the geometric proof.

## Originality — PASS, with stated residual risk
Borisov–Rito (arXiv:2608.12305v1) explicitly give two overlapping twelve-cusp structures for their 2026 family; their text does not calculate the complete code or describe the smooth maximal degree-nine cover. Tan’s 1999 classification covers weights and invariants for individual cyclic triple covers, and Barth–Rams establish general cusp-code/contact-cubic theory; none is misattributed. Eight actual semantic published-finding corpus queries, independent related-source searches and direct primary-literature comparisons found no equivalent or stronger statement implying this complete claim. This is not proof of global historical uniqueness: a previously unindexed equivalent cover, particularly in earlier quintic literature, remains possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The complete cusp-code geometry forces precisely three twelve-cusp divisibility classes, one fifteen-cusp class and a distinguished omitted cusp pair. Its new smooth, canonically polarized ninefold cover has explicitly determined \(K^2=45\), \(p_g=10\), \(q=0\), \(c_2=87\), four inertia fixed-point counts and intermediate cyclic quotients. This naturally advances the geometric understanding of the cited 17-cusp family rather than recomputing an isolated textbook invariant.

## Limitations and closest literature

The new result is conditional on those source hypotheses and is not a classification of all 17-cusp quintics or their contact-cubic presentations.
