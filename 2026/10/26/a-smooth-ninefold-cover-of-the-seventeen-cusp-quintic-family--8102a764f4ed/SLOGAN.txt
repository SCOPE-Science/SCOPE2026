For the seventeen-cusp quintics constructed from two intersecting Barth–Rams structures, the full cusp code has precisely three twelve-cusp words up to sign and one fifteen-cusp word up to sign. These four characters assemble into a smooth degree-nine \((\mathbb Z/3)^2\)-cover with \(K^2=45\), \(p_g=10\), \(q=0\) and order-three fixed-point profile \((15,15,15,6)\).
