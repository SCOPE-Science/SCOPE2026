For smooth characteristic-five Picard curves \(y^3=x^4+ax^2+bx+c\), the Jacobian has \(a\)-number \(1\) and \(5\)-rank \(0\) exactly when \(c^2+a^2c+ab^2=0\), and otherwise has \(5\)-rank \(2\). The zero-rank divisor is smooth and geometrically irreducible, has motivic class \((\mathbb L-1)^2\), and contains exactly \((q-1)^2\) marked smooth parameter triples over every \(\mathbb F_{q}\) with \(q=5^n\).
