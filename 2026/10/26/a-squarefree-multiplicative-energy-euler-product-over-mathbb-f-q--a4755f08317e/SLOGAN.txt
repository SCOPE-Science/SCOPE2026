For fixed \(q\), equal-degree products of four monic squarefree polynomials over \(\mathbb F_q[T]\) have multiplicative energy asymptotic to \((n+1)q^{2n}\) times an explicit positive Euler product \(\prod_P(1-|P|^{-1})^4(1+4|P|^{-1}+|P|^{-2})\). The local constant is \(1-9/q+O(q^{-2})\) in the large-field limit.
