# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof reduces missing coupons to intersections of independent uniformly chosen complements. A direct hypergeometric likelihood-ratio identity gives stochastic ordering of pairwise intersections under size equalization; uniform-permutation nesting extends it to arbitrarily many rounds. Finite majorization transfers and an explicit strictly Schur-concave expectation complete the global result. The independent checker reproduces 6707 exact assertions. A finite checker alone is not the proof.

## Originality — PASS (priority conditional)
Betken–Thäle (arXiv:2202.07485v1) treats a constant packet size and Gaussian/Poisson approximation. Stadje (1990) optimizes stopping/purchasing with a fixed packet size; Schilling (2021) and Berend–Sher (2026) optimize distributions over packets of a fixed size. No inspected source or searched published-record result covered the arbitrary deterministic packet-size vector stochastic majorization law. Hypergeometric probability and majorization methods are classical; the new claim is their combination into an all-round coverage ordering, not those tools. A broad overlooked stochastic-order theorem or unread older full text may subsume the result, and independent priority adjudication is outstanding.

## Value — PASS
A fixed budget of coupon exposures can be allocated across multiple independent packets in different ways. The new theorem completely ranks packet profiles by majorization at every coverage threshold, with sharp balanced and concentrated allocation extremizers. Stochastic order is a stronger planning guarantee than a mean comparison.
