For a harmonically trapped direction-reversing active Brownian particle, the stationary fourth cumulant vanishes along a known parameter curve, but the exact standardized sixth cumulant equals \(-15(3d+1)/[2(d+1)^2(8d+3)]\), where \(d\) is angular diffusivity divided by trap relaxation. It remains strictly negative and sweeps the exact interval \((-5/2,0)\): a quantitative non-Gaussian witness invisible to kurtosis.
