In a two-state randomly switching harmonic trap, the difference \(-C'(t)-DR(t)\) between stationary position-correlation decay and the equilibrium-form drift response is strictly positive at every \(t>0\) whenever the two stiffnesses differ, with an exact two-exponential profile and one finite-time peak. If one stiffness is zero, its integrated area is exactly \(Da/[b(a+b)]\), independent of the nonzero trap stiffness.
