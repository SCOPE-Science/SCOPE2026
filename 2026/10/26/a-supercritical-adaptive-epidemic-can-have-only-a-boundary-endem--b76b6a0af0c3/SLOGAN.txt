A supercritical adaptive SIS model with two strongly connected communities and smooth monotone contact responses can have **no fully endemic equilibrium**, despite \(\mathcal R_0=(9+\sqrt{65})/8>1\): its only nonzero equilibrium instead has infection in one community and is attracting. Conversely, strict creation of every infection-carrying link at interior prevalence restores an endemic-existence theorem for all finite irreducible multigroup networks.
