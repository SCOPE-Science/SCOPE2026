Every nonrectangular isosceles trapezoid in an explicit fixed-area family has an isolated real Fourier-zero set assembled from one lattice and countably many vertical branches. Its quadratic zero density has a Thomae-type arithmetic jump: for irrational \(\alpha\) it is \(2(1+\alpha)/\pi\), while for reduced rational \(\alpha=p/q\) it decreases by exactly \(2/(\pi q)\).
