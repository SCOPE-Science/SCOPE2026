## Correctness
The idempotent normal form is proved from regressive monotonicity and idempotence. The path criterion follows by comparing the deepest fixed ancestors in the two compositions. The three-state recurrence counts precisely the labelings satisfying that criterion, with the root forced to be common. Boundary cases and the chain specialization follow algebraically from the recurrence.

## Originality
The closest primary source inspected is Ganyushkin–Mazorchuk, arXiv:1006.0316v1, which identifies the Catalan chain monoid and discusses its idempotents. It does not state the rooted-tree ordered-pair recurrence in the inspected abstract, introduction, and theorem discussion. Targeted published-finding corpus searches returned no direct match; the closest results concern finite weak-order mapping counts or unrelated rooted-tree orbit counts. These searches are not a proof of absolute novelty.

## Value
The recurrence computes an exact, motivated semigroup invariant—the proportion of idempotent pairs that commute—for every finite rooted tree in linear many state updates. It separates two natural extremes: stars have proportion \(1\), while chains obey a nontrivial second-order recurrence. This supplies a reusable structural count rather than a one-off enumeration.

## Limitations
Only rooted-tree posets are covered. Arbitrary finite posets, maximal commuting families, and asymptotic distributions of tree shapes remain open here.
