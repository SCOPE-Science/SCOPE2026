For every isolate-free graph with \(n\ge5\) and \(\gamma_{\mathrm{sp}}(G)\ge n-2\), optimal super-coalition partitions have four blocks precisely when the graph of super-dominating two-vertex deletions contains a triangle; those triangles count the optimal partitions exactly. When no triangle exists, optimal partitions have three blocks and their number has an exact formula in the edges and small matching counts of that deletion graph.
