Changing the common link-creation and link-breaking speed in a one-community adaptive SIS model leaves endemic prevalence unchanged but causes a sharp node–focus–node transition, with distinct exact optima for damping speed and relative oscillation. Even more strikingly, starting both systems at the same nearby state, the prevalence ranking of the relative-oscillation optimum versus any faster turnover policy reverses infinitely many times while both converge to that same equilibrium.
