The characteristic-two special fibre of Schur's quartic has a unique non-rational singularity whose minimal resolution is \(\mathbf P^2\) blown up at twelve explicit \(\mathbf F_4\)-points. Its three exceptional \((-3)\)-curves meet at one point, its twelve lines through the singularity are exactly the projected basepoint lines, and the singularity has geometric genus \(1\) and Jacobian length \(16\).
