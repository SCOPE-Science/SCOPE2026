On every cubic \(2n\)-vertex cyclic ladder, crossing the two boundary edges preserves the entire recurrent-state cardinality and each eventual-phase fibre size under binary XOR-neighbour updates. Nevertheless, when \(3\mid n\), the crossed seam changes the sharp transient from two steps to one for odd \(n\), and from \(2^{\nu_2(n)}\) to \(2^{\nu_2(n)+1}\) for even \(n\); the full transient distributions and Möbius-ladder periodic-point counts are exact.
