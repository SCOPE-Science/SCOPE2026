Binary neighbour-XOR dynamics on the wheel \(K_1\vee C_n\) is fully reversible for every odd rim size but has exactly \(2^{n-2^{\nu_2(n)}}\) recurrent configurations for even rim size, with a sharp transient length governed by \(\nu_2(n)\). Explicit polynomial-gcd formulas count every primitive cycle, and the complete distribution of convergence times is determined for all wheel sizes.
