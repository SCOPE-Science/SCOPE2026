In a Bernoulli full shift subjected to homogeneous geometric pantograph delay, the ordinary-time Cesàro averages almost surely accumulate on the whole interval \([0,1]\), while the completed-plateau averages accumulate on a Cantor set when \(b\ge3\). The empirical law of those endpoint averages converges to an explicit self-similar Bernoulli measure of Hausdorff dimension \(H(p)/\log b\), giving two sharply different statistical pictures of the same delayed orbit.
