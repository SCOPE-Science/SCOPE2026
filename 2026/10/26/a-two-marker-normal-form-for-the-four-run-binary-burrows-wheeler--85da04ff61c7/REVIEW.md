# Scientific review: four-run sentinel BWT normal form

## Correctness — PASS
The result applies to every positive integer quadruple and fixes the sentinel convention explicitly. The two suffix classes are lexicographically ordered by the number of leading equal symbols, with a fully resolved tie rule. Their predecessor labels yield a complete word identity, not merely a numerical guess. The exact run count follows from at most two marked positions in each suffix block. All nine-run equality cases follow from the nonnegative corrections in the identity; the two parameter families were checked in both directions. The exact generating function counts these families without overlap. Its pole gives the extremizer proportion, and an \(O(n^2)\) exceptional-set count proves the full two-point limit. A separate suffix-block argument proves the sharp reversal ratio. The standalone verifier reconstructs literal sorted suffixes and checks the complete transformed words, not only run counts, on 4096 quadruples; large instances, small-length full censuses, and the reversal witness are also checked. Finite tests support but do not replace the unrestricted algebraic proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
The closest relevant research is Giuliani et al., arXiv:2008.08506v1 (2020), on reversal asymmetry for Fibonacci/standard-word extensions using a primarily sentinel-free cyclic convention; Nishimoto et al., arXiv:2202.07885v1 (2022), on a general sentinel suffix-BWT construction algorithm; and Giuliani, Lipták and Masillo, ICTCS 2022, on inserting a sentinel into potential BWT **output** words and fully clustered output. The inspected relevant sections do not assert the forward transform identity for every four-run input, the sharp nine-run optimizer classification, its exact length-generating function, or the equidistributed eight/nine limiting law. These comparisons concern mathematical implications and not title similarity. General BWT algorithms can compute individual examples but do not directly state this structural classification. The risk is an equivalent explicit special-family calculation in unindexed literature or a derivation hidden in an unexamined source; neither global uniqueness nor independent novelty certification is claimed.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The BWT run count is a genuine parameter determining the space of run-length compressed indexes. The first nontrivial family of two zero runs and two one runs already shows systematic run-count expansion from four source runs to as many as nine transform runs. Rather than an ad hoc finite example, the theorem gives every transform symbol for all positive run lengths, characterizes **all** extremizers, derives their exact length distribution, proves a nondegenerate two-point limiting law, and relates this natural family to BWT reversal asymmetry through a sharp ratio. The family and invariant are motivated independently of computation; the enumeration and probability law are not routine parameter substitutions.

## Limitations and remaining risk
One terminal sentinel and the alphabet order \(\$<0<1\) are essential. A cyclic sentinel-free transform or a changed beginning symbol need not obey the same formula. The random model is the uniform positive-composition model. The work does not settle unrestricted BWT-run maxima or ratios. Existing general string-processing theory and unindexed special-family treatments are possible originality risks. There has been no independent audit.
