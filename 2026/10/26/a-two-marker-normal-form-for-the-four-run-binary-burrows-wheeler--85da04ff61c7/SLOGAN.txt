For the sentinel Burrows–Wheeler transform of every binary four-run word \(0^a1^b0^c1^d\), four explicit exceptional positions determine the entire transformed string, and its run count never exceeds nine. The nine-run inputs have two exact run-length families, their length-generating function is \(2z^9/((1-z)^2(1-z^2)^2)\), and for a uniformly chosen four-run composition the BWT run count approaches an equal mixture of eight and nine; the sharp forward/reverse BWT-run ratio is \(9/4\).
