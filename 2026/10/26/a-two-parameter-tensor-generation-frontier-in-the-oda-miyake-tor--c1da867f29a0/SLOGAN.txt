In the two-parameter Oda–Miyake family \(Z''_{13}(a,2,a+2,d)\), the canonical bundle is tensor-generating exactly when \(d-a=\pm1\), or \(d=a\) with \(a\ne-1,-2\). Infinite parameter ranges fail because of invariant divisors although every torus-invariant curve has nonzero canonical degree, showing a sharp boundary beyond the previously classified one-parameter families.
