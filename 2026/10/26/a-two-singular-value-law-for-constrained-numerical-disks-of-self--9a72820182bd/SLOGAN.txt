For every self-adjoint conjugate-linear operator on \(\mathbb C^n\) with \(n\ge3\), its constrained \(q\)-numerical range is a closed disk of radius \(\frac12[(1+|q|)s_1+(1-|q|)s_2]\), where \(s_1,s_2\) are the largest two Takagi singular values. The two endpoint radii reconstruct these singular values exactly; all lower singular values are invisible to this family.
