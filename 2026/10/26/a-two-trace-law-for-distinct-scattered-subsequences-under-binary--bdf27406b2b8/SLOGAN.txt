For every nonempty binary seed, repeatedly appending the bitwise complement has an exact two-integer recurrence for its total number of distinct scattered subsequences, together with a rigorously convergent exponential-growth formula. The Thue–Morse prefixes begin with \(1,3,10,72,2664,3647088,\ldots\) distinct nonempty subsequences, and their per-letter growth base is \(1.5686647891503443376\ldots\).
