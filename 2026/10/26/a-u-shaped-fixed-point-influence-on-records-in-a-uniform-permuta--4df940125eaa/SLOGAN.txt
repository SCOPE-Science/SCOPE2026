For a uniformly random permutation, conditioning on a fixed point raises the expected record count at the four end-nearest positions but lowers it at every interior position when \(n\ge5\). The exact conditional shift is \(\binom{n-1}{j-1}^{-1}-1/n\), even though the total number of fixed points and total records have a small positive covariance asymptotic to \(2/n^2\).
