For the Feres random-angle billiard with reduced rational microgeometry \(\alpha=m\pi/n<\pi/6\), every nongrid angle has an \(n\)-state transition graph consisting of a single \((n-2m)\)-cycle with \(2m\) pendant leaves. Its unique stationary weights are proportional to \(\sin\theta\), and the fiber chain mixes precisely when \(n\) is odd; even denominators give an exact period-two oscillatory limit.
