For every finite commutative chain ring of length \(n\) and residue size \(q\), square-zero two-by-two matrices have an explicit image-module-length distribution independent of ring characteristic. Its total is \(q^{4\lfloor n/2\rfloor}+\lceil n/2\rceil(q^2-1)q^{2n-2}\), with a rational length-generating function and an exact acyclic-differential count.
