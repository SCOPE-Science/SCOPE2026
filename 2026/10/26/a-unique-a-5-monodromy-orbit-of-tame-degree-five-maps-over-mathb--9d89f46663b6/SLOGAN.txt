Exactly 36 degree-five rational maps \(\mathbf P^1_{\mathbf F_2}\to\mathbf P^1_{\mathbf F_2}\) have everywhere tame ramification and geometric monodromy \(A_5\); they form a single two-sided Möbius orbit, represented by \((x^2+x+1)/(x^5+x^3+1)\). All four geometric ramification points have index \(3\), with Frobenius orbit degrees \(1+3\).
