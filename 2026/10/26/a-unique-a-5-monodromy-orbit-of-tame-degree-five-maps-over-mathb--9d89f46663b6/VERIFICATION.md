# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Coprime affine coefficient pairs with at least one degree five are exactly the base-point-free homogeneous pairs in fixed coordinates; odd degree makes them separable. The Wronskian has degree at most eight with only even exponents (its derivative is zero), so its square root has degree at most four. The finite critical locus is therefore exhausted by F16 and F8; F16 also contains F4, and duplicated F2 points are removed. At any point, including a pole, the multiplicity in the fiber polynomial B(u)A−A(u)B equals the ramification index. I independently computed those multiplicities by repeated synthetic division, including infinity by reversing the binary forms, and computed the two-sided orbits using inversion and translation as generators on both sides. The replay reproduced 1,536 maps, literal equality with the frozen 60-mask set, profiles 36×(3,3,3,3) and 24×(5,5), sizes 6,18,36, and the witness's 1+3 signature. An index-3 branch fiber cannot contain a second ramified point of this profile, so inertia acts by a single 3-cycle. The geometric group is generated by inertia conjugates because the quotient would give an unramified cover of P¹. Hence it is in A5 and has order divisible by 15. Order 15 would force a normal Sylow 5 contained in its order-10 normalizer in A5; order 30 is excluded since A5 is generated by 3-cycles. The (5,5) profile is equivalent geometrically to x↦x⁵ by moving its two totally ramified points and images to 0 and ∞, hence is cyclic. The size-36 orbit is free. For (7,41) the finite critical cubic is x³+x+1, irreducible, and infinity has index 3 by degree difference.
