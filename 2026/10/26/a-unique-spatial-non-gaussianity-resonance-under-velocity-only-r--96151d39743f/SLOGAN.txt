A harmonically trapped Brownian particle with Poisson resets of velocity alone has the exact stationary positional non-Gaussian parameter \(\alpha_X(r)=6\kappa r/[r^3+9\gamma r^2+(26\gamma^2+10\kappa)r+24\gamma^3+32\gamma\kappa]\), with one and only one positive maximum. As resetting becomes arbitrarily frequent, positional kurtosis returns to its Gaussian-normalized value even though the velocity's normalized excess remains nonzero.
