For two alternating mirror-oriented **directed** SIS contact layers, the exact disease-free Floquet growth rate strictly decreases with the switching period and has a unique zero precisely when the recovery rate lies strictly between the geometric- and arithmetic-mean contact strengths. This gives a sharp period cutoff: two individually subcritical directed layers jointly destabilize the disease-free state under sufficiently fast alternation, with an explicit square-root onset of the critical period.
