A two-point signed chord of the stationary Brownian gyrator has a unique mean-to-noise-optimal observation lag, independent of the two bath temperatures, determined by \(v\coth(vt_*)=(1-e^{-2t_*})^{-1}\) with \(v\) the absolute coupling. The optimal lag increases with coupling and diverges as \(\tfrac12\log(1/(1-v))\) near the stability boundary. The optimal statistical lag is always strictly shorter than the lag maximizing the unstandardized mean signed area.
