Each dressed frequency forms two commuting two-state flips; summing stationary hot-contact flux yields the displayed channel currents. Their exact bath-swap difference is (g p q (p−q)/2)[omega− G(omega−/(2T))−omega+ G(omega+/(2T))], with G=U/(1+pq U), U=4 sinh²((1−1/k)x)/(sinh(2x/k)sinh(2x)). It tends to omega−/omega+<1 as T→infinity and infinity as T→0, proving unique crossing and its signs. ## originality

PASS

Upadhyay et al. (arxiv:2106.02891) study the locally phase-equivalent two-qubit DM hopping model and give the same stationary heat-current structure and opposing transition-frequency contributions (especially Eq.26). Their inspected results discuss rectification direction versus detuning but do not give the proved fixed-temperature-ratio all-absolute-temperature unique inversion law. Fogedby (2023) derives global heat currents and thermodynamic consistency; Khandelwal et al. optimize efficiency/rectification under other contact regimes. The contribution is the strictly monotone scalar ratio and exact global uniqueness, not the already known stationary current formulas.
