For two equally pumped wells beside an ideal straight stream, the sequence minimizing depletion **at the end of two equal pumping intervals** reverses exactly once as interval duration increases: far-first wins for short intervals, near-first for long intervals. The unique switch has a strictly distance-ratio-dependent dimensionless duration bounded sharply between \(1/(3\log2)\) and \(1/z_p^2\), with \(z_p\) the unique positive zero of \(\operatorname{erfc}(z/\sqrt2)-2\operatorname{erfc}(z)\).
