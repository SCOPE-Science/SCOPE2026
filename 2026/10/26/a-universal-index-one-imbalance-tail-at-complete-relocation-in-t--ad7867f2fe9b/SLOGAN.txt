At complete relocation away from the origin, a one-dimensional reinforced monkey walk has a deterministic \(1/(a\log n)\) decay of its origin mass but a nondegenerate random signed-neighbour imbalance of order \(1/\log n\). The limit has an exact joint transform and the universal heavy-tail law \(\Pr(|Q_a|>x)\sim1/x\), independent of the impurity parameter \(a\).
