In every Mallows permutation size \(n\ge3\), the covariance between total cycle count and inversion count is negative at \(q=1\) but positive for sufficiently strong reverse-order bias \(q>1\); the leading positive term is \((n-1)/q\) for odd \(n\) and \((n-3)/q\) for even \(n\). Size \(n=2\) is the sharp exception: its covariance stays negative for every \(q>0\).
