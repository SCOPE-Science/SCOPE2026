Exact-degree spherical-harmonic interpolation on the vertices of a \(d\)-dimensional cube has parity-compatible rank \(2^{d-1}\) at degrees \(d\) and \(d+4\), but loses exactly one Walsh data direction at degree \(d+2\), for every \(d\ge3\). The unique missing character is the product of all coordinate signs, which reappears two degrees later.
