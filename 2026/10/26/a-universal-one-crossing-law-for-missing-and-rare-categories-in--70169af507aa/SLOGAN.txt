In a Dirichlet-multinomial sample with arbitrary positive concentrations, a specified category being missing and another occurring exactly \(r\) times switch from positive to negative covariance exactly once as sample size grows. For symmetric concentrations, the crossing is \(rm-r(\alpha+1)/(2\alpha)+O(m^{-1})\), and for \(\alpha=1\) it is exactly \(r(m-1)\).
