# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Condition on an induced copy of arbitrary m-vertex H inside a fixed vertex subset of G(n,p) and partition r-clique tests by overlap s. For s<2 edges are unconstrained. Summing for s>=2 gives normalized covariance sum_s binom(n−m,r−s)[c_s(H)p^(−binom(s,2))−binom(m,s)]. For 3<=r<=m and n>=m+r−2, the s=2 coefficient is positive. For nonempty noncomplete H, its derivative wrt p is strictly negative, limits +infinity at p↓0 and negative at p↑1; thus unique sign reversal. Empty/complete patterns have fixed signs. Expand s=2,3 overlap ratios to obtain rho plus O(1/(n−m)) with triangle-excess coefficient, exact r=3 sign. Independent exact graph-state enumeration checked three m<=5 examples on n=5. No finite computation substitutes for arbitrary H proof.
