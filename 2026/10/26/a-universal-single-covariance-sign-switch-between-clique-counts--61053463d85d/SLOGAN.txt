For every nonempty, noncomplete induced motif \(H\), its covariance with the number of \(r\)-cliques in \(G(n,p)\) changes sign exactly once, from positive to negative, provided \(n\ge |V(H)|+r-2\) and \(3\le r\le |V(H)|\). The critical density tends to the motif's edge density, with a first-order shift determined by its triangle excess; motifs with the same number of edges can have opposite covariance signs.
