A nonsymmetric four-site exclusion ring can hide bias rectification at quadratic order without preserving bias reversal: at site rates \((2,3,4,11)\), its first even current term is a strictly positive multiple of \(\varepsilon^4\), and its exact even response remains positive throughout the partial-bias range. Four sites are minimal for rectification in this site-trap model, exposing an exceptional cancellation beyond spatial-inversion protection.
