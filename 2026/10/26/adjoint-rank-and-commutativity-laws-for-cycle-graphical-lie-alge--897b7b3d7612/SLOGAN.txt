For the two-step Lie algebra of a cycle with a distinct central basis vector for every edge, a four-state transfer matrix gives **every adjoint-rank multiplicity**, and the probability that two random elements commute is exactly \(q^{-n}(\lambda_+^n+\lambda_-^n+q(1-1/q)^n)\), where \(\lambda_\pm=(1\pm\sqrt{5-4/q})/2\). The adjoint rank of a random element obeys a Gaussian limit with explicit positive variance, uniformly covering every finite-field characteristic for each fixed field size.
