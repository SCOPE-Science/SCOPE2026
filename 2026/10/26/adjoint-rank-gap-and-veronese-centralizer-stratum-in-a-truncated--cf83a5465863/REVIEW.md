# Research review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The homogeneous-degree decomposition reduces every adjoint to two explicit blocks. For nonzero linear component, the quadratic-to-cubic block has rank three and the remaining independent linear-to-quadratic component has rank one; the degree-three image of the linear kernel lies in the earlier image. For zero linear component, a quadratic commuting with a nonzero linear form is precisely a scalar square of that form, giving ranks zero, one, and two and the exact Veronese count. The counts total the full algebra size and yield the probability by the centralizer-size identity. The packaged checker exhaustively compares all linear/quadratic coefficient patterns over the prime fields of orders two and three and runs 3,000 additional direct-rank spot checks.

## Originality

**PASS, best knowledge.** The inspected Dutta–Basnet–Nath paper provides bounds and the centralizer-sum identity, not these four strata; Bremner gives free associative word-basis background, not a centralizer census. The untruncated Bergman theorem is inapplicable to the cubic-socle quotient. Directed searches for truncated-free-algebra centralizer ranks, commuting probabilities and the Veronese rank-one condition found no covering statement. The closest related finite-algebra and Lie-rank results have different multiplication structures. Priority cannot be proven by a bounded search.

## Value

**PASS.** The first noncommutative two-generator word-length-four quotient has an exact full centralizer-size distribution. The rank-three gap and the rank-one Veronese locus are structural geometric features not visible from the scalar probability alone; they offer a precise baseline for further truncated-tensor calculations. The invariant is canonical, not an arbitrary parameter slice.
