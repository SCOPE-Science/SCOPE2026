In single-agent \(\mathsf{S5}\), the \(k\)-variable fragment built from exclusive-or, constants and contingency has exactly \(2^{k+2^k}\) inequivalent formulas, all admitting unique depth-one affine-contingency normal forms. Each formula needs exactly \(k+2^k\) pointed examples to teach, and \(k+1\) worlds are the sharp cutoff for detecting every inequivalence.
