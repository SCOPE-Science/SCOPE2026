With polynomially ageing rate-refresh intervals, the first-passage survival exponent is \(\gamma=(1+\alpha\beta)/[2\alpha(1+\beta)]\), not the fixed-refresh exponent \(1/(2\alpha)\). At the critical mean exponent \(\alpha=1/(\beta+2)\), a logarithmic refresh factor makes the mean finite exactly when its power \(\delta<-2\).
