For every finite open SSH chain with one lossy site, the hopping ratios permitting exactly undamped fermionic orbitals form an explicit finite algebraic set, and their exact count determines whether the vacuum is globally attractive. At each resonance the weak-loss decay coefficient reopens quadratically under detuning with an explicit positive constant; a nonuniform 28-site example protects four orbitals and a 16-dimensional Fock sector.
