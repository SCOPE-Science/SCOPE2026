Hub preservation is the precise jammed-state rare event characterizing whether every blade blocks its spoke edges in time. The evaluated finite-size law, algebraic exponent depending on number of modules and adsorption contrast, and sharp joint crossover quantify how clustering fundamentally changes hub jamming; these are not known coverage-table substitutions or arbitrary small computations. No claim of downstream generality is made.
