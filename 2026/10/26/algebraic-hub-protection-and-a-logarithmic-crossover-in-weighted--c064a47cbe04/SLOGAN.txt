For weighted irreversible dimer adsorption on a windmill of \(r\) even-order clique blades, the probability that the common hub survives is an exact gamma-function product and decays algebraically with exponent \(rb\), where \(b\) is the spoke-to-internal deposition-rate ratio. Allowing the blade count and rate to vary reveals a sharp joint crossover at \(rb\log k\) of order one.
