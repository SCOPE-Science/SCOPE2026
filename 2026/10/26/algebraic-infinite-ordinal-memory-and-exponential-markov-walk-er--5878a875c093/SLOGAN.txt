For the Lebesgue-invariant tripling map, the probability of \(n\) successive ordinal rises is exactly \((n+2)/(2\cdot3^n)\), so its binary ordinal observations have no finite Markov order. The one-step Markov walk calibrated to those same observations has a spectral gap of \(8/9\) but overpredicts long rising runs by the exponentially unbounded factor \(3(4/3)^{n-1}/(n+2)\).
