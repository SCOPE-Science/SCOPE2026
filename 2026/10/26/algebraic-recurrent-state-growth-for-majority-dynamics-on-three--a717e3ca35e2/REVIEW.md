# Scientific review: exact recurrent majority states on three-row cylinders

## Correctness — PASS
The update operator is defined on the simple labelled cylindrical graph for all circumferences at least three; the middle-row tie case is implemented explicitly. Each fixed configuration corresponds to one cyclic walk in the 64-state column-pair graph. Each configuration satisfying two-step recurrence corresponds to exactly one cyclic walk in the 4096-state two-time graph. The finite directed-graph decomposition leaves one primitive component of size 20 or 200, respectively, with the stated singleton and two-cycle contributions. Integer Newton identities reconstruct both complete characteristic determinants, and exact coordinatewise inequalities certify disjoint Perron intervals. The period-at-most-two argument is proved by a symmetric, strictly decreasing two-time energy, not inferred from experiments. The standalone program verifies all finite computational steps and separately enumerates all states for circumferences three through six.

## Originality — PASS with acknowledged residual risk
The general symmetric-threshold period-two theorem and the generic finite transfer-matrix technique are existing results and are explicitly excluded from the contribution. The new quantitative result concerns the first three-row cylindrical strip with both odd-degree boundary sites and even-degree tie-retaining interior sites: its 20/200-component census and algebraic limiting fixed-versus-two-cycle rates. A prior circular-prism calculation applies to a two-row graph with uniformly odd degree and cannot yield the present mixed-degree transition constants. The inspected full text on one-dimensional majority automata with thermal noise studies a distinct operator and stationary distribution; its 2013 paper does not supply the present deterministic recurrent-state invariant. The 1988 generic cylindrical-automata paper was identified but not fully accessible; its available description identifies general transfer methodology and two-neighbour case studies, not these explicit constants. A specialized or unindexed earlier calculation remains possible, and database searches do not establish uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is an exact natural two-time recurrence census over an infinite family, rather than a one-off small graph computation. The first mixed-parity-degree cylindrical strip gives an algebraic Perron constant of degree thirteen and an independently different fixed-point growth constant, proving that asymptotically almost every recurrent state belongs to a two-cycle. The precise constants and counts are relevant for comparing strip widths, distinguishing mixed-degree tie dynamics from uniformly odd-degree networks, and validating more general transfer or statistical studies. The generic possibility of a transfer-matrix computation does not give these specific algebraic invariants without substantial exact finite elimination.

## Scope, closest literature, and limitations
The full recurrence formula, dominant-root identification, and exponential separation are supported; there is no assertion about arbitrary strip width, transient depth, or attraction basins. Exact matrix computations are furnished in a standalone verifier; the mathematical extension to all circumferences comes from closed-walk bijections. Closest literature: Goles--Olivos (1980) on period bounds, Jen (1988) on generic cylindrical transfer matrices, Agur--Fraenkel--Klein (1988) on cyclic one-dimensional fixed points, and Lemoy--Mozeika--Seki (2013) on noisy one-dimensional self-inclusive majority. Jen full-text access remained unavailable; this is a stated originality risk, not treated as proof of novelty.
