# Mathematical review

**Correctness — PASS.** P1–P4 reduce the operation to constant and identity endpoint rows and finite isotone meet-factor rows. P5 is proven equivalent to the self-absorption, bottom-value and sole nontrivial comparable-interior-pair inequalities. Explicit complete lists of the 7, 6 and 9 surviving middle rows and an independently generated 5,250-table direct-axiom checker give exactly 276 operations, 96 unary-induced, 180 non-induced and 59 satisfying modus ponens. No infinity claim is inferred from experiments.

**Originality — PASS, with priority risk.** Chen (arXiv:2607.20221, algebraic sections 3.1–3.2) supplies K semantics, not the unrestricted N5 binary-operation count. Holliday (arXiv:2402.02296, sections 2.1–2.3) supplies axioms and the precomplementation formula, explicitly credited; the latter is not claimed new. Existing narrow stronger-class and other-lattice enumerations do not imply the unrestricted 276-class or its 180 non-induced stratum. Searches for N5/pentagon preconditional censuses and equivalent forms did not identify coverage. An older differently named enumeration remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** This is the full, naturally motivated K-algebra classification on the minimal nondistributive nonmodular pentagon, with a constructive normal form and an exact comparison between general binary conditionals and the classical precomplementation-derived subfamily. It also determines the exact modus-ponens layer, giving complete finite benchmark structures without asserting larger general theorems.

**Limitations and closest literature.** All five operations axioms are as originally published. The 96 induced operations use a *previously known* construction. The strong F pentagon layer is not asserted as novel and is excluded from the new claim. Further priority work could identify an older abstract algebraic classification that subsumes this finite instance. Independent validation is not claimed.
