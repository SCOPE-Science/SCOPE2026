## originality

PASS

Best-of-knowledge novelty belongs only to the ramified characteristic-five specialization: an exhaustive geometric 65-point reduced F5-rational Jacobian scheme, not to Barth’s characteristic-zero equation or classical 65-node count. The closest complete mathematical exposition (Bauer–Hulek–Rams–Sarti–Szemberg, 2017, §2.4) proves/describes 65 nodes over C using icosahedral invariant polynomials; Jaffe (1994) explicitly works over C; the Orbiter 2025 manual (§7.6, §17.2) computes 65 singular points over F11 with phi=8, not F5. Complex nodes and an F11 model do not by themselves imply absence of collision or degeneration at the ramified prime over 5. Direct comparison of mathematical statements, including the square-map/Cayley-cubic alias and an exact finite-field table check, found no coverage of the F5 reduced Jacobian scheme. Original Barth 1996 full text was unavailable despite public searches and a failed authorized Oxford request, so explicit earlier coordinates or equivalent modular deductions remain material priority risks; search failure is not a novelty certificate.
