The twenty-vertex dodecahedron is a natural highly symmetric five-design and exact spherical sampling grid. The theorem classifies **every exact-degree sampling rank**, rather than only the dimension-theoretic lower-degree constraints, and supplies the entire positive singular-value spectrum and sharp persistent interpolation threshold. Its degree-\(5\) rank reversal and the exceptional degree-\(14\) defect are meaningful boundary phenomena, and the all-degree proof excludes unexpected higher-degree spectral gaps. The result has potential use in symmetry-adapted interpolation and covariance regularity; no practical optimality claim is made.
