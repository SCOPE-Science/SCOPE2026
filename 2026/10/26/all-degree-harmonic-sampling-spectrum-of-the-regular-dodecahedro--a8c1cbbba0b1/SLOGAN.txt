For the twenty vertices of a regular dodecahedron, the complete exact-degree Legendre sampling spectrum has six rational eigenvalue branches and rank ten on the antipodally compatible sector at every degree except \(0,1,2,3,4,5,8,14\). In particular, every parity-compatible dataset is interpolable by harmonics of each single degree \(\ell\ge15\), with an exact rational finite check plus an analytic infinite-degree bound proving that no further exceptions occur.
