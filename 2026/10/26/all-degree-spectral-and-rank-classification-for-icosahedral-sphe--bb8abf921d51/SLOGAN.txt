For the twelve vertices of the regular icosahedron, exact-degree spherical-harmonic sampling has a complete two-level singular spectrum determined by \(P_\ell(1/\sqrt5)\). Its rank is \(1\) at degree zero, \(3\) at degrees \(1,3\), \(5\) at degrees \(2,4,8,14\), and \(6\) at every other degree: arbitrary antipodally compatible sample data are therefore interpolable at every degree \(\ell\ge15\), with asymptotically equal nonzero singular values.
