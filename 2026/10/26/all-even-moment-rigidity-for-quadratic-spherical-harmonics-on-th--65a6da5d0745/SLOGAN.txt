For every integer \(m\ge3\), the normalized \(2m\)-th moment of a real quadratic spherical harmonic on \(S^2\) is an explicit strictly increasing polynomial in its squared cubic anisotropy \(6(\operatorname{tr}A^3)^2/(\operatorname{tr}A^2)^3\), with sharp uniaxial upper and biaxial lower extremes. The sharp upper constant and its zonal equality class remain valid for all complex quadratic spherical harmonics; the sixth moment first separates shapes, with exact deficit \(1000(1-\delta)/1001\).
