Every finite stalling length in the compact-alphabet substitution \(j\mapsto j+1\) below a threshold and \(j\mapsto 0\,(j-1)\,(j+1)\) above it yields a strictly ergodic system with an explicit Perron root \(\lambda_m>2\). The spectral gap is asymptotic to \(4^{1-m}\), disproving the published prediction of non-unique ergodicity at seven stalls despite the misleading first thousand iterates.
