For every \(n\ge5\), the inclusion-minimal dominating sets of the Kneser graph \(KG(n,2)\) are exactly triangles, three-edge matchings, full stars, five-cycles and balanced five-edge double stars, interpreted as edge families of \(K_n\). This five-type classification yields an exact cardinality enumerator and the upper domination number \(\max\{5,n-1\}\), beyond the previously known minimum domination number.
