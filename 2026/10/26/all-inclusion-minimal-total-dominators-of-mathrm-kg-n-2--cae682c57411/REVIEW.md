The four-set permutation argument proves an absolute six-edge bound, and the bounded exhaustive isomorphism-class augmentation establishes that precisely \(3K_2\), \(K_3\sqcup K_2\), \(C_5\), and \(K_4\) meet that condition. All four can be checked directly. Label-support counts give the displayed polynomial and sharp upper total domination value. The bundled scripts run successfully and the independent direct Kneser checker reproduces the count distribution for \(4\le n\le8\). The proof for arbitrary \(n\) is not inferred from this bounded direct test.
