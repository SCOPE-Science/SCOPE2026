Every inclusion-minimal total dominating set of \(\mathrm{KG}(n,2)\), for \(n\ge4\), is the edge set of \(3K_2\), \(K_3\sqcup K_2\), \(C_5\), or \(K_4\), on any admissible labeled support. Their exact size polynomial is \(15\binom n6 z^3+10\binom n5 z^4+12\binom n5 z^5+\binom n4 z^6\), and the upper total domination number equals \(6\).
