For \(0^a1^b0^c\), every smallest string attractor is classified explicitly, and their length generating function is \(2z^3(1+2z+2z^2)/((1-z)^3(1+z)^2)\). Almost every three-run binary word has just one smallest attractor, yet their mean number tends to \(5/2\) because the rare words with a singleton middle run contribute disproportionately.
