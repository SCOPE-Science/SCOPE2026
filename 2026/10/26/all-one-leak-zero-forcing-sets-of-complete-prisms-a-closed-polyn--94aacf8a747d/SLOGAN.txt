For every complete prism \(K_n\square K_2\) with \(n\ge3\), the one-leak zero-forcing sets admit a closed all-cardinality polynomial and a complete inclusion-minimal classification. Although one-leak resilience costs no extra vertices at the optimum \(Z_{(1)}=n\), only the two entire clique layers are robust minima, while \(2n\binom{n-1}{2}\) additional inclusion-minimal configurations of size \(n+1\) occur for \(n\ge4\).
