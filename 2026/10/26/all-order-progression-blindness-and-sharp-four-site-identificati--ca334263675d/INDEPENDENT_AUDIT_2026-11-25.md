## correctness

PASS

For any finite offsets the product on Z_2 is locally constant off finitely many translated zeros. Omitting cylinders around those zeros gives uniform density control for integer shift averages and proves agreement with Haar measure for arbitrary instructions. In the exact partition mod 2^K, a cylinder with an unresolved valuation at least K-1 has at most one such factor when 2^(K-1) exceeds the diameter. All other factors are fixed there, and that factor integrates to zero by valuation and odd-unit sign symmetry. For an odd-length progression reflection x -> -x-(m-1)h flips the entire product. With odd h and m=2L, parity yields an L-site progression for shifted instructions and an odd-site product constant for even L; for odd L it inserts the twist (-1)^y. The shorter odd-length reversal preserves this twist and negates the product. Iterating the even-L reduction settles every odd spacing. If h=2^a u with a>=1 and m even, the classes v_2(x)<=a-2, v_2(x)=a-1, v_2(x)>=a have contributions 1-2^(1-a), (-1)^(m/2)2^(-a), 0 respectively. For (0,1,2,8) only the x-even, x/2-even branch contributes: writing x=4v, the odd factors at x+1 and (x+2)/2 give epsilon_0 epsilon_1 (-1)^v and the remaining shifted pair is at v,v+2. Odd v gives +epsilon_0 epsilon_1 and has total mass 1/8; even v leaves the vanishing adjacent two-point mean. The other parity branches vanish by the one-factor mean or one-factor twisted reflection. For scaled offsets the lower valuation classes contribute 1-2^(1-k) and -2^(-k); the residual contributes 2^(-k) times the base witness with shifted instructions. This recovers adjacent products and exactly the common-sign ambiguity, not individual signs. The exact dyadic replay exhausts all 35 normalized four-site shapes of diameter 3–7 and confirms sign-blindness, the diameter-eight polynomial and the scaled identities. Independently, the sharp cutoff also follows by parity: for a 2+2 split each odd pair has a fixed product and the remaining pair has the sign-blind two-point function. If all offsets have the same parity, halving reduces the only even branch to four consecutive sites. For a 3+1 split the one-even branch has zero twisted one-point mean; the three-even branch reduces to a twisted triple of diameter <=3. Up to translation and reflection the triples are (0,1,2) and (0,1,3). The first is killed by reversal preserving the twist; parity in the second leaves a zero one-point mean in one half and a twisted adjacent pair, killed by reversal flipping the twist, in the other half. Thus diameter eight is attained and sharp.
