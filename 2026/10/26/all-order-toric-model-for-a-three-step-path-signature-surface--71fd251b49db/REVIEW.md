# Review

- **Correctness — PASS.** Multiplication of the three exponential signatures gives the coefficient formula. The invertible substitution \(u=a+c\), \(v=a\) makes each fixed-\(j\) block span all monomials \(u^{k-j-q}v^q w^j\). These are exactly the lattice points of \(P_k\); the normalized area is \(k(k-1)\), and the primitive cones give the two quotient types stated in the result.
- **Originality — PASS, with residual literature risk.** The primary paper proves toricness for all shapes only at orders two and three, gives the \((1,2,1)\) order-three example, and explicitly asks whether all axis-path varieties are toric. The all-order polygon and singularity formula for this fixed shape are not stated there. published-finding corpus searches for the shape, order four, the all-order monomialization, and the polygon found no matching claim; these searches are not exhaustive.
- **Value — PASS.** The result resolves the all-order geometry of a natural three-step path family, turning a computed order-three example into a uniform description of the degree, projective normality, and singularity types. These invariants are relevant to equations and degeneracies of path-signature varieties.

Closest source: Colmenarejo–Galuppi–Michałek, *Toric geometry of path signature varieties*, arXiv:1903.03779, §3.1, Example 3.7 and Proposition 3.9. Limitations: no general conclusion for other shapes; targeted searches do not establish global novelty.
