# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
For the exponential comparison diffusion, joint moments factor exactly as a product of edge factors \(1+w_{ab}\). Möbius inversion isolates connected graphs, and their minimal edge count produces the weighted spanning-tree coefficient. Coupling the exponential solution with a compactly cut off coefficient yields moment differences exponentially small in the inverse squared noise amplitude; therefore the Taylor coefficients coincide with those of a diffusion satisfying the original smooth bounded-coefficient hypotheses. Positive tree weights prove nonvanishing at all ranks. Independent exact rational checks of 107 instances verify the combinatorial coefficient calculation, but the infinite statement rests on the analytic proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The motivating work arXiv:2610.21986v1 already proves \(k<2r-2\) vanishing and provides low-rank equations. The present result does not reassert that theorem or claim novelty for cumulant–graph inversion. Its separate assertion is the **simultaneous sharpness of the boundary for every tensor component at every rank within globally bounded smooth diffusion coefficients**, with the exact weighted-tree coefficients and a cutoff transfer. The earlier arXiv:2609.18229v1 discusses related finite expansions without this construction. Searches for equivalent graph and rank formulations, including published mathematical research records, identified no directly matching claim. The principal residual risk is that the specific embedding is an unremarked corollary of classical lognormal cumulant diagram results, or that a more general older diffusion result subsumes it.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The recent finite-cumulant-tower bound is intended to delimit high-rank terms that a higher-order filtering scheme may discard. Showing that every top-rank cumulant coefficient can be genuinely nonzero, simultaneously and with bounded smooth coefficients, is a motivated structural boundary for universal truncation rules. The all-rank connected-tree expression quantifies exactly how this obstruction scales across ranks and coordinates. This is not a lower bound on minimal ODE realization dimension, and the example does not address informative observations.

## Closest literature and limits
Kurisaki, arXiv:2610.21986v1, Theorem 4.6 supplies the vanishing half of the sharpness comparison. The connected graph formula belongs to standard Gaussian cumulant combinatorics, and only the bounded-diffusion all-rank boundary application is proposed as a new result. Sources were checked to the extent described in the structured review. Broader unindexed diagram literature remains a residual originality risk.
