# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Every two-dimensional coefficient subspace has a unique RREF basis, giving an exact finite exhaustion of all \(20\,306\) pencils. The invariant-based discriminant and independent polynomial-gcd tests both count \(3\,820\) pencils with six squarefree quartics. Exhaustive group images yield the \(47\) orbits, and two implementations agree on base-divisor degrees and all \(14\) Frobenius fibre-profile frequencies. The code and complete representative catalogue are embedded.

**Originality — PASS with priority caveat.** The full primary text of Wall's 1998 binary-quartic-pencil study gives geometric ramification strata and invariants, not the exact \(\mathbf F_5\) rational smooth-pencil frequencies, \(47\)-orbit partition, or six-fibre splitting table. The Asgarli–Ghioca 2022 smooth-pencil existence paper and the published 2022 finite-field *cubic*-pencil orbit study do not imply this arithmetic quartic census. Search and comparison of nearby published research records and accessible prior claims did not identify equivalent coverage. Unindexed computations remain a residual uncertainty; the negative search alone is not used as novelty proof.

**Value — PASS.** The exact orbit-and-fibre classification enriches a natural small-field smoothness problem in a geometric family: degree-four rational covers of the projective line with all rational target fibres reduced. It distinguishes \(47\) source-equivalence types, separates \(3\,710\) actual degree-four morphisms from pencils with non-rational base divisors, and classifies the arithmetic Frobenius factors of six genus-one branch divisors simultaneously. These are not merely individual squarefree-form counts.

**Limitations.** The complete classification is only over \(\mathbf F_5\), and it is not a classification of elliptic curves. The same methods could be applied to other fields, but no extension is asserted here. External independent evaluation has not been performed.
