Over \(\mathbf F_5\), exactly \(3\,820\) pencils of binary quartics have all six rational members geometrically squarefree; they form \(47\) orbits under source \(\operatorname{PGL}_2\). Exactly \(3\,710\) such pencils are basepoint-free, and the complete six-fibre Frobenius splitting profiles have \(14\) types.
