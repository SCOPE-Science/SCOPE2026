Changing the arrival rate of one bond in irreversible one-dimensional dimer adsorption produces a strictly alternating change in the jammed occupation probabilities of successively farther bonds. At fixed anomalous rate \(\lambda\ne1\), the distant response has the sharp factorial form \(p_j-p_*\sim(-1)^j(\lambda-1)e^{-1}/(j+1)!\), exhibiting exact screening of a localized kinetic defect.
