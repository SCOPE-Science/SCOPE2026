The least Gaussian-kernel norm of a function interpolating constant data on \(N\) consecutive lattice sites has an exact theta-function bulk term and a finite edge constant, followed by a universal-in-form, nonzero alternating \((-p)^N\) correction for every fixed \(0<p<1\). The signed coefficient is exactly \(-2p/((1+p)^2\Theta(p))\), with remainder \(O_p(p^{2N})\).
