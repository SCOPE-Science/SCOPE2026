The canonical inclusion \(\ell_1\hookrightarrow\ell_{(p_n)}\) has essential norm, limiting approximation number, and Hausdorff noncompactness radius all equal to \((1+e^{-s_*})^{-1}\), where \(s_*\) is the convergence abscissa of \(\sum_n e^{-tp_n}\). For nondecreasing exponents, \(s_*=\limsup_n (\log n)/p_n\), giving a sharp continuum of noncompactness constants between \(1/2\) and \(1\).
