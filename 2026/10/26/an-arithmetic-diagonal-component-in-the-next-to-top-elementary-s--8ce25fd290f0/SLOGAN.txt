For next-to-top derivatives of elementary symmetric forms in positive characteristic, the reduced zero variety is either a union of \(n\) two-dimensional coordinate-exception planes or a union of \(n\) coordinate axes with one arithmetically triggered diagonal line. The exceptional diagonal survives without increasing dimension, giving one additional projective component exactly when \(p\) is odd and \(p\mid n-d+2\), or \(p=2\) and \(4\mid n-d+2\).
