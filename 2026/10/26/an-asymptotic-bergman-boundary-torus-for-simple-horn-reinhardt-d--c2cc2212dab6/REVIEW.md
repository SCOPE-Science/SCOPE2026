## Correctness — PASS
The primary paper's horn definition guarantees a complex ray at each phase, and Theorem 3.1 gives a complete monomial basis with all degrees up to \(m\). Monomial orthogonality and the standard Fubini--Study pullback give an exact finite formula for the Bergman metric. The radial speed is the standard deviation of monomial total degree divided by the radial parameter. The adjacent top two positive degree layers determine the leading variance \((A_{m-1}/A_m)t^{-2}\), so integration rigorously gives the stated \(t^{-1}\) coefficient and the \(O(t^{-3})\) remainder. Angular covariance proves that only the diagonal phase direction collapses. Projective distance distinguishes all remaining phases, and uniform tails give the required continuous embedding into the metric completion. The proof does not infer an infinite theorem from a finite experiment.
