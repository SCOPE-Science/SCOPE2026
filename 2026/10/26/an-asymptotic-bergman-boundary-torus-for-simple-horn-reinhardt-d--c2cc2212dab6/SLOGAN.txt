For every simple horn Reinhardt domain with a nondegenerate finite-dimensional Bergman metric, infinity contributes an embedded \((n-1)\)-torus to the metric completion, with two escaping radial phase rays merging exactly under common phase rotation. Their remaining length is \(\sqrt{A_{m-1}/A_m}/t+O(t^{-3})\), and the fixed-radius phase metrics converge to an explicit positive flat covariance form transverse to diagonal rotation.
