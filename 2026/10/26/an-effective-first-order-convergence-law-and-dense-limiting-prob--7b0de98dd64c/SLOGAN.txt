Two independently sampled uniform involutions, regarded as a two-function finite structure, obey an effective first-order convergence law with **exactly** the limiting probabilities of finite Boolean events in independent Poisson counts of alternating path and cycle components. Every such probability belongs to \(\mathbb Q[e^{-1/D}]\) for an effectively determined integer \(D>0\), and the full first-order spectrum is dense in \([0,1]\).
