PASS

The distinct supported claim is the exact fixed-coordinate count of all-smooth projective planes in the quartic parameter space. Prior work gives existence and the maximal system dimension for these very parameters, and nearby records tabulate individual quartics, pencils, or a restricted plane-filling family. None of those inspected results gives or implies the all-smooth plane count. Neither existence, the dimension bound, the smooth-form and pencil counts, nor the elementary rational-jet consequence is credited as a new census result.

The census supplies the number of maximal-dimensional all-smooth families for quartics in the smallest field, rather than another existence example or a count of individual equations. It is a concrete incidence invariant of the smooth quartic locus and provides the exact fixed-coordinate total needed to benchmark a future orbit classification or compare line-to-plane extensions.
