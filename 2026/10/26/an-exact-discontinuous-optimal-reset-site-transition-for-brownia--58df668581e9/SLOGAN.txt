The optimal fixed reset site for a Brownian searcher covering both reflecting ends of an interval jumps exactly from either boundary to the midpoint at \(rL^2/(4D)=\operatorname{arcosh}^2((1+\sqrt{17})/4)\). A distinct lower threshold marks when the midpoint first becomes a local, but not yet global, optimum; neither result requires claiming the known asymmetric mean-cover-time formula as new.
