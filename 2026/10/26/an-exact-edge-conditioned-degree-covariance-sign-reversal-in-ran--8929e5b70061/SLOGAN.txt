In a uniform random chord diagram, degrees of two *noncrossing* chords are positively correlated for \(3\le n\le5\), but negatively correlated for every \(n\ge6\); degrees of two *crossing* chords remain positively correlated for all \(n\ge3\). The exact conditional covariances are \((n-2)(16-3n)/700\) for a noncrossing pair and \((n-2)(n+18)/525\) for a crossing pair.
