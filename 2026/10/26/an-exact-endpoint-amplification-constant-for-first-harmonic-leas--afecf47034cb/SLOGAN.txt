The continuous \(L^2\)-least-squares projector onto affine coordinate functions on a semicircle has exact sharp \(L^1\) and \(L^\infty\) norms \(2.0480992714794347668\ldots>2\), attained by endpoint kernel signs. A continuum proof locates all maximizing evaluations at the arc endpoints, quantitatively exposing half-range instability absent from the full-circle first-harmonic projection.
