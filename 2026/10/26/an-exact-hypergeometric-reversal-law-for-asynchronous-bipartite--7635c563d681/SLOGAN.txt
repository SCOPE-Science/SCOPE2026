For asynchronously updated binary majority dynamics on odd-by-odd complete bipartite networks, the probability that final unanimity reverses the initial strict global majority has an exact hypergeometric formula. When both communities grow with finite size ratio \(\rho\ge1\), this probability converges to \(\pi^{-1}\arcsin((\rho-1)/(\rho+1))\), zero at balance and \(1/6\) at ratio three.
