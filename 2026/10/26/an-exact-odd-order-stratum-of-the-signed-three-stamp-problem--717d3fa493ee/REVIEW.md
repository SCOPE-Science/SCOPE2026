# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Theorem 4.1 of van Doorn–Monticone–Nathanson v1 supplies the strict signed-versus-ordinary bound at every odd h≥3; their Theorem 1.1 gives ordinary range 9t²−2 at h=6t−3. Integrality bounds the signed optimum by 9t²−3. For the proposed basis, z copies of 2t+1 yield the full step-2t progression from −ht+(3t+1)z to ht+(t+1)z. Even t makes t+1 invertible modulo 2t, and the initial progressions start at or before their least nonnegative representatives. The gap between successive blocks in a residue is 2t(z+3t+1−h), which joins the first two in every residue and a third for z₀≤t−3. The latter reach past 9t²−3; for z₀≥t−2 the first two reach at least 9t²−2t−2, so the next point in that residue cannot come before 9t²−2. At z₀=t−2 the third block starts at 9t²+2t−2, proving the claimed first gap. The proof's parenthetical suggesting this third block might not be admissible is unnecessary and false (z₀+4t=5t−2≤h throughout); it does not affect the argument. Independent exact-multiplicity enumeration reproduced the first gaps 34, 142, 322 and 1294 at t=2,4,6,12. Frozen checks are finite support only.

PASS

The sharp equality on h≡9 mod 12 uses a known global obstruction and a matching uniform construction. The prior same-problem paper gives the obstruction but leaves the odd exact values open; the other inspected exact signed-range result is at h=2. No inspected exact match or earlier stronger covering statement was identified.

## value

PASS

The exact signed range was an open part of a natural extremal problem. The construction closes the gap to the strict prior upper bound at infinitely many odd orders, with a simple uniform family and an explicit first missing integer. This gives an exact answer on a non-arbitrary stratum selected by parity and residue coverage, without claiming the other odd orders.
