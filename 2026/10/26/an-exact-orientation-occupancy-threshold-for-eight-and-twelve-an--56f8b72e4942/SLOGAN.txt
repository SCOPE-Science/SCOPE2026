The four principal orientations occur with limiting frequency \((17+\sqrt{17})/136\) each in the eight-angle manufacturing language, but with frequency \(1/12\) each in the twelve-angle language. Consequently a 10-percent per-principal-orientation quota rejects an exponentially vanishing fraction of otherwise valid eight-angle symmetric half-stacks, yet retains at most an exponentially vanishing fraction of valid twelve-angle half-stacks.
