For \(0<\beta<1\), the degenerate reaction–diffusion–advection model has an exact sharp front on the curve \(k=3\beta\lambda_\beta\), where \(\lambda_\beta=(1+\sqrt{9-8\beta})/[4(1-\beta)]\). The result is a complete classification within the exponential-front ansatz, not a classification of all traveling waves.
