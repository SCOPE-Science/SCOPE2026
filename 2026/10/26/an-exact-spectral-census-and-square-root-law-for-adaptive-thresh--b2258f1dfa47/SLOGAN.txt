For adaptive evolving-threshold dynamics on every labelled complete bipartite graph \(K_{a,b}\), the exact number of extended-state equilibria is a finite spectral sum with factors \((a)_{\underline{k}}(b)_{\underline{k}}/((a+2)(b+2))^k\). As both parts grow, the normalized count has the sharp square-root law \(F(a,b)/[(b+2)^a(a+2)^b]\sim\sqrt{\pi ab/[2(a+b)]}\).
