Alternating preferences in a two-source network urn model produce a surprising finite-size fluctuation classification: the two-site and six-site cycles have Gaussian \(\sqrt t\) fluctuations, while the four-site cycle and every even cycle of size at least eight have a provably nonzero, slower random spectral amplitude. The exceptional six-cycle follows from the exact noncommuting signed-kernel identity \(4B^2=P^4-P^2\), not from an assumed single-neighbour reduction.
