The cyclic action is free, making the quotient étale. The discriminant determines a smooth genus-two curve, and the identity \(z^3-z=W/(u+v)^3\) gives a connected Artin–Schreier torsor. The verifier independently reproduces the critical identities and the Cartier–Manin calculation. Connectedness and the quotient theorem are mathematical arguments, not inferred from finite experiments.

The narrower result here is the coefficientwise characteristic-three elementary-symmetric model and explicit, nontrivial, everywhere-étale Artin–Schreier class. The present computation is model-specific; it does not classify every special-fibre automorphism and does not supply an everywhere-regular affine Artin–Schreier equation.
