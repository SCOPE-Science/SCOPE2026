The elementary-symmetric Bring complete intersection has a smooth characteristic-three fibre whose free three-cycle gives an explicit unramified Artin–Schreier torsor \(z^3-z=W/(u+v)^3\) over the genus-two curve \(W^2=-u^6+u^4v^2+u^3v^3+u^2v^4-v^6\). This identifies the wild special-fibre torsor rather than re-claiming the already-known genus-two quotient over characteristic zero.
