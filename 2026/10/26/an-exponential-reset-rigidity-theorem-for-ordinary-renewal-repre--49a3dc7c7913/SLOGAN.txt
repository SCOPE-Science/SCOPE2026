For a Poisson nearest-neighbor lattice walk under arbitrary renewal resetting, an ordinary independent-and-identically-waiting no-reset CTRW can reproduce **every one-time position law** if and only if resetting is exponential (including zero-rate no-resetting). At positive reset rate the unique surrogate must have a defective waiting law and eventually freezes, even while the true reset walker has nonzero long-run squared increments.
