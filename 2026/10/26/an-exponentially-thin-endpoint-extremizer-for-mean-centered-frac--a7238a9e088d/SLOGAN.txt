For the spatially mean-centered Riemann–Liouville integral of every order \(\frac12\le\alpha<1\), the exact sharp uniform norm is attained at a unique interior point determined by \(x^{1-\alpha}(1-x)^\alpha=\alpha/2\), with a sign-step input. As \(\alpha\uparrow1\), this point collapses to the initial endpoint at the exponentially thin scale \(e^{-1}2^{-1/(1-\alpha)}\), and the corresponding norm excess has an exact beyond-all-orders coefficient; the Abel half-order norm equals \((3\sqrt6+\sqrt2-4)/(3\sqrt\pi)\).
