For every integer \(r\ge4\), the ternary uniform substitution \(a\mapsto a^r(a+1)\) produces a binary automatic sequence whose abelian complexity is not regular in base \(r+1\). Its complexity along lengths \((r+1)^m\), after scaling by \((r^2-r+1)^{m/2}\), has an exact, nondegenerate interval of accumulation values; infinitely many distinct examples are already \(2\)-automatic.
