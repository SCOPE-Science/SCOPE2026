The initial exponents whose dyadic arithmetic-partial-derivative valuation trajectories have least eventual period two possess an explicit, irrational natural density \(\alpha=\sum_{r\in A}2^{-r}=0.297058823704034\ldots\), with logarithmic counting discrepancy. The period-two class is governed by a self-similar binary set \(A\), and its provably nonperiodic indicator is the binary expansion of \(\alpha\).
