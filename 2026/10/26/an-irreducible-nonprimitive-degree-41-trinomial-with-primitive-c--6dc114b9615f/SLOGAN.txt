Over \(\mathbb F_{41^2}\), the irreducible polynomial \(X^{41}+X+(2+16u)\) with \(u^2=3\) has a primitive constant term but nonprimitive roots: their orders divide \((41^{82}-1)/83\). This explicit example refutes Sharma’s 2026 Conjecture 4.2, which predicts primitivity for every primitive constant term.
