For Brownian diffusion with a Poisson-refreshed exponential reset intensity, a positive but exponentially faint isolated spectral mode controls the strict stationary far tail. Its exact decay rate is \(\mu-s_*\), with \(\mu\mathbb E[(s_*+\lambda)^{-1}]=1\), and differs by an unbounded factor from the rate inferred from mean-square displacement as refreshes become rare.
