Every nonzero characteristic-three member of the symmetric plane-sextic pencil \(X^6+Y^6+Z^6+\lambda X^2Y^2Z^2=0\) has six cusps and an ordinary genus-four normalization. Its Jacobian is isogenous to \(E_\lambda\times F_\lambda^3\) for two explicit ordinary elliptic curves, giving a uniform zeta-function factorization and two pointless genus-four curves over \(\mathbb F_3\).
