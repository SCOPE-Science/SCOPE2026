# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

With λ=α+2, the probability-normalized Bergman kernel is (1−z·conj(w))^(−λ). In the weighted R R* kernel the Jacobian factors of x=tanh(u) turn each endpoint factor into sech^λ; 1−tanh(u)tanh(v)=cosh(u−v)/(cosh(u)cosh(v)) leaves exactly sech^λ(u−v). This also proves boundedness without assuming it: on compactly supported chord tests the kernel computes the squared norm of the adjoint, bounded by the L¹ convolution norm. The range of R is dense since orthogonality to weighted polynomials implies a function times the bounded, positive-a.e. weight is orthogonal to all polynomials; R is injective by analyticity. Thus the one-diameter Gram operator is unitarily equivalent to the positive beta-integral multiplier. Its essential range is [0,Cα] and every subspace above a strict lower cutoff below Cα is infinite-dimensional. When θ∈(0,π), the cross-kernel denominator is bounded away from zero and the endpoint factors give a Hilbert–Schmidt operator. The positive Calkin images multiply to zero, so the spectrum of their sum is the union of the bands. The unit constant test gives Dα=2∫sech^(2λ)(u) du, strictly between Cα and 2Cα by the stated pointwise cosh comparison. Hence the spectral top is isolated with finite multiplicity. At coincidence the sum is 2Q; strong rotation continuity is uniform on each fixed finite-dimensional test space, so min–max gives arbitrarily many eigenvalues above each fixed μ in the claimed gap for all sufficiently small θ. For each fixed distinct angle there is no accumulation on the compact interval [μ,2Cα] outside the essential spectrum. The triangle bound and strong limit give the norm limit. The beta/gamma checker is only a diagnostic and was read, not executed. The verification sentence calling integrability exact in α>−1 should be read within the Bergman regime: integrability of that squared weight alone in fact needs only α>−2. This does not alter the proof or any claimed range.
