For the independent-coefficient inclusion-induced bootstrap walk, annealing turns fixed-environment path-space singularity into equivalence with an independent-walk reference (after the shared first step). The smallest expected number of increment edits in a coupling fixing the original walk is exactly \(\frac{|1-2p|}{2}+\sum_{k\ge1}|1-2p|^{2^k}\), finite precisely for interior Bernoulli parameters.
