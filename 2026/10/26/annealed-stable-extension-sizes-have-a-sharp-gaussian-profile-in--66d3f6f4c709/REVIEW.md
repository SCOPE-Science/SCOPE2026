## correctness

PASS

Under attacks f(v)→v, conflict-freeness on S and domination of its complement are exactly the two crossing conditions for f. For a fixed k-set these give (n−k)^k k^(n−k) independent choices, and both endpoint sizes are impossible. The gamma-binomial interpolant has concave log and the remaining logarithm has second derivative −1/t−1/(n−t)−n/t²−n/(n−t)²<0, giving the strict interior inequalities and central modes (including the vacuous short cases). Stirling gives exponent n(1−2p)log(p/(1−p)), which is −8x²+O(n⁻¹) for bounded x=(k−n/2)/√n. Globally the exponent is ≤−8x²; in the central p-band the prefactor is O(n⁻¹/²), and beyond p∈[1/4,3/4] the entropy bound gives e^(−n/2). Riemann sums, also weighted by x², give total weight 1/2, the normalized N(0,1/16) law for x, and variance asymptotic n/16. The normalizing sum is an expectation, not the probability of solvability. The frozen checker is bounded corroboration and was inspected, not executed; the audit uses the analytic bounds.
