Inspected argumentation-diversity work treats semantics and extension distances rather than this cardinality-weighted random law. An equivalent enumeration could appear in older studies of two-colorings of functional digraphs; this remains a residual priority risk.

The result also gives exact finite benchmark counts, rather than another existence criterion or a routine cycle-count refinement.

No unconditional uniqueness or independent priority certification is claimed. The model has exactly one incoming attack at each vertex and permits self-attacks.
