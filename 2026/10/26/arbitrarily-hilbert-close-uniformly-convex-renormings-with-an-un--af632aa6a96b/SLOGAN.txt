There are uniformly convex renormings of real Hilbert space arbitrarily close to the Hilbert norm for which the **same unbounded closed convex set** has a fixed point under every nonexpansive selfmap. Their moduli of convexity admit the explicit quadratic lower bound \(\delta(\varepsilon)\ge\varepsilon^2/[8(1+2\beta)]\), while the fixed-point phenomenon disappears at the exact Hilbert norm.
