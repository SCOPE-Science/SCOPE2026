For finite one-attacker argumentation frameworks, ordered stable-extension disagreement is governed exactly by \(2^c\prod_i(1+y^{s_i})\), where \(s_i\) are the sizes of even-cycle components. Uniformly counting a random mapping together with an ordered pair of its stable extensions gives the exact series \((1-T(z)^2)^{-1}(1-T(zy)^2)^{-1}\) and an arcsine \(\mathrm{Beta}(1/2,1/2)\) limit for the fraction of disagreeing vertices.
