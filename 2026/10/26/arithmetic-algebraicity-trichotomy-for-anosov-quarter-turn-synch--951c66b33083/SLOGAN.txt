For an Anosov toral automorphism paired with the quarter-turn, the two integers \(\operatorname{tr}(A)\) and \(A_{12}-A_{21}\) determine every synchronization count. The synchronization zeta is algebraic but nonrational exactly when \(A\) is symmetric, transcendental for every other tame pair, and undefined precisely at the singular skew parameter \(A_{12}-A_{21}=-2\); symmetry is also exactly where the root-growth limit fails.
