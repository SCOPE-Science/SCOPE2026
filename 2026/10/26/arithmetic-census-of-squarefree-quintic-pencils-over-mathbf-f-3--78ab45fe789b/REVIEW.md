The statement concerns exactly the \(11\,011\) rational two-spaces of a six-space. The homogeneous root-at-infinity check and affine gcd compute geometric squarefreeness, not merely rational-point separability. The pair enumeration gives \(1\,098\) unique pencils and the independent RREF enumeration returns identical identifiers. The full order-\(24\) projective source group acts on the verified catalogue, with \(54\) complete orbits; the gcd, factorization, and graph edge data are checked from that same complete finite list. All claimed data refer to exact finite domains; no finite observation is promoted to a theorem over varying fields.

## Originality — PASS, with publication-priority risk
Asgarli–Ghioca (arXiv:2203.07169v1, primary MSC 14N05, 2022) prove existence of fully smooth pencils of hypersurfaces in larger ambient spaces and construct quadric-surface pencils, not this fixed \(\mathbf F_3\) binary-quintic arithmetic census. Günay–Lavrauw (arXiv:2104.04756v1) classify selected orbit invariants for *cubic* pencils in fields excluding characteristic three; their finite-dimensional orbit statements neither specialize nor imply the current quintic result. The 2026 exceptional-degree-five map literature concerns a narrower class than arbitrary degree-five covers with rational unramified fibres. Current search did not identify an equivalent count, table, graph, or stronger covering classification; this does not establish global novelty. An unindexed computation remains a residual risk.

## Value — PASS
Reduced rational fibres are a natural arithmetic branch-avoidance condition for a degree-five morphism from the projective line to itself. The exact count of \(1\,029\) covers with all four rational values unramified, their \(49\) source/target coordinate types, complete four-fibre Frobenius splitting census and the two distinct degree classes of the irreducible-fibre compatibility graph give motivated finite-field Hurwitz structure, not a random parameter slice. The graph records which irreducible degree-five places can simultaneously be unramified inert fibres of a cover. In particular the sharp maximum of two such fibres is a nontrivial arithmetic boundary.
