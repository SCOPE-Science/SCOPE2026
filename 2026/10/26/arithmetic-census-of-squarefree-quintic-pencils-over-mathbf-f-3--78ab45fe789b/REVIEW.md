# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For each homogeneous binary quintic the projective normalization leaves (3^6-1)/2=364 forms. Geometric reducedness is equivalent to affine degree >=4 and gcd(f,f′)=1, including the infinity root; exactly 216 forms pass. Every 2-space has four projective members and six unordered spanning pairs, so the independently enumerated 6,588 qualifying pairs give 1,098 pencils. An entirely fresh finite-field pair/normal-form enumeration checked all 11,011 rational 2-spaces and reproduced the 54 PGL2(F3) source-coordinate orbit sizes (3^2,6^2,12^10,24^40), 1,029 basepoint-free pencils (49 orbits), base degrees 0:1029,2:24,3:24,4:21, 15 factor-degree fibre types, 0/1/2 irreducible member distribution 330/576/192 and connected 48-vertex 192-edge compatibility graph with 24 vertices of degree 7 and 24 of degree 9. No rational base point follows because directional differentiation on a pencil would force an F3 member to vanish to second order there. The finite field census genuinely exhausts its stated 364 forms and 11,011 pencils, not a sample; a separate geometric theorem beyond F3 is neither claimed nor proved.

## originality

PASS

The natural object is a pencil of binary quintic divisors over F3, with every F3-rational member squarefree. The complete pencil catalogue, source-coordinate orbits and inert-fibre compatibility graph are not consequences of the inspected existence results about higher-dimensional smooth hypersurface pencils nor of the classification of cubic pencils in other characteristics. Qualified best-of-knowledge originality is supported by actual comparison of statement/domain and bounded published-finding search; unindexed exhaustive tables and thesis computations remain risks.

## value

PASS

A complete classification of all smooth rational fibres of degree-five maps P1->P1 over the smallest odd field, with exact orbit, base-divisor and irreducible-fibre adjacency invariants, is a motivated arithmetic enumerative object. The squarefree condition and source coordinate symmetry are intrinsic. Future researchers can use its complete exact 1,098-pencil and 192-edge incidence data; it is not an arbitrary unmotivated subset nor a mere reproduction of known cubic-pencil tables.
