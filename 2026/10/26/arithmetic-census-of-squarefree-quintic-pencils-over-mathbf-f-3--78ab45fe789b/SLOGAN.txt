Over \(\mathbf F_3\), exactly \(1\,098\) pencils of binary quintics have all four rational members geometrically squarefree, and they form \(54\) source-coordinate orbits. Among their \(1\,029\) basepoint-free degree-five covers, all four rational target fibres are unramified; the associated \(48\)-vertex irreducible-fibre compatibility graph has exactly \(192\) edges and degree classes \(7^{24},9^{24}\).
