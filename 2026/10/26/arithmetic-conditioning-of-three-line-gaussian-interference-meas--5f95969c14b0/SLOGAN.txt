For symmetric two-tone Gaussian signals, three parallel Gabor-intensity profiles yield an exact \(3\times2\) interference matrix whose least squared singular value is \((3-|1+e^{2\pi it}+e^{2\pi i\rho t}|)/2\). At irrational line-spacing ratio \(\rho\), the precise worst-frequency instability exponent is \(2(\mu(\rho)-1)\), tying optimal phase conditioning to Diophantine approximation rather than merely qualitative uniqueness.
