For binary codings of an irrational circle rotation, the exact cost of sparse observation depends sharply on whether the two cut points lie on the same orbit. Distinct cut orbits force density \(1/(2R+1)\); coincident cut orbits reduce the problem to a finite-pattern minimum-mean-cycle computation, yielding density \(1/2\) at observation radius zero and \(2/11\) for cut separation five at radius one.
