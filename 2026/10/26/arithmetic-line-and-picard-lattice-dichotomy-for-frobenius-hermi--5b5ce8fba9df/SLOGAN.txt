Every smooth quartic \(x^{(3)T}Ax=0\) with symmetric invertible \(A\) over \(\mathbb F_3\) lies in one of exactly two arithmetic types, determined by the determinant square class. The two types have arithmetic Picard lattices of ranks \(12\) and \(11\), discriminants \(-3072\) and \(6144\), and respectively eight and zero rational lines, despite sharing all \(112\) geometric Hermitian lines. Their arithmetic Picard lattices are integrally generated by rational line classes and Galois traces of degree-two line pairs.
