For diagonal cubic surfaces over \(\mathbb F_7\), five coefficient cube-class patterns give arithmetic (Picard rank, effective-ray count, nef-ray count) \((7,27,99)\), \((1,1,1)\), \((1,1,1)\), \((5,15,21)\), and \((3,6,6)\). Every effective ray comes from a Frobenius trace of a geometric line, with six extremal trace rays in the rank-three case.
