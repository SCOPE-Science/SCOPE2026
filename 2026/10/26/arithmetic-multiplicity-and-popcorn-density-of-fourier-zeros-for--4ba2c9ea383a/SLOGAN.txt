For the symmetric three-digit triadic probability measure, Fourier zeros undergo a sharp zero-free transition at middle-digit weight \(1/2\), while their real analytic multiplicities obey an exact geometric distribution at every rational resonance. The distinct-zero density is a popcorn function with downward jumps controlled by the first \(L\) satisfying \(3^L\equiv\pm1\pmod q\); nevertheless the multiplicity-weighted density remains exactly \(1\) throughout the zero-bearing regime.
