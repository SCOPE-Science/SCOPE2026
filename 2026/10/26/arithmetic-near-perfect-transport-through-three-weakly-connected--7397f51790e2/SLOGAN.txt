For three quantum-walk communities joined in a weak-edge path, asymptotically near-perfect end-to-end transfer is possible exactly when the outer internal-arc counts and bridge multiplicities match and the frequency ratio is irrational or an even-over-odd reduced rational. Balanced communities have irrational ratio \(\sqrt3\): they never transfer perfectly at a finite reduced time, yet explicit Pell times achieve infidelity below \(\pi^2/(72q^2)\).
