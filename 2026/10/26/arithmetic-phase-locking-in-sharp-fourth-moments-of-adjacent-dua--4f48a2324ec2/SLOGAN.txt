For adjacent dual symmetric-power characters \(\chi_n\) and \(\overline{\chi_{n+1}}\) on \(SU(3)\), the sharp two-sided fourth-moment inequalities have exact algebraic extremizers and phase locking precisely when \(n\equiv1\pmod3\). This ternary resonance creates a persistent \(5/2\) shift in the asymptotic sharp fourth-power norm and reverses its usual growth at every transition from degree \(3k+1\) to \(3k+2\).
