Among the \(78\) smooth plane-quartic classes over \(\mathbf F_2\), exactly \(17\) admit a rational projection with a nontrivial arithmetic fiberwise automorphism; their center-group profiles occur with multiplicities \(61,14,1,1,1\) for \((n_2,n_4)=(0,0),(1,0),(3,0),(2,1),(0,7)\), respectively. Every such center is outer, and the only two classes with degree-four rational Galois projections have one and seven such centers.
