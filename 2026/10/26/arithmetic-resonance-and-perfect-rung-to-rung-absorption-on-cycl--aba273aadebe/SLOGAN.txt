For a quantum particle on the cyclic two-leg ladder \(C_n\square K_2\), a single absorber captures a particle started at the opposite end of its rung with probability one exactly when \(4\nmid n\) and \(6\nmid n\), despite the presence of other nondecaying states. Otherwise its exact survival probability is \(\frac{8}{3n}\mathbf1_{4\mid n}+\frac{2}{n}\mathbf1_{6\mid n}\), and the absorbing-subspace dimension is \(2(\lfloor n/2\rfloor+1)-2\mathbf1_{4\mid n}-\mathbf1_{6\mid n}\).
