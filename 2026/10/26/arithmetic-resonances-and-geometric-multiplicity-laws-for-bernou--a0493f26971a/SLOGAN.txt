For every Bernoulli convolution, all positive real Fourier zeros are simple unless some positive integer power of its contraction is a ratio of odd integers. In the resonant case with least such power \(\lambda^k=a/b\), the exact asymptotic density of order-\(m\) zeros is \((b-1)^2/[\pi b^{m+1}(1-\lambda)]\), and their earliest occurrence is exactly at frequency \(\pi b^{m-1}/2\).
