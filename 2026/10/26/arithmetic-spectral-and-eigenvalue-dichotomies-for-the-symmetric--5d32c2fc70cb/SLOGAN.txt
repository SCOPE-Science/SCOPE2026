The symmetric two-root recurrence derivative on \(H^2\) is nilpotent at every rational rotation angle but has the exact positive disk spectrum of radius \((2\sin(\pi\alpha))^{-1}\) at every irrational angle. Among irrationals, continued-fraction coefficients eventually at most three give a full open eigenvalue disk, while an explicit super-Liouville angle has no nonzero eigenvalues at all.
