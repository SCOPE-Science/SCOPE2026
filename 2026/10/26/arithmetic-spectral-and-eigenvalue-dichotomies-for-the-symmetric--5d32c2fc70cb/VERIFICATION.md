# Verification and reproducibility

The formula can be reconstructed from the orthonormal Hardy basis \(e_n(z)=z^n\), with \(D_\alpha e_n=\sin(n\pi\alpha)e_{n-1}/\sin(\pi\alpha)\). Exact infinite proofs are in `RESULT.md`: the power-norm reduction, uniform irrational-rotation upper bound, point-spectrum coefficient recurrence, and super-Liouville construction. The published Sudler-product lower bound used in one branch is Gayfulin--Hauke, Theorem 1, arXiv:2312.06548v2. The disk-spectrum implication is the standard unilateral weighted-shift theorem recorded as Proposition 2.2.5 in D'Aniello--Maiuriello, arXiv:2210.01510v1.
