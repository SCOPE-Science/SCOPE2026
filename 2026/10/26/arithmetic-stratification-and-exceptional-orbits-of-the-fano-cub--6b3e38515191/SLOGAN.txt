For the Fano-plane cubic net over every \(\mathbf F_{2^m}\), the geometrically cuspidal members number exactly \((2^m-2)(2^m-4)\), and their rational projective symmetry orbits are free except for one 24-element orbit when \(3\mid m\). The same classification determines all tangent line–conic and triple-line degenerations and their rational-point totals.
