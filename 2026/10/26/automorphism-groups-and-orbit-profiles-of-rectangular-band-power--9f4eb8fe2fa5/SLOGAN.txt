For every finite proper rectangular band, all automorphisms of its nonempty-subset power semigroup are determined exactly by cardinality-preserving row/column support permutations and arbitrary permutations within each nonrectangle support fiber. The resulting explicit product-of-symmetric-groups formula gives exactly \(2ab-a-b+1\) element orbits; the power semigroup of the \(2\times2\) band has \(2880\) automorphisms, compared with \(4\) for the band itself.
