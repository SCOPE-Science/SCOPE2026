# Scientific review: finite-field Rota–Baxter orbit arithmetic

## Correctness — PASS

For every odd-characteristic field, the defining identity first forces triangularity and then forces all internal off-diagonal coefficients to vanish. The remaining diagonal recurrence and terminal-column restrictions are derived directly rather than extrapolated from published characteristic-zero classifications. The annihilator filtration and orthogonality of images of a natural basis determine the full algebra automorphism group. Direct conjugation gives the diagonal-preserving weight action with an additive shear in the first coordinate. The disjoint threshold strata and Burnside's lemma establish the divisor sums. The characteristic-three closed expression follows by parity of the integer weights. The attached checker confirms the arithmetic and action with exhaustive finite tests; the proof is uniform and does not depend on those tests. Limitation: only pure-chain evolution algebras and weight-zero operators in odd characteristic are covered.

## Originality — PASS with residual implication risk

Qaralleh–Mukhamedov–Khakimov (arXiv:2601.08669, §§3–4) classify these operators in characteristic zero; Mukhamedov–Khakimov–Omirov–Qaralleh (arXiv:1805.10285, §5) determine the automorphism group; and Qaralleh–Mukhamedov–Khakimov (arXiv:2606.22623, §§2,6) describe local operators and conditions for an individual operator to commute with an automorphism. These results contain strong separate ingredients but do not state the complete odd-finite-field orbit count or its modular resonance. Searches of related public indexed research records and broader published literature found no equivalent result, but failure to locate one is not a uniqueness proof. In particular, the individual-commutant formulas in arXiv:2606.22623 and Burnside's lemma are an explicit route by which this counting result may have been implicitly accessible. That implication risk remains real; the arithmetic orbit stratification and positive-characteristic proof are the claimed addition.

## Value — PASS

Operator equivalence under algebra automorphisms, rather than enumeration of labelled matrices, is a standard structural classification target. The theorem gives complete orbit numbers for a natural all-dimension family, identifies a genuine characteristic-dependent first-coordinate shear resonance, and reduces an otherwise growing conjugacy problem to explicit divisor arithmetic. The closed ternary-field formula is an exact nontrivial all-dimension specialization. No larger classification of all maximal-nilindex algebras is claimed.
