For affine Fourier least-squares projection on an \(n\)-bit Bernoulli product cube, the sharp \(L^1\) and \(L^\infty\) amplification is an exact binomial absolute deviation, uniquely minimized by fair bits for every \(n\ge2\). The fair-bias optimum repeats across adjacent even–odd dimensions, while rare-bit scaling produces a distinct, piecewise-smooth Poisson limit with integer cusp points.
