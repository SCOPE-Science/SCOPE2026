On the finite-chain monoid of order-preserving, order-decreasing contractions, independently Bernoulli-weighted increment measures multiply by multiplying their parameters. Consequently every rank-\(r\) element has exactly \((2^k-1)^{n-r}\) uniform ordered \(k\)-factor representations, and random products collapse to the constant map across a sharp \(\log_2(n-1)\) total-variation window.
