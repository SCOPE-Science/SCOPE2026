In a three-velocity run-and-tumble harmonic trap, the stationary position and run/tumble flag have an exact two-Beta, one-Bernoulli representation, making every radial tail strictly larger during runs and exactly monotone under either switching-rate change. Near either turning point, the conditional probability of tumbling is \(\frac12(1-y^2)+O((1-y^2)^2)\) for normalized position \(y\), with a leading coefficient independent of both transition rates.
