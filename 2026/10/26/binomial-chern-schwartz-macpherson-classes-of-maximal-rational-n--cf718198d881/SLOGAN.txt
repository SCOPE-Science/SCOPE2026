For every complex rational normal curve in \(\mathbb P^{2r}\), the Chern–Schwartz–MacPherson class of the complement of its maximal proper secant hypersurface has dimension-\(i\) coefficient \(\binom r{\lfloor i/2\rfloor}\binom r{\lceil i/2\rceil}\). An apolar-incidence resolution and exact generating-function evaluation establish the all-rank formula and hence both parts of Nogueira's polynomiality conjecture for the associated polar map.
