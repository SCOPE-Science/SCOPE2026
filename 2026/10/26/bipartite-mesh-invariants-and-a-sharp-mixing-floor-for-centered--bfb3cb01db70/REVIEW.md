# Mathematical review

**Correctness — PASS.** The edgewise energy pairing yields uniqueness of centered weights on active edges; cycle circulations show that membership in the common kernel forces all pairs of neighbors of a vertex to agree. Two-vertex connectivity supplies such a cycle for every neighbor pair; even-walk parity gives the exact 1/2-dimensional classification. The checkerboard is annihilated edge by edge, not merely on average. Weighted skew-adjointness preserves its projection for continuous flow and Crank–Nicolson steps. Cauchy–Schwarz supplies the inverse-Laplacian bound, and the periodic checkerboard eigenvector saturates it. Exact-rational graph cases and the 4-by-6 grid calculation corroborate intermediate identities, but do not replace proof.

**Originality — PASS with historical risk.** The 2026 mixing scheme already proves mass/energy conservation, Coppola–Veldman (2022) already identify the energy-preserving midpoint rule and note odd-even effects in collocated pressure solvers, and the signless-Laplacian kernel/bipartite equivalence is classical. None of those inspected claims provides the all-divergence-free-graph common transport-kernel classification or the sharp controlled inverse-Laplacian lower bound. Published mathematical-record searches for circulation, bipartite mesh, checkerboard, finite-volume mixing, and Crank–Nicolson did not identify direct stronger coverage. This does not rule out older equivalent graph-advection theory.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The result distinguishes exact conservation, which motivates centered fluxes, from the stronger numerical property needed by mixing optimization, namely absence of inaccessible tracer directions. It supplies a complete natural graph classification and a nonzero, attainable optimization floor whenever the initial data have a checkerboard component. Neither a selected arbitrary graph slice nor a generic numerical warning is the mathematical object: the all-controls, all-timesteps, mesh-topology criterion is. Limitations include smooth-data suppression of the checkerboard coefficient, the algebraic rather than physically parameterized control envelope for the converse, and no conclusion of controllability for nonbipartite grids.

Closest literature: Hu et al. (arXiv:2601.06294), §§2–3; Coppola–Veldman (arXiv:2208.14067), §5.2; Cvetković–Rowlinson–Simić (signless-Laplacian survey). Independent validation has not been performed.
