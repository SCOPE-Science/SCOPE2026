For centered conservative finite-volume advection, bipartite cell-adjacency graphs carry a checkerboard tracer that no divergence-free face-control schedule can move: among 2-vertex-connected simple meshes, this is the only nonconstant direction stationary for every admissible face circulation. The resulting invariant gives an exact control-independent discrete \(H^{-1}\) mixing floor, attained by a pure checkerboard on even-by-even periodic Cartesian grids.
