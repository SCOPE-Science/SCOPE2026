The \(3\pmod8\) slice of the binary reciprocal of the sum-of-divisors parity series has Bombieri–Vinogradov level of distribution \(1/2\), with explicit square-weighted logarithmic-integral main terms in every compatible reduced progression. Its total count is asymptotic to \((\pi^2/32)X/\log X\); the previously known prime–square characterization is an input rather than a discovery.
