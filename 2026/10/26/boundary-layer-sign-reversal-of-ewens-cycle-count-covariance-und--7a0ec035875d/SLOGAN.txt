In Ewens random permutations, forbidding only the one or two longest possible cycle sizes eventually makes fixed-point and transposition counts negatively correlated. For \(0<\theta<1\), forbidding three or more such sizes reverses this sign to positive, with a sharp \(\theta=2\) change in the covariance decay scale; an exact coefficient law and a separate \(\theta=1\) classification complete the fixed-cutoff result.
