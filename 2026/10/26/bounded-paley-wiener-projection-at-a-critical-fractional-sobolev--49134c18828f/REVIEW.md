# Review

## Correctness

**PASS.** On the homogeneous Sobolev quotient, \(\Delta^{s/2}\) conjugates the Hilbert projection to the interval Fourier multiplier \(T_a\). The multiplier is bounded on \(L^p(\mathbb R)\) for \(1<p<\infty\), and dilation conjugacy makes its norm independent of \(a\). The result is restricted to \(1<p<2\), where the Hilbert-side polynomial order matches the critical target order.

## Originality

**PASS, with residual priority risk.** The primary paper explicitly lists critical projection boundedness as open and proposes the quotient realization used here. Searches of published research records and accessible primary literature found no statement covering this exact fractional critical quotient projection. The closest related BMO/projection literature treats ordinary Bernstein spaces, not the fractional Sobolev critical indices. This is not a claim of exhaustive novelty.

## Value

**PASS.** The boundedness of the Paley–Wiener projection is a structural question for the endpoint function space and is explicitly identified as open in the motivating paper. The exact norm reduction covers every integer critical order for \(1<p<2\), while leaving the interpolation identification and other exponent ranges open.
