# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The claim is a complete formula and uniqueness statement for the second Laplacian eigenvalue of an independently weighted double star with a priced bridge. Both price regimes have positive, exactly budget-feasible conductances. The proof gives an explicit positive-semidefinite upper-bound certificate with exact squared edge distances and constructs eigenvectors saturating the bound. Four-node weighted Jacobi oscillation and the separately computed transverse leaf eigenvalues show that the saturated eigenvalue is the actual spectral gap, not a higher eigenvalue. Equality in the dual trace identity determines **every individual** conductance and establishes uniqueness, including the phase boundary. The checker passed 848 exact rational certificate/vertex-equation cases, an exact polynomial identity, all corresponding numerical spectra/multiplicities and 1,000 asymmetric comparisons. Computations support but do not replace the all-parameter argument.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
The 2006 Sun–Boyd–Xiao–Diaconis study already introduces the general spectral-gap conductance-budget SDP and geometric dual, so those are attributed. Fiedler's 1990 result concerns uniform-cost absolute algebraic connectivity for all trees. Richter and Rocha's 2014 preprint/2015 study gives an explicit *uniform-price* double-broom classification; in particular, the price-one slice and the existence of an imbalance phase change are covered and not independently novel. Unlike these recorded statements, the present all-positive-price classification evaluates the parameter-dependent transition \(s_c=(p-q)/(q+1)\), the different optimal weight branches and gap multiplicities, and uniqueness under arbitrary asymmetric weighting. Searches of the published-finding index and accessible primary literature found no exact cost-weighted formula or parameter-dominating result. This negative search is not a uniqueness proof: an exact priced-bridge result in specialized literature remains possible. Full text of the 1990 paper could not be reached through available open routes or institutional access; its accessible abstract concerns uniform costs.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The bridge is a natural, differently priced communication link in a two-cluster diffusion/consensus network. The result does more than optimize a scalar: it identifies a sharp dimensionless bridge-price boundary, two genuinely different optimal rate patterns, a change from a \(p\)-fold eigenvalue to a simple one, and a smooth value function with changed curvature. It also proves that none of the independent-edge degrees of freedom can improve on the explicit design. These results support exact engineering tradeoffs between single-link cost and mixing rate, not an arbitrary reparameterization or numerical example.

## Scope and risks
The classification is for double stars only, with identical prices for all leaf edges and a single relative bridge price. The methods of the general SDP and unpriced tree embeddings are prior art. The precise priced classification should be compared by an independent researcher against broader historical eigenvalue-optimization and weighted-tree sources. No independent mathematical audit has been performed.
