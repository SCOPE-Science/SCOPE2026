The entire foliated canonical ring of Guo–Hu–Lü's geometric-genus-one family is a normal weighted rational-normal-curve algebra generated in degrees \(1,2,\ldots,n+1\), with all relations given by catalecticant minors. The sharp-volume \(1/6\) member has polynomial canonical ring of degrees \(1,2,3\) and canonical-model surface \(\mathbb P(1,2,3)\).
