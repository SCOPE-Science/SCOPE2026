In the stationary balanced expanding/contracting base-\(b\) interval model, every centered nonconstant leading-digit observable has exact Catalan correlations: odd lags vanish and lag \(2m\) equals \(\sigma_f^2 C_m[p(1-p)]^m\). Its integrated variance is maximized precisely at the zero-Lyapunov switch \(p=1/2\), where the finite-time variance has a sharp negative \(8\sigma_f^2\sqrt{2N/\pi}\) correction to \(3\sigma_f^2N\), unlike the bounded finite-size correction away from criticality.
