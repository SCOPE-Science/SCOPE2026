For the mixed-Euler memristive-cell recurrence, the centered choice \(\lambda=1/2\) is the unique volume-preserving parameter and its second iterate is globally a family of two-shear symplectic maps. Every pair of unequal flux values generates a prime two-cycle, with two neutral multipliers and a reciprocal transverse pair determined exactly by \(L'(u)L'(v)\); no periodic orbit is asymptotically attracting.
