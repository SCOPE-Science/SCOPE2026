# Verification of the centered memristive-cell resonance

It checks Laurent coefficients and companion determinants for five exact rational mixing coefficients, 245 exact parity-invariance relations, 25 exact rational global conjugacy examples, and four passive examples with direct two-cycle and Floquet substitutions. These are supplementary cross-checks, not proofs of the universal theorem.
