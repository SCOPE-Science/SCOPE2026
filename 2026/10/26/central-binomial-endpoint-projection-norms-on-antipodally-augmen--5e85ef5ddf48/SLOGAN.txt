Adding all antipodal matching edges to the binary \(n\)-cube has an exact positive endpoint cut-projection cost: for \(n\ge5\) the norm rises by \(\frac12-4^{-\lceil n/2\rceil}\binom{2\lceil n/2\rceil}{\lceil n/2\rceil}\). The cut and cycle norms of the augmented cube admit closed central-binomial formulas, with a two-dimension parity plateau in their non-linear correction.
