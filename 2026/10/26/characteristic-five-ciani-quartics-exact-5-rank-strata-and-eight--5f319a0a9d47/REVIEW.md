# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

For the smooth normalized family the standard genus-three Hasse–Witt formula is diagonal and direct multinomial expansion yields the three displayed \(h_i\). The independent elliptic quotient computation matches these invariants; the known degree-eight isogeny is prime to \(5\), preserving the Jacobian \(5\)-torsion and its \(a\)-number. The differences \((t^2-s^2)(r^2+1)\) and cyclic variants force equal squares on the smooth rank-zero locus. The factorization \((a-2)^2(a^2-2a-2)\) and nonzero discriminant give all eight smooth geometric rank-zero coefficient triples. Exact full enumeration of all \(25^3\) triples by two different field representations confirms the reported ranks and smoothness totals.

## Originality — PASS with specified residual risk

Ohashi's 2022 results already give the Ciani model, smoothness test, elliptic quotient isogeny, and superspeciality descent to \(\mathbb F_{p^2}\); these are not new. Moriya–Kudo give general genus-three Howe superspecial constructions/algorithms, not the present all-rank smooth marked-family count over \(\mathbb F_{25}\). Existing arithmetic tables and accessible previously recorded claims were checked for statement implication, including characteristic-three Ciani distributions; none identified an exact table or a stronger implication for all four characteristic-five rank strata. Brock's superspecial Ciani enumeration is cited by Ohashi but its thesis was not directly read; the geometric eight-parameter classification could overlap it. The accepted scientific contribution is the complete rank-stratum classification, not an unverified claim of first-ever superspecial Ciani equations.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This is a complete arithmetic classification of the natural three-parameter normalized Ciani family in the first field \(\mathbb F_{p^2}\) relevant to superspecial Ciani descent at characteristic five. It distinguishes all ordinary and intermediate-rank strata, excludes superspecial forms over \(\mathbb F_5\), and proves geometrically that the superspecial locus is a finite two-Galois-orbit signed locus. It provides a concrete benchmark for genus-three Jacobian arithmetic and quotient-based enumeration. Parameter counts are explicitly **not** conflated with isomorphism class counts.

## Limits

The base-field rank counts concern only the fixed normalized family; they are not a uniform extension-field formula or a moduli-class census. Unindexed prior tables and inaccessible Brock thesis constitute residual originality risks. The review and proof checks were performed in the same research process, not an independent audit.
