Every smooth normalized Ciani quartic in characteristic \(5\) has a diagonal three-factor Hasse–Witt description; over \(\mathbb F_{25}\) the exact numbers of ordered smooth parameter triples of \(5\)-rank \(0,1,2,3\) are \(8,96,1110,10469\). The eight geometrically superspecial parameter triples are precisely the four even-sign patterns applied to the two roots of \(a^2-2a-2=0\).
