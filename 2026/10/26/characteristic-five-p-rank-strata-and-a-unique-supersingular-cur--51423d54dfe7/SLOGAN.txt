For the smooth characteristic-five cyclic-six genus-four family \(y^2=x^{10}+x^7+ax^4+bx\), two explicit polynomial conditions classify every Jacobian \(5\)-rank and \(a\)-number. Exactly one marked member, \((a,b)=(4,2)\), is supersingular but not superspecial, and its zeta numerator is \((1+5T^2)^2(1-5T^2+25T^4)\).
