The characteristic-five reduction of Bonnafé–Sarti's distinguished \(M_{20}\) complete-intersection K3 model has exactly \(96\) points over \(\mathbb F_5\) and \(1176\) over \(\mathbb F_{25}\), forcing the entire zeta function \(Z(T)=[(1-T)(1-25T)(1-5T)^{18}(1+5T)^4]^{-1}\). Thus its Frobenius slopes are all one, and its point count is explicit over every finite extension of \(\mathbb F_5\).
