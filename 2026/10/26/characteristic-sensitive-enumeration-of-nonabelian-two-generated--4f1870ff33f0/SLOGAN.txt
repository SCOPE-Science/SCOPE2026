The six-dimensional Lie algebra of strictly upper-triangular \(4\times4\) matrices has exactly two nonabelian types of Lie subalgebra generated by at most two elements: three-dimensional Heisenberg and four-dimensional filiform. Their exact embedding counts over \(\mathbb F_q\) change sharply in characteristic two: \(((q+3)q^4,(q^2-1)q^2)\) versus \((4q^4,(q^2+q-2)q^2)\) in odd characteristic.
