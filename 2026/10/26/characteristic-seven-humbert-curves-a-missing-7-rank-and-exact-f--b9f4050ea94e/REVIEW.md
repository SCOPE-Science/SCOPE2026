## Correctness — PASS

The complete-intersection cover has genus five by tame Riemann--Hurwitz. Character orthogonality and the identity \(\Phi\Psi=[8]\) show the sum-of-pullbacks isogeny has a two-primary kernel, so it preserves \(7\)-divisible groups and therefore \(7\)-rank, \(a\)-number and Newton slopes. The characteristic-seven Legendre Hasse polynomial factors with roots precisely the three harmonic ratios. If two four-point subsets are harmonic, the entire five-point set normalizes into \(\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_7)\), where triple transitivity and an explicit representative calculation force exactly three harmonic four-subsets. Double-counting incidences gives the three polynomial formulas, checked independently at \(q=7\) and \(q=49\) by `check.py`. Finite tests are corroboration only; the proof covers all algebraically closed fields of characteristic seven and every finite extension \(\mathbb F_{7^n}\).

## Originality — PASS, with historical qualifications

Carvacho--Hidalgo--Quispe (arXiv:1507.02903, Section 5.1) already establish the genus-five decomposition into five elliptic quotients in the complex setting, and Yamauchi (2008) studies split-Jacobian constructions. Hidalgo (arXiv:1710.01349) studies characteristic-three Humbert Cartier operators. Those sources are acknowledged as structurally relevant, not passed off as new. The assessed addition is the characteristic-seven missing rank, unique minimum branch orbit, Newton-slope classification, and all-extension marked arithmetic census. Searches for equivalent cross-ratio, generalized Fermat, finite-field and Hasse-stratification formulations found no matching complete claim. The 2008 full text was not accessible; independent historical verification is still needed. The recent other-characteristic Humbert results in available comparison records do not imply the characteristic-seven incidence geometry or its counts.

## Mathematical value — PASS

This is a complete natural arithmetic stratification of the two-dimensional smooth Humbert family in a special characteristic, not a computation for a handpicked curve. The impossible intermediate rank and the isolated rank-two configuration encode a geometric restriction on how supersingular elliptic subcovers coexist; the exact extension-field counts quantify each stratum. The a-number and Newton-polygon statements give further directly usable moduli invariants. Its core is not implied by the pre-existing split-Jacobian theorem without the additional harmonic configuration argument.
