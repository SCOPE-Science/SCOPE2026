In characteristic \(7\), smooth genus-five Humbert Jacobians have exactly the \(7\)-ranks \(\{2,4,5\}\): rank \(3\) is impossible, and the rank-two branch configurations form a unique projective orbit. For every \(q=7^n\), the numbers of ordered marked parameters with ranks \(2,4,5\) are exactly \(20\), \(15(q-7)\), and \((q-7)(q-13)\).
