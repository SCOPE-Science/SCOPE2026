For every degree \(d\ge3\), the tangential surface of a rational normal curve in characteristic three has a Jacobian singular scheme that is a triple thickening of the original curve, with degree \(3d\), Hilbert polynomial \(3dn-9\), and nilpotent graded pieces \(\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1}(-4)\) and \(\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1}(-8)\). Outside characteristic three (and two), the corresponding thickening has multiplicity two; in characteristic two the tangential surface is smooth.
