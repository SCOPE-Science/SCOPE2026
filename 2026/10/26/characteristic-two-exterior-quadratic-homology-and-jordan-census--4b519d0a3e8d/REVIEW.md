# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof uses characteristic-two cancellation to show \(L_\omega^2=0\), symplectic normal form, a local two-term contractible pair with two surviving degree-one generators, and a tensor-product homology decomposition. Crucially, the local homology polynomial is \(2t\), **not** \(t\), so the global polynomial is \(2^r t^r(1+t)^{n-2r}\), consistent with the Jordan kernel quotient dimension \(2^{n-r}\). The rank-to-Jordan formula and the Gaussian/symplectic orbit count then follow. Exhaustive small-field computation, including extension-field arithmetic, agrees with both rank and fiber formulas.

## Originality — PASS within inspected coverage
The closest inspected 2021 treatment by Korhonen addresses induced actions on exterior or symmetric square modules, not left multiplication by an arbitrary quadratic element of the full exterior algebra. Existing alternating-form ranks, orbit counts, and the Kunneth argument are explicitly treated as classical premises rather than new theorems. The claimed whole-object exact combination is the graded homology with all Jordan fibers in characteristic-two finite fields; no equivalent full statement was located in the searches below. The strongest residual risk is unindexed symplectic differential-complex literature giving the same homology statement, from which the census would follow upon standard finite-field counting. Historical uniqueness is not certified.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The object is the canonical exterior algebra over a finite field; its quadratic multiplication operators define a natural finite-field family of nilpotent linear maps. The result simultaneously classifies every graded homology profile, every Jordan partition and the exact cardinalities of their parameter loci, uniformly in dimension and field size. The concentration of homology in degrees from \(r\) to \(n-r\), the explicit multiplicity \(2^r\binom{n-2r}{k-r}\), and complete Jordan frequencies provide natural information beyond merely restating alternating-form normal forms. No unproved application is asserted.
