For square-zero log-canonical Poisson algebras in \(m\ge2\) generators, the cotangent image of the Poisson automorphism group is the diagonal torus outside characteristic two but all of \(\operatorname{GL}_m(k)\) in characteristic two, with a nilpotent kernel in both cases. Thus the group is solvable in odd or zero characteristic and nonsolvable in characteristic two whenever \(m\ge3\).
