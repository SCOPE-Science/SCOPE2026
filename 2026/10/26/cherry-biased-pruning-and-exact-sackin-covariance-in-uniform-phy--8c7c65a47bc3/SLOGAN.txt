For a uniformly sampled rooted, leaf-labeled binary phylogenetic tree, marking and contracting a random cherry transfers its Sackin-depth distribution exactly to a one-leaf-smaller uniform tree with a distinguished leaf. Consequently cherry count and Sackin index have strictly negative covariance for every \(n\ge4\), equal to \(\frac{n}{4(2n-3)}(4(n-1)-n\cdot 4^{n-1}/\binom{2n-2}{n-1})\), with asymptotic \(-\frac{\sqrt\pi}{8}n^{3/2}\).
