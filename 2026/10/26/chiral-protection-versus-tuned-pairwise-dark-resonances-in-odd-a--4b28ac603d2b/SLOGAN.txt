On an odd alternating-coupling quantum chain, a sink at the second site preserves a chiral zero mode for every positive hopping ratio, whereas a sink at the third site preserves exactly two modes only at \(\lfloor m/2\rfloor\) Chebyshev-selected ratios. The resulting endpoint survival probability at a third-site resonance is exactly \(2\sin^2(k\pi/(m+1))/(m+1)\), independently of the positive absorption strength.
