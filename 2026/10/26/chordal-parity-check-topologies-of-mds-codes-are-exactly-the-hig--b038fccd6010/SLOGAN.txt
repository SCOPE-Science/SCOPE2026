For every fixed \([n,k]\) MDS code, its attainable chordal parity-check primal graphs are exactly the \(k\)-vertex-connected chordal graphs, independently of the field or representation. Their minimum interaction-edge count is \(kn-\binom{k+1}{2}\), and precisely the \(k\)-trees attain it; every labeled optimizer is realized by a minimum-weight dual-check basis.
