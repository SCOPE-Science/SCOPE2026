For \(k\ge2\) and \(q\ge3\), every minimum independent dominating set of \(C_{3k}\times K_q\) is a three-phase pattern of monochromatic two-fiber blocks whose colors properly color a cyclic \(k\)-word. There are exactly \(3((q-1)^k+(-1)^k(q-1))\) such labeled sets, and no two are related by a single token jump: the minimum-solution reconfiguration graph is edgeless.
