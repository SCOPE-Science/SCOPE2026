# Scientific assessment

**Correctness.** The forward implication uses actual repeated factorization, two-sided Laurent coefficient estimates, unique continuation to the punctured plane, continuity of the extension operator by a closed graph, then compactness of a norm ball on a wider annulus to rule out infinite dimension. The finite-dimensional algebra is classified through a finite separating set of Laurent coefficient evaluations and zero-constant Lagrange interpolation; its primitive idempotents necessarily have finite disjoint supports. The converse is verified by coefficientwise products and complex square roots. The closed-graph and compactness steps require the specified complex-linear closed Fréchet hypothesis.

**Originality.** The inspected primary source [1] proves analytic continuation for general self-reproducing sets, uniform finite support for uniformly bounded families, and classification of convolution ideals; these do not directly settle closed self-reproducing **linear subalgebras**. A block such as \(\mathbb C(z^2+z^4)\) is self-reproducing but not an ambient ideal, so the ideal theorem does not subsume the classification. Full primary material [2] concerns closed ideals, not the present two-sided annular block algebras. Semantic database searches for compact-open closed self-reproduction, annular Hadamard algebras and finite Laurent blocks returned no dominating statement; this is not a uniqueness proof. The potentially relevant broad 1996 study [3] could be checked only through an abstract, so priority remains provisional there.

**Value.** This is a natural **complete structural classification** of an otherwise unclassified topologically closed linear subclass in the current self-reproduction problem. It upgrades elementwise continuation to global finite dimensionality and describes precisely which coefficient identifications, beyond singleton-monomial ideals, are possible. It separates the finite-dimensional closed-linear regime from the dense nonclosed maximal family of [1], with exact boundary examples.

**Limits.** No statement is made for closed nonlinear semigroups, all closed subalgebras without exact factorization, or infinite-dimensional closed ideals. The argument is self-contained modulo standard closed-graph, Montel, and finite-dimensional norm compactness theorems. The full text of [3] remains an originality-risk item.
