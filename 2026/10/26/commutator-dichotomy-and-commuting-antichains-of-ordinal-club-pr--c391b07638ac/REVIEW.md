# Review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof identifies the two point maps inducing the commutator. If they differ, a compactly supported clopen step function gives the lower bound \(2\), and the triangle inequality gives the matching upper bound. The club-membership criterion follows from the three possible orderings of the next club points. The explicit family is closed and unbounded and verifies both conditions pairwise.

The closest composition-operator distance source concerns Hardy spaces, not ordinal \(C_0\) retractions. A targeted search is not an exhaustive novelty guarantee.

The continuum-sized antichain shows that the commutative structure is not limited to nested clubs, a useful structural fact for the operator algebra generated by the canonical family.
