For every nonempty compact \(K\subset(0,\infty)\), a single injective weighted shift has a precisely classified cluster set of \(|T^n|^{1/n}\) containing countably many clopen copies of \(K\), with identical weak- and strong-operator limits. Although this set can have uncountably many connected components, the scalar asymptotic numerical range at every unit vector is the same interval \([e^{-\max K},1]\).
