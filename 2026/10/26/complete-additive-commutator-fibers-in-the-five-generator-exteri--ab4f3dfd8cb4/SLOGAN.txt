For the exterior algebra on five generators over every odd finite field, the ordinary additive commutator has a complete target-by-target fiber formula: its image is a Plücker cone times the fourth exterior power, with two distinct nonzero-target multiplicities and an exceptional zero fiber. The exact image size is \(q^{12}+q^{10}-q^7\); the previously known commuting-pair count is recovered as a consistency check.
