For the generalized Jacobsthal recurrence \(U_{n+2}=(b-1)U_{n+1}+bU_n\), the infinite shifted semigroup \(\langle U_4+U_n:n\ge0\rangle\) has an explicit four-generator presentation and a complete Apéry, genus, and Frobenius classification. Its pseudo-Frobenius set has exactly two explicit elements for every integer \(b\ge2\).
