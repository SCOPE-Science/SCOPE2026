The three associator nuclei and the commutant of the dihedral quandle algebra \(K[R_n]\) admit complete coefficient-wise classifications over every field: their dimensions are governed by parity, \(n\bmod4\), and \(\gcd(n,3)\). For \(4\mid n\), the full associative nucleus is zero outside characteristic \(2\), but jumps to a two-dimensional zero-multiplication algebra in characteristic \(2\); every even-order algebra has zero center.
