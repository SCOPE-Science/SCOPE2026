The last nonzero digits of \(n^n\) are eventually periodic in base \(b\ge2\) exactly when \(b=2^{2^s}\) for an integer \(s\ge0\); in those cases the sequence is periodic from its first term. A constructive Chinese remainder argument eliminates every base with at least two distinct prime factors, completing the previously conjectured classification together with the published prime-power theorem.
