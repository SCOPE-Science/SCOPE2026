# Scientific review: binary four-window permutation-ball distribution

**Correctness — PASS.** The pointwise first-difference decomposition is established by direct counting and agrees with the 2026 source's general window formula. Complement-paired three-bit suffixes give a four-state transfer matrix whose path weights are exactly the deficiencies from the universal bound. Its rational generating function gives the complete finite histogram and the near-maximal coefficient identities. The maximizing paths are exactly the two alternating and four period-four phases. Enumerating local overlapping configurations gives the covariance table, and a bounded three-dependent blocking argument gives the Gaussian limit. An independent direct set-of-output enumerator agreed with the histogram computation for every word of length four through twelve. A pure-integer-polynomial determinant certificate also verifies the exact generating-function coefficients, without relying on finite-length agreement. Residual mathematical risk is primarily accidental confusion between unrestricted permutations and cyclic shifts; the definitions, code and proof consistently use unrestricted permutations.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** Chen and Milenkovic, arXiv:2610.15701v1, Section II, prove a general pointwise first-difference formula and a mean and derive concentration-based coding bounds. Those ingredients are prior work. The present explicit four-state population law, six-center extremal classification, polynomial near-maximum frequency counts, exact variance and Gaussian limit were not found in the inspected relevant sections or targeted literature-index searches. Pointwise knowledge of every center does in principle determine any population statistic by exhaustive summation, so this result is an evaluated structural consequence rather than an unrelated discovery. Results for cyclic-only rotations, disjoint adjacent swaps and deletion balls have different allowed outputs and do not imply these particular formulas. An unindexed or independently derived equivalent remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** The exact four-symbol permutation model has an explicit experimental motivation in nanopore sensing. The complete histogram permits exact finite-length assessment of local ambiguity, not just a mean or a rough concentration estimate. The extremal set consists of only six periodic centers at every length, while the near-maximum populations grow as explicit low-degree polynomials; the variance and limit law quantify the separation of typical from worst-case ambiguity. The claims are scoped to binary, one window, and fixed length four; they do not purport to solve code-cardinality optimality or generalized multiple-burst decoding.

The most relevant inspected source is Y.-P. Chen and O. Milenkovic, *Correcting Burst Errors with Applications to DNA-Based Data Storage*, arXiv:2610.15701v1 (11 October 2026), especially Section II and the discussions of coding and cyclic models in Sections III–V. The remaining novelty risk is an equivalent formula in unindexed literature or a less obvious reformulation of the source's general center-dependent expression.
