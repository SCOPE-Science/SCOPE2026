For the smooth Hurwitz curve \(X^{p^e+1}Y+Y^{p^e+1}Z+Z^{p^e+1}X=0\), every Cartier iterate has an explicit rank given by base-\(p\) no-carry digit counting. The formula determines the exact Jacobian \(p\)-rank \((p(p+1)/2)^e\) and every nilpotent Cartier-string multiplicity, refining the previously known single-step \(a\)-number.
