For every cycle prism \(C_n\square K_2\), the numbers of minimum singleton three-rainbow dominating assignments are exactly \(12\), \(12n\), \(18n(3n-5)\), or \(18n(3n-1)\), according to whether \(n\) is divisible by six, odd, congruent to two modulo six, or congruent to four modulo six. A two-terminal three-color kernel also determines all assignments of weights \(n\), \(n+1\), and \(n+2\), explaining the constant, linear, and quadratic multiplicity regimes.
