For Gaussian Hermitian matrices with independent circular off-diagonal entries and arbitrary positive-semidefinite diagonal covariance \(\Sigma\), every unitary conjugation symmetry is a product of block-unitary rotations on classes of identical rows of \(\Sigma-I\) and covariance-preserving coordinate permutations. This exactly classifies the exceptional continuous symmetries left outside the generic spectral hypothesis, including singular covariances.
