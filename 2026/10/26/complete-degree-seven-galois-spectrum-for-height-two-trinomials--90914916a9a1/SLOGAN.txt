The complete Galois spectrum of the twenty-four trinomials \(X^7+aX^k+b\), with \(1\le k\le6\), \(|a|=2\), and \(|b|=1\), consists of eight \(S_7\), eight \(S_6\), four \(S_3\times S_3\), and four \(S_3\times S_4\) splitting fields. In the reducible cases, the fields of the distinct nonlinear factors are linearly disjoint, certified by different quadratic discriminant fields.
