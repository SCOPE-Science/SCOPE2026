Every distance-two mutually visible vertex set in a binary hypercube is a subset of a radius-one Hamming ball or one complete affine parity tetrahedron; these give the two exact facet families. The complete size polynomial is \(1+2^dz+2^{d-1}(d+\binom d2)z^2+2^d\sum_{k=3}^{d+1}\binom{d+1}{k}z^k+2^{d-2}\binom d3z^4\) for every \(d\ge3\), and the minimum size of an inclusion-maximal set is exactly \(4\).
