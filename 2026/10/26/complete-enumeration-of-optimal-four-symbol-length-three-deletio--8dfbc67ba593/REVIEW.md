# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Every length-three word contributes at most three ordered-pair descendants, proving the sharp six-codeword lower bound, with an explicit cover. The counting program implements two complete and differently structured finite enumerations. A decreasing-cardinality subset DP computes the number of six-subsets whose union is the full 16-pair universe; a lexicographic six-loop enumerator with mathematically justified remaining-capacity pruning directly reconstructs all 644 covers. Every equivalence class is computed from the exact 48-element action, and all incidence and orbit totals reconcile. Proof scope is the finite parameter set only.

**Originality — PASS, subject to residual prior-art risk.** Xie–Sun–Ge study insertion/deletion coverings and asymptotic constructions, not this exact finite population census. Chee–Ge–Ling's 2010 size-spectrum theorem and examples already settle the minimum-size and perfect-existence question; they do not imply counts of labeled codebooks, overlap/nonoverlap separation, or symmetry orbit distribution. Related published-finding corpus records on length-three odd-alphabet covering *number*, ternary code maxima and other deletion-code censuses concern different problems. No equivalent count was located under searches for enumeration of quaternary length-three deletion covers and directed-design classifications. Unindexed equivalent directed designs remain possible.

**Value — PASS.** The smallest alphabet directly relevant to four-letter sequence coding has a natural sharp covering-size extremum. A complete classification of its minima, not merely one optimizer, determines how strongly minimum-cardinality covering differs from error correction: only 120 of 644 optima have disjoint inverse shadows. The 21 symmetry orbits and full excess-incidence profiles are exact structural invariants of a canonical short synchronization model; they are not implied by the known cardinality spectrum. No claim of improved coding rate or broader-alphabet asymptotics is made.

**Literature and limits.** Xie–Sun–Ge (2026), arXiv:2606.15379v1, entire accessible HTML, Sections I and V; Chee–Ge–Ling (2010), arXiv:1008.1343v1, full 23-page preprint, especially Sections 2 and 6. Possible unindexed directed-design census and the choice of equivalence group are disclosed; independent validation is outstanding.
