All minimum five-landmark distance-equalizer sets of the rectangular Hamming graph \(K_p\square K_q\), \(p,q\ge5\), fall into exactly nine row-column patterns, only five of which persist when both board dimensions exceed five. Their exact number is \(\frac94(p)_3(q)_3+\frac{(p)_5(q)_5}{120}+\mathbf1_{p=5}q(5q-4)+\mathbf1_{q=5}p(5p-4)\), refining the previously known minimum size of five.
