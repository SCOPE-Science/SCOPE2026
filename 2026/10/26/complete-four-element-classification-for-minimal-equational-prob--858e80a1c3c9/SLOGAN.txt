Exactly ten isomorphism classes of four-point groupoids have the minimal equational probability spectrum \(\{1/4,1\}\), corresponding to exactly 52 labeled multiplication tables. Beyond five nonquasigroup classes, the five quasigroup classes are precisely the affine operations \(x*y=ax+by\) on \(\mathbb F_4\) with \((a,b)=(1,1),(\omega,1),(1,\omega),(\omega,\omega),(\omega,\omega^2)\).
