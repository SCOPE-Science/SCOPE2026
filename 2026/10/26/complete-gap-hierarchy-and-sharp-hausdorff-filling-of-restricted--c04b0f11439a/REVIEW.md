# Review of the complete Gauss-survivor gap theorem

## Correctness — PASS
The proof identifies the exact inverse branches and all first-level hull gaps, then proves every complementary interval appears at a finite stage because cylinder diameters vanish uniformly. It checks endpoints, finite continued fractions, Möbius continuants, strict contraction of descendants, the unique largest primary gap and the identity between maximal gap length and Hausdorff distance. The zero-measure conclusion follows from a geometric cylinder-cover estimate. The standalone exact-fraction program verifies 2,975 finite examples without substituting them for an infinite argument.

## Originality — PASS under examined evidence
The full available HTML of Liu–Liao (arXiv:2610.14534v1) deals with survivor plateaus, bifurcation parameters and Hausdorff *dimension* but does not state the exact Euclidean gap hierarchy or sharp rescaled Hausdorff *distance*. Older Mauldin–Urbański works supply the general countable continued-fraction inverse-branch framework; this tool is fully credited. Searches under aliases such as restricted-digit continued fractions, largest complement gap, Hausdorff filling and porosity did not establish an identical formula. The 1999 full text could not be accessed; it remains a concrete residual priority risk. The conclusion is conditional on the evidence actually available, not on search silence alone.

## Value — PASS
The dimension jump from zero to one-half at the Gauss-hole boundary leaves the Euclidean geometry of the increasingly small survivor sets undetermined. The result provides all gap lengths rather than an arbitrary isolated parameter count and gives the sharp Hausdorff-metric rate at the canonical reciprocal hole thresholds. It exhibits dimension collapse and geometric filling simultaneously and supplies a complete exact invariant for a natural countable-branch fractal family.

## Limitations
Only the reciprocal thresholds and the Euclidean complement in the survivor hull are classified. Earlier exact gap formulas under equivalent arithmetic coding may exist. No independent audit has been performed.
