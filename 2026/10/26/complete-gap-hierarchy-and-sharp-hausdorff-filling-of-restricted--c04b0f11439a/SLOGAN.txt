For every integer \(m\ge2\), the Gauss-map survivor with hole \((1/m,1]\) has an explicit complete continued-fraction hierarchy of complementary gaps, with a unique largest gap. Despite every such survivor being Lebesgue-null, its rescaling by \(m\) approaches the full unit interval at the *exact* Hausdorff distance \(\frac{m(m-1)}{2(m^2+1)(m+1)}\).
