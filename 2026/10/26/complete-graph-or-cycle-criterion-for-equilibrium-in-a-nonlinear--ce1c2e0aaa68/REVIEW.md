# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The proof uses the exact single-spin rate ratio in the cited strong-dephasing population model and derives an elementary-square Kolmogorov affinity. Strict curvature makes the graph condition parameter-independent for \(J>0\), \(0<s<1\); squared-spin Fourier coefficients force true twins at every edge of degree at least three, yielding exactly cliques or degree-two cycles. Explicit stationary potentials establish the converse. A standalone finite graph test independently checked 1,099 labelled graphs through five vertices and 608 detailed-balance equations for \(K_4\), \(C_5\), and \(K_6\).

## Originality
PASS, best-knowledge. The closest source, Zhang–Chen–Kwek–Wang (arXiv:2609.19591v1, main text and supplemental Sec. S4), already gives the model, periodic-chain reversibility, complete-graph mean-field stationary laws and square-lattice currents, but not the universal graph classification. Godrèche–Bray (arXiv:0911.4011, full text) treats different directed/lattice rules. Classical four-cycle and Kolmogorov integrability methods are antecedents. The exact if-and-only-if graph family, including a minimal open-chain obstruction and reversible non-pairwise clique state, was not identified in the investigated sources and accessible research records. The companion finite-interval measurement-feedback manuscript was only accessible in abstract and describes a distinct protocol; residual literature overlap cannot be ruled out.

## Value
PASS. This classification answers a natural structural design question left open by an observed reversible one-dimensional cycle and irreversible square lattice: which finite network topologies force nonzero steady-state circulation for the same local rule? It reveals that even an open three-spin chain is irreversible, whereas arbitrarily large complete graphs can remain reversible with genuine multispin effective potentials. The distinction between multi-spin stationary structure and entropy-producing current is physically meaningful and cannot be inferred from either property alone.

## Scope and risk
The strong-dephasing population generator alone is addressed; no finite-noise quantum theorem or quantitative entropy-production bound is claimed. General rate-dependent spin-flip reversibility criteria may exist beyond inspected papers, so priority requires external scrutiny.
