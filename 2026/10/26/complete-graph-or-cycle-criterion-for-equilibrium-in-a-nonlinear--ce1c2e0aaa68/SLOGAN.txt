For the strong-dephasing, zero-field quantum-Boltzmann-sampler spin-flip rates with uniform \(J>0\) and \(0<s<1\), detailed balance holds exactly on finite graphs whose components are cliques or cycles, and fails on every other graph. A three-vertex open path already has a nonzero cycle affinity, while a four-vertex clique has a nonzero four-spin Gibbs interaction yet remains reversible.
