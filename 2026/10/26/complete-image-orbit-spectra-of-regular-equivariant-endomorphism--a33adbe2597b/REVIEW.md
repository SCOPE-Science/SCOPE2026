# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The image-orbit-count formula follows from (i) classification of equivariant orbit maps by subgroup containment, (ii) the known equal-stabilizer-preimage criterion for regularity, and (iii) a separate surjective-occupancy inclusion–exclusion for each subgroup type. Both directions of the regularity criterion and the absence of image overcounting have been checked, including zero multiplicities. A direct witness-based checker independently enumerated 3253 maps across four group-action configurations and matched every profile. The prime-cyclic asymptotic follows from a finite sum of elementary exponential limits.

## Originality — PASS within investigated coverage
The 2017 regular cellular automata result already establishes a criterion for regularity and is expressly acknowledged as antecedent. The 2022 finite-G-set result determines relative rank (minimum generators), while the 2025 cardinalities paper determines total maps and automorphisms; neither inspected full text provides the complete subgroup-refined regular-element image enumerator or the stated limiting regular density. A second 2025 study concerns Green relations and was inspected in pertinent portions. Searches for equivalent orbit-occupancy counts, stronger covering results, and existing numerical tables did not reveal a dominating prior statement, but an unindexed source remains a genuine residual risk. The novelty claim is limited to the explicit complete regular-image distribution and corollary, not the regularity criterion or generic inclusion–exclusion.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The statement solves a natural complete counting problem for the regular part of an infinite family of equivariant endomorphism monoids, retaining the full stabilizer-indexed image profile instead of a scalar cardinality. This profile distinguishes how orbit types survive under generalized inverses; the prime-cyclic limit shows that nonregularity can persist with positive asymptotic density despite all free-orbit maps being individually regular. The exact statistic is motivated by the established structural theory of equivariant endomorphism monoids, rather than by an arbitrary finite parameter choice.

The general proof requires a finite abelian group. The checker is only finite corroboration. Related structural papers may contain an equivalent calculation not found through the searches performed, and independent mathematical priority assessment is still necessary.
