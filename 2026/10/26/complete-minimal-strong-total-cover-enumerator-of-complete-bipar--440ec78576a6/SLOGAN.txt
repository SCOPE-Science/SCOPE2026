For every complete bipartite graph \(K_{m,n}\) with \(m,n\ge2\), all inclusionwise minimal strong total covers have an explicit cardinality polynomial, including exactly \(m(2^n-1)+n(2^m-1)-mn\) minimum covers. The largest minimal cover is uniquely all edges when \(m,n\ge3\), and \(\min\{m,n\}-2\) consecutive sizes immediately below the largest are impossible, giving unbounded gaps in a natural domination spectrum.
