**Correctness — PASS.** Nilpotence lifts through the nilpotent Jacobson radical; finite Artin–Wedderburn factors separate. The matrix-block restriction lemma and Fitting-based nilpotent count yield the all-length formula, and extraction of the dominant exponential successively reconstructs the entire multiset. Exact tuple tests on four ring families agree with the unrestricted proof.

**Originality — PASS in self-review.** Wästlund's 2020 rectangular two-matrix law and Dolžan's 2025 two-factor multiplication fibers are earlier coverage of the two-factor semisimple case; that case is not claimed new. Sarma–Subedi's 2026 NPP paper treats nilpotent products of two elements, and their 2025 k-factor paper treats zero products in commutative rings. The assessed contribution combines all k in arbitrary finite associative unital rings with a converse equality classification of complete probability profiles. Searches of indexed mathematical records and accessible prior results found no dominating all-k statement. Equivalent older random-matrix literature is a residual risk.

**Value — PASS.** A previously partial ring-probability invariant is determined for every finite unital ring and every product length; its exact equality classes are identified by simple-module cardinalities, separating the invariant's reach from ring isomorphism.

**Limitations.** Independence and uniformity are essential. The known two-factor and nilpotent-matrix enumerations are credited. Unindexed earlier literature may contain an equivalent all-k formula; no independent audit was performed.
