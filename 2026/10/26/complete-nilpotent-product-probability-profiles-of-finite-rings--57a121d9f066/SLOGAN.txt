For every finite associative unital ring, the entire probability sequence for nilpotence of a product of \(k\) independent uniform elements is \(\prod_i[1-(1-q_i^{-n_i})^k]\), where its semisimple quotient has factors \(M_{n_i}(\mathbb F_{q_i})\). Two such rings have identical sequences for every \(k\) precisely when their multisets of \(q_i^{n_i}\) agree, regardless of their radicals.
