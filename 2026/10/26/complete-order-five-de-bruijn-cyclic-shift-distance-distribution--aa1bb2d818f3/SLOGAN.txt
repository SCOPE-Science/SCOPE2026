Among all 2,048 binary de Bruijn cycles of order five, the minimum distance to a nontrivial rotation has exact distribution \(8:36,\ 10:114,\ 12:1212,\ 14:682,\ 16:4\). Exactly four cycles attain the optimal shift distance \(16\); they form one reversal/complement orbit and have no positive nontrivial bipolar autocorrelation.
