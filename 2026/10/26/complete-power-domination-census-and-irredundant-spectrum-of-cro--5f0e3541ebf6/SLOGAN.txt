For the crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M_n\) with \(n\ge4\), a vertex set power dominates exactly when it meets both sides or contains at least \(n-2\) vertices of one side; this yields the complete bivariate power-domination polynomial. Its inclusion-minimal sets have sizes precisely \(2\) and \(n-2\), so the upper-minus-lower power-domination gap is unbounded.
