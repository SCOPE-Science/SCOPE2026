For a projective quadrangle in \(\mathrm{PG}(2,p^2)\), ordinary colour refinement gives exactly \(4(p^2+p+1)\) stable cells: the \(2(p^2+p+1)\) Baer-subplane vertices become singletons, while every exterior cell has precisely \(p(p-1)\) vertices. The exterior point and line cells are indexed respectively by the lines and points of the unique Baer subplane.
