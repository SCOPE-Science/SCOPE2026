For every fan \(F_n=K_1\vee P_n\), \(n\ge2\), one explicit composition polynomial counts all named proper edge-colourings with rainbow diamonds (equivalently, rainbow four-cycles) at every palette size. Its sharp palette is \(3\) at \(n=2\), \(5\) at \(3\le n\le5\), and \(n\) thereafter; optimal colourings are unique modulo colour names exactly through \(n=5\), with \(16\) inequivalent optima already at \(n=6\).
