For every star-incidence poset with at least two arms, classical rowmotion has exactly one \(2\)-cycle, \(1+(3^n-3\cdot2^n+3)/3\) cycles of length \(3\), and \(2^{n-1}-1\) cycles of length \(8\). The complete distribution follows from a ternary-state orbit decomposition and refines the previously studied period-only behavior.
