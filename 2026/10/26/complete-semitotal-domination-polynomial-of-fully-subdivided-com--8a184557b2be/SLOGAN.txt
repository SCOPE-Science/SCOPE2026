All semitotal dominating sets of the full subdivision \(S(K_n)\), for \(n\ge3\), admit a complete cardinality enumerator determined by the omitted original vertices and a single cross-edge witness condition. The minimum weight \(n\) was previously known, but there are exactly \(1+n(n-1)^2+\binom n3-4\mathbf1_{\{n=4\}}\) minimum sets, and the formula counts every larger cardinality as well.
