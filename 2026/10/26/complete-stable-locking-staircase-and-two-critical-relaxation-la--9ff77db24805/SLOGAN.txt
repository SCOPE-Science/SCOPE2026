For an attractive \(2m\)-oscillator Kuramoto cycle with alternating natural frequencies \(\pm a\), every linearly stable phase-locked state is completely determined by a winding \(q\), and it exists exactly above \(K_q=a/[1+\cos(2\pi q/m)]\); no stable state with an obtuse edge can occur. The exact relaxation gap turns on as a square root for zero winding but linearly for every nonzero winding, producing two distinct critical-slowing laws in one natural network family.
