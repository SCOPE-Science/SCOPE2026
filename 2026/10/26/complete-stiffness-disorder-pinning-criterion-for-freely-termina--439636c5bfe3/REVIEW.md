## Correctness — PASS
For every strictly positive chain, the generalized vibration pencil is an irreducible symmetric Jacobi problem with simple eigenvalues. Hellmann–Feynman gives exactly \(\partial_{b_i}\lambda=(u_{B_i}-u_{A_{i+1}})^2\) under mass normalization. At a simultaneous stationary point every external bond is unstretched; local optical equations and nonvanishing propagation force the same optical eigenvalue in every isolated dimer. Conversely the explicit recurrence makes each external bond unstretched for **every** positive coupling, yielding the same exact squared eigenfrequency and unchanged eigenvector. Discrete Sturm node counting yields the ordinal \(N+1\), without a uniform spectral-gap assumption. The standalone finite-matrix checker passed 5,500 categorized tests. The proof makes the universal claims; finite experiments do not.

## Originality — PASS, with named residual risks
Wallis (1957) already established a free-end surface mode for an alternating-mass, uniform-spring chain; Allein et al. (2023) analyzed its hidden strain-coordinate symmetry; Vishwakarma et al. (2025) proved robustness for specified symmetry-preserving correlated parameter changes. None of these special or constrained cases is presented as new. The submitted claim is exactly the necessary-and-sufficient condition for a pinned positive mode under **all independent positive intercell spring changes** with completely heterogeneous dimer masses and internal springs, the equivalence with simultaneous derivative zero at one point, and the exact \((N+1)\)-st spectral index. Full primary text was inspected for the relevant 2023, 2025, 2014 and 2026 works; Wallis's 1957 publication date and abstract were verified, but its full article was unavailable. The 2023 supplement was not independently inspected. General symmetry results could imply a related special case beyond the bounded searches, so historical priority is not conclusively certified. ## Mathematical value — PASS
Arbitrarily large independent changes to connectors of a finite vibration chain normally affect mode frequencies. The theorem instead gives the complete exact design criterion for an unchanged positive eigenfrequency and mode profile, with all local stationary points being globally invariant. Its fixed spectral rank and tunable mass-ratio envelope are useful structural facts for localized vibration design rather than merely a computed example. It claims no broad experimental robustness, bulk topology, or uniform spectral gap.

## Limits and reproducibility
Results require finite freely terminated nearest-neighbour scalar chains and strictly positive parameters. Zero coupling disconnects the chain; grounding, damping, nonlinear motions and perturbations violating local optical matching are outside scope. Use `python verify.py` for randomized matrix checks; analytic details are in `RESULT.md`. Historical priority uncertainty remains, and no independent scientific audit was performed.
