A positive squared vibration frequency of a freely terminated heterogeneous mass-dimer chain is insensitive to every independent inter-dimer spring stiffness if and only if all isolated dimers share one optical squared frequency \(\Lambda\). That exact line is always the simple \((N+1)\)-st eigenvalue of the \(2N\)-mass chain, and its entire zero-inter-dimer-strain mode profile is unchanged under arbitrary positive coupling disorder.
