The full length-and-index distribution of subsequence-universal reverse-complement palindromes has an explicit rational generating function on every even-sized, fixed-point-free complemented alphabet. The shortest such words attaining index \(h\) have length exactly \(qh\), and their number is \((q!)^{\lfloor h/2\rfloor}\) times \(1\) for even \(h\) or \(2^{q/2}(q/2)!\) for odd \(h\).
