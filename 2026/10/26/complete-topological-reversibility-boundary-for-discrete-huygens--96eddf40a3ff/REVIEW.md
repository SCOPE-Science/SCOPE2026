# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The monotonicity proof uses the strict sine-secant bound on every nonzero edge difference. Spectral control extends injectivity to the equality endpoint, even though the explicit parity configurations make its differential singular. Above the threshold, the closure-respecting cosine configurations have one negative Jacobian eigenvalue: the even-cycle top mode is simple, while the odd-cycle double top mode is reduced by a positive rank-one edge correction. Degree \(+1\) then forces at least three regular inverse images for an open set of target points. The numerical script checks 228 samples, but the universal claim rests on the explicit analytic argument.

## Originality — PASS under examined evidence
The closest primary literature is Buescu--Oliveira (arXiv:2610.19953v1, Theorem 2.1) and Buescu--D'Aniello--Oliveira (arXiv:2610.10993v1, Theorem 3.2 and Remark 3.3). Those papers give sufficient spectral invertibility and almost-global convergence on bounded parameter ranges, and optimality for trees. Local singularity/three-branch calculations near the first threshold do not entail continued noninjectivity at arbitrarily large coupling. The new contribution is the complete closed-threshold homeomorphism classification and a regular three-preimage open target set for **every** supercritical parameter. Earlier equivalent work under other terminology remains a historical-priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The complete all-coupling reversibility boundary is a natural dynamical-system invariant. The endpoint displays singular Jacobian without loss of topological reversibility, whereas all larger parameters have robust multi-valued inverse dynamics. This distinction directly addresses the gap between a sufficient diffeomorphism range and the global loss of injectivity; it works uniformly for odd and even rings, without any restriction to weakly nonlinear or Lyapunov-stable coupling.

## Limits
Only unweighted finite cycles are treated. The theorem does not count every inverse fibre, determine all singular sets above threshold, or classify long-time attractors. Independent historical-priority and mathematical audits remain to be performed.
