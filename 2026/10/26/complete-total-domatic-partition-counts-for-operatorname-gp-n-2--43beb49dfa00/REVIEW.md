# Self-review

**Correctness (PASS).** The two-class bijection with closed walks in the explicitly constructed 256-state transfer graph holds for every simple \(\operatorname{GP}(n,2)\), \(n\ge5\); the finite determinant identity and exact Newton computation establish the all-order generating function. All 256 integer determinant coefficients were checked, including zeros beyond degree 64. Independent direct enumeration agrees for \(5\le n\le9\). The three-class recurrence over \(\mathbb F_3\), its period-30 consequence, and exhaustive 729-case inequality check prove exactly six named assignments and therefore one unnamed partition when \(3\mid n\).

**Originality (PASS, with historical risk).** Published work establishes the total-domatic threshold and generalized Petersen three-balanced existence, not the full two-class counting series. The closest accessible predecessors are Zelinka (2002), Zhao et al. (2020), Minyard–Sepanski (2024/2026), and Choi–Kwon–Park (2026). published-finding corpus searches for the family, aliases, enumerator, transfer polynomial, exact initial values, and the equivalent two-coupon formulation revealed no prior statement that implies the enumerator. Absence of a search hit alone cannot establish global priority; unindexed enumerations remain a risk.

**Value (PASS).** \(\operatorname{GP}(n,2)\) is a canonical, nontrivial, infinite cubic graph family already studied for total-domatic invariants. The exact full distribution over all possible numbers of total-dominating classes replaces a yes/no parameter by a sequence, a degree-64 rational generating function, and rigidity of the top class; these are not read off from previous threshold theorems. Counting all feasible two-class partitions is relevant when studying multiplicity and robustness of such decompositions, rather than a chosen artificial subcase.

The factorization is computer-assisted with a small, transparent exact checker; there is no claim for other generalized Petersen step sizes or for arbitrarily many total-dominating sets. Independent historical and mathematical validation remains outstanding.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
