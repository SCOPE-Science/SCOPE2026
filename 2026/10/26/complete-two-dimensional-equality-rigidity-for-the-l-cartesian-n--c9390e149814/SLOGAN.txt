For every complex \(2\times2\) matrix, equality in the new operator-sum lower bound for the \(L^2\)-Cartesian numerical radius holds exactly for traceless matrices or phase multiples of Hermitian matrices; equality at the upper numerical-radius bound holds exactly for square-zero matrices. Every traceless matrix moreover satisfies \(\Omega_2(T)=\|T\|_{\mathrm{HS}}/2\), exposing two distinct mechanisms for lower sharpness.
