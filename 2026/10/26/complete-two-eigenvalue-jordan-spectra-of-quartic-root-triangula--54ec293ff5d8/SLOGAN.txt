The complete two-eigenvalue Jordan-type distribution for upper-triangular solutions of \(A^2(A-I)^2=0\) is a weighted convolution of the classical square-zero rank polynomials: the coefficient for one-eigenspace dimension \(a\) and size-two block counts \(i,j\) is \(\binom na q^{a(n-a)}f_{a,i}(q)f_{n-a,j}(q)\). A canonical polynomial idempotent makes this valid over every finite field, including characteristic two.
