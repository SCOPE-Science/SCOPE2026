For every triangular graph \(J(n,2)\) with \(n\ge5\), the damage from any two unit-edge failures falls into five exact classes with a strict universal ordering. The uniquely worst *structural type* is two edges in a common triangle, realized by exactly \(n(n-1)(n-2)^2/2\) unordered failure pairs; this reveals a second-failure vulnerability that single-edge symmetry cannot distinguish.
