# Scientific review: complete two-local symmetry census

## Correctness

PASS. The ordinary derivation/automorphism formulas follow from the defining bracket. Two-point interpolation proves the necessary homogeneous and projective/fibre constraints. Conversely, interpolation for independent quotient vectors, dependent quotient but independent Lie-algebra vectors, and dependent pairs constructs genuine trace- or determinant-constrained linear witnesses in every field characteristic. The projective-point counting argument yields both exact cardinalities. `verify.py` directly tests all classified maps and all pairs over the binary field; ternary pair witnesses are checked for 150 samples per family. These finite tests are corroboration, not a proof for all finite fields.

## Originality

PASS relative to inspected sources, with stated risk. The 2015 and 2016 Lie-algebra papers and 2017 Leibniz-algebra paper prove existence of exceptional two-local maps or give explicit homogeneous central families, not the complete arbitrary-map finite-field structural classification. The 2025 Heisenberg paper explicitly restricts its two-local treatment to linear automorphisms, an already studied subproblem. Prior linear-local Heisenberg derivation and automorphism results are not claimed as new. A full-text comparison of the 2015 derivation paper and 2025 Heisenberg paper was unavailable; equivalently phrased results under alternative terminology remain a publication-priority risk.

## Mathematical value

PASS. Two-local symmetries are a natural Lie-theoretic local-to-global invariant. For the first nonabelian nilpotent Lie algebra, this result determines **every** nonadditive two-local derivation and automorphism, not just isolated counterexamples, and yields exact cardinalities for every finite field. The explicit projective-line and vertical-fibre decomposition is a structural classification, and it exhibits the large gap between ordinary linear symmetries and full two-local symmetry sets.

## Scope and limitations

All finite fields, including characteristic two, are proved. No infinite-field cardinality theorem or exhaustive publication-priority claim is made. The substantive count formulas are established by proof; the verifier covers specified finite test instances only. Independent review has not occurred.
