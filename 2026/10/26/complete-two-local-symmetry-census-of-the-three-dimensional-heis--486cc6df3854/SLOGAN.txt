Every two-local derivation of the three-dimensional Heisenberg Lie algebra over \(\mathbb F_q\) is determined by two freely chosen homogeneous functions, giving exactly \(q^{q^2+3q+3}\) maps. Every two-local automorphism is determined by a permutation and rescalings of the projective quotient lines, independent vertical fibre permutations, and one central multiplier, giving exactly \((q+1)!(q-1)^{q+2}(q!)^{q+1}\) maps, even without an additivity assumption.
