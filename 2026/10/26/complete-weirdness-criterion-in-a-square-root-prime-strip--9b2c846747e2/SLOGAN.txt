For primes \(p=M+x<q\), where \(M=2^{k+1}-1\) and \((x-1)^2\le M\), weirdness of \(2^kpq\) is exactly one short modular-residue condition together with two rational interval bounds on \(q\). Every weird number in this growing prime strip has abundance smaller than \(q\), proving the conjectured one-third-scale abundance bound on the whole strip.
