## Originality — PASS (best-knowledge)

The motivating primary study (Chattopadhyay–Pendse–Gupta, arXiv:2610.19904v1, Sections III.2–III.3) already obtains the stationary qutrit matrix and Wigner formula and demonstrates selected negative plots. The accepted claim does not reproduce those as discoveries: it establishes a globally exhaustive parameter-and-topology diagram, sharp tangency/splitting transitions, and the nonclassicality blind spot of angular averaging. Ben Arosh–Cross–Lifshitz (arXiv:2011.02706) investigates a distinct oscillator family, without this truncated model's global diagram. Kenfack–Życzkowski (arXiv:quant-ph/0406015) provides the general negativity witness, not a model-specific Lindblad classification. Indexed related results on vacuum–Fock coherence investigate a different family without the strictly positive one-photon population and these dissipation parameters. Source coverage cannot establish literature-wide priority; a more general uninspected theorem is a residual risk.

## Value — PASS

The Wigner-negative set is a direct phase-space signature of nonclassicality and its location and connectivity matter for where quadrature-sensitive measurements would detect negativity. The two distinct rate-path transitions explain the qualitative onset and fragmentation of nonclassical patches beyond isolated plots. The exact radial blind spot shows that a natural summary observable can remain nonnegative despite locally negative phase-space regions, furnishing a physically interpretable warning about angular averaging. This is a complete natural parameter classification rather than an arbitrary finite slice or mere reproduction of an existing figure.

## Limitations

The rates are positive, the Hilbert space is the truncated qutrit, and the Wigner representation is the conventional infinite-Fock embedding. The result does not establish claims about the full nonlinear oscillator, measurement statistics of a discrete qutrit Wigner function, or other witnesses of quantum nonclassicality. An independently established literature-priority assessment is absent.
