For the stationary Arosh qutrit, Wigner negativity is either absent, split into two antipodal lobes, or a single annulus, with sharp inequalities in the two dissipative rates. Equal pumping and loss rates exhibit an exact negativity onset that is completely invisible to the angularly averaged Wigner function, whereas the rate ratio \(2:1\) produces an exact annulus-to-two-lobes transition.
