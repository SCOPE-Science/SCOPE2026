## correctness

PASS

Cyclic trigram multiplicities are exactly integer balanced connected Eulerian edge-multisets of the binary order-two de Bruijn graph. Vertex flow balance gives the unique vector (a,x,t-y,y,x,t-x,y,h), with t≥1,0≤x,y≤t and loop-connectivity restrictions (x=0⇒a=0),(y=0⇒h=0). For given zero/one composition, p=t+x+a and q=t+y+h; independent x,a and y,h choices count F_t(p)F_t(q), F_t(s)=1_{s=t}+max(0,min(t,s-t)). Monochromatic cases have one spectrum. The rational bivariate series follows by summing the finite geometric factors, and its diagonal has a single pole of maximal order five at z=1 with coefficient 1/12, yielding P_n∼n^4/288. Independent full enumeration of all labelled binary circular words through length 11 matched the fixed-composition formula and totals (2,3,4,6,8,13,18,27,38,53,72); this does not replace the all-length Eulerian proof.
