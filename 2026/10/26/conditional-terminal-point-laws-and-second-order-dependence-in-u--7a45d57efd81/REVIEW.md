# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem fixes the uniform mapping sample space and exact definitions of cyclic/terminal vertices. The conditional decomposition into a permutation on the prescribed cyclic set and a uniform rooted forest is supported by Pitman's conditional-root formula. A direct directed matrix-tree determinant gives \(k n^{n-k-1}\) such forests, and deletion of prescribed terminal vertices gives the ratio \((1-r/n)^{n-k-1}\). These establish every conditional factorial moment, with the all-cyclic boundary handled separately. A monotone regression argument establishes strict finite covariance negativity; a separate terminal-vertex deletion argument proves the exact covariance identity. Gaussian-dominated Rayleigh limits and a uniform second-order expansion prove the covariance and correlation constants. Exact rational exhaustive enumeration of all mappings through seven vertices independently checks the finite claims. Limitations: exhaustive enumeration is finite; the asymptotic and unrestricted sign assertions rely on the proofs.

## Originality — PASS on inspected evidence

The closest classical source, Pitman (2001), already supplies the uniform conditional forest representation, so that structural decomposition is expressly not claimed new. Flajolet–Odlyzko (1990) give cyclic-point and terminal-point marginal expectations through separate marking schemes. Hansen–Jaworski (2010) condition on cyclic counts but investigate component sizes; Mutafchiev–Finch (2023) investigate the deepest cycle. Drmota (1995) studies sizes of distance-to-cycle strata, not indegree-zero points. The newer term *terminal closer* refers to a different statistic. None of the accessible inspected primary material states the mixed conditional terminal factorial moments together with the finite strict sign, the order-one conditional regression, or the sharp mixed covariance constant, nor presents an implication that settles that combination without fresh counting and asymptotic comparison. General enumerators may imply portions by specialization; therefore priority of the complete combined result remains provisional, not independently certified. Mylläri's work on leaves in forests was available only at abstract level and is explicitly a residual risk.

## Value — PASS

The two statistics measure complementary features of a random functional graph: recurrent (cycle) mass and source/terminal mass. Their separate marginal scales, order \(\sqrt n\) and \(n\), do not determine their dependence. The proved cancellation of the apparent order-\(\sqrt n\) conditional mean shift, the universal order-one regression profile across the typical cycle scale, strict finite anticorrelation, and sharp nonzero mixed-covariance constant are mathematically informative for functional-graph sampling and calibrated joint summaries. This is a specific structural dependence result, not a generic restatement of tree counting or an arbitrary finite enumeration.
