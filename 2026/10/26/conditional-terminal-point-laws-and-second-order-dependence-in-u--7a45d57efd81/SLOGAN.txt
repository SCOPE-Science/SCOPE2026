In a uniform random mapping, conditioning on \(k\) cyclic vertices gives an exact factorial-moment law for the number of indegree-zero vertices, and the conditional mean decreases strictly with \(k\). Despite \(\Theta(\sqrt n)\) fluctuations in the cyclic count, the mean terminal count shifts by only order one, yielding \(\operatorname{Cov}(C_n,Z_n)\sim-\sqrt{\pi/2}\,\sqrt n/(2e)\) and a correlation of order \(-n^{-1/2}\).
