For a fair random tournament, conditioning on one player's score gives exact formulas for both the mean and variance of the total number of circular triads. Among every additive score diagnostic \(\sum_v f(S_v)\), its covariance with the circular-triad count is determined solely by the degree-two Krawtchouk component; covariance with the number of players scoring exactly \(k\) changes sign across \(|2k-(n-1)|=\sqrt{n-1}\), with exact zeros precisely at square \(n-1\).
