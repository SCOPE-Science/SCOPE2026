For arithmetic overflow semirings \(\mathbb N_0\uplus_{\mathrm{ord}}\{\omega_1<\cdots<\omega_k\}\), every proper congruence either separates the integer and overflow parts or absorbs a complete integer tail into the lowest overflow block. There are exactly \(2^{k-1}\) infinite-index congruences, and the numbers of finite-index congruences have generating function \(z+z^2(1+z)^{k-1}/(1-z)^2\).
