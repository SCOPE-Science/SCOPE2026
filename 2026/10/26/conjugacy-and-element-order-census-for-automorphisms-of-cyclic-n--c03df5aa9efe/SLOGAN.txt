For the standard nilpotent cyclic Leibniz algebra \(L_n(\mathbb F_q)\), an exact double divisor sum gives every conjugacy-class count of its automorphism group, and a second explicit formula gives the number of automorphisms of each possible order \(dp^r\). Both formulas hold for all dimensions \(n\ge2\) and all finite fields, with the conjugacy and order stratifications controlled by truncated polynomial norms and coefficient divisibility.
