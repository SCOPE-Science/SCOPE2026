# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The result is an all-prime, all-\(b\ge2\) theorem with a complete constrained-matrix parametrization. Comparing the four matrix entries in their correct quotient rings gives exactly three equations. The three cases for the residue-diagonal difference and the off-diagonal vector are exhaustive and disjoint. Their centralizer orders and element multiplicities yield precisely the asserted class-size counts. The identity \(\operatorname{cp}(G)=k(G)/|G|\) then gives the constant commuting probability. The standalone checker independently enumerates all elements and centralizers in seven small instances. Limitation: enumeration is not the infinite proof, which rests on the symbolic equations.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk
Hillar–Rhea's inspected complete 2006 paper gives the automorphism matrix representation and total group order, but no internal class equation. Mann's inspected complete 2008 paper gives constraints on possible class sizes in general groups, not the present family-wise counts. Searches for the same group under additive-cyclic and integer-module notations, direct conjugacy-class wording, and the equivalent commuting-probability formula did not identify an explicit stronger covering statement. Prior counts of automorphisms with given fixed-point sets, and of involutions, do not imply the new full class-size distribution. Ranum's 1907 paper is directly related to the representation of these automorphism groups but its full text was not accessible here; indirect coverage by it or other older work remains possible.

## Value — PASS
The class equation is a complete natural invariant of each group in an infinite, arithmetic family rather than a sample of individual automorphisms. It determines the number of irreducible complex characters and every possible orbit size under inner conjugation, and it exhibits exact invariance of the commuting probability across arbitrarily large exponent gaps. The prime-two endpoint attains the classical maximal commuting probability \(5/8\) for nonabelian finite groups and has centre quotient \(C_2\oplus C_2\). This is a motivated invariant beyond a routine restatement of the known automorphism-group order; it does not claim a general solution for all rank-two invariant factors.
