For every prime \(p\) and \(b\ge2\), the automorphism group of \(C_p\oplus C_{p^b}\) has only conjugacy-class sizes \(1\), \(p(p-1)\), and \(p^2\), with explicit multiplicities. Its commuting probability is exactly \((p^2+1)/(p^3(p-1))\), independent of the larger cyclic exponent; in particular it is \(5/8\) for every binary member of the family.
