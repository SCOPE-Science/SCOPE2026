# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The key one-vertex-elimination lemma has an exact proof: a vertex of degree at most two lies in at most one triangle, so every valid coloring of the reduced graph has a nonempty connected set of extensions. For any two reduced colorings differing at one vertex, the sets of permitted colors for the eliminated vertex intersect; otherwise both its triangle neighbors would have to change at once. Lifting a connected recoloring path proves global connectivity for every finite \(2\)-degenerate graph. A path to the complementary coloring necessarily attains the parity-optimal color-class discrepancy. The independent-set join families are established by an exhaustive mathematical classification of all hub cases, including six isolated states in the \(3\)-degenerate obstruction and two cubes linked at their two antipodal corners in the book case. Finite checking alone is not treated as a proof for unbounded graphs.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The 2017 and 2021 no-monochromatic-triangle papers by Karpiński and Piecuch prove existence and give algorithms, not connectivity of *every* feasible two-coloring. Asayama and Matsumoto (2022) already give balanced polychromatic two-colorings for three-colorable triangulations, and the balance property by itself also follows straightforwardly from any proper three-coloring by splitting its third independent class. No originality is assigned to the balance conclusion alone. The motivating 2026 triangulation-discrepancy work of Arevalo Loyola and coauthors treats discrepancy bounds on general triangulations, but its compared statements do not establish all-solution connectivity for \(2\)-degenerate graphs. Related proper-coloring recoloring and triangulation-flip papers have different states and operations. Searches also considered equivalent Property-B and outerplanar formulations, the exact book graph, and the sharp degeneracy-three obstruction. No stronger implication or exact prior formula was found among inspected material. Remaining risks include earlier generic constraint-satisfaction reconfiguration theorems and alternate terminology; no historical-priority guarantee or independent check is claimed.

## Value — PASS

The theorem upgrades ordinary existence and balanced attainability to complete connectedness of the legitimate recoloring space for a broad natural class containing maximal outerplanar graphs. The balanced-coloring consequence illustrates its robustness but is not in itself the novelty claim. The precise degeneracy-three obstruction shows the boundary cannot be improved in general, while an exact book-state graph, coloring polynomial and diameter provide structural information beyond existence. The question is motivated by the discrepancy gap in recent triangulation work and by the algorithmic interest in reconfiguration of constrained colorings.
