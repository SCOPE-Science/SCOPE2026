The modal subalgebras of an \(n\)-world reflexive linear frame are exactly the unions of consecutive blocks, forming a Boolean lattice on its \(n-1\) cuts. A random \(k\)-generator tuple produces a subalgebra with one atom per run of its binary membership colors; the number of collapsed boundaries is exactly binomial and converges to a Poisson law when \(n/2^k\) has a finite limit.
