# Review

## Correctness

PASS. The proof identifies the image of entrywise Frobenius on \(\mathrm{PGL}_n(k)\) with \(\mathrm{PGL}_n(k^p)\), proves that the coordinate-span homothety class is invariant, and proves the converse by relating two surjective linear maps onto the same \(k^p\)-space. The perfect/imperfect dichotomy follows from the same invariant.

## Originality

PASS under the available literature comparison. The recent source discusses failures of transitivity over imperfect fields and leaves general orbit descriptions as an open direction; the inspected text does not state this exact coordinate-span parametrization. Three targeted literature-record searches returned no matching orbit classification. The detailed example section of the primary source was not available for inspection, so a possible special-case overlap remains a residual risk rather than a claim of definitive novelty.

## Value

PASS. The result is a complete classification of a natural rational-point orbit invariant for a standard Frobenius-twisted projective-space action. It gives an exact criterion for transitivity and explains the perfectness boundary, rather than merely exhibiting two non-equivalent points.
