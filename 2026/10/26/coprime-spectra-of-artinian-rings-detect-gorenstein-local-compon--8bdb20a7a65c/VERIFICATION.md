# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

Reconstructed the defining ideal implication, including the zero ideal, field factors, socle essentiality and product annihilators. In a local Artinian ring a socle line L has ann(L)=m. For a candidate nonzero C not equal to the sole socle line, choose L with C not contained in L; I=L,J=mC give C⊆(J:ann(I)), C⊄I and C⊄J by Nakayama. A one-dimensional socle S belongs to every nonzero ideal; if I=0 then [0:J]=J, proving S is coprime. A multi-coordinate C is excluded by complementary idempotents I=e_iR,J=(1-e_i)R, for which [I:J]=R. Single-coordinate reduction and finite discrete topology complete all quantifiers. Independent direct ideal enumeration on eight integer quotient rings and six ideals of square-zero F2[x,y]/(x,y)^2 corroborates, but does not prove the general theorem.

## originality

PASS

The source paper defining coprime ideals proves CoSp ≅ Spec on dual rings and exhibits a square-zero negative example, but its accessible statement does not establish complete non-dual local exclusion or mixed-product Gorenstein locus. The general Artinian decomposition and socle criterion are standard, yet do not alone prove the nonstandard coproduct condition; the local Nakayama witness provides the missing implication.

## value

PASS

Coprime spectrum is a recently defined invariant whose relationship to ordinary prime spectrum outside the dual/Gorenstein class is genuinely natural. This fully determines the spectrum for every commutative Artinian ring, detects precisely Gorenstein local components, explains emptiness and mixed products, and is not just a negative tiny example.
