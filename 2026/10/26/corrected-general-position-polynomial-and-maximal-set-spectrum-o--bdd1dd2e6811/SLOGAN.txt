For \(n\ge3\), every general-position set of the crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M_n\) either lies entirely in one bipartition side or has at most two vertices, so its exact general-position polynomial is \(2(1+z)^n-1+n^2z^2\). Its only inclusion-maximal sizes are \(2\) and \(n\), and the formula identifies a concrete overcount in the published \(T^*(r,2)\) polynomial, already visible in the six-cycle.
