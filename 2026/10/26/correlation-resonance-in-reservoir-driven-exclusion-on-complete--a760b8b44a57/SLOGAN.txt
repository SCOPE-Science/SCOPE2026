In an open symmetric-exclusion gas on a complete bipartite graph, every distinct-site stationary covariance is explicitly negative. At fixed source-to-sink rate ratio, the cross-part anticorrelation has exactly one maximizing reservoir speed; on balanced networks the optimum is asymptotic to \(\frac{1+\sqrt3}{2}N\), with a universal peak covariance magnitude \(\frac{\sqrt3}{18N}\).
