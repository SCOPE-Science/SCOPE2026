The cospectral two-token graphs of the rook graph \(K_4\square K_4\) and the Shrikhande graph both have independence number \(32\), but possess respectively \(144\) and \(54\) maximum independent sets. Every maximizer yields either \(2K_{4,4}\) or the independent two-fold blowup \(C_8[\overline{K_2}]\), with a complete automorphism-orbit classification.
