# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The necessity of transitivity follows from the universally valid operator inequality \(\omega^2(A)\subseteq\omega(A)\), transferred to the injective inverse-image Boolean algebra. A source-frame loop similarly creates a nonempty closed \(\omega\)-perfect set, prohibiting an \(\omega\)-scattered host. For sufficiency, finite transitive relations split into strong-accessibility clusters, ordered strictly. The explicit poset on a countable copy of each world makes every inverse-image successor set either finite or infinite exactly according to the original Kripke edge. The proof handles reflexive cycles, isolated irreflexive points, and empty relations separately. An independent finite-model checker of the key exact counting identity exhausts all \(4180\) transitive relations of size at most four. The unrestricted result follows from the written symbolic proof, not the experiments.

## Originality — PASS within inspected sources

The motivating source proves \(\mathsf{K4}\)/\(\mathsf{GL}\) completeness for classes of infinite spaces through metric derivative-map representations, not the if-and-only-if embedding theorem for arbitrary finite frames, the countable \(T_0\) Alexandrov model, or the sharp one-space cardinality conclusion. The previously established Alexandrov/interior semantics applies to a different modality and starts with reflexive-transitive relations, whereas the present \(\omega\)-construction covers all and only transitive relations. Indexed published findings on a countable indiscrete \(\mathsf{KD45}\) halo and other modal algebras have different operators or different representation requirements and do not imply the claim. Remaining priority risk is an older equivalent blow-up construction not captured by searched terminology.

## Value — PASS

The result supplies an exact representability criterion rather than an isolated example or routine census; it shows why transitivity is the complete obstruction and why \(\omega\)-scatteredness cuts the finite class down precisely to irreflexive frames. The explicit one-countable-space completeness models and sharp impossibility of finite carriers strengthen the abstract class-based results and give reusable semantic constructions for normal modal logics.
