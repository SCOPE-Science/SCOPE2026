A finite Kripke relation admits a full complex-algebra embedding into topological \(\omega\)-accumulation semantics precisely when it is transitive; it admits an \(\omega\)-scattered representation precisely when it is also irreflexive. Explicit countable \(T_0\) Alexandrov realizations give single countable spaces with exact modal logics \(\mathsf{K4}\) and \(\mathsf{GL}\), and countability is optimal.
