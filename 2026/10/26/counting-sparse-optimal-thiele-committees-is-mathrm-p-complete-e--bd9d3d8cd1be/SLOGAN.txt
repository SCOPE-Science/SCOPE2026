In the cubic-bipartite approval construction, every exactly optimal PAV committee is a perfect matching and has score \(2n\). Counting those committees is \(\#\mathrm{P}\)-complete although their score is known and one can be found in polynomial time; the correspondence extends to any fixed rational Thiele score satisfying \(u(2)<2u(1)\) and \(u(3)<3u(1)\).
