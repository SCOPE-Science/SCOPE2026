A uniformly random square-zero operator over a fixed finite field, probed by an independent random half-dimensional subspace, has an explicit three-stage limiting homology-detection law. The first-order event that the probe contains a nonbounding cycle has different limiting probabilities in even and odd ambient dimensions (approximately \(0.337416\) and \(0.436153\) over \(\mathbb F_2\)), so full-sequence first-order convergence fails.
