In a two-clique discrete-time SIS mean-field network, weak infection spillover into a locally critical community creates prevalence proportional to \(\sqrt{\varepsilon}\), rather than the \(\varepsilon\) law below threshold. The complete near-threshold crossover has the exact positive-root profile \(Z(c)=(\delta c+\sqrt{\delta^2c^2+4qB})/(2q)\), with global uniqueness and attraction for every positive cross-coupling.
