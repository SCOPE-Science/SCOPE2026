# Review

## Correctness

The proof conditions on the number of failed edges, uses the Poisson limit for \(M_n\), and identifies the seed's component as a size-biased Dirichlet spacing. The Poisson mixture yields the stated density and atom. The CDF, stochastic order, second moment, and edge-cost normalization are checked analytically. The result is a limit theorem; no finite computation is used to infer an infinite claim.

## Originality

The inspected network-formation paper establishes equilibrium sparsity and welfare bounds but does not state the near-one transmission scaling law. Published percolation work covers exact finite-graph component distributions and first/second moments on rings. Those results are close and limit novelty claims about moments; the claim retained here is the explicit limiting distribution, including its atom and stochastic ordering. The inaccessible full text of the ring-moment paper remains a residual risk.

## Value

The law describes both the probability of total cascade and the full distribution of the surviving network fraction in a natural critical window. This is a resilience benchmark richer than an expected-welfare estimate alone. The result does not assert that the cycle is an equilibrium.
