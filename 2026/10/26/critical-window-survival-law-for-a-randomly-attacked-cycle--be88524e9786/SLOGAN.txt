For a cycle with edge-transmission probability \(1-\lambda/n\), the surviving fraction has a limiting law with atom \((1+\lambda)e^{-\lambda}\) at zero and density \(\lambda^2(1-y)e^{-\lambda(1-y)}\) on \((0,1)\). The law shifts stochastically toward larger surviving fractions as \(\lambda\) increases, and its second moment gives the asymptotic connectivity-welfare fraction.
