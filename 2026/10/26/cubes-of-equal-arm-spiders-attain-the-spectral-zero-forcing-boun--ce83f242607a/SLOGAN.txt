For every equal-arm spider \(S_m(\ell)\) with \(m\ge2\) and \(\ell\ge4\), the ordinary cube has exactly \(Z(S_m(\ell)^3)=M(S_m(\ell)^3)=3(m-1)\). A cubic polynomial in the tree adjacency matrix gives the matching real-nullity lower bound, resolving the third-power case of the 2026 spider-power conjecture.
