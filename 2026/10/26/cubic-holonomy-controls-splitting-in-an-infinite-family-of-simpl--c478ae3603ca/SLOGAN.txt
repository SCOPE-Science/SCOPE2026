For weighted doubled-cycle evolution algebras, a single cubic holonomy \(P\) determines whether the full automorphism group is \(C_3\times C_n\) or \(C_{3n}\); the central extension splits precisely when \(P=1\) or \(3\nmid n\). This yields simple nonsplit examples in every dimension \(2n\) with \(3\mid n\), extending the known six-dimensional instance.
