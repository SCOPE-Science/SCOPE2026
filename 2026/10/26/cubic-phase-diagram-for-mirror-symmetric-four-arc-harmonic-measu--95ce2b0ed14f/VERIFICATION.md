# Reproducible verification

Install `requirements.txt` and execute `python check.py` in the directory containing the two files. The recorded output is `check_output.txt`.

The checker establishes the exact Joukowski and reciprocal-polynomial identities symbolically; two exact cubic discriminants of opposite sign; strict geometric arc separation for both examples; three disk-mapped critical roots in each case, classified as real/nonreal as predicted; an exact third-order root in the square configuration; and two high-precision Fourier/rational comparisons. These finite numerical instances are not used to infer the theorem for all admissible angles. That step rests on the analytic formula, boundary nonvanishing, reciprocal root pairing, and the real cubic discriminant theorem.

The checked statements do not imply a comprehensive classification of arbitrary four arcs or of harmonic-measure level sets. Full historical priority against Walsh's older unpublished-in-full accessible material is not resolved. No independent mathematical audit was performed.
