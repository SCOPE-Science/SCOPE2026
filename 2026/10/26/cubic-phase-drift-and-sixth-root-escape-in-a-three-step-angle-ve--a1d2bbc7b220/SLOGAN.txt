For the three-step rational-angle case of the Khandelwal–Mukherjee velocity map, the exact periodic phase grid is the entire high-energy period-three locus: the return drift has sign \(-\operatorname{sgn}(P^3\sin3\theta)\) off that grid. Escaping phases obey the sharp sixth-power law \(\omega_{3m}^{6}/m\to-9P^3\sin3\theta\), while descending phases have a matching cubic-in-squared-speed first-passage time.
