On the Fermat quartic \(X^4+Y^4+Z^4=0\) over \(\mathbb F_{625}\), precisely \(80\) of its \(656\) torus points have a cubic coordinate product; the two nontrivial cubic classes occur \(288\) times each. The cube locus consists of exactly five diagonal-symmetry orbits, and the associated cyclic triple cover has genus \(19\) and \(252\) rational points.
