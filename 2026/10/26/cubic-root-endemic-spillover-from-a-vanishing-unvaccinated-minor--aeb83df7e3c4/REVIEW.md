# Scientific review: cubic-root endemic spillover

## Correctness

PASS. The proof derives the stationary logistic fixed-point equations directly from the six SIQS compartments. Positivity, exact determinant one, trace above two, Perron subsolution and strict subhomogeneity establish unique endemic existence for every positive minority fraction. A nonsingular implicit-function argument proves the cubic-root/two-thirds-power asymptotics with explicit constants. Reconstruction conserves the two group totals and satisfies the full original stationary system. The standalone checker passed exact matrix identities and high-precision original-equation residuals; finite calculations are not used as an infinite proof.

## Originality

PASS, with unresolved publication-priority risk. Frieswijk, Zino and Cao (arXiv:2209.08045v1; IEEE TNSE 2023) derived the original equations and invasion threshold. Their later endemic theorem explicitly assumes fully responsible vaccinated infecteds, sigma_v=1, unlike the present two-way transmissive setting sigma_v<1. Hiraoka et al. (arXiv:2112.07538) already explain qualitative rare-group epidemic hazards in a distinct network SIR model. Wang et al. (arXiv:2106.00122) study a separate controlled SIS model. Inspected accessible full texts and scientific claim comparisons yielded no direct implication of this nonlinear critical singular-limit law. A more general uncatalogued bifurcation result could nevertheless cover it.

## Value

PASS. The exact epsilon^(2/3) total-prevalence law and group-specific epsilon^(1/3) and epsilon^(2/3) laws quantify disproportionate endemic spillover in a biologically motivated critical mixing limit. The result gives parameters and explicit leading constants that a linear disease-invasion threshold does not provide.

## Scope and limitations

The two critical parameter equalities are necessary for the claimed scaling regime. The mean-field limit precedes the vanishing-minority limit. No conclusion about finite-population stochastic persistence, global endemic stability, or efficacy of interventions outside the specified regime is implied. Originality comparisons are nonexhaustive; independent validation remains outstanding.
