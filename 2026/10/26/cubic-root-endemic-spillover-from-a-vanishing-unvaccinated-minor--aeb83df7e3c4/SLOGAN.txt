At a jointly critical homophily and testing threshold in a two-group SIQS epidemic, a vanishing unvaccinated minority of size \(\varepsilon\) produces unique endemic prevalence of order \(\varepsilon^{2/3}\), rather than order \(\varepsilon\). The nonvaccinated conditional prevalence is of order \(\varepsilon^{1/3}\); both powers and their positive leading constants are proved exactly.
