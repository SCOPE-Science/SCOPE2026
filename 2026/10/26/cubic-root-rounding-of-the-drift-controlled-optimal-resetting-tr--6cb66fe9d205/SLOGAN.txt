For drifted Brownian motion with a finite-strength point trap, Poisson resetting has exactly one positive rate minimizing mean absorption time, even where a perfect trap would favor no resetting. As trap reactivity increases, the optimum vanishes as \(K^{-1/2}\) for strong drift but as \(K^{-1/3}\) at the critical drift, with an explicit universal crossover \(t^3+3\lambda t^2=24\).
