For \(r\) independent random linear-order successor relations on \(n\) labelled worlds, the two-variable sentence detecting one directed arc common to three relations has a sharp transition at \(r\asymp n^{1/3}\), with limiting truth probability \(1-e^{-c^3/6}\) when \(r/n^{1/3}\to c>0\). At criticality, the triple coincidences have a Poisson count and are asymptotically isolated in both worlds and relation indices.
