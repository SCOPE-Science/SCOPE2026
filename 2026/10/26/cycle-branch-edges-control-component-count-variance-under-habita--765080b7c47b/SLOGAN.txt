For degree-matched unicyclic habitat networks of equal cycle length \(\ell\), the difference in surviving-component variances is exactly \(-2\Delta B p^{\ell+1}(1-p)\) under independent site retention, where \(B\) counts cycle-to-branch edges. Under uniform retention of exactly \(k\) sites, that structural difference is invisible for \(k\le\ell\) and becomes strictly detectable for \(k\ge\ell+1\); six sites are the sharp minimum for such degree-matched examples.
