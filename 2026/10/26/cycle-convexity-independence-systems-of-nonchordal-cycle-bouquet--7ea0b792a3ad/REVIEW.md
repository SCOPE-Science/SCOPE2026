PASS: A bridge vertex cannot be generated by cycle convexity. In each cycle, a missing peripheral vertex is generated only after the hub and all other peripherals are selected; the missing hub is generated if a full peripheral cycle block is selected. Iterating gives exactly (hub chosen and no full peripheral block) or (hub absent and at most one full block) as the convex independence criterion. Under either condition, each two-part split has one part with no hull growth, and no selected element lies in another deletion hull: Radon independence follows; the deletion-hull intersection is empty, proving Helly independence.
