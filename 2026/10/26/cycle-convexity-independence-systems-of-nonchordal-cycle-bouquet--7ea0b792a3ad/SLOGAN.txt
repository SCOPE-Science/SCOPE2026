For nonchordal bouquets of at least three cycles sharing one hub, with arbitrary attached bridge trees, cycle-convexity Helly, Radon, and convex independence coincide for every nonempty vertex subset. Their complete cardinality polynomial is explicit, and the three maximum parameters equal \(|V|-k\) for \(k\) cycles, with an exactly enumerated set of optimizers and an unbounded \(k-1\) gap from cycle-convexity general position.
