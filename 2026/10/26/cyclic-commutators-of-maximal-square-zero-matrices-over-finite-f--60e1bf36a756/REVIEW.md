# Scientific assessment: cyclic commutators of maximal square-zero matrices

## Correctness — PASS

The exact event identity follows because the two rank-half square-zero kernels must be complementary for the commutator to be invertible. The resulting block commutator has similarity type \(T\oplus(-T)\) with uniform \(T\in\operatorname{GL}_m(\mathbb F_q)\). The cyclicity criterion is equivalent to cyclic \(T\) together with coprimality of its characteristic polynomial and its opposite. The general-linear centralizer formula yields the finite Euler product. Normal convergence of its cycle-index quotient on \(|z|<\sqrt q\) proves coefficient convergence, and the quadratic-character polynomial identity evaluates the limit to \((1-q^{-1})(1-q^{-3})/(1-q^{-4})\). The explicit checker reproduces exact counts through dimension six, but is not used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS within examined coverage

Closest sources: de Seguins Pazzis (arXiv:2210.03955, §§1.1–1.3) characterizes sums of two square-zero maps and associated symmetry constraints; Fulman (*Random matrix theory over finite fields*, pp. 54–57) supplies general cyclic-matrix cycle indices; Suwama (arXiv:2111.04884) studies trace-zero commutator representability. The new claim concerns the **random pair of maximal-rank square-zero factors**, the exact conditional commutator similarity pushforward, its cyclicity series, and the rational characteristic-sensitive limiting probability. The cited results supply components but do not state these combined probability assertions. A specialized unindexed decomposition or enumeration may already imply an equivalent result; independent priority checking remains necessary. The earlier related square-zero product-rank result determines \(\operatorname{rank}(EF),\operatorname{rank}(FE)\), not cyclicity or the rational-canonical law of \(EF-FE\).

## Mathematical value — PASS

Cyclicity is a basic matrix-similarity invariant with direct computational and centralizer consequences. The finding gives its **exact law** for a natural constrained commutator ensemble, finds a sharp distinction between even and odd field characteristic, and evaluates the conditional large-dimension density to a simple, unexpectedly rational expression rather than leaving it as an infinite Euler product. This requires both finite-field module structure and a nontrivial quadratic-character Euler identity, rather than a mere parameter substitution. Its value is the self-contained infinite-family invariant, not any claimed downstream theorem.

## Limits and further priority risk

The full similarity law is expressed as a pushforward instead of listing every rational-canonical class individually. The cyclicity probability requires an invertible commutator. General results on representations of two square-zero generators and specialized cyclic-matrix spectra remain potential sources of unindexed equivalences. Verification is exhaustive only for the tested prime fields; the general proof covers extension fields and unbounded dimension. No independent audit or external certification has been performed.
