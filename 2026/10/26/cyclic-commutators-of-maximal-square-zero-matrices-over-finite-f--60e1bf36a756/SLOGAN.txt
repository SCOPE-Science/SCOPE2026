For two uniformly random maximal-rank square-zero matrices in even ambient dimension, an invertible commutator has exactly the similarity law of \(T\oplus(-T)\) with \(T\) uniform in a smaller general-linear group. The chance that this commutator is cyclic is identically zero in characteristic two but, for every fixed odd field order \(q\), tends to the positive explicit value \(\Phi_q(1-q^{-1})(1-q^{-3})/(1-q^{-4})\).
