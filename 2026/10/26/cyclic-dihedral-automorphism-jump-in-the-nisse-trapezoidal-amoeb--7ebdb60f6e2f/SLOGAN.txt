For Nisse's non-Harnack trapezoidal amoeba family in every degree \(m\ge6\), the entire algebraic automorphism group of the affine curve is generically cyclic of order \(m\). It jumps to dihedral order \(2m\) exactly on the natural geometric-mean locus \(a^2=bc\), where an explicit torus involution exchanges the two puncture polygons.
