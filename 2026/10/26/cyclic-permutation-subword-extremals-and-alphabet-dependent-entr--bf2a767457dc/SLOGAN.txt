For a word formed by repeating a permutation of \(q\ge3\) distinct letters, the most frequent scattered subsequences are exactly complete repetitions of that permutation, with an explicit binomial maximum and rational generating function. The resulting subword-entropy rate is at most \(\log_2\beta_q\), where \(\beta_q^q-\beta_q^{q-1}=1\), yielding a strict improvement over the previously published alphabet-independent bound for every \(q\ge3\).
