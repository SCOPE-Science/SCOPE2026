For two-generator quantum complete intersections \(\mathbb F_Q\langle x,y\rangle/(x^2,y^m,xy-\zeta yx)\), the full element-centralizer dimension distribution has an explicit valuation formula depending only on the multiplicative order of \(\zeta\). Cyclotomic resonances change the rank spectrum and commuting probability; if that order is at least \(m\), the probability simplifies to \((Q+1)Q^{-m}-Q^{1-2m}\).
