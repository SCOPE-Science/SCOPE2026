The 28 bitangent lines of the Klein quartic have exact splitting field \(\mathbb Q(\zeta_7)\). In every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\) of odd characteristic other than seven, the complete Frobenius orbit structure depends only on \(q\bmod7\); the count over \(\mathbb F_{q^n}\) is exactly \(28\), \(4\), or \(1\) according as \(q^n\bmod7\) is \(1\), \(6\), or neither.
