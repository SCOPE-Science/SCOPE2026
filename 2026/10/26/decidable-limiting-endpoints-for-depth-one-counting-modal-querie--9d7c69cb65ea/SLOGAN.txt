For every fixed depth-one counting modal query on a uniformly random labelled directed graph, exact limiting zero and one probabilities are decidable by finite rational linear-feasibility tests, and the limiting probability is a finite Gaussian-cone expression. The balanced two-letter query \((2\#p\geq\#\top)\land(4\#(p\land q)\geq\#\top)\) has the explicit irrational limit \(1/4+\arcsin(1/\sqrt3)/(2\pi)\).
