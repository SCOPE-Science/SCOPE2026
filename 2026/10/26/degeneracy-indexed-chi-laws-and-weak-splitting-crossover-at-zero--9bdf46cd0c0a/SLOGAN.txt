For a fixed number \(r\ge2\) of equally slow sites in a canonical zero-range ring at the condensation threshold, the combined site occupation divided by \(\sqrt L\) converges to \(\sqrt v\,\chi_r\), while its division among the sites is independently Dirichlet-uniform. A joint \(L^{-1/2}\) tuning of density and relative site rates produces an explicit multivariate Gaussian-kernel exponential-tilt law; two competing defects give a Rayleigh-to-tilted crossover.
