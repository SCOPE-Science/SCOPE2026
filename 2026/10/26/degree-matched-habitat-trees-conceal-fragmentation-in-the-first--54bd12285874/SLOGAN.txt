For independent patch survival on two habitat trees with the same degree multiset, the number of surviving connected fragments has identical mean and variance, but its third cumulant differs by \(-6\Delta L\,p^4(1-p)^2\), where \(\Delta L\) counts differences in three-edge paths. The first distinguishable pair occurs at six patches, and its exact generating-function difference is \(p^4(1-p)^2z(z-1)^3\).
