# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof reduces every monotone map to two upset thresholds, characterizes the bounded-morphism back condition at the maximal zero worlds, and derives a complete and uniquely oriented transfer normal form. The generating-function recurrence is solved and coefficients extracted for all positive dimensions. Direct exhaustive finite tests and independent recurrence/Delannoy tests corroborate the argument; they are not substitutes for its general proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
Targeted searches of indexed results and accessible claim records for grid-to-diamond bounded morphisms, open maps, equivalent Heyting embeddings, exact Delannoy corrections and small tables did not locate a covering theorem. Bezhanishvili (2006) supplies general p-morphism semantics. Caughman and colleagues (2011) count Delannoy paths/chain statistics, and Codara, D'Antona and Marra (2007) discuss a different Gödel-algebra relationship. Primary-paper full text for the last two was not accessible, so an implicit earlier result remains possible. General map enumeration is not an implication of the back-condition count.

## Value — PASS
This is a natural, complete family classification of finite Kripke frame quotients, with an exact closed enumerator and sharp nonexistence threshold, not a small unrelated calculation. Its corrected Delannoy relation is an unexpected bridge between modal-frame morphisms and rectangular lattice paths.

## Limits
The theorem concerns labelled maps to a four-world target only. Independent scientific audit has not been performed.
