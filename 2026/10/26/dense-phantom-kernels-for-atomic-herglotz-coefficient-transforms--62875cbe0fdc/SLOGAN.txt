For every Herglotz probability measure with infinitely many atoms, the coordinatewise Hardy-space coefficient transform has a dense zero kernel with arbitrary prescribed finite Taylor jets; its graph closure is the entire product \(H^2\oplus H^2\). Every nonzero zero-output function lies outside the corresponding de Branges–Rovnyak space, making the failure of the formal coefficient test maximally nonclosable rather than an isolated counterexample.
