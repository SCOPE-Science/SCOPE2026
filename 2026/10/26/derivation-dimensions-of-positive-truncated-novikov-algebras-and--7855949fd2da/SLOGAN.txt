For the positive truncated polynomial Novikov algebra, the derivation dimension is \(1\) in characteristic zero, an explicit two-floor expression in odd prime characteristic, and \(\lfloor3n/2\rfloor-1+\mathbf1_{4\mid n}\) in characteristic two. The unexpected mod-four increase comes from a degree-lowering derivation that appears exactly when the characteristic-two algebra's odd and even power blocks have matching even length.
