The medialization of a group conjugation quandle identifies precisely the elements conjugate by its derived subgroup. For \(S_n\), its full operation has a universal pair-swap normal form with \(p(n)+q(n)\) points and automorphism group \((C_2\wr S_{q(n)})\times S_{o(n)}\times S_{e(n)}\) when \(n\ge3\); the dihedral family admits an explicit four-rule operation and has \(m+\gcd(m,4)\) points.
