For the genus-\(9\) characteristic-seven Artin–Schreier family \(y^7-y=x^4+ax^2+bx\), the a-number is \(6\), \(7\), or \(9\) according as \(a\ne0\), \(a=0\ne b\), or \(a=b=0\). Its Hasse–Witt rank-drop schemes have exact ideals \(I_2=(a,b)^2\) and \(I_3=a(a,b)^2\), so the generic rank-jump divisor contains an embedded point of length \(3\).
