For a uniform random permutation, the upper-record indicator at position \(i\) and the upper-record indicator at inverse position \(j\) are independent unless \(i=j\); their exact cross-covariance matrix is diagonal with entry \((i-1)/(i^2\binom ni)\). The two total record counts consequently have covariance \(1/n+1/(2n^2)+O(n^{-3})\) and correlation asymptotic to \(1/(n\log n)\).
