On a dyadic rectangular binary torus, the north-east XOR automaton loses every state after \(L\) updates, yet its full initial state can be reconstructed from fixed sensors observed for just \(L\) frames exactly when every diagonal residue modulo the shorter side length \(s\) is covered. The minimum is \(s\) sensors, with exactly \(L^s\) optimal placements, while uniformly random placements have a sharp observability threshold at \(s\log s\).
