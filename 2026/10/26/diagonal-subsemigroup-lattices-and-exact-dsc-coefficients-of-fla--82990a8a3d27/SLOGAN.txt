The reflexive meet-compatible relations on the flat semilattice with \(m\) atoms form a distributive lattice of bipartite-edge order ideals, with exact count \(D_m=\sum_{L,U\subseteq[m]}2^{|L||U|-|L\cap U|}\). Exactly \(2^m\) are congruences, and their proportion satisfies \(\chi(F_m)\sim2^{-m(m-2)}\).
