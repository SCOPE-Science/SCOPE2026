For two coupled particles in unequal-temperature optical traps, the coupling maximizing delayed cross-covariance asymmetry differs sharply from that maximizing its integrated lag signal: \(u_{\mathrm{peak}}/k=1.111627470050146\ldots\) versus \(u_{\mathrm{area}}/k=1/\sqrt2\). Both optimal ratios are unique and temperature-independent, even as the already-known instantaneous circulation grows monotonically with coupling.
