For degree-one least-squares projection on the positive spherical orthant, every coordinate-delocalized evaluation sequence has a sharp Gaussian amplification profile governed solely by its normalized \(\ell_1\)-mass. Mesoscopically sparse directions attain the exact diffuse envelope \(C_*=3.27783611857179\ldots\) after \(\sqrt d\) normalization; among diffuse sequences they are asymptotically extremal exactly when the normalized \(\ell_1\)-mass vanishes, giving a global operator-norm lower bound without asserting global sharpness.
