# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

Idempotence implies pointwise fixation of the image; connected proper images of a loopless cycle are induced path arcs. The complementary arc is a path walk whose endpoint parity forces even cycle length and whose length bounds the permitted rank. Dihedral conjugation reduces to a two-element arc stabilizer acting by reflected-complement walks. Its fixed walks are completely determined by a central vertex (odd path size) or central edge (even path size), proving both orbit-size multiplicities. All parity and boundary cases \(n=3,4\) were checked. Direct complete enumeration of cycle sizes \(3\) through \(14\) reproduced the rank and orbit histograms. Finite checks are supporting evidence; the displayed bijections prove the infinite statement.

## Originality — PASS within investigated coverage

The closest fully inspected primary paper, Dimitrova–Fernandes–Koppitz–Quinteiro (arXiv:2310.04149v1), characterizes endomorphisms, cardinalities, regularity, Green relations and a two-sided unit action, but does not enumerate conjugation orbits of its idempotents. Lin–Zeng (arXiv:1112.4026) supplies general path-walk counting tools, not the conjugacy stabilizer involution or rank-by-orbit-size distribution. Existing cycle-endomorphism counts or Green-class formulas do not determine this more refined equivalence-class count. The 2009 Michels–Knauer full text remained inaccessible; this is a specific residual originality risk, not a claim of exhaustive priority certification.

## Value — PASS

Conjugacy classes of idempotents under units are intrinsic symmetry invariants of a transformation monoid; they are distinct from raw counts and two-sided Green relations. The theorem gives the entire infinite-family rank-and-stabilizer spectrum, identifies the unique possible proper orbit sizes, and exhibits reflection-symmetric versus asymmetric retractions explicitly. The central-vertex/central-edge parity dichotomy is the structural feature determining this refinement. This is a natural complete classification, not an arbitrary small-rank calculation.

## Limits

No claim is made about weak endomorphisms, partial isometries, conjugacy by nonunits, or stronger semigroup-conjugacy relations. A full historical literature guarantee is unavailable. Complete finite verification covers only the stated tested sizes; the proof handles every integer \(n\ge3\).
