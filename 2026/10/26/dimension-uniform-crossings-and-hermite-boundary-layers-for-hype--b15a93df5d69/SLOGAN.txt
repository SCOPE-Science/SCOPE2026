For exact-degree spherical harmonics on every sphere \(S^d\), successive polar-order cap-concentration eigenvalues differ by one explicit Gegenbauer-derivative boundary product, yielding \(\ell(\ell-1)/2\) simple adjacent crossings and a complete consecutive-triple-resonance count independent of dimension. As dimension increases with degree fixed, every crossing is confined to a hemispherical \(d^{-1/2}\) boundary layer whose rescaled locations are precisely the positive zeros of consecutive Hermite polynomials.
