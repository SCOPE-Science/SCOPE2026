# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS

For every odd prime power \(q\) and every integer \(n\ge3\), invertibility of all rational pencil members is equivalent to absence of roots in the characteristic polynomial of \(T=B_0^{-1}B_1\). Geometric smoothness of the base complete intersection is equivalent to separability of this polynomial: over an algebraic closure, a multiple eigenvalue produces an isotropic eigenvector; distinct eigenvalues diagonalize the self-adjoint operator with nonzero orthogonal norms and exclude a singular common zero. For each fixed monic squarefree polynomial \(f\), the regular-representation and trace-pairing argument counts exactly \(|\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbf F_q)|\) ordered matrix pairs independently of factorization. Dividing by \(|\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbf F_q)|\), with all ordered bases admissible, proves both the exact counts and the partition refinements. Euler products and fixed-degree limits establish the generating function and derangement density. Independently implemented finite enumerations reproduce \(q=3,5\) ternary cases and the full \(q=3\), four-variable matrix-pair census, as well as quartic polynomial counts for \(q=3,5,7\). The proof, not the experiments, supplies the all-field statement.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Fevola–Mandelshtam–Sturmfels, arXiv:2009.04334, develops Segre–Weierstrass classification over complex numbers, not the claimed finite-field orbit-weighted partition count. Pott–Schmidt–Zhou, DOI 10.37236/4072, counts pairs under three selected rank constraints, not simultaneous nondegeneracy of all \(q+1\) rational quadrics and smoothness of the geometric base. The previously recorded isolated \(q=3\) and \(q=5\) ternary pencil counts are explicitly excluded as independent novelties; the uniform exact dimension- and factorization-type formulas are not logically implied by these instances. Relevant published-record searches found no formula dominating this theorem. Leep–Schueller, DOI 10.1006/jabr.1999.8168, studies regular pencil equivalence and is plausibly related; only the abstract was accessible, so an earlier unindexed equivalent enumeration is a real publication-priority risk. Neither database nonappearance nor this review establishes uniqueness or priority.

## Value — PASS

Counting smooth codimension-two intersections of quadrics is a natural moduli/enumerative problem, and the additional condition that the whole rational pencil avoid singular quadrics records a sharply defined arithmetic locus. The formula determines this locus for every dimension and every odd finite field, resolves all Frobenius partitions of the singular geometric members, and identifies an unexpected universal derangement-density limit. Unlike isolated small-field numerical counts, this uniform structural classification is not a mere recomputation of a known table; it predicts infinitely many exact geometric counts with distinguishable arithmetic types.

## Scope and limitations

Only coordinate-fixed linear pencils, not isomorphism classes, are counted. The field has odd characteristic, and the first polynomial source was publicly available on 2020-09-09. Complete text for the most relevant inaccessible older paper could not be inspected. Small-field verification is independent but finite.
