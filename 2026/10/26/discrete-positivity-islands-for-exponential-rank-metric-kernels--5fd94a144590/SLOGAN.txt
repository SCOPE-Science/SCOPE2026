For \(m\ge n\ge2\) matrices over \(\mathbb F_q\), the rank-exponential covariance \(K_t(X,Y)=t^{\operatorname{rank}(X-Y)}\) is positive semidefinite exactly for \(0\le t\le q^{-(n-1)}\) and the isolated strengths \(t=q^{-k}\) with \(0\le k\le n-2\). The open interval gives strict positivity, while the isolated points admit exact lower-rank Fourier supports.
