For a degree-\(d\) plane curve whose projection from a point has residual quadratic \(x^2L+xQ+R\), multiplying the equation by its branch-direction discriminant \(Q^2-4LR\) produces, whenever reduced, a free curve with exponents \((d,2d-3)\). In particular, every smooth complex plane cubic joined with the four ramification-direction lines from any point on it is a free septic.
