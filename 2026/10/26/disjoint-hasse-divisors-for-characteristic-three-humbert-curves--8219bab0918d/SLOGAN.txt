For smooth marked genus-five Humbert curves in characteristic \(3\), the nonordinary Hasse locus is five pairwise disjoint four-punctured rational curves: at most one of the five elliptic quotient Jacobians can be supersingular. Thus the Jacobian \(3\)-rank is exactly \(4\) or \(5\), with respective marked counts \(5(q-3)\) and \((q-3)(q-7)\) over every \(\mathbb F_q\) of characteristic \(3\).
