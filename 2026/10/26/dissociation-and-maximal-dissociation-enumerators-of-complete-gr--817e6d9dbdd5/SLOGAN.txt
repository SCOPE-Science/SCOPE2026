The complete dissociation and inclusion-maximal dissociation polynomials of the full subdivision \(S(K_n)\), for every \(n\ge3\), admit closed finite sums. The maximal-set classification has a sharp surjectivity obstruction when exactly two original vertices are omitted, and for \(n\ge5\) the smallest and largest maximal sets are uniquely the original-vertex set and subdivision-vertex set, with a quadratic cardinality gap.
