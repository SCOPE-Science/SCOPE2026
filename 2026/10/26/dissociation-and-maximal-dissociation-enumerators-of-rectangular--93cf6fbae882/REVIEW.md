# Review: dissociation and maximal-dissociation counts of rook graphs

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof establishes the component classification via the exact line-graph degree identity, derives the labeled exponential-formula weights, proves both directions of the one-cell-extension maximality criterion, and attains the extremal upper and lower bounds by explicit nonnegative integer component profiles. Direct graph-based enumeration on 11 boards (1,419,088 subsets) and exact-formula comparisons on 735 further pairs support these independent proof steps. Finite checks are not substitutes for the proof.

**Originality — PASS, with stated uncertainty.** The verified 2024 grid-graph enumeration treats path-factor lattices, not rook graphs. The 2011 line-graph packing correspondence and a maximum-size result are older and are expressly excluded from novelty; the 2023 cubic-graph count and 2024 grid maximal-set methods do not establish the new complete rook-graph pair of polynomials or the well-dissociated classification in inspected materials. The maximal-grid source was only accessible as an abstract; equivalent unindexed work on two-edge path/edge packings remains a priority risk.

**Value — PASS.** The result counts both all feasible subsets and those inclusion-maximal under extension on a two-parameter family of line graphs, determines the two different extremal cardinalities and the rare graphs where they agree, and reveals an infinite wide-board parity gap in attainable maximal-set sizes. These properties are not recoverable merely from the known scalar dissociation maximum.

**Limits.** The proof covers only \(K_p\mathbin{\square}K_q\) with \(p,q\ge2\); equivalent unpublished or unindexed enumerators cannot be ruled out. The computational verification is finite, and independent validation has not been performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
