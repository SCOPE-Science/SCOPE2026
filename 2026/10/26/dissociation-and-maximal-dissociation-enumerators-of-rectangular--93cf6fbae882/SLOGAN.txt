For every rectangular rook graph \(K_p\mathbin{\square}K_q\), explicit finite sums determine both the full dissociation-set polynomial and the inclusion-maximal-set polynomial. The only well-dissociated nontrivial rook graphs are the \(2\)-by-\(2\) and \(3\)-by-\(4\) boards, and wide boards exhibit an exact even-only spectrum of maximal-set sizes.
