For a uniform labeled Cayley tree, expected total leaf count conditioned on the distance between two fixed vertices is exactly unimodal, with a maximizing distance of order \(n^{1/3}\), even though typical distances have order \(n^{1/2}\). Double maxima occur at exactly the infinitely many sizes \(n=(k+2)(k^2+1)/2\) for integers \(k\ge2\), and forcing adjacency leaves the mean leaf count unchanged.
