For an independently sampled row of continuous exchangeable Gödel-modal weights and atomic truth degrees, \(\Pr(\Box p>\Diamond p)=\Pr(\Box p=\Diamond p)=2^{-n}\), and the three comparison outcomes are independent of all unordered local values. For iid common-distribution inputs, the entire joint law is explicit and the box minimum and diamond maximum approach independent exponential and Rayleigh-type extremes on two different scales.
