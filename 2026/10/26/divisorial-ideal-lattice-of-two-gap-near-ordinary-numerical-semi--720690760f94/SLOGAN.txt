For the two-gap numerical semigroups \(S_m=\{0\}\cup[m,2m-3]\cup[2m,\infty)\), normalized divisorial ideals correspond bijectively to binary words of length \(m-2\) avoiding \(010\), plus the semigroup itself. Their count obeys a cubic recurrence and their lattice is nonmodular exactly when \(m\ge5\).
