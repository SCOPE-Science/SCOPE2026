All double dominating sets of the one-step subdivision of \(K_{p,q}\), for \(2\le p\le q\), admit a three-family classification and an exact cardinality polynomial combining edge covers and forced subdivision vertices. The number of minimum sets is a surjection count for \(p\ge3\), but jumps to \(3^q+2q-2\) for \(p=2<q\), with a separate \(12\)-set boundary at \(p=q=2\).
