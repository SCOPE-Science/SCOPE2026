On the dyadic open clique strips \(P_{2^s-1}\square K_q\), binary neighbour-parity dynamics has the same exact primitive-period orbit census at each even width \(q\) and the following odd width \(q+1\), although every state is recurrent at the even width and only a fraction \(2^{-(2^s-1)}\) is recurrent at the odd width. The full Jordan forms explain this paradox and give the exact transient-depth distribution, with half of odd-width initial states needing the sharp maximum \(2^s-1\) updates.
