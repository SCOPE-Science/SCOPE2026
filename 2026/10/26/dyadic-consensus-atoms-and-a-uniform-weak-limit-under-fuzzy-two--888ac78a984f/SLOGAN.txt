In the two-agent fuzzy bounded-confidence model with independently sampled directional influence, every positive confidence width yields a purely atomic distribution of exact consensus opinions on dyadic midpoints, yet as the width vanishes that distribution approaches uniform on the full initial opinion interval. The mean exact-consensus time simultaneously grows with the sharp factor \(e^{\beta d/\varepsilon}/[2(1+e^\beta)]\), separating finite-time polarization from eventual random consensus.
