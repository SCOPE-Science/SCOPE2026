Among integers \(p^{2^k}q\) with distinct primes \(p,q\) and \(k\ge1\), equality between the unitary and exponential divisor sums occurs only at \(20\) and \(45\). A uniform modular interval proves that \(\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}x^{2^j}-1\) never divides \(x^{2^k}+1\) for any integer \(x\ge2\) and \(k\ge2\).
