For synchronous six-neighbour binary parity on every triangular torus, the exact recurrent-state count is \(2^{k\ell((r-1)(s-1)-(\gcd(r,s)-1))}\) when the circumferences have odd parts \(r,s\) and dyadic parts \(k,\ell\). Every state goes extinct exactly when at least one circumference is a power of two; the sharp transient time and final-step proportions reveal a strong square-versus-rectangular dyadic effect.
