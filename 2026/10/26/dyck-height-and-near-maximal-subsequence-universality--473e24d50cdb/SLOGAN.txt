For Dyck words of semilength \(n\), maximum height \(H\) imposes the sharp bound \(\iota\le n-H+1\) on scattered-subsequence universality. The words with \(\iota\ge n-1\) are exactly the Dyck paths of height at most two, giving precisely \(2^{n-1}\) such words and an explicit normal form for all but the unique maximizer.
