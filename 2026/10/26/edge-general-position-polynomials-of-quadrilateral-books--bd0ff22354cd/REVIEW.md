# Mathematical review: edge-general-position polynomials of quadrilateral books

## Correctness — PASS
The graph has diameter three for every \(r\ge2\), and the only distance-three endpoint pairs are the opposite-page \(x_i,y_j\) pairs. The three geodesics for each such pair list every forbidden triple of selected edges. Their translation to the three conditions on \(A,B,C\) and the spine selection is bidirectional, so counting the disjoint classes proves the polynomial. The inclusion-exclusion evaluation at \(z=1\) and the cardinality inequalities establish the total count, maximum, and complete extremizer classification. The two independent finite checks agree coefficientwise for \(1\le r\le5\); these experiments are supporting evidence, not the proof for unbounded \(r\).

## Originality — PASS, conditional on searchable priority
The first-public 2021 paper by Manuel, Prabha, and Klavžar proves the numeric \(r=2\) case through its grid theorem, but it does not cover square-page books with \(r\ge3\) or their entire edge-subset enumerator. Tian, Klavžar, and Tan's 2023 arXiv manuscript classifies selected extremal graph classes and treats block graphs; a multi-page square book is two-connected and not complete, so it is not among their block graphs. The 2022 arXiv / 2025 journal generalization studies torus networks, hypercubes, and Benes networks and does not supply this book enumerator. Searchable results on spider trees concern a different graph family, while dual general position of square books concerns *vertex sets* with a different condition. Literature queries did not identify an equivalent, stronger, or tabulated complete polynomial for these books. An unindexed or uncatalogued independent derivation remains a residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A book of four-cycles is the smallest natural family of cyclic prisms of trees, with distinct geodesic interactions between pages. The theorem does not merely specialize a published formula: it gives an exact cardinality distribution of **all** admissible edge subsets, an elementary-expression total-count sequence with three exponential scales, and a sharp phase change from page-spine maximizers to a unique spine-free maximizer at four pages. The three forbidden-triple types and their global set constraints are reusable when exploring geodesic-avoidance enumerators on more complicated tree prisms. The result is self-contained; it makes no unsupported assertion about those larger families.

## Closest sources and limitations
See Manuel–Prabha–Klavžar (arXiv:2105.04292, Theorem 4.1), Tian–Klavžar–Tan (arXiv:2302.01587, Sections 2–3), and Manuel–Prabha–Klavžar (arXiv:2207.07357; 2025 journal version). The claim is restricted to the stated square-page books and to edge general position, not vertex or dual general position. The 2026 article on edge general position for hierarchical, corona, and edge-corona products was available only at the descriptive abstract level for this comparison; overlap on a Cartesian star prism is possible but not indicated by its stated scope. The complete literature is not provably exhausted.
