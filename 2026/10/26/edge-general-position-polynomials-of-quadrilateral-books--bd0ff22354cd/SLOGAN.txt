The complete edge-general-position polynomial of the square-page book \(B_r=K_{1,r}\square K_2\) is determined by three forbidden geodesic-triple patterns, giving exactly \(5^r+(2r+6)3^{r-1}+(r^2+9r-4)2^{r-2}\) admissible edge subsets for every \(r\ge2\). The maximum size is \(\max\{2r,r+3\}\), and the optimum is unique as soon as \(r\ge4\).
