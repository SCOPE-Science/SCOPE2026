For a complete bipartite graph with a deleted matching, every edge-open-packing is a star subset or a pair of edges crossing two deleted diagonals; this gives closed all-set and maximal-set counting polynomials. Deleting just two independent edges makes the smallest inclusion-maximal packing size collapse from \(n\) to \(2\) in \(K_{n,n}\), while maximum packing size stays \(n\).
