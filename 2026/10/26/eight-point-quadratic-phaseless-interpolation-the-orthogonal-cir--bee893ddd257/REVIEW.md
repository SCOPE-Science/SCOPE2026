# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The five-point Fourier interpolation reduces every lifted Hermitian kernel to the explicit four-real-parameter matrix. The off-circle constraints have rank three for every triple of distinct anchors; the residual determinant factors as a nonzero positive circle-discriminant times the exact orthogonality defect. The rank-two lifting criterion proves both sufficiency and necessity, and an independent odd-cubic argument establishes the universal eight-measurement lower bound. Exact symbolic checks cover the critical algebra and representative configurations, while the proof covers all complex parameters.

## Originality

PASS on the inspected evidence, subject to unindexed literature. Bodmann–Hammen (arXiv:1302.5487), Theorem 2.3, supplies a constructive two-circle sufficiency theorem, not a necessary-and-sufficient criterion for three arbitrary anchors appended to five circular samples. Chalendar–Partington (arXiv:2403.16255), Theorem 2.16, treats magnitudes on *entire* circles and lines for analytic functions; its rational-angle obstruction does not classify finite three-anchor data. Grohs–Liehr–Rathmair (arXiv:2207.10620) treats multiple-window STFT recovery. Separate previously reported quadratic eight-point designs using two squares or two parallel four-point rows have different exact conditions and do not cover this family. In particular, no asserted novelty rests only on different terminology. The same continuous three-anchor family and orthogonality threshold were not found in the inspected materials; undiscovered equivalent formulations remain possible.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The precise degree-two right-angle obstruction explains when a minimal number of otherwise arbitrary additional interpolation sites succeeds or fails, including both noncollinear circles and collinear straight lines. The criterion covers a continuous full family and gives a readily testable geometric invariant, rather than a single isolated set or unmotivated computational constant. The proof includes a sharp global lower bound and explicit positive and negative designs, but makes no noise-stability or higher-degree claim.
