The entire family of finite Kneser disjointness graphs has exactly eight first-order theories at quantifier rank three, separated by the boundaries \(n=k\), \(n=2k\), \(n=3k-1\), and \(n=3k\) together with the singleton-set cases. Each class has a representative of at most 35 vertices, sharply attained by \(\mathrm{KG}(7,3)\).
