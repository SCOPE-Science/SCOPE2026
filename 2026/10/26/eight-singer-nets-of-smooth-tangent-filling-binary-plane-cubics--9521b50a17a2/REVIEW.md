The exhaustive checker uses unit Gröbner ideals in every projective chart for geometric smoothness and exact incidence arithmetic for tangent-filling; it finds 154 admissible forms, 392 admissible pencils, and eight admissible three-spaces. Every candidate three-space must arise from the enumerated pencil-plus-vector construction. The independent 512-splitting checker produces exactly the same eight nets. The seven rational zero sets and rational-flex restrictions were checked for every net member. The claim does not present known classifications of individual cubics as new.

## Value — PASS

This is a sharp and complete classification of the simultaneous occurrence of two independently motivated geometric properties—smoothness of every rational member and tangent-line coverage—for the smallest plane-cubic coefficient field. It proves existence and sharp maximal vector-space dimension three, exhibits a rigid eight-object projective orbit, and connects its members to the full Fano-line design and its Singer-normalizer symmetries. These are structural constraints on linear families, not a recomputation of individual cubic isomorphism classes.

## Scope and remaining limitations

Only the seven rational members of each net are required smooth; nonrational geometric members may be singular. The statement is about embedded projective equivalence, not abstract isomorphism. The argument is an exact exhaustive computer-assisted proof with independently reproduced finite subsearch, not a fully conceptual closed-form classification or third-party validation.
