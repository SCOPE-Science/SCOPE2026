The \(24\) smooth minimal-degree plane-filling quartics over \(\mathbf F_2\) belong to exactly eight disjoint three-member pencils, canonically indexed by the eight Singer subgroups of \(\operatorname{PGL}_3(\mathbf F_2)\). All eight pencils are projectively equivalent, their stabilizers have order \(21\), and no all-smooth plane-filling quartic net exists.
