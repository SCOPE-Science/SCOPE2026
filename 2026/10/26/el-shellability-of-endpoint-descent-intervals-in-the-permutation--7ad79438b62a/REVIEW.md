## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

The primary paper by Smith states the all-one-descent shellability assertion as a conjecture, while its proved theorem applies only when the two endpoints have the same descent count. The claim here is a strict infinite subfamily and the proof supplies a direct EL-labeling. The inspected layered-permutation theorem does not cover the non-layered members with \(k\ge3\). This comparison supports non-coverage by the inspected results, but cannot certify global novelty.
