# Eleven nodal contractions in the anticanonical model of a five-point blow-up of \\(\\mathbb P^1\\times\\mathbb P^2\\)

## Finding

For five general distinct complex points \\(p_1,\\ldots,p_5\\in\\mathbb P^1\\times\\mathbb P^2\\), let \\(X=\\operatorname{Bl}_{p_1,\\ldots,p_5}(\\mathbb P^1\\times\\mathbb P^2)\\). The anticanonical contraction of this smooth weak Fano threefold has **exactly eleven** irreducible exceptional curves, all mutually disjoint and each with normal bundle \\(\\mathcal O_{\\mathbb P^1}(-1)\\oplus\\mathcal O_{\\mathbb P^1}(-1)\\). Its anticanonical model is a degree-\(14\) Gorenstein Fano threefold with exactly eleven ordinary double points and no other singularities. Ten exceptional curves span rays already visible in the Mori-cone description; the eleventh is an irreducible curve lying in the interior of the same anticanonical face and is not an extremal ray.

## Assumptions and scope

Work over \\(\\mathbb C\\). The five points are in a nonempty general-position Zariski-open set for which their second-factor images, together with the base point of the unique bidegree-\\((1,1)\\) divisor through them, form six points in general position in \\(\\mathbb P^2\\). Write \\(H_1,H_2\\) for pullbacks of the two hyperplane classes, \\(E_i\\) for exceptional divisors, and \\(h_1,h_2,e_i\\) for the corresponding curve classes, with \\(E_i\\cdot e_i=-1\\). Then \\(D=-K_X=2H_1+3H_2-2\\sum_{i=1}^{5}E_i\\) is nef and big and \\(D^3=14\\), by the cited results.

## Proof

**Step 1: the anticanonical face.** The published Mori-cone generators are \\(h_1-e_i\\), \\(h_2-e_i\\), \\(e_i\\), and \\(h_1+2h_2-\\sum_{j\\ne i}e_j\\) for \\(1\\le i\\le5\\). Intersection with \\(D\\) is respectively \\(0,1,2,0\\). Put \\(A_i=h_1-e_i\\) and \\(B_i=h_1+2h_2-\\sum_{j\\ne i}e_j\\). The \\(D\\)-zero face is generated by the ten classes \\(A_i,B_i\\).

**Step 2: the distinguished cubic surface.** The unique strict transform \\(S\\) of a \\((1,1)\\) divisor through the five points has class \\(H_1+H_2-\\sum E_i\\). Geometrically it is the blow-up of \\(\\mathbb P^2\\) at six general points \\(q_0,q_1,\\ldots,q_5\\), with first-factor projection arising from the pencil of lines through \\(q_0\\). Denote the plane-line class by \\(h\\) and the six exceptional divisors on \\(S\\) by \\(u_0,\\ldots,u_5\\). Restrictions are \\(H_1|_S=h-u_0\\), \\(H_2|_S=h\\), and \\(E_i|_S=u_i\\). Hence

\\[
D|_S=5h-2\\sum_{j=0}^{5}u_j,\\qquad S|_S=2h-\\sum_{j=0}^{5}u_j.
\\]

The Mori cone of the smooth cubic del Pezzo surface \\(S\\) is generated by its \\(27\\) \\((-1)\\)-curves: the six \\(u_i\\), the fifteen \\(h-u_i-u_j\\), and the six conics \\(C_i=2h-\\sum_{j\\ne i}u_j\\) for \\(0\\le i\\le5\\). Their \\(D|_S\\)-degrees are, respectively, \\(2,1,0\\). Thus a \\(D\\)-null irreducible curve inside \\(S\\) is one of the six conics \\(C_i\\). All six are disjoint since \\(C_i^2=-1\\) and \\(C_i\\cdot C_j=0\\) for \\(i\\ne j\\).

For \\(i\\ge1\\), the pushforward of \\(C_i\\) into \\(X\\) has class \\(B_i\\). The sixth has class

\\[
[C_0]=2h_1+2h_2-\\sum_{j=1}^{5}e_j=A_i+B_i\\quad(1\\le i\\le5).
\\]

It is therefore an *irreducible, anticanonical-null curve whose class is not extremal*. The identity of its class with a sum is a numerical identity, not a decomposition of the irreducible curve.

**Step 3: exclude all remaining curves.** For any irreducible \\(D\\)-null curve \\(T\\not\\subset S\\), effectivity of \\(S\\) gives \\(S\\cdot T\\ge0\\). But \\(S\\cdot A_i=0\\) and \\(S\\cdot B_i=-1\\). Every \\(D\\)-null class is a nonnegative linear combination of the ten generators from Step 1; therefore \\([T]\\) is a nonnegative combination of the \\(A_i\\) only. Consequently \\(H_2\\cdot T=0\\), so its image in \\(\\mathbb P^2\\) is a point and \\(T\\) lies in a projection fiber. The only such \\(D\\)-null fibers are the strict transforms of the five fibers through the blown-up points; these are the five \\(A_i\\). They are disjoint from each other and from \\(S\\), the latter because \\(S\\cdot A_i=0\\). Together with the six disjoint curves in \\(S\\) this gives exactly eleven disjoint exceptional curves.

**Step 4: analytic type of contraction.** The strict transform of a \\(\\mathbb P^1\\)-fiber through one blown-up point has normal bundle \\(\\mathcal O(-1)^{\\oplus2}\\): both trivial normal directions acquire a simple vanishing at the blown-up point. For each conic \\(C_i\\subset S\\), the normal sequence has left term \\(N_{C_i/S}=\\mathcal O(-1)\\) and right term \\(N_{S/X}|_{C_i}=\\mathcal O(S\\cdot C_i)=\\mathcal O(-1)\\). The sequence splits since \\(H^1(\\mathbb P^1,\\mathcal O)=0\\). Thus all eleven curves have normal bundle \\(\\mathcal O(-1)^{\\oplus2}\\). Since \\(D\\) is nef and big, the basepoint-free theorem makes some multiple of \\(D\\) basepoint-free. Its connected-fiber contraction is birational and has exactly the eleven listed smooth rational curves as its exceptional locus. Standard local theory of the contraction of a \\((-1,-1)\\)-curve in a smooth complex threefold gives an ordinary double point. Disjointness and connected fibers give eleven *distinct* nodes; the morphism is an isomorphism elsewhere. It is crepant, so the normal projective image is Gorenstein Fano, with \\((-K)^3=14\\).

## Verification

The accompanying `verify.py` checks exact intersection pairings for all \\(27\\) lines on the cubic surface, the six pairwise disjoint conics, the class identity of the eleventh curve, and the vanishing of the anticanonical degree. It also interpolates an explicit five-point configuration and checks that the five \\(B_i\\) representatives meet pairwise only at the three blown-up common points; its arithmetic returns `VERIFY_OK`. This is supplementary finite algebra, not a substitute for the general geometric argument.

## Relationship to prior work

Bolognesi–Massarenti–Poma compute the Mori cone and include the numerical decomposition of the sixth conic class in their proof; neither the class decomposition nor the weak Fano property is asserted as new. Grange–Postinghel–Prendergast-Smith prove nefness, bigness, and volume of the anticanonical class. The distinct further statement here is the **complete eleven-component geometric contraction locus** and the **exact eleven-node analytic singularity basket**, including an irreducible contracted curve not corresponding to an extremal ray.

## Limitations

The statement requires five general complex points and a smooth cubic del Pezzo auxiliary surface. It does not assert the same singularity count for special point configurations, arbitrary characteristics, or every threefold of anticanonical degree \\(14\\). The singularity classification uses standard characteristic-zero basepoint freeness and the local classification of a \\((-1,-1)\\)-curve contraction. A complete search of every unindexed classical Fano-threefold classification was not possible.
