For the blow-up of \\(\\mathbb P^1\\times\\mathbb P^2\\) at five general complex points, the anticanonical morphism contracts exactly eleven disjoint \\((-1,-1)\\)-curves, producing a degree-\\(14\\) Fano threefold with eleven ordinary double points. One of the contracted curves is irreducible but numerically non-extremal, explaining why counting extremal rays alone gives only ten.
