For every unweighted complete multipartite graph with at least three parts, the canonical orthogonal cut-projection endpoint norm is exactly the sum of two part-size scores for the two largest parts, while the complementary cycle norm selects its pair by a different score. In every graph \(K_{k,2,1}\) with \(k\ge3\), the two projections have strictly different maximizing edge types, with a matched loss/gain margin of \((k-1)/(3(k+3))\).
