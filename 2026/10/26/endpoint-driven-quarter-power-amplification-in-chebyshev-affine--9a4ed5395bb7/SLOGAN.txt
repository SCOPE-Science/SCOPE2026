For the exact Chebyshev-coefficient matrix of affine restriction to any fixed nondegenerate proper subinterval, the one-step spectral norm grows as \(\Theta(N^{1/4})\) despite spectral radius \(1\). Repeated restriction has the exact limiting norm \(\sqrt{1+2\sum_{j=1}^N T_j(t_*)^2}=\Theta(N^{1/2})\), separating one-step amplification, asymptotic powers, and eigenvalue information.
