# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The projection definitions are orientation-independent and explicit. Product Laplacian splitting isolates the inverse of the massive cyclic Laplacian; a positive, reflected Green kernel determines both edge-column orbits. The signed rim-column gradients were evaluated with their absolute values, and the diagonal identity converts cut to complementary cycle column norms. Strict orbit domination is established algebraically, not from finite sampling. The scalar crossing is monotone in rim size for the exceptional three-vertex cross-section; exact rational boundary values determine the cutoff. The logarithmic correction follows from a proved Green-level threshold with \(O_m(n^{-1})\) tail control. The separate standard-library checker reproduced every edge column in seven graphs, not just orbit representatives.

## Originality — PASS (qualified by literature-access uncertainty)
The 2023 preprint and 2024 open version of Dilworth–Kutzarova–Ostrovskii were inspected for graph domains and actual statements: tori and Hamming graphs do not imply the \(K_m\square C_n\) theorem for \(m\ge3,n\ge5\). Existing circular-ladder work covers the binary clique only; earlier rook and mixed Hamming formulas require all Cartesian factors to be complete. The statements were compared by implication, including the exact cutoff and sharp logarithmic coefficient. Multiple exact and alias searches found no dominating result. An equivalent unindexed formula is a genuine residual risk; absence from search results is not a uniqueness proof.

## Value — PASS
The result closes both canonical endpoint norms for a natural two-parameter family of cyclic-clique graph products, proves that just one of two geometrically different edge orbits always determines both operator norms, and finds a sharp, nonmonotone switch in which complementary projector is larger. The common precise logarithmic correction, caused by screening of the cyclic Green kernel against its uniform mean, gives information not available from a mere graph-Laplacian pseudoinverse identity. This is a motivated infinite-family Banach-space operator classification, not a table recomputation.

## Scientific limitations
Only canonical orthogonal projections are optimized over vectors; the theorem makes no claim that they minimize norm among arbitrary projections. The asymptotics are for fixed clique size. General weighted graphs, intermediate exponents, and completeness of external bibliographic discovery are outside the proof.
