For the canonical real cut and cycle projections on every cyclic clique cylinder \(K_m\square C_n\) with \(m\ge3,n\ge5\), rim edges uniquely determine both sharp endpoint norms, given by explicit cyclic Green-function sums. The larger projection switches from cycle to cut exactly between \((m,n)=(3,5)\) and \((3,6)\); for \(m\ge4\), the cycle projection is always larger, and both norms have the same sharp \((\log n)/n\) correction at fixed clique size.
