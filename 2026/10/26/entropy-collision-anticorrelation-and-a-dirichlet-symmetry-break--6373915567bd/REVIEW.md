# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** The claim is proved for each finite alphabet with at least two categories and every positive real Dirichlet shape vector. Differentiation of the normalized Dirichlet moment identity yields the mixed entropy–quadratic moment. The sign splits into a strictly positive pairwise shape product and a nonnegative weighted covariance of an increasing function. The local stability calculation uses a tangent-plane Taylor expansion at a symmetric shape vector; uniqueness of the curvature root follows from an explicit positive integral with a pointwise monotonic integrand. No finite experiment is used to infer an all-parameter statement. The separate raw-moment, beta-integral and curvature checker passed 20 comparisons. At critical total concentration only Hessian degeneracy, not higher-order extremality, is asserted.

**Originality (PASS, residual risk).** The closest sources distinguish several nearby objects. Willis and Martin (2020) estimate Shannon and Simpson diversity using log-ratio multivariate models, without Dirichlet cross-covariance formulas. Botha, Ferreira and Bekker (2021) derive Dirichlet-type product moments and power-sum estimators; raw moments are not new and imply that a mixed formula is in principle computable, but their stated results do not give the sign decomposition or the unique constrained-Hessian transition. Bissiri, Corradin and Ongaro (2026), including its appendices, study Gini–Simpson mean, variance, posterior estimates, and asymptotics under symmetric, dynamic and Poisson–Dirichlet priors, not the same entropy cross-covariance or asymmetric-shape Hessian. Closest published-record results on occupancy richness–diversity covariance and Bernoulli-sum collision entropy concern different probability spaces and neither implies the result. An unlocated older joint-moment or covariance identity could have priority over a portion of the claim, so exhaustive historical novelty is not certified.

**Value (PASS).** Shannon and collision/Gini–Simpson diversity are canonical paired descriptors of the same random categorical composition. A universal sign for arbitrary unequal prior shapes and an exact local change in sensitivity to unequal pseudo-counts give a concrete prior-calibration fact: the same balancing operation can locally strengthen or weaken joint uncertainty depending on total concentration. The sharp transition is not just a routine evaluated moment or a small numerical improvement; it classifies the sign of every tangent direction at symmetry across all alphabets, with a unique threshold.

**Limitations.** Only fixed finite alphabets, positive Dirichlet shapes and fixed-total local perturbations are covered. There is no global optimizer claim, no claim about the critical Hessian-degenerate case beyond the quadratic term, and no result for posterior model mixtures or alternative priors. Independent scientific audit and comprehensive priority determination remain unperformed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
