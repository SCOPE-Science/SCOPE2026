In a two-strategy logit game coupled to an evolving resource, the Hopf coefficient is \(\ell_1=-[(2+\chi)+(2-\chi)\omega^2]/(16q)\): sufficiently strong payoff–environment coupling permits the environmental response speed alone to switch a small Hopf cycle from attracting to repelling. An explicit admissible game is supercritical at environmental speed \(1\) and subcritical at speed \(40\).
