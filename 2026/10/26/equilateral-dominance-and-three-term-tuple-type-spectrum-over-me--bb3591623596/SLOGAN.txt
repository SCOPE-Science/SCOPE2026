Over an individually named finite metric star in the three-distance homogeneous Urysohn space, every fixed-arity injective tuple-type count is a positive integer exponential sum in the number of leaves. For arity \(k\ge3\), its three largest bases are exactly \(3^k\), \(8\cdot3^{k-2}\), and \(22\cdot3^{k-3}\), with multiplicities \(1\), \(\binom{k}{2}\), and \(3\binom{k}{3}\); almost every type is equilateral at exponential rate.
