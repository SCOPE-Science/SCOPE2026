For every fixed residue modulus and integer divisor, the counts of totatives in each residue class of sufficiently large primorial wheels are all divisible by that divisor. Consequently the prime-generating binary states of Shunia and Prunescu obey \(T_k\equiv2(2^{P_k}-1)\pmod m\) eventually for every odd \(m\), while modulo \(17\) they take exactly the two residues \(6\) and \(7\) infinitely often.
