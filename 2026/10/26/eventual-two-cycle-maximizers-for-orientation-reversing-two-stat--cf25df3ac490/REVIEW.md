# Scientific review: negative-determinant two-state maximizers

**Correctness — PASS.** Representing each letter by a strictly decreasing affine map makes the max/min envelopes reverse order, and their compositions monotone. Extremal values on either length parity are monotone, so finite affine comparisons eventually fix each optimum-letter set, including ties. Exact extremal word multiplicities are nondecreasing at two-step intervals; unique extremizers therefore eventually recur by one two-letter suffix per parity or disappear. The upper/lower envelope no-return argument separately proves the parity-run restriction and its run-composition upper bound. Direct exact-rational tests corroborate the recurrences and structural condition; they are not used as infinite proofs.

**Originality — PASS within inspected evidence.** The most relevant published binary-alphabet regularity result is Gill's Corollary 4.16 (arXiv:2402.13376); it is explicitly binary and thus cannot imply the three-or-more-letter statement. Kjos-Hanssen (arXiv:2607.26275, first published July 28, 2026) supplies a universal three-state complexity bound but no corresponding classification for two-state PFAs over larger alphabets. The orientation-preserving determinant case concerns disjoint letter matrices. Semantic research-record searches for affine, probabilistic automata, and unique-maximizer analogues returned no theorem covering all strictly orientation-reversing letters on arbitrary alphabets. Unindexed, unpublished, or differently formulated equivalents remain a priority risk; a negative search is not proof of novelty.

**Value — PASS.** Extending a literature-highlighted binary regularity phenomenon to an arbitrary finite alphabet under a natural uniform sign hypothesis is a structural infinite-family result, not a finite sample. The additional parity-run property constrains every potential witness word and gives a polynomial combinatorial envelope. The ternary exact example demonstrates genuine use of the extra letter. No claim is made for unrestricted mixed-sign PFAs, and the exponent of the upper bound is not asserted sharp.

**Scientific limits.** All determinants strictly negative; two states; fixed automaton; unique winners only. The available inspected texts do not establish absolute novelty, and independent validation is pending.
