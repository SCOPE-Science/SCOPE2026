For unweighted path graphs, the sharp \(\ell^1\) and \(\ell^\infty\) norms of the mean-zero Laplacian inverse are eventually attained only at endpoints and admit an exact floor-polynomial formula. For every path size, the sharp norm differs from \(2n^2/(9\sqrt3)\) by at most \(7/8+1/(4\sqrt3)\), and the strict endpoint advantage over the fixed \(k\)-th row tends to \(k(k+1)/\sqrt3\).
