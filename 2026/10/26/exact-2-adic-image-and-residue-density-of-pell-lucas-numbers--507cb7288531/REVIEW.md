# Scientific review

## Correctness
The matrix identities \(T^4=I+4B\) and \(T^8=I+8D\) are exact. The four first binomial coefficients are odd and the higher coefficients have the required factorial-sensitive divisibility, giving four global 2-adic isometries. The even-index trace identity exhausts the remaining indices. These arguments establish exact reachability for all powers of two. The standalone program checks matrix values and entire finite state cycles, not an infinite theorem by extrapolation.

## Originality
Bragman–Rowland (arXiv:2202.00704, read as full primary text) give a density classification for ordinary Fibonacci numbers, not Pell–Lucas values. Lang–Lang (arXiv:1307.7463, main-results section inspected in full primary text) characterize which moduli admit every Pell–Lucas residue, but do not state the exact attained-residue image or the constant \(9/64\). Related published work on companion-sequence periods establishes periodicity and valuations, not this image. Exact-phrase, recurrence-alias, implication, and existing-record searches did not identify the same three cosets. Absence of a retrieved statement is not proof of absolute novelty: general recurrence literature and unindexed tables remain residual risks.

## Mathematical value
Exact residue images measure the arithmetic restrictions of a natural classical recurrence, and strengthen mere failure of residue completeness to a complete, stable description across infinitely many moduli. The result gives an exact 2-adic density without invoking an unproved independence assumption, and the three-coset decomposition exposes the index branches responsible for the obstruction.
