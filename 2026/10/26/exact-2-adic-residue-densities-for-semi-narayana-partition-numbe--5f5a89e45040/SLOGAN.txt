Semi-Narayana binary-partition counts have an exactly uniform distribution of consecutive coefficient triples over all primitive residue triples modulo every \(2^r\). An individual coefficient lies in each odd residue with density \(8/(7\cdot2^r)\) and in each even residue with density \(6/(7\cdot2^r)\), yielding the exact \(2\)-adic valuation law \(\mathbb P(\nu_2(c(n))=j)=3/(7\cdot2^j)\) for \(j\geq1\).
