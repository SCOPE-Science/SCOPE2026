For binary directed necklaces of length \(3p\) with exactly three ones, the number of indexed cyclic cuts yielding an Abelian cube is \(3\max\{0,\min(d_1,d_2,d_3,2p-\max_i d_i)\}\), where the \(d_i\) are the positive inter-marker distances. The exact phase-count distribution has \(p(p-1)/2\) zero-phase necklaces, \(2(p-k)\) necklaces with \(3k\) phases for \(1\le k<p\), and one with \(3p\) phases.
