# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact domain includes every nonnegative triple of zero gaps with exactly two ones; the word `11` is explicitly excepted. Every Abelian border longer than half the word has an equally informative shorter complementary border. Factors with equal endpoint symbols are immediately excluded, and the remaining factors with zero, one, and two ones are completely classified. The two-one criterion is the strict inequality between outer and inner zero gaps. Disjoint factor families yield the integer formula and its exact extrema. A two-dimensional lattice-slice count yields the finite distribution, and normalized composition-lattice convergence yields the continuous CDF, mean, and variance. The standalone checker exhausts 2,300 gap triples at total zero count at most 22, checks the exact finite CDF, and corroborates the rational moment asymptotics. No finite observation replaces an infinite quantifier.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority uncertainty

Published work on all binary Abelian borders, global enumerations of Abelian-bordered words, and qualitative periodicity via unbordered factors does not state or imply the conditional **distinct-factor** count and its distribution without a separate factor classification. The full Goč–Rampersad–Rigo–Salimov and Charlier–Harju–Puzynina–Zamboni manuscripts were inspected for stronger/specialized results. Published mathematical search records were searched using fixed-composition, gap-parameter, Abelian border and unbordered factor aliases; closer indexed records addressed unrelated Abelian-pattern support bounds or adjacent-pair multispectra. The Christodoulakis–Christou–Crochemore–Iliopoulos 2014 article's abstract was directly examined, but its primary full text was unavailable. An independently existing equivalent specialization remains possible, and search absence alone does not establish priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Abelian-unbordered factors are a canonical finite-word analogue of ordinary unbordered factors, relevant to the published border/periodicity question. Restricting to exactly two marked symbols yields a natural minimal sparse family in which an exact distinct-factor spectrum, sharp extrema and a complete **conditional distribution**, not just a single count, can be proved for all lengths. The nondegenerate continuum limit separates the effects of outer gaps and the internal gap: the CDF changes quadratic regimes at one-half, while variance remains quadratic in the word length. This is a coherent classification rather than an arbitrary sampled parameter or a numerical table.
