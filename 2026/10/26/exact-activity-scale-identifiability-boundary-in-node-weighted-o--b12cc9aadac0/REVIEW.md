# Review: Exact activity-scale identifiability boundary in node-weighted opinion dynamics

## Correctness — PASS

The theorem exactly conditions an undirected sampled interaction on the side of the bipartition from which the first vertex was selected. The mixture, total variation law, and weighted-biregular equality test follow algebraically. A missing edge yields a nonzero polynomial whose vanishing is necessary for activity-scale invisibility; this proves both the universal complete-bipartite characterization and generic identifiability. The opinion-trajectory conclusion is justified by the identical law of the unordered interacting edge at each step. The independent exact-rational verifier checks 465 finite instances. The continuum and arbitrary graph-size assertions rest on the proof, not computational extrapolation.

## Originality — PASS (priority provisional)

Li and Porter's equation (2) defines the weight-biased first-vertex/neighbor protocol. The primary paper investigates simulation outcomes but does not report an exact scale-invariance or inverse-identifiability theorem. The earlier activation-regime and algorithmic-bias models inspected use distinct sampling mechanisms, and available research records did not produce a directly covering claim. Classical conditional probability and polynomial zero-set arguments are proof ingredients, not new mathematical ingredients. A broader unindexed Markov-kernel theorem could still subsume the result; an independent priority audit is outstanding.

## Value — PASS

An often overlooked experimental distinction is whether an interaction trace records an unordered pair or the initiator. The finding is a full structural classification of which connected bipartite networks can hide an entire population's relative activity level for every weight profile, plus a measure-zero classification of exceptional profiles elsewhere. It gives exact observable total-variation sensitivity and demonstrates equality of complete opinion-process laws when the scale is invisible. This provides usable identifiability boundaries for model calibration and empirical study design, without claiming any practical social effect beyond the stated model.

## Scope and limitations

Connected simple bipartite graphs, all weights positive; known within-class normalized weights are necessary for the inverse recovery statement. Undirected interaction traces, not directed first-selection traces. The dynamic corollary assumes symmetric edge-only updates and no independent physical-time event intensity. Independent scientific audit and publication-priority verification remain outstanding.
