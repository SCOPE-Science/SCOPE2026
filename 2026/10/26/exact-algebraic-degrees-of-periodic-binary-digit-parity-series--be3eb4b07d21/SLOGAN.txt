For every periodic binary mask \(a\), the digit-parity sequence has a generating series over \(\mathbb F_2(x)\) whose exact algebraic degree is \(2^{\deg((1+T^d)/\gcd(1+T^d,\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}a_jT^j))}\). In particular, the recently introduced meta-automatic sequence \(\mathcal M_2\) has an irreducible quartic equation for its generating series, whereas other period-three masks have algebraic degrees \(1\), \(2\), \(4\), or \(8\).
