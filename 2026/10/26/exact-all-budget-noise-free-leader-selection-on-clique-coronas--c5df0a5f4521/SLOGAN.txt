For every leader budget on the clique corona \(K_n\circ K_1\), the exact noise-free coherence optimum either selects one core and distinct other leaves, selects only leaves, or (above \(n\) leaders) selects every leaf before additional cores. A quadratic budget threshold decides between the first two patterns, never permits integer ties, and asymptotically occurs at \((\sqrt3-1)n\) leaders.
