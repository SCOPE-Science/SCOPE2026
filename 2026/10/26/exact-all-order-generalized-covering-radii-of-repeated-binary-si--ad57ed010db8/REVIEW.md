# Review: exact generalized covering hierarchy of a repeated binary simplex code

## Correctness
**PASS.** Every \(t\)-row code matrix has three constant column blocks \(u\), \(v\), \(u+v\). Agreement with an arbitrary target is the sum of three histogram entries. Choosing a largest first-block entry and averaging the other two proves the universal ceiling lower bound for the agreement maximum. Constructed histogram triples supported on an index-two subgroup and its complementary coset attain the bound in every residue regime. Exhaustive finite checks and direct raw-matrix enumeration agree with the proof. The argument proves the all-integer quantifiers independently of experiments.

## Originality
**PASS, with residual priority risk.** Elimelech–Firer–Schwartz (2021) defines the block metric and its generalized covering radius, while Yu–Schwartz (arXiv:2609.14477v1) studies related general packing-covering inequalities. Alfarano and collaborators (arXiv:2606.16669) give finite-geometric equivalents. Searches for repeated simplex, rank-two general covering hierarchies, and additive histogram minimax found no equivalent exact ceiling formula; a database miss cannot certify novelty. Most relevant earlier full-text definitions were inspected, but the later sections of the 2026 source were inaccessible and an implicit older equivalent remains possible.

## Value
**PASS.** The theorem gives an all-order and all-repetition exact family, not just sampled values. The subgroup construction identifies a genuine additive balancing mechanism, and the formula determines when the hierarchy first saturates at the code redundancy. It supplies a checkable baseline for simultaneous-access and generalized-covering questions. It makes no claim for higher-dimensional/nonbinary simplex codes.
