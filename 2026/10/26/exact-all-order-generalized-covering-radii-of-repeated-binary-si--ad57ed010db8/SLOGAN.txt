For the binary \(m\)-fold two-dimensional simplex code, every generalized covering radius is exactly \(R_t=3m-\lceil m/2^t\rceil-\lceil2m/2^t\rceil\). An index-two-subgroup construction attains the bound at every order and shows the hierarchy reaches the maximal radius at \(t=\lceil\log_2(2m)\rceil\).
