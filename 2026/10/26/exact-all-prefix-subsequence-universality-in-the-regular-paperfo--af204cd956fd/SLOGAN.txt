For the regular binary paperfolding word, the maximum subsequence length universally present in its prefix of size \(N\) has an exact formula: \(U(N)=\lfloor(3N+1)/8\rfloor\), minus two explicitly characterized, disjoint indicator corrections. Its universality rate is exactly \(3/8\), with the sharp all-prefix discrepancy \(-1\le U(N)-3N/8\le1/8\).
