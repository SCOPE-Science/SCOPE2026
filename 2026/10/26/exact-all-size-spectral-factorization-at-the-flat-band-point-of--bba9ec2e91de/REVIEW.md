# Review

## Correctness

PASS. The site-local orthonormal basis \(u=(i,1)^T/\sqrt2\), \(v=(-i,1)^T/\sqrt2\) converts each hopping block into a rank-one dimer coupling. The gain/loss terms then split the matrix into the stated three-, four-, or six-dimensional blocks plus untouched dimers. The characteristic polynomials follow from explicit determinant recurrences. The central six-dimensional block has imaginary first-order eigenvalue shifts at every sufficiently small positive gain/loss strength.

## Originality

PASS, narrowly scoped. The edge threshold \(2J\), the edge exceptional point, and numerical position-dependent threshold profiles are prior results. The claim is limited to the exact characteristic-polynomial factorization and complete threshold classification for every mirror-pair position in every even finite chain at \(\mu=0,\delta=J\). The inspected primary texts do not state these all-size formulas. An equivalent unpublished or unindexed derivation remains a priority risk.

## Value

PASS. The formulas replace a \(2N\times2N\) spectral calculation by at most a six-dimensional boundary block and explain why the end-site perturbation has a positive threshold while any interior pair has zero threshold. This is a natural exact finite-size classification at the Majorana dimerization point, not a numerical extrapolation.

## Closest literature and limits

Agarwal–Joglekar, arXiv:2103.07058v1, gives position-dependent numerical threshold profiles and the edge exceptional point. Kawabata–Ashida–Katsura–Ueda, arXiv:1801.00499v1, gives the edge-gain/loss Majorana formulation and the sweet-spot boundary \(\gamma=2J\). Neither inspected text states the full all-position polynomial factorization. The result does not extend here to odd length, nonzero chemical potential, unequal hopping and pairing, disorder, or interacting many-body spectra.
