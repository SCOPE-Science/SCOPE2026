Every nontrivial positive sum of two centered Gaussians of unequal widths has infinitely many real self-ambiguity zeros, whose sharp asymptotic cone is bounded by the two Gaussian precision parameters. As the widths coalesce, the first balanced-ray zero escapes at order \(\varepsilon^{-1/2}\) for equal amplitudes, but \(\varepsilon^{-1}\) for unequal amplitudes.
