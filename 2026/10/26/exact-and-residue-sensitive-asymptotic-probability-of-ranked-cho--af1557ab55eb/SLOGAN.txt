For three-candidate instant-runoff voting with independent uniformly sampled single-peaked rankings and an odd number \(n\) of voters, the tie-independent probability of rejecting the Condorcet winner has an exact multinomial formula. Its sharp asymptotic is \(\frac{3\sqrt3}{2\pi n}(27/32)^{n/3}\) times one of three explicit, different constants determined by \(n\bmod3\); the deterministic center-squeeze mechanism itself is established prior work.
