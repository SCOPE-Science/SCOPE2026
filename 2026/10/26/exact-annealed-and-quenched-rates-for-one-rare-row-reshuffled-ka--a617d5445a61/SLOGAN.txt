For planar Kaczmarz with \(a\ge1\) repeated copies of one row and one distinct row reshuffled independently each epoch, the exact squared-error almost-sure factor is \(x^{2(1-(a+1)^{-2})}\), whereas the exact mean-square factor is \((x^2+\sqrt{x^4+4x^2(1-x^2)/(a+1)^2})/2\), where \(x\) is the squared row-normal overlap. The strict gap between these factors and the best uniform per-epoch squared bound \(x\) quantifies how rare cross-epoch row adjacency controls the expectation while typical trajectories contract faster.
