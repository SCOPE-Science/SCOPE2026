Every binary majority trajectory on a friendship graph of \(m\) triangles fixes within two simultaneous updates, and each individual equilibrium with \(j\) dissenting triangle-pairs has an exact basin of size \(3^{m-j}+3^j\) (or \(3^j\) at a hub tie). For uniform initial opinions, consensus has probability exactly \((3^m+1)/4^m\), which vanishes exponentially even though the universal hub retains its own initial opinion with probability tending to one.
