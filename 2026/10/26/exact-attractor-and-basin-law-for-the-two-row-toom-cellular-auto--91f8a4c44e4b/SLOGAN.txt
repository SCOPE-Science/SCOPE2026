On a two-row periodic strip, noiseless North–East–Center majority dynamics has exactly \(2^{n+1}\) recurrent states: column-uniform fixed patterns and freely travelling binary necklaces. Its fixed-point basins obey the sharp law \(3^{n-b}\) for targets with \(b>0\) cyclic color boundaries (and \(3^n-2^n\) for consensus), while typical convergence takes \(\log_2n\) steps against a worst case of \(n-1\).
