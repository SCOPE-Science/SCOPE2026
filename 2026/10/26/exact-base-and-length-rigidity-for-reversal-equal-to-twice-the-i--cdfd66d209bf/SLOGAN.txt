For every integer base \(b\ge4\), the affine reversal equation \(r_b(N)=2N-4\) has exactly one full-length solution per digit length \(L\ge2\) when \(b\ge10\) and \(b\equiv1\pmod3\), and none otherwise. Its forced digits give a sharp restriction to even bases \(b\equiv4\pmod6\) for prime \(v\)-palindromes whose reversals equal twice another prime.
