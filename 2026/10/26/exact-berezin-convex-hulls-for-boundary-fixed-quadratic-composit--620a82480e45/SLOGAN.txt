For every boundary-fixed quadratic symbol \(\varphi_\mu(z)=(1-\mu)z+\mu z^2\) with \(0<\mu<1\), the closed convex hull of the Hardy composition operator's Berezin range is exactly the hull of \(1\) and the disk of center and radius \((2+\mu)^{-1}\). Its area is explicit, the range omits both open tangent segments, and the convexified ranges stay Hausdorff distance \(1\) from the identity's singleton range even as the symbols converge uniformly to the identity.
