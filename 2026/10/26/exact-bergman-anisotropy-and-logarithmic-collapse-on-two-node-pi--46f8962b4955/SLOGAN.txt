The two-node Nevanlinna–Pick body has an exactly calculable Bergman metric along its diagonal: the tangential eigenvalue is \(2/(1-|a|^2)^2\), and the transverse-to-tangential ratio is a strictly decreasing beta-moment quotient. The ratio diverges like \(10/(3\rho^2)\) for colliding nodes and approaches the bidisk value with the sharp correction \(4(1-\rho^2)\log(1/(1-\rho^2))\).
