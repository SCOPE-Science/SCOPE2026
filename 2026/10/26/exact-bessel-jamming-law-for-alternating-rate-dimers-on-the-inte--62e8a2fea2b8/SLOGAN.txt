Alternating the Poisson arrival rates of irreversible dimers on an infinite chain yields an exact two-parameter hypergeometric jamming law, reducing to the classical \(1-e^{-2}\) coverage for equal rates. When the weaker bond rate is a fraction \(\varepsilon\) of the stronger, the residual vacancy fraction is \(\varepsilon/2-5\varepsilon^2/6+O(\varepsilon^3)\), quantifying the near-perfect packing enabled by a strongly staggered substrate.
