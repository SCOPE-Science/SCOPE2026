In recursive cographs formed by uniformly duplicating a vertex and randomly joining the twin, a uniform vertex's degree has an exact beta-binomial law with parameters \((2q,2(1-q))\), while the founding vertex has parameters \((q,1-q)\). At symmetric joining, every possible degree has probability exactly \(1/n\) at every size \(n\), and the two normalized degree laws have different beta limits.
