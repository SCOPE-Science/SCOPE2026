# Reproducibility checks

Run `python VERIFY.py` in a directory containing the accompanying files. It prints a single JSON line, exactly matching `certificate.json`, with rational stationary means, second factorial moments and endpoint probabilities for each star having one through seven updating follower leaves. The verifier independently constructs the 2(m+1) by 2(m+1) Markov generator, solves the exact rational stationary linear system and confirms that the candidate weights satisfy stationarity and normalization. The finite calculation does not certify the arbitrary-size result, which follows from the proof in `RESULT.md`; the rare-event asymptotics follow separately from the stated elementary product bounds.
