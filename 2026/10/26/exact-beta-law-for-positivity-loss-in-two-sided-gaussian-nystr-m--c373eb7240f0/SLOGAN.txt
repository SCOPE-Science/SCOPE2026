On the identity matrix, two-sided Gaussian generalized Nyström followed by rank-one SVD truncation almost surely produces an indefinite symmetric part. Its negative-eigenvalue magnitude has an exact transformed-beta law with tail exponent \(\ell+1\) and finite \(q\)-th moment precisely for \(q<\ell+1\), quantifying a structural positivity defect not covered by Frobenius-error bounds.
