For a fixed rank-one normal forcing in Gaussian two-sided oblique low-rank dynamics, the exact squared spurious tangent leakage is a reciprocal product of two independent beta variables minus one. Its tail is \(t^{-s/2}\log t\) with a sharp coefficient, whereas one-sided Gaussian sketching has no logarithmic tail penalty; the mean squared leakage diverges at the practically relevant sketch size \(s=2\).
