On the eight-element Boolean algebra there are exactly \(7820\) binary \(\mathsf T\)-preconditionals and \(1021\) binary \(\mathsf F\)-preconditionals, despite only \(125\) and \(8\) respective unary negation reducts. This is the smallest Boolean carrier where negation fails to determine the binary conditional; one \(\mathsf F\)-negation has \(729\) distinct lifts.
