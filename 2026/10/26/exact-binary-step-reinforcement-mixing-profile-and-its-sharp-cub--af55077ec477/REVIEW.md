# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The binary Fourier bias equals the probability of all-even components in the Bernoulli-percolated random recursive tree. The two root-parity generating functions satisfy the proved coupled differential system. Formal inversion is justified at the origin; at \(\alpha=1/3\) it reduces to the cubic \(z=3u-u^3\). Lagrange inversion gives the stated exact binomial sum at every even length. The negative-binomial central limit theorem gives the sharp constant \(3/4\) for total variation. A standalone exact-rational verifier checked 60 coefficient comparisons, 30 direct increment-history comparisons, and six explicit-sum cases.

## Originality

**PASS, with a residual historical terminology risk.** The 2026 finite-group source proves the forest representation and exponential mixing and already implies the elementary parity-evenness reduction. That reduction alone is not counted as new. The new claim is the exact enumeration through a coupled increasing-tree system and the resulting complete rational parity profile, including its sharp asymptotic constant, at \(\alpha=1/3\). The companion paper gives transition estimates, while the earlier recursive-tree work treats root-cluster moments. A matching all-even-component enumeration under other terminology may exist; this is disclosed in the structured assessment.

## Value

**PASS.** The exact one-time profile resolves a natural small-group benchmark for non-Markovian mixing, quantifying an infinite odd/even alternation and an explicit exponentially small even-time defect with the true prefactor. The all-parameter differential system is a reusable enumerative tool, not a numerical census.

## Closest literature and limitations

The relevant primary works are Peres and Qin (arXiv:2604.07207v1 and arXiv:2604.07227v1) and Kürsten (arXiv:1512.05275v1). The result is confined to one-time binary marginals; the elementary cubic closed form is proved for copying probability \(1/3\), while other parameters are represented by formal power series. Independent validation has not been performed.
