For the two canonical recursively randomly twisted hypercube models, the exact probability of retaining bipartiteness is a product of central-binomial reciprocals, but its negative logarithm grows as \((\log 2)n2^n/2\) with independent children versus \((\log 2)2^n\) when children are duplicated. Conditioning on this exceptional period-two random-walk event yields an explicit independent fair-orientation matching law at every genuinely sampled recursion node.
