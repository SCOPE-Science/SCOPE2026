Globally onto bounded morphisms between finite crown Kripke frames are exactly edge-covering cyclic lazy walks, except at the four-point target where each crossing has two independent minima choices. The exact counts have a transfer-matrix inclusion–exclusion formula, an explicit four-point-target expression \(3^n+(-1)^n-2^{n+1}+2\), and a first zero-winding edge-cover threshold at source length \(2m\).
