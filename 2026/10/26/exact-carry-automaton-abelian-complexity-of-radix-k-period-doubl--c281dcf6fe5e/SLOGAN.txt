For binary length-\(k\) period-doubling substitutions with \(k\ge3\), every Abelian-complexity value has an exact two-state radix-carry formula computable in logarithmic time. Their complexity reaches \(m+1\) at the explicit lengths \((k^{2m}-1)/(k+1)\), even though every window of length \(k^r\) is balanced to within one occurrence.
