Over every finite field of size q in characteristic five, the smooth equations y³ = x⁵ + ax³ + bx + c have exactly q(q−1), (q−1)², and (q−1)³ coefficient triples in the geometric (5-rank, a-number) strata (0,4), (0,2), and (2,2), respectively. The count is of fixed-coordinate equations, not isomorphism classes.
