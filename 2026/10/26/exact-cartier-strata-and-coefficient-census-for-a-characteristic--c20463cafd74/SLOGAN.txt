For the smooth cyclic trigonal genus-four curves \(y^3=x^5+a x^3+b x+c\) in characteristic \(5\), the Jacobian's \((5\text{-rank},a\text{-number})\) is exactly \((0,4)\), \((0,2)\), or \((2,2)\) according as \(a=0\), \(a\ne0,\ b=-a^2\), or \(a\ne0,\ b\ne-a^2\). Over every \(\mathbb F_q\) of characteristic \(5\), the corresponding numbers of smooth coefficient-marked presentations are \(q(q-1)\), \((q-1)^2\), and \((q-1)^3\), respectively; these are not isomorphism-class counts.
