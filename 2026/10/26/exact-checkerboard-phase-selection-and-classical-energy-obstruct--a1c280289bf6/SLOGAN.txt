For Allen–Cahn splitting on every finite regular bipartite graph, the two symmetric Strang orientations select different exact checkerboard equilibria: \(Y_{\mathrm{DRD}}=\sinh((1-\delta)h)/\sinh h\), while \(Y_{\mathrm{RDR}}=\sinh((1-\delta)h)/(\sinh((1-\delta)h)+\sinh(\delta h))\). Except for a sharp RDR symmetry point, stepping from a true nonconstant equilibrium **increases the original physical energy** for every positive step, even though established dissipation results concern a modified energy.
