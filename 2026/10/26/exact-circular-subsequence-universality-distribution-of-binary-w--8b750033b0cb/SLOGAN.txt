The full distribution of binary words by circular subsequence-universality index is a rational bivariate series: \(\frac{z(1+4zu)}{1-z-2uz^2}+\frac{z}{1-z}-\frac{2uz^2}{1-uz^2}\). An exact matching characterization converts the circular word problem into parity-restricted runs of cyclic transitions and gives a closed formula for every length and index.
