For unordered multisets with indexed occurrences, at every d≥2 and n≥2, taking the complement changes the zero-sum condition into a pair of occurrences summing to the total T. At T=0 the allowable objects are distinct-element zero-sum subsets, giving the stated binomial and W term by character orthogonality. At T≠0 at most one endpoint of each {x,x+T} pair is occupied. Projection onto the quotient by <T> gives the U term: each nontrivial quotient character gives (E²−O²)^(m/2)=1 for the even and odd occupancy series, hence zero coefficient in positive degree. Remove the V term of all-even occupancies, and flip the endpoint of a fixed odd-occupancy pair to divide the projected-zero remainder equally between totals 0 and T. The same criterion gives B_d(2)=0. At d=3,n=8 a nonzero total forces exactly four odd multiplicities on four points, since zero odd counts give T=0 and two give a prohibited pair. The four points must form an affine basis, and conversely each such basis works. There are 56 bases as unordered sets; the two multiplicity patterns each form an orbit by affine transitivity. Affine translations preserve the condition because six is even. The resulting counts are 1, 224, and 336.
