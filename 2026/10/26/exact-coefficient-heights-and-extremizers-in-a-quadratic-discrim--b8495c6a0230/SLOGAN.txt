For the positive-density set of integer parameters defining imaginary quadratic fields with fundamental discriminant \(-[4m(m+1)-1]\), the smallest defining quadratic has coefficient height exactly \(m+1\), whereas the smallest *monic* one has height \(m(m+1)\). All minimum-height polynomials are classified by four consecutive-coefficient forms, with two additional symmetric forms only when \(4m+5\) is an appropriately bounded square.
