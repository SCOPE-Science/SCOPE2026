For the truncated Witt Lie algebra \(W=\operatorname{Der}_{\mathbb F_q}(\mathbb F_q[x]/(x^p))\), with odd prime \(p\) and \(t=(p-1)/2\), the exact number of ordered pairwise-commuting triples is \((q+1)^2q^{3t-1}+(q^2-1)q^{2t+1}-(2q+1)q^{2t-1}\). A valuation-based centralizer decomposition yields a field-uniform count without assuming algebraic closure.
