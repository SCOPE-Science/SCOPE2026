A binary word has no mixed Abelian-square factor precisely when it has at most two runs, has three runs with an odd middle run, or has four runs with odd internal runs dominating their adjacent external runs. For positive zero/one counts \(x,y\), there are exactly \(2+(y-1)\mathbf 1_{2\nmid x}+(x-1)\mathbf 1_{2\nmid y}+2\lfloor x/4\rfloor\lfloor y/4\rfloor\) such words, with total length-\(n\) count asymptotic to \(n^3/48\).
