# Scientific review: Exact conservative flux-cap feasibility for Active Flux total variation

**Correctness — PASS.** The paper states the exact periodic domain, frozen endpoints, signed capped flux corrections, global objective and precise quantifiers. Interval-distance decomposition, LP minimax, signed ternary coarea, cyclic dynamic programming, and connected-component cut decomposition give a complete proof. Independent exact-rational verification exhausts \(3^n\) labels in 504 small test instances and compares a separate primal LP implementation; six examples independently check the growing bottleneck family. Numerical checks are corroboration, not proof.

**Originality — PASS with remaining risk.** The relevant full arXiv text of Barsukow (2026) contains matching variation and conservative update formulas but gives local Harten-style sufficient flux limiter conditions, not the exact global capped signed-correction frontier. The full open-access Lu–Hochbaum (2021) article gives generic convex difference-penalty algorithms rather than the sharp specialized three-state exact formula or linear-time cap evaluation. Published-record searches of the source, formula, aliases, and cyclic interval cut yielded no matching claim. Classical graph-cut duality is recognized as an underlying tool rather than claimed as invented. An older comprehensive convex/min-cut theorem may subsume this exact specialization; Weiss et al. (2009) and Rider–Liles (2024) were accessible only at abstract/metadata level in this review, so a full-text noncoverage assessment of those is withheld.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** A mathematically natural quantitative question in the Active Flux two-DOF formulation—whether globally TV-controlled conservative means can be reached by uniformly capped signed corrections—has an exact and computable answer, not merely sufficient conditions. The exact O(n) variation evaluation and O(n) no-extra-variation cap replace potentially high-dimensional LP searches. The explicit alternating endpoint family shows an unavoidable collective bottleneck growing linearly in mesh size even when isolated-cell excesses are identical and bounded.

**Scope and limitations.** Fixed updated point values; periodic scalar data; independent capped signed flux corrections. No promise that a particular PDE flux quadrature realizes these corrections, nor a CFL theorem, accuracy result, or multistep stability. General convex optimization might provide an implicit equivalent; external independent originality validation is still desirable.
