For weighted least-squares fitting of a two-row supermodular array, the exact coordinatewise Lipschitz constant is \(1+2\max_j\frac{\max(a_j,b_j)}{a_j+b_j}(1-c_j/\sum_i c_i)\), where \(c_j=a_jb_j/(a_j+b_j)\). Across all positive weights, its complete range is \([2-1/m,3)\): equal weights are uniquely best, while extreme heterogeneous weights approach amplification three.
