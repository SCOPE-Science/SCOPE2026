For every \(n\ge3\), the number of binary shorthand universal cycles covering each length-\(n-1\) word of weight one or two exactly once, modulo rotation, is \(2^{\lfloor(n-2)/2\rfloor}\). Forced-path contraction turns their de Bruijn graph into a rotation-reflection digraph, whose spanning in-tree count has a closed Fourier evaluation.
