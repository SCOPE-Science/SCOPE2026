For every finite \(k\)-regular graph with \(k\ge2\), the full-degree coalition number is four exactly when the graph is bipartite, and three otherwise. For a bipartite graph with \(2m\) vertices and \(c\) components, the exact number of unlabeled optimal four-block partitions is \(2^{c-1}(2^{m-1}-1)^2\), minus the component-orientation correction \(2^{c-2}(2^{c-1}-1)\) when \(c\ge2\).
