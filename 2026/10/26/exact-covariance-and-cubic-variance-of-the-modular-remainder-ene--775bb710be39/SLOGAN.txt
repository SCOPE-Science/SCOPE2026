For modular energy sampled across its full least-common-multiple period, each pair of remainders has covariance \((\gcd(a,b)^2-1)/12\), yielding an exact Jordan-totient variance formula. The variance grows as \(\frac{2\zeta(2)-\zeta(3)}{36\zeta(3)}m^3\), and the energy divided by \(m^2\) tends to \(1/4\) almost surely under Haar-uniform compatible residues.
