For a uniform random vertex coloring with exactly prescribed class sizes, the entire pairwise same-color covariance matrix has two nonconstant eigenvalues, determined by the size-biased variance of the class sizes. These yield an exact variance formula and classify every graph whose monochromatic-edge count is deterministic: the exceptional classes are regular graphs for the \((N-1,1)\) composition and stars or their complements for nontrivial balanced compositions.
