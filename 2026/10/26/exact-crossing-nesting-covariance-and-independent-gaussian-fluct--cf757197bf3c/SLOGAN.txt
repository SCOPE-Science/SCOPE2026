For a uniformly random perfect matching on \(2n\) ordered endpoints, crossing-pair and nesting-pair counts have exact correlation \(-5/[2(n+3)]\), yet their centered \(n^{-3/2}\)-scaled limits are independent Gaussians of variance \(1/45\). Orthogonal first-order chord projections explain why the finite negative covariance is one order smaller than the individual variances; the finite joint distribution itself was previously described by a generating function.
