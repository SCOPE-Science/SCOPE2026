For a one-dimensional lattice perturbed by a stationary Gaussian AR(1) field of marginal variance \(\sigma^2\) and correlation parameter \(0\le\rho<1\), the probability of an empty interval of length \(L\) has exact cubic logarithmic cost \(\lim_{L\to\infty}-L^{-3}\log V(L)=(1-\rho)/(24\sigma^2(1+\rho))\). The dependence-sensitive constant is the reciprocal of twenty-four times the displacement field's long-run variance.
