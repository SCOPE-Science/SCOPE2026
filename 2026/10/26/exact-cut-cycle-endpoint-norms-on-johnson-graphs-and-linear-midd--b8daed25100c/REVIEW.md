# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The claim fixes the Johnson graph, real edge-coordinate norm and canonical orthogonal projections. The endpoint formula is obtained from a dipole Laplace problem, an exact antisymmetric orbit reduction, strict positivity and monotonicity from the diagonally dominant orbit recurrence, detailed-balance moment identities, and a complete count of edge-gradient magnitudes. Edge transitivity makes one verified column norm the global \(\ell_1\) norm, symmetry gives \(\ell_\infty\), and the trace identity independently yields the complementary cycle norm. The source proof covers every integer \(n\ge2k\ge6\) and the claimed limits. Exact-fraction code verifies 308 orbit configurations and five independently constructed full graphs (2,036 edges altogether). The argument is not an experimental extrapolation.

## Originality — PASS, with identified residual risk
The nearest primary source is Dilworth–Kutzarova–Ostrovskii (2023, arXiv:2305.12582), whose inspected body concerns general cut/cycle spaces, discrete tori and Hamming product graphs, not constant-weight Johnson slices. An earlier exact rank-two triangular-graph result is already known and intentionally excluded by \(k\ge3\). The indexed Johnson primitive-idempotent endpoint result concerns a projector on **vertex functions**, rather than edge cut/cycle projections, and does not imply the present expressions. Searches for Johnson/Hodge/cut/transport-current aliases found no directly covering result. The possibility of a less-indexed Johnson current calculation remains a bibliographic risk; absence from searches is not proof of uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The theorem gives closed, sharp endpoint norms for the full two-parameter Johnson graph family beyond rank two. More substantively, it identifies a crossover from bounded norm at fixed slice rank to linear norm growth for proportional ranks, with exact coefficient \(\alpha(1-\alpha)\) and sharp \(1/4\) at balanced slices. This determines how the canonical edge Hodge decomposition amplifies \(\ell_1\) signals on graphs whose degree already grows quadratically in the balanced case, a natural question in finite graph functional analysis.

## Scope and limitations
Only canonical orthogonal projections on real unweighted graph edge spaces are studied; no minimum over all projections is asserted. Literature access to one Johnson vertex-spectral result was abstract-only, and equivalences in less-indexed electrical-network work cannot be excluded.
