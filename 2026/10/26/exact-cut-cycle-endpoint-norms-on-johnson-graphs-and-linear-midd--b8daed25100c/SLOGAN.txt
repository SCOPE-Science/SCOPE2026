For every Johnson graph \(J(n,k)\) with \(n\ge2k\ge6\), the canonical real edge cut projector has exact endpoint norm \(k+1-2/n-k(k-1)/(n-1)\), and the complementary cycle norm differs by precisely \(1-4(1-1/\binom nk)/[k(n-k)]\). On balanced hypersimplex slices both norms grow sharply like \(n/4\), although for each fixed rank \(k\) they remain bounded as \(n\to\infty\).
