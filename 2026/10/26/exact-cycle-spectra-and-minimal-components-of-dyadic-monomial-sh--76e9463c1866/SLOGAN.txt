For every degree \(d\ge3\), the dyadic permutation \(x\mapsto x+2x^d\) has an exact orbit-length formula at every power-of-two modulus. Its \(2\)-adic dynamics has one or two minimal components on the odd units according to degree parity and exactly \(2^{(d-1)s}\) components on valuation shell \(s\ge1\).
