For a binary circle with four nonempty alternating runs \((a,b,c,d)\), the maximum cyclic antipalindromic subsequence length is exactly \(2\max\{\min(a+c,b),\min(a+c,d),\min(b+d,a),\min(b+d,c),\min(a,d)+\min(b,c),\min(a,b)+\min(c,d)\}\). Every balanced such circle with \(s\) copies of each symbol achieves at least \(2\lceil3s/4\rceil\) matched positions, and this bound is attained for every \(s\ge2\); the sharp example for divisible lengths was known previously.
