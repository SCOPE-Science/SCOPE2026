Among commutative degree-three nilpotent subsemigroups of the full transformation semigroup on \(n=4,5,6,7\) points, the sharp largest orders are \(3,6,12,36\). Every extremizer belongs to one explicit two-layer construction, yielding \(24,120,1080,2520\) labeled extremizers respectively, and a unique conjugacy class on seven points; the cutoff is proved by exhaustive finite commuting-clique enumeration.
