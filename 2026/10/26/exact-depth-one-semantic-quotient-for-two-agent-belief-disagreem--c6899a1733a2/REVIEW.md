# Review of the finite-variable depth-one strong-disagreement quotient

**Correctness — PASS.** Formula (1) is an immediate, exact calculation from universal Kripke truth, including both orientations. The overlap case forces all tests false, whereas the disjoint case has exactly two inclusion-minimal positive event supports; this proves the complete invariant in both directions. Every proposed invariant is realized by explicitly defined successor rows satisfying seriality, transitivity and Euclideanness. The multinomial support count and the no-fixed-point involution justify the formula. The world lower bound counts mutually incompatible successor valuations and a necessary extra root; the construction attains it. The two two-world frames and the value of the nested formula are derived directly. The embedded checker exhaustively checks finite support sets at five sizes and both two-world frames, not the universal quantifiers.

**Originality — PASS within accessible primary evidence.** Ding and Yang (arXiv:2603.26268, 2026) explicitly introduce/axiomatize the group-disagreement bundle on KD45 and give an abstract expressivity/bisimulation theory, so neither that formalism nor general completeness is presented as new. The inspected sections on bundle expressivity and group disagreement do not give this finite-variable depth-one semantic quotient, its exact enumeration, minimum representative sizes, or the sharp depth-two boundary. Chen and Pan's 2017 prior strong-disagreement work is a closer possible priority source: its abstract was inspected, but its full text was inaccessible and the full-text comparison is not claimed. Fan's 2025 bundled-operator paper addresses axiomatization rather than these counts. Semantic-record searches, including searches of older support-equivalence terminology, did not identify an explicitly covering published claim. The unresolved 2017 text, other older two-replica formulations, and the possibility of extracting some part from generic bisimulation results are residual risks; a negative search is not a uniqueness certificate.

**Value — PASS.** The quotient is a natural complete classification of every depth-one observable state, not a single chosen parameter or example. Exact type/formula counts and individually minimal world sizes characterize finite-variable expressivity and provide optimal small-model bounds. The two-world distinction determines the first strict nesting level and shows exactly where the finite invariant ceases to suffice. Together these supply a mathematically motivated boundary for an operator whose general axiomatization is an active subject.
