# Self-review

## Correctness
PASS. The repeated-attempt process and torus size are specified. The argument conditions on a root B attempt and exactly one earlier off-root B attempt to quadratic order. The competing-clock integrals, exact overlaps, parameter change, and fixed-volume Taylor remainder are reconstructed; the supplied checker verifies the rational algebra. This is an analytic small-parameter result, not a Monte Carlo extrapolation.

## Originality
PASS within the present literature comparison. Martins, Dickman, and Ziff (arXiv:2211.04622, Sections II–III.2) supply the exact microscopic model and the linear coefficient, and discuss a tentative but different quadratic number, 493/1800, obtained via a two-designated-B-attempt calculation. The present exact 71/225 reflects delayed B attempts after blocked A attempts, which are allowed by the original process. Related one-attempt adsorption and general graph-deposition literature were considered; other equivalent derivations may exist and require independent priority assessment.

## Scientific value
PASS. The second-order dilute-minority curvature is a natural observable expressly studied by the motivating work. An exact finite-size-independent coefficient distinguishes genuine repeated-attempt adsorption kinetics from a simplified sparse-input approximation, clarifying early minority clustering effects.

## Limitations
The expansion is proved for each fixed square torus L>=7, not uniformly as L tends to infinity. Higher-order terms and percolation statistics are not determined. Mathematical priority and replication have not been independently evaluated.
