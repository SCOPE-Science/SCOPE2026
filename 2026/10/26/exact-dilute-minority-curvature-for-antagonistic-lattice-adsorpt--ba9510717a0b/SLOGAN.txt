For square-lattice two-species antagonistic adsorption with repeated Poisson attempts, the rare-minority jammed coverage has the exact finite-torus expansion \(\theta_B(p)=p/5+(71/225)p^2+O_L(p^3)\) for every \(L\ge7\). A later minority attempt can succeed after its site's earlier majority attempt was blocked, changing the quadratic curvature relative to a two-designated-attempt approximation.
