For a Brownian particle searching for one transparent blinking target, the probabilities of first capture from either side have exact closed forms even when position resets leave the target's state untouched. Resetting strictly increases capture from the initial side for every gate initialization, while the wrong-side probability has the universal strong-reset law \(\alpha/(4r)\) and the stationary-gate weak-reset response begins with a \(\sqrt r\) cusp.
