# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The proof covers every integer \(n\ge3\): the complete-graph small cases; the characterization of four-colourability by perfect codes, with a self-contained binary-equation argument; the exceptional sixteen-vertex packing bound; a residue-four construction; and five explicit finite base colourings with a local four-column extension lemma. Two independently built graph implementations check all base patterns and the one-step repeat, and additional exact searches test the small boundary cases. Finite checks are used only for finite pattern obligations, not as a substitute for the infinite induction.

## Originality

PASS subject to publication-priority uncertainty. The closest same-graph primary result is the previously established efficient-domination classification [3, Lemma 2.2]. It implies the four-colour obstruction but not the full square-chromatic values or the explicit optimal five-colour constructions. The closest square-colouring study [2] concerns Cartesian products of cycles and untwisted prisms, not a Möbius-twisted ladder. Petrosyan's edge interval colouring and the Möbius-ladder strong edge index address distinct invariants. Accessible research-index comparisons and search variants found no dominating published statement. Full-source access limitations and unindexed equivalent descriptions remain risks.

## Value

PASS. Exact square colouring is a natural radio/channel-separation invariant for a standard infinite family of cubic nonplanar networks. The complete classification identifies an arithmetic perfect-code class, isolates a surprisingly sharp sixteen-vertex exception, and supplies uniform five-colour constructions for every remaining large width. The central achievement is an infinite exact optimum, not a table or cosmetic parameter variant.
