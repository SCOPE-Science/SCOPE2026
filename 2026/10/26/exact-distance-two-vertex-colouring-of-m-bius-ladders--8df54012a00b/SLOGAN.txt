The square of the Möbius ladder \(M_{2n}\) needs four colours precisely when \(n\equiv2\pmod4\), apart from the separately specified smallest widths; otherwise it needs five, with the exceptional values \(6,8,6\) at \(n=3,4,8\). A perfect-code obstruction and repeatable four-column colour patterns give a classification for every \(n\ge3\).
