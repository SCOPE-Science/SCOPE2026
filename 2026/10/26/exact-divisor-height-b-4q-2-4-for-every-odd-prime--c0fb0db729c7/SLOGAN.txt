For every odd prime \(q\), every integer polynomial divisor of \(X^{4q^2}-1\) has coefficients of absolute value at most four, and the bound is attained: \(B(4q^2)=4\). A residue-class coefficient-mass lemma for divisors of \(X^{4q}-1\) supplies the uniform upper bound and proves the \(p=2\) case of a 2010 prime-square-pair height conjecture.
