For the cumulative binary digit deficit \(a(n)\) (OEIS A268289), every dyadic shell \(2^k\leq n<2^{k+1}\) has exact minimum \(\lfloor2^{k+1}/3\rfloor\). All minimizers are classified by a finite binary-word grammar made of `01`/`10` pairs with parity-dependent terminal bits, resolving the attainment question left open in the motivating study.
