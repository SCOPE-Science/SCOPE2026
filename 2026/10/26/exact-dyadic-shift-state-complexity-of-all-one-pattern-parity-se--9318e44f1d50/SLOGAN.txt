For the parity of overlapping runs of \(m\ge3\) consecutive ones in binary integers, a dyadic shift \(2^r\) has exactly \(2m+\sum_{k=0}^r A_m(k)+\mathbf1_{\{r=m\}}\) states in its minimal least-significant-digit-first automaton, where \(A_m(k)=k+1\) for \(k<m\) and \(A_m(k)=m+1+\min(m-1,k-m)\) otherwise. The complexity stabilizes at \(2mr+m(5-2m)\) when \(r\ge2m-1\), because no distinct reachable carry/run/parity states can merge.
