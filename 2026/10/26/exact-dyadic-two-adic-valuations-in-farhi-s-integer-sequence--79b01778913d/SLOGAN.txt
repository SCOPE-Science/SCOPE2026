At every dyadic index \(n=2^m\) with \(m\ge4\), Farhi's integer sequence satisfies the exact divisibility law \(\nu_2(a_n)=n+2m-3\). The extra cancellation arises because both the two-adic logarithmic and dilogarithmic sums vanish at the argument \(2\), leaving a uniquely dominant trilogarithmic term.
