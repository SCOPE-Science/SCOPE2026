# Scientific review: exact cycle-prism edge-open packing

**Correctness — PASS.** The independent-leaf argument gives \(\rho_e^{\mathrm o}\le\alpha\). Alternation along the rungs establishes the odd-length bound; if an even prism attains \(n\), the full bipartition of leaves requires disjoint three-leaf stars, forcing \(3\mid n\). Nine directly checkable cyclic mask patterns, together with a specified state-preserving splice, attain the claimed values in all lengths. Period-three binary center equations prove exactly six maximizing edge sets when \(6\mid n\). The independent implementation checks exact optima through 40 rungs and all construction outputs through 200 rungs. No extrapolation is used.

**Originality — PASS with residual risk.** The 2022 founding article gives elementary families, while the 2024/2025 Cartesian-product article gives only general inequalities for this product and covers certain hypercubes. The 2026 algorithmic articles address graph classes rather than an exact cycle-prism formula. Direct full-text section comparisons and searches did not identify an implication giving both the mod-six formula and sixfold rigidity. Vertex open packings of prisms are a distinct invariant. An uncatalogued equivalent result under injective edge colouring remains a bibliographic risk.

**Value — PASS.** Circular ladders are a canonical cubic Cartesian-product family. The theorem determines a natural packing invariant uniformly, isolates the precise divisibility obstruction to saturating the independence bound, and classifies all saturating configurations. These periodic sharpness and rigidity phenomena are not generic consequences of the published algorithmic tractability statements. No claim is made about larger graph-product classes.

Limitations: the finite computations are corroboration, not a substitute for the two combinatorial arguments. Definitive literature priority and independent validation remain open.
