For a normal matrix with eigenvalues at the four corners \(z_0\pm u\pm v\) of an oblique parallelogram, the sharp two-dimensional elliptical width is \(\sqrt{8\operatorname{Im}(\overline u v)^2/(A+\sqrt{A^2-4B^2})}\), where \(A=|u|^2+|v|^2\) and \(B=\operatorname{Re}(\overline u v)\). Every maximizing compression has a circular numerical range centered at \(z_0\), and its unique optimal opposite-vertex probability weighting is explicit; the same width is the exact optimal positive-block norm defect by a known extremal identity.
