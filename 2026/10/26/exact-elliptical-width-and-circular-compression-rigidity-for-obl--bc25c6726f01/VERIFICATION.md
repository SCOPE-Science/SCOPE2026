# Verification record

The closed-form theorem is analytically proved by a Schur-eigenvector projection identity, strict covariance symmetrization, unique scalar optimization, and explicit construction of the maximizing compression. Uniqueness of every maximizing subspace is derived from equality in the projection and covariance steps. The previously published general positive-block identity is cited, not independently re-proved.

The bundled `verify_oblique.py` is a reproducible standard-library numerical comparison and witness checker. It tests five oblique or orthogonal spectral pairs, validates the normalized Schur witnesses, and compares 144000 deterministic trial probability distributions with the claimed optimum. Arithmetic tolerances are finite checks and do not establish the continuum theorem. The remaining scientific limitations are non-parallelogram and higher-rank compressions, and historical originality under unindexed alternate terminology.
