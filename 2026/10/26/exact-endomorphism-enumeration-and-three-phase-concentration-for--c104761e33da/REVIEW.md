The binomial expression computes exact endomorphism sizes for all \(r\), the sharp asymptotic reveals a factor of three missed by naive unrestricted Boolean-image counting, and its three regimes explain the leading behavior structurally. These are natural global semigroup invariants rather than arbitrary finite parameter slices.
