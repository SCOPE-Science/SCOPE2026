For an alternating \(2m\)-site quantum chain with one lossy endpoint, the remote-end mean absorption time is exactly \(m/\gamma+\gamma[(m-1)/b^2+b^{2m-2}/(2a^{2m})]\). Optimizing the loss strength produces an exponential lifetime penalty on the \(b>a\) boundary-mode side of the SSH transition but only linear growth for \(a\ge b\).
