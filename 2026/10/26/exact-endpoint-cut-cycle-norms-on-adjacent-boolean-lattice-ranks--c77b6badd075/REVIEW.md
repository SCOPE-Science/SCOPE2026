# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The incidence Schur complement is exactly the Johnson Laplacian. Radial shell-current equations give positive Green differences; those differences establish the sign pattern of every projected edge current, with the only nontrivial sign inequality proved using decreasing shell ratios. A separately verified polynomial dual potential evaluates the signed current sum. The diagonal effective resistance and complementary norm follow from the source-vertex equation. An exact-rational checker independently agrees in 54 orbit configurations and nine grounded whole-graph instances. Computation supports, but does not replace, the all-parameter proof.

**Originality — PASS, subject to residual literature risk.** Primary comparison: Dilworth–Kutzarova–Ostrovskii (2023), whose main Hamming-cube theorem is for the entire cube rather than a two-level inclusion graph; the corresponding edge projections are not restrictions of one another. Their 2018 paper develops broader transportation-cost and cycle-projection machinery but not the exact two-rank endpoint formula in its inspected relevant sections. A Johnson-graph Schur complement provides the vertex Green equation, not the absolute-current sum on the bipartite edge space. Exact-phrase and equivalent-object searches did not identify an implication that covers the displayed formulas. An older or differently named inclusion-graph electrical calculation remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** Two consecutive Boolean ranks form a canonical nonregular truncation of the hypercube. The theorem gives both natural endpoint operator invariants for every pair of ranks and exposes a fixed-rank versus proportional-rank growth change. The linear middle-rank amplification cannot be read from the full hypercube's projection constant or from simple graph degree arguments.

The result does not claim minimum norms among nonorthogonal projections, weighted versions or independent validation.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
