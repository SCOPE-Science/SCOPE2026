The canonical cut projection on the bipartite inclusion graph between Boolean-lattice ranks \(k\) and \(k+1\) has exact endpoint norm \(2k+1-2k(k+1)/n\); its cycle complement has a closed formula determined by the same norm and a single effective resistance. Fixed ranks have bounded norms, while proportional ranks exhibit linear growth with limiting coefficient \(2\alpha(1-\alpha)\).
