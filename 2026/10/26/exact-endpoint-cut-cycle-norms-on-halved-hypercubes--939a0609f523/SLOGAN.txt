For every halved binary cube in dimension \(n\ge6\), the canonical real cut and cycle projections have exact endpoint norms \((n+5)/4-1/(n-1)\) and \((n+9)/4-1/(n-1)-(8-2^{4-n})/[n(n-1)]\), respectively. A positive Walsh–Green integral evaluates all absolute edge currents and shows that both norms grow with sharp slope \(1/4\), despite quadratic vertex degree.
