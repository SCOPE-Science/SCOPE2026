# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The unsigned-incidence reduction is an identity of concrete Laplacians. A cube heat-kernel calculation derives all Green levels; strict monotonicity fixes current signs; binomial-integral identities count the full edge gradient exactly, and the orthogonal projector trace gives the cycle norm. A separate exact grounded-Laplacian computation reproduces 124 columns in four graph sizes; finite checks corroborate but do not replace the proof.

**Originality — PASS, with residual bibliographic risk.** The most relevant earlier cut-projection theorem is Dilworth–Kutzarova–Ostrovskii (arXiv:2305.12582, Theorem 45), which treats the original cube. Liu–Cao–Pan–Elaiw (DOI:10.1155/2013/543189, Theorem 11) treats the same line graphs but calculates Kirchhoff indices, which cannot supply signed-current absolute column sums. Searches by line-graph, rectified-hypercube, endpoint-norm, and electrical-current formulations did not locate direct coverage. This does not exclude unindexed equivalent work.

**Value — PASS.** The line-graph transformation is a standard natural graph operation and the exact Banach endpoint projection norms had not been supplied by the consulted resistance-spectrum literature. The two all-dimensional formulas distinguish electrical-current amplification from Kirchhoff traces and quantify a structural halving of the leading slope relative to the ordinary cube.

**Limits.** The result concerns real, unweighted graphs and the canonical orthogonal projections only; neither optimal nonorthogonal projections nor intermediate \(\ell_p\) norms are covered. Novelty is assessed against accessible literature and indexed claims, not independently certified.
