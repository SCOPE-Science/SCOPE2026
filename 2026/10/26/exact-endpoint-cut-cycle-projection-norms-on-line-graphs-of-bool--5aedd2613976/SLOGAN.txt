The line graph of the Boolean \(n\)-cube has exact canonical cut-projection endpoint norm \(\frac{n^2+11n-14}{4n}-\frac{2}{n(n-1)}(1-\frac{n}{2^{n-1}})\) for every \(n\ge3\), with an equally explicit complementary cycle norm. Both norms grow with leading slope \(1/4\), half the corresponding \(1/2\) slope of the ordinary cube's cut projection.
