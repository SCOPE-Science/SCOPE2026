The canonical cut and cycle projection norms of every circular ladder \(C_n\square K_2\) are exact finite sums of a positive cyclic Green kernel. The maximizing cut edge is a rung at \(n=3\), ties at \(n=4\), and is a rim thereafter, while the norms exhibit a sharp logarithmic correction of \(2\log n/[n\log(2+\sqrt3)]\) and the cut projection strictly dominates for every \(n\ge3\).
