# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** The proof independently reconstructs all finite-dimensional estimates: \(A-M\) is the positive rank-one matrix \(dww^*\), \(A+M\) is the sum of the positive leaf-sum-zero projector \(2P\) and a positive rank-one matrix, and every Gram diagonal equals \(t\). The standard explicit tensor embedding then bounds both operator and completely bounded norms. The rank-one input has trace norm one, and direct spectral decomposition of its masked image yields exactly \(t\). Trace duality and finite-corner exhaustion prove the infinite case. The exact-arithmetic checker reproduces the identities at 26 selected dimensions but is not the infinite proof.

**Originality (PASS, conditional on the inspected literature).** The October 2026 radial-tree paper treats Schatten exponents strictly between one and infinity and does not state an exact endpoint star formula. Levene (2014) **already proves the two-leaf special case** with norm \(9/7\), and his separate rectangular \([\mathbf1\ I_n]\) formula is not the square symmetric leaf-diagonal mask. The new general \(n\ge3\) formula, exact finite-size deficit, and infinite-star constant do not follow numerically from that special case or from general factorization criteria. The prior claim about the minimal row-and-column mask without the leaf diagonal, with Schatten exponents at most one, is a different projection. No independent comprehensive historical-priority audit has been performed.

**Value (PASS).** An exact endpoint norm along arbitrarily high-degree stars offers a quantitative counterpoint to qualitative endpoint difficulties for radial Schur multipliers. Explicit optimal trace-class inputs and completely bounded Gram certificates capture nontrivial finite-size behavior and the sharp \(5/3\) limiting obstruction. This is more than the isolated three-by-three calculation already in the literature.

**Residual risks.** A historical calculation of the same star Schur symbol might be unindexed or hidden in the literature. The result does not extend automatically to other tree geometries or Schatten exponents.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
