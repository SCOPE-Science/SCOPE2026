For a bi-infinite linear-spline grid whose successive knot spacings alternate between \(a\) and \(b\), the exact endpoint norm of orthogonal \(L^2\) projection is \(2-(a-b)^2/((3a+b)(a+3b))\). Equal spacing is uniquely worst within this family: increasing imbalance reduces the sharp constant toward \(5/3\), and the same constant is the optimal uniform bound for all finite periodic repeats of the two-cell mesh.
