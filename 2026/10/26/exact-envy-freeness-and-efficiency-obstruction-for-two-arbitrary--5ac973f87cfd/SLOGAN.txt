For two agents with arbitrary binary-valued preferences over complete item bundles, different valuation tables always permit an allocation that is both envy-free and Pareto optimal; all failures arise from identical nonzero valuations with no complementary pair of valued bundles. Under independent fair truth tables, the exact failure probability is \( (3^{2^{m-1}}-1)/16^{2^{m-1}} \) for \(m\ge1\) items.
