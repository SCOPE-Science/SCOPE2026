On a cycle whose vertices independently become conformists or rebels with equal probability, the number of binary pure Nash equilibria has exact first and second moments given by a two-state parity transfer and its four-state tensor lifts. Although an equilibrium exists with probability tending to one, the relative variance of the equilibrium count grows asymptotically as \(\bigl((5+\sqrt{17})/9\bigr)^n\), exposing strongly nonconcentrated equilibrium multiplicity.
