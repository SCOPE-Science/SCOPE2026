For the negative-doubling map with a symmetric open hole \((a,1-a)\), the Lebesgue mass surviving through time \(n\) is exactly \(2^{-n}[2a+2n(3a-1)_+]\) for \(0<a\le2/5\). The zero-entropy survivor family undergoes a sharp transition at \(a=1/3\) from geometric decay to decay with a linear prefactor, while the formula breaks immediately above \(a=2/5\).
