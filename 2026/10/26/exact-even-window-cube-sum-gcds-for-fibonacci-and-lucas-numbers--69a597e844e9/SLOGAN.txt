For every even window \(2m\), the GCD of all consecutive Fibonacci cubes equals \(2^{-\mathbf1_{3\mid m}}3^{\mathbf1_{4\mid m}}F_m\) for even \(m\), or the same factor times \(L_m\) for odd \(m\). The corresponding Lucas-cube GCD is exactly \(5\) times larger when \(m\) is even, and equal when \(m\) is odd, giving a complete solution to the even-window cubic GCD question for these sequences.
