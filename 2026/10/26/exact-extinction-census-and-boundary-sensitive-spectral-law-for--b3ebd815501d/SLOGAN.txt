For deterministic three-state excitation on an open ladder with \(n\) rungs, the exact number of extinguishing initial configurations obeys \(E_1=9\), \(E_2=57\) and \(E_n=7E_{n-1}-4E_{n-2}\). The extinction probability is asymptotic to a positive constant times \(((7+\sqrt{33})/18)^n\), without the Gaussian penalty imposed by the additional longitudinal cycle of a closed ladder.
