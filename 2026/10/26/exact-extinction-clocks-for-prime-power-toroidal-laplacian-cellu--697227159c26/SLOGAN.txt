The standard Laplacian over \(\mathbb F_p\) on a periodic \(p^a\)-by-\(p^b\) grid extinguishes every state after exactly \(2^{a-1}\) steps in characteristic two, or \((p^a+p^b)/2\) steps for odd \(p\) (with \(a\ge b\ge1\)). In odd characteristic its last nonzero iterate is the explicit rank-one total-mass operator, determining exactly which initial states survive until the final step.
