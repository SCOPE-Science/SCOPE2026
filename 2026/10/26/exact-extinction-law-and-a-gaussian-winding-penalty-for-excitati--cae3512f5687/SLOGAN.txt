For synchronous three-state Greenberg–Hastings excitation on every circular prism \(C_n\square K_2\), the number of extinguishing initial configurations is exactly the zero-Laurent coefficient of an explicit nine-state transfer-matrix trace. Its uniform-start extinction probability is asymptotic to \(1.839898348\,n^{-1/2}(0.7080312581)^n\), revealing a Gaussian winding penalty beyond the local square constraints.
