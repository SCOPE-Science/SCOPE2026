For consecutive integer speeds \(1,\ldots,n\), the measure of times simultaneously more than \(\delta\) away from every integer multiple has an exact finite Farey-neighbor formula at **every** threshold. Its slope breaks at every reciprocal \(1/s\) with \(n+1\le s\le2n-1\), except \(s=2n-2\) when \(n\) is even; at the union-bound radius \(1/(2n)\), the safe-time measure tends to \(1-6/\pi^2\).
