For a complete graph with one private pendant leaf at every vertex, the globally fastest symmetric Markov chain has an explicit optimal edge weighting and its second-largest eigenvalue modulus tends to \(4/5\) as the core grows. The maximum-degree and Metropolis prescriptions instead have spectral gaps of order \(1/m\), with an exact asymptotic optimal-to-heuristic gap ratio of \(m/5\).
