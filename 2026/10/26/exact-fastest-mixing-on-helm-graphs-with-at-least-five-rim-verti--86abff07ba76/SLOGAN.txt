For every helm graph with at least five rim vertices, fastest symmetric Markov mixing has a closed-form optimum: hub-to-rim transition probability \(1/n\), zero holding at the hub and rim, and an explicit positive rim-to-leaf weight. The sharp spectral gap is asymptotic to \(1/(2n)\), improving the maximum-degree spectral gap by the factor \((3+\sqrt5)/4\) in the large-graph limit.
