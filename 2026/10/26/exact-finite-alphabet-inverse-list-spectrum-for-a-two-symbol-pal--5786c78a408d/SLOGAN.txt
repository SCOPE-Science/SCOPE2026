For any alphabet size \(q\ge2\), the complete distribution of the number of distinct parents of a received word under one length-two palindromic duplication is encoded by an explicit rational bivariate generating function; in particular the nonbinary law is not a simple rescaling of the known binary run count. The inverse-list size for a uniform length-\(N\) receiver has a nondegenerate Gaussian limit with mean slope \((q^2-1)/q^4\) and variance slope \((q^2-1)(q^4-3q^2-2q+7)/q^8\), and the same law transfers to reverse-complement duplication through the established even-length conjugacy.
