For every fixed small side \(a\), the intersection size of two independent uniform spanning trees of \(K_{a,b}\), when \(b\ge2a-2\), splits exactly into an independent \(\operatorname{Bin}(b-2a+2,1/a)\) term and a bounded correction. For \(K_{3,n}\) the entire correction polynomial is explicit, yielding precisely \(9n(n-1)(n-2)(n-3)6^{n-4}\) ordered edge-disjoint spanning-tree pairs for \(n\ge4\).
