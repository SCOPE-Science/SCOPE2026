It provides finite-field fiber counts relevant to distributions of commuting nilpotent pairs, not an arbitrary numerical slice.

The result is not a classification of all commuting pairs, and the bundled finite computations do not verify extension fields. The literature search was bounded, and priority is not guaranteed.
