For \(n\ge3\), the exact number of nilpotent matrices of Jordan type \((n,n)\) commuting with a fixed \(J_{n+1}\oplus J_{n-1}\) over \(\mathbb F_q\) is \(q^{4n-8}(q-1)^2\bigl(q-(\gcd(n,q-1)+\gcd(n,q+1))/2+\mathbf1_{\mathrm{char}(\mathbb F_q)\nmid n}\bigr)\). This refines the known finite-field existence criterion to a complete exact cardinality and density formula.
