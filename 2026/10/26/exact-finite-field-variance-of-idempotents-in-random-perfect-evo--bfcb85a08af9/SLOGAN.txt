For a uniformly random perfect \(n\)-dimensional evolution algebra over \(\mathbb F_q\), \(n\ge2\), the number of nonzero idempotents has mean \(1\) and variance exactly \(1\) in characteristic two, but a strictly smaller explicit variance \(1-\frac{(q-1)((2q-1)^n-2q^n+1)}{2(q^n-1)(q^n-q)}\) in odd characteristic. The defect comes precisely from independent vectors whose coordinatewise squares become dependent.
