For positive-semidefinite Richardson iteration, the exact worst-case matrix-only backward error after \(k\) steps is \(\max\{1/(\alpha k),|1-\alpha|^k/[1-(1-\alpha)^k]\}\), attained by a null mode or top eigenmode. The original universal \(1/k\) guarantee survives all horizons exactly for \(1\le\alpha\le1+1/\sqrt3\), and a unique larger-than-unit step minimizes the worst error at each fixed horizon \(k\ge2\).
