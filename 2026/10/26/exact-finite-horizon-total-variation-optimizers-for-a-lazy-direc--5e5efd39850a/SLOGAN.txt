For a stay-or-clockwise random walk on the directed triangle, the unique spectral-rate optimizer \(p=1/2\) is never total-variation optimal after any fixed \(h\ge2\) steps: the only minimizers are \(p=1/2\pm\tan(\pi/(6h))/(2\sqrt3)\). Their exact worst-start distance is \(\sqrt3\sec^h(\pi/(6h))/(3\,2^h)\), yielding a persistent limiting relative improvement of \(1-\sqrt3/2\) over spectral tuning.
