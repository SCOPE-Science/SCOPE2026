# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. Proposition 4.6 of Huang–Jenkinson–Xu–Zhang gives the uniform invariant digit bound; integer q-block counts immediately give the upper bound. A rational upper mechanical word is admissible for every q because its every cyclic length-k block has at most \(\lceil kp/q\rceil\) ones, no more than the corresponding upper Sturmian prefix. For coprime content, the classical Borel–Laubie theorem makes that mechanical word the unique minimizer of the maximal cyclic rotation. The precise \(1/q^2\) proximity condition synchronizes the first q−1 digits of the irrational and rational ceiling sequences; equal digit counts prevent a first disagreement solely in the last digit. This proves rigidity. Infinitely many lower continued-fraction convergents meet the hypothesis. Exact integer enumeration corroborates the steps at finite sizes but is not the proof.

## Originality
PASS under examined evidence. The directly relevant 2026 source proves maximal digit density \(\alpha\) over all invariant measures and constructs some approximating periodic return loops. It does not provide the exact period-by-period floor formula or a theorem eliminating every competing orbit at lower rational convergents. Borel–Laubie’s classical extremality theorem is central and expressly credited, but it concerns fixed-Parikh Lyndon words rather than admissibility in a fixed irrational beta-shift. The historical survey of balanced-word optimization concerns distinct numerical and invariant-measure objectives. Published research-record searches and examined source texts show closely related work but no known covering same-domain statement. Alternative old terminology remains a genuine priority risk.

## Mathematical value
PASS. Quantitatively optimal bounded-period approximation to an aperiodic maximizing measure is an intrinsic ergodic-optimization invariant; the exact digit deficit and the infinite sequence of uniquely maximizing periodic orbits express an arithmetic rigidity mechanism rather than a small numerical improvement. The result distinguishes global irrational optimization, constrained rational approximation and uniqueness of periodic realizers.
