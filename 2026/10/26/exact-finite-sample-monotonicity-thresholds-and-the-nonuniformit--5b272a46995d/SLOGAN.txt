For the standard second-difference Whittaker–Henderson smoother, preserving the order of every nondecreasing input is restored on four, five, and six samples at the exact sharp strengths \(1/6\), \(7/10\), and \((14+\sqrt{181})/15\), respectively, although zero strength is always safe. Each fixed finite sample length eventually regains monotonicity under strong smoothing, but no single positive finite strength works uniformly over all sample lengths; on four samples the largest possible fitted adjacent decrease is exactly \((3-2\sqrt2)/8\) of the input range.
