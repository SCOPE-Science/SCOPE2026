# Scientific assessment

**Correctness.** Strict convexity of the fourth-power objective gives uniqueness. Conjugation and reversal then reduce the unrestricted complex problem to a one-variable problem. Fourier orthogonality gives the exact quartic; the derivative signs give a unique minimizing root, and signs of the rescaled cubic give the boundary constant.

**Originality and value.** The new scope is the exact solution and inverse-degree asymptotic for the specified polynomial family. General first-degree stationarity formulas and a positive lower bound for even exponents are given in Centner–Cheng–Felder (arXiv:2305.16068, Theorems 4.5 and 5.16). The rational-input construction in Cheng–Felder (arXiv:2310.16010, Proposition 3.2.3) already rules out a universal exclusion radius strictly larger than one. The present coefficients and rate give a quantitative benchmark for a natural increasing-input-degree family, not a new general obstruction or an all-input classification. An equivalent unindexed family calculation remains possible.
