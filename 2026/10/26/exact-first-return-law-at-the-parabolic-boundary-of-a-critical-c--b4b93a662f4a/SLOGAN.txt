At the neutral fixed-point boundary of a square-root cusp family, an explicit invariant density gives the **exact conditional first-return law**, \(\mu_J(\tau_J>n)=4a_n^2\), with \(a_{n+1}=a_n-a_n^2/2\) for every \(n\ge0\). The return tail is asymptotic to \(16n^{-2}\); its mean is exactly \(4\), while its second moment diverges.
