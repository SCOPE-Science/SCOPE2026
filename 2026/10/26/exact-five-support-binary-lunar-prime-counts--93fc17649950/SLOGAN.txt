Among odd binary lunar integers with exactly five one digits and highest bit at position \(m\), the number of composites is the explicit linear quasipolynomial \(2\lfloor(m-1)/2\rfloor+\mathbf1_{2\mid m}(m/2-1)-2\mathbf1_{3\mid m}-2\mathbf1_{4\mid m}\). Every composite arises from one of two classified five-point sumset families; consequently the composite fraction has sharp order \(m^{-2}\), rather than merely tending to zero.
