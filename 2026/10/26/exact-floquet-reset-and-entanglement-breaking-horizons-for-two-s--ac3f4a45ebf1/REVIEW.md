# Scientific review

## Correctness

PASS. The exact Bloch affine compositions, strictly positive factorizations of both fixed-state spectral defects, independent qubit Choi/PPT criterion, section-index argument from complete positivity, and rapid-switching series together establish the one claim for every positive dwell. Reproducibility checks numerically agree on the sampled domain, but finite checks do not establish the universal claims by themselves.

## Originality

PASS, best-knowledge, not a statement of independently certified priority. Wang's same physical Q6 example establishes the averaged pure attractor but not finite-dwell reset vectors or EB horizons. Trinh's generalized Bell-mixture separability bound covers the relevant PPT test and is acknowledged as prior art. Hanson, Rouzé and França establish a broad eventually-EB framework. Targeted published-record comparisons identify no equivalent finite-dwell/phase-shift formula; the main residual risk is an unlocated general Floquet-qubit theorem from which the special case follows.

## Value

PASS. The finding resolves a concrete operational gap in switching a continuous-time pure stabilizer: at every nonzero switching period the exact target is full-rank and the map becomes entanglement breaking after finitely many cycles, yet the continuous average never does. The sharp logarithmic physical horizon and one-cycle sampling-phase bound quantify this singular limit.

## Limitations

Only the specified qubit jumps, equal time intervals, ideal GKSL generators and cycle-to-cycle channels are treated. It is not a claim about arbitrary intermediate times, noisy controls, general dimensions or literature-wide priority.
