Alternating two single-jump qubit dissipators has an exact finite-dwell Floquet channel that is a mixture of the identity and a full-rank state reset, even though its continuously averaged generator resets toward a pure state. The entanglement-breaking horizon is finite at every positive dwell but grows as \(\tfrac12\log(4\sqrt3/h)\) in physical time for small dwell \(h\); two sampling sections have strictly ordered purities and indices differing by at most one cycle.
