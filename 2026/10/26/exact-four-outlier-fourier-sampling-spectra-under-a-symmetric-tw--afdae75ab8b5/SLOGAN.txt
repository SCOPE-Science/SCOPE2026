Moving two neighboring nodes symmetrically toward a collision in an otherwise uniform odd Fourier grid changes at most four sampling-Gram eigenvalues, all given by explicit Dirichlet-kernel radicals. At the critical square-sampling density, the least singular value is asymptotic to an exact multiple of the normalized gap, and the exact condition-number coefficient converges to \(\frac\pi4\sqrt{(3+\sqrt{9-64/\pi^2})/2}\).
