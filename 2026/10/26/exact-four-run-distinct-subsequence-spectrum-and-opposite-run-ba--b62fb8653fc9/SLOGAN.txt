For every four-run binary word \(0^a1^b0^c1^d\), the count of distinct nonempty scattered subsequences is exactly \(n+(a+c)(b+d)+bc(a+d)+abcd\). Opposite runs must be as equal as possible in every constrained extremizer, reducing the unrestricted four-run optimum to one explicit integer parameter and yielding \(M_4(n)=n^4/256+n^3/32+O(n^2)\).
