A half-circle coding of an affine two-torus skew rotation gives fair, jointly independent signs at every three distinct times, but its exact four-consecutive-bit law has the nonconstant fourth-order correlation \(C=1/3-16d^2+48(d-1/6)_+^2\), with \(d=\operatorname{dist}(\alpha,\frac12\mathbb Z)\). Four consecutive bits are perfectly independent exactly at the irrational phases \(\alpha\equiv\pm\sqrt3/12\pmod{1/2}\), even though the stationary process has zero entropy.
