For finite reflexive partial orders of height at most two, the \(k\)-generated Boolean modal algebra has exactly \(2^{q(2^q-1)}\) elements, where \(q=2^k\). The full modal-depth filtration stabilizes sharply at depth two, with \(q\), \(q2^{q-1}\), and \(q(2^q-1)\) distinguishable pointed types at depths zero, one, and two.
