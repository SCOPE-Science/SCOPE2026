For the natural three-point positive path tensor \(I_3+tS_3\) in real \(\ell_2^3\), the exact Fremlin projective norm switches from \(3\) to \(1+t^2+t^{-2}\) at \(t=1\), then to \(2\sqrt{1+t^2}\) at the golden-ratio threshold \(t=\sqrt{(1+\sqrt5)/2}\). Explicit positive rank-one majorants and dual contractions show both transitions are sharp and continuously differentiable but not twice differentiable.
