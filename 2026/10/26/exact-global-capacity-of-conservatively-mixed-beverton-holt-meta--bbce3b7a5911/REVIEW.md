# Scientific review of the global Beverton–Holt abundance bound

## Correctness — PASS

For every specified positive patch parameter and positive column-stochastic dispersal matrix, the positive fixed point exists uniquely by Perron–Frobenius, monotonicity, strict subhomogeneity, and bounding sub-/supersolutions. The explicit candidate has common local derivative \(\tau\in(0,1)\), and its total growth equals its total input by the identity \(C=B(t+t^{-1})\). The rank-one matrix \(D^*=v\mathbf 1^{\mathsf T}\) realizes that candidate as an equilibrium. A direct exact-tangent remainder, with positive denominators, proves the global deficit and all equality cases. Independent linear constraint counting gives dimension \((p-1)^2\). Comparing the tangent identity at the isolated carrying-capacity vector proves strict enhancement exactly for unequal local growth parameters. An exact \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\) benchmark and 780 deterministic random tests were run successfully; neither finite experiment is used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual historical-priority risk

The 2026 Bravo de la Parra study treats a general conservatively dispersed discrete-time model and characterizes sign changes of total asymptotic population; its explicit Beverton–Holt inequalities and constructive finite-dispersal arguments concentrate on two patches. The research question addressed here is different: the simultaneous **exact global supremum over all positive stochastic mixing matrices**, for every finite patch count, including a complete matrix-optimizer set and a quantitative loss identity. The source's inspected abstract, introduction, relevant two-patch discussion, conclusion, and proof excerpts do not report this result. Grumbach et al. (2023) analyze a symmetric two-patch parameter path, whereas Jorba-Cuscó et al. (2024) address continuous-time two-patch diffusion and Segura et al. (2025) address dispersal topology and prescribed families. Those theorems do not by their stated scope dominate the all-matrix result. Searches for optimal Beverton–Holt dispersal, maximal total abundance across stochastic matrices, and common-marginal allocations did not locate an implication-equivalent published result. Nevertheless, optimal resource allocation via concavity is a broad classical motif; an earlier equivalent formulation might exist. The 2026 publisher page was read in indexed sections because the page request was rate limited, and one relevant source was inspected via lawful accessible author/full-text material; these are stated access limits rather than proof of absence of prior art.

## Mathematical value — PASS

An explicit all-matrix optimum of equilibrium metapopulation capacity resolves a natural design problem posed by positive conservative dispersal, for arbitrary numbers of heterogeneous habitat patches. The closed form, all-optimal-matrices affine geometry, exact loss identity, and necessary-and-sufficient growth-heterogeneity criterion are stronger than a binary assessment of whether a given mixing rate helps. The deficit identity quantifies the cost of deviating from the optimal pre-growth spatial profile, and the rank-one formula is a constructive optimizer without requiring infinite-rate mixing. The result remains confined to the stated equilibrium model and does not address geography or migration costs.
