The analytic argument uses the first-passage generating-function recurrence, a palindromic quotient polynomial, explicit coefficients up to the midpoint, Descartes's rule of signs, and strict convexity of a Brownian exit-time Laplace transform. These arguments cover all integer cycle lengths at least three and all allowed distances and reset probabilities.

The standalone `verify.py` performs independent exact-rational finite-chain calculations and symbolic checks for many graph sizes and distances. The checks replay against the specific embedded script; such finite checking is corroborating evidence, not a proof of the universal theorem.
