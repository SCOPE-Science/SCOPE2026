The Ungar game on the vertex–edge incidence poset of a star with \(n\) leaves has exact nimber \(2n+1-\kappa(n)\), where \(\kappa(n)\) is the greatest integer with \(\kappa(n)(\kappa(n)+1)/2\leq n\). An accompanying two-parameter formula determines all independent-chain residual positions and gives an inductive explanation for the triangular-number transitions.
