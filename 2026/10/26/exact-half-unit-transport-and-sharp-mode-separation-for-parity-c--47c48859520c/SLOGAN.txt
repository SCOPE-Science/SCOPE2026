For every sum of independent \(\{0,\tfrac12,1\}\)-valued variables with nontrivial integer and half-integer components, the two conditional score distributions admit a coupling at **exact distance \(\tfrac12\)**, so \(W_p=\tfrac12\) for every \(1\le p\le\infty\). Their modes differ by at most the **sharp** \(\tfrac32\), improving the previously published \(\tfrac52\) comparison.
