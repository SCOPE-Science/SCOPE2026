On the natural odd 7-smooth semigroup, the second Schemmel-totient iteration height has an exact exponent formula and its height levels have sizes \(1,3,k(k+3)/2\). Each level \(k\ge2\) has the unique maximum \(15\cdot7^{k-2}\), proving the sharp restriction of Defant's conjectured bound on that entire invariant semigroup.
