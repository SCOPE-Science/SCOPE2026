# Review

## Correctness

The map-to-walk bijection and winding classification are exact for every cycle with at least three vertices. Noninvertible maps are exactly zero-sum nearest-neighbor bridges, and each bridge image size is its integer span plus one. The trace identity enumerates all translations of these bridges into finite paths; its second difference gives the exact rank histogram. The classical reflection argument yields the full first moment and the central-binomial asymptotic. `verify.py` checks histograms and counts for five even sizes but is not the general proof.

## Originality

Dimitrova–Fernandes–Koppitz–Quinteiro (arXiv:2310.04149, Proposition 1.9) give the aggregate number of cycle endomorphisms, together with Green relations, regularity and monoid generator rank. The new image-rank polynomial refines their total and addresses a distinct rank statistic. Michels–Knauer (2009) treat total and congruence-class counts; their full text could not be inspected, so an overlapping refinement remains a substantive residual risk. Existing idempotent-retraction counts do not cover the full endomorphism monoid.

## Value

The exact all-ranks census characterizes the transformation-size profile, and the closed mean formula describes typical image compression: image cardinality is asymptotic to the square root of cycle size, up to a constant. This is structurally more informative than aggregate cardinality and distinguishes generic maps from automorphisms.
