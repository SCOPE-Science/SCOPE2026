Binary one-jumble four-Simon equivalence has exactly \(66\) classes, classified by truncated symbol counts and the absence of six balanced four-letter patterns. Among length-\(n\) words, the class count is \(n+1\) through \(n=3\), then \(10,16,21,26,31\) at lengths \(4\) through \(8\), and exactly \(31\) thereafter.
