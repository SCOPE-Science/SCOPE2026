The symmetric mutual-zero-product matrix of all partial permutations on an \(n\)-element set has rank exactly \(\binom{2n}{n}\); its inertia has a parity-sensitive excess of \(\binom n{n/2}\) positive directions precisely when \(n\) is even. Its entire nonzero eigenvalue product is \((-1)^{(M_n-F_n)/2}\prod_{r=0}^n(r!)^{\binom nr^2}\), where \(M_n=\binom{2n}{n}\) and \(F_n=\binom n{n/2}\) for even \(n\) and zero otherwise.
