For the period-doubling word, the first repeated length-\(n\) factor begins exactly at \(3q/2\) or \(2q\), according as the leading binary digits of \(n\ge2\) are \(10\) or \(11\), where \(q\) is the largest power of two not exceeding \(n\). The unique earlier copy begins at \(q\) or \(0\), respectively, giving a complete formula for initial non-repetitive complexity.
