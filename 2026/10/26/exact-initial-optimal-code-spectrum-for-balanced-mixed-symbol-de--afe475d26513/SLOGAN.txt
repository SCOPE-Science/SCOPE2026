For balanced binary words transmitted through a channel deleting exactly one zero and one one, the largest zero-error codebooks at lengths \(2,4,6,8,10\) have sizes \(1,2,2,6,10\). At length \(10\), there are exactly \(666\) maximum codebooks, forming \(174\) orbits under reversal and bit complementation; these numbers are established by an exhaustive clique search with a mathematically valid coloring bound.
