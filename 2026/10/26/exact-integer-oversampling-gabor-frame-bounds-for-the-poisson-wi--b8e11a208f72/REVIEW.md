# Scientific review: sharp Poisson-window Gabor spectral bounds

## Correctness — PASS

The proof specifies \(L^2(\mathbb R)\) normalization, the unscaled rational window, and the time-frequency lattice before identifying the scalar multiplier. The Zak transform identity is a genuine unitary Parseval identity, not a heuristic approximation. Poisson summation gives a two-geometric-series expression; after squaring, the multiplier is strictly monotone in the time cosine. Its frequency extremizers follow from a strictly convex exponential sum. The positive minimum for all integer \(q\ge2\) is a sum of squares that cannot vanish simultaneously. Exact hyperbolic rewriting and power/exponential expansions establish both sharp asymptotic coefficients. Finitely many independently computed Zak sums and rational formulas were compared in `verify.py`; those checks are supplementary and do not replace the analytic argument. Risk: no bounds outside the stated lattice/density domain.

## Originality — PASS with residual priority risk

The claim is specific to the **even two-pole Poisson window** and to the exact optimal constants for **all integer oversampling factors**. Belov–Kulikov–Lyubarskii's rational-window paper (arXiv:2103.08959) proves a frame property, not the numerical spectral extrema. Semenov's eligible recent source (arXiv:2510.25930, v1 2025-10-29) constructs a qualitative universal frequency set; it does not calculate the Poisson constants. Faulhuber–Shafkulovska (arXiv:2204.02917) derive sharp constants for different Gaussian, hyperbolic-secant and exponential windows, so these formulas cannot imply the present constants without an unproved identification of windows. Belov–Kulikov–Lyubarskii (arXiv:2212.08915) give an explicit operator and estimates for a one-pole complex Cauchy window, which is not equivalent to a two-pole Poisson window under a scalar modulation or dilation. The 2026-10-21 Belov–Semenov paper (arXiv:2610.30037) treats the exact same Poisson window but proves a **qualitative semi-regular frame-set criterion**, with no sharp closed-form integer-oversampling bounds in the inspected full text. The historical ledger's critical-density causal completely-monotone result addresses \(q=1\) and a one-sided window; it neither implies the present \(q\ge2\) even-window constants nor their degeneration. Targeted semantic searches found related Gabor results but no implication covering the claim. Exact historical priority cannot be proved by search: Janssen's 1996 frame-bound paper was not available in full text, and unindexed computations might predate this derivation.

## Value — PASS

For this natural harmonic-extension/Poisson rational window, numerical frame stability is governed by the exact **optimal** constants, not merely qualitative existence. The theorem solves those constants simultaneously for infinitely many natural lattice densities, locates every extremal fiber, and reveals two different conditioning mechanisms as pole distance tends to zero or infinity. These are reproducible, directly usable reconstruction-conditioning invariants, not an arbitrary finite sample or a restated positivity criterion. The estimates are sharp at every parameter; the asymptotic constants are proved rather than fitted. The result does not claim a general solution of irregular-density Gabor optimization.
