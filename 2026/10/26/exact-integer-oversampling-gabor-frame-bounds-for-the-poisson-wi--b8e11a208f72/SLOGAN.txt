For the Poisson window \(g_w(t)=1/(t^2+w^2)\), both optimal Gabor frame bounds at every integer redundancy \(q\ge2\) admit explicit hyperbolic formulas and have unique extremal Zak fibers. The sharp condition number diverges like \(w^{-4}\) as \(w\downarrow0\), but like \(\tfrac12 e^{2\pi w/q}\) as \(w\to\infty\).
