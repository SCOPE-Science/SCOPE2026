For uniform lower bounds of \(r\) true discoveries on every \(k\)-subset, repeated interpolation certifies exactly \(r\lfloor |S|/k\rfloor+\max\{0,|S|\bmod k-(k-r)\}\), whereas perfect coherence certifies \(\max\{0,|S|-(k-r)\}\). At \(n=2k-r\) the full-set bounds are \(r\) versus \(k\), yielding an unbounded sharp multiplicative gap as \(k/r\) grows.
