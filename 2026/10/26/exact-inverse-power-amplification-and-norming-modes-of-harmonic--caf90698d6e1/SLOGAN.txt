For the harmonic Bergman Toeplitz operator \(T_m=P_hM_{z^m}\), every inverse-power norm has the exact value \(\|T_m^{-N}\|=\sqrt{(m\lfloor N/2\rfloor+1)(m\lceil N/2\rceil+1)}\), and only the one or two balanced nonnegative Fourier modes attain it. Although its spectrum is the unit circle and \(\|T_m^N\|=1\), inverse powers grow linearly with sharp slope \(m/2\).
