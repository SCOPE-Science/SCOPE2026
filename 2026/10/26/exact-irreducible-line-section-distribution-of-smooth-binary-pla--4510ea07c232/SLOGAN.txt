Across all geometrically smooth plane quartics over \(\mathbf F_2\), the numbers of rational lines whose four intersection points form one Frobenius orbit of length four have the exact equation-weighted distribution \((4666,3444,1722,504,420,84,56,24)\). In particular 4,666 smooth quartic equations have no such line, while 6,254 have at least one; the previously known extreme is not asserted to be new.
