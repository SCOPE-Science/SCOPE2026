For any family of \(k\) unit-coefficient diagonal binary quadratic forms over \(\mathbb Z_p\), the exact density of primitive inputs avoiding valuation one in every form depends only on quadratic-root slope classes modulo \(p\) and their collisions modulo \(p^2\). The smallest possible density is \(1-\frac{2}{p+1}(m-\frac{(2m-k)_+}{p})\), with \(m=\min(k,(p-1)/2)\), and all equality families are characterized.
