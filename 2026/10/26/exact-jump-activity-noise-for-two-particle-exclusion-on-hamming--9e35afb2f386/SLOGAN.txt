For two hard-core walkers on every \(q\)-ary Hamming graph with at least two coordinates, the full successful-hop count is strictly super-Poissonian, with an exact finite binomial-tail Fano formula. At fixed alphabet size, its excess noise obeys the sharp law \(F-1\sim 2/[d(q-1)q^d]\); a one-coordinate complete graph instead has exactly Poisson jump counts.
