Božović's conjecture on classical Sierpiński graphs holds in full: for all integers \(n\ge1\), \(m\ge2\), and \(1\le k\le m-1\), the exact \(k\)-limited domination number is \(\gamma_k^{\mathrm L}(S(n,m))=(m-k)m^{n-1}\). A grouped-clique lower bound and an inversion-symmetric residue construction give matching bounds; for \(n\ge2\), \(k\ge2\), every minimum set has exactly \(m-k\) vertices in each base clique.
