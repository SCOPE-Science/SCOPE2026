For threshold-two three-state excitation on every labelled \(K_{a,b}\), the configurations needing the full five-step transient are exactly a thin–fat pair of initial colour populations, counted by \(2(P_aQ_b+Q_aP_b)\), where \(P_n=\sum_{j=2}^{n-2}\binom nj2^{n-j}\) and \(Q_n=n\) for \(n\ge3\) and zero otherwise. Five updates are genuinely necessary exactly when the smaller part has at least three vertices and the larger at least four; on balanced \(K_{n,n}\), the maximal-delay probability is asymptotic to \(4n/3^n\).
