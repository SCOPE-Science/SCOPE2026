For a fixed one-variable, depth-one two-agent believed announcement on finite \(\mathsf{S5}\) frames with two-world information cells, the full iteration is exactly endpoint peeling on alternating colored paths, while colored cycles persist. Stabilization takes precisely the maximum path half-length \(\lceil L/2\rceil\), giving a sharp bound \(\lceil |W|/2\rceil\) and arbitrarily long sequences already with one atom and two agents.
