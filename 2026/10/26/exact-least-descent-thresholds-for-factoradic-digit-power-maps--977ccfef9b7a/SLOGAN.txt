For every integer exponent \(r\ge2\), the exact last failure of strict descent under the factoradic digit-power map is a fully specified factorial-block endpoint \(C_r-1\), with \(C_r=(a_r+1)j_r!\). This sharpens the known all-exponent sufficient bound \((j_r+1)!\) to a computable exact threshold and supplies a witness at the boundary.
