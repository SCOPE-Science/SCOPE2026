# Correctness, originality, and value assessment

**Correctness (PASS).** The age renewal equation follows directly from the clock specification: no-reset survival is \((1-\gamma)_n/n!\) and the renewal generating function is \((1-z)^{-\gamma}\). This yields the complete age law for every finite integer horizon. At the self-dual hazard \(\gamma=1/2\), factorization of its characteristic-function generating series reduces it exactly to the Legendre generating series. Direct spin-flip evaluation gives the concurrence as the Fourier-modulus. Classical orthogonal-polynomial facts prove the exhaustive simple-root count and the local cusp; dominated convergence of the beta-binomial mixture plus Hurwitz establishes the Bessel scaling, and the fixed-phase Hilb formula establishes the time envelope. Four-dimensional quantum-state, recursion, and root computations passed. The proof does not infer infinite claims from numerical checking.

**Originality (PASS, residual risk documented).** The full text of Wald et al. (arXiv:2410.11497, equations (1)–(5), section 4 and appendices) establishes the underlying discrete reset formalism and the inverse-power hazard family; it does not state the symmetric-hazard Legendre concurrence law, the exact \(t\)-node separability classification, or the Bessel node limit. Sevilla–Valdés-Hernández arXiv:2209.05643 develops continuous-time resetting, and Carollo–Lesanovsky–Garrahan Phys. Rev. E 109, 044129 treats generalized trajectory orderings; no corresponding discrete-time theorem was identified. Searches of relevant published mathematical records for aliases, special functions, renewal-age and entanglement regimes produced nearby but none that implied the claim. The Legendre, beta-binomial and Bessel facts individually are classical; no novelty is claimed for them. Possible alternate descriptions in unindexed works, including work on dephasing random-unitary ensembles, remain a residual risk.

**Mathematical value (PASS).** The symmetric critical reset clock is distinguished by the exchangeable beta-binomial age law with equal exponents and an arcsine scaling measure. In a model class where earlier analysis emphasized stationary or numerical correlations, the theorem completely identifies a physically controllable finite-horizon set of entanglement-death/revival nodes and a universal shrinking phase scale. Exact finite-time zeros with maximally entangled conditional trajectories separate uncertainty-induced loss of entanglement from trajectory-level entanglement degradation. The node count and Bessel law are neither a generic normalization check nor an arbitrary truncation.

**Limitations.** The finding covers the symmetric inverse-linear renewal hazard, Bell-state replacement and a fixed local phase gate only. It does not claim independent verification, numerical noise stability, or entanglement results for other reset channels. The comparisons do not establish absolute uniqueness in all literature.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
