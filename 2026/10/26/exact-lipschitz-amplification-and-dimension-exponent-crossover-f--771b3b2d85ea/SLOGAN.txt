For antipodal two-label \(p\)-Dirichlet interpolation on an unweighted Boolean cube, the exact Lipschitz amplification is \(d/\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}\binom{d-1}{j}^{-1/(p-1)}\), even though a norm-one linear extension exists. Its sharp dimension–exponent crossover occurs at \(p\) proportional to \(d\), with a nontrivial entropy-integral limit, divergence below that scale, and convergence to one above it.
