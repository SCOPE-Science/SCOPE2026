For continuous-time heat on a four-cycle with three unit jump rates and one variable rate \(w\), the exact graph-distance Lipschitz norm has a two-branch closed form and is contractive at all times exactly when \(w\le3\). Every finite parameter has amplification strictly below \(3/2\), yet the sharp \(3/2\) envelope emerges when the exceptional edge becomes infinitely fast on a vanishing time scale.
