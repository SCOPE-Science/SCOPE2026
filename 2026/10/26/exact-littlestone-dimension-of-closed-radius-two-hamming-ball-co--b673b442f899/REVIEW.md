# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** A self-contained proof bounds arbitrary depth using two explicit double-ball intersection lemmas and a translated-star lemma; every such star admits at most two shattered rounds. For dimensions at most five the argument improves the general five bound by exact branch sizes. Four complete explicit trees give matching depths, their 58 leaf paths independently checked; a separate minimax computation confirms all cases through dimension six. None of the finite calculations is substituted for the proof of the unbounded case.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risks.** Full-text inspection of Adler–Benediktsson–Macpherson's Hamming-neighbourhood VC-dimension framework, Malliaris–Moran's Littlestone/stability framework and Chase–Freitag–Reyzin's learning framework did not find an exact claim for this closed-radius-two indicator family. The similarly named d-Hamming-ball concept class defined in Attias–Hanneke–Ramaswami treats functions close to a fixed labeling, not metric-ball indicators, so its online bound does not imply this theorem. A related closed-ball VC-dimension formula and an exact-radius-two sphere Littlestone formula concern inequivalent properties/classes and do not settle this theorem. The literature search is not a proof of worldwide priority, and possible equivalent formulations in metric combinatorics remain a risk.

**Value — PASS.** The radius-two power of the cube is a canonical metric neighbourhood system. Its hypothesis count grows exponentially with dimension, yet the optimal worst-case online mistake bound stops at five, with a provably sharp first saturation dimension. The proof explains the uniform bound through structural double-ball intersections rather than brute-force enumeration and supplies exact sharp boundary witnesses. This is a natural finite transition and infinite family result.

**Remaining limitations.** No extension is claimed for other radii or other alphabets. Complete independent audit and publication-priority confirmation are not available.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
