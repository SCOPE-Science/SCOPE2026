For all binary Hamming cubes, the Littlestone dimension of the closed radius-two ball-indicator class is exactly \(0\) in dimensions one and two, \(1\) in dimension three, \(3\) in dimension four, \(4\) in dimension five, and \(5\) thereafter. A sharp five-mistake bound holds independently of ambient dimension, with the first depth-five shattered tree at dimension six.
