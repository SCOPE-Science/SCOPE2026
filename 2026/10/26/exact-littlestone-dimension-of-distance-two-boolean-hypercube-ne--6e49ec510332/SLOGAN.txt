Exact distance-two Hamming-sphere membership on the binary cube has Littlestone dimension \(0,1,2,2,4,4,5\) in dimensions \(1,2,3,4,5,6,\ge7\), respectively. A dimension-independent five-mistake upper bound follows from a two-star reduction, while explicit complete mistake trees prove the sharp transitions and a strict adaptive-versus-VC gap in dimension five.
