In a homogeneous damped-and-dephased qubit array, the least generic locally identifying prefix of sorted real Liouvillian eigenvalues is exactly \(2n+1\) below the ratio \(\gamma_\phi/\gamma_1=1/4\), and above that threshold follows an exact cumulative-binomial staircase. On a fixed bounded rate rectangle the maximal required prefix becomes polynomial past \(n=20\), while a moving-rate window around \(\gamma_\phi/\gamma_1=n/4\) has a universal Gaussian onset profile.
