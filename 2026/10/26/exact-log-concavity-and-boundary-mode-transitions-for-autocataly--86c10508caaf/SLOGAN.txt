For the quadratic-rate two-species autocatalytic birth–death chain, stationary count log-concavity at each finite population size reduces to two boundary inequalities, and log-concavity for every size holds exactly when both mutation-to-autocatalysis ratios are at least \(1/3\). In the symmetric model, the boundary-peak and log-concavity transitions are strictly separated, leaving a sharp regime of central unimodality without log-concavity.
