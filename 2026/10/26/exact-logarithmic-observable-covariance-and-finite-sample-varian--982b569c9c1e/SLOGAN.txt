For the doubling map observed through \(-\log x\), each lag covariance is positive and has the sharp law \(C_k=(k\log2/4+c)2^{-k}+O(4^{-k})\). The finite-sample variance has an exact nonzero intercept and an explicit \(N2^{-N}\) correction with an all-time \(8\cdot4^{-N}/9\) remainder bound, refining the previously numerical asymptotic variance.
