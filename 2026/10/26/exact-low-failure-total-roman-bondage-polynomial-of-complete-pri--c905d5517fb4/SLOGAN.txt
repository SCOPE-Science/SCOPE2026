For the matched two-clique prism \(K_n\square K_2\), all total-Roman-domination failures caused by fewer than \(n\) deleted edges are counted exactly by the low-degree coefficients of \(\sum_{j=0}^n(-1)^j\binom nj(1+t)^{(n-j)^2}\). The sharp minimum number of failures is \(\lceil n/2\rceil\), and the number of minimum deletion sets has explicit and different even- and odd-order factorial formulas.
