For synchronous OR dynamics on the distance-two graph of the \(n\)-cube, \(n\ge6\), the sharp relaxation time is \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\). The number of maximally slow initial states is exactly \(2A_n2^{2^{n-1}}-A_n^2\), with an explicit parity-sensitive formula for \(A_n\) arising from antipodal singletons and Hamming-neighborhood subsets.
