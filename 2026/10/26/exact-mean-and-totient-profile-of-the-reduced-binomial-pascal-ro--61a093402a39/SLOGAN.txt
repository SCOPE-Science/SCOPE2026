The normalized loss from replacing every \(\binom nk\) by its gcd-reduced binomial coefficient equals \(1-\varphi(n)/n+o(1)\), uniformly over integer indices \(n\to\infty\). Its exact Cesàro mean is \(1-6/\pi^2=0.392072898145973\ldots\), resolving the mean-ratio conjecture associated with OEIS A271834.
