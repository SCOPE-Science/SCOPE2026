The total number of shortest absent scattered-subsequence strings across all binary source words, jointly counted by source length and subsequence-universality index, has the exact generating function \(2/(1-z-uz^2(3-z))\). For uniformly random length-\(n\) binary source words, the mean is asymptotic to \(-2/(rQ\prime(r))\,(1/(2r))^n\), where \(Q(z)=1-z-3z^2+z^3\) and \(r\) is its smallest positive root; its exponential base is approximately \(1.08504324\).
