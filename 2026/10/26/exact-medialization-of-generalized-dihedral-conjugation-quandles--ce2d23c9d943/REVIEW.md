# Review: Exact medialization of generalized-dihedral conjugation quandles

## Correctness — PASS

The group law and four conjugation identities are obtained directly for an arbitrary abelian group, without assumptions on order or characteristic. A single substitution in the medial identity forces all reflection identifications modulo \(4A\). The proposed relation is a congruence: operations and right division respect it. Its quotient has commuting right translations, which is sufficient for mediality by the quandle conjugacy identity for right translations. These two inclusions prove exact minimality and prevent any collapse of rotations. For finite groups, the direct-product order formula follows from the elementary fourth-multiples quotient. Twenty-four exact finite congruence closures, including noncyclic examples, support but are not substitutes for the general proof.

## Originality — PASS (self-review)

The motivating paper [1] gives general lower bounds and the isolated \(S_3\) example for \(\operatorname{Conj}(G)^{\mathrm{med}}\); neither determines the congruence for generalized dihedral groups. The full text of [2] addresses rotation/reflection quandle-coloring signature modules, not universal medial quotients. The older structure theorem [3] classifies medial quandles via affine data but does not directly state a maximal medial quotient of \(\operatorname{Conj}(\operatorname{Dih}(A))\). The new exact \(4A\)-congruence and its universal minimality are not implied simply by the cited lower bounds or coloring equations. Searches for generalized-dihedral medialization, conjugation racks, universal entropic quotients, and the \(4A\) reflection-fiber law found no directly covering statement. Search absence is not proof of priority. Particular small groups are already covered by [1] or finite-quandle classifications, and equivalent older work may remain unindexed.

## Value — PASS

The exact universal quotient answers a natural sharpened version of the medialization problem in [1] for all inversion semidirect products. It explains a uniform fourfold obstruction in the reflection fibers while proving complete survival of the rotation set, beyond group-quotient lower bounds. In finite groups it yields exact cardinalities for all abelian kernels, including all products of cyclic factors. This is a structural classification rather than an isolated calculation or a restatement of general mediality criteria.

## Literature and limitations

Closest primary sources: [1] Bardakov–Sangare, arXiv:2610.18676, Section 5, Theorem 5.4; [2] Fan, arXiv:2609.13326, Sections 3 and 10; [3] Jedlička et al., arXiv:1409.8396. Risk of alternate equivalent derivations and unindexed special-family results remains. The theorem does not address non-inversion semidirect actions, and finite checks do not certify the general case.
