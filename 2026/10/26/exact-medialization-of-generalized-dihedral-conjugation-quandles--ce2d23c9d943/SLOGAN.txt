For every abelian group \(A\), the universal medial quotient of the conjugation quandle of \(A\rtimes C_2\) (inversion action) keeps every rotation distinct and identifies reflections exactly modulo \(4A\). For finite \(A\) its size is \(|A|+|A/4A|\), giving \(n+\gcd(n,4)\) for an ordinary dihedral group of order \(2n\).
