At the commensurate grids \(N=k^2\), a positivity-preserving periodic four-branch elliptic stencil has exactly \(k^2\) (odd \(k\)) or \(2k^2\) (even \(k\)) nondamped Fourier modes. A resolved mesh-dependent cosine forcing then gives a nonvanishing exact-solve error approaching \(1/r\), even though fixed-smooth-data convergence and positivity remain intact.
