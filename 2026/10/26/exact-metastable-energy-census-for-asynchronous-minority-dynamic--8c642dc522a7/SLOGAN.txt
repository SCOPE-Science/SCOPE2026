On circular prism graphs, asynchronous minority dynamics freezes into singleton attractors, whose full energy distribution is a parity-restricted independent-set polynomial with exactly two additional coherent-rung states at even sizes. The number of attractors is \(L_n+2\cos(2\pi n/3)+2\mathbf 1_{2\mid n}\), and every initial state reaches one in expected at most \(6n^2\) updates.
