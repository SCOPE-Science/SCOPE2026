For \(N=2^ap^b\) with \(p>2^a+1\), the distinct degrees of monic integral divisors of \(X^N-1\) form a uniquely digit-coded sparse set with an exact multiscale gap census. As \(b\) grows, their normalized uniform distribution converges to a singular Cantor measure of dimension \(\log(2^a+1)/\log p\).
