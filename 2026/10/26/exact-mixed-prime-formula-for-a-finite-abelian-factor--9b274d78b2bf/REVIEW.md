# Review

## Correctness
The proof gives a kernel identity for each irreducible tensor factor, a lower bound from the minimum numbers of generators of \(Z(P)\) and \(A\), and an explicit construction attaining the bound in both regimes \(d(A)\le d(Z(P))\) and \(d(A)>d(Z(P))\). The statement is restricted to finite groups and complex representations with coprime primes.

## Originality
The closest recent source proves same-prime additivity and gives examples of mixed-prime nonadditivity. The older center-rank and abelian-group results are inputs, but the inspected statements do not give the full formula for arbitrary \(P\) and a coprime abelian factor. A broader implicit result may exist outside the searched sources.

## Value
The result gives a closed exact correction to additivity across a natural family of nilpotent direct products and explains the savings by the ranks of the two central/abelian factors. It is stronger than a numerical example and is independent of a fixed group order or nilpotency class.
