For a nontrivial finite \(p\)-group \(P\) and a finite abelian \(q\)-group \(A\) with \(p\ne q\), the minimum faithful complex representation degree is \(\delta(P\times A)=\delta(P)+\max\{0,d(A)-d(Z(P))\}\). Thus the dimension saving relative to separate representations is exactly \(\min\{d(A),d(Z(P))\}\).
