For the genuinely nonlinear shifted one-transposition Yang–Baxter cellular automata on \(\mathbb F_{2^n}\), \(n\ge3\), the entire helical site-and-carrier cycle distribution is given exactly by elementary formulas, with no periods beyond four. When the site count is even and the shift lies outside the swapped-pair difference subgroup, primitive two- and four-cycles coexist, and exactly \(1-(1-4/2^n)^m\) of full states lie on four-cycles.
