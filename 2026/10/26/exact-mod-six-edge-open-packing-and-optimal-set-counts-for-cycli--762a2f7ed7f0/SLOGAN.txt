For the cyclic prism \(C_n\square K_2\), \(n\ge7\), the largest edge open packing contains \(n\) edges exactly when \(6\mid n\), and otherwise contains \(n-1\). The number of optimum edge subsets is exactly \(6\), \(4n\), or \(2n\), according to the specified residue class modulo six; an exact 17-state deficiency transfer proves the periodic classification.
