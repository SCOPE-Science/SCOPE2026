On the labelled \(n\)-fold power of the three-element Kleene algebra, every modal-Kleene box has a unique two-threshold coordinate form: there are exactly \((3^n+2^n)^n\) boxes, of which exactly \(3^{n^2}\) preserve the top element. The non-normal fraction decays asymptotically like \(n(2/3)^n\), so normality becomes overwhelmingly typical in high-dimensional Kleene cubes.
