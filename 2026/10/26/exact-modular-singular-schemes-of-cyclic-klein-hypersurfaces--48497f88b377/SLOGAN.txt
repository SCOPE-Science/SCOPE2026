For cyclic Klein hypersurfaces \(\sum X_i^{d-1}X_{i+1}=0\) in characteristic \(p\mid d\), smoothness is equivalent to \(p\nmid m\), where \(m\) is the number of coordinates. When \(p\mid m\), the entire singular scheme is a one-variable roots-of-unity thickening; its exact local length has an additional valuation jump in characteristic two when \(d\equiv2\pmod4\).
