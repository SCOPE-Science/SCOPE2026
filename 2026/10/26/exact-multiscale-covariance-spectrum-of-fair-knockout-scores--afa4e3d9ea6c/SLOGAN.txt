In a fair fixed knockout bracket with \(n=2^\ell\) entrants, the complete covariance spectrum of players' win counts is \(\lambda_d=6/2^d-[2(\ell-d)+5]/n\), with multiplicity \(n/2^d\) for each bracket level \(d\), plus one zero eigenvalue. The exact condition number is \(3n-2\ell-3\), and the geometric-mean generalized variance of the fixed-draw covariance is asymptotically three quarters of that under a uniformly randomized draw.
