For deterministic coordinatewise RMSProp on a strictly convex diagonal quadratic, crossing \(k\) curvature thresholds produces exactly \((3^k-1)/2\) prime two-cycles, of which \(2^{k-1}\) are locally attracting off threshold equalities. Their sharp two-step spectral-rate floor is the memory coefficient \(\beta\); when \(\beta<1/9\), attaining it simultaneously in all coordinates is possible exactly below an explicit Hessian condition-number cutoff.
