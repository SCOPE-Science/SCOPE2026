For deterministic coordinatewise RMSProp on a strictly convex diagonal quadratic, exactly 2^(k−1) prime two-cycles attract when k>0 curvature thresholds have been crossed and none is at equality. The all-active two-step spectral radius reaches its memory bound β exactly on an explicit stepsize band, which for β<1/9 is nonempty if and only if the curvature ratio is at or below a sharp cutoff. The underlying total orbit count specializes the prior reduced period-two equation of Loh et al.
