For every fixed radial order \(\alpha>-\tfrac12\) and eigenvalue index \(n\), the circular-prolate concentration eigenvalue decays as \(\lambda_{\alpha,n}(c)\sim L_{\alpha,n}^2c^{4n+2\alpha+2}\) as bandwidth \(c\downarrow0\), with a completely explicit Jacobi–Gamma constant \(L_{\alpha,n}\). Thus every successive fixed radial mode incurs an asymptotic factor of \(c^4\), with an exact coefficient.
