For every finite characteristic-two extension field \(\mathbb F_{2^e}\) with \(e\ge2\), all graph types realized by natural bases of every nondegenerate rank-one evolution algebra, and the exact numbers of bases modulo rescaling and reordering realizing them, follow from a four-case orthogonal-orbit and Hamming-weight formula. Depending on the product generator, precisely one, \(d\), or \(d-1\) graph types occur in dimension \(d\).
