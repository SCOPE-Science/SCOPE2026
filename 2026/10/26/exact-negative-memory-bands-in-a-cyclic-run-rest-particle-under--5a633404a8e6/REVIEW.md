# Scientific review: negative memory in a cyclic run–rest trap

**Correctness — PASS.** The Fourier spectrum of the directed Markov cycle gives a damped-cosine motor autocovariance. The harmonic Green filter yields the stated exact trapped covariance. A strict nested Laplace-kernel identity establishes uniqueness of the onset; each negative cosine sector has one trough and the exact count follows from a strict exponential inequality. Independently reconstructed joint-moment evolution passed 780 comparisons with maximum absolute discrepancy below \(1.34\times10^{-15}\). Finite checks are corroboration, not proof.

**Originality — PASS, with residual publication-priority risk.** Sun–Ye–Podgornik (arXiv:2409.00578) independently resample the run direction after resting. Datta–Beta–Großmann (arXiv:2406.15277) analyze intermittent unconfined motility and its correlations. Basu and collaborators (arXiv:1910.10083) and Smith and collaborators (arXiv:2207.10445) have other trapping transition graphs and mainly spatial laws. Their results supply context and standard techniques, but no complete directed-cycle covariance-sign classification, onset uniqueness or lobe divergence was identified. A wider sign-variation theorem might imply part of this result; priority is not certified.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Exact lag-by-lag signs and the finite-to-infinite lobe transition are motivated by the observable question of whether a non-inertial trapped swimmer develops alternating temporal memory. The unique critical threshold, exact number of negative bands and their blowup are a natural complete classification, not selected numerical examples.

**Limits.** Equal transition rates, stationary preparation, no translational thermal noise, harmonic confinement and deterministic sign reversal are essential to the displayed form. No claims are made for unequal rates, free randomization, higher dimensions or nonlinear confinement.
