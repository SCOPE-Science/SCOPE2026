A passive harmonic restoring force can transform a directed run–rest particle's stationary motility into an overdamped position autocorrelation with a precisely countable set of negative-lag bands. A unique confinement-to-switching ratio \(h_*=0.8914223658803029\ldots\) first permits negative correlation; the number of negative bands diverges as \(\log5/[4\pi(1-h)]\) when \(h\uparrow1\), after which infinitely many alternating sign intervals persist.
