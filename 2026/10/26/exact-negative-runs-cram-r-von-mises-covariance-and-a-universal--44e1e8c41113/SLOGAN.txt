For the classical continuous two-sample null, the Wald–Wolfowitz run count and the Cramér–von Mises empirical-squared-bridge statistic have exact covariance \(\operatorname{Cov}(R,T)=(N-2ab)/(3N^2)\), strictly negative for every pooled size \(N\ge3\). At every fixed limiting nonzero sample allocation, their correlation obeys the universal law \(\sqrt N\operatorname{Corr}(R,T)\to-\sqrt5\).
