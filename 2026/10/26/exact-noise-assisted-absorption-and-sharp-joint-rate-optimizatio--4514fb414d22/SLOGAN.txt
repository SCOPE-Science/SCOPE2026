For a localized excitation on an \(m\)-leaf quantum star, independent leaf dephasing unlocks a dark sector and gives an exact mean hub-absorption time \(\tau=(m+1)/\kappa+(m-1)/\gamma_L+(\kappa+\gamma_L+\gamma_H)/(4J^2)\). Jointly optimal rates \(\kappa=2J\sqrt{m+1}\), \(\gamma_L=2J\sqrt{m-1}\), and zero hub dephasing yield the sharp \(2\sqrt m/J\) asymptotic, while exactly zero leaf dephasing traps a fraction \(1-1/m\) forever.
