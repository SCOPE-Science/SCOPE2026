For the spider with \(k\geq3\) identical three-edge arms, the fastest symmetric nonnegative walk with uniform stationary law has edge probabilities \(1/k\), \((2-\gamma_k)/3\), and \((1+\gamma_k)/3\), where \(\gamma_k=2/(3k+5+\sqrt{(3k+5)^2-24k})\). Its exact minimum second-largest eigenvalue modulus is \(1-\gamma_k\), and these probabilities are unique among arm-permutation-invariant transition matrices.
