For the ternary additive basis whose summands use either only even or only odd digit positions, exactly \(8\cdot5^{k-1}-2^{2k-1}-5k\) integers below \(9^k\) have more than one unordered two-summand representation. This gives the sharp exceptional exponent \(\log_9 5\) in a natural periodic instance where density-one uniqueness was previously known.
