For every integer base \(g\ge4\), the number of integers below \(g^{2k}\) with multiple unordered two-summand representations by the even–odd digit-partition basis is exactly \((3g-1)(2g-1)^{k-1}-2^{2k-1}-5k-1\). This sharpens qualitative density-one uniqueness to a full finite-cutoff count and the exponent \(\log_{g^2}(2g-1)\).
