# Scientific review

## Correctness (PASS)
The unital algebra generated by two integral quaternions is the lattice spanned by \(1,x,y,xy\), by reduced quadratic identities and the integral reverse-order multiplication identity. Computing its covolume relative to the explicit Hurwitz lattice gives the exact criterion \(\|U\times V\|^2=8\) for doubled imaginary coordinates. The norm-\(3\) equation has exactly \(96\) parity-compatible solutions. The finite squared-length obstruction confines generating pairs to the three unordered imaginary-length patterns \((3,3),(3,8),(3,11)\); a signed-coordinate count gives \(36,8,4,0\) valid partners per trace stratum, totaling \(960\). Full-generation implies central stabilizer \(\{\pm1\}\), yielding orbit size \(12\) and \(80\) orbits. The actual standalone checker independently verifies all \(9216\) lattice determinants. No infinite claim is inferred from finite enumeration.

## Originality (PASS, with residual risk)
The October 2026 quaternion-generation paper gives global at-most-three existence and prime-power generation bounds, and its Remark 8.3 supplies the general two-generator commutator-norm identity; it does not provide the norm-\(3\) trace-stratified pair incidence or the \(80\)-orbit census. The earlier Goren–Love work determines orders from norm elements/theta data and separately notes the Hurwitz integer order; it likewise does not count ordered pairs of first-level generators. Direct comparison of the actual statements, sections, and relevant primary full texts distinguishes the new finite classification from those stronger but nonenumerative prior theorems. Searches for the exact counts, commutator formulation, and order aliases found no decisive covering source. Residual risk: unindexed examples or external computational tables could already record this case.

## Mathematical value (PASS)
The first allowable positive \(3\)-power norm is a natural sharp end of the 2026 existence problem, not an arbitrarily chosen truncation. The result quantitatively identifies the abundant two-generator pairs in a maximal order where the general literature guarantees at most three, and proves the structural and necessary trace-\(3\) obstruction, exact partner multiplicities, and symmetry-orbit census. The exact first-level incidence is not entailed by the general theorems or the prior theta count; its evidence is a complete elementary classification and a reproducible checker, not a mere isolated numerical coincidence.

## Scope and limitations
Only the classical rational Hurwitz integer order and norm \(3\) are addressed. Neither all prime powers nor larger discriminants are classified. The unit-conjugation orbits are not asserted to be full automorphism-group orbits. Unindexed previous tables remain a possible coverage risk.
