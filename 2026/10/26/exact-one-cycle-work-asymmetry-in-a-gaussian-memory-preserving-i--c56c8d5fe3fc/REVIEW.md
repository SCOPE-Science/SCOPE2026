# Scientific review — Exact one-cycle work asymmetry in a Gaussian memory-preserving information engine

## Correctness — PASS

The coupled OU and feedback system is a stable linear Gaussian affine recursion for every \(0<g<2\) and positive cycle time. Its exact stationary covariance follows by solving the source's pre-/postfeedback Lyapunov balances. The work quadratic form has trace \(m\) and determinant \(-d/4<0\); diagonalization gives the complete moment-generating function, Bessel-\(K_0\) density, exponential signed-work ratio, success probability and variance. The analytic proof specifies the time-scale coincidence limit and the singular behavior at zero work. The accompanying numerical program independently solves the periodic Lyapunov system and passed 114 checks.

## Originality — PASS, with documented risk

The exact stochastic model, feedback update, mean work and covariance propagation are from Bahng–Lee–Ghim (arXiv:2508.16866); none is claimed as new. The 2020 Paneru et al. passive thermal full-shift engine (arXiv:1911.09833) already exhibits the noisy work distribution and its symmetry crossover, and its published variance agrees with the corresponding special case. The actual new object-specific outcome is the explicit finite-period Gaussian active-bath law for arbitrary proportional gain with its distributional work asymmetry and positive-extraction probability. General Gaussian quadratic-form/variance-gamma mathematics is classical. The accessible source text and targeted database searches did not identify full coverage, but prior passive fluctuation literature, uninspected related complete texts, and possible implicit generalizations remain real residual risks.

## Value — PASS

This is a motivated fluctuation-level extension of an engine for which the source expressly identifies higher-work-moment and fluctuation-theorem questions as open directions. Mean work does not determine the risk of work injection or its work-tail asymmetry. The derived closed formulas provide these experimentally testable observables and show precisely when the full one-cycle work law, not just its mean, becomes symmetric. The distinction between per-cycle work asymmetry and true thermodynamic entropy fluctuation relations is explicit.

## Scope and residual risks

The result requires positive independent Gaussian measurement error, Gaussian OU forcing, harmonic confinement, instantaneous proportional feedback, and the periodic steady state. It does not apply as a multi-cycle current fluctuation theorem; successive cycle work values are correlated. A relevant thermal feedback uncertainty-bound paper (arXiv:1911.09835) was compared at abstract level only. An independent originality review might find the engine specialization implicit in broader published Gaussian work-statistics machinery.
