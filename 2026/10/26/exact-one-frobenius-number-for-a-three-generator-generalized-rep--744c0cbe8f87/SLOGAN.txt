For every integer \(b\ge2\), the one-Frobenius number of \(\langle b^2+b+1,(b+1)^2,2b^2+2b+1\rangle\)—the largest integer with at most one representation by its three generators—is exactly \(4b^3+2b^2-2\). Its sharp second-representation threshold has a unique maximizing residue modulo \(b^2+b+1\), namely \(b^2-b+1\).
