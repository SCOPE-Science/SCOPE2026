For an individually named \(a\times b\) product-of-chains grid in the homogeneous random poset, the exact number of complete one-types is \((a+b+3)\binom{a+b}{a}-\binom{a+b+1}{a}-\binom{a+b+1}{b}\). The resulting spectrum has polynomial growth at fixed positive width, but grows asymptotically like \(2\sqrt{n}\,4^n/\sqrt\pi\) on square grids.
