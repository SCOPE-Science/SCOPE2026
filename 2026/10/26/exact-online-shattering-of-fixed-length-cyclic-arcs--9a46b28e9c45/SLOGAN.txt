For the \(n\) translates of a cyclic arc of length \(k\), the exact Littlestone dimension is \(1+\lfloor\log_2\min(k,n-k)\rfloor\), while the VC dimension is only one or two. Hence cyclic sliding-window concept classes attain information-theoretically maximal online depth at nearly semicircular lengths and have unbounded online complexity despite uniformly bounded VC dimension.
