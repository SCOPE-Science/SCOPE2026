The open-neighborhood VC dimension of the \(r\)-th power of a finite cycle is exactly one in the complete and near-complete regimes, two at the next boundary, and three for every \(r\ge3\) once \(n\ge2r+4\); the only radius-two exception is \((n,r)=(8,2)\). A sixteen-transition counting argument proves the universal three-point ceiling, and explicit cyclic witnesses attain it.
