Every minimum double vertex-edge dominating set of a labeled cycle has gaps all equal to \(2\), or one cyclic alternating \(1,3\) run with the remaining gaps \(2\). Their number is exactly \(6\) when \(4\mid n\), \(2\) when \(n\equiv2\pmod4\), and \(n\lfloor(n+3)/4\rfloor\) for odd \(n\), with the rotation orbits completely classified.
