On a wheel with any rim length \(n\ge4\), synchronous binary neighbor-majority dynamics with tie retention has only the two unanimous fixed points, yet its genuine two-cycle orbits admit an exact binomial-run census and include at least \(2^{n/12}\) hub-flipping orbits for every \(n\ge96\). The count distinguishes hub-flipping oscillators from the two additional fixed-hub oscillators on even rims; it is an attractor count, not a basin probability.
