For every complete multipartite graph with at least three parts, the nontrivial \(p\)-strong Roman domination number has an exact quadratic-time formula using only one-defender and two-defender strategies. A third part can reduce the two-defender optimum by exactly one when two neighborhood sizes are both congruent to \(1\) modulo \(p\); this correction makes \(\gamma^4_{\mathrm{StR}}(K_{5,5,5})=7\), rather than \(8\).
