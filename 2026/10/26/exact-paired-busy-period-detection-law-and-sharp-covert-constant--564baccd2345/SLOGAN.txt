In a stable \(M/M/1\) queue, revealing the job count and duration of each busy period is statistically equivalent, for arrival-rate inference, to disclosing its entire unlabelled within-busy trajectory. This paired observation yields an exact finite-sample optimal-testing series and a sharp covert insertion constant \(K=\mu\sqrt{2/[\lambda(\mu-\lambda)]}\), with strictly better detection than either marginal statistic alone.
