For the regular paperfolding sequence's one-positions \(A\) and zero-positions \(B\), every nonnegative integer lies in \(A+A\), while \(A+B\) misses exactly \(\{0,1,4\}\). The complement of \(B+B\) is exactly \(\{1,3,5,9\}\cup\{2^k-2:k\ge1\}\): a fully classified infinite family with only logarithmically many exceptions up to \(X\).
