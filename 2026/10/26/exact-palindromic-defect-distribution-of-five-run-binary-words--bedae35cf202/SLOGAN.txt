For the five-run binary word \(0^a1^b0^c1^d0^e\), the exact palindromic defect is \(\mathbf1_{b\ne d}\max(0,\min(a,e)-c)\). Its complete length-and-defect distribution has a rational bivariate generating function; among uniformly selected five-run words, asymptotically one third have positive defect and the mean defect is \(n/30+O(1)\).
