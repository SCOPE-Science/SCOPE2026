At band centre, two absorbing lattice sites show an exact separation-parity switch: even separations erase all dependence on how a fixed loss budget is divided, whereas odd separations have a unique strictly interior optimal split. As the budget increases, this optimum captures almost all incoming flux with a deficit \(1/(4b^2)+O(b^{-3})\), while concentrating the same budget at one site captures asymptotically none.
