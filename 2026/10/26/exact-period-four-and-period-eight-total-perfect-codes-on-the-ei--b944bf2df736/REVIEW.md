# Self-review of exact eight-row torus total perfect codes

**Correctness: PASS.** The exact-one-neighbor condition is equivalent coordinate-by-coordinate to a unique Boolean successor for every pair of columns. Every code and only a code is a periodic state orbit whose period divides the torus length. Complete enumeration over all \(65{,}536\) states gives \(4\) four-cycles and \(16\) eight-cycles with no others; the program's deterministic-cycle traversal is complete. An unrelated exact-cover formulation reproduces individual counts, including \(144\) on \(C_8\square C_8\). The proof relies on complete finite enumeration plus a general cyclic-closure argument, not on finite-length extrapolation.

**Originality: PASS, with residual risk.** Dejter's 2007/2008 work treats existence of parallel total perfect codes on tori, not a classification of all codes. Kuziak–Peterin–Yero (2014, Section 5.1) state the full existence conjecture and explicitly treat the first-factor cases only up to seven; Kraner Šumenjak–Peterin–Rall–Tepeh (2015/2016) restate it. Searches of mathematical-result records and published literature for exact counts, variants and broader torus claims did not identify prior coverage of this full fixed-width result. The entire 2007/2008 full text was not available after lawful access attempts; a later/unindexed equivalent remains possible.

**Value: PASS.** This resolves the first next-width instance of an explicit published torus conjecture, rather than an arbitrary fixed example, and determines the entire labeled counting function and the strong uniform period bound. The exact classification is meaningful even if the general conjecture remains open.

The primary literature gives parallel-code constructions and settled narrower widths, not the converse for \(C_8\square C_t\). The present result does not establish uniqueness, solve all torus sizes, or claim publication priority certification.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
