The eight-row toroidal grid \(C_8\square C_t\) has a total perfect code exactly when \(4\mid t\), settling the first circumference beyond the cases resolved in the 2014 toroidal domination conjecture. Every code has least column period \(4\) or \(8\), and the exact labeled count is \(16\) when \(t\equiv4\pmod8\) and \(144\) when \(8\mid t\).
