Three-color cyclic competition on every complete bipartite graph has only fixed points, primitive three-cycles, and primitive four-cycles, with exact labeled counts \(3\), \(U_3(a)U_3(b)\), and \(6U_2(a)U_2(b)\). Every initial state becomes periodic in at most two updates, and the entire first-entry-depth distribution is explicit; in particular, primitive four-cycles appear as soon as both vertex parts contain two vertices.
