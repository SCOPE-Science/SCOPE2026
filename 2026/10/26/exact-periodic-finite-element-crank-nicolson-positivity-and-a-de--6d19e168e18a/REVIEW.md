# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The asserted equivalences for every integer \(n\ge3\) and every real \(r\ge0\) follow from explicit element assembly, an exact periodized cyclic inverse, a negative distance-two entry for \(0<r<1/3\) when \(n\ge4\), and an inverse-positive Stieltjes regime above \(1/3\). The Fourier diagonal is strictly decreasing in \(r\) and has a unique zero. A positive finite-size correction to the infinite-lattice Green kernel proves the strict decrease of these zeros with \(n\) and yields the exact limiting radical. The three-node case is computed separately. There is no unproved finite-to-infinite extrapolation; the 121 exact-rational tests are supplementary.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
Faragó–Horváth's Theorems 6.4–6.5 study uniform one-dimensional finite elements with Dirichlet-type boundaries. Li–Huang's Theorem 3.1 gives sufficient bounds, not the sharp periodic cutoff. Higueras–Roldán analyze finite differences with Dirichlet data, not consistent-mass periodic elements. Full text of two related indexed research reports was compared: filtered nearest-neighbor Crank–Nicolson changes the update, and Markov-generator resolvent positivity does not apply to this non-Metzler semidiscrete finite-element generator. None of those inspected claims dominates the asserted unfiltered periodic classification. Specialized older cyclic tridiagonal positivity studies remain a literature risk, not proof of novelty.

## Value — PASS
Periodic diffusion is a natural spatial discretization without boundary effects. The result supplies exact necessary-and-sufficient time-step restrictions for every mesh size, including the exceptional triangle, identifies the negative-entry mechanism at both ends, and establishes an explicit monotone finite-size effect. The sharp limiting upper endpoint \((5+\sqrt{33})/12\) is appreciably larger than the standard sufficient \(2/3\), providing a concrete positivity design margin. This is not a parameter renaming of a Dirichlet theorem.

## Limits
No classification beyond the stated uniform periodic one-dimensional model is claimed. Exact step thresholds do not imply convergence-error improvements or floating-point nonnegativity. Even after targeted full-text comparison, completely unindexed equivalent earlier work cannot be excluded.
