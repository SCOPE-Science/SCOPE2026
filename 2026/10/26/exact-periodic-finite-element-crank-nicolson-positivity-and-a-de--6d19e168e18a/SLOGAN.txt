On a uniform periodic \(P_1\) finite-element mesh, consistent-mass Crank–Nicolson heat steps are nonnegative exactly at zero step or within a sharp interval beginning at \(\tau/h^2=1/3\) (with a three-node exception). Its upper endpoint decreases strictly with mesh size toward the exact radical \((5+\sqrt{33})/12\), exceeding the familiar sufficient bound \(2/3\).
