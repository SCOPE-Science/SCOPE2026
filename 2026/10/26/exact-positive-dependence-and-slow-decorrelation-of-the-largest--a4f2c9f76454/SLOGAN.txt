The largest gap and Greenwood's squared-spacing sum have strictly positive covariance for every finite uniform sample, with the exact harmonic-number formula \(\operatorname{Cov}(M_n,Q_n)=[H_n^2+H_n^{(2)}-2H_n]/[n(n+1)(n+2)]\). Their Pearson correlation nevertheless tends to zero at the sharp slow rate \((\sqrt6/(2\pi))(\log n)^2/\sqrt n\), calibrating how two classical spacing diagnostics share information under the uniform null.
