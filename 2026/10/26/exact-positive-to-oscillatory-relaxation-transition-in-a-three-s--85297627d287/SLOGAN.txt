A primitive three-state Gibbs shift whose zero-energy configurations consist of a fixed point and a two-cycle has an exact all-temperature switch: its slowest nonstationary mode is positive for \(r<(a+b)/2\), negative for \(r>(a+b)/2\), and tied at equality. The critical inverse-temperature window has an explicit continuous equilibrium-mixture profile but a piecewise spectral relaxation profile, with an exactly factorizable transfer polynomial at the transition.
