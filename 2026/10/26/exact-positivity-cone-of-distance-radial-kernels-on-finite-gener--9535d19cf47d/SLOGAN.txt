On every finite thick generalized quadrangle, positivity of an arbitrary real distance-radial kernel reduces exactly to two scalar inequalities and two explicit two-by-two positive-semidefinite tests, with full spectral multiplicities even when the point and line orders differ. For the exponential graph-distance kernel, the sharp positive-scale cutoff is \(a=\tfrac12\log(st)\), with a one-dimensional boundary nullspace.
