# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** On constant linear generators, the source-proved stability polynomial gives the exact matrix endpoint. Independent binomial expansion gives all transient entries; the absorbing entry follows from the powers of the terminal generator column. The sign/unique-root arguments cover every entry without missing endpoints, and the staircase and asymptotic formulas follow from strictly ordered roots and Taylor's integral remainder. Exact rational matrix calculations support but do not replace the all-parameter arguments.

**Originality (PASS, best knowledge).** The common stability polynomial is explicitly prior work of Micalizzi and Torlo. Horváth treats universal classwise positivity conditions rather than the exact order-by-dimension pure-birth horizon. Modified Patankar DeC has a different nonlinear update; Szegő-curve asymptotics predate this work. Published record searches and comparisons with the closest recovered prior claims did not identify a covering result for the *combined* exact root formula, staircase, and dimension-sensitive parity limit. Earlier equivalent results under Jordan-block absolute-monotonicity terminology remain a genuine risk, not evidence of uniqueness.

**Value (PASS).** Pure-birth chains are natural finite-state positive conservative systems, and high-order explicit deferred correction is a relevant integrator family. A precise necessary-and-sufficient endpoint-stochasticity threshold, its dimension staircase, and the distinct asymptotic regimes give concrete step-size and method-order guidance beyond routine consistency or scalar linear stability. No theorem is claimed for general Markov generators or internal stages.

**Closest literature and limits.** Micalizzi–Torlo, arXiv:2210.02976, Theorem 2; Horváth, DOI 10.1016/j.apnum.2004.08.026; Öffner–Torlo, arXiv:1905.09237; Varga's discussion of exponential partial-sum zeros, DOI 10.1137/1.9781611970111.ch4. The proof is confined to equal-rate absorbing pure-birth chains and fixed dimension in its large-order limits. Independent verification has not been conducted.
