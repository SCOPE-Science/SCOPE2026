With exactly one empty vertex, fractional diversity-seeking jump games on every complete bipartite graph \(K_{a,b}\) with \(a,b\ge2\) have an explicit exact potential for arbitrarily many agent types, even when the graph is nonregular. For two types, equilibria are exactly the profiles whose fully occupied side is monochromatic, and their generating polynomial is \(a(1+z)^{a-1}(1+z^b)+b(1+z)^{b-1}(1+z^a)\).
