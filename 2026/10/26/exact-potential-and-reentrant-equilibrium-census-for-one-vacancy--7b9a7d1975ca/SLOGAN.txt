Single-vacancy fractional diversity jumps admit an exact potential on every complete multipartite graph, even with unequal part sizes; with two colors, all fully occupied parts in equilibrium must be monochromatic and satisfy explicit size-sensitive inequalities. These inequalities give a complete equilibrium-count polynomial and an integer reentrance on \(K_{2,2,b}\): opposite-colored full parts are stable for vacancy-part sizes \(b=3\) and \(b=5\), but not \(b=4\).
