In a cyclic copy automaton, the shortest word enlarging the inverse image of a specified subset is exactly one plus the earliest unoccupied-to-occupied crossing along the copy displacement. When that displacement is coprime to the number of states, path-component products determine the entire extension-length distribution, and precisely \(n-1\) of the \(2^n-2\) proper nonempty subsets require the maximal length \(n\).
