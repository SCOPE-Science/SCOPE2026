The exact dyadic sum of squares of the divisor-counting sequence A383066 has rational generating function \(2z(1+z+2z^2)/((1+z)(1-2z)(1-11z+2z^2))\). Consequently the squared coefficient of variation across a uniformly sampled dyadic row grows exponentially at base \(4(11+\sqrt{113})/(5+\sqrt{17})^2>1\), despite the known exact formula for its first moment.
