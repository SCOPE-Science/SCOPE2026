The pure first-order quantifier-rank theory of each finite carousel tournament is exactly the finite-linear-order theory shifted upward by one quantifier: \(R_{2m+1}\equiv_q R_{2n+1}\) if and only if \(\min(m,2^{q-1}-1)=\min(n,2^{q-1}-1)\). At rank one there is one class represented by the three-vertex tournament; for every \(q\ge2\), there are exactly \(2^{q-1}-1\) classes, with sharp largest minimal representative order \(2^q-1\).
