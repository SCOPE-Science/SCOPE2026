# Review of exact Haar-coin quantum-memory capacity

**Correctness — PASS.** Quillen's averaged channel (equations (20)–(23)) directly supplies the CPTP premise. Applying the explicit conditional-shift inverse to its iterates leaves a fixed coin state and the position convolution channel at exponent one smaller. The latter is a generalized dephasing channel whose complement measures the Fourier diagonal, admitting an explicit degrading map; coherent-information concavity and full Weyl covariance give an optimum at the maximally mixed input. The orthogonality of distinct regular translations gives the entropy of the classical convolution noise exactly. Symmetry and generation rule out other unit-modulus characters. On the correct parity coset, a character-orthogonality identity provides the factor \(1/h\) in the variance and hence the constant \(M/(2h\ln 2)\). The exact two-dimensional hypercube exception and the nonzero-mode hypothesis are included. All stated finite computations are ancillary; the infinite claim rests on proof and the standard degradable-channel coding theorem.

**Originality — PASS (best-knowledge, with a substantive prior-theorem dependency).** Quillen [1] proves the channel and its fixed Fourier modes; it does not give operational quantum capacity. Brádler et al. [2] already prove generalized-dephasing capacity single-letterization, so neither this capacity principle nor ordinary Fourier diagonalization is new. The new evaluated statement is the full coin-retaining \(m-1\) capacity law, the exhaustive bipartite residual-capacity criterion and the sharp, explicit spectral-multiplicity correction for every finite Abelian Cayley example. Primary sections of both relevant sources were examined. Searches of public literature, indexed mathematical findings, and indexed mathematical findings did not identify the evaluated correction or a stronger same-parameter result. The main remaining risk is an unlocated general spectral entropy asymptotic or existing Cayley dephasing-capacity specialization that would subsume this elementary-looking synthesis. Thus this is not an assertion of certified priority.

**Value — PASS.** An exactly preserved Fourier population is not itself a measurement of *quantum transmission ability*: the operational question is whether entanglement can be transmitted indefinitely, and at what finite-depth rate it decays. The binary zero-versus-one-qubit limit and the nontrivial coefficient \(d/(2\ln2)\) answer these motivated questions in Quillen's physically defined model. The final coin supplies recoverable noise-side information which shifts the decay by a whole step. The result is graph-wide rather than a hand-selected small example, and its explicit rate resolves the leading finite-depth memory deficit on a canonical family.

**Closest prior work and limitations.** [1] gives the physical average and spectral structure; [2] subsumes the degradability and single-letter parts. The result is for finite Abelian undirected connected Cayley graphs, independent Haar averaging, access to both output registers, and a fixed graph in the depth limit. It does not address the quenched walk or arbitrary quantum control. The search cannot exclude every prior equivalent entropy-capacity theorem.
