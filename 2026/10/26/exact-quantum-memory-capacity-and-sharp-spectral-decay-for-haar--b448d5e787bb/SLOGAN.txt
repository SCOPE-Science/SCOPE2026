For Haar-coin averaged walks on finite Abelian Cayley graphs, the exact depth-\(m\) quantum communication capacity is \(\log_2|G|-H_2(\mu^{*(m-1)})\): retaining the last coin flag saves one noise step. The long-time memory is zero or one qubit according to graph bipartiteness, with a sharp spectral-multiplicity decay coefficient; on the \(d\)-cube the excess above one qubit is asymptotic to \(\frac{d}{2\ln2}(1-2/d)^{2(m-1)}\).
