Fourth-degree spherical-harmonic sampling on antipodal representatives of the type-\(D_d\) roots has an explicit four-level spectrum for every \(d\ge5\): it is onto except for a seven-dimensional obstruction at \(d=8\). The least positive squared singular value is universally at least \(11/640\), with exact equality at \(d=9\), giving an optimal dimension-uniform interpolation bound.
