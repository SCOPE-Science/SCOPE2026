# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. Cyclic ordering translates independence into minimum gaps \(k+1\), while forward two-step domination gives the maximum gap \(2k+1\). Marking a selected vertex counts each quasi-kernel exactly \(s\) times and derives the enumerator. Inclusion-exclusion is explicit; independent direct enumeration of 90 parameter pairs agrees with the claimed formula. The proof is uniform in \(n\) and \(k\).

## Originality
PASS, subject to historical-priority limits. The 2026 weighted-transfer paper proves size inequalities, not a cardinality distribution. The 2004 number-of-quasi-kernels theorem classifies one/two-solution digraphs, not exact counts for circular powers. The main claim does not follow as a stated conclusion of either. Literature and mathematical-record searches under quasi-kernel, circular powers, independent distance-two domination and gap compositions identified no equivalent complete enumerator. A general cyclic-code or domination-polynomial formulation could predate this terminology.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The exact number of independent distance-two dominating selections of every size is a natural combinatorial invariant of directed circular power graphs, motivated by the small-quasi-kernel question and the older counting theory. The result determines both extremal sizes, all feasible intermediate cardinalities, and their exact multiplicities simultaneously, for all \(n\) and \(k\). It is more informative than a minimum-size bound or a tabulated example.
