For the directed circular power with steps \(1,\ldots,k\), quasi-kernels are exactly the cyclic selections whose consecutive gaps belong to \([k+1,2k+1]\). This yields a closed cardinality enumerator \(a_{n,k}(s)=\frac ns[x^{n-s(k+1)}](1+\cdots+x^k)^s\), with every size from \(\lceil n/(2k+1)\rceil\) through \(\lfloor n/(k+1)\rfloor\) attained.
