# Exact quasipolynomial growth for fixed rows of the natural-seed Z-game

## Correctness: PASS
The exact local formula \(\nu_p(Z(a,b))=|\nu_p(a)-\nu_p(b)|\) reduces the theorem to a finite absolute-difference triangle. For primes larger than the fixed row index, a single exponent spike propagates exactly as Pascal parity. For smaller primes, a high-spike stability induction shows that reducing an arbitrarily large valuation to the effective threshold changes both the row exponent and the polynomial exponent by the same amount. Hence the ratio is a positive rational function of finitely many residue classes. The binary support determines the claimed degree, and residue equidistribution gives the finite atomic limit. Separate exact computations and synthetic high-spike trials passed; the infinite conclusions follow from the written induction.

## Originality: PASS (inspected evidence)
Cobeli–Zaharescu (arXiv:1411.1334v1) established the game and prime-seed results. Cobeli–Prunescu–Zaharescu (arXiv:1511.04315v1) analyzed natural-seed \(2\)-adic tomographies and the different squarefree-kernel seed's periodic evolution. Their inspected statements do not entail the all-prime fixed-generation quasipolynomial and its degree. The 2013 survey and OEIS A222311/A222313 give context and finite west-edge data. Targeted mathematical literature searches found no covering statement. The substantial remaining risk is an unindexed equivalent fixed-row theorem.

## Value: PASS
Each fixed generation is a natural arithmetic function. Exact growth exponent \(2^{s_2(m)}\), explicit periodic corrections, and a finite atomic limiting distribution classify its global horizontal behavior in a way that neither a west-edge table nor a single-prime tomography can provide. The period bound is constructive but not optimal.
