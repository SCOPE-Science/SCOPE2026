Every Schatten metric on the qubit state ball has an exact finite-torus metric-cotype-2 constant \(\sqrt{2n/(1-((1+2\cos(2\pi/m))/3)^n)}\). At side length \(m=4\) and torus dimension \(n=2\), three-outcome diagonal states already force squared constant at least \(5\), strictly exceeding the sharp qubit value \(9/2\).
