# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The Bogoliubov singular-value representation is matched to an exact discrete Dirichlet–Neumann string. The Green kernel, operator-norm homogenization on the Lipschitz space, isolated leading eigenvalue, and first perturbative coefficient are checked. Bounded independent inverse-square weights obey a weighted central limit theorem; the delta-method constant is the declared variance. Finite calculations test the algebra and asymptotic trends but are not used to infer a theorem.

**Originality — PASS, with residual coverage risk.** Juhász (arXiv:2206.11575, especially Eq. (34) and Sec. V.2), Shirai–Tanaka (arXiv:2007.07439, Sec. 3), and Juhász–Roósz (arXiv:2309.12827, Sec. II) motivate exactly balanced field–coupling disorder, but the inspected texts establish scaling powers and physical dynamics without this model-specific almost-sure amplitude or Gaussian sample fluctuation variance. Bal (arXiv:0710.0363, Sec. 4) supplies general spectral central-limit machinery for other random elliptic operator families. A broader stochastic-string specialization not found in these sources remains a literature risk.

**Value — PASS.** Knowledge that the correlated critical chain has dynamical exponent \(z=1\) does not identify the finite-size quantum excitation timescale or the coefficient controlling disorder-sample uncertainty. Both are computed exactly from the inverse-square coupling law. The argument is structural rather than a numerical fit, with an exact finite-matrix reduction and explicit first variation.

**Limitations.** Bounded iid couplings, the specified boundary field, and the smallest single-fermion excitation are essential to the stated theorem. No claim is made about heavy-tailed couplings, altered balance rules, the parity-conserving excited state, finite-sample error rates, or independent validation.
