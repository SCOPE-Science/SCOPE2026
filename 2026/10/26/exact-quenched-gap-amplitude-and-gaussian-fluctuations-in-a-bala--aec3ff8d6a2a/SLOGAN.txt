In the exactly balanced, bounded-disorder transverse-field Ising chain, the lowest fermionic gap has the deterministic amplitude \(N\varepsilon_N\to\pi/(2\sqrt{\mathbb E[J^{-2}]})\) almost surely. Its sample-to-sample \(N^{-1/2}\) correction is Gaussian with exact asymptotic variance \(3\pi^2\operatorname{Var}(J^{-2})/(32(\mathbb E[J^{-2}])^3)\).
