For a cyclic \(n\)-state system with two coordinate actuators separated by \(r\) and exactly one independent fair actuator choice per step, the rank deficiency after \(qn\) steps has generating function \(J_{n/\gcd(n,r)}(4^{-q}(z-1))^{\gcd(n,r)}\), where \(J_d\) is the independence polynomial of a cycle. Exactly \(2^{\gcd(n,r)}\) of the \(2^n\) shortest-horizon schedules are controllable; the missing-coordinate count becomes Poisson with mean \(n4^{-q}\) in its critical window, giving a sharp random full-rank transition near \(n\log_4 n\) steps.
