For first-harmonic positive-weight kernel quadrature on a circle sampled from a positive antipodally symmetric density, the exact probability of nonzero optimal error is the classical \(N2^{-(N-1)}\), but conditional error severity has a density-dependent mixture-of-exponentials limit. The leading expected error is universal, while the leading squared-error risk is proportional to \(\int_0^{2\pi}f(\theta)^{-1}\,d\theta\), uniquely minimized by uniform angular sampling.
