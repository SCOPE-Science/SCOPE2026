The complete all-length number of distinct ordered adjacent-pair multiplicity profiles of ternary linear words has an explicit rational generating function. Its initial counts are \(1,9,24,65,156,342,683,1272,2226,3708\), and its asymptotic growth is \(7n^6/8640\); the proof separates six open-trail endpoint types from balanced closed trails and excludes disconnected arc profiles.
