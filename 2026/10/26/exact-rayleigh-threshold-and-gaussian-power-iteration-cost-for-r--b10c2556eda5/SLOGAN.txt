For a one-spike quadratic, rank-one spectral preconditioning improves optimally tuned gradient descent exactly when the approximate eigenvector's Rayleigh score exceeds the midpoint of the two eigenvalues; otherwise every nontrivial correction worsens the sharp contraction rate. Gaussian power initialization crosses this useful-direction threshold with an exact beta-law probability, and its high-dimensional transition occurs after order \((\log n)/(2\log\kappa)\) iterations.
