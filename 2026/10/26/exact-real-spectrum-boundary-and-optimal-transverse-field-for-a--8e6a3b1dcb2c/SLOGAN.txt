For the three-spin Ising chain with a single central raising defect, the full real-spectrum boundary is the smaller positive root of an explicit cubic discriminant. Its sharp critical defect strength has a unique positive minimum \(\gamma/J=0.377057759710\ldots\) at \(h/J=0.186548919829\ldots\), proving nonmonotone field control and a finite all-field stability floor for this trimer.
