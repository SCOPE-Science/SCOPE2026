# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Conditional Gaussian moments reduce excess kurtosis to the variance of integrated diffusivity, and stationarity gives its covariance convolution. Reversible-generator spectral projections supply positive exponential weights. Conversely, the explicitly specified independent Bernoulli switches have positive flip rates, obey detailed balance, and generate exactly the requested covariance weights at any prescribed positive mean; the diffusivity floor is strictly positive. Linear independence of distinct exponentials recovers the active spectrum uniquely. The exact-rational finite verifier checks detailed balance, spectral eigenmodes, amplitudes and positivity in three examples. These examples supplement, and do not replace, the general proof.

**Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk.** The forward covariance-to-kurtosis formula and the multicomponent positive spectral expansion are prior art, notably Chubynsky–Slater (2014) and Jensen (2023); no originality is asserted for either. The specifically examined gap is *converse unrestricted spectral realizability with strictly positive diffusivity at an arbitrary fixed mean*, together with unique observable spectral data but infinitely many microscopic models. The closest retrieved descriptions of Erland–Greenwood (2007) concern selected reversible-chain noise spectral synthesis and might contain broader tools; Jensen's accessible abstract addresses the forward multicomponent formula and log-convexity. Their full text was not accessible, leaving a genuine priority risk; lack of matches in database queries is not itself proof of novelty. Neither the accessible passages nor the accessible source material states the exact positive-mean converse construction.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Given experimentally inferred finite relaxation rates and positive amplitudes, the theorem answers a natural inverse feasibility question exactly: no additional Markov reversibility or positivity obstruction exists when only the mean is fixed and no diffusivity ceiling is imposed. The construction gives explicit finite reversible kinetics rather than formal covariance functions, and its identification/nonidentification distinction describes exactly what ideal fourth-moment time data can and cannot recover. This is a complete natural class classification, not a finite-parameter rounding, simulation, or small numerical increment.

**Scientific limitations.** This is a one-dimensional stationary independent-driver result for finite-state reversible chains, not a statement about experimental noisy inference, higher cumulants, full displacement-law identification, nonreversible exchange, or fixed maximum diffusivity. Full text of Jensen (DOI:10.1002/mrm.29926) and Erland–Greenwood (DOI:10.1103/PhysRevE.76.031114) was not obtained; abstract-level overlap comparisons do not rule out hidden stronger results.
