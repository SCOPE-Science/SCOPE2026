At any prescribed positive mean diffusivity, **every finite positive mixture** of the relaxation kernels \(K(\lambda t)=2(\lambda t-1+e^{-\lambda t})/(\lambda t)^2\) is exactly the displacement excess-kurtosis curve of a strictly positive, stationary reversible finite-state Markov-switching Brownian diffusivity. Independent binary switches explicitly realize arbitrary rates and weights, and the curve identifies their observable spectrum but not the underlying kinetics.
