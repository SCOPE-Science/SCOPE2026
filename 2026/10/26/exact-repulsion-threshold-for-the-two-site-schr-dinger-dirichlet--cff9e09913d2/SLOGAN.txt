In the untempered two-site, two-particle Schrödinger–Dirichlet boson sector, repulsion produces an exact ground-state transition at \(U=2g\): the outer-mode probabilities are \((2g-U)/(7g)\) below it and vanish at and above it. The same threshold is detected by the transverse linearized frequencies \(\pm\sqrt{U(U-2g)}\), giving a sharp instability exponent and a site-mode entanglement onset.
