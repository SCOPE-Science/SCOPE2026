A two-/three-site adsorption mixture on a complete substrate has an exact, nontrivial large-size probability of leaving one vacancy, given by a convergent one-dimensional integral for every positive rate ratio. Even arbitrarily rare trimers erase the dimer parity oscillation as size grows; at trimer rate \(3\), the vacancy probability tends to \(3^{-1/2}e^{-\pi/(6\sqrt3)}\).
