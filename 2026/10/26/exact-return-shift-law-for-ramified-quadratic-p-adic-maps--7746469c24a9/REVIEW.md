# Review

## Correctness
PASS. The affine coordinate \(x=\frac12+pt\) gives \(g(t)=t+a+pt^2\) with \(a=-(1-4c)/(4p)\) a unit. The recursion for \(g^p\), the sum-of-squares identity for primes \(p\ge5\), and the derivative-controlled induction establish the exact congruence for all \(n\ge0\). The shift has valuation exactly \(n+1\), which proves one cycle at each finite precision and minimality on the ball.

## Originality
PASS on the inspected literature and bounded claim-index searches. Fan–Liao's general decomposition theory and cycle-lifting machinery cover the broad framework; their square-map paper concerns \(x^2\), and Jeong's criterion covers ergodicity/minimality after conjugacy. Those sources do not state the explicit family-level displacement \(-p^n(1-4c)/4\) modulo \(p^{n+2}\). The novelty claim is limited to that exact return-shift formula, not to minimality by itself. Unindexed literature and a short corollary of general lifting lemmas remain residual risks.

## Value
PASS. The discriminant condition \(v_p(1-4c)=1\) creates a double fixed residue modulo \(p\) but no fixed point in its p-adic ball. The exact return shift resolves how this apparent fixed point lifts: at every precision the ball's residue classes form one cycle, with a specified increment rather than only an existence or period-growth statement. This is a natural boundary phenomenon in p-adic quadratic dynamics and gives precise finite-precision orbit periods.
