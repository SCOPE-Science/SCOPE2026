The exact Ritt resolvent constant for \(T_q=qI+(1-q)J_2\) switches from an antipodal boundary maximizer to two conjugate oblique maximizers at \(q_*=(7-2\sqrt6)/5\). The two angles split at square-root order, and the constants approach \(\sqrt5/2\), rather than \(1\), as these contractions converge to the identity.
