For every connected noncomplete strongly regular graph, classical RK4 heat diffusion has one exact entrywise-positivity cutoff determined by a strictly decreasing cubic; all nonedge transition entries stay positive for every positive timestep. Positivity and spectral stability have the same sharp horizon exactly for complete multipartite graphs, with a strict gap on every primitive strongly regular graph; on triangular graphs, the RK4 limit first exceeds twice forward Euler's at nine underlying vertices.
