With one vertex individualized, counting refinement on the \(k\)-th power of an \(n\)-cycle for \(n\ge4k+1\) reaches exactly the reflection-pair orbit partition after \(\lceil(\lfloor n/2\rfloor-1)/k\rceil\) strict rounds. At the sharp boundary \(n=4k\), the same orbit partition needs three rounds rather than the two predicted by that formula.
