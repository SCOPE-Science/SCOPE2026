# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** Weighted radial monomial moments yield exact bilateral-shift weights. Each of the \(n\) residue chains has a globally minimal bridge weight by strict log-convexity, so total variation yields the self-commutator trace norm and cancellation yields trace zero. Differentiation of the beta moments proves strict weight monotonicity. The two infinite weight tails yield the sharp polar defect singular-value coefficient and Schatten cutoff. The embedded exact-rational finite tests are supplementary, not infinite proof.

Nishio–Tanaka (2017) chiefly treat radial symbols. Indexed result comparisons for aliases and stronger claims found no same-object implication. The complete 2021 Wang–Zhao article and some earlier weighted-shift literature were not materially read; publication priority is not absolutely certified.

**Value — PASS.** The formulas determine a sharp weighted nonnormality invariant at every monomial degree, including a closed rational unweighted law and a strict parameter transition. The polar defect has a precise borderline weak-trace-class coefficient despite the selfcommutator being trace class. These exact distinctions are not consequences of the known polynomial spectral-set characterization.

**Limitations.** Only monomial symbols; no claim for general polynomials. Finite tests corroborate but do not establish infinite-rank conclusions. Independent validation is outstanding.
