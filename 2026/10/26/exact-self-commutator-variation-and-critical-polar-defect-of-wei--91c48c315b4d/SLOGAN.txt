For weighted harmonic Bergman monomial Toeplitz operators, the self-commutator has the exact trace norm \(2\sum_{r=0}^{n-1}(1-m_n^2/(m_rm_{n-r}))\), while the polar defect has critical weak-trace-class coefficient \(n(\alpha+1)\). This gives a sharp quantitative nonnormality and singular-value profile in every degree and every beta weight \(\alpha>-1\).
