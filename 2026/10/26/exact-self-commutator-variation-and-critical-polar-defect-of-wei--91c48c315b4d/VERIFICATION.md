# Mathematical verification and limits

The analytic argument establishes the theorem for all \(\alpha>-1\) and \(n\ge1\) by monomial Gram moments, log-convexity, residue-chain variation and exact reciprocal tail expansions. The proof shows summability of the commutator diagonal and the two-tail counting asymptotic for the polar defect. The independent mathematical audit has not been performed.

The standalone script `verify_harmonic.py` was executed successfully. It checked 7,665 exact independent Gram/weight comparisons, 840 finite residue valleys, 60 rational unweighted trace-norm formulas and 32 large-index tail approximations. These finite computations are not a certificate for the universal parameter ranges or the infinite asymptotic.

Literature limitations are recorded in `REVIEW.md` and `AUDIT.json`, including an inaccessible related 2021 original article and possible unindexed equivalent weighted-shift computations. No claim of independent priority certification is made.
