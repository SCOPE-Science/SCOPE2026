In the \(k\)-variable Boolean knowing-whether language over \(\mathrm{KD45}\), two pointed models are indistinguishable exactly when the root valuations agree and their successor-valuation supports agree, except that every singleton support collapses to one class. This gives exactly \(2^{2^k(2^{2^k}-2^k)}\) semantic formula classes, depth-one expressive completeness, and a sharp \(2^k\)-world finite-model bound.
