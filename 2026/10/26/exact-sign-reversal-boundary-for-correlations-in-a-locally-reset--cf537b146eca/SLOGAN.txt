In the locally reset one-dimensional voter model, a frozen Markov-correlated opinion template induces an exact stationary nearest-neighbor correlation with a unique reset-rate sign reversal precisely when the template is anticorrelated. No sign reversal is possible below the universal rate \(r_*=1.446156936667017\ldots\), attained by perfectly alternating templates; weak initial anticorrelation requires \(r_c\sim1/|c|\).
