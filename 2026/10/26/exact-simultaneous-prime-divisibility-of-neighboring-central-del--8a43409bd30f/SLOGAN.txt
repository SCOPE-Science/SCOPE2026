For every prime \(p\ge5\), the number of indices \(n<p^L\) at which \(p\) divides both neighboring central Delannoy numbers has an exact closed formula determined by the one-digit zero set. Since every prime \(p\equiv3\pmod4\), \(p\ge7\), has a guaranteed zero digit, the gcd of two consecutive central Delannoy numbers exceeds any fixed bound on a set of natural density one.
