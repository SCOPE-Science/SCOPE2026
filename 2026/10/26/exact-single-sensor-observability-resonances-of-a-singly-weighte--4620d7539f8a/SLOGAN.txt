On a cycle with one exceptional edge of weight \(w>0\), measuring the vertex immediately inward from an endpoint is sufficient to reconstruct every initial state except at explicitly classified resonances. The sensor fails for all weights exactly when \(6\mid n\); otherwise it fails at \(w=1\) and the positive weights \(\tfrac12-\cos(2\pi k/(n-3))\) for \(1\le k\le\lfloor(n-4)/2\rfloor\).
