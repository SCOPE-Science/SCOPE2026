For balanced complete multipartite graphs, singleton rainbow \(k\)-domination with \(k\ge3\) has an exact residue formula: if \(k=qm+s\le(r-1)m\) and \(0\le s<m\), then the minimum is \(qm+\min\{2s,m\}\), while for \(k>(r-1)m\) every vertex must be coloured. All minimum functions are classified by the choice between duplicating each residual colour in two parts and filling one additional part; explicit formulas count every optimum with named colours.
