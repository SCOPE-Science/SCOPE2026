For six-period repunit prime covers of symmetrically padded decimal integers, every covered center is divisible by \(3\), unless it has even digit length and is divisible by \(11\). The complete cover spectrum modulo \(111111\) has exactly \(60,10121,111,10121,60,10101\) classes across the six digit-length residues, proving a sharp limitation of the canonical six-period covering method without making any primality assertion outside that method.
