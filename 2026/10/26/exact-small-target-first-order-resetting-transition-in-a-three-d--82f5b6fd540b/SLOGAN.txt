For three-dimensional Brownian search between a shrinking absorbing inner sphere and a fixed outer sphere, the first-order resetting transition occurs at the exactly calibrated radius \(Zc^{1/3}+Kc+O(c^{4/3})\), with \(Z=(2/(3e^2))^{1/3}\) and \(K=1/3+1/(9e^2)\). Its competing nonzero reset rate diverges as \((4/Z^2)c^{-2/3}\); both asymptotic exponents and the global-optimum coexistence are proved rather than inferred from finite-size fits.
