For elementary cellular automaton Rule 110, the one-step spatial image is strictly sofic, with a five-state deterministic language automaton and exact word-count generating function \((1-z+z^2)/((1-z)(1-2z+z^2-z^3-z^4))\). Its spatial image entropy is \(\log\lambda\), where \(\lambda\) is the Perron root of \(\lambda^4-2\lambda^3+\lambda^2-\lambda-1\), and an infinite family of minimal forbidden words proves it is not of finite type.
