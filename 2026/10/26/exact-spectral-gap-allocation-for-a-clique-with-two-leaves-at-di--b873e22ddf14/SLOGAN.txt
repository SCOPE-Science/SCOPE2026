For a complete graph with a pendant leaf attached at each of two different clique vertices, the fastest continuous-time mixing rate under a total conductance budget \(B\) is exactly \(B/V_k\), where \(V_k\) is given by a unique scalar algebraic root. An explicit positive allocation attains the optimum on every edge and has Fiedler eigenvalue multiplicity \(k+1\); the optimal rate is asymptotic to \(2B/k\).
