The maximum mutual-visibility token-sliding layer of \(K_{m,m,m}\), for every \(m\ge4\), is connected and \(2m\)-regular on \(3m^2\) vertices, with adjacency spectrum \(\{(2m)^{[1]},m^{[3(m-1)]},(-m)^{[3m-1]},0^{[3(m-1)^2]}\}\). Its exact spanning-tree count follows from the spectrum, in sharp contrast to the isolated maximum layer for the complete bipartite family.
