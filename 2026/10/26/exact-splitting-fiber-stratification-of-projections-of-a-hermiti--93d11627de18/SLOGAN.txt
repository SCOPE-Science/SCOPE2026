For the smooth Hermitian elliptic cubic over \(\mathbb F_{64}\), the numbers of completely split rational fibers of the degree-three projections from all exterior centers take exactly six values \(11,14,16,17,18,26\), with multiplicities \(72,648,2052,864,432,12\). The twelve centers attaining \(26\) are precisely the points of the embedded \(\mathbb F_4\)-subplane lying off the curve; previously known completeness only guarantees at least one split fiber at each exterior center.
