For a Hermite oscillator truncated after level \(N\), observing the complex Weyl characteristic function on \(K\) arbitrary distinct positive-radius circles leaves exactly \((N+1-\min(K,N))^2-1\) real traceless degrees of freedom invisible. Every choice of \(N\) circles gives full mixed-state tomography, and each complete ring can be replaced by \(N+1\) equispaced half-circle samples when \(N\ge1\).
