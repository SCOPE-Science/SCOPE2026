For normalized Zernike modes of one total degree, shrinking a centered pupil can never make a higher adjacent angular order carry more energy than the lower one: their concentration difference is exactly a positive multiple of a squared Jacobi polynomial. In every ball dimension, the two modes touch without exchanging order at precisely the Jacobi quadrature nodes, with \((n-m)/2-1\) interior contacts for angular orders \(m,m+2\).
