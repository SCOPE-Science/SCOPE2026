The language of binary words containing exactly \(k\ge1\) scattered occurrences of \(01\) has minimal complete DFA size \(\lfloor k^2/4\rfloor+\lceil k/2\rceil+3\). Every residual is either initial, accepting-zero-only, rejecting, one of \(\lceil k/2\rceil\) exact-one-future-one states, or one of \(\lfloor k^2/4\rfloor\) distinct multiple-one states.
