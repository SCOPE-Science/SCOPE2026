For a binary word with exactly two marked bits and cyclic zero gaps \(a,b\), the minimal complete DFA accepting all distinct rotations has exactly \((2\ell+4)h+4-\ell^2-\min\{(h-\ell)^2,(\ell+1)^2\}\) states, where \(\ell=\min(a,b)\) and \(h=\max(a,b)\). The square correction counts all and only the quotient collisions between prefixes facing opposite marker gaps.
