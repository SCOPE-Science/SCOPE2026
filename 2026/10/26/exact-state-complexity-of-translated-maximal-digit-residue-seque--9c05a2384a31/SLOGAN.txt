For every base \(b\ge3\), modulus \(m\ge2\), and shift, the minimal least-significant-first automaton tracking the number of maximal digits modulo \(m\) has an exact digit-interval state count. At adjacent shifts \(b^r-1\) and \(b^r\), its state complexity drops by exactly \(r-m\) for \(r\ge m\), an arbitrarily large one-step change.
