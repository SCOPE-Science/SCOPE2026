## correctness

PASS

For Q=P−I with P^8=I, invert the denominator M=12−6h(P−I)+h²(P−I)² in Q[h,P]/(P^8−1), whose scalar denominator has poles in Re z>0 and hence is nonsingular for h≥0. Exact integer-polynomial multiplication gives M∑A_jP^j=ΔN with Δ having positive ascending coefficients. Disjoint supports of P^j reduce positivity to A_j≥0. Four A_j have coefficientwise nonnegative factors; four others factor as −3hF, −9h²(h+2)F2, −3h³F3, +3h^7G. Descartes gives unique positive roots ordered 0<root(G)<1<3<root(F)<4<root(F2),root(F3); therefore exactly h=0 or [root(G),root(F)]. Independent reconstruction verified all 8 exact polynomial identities, and four roots approximately 0.51225154437, 3.52719746627, 8.64387916, 30.25083321. Nonzero interval is strictly positive.
