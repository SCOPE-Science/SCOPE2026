# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The five-edge conflict clique forces alternating rung colors under any six-color strong coloring, and four available horizontal colors force period four. Both implications are verified against the explicit prism adjacency rule. Three small exceptional orders are settled by exhaustive induced-matching histograms. Nine seed colorings and the period-four word have been checked by two independent edge-conflict implementations, including their splice interfaces and sizes up to 100. The full construction is symbolic and therefore not an extrapolation from finite samples.

## Originality — PASS, with risk
The complete 2007 Togni article and the 2022 Dara–Mishra–Narayanan–Tuza article were examined for Cartesian product bounds and prism/cycle cases. Their published statements give overlapping known exact values and upper/lower bounds but do not state this complete all-order classification. Searches used synonyms strong chromatic index, strong edge chromatic number, circular ladder, prism, and cycle times a single edge, plus related mathematical-record results. There is residual risk of a missed equivalent older case classification, particularly in strong-edge-coloring databases.

## Value — PASS
This resolves the gap between known cycle-product bounds throughout a natural infinite cubic graph family, detects a unique small exceptional five-prism, and supplies a structural four-periodicity obstruction with an explicit repeating construction. The value is the complete natural classification rather than isolated small numerical improvements.

## Limitations
The result does not extend to arbitrary tree–cycle products. Finite checks do not independently certify the all-orders theorem. Publication priority and independent validation remain open.
