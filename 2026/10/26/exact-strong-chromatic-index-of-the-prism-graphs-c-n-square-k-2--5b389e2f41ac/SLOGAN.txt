The strong chromatic index of every prism graph \(C_n\square K_2\) equals \(9\) for \(n=3\), \(8\) for \(n\in\{5,6\}\), \(6\) for \(4\mid n\), and \(7\) otherwise. A forced four-column coloring period explains why six colors occur only at multiples of four, while explicit repeatable color patterns give the full upper bounds.
