For the Möbius ladder \(M_{2n}\), strong odd coloring has exact chromatic number \(2\) for odd \(n\), \(4\) when \(n\equiv2\pmod4\), and \(8,6,5\) respectively at \(n=4\), \(n=8\), and all multiples of four \(n\ge12\). For every even \(n\), strong odd colorings coincide *exactly* with proper colorings of the square graph, a rigidity arising from parity propagation across the twisted seam.
