For a single-vector Gaussian-sketched Krylov approximation of \(e^{-tA}b\) with positive-definite \(A\), the sketched Ritz value is exactly a shifted Student variable whenever \(b\) is not an eigenvector. Every finite sketch size then gives infinite moments of **all positive orders** for the numerical error, with sharp logarithmic tail \(\Pr\{\mathrm{error}>R\}\sim C(\log R)^{-\ell}\), although successful subspace embeddings can still yield accurate approximations.
