For binary words forbidding runs of more than \(r\) zeros, the exact all-length distribution of scattered-subsequence universality has the rational generating function \((1+z-z^{r+1}-uz^{r+2})/(1-z-2uz^2+uz^{r+2}+u^2z^{r+3})\). The typical universality index grows at a rate below \(1/3\) for \(r=1\), but strictly above \(1/3\) for every \(r\ge2\), approaching \(1/3\) again as the run limit tends to infinity.
