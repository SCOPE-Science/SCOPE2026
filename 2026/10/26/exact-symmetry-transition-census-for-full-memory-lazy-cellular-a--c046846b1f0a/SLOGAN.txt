For full-memory lazy cellular automata on any finite abelian group, the exact number of rules raising a prescribed stabilizer \(H\) to a larger stabilizer \(K\) is \((q-1)M_q(K)\), independent of \(H\), with \(M_q\) determined by subgroup Möbius inversion. At least half of all \(q\)-colorings of any abelian quotient are aperiodic, with equality exactly for binary quotients \(C_2\) and \(C_2\times C_2\).
