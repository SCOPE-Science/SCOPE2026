The exact false-positive spectrum of uniform fixed-size Boolean tasks under Reed–Solomon tagging is a finite alternating binomial sum for every alphabet, evaluation set and polynomial dimension. This also counts all sharp worst-case tasks and proves that a typical full-length tag with \(\lfloor\alpha q\rfloor\) positives has error converging to \(1-e^{-\alpha}\), even where the worst-case bound is one.
