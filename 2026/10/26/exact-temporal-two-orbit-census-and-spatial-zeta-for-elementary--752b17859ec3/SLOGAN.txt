For elementary cellular automaton 18, the exact count of temporal two-cycles on every cyclic spatial ring follows a six-lag recurrence, and the spatial zeta of its period-two subshift is \(1/[(1-z)(1-z^4-2z^5-z^6)]\). Nontrivial temporal two-cycles exist at exactly the ring lengths in \(\langle4,5,6\rangle\), so seven is the last excluded length, and their configurations grow at the plastic-constant exponential rate.
