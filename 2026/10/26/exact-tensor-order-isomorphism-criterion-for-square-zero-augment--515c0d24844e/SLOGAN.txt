For normalized augmented Rota–Baxter algebras with square-zero radicals, reversal of the oriented tensor product is possible exactly when radical dimensions and operator ranks match, or when both operators are the same extreme scalar type. In particular, two \(15\)-dimensional products can have linearly similar Rota–Baxter operators of rank \(7\) yet fail to be isomorphic as algebras, ruling out braidings on full fixed-intermediate-rank-ratio subcategories.
