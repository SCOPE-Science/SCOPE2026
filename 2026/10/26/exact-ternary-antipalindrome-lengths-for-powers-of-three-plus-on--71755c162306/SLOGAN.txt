For the natural radix-boundary sequence \(3^n+1\), the exact number of ternary antipalindromic summands is one at \(n=1\), two at \(n=2\) and every even \(n\ge6\), and three at every other \(n\ge3\). Every two-summand decomposition is unique up to order, and an explicit construction proves that the three-summand bound is attained on infinitely many indices.
