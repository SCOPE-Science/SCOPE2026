For ternary words of composition \((2,m,1)\), every Parikh-matrix ambiguity class has an explicit three-skeleton multiplicity formula. The number of distinct matrices is \((m+1)(m^2+11m+6)/6\), while the largest ambiguity class has size \(\max\{3,\lfloor m/2\rfloor+2\}\): worst-case ambiguity grows with \(m\) even though mean occupancy approaches three.
