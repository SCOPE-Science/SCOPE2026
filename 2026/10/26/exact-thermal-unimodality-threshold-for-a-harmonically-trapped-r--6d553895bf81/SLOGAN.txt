For a harmonically trapped run-and-tumble particle with additive thermal diffusion, an exact hypergeometric equation has one and only one positive root governing the entire bimodal-to-unimodal transition when \(0<\lambda/\kappa<1\). The critical thermal noise varies continuously from \(D\kappa/v^2=1\) at vanishing tumbling to zero at unit tumbling-to-trap ratio, and the boundary peak is quartically flat.
