# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The claim fixes the full admissible graph class, noise-free three-leader model and exact unnormalized coherence. The explicit strongly regular resolvent is obtained by multiplying matrices using the defining adjacency algebra. Jacobi's complementary-minor determinant identity and differentiation at zero produce the exact four rational costs. Exact positive numerator factorizations prove their strict order for every allowed parameter, including complete multipartite cases. Complement triangle/wedge/matching counting supplies mutually exclusive exhaustive optimal classes and counts. The standalone checker reproduces symbolic identities and independent rational inverses on nine sample graphs; this is support, not an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS (self-review).** Fitch–Leonard arXiv:1407.1569 establishes a general joint-centrality framework, including two-leader results and explicit cycles/paths, rather than the strict all-graph parameter-only three-leader classification and optimal-set count here. Patterson–Yi–Zhang arXiv:1708.06873 develops an effective-resistance expression and special classes (cycles, paths, trees), not the claimed strongly regular triple type hierarchy. Zhou–Wang–Bu (2016) characterizes strongly regular graphs via two resistance classes but does not optimize three pinned leaders. Targeted mathematical-record searches found no direct three-leader strongly regular equivalent. Since a general joint-centrality framework exists, the nontrivial step beyond it is the induced-type determinant reduction, three positive gap identities and complete optimal-triple enumeration. Unindexed variants remain a risk.

**Value — PASS.** This gives an exact, computable and exhaustive design rule for positioning three disturbance-free reference agents throughout a natural large class of diffusion/consensus networks. The rule needs only four strongly regular parameters rather than searching the \(\binom v3\) leader sets or inverting grounded Laplacians. It covers non-vertex-transitive graphs, distinguishes edgeless, one-edge and two-edge optimum regimes, and counts every optimum. The sharp strict gaps quantify the cost of suboptimal leader geometry.

**Limits.** Three perfect leaders only, unweighted undirected connected noncomplete strongly regular graphs, and equal independent follower-noise intensity. A general arbitrary-leader formula is already published; specialized literature could contain this explicit parameter-only reduction. No independent audit was conducted.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
