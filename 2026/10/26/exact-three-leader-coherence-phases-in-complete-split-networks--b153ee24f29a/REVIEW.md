# Review: exact three-leader coherence phases in complete split networks

## Correctness
PASS. The exact follower Laplacian is diagonalized into two within-class contrasts and a two-dimensional collective block. Its determinant is positive throughout the claimed domain. Four rational allocation costs are compared by algebraic inequalities; for the infinite region with at least seven peripheral vertices, two strict positive-coefficient polynomial certificates establish domination, and the remaining difference has exact sign controlled by the clique count relative to six. Boundaries with no peripheral followers are evaluated directly. Independent assembly of small grounded matrices and symbolic comparisons passed, but those finite tests are not substituted for the proof.

## Originality
PASS under the evidence inspected, with residual literature uncertainty. Fitch--Leonard (arXiv:1407.1569) gives the general joint-centrality formulation and solved cycle/path cases; Patterson--Yi--Zhang (arXiv:1708.06873) gives resistance-based methods and solved cycle/path/tree cases. Accessible adjacent mathematical calculations concern a different leader budget (two on split graphs) or a different network (three on complete bipartite graphs). A known general formula alone does not display the exact phase diagram or tie boundaries. Targeted mathematical-record, primary-text and literature queries disclosed no directly covering result, but incomplete indexing may conceal one.

## Value
PASS. The classification answers which core versus peripheral nodes should have one of three noise-free direct measurements for an infinite, common core-periphery network family. The optimal composition changes sharply at core counts twenty, ten and six as the periphery changes, and the triple tie at equal counts of six gives genuine degeneracy. Exact optimizer counts allow reliability/design comparisons; this is not merely a numerical example or a restatement of a general inverse identity.

## Limitations
All graph edges have unit weights; clique count is at least four, periphery count at least three, and exactly three leaders are noise-free. Results do not apply automatically to weighted/noisy/finite-gain leaders or different budgets. Novelty is assessed against accessible primary sources, published search results and closest recoverable related claims, not asserted as a universal absence theorem. Independent mathematical audit is outstanding.
