For a noiseless harmonically trapped chiral Brownian swimmer whose trap and orientational decorrelation rates match, the stationary coordinate spectrum changes sharply from one peak to three at \(\omega^2/\gamma^2=108/17\), with a square-root birth of symmetric sidebands. An unequal-relaxation parameter region instead has a central spectral dip, demonstrating distinct frequency-domain phases not inferable from static position multimodality alone.
