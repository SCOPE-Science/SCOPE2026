A qubit with a fixed Hamiltonian frequency and a single tiltable Hermitian dephasing axis has an exactly solvable optimal Liouvillian spectral gap with transitions at \((\omega/\Gamma)^2=1/4\) and \((\omega/\Gamma)^2=1/3\). The optimum moves from a perpendicular dephasing axis through an EP2 branch to a trace-saturating oscillatory branch, meeting at a known EP3 point; the result is a global axis-optimization theorem, not a claim to discover the exceptional points themselves.
