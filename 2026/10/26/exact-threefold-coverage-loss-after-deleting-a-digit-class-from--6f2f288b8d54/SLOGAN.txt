For alternating three-class base-\(g\) digit bases with \(g\ge4\), removing one entire class leaves exactly \((2g^2-g)^k+(3g-2)^k-(2g-1)^k-\binom{k+2}{2}\) integers below \(g^{3k}\) representable by three positive surviving summands. This gives the sharp coverage-loss exponent \(\log_{g^3}(2g^2-g)<1\), contrasting with the original cofinite threefold sumset.
