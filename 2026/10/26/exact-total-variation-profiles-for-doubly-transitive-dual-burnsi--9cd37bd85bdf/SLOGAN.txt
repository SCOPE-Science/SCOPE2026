For doubly transitive finite actions, dual Burnside total-variation distance decays by exactly the same factor \(\lambda\) at every step after the first; with a singleton fixer the sharp worst-start distance is \((1-2/n)\lambda^{t-1}\). The natural symmetric-group factor approaches \(1-e^{-1}\), but the affine-group factor is \((q-2)/(q-1)\), giving fundamentally different mixing scales even though conjugacy-class statistics reach stationarity immediately.
