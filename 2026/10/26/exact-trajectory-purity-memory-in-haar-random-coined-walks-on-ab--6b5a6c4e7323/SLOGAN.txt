For two Fourier modes of a Haar-random coined walk, the expected single-trajectory vertex purity is exactly \(\frac{1+1/d}{2}+\frac{1-1/d}{2}\gamma^t\), with \(\gamma=(d^2|\lambda|^2-1)/(d^2-1)\), even though the purity of the averaged state is \((1+|\lambda|^{2t})/2\). Whenever \(|\lambda|<1\), this noncommutation-of-averaging gap tends to \(1/(2d)\), uniformly in the number of vertices.
