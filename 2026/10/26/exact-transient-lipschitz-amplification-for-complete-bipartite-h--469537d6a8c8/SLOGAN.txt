For unnormalized heat flow on every finite unit-edge complete bipartite graph, the exact graph-Lipschitz amplification is \(\left(2-2/M\right)e^{-mt}-\left(1-2/M\right)e^{-(M+m)t}\), where \(M\) and \(m\) are the larger and smaller part sizes. It exceeds one at positive time exactly when \(M-m>2\), and a single time-independent Lipschitz function attains the norm throughout.
