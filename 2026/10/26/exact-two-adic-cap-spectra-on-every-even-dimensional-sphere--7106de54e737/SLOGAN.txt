Every normalized spherical-cap average on an even-dimensional sphere is injective when its height is a reduced rational with even denominator. More precisely, its degree-\(\ell\) harmonic eigenvalue has exactly the two-adic valuation \(-\ell\nu_2(q)-\nu_2((\ell+2k-1)!/(2k-2)!)\), independent of the height numerator; the operators remain compact and ill-conditioned to invert.
