In a strictly injective four-level Pauli matrix-product chain, **all** pairs of nonempty contiguous blocks separated by \(L\) sites are separable exactly when \(|\lambda_x|^L+|\lambda_y|^L+|\lambda_z|^L\leq1\); below this sharp threshold every such pair is NPT. For isotropic transfer multiplier \(s\), the separation threshold is \(\lceil\log3/(-\log s)\rceil\), diverging as the chain approaches a product state.
