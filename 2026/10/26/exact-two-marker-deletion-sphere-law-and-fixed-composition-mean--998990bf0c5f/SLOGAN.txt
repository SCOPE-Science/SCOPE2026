The exact \(t\)-deletion descendant count of every binary word with two ones is determined by its three zero gaps, and the sum over all \(\binom n2\) such words is \(\binom{t+2}{2}\binom{n-t}{2}+t(n-t)^2+\binom t2(n-t)+\mathbf1_{\{t\ge2\}}\binom n2\). For fixed \(t\ge1\), the largest deletion sphere stabilizes sharply at length \(3t+2\), attained exactly by words whose three zero gaps each have length at least \(t\).
