The canonical conjugate symmetric-power character pair of \(SU(3)\) has exact sharp upper and lower fourth moments for every degree \(n\ge2\); a divisibility-by-three resonance changes both constants and forces phase alignment or cancellation. In particular, when \(3\mid n\), the optimal fourth-power ratio ranges from \(n+1-2/(n+2)\) to \(3n/2+4\), with all equality cases explicitly classified.
