For shrinking alternating departures from uniform multinomial sampling, discrete total variation and Pearson's chi-square have an exact two-dimensional Gaussian local-power law with squared standardized shift ratio \(8/(\pi\sigma^2)=2.420001\ldots\). The best linear combination of these two statistics counterintuitively assigns Pearson a negative coefficient; the theorem applies to a specified iterated sample-size then bin-count limit.
