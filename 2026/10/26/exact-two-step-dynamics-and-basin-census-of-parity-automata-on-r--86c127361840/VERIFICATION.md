The complete proof reduces adjacency to commuting all-ones operators over the binary field. The even-factor tensor complex has homology dimension equal to the product of the dimensions of its zero summands, proving the only nontrivial rank statement used in the exact census.

The standalone `verify.py` uses graph vertices and open neighbours directly; it checks the map square identity, counts exact entry-time classes, counts periodic and fixed configurations, and verifies relevant binary matrix ranks. The accompanying `verification_output.txt` contains its actual `VERIFY_OK` result. Enumeration is bounded to the listed finite cases and is not offered as proof for infinitely many dimensions.

There has been no independent audit. Scientific limitations and possible undiscovered equivalent prior literature are stated in `RESULT.md` and `REVIEW.md`.
