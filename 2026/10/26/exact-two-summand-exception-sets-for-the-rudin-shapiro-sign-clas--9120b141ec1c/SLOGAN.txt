The two Rudin–Shapiro sign classes have completely explicit pairwise two-summand coverage: the even-parity class plus itself covers every nonnegative integer, the mixed sum misses only \(0,1,2,9\), and the odd-parity class plus itself misses exactly twelve small integers, ending at \(20\). An exhaustive 32-state binary-addition automaton and an 88-state finite product reachability certificate establish all three infinite classifications.
