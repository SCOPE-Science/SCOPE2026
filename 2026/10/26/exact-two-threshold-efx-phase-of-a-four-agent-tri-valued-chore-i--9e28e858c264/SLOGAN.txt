The four-agent thirteen-chore example with singleton costs \((r,1,7)\) and \((r,7,1)\), for real \(r>7\), admits a complete EFX allocation exactly when \(r\le19\) or \(r\ge21\). Thus its published nonexistence point \(r=20\) belongs to the sharp open failure interval \((19,21)\), with explicit feasible allocations on both sides.
