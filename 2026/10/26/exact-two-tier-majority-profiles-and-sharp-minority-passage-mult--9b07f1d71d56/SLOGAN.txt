Every binary voting pattern on a cycle is counted exactly by its initial supporters and its one-step local-majority proponents, through an explicit circular-run formula. For odd cycles, the smallest coalition that can pass a second-stage majority has size \(\lceil(n+3)/3\rceil\), and the exact number of winning placements at that sharp boundary has explicit formulas in the three residue classes modulo six, with small-order boundary cases.
