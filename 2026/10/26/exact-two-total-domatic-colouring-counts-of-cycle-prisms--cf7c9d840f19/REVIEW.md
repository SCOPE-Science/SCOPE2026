## Value — PASS

This is a natural infinite-family instance of the explicit Cartesian-product partition-enumeration direction posed in the 2025 preprint and refines the 2026 existence theorem numerically. A residue-six Lucas law is a closed exact enumerator rather than a routine local deduction or arbitrary short table. The construction handles all cycle lengths and distinguishes named colourings from unordered partitions.
