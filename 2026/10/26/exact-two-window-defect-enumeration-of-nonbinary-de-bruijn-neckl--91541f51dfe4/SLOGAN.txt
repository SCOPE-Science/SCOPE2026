For every nonbinary alphabet, cyclic words exactly two windows short of a de Bruijn cycle have a closed enumeration, with every missing pair classified as two constant words or two alternating words. The exact count for \(q\ge3\) is \(\frac{(q!)^{q^{n-1}}}{q^n}\frac{q^2+(-1)^{n-1}q^{2-n}}{q^2-1}\), and these words coincide with the corresponding balanced arbitrary-length necklaces.
