Uniform counting colour refinement on the \(d\)-fold Cartesian power of a path with \(n\) vertices recovers exactly its automorphism orbits after precisely \(\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor\) rounds, independently of \(d\). At every intermediate round the colour records exactly the histogram of coordinates within the corresponding distance from a path endpoint; there are \(\binom{d+\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor}{\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor}\) stable classes, with explicit multinomial sizes.
