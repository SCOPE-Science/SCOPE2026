For every prime \(p\) and multiplier \(m\) coprime to \(p\), the complete \(p\)-adic valuation profile of the Narayana row \(mp^r\) is an explicit polynomial in which the multiplier enters only through two finite carry polynomials. After subtracting valuations from \(r\), the distribution converges to a shifted geometric mixture at total-variation distance at most \(2p^{-r}\).
