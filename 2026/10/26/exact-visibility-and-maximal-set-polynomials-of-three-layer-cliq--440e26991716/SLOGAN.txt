Every mutual-visibility set of \(K_n\square P_3\) is either a product of five allowed column states or has one double-selected adjacent-layer pivot, yielding the exact polynomial \((1+3z+z^2)^n+2nz^2(1+3z)^{n-1}\). Its inclusion-maximal sets have polynomial \(2n3^{n-1}z^{n+1}+z^n((1+z)^n-1)\), so their possible sizes range from \(n+1\) to \(2n\).
