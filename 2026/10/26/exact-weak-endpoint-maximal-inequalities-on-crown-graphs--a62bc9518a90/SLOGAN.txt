For the centered Hardy–Littlewood maximal operator on the crown graph obtained by deleting a perfect matching from \(K_{n,n}\), the exact weak \(\ell^1\)-endpoint norm is \(2(n-1)/n\) for every \(n\ge3\), attained by uniform mass on one bipartition class. Restoring the matching increases the sharp constant by exactly \(2/[n(n+1)]\).
