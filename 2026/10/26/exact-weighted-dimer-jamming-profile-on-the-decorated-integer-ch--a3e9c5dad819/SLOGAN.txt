For irreversible dimers with distinct spine and pendant rates on a decorated path, every pendant-edge jamming probability has an exact finite sum with superfactorially small bulk-boundary error. On the infinite comb the probability is strictly increasing in the rate ratio and approaches \(e^{-2}\) as the pendant rate decreases to zero, even though it vanishes at exactly zero rate; the missing mass is recovered on the slow time scale \(\beta^{-1}\).
