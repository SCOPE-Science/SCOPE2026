For wheels with unit rim edges and uniform nonnegative spoke weight \(t\), the basic Max-Cut SDP, integer optimum, and all exactness thresholds admit closed forms. The spoke weight maximizing the multiplicative integrality gap switches sharply from \(t=0\) at odd rim sizes \(3,5,7\) to \(t=2\) at every odd rim size at least \(9\); even rims also maximize at \(t=2\).
