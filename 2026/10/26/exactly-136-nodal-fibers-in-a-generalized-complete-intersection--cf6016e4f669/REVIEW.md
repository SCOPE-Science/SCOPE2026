# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The fixed-base invertible coefficient transformation yields free quadric parameters. First- and second-jet incidence counts prove isolated Morse critical points away from the forced line, and two-point incidence proves no repeated critical value. The Euler formula and exact Chern check then give \(136\) fibers. The script verifies arithmetic and the nine-dimensional two-point jet image but does not certify the transversality argument.

**Originality — PASS (priority caveat).** Kanazawa and Garbagnati–van Geemen supply the threefold, its K3-fibered geometry and Hodge data, but not the complete generic nodal profile. Relevant full source sections and geometric implications were compared. Searches also found unrelated conic-bundle discriminants, K3 double planes and surface-fibration monodromy calculations. The possibility of an implicit earlier derivation and a partially uninspected companion paper remains.

**Value — PASS.** A complete reduced discriminant and local singularity description of the natural K3 fibration clarifies a geometric ingredient of current gCICY mirror-symmetry study. It is not claimed that the Euler characteristic or standard Picard–Lefschetz theorem alone is new.

**Scope.** General complex coefficients only, with no global monodromy image, higher singularities of special members or arithmetic extension.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
