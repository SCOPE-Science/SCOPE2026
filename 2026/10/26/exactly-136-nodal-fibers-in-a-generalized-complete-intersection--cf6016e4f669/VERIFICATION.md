# Reproducibility and limits

Run `python3 verify.py` (Python standard library only). This checks the degree-six zero-locus Euler integral \(-88\), the resulting count \(136\), and rank \(9\) of the two-point quadratic first-jet map. These checks are finite exact algebra and not substitutes for the separate general-position incidence proof.

The theorem's smoothness and distinct-critical-value assertions use projective jet-spanning and dimension arguments for general coefficients. Special coefficients may have worse singular fibers. The Hodge numbers, flop and Calabi–Yau construction are credited to the literature. Independent audit was not performed.
