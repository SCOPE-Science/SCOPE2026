The numerical semigroup generated by three consecutive ordinary pentagonal numbers, \(\langle P(n),P(n+1),P(n+2)\rangle\), is symmetric for exactly \(n\in\{2,3,5,40\}\), for all integers \(n\ge2\). The isolated three-generator exception \(n=40\) follows from a gluing representation, while pairwise-gcd and integrality obstructions rule out every other index.
