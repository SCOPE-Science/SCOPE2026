For the greedy two-base map, the absolutely continuous stationary binary digits have exactly two first-order Markov parameter loci: \(q_0=1+1/q_1\) gives a genuinely dependent golden-mean chain, while \(q_0=q_1/(q_1-1)\) gives independent digits. Every other admissible parameter is ruled out by a positive short transition that cannot be iterated arbitrarily long.
