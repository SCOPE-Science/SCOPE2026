The central zero-free radial band of the ordinary Bergman kernel on a thin annulus has width asymptotic to \(\frac{4\sqrt r}{\pi}\log(1/r)\exp[-\pi^2/\log(1/r)]\), where \(r\uparrow1\) is the inner radius. This sharp exponentially small rate quantifies the previously known qualitative annular zero-band classification; the exponential rate and leading constant are both proved.
