In the expiring uniform coupon collector with \(M=\lfloor\alpha n\log n\rfloor\), \(0<\alpha<1\), the consecutive complete-window episode begun at a stationary new entry has asymptotically \(\operatorname{Exp}(1)\) lifetime after division by \(n^\alpha\). Its exact mean is \(nS(M,n)/[(n-1)S(M-1,n-1)]\sim n^\alpha\): complete coverage is rare, but once attained it persists for a polynomial number of shifts.
