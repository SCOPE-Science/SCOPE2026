# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The equations and singularity types are taken from Uppal's three explicit examples. Exhaustive normalized weighted-projective enumeration reproduces the total counts through \(\mathbf F_{64}\), the Jacobian tests reproduce the smooth counts, and Newton's identities recover the stated quotient Frobenius polynomials. Their power traces give the formulas for every extension degree. The canonical-class factor is included in each zeta function.

**Originality — PASS, with bounded priority risk.** Uppal proves the base-field counterexamples but does not give these extension profiles. Blache–Hallouin provide a general zeta formula and degree-three-through-six arithmetic-type tables, while Tamanoi addresses unirationality; none of the inspected results states the three degree-two profiles. This is not a global uniqueness certificate.

**Value — PASS.** The profiles give a complete extension-field arithmetic description of three explicit counterexamples to smooth rational-point existence, including their zeta functions and the exact least extension degree where smooth points appear. This is a natural refinement of the motivating existence problem rather than a routine single-field recount.

**Limitations.** Only the three named surfaces are covered. The infinite formulas rely on the cited point-count theorem and Frobenius-polynomial reconstruction; enumeration is used only for the finite trace inputs. No independent external audit has been performed.
