In every multidimensional Cartesian torus whose cyclic factors have lengths at least four, the largest proper \(P_3\)-convex sets are exactly the complements of two consecutive layers perpendicular to a longest factor. Their common size is \(N-2N/K\), there are exactly \(tK\) of them when \(t\) factors have maximum length \(K\), and these are also precisely the largest starting sets that do not percolate under the two-neighbor rule; the numerical size bound itself was known, but the equality-case classification is the new assertion.
