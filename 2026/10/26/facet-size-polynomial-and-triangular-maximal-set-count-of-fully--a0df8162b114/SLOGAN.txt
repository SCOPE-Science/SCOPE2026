For fully whiskered paths with \(m\ge2\) spine vertices and arbitrary positive pendant multiplicities, every inclusion-maximal edge-general-position set belongs to one of three explicit structural families; their size polynomial is \(\mathcal F_{\mathbf t}(z)=z^{T_0}+z\sum_i(z^{L_i}+z^{R_i})+z^2\sum_{i<j}z^{M_{ij}}\). In particular, there are exactly \(m(m+1)/2\) maximal sets regardless of the number of leaves attached at each spine vertex, even though their sizes can have unbounded spread.
