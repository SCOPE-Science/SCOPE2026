For optimal proper \(k\)-step graph-burning schedules on the labeled cycle of order \(k^2-1\), relocating one ignition at a time leaves exactly \((k-2)!\) components, each of size \((k^2-1)k(k-1)\), even though every schedule has a legal move. At order \(k^2\), all \(k^2(k-1)!\) optimal schedules are isolated; a single vertex of additional coverage slack creates mobility but, from \(k=4\) onward, not connectivity.
