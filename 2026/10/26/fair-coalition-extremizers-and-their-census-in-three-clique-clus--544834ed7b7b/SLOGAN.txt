For a disjoint union of exactly three cliques, each of size at least three, the 1-fair coalition number is exactly the number of vertices minus three. Every optimal partition has three mixed doubletons; their component-pair multiplicities yield a closed, size-sensitive formula for the exact number of optimal unordered partitions, stabilizing at \(\prod_i n_i(n_i-1)\) when all three clique sizes are at least four.
