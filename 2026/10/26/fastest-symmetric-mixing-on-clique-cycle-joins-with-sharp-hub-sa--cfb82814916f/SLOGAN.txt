For the multi-hub wheel \(K_r\vee C_n\) with \(r\ge2\) and \(n\ge4\), the fastest symmetric stochastic chain has an explicit optimal eigenvalue modulus and exact hub, spoke, and rim weight formulas. Its optimal spokes exhaust the hub transition budget precisely when \(n\ge2r+2\) for \(2\le r\le4\), or \(n\ge2r+1\) for \(r\ge5\); all automorphism-invariant optimizers are classified.
