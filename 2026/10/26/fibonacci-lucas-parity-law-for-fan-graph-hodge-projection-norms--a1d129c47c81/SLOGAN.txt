For fan graphs \(K_1\vee P_n\), the canonical cut and cycle projection endpoint norms are exactly \(2-1/f_n\) and \(2-2/\ell_n\), interchanging which projection gets which value with the parity of \(n\). The cut maximum is attained at central path edges and the cycle maximum at central spokes, with an exponentially small alternating dominance gap \(f_{n-3}/f_{2n}\) for \(n\ge3\).
