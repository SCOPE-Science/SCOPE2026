For a uniformly random perfect evolution algebra over \(\mathbb F_{2^m}\), the full distribution of the number of nonzero idempotent elements is independent of \(m\): it is exactly \(2^D-1\), where \(D\) is the fixed-space dimension of a uniformly random element of \(\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb F_2)\). In dimension three the idempotent-free probability is always \(2/7\), contradicting the unrestricted-prime-power fixed-point-law consequence proposed in recent literature without disputing the characteristic-zero Galois-group conjecture.
