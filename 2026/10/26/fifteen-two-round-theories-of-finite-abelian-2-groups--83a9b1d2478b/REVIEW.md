## Correctness — PASS

The language has only the constant \(0\), equality, and ternary addition. The eight specified atomic predicates exactly exhaust the pair diagrams; the two-round game reduces to equality of the sets of one-round pointed profiles. The proof computes every such profile by considering \(0,x,-x,2x\), the full doubling fibre, and the remaining involution/non-involution witnesses, with their counts. It then identifies precisely when each of seven pointed categories exists, for arbitrary invariant-factor exponents and multiplicities, yielding the fifteen exhaustive classes and their pairwise distinct profiles. The largest least representative has order \(128\), and a smaller group cannot have that class's signature. Both finite computations were replayed from the packaged scripts and outputs; their finite scopes are identified separately from the all-parameter argument.

## Value — PASS

An all-parameter, exact bounded-rank classification for the natural family of finite abelian 2-groups gives a concrete hierarchy of which invariant-factor multiplicities remain logically visible when only two elements can be selected. The sharp order-128 finite representative bound and the persistent invisibility of cyclic exponents above sixteen are structurally informative; this is not an arbitrary finite computation or routine special-case lookup. The result provides a finite decision rule for any such group, independent of its size.
