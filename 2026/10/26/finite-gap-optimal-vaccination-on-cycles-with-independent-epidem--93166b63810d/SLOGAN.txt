When every unvaccinated person can independently seed a bond-percolation epidemic on a cycle, the optimal locations of two perfect vaccines become asymmetrically spaced on large cycles: the shorter surviving arc eventually has the fixed length \(\lfloor(1-q)[pq+2(1-p)]/[q(1-p+pq)]\rfloor+1\). At \(p=q=1/2\), precisely two unvaccinated vertices lie on that shorter arc for every cycle with at least six vertices, except that the eight-cycle also admits the tied balanced placement.
