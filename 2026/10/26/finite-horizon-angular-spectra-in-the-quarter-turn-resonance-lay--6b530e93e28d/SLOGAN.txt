For anisotropically conjugated planar rotations at angle \(\pi/2+t/N\), the finite-horizon one-dimensional angular spectrum converges at the explicit rate \(O_\kappa(|t|/N)\) to a sliding-window interval. Its width is positive except at nonzero detunings \(t\in(\pi/2)\mathbb Z\), where the interval collapses with an exact linear-cusp slope.
