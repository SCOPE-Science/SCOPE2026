On a finite rooted \(\mathsf{S4}\) bouquet, the simplex of automorphism-invariant valuation priors is larger than the simplex visible to **all** ordinary modal formulas at the root exactly when there are at least three terminal clusters or a terminal cluster of size at least three (with at least one propositional atom). Exact formulas count both dimensions and show that two modal levels suffice to observe everything that can be observed.
