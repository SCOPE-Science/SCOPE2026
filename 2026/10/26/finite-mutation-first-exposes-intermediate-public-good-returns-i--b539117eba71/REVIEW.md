# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Direct hypergeometric sampling gives the three displayed payoff gaps. Exact neutral detailed balance and the stationary mean-drift identity yield the rational derivative for every fixed positive mutation probability; analytic dependence justifies the small-selection sign claim. The reflection argument proves the group-size-two boundary for all population sizes. The independent detailed-balance-derivative checker passed 302 exact rational comparisons and two direct nonlinear numerical tests. The mathematical result concerns the stated update convention only.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** Wang et al. prove the low-mutation endpoint-only law, not a positive-mutation finite-four-person coefficient. Gokhale–Traulsen give general multiplayer mutation theory but their inspected three-player formulas use a large-population approximation rather than this exact finite Fermi-flip process. Kroumi–Lessard use a distinct Moran birth-death mechanism. Wu et al. also provide the general exact Fermi-chain detailed-balance stationary formula and quantify the validity of low-mutation approximations; this algebraic starting point is known, but it does not itself state the sharp return-specific boundary or reversal for the four-person three-player game. These sources establish strong related theory but do not directly state the sharp smallest-interaction return-sensitivity classification or the explicit two-profile reversal. A further algebraic specialization of general results could overlap this claim; absence from search results alone cannot establish novelty.

**Value — PASS.** The motivating endpoint-only criterion could be misapplied at non-negligible mutation rates. A sharp minimal group-size boundary, exact mutation-dependent sensitivity coefficient and sign reversal inside monotone returns establish when the criterion is no longer predictive; this is a structural counterexample, not a routine numerical increment.

**Limits.** The model is finite, well mixed and two-strategy; mutation flips the focal state and imitation samples a distinct model. The weak-selection expansion is at fixed positive mutation, and its remainder is not uniform toward rare mutation. Independent scientific auditing and publication-priority certification remain outstanding.
