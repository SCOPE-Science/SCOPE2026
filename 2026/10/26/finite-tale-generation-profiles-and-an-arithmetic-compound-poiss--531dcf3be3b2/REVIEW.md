# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The exact type decomposition is proved via the kernels of coordinate evaluation maps from the polynomial algebra; Frobenius-conjugate columns have precisely the same kernel and otherwise the Chinese remainder theorem separates them. Möbius inversion counts each orbit length, and independent labelled occupancy of each orbit yields the exact generating series. For fixed \(q,m\), bad-subfield coordinates converge to a Poisson variable only for the index-two subfield; full-degree orbit pair collisions converge independently to a second Poisson variable because triple and higher collisions vanish. The computed dimension loss is exactly the stated sum on an event of probability tending to one. The proof includes \(m=1\), \(m=2\), and \(c=0\), and allows any finite field. Direct exhaustive algebra-generation and profile tests passed in eight configurations, 16,403 generator arrays, but are not offered as infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS within examined literature

The closest full primary source is Kravchenko–Mazur–Petrenko, arXiv:1001.2873, Section 6 (especially Theorems 6.1 and 6.3). It determines when a tuple generates a whole direct power, including an exact full-generation count, but it does not state the complete joint isomorphism-type distribution of all *proper generated subalgebras*, nor the even-degree two-source dimension-defect limit. Sercombe–Shalev, arXiv:2009.01115, treats full-generation probabilities and generator thresholds for finite associative algebras; the reviewed results likewise do not give these claims. The structural orbit technique and the one-bit full-generation formula are acknowledged as earlier results, and the new count has been compared with both by mathematical implication rather than title. Residual risk: an equivalent full-type enumerator or collision/subfield limit might exist in unexamined finite-étale, generating-set, or combinatorial species literature. No claim of exhaustive priority or independent verification is made.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Generation by random tuples is a natural structural question for finite associative algebras, but the existing full-generation probability is only a one-bit statistic. The complete field-factor profile determines *all* random isomorphism types and dimensions of generated subalgebras, across arbitrary finite-field orders, extension degrees, generator counts, and product sizes. The critical window exhibits two independent mathematical obstructions—Frobenius-orbit collisions and subfield trapping—with an arithmetic parity boundary. This goes beyond the older full-generation criterion and supplies a nontrivial exact-and-asymptotic invariant usable to study proper subalgebras.
