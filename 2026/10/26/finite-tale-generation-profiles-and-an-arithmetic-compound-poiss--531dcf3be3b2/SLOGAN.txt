Random unital subalgebras generated inside \((\mathbb F_{q^m})^r\) have an exact multivariate Frobenius-orbit occupancy series specifying every field-factor isomorphism type. At the critical scale \(r\asymp q^{m\ell/2}\), their dimension deficit converges to a sum of independent Poisson terms, with an additional proper-subfield contribution occurring exactly when \(m\) is even.
