For every finite acyclic quiver over an algebraically closed field, its face algebra is representation-finite exactly for disjoint directed paths, tame infinite exactly when directed paths and at least one two-arrow fork are its only components, and wild otherwise. Each ordered pair of forks contributes one affine factor of type \(\widetilde D_4\) or \(\widetilde A_3\); finite-type indecomposables are counted exactly by the face algebra's vector-space dimension.
