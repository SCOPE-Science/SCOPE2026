**Correctness — PASS.** The main identity is reconstructed from independent Poisson first-arrivals of seven support types, classified by zero, one, or two target-incident pair equations. Peeling closure is explicit, including the special case with no singletons. Integrating each of the seven exponential terms gives the expectation formula. A global quadratic lower certificate proves positivity of the exact quartic representing the gap above \(27921/10000\) for every degree-one/two distribution, not merely at sampled mixtures. The 100-column witness and strict gap are exact fractions. The accompanying independent rational state recurrence agrees with the survival formula on six cases.

**Originality — PASS subject to residual literature risk.** The 2026 Hofmeister–Bitar–Yaakobi study analyzes fully symmetric LT peeling asymptotically in the number of source symbols, whereas the statement here is the explicit exact three-source survival polynomial and qualitative strict necessity of degree-three equations. The exact random-access algorithms of Wang–Yaakobi and constructions of Boruchovsky et al. use unrestricted linear decoding, which permits recovery from equations that iterative peeling cannot use. Closest accessible mathematical-record summaries address asymptotic degree-one/two ideal decoding rather than finite three-source peeling. Neither the differing decoder nor the asymptotic limit proves the new exact threshold. Exhaustiveness of priority is not claimed.

**Value — PASS.** Finite source dimension is a naturally motivated missing regime in the recent LT/fully-symmetric retrieval model. This is a complete explicit finite-dimension peeling law together with a structural support-necessity result, not a numerical optimization claim. The numerical gain of the displayed example is small and has no asserted practical impact; the value lies in the exact qualitative boundary between supports of degree at most two and degree three.
