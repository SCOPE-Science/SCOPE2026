# Reproduction and limits

Run `python3 verify.py certificate.json` with a standard Python 3 interpreter. The script checks exactly the published cycle range, independently constructs and agrees on each hypothesis family, independently computes two complete adaptive ranks, exhaustively checks all VC traces, and verifies the two decision-tree certificate leaves against the complete hypothesis lists. The checked terminal sentinel is `ALL_CYCLE_CHECKS_PASS`; the bundled `verification_output.txt` was generated by the same program without a certificate argument. Every computation is finite. The proof of disjoint-union additivity appears in `RESULT.md`; the finite computation is not offered as a proof of untested orders or of historical novelty.
