Adding the trivial character to the two high-weight \(SU(2)\) characters \(\chi_n,\chi_{2n}\) strictly improves the optimal fourth moment for every \(n\ge1\), but the gain is only \(\frac{100}{63n}+O(n^{-2})\) for odd \(n\) and \(\frac{333+180\sqrt3}{169n}+O(n^{-2})\) for even \(n\). The trivial-mode amplitude at every optimizer is asymptotic to an explicit positive parity-dependent multiple of \(1/n\).
