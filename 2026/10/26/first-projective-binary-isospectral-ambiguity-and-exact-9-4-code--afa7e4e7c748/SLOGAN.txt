The first unequal projective binary codes with the same complete Hamming weight enumerator occur at length eight and dimension five; none occur in lower dimension or at smaller length. An explicit pair has exactly the same \(1+3z^2+8z^3+7z^4+8z^5+5z^6\) enumerator but different weight-two span ranks, and their exact minimum linear code distortion is \(9/4\).
