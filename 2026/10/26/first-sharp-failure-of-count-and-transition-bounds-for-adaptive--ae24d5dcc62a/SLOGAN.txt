The natural symbol-count and cyclic-transition bound for rotation-adaptive binary scattered subsequences is always sharp through circumference 13, but at circumference 14 it fails for exactly one necklace, `00000101111101`. The obstruction persists for every circular word \(0^a\,1\,0\,1^b\,0\,1\) with integers \(a,b\ge5\), where the bound is seven but the exact adaptive universality index is six.
