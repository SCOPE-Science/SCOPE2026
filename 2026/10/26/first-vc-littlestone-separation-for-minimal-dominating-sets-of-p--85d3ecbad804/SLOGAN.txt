The eleven-vertex path is the first path whose inclusion-minimal dominating-set concepts have different static and adaptive shattering dimensions: \(\operatorname{VCdim}=4\), but \(\operatorname{Ldim}=5\). Disjoint unions of \(s\) such paths give the exact unbounded gap \(\operatorname{Ldim}-\operatorname{VCdim}=s\) in maximum-degree-two forests.
