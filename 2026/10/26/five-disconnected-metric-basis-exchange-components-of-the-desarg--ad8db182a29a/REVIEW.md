# Review of the Desargues metric-basis classification

## Correctness

PASS. The distance-transfer lemma follows from even/odd walks in the Petersen graph and was checked against every independently computed distance in the twenty-vertex lift. The classification of all three-edge subgraphs of \(K_5\) and explicit separating signatures establish the only Petersen metric bases. The center-preserving exchange invariant and three-replacement construction establish the exact components and diameter without extrapolation from small examples. Two standalone exhaustive programs on independently constructed graph models enumerate all \(1140\) three-sets and reproduce each reported count.

## Originality

PASS, with residual literature uncertainty. Boutin (arXiv:0808.1427v1) exhibits a Petersen three-star resolver and discusses failure of a general exchange property, but does not characterize the independently signed Desargues lifts or establish the five-component exchange graph. Shao and colleagues (2018, DOI 10.1155/2018/4531958) establish the known scalar metric dimension three for \(GP(10,3)\), not the family of \(160\) bases and their exchange geometry. Imran and colleagues (2014) study the broader \(GP(n,3)\) dimension problem, without the asserted all-bases result in inspected sections. Published topology results on this same graph concern other invariants. An exact equivalent enumeration in unindexed work cannot be ruled out.

## Value

PASS. The established failure of exchange phenomena for Petersen resolving sets motivates determining what happens at the **minimum** size in its canonical bipartite double. A complete classification and a sharp five-component obstruction are structural and use the natural symmetry of the graph. Every same-center pair of optimal landmark placements is connected in an explicitly minimal number of moves, whereas no cross-center reconfiguration exists. The new classification is mathematically more informative than the previously known minimum cardinality.

## Limits

No independent external audit; no claim of verified publication priority. Only minimum resolving sets in this one graph are classified.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
