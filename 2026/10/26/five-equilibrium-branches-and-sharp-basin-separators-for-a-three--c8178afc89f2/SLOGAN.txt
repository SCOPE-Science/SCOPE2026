For one persuadable opinion agent between zealots at \(-1\) and \(1\), the smooth sigmoidal confidence rule has exactly five equilibria and three attracting basins for every fixed \(1<\delta<4\) at sufficiently high steepness. Its basin separators approach \(\pm(\sqrt\delta-1)\) with a computed \(1/\gamma\) correction, while the polarized attractors approach \(\pm1\) exponentially fast.
