In heterogeneous sampling-best-response coordination games with payoff ratio \(u>1\), no population whose agents draw at most four opponents can have a stable interior state, but five opponents suffice. For \(1<u<3/2\), mixing two-draw and five-draw learners yields stable miscoordination exactly when the two-draw share satisfies \(1/2<\alpha<7\sqrt7/(18+7\sqrt7)\), with a sharp saddle-node boundary and an explicit competing coordination basin.
