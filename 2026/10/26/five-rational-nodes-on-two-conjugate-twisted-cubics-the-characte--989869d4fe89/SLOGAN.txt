For the flat elementary-symmetric Bring complete intersection \(e_1=e_2=e_3=0\), the characteristic-two fibre is precisely two Frobenius-conjugate twisted cubics meeting in five \(\mathbb F_2\)-rational nodes. Its point counts are \(5\) over odd-degree extensions and \(2^{n+1}-3\) over degree-\(n\) even extensions, and its zeta function is \(\frac{(1+T)^4}{(1-T)(1-4T^2)}\).
