Minimum exact-distance-two dominating sets of the open ladder \(P_n\square K_2\) have size \(2\lceil n/5\rceil\), and their exact multiplicity is the square of a five-residue bounded-composition coefficient. At every length divisible by five the optimum is unique, while the other four residue classes have polynomially many optima of degrees two, four, six, and eight.
