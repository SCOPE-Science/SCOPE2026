Every mixed-symbol contiguous square in a five-run binary word has the same length, and the complete distinct-square set admits an exact run-length classification with simultaneous sharp total and mixed-square bounds. Three-run-root squares arise only at a rare synchronization of the two one-runs: their number has a rational generating function, asymptotic mean \(1/8\), and positive-event probability asymptotic to \(3/(2n)\).
