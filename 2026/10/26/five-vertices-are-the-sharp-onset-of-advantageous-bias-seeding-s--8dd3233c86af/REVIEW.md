# Review: Five vertices are the sharp onset of advantageous-bias seeding switches

## Correctness — PASS

The finite-state death-first copying process is fully specified on connected simple undirected graphs, with fitness \(r>1\) and exactly two initial mutants. The lumped \(K_{2,3}\) transition rates and their rational fixation solutions were independently checked symbolically in the actual `verify.py`. The three pairwise differences have explicitly positive denominators for \(r>0\); Descartes' rule, the signs at \(1\) and \(3\), and the two strict dominances establish exactly one optimal-orbit transition. Eight small connected graph representatives cover every isomorphism class on three or four vertices, and the two-vertex case has only one pair. For each small graph every competing pair's difference is zero or is certified positive for \(r>1\) by a coefficientwise nonnegative numerator and denominator after \(r=1+z\). A separate full 32-state chain agrees with the lumped solution at rational biases \(2,3\). Exhaustive finite enumeration is the proof of the finite lower bound, not an extrapolation to all orders.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

Durocher–Karras–Pavlogiannis–Tkadlec, *Invasion Dynamics in the Biased Voter Process*, arXiv:2201.08207v1 (2022), studies the exact death-first rule, optimizing seed sets, neutral-fitness degree rankings and favorable-bias structure, as well as a seven-node example of changing seed quality. **The mere existence of a crossover on \(K_{2,3}\) is implied by such known endpoint statements and is not claimed original.** The independently tested novelty is the universal sharp smallest graph order five for two-seed optimal identity on all finite connected undirected graphs, and a complete all-fitness three-orbit phase diagram with one uniquely located algebraic transition. The 2012 Zhang–Wu–Liu–Yang complete-bipartite fixation calculations use a birth-first rather than a death-first rule, so their exact formulas do not imply ours. The accessible full HTML for both primary sources was read in the relevant model, theorem, examples, section and appendix portions. Multiple actual published-finding corpus searches for the precise object, aliases, model and threshold yielded no comparable covering result, but absence from ranked search is not a proof of global priority. Residual risk: an older specialty calculation may give an equivalent exact threshold for this five-node graph, and the small-graph minimality may exist under different terminology.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Optimal placement of a small initial committed group is a natural design question in contagion/opinion dynamics. The theorem resolves the first network order at which an advantageous selection parameter forces the identity of the globally most effective two-site intervention to change, over **every** undirected connected network of smaller order. Its complete explicit algebraic switching boundary shows that the two-seed location need not be fixed even on five vertices, while its absence on all smaller graphs is exact rather than heuristic. This is a motivated extremal cutoff plus phase classification, not simply recomputation of fixation probability at an arbitrary graph. The result does not claim all higher-order networks, bias below unity, or other evolutionary update conventions.
