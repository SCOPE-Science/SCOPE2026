The edge sets shattered by fixed-size vertex-cover traces are exactly star forests with enough unused vertices and a sharp componentwise selection budget. Consequently, their VC dimension is \(\max\{0,\min(2r,n-r-1,\lfloor n/2\rfloor)\}\); for closed neighborhoods of the triangular graph \(J(n,2)\), the exact threshold for VC dimension four is \(n=8\), and every shattered edge-set type is explicitly enumerated.
