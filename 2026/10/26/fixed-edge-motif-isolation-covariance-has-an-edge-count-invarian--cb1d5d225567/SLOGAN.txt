In the uniform fixed-edge random graph \(G(n,m)\), the covariance of isolated-vertex count with any non-induced motif count has a sign independent of \(m\) and determined solely by the motif's vertex and edge numbers. Two-edge wedges have positive covariance whereas two-edge matchings have negative covariance, and a wedge-plus-edge motif switches sign sharply at \(n=11\), contrasting with the uniformly negative covariance in \(G(n,p)\).
