For a fixed positive denominator floor, the final-only Patankar correction can preserve positivity and mass while timestep refinement converges to the wrong reversible-exchange dynamics: \(\dot y=G\big(y_i^2/\max(y_i,\varepsilon)\big)_i\). Its attracting equilibrium has a sharp square-root / mixed-quadratic / true-equilibrium phase diagram, and the exact original equilibrium can move under every positive timestep.
