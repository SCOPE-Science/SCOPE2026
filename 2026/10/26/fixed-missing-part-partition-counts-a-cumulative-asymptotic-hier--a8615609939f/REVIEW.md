## Correctness
The source generating function is factored, the leading Bell-polynomial term isolated, and the constant calculated by an absolutely convergent integral. A uniform complex-sector estimate is explicitly established before applying the corrected cumulative form of Ingham’s theorem. Exact small-index enumeration reproduces the generating function, without serving as the infinite proof.

## Originality
The full original and follow-up texts discuss generating functions, identities and congruences, not this fixed-m cumulative asymptotic. The general Tauberian theorem is acknowledged, and possible implicit earlier derivations are a residual bibliographic risk.

## Mathematical value
This is a natural complete asymptotic hierarchy for partitions with exactly a fixed number of absent part sizes, including an explicit \(1-\log2\) rarity constant relative to gap-free partitions. It is not a pointwise or growing-parameter theorem.
