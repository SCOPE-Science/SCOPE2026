# Review

## Correctness — PASS
The automorphism form follows from preservation of the derived center and the alternating bracket. The fixed-vector condition reduces exactly to a nontrivial similitude multiplier and an invertible \(g-I\). Möbius inversion over isotropic subspaces, the pointwise parabolic stabilizer order, and the order of the symplectic group yield the stated formula. The verifier independently checks the low-dimensional finite-field counts, without treating them as proof of the general theorem.

## Originality — PASS, provisional
The closest exact mathematical record located gives only the three-dimensional case \(n=1\). The inspected 2013 paper on classical-group subspace derangements studies invariant subspaces and proportions, not the exact count of linear fixed-point-free automorphisms of every finite Heisenberg Lie algebra. The 2013 automorphism tables concern real Lie algebras of dimension at most five. The exact all-\(n\) finite-field count and its limiting proportion were not found in the searched literature. The full text of the 1998 eigenvalue-free-elements paper was inaccessible, so a residual priority risk remains.

## Value — PASS
Fixed-point-free automorphisms are a natural invariant in finite Lie algebra theory. The formula gives an exact count across every Heisenberg dimension and every finite field, makes the characteristic-two zero case immediate, and yields a limiting proportion expressed by an Euler product. The result is a reusable all-dimension formula rather than an isolated numerical example.
