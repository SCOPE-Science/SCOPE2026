# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The rational generating identity and coefficient expression are exact. An inductive differential recurrence proves all numerator zeros simple and negative. Rouché's theorem under strict exponential coefficient-rate separation locates each fixed-rank root, and Stirling's formula determines its first sharp prefactor. The numerical checker tests finite examples but does not replace the proof.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The polynomial is an instance of the classical Veronese numerator, and negative real-rootedness and reciprocal symmetry are already covered by the foundational literature. The distinguishing claim is the all-fixed-rank coupled-parameter exponential zero scale, including the prefactors and Cauchy-jet interpretation. Inspected Brenti--Welker and Jochemko treat fixed-degree large-Veronese regimes; Mallat--Waldspurger and Durán study different objects. An older asymptotic theorem under another terminology could subsume these rates; publication priority is not independently established.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result identifies the natural high-order complex Cauchy-transform zero annuli in the singular limit, producing explicit persistent radii and sharp first-order radial corrections for each fixed ring. It is an infinite asymptotic structural classification rather than a small computational novelty.

## Limitations
No uniform result for ring indices growing with polygon size. Historical literature may imply the rates by an alternate Veronese formulation. All numerical tests are illustrative and only finite.
