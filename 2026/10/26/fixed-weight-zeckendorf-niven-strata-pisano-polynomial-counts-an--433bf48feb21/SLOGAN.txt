For every fixed Zeckendorf summand count \(s\ge2\), the number of Zeckendorf–Niven integers below a Fibonacci cutoff is eventually quasi-polynomial in the index, with an explicit Pisano-convolution leading term and a Fibonacci-specific adjacency correction. The conditional proportions for two, three, four, and five summands are respectively \(5/9\), \(85/256\), \(7/27\), and \(1/5\), and the first four full-period counts admit exact polynomials at every positive block length.
