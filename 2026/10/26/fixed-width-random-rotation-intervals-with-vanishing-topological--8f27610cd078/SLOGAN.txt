Even fixing both the random rotation interval at \([0,1]\) and the expected maximal one-step lift displacement at \(1\), iid degree-one circle maps can have arbitrarily small **positive** random topological entropy. Explicit Bernoulli insertion of identities gives the sharp time-thinning identity \(h_{\mathrm{top}}^{(r)}=h_{\mathrm{top}}(f_m)/m\le\log(2m+1)/m\).
