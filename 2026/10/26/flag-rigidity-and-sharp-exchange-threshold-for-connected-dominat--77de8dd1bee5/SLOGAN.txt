For every finite projective plane of order \(q\ge2\), the minimum connected dominating sets of its point–line incidence graph are **exactly** the double stars indexed by incident point–line flags, giving precisely \((q^2+q+1)(q+1)\) maximum-leaf spanning trees, each with \(2q^2\) leaves. Distinct optimal sets require at least \(q\) simultaneous vertex replacements to exchange, and at that sharp threshold their reconfiguration graph is the connected line graph of the incidence graph, with diameter three.
