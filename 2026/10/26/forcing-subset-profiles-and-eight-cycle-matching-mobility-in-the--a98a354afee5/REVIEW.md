# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The canonical generalized-Petersen exact-cover recursion enumerates all 36 perfect matchings. Both scripts test all 1024 subsets per matching with definitionally equivalent but independently encoded forcing predicates. They reproduce the polynomial stratification 6/30. For each unordered pair of perfect matchings, symmetric difference eight is equivalent to one eight-cycle flip because the dodecahedral graph has no four-cycle. The independently generated legal local replacements reproduce the 120 edges, degrees, connectivity and all eccentricities equal to three. Limit: finite classification of one twenty-vertex graph; no claim for all fullerenes.

## Mathematical value — PASS
Forcing number alone cannot distinguish any two C20 Kekulé structures, whereas the full subset polynomial separates the natural 6+30 matching classes, quantifying the redundancy of unique-identification edge choices across all cardinalities. The same separation exactly determines the ten-versus-six local mobility classes in the shortest legal alternating-cycle flip graph, which is connected with diameter three. These are concrete exact invariants of the smallest fullerene, not an arbitrary induced slice or a recomputation of the known minimum.
