In the dodecahedral graph, all \(36\) perfect matchings need three edges for unique identification, yet their complete forcing-subset polynomials split into two types of sizes \(6\) and \(30\). The six matchings with \(20\) forcing triples are exactly the degree-ten states of the connected, diameter-three alternating-eight-cycle flip graph; the other thirty have \(24\) forcing triples and flip degree six.
