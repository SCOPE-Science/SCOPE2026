# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** The tensor-product evaluation kernel has dimension 11; a smooth filling form necessarily restricts to the same nonzero rational-root cubic on every rational fiber of the second projection. Exhausting the resulting 256 candidates with exact four-chart Jacobian Gröbner bases gives 66 smooth masks. A second independently written SymPy computation scans all 2047 filling forms and reproduces every mask. Direct group-substitution enumeration verifies disjoint orbit sizes 18, 6, 36 and 6; exact extension-field point counts distinguish these four types. Each claim is supported by `verify_exact.py`, `verify_sympy.py`, `smooth_masks.txt`, and `exact_output.json`. A finite computation proves the finite classification because its search domain is exhaustive and algebraic closure is addressed by unit-ideal tests.

**Originality (PASS, publication priority qualified).** The closest original source, Homma and Kim, arXiv:2203.11595v1 (2022), determines minimum exceptional binary bidegrees and supplies smooth constructions, not this complete coefficient/orbit census. Ahammed, Campbell and Moon, arXiv:2512.02796v1 (2025), study related smooth filling families and other field-size enumerations without this specific classification. Published mathematical-record searches using the 66-count, bidegree, finite-field aliases, and automorphism-orbit parameters returned no statement implying the complete four-orbit classification; different bidegrees, tropical curve enumeration and different ambient spaces do not imply it. Searches cannot exclude inaccessible or unindexed older calculations.

**Value (PASS).** The complete embedded classification concerns the *smallest possible smooth filling bidegree* over the smallest field, not an arbitrary larger-degree slice. Moreover four rationally distinct genus-six arithmetic types and the obstruction to all-smooth pencils clarify the structure behind existence alone. Counting 66 is part of a motivated natural complete classification, not merely a computational benchmark.

**Residual limitations.** Unknown unindexed prior classifications might affect priority. Equivalence is under the 36-element bidegree-preserving group, not all abstract isomorphisms. Four point-count extensions distinguish the four projective types but do not determine their zeta functions. The two programs are reproducibility checks; no external independent audit was performed.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
