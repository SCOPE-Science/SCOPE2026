The binary split quadric has exactly 66 geometrically smooth bidegree-\((3,4)\) curves containing all nine rational points, in four embedded projective orbits of sizes 18, 6, 36 and 6. The four orbits represent pairwise nonisomorphic genus-six curves, distinguished by rational-point counts over extensions of degree at most four, and no pencil can have all three nonzero filling members smooth.
