For squarefree integers \(n\), the closure of the values \(\sigma_{-2}(n)\) is exactly four disjoint intervals: \([1,54/(5\pi^2)]\cup[10/9,12/\pi^2]\cup[5/4,27/(2\pi^2)]\cup[25/18,15/\pi^2]\). The primes at least five fill a complete logarithmic tail; the primes two and three split it into four components.
