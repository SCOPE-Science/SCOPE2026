For pairs of strictly upper-triangular \(4\times4\) matrices satisfying the one-sided relation \(AB=0\), the original defining scheme is reduced but has four rational irreducible components of dimensions \(10,9,9,9\). It is the first matrix size where this flagged zero-product scheme loses equidimensionality, and over every finite field its point count is \(q^{10}+3q^9-4q^8+q^7\).
