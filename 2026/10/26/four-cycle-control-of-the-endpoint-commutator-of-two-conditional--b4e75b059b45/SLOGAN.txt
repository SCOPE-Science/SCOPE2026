For two conditional expectations on the edges of any finite \(d\)-regular bipartite graph, the sharp \(L^1\) and \(L^\infty\) norms of their commutator depend only on the minimum number of four-cycles through an edge: \(2((d-1)^2-\min_e c_4(e))/d^2\). Projective-plane incidence graphs make these endpoint norms tend to \(2\) while the Hilbert norm tends to \(0\), exhibiting a precise, graph-controlled separation between endpoint and Hilbert stability.
