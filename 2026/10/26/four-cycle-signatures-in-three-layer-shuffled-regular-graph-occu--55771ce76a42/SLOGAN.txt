In a two-regular graph whose path piles are independently shuffled between layers, four-cycle components are exactly the graph motif seen by the variance of the number of empty vertices after two layers and by the expected empty fraction after three layers. The effect first distinguishes graphs at seven vertices, with an exact third-layer gap of \(2/441\) between \(C_7\) and \(C_3\sqcup C_4\).
