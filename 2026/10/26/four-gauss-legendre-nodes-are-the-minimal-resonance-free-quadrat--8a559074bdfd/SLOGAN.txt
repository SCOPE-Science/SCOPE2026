For the rank-one planar oscillator Gramian, the standard four-node Gauss–Legendre rule is the smallest Gaussian quadrature that is strictly positive definite for every positive step: the one-, two-, and three-node rules fail at explicitly classified resonant steps. Its exact positivity conceals an arithmetic conditioning defect: along infinitely many large undamped steps, the normalized least eigenvalue is at most \(\pi^2/q_k^2\) even though the normalized exact Gramian tends to \(I/2\).
