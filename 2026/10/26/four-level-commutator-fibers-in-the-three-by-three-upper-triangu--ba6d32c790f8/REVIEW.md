# Mathematical assessment

**Correctness: PASS.** Literal triangular-matrix multiplication gives the three commutator coordinates. For an arbitrary additive character, the Fourier transform of each target fiber is determined by the rank of a displayed five-by-five alternating matrix. The characteristic-independent rank trichotomy is proved by the disjoint two-by-two blocks at the central-zero dual stratum and a nonzero fourth-order minor otherwise. Fourier inversion then yields the complete four-level formula; the embedded verifier agrees with literal products over two prime fields and confirms pairing ranks over a binary extension field.

**Originality: PASS (best knowledge, residual priority risk).** The closest primary literature treats (i) commuting similarity classes of full matrix algebras, (ii) the zero-commutator variety of a nilradical in characteristic zero, and (iii) conjugacy branching in invertible triangular groups. These do not state or imply the full fixed-target additive commutator distribution for all upper triangular matrices. The closest public fixed-commutator enumeration treats radical-square-zero two-vertex Kronecker algebras, not this algebra with a surviving length-two product. Search results and inspected primary passages did not show the stated four-level formula. An older coadjoint-orbit computation might implicitly cover it; that remains a priority risk, not a claim of uniqueness.

**Value: PASS.** The fixed-size algebra is the first full triangular matrix algebra whose radical has a nonzero product of two arrow generators. The calculation answers the complete, natural commutator-pushforward problem at this structural boundary, not only the commuting probability. Its surprising uniformity in the central coordinate outside the zero-adjacent-entry locus, exact central spike, and four rank-controlled strata provide interpretable data for finite Lie-algebra and ring commutativity questions. The result is exact over every finite field, not a sample-only numerical fact.

**Closest literature and limits.** Britnell–Wildon, arXiv:1001.0811 (2010), §2; Goodwin–Röhrle, arXiv:1209.1289 (2012), Theorem 1.1 and §2; Kaur–Sharma–Singh, arXiv:2006.01493 (2020), Theorem 3.1 and its proof; and the public full-text record “Commutator fibers and commuting probabilities of finite Kronecker path algebras.” Exact historical priority has not been certified. The present conclusion is limited to the full associative upper triangular algebra of size three, not the invertible group or larger sizes.
