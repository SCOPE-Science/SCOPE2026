For the full algebra of upper triangular \(3\times3\) matrices over every finite field, the additive commutator map has exactly four fiber sizes, governed only by which of the two adjacent superdiagonal target entries vanish and whether the target is zero. Its exact count is \(q^5(q^2-1)^2+q^6(q^2-1)(\mathbf1_{s=0}+\mathbf1_{t=0})+q^8\mathbf1_{s=t=h=0}\), exhibiting an exceptional central spike amid an otherwise uniform central-coordinate distribution.
