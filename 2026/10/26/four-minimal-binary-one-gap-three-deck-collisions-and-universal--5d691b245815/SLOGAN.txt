Exactly four pairs of binary length-\(13\) words are confusable under the full one-gap three-deck; each pair can be substituted inside **arbitrary** surrounding words without changing that deck. This yields \(2^m\) indistinguishable words at length \(13m\), while the exact optimal length-\(13\) reconstruction code size is \(8188\), attained by \(16\) codebooks in \(6\) reversal/complement classes.
