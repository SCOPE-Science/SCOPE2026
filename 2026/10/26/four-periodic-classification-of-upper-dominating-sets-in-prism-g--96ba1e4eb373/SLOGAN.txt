Every circular prism \(C_n\square K_2\), for \(n\ge3\), has upper-domination number \(n\). Its maximum-cardinality minimal dominating sets are exactly the two whole layers, two alternating sets when \(n\) is even, and four period-four paired-rung sets when \(4\mid n\), giving counts \(2\), \(4\), or \(8\) according to \(n\bmod4\).
