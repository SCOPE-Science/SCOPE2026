Maximum dissociation sets of the squared cycle \(C_m^2\) exhibit a sharp four-residue exchange phase: four frozen optima for \(m\equiv0\pmod4\), a connected sun graph for \(m\equiv1\), a connected three-degree-stratum graph for \(m\equiv2\), and a single cycle for \(m\equiv3\). Explicit block decompositions give every optimum, its count, and the exact exchange-degree spectrum for all \(m\ge7\).
