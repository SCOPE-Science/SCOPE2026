# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The set of the first s distinct indices is uniform, and while that set does not recover a file the mean wait for the next new index is 4/(4-s). Summing over s=0,1,2,3 proves the exact subset formula. A zero column is strictly improvable for F1 and no worse for F2 by replacing it with e1 under the same draws. The unique relation on four nonzero columns has support 2, 3, or 4. With support 4, all triples are independent, at most two columns lie in F2, and two pairs meeting at a column not proportional to e1 cannot both span e1 without a dependent triple. With support 3, the dependent triple spans a plane: E1<3 is possible only if it contains a column in the e1 direction, in which case E1=8/3, the dependent triple does not recover F2 and at most one pair can recover F2, giving E2>=5. With support 2, E1<3 requires the parallel pair to lie along e1, giving E1=2 and E2>=6. Separately, E2<4 requires at least two recovering pairs and hence three F2 columns; the fourth must be outside F2, so the F2 triple fails to recover e1 and E1>=4. The universal E2 lower bound is 10/3. The four witnesses have recovery counts (a1,a2,a3;b2,b3) equal to (2,5,4;1,2), (1,4,4;1,3), (1,3,4;1,4), and (1,3,3;3,4); all ranks and directions persist in every characteristic. The points are pairwise incomparable and exhaust the lower boundary. The supplied script and output were inspected, not run: projective enumeration is over q=2,3, and witness checks additionally cover q=5,7. The all-field conclusion rests on the proof, not those finite checks.
