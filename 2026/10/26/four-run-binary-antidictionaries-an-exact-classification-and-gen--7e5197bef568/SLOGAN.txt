Every four-run binary word \(0^a1^b0^c1^d\) has exactly \(b+c+(1+\mathbf1_{a>c})(1+\mathbf1_{d>b})\) minimal absent contiguous factors; their complete list and the joint length/count generating function are explicit. Among four-run words of length \(n\), the maximum is \(n-1\) except for a unique (up to alphabet exchange) six-letter exception with \(n\) minimal absent factors.
