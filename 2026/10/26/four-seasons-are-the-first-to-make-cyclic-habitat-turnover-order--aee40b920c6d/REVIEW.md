# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. Every season map is a strict positive affine contraction, giving the unique periodically stationary occupancy. The change of clock and causal-convolution calculation give the nonnegative squared-difference identity without differentiating the seasonally discontinuous forcing. Reflection of this quadratic integral establishes reversal invariance. A direct four-step fixed-point expansion yields both gap identities with strictly positive shared prefactor. The worked example and additional exact rational checks reproduced the formulas. The proof applies to expectations of actual jumps, not to snapshots or finite-time transients.

## Originality
PASS within the stated finite-block turnover classification, with acknowledged risk. Brown and Kodric-Brown (1977), MacKenzie et al. (2003), Hines et al. (2014) and Yackulic et al. (2015) motivate and formulate extinction/colonization dynamics but the inspected sections or available abstracts do not supply the explicit three-to-four-season turnover ordering theorem. Rahav, Horowitz and Jarzynski (2008) analyze signed probability flow under periodic Markov forcing; zero net current does not determine unsigned turnover. A broader autocorrelation or rearrangement theorem could subsume the formulas, so search absence is not a proof of novelty. Publisher access limited complete inspection of some ecological sources; the closely relevant 2014 occupancy-model full text and 2008 Markov-pump full text were materially inspected.

## Mathematical value
PASS. The expected number of local extinction-and-recolonization events is a conservation-relevant quantity distinct from occupancy at census times. The exact onset at four seasonal blocks gives a natural minimal classification, and its strict optimal ordering and positive numerical gaps apply uniformly over all positive rates and durations. The nonnegative energy decomposition also separates unavoidable equilibrium turnover from extra turnover generated by switching. This is a motivated structural result rather than a selected table entry or parameter fit.

## Limits
The claim concerns a single-site two-state continuous-time Markov process with fixed piecewise-constant seasonal rates in its periodic state. It makes no assertion about correlated patches, imperfect detection, real-world management benefits, or independence of prior mathematical work. Exact tests sample finitely many rational parameter sets; the general proof is analytic. Broader linear-response and rearrangement results remain an originality risk.
