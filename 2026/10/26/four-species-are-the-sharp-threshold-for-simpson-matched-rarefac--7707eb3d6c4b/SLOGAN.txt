For iid species sampling, equal species count and equal Simpson diversity prevent every reversal of expected sample-richness rankings with at most three species, but four species already permit a strict reversal. The exact four-species vectors \((1,4,6,10)/21\) and \((2,2,8,9)/21\) reverse their expected-richness ordering between seven and eight draws, with no later reversal; the phenomenon extends to every larger species count.
