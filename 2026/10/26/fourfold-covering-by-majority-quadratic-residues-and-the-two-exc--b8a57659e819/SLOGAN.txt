Every strict-majority subset of the nonzero quadratic residues modulo a prime \(p\ge13\) has \(4A=\mathbb F_p\), and this uniform order is sharp already at \(p=13\). The only prime fields \(p\ge7\) requiring more are \(p=11\) (five sums) and \(p=7\) (six sums), with exact obstructions arising from short arithmetic progressions.
