Every complex algebra automorphism of an odd-order finite abelian core-quandle algebra is monomial in its Fourier basis, with an explicit finite group \(T\rtimes C_{\mathrm{Sym}(\widehat A)}(\langle2,-1\rangle)\). In particular, the \(11\)-point dihedral quandle algebra has \(330\) automorphisms, triple the \(110\) automorphisms of the underlying quandle.
