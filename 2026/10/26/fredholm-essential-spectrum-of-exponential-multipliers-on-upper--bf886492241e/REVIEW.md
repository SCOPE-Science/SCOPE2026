# Review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The multiplier bound follows from the Leibniz formula and bounded derivatives of \(e^{iaz}\). For \(|\lambda|>1\), the reciprocal multiplier is bounded. For nonzero \(|\lambda|<1\), the infinitely many zeros of \(e^{iaz}-\lambda\) give arbitrarily large independent evaluation constraints on the range, so the range cannot be closed with finite codimension. For \(|\lambda|=1\), normalized rational functions concentrated at a boundary phase point give approximate null vectors; the operator is injective, so it cannot be Fredholm. Closedness of the Fredholm essential spectrum includes zero.

## Originality

**PASS, with bounded-search limitations.** The closest recent source establishes the ordinary spectrum formula and compact-multiplier rigidity for \(H_n^p(\mathbb C^+)\), not the Fredholm essential spectrum of this symbol. The older essential-spectrum theorem is for Hilbert Hardy–Sobolev spaces on the ball. The current claim proves the explicit classification across the full Banach range. A more general theorem outside the searched sources remains a residual risk.

## Value

**PASS.** The finding supplies an exact Fredholm classification for a natural exponential inner multiplier on a newly developed Banach Hardy–Sobolev scale. The distinction between ordinary spectrum and Fredholm essential spectrum is substantive: the proof establishes infinite codimension throughout the punctured disk and non-Fredholmness on the boundary, including the endpoint Banach exponents.
