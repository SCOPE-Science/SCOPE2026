# Review of free class-two Lie retractions

**Correctness: PASS.** Every Lie endomorphism has unique generator blocks \((A,B)\) and central action \(\bigwedge^2 A\). Idempotence is equivalent to \(A^2=A\) and \(BA+(\bigwedge^2A)B=B\). The central-shear conjugacy explicitly solves this equation, and complement counting yields the orbit and distinct-retract counts. The cubic saddle estimate is algebraically exact and yields exponential concentration. Direct finite-field enumeration of all generator images in four independent small cases confirms the formulas and complete image fibres.

**Originality: PASS against inspected sources, with residual risk.** Benito–Roldán-López (arXiv:1911.01897) and Benito–de-la-Concepción–Laliena (arXiv:1604.02923) provide the free-nilpotent homomorphism and automorphism machinery. Cagliero–Gutierrez–Szechtman (arXiv:1703.06214) treats representations. The inspected results do not provide the complete finite-field idempotent Lie-endomorphism orbit-size polynomial, retract-image counts, or the two-rank law. Related fixed-space and involutive automorphism spectra concern invertible maps; the Heisenberg group retract spectrum has a different domain. A more general conjugacy theorem could cover part of the structural claim, so global novelty is not asserted.

**Value: PASS.** Idempotent endomorphisms and retracts are canonical structural invariants of free Lie algebras. The result gives their full conjugacy and image census in every rank and a sharp, quantitatively bounded, non-Gaussian concentration at a nontrivial fraction \((1+\sqrt7)/6\) of the free generators. This goes beyond merely rephrasing the universal mapping property.

**Limits.** The theorem does not cover group endomorphisms, arbitrary additive maps, higher nilpotency classes or the joint large-field limit. Exhaustive literature priority and independent replication remain open.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
