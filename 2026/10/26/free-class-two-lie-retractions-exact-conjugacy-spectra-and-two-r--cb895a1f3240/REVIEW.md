# Scope of the result

The exact rank enumerator and earlier conjugacy classification are background. The exponential upper bound localizes the random quotient rank to two adjacent integers. The additional assertion is that neither integer can be omitted on an explicit infinite sequence: two consecutive exponents of the known enumerator are equal, and their residual Gaussian factors are asymptotically equal. The norm equation and congruence in the proof establish this sequence without relying on numerical evidence.

The conclusion concerns labeled Lie retractions at fixed field order; it is not a concentration statement for uniformly chosen image subalgebras. No assertion of optimal numerical constants in the upper bound or of universal publication priority is made.
