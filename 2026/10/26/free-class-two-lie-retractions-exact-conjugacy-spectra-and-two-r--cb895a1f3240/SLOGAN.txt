For the free class-two Lie algebra on \(n\ge3\) generators over \(\mathbb F_q\), idempotent Lie endomorphisms have exactly \(n+1\) conjugacy types and explicitly counted retract images and fibres. The generator rank of a uniformly random retraction concentrates exponentially on the two integers adjacent to \((n-2+\sqrt{7n^2+8n+4})/6\).
