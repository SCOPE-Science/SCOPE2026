The free subgroups of the diagonal group on every smooth Calabi–Yau generalized Fermat complete intersection are completely determined: composite degree allows none, while prime degree \(p\) permits precisely balanced full-character arrays, with maximum rank \(1+v_p(t)\) and an exact factorial counting formula. In particular, intersections of three cubics in \(\mathbb P^8\) carry exactly \(840\) labelled free diagonal \((\mathbb Z/3)^2\) subgroups, yielding smooth quotients with this fundamental group and Euler number \(-1080\).
