The \(325\) lines on the Fermat quintic over \(\mathbb F_2\) form an étale Fano scheme with Frobenius orbit lengths \(1^3,2^1,4^{80}\). Its \(80\) four-line Galois orbits split by intersection type into \(48\) empty graphs, \(14\) matchings, \(12\) four-cycles, and \(6\) four-cliques; the four-cliques occur only among the \(75\) classical coordinate-pair lines, while the nontrivial matching and cycle types occur only among the \(250\) exceptional Hermitian lines.
