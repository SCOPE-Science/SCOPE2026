The four genus-six Artin–Schreier fibre products over \(\mathbb F_4\) have exactly three rational Frobenius types, built from cyclotomic orders \(4\), \(12\), and \(5\). Their Jacobians first become isogenous in the pairwise extension degrees \(3\), \(20\), and \(60\), even though the nonzero-parameter curves share one geometric Ekedahl–Oort type.
