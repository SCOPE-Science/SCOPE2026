Over \(\mathbf F_5\), the Klein quartic's 28 bitangents form Frobenius orbits of lengths \(1,3,6,6,6,6\). Its unique rational bitangent has a conjugate quadratic contact pair, while the other 27 bitangents already split their contact points over each line's field of definition; the full configuration becomes rational over \(\mathbf F_{5^6}\).
