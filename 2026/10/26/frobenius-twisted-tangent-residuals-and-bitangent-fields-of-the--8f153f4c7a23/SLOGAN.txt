For the characteristic-three cyclic Klein quartic \(X^3Y+Y^3Z+Z^3X=0\), every tangent meets the curve in \(3P+\Phi(P)\), where \(\Phi([X:Y:Z])=[Y^9:Z^9:X^9]\). Its \(28\) bitangents are exactly the hyperflex tangents, with Frobenius orbit degrees \(1,3,6,6,6,6\), so their minimal fields of definition and finite-scheme zeta function are completely determined.
