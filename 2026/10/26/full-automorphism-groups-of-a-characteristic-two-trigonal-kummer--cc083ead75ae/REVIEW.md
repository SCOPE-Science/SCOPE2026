## correctness

PASS

The degree-three Kummer extension is nontrivial because the valuation of f at 0 is -M, not divisible by three. In characteristic two f=(x^M+1)^2/x^M; since M is odd its roots are distinct, and the M zeros and two poles are exactly the tame totally ramified points. Riemann–Hurwitz gives genus M. Any other degree-three rational subfield together with K(x) generates the full field: containment would force equality by the equality of degrees, and a proper intermediate extension would contradict prime degree. The resulting map is birational onto a (3,3) curve of arithmetic genus 4, impossible even at M=5. Every automorphism therefore descends to PGL2 with kernel C3, and conjugation of the deck generator gives sigma(y)=u(x)y^s, s=1 or 2, by its three eigenspaces. Modulo three the branch residues are 1 at the poles (population 2) and 2 at the roots (population M); s=2 would swap unequal populations. Hence the descended map preserves {0,infinity} and the roots of unity separately. These conditions impose x -> cx or c/x with c^M=1, all of which fix f and lift fixing y. They form an order-2M complement centralizing the deck group; the coprime rotation factors yield the normal index-two C_(3M). This proves the full group and genus uniformly, including order 30 at M=5. No finite sample or formal verification is needed for this proof; the supplied supplementary checker was only inspected.
