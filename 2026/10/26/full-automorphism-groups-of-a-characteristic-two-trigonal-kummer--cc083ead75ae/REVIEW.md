# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The genus computation follows from a tame degree-three Kummer cover with \(M+2\) fully ramified points. Two distinct degree-three rational subfields would give a birational \((3,3)\) curve in \(\mathbf P^1\times\mathbf P^1\) with arithmetic genus four, contradicting \(g=M\ge5\). The unique pencil forces preservation of the cyclic deck subgroup; Kummer exponent multiplicities \(2\) versus \(M\) force the base action to preserve \(\{0,\infty\}\) and \(\mu_M\) separately. All such base maps and lifts are exhibited, completing both inequalities for the automorphism order. The exact arithmetic/permutation checker is supplementary, not the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS (qualified by residual risk)
The closest source is Timpanella, arXiv:2609.34709v1, Theorem 1.2, Example 4.7, Proposition 6.6 and Remark 6.7. Its published implication “normality of the cyclic subgroup if and only if the exhibited group is full” does not establish the needed normality, and Remark 6.7 explicitly names the genus-five test case as unresolved. A separate source, arXiv:2607.15983, discusses cyclic-index-two groups but not this characteristic-two full-group determination. Searches for exact curves, alternative equations, cyclic deck normalizers, and published result records did not identify covering statements. Residual risk: an unindexed broader automorphism classification may imply this assertion; failed searches do not prove uniqueness.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This settles the first concrete full-automorphism question singled out in the primary paper, and does so for an infinite arithmetic progression of genera rather than one isolated curve. Determining the full group, not just exhibiting a subgroup, identifies normality of the genus-dependent large cyclic subgroup and restricts all geometric symmetries of the curves. The genus restriction and exceptional residue regime are mathematically necessary for the present method.

## Limits and independence
The assertion is for algebraically closed characteristic-two fields and odd \(M\ge5\) congruent to two modulo three. Other parameters are untreated. Independent audit is not claimed.
