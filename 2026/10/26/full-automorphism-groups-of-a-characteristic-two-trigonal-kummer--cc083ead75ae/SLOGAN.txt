For every odd \(M\ge5\) with \(M\equiv2\pmod3\) in characteristic two, the trigonal curve \(y^3=x^M+x^{-M}\) has full geometric automorphism group \(C_3\times D_{2M}\), of order \(6M\). In particular, the previously unresolved genus-five case has exactly \(30\) automorphisms: uniqueness of its trigonal map and an asymmetry of Kummer branch residues exclude all extras.
