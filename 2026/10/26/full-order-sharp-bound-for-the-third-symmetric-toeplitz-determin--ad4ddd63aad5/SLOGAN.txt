The known sharp third symmetric Toeplitz determinant bound for starlike mappings of order \(\alpha\), previously proved only for \(0\le\alpha\le2/3\), remains sharp for the entire interval \(0\le\alpha<1\). A uniform two-factor Schur estimate proves the original formula without a phase transition, and equality in one variable occurs only for the two explicit rotations \(\zeta/(1\mp i\zeta)^{2(1-\alpha)}\).
