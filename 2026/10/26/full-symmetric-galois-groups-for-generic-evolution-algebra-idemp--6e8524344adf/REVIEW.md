# Review: full symmetric Galois groups for generic evolution-algebra idempotents

**Correctness — PASS.** In a perfect sparse family the idempotent equations reduce exactly to the \(N=2^n-1\)-degree trinomial \(T^N+aT^M-1\), \(M=2^{n-1}-1\). As \(N=2M+1\), the associated rational map has \(N\) distinct simple finite branch points and only one further branch value, infinity. The monodromy of every finite simple branch value is a transposition. The infinity loop is a product of those finite loops. Connectedness plus transposition generation forces \(S_N\). The finite étale generic idempotent cover restricts to this family, so its monodromy contains \(S_N\) and is equal to it. The Bézout count, zero-section multiplicity, critical-point derivatives, and specialization all have explicit proofs. Exact finite-field checks give additional evidence in dimension three, but do not substitute for those proofs.

**Originality — PASS (self-review).** The closest primary source, Kaygorodov–Martín González–Viruel, arXiv:2610.22248v1, Section 2.3, Conjecture 2, asks for this full symmetric Galois group and states that only dimension two was proved; its existing idempotent count does not imply the group. García-Martínez–Pérez-Rodríguez (2026), Sections 2 and 3.3, establish existence and subalgebra implications from quadratic systems but no transposition/monodromy group. Searches of publicly indexed mathematical results, plus recorded semantic mathematical-record comparisons, did not identify coverage of the all-dimensional sparse trinomial family or of the generic full symmetric group. General rational-map monodromy theory could hide implicit coverage and remains a residual risk; absence of a search hit does not prove novelty.

**Value — PASS.** The result resolves a precisely formulated conjecture across the entire infinite range of dimensions, supplies an explicit perfect witness family, and identifies the mechanism behind the predicted fixed-point statistics. This is not merely a reformulation of a known idempotent count, because the latter does not control permutation monodromy.

**Scientific limitations.** Characteristic zero, generic coefficients, and the stated perfect family only; no claim that all specializations retain maximal Galois group. The dimension-three modular verification is finite, while the general proof is geometric. Earlier implicit work remains possible.
