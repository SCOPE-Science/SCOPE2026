For every dimension \(n\ge2\), the \(2^n-1\) nonzero idempotents of the generic evolution algebra have the full symmetric Galois group \(S_{2^n-1}\). A perfect one-parameter family already realizes this group via the trinomial \(T^{2^n-1}+aT^{2^{n-1}-1}-1\), whose simple finite branch points force full symmetric monodromy.
