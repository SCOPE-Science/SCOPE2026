Parameterizing a=t^{-M}-t^{N-M} gives a degree-N connected branched rational cover. Its N finite simple critical points satisfy t^N=-M/(N-M) and have distinct critical values a=N/(N-M)*t^{-M}, by coprimality. Each finite inertia is one transposition; the infinity inertia lies in their generated subgroup by the sphere fundamental group relation. Thus the entire transitive monodromy is generated by transpositions and hence is S_N.
