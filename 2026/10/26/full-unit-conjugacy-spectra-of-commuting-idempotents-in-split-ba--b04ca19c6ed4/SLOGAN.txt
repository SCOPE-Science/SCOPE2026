For every finite split basic algebra, the complete full-unit conjugacy class-size spectrum of ordered commuting-idempotent tuples is a Potts coloring polynomial of its symmetrized off-diagonal Peirce dimensions, independent of radical multiplication. Distinct two-cycle truncated path algebras with Loewy lengths \(2s\) and \(2s+1\) have identical spectra in every tuple arity over every finite field.
