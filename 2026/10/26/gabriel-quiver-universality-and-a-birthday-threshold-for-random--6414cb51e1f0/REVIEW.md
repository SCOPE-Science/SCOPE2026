# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The reduction from \(A\) to \(A/J^2\) follows by nilpotence and the inclusion \(J=(B\cap J)+J^2\). The square-zero split basic model separates independent vertex labels and independent bimodule blocks. Distinct vertex labels give full semisimple generation; conjugacy of complements removes exactly one generator direction for every nonloop arrow space, while loops retain all directions. Independent full-rank finite-field matrices yield the exact product. The birthday result follows from the birthday logarithm expansion and a uniform union bound on sparse bounded-multiplicity arrow spaces. Thirteen exhaustive small cases, including a relation-changing pair, reproduce the formula; finite tests are corroboration, not infinite proof.

## Value — PASS

Random generator probabilities are natural quantitative invariants of finite algebras. The result gives every generator-number probability for an infinite relation-rich class, an exact minimum-generator threshold, and a new universal asymptotic for sparse quivers. The distinction between loop constraints (\(r\) samples) and nonloop constraints (\(r-1\) effective samples) encodes a genuine structural bimodule phenomenon. Relation invariance makes the formula reusable across nonisomorphic finite-dimensional quotients without repeating enumerations.
