# Galois fields of definition of the 256 nodes on the magic-square surface

## Finding

Let \(V\subset\mathbb P^8_{\mathbb Q}\) be the surface parameterizing \(3\times3\) magic squares of squares in Auel–Singer. Its 256 geometric nodes split under \(\mathrm{Gal}(\mathbb Q(\sqrt2,i)/\mathbb Q)\cong C_2\times C_2\) into exactly 112 Galois orbits: 96 orbits of size two and 16 orbits of size four. More precisely, the 128-point geometric-automorphism orbit represented by \(p_1\) gives 64 pairs with field of definition \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)\); the 64-point orbit represented by \(p_3\) gives 32 pairs with field of definition \(\mathbb Q(i)\); and the 64-point orbit represented by \(p_2\) gives 16 quartets with field of definition \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt2,i)\). Consequently, none of the 256 nodes is \(\mathbb Q\)-rational.

## Assumptions and scope

Work over \(\mathbb Q\). Let \(K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt2,i)\), and write \(\sigma_2\) for the automorphism changing the sign of \(\sqrt2\), and \(\sigma_i\) for complex conjugation. The nodes and their three geometric-automorphism orbits are those explicitly listed in Auel–Singer, arXiv:2609.09351v1, §1.2–§1.3. Coordinates below are in the order \((A,B,C,D,M,E,F,G,H)\). The claim concerns the fields of definition and Galois orbits of the nodes only; it does not determine the full Galois representation on the Picard group.

## Proof

Auel–Singer give representatives
\[
\begin{aligned}
p_1&=[0:2:\sqrt2:2:\sqrt2:0:\sqrt2:0:2],\\
p_2&=[i:0:1:\sqrt2:0:i\sqrt2:i:0:1],\\
p_3&=[i:1:0:1:0:i:0:i:1],
\end{aligned}
\]
for the three geometric-automorphism orbits, of sizes 128, 64, and 64 respectively. Their geometric automorphism group is generated by rational coordinate sign changes (modulo simultaneous sign change) and rational symmetries of the square. Thus its action commutes with the Galois action.

For \(p_1\), \(\sigma_i\) fixes the representative, while \(\sigma_2\) changes the signs of the three nonzero-coordinate positions \(C,M,F\). That is a nontrivial projective change, so \(\sigma_2\) has no fixed point in this transitive automorphism orbit. Equivariance implies that \(\sigma_i\) fixes every point of the orbit and \(\sigma_2\) pairs its points. Hence this orbit contributes \(128/2=64\) Galois orbits, each with field of definition \(K^{\langle\sigma_i\rangle}=\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)\).

For \(p_2\), the three nonidentity elements \(\sigma_i\), \(\sigma_2\), and \(\sigma_i\sigma_2\) change signs respectively in positions \((A,E,F)\), \((D,E)\), and \((A,D,F)\). Each changes a nonempty proper subset of its six nonzero coordinates, so none fixes \(p_2\) projectively. Equivariance implies that none fixes any point in its transitive automorphism orbit. The Galois action on this 64-point orbit is therefore free, giving \(64/4=16\) orbits of size four, with field of definition \(K\).

For \(p_3\), \(\sigma_2\) fixes the representative, whereas \(\sigma_i\) changes the signs in positions \((A,E,G)\), a nontrivial projective change. Thus \(\sigma_2\) fixes the whole 64-point orbit and \(\sigma_i\) pairs its points. This contributes \(64/2=32\) Galois orbits with field of definition \(K^{\langle\sigma_2\rangle}=\mathbb Q(i)\).

The total is \(64+16+32=112\) Galois orbits. Their points account for \(64\cdot2+16\cdot4+32\cdot2=256\) nodes. Every orbit has size greater than one, so no node is rational over \(\mathbb Q\). The proof uses only the listed representatives, the stated automorphism-orbit sizes, and the fact that the coordinate symmetries are defined over \(\mathbb Q\).

## Verification

The three representative vectors were checked against the coordinate order and the explicit representatives in §1.3 of arXiv:2609.09351v1. Each Galois conjugation was evaluated coordinatewise. The stabilizer orders in the four-element Galois group are 2, 1, and 2 on the \(p_1,p_2,p_3\) automorphism orbits, respectively; dividing the published orbit sizes by the corresponding Galois orbit sizes gives 64, 16, and 32. The counts sum to 256, and the degree-weighted count is \(128+64+64=256\).

## Relationship to prior work

Auel–Singer identify all 256 nodes, give representatives for the three geometric-automorphism orbits, state their sizes 128, 64, and 64, and place the nodes over \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt2,i)\). They do not state the orbit decomposition under the Galois group or the distribution of the individual fields of definition. The result here refines geometric-automorphism orbits to arithmetic Galois orbits. The comparison is specifically about the 256 singular nodes; geometric orbit information for other curves on the surface does not determine these fields of definition.

## Limitations

This calculation is an explicit Galois-orbit census of the 256 nodes, not a determination of the full Galois module on the rank-518 Picard sublattice. It gives no Brauer–Manin obstruction and does not settle the rational or integral magic-square problem. The independent audit has not been performed.
