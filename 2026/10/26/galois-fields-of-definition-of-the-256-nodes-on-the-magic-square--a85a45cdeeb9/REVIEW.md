# Review: Galois fields of definition of the 256 nodes on the magic-square surface

## Correctness

**PASS.** The coordinatewise action of the four elements of \(\mathrm{Gal}(\mathbb Q(\sqrt2,i)/\mathbb Q)\) gives stabilizer orders 2, 1, and 2 on the three geometric-automorphism orbits. Orbit-stabilizer yields 64, 16, and 32 Galois orbits, accounting for all 256 nodes. The result is limited to the listed nodes and does not assert anything about rational points on the open distinct-entry locus.

## Originality

**PASS.** The primary source gives the geometric-automorphism orbit sizes and representatives but does not state the Galois-orbit counts or the smaller fields of definition. The new calculation refines the geometric orbit partition arithmetically. It concerns the fields of definition of the exceptional nodes, not orbit data for other curves on the surface.

## Value

**PASS.** The exact field-of-definition distribution is a concrete input for studying the Galois action on exceptional divisor classes. It does not determine the full Picard-lattice Galois representation or produce a Brauer–Manin obstruction.
