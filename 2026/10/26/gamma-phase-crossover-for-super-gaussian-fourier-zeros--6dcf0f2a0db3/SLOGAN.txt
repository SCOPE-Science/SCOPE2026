For \(e^{-|t|^p}\) as \(p\to\infty\), the first linearly many positive Fourier zeros admit a uniform gamma-phase quantization law, with an explicit second-order correction. A unique universal phase value \(s_*=1.805547\ldots\) reverses their displacement relative to the sinc zeros; a separate fixed-index expansion reveals a sharp \(\zeta(3)\) skewness correction.
