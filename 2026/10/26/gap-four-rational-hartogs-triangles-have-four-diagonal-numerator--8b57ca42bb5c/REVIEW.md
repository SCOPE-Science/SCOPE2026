# Review

## Correctness

The claim is restricted to odd integers \(n\ge5\), with \(m=n+4\). The diagonal numerator coefficients are listed explicitly in the result. After the substitution \(x=s+s^{-1}\), absence of unit-circle roots reduces to positivity of a quartic on \([-2,2]\). Its Bernstein coefficients on both halves of the interval are explicitly positive for every integer parameter \(k\ge1\). Palindromicity and nonvanishing on the unit circle give exactly four roots in the disk, counted with multiplicity. A nonzero disk root yields a kernel zero because the denominator is nonzero and the product coordinates are realizable by points in the domain.

## Value

This is a complete exact root-count statement for a natural infinite arithmetic family of rational Hartogs triangles, not a single numerical example. It supplies a symbolic positivity certificate for the diagonal numerator and a reproducible path from that root count to kernel-zero existence. It does not claim a general solution of the Lu Qi-Keng problem.
