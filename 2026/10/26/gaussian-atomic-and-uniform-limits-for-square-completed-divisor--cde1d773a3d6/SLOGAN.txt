For the divisor ratio evaluated at the next perfect square, the normalized logarithm, its exact squareful-prime correction, and the fractional square-completion gap converge jointly to independent Gaussian, discrete-Euler, and uniform laws. The discrete correction has probability \(6/\pi^2\) of being zero and probability \((6/\pi^2)\sum_p1/(p(p+1))\) of taking its smallest positive value \(\log(5/3)\).
