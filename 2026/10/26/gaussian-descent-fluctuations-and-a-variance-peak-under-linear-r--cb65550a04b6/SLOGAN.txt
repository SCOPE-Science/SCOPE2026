For record-biased permutations with \(\theta_n/n\to\lambda>0\), the number of descents has a Gaussian limit with variance per element \((6\lambda^2+4\lambda+1)/(12(1+\lambda)^3)\). This fluctuation coefficient rises to a unique maximum at \(\lambda=(2+\sqrt{10})/6\) before falling, even though the mean descent density decreases with bias.
