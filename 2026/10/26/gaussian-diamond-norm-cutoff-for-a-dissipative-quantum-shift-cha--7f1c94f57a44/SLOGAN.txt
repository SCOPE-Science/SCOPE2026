The dissipative qubit shift channel has a sharp operational mixing cutoff: its diamond-norm distance to complete depolarization converges to the Gaussian tail \(\mathsf G(-s)\) at cycle times \(t=(N+s\sqrt{N(1-g)})/g\). A binomial erasure coupling supplies an ancilla-uniform upper bound, while an explicit product-state measurement matches it, revealing linear-in-size memory persistence despite size-independent nonstationary Floquet multipliers.
