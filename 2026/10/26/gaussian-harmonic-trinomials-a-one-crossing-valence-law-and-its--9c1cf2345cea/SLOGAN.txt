A monic Gaussian harmonic trinomial \(z^n+sA\overline z+B\) switches from \(n\) to \(n+2\) zeros at a single random threshold with an exact hypocycloid–Gaussian moment law. Its maximal-valence probability tends to \(s^2/(1+s^2)\) as the degree grows, with the sharp negative correction \(-s^2[2\log n+\gamma+\log(1+s^{-2})]/[n(1+s^2)^2]\).
