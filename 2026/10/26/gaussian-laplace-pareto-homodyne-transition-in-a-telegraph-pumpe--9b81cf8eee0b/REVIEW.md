## Mathematical value — PASS

The theorem distinguishes Gaussian, exponential and power-law measurement regimes, and reveals that a normalizable stationary optical state may have divergent energy and separately divergent two-photon factorial statistics. These exact thresholds help interpret stochastic parametric pumping and the limits of deterministic-threshold intuition. Assumptions of a linear undepleted cavity, fixed pump phase and intracavity single-time measurement are essential.
