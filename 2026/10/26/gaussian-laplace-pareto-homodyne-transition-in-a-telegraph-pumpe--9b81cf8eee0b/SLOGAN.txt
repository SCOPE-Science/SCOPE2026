With a randomly switched on/off pump, a Gaussian parametric cavity develops an exact Gaussian-to-exponential-to-Pareto transition in its *unconditioned intracavity homodyne tails*. The stationary Pareto exponent is \(2[b/(h-1)-a]\), and a proper stationary optical state can have infinite mean photon number, with a separate sharp threshold for its second factorial photon moment.
