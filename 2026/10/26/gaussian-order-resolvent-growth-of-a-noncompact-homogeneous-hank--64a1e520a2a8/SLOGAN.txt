For the noncompact quasinilpotent integral Hankel operator with kernel \((x+y)^{-1+ia}\), every power norm has an explicit hyperbolic-cosine product and decays like \(\exp(-\pi|a|n^2/4+O_a(n))\). Its resolvent nevertheless has a precisely quantified essential singularity: \(\log\|(\lambda I-H_a)^{-1}\|\sim(\log(1/|\lambda|))^2/(\pi|a|)\), uniformly in the argument of \(\lambda\).
