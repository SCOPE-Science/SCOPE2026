A single Gaussian pair-sum law can hide an arbitrary mixing law \(\rho\) in an infinite exchangeable sequence: \(X_i+X_j\sim N(0,1)\) for every \(i\ne j\), while nondegenerate \(\rho\) makes the process non-Gaussian. Within this family, the pair-difference characteristic function \(\int e^{-vt^2}\rho(dv)\) recovers \(\rho\) exactly.
