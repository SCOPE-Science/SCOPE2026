For cycle graphical groups at odd primes, the vertex-support size of a uniformly chosen abelian subgroup lying exactly two dimensions above the commutator is asymptotically Gaussian, with explicit density \(\mu_p=\tfrac12+\frac{p-2}{2\sqrt{5p^2-4p}}\) and variance coefficient \(p(p^2-1)/(5p^2-4p)^{3/2}\). An exact two-state transfer formula counts every support size, and the density approaches \((1+1/\sqrt5)/2\) as the fixed field order subsequently grows.
