Every general-position set of the rook graph \(K_p\square K_q\) is exactly a bipartite star forest, giving \(\Psi_{p,q}(z)=p!q![x^py^q]\exp(xe^{zy}+ye^{zx}-xyz)\). Inclusion-maximal sets have a complete three-family classification and cardinality spectrum \(\{p,q\}\cup\{p+q-k:2\le k\le\min(p,q)\}\); in particular, two-row rook graphs have arbitrarily long gaps between achievable maximal-set sizes.
