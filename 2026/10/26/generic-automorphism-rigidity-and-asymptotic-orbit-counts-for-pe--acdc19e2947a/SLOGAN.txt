For every fixed finite field \(\mathbb F_q\) with \(q\ge3\), almost every high-dimensional perfect evolution algebra has a trivial automorphism group, both when sampling invertible structure matrices and when sampling isomorphism classes. The number of isomorphism classes is asymptotic to \(|\operatorname{GL}_n(\mathbb F_q)|/(n!(q-1)^n)\), with an explicit exponentially decreasing relative-error bound.
