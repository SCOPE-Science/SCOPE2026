For a generic four-branch cyclic degree-\(p\) cover of \(\mathbb P^1\), the normalizer of its deck group is exactly \(C_p\), \(D_p\), or \(D_p\times C_2\); the last two types occur in exactly \(\lfloor(p-1)/4\rfloor\) and one topological classes respectively. The total number of action classes was previously known; the new information is the distribution by generic geometric deck-normalizer type.
