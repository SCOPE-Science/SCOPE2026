For every odd \(r\ge3\) in characteristic two, the conormal map of the binary Segre variety \((\mathbb P^1)^r\) has purely inseparable degree exactly \(2\): a general tangent hyperplane has a unique contact point with scheme length two. This quantifies the classical nonreflexivity theorem, and for \(r=3\) the reduced dual is nonetheless a smooth quadric.
