For every fixed finite field \(\mathbb F_q\), a uniformly random perfect evolution algebra of growing dimension is almost surely automorphism-rigid, with nonrigidity probability \(O_q(n^2(1-q^{-1})^n)\). Hence its number of isomorphism classes is asymptotic to \(|\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb F_q)|/[n!(q-1)^n]\), with the same exponential relative-error bound.
