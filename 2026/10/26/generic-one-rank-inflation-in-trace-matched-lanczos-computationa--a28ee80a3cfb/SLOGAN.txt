For every nonterminating Lanczos step with at least two unresolved directions, the rank-one-boundary-plus-trace-matched-isotropic approximation gives its computational-uncertainty covariance rank \(d-m+1\), while the exact Gaussian-conditioning covariance has rank \(d-m\). The resulting Gaussian posteriors are mutually singular even when the covariance matrix error approaches zero, and a \(3\times3\) exact-rational example assigns variance \(4/25\) to an already determined observation.
