For the ecological Lotka–Volterra model with hard triadic interactions, a single completely observed, unperturbed positive trajectory generically identifies all pairwise and triadic coefficients from \(1+n+\binom{n-1}{2}\) nearby exact growth-rate snapshots. Identifiability fails exactly when a permitted nonzero growth polynomial vanishes along that trajectory, and a globally positive nonstationary three-species example shows that this failure can hide a genuine triadic interaction perfectly.
