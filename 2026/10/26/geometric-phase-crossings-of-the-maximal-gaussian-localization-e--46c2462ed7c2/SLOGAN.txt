For every integer \(m\ge0\), sufficiently small equal disks at the vertices of an equilateral triangle admit a nearby exact, transverse double *maximal* Gaussian localization eigenvalue. The limiting triangle radius is \(R_m=\sqrt{2(2m+1)\pi/(3\sqrt3)}\); infinitely many such resonances survive from a coherent-state Gram matrix to genuine infinite-dimensional localization operators.
