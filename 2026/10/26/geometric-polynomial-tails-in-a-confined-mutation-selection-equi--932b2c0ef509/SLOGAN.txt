For a symmetric discrete-Laplace mutation kernel and symmetric mortality growing like \(c|i|^\alpha\) with \(\alpha>1\), the equilibrium trait mass has the sharp tail \(\phi_{\pm n}\sim K\theta^n n^{-\alpha}\). The exact mass omitted beyond cutoff \(I\) is asymptotic to \(2K\theta^{I+1}(I+1)^{-\alpha}/(1-\theta)\), and the critical exponential-moment rate is \(-\log\theta\).
