For the normalized lossy saturable optical dimer with \(0<\Gamma<J\), precisely four nonlinear eigenmodes occur in a sharply bounded detuning interval if and only if \(g>9\sqrt{J^2-\Gamma^2}/2\); otherwise there are always two. The interval opens with an exact \((g-g_c)^{3/2}\) cusp, despite the absence of any underlying linear exceptional point.
