For the free class-two Lie algebra on three generators over every finite field, the complete joint distribution of automorphism fixed-space dimensions on the abelianization and commutator ideal is given by Gaussian-binomial inversion. The resulting exact fixed-point-free count has a characteristic-parity correction, and unlike the two-generator Heisenberg case, fixed-point-free automorphisms already exist over \(\mathbb F_2\).
