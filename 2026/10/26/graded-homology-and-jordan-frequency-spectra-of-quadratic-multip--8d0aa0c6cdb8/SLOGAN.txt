For homogeneous quadratics in every characteristic-two squarefree complete intersection, the alternating rank \(2r\) determines the entire graded homology \((2t)^r(1+t)^{n-2r}\) and Jordan spectrum, with exact Gaussian-binomial frequencies over every even finite field. Moreover, whenever the quadratic is nonzero its annihilator modulo its principal ideal is a square-zero algebra.
