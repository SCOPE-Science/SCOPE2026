In a damped harmonic graph driven by spatially correlated active noise, the unique commuting noise covariance proportional to \((1+\gamma\tau)I+\tau^2L\) makes every stationary velocity independent even as active power stays positive and the positional and kinetic Gaussian temperatures differ. On a periodic ring, one exact admissible nearest-neighbor anticorrelation threshold reverses the stationary velocity-covariance sign of every pair of distinct particles simultaneously.
