For every finite quiver, the exact number of nonzero rank-one weight-zero Rota–Baxter operators on its radical-square-zero algebra splits into a Tutte-polynomial evaluation of the separated-support graph and a directed-cut partition function. For linearly oriented paths this yields a closed recurrence at every finite-field order, and reversing one arrow in the three-vertex path changes the count by exactly \(2q^2(q-1)^2\).
