For positive beta densities with total shape \(0<s<2\), every complex zero of their Laplace transform \({}_1F_1(a;s;z)\) lies strictly to the right when \(a<s/2\) and strictly to the left when \(a>s/2\); only the symmetric transform has imaginary-axis zeros. A parameter-uniform high-frequency argument rules out finite exceptional zeros beyond the known large-parameter half-plane theorem.
