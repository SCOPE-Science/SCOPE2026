For a general complete embedding of any genus-\(g\ge3\) hyperelliptic curve in \(\mathbb P^4\), the normal bundle has exactly three Harder–Narasimhan line-quotient degrees \(4g+4\), \(2g+6\), and \(g+8\). The scroll line is the unique maximal destabilizing subbundle, while a canonical intermediate line comes from the cubic-scroll normal quotient.
