For the full Haar-orthogonal central-difference gradient estimator at a cubic stationary point, the orientation variance is exactly \(\delta^4\|H\|_F^2/[6(n+2)(n+4)]\), so it vanishes precisely when the cubic's trace-free harmonic component is zero. On unit rank-one cubic perturbations, random orthogonal frames beat fixed coordinates in mean-square error exactly when \(\sum_i a_i^6>15/[(n+2)(n+4)]\); both rankings occur at the unique minimizer of globally strongly convex objectives.
