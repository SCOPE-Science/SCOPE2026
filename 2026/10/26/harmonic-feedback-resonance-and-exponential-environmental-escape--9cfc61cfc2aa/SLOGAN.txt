The three edge environments of a two-community evolutionary game satisfy an exact affine log-odds law whose drift vanishes precisely on the harmonic-feedback resonance \(2/(1+\theta_{12})=1/(1+\theta_1)+1/(1+\theta_2)\). Off resonance every trajectory approaches a specified union of environmental boundary faces at an explicit exponential rate, ruling out all compact fully interior recurrent states; on resonance an explicit admissible game realizes a continuum of asymmetric interior equilibria.
