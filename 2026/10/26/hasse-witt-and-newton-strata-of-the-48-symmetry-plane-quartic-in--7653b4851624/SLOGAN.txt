For the smooth 48-symmetry quartic \(X^4+Y^4+XZ^3=0\), all primes \(p\geq5\) split into four congruence classes modulo \(12\) with Jacobian \(p\)-ranks \(3,2,1,0\) and \(a\)-numbers \(0,1,2,3\). Its two intermediate Newton polygons are \(0^{[2]},(1/2)^{[2]},1^{[2]}\) and \(0^{[1]},(1/2)^{[4]},1^{[1]}\); the superspecial endpoint was previously known.
