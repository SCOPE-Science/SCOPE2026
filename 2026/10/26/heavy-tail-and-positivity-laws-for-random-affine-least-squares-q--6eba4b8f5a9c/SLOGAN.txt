Unmodified affine least-squares quadrature with \(M\) independent uniform nodes has an exact condition-number tail exponent \(M-1\), so its \(p\)-th condition-number moment is finite precisely for \(p<M-1\). Three nodes give positive weights with probability \((14-\pi)/16\), yet already have infinite unconditional mean-square amplification of independent measurement noise; four nodes are the sharp finite-variance threshold.
