All idempotent Lie endomorphisms of a Heisenberg algebra are either the identity or abelian retractions organized into affine bundles over symplectic isotropic Grassmannians, giving an exact rank-by-rank finite-field count in every characteristic. From dimension \(11\) onward, the largest-rank proper retractions no longer occupy the largest-dimensional components: the dominant rank is near four-fifths of the maximal possible proper rank.
