In a quantum walk that conditionally resets to the two sites \(\pm a\), symmetric one-time statistics conceal reset-label memory with exact integrated lifetime \(\mathcal T_a=1/(2\lambda p_a)\). The lifetime grows sharply as \(\sqrt{a}\,e^{2a\operatorname{arsinh}(\lambda/(2\gamma))}\), while its short-time decay begins only at order \(t^{2a+1}\).
