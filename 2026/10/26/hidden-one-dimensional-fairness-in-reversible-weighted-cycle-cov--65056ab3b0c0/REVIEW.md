# Scientific review: local inverse geometry of weighted cycle cover tours

## Correctness — PASS

For strictly positive fixed reversible edge conductances on a finite cycle, last discovery is almost surely well-defined. Deleting a candidate terminal vertex gives a path, and the harmonic hitting equation has a constant current, giving equation (1) in RESULT.md. Conditioning on which adjacent endpoint is first hit and on then hitting the other endpoint before the candidate derives that identity exactly. The derivative on the normalized positive simplex is equation (2). Solving its recurrence yields the alternating binomial tangent; the kernel has dimension one, so analytic submersion proves local onto inverse design and one-dimensional fibers. The four-cycle classification follows from two independent equal-probability equations, with positivity checked explicitly. The all-start criterion follows by comparing two starts at the candidate's neighbours. The proof treats all \(n\ge3\), not just enumerated sizes. The separate full visited-set Markov-chain computation passed 551 exact checks up to six vertices; symbolic derivative and rank checks reached ten vertices. Finite tests are corroborative only.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The 2026 study by Idnani and Salem treats homogeneous spatial bias and time-dependent bias and names site-dependent probabilities as future work. Mosbah and Saheb (2000) explicitly already derived probabilities of termination on a weighted cycle, so **no novelty is claimed for merely having a weighted last-coverage formula**. The claim assessed is the inverse map's local surjectivity, exact one-dimensional fibers, the alternating-binomial infinitesimal nonidentifiability, the global four-cycle fairness family, and its contrast with all-start rigidity. These are not statements of the cited summaries or inspected accessible portions. Lovász–Winkler (1993) and Buchanan–Horn–Rombach (2023) classify unweighted underlying graphs under all-start fairness, not inverse conductance parameters on a prescribed cycle. The main residual risk is the inaccessible complete 2000 weighted-cycle article: its previously known forward law might also contain or imply an equivalent inverse classification. The available excerpts demonstrate coverage of the forward law, but not affirmative coverage of the new inverse-map theorem. Search absence alone does not prove novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The inverse problem is natural: in cyclic patrols and exploration, does an observed balanced (or almost balanced) distribution of the last-unvisited site identify a homogeneous travel mechanism, and can one synthesize prescribed target coverage probabilities? The theorem answers both locally in all cycle lengths, with an exact one-dimensional identifiability defect and a highly structured binomial tangent. The nonconstant globally fair four-cycle family and all-start rigidity show that a single-source observation and all-source experimental design differ qualitatively. This distinction is not a marginal numerical improvement or simply another forward coverage-probability formula. It provides a directly usable mathematical boundary for inverse network design. The result does not classify nonreversible transitions or the global inverse fibers for cycles with more than four vertices.
