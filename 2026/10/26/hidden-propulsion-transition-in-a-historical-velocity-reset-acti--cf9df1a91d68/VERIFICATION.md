The moment identity was derived from an exact conditional drift calculation for the accumulated signed velocity, then solved as a regular confluent-hypergeometric ODE. Analytic Kummer asymptotics prove all three infinite-time limits. The finite-time solution was compared against numerical ODE integration and independently simulated event-driven paths in `check_moments.py` at four parameter choices. At 16,000 paths per choice the discrepancies were within estimated Monte Carlo standard errors. The largest of the four standardized discrepancies was under 1.4.

Limits: a finite Monte Carlo test does not prove an asymptotic law. No distributional limit, joint two-time law, or independent mathematical review is supplied. Historical-position copying must be performed jointly with historical orientation copying; changing the reset protocol changes the theorem.
