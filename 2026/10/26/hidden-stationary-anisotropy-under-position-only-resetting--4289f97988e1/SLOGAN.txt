For a passively diffusing anisotropic colloid whose position, but not orientation, is Poisson-reset, the stationary spatial law is rotation-invariant yet retains shape information in every projected fourth moment. Its exact excess kurtosis is \(3+3((D_{\parallel}-D_{\perp})/(D_{\parallel}+D_{\perp}))^2r/(r+4q)\), strictly exceeding the symmetric-exponential value \(3\) for unequal body-frame diffusivities and always remaining below \(6\).
