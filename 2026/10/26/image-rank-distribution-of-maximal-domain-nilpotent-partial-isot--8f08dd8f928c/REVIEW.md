# Scientific review

PASS. A nilpotent partial isotone map of maximal domain has exactly one omitted point. Orbit monotonicity forces all left-side values strictly up toward that point and all right-side values strictly down toward it, and proves that the two sides glue uniquely. Each side is bijective with weakly increasing parking-function sequences. Distinct values are counted by the classical Narayana distribution, and Lagrange inversion yields the exact stated coefficient. The companion finite verifier independently constructs maps and follows their actual trajectories for all small degrees.
