# Review of incidence-algebra regularity spectra and theta fluctuations

## Correctness
PASS. The Schur complement is computed using units supported on comparable pairs; its zero-diagonal restriction lies in a nilpotent radical, so the equation \(S=SYS\) forces \(S=0\). The explicit inverse witness and strict-interval count prove the full-poset law. The square-zero proof is independent. For chains, the ratio \(w_{k+1}/w_k\) and a uniform summable discrete Gaussian error prove the stated phase theta limit at \(O_q(1/\log n)\). Two standalone checkers corroborate finite cases, not infinity.

## Originality
PASS for the **paired incidence/square-zero support spectrum and quantitative conditional phase law**, not for pre-existing qualitative classification or binary chain totals. Khripchenko–Novikov Theorem 4 covers diagonal unit-equivalence; Gould–Johnson–Naz studies matrix-semigroup regularity conditions; Wilf §5.6 and OEIS A079491 already cover the binary exact sum and logarithmic mode bounds. Their implication scope and residual specialized asymptotics risk are documented in AUDIT.json.

## Value
PASS. The formula links a complete natural semigroup invariant for *all* finite posets to the induced-comparability graph, invariant under a substantial change in algebra multiplication. The theta result gives a precise conditional random-regular-element law with nontrivial lattice-phase fluctuations and quantitative convergence beyond known binary totals and modal order.

## Limitations
Only finite posets/finite fields, and fixed-field large-chain asymptotics. No field-uniform estimate, no algebra isomorphism, and no guarantee against unindexed equivalent asymptotic formulas. The general proof and the finite checks are distinct evidence classes.
