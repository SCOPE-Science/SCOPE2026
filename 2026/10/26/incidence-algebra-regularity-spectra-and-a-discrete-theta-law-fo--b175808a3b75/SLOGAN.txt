Every finite-field incidence algebra and its square-zero interval-pattern companion have exactly the same von Neumann regular-element count at each prescribed diagonal-zero set, even though their radical multiplication differs. For upper-triangular matrices conditioned to be regular, the number of zero diagonal entries has a sharp, phase-dependent discrete-theta limit near \(\log_q n-\log_q\log_q n\), with total-variation error \(O_q(1/\log n)\); the binary exact totals themselves are known and credited.
