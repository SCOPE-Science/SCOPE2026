In the complete prism \(K_n\mathbin{\square}K_2\), every inclusion-minimal total dominating set is either a matching pair, one whole clique layer, or two vertices from each layer with four distinct matching indices. Consequently, the full minimal-set polynomial is \(nz^2+6\binom n4z^4+2z^n\), producing arbitrarily long missing-cardinality intervals even on connected vertex-transitive diameter-two graphs.
