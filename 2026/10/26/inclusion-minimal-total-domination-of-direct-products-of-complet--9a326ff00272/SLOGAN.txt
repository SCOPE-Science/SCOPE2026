Every inclusion-minimal total dominating set of \(K_p\times K_q\) is a three-cell coordinate matching or a four-cell configuration with exactly two selections in each of two rows or columns; when \(\min(p,q)=2\), only the four-cell configurations survive. Their closed counting polynomial implies upper total domination number \(4\) for every \(p,q\ge2\), and identifies precisely the well-totally-dominated products.
