A uniformly chosen nonzero square-zero integer \(2\times2\) matrix of entry height at most \(T\) has three asymptotically independent statistics: logarithmic entry gcd, normalized entry height, and projective kernel slope. The first two tend to uniform distributions on \([0,1]\), and the slope has density \(\tfrac14\min\{1,|z|^{-2}\}\); the exact unrefined count was already known.
