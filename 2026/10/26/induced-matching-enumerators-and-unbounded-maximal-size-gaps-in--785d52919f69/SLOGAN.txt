For one-edge subdivisions of all complete bipartite graphs \(K_{p,q}\), explicit Stirling-number formulas count every induced matching and every inclusion-maximal induced matching. When \(2\le p<q\), maximal induced matchings have size either \(p\) or one of \(q,\ldots,p+q-2\), giving an arbitrarily long forbidden interval of sizes already in connected bipartite graphs of girth eight.
