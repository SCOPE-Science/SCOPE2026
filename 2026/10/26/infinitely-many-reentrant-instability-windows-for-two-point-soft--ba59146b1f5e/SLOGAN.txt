For an infinite Winkler-supported Euler–Bernoulli beam with two identical destabilizing point softeners of strength \(\sqrt2<a<2\sqrt2\), the number of disjoint unstable separation windows is exactly \(\left\lceil\pi^{-1}\log\bigl(a/(2\sqrt2-a)\bigr)\right\rceil\). This count diverges toward the isolated-softener threshold, with successive windows born at the sharp strengths \(a_n=2\sqrt2/(1+e^{-n\pi})\) for integers \(n\ge1\).
