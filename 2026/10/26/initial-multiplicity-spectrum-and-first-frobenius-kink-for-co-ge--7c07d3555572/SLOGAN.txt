For coprime denominations \(2\le a<b\) and their sum \(a+b\), the number of integers with exactly \(k\) three-coin representations is \(a+b-k\) throughout \(1\le k\le a\), with a complete matching formula for the generalized Frobenius number and genus. The Frobenius threshold rises by \(b\) at each of those levels, then rises by only \(a\) at level \(a+1\), its first change of slope.
