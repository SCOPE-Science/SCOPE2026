The possible full left-minimal two-symbol co-occurrence profiles of length-\(n\) binary words are in bijection with the integer partitions of all integers at most \(n-1\), giving exactly \(\sum_{t=0}^{n-1}p(t)\) profiles. Each profile's ambiguity-class size follows from permutations of its noninitial run lengths, and the exact maximum number of nonzero sparse-difference entries has a triangular-number threshold.
