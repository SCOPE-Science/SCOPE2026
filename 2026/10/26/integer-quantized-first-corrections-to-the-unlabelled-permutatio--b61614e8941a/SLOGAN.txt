For a uniformly chosen unlabelled finite bijection, every first-order sentence has a computable \(0\)-or-\(1\) limit and a first correction \(\pi M/\sqrt{6n}\), where \(M\) is an integer with the sign forced by the limit and the error is \(O(n^{-1})\). Every permitted integer coefficient is attained: the quantization refines the previously known unlabelled-permutation zero-one law.
