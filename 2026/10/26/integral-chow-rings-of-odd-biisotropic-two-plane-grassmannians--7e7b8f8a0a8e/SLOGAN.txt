For every \(n\ge3\), the odd biisotropic two-plane Grassmannian \(\operatorname{S_2Gr}(2,2n+1)\) has a completely determined integral Chow and cohomology ring: the Schubert ring of \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,2n+1)\) modulo the annihilator of the square of its hyperplane class. Its middle Schubert intersection lattice is unimodular, while its Catalan-number Plücker degree proves that the ring differs from that of \(\operatorname{Gr}(2,2n)\), despite identical integral Betti numbers.
