The multiplicity-weighted multiset-deletion matrix underlying the balanced weighting of the order-preserving transformation monoid has an explicit integral cokernel \(\bigoplus_{j=1}^r(\mathbb Z/j\mathbb Z)^{\binom{n-j-1}{r-j}}\) when \(r<n\); it loses full row rank in characteristic \(p\) exactly for \(p\leq r\). By contrast, the accompanying binary interlacing incidence matrix has an explicit unit-determinant maximal minor, so its row rank never drops modulo any prime.
