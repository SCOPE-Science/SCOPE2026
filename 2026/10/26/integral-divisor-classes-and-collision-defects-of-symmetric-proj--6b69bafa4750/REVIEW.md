# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The quotient map is étale away from codimension-\(n\) diagonals, so algebraic and Borel--Moore localization reduce codimension-one cycle classes to the free configuration quotient. Equivariant Picard descent computes a free generator and one sign class. The integral Cartan--Leray spectral sequence, the first two cohomology groups of the ordered configuration space, and the descended primitive generator yield exactly the same \(\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/2\) in cohomology. The cycle map identifies corresponding generators. Stabilizer products and the Benson--Nakajima quotient class formula verify the local strata. The canonical sign, index, and singularity ages follow from permutation matrices on \(n\)-dimensional blocks. All assertions cover every integer \(n,d\ge2\); no finite sampling is treated as proof.

## Originality — PASS, with explicit residual risk
The closest originating source is Chen--Hu (arXiv:2610.20239v1). Its Theorem 1.1 gives split injectivity and bounded cokernel rather than a codimension-one zero cokernel; its Proposition 5.7 identifies sign torsion for the special case \(\operatorname{Sym}^{2}(\mathbb P^{2})\) and a different fixed-cycle-degree stabilization. The current all-parameter total computation and global-to-completed-local diagonal restriction do not follow merely by repeating that special-case statement. Schmitt's recall of Benson--Nakajima gives general finite linear quotient class groups, but does not supply the global integral cycle comparison. The previously described alternating-group projective quotient is a different variety with a different stabilizer. General symmetric-product and configuration-space literature could contain an implicit equivalent computation; historical priority is not independently established.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A recent article exhibits nonalgebraic integral torsion in codimension two. This theorem isolates the preceding sharp codimension-one boundary, computes every torsion and free summand, and identifies which local collision divisors cannot globalize. The local-to-global obstruction grows with the number of distinct repeated supports, and the same parity character controls the Fano variety's Gorenstein index. These are natural intrinsic invariants, not arbitrary parameter samples or a rebranding of the codimension-two obstruction.

## Limitations and relationship to literature
Exact statements are restricted to coarse complex symmetric powers, divisor-degree cycles, and completed local class groups. Quotient class-group and Reid--Tai theorems are prior art; their use is not claimed as new. The finite literature search leaves implicit-priority risk; no independent audit has been undertaken.
