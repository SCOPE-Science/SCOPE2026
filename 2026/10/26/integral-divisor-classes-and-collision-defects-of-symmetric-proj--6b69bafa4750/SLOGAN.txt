For every \(n,d\ge2\), the divisor cycle map on \(\operatorname{Sym}^d(\mathbb P^n)\) is an **integral isomorphism** \(\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/2\to\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/2\), with its torsion supplied by the non-Cartier sign-character Weil class. At a collision with \(r\) repeated support blocks, the completed local divisor class group is \((\mathbb Z/2)^r\), but only its diagonal torsion comes from a global divisor; the canonical index is one in even projective dimension and two in odd dimension.
