# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The smooth free construction is verified by generic avoidance of both projective fixed spaces; the complete intersection has dimension \(2m\). The integral Lefschetz theorem gives the exact isomorphism range \(q<2m\). The Borel spectral sequence is multiplicative and has only even-total-degree terms in the relevant range, so there are no possible low-degree differentials. The ambient Borel quotient is an explicit projective bundle over \(BC_2\); its integral Chern relation has degree \(8m+4\), leaving precisely \(\mathbb Z[h,t]/(2t)\) below degree \(2m\). Canonical order-two and Hodge vanishings justify the identification of \(t\) and the Picard assertion. The proof is universal and does not extrapolate finite tests.

Statement-level searches of mathematical research records and available earlier claims found no dominating claim. Residual risk: the calculation is accessible from standard equivariant projective-bundle and Lefschetz machinery and may have an earlier implicit formulation, not necessarily indexed by the searched records.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The full low-degree integral ring is a natural deformation-stable topological invariant of an infinite-dimensional family now studied by rational Betti numbers. It exposes \(j\) independent order-two classes in degree \(2j\) and nonzero powers of the canonical torsion class arbitrarily far up the cohomological grading, phenomena that rational cohomology misses. The strict cutoff precisely locates where new primitive middle cohomology may invalidate an extension of the formula. Determining all torsion monomials is more substantive than the known single canonical torsion class or a normalization check.
