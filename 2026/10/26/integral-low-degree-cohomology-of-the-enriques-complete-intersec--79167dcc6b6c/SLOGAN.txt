For the free involution quotients of smooth \(2m\)-dimensional intersections of \(2m+1\) quadrics, the integral cohomology ring strictly below middle degree is \(\mathbb Z[h,t]/(2t)\), with \(t=c_1(\omega)\). In particular, \(t^j\neq0\) for every \(1\le j<m\), giving unbounded cup-power survival of canonical two-torsion invisible to rational Betti numbers.
