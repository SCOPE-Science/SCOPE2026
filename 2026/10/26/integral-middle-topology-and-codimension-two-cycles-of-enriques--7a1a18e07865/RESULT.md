# Integral middle topology and codimension-two cycles of Enriques Fano hosts

## Finding
Let \(S\) be a general smooth complex Enriques surface and let \(Y\) be the smooth Fano fourfold constructed by Tufo as \(\operatorname{Bl}_{S}(\mathbb P^2\times\mathbb P^2)\). There are three simultaneous conclusions:

1. The integral codimension-two cycle map is an isomorphism:
\[
\operatorname{CH}^{2}(Y)\xrightarrow{\;\simeq\;}H^{4}(Y,\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^{13}\oplus\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z.
\]
The nonzero torsion cycle is the exceptional pushforward of the Enriques canonical divisor.

2. The missing odd-degree torsion is
\[
H^{5}(Y,\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z,
\quad H^1(Y,\mathbb Z)=H^3(Y,\mathbb Z)=H^7(Y,\mathbb Z)=0.
\]
Consequently the integral cohomology groups in degrees \(0,\ldots,8\) are, in order, \(\mathbb Z,0,\mathbb Z^3,0,\mathbb Z^{13}\oplus\mathbb Z/2,\mathbb Z/2,\mathbb Z^3,0,\mathbb Z\).

3. The middle intersection lattice, modulo torsion and with complex orientation, is
\[
\left(H^{4}(Y,\mathbb Z)/\mathrm{tors},\smile\right)
\cong U^{\oplus2}\oplus E_8(+1)\oplus\langle1\rangle,
\]
with rank \(13\), determinant \(1\), and signature \((11,2)\). Here \(U\) is the integral hyperbolic plane and \(E_8(+1)\) the positive-definite even unimodular rank-eight lattice. The cohomological torsion linking pairing \(\mathrm{Tor}\,H^4(Y,\mathbb Z)\times\mathrm{Tor}\,H^5(Y,\mathbb Z)\to\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z\) is perfect and takes the value \(1/2\) on their nonzero elements.

## Assumptions and scope
Everything is over \(\mathbb C\), with singular cohomology in integral coefficients. Fix the Tufo fourfold associated with a general Enriques surface \(S\), so the center of the blow-up is smooth, embedded in \(X=\mathbb P^2\times\mathbb P^2\), and has complex codimension two. Denote by \(\pi:Y\to X\) the blow-up, by \(E=\mathbb P(N_{S/X})\) its exceptional divisor, by \(j:E\hookrightarrow Y\), and by \(q:E\to S\) the projection. The intersection lattice is evaluated on the torsion-free quotient of middle cohomology. The assertions do not depend on the particular general Enriques surface or embedding within this construction.

## Proof
For an Enriques surface, \(\pi_1(S)=\mathbb Z/2\), \(q(S)=p_g(S)=0\), and the torsion-free intersection lattice of \(H^2(S,\mathbb Z)\) is \(U\oplus E_8(-1)\), with one extra \(\mathbb Z/2\) summand generated by \(c_1(K_S)\). The universal coefficient theorem gives \(H^1(S,\mathbb Z)=0\), and integral Poincaré duality gives \(H^3(S,\mathbb Z)\cong H_1(S,\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z/2\). Finally \(H^0(S,\mathbb Z)=H^4(S,\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z\). These are the classical integral invariants of complex Enriques surfaces.

For a blow-up along a smooth codimension-two center, the integral cohomology splitting is
\[
H^k(Y,\mathbb Z)\cong\pi^*H^k(X,\mathbb Z)\oplus j_*q^*H^{k-2}(S,\mathbb Z).
\]
It follows integrally from the exceptional projective-bundle formula and the blow-up excision sequence; no rationalization is involved. Since \(H^*(X,\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z[a,b]/(a^3,b^3)\), with \(\deg a=\deg b=2\), the stated complete groups, including degree-five torsion, follow. In particular, a nonzero \(\mathbb Z/2\) summand in degree four forces nonzero degree-five torsion by Poincaré duality and universal coefficients as an independent consistency check.

The corresponding integral blow-up decomposition for Chow groups gives
\[
\operatorname{CH}^{2}(Y)\cong\pi^*\operatorname{CH}^{2}(X)\oplus j_*q^*\operatorname{CH}^{1}(S).
\]
The cycle map on \(X\) is an isomorphism, since \(X\) has an affine-cell decomposition. The exponential sequence on \(S\), together with \(H^1(S,\mathcal O_S)=H^2(S,\mathcal O_S)=0\), gives \(\operatorname{Pic}(S)\simeq H^2(S,\mathbb Z)\), including the torsion canonical class. Naturality of the two blow-up splittings proves the integral cycle-class isomorphism. Its nonzero two-torsion class is \(j_*q^*c_1(K_S)\).

For middle classes \(\alpha,\beta\in H^2(S,\mathbb Z)/\mathrm{tors}\) the exceptional normal bundle has \(c_1(N_{E/Y})=-\xi\), where \(\xi=c_1(\mathcal O_E(1))\) integrates to \(1\) on a projective-line fiber. The self-intersection formula gives
\[
\int_Y(j_*q^*\alpha)(j_*q^*\beta)
=\int_Eq^*(\alpha\beta)(-\xi)=-\int_S\alpha\beta.
\]
The ambient and exceptional degree-four summands are orthogonal by the projection formula: \(q_*(1)=0\) for the one-dimensional fibers. In the ambient basis \((a^2,ab,b^2)\), the intersection matrix is \(U\oplus\langle1\rangle\). Negating the Enriques numerical lattice contributes \(U\oplus E_8(+1)\). Therefore the complete free intersection lattice has the stated orthogonal decomposition, determinant and signature.

On every closed connected oriented eight-manifold, the cohomological linking form pairs \(\mathrm{Tor}\,H^4\) perfectly with \(\mathrm{Tor}\,H^5\). Both are cyclic of order two here, so their unique nonzero elements link to \(1/2\in\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z\).

## Relationship to prior work
Tufo (2026), Theorem 3.1, constructs this Fano fourfold as a blow-up; Corollary 3.2 gives its Hodge diamond and degree-four two-torsion, but the public arXiv version 1 integral-cohomology table lists every other degree as zero, including degree five. The degree-five group above is forced nonzero; the present complete torsion pairing, free middle lattice, and integral codimension-two cycle-class statement are not stated there. The published journal version was identified bibliographically, but its full text was not used to establish whether that table was updated. The Enriques lattice and blow-up formulas are classical input, not new claims.

Ottem--Rennemo--Kollár study Fano varieties with degree-three integral torsion, a distinct birational invariant; our rational Fano fourfold has trivial degree-three cohomology but nontrivial degree-five torsion. Huryn--Newman's study of Enriques motives concerns torsion on the surface, not this host's middle lattice or linking form. The result is a complete computation of natural integral invariants for Tufo's family, not a new general blow-up formula.

## Limitations
The claims are for the smooth complex fourfolds of Tufo's construction. They neither determine the full multiplicative Chow ring nor assert uniqueness among all Fano hosts. The group isomorphisms do not provide an explicit geometric representative for the degree-five torsion beyond the exceptional Gysin summand. The positive \(E_8\) summand uses complex orientation. Priority remains subject to verification of all versions of the journal publication and less directly indexed work; a standard blow-up theorem is the primary computational input.
