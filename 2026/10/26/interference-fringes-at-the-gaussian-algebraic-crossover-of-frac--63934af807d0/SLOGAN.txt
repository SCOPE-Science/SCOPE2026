For a freely evolving Gaussian packet under fractional dispersion, the Gaussian-to-algebraic spatial crossover as \(\alpha\uparrow2\) occurs at a sharply centered distance of order \(\sqrt{\log(1/(2-\alpha))}\). The crossover probability has a universal interference form with a continuously winding phase, and its normalized sharp subsequential envelopes are \((1+e^s)^2\) and \((1-e^s)^2\).
