For Brownian diffusion with independent random proportional Poisson resets, a symmetric multivariate variance-gamma equilibrium \((1+\beta\|t\|^2)^{-q}\) is attainable exactly when \(q\ge1\) and \(\beta\ge D/\lambda\). Its reset fraction is uniquely a mixture of an ineffective event and a beta contraction, with sharp impossibility boundaries for all other shapes and scales.
