The standalone program independently constructs literal finite-field group multiplication and verifies both the seven-vertex binary examples and further test graphs. Computation proves only the fixed pair's finite certificate; the universal support formula has a separate mathematical proof.

The two explicitly specified groups have that entire invariant equal, and also have equal counts of square-one elements, but their *joint* order/conjugacy class-size distribution differs over every finite field of characteristic two. This is a mathematically motivated strict discrimination phenomenon, not an arbitrary size lookup. The universal independent-support formula explains the separation structurally.
