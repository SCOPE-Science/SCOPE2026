# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The p-adic valuation of the arithmetic p-partial derivative obeys f_p(v)=v−1+ν_p(v). Starting m≥p reaches p floor(m/p); if a divisible state has r=ν_p(v)≤p its orbit is a cycle of least period exactly r. For r>p, one reduction to the next multiple adds p floor((r−1)/p), whose valuation is exactly 1+ν_p(floor((r−1)/p)) because p floor((r−1)/p)<p^r; this strictly reduces r and proves the recursive period label H_p. The full initial-exponent formula per_p(m)=H_p(1+ν_p(floor(m/p))) follows. Counting q with ν_p(q)=r−1 gives (p−1)/p^r plus O(1) per occupied valuation and therefore α_{p,t}X+O_p(log X). Each t appears via r=t. For irrationality base-p digits (p−1)1_{H_p(r)=t}, restrict to positions pm+1 to obtain c_{ν_p(m)}. For every s∈1..p, H_p(1+p^s u)=s when p∤u, making c_k repeatedly 0 and 1 for arbitrarily high k. If eventually periodic with L and h=ν_p(L), choosing m=p^k≫L forces c_k=c_h, contradiction. Therefore α_{p,t} irrational for every odd p and t. Independent direct-cycle enumeration for p=3,5,7 and 3≤m<1000 agreed with H_p; sampled 10,000 count strata and truncated exact series corroborate asymptotics but do not prove all-prime assertion.

## originality

PASS

Emmons–Xiao 2022 Theorem 8 gives individual-orbit reduction and Theorem 9 bounds period by p; a single trajectory classification is not a natural density theorem over all integer starting exponents. Their 2024 generalized p-adic arithmetic derivative paper Theorem 2.8 treats valuation dynamics over larger local-field domains, again without the inspected fixed-prime global frequency law or base-p irrationality proof. The existing dyadic (p=2) frequency result is expressly excluded; odd p=3 is independently covered by the present uniform derivation. A Resultary search for odd-prime density irrationality and arithmetic partial derivative returned related prime-power valuation questions, but no same theorem. Potential implicit density corollary of prior orbit classification is the main residual priority question, but it requires a nontrivial counting and nonperiodicity argument.

## value

PASS

Every odd prime has p natural possible eventual period classes, and the theorem gives their complete exact limiting frequencies, O_p(log X) discrepancy and the unexpected irrationality of every class density. This is a coherent global statistical classification of a known local dynamical system; neither a finite survey nor a mere parameter substitution of the dyadic case.
