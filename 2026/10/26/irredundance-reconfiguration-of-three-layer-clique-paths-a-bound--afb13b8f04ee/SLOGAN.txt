For every \(n\ge4\), the maximum-irredundance slide graph of the connected three-layer clique path \(K_n\square P_3\) has exactly \(2^n\) frozen optimum sets and one nontrivial component of diameter \(4\), obtained by joining two rook graphs to a single hub along their diagonals. All inclusion-maximal irredundant sets have size \(3\) or \(n\), with exact polynomial \(n^3z^3+(2n^2+2^n+1)z^n\).
