A weighted wheel with a single absorbing rim vertex captures a hub-localized quantum excitation completely except at an explicit finite set of spoke strengths \(\tau_{n,k}=\sqrt{\lambda_{n,k}(\lambda_{n,k}-2)/n}\), where exact dark-state survival emerges. For unit spokes, the only exceptional rim lengths are \(n=3\) and \(n=8\), with eventual survival \(2/3\) and \(1/2\), respectively.
