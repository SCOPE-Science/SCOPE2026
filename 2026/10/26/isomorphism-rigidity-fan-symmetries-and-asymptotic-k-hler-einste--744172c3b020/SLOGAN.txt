The nonzero zero-sum root-twisted toric \(\mathrm{dP}_3\)-fibrations have a rigid abstract-isomorphism classification by four nonnegative integers, and their finite fan symmetry groups admit an exact formula. Their isomorphism-class series is \(1/[(1-z^2)(1-z^3)(1-z^4)(1-z^6)]-1\); despite stable tangent bundles, the proportion of Kähler–Einstein classes tends to zero in increasing dimension.
