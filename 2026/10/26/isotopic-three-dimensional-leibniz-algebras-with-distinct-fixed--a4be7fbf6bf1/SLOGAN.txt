Two isotopic but nonisomorphic three-dimensional non-Lie Leibniz algebras over every odd finite field have different fixed-point-free automorphism densities: \(((q-2)/(q-1))^2\) versus \((q-2)/(q-1)\). Thus this natural automorphism invariant is not preserved by isotopism, even in the smallest nontrivial one-dimensional-derived-ideal setting.
