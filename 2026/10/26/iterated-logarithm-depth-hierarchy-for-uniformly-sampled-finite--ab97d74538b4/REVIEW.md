# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The finite level-vector enumeration is an exact bijection. A labelled-species Boltzmann distribution has the stated Poisson branching transitions; conditioning on total size gives the uniform unary-model distribution. A positive saddlepoint selects total mean exactly \(n\). Standard Poisson Chernoff bounds yield exponential simultaneous preconditioning concentration. A near-mean integer cascade has polynomial mass by Stirling, so conditioning preserves exponential strength. The iterated-logarithm profile follows from the recursive saddlepoint equations. Independent exhaustive enumeration for sizes through six checks the finite formulas, not the asymptotics.

## Originality
**PASS within inspected evidence, with priority limits.** The historical bounded-height tree EGF is already known and is explicitly excluded from novelty. Two relevant recent model-theoretic papers study fixed-tree expansions and finite-model properties, not uniform random unary forests. A prior height-two concentration result does not supply the family of simultaneous bounds for every fixed \(h\ge3\). Searches for conditioned random forests, bounded-height profile asymptotics, iterated logarithms and exact cascade reductions did not locate identical claims. Older random-tree strata and general conditioned-tree theory remain live priority risks; a missing search hit alone does not prove novelty.

## Value
**PASS.** The definable class \(f^{h+1}=f^h\) is a natural family of finite unary models. Its typical shape is not captured by exact total counts: the theorem supplies all nontrivial layer scales and a quantitative simultaneous large-deviation guarantee. It isolates a reusable conditional branching mechanism and quantifies why almost every world sits at maximum allowed depth, across all fixed heights.

## Scientific limitations
The result does not claim an independent audit, a central limit theorem, a growing-height theorem or a general first-order limit law. Historical priority outside the material inspected is uncertain.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
