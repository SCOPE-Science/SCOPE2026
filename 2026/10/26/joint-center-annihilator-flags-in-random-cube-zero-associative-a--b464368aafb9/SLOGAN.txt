The complete joint flag of the two-sided annihilator inside the center of a uniformly chosen surjective square-cube-zero algebra over \(\mathbb F_q\) admits an explicit Gaussian-binomial double sum for every dimension and every characteristic. With fixed output dimension \(s\ge2\), the center is generically minimal, and the conditional chance of a nonminimal annihilator given a nonminimal center is asymptotic to \(q^{-sr}\).
