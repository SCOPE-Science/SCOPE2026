# Scientific review — joint descent–plateau enumeration

## Correctness (PASS)

The statement quantifies over every integer \(n\ge1\) and uses only internal adjacent comparisons. Under the established type-B bijection, a nonzero signed block of absolute size \(s\) has exactly the three weighted sign patterns appearing in \(W\); the special zero block has weights \(u\) and \(tu^z\). Block boundaries introduce only ascents. The labeled SET decomposition gives the claimed EGF over the polynomial coefficient ring. Direct generation of all relevant words and an independently written block recurrence agree coefficient by coefficient through \(n=8\). Finite checks support, but do not replace, the all-order proof.

## Originality (PASS, with residual priority risk)

The closest directly relevant prior result is Shankar (2023), Corollary 10: it is recovered at \(u=1\), and so its entire univariate descent assertion is prior art, **not** a new discovery. Shankar, Section 7 question 4, explicitly asks for joint ascent and plateau enumeration, which the asserted \(k=2\) formula supplies. Buck et al. give the bijection but not this weighted joint enumeration. Ma–Ma–Yeh (2019) treats unrestricted Stirling permutations; Ma–Liu–Yeh–Yeh (2025) treats a distinct once-smallest-letter family. Searches of published mathematical-record summaries and public literature did not identify a matching bivariate flattened enumeration. The strongest remaining uncertainty is unindexed work or an implicit equivalent specialization elsewhere.

## Mathematical value (PASS)

The result gives a complete coefficient-extractable joint law in every size for a natural permutation family, specializing to a known generating function and answering the original two-copy case of a named open direction. This is a nontrivial refinement: the descent marginal alone does not determine the plateau covariance or joint polynomial.

## Relationship and limitations

Relevant primary texts: Buck et al. arXiv:2306.13034; Shankar arXiv:2307.07730; Ma–Ma–Yeh arXiv:1801.08056; Ma–Liu–Yeh–Yeh arXiv:2506.16438. The result does not cover general \(k\), nor establish an independent priority audit. The proof depends on the standard published type-B bijection, with its block description reproduced for the statistic computation.
