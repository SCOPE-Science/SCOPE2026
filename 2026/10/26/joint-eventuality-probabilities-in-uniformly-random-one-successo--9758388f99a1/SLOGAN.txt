In uniform random one-successor Kripke systems, joint CTL eventuality has an exact multivariate cycle-weighted generating function and a nontrivial closed limiting probability. Two independent eventuality requirements have a success probability that increases strictly from \(pq\) on one state to \(pq/(p+q-pq)\) as the state count grows, even though either requirement alone holds globally with probability equal to its label rate at every size.
