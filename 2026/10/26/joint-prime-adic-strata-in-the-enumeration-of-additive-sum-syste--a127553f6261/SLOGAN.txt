For every fixed number of labelled additive sum-system parts and every finite prescribed vector of prime valuations, the number of systems of size at most \(X\) has a sharp asymptotic \(C X^{\rho}(\log X)^E\), with an explicit Euler-factor-deleted zeta root and a factorial-product leading constant. The resulting prime-adic valuations diverge jointly in probability under uniform sampling of sum systems by bounded size.
