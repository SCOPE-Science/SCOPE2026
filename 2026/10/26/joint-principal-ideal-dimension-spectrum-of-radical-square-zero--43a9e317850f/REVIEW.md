## originality

PASS

Standard radical-square-zero quiver algebra structure and principal ideal rank interpretation are prior mathematics. Męcel 2016 studies finiteness of conjugacy classes of left ideals over algebraically closed fields, not element-weighted simultaneous left/right ranks over finite fields. Elia–Gorla 2014 algorithm treats individual ranks in group algebras; Lanzilotta et al and Nasr-Isfahani concern homological/singular properties. None of those inspected theorem statements gives the full bivariate quiver polynomial or orientation distinction. The rank calculation is elementary, so a hidden general matrix-support or module enumeration formula is a real priority risk; novelty is only claimed best-of-knowledge.

## value

PASS

The object is standard in representation theory and finite algebra, and the measured invariant is natural: the joint sizes of two principal one-sided ideals generated by a uniformly chosen element. A closed coefficient formula for every finite quiver including loops and multiple arrows is a complete classification of this statistic. It separates all orientations of a three-vertex path despite identical pairwise commuting equations, demonstrating nonredundant algebraic information with exact computable counts.
