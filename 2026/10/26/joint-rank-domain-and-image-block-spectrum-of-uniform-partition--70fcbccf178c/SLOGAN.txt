For partial transformations preserving a uniform set partition, a rooted-star decomposition gives the complete joint distribution of image rank, domain size, and the number of image-bearing blocks via a closed exponential generating function. In particular, exactly \(mn\,2^{mn-1}\) such idempotents have rank one, and for \(n\ge2\) exactly \(mn^2\) have rank \(mn-1\).
