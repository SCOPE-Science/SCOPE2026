For a fixed maximal-rank square-zero matrix \(A\), the square-zero matrices \(B\) anticommuting with \(A\) admit a closed joint distribution of \(\operatorname{rank}B\) and \(\operatorname{rank}(AB)\) over every finite field. Conditional on the second rank, the first has precisely the rank law of an unconstrained endomorphism of a naturally defined homology space, yielding explicit smooth rank strata.
