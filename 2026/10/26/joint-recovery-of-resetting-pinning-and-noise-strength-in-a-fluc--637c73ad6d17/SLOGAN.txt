For a one-dimensional harmonically pinned Edwards–Wilkinson interface undergoing whole-profile Poisson resetting, a stationary height variance, one normalized spatial covariance and one pointwise excess kurtosis recover the resetting rate, uniform pinning and noise intensity uniquely. The exact realizable observable region is \(V>0\), \(0<R<1\), \(0<K<12/\pi-3\): non-Gaussianity separates two physical damping mechanisms that second moments alone cannot distinguish.
