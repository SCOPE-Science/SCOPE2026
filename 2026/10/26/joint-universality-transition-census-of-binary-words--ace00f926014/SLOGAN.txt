For binary words, the scattered-subsequence universality index and the number of adjacent symbol changes have the exact joint generating function \(1+\frac{2z(1+ztu)}{1-z-z^2tu(1+t)}\). In particular, every nonconstant word satisfies the sharp tradeoff \(\lceil r/2\rceil\le\iota\le r\), and all feasible length/index/transition triples are counted by a two-term binomial formula.
