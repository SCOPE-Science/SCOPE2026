In a uniform random recursive tree, forcing any specified pair of vertices to be sibling leaves changes the expected root degree by an amount independent of their birth labels: only whether their common parent is the root matters. The resulting covariance between root degree and the total number of sibling-leaf pairs is exactly \((4n^2-15n+12)/(6(n-1)(n-2))\), strictly positive and tending to \(2/3\).
