For the uniform rank-nine symplectic bundle on \(\mathbb P^5\), the space of planes where \(h^0(E|_{\mathbb P^2}(-1))\) rises from \(2\) to \(3\) is exactly the smooth Lagrangian Grassmannian \(\operatorname{LG}(3,6)\). Thus a line-uniform bundle has a canonical six-dimensional jumping-plane geometry.
