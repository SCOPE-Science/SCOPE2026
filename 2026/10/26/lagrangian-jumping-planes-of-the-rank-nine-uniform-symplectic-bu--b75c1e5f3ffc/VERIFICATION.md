# Verification notes
The proof is symbolic and holds for every three-dimensional subspace of a six-dimensional complex symplectic vector space. The program `verify_planes.py` checks exact coordinate cases using rational matrices; these cases cannot prove the exhaustive assertion. Critical ingredients requiring human examination are the Euler–Koszul exact sequence, restriction and splitting on a plane, the equivalence of bivector projection vanishing with Lagrangianity, and the Euler-sequence cohomology calculation. No independent audit has been performed.
