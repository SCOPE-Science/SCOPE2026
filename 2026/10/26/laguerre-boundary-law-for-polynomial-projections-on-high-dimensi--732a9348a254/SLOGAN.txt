For each fixed polynomial degree \(m\ge2\), the sharp vertex \(L^\infty\) evaluation norm of the uniform-simplex \(L^2\) polynomial projector grows as \(c_m d^m\), where \(c_m\) is an explicit absolute Laguerre integral. At degree two this has a closed beta-root formula for every dimension and \(c_2=0.310012507574629\ldots\), giving an equally sharp vertex positivity-loss law; no claim about the global projector norm is made.
