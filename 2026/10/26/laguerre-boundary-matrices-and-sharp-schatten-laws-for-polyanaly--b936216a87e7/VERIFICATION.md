# Verification

`CHECK.py` verifies the exact finite-sector generalized eigenvalue formulas for the unweighted bianalytic disk at powers one and two, the Laguerre pencil spectra, and numeric asymptotic convergence from finite beta moments. The proof of convergence for all frequencies and of sharp Schatten membership appears in `RESULT.md`; finite calculations alone are not treated as proof. The standard Python library suffices to run the checker. No independent audit or external certification has been performed.
