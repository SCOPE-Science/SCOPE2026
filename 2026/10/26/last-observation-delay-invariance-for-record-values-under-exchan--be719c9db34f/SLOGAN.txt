Under every exchangeable observation law, including discrete laws with ties, delaying the final observation in a fixed sampled subsequence leaves its record count jointly unchanged in distribution with the entire ordered list of full-sequence record heights. For two sampled positions under iid continuity, its covariance with the total full-record count is exactly \(1/[2k(k+1)]\), depending on the first sampled time \(k\) but not the final one.
