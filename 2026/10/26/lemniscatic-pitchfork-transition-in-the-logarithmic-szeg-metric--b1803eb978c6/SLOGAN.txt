For the ordinary Szegő Hardy space on an annulus, the logarithmic infinitesimal projective metric has exactly one radially minimizing circle when \(q\ge e^{-\pi}\), but exactly two reciprocal minimizing circles when \(q<e^{-\pi}\). At the sharp threshold \(q=e^{-\pi}\) its unique centered minimum is quartically flat, and all optimizing radii and optimal values have explicit Jacobi-elliptic formulas.
