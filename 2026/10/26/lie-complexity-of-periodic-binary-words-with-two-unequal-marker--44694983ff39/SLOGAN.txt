Every Lie class of the unequal-two-gap periodic binary word \((01^b01^d)^\omega\), \(1\le b<d\), belongs to one of five explicit families, yielding its complete length spectrum and generating function. Its first-period Lie mass is \(d+3+\min(d,2b+1)\), with sharp fixed-period maximum \(\lfloor4p/3\rfloor+1\) and a three-class transition precisely when \(d\ge2b+2\).
