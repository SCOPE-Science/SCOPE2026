For the cobalancing numbers \(b_n\), the GCD of all \(k\)-term consecutive squared sums always divides \(40k\) and is determined by only four initial window sums and \(40k\). On the infinite dyadic family \(k=12\cdot2^r\), this GCD is exactly \(10k\), with every window attaining the same exact two-adic valuation.
