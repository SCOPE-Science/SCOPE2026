# Verification details

Run `python3 verify.py` using Python's standard library. The program generates exact integers by the cobalancing recurrence and checks the fourth-order identity, local cycles, the matrix lifting congruences for a finite range of exponents, the universal finite GCD formula for a finite range of lengths, and the stated dyadic formula at initial exponents. Its output is an executed computational cross-check, not an infinite proof or independent audit. The proof of every infinite assertion is in `RESULT.md`.
