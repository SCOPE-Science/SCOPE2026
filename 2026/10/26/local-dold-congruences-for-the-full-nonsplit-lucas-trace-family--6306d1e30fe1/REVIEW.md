# Review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof treats the repeated-root (ramified) case separately, then derives a first trace-zero index from the multiplicative order of the ratio of the two characteristic roots. Odd-power lifting gives the exact valuations; Möbius inversion produces the complete signed tower. A level-one lifted tower term proves the *least* repair factor, avoiding assumptions about the parity of the initial valuation. The Frobenius relation resolves split/inert primes and two-adic duplication resolves even/odd recurrence parameter. An unramified real quadratic character, independent of the character modulo four, yields density \(1/4\) for the bad odd-prime set. The finite exact checker cross-validates 121,680 transform cells for 18 parameters and 26 small primes, but cannot replace the infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual priority risk
The 21 September 2026 Lucas/Fibonacci paper explicitly raises the general companion problem; its worked results are not the complete \(V(P,-1)\) family for \(P\ge3\). Closest published related research results cover positive square-discriminant split traces or period equality. Earlier first-kind \(U(P,-1)\) local Dold classifications and first-kind valuation theorems provide potential routes to derive the new result, but the compared claims do not state or imply its signed trace transform and least repair and full-prime-density theorem without additional arguments. The known special \(P=2\) result is excluded. Wider unpublished or unindexed general second-kind valuations remain possible and require independent priority checks.

## Value — PASS
The result settles a natural nonsplit full-parameter test case of the recent open companion-Lucas question: exact integrality/nonnegativity dichotomy, sharp repair obstruction, all-index signed local formulas, and a uniform arithmetic prime density. The distinction between Dold congruence and positive realizability is structural, not a small isolated numerical gain.
