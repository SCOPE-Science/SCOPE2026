For the companion Lucas trace recurrence \(V_{n+2}=PV_{n+1}+V_n\) with every fixed integer \(P\ge3\), its local odd-prime part fails Dold congruences exactly at inert primes \(p\equiv3\pmod4\), a set of density \(1/4\); the least repair factor there is the first trace-zero index. Complete signed local transforms, the binary exceptional behavior, and the local nonnegative-realizability criterion are also explicit.
