In every connected noncomplete strongly regular graph, the canonical cut and cycle endpoint projection norms are determined exactly by one local statistic: the minimum total number of edges induced by the two exclusive-neighbor sets of an adjacent pair. This distinguishes the cospectral \(4\times4\) rook and Shrikhande graphs: their cut norms are respectively \(9/4\) and \(19/8\), and their cycle norms are \(21/8\) and \(11/4\).
