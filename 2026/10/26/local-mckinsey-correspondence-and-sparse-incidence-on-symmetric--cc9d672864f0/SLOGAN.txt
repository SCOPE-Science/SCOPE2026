For arbitrary symmetric Kripke frames, the McKinsey formula \(\Box\Diamond p\to\Diamond\Box p\) holds at a world exactly when it sees a vertex whose sole successor is that world, even without weak transitivity. Globally validating frames are precisely involution relations, while the exact rooted frequency in sparse loop-free random graphs converges to \(1-\exp(-ce^{-c})\), maximized at \(c=1\).
