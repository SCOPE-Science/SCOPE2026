The mathematical verification uses the explicit birth–death scale function, the Doob h-transform, the first-return asymptotic for Bessel-like reflecting walks, and the local defective-renewal asymptotic for a regularly varying sequence with total mass below one.

The scale ratio is summable because its power exponent is \(2\beta+1>1\). The transformed drift parameter is \(\delta=2\beta-1>-1\), and its slowly varying factor is asymptotic to a positive constant times \((\log x)^{-2d}\). The conditional first-return mass is therefore asymptotic to a positive constant times \(n^{-\beta-1}(\log n)^{-2d}\). The renewal equation gives the same local order for the original return probability. The moment series is equivalent to \(\sum_{n\ge2}1/[n(\log n)^{2d}]\), whose convergence threshold is \(d>1/2\).

No finite experiment is used to infer an infinite asymptotic statement. The leading constant is asserted positive and finite but is not reduced to a closed form. The proof does not cover non-nearest-neighbour chains or remainders that change the slowly varying factor. Independent audit and independent validation remain unperformed.
