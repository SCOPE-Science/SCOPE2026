# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** A homogeneous non-atomic biased binary product law meets the stated 2018 Fourier-decay theorem; both nonlinear maps and all affine-cylinder compositions have positive second derivatives. Function-independence of the exponent proves uniformity. The strong law and Fourier-decay normality yield the square-root bias and simultaneous absolute normality. Plancherel at \(2k_\theta\sigma_\theta>1\) establishes absolute continuity of finite convolutions and Steinhaus proves an interval in the difference set. Borel normality and Lipschitz images give nullity. No finite experiment is used as an infinite proof.

## Originality

**PASS, with residual risk.** Stephan (2026), Remark 4.5(ii), already gives biased binary counterexamples with Hausdorff dimension arbitrarily close to one; that is not new here. The primary source uses Fourier decay for the shifted square root, not for the inputs or all local cylinders. Mosquera–Shmerkin's 2018 theorem supplies the essential general method, but does not state the new simultaneous normality and locally uniform sumset result. Targeted published-record and literature comparisons did not find a covering statement. The deduction uses strong existing machinery and may have implicit earlier coverage.

## Value
