# Verification steps and limits

Read Mosquera–Shmerkin (2018), Theorem 3.1, verifying that its exponent is independent of the strictly convex \(C^2\) map. For the bias-\(\theta\) binary law, verify absence of atoms by cylinder masses and direct derivatives \(\phi''=1/2\), \(\psi''(x)=1/[2(1+x^2)^{3/2}]>0\). Check the same inequalities after each dyadic affine-cylinder composition. By the strong law the first square root has digit frequency \(\theta\); by polynomial Fourier decay all three specified other quantities are normal to every integer base. The convolution Fourier transform is \(L^2\) whenever \(2k\sigma>1\), giving a positive-measure sumset by Plancherel and an interval in its difference set by Steinhaus. Borel's normality theorem and Lipschitz mapping give the null exceptional set. These are deductive checks, not a finite computational certificate. No optimal exponent or sumset order is asserted.
