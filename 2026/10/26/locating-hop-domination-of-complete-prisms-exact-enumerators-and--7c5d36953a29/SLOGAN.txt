For \(n\ge3\), locating-hop dominating sets of the complete prism \(K_n\square K_2\) admit an exact structural characterization and closed enumerators for all sets and inclusion-minimal sets. At \(n=5\), inclusion-minimal cardinalities split into \(n-1\) and \(n\), whereas at \(n=3,4\) all inclusion-minimal sets have one common size; equivalently these results enumerate ordinary locating-dominating sets of crown graphs.
