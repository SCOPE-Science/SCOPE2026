A geometrically spaced collection of circle measures on Bergman space produces eigenvalues with an exact, nonconstant log-periodic Mellin profile and explicit nonvanishing Gamma Fourier modes. Nevertheless, the critical power is Dixmier-measurable, with trace \(\int_0^1 G(t)^{1/(\alpha-1)}\,dt\); at \(\alpha=2\) the original operator has the exact trace \(1/\log b\).
