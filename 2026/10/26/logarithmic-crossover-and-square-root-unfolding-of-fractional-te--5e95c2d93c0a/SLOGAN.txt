For the Fourier transform of the fractional tent \((1-|x|)_+^\alpha\), paired zeros split from every triangular-window double zero at a square-root rate as \(\alpha\uparrow1\). At exponentially increasing frequencies with \((1-\alpha)\log(2\pi n)\to\lambda>0\), the two zero offsets instead converge to \(\pm\arccos(e^{-\lambda})\), exhibiting a logarithmic boundary layer.
