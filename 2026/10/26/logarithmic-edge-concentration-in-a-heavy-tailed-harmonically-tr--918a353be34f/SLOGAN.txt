For a harmonically trapped one-dimensional run-and-tumble particle with independent heavy-tailed residence times \(\Pr(S>t)\sim c t^{-\beta}\), \(\beta>1\), the stationary mass within distance \(\varepsilon\) of either edge is asymptotic to \(c[\log(1/\varepsilon)]^{1-\beta}/[2\mathbb ES(\beta-1)]\), and every positive reciprocal boundary-gap moment diverges. For Lomax residence times the one-sided coefficient is universally \(1/2\), independent of the tail exponent.
