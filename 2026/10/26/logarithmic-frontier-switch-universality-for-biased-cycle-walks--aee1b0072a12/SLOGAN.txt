The number of times a cycle walk's *discovery frontier* switches direction has an exact independent-Bernoulli law for unbiased walking, and under arbitrary fixed-within-cycle biases it lies within a universal bounded \(L^1\) distance of a Bernoulli sum stopped at the effective scale \(\min\{n,|\log(p/(1-p))|^{-1}\}\). This yields a Gaussian law with logarithmic mean and variance throughout the near-unbiased regime, but only finitely many switches under any fixed nonzero drift.
