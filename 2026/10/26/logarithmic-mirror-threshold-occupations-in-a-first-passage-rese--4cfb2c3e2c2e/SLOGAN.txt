In the stationary Brownian gas collectively reset whenever one particle reaches \(+L\), the probability that **any** particle lies beyond the opposite level \(-L\) is asymptotic to \(\log(3/2)/\log N\), even though the minimum's leading limiting distribution ends at \(-L\). Conditionally on this rare event, the number beyond the mirror level has a logarithmic-series law with weights proportional to \(1/(k3^k)\), and the reset-age boundary layer has an explicit Gamma law.
