Positive stable sampling of degree-\(N\) polynomials in every fixed power-weighted Bergman space requires at least \(c_\alpha(A/B)\log(N+1)\) distinct sample radii, regardless of angular oversampling. Explicit dyadic-radius root-of-unity samplers attain the matching \(O_\alpha(\log(N+1))\) rate with degree-independent positive frame bounds.
