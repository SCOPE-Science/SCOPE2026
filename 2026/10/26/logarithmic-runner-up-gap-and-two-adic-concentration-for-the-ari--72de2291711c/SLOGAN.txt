For the arithmetic derivative, the gap between the dyadic record \(D(2^k)\) and the best value at \(n<2^k\) has exact order \(2^k\log k\), rather than a fixed fraction of the record. Every runner-up is divisible by \(2^{k-O(\log k)}\) and sits relatively within \(O(\log k/k)\) of the dyadic endpoint.
