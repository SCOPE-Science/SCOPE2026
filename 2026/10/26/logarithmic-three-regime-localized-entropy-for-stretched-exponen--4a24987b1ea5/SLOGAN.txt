The stretched-exponential radii \(1+e^{-n^\kappa}\) have a three-regime scale-resolved Legendre–Assouad entropy: a pure logarithm for \(b\le0\), a sharp mixed power–logarithm for \(0<b<1\), and a pure power for \(b\ge1\). The intermediate logarithmic exponent \((1-\kappa)(1-b)/\kappa\) is not recorded by the ordinary dimension exponent.
