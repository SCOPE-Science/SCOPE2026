In dimer adsorption on a growing clique with finitely many nonadjacent impurities, edge reactivities of size \(c_i/\log n\) leave independently selected bulk survivors with probabilities \(e^{-c_i}\). Terminal parity then removes at most one survivor, with a rate-weighted choice, giving a complete labeled Poisson-binomial jamming law and an exact finite product for two impurities.
