Stark's 2021 fixed-partition injection model gives earlier component counts and Poisson-type results. Effective Hanf locality and general combinatorial convergence methods are likewise prior. The assessed combination gives the explicit complete first-order rational-exponential probability class and dense probability spectrum for uniformly sampled arbitrary-domain partial injections. Inspected primary materials did not exhibit the full statement, but Compton's general framework and unlocated specialized work remain material priority risks. This is a best-knowledge comparison, not a uniqueness certification.

## Value — PASS
The ensemble has a natural finite-model-theoretic meaning: a uniformly random labelled binary relation with indegree and outdegree at most one. Its complete fixed-sentence convergence law, effective arithmetic restriction on all limits, and dense attainable spectrum characterize how local logical properties interact with the competing path and cycle components. The density result is an exact structural distinction between convergence and zero-one laws, not a numerical recomputation of known component tables.
