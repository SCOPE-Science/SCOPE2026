For every \(n\ge4\), the four-cycle-switch graph of perfect matchings of the \(2n\)-vertex Möbius ladder is exactly the Lucas cube \(\Lambda_n\) when \(n\) is even and \(\Lambda_n\sqcup2K_1\) when \(n\) is odd. A direct matching-to-cyclic-independent-set correspondence yields exact flip distances and shows that the two extra odd-width boundary matchings are frozen.
