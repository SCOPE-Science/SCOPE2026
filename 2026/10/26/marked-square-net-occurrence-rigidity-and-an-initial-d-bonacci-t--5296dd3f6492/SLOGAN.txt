A word built as \(XzXXzX^-\) has exactly three net occurrences whenever \(X\) begins and ends with the same letter but avoids that letter doubled, and \(z\) is a new marker. This proves the sharp \(0,2,3\) initial net-occurrence transition of the finite \(d\)-bonacci iterates, including the first iterate outside a completed directive period for every \(d\ge3\).
