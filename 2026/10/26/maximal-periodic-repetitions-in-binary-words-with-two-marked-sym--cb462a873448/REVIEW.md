# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The all-length proof classifies period-one runs and shows that any higher-period run requires both occurrences of the sparse symbol, so its period is uniquely their distance. The exact maximal extension and length threshold yield the formula. Independent full-run-set enumeration agrees for 1,330 gap triples; separate coefficient checks match the bivariate and four-run generating functions. The closed-form extremal and asymptotic conclusions follow algebraically, not merely from experiments.

**Originality — PASS (subject to historical-priority risk).** The two closest accessible original research papers, Bannai et al. (arXiv:1406.0263) and Fischer et al. (arXiv:1502.04644), provide global length/alphabet bounds for the same maximal-periodic-repetition definition. Their results do not entail the exact zero-gap classification or complete distribution for precisely two ones. Madden (arXiv:1707.04351) studies runs in the different constant-symbol-block sense and does not imply the extra higher-period run. Text of the relevant primary articles and their statements was compared. An unindexed equivalent calculation remains possible; absence of a search hit is not proof of historical priority.

**Value — PASS.** The number of maximal periodic repetitions is a natural invariant used in string indexing and repetition algorithms. A sharp constant bound that is independent of word length, complete parametric run location classification, a rational counting series and limiting extremal density together resolve the entire fixed-two-marker family rather than a single isolated example. The concrete gap between global length-linear bounds and sparse-family behavior motivates the restriction.

**Scope and risks.** Only finite linear binary words with exactly two ones are covered. Search coverage cannot guarantee priority, and independent audit has not been performed. The computational checks confirm instances but the all-length result rests on the included proof.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
