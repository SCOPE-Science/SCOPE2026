Every inclusion-maximal abelian subalgebra of the free anticommutative antiassociative algebra on at least three generators is automatically an ideal, and all such subalgebras form just two automorphism orbits. Over an odd finite field, their exact number is \(1+q^{\binom{r-1}{2}}(q^r-1)/(q-1)\), with explicitly determined dimensions and pairwise intersections.
