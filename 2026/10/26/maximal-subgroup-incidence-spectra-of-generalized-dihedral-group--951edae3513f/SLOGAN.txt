The maximal-subgroup incidence degree of every element in \(\operatorname{Dih}(A)\) is explicit for any nontrivial finite abelian kernel \(A\), including even-order kernels. Its uniform intersection number is \(2+\sum_{p\mid |A|}p(p^{r_p}-1)/(p-1)-\min_{p\mid |A|}p^{r_p}\), and the complete incidence-degree enumerator follows from Sylow–Frattini residue patterns.
