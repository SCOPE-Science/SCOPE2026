# Review of three-factor Hamming total-visibility census

## Correctness

PASS. The proof relies on the established total-visibility/Hamming-distance-two equivalence and supplies separate exhaustive arguments for the two-vertex maximal-set classification, the diagonal versus adjacent-cell dichotomy, the two coefficient polynomials, and the sphere-with-bridges homotopy calculation. Every parameter boundary is explicit: general dimensions \(a,b\ge2\), specialized third factor \(m\ge2\), and the degree-two coincidence at \(m=2\). The standalone finite verifier independently evaluates the original geodesic definition for all subsets in the first two cases and enumerates all cliques and facets in larger small instances. Infinite claims rest on proof, not enumeration.

## Originality

PASS, subject to the stated priority limitations. The closest primary paper on lower total mutual-visibility, Brešar–Yero (arXiv:2307.02951), proves a two-factor formula and does not supply this three-factor minimal-facet census or complete polynomial. The closest three-factor primary source, Bujtás–Klavžar–Tian (arXiv:2307.05168), already establishes both the distance-two criterion and the maximum cardinality; neither is asserted new. Its broad feasibility characterization is used to derive a previously unlocated complete labeled face/facet census for \(K_2\square K_2\square K_m\), the broader exact minimal-facet count, and their topology. An equivalent enumeration under distance-two-code or complex terminology could remain unindexed, and the 2025 mutual-visibility polynomial was compared at abstract level only.

## Value

PASS. The lower invariant captures worst-case stopping points of greedy augmentation, and here a minimum facet stays of size two while the optimum grows without bound. In the natural doubly binary family, the complete face polynomial, complete facet polynomial, and homotopy type identify both the count and geometry of every admissible configuration. This is a natural infinite-family classification in a research-motivated graph product, rather than a numerical slice selected after computation.

The proof does not classify larger facets when both remaining factors exceed two, nor more than three factors. The exact homotopy type is asserted only for two binary factors. Full bibliographic priority and independent mathematical audit are not established.
