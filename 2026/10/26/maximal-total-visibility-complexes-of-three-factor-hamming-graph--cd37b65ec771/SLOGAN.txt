In the three-factor Hamming graph \(K_2\square K_a\square K_b\), maximal total mutual-visibility sets can have only two vertices even though the largest sets have \(a+b-2\) vertices; their exact two-vertex census is determined. For \(K_2\square K_2\square K_m\), all feasible sets have polynomial \(2(1+2z)^m-1+4mz^2\), the maximal sets have polynomial \(2^{m+1}z^m+4mz^2\), and their simplicial complex has the homotopy type of two \((m-1)\)-spheres joined by \(4m\) edges.
