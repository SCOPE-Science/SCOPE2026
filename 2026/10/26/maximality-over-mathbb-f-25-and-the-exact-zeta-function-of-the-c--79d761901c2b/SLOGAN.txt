The Hessian quartic of the Clebsch diagonal cubic acquires eleven rational nodes in characteristic five, and its minimal resolution is a K3 surface maximal over \(\mathbb F_{25}\). Its exact zeta function is \(1/((1-T)(1-25T)(1-5T)^{18}(1+5T)^4)\), determined by two exhaustive finite-field counts and the Weil bounds.
