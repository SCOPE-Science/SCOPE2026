Every isometric map from a weighted Boolean cube of dimension \(n\ge3\) into complex trace-norm matrices is the restriction of a complex-linear \(\ell_1\) isometry made from mutually orthogonal positive blocks. An embedding into \(p\times q\) matrices exists precisely when \(\min\{p,q\}\ge n\), and in the minimum square size every configuration is a two-unitary image of the weighted diagonal cube.
