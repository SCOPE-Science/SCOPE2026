For Euclidean-distance weighted Bergman kernels on complex unit balls in every fixed dimension \(d\ge2\), all large **real** powers exhibit a microscopic Bessel zero pattern: \(t^{-2d}K_{d,t}(u/t^2)\to C_d{}_0F_1(;d+1/2;u/4)\). Each fixed negative Bessel zero contracts by the same first relative factor \(1-2(d+1)/t\), yielding arbitrarily many genuine kernel zeros even for noninteger weights.
