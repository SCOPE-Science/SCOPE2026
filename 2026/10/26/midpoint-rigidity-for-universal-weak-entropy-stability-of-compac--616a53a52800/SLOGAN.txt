For uncorrected piecewise-affine compact DG applied to linear advection, all two-stage second-order Runge–Kutta tableaus with nonnegative output weights produce the **same numerical solution**, yet only explicit midpoint obeys the RK-averaged weak cell-entropy inequality for **every convex entropy** at a positive timestep; its sharp Courant interval is \(0<r\leq1\). Every other tableau fails at arbitrarily small \(r>0\) even for some smooth strongly convex entropy, while full-state Fourier amplification may occur within the midpoint entropy-safe interval.
