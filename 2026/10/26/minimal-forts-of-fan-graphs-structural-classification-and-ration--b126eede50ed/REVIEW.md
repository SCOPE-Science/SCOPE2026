# Scientific review: minimal forts of fan graphs

## Correctness — PASS

The fort condition reduces to a binary path restriction without the universal vertex and to path domination with it. Every path fort dominates the path, so the only possible hidden subfort in a hub-containing candidate is the whole minimally dominating base set. That coincidence occurs precisely for the odd alternating independent dominating set. The two-type minimal-domination run decomposition gives the transfer expression and the stated rational function. Direct neighborhood enumeration checks all fan subsets through \(n=16\), including the stated boundary and parity cases.

## Originality — PASS, with priority uncertainty

Becker et al. (arXiv:2404.05963v1, Proposition 20) already preserve old path forts under joins and count path minimal forts. A public work on fan zero-forcing polynomials already characterizes every successful fan forcing set by trigger patterns. That known predicate **can in principle be dualized** to obtain minimal forts, so the prior work is very close. Its inspected mathematical statement and proof do not supply the proposed explicit inclusion-minimal blocker classification or its all-length rational count. The asserted addition is the minimized blocker enumeration and one-parity exception, not a new recognition algorithm for ordinary zero forcing. Known minimal-domination path counts are likewise used, not rebranded. This distinction is susceptible to a priority judgment that regards complete forcing-family description as implicit coverage.

## Value — PASS

A minimal-fort hypergraph is the inclusion-minimal obstruction system behind zero forcing, not just a scalar optimum. The fan has an infinite, natural, non-tree family with two competing structural types; attaching one universal vertex creates a second family whose count is governed by minimal dominating sets, changing its enumerative growth from the Padovan-type path count. The exact rational census and parity obstruction provide interpretable structural and enumerative information not supplied by the ordinary forcing-set polynomial's coefficients.
