For every fan \(K_1\vee P_n\), inclusion-minimal forts are exactly the \(00\)-free, \(111\)-free endpoint-selected path patterns and universal-vertex extensions of minimal path dominating sets, except for one alternating odd-path exception. Their numbers have the exact rational generating function \(\frac{1+z+z^2-z^5}{1-z^2-z^3-z^4+z^6}+\frac{z+z^2}{1-z^2-z^3}-\frac{z}{1-z^2}\), with the constant and linear coefficients equal to one.
