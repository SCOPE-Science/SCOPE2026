Every one-leaf cycle corona \(C_n\circ K_1\) has exactly \(n(n+1)/2\) minimal forts for odd \(n\), or \(n(n-2)/2+2\) for even \(n\), classified by connected distance-two rim supports. A six-cycle example also shows that a previously asserted general lifting of minimal cycle forts to corona forts need not preserve minimality.
