The inclusion-minimal forts of the triangular graph \(J(n,2)\) are exactly nonspanning simple cycles and spanning star forests without isolated vertices, viewed as edge sets of \(K_n\). This dichotomy yields the complete cardinality enumerator \(n![u^n]\exp(u(e^{zu}-1)-zu^2/2)+\frac12\sum_{k=3}^{n-1}\binom nk(k-1)!z^k\), for every \(n\ge3\).
