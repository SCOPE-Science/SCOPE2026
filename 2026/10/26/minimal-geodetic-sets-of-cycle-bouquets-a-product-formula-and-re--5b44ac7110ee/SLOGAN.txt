In a bouquet of \(t\ge2\) cycles, all inclusion-minimal geodetic sets arise independently from antipodes and admissible vertex pairs on individual petals, and their exact polynomial is \(\mathcal M_B(z)=\prod_i\bigl(\binom{\lceil n_i/2\rceil}{2}z^2+\mathbf1_{2\mid n_i}z\bigr)\). The polynomial is real-rooted, and its minimum-to-maximum minimal-set cardinality gap equals the number of even petals.
