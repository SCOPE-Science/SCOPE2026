All inclusion-minimal skew forts of the complete prism \(K_n\square K_2\), for \(n\ge3\), belong to three explicit families, yielding a closed polynomial for **every** skew-zero-forcing initial set. The minimum skew forcing number is \(n\) for \(n\ge4\), with exactly \(2n^3-3n^2+n+2\) minimum sets when \(n\ge5\); the exceptional orders are treated separately.
