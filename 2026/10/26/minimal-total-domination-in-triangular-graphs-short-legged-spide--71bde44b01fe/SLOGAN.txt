Every inclusion-minimal total dominating set of the triangular graph \(T(n)\), \(n\ge3\), corresponds either to a spanning disjoint union of three-vertex paths or to a forest of short-legged spiders with one unused ground vertex. This gives an exact bivariate enumerator of all minimal sets and \(\Gamma_t(T(n))=\max\{2,n-2\}\); the previously known minimum-size identity is not claimed as new.
