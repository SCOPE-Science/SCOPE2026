The minimum-edge-cover exchange graph of \(K_{n+1,n}\) is the line graph of the permutation star-transposition network, and its exact diameter is \(\lfloor3n/2\rfloor\) for every \(n\ge2\). By contrast, every optimal cover of balanced \(K_{n,n}\) is exchange-isolated: a single additional vertex on one side changes the optimum reconfiguration graph from discrete to connected.
