## Checks

- Recomputed the square and arithmetic genus of \(D=6F+3O+G\) from the stated intersection matrix.
- Reconstructed the family of section classes \(S_w\) and checked the exact intersection identity used in the lower bound.
- Checked nefness against vertical curves, classes of nonnegative square, and every root class in the lattice.
- The basepoint-free step uses the standard K3 criterion and an explicit exclusion of isotropic classes with intersection one.

## Limits

The argument assumes the full lattice is \(U\oplus\langle-4\rangle\) and does not address specializations with extra algebraic classes. Independent audit and external validation have not been performed.
