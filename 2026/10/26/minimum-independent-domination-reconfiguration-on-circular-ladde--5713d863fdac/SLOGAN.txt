Minimum independent dominating sets of the prism \(C_n\square K_2\) have a binary-gap exchange geometry: their graph has \(\frac{2n}{k}\binom{k}{n-k}\) vertices and \(2n\binom{k-2}{n-k-1}\) edges, where \(k=2\lceil n/4\rceil\). The graph is always bipartite, is four isolated vertices when \(4\mid n\), and otherwise is connected, reducing to \(C_{2n}\) when \(n\equiv3\pmod4\); its complete degree distribution is given in the result.
