Every minimum Italian dominating function on a one-leaf graph corona is encoded by an almost-dominating subset of the original graph, with two independent label choices at each selected vertex. For the labeled sunlet \(C_n\circ K_1\), the exact optimum count is \(3\cdot2^m\), \(n(m+3)2^m\), or \(n2^{m+1}\) as \(n=3m+r\) has residue \(r=0,1,2\), respectively.
