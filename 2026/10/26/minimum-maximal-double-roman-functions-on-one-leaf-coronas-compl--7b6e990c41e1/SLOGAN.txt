All minimum-weight maximal double Roman functions on a one-leaf corona \(G\circ K_1\) are classified bijectively by minimum dominating sets and subsets that defend vertices outside those sets; this yields an exact optimizer-counting formula. For sunlets \(C_n\circ K_1\), the number is \((2^{\lceil n/3\rceil}-1)\) times \(3\), \(n(n+11)/6\), or \(n\), according as \(n\equiv0,1,2\pmod3\); the scalar minimum weight itself was already implied by prior work.
