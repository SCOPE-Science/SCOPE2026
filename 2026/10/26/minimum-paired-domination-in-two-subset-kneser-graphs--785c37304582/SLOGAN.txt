For every \(n\geq6\), the minimum paired-dominating sets of \(\operatorname{KG}(n,2)\) are exactly three explicit four-edge graph types, yielding \(180\binom n6+315\binom n7+105\binom n8\) labeled optima. The small Petersen boundary has minimum size six and exactly 125 optima in three symmetry orbits.
