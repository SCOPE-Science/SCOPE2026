For every square rook graph \(K_n\square K_n\) with \(n\ge2\), all minimum total Roman \(\{2\}\)-dominating functions are binary and correspond exactly to labeled spanning bipartite forests with \(\lfloor2n/3\rfloor\) nontrivial components. Their complete exact count is given by separate closed formulas for the three residue classes of \(n\) modulo three.
