For a truncated coordinate-cross algebra with branch lengths \(d_i\ge2\), the automorphisms are classified by a general linear action on the square-zero branches, exponent-preserving permutations of longer branches, and explicit branch substitutions and socle shears. Over \(\mathbb F_q\), this gives an exact closed formula for the automorphism-group order for every mixed length vector \(\mathbf d\).
