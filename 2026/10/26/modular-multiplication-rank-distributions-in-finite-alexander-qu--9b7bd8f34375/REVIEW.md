# Mathematical review: modular multiplication-rank distributions

## Correctness
PASS. The bilinear transport law reduces the complete calculation to componentwise multiplication in a free module over the displacement group algebra. In characteristic \(p\), a cyclic \(p\)-Sylow subgroup gives a truncated polynomial chain ring. Explicit valuation counts prove each rank enumerator and the ordered zero-product formula. All four enumerated prime-field examples agree with directly computed multiplication-matrix ranks. The cases \(u=0\), \(v=0\), nonsplitting coefficient fields, and non-Latin input quandles are accounted for.

## Originality
PASS, with residual literature risk. The transport identity is Zhang's Proposition 2.1, not a new result. Published studies of idempotents, existence of zero divisors, and selected extended-quandle zero-divisor graphs do not provide the two full modular rank enumerators and joint zero-product count under cyclic Sylow displacement. In particular, the nonmodular semisimple distributions do not imply formulas in the characteristic dividing the displacement order. Equivalence through a broader unindexed ring-theoretic result cannot be excluded.

## Value
PASS. Operator rank profiles and the ordered zero-product census are natural structural and graph-theoretic invariants. The theorem handles arbitrarily large modular cyclic displacement factors, nonsplit étale factors, and non-Latin input, giving a reusable exact classification rather than an isolated calculation. For the dihedral quandle on \(C_{12}\) over \(\mathbb F_2\), the nontrivial nonsplitting factor and non-Latin kernel jointly contribute to the exact count \(444928\).

## Closest literature and limitations
Zhang (arXiv:2610.08204v1, Proposition 2.1) gives the known transport factorization; Churchill et al. (arXiv:2608.09149v1, Section 6) investigate extended-quandle graph examples; Ta (arXiv:2610.20306v1, Theorem 4.3) analyzes zero-divisor existence; and Elhamdadi–Ta–Virgin (arXiv:2609.03799v1) treat Fourier methods and idempotents. The theorem requires cyclic nontrivial modular Sylow displacement. Independent auditing has not occurred, and an equivalent general result outside the inspected literature remains possible.
