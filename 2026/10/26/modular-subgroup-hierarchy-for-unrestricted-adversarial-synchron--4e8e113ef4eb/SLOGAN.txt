For a two-letter automaton with one full cyclic permutation letter, unrestricted adversarial synchronization is characterized exactly by an increasing chain of modular difference subgroups; the chain reaches the whole state group if and only if the synchronizing player wins. Nonconstant winning second letters exist precisely at composite state counts, and at semiprime order \(n=pq\) their complete labeled counts, including constant maps, are \(p^{p+1}\) for \(p=q\) and \(p q^p+q p^q-pq\) for distinct primes.
