Open-neighbour binary parity dynamics on the circular prism \(C_n\square K_2\) is reversible exactly when \(3\nmid n\); otherwise it has exactly \(2^{2n-4\cdot2^{\nu_2(n)}}\) recurrent states and a sharp two-adic transient. The complete basin and entry-time distributions are explicit, and polynomial-gcd formulas count every temporal period.
