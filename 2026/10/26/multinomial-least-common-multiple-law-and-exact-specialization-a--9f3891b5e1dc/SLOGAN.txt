The least common multiple of every \(r\)-part Gaussian multinomial row has an explicit cyclotomic factorization for all \(r\ge2\), and its value at \(q=1\) is exactly the LCM of the corresponding ordinary multinomial coefficients. A coordinatewise-maximal base-\(p\) carry vector makes the prime-power maxima compatible, even when the cyclotomic exponents exceed one.
