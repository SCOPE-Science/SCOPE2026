For a spherical-harmonic band through degree \(L\), restriction to an equatorial belt of half-width \(\varepsilon\) has exactly \(2L+1,2L-1,\ldots,1\) eigenvalues at successive \(\varepsilon,\varepsilon^3,\ldots,\varepsilon^{2L+1}\) scales, with explicit positive Jacobi-kernel leading coefficients. The uniquely least observable mode has sharp energy coefficient \((2^L/\binom{2L}{L})^2\), giving the precise \(\varepsilon^{-2L}\) condition-number blowup.
