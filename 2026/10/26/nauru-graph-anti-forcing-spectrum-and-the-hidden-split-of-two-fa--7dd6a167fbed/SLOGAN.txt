The Nauru graph \(GP(12,5)\) has anti-forcing polynomial \(6t^5+93t^6+18t^7+3t^8\) and exactly \(25{,}092\) minimum anti-forcing matching–deletion pairs. Its complementary two-factor cycle lengths determine the anti-forcing number, but the \((6,18)\) class splits into two symmetry orbits with different numbers of minimum deletion sets, \(207\) and \(24\).
