For two parallel-line sampling grids whose spacing ratio approaches an integer, a fixed Gaussian ambiguity pair remains a positive phase-invariant distance apart while its magnitude-data discrepancy decays linearly in \(|\varepsilon|\). The exact leading coefficient is \(2\pi\sqrt{I(a)S_m(a)}\), quantifying a near-resonance loss of robustness even though every irrational spacing ratio gives uniqueness.
