The genus-five Howe curves obtained by fiber-product normalization from \(u^2=x^4+1\) and \(v^2=x^4+bx^2+c\) in characteristic three have exactly three Newton-polygon strata, with \(3\)-ranks \(4,3,2\) and a unique rank-two parameter. Their non-generic rank locus is two transverse lines in the smooth two-parameter space, and the exact rational-parameter counts over every \(\mathbb F_{3^m}\) admit uniform formulas with a parity-dependent correction.
