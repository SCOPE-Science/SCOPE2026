Nilpotent partial isometries of a finite star have an exact joint rank–nilpotency-index polynomial: all but \(m(m+1)\) of them are cycle-free partial injections on the leaves, with two explicit hub-crossing corrections. For a uniformly chosen nilpotent star partial isometry, its nilpotency index, centered at \(\frac12\sqrt m\log m\) and divided by \(\sqrt m\), converges to the standard Gumbel law.
