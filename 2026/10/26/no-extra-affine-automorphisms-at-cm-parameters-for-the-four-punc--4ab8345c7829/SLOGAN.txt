For every algebraically closed field and every smooth parameter, the four-punctured elliptic curve \((u^2+u)(v^2+v)=d\) has **exactly** the visible dihedral group of eight regular automorphisms, even where its projective completion has extra automorphisms. Its complete affine quotient is \(\mathbb A^1\) with coordinate \(J=(u^2+u)+(v^2+v)\); the explicit eight-element subgroup itself was previously known.
