Every cyclic six-point metric with an equilateral source triple has sharp simultaneous real-linear Lipschitz extension norm \(\max\{1,2L/(r_0+r_1+r_2)\}\), independently of the spacing between the three new points. For the \(120^\circ\) rotational orbit of any planar point around an equilateral triangle, norm-one extension begins exactly at the three-focus boundary \(\sum_i|x-a_i|=2L\).
