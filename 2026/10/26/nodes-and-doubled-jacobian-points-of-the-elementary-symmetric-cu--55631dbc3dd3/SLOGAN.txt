For the characteristic-two elementary symmetric cubic \(e_3\) in \(n\) variables, whenever \(n\not\equiv2\pmod4\) the projective hypersurface has exactly one ordinary quadratic node at each \(0\)-\(1\) point whose support size is \(1\pmod4\), and no other singular points. Its projective Jacobian scheme is reduced for odd \(n\), but each node is doubled when \(4\mid n\), giving total Jacobian length \(2^{n-1}\) in that case.
