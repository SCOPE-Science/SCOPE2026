## correctness

PASS

For an edge e, V_e squared is the projector onto its two endpoints. On populations the gain term exchanges endpoint occupations and the loss term gives exactly the weighted Laplacian. On the span of E_ij and E_ji all other edges contribute an equal diagonal damping from edges incident to one endpoint; the common edge contributes the matrix with diagonal -w_ij and off-diagonal w_ij. Its symmetric and antisymmetric combinations give exactly r_ij and s_ij. These orthogonal sectors exhaust the operator space. Removing only the identity retains every extra zero mode, and a sufficiently small slow traceless Hermitian perturbation of I/N is a physical density matrix realizing the sharp rate. Averaging the r_ij gives (N-2)B divided by the edge count. The uniform allocation attains it because its population rate is Na and its coherence rate (N-2)a for a=B/binom(N,2). Equality requires every r_ij to equal their mean c, which by summing w_ij=(d_i+d_j)/2-c gives (N-1)c=B+(N-4)d_i/2. Thus all rates are uniform when N is not four. For N=4, the population eigenvectors comparing sites within each of the two opposite pairs and comparing the pair averages have rates 4a+2t, 4a-2t and 4a. The whole displayed closed interval has population gap at least 2a and coherence gap equal to 2a, including its endpoints with a zero edge rate. Strictly positive nonuniform choices occur precisely for 0<|t|<a within this family.
