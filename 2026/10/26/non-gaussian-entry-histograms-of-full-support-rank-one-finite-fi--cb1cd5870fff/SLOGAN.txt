A uniformly random rank-one finite-field matrix with no zero entries has pairwise independent entries, yet its centered nonzero-value histogram converges to an explicitly diagonalized, non-Gaussian Gaussian-product law as both dimensions grow. The one-value limit is product-normal over \(\mathbb F_3\) and Laplace over \(\mathbb F_4\), despite exact finite-size covariance equal to that of independent uniformly distributed entries.
