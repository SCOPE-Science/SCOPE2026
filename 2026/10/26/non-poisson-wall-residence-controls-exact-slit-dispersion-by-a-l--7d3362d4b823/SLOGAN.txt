In a four-direction run-and-tumble slit, arbitrary independent wall residence times change the exact longitudinal diffusivity through the single Laplace-transform value \(\mathbb E[e^{-\beta S}]\) while leaving bulk occupancy dependent only on \(\mathbb ES\). At fixed mean, spreading the wall residence times in convex order strictly enhances diffusion, and varying the surface turning rate recovers the entire wall-time distribution.
