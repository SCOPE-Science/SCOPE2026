The binary four-cube \(Q_4\) has exactly \(1920\) named optimal strong odd four-colorings: \(96\) affine and \(1824\) nonlinear, giving \(80\) color partitions of which \(76\) are nonlinear. A nonlinear four-cube seed extends to every higher even-dimensional binary cube, whereas all optimal four-colorings of \(Q_2\) are affine.
