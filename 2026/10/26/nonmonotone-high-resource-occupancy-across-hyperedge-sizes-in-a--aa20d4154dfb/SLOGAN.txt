In the two-state public-goods hyperedge transition with sensitivities \(\alpha_1=1\), \(\alpha_2=2\), a frozen cooperative population can have *less* high-value resource occupancy in three-person groups than in both two-person groups and sufficiently large groups. The strict ordering already occurs with seven agents and five cooperators, while the fixed-density group-size phase diagram has exact boundaries \(1/\sqrt2\), \(\sqrt3-1\), and \(5/6\).
