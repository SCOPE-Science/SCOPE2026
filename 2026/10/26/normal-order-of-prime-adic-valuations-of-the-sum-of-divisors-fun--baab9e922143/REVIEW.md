# Review

## Correctness (C)
PASS. Multiplicativity reduces the valuation to local prime-power terms. The comparison with the strongly additive function g_ell has bounded mean absolute error because repeated prime powers contribute a convergent sum of O(e log p / p^e). Mertens in reduced residue classes gives the coefficient for the truncated additive function; Turan–Kubilius gives concentration, and the uniform prime-progression tail estimate permits removal of the truncation. The geometric series of reciprocal totients evaluates to \(\ell/(\ell-1)^2\).

## Originality (O)
PASS on the searches performed. The closest recent public note treats exact 5-divisibility and the valuation-one case modulo 25, not a normal-order constant. The full text of arXiv:2007.03088 gives local valuation formulas and pointwise bounds but no normal-order theorem. searches of the public research-record index for the exact normal-order statement and aliases returned no matching claim. A search failure is not proof of novelty; residual literature risk is recorded in AUDIT.json.

## Value (V)
PASS. The coefficient \(\ell/(\ell-1)^2\) gives a precise typical size for a natural arithmetic invariant, sharpening a density-one divisibility statement into a quantitative normal-order law. The constant has an explicit local interpretation through the nested prime classes p ≡ −1 (mod ell^j).

Closest literature: Amdeberhan–Moll–Sharma–Villamizar (2020), local valuation formulas; the public 2026-10-04 note on exact 5-divisibility. The result is for fixed ell and leaves explicit exceptional-set rates and distributional fluctuations open.
