# Review

## Correctness

PASS. The normalization chart is obtained by adjoining \(t=y/x\) with \(t^2=q\); the quotient is generated by the class of \(t\), annihilated by \((x,r^2)\), and injectivity follows from the even/odd decomposition in \(k[t,r]/(r^2)\). The symmetric chart gives transition \(q^{-1}\), identifying the quotient with the pullback of \(\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1}(-1)\). The filtration and \(h^1=1\) follow from the standard cohomology of \(\mathbb P^1\).

## Originality

PASS with residual risk. The closest inspected source is Schröer’s treatment of the superspecial case. It establishes nonnormality, the common normalization, the conductor, and the Frobenius map on reduced fibers, but does not explicitly state the full normalization-defect quotient module or its filtration. Related normalization/conductor literature found in searches concerns different varieties and does not imply this exact module. Historical cited works were not all inspected in full.

## Value

PASS. The quotient module refines a set-theoretic description of the nonnormal locus to an exact coherent sheaf, which determines the nilpotent filtration and its first cohomology. This is a natural local invariant for comparing the two crepant partial resolutions, not a routine numerical restatement.
