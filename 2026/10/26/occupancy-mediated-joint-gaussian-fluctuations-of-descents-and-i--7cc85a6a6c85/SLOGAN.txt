After \(r_n=\lfloor cn\rfloor\) uniform random-to-top moves, appropriately scaled descent and inversion counts converge jointly to a Gaussian with strictly positive covariance \(e^{-c}[e^{-c}-(1+c)e^{-2c}]/4\) for each fixed \(c>0\). Their conditional-order Gaussian noises are asymptotically independent; all limiting cross-covariance is generated by fluctuations in the number of distinct cards moved.
