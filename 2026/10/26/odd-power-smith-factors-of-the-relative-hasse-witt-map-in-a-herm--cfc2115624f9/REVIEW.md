# Scientific review: odd-power relative Hasse–Witt Smith factors

## Correctness — PASS
The relative Hodge basis and binomial Cartier expansion yield a matrix over \(\mathbb F_p[a]\). A decomposition into residue-graded rectangular blocks is exact, including all zero columns. Each size-\(t\) block minor has coefficient-parameter order at least \(t^2\), and the upper-left size-\(t\) determinant is a nonzero factorial Vandermonde scalar, valid since \(2t-1<p\). Differences \(t^2-(t-1)^2=2t-1\) produce every invariant factor and explicit counting gives their multiplicities. The packaged verifier independently reconstructs the entries and tests eight odd primes. These finite tests are secondary to the universal proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
Almeida Filho–Tafazolian–Vieira, arXiv:2610.17476, Theorem 8.4 and Corollary 8.5, already settle the pointwise rank difference for the **same** one-parameter family. No rank or a-number jump is claimed as new. Their closest block-rank argument in Section 8 was compared directly: it does not state the coefficient-line Smith orders of all minors. Pries–Weir handle the Hermitian special fibre; Bouw and coauthors the broader additive curve family; Farnell–Pries have an inapplicable congruence hypothesis. Searches of indexed research records and recoverable ledger claims did not find an equivalent complete Fitting-order theorem. Unlocated implicit proofs remain a risk; searches alone cannot certify novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The odd-power profile measures scheme-theoretic degeneracy of relative Frobenius. It is a complete uniform answer over the natural coefficient line and is not determined by geometric fibre ranks. In characteristic seven, for example, the maximal minor ideal has order eight although all nonzero parameters give the same generic rank. These higher orders carry structural information about infinitesimal deformation that the known a-number table cannot recover.

## Scope and limitations
The scheme structure depends on the chosen coefficient parameter and must not be transported as an intrinsic statement on the moduli stack without further analysis. No statement about full Ekedahl–Oort types or arbitrary additive polynomial families is made. Some historical sources and unavailable earlier private records could hide related results. Independent validation has not occurred.
