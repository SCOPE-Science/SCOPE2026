The coefficient-line relative Hasse–Witt map of \(y^p-y=x^{p+1}+ax^2\), for every odd prime \(p\), has Smith factors \(a^{2j-1}\) with exact multiplicity \(p+2-4j\). This determines every determinantal ideal and the infinitesimal torsion thickness at the Hermitian special fibre, refining previously known pointwise Cartier ranks.
