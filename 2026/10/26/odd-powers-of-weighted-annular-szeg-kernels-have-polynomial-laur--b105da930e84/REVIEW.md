# Scientific review

## Correctness
**PASS.** The theorem is fully quantified for positive annulus parameters and odd integer exponents. The proof determines the principal parts of the meromorphic theta kernel, constructs a finite triangular coefficient polynomial, and excludes every remaining global holomorphic quasi-periodic function because its Laurent multipliers cannot equal a negative real number. Strict integer-index coefficient positivity follows from the Cauchy product. The exact cubic expression and thin-annulus coefficient are derived without numerical inference. The shipped standalone checker passed 123 complex-kernel/cubic comparisons, along with auxiliary positivity and leading-term checks.

## Originality
**PASS on inspected sources; historical priority remains provisional.** Katori and Shirai (arXiv:2008.04177v1) define the identical Gaussian covariance powers and explicitly indicate the absence of explicit Laurent series for \(L\ge3\). They provide the exponent-two solution but not the present odd-power polynomial numerator. Gafai, Murid and Wahid (arXiv:2202.09114v1) give bilateral/product representations for the first-power kernel; McCullough and Shen provide earlier functional equations. Neither source, in its inspected relevant statements, states a general all-odd finite Euler-derivative reduction or the exact cubic numerator. An unevaluated convolution is strictly weaker than this identity. Elliptic-function identities could furnish an unrecognized equivalent reformulation, and the original archival paper was not fully inspected visually.

## Value
**PASS.** The result addresses a specifically identified analytic random-function coefficient gap, extends it to all odd exponents and positive weights, and gives independent-Gaussian simulation coefficients with a finite-recursion algorithm. A sharp thin-annulus scale further characterizes the distinguished cubic correction.

## Boundaries
The theorem is not an even-power result. Positivity concerns integer indices only. No new point-process correlation law, complete historical uniqueness, or independent validation is claimed. The finite computations are reproducibility checks, not universal proof certificates.
