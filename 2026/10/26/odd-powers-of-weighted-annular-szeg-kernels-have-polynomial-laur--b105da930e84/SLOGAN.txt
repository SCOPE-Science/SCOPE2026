Every odd power of a weighted annular Szegő kernel has an explicit single Laurent series whose coefficients are degree-\(L-1\) polynomials in the Fourier index divided by \(1+r^Lq^{2n}\). In the conformally invariant cubic case, the numerator is exactly \(\binom{n+2}{2}+3\sum_{k\ge0}(2k+1)q^{2k+1}/(1+q^{2k+1})\), yielding direct independent-Gaussian Laurent coefficients for a higher-power annular random analytic function.
