For odd truncated quadratic-Galois Ore rings \(\mathbb F_{s^2}[x;\sigma]/(x^{2a+1})\), element commutation is exactly the vanishing of three exterior minors over \(\mathbb F_s[z]/(z^a)\). This identifies every centralizer order and gives commuting probability \(s^{-3a}+s^{-2a}(1+s^{-1}+s^{-2})(1-s^{-a})\), uniformly across characteristics.
