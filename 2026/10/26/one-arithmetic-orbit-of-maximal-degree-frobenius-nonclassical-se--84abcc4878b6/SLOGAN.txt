All geometrically smooth degree-six Frobenius-nonclassical plane curves over \(\mathbf F_4\) form a single \(\operatorname{PGL}_3(\mathbf F_4)\)-orbit of exactly \(960\) curves, each with rational projective stabilizer of order \(63\). They have no \(\mathbf F_4\)-points, and one explicit representative is \(xyz(x^3+y^3+z^3)+(\alpha+1)x^6+\alpha y^6+z^6=0\) for \(\alpha^2+\alpha+1=0\).
