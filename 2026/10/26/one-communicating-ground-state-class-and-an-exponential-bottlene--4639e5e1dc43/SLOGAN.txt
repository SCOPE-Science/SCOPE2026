For fully asynchronous stochastic minority dynamics on \(K_{m,m,m}\), all \(2^{3m}\) Boolean configurations ultimately enter a single recurrent class of exactly \(6(2^m-1)\) states, arranged as six Boolean hypercubes joined at monochromatic junctions. Its stationary law is uniform, yet rare junctions force a total-variation mixing time of at least \(2^{m-1}\) updates for \(m\ge2\).
