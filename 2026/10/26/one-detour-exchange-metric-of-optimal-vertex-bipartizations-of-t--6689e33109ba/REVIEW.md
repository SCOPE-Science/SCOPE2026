# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The two standalone exact enumeration algorithms test every deletion mask up to size six, every legal optimum-preserving exchange, and all 3,240 breadth-first shortest-path trees, independently reproducing 3,240 optima, 31,800 exchange edges, diameter six, 67,480 ordered detour pairs, and the full ten-cell distance table. Their graph presentations are linked by an explicit checked isomorphism. The exhaustive search is a finite proof, not an extrapolation; checking source files and recreating their records remains reproducible in Python. The vertex-vs-edge deletion distinction and labels are explicit.

## Originality — PASS with residual risks
The previously studied scalar induced-bipartite extremal property of the dodecahedral graph is credited rather than treated as new. The 2012 full-text Faria–Klein–Stehlík paper concerns edge removals, not minimum vertex transversals or their exchange distances. General planar vertex transversal theorems and generalized token-jumping results do not furnish this exact 3,240-state metric. Actual mathematical-record searches for the graph, object, counts, aliases, and Johnson-distance formulation found related graph homology and ambient-hypersimplex facts but no result implying this constrained metric. The Zhu full text was inaccessible, and differently indexed tables may contain the same information; this novelty assessment is best knowledge, not proof of priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS
This is the full exact exchange geometry of optimum bipartizations of the canonical exceptional fullerene. Its nonuniform state degrees, self-centered radius six, remarkably sharp one-detour bound, and precise obstruction count identify a substantive robustness phenomenon beyond a known minimum-cardinality invariant. A concrete violation of matroid basis exchange shows that the near-Johnson metric does not follow from a generic matroid theorem. The result is intentionally a finite, natural, fully classified object; no claim of extension to all fullerenes is made.
