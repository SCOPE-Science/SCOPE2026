Deleting just one edge from \(J(n,k)\) with even \(n\ge4\) and even \(2\le k\le n-2\) changes nilpotent XOR dynamics into a system with exactly one fixed point, one three-cycle and two six-cycles, all reached within two updates. The sixteen periodic states are universal, and the exact number of configurations periodic by one update is \(2^{\binom nk-\binom{n-2}{k-1}+2}\).
