For a positive-definite \(3\times3\) matrix, permitting one additional entry in a Frobenius-optimal sparse approximate inverse strictly decreases the residual yet can make the preconditioner singular; no such one-entry failure is possible in dimensions below three. An arbitrarily small SPD perturbation makes both preconditioners nonsingular while the enriched preconditioned condition number diverges like \(\varepsilon^{-1}\), despite continuing strict residual improvement.
