For a qubit Hamiltonian \(H_j=\mathbf n_j\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma\), where \(\|\mathbf n_j\|=1\), if the unknown evolution time is uniformly distributed on \([0,\pi]\), the two Hamiltonians have equal prior probability, and only one query is allowed, then the optimal average success probability over all probes (including ancillas) is \(P_{\rm succ}=\frac12+\frac14\sqrt{1-(\mathbf n_0\cdot\mathbf n_1)^2}\). The formula interpolates between no information for parallel or antiparallel axes and success probability \(3/4\) for orthogonal axes.
