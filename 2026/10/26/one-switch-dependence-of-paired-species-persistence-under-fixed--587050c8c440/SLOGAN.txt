Under uniform fixed-quota partner survival, the covariance of two dependent species' persistence can change from positive to negative as the number \(q\) of retained partners increases, but it can switch signs at most once. The exact dividing condition is \(N|A\cap B|>|A||B|\) for a strict partial overlap, and five partners are necessary and sufficient for a reversal; by contrast, independent partner survival always gives nonnegative covariance.
