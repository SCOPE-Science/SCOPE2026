For every odd polygon order \(k\ge3\), the semigroups generated by three consecutive centered \(k\)-gonal numbers with starting index \(n\ge k\) are symmetric at exactly one index, \(n=(3k+7)/2\). At this index their Frobenius number is the explicit quintic \((27k^5+432k^4+2058k^3+3912k^2+2779k+328)/64\), with genus one-half greater than half this value.
