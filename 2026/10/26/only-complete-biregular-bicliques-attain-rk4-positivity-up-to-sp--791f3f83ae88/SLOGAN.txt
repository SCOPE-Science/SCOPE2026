For every connected unweighted biregular bipartite graph of degree pair \((a,b)\), classical RK4 diffusion is entrywise nonnegative up to the spectral-stability limit \(\rho/(a+b)\) exactly for complete bipartite graphs; every incomplete graph instead has the sharp cutoff \(2/(a+b)\). The universal spectral-to-positivity ratio \(\rho/2\) in every incomplete graph is witnessed by a three-step opposite-class nonedge, and positivity never returns beyond either cutoff.
