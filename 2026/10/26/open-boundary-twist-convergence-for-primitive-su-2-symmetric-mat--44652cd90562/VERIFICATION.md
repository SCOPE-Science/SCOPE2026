The claim was checked from the stated transfer-matrix assumptions. The twist increment is \(\delta=4\pi/L\); covariance reduces the site-dependent twist to a constant virtual insertion. Simplicity of the dominant eigenvalue gives analytic perturbation, and invariance of the unique virtual fixed point implies \(\operatorname{Tr}(\rho G)=0\). Therefore \(\lambda(\delta)=1+O(\delta^2)\), so \(\lambda(4\pi/L)^L=1+O(L^{-1})\). The leading boundary coefficient is analytic and nonzero, giving only an additional \(O(L^{-1})\) term.

Limits checked: the result requires exact \(SU(2)\) covariance, primitive transfer, fixed open boundaries, and a nonzero leading norm coefficient. It does not determine the leading constant or extend to arbitrary gapped open-chain ground states. Independent audit has not been performed.
