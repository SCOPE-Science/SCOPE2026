On a complete two-level finite Alexandrov space with \(p\) lower and \(q\) upper points, the open continuous selfmaps form \(T_p\times S_q\) together with \(q\) upper constant maps when \(q\ge2\), yielding exactly \(p^p q!+q\) maps. When \(q=1\), the monoid changes to all \((p+1)^p\) selfmaps fixing the unique top point.
