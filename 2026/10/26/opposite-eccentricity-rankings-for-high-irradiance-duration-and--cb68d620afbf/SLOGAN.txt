At fixed semimajor axis, the Kepler orbit spending the largest fraction of its year above any prescribed super-circular stellar-flux threshold has the unique eccentricity \(e_*=\sqrt{1-\sqrt{F_0/F_\mathrm c}}\). Beyond this eccentricity, greater eccentricity **shortens high-flux exposure** while it **strictly increases the integrated excess irradiance** above the same threshold.
