On every real unweighted Cartesian product of two nontrivial stars, the canonical cut and cycle projections have exact two-branch endpoint norms with **different** edge-orbit transition curves. On the infinite family \(K_{1,3}\mathbin{\square} K_{1,n}\) with \(n\ge7\), the two complementary projections attain their norms on opposite edge directions, and their sharp global suprema over all factor sizes are \(5\) and \(6\).
