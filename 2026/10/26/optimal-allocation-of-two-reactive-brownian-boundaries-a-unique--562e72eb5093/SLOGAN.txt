For a Brownian particle between two partially absorbing endpoints, fixing the sum of endpoint reactivities yields exactly one mean-capture-time-optimal split, always with both endpoints active—even when the particle starts at one endpoint. Its optimal left fraction crosses over from \(3/4-y/2\) at weak total reactivity to \(\sqrt{1-y}/(\sqrt{1-y}+\sqrt y)\) at strong total reactivity, with a sharp square-root budget allocation to the distant endpoint in the boundary-start case.
