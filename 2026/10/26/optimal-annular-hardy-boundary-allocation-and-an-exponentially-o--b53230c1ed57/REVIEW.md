# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** With angular probability measures on each boundary component, the Laurent coefficient Gram diagonal and evaluation formula follow exactly from Parseval. The paired positive-negative Fourier modes yield an everywhere strictly positive weight Hessian and divergence at both endpoints, ensuring a unique global optimum. Differentiating the paired series establishes monotonicity in radius and reflection symmetry. A computed beta-integral Fourier transform and justified Poisson summation give the exponentially small first harmonic; the stationary-envelope Hessian fixes the optimizer's correction coefficient, while a uniform relative bound keeps the global minimizer in the perturbative region. The thick-annulus leading objective is an exact two-mode convex problem and its remainder is controlled. All quantifiers and normalization are explicit. Numerical verification is supplementary: 270 ordinary numerical assertions passed, together with high-precision exponentially small bias checks. Risks: arithmetic cancellation of the first Fourier term at isolated thicknesses does not invalidate the stated additive error; no claim of relative asymptotic is made there.

**Originality — PASS, provisionally.** Gafai–Murid–Wahid (arXiv:2202.09114v1, §§2 and 5) already give the weighted annular Szegő bilateral series and special-function expressions; that formula is used as a known starting point, not renamed as a new result. Jain–Verma (arXiv:2308.07021v1, introductory theorems and §2) address smoothness and dependence on weights; Ameur–Charlier–Cronvall (doi:10.1007/s10955-024-03314-8, §3) derive oscillatory annular kernels in random-matrix asymptotics. None of their inspected statements optimizes the diagonal evaluation norm over the total outer/inner boundary mass, determines global uniqueness and radial monotonicity of the optimal design, or states the beyond-all-orders *optimization bias* and logistic thick-annulus crossover. Closest separately relevant work on conformal zero computation (doi:10.3934/math.2023607) similarly does not address boundary-mass optimization. Historical-priority uncertainty and possible equivalent formulations in uninspected work remain explicit risks; finite searches cannot certify novelty.

**Value — PASS.** This is an all-parameter, sharp two-boundary allocation problem for a natural reproducing-kernel functional, not an arbitrary data slice. The thin-annulus harmonic-measure principle comes with a surprisingly nonperturbative signed correction, exhibiting infinitely many reversals not visible to algebraic asymptotic orders; the thick-annulus logistic transition identifies a second universal scale. These results give principled boundary-sampling allocations and resolve their quantitative stability limits.

**Limits and closest published material.** Weighted kernel formulas, theta functions, and annular Fourier decompositions are previously established; only the stated mass-optimization laws and their proofs are assessed as new. No results for nonconstant boundary density, general connectivity, or human-independent certification are claimed.
