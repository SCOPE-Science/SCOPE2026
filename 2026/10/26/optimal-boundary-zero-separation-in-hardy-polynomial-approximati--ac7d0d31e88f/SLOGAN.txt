Among all Hardy degree-\(n\) polynomial approximations to reciprocals of quadratic factors with two unit-circle zeros, the asymptotically optimal angular separation is uniquely \(7.54031418959\ldots/(n+3)\), with sharp scaled squared error \(1.79126916076\ldots\). The universal sinc crossover has infinitely many interference windows in which two nearby zeros are easier to approximate than a fixed separated pair.
