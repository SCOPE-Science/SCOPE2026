For every \(n\ge6\), the crown graph \(K_{n,n}\) minus a perfect matching has edge-locating chromatic number exactly \(n+1\): its optimal edge colorings arise from modular Latin rectangles with two local swaps when \(3\mid n+1\). The undeleted balanced biclique requires \(n+2\) colors, so deleting its perfect matching saves exactly one.
