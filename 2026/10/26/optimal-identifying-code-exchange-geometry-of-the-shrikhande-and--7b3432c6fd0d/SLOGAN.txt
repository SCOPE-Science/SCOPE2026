The Shrikhande graph has exactly \(96\) minimum identifying codes of size \(5\), forming sixteen disjoint six-cycles under optimal one-sensor replacement. Its cospectral \(4\times4\) rook-graph companion has \(2208\) minimum codes of size \(6\), whose replacement graph is connected with diameter \(6\), showing that identical strongly regular parameters do not determine optimal identifying-code mobility.
