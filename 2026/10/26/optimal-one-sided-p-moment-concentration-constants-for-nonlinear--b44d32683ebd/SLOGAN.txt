For every \(1<p\leq2\), the universal one-sided concentration exponent under conditional \(p\)-moment deficits has an explicit *best possible* coefficient \(c_p\), determined by a unique scalar exponential-remainder maximizer. Rare independent Bernoulli deficits attain its asymptotic obstruction even though the inequality applies to arbitrary nonlinear functions of independent inputs; as \(p\downarrow1\), the sharp coefficient approaches one while the previous coefficient approaches zero.
