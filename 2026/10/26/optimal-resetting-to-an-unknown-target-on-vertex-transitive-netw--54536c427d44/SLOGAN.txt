For a hidden uniformly chosen target on any finite vertex-transitive network, resetting to uniformly chosen vertices uniquely minimizes both mean and worst-target search time at every positive geometric resetting rate; every nonuniform rule pays an exact resolvent-weighted heterogeneity penalty. The jointly optimal rate has an exact spectral criterion, and on simple cycles nonzero resetting is beneficial if and only if there are at least five vertices, with the five-cycle optimum \(\gamma_*=5-2\sqrt5\).
