For bounded monotone data, the unique symmetric four-node cubic interpolation grid minimizing worst-case full-interval range escape has interior nodes \(\pm a_*\), where \(a_*\approx0.4458209225\); its sharp defect is \(0.1296867734\ldots\). This improves the Chebyshev–Lobatto four-node defect \(0.1760509825\ldots\) because it balances two distinct step-data extremizers.
