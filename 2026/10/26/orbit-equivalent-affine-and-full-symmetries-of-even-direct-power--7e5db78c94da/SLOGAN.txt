For each \(k,d\ge2\), the full automorphism group of the even dihedral product \(R_{2^k}^{\,d}\) and its canonical affine subgroup have exactly the same \(k+1\) ordered-pair orbits, yet the full group is larger by the factor \(2^{d(2^d-d-1)}\). In particular, \(R_4\times R_4\) has \(6144\) automorphisms, four times its affine group, without any new binary orbital relation.
