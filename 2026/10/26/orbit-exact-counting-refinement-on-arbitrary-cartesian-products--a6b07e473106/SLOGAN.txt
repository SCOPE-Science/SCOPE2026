Unmarked counting colour refinement recovers every automorphism orbit of an arbitrary Cartesian product of stars \(\square_i K_{1,p_i}\), where \(p_i\ge2\), in at most \(3k+1\) rounds for \(k\) factors, independently of the leaf counts. If \(m_p\) factors have leaf count \(p\), the stable partition has exactly \(\prod_p(m_p+1)\) classes, with sizes \(\prod_p\binom{m_p}{j_p}p^{j_p}\).
