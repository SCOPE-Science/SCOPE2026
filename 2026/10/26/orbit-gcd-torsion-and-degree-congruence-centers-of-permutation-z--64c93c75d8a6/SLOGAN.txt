For a Zhang twist of a free commutative monoid by any finite permutation, the fraction-group abelianization is exactly \(\mathbb Z^c\oplus\mathbb Z/g\mathbb Z\), where \(c\) is the number of permutation cycles and \(g\) their length gcd. Its center consists precisely of vectors constant on cycles whose total degree is divisible by the permutation order, giving center index \(m/g\) in the fixed lattice.
