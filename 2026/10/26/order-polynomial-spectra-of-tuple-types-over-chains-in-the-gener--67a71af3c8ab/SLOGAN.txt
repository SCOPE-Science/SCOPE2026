Over an individually named \(n\)-element chain in the generic homogeneous poset, the number of injective ordered \(r\)-tuple types is an explicit positive sum of order polynomials of \(2r\)-element endpoint posets, hence a polynomial of exact degree \(2r\). At arity two the count is \(\frac{(n+1)(n+2)(2n^2+8n+9)}6\), with the strict-order and incomparability proportions tending to \(1/4,1/4,1/2\).
