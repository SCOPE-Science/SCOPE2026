In the Asgarli–Ghioca degree-\(p+3\) plane-filling family over prime fields, the smooth members for \(p=3\) and \(p=5\) are ordinary, but both smooth members for \(p=7\) have \(7\)-rank \(35\) and \(a\)-number \(1\) in genus \(36\). This gives a natural first nonordinary boundary within the first three odd prime-field cases.
