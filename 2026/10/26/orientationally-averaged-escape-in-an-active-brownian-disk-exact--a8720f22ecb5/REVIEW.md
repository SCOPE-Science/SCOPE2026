# Review of the quadratic activity response of disk escape

**Correctness — PASS.** The normalized stochastic generator, absorbing boundary and angular average are explicit in `RESULT.md`. Exact angular harmonic decomposition gives the previously established first-order Bessel solution and the identity \(G_\gamma'=-f_\gamma/2\); radial integration yields the claimed coefficient. Positivity of the modified Bessel Dirichlet resolvent proves strictly negative response and strict increase in \(\gamma\). Uniform Bessel-ratio asymptotics establish the \(\gamma^{-1/2}\) wall layer without inferring an infinite-parameter result from finite tests. The independent calculations in `verify.py` are reproducible diagnostics, not replacements for proof.

**Originality — PASS, with risk.** Di Trapani–Franosch–Caraglio (arXiv:2306.12702v1) derive the propagator and second-order first-passage distribution and thus implicitly encode MFPT, but do not isolate its explicit isotropic quadratic coefficient or strict parameter and sharp boundary-layer laws. Iyaniwura–Peng (arXiv:2506.15813v1) already derive the first-order Bessel harmonic (their Eq. 12); it is **not** presented as new. They identify the second-order response as the first nonzero angular-average term but do not calculate or asymptotically classify it in the inspected main article. The 1-D second-order results of arXiv:2310.04446 concern another model. General resolvent methods and undiscovered supplementary results are the principal residual coverage risks.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The leading uniform-orientation activity coefficient controls a natural measurable mean escape time. Its exact sign and global rotational-diffusion monotonicity go beyond finite examples. Its universal wall-layer function quantifies a genuine failure of the bulk effective-diffusion expansion near the absorbing wall, and provides a precise crossover length and profile in the coefficient itself. The result does not establish a uniform expansion when propulsion and rotational rate scale simultaneously.
