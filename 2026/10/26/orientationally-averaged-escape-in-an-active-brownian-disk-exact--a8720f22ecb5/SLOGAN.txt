For a weakly self-propelled Brownian particle in an absorbing disk with uniformly randomized starting orientation, the first nonzero activity correction to mean exit time is an explicit negative Bessel-function coefficient, increasing strictly with rotational diffusivity. At fast turning, a universal \(1-e^{-z}-z\) absorbing-wall profile appears on the \(\gamma^{-1/2}\) distance scale, beyond bulk effective diffusion.
