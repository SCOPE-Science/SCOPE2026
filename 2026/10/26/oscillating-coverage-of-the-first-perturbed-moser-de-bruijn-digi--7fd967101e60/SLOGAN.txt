The Moser–de Bruijn set \(M\) represents every nonnegative integer uniquely as \(x+2y\), but the first nontrivial locally injective coefficient six produces \(M+6M\) with unique representations and no natural density. Exactly half the integers below \(4^L\), versus asymptotically two thirds below \(2\cdot4^L\), belong to this weighted sumset.
