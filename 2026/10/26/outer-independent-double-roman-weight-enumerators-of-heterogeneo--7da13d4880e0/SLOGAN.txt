For every clique bouquet \(K_1\vee(\bigsqcup_{i=1}^t K_{m_i})\) with \(t\ge2\) and \(m_i\ge2\), four hub-label cases give an exact product formula counting every outer-independent double Roman labeling by its weight. The minimum weight is \(3+\sum_i(m_i-1)\), attained by exactly \(\prod_i m_i\) labelings, and the next-weight coefficient has a two-blade-only correction.
