For every analytic degree \(n\) greater than coanalytic degree \(m\), a Gaussian line-restriction construction produces harmonic polynomials with at least the parity-admissible ceiling of \(n(1+\sqrt m)\) distinct zeros, improving the earlier \(n\sqrt m\) existence scale. The additional escaping roots have an exact arithmetic two-point count law, and the bound yields at least \(251\) zeros already at degrees \((77,5)\).
