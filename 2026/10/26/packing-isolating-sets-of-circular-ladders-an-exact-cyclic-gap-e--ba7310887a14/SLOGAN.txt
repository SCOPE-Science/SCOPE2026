Packing-isolating vertex sets of the circular ladder \(C_n\square K_2\) exist for all \(n\ge4\) except \(n=7\); they are exactly the alternating-row cyclic placements with successive column gaps two or three. Their complete size-refined generating function is \(\frac{2tu^2(t^2+t^3)(2t+3t^2)}{1-u^2(t^2+t^3)^2}\), giving explicit binomial coefficients for every size and circumference.
