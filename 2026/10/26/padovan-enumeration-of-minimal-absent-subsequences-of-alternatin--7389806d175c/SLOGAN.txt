For the alternating binary word \((01)^k\), the total number of minimal absent scattered subsequences has the Padovan generating function \(z(3-z+2z^2)/(1-2z+z^2-z^3)\) and grows exponentially as \(1.754877666\ldots^k\). In contrast, the number of *shortest* absent subsequences is exactly \(k+2\), yielding an explicit exponential-versus-linear separation on a binary alphabet.
