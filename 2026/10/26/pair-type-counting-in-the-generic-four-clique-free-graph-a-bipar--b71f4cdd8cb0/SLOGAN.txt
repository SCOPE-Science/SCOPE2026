For bipartite parameter graphs, the generic four-clique-free Henson graph has only \(2^n\) nonalgebraic one-types but \(4^n+3^n I_G(1/3)\) distinct ordered two-point types outside the parameters. Exact counting of these two-point types is \(\#\mathrm P\)-complete under polynomial-time Turing reductions even on bipartite parameters, exposing a sharp arity-two complexity jump.
