For quadrilateral books \(B_r=K_{1,r}\square K_2\) with \(r\ge2\), the full paired-domination polynomial is \(z^2(1+z^2)^r+r(r-1)z^4(1+z^2)^{r-2}+(2r+1)z^{2r}\). Its inclusion-minimal sets have only sizes \(2\) and \(2r\), yielding arbitrarily many missing even sizes in a planar bipartite two-vertex-connected graph family.
