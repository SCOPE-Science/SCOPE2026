For every incomplete \(m\)-step symmetric Lanczos approximation of a positive diagonal semigroup started at a strictly positive vector, the largest-eigenvalue coordinate remains positive for all times if \(m\) is odd, but has exactly one finite sign transition if \(m\) is even. The transition time decreases strictly with the extrapolation eigenvalue above the Ritz spectrum; even the sign-protected odd-depth approximation suffers exponentially growing relative error in that extreme mode.
