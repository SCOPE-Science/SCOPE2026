For unweighted discrete Huygens rings, the known global-diffeomorphism range \(0<h<1/\Lambda_n\) is sharp at **every** length: the first singular locus comprises one antipodal configuration for even \(n\) and \(n\) one-zero, otherwise antipodal configurations for odd \(n\). Immediately above threshold, each singular target gains two additional local preimages by an explicit cubic branch law with amplitudes \(\sqrt{6n(h\Lambda_n-1)}\) (even) or \(\sqrt{4n(h\Lambda_n-1)}\) (odd).
