For geometric runner speeds \(1,b,\ldots,b^{m-1}\), the exact near-critical safe-time measure decays by \(1/b\) with every additional runner: the surviving set has one interval when \(b\) is odd and two when \(b\) is even. The sharp maximum loneliness is \(1/2\) in odd base and \(b/(2(b+1))\) in even base, with all maximizers determined.
