For clique bouquets \(K_1\vee\bigsqcup_i K_{r_i}\), every globally minimal defensive alliance is either a majority-sized subset of exactly one peripheral clique or the apex together with precisely \(\lfloor r_i/2\rfloor\) vertices from each clique. The apex-containing family exists exactly when at most one clique has odd order, giving an explicit all-size counting polynomial and unbounded gaps between the smallest and largest globally minimal alliance sizes.
