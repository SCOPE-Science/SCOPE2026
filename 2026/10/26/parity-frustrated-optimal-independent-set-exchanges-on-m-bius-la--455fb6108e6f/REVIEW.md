# Scientific review: optimal independent-set exchanges on Möbius ladders

## Correctness — PASS
The antipodal edges yield a partition into \(n\) vertex pairs; every independent set chooses at most one vertex per pair. The ordinary outer-cycle links force alternating choices; the single cross-layer seam enforces equal choices. This signed-cycle compatibility condition is both necessary and sufficient, giving exactly two full assignments for odd \(n\), and exactly two assignments per omitted pair for even \(n\). In the even case, a one-token exchange must move the unique omitted pair one step, and the step \(n-1\) generates all \(2n\) rotations. The proof handles the boundaries \(n=3\) and \(n=4\). Two independent exhaustive enumerators and two independently constructed adjacency graphs agree through \(n=15\); an explicit rotational adjacency comparison passes throughout that range. This finite check is corroboration, not an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
A previously established graded independent-domination polynomial for this exact Möbius-ladder family already yields the number of optimum states in either parity; both these configuration counts and the scalar independence number are excluded from the new contribution. The general token-jumping/token-sliding equivalence is also acknowledged. Another prior finite-layer theorem determines slides between near-maximum maximal independent sets for odd ladder widths; it makes no assertion about exchanges between the maximum independent sets studied here. Proposition 1 of Bonsma–Kamiński–Wrochna (2014) gives general equivalence of move models but not the full \(M_{2n}\) exchange graph; the broader algorithmic claw-free results do not cover this claw-containing family. The later Agarwal–Basak (2026) full text computes independence-complex homotopy types, not the maximum-set solution graph, and their reported homotopy types do not logically determine optimum exchange adjacency. The inspected mathematical research records on symmetry and homology are similarly noncovering. Direct searches for Möbius-ladder maximum stable-set reconfiguration, minimum-cover exchange, facet exchange, and exact tabular formulations did not identify a matching theorem. This evidence supports best-knowledge originality of the exact maximum-layer exchange graph, not of the optimum-set counts, but it does not eliminate obscure earlier statements or uncatalogued computations.

## Value — PASS
Optimal-solution geometry is different from the numerical independence optimum and from the homotopy type of the full independence complex. The full exact classification across a canonical cubic circulant family gives a qualitative parity transition: the two optimum placements are frozen for odd \(n\), whereas even \(n\) admits exactly one reachable cycle traversing all \(2n\) optimum placements. The signed-cycle parity obstruction and a movable single defect explain this transition and give an explicit optimal reconfiguration route. Thus the fact identifies a natural structural boundary rather than a small numerical refinement or an arbitrary finite slice.

## Relationship and limitations
Sources: Bonsma–Kamiński–Wrochna, arXiv:1403.0359v1 (2014); Agarwal–Basak, arXiv:2608.30601 (2026); MathWorld independence-number and independent-vertex-set entries. Comparisons also considered the published results on dihedral homology of Möbius-ladder independence complexes, which concern a different invariant. Primary arXiv texts were materially inspected. The result is restricted to \(n\ge3\) and exactly maximum sets; hidden prior coverage remains a bibliographic risk, and only the elementary proof establishes the infinite range.
