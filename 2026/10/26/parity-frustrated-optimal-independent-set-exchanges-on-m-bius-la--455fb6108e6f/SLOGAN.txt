For the Möbius ladder \(M_{2n}\), the one-vertex-exchange graph of maximum independent sets is \(2K_1\) when \(n\) is odd and the full cycle \(C_{2n}\) when \(n\) is even. This sharp parity split follows from whether the antipodal-pair sign constraints admit a defect-free cyclic assignment or force exactly one movable omitted pair.
