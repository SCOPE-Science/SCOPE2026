For a fixed nilpotent matrix of Jordan type \((n,1)\), the reduced variety of commuting square-zero matrices switches with the parity of \(n\): two intersecting affine components for even \(n\), versus one irreducible quadratic cone times affine space for odd \(n\). Its entire finite-field rank distribution is explicit in every characteristic, including the exact number of acyclic differentials commuting with the fixed nilpotent.
