For a nearest-neighbor walk on an odd cycle with clockwise probabilities alternating between \(p\) and \(1-p\), the last visited noninitial vertex is exactly uniform within each parity class, with class masses \(1-p\) and \(p\): its total-variation distance from uniform is \(|p-1/2|\) at every odd size. On even cycles that distance instead tends to \(|p-1/2|/2\), giving a sharp size-parity resonance despite zero two-step average drift.
