Opening a nearly disconnected conformal link in a zero-mode free-fermion chain changes the even half-chain entropy by \(-\tfrac14\lambda^2\log(1/\lambda)\) but the odd half-chain entropy by the analytic leading term \(\tfrac14(\log2-\tfrac12)\lambda^2\). An exact continuum-versus-edge cancellation eliminates the logarithm only for odd parity.
