For synchronous \(q\)-color excitation on any complete bipartite graph, every recurrent state is either all-rest, a fully occupied period-\(q\) rotation, or an alternating wave of period \(2\lceil q/2\rceil\). The alternating waves appear sharply when each vertex part has at least \(\lceil q/2\rceil\) vertices, yielding exact Stirling-number attractor counts and an odd–even period bifurcation.
