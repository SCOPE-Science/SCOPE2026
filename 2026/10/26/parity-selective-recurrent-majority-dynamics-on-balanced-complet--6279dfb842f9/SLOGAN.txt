Synchronous binary majority dynamics on every regular complete multipartite graph \(K_{s,\ldots,s}\) has an exact two-parameter periodic-state census, with a parity-dependent coefficient formula and a globally sharp three-step convergence bound. For odd part size \(s\ge3\), changing the number of parts from even to odd changes the recurrent-state count's polynomial prefactor by a factor of order \(r\); even part sizes have a different exponential growth base.
