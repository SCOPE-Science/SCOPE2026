Synchronous binary majority on a clique joined to a path of \(\ell\ge2\) vertices has sharp voting time \(\ell+2\) for odd clique size and \(\ell+1\) for even clique size, attained by exactly \(2\binom{m-1}{(m-1)/2}\) or \(2^{m-1}\) labelled initial states, respectively. Only even cliques admit two-cycles, which must alternate throughout the entire graph and have no transient predecessors.
