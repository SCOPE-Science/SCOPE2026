For every circular prism \(C_n\square K_2\), the whole open-packing polynomial factors as the square of the independent-set polynomial of \(C_n^2\) when \(n\) is even, but becomes that of \(C_{2n}^2\) when \(n\) is odd. Exact optimizer counts follow, and perfect injective colorability occurs precisely when \(3\mid n\) or \(n\in\{4,8\}\).
