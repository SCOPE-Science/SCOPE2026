For the gear graph obtained by subdividing each rim edge of a wheel, strong odd colorings have a forced two-mode structure and an exact minimum palette of \(3,4,5\), or \(6\) colors according to the rim length, with the isolated six-color exception at \(n=5\). Every minimum coloring is counted exactly: an explicit recurrence covers odd rim lengths, while a fixed-size parity-transfer trace covers all multiples of four.
