On the single-trap complete bipartite quantum network \(K_{m,n}\), infinitesimal partition-dependent site dephasing makes every state absorb while producing an exact mean-lifetime divergence with coefficient \(\frac{m-1}{\alpha}\operatorname{tr}(Q_A\rho)+\frac{n}{\beta}\operatorname{tr}(Q_B\rho)\). For localized starting vertices, the coefficients are exactly \((m-2)/\alpha\) on the trap's side and \((n-1)/\beta\) on the opposite side, independent of hopping and trap strengths.
