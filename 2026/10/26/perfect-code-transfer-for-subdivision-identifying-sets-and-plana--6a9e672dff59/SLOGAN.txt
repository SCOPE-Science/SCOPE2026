For every finite simple graph \(G\) of order at least two, open-independent open-locating-dominating sets in the full one-edge subdivision \(S(G)\) correspond one-to-one to perfect codes of \(G\) whose code vertices have degree at least two. As a consequence, their existence is NP-complete even for planar bipartite subcubic graphs of girth at least six that arise by subdividing every edge of a planar cubic graph once.
