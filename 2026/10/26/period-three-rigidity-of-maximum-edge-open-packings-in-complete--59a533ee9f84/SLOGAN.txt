For every complete rooted \(b\)-ary tree of height \(h\), with \(b\ge2\), the unique maximum edge open packing is the union of full edge layers at depths \(h-1,h-4,h-7,\ldots\); its size is \(\sum_{j=0}^{\lfloor(h-1)/3\rfloor}b^{h-3j}\). Its asymptotic edge density is \(b^2/(b^2+b+1)\), displaying period-three rigidity across every branching factor.
