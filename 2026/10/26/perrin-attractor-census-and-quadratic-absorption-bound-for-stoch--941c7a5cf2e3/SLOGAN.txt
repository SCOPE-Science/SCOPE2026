For random single-vertex closed-neighborhood minority dynamics on a wheel with \(n\) rim vertices, exactly \(2P_n\) configurations are recurrent, where \(P_n\) is the Perrin sequence: odd rims yield only absorbing states, whereas even rims have exactly two hub-switching recurrent pairs. Every initial state reaches a recurrent class within expected time at most \(6n(n+1)\), contrasting a quadratic global absorption bound with an exponentially growing attractor census.
