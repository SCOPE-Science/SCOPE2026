The 24 vertices of the truncated octahedron admit single-degree spherical-harmonic interpolation of every antipodal-parity-compatible dataset for all degrees \(\ell\ge9\), and this persistent threshold is sharp. Curiously, degree \(7\) already achieves full parity rank \(12\), but degree \(8\) falls to rank \(11\), with the missing direction represented by a degree-six alternating cubic-symmetry harmonic.
