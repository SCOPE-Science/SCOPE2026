At the exact zero-invasion boundary for a periodically pulsed two-species Lotka–Volterra competition model, every positive solution loses one species at the global rate \(x(t)\sim P(t)/(\kappa\overline P\,t)\), with an explicit phase-dependent periodic envelope. Staggering two species' interventions changes that leading envelope even when their per-period pulse products and critical invasion exponents are unchanged.
