For radial Itô power-law diffusion reset at a spherical threshold, the radius raised to exponent \(\alpha+2\), under its natural deterministic time change, is exactly a reset squared-Bessel diffusion. Dimension two is uniquely exponent-universal in these coordinates: the stationary radius power is the product of two uniforms, its logarithm is Gamma, and reset-count noise has limiting Fano factor \(1/2\).
