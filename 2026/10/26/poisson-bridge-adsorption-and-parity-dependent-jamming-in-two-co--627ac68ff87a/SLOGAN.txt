In irreversible dimer adsorption on two size-\(n\) cliques joined by a paired matching of fixed edge-attempt rate \(\lambda\), the number of deposited bridge dimers converges to \(\operatorname{Poisson}(\lambda)\). The final number of uncovered sites is exactly zero or two according to bridge-count parity, leaving asymptotic defect probabilities \((1\mp e^{-2\lambda})/2\) along even and odd clique sizes.
