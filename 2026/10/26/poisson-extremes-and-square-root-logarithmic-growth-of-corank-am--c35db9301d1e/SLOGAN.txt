For successive upper-left blocks of one Haar-uniform finite-field matrix, the largest corank through time \(N\) grows almost surely as \(\sqrt{\log_qN}\). At the natural rare-event scale \(N\asymp q^{k^2}\), exceedances of corank \(k\) have a sharp Poisson law with intensity \(1/P_\infty(q)\), where \(P_\infty(q)=\prod_{j\ge1}(1-q^{-j})\).
