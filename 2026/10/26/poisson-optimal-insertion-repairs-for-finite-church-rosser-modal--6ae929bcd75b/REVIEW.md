# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The classical Church–Rosser frame correspondence is reconstructed with all quantifiers, including the diagonal successor case. The factorial-moment argument controls arbitrary fixed overlap configurations. Exact estimates make uncovered pairs a matching, ensure activation by common predecessors, and exclude zero outdegrees with high probability. On that event the insertion-only lower bound and explicit constructions agree. A disjoint-tail choice argument proves the exact optimal-repair product. Uniform degree concentration and tightness prove the normalized multiplicity assertion. The embedded checker independently tests finite semantics, moments, optimization and multiplicity; the all-size claims rest on proof, not enumeration.

## Originality — PASS, subject to literature risk

The classical frame correspondence and common-neighbor Poisson machinery are acknowledged prior art. The assessed contribution is the conjunction of Poisson-minimal **insertion-only** repair distance, a sharp high-probability matching certificate, and the exact multiplicity of minimum repairs. Material portions of accessible primary literature on abstract CTL model repair, undirected random-graph edit distance, and modal reduction FMP were inspected; no dominating statement was identified. Semantic searches in published mathematical records did not find an equivalent. The 2026 motivating frame-definability paper's full text remained unavailable; an unknown stronger directed repair theorem could subsume this result. Historical priority is not independently certified.

## Value — PASS

General frame confluence is nonmonotone under insertions. In a natural sparse probabilistic ensemble, repairing only the rare uncovered pairs is *globally optimal* and has a computable number of independently selectable repairs. This is a meaningful quantitative structural boundary for modal-frame repair, beyond finite arithmetic or a direct citation of a standard Poisson count.

## Limitations and closest literature

The result does not cover mixed editing, deletions, self-loop insertion, dense regimes, or all graded/Geach axioms. For contextual distinctions and the residual risk of broad domination, see arXiv:2608.22822 (abstract only); arXiv:1506.06165 (model repair); arXiv:2007.08409 (undirected random edit distance); and arXiv:2510.00864 (modal reduction).
