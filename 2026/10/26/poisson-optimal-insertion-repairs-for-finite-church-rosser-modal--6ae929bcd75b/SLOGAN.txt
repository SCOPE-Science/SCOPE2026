In the critical sparse random directed-frame window \(np^2=2\log n+t\), the minimum number of inserted edges needed to validate \(\Diamond\Box q\to\Box\Diamond q\) converges to \(\operatorname{Poisson}(e^{-t}/2)\). With high probability every obstruction requires exactly one insertion, and all optimal repairs are counted by an explicit product of the two endpoint outdegrees (with corrections for mutual adjacency).
