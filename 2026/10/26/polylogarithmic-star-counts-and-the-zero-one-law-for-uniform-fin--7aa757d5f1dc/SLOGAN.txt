A uniformly random finite idempotent map has approximately \(\log n\) small stars of size one, \(\log^2 n\) stars of size two, and more generally \(\log^{s+1}n/s!\) stars with \(s\) leaves, all concentrated in probability. Its first-order almost-sure theory is complete, has the finite model property, satisfies a zero–one law, and is not \(\omega\)-categorical.
