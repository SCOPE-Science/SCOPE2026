In the two-subset Kneser graph \(KG(n,2)\), every three-vertex selection power dominates except a sufficiently short common-center star: exactly those stars with at least \(n-4\) spokes succeed. The complete power-domination polynomial follows, and for every \(n\ge6\) the number of inclusion-minimal power-dominating sets is exactly \(\binom{\binom n2}{3}\), even though their possible cardinalities separate for \(n\ge8\).
