A continuous-time quantum walk on the locally infinite hypercube with summable power-law coordinate couplings spreads to typical Hamming radius \(K_\alpha(ct)^{1/\alpha}\), with an explicit sharp constant. Its radial fluctuations are Gaussian, and the variance-to-mean ratio converges to \(2^{1/\alpha-2}\), strictly below one half for every \(\alpha>1\).
