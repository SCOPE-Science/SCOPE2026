On a finite height-two lattice with \(m\) labelled atoms, the counts of \(\mathsf K\)-, \(\mathsf T\)- and \(\mathsf F\)-preconditionals are exactly \((m+3)^m+2^m+2m3^{m-1}\), \(m^m\), and the number of partial matchings of the atoms. Fundamental negations pair some atoms and send the rest to bottom; the involutive cases are exactly perfect-match Sasaki hooks.
