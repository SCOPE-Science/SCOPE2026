Every reciprocal-summable prescription of radii \(s_j^{1/n}\), with \(n\ge3\), occurs as circles having exactly \(n\) global maximum-modulus points for a single entire function of order \(n\), type one, and first Taylor gap one. In particular, such exceptional circles can number at least \(c_nR^n/(\log R)^2\) below radius \(R\).
