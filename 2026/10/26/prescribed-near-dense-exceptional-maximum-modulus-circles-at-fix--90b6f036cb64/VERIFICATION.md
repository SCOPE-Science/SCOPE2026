# Analytic verification and limits

The central comparisons can be checked line-by-line without a numerical truncation: on \(|w|=s_j\), the identity \(|w-s_j|^2=2s_j(s_j-\operatorname{Re}w)\) and \(1-e^{-u}\ge u/(1+u)\) give the base angular gap. The factorization \(G(w)=(1-w/s_j)^2G_j(w)\) and bound \(|G_j(w)|\le H(s_j)^2\) make the perturbation at most \(\varepsilon B_j\) times that gap, with \(\varepsilon B_j<1/2\) uniformly in \(j\). The number of equality points is exactly \(n\) because they solve \(z^n=s_j\).

Check of the infinite part: \(\log H(T)/T\) is a sum dominated by \(\sum1/s_j\) and converges to zero termwise, so \(H(T)^2=e^{o(T)}\). This establishes both finite \(B=\sup B_j\) and the global upper type estimate. On all prescribed radii, the value is exactly \(e^{s_j}\), yielding the matching lower estimate. For the density example, use the reciprocal-summable sequence \(s_j=(j+1)(\log(j+2))^2\) and count indices below \(R^n/[16n^2(\log R)^2]\).
