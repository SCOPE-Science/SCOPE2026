In the truncated one-generator Zinbiel half-shuffle algebra in characteristic \(p\), the maximal abelian-ideal dimension is exactly \(p^{k+1}\) throughout \((p+\lceil p/2\rceil)p^k\le n\le(2p-1)p^k\), and its maximizing ideal is unique. Base-\(p\) carries create sharp plateaux absent over characteristic zero; in particular, \(\beta(Z_{3\cdot2^k})=2^{k+1}\) in characteristic \(2\).
