# Review

## Correctness

**PASS.** For \(E_b\), the structure matrix has determinant \(-1\), so every prime reduction is perfect. Direct substitution gives the idempotent equation \(x(x^3-bx-1)=0\) and \(y=x^2\), so the nonzero idempotents are exactly the roots of the stated cubic. For \(b\ne0,2\), the cubic is irreducible; its discriminant and Galois group determine the Frobenius cycle types. Chebotarev gives the listed densities.

## Originality

**PASS.** Literature searches compared the exact claim, aliases, and parameter family. The closest results cover existence of some idempotent-free algebra over each finite field and a general two-dimensional cubic criterion, but no retrieved record states the complete prime-density distribution for \(E_b\). The claim is the explicit family-level arithmetic classification, not a claim that the cubic-root criterion or Chebotarev is new. Residual risk: the earlier preprint *Order structures around evolution algebras* (arXiv:2505.01444) was cited by the survey for a related rational example but could not be directly inspected after a bounded retrieval attempt.

## Value

**PASS.** This supplies a precise, reusable answer for a natural infinite family to the literature's question about reduction modulo primes. It quantifies how often a perfect integral algebra acquires nonzero idempotents after reduction and distinguishes the cyclic and symmetric cubic Galois cases.
