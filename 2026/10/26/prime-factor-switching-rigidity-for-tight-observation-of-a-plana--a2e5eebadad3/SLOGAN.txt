For a planar rotation observed by one of two coordinate sensors at each of \(N\) equally spaced times over a quarter-turn, exact isotropic information is possible precisely when \(N\) has an odd prime factor; if \(p\) is the least such prime, its sharp minimum switching cost is \(p-1\), achieved by exactly two complementary equal-block schedules. For \(N=2^a p^b\) the entire family has exactly \(2^{N/p}\) schedules, and isotropy simultaneously attains the best smallest eigenvalue, determinant, and posterior covariance trace.
