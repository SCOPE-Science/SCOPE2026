For every odd window length \(k\) coprime to five, the GCDs of translated sums of fourth powers of Fibonacci and Lucas numbers are identical. Their odd prime factors are precisely the divisors \(p\ge7\) of \(k\) whose quadratic-unit order \(r_p\) divides \(k\), with exponent \(\nu_p(k)\); a single factor of two appears exactly when three divides \(k\).
