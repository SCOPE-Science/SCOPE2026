The 2022 Fibonacci-square theorem has different recurrence assumptions. Targeted mathematical literature and record searches did not reveal an equivalent theorem. Unindexed earlier work and an implicit broad congruence theorem remain possible. Originality: PASS within inspected evidence.

## Mathematical value
