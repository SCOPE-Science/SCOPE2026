For every positive window length \(k\), the GCD of sums of \(k\) consecutive squared cobalancing numbers has an exact prime factorization: beyond primes \(2,3,5\), a prime \(p\mid k\) occurs to full exponent \(\nu_p(k)\) exactly when the order of \(3+2\sqrt2\) modulo \(p\) divides \(k\). The exceptional prime \(5\) gains one additional valuation when \(6\mid k\), and the remaining local rules are explicit.
