For each fixed exponent \(a\ge2\), the integers \(p^a q\) with distinct primes and unitary divisor sum greater than exponential divisor sum have an explicit two-prime asymptotic \(K_a X^{(b+1)/(a+b)}/(\log X)^2\), where \(b\) is determined by the largest proper divisor of \(a\). The asymptotic exponent changes with the factorization of \(a\), although such integers have relative density zero inside their own prime-power layer.
