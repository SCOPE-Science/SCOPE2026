For every prime \(p\) and \(r\ge2\), counting scattered copies of \(0^r1\) modulo \(p\) gives a regular language with exactly \(p^{2+\lfloor\log_p r\rfloor}\) minimal complete-DFA states, regardless of the target residue. Lucas's theorem produces the compressed automaton, while a translation-rigid support argument proves every state necessary.
