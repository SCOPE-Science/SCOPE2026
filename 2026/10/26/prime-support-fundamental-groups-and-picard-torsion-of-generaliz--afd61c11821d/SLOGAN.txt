For composite-order cyclic generalized Fermat Calabi–Yau quotients, coarse fundamental groups and torsion line bundles retain exactly a prime-order subgroup when the action order is a prime power, and vanish when it has two distinct prime factors. In contrast, the torsion Weil class group retains the full cyclic covering-group order, giving an explicit Cartier–Weil torsion gap and simply connected log-Enriques examples with non-Cartier canonical torsion.
