# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For a simply connected smooth Calabi–Yau complete intersection M of dimension t(k−1)−1≥2 with fixed-point-free cyclic generator phi of composite order k≥4, the primary Billi–Palomino–Sarti Proposition 4.3/Remark 4.4 gives nonempty fixed loci for the powers phi^a exactly when gcd(a,k)>1, and Proposition 4.7 puts all such fixed loci in codimension ≥2. Armstrong’s orbit-space theorem therefore makes pi_1(M/C_k) the quotient C_k by the subgroup generated by all elements having fixed points. Elementary divisor arithmetic gives order p when k=p^e is a proper prime power and order one when k has two distinct primes. For any nontrivial proper subgroup H=<phi^s>, its generator phi^s has fixed points, giving pi_1(M/H)=0. Universal coefficients identify torsion H^2 with torsion H_1 for finite CW quotients, and the exponential sequence with H^1(O)=0 identifies Pic torsion with that H^2 torsion. The quasi-etale cyclic class-group exact sequence of Boissiere–Camere–Sarti Proposition 3.2 has kernel C_k and torsion-free target Pic(M)^G; thus Cl(Y)_tors=C_k, a genuinely different invariant. For even d, the source’s canonical-character result yields canonical Weil divisor of order two; since the computed Pic torsion vanishes for mixed-prime k, it is not Cartier. The independent exact integer subgroup test covers all 4≤k≤500 and all nontrivial proper subgroups; this supports the arithmetic but cannot replace Armstrong/UCT/normality hypotheses.

## originality

PASS

Qualified best-of-knowledge PASS for the complete prime-support/Cartier–Weil–Picard package, not for the underlying generalized Fermat examples. Billi–Palomino–Sarti completely describes the automorphism fixed powers, quotient smoothness, singularity strata, and canonical index, but does not compute the ordinary topological fundamental group or the full Cartier/Weil torsion divergence. Boissiere–Camere–Sarti already prove the required class-group exact sequence in general. Their combination with stabilizer subgroup arithmetic and UCT is an actual mathematical deduction yielding a new explicit dichotomy for these examples, but unindexed derived consequences in recent GFM literature are a priority risk.

## value

PASS

These are natural and distinct invariants of a currently studied family of quasi-etale Calabi–Yau quotients: topological pi1, Cartier Pic torsion, Weil class torsion and canonical Cartier obstruction. The prime-power/mixed-prime transition and C_k versus C_p discrepancy are complete structural classifications not numerical curiosities. The proof is a short application of serious established theorems but requires correct fixed-locus synthesis, and is not mere restatement of Proposition 4.8.
