For every finite height-two poset over a finite field, an explicit four-state edge model gives the complete three-rank distribution of ordered idempotent pairs whose product is again idempotent. For the star with \(m\) leaves this event has probability \(\frac34+\frac14\bigl((1+3q)/(1+q)^2\bigr)^m\), tending to \(3/4\) over every fixed \(\mathbb F_q\).
