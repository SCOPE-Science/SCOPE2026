Ordered commuting idempotents of the full monotone transformation monoid on a finite chain admit a decomposition into rooted interval blocks, giving the complete common-product-rank series \(uB(t)/(1-uB(t))\) with \(B(t)=t((1-t+t^2)/((1-t)(1-3t+t^2)))^2\). Their total count has a degree-six rational generating function and their commuting probability decays at an explicit sharp exponential rate.
