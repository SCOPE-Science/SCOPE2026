All (even nonlinear) two-local automorphisms of the three-dimensional Heisenberg Lie algebra arise from arbitrary injections of projective directions and independent injective maps on each central fiber, subject only to homogeneity. Over \(\mathbb F_q\) their exact number is \((q-1)^{q+2}(q+1)!(q!)^{q+1}\), compared with only \(q^2(q^2-1)(q^2-q)\) ordinary automorphisms.
