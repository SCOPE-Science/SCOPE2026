For every graphical two-step nilpotent Lie algebra with distinct edge brackets and a defining graph without isolated vertices, the nontrivial components of its projective commuting graph are indexed exactly by the nonsingleton connected components of the complement of the defining graph; a connected-domination polynomial counts their vertices. For any tree with \(n\ge3\) vertices and \(\ell\) leaves, the number of isolated projective commuting points is exactly \(q^{\ell}(q-1)^{n-\ell-1}\) over every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\).
