The full pseudo-Frobenius set of the Lucas-shift semigroup \(\langle L_a,L_a+L_n:n\ge0\rangle\) is classified by maximal independent sets of a path plus two odd-index exceptions. Its exact Cohen–Macaulay type is \(P_{a-3}+P_{a-5}+2\mathbf1_{a\text{ odd}}\) for every \(a\ge5\), where \(P_n\) counts maximal independent sets of an \(n\)-vertex path.
