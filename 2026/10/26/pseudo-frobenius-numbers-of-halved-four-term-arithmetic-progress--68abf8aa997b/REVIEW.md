# Review of the halved four-term progression classification

## Correctness — PASS
For odd coprime \(a\ge5\), \(d\ge1\), parity pairing proves the quotient-generator identity. Reducing triples of \(x\) yields normal forms \(W(u)\), and the strict inequality \(W(u+a)>W(u)\) proves completeness of the Apéry set. Applying maximal-Apéry criteria to generators \(x\) and \(y\) yields all pseudo-Frobenius elements, the Frobenius maximum, and type. Independent Dijkstra enumeration passed 1,550 parameter pairs and 2,697,707 quotient-membership comparisons. These computations corroborate rather than replace the general proof.

## Originality — PASS within inspected evidence
Adeniran et al. (arXiv:1809.09360) explicitly pose the intermediate-progression quotient Frobenius problem. Liu (arXiv:2312.06096) assumes the denominator divides the first generator, unlike the odd-generator quotient by two here; Liu (arXiv:2604.26415) does not cover this parameter family either. Equivalent-triple searches and mathematical-record comparisons revealed no explicit complete pseudo-Frobenius/type statement. Lee and Nam (DOI:10.1142/S1793042124500891) address genus of related quotients, but only their abstract and first page were accessible; an implicit specialization in the full paper or an unindexed general semigroup theorem remains an originality risk. General Apéry methods themselves are not claimed new.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The theorem solves a natural first interior-length, nondivisor-denominator quotient family for Frobenius number and simultaneously classifies the pseudo-Frobenius set, symmetry, and type by a simple mod-three law. It provides all parameters, not an isolated calculation. Other denominator and progression-length regimes remain open here.
