For every coprime odd \(a\ge5\) and \(d\ge1\), halving the numerical semigroup \(\langle a,a+d,a+2d,a+3d\rangle\) yields exactly \(\langle a,(a+d)/2,(a+3d)/2\rangle\), whose complete pseudo-Frobenius set has one element if \(3\mid a\) and two otherwise. An explicit Apéry staircase gives all pseudo-Frobenius elements and a closed Frobenius formula, settling the denominator-two nondivisor branch for this four-term progression.
