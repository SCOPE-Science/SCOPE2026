For the numerical semigroup generated by four consecutive Mersenne numbers beginning at \(2^m-1\), the exact pseudo-Frobenius set is \(\{F,F-1,F-2\}\) when \(m\equiv1\pmod3\), and \(\{F,F-4(2^m)\}\) when \(m\equiv2\pmod3\), for every \(m\ge5\). These two uncovered branches have Cohen–Macaulay types three and two respectively; the third branch was previously known.
