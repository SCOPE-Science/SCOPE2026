The adjacent-swap graph on the \(132\)-avoiding permutations of length \(n\) is connected and has exactly \(\binom{2n-2}{n-2}\) edges. Moving from \((1,2,\ldots,n)\) to \((n,1,2,\ldots,n-1)\) requires exactly \((n-1)^2\) legal swaps, although unrestricted sorting takes only \(n-1\): an explicit unbounded geodesic detour.
