In a conservative mass-in-mass chain with two nearly coincident local resonance frequencies, the formerly dark flat band acquires a strictly positive bandwidth only at **quadratic order in the squared-frequency detuning**: \(W_\lambda=4cab\varepsilon^2/[r^2(a+b)^3]+O(\varepsilon^3)\). The host kinetic-energy fraction has the same leading coefficient, and an exact nonasymptotic slope bound limits the leakage for any two distinct positive local frequencies.
