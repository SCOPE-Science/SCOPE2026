Equal-sized communities can have epidemic risk rankings that reverse by an arbitrarily large factor: a path community that is harder to invade in isolation carries more than \(m^2/8\) times the near-onset endemic infection burden of a denser clique community when cross-community links are included. The theorem holds for the discrete-time independent-state SIS product map on an explicit connected three-block graph with \(m\ge4\) vertices per block.
