In a collectively dephased precessing spin, every dipole mode stops oscillating above \(\eta=2\), but a physical quadrupole retains damped oscillations until the exact threshold \(\eta_*=3.379224327784561\ldots\). The quadrupole sector has separate order-two exceptional points at \(\eta=2/3\) and \(\eta=\eta_*\), valid for every spin \(j\ge1\).
