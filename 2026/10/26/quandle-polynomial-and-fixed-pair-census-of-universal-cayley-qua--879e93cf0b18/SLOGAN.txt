The ordinary quandle polynomial of the universal Cayley quandle \(Q_n=[n]\times S_n\) has an exact formula indexed by cycle-type partitions, and its ordered fixing-pair count is \(n\,n!\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}p(j)\). The calculation is a specialization of an action-quandle formula involving centralizer orbits and point stabilizers, and it yields a sharp minimum monomial of coefficient \(n!\) for \(n\ge3\).
