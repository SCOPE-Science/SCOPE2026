For the normalized-volume affine \(L^2\) projector on a solid circular cone, the apex is the unique worst-conditioned evaluation point in every dimension \(d\ge2\), with an explicit strict gap at every positive height. Uniformly throughout the cone, the endpoint kernel-row norm divided by \(d\) converges to \(2(1-s)/e\); asymptotically norming points are exactly those whose heights vanish.
