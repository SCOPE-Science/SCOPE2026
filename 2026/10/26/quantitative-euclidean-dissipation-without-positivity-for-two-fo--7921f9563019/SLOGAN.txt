The two fourth-order nonsymmetric Dirichlet Laplacians have uniformly positive Euclidean numerical ranges, with explicit bounds \(\operatorname{Re}(u^*Mu)\ge\beta u^*Du+\|Du\|_2^2\), so their discrete heat semigroups and implicit time steps contract in the ordinary Euclidean norm. Neither matrix has a positive diagonal symmetrizer, and its heat propagator has negative entries at arbitrarily small positive times.
