# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The defining presentation gives a group algebra of dimension \(8m\). For odd \(m\), the cyclic subalgebra has length-four primary factors, and inversion has no nonidentity fixed odd-order roots; the principal component is \(k[Q_8]\), and the remaining components split as matrix rings over length-four finite chain rings. The proof of the splitting uses the surjectivity of the norm for quadratic finite étale local extensions. In the quaternion component, direct multiplication forces all four reduced coefficients to vanish, so the square-zero locus is exactly the central ideal \((1+z)k[Q_8]\). Its principal-ideal rank census produces \(H_q\). A disjoint valuation/Smith-form partition produces \(P_Q\). These factors multiply without cross-coupling and yield the exact rank polynomial, total, and maximal-rank coefficient. The symbolic totals and boundaries agree with all independent exhaustive checks listed in the verification file.

## Originality — PASS, with limitations

The closest recoverable result treats **dihedral**, not dicyclic, modular group algebras of the same order. It has the same nonprincipal length-four matrix factors but a different eight-dimensional local factor and therefore does not imply the quaternionic square-zero collapse, the dicyclic count, or the dicyclic regular-rank polynomial. Ivanov–Ivanov–Volkov–Zhou (arXiv:1407.4341) gives an explicit quaternion group-algebra presentation and Hochschild structures, not this element-by-element rank census. Altunbulak Aksu–Green (arXiv:1510.09078) determines ghost number rather than individual square-zero multiplication ranks. Eisele (arXiv:1506.05159) is a related block-theoretic work, but only its abstract was available for comparison and no full-text exclusion is asserted. Targeted searches of the research index and primary literature did not identify an equivalent all-parameter theorem. Publication-priority uncertainty remains, particularly for an indirect consequence of a general modular group-ring classification or a local matrix enumeration.

## Value — PASS

The object \(k[G_m]\) is a natural infinite family of noncommutative modular group algebras. The complete square-zero rank polynomial refines ordinary nilpotent counts into homological invariants for every regular differential. The quaternionic collapse to a four-dimensional linear ideal is a structural contrast with the dihedral local algebra, not a change of notation or a known-table recomputation. The theorem supplies exact, usable counts for all finite fields of characteristic two and all odd \(m\), and its maximal-rank term characterizes acyclic differentials.
