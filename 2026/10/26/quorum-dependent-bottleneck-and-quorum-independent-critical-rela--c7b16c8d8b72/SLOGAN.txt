At the sharp lower-cost threshold of a three-variable collective-risk game, maximal-risk/minimal-cost cooperation relaxes at the quorum-independent normalized rate \(t[x(t)-(M-1)/(N-1)]\to 2/[(N-1)\beta_*]\). A small excess cost \(\epsilon\) instead creates a loss-of-cooperation bottleneck lasting asymptotically \(\pi\sqrt{2(N-1)/[\beta_*(M-1)(N-M)\epsilon]}\), exposing a distinct quorum dependence.
