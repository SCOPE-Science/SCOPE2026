# Reproducibility and limits

Run `python3 verify.py` in the folder containing `classification.csv`. The program regenerates the catalog, checks exact counts for exclusion ranges two, three, four, and repeats direct finite-preimage language enumeration for all preserving rules at ranges two and three. It checks sharp witnesses and exhaustively explores each finite subset automaton. For exclusion range at least four, the unrestricted proof is the elementary single-particle argument in `RESULT.md`; finite checks at range four are a boundary test only. The computations do not establish originality or substitute for independent mathematical review.
