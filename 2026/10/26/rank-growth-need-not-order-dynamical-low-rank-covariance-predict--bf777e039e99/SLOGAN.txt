Even with positive noise, the exact dynamical low-rank Lyapunov covariance forecasts at ranks one and two need not be ordered by positive-semidefinite difference: a three-dimensional rank-one-noise example yields a negative quadratic-form gap of \(-t^2/18+O(t^3)\) at short times. Each forecast nevertheless remains positive semidefinite and below the exact untruncated covariance.
