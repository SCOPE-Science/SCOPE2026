The cyclic inverse monoid of restrictions of an \(n\)-cycle has a complete rank–nilpotency-index spectrum given by a totient-weighted divisor sum of cyclic forbidden-run transfer-matrix traces. Its high-index ranks have exact binomial laws with multiplicity \(n\varphi(n)\), while a uniformly chosen nilpotent has index asymptotic in probability to \(\log_2 n\).
