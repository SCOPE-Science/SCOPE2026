# Review of the level-two membrane-signature rank-one and principal-minor theorem

**Correctness — PASS.** The level-two shuffle identity supplies the full equations \(v_iv_j=M_{ij}\), and the independent linear forms \(M_{ij}\) can be completed to ambient coordinates. Eliminating \(v\) gives the second Veronese invariant ring and the *prime ideal of every* \(2\times2\) minor, including the nonprincipal ones. The straightening argument, even-degree Hilbert function, signed-component cover and vertex-sign normalization prove the claimed ideals, dimensions, degrees and full component count for every integer \(d\ge2\). Reducedness of the complete intersection follows from unmixedness and generic nonsingularity. The exact counterexample for \(d=3\) disproves set-theoretic equality with the source's larger principal-minor locus. The standalone arithmetic script confirms the sign-orbit formula for \(2\le d\le7\) and the precise counterexample, without substituting finite tests for proof. Scope is complex, second-level, and includes the exceptional agreement at \(d=2\).

**Originality — PASS within the carefully restricted claim; residual priority risk acknowledged.** Sections 4 and 5 of Améndola–Riffo–Schmitz (arXiv:2610.16724v1) define the specific membrane coordinates and universal elimination variety, prove prime elimination, then claim that the principal-minor complete intersection is that variety with degree \(2^{\binom d2}\). The present theorem identifies the missing rank-one equations and correct degree \(2^{d-1}\) for that newly defined membrane object, and explains the excess degree via signed components. Améndola–Friz–Sturmfels (2019) contains analogous rank-one path-signature arguments; a July 2025 Mathematics Stack Exchange discussion independently treats *abstract symmetric matrices with vanishing principal minors* and derives eight components in size four. These precedents imply the general mathematical ingredients but do not give this explicit correction to the membrane variety's published degree. Neither nomenclature change nor generic Veronese calculations alone are claimed new. Searches of the published mathematical finding database for membrane degree, shuffle elimination, and sign-twisted rank-one components yielded no directly covering result. The possibility of an unindexed correction or prior implicit specialization cannot be excluded.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** A central theorem of an October 2026 algebraic-geometry preprint assigns the degree of a reducible, geometrically larger complete intersection to a prime universal membrane signature variety. The difference grows exponentially, from degree \(4\) versus \(8\) at \(d=3\) to degree \(8\) versus \(64\) at \(d=4\), and affects geometric intersection counts. The finding provides the actual homogeneous prime ideal, exact degrees/Hilbert series, an explicit excluded point and a global explanation of the mistaken component count. This corrects a naturally motivated invariant rather than adding a routine arbitrary instance.

**Limits and closest literature.** The result applies only to complex second-level membrane signatures; it does not re-evaluate higher-level results or the entire membrane realization theorem. The 2019 path-signature article and 2025 symmetric-principal-minor discussion are substantial mathematical precedents, so claims of broad first discovery of rank-one or signed-matrix geometry would be unwarranted.
