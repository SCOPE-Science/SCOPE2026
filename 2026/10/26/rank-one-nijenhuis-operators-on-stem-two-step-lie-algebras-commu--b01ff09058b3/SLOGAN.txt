For every finite stem two-step nilpotent Lie algebra, nonzero rank-one Nijenhuis maps are counted exactly by the alternating-rank spectrum of its central commutator pencil, with a uniform formula valid in every characteristic. Free class-two Lie algebras admit an explicit Gaussian-binomial census, and the square-nonzero stratum has the simple size \((q^d-1)q^{D-1}\).
