For \(n\ge4\) and \(A_n=k[t]/(t^n)\), the rank-at-most-one weight-zero Rota–Baxter locus is an equidimensional union of exactly \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\) filtration-indexed Segre cones, with explicit intersections and singular locus. Over every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\), it has exactly \(1+\lfloor n/2\rfloor q^{n-1}-\sum_{j=0}^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor-1}q^j\) points, including zero.
