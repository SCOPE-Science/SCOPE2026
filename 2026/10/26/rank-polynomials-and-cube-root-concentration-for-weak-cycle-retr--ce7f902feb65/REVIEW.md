# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

An idempotent fixes precisely its image. Proper connected images of the undirected cycle are uniquely determined arcs, and the unused source edges provide a bijective bounded-lazy-walk encoding. The matrix formula and endpoint coefficients follow without unproved enumeration assumptions. The spectrum of the tridiagonal looped-path matrix is explicit, the leading endpoint eigenvector coefficient is positive and of order \(\ell^{-3}\), and the competing modes are exponentially suppressed for \(\ell\asymp n^{1/3}\). Spectral-norm estimates control the very-small and very-large image tails. Strict convexity of the confinement free energy gives the claimed logarithmic asymptotic and image-rank concentration. Exhaustive finite checks agree with the formula; they do not prove the limits.

## Originality — PASS against inspected evidence

The 2023 cycle-endomorphism monoid paper supplies the definitions, graph-arc framework, regularity and generating-set structure, rather than the coefficientwise **idempotent** rank polynomial or the \(n^{1/3}\) concentration law. Its full Sections 1–3 were read and compared for implication coverage. The 2022 weak path-homomorphism article concerns path-to-path counts and not the cycle retraction distribution. Accessible previous work counting idempotents in *strict* cycle endomorphism monoids does not encompass weak maps with edge contractions. The searches and comparison establish a substantive gap within the inspected sources, not global uniqueness. Residual risk: a general bounded-walk or graph-retraction counting theorem could imply some parts without the same terminology.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The idempotent rank distribution and its typical rank are natural structural invariants of the full weak cycle-endomorphism semigroup. The exact all-rank formula refines known total-monoid cardinality results, and its cube-root concentration exhibits a nontrivial entropy–spectral-confinement balance. Both results hold for an entire infinite family and are more informative than a routine finite enumeration.

## Scope and literature

The rank bounds for weak cycle endomorphisms and the original monoid definitions are credited to Dimitrova–Fernandes–Koppitz–Quinteiro (arXiv:2310.04149v1). Pomsri et al., *Finding the Number of Weak Homomorphisms of Paths* (DOI:10.1155/2022/2153927), give related walk-enumeration context but study different domain/codomain constraints. The exact multiplicative prefactor for the asymptotic count and a full local limit law are not established. Publication-priority verification remains incomplete.
