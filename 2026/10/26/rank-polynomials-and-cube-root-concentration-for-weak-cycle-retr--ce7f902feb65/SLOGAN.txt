For full weak endomorphisms of the undirected cycle \(C_n\), all idempotent image-rank counts are given by a looped-path adjacency-matrix formula. A uniformly chosen idempotent has image rank concentrated at \(\left(2\pi^2/(3\log3)\right)^{1/3}n^{1/3}\), while the logarithm of their total number is \(n\log3-\frac32\left(2\pi^2/(3\log3)\right)^{1/3}(\log3)n^{1/3}+o(n^{1/3})\).
