For nilpotent partial injections on a labeled \(n\)-set, the exact number of ordered mutually annihilating pairs of prescribed ranks \(r,s\) is \(\frac{n!(n-2)!}{r!s!(n-r-s)!^2}((n-1)(n-r-s)+rs)\) when \(n\ge2\) and \(r+s\le n\), and zero outside the feasible ranks. A cycle-inclusion–exclusion sieve collapses to two terms determined by the first map's rank and number of isolated chains.
