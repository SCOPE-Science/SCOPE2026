For every \(n\ge3\) and finite field \(\mathbb F_q\), the rank-by-rank number of factorizations \(A=EN\) of a fixed rank-one matrix into an idempotent and a rank-one square-zero factor is given by Gaussian-binomial formulas depending only on whether \(\operatorname{tr}A=0\). Every idempotent-rank stratum has strictly more such factorizations when the trace vanishes.
