## correctness

PASS

Fix E with image R and kernel K. For a second idempotent with image U, EF fixes U∩R pointwise; square-zero therefore forces U∩R=0. Each allowed U is the graph of a map from an s-subspace of K to R, and the rank of this map is b because F surjects onto U. Choosing that domain, the image T, and a surjection to T gives the two Gaussian factors and the product of q^s−q^i. A retraction p onto U already fixes U; (EF)^2=0 says exactly p(T)⊂U∩K, of dimension s−b. Since T⊂R and U∩R=0, these free values count q^(b(s−b)); on a complement of U⊕T the choices count q^(s(n−s−b)). Their product is q^(s(n−s)−b²). Choosing R and a complementary kernel gives the remaining factor [n r]_q q^(r(n−r)). The dimensions are nonnegative on the asserted support, the construction is reversible, and it includes zero ranks and n=0. Rank-b square-zero maps form one similarity class over every F_q by the length-two chains. Their number is obtained by choosing the image, a codimension-b kernel containing it, and an isomorphism from the quotient by the kernel onto the image. Simultaneous conjugation transports fibers bijectively across this class, so its cardinality divides the total and gives the stated individual count.
