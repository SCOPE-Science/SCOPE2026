The proof uses an exact, reversible parametrization: a rank-\(r\) idempotent \(E\) determines \(V=R\oplus K\); nilpotence of \(EF\) forces \(U=\operatorname{im}F\) transverse to \(R\), hence \(U\) is the graph of a rank-\(b\) linear map from a dimension-\(s\) subspace of \(K\) to \(R\). The residual condition \((EF)^2=0\) is equivalent to \(p(T)\subseteq U\cap K\). The dimensions and subspace counts give the formula without unstated genericity hypotheses. The individual-product statement follows from simultaneous conjugacy of square-zero matrices with fixed rank. The standalone verifier exhaustively checked \(342\) rank cells and \(2378\) exact individual-target factors in small prime fields, including boundary cases. Finite checks are not substitutes for the general proof.
