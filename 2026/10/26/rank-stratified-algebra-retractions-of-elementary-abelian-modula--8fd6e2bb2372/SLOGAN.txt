Every unital algebra retraction of the elementary abelian modular group algebra \(\mathbb F_q[C_p^m]\), for \(q=p^e\), is conjugate to a coordinate projection, and there are exactly \(m+1\) conjugacy classes. Its cotangent-rank-\(r\) census factors into \({m\brack r}_q q^{r(p^m-p^r-m+r)}\) distinct image subalgebras and \(q^{(m-r)(p^r-1)}\) retractions per image.
