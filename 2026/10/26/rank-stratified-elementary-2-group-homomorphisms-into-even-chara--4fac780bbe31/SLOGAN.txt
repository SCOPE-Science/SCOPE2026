For \(q\) a power of two, the homomorphisms \((C_2)^r\to H_b(q)\) admit a closed Gaussian-binomial enumeration refined by the rank of their first horizontal-coordinate matrix. For fixed \(q,r\), the probability that \(r\) independent uniform Heisenberg elements are commuting involutions approaches \(q^{-\binom{r+1}{2}}\) as \(b\to\infty\).
