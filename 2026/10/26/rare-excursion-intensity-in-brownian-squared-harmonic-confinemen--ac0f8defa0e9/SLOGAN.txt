A Brownian particle confined by a trap whose stiffness is the square of another Brownian motion has fixed-threshold excursion probabilities asymptotic to a positive, explicitly represented multiple of \(t^{-1/2}\). The resulting Gaussian-mixture intensity has the sharp universal small-threshold divergence \(J(u)\sim 2/(\pi\sqrt\sigma\,u)\), complementing rather than restating the known Cauchy central scaling.
