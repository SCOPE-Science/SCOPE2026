# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The conditional law given a missing taxon is exactly the renormalized remaining species law; subtracting all missing-taxon indicators gives the exact covariance identity. The fixed-alphabet binomial entropy expansion supplies the universal negative first correction and the explicit second correction. Rarest-taxon dominance follows from strict positivity of the entropy deletion gap at a minimal mass and exponentially smaller omission probabilities of more common taxa. The check script verifies 180 exact rational log coefficients; this finite verification is separate from the general proof.

The checked ecological literature defines Shannon diversity and emphasizes its distinction from richness. Exact occupancy/entropy covariance identities may exist under different terminology; this remains a bibliographic risk, and one closely related public finding was available only in summary.

## Value — PASS

The joint uncertainty of observed richness and sample Shannon entropy matters when ecologists report both metrics. The result specifies an exact finite-sample covariance calculation and a nonzero exponential asymptotic constant controlled by the rarest taxa, identifying the otherwise hidden scale once richness nearly saturates. The universal first correction and explicit second correction sharpen that relationship. It is a nontrivial structural joint-statistic result, not merely an entropy-bias correction or a finite example. Its validity is restricted to fixed IID community probabilities.
