The entire partial Petrial genus distribution of every sunlet \(C_n\circ K_1\) follows from one explicit rational generating function: leaf elimination turns the enumeration into cyclic strings of path blocks, with a six-state correction for the unbroken cycle. The minimum rank is \(2n-\lfloor n/2\rfloor\) for \(n\ge4\), and \(4\) at \(n=3\).
