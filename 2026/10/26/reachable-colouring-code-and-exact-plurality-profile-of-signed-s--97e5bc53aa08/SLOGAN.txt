The two-colour signed-burning process on any signed star with at least two leaves reaches exactly \(2(n+1)\) final colourings: each is the spoke-sign pattern dictated by the centre, with at most one leaf reversed. This yields the entire colour-count polynomial, exact plurality for every signing, and the sharp worst-signing threshold \(\lceil(n+3)/2\rceil\) for \(n\ge3\).
