The standalone `verify.py` uses only the Python standard library. It constructs the field-grading of \(M_2(\mathbb F_2)\), checks that the degree-zero component is a copy of \(\mathbb F_4\), checks the direct-sum decomposition, and verifies all products respect the \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\)-grading. This is a finite illustrative check, not a proof of the all-algebra theorem. The universal proof is given in RESULT.md.
