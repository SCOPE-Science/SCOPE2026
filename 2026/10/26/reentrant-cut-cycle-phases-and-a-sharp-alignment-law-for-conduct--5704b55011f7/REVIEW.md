# Scientific review

## Correctness

**PASS.** Three orthogonal Laplacian eigenspaces give the complete potential solution. The proof evaluates the two edge-source orbits without neglecting any edge class, records the current signs needed for absolute sums, and obtains all four norm formulas exactly. The comparison polynomials follow from direct algebra. For large cliques, a one-positive-root argument and a factored comparison at a common reference value prove the alignment law. The low-clique quadratics are analyzed separately, including the degenerate boundary at two vertices per part. An independent grounded-Laplacian checker supports the computations but is not used as an infinite proof.

## Originality

**PASS, with literature-access uncertainty.** The 2023 primary cycle-space paper treats Hamming graphs and discrete tori as principal exact families; it does not state the weighted complete split-graph phases in the inspected material. Related indexed split-graph work concerns Laplacian spectral classification, not absolute edge-transfer column norms. General electrical-current formulas alone do not settle which of the two projection-column orbits dominates at every positive conductance. The 2020 spectral split-graph article was accessible only at abstract/reference level, and equivalent unindexed calculations cannot be excluded.

The unweighted split graph has previously been studied in projection theory; no isolated unweighted special case is claimed as new. The reviewed claim is the positive-conductance parameter classification, low-clique reentrance and sharp integer alignment locus.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** This gives the exact endpoint operator invariants for all sizes and all positive within-clique conductances of a natural, non-edge-transitive family. Two independently defined projection norms exhibit different optimizer transitions: the order reverses at a precise dimension ratio, is simultaneous only along an infinite arithmetic locus, and becomes reentrant exactly at the two smallest clique sizes. This classifies a substantive structural phenomenon instead of extracting an arbitrary isolated parameter value.

## Limits and references

The operators are canonical, real and Euclidean-orthogonal; no minimality over arbitrary projections or general heterogeneous-conductance classification is established. Independent mathematical validation has not occurred. The closest references are Dilworth–Kutzarova–Ostrovskii, arXiv:2305.12582 (2023), and Li–Sun, DOI:10.1002/jcd.21743 (2020). The latter full text remains uninspected.
