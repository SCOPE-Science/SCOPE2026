# Scientific assessment

The common characteristic polynomial of the two exact epoch propagators has simple roots of modulus \(1/2\). The leading principal minors of the average squared-norm form shifted by \((49/2)I\) are positive. The all-state and all-epoch mean-square bounds follow by conditioning on the current epoch state; no sample-path instability follows from this argument alone.

The separate cyclic frontier was previously reported and is not part of the contribution. The OpenReview abstract cited in `RESULT.md` describes a qualitatively related frozen-versus-fresh instability in a regime with Schur-stable component maps, which cannot realize the stated uniform conditional factor. The full text was not available for comparison. The contribution is the explicit simultaneous small spectral factor and large conditional average norm gain in the natural two-iterate state, not priority for qualitative instability.
