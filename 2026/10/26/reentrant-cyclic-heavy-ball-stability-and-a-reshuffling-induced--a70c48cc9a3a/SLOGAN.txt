On two strictly convex scalar losses, fixed-order heavy-ball training has a sharp reentrant learning-rate stability diagram, while the fastest fixed-order spectral rate can occur in two distinct learning-rate bands. At an exact rational setting both frozen data orders converge at factor \(1/2\) per epoch, yet independent epoch reshuffling forces expected squared optimizer-state error to grow at least \((49/2)^k\) after \(k\) epochs.
