Every affine resolvable design has an exact five-parameter distance-radial positive-semidefinite cone controlled by a scalar and two \(2\times2\) matrices. For the 66-vertex incidence graph of \(\mathrm{AG}(3,3)\), the exponential-distance kernel is positive semidefinite in two disjoint positive-bandwidth intervals, with algebraic endpoints \((27\pm\sqrt{313})/416\) for \(e^{-2a}\) and sharp boundary ranks \(40\) and \(65\).
