On every consistent-mass periodic linear finite-element heat mesh with at least six cells, the coefficient linking nodes three steps apart is positive immediately, negative at time \(t=\ell^2/3\), and positive again eventually. Six cells are minimal for this reentrant sign effect, and their exact complete nonnegativity threshold is \(t=-(5\ell^2/6)\log\alpha\), where \(\alpha\in(1/2,3/5)\) is the unique root of \(x^8+x^6+x^4-2x^3+x^2-2x+1=0\) in that interval.
