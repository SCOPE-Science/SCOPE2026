On the 120 projective \(E_8\) root lines, even-degree spherical-harmonic evaluation has ranks \(1,35,84,119,120,119\) in degrees \(0,2,4,6,8,10\) and full rank in every even degree at least \(12\). Degree \(8\) therefore interpolates all data with exact singular-value condition number \(\sqrt{18/7}\), but degree \(10\) unexpectedly loses the constant data mode.
