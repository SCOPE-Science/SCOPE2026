In a two-species transcription--translation circuit with negative feedback, the stationary Gaussian mRNA spectrum develops and then loses a unique nonzero-frequency global maximum as feedback increases, despite distinct real negative relaxation eigenvalues throughout the transition. The exact reentrant interval is between the first two positive roots of \(2q^3-91q^2+220q-10\), whereas the protein spectrum stays low-pass throughout the real-eigenvalue regime.
