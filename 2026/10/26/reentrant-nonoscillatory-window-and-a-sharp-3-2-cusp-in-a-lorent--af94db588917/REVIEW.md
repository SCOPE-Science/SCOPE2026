# Scientific review: a reentrant pseudomode spectral window

## Correctness — PASS
The claim is restricted to the strictly positive-parameter three-by-three matrix printed in Lin et al. The characteristic polynomial and its real cubic under \(E=i\Lambda y\) were reconstructed directly. The repeated-root equations give the *complete* positive exceptional curve, not a finite sample of candidate points. Monotonicity of \(r^2(1-2r)\), the exact discriminant derivative at each double root, the sign at zero and infinity, and a separate factorization at the cusp exclude additional spectral phases. The irreducible tridiagonal recurrence forces one-dimensional eigenspaces at repeated roots, establishing actual EP2/EP3 rather than merely coincident eigenvalues. The sharp width coefficient follows from exact cubic identities about \(r=1/3\), not numerical fitting. The included symbolic/eigenspectrum program passed from its supplied package path. The Routh–Hurwitz argument separately guarantees positive decay rates. The physical evolution and normalization follow the **specific source matrix**, not a separately normalized wide-band approximation.

## Originality — PASS, residual equivalent-formulation risk
The source explicitly establishes the three-mode matrix and the EP3 at \((u,v)=(1/27,8/27)\) and plots EP2/EP3 spectral curves. These are existing results and are not credited as new. The present incremental statement is the exact necessary-and-sufficient *global interval in reservoir coupling* at fixed positive coherent coupling, plus its fully determined endpoint multiplicities, monotone boundaries and \(12\)-coefficient \(3/2\)-cusp. The relevant preprint and published full Results section were inspected around the same matrix and figure; no analytic two-boundary formula, global exclusion, or cusp-width coefficient was found in those inspected portions. A prior general approach for bosonic Heisenberg–Langevin operators analyzes an explicitly different two-mode damped/amplified Hamiltonian, not this Lorentzian three-amplitude cubic. A separate exact Liouvillian study treats a double quantum dot. Literature searches and a mathematical-research record comparison did not identify an equivalent statement. Since the exact Hamiltonian and qualitative numerical phase curves are known and standard cubic algebra can recover the result, the primary residual risk is an unpublished or unindexed analytic evaluation of those curves, or an equivalent result in more general tridiagonal non-Hermitian literature. A visual plot is not treated as proof of the quantitative global theorem, and absence of a hit is not treated as proof of novelty.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The natural physical question is whether tuning reservoir coupling at fixed coherent coupling can suppress amplitude oscillations, and over what **entire** range it does so. The result proves a precise operational distinction between pure decay and damped oscillations, with a reentrant transition and two distinct exceptional endpoints below a sharp coupling threshold. The cusp coefficient quantifies how accurately reservoir parameters must be tuned near the previously identified EP3; the interval width vanishes with a nontrivial \(3/2\) power rather than linearly. The limit is therefore more informative than merely recomputing the known EP3 coordinates, and it uses a naturally defined invariant (the length of the purely exponential spectral window). This is a model-specific analytic classification, not an assertion of new general cubic theory or an experimental discovery.
