In the Lorentzian-pseudomode bosonic trimer, varying reservoir coupling at fixed coherent coupling produces **exactly one** nonoscillatory interval, bounded by two second-order exceptional points, if and only if \((\chi/\Lambda)^2<1/27\). The interval collapses at the previously known third-order exceptional point with the new sharp width law \((\Gamma_+-\Gamma_-)/\Lambda\sim24(1/27-(\chi/\Lambda)^2)^{3/2}\).
