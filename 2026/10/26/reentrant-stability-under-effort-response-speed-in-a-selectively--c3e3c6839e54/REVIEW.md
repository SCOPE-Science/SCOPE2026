# Review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The model is analyzed on its strictly positive domain. Positive-equilibrium equations uniquely fix the coordinate triple independent of effort speed; exact differentiation gives the characteristic cubic. All coefficients are positive, and the Routh–Hurwitz determinant has exactly two simple positive zeros, with the stable–unstable–stable sign pattern. At each zero the polynomial factors, and direct implicit differentiation certifies a transverse imaginary pair. Exact arithmetic reproduces the calculations. The proof does not infer nondegenerate Hopf branches from an imaginary crossing.

## Originality

**PASS, subject to the recorded residual risks.** The identical primary source [1] gives a general cubic and varies the harvested fraction in its local-bifurcation theorem. It does not specify two effort-speed thresholds for an unchanged coexistence equilibrium, and its convenient sign restrictions exclude the new low-speed stable interval. The delay/pollution fishery model [2] does not encompass the same ODE. Literature searches and comparisons of implication found no decisive coverage. The older Kar–Chakraborty (2010) paper was located bibliographically but not read in full, so possible broader coverage remains unresolved rather than certified absent.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** A policy response speed is not automatically stabilizing: the exact admissible system changes from stable to unstable and back to stable as that speed increases, at a constant biological/economic equilibrium. The two exact thresholds and persistence under small parameter perturbations identify a meaningful phase-space boundary and a nonmonotone timescale design effect.

## Limits

This is a local spectral classification in one analytically specified bioeconomic family. No periodic orbit, super/subcritical Hopf classification, global attraction or economic optimum is proved. Prior-work comparisons are not exhaustive; independent audit has not been performed.
