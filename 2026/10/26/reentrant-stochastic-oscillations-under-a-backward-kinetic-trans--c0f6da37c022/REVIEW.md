# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The quadratic characteristic polynomial is exact for the stated three-state row generator, and its discriminant is a shifted square minus \(4ab\); this gives necessary and sufficient oscillation thresholds for all nonnegative backward rates. Strict positivity of the stationary weights proves both the explicit decreasing cycle current and defectiveness at each double nonzero eigenvalue. The coherence derivative has the claimed linear numerator and gives an exhaustive global maximum classification, including cases where no oscillations occur. The return-probability crossing interpretation follows from its nonzero projection onto the two-dimensional transient spectral subspace. A standalone checker verifies the key determinant, stationary and derivative identities symbolically, and compares 2,200 full eigensystems and stationary laws, 900 optimizations, 600 Jordan ranks and 900 transient matrix exponentials. The numerical checks support but do not replace the proof.

## Originality
PASS with historical-priority risk. The exact formula for arbitrary three-state generator eigenvalues is not new. Takahashi and Ohzeki (arXiv:1509.08212), inspected in accessible full text, analyze eigenvalue reality versus complexity in a three-state fastest-relaxation problem, but do not furnish the one-reverse-edge reentrant phase portrait, stationary-current opposition and unique coherence-optimal tuning of this specific family. Weber and Pande (2015), inspected in complete accepted-manuscript text, treat entropy-driven oscillation in a 101-state triangle network, not the controlled single-triangle spectral classification. Qian (2006) and Pressé et al. (2010) are closely relevant foundational treatments of biochemical cycle kinetics; their accessible abstracts and excerpts do not establish coverage, but full-text retrieval failed, so equivalent earlier treatment remains a nontrivial possibility. Actual published-literature and prior-result searches found no direct matching statement; absence of a match alone does not prove novelty.

## Value
PASS. A controlled backward reaction simultaneously reduces the stationary cycle flux and can create previously absent damped oscillations; the sharp two-boundary window distinguishes transient spectral visibility from stationary transport. The globally unique optimum and the full threshold hierarchy offer an interpretable exact design rule for a minimal physical kinetic cycle and exclude changes at unexamined intermediate rates. These facts have intrinsic meaning for three-state kinetic mechanisms even without a proposed device or more complicated network theorem.

## Closest literature and limits
Qian (2006) establishes the nonequilibrium single-molecule/chemical-cycle background. Pressé et al. (2010) study cycle fluctuations and backward-spin probabilities. Weber and Pande (2015) study a related but larger entropy-driven oscillatory system. Takahashi and Ohzeki (arXiv:1509.08212) provide strongly relevant general three-state spectral tools; merely specializing those known tools is not claimed new. Full text of Qian and Pressé was not obtained; direct priority and implications there remain a residual risk. Scope excludes finite reverse rates on every link, thermal detailed balance, and nonlinear/sustained oscillations.
