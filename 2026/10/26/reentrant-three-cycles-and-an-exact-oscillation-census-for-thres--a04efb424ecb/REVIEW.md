PASS. Each vertex's advancement depends exclusively on the opposite shore's three successor-color counts clipped at two. The class-homogeneity and saturation lemma justifies the reduction to 676 support pairs and 2808 tagged states for arbitrary multiplicities. The attached verifier exhausts this entire state space, determines allowable tagged cycles and entry times, and rejects mixed-three/four tagged periods from a single configuration. It verifies the exact primitive-period-three and period-four generating polynomials. Six direct vertex-level enumerations totalling 94,770 configurations independently agree with the formulas, and explicit witnesses establish sharpness. Limits: no claim beyond three colors, threshold two, complete bipartite graphs.

## Originality

PASS, with bounded-search risk. The threshold-two three-color successor rule is present in Fisch–Gravner–Griffeath's 1991 lattice framework, but the inspected material analyzes spatial wave/fixation phenomena rather than labelled bipartite recurrence counts. Durrett–Griffeath treat lattice asymptotics. Bello–Sivakoff concern stochastic infinite-tree threshold dynamics; Gravner–Lyu–Sivakoff consider threshold-one graph dynamics. The closest accessible earlier complete-bipartite census concerns threshold one, whose primitive three-orbit existence is monotone in each shore's size. Here threshold two produces a genuinely nonmonotone size-four/five exclusion, not implied by the earlier theorem. Database and literature searches did not identify equivalent or stronger covering results, but indexing gaps and unidentified papers remain possible.

## Mathematical value

PASS. The natural question is how raising a cyclic contact threshold changes oscillations on a minimal two-community interaction network. The result identifies a reentrant oscillation regime, gives exact primitive orbit multiplicities and all-size sharp convergence, and exhibits a robust asymptotic dominance of three-cycles. This is a structural classification and counterexample to monotone dependence of three-cycle existence on shore size, not an arbitrary finite tabulation.
