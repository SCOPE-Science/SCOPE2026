# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** Every strict path endomorphism is a nearest-neighbor walk. The proof shows exactly that idempotents retract onto an interval and are independent target-interval walks outside it. Reflection invariance forces a centered interval and paired mirror-image exterior walks. The adjacency-matrix identities and the stable-deficiency ballot counts follow formally. The standalone independent direct enumeration confirms all rank coefficients and orbit sizes for orders \(2\) through \(14\). This finite enumeration cannot prove the infinite-family statement.

**Originality — PASS within examined coverage.** Dimitrova–Fernandes–Koppitz–Quinteiro (arXiv:1810.09307) establish the strict graph-endomorphism framework and generator ranks, not the full idempotent image-rank and conjugacy spectra. Lin–Zeng count equivalence kernels, not retraction orbits, and Eggleton–Morayne count ordinary path homomorphisms. Cycle idempotent orbit formulas concern a different topology with joined exterior arcs. An inaccessible earlier specialist work on path congruences remains an explicit priority risk.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The result determines a complete natural finite-monoid invariant throughout an infinite graph family and identifies the stabilizer structure and universal near-full-rank behavior. It is motivated independently of the finite test cases.

**Limitations.** This applies only to strict total endomorphisms of loopless paths, not weak or partial variants. The historical literature check was not exhaustive, and there is no independent mathematical audit.
