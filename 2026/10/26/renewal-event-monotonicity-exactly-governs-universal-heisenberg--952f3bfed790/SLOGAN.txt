For discrete-time quantum evolution with an independent age-dependent renewal reset and any fixed unitary between resets, **universal Heisenberg P- and CP-divisibility coincide and hold exactly when the absolute-time renewal-event probabilities are nondecreasing**. Even increasing waiting-age reset hazards can violate this criterion, producing an explicit three-level example that is Schrödinger CP-divisible but Heisenberg non-positive-divisible.
