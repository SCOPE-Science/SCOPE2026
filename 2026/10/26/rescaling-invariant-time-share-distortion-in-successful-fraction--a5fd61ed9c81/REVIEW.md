# Review of a sharp fractional-resetting time-share law

## Correctness — PASS
The model specifies full waiting-clock renewal after each Poisson reset, the two absorbing endpoints, and the final excursion conditioned on its absorption side. Exact path weights are \((\widehat\psi(r)/2)^K\). On a fixed side, the unique shortest route dominates all other paths by two jump factors; bounding all longer path weights gives a controlled \(O((r\tau)^{-2\beta})\) remainder. The exponentially tilted Mittag-Leffler waiting law has the exact Laplace transform \((1+a)/((1+s)^\beta+a)\) with \(a=(r\tau)^{-\beta}\); for \(a<1\), an absolutely convergent Gamma signed series supplies an \(L^1\) first-order approximation. Its product measure pushes forward under normalization to Dirichlet measures, and standard Beta-Gamma identities yield the coefficient. The signed density integrates to zero and is nonconstant for \(0<\beta<1\), \(m\ge2\), establishing positivity. For \(\beta=1\), the exponential tilt preserves the exponential family and Dirichlet waiting-time shares exactly, while explicit lattice detour counting determines the first nonzero term. The accompanying independent finite checks passed 2804 deterministic comparisons, including boundary detour cases, matrix resolvents, Gamma-mixture transforms and numerical density integrals. The proof is analytical; the finite checks do not establish the asymptotic quantifiers.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risks
The exact published renewal-flux/conditional-time formulas of Pal–Das–Pal, prior CTRW restarting theory, finite-chain splitting resolvents and frequent-reset total first-passage asymptotics are acknowledged. Searches and inspected primary sections did not locate a complete **marked successful-excursion time-share** theorem with the first-order Dirichlet signed correction or the strict contrast between \((r\tau)^{-\beta}\) for fractional waiting and \((r\tau)^{-2}\) for exponential waiting. The classical Gamma-to-Dirichlet fact, single-hop tilted waiting law, and first-passage splitting transforms by themselves are not claimed to be new. The sharper object is the normalized vector of intrapath waiting intervals conditioned on final success, rather than total arrival time. Prior unexamined or more general marked-renewal results may imply the exact statement; independent originality assessment is needed. Colantoni–Pagnini's abstract was inspected, but its unavailable full text creates additional bounded comparison uncertainty.

## Value — PASS
For non-Markovian transport, a successful excursion's *division of time among successive trapping sites* is observable and invariant under a change of time units. The complete sharp total-variation expansion determines whether the limiting Dirichlet approximation is accurate at a specified reset rate; the coefficient is strictly positive in the entire fractional parameter range. The cancellation at the exponential endpoint shows a discontinuous change of the leading error exponent, rather than a routine first-passage moment computation. This offers an explicit probabilistic benchmark for inference from path-resolved residence times, and sharpens a natural observable not determined by endpoint splitting or aggregate duration alone.

## Scope and comparison limits
The result is finite-lattice, symmetric-jump, full-reset, fixed-parameter and Mittag-Leffler specific. The one-step case is degenerate and excluded. The proof gives neither a uniform \(\beta\to1\) crossover nor a continuum spatial limit. The accessible inspected primary texts are listed in the scientific audit. A broader theorem on marks of strongly tilted renewals could imply the present statement and is the chief remaining literature risk.
