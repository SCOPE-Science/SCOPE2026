Under high-rate complete resetting, the fraction of a successful fractional continuous-time random walk's excursion spent in each waiting interval approaches a Dirichlet law, but the total-variation error is sharply \(C_{m,\beta}(r\tau)^{-\beta}\) for \(0<\beta<1\). For exponential waiting times, the intrinsic fraction error vanishes exactly and the first nonshortest routes leave only an order-\((r\tau)^{-2}\) error.
