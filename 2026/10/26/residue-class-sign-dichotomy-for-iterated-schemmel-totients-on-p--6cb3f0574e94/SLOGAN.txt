For every even Schemmel parameter \(m\ge2\), all sufficiently large prime powers are strictly abundant or deficient under the iterated sum \(D_m\), according exactly to whether \(p-m\) is coprime to the primes at most \(m\); the abundant prime bases have an explicit Dirichlet-density product. For \(m=4\), this becomes an exact classification at every prime power, with abundance precisely for primes \(p\ge13\) congruent to \(2\pmod3\).
