For odd-characteristic truncated \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\) current Lie algebras, every kernel size of the iterated restricted \(p\)-power map has an explicit finite sum from smooth quadratic-cone lifts. Among nonzero restricted-nilpotent elements the maximal height is \(\lceil\log_p(2m+1)\rceil\), while a uniform element has height asymptotic to \(\log_p m\) in probability.
