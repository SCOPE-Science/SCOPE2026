For \(q=p^e\) with odd \(p\) and \(e\ge2\), every proper nontrivial retract of \(\operatorname{UT}_3(\mathbb F_q)\) is an elementary abelian graph subgroup of rank at most \(e\), with exactly \((q+1){e\brack r}_p p^{r(3e-r)}\) retractions of image order \(p^r\). The rank defect of a uniformly sampled nontrivial proper retraction has an explicit nondegenerate discrete limit as \(e\to\infty\), and no such proper retract is normal.
