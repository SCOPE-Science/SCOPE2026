# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For any index set of size at least two, a central subset can contain no off-diagonal unit: a suitable singleton multiplied on the left produces a nonzero unit with a second coordinate impossible in the right product. Commuting with every singleton then forces equal diagonal-support membership for all indices. The full diagonal without zero squares to the diagonal with zero, so the central idempotents are exactly {0} and E, the diagonal with zero. Multiplying by E adjoins zero; its characteristic image identifies with all binary relations, the absorbing singleton corresponding to the empty relation. Nonempty rectangles give the least nonzero Green J-class by singleton sandwiches. Their right and left Green classes recover the row and column supports. The rectangle zero-product condition recovers disjointness between supports, hence inclusion; singleton atoms recover row and column relabelings which agree by singleton incidence. Edge membership of every relation is recovered by singleton sandwiches. After removing that simultaneous relabeling, an automorphism fixes the relation copy pointwise and has E Phi(X)=EX. A set not containing zero cannot map to its fixed zero-adjoined partner by injectivity. Thus there are no additional automorphisms. In finite rank, each pair of permutation cycles of lengths a,b yields gcd(a,b) ordered-pair orbits, so Burnside counts all fixed subsets as 2^(c+1)-1 after removing the empty one. The independent cycle-type calculation gave 19, 207, 6087, 583935, and 193857983, with direct orbit enumeration also confirming n=2,3. For support size k, all ordered pairs involving a moved index have orbit length at least two, so c<=n^2-nk/2; at most n^k permutations move k indices. The corresponding normalized nonidentity sum is bounded by the geometric tail starting at k=2 of ratio n*2^(-n/2), which tends to zero. The subtracted singleton count in the Burnside formula does not affect the asymptotic.

## originality

PASS

The supported distinct result is the intrinsic recovery of the relation subsemigroup and the proof that its simultaneous relabelings exhaust the automorphisms of the nonempty matrix-unit power semigroup. The internal relation theorem is classical. The orbit formula, terms and asymptotic, as orbit-enumeration facts, are the known loop-allowed relation results with an extra invariant zero-membership bit and the exclusion of the empty subset. Their relevance here follows from the structural theorem, and they receive no independent novelty credit. A particularly close prior power-semigroup paper, Tamura (1986), remains an access and priority risk but no inspected statement establishes the same automorphism classification.

## value

PASS

The central idempotent and injective restriction determine exactly what multiplication alone remembers about the indices when no union structure is imposed and the base zero creates distinct lifts of a relation. That is a natural structural question for a noncommutative power semigroup and is resolved even for arbitrary index sets. The transformed classical counts clarify the consequence of rigidity; they are not the reason for the value pass.
