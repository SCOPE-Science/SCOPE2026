For every finite matrix-unit Brandt semigroup with at least two indices, the entire nonempty power semigroup has exactly the original index-permutation symmetries: its automorphism group is \(S_n\), with no hidden zero-adjoining symmetries. The number of element orbits is \(\frac{2}{n!}\sum_{\sigma\in S_n}2^{\sum_{a,b\in\lambda(\sigma)}\gcd(a,b)}-1\), asymptotic to \(2^{n^2+1}/n!\).
