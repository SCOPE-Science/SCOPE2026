Four consecutive four-letter greedy arches cannot occur in any ternary squarefree word: three are forced to alternate as \(abac\,babc\,abac\), and a fourth would create a square. This yields \(\iota(w)\ge\lfloor4(|w|-4)/15\rfloor+1\) for \(|w|\ge4\), and a sharp five-universality forcing threshold at length \(19\).
