Exactly \(100\) unordered triples of translates of the quadratic residues (including zero) cover \(\mathbb F_{25}\), and all \(100\) arise from the single triple \(\{0,u,1+3u\}\) by translation and multiplication by a nonzero square, where \(u^2=2\). These are precisely the minimum dominating triples of the \(25\)-vertex Paley graph, forming one affine-square orbit with a three-element stabilizer.
