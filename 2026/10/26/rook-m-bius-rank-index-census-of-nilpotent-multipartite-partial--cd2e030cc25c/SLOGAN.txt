For complete multipartite graphs with at least three nonempty parts, a rook-monoid Möbius transform of colored directed-chain exponentials gives the exact joint rank and nilpotency-index census of partial isometries. When every part has size at least two, the largest index is \(\max_s(s c_s+c_{s+1})\), with a spanning nilpotent chain precisely for part sizes differing by at most one.
