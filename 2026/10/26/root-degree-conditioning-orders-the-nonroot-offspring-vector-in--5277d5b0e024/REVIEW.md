# Review

## Correctness

**PASS.** The root-parent Bernoulli decomposition is exact. Conditioning on its cardinality yields weights \(w_j=1/(j-2)\), and positive elementary symmetric log concavity establishes nested inclusion couplings. Sharing independent nonroot parent labels makes all nonroot offspring counts decrease coordinatewise. The positive-probability distinct-parent event establishes strict leaf-count mean inequalities, including endpoint root degrees. Exact finite checks pass, but no finite enumeration substitutes for the infinite-family proof.

## Originality

Janson's degree-count limit theory is not the stated ordering. Eslava, López and Ortiz's Section 3 (2024) gives the crucial representation and an arbitrary conditional-window coupling, but not monotone inclusion across all exact degrees. Full retrieval of Janson's author PDF failed; general stochastic covering is a plausible alternate route. These are recorded historical-priority limitations, not proof of independent discovery.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** Conditioning on one natural local statistic (root offspring count) orders the entire remaining offspring vector. Consequent nested leaf sets control every increasing property of labeled leaves. This is strictly stronger than a sign or closed scalar covariance, and holds at every finite tree size and every attainable root degree. It does not apply to arbitrary random-tree models or identify exact leaf-count distributions.
