For a degree-proportional attachment tree seeded by one edge, an initial vertex's degree has **strictly positive covariance** with the tree's total leaf count for every \(n\ge4\), with exact covariance \(\frac{2p_n}{3}[n-2(2n-3)p_n]\), where \(p_n=4^{-(n-2)}\binom{2n-4}{n-2}\). The covariance grows on the \(\sqrt n\) scale even though \(\sqrt n\,\operatorname{Corr}(D_n,L_n)\to\sqrt{2/(\pi-2)}\), distinguishing degree-proportional growth from uniform attachment.
