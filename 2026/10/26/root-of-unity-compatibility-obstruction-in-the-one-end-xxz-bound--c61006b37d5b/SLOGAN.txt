At the free-fermion point \(q=i\), the normalized boundary recurrence with \(u_0=1\) and an x-directed field is compatible only when \(g^2=1/2\), provided \(\sin(\pi s)\ne0\); even then, it leaves \(u_2\) undetermined. This is a limitation of that recurrence, not a claim that the physical steady state fails to exist.
