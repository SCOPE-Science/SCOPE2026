In the stationary Moran forest, turning an attachment into a new root can only increase the terminal-vertex count, with a strictly increasing birth-time influence. The total numbers of roots and terminal vertices have an exact positive covariance and limiting correlation \(\frac{2e-5}{2}\sqrt{\frac6{e-2}}\approx0.63088\), even though distinct terminal-vertex indicators are nonpositively correlated.
