For every coprime gap-four rational Hartogs triangle, the diagonal Bergman numerator has two simple negative-real interior zeros and a simple conjugate pair. As the denominator \(n\to\infty\) through odd integers, three roots approach the unit circle at two distinct sharp radial speeds, \(2\sqrt3/n\) and \(\sqrt3/n\), while the fourth converges to \(-2+\sqrt3\).
