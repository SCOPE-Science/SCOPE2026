For every nondegenerate rank-one evolution algebra \(xy=B(x,y)u\) in odd characteristic, weight-\(\lambda\) Rota–Baxter operators form an explicit three-family union determined by isotropic images and adjoints. In dimension three over \(\mathbb F_q\), the exact count is \(q^3+q^2-q\) for zero weight and twice that for nonzero weight; the square-zero subcount detects whether \(B(u,u)=0\).
