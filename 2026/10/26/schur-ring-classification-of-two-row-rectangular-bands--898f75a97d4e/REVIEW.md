# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For primitive block sums [X],[Y], coefficient at (i,j) in [X][Y] equals row count r_X(i) times column count c_Y(j). Closure iff both profiles are constant on every primitive block. When two row profiles differ, all blocks split by row and induce identical column partitions. When they agree, a column-profile fiber is either a full strip or two complementary one-point-per-column blocks; row balance requires equal-size half-subsets and |C| even. Automorphism subgroups generated by within-fiber column permutations and a simultaneous row-swap/half-swap realize every partition. Weighted Bell enumeration yields Ω=B_m+W_m, W_m via b_j=1+(1/2)binom(j,j/2) even j, EGF and counts m=1..6 as given. The all-m proof reconstructed; independent enumeration of all partitions for m≤4 gives 2,5,13,48, not an infinite proof.
