Every semigroup Schur ring over the two-row rectangular band \(R_{2,m}\) is an automorphism-orbit ring, and all its blocks have an explicit classification by paired row partitions or balanced even-split column strips. Its number is \(B_m+W_m\), where \(B_m\) is the Bell number and \(W_m=\sum_{\pi\vdash[m]}\prod_{C\in\pi}b_{|C|}\), with \(b_k=1\) for odd \(k\) and \(b_k=1+\binom{k}{k/2}/2\) for even \(k\); exactly \(W_m-B_m\) are not direct products.
