For any finite irreducible loop-free semi-Markov network, agreement of **all** second first-passage moments with its pseudo-rate Markov chain is equivalent to two local identities: exponential-sized holding-time second moments and zero first-order correlation between holding time and next destination. Three-state networks with destination-dependent, non-exponential lognormal holding times can satisfy both identities, so even the complete first-and-second hitting-moment table does not identify memorylessness.
