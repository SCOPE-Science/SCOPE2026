# Scientific review: secure total domination on clique bouquets

## Correctness (PASS)

The theorem is checked directly from the original open-neighborhood total-domination condition and one-defender exchange definition. The proof separates cases according to whether the common center is selected; each case gives a necessary-and-sufficient per-blade occupancy rule. Independent subset enumeration over 66 heterogeneous clique bouquets agrees with the rule, the complete polynomial, and the inclusion-minimal polynomial for 127904 subsets. The finite experiment corroborates but does not replace the proof.

## Originality (PASS with residual priority risk)

The earliest dated foundational secure-total-domination source used here is Cabrera Martínez et al. (2019, DOI 10.3390/sym11091165). Jha (2020, DOI 10.1016/j.akcej.2019.10.005) covers minimization on all cographs, including this graph family, but provides scalar algorithms rather than the complete two-polynomial classification. The published complete-multipartite secure-total-domination enumerator is inapplicable to clique bouquets, and the published generalized-windmill dissociation enumerator treats a different parameter. No indexed exact clique-bouquet secure-total-domination polynomial was found in the targeted queries. This is an evidence-limited priority assessment, not a claim that search failure proves novelty. Accessible indexed excerpts of primary text were inspected; some publisher full-page fetching was limited. Unindexed equivalent literature is a residual risk.

## Value (PASS)

Secure total domination models defender relocation while preserving open-neighborhood coverage. The exact product formula resolves all cardinalities for every heterogeneous clique bouquet rather than only the known-tractable minimum. More importantly, the inclusion-minimal spectrum contains only \(t+1\) and \(2t\), producing \(t-2\) missing sizes in natural connected chordal block graphs. This unbounded gap is a structural distinction between minimum and inclusion-minimal robust guarding placements and is not determined by existing optimization bounds.

## Scientific limits

Finite checks used graphs with at most 12 vertices and do not establish the infinite formulas. Priority outside the inspected records and materials is not proven. Independent validation remains outstanding.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
