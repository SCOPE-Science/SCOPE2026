In a voting game with one required veto seat and \(m\) alternative disjoint committees of \(a\) voters, replacing the single veto seat by \(t\) jointly necessary veto seats can **increase**, not decrease, squared normalized Deegan–Packel concentration. The increase has an exact parameter criterion, can approach a factor of \(t\), and first occurs for an original electorate of 13 seats within this family.
