# Scientific review: matching-weighted complete graph projections

## Correctness — PASS
The claim covers all even orders \(N\ge8\) and every real conductance square root \(x>0\). An explicit two-eigenspace decomposition of the weighted Laplacian gives the exact vertex voltages for both edge orbits. Each absolute-current contribution is enumerated, including the sign change at \(x=1\); the complementary projection follows from its positive diagonal entries below one. The differences of the two orbit sums factor into displayed linear thresholds with positive denominators. Differentiation and sharp comparison with the nonmatching branches prove global weight maxima, including the exceptional order eight. An exact rational implementation independently reconstructed 1,840 full columns across 32 weighted complete graphs. The finite checks do not substitute for the unrestricted algebraic argument.

## Originality — PASS, with explicit residual risk
The relevant 2023 primary work treats invariant cycle-space projections, discrete tori, and Hamming graphs. Its examined statements and proof sections do not classify two conductance-weighted edge orbits in complete graphs. A 2026 weighted-network theorem uses the same symmetrized transfer-current matrix but supplies a spectral bound, not exact endpoint column sums or maximizing-edge transitions. Weighted effective-resistance optimization concerns a different aggregate functional. Closely related single-exception biclique projections, unweighted complete-graph deletions, and matching-deleted bicliques do not imply this fully connected two-conductance phase diagram. Several targeted exact-phrase, parameter, and equivalence searches did not identify a covering theorem. This is evidence of distinction, not a mathematical proof of global novelty; less-indexed explicit network calculations remain a residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A complete graph is maximally connected in the unweighted sense, yet a single matching-weight parameter causes two strictly separated transitions in the maximizing columns of complementary orthogonal projections. The family supplies the full phase diagram for a natural matching-symmetric perturbation, an order-eight exception, and exact equal global amplification factors \((1+\sqrt{2N-3})/2\sim\sqrt{N/2}\) at two different weights in every larger eligible dimension. The object and invariant are independently motivated by weighted transfer-current and cycle-space operator theory; the result is not a parameter renaming or a mere finite table entry.

## Limits and closest literature
Canonical real square-root-conductance orthogonal projections only; no minimum over nonorthogonal projections and no general heterogeneous weighting. Closest inspected literature: Dilworth–Kutzarova–Ostrovskii (2023), arXiv:2305.12582, particularly Sections 1, 2.1, 2.6, 2.7; Gurel-Gurevich–Nachmias–Sachdeva (2026), arXiv:2605.24130, especially Theorem 1.3 and weighted definitions. Ghosh–Boyd–Saberi (2008), DOI:10.1137/050645452, addresses sum-of-resistances optimization rather than these edge-space \(\ell_1\) norms.
