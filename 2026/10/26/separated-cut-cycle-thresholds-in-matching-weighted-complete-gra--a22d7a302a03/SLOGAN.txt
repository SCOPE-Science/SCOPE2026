In a complete graph with one perfect matching given conductance \(x^2\), the cut- and cycle-projection endpoint norms switch maximizing edge classes at two different explicit thresholds, separated asymptotically by a factor of three in \(x\). For every even order \(N\ge10\), their globally optimized norms coincide at \((1+\sqrt{2N-3})/2\), but the two maxima occur at different conductances.
