For rank-three Kneser disjointness graphs, the canonical cut and cycle projection endpoint norms have explicit rational formulas at every \(n\ge7\), with uniquely different optimizing dimensions: the cut norm peaks at \(n=8\) with \(257/77\), while the cycle norm peaks at \(n=9\) with \(143/35\). An exact four-orbit Laplacian calculation proves both formulas and the eventual strict decrease, extending the analysis beyond strongly regular disjointness graphs.
