In characteristic five, the smooth reciprocal genus-four family \(y^2=x^{10}+a x^8+b x^6+b x^4+a x^2+1\) has exactly seven coefficient-marked Jacobian \(5\)-rank-zero members: one over \(\mathbf F_5\) and six conjugates first defined over \(\mathbf F_{5^6}\). Its smooth parameter locus has only ranks \(0,2,4\) and no superspecial members.
