The ternary three-dimensional Hamming graph admits a proper seven-colour edge-colouring in which every four-cycle is rainbow, resolving its previously open seven-versus-eight \(B\)-colouring gap. Repeating the explicit three-periodic certificate gives \(q_B(C_{3a}\square C_{3b}\square C_{3c})=7\) for all positive odd integers \(a,b,c\).
