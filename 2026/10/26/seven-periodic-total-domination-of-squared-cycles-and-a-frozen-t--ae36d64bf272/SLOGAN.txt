For the square \(C_n^2\) of each cycle of length \(n\ge7\), total domination has an exact seven-periodic minimum, correcting an incompatible five-periodic published formula. Among minimum configurations, one-vertex exchanges freeze all seven optima at orders \(7k\) for \(k\ge2\), whereas at orders \(7k+2\) they form a single cycle of length \(7k+2\).
