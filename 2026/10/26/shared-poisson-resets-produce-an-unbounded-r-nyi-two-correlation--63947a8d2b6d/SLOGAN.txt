For two noninteracting tight-binding quantum walkers, resetting with one shared Poisson clock gives exactly the same one-particle stationary density matrices as two independent reset clocks, but a separable, strictly more collision-pure joint state. Its positive Rényi-two correlation contrast diverges as \(\log(J/r)-2\log\log(J/r)+O(1)\) in the rare-reset limit \(r\downarrow0\).
