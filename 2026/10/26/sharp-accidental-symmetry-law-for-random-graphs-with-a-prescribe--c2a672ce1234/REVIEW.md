# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The graph sample is exactly \(m^2\) independent fair binary parameters. The exact automorphism kernel follows from the disagreement relation; the independent \(G(m,1/2)\) graph and two support-size union bounds establish a quantitative negligible error. The finite exhaustive checker independently confirms the exact fibre-kernel identity for every graph through four paired orbits. No finite computation is treated as an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS, qualified.** Searched aliases including random involution-invariant graphs, graph-cover switching, wreath-product stabilizers, and prescribed-automorphism symmetry defects. The nearest primary text on standard double covers of abelian Cayley graphs (Xia–Zhang–Zheng, arXiv:2601.20214) studies a distinct constrained Cayley and bipartite model; the Koponen arXiv:2610.16163 finite-model-property paper studies a different theorem. Broad asymmetry theorems do not directly imply the samplewise disagreement-graph kernel or the sharp first exponential correction. An unidentified broader graph-cover automorphism theorem may still exist; historical priority is not certified.

**Value — PASS.** Identifying the exact accident mechanism and its sharp exponential probability clarifies how a prescribed unary involution changes generic symmetry: a typical expansion has only its unavoidable symmetry, with leading exceptions localized to one disconnected fibre. This sharp structural result is materially stronger than a qualitative almost-sure rigidity or a small finite count, and is directly relevant to random finite relational expansions and symmetry-weighted model counting.

**Limits and risks.** Only automorphisms commuting with the named involution are studied. Arbitrary automorphisms of the bare graph are not classified. The result is for uniformly sampled undirected graphs with an involution of \(m\) 2-cycles, as \(m\to\infty\), and is not independently validated. Priority among specialized cover-graph literature remains a risk.
