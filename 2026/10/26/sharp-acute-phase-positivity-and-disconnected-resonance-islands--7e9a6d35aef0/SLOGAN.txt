For the damped-cosine Hamming kernel \(r^{d_H}\cos(\theta d_H)\), positive semidefiniteness in the acute-angle sector is equivalent to two exact sine inequalities for every alphabet and product dimension. The admissible angle set splits into two separated islands in every binary dimension and, surprisingly, for nonbinary alphabets only in dimension two; the nonbinary collision point is an exactly tight first-harmonic projector.
