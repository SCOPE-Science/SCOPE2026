For the geodesic covariance \(e^{-a\theta}(1+b\theta)\) on every odd sphere \(\mathbb S^{2m+1}\), the sharp admissibility interval has a dimension-independent upper endpoint and a strictly tightening negative endpoint, even though both boundary kernels remain strictly positive definite on distinct sites. Across all sphere dimensions the exact common interval is \([-2/\pi,\,a(1-e^{-\pi a})/(1-(1-\pi a)e^{-\pi a})]\), with the lower boundary given by random-hemisphere sign correlation.
