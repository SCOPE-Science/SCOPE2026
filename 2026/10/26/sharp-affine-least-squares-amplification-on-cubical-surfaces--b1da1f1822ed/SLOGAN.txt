For affine least-squares projection sampled uniformly on the facets of the \(d\)-cube, the exact sharp endpoint norm is a two-shift Irwin--Hall moment and equals \(\sqrt{6d/\pi}(1-47/(60d)+O(d^{-2}))\). Compared with normalized-volume sampling on the same cube, the surface norm is asymptotically smaller by precisely \(\sqrt{6/(\pi d)}+O(d^{-3/2})\).
