For local affine \(L^2\) least-squares projection on every \(d\)-dimensional parallelepiped, the exact \(L^\infty\)-norm is a rational alternating-power sum with asymptotic \(\sqrt{6d/\pi}\bigl(1+13/(60d)+o(d^{-1})\bigr)\). Strictly increasing amplification makes dimension five the first dimension in which some input between zero and one projects below minus one, in contrast to dimension-linear growth on simplices.
