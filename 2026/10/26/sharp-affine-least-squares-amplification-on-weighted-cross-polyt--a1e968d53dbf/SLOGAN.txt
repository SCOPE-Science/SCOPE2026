For weighted affine \(L^2\)-least-squares projection on every stretched cross-polytope, the exact sharp \(L^1\) and \(L^\infty\) norm depends only on \(m=d+\alpha\), with formula \(1+\frac{m(m+3)}{2(m+1)}(\frac{m(m+3)}{(m+1)(m+2)})^m\). The norm increases strictly with effective dimension and grows like \(m/2\), even when the underlying weighted measures concentrate at a single point.
