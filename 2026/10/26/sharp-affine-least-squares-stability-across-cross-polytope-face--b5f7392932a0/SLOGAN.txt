Uniform sampling on every intermediate-dimensional face skeleton of the regular cross-polytope has an exact affine least-squares projection norm: \(1+\frac{s+1}{2}(1-\frac{2}{d(s+1)})^s\), where \(d\) is ambient dimension and \(s-1\) is face dimension. Vertex evaluations are uniquely sharp, the constants increase strictly with face rank, and their dimensional error lies in an explicit interval of width \(1/d\).
