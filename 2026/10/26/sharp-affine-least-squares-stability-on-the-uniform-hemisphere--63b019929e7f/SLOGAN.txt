The affine \(L^2\) projector on the uniformly measured curved hemisphere of \(S^2\) has sharp \(L^1\) and \(L^\infty\) norms \(61\sqrt5/15-7\), strictly above the full-sphere norm \(5/3\). Exactly the equatorial evaluations are worst-case, their spherical-cap kernel signs give every bounded extremizer, and no integrable extremizer attains the corresponding \(L^1\) norm.
