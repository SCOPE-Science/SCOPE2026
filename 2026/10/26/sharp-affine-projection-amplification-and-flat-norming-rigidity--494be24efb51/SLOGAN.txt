For each fixed finite \(p>2\), affine least-squares projection on the uniform \(d\)-dimensional \(\ell_p\) ball has the sharp asymptotic endpoint norm \(c_p\sqrt d\), where \(c_p=\sqrt{2/\pi}\,p^{-1/p}\sqrt{\Gamma(1/p)/\Gamma(3/p)}\). The complete leading-order Lebesgue-function profile is governed by normalized Euclidean length, and asymptotically worst evaluation points are exactly those whose coordinate magnitudes become flat in normalized \(p\)-mean.
