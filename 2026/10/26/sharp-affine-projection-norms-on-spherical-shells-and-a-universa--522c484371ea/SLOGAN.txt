For every Euclidean spherical shell and every dimension at least two, the affine \(L^2\)-projection has an exact sharp \(L^1/L^\infty\) norm given by the difference of two explicit ball-cap moments. When an expanding concentric hole first meets the negative projection-kernel cap, the sharp norm develops a universal one-sided singularity of order \((d+3)/2\); in the plane the exact contact radius is \(2-\sqrt3\).
