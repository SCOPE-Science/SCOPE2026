The first five powers \((\sin r/r)^m\), for \(m=1,\ldots,5\), are radial positive-definite kernels exactly through Euclidean dimensions \(3,5,5,7,7\), respectively. Their critical spectra exhibit positive-radius atoms, two genuine unit-ball spectral holes, and an upward density jump that blocks an otherwise plausible one-dimensional lift.
