For every power depth, a single Rademacher sketch beats an isotropic Gaussian sketch on some orientations of a rotated \(2\times2\) positive-definite matrix and loses on others, with the exact crossover \(\sin^2(2\theta)=(1+r^2)/(1+r)^2\), where \(r=(b/a)^{2q+1}\). At diagonal resonance its mean squared Frobenius error is \((a^2+b^2)/2\) at every depth, whereas Gaussian error converges to the optimal \(b^2\); the worse-than-Gaussian angular window shrinks at rate \((b/a)^{q+1/2}\).
