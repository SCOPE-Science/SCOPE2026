For constant positive drift and homogeneous dispersion, two absorbed tracer-arrival distributions have an exact first-order stochastic dominance test: both dispersion and the squared-speed-to-dispersion ratio must be ordered in the same direction; otherwise their cumulative arrival curves cross exactly once. At equal drift but unequal dispersion, the crossing lies after mean travel time and escapes to infinity as inverse squared drift, with a uniquely determined scaling constant.
