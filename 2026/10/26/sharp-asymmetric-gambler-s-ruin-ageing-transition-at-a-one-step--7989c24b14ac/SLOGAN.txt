For a biased gambler’s ruin walk absorbed at \(-1\) and \(b\), the two-step-grouped exit lifetime has an increasing failure rate only at \(b\le2\), at \(b=3\) for \(p\ge1/2\), and at \(b=4\) above the sharp golden-ratio threshold \((\sqrt5-1)/2\). For every \(b\ge5\) the hazard strictly drops immediately.
