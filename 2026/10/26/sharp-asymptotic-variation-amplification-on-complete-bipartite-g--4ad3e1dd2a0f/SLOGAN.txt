For centered maximal operators on unweighted complete bipartite graphs \(K_{a,b}\), sharp total-variation amplification has an explicit two-sided finite-size bracket of width below \(2/(\min\{a,b\}+1)\). As both part sizes grow, its exact asymptotic value is \(1+\max\{a,b\}/(a+b)\), ranging from \(3/2\) for balanced communities to \(2\) under strong imbalance; the finite \(K_{4,4}\) already exhibits strict variation amplification.
