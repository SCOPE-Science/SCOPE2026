For central trinomial coefficients modulo five, the four nonzero residue counts have **exact** prefix-discrepancy envelopes at every cutoff \(5^L\), with sharp maximum \(3L+\alpha_r\) and minimum \(-\lfloor(5L+\beta_r)/2\rfloor\). Every residue has density \(1/4\), yet its attained positive and negative discrepancies grow at distinct optimal logarithmic rates.
