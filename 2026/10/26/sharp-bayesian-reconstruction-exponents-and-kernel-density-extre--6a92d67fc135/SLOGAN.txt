A noninjective binary additive cellular automaton becomes invertible almost surely under every biased full-support Bernoulli source; its sharp finite-window decoding error exponent equals a periodic kernel-density invariant times a universal Bernoulli large-deviation rate. Across all support-width-\(d\) rules, the kernel-density range has sharp endpoints \(1/d\) and \(2^{d-1}/(2^d-1)\), with a unique hardest rule and primitive-polynomial examples attaining the fastest reconstruction.
