The annular Bergman zeros of rational finite-holonomy perturbations of punctured-elliptic pseudoconvex domains form two \(q\)-fold off-diagonal zero circles, while irrational holonomy remains zero-free. Their interior product-root obeys the sharp law \(S_{q,-}=-2q\log2+2+O(q^{-1})\), so both circles reach their limiting boundary at the universal \((\log q)/q\) scale.
