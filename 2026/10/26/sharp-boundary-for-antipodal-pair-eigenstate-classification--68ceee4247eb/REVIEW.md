The exact annihilation condition is one nonzero linear equation in three real coefficients, so its kernel has dimension two. The proposed non-strict extension of the source's pair rule imposes two independent constraints.

Searches returned no matching indexed statement. **Value — PASS.** The cutoff is a hypothesis of a classification theorem for exact thermal eigenstates. The example identifies why equality cannot be included: overlapping supports create an additional independent annihilator direction. It is a sharpness witness, not a general boundary classification.
