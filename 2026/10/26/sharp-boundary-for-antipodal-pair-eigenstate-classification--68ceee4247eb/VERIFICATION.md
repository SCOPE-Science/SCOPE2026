# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The four displayed sites are distinct for every m>=1 and the two Bell pairs link the first to the third and the second to the fourth. Pushing the first site's X through its pair and commuting X operators on distinct sites gives P_0|Ψ>=ω_1^x P_1|Ψ>; pushing the second site's X through its pair gives P_2|Ψ>=ω_{1+m}^x P_1|Ψ>. Since P_1 is unitary, its image vector is nonzero and the three coefficients have one nonzero real constraint. The plane contains (0,1,-ω_{1+m}^x). Chiba–Yoneta use ordinary index diameter. With N=4m, translation by N/2 interchanges P_0 and P_2 and sends P_1 to the string on {1,1+3m}, of diameter 3m. The proposed inclusive extension would disallow this last coefficient and force b=0; the P_0,P_2 relation then leaves only the stated line. The counterexample is an actual zero-eigenvalue state for a Hamiltonian of maximal diameter m. It does not contradict the strict theorem.
