For first returns of the intermittent LSV map, the conditioned excess-return law is Pareto, but its sharp finite-time error undergoes a phase transition at the initial density's boundary exponent \(\beta=\alpha\): it decays as \(n^{-\beta/\alpha}\) below the threshold and as \((\log n)/n\) at and above it. Both correction profiles and their exact optimal uniform constants are explicit for every \(\alpha>0\) and every integrable boundary power \(\kappa>-1\).
