For a qubit gate compiled using at most \(m\) tilted-axis rotations surrounded by freely chosen first-axis rotations, the sharp attainable process fidelity is \(\cos^2((\beta-\min\{2m\theta,\pi\})_+/2)\), where \(\beta\) measures the target's Bloch polar displacement. For Haar-random targets, the exact fidelity distribution and mean imply a quartic vanishing of average infidelity near complete reachability, despite quadratic worst-case error.
