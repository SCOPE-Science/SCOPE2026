For the energy-normalized edge-gradient projector of every anisotropically weighted rook graph, the exact maximum-coordinate amplification is the larger of two explicit row-sum formulas. The unique most amplifying conductance ratio is \(c/d=b/a\); unequal rook dimensions exhibit two switches of the worst edge direction, and optimal amplification grows as \(\sqrt{b/2}\) when \(a=2\) and \(b\) tends to infinity.
