On an \(n\)-cycle, the three-point running median decreases each signal's total variation, but its exact Lipschitz amplification of **differences** in that seminorm is \(0\) for \(n=3\), \(2\) for \(n=4,5\), and \(3\) for every \(n\ge6\). The sharp factor three is attained by two datasets differing by a single cyclic arc indicator.
