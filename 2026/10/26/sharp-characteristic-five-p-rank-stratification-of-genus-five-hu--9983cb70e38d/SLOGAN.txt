For smooth genus-five Humbert curves in characteristic \(5\), the Jacobian \(5\)-rank is exactly \(3\), \(4\), or \(5\): no more than two of the five elliptic quotients can be supersingular. The sharp minimum \(3\) occurs at one projective class of five branch points, \(\{\infty,0,1,u,v\}\) with \(u,v\) the two roots of \(t^2-t+1=0\).
