For diffusion on the full edge subdivision of **any** connected, unweighted \(d\)-regular graph, classical RK4 preserves nonnegative states in a completed step exactly for \(0\le h\le2/(d+2)\), independently of all other graph structure. This sharp range is strictly larger than the forward-Euler range when \(d\ge3\) but strictly smaller than RK4's spectral-stability range.
