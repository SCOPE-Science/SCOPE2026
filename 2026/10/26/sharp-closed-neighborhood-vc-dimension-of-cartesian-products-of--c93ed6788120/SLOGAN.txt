For every Cartesian product of nontrivial cliques, radius-one closed-neighborhood VC dimension is at most three and is exactly three when there are at least three factors. A two-factor product has VC dimension three precisely when its smaller alphabet has at least three symbols and its larger at least four; otherwise it has dimension two except for the \(2\times2\) board, which has dimension one, while one-factor products have dimension zero.
