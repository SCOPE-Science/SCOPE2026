In an affine qubit semigroup with a non-GKSL generator, individual maps can first become completely positive at an exact finite threshold and only later become entanglement breaking. Near a pure stationary state, the intervening time window vanishes with a sharp fractional-power law, but a critical \(1/\log(1/s)\) tuning of the transverse decay exponent produces a nonzero logarithmic crossover profile.
