All binary length-\(N+2\) words with exactly two ones have a common scattered supersequence with precisely \(m\ge2\) ones and as few as \(N+\lfloor 2N/(m-1)\rfloor\) zeros, and no fewer. Allowing any composition, the exact shortest length is \(N+1+\lfloor\sqrt{8N+1}\rfloor\), attained by explicit balanced zero-gap constructions.
