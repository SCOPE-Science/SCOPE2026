For harmonic extension from the leaves of any unequal-arm metric star, the best achievable Lipschitz amplification over positive edge conductances is an explicit finite water-filling minimum. When one arm has length \(r\) and the other \(k\) arms have unit length, the optimizer switches sharply from a strictly positive conductance vector to a degenerate short-arm limit at \(r=(k-2)/k\), whenever \(k\ge3\).
