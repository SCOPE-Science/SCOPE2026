For scalar Gaussian-target exponential integration with adaptive cap \(h=\min\{H,\beta/|s-1|\}\), global convergence of the coupled mean and covariance holds exactly when \(0<H<2\), for every \(\beta>0\), while uniform one-step mean nonexpansivity holds exactly when \(H+\beta\le2\). At the critical cap \(H=2\), covariance still converges but certain nonzero mean errors oscillate indefinitely, even after the covariance reaches its target exactly.
