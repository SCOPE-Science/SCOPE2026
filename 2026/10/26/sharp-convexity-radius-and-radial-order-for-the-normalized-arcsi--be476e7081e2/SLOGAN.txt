The normalized-arcsine Ma--Minda starlike class has sharp convexity radius \(R=0.538585996535\ldots\), characterized by \(\sqrt{1-R^2}(1-(2/\pi)\arcsin R)^2=2R/\pi\). Its exact radial order of convexity is \(1-(2/\pi)\arcsin r-(2r/\pi)/(\sqrt{1-r^2}[1-(2/\pi)\arcsin r])\) for \(0\le r\le11/20\), with one explicit extremizer attaining all these bounds.
