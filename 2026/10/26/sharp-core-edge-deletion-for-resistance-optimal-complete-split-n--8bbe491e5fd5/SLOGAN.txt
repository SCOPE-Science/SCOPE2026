Under a fixed total conductance budget, the unique Kirchhoff-index-minimizing allocation on the complete split graph \(K_r\vee\overline{K_s}\) deletes **every edge inside the clique** exactly when \(s\ge r+1\), while all core-to-periphery edges stay positive. Closed-form optimal weights and minimum resistance hold for all integers \(r\ge2\), \(s\ge1\), even against asymmetric allocations.
