For two-node positive exponential deferred correction on logistic growth, repeated corrections converge to their positive collocation endpoint exactly when \(c=(ha/2)e^{h(1-a/2)}\le e\); beyond that sharp threshold they approach a unique attracting two-cycle. The cycling can begin while the collocation endpoint itself is still below the carrying capacity, and the uniform all-initial-data convergence frontier is \(h=2\).
