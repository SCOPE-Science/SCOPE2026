In an inertial run-and-tumble harmonic trap, stationary position correlations are positive at every lag unless the mechanical response is underdamped and the flip rate exceeds \(\gamma/(4m)\); beyond that sharp boundary they change sign infinitely often. On approaching the boundary from above, the first negative-lag crossing is delayed by a precise inverse-distance law, even though the integrated covariance remains \(a_0^2/(2\rho\mu^2)\).
