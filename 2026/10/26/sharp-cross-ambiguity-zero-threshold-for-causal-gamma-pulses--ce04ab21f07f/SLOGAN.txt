The causal gamma pulses \(t^m e^{-t}\mathbf1_{t>0}\) individually have zero-free self-ambiguity, but their cross-ambiguity with \(e^{-t}\mathbf1_{t>0}\) is zero-free exactly through order \(m=4\), acquiring transverse conjugate-frequency zeros for every \(m\ge5\). The sharp threshold is governed by right-half-plane roots of the truncated exponential \(\sum_{k=0}^m z^k/k!\).
