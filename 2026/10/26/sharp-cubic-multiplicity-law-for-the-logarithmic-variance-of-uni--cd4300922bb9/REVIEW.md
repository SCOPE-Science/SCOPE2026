# Mathematical assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The logarithmic Fourier expansion has finite \(L^2\) norm for each unimodular factor, so Parseval rigorously gives the squared-power-sum energy. The periodic Bernoulli identity turns that energy into an exact quadratic in angular coordinates on any fixed cyclic-order chamber. The Hessian and the critical value at regular \(n\)-gons fix the entire quadratic. Orthogonal projection to contiguous equal-angle blocks gives an exact, nonnegative weighted-angular remainder and the cubic multiplicity minimum. Strict positivity of the weighted target gaps proves admissibility and uniqueness up to rotation. Integer cube convexity proves the additional fixed-distinct-root classification. The attached exact-rational checker confirms 1023 finite compositions but is not the proof.

## Originality — PASS, with explicit historical-priority limits
Teng Zhang (arXiv:2610.02009v1), especially Corollary 7.4, supplies the unrestricted \(\pi^2/12\) bound and the power-sum representation. Nicolas Nagel (2025), Proposition 2.1, supplies the same classical one-dimensional Bernoulli kernel in weighted periodic discrepancy. These cover key premises, not the fixed-integer-multiplicity optimum, full angular equality classification, or global weighted defect. A related local simple-root Fourier Hessian does not imply the repeated-root sharp profile. Focused mathematical-record and published-literature searches found no direct covering claim. However, classical one-dimensional prescribed-weight quadrature might contain an implicit equivalent and has not been exhaustively excluded.

## Value — PASS
Repeated zeros are the natural degeneration of unimodular-zero configurations. Their sharp variance floor increases according to the *sum of cubes* of multiplicities, with an exactly computable angle configuration and a global quantitative stability identity. This provides a complete classification for any partition, and for fixed degree and number of distinct roots, not merely another numerical bound or local Taylor coefficient.

## Scientific limits
The result applies only to unit-circle zeros and centered logarithmic second moments, not to positive-exponent norm inequalities or zeros off the circle. The nonnegative defect is referenced to the appropriate fixed cyclic order. The Bernoulli kernel, Fourier representation, unconditioned lower bound, and local simple-root Hessian are not new priority claims.
