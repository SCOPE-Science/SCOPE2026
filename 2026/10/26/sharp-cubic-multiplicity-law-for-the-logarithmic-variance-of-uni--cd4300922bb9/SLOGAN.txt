For degree-\(n\) polynomials with unimodular zeros of prescribed positive multiplicities \(m_1,\ldots,m_r\), the centered logarithmic boundary variance has sharp minimum \(\pi^2(\sum_j m_j^3)/(12n)\). Every minimizing cluster configuration has consecutive gaps \(\pi(m_j+m_{j+1})/n\), and its full excess is exactly a weighted quadratic variance of angular displacements; prescribing only the number of distinct roots forces the multiplicities to be as balanced as possible.
