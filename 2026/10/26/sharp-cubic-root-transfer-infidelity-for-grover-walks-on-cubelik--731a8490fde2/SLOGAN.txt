For a Grover-coined quantum walk on any cubelike graph, the one-shot failure probability at the parity-matched XOR-target time is at most \(16\Delta^{-1/3}\), improving the prior universal \(O(\Delta^{-1/5})\) estimate. This decay exponent is optimal: an explicit connected graph family with a nonzero target has \(\Delta^{1/3}(1-p_T)\to2\mathbb E[1-\cos(\pi Z^3/12)]\approx0.3340087115\) for a standard Gaussian \(Z\).
