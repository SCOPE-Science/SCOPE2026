For the second-order three-stage polynomial \(R_\beta(z)=1+z+z^2/2+\beta z^3\), Euclidean small-step contraction on every rank-one damped planar oscillator is sharp at \(\beta=1/4\); equality splits by the damping-to-coupling ratio. At the boundary, equal damping and coupling produce an exact identity step, while a rational unequal-rate example contracts at an intermediate step despite small-step amplification.
