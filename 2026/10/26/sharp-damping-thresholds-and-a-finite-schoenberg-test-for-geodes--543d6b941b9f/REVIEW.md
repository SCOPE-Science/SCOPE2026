# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The Schoenberg coefficient integral uses the exactly normalized three-sphere Gegenbauer basis and the \(\sin^2\theta\) measure. Elementary Laplace integration yields a rational coefficient formula, and multiplication by a nonzero squared denominator reduces each sign to the real quadratic \(P_\varepsilon((n+1)^2)\). A quadratic reaches its sampled minimum on each of the two parity square sequences at the first point or the two points adjacent to its vertex; the special linear and negative-leading cases are handled separately. The real polynomial cannot disappear on an entire parity subsequence when \(a>0\), so all accepted kernels are strictly positive definite. The exact integer-frequency formula is increasing for \(X\ge1\) and has minimum \(r(a^2+4-3m^2)\), proving threshold inclusion and the precise initial negative-coefficient block. Symbolic algebra and supplementary direct-quadrature/finite checks passed; they are not substitutes for the proof.

## Originality — PASS under bounded source review
Gneiting's Schoenberg criteria and dimension-three coefficient identity are necessary background but do not give the finite test or the integer phase boundary. Beatson–zu Castell–Xu supplies a sufficient Pólya positivity criterion, not this oscillatory necessity classification. Huang–Zhang–Robeson treats exponential and sine-over-angle hole-effect families on the two-sphere; Jeong–Jun covers the degree-one cosine product but not higher-frequency classification on the three-sphere. Accessible related mathematical claims treat Euclidean damped-cosine, circle geodesic modulation, and two-sphere linear cosine factors. Those are distinct domains and distance operators and do not imply the stated result. Unindexed equivalent work remains a meaningful residual originality risk.
