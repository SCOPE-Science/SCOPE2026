For every connected simple graph of maximum degree \(\Delta\), the canonical cut-projection norm exceeds the complementary cycle-projection norm by at least \((3-\Delta)/(\Delta+1)\), sharply for complete graphs. All subcubic graphs therefore have cut-norm dominance, while \(K_5\) is a sharp degree-four reversal; the proof also characterizes equality in the local degree-based resistance bound by adjacent true twins.
