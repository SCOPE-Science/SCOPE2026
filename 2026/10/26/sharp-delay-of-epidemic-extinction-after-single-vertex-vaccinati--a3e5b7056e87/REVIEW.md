# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The live-edge coupling gives a precise pathwise interpretation. The proof of the maximal delay uses only deletion monotonicity of reachability and the shortest-path length bound, treats the seed and vaccinated vertex separately, and supplies attaining examples for every admissible size. For the fixed five-person graph, a tail-event derivation and an independent complete 64-configuration enumeration produce the same exact degree-six duration-difference polynomial. The critical probability is unique by strict concavity of the quintic, with rigorous rational bracketing. Finite numerical tests are supplementary and do not replace the all-size proof.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** The previously published live-edge path lemma and final-infection monotonicity are explicitly credited to Floyd, Kay and Shapiro. Pulliam et al. already establish a different kind of epidemic-duration enhancement with replenishing immunity, Gutfraind studies final-size monotonicity, and Holme studies extinction time and its relationship to network eccentricity without vaccination perturbations. Comparison of the objects and logical consequences did not identify the exact sharp \(n-4\) finite-graph delay nor the five-person homogeneous-edge probability threshold. These claims are specialized consequences of the live-edge framework plus an extremal construction and exact probabilistic enumeration, not a claim of inventing duration enhancement. Generic graph eccentricity results or unindexed small-network studies may imply part of the statement; historical priority is not independently established.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Outbreak duration is an operational endpoint distinct from final size. The theorem sharply quantifies how much a perfectly protective vertex intervention can lengthen duration while decreasing the infected set, and proves an exact finite-transmission threshold on the smallest eligible population. This exposes a concrete limitation of inferring time-to-extinction improvement from case-count monotonicity. The precise polynomial boundary is motivated by this minimal witness and need not be extrapolated to real interventions.

(2008, online 2007), DOI 10.1007/s11538-007-9275-0, especially Lemma 3.5; Gutfraind (2010 preprint), arXiv:1005.3470; Holme (2013), DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0084429.
