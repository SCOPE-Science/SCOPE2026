Removing one vaccinated vertex in a fixed-contact, one-generation SIR network can extend the epidemic's last-infection generation by at most \(\max\{0,n-4\}\), a sharp bound first attainable with five people even though no additional person is infected. On a natural six-contact five-person graph with common transmission probability \(p\), the expected epidemic duration increases after vaccination precisely when \(p\) exceeds the unique root \(p_c\approx0.8365901884\) of \(p^5-p^4-3p^3+p+1=0\).
