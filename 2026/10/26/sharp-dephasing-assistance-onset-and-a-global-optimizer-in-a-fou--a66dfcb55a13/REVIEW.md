# Self-review

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof uses the exact, closed stationary two-point equations for the published four-site Lindblad model, not the tridiagonal approximation. A full symbolic Cramer calculation yields the positive-denominator current and its zero-dephasing derivative. The positive coefficient certificate establishes the sole onset root for every boundary coupling. At equal rates, exact derivative coefficient signs and rationally isolated roots prove a unique positive optimizer, or strict monotonic decrease. Five direct full-state Lindblad solutions reproduce the current independently of the two-point construction. The strong-tilt optimizer limit follows by a scaled-derivative bracket and uniqueness. No finite numerical sample is used as an infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS (with residual risk).** Jacob et al., arXiv:2407.21715, supply the model, full equations, exact chains of length two and three, and the approximation for longer chains. They do not display the new four-site all-contact threshold polynomial or its singular endpoint laws, nor an equal-contact global optimum classification. Žnidarič and Horvat, arXiv:1207.5952, supply the general stationary correlation formalism, but focus on disordered large-chain conductivity and periodic-disorder resonances; they do not solve the four-site linear-gradient threshold. Searches of research records and primary literature did not find a matching explicit theorem. The strongest caveat is that the exact finite answer is algebraically obtainable from previously known stationary equations and might already have been calculated elsewhere.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The four-site chain is the first one beyond the sizes for which the motivating paper presents closed exact transport formulas. The theorem supplies an all-coupling sharp onset, countervailing weak-contact and Zeno-contact scaling laws, and a complete global optimization classification at equal coupling. Its nontrivial coefficient-sign argument quantifies when the long-range coherences omitted by the standard tridiagonal approximation matter. These are motivated observables of the published localization-versus-noise problem, not an arbitrary reparameterization or a numerical fit.

**Scientific limitations.** The global one-maximizer statement is confined to \(G=1\); other positive contacts have only the proved infinitesimal-response criterion. The result concerns a fixed four-site finite chain, not thermodynamic criticality. It neither establishes a unique coupling maximizing onset nor extends to interactions, different reservoirs, or longer chains.
