In the four-site tilted XX chain, there is a unique critical tilt for the onset of an initially dephasing-enhanced current at every positive boundary coupling; the tilt vanishes asymptotically for both weak and very strong contact. At equal hopping and boundary coupling, the entire current is either strictly decreasing with dephasing or has exactly one interior maximum, separated by the sharp threshold \(U_c=0.148539003114375\ldots\).
