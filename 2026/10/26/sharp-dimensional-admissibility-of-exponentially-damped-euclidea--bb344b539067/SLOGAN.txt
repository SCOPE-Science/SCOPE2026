For the Euclidean wave kernel multiplied by exponential damping, \(e^{-ar}\sin(\omega r)/(\omega r)\), positive definiteness in odd dimension \(d\ge3\) holds exactly when \((d-1)\arctan(\omega/a)\le\pi\), including equality. The complex-power Fourier transform identifies all negative spectral bands outside this sharp regime and proves strict positive definiteness inside it.
