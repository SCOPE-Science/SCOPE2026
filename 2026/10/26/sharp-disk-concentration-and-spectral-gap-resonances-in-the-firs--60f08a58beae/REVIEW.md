The exact two-eigenvalue differences resolve the two small-radius regimes; strict upper bounds rule out all remote modes. ## Mathematical value — PASS
For the simplest non-Gaussian Hermite analysis window, this solves the natural sharp disk-localization problem completely: explicit concentration coefficient, equality modes and spectral-gap robustness for every radius. The rank-three critical resonance and the runner-up triple resonance are structural information unavailable from mere diagonalization. The gap can directly quantify distance to optimal signals. The claim is not a theorem about arbitrary shapes or Hermite levels.
