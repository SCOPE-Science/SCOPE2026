For a four-state continuous-time Markov cycle with opposite transition rates \(a,b\), fourth-order Gauss–Legendre time stepping preserves every nonnegative state at every step exactly when \(1/\sqrt3\le a/b\le\sqrt3\). Beyond that sharp drift boundary, nonnegativity holds only through one explicitly defined quartic-root timestep, after which one directional transition becomes negative.
