Taking differences gives every projection-matrix entry; the counting by five edge types gives exact absolute row sums, and the diagonal complement identity yields the circulation norm. ## Value: PASS
The edge-coordinate \(\ell^\infty\) projection norm is the exact worst-case amplification of bounded edge-by-edge errors in least-squares graph-gradient decomposition. The theorem covers an entire natural graph family, identifies all sharp row sums and the extremal edge classes, and shows a structural discrepancy between gradient and circulation projections: in \(K_{N-2,1,1}\), the circulation worst edge joins the two singleton parts. It classifies the complete finite fixed-part-count gradient maximizers and resolves strict versus limiting amplification constants. This is more than a norm-one Hilbert-space observation or a routine special-case estimate.

## Scope and limitations
The results are restricted to real edge spaces of unit-weight complete multipartite graphs. Rational numerical checks independently corroborate only finite sizes. Novelty remains provisional in the presence of unindexed equivalent formulations, and no independent bibliographic or proof audit was performed.
