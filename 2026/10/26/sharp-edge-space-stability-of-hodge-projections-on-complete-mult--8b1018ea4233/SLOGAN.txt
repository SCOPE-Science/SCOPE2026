For every unweighted complete multipartite graph, the sharp coordinatewise amplification of the canonical edge-gradient Hodge projector depends only on its two largest part sizes, while the complementary circulation projector instead selects the two largest scores of a nonlinear part-size function. At fixed numbers of vertices and parts, the gradient projection is maximally unstable for two balanced large parts and all remaining parts singleton; the universal norm bounds \(3\) and \(4\) for the two complementary projectors are strict but asymptotically sharp.
