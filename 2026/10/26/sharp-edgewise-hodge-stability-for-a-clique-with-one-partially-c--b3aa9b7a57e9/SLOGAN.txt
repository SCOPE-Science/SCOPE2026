Deleting a star from a clique creates three sharply different regimes for the maximum-coordinate norm of its graph-gradient projector, with the maximizing edge class changing at attachment degrees \(h=2\) and \(h=3\). Its first norm larger than \(2\) occurs on ten vertices, while optimal near-complete examples have attachment degree asymptotic to \(\sqrt{2n}\) and norm approaching \(3\) without attaining it.
