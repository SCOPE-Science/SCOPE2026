The sharp coordinatewise amplification of least-squares gradient reconstruction on an unweighted finite two-rail ladder is attained at a central rung: it first exceeds \(2\) at five rungs and converges strictly from below to \(1+2/\sqrt3\). The finite-size deficit has different explicit parity-dependent leading constants, both decaying geometrically at rate \((2-\sqrt3)^{n/2}\).
