# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For positive entries and exactly three firms and workers, complete EF1 forces three singleton bundles, and conversely singleton bundles are EF1. The financing inequalities suffice: assign each deviating worker to a best firm in the coalition and sum that firm's deviation surplus covered by its profit. The high-edge maximum matching gives one, two or three high assignments; size one forces one common high firm, and size two forces the unfilled pair to be low. Substituting the specified normalized wages into the surplus bounds gives respectively (1+2δ)/3, at least (1+δ)/2, and one. The common-high instance has EF1 welfare 1+2δ versus unrestricted welfare three, and the grand coalition bounds the factor. At δ=1 the actual high graph is complete, the frontier is one, and subcases for noncomplete labeled graphs merely duplicate the same valuation matrix in the finite tests. An independent exact-rational reproduction checked EF1, financing and all 56 coalition inequalities for each of 1,715 certificates; matched-size counts 3, 93, 247. That replay does not replace the continuum argument.

The supported distinct result is the sharp lower guarantee and simple constructive certificates under the simultaneous restrictions, in the open interval 1/2<δ<1 where Qin's unrestricted bounds differ. The frozen prose's comparison 'δ>1/2' should be read as referring to the gap on this open interval, not at δ=1. No distinct claim about closing the unrestricted gap is accepted.
