# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The row-selector identity is exact for nonnegative inputs; \(BB^\mathsf T\) has the stated Perron root. For \(m\ge4\), explicit positive comparison vectors bound every selected row Gram matrix. For \(m=2,3\), a standalone exact-integer certificate exhausts the radius choices up to triangle permutations. The positive singular vector activates the claimed rows and attains equality. The computation is finite and the rest is analytical; no finite experiment is offered as a proof of infinite parameters.

## Originality
PASS within inspected literature. The 2014 finite-graph theorem gives sharp estimates in the different exponent range \(0<p\le1\), while the 2020 sharp strong \(\ell^2\) results treat complete and star graphs. The present friendship family is neither complete (except the excluded novelty benchmark \(m=1\)) nor a star. Searches by the friendship/windmill aliases, norm formula, and cone-over-a-matching reduction found no covering statement. Full specialist priority remains provisional; in particular unindexed results cannot be ruled out.

## Value
PASS. Sharp strong-type constants at \(p=2\) are nontrivial even for basic finite-graph maximal operators. The friendship graphs are a natural diameter-two family with both local triangle and global averaging choices, separating them from the star case. The result gives an attained formula for every number of triangles and an exact leading growth law, supported by a structural Perron bound and small-order exact certificate.
