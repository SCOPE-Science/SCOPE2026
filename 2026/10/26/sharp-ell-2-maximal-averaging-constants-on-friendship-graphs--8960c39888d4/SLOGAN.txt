The centered Hardy--Littlewood maximal operator on the friendship graph \(K_1\vee(mK_2)\), for every \(m\ge2\), has the exact attained sharp \(\ell^2\) norm \(\sqrt{(2m+13+\sqrt{(2m+13)^2-144})/18}\). Its norm grows as \(\sqrt{2m}/3\), and the extremizer is positive and constant on the outer vertices.
