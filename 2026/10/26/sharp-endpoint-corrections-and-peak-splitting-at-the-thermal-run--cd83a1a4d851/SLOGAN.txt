For the known Gaussian-smoothed stationary law of a harmonically trapped run-and-tumble particle, the critical thermal variance has a new explicit linear persistence correction and a \(\tfrac32\log\log(1/\delta)\) correction near uniformity. The two symmetric stationary peaks meet at \(m^2=3(t_c-t)+O((t_c-t)^2)\); the underlying phase boundary and its leading endpoint limits were already known.
