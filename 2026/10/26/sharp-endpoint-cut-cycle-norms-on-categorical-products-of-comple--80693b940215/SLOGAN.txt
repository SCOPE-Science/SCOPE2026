For the categorical product \(K_a\times K_b\) with \(a,b\ge3\), the canonical edge cut and cycle projections have exact endpoint norms given by a rational symmetric baseline plus a strictly positive imbalance correction proportional to \(|a-b|\). Across the entire two-parameter family their sharp unattained supremum norms are \(8/3\) and \(11/3\), approached by fixing one factor at three vertices and letting the other grow.
