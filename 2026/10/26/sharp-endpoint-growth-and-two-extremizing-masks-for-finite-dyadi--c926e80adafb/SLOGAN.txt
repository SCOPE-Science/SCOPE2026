Among all projections retaining arbitrary whole levels of a depth-\(n\) dyadic Haar expansion, the largest sharp \(L^1\) and \(L^\infty\) norm is exactly \(n/3+7/9+(-1)^n/(9\,2^{n-1})\). For \(n\ge3\), exactly two nearly alternating level masks attain it, revealing linear growth of worst-case endpoint amplification despite the uniformly bounded all-level projection.
