# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The endpoint norms are ordinary induced coordinate norms of the symmetric projector, so both equal the largest absolute row sum. The zero, contrast and within-part Laplacian eigenvalues are respectively 0, 2mb and m(a+b), yielding the stated cross-edge potential correction k=(a−b)/(2m²b(a+b)). An intra-part row has contribution (2m−2)a/d from own-part edges and 2m√(ab)/d from incident cross edges, d=m(a+b). In a cross row, same or singly incident cross-edge coefficients have positive sign in the common A-to-B orientation, and only disjoint cross edges change sign at a=b. The signed cross-to-cross row sum is one, and when a<b the absolute contribution adds 4b|k|(m−1)². These orbit counts give the two formulas and the factored differences select the claimed row on each branch. The first branch's distinct top eigenvector has y=√(1+4h²)−2h∈(0,1), with value 1+h√(1+4h²). The other branch is bounded by h+√(1+h²), which is strictly smaller: for s=√(1+4h²), (1−h+hs)²−(1+h²)=h²(4h²−1)+2h(1−h)(s−1)>0 on 1/2≤h<1. This proves uniqueness as a ratio, not as a pair (a,b) up to scale. The boundary limits, strict comparison to the equal-conductance value, and m→∞ limits follow. An independent grounded-Laplacian double-precision check confirmed 392 individual row sums for m=2,3,4 at eight positive ratios y, with tolerance 10⁻⁹; the proof is exact and does not rely on that check.
