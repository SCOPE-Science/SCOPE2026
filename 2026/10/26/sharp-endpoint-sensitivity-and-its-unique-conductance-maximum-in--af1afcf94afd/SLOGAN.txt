A two-community network can amplify edgewise errors most strongly at a *strictly positive, unequal* within-to-between conductance ratio, rather than at an unweighted or disconnected extreme. For two \(m\)-vertex communities the exact worst sharp endpoint amplification is \(1+\frac{m-1}{m}\sqrt{1+4((m-1)/m)^2}\), converging to \(1+\sqrt5\).
