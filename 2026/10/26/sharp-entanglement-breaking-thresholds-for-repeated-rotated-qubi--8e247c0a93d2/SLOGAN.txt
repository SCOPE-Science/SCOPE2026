A qubit dephasing channel, repeated with the same interleaved unitary rotation, has an exactly solvable optimum for destroying entanglement: the minimum Bloch singular-value sum after \(n\geq3\) steps is \(3q^{2n/3}\), attained by a rotation whose \(n\)-th channel power is exactly depolarizing. This yields the sharp breaking condition \(q\leq3^{-3/(2n)}\), whereas fixed-axis dephasing with \(q>0\) never becomes entanglement breaking at finite depth.
