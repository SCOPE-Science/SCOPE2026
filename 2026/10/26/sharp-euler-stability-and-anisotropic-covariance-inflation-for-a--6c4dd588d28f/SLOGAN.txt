For the critically accelerated two-mode reversible Stratonovich Langevin sampler, explicit Euler is mean-square stable exactly when \(0<h<h_*(\lambda)\), where \(h_*(\lambda)=[3+\lambda-\sqrt{(1+\lambda)^2-4(1+\lambda)+8}]/[2(1+\lambda)]\). Throughout that range its unique finite-second-moment invariant law overdisperses both Gaussian target coordinates, with exact normalized anisotropy \(\lambda\mathbb E[Y^2]-\mathbb E[X^2]=h(1-\lambda)^2/[2F_\lambda(h)]\); beyond the sharp threshold, mean convergence may persist although second moments diverge.
