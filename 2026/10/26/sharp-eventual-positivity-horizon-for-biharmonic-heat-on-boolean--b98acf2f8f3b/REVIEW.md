# Scientific review

## Correctness
The Walsh-character eigenvalues are \(2k\), hence the biharmonic multipliers are \(z^{k^2}\) with \(z=e^{-4t}\). The shell sum follows from the elementary Krawtchouk generating function. A universal tail bound of \(0.81/d^2\), a strict derivative bound, and a uniform nonantipodal lower bound jointly prove that the late antipodal root alone determines the persistent horizon. The expansion follows by isolating the quadratic binomial term at the root. The proof treats every integer \(d\ge3\) and does not extrapolate from computation.

## Originality
Published analyses of eventually positive semigroups give qualitative spectral criteria, not the sharp time for this exponentially large family. The closest known hypercube work addresses the resolvent rather than the exponential, and comparisons to explicit square-Laplacian kernels for rook and triangular graphs do not imply the Hamming-shell result. A direct unindexed duplicate remains possible. Searches included the exponential, Krawtchouk, antipodal and graph bi-Laplacian formulations.

## Value
The dimension-dependent restoration time identifies a natural, precise maximum-principle boundary in a canonical family of finite Banach lattices: it grows logarithmically in hypercube dimension with an explicit second-order correction. The antipodal extremality proof reduces an exponentially large sign-checking problem to one scalar equation with uniform bounds; the result is not a routine fixed-dimension calculation.

## Limitations
No conclusion about earlier disconnected positivity intervals, general Cartesian products, weighted cubes, or independent verification. The numerical checker is corroborative only.
