For binary quasinecklaces containing exactly \(z\) zeros and \(r\) ones, an explicit bounded-composition formula counts the candidates exactly. Their number is at most \(z\) times the number of same-content necklaces, and this factor is asymptotically sharp as \(r\to\infty\) for every fixed positive \(z\); thus exact-content filtering of this quasinecklace superset has no content-independent candidate/output bound.
