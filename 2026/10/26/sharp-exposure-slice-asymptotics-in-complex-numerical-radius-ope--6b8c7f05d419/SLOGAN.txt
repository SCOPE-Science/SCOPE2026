The complex numerical-radius dual ball on \(\ell_p^d\) has an exact sharp exposure-slice radius of order \(\varepsilon^{1/\max\{p,q\}}\) at a coordinate state, with a dimension-independent coefficient. The coefficient equals \(p/(p-1)^{(p-1)/p}\) away from \(p=2\), but jumps to \(\sqrt6\) at \(p=2\), where phase and two off-diagonal perturbations couple.
