Among all nonzero sums of at most two real-frequency complex exponentials, the exact squared point-evaluation amplification from averaged \(L^2([-1,1])\) data at every \(|x|\ge1\) is \(1+3x^2\). No distinct frequency pair attains it; the sharp constant is approached by a coalescing-frequency limit exhibited explicitly by \(\cos(ht)+3x\sin(ht)/h\).
