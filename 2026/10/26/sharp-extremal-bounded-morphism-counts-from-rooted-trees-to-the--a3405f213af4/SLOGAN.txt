For the four-world Boolean-diamond Kripke frame, the number of bounded morphisms from a finite rooted ancestor-order tree obeys the surprising recursion \(N(T)=2+\prod_iN(T_i)\) at every internal vertex. Among all rooted trees on \(n\) vertices, the unique maximum is attained by a root with two-vertex arms and, for even \(n\ge4\), one three-vertex arm, giving the exact maximum \(2+3^{(n-1)/2}\) for odd \(n\ge3\), \(2+5\cdot3^{(n-4)/2}\) for even \(n\ge4\), and \(3\) at \(n=2\); the unique minimum for \(n\ge2\) is the rooted star, with three morphisms.
