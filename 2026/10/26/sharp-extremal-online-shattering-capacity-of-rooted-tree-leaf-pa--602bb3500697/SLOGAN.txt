Root-to-leaf path concepts on rooted trees have VC dimension at most one, yet attain adaptive shattering depth \(d\) precisely from a sharp minimum budget of \(3\cdot2^{d-1}\) vertices in the best-shaped tree. More strongly, with \(I\) nonleaf vertices and \(L\) leaves, the maximum Littlestone dimension is exactly \(\min\{\lfloor\log_2 L\rfloor,1+\lfloor\log_2 I\rfloor\}\).
