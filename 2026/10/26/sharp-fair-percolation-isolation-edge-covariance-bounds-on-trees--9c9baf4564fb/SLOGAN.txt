In independent fair bond percolation on any fixed \(n\)-vertex tree, the covariance between isolated vertices and retained edges is minimized by the path, but the sharp maximizers for \(n\ge5\) have almost exclusively degree-five hubs with a complete four-residue correction. Their covariance equals \(-n/4+(11(n-2)-\delta_{(n-2)\bmod4})/256\), with \((\delta_0,\delta_1,\delta_2,\delta_3)=(0,3,2,1)\).
