For an odd electorate of \(2r+1\) voters with strict single-peaked preferences over \(m\) ordered alternatives, the unique Borda winner can be at most—and can be exactly—\(\lfloor[r(m-1)-1]/(r+1)\rfloor\) axis steps from the Condorcet median peak. The maximum possible displacement of \(m-2\) first becomes attainable with \(2m-1\) voters.
