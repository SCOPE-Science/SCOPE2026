For the \(L^2\)-orthogonal projector onto equally spaced periodic linear splines, every finite-circle sharp \(L^\infty\) norm is explicitly computable and strictly below \(2\). Its finite-size deficit is of exact geometric order \((2-\sqrt3)^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}\), with an optimal \(n\)-th-root rate of \(\sqrt{2-\sqrt3}\).
