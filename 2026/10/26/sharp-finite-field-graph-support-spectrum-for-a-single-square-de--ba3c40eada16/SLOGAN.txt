For a nondegenerate finite-field evolution algebra with one dependent pair of basis squares and all remaining square representatives independent, every block-marked natural-basis graph support is determined by one orthogonal pair of projective lines. If \(t\) distinct pairs are hit by nonisotropic projections of the square representatives, the exact number of graph-support patterns is \(t+\mathbf1_{t<N}\), and the sharp maximum at dimension \(n\) is \(\min\{n,N\}\), where \(N\) is the finite-field count of orthogonal pairs.
