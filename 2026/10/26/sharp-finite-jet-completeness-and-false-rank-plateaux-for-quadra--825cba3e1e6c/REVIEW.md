# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** With \(M=2n-2\), the adjugate numerator has degree at most \(M\). A dual functional annihilating the first \(M+1\) solution jets forces the corresponding numerator polynomial to vanish identically, since the prefactor and denominator are nonzero at the center. Conversely all jets belong to the solution-value span. In the positive definite diagonal example the coefficient vector at each pair of orders \(2j,2j+1\) is a nonzero multiple of \(K^jb\); Vandermonde independence gives exactly the stated rank staircase, for every dimension and derivative order. Exact arithmetic checks cover finite sample dimensions but are not substituted for proof.

**Originality — PASS with explicit residual risk.** The motivating October 2026 manuscript studies derivative recurrences and approximation (Section 4, Appendix A), without stating a universal sharp finite-jet cutoff or a positive definite rank-stagnation staircase in the inspected material. The 2001 quadratic-eigenvalue survey treats companion linearization, a concept that can inform the proof but does not, on its own, supply the exact rank profile. Published-claim searches for equivalent finite-jet and rational-reachability formulations did not locate an exact match. This is best-knowledge originality, not a proof of uniqueness: broad rational realization theory remains a residual risk.

**Value — PASS.** An algorithm constructing a basis from successive parameter derivatives needs to distinguish a temporary failure to increase dimension from completeness. The result gives a problem-size dependent, worst-case exact stopping horizon, and shows false plateaux in a simple symmetric positive definite family, not merely in a contrived singular or nonnormal model. The result makes no claim about convergence rates, optimal compression, or the numerical viability of computing high-order derivatives.

The work is a self-contained structural algebraic fact motivated by a recent numerical method, rather than a general-purpose performance theorem. The narrower scope and non-exhaustive originality evidence are material limits.
