For every integer \(i\ge1\), the first excluded cycle size has exact root-gap mean \((i+4)a_i\) at \(K=i+3\), and the first excluded variance size has a strictly positive Brownian-bridge trace correction \((2i+6)\delta_i\) at \(K=2i+6\). Consequently both previously established onset thresholds for exactly linear moments are sharp for all gap lengths, not only the finitely tested cases.
