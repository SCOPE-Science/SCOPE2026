For random sequential covering of a periodic chain by fixed \(\ell\)-site segments, the leading Gumbel completion-time law acquires a sharp positive correction of order \(n^{-1/\ell}\), with explicitly evaluated coefficient \(e^{-(1+1/\ell)x}\) on the normalized time scale. An exact finite-ring transfer trace and a proof of the correction expose how overlapping late vacancies accelerate complete coverage relative to the leading independent-vacancy approximation.
