For \(\exists^k\forall^\ell\) first-order sentences over nonempty finite equivalence structures, with \(\ell\ge2\), the sharp largest endpoint of a nonempty finite relative spectrum is \(k\ell+(\ell-1)^2\). Above that bound every cardinality at least \(k+\ell\) occurs, by a class-size or class-count pumping argument, and an explicit universal prohibition attains equality.
