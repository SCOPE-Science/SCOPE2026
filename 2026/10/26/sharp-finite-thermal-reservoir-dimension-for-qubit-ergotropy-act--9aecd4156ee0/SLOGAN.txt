For a passive diagonal qubit coupled to a finite Gibbs ladder with \(d\) equally spaced levels, the maximal ergotropy is obtained by exact energy-block swaps, and it becomes positive for some input precisely when \(\sum_{k=1}^{d-1}e^{-k\beta\Delta}>1\). Thus the exact minimum ladder dimension is \(1+\lfloor\log(2q-1)/\log q\rfloor\) for \(1/2<q=e^{-\beta\Delta}<1\), with a sharp finite-temperature obstruction and an explicit exponential cutoff penalty relative to the infinite-bath optimum.
