For binary exact-\(t\)-substitution spheres at \(n\ge4t\ge8\), three distinct centers have at most \(\binom{n-2}{t-1}\) common outputs, with equality exactly for three pairwise distance-two centers; the exact runner-up is \(4\binom{n-4}{t-2}\), attained exactly at distance pattern \(2,2,4\). Consequently \(\binom{n-2}{t-1}+1\) distinct observations are necessary and sufficient for guaranteed reconstruction to a list of at most two source words.
