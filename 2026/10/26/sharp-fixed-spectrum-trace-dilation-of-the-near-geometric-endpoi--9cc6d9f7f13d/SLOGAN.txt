For the near-geometric endpoint \(A\star_1B\), the trace excess over \(B\) is exactly a squared weighted commutator energy. When \(A\) has two spectral levels, its complete sharp fixed-spectrum trace range is given by pairing the largest and smallest square-root eigenvalues of \(B\) across the two eigenspaces of \(A\) at balanced angles; this gives an attained spectral-spread formula in every dimension.
