A lossy three-site chiral quantum ring has exact mean capture time \(T=3/(2g)+g/(2\sin^2\phi)+1/(2\sin\phi)\), revealing a sharp optimal-flux transition at \(g=1/2\). Jointly tuning magnetic flux and absorber strength yields the unique global design \((g,\phi)=(\sqrt3,3\pi/2)\) and sharp capture-time floor \(\sqrt3-1/2\) in hopping-time units.
