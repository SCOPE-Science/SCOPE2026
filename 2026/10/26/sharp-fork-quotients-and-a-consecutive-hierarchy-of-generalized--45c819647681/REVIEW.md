# Self-review

## Correctness — PASS
The maximal-colour criterion is proved in both directions using the back-lifting condition and the order relation on a fork. Every maximal point of a topless power has exactly one active arm coordinate. When a root has two active coordinates, every pair of maximal extensions from different coordinates has a common lower extension with precisely those two maxima; for three or more target arms this forces equality of their colours. Connectedness of the cross-coordinate comparisons makes the full maximal set monochromatic, contradicting the need to lift all target arms. With a single active coordinate there are at most the original number of arms. Sharpness follows from the one-fold identity map (at least two arms) or an explicit two-coordinate map (one arm). Surjective factor maps of the generators, top-fiber control and the finite Jankov--de Jongh correspondence yield strict containment at every consecutive width. The equality at widths one and two is a known distributive-lattice result, correctly credited to Grilletti and Xiao. The self-contained checker tested generated-subframe capacity through three-fold powers, exhaustive maximal colorings and independent direct enumeration in five small cases. Finite evidence is supportive, not substituted for the proof.

## Originality — PASS (historical priority qualified)
The closest directly comparable source is Xiao, arXiv:2606.31893, Theorem 5.4, which constructs only the geometrically spaced sequence of widths 1,4,16,... using the general branching estimate of Lemma 4.6. That estimate bounds a target fork of depth two only by a multiple of four times the generating branching and does not establish exact capacity or separate consecutive widths. Xiao’s earlier 2024 thesis, Chapter 4.3, likewise gives geometric spacing, and its Chapter 5 treats different nerve questions. Grilletti’s distributive-lattice theorem covers the equality of the initial logics, not the strict hierarchy of non-distributive rooted fans. One earlier related mathematical record concerns morphisms between full nonempty powerset frames; it does not address three-or-more-arm fans as target quotients of non-distributive fan powers. Semantic searches and available historical summaries did not reveal an equivalent or stronger theorem. A general unpublished or differently phrased result on p-morphic images of poset products remains a residual priority risk.

## Value — PASS
Comparability of generalized Medvedev logics associated with concrete finite rooted frames is identified explicitly as an open structural direction in Xiao’s Section 6. A previous descending sequence has artificial factor-four gaps because it relies on a coarse height-versus-branching estimate. The exact, all-power fan-quotient invariant resolves every adjacent pair and all comparisons in a natural one-parameter subfamily, and it provides explicit characteristic-formula separators. The result therefore refines a motivated classification problem rather than changing only arbitrary numeric constants.
