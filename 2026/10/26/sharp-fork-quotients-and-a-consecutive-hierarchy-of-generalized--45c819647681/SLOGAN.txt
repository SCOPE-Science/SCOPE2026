For the generalized Medvedev logics generated by topless powers of a rooted fork with \(b\) middle points, the largest fork obtainable as a generated-subframe \(p\)-morphic image has exactly \(\max\{b,2\}\) arms. This sharp invariant separates every consecutive nontrivial width: \(\mathcal L_2\supsetneq\mathcal L_3\supsetneq\mathcal L_4\supsetneq\cdots\), improving the previously known geometrically spaced chain.
