# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The general upper bound follows from strict growth of the reachable nonempty nonsingleton subsets, of which there are eleven. The explicit four-state tables give exactly the masks `15,13,7,11,14,12,5,3,10,9,6,4` at successive first-appearance heights. Full closure over all unary and binary images is checked directly. The explicit height-eleven full context has twelve independent leaves and constant root output. The single-symbol impossibility follows from the operation-image restriction to at most three states and the same growth bound.

**Originality — PASS within inspected evidence.** Blažej–Janoušek–Plachý establish the general \(2^n-n-1\) upper bound, but their explicit constructions require larger ranked alphabets and do not imply the two-symbol four-state extremum. Herrmann–Mörbitz establish a two-symbol Landau-function family for \(n\ge5\), not exact four-state saturation. Searches for full-context equivalents, ranked binary operations, and the four-state bound did not identify a covering theorem. No database search alone establishes uniqueness or absolute historical priority; independently archived equivalent witnesses remain possible.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** The result answers the natural first nontrivial sharpness case for the universal tree reset-height bound at a minimal ranked alphabet. It exhibits a full eleven-step traversal of every nonsingleton image, not a routine adjustment of the Landau construction, and shows why genuine binary cooperation can attain the extremum when one operation cannot. It does not claim an asymptotic exponential lower bound or classify larger alphabets.
