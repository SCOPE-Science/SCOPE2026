The exact real harmonic quadratic \(L^4/L^2\) norm on the probability-normalized disk has fourth power \(4.330553360251238\ldots\), determined by one unique positive quartic root with all extremizers classified up to rotation and nonzero real scaling. The best polynomial using at most two homogeneous degrees has fourth-power ratio only \(297/86\), so constant, linear, and quadratic harmonic modes must cooperate to attain the sharp norm.
