# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The complete stationary generator closes on rotation-invariant mixed moments through degree four. Its explicit solution yields the stated second/fourth radial moments; independent thermal Brownian motion contributes the displayed Gaussian corrections. Jensen's inequality supplies the exact feasible second-harmonic range and the rigidity of its endpoints, while the fourth-moment rational function is strictly decreasing in the second-mode damping rate. Its high-rate ratio and two-harmonic inversion are algebraic consequences. An independent finite-heading Markov calculation and stationary exact-jump simulation corroborate, but do not replace, the proof. Hypotheses and the rotationally invariant stationary selection are explicit.

## Originality — PASS (self-review)

Britton et al. (arXiv:2608.23519) already analyze free-space higher moments for correlated reorientation, and Smith et al. (arXiv:2207.10445) already solve the right-angle turning special case in a harmonic trap. Neither special calculation is claimed as new. Sun et al. (arXiv:2409.00578 and arXiv:2504.20352) and Guéneau et al. (arXiv:2602.08436) study trapped models with independent orientation redraws or additional tumble phases, unlike a general correlated incremental-turn kernel. The new claim is the exact all-kernel sharp stationary fourth-moment envelope at fixed first Fourier mode, its equality laws, and the second-harmonic fast-tumble crossover. General moment-generator technology may implicitly contain some identities; independent priority assessment is needed.

## Value — PASS

The first angular harmonic fixes the particle's stationary cloud width, yet the second harmonic can produce a quantitatively different shape. The sharp, attainable envelope is a physically motivated inverse/experimental benchmark, not an arbitrary finite numerical example; the limiting kurtosis crossover reveals a non-Gaussian persistence channel invisible to mean-square measurements.

## Limitations and review state

Reflection-symmetric relative-angle tumbles, planar isotropic harmonic confinement, known parameters for inversion, stationary isotropic preparation; only two radial moments and their sharp envelope, not full densities or Gaussian distributional convergence. The right-angle endpoint and standard moment methods are established prior art. Broad implicit-coverage risk and independent validation remain open.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
