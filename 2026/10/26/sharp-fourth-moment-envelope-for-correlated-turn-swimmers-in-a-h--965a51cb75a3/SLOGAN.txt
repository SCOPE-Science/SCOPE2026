At fixed first angular reorientation mode, a harmonically trapped planar run-and-tumble swimmer has an exact, nondegenerate range of stationary radial fourth moments: symmetric fixed-angle turning minimizes it and forward/reversal-only turning maximizes it. In the athermal fast-tumble regime, the normalized fourth moment can approach any value between \(2\) and \(3\), controlled by the second angular mode, while the mean-squared radius is unchanged.
