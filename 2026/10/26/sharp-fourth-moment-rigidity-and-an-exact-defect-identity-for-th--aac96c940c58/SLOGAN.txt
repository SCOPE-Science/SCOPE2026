The radius-one planar Fourier cross has the exact sharp normalized fourth-moment constant \(27/11\), attained only by affine-phase coefficients with central-to-arm squared-magnitude ratio \(3:2\). An exact nonnegative deficit identity quantifies shell imbalance, axis imbalance, opposite-arm asymmetry, and phase frustration; the same theorem holds on nonaliasing finite rectangular tori.
