# Scope of the stability result

The claim is the quantitative squared-magnitude estimate for complete square-free quadratic Fourier layers in \(n\ge5\) variables, with arbitrary complex coefficients. The comparison with the all-square-free-layer proof treats the sharp constant and qualitative equality as shared background and separates the unit-coefficient balancing estimate from the larger stability coefficients obtained by the two edge-pair majorants. No inference of historical priority is made from catalogue dates.

The exact convolution identity and the unsigned incidence diagonalization prove the global estimate even when coefficients vanish. The bound turns a fourth-moment deficit into an explicit estimate on the variance of squared coefficient magnitudes. It does not quantify phases, claim the largest possible stability coefficient, or apply to arbitrary graph supports. The source checker is finite corroboration only.
