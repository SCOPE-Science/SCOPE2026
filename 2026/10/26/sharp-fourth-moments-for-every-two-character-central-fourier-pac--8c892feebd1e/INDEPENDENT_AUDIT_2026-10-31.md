# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For 0<=n<m, Haar-orthonormal unnormalized SU(2) characters have Clebsch–Gordan tensor decomposition in degree-two step progressions. Direct intersection counts yield integrals chi_n^4=A, chi_m^4=B, chi_n^2 chi_m^2=A and triple products H=max(0,A-(m-n)/2), J=A for even gaps (both zero for odd gaps). Expansion for arbitrary complex a,b depends on x=Re(a bar b) as A|a|^4+B|b|^4+2A|ab|^2+4Ax^2+4H|a|^2x+4J|b|^2x. For odd gaps, choosing aligned/opposed phase and maximizing B+2(3A-B)u-(5A-B)u^2 gives the precise B<3A mixing and B>=3A pure-m threshold. For even gaps, convexity in relative phase forces positive alignment, reducing the sharp value to unique maximum of F(t)=(At^4+4Ht^3+6At^2+4At+B)/(1+t^2)^2. Its derivative has sign p(t)=A+(3A-B)t+3(H-A)t^2-2At^3-Ht^4, one positive root by Descartes, and no better boundary. Independent exact character fusion checked 63 (n,m) pairs x 3 nonreal coefficient choices; sympy independently verified derivative factorization.
