For a two-feature Gaussian monomial excitation matrix \((G_j,G_j^d)\), the pseudoinverse has a finite \(p\)-th moment exactly for \(p<m/d\), so its expected norm becomes finite precisely after more than \(d\) observations. The smallest-singular-value tail has exponent \(m/d\), with a logarithmic correction at \((m,d)=(2,2)\).
