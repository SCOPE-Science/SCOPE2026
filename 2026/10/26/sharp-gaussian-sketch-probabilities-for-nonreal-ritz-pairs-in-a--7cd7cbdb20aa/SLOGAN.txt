Two-step Gaussian-sketched Rayleigh–Ritz on an explicit positive-definite Lanczos triple produces nonreal eigenvalue pairs with an exact Student-integral probability. For \(\nu=\ell-1\) sketch degrees of freedom, that probability is asymptotic to \(\sqrt{(b^2+c^2)/(3\pi b^2\nu)}(1+b^2/c^2)^{-\nu/2}\), giving both the sharp decay rate and prefactor.
