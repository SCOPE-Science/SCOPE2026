# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof isolates the exact two obstructions for generating \(\mathbf K_3^m\) using the information order, constructs explicit separating terms for sufficiency, and proves the capacity bound with a tight edge matching. For random generators it counts the compatible letter pairs and overlapping-pair triples directly, applies a standard dependency-neighbourhood Poisson bound, and checks that undefined-symbol obstruction vanishes. Every quantified statement uses the signature without named \(u\); a signature containing such a constant would need a different criterion. The included checker computes the actual generated subalgebras exhaustively through \(m,k\le3\) and checks hypercube matching counts through dimension four. Finite tests do not establish the unbounded statements.

**Originality — PASS within inspected sources.** The 2026 motivation establishes three-valued partial-function semantics. Falgas-Ravry–Markström (2016) already study the *identical* random-subcube model and derive its coarse edge-onset scale, so that fact is prior and not represented as new. Their available primary theorem does not assert the constant-window Poisson law. The algebraic generation criterion and its sharp rank/matching consequence are not present in the inspected motivating definitions or indexed scientific comparisons. Older free-Kleene-algebra literature is a recognized residual overlap risk, particularly where full text was not accessible.

**Value — PASS.** The result solves the algebraic finite-power generation question completely, gives a sharp capacity and multiplicity at saturation, and quantitatively distinguishes typical random generators from optimal ones. The application to partial-valued semialgebra semantics motivates both the precise object and the critical probability being measured.

**Closest literature and limitations.** Abou El Wafa–Platzer, *Subreflexive Logic: Completeness without Identity*, arXiv:2607.24623; Falgas-Ravry–Markström, *Random Subcube Intersection Graphs I: Cliques and Covering*, arXiv:1309.7375; Berman–Mukaidono, *Enumerating fuzzy switching functions and free Kleene algebras*, doi:10.1016/0898-1221(84)90083-X (abstract inspected, not full text). The sampling model assumes independent uniform ternary entries; extensions to other signatures or biased sampling are not proved. Priority is limited by inaccessible older full text and alternative terminology.
