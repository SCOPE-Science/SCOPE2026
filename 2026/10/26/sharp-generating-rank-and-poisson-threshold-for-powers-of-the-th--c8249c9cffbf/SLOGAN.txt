A \(k\)-tuple generates the \(m\)-fold three-valued Kleene Boolean semialgebra exactly when its \(m\) coordinate words define pairwise disjoint, positive-dimensional Boolean subcubes, yielding the sharp generator rank \(1+\lceil\log_2 m\rceil\). Uniform random generators exhibit a Poisson critical window: if \(m/(9/7)^{k/2}\to\lambda\), their generation probability tends to \(e^{-\lambda^2/2}\).
