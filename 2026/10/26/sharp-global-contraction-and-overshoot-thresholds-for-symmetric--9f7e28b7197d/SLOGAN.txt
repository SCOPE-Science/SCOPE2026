For symmetric two-state exchange, conservative-stage \(\operatorname{MPRK22}(1/2)\) is globally contractive at every positive time step, with an exactly optimal worst-case normalized step \(t_*\approx1.7511007024\). It crosses equilibrium for some positive states precisely when \(t>(\sqrt{17}-1)/2\), while the special step \(t=2\) has an exact cubic error recursion.
