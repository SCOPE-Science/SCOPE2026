# Verification

The analytical proof uses normalized \(SU(2)\) characters and the exact Clebsch–Gordan decomposition. The critical discrete-to-continuum estimate is uniform in all complex coefficient arrays of squared norm one. The two-channel weak limit and compact triangular kernels prove an infinite-dimensional limit; no finite computation is used in place of this argument.

`checks.py` reproduces the Haar integral and fusion expansion for four complex coefficient arrays, and tests finite \(N\) convergence for the two explicitly integrable continuum profiles. The flat and ramp limits are \(1/12\) and \(34/315\) respectively. These tests verify normalization only; they cannot certify uniqueness, a closed-form optimum, or all infinite-dimensional steps.
