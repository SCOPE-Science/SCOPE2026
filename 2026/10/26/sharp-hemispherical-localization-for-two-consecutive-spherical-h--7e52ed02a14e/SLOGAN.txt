For the pair of consecutive spherical-harmonic degrees \(\ell\) and \(\ell+1\), north-hemisphere concentration has a completely explicit two-by-two spectral decomposition and a sharp localization constant \(\tfrac12+\sqrt{2\ell+3}\,\Gamma(\ell+3/2)/(2(\ell+1)\sqrt\pi\,\Gamma(\ell+1))\). The only maximizing azimuthal orders are \(m=\pm\ell\), and the constants decrease to \(\tfrac12+1/\sqrt{2\pi}\).
