In the repeated donation game with independent heterogeneous generous-tit-for-tat parameters, replacing the strategy distribution by its mean always overstates expected pair payoff, strictly so for any nonconstant generosity law. At fixed mean and allowed generosity range, the exact worst-case bias is attained uniquely by mixing the two extreme generosity values; the bias persists for stationary incremental-generosity-tuning populations of arbitrarily large size.
