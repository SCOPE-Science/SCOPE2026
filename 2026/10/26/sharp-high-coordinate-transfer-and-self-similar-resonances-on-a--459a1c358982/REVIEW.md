# Scientific review: sharp high-coordinate transfer on a dyadic infinite hypercube

## Correctness (C) — PASS

The claim is restricted to the exact norm-convergent sum of commuting coordinate flips, a specified vacuum initial state, singleton targets and a high-index asymptotic. The product state gives independent Bernoulli coordinate probabilities with summable flip intensities. Viète's formula yields exact resonant probability \(4/\pi^2\). The finite duplication identity and global bounds force every all-time maximizer into one shrinking resonance window. The local sinc envelope has a unique strict maximizer, with analytic corrections \(-3/(\pi2^m)\) in the time and \(3/(\pi^2 4^m)\) in relative probability. A separate upper-tail argument excludes perfect singleton transfer for every finite index. The standalone verifier makes 142 computational cross-checks but is not treated as an infinite proof.

## Originality (O) — PASS, best knowledge

Wang's 2026 full text [1] defines and studies the graph and dyadic weight, but its published sections analyze spectrum and time reversal, not this exact resonant transport and sharp all-time optimizer. The earlier Wang paper [2] proves finite equal-weight antipodal perfect transfer and thus cannot be substituted for a countably infinite geometric-weight singleton law. The relevant peak-transfer paper by Coutinho, Guo and Schmeits [3] studies finite matrix spectral criteria and weighted paths, not the present operator and limiting constant. Scientific literature searches and statement-level comparisons of related published transport results found no implication subsuming the precise claim. General product factorization is conceded as prior; novelty rests on the complete high-coordinate global transfer law and exactly translated infinite tail. An unlocated broader theorem remains a residual risk; priority is not independently certified.

## Value (V) — PASS

The observable is the global optimum of experimentally interpretable single-vertex readout probability, not an arbitrary auxiliary coefficient. The special dyadic coupling is explicit in the motivating model, and yields a universal sharp non-perfect high-coordinate ceiling despite resonant deterministic excitation of a selected bit. The invariant probability tail and the quantitatively shifted optimum distinguish transport to a *single vertex* from transfer into an infinite superposition. This is a meaningful transport boundary beyond the earlier spectral or fixed-time symmetry results.

## Limits and primary comparisons

The result is not uniform under perturbing the weight profile or when the initial state and target are changed. It provides no finite-index optimizer uniqueness except for all sufficiently high indices. The claims and proof use the natural adjacency normalization, rather than a scaled graph Laplacian.
