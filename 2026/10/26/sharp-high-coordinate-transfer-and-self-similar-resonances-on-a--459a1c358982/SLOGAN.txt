In a dyadically weighted countable hypercube, exact resonant transport to the distant singleton \(\{m\}\) has a universal fidelity \(4/\pi^2\) and leaves a self-similar independent tail of higher-coordinate excitations. Optimizing over *all* observation times gives a unique sufficiently-high-index optimum at \(\pi2^m-3/(\pi2^m)+O(2^{-3m})\) and probability \((4/\pi^2)(1+3/(\pi^2 4^m)+O(16^{-m}))\), establishing a sharp non-perfect transfer ceiling.
