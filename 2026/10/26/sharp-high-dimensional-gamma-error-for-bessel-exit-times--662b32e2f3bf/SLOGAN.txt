For the one-factor, mean-and-variance-matched Gamma approximation to a unit-ball Bessel exit time, the high-dimensional Kolmogorov error is sharply \(1/(3\sqrt{2\pi a})+O(a^{-1})\) as the Bessel index \(a-1\) grows. The entire leading signed CDF error is the explicit profile \(a(z^2-1)\varphi(z)/[3(a+2)\sqrt{a+1}]\), uniformly in standardized threshold \(z\).
