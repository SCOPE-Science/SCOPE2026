## Correctness
The cyclic-basis argument, polynomial stabilizer calculation, high-power identity, and uniform coefficient count jointly prove all claimed statements. The cutoff equality belongs to the complementary regime. Exact finite checks over multiple small truncated rings agreed, but are not a substitute for the general proof.

## Originality
The similarity literature supplies companion-matrix normal forms, not the strict high-tail nilpotency gaps and their element multiplicities. The published exact distribution over dual numbers assumes \(e=2\), where there are no high-tail missing integers; it does not settle \(e\geq3\). The general ring-length hypothesis in Avni et al. is relevant, but their classification is about similarity classes rather than the stated index-counting invariant. A bounded search cannot certify absolute priority.

## Value
The strict upper tail records how long nilpotent Jordan chains persist under thickening. The sharp arithmetic-progression support and closed fiber counts, uniform in \(m,e,q\), are meaningful structural information even without classifying the entire lower-index distribution.
