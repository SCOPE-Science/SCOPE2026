Over \(R=\mathbb F_q[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^e)\) with \(e\geq3\), lifts of one regular nilpotent Jordan block have high nilpotency indices exactly \(em-(e-1)v\) for \(0\leq v\leq\lfloor(m-1)/(e-1)\rfloor\), with respective multiplicities \((q-1)q^{(e-1)m^2-v-1}\). These arithmetic gaps are sharp in every characteristic; the maximum index occurs with probability \(1-q^{-1}\).
