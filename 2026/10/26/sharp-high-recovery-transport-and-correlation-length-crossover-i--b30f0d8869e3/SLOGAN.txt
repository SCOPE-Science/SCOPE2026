In the stationary tandem exclusion process, the transport-optimal vacancy fraction is asymptotic to \(2^{-2/3}q^{-1/3}\) and the optimized current approaches its high-recovery ceiling with the sharp deficit \(3\,2^{-2/3}q^{-1/3}\). The clustering length grows like \(2^{-1/3}q^{1/3}\) at the optimizing density, versus \(\sqrt{q\rho(1-\rho)}\) at any fixed interior density, correcting a previously stated general-density coefficient. Along the optimum, the integrated occupation covariance nevertheless tends to the finite universal value \(1\).
