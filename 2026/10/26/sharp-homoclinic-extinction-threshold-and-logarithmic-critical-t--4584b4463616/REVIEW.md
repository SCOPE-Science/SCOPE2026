# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The reaction stoichiometry gives the stated quadratic system. For the strictly positive ratio \(r=y/x\), direct differentiation gives \(\dot r=xr[(b-a)+(b-c)r]\) and the exact logarithmic quadrature in the result. In all three qualifying rate regimes, the integrated orbit profiles vanish at both ratio endpoints. Monotonicity, uniqueness and compactness prove completeness and homoclinicity, not merely a sampled observation. Reciprocal-concentration derivatives establish the universal dominant tails. Integrating the ratio at each equality threshold produces the asserted logarithms; for strict inequalities the parametric endpoint powers produce the minority exponents. Explicit one-sided Riccati inequalities prove the sharp converse. Exact checker output confirms key algebra but is not presented as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS under inspected evidence
The most directly relevant source, Yeung–Erban arXiv:2610.06130v1, Example 2.1, already proves homoclinic topology and a first integral at the *single* rate triple \(a=c=1,b=3\); these are credited, not claimed new. Their Section 4 emphasizes qualitative class realizability rather than a three-rate quantitative threshold. Algaba–García–Reyes arXiv:1805.02873v1 characterizes analytic integrability of related homogeneous Lotka–Volterra germs; its examined statements do not specify the complete all-rate global homoclinic region or original-time logarithmic boundary constants. Searches of the mathematical record and recoverable prior claims identified no direct statement of this combined theorem. General quadratic flow classification may imply parts of the qualitative region, and historical priority of exact logarithmic tails remains a residual risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The network is the three-reaction example of a contemporary classification of strongly nonhyperbolic chemical systems. The theorem upgrades one phase-portrait example to an exact necessary-and-sufficient **three-rate** condition for all positive kinetic states to be complete homoclinics despite autocatalysis. Two natural codimension-one rate equalities cause universal logarithmic corrections and distinguish critical kinetics from generic algebraic relaxation. Existence of open sets with one-sided finite-time explosion proves sharpness and has direct structural meaning; this is not a gratuitous single initial state or a renaming of the source's invariant.

## Limits and closest prior results
This concerns only deterministic three-reaction mass-action kinetics. The general homogeneous Lotka–Volterra literature gives much of the background integrability; an equivalent all-rate dynamical treatment in obscure earlier literature cannot be ruled out without independent historical review. No generalization to reaction-diffusion systems or stochastic models is claimed.
