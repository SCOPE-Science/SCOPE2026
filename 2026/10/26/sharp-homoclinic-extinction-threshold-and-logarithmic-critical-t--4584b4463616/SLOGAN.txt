The three-reaction quadratic chemical system \(\dot x=x(ax-by),\ \dot y=y(bx-cy)\) has every positive trajectory homoclinic to extinction exactly when \(b\ge\max\{a,c\}\) but the three rates are not all equal. At either equality boundary, the minority concentration switches from an algebraic tail to a universal \(1/(|t|\log|t|)\) law; outside this rate region some trajectories blow up in finite time in one direction.
