For Kann--Feng repulsive bounded-confidence averaging on a signed complete network, every initially receptive pair stays receptive for all time, for every initial profile, exactly when the two largest hostile degrees satisfy \(d_{(1)}+d_{(2)}<n\). Below this sharp threshold, every trajectory becomes exactly stationary in finitely many steps at a quantized opinion profile; at or above it, some initially receptive profiles lose a pair immediately.
