For two cliques linked by a single edge, degree-normalized quantum evolution transfers a coherent clique-uniform state with sharp asymptotic all-time fidelity \(4\rho^2/(1+\rho^2)^2\), where \(\rho\) is the limiting clique-size ratio. A single nonbridge-vertex preparation loses an additional factor of its clique size, even though the Hamiltonian and observation are unchanged.
