For every \(n\ge5\), the two-subset Kneser graph has maximum induced-forest order \(\max\{7,n\}\), with a complete sharp classification into four-clique-with-pendant families at \(n\le7\) and star-plus-edge families at \(n\ge7\). The exchange graph of optimum families is an explicit disjoint union of cliques, with two extremal shapes coexisting only at \(n=7\).
