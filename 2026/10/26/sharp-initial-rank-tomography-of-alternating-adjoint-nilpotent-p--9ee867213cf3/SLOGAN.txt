For an alternating \(T,T^*\) nilpotent path with \(d\)-dimensional blocks and \(m\) vertices, every higher-rank numerical range is an explicitly computable disk or the empty set, determined solely by the singular values of \(T\). Exactly \((d-1)\lfloor m/2\rfloor+1\) consecutive initial ranks suffice—and are necessary in general—to recover all \(d\) singular values.
