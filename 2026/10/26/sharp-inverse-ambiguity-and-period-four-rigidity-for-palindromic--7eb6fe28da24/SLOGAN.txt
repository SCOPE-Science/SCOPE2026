For one length-\(k\) palindromic duplication over every \(q\)-letter alphabet, all \(k\ge3\) not divisible by four have sharp worst-case inverse-list size \(\lceil(n-k+1)/2\rceil\). At odd numbers of possible starts greater than one, precisely \(q(q-1)\) period-four received words attain it; lengths divisible by four already fail the same threshold at \(n=k+2\).
