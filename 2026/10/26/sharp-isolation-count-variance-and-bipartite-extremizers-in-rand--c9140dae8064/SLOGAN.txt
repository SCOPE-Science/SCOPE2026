For the uniform-mark irregular subgraph of every triangle-free \(d\)-regular host, the number of isolated vertices has variance at most \(R_d n/(d+1)<2n/(d+1)\), where the explicit sharp \(R_d\) tends to \(2\). Equality for the finite-degree bound characterizes disjoint unions of \(K_{d,d}\), giving both a sharp extremal graph theorem and the optimal universal constant.
