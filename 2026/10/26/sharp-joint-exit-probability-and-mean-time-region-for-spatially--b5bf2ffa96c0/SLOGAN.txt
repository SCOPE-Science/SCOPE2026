For inward-injected, one-dimensional persistent particles with arbitrary nonnegative continuous spatial reversal rates, the right-exit probability depends only on total reversal intensity, while the mean absorption time also measures its spatial centroid. Their complete attainable probability–time region is exactly \(0<p<1\) and \(pL/v<\tau<(2-p)L/v\), together with the ballistic point \((1,L/v)\); infinitely many distinct smooth profiles share each interior pair.
