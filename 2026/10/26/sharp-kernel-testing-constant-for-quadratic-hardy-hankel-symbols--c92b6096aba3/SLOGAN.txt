Among all nonzero quadratic Hardy Hankel symbols \(\psi(z)=az+bz^2\), the normalized-Szegő-kernel testing norm is at least \(5/(3\sqrt3)\) times the full Hankel norm, and the constant is sharp. Equality holds exactly at the mixed-coefficient modulus ratio \(|a|/|b|=1/\sqrt6\), with a maximizing kernel of the same radius.
