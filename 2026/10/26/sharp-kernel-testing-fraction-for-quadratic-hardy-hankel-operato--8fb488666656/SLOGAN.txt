For every nonzero quadratic analytic Hardy Hankel symbol \(\psi(z)=az+bz^2\), the normalized Szegő-kernel tests capture at least exactly \(5/(3\sqrt3)\) of the operator norm. The worst loss occurs precisely when \(|a|=|b|/\sqrt6\), with a unique maximizing kernel radius \(1/\sqrt6\), giving a sharp quantitative benchmark for the first nonmonomial family.
