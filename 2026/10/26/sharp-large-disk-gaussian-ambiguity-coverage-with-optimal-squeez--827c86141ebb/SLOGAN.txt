Three equally precise rotated squeezed Gaussian windows are the smallest family that beats isotropic Gaussian ambiguity decay on expanding disks. For each fixed \(m\ge3\), optimal squeezing and equispacing give the sharp squared-multiplier floor \((2/m+o(1))\exp[-\pi R^2\sin(\pi/m)]\), with a quantified angular-gap and squeeze perturbation penalty.
