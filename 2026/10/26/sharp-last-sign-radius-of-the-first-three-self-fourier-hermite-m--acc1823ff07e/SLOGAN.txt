The first three real Fourier-self-dual Hermite modes, of orders \(0,4,8\), have sharp last-sign radius \(\sqrt{(14-2z_*)/(2\pi)}=0.600964387854839\ldots\), where \(z_*\) is the unique real root of \(2z^3-23z^2+84z-105=0\). The only extremizers are positive multiples of the Gaussian times \(u(u-14+2z_*)(u-z_*)^2\), with \(u=2\pi x^2\); two contact points prove global optimality.
