For strictly positive one-zero \(3\times3\) ReLU completion matrices with a rank-one leading block, a single adjugate pairing exactly distinguishes attainable rank two from nonattaining rank-two approximation. In the latter class, clipping the hidden negative entry at magnitude \(B\) produces the sharp global squared-error asymptotic \(E(B)\sim\gamma^2/(B^2\|A\|_F^2)\).
