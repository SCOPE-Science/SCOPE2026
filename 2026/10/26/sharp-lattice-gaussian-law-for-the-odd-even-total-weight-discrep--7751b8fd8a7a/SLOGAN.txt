For uniformly random ordinary partitions, the odd-minus-even **total-weight** discrepancy has a sharp lattice Gaussian law on scale \(n^{3/4}\), with limiting variance \(2\sqrt6/\pi\). Precisely when \(4\mid n\), the fraction of partitions with exactly balanced odd and even weights is asymptotic to \(2/(6^{1/4}n^{3/4})\).
