For independently stochastically rounded \(2\times2\) scaled rotations, the expected squared smaller singular value strictly decreases despite growth of total squared singular-value energy; its sharp relative floor is \((5-\sqrt5)/4\). The worst input orientation switches from symmetric to two asymmetric orientations at the exact norm \(5/(4\sqrt2)\), and a perfectly conditioned input can become singular with probability \(9/16\).
