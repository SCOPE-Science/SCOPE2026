# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The exact mass matrix, invertible regular-tree Green kernel, absolute edge integral, extremizing sign function, duality and Neumann tail estimates reconstruct the entire claimed theorem. Root and off-root matrix identities, summability, and finite-graph locality were checked separately. The proof covers all integer degrees at least three and all finite connected graphs with unit edges; finite experiments are supplementary.

**Originality — PASS under available primary evidence.** Oswald (arXiv:math/0611573v1, inspected full accessible text) establishes uniform one-dimensional spline bounds and planar triangulation counterexamples, not the sharp regular-tree degree profile. Post (arXiv:0712.1507v1, inspected accessible text) treats spectral metric-graph operators rather than this mass projector. Existing periodic-line and rank-one complete-bipartite cases are distinct and not reclaimed. A historically relevant 1987 finite-element stability paper was only accessible at the abstract/description level; undiscovered stronger coverage remains a risk. The universal three-bound itself is not represented as original.

**Value — PASS.** The exact branching constant, finite-tree sharpness of a universal endpoint envelope, and high-girth norm convergence give natural stability information for least-squares approximation on networks. The results separate regular metric-tree behavior from intervals and from nonuniform multidimensional triangulations.

**Limits.** Edges are unit length; the result does not establish optimal high-girth convergence rates or arbitrary weighted/higher-order extensions. Search nonappearance is not a historical priority certificate. The standalone verifier checks 1,648 finite exact and high-precision identities, but the infinite theorem is proven analytically.
