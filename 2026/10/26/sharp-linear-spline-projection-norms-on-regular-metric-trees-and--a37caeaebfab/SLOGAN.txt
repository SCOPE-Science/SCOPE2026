On the infinite unit-edge \(d\)-regular metric tree, the sharp \(L^1\)/\(L^\infty\) norm of continuous linear-spline least-squares projection is \(3(1+q_d^2)/(1+q_d)^2\), where \(q_d=1/(d+\sqrt{d^2-d+1})\). These constants rise strictly toward \(3\), which is the best uniform bound even across finite metric trees, and finite regular high-girth graph norms converge to the same degree-dependent constants at an explicit exponential rate.
