On the unit Lipschitz ball over \([-1,1]\), the exact endpoint errors of Legendre and Chebyshev spectral projections are explicit absolute kernel integrals, attained by derivative-sign extremizers. Their sharp asymptotic errors separate in order: \(\mathcal E_n^L\sim\sqrt{2/(\pi n)}\), whereas \(\mathcal E_n^C\sim8/(\pi^2n)\).
