The prime parts of the Pell sequence and its trace companion admit complete closed-form Möbius spectra and sharp integral Dold-repair factors, including a negative-orbit obstruction for the trace companion. For odd primes the Pell prime part is Dold exactly in residues \(1,7\pmod8\), whereas the trace companion is Dold exactly in residues \(1,5,7\pmod8\); at \(2\) their least repairs are \(2\) and \(1\), respectively.
