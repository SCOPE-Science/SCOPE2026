At perfect off-origin preferential resetting, the empirical origin-visit fraction of the impurity monkey walk is asymptotic to \(1/[(1-\xi_0)\log n]\) almost surely. More sharply, its reciprocal has a universal negative \((1-\xi_0)\log\log n\) correction and an almost-sure finite random additive limit, a quantitative endpoint effect not contained in the previously known stationary-profile prediction.
