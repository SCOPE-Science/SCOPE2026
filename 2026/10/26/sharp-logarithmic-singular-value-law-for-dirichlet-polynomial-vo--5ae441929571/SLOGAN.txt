For every nonconstant Dirichlet-polynomial symbol, the compact Volterra operator on \(\mathcal H^2\) has singular values \(s_N\sim C_g/\log N\), where \(C_g\) is exactly the finite-torus supremum norm of the logarithmically differentiated symbol. This gives a sharp logarithmic singular-value scale for arbitrary interacting frequencies, far beyond the previously known compactness and absence of Schatten-class membership.
