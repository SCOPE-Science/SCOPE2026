For a prescribed transverse bang-bang switching trajectory, normalized-algebraic smoothing has an exact universal \(\delta\log(1/\delta)\) leading bias in smooth weighted terminal observations, despite an \(L^1\) control error of order \(\sqrt\delta\). For a double integrator with any interior single switch, terminal position error is \(\tfrac12\delta\log(1/\delta)+O(\delta)\) while velocity error is only \(O(\delta)\).
