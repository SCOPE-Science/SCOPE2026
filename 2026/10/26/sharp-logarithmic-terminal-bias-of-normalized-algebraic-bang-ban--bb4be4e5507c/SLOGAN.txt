For a prescribed transverse bang-bang switching trajectory, normalized-algebraic smoothing has a \(\delta\log(1/\delta)\) term in smooth weighted observations with an explicit, possibly zero coefficient, while the \(L^1\) control error is of order \(\sqrt\delta\) when a switch is present. For a double integrator with any interior single switch, terminal position error is \(\tfrac12\delta\log(1/\delta)+O(\delta)\) while velocity error is \(O(\delta)\).
