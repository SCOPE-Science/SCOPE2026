For classical two-stage Heun integration of the logistic ODE, the full biological interval is invariant for every initial value and arbitrarily many fixed steps exactly when \(ah\le\sqrt{9+6\sqrt3}-1\). Crossing this sharp boundary creates a one-step negative-output interval whose width grows as an explicit constant times \(\sqrt{ah-(\sqrt{9+6\sqrt3}-1)}\); the approximate threshold had previously been observed numerically.
