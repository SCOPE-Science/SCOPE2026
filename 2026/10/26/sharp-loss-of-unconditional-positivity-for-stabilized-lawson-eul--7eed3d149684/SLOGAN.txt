For a symmetric three-node diffusion path, the stabilized Lawson–Euler step preserves nonnegativity at every time step exactly when the two edge rates agree and the exponential-stabilization fraction is at least \(2/(3e)\). An arbitrarily small edge imbalance creates a negative transition coefficient beyond a unique, explicitly computable step, whose onset is delayed logarithmically as the imbalance vanishes—even throughout the unconditionally spectrally stable regime.
