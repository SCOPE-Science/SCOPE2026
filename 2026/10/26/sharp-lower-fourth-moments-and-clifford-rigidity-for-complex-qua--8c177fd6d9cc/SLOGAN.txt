For complex quadratic spherical harmonics in every even ambient dimension \(n\), the sharp normalized fourth-moment minimum is \(2(n+2)/(n+6)\); all extremizers are orthogonal images of scalar multiples of \(\sum_j(x_j+i x_{n/2+j})^2\). An exact three-term tensor sum of squares measures the deficit, and its equality conditions explain why the same coefficient cannot be attained in odd dimension.
