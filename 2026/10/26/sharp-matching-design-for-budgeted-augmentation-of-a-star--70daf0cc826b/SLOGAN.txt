For a star with \(n\) leaves, at most \(k\le\lfloor n/2\rfloor\) new leaf links and total conductance budget \(W\), the unique optimal designs up to relabeling add \(k\) disjoint, equally weighted links. Their exact Laplacian-pseudoinverse trace is \(n-1+1/(n+1)-2Wk/(k+2W)\), with an explicit strictly positive penalty for link overlap or unequal weights.
