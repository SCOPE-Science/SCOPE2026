For the unstabilized exponential-Euler step of complete-graph Allen–Cahn dynamics, the exact maximum-bound-preserving step is the unique \(h_*\in(0,1/2)\) satisfying \(2[h_*+(n-1)(1-e^{-\gamma h_*})/\gamma]=1+(n-1)e^{-\gamma h_*}\). The safe step has exactly one **intermediate-diffusion minimum**, and beyond it the worst-case overshoot turns on quadratically, despite the exact flow preserving the cube.
