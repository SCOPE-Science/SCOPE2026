For the two-node mass-lumped stochastic heat equation, positivity-preserving exponential splitting has an explicit sharp mean-square stability cutoff \(\sigma^2<s_*(h)\). Unlike the exact equation, which remains stable up to \(2a(a+2b)/(a+b)\), its scheme is stable at every timestep precisely for \(\sigma^2\le2a\); a concrete two-node finite-element example loses mean-square stability while remaining positive.
