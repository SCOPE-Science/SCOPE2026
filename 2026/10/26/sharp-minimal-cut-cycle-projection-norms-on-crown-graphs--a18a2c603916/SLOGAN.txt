For every crown graph \(\mathrm{Cr}_n=K_{n,n}-M\), the canonical orthogonal cut and cycle projections are minimum-norm projections under every simultaneous-index permutation-invariant edge norm. In real edge-\(\ell_1\), their sharp constants are \(3-4/n\) and \(2(2n^2-6n+3)/(n(n-1))\), with cut dominance at \(n=3,4\) and cycle dominance for all \(n\ge5\).
