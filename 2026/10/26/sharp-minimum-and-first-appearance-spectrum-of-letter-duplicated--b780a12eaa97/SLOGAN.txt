For every alphabet size \(q\ge2\) and word length \(n=(q+1)t+s\) with \(0\le s\le q\), the least possible longest letter-duplicated subsequence length is exactly \(2t+\mathbf{1}_{\{t\ge1,\ s=q\}}\), attained by the periodic alphabet-order word. A rational bivariate generating function additionally gives the exact length and first-appearance distribution of every distinct letter-duplicated subsequence of that extremal periodic word.
