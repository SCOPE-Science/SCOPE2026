For every nonterminal SPD Lanczos step, unconditioned Gaussian-sketched Galerkin has infinite expected energy-norm error regardless of oversampling, and its error has a sharp positive \(R^{-1}\) tail. On the same space, sketched minimal residual has finite error moment of order \(p>0\) exactly when \(p<\ell-k+1\), where \(\ell\) is the sketch row count and \(k\) the Krylov dimension.
