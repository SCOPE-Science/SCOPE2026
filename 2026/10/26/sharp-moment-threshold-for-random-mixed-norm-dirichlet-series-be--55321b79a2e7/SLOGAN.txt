For every nonconstant iid randomization of a mixed-norm Dirichlet Hardy space with \(0<q<4\), the exact threshold for its random symbol space to equal \(\mathscr H^{2,q}_{\alpha}\) is \(\mathbb E|X|^{\max(2,q)}<\infty\), with mean zero additionally necessary precisely when \(p>2\). A Bernoulli-thinning argument also proves the reverse inclusion without any moment hypothesis.
