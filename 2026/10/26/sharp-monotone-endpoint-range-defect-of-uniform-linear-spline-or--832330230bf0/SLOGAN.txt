For the uniform piecewise-linear \(L^2\) projector, nondecreasing data in \([0,1]\) have a sharp negative left-boundary undershoot \(\delta_n=3/[(2-r_n)(1+r_n)]-1\) with \(r_1=1/2\), \(r_{n+1}=1/(4-r_n)\); the reflected right-boundary overshoot is equal. The optimal defect increases strictly to \((\sqrt3-1)/2\), and a single jump in the first cell attains each finite-grid bound.
