On the canonical \(35\)-vertex odd graph \(O_4\), the sharp negative-type exponent is the unique \(p_*\in(0,1)\) with \(3(3^{p_*}-2^{p_*})=1\), and its squared-distance Euclidean embedding rank falls from \(34\) to \(20\) at the endpoint. The ordinary distance covariance \(e^{-t d}\) is positive semidefinite exactly at \(t=0\) or \(t\ge\frac12\log3\), with rank \(21\) at the positive cutoff.
