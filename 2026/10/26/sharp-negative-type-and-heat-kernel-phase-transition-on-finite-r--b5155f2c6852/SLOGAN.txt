The finite-field rank-two Grassmann graph has sharp negative-type exponent \(p_*=\log_2(1+q^{-1})\): its least snowflake Euclidean embedding dimension drops from \(\binom{n}{2}_q-1\) to \([n]_q-1\) exactly at the endpoint. For \(p>p_*\), the powered-distance covariance \(\exp(-a d^p)\) is admissible only at \(a=0\) or beyond an explicit positive spectral threshold; at \(p=1\) that threshold is \(\log q\).
