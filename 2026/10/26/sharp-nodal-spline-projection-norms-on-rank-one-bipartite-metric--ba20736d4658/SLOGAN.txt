For a complete bipartite metric graph with factorized edge lengths \(\ell_{ij}=u_iv_j\), the continuous nodal linear-spline \(L^2\) projector has exact \(L^1\) and \(L^\infty\) norms \(7/3-4\vartheta/[3(1+\vartheta)]\), governed only by the smallest normalized length factor \(\vartheta\). The theorem also determines every worst-case vertex, all optimal length-factor allocations at fixed bipartition sizes, and the sharp negative overshoot from nonnegative data.
