For the Stern diatomic sequence, the number of dyadic indices with consecutive residues \((1,2)\bmod9\) has discrepancy \(C_k-2^k/72\) whose exact root-growth rate is \(1.4390667245\ldots>\sqrt2\). An exact \(72\)-state transfer-matrix factorization and a nonvanishing scalar certificate show that square-root dyadic discrepancy fails for this residue pair.
