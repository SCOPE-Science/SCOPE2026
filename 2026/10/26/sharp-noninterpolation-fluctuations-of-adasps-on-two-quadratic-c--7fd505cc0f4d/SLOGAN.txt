On two noninterpolating strongly convex quadratic clients, the original loss-accumulating AdaSPS has a sharp last-iterate expected objective gap of \(1/(4\sqrt2 c\sqrt n)+o(n^{-1/2})\) for every fixed \(c\ge1\). Its rescaled iterates \(n^{1/4}x_n\) converge to a centered Gaussian of variance \(1/(2\sqrt2 c)\), despite the step depending on the current sampled loss.
