The exactly solvable active lattice-gas hydrodynamics amplifies some initially small species-density disturbances even while every eigenmode decays, except at one density that is uniquely self-adjoint under nonzero activity \(r=2/3\) where the two thresholds coincide. At half filling, despite an activity-independent decay spectrum, the maximal transient gain grows linearly with the Péclet number with an explicit sharp Fourier coefficient.
