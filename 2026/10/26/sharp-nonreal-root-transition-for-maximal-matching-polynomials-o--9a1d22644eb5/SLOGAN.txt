For the connected triangle-corona graphs \(H_r=T_r\circ K_2\) over subdivided stars, the maximal-matching polynomial is \(z^{2r+1}[2(z+8)^r+(z+6)^r+3rz(z+8)^{r-1}]\); its nonzero roots comprise exactly one real root for odd \(r\) and two for even \(r\). The first nonreal zeros arise at the seven-vertex core \(T_3\), and an exhaustive exact check shows that every triangle corona over a core with at most six vertices has a real-rooted maximal-matching polynomial.
