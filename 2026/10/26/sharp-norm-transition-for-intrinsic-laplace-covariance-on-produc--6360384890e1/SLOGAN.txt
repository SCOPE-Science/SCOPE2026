On a product of at least two circles, the intrinsic Laplace kernel of the coordinate \(\ell_p\) distance is positive definite for every damping strength precisely at the additive exponent \(p=1\); every finite \(p>1\) fails at every positive damping strength. The obstruction is a universal negative even-axial Fourier tail with an explicit antipodal coefficient, while the additive endpoint has strictly positive Fourier spectrum.
