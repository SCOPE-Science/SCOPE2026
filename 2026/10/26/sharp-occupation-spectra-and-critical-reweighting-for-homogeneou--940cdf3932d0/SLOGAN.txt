For homogeneous proportional pantograph dynamics, polynomially weighted physical-time occupations have exact polygonal weak-star limit sets, collapsing to ordinary Birkhoff statistics at the critical harmonic weight. For a square-root delay, ordinary sampling reaches the outer polygon of periodic states, logarithmic sampling yields a tunable inner polygon, and the critical double-logarithmic clock recovers the undelayed Birkhoff limit set for *every* underlying orbit.
