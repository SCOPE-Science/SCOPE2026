# Mathematical review

**Correctness — PASS.** The definition of a full line is unambiguous; each component contributes exactly two distinct affine rational points. In odd characteristic the intersection discriminant is \(t^2+4(c_i-c_j)\). Pairwise distinct shifts make every nonempty discriminant product squarefree, permitting the uniform Weil character-sum bound. Degree concentration, positivity, pair codegrees at most one, and fixed uniformity verify each hypothesis of the published hypergraph covering theorem. The point at infinity is covered by one added vertical line, and the quantifiers remain uniform over the shifts.

**Originality — PASS with residual bibliographic uncertainty.** The nearest primary paper introduces precisely these reducible conic unions but proves only the lower bound in Remark 1.4. Its asymptotic theorem explicitly requires irreducibility, so it does not imply this upper bound. The key new component is the simultaneous quadratic-character splitting estimate, leading to an odd-characteristic asymptotic covering of the reducible union. Published and indexed searches did not locate an equivalent theorem; this is not a proof of exhaustive priority.

**Value — PASS.** This answers a natural missing half of the asymptotic question for the explicit obstruction family highlighted in recent algebraic-geometry literature. It establishes the sharp constant uniformly for arbitrary fixed numbers of distinct translates, despite irreducibility being essential to the more ambitious degree-based bound. The argument gives a structural connection between finite-field squarefree character sums and asymptotically optimal incidence covers.

**Closest prior literature.** Asgarli et al., arXiv:2610.13042v1, especially Remark 1.4 and Section 3; classical Weil bounds; Pippenger–Spencer. The conclusion is existential, odd-characteristic only, and fixed-component-number only. It does not give exact small-field covers or an explicit threshold. Unindexed parallel incidence arguments remain a priority risk.
