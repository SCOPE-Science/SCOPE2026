For one-vector sketched FOM applied to a symmetric positive-definite matrix, an \(\varepsilon\)-embedding on the two-vector Krylov space admits the exact sharp Ritz interval \((\mu+L)/2\pm(L-\mu)/(2\sqrt{1-\varepsilon^2})\). The robust positivity frontier is precisely \(\varepsilon<2\sqrt{\mu L}/(\mu+L)\); above it even an admissible sketch can turn a decaying exponential surrogate into an exponentially growing one.
