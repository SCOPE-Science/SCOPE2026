For every \(0<\theta<\varphi<\pi\), degree-one complex operator pencils satisfy the sharp all-dimensions arc transfer bound \(\sup_{|t|\le\varphi}\|A+e^{it}B\|\le[\sin(\varphi/2)/\sin(\theta/2)]\sup_{|t|\le\theta}\|A+e^{it}B\|\), attained by \(A=-B\). This determines the exact ordinary and completely bounded norm, and the corresponding canonical distortion, of coefficient-preserving maps between the newly studied circular-arc function planes; no claim is made about their unrestricted Banach--Mazur distance.
