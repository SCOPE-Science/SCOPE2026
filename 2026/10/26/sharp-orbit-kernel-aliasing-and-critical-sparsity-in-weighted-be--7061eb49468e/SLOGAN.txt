For degree-\(N\) monomials in a weighted Bergman space, approximation by kernels on a freely chosen regular \(m\)-gon has an exact Fourier-aliasing formula. Its optimal captured energy is asymptotic to an explicit multiple of \(m/N\) for sublinear \(m\), stays strictly below one when \(m/N\) tends to a fixed number below one, and approaches one with a sharp square-root deficit as \(m/N\to1\) from below.
