On \(\mathbb H^3\), the oscillatory squared-secant distance kernel \(\operatorname{sech}^2(d)\cos(\omega d)\) stays positive semidefinite exactly up to \(\omega=\frac{2u_*}{\pi}\), where \(u_*\tanh u_*=1\). Beyond this sharp threshold, its spherical transform develops exactly one negative low-frequency band, with an explicitly characterized endpoint.
