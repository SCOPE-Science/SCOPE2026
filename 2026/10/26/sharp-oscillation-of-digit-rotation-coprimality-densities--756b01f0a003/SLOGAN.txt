For fixed integer base \(b\ge2\), the proportion of \(m\)-digit numbers whose cyclic digit rotations have common gcd one differs exponentially little from \(\varphi(b^m-1)/(b^m-1)\). These proportions oscillate sharply between lower limit \(0\) and upper limit \(\varphi(b-1)/(b-1)\), and every ratio \(\varphi(b^d-1)/(b^d-1)\) is a subsequential limit.
