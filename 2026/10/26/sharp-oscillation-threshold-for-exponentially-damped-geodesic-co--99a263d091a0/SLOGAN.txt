On hyperbolic three-space, the geodesic damped-cosine covariance \(e^{-ar}\cos(\omega r)\), for \(a>1\), is positive semidefinite exactly when \(3\omega^4+2(a^2+1)\omega^2\le(a^2-1)^2\). A direct spherical-transform factorization proves the sharp curvature-dependent boundary and identifies the low-frequency obstruction beyond it.
