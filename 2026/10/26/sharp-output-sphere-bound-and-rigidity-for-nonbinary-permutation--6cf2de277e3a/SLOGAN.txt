For the non-wrapping one-block permutation channel over \(q\ge k\ge4\) symbols, the sharp output-sphere maximum is \(1+\sum_{m=2}^k(n-m+1)[m!-2(m-1)!+(m-2)!]\). Equality holds exactly for words whose every \(k\)-symbol window is repetition-free, and precisely \(q!/(q-k)!\,(q-k+1)^{n-k}\) inputs achieve it.
