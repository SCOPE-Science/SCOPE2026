# Scientific review: overlap-matching type-spectrum bound

## Correctness — PASS
The admissible one-point diagrams are exactly the Boolean assignments to old pair variables that avoid simultaneously selecting all three pairs of any old hyperedge. This follows directly from tetrahedron avoidance and free amalgamation. Each forbidden triple involves three independent variables. When two old triples share a pair, their corresponding clauses share one variable and the simultaneous satisfaction probability is precisely \(1-2p^3+p^5\). Partitioning clauses along a maximum overlap matching and applying the Harris inequality yields the bound. Strict conditional positive correlation whenever two different blocks share a relevant pair variable proves that the equality cases are exactly matching-plus-isolate overlap graphs. Disjoint-block constructions realize every admissible matching number. The four-point and pencil boundary calculations are explicit; the finite checker verifies all bases through five points and selected larger cases. No infinite assertion relies on finite experiments.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The correlation inequality is classical and is explicitly credited to Harris; the source by Kalai, Keller and Mossel also studies more general correlation bounds. Conant's structural treatment of generic forbidden-clique hypergraphs is background. The published finite-type polynomial for the stronger \(K_4^-\)-free age concerns a different forbidden configuration, and does not address the arbitrary-base matching inequality or its complete equality criterion. The completed comparison found no paper asserting the sharp \((1-2p^3+p^5)^{\nu}\) block factor, full overlap-graph equality classification, and attainment for all matching parameters in this one-type setting. This is a best-knowledge determination; an implicit application of general product-correlation methods remains a genuine priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The weighted one-point type spectrum is a natural invariant of a homogeneous finite-constraint structure. The bound connects it directly to the overlap matching number of old triples, while the exact equality classification distinguishes linear constraints, isolated overlaps, and genuine multi-clause dependence. The correction factor is greater than one and its effect multiplies exponentially over independent overlap blocks; the pair-pencil family gives a persistent half-density of legal types while the independent-obstruction estimate tends exponentially to zero. These distinguishability and extremal statements extend beyond a single finite example and identify when the naïve independent-obstruction heuristic is mathematically exact.

Strict correlation and the matching bound use general prior methods, so implicit equivalent formulations are possible. The result covers nonalgebraic one-point types only, not higher arities.
