For the generic tetrahedron-free 3-hypergraph, the Bernoulli-weighted number of finite-base nonalgebraic one-point types exceeds the independent-obstruction baseline by a sharp multiplicative correction for every matching of pair-overlapping old triples. Equality is characterized exactly by disjoint overlapping pairs of triples, and the first nontrivial four-point example has \(50\) types instead of \(49\).
