For every shift \(a\ge2\), the two-prime \((a,a)\)-Carmichael integers with both primes above \(a\) have an exact two-parameter classification and a finite search bound. Their largest prime is at most \(4a-3\) for odd \(a\) and at most \((12a-5)/7\) for even \(a\); both parity-dependent bounds are attained.
