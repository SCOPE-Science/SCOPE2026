For a magnetic quantum ring subjected to \(m\) complete position measurements at equal intervals, clockwise-minus-counterclockwise arrival probability is exactly zero on every even cycle but is generically proportional to \(\sin\Phi/m^{n-1}\) on an odd \(n\)-cycle, with an explicit factorial coefficient. Total escape decays only as \(m^{-1}\), so circumference determines an exact hierarchy of how rapidly complete monitoring erases directional flux information.
