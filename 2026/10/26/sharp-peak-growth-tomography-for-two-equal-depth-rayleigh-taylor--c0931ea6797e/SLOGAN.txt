For two equally deep, inviscid Rayleigh–Taylor layers with known densities and gravity, measuring only the fastest-growing wavenumber and growth rate uniquely determines both the unknown depth and the interfacial tension. The exact feasible region is \(k_*>0\) and \(0<\Gamma_*^2/(Ak_*)<2/3\), where \(A=(\rho_h-\rho_l)g/(\rho_h+\rho_l)\); neither boundary is attained by a finite positive depth.
