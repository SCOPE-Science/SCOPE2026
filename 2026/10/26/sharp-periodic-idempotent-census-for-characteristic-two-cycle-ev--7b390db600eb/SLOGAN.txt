For the evolution algebra attached to an undirected cycle \(C_n\) over \(\mathbb F_{2^m}\), the exact number of idempotent elements is \(2^{\deg\gcd(X^n-1,(X^2+1)^m+X^m)}\), so it never exceeds \(2^{2m}\), independently of the cycle length. The bound is attained exactly on cycle lengths divisible by an explicitly defined least period \(N_m\); the first three extension degrees have periods \(3,6,15\), respectively.
