For Gaussian Berezin convolution on each finite exponential-polynomial sector, the resolvent has an exact sharp pole coefficient in the inherited physical \(L^2\) norm: \(|e^{\alpha\beta}|^m\sqrt{m!}(|\alpha|^2+|\beta|^2)^{m/2}\). A shifted complex-Hermite polynomial attains this coefficient, which determines the leading pseudospectral radius near resonant fixed points.
