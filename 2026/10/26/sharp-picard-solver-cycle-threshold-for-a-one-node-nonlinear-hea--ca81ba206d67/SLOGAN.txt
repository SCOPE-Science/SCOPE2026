For one-node quadratic-conductivity heat flow, a positive backward-Euler nonlinear solve has an exact Picard failure threshold \(\tau b^2/(2h^2)=4\): above it, the inner solver approaches an attracting two-cycle rather than the implicit solution. At the threshold the error is of order \(j^{-1/2}\), while fixed damping at most \(2/3\) guarantees local stability for every finite stiffness.
