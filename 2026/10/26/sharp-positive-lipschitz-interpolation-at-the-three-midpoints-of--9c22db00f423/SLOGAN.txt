Every obtuse or right isosceles Euclidean triangle admits a sharp positive simultaneous linear Lipschitz extension from its three vertices to all three edge midpoints, with optimal norm \(\lambda_h=(1+h^2)/(\sqrt{1+h^2}+h(\sqrt{1+h^2}-1))\). Ordinary affine midpoint averaging becomes arbitrarily ill-conditioned as the triangle flattens, while the optimal six-point extension norm converges to \(1\).
