For the optimal A-stable two-step stiff-solver family, a backward-Euler start preserves nonnegative scalar-decay iterates for all time exactly when \(z\le(1+\kappa)/[2(1-\kappa)]\) below \(\kappa=1/2\), or \(z\le(1-\kappa^2)/\kappa\) above it. The unique maximum positivity budget is \(z=3/2\) at \(\kappa=1/2\), even though every family member is A-stable.
