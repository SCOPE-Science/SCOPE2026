For the biharmonic heat semigroup \(\exp(-tL_n^2)\) on the triangular graph \(J(n,2)\), the only transiently negative kernel entries join disjoint pairs, so the exact endpoint amplification and positivity horizon follow from one two-exponential scalar equation. The unique amplification peak approaches \(3\cdot2^{-1/3}-1\), with an explicit nonzero \(1/n\) correction.
