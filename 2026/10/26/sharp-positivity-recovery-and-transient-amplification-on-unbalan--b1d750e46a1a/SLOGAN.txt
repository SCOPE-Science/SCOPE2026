For the squared-Laplacian heat semigroup on every unweighted \(K_{a,b}\), \(2\le a\le b\), the larger vertex part alone determines the sharp \(\ell^\infty\) amplification and eventual entrywise-positivity time. At fixed \(a\) and \(b\to\infty\), the two within-part sign-recovery scales separate as \(c_*/b^2\) versus \(1/(ab)\), while peak amplification is \(1+2a/b+o(1/b)\).
