The consistent-mass Crank–Nicolson step for a uniform one-dimensional Dirichlet heat mesh with \(n\ge3\) interior nodes is entrywise nonnegative exactly at zero step or when \(1/3\le\tau/h^2\le\rho_n\), with an explicit finite-size polynomial defining \(\rho_n\). These upper thresholds increase to the algebraic constant \(0.747597827128115\ldots\), beyond the usual elementwise-sign sufficient bound.
