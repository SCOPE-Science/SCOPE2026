The two-dimensional real norm \(N_t(x,y)=\max\{|x|,|y|,|x+ty|,|tx+y|\}\) has exact absolute projection constant \(\lambda=2(1+t)/(2+t)\) for all \(0\le t\le1\), with a unique coordinate-swap-equivariant minimizing projection when \(0<t<1\). This interpolation from the injective square to the extremal hexagon also forces the sharp first-order Banach–Mazur behavior \(d(X_t,\ell_\infty^2)=1+t/2+O(t^2)\), without asserting an exact interior distance.
