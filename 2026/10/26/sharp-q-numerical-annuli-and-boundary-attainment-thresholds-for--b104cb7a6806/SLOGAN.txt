For every conjugate-linear scaled isometry, the outer \(q\)-numerical radius has an exact two-branch plateau law; in complex dimension two, the complete annulus has an explicit inner radius whose hole closes precisely at \(|q|=\sqrt{1-(w(A)/\|A\|)^2}\). In higher dimensions the range is a disk, and its boundary-attainment transition is governed exactly by ordinary numerical-radius attainment.
