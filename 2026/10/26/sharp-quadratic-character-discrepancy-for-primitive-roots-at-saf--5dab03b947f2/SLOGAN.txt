For safe primes \(p\ge23\), every separable quadratic or eligible nonconstant linear polynomial takes both nonzero quadratic-character signs at primitive roots; the exact signed counts are governed by a cubic character sum. The primitive-root character imbalance obeys the sharp-at-\(23\)-and-\(47\) bound \(|S_p(Q)|\le\lfloor\sqrt p\rfloor+2\).
