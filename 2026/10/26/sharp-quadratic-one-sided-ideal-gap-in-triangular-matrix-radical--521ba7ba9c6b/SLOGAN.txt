For a rank-\(r\) radical element of the upper triangular matrix algebra, the difference between the dimensions of its principal right and left ideals attains every integer from \(-r(n-r-1)\) through \(r(n-r-1)\). The maximum absolute gap is \(\lfloor(n-1)^2/4\rfloor\); its extremizers are explicit torus-affine varieties with exact finite-field cardinalities.
