For the normalized random-transposition quantum shuffle on \(S_n\), the least projective revival is exactly \(2\pi\), \(6\pi\), or \(\pi n(n-1)\) for \(n=3\), \(n=4\), or \(n\ge5\), respectively. Coherence strictly decreases the measured squared \(\ell^2\)-distance to uniformity at every finite non-revival phase, with a sharp positive quadratic deficit near each revival; classical mixing is recovered exactly on the revival lattice.
