For the nonempty triangular graphs \(T_n=J(n,2)\), first-order quantifier rank \(q\) sees the ground-set size exactly below \(2q\) and merges all orders at least \(2q\), yielding precisely \(2q-1\) logical classes. Remarkably, existential rank-\(q\) sentences already capture every rank-\(q\) distinction within this family.
