For the minimal two-sided Schur mask retaining exactly one row and one column, the sharp \(\mathcal S_p\) multiplier constant for every \(0<p<1\) is a unique one-variable maximum attained by a positive rank-one projection, independently of matrix dimension. At \(p=1/2\), its maximizing coordinate mass is \((7-\sqrt{13})/6\), giving the exact quasi-norm amplification \(2.1327104114\ldots\).
