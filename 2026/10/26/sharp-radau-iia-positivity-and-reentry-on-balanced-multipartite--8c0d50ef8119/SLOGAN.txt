For two-stage Radau IIA diffusion on equal-part complete multipartite graphs with at least three parts of size at least two, unconditional entrywise positivity occurs for exactly four parameter pairs: \((3,2),(3,3),(4,2),(5,2)\). Every other pair has one exact bounded interval of negative diagonals, followed by positivity reentry, with boundaries given by the two positive roots of an explicit quartic.
