# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. Reversible self-adjointness supplies a nonnegative diagonal spectral measure with zero-mode weight exactly the stationary mass. The Radau scalar factor has a unique global minimum at the stated argument; the weighted average gives the sharp bound. The equality characterization follows directly from the centered coordinate eigenvector and detailed balance. Exact quadratic identities yield the sharp stationary-mass and eleven-vertex cutoffs. The standalone rational-matrix replay verifies representative independent constructions, but the general theorem is analytic.

## Originality
PASS, subject to residual literature risk. The available earlier same-method graph-family claims concern cycle/path off-diagonal failures, hypercube or rook-graph diagonal phases, and balanced multipartite graphs. These statements do not imply the universal, sharp stationary-mass diagonal lower envelope, its local equality characterization, or the all-graphs eleven-state cutoff. Gottlieb–Shu–Tadmor analyze implicit SSP restrictions and Blanes–Iserles–Macnamara analyze graph-Laplacian positivity-preserving designs; neither inspected paper states or implies the specific extremal reversible return-coefficient theorem without additional argument. Searches included equivalent stability-function notation and reset-chain formulations; unindexed specialized Markov-chain or numerical-analysis literature remains a risk.

## Value
PASS. The precise question is when a stable completed implicit step can assign a negative coefficient to *remaining at the initial state*. The result finds the optimal lower envelope from the stationary mass alone, classifies every equality generator at a specified state, and identifies the exact smallest order of an undirected unweighted graph on which diagonal positivity can fail (twelve). The universal-hub witness shows the phenomenon is insensitive to arbitrary other network connections. This gives a sharp diagnostic distinct from global entrywise positivity and from linear/spectral stability, not an arbitrary numerical example.

## Limitations
Reversibility is required; the result controls diagonals only, and does not claim overall entrywise nonnegativity or positivity of intermediate stages. Later audit and comparison with additional specialized literature may change the novelty assessment.
