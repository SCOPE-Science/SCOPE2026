For third-order Radau IIA on any reversible continuous-time Markov chain, the completed diagonal at state \(i\) is bounded sharply below by \(\pi_i+(1-\pi_i)(5-3\sqrt3)/2\), with explicit equality generators. Thus every unweighted undirected graph on at most eleven vertices has positive completed diagonals for every timestep, whereas any twelve-vertex cone graph already has a negative hub diagonal at timestep \(3/4\).
