A Bell-uniform random finite \(\mathsf{S5}\) algebra is generated by \(k\) independent random predicates with exact probability \(\frac{n!}{B_n2^{kn}}[z^n]\prod_{s=1}^{2^k}(1+z^s)^{\binom{2^k}s}\). Its sharp observation threshold is \(2^k\asymp n\log n\), with critical probability \(e^{-1/(2c)}\) at \(2^k\sim c n\log n\) and a genuine integer-rounding cluster interval \([e^{-1},e^{-1/2}]\).
