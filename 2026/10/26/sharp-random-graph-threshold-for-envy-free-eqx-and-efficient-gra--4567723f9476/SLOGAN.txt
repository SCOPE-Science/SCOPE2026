In sparse random simple-graph binary-goods markets, simultaneous exact envy-freeness, equitability up to any positively valued good, and fractional Pareto efficiency emerges sharply at edge probability \(p=\frac{2}{1-\log2}\frac{\log n}{n}\). Above this threshold, balanced edge orientations ensure all three properties with high probability; below it, agents of insufficient degree block every such allocation.
