If nonzero complex rectangular matrices have maximal trace-norm distance from one another, the sum of their ranks is at most twice the smaller matrix dimension; equality is attained by signed coordinate units. Maximal square-matrix families are exactly balanced orthonormal-basis constructions, and an \(m\)-leaf unit-edge star embeds isometrically in \(d\times d\) trace-norm matrices exactly when \(m\le2d\).
