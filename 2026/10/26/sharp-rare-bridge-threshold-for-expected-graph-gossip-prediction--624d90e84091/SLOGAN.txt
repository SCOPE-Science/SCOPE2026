In a connected path network with one rare random shortcut, replacing the random graph by its expected weighted graph can predict almost complete opinion mixing even though the actual long-time mean opinion profile remains polarized with probability tending to one. When the shortcut probability scales as \(p_L\sim c/L\) and the detour is much longer than the arms, the population-averaged absolute discrepancy converges exactly to \(c/[2(1+2c)]\), reaching \(1/4\) when \(Lp_L\to\infty\) while \(p_L\to0\).
