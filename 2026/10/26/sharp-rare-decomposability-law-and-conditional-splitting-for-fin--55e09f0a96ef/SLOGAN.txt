For fixed finite field \(\mathbb F_q\) and sink dimension \(r\ge3\), a random nonzero-arrow inward star-quiver representation is decomposable with sharp probability \(Dq^{r-1}((H+1)/D)^n(1+O_{q,r}((H/(H+1))^n))\), where \(D=(q^r-1)/(q-1)\) and \(H=(q^{r-1}-1)/(q-1)\). Conditioned on this rare event, it almost surely splits uniquely into line and hyperplane indecomposables, with asymptotically binomial line-leaf count.
