The sharp mean-zero periodic \(\ell^1\)-to-first-difference-\(\ell^2\) constant on an \(N\)-cycle equals \(\sqrt{m(N-m)[m(N-m)+2]/(3N)}\) for real scalars (\(m=\lfloor N/2\rfloor\)), but \(\sqrt N/[2\sin(\pi/N)]\) for complex scalars. Real extremizers come from contiguous half-cycle sign patterns under the Green operator, whereas complex extremizers are Fourier waves; the real-to-complex ratio tends to \(\pi/(2\sqrt3)\).
