For a two-row Gaussian Hankel matrix with \(m\ge2\) overlapping columns, the least-singular-value small-ball exponent is exactly \(\beta(m-1)\), with \(\beta=1\) over the reals and \(\beta=2\) over the complex numbers. Consequently its pseudoinverse norm and condition number have finite \(p\)-th moments exactly when \(p<\beta(m-1)\), despite almost-sure full row rank.
