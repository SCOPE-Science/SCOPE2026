For the Ledrappier three-dot shift, the \(n\)-square-periodic points realize exactly \(2^{\min(d_n,w+h-1)}\) of the \(2^{w+h-1}\) locally legal \(w\times h\) rectangles, where \(d_n=\deg\gcd(t^n-1,(1+t)^n-1)\). This gives a sharp universal extension threshold and an arithmetic classification of its maximal cases, attained precisely at powers of two times Mersenne numbers.
