For Brownian resetting to finitely many sites on a circle, the more probable side of arrival at a single absorbing target can reverse at most \(m-1\) times as the reset rate increases, where \(m\) counts noncancelling antipodal reset-distance pairs; every bound is sharp. Three explicit resetting sites already realize two exact successive direction reversals at \(r=D\operatorname{arcosh}(2)^2/d^2\) and \(r=D\operatorname{arcosh}(3)^2/d^2\).
