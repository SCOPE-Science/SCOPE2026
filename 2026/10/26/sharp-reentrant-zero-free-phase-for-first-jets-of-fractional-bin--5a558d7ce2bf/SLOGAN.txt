For the fractional binomial analytic kernel \(K_\beta(z,w)=(1-z\overline w)^{-\beta}\) with \(0<\beta<1\), the Gram kernel of value plus first-derivative evaluation is zero-free precisely when the derivative-to-value coefficient ratio has modulus at least \(2/\sqrt{1-\beta}\), whereas both pure evaluations are always zero-free. At the Hardy endpoint \(\beta=1\), every finite nontrivial mixture instead has zeros: the reentrant zero-free phase disappears.
