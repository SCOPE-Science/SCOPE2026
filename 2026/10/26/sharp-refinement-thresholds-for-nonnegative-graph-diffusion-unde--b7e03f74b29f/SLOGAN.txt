For two-level Richardson extrapolation of backward Euler on every finite positively weighted graph, three refined substeps are the first that guarantee nonnegative diffusion transitions for all sufficiently small steps on diameter-three graphs, and eighteen are the first that guarantee them on diameter-four graphs. These are sharp: longer graph distances create negative leading transition coefficients, and the standard two-substep extrapolate can fail even on a diameter-three unweighted graph.
