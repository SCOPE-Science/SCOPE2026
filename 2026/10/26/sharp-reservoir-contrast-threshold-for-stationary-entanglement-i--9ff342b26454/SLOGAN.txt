For two exchange-coupled qubits with equal total local gain–loss rates, stationary entanglement can be generated at some coupling if and only if the squared reservoir-occupation contrast satisfies \(d^2>q^2(1-q)^2/[1-2q(1-q)]\). At complementary occupations \(p_1+p_2=1\), this becomes the sharp bias condition \(|p_1-p_2|>1/\sqrt2\), and the entire entangled coupling window has exactly square-root width.
