For the classical explicit fourth-order Runge–Kutta heat step on a star with arbitrary positive spoke conductances, the completed transition matrix is nonnegative exactly through the unique positive root of an explicit cubic determined by the **heaviest spoke**. Every diagonal and leaf-to-leaf entry stays positive, all lighter hub-to-leaf entries fail later, and the sharp positivity horizon strictly exceeds the forward-Euler limit \(1/\sum_i w_i\).
