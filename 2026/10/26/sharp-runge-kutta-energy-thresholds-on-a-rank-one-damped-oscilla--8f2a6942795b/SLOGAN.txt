On the canonical two-coordinate hypocoercive oscillator, third-order explicit Runge–Kutta preserves Euclidean energy exactly through the sharp step \(h=2.375701168186\ldots\), but classical fourth-order Runge–Kutta loses that property immediately above \(h=2\). At \(h=9/4\) the fourth-order update is spectrally stable yet amplifies some vectors, while the third-order update contracts all nonzero vectors.
