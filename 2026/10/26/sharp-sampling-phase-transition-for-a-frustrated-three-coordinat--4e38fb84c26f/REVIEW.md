The exact coordinate update reduces the conditional energy decrement to the smallest eigenvalue of \(C^{1/2}PC^{1/2}\). Concavity under coordinate-probability symmetrization gives an unrestricted global reduction to \((u,u,1-2u)\). The two invariant spectral blocks yield a linear antisymmetric branch and a strictly concave symmetric lower-eigenvalue branch. The characteristic-determinant sign controls their unique crossing; its position relative to the symmetric branch's unique maximum changes at the stated quadratic critical curve. Endpoint behavior, positive definiteness, and equality at the boundary were separately reconstructed. Exact-rational spectra and independently optimized numerical cases support the analytic argument.

## Originality — PASS (best knowledge; residual risk recorded)

The closest mathematical result is the indexed theorem on exact optimal coordinate sampling for inverse-positive SPD quadratics. Full-text comparison confirms that its decisive hypothesis fails here: all three off-diagonal entries of \(C^{-1}\) are negative and their sign product forbids sign-gauge repair. The full arXiv treatments by Richtárik–Takáč and Gower–Richtárik give, respectively, optimized complexity surrogates and a general SDP but neither the proposed exact two-phase analytic evaluation nor its active-mode interpretation. The inspected 1991 Pukelsheim–Torsney full paper supplies general independent-support optimal-design characterizations rather than this explicit frustrated-triangle classification. Generic SDP reducibility is acknowledged, not misreported as absence of prior coverage. Search and review cannot prove absolute novelty, especially across older E-optimal design equivalences.

## Mathematical value — PASS

Nonuniform iid coordinate sampling is a concrete optimization design choice, and simple inverse-positive formulae fail for the first frustrated three-coordinate interaction. The exact two-parameter family has equal coordinate Lipschitz constants, yet the optimal allocation is typically unequal and undergoes a nontrivial eigenmode-activation transition. The closed form replaces a numerical SDP for this natural minimal signed-inverse class, and explains when two minimax modes compete. This is a motivated structural classification rather than an arbitrary rational instance or restatement of the SDP.
