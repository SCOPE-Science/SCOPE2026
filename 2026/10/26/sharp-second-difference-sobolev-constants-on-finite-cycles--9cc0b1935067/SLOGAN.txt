The square of the sharp real mean-zero cyclic \(\ell^1\)-to-second-difference \(\ell^2\) Sobolev constant has an explicit polynomial formula in the balanced block parameter, whereas the complex constant is \(\sqrt N/[4\sin^2(\pi/N)]\). The two constants coincide only on the four-cycle; their real-to-complex ratio tends to \(\pi^2/\sqrt{120}\).
