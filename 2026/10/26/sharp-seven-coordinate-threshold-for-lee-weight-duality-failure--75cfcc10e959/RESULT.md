# Sharp seven-coordinate threshold for Lee-weight duality failure in quinary two-dimensional codes

## Finding

Write \(\mathbb F_5=\{0,1,2,3,4}\) and use the Lee weight \(w(a)=\min(a,5-a)\) for these representatives. The Lee enumerator of a linear code \(C\le\mathbb F_5^n\) is \(L_C(z)=\sum_{c\in C} z^{\sum_i w(c_i)}\). The sharp smallest length at which two **two-dimensional** quinary linear codes can have equal Lee enumerators but unequal Lee enumerators of their Euclidean duals is **seven**. In particular, for every \(2\le n\le6\) and every pair of \([n,2]_5\) codes \(C,D\),
\[
L_C(z)=L_D(z)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad L_{C^\perp}(z)=L_{D^\perp}(z).
\]
At \(n=7\), this implication fails. This is a sharp **dimension-two** threshold and makes no assertion about the least failure length when larger code dimensions are allowed.

## Assumptions and scope

All algebra is over the prime field \(\mathbb F_5\). A \([n,2]_5\) code means a two-dimensional linear subspace of \(\mathbb F_5^n\); zero columns and repeated columns are allowed. The dual is the ordinary Euclidean orthogonal subspace. Coordinates are labeled, though coordinate permutations and sign changes preserve both enumerators in question. The exact cutoff covers all such codes and all lengths \(2\) through \(6\), not a chosen code family. The length-seven witness is generated by
\[
G_A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1&2&2&2&2&2\\4&4&0&1&2&4&4\end{pmatrix},\qquad
G_B=\begin{pmatrix}1&1&1&2&2&2&2\\3&4&4&0&0&2&4\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Each matrix has row rank two.

## Proof

**Exhaustive normalization lemma.** Since \(w(-a)=w(a)\), multiplying a coordinate by \(-1\) preserves Lee weight and carries the Euclidean dual to the correspondingly rescaled dual; coordinate permutations do likewise. The \(25\) column vectors in \(\mathbb F_5^2\), modulo the involution \(g\mapsto-g\), give exactly \(13\) column types, including zero. Each full-row-rank two-row generator is therefore represented, for purposes of this implication, by a multiset of \(n\) such types. Conversely every multiset whose nonzero columns span \(\mathbb F_5^2\) represents a valid code. A nonzero one-dimensional subspace has exactly two nonzero sign types, and there are six such subspaces. Inclusion–exclusion therefore gives exactly
\[
\binom{n+12}{12}-6\binom{n+2}{2}+5
\]
rank-two normalized multisets at length \(n\). The counts for lengths \(2,3,4,5,6\) are respectively \(60,400,1735,6067,18401\).

**Exact primal and dual evaluations.** For a normalized generator with column multiset \(g_1,\ldots,g_n\), evaluate the \(25\) distinct codewords \(uG\) for \(u\in\mathbb F_5^2\) to obtain \(L_C\). For its dual, let \(\zeta\) be a complex primitive fifth root of unity and put \(\alpha=\zeta+\zeta^{-1}\). Then \(\alpha^2=1-\alpha\) and \(\beta=\zeta^2+\zeta^{-2}=-1-\alpha\). Character orthogonality proves the identity
\[
L_{C^\perp}(z)=\frac1{25}\sum_{u\in\mathbb F_5^2}\prod_{j=1}^n F_{u\cdot g_j}(z),
\]
where \(F_0(z)=1+2z+2z^2\), \(F_{\pm1}(z)=1+\alpha z+\beta z^2\), and \(F_{\pm2}(z)=1+\beta z+\alpha z^2\). Indeed, expanding the product weights each candidate dual vector \(x\in\mathbb F_5^n\) by the character average \(25^{-1}\sum_u\zeta^{u\cdot Gx^{\mathsf T}}\), which is one precisely when \(Gx^{\mathsf T}=0\) and zero otherwise. All calculations can be carried out exactly using the pair representation \(a+b\alpha\in\mathbb Z[\alpha]\) with \(\alpha^2=1-\alpha\).

The complete enumerator in `lee_exact_census.cpp` visits **every** rank-two normalized multiset at each length \(2\) through \(6\), evaluates both enumerators by these exact formulas, and groups the resulting dual enumerators by the entire primal enumerator. For every primal enumerator at each such length, the grouped dual enumerator is unique. The numbers of distinct primal enumerators are \(1,5,18,49,129\). This constitutes a finite, complete, reproducible proof of the lower-bound side; no untested lengths below seven remain. No group-action orbit elimination is assumed, so overcounting equivalent codes cannot lose a possible counterexample.

**Length-seven witness.** Directly evaluating the \(25\) linear combinations of either displayed matrix gives
\[
L_{C_A}(z)=L_{C_B}(z)=1+2z^6+6z^7+4z^8+4z^9+2z^{10}+4z^{11}+2z^{12}.
\]
Direct enumeration of both dual codes (each of cardinality \(5^5=3125\)) gives Lee-weight-four coefficients \([z^4]L_{C_A^\perp}=56\) and \([z^4]L_{C_B^\perp}=60\). Hence their dual Lee enumerators differ. This proves the attained upper bound and the sharp cutoff.

## Verification

Compile and run `lee_exact_census.cpp` using a C++17 compiler, for example `g++ -O3 -std=c++17 lee_exact_census.cpp -o lee_census && ./lee_census`. It asserts exact vanishing of the irrational coefficient and exact divisibility by \(25\) in every dual coefficient. All normalized rank-two multisets through length six are checked, and the first differing witness is printed at length seven. The unoptimized mathematical enumeration formula above independently predicts the normalized multiset counts.

The independent standalone script `lee_direct_witness.py` uses no character sums and instead enumerates all \(5^7\) received vectors directly, retaining those orthogonal to the displayed generators. It independently returns the full dual distributions and checks the difference at Lee weight four. Recorded exact outputs are in `checks.txt`.

## Relationship to prior work

The motivating 2026 paper of Wood proves broad failures of duality for non-Hamming weights on finite fields, constructs dimension-two examples, and develops the column-multiplicity framework. Its explicit example for the Euclidean weight on \(\mathbb F_5\) has lengths \(18\) and \(30\). The present result asks a distinct extremal question: the earliest length of a Lee-weight-only duality failure *among dimension-two quinary codes*. The general existence theorem does not itself supply the exhaustive short-length exclusion or this seven-coordinate Lee witness.

Abdelghany and Wood (2020) proved nonexistence of ordinary Lee MacWilliams identities over residue rings of modulus at least five; that theorem already establishes some failure of Lee duality. The complete 2020 article could not be inspected for any particular short example, so implicit earlier coverage of this exact threshold is a residual priority risk. The symmetrized-enumerator duality described by Bariffi–Cavicchioni–Weger (2024) is consistent with, and does not negate, the failure of the coarser single-variable enumerator.

## Limitations

This is a finite sharp classification restricted to dimension two over \(\mathbb F_5\), not a universal minimum over all code dimensions, a theorem over arbitrary alphabets, or a new MacWilliams transform. The exclusion below seven depends on a complete exact enumeration whose short source and normalization argument are included; the length-seven witness is additionally independently checked without Fourier techniques. The cited 2020 paper's inaccessible full text leaves an explicit bibliographic originality risk. There is no independent scholarly audit of priority or proof.
