The first three Fibonacci-cube dimensions beyond the published \(n\le5\) result have exact edge-general-position numbers \(16,26,42\), respectively. Explicit families of \(8,13,21\) shortest paths cover every edge and are optimally small, so \(c_{\mathrm{geo}}(\Gamma_n)=F_n\) and \(\operatorname{gp}_{\mathrm e}(\Gamma_n)=2F_n\) for \(n\in\{6,7,8\}\), without asserting the full conjecture.
