For the consecutive-pair voter-interval election with \(2k\) voters and \(2k-1\) candidates, every single-swap local PAV maximum achieves at least \(2k-\tfrac12\lfloor k/3\rfloor\), and this bound is attained for every \(k\). The exact worst-case ratio to global PAV is \(1-\lfloor k/3\rfloor/(4k)\), with sharp uniform value \(11/12\).
