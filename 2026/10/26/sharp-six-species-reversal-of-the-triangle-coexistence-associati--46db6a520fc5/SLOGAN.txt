A single degree-preserving edge switch on six competing species creates a triangle and raises clustering, yet lowers the exact stable-coexistence threshold from the root of \(1-2\tau-\tau^2+\tau^3=0\) in \((11/25,9/20)\) to \((\sqrt{17}-1)/8\). Six species is the sharp minimum for this connected-graph reversal: no pair with at most five vertices can exhibit it.
