For a binary word of length \(n\ge6\), one cyclic rotation of any contiguous five-bit window has at most \(3n-9+\lfloor(n-4)/6\rfloor\) distinct possible outcomes including the unchanged word, and the bound is attained by prefixes of \((001101)^\infty\). The sharp maximum grows by exactly \(19\) whenever the length increases by \(6\); at length \(5\), the maximum is \(5\).
