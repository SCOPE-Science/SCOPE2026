For Fourier polynomials supported on the antipodal coordinate characters \(\{\pm e_j\}_{j=1}^d\), the sharp normalized sixth moment is \(15-\frac{45}{2d}+\frac{10}{d^2}\), with balanced coefficients and aligned antipodal products as the only equality cases. An exact positive three-part deficit identity separates coordinate-energy imbalance, within-pair imbalance, and relative phase mismatch; the same theorem persists on finite product tori of cyclic orders at least seven, and fails at order six.
