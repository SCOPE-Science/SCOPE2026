For analytic three-term Fourier polynomials, the sharp ratio between the largest and smallest sixth moments obtainable by varying phases at fixed coefficient magnitudes is \(3.7977375224747556\ldots\). It is uniquely maximized, up to scale and endpoint exchange, by equal endpoint magnitudes and squared middle-to-endpoint magnitude ratio equal to the positive root of \(t^4+6t^3-40t-60=0\); aligned and opposed quadratic phase relations realize the two endpoints.
