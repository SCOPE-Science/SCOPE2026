# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
Haar character orthogonality converts the variance exactly into a sum of squared nonconstant Fourier-character multiplicities. The balanced two-pair and triple-triple configurations contribute respectively \(216n^5+O(n^4)\) and \((99/5)n^5+O(n^4)\); all remaining support patterns are bounded by \(O(n^4)\). The threefold convolution integral is exactly \(11/20\). The independent finite checker verifies multiplicities for sizes 4 through 16. The proof, not finite testing, establishes the asymptotic law.

## Value — PASS
The result supplies the sharp first-order fluctuation scale of a natural higher even norm of random unimodular polynomials. It distinguishes the four-index phase-relation contribution \(216\) from the six-index relation contribution \(99/5\), showing the latter has a non-negligible effect that fourth-moment analysis cannot capture. It supplies an exact benchmark for stochastic Fourier flatness without requiring a central limit theorem.
