On the two-character subspace spanned by the fundamental and adjoint trace characters of \(SU(3)\), the sharp sixth-moment constant is the previously known pure-adjoint moment \(145\), with no mixed maximizers. The optimal global stability coefficient is the new algebraic constant \(72.227917510590\ldots\), strictly smaller than the local coefficient \(75\), and its nontrivial equality directions have a threefold relative-phase rigidity.
