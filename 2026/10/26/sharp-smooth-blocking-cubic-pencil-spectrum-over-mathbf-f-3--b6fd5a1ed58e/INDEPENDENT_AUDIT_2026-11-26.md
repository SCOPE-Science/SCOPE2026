# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

With no rational base point, exactly one of the four members vanishes at each of the 13 rational points. A smooth blocking cubic cannot contain a rational line's four points and therefore has at least six points by the nontrivial prime-plane blocking bound; each smooth cubic has at least one point by Hasse–Weil. Thus two blocking members would require at least 14 points. An independent exact evaluation of all 29,524 normalized cubic equations found 4,940 blocking equations, including 936 smooth ones with six points each (234 supports), and matched their projective evaluation signatures exactly with the orbit of the stated F. Projective evaluation signatures are injective on cubic classes because a cubic vanishing at all 13 points would have all 13 line factors. Smoothness was tested over F₃, F₉ and F₂₇: an absolutely irreducible singular cubic has just one singular point, fixed by Frobenius; a line plus irreducible conic has an intersection point of degree at most two; three distinct lines give at most three singular intersections permuted by Frobenius; and a multiple line is unique and rational. Hence the finite test is exhaustive for this degree. Fixing G(P₀)=1 and its X³ coefficient to zero gives exactly one generator per eligible pencil through F. Of 6,561 candidates, 864 have no rational base point, 576 also have G smooth, and 312 have all four members smooth. Their local profile counts are (1,1,5):24, (1,2,4):132, (1,3,3):42, (2,2,3):114. The complete accepted coefficient set matches the frozen reference list, including the explicit witness with counts 6,4,1,2. Transitivity and uniqueness of the blocking member permit multiplication by 936 without double counting, which gives all the displayed global multiplicities.
