For the original SAV backward-Euler step, \(m\) active positive spectral modes permit at most \(\lfloor m/2\rfloor+1\) separate stepsize intervals where the auxiliary scalar stays positive, and this upper bound is attained for arbitrarily prescribed sign-change thresholds even with globally positive strictly convex quadratic energies. Two modes already permit positivity to disappear and then return as the stepsize increases.
