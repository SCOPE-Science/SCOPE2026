At the maximal admissible collective-jump normalization in a Dicke-state open quantum walk, the exact position spectral gap has the sharp two-term law \(\Delta_N=\pi^4/[3(N+1)H_N^3]-\pi^8/[90(N+1)H_N^5]+O(1/(NH_N^6))\). The actual relaxation scale exceeds the mean cycle time by the diverging factor \(6H_N^2/\pi^4\), while the largest jump probability is of order one (and equals one for odd \(N\)).
