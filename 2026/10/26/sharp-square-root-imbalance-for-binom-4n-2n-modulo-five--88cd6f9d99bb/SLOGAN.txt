In the exceptional three-residue family \(\binom{4n}{2n}\pmod5\), the exact excess of residue \(1\) over residue \(4\) among \(0\le n<5^L\) is \(\operatorname{Re}((1+i)(2+i)^L)\), while exactly \(3^L\) values are nonzero. For every cutoff the imbalance is \(O(\sqrt X)\), and the square-root exponent and normalized oscillation amplitude \(\sqrt2\) are sharp.
