For two-row Kaczmarz with \(K\) simultaneous stale-snapshot corrections, mixing uniform and greedy row selection yields a sharp expected-error safety limit \(\beta<4/[K+1+(K-1)\sqrt{\theta^2+(1-\theta^2)c^2}]\). With two unscaled workers at full relaxation, pure greed generically preserves the error norm forever by reflection, but any positive uniform-exploration probability restores geometric mean-square convergence.
