In postselected bidirectional spin-star transport, individual hub–leaf and leaf–leaf concurrences both decay like \(m^{-2}\), but their exact coefficients are different. Their asymptotic ordering reverses at the sharp coherent-to-incoherent coupling threshold \(2J=\gamma(1-\eta)^2\), while the hub's stationary probability is asymptotic to \((1-\eta)/m\).
