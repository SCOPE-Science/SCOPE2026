For the critical backward shift on \(\ell^p(i^{-1})\), the sharp \(q\)-orbit Cesàro constant for every fixed \(0<q<p\) grows like \((1-q/p)^{(p-q)/(pq)}(\log N)^{1/p}\). Diffuse coordinate-energy profiles attain the leading coefficient, which is strictly below the single-coordinate \(p\)-moment coefficient.
