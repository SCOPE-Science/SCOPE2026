For the Rudin–Shapiro word, the number of universally present binary scattered-subsequence symbols in its length-\(N\) prefix is \(5N/16\) up to an error of at most the binary digit sum \(s_2(N)\). At all lengths \(N=2^k\) with \(k\ge4\), the index is exactly \(5\cdot2^{k-4}-1\) for even \(k\) and \(5\cdot2^{k-4}\) for odd \(k\).
