For \(0<p\le2/3\), the exact quasi-triangle constant of the \(p\)-Bures expression on all complex pure-state projections is \(K_p=[2\sqrt{1-2^{-p/2}}]^{-1}\), attained by two orthogonal endpoint rays and their equal-angle midpoint. The exponent \(2/3\) is the sharp convexity boundary for the associated angular profile, and \(K_p\sim(2p\log2)^{-1/2}\) as \(p\downarrow0\).
