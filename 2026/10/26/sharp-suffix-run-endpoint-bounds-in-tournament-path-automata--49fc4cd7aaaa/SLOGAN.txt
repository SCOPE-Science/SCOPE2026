For any tournament on \(n\ge2\) vertices, a binary forward/reverse walk word avoiding \(n\) identical consecutive directions leaves at least \(n-r\) possible endpoints, where \(r\) is the final constant-run length; the bound is sharp. Consequently, every simple graph with clique number and chromatic number both equal to \(n\) has exactly the run-avoiding remanent language, with rational word-count series and \(2n\) minimal deterministic states.
