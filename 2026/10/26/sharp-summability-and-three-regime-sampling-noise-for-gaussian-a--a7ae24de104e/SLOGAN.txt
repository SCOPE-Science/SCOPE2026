For Gaussian additive ANOVA quasi-interpolation, dimension-uniform \(\rho^{-1}/M\) sampling variance holds exactly when the fourth roots of the coordinate weights are summable. Power weights \(\gamma_j=j^{-p}\) exhibit a sharp \(p=4\) transition: \(\rho^{-1}\) above, \(\rho^{-1}\log(1/\rho)\) at, and \(\rho^{-4/p}\) below, with explicit leading constants even for a constant target function.
