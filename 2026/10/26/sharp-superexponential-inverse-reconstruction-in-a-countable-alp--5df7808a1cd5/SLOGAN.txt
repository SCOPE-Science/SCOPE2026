A bijective countable-alphabet cellular automaton may have no finite-memory inverse, yet for iid geometric ranks and fair bits its optimal radius-\(R\) inverse reconstruction error is exactly \(\frac{(1-q)^{R+2}q^{(R+1)(R+2)/2}}{2(1-q^{R+2})}\). This best-possible same-input error is positive at every finite radius but vanishes superexponentially, sharply separating worst-case inverse nonlocality from typical reconstruction cost.
