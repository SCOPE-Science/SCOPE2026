## Mathematical value — PASS

Stationary kurtosis is a natural laboratory diagnostic for how switching optical-trap stiffness alters Gaussian equilibrium fluctuations, and a switching-rate response is an experimentally relevant question. The exact positive threshold identifies when increasing switching **enhances** normalized non-Gaussianity even as both unnormalized moments decrease, and gives a unique optimum in the entire strong-contrast region rather than numerical exemplars. The result delineates two qualitatively different regimes of a well-motivated minimal model. It requires no claimed larger downstream theorem.
