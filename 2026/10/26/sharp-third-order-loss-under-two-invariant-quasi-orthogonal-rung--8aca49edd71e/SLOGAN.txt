For two independent nonzero-frequency oscillators, a three-stage RK3 method corrected to conserve each modal energy in its stage-derivative space unavoidably incurs a sharp \(\mathcal C h^3\) correction and, on its near-predictor quasi-orthogonal branch, an exact global \(T\mathcal C h^2\) error. The three-stage/two-invariant dimension count holds, but the projected-gradient rank degenerates in the small-step limit.
