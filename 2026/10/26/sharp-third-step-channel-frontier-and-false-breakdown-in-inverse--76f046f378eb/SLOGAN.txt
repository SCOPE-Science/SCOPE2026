At the third inverse-leading extended Krylov step, the mixed/split orthogonalization indicator ratio has the exact frontier \(\gamma_3^2-1=(2\alpha m_1-1)/(1-2\alpha m_1+\alpha^2m_2)\), even without symmetry. A symmetric indefinite matrix with \(\kappa_2(A)=5/2\) makes the mixed recurrence break down while the split recurrence still extends the space, with arbitrarily severe nearby relative conditioning.
