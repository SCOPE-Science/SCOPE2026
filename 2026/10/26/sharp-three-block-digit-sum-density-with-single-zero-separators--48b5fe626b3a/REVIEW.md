# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The statement quantifies over all positive integer one-block lengths with both zero gaps exactly one. The proof derives the carry generating function, proves the strict finite-degree cutoff, explicitly evaluates the maximal carry coefficient, and multiplies the two-state transfer matrices. The algebra yields the claimed three-variable formula. Each partial derivative in the inverse-square block variables is positive throughout the admissible cube, proving strict monotonicity and the infimum. The checker certifies the universal rational matrix identity by exact multiaffine interpolation, and separately tests the interpretation by enumerating finite low-bit residues with the remaining infinite carry tail summed geometrically. There is no unproved extrapolation from finite experiments.

## Originality — PASS with residual risk
Drmota–Kauers–Spiegelhofer (2015 first public version, arXiv:1509.08623) introduced the density and its recurrences, not this finite three-parameter sharp inequality. Sobolewski–Spiegelhofer (2024, arXiv:2411.07779) gave the full two-block sharp bound and in Conjecture 13 proposed the all-block extremal lower bound. Their Proposition 16 computes the infinite-three-block candidate value \(115/216\); that constant alone is not claimed new. Section 6.1 does not establish the three-block finite-word monotonicity and notes that monotonicity fails for some longer block patterns. Cheng (2026, arXiv:2606.23398) proves universal positivity with a Hamming-weight-dependent lower bound, which is weaker than the present uniform three-block constant and does not give the exact finite-word polynomial or monotonicity. The precise finite-parameter theorem is not an explicit statement or logical consequence of these previous stated inequalities; the general transfer formula in the 2024 paper permits, but does not itself accomplish, the new sharp inequality. Searches against published-finding corpus and targeted literature did not locate an equivalent; this is not a proof of uniqueness. An unlocated specialized result remains a risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The shortest zero separators are precisely the canonical proposed extremal shape in Conjecture 13; arbitrary selection of block sizes is not involved. Resolving the proposed sharp inequality for all three positive one-block lengths on this whole canonical slice is a motivated partial resolution of an established structural prediction, not a mere isolated decimal count. Strict monotonicity is additional structural information and is nontrivial because the published work gives counterexamples to block-length monotonicity in larger families. The exact formula quantifies the approach to \(115/216\) in all three directions. The example \(567/1024<5/9\) shows how the previous sharp two-block barrier breaks on introducing a third one-block.

## Limitations and closest literature
Only two zero gaps of length one are covered; full three-block Conjecture 13 remains open as a statement about arbitrary zero-gap lengths (even though universal positivity was proved in 2026). The exact number \(115/216\) and general carry-transfer framework are prior art. An equivalent special-case formula in unlocated literature is a residual originality risk. Independent audit has not been conducted.
