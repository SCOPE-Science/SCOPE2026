For three exponentials with fixed outer frequencies \(\pm\varepsilon\), the optimally stable middle frequency is uniquely at zero whenever \(\varepsilon\) is sufficiently small, with sharp lower Gram eigenvalue \(2\varepsilon^4/135+2\varepsilon^6/1701+O(\varepsilon^8)\). Perfect conditioning occurs exactly at \(\varepsilon=m\pi/2\) for integers \(m\ge2\), and all optimal middle locations are classified; odd resonances force nonuniform frequency gaps.
