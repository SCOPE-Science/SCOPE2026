# Sharp three-rational-branch ceiling for tame quartic covers over \(\mathbf F_3\)

## Finding
Let \(k=\mathbf F_3\). For every finite separable everywhere-tamely-ramified morphism \(f:\mathbf P^1_k\to\mathbf P^1_k\) of degree four, let \(b_k(f)\) be the number of \(k\)-rational points in its **geometric** branch locus. Then
\[
 b_k(f)\leq3.
\]
Exactly \(864\) morphisms attain equality, with fixed source and target homogeneous coordinates. Under independent \(\operatorname{PGL}_2(k)\) transformations of source and target, they form exactly five orbits, of sizes \(288,288,72,24,192\). The first two have geometric monodromy \(D_4\), the next two have \(V_4\), and the last has \(S_4\). In particular no everywhere-tame degree-four map can be branched at all four \(k\)-rational target points.

For homogeneous coordinates \([X:Z]\), representatives \(f=[A(X,Z):B(X,Z)]\) and their types are:

| Type | \(A\) | \(B\) | Orbit | Stabilizer |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Dihedral I | \(Z^4\) | \(X^4+X^2Z^2\) | 288 | 2 |
| Dihedral II | \(Z^4\) | \(X^4+2X^2Z^2\) | 288 | 2 |
| Klein I | \(X^2Z^2\) | \(X^4+Z^4\) | 72 | 8 |
| Klein II | \(X^3Z+2XZ^3\) | \(X^4+2X^2Z^2+Z^4\) | 24 | 24 |
| Symmetric | \(X^2Z^2+Z^4\) | \(X^4+X^3Z+Z^4\) | 192 | 3 |

The two dihedral types have three rational branch values with fiber partitions \((4),(2,2),(2,1,1)\); the two Klein types have three rational branch values all with partition \((2,2)\). These four orbits, totaling \(672\) maps, have no other geometric branch values. The symmetric type has three rational simple branch values and three additional branch values forming one degree-three Frobenius orbit; all six geometric branch values are simple.

## Assumptions and scope
A degree-four map means a pair \((A,B)\) of nonzero homogeneous quartic forms over \(k\), without a common geometric projective zero, modulo one common nonzero scalar. The ramification index at every geometric source point must be prime to three. The branch locus is taken over an algebraic closure, but \(b_k\) counts only target points defined over \(k\). Maps are counted in fixed coordinates before quotienting by independent source and target fractional linear transformations. The action group has order \(24^2=576\).

## Proof
1. Every quartic form is encoded by its five coefficients of \(X^{4-i}Z^i\), for \(0\leq i\leq4\). Normalize a pair by making the first nonzero coefficient among all ten entries equal to one. A projective pair has no common zero precisely when the two forms have disjoint irreducible projective factor supports, including the root at infinity. Exhaustive enumeration gives \(17{,}496\) normalized, basepoint-free degree-four pairs. This agrees with the general count \(3^7(3^2-1)=17{,}496\).
2. For each \(t\in\mathbf P^1(k)=\{0,1,2,\infty\}\), calculate the geometric partition of the degree-four fiber \(A-tB\) (with fiber \(B\) over \(\infty\)). A rational target value is branched precisely when the partition is not \((1,1,1,1)\). In a tame degree-four map, every fiber partition must be one of \((1,1,1,1)\), \((2,1,1)\), \((2,2)\), or \((4)\): index three would be wild. The tame Riemann–Hurwitz formula forces the sum of \(\sum_P(e_P-1)\) over these four fibers to be at most \(2\cdot4-2=6\).
3. Exact enumeration of the normalized pairs satisfying those necessary conditions gives **zero** with all four rational target values branched. With exactly three rational branch values, the only candidates are \(576\) with rational ramification partitions \((4),(2,2),(2,1,1)\), \(96\) with \((2,2),(2,2),(2,2)\), and \(192\) with three \((2,1,1)\) fibers. In the first two cases the contributions already sum to six, proving tameness and absence of further ramification. In the last case, the Wronskian \(A_XB_Z-A_ZB_X\), the homogeneous Jacobian sextic, has three distinct linear factors over \(k\) and one irreducible cubic factor, each with exponent one. Consequently all six geometric ramification indices equal two, proving tameness and giving three rational plus one conjugate triple of nonrational branch values. Thus all \(864\) candidates are precisely the equality maps.
4. An independent \(\operatorname{PGL}_2(k)^2\)-orbit calculation generates the orbits of the five displayed pairs by exact polynomial substitution and target matrix multiplication. Their sizes are \(288,288,72,24,192\); they are disjoint and their union equals the complete set of \(864\) maps. Orbit–stabilizer gives the stated stabilizers.
5. For a three-point tame cover, the inertia cycles generate the geometric monodromy and multiply to the identity. The profiles \((4),(2,2),(2,1,1)\) generate \(D_4\), and three \((2,2)\) cycles generate \(V_4\). In the last orbit all six geometric branch values are distinct and have a single simple ramification point, so the monodromy is a transitive subgroup of \(S_4\) generated by transpositions, necessarily \(S_4\).

## Verification
The package includes independent exact finite-field enumerators `verify_fibers.py` (standard library) and `verify_symbolic.py` (SymPy). Each enumerates normalized quartic pairs and independently reports \(17{,}496\) degree-four maps, \(864\) equality maps, and zero everywhere-tame maps with four rational branch values. Both report the refined counts \(576,96,192\). The separate standard-library script `verify_orbits.py` exhausts all \(24^2\) two-sided transformations of each representative, verifies five disjoint orbits, and checks their exact union against the enumerated equality set. Recorded outputs and replay instructions appear in `checks.txt` and `VERIFICATION.md`.

## Relationship to prior work
Kedlaya–Litt–Witaszek (first arXiv version 2020-10-02), *Tamely ramified morphisms of curves and Belyi's theorem in positive characteristic*, proves broad existence of tame maps and tame Belyi maps over finite fields, but does not state this small-field sharp rational-branch ceiling or the five-orbit equality classification. Ding–Zieve (first arXiv version 2020-10-29), *Low-degree permutation rational functions over finite fields*, classifies quartic maps that permute the rational projective line; its Theorem 1.4 and Table 1 already include the **192-element permutation-map orbit** represented here as the symmetric type, so that orbit is **not** independently claimed as new. Their Lemma 5.1 also explains the geometric \(V_4\) template. Classical power, dihedral, and Klein covers account for the geometric templates of the other orbits. The present statement is the combined arithmetic sharp bound and exhaustive classification of all its attaining tame covers, including nonpermutation maps and their rational twists. Bary-Soroker–Entin–Fehm's 2021 minimal-ramification work concerns Galois extensions and minimal ramified places, rather than this degree-four rational-target branch-incidence census.

## Limitations
The calculation is specific to degree four and \(\mathbf F_3\); it does not classify all tame degree-four maps (only those maximizing rational branch points), nor all covers over extension fields. The novelty assessment is best knowledge after targeted comparisons, not a priority guarantee; unindexed Hurwitz-space or earlier small-field tables may contain overlapping enumerations. The proof is exhaustive finite algebra, with the scripts supplied so every coefficient and group-action check can be reproduced.
