On a balanced double-star tree with \(d\) leaves at each of its two hubs, unnormalized heat flow has an exact all-time sharp Lipschitz norm, always attained across the connecting hub edge. Its unique positive-time peak for \(d\ge2\) approaches \(3\), with the sharp deficit \(3-M_d=8/d+O((\log d)/d^2)\), even though heat flow contracts the vertex supremum norm.
