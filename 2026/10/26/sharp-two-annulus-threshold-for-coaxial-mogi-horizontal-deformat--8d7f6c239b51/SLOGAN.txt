In the ideal two-coaxial-source Mogi model, two concentric maxima of horizontal ground displacement are possible only when the depth ratio exceeds \(\sqrt{11+2\sqrt{30}}\approx4.68556\), and then only inside an explicit strict strength-ratio interval. That interval opens at the critical depth ratio like a positive constant times the depth-contrast excess raised to the \(3/2\) power; outside it, the radial profile has one maximum.
