For an equilateral frame divided into two equal classes, the sharp universal Banach-valued two-point Lipschitz extension factor is \(\max\{1,(2m-1)/[m(r+s)],(m-1)/(mr),(2m-1)/(2mr+t)\}\), and an explicit positive operator attains it against all signed linear competitors. The final term captures a genuine simultaneous coupling obstruction absent from either one-point problem.
