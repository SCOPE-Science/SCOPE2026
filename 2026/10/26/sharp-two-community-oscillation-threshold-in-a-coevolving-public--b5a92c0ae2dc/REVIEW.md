## Mathematical value: PASS

The result resolves a specific conjectural limit of the newly introduced social-dilemma model with an open, attracting nonconvergence regime, rather than merely reporting a transient simulation. The exact mixing threshold and decay of nearby errors distinguish genuine robust cyclic behavior from pathological periodic graphs or fragile ties. Since the influence graph is complete and aperiodic, network bipartiteness is not the cause. This gives a concrete boundary for convergence analyses and model interpretation.
