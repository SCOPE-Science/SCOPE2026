The synchronous public-goods opinion model of Davidson and collaborators admits a locally attracting, nonconvergent two-cycle on a symmetric, primitive, loopless complete social network, coexisting with stable full-cooperation and full-defection states. For two equal communities with within-community influence \(\varepsilon\), the alternating block-constant cycle exists exactly when \(0<\varepsilon<(1-\theta)(1-p)/(1-2p(1-\theta))\), with explicit exponential attraction.
