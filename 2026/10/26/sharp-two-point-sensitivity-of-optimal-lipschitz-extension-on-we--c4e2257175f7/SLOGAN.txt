On a finite metric star, the unique optimal real-valued Lipschitz extension preserves every datum's Lipschitz seminorm but can amplify the seminorm of a difference by the exact factor \(1+2\ell_{[m-1]}\ell_{[m]}/((\ell_{[m-1]}+\ell_{[m]})\ell_{[1]})\) when at least three leaves are present. The factor is attained; it equals two on equilateral stars and grows without bound under arm-length imbalance.
