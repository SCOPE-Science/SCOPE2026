Sorting values is nonexpansive in Euclidean norm but has exact graph-Sobolev two-point amplification \((\sqrt{4+\delta}+\sqrt\delta)/2\) on an \(n\)-leaf star, with \(\delta=\gamma^2(n^2-1)/[(1+\gamma)(1+(n+1)\gamma)]\). The loss occurs precisely in sorting chambers that relocate the hub, grows as \(\sqrt{\gamma n/(1+\gamma)}\) for fixed coupling, and tends to \((\sqrt{n+3}+\sqrt{n-1})/2\) in the homogeneous gradient norm.
