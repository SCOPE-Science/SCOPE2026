For normalized degree-\(N\) complex polynomials, the first \(N\) intensity coefficients on each of two lines form a globally invertible polynomial measurement map exactly when no angular resonance occurs. The unique sharp families of locally best-conditioned line angles are \(\theta=p\pi/(N+1)\) with \(\gcd(p,N+1)=1\), and their optimal least singular value is \(2\sqrt{1-\cos(\pi/(N+1))}\).
