In a two-row Gaussian forward-only reconstruction, every row has the same individual Student-tail risk, yet the probability that both squared errors exceed \(t\) decays as \(t^{-q}\) for any fixed noncoincident known directions and only as \(t^{-q/2}\) for identical directions. The exact noncoincident coefficient is proportional to \((1-\rho^2)^{-q/2}\), and the paired errors retain excess joint risk even for orthogonal known directions.
