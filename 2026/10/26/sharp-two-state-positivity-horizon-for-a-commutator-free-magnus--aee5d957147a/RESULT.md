# Sharp two-state positivity horizon for a commutator-free Magnus endpoint

## Finding

Let \(s>0\) and \(h>0\). For any two two-state continuous-time Markov generators with identical total exit intensity \(s\), consider the ordered two-exponential Magnus endpoint from Blanes--Iserles--Macnamara, equation (3.18). **Every such pair yields a nonnegative, column-stochastic endpoint matrix if and only if**
\[
0<hs\le 2\log(7+4\sqrt3)=5.267831587699267\ldots.
\]
For larger steps the complete necessary-and-sufficient positivity criterion is an explicit pair of inequalities on the directed transition rates, given below. The threshold is sharp even when both input generators have strictly positive rates, and there are input pairs for which **both individual exponential factors have negative entries** but their product is entrywise nonnegative throughout the stated interval.

## Assumptions and scope

Use column probability vectors and set
\[
Q_j=\begin{pmatrix}-a_j&b_j\\a_j&-b_j\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 a_j,b_j\ge0,\quad a_j+b_j=s,\qquad j=1,2.
\]
Write \(\alpha=\frac12+\frac{\sqrt3}{3}>0\) and \(\beta=\frac12-\frac{\sqrt3}{3}<0\). Define precisely the **last two factors**, not the entire nonlinear seven-exponential construction,
\[
P_h=\exp\!\left(\frac h2(\beta Q_2+\alpha Q_1)\right)
\exp\!\left(\frac h2(\alpha Q_2+\beta Q_1)\right).
\]
The matrices \(Q_1,Q_2\) are arbitrary subject to the above constraints. No assertion is made that every such pair is generated by the earlier internal stages of the full nonlinear integrator. Equal total exit intensities express a common relaxation timescale, while the two directional rate splits may be entirely different.

Let \(t=hs/2\), \(r=e^{-t}\), and \(q=1-r\). The exact endpoint is
\[
P_h=I+\frac qs\big(w_1Q_1+w_2Q_2\big),\qquad
w_1=\alpha+\beta r,\quad w_2=\beta+\alpha r.
\]
The two independent off-diagonal entries are
\[
(P_h)_{21}=\frac qs(w_1a_1+w_2a_2),\qquad
(P_h)_{12}=\frac qs(w_1b_1+w_2b_2).
\]
For \(hs>2\log(7+4\sqrt3)\), define
\[
\rho_h=\frac{-\beta-\alpha e^{-hs/2}}
{\alpha+\beta e^{-hs/2}},\qquad 0<\rho_h<\frac{-\beta}{\alpha}=7-4\sqrt3.
\]
In this larger-step regime, \(P_h\) is nonnegative **if and only if**
\[
a_1\ge\rho_h a_2\quad\text{and}\quad b_1\ge\rho_h b_2.
\]
At the threshold equality is allowed and all admissible pairs remain nonnegative.

## Proof

**Common-rate multiplication identity.** For any real \(a,b,c,d\) with \(a+b=s\), direct multiplication gives
\[
Q(a,b)Q(c,d)=-sQ(c,d),\qquad
Q(a,b)=\begin{pmatrix}-a&b\\a&-b\end{pmatrix}.
\]
This identity does not require nonnegative entries. In particular, any linear combination \(C\) of \(Q_1,Q_2\) whose scalar coefficients sum to one obeys \(C^2=-sC\). Its exponential is therefore
\[
e^{(h/2)C}=I+\frac{1-e^{-hs/2}}sC=I+\frac qs C.
\]
Set \(L=\alpha Q_1+\beta Q_2\) and \(R=\beta Q_1+\alpha Q_2\). Both coefficient sums equal one, so \(LR=-sR\) and
\[
P_h=(I+qL/s)(I+qR/s)=I+\frac qs(L+rR).
\]
Grouping the coefficients of \(Q_1,Q_2\) proves the claimed explicit formula.

**Positivity is exactly an off-diagonal test.** The column sums of \(P_h\) are one. Its two off-diagonal entries have the expressions displayed above, and their sum is
\[
(P_h)_{12}+(P_h)_{21}
=\frac qs(s(w_1+w_2))=1-r^2<1.
\]
If both are nonnegative, each is less than one; hence both diagonal entries are strictly positive. Conversely, any negative off-diagonal entry destroys positivity. Thus \(P_h\) is entrywise nonnegative precisely when
\(w_1a_1+w_2a_2\ge0\) and \(w_1b_1+w_2b_2\ge0\).

**Universal sharp threshold.** Since \(\alpha>1\), \(-\beta=\alpha-1>0\), and \(0<r<1\), the coefficient \(w_1=\alpha+\beta r\) is strictly positive. The coefficient \(w_2=\beta+\alpha r\) is nonnegative exactly when \(r\ge-\beta/\alpha\). Using the exact identity
\[
\frac\alpha{-\beta}=7+4\sqrt3
\]
converts this to \(hs\le2\log(7+4\sqrt3)\). This condition guarantees positivity for **every** pair.

For the reverse direction take \(Q_1=Q(0,s)\) and \(Q_2=Q(s,0)\). Then \((P_h)_{21}=qw_2\), which is negative beyond the threshold. Therefore no larger step works uniformly. For any fixed supercritical step, replacing the zero rates with \(a_1=b_2=\varepsilon s\) and \(b_1=a_2=(1-\varepsilon)s\) preserves a negative off-diagonal entry for every sufficiently small \(\varepsilon>0\), establishing sharpness among irreducible input generators as well.

**Exact supercritical classification.** Above the threshold, \(w_2<0<w_1\). Divide the two off-diagonal inequalities by \(w_1\) to obtain exactly the two rate inequalities with \(\rho_h=-w_2/w_1\). The stated bounds on \(\rho_h\) follow from its formula and \(0<r<-\beta/\alpha\).

**Endpoint recovery despite negative stages.** For the one-way sharpness pair, \(L_{21}=\beta s<0\) and \(R_{12}=\beta s<0\). Since each exponential equals \(I+(q/s)C\), both individual factors have a negative off-diagonal entry for **every** \(h>0\), even though their product is nonnegative for every \(0<h s\le2\log(7+4\sqrt3)\). This proves cancellation at the complete endpoint rather than merely nonnegativity of the internal exponentials.

## Relationship to prior work

Blanes, Iserles and Macnamara define the third-order commutator-free Magnus endpoint and identify its negative coefficient in equations (3.18)--(3.19); they give the **sufficient** requirement that both internal exponent matrices retain graph-Laplacian structure. They do not derive this two-state, common-relaxation-time **endpoint** positivity classification. Their conclusion explicitly identifies quantitative almost-positivity of commutator-free schemes as an open direction. Hofstätter and Koch derive an order barrier for positive-coefficient commutator-free exponential integrators on parabolic problems; their condition concerns coefficient sums and fifth-or-higher-order existence, not entrywise positivity of this two-exponential Markov endpoint at a finite step.

## Limitations

The result concerns exact matrix exponentials of precisely the displayed two factors, dimension two, equal total exit intensities, and column-generator convention. It is not a statement that all stages of the full nonlinear method are positive or that all admissible generator pairs arise from a specific nonlinear model. The threshold is **universal over the stated pair class** and not the positivity boundary for every individual pair: the exact supercritical inequalities permit many pairs to stay positive for longer. No claim is made for unequal total exit intensities or higher dimensions. Originality is a best-knowledge assessment subject to independent priority review.
