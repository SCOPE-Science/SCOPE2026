For the two-exponential third-order commutator-free Magnus endpoint, every pair of two-state Markov generators with the same total exit rate has a nonnegative endpoint exactly up to the sharp universal step \(hs=2\log(7+4\sqrt3)\). Beyond it, two explicit rate inequalities give complete individual-pair positivity, while a pair of nonpositive exponential factors can still multiply to a stochastic matrix below the threshold.
