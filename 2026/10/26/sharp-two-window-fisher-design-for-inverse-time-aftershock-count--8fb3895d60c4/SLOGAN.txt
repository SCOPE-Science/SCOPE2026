For a pure inverse-time aftershock Poisson process, the unique Fisher-optimal dividing time for retaining just two event counts is given by a one-dimensional monotone equation. As the observation horizon grows, that time approaches \(2.5128624\ldots\) times the offset scale, and the counts retain \(81.4529\ldots\%\) of the arrival-time information about the offset when event-rate amplitude is unknown.
