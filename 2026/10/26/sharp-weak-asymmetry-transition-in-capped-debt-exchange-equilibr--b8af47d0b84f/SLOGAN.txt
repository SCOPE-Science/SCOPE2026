Under coin-transfer bias of exact order \(1/N\), a capped-debt exchange equilibrium has a sharp bank-saturation transition at \(\tau_c=1/[\kappa\sqrt{r(1+r)}]\): below it the debt ceiling binds, but above it an explicit smaller positive debt density remains. All fixed-coordinate wealth marginals converge to a computable two-sided exponential law, showing why fixed-rate Gamma/exponential limits do not describe this intermediate regime.
