For an attractive sinusoidal cycle with one weak edge, winding-one and winding-minus-one stable locked states disappear at the same exact positive edge-weight threshold, where each collides with a one-unstable-direction saddle. On a cycle of \(n\) sites the threshold is \(\sec(t_*)/(n-1)+O((n-1)^{-3})\), with \(\tan t_*=\pi+t_*\), giving a sharp size-dependent weak-link law.
