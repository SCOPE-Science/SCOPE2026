For the quasiconformal harmonic Koebe shear, the weighted Bergman \(q\)-moment has a sharp power-law explosion as distortion tends to infinity, throughout the natural gap \((\beta+2)/3<q<(\beta+2)/2\). Its exact positive coefficient is governed by the explicit two-dimensional boundary profile \(2\operatorname{Re}[y^{-2}-2y^{-1}+2\log(1+y^{-1})]\).
