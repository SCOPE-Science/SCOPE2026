The sharp worst-case extra correction required by cell-mean-anchored nodal positivity scaling, compared with the nearest conservative nonnegative nodal vector, is \(\sqrt{(1-w_{(1)})(1-w_{(2)})/(w_{(1)}w_{(2)})}\). For an \(N\)-point Legendre--Gauss--Lobatto rule the exact factor is \(N(N-1)-1\), so inexpensive radial limiting can be quadratically more perturbative in degree even for a single negative node.
