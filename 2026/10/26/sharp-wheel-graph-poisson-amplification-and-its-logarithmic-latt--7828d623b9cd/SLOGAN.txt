The sharp \(\ell^1\)- and \(\ell^\infty\)-norms of the unnormalized wheel-graph Laplacian pseudoinverse are exact, attained sums of positive Green entries. Their approach to the limiting norm \(2\) has a logarithmic leading defect and a nonconstant, explicitly determined lattice-phase correction whose accumulation interval is \([3.996990\ldots,2\sqrt5]\).
