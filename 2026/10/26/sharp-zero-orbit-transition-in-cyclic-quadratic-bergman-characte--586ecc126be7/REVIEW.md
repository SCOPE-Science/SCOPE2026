# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The monomial norm gives the stated generating series with denominator \((1-s)^4\). The numerator coefficients and endpoint factorization follow from elementary power-sum identities. A degree-by-degree recurrence proves the required simple negative roots, and the signs at \(s=-1,0\) together with the constant-to-leading coefficient ratio establish the complete inside/outside root count. In particular, the boundary resonance and the exceptional lowered-degree polynomial are treated separately, and the equivalence of \(|s|<1\) to attainable zero products is explicit. Exact symbolic and Sturm checks for \(1770\) pairs corroborate the proof but are not substituted for the universal argument.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)

The Veronese literature, especially Jochemko's 2016 work, already establishes a much broader real-rootedness phenomenon. That part is expressly not claimed as novel. The inspected orbifold and Bergman-kernel papers do not directly give the exact number of nonzero zero orbits under the open-disk constraint, nor the sharp \(q=2r+4\) boundary and \(q=2r+5\) transition. Earlier Hilbert-series or character-kernel tables could encode the same count and remain a publication-priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

A natural isotypical decomposition of a zero-free weighted disk kernel yields sector kernels whose attainable zero fibers switch sharply between zero, one, and two rotation orbits. The result gives a complete answer in the integer quadratic-weight class and makes the boundary of the physical product domain essential, rather than computing an arbitrary coefficient slice.

## Limitations and comparison

The main restriction is to weight exponent \(2\); a general Veronese real-rootedness theorem covers the negative-root lemma but does not itself classify which roots are inside the disk. Nearby studies concern full radial weights, general orbifold asymptotics, jet kernels, or distinct Reinhardt domains. No independent publication-priority audit or independent proof validation has been undertaken.
