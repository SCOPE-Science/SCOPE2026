For the quadratic-weighted Bergman space of the disk, projecting onto a nontrivial cyclic rotation character creates an exact sharp phase change: the reproducing kernel has one orbit of nonzero interior zeros when \(q\le2r+4\) (except \((q,r)=(2,1)\)), but two orbits when \(q\ge2r+5\). The boundary resonance \(q=2r+4\) is excluded by the open-disk geometry; general negative-root realness is known from Veronese theory, while the sharp attainable-orbit count is the specific classification.
