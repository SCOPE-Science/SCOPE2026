For any graph, the edge sets shattered by its vertex-induced-edge ranges are exactly the star forests, so their maximum size is the vertex count minus the domination number. On complete graphs the entire shattering polynomial has a simple exponential generating function, and a uniformly random \(m\)-edge set loses shatterability in a sharp \(m\asymp n^{2/3}\) Poisson window with limiting probability \(e^{-4c^3}\).
