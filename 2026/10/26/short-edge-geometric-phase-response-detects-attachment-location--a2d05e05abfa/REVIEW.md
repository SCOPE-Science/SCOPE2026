# Scope of the short-edge response

The analytic spectral proof concerns the normalized first positive eigenstate and verifies its simple branch using a pole-free determinant at the star. The ratio compares endpoint and hub intensities in the limiting interval. The phase-related calculation is confined to the explicit periodic functional in arXiv:2210.14652v1, Eqs. (47)–(50). The published theory instead gives a Berry connection, which vanishes on small retraced single-length cycles for this real simple branch. No comparison of nonzero Berry phases is claimed.
