On a bounded, mass-conserving two-state exchange equation, the positive second-order SPIDeC-GL(2) step with one correction sweep changes a universally positive predictor mass error into a negative cubic defect at small steps, yet its corrected mass grows on the exponential-of-exponential scale \(\log M\asymp s\exp(s|r-r^{-1}|)\) for every nonequilibrium initial ratio \(r\). Exact positivity and scalar linear stability therefore do not prevent extreme mass inflation on this coupled linear system.
