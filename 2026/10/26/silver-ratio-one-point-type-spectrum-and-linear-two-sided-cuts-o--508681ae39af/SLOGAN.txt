For a natural cyclic height-two poset in which each maximal element covers three consecutive minima, the nonalgebraic one-point type count over its \(2n\) named vertices is exactly \(2((1+\sqrt2)^n+(1-\sqrt2)^n)+12n+1\), for \(n\ge5\). Exactly \(12n\) types lie strictly between named lower and upper parameters, so two-sided cuts become exponentially rare even as the total spectrum grows at the silver ratio.
