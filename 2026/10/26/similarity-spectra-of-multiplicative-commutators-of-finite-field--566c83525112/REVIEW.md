The zero and nonzero parameter branches are exhaustive with disjoint hypotheses; all partners are counted exactly, including \(n=2\). These finite tests are not an infinite proof.

**PASS.** Transvections are fundamental generators of finite classical linear groups, while the conjugacy type of their multiplicative commutator records how two directions interact. The result enumerates the complete natural class-function law over all \(\operatorname{SL}_n(\mathbb F_q)\), not merely existence, and sharply separates characteristic-two semisimplicity from the odd-characteristic repeated-root stratum. The dimension-stable limiting masses make this an invariant rather than an isolated small-instance calculation.
