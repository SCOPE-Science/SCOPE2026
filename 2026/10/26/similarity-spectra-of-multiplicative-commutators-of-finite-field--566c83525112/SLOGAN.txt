For independent uniform nonidentity transvections in \(\operatorname{SL}_n(\mathbb F_q)\), the multiplicative commutator has exactly three kinds of similarity strata: identity, a rank-one unipotent, and a cyclic determinant-one block of degree two whose characteristic polynomial is \(X^2-(2+t^2)X+1\). Every nonzero \(t\) occurs with exactly \(q^{2n-3}\) partners, yielding an explicit characteristic-sensitive split/nonsplit/Jordan law and stable limiting probabilities.
