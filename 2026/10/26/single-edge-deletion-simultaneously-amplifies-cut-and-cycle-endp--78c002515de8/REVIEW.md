# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The key nonstandard sign step follows from a positive Walsh heat-kernel integral: adding a neighboring parallel current column cancels only the direction perpendicular to its square, and there with exactly equal magnitudes. The rank-one inverse update yields exact witnessed post-deletion column sums. The positivity inequalities prove the infinite-dimensional threshold without extrapolating a finite census. The dimension-three boundary is exhaustively checked by a second, exact-rational grounded-Laplacian reconstruction.

**Originality — PASS (residual bibliographic risk).** The closest source, Dilworth–Kutzarova–Ostrovskii (arXiv:2305.12582, Section 2.7, Theorem 45 and Corollary 60), establishes the intact-cube norm but not single-edge deletion. General localization bounds of Schild–Rao–Srivastava (arXiv:1708.01632) and Gurel-Gurevich–Nachmias–Sachdeva (arXiv:2605.24130) do not determine the exact signed perturbation response. Related one-edge deletion classifications for cliques and bicliques concern different graph families and yield different transitions; they do not imply the cube result. The literature search cannot exclude every unindexed equivalent electrical-network calculation.

**Value — PASS.** The theorem gives a natural, precise counterintuitive sensitivity result for two complementary Banach-space operator norms: removing one edge from a highly symmetric family simultaneously amplifies both endpoint norms, with a sharp onset at dimension four. The exact positive gains show the effect at every higher dimension, rather than only at a finite example, and quantify its decay. No claim of a general operator-norm classification after deletion is made.

The assumptions concern canonical Euclidean-orthogonal projections and real unweighted graphs; arbitrary nonorthogonal projection minima are outside scope. Same-family dimensional onset, full exact sign steps and the independent rational reconstruction are the evidence, not a claim of external certification.
