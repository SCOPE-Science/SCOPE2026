For a word represented by \(r\) maximal constant runs with binary-encoded lengths, exact shuffle-square recognition reduces to at most \(3^r\) capacitated degree-constrained matching instances, each with only \(O(r)\) vertices and edges. Thus an NP-complete recognition problem is solvable in \(3^r\operatorname{poly}(r,S)\) time on run-length-compressed input, with a compressed witness.
