For a stationary symmetric two-state diffusivity, measuring displacement cumulants of orders \(2\), \(4\), and \(8\) at just one known time uniquely determines both positive diffusivities and their switching rate. The dimensionless ratio \(-\kappa_8/\kappa_4^2\) decreases strictly from \(70/3\) to zero, and one additional sharp inequality exactly characterizes physically realizable triples.
