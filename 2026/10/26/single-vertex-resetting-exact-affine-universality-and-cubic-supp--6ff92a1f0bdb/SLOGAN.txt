In an unwrapped linear oscillator network, resetting one vertex at any positive Poisson rate leaves every stationary mean phase difference exactly equal to its permanent-pinning value, and all centered fluctuations depend on the intrinsic frequencies only through their sum. The reset-site variance decays as \(\lambda^{-2}\), while the covariance at any two never-reset vertices decays as a strictly positive graph-dependent multiple of \(\lambda^{-3}\).
