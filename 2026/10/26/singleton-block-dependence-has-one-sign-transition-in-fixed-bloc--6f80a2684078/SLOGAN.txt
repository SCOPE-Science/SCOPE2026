In a uniform set partition conditioned to have exactly \(k\) blocks, the covariance between singleton count and size-\(s\) block count has at most one zero and can change sign only from negative to positive as \(s\) increases. For fixed \(k\ge3\) its transition lies at the explicit fraction \(1-\log(k/(k-1))/\log((k-1)/(k-2))\) of the ground-set size.
