For the canonical Lie affgebra of the three-dimensional Heisenberg Lie algebra, affine weight-minus-one Rota–Baxter operators form exactly six automorphism classes over every field of characteristic different from two. Over \(\mathbb F_q\) with odd \(q\), the classes have an exact six-term orbit distribution and a total of \(q^3(2q+3)+1\) operators.
