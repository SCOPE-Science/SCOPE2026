**Correctness — PASS.** The scheme-theoretic cone reduction is an exact binomial identity. Newton identities reduce the base intersection to the distinct-root polynomial \(T^5+e_4T\), forcing all six projective roots over the entire algebraic closure; Bézout yields reducedness. The local complete-intersection Hilbert series and evaluation maps give delta \(9\) and exact conductor. A standalone exhaustive \(\mathbb F_5\) checker confirms the six directions, polar determinant \(2\), projective point count \(31\), evaluation ranks and faithful 120-element group action. The finite computation does not substitute for the geometric proof.

The 2021 Korchmáros–Lia–Timpanella article studies positive characteristics \(p\ge7\) and explicitly excludes \(p=5\) for five variables. The open-access 2023/2024 Braden–Disney-Hogg study addresses the complex curve. Database and literature searches found no result determining this reducible characteristic-five fibre, its conic directions, local conductor, or full exceptional group action. Earlier Bring–Jerrard/Edge work has not been exhaustively excluded, and no claim of proven global uniqueness is made.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** This natural exceptional-prime degeneration, ruled out from the positive-characteristic smooth-curve regime in published work, records the entire geometric special fibre rather than an arbitrary numerical slice. The exceptional six-point \(S_5\) action and exact conductor and zeta function give structural and arithmetic information directly relevant to bad reduction of the classical genus-four curve.

**Limits:** The claim is model-specific and determines neither semistable reduction nor the monodromy of a smoothing. No historical uniqueness theorem is asserted.
