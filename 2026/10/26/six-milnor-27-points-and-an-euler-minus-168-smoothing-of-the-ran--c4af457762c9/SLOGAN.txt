The six singular points of the Mordell–Weil-rank-twelve anticanonical quartic each have threefold Milnor number \(27\), and their global projective smoothing is a Calabi–Yau threefold of Euler characteristic \(-168\). Relative to the crepant resolution's Euler characteristic \(48\), this gives a six-point topological transition of total size \(216\).
