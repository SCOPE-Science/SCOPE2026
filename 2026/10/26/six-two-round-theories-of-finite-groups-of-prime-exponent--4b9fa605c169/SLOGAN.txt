All nontrivial finite groups of prime exponent collapse to exactly six first-order two-round theories in the ternary group language, represented by \(C_2,C_2^2,C_3,C_3^2,C_5,C_7\). In particular, every nonabelian group of exponent at least \(5\) (prime) is rank-two equivalent to \(C_7\), and the sharp largest minimal representative has \(9\) elements.
