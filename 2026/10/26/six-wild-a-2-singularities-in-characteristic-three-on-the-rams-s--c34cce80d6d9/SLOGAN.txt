On a nonempty open part of the characteristic-three Rams–Schütt quartic \(\mathcal Z\) family, the surface acquires exactly six wild \(A_2\) singularities, three over each root of its defining binary quadratic. Every point supports a curvilinear Jacobian singular scheme of length \(3\), so the geometric six-point support conceals a total singular-scheme length of \(18\).
