The Shrikhande graph has exactly \(96\) minimum radius-one identifying codes, all of size five, explicitly parametrized by a missing point in a four-element parity coset and an edge of its six-neighbor cycle. Their single-vertex exchange graph is exactly \(16C_6\), so each optimal code has two legal one-vertex replacements but no sequence of such exchanges can change its missing parity-coset point.
