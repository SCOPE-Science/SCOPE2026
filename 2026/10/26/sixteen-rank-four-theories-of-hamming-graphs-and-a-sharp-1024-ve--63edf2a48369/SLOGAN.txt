Among all Hamming graphs \(H(d,q)\) with \(d\ge1\) and \(q\ge2\), there are exactly sixteen first-order theories at quantifier rank four: six binary, five ternary, and five shared by all alphabets of size at least four. The sharp smallest-universal-representative bound within this family is \(1024\) vertices, attained by \(H(5,4)\).
