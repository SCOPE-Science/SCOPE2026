For the projective toric variety of the hypersimplex \(\Delta(k,n)\), there are exactly \(2^{\binom nk}\) smooth toric small resolutions over the fixed target, but only the choices realized by chambers of the \(k\)-subset-sum hyperplane arrangement are projective. In rank two this is the labeled threshold-graph arrangement: of the \(64\) small resolutions for \(\Delta(2,4)\), exactly \(46\) are projective.
