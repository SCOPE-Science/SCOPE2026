For every squarefree \(n\) with at least two prime factors, deleting the unit row and column of the divisor-indexed Ramanujan-sum matrix leaves exactly \(2^{\omega(n)-1}\) unit Smith factors, \(2^{\omega(n)-1}-2\) factors equal to \(n\), and one factor equal to \(n\varphi(n)\). This determines its entire integral cokernel and the kernel size modulo every positive integer.
