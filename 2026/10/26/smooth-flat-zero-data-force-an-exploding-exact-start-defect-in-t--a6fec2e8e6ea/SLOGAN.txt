For the uncapped two-stage Gaussian RBF Runge–Kutta formula, one fixed \(C^\infty\), globally state-Lipschitz scalar IVP has exact-data one-step errors exploding at least as fast as \((3h/4)e^{1/(2h^4)}-O(1)\) on every fine uniform grid, although the shape denominator never vanishes at grid nodes. This quantifies a failure outside the original bounded-shape convergence hypothesis rather than contradicting it.
