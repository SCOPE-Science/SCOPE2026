## correctness

PASS

PASS: The unique lowest common ancestor of two leaves partitions inversion pairs by internal tree node. A swap of node children creates exactly 4^(n-j-1) inversions at depth j, and the independent fair swap bits give the finite polynomial product. Geometric sums produce E I_n=N(N-1)/4 and Var I_n=N^4(1-8^-n)/56. Under an infinite-bit coupling X_n=I_n/N² is a growing partial sum with deterministic tail ≤2^(-n-1), giving almost-sure and Lp convergence to X. The complement of all bits proves X and 1/2-X equidistribution; the weight sequence has each weight <=remaining deeper tail so a greedy subsum argument yields full support [0,1/2]. Its characteristic function equals e^(it/4) times the product of cos(t/(2*4^(j+1)))^(2^j). Choosing level j=floor(log4|t|)-1 gives cos argument in [1/2,2), hence |phi(t)|<=exp[-(-log cos(1/2))/4*sqrt(|t|)] for |t|>=4. All polynomially weighted phi are integrable: Fourier inversion supplies compactly supported C-infinity density. Independent enumeration of every group element for n<=4 reproduces the exact polynomial and moments, and independent Fourier-level numerical checks corroborate, not replace, the analytic bound.

## originality

PASS

PASS (qualified): Peca-Medlin–Zhong (2024) provide the exact group-law/uniform permutation premise and study longest increasing subsequences and cycle counts. Gunawan–Peca-Medlin–Zhong (2026) restate that uniform law for general scalar butterfly pivot ensembles, without the inversion product or smooth limit in the read sections. Cai–Holmgren–Janson et al. study ancestor/descendant tree inversions for random vertex labels, a different statistic and randomization. Neither published inversion result directly implies the iterated-wreath fair-leaf-swap law, although a general inversion theorem could. Resultary semantic search did not find an equivalent.
