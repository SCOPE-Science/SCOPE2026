For uniform general butterfly pivot permutations of size \(N=2^n\), the inversion count divided by \(N^2\) converges to a nondegenerate, compactly supported \(C^\infty\) law on \([0,1/2]\), with variance \(1/56\). Its exact finite distribution is a sum of independent dyadically weighted fair bits, and its characteristic function decays at least as \(e^{-c\sqrt{|t|}}\); thus pairwise discordance remains macroscopically random even though ordinary uniform permutations concentrate.
