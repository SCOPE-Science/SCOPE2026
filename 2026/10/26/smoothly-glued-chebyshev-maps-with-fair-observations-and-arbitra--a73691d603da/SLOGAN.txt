Two Chebyshev polynomials of degrees congruent to \(2\pmod4\) can be glued into a \(C^1\) interval map whose arcsine-invariant dynamics give perfectly fair iid sign observations, while its unique maximal-entropy dynamics give biased iid signs. The intrinsic entropy gap is exactly \(\log((m+n)/(2\sqrt{mn}))\), and is unbounded across this family.
