# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

An R-algebra endomorphism of R[t]/t^m is determined by u=c0+c1 t+...+c_{m−1}t^{m−1} with relation u^m=0. Its constant term obeys c0^m=0, so c0 is nilpotent on every base ring; the reduction of the substitution matrix is triangular with diagonal powers of c1, yielding invertibility iff c1 is a unit. Reduced support is G_m×A^{m−2} of dimension m−1. Derivations are determined by v=D(t) with relation m t^{m−1}v=0, so the identity tangent dimension equals m when p divides m and m−1 otherwise. For p∤m, the t^{m−1} coefficient of u^m is c0(m c1^{m−1}+c0 H), and the factor in parentheses is a unit; hence c0=0 scheme-theoretically and the scheme is smooth. For p|m tangent dimension exceeds support dimension, so nonsmoothness is forced. For m=p^e, Frobenius yields u^m=c0^m exactly, giving the entire coordinate ring k[c0,c1,c1^{-1},c2,...,c_{m−1}]/(c0^m) over arbitrary commutative bases. The proof uses functorial equations, not just field-valued automorphism counts. Characteristic 2,3,5,7 small-parameter tangent dimension cases were cross-checked but are illustrative only.

## originality

PASS

A positive-characteristic example m=p and the derivation-tangent principle are definitely prior work (Tziolas 2024). The assessed increment is exact smooth iff p∤m for every m≥2 plus an explicit scheme-level coordinate ring for all p-power truncations. The earlier m=p result is a special case but not a statement of the full infinite m-criterion, and the finite-field-extension literature concerns a reduced, étale-type object rather than nilpotent quotients. The extension follows by short elementary algebra, so priority risk is relatively high; a stronger textbook functor-of-points treatment could cover it. Fresh Resultary query found different Leibniz and coordinate-cross automorphism examples, not this full truncation classification.

## value

PASS

The result exactly characterizes where the full automorphism functor of the canonical length-m local algebra becomes nonsmooth, and supplies an all-prime-power explicit nilpotent-coordinate scheme. The extra infinitesimal symmetry invisible to pointwise groups is a natural structural invariant relevant in deformation theory; the infinite sharp divisibility boundary is more than a small example even though its proof is short. This is a modest structural result, not a new general theory of group schemes.
