# Review

**Correctness — PASS.** The proof reconstructs the automorphism functor on arbitrary commutative base algebras, determines the reduced support and dimension, computes the tangent space from the relation \(t^m=0\), and verifies the exact coordinate equation when \(m=p^e\). The coefficient equation for \(t^{m-1}\) proves the smooth case scheme-theoretically.

**Originality — PASS, bounded assessment.** The closest inspected primary source treats \(k[t]/(t^p)\) and its non-smooth automorphism scheme (Tziolas, 2024, Example 3.23). The claim extends the smoothness criterion to every \(m\ge2\) and gives the full coordinate scheme for every prime-power length. Searches did not locate the same statement, but they do not prove universal novelty. The adjacent 2000 paper on automorphism schemes of finite field extensions does not establish this result for the nonreduced quotient algebra.

**Value — PASS.** The criterion identifies exactly when infinitesimal automorphisms add a tangent direction not visible in the reduced scheme. The prime-power formula gives a complete, reusable representing-scheme description, relevant to deformation and moduli questions for finite local algebras.

**Closest literature and limitations.** Tziolas (2024), §§3.3–3.4, Example 3.23, and Proposition 3.19; Sancho de Salas (2000), a related but different field-extension setting. The statement does not classify the group law for arbitrary non-smooth lengths, nor multivariable or noncommutative truncations.
