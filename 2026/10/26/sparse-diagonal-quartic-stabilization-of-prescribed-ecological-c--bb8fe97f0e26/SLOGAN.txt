Any prescribed positive coexistence state can be made an arbitrarily fast local sink while retaining all original pairwise ecological interactions: just add a positive own-density quadratic and negative own-density cubic term to each species' per-capita growth. This self-only \(2n\)-coefficient construction makes every nonnegative trajectory bounded and uses the fewest coefficients possible within the degree-two/degree-three diagonal intervention class in the worst case.
