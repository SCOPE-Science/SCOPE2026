Sparse independent point markings of growing vector spaces over a fixed finite field carry no asymptotic first-order information beyond their count, despite the availability of full vector addition and scalar multiplication. Their effective limit law has the same Poisson-subsums spectrum already known for sparse ordered bit strings, and an explicit vector-space Ehrenfeucht–Fraïssé strategy explains this otherwise nonautomatic coincidence; the closed spectrum has Hausdorff dimension zero.
