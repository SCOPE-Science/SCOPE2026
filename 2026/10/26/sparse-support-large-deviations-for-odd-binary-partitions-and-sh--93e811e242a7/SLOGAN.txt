For positive odd \(n\), the semi-Pell/semi-Fibonacci ratio has sharp polynomial exponent one: \(\operatorname{sp}(n)/\operatorname{sf}(n)=n^{1+o(1)}\). More precisely, among odd-multiplicity binary partitions, the proportion occupying at most \((1-\varepsilon)\lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor\) distinct powers has sharp base-two logarithmic rate \(-\varepsilon^2\lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor^2/2\).
