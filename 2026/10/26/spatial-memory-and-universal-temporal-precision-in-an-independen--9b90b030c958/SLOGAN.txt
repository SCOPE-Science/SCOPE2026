For independently reset Brownian particles with spatially correlated fixed anchors, the long-time variance of the stationary time-averaged crossing count separates into an anchor-dependent plateau and a universal \(7\rho/(16r\alpha T)\) temporal correction, where \(\alpha=\sqrt{r/D}\). A randomly shifted lattice suppresses the plateau below the Poisson value at every density, approaching \(1/48\) at high density while preserving the same leading temporal correction.
