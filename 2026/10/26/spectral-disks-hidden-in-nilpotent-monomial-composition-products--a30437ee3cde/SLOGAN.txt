For distinct coprime power symbols on a regularly varying analytic coefficient Hilbert space, the product \(C_{z^m}C_{z^n}^*\) is an orthogonal sum of finite nilpotent paths plus constants, yet its Fredholm essential spectrum is the entire disk of radius \((mn)^{-\beta}\). Its only eigenvalues are \(0\) and \(1\), and the spectral transition across Hardy, Bergman and Dirichlet scales is exact.
