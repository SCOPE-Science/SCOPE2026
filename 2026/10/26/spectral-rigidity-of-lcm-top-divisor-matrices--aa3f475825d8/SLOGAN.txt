The nonzero spectrum of the divisor-index indicator matrix \(\mathbf1_{\operatorname{lcm}(d,e)=n}\) reconstructs exactly the unordered prime-power exponents of \(n\), even when the prime bases are unknown. Its complete spectrum factors into the two roots of \(t^2-t-a\) for each exponent \(a\), yielding an exact classification of cospectral divisor lattices.
