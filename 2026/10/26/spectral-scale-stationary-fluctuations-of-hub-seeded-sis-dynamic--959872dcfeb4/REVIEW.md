# Scientific review: spectral-scale hub-seeded SIS dynamics on stars

## Correctness — PASS

The exact finite two-coordinate Markov generator follows from independent recovery, edge transmission and permanent hub activation. Stationary moment bounds of all fixed orders give tightness and uniform integrability of the infected-leaf count normalized by the square root of the star degree. On compact sets, generator convergence identifies a two-mode piecewise-deterministic process. A positive switching rate eliminates invariant atoms, current integrability forces zero current, and the forward equations uniquely determine the two densities. Exchangeability converts joint convergence to the stated scaled pairwise covariance laws. These steps prove the indicated limits for every fixed positive transmission parameter and permanent hub-seeding rate. The accompanying script checks symbolic balance equations, quadratures, finite generators and exact finite stationary moment identities; this computational evidence does not substitute for the limiting argument.

## Originality — PASS against the inspected material, with recorded risks

The 2013 external-agents article of Banerjee, Chatterjee and Shakkottai studies an infection source that shuts off once the epidemic is extinct and proves lifetime bounds, including an appendix about hub-targeting strategies; it does not give the permanently seeded stationary limit. The absorbing contact-process star lemma of Chatterjee and Durrett concerns long survival under different scaling assumptions. Cator and Van Mieghem's published abstract describes exactly analyzed uniform-self-infection and nonabsorbing modified SIS models on stars; its full text was not accessible despite lawful routes. It remains a genuine risk because a more general star formula might cover the present specialization. Search comparisons of equivalent model terminology, density form, and star-process parameters did not identify a result that states or implies the present complete limiting stationary law and its scaled covariance consequences. Absence of a search result does not establish universal novelty. More general promoter-switching PDMP laws may supply a separate route to the same density; applicability has not been exhaustively excluded.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The star spectral scale is dictated by the graph's adjacency eigenvalue. Permanent infection targeted at its hub is a natural finite-virulence intervention, and the stochastic center switches on an order-one timescale while the leaf infection count is order square root of the degree. The complete, non-Gaussian joint stationary limit identifies a non-self-averaging infection reservoir, a conditional hub-infection probability, nontrivial rescaled hub–leaf and leaf–leaf correlations, and a sharp change in the shape of the limiting density. These are motivated structural observables rather than arbitrary finite table entries. The result does not claim the epidemic survival threshold for the absorbing process.
