At infection rate \(\beta/\sqrt m\) on an \(m\)-leaf star with permanent external infection only at the hub, the stationary infected-leaf count divided by \(\beta\sqrt m\) converges to a non-Gaussian beta–exponential law, and the hub is infected conditionally with probability equal to the limiting rescaled leaf count. This law yields exact nonzero scaled hub–leaf and leaf–leaf correlations and a sharp change in density profile at \(\beta^2=1-\eta\) when \(0<\eta<1\).
