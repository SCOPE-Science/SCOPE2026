# Verification

Execute `python3 verify.py`. The test implements Hermitian matrices over the exact integral ring generated by the sixth root of unity and evaluates characteristic polynomials using a direct Faddeev–LeVerrier algorithm, independently of the path/determinant formula. It reports `VERIFY_OK exact_characteristic_checks=880 cycle_phase_sizes=3,5,7,9`.

The verifier compares the full characteristic polynomial, its parity/spectral-symmetry predicate, and the closed-form prediction. It enumerates every one of the 512 orientations of the three-triangle bouquet, finding exactly 216 symmetric ones, and enumerates all orientations of single odd cycles of lengths 3, 5, 7 and 9 to check phase counts. Several larger mixed-length bouquets are sampled rather than exhaustively enumerated. `verify_output.txt` contains the actual execution output; entries labelled `samples` and `matching` refer only to the displayed sample sizes and must not be interpreted as full censuses.

The unrestricted classification depends on the hub Schur-complement identity, polynomial parity, independence of length-dependent rational functions, and the exact independent-edge phase generating sum. The checker corroborates but does not establish all-parameter correctness. Independent audit and external validation have not been performed.
