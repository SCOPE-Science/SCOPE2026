## Correctness
The cosh-product identity and Euler beta integral give the exact differentiated Fourier formula. A leading oscillatory term and derivative establish simple high-frequency roots, and a locally uniform rescaling gives spherical-Bessel root convergence with an order-two perturbation. The squared-secant case is classified by a monotone tangent equation. The proof is analytical; finite high-precision comparisons test constants and normalizations only.

## Originality
The closest inspected works are Kloos and Stöckler (arXiv:1311.7359), Gröchenig, Jaming and Malinnikova (arXiv:1811.03937), and Boyadzhiev (arXiv:0903.0117). These address nearby Zak/Gabor, ambiguity zero-freeness and classical sech derivatives, respectively; none of their inspected relevant sections establishes the combined all-integer-power zero count and small-delay Bessel root convergence. Equivalent results under another formulation remain possible, so publication priority is not certified.

## Mathematical value
The nodal transition and singular Bessel rescaling classify a natural zero-geometry phenomenon for positive smooth signals. The explicit second-power classification supplies a global boundary example, whereas other exponents are only classified asymptotically.

## Limitations
No all-frequency classification for every exponent, no exclusion of roots whose rescaled Doppler frequency tends to zero, no estimates uniform in increasing exponent, and no independent mathematical audit.
