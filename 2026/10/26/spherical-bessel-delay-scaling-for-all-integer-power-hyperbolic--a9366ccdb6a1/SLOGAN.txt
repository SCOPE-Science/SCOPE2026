For every integer power of the hyperbolic-secant window, the real ambiguity function is strictly positive at zero delay but has asymptotically \( |T|R \) positive Doppler zeros up to \(R\) at any fixed nonzero delay. As delay vanishes, the rescaled nodal curves converge near every positive root to those of a spherical Bessel function; the squared-secant case has an exact one-root-per-lobe classification.
