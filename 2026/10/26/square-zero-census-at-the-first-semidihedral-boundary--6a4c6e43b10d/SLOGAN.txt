For the smallest dihedral, semidihedral and generalized-quaternion groups, the square-zero counts in characteristic-two group algebras are respectively \(q^8(4q-3)\), \(q^8(3q-2)\), and \(q^8(2q-1)\). Over \(\mathbb F_2\), their complete regular-multiplication rank spectra coincide below rank \(8\) and separate exactly at the acyclic rank \(8\).
