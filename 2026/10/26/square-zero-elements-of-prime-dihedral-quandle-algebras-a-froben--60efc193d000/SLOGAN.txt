For the prime dihedral quandle algebra \(\mathbb F_q[R_p]\), every square-zero element is counted by a weighted independent-set polynomial on cycles of Frobenius cosets: \(P_\ell(q^d-1)^{(p-1)/(d\ell)}\). Nonzero square-zero elements exist exactly when the class of \(-2\) is nontrivial modulo \(\langle q\rangle\) in \((\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z)^\times\).
