For \(C_{p^a}\oplus C_{p^b}\) with \(a=1\) or \(2\) and \(b>a\), the number of square-zero endomorphisms has an explicit parity-dependent formula at every prime, with exact two-step stabilization \(Q_{a,b+2}(p)=pQ_{a,b}(p)\). The exponent-one and exponent-two families have different coefficients, reflecting product-fiber geometry over \(\mathbb Z/p^2\mathbb Z\).
