For the incidence algebra of a branched interval with \(r\ge2\) incomparable middle points over \(\mathbb F_q\), the full pointwise count of idempotent-plus-square-zero decompositions is controlled by a finite-field dot-product equation. Its exact sharp multiplicity is \(q^{2r-1}+q^r-q^{r-1}\), strictly below the ordinary nil-clean index \(q^{2r}\).
