# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

The square is empty exactly when the domain and image are disjoint. Extensiveness is then strict at each source; weak order preservation forces the preimages of successive sinks to be consecutive blocks in the ordered domain. Consequently the signed source/sink word has nonnegative prefixes, and consuming the oldest outstanding sources at each sink is the unique inverse. Reversal with sign change and one appended −1 gives a preorder tree word of total −1; the appended −1 is necessarily the last leaf and its removal makes the transformation invertible. Leaves, unary vertices and branching vertices number d+1, n−d−r and r. The positive branch excesses sum to d and have binomial(d−1,r−1) ordered compositions; the type word count divided by n+1 by the cycle lemma gives the exact formula. No periodic word causes a multiplicity problem because the total is −1. For d=r=0 the tree is the unary chain. The leaf-generating equation follows by writing G=t(1+H) for the all-leaf weighted plane-tree series, which satisfies G=t+zG/(1−zG), and then applying Lagrange inversion to H=z(1+H)(1+tH). The rank bound and both extreme counts follow by substitution of the one or two admissible d values; n=1 has only the empty map, agreeing with the odd formula at m=0.
