# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

The condition \(f^2=0\) is exactly disjointness of domain and image. A weakly increasing map assigns consecutive groups of ordered sources to increasing sink values; strict extensiveness follows from disjointness. These facts give a reversible FIFO-queue encoding with nonnegative prefix sums and total zero. Reversal, negation, and a final \(-1\) identify the queue paths with preorder Łukasiewicz encodings of rooted plane trees. The cyclic lemma yields the stated positive-composition factorial formula for every admissible \((n,d,r)\), including the zero-map boundary. The Narayana and Catalan marginals follow by Lagrange inversion. Independent exhaustive and recurrence computations validate every coefficient through \(n=10\), and arithmetic identities through \(n=100\). Risk: finite verification cannot establish the universal result by itself; the combinatorial bijection and cycle-lemma hypotheses carry the proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual historical risk

The 2004 Laradji–Umar paper gives Catalan/Narayana enumeration for *different* full/order-decreasing semigroups, not the joint \((|\operatorname{dom}f|,|\operatorname{im}f|)\) distribution for square-zero maps in \(\mathcal{POE}_n\). Their 2013 preprint has lattice-path models for related partial semigroups but no direct equivalent of this square-zero rank formula. The 2012 full source of Dimitrova–Koppitz studies the same monoid with structural maximum-subsemigroup and rank questions, not this enumeration. Garba's 1994 primary paper studies nilpotent-generated subsemigroups in the broader partial order-preserving monoid. Actual semantic and phrase-based searches for zero-square, nilpotent-index-two, queue paths, Łukasiewicz trees, domain size, image rank, and the factorial formula revealed no covering assertion. Available published mathematical statements were compared for algebraic implication, not merely matching titles. Risk: a historical equivalent under a different noncrossing-partition or Catalan-path encoding may still exist, and no negative search proves novelty absolutely.

## Value — PASS

The index-two nilpotent stratum is natural in a partial-transformation semigroup, and domain width and image rank are fundamental distinct size invariants. The theorem determines **every feasible joint parameter pair** and its exact multiplicity for every finite degree; it identifies an unexpected Catalan-sized stratum and a Narayana domain distribution. The queue/tree bijection explains the coefficient, not merely computes a table. Exact extremal image-rank counts and attainable boundaries give reusable constraints on zero-square elements. This is not offered as a new enumeration of generic plane trees or a rebranding of known Narayana numbers.
