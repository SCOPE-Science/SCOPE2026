Among weakly increasing extensive partial maps on a finite chain, the square-zero maps correspond canonically to ordered plane trees. Their exact joint distribution by domain cardinality \(d\) and image rank \(r\) is \(\frac{n!}{(d+1)!r!(n-d-r)!}\binom{d-1}{r-1}\), yielding Narayana domain marginals and Catalan totals.
