The complete domain-rank polynomial of square-zero partial isometries of a finite cycle has an explicit divisor-sum formula with a sharp antipodal correction. Their proportion among all cycle partial isometries decays as a parity-dependent constant times \((\sqrt3/2)^n\), while a uniformly sampled square-zero element has asymptotically Gaussian rank centered at \(n/3\) with variance \(n/9\).
