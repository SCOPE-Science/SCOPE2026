For every \(q=2^f\) and every odd \(m\ge1\), square-zero elements of \(\mathbb F_q[D_{8m}]\) have an explicit complete left-regular rank enumerator, factored into one dihedral-eight polynomial and degree-weighted length-four matrix polynomials indexed by reciprocal irreducible factors of \(T^m-1\). The leading coefficient counts all acyclic regular-module differentials and is positive for every parameter.
