For any odd-residue finite chain ring of length two, every rank-\(r\) square-zero self-adjoint endomorphism over its residue field admits exactly \(q^{r^2+r(n-2r)+(n-2r)(n-2r+1)/2}\) self-adjoint square-zero lifts. Combined with polar-Grassmannian and symmetric-form counts, this yields the complete exact rank-resolved census in every dimension and for both equal- and mixed-characteristic rings.
