For monic degree-\(n\) polynomial solutions of \(f_1f_2=f_3f_4\) over \(\mathbb F_q[T]\), the fraction with all four entries squarefree tends to an explicit positive Euler product divided by \(1-q^{-1}\). This fraction is strictly smaller than the independent squarefree proportion \((1-q^{-1})^4\), with first-order large-field deficit \(1-8/q\) rather than \(1-4/q\).
