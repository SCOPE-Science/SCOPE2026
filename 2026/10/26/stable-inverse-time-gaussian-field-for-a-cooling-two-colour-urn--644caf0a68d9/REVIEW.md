# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The new claim is the stable functional limit around the terminal martingale. Conditional expectations give a precise martingale increment identity and bracket, and deterministic vanishing reversals force slow variation. On compact positive time intervals, the bracket converges uniformly almost surely, jumps vanish, and the stable martingale FCLT applies. Karamata's tail bound removes finite-block truncation with limiting squared error at most \(4/R\). Replacing the reference normalizer by the time-dependent one is uniform via tightness. The product criterion follows from summable quadratic log corrections. The 320 finite rational checks verify only finite-step identities, not asymptotic convergence.

## Originality — PASS, residual risk
Fan–Qi 2026 supplies the general time-dependent-memory first-order martingale limit but not this terminal-centered functional fluctuation field. James–James–Qi 2008 handles time-varying correlated Bernoulli sequences but states first-order Gaussian limits in the divergent-variance regime rather than a functional law around a convergent terminal martingale. González-Navarrete et al. 2024 and Tokumitsu 2026 prove functional or fluctuation laws at a fixed memory parameter, not arbitrary cooling to perfect recall. These objects are directly equivalent or closely related, rather than being dismissed by title. Gava–Rezende 2021 is a specific residual risk: the accessible abstract mentions Gaussian fluctuations and weak invariance for dependent Bernoulli sequences but its full text was not accessible, so possible implication of some claims cannot be excluded. Searches found no more directly covering record; absence in search results alone does not establish priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The path-level inverse-time Brownian covariance and stable independence from historical randomness extend a recent first-order time-dependent-memory theorem. They distinguish deterministic Gaussian noise after cumulative memory erasure from conditional Gaussian variance mixtures after preserved random composition, for every vanishing reversal sequence without monotonicity. This gives a natural complete second-order description for a cooling regime, rather than a single reparameterization or finite example.

## Limits
The result does not cover nonvanishing reversal probabilities, growing colour spaces, time zero, convergence rates, or an observable replacement for the terminal centering.
