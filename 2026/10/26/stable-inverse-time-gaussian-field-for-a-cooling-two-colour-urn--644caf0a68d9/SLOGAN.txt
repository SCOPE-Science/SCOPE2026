For every deterministic cooling schedule with opposite-colour probability tending to zero, a two-colour urn has a stable inverse-time Brownian fluctuation field around its terminal martingale drift, with conditional covariance \((1-Q_\infty^2)/\max\{s,t\}\). The noise is unmixed Gaussian exactly in the memory-erasure regime; surviving colour memory can retain a nonconstant Gaussian variance mixture.
