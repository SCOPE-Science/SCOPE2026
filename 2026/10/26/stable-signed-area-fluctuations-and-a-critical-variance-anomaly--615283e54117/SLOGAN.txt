For stationary Brownian motion with Pareto renewal resets, the signed time-average exhibits a stable-law regime of index \(2\alpha/3\) when \(2<\alpha<3\), a logarithmically corrected Gaussian limit at \(\alpha=3\), and an ordinary Gaussian regime above it. Exactly at criticality the normalized second moment is three times the variance of the weak Gaussian limit, revealing rare-excursion dominance missed by ordinary diffusion scaling.
