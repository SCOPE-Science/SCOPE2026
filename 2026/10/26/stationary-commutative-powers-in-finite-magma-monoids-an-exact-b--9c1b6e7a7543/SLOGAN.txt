For each labeled \(n\)-element set, the binary operations whose magma-monoid powers eventually stabilize to a commutative operation have exact limiting-rank polynomial \(n^{n^2-n-1}z(n+z)^{n-1}\). A stationary constant power occurs for exactly a proportion \(1/n^2\) of all operations, while the stabilized rank conditioned on convergence is one plus an exact binomial variable and tends to one plus a Poisson variable.
