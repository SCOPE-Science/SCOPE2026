For every positive reversible three-state birth–death chain, the complete graph-distance Lipschitz heat norm has a closed two-exponential profile and one time-independent extremizing potential. Fixing the invariant masses \(\pi_0,\pi_1,\pi_2\), the sharp amplification envelope over all jump conductances and times is \(1+\max\{\pi_0/(\pi_0+\pi_1),\pi_2/(\pi_1+\pi_2)\}\), attained only as a weak-edge degeneration rather than by an irreducible chain.
