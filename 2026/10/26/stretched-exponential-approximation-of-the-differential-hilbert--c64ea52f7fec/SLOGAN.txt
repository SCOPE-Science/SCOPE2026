In the compact two-weight regime \(-1<\alpha<0\), \(\beta>\alpha+2\), the differential Hilbert operator has explicit dyadic Gauss–Legendre finite-rank approximants with trace-norm error \(O(4^{-m}+2^{-J\theta})\) at rank at most \(Jm\), where \(\theta=\min\{\beta-\alpha-2,-\alpha\}/4\). Thus its singular values decay at least stretched-exponentially as \(e^{-c\sqrt n}\), and it belongs to every Schatten quasi-class \(\mathcal S_p\) for \(p>0\); no optimal decay rate is claimed.
