In uniform coupon sampling, the first repeated-label time and the full collection time have strictly negative covariance for every number of labels \(m\ge2\), with an exact positive-sum formula for its magnitude. Their conditional completion-time laws strictly decrease with the first-repeat index, while \(\operatorname{Cov}(B_m,C_m)\sim-\sqrt{\pi m/8}\) and \(m\operatorname{Corr}(B_m,C_m)\to-\sqrt{3/[2\pi(4-\pi)]}\).
