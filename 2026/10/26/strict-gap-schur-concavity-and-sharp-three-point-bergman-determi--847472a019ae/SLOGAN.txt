For three normalized Bergman point kernels anywhere on a fixed circle of radius \(0<r<1\), the Gram determinant is strictly Schur concave in the three successive angular gaps and is maximized uniquely by equally spaced points. Its exact maximum, with \(t=r^2\), is \(81t^3(t^3+2)(2t^3+1)/(1+t+t^2)^6\).
