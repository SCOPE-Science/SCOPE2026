For the Matsumoto–Yor two-species Boltzmann collision operator, unweighted Boltzmann \(H\) can increase even arbitrarily near every balanced GIG/Gamma equilibrium. Relative entropy to that equilibrium is the genuine Lyapunov functional: its exact dissipation is minus the KL divergence between the incoming independent-pair law and its collision pushforward, while \(H\) additionally contains a sign-indefinite logarithmic-Jacobian term.
