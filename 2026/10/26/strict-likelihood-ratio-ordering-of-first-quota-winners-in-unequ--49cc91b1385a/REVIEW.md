# Review of strict likelihood-ratio ordering for the first quota winner

**Correctness — PASS.** A finite multinomial-prefix count yields the complete joint probability in the exact domain of positive category weights, a finite number of types and an integer common quota of at least two. Strict log-concavity of factorial-truncated generating-function coefficients survives finite convolution by explicit order-two Cauchy–Binet witnesses. A second Cauchy–Binet calculation supplies a strictly positive adjacent marked-time determinant for every feasible time and every unequal pair of weights. Endpoint calculations and strictness have been checked; the proof does not extrapolate from finite experiments. The embedded exact-rational checker reproduces the joint masses by independent coefficient and absorbing-occupancy recurrences on 1,737 profiles and verifies small instances by full-word enumeration.

**Originality — PASS with residual historical risk.** Relevant primary full text of Camarri–Pitman (2000; public report 1998), Camarri (1998; public report 1998) and Aston–Martin (2005) was examined at the formulas and results level. They supply marginal repeat times, general marked finite-state calculations and/or asymptotic repeat-time distributions, but do not give the uniform strict likelihood-ratio order comparing rarity-conditioned quota-winner times. Published result searches and claim-level comparisons with existing research records found no statement covering the determinant theorem. Anderson–Sobel–Uppuluri (1982), a relevant direct quota-waiting paper, was accessible only by abstract; a full-text historical priority check remains outstanding and no claim that its entire text was examined is made. The inaccessible source, despite its relevance, does not specifically assert the stronger order in its accessible abstract; its risk is preserved.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Unequal-probability quota races are classical occupancy stopping problems. Their marked winning category carries substantially more information than the marginal waiting time. The strict likelihood-ratio comparison works at *every* quota and category count with arbitrary positive probability profiles, strengthens ordinary winner-frequency comparisons to a stochastic ordering of the entire conditional stopping-time distributions, and identifies exact extremal odds. It is a motivated structural theorem, not an arbitrary parameter computation or a routine change of notation.

**Limits.** Equal quotas, iid fixed positive weights, and finite support are required. Claims about heterogeneous quotas or dependent sampling are unproved. Historical priority has not been independently certified.
