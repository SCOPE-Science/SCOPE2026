In unequal-probability sampling, the later the first category completes a common quota of \(r\ge2\) appearances, the greater the conditional odds that a rarer category won: those odds increase strictly at every possible completion time. The same theorem holds for any finite number of categories and gives exact rare-versus-common odds \((p_i/p_j)^r\) at the earliest finish and \(p_i/p_j\) at the latest.
