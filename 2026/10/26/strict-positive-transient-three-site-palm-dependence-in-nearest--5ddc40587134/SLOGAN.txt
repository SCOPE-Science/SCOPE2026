At every intermediate deposition time \(0<t<1\), two distance-two occupancy indicators on opposite sides of a newly accepted site have strictly positive covariance conditional on that site being occupied, equal to \(2(t-t^2+t^3/3)/(e^{2t}-1)-4t^2/(e^{2t}-1)^2\). The exact transient higher-order dependence is explained by conditionally independent half-lines mixed over the successful central attempt time; its saturated gap specialization, already implied by known pair correlations, is supplied as a consistency check rather than claimed as new.
