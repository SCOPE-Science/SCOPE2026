For the known thermal-noise-induced merging of the two stationary peaks of a harmonically trapped run-and-tumble particle, the critical variance decreases strictly with the tumbling rate and has an exact negative covariance derivative. The merging peak positions exhibit the universal nondegenerate law \(m_q(\delta)\sim\sqrt{3(\delta_c(q)-\delta)}\), even though the boundary itself depends on the rate.
