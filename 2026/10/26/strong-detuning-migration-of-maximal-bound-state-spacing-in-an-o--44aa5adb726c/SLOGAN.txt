For an exactly solvable light–matter oscillator, the widest gap between neighboring bound-state energies moves to index \(n\sim0.233621921565\,\Omega^2/g\) under strong detuning, while the widest energy gap scales as \(1.070105058314\,g/\Omega\). These sharp constants follow from a nonuniform two-parameter Airy spectral limit, rather than the known fixed-detuning high-mode asymptotic.
