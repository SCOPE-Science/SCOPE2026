Deleting a nonperfect matching from a complete bipartite graph forces every unmatched-side color class to have odd size and every matched endpoint to be alone, except that a deleted pair may share one color. This yields an exact strong odd coloring polynomial for all named palettes and the parity-sensitive formula \(\chi_{\mathrm{so}}=t+\varepsilon(p-t)+\varepsilon(q-t)\) with explicit optimal-coloring multiplicities.
