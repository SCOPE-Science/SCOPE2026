# Scientific review: subalgebra–ideal separation in a free cube-zero algebra

## Correctness — PASS

The algebra \(A_r=V\oplus(V\otimes V)\) is associative, has \(A_r^3=0\), and has independent degree-two ordered words. Every linear subspace is encoded uniquely by \(U=\pi(S)\), \(T=S\cap A_r^2\), and a graph map into \(A_r^2/T\). Multiplication preservation is equivalent to \(U\otimes U\subseteq T\); two-sided ideal preservation strengthens this to \(V\otimes U+U\otimes V\subseteq T\). The resulting Gaussian factors and graph-map counts yield the two displayed dimension polynomials. The asymptotic separation follows by isolating projected dimensions zero and one, applying the Gaussian recursion, and bounding all larger projections by a quadratic-exponent gap. Independent direct enumeration matches all polynomial coefficients for the four declared small cases. The finite checks support the algebraic argument but are not used as its proof.

## Originality — PASS against inspected prior work

Bauer–Schein (arXiv:2202.10865, introduction and Theorems 1.1–1.2) give p-adic ideal-zeta rationality results for class-two nilpotent algebra amalgams, not finite-field enumerations of all subalgebras of the free associative degree-two truncation. Siconolfi–Vantomme–Voll (arXiv:2305.19665, introduction, Section 4, Proposition 4.4) compute subalgebra zeta functions for free class-two *Lie* rings over complete discrete valuation rings, with exterior-square multiplication rather than ordered associative tensor words. Mainellis (arXiv:2302.01422, introduction and Theorem 3.0.1) studies Schur-multiplier defects and classifications rather than subalgebra counts. Searches for equivalent names, including nonunital cube-zero algebras, truncated tensor algebras, associative class-two nilpotent algebras, and ideal-to-subalgebra asymptotics, returned no result proving both exact distributions or the different typical projection dimensions. Absence from searchable results is not a proof of publication priority; the unresolved risk is earlier use of these elementary graph coordinates in unindexed work or under alternative nomenclature.

## Value — PASS

Subalgebra and ideal enumeration are natural invariants in algebra-growth research. These formulas cover every finite field, generator count and subalgebra dimension, including nongraded subspaces. The asymptotic is not merely a restatement of a Gaussian count: the count of subalgebras is overwhelmingly governed by a one-dimensional image in the generator space, whereas almost every ideal is entirely within the square; the fraction of subalgebras that are ideals decays with the precise leading constant \(q-1\). That divergence is structurally relevant when comparing growth problems for free associative versus free Lie nilpotent objects. It is not claimed to answer higher nilpotency-class or p-adic enumeration questions.
