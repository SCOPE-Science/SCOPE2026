For the free nonunital associative algebra with cube-zero identity on \(r\) generators over \(\mathbb F_q\), explicit Gaussian-binomial polynomials count every subalgebra and every two-sided ideal by dimension. For fixed \(q\) and growing \(r\), almost every subalgebra projects to a line modulo the square, almost every ideal lies in the square, and the ideal-to-subalgebra ratio is asymptotic to \((q-1)q^{-r}\).
