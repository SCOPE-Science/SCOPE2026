The mathematical check reconstructs the claim from the orbit-average formula for the unstable expansion potential, the Livšic periodic-orbit criterion, specification/entropy counting for transitive Anosov flows, and Theorem 1.3 of arXiv:2402.05518v1. The contradiction is that a non-coboundary would force exponentially many periodic orbits with nonzero centered average, whereas every such orbit belongs to the exceptional set. The conclusion is \(K_g\equiv-\alpha^2\).

Limits: the result is restricted to closed negatively curved surfaces and a subexponential exceptional count. It does not establish a finite cutoff or address positive-exponential exceptions. Independent audit and independent validation have not been performed.
