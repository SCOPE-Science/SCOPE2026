The mathematical check reconstructs the exact conditional posterior expression, bounds the sum of later-list likelihoods between the second likelihood and the list-size multiple of that likelihood, and applies the stated large-deviation limits to the unseen tail. The proof supports only the strict-sign cases \(\Delta>0\) and \(\Delta<0\); it does not decide the boundary \(\Delta=0\). The model is a uniform random codebook with independent additive noise. No finite experiment is treated as an infinite proof. Independent audit and external validation have not been performed.
