For every prime \(p=6m+5\), the genus-three curves \(y^3=x^4+a x^2+1\) have exactly \(m\) reduced marked superspecial parameters, given by the roots of the explicit degree-\(m\) Hasse polynomial \([t^m](t^2+at+1)^{2m+1}\). Everywhere else in the smooth family, the Jacobian has \(p\)-rank two and \(a\)-number one.
