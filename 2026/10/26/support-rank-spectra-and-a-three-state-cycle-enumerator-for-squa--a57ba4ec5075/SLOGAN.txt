For the square-zero Artinian face algebra of a graph, the rank of multiplication by a linear form is determined exactly by its coefficient support and the induced support components, with bipartiteness relevant precisely outside characteristic two. On every cycle \(C_n\), \(n\ge4\), the complete finite-field rank distribution is the trace of one explicit \(3\times3\) transfer matrix, with a single characteristic-dependent full-support correction.
