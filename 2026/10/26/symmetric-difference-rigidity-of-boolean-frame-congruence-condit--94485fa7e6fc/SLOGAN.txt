For every Boolean algebra, the symmetric-difference condition \(x\oplus y\leq z\Rightarrow f(x)\oplus f(y)\leq f(z)\) is **equivalent** to preservation of binary joins, so it cannot witness strict nonadditivity. On an \(n\)-atom powerset algebra the condition holds for exactly \((2^n+1)^n\) unary operations, and the proposed nonadditive example in a 2026 paper has a three-set counterexample to the condition.
