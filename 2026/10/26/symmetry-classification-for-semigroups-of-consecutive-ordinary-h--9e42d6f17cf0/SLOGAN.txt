The semigroup generated by three consecutive ordinary hexagonal numbers \(n(2n-1)\), \((n+1)(2n+1)\), and \((n+2)(2n+3)\) is symmetric exactly when \(n\in\{2,3,6,7,8,18\}\), for every integer \(n\ge2\). A uniform gcd bound and twenty-four exact gluing checks give the complete classification, including the isolated exceptional index \(18\).
