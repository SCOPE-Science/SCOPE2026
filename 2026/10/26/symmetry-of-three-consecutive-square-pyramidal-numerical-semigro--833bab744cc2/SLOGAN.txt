The numerical semigroup generated by three consecutive square-pyramidal numbers \(Q(n)=\sum_{j=1}^n j^2\) is symmetric exactly for \(n=1,2\) or \(n\equiv0,4\pmod6\). For the infinite symmetric families its Frobenius number is \((4n^5+32n^4+101n^3+175n^2+102n-36)/36\), and its genus is half that number plus one-half.
