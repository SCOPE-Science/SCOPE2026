For every biclique \(K_{m,n}\), double-centering and its complement minimize projection norms onto the cycle and cut spaces under every row-column permutation-invariant ambient norm. In entrywise \(\ell_1\), the optimal cycle-minus-cut gap equals \(((m-2)(n-2)-2)/(mn)\), with a tie only for \(K_{3,4}\); moreover the orthogonal cycle projection is the unique **global** minimizer only on the square \(K_{2,2}\), and every other biclique admits a continuous family of distinct optimal cycle projections.
