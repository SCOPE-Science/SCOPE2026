Cyclic independent-set blow-ups of the directed triangle have exactly ten first-order theories at quantifier rank three. Their complete invariant is the set of rooted neighborhood-size triples \(\{(a\wedge3,b\wedge2,c\wedge2),\text{ cyclic rotations}\}\), with a single unexpected merger between the patterns \((2,2,3)\) and \((2,3,3)\); every class has a representative on at most nine vertices, sharply.
