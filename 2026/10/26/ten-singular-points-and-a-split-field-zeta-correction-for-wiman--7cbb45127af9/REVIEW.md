# Review of the characteristic-five Wiman sextic singularity and zeta claim

## Correctness

**PASS.** The equality of the sextic with the quadratic-coordinate pullback of \(abc+2(a+b+c)^3\) is an exact polynomial congruence. The nodal cubic has a unique geometric node; valuations of \(a/c\) and \(b/c\) at its three coordinate intersection points prove independence of two Kummer square classes and hence geometric integrality of the pullback. The complete Jacobian equations produce four nondegenerate nonsplit nodes and six cusps with exact local equation \(U^2+2V^3\). Their delta sum equals the sextic arithmetic genus, proving normalization genus zero, while rational unibranch cusp data supplies a rational point. Branchwise Frobenius counts give the exact zeta function over every extension. Finite-field enumerations independently reproduce the first two counts, without serving as a substitute for the geometric proof.

## Originality

**PASS, with residual literature risk.** Edge (1981) and Inoue--Kato (2005/2006) supply the classical equation and complex or good-characteristic geometry, but not this characteristic-five singular classification; Inoue--Kato explicitly exclude characteristic five. Giulietti--Kawakita--Lia--Montanucci (2018), Remark 3.1, already establish rationality of the characteristic-five curve, which is **not** claimed to be new. Their inspected statement does not determine the additional six cusps, the four nodes' nonsplit Frobenius action, the Jacobian scheme's length \(16\), or the singular-model zeta function. The present arithmetic count is not logically implied by knowing only that the normalization is rational: branch fields at the nodes matter. Relevant published mathematical-record searches yielded no statement dominating this refinement. Possible older local classifications or unpublished tables remain a risk.

## Mathematical value

**PASS.** The characteristic-five fibre of a classical genus-six plane model is a natural arithmetic object because the bad-prime special fibre controls direct reductions of the defining equation. Although its geometric rationality is known, the full singularity basket with Frobenius splitting and exact zeta correction determines previously unspecified local and arithmetic information for this specific integral model. This goes beyond merely recounting rational points: it proves a complete local classification that forces all extension-field counts.

## Limitations and evidence

The singular model is not a stable arithmetic model, and neither the generic Jacobian's conductor nor semistable reduction is determined. The full original 1897 Wiman article was not inspected; Edge and Inoue--Kato reproduce its relevant defining equation. The 2005 preprint record has year-only public-date evidence; the independently day-resolved journal publication record is dated July 14, 2006. A 2018 arXiv paper explicitly covers geometric rationality at the exceptional prime and must be credited for that stronger background component.

The standalone checker verifies polynomial identities, all \(\mathbb F_5\)-rational singular points, \(\mathbb F_5\)- and \(\mathbb F_{25}\)-counts, and tangent-type invariants. The proof, not these finite runs, establishes geometric completeness and the formula for every positive integer \(r\).
