For the standard two-level Richardson-extrapolated backward-Euler heat step on a connected simple unweighted graph of diameter at most \(3\), every graph on at most \(9\) vertices is entrywise positive at all sufficiently small positive times. Ten vertices are necessary and sufficient for a negative entry arbitrarily close to time zero: a sharp example has \((L^4)_{uv}=4\) and \((T(h))_{uv}=-3h^4/2+O(h^5)\).
