With exactly three candidates, tie-independent house-monotonicity failure under ordinary Proportional Approval Voting requires at least ten voters, and all three labeled ten-voter extremizers are relabelings of one profile: two \(B\)-only, three \(AB\), three \(AC\), and two \(C\)-only ballots. The example already has voter-interval preferences; its optimal singleton is \(A\) but its optimal pair is \(BC\).
