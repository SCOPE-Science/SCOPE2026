For cyclic permutations of one four-symbol window, the worst ternary output ball has size \(3n-8\) at every length \(n\ge4\), even though a four-window cannot contain four distinct ternary symbols. The complete infinite census of maximizing input words has an explicit degree-ten rational generating function and grows as \(c(1.931675158\ldots)^n\), rather than as all \(3^n\) ternary words.
