On the characteristic-two Fermat cubic threefold, the \(165\) Hermitian cone-point elliptic curves on its Fano surface have Gram spectrum \(33^1,0^{120},(-12)^{44}\), and their classes rationally generate the entire rank-\(45\) geometric Néron–Severi group. This supplies an explicit geometric realization of the previously known maximal Picard rank.
