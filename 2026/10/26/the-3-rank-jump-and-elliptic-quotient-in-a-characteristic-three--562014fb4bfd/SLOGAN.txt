In characteristic \(3\), the cyclic Klein quartic pencil \(X^3Y+Y^3Z+Z^3X+\lambda XYZ(X+Y+Z)=0\) is smooth precisely away from \(\lambda=\pm1\). Its elliptic quotient has \(j=\lambda^6/(\lambda^2-1)\), and the covering genus-three curve has \(3\)-rank zero only at \(\lambda=0\), with every other smooth member ordinary.
