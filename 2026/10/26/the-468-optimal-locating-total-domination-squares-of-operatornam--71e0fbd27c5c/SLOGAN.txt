The point–line incidence graph of \(\operatorname{PG}(2,3)\) has locating-total domination number \(10\), and its \(1{,}872\) minimum configurations are parametrized by a nonincident point–line pair together with three local geometric choices. Their single-vertex exchange graph is exactly a disjoint union of \(468\) four-cycles, so minimum-preserving motion is possible but confined to four-state islands.
