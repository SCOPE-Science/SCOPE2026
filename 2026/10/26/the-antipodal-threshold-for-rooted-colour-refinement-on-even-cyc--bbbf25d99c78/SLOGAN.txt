A single marked vertex makes counting colour refinement reveal the coordinate-distance histograms of \(C_{2r}^{\square d}\), for every \(r\ge3\), one entire distance shell per round; it stabilizes sharply at round \(rd-1\) with a Gaussian-binomial colour profile. The four-cycle is an exact exception: \(C_4^{\square d}\) is a hypercube and remembers only total distance, with \(2d+1\) rather than \(\binom{d+2}{2}\) final colours for \(d\ge2\).
