The characteristic-three Ballico–Hefez quartic's minimal resolution has Frobenius spectrum \(3^{14},(-3)^8\), hence zeta function \(1/[(1-T)(1-9T)(1-3T)^{14}(1+3T)^8]\). Its arithmetic Picard rank jumps from \(14\) over \(\mathbb F_3\) to \(22\) over \(\mathbb F_9\), although it is geometrically the familiar supersingular K3 surface of Artin invariant one.
