**Correctness — PASS.** The normal bundle formula gives \(c_3=-3H^3\); since \(\int_VH^3=8\), \(\chi(V)=-24\) and \(b_3=28\). Eight isolated holomorphic fixed points contribute \(+1\) each to the Lefschetz number. The even-degree traces sum to \(4\), so \(\operatorname{tr}(\sigma|H^3)=-4\), giving eigenspace dimensions \(12,16\). Hodge symmetry gives dimensions \(6,8\) on \(H^{2,1}\).
