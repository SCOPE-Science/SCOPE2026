All \(P_3\)-convex vertex sets of every circular ladder \(C_n\square K_2\) have an exact cardinality distribution encoded by a rational generating function with a degree-seven denominator. The largest proper convex sets have size \(2n-4\) for \(n\ge4\), with exactly \(n\) maximizers for \(n\ge5\) (six for \(n=4\)).
