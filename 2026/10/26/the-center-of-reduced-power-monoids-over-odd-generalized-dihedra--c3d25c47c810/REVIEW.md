# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For a nontrivial finite abelian odd-order H, commutation of A with every {1,g} is equivalent to commutation with every identity-containing B by distribution over unions. Taking complements gives C∩Cg=C∩gC. In additive notation on H, the rotation tests give S∩(S-k)=S∩(S+k); testing at a∈S with k=b-a shows closure under x↦2a-x. Such reflections compose to translations by twice each difference; multiplication by two is invertible on every subgroup of H, yielding S=a+K for K generated by the differences, and then K=S-S. Conversely a coset passes every rotation test. The reflection tests are automatic for S empty; otherwise the rotation component at r∈R says 2r stabilizes S, equivalent to r∈K, and the reflection component at any t∈S says membership of x and -x in R agrees for every x. These conditions suffice as well. There are [H:K] cosets, and (|K|-1)/2 inverse pairs outside the identity, giving exactly the subgroup sum plus the 2^(|H|-1) unrestricted branch. The subgroup counts for the cyclic and elementary abelian groups of order nine give 287 and 305. /workspace/branches/independent_dihedral.py tested direct products against every B for H=C3, and direct two-point products for C5,C7,C9,C3×C3, with counts 9,25,79,287,305 and element-wise agreement with the classification; the general proof uses the above subgroup argument, not finite extrapolation.
