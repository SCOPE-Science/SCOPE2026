For every finite odd-order abelian \(H\), the center of the reduced power monoid of \(\operatorname{Dih}(H)\) admits a complete coset-and-inversion classification and has exactly \(2^{|H|-1}+\sum_{K\le H}|H:K|2^{(|K|-1)/2}\) elements. The count detects the subgroup lattice: it is \(287\) for \(H=C_9\), but \(305\) for \(H=C_3\times C_3\).
