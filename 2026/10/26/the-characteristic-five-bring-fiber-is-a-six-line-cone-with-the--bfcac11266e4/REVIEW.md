# Mathematical review

## Correctness — PASS
The original five-coordinate equations transform *scheme-theoretically* into a quadratic/cubic complete intersection independent of the vertex coordinate. The nonsingular conic and split cubic meet in six distinct rational directions, saturating their Bézout intersection number. This proves reducedness and geometric completeness rather than merely enumerating finite points. The six-line decomposition gives the singular locus, the normalization, the Hilbert–Samuel multiplicity, \(p_a=4\), \(\delta=9\) and all-extension point counts. The component action is a faithful \(S_5\)-action on the six conic points, identified with \(\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbf F_5)\) by exact group orders. The standalone script independently recomputes key finite checks. Limits: it does not identify the stable reduction of the generic fiber.

## Originality — PASS, qualified
The central overlapping geometry in Korchmáros–Lia–Timpanella, arXiv:2112.10886, treats power-sum curves but explicitly restricts positive characteristic to \(p\ge7\) and the number of coordinates to at most \(p-1\). The present five-coordinate characteristic-five case is outside those assumptions. Braden–Disney-Hogg, arXiv:2208.13692, records the symmetric model and its characteristic-zero geometry while setting positive characteristic aside. The exact scheme, six rational lines, \(\delta\)-invariant and exceptional six-component \(S_5\)-action were not identified in those inspected results or the targeted mathematical-record searches. This is a best-knowledge novelty assessment, not a proof of priority; the most important residual risk is a classical or modular-model source explicitly treating this naive special fiber.

## Value — PASS
Characteristic five is the first excluded boundary for the five-coordinate power-sum family, not an arbitrarily selected finite-field computation. Describing the entire geometric failure of smoothness as a reduced six-line cone, including the normalization discrepancy, explains the exclusion structurally. The exceptional degree-six action is a natural concrete realization of \(S_5\cong\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbf F_5)\) arising from the same degeneration. Exact extension-field point counts and the local arithmetic genus identify meaningful geometric invariants of the specific special fiber.

## Limitations and closest literature
This concerns the naive scheme-theoretic reduction of a specified integral complete-intersection model, not a stable or minimal model. Prior work may include equivalent descriptions under other models or older names. The result is not independently verified and should not be represented as such.
