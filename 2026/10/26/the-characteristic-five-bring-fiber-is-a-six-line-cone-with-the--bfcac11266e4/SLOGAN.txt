The literal characteristic-five power-sum model of Bring's genus-four curve becomes six \(\mathbf F_5\)-lines through one point, with arithmetic genus \(4\) and singularity invariant \(\delta=9\). Its component permutation action realizes \(S_5\cong\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbf F_5)\), and the singular fiber has exactly \(6q+1\) points over every \(\mathbf F_q\) with \(q=5^m\).
