In characteristic \(7\), the reduced projective dual of the Klein quartic is the rational quintic \(A^3C^2-3A^2B^3+2B^2C^3=0\), with exactly three \(A_2\) cusps and one wild \(A_6\) cusp. Its intrinsic singular scheme has length \(13\), and the Gauss map is given by an explicit basepoint-free quintic normalization.
