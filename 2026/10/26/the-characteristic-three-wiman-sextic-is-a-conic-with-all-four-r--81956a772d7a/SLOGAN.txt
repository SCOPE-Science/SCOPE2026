The characteristic-three reduction of the classical Wiman sextic is the smooth conic \(X^2+Y^2+Z^2=0\) together with all four of its \(\mathbb F_3\)-rational tangent lines. Its exact singularity basket is \(4A_3+6A_1\), with normalization defect \(14\) and intrinsic Tjurina length \(18\); for every \(r\ge1\) it has exactly \(5\cdot3^r-5\) points over \(\mathbb F_{3^r}\), and all ten \(\mathbb F_3\)-points are singular.
