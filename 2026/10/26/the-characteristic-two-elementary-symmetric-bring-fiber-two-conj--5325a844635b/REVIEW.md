## Originality — PASS with residual risk

The primary Bring article (Braden–Disney-Hogg, arXiv:2208.13692, Introduction and §2) studies the characteristic-zero **power-sum** model and expressly does not pursue positive characteristic. The other closely titled primary work (Korchmáros–Lia–Timpanella, arXiv:2112.10886, abstract and introduction) restricts positive-characteristic conclusions to primes at least seven. They do not imply this characteristic-two elementary-symmetric model's two-component decomposition or zeta function. Related discussions of twisted cubics and nodes concern different objects; the existing special-fiber claim at characteristic five concerns the **power-sum** equations, not this model or prime. Exact-form, alias, dual-quadric and stronger-coverage searches identified no equivalent result, but unsuccessful discovery does not certify novelty; unindexed historical reductions remain a residual risk.
