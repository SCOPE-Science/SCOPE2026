The elementary-symmetric model of Bring's genus-four curve degenerates in characteristic two into two Frobenius-conjugate twisted cubics meeting at exactly five ordinary rational nodes. Its \(\mathbb F_{2^n}\)-point count is \(5\) for odd \(n\) and \(2^{n+1}-3\) for even \(n\), with zeta function \((1+T)^4/((1-T)(1-4T^2))\).
