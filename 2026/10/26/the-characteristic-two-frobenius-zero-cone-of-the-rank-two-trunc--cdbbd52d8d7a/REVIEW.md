## Correctness — PASS

The square-zero derivation equations are independently reconstructed from the action on the four-element basis. Every zero-constant solution satisfies the rank-one nilpotent matrix relation \(L^2=0\), and the explicit degenerations show that no closed-stratum component lies outside the closure of the smooth dense open stratum. ## Value — PASS

This is a complete geometric and arithmetic determination of the natural restricted height-one \(p\)-nilpotent locus for the first two-generator characteristic-two truncated Witt derivation algebra. The excluded small characteristic matters mathematically: a higher nilpotent restricted power need not equal the square-zero condition, so the locus is a substantive invariant rather than a parameter substitution into the classical characteristic-\(p>2\) theorem. Uniform all-extension-field point counts and the geometric boundary degeneration complement each other. The result does not pretend to classify higher-dimensional Witt algebras or all nilpotent derivations.
