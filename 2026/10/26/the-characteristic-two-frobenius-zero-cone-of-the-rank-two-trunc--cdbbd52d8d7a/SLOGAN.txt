For the eight-dimensional characteristic-two restricted Witt derivation algebra of \(k[x,y]/(x^2,y^2)\), the reduced square-zero cone is an irreducible rational fourfold. Over every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\) of characteristic two it has exactly \(q^4+q^3-q\) points, with a geometrically dense nonzero-constant stratum and an explicitly counted boundary.
