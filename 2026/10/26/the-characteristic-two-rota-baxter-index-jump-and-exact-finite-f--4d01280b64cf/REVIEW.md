# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof treats arbitrary ground characteristic directly, before using any source's characteristic-zero conclusions. Substituting basis elements into the weight-zero Rota–Baxter identity forces scalar coefficients of the operator to vanish and gives exactly \(L^2=0\) and \(2L(v)=0\). Conversely, these relations make both sides of the identity zero. The formula \(P^2(1)=L(v)\) gives the sharp characteristic-two index jump. The orbit proof uses the full centralizer of a square-zero Jordan form, not merely examples, and shows transitivity on each of the four rank/flag strata. The count \({n\brack r}_q{n-r\brack r}_q|\mathrm{GL}_r(q)|\) follows from image, kernel, and quotient-to-image isomorphism choices. The asymptotic bound follows from explicit upper/lower Gaussian-product inequalities. The standalone exact-arithmetic checker exhaustively tests the identity, ranks, flag strata, and automorphism orbits in the indicated small prime fields. No finite test is used as proof for infinitely many fields or dimensions.

## Originality — PASS, with remaining risk
The nearest direct source is Farooq, *Rota Baxter Operators on Truncated Polynomial Algebras*, arXiv:2605.15670 (2026-05-15), which explicitly assumes characteristic zero and obtains square-zero operators. The present work does not claim that result as novel. Its main differential assertion is that in characteristic two the unit image is not constrained by \(L(v)=0\), yielding nilpotency exponent three, together with a complete all-finite-field orbit structure and probability law. Gubarev's *Rota–Baxter operators on unital algebras*, arXiv:1805.00723 (2018), supplies the general concept of Rota–Baxter index and characteristic-zero bounds; the relevant statements do not yield this characteristic-two classification or the finite-field formulas. Searches of mathematical records for the precise characteristic-two algebra, marked-square-zero similarity orbits, finite-field counts, and nilpotency-index laws identified no covering statement. A published record concerning weight-zero operators on the four-dimensional exterior algebra over \(\mathbb F_2\) concerns a different algebra with different multiplication, so its classification does not imply this one. The underlying Jordan module technique is standard, and an implicit prior description of the moduli or modular identity remains possible. The outcome is a self-review originality assessment, not an assertion of proven uniqueness.

## Value — PASS
The coefficient-characteristic boundary changes the uniform nilpotency exponent of a natural associative local algebra from two to three. In characteristic two, the index-three behavior is not exceptional: it occurs with asymptotic probability one as the radical dimension grows over any fixed finite field. The theorem also gives an exact isomorphism classification, orbit sizes, and enumerator across arbitrary finite fields. These are natural structural invariants of the first infinitesimal neighborhood and make the characteristic dependence quantitatively precise, rather than merely observing a small counterexample.
