The proof derives the class by separating the three radical Hilbert functions of length-three modules, describing their surjection loci, and dividing by explicitly given special automorphism groups whose torsors are locally trivial. The accompanying `verify.py` checks polynomial integrality and rank-one specializations, a degree-two sample, and independently enumerates all codimension-three stable subspaces of the rank-one truncated maximal ideal over the two-element field.
