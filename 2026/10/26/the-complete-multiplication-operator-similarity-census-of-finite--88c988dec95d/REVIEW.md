# Review: multiplication-operator similarity census

## Correctness
PASS. The operator on each permutation cycle is explicitly \(L_x(e_i)=a_ix_i e_{\pi(i)}\); nonzero coordinate scaling makes the weights independent and complete. If every edge is nonzero, the operator has a cyclic vector and exact annihilator \(t^d-c\), without a characteristic restriction. Otherwise the cut weighted cycle splits into Jordan chains. The circular-gap count \(d(z-1)!/\prod m_r!\) is reconstructed from distinguished starting edges, and independent cycles multiply their distributions. The involution example is checked by solving the idempotent equations over \(\mathbb F_7\). Exhaustive independent finite checks agree; they are not the proof.

## Originality
PASS against inspected relevant material. The 2013 operator/enveloping-algebra treatment is stronger background about the same family but does not enumerate **all** similarity classes and their multiplicities. The 2018 and 2020 two-permutation treatments focus on nilpotents, idempotents, and decompositions; Paniello discusses Markov permutation examples over \(\mathbb R\); the 2022 and 2023 papers address automorphism or isomorphism invariants. Exact-equivalence checks examined cyclic weighted matrices, nilpotent Jordan decompositions, and the finite-field rational-canonical count. No examined source states the complete multiset formula, but an equivalent older monomial-matrix spectrum count remains a real priority risk. Published mathematical-index similarity-search hits on cyclic graphical groups, monomial rigidity, and finite dual-number Jordan lifts address different objects or consequences, not the full operator census.

## Value
PASS. For a naturally defined class of nonassociative algebras the formula gives, for every finite field and every dimension, the complete empirical similarity distribution of all multiplication operators, refining invertibility/rank/characteristic-polynomial statistics to rational canonical structure. It handles inseparable cases and arbitrary disjoint cycle types at once. The concrete nonisomorphic \(\mathbb F_7\) pair shows the exact limit of this natural operator invariant. This is not a claim that each constituent Jordan fact is new.

Equivalence to previously unlocated finite-field monomial-matrix counting is not fully excluded. Independent mathematical audit is required for stronger certification.
