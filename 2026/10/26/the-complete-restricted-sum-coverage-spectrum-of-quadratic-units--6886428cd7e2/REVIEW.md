# Mathematical review

**Correctness — PASS.** The exact fiber decomposition and lifting mechanism are proved, the three-fold restricted sum estimate handles all interior cardinalities, and the boundary/small-prime conditions are classified without omitted cases. The finite verifier confirms each cardinality and each target for eight small primes, but serves only as a check of the infinite proof.

**Originality — PASS (with residual risk).** The available public abstracts for recent prime-power representation work treat a different two-summand mixture, while earlier Galois-ring subset-sum literature gives asymptotic estimates and sufficient representation criteria. The latter articles were not accessible in full and could implicitly imply part of the classification; their inapplicability is *not* asserted. No complete equivalent sharp all-prime cardinality spectrum was identified by the searches performed.

**Value — PASS.** The theorem gives a natural all-cardinality coverage classification, a stable interval of universal cardinalities, a sharp quadratic-character boundary, and the unexpected three-target hole at the midpoint modulo \(25\). Its proof provides a reusable exact lifting construction.

Limitations: only nonzero squares modulo odd prime squares; no extension claimed to general Galois rings, higher exponents, or different power-residue subgroups. The stated originality risks require future independent checking.
