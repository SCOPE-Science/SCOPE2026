Modulo the square of any prime \(p\ge7\), every target is a sum of exactly \(k\) distinct quadratic units for every cardinality \(3\le k\le p(p-1)/2-3\); the two-summand boundary depends precisely on \(p\bmod4\). The unique exceptional prime-square behavior occurs modulo \(25\), where only four- and six-element sums cover everything, while five-element sums miss exactly three targets.
