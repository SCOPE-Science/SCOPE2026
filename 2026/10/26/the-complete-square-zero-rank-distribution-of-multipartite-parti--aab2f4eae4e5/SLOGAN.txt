For every finite complete multipartite graph, the square-zero elements of its partial-isometry monoid have an exact rank polynomial indexed by injections between active vertex parts. For \(K_{a,b}\), the largest rank is \(\max\{\lfloor a/2\rfloor+\lfloor b/2\rfloor,\min(a,b)\}\): an even-order graph loses one rank below the unrestricted bound exactly when its two part sizes are odd and unequal.
