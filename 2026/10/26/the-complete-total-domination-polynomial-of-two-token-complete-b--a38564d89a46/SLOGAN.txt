Every total dominating set of the two-token graph of \(K_{m,n}\), for \(m,n\ge2\), splits into an edge-cover choice on at least one side and a mixed-token incidence matrix with at most one empty row and column. This gives the complete all-cardinality polynomial, \(\gamma_t(F_2(K_{m,n}))=\max\{m,n\}-1+\lceil\min\{m,n\}/2\rceil\), and an exact census of all minimum sets.
