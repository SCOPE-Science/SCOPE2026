The plane-filling sextic \(X^5Y+X^5Z-X^3Y^3+X^3Z^3+XZ^5+Y^5Z-Y^3Z^3=0\) over \(\mathbb F_3\) has an explicitly determined irreducible degree-\(20\) Weil numerator, with coefficients beginning \(1,9,46,173,527\). Its genus-ten Jacobian is ordinary and simple over \(\mathbb F_3\); the plane-filling construction, smoothness and prior ordinarity are not claimed as new.
