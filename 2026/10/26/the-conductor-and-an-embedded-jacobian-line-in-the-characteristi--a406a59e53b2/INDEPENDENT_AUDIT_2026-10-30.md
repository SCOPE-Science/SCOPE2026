## correctness

PASS

The reduced characteristic-two quartic discriminant is Q=ad²+bcd+eb², irreducible by primitivity as a polynomial linear in a. Exact differentiation in F2 gives J=(d²,cd,bd,bc,b²)=(b,d)(b,c,d)=(b,d)∩(b,c,d)², radical (b,d) and embedded associated prime (b,c,d). The monomial quotient consists of k[a,c,e] and the b/d classes times k[a,e], so h(n)=binom(n+2,2)+2n for every n≥0. The displayed P1×P2 parametrization satisfies Q identically, with inverse [u:v]=[b:d] off the singular plane. Its projective fibers are finite quadratic length-two schemes on the plane and singleton fibers off it; the proper morphism from a normal smooth source is finite birational, giving normalization. On e=1, B=k[c,d,t], a=t²+ct,b=dt; B/A generated by t with annihilator (b,d); homogeneous missing Segre generator of degree 1 gives quotient i_*O_P²(-1). The marked-root equation x²=d/b=1/t is inseparable of degree 2 in the generic rational function field. Checked the exact characteristic-two Jacobian, Segre substitution and Hilbert series independently, degrees 0–19. These do not by themselves prove global normalization or inseparability; those follow from the ring/field arguments.
