In characteristic \(2\), the reduced discriminant of binary quartics is a nonnormal cubic threefold normalized by \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^2\), with conductor a plane and normalization quotient \(\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^2}(-1)\). Its intrinsic Jacobian scheme has a line embedded inside that plane, while the ordinary marked-double-root incidence is generically purely inseparable of degree \(2\), rather than being the normalization.
