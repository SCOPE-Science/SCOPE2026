The 45-state two-token exclusion graph of the Petersen graph has an exact critical group with 16 nontrivial invariant factors, including three copies of \(\mathbb Z/191520\mathbb Z\). Its spanning-tree count is \(49,253,533,675,382,566,432,014,336,000\), and an exact prime-power Smith computation establishes its full sandpile-group structure.
