A nested weak-majority ratio modality on uniformly fair-labelled regular trees has a unique critical ratio \(1/2\); its root probability first fails to converge at modal depth two because even-branching ties amplify to the limit \(\Phi(\sqrt{2/\pi})\), while odd branching stays exactly fair. At all fixed depths at least three, even branching saturates to one for weak majority and zero for strict majority, whereas odd branching stays at one half.
