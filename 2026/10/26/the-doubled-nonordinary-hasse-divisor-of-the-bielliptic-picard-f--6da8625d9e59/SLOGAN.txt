For every prime \(p\equiv1\pmod6\), the nonordinary locus of \(Y^3Z=X^4+aX^2Z^2+Z^4\) is an explicitly determined doubled divisor: its \(m=(p-1)/6\) distinct geometric parameters are the simple roots of \(Q_m(a)=[t^{3m}](t^2+at+1)^{2m}\). Precisely at these roots the Jacobian has \(p\)-rank one and \(a\)-number two; all other smooth fibers are ordinary.
