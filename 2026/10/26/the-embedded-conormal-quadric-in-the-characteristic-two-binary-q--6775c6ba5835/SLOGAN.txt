For the binary-quadratic resultant hypersurface in characteristic two, the reduced singular support is \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^2\), but its intrinsic singular scheme contains a square-zero conormal thickening along a quadric \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\). The embedded bundle is \(\mathcal O(-2,-2)\oplus\mathcal O(-2,-1)\), and its presence determines the singular scheme's exact Hilbert series.
