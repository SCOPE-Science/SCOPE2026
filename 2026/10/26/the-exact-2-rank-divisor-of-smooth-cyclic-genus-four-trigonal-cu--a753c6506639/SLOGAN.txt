For every smooth characteristic-two cyclic trigonal genus-four curve \(y^3=x^5+a_4x^4+a_3x^3+a_2x^2+a_1x+a_0\), the Jacobian has \(a\)-number two and \(2\)-rank zero exactly when \(a_3=0\), otherwise \(2\)-rank two. The marked rank-zero locus is a nonempty reduced divisor, with Cartier nilpotent of exact index three.
