For Lucas-balancing numbers \(C_n\), the gcd of sums of \(k\) consecutive squares over every starting index is exactly \(\gcd(k,B_k)\), where \(B_k\) is the balancing number. This supplies a closed form for the Lucas-balancing squared-sum case posed in Question 30 of Mbirika--Schrader--Spilker (2023).
