# Scientific review: the first attracting period-three eye

## Correctness
The analytic proof solves the full three-branch affine return equation, explicitly verifies all six strict branch inequalities (not just the two defining the parameter interval), and proves that its return multiplier is less than one. The interval width factorization gives exact sharp endpoints for the specified carry code. A separate short integer-lift argument excludes attracting two-cycles having two right-branch visits, establishing the sharp global minimal eye period. The exact rational three-cycle and 231 rational verifier assertions are auxiliary checks, not substitutes for universal reasoning.

## Originality
The closest primary source (Bhattacharyya--Banerjee, arXiv:2510.11441v1) proves that an existing multi-minus attracting cycle belongs to an eye, but its period-three construction is a single-minus tongue. Its eye illustrations concern period five. Thus the source's results neither imply existence of a period-three eye nor determine this exact carry-specific parameter strip or the minimal eye period. Records of generic period-three orbits in other maps do not imply the same branch itinerary in this parameter family. Historical priority under another nomenclature is still possible.

## Value
The source introduces a distinctive second class of attracting components and studies them experimentally; determining their earliest possible primitive period is a natural structural cutoff. The exact band additionally identifies an explicit two-dimensional region of genuine attracting eye dynamics with algebraic endpoints, rather than an isolated numerical point. The result does not purport to classify all eye components or solve realization questions for arbitrary longer words.
