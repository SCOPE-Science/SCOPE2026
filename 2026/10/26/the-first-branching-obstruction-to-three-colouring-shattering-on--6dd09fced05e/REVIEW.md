# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS

The claim is finite exact uniqueness through seven vertices together with a universal hereditary obstruction. The induced-four-path argument and the center-colour exclusion argument are human-checkable deductions with explicit cases. Two separately implemented enumeration procedures (Prüfer/canonical-tree generation plus exhaustive colour traces; independent unlabeled-tree generation plus list-colouring dynamic programming) both check all \(1687\) admissible marked pairs over tree orders one through seven, obtaining precisely one failure, the named spider. The \(32\) patterns of an explicit five-query witness are replayed separately. Assumptions cover nonempty connected trees, labelled palette size exactly three and one target colour per selected vertex.

## Originality — PASS, subject to publication-priority risk

Closest sources considered include arXiv:1708.05118 (sampling-based graph reconstruction from proper colourings), arXiv:2203.12211 (general Sauer–Shelah and tree-dimension theory) and arXiv:2211.01443 (approximation hardness for general explicit VC/Littlestone dimension). Available texts and the published research database were searched for equivalences, generalizations and coverage of the precise marked \((3,2,1)\)-spider obstruction; none of the inspected statements implies this first finite branching exception. Search incompleteness and unindexed graph-colouring shattering results remain residual risks; a negative search is not a proof of priority.

## Value — PASS

The invariant is the VC shattering of coordinate-colour tests on proper colourings, a natural combinatorial learning family. Induced four-vertex paths give the immediate local obstruction, tempting an induced-star-forest characterization. The theorem gives the sharp smallest order, a unique structural failure and an exact quantitative drop from six to five; the obstruction is inherited by every larger tree containing the marked spider. It isolates the first genuinely branching obstruction to a path-based heuristic rather than an arbitrary finite statistic. No unrestricted complete classification is claimed.

## Limits

The positive characterization is proved only up to seven vertices by exhaustive enumeration, not for all trees. Structural necessity of the spider obstruction is universal. No absolute publication-priority guarantee is made.
