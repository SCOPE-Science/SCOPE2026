All binary circular words of length at most \(11\) are reconstructed up to rotation and reversal by the histograms of their consecutive cyclic window compositions. At length \(12\) exactly two of the \(224\) bracelets collide, leaving \(223\) distinguishable observations; those two words yield a collision at every positive multiple of \(12\), while a strictly weaker autocorrelation measurement already has \(23\) collisions at length \(12\).
