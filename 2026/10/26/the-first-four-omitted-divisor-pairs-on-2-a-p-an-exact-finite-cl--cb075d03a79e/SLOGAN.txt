Among integers \(2^a p\) with \(p\) an odd prime, the first strongly pseudoperfect representation omitting exactly four complementary divisor pairs occurs at \(630194176=2^{20}\cdot601\), with a unique such set of pairs. No three-pair omission occurs at any exponent \(a\leq27\), while exactly three prime-power–times-prime integers at these exponents admit a four-pair omission.
