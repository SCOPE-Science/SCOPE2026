For dimension-two linear codes, the ordinary Hamming weight enumerator determines monomial equivalence over \(\mathbb F_2\) and \(\mathbb F_3\), but first fails over \(\mathbb F_4\) at blocklength \(10\): exactly two inequivalent codes share the exceptional spectrum, at exact distortion \(100/81\). An infinite family of Hamming-isospectral, strictly \(\mathbb F_4\)-linearly inequivalent pairs has sharp distortion \(\bigl((4t+10)/(4t+9)\bigr)^2\) for every integer \(t\ge0\), tending to \(1\); the pairs are equivalent if field automorphisms are also permitted.
