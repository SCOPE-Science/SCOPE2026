For edge-indexed two-variable conjunctions queried by vertex subsets, every graph on at most seven vertices reaches the information-theoretic Littlestone bound except precisely two seven-vertex, sixteen-edge graphs: a clique \(K_6\) with a leaf attached, and a clique \(K_6\) with one edge subdivided. Both lose exactly one unit of online shattering depth; these are the first such failures by graph order.
