The fourth Fejér summation operator is the first whose sharp one-step oscillation contraction occurs at a **non-antipodal** pair of circle points. Its exact constant is \(0.883882377829196849\ldots\), attained as a kernel-pair separation at angle \(2.4517828410692279\ldots\) determined by a unique quintic root; the first two nontrivial orders optimize antipodally.
