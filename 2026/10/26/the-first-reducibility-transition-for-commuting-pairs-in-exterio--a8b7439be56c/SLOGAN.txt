The reduced commuting-pair variety of an ordinary exterior algebra is irreducible through five generators, but at six generators it has exactly two rational irreducible components of unequal dimensions \(112\) and \(115\), over every algebraically closed field of odd characteristic. The six-generator finite-field commuting-pair count is \(q^{115}+q^{112}+q^{111}-q^{106}-q^{105}+q^{96}-q^{95}\).
