# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** The completion time for a fixed scattered pattern is a sum of independent geometric waits. The palindrome language has the exact even/odd decomposition used to prove the rational generating function. The unique dominant positive pole gives the exact exponential rate and residue. The nonnegative identity \(\pi-(4\pi/(1+4\pi))^2=\pi(4\pi-1)^2/(1+4\pi)^2\) proves the all-alphabet bound with equality only for four equal probabilities. Five exact rational source distributions, 15,091 exhaustive sampled words (counted across tested lengths), seven sharp-inequality instances and the symbolic four-letter specialization passed. Enumeration is supplementary, not the proof.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** Biers-Ariel, Godbole and Kelley (arXiv:1704.08661, Sections 2–3) give distinct-*all*-subsequence expectations for nonuniform random sources. Mahalingam and Pandoh (arXiv:2108.02729, Sections 3–5 and Table 4) give deterministic scattered-palindrome extrema. Kosche et al. (arXiv:2108.13968, Sections 2–4) address absent scattered strings. Rubinchik and Shur (arXiv:1505.08043, Sections 1,3–5) study **contiguous** palindrome factors in random words. Their inspected statements and implications do not supply the all-source palindromic scattered first moment together with the sharp four-letter optimizer. An equivalent unindexed weighted-pattern result remains possible; search absence is not novelty proof.

**Value — PASS.** The result solves a natural expected-complexity problem for distinct palindromic subsequences over general finite independent sources. It identifies a sharp and unexpectedly finite alphabet-size optimum for the exponential rate, with an exact rigidity criterion, not just a numeric bound. Such rates provide baselines for palindrome-pattern diversity in random sequences.

**Scientific limits.** Only first moments for a fixed finite-support i.i.d. source are asserted. The result gives neither a concentration theorem nor the worst-case count for individual source words. Literature coverage is bounded, with no independent audit.
