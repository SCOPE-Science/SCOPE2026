For centered isotropic planar finite-state increments with vanishing cubic moments, exactly four states force an orthogonal antipodal rule and a worst-direction quadratic defect of at least \(1/2\), whereas five states attain the universal \(1/4\) optimum. Every five-state rule whose defect is within \(\varepsilon\le10^{-8}\) of that optimum must have all weights within \(6\sqrt\varepsilon\) and all nodes within \(440\sqrt\varepsilon\) of a rotated uniform regular pentagon.
