PASS. Success and strong centering verify P1–P4, identity, semicomplementation and double-negation inflation directly. Universality of P5 in the Boolean power-set algebra is exactly the functional strong-density equation, by singleton separation. The reduction from 24 local choices to six rooted orders per world and the remaining cross-world constraint are proved. The result is finite and makes no infinite inference.

This is a finite structural classification, not a general complexity or downstream theorem.
