The intersection of two uniformly chosen free direct summands over any finite commutative chain ring of length two has a universal joint free-and-torsion Smith-type distribution determined by Gaussian coefficients and a finite-field matrix-rank law. At the critical dimension \(n=2k+s\) with \(k\to\infty\), that joint distribution converges in total variation to an explicit Euler-product mixture, retaining a positive probability of nonzero torsion-only intersections.
