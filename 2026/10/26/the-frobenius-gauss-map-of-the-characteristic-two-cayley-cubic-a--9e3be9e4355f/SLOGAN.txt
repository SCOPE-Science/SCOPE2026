For the characteristic-two Cayley cubic, every tangent plane passes through one smooth point, the reduced dual collapses to a plane, and its Gauss map is exactly the exponent-one purely inseparable degree-\(4\) Frobenius map on function fields. The Gauss map factors through two degree-\(2\) inseparable maps, one given by the complete cubic net through the seven points of the Fano plane.
