The characteristic-three elementary-symmetric Clebsch cubic has 15 rational lines and six Frobenius-conjugate pairs, with Frobenius acting as an \(A_1\) reflection on its Picard lattice. Its point counts are \(1+3^{2n}+3^n(6+(-1)^n)\) over \(\mathbb F_{3^n}\), giving an explicit zeta function for the correct exceptional-characteristic model.
