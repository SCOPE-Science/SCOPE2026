For uniformly sampled isomorphism classes of finite bijections with at most \(r\) cycles of any one length, first-order limiting probabilities exist effectively and form exactly \(\mathbb Z[1/(r+1)]\cap[0,1]\). The reason is that any fixed collection of short-cycle counts becomes independent and uniform on \(\{0,\ldots,r\}\), while first-order locality eventually sees only these counts.
