For a uniform length-\(n\) binary word avoiding `11`, every first-order sentence in successor and the letter predicate has a limiting probability, and the exact set of attainable limits is \(\mathbb Z[\varphi]\cap[0,1]\) with \(\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\). The set is dense, yet its only rational elements are \(0\) and \(1\).
