Every independent dominating set of \(\mathtt C^2(G)\) is uniquely encoded by a clique of \(\mathtt C(G)\), yielding the exact polynomial of all independent dominating sets from the independent-set counts \(a_s(G)\) and \(|E(G)|\). For connected \(G\) of order at least four, minimum-set multiplicities are governed by maximum independent sets, except for precisely characterized complete-graph and star cases.
