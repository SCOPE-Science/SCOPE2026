The irregular smooth characteristic-two Fano fourfold of Waldron--Witaszek is linked by degree-two universal homeomorphisms to a smooth cubic fourfold. This identifies its prime-to-two étale cohomology and rational Chow groups, gives fourth Betti number \(23\), and determines its complete Hasse--Weil zeta function over \(\mathbf F_{128}\) with middle Frobenius eigenvalues \(q^2\) and \(-q^2\) of multiplicities \(16\) and \(7\).
