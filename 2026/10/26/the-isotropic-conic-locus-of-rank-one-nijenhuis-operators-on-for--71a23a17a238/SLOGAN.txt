For every form of \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\) in characteristic other than two, the nonzero rank-one Nijenhuis operators are exactly the tensors whose dual factor lies on the isotropic Killing conic. Over \(\mathbb F_q\) for odd \(q\), the resulting smooth projective locus is \(\mathbb P^2\times\mathbb P^1\), and there are exactly \((q^2+q+1)(q^2-1)\) rank-one operators, with an explicit distribution by trace.
