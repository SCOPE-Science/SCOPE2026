For two independently sampled fixed-rank square-zero matrices over any finite field, both opposite product ranks have a complete joint Gaussian-binomial distribution, with conditional independence emerging after fixing their image overlap. At equal rank \(m\) in dimension \(n\), their rank discrepancy is sharply bounded by \(\min(m,n-2m)\); the discrepancy vanishes in even maximal-rank dimension but approaches a positive explicit Euler-series probability in odd dimension.
