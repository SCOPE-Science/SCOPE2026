For balanced complete bipartite graphs with \(t\) disjoint edges removed, the exact canonical cut- and cycle-projection endpoint norms both decrease strictly with \(t\), while their maximizing columns are exactly the \((s-t)^2\) untouched–untouched edges until the matching saturates. The final deletion produces an order-\(s^{-2}\) norm jump and restores all \(s(s-1)\) remaining edges as maximizers, after all earlier deletions together changed either norm by only order \(s^{-3}\).
