Within well-formed quasismooth six-variable loop hypersurfaces with exponents at least two, primitive well-formed ambient weights, and the determinant rationality criterion, the least weighted degree with ample canonical class is \(17279\). It is attained by type \((4,6,5,6,6,4)\), with weights \((3723,2387,2957,2494,2315,3389)\) and \(K_X\sim_{\mathbb Q}14H\); the example is explicitly noncanonical.
