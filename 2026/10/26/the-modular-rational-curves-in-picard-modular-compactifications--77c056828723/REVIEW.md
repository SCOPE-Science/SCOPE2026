# Review of the normal-bundle splitting

## Correctness: PASS

The primary construction gives the precise block-diagonal stabilizer of its modular geodesic, a closed embedding, and four genuine transverse cusps [1, Lemma 2.1, Lemma 3.1, Proposition 3.3]. Directly differentiating the block action gives the same scalar weight-one multiplier on each normal direction and weight two on the tangent direction. The ambient cusp parameter and the modular cusp parameter coincide, so the normal extension has nonvanishing transverse frames and its square is the logarithmic tangent bundle, not the ordinary tangent bundle. Four cusps on a rational curve give degree minus two for the logarithmic tangent bundle and degree minus one for its unique square root. This establishes every stated rank and every prime in the construction. The degree agrees with [1, Proposition 4.1]. The Hilbert tangent and obstruction groups vanish because each summand is the degree-minus-one line bundle. The finite coordinate checker is corroborative, not an infinite proof.

## Originality: PASS (qualified)

The strongest inspected source is the 8 October 2026 full text of [1], which already constructs the curves and computes canonical degrees; those contributions are excluded from the new claim. Its explicit Sections 2–4 do not provide the ordinary normal-bundle splitting, its balancedness, or the vanishing Hilbert deformation groups. Literature searches using the exact construction and equivalent weight-one/logarithmic-square terminology and comparisons with accessible research records found no statement implying the complete splitting. A general degree formula for a complex geodesic would give at most a first Chern class without identifying the separate summands. The unresolved risk is an older general theorem applying to the same compactified normal representation; search failure is not proof of novelty.

## Mathematical value: PASS

The result determines the full normal vector bundle of a newly constructed infinite family, rather than recovering only its known total degree. It shows the embedded curves are rigid as subschemes even in dimensions where the ambient canonical class is ample; for threefolds it identifies the distinguished numerically canonical-trivial curves as normal type \\(\\mathcal O(-1)\\oplus\\mathcal O(-1)\\). The exact splitting is a natural datum for local deformation theory and the geometry of extremal rays; no unproved global contraction is claimed.

## Scientific limitations

The proof depends on the explicit neat level-three quotient, the absence of stabilizer rotations in all normal directions, and the equality of the shortest vertical cusp periods. Its statement is not universal for other ball quotients. Searches and mathematical review are not an independent priority guarantee.
