For every dimension \\(n\\ge2\\), the embedded four-cusped modular rational curve in the level-three Picard modular toroidal compactification has ordinary normal bundle \\(\\mathcal O_{\\mathbb P^1}(-1)^{\\oplus(n-1)}\\). Its embedded Hilbert point is isolated and reduced; in dimension three it is a \\(K\\)-trivial curve of normal type \\(\\mathcal O(-1)\\oplus\\mathcal O(-1)\\).
