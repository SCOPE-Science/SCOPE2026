# Review

## Correctness

PASS. The cycle equations identify the integral norm kernel with \(A_{n-1}\). The restricted form is \((a+b)\) times the standard root-lattice form, and the Cartan determinant recurrence gives \(n(a+b)^{n-1}\). The included script is a finite sanity check only; the recurrence proves the all-\(n\) statement.

## Originality

PASS, with a priority limitation. Mohajer (arXiv:2604.01574v1) treats free cyclic covers generally and computes the volume for free \(\mathbb Z_3\)-covers, but does not state this all-degree integral lattice identification for the two-loop rose. published-finding corpus searches found no direct match. The related Len–Zakharov paper was identified but its full text was not read, and a general graph-homology theorem may subsume the calculation.

## Value

PASS. The exact lattice and metric form give a natural all-degree benchmark for the norm kernel of the simplest genus-two cyclic-cover family. The result is explicitly limited to the specified monodromies and the unscaled restriction of the ambient Jacobian quadratic form.
