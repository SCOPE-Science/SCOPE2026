For unramified quadratic triples into the toric threefold \((\mathbb P^1)^3\), the normal bundle is \(\mathcal O(5)^{\oplus2}\) off a smooth irreducible Wronskian-determinant divisor and \(\mathcal O(4)\oplus\mathcal O(6)\) on it, provided the characteristic is not two. In characteristic two the determinant vanishes identically and every unramified quadratic triple has the latter splitting.
