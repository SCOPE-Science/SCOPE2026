For \(f(m)=m^2\), the generalized Möbius recurrence has an exact squarefree formula with ordered Bell coefficients: \(s(p_1\cdots p_r)=(-1)^r\left[1+2\sum_{j=1}^{r-1}F_j e_j(p_1-1,\ldots,p_r-1)\right]\). All nonsquarefree values are even, and consequently \(s(n)=-1\) holds exactly at primes \(n\ge2\).
