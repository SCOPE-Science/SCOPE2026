The rank of a product of two uniform independent order-preserving partial isometries of an \(n\)-point chain has a complete explicit polynomial enumeration and is within \(O(n^{-1/2})\) total variation of \(\operatorname{Bin}(n,1/4)\). Its mean has the nontrivial asymptotic shift \(\mathbb E R_n=n/4-16/27+O(n^2 2^{-n})\).
