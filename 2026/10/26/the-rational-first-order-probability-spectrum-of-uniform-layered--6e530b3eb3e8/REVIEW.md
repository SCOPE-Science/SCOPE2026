# Scientific review: complete rational limit spectrum

## Correctness — PASS

The assertion is a statement about uniform layered permutations with two strict total orders, not merely a statement about independent binary bits. Both reductions were checked: the cut predicate is definable from adjacent positions and their value comparison, and the full second order is first-order definable from the cuts and the first order. Thus the induced distribution on cut words is exactly uniform. Regularity of first-order word languages, the classical convergence theorem for layered permutations, and the rational Abelian limit jointly give rationality. The converse follows from a family of length-equal mutually cross-bifix-free markers, a reversible switching bijection at the first marker, and an exponential bound on avoidance in disjoint windows. The explicit formula selecting the earliest marker is first-order. Endpoint values and the empty/small-length cases do not require limiting exceptions beyond the stated vanishing avoidance probability. The accompanying finite checker is supplemental evidence, not a proof for all sizes.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

Braunfeld--Kukla (arXiv:2111.06307; published 2022, Sections 1--4) prove first-order convergence for layered permutations and uniformly interdefine them with convex ordered equivalence relations. Markström--Räty (arXiv:2610.22218, first public October 15, 2026, especially introduction, Theorem 1, Sections 2.2 and 6) prove general rational generating functions and first-order convergence for small permutation classes. Together they imply existence of rational limits here, and this implication is not presented as new. Their inspected statements do not give the converse realization of every rational number through explicit first-order two-order formulas. The first-marker switching construction, especially non-dyadic values such as \(1/3\), is the distinguishing assessed addition.

Available mathematical-record searches for layered permutations, FO limit spectra, first-occurrence pattern races, and regular-language equivalences did not yield a covering record. Closest mathematical-record hits on finite coloured linear orders, random permutation comparisons and word subsequence universality use different objects or invariants and do not imply the spectrum result. A separate 2026 manuscript by Falgas--Ravry, Markström, Skerman and Räty, cited in the later paper as establishing density, could not be examined in full. Its unknown finer content is a genuine historical-priority risk. Failure to locate a stronger statement is not proof of absolute novelty.

## Value — PASS

The logical limit law had been known without a complete arithmetic description of its attainable probabilities. This natural exact classification distinguishes rational from merely dyadic outcomes for a model encoded by fair bits, and supplies effective exact calculation plus first-order constructions for any target rational. The pairwise-overlap-free marker mechanism is a reusable structural device for making finite-state first-hit symmetries observable by first-order logic. The result is a complete classification for a natural model, not an arbitrary finite slice or a small numerical improvement.

## Scientific limits and references

This does not extend the spectrum statement to stronger logics, other permutation classes or nonuniform measures. The generic 2026 work already contains the rational-generating-function mechanism, and a cited unpublished manuscript has not been fully compared. Independent scientific audit is absent.
