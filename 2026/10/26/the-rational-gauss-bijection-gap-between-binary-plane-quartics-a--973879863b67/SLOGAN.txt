Among smooth plane curves containing every point of \(\mathbf P^2(\mathbf F_2)\), a bijection from seven rational points to their seven rational tangent lines occurs for exactly \(24\), \(0\), and \(3048\) quartic, quintic, and sextic equations, respectively. The sextics fall into \(19\) projective orbits, one of size \(24\) and eighteen of size \(168\).
