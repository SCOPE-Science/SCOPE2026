For a uniform labeled tree, conditioning on the degree of one vertex produces a sharp nonmonotone stochastic-order pattern for the total number of leaves: degree two is minimal, degrees three and above form an increasing chain, and degree one is incomparable with every interior degree from three through \(n-2\). The conditional means at degrees one and three reverse their order precisely between \(n=8\) and \(n=9\), even though their distributions remain incomparable.
