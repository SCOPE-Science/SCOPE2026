For one-step DEIM-selected single-row sketched GMRES on an \(n\)-dimensional real SPD system, the **exact** worst-case true-residual factor is \(\sqrt n\,q/(1-\sqrt{n-1}\,q)\) below \(q=1/\sqrt{n-1}\) and is unbounded at and above that threshold, where \(q=(\kappa-1)/(2\sqrt\kappa)\). This gives a sharp dimension-dependent condition-number frontier for uniform residual nonexpansion, despite full GMRES remaining residual-monotone.
