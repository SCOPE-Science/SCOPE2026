# Independent mathematical audit

## correctness

PASS

For one-good/two-good final split, all monotone valuations satisfy EFX exactly when the singleton holder values her good at least as highly as either other singleton. The stated random first assignment and second rule produce at least two successful equally likely prefixes in the agreement case; in the disagreement case one of the two equiprobable first branches succeeds because agent one ranks g1 or g3 highest. Third arrival can decide from known singleton values. Two additive positive instances with identical first-arrival reports force opposite first recipients, giving upper bound 1/2 for every randomized policy against fixed oblivious inputs. Own direct EFX predicate checked 4096 additive profiles and both upper witnesses.

## originality

PASS

Cohen–Teh–Wooldridge establish n-agent upper 1/n! and lower 2/((n+1)n!), which at n=2 and three goods is a nonmatching [1/3,1/2] interval. The present rank-based online rule proves the missing lower 1/2 and is not a corollary of their stated bound. Other offline counting and unknown-horizon impossibilities do not imply this exact fixed known horizon minimax.

## value

PASS

Sharp and motivated first nontrivial fixed-horizon two-agent EFX success probability, with a new explicit online rule and a matching unavoidable-information witness. Relevant to online fair-division chance guarantees.
