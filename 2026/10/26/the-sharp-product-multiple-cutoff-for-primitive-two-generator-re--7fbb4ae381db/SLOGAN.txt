For every coprime pair of generators \(a,b\ge2\), each product multiple \(kab\) with \(2\le k\le15\) has a nonnegative representation with coprime coefficients. This universal property fails first at \(k=16\): the pair \((a,b)=(273,55)\) makes \(240240\) non-primitively-representable, and infinitely many generator pairs share the obstruction.
