In the uniform finite \(r\)-agent S5 frame model, for each fixed \(r\ge2\), failure of universal common-knowledge reachability has probability asymptotic to \(n(B_{n-1}/B_n)^r\sim (\log n)^r/n^{r-1}\). Conditioned on failure, almost surely one world is isolated for every agent while all remaining worlds form one common-knowledge component.
