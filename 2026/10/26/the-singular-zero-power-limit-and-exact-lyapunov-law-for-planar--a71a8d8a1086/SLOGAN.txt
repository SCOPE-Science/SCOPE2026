For exact planar residual-power Kaczmarz, every positive sampling exponent has a closed-form almost-sure contraction rate given by a reversible, angle-weighted edge average; increasing the exponent never slows that rate. The limit as the exponent tends to zero is **not** uniform randomized Kaczmarz: it is faster in logarithmic rate by the exact factor \(m/(m-1)\) for \(m\) distinct nonorthogonal row lines.
