Over \(\mathbb F_7\), every degree-three genus-one cover of \(\mathbb P^1\) with six distinct rational branch points is equivalent, allowing a base automorphism, to projection of \(Z^3+X^2Z+2Y^3=0\). There are exactly two covers over one fixed six-point branch divisor, interchanged by a target involution; their source elliptic curve has the maximal \(13\) rational points.
