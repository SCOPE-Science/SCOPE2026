# Review

## Correctness

The star construction gives the lower bound 10. The included verifier exhausts every labeled 3-graph on six vertices, tests all 180 distinct embeddings of \(F_5\), finds maximum edge count 10, and confirms that the six labeled maxima are precisely the six stars. The finite domain and exact search space are stated explicitly; no asymptotic extrapolation is made.

## Originality

The inspected recent sources give eventual extremal results or minimum-degree thresholds, not the six-vertex edge maximum. Exact searches for \(\operatorname{ex}_3(6,F_5)\) returned nearby but different results about \(C_5^{(3)}\)-free systems, \(K_5^{(3)}\)-free clique counts, and 3-union-free families. The original 1983 article was available only through its abstract and later citations in this check, leaving a limited historical-priority risk.

## Value

The result pins down a concrete small-order boundary for a classical hypergraph Turán problem: at order six, the extremal graph has 10 edges and is a star, not the balanced complete tripartite construction with 8 edges that governs sufficiently large orders. The full finite certificate makes this boundary reproducible.
