A symmetric monitored coined quantum walk first benefits from arbitrarily weak geometric resetting when detectors are at \(\pm4\), whereas half-separations \(1,2,3\) do not: the exact mean-time derivatives are \(0,2,4,-32\). At the same detector separation the classical walk instead has positive derivative \(40\), so the smallest weak-reset advantage is detectable without any difference in the unreset mean hitting time.
