The strong-majority edge index of the rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\) for \(2\le m\le n\) is two only for the four-cycle \(K_2\square K_2\), and three otherwise. The four-cycle has exactly four named two-colourings, while every other rook graph has none; a spectral obstruction and explicit degree-four constructions close the cases not implied by the existing minimum-degree-five upper bound.
