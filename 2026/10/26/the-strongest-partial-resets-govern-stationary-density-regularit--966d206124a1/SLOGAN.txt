For Brownian motion with Poissonian random partial resets, full resets create an exact cusp with slope jump \(-rp_0/D\), whereas reset factors bounded away from zero produce a smooth but nonanalytic stationary density. Its order-\(2k\) derivative at the origin grows on the sharp logarithmic scale \(\lim_{k\to\infty}k^{-2}\log|f^{(2k)}(0)|=-\log\operatorname*{ess\,inf}Q\): even exceedingly rare strong contractions determine all-order regularity.
