For the rank-nine uniform non-homogeneous bundle on \(\mathbb P^5\) defined by fourteen symplectic Koszul sections, every smooth twisted cubic has exactly one of two splitting types: \((2,2,2,2,2,2,1,1,1)\) generically, or \((3,2,2,2,2,1,1,1,1)\) when its spanning four-plane has isotropic annihilator. The exceptional spans form the smooth seven-dimensional symplectic isotropic Grassmannian \(\operatorname{IG}(2,6)\), a divisor, even though the bundle has no jumping lines.
