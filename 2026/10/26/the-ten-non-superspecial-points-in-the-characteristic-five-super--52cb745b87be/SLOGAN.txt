For characteristic-five Howe curves built from supersingular \(j=0\) elliptic double covers, the entire non-superspecial supersingular locus consists of exactly ten normalized marked parameters, cut out by \(2s^{10}+2s^8-s^6+1=0\). These split into six degree-three and four degree-four parameters over \(\mathbb F_5\), yielding an exact coefficient-marked census over every finite extension; the previously known zero-shift superspecial branch is kept separate.
