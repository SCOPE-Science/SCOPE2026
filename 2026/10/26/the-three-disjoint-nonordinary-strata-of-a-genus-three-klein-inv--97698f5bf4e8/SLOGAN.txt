For the smooth two-parameter genus-three hyperelliptic family with commuting involutions in characteristic three, nonordinarity occurs on exactly three pairwise disjoint punctured affine lines, and every nonordinary Jacobian has \(3\)-rank \(2\). Over \(\mathbb F_q\), the exact ordered-parameter counts are \(3(q-3)\) nonordinary and \((q-3)(q-5)\) ordinary for every \(q=3^m\).
