For the free two-generated left \(\delta\)-mock-Novikov algebra in characteristic zero, degree four is one-dimensional for every nonzero \(\delta\ne-1\), but exactly four-dimensional at \(\delta=-1\). The exceptional bidegree pattern is \((1,2,1)\) across \((3,1),(2,2),(1,3)\), with an explicit four-monomial basis and exact rank certificates.
