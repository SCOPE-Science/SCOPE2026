For every \(s>t\ge1\) with \((s,t)\ne(2,1)\) and \(n,r\ge1\), the inverse reciprocal tail of \(U_{j+2}=sU_{j+1}-tU_j\), \(U_0=0\), \(U_1=1\), has exact floor \(U_{rn}-U_{r(n-1)}-1\). A uniform Binet-error bound establishes the first part of a published generalized-Fibonacci conjecture, including the formerly untreated small parameters.
