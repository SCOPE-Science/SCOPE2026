Finite elementary abelian groups, viewed only through the ternary relation \(R(x,y,z)\iff x+y=z\), have exactly 13 first-order theories at quantifier rank three, across every prime and every positive dimension. Every theory has a representative of at most 121 elements, and the bound is sharp; all characteristics at least 17 collapse into a single theory, which also contains dimension-at-least-two groups of characteristic 13.
