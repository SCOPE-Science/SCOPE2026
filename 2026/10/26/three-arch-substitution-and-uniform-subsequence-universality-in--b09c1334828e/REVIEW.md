The \(5/2\) estimate combines these proved statements with the published Tribonacci factor-balance bound \(<3/2\). The symbolic proof handles all lengths and positions, not only the finite checks.

Related semantic searches of mathematical records and literature did not show a dominating equivalent result. Derived-word or return-substitution descriptions under different terminology remain a genuine residual priority risk.

The unusual equality with the rare-letter count and the irrational limiting density give a testable, natural quantitative invariant beyond a one-off coefficient computation.

**Limits.** The numerical coefficient \(5/2\) is not claimed optimal; the balance estimate and general arch theorem are credited to earlier sources, rather than declared novel. The verifier is finite and cannot itself establish the bound for every factor.
