# Verification
The companion `verify_belyi.py` runs exact polynomial arithmetic over the quadratic extension generated by \(\sqrt{-3}\). It checks every explicit discriminant factorization, noncollision of the six \(I_2\) parameters, all three degree-twelve ramification passports (including the point at infinity), the \(j-1728\) square identity, and the Riemann–Hurwitz total \(22\). Run `python verify_belyi.py` from the directory containing the script. Output `VERIFY_OK` indicates those finite symbolic identities and checks passed.

The completeness proof is mathematical: the quadratic Legendre map has exactly two branch values, its admissible Belyi branch values belong to a five-point set, and the anharmonic group has exactly three orbits on unordered distinct pairs. The symbolic checker supplements but does not replace these arguments. Isomorphisms are those of elliptic fibrations with chosen zero section. No independent audit was carried out.
