For every field, square-zero pairwise-anticommuting tuples in the strictly upper-triangular \(4\times4\) algebra form three rational irreducible components of dimensions \(4r,3r+1,3r+1\) for \(r\ge2\), controlled by the two rulings of a rank-one matrix quadric. Their exact finite-field count in every characteristic is \(q^{4r}-q^{3r}+q^r[1+(q+1)^2(q^r-1)+2(q+1)(q^r-1)(q^r-q)]\).
