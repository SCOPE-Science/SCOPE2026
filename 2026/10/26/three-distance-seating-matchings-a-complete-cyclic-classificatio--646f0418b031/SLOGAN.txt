For every \(n\ge3\), a prescribed circular perfect matching using only distances \(1,2,3\) exists exactly when the number of distance-two edges is even, apart from the unique profile with one distance-one edge and \(n-1\) distance-three edges when \(3\mid n\). Six explicit local tiles and a step-three Hamiltonian cycle give constructive solutions, including every parity-admissible genuinely three-distance profile.
