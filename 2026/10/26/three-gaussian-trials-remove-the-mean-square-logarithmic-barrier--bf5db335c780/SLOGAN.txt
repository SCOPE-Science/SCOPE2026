For orthogonal randomized joint diagonalization of the almost-commuting pair \(\{\mathrm{diag}(1,-1),\varepsilon\begin{psmallmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{psmallmatrix}\}\), the actual best-of-Gaussian-trials loss has an exact finite-\(\varepsilon\) distribution. One trial has mean squared loss \(2\varepsilon\), two still incur a sharp \(\varepsilon^2\log(1/\varepsilon)\) penalty, and three are necessary and sufficient to recover the optimal \(\varepsilon^2\) expected squared-error order.
