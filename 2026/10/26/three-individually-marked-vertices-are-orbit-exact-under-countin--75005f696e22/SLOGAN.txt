For any three distinct individually marked words in \(H(d,q)\), with \(d\ge3\) and \(q\ge4\), counting color refinement recovers exactly their pointwise-stabilizer orbits within \(d\) rounds. The orbit count is \(\prod_{\pi\vdash\{1,2,3\}}\binom{n_\pi+|\pi|}{|\pi|}\), with explicit multinomial class sizes.
