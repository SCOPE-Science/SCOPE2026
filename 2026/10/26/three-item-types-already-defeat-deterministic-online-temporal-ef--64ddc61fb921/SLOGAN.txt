Two distinct item types admit guaranteed online temporal EF1, as previously known; three already suffice to defeat every deterministic irrevocable rule, with three agents and seven arriving goods of types \((0,1,2)\), \((0,2,1)\), and \((1,2,2)\). The seven-turn obstruction is purely online: every fixed sequence of up to seven arrivals still has an EF1 allocation at every prefix when its future is known.
