For every finite field \(\mathbb F_q\), the group-commutator map of \(UT_4(\mathbb F_q)\) has exactly three nonempty fiber levels: \(q^7(q^2-1)\), \(2q^7(q^2-1)\), and \(2q^7(q^2-1)+q^8\). These distinguish noncentral derived targets, nonidentity central targets, and the identity; all derived elements are single commutators.
