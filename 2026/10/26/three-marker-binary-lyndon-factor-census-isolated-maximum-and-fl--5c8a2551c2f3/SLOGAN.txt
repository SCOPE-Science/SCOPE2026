The distinct Lyndon factors of every binary word with exactly three ones obey an explicit zero-gap formula, yielding a rational generating function for their total count at every length. The maximum is uniquely attained by all zeros followed by three ones, with a three-unit gap to the runner-up for all sufficiently long words; uniform fixed-weight words have linear mean coefficient \(19/24\) and quadratic variance coefficient \(563/2880\).
