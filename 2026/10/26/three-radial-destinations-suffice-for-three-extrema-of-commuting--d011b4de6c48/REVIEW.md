**Correctness: PASS.** The finite radiation probabilities normalize by telescoping. SymPy independently verifies the variance and its derivative factorization. Rational interval sign arguments on the two quadratic factors prove exactly three positive critical points with alternating types; a separate Bernoulli derivative argument proves at most one stationary point for two destinations. The outward stochastic-order result follows from an exact positive derivative of every upper-tail probability. These are analytic proofs, not extrapolations of simulation.

The exact three-extrema example despite strict outward stochastic drift establishes an unexpected limitation of monotone-distance intuition. The proof that one or two destinations cannot reproduce three strict extrema gives a sharp minimal structural boundary, beyond a numerical curve plot.
