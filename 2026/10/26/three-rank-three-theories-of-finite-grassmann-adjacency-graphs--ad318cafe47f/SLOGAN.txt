All finite-field Grassmann adjacency graphs have exactly three first-order rank-three theories, determined solely by whether the smaller of the subspace dimension and codimension is one, two, or at least three; field size and characteristic are invisible. Their sharp largest least-order representative within the Grassmann family has \(1395\) vertices, while the three theories also admit ordinary Johnson-graph representatives of orders \(3\), \(15\), and \(35\).
