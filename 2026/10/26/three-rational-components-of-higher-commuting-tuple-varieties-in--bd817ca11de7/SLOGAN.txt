For the strictly upper-triangular \(4\times4\) Lie algebra, every commuting \(r\)-tuple variety with \(r\ge3\) has exactly three rational irreducible components, of dimensions \(3r+3,3r+3,4r\). A symplectic-isotropic rank decomposition also gives an exact polynomial in the field size for every finite-field commuting-tuple count, with equidimensional triples but non-equidimensional varieties from \(r=4\) onward.
