For a mean-square-stable two-state stochastic Lyapunov problem with signed error modes \(\pm r\), the sharp three-inner-step Smith tuning changes at \(r=2/5\) and \(r=1/2\). The best contraction improves from unrelaxed \(r^3\) to \(r^4+O(r^6)\) at weak coupling, but unrelaxed tuning is exactly optimal for \(r\ge1/2\).
