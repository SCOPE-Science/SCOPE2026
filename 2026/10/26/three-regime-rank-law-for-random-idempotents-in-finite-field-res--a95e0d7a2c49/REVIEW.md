Fulman--Goldstein treats ranks of unrestricted random finite-field matrices. The elementary Gaussian count is known in substance from classical complement counting and is not itself presented as novel; the assessed new claim is the three-regime asymptotic law, including the codimension threshold and free-energy differentiability boundary. Semantic published-finding corpus comparisons found no covering statement among the inspected closest records; this does not prove uniqueness.
