For uniformly sampled idempotents whose images lie in a fixed \(k\)-space over \(\mathbb F_q\), the rank exhibits a discrete-Gaussian interior law, a nondegenerate corank law when the ambient codimension is \(2k+O(1)\), and full-image concentration beyond this window. The logarithmic idempotent count has a second-derivative phase transition at ambient codimension ratio \(2\).
