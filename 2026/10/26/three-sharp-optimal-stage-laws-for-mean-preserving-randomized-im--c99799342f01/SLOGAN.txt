Among all randomized implicit Runge–Kutta stages on a scalar decay mode with mean stage fraction one half, the exact mean-square-optimal law switches from equal endpoint sampling to asymmetric two-point sampling to a deterministic midpoint at the sharp stiffness thresholds \((\sqrt{17}-1)/4\) and \(2/\sqrt3\). The interior random law can strictly beat deterministic midpoint damping at the same step size, but no randomization improves it past the second threshold.
