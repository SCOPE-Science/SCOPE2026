# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The evaluation kernel is exactly 20-dimensional; the two independent complete enumerations (monomial kernel and pair-generators) each count all 1,048,576 quartic forms and independently agree that 124,992 are rationally nonsingular with 10/11/12 Gauss-image values in frequencies 24,192/80,640/20,160. A third exact symbolic program checks the 256-case plane-curve singularity lemma, the geometrically smooth explicit extremizer, and all 15 plane restrictions. The deduction from missing tangent-plane values to smooth sections uses the checked plane lemma and the ambient smoothness hypothesis.

## Originality — PASS (with priority risk)
The directly relevant Bruggemann–Choi–Freidin–Whyte 2024 result only excludes plane-transverse-free quartic surfaces when the field size is odd; its Theorem 3.11 therefore does not establish the even-field bound or sharp three-plane minimum. The transverse-line results of Asgarli–Duan–Lai do not determine plane-section counts. Searches for binary Gauss-image/tangent-plane distributions and comparison to related results located no published equivalent. A comprehensive survey of all unpublished finite-field catalogues was not possible, so publication priority is not independently guaranteed.

## Value — PASS
The complete sharp guarantee resolves the first even-characteristic parameter omitted from an existing geometric nonexistence theorem. It is an intrinsic assertion about **every** smooth space-filling surface over the smallest finite field, not an isolated computational curiosity; the explicit extremizer demonstrates optimality, and the Gauss-image histogram describes the mechanism of forced transversality.

## Scope and residual risks
The all-field analog is not proved. The catalogue is for binary coordinate-fixed quartics, not projective classes. Full textual comparison with a potentially relevant older plane-filling-curves article was unavailable; its abstract alone was not used as a negative coverage decision. Computational completeness follows from exhaustive finite-domain methods, but independent human validation has not occurred.

Self-review: passed. Independent audit: not yet performed.
