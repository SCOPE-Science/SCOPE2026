Under the fixed-composition two-sample permutation null, centered Wilcoxon rank sum and Wald–Wolfowitz runs are linearly uncorrelated, but squared rank sum is strictly negatively correlated in covariance with the number of runs whenever there is more than one pair of opposite labels. Its covariance with each individual run boundary is an exact quadratic in position, producing three sharply separated regimes: positive boundary covariances at the edges, nowhere, or near the center as group imbalance changes.
