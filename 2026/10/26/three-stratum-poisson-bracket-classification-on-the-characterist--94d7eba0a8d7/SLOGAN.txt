Every Poisson bracket on \(k[x,y]/(x^2,y^2)\) in characteristic two is isomorphic to exactly one of \(\{x,y\}=1+dxy\), \(\{x,y\}=x+dxy\), or \(\{x,y\}=dxy\), with \(d\) a strict isomorphism invariant. Over \(\mathbb F_q\) there are exactly \(3q\) classes with explicit orbit sizes and Poisson automorphism-group orders.
