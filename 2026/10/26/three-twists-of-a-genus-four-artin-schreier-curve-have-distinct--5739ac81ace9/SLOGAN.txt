The three \(\mathbb F_3\)-forms \(y^3-y=x^2+x+x^{-2}+c\), \(c=0,1,2\), of the minimal-automorphism genus-four Artin–Schreier curve have three explicit, distinct, \(\mathbb Q\)-irreducible Weil numerators. Thus their Jacobians are pairwise non-isogenous and individually simple over \(\mathbb F_3\), despite becoming isomorphic after extending the ground field.
