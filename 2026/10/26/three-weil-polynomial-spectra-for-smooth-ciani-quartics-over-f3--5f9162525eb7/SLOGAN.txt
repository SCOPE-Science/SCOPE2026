Among Ciani sextuple quartics over \(\mathbf F_3\), only 16 scalar-normalized equations are geometrically smooth, and these form three projective classes with exactly 2, 4, or 6 rational points. Their Jacobian Weil numerators are precisely \((1+3T^2)^3\) and \((1\pm2T+3T^2)(1+3T^2)^2\), so none is ordinary and every Jacobian has \(3\)-rank at most one.
