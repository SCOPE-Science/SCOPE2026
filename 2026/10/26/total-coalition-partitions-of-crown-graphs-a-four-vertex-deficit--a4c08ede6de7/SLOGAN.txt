Every crown graph with at least five vertices in each bipartition class has exactly four fewer total-coalition blocks than vertices: the only optimum groups one \((1,2)\) triple and one \((2,1)\) triple and leaves everything else singleton. The exact number of optimum partitions is \(\bigl(n(n-1)(n-2)/2\bigr)^2\); the smaller crown graphs have exact optimum counts \(48\) and \(857\).
