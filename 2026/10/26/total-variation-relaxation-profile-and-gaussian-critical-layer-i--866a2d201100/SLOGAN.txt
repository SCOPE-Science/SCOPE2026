In an equal-rate chain of nested Brownian resetting particles, the tagged particle's distance from stationarity has an explicit nontrivial total-variation profile at times \(t=\theta n/r\) for \(0<\theta<1\), and a universal \(n^{-1/2}\) critical layer at \(t=(n+s\sqrt n)/r\). The exact finite-index transient-minus-stationary density changes sign only once on the positive half-line, so relaxation can be measured by a single central-interval probability difference.
