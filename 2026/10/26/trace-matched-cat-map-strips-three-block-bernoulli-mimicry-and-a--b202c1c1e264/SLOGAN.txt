For every hyperbolic determinant-one toral automorphism with positive integer trace \(q\ge3\), partitioning any nonzero integer linear observable into \(q\) equal bins yields three consecutive iid uniform symbols but a nonuniform fourth-symbol conditional law. The exact probability of the fourth symbol after three zeros is \(q/(q^2-1)\) for the first \(q-1\) symbols and \(1/(q+1)\) for the last.
