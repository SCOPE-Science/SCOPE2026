For rectangular periods \(2^u(2^a-1)\) by \(2^v(2^b-1)\), the finite Ledrappier three-dot shift has an explicit linear-equivariant type depending only on \(\gcd(a,b)\) and the shared power-of-two factor, and has a faithful two-generator translation action after reducing each direction to its exact order, except when \(\gcd(a,b)=2\), where precisely a diagonal three-element kernel survives. A single configuration attains the resulting maximal translation-orbit size in every nontrivial case.
