Among the ten reversal–exchange reduct groups of the homogeneous random permutation, all injective tuple-orbit counts are \(\bigl((n!)^2+s(H)n!\bigr)/|H|\) for \(n\ge2\), and the ten groups yield only six distinct numerical profiles. The full orbit partition on triples nevertheless recovers every group, sharply improving on pairs, whose orbit partitions distinguish only seven of them.
