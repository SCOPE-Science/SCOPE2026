# Self-review

## Correctness: PASS

The domain is every nonempty finite simple undirected 2-regular graph. Brown–Hoshino's proved three-round cycle threshold permits replacement of every cycle of length at least seven by a seven-cycle; the componentwise EF strategy and the three-component cap reduce the general case to exactly 1023 finite canonical cases. The accompanying source code implements the exact rank-three back-and-forth types rather than a heuristic invariant; its exhaustive partition is exactly equal to the partition induced by six displayed first-order sentences. A separate direct game solver independently of the type-code implementation checks 144 representative comparisons and five additional positives. Limits: this does not prove an analogous statement at other ranks or for other graph classes.

## Originality: PASS, with residual literature risk

The closest full text examined is Brown–Hoshino (2007), *The Ehrenfeucht–Fraïssé Game for Paths and Cycles*, pp. 193–212, especially their Theorems 4.1 and 4.9 and conclusion: its complete classifications compare individual connected paths or individual connected cycles, not disconnected unions of cycles. Their result is used, not recast as a new theorem. The general disjoint-union composition principle (Ebbinghaus–Flum; Dawar's 2016 lecture) proves finite reducibility but does not supply the twelve-class catalogue or six-formula signature. Focused published-finding corpus and literature searches found no match for the explicit claim, including searches on 2-regular graphs, three-round games, disjoint unions of cycles, and logical equivalence. Similarity retrieval is not a uniqueness theorem. A specialized unindexed prior treatment remains possible.

## Value: PASS

The finite-model-theory problem of classifying Ehrenfeucht–Fraïssé equivalence on graph families has a standard motivation and is explicitly highlighted as open beyond connected paths and cycles in the motivating 2007 source. The result gives the **complete** first nontrivial disconnected degree-two classification at rank three: an exact natural invariant (twelve theories), a human-readable six-sentence decision signature, concrete canonical representatives, and a finite complete proof certificate. The collapse from arbitrary many and arbitrarily long cycles to twelve classes is not a renamed single-cycle threshold and has immediate use in deciding rank-three definability on the class of 2-factors. Its scope is deliberately limited to quantifier rank three, and no broader theorem is claimed.

## Literature and scope limitations

The 2007 primary source was read at its relevant definitions, circle theorems, proof, and conclusion. A 2016 general paper on definability over **graph-order structures** was found but uses graphs as elements of a minor/subgraph order and does not study quantifier-rank-three theory of adjacency graphs. Literature searches can miss unindexed special cases. The program checks finitely many representatives after a mathematically justified reduction, not every graph in an infinite class by brute force. Independent audit has not been performed.
