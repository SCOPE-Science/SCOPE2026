The literal characteristic-\(5\) Clebsch cubic is a split one-nodal surface obtained from the plane by blowing up the six \(\mathbb F_5\)-points of a conic and contracting its strict transform. All its \(21\) geometric lines are \(\mathbb F_5\)-rational, the coordinate \(S_5\) acts sharply three-transitively on the six lines through the node, and every extension field \(\mathbb F_q\) has exactly \(q^2+6q+1\) points on the surface.
