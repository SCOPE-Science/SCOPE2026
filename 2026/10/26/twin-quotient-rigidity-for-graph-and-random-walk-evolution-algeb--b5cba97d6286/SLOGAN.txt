For every connected nonsingular graph \(H\), the graph and symmetric-random-walk evolution algebras of any independent-set blow-up \(H[a_1,\ldots,a_k]\) over \(\mathbb R\) are isomorphic exactly when the blow-up is regular or bipartite biregular. This settles the comparison for all complete multipartite graphs and for a natural infinite class of singular graphs with unbounded adjacency nullity.
