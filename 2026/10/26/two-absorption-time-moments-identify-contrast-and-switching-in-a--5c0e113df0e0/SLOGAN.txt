For symmetric two-mobility Brownian diffusion prepared in the reflecting–absorbing principal spatial mode, the mean and squared coefficient of variation of absorption time uniquely recover switching rate and mobility contrast. Their exact compatible data are \(m>1\) and \(1+2(1-m^{-1})^2<c<1+2(1-m^{-1})\), with both boundaries physically unattainable under strictly positive rates and diffusivities.
