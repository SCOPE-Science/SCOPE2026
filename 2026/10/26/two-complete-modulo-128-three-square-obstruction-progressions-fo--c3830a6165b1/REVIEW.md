The explicit finite numerator \(U\) has degree \(1538\), and the extracted polynomials for \(36\) and \(62\) satisfy exact degree-20 identities modulo \(128\), certified independently by `verify.py`. Coefficient extraction and \(B=T^{-2}\) then prove the congruences at every index. The other \(126\) residue classes are excluded by their first two coefficients. Legendre's theorem and the density of two disjoint progressions give the stated obstruction and bound. No extrapolation from sampling is needed.

Targeted mathematical-record searches and exact-phrase searches found no equivalent progressions; this is not proof of exhaustive publication priority. The method is related to established rational extraction, and unindexed implications remain possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The result advances an explicitly motivated Diophantine partition-value question in the first even-colour case, establishing two complete universal residue classes with no three-square representations. Its all-index congruence and complete modulus-128 residue-class qualification are more informative than a finite table, and yield an explicit positive-density lower bound rather than an isolated example. The full characterization and exact density remain open.
