The two-colour binary-partition counts satisfy \(b_2(128n+36)\equiv b_2(128n+62)\equiv112\pmod{128}\) for every \(n\ge0\), and these are exactly the universally residue-112 classes modulo \(128\). Both progressions consist entirely of non-three-square values, giving a lower natural density of at least \(1/64\) for three-square obstructions.
