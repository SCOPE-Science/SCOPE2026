Cusps and nodes are allocated using the nonzero quadratic discriminants away from \(A(v)=0\) and the complete singularity inventory of Burek [1, Lemma 8.2]. Kumar--Kuwata [2] proves results for the related elliptic K3 pencils rather than this specialized global discriminant; published-record searches and deduplication of prior local claims did not identify an implication covering the new statement. Residual risk: an unindexed older calculation could contain the same special factorization; failure to find one is not proof of uniqueness.

The split versus conjugate arithmetic and cyclic-cover structure are natural invariants of a high-rank elliptic Calabi--Yau construction, not a routine perturbation or arbitrary parameter slice.
