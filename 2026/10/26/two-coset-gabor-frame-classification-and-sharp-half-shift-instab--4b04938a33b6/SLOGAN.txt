For the odd rational window \(g(t)=t/((t^2+1)(t^2+4))\), the integer-translate Gabor system using frequency cosets \(\mathbb Z\) and \(\mathbb Z+\delta\) is a frame precisely when \(0<\delta<1\) and \(\delta\ne1/2\). Its optimal lower frame bound collapses quadratically at the half-shift with exact coefficient \(c_0c_h/(c_0+c_h)\), where \(c_0=\frac{4\pi^4}{9}((1-e^{-2\pi})/(1+e^{-2\pi}))^2\) and \(c_h=\frac{16\pi^4e^{-2\pi}}9(1-e^{-\pi})^4/(1-e^{-4\pi})^2\).
