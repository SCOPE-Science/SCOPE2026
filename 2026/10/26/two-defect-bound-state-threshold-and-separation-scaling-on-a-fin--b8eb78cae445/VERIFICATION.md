The resolvent identity was checked against the discrete Fourier sum on four finite cycles. The exact threshold was checked with a cancellation-stable expression for the antisymmetric channel. Both symmetry-channel equations were solved numerically above and below the predicted threshold, and the scaled roots were compared for cycle sizes \(N=80,160,320\); the checker reports 40 passing checks.

The analytic proof establishes the all-size result. Numerical checks are corroborative only and do not establish uniqueness, absence of other states, or the scaling limit. The full text of Joglekar et al. (2010) was inspected; only the abstract and bibliographic record of Bierman (1970) were accessible. Independent audit and external validation have not been performed.
