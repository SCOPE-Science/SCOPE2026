In a uniform spanning tree of \(K_{a,b}\), fixing the odd distance between vertices in opposite parts gives an exact bivariate generating function for their degrees and conditional covariance \(-\frac{(a-t)(b-t)}{t^2(a+b-t)^2}\) at distance \(2t-1\). Both endpoint degrees strictly decrease in stochastic order as their separation grows, even though they remain negatively correlated at each nonmaximal separation.
