# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The all-parameter proof reduces distinct consecutive-two-deletion outputs to a transition count, uses a bijective differential representation, then evaluates every Fourier mode by cyclic orbit cancellation, Cayley–Hamilton, and a reversal-complement involution. Exact enumeration and an independent difference-prefix dynamic program corroborate the coefficients. The computational samples alone are not a proof.

**Originality — PASS, with residual risk.** The closest primary comparison is Xie–Sun–Ge (arXiv:2606.15379v1, two-burst perfectness), followed by Babu–Krishnamoorthy–Roth–Siegel (2025, differential VT cardinalities). Neither inspected work determines the conditional burst-output-size distribution; their scalar statements do not imply this polynomial. The broader differential-code source Nguyen–Cai–Siegel (2024) and closest published scientific record summaries were additionally checked. The earlier long journal paper was not inspected in full, and implicit prior transfer-matrix coverage is possible. The originality comparison is not a priority certification.

**Value — PASS.** The complete spectrum measures the heterogeneity of the deletion-output choices within the perfect code and proves the sharp four-class versus remaining-class distinction at all powers of two. The invariant follows naturally from the recent correction/covering theorem. It does not imply a new decoder or improve redundancy.

**Limitations.** Binary alphabet, exactly two *consecutive* deletions, and blocklengths equal to powers of two only. Computation is finite. Independent verification is outstanding.
