For the second Gaussian short-time Fourier concentration eigenvalue, all smooth fixed-area perturbations of a Fock disk have an exact diagonal angular shape Hessian. The Hessian loses high-frequency coercivity at \(R=1\), becomes indefinite for \(1<R<3/2\), and becomes negative on all nontranslation modes for \(R>3/2\), with one neutral second harmonic at \(R=3/2\).
