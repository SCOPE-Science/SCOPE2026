Every stationary two-cosine exponential on the circle, \(\exp(a[\cos(pt)-1]+b[\cos(qt)-1])\) with \(1\le p<q\), is positive definite for all configurations exactly when \(a,b\ge0\). Any negative parameter forces infinitely many negative Fourier coefficients, while the nonnegative parameters have an exact greatest-common-divisor spectral support and quotient-circle strictness criterion.
