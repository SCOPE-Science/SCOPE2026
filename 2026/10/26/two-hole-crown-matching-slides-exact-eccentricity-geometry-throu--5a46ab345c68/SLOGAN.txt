For the graphs of size-\(n-1\) matchings in crown graphs under single-edge slides, the exact diameter is \(n+\lfloor n/2\rfloor-1\) for every tested \(3\le n\le8\). Odd tested orders are self-centered, while even tested orders have precisely the configurations with coincident hole indices as their central vertices; at \(n=8\) this classifies all \(133{,}496\) states and \(2{,}593{,}920\) diametral pairs. An all-order derangement bijection gives the degree distribution and a parity obstruction to any Hamilton path, without claiming an all-order distance formula.
