Its Perron root \(\lambda\in(1,2)\) has no rational positive power. Differentiating the established bivariate border-polynomial generating function shows that density \(1/2\) is equivalent to the vanishing of a sum of at most two nonzero integer multiples of distinct powers of \(\lambda\), which is impossible. The explicit mixed-sign family has exactly the two claimed proper borders and its bias has the predicted sign. NumPy transfer-matrix tests support the proof, without replacing it.
