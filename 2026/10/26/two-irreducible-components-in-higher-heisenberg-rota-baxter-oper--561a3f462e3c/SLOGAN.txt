The weight-zero Rota--Baxter operators on a \((2n+1)\)-dimensional Heisenberg Lie algebra split into a symplectic branch and an isotropic-image boundary. Their reduced parameter variety is irreducible only for \(n=1\); for every \(n\ge2\) it has exactly two components, with a sharp dimension crossover at \(n=3\) and an exact all-finite-field counting formula.
