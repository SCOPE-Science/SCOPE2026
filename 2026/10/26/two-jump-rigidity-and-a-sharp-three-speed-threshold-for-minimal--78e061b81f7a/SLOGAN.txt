For a minimal Cantor homeomorphism, a continuous jump taking values \(a<b\) gives an invertible time change exactly when it is invariant under the \((b-a)\)-th iterate; its exact orbit number is an elementary residue count. For every totally minimal Cantor system, nonconstant invertible positive speedups require at least three jump values, and a minimal speedup with precisely \(\{1,2,3\}\) always exists.
