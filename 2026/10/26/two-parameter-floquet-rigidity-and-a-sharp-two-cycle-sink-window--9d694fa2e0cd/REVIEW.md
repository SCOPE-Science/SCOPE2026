# Scientific review: two-parameter Floquet rigidity and sharp two-cycle sink window

## Correctness
**PASS.** The claim concerns the full real six-coefficient quadratic family under the exact nondegeneracy condition \(a_3b_2c\ne0\). Period-two states reduce bijectively to two-cycles of a scalar quadratic. Its second-iterate factorization and cofactor discriminant give exactly one primitive cycle for positive \(\Delta\). A direct derivative-product computation yields a cubic involving only \((A,\Delta)\). Applying the strict cubic Schur conditions yields the complete sink test. Optimizing one concave quadratic in \(\Delta\), combined with the return-trace obstruction and an explicit coefficient realization, proves both sharp endpoint exclusions and sufficiency. The accompanying checker confirms the critical polynomial identities and a rational witness; random tests are supplementary, not a proof of universal claims.

## Originality
**PASS under accessible historical comparison; residual priority risk remains.** Zhou–Liu–Ren (2017) already wrote the implicit quartic period-two equation and two-step Jacobian for this map. Those are treated as known, not independently discovered. Muni (2024) investigates bifurcation routes and stability of selected periodic orbits, but the inspected full arXiv text does not establish the two-parameter universal multiplier cubic or the complete exact projected sink interval. The open-access 2025 perturbation paper emphasizes torus doubling and perturbed maps. Published mathematical-finding searches yielded classifications for distinct maps, not an equivalent six-parameter Schur region or sharp \(a_1\)-window. The 2024 torus-doubling journal article was not accessible in full; its abstract alone cannot rule out additional coverage. The historical priority claim is therefore qualified, and independent literature verification remains worthwhile.

## Value
**PASS.** An explicit necessary-and-sufficient sink region and sharp projection onto one native map coefficient answer a natural stability-feasibility question in a model used to examine period doubling and hyperchaotic transitions. The reduction removes four apparently relevant parameters from the local two-cycle multiplier problem, and the non-obvious algebraic endpoint separates permitted and forbidden choices of attracting two-cycles. Neither a numerical bifurcation diagram nor the previously known quartic two-cycle equation directly provides the all-coefficient exclusion and realization theorem.

## Limits
No claim is made for degenerate affine subfamilies, global attractor basins, higher-periodic states, or independence of historical priority. Full text of one closely related 2024 paper could not be inspected under currently available access.
