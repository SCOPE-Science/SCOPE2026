A three-dimensional quadratic map has a primitive period-two orbit exactly when a single discriminant is positive, and its entire two-step Floquet spectrum depends on only two combinations of the six coefficients. As the other coefficients vary freely, an attracting primitive two-cycle is possible at a prescribed linear self-coupling \(a_1\) exactly in the sharp interval \((-3/2,\eta)\), where \(\eta\) is the unique root in \((0,1)\) of \(t^4-8t^2-8t+4\).
