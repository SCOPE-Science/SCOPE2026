# Scientific review

**Correctness (PASS).** The Jacobian criterion gives geometric smoothness precisely for invertible matrices; the anticanonical embedding identifies surface isomorphisms with matrix congruences. The ten orbits partition all 20160 matrices exactly. A second program enumerates the full change-of-basis group directly and evaluates every representative over \(\mathbf F_4\) independently. Outputs agree. The two field counts are distinct for all ten and \(N_1\) repeats.

**Originality (PASS, with priority qualification).** Karaoğlu's full finite-field cubic classification already includes these projective classes in principle and supplies \(\mathbf F_2\) point counts and some extension-field incidence data; that classification is **not** claimed as new. Cheng and Kadyrsizova et al. cover geometric \(2\)-bic/non-Frobenius split structure. The inspected principal results do not explicitly establish complete recognition by just \(\big(\#S(\mathbf F_2),\#S(\mathbf F_4)\big)\), nor list the ten signatures as a complete criterion. An older arithmetic table or uninspected supplementary dataset could still cover it. No search absence is treated as proof of global uniqueness.

**Value (PASS).** The exact two-field recognition is an intrinsic arithmetic rigidity result for the ten twists of one geometric cubic type. It settles the smallest natural extension-count cutoff: \(\mathbf F_2\) counts repeat, whereas adding \(\mathbf F_4\) counts suffices. The claim is narrower than a general cubic-surface Torelli theorem and does not depend on that unsupported generalization.
