Independent-edge Kripke frames validate weak connectedness with a Poisson probability \(\exp(-c^2/2)\) in two sharply different windows: \(p_n\sim cn^{-3/2}\) and \(1-p_n\sim c/n\). Validity fails with probability tending to one between the windows, because the sparse obstructions are rare outgoing forks while the dense obstructions are rare mutually nonadjacent successor pairs.
