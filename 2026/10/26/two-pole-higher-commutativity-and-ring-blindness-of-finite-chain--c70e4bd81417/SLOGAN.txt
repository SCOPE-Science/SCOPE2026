For the Heisenberg group over any finite commutative chain ring of length \(m\ge2\) and residue size \(q\), the complete higher commuting-probability hierarchy is a universal two-exponential sequence determined by \((q,m)\). It recovers those two parameters yet cannot distinguish \(\mathbb Z/p^m\mathbb Z\) from \(\mathbb F_p[t]/(t^m)\): their Heisenberg groups have identical hierarchies for every prime \(p\) and are not isoclinic.
