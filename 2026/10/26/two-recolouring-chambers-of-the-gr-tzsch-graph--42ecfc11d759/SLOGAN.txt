The eleven-vertex Grötzsch graph has exactly two single-vertex-recolouring components on its proper four-colourings, each with \(6,240\) states and diameter \(17\), and neither contains a frozen colouring. Odd permutations of the colour names and reflections of its founding pentagon exchange the two components, whereas even palette permutations and rotations preserve them; five colours suffice for connected recolouring.
