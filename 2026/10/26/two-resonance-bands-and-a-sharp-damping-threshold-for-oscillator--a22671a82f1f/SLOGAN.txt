On every infinite regular tree of degree at least three, geometrically damped cosine covariances have an exact two-band positive-definiteness phase diagram: all modulation angles work up to the sharp radius \((2q-1)^{-1/2}\), and beyond \(q^{-1/2}\) only the pure geometric and parity-geometric phases survive. At the intermediate spectral boundary, the right-angle phase survives but loses strict positive definiteness.
