For any rectangular rook graph \(K_m\square K_n\), individualizing **any** subset of vertices with distinct labels makes counting colour refinement exactly recover the pointwise-stabilizer orbits within at most two strict rounds. The orbit counts depend only on the numbers of marked rows and columns, except for a precise square-board nonattacking-rook symmetry, and the two-round bound is sharp on every nonsquare board and every square board of side at least four.
