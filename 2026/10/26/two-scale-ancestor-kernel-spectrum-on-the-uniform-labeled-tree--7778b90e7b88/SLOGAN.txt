For the uniform spanning tree of \(K_n\), the singleton-deletion quadratic ancestor matrix has exactly two eigenspaces: a constant-vector mode tending to \(\pi/2-1\), and an \((n-3)\)-dimensional zero-sum mode with eigenvalue asymptotic to \(1/n\). Exact finite-size eigenvalues come from the Cayley-tree path-length law and symmetry of the three-point tree median.
