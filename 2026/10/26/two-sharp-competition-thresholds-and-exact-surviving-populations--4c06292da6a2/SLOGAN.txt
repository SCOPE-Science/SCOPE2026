For the synchronous discrete ecological competition model with two biomass levels on a ring, two sharp coupling boundaries completely determine the long-run fate of every initially occupied population. Between competition strengths \(1\) and \(2\), isolated two-site patches oscillate and the expected surviving fraction is \(3/8\); above \(2\), oscillations disappear and survival falls to \(1/8\), with exact finite-cycle proofs.
