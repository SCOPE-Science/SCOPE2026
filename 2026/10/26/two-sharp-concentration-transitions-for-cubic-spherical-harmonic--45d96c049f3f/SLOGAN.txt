For cubic spherical harmonics on the antipodal polar-cap region \(x_d^2\ge t\), the optimally concentrated mode changes exactly twice, at \(t=1/(d+2)\) and \(t=3/(d+2)\), in every dimension \(d\ge3\). The first transition is a three-branch coincidence and the second a two-branch coincidence, with all maximizing harmonic subspaces classified explicitly.
