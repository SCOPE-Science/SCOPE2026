For symmetric stochastic mixing on the complete split graph with a clique of size \(r\ge2\) and an independent class of size \(m\ge2\), the exact optimal SLEM is \(m/(m+2r)\) up to \(m=2r\), then \(1-r/m\). Clique-internal transitions are necessary in every optimum when \(m<r\), optional when \(r\le m<2r\), and absent in every optimum when \(m\ge2r\).
