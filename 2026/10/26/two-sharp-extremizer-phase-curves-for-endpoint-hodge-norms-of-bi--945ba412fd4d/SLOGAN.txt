For every \(K_{p,q}\square K_2\) with \(2\le p\le q\), the canonical cut and cycle projection endpoint norms have exact rational formulas with distinct maximizing-edge switches at \(pq=2(p+q)\) and \(pq=4(p+q)+2\). The single exceptional \((3,3)\) cycle norm is \(73/30\), attained by a horizontal edge rather than a vertical edge.
