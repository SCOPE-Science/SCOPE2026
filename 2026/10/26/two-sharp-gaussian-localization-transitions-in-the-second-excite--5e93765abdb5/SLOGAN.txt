In the second excited true-polyanalytic Fock level, Gaussian radial Toeplitz localization has a sharp three-mode norm envelope: the maximizing mode changes exactly at damping strengths \(\tau=1/2\) and \(\tau=1\). Both crossings are transverse, each produces a two-dimensional norm-attaining subspace, and every higher angular mode is strictly suboptimal for every positive damping strength.
