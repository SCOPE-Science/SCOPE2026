# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The exact free resolvent reduces the infinite generalized eigenvalue problem to three scalar monotone eigenvalue branches. Every possible localized eigenfrequency is counted, and the Taylor coefficients follow from explicit second-order expansions at the band edge. The packaged standard-library checker compares five separations, three mass-deficit regimes, and both onset laws. Finite tests are corroboration rather than infinite proofs.

**Originality — PASS, with priority risk.** Montroll and Potts (1955) developed the broader defect Green-function framework; Theocharis et al. (2009) and Man et al. (2011/2012) investigated one and two granular defects. Their accessible materials do not state the sharp triple-defect two-threshold classification or the pair of spacing-independent edge constants. The 1955 full text could not be examined, and historical implicit coverage cannot be excluded.

**Value — PASS.** A three-defect cluster is the first equispaced case with two independent upper-band edge appearances. The exact thresholds and universal 16-to-144 coefficients supply sharp defect-concentration and frequency-onset design laws for ideal mass substitutions, without assuming experimental observability.

**Scope and limitations.** Conservative infinite, identical-spring, identical-deficit three-impurity chains only; not nonlinear granular Hertzian chains, defects that modify contacts, finite measurements, or uniform large-separation asymptotics.
