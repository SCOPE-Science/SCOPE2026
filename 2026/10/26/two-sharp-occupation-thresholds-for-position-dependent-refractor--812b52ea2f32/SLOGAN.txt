For Brownian partial resetting with refractory mean proportional to \(e^{\lambda|y|}\) at reset position \(y\), distant stationary observations switch sharply from exploring to resting at \(\lambda=\sqrt{r/D}(a^{-1}-1)\). Stationarity itself disappears only at the strictly higher threshold \(\lambda=\sqrt{r/D}/a\), where the active occupation fraction vanishes linearly with an explicit coefficient.
