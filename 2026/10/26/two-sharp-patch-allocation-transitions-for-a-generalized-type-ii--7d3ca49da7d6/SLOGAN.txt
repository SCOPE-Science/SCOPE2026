For two fixed predators in identical patches with generalized Type III feeding, the intake-maximizing allocation switches sharply from complete prey concentration to an even split when the normalized prey budget reaches \((2^p-2)^{1/p}\). A smaller threshold \(2((p-1)/(p+1))^{1/p}\) creates a locally intake-maximizing but globally inferior even split, with off-center minima emerging at a universal square-root rate.
