This forces a single inflection of the flow and a single maximum of every fixed-width finite difference. Strict signs at the interval endpoints and near the Allee threshold prove exactly two crossing densities. The attached finite numerical checks supplement the proof.

**Mathematical value — PASS.** Rather than one numerical intervention scenario, the result solves the fixed-dose beginning-versus-end release comparison for every positive horizon and every Allee threshold under a natural conservative-release size condition. Both switching densities have explicit short-time limits and sharp long-time limits. The transitions remain relevant even when final abundance is not allowed to exceed carrying capacity. The result is a principled benchmark for Allee-sensitive release timing, with no unsupported population-level management recommendation beyond the model.
