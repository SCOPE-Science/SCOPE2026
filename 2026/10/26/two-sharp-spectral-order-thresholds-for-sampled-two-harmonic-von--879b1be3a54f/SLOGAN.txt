For the sampled two-harmonic von Mises kernel on every regular \(N\)-gon with \(N\ge7\), all Fourier eigenvalues remain ordered by cyclic distance at every positive scale exactly for \(0\le\rho\le1\). Above this threshold the first two positive modes initially reverse, yet full strict spectral order eventually returns exactly when \(1<\rho<1/[2(d_N-c_N)]\), where the sharp large-scale boundary has different even- and odd-\(N\) asymptotics.
