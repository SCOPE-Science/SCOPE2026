# Scientific review: two sixteen-point genus-two curves over \(\mathbf F_7\)

## Correctness — PASS

The hyperelliptic double cover of a split conic has at most 16 rational points; equality requires exactly eight split unramified rational fibers. The interpolation argument proves that all possible binary-sextic equations are the 924 square-valued vectors lying in a linear hyperplane, so the candidate domain is exhaustive. The squarefree criterion removes 336 to leave 588. All 336 source projective maps and all three square multipliers split the smooth set into exactly two orbits, sizes 84 and 504, covering every eligible polynomial. A separate enumeration of all 823543 coefficient vectors independently agrees. The branch splitting degrees are established by exact factorization and an exact degree-six irreducibility criterion, not bounded point sampling. Finite-field point counts over 7 and 49 establish both Weil quartics by Newton identities and the functional equation. The three programs were executed from the delivered paths and their expected outputs reproduced.

## Originality — PASS, qualified

The sharp 16-point upper bound and attainment are previously known, and no novelty is attributed to those. The distinct claim is the complete classification into two arithmetic isomorphism classes, including both branch partitions, both nonidentical Weil polynomials, and rational automorphism orders. Faber–Grantham–Howe 2022 treats genus-dependent gonality bounds, rather than this two-class arithmetic locus. Zobernig 2021 describes genus-two characteristic strata, not the precise 16-point \(\mathbf F_7\) orbit classification. Rökaeus 2011 explicitly performed a computational genus-two census over small fields, but the inspected full text publishes higher-genus covering curves rather than the specific two-class genus-two result. No directly covering statement was located in targeted mathematical-record and literature searches. There is nonetheless a real priority risk from older small-field catalogues or unpublished census tables. Absence from search results does not establish mathematical novelty in isolation; the originality judgement reflects the inspected implications and this qualified risk.

## Value — PASS

These are precisely the curves saturating the full rational hyperelliptic fiber count over the first odd field larger than five for which the extremal locus has two distinct arithmetic types. An exact isomorphism census of an extremal arithmetic moduli locus is a natural invariant, not an arbitrary sliced statistic. Despite identical rational point counts, the two families have radically different Weierstrass Frobenius splitting and distinct Jacobian isogeny factors; these details matter for constructing and distinguishing optimal-point curves, coverings and arithmetic Jacobians. The full 588-form and two-orbit count provides a reproducible finite classification, with genuine structural meaning beyond merely reproducing the known bound.
