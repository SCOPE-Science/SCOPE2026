For every continuous-time Markov generator on at most two states, the inverse time-averaged transition matrix is an M-matrix; the directed three-cycle is the sharp counterexample at every positive time, with a strictly positive inverse two-jump coefficient. That coefficient has an exact damped oscillatory formula and crosses its limiting value \(1/3\) precisely at times \(2k\pi/\sqrt3\), for every positive integer \(k\).
