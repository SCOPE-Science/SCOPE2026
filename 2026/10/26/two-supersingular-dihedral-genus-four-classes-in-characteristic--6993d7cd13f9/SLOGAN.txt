In characteristic seven, the entire smooth genus-four dihedral hyperelliptic family \(y^2=x^{10}+ax^5+1\) is ordinary except at \(a=0\) and \(a^2=-1\), where exactly two geometric classes are supersingular with \(7\)-rank zero and \(a\)-number two. Both classes become maximal over \(\mathbb F_{2401}\) and share the zeta numerator \((1+49T^2)^4\) over \(\mathbb F_{49}\).
