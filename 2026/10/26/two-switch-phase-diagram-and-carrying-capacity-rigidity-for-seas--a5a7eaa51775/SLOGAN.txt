Two different seasonal Ricker environments can reverse their preferred order exactly twice as initial abundance grows, and this happens precisely when \(R_1>1\) and \(1+(b/a)(R_1-1)<R_2<R_1^{b/a}\) for \(0<a<b\). Equal positive carrying capacities forbid any strict reversal; the pair of crossovers emerges from a tangency with an explicit square-root law.
