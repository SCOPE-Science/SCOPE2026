For the exactly solvable repulsive harmonic Kronig–Penney delta lattice, the \(n\)-th low spectral band, at fixed \(n\), has width \(\frac{2^{n+2}}{n!\sqrt\pi}a^{2n+1}e^{-a^2}(1-(n+1)^2/(2a^2)+O_n(a^{-4}))\) as the cell half-width \(a\) diverges. The explicit second coefficient captures a correction from the neighboring confined-oscillator level that is invisible in the known leading confinement shift.
