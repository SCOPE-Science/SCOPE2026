For fourth-order graph diffusion on highly unbalanced complete bipartite graphs, the exact largest endpoint norm occurs at time \(\theta\sim\log(n/m)/n^2\), yet entrywise positivity does not recover until \(\tau\sim1/(mn)\). Their ratio diverges as \((n/m)/\log(n/m)\), even though the maximal norm excess shrinks as \(2m/n\).
