Every four-run binary word \(0^a1^b0^c1^d\) has minimum string-attractor size either \(2\) or \(3\): three positions are necessary precisely when \((a>c,d>b)\) or \((c\ge a+2,b\ge d+2)\). Across all four-run binary words of length \(n\), the exact three-attractor generating function is \(2z^6(1+z^2)/((1-z)^2(1-z^2)^2)\), and their limiting proportion is \(1/2\).
