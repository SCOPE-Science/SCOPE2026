For stationary Poisson-reset power-law cooling, the long-time temperature-integral variance diverges as resetting becomes rare exactly for exponents below \(1/2\), even though instantaneous temperature variance always vanishes. The normalized correlation time has five regimes with transitions at \(1/2\) and \(1\), and tends to \(3\tau/2\) for Haff's \(\alpha=2\) law.
