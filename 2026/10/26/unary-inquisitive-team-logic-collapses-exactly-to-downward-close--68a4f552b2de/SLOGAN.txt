In pure equality, an inquisitive team-logic formula with at most one free variable defines exactly a downward-closed first-order property of its unary team relation that accepts all empty teams. On all sufficiently large domains, every such property has one of two forms on nonempty teams: \(|X|\le k\) or \(|D\setminus X|\ge d\); the known binary counterexample makes one free variable the sharp arity boundary for the first-order collapse.
