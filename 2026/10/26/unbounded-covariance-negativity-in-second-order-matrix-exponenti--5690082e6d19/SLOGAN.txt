In a two-mode differential Lyapunov equation with positive rank-one forcing, the symmetrized METD1 covariance is indefinite at every unequal pair of decay rates. The published METD2 and METD2RK updates can fare arbitrarily worse: at fixed \(b>0\), the minimum symmetrized eigenvalue divided by the other decay rate \(a\) tends to \(-[1-(1+b)e^{-b}]/(2b^2)\), although the exact covariance remains positive and bounded.
