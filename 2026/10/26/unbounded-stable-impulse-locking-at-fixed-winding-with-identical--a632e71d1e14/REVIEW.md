The Chebyshev identity creates the exact phase-balance function \(H_m=\sin((4m-1)\psi)-\tfrac12\cos\psi\). The full planar impulsive conclusion is a direct, hypothesis-checked application of Buescu–Oliveira Theorem A to each non-simultaneous zero. Winding is fixed by an explicit nonzero vector homotopy. The theorem asserts at least these local orbits, not global exhaustiveness.

The infinitely scalable multiplicity is not a routine parameter tweak of an already exhibited example.
