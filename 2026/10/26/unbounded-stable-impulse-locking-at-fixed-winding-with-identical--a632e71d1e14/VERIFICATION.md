# Verification

The analytic steps are exact radial contraction, the conjugate asymptotic phase, the Chebyshev identity, the degree-counting argument for trigonometric roots, and the homotopy computation for relative winding. Full impulsive periodic states and their three-dimensional transverse stability follow from the cited local theorem at distinct, non-simultaneous simple phase-balance zeros.

The standalone script `CHECKER.py` evaluates \(m\in\{1,2,3,5,8,13\}\), checks the trigonometric identity at representative angles including \(\pi/2\) and \(3\pi/2\), and brackets all zero crossings for each corresponding harmonic. It checks exactly \(2(4m-1)\) zeros and \(4m-1\) downward crossings. Its recorded output is in `CHECKER_OUTPUT.txt`. The finite checks are not a uniform proof, do not integrate the full impulsive system, and do not certify an \(\varepsilon\) threshold.

The theorem is only for some sufficiently small positive impulse strengths depending on \(m\). It asserts no global uniqueness and requires no independent audit claim.
