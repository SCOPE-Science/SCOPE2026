A signed-cut criterion classifies the diagonal heteroclinic obstructions of unit-weight discrete Huygens trees: unequal signed incident sums first become possible on a unique nine-vertex signed tree, yielding coincident seven-dimensional stable/unstable tangent spaces along a nontransverse arc. An infinite family of such unbalanced networks has exact scalar dynamics \(u\mapsto u+(k+1)h\sin u\) for every sufficiently small positive step.
