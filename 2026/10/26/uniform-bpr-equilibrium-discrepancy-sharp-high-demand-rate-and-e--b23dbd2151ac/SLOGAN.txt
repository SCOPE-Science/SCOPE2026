In finite polynomial BPR congestion games, removing all free-flow delay constants changes the unique Wardrop resource loads by a demand-independent amount; on any fixed-demand-proportion ray, the exact set of minimum-cost strategies eventually stops changing. In singleton games the remaining load discrepancy decays at the sharp rate \(t^{1-p}\), giving \(t^{-3}\) for the standard fourth-power BPR latency.
