For a uniform spanning tree of any balanced complete multipartite graph with \(n\) vertices, even when the number and sizes of its equal parts change with \(n\), the number of leaves obeys a universal Gaussian limit: \((L-\mathbb EL)/\sqrt n\Rightarrow N(0,e^{-1}-2e^{-2})\). Exact finite-size second moments and a uniform cumulant argument explain why this fluctuation law agrees for complete graphs and balanced complete bipartite graphs.
