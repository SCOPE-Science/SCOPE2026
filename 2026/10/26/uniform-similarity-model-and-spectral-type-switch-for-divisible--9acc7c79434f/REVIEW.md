# Scientific assessment: divisible monomial cross-adjoint products

## Correctness — PASS
The unit-normalized Bergman monomial computation yields a one-to-one weighted partial permutation of basis vectors with exact weights \(\beta_k=s_{mk}s_{nk}/s_k^2\). Under distinct divisibility, arithmetic factorization partitions positive multiples of the smaller degree into countably many infinite one-sided chains. The gamma-ratio estimate \(\log(\beta_k/c)=O(1/k)\) gives **uniformly** bounded chain conjugacies because the chain indices grow geometrically. The Hilbert direct sum of conjugacies is bounded and has bounded inverse. Similarity transfers the shift spectra and point/residual/continuous types. A separate compact-perturbation argument, not similarity invariance, computes the essential norm. No finite check is treated as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with limits
The October 2026 source examines Schatten membership, compactness and trace of composition–adjoint products, not a uniform bounded similarity to countably many unilateral shifts. Previous monomial-product work in the distinct coprime-degree regime concerns finite-chain spectral geometry; that hypothesis cannot include distinct divisible integers. The present infinite-ray similarity and the resulting reversal between infinite point spectrum and residual spectrum are not logical corollaries of the coprime theorem. Classical unilateral shift spectral theory is cited as a known input, not original. A possible earlier equivalently phrased calculation for divisible monomial symbols remains a residual priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS
A complete canonical similarity classification for a natural parameter class gives more than isolated eigenvalues or standard shift identities: it proves uniform control across infinitely many arithmetic chains and locates an exact divisibility-sensitive point/residual transition throughout an essential disk. The necessity of an \(O(1/k)\) weight defect shows why unrestricted regular-variation assumptions do not automatically yield the model. This is a structural boundary case complementary to the earlier coprime regime, not a renamed single-shift exercise.
