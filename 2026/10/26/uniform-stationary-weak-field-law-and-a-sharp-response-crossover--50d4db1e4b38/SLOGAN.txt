In a threshold-resetting drift-diffusion whose diffusivity is fed back from its resetting rate, the vanishing-field low-diffusivity stationary law is **uniform on the interval leading to the reset threshold**, not concentrated at that threshold. The leading mean correction changes sign across the sharp boundary \(\beta=2\), with a second critical coupling \(\alpha=3/4\) and a positive quadratic correction \(45h^2/16\).
