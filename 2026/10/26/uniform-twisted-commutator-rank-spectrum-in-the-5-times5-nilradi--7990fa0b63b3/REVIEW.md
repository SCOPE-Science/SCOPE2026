# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The six-by-nine coefficient matrix is derived entry by entry from the twisted matrix product; the highest central coordinate is irrelevant. Independent diagonal rescaling of the four initial superdiagonal coordinates justifies the sixteen support masks, with weights \((q-1)^{\mathrm{popcount}(s)}\). The displayed complete support table partitions all five remaining free coordinates, and its seven weighted sums reproduce the claimed polynomials and \(q^{10}(5q^4-5q^2+1)\). Critical pivots and exceptional dependency equations are supplied explicitly; all depend only on invertible coordinates and nonzero twist, so they hold in any characteristic. The standalone finite-field checker agrees for every support and all nonzero twist values in fields of sizes 2,3,4,5. Such finite checks support, but cannot replace, the symbolic argument. A reviewer may independently expand the elimination for all grouped masks.

## Originality — PASS subject to stated residual risk
The closest source, Goodwin–Röhrle arXiv:1209.1289 §3.4, gives five irreducible components of the untwisted commuting nilradical variety, not its complete finite-field centralizer-rank histogram or twisted counts. Huang arXiv:2110.15570 gives unrestricted quantum-plane pair counts and one-nilpotent counts, and Chen–Lu arXiv:1910.02198 describes unrestricted representations, neither imposing the same full flag on both matrices. The actual published-finding corpus comparisons identified no equivalent fixed-flag rank distribution; the closest graph-/filiform-/Kronecker examples concern different algebras. The bounded comparison leaves a genuine risk from the unavailable full text of D. Kobal's 2005 canonical-form study; lack of a located formula is not proof of priority.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The type-\(A_4\) nilradical is the first natural case with five commuting-variety components, and its ten-dimensional fixed-flag pair problem is a motivated refinement of the established geometric decomposition. The result supplies seven complete fiber-rank strata, closed rational polynomial counts valid for every finite field, and the unexpected invariance under all nonzero quantum-plane twist parameters. It distinguishes field arithmetic and fixed-flag incidence information invisible in known component counts and in unrestricted quantum-plane counts. No general dimension theorem is claimed.

## Limitations and comparison risk
No scheme-theoretic assertions, full classification of twisted irreducible components, or extension to sizes beyond five. The complete 2005 Kobal paper was not accessible; the originality conclusion is based on directly inspected nearby preprints and database results, not an exhaustive survey. Tests do not constitute independent external verification.
