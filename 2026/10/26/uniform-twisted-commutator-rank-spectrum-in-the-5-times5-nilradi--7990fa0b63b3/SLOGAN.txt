For strictly upper-triangular \(5\times5\) matrices over every finite field, the full rank distribution of \(Y\mapsto XY-\lambda YX\) is independent of every nonzero twist \(\lambda\). In particular, the number of ordered pairs obeying \(XY=\lambda YX\) is exactly \(q^{10}(5q^4-5q^2+1)\), extending the known five-component geometry to a finite-field fiber census.
