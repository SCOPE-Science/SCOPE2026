For a finite point-source release into a three-dimensional diffusing medium, uniform first-order uptake changes the late post-release concentration-peak lag from inverse-logarithmic at zero uptake to inverse-duration at fixed positive uptake. The exact two-term peak-lag expansion is uniform across all nonnegative uptake rates, with the crossover controlled by \(kT/\log(T/a)\), where \(a\) is the diffusion travel-time scale.
