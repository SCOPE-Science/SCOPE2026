# Scientific assessment

**Correctness — PASS.** Standard classification of idempotents is used with exact citations; commuting idempotents have an idempotent product whose support is the union, and the two-stage adjacent-swap argument proves the converse obstruction. Component absorption proves sufficiency. A four-state run decomposition gives the exact bivariate series. Analytic characteristic roots yield first two moments and concentration. A standalone independent implementation exhaustively tests all sizes through eight positions; finite tests are corroborative rather than the general proof.

**Originality — PASS for the refined rank statistic within examined evidence.** Earlier Sentinelli papers explicitly study the noncommuting graph of this same idempotent family, so priority for the graph or its pairwise criterion is NOT claimed. The full 2020 graph paper remained inaccessible, and coverage of support-refined counts is a residual risk. Elias–Ko classify the idempotents but not the rank enumerator. Li et al give a general Demazure multiplication algorithm, not the theorem. Denton's orthogonal idempotents belong to the algebra rather than these monoid elements. Berenstein et al study a different special abelian parabolic family. OEIS A007070 contains the unrefined integer sequence and recurrence, so numerical priority is expressly excluded. The inspected results and database tables do not give the bivariate union-support enumerator, but an older equivalent refinement remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** Conditional union-support rank is a natural quantitative invariant of the established commuting-idempotent graph; the result determines its entire finite-rank law, a typical density, and variance for arbitrary size. The bivariate refinement is more informative than the already-tabulated unrefined sequence.

**Remaining limitations.** The construction is type-A specific. The literature search does not prove global novelty. Independent audit is not performed.
