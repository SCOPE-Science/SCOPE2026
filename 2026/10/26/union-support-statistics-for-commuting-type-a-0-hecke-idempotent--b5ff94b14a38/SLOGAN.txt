For ordered commuting parabolic idempotents in type-A \(0\)-Hecke monoids, the number of pairs by cardinality of their combined generator support admits an exact all-rank distribution and a limiting support-density law. The complete union-support polynomial has bivariate generating function \(1/[1-(1+3z)t+2z^2t^2]\), refining the already known A007070 total-count sequence.
