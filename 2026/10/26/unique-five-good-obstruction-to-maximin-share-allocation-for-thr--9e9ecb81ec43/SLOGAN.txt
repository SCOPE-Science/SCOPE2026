For three agents and five indivisible goods, exactly 30 of the 15,625 ordered triples of MMS partitions can obstruct selecting one pairwise-disjoint block per agent, and all 30 form one relabeling class: a common singleton plus the three different perfect matchings of the other four goods. Every failure of exact MMS for normalized monotone valuations at this size must exhibit that partition obstruction, although the obstruction pattern by itself is not sufficient for failure in an arbitrary valuation profile.
