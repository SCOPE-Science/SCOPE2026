A variance-standardized Brownian motion with Poissonian proportional resetting has exactly one radius where its symmetric interval coverage equals Gaussian coverage; as resetting weakens, the crossover tends to \(\sqrt3\), or 91.6735% nominal Gaussian coverage. The total-variation and Kolmogorov distances have explicit, sharp first-order coefficients in \((1-a^2)/(1+a^2)\), even though earlier results only establish weak convergence to the Gaussian law.
