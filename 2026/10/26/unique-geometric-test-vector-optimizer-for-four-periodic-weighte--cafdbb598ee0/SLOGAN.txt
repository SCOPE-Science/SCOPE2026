For every \(0<q<1\) and \(s,t>0\), the four-periodic geometric-shift numerical-radius lower bound has a unique maximizer \(z_*\in(1/3,1)\): its degree-eight stationarity polynomial has exactly one simple zero in \((0,1)\). This answers the full-parameter uniqueness question for the geometric test-vector bound, not for all numerical-radius-maximizing vectors.
