For every cycle prism \(C_n\square K_2\) with \(n\equiv2\pmod3\) and \(n\ge5\), the minimum paired-domination number is \(2(n+1)/3\), and exactly \(n(2n+5)/3\) labelled vertex sets attain it. Every minimum set induces a unique perfect matching, characterized by one two-column gap and otherwise three-column gaps between matching-edge starts.
