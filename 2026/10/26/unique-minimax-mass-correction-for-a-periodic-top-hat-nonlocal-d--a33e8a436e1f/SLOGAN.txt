For the periodic one-dimensional top-hat nonlocal diffusion operator, the ideal inverse-Poisson-plus-mass preconditioner has a unique coefficient that minimizes the worst spectral condition number over all periods and horizons. The kernel-mass coefficient \(\beta=1/2\) has condition number greater than four, while \(\beta=1/6\) has condition number at most two; the unique minimax coefficient is given by an explicit one-variable maximum.
