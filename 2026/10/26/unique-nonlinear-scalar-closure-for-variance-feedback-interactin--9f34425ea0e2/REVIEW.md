# Scientific review

## Correctness
PASS. The classification checks all \(N\ge2\), \(d\ge1\), and \(p\ge0\). The only exceptional fourth-power identity follows from the zero-sum three-point Newton relation; two matched-variance configurations rule out all \(N\ge4\), strict Jensen inequality rules out \(N=3,d\ge2\), and strict convexity of a two-exponential function leaves only \(p=2\) for \(N=3,d=1\). Independent Itô reconstruction yields the exact squared-Bessel dimension \(2+27/\lambda\) and its noncentral chi-square transition law. Positive squared-Bessel paths and Poisson-mixture negative moments justify boundary and moment statements.

## Originality
PASS, qualified. Hambly--Nguyen (arXiv:2610.20771v1, original HTML sections 2, 3, and 7 inspected) has general shape-dependent variance dynamics and exact linear-interaction solutions, but no complete shape-closure classification or explicit exceptional nonlinear inverse-gamma law was identified. The squared-Bessel distributional input is standard and is not claimed as new; see Malham--Wiese--Xu (arXiv:2412.16655, Section 2). Targeted searches for cubic-interaction three-particle variance, equivalent Bessel transforms, and power-law shape-closure identities found no directly covering work. Risk: an unindexed algebraic reduction or general diffusion theorem could subsume the statement. Independent priority scrutiny is needed.

## Value
PASS. Scalar closure is the exact structural obstacle in a nonlinear feedback population model: classifying every exception explains why the generic shape process cannot simply be discarded. The unique nontrivial exception has an exact distribution at each finite time, a nondegenerate rescaled consensus law, a universal leading mean, and noise-dependent critical higher moments; these sharpen qualitative consensus and distinguish typical from heavy-tail behavior.

## Limits
The result does not classify every possible scalar Markov observable; it classifies global configuration-independent dissipation of the given power-law form. The distributional law is for the positive-variance cubic three-particle, one-dimensional, critical-feedback case only. The finite verification is supplementary, not a proof of infinite-time claims.
