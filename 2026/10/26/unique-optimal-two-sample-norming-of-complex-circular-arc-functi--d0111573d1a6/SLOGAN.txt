For the complex affine-function plane on any proper unit-circle arc, two point evaluations have the unique optimally conditioned placement \(e^{\pm i a_\theta}\), where \(a_\theta=2\arctan(\sin(\theta/2))\). The exact worst-case norming loss is \(\sqrt{1+\sin^2(\theta/2)}\), and an explicit formula determines the loss for every other pair of sampling points.
