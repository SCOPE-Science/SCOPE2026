All geometrically smooth blocking plane cubics over \(\mathbf F_3\) form exactly one \(\operatorname{PGL}_3(\mathbf F_3)\)-orbit, represented by \(Y^2Z-X^3-X^2Z+XZ^2=0\). The orbit has \(936\) projective equations, stabilizer order \(6\), and six rational points on each curve; the field-size existence boundary itself was already known.
