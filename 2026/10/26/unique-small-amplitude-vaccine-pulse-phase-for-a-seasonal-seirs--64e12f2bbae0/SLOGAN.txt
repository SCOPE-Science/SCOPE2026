For weak sinusoidal transmission seasonality and a small annual susceptible-only pulse in a homogeneous SEIRS model, the exact linear invasion exponent has one optimal pulse phase, located \(\arctan[\omega/(b+\delta)]/\omega\) ahead of peak transmission to first order. This timing law is independent of exposed progression and infectious recovery rates, although the improvement magnitude is not; it does not assert finite-amplitude or nonlinear optimality.
