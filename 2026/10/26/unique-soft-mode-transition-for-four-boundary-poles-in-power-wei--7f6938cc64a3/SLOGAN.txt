In the power-weighted Bergman energy of four equally spaced boundary Cauchy poles, the least-curvature perturbation switches exactly once as the exponent increases above \(2\). A uniquely defined exponent \(\alpha_*\in(2,3)\) makes the whole nonrotational Hessian isotropic; below it the first Fourier modes are softest and above it the alternating mode is softest.
