In the interacting Pólya social-pressure model on any connected undirected nonbipartite network, nonconsensus equilibria are never multiple: when both endpoint Perron radii exceed one, there is exactly one interior equilibrium, and the time-averaged stochastic opinions converge almost surely to that same deterministic vector. A strict odds-ratio/Jensen argument resolves the interior-uniqueness question explicitly left open by the motivating model paper.
