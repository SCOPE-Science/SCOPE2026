For every cyclic Latin Alexander quandle of odd prime-power order in its defining characteristic, the quandle algebra is uniserial on **both** sides: its entire left-, right-, and two-sided ideal lattices coincide with the \(p^s+1\) powers of the augmentation ideal. Ideal multiplication follows the truncated valuation law, exposing a sharp modular contrast with semisimple Fourier ideal lattices.
