Near the critical reset rate that traps a prescribed fraction of a heterogeneous planar diffusion inside a disk, the last finite crossing time has a universal \(\log(1/\epsilon)-(\beta+1)\log\log(1/\epsilon)\) clock with an explicit Bessel-function intercept. Its sharp finite-time crossover distinguishes the Itô, Stratonovich and Hänggi--Klimontovich radial laws even when their leading logarithmic divergence agrees.
