# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The exact quantile is reconstructed from the published stationary rank inverse and its edge-shock jump: for \(1/2<b<1\), the positive quantile clamps at rank \(2b-1\); for \(b\ge1\), it equals \(u+(b^2-1)\operatorname{artanh}u\). A direct monotone-ratio comparison, followed by an explicit monotone-likelihood covariance identity, proves strict ordering for all integer even moments, not merely sampled ratios. Beta-generating functions give exact second/fourth moments and the cumulant polynomial, whose unique positive root follows from Descartes' sign rule. The included checker independently validates the symbolic identities and representative integrations. The paper's underlying stationary limit is an input, not reproved here.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk
The primary source (Touzo–Le Doussal, arXiv:2308.06118v1) explicitly gives the stationary density and a general moment integral, including the variance; those results are preexisting and receive full attribution. Its main text and supplement (including the general moments section) do not state a parameterwise ordering of *all normalized even moments* across both shock and smooth phases or the exact single kurtosis sign boundary. The 2025 follow-up (arXiv:2502.09466v1) treats a different trap and nonreciprocal models. Targeted literature and research-index comparisons revealed no equivalent claim. A published moment representation makes the result derivable, and an unindexed equivalent derivation remains possible; no uniqueness of discovery or independent audit is asserted.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The shape-ordering theorem gives a complete non-Gaussian hierarchy for an interacting active-matter stationary law, across a morphological support transition with atomic edge clusters. It locates a sharp kurtosis-neutral point \(b_*>1\) distinct from both phase boundaries \(1/2\) and \(1\), separating compact/edge-dominated statistics from heavy-tailed statistics. The comparison holds at every even order, not just for one numerical moment or a change of notation.

## Scope and risk
No result is claimed for finite-population stationary statistics, for traps, or for positive thermal noise. General star-order tools are older; the theorem's new part is their rigorous model-specific ordering, including the shock quantiles. Equivalence in less-indexed work remains the principal originality risk. The result is not independently verified.
