In two narrow, symmetrically separated time windows, successive even–odd bandpass-prolate concentration crossings occur at bandwidths \(W_n(\varepsilon)=\frac{n\pi}{2a}(1+\varepsilon^2/(6a^2)+O_{a,n}(\varepsilon^4))\). This sharp positive aperture correction locates transverse, exactly double leading eigenvalues and quantifies the parity switches of the optimal bandlimited signal; the qualitative existence of bandpass degeneracies was already known.
