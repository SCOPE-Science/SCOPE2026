For every non-self-opposite modular symbol alphabet and every bounded-zero-run subshift, every higher-lag difference lift has an exactly computable finite palindrome-complexity profile: its longest palindrome has length \(m+L\), with explicit reflection-orbit counts below the cutoff. In particular, strictly ergodic Fibonacci lifts of every lag have no longer palindromes, each lag at least two supports nonconstant palindromes unlike the first-difference case, and an even lag produces alternating higher and lower tail counts.
