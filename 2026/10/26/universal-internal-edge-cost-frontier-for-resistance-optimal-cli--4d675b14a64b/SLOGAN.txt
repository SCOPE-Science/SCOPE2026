Optimal conductance allocation on every heterogeneous clique bouquet has a closed form under petal-wise edge costs and a common budget. An internal clique edge receives positive conductance exactly when its cost relative to a hub spoke is below the universal threshold \(2N/(N-1)\), independent of clique size; the minimum sum of effective resistances equals \(S^2/B\) for an explicit cost-weighted radical sum \(S\).
