At the critical Dirichlet index for normalized monomial boundary zeros on the complex ball, every fixed power \((1-u)^r\) has the same leading squared optimal-approximation error \(C/\log K\). Its exact excess at the next order is universal: \((\log K)^2(E_{K,r}-E_{K,1})\to2CH_{r-1}\), regardless of dimension or monomial exponents.
