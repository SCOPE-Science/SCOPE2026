A strictly positive stationary Gaussian spectral density can produce a diverging correction to the expected analytic zero count solely through jump discontinuities: each interface contributes \(\frac{(w_+-w_-)^2}{4\pi^2w_-w_+}\log\frac1{1-r}\) to the universal deficit. The same coefficient arises from an explicit arctangent boundary profile, independently of jump positions and other smooth spectral details.
