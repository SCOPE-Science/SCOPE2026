In a uniform fixed-edge random graph, covariance between an isolated-vertex factorial count and a non-induced tree-copy count follows a finite-size single-crossing law independent of tree shape and admissible edge budget. For fixed factorial order and a tree size proportional to graph order, the eventual sign changes from positive to negative at \(\rho=0.79681213\ldots\), determined by \((1-\rho)e^{2\rho}=1\); in the independent-edge model the covariance is always negative at tree orders at least two.
