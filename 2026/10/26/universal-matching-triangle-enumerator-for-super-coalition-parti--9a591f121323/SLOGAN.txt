For any isolate-free graph on at least seven vertices with super-domination number at least \(N-2\), every super-coalition partition has two, three or four blocks, and its complete counting polynomial is determined by the edges, disjoint edge pairs and triangles of an auxiliary admissible-deletion graph. Four blocks are possible exactly when that auxiliary graph contains a triangle; for complete multipartite graphs this happens exactly when three parts have size at least two.
