# Scientific review: Shannon–Simpson covariance under finite Dirichlet priors

## Correctness — PASS
The theorem has a complete analytic moment proof for every real positive parameter vector and every finite \(m\ge2\). Both entropy and quadratic concentration are bounded. The quadratic Dirichlet tilt is normalized correctly, the digamma recurrence is invoked at positive arguments, the cross moment is explicitly reconstructed, and the rearrangement identity yields a strictly negative answer because the pair-mass term is positive. Exact-rational and symbolic calculations provide independent spot checks but are not treated as proof of the all-parameter statement.

## Originality — PASS (provisional historical scope)
Huillet–Paroissin (2008) handle symmetric finite Dirichlet species samples and the mean Simpson statistic; Archer–Park–Pillow (2014) derive entropy moments; Cerquetti (2014) unifies entropy families and computes individual index moments in a broader prior model but does not exhibit the arbitrary asymmetric cross-order covariance formula. The closest published occupancy-richness/Gini–Simpson record concerns different sample variables. Targeted searches for aliases, opposite-sign Gini–Simpson normalization, joint entropy–concentration moments and stronger general-prior theorems disclosed no covering result. The residual risk of an uncited equivalent formula remains.

## Mathematical value — PASS
The exact covariance decomposition identifies a universal sign and the mechanism preventing sign reversal under unequal Dirichlet parameters; a sharp, explicitly quantified pair-mass bound has an equality characterization. The two-category fixed-total example makes a substantive distinction between equality in that lower bound and global extremality, which is useful when selecting asymmetric Dirichlet priors for uncertainty over ecological diversity indices. No downstream theorem beyond this joint-diversity fact is claimed.

## Limits and source comparisons
The result is finite-dimensional and population-level. It is not a statement about estimated richness, plug-in entropy, all Bayesian priors, or a fixed-total global extremum. The relevant primary works are Huillet–Paroissin, arXiv:0809.4181; Archer–Park–Pillow, JMLR 15 (2014); and Cerquetti, arXiv:1404.3441. Independent historical priority assessment and expert validation remain outstanding.
