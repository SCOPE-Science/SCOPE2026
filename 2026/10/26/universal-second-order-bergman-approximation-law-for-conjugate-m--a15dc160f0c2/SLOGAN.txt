For every fixed conjugate monomial order \(m\ge3\), the sharp weighted Bergman approximation error has a universal two-term semiclassical law: \(C_{\alpha,m}^2=K_m/(\alpha+1)+O_m(\alpha^{-3})\), where \(K_m=4m^2(m-1)^{m-1}/(m+1)^{m+1}\). All maximizing polynomial degrees lie within a bounded distance of \(\alpha(m-1)/2\); the cancellation of the next correction is shared by every order.
