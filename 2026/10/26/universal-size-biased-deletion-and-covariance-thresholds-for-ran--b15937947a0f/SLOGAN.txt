Weighting an independent-edge uniform hypergraph by any connected component on \(s\) vertices leaves a fresh binomial hypergraph on the other \(n-s\) vertices. Consequently the covariance of isolated vertices with every nonnegative size-\(s\) component statistic has the same exact, strictly ordered zero \(p_*(n,r,s)\), with endpoint scales ranging from giant-component emergence to isolated-vertex disappearance.
