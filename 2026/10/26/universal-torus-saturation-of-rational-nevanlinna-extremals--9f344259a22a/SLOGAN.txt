Every extreme bidisk rational-inner function arising from the Fagrell–Luger family \(h(z,w)=-1/(\varphi(z)+w-1/w)\), for nonconstant real rational Herglotz–Nevanlinna \(\varphi\) of degree \(m\), has a \(\mathbb T^2\)-saturated scattering-stable denominator: exactly \(2m\) torus base points, each of multiplicity two. Their entire intersection divisor is controlled by the pole divisor of \(\varphi\), with universal binary resultant \(-A^4\) for its homogenized denominator \(A\).
