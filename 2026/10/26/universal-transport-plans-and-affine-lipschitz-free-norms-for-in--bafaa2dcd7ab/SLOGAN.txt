For every finite multipartite metric with unit cross-part distances and independently varying within-part distances \(t_i\in[1,2]\), the real Lipschitz-free norm is exactly \(P+\max_i(t_i-1)(U_i-P)_+\), where \(P\) is the total positive mass and \(U_i\) the total variation in part \(i\). A single transport coupling attains that norm for every admissible parameter vector, so every mass vector has an affine norm profile across the entire parameter cube.
