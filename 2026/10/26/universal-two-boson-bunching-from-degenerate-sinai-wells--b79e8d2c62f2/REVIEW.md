# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The stationary number-sector population law follows from an explicit ratio of forward/reverse inclusion rates and irreducibility; its general form is independently established by Grosskinsky, Redig and Vafayi (2011). For fixed chain length, the complete-well occupation fraction limit uses a dominated geometric-tail argument and the uniform weak-composition Riemann-sum limit. The random-walk minimum-count generating function is obtained by strict meanders and Catalan excursions; coefficientwise convergence follows from absolute summability at the critical fugacity. The bivariate series yields the exact finite-chain bunching generating function and the limiting geometric sum. Rational finite-state comparisons and combinatorial enumeration passed 142018 deterministic assertions. There is no inference of an infinite theorem from finite tests.

**Originality — PASS under self-review, with qualification.** Longhi's paper treats the same binary-disorder Lindblad hopping but develops mainly single-particle localization, Sinai transport, and dilute mean field, not this exact high-loading bosonic observable. Grosskinsky–Redig–Vafayi already prove the general canonical reversible measure and a unique-favored-site condensation theorem, so neither is claimed as original here. The new assessed statement is the **full ensemble-specific repeated-well multiplicity law** and the **exact finite-chain and infinite-chain same-site bunching values** under fixed positive binary disorder. Garbe et al. treat driven reservoir boundaries with homogeneous bias, and Jatuviriyapornchai et al. treat homogeneous small-diffusion scalings, neither the present ordered limiting regime. Random-walk excursion results are classical and may implicitly give the multiplicity law alone; broader inclusion-process or random-environment synthesis remains a residual priority risk.

**Value — PASS.** The prior motivating quantum-lattice article leaves the dense many-boson regime unresolved by its dilute mean-field analysis. An experimentally interpretable second-order bosonic bunching observable quantifies that regime exactly. The finite-chain generating function and its universal large-chain constant classify the influence of degenerate Sinai wells, and the discontinuity at zero binary disorder distinguishes the singular high-loading limit from the clean case. The result is a model-specific natural invariant, not an arbitrarily selected parameter table.

**Boundaries.** No fixed-density joint limit, nonzero coherent hopping, full quantum stationary uniqueness, or transport-time theorem is claimed. The prior mathematical product-measure theorem must be credited and the possibility of an implicit stronger special-case coverage remains.
