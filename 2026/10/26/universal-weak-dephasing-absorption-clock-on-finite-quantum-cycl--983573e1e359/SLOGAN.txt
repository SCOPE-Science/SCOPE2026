For a quantum particle on any finite cycle with one irreversible trap and homogeneous site dephasing of rate \(\kappa\), the mean absorption time satisfies \(\kappa T\to2\operatorname{Tr}(Q\rho)\), where \(Q\) projects onto reflection-odd dark states. A particle localized away from the trap and its even-cycle antipode has universal leading lifetime \(1/\kappa\), independent of ring size and sink strength.
