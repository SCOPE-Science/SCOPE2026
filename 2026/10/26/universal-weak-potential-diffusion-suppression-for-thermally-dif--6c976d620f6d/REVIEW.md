# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The potential-amplitude perturbation applies to the simple principal eigenvalue of the tilted elliptic generator for positive Brownian diffusion. The derived second-order resolvent matches the asserted rational kernel exactly; every numerator coefficient is positive and differentiating the kernel proves the strict monotonicity. Fourier truncations independently corroborate the algebra. The proof is asymptotic for a fixed smooth potential, not a global-amplitude bound.

**Originality — PASS, with priority limitations.** The D>0 ratchet model and drift are in Roberts--Zhen (2023), but the paper's observables do not include this long-time diffusion law. Le Doussal--Majumdar--Schehr (2020) give dispersion for zero independent Brownian noise. Pavliotis (2005) gives a general colored-Markov-noise corrector encompassing telegraph forcing but does not evaluate the all-frequency weak-potential telegraph multiplier or prove its sign and strict frequency ordering. A general unpublished or overlooked weak-potential spectral identity could imply the result; independent priority review is required.

**Scientific value — PASS.** A spectrum-wide strict sign and wavelength-ordering theorem quantifies how a small periodic landscape suppresses transport spreading in a persistent active suspension. It supplies a normalized prediction for comparing equal-potential-amplitude spatial harmonics and recovers the classical passive limit without claiming it is new.

**Limits.** \(D,\gamma,v>0\), smooth periodic \(U\), \(\varepsilon\to0\); no finite-amplitude, zero-temperature, or experimental signal-to-noise claim.
