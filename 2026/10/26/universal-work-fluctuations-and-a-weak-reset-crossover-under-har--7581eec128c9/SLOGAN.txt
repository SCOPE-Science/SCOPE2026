For Brownian harmonic partial resetting, the mean work per completed reset is independent of reset strength, but its **exact long-run variance** is \(\kappa^2D^2[2m^2(1+a^2)/(1-a^2)+3\operatorname{Var}(T)]\). In the weak-reset window \(n(1-a^2)\to\ell\), normalized cumulative work converges not to a Gaussian but to the integrated square of a stationary Ornstein--Uhlenbeck process.
