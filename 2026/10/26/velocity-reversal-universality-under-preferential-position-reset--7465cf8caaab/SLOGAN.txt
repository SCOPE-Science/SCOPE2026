For a run-and-tumble particle that repeatedly relocates to a uniformly sampled historical position, the coefficient \(2v^2/[r(2\gamma+r)]\) of logarithmic mean-square spreading is **identical for every velocity-reversal probability** \(\eta\in[0,1]\), even though the short-time cubic term detects \(\eta\). A mixed position–orientation plateau, that logarithmic coefficient, and the cubic term together determine the tumbling rate, relocation rate and reversal probability uniquely when speed is known.
