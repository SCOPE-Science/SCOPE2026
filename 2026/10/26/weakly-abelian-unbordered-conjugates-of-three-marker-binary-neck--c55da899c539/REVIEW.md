# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The theorem's domain and distinct-rotation convention are explicit. The linear three-one criterion is proved in both directions: strict signed chord heights separate every proper prefix density from every proper suffix density; the reverse direction gives actual prefix/suffix length witnesses in three exhaustive cases. Reversal supplies the zero-start condition. The three-gap sign pattern establishes the crossing count; Burnside's lemma and arithmetic-progression parametrization give all fixed-length counts. The accompanying program directly computes weak Abelian borders without relying on the proof's shortcut and checks every necklace through 28 zeros and the distribution through 149 zeros. Finite tests corroborate rather than replace the proof.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
The closest primary text is Charlier–Harju–Puzynina–Zamboni (arXiv:1501.07464v1): Lemma 2 gives only a sufficient strict-chord condition, and the paper expressly disproves its general converse. The present result proves its special converse for three markers and derives new cyclic arithmetic and exact frequencies. Goč–Rampersad–Rigo–Salimov count standard Abelian borders (equal-length tests), while Clokie–Gabric–Shallit count ordinary literal unbordered conjugates. Neither directly determines the claimed weak Abelian cyclic statistic. The related literal three-marker criterion likewise cannot determine the weak counts: \((0,1,2)\) and \((0,1,3)\) have the same literal count but different weak counts. Semantic searches in the published research index and public literature did not identify covering equivalents. Unrecognized earlier formulations remain a genuine priority risk, not a claim of certified uniqueness.

## Value — PASS
A general weak-Abelian-border lemma is known not to be reversible. The result isolates an infinite natural family in which it becomes exact, classifies *every* cyclic conjugate, identifies an arithmetic-progression exception, and enumerates each exceptional type at every word length. The zero/one/two transition and overwhelmingly two-rotation asymptotics are new structural information about sparse cyclic pattern ambiguity, not an arbitrary finite table or a restatement of ordinary unborderedness.

## Scope and residual risk
The published sufficient lemma is credited; no general reverse implication, higher-marker formula, independence claim, or external certification is asserted. Historical priority remains qualified by possible unknown earlier statements under other terminology. The automated checker is finite; all-length claims rest on the supplied proof.
