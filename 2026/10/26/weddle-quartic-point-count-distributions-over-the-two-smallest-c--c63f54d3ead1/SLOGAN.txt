For Weddle quartics attached to six ordered general-position rational points, all 120 projective frame classes over \(\mathbb F_8\) have 145 rational points; the 2184 classes over \(\mathbb F_{16}\) split into exact point-count strata \(417^{1440}\), \(433^{144}\), and \(449^{600}\). These are counts on the singular quartics rather than on their K3 resolutions.
