All weight-zero Rota–Baxter operators on the unital local algebra \(k[t]/(t^3)\) have a three-parameter normal form cut out by \(a(a-2c)=0\). Over \(\mathbb F_q\) the operator count is \(q^2\) in even characteristic and \(q(2q-1)\) in odd characteristic, with respectively \(q+2\) and \(q+4\) conjugacy classes; nilpotency index three occurs only in odd characteristic.
