For the characteristic-two Weddle quartic, the unique torus fixed point of the Hutchinson involution carries exactly the same infinitesimal scheme as the quartic's local Jacobian singular scheme. Both are \(\operatorname{Spec}k[u,v,t]/(u^2,v^2,t^2)\), of length \(8\), for every choice of pairwise distinct nonzero Weddle parameters.
