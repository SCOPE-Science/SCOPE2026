# Scientific assessment: minimum-set reconfiguration in rook graphs

**Correctness — PASS.** Use the tree-plus-isolate model with an opposite-side leaf. Basis exchanges connect trees at a fixed isolate and fixed leaf; explicit exchanges then connect the thick-board isolate classes. In the thin case the dominant column gives the claimed invariant, and the omitted-row and added-row coordinates give exact adjacency. The two small boundary cases are included.

**Originality — PASS for the exchange theorem.** The earlier rook-polynomial finding covers the all-set criterion and exact minimum-set enumeration; both are outside the revised contribution. The general exchange-graph theory and scalar theorem do not give the transition. A later complete-prism finding states the same thin decomposition, and the triangular-graph finding treats a different family. No exhaustive priority guarantee is made.

**Value — PASS.** The transition determines exactly where minimum-set reconfiguration is obstructed in a natural two-parameter family and identifies the resulting component graphs, not just the number of optimal solutions.
