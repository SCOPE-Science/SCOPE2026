For every \(m,n\geq3\), deleting \(t\) disjoint edges from \(K_{m,n}\) yields an explicit five-term zero forcing polynomial determined by the matched white vertices. The zero forcing number drops from \(m+n-2\) to \(m+n-3\) after the first deletion and to \(m+n-4\) after the second, then stabilizes; the formula also counts every minimum forcing set of every crown graph.
