For every field, the nilpotent squarefree radical \(J_r=(x_1,\ldots,x_r)\) in \(K[x_1,\ldots,x_r]/(x_1^2,\ldots,x_r^2)\) is zero-product determined at arbitrarily high nilpotency index. Its unitization has a completely explicit universal zero-product bilinear quotient \(K\oplus J_r\oplus J_r\oplus J_r^2\), with defect \(2^{r+1}-r-2\) and a sharp symmetric-map transition at \(r=2\).
