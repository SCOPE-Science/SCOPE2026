On every two-hub clique book \(K_2\vee(mK_s)\) with \(m,s\ge2\), the unique conductance-budget minimizer of the all-pairs Kirchhoff index **deletes the existing hub-to-hub edge**, while assigning strictly positive weight to every other edge. The optimal two positive weight classes and the minimum Kirchhoff index have explicit radical formulas that remain globally optimal against asymmetric allocations.
