The exceptional ordinary genus-six Artin–Schreier curve \(y^3-y=x+1/(x^3-x)\) over \(\mathbb F_3\) has zeta numerator \((1-T+3T^2)^2(1+T-2T^2+3T^3+9T^4)^2\). Its Jacobian is isogenous over \(\mathbb F_3\) to the square of an elliptic curve times the square of a simple abelian surface, and has exactly \(1296\) rational points.
