# Scientific review: fresh-identity group monoids

## Correctness — PASS

The restriction of any reflexive multiplication-compatible relation on \(G\sqcup\{\ast\}\) to \(G\times G\) is a subgroup containing the diagonal because \(G\) is finite; hence it is determined by a normal subgroup. Each cross-section is empty or a finite subgroup and is forced into that normal subgroup. The converse closure check treats all block products. Symmetry and transitivity then give exactly \(s(N)+1\) symmetric relations and two congruences for each \(N\). Independent exhaustive direct-relation checks for five small groups, plus all-form checks for \(S_3\), confirmed the formulas. No finite experiment is used as an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS, with residual risk

The 2026 Barber–Ruškuc paper, Section 4, explicitly presents the adjoined-identity example to show its Clifford construction is incomplete, and proves an upper bound on the coefficient; it does not provide the complete triple parametrization or exact count. Their earlier paper supplies only the finite-group diagonal/congruence baseline; the published left-group calculation concerns different semigroups. Searches for the exact special family, aliases, normal-subgroup formulas, and stronger Clifford claims did not expose a covering theorem. A specialized enumeration under older terminology is an unresolved historical-priority risk.

## Mathematical value — PASS

The complete relation classification directly resolves the precise elementary Clifford example singled out in recent literature. The coefficient is governed by subgroup lattices of the normal subgroups, and the cyclic-prime-power specialization proves that no positive lower bound can depend only on the fixed two-element idempotent semilattice. The inclusion criterion also determines the complete order structure of the relations. These are motivated structural and numerical invariants, not arbitrary small cases.

## Limits

Only finite groups and exactly one fresh identity are covered. The classification is not claimed for all Clifford semigroups. Enumeration distinguishes labeled relations, and historical priority has not been independently certified.
