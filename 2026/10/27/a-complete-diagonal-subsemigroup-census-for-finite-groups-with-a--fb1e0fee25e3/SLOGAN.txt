For every finite group \(G\), adjoining a genuinely new identity yields a monoid whose diagonal subsemigroups are classified by a normal subgroup of \(G\) and two independently chosen subgroups (or empty cross-sections). Its exact DSC coefficient is \(2k(G)/\sum_{N\trianglelefteq G}(s(N)+1)^2\), and it tends to zero for adjoined-identity cyclic prime-power groups even though their idempotent semilattice never changes.
