For a symmetric coupled two-dimensional quadratic, uncorrected RMSProp has exactly \(0\), \(1\), \(2\), or \(4\) prime-two orbits as learning rate crosses three explicit thresholds. The final pair consists of noncorner asymmetric cycles absent from corner-only existence guarantees, and its merger threshold is \(2\varepsilon(a+b)/(a-b)^2\).
