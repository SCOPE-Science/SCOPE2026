On every unit-conductance clique windmill \(K_1\vee(tK_m)\) with \(t\ge2,m\ge3\), deleting two hub spokes within the same clique uniquely maximizes Kirchhoff-index damage, and the exact second-worst pattern changes from an incident spoke–rim pair at \(m=3\) to spokes in different cliques at \(m\ge4\). Two edge failures compound strictly only when they meet inside the same clique; even two hub spokes sharing the universal vertex have exactly additive damage when they belong to different cliques.
