Among integers with exactly two different prime factors, all adjacent divisor gaps divide the integer precisely in two explicit families: \(2^a3^b\) with \(a\le4\) or \(b\le2\), and \(2^a p^b\) with Fermat prime \(p=2^a+1\ge5\). The boundary is sharp: \(27,32\) are consecutive divisors of \(2^5 3^3\) but their gap \(5\) is not a divisor.
