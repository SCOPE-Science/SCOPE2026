For the canonical upper-half-plane three-jet Bergman Gram kernel with \(\xi=(1,u+iv,b)\) and real \(b\), the exact number of attainable scalar zero fibres is always \(0\), \(2\), or \(4\). A sharp circle \(u^2+v^2=5|b|/6\) for \(b<0\), together with an even-quartic discriminant boundary, determines the complete phase diagram.
