For any finite graph, the endpoint norms of both canonical cut and cycle projections under independent integer edge subdivisions are bounded by the largest norm at a corner of the allowed length box. The underlying functions are quasiconvex in each length separately, but not monotone: subdividing the central edge of a diamond to length \(2\) uniquely lowers its cycle-projection norm from \(3/2\) to \(4/3\) before the norm rises again.
