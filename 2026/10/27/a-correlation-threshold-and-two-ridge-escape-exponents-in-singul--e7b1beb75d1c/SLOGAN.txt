In the minimal correlated total-least-squares problem with one unpenalized variable, the threshold \(\beta=1+\gamma^2\) separates attained from nonattained minima. Adding a vanishing full ridge sends optimal solutions to infinity at rate \(\varepsilon^{-1/4}\) above this boundary but at the different rate \(\varepsilon^{-1/6}\) exactly on it, with sharp leading constants.
