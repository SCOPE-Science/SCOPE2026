When weak absorption and weak bulk resetting are tuned together in a sticky run-and-tumble search, the rescaled completion time acquires an explicit crossover law with Laplace transform \(1/(1+c\sqrt z+A z/\sqrt{z+R})\). The Lévy mechanism separates untempered binding delays from reset-tempered excursions, and the far-tail amplitude jumps at zero resetting despite weak convergence of the full distributions.
