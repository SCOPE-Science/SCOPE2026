For the completed fourth-order Gauss–Legendre heat step, every connected unweighted \(d\)-regular graph on \(N\ge3d\) vertices eventually has a negative transition along an existing edge, despite unconditional spectral stability. Among all unweighted square tori \(C_m\square C_n\) with \(m,n\ge3\), exactly \(C_3\square C_3\) retains entrywise positivity for all step sizes.
