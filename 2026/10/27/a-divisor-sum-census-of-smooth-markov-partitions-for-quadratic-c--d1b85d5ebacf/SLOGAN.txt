Among expanding integral quadratic companion toral maps with \(|\operatorname{tr}A|,|\det A|\le H\), the number admitting a smooth Markov partition is exactly \(2\sum_{j=1}^{H}\lfloor H/j\rfloor-2H+2\lfloor\sqrt{H/2}\rfloor+2\lfloor\sqrt{H/3}\rfloor\). Their sharp relative frequency is \((\log H+2\gamma-2)/H+O(H^{-3/2})\): smooth boundaries are arithmetically rare even though there are \(\asymp H\log H\) such systems.
