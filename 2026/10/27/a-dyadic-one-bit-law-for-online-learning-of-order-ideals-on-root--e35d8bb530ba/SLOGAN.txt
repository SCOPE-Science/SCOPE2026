For the ideal-membership concepts of a rooted poset consisting of one minimum below disjoint chains, the Littlestone dimension equals \(\sum_i\lfloor\log_2(\ell_i+1)\rfloor\), with exactly one additional level if and only if every arm length has the form \(\ell_i=2^{k_i}-2\). This sharp dyadic effect is caused by a single extra hypothesis, the empty ideal, and persists with arbitrarily many arms.
