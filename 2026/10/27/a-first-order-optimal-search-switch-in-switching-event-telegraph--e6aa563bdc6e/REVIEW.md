The mean transform itself is known machinery and is not assigned priority; the sharp first-order global switch and fold are the compared claims. Access to the Evans full manuscript and to Li et al. full text was incomplete, so implicit stronger coverage remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** The nontrivial physical design choice is whether each collision opportunity should reset a target-facing particle or reverse its direction. Fixing the event opportunity rate produces an exact and unique discontinuous optimum as target distance grows, alongside a second threshold for the emergence of metastable strategies. The asymptotic interior optimum explains the transition to diffusion-style resetting at long distances.

**Scientific limits.** Positive equal-magnitude velocities, initial positive heading, exponential decision clock, full zero-time reset, one unbounded half-line and optimization of the expected hitting time only. Earlier renewal formulas are not novel. Independent verification of mathematical priority remains outstanding.
