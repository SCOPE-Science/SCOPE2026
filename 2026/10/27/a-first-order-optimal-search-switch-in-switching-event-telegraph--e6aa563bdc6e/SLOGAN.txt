For a telegraph searcher that must choose between resetting toward its target and reversing direction at a fixed total decision rate, the globally optimal reset probability jumps from \(1\) to \(0.1204656\ldots\) at the sharp target-distance threshold \(\lambda L/v=3.7295732\ldots\). This first-order switch separates an all-reset ballistic strategy from a mixed persistent-diffusive strategy, with a unique optimizer on either side.
