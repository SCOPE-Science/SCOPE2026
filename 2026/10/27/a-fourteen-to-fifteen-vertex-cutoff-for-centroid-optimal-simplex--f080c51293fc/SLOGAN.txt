For a regular Euclidean simplex with \(n\) vertices, extending real Lipschitz data linearly to the centroids of **every nonempty face at once** has the centroid-only sharp norm \(\sqrt{2(n-1)/n}\) precisely for \(3\le n\le14\). Starting at \(n=15\), even the full centroid together with seven-vertex face centers forces a strictly larger norm; the positive range has an exhaustive rational certificate.
