For every smooth complex plane curve of degree \(m\ge4\), the rank-three length-\(m\) secant Quot locus of \(\mathcal O_C(2)^{\oplus2}\) has a generically smooth threefold component parameterized by its line-section divisors and constant quotient directions. Its tangent dimension is exactly three at every transverse line section; for degrees six through eight this exceeds the natural dual-section version of a proposed Martens-type numerical bound, without contradicting that conjecture as literally printed.
