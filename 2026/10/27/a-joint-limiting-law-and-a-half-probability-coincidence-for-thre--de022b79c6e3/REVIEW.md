# Review: three overpartition minimal excludants

## Correctness
**PASS.** The local restricted generating functions and two exact event enumerators follow from the two choices of overline for each occupied size. A saddle-point coefficient ratio is justified by a Gaussian central integral and an untouched block of unrestricted part sizes controlling all other arcs. The joint distribution, independent geometric coordinate, and the literal half-coincidence frequency follow from these formulas; exact small-size enumeration supports the algebra. The finite experiment itself does not prove the limit.

## Originality
**PASS, with bibliographic limitations.** The same-object source Donato (2025) introduces both the overlined-only and total-part mex beside the prior nonoverlined convention, but develops individual sums rather than joint laws. Dhar--Mukhopadhyay--Sarma (2024) investigate different ordered and odd-class variants, Aricheta--Donato (2024) give the original nonoverlined mex, and Veena--Fathima (2026) give odd-mex summatory congruences. Full public theorem-bearing text was read for the first, third and fourth listed preprints; the complete original 2024 JIS article was not inspected, only its abstract and the follow-up exposition. No relevant published-record comparison implies the joint law, though database absence cannot certify priority.

## Mathematical value
**PASS.** The three established conventions on one overpartition exhibit sharply different probabilistic behavior: one geometric first gap, two coupled Rayleigh-type first gaps, plus an honest integer-level coincidence proportion of one half. These are motivated simultaneous invariants, not arbitrary restrictions of a familiar one-variable asymptotic.

## Limitations
No rate of convergence or growing-parameter uniformity is provided. Other definitions that impose an order on overlined and plain symbols are outside scope. More general prior results could cover the coupling and should be independently checked before claiming historical priority.
