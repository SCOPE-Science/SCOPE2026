The three natural overpartition first-gap conventions exhibit two different scales: the overlined-only gap has a limiting geometric law, while the total-part and nonoverlined gaps converge jointly to \((T_0,\min(T_0,T_1))\) after scaling by \(n^{1/4}\), with independent identically distributed Rayleigh-type \(T_0,T_1\). Exactly half of large overpartitions have *identical* total-part and nonoverlined minimal excludants in the limiting proportion.
