A three-message clique-block recursion computes every coefficient of the \(P_3\)-convex-set size polynomial for any block graph, with a linear-size arithmetic circuit even when clique sizes are unbounded. For cliques joined at one common vertex, the entire polynomial collapses to \(1+(\sum_i(m_i-1))z+z\prod_i(1+z^{m_i-1})\).
