For \(B=b^k\) with \(k\ge2\), an arithmetic progression contains infinitely many integers whose digit sums agree in bases \(b\) and \(B\) and divide the integer if and only if its base-divisible residue is a permitted low-block prefix. Modulo \(B^v\), exactly \(b^v\) of the \(B^v\) residue classes are attainable, and the others are completely excluded by the digit-sum equality.
