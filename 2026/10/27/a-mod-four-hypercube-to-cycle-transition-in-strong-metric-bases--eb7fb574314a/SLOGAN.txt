For every \(n\ge6\), the single-vertex-exchange graph of all minimum strong metric bases of the squared cycle \(C_n^2\) is a hypercube \(Q_{n/2}\) exactly when \(n\equiv2\pmod4\), and otherwise it is the ordinary cycle \(C_n\). Thus the number of optimal landmark sets jumps from linear to exponential in one residue class, although their exchange space remains connected.
