For the restricted-nilpotent commuting triples of the characteristic-\(p\) Witt algebra, \(p\ge5\), the reduced Grothendieck class is exactly \(\mathbb L^{(3p-5)/2}(\mathbb L+1)^2-\mathbb L^{p-2}(\mathbb L^2+\mathbb L+1)\). Consequently, its finite-field point counts and Hasse–Weil zeta function admit closed formulas in every extension degree.
