# Scientific review

## Correctness — PASS
The proof identifies all three Cantor--Bendixson strata, obtains the unique shift action on the singleton stratum, and uses a finite sliding-block radius to show that every sufficiently wide two-particle gap is pointwise fixed. The converse finite-support gap maps are homeomorphisms with explicit inverses. The direct-product law, embedded infinite finitary alternating group, and local virtual-abelianness follow from this exact classification. The finite executable test exercises the orbit action and local consistency; it is not used to infer universal statements.

The closest countable-sofic precursor arXiv:1408.6695v1 focuses on Cantor--Bendixson rank two; the present system has rank three. Focused searches for gap-permutation descriptions, restricted wreath products, and two-marker shifts found no statement implying the complete group calculation. The 2014 dissertation is a plausible incompletely inspected historical source. An equivalent earlier result cannot be ruled out.

The exact group description distinguishes residual finiteness from local residual finiteness in a canonical, countable, zero-entropy symbolic system. Its simple embedded finitary alternating subgroup demonstrates non-residual finiteness without an auxiliary full shift, while the local virtual-abelianness sharply contrasts the finitely-generated obstruction constructions of the motivating work. The exact quadratic block complexity and rank-three stratification make the structural example natural rather than an arbitrary parameter slice.
