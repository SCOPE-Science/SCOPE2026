For a stationary Gaussian power series whose spectral density has two double zeros merging into a quartic zero, the expected disk-zero deficit has an exact uniform \((1-r)^{-3/4}\) crossover profile \(F(\lambda)=\frac12\operatorname{Re}(\lambda^2+2i)^{-1/2}\). Its unique maximum occurs at nonzero separation \(\lambda=\sqrt{2/\sqrt3}\), where the coefficient is \(3^{3/4}/8\), strictly larger than the quartic-collision coefficient \(1/4\).
