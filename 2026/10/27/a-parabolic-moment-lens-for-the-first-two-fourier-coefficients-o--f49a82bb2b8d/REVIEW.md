**Correctness (C): PASS.** The coefficient normalization is explicit, and the upper/lower curves follow from exactly integrated one/two-arc extremizers. Dual trigonometric quadratics have signs matching the maximizer/minimizer sets and yield exact weighted symmetric-difference deficits. The weak-* compactness plus a four-piece perturbation proves realization of every interior moment pair by an indicator. Rotational projection establishes both complex disk inequalities and all equality classes. The standard-library checker passed 41,864 supplementary assertions; these do not replace infinite-dimensional proofs.

**Originality (O): PASS, best knowledge only.** The source most directly motivating the question is Huang (arXiv:2407.19424), which reconstructs arc measures from Fourier series, not a sharp fixed-mass feasible region for the first two indicator coefficients. Courtney (arXiv:1002.0639) addresses injectivity of a finite Fourier vector for a *known at-most-two-arc* mask, not optimization over arbitrary masks. Chalendar et al. (arXiv:1307.2646) address Hardy-space distance/extremal functionals of fixed indicator symbols. Targeted published-result searches for Fourier indicator joint moments, arcsine moment lens, coefficient disks, and polynomial equivalences returned no mathematical statement giving or dominating both sharp parabolas and the complex disks. Older Chebyshev-system moment theory may give an equivalent specialized formula after calculation; it remains a residual bibliographic risk.

**Mathematical value (V): PASS.** This is a complete feasible-body classification for a naturally constrained Fourier-moment problem, rather than a single routine inequality. It includes the entire continuously varying mass/first-moment domain, the two exact nonlinear boundary curves, uniqueness and stability through explicit deficit integrals, and rotationally covariant sharp complex bounds. These constraints describe the first two off-diagonals in finite Hardy/Toeplitz compression matrices of arbitrary circular apertures. General moment convexity and rearrangement are acknowledged as inputs, not asserted as new results.

**Limitations.** The complex disk inequalities are individually sharp but not asserted to be jointly sufficient. The real body is exact only for cosine projections of the two lowest nonconstant modes. Independent mathematical priority review has not been performed.
