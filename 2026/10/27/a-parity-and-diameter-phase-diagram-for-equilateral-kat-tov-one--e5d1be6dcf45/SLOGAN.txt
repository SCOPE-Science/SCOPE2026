One-point types over an equilateral \(n\)-set in the universal integer-distance metric space admit a closed count for every ambient diameter and every parameter distance. Their asymptotics have a sharp phase: precisely for odd parameter distance \(s\ge3\) and ambient diameter \(s\le D\le(3s-1)/2\), almost every type has one uniquely short distance, producing a factor of \(n\) in the leading count; otherwise the count has a pure exponential leading term.
