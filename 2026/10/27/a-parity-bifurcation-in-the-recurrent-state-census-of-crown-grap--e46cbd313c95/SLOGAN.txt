On the crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M_n\), synchronous binary majority updating has \((\binom{n}{n/2}+2)^2\) recurrent configurations when \(n\) is even, but only \((n+3)\binom{n}{(n-1)/2}+4\) when \(n\) is odd. The already-recurrent fraction falls only as \(2/(\pi n)\) along even \(n\) but as \(\sqrt{2n/\pi}\,2^{-n}\) along odd \(n\); every odd-case recurrent orbit except one two-cycle is fixed.
