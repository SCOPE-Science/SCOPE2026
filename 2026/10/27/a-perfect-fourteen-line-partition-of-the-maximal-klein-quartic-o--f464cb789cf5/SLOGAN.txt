The exceptional \(\mathbb F_{25}\)-maximal genus-three Klein quartic has exactly \(56\) rational points and an optimal partition into fourteen four-secant rational lines, giving \(\tau(C)=14\). Its complete rational-line spectrum is \((N_0,N_1,N_2,N_3,N_4)=(63,168,196,0,224)\), established by an exhaustive finite-field checker.
