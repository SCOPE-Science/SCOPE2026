For every crown graph \(K_{n,n}-M\) with \(n\ge3\), an explicit bivariate polynomial counts every locating-dominating set and every optimal configuration. For \(n\ge5\), deleting one perfect matching from \(K_{n,n}\) exactly halves the location-domination number, and there are \(n(2^{n-1}-2n)\) minimum sets.
