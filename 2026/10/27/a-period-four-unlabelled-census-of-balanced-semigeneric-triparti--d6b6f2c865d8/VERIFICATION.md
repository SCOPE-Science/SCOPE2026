The standalone `verify.py` reconstructs the fixed-point affine equations independently from the counting derivation. Three edge-orientation blocks each have \(2n-1\) binary coordinates. The program computes exact ranks of their linear fixed-point constraints over \(\mathbf F_2\), including the orientation-complement term whenever a class permutation reverses a block's order.

All three class-permutation conjugacy types are exhaustively checked for every internal permutation triple when \(1\le n\le4\). Their Burnside averages and final orbit counts match the closed formula without floating-point approximations. Further sizes are checked for integer-valuedness and consistency of the Burnside decomposition. The saved `verification_stdout.txt` is generated by running `python3 verify.py` from these exact sources.

The exhaustive experiments concern only these finite sizes; they are not a proof for arbitrary size. The general proof is the symbolic cycle-index and fixed-character argument in `RESULT.md`. Independent audit was not performed.
