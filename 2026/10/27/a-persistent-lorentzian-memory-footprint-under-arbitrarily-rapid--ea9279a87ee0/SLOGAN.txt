Resetting a Brownian probe arbitrarily fast cannot eliminate the time-domain signature of a harmonically coupled bath that is never reset: after multiplying the stationary fixed-positive-lag covariance by \(r^2\), it converges to \(e^{-t/g}\). The rescaled power spectrum retains the Lorentzian \(2g/(1+g^2\nu^2)\), and the exact normalized correlation time is always greater than \(1/r\), approaching \((1+g/2)/r\).
