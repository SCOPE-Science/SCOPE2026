# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS

For any returning three-step orbit, conservation and the graph Laplacian give a constant firing count across vertices. Counts zero and three imply fixed points; at one firing the three local inequality intervals are necessary and sufficient and yield an independent product because neighbor firing phase schedules are already fixed. The recurrent-box bound follows by backward propagation of any excessive chip count, and the chip complement rigorously exchanges counts one and two, giving the exact chip-total palindromic identity. The bipartite multinomial formula and the \(K_{3,b}\) specialization follow by summing phase assignments, not by extrapolating finite computations. Direct code verifies small graphs and their full chip histograms.

## Originality — PASS (bounded)

The 2010 bipartite period feasibility and activity-harmonic lemma are explicitly prior art; the theorem here is the all-connected-graph weighted **state** and chip-total enumeration for exact period three. The inspected 2011 period-two description does not imply this. The 2012/2015 firing-pattern article studies which temporal patterns occur, and does not provide the coupled labelled state count on an arbitrary fixed graph or the chip-total polynomial. published-finding corpus searches for chip-firing phase colorings, exact period-three state counts, and recurrence zeta profiles found no covering statement; the nearest structured graph-automaton result uses binary parity updates rather than integer threshold-degree firing. An unindexed or uninspected equivalent is possible.

## Value — PASS

Periodic configurations at fixed conserved energy are natural invariants of fixed-energy sandpile/parallel chip-firing dynamics. The interval-weighted graph-colouring representation is a structural reduction for **every** connected graph, explicitly counts recurrent configurations rather than just establishing possible periods, and yields exact energy-resolved multiplicities. The \(K_{3,b}\) formula resolves a nontrivial infinite one-parameter labelled family at a minimal shore size. It is not presented as a new existence-threshold theorem.

## Scope and risks

The result says nothing about periods other than three, basins of attraction or transient convergence. Database searches cannot establish uniqueness; some relevant mathematical literature may be unindexed or inaccessible. Source versions and the 2010 feasibility threshold are distinguished from the new census.
