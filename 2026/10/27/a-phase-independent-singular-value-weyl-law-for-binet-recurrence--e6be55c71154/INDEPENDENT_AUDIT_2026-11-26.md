## correctness

PASS

The normalized monomial matrix is a backward shift with weights U_n/√n and kills constants; hence the nonzero squared singular values are the decreasing rearrangement of b_n/n for b_n=|U_n|². If R>1, the R-normalized sum of dominant modes has positive Cesàro mean square by distinct-phase geometric sums, so it is bounded away from zero along infinitely many n even after the subdominant error; the weights are unbounded. For R<1 they decay geometrically, and for R=1 they are O(n^{-1/2}), proving the compact extensions. At R=1 the closure of the phase powers has Haar empirical law by character averages and density of trigonometric polynomials; the decaying remainder preserves it. Its mean square is the sum of squared unit-mode amplitudes. To count b_n/n>ε, truncate at B/ε for B>sup b_n; on each block with εn between two continuity points, prefix differences give limiting upper and lower sums for the distribution's survival function. Comparing the count at (c_U±δ)/k traps the kth ordered square, including in the presence of ties or zeros. Schatten and rearranged-log claims follow; geometric summation by parts gives the stronger O(1) monomial-index remainder. In Jackson's case the root 1 is always critical for |q|≤1, and the distinct root q contributes exactly one additional squared amplitude only on the circle. The periodic, irrational and Jackson numerical or exact finite checks are consistent.

## originality

PASS

The supported distinct claim is the sharp pointwise ordered Weyl constant for finite critical Binet symbols and the Jackson boundary consequence, not the recurrence quotient, monomial identity, or elementary weighted-shift compactness. The closest abstract density-of-states theorem compared gives a Dixmier trace, which is an averaged conclusion and does not imply this pointwise asymptotic under that conclusion alone. The other close Fock and Jackson results use different operators or different norms. No equivalent exact law or broader applicable pointwise theorem was established in the sources checked.
