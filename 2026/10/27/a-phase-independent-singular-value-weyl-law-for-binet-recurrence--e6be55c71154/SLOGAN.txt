For Ward-normalized finite Binet recurrences, the recurrence derivative on the classical Gaussian Fock space has a complete characteristic-root-radius compactness trichotomy; at the unit spectral radius its singular values obey \(s_k\sim\sqrt{\sum_{|\lambda_j|=1}|A_j|^2/k}\), independently of root phases and arithmetic cancellations. The sharp coefficient of the Jackson derivative jumps by a factor of two when its parameter crosses from the open unit disk to any nontrivial point of the unit circle.
