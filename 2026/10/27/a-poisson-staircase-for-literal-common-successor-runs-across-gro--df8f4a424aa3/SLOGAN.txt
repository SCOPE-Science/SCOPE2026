For \(r\) independent random successor orders, common literal runs of \(\ell\ge2\) edges first appear at the sharp scale \(r\asymp n^{(\ell-1)/2}\), where their count converges to \(\operatorname{Poisson}(c^2/2)\) and their order indices and labelled vertices are asymptotically disjoint. The longest shared run undergoes an exact two-point staircase transition between lengths \(\ell-1\) and \(\ell\).
