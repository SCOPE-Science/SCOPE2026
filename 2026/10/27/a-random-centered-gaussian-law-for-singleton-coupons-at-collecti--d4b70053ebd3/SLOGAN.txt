At the moment a uniform coupon collection is completed, the number of coupons appearing once has a known exponential-scale fluctuation, but its residual fluctuation is Gaussian after centering at the observed completion-time predictor \((\log n)\exp[-(T_n/n-\log n)]\). The residual, divided by its predicted standard deviation, converges jointly with the Gumbel completion time to an **independent** standard normal.
