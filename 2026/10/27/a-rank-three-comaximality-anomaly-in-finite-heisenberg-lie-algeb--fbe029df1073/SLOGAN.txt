In the comaximal graph of the finite Heisenberg Lie algebra \(H_{2r+1}(\mathbb F_q)\), an exact Gaussian–symplectic formula counts every edge. For fixed \(r\), the probability that two distinct uniformly chosen vertices generate the algebra tends to \(4/9\) when \(r=3\) and to \(1\) in every other rank, a sharp rank-three asymptotic anomaly.
