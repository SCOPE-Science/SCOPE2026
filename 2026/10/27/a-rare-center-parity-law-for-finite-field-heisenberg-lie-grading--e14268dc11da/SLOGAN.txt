For the \((2m+1)\)-dimensional Heisenberg Lie algebra over any finite field, the complete \(\mathbb Z_2\)-grading count splits into \(m+2\) explicit automorphism orbits. In a uniformly chosen grading, the center lies in degree zero with probability asymptotic to an explicit parity-dependent theta constant times \(q^{-m}\); conditioned on this rare event, the symplectic dimension split has a discrete-theta limit.
