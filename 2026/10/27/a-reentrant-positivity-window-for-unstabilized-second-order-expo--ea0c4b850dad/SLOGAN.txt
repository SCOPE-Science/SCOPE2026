A standard second-order exponential Runge–Kutta method can contract energy at every timestep on a scalar positive decay equation yet produce a negative solution only at intermediate step sizes. As the remainder-to-stiff split ratio \(\rho\) vanishes, that sign-failure window begins near \(\log(1/\rho)+\log\log(1/\rho)\) and ends near \(1/\rho\), while its most negative multiplier scales as \(-\rho/\log(1/\rho)\).
