At fixed mean reset rate, slowly switching telegraph-rate disorder always delays Brownian first passage, but sufficiently rapid switching accelerates it whenever the mean rate exceeds the classical Brownian optimal threshold. An exact matrix-square-root formula yields the correction \(H_\nu-f(m)=-\delta^2 f'(m)/(2\nu)+O(\nu^{-2})\), proving that a genuine speed-induced reversal occurs at finite switching rates.
