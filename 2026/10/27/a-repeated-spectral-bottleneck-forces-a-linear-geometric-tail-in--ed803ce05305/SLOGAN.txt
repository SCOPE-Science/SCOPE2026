For a repeated random-permutation card-merger process started from any fixed \(n\ge3\), the probability of surviving beyond \(t\) rounds is asymptotic to \(c_n t2^{-t}\) with an explicit positive rational coefficient, whereas the two-card process has survival exactly \(2^{-t}\). The linear correction is forced by the unique pair of consecutive transient states sharing maximal holding probability \(1/2\).
