Three-dimensional complex evolution algebras with square equal to the idempotent-free algebra \(u^2=0,\ uw=u/2,\ w^2=u+w\) and three distinct natural square-lines form a complete rigid two-parameter stratum. Their isomorphism classes are exactly the points \((p,q)\in\mathbb C^2\) with \(-4p^3-27q^2\ne0\); every algebra in the stratum has no nonzero idempotents and has trivial automorphism group.
