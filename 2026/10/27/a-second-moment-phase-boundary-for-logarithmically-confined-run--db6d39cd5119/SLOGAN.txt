A logarithmically confined run-and-tumble search can have finite mean hitting time but infinite variance: for starts beyond the velocity turning point, the exact second-moment threshold is \(D_{\mathrm{eff}}=1/3\), three times stricter than the known mean threshold. Below it, the second moment grows like \(x^4/[4(1-D_{\mathrm{eff}})(1-3D_{\mathrm{eff}})]\) at large starting distance.
