For the positive causal pulse \(g_d(t)=(1+dt^2)e^{-t}\mathbf1_{t\ge0}\), its self-ambiguity function is zero-free exactly when \(0\le d<4(1+\sqrt6)/5\). At the threshold a zero-delay frequency pair appears, while every larger coefficient creates nonzero-delay ambiguity zeros—even though both constituent exponential moments separately have zero-free ambiguity.
