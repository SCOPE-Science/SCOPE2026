# Scientific review

**Correctness — PASS.** The finite-test backward-Euler objective reduces exactly to two scalar multipliers. Their independent product gives the second-moment factor and sample-path Lyapunov exponent. Strict convexity and monotonicity supply precisely two second-moment crossings when one test has negative effective stiffness. A one-variable rational maximization gives the sharp inclusive angular threshold. The rational heat-mode witness and packaged checker independently confirm sensitive identities. The pole is explicitly excluded because the minimizer loses uniqueness, not treated as an infinite numerical state.

**Originality — PASS, with bounded bibliographic risk.** The motivating parabolic finite-test MinRes paper acknowledges unresolved residual complements; classical Petrov–Galerkin inf–sup theory and operator-turning-angle inequalities provide general background. Neither the material available from that primary source nor the closest mathematical records located state the full randomized two-test angular/timestep phase diagram, including reentrant mean-square stability and an open almost-sure/second-moment separation. The independent-product law and the stand-alone angular optimization are acknowledged as known ingredients, not claimed original. Full appendix-level inspection of the motivating paper was unavailable; an earlier specialized treatment remains possible.

**Value — PASS.** Independently reselecting a small variational test space is a natural idealization for randomized finite-test residual measurements. The result gives a sharp dimensionless compatibility tolerance and quantifies an otherwise hidden failure of residual-only diagnostics. The reentrant timestep behavior and rigorous divergence of moments despite typical-path decay warn against equating successful sampled runs with reliable error control. It identifies the role of the unresolved complement rather than disputing the published fully discrete reliability framework.

**Limitations.** Two heat eigenmodes, fixed one-dimensional trial space, independently resampled symmetric test directions, constant timestep, exact arithmetic, and unforced dynamics. This is a new randomized-test specialization, not a claim about implementation in the cited method or about arbitrary parabolic discretizations. The result has not undergone independent assessment.
