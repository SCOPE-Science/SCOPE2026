Balanced random resampling of a one-dimensional heat-step residual test has a sharp universal safety angle \(2\arctan\sqrt{m/L}\); above it the same minimum-residual update is mean-square stable for small and large steps but unstable for intermediate steps. Every supercritical configuration also has an open timestep window of almost-sure exponential decay alongside divergent second moments, despite zero measured residual at every uniquely solved step.
