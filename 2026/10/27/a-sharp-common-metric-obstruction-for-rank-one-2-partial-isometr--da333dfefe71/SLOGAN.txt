For every nonzero rank-one \(2\)-partial isometry \(T\) on a complex Hilbert space, the canonical inverse \(2T^*-T^{*2}T\) is Moore--Penrose for some **single** equivalent Hilbert metric exactly when \(\|T\|^2<2\). The sharp rank-one threshold answers an explicit common-metric question negatively already in dimension two; on the nondegenerate positive side the least possible metric condition number is \(2/(2-\|T\|^2)\).
