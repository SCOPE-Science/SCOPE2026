For the Type-II additive reduced-basis Richardson step on an SPD star matrix with reduced diagonal \(a\), isotropic omitted diagonal \(b\), and cross-coupling norm \(k\), the spectrally optimal complement weight is \(1/b\), but a strictly Euclidean-contractive weight exists exactly when \(k<a\). The complete safe interval is explicit, and even with a fixed spectral radius \(1/\sqrt2\), every complement weight can allow arbitrarily large one-step Euclidean error and residual amplification.
