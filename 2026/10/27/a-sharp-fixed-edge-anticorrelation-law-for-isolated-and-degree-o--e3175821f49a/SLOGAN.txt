In the uniform fixed-edge random graph \(G(n,m)\), the counts of isolated and degree-one vertices have strictly negative covariance at every nondegenerate edge count admitting an isolated vertex, and zero covariance at all other edge counts. For the matched independent-edge ensemble \(G(n,p)\), the classical covariance instead becomes positive once \(np>2\), giving an exact sign reversal at identical expected edge counts.
