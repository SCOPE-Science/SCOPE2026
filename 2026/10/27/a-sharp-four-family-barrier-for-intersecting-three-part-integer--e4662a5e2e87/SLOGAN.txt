Among three-part integer partitions of a fixed sum, every pairwise intersecting family with no common part has at most four members, and this is sharp at every sum \(n\ge8\). Consequently the exact maximum is \(\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor\) except at \(n=8\), where it is four; all maximizers for \(n\ge11\) are explicitly determined full stars.
