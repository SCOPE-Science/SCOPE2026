On an equilateral metric star with at least three leaves, the \(p\)-energy Dirichlet extension has sharp two-data Lipschitz amplification \(2\) for every \(1<p<\infty\) except at \(p=2\), where it drops to \(2(m-1)/m\). For nonquadratic exponents the supremum is never attained, and explicit leaf perturbations exhibit different sharp cusp exponents on the two sides of \(p=2\).
