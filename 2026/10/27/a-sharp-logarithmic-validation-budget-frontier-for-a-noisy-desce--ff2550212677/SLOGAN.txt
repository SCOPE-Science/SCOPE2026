For a strongly convex two-component quadratic, an independently validated unit stochastic-gradient step eventually abandons the **exact global minimizer almost surely** at every fixed finite batch size, and cannot return. The sharp escape-versus-retention boundary for batches growing as \(c\log k\) is \(c=1/D_{\mathrm{KL}}(3/4\,\|\,1/2)\); at the boundary, a further \(\tfrac12\log\log k\) correction is critical.
