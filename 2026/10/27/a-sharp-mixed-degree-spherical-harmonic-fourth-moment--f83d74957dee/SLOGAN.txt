On the mean-zero spherical-harmonic band \(\mathcal H_1\oplus\mathcal H_2\) of \(S^2\), the sharp complex \(L^2\)-to-fourth-moment constant is \(375/112\), strictly greater than that of either degree alone. Every extremizer is a constant-phase zonal mixture with exactly \(15/32\) of its squared \(L^2\) energy in degree one and \(17/32\) in degree two.
