# Scientific assessment

## Correctness — PASS
The type-to-ideal/filter correspondence is proved with necessary and sufficient transitivity conditions. The transfer-matrix argument and the independent path-splitting argument establish both infinite formulas. The Fibonacci–Lucas identity makes the one-chord spectrum strictly monotone in the cyclic separation. Degree and common-neighborhood invariants distinguish the two-chord twins; a complete sixteen-subset calculation establishes the sharp eight-parameter example. Direct exhaustive tests reproduce the small instances and exact formulas for a range of larger inputs. These tests are corroboration, not an infinite proof.

## Originality — PASS (qualified)
Relevant preceding work counts ideals of unperturbed crowns and gives general one-type encodings, hardness, and one-comparability sensitivity for arbitrary finite height-two parameters. Such results allow particular type numbers to be calculated, so the isolated closed-form evaluations are not treated as independent claims to novelty. The assessed contribution is the **structural inverse and noninverse classification**: one added comparison is recovered by a single count, whereas two admit explicit all-size nonisomorphic twins and a smallest-size counterexample. No equivalent statement or stronger explicit classification was found in the completed source and semantic comparisons. The 2005 garland source was available at abstract level but its scanned full text could not be read due to an access/rate-limit error, and implicit prior coverage remains possible.

## Mathematical value — PASS
One-point type counts are natural invariants of named finite parameter sets in homogeneous structures. The problem asks how much finite-parameter geometry a single number retains under a minimal local perturbation. All examples have the same cardinality and incidence-degree multisets within each comparison. The theorem gives a sharp boundary between recovery and failure, and an infinite explicit collision family whose equality has a structural path-decomposition explanation rather than relying only on computation. The limited family and elementary ingredients are acknowledged without inventing downstream applications.
