On a two-component strongly convex quadratic, constant-step random reshuffling can have strictly smaller mean-square error at **every epoch** yet deterministically avoid an entire neighborhood of the minimizer, while with-replacement SGD has stationary support reaching the minimizer. The sharp reshuffling gap opens at \(\eta>1-1/\sqrt2\); at \(\eta=1/2\) reshuffling has one-fifth the mean-square error but the two epoch laws separate in total variation as \(1-2^{-m}\) after \(m\) epochs.
