The geodesic kernel \(e^{-a d(x,y)}\cos(\omega d(x,y))\) on real hyperbolic five-space has a sharp positive-definiteness threshold for every \(a>4\): exactly \(\omega^2\leq a^2-12/5-(2/5)\sqrt{5a^4-20a^2+16}\). A rational spherical transform makes the low-frequency boundary and the distinct high-frequency failure witness explicit.
