For Brownian target search with \(n\) independent walkers and \(k\) independent Poisson reset clocks, assigning nearly equal numbers of walkers to each clock minimizes the entire first-detection-time law in stochastic order, while assigning \(n-k+1\) walkers to one clock and one to every remaining clock maximizes it. The exact, strict comparison holds at every positive time and positive reset rate and follows from log-convex moments of schedule-conditional survival.
